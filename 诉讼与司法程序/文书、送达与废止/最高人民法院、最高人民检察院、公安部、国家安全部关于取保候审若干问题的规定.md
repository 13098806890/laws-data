# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部关于取保候审若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：0000-00-00

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了严格执行刑事诉讼法，保证正确适用取保候审，根据刑事诉讼法和有关法律规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law and relevant legal provisions for the purpose of strictly implementing the Criminal Procedure Law and ensuring the correct application of release on bail pending investigation or trial.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人取保候审的，由公安机关、国家安全机关、人民检察院、人民法院根据案件的具体情况依法作出决定。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院决定取保候审的，由公安机关执行。国家安全机关决定取保候审的，以及人民检察院、人民法院在办理国家安全机关移送的犯罪案件时决定取保候审的，由国家安全机关执行。

**Article 2** The decision to release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial shall be made by the public security organ, state security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court according to law based on the specific circumstances of the case.  
Where a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court decides to release on bail, the public security organ shall execute such decision. Where a state security organ decides to release on bail, or where a People's Procuratorate or People's Court decides to release on bail when handling a criminal case transferred by a state security organ, the state security organ shall execute such decision.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人决定取保候审的，不得中止对案件的侦查、起诉和审理。严禁以取保候审变相放纵犯罪。

**Article 3** Where a decision is made to release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial, the investigation, prosecution, and adjudication of the case shall not be suspended. It is strictly prohibited to use release on bail as a disguised form of indulgence in crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人决定取保候审的，应当责令其提出保证人或者交纳保证金。  
对同一犯罪嫌疑人、被告人决定取保候审的，不得同时使用保证人保证和保证金保证。

**Article 4** Where a decision is made to release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial, such person shall be ordered to provide a guarantor or pay a surety bond.  
For the same criminal suspect or defendant, release on bail shall not be granted by concurrently using both guarantor and surety bond.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　采取保证金形式取保候审的，保证金的起点数额为一千元。  
决定机关应当以保证被取保候审人不逃避、不妨碍刑事诉讼活动为原则，综合考虑犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的社会危险性，案件的情节、性质，可能判处刑罚的轻重，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人经济状况，当地的经济发展水平等情况，确定收取保证金的数额。

**Article 5** Where release on bail is granted by means of a surety bond, the minimum amount of the bond shall be 1,000 yuan.  
The decision-making organ shall, on the principle of ensuring that the person released on bail does not abscond or obstruct criminal proceedings, determine the amount of the surety bond by comprehensively considering the social danger posed by the criminal suspect or defendant, the circumstances and nature of the case, the severity of the possible punishment, the financial situation of the criminal suspect or defendant, and the local economic development level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　取保候审保证金由县级以上执行机关统一收取和管理。没收保证金的决定、退还保证金的决定、对保证人的罚款决定等，应当由县级以上执行机关作出。

**Article 6** The surety bond for release on bail pending investigation or trial shall be uniformly collected and managed by the executing organ at or above the county level. Decisions on confiscation of the surety bond, return of the surety bond, and fines on guarantors shall be made by the executing organ at or above the county level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上执行机关应当在其指定的银行设立取保候审保证金专户，委托银行代为收取和保管保证金，并将指定银行的名称通知人民检察院、人民法院。  
保证金应当以人民币交纳。

**Article 7** The executing organ at or above the county level shall establish a special account for the surety bond for release on bail at a designated bank, entrust the bank to collect and keep the bond on its behalf, and notify the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court of the name of the designated bank.  
The surety bond shall be paid in Renminbi.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　决定机关作出取保候审收取保证金的决定后，应当及时将《取保候审决定书》送达被取保候审人和为其提供保证金的单位或者个人，责令其向执行机关指定的银行一次性交纳保证金。  
决定机关核实保证金已经交纳到执行机关指定银行的凭证后，应当将《取保候审决定书》、《取保候审执行通知书》和银行出具的收款凭证及其他有关材料一并送交执行机关执行。

**Article 8** After the decision-making organ makes a decision to release on bail and collect a surety bond, it shall promptly serve the "Decision on Release on Bail" on the person released on bail and the entity or individual providing the bond, and order such person to pay the bond in a lump sum to the bank designated by the executing organ.  
After verifying the receipt evidencing that the bond has been paid into the bank designated by the executing organ, the decision-making organ shall deliver the "Decision on Release on Bail", the "Notice on Execution of Release on Bail", the receipt issued by the bank, and other relevant materials to the executing organ for execution.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　执行机关在执行取保候审时，应当告知被取保候审人必须遵守[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)的规定及其违反规定，或者在取保候审期间重新犯罪应当承担的后果。

**Article 9** When executing release on bail, the executing organ shall inform the person released on bail that he or she must comply with the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law and the consequences of violating such provisions or committing a new crime during the period of release on bail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被取保候审人违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)规定，依法应当没收保证金的，由县级以上执行机关作出没收部分或者全部保证金的决定，并通知决定机关；对需要变更强制措施的，应当同时提出变更强制措施的意见，连同有关材料一并送交决定机关。

**Article 10** Where a person released on bail violates the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law and the surety bond shall be confiscated according to law, the executing organ at or above the county level shall make a decision to confiscate part or all of the bond and notify the decision-making organ; if it is necessary to change the compulsory measures, it shall at the same time submit an opinion on changing the compulsory measures together with relevant materials to the decision-making organ.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　决定机关发现被取保候审人违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)的规定，认为依法应当没收保证金的，应当提出没收部分或者全部保证金的书面意见，连同有关材料一并送交县级以上执行机关。县级以上执行机关应当根据决定机关的意见，及时作出没收保证金的决定，并通知决定机关。

**Article 11** Where the decision-making organ discovers that a person released on bail has violated the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law and considers that the surety bond shall be confiscated according to law, it shall submit a written opinion on confiscating part or all of the bond together with relevant materials to the executing organ at or above the county level. The executing organ at or above the county level shall, based on the opinion of the decision-making organ, promptly make a decision to confiscate the bond and notify the decision-making organ.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被取保候审人没有违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)的规定，但在取保候审期间涉嫌重新犯罪被司法机关立案侦查的，执行机关应当暂扣其交纳的保证金，待人民法院判决生效后，决定是否没收保证金。对故意重新犯罪的，应当没收保证金；对过失重新犯罪或者不构成犯罪的，应当退还保证金。

**Article 12** Where a person released on bail has not violated the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law but is suspected of committing a new crime during the period of release on bail and the judicial authority files a case for investigation, the executing organ shall temporarily withhold the bond paid by such person and, after the People's Court's judgment becomes effective, decide whether to confiscate the bond. If the new crime is intentional, the bond shall be confiscated; if the new crime is negligent or does not constitute a crime, the bond shall be returned.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　决定机关收到执行机关已没收保证金的书面通知，或者变更强制措施的意见后，应当在五日内作出变更强制措施或者责令犯罪嫌疑人重新交纳保证金、提出保证人的决定，并通知执行机关。  
决定重新交纳保证金的程序，适用本规定的有关规定。

**Article 13** After receiving the written notice from the executing organ that the bond has been confiscated or the opinion on changing compulsory measures, the decision-making organ shall, within five days, make a decision to change the compulsory measures or order the criminal suspect to pay a new bond or provide a guarantor, and notify the executing organ.  
The procedure for deciding to pay a new bond shall be governed by the relevant provisions of these Provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　执行机关应当向被取保候审人宣布没收保证金的决定，并告知其如不服本决定，可以在收到《没收保证金决定书》后的五日以内，向执行机关的上一级主管机关申请复核一次。上一级主管机关收到复核申请后，应当在七日内作出复核决定。

**Article 14** The executing organ shall announce to the person released on bail the decision to confiscate the bond and inform him or her that if he or she disagrees with the decision, he or she may, within five days after receiving the "Decision on Confiscation of Surety Bond", apply to the competent organ at the next higher level of the executing organ for one review. The competent organ at the next higher level shall, within seven days after receiving the review application, make a review decision.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　没收保证金的决定已过复核申请期限或者经复核后决定没收保证金的，县级以上执行机关应当及时通知银行按照国家的有关规定上缴国库。

**Article 15** Where the time limit for applying for review of the decision to confiscate the bond has expired or the bond is decided to be confiscated after review, the executing organ at or above the county level shall promptly notify the bank to turn over the bond to the State Treasury in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　采取保证人形式取保候审的，被取保候审人违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)的规定，保证人未及时报告的，经查证属实后，由县级以上执行机关对保证人处一千元以上二万元以下罚款，并将有关情况及时通知决定机关。

**Article 16** Where release on bail is granted by means of a guarantor, and the person released on bail violates the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law but the guarantor fails to promptly report, upon verification, the executing organ at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the guarantor, and promptly notify the decision-making organ of the relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　执行机关应当向保证人宣布罚款决定，并告知其如不服本决定，可以在收到《对保证人罚款决定书》后的五日以内，向执行机关的上一级主管机关申请复核一次。上一级主管机关收到复核申请后，应当在七日内作出复核决定。

**Article 17** The executing organ shall announce to the guarantor the decision to impose a fine and inform him or her that if he or she disagrees with the decision, he or she may, within five days after receiving the "Decision on Fine on Guarantor", apply to the competent organ at the next higher level of the executing organ for one review. The competent organ at the next higher level shall, within seven days after receiving the review application, make a review decision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　没收取保候审保证金和对保证人罚款均系刑事司法行为，不能提起行政诉讼。当事人如不服复核决定，可以依法向有关机关提出申诉。

**Article 18** The confiscation of a surety bond for release on bail and the imposition of a fine on a guarantor are both criminal judicial acts and shall not be subject to administrative litigation. If a party disagrees with the review decision, such party may, according to law, file a petition with the relevant authority.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　采取保证人形式取保候审的，执行机关发现保证人丧失了担保条件时，应当书面通知决定机关。  
决定机关收到执行机关的书面通知后，应当责令被取保候审人重新提出保证人或者交纳保证金，或者作出变更强制措施的决定，并通知执行机关。

**Article 19** Where release on bail is granted by means of a guarantor, and the executing organ discovers that the guarantor has lost the eligibility to act as surety, it shall give written notice to the decision-making organ.  
After receiving the written notice from the executing organ, the decision-making organ shall order the person released on bail to provide a new guarantor or pay a surety bond, or make a decision to change the compulsory measures, and notify the executing organ.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　取保候审即将到期的，执行机关应当在期限届满十五日前书面通知决定机关，由决定机关作出解除取保候审或者变更强制措施的决定，并于期限届满前书面通知执行机关。  
执行机关收到决定机关的《解除取保候审决定书》或者变更强制措施的通知后，应当立即执行，并将执行情况及时通知决定机关。

**Article 20** Where the period of release on bail is about to expire, the executing organ shall, fifteen days before the expiration of the period, give written notice to the decision-making organ, which shall make a decision to terminate the release on bail or change the compulsory measures, and notify the executing organ in writing before the expiration of the period.  
After receiving the "Decision on Termination of Release on Bail" or the notice on changing compulsory measures from the decision-making organ, the executing organ shall immediately execute it and promptly notify the decision-making organ of the execution status.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被取保候审人在取保候审期间没有违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)的规定，也没有故意重新犯罪的，在解除取保候审、变更强制措施或者执行刑罚的同时，县级以上执行机关应当制作《退还保证金决定书》，通知银行如数退还保证金，并书面通知决定机关。  
执行机关应当及时向被取保候审人宣布退还保证金的决定，并书面通知其到银行领取退还的保证金。

**Article 21** Where a person released on bail has not violated the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law during the period of release on bail and has not intentionally committed a new crime, at the same time as the termination of release on bail, change of compulsory measures, or execution of punishment, the executing organ at or above the county level shall issue a "Decision on Return of Surety Bond", notify the bank to return the bond in full, and give written notice to the decision-making organ.  
The executing organ shall promptly announce to the person released on bail the decision to return the bond and notify him or her in writing to collect the returned bond from the bank.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在侦查或者审查起诉阶段已经采取取保候审的，案件移送至审查起诉或者审判阶段时，如果需要继续取保候审，或者需要变更保证方式或强制措施的，受案机关应当在七日内作出决定，并通知执行机关和移送案件的机关。  
受案机关决定继续取保候审的，应当重新作出取保候审决定。对继续采取保证金方式取保候审的，原则上不变更保证金数额，不再重新收取保证金。  
取保候审期限即将届满，受案机关仍未作出继续取保候审、变更保证方式或者变更强制措施决定的，执行机关应当在期限届满十五日前书面通知受案机关。受案机关应当在原取保候审期限届满前作出决定，并通知执行机关和移送案件的机关。

**Article 22** Where release on bail has been granted during the investigation or examination and prosecution stage, and when the case is transferred to the examination and prosecution or adjudication stage, if continuation of release on bail or change of the method of guaranty or compulsory measures is necessary, the accepting authority shall make a decision within seven days and notify the executing organ and the authority transferring the case.  
Where the accepting authority decides to continue release on bail, it shall make a new decision on release on bail. Where release on bail continues by means of a surety bond, the amount of the bond shall in principle remain unchanged, and the bond shall not be collected again.  
Where the period of release on bail is about to expire, and the accepting authority has not yet made a decision to continue release on bail, change the method of guaranty, or change the compulsory measures, the executing organ shall, fifteen days before the expiration of the period, give written notice to the accepting authority. The accepting authority shall make a decision before the expiration of the original period of release on bail and notify the executing organ and the authority transferring the case.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　原决定机关收到受案机关作出的变更强制措施决定后，应当立即解除原取保候审，并将《解除取保候审决定书》、《解除取保候审通知书》送达执行机关，执行机关应当及时书面通知被取保候审人、保证人；受案机关作出继续取保候审或者变更保证方式决定的，原取保候审自动解除，不再办理解除手续。

**Article 23** After receiving the decision on changing compulsory measures made by the accepting authority, the original decision-making organ shall immediately terminate the original release on bail and serve the "Decision on Termination of Release on Bail" and the "Notice on Termination of Release on Bail" on the executing organ, which shall promptly give written notice to the person released on bail and the guarantor; where the accepting authority makes a decision to continue release on bail or change the method of guaranty, the original release on bail shall be automatically terminated without going through the termination procedures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被告人被取保候审的，人民法院决定开庭审理时，应当依照刑事诉讼法的有关规定传唤被告人，同时通知取保候审的执行机关。

**Article 24** Where a defendant is released on bail pending investigation or trial, when the People's Court decides to open a court session for trial, it shall summon the defendant in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and at the same time notify the executing organ for release on bail.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对被取保候审人判处罚金或者没收财产的判决生效后，依法应当解除取保候审，退还保证金的，如果保证金属于其个人财产，人民法院可以书面通知执行机关将保证金移交人民法院执行刑罚，但剩余部分应当退还被取保候审人。

**Article 25** After a judgment imposing a fine or confiscation of property on a person released on bail becomes effective, if the release on bail shall be terminated according to law and the surety bond returned, where the bond belongs to such person's personal property, the People's Court may give written notice to the executing organ to transfer the bond to the People's Court for execution of the punishment, but the remaining part shall be returned to the person released on bail.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　保证金的收取、管理和没收应当严格按照本规定和国家的财经管理制度执行，任何单位和个人不得截留、坐支、私分、挪用或者以其他任何方式侵吞保证金。对违反规定的，应当依照有关规定给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** The collection, management, and confiscation of surety bonds shall be carried out strictly in accordance with these Provisions and the national financial management system. No entity or individual may withhold, use without authorization, privately divide, misappropriate, or otherwise embezzle the bond. Those who violate the provisions shall be given administrative sanctions in accordance with relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　司法机关及其工作人员违反本规定，擅自收取、没收或者退还取保候审保证金的，依照有关法律和规定，追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的责任。

**Article 27** Where judicial authorities and their staff members violate these Provisions by collecting, confiscating, or returning a surety bond for release on bail without authorization, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be held liable in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of issuance.
