### 最高人民法院关于加强和改进委托执行工作的若干规定

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于加强和改进委托执行工作的若干规定  
  
（2000年2月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1102次会议通过，自2000年3月11日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕9号  
  
为了加强和改进高级人民法院对委托和受托执行案件的统一管理，严格依法执行受托案件，切实保障跨辖区案件当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的规定，对委托执行工作的有关问题作如下规定。  
  
1、被执行人或被执行的财产在本省、自治区、直辖市辖区以外的案件，除少数特殊情况之外，应当委托执行。  
  
被执行人或被执行的财产在本省、自治区、直辖市辖区内，需跨中级人民法院、基层人民法院辖区执行的案件，亦应以委托执行为主。  
  
直辖市内法院间的跨辖区的执行案件，以及设区的市内跨辖区的执行案件，是否以委托执行为主，由各高级人民法院根据实际情况自行确定。  
  
2、有下列特殊情况的，可以不委托执行：  
  
（1）被执行人在不同辖区内有财产，且任何一个地方的财产不足以单独清偿债务的；  
  
（2）分布在不同法院辖区的多个被执行人对清偿债务责任的承担有一定关联的；  
  
（3）需要裁定变更或追加本辖区以外的被执行人的；  
  
（4）案件审理中已对当事人在外地的财产进行保全，异地执行更为方便的；  
  
（5）因其他特殊情况不便委托执行，经高级人民法院批准的。  
  
3、执行法院到异地办理执行案件时，应当主动请求当地法院协助执行。当地法院应当按照外地法院的请求予以积极办理。  
  
4、受托法院应当于收到委托执行手续后十五日内开始执行，一般应当在三十日内执行完毕，最迟应当在六个月内执行完毕。委托手续不全的，执行期限自受托法院收到齐全手续之日起算。  
  
5、申请执行费和执行中实际支出的费用，在案件委托后，由受托法院直接向当事人收取。委托法院已经预收的，应当在办理委托手续后三日内，将预收的费用退给申请执行人，由其直接向受托法院缴纳。  
  
6、受托法院接受委托执行案件后，应当依法自行决定采取执行措施和强制措施，也可以与委托法院共同商定执行方式方法。  
  
7、委托执行后，委托法院应当及时告知申请执行人。申请执行人可以直接向受托法院或者请求委托法院向受托法院催促执行，也可以向受托法院的上级法院请求督促执行。  
  
委托法院或者申请执行人要求了解案件执行情况的，受托法院应当及时告知。  
  
8、委托执行后，委托法院发现被执行人在本辖区内有可供执行的财产或者其他有关情况时，应当及时向受托法院通报。受托法院可以与委托法院协商，视情决定共同执行或采取其他措施执行。  
  
9、委托执行后，遇有下列情况，由受托法院依照法律和司法解释的规定处理：  
  
（1）需要变更或追加被执行主体的；  
  
（2）因执行担保需要暂缓执行的；  
  
（3）当事人之间达成执行和解协议的；  
  
（4）案外人对非属据以执行的生效法律文书指定交付的执行标的物提出异议的。  
  
10、案外人对据以执行的生效法律文书指定交付的财物和票证提出异议的，受托法院应当及时将案外人的书面异议转交委托法院处理。委托法院应当及时作出中止执行或驳回异议的裁定，并通知受托法院。  
  
11、受托法院在办理受托执行案件过程中，发现据以执行的生效法律文书有明显错误，如予执行将造成严重后果的，应当首先对被执行人的财产采取查封、扣押、冻结等措施，然后将书面意见及时转交作出生效法律文书的法院处理。  
  
作出生效法律文书的法院收到受托法院的书面意见后，应当于二个月内作出书面答复。受托法院应当按照其答复意见执行。超过二个月未作出答复的，受托法院可以将案件退回委托法院，并抄告其上级法院。  
  
12、高级人民法院统一管理和协调本辖区内各级人民法院的委托执行工作。  
  
中级人民法院辖区内基层人民法院间的委托执行案件，可以由中级人民法院进行统一管理和协调。  
  
13、高级人民法院和中级人民院法院对所属下级法院办理的受托执行案件，应当采取有效措施监督执行，必要时可以采取指定执行、共同执行和提级执行以及统一集中清理的办法执行。  
  
14、各高级人民法院可以参照本规定，制定本辖区委托执行工作的实施细则。  
  
15、本规定自公布之日起执行。

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2000] No. 9  
In order to strengthen and improve the unified management of commissioned and entrusted enforcement cases by the Higher People's Courts, strictly enforce entrusted cases according to law, and effectively protect the legitimate rights and interests of parties in cross-jurisdictional cases, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the following provisions are hereby made on relevant issues concerning commissioned enforcement work.  
1. For cases where the person subject to enforcement or the property subject to enforcement is outside the jurisdiction of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, enforcement shall be commissioned, except for a few special circumstances.  
Where the person subject to enforcement or the property subject to enforcement is within the jurisdiction of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government but needs to be enforced across the jurisdictions of the Intermediate People's Court or Primary People's Court, commissioning enforcement shall also be the primary method.  
For cross-jurisdictional enforcement cases between courts within a municipality directly under the Central Government or within a city with districts, whether commissioning enforcement shall be the primary method shall be determined by the Higher People's Court of that jurisdiction based on actual circumstances.  
2. Under the following special circumstances, enforcement may not be commissioned:  
(1) The person subject to enforcement has property in different jurisdictions, and the property in any one place is insufficient to satisfy the debt alone;  
(2) Multiple persons subject to enforcement located in different court jurisdictions have a certain correlation in their assumption of the debt repayment obligation;  
(3) It is necessary to rule to change or add a person subject to enforcement outside the jurisdiction;  
(4) During the trial of the case, property of a party located in another place has already been preserved, and enforcement in a different location is more convenient;  
(5) Other special circumstances make it inconvenient to commission enforcement, and the Higher People's Court has approved.  
3. When a court handling enforcement conducts enforcement in another place, it shall actively request the local court to assist in enforcement. The local court shall actively handle the matter in accordance with the request of the court from another place.  
4. The entrusted court shall commence enforcement within fifteen days after receiving the commissioned enforcement documents, shall generally complete enforcement within thirty days, and shall, at the latest, complete enforcement within six months. If the commissioned documents are incomplete, the enforcement time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the entrusted court receives the complete documents.  
5. The application enforcement fee and the expenses actually incurred during enforcement shall, after the case is commissioned, be directly collected from the parties by the entrusted court. If the commissioning court has already collected the fees in advance, it shall refund the prepaid fees to the applicant for enforcement within three days after completing the commissioning procedures, and the applicant shall directly pay them to the entrusted court.  
6. After accepting a commissioned enforcement case, the entrusted court shall, according to law, decide on its own to take enforcement measures and compulsory measures, and may also negotiate with the commissioning court on the methods and means of enforcement.  
7. After the enforcement is commissioned, the commissioning court shall promptly inform the applicant for enforcement. The applicant for enforcement may directly urge the entrusted court or request the commissioning court to urge the entrusted court to enforce, and may also request the higher court of the entrusted court to supervise enforcement.  
If the commissioning court or the applicant for enforcement requests to be informed of the enforcement status of the case, the entrusted court shall promptly provide such information.  
8. After the enforcement is commissioned, if the commissioning court discovers that the person subject to enforcement has property available for enforcement or other relevant circumstances within its jurisdiction, it shall promptly notify the entrusted court. The entrusted court may consult with the commissioning court and, depending on the circumstances, decide to jointly enforce or take other measures to enforce.  
9. After the enforcement is commissioned, in the following circumstances, the entrusted court shall handle them in accordance with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations:  
(1) It is necessary to change or add the person subject to enforcement;  
(2) It is necessary to suspend enforcement due to enforcement security;  
(3) The parties have reached an enforcement settlement agreement;  
(4) A person not involved in the case raises an objection to the subject matter of enforcement that is not the object specified for delivery in the effective legal document on which the enforcement is based.  
10. If a person not involved in the case raises an objection to the property or instruments specified for delivery in the effective legal document on which the enforcement is based, the entrusted court shall promptly transfer the written objection of the person not involved in the case to the commissioning court for handling. The commissioning court shall promptly make a ruling to suspend enforcement or dismiss the objection, and notify the entrusted court.  
11. During the process of handling a commissioned enforcement case, if the entrusted court discovers that the effective legal document on which the enforcement is based has obvious errors and enforcement would cause serious consequences, it shall first take measures such as sealing, seizure, or freezing of the property of the person subject to enforcement, and then promptly transfer its written opinion to the court that rendered the effective legal document for handling.  
After receiving the written opinion of the entrusted court, the court that rendered the effective legal document shall make a written reply within two months. The entrusted court shall enforce in accordance with the reply opinion. If no reply is made within two months, the entrusted court may return the case to the commissioning court and send a copy to its higher court.  
12. The Higher People's Court shall uniformly manage and coordinate the commissioned enforcement work of courts at all levels within its jurisdiction.  
For commissioned enforcement cases between Primary People's Courts within the jurisdiction of an Intermediate People's Court, the Intermediate People's Court may carry out unified management and coordination.  
13. The Higher People's Court and the Intermediate People's Court shall take effective measures to supervise the enforcement of commissioned enforcement cases handled by their subordinate courts, and when necessary, may adopt the methods of designated enforcement, joint enforcement, escalated enforcement, and unified centralized clearance.  
14. Each Higher People's Court may, by reference to these Provisions, formulate implementing rules for commissioned enforcement work within its jurisdiction.  
15. These Provisions shall be implemented from the date of promulgation.
