最高人民法院  
  
关于对人民法院终结执行行为提出执行异议期限问题的批复  
  
法释〔2016〕3号  
  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于咸宁市广泰置业有限公司与咸宁市枫丹置业有限公司房地产开发经营合同纠纷案的请示》（鄂高法〔2015〕295号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
当事人、利害关系人依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定对终结执行行为提出异议的，应当自收到终结执行法律文书之日起六十日内提出；未收到法律文书的，应当自知道或者应当知道人民法院终结执行之日起六十日内提出。批复发布前终结执行的，自批复发布之日起六十日内提出。超出该期限提出执行异议的，人民法院不予受理。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** February 14, 2016  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply concerning the Issue of the Time Limit for Filing an Objection to the Conclusion of Enforcement Conduct by a People's Court  
Interpretation No. 3 [2016] of the Supreme People's Court  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
Your Request for Instructions regarding the Case of Dispute over a Real Estate Development and Operation Contract between Xianning Guangtai Real Estate Co., Ltd. and Xianning Fengdan Real Estate Co., Ltd. (E Gao Fa [2015] No. 295) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a party or an interested person raises an objection to the conclusion of enforcement conduct in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law, the objection shall be filed within sixty days from the date of receipt of the legal instrument on the conclusion of enforcement; if no legal instrument is received, the objection shall be filed within sixty days from the date on which the party or interested person knew or should have known that the People's Court had concluded the enforcement. Where the enforcement was concluded before the issuance of this Reply, the objection shall be filed within sixty days from the date of issuance of this Reply. If an objection to enforcement is filed beyond this time limit, the People's Court shall not accept it.  
This reply is hereby given.
