# 最高人民法院关于综合治理类司法建议工作若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕11号  
**公布日期**：2023-10-19  
**生效日期**：2023-11-16

> 《最高人民法院关于综合治理类司法建议工作若干问题的规定》已于2023年10月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1902次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年11月16日起施行。 2023年11月15日 最高人民法院 关于综合治理类司法建议工作若干问题的规定 法释〔2023〕11号 为进一步加强和规范综合治理类司法建议工作，更好发挥审判机关在国家和社会治理中的重要作用，根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》等法律规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院在履行审判执行职责时发现社会治理领域中存在引起矛盾纠纷多发高发，影响经济社会发展和人民群众权益保护的突出问题，需要向有关主管机关或者其他有关单位提出改进工作、完善治理的司法建议的，适用本规定。 第二条　人民法院提出司法建议，应当遵循确有必要的原则，确保司法建议的针对性、规范性和实效性。 第三条　人民法院提出司法建议时，应当根据综合治理问题涉及的行业、领域等向相应的主管机关或者其他有关单位提出；向主管机关提出的，一般应当向本院辖区范围内的同级主管机关提出。发现的综合治理问题需要异地主管机关采取措施的，可以提出工作建议，层报相应的上级人民法院决定。 第四条　司法建议应当以人民法院名义提出。 第五条　人民法院提出司法建议前，应当结合审判执行工作中发现的问题充分调查研究，并积极与被建议单位沟通，听取其意见。 第六条　人民法院提出司法建议，应当制作司法建议书。 司法建议书实行统一编号。 第七条　司法建议书起草完成后，应当依照《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》第三十七条第一款第四项的规定提请审判委员会审议。 司法建议书审议通过后，由院长签发。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院在履行审判执行职责时发现社会治理领域中存在引起矛盾纠纷多发高发，影响经济社会发展和人民群众权益保护的突出问题，需要向有关主管机关或者其他有关单位提出改进工作、完善治理的司法建议的，适用本规定。

**Article 1** 第一条 These Provisions shall apply where a People's Court, in the exercise of its adjudication and enforcement functions, discovers prominent issues in the field of social governance that give rise to frequent and concentrated disputes and conflicts, affect economic and social development, and impair the protection of the rights and interests of the people, and the Court deems it necessary to propose judicial suggestions to the competent authorities or other relevant entities for improvement of work and governance.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院提出司法建议，应当遵循确有必要的原则，确保司法建议的针对性、规范性和实效性。

**Article 2** 第二条 A People's Court, when proposing judicial suggestions, shall adhere to the principle of genuine necessity, ensuring the pertinence, standardization, and effectiveness of the judicial suggestions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院提出司法建议时，应当根据综合治理问题涉及的行业、领域等向相应的主管机关或者其他有关单位提出；向主管机关提出的，一般应当向本院辖区范围内的同级主管机关提出。发现的综合治理问题需要异地主管机关采取措施的，可以提出工作建议，层报相应的上级人民法院决定。

**Article 3** 第三条 When proposing judicial suggestions, a People's Court shall address them to the corresponding competent authorities or other relevant entities based on the industries, fields, etc. involved in the comprehensive governance issues; where addressed to a competent authority, they shall generally be addressed to the competent authority at the same level within the Court's jurisdiction. Where the discovered comprehensive governance issues require measures to be taken by a competent authority in another locality, a work suggestion may be submitted to the corresponding higher People's Court for decision.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　司法建议应当以人民法院名义提出。

**Article 4** 第四条 Judicial suggestions shall be proposed in the name of a People's Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院提出司法建议前，应当结合审判执行工作中发现的问题充分调查研究，并积极与被建议单位沟通，听取其意见。

**Article 5** 第五条 Before proposing a judicial suggestion, a People's Court shall conduct full investigation and research in conjunction with the issues discovered in adjudication and enforcement work, and actively communicate with the proposed recipient unit to seek its opinions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院提出司法建议，应当制作司法建议书。  
司法建议书实行统一编号。

**Article 6** 第六条 A People's Court, when proposing a judicial suggestion, shall produce a Judicial Suggestion Document.  
Judicial Suggestion Documents shall be uniformly numbered.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　司法建议书起草完成后，应当依照[《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》第三十七条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民法院组织法.md#art-37)第一款第四项的规定提请审判委员会审议。  
司法建议书审议通过后，由院长签发。

**Article 7** 第七条 After the draft of the Judicial Suggestion Document is completed, it shall be submitted to the Adjudication Committee for deliberation in accordance with Article 37, Paragraph 1, Item 4 of the Organic Law of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China.  
After the Judicial Suggestion Document is approved through deliberation, it shall be signed and issued by the President of the Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院提出司法建议时，应当告知被建议单位就建议采纳落实情况等予以书面答复。答复期限根据具体情况确定，一般不超过两个月；法律、司法解释另有规定的，依照其规定。  
人民法院应当结合审判执行工作支持、配合、督促被建议单位采取相应措施，协同抓好司法建议相关工作的落实。

**Article 8** 第八条 When proposing a judicial suggestion, a People's Court shall inform the proposed recipient unit to provide a written reply regarding the adoption and implementation of the suggestion. The reply period shall be determined according to the specific circumstances, generally not exceeding two months; where laws or judicial interpretations provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.  
A People's Court shall, in conjunction with its adjudication and enforcement work, support, cooperate with, and supervise the proposed recipient unit in taking corresponding measures, and collaborate to ensure the implementation of the relevant work of the judicial suggestion.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　司法建议涉及的问题重大，需要引起高度重视的，人民法院可以将司法建议书抄送被建议单位的上级主管机关或者其他有关单位。

**Article 9** 第九条 Where the issues involved in a judicial suggestion are significant and require high attention, a People's Court may serve a copy of the Judicial Suggestion Document to the higher competent authority of the proposed recipient unit or other relevant entities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民法院应当确定司法建议工作日常管理机构，完善审核、指导、考核、激励等机制，并将司法建议工作质效纳入绩效考核。

**Article 10** 第十条 People's Courts at all levels shall designate a daily management institution for judicial suggestion work, improve mechanisms for review, guidance, assessment, incentives, etc., and incorporate the quality and efficiency of judicial suggestion work into performance appraisal.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级人民法院应当依照[《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》第九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民法院组织法.md#art-9)的规定，将司法建议工作情况列入向同级人民代表大会及其常务委员会报告的事项。

**Article 11** 第十一条 People's Courts at all levels shall, in accordance with Article 9 of the Organic Law of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China, include the work of judicial suggestions in the matters reported to the People's Congress and its Standing Committee at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院提出综合治理类司法建议以外的其他司法建议的，依照有关法律、司法解释、其他规范性文件的规定办理。有关法律、司法解释、其他规范性文件没有规定的事项，可以根据本辖区的具体情况和实际需要，参照本规定办理。

**Article 12** 第十二条 Where a People's Court proposes judicial suggestions other than those of the comprehensive governance type, they shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, judicial interpretations, and other normative documents. Matters not provided for in relevant laws, judicial interpretations, or other normative documents may be handled by reference to these Provisions based on the specific circumstances and actual needs of the jurisdiction.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定自2023年11月16日起施行。最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释和其他规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

附件1  
人民法院司法建议书制作规范  
为指导全国法院司法建议书的制作，确保司法建议文书格式统一、要素齐全、结构完整、繁简得当、逻辑严密、用语准确，提高文书质量，制定本规范。  
一、基本要素  
司法建议书由首部、主文、尾部三部分组成。  
二、首部  
首部包括标题、司法建议书编号、被建议单位名称。  
标题包括法院名称、事由和司法建议书。例如：“××人民法院关于××的司法建议书”。  
司法建议书实行统一编号。最高人民法院的司法建议书以“法建”+〔文书年度〕+文书编号+“号”形式编号，地方各级人民法院的司法建议书以地名简称+“法建”+〔文书年度〕+文书编号+“号”形式编号。  
被建议单位名称应使用该单位全称、规范化简称或者同类型单位统称。  
三、主文  
简要说明抓好相关领域工作的重要性和被建议单位在该领域已采取的主要举措及成效。然后列明在审判执行工作中发现的该领域需要重视和解决的问题，依据法律法规及政策提出的具体建议，反馈时限，以及其他需要说明的事项。建议与问题应当一一对应。  
四、尾部  
包括院印、法院名称和日期、联系人姓名和电话、附件和抄送单位名称。  
司法建议书如有附件，应当在正文之后、成文日期之前注明附件的顺序号和名称。  
抄送单位应当使用该机关全称、规范化简称或者同类型机关统称。  
五、封面  
1.法徽图案高55mm，宽50mm。上页边距为60mm，法徽下沿与标题文字上沿之间距离为40mm。  
2.标题文字为“××人民法院司法建议书”，位于法徽图案下方，字体为小标宋体字；标题分两行或三行排列，法院名称字体大小为30磅，司法建议书字体大小为36磅。  
3.封面应庄重、美观，页边距、字体大小及行距可适当进行调整。

Annex 1  
Specification for the Preparation of Judicial Suggestion Documents of People's Courts  
To guide the preparation of Judicial Suggestion Documents by courts across the country, ensure uniform format, complete elements, complete structure, appropriate length, logical coherence, accurate terminology, and improve document quality, this specification is formulated.  
I. Basic Elements  
A Judicial Suggestion Document consists of three parts: heading, main text, and closing.  
II. Heading  
The heading includes the title, the Judicial Suggestion Document number, and the name of the proposed recipient unit.  
The title includes the court name, subject matter, and the words "Judicial Suggestion Document". For example: "×× People's Court Judicial Suggestion Document on ××".  
Judicial Suggestion Documents shall be uniformly numbered. The Judicial Suggestion Document of the Supreme People's Court shall be numbered in the form of "Fajian" + [year of document] + document number + "No."; the Judicial Suggestion Documents of local People's Courts at various levels shall be numbered in the form of abbreviation of place name + "Fajian" + [year of document] + document number + "No.".  
The name of the proposed recipient unit shall use its full name, standardized abbreviation, or the collective name for the same type of unit.  
III. Main Text  
Briefly state the importance of working in the relevant field and the main measures and achievements already taken by the proposed recipient unit in that field. Then list the issues found in adjudication and enforcement work in that field that need attention, specific suggestions based on laws, regulations, and policies, the feedback deadline, and other matters requiring explanation. Suggestions and issues shall correspond one by one.  
IV. Closing  
Includes the court seal, court name and date, contact person's name and telephone number, annexes, and names of copied units.  
If there are annexes to the Judicial Suggestion Document, the serial numbers and names of the annexes shall be noted after the main text and before the date of issuance.  
The copied units shall use their full names, standardized abbreviations, or collective names for the same type of organization.  
V. Cover  
1. The court emblem pattern is 55mm high and 50mm wide. The top margin is 60mm, and the distance from the lower edge of the court emblem to the upper edge of the title text is 40mm.  
2. The title text is "×× People's Court Judicial Suggestion Document", located below the court emblem pattern, in small-standard Song typeface; the title is arranged in two or three lines, with the court name font size 30pt and the Judicial Suggestion Document font size 36pt.  
3. The cover shall be solemn and aesthetically pleasing; margins, font sizes, and line spacing may be appropriately adjusted.
