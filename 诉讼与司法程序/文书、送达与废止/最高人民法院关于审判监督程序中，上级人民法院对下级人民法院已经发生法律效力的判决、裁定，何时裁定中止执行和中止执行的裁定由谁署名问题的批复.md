# 最高人民法院关于审判监督程序中，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，何时裁定中止执行和中止执行的裁定由谁署名问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1985〕41号

---

### 正文

一、上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，须发现确有错误，并作出了提审或者指令再审决定的，才可裁定中止执行。所以，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，在调卷审查的过程中，如尚未发现确有错误，民未作出提审或者指令再审决定的，不得裁定中止执行。

**Article None** 1. The higher People's Court may only rule to suspend enforcement of a legally effective judgment or ruling of a lower People's Court if it finds that there is indeed an error and has made a decision to bring the case up for trial or to order a retrial. Therefore, where a higher People's Court, during the process of reviewing the case files, has not yet found that there is indeed an error in a legally effective judgment or ruling of a lower People's Court and has not made a decision to bring the case up for trial or to order a retrial, it shall not rule to suspend enforcement.

二、上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，发现确有错误，可作出提审或者指令下级人民法院再审的裁定。此裁定应包括中止执行的内容，由院长署名，并加盖人民法院印章。

**Article None** 2. Where a higher People's Court finds that there is indeed an error in a legally effective judgment or ruling of a lower People's Court, it may make a ruling to bring the case up for trial or to order the lower People's Court to conduct a retrial. Such ruling shall include the content of suspending enforcement, shall be signed by the president of the court, and shall be affixed with the seal of the People's Court.
