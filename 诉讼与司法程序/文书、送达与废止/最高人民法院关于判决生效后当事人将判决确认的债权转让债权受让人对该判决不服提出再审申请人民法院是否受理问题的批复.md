### 最高人民法院关于判决生效后当事人将判决确认的债权转让债权受让人对该判决不服提出再审申请人民法院是否受理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>中华人民共和国最高人民法院  
  
公    告  
  
最高人民法院《关于判决生效后当事人将判决确认的债权转让债权受让人对该判决不服提出再审申请人民法院是否受理问题的批复》已于2010年12月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1506次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年2月1日起施行。  
  
2011年1月7日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于判决生效后当事人将判决确认的  
  
债权转让债权受让人对该判决不服提出再审申请  
  
人民法院是否受理问题的批复  
  
法释〔2011〕2号  
  
海南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于海南长江旅业有限公司、海南凯立中部开发建设股份有限公司与交通银行海南分行借款合同纠纷一案的请示报告》〔（2009）琼民再终字第16号〕收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
判决生效后当事人将判决确认的债权转让，债权受让人对该判决不服提出再审申请的，因其不具有申请再审人主体资格，人民法院应依法不予受理。

**Article 1** January 7, 2011  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept a Retrial Application Filed by an Assignee of Creditor's Rights Who Is Dissatisfied with the Judgment After the Parties Thereto Have Assigned the Creditor's Rights Confirmed by  
the Judgment — Assignment of Creditor's Rights and the Assignee Thereof Files a Retrial Application for Dissatisfaction  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the People's Court Shall Accept  
Fa Shi [2011] No. 2  
Higher People's Court of Hainan Province:  
Your Court's Request for Instructions on the Case of the Loan Contract Dispute between Hainan Changjiang Tourism Co., Ltd., Hainan Kaili Central Development and Construction Co., Ltd., and the Hainan Branch of Bank of Communications [(2009) Qiong Min Zai Zhong Zi No. 16] has been received. After study, the reply is as follows:  
After a judgment takes effect, a party assigns the creditor's rights confirmed by the judgment. If the assignee of the creditor's rights, being dissatisfied with the judgment, files a retrial application, because the assignee does not have the subject qualification as a retrial applicant, the People's Court shall not accept it according to law.
