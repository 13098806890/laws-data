# 最高人民法院关于成渝金融法院案件管辖的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕20号  
**公布日期**：2022-09-19  
**生效日期**：2023-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于成渝金融法院案件管辖的规定》已于2022年9月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1875次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年1月1日起施行。 2022年12月20日 最高人民法院 关于成渝金融法院案件管辖的规定 法释〔2022〕20号 为服务和保障成渝地区双城经济圈及西部金融中心建设，进一步明确成渝金融法院案件管辖的具体范围，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于设立成渝金融法院的决定》等规定，制定本规定。 第一条　成渝金融法院管辖重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内的应由中级人民法院受理的下列第一审金融民商事案件： （一）证券、期货交易、营业信托、保险、票据、信用证、独立保函、保理、金融借款合同、银行卡、融资租赁合同、委托理财合同、储蓄存款合同、典当、银行结算合同等金融民商事纠纷； （二）资产管理业务、资产支持证券业务、私募基金业务、外汇业务、金融产品销售和适当性管理、征信业务、支付业务及经有权机关批准的其他金融业务引发的金融民商事纠纷； （三）涉金融机构的与公司有关的纠纷； （四）以金融机构为债务人的破产纠纷； （五）金融民商事纠纷的仲裁司法审查案件； （六）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件，以及申请承认和执行外国法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件。 第二条　下列金融纠纷案件，由成渝金融法院管辖： （一）境内投资者以发生在中华人民共和国境外的证券发行、交易活动或者期货和衍生品交易活动损害其合法权益为由向成渝金融法院提起的诉讼；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　成渝金融法院管辖重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内的应由中级人民法院受理的下列第一审金融民商事案件：  
（一）证券、期货交易、营业信托、保险、票据、信用证、独立保函、保理、金融借款合同、银行卡、融资租赁合同、委托理财合同、储蓄存款合同、典当、银行结算合同等金融民商事纠纷；  
（二）资产管理业务、资产支持证券业务、私募基金业务、外汇业务、金融产品销售和适当性管理、征信业务、支付业务及经有权机关批准的其他金融业务引发的金融民商事纠纷；  
（三）涉金融机构的与公司有关的纠纷；  
（四）以金融机构为债务人的破产纠纷；  
（五）金融民商事纠纷的仲裁司法审查案件；  
（六）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件，以及申请承认和执行外国法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件。

**Article 1** The Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court shall have jurisdiction over the following first-instance financial civil and commercial cases that shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province:  
(1) Financial civil and commercial disputes concerning securities, futures trading, business trusts, insurance, negotiable instruments, letters of credit, independent guarantees, factoring, financial loan contracts, bank cards, financial leasing contracts, entrusted wealth management contracts, savings deposit contracts, pawn, bank settlement contracts, and other similar matters;  
(2) Financial civil and commercial disputes arising from asset management business, asset-backed securities business, private fund business, foreign exchange business, financial product sales and suitability management, credit reference business, payment business, and other financial businesses approved by the competent authorities;  
(3) Company-related disputes involving financial institutions;  
(4) Bankruptcy disputes where the debtor is a financial institution;  
(5) Cases for judicial review of arbitration in financial civil and commercial disputes;  
(6) Cases applying for recognition and enforcement of judgments and rulings of courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region on financial civil and commercial disputes, as well as cases applying for recognition and enforcement of judgments and rulings of foreign courts on financial civil and commercial disputes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列金融纠纷案件，由成渝金融法院管辖：  
（一）境内投资者以发生在中华人民共和国境外的证券发行、交易活动或者期货和衍生品交易活动损害其合法权益为由向成渝金融法院提起的诉讼；  
（二）境内个人或者机构以中华人民共和国境外金融机构销售的金融产品或者提供的金融服务损害其合法权益为由向成渝金融法院提起的诉讼。

**Article 2** The following financial dispute cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court:  
(1) Lawsuits filed with the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court by domestic investors on the ground that securities issuance, trading activities, or futures and derivatives trading activities occurring outside the People's Republic of China have infringed upon their lawful rights and interests;  
(2) Lawsuits filed with the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court by domestic individuals or institutions on the ground that financial products sold or financial services provided by financial institutions outside the People's Republic of China have infringed upon their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　以住所地在重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内依法设立的金融基础设施机构为被告或者第三人，与其履行职责相关的第一审金融民商事案件和涉金融行政案件，由成渝金融法院管辖。

**Article 3** First-instance financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that are related to the performance of duties of a financial infrastructure institution legally established within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province, where such institution is the defendant or a third party, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内应由中级人民法院受理的对金融监管机构以及法律、法规、规章授权的组织，因履行金融监管职责作出的行政行为不服提起诉讼的第一审涉金融行政案件，由成渝金融法院管辖。

**Article 4** First-instance financial-related administrative cases that shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province, where a lawsuit is filed against an administrative act taken by a financial regulatory authority or an organization authorized by laws, regulations, or rules in the performance of its financial regulatory duties, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内基层人民法院涉及[本规定第一条](#art-1)第一至三项的第一审金融民商事案件和第一审涉金融行政案件的上诉案件，由成渝金融法院审理。

**Article 5** The appeal cases of first-instance financial civil and commercial cases and first-instance financial-related administrative cases as specified in subparagraphs (1) to (3) of Article 1 of these Provisions, which are tried by the Primary People's Courts within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province, shall be heard by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内应由中级人民法院受理的金融民商事案件、涉金融行政案件的申请再审和再审案件，由成渝金融法院审理。  
本规定施行前已生效金融民商事案件、涉金融行政案件的申请再审和再审案件，仍由原再审管辖法院审理。

**Article 6** The cases of application for retrial and retrial of financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province shall be heard by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.  
The cases of application for retrial and retrial of financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that have already taken effect before the coming into force of these Provisions shall still be heard by the court originally having jurisdiction over the retrial.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　成渝金融法院作出的第一审民商事案件和涉金融行政案件生效裁判，重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内应由中级人民法院执行的涉金融民商事纠纷的仲裁裁决，由成渝金融法院执行。  
成渝金融法院执行过程中发生的执行异议案件、执行异议之诉案件，重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内基层人民法院涉金融案件执行过程中发生的执行复议案件、执行异议之诉上诉案件，由成渝金融法院审理。

**Article 7** The legally effective judgments and rulings of first-instance civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases rendered by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court, and the arbitral awards on financial civil and commercial disputes that shall be enforced by the Intermediate People's Court within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province, shall be enforced by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.  
The cases of execution objection and lawsuits of execution objection arising during the execution process of the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court, and the cases of execution reconsideration and appeals of execution objection lawsuits arising during the execution process of financial cases by the Primary People's Courts within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province, shall be heard by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人对成渝金融法院作出的第一审判决、裁定提起的上诉案件，由重庆市高级人民法院审理。  
当事人对成渝金融法院执行过程中作出的执行异议裁定申请复议的案件，由重庆市高级人民法院审查。

**Article 8** The appeal cases filed by a party against a first-instance judgment or ruling of the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court shall be heard by the Higher People's Court of Chongqing Municipality.  
The cases where a party applies for reconsideration of an execution objection ruling made by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court during the execution process shall be reviewed by the Higher People's Court of Chongqing Municipality.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　成渝金融法院作出发生法律效力的判决、裁定和调解书的申请再审、再审案件，依法应由上一级人民法院管辖的，由重庆市高级人民法院审理。

**Article 9** The cases of application for retrial and retrial concerning the legally effective judgments, rulings, and mediation statements made by the Chengdu-Chongqing Financial Court, which shall be under the jurisdiction of the next higher people's court according to law, shall be heard by the Higher People's Court of Chongqing Municipality.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　重庆市以及四川省属于成渝地区双城经济圈范围内各中级人民法院在本规定施行前已经受理但尚未审结的金融民商事案件和涉金融行政案件，由该中级人民法院继续审理。

**Article 10** The financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that have been accepted but not yet concluded by the Intermediate People's Courts within the scope of the Chengdu-Chongqing Dual-City Economic Circle in Chongqing Municipality and Sichuan Province before the coming into force of these Provisions shall continue to be heard by such Intermediate People's Courts.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定自2023年1月1日起施行。

**Article 11** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2023.
