### 最高人民法院关于依据何种标准计算电话费滞纳金问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于依据何种标准计算电话费滞纳金问题的批复  
  
（1998年11月19日最高人民法院审判委员会第1030次会议通过，自1999年1月6日起施行。）  
  
浙江省高级人民法院：  
  
你院浙高法〔1998〕34号《关于依据何种标准计算电话费滞纳金的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
原邮电部1998年3月12日印发的邮部〔1998〕125号《关于调整电信资费滞纳金标准的通知》规定，自1998年4月1日起，“用户超过规定期限未付电信费用的，电信企业从逾期之日起至实际还款时止，每天按用户所欠费用款额的照邮电部〔1998〕125号通知规定的比例确定；滞纳金跨越3‰收取滞纳金”。因此，凡是在1998年4月1日前发生的电话费滞纳金的行为，按照我院法经〔1998〕14号函确定的滞此复。纳金标准执行；在1998年4月1日后的电话费滞纳金可参照邮电部〔1998〕125号通知规定的比例确定；滞纳金跨越1998年4月1日的，按新旧标准分段计算。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Zhejiang Province:  
  
Your Request for Instructions (Zhe Gao Fa [1998] No. 34) regarding the standards for calculating late fees on telephone charges has been received. After study, the reply is as follows:  
  
The Notice on Adjusting Standards for Late Fees on Telecommunications Charges (You Bu [1998] No. 125) issued by the former Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications on March 12, 1998 stipulates that, from April 1, 1998, "where a user fails to pay telecommunications charges within the prescribed time limit, the telecommunications enterprise shall, from the date of default until the actual payment date, charge late fees on a daily basis at a proportion determined in accordance with the provisions of the Notice of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications (You Bu [1998] No. 125); the late fee collection rate shall be 3‰ for overdue amounts." Therefore, for acts giving rise to late fees on telephone charges that occurred before April 1, 1998, enforcement shall be carried out in accordance with the late fee standards determined in the letter Fa Jing [1998] No. 14 of this Court; for late fees on telephone charges after April 1, 1998, reference may be made to the proportion specified in the Notice of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications (You Bu [1998] No. 125); for late fees spanning April 1, 1998, the calculation shall be made in segments based on the old and new standards.  
  
This is our reply.
