# 人民检察院公益诉讼办案规则

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2021-06-29  
**生效日期**：2021-07-01

> 第五十二次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年7月1日起施行） 目    录 第一章  总  则 一般规定 第一节  管  辖 第二节  回  避 第三节  立  案 调  查 提起诉讼 第六节  出席第一审法庭 第七节  上  诉 第八节  诉讼监督 第三章  行政公益诉讼 第一节  立案与调查 第二节  检察建议

### 第一章 总 则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  为了规范人民检察院履行公益诉讼检察职责，加强对国家利益和社会公共利益的保护，根据《中华人民共和国人民检察院组织法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律规定，结合人民检察院工作实际，制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the Organizational Law of the People's Procuratorate of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other legal provisions, and in light of the actual work of the People's Procuratorate, for the purposes of regulating the performance of public interest litigation procuratorial functions by the People's Procuratorate and strengthening the protection of national interests and social public interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件的任务，是通过依法独立行使检察权，督促行政机关依法履行监督管理职责，支持适格主体依法行使公益诉权，维护国家利益和社会公共利益，维护社会公平正义，维护宪法和法律权威，促进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化。

**Article 2** The task of the People's Procuratorate in handling public interest litigation cases is to, through independently exercising the procuratorial power according to law, supervise administrative organs in performing their regulatory duties according to law, support eligible subjects in exercising the right of public interest actions according to law, safeguard national interests and social public interests, uphold social fairness and justice, uphold the authority of the Constitution and laws, and promote the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，应当遵守宪法、法律和相关法规，秉持客观公正立场，遵循相关诉讼制度的基本原则和程序规定，坚持司法公开。

**Article 3** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall abide by the Constitution, laws, and relevant regulations, maintain an objective and impartial stance, follow the basic principles and procedural rules of relevant litigation systems, and adhere to judicial openness.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民检察院通过提出检察建议、提起诉讼和支持起诉等方式履行公益诉讼检察职责。

**Article 4** The People's Procuratorate performs its public interest litigation procuratorial functions by means of issuing procuratorial suggestions, initiating litigation, and supporting actions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，由检察官、检察长、检察委员会在各自职权范围内对办案事项作出决定，并依照规定承担相应司法责任。  
检察官在检察长领导下开展工作。重大办案事项，由检察长决定。检察长可以根据案件情况，提交检察委员会讨论决定。其他办案事项，检察长可以自行决定，也可以授权检察官决定。  
以人民检察院名义制发的法律文书，由检察长签发；属于检察官职权范围内决定事项的，检察长可以授权检察官签发。

**Article 5** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall have the prosecutor, the chief prosecutor, and the procuratorial committee make decisions on case-handling matters within their respective terms of reference and assume corresponding judicial responsibilities in accordance with the provisions.  
Prosecutors shall work under the leadership of the chief prosecutor. Major case-handling matters shall be decided by the chief prosecutor. The chief prosecutor may, based on the circumstances of the case, submit the matter to the procuratorial committee for discussion and decision. Other case-handling matters may be decided by the chief prosecutor directly or may be delegated to a prosecutor for decision.  
Legal documents issued in the name of the People's Procuratorate shall be signed by the chief prosecutor; for matters within the scope of decision by the prosecutor, the chief prosecutor may authorize the prosecutor to sign.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，根据案件情况，可以由一名检察官独任办理，也可以由两名以上检察官组成办案组办理。由检察官办案组办理的，检察长应当指定一名检察官担任主办检察官，组织、指挥办案组办理案件。  
检察官办理案件，可以根据需要配备检察官助理、书记员、司法警察、检察技术人员等检察辅助人员。检察辅助人员依照法律规定承担相应的检察辅助事务。

**Article 6** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate may, depending on the circumstances of the case, have the case handled by a single prosecutor or by a case-handling group composed of two or more prosecutors. Where the case is handled by a prosecutor case-handling group, the chief prosecutor shall designate a prosecutor to serve as the lead prosecutor to organize and direct the case-handling group in handling the case.  
When handling a case, a prosecutor may, as necessary, be assisted by assistant prosecutors, clerks, judicial police, procuratorial technicians, and other procuratorial support personnel. The procuratorial support personnel shall undertake corresponding procuratorial support duties in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  负责公益诉讼检察的部门负责人对本部门的办案活动进行监督管理。需要报请检察长决定的事项，应当先由部门负责人审核。部门负责人可以主持召开检察官联席会议进行讨论，也可以直接报请检察长决定。

**Article 7** The head of the department responsible for public interest litigation procuratorial work shall supervise and manage the case-handling activities of that department. Matters that need to be submitted to the chief prosecutor for decision shall first be reviewed by the department head. The department head may preside over a joint meeting of prosecutors for discussion, or may directly submit the matter to the chief prosecutor for decision.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  检察长不同意检察官处理意见的，可以要求检察官复核，也可以直接作出决定，或者提请检察委员会讨论决定。  
检察官执行检察长决定时，认为决定错误的，应当书面提出意见。检察长不改变原决定的，检察官应当执行。

**Article 8** If the chief prosecutor disagrees with the prosecutor's handling opinion, the chief prosecutor may request the prosecutor to review, or may directly make a decision, or submit the matter to the procuratorial committee for discussion and decision.  
When executing a decision of the chief prosecutor, if the prosecutor considers the decision to be erroneous, the prosecutor shall submit a written opinion. If the chief prosecutor does not change the original decision, the prosecutor shall execute it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民检察院提起诉讼或者支持起诉的民事、行政公益诉讼案件，由负责民事、行政检察的部门或者办案组织分别履行诉讼监督的职责。

**Article 9** For civil and administrative public interest litigation cases in which the People's Procuratorate initiates an action or supports an action, the departments or case-handling organizations responsible for civil and administrative procuratorial work shall respectively perform the duties of litigation supervision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  最高人民检察院领导地方各级人民检察院和专门人民检察院的公益诉讼检察工作，上级人民检察院领导下级人民检察院的公益诉讼检察工作。  
上级人民检察院对下级人民检察院作出的决定，有权予以撤销或者变更；发现下级人民检察院办理的案件有错误的，有权指令下级人民检察院予以纠正。  
下级人民检察院对上级人民检察院的决定应当执行。如果认为有错误的，应当在执行的同时向上级人民检察院报告。

**Article 10** The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall lead the public interest litigation procuratorial work of local people's procuratorates at all levels and special people's procuratorates, and a people's procuratorate at a higher level shall lead the public interest litigation procuratorial work of a people's procuratorate at a lower level.  
A people's procuratorate at a higher level shall have the authority to revoke or modify a decision made by a people's procuratorate at a lower level; if it finds an error in a case handled by a people's procuratorate at a lower level, it shall have the authority to instruct the people's procuratorate at the lower level to correct it.  
A people's procuratorate at a lower level shall execute the decision of a people's procuratorate at a higher level. If it considers the decision to be erroneous, it shall report it to the people's procuratorate at the higher level at the same time as executing it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，实行一体化工作机制，上级人民检察院根据办案需要，可以交办、提办、督办、领办案件。  
上级人民检察院可以依法统一调用辖区的检察人员办理案件，调用的决定应当以书面形式作出。被调用的检察官可以代表办理案件的人民检察院履行调查、出庭等职责。

**Article 11** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall implement an integrated working mechanism. A people's procuratorate at a higher level may, based on the needs of case handling, assign, take over, supervise, or lead the handling of cases.  
A people's procuratorate at a higher level may, in accordance with law, uniformly deploy procuratorial personnel within its jurisdiction to handle cases, and the decision on deployment shall be made in writing. The deployed prosecutor may, on behalf of the people's procuratorate handling the case, perform duties such as investigation and appearing in court.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，依照规定接受人民监督员监督。

**Article 12** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall, in accordance with the provisions, accept supervision by people's supervisors.

### 第二章 一般规定

#### 第一节 管 辖

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  人民检察院办理行政公益诉讼案件，由行政机关对应的同级人民检察院立案管辖。  
行政机关为人民政府，由上一级人民检察院管辖更为适宜的，也可以由上一级人民检察院立案管辖。

**Article 13** For administrative public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate, the people's procuratorate at the same level corresponding to the administrative organ shall have jurisdiction over the case for filing.  
If the administrative organ is a people's government, and it is more appropriate for a people's procuratorate at the next higher level to have jurisdiction, the case may also be filed by the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for filing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  人民检察院办理民事公益诉讼案件，由违法行为发生地、损害结果地或者违法行为人住所地基层人民检察院立案管辖。  
刑事附带民事公益诉讼案件，由办理刑事案件的人民检察院立案管辖。

**Article 14** For civil public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate, the basic-level people's procuratorate at the place where the illegal act occurred, the place where the damage occurred, or the place of domicile of the person who committed the illegal act shall have jurisdiction over the case for filing.  
For a criminal case with a collateral civil public interest action, the people's procuratorate handling the criminal case shall have jurisdiction over the case for filing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  设区的市级以上人民检察院管辖本辖区内重大、复杂的案件。公益损害范围涉及两个以上行政区划的公益诉讼案件，可以由共同的上一级人民检察院管辖。

**Article 15** The people's procuratorate at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall have jurisdiction over major and complex cases within its jurisdiction. For public interest litigation cases where the scope of public interest damage involves two or more administrative divisions, the common people's procuratorate at the next higher level may have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  人民检察院立案管辖与人民法院诉讼管辖级别、地域不对应的，具有管辖权的人民检察院可以立案，需要提起诉讼的，应当将案件移送有管辖权人民法院对应的同级人民检察院。

**Article 16** If the jurisdiction of the People's Procuratorate for case filing does not correspond to the level and territorial jurisdiction of the People's Court for litigation, the people's procuratorate with jurisdiction may file the case. If it is necessary to initiate an action, the case shall be transferred to the people's procuratorate at the same level corresponding to the people's court with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  上级人民检察院可以根据办案需要，将下级人民检察院管辖的公益诉讼案件指定本辖区内其他人民检察院办理。  
最高人民检察院、省级人民检察院和设区的市级人民检察院可以根据跨区域协作工作机制规定，将案件指定或移送相关人民检察院跨行政区划管辖。基层人民检察院可以根据跨区域协作工作机制规定，将案件移送相关人民检察院跨行政区划管辖。  
人民检察院对管辖权发生争议的，由争议双方协商解决。协商不成的，报共同的上级人民检察院指定管辖。

**Article 17** A people's procuratorate at a higher level may, based on the needs of case handling, designate another people's procuratorate within its jurisdiction to handle a public interest litigation case that is under the jurisdiction of a lower-level people's procuratorate.  
The Supreme People's Procuratorate, a provincial-level people's procuratorate, and a people's procuratorate at the level of a city divided into districts may, in accordance with the provisions of the cross-regional cooperation mechanism, designate or transfer a case to a relevant people's procuratorate for cross-administrative-division jurisdiction. A basic-level people's procuratorate may, in accordance with the provisions of the cross-regional cooperation mechanism, transfer a case to a relevant people's procuratorate for cross-administrative-division jurisdiction.  
If a dispute over jurisdiction arises between people's procuratorates, it shall be resolved through consultation between the disputing parties. If consultation fails, the matter shall be submitted to the common people's procuratorate at the next higher level for designation of jurisdiction.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  上级人民检察院认为确有必要的，可以办理下级人民检察院管辖的案件，也可以将本院管辖的案件交下级人民检察院办理。  
下级人民检察院认为需要由上级人民检察院办理的，可以报请上级人民检察院决定。

**Article 18** If a people's procuratorate at a higher level considers it truly necessary, it may handle a case under the jurisdiction of a lower-level people's procuratorate, or may transfer a case under its own jurisdiction to a lower-level people's procuratorate for handling.  
If a lower-level people's procuratorate considers that a case needs to be handled by a higher-level people's procuratorate, it may submit a request to the higher-level people's procuratorate for decision.

#### 第二节 回 避

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  检察人员具有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，当事人、诉讼代理人有权申请其回避：  
（一）是行政公益诉讼行政机关法定代表人或者主要负责人、诉讼代理人近亲属，或者有其他关系，可能影响案件公正办理的；  
（二）是民事公益诉讼当事人、诉讼代理人近亲属，或者有其他关系，可能影响案件公正办理的。  
应当回避的检察人员，本人没有自行回避，当事人及其诉讼代理人也没有申请其回避的，检察长或者检察委员会应当决定其回避。  
前两款规定，适用于翻译人员、鉴定人、勘验人等。

**Article 19** A procuratorial personnel shall voluntarily withdraw in any of the following circumstances, and the party and the litigation agent shall have the right to apply for the withdrawal of such personnel:  
(1) being a close relative of the legal representative or principal responsible person of the administrative organ in an administrative public interest litigation, or the close relative of a litigation agent, or having other relationships that may affect the impartial handling of the case;  
(2) being a close relative of a party or litigation agent in a civil public interest litigation, or having other relationships that may affect the impartial handling of the case.  
If a procuratorial personnel who should withdraw does not voluntarily withdraw, and neither the party nor its litigation agent applies for the withdrawal, the chief prosecutor or the procuratorial committee shall decide on the withdrawal.  
The provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply to interpreters, expert evaluators, inspectors, and other such personnel.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  检察人员自行回避的，应当书面或者口头提出，并说明理由。口头提出的，应当记录在卷。

**Article 20** If a procuratorial personnel voluntarily withdraws, the withdrawal shall be proposed in writing or orally, with reasons stated. If proposed orally, it shall be recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  当事人及其诉讼代理人申请回避的，应当书面或者口头提出，并说明理由。口头提出的，应当记录在卷。  
被申请回避的人员在人民检察院作出是否回避的决定前，不停止参与本案工作。

**Article 21** If a party or its litigation agent applies for withdrawal, the application shall be made in writing or orally, with reasons stated. If made orally, it shall be recorded in the case file.  
The person whose withdrawal is applied for shall not suspend participation in the work of this case before the People's Procuratorate makes a decision on whether to withdraw.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  检察长的回避，由检察委员会讨论决定;检察人员和其他人员的回避，由检察长决定。检察委员会讨论检察长回避问题时，由副检察长主持。

**Article 22** The withdrawal of the chief prosecutor shall be discussed and decided by the procuratorial committee; the withdrawal of procuratorial personnel and other personnel shall be decided by the chief prosecutor. When the procuratorial committee discusses the withdrawal of the chief prosecutor, the deputy chief prosecutor shall preside.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  人民检察院对当事人提出的回避申请，应当在收到申请后三日内作出决定，并通知申请人。申请人对决定不服的，可以在接到决定时向原决定机关申请复议一次。人民检察院应当在三日内作出复议决定，并通知复议申请人。复议期间，被申请回避的人员不停止参与本案工作。

**Article 23** The People's Procuratorate shall make a decision within three days after receiving an application for withdrawal from a party and notify the applicant. If the applicant disagrees with the decision, the applicant may apply for reconsideration once to the original deciding authority at the time of receiving the decision. The People's Procuratorate shall make a reconsideration decision within three days and notify the reconsideration applicant. During the reconsideration period, the person whose withdrawal is applied for shall not suspend participation in the work of this case.

#### 第三节 立 案

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  公益诉讼案件线索的来源包括：  
（一）自然人、法人和非法人组织向人民检察院控告、举报的；  
（二）人民检察院在办案中发现的；  
（三）行政执法信息共享平台上发现的；  
（四）国家机关、社会团体和人大代表、政协委员等转交的；  
（五）新闻媒体、社会舆论等反映的；  
（六）其他在履行职责中发现的。

**Article 24** Sources of leads for public interest litigation cases include:  
(1) those reported or accused by natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to the People's Procuratorate;  
(2) those discovered by the People's Procuratorate in the course of handling cases;  
(3) those discovered on the administrative law enforcement information sharing platform;  
(4) those transferred by state organs, social organizations, and deputies to the People's Congress and members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, etc.;  
(5) those reflected by news media, public opinion, etc.;  
(6) others discovered in the performance of duties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  人民检察院对公益诉讼案件线索实行统一登记备案管理制度。重大案件线索应当向上一级人民检察院备案。  
人民检察院其他部门发现公益诉讼案件线索的，应当将有关材料及时移送负责公益诉讼检察的部门。

**Article 25** The People's Procuratorate shall implement a unified registration and record-filing management system for leads of public interest litigation cases. Major case leads shall be filed with the people's procuratorate at the next higher level.  
If other departments of the People's Procuratorate discover leads for public interest litigation cases, they shall promptly transfer the relevant materials to the department responsible for public interest litigation procuratorial work.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  人民检察院发现公益诉讼案件线索不属于本院管辖的，应当制作《移送案件线索通知书》，移送有管辖权的同级人民检察院，受移送的人民检察院应当受理。受移送的人民检察院认为不属于本院管辖的，应当报告上级人民检察院，不得自行退回原移送线索的人民检察院或者移送其他人民检察院。  
人民检察院发现公益诉讼案件线索属于上级人民检察院管辖的，应当制作《报请移送案件线索意见书》，报请移送上级人民检察院。

**Article 26** If the People's Procuratorate finds that a lead for a public interest litigation case is not within its jurisdiction, it shall prepare a Notice of Transfer of Case Lead, transfer it to the people's procuratorate at the same level that has jurisdiction, and the receiving people's procuratorate shall accept it. If the receiving people's procuratorate considers that the lead is not within its jurisdiction, it shall report to the higher-level people's procuratorate and shall not return the lead to the original transferring people's procuratorate or transfer it to another people's procuratorate on its own.  
If the People's Procuratorate finds that a lead for a public interest litigation case is within the jurisdiction of a higher-level people's procuratorate, it shall prepare an Opinion on Requesting Transfer of Case Lead and request transfer to the higher-level people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  人民检察院应当对公益诉讼案件线索的真实性、可查性等进行评估，必要时可以进行初步调查，并形成《初步调查报告》。

**Article 27** The People's Procuratorate shall assess the authenticity and verifiability of leads for public interest litigation cases, and may conduct a preliminary investigation if necessary, and shall form a Preliminary Investigation Report.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  人民检察院经过评估，认为国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害，可能存在违法行为的，应当立案调查。

**Article 28** If after assessment, the People's Procuratorate considers that national interests or social public interests are infringed upon and there may be illegal acts, it shall file a case for investigation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  对于国家利益或者社会公共利益受到严重侵害，人民检察院经初步调查仍难以确定不依法履行监督管理职责的行政机关或者违法行为人的，也可以立案调查。

**Article 29** For cases where national interests or social public interests are seriously infringed upon, and the People's Procuratorate, after preliminary investigation, still has difficulty in determining the administrative organ that fails to perform its regulatory duties according to law or the person who committed the illegal act, it may also file a case for investigation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  检察官对案件线索进行评估后提出立案或者不立案意见的，应当制作《立案审批表》，经过初步调查的附《初步调查报告》，报请检察长决定后制作《立案决定书》或者《不立案决定书》。

**Article 30** If a prosecutor, after assessing the case lead, proposes an opinion to file or not to file a case, the prosecutor shall prepare a Form for Approval of Case Filing, and attach the Preliminary Investigation Report if a preliminary investigation has been conducted, and submit it to the chief prosecutor for decision, and then prepare a Decision on Case Filing or a Decision on Not Filing the Case.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  负责公益诉讼检察的部门在办理公益诉讼案件过程中，发现涉嫌犯罪或者职务违法、违纪线索的，应当依照规定移送本院相关检察业务部门或者其他有管辖权的主管机关。

**Article 31** If, in the course of handling a public interest litigation case, the department responsible for public interest litigation procuratorial work discovers leads suspected of crimes or duty-related violations or disciplinary offenses, it shall, in accordance with the provisions, transfer them to the relevant procuratorial business department of this court or other competent authorities with jurisdiction.

#### 第四节 调 查

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，应当依法、客观、全面调查收集证据。

**Article 32** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall investigate and collect evidence lawfully, objectively, and comprehensively.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条  人民检察院在调查前应当制定调查方案，确定调查思路、方法、步骤以及拟收集的证据清单等。

**Article 33** Before investigation, the People's Procuratorate shall formulate an investigation plan, determining the investigation approach, methods, steps, and the list of evidence to be collected, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件的证据包括书证、物证、视听资料、电子数据、证人证言、当事人陈述、鉴定意见、专家意见、勘验笔录等。

**Article 34** Evidence in public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate includes documentary evidence, physical evidence, audio-visual materials, electronic data, witness testimony, statements of parties, expert evaluation opinions, expert opinions, inspection records, etc.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，可以采取以下方式开展调查和收集证据：  
（一）查阅、调取、复制有关执法、诉讼卷宗材料等；  
（二）询问行政机关工作人员、违法行为人以及行政相对人、利害关系人、证人等；  
（三）向有关单位和个人收集书证、物证、视听资料、电子数据等证据；  
（四）咨询专业人员、相关部门或者行业协会等对专门问题的意见；  
（五）委托鉴定、评估、审计、检验、检测、翻译；  
（六）勘验物证、现场；  
（七）其他必要的调查方式。  
人民检察院开展调查和收集证据不得采取限制人身自由或者查封、扣押、冻结财产等强制性措施。

**Article 35** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate may conduct investigations and collect evidence by the following methods:  
(1) consulting, retrieving, and copying relevant law enforcement and litigation voluminous materials, etc.;  
(2) inquiring of staff members of administrative organs, persons who committed illegal acts, as well as administrative counterparts, interested parties, witnesses, etc.;  
(3) collecting documentary evidence, physical evidence, audio-visual materials, electronic data, and other evidence from relevant entities and individuals;  
(4) consulting professionals, relevant departments or industry associations, etc., on their opinions on specialized issues;  
(5) entrusting for expert evaluation, appraisal, audit, inspection, testing, and translation;  
(6) inspecting physical evidence and the scene;  
(7) other necessary investigation methods.  
When conducting investigations and collecting evidence, the People's Procuratorate shall not adopt compulsory measures such as restricting personal liberty or sealing, seizing, or freezing property.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条  人民检察院开展调查和收集证据，应当由两名以上检察人员共同进行。检察官可以组织司法警察、检察技术人员参加，必要时可以指派或者聘请其他具有专门知识的人参与。根据案件实际情况，也可以商请相关单位协助进行。  
在调查收集证据过程中，检察人员可以依照有关规定使用执法记录仪、自动检测仪等办案设备和无人机航拍、卫星遥感等技术手段。

**Article 36** When conducting investigations and collecting evidence, the People's Procuratorate shall have two or more procuratorial personnel work together. A prosecutor may organize judicial police and procuratorial technicians to participate, and if necessary, may assign or engage other persons with specialized knowledge to participate. According to the actual circumstances of the case, assistance may also be requested from relevant entities.  
In the process of investigating and collecting evidence, procuratorial personnel may, in accordance with relevant provisions, use case-handling equipment such as law enforcement recorders and automatic detectors, and technical means such as drone aerial photography and satellite remote sensing.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条  询问应当个别进行。检察人员在询问前应当出示工作证，询问过程中应当制作《询问笔录》。被询问人确认无误后，签名或者盖章。被询问人拒绝签名盖章的，应当在笔录上注明。

**Article 37** Inquiries shall be conducted individually. Before an inquiry, the procuratorial personnel shall present their work ID. During the inquiry, an Inquiry Record shall be made. After the person being inquired confirms that it is correct, that person shall sign or affix a seal. If the person being inquired refuses to sign or affix a seal, this shall be noted in the record.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条  需要向有关单位或者个人调取物证、书证的，应当制作《调取证据通知书》和《调取证据清单》，持上述文书调取有关证据材料。  
调取书证应当调取原件，调取原件确有困难或者因保密需要无法调取原件的，可以调取复制件。书证为复制件的，应当注明调取人、提供人、调取时间、证据出处和“本复制件与原件核对一致”等字样，并签字、盖章。书证页码较多的，加盖骑缝章。  
调取物证应当调取原物，调取原物确有困难的，可以调取足以反映原物外形或者内容的照片、录像或者复制品等其他证据材料。

**Article 38** If it is necessary to retrieve physical evidence or documentary evidence from a relevant entity or individual, the People's Procuratorate shall prepare a Notice of Retrieval of Evidence and a List of Retrieved Evidence, and retrieve the relevant evidentiary materials with the above documents.  
To retrieve documentary evidence, the original shall be retrieved; if it is truly difficult to retrieve the original or it cannot be retrieved due to confidentiality requirements, a copy may be retrieved. If the documentary evidence is a copy, it shall be noted with the words 'this copy is consistent with the original after verification', the retriever, provider, time of retrieval, and source of the evidence shall be indicated, and it shall be signed and sealed. If the documentary evidence has many pages, a cross-page seal shall be affixed.  
To retrieve physical evidence, the original object shall be retrieved; if it is truly difficult to retrieve the original object, other evidentiary materials such as photographs, videos, or reproductions that sufficiently reflect the appearance or content of the original object may be retrieved.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条  人民检察院应当收集提取视听资料、电子数据的原始存储介质，调取原始存储介质确有困难或者因保密需要无法调取的，可以调取复制件。调取复制件的，应当说明其来源和制作经过。  
人民检察院自行收集提取视听资料、电子数据的，应当注明收集时间、地点、收集人员及其他需要说明的情况。

**Article 39** The People's Procuratorate shall collect and extract the original storage media of audio-visual materials and electronic data. If it is truly difficult to retrieve the original storage media or it cannot be retrieved due to confidentiality requirements, copies may be retrieved. When copies are retrieved, their source and production process shall be explained.  
If the People's Procuratorate collects and extracts audio-visual materials and electronic data on its own, it shall indicate the time, place, collecting personnel, and other circumstances that need to be explained.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条  人民检察院可以就专门性问题书面或者口头咨询有关专业人员、相关部门或者行业协会的意见。  
口头咨询的，应当制作笔录，由接受咨询的专业人员签名或者盖章。书面咨询的，应当由出具咨询意见的专业人员或者单位签名、盖章。

**Article 40** The People's Procuratorate may consult relevant professionals, relevant departments, or industry associations for opinions on specialized issues in writing or orally.  
If the consultation is oral, a record shall be made, and the professional who accepted the consultation shall sign or affix a seal. If the consultation is in writing, the professional or entity that provided the consultation opinion shall sign or affix a seal.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条  人民检察院对专门性问题认为确有必要鉴定、评估、审计、检验、检测、翻译的，可以委托具备资格的机构进行鉴定、评估、审计、检验、检测、翻译，委托时应当制作《委托鉴定（评估、审计、检验、检测、翻译）函》。

**Article 41** If the People's Procuratorate considers it truly necessary to conduct expert evaluation, appraisal, audit, inspection, testing, or translation on a specialized issue, it may entrust a qualified institution to carry out the expert evaluation, appraisal, audit, inspection, testing, or translation. When entrusting, it shall prepare a Letter of Entrustment for Expert Evaluation (Appraisal, Audit, Inspection, Testing, Translation).

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条  人民检察院认为确有必要的，可以勘验物证或者现场。  
勘验应当在检察官的主持下，由两名以上检察人员进行，可以邀请见证人参加。必要时，可以指派或者聘请有专门知识的人进行。勘验情况和结果应当制作笔录，由参加勘验的人员、见证人签名或者盖章。  
检察技术人员可以依照相关规定在勘验过程中进行取样并进行快速检测。

**Article 42** If the People's Procuratorate considers it truly necessary, it may inspect physical evidence or the scene.  
An inspection shall be conducted under the auspices of a prosecutor by two or more procuratorial personnel, and witnesses may be invited to participate. If necessary, persons with specialized knowledge may be assigned or engaged to carry out the inspection. A record of the circumstances and results of the inspection shall be made, signed or sealed by the persons participating in the inspection and witnesses.  
Procuratorial technicians may, in accordance with relevant provisions, take samples and conduct rapid testing during the inspection.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，需要异地调查收集证据的，可以自行调查或者委托当地同级人民检察院进行。委托时应当出具委托书，载明需要调查的对象、事项及要求。受委托人民检察院应当在收到委托书之日起三十日内完成调查，并将情况回复委托的人民检察院。

**Article 43** If the People's Procuratorate needs to conduct an investigation and collect evidence in another locality when handling a public interest litigation case, it may conduct the investigation on its own or entrust the local people's procuratorate at the same level to do so. When entrusting, it shall issue a letter of entrustment, specifying the object, matters, and requirements of the investigation. The entrusted people's procuratorate shall complete the investigation within thirty days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment and reply to the entrusting people's procuratorate with the information.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条  人民检察院可以依照规定组织听证，听取听证员、行政机关、违法行为人、行政相对人、受害人代表等相关各方意见，了解有关情况。  
听证形成的书面材料是人民检察院依法办理公益诉讼案件的重要参考。

**Article 44** The People's Procuratorate may, in accordance with the provisions, organize a hearing to hear opinions from hearing officers, administrative organs, persons who committed illegal acts, administrative counterparts, representatives of victims, and other relevant parties, and to understand relevant circumstances.  
The written materials formed from the hearing are important references for the People's Procuratorate in handling public interest litigation cases according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条  行政机关及其工作人员拒绝或者妨碍人民检察院调查收集证据的，人民检察院可以向同级人大常委会报告，向同级纪检监察机关通报，或者通过上级人民检察院向其上级主管机关通报。

**Article 45** If an administrative organ or its staff refuses or obstructs the People's Procuratorate in investigating and collecting evidence, the People's Procuratorate may report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level, notify the disciplinary inspection and supervision authority at the same level, or, through the people's procuratorate at a higher level, notify its higher competent authority.

#### 第五节 提起诉讼

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条  人民检察院对于符合起诉条件的公益诉讼案件，应当依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
人民检察院提起公益诉讼，应当向人民法院提交公益诉讼起诉书和相关证据材料。起诉书的主要内容包括：  
（一）公益诉讼起诉人；  
（二）被告的基本信息；  
（三）诉讼请求及所依据的事实和理由。  
公益诉讼起诉书应当自送达人民法院之日起五日内报上一级人民检察院备案。

**Article 46** For public interest litigation cases that meet the conditions for prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall initiate an action in the People's Court according to law.  
When initiating a public interest litigation action, the People's Procuratorate shall submit a public interest litigation indictment and relevant evidentiary materials to the People's Court. The main contents of the indictment include:  
(1) the public interest litigation prosecutor;  
(2) the basic information of the defendant;  
(3) the claims and the facts and grounds on which they are based.  
The public interest litigation indictment shall be reported to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for filing within five days from the date of service to the People's Court.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条  人民检察院办理行政公益诉讼案件，审查起诉期限为一个月，自检察建议整改期满之日起计算。  
人民检察院办理民事公益诉讼案件，审查起诉期限为三个月，自公告期满之日起计算。  
移送其他人民检察院起诉的，受移送的人民检察院审查起诉期限自收到案件之日起计算。  
重大、疑难、复杂案件需要延长审查起诉期限的，行政公益诉讼案件经检察长批准后可以延长一个月，还需要延长的，报上一级人民检察院批准，上一级人民检察院认为已经符合起诉条件的，可以依照[本规则第十七条](#art-17)规定指定本辖区内其他人民检察院提起诉讼。民事公益诉讼案件经检察长批准后可以延长一个月，还需要延长的，报上一级人民检察院批准。

**Article 47** For administrative public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate, the time limit for review of prosecution shall be one month, calculated from the date on which the rectification period of the procuratorial suggestion expires.  
For civil public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate, the time limit for review of prosecution shall be three months, calculated from the date on which the announcement period expires.  
If the case is transferred to another people's procuratorate for prosecution, the time limit for review of prosecution by the receiving people's procuratorate shall be calculated from the date of receipt of the case.  
If a major, difficult, or complex case requires an extension of the time limit for review of prosecution, for an administrative public interest litigation case, it may be extended by one month after approval by the chief prosecutor. If further extension is needed, approval from the people's procuratorate at the next higher level shall be obtained. If the people's procuratorate at the next higher level considers that the conditions for prosecution are already met, it may, in accordance with Article 17 of these Rules, designate another people's procuratorate within its jurisdiction to initiate the action. For a civil public interest litigation case, it may be extended by one month after approval by the chief prosecutor. If further extension is needed, approval from the people's procuratorate at the next higher level shall be obtained.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条  人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，委托鉴定、评估、审计、检验、检测、翻译期间不计入审查起诉期限。

**Article 48** When handling public interest litigation cases, the period for entrusting expert evaluation, appraisal, audit, inspection, testing, and translation shall not be counted in the time limit for review of prosecution.

#### 第六节 出席第一审法庭

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条  人民检察院提起公益诉讼的案件，应当派员出庭履行职责，参加相关诉讼活动。  
人民检察院应当自收到人民法院出庭通知书之日起三日内向人民法院提交《派员出庭通知书》。《派员出庭通知书》应当写明出庭人员的姓名、法律职务以及出庭履行的职责。  
人民检察院应当指派检察官出席第一审法庭，检察官助理可以协助检察官出庭，并根据需要配备书记员担任记录及其他辅助工作。涉及专门性、技术性问题，可以指派或者聘请有专门知识的人协助检察官出庭。

**Article 49** For a case in which the People's Procuratorate initiates a public interest litigation action, it shall send personnel to appear in court to perform duties and participate in relevant litigation activities.  
The People's Procuratorate shall submit a Notice of Sending Personnel to Appear in Court to the People's Court within three days from the date of receiving the notice of appearance from the People's Court. The Notice of Sending Personnel to Appear in Court shall specify the names, legal positions, and duties to be performed in court by the appearing personnel.  
The People's Procuratorate shall assign a prosecutor to appear in the first-instance trial. An assistant prosecutor may assist the prosecutor in appearing in court, and a clerk may be assigned to perform recording and other auxiliary work as needed. If specialized or technical issues are involved, persons with specialized knowledge may be assigned or engaged to assist the prosecutor in appearing in court.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条  人民法院通知人民检察院派员参加证据交换、庭前会议的，由出席法庭的检察人员参加。人民检察院认为有必要的，可以商人民法院组织证据交换或者召开庭前会议。

**Article 50** If the People's Court notifies the People's Procuratorate to send personnel to participate in evidence exchange or pre-trial conference, the procuratorial personnel appearing in court shall participate. If the People's Procuratorate considers it necessary, it may consult with the People's Court to organize evidence exchange or convene a pre-trial conference.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条  出庭检察人员履行以下职责：  
（一）宣读公益诉讼起诉书；  
（二）对人民检察院调查收集的证据予以出示和说明，对相关证据进行质证；  
（三）参加法庭调查、进行辩论，并发表出庭意见；  
（四）依法从事其他诉讼活动。

**Article 51** The prosecutors appearing in court shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Reading the public interest litigation indictment;  
(2) Presenting and explaining the evidence collected by the People's Procuratorate through investigation, and cross-examining relevant evidence;  
(3) Participating in the court investigation, conducting debates, and presenting opinions in court;  
(4) Engaging in other litigation activities according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条  出庭检察人员应当客观、全面地向法庭出示证据。根据庭审情况合理安排举证顺序，分组列举证据，可以使用多媒体等示证方式。质证应当围绕证据的真实性、合法性、关联性展开。

**Article 52** The prosecutors appearing in court shall objectively and comprehensively present evidence to the court. They shall reasonably arrange the order of adducing evidence based on the trial circumstances, group and list evidence, and may use multimedia and other methods of presenting evidence. Cross-examination shall center on the authenticity, legality, and relevance of evidence.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条  出庭检察人员向被告、证人、鉴定人、勘验人等发问应当遵循下列要求：  
（一）围绕案件基本事实和争议焦点进行发问；  
（二）与调查收集的证据相互支撑；  
（三）不得使用带有人身攻击或者威胁性的语言和方式。

**Article 53** The prosecutors appearing in court shall observe the following requirements when questioning the defendant, witnesses, expert evaluators, inspectors, and others:  
(1) Conducting questioning around the basic facts of the case and the disputed issues;  
(2) The questioning shall be mutually supportive with the evidence collected through investigation;  
(3) No language or manner of personal attack or threat shall be used.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条  出庭检察人员可以申请人民法院通知证人、鉴定人、有专门知识的人出庭作证或者提出意见。

**Article 54** The prosecutors appearing in court may apply to the People's Court to notify witnesses, expert evaluators, or persons with specialized knowledge to testify in court or provide opinions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条  出庭检察人员在法庭审理期间，发现需要补充调查的，可以在法庭休庭后进行补充调查。

**Article 55** If, during the trial, the prosecutors appearing in court discover that supplementary investigation is necessary, they may conduct supplementary investigation after the court adjourns.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条  出庭检察人员参加法庭辩论，应结合法庭调查情况，围绕双方在事实、证据、法律适用等方面的争议焦点发表辩论意见。

**Article 56** The prosecutors appearing in court participating in court debates shall, in light of the court investigation, present their arguments focusing on the disputed issues between the parties regarding facts, evidence, and application of law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条  出庭检察人员应当结合庭审情况，客观公正发表出庭意见。

**Article 57** The prosecutors appearing in court shall, in light of the trial circumstances, objectively and impartially present their opinions in court.

#### 第七节 上 诉

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条  人民检察院应当在收到人民法院第一审公益诉讼判决书、裁定书后三日内报送上一级人民检察院备案。  
人民检察院认为第一审公益诉讼判决、裁定确有错误的，应当提出上诉。  
提出上诉的，由提起诉讼的人民检察院决定。上一级人民检察院应当同步审查进行指导。

**Article 58** The People's Procuratorate shall, within three days after receiving the judgment or ruling on first-instance public interest litigation from the People's Court, submit the documents to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for recordation.  
If the People's Procuratorate considers that the first-instance judgment or ruling on public interest litigation is indeed erroneous, it shall file an appeal.  
The decision to file an appeal shall be made by the People's Procuratorate that initiated the lawsuit. The People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall simultaneously review the case to provide guidance.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条  人民检察院提出上诉的，应当制作公益诉讼上诉书。公益诉讼上诉书的主要内容包括：  
（一）公益诉讼上诉人；  
（二）被上诉人的基本情况；  
（三）原审人民法院名称、案件编号和案由；  
（四）上诉请求和事实理由。

**Article 59** If the People's Procuratorate files an appeal, it shall prepare a public interest litigation appeal document. The main contents of the public interest litigation appeal document include:  
(1) The appellant in the public interest litigation;  
(2) The basic information of the respondent;  
(3) The name of the original trial People's Court, case number, and cause of action;  
(4) The appeal requests and the facts and reasons.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条  人民检察院应当在上诉期限内通过原审人民法院向上一级人民法院提交公益诉讼上诉书，并将副本连同相关证据材料报送上一级人民检察院。

**Article 60** The People's Procuratorate shall, within the appeal period, submit the public interest litigation appeal document to the People's Court at the next higher level through the original trial People's Court, and shall submit a copy together with the relevant evidence materials to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条  上一级人民检察院认为上诉不当的，应当指令下级人民检察院撤回上诉。  
上一级人民检察院在上诉期限内，发现下级人民检察院应当上诉而没有提出上诉的，应当指令下级人民检察院依法提出上诉。

**Article 61** If the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level considers the appeal to be improper, it shall instruct the lower People's Procuratorate to withdraw the appeal.  
If, within the appeal period, the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level discovers that the lower People's Procuratorate should have filed an appeal but did not, it shall instruct the lower People's Procuratorate to file an appeal according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条  被告不服第一审公益诉讼判决、裁定上诉的，人民检察院应当在收到上诉状副本后三日内报送上一级人民检察院，提起诉讼的人民检察院和上一级人民检察院应当全面审查案卷材料。

**Article 62** If the defendant, dissatisfied with the first-instance judgment or ruling on public interest litigation, files an appeal, the People's Procuratorate shall, within three days after receiving a copy of the petition for appeal, submit it to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level. The People's Procuratorate that initiated the lawsuit and the Procuratorate at the next higher level shall comprehensively review the case file materials.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条  人民法院决定开庭审理的上诉案件，提起诉讼的人民检察院和上一级人民检察院应当共同派员出席第二审法庭。  
人民检察院应当在出席第二审法庭之前向人民法院提交《派员出庭通知书》，载明人民检察院出庭检察人员的姓名、法律职务以及出庭履行的职责等。

**Article 63** For appeal cases in which the People's Court decides to hold a hearing, the People's Procuratorate that initiated the lawsuit and the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall jointly send personnel to appear in the second-instance court.  
The People's Procuratorate shall, before appearing in the second-instance court, submit a "Notice of Sending Personnel to Appear in Court" to the People's Court, specifying the names, legal positions, and duties to be performed in court of the prosecutors from the People's Procuratorate.

#### 第八节 诉讼监督

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条  最高人民检察院发现各级人民法院、上级人民检察院发现下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的公益诉讼判决、裁定确有错误，损害国家利益或者社会公共利益的，应当依法提出抗诉。

**Article 64** If the Supreme People's Procuratorate discovers that any judgment or ruling on public interest litigation that has become legally effective from People's Courts at any level is indeed erroneous and harms national interests or the public interest, or if a People's Procuratorate at a higher level discovers that a judgment or ruling from a lower People's Court is such, it shall file a protest according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条  人民法院决定开庭审理的公益诉讼再审案件，与人民法院对应的同级人民检察院应当派员出席法庭。

**Article 65** For retrial cases of public interest litigation in which the People's Court decides to hold a hearing, the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the People's Court shall send personnel to appear in court.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条  人民检察院发现人民法院公益诉讼审判程序违反法律规定，或者审判人员有[《中华人民共和国法官法》第四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国法官法.md#art-46)规定的违法行为，可能影响案件公正审判、执行的，或者人民法院在公益诉讼案件判决生效后不依法移送执行或者执行活动违反法律规定的，应当依法向同级人民法院提出检察建议。

**Article 66** If the People's Procuratorate discovers that the trial procedure of the People's Court in public interest litigation violates the provisions of law, or that the judge has committed an illegal act as specified in Article 46 of the Judges Law of the People's Republic of China, which may affect the fair trial or enforcement of the case, or that the People's Court, after the judgment in a public interest litigation case takes effect, fails to transfer the case for enforcement according to law or that the enforcement activities violate the provisions of law, the People's Procuratorate shall, according to law, issue a procuratorial recommendation to the People's Court at the same level.

### 第三章 行政公益诉讼

#### 第一节 立案与调查

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条  人民检察院经过对行政公益诉讼案件线索进行评估，认为同时存在以下情形的，应当立案：  
国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害；  
（二）生态环境和资源保护、食品药品安全、国有财产保护、国有土地使用权出让、未成年人保护等领域对保护国家利益或者社会公共利益负有监督管理职责的行政机关可能违法行使职权或者不作为。

**Article 67** After evaluating the leads of an administrative public interest litigation case, if the People's Procuratorate considers that the following circumstances both exist, it shall open a case:  
(1) The national interests or the public interest are infringed;  
(2) In areas such as ecological environment and resource protection, food and drug safety, state-owned property protection, transfer of state-owned land use rights, and protection of minors, an administrative organ that is responsible for supervision and management over the protection of national interests or the public interest may have illegally exercised its powers or failed to take action.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条  人民检察院对于符合[本规则第六十七条](#art-67)规定的下列情形，应当立案：  
（一）对于行政机关作出的行政决定，行政机关有强制执行权而怠于强制执行，或者没有强制执行权而怠于申请人民法院强制执行的；  
（二）在人民法院强制执行过程中，行政机关违法处分执行标的的；  
（三）根据地方裁执分离规定，人民法院将行政强制执行案件交由有强制执行权的行政机关执行，行政机关不依法履职的；  
（四）其他行政强制执行中行政机关违法行使职权或者不作为的情形。

**Article 68** The People's Procuratorate shall open a case for the following circumstances that meet the provisions of Article 67 of these Rules:  
(1) With respect to an administrative decision made by an administrative organ, the administrative organ has the power of compulsory execution but is idle in exercising it, or has no such power but is idle in applying to the People's Court for compulsory execution;  
(2) During the compulsory execution by the People's Court, the administrative organ illegally disposes of the subject matter of execution;  
(3) According to the local provisions on separation of adjudication and execution, the People's Court assigns the administrative compulsory execution case to an administrative organ with the power of compulsory execution, but the administrative organ fails to perform its duties according to law;  
(4) Other circumstances where an administrative organ illegally exercises its powers or fails to take action in administrative compulsory execution.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条  对于同一侵害国家利益或者社会公共利益的损害后果，数个负有不同监督管理职责的行政机关均可能存在不依法履行职责情形的，人民检察院可以对数个行政机关分别立案。  
人民检察院在立案前发现同一行政机关对多个同一性质的违法行为可能存在不依法履行职责情形的，应当作为一个案件立案。在发出检察建议前发现其他同一性质的违法行为的，应当与已立案案件一并处理。

**Article 69** Where, for the same harmful consequence infringing national interests or the public interest, several administrative organs with different supervision and management duties may all have failed to perform their duties according to law, the People's Procuratorate may open separate cases against each administrative organ.  
If, before opening a case, the People's Procuratorate discovers that the same administrative organ may have failed to perform its duties according to law in respect of multiple illegal acts of the same nature, it shall open one case. If, before issuing a procuratorial recommendation, it discovers other illegal acts of the same nature, they shall be handled together with the case that has already been opened.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条  人民检察院决定立案的，应当在七日内将《立案决定书》送达行政机关，并可以就其是否存在违法行使职权或者不作为、国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的后果、整改方案等事项进行磋商。  
磋商可以采取召开磋商座谈会、向行政机关发送事实确认书等方式进行，并形成会议记录或者纪要等书面材料。

**Article 70** If the People's Procuratorate decides to open a case, it shall serve the "Decision on Opening a Case" on the administrative organ within seven days, and may conduct consultations on matters such as whether there is illegal exercise of powers or inaction, the consequences of the infringement of national interests or the public interest, and the rectification plan.  
Consultations may be conducted through such methods as holding consultation seminars or sending a confirmation of facts to the administrative organ, and written materials such as meeting minutes or summaries shall be formed.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条  人民检察院办理行政公益诉讼案件，围绕以下事项进行调查：  
（一）国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的事实；  
（二）行政机关的监督管理职责；  
（三）行政机关不依法履行职责的行为；  
（四）行政机关不依法履行职责的行为与国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的关联性；  
（五）其他需要查明的事项。

**Article 71** When handling an administrative public interest litigation case, the People's Procuratorate shall conduct an investigation concerning the following matters:  
(1) The facts of the infringement of national interests or the public interest;  
(2) The supervision and management duties of the administrative organ;  
(3) The act of the administrative organ failing to perform its duties according to law;  
(4) The correlation between the administrative organ's failure to perform its duties according to law and the infringement of national interests or the public interest;  
(5) Other matters that need to be ascertained.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条  人民检察院认定行政机关监督管理职责的依据为法律法规规章，可以参考行政机关的“三定”方案、权力清单和责任清单等。

**Article 72** The basis for the People's Procuratorate to determine the supervision and management duties of an administrative organ shall be laws, regulations, and rules, and it may refer to the administrative organ's "three fix" plan (fixed functions, fixed institutions, and fixed staffing), power list, and responsibility list.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条  调查结束，检察官应当制作《调查终结报告》，区分情况提出以下处理意见：  
（一）终结案件；  
（二）提出检察建议。

**Article 73** After the investigation is concluded, the prosecutor shall prepare a "Report on Conclusion of Investigation" and, depending on the circumstances, propose one of the following handling opinions:  
(1) Closing the case;  
(2) Issuing a procuratorial recommendation.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条  经调查，人民检察院认为存在下列情形之一的，应当作出终结案件决定：  
（一）行政机关未违法行使职权或者不作为的；  
（二）国家利益或者社会公共利益已经得到有效保护的；  
（三）行政机关已经全面采取整改措施依法履行职责的；  
（四）其他应当终结案件的情形。  
终结案件的，应当报检察长决定，并制作《终结案件决定书》送达行政机关。

**Article 74** After investigation, if the People's Procuratorate considers that any of the following circumstances exists, it shall make a decision to close the case:  
(1) The administrative organ has not illegally exercised its powers or failed to take action;  
(2) The national interests or the public interest have been effectively protected;  
(3) The administrative organ has comprehensively taken rectification measures and performed its duties according to law;  
(4) Other circumstances under which the case shall be closed.  
If the case is closed, the decision shall be submitted to the chief procurator for approval, and a "Decision on Closing the Case" shall be prepared and served on the administrative organ.

#### 第二节 检察建议

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条  经调查，人民检察院认为行政机关不依法履行职责，致使国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的，应当报检察长决定向行政机关提出检察建议，并于《检察建议书》送达之日起五日内向上一级人民检察院备案。  
《检察建议书》应当包括以下内容：  
（一）行政机关的名称；  
（二）案件来源；  
（三）国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的事实；  
（四）认定行政机关不依法履行职责的事实和理由；  
（五）提出检察建议的法律依据；  
（六）建议的具体内容；  
（七）行政机关整改期限；  
（八）其他需要说明的事项。  
《检察建议书》的建议内容应当与可能提起的行政公益诉讼请求相衔接。

**Article 75** After investigation, if the People's Procuratorate considers that the administrative organ has failed to perform its duties according to law, thereby causing infringement of national interests or the public interest, it shall submit the matter to the chief procurator for a decision to issue a procuratorial recommendation to the administrative organ, and shall report it to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for recordation within five days from the date of service of the "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter".  
The "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter" shall include the following contents:  
(1) The name of the administrative organ;  
(2) The source of the case;  
(3) The facts of the infringement of national interests or the public interest;  
(4) The facts and reasons for determining that the administrative organ has failed to perform its duties according to law;  
(5) The legal basis for issuing the procuratorial recommendation;  
(6) The specific content of the recommendation;  
(7) The period for rectification by the administrative organ;  
(8) Other matters that need to be explained.  
The content of the recommendation in the "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter" shall be connected with the claims that may be raised in an administrative public interest litigation.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条  人民检察院决定提出检察建议的，应当在三日内将《检察建议书》送达行政机关。  
行政机关拒绝签收的，应当在送达回证上记录，把《检察建议书》留在其住所地，并可以采用拍照、录像等方式记录送达过程。  
人民检察院可以采取宣告方式向行政机关送达《检察建议书》，必要时，可以邀请人大代表、政协委员、人民监督员等参加。

**Article 76** If the People's Procuratorate decides to issue a procuratorial recommendation, it shall serve the "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter" on the administrative organ within three days.  
If the administrative organ refuses to sign for receipt, the service shall be recorded on the proof of service, the "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter" shall be left at its domicile, and the service process may be recorded by means such as photography or video recording.  
The People's Procuratorate may serve the "Procuratorial Recommendation Letter" on the administrative organ by means of a declaration, and, if necessary, may invite deputies to the People's Congress, members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, people's supervisors, and others to participate.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条  提出检察建议后，人民检察院应当对行政机关履行职责的情况和国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的情况跟进调查，收集相关证据材料。

**Article 77** After issuing a procuratorial recommendation, the People's Procuratorate shall conduct follow-up investigations on the performance of duties by the administrative organ and the status of the infringement of national interests or the public interest, and collect relevant evidence materials.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条  行政机关在法律、司法解释规定的整改期限内已依法作出行政决定或者制定整改方案，但因突发事件等客观原因不能全部整改到位，且没有怠于履行监督管理职责情形的，人民检察院可以中止审查。  
中止审查的，应当经检察长批准，制作《中止审查决定书》，并报送上一级人民检察院备案。中止审查的原因消除后，应当恢复审查并制作《恢复审查决定书》。

**Article 78** If, within the rectification period prescribed by law or judicial interpretation, the administrative organ has made an administrative decision or formulated a rectification plan according to law, but cannot complete full rectification due to objective reasons such as emergencies, and there is no circumstance of being idle in performing supervision and management duties, the People's Procuratorate may suspend the review.  
If the review is suspended, it shall be approved by the chief procurator, a "Decision on Suspension of Review" shall be prepared, and it shall be submitted to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for recordation. After the reason for suspension is eliminated, the review shall be resumed and a "Decision on Resumption of Review" shall be prepared.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条  经过跟进调查，检察官应当制作《审查终结报告》，区分情况提出以下处理意见：  
（一）终结案件；  
（二）提起行政公益诉讼；  
（三）移送其他人民检察院处理。

**Article 79** After conducting follow-up investigation, the prosecutor shall prepare a "Report on Conclusion of Review" and, depending on the circumstances, propose one of the following handling opinions:  
(1) Closing the case;  
(2) Initiating an administrative public interest litigation;  
(3) Transferring the case to another People's Procuratorate for handling.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条  经审查，人民检察院发现有[本规则第七十四条](#art-74)第一款规定情形之一的，应当终结案件。

**Article 80** After review, if the People's Procuratorate discovers that any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 74 of these Rules exists, it shall close the case.

#### 第三节 提起诉讼

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条  行政机关经检察建议督促仍然没有依法履行职责，国家利益或者社会公共利益处于受侵害状态的，人民检察院应当依法提起行政公益诉讼。

**Article 81** If, after being urged by the procuratorial recommendation, the administrative organ still fails to perform its duties according to law, and the national interests or the public interest remain in a state of infringement, the People's Procuratorate shall initiate an administrative public interest litigation according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条  有下列情形之一的，人民检察院可以认定行政机关未依法履行职责：  
逾期不回复检察建议，也没有采取有效整改措施的；  
（二）已经制定整改措施，但没有实质性执行的；  
（三）虽按期回复，但未采取整改措施或者仅采取部分整改措施的；  
（四）违法行为人已经被追究刑事责任或者案件已经移送刑事司法机关处理，但行政机关仍应当继续依法履行职责的；  
（五）因客观障碍导致整改方案难以按期执行，但客观障碍消除后未及时恢复整改的；  
（六）整改措施违反法律法规规定的；  
（七）其他没有依法履行职责的情形。

**Article 82** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Procuratorate may determine that the administrative organ has failed to perform its duties according to law:  
(1) Failing to respond to the procuratorial recommendation within the time limit and taking no effective rectification measures;  
(2) Having formulated rectification measures but failing to implement them substantially;  
(3) Having responded within the time limit but taking no rectification measures or only taking partial rectification measures;  
(4) The person who committed the illegal act has been investigated for criminal liability or the case has been transferred to a criminal justice organ for handling, but the administrative organ still shall continue to perform its duties according to law;  
(5) The rectification plan is difficult to be implemented on time due to objective obstacles, but rectification is not resumed promptly after the obstacles are removed;  
(6) The rectification measures violate the provisions of laws or regulations;  
(7) Other circumstances of failing to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条  人民检察院可以根据行政机关的不同违法情形，向人民法院提出确认行政行为违法或者无效、撤销或者部分撤销违法行政行为、依法履行法定职责、变更行政行为等诉讼请求。  
依法履行法定职责的诉讼请求中不予载明行政相对人承担具体义务或者减损具体权益的事项。

**Article 83** The People's Procuratorate may, according to the different illegal circumstances of the administrative organ, raise claims to the People's Court such as confirming that the administrative act is illegal or void, revoking or partially revoking the illegal administrative act, performing legal duties according to law, or modifying the administrative act.  
In the claim for performing legal duties according to law, matters concerning the administrative counterpart undertaking specific obligations or suffering specific impairment of rights and interests shall not be specified.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条  在行政公益诉讼案件审理过程中，行政机关已经依法履行职责而全部实现诉讼请求的，人民检察院可以撤回起诉。确有必要的，人民检察院可以变更诉讼请求，请求判决确认行政行为违法。  
人民检察院决定撤回起诉或者变更诉讼请求的，应当经检察长决定后制作《撤回起诉决定书》或者《变更诉讼请求决定书》，并在三日内提交人民法院。

**Article 84** During the trial of an administrative public interest litigation case, if the administrative organ has performed its duties according to law and all the claims have been realized, the People's Procuratorate may withdraw the prosecution. If truly necessary, the People's Procuratorate may modify the claims and request a judgment confirming that the administrative act is illegal.  
If the People's Procuratorate decides to withdraw the prosecution or modify the claims, it shall, after the decision is made by the chief procurator, prepare a "Decision on Withdrawal of Prosecution" or a "Decision on Modification of Claims", and submit it to the People's Court within three days.

### 第四章 民事公益诉讼

#### 第一节 立案与调查

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条  人民检察院经过对民事公益诉讼线索进行评估，认为同时存在以下情形的，应当立案：  
（一）社会公共利益受到损害；  
（二）可能存在破坏生态环境和资源保护，食品药品安全领域侵害众多消费者合法权益，侵犯未成年人合法权益，侵害英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉等损害社会公共利益的违法行为。

**Article 85** After evaluating the leads of a civil public interest litigation case, if the People's Procuratorate considers that the following circumstances both exist, it shall open a case:  
(1) The social public interest is damaged;  
(2) There may be illegal acts that damage the social public interest, such as damaging the ecological environment and resource protection, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous consumers in the field of food and drug safety, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of minors, or infringing upon the names, portraits, reputations, honors, etc. of heroes and martyrs.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条  人民检察院立案后，应当调查以下事项：  
（一）违法行为人的基本情况；  
（二）违法行为人实施的损害社会公共利益的行为；  
（三）社会公共利益受到损害的类型、具体数额或者修复费用等；  
（四）违法行为与损害后果之间的因果关系；  
（五）违法行为人的主观过错情况；  
（六）违法行为人是否存在免除或者减轻责任的相关事实；  
（七）其他需要查明的事项。  
对于污染环境、破坏生态等应当由违法行为人依法就其不承担责任或者减轻责任，及其行为与损害后果之间不存在因果关系承担举证责任的案件，可以重点调查（一）（二）（三）项以及违法行为与损害后果之间的关联性。

**Article 86** After opening a case, the People's Procuratorate shall investigate the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the person who committed the illegal act;  
(2) The act committed by the person who committed the illegal act that harms the social public interest;  
(3) The type, specific amount, or restoration cost of the damage to the social public interest;  
(4) The causal relationship between the illegal act and the harmful consequences;  
(5) The subjective fault of the person who committed the illegal act;  
(6) Whether there are facts that exempt or mitigate the liability of the person who committed the illegal act;  
(7) Other matters that need to be ascertained.  
For cases such as environmental pollution or ecological damage where the person who committed the illegal act shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding exemption or mitigation of liability and the absence of a causal relationship between the act and the harmful consequences, the investigation may focus on items (1), (2), and (3) and the correlation between the illegal act and the harmful consequences.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条  人民检察院办理涉及刑事犯罪的民事公益诉讼案件，在刑事案件的委托鉴定评估中，可以同步提出公益诉讼案件办理的鉴定评估需求。

**Article 87** When handling a civil public interest litigation case involving a criminal offense, the People's Procuratorate may, in the entrusted expert evaluation of the criminal case, simultaneously raise the needs for expert evaluation for handling the public interest litigation case.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条  刑事侦查中依法收集的证据材料，可以在基于同一违法事实提起的民事公益诉讼案件中作为证据使用。

**Article 88** Evidence materials lawfully collected during criminal investigation may be used as evidence in a civil public interest litigation case initiated based on the same illegal facts.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条  调查结束，检察官应当制作《调查终结报告》，区分情况提出以下处理意见：  
（一）终结案件；  
（二）发布公告。

**Article 89** After the investigation is concluded, the prosecutor shall prepare a "Report on Conclusion of Investigation" and, depending on the circumstances, propose one of the following handling opinions:  
(1) Closing the case;  
(2) Issuing an announcement.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条  经调查，人民检察院发现存在以下情形之一的，应当终结案件：  
（一）不存在违法行为的；  
（二）生态环境损害赔偿权利人与赔偿义务人经磋商达成赔偿协议，或者已经提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼的；  
（三）英雄烈士等的近亲属不同意人民检察院提起公益诉讼的；  
（四）其他适格主体依法向人民法院提起诉讼的；  
（五）社会公共利益已经得到有效保护的；  
（六）其他应当终结案件的情形。  
有前款（二）（三）（四）项情形之一，人民检察院支持起诉的除外。  
终结案件的，应当报请检察长决定，并制作《终结案件决定书》。

**Article 90** After investigation, if the People's Procuratorate discovers that any of the following circumstances exists, it shall close the case:  
(1) There is no illegal act;  
(2) The right holder of ecological environment damage compensation and the obligor have reached a compensation agreement through consultation, or have already initiated an ecological environment damage compensation lawsuit;  
(3) The near relatives of heroes and martyrs do not consent to the People's Procuratorate initiating a public interest litigation;  
(4) Other eligible subjects have initiated a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law;  
(5) The social public interest has been effectively protected;  
(6) Other circumstances under which the case shall be closed.  
Except where the People's Procuratorate supports the prosecution in the case of any of the circumstances in items (2), (3), or (4) of the preceding paragraph.  
If the case is closed, the decision shall be submitted to the chief procurator for approval, and a "Decision on Closing the Case" shall be prepared.

#### 第二节 公 告

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条  经调查，人民检察院认为社会公共利益受到损害，存在违法行为的，应当依法发布公告。公告应当包括以下内容：  
（一）社会公共利益受到损害的事实；  
（二）告知适格主体可以向人民法院提起诉讼，符合启动生态环境损害赔偿程序条件的案件，告知赔偿权利人启动生态环境损害赔偿程序；  
（三）公告期限；  
（四）联系人、联系电话；  
（五）公告单位、日期。  
公告应当在具有全国影响的媒体发布，公告期间为三十日。

**Article 91** After investigation, if the People's Procuratorate considers that the social public interest is damaged and there is an illegal act, it shall issue an announcement according to law. The announcement shall include the following contents:  
(1) The facts of the damage to the social public interest;  
(2) Informing eligible subjects that they may file a lawsuit with the People's Court, and for cases that meet the conditions for initiating an ecological environment damage compensation procedure, informing the compensation right holder to initiate the ecological environment damage compensation procedure;  
(3) The period of the announcement;  
(4) Contact person and contact number;  
(5) The issuing authority and the date.  
The announcement shall be published in media with national influence, and the announcement period shall be thirty days.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条  人民检察院办理侵害英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉的民事公益诉讼案件，可以直接征询英雄烈士等的近亲属的意见。被侵害的英雄烈士等人数众多、难以确定近亲属，或者直接征询近亲属意见确有困难的，也可以通过公告的方式征询英雄烈士等的近亲属的意见。

**Article 92** When handling a civil public interest litigation case involving infringement upon the names, portraits, reputations, honors, etc. of heroes and martyrs, the People's Procuratorate may directly seek the opinions of the near relatives of the heroes and martyrs. If the number of heroes and martyrs whose rights have been infringed is large, making it difficult to identify the near relatives, or if directly seeking the opinions of the near relatives is truly difficult, the opinions of the near relatives of the heroes and martyrs may also be sought by means of an announcement.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条  发布公告后，人民检察院应当对赔偿权利人启动生态环境损害赔偿程序情况、适格主体起诉情况、英雄烈士等的近亲属提起民事诉讼情况，以及社会公共利益受到损害的情况跟进调查，收集相关证据材料。

**Article 93** After issuing the announcement, the People's Procuratorate shall conduct follow-up investigations on the circumstance of the compensation right holder initiating the ecological environment damage compensation procedure, the circumstance of eligible subjects filing lawsuits, the circumstance of near relatives of heroes and martyrs initiating civil lawsuits, and the status of damage to the social public interest, and collect relevant evidence materials.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条  经过跟进调查，检察官应当制作《审查终结报告》，区分情况提出以下处理意见：  
（一）终结案件；  
（二）提起民事公益诉讼；  
（三）移送其他人民检察院处理。

**Article 94** After conducting follow-up investigation, the prosecutor shall prepare a "Report on Conclusion of Review" and, depending on the circumstances, propose one of the following handling opinions:  
(1) Closing the case;  
(2) Initiating a civil public interest litigation;  
(3) Transferring the case to another People's Procuratorate for handling.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条  经审查，人民检察院发现有[本规则第九十条](#art-90)规定情形之一的，应当终结案件。

**Article 95** After review, if the People's Procuratorate discovers that any of the circumstances specified in Article 90 of these Rules exists, it shall close the case.

#### 第三节 提起诉讼

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条  有下列情形之一，社会公共利益仍然处于受损害状态的，人民检察院应当提起民事公益诉讼：  
（一）生态环境损害赔偿权利人未启动生态环境损害赔偿程序，或者经过磋商未达成一致，赔偿权利人又不提起诉讼的；  
（二）没有适格主体，或者公告期满后适格主体不提起诉讼的；  
（三）英雄烈士等没有近亲属，或者近亲属不提起诉讼的。

**Article 96** In any of the following circumstances, if the social public interest remains in a state of damage, the People's Procuratorate shall initiate a civil public interest litigation:  
(1) The right holder of ecological environment damage compensation has not initiated the ecological environment damage compensation procedure, or after consultation no agreement is reached, and the compensation right holder does not file a lawsuit;  
(2) There is no eligible subject, or after the announcement period expires, the eligible subject does not file a lawsuit;  
(3) Heroes and martyrs have no near relatives, or the near relatives do not file a lawsuit.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条  人民检察院在刑事案件提起公诉时，对破坏生态环境和资源保护，食品药品安全领域侵害众多消费者合法权益，侵犯未成年人合法权益，侵害英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉等损害社会公共利益的违法行为，可以向人民法院提起刑事附带民事公益诉讼。

**Article 97** When initiating a public prosecution in a criminal case, the People's Procuratorate may, for illegal acts that damage the social public interest such as damaging the ecological environment and resource protection, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous consumers in the field of food and drug safety, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of minors, or infringing upon the names, portraits, reputations, honors, etc. of heroes and martyrs, initiate a criminal incidental civil public interest litigation with the People's Court.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条  人民检察院可以向人民法院提出要求被告停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、恢复原状、赔偿损失等诉讼请求。  
针对不同领域案件，还可以提出以下诉讼请求：  
（一）破坏生态环境和资源保护领域案件，可以提出要求被告以补植复绿、增殖放流、土地复垦等方式修复生态环境的诉讼请求，或者支付生态环境修复费用，赔偿生态环境受到损害至修复完成期间服务功能丧失造成的损失、生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失等诉讼请求，被告违反法律规定故意污染环境、破坏生态造成严重后果的，可以提出惩罚性赔偿等诉讼请求；  
（二）食品药品安全领域案件，可以提出要求被告召回并依法处置相关食品药品以及承担相关费用和惩罚性赔偿等诉讼请求；  
（三）英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉保护案件，可以提出要求被告消除影响、恢复名誉、赔礼道歉等诉讼请求。  
人民检察院为诉讼支出的鉴定评估、专家咨询等费用，可以在起诉时一并提出由被告承担的诉讼请求。

**Article 98** The People's Procuratorate may raise claims to the People's Court requiring the defendant to cease the infringement, remove the obstruction, eliminate the danger, restore the original condition, or compensate for losses.  
For cases in different fields, it may also raise the following claims:  
(1) For cases in the field of damaging the ecological environment and resource protection, it may raise claims requiring the defendant to restore the ecological environment by means such as replanting trees and restoring greenery, releasing fry and breeding, and land reclamation, or claims for payment of ecological environment restoration costs, compensation for losses caused by the loss of service functions during the period from the ecological environment damage to the completion of restoration, and compensation for losses caused by permanent damage to ecological environment functions, and if the defendant intentionally pollutes the environment or damages the ecology in violation of law, causing serious consequences, it may raise claims such as punitive damages;  
(2) For cases in the field of food and drug safety, it may raise claims requiring the defendant to recall and dispose of relevant food and drugs according to law and bear the relevant costs and punitive damages;  
(3) For cases involving the protection of the names, portraits, reputations, honors, etc. of heroes and martyrs, it may raise claims requiring the defendant to eliminate the impact, restore the reputation, and make an apology.  
The costs of expert evaluation and expert consultation expended by the People's Procuratorate for the litigation may also be claimed to be borne by the defendant at the time of filing the lawsuit.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条  民事公益诉讼案件可以依法在人民法院主持下进行调解。调解协议不得减免诉讼请求载明的民事责任，不得损害社会公共利益。  
诉讼请求全部实现的，人民检察院可以撤回起诉。人民检察院决定撤回起诉的，应当经检察长决定后制作《撤回起诉决定书》，并在三日内提交人民法院。

**Article 99** A civil public interest litigation case may be mediated under the auspices of the People's Court according to law. The mediation agreement shall not reduce or exempt the civil liability specified in the claims, and shall not harm the social public interest.  
If all the claims are realized, the People's Procuratorate may withdraw the prosecution. If the People's Procuratorate decides to withdraw the prosecution, it shall, after the decision is made by the chief procurator, prepare a "Decision on Withdrawal of Prosecution", and submit it to the People's Court within three days.

#### 第四节 支持起诉

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条  下列案件，人民检察院可以支持起诉：  
生态环境损害赔偿权利人提起的生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件；  
适格主体提起的民事公益诉讼案件；  
英雄烈士等的近亲属提起的维护英雄烈士等的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉的民事诉讼案件；  
（四）军人和因公牺牲军人、病故军人遗属提起的侵害军人荣誉、名誉和其他相关合法权益的民事诉讼案件；  
（五）其他依法可以支持起诉的公益诉讼案件。

**Article 100** The People's Procuratorate may support the prosecution in the following cases:  
(1) Cases of ecological environment damage compensation lawsuits initiated by the right holder of ecological environment damage compensation;  
(2) Cases of civil public interest litigation initiated by eligible subjects;  
(3) Cases of civil litigation initiated by near relatives of heroes and martyrs to protect the names, portraits, reputations, honors, etc. of heroes and martyrs;  
(4) Cases of civil litigation initiated by survivors of military personnel and military personnel who died in the line of duty or died of illness, concerning infringement upon the honor, reputation, and other related lawful rights and interests of military personnel;  
(5) Other public interest litigation cases that may support prosecution according to law.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条  人民检察院可以采取提供法律咨询、向人民法院提交支持起诉意见书、协助调查取证、出席法庭等方式支持起诉。

**Article 101** A People's Procuratorate may support the prosecution by means of providing legal consultation, submitting opinions in support of prosecution to the People's Court, assisting in investigation and evidence collection, appearing in court, etc.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条  人民检察院在向人民法院提交支持起诉意见书后，发现有以下不适合支持起诉情形的，可以撤回支持起诉：  
（一）原告无正当理由变更、撤回部分诉讼请求，致使社会公共利益不能得到有效保护的；  
（二）原告撤回起诉或者与被告达成和解协议，致使社会公共利益不能得到有效保护的；  
（三）原告请求被告承担的律师费以及为诉讼支出的其他费用过高，对社会公共利益保护产生明显不利影响的；  
（四）其他不适合支持起诉的情形。  
人民检察院撤回支持起诉的，应当制作《撤回支持起诉决定书》，在三日内提交人民法院，并发送原告。

**Article 102** After submitting the opinion in support of prosecution to the People's Court, if the People's Procuratorate finds that there are circumstances unsuitable for supporting prosecution as follows, it may withdraw the support for prosecution:  
(1) the plaintiff changes or withdraws part of the litigation claims without justifiable grounds, resulting in the social public interest not being effectively protected;  
(2) the plaintiff withdraws the action or reaches a settlement agreement with the defendant, resulting in the social public interest not being effectively protected;  
(3) the attorney's fees and other expenses incurred for litigation claimed by the plaintiff against the defendant are excessively high, causing obvious adverse effects on the protection of social public interest;  
(4) other circumstances unsuitable for supporting prosecution.  
Where the People's Procuratorate withdraws support for prosecution, it shall prepare a "Decision on Withdrawal of Support for Prosecution", submit it to the People's Court within three days, and send it to the plaintiff.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条  人民检察院撤回支持起诉后，认为适格主体提出的诉讼请求不足以保护社会公共利益，符合立案条件的，可以另行立案。

**Article 103** After withdrawing support for prosecution, if the People's Procuratorate considers that the litigation claims filed by the qualified subject are insufficient to protect the social public interest and the conditions for filing a case are satisfied, it may file a separate case.

### 第五章 其他规定

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条  办理公益诉讼案件的人民检察院对涉及法律适用、办案程序、司法政策等问题，可以依照有关规定向上级人民检察院请示。

**Article 104** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate may request instructions from the higher People's Procuratorate in accordance with relevant provisions on issues concerning legal application, case handling procedures, judicial policies, etc.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条  本规则所涉及的法律文书格式，由最高人民检察院统一制定。

**Article 105** The formats of legal documents involved in these Rules shall be uniformly formulated by the Supreme People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条  各级人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，应当依照有关规定及时归档。

**Article 106** People's Procuratorates at all levels handling public interest litigation cases shall timely archive in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条  人民检察院提起公益诉讼，不需要交纳诉讼费用。

**Article 107** A People's Procuratorate that initiates a public interest litigation shall not be required to pay litigation costs.

### 第六章 附 则

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条  军事检察院等专门人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，适用本规则和其他有关规定。

**Article 108** Special People's Procuratorates such as the Military Procuratorate shall apply these Rules and other relevant provisions when handling public interest litigation cases.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条  本规则所称检察官，包括检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员、检察员。  
本规则所称检察人员，包括检察官和检察辅助人员。

**Article 109** For the purposes of these Rules, "procurator" includes the chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators.  
For the purposes of these Rules, "procuratorial personnel" includes procurators and procuratorial assistants.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条  《中华人民共和国军人地位和权益保障法》《中华人民共和国安全生产法（2021修正）》等法律施行后，人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件的范围相应调整。

**Article 110** After the 'Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of the Status and Rights and Interests of Military Personnel' and the 'Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China (2021 Amendment)' and other laws come into force, the scope of public interest litigation cases handled by the People's Procuratorate shall be adjusted accordingly.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条  本规则未规定的其他事项，适用民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 111** For other matters not provided for in these Rules, the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the Administrative Procedure Law, and relevant judicial interpretations shall apply.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条  本规则自2021年7月1日起施行。
