### 最高人民法院关于情节严重的传销或者变相传销行为如何定性问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于情节严重的传销或者变相传销行为如何定性问题的批复  
  
（2001年3月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第1166次会议通过，自2001年4月18日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕11号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院粤高法〔2000〕101号《关于情节严重的传销和变相传销的行为是否构成非法经营罪问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对于 1998年 4月 18日国务院《关于禁止传销经营活动的通知》发布以后，仍然从事传销或者变相传销活动，扰乱市场秩序，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
  
实施上述犯罪，同时构成刑法规定的其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2001] No. 11  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your Court's Request for Instructions (Yue Gao Fa [2000] No. 101) on the Issue of Whether Serious Pyramid Selling and Disguised Pyramid Selling Activities Constitute the Crime of Illegal Business Operations has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
After the issuance of the Notice on Prohibiting Pyramid Selling Activities by the State Council on April 18, 1998, those who still engage in pyramid selling or disguised pyramid selling activities, disrupt market order, and the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225(4) of the Criminal Law.  
If the commission of the aforementioned crime also constitutes another crime provided for in the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.  
It is so replied.
