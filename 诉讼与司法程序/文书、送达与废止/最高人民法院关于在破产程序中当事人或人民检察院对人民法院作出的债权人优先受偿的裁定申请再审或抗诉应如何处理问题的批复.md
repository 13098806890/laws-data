### 最高人民法院关于在破产程序中当事人或人民检察院对人民法院作出的债权人优先受偿的裁定申请再审或抗诉应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于在破产程序中当事人或人民检察院对  
  
人民法院作出的债权人优先受偿的裁定  
  
申请再审或抗诉应如何处理问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕14号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1994〕119号请示收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
在破产程序中，债权人根据人民法院已发生法律效力的用抵押物偿还债权人本金及利息的判决书或调解书行使优先权时，受理破产案件的人民法院不能以任何方式改变已生效的判决书或调解书的内容，也不需要用裁定书加以认可。如果债权人据以行使优先权的生效法律文书确有错误，应由作出判决或调解的人民法院或其上级人民法院按照审判监督程序进行再审。如果审理破产案件的人民法院用裁定的方式变更了生效的法律文书的内容，人民法院应当依法予以纠正。但当事人不能对此裁定申请再审，亦不涉及人民检察院抗诉的问题，对于人民检察院坚持抗诉的，人民法院应通知不予受理。  
  
1996年8月13日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Handle Applications for Retrial or Protests Filed by Parties or the People's Procuratorate Against a People's Court's Ruling on Creditors' Priority of Repayment in Bankruptcy Proceedings  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 14  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your request, Chuan Gao Fa [1994] No. 119, has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
In bankruptcy proceedings, when a creditor exercises priority rights based on a judgment or mediation document that has taken legal effect and requires the use of mortgaged property to repay the creditor's principal and interest, the People's Court handling the bankruptcy case shall not alter the content of the effective judgment or mediation document in any manner, nor shall it need to recognize it by a ruling. If the effective legal instrument based on which the creditor exercises priority rights is indeed erroneous, the People's Court that rendered the judgment or mediation, or the People's Court at a higher level, shall conduct a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure. If the People's Court handling the bankruptcy case changes the content of an effective legal instrument by means of a ruling, the People's Court shall correct it according to law. However, a party may not apply for retrial against such a ruling, and the issue of protest by the People's Procuratorate shall not be involved. If the People's Procuratorate insists on filing a protest, the People's Court shall notify it that the protest will not be accepted.  
August 13, 1996
