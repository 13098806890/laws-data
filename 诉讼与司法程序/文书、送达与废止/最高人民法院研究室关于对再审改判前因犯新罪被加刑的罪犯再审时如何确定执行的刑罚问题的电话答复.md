<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院研究室  
关于对再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的罪犯  
再审时如何确定执行的刑罚问题的电话答复  
（1989年5月24日）  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
你院鄂法研〔1988〕33号《关于对再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的罪犯再审时应如何确定执行的刑罚问题的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
原则上同意你院意见，即对于再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的罪犯，在对其前罪再审时，应当将罪犯犯新罪时的判决中关于前罪与新罪并罚的内容撤销，并把经再审改判后的前罪没有执行完的刑罚和新罪已判处的刑罚，按照[刑法第六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-66)的规定依法数罪并罚。关于原前罪与新罪并罚的判决由哪个法院撤销，应视具体情况确定：如果再审法院是对新罪作出判决的法院的上级法院，或者是对新罪作出判决的同一法院，可以由再审法院撤销；否则，应由对新罪作出判决的法院撤销。对于前罪经再审改判为无罪或者免予刑事处分的，其已执行的刑期可以折抵新罪的刑期。执行本答复中遇有新的情况或问题，请及时报告我们。  
附：  
湖北省高级人民法院  
关于对再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的罪犯  
再审时应如何确定执行的刑罚问题的请示报告  
1988年9月20日      鄂法研〔1988〕33号  
最高人民法院：  
我省法院在审判实践中，经常碰到对再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的罪犯在再审时难以确定其执行的刑罚的问题。我们认为，对再审改判前因犯新罪被加刑的，再审时应按[刑法第六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-66)的规定，将前罪经再审改判而未执行完的刑罚同后罪所判处的刑罚合并，依照数罪并罚的原则，决定执行的刑罚。  
但上述做法中存在两个问题：一是对罪犯犯新罪时的判决中关于新罪与前罪并罚的内容是否撤销？同哪几个法院撤销？二是对前罪再审改判无罪或者免予刑事处分的，其已执行的刑期是否应当折抵新罪的刑期？我们认为，对同一犯罪事实不能有两个相互矛盾的发生法律效力的判决并立，再审改判时，对罪犯犯新罪进行判处的判决中关于新罪与前罪并罚的内容应当撤销。如果再审法院与对新罪作出判决的是同一法院，或者再审法院是对新罪作出判决法院的上级法院，上述内容由再审法院撤销；若不是，则由对新罪作出判决的法院撤销为宜，前罪经再审改判为无罪或免予刑事处分的，其已执行的刑期与新罪判处的刑罚虽然所依据的不是同一事实，但考虑到罪犯是不应受到的限制自由，因而将原已执行的刑期折抵新罪的刑期比较合理。  
当否，请指示。

**Article 1** Research Office of the Supreme People's Court  
Telephone Reply on How to Determine the Penalty to Be Executed During Retrial for a Criminal Who Was Given an Additional Punishment for a New Crime Before the Original Sentence Was Changed on Retrial  
(May 24, 1989)  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
Your Request for Instructions No. E Fa Yan [1988] 33, titled "Request for Instructions on How to Determine the Penalty to Be Executed During Retrial for a Criminal Who Was Given an Additional Punishment for a New Crime Before the Original Sentence Was Changed on Retrial," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
In principle, we agree with your opinion. That is, for a criminal who was given an additional punishment for a new crime before the original sentence was changed on retrial, when conducting a retrial of the previous crime, the court shall revoke the part of the judgment concerning the concurrent punishment of the previous crime and the new crime when the criminal committed the new crime, and shall impose a combined punishment for multiple crimes in accordance with the provisions of Article 66 of the Criminal Law, by merging the unexecuted portion of the sentence for the previous crime after retrial and the sentence already imposed for the new crime. As to which court shall revoke the original judgment concerning the concurrent punishment of the previous crime and the new crime, it shall be determined according to the specific circumstances: if the retrial court is a superior court of the court that rendered the judgment on the new crime, or is the same court that rendered the judgment on the new crime, the retrial court may revoke it; otherwise, it shall be revoked by the court that rendered the judgment on the new crime. If the previous crime is changed on retrial to a finding of innocence or exemption from criminal punishment, the term already executed may be deducted from the term of the new crime. If any new circumstances or problems arise in implementing this reply, please promptly report them to us.  
Appendix:  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province  
Request for Instructions on How to Determine the Penalty to Be Executed During Retrial for a Criminal Who Was Given an Additional Punishment for a New Crime Before the Original Sentence Was Changed on Retrial  
September 20, 1988 E Fa Yan [1988] No. 33  
Supreme People's Court:  
In the adjudication practice of our province's courts, we often encounter difficulties in determining the penalty to be executed during retrial for a criminal who was given an additional punishment for a new crime before the original sentence was changed on retrial. We opine that for a criminal who was given an additional punishment for a new crime before the original sentence was changed on retrial, during the retrial, in accordance with the provisions of Article 66 of the Criminal Law, the unexecuted portion of the sentence for the previous crime after retrial shall be merged with the sentence imposed for the subsequent crime, and the penalty to be executed shall be determined in accordance with the principle of combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
However, there are two issues in the above approach: First, shall the part of the judgment concerning the concurrent punishment of the new crime and the previous crime when the criminal committed the new crime be revoked? Which courts shall revoke it? Second, if the previous crime is changed on retrial to a finding of innocence or exemption from criminal punishment, shall the term already executed be deducted from the term of the new crime? We opine that two mutually contradictory judgments with legal effect cannot coexist for the same criminal facts. When a retrial changes the sentence, the part of the judgment imposing punishment on the criminal for the new crime that concerns the concurrent punishment of the new crime and the previous crime shall be revoked. If the retrial court is the same court that rendered the judgment on the new crime, or if the retrial court is a superior court of the court that rendered the judgment on the new crime, the above part shall be revoked by the retrial court; otherwise, it shall be more appropriate for the court that rendered the judgment on the new crime to revoke it. If the previous crime is changed on retrial to a finding of innocence or exemption from criminal punishment, although the term already executed and the punishment imposed for the new crime are not based on the same facts, considering that the criminal suffered restrictions on liberty that should not have been imposed, it is reasonable to deduct the term already executed from the term of the new crime.  
Please advise whether this approach is appropriate.
