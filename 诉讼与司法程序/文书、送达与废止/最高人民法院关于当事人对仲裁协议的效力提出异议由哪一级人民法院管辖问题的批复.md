### 最高人民法院关于当事人对仲裁协议的效力提出异议由哪一级人民法院管辖问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于当事人对仲裁协议的效力提出异议  
  
由哪一级人民法院管辖问题的批复  
  
（2000年7月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1126次会议通过，自2000年8月12日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕25号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鲁高法〔1998〕144号《关于当事人对仲裁协议的效力有异议应该向何人民法院请求作出裁定以及人民法院如何作出裁定的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
关于请示的第一个问题，当事人协议选择国内仲裁机构仲裁后，一方对仲裁协议的效力有异议请求人民法院作出裁定的，由该仲裁委员会所在地的中级人民法院管辖。当事人对仲裁委员会没有约定或者约定不明的，由被告所在地的中级人民法院管辖。  
  
关于请示的第二个问题，我院法释〔1998〕27号批复已有明确规定，在此不再答复。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2000] No. 25  
To the Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your Request for Instructions No. Lu Gao Fa [1998] No. 144, titled "Request Concerning Which People's Court a Party Shall Petition for a Ruling When Disputing the Validity of an Arbitration Agreement and How the People's Court Shall Make Such a Ruling", has been received. After study, our reply is as follows:  
Regarding the first question in the request: Where the parties have agreed to submit to arbitration by a domestic arbitration institution and one party disputes the validity of the arbitration agreement and requests the People's Court to make a ruling, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the arbitration commission is located. Where the parties have not agreed on an arbitration commission or the agreement is unclear, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the defendant is domiciled.  
Regarding the second question in the request, the Reply No. 27 [1998] of this Court has already provided clear provisions, and therefore no further reply is given herein.  
This is our reply.
