# 最高人民法院关于人民法院合议庭工作的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕25号  
**公布日期**：2002-07-30  
**生效日期**：2002-08-17

> （2002年7月30日最高人民法院审判委员会第1234次会议通过，自2002年8月17日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕25号 为了进一步规范合议庭的工作程序，充分发挥合议庭的职能作用，根据《中华人民共和国法院组织法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的有关规定，结合人民法院审判工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条 人民法院实行合议制审判第一审案件，由法官或者由法官和人民陪审员组成合议庭进行；人民法院实行合议制审判第二审案件和其他应当组成合议庭审判的案件，由法官组成合议庭进行。 人民陪审员在人民法院执行职务期间，除不能担任审判长外，同法官有同等的权利义务。 第二条 合议庭的审判长由符合审判长任职条件的法官担任。 院长或者庭长参加合议庭审判案件的时候，自己担任审判长。 第三条 合议庭组成人员确定后，除因回避或者其他特殊情况，不能继续参加案件审理的之外，不得在案件审理过程中更换。更换合议庭成员，应当报请院长或者庭长决定。合议庭成员的更换情况应当及时通知诉讼当事人。 第四条 合议庭的审判活动由审判长主持，全体成员平等参与案件的审理、评议、裁判，共同对案件认定事实和适用法律负责。 第五条 合议庭承担下列职责： （一）根据当事人的申请或者案件的具体情况，可以作出财产保全、证据保全、先予执行等裁定； （二）确定案件委托评估、委托鉴定等事项； （三）依法开庭审理第一审、第二审和再审案件； （四）评议案件； （五）提请院长决定将案件提交审判委员会讨论决定；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 人民法院实行合议制审判第一审案件，由法官或者由法官和人民陪审员组成合议庭进行；人民法院实行合议制审判第二审案件和其他应当组成合议庭审判的案件，由法官组成合议庭进行。  
人民陪审员在人民法院执行职务期间，除不能担任审判长外，同法官有同等的权利义务。

**Article 1** When a People's Court adjudicates a case of first instance under the collegial system, a collegial panel composed of judges or of judges and people's assessors shall conduct the adjudication; when a People's Court adjudicates a case of second instance or other cases that shall be adjudicated by a collegial panel under the collegial system, a collegial panel composed of judges shall conduct the adjudication.  
While performing their duties in a People's Court, people's assessors shall have the same rights and obligations as judges, except that they may not serve as the presiding judge.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 合议庭的审判长由符合审判长任职条件的法官担任。  
院长或者庭长参加合议庭审判案件的时候，自己担任审判长。

**Article 2** The presiding judge of a collegial panel shall be a judge who meets the qualifications for serving as presiding judge.  
When the president of the court or the chief judge of a division participates in the adjudication of a case as a member of the collegial panel, such president or chief judge shall serve as the presiding judge.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 合议庭组成人员确定后，除因回避或者其他特殊情况，不能继续参加案件审理的之外，不得在案件审理过程中更换。更换合议庭成员，应当报请院长或者庭长决定。合议庭成员的更换情况应当及时通知诉讼当事人。

**Article 3** After the composition of a collegial panel is determined, the members thereof shall not be replaced during the course of trial, except where a member is unable to continue participating in the trial due to withdrawal or other special circumstances. Any replacement of a member of the collegial panel shall be reported to the president of the court or the chief judge of a division for decision. The replacement of a member of the collegial panel shall be promptly notified to the litigant parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 合议庭的审判活动由审判长主持，全体成员平等参与案件的审理、评议、裁判，共同对案件认定事实和适用法律负责。

**Article 4** The adjudicative activities of a collegial panel shall be presided over by the presiding judge, and all members shall participate equally in the trial, deliberation and adjudication of the case, and shall jointly be responsible for the determination of facts and application of law in the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 合议庭承担下列职责：  
（一）根据当事人的申请或者案件的具体情况，可以作出财产保全、证据保全、先予执行等裁定；  
（二）确定案件委托评估、委托鉴定等事项；  
（三）依法开庭审理第一审、第二审和再审案件；  
（四）评议案件；  
（五）提请院长决定将案件提交审判委员会讨论决定；  
（六）按照权限对案件及其有关程序性事项作出裁判或者提出裁判意见；  
（七）制作裁判文书；  
（八）执行审判委员会决定；  
（九）办理有关审判的其他事项。

**Article 5** A collegial panel shall undertake the following duties:  
(1) It may render rulings on property preservation, preservation of evidence, advance execution, etc., upon application of a party or according to the specific circumstances of the case;  
(2) It shall determine matters such as entrusting assessment and entrusting expert evaluation for the case;  
(3) It shall hold hearings in accordance with law for cases of first instance, second instance, and retrial;  
(4) It shall deliberate on the case;  
(5) It shall propose to the president of the court to decide whether to submit the case to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision;  
(6) It shall render adjudication or provide adjudicative opinions on the case and related procedural matters within its authority;  
(7) It shall draft adjudicative documents;  
(8) It shall implement the decisions of the adjudication committee; and  
(9) It shall handle other matters related to adjudication.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 审判长履行下列职责：  
（一）指导和安排审判辅助人员做好庭前调解、庭前准备及其他审判业务辅助性工作；  
（二）确定案件审理方案、庭审提纲、协调合议庭成员的庭审分工以及做好其他必要的庭审准备工作；  
（三）主持庭审活动；  
（四）主持合议庭对案件进行评议；  
（五）依照有关规定，提请院长决定将案件提交审判委员会讨论决定；  
（六）制作裁判文书，审核合议庭其他成员制作的裁判文书；  
（七）依照规定权限签发法律文书；  
（八）根据院长或者庭长的建议主持合议庭对案件复议；  
（九）对合议庭遵守案件审理期限制度的情况负责；  
（十）办理有关审判的其他事项。

**Article 6** The presiding judge shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Direct and arrange for judicial assistants to properly carry out pre-trial mediation, pre-trial preparation, and other auxiliary work related to adjudication;  
(2) Determine the trial plan and outline for the case, coordinate the division of trial duties among members of the collegial panel, and undertake other necessary trial preparations;  
(3) Preside over the court session;  
(4) Preside over the collegial panel's deliberation on the case;  
(5) In accordance with relevant provisions, propose to the president of the court to decide whether to submit the case to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision;  
(6) Draft adjudicative documents and review the adjudicative documents drafted by other members of the collegial panel;  
(7) Issue legal documents within the prescribed authority;  
(8) Preside over the reconsideration of the case by the collegial panel upon the suggestion of the president of the court or the chief judge of a division;  
(9) Be responsible for the collegial panel's compliance with the system of case trial time limits; and  
(10) Handle other matters related to adjudication.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 合议庭接受案件后，应当根据有关规定确定案件承办法官，或者由审判长指定案件承办法官。

**Article 7** After accepting a case, the collegial panel shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, designate the judge responsible for the case, or the presiding judge shall designate such judge.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 在案件开庭审理过程中，合议庭成员必须认真履行法定职责，遵守《中华人民共和国法官职业道德基本准则》中有关司法礼仪的要求。

**Article 8** During the trial of a case, members of the collegial panel must conscientiously perform their statutory duties and comply with the requirements concerning judicial etiquette set forth in the Basic Standards of Professional Ethics for Judges of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 合议庭评议案件应当在庭审结束后五个工作日内进行。

**Article 9** The collegial panel shall deliberate on a case within five working days after the conclusion of the court session.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 合议庭评议案件时，先由承办法官对认定案件事实、证据是否确实、充分以及适用法律等发表意见，审判长最后发表意见；审判长作为承办法官的，由审判长最后发表意见。对案件的裁判结果进行评议时，由审判长最后发表意见。审判长应当根据评议情况总结合议庭评议的结论性意见。  
合议庭成员进行评议的时候，应当认真负责，充分陈述意见，独立行使表决权，不得拒绝陈述意见或者仅作同意与否的简单表态。同意他人意见的，也应当提出事实根据和法律依据，进行分析论证。  
合议庭成员对评议结果的表决，以口头表决的形式进行。

**Article 10** When the collegial panel deliberates on a case, the judge responsible for the case shall first give an opinion on the determination of facts, whether the evidence is truthful and sufficient, and the application of law, and the presiding judge shall give the opinion last; where the presiding judge is the judge responsible for the case, the presiding judge shall give the opinion last. When deliberating on the adjudicative result of the case, the presiding judge shall give the opinion last. The presiding judge shall, based on the deliberation, summarize the collegial panel's conclusive opinions.  
When members of the collegial panel deliberate, they shall be conscientious and responsible, fully state their opinions, exercise their voting rights independently, and shall not refuse to state their opinions or merely indicate approval or disapproval with simple expressions. Those who agree with the opinions of others shall also state the factual basis and legal basis and conduct analysis and argumentation.  
The voting by members of the collegial panel on the deliberation result shall be conducted by oral vote.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 合议庭进行评议的时候，如果意见分歧，应当按多数人的意见作出决定，但是少数人的意见应当写入笔录。  
评议笔录由书记员制作，由合议庭的组成人员签名。

**Article 11** When the collegial panel deliberates, if opinions diverge, the decision shall be made in accordance with the opinion of the majority, but the opinions of the minority shall be recorded in the minutes.  
The minutes of the deliberation shall be prepared by the court clerk and signed by the members of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 合议庭应当依照规定的权限，及时对评议意见一致或者形成多数意见的案件直接作出判决或者裁定。但是对于下列案件，合议庭应当提请院长决定提交审判委员会讨论决定：  
（一）拟判处死刑的；  
（二）疑难、复杂、重大或者新类型的案件，合议庭认为有必要提交审判委员会讨论决定的；  
（三）合议庭在适用法律方面有重大意见分歧的；  
（四）合议庭认为需要提请审判委员会讨论决定的其他案件，或者本院审判委员会确定的应当由审判委员会讨论决定的案件。

**Article 12** The collegial panel shall, in accordance with its prescribed authority, promptly render a judgment or ruling directly on cases where the deliberation opinions are unanimous or a majority opinion is formed. However, for the following cases, the collegial panel shall propose to the president of the court to decide to submit the case to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision:  
(1) Cases where a death penalty is to be imposed;  
(2) Cases that are difficult, complex, significant, or of a new type, and the collegial panel deems it necessary to submit them to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision;  
(3) Cases where there are significant differences of opinion among the collegial panel on the application of law;  
(4) Other cases that the collegial panel deems necessary to submit to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision, or cases that the adjudication committee of the People's Court has determined shall be discussed and decided by the adjudication committee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 合议庭对审判委员会的决定有异议，可以提请院长决定提交审判委员会复议一次。

**Article 13** If the collegial panel has objections to a decision of the adjudication committee, it may propose to the president of the court to decide to submit the case to the adjudication committee for one reconsideration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 合议庭一般应当在作出评议结论或者审判委员会作出决定后的五个工作日内制作出裁判文书。

**Article 14** The collegial panel shall generally draft the adjudicative document within five working days after making the deliberation conclusion or after the adjudication committee makes its decision.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 裁判文书一般由审判长或者承办法官制作。但是审判长或者承办法官的评议意见与合议庭评议结论或者审判委员会的决定有明显分歧的，也可以由其他合议庭成员制作裁判文书。  
对制作的裁判文书，合议庭成员应当共同审核，确认无误后签名。

**Article 15** An adjudicative document shall generally be drafted by the presiding judge or the judge responsible for the case. However, if the deliberation opinion of the presiding judge or the judge responsible for the case is clearly divergent from the collegial panel's deliberation conclusion or the decision of the adjudication committee, another member of the collegial panel may draft the adjudicative document.  
Members of the collegial panel shall jointly review the drafted adjudicative document and sign it after confirming its accuracy.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 院长、庭长可以对合议庭的评议意见和制作的裁判文书进行审核，但是不得改变合议庭的评议结论。

**Article 16** The president of the court or the chief judge of a division may review the deliberation opinions of the collegial panel and the drafted adjudicative documents, but shall not alter the deliberation conclusion of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 院长、庭长在审核合议庭的评议意见和裁判文书过程中，对评议结论有异议的，可以建议合议庭复议，同时应当对要求复议的问题及理由提出书面意见。  
合议庭复议后，庭长仍有异议的，可以将案件提请院长审核，院长可以提交审判委员会讨论决定。

**Article 17** If, during the review of the collegial panel's deliberation opinions and adjudicative documents, the president of the court or the chief judge of a division has objections to the deliberation conclusion, such president or chief judge may propose that the collegial panel reconsider the matter, and shall at the same time submit a written opinion on the issues and reasons for requiring reconsideration.  
After the collegial panel's reconsideration, if the chief judge of the division still has objections, the chief judge may submit the case to the president of the court for review, and the president of the court may submit the case to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 合议庭应当严格执行案件审理期限的有关规定。遇有特殊情况需要延长审理期限的，应当在审限届满前按规定的时限报请审批。

**Article 18** The collegial panel shall strictly implement the relevant provisions on case trial time limits. Where there are special circumstances requiring an extension of the trial time limit, a report for approval shall be submitted within the prescribed time limit before the expiration of the trial time limit.
