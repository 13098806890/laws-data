### 最高人民法院关于人民法院审理离婚案件处理子女抚养问题的若干具体意见

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院审理离婚案件  
  
处理子女抚养问题的若干具体意见  
  
法发〔1993〕30号  
  
人民法院审理离婚案件，对子女抚养问题，应当依照《中华人民共和国婚姻法》第二十九条、第三十条及有关法律规定，从有利于子女身心健康，保障子女的合法权益出发，结合父母双方的抚养能力和抚养条件等具体情况妥善解决。根据上述原则，结合审判实践，提出如下具体意见：  
  
1、两周岁以下的子女，一般随母方生活。母方有下列情形之一的，可随父方生活：  
  
（1）患有久治不愈的传染性疾病或其他严重疾病，子女不宜与其共同生活的；  
  
（2）有抚养条件不尽抚养义务，而父方要求子女随其生活的；  
  
（3）因其他原因，子女确无法随母方生活的。  
  
2、父母双方协议两周岁以下子女随父方生活，并对子女健康成长无不利影响的，可予准许。  
  
3、对两周岁以上未成年的子女，父方和母方均要求随其生活，一方有下列情形之一的，可予优先考虑：  
  
（1）已做绝育手术或因其他原因丧失生育能力的；  
  
（2）子女随其生活时间较长，改变生活环境对子女健康成长明显不利的；  
  
（3）无其他子女，而另一方有其他子女的；  
  
（4）子女随其生活，对子女成长有利，而另一方患有久治不愈的传染性疾病或其他严重疾病，或者有其他不利于子女身心健康的情形，不宜与子女共同生活的。  
  
4、父方与母方抚养子女的条件基本相同，双方均要求子女与其共同生活，但子女单独随祖父母或外祖父母共同生活多年，且祖父母或外祖父母要求并且有能力帮助子女照顾孙子女或外孙子女的，可作为子女随父或母生活的优先条件予以考虑。  
  
5、父母双方对十周岁以上的未成年子女随父或随母生活发生争执的，应考虑该子女的意见。  
  
6、在有利于保护子女利益的前提下，父母双方协议轮流抚养子女的，可予准许。  
  
7、子女抚育费的数额，可根据子女的实际需要、父母双方的负担能力和当地的实际生活水平确定。  
  
有固定收入的，抚育费一般可按其月总收入的百分之二十至三十的比例给付。负担两个以上子女抚育费的，比例可适当提高，但一般不得超过月总收入的百分之五十。  
  
无固定收入的，抚育费的数额可依据当年总收入或同行业平均收入，参照上述比例确定。有特殊情况的，可适当提高或降低上述比例。  
  
8、抚育费应定期给付，有条件的可一次性给付。  
  
9、对一方无经济收入或者下落不明的，可用其财物折抵子女抚育费。  
  
10、父母双方可以协议了女随一方生活并由抚养方负担子女全部抚育费。但经查实，抚养方的抚养能力明显不能保障子女所需费用，影响子女健康成长的，不予准许。  
  
11、抚育费的给付期限，一般至子女十八周岁为止。  
  
十六周岁以上不满十八周岁，以其劳动收入为主要生活来源，并能维持当地一般生活水平的，父母对停止给付抚育费。  
  
12、尚未独立生活的成年子女有下列情形之一，父母又有给付能力的，仍应负担必要的抚育费：  
  
（1）丧失劳动能力或虽未完全丧失劳动能力，但其收入不足以维持生活的；  
  
（2）尚在校就读的；  
  
（3）确无独立生活能力和条件的。  
  
13、生父与继母或生母与继父离婚时，对曾受其抚养教育的继子女，继父或继母不同意继续抚养的，仍应由生父母抚养。  
  
14、《中华人民共和国收养法》施行前，夫或妻一方收养的子女，对方未表示反对，并与该子女形成事实收养关系的，离婚后，应由双方负担子女的抚育费；夫或妻一方收养的子女，对方始终反对的，离婚后，应由收养方抚养该子女。  
  
15、离婚后，一方要求变更子女抚养关系的，或者子女要求增加抚育费的，应另行起诉。  
  
16、一方要求变更子女抚养关系有下列情形之一的，应予支持。  
  
（1）与子女共同生活的一方因患严重疾病或因伤残无力继续抚养子女的；  
  
（2）与子女共同生活的一方不尽抚养义务或有虐待子女行为，或其与子女共同生活对子女身心健康确有不利影响的；  
  
（3）十周岁以上未成年子女，愿随另一方生活，该方又有抚养能力的；  
  
（4）有其他正当理由需要变更的。  
  
17、父母双方协议变更子女抚养关系的，应予准许。  
  
18、子女要求增加抚育费有下列情形之一，父或母有给付能力的，应予支持。  
  
（1）原定抚育费数额不足以维持当地实际生活水平的；  
  
（2）因子女患病、上学，实际需要已超过原定数额的；  
  
（3）有其他正当理由应当增加的。  
  
19、父母不得因子女变更姓氏而拒付子女抚育费。  
  
20、在离婚诉讼期间，双方均拒绝抚养子女的，可先行裁定暂由一方抚养。  
  
21、对拒不履行或妨害他人履行生效判决、裁定、调解中有关子女抚养义务的当事人或者其他人，人民法院可依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的规定采取强制措施。  
  
1993年11月3日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Several Specific Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Handling of Child Custody in Divorce Cases  
Fa Fa [1993] No. 30  
When a People's Court hears a divorce case, it shall handle the issue of child custody in accordance with Articles 29 and 30 of the Marriage Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant legal provisions, starting from the perspective of benefiting the child's physical and mental health, protecting the child's lawful rights and interests, and taking into full consideration the specific circumstances such as the custody capacities and conditions of both parents. Based on the above principles and in light of adjudication practice, the following specific opinions are put forward:  
1. A child under the age of two shall generally live with the mother. Under any of the following circumstances, the child may live with the father:  
(1) The mother suffers from a protracted infectious disease or other serious disease that makes it inappropriate for the child to live with her;  
(2) The mother has the custody conditions but fails to perform the custody obligation, and the father demands the child to live with him;  
(3) Due to other reasons, the child is truly unable to live with the mother.  
2. Where both parents agree that a child under the age of two shall live with the father, and such arrangement has no adverse impact on the child's healthy growth, it may be permitted.  
3. For a minor child aged two or above, if both the father and the mother demand the child to live with them, and one of the following circumstances exists with one party, that party may be given priority consideration:  
(1) The party has undergone sterilization surgery or has lost the ability to have children due to other reasons;  
(2) The child has lived with that party for a relatively long time, and changing the living environment would be clearly detrimental to the child's healthy growth;  
(3) The party has no other child, while the other party has other children;  
(4) Living with that party is beneficial to the child's growth, while the other party suffers from a protracted infectious disease or other serious disease, or has other circumstances detrimental to the child's physical and mental health, making it inappropriate for the child to live with that other party.  
4. Where the conditions of the father and the mother for raising the child are basically the same, and both demand the child to live with them, but the child has lived alone with the paternal or maternal grandparents for many years, and the grandparents demand and are capable of helping the child or grandchildren, this may be considered as a priority condition for the child to live with the father or mother.  
5. Where the parents dispute over whether a minor child aged ten or above shall live with the father or the mother, the child's opinion shall be taken into consideration.  
6. On the premise of being conducive to protecting the child's interests, if both parents agree to take turns raising the child, it may be permitted.  
7. The amount of child support may be determined based on the child's actual needs, the financial capacities of both parents, and the local actual living standard.  
For parties with a fixed income, child support shall generally be paid at a rate of 20% to 30% of their monthly total income. If the party bears the support for two or more children, the proportion may be appropriately increased, but shall generally not exceed 50% of the monthly total income.  
For parties without a fixed income, the amount of child support may be determined by reference to the above proportion based on the total annual income or the average income of the same industry. Under special circumstances, the above proportion may be appropriately increased or decreased.  
8. Child support shall be paid periodically; where conditions permit, it may be paid in a lump sum.  
9. Where one party has no economic income or is whereabouts unknown, the child support may be offset by the party's property.  
10. Both parents may agree that the child shall live with one party and that the custodial party shall bear all child support. However, if it is verified that the custodial party's custody capacity is clearly insufficient to guarantee the expenses needed for the child, affecting the child's healthy growth, such agreement shall not be permitted.  
11. The term for payment of child support shall generally last until the child reaches the age of 18.  
For a child between 16 and under 18 years old, if the child's labor income is the main source of livelihood and can maintain the local general living standard, the parents may stop paying child support.  
12. Where an adult child who has not yet become independent has one of the following circumstances, and the parents still have the ability to pay, the parents shall still bear the necessary child support:  
(1) The child has lost the ability to work, or although not completely lost the ability to work, the child's income is insufficient to maintain living;  
(2) The child is still studying at school;  
(3) The child truly lacks independent living ability and conditions.  
13. When the biological father and stepmother, or the biological mother and stepfather divorce, if the stepparent who once provided custody and education to the stepchild does not agree to continue custody, the biological parents shall still raise the child.  
14. For a child adopted by one spouse before the Adoption Law of the People's Republic of China came into force, if the other spouse did not object and formed a de facto adoptive relationship with the child, after divorce, both spouses shall bear the child support; if the other spouse consistently objected, after divorce, the adopting spouse shall raise the child.  
15. After divorce, if one party demands a change in the child custody arrangement, or if the child demands an increase in child support, a separate prosecution shall be filed.  
16. Where one party demands a change in the child custody arrangement under any of the following circumstances, the demand shall be supported:  
(1) The party who lives with the child is unable to continue raising the child due to serious illness or disability;  
(2) The party who lives with the child fails to perform custody obligations or abuses the child, or living with that party is indeed detrimental to the child's physical and mental health;  
(3) A minor child aged ten or above is willing to live with the other party, and that other party has the capacity to raise the child;  
(4) There are other justifiable reasons necessitating a change.  
17. Where both parents agree to change the child custody arrangement, such change shall be permitted.  
18. Where the child demands an increase in child support under any of the following circumstances, and the father or mother has the ability to pay, the demand shall be supported:  
(1) The originally determined amount of child support is insufficient to maintain the local actual living standard;  
(2) Due to the child's illness or schooling, the actual needs have exceeded the originally determined amount;  
(3) There are other justifiable reasons for an increase.  
19. Parents shall not refuse to pay child support because the child changes the surname.  
20. During the divorce proceedings, if both parties refuse to raise the child, a ruling may be made in advance that one party shall temporarily raise the child.  
21. Where a party or any other person refuses to perform or obstructs the performance of obligations concerning child custody in effective judgments, rulings, or mediation statements, the People's Court may take compulsory measures in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
November 3, 1993
