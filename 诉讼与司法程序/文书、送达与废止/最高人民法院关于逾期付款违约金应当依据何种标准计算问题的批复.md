### 最高人民法院关于逾期付款违约金应当依据何种标准计算问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于逾期付款违约金应当依据何种标准计算问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕7号  
  
黑龙江省高级人民法院：  
  
你院黑高法〔1996〕16号请示收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
本院曾于 1994年3月12日以法函〔1994〕10号复函你院：经济合同法及其有关条例中规定的逾期付款的违约金，应按逾期付款金额每日万分之三计算。该复函内容与实际情况现已不相适应。参照中国人民银行银发〔1994〕256号通知印发的修订后的《异地托收承付结算办法》和《异地托收承付结算会计核算手续》的有关规定，逾期付款违约金，应当按照逾期付款金额每日万分之五计算。但本批复下发前发生的逾期付款行为，其违约金的计算标准，则仍应按照本院〔1994〕10号复函处理。  
  
1996年5月16日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of the Standard for Calculating Liquidated Damages for Overdue Payment  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 7  
Higher People's Court of Heilongjiang Province:  
We have received your request for instructions, Hei Gao Fa [1996] No. 16. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
This Court previously, in a reply letter to your court dated March 12, 1994 (Fa Han [1994] No. 10), stated that the liquidated damages for overdue payment as provided in the Economic Contract Law and its relevant regulations shall be calculated at the rate of 3/10,000 per day of the overdue payment amount. However, the content of that reply letter is no longer adapted to the actual circumstances. With reference to the relevant provisions of the revised Measures for the Settlement of Intercity Collection and Acceptance and the Accounting Handling Procedures for Intercity Collection and Acceptance Settlement, issued by the People's Bank of China under the notice Yin Fa [1994] No. 256, the liquidated damages for overdue payment shall be calculated at the rate of 5/10,000 per day of the overdue payment amount. However, for overdue payment conduct that occurred before the issuance of this reply, the calculation standard for liquidated damages shall still be handled in accordance with this Court's reply letter Fa Han [1994] No. 10.  
May 16, 1996
