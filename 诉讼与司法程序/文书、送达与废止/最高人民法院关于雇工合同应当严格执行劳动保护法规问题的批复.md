### 最高人民法院关于雇工合同应当严格执行劳动保护法规问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于雇工合同应当严格执行劳动保护法规问题的批复  
  
（88）民他字第1号  
  
天津市高级人民法院：  
  
你院〔1987〕第60号请示报告收悉。据报告称，你市塘沽区张学珍、徐广秋开办的新村青年服务站，于1985年6月招雇张国胜（男，21岁）为临时工，招工登记表中注明“工伤概不负责任”的内容。次年11月17日，该站在天津碱厂拆除旧厂房时，因房梁断落，造成张国胜左踝关节挫伤，引起局部组织感染坏死，导致因脓毒性败血症而死亡。张国胜生前为治伤用去医疗费14151.15元。为此，张国胜的父母张连起、焦容兰向雇主张学珍等索赔。张等则以“工伤概不负责任”为由拒绝承担民事责任。张连起、焦容兰遂向法院起诉。  
  
经研究认为，对劳动者实行劳动保护，在我国宪法中已有明文规定，这是劳动者所享有的权利，受国家法律保护，任何个人和组织都不得任意侵犯。张学珍、徐广秋身为雇主，对雇员理应依法给予劳动保护，但他们却在招工登记表中注明“工伤概不负责任”。这是违反宪法和有关劳动保护法规的，也严重违反了社会主义公德，对这种行为应认定无效。  
  
此复  
  
1988年10月14日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue That Labor Protection Regulations Shall Be Strictly Enforced in Employment Contracts  
(88) Min Ta Zi No. 1  
Higher People's Court of Tianjin:  
We have received your Request for Instructions No. 60 [1987]. According to the report, the Xincun Youth Service Station established by Zhang Xuezhen and Xu Guangqiu in Tanggu District, Tianjin, hired Zhang Guosheng (male, 21 years old) as a temporary worker in June 1985, with the entry form stating "not responsible for work-related injuries." On November 17 of the following year, while dismantling an old factory building at Tianjin Soda Plant, a roof beam collapsed, causing Zhang Guosheng to suffer a contusion of the left ankle joint, which led to local tissue infection and necrosis, resulting in death from septicopyemia. Prior to his death, Zhang Guosheng incurred medical expenses of 14,151.15 yuan for treatment. Consequently, Zhang Guosheng's parents, Zhang Lianqi and Jiao Ronglan, claimed damages from the employers, Zhang Xuezhen and others. The latter refused to bear civil liability on the ground that "not responsible for work-related injuries." Zhang Lianqi and Jiao Ronglan then filed a prosecution with the court.  
Upon study, it is held that the implementation of labor protection for workers is expressly provided for in the Constitution of China, which is a right enjoyed by workers and protected by state law, and no individual or organization shall infringe upon it arbitrarily. Zhang Xuezhen and Xu Guangqiu, as employers, shall provide labor protection to employees according to law, but they indicated "not responsible for work-related injuries" in the employment registration form. This violates the Constitution and relevant labor protection regulations, and also seriously violates socialist public morality. Such conduct shall be deemed invalid.  
This is hereby replied.  
October 14, 1988
