# 最高人民法院关于严格执行案件审理期限制度的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕29号  
**公布日期**：2000-09-14  
**生效日期**：2000-09-28

> （2000年9月14日最高人民法院审判委员会第1130次会议通过，自2000年9月28日起施行） 法释〔2000〕29号 为提高诉讼效率，确保司法公正，根据刑事诉讼法、民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法和海事诉讼特别程序法的有关规定，现就人民法院执行案件审理期限制度的有关问题规定如下： 一、各类案件的审理、执行期限 第一条 适用普通程序审理的第一审刑事公诉案件、被告人被羁押的第一审刑事自诉案件和第二审刑事公诉、刑事自诉案件的期限为一个月，至迟不得超过一个半月；附带民事诉讼案件的审理期限，经本院院长批准，可以延长两个月。有刑事诉讼法第一百二十六条规定情形之一的，经省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院批准或者决定，审理期限可以再延长一个月；最高人民法院受理的刑事上诉、刑事抗诉案件，经最高人民法院决定，审理期限可以再延长一个月。 适用普通程序审理的被告人未被羁押的第一审刑事自诉案件，期限为六个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。 适用简易程序审理的刑事案件，审理期限为二十日。 第二条 适用普通程序审理的第一审民事案件，期限为六个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长六个月，还需延长的，报请上一级人民法院批准，可以再延长三个月。 适用简易程序审理的民事案件，期限为三个月。 适用特别程序审理的民事案件，期限为三十日；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三十日，但审理选民资格案件必须在选举日前审结。 审理第一审船舶碰撞、共同海损案件的期限为一年；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长六个月。 审理对民事判决的上诉案件，审理期限为三个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。 审理对民事裁定的上诉案件，审理期限为三十日。 对罚款、拘留民事决定不服申请复议的，审理期限为五日。 审理涉外民事案件，根据民事诉讼法第二百五十条的规定，不受上述案件审理期限的限制。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 适用普通程序审理的第一审刑事公诉案件、被告人被羁押的第一审刑事自诉案件和第二审刑事公诉、刑事自诉案件的期限为一个月，至迟不得超过一个半月；附带民事诉讼案件的审理期限，经本院院长批准，可以延长两个月。有[刑事诉讼法第一百二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-126)规定情形之一的，经省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院批准或者决定，审理期限可以再延长一个月；最高人民法院受理的刑事上诉、刑事抗诉案件，经最高人民法院决定，审理期限可以再延长一个月。  
适用普通程序审理的被告人未被羁押的第一审刑事自诉案件，期限为六个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。  
适用简易程序审理的刑事案件，审理期限为二十日。

**Article 1** The trial period for first-instance criminal public prosecution cases, first-instance criminal private prosecution cases where the defendant is in custody, and second-instance criminal public prosecution and criminal private prosecution cases subject to ordinary procedure shall be one month, and shall not exceed one month and a half at the latest; the trial period for cases with incidental civil actions may be extended by two months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case. If any of the circumstances specified in Article 126 of the Criminal Procedure Law exists, the trial period may be extended by another one month upon approval or decision by the Higher People's Court of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; for criminal appeals and criminal protests accepted by the Supreme People's Court, the trial period may be extended by another one month upon decision by the Supreme People's Court.  
The trial period for first-instance criminal private prosecution cases where the defendant is not in custody and subject to ordinary procedure shall be six months; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by three months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case.  
The trial period for criminal cases subject to summary procedure shall be twenty days.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 适用普通程序审理的第一审民事案件，期限为六个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长六个月，还需延长的，报请上一级人民法院批准，可以再延长三个月。  
适用简易程序审理的民事案件，期限为三个月。  
适用特别程序审理的民事案件，期限为三十日；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三十日，但审理选民资格案件必须在选举日前审结。  
审理第一审船舶碰撞、共同海损案件的期限为一年；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长六个月。  
审理对民事判决的上诉案件，审理期限为三个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。  
审理对民事裁定的上诉案件，审理期限为三十日。  
对罚款、拘留民事决定不服申请复议的，审理期限为五日。  
审理涉外民事案件，根据[民事诉讼法第二百五十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-250)的规定，不受上述案件审理期限的限制。  
审理涉港、澳、台的民事案件的期限，参照审理涉外民事案件的规定办理。

**Article 2** The trial period for first-instance civil cases subject to ordinary procedure shall be six months; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by six months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case; if further extension is needed, it may be extended by another three months upon submission to and approval by the People's Court at the next higher level.  
The trial period for civil cases subject to summary procedure shall be three months.  
The trial period for civil cases subject to special procedure shall be thirty days; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by thirty days upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case, but the trial of voter qualification cases must be concluded before the election day.  
The trial period for first-instance cases of collision of ships and general average shall be one year; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by six months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case.  
The trial period for appeals against civil judgments shall be three months; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by three months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case.  
The trial period for appeals against civil rulings shall be thirty days.  
The period for hearing applications for reconsideration of decisions on fines or detention in civil matters shall be five days.  
The trial of foreign-related civil cases shall not be subject to the restrictions on the trial periods mentioned above, in accordance with Article 250 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
The trial periods for civil cases involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan shall be handled by reference to the provisions on the trial of foreign-related civil cases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 审理第一审行政案件的期限为三个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，经高级人民法院批准可以延长三个月。高级人民法院审理第一审案件需要延长期限的，由最高人民法院批准，可以延长三个月。  
审理行政上诉案件的期限为两个月；有特殊情况需要延长的，由高级人民法院批准，可以延长两个月。高级人民法院审理的第二审案件需要延长期限的，由最高人民法院批准，可以延长两个月。

**Article 3** The trial period for first-instance administrative cases shall be three months; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by three months upon approval by the Higher People's Court. Where the Higher People's Court needs to extend the trial period for a first-instance case it hears, the extension may be three months upon approval by the Supreme People's Court.  
The trial period for administrative appeals shall be two months; if extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by two months upon approval by the Higher People's Court. Where the Higher People's Court needs to extend the trial period for a second-instance case it hears, the extension may be two months upon approval by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 按照审判监督程序重新审理的刑事案件的期限为三个月；需要延长期限的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。  
裁定再审的民事、行政案件，根据再审适用的不同程序，分别执行第一审或第二审审理期限的规定。

**Article 4** The trial period for criminal cases retried under the trial supervision procedure shall be three months; if extension of the period is necessary, it may be extended by three months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case.  
For civil and administrative cases where a retrial is ordered, the provisions on the trial periods for first-instance or second-instance trials shall be followed respectively, depending on the procedure applicable to the retrial.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 执行案件应当在立案之日起六个月内执结，非诉执行案件应当在立案之日起三个月内执结；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月，还需延长的，层报高级人民法院备案。  
委托执行的案件，委托的人民法院应当在立案后一个月内办理完委托执行手续，受委托的人民法院应当在收到委托函件后三十日内执行完毕。未执行完毕，应当在期限届满后十五日内将执行情况函告委托人民法院。  
刑事案件没收财产刑应当即时执行。  
刑事案件罚金刑，应当在判决、裁定发生法律效力后三个月内执行完毕，至迟不超过六个月。  
二、立案、结案时间及审理期限的计算

**Article 5** Enforcement cases shall be concluded within six months from the date of case filing; non-litigation enforcement cases shall be concluded within three months from the date of case filing. If extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended by three months upon approval by the president of the People's Court trying the case; if further extension is needed, it shall be reported level by level to the Higher People's Court for record.  
For cases entrusted for enforcement, the entrusting People's Court shall complete the entrustment procedures within one month after case filing; the entrusted People's Court shall complete enforcement within thirty days after receiving the letter of entrustment. If enforcement is not completed, it shall inform the entrusting People's Court in writing of the enforcement status within fifteen days after expiration of the time limit.  
The penalty of confiscation of property in criminal cases shall be enforced immediately.  
The fine penalty in criminal cases shall be enforced within three months after the judgment or ruling becomes legally effective, and shall in no case exceed six months.  
II. Calculation of Case Filing, Case Conclusion Time and Trial Periods

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 第一审人民法院收到起诉书（状）或者执行申请书后，经审查认为符合受理条件的应当在七日内立案；收到自诉人自诉状或者口头告诉的，经审查认为符合自诉案件受理条件的应当在十五日内立案。  
改变管辖的刑事、民事、行政案件，应当在收到案卷材料后的三日内立案。  
第二审人民法院应当在收到第一审人民法院移送的上（抗）诉材料及案卷材料后的五日内立案。  
发回重审或指令再审的案件，应当在收到发回重审或指令再审裁定及案卷材料后的次日内立案。  
按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，应当在作出提审、再审裁定（决定）的次日立案。

**Article 6** After receiving a bill of indictment (complaint), a statement of complaint, or an application for enforcement, the first-instance People's Court shall, upon examination, file the case within seven days if it deems the conditions for acceptance are met; after receiving a private prosecution complaint or an oral complaint from a private prosecutor, the court shall, upon examination, file the case within fifteen days if it deems the conditions for acceptance of private prosecution cases are met.  
For criminal, civil, or administrative cases where jurisdiction is changed, the case shall be filed within three days after receipt of the case file materials.  
The second-instance People's Court shall file the case within five days after receiving the appeal (protest) materials and case file materials transferred by the first-instance People's Court.  
For cases remanded for retrial or ordered for retrial, the case shall be filed on the next day after receipt of the ruling on remand for retrial or order for retrial and the case file materials.  
For cases retried under the trial supervision procedure, the case shall be filed on the next day after the ruling (decision) on taking over the case for trial or retrial is made.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 立案机构应当在决定立案的三日内将案卷材料移送审判庭。

**Article 7** The case-filing office shall transfer the case file materials to the trial division within three days after the decision to file the case.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 案件的审理期限从立案次日起计算。  
由简易程序转为普通程序审理的第一审刑事案件的期限，从决定转为普通程序次日起计算；由简易程序转为普通程序审理的第一审民事案件的期限，从立案次日起连续计算。

**Article 8** The trial period of a case shall be calculated from the day following the date of case filing.  
The period for first-instance criminal cases switched from summary procedure to ordinary procedure shall be calculated from the day following the date of the decision to switch to ordinary procedure; the period for first-instance civil cases switched from summary procedure to ordinary procedure shall be calculated continuously from the day following the date of case filing.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 下列期间不计人审理、执行期限：  
（一）刑事案件对被告人作精神病鉴定的期间；  
（二）刑事案件因另行委托、指定辩护人，法院决定延期审理的，自案件宣布延期审理之日起至第十日止准备辩护的时间；  
（三）公诉人发现案件需要补充侦查，提出延期审理建议后，合议庭同意延期审理的期间；  
（四）刑事案件二审期间，检察院查阅案卷超过七日后的时间；  
（五）因当事人、诉讼代理人、辩护人申请通知新的证人到庭、调取新的证据、申请重新鉴定或者勘验，法院决定延期审理一个月之内的期间；  
（六）民事、行政案件公告、鉴定的期间；  
（七）审理当事人提出的管辖权异议和处理法院之间的管辖争议的期间；  
（八）民事、行政、执行案件由有关专业机构进行审计、评估、资产清理的期间；  
（九）中止诉讼（审理）或执行至恢复诉讼（审理）或执行的期间；  
（十）当事人达成执行和解或者提供执行担保后，执行法院决定暂缓执行的期间；  
（十一）上级人民法院通知暂缓执行的期间；  
（十二）执行中拍卖、变卖被查封、扣押财产的期间。

**Article 9** The following periods shall not be included in the trial or enforcement period:  
(1) The period for conducting a psychiatric evaluation of the defendant in a criminal case;  
(2) In a criminal case, where the court decides to adjourn the trial because a new defender is to be retained or appointed, the time for preparation of defense from the date of announcing the adjournment to the tenth day;  
(3) The period during which the public prosecutor discovers that supplementary investigation is needed and proposes an adjournment, and the collegial panel agrees to the adjournment;  
(4) In a criminal case at second instance, the time exceeding seven days during which the People's Procuratorate reviews the case file;  
(5) The period not exceeding one month during which the court decides to adjourn the trial because a party, litigation agent, or defender applies to notify new witnesses to appear in court, to obtain new evidence, or to apply for a new expert evaluation or inspection;  
(6) The period for public announcement or expert evaluation in civil or administrative cases;  
(7) The period for hearing a challenge to jurisdiction raised by a party or resolving a jurisdictional dispute between courts;  
(8) The period during which auditing, appraisal, or asset liquidation is conducted by relevant professional institutions in civil, administrative, or enforcement cases;  
(9) The period from suspension of the action (trial) or enforcement to resumption of the action (trial) or enforcement;  
(10) The period during which the enforcement court decides to suspend enforcement after the parties reach an enforcement settlement or provide enforcement security;  
(11) The period during which the People's Court at a higher level notifies the suspension of enforcement;  
(12) The period for auction or sale of sealed or seized property during enforcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院判决书宣判、裁定书宣告或者调解书送达最后一名当事人的日期为结案时间。如需委托宣判、送达的，委托宣判、送达的人民法院应当在审限届满前将判决书、裁定书、调解书送达受托人民法院。受托人民法院应当在收到委托书后七日内送达。  
人民法院判决书宣判、裁定书宣告或者调解书送达有下列情形之一的，结案时间遵守以下规定：  
（一）留置送达的，以裁判文书留在受送达人的住所日为结案时间；  
（二）公告送达的，以公告刊登之日为结案时间；  
（三）邮寄送达的，以交邮日期为结案时间；  
（四）通过有关单位转交送达的，以送达回证上当事人签收的日期为结案时间。  
三、案件延长审理期限的报批

**Article 10** The date on which the written judgment, written ruling, or written mediation agreement is pronounced or delivered to the last party shall be the case conclusion date. If the pronouncement or service is to be entrusted, the entrusting People's Court shall deliver the written judgment, written ruling, or written mediation agreement to the entrusted People's Court before the expiration of the trial period. The entrusted People's Court shall complete service within seven days after receiving the letter of entrustment.  
Where the service of a written judgment, written ruling, or written mediation agreement by a People's Court falls under any of the following circumstances, the case conclusion date shall be determined in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If service is made by leaving the document at the domicile, the date on which the adjudicatory document is left at the domicile of the person on whom service is to be made shall be the case conclusion date;  
(2) If service is made by public announcement, the date of publication of the announcement shall be the case conclusion date;  
(3) If service is made by mail, the date of consignment for mailing shall be the case conclusion date;  
(4) If service is made through a relevant unit for onward delivery, the date of receipt and signature by the party on the service certificate shall be the case conclusion date.  
III. Submission and Approval of Extension of Trial Periods

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 刑事公诉案件、被告人被羁押的自诉案件，需要延长审理期限的，应当在审理期限届满七日以前，向高级人民法院提出申请；被告人未被羁押的刑事自诉案件，需要延长审理期限的，应当在审理期限届满十日前向本院院长提出申请。

**Article 11** For criminal public prosecution cases and private prosecution cases where the defendant is in custody, if extension of the trial period is necessary, an application shall be submitted to the Higher People's Court seven days before the expiration of the trial period; for criminal private prosecution cases where the defendant is not in custody, if extension of the trial period is necessary, an application shall be submitted to the president of the People's Court trying the case ten days before the expiration of the trial period.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 民事案件应当在审理期限届满十日前向本院院长提出申请；还需延长的，应当在审理期限届满十日前向上一级人民法院提出申请。

**Article 12** For civil cases, an application shall be submitted to the president of the People's Court trying the case ten days before the expiration of the trial period; if further extension is needed, an application shall be submitted to the People's Court at the next higher level ten days before the expiration of the trial period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 行政案件应当在审理期限届满十日前向高级人民法院或者最高人民法院提出申请。

**Article 13** For administrative cases, an application shall be submitted to the Higher People's Court or the Supreme People's Court ten days before the expiration of the trial period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 对于下级人民法院申请延长办案期限的报告，上级人民法院应当在审理期限届满三日前作出决定，并通知提出申请延长审理期限的人民法院。  
需要本院院长批准延长办案期限的，院长应当在审限届满前批准或者决定。  
四、上诉、抗诉二审案件的移送期限

**Article 14** With respect to a report from a lower People's Court requesting extension of the case-handling period, the higher People's Court shall make a decision three days before the expiration of the trial period and notify the People's Court that requested the extension.  
Where approval by the president of the People's Court is required for extending the case-handling period, the president shall approve or decide before the expiration of the trial period.  
IV. Transfer Periods for Second-Instance Cases on Appeal or Protest

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人通过第一审人民法院提出上诉的刑事案件，第一审人民法院应当在上诉期限届满后三日内将上诉状连同案卷、证据移送第二审人民法院。被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人直接向上级人民法院提出上诉的刑事案件，第一审人民法院应当在接到第二审人民法院移交的上诉状后三日内将案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院。

**Article 15** For criminal cases in which the defendant, private prosecutor, or the plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action files an appeal through the first-instance People's Court, the first-instance People's Court shall transfer the appeal petition together with the case file and evidence to the second-instance People's Court within three days after the expiration of the appeal period. For criminal cases in which the defendant, private prosecutor, or the plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action files an appeal directly with a higher People's Court, the first-instance People's Court shall transfer the case file and evidence to the People's Court at the next higher level within three days after receiving the appeal petition transferred by the second-instance People's Court.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 人民检察院抗诉的刑事二审案件，第一审人民法院应当在上诉、抗诉期届满后三日内将抗诉书连同案卷、证据移送第二审人民法院。

**Article 16** For criminal second-instance cases in which the People's Procuratorate protests, the first-instance People's Court shall transfer the protest petition together with the case file and evidence to the second-instance People's Court within three days after the expiration of the appeal or protest period.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 当事人提出上诉的二审民事、行政案件，第一审人民法院收到上诉状，应当在五日内将上诉状副本送达对方当事人。人民法院收到答辩状，应当在五日内将副本送达上诉人。  
人民法院受理人民检察院抗诉的民事、行政案件的移送期限，比照前款规定办理。

**Article 17** For second-instance civil or administrative cases in which a party appeals, the first-instance People's Court shall, within five days after receiving the appeal petition, serve a copy of the appeal petition on the opposing party. The People's Court shall, within five days after receiving the statement of defense, serve a copy thereof on the appellant.  
The time limit for transfer of civil or administrative cases accepted by a People's Court upon protest by the People's Procuratorate shall be handled by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 第二审人民法院立案时发现上诉案件材料不齐全的，应当在两日内通知第一审人民法院。第一审人民法院应当在接到第二审人民法院的通知后五日内补齐。

**Article 18** Where the second-instance People's Court, at the time of case filing, finds that the materials of an appeal case are incomplete, it shall notify the first-instance People's Court within two days. The first-instance People's Court shall complete the materials within five days after receiving the notice from the second-instance People's Court.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 下级人民法院接到上级人民法院调卷通知后，应当在五日内将全部案卷和证据移送，至迟不超过十日。  
五、对案件审理期限的监督、检查

**Article 19** After receiving the notice from a higher People's Court to transfer the case file, the lower People's Court shall transfer all case files and evidence within five days, and in no case exceed ten days.  
V. Supervision and Inspection of Trial Periods

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 各级人民法院应当将审理案件期限情况作为审判管理的重要内容，加强对案件审理期限的管理、监督和检查。

**Article 20** People's Courts at all levels shall take the trial period of cases as an important part of trial management, and strengthen the management, supervision and inspection of trial periods.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 各级人民法院应当建立审理期限届满前的催办制度。

**Article 21** People's Courts at all levels shall establish a system of reminders before the expiration of trial periods.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 各级人民法院应当建立案件审理期限定期通报制度。对违反诉讼法规定，超过审理期限或者违反本规定的情况进行通报。

**Article 22** People's Courts at all levels shall establish a system of regular notification of trial periods. They shall notify any violation of the provisions of the procedural laws, exceeding the trial period, or violation of these Provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 审判人员故意拖延办案，或者因过失延误办案，造成严重后果的，依照《人民法院审判纪律处分办法（试行）》第五十九条的规定予以处分。  
审判人员故意拖延移送案件材料，或者接受委托送达后，故意拖延不予送达的，参照《人民法院审判纪律处分办法（试行）》第五十九条的规定予以处分。

**Article 23** If a judicial officer intentionally delays case handling, or negligently delays case handling, causing serious consequences, he or she shall be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with Article 59 of the Measures on Disciplinary Sanctions for Judicial Work (for Trial Implementation) of the People's Courts.  
If a judicial officer intentionally delays the transfer of case materials, or after accepting a service entrustment, intentionally delays and fails to serve, he or she shall be subject to disciplinary action by reference to Article 59 of the Measures on Disciplinary Sanctions for Judicial Work (for Trial Implementation) of the People's Courts.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 本规定发布前有关审理期限规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 24** Where any provision on trial periods promulgated before the issuance of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
