### 最高人民法院关于人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决或驳回当事人申请后当事人能否上诉问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决或  
  
驳回当事人申请后当事人能否上诉问题的批复  
  
法复〔1997〕5号  
  
广西壮族自治区高级人民法院：  
  
你院桂高法［1996］144号请示及1997年1月20日补充意见均已收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百四十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-140)、第一百四十一条和[《中华人民共和国仲裁法》第九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-9)第二款规定，对人民法院依法作出的撤销仲裁裁决或驳回当事人申请的裁定，当事人无权上诉。人民法院依法裁定撤销仲裁裁决的，当事人可以根据双方重新达成的仲裁协议申请仲裁，也可以向人民法院起诉。  
  
此复  
  
1997年4月23日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether a Party May Appeal after a People's Court Renders a Ruling to Set Aside an Arbitral Award or Dismiss the Party's Application  
Fa Fu [1997] No. 5  
The Higher People's Court of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region:  
Your court's Gui Gao Fa [1996] No. 144 request for instructions and the supplementary opinion of January 20, 1997 have been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
According to Article 140 and Article 141 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 9, Paragraph 2 of the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China, a party shall have no right to appeal against a ruling made by a People's Court according to law that sets aside an arbitral award or dismisses a party's application. Where a People's Court renders a ruling according to law to set aside an arbitral award, the parties may apply for arbitration according to a new arbitration agreement reached between them, or may file a prosecution with the People's Court.  
This is hereby replied.  
April 23, 1997
