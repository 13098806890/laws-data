# 最高人民法院关于适用停止执行死刑程序有关问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕16号  
**公布日期**：2008-11-24  
**生效日期**：2008-12-26

> 最高人民法院《关于适用停止执行死刑程序有关问题的规定》已于2008年11月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1455次会议通过。现予公布，自2008年12月26日起施行。 2008年12月15日 最高人民法院 关于适用停止执行死刑程序有关问题的规定 法释〔2008〕16号 为确保死刑案件停止执行死刑程序依法规范进行，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关规定，结合刑事审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条  刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条、第二百一十二条规定的判决“可能有错误”包括下列情形： （一）发现罪犯可能有其他犯罪的； （二）共同犯罪的其他犯罪嫌疑人归案，可能影响罪犯量刑的； （三）共同犯罪的其他罪犯被暂停或者停止执行死刑，可能影响罪犯量刑的； （四）判决可能有其他错误的。 第二条  下级人民法院在接到最高人民法院执行死刑命令后、执行前，发现有刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条第一款、第二百一十二条第四款规定的情形的，应当暂停执行死刑，并立即将请求停止执行死刑的报告及相关材料层报最高人民法院审批。 第三条  最高人民法院经审查，认为不影响罪犯定罪量刑的，应当裁定下级人民法院继续执行死刑；认为可能影响罪犯定罪量刑的，应当裁定下级人民法院停止执行死刑。下级人民法院停止执行后，应当会同有关部门调查核实，并及时将调查结果和意见层报最高人民法院审核。 第四条  最高人民法院在执行死刑命令签发后、执行前，发现有刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条第一款、第二百一十二条第四款规定的情形的，应当立即裁定下级人民法院停止执行死刑，并将有关材料移交下级人民法院。下级人民法院会同有关部门调查核实后，应当及时将调查结果和意见层报最高人民法院审核。 第五条  对于下级人民法院报送的请求停止执行死刑的报告及相关材料，由最高人民法院作出核准死刑裁判的原合议庭负责审查，必要时，依法另行组成合议庭进行审查。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  [刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-211)、第二百一十二条规定的判决“可能有错误”包括下列情形：  
（一）发现罪犯可能有其他犯罪的；  
（二）共同犯罪的其他犯罪嫌疑人归案，可能影响罪犯量刑的；  
（三）共同犯罪的其他罪犯被暂停或者停止执行死刑，可能影响罪犯量刑的；  
（四）判决可能有其他错误的。

**Article 1** The judgment that "may be erroneous" as prescribed in Articles 211 and 212 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China includes the following circumstances:  
(1) It is discovered that the criminal may have committed other crimes;  
(2) Other criminal suspects in a joint crime have been brought to justice, which may affect the sentencing of the criminal;  
(3) The execution of the death penalty against other criminals in a joint crime has been suspended or stopped, which may affect the sentencing of the criminal;  
(4) The judgment may have other errors.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  下级人民法院在接到最高人民法院执行死刑命令后、执行前，发现有[刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-211)第一款、第二百一十二条第四款规定的情形的，应当暂停执行死刑，并立即将请求停止执行死刑的报告及相关材料层报最高人民法院审批。

**Article 2** Where a lower People's Court, after receiving the order of the Supreme People's Court for execution of a death penalty and before execution, discovers a circumstance as prescribed in Article 211, paragraph 1, or Article 212, paragraph 4, of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall suspend the execution of the death penalty and immediately submit a report requesting the suspension of execution of the death penalty and the relevant materials level by level to the Supreme People's Court for examination and approval.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  最高人民法院经审查，认为不影响罪犯定罪量刑的，应当裁定下级人民法院继续执行死刑；认为可能影响罪犯定罪量刑的，应当裁定下级人民法院停止执行死刑。下级人民法院停止执行后，应当会同有关部门调查核实，并及时将调查结果和意见层报最高人民法院审核。

**Article 3** After examination, if the Supreme People's Court deems that the circumstances do not affect the conviction and sentencing of the criminal, it shall render a ruling ordering the lower People's Court to proceed with the execution of the death penalty; if it deems that the circumstances may affect the conviction and sentencing of the criminal, it shall render a ruling ordering the lower People's Court to suspend the execution of the death penalty. After the lower People's Court suspends execution, it shall conduct investigation and verification jointly with the relevant departments, and shall promptly submit the investigation results and opinions level by level to the Supreme People's Court for review.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  最高人民法院在执行死刑命令签发后、执行前，发现有[刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-211)第一款、第二百一十二条第四款规定的情形的，应当立即裁定下级人民法院停止执行死刑，并将有关材料移交下级人民法院。下级人民法院会同有关部门调查核实后，应当及时将调查结果和意见层报最高人民法院审核。

**Article 4** Where the Supreme People's Court, after issuing the order for execution of a death penalty and before execution, discovers a circumstance as prescribed in Article 211, paragraph 1, or Article 212, paragraph 4, of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall immediately render a ruling ordering the lower People's Court to suspend the execution of the death penalty and transfer the relevant materials to the lower People's Court. After the lower People's Court conducts investigation and verification jointly with the relevant departments, it shall promptly submit the investigation results and opinions level by level to the Supreme People's Court for review.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  对于下级人民法院报送的请求停止执行死刑的报告及相关材料，由最高人民法院作出核准死刑裁判的原合议庭负责审查，必要时，依法另行组成合议庭进行审查。

**Article 5** With respect to the reports requesting suspension of execution of a death penalty and the relevant materials submitted by the lower People's Court, the original collegial panel of the Supreme People's Court that rendered the ruling approving the death penalty shall be responsible for examination. When necessary, a separate collegial panel shall be formed according to law to conduct examination.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  最高人民法院对于依法已停止执行死刑的案件，依照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）确认罪犯正在怀孕的，应当依法改判；  
（二）确认原裁判有错误，或者罪犯有重大立功表现需要依法改判的，应当裁定不予核准死刑，撤销原判，发回重新审判；  
（三）确认原裁判没有错误，或者罪犯没有重大立功表现，或者重大立功表现不影响原裁判执行的，应当裁定继续执行原核准死刑的裁判，并由院长再签发执行死刑的命令。

**Article 6** The Supreme People's Court shall, with respect to cases where the execution of a death penalty has been suspended according to law, handle the cases separately in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) If it is confirmed that the criminal is pregnant, a modified judgment shall be made according to law;  
(2) If it is confirmed that the original judgment is erroneous, or the criminal has performed major meritorious service and a modified judgment is required according to law, it shall render a ruling disapproving the death penalty, quashing the original judgment, and remanding the case for retrial;  
(3) If it is confirmed that the original judgment is not erroneous, or the criminal has not performed major meritorious service, or the major meritorious service does not affect the execution of the original judgment, it shall render a ruling ordering the continued execution of the original judgment approving the death penalty, and the President shall re-issue an order for execution of the death penalty.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  本规定施行后，以前的有关规定与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 7** After these Provisions come into force, if any previous relevant provisions are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
