# 最高人民法院关于审理发生在我国管辖海域相关案件若干问题的规定（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕16号  
**公布日期**：2015-12-28  
**生效日期**：2016-08-02

> 最高人民法院《关于审理发生在我国管辖海域相关案件若干问题的规定（一）》已于2015年12月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1674次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年8月2日起施行。 2016年8月1日 最高人民法院 关于审理发生在我国管辖海域相关案件若干问题的规定（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称我国管辖海域，是指中华人民共和国内水、领海、毗连区、专属经济区、大陆架，以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域。

**Article 1** : The sea areas under the jurisdiction of China as mentioned in these Provisions refer to the internal waters, territorial sea, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone, continental shelf of the People's Republic of China, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国公民或组织在我国与有关国家缔结的协定确定的共同管理的渔区或公海从事捕捞等作业的，适用本规定。

**Article 2** : These Provisions shall apply to Chinese citizens or organizations engaging in fishing and other operations in the fishery areas jointly managed under the agreements concluded between China and relevant countries or on the high seas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国公民或者外国人在我国管辖海域实施非法猎捕、杀害珍贵濒危野生动物或者非法捕捞水产品等犯罪的，依照我国刑法追究刑事责任。

**Article 3** : Where a Chinese citizen or a foreigner commits a crime of illegally hunting or killing rare and endangered wild animals or illegally fishing aquatic products in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the Criminal Law of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有关部门依据出境入境管理法、治安管理处罚法，对非法进入我国内水从事渔业生产或者渔业资源调查的外国人，作出行政强制措施或行政处罚决定，行政相对人不服的，可分别依据[出境入境管理法第六十四条](../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国出境入境管理法.md#art-64)和[治安管理处罚法第一百零二条](../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-102)的规定，向有关机关申请复议或向有管辖权的人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 4** : Where the relevant authorities make a decision on administrative compulsion or administrative penalty against a foreigner who illegally enters China's internal waters to engage in fishery production or fishery resource investigation in accordance with the Exit and Entry Administration Law and the Public Security Administration Punishments Law, and the administrative counterpart is dissatisfied, the administrative counterpart may, in accordance with Article 64 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law and Article 102 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law respectively, apply for reconsideration to the relevant authority or initiate an administrative action with the People's Court with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　因在我国管辖海域内发生海损事故，请求损害赔偿提起的诉讼，由管辖该海域的海事法院、事故船舶最先到达地的海事法院、船舶被扣押地或者被告住所地海事法院管辖。  
因在公海等我国管辖海域外发生海损事故，请求损害赔偿在我国法院提起的诉讼，由事故船舶最先到达地、船舶被扣押地或者被告住所地海事法院管辖。  
事故船舶为中华人民共和国船舶的，还可以由船籍港所在地海事法院管辖。

**Article 5** : For an action filed for compensation for damage caused by a marine accident occurring within the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, the maritime court having jurisdiction over such sea areas, the maritime court of the first port of arrival of the accident vessel, the maritime court of the place where the vessel was arrested, or the maritime court of the defendant's domicile shall have jurisdiction.  
For an action filed in a Chinese court for compensation for damage caused by a marine accident occurring on the high seas or outside the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, the maritime court of the first port of arrival of the accident vessel, the maritime court of the place where the vessel was arrested, or the maritime court of the defendant's domicile shall have jurisdiction.  
Where the accident vessel is a vessel of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court of the place of the ship's port of registry may also have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  在我国管辖海域内，因海上航运、渔业生产及其他海上作业造成污染，破坏海洋生态环境，请求损害赔偿提起的诉讼，由管辖该海域的海事法院管辖。  
污染事故发生在我国管辖海域外，对我国管辖海域造成污染或污染威胁，请求损害赔偿或者预防措施费用提起的诉讼，由管辖该海域的海事法院或采取预防措施地的海事法院管辖。

**Article 6** : For an action filed for compensation for damage caused by pollution from maritime shipping, fishery production, and other offshore operations within the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, which damages the marine ecological environment, the maritime court having jurisdiction over such sea areas shall have jurisdiction.  
Where a pollution accident occurs outside the sea areas under China's jurisdiction but causes pollution or a pollution threat to the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, an action for compensation for damage or expenses of preventive measures filed with a Chinese court shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court having jurisdiction over such sea areas or the maritime court of the place where the preventive measures were taken.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定施行后尚未审结的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 7** : For cases that have not yet been finally adjudicated after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall apply; for cases that have already become final before these Provisions come into force and are subject to an application for retrial by a party or a decision to retry in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定自2016年8月2日起施行。

**Article 8** : These Provisions shall come into force as of August 2, 2016.
