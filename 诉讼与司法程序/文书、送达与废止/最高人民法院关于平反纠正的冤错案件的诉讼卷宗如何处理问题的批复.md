<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于平反纠正的冤错案件的诉讼  
卷宗如何处理问题的批复  
1978年9月5日      〔78〕法办研字第19号  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
你院辽法字〔1987〕22号请示已收阅。关于经人民法院平反纠正的冤错案件的诉讼卷宗如何处理的问题，最高人民法院、国家档案局〔60〕法刑字第122号〔60〕档二字第171号《关于全国地方各级人民法院档案管理工作中若干规定的通知》中有过原则规定，即：“在诉讼卷宗的保管期限没有确定以前，所有的诉讼卷宗，一律不作鉴定，也不得销毁。”根据这一规定的精神，我们同意你们的意见，对人民法院平反纠正的冤错案件（包括“三类案件”），就将原判和改判形成的全部诉讼卷宗归档保存，不应销毁。至于有的冤错案件的当事人要求销毁该案诉讼卷宗，有关的人民法院应对当事人说明理由，进行耐心细致的政治思想工作，妥善加以解决。对审判案卷以外的材料同意销毁。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of How to Handle Litigation Files of Wrongful Convictions That Have Been Reversed and Corrected  
September 5, 1978 [78] Fa Ban Yan Zi No. 19  
Liaoning Higher People's Court:  
We have received your court's request No. Liao Fa Zi [1987] No. 22. Regarding the issue of how to handle litigation files of wrongful convictions that have been reversed and corrected by the People's Courts, the Supreme People's Court and the State Archives Administration, in their Notice on Several Provisions Concerning the Archives Management Work of Local People's Courts at All Levels (No. [60] Fa Xing Zi No. 122, [60] Dang Er Zi No. 171), have provided a principle: "Before the retention period of litigation files is determined, all litigation files shall not be subject to expert evaluation, nor shall they be destroyed." In accordance with the spirit of this provision, we agree with your opinion that for wrongful conviction cases (including "three categories of cases") that have been reversed and corrected by the People's Courts, all litigation files formed from the original judgment and the amended judgment shall be archived and preserved, and shall not be destroyed. As for cases where the parties of some wrongful conviction cases request the destruction of the litigation files of that case, the relevant People's Court shall explain the reasons to the party and conduct patient and meticulous political and ideological work to properly resolve the matter. Materials other than the adjudication files may be destroyed.
