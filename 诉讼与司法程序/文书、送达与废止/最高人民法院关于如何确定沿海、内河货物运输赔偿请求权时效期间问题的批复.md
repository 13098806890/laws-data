### 最高人民法院关于如何确定沿海、内河货物运输赔偿请求权时效期间问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于如何确定沿海、内河货物运输赔偿  
  
请求权时效期间问题的批复  
  
（2001年5月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1176次会议通过，自2001年5月31日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕18号  
  
浙江省高级人民法院：  
  
你院浙高法〔2000〕267号《关于沿海、内河货物运输赔偿请求权诉讼时效期间如何计算的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国海商法》第二百五十七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-257)第一款规定的精神，结合审判实践，托运人、收货人就沿海、内河货物运输合同向承运人要求赔偿的请求权，或者承运人就沿海、内河货物运输向托运人、收货人要求赔偿的请求权，时效期间为一年，自承运人交付或者应当交付货物之日起计算。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2001] No. 18  
  
The Higher People's Court of Zhejiang Province:  
  
We have received your petition, Zhe Gao Fa [2000] No. 267, titled "Request for Instructions on How to Calculate the Limitation Period for Claims for Compensation in Coastal and Inland Waterway Cargo Transport". Upon review, we hereby reply as follows:  
  
In accordance with the spirit of the first paragraph of Article 257 of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China, and in light of adjudication practice, the limitation period for a claim by the shipper or consignee against the carrier for compensation under a coastal or inland waterway cargo transport contract, or for a claim by the carrier against the shipper or consignee for compensation in coastal or inland waterway cargo transport, shall be one year, commencing from the date on which the carrier delivered or should have delivered the goods.  
  
This is hereby replied.
