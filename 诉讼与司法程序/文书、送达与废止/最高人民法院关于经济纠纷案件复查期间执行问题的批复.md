### 最高人民法院关于经济纠纷案件复查期间执行问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于经济纠纷案件复查期间执行问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1989〕6号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 88）粤法经行字第 144号《关于经济纠纷案件复查期间执行问题的请示》收悉。经研究，现答复如下：  
  
人民法院对已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的申诉，经过复查，发现原判决、裁定确有错误的，应按审判监督程序依法及时裁定再审或者提审。在复查期间，不停止生效判决、裁定的执行。但是，案情复杂，一时难以决定是否再审而继续执行又有可能造成再审后执行回转困难或不能执行回转的，可根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第一百八十二条第一款第（五）项的规定，裁定中止执行。经复查决定不予再审的案件，则恢复执行。  
  
1989年8月8日

**Article 1** Reply on Issues Concerning Enforcement During the Retrial Review Period of Economic Dispute Cases  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1989] No.6  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your request (88) Yue Fa Jing Xing Zi No. 144, "Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning Enforcement During the Retrial Review Period of Economic Dispute Cases," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a People's Court, after reviewing petitions against judgments or rulings that have taken legal effect, finds that the original judgment or ruling is indeed erroneous, it shall promptly order a retrial or bring up the case for trial according to the trial supervision procedure in accordance with law. During the review period, enforcement of the effective judgment or ruling shall not be suspended. However, where the case is complex and it is difficult to decide whether to retry at once, and continued enforcement may cause difficulties in or impossibility of enforcement restitution after retrial, the court may, in accordance with Article 182, paragraph 1, item (5) of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation), rule to suspend enforcement. For cases where the review decides not to retry, enforcement shall be resumed.  
August 8, 1989
