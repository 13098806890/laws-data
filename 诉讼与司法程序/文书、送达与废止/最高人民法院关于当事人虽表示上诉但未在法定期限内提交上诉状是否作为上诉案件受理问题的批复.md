# 最高人民法院关于当事人虽表示上诉但未在法定期限内提交上诉状是否作为上诉案件受理问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1989〕7号  
**公布日期**：1989-08-21

---

### 正文

一、当事人在法定上诉期限内虽表示要上诉，但未递交上诉状的，原审人民法院应当告知其须在法定期限内按照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第一百四十六条、第一百四十七条第一款规定的要求，提出上诉状及副本。

**Article None** Where a party indicates an intent to appeal within the statutory appeal period but does not submit an appeal petition, the original trial People's Court shall inform the party that it must, within the statutory period, submit an appeal petition and copies in accordance with the requirements of Article 146 and Article 147, paragraph 1 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation).

二、当事人在法定上诉期限内表示上诉的同时，请求延期递交上诉状的，人民法院经审查认为理由正当，可以酌情准许适当延长。

**Article None** Where a party, while indicating an appeal within the statutory appeal period, also requests an extension for submitting the appeal petition, the People's Court may, upon review and finding the reason justified, appropriately grant a suitable extension.

三、当事人在法定上诉期限内仅表示上诉，既未递交上诉状，又未请求延期的；或者请求延期，但未获人民法院准许，而且在法定上诉期限内又未递交上诉状的；或者请求延期获准后，在延长的期限内仍未递交上诉状的，原审判决或者裁定在法定上诉期限或准许延长期限届满后即发生法律效力。当事人不服该判决或者裁定的，人民法院应当作为申诉处理，而不应作为上诉案件受理。  
1989年8月21日

**Article None** Where a party, within the statutory appeal period, only indicates an appeal but neither submits an appeal petition nor requests an extension; or requests an extension but is not granted by the People's Court and fails to submit an appeal petition within the statutory appeal period; or, having been granted an extension, still fails to submit an appeal petition within the extended period, the original judgment or ruling shall become legally effective immediately upon the expiration of the statutory appeal period or the permitted extension period. Where a party disagrees with such judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall handle the matter as a petition and shall not accept it as an appeal case.  
August 21, 1989
