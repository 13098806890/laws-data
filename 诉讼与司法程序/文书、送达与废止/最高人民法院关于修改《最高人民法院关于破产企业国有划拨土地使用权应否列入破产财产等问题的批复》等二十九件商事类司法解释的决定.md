# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》等二十九件商事类司法解释的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕18号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-23  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复〉等二十九件商事类司法解释的决定》已于2020年12月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年1月1日起施行。 2020年12月29日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划 拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》 等二十九件商事类司法解释的决定 法释〔2020〕18号 根据审判实践需要，经最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》等二十九件司法解释作如下修改： 一、修改《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》 1.将第一条修改为： “根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》第五十八条第一款第（三）项及《城镇国有土地使用权出让和转让暂行条例》第四十七条的规定，破产企业以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权不属于破产财产，在企业破产时，有关人民政府可以予以收回，并依法处置。纳入国家兼并破产计划的国有企业，其依法取得的国有土地使用权，应依据国务院有关文件规定办理。” 2.将第二条修改为： “企业对其以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权无处分权，以该土地使用权设定抵押，未经有审批权限的人民政府或土地行政管理部门批准的，不影响抵押合同效力；履行了法定的审批手续，并依法办理抵押登记的，抵押权自登记时设立。根据《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》第五十一条的规定，抵押权人只有在以抵押标的物折价或拍卖、变卖所得价款缴纳相当于土地使用权出让金的款项后，对剩余部分方可享有优先受偿权。但纳入国家兼并破产计划的国有企业，其用以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权设定抵押的，应依据国务院有关文件规定办理。” 3.将第三条修改为： “国有企业以关键设备、成套设备、建筑物设定抵押的，如无其他法定的无效情形，不应当仅以未经政府主管部门批准为由认定抵押合同无效。”

### 正文

一、修改《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》  
1.将第一条修改为：  
“根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》第五十八条第一款第（三）项及《城镇国有土地使用权出让和转让暂行条例》第四十七条的规定，破产企业以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权不属于破产财产，在企业破产时，有关人民政府可以予以收回，并依法处置。纳入国家兼并破产计划的国有企业，其依法取得的国有土地使用权，应依据国务院有关文件规定办理。”  
2.将第二条修改为：  
“企业对其以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权无处分权，以该土地使用权设定抵押，未经有审批权限的人民政府或土地行政管理部门批准的，不影响抵押合同效力；履行了法定的审批手续，并依法办理抵押登记的，抵押权自登记时设立。根据《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》第五十一条的规定，抵押权人只有在以抵押标的物折价或拍卖、变卖所得价款缴纳相当于土地使用权出让金的款项后，对剩余部分方可享有优先受偿权。但纳入国家兼并破产计划的国有企业，其用以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权设定抵押的，应依据国务院有关文件规定办理。”  
3.将第三条修改为：  
“国有企业以关键设备、成套设备、建筑物设定抵押的，如无其他法定的无效情形，不应当仅以未经政府主管部门批准为由认定抵押合同无效。”

**Article None** I. Revision of the "Reply of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning Whether State-owned Land Use Rights Obtained by Allocation Should Be Included in Bankruptcy Property of Bankrupt Enterprises"  
1. Article 1 is revised to read:  
"According to Article 58, Paragraph 1, Item (3) of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 47 of the Interim Regulations on the Assignment and Transfer of the Right to Use State-owned Land in Cities and Towns, the state-owned land use rights obtained by a bankrupt enterprise by means of allocation shall not be regarded as bankruptcy property. At the time of bankruptcy of the enterprise, the relevant people's government may reclaim such land use rights and dispose of them according to law. For state-owned enterprises included in the national merger and bankruptcy plan, their state-owned land use rights obtained according to law shall be handled in accordance with the relevant documents of the State Council."  
2. Article 2 is revised to read:  
"An enterprise has no right to dispose of the state-owned land use rights obtained by means of allocation. Where a mortgage is created on such land use rights without the approval of the people's government or the land administration department with examination and approval authority, the validity of the mortgage contract shall not be affected. Where the statutory approval procedures are fulfilled and the mortgage registration is completed according to law, the mortgage right shall be established from the date of registration. According to Article 51 of the Urban Real Estate Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, a mortgagee may enjoy the right to be paid in priority only for the remaining part after the proceeds from the discount, auction, or sale of the mortgaged property are used to pay the amount equivalent to the land use right transfer fee. However, for state-owned enterprises included in the national merger and bankruptcy plan, the creation of a mortgage on state-owned land use rights obtained by means of allocation shall be handled in accordance with the relevant documents of the State Council."  
3. Article 3 is revised to read:  
"Where a state-owned enterprise creates a mortgage on key equipment, complete sets of equipment, or buildings, if there are no other statutory circumstances rendering the mortgage void, the mortgage contract shall not be deemed void solely on the ground that it has not been approved by the competent government department."

二、修改《最高人民法院关于审理存单纠纷案件的若干规定》  
1.将引言修改为：  
“为正确审理存单纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》的有关规定和在总结审判经验的基础上，制定本规定。”  
2.将第三条修改为：  
“存单纠纷案件的受理与中止  
存单纠纷案件当事人向人民法院提起诉讼，人民法院应当依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十九条的规定予以审查，符合规定的，均应受理。  
人民法院在受理存单纠纷案件后，如发现犯罪线索，应将犯罪线索及时书面告知公安或检察机关。如案件当事人因伪造、变造、虚开存单或涉嫌诈骗，有关国家机关已立案侦查，存单纠纷案件确须待刑事案件结案后才能审理的，人民法院应当中止审理。对于追究有关当事人的刑事责任不影响对存单纠纷案件审理的，人民法院应对存单纠纷案件有关当事人是否承担民事责任以及承担民事责任的大小依法及时进行认定和处理。”  
3.将第四条修改为：  
“存单纠纷案件的管辖  
依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二十三条的规定，存单纠纷案件由被告住所地人民法院或出具存单、进账单、对账单或与当事人签订存款合同的金融机构住所地人民法院管辖。住所地与经常居住地不一致的，由经常居住地人民法院管辖。”

**Article None** II. Revision of the "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Disputes over Deposit Certificates"  
1. The preamble is revised to read:  
"For the purpose of correctly trying cases of disputes over deposit certificates, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and based on the summary of trial experience."  
2. Article 3 is revised to read:  
"Acceptance and Suspension of Cases of Disputes over Deposit Certificates  
Where a party to a dispute over a deposit certificate files a lawsuit with a People's Court, the People's Court shall examine the case in accordance with Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and shall accept the case if it meets the requirements.  
After accepting a case of a dispute over a deposit certificate, if the People's Court discovers clues of a crime, it shall promptly notify the public security or procuratorial organ in writing of the clues. If a relevant state organ has already opened an investigation due to the forgery, alteration, or fraudulent issuance of deposit certificates or suspected fraud by a party to the case, and the dispute over the deposit certificate can only be tried after the conclusion of the criminal case, the People's Court shall suspend the trial. If the investigation of criminal liability of the relevant parties does not affect the trial of the case of the dispute over the deposit certificate, the People's Court shall promptly determine and handle, according to law, whether the relevant parties to the dispute over the deposit certificate shall bear civil liability and the extent of such liability."  
3. Article 4 is revised to read:  
"Jurisdiction over Cases of Disputes over Deposit Certificates  
In accordance with Article 23 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, a case of a dispute over a deposit certificate shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the defendant or the People's Court at the domicile of the financial institution that issued the deposit certificate, the credit advice, the account statement, or entered into the deposit contract with the party. If the domicile and the habitual residence are inconsistent, the People's Court at the habitual residence shall have jurisdiction."

三、修改《最高人民法院关于审理军队、武警部队、政法机关移交、撤销企业和与党政机关脱钩企业相关纠纷案件若干问题的规定》  
1.将引言修改为：  
“为依法准确审理军队、武警部队、政法机关移交、撤销企业和与党政机关脱钩的企业所发生的债务纠纷案件和破产案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国公司法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国企业破产法》的有关规定，作如下规定：”  
2.将第一条修改为：  
“军队、武警部队、政法机关和党政机关开办的企业（以下简称被开办企业）具备法人条件并领取了企业法人营业执照的，根据民法典第六十条的规定，应当以其全部财产独立承担民事责任。”  
3.将第二条修改为：  
“被开办企业领取了企业法人营业执照，虽然实际投入的资金与注册资金不符，但已达到了《中华人民共和国企业法人登记管理条例施行细则》第十二条第七项规定数额的，应当认定其具备法人资格，开办单位应当在该企业实际投入资金与注册资金的差额范围内承担民事责任。”  
4.将第三条修改为：  
“被开办企业虽然领取了企业法人营业执照，但投入的资金未达到《中华人民共和国企业法人登记管理条例施行细则》第十二条第七项规定数额的，或者不具备企业法人其他条件的，应当认定其不具备法人资格，其民事责任由开办单位承担。”  
5.删除第四条、第六条、第八条、第九条、第十四条、第十六条。  
6.将第七条修改为：  
“开办单位或其主管部门在被开办企业撤销时，向工商行政管理机关出具证明文件，自愿对被开办企业的债务承担责任的，应当按照承诺对被开办企业的债务承担民事责任。”  
7.将第十三条修改为：  
“被开办企业向社会或者向企业内部职工集资未清偿的，在破产财产分配时，应当按照《中华人民共和国企业破产法》第一百一十三条第一款第一项的规定予以清偿。”  
8.条文顺序作相应调整。

**Article None** III. Revision of the "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Cases Related to Enterprises Transferred or Dissolved by the Armed Forces, Armed Police Forces, and Political and Legal Organs and Enterprises Disaffiliated from Party and Government Organs"  
1. The preamble is revised to read:  
"For the purpose of accurately trying, according to law, cases of debt disputes and bankruptcy cases arising from enterprises transferred or dissolved by the armed forces, armed police forces, political and legal organs, and enterprises disaffiliated from party and government organs, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China:"  
2. Article 1 is revised to read:  
"Where an enterprise established by the armed forces, armed police forces, political and legal organs, or party and government organs (hereinafter referred to as the 'established enterprise') meets the requirements for legal person status and has obtained a business license for a legal person enterprise, it shall, in accordance with Article 60 of the Civil Code, independently bear civil liability with all its property."  
3. Article 2 is revised to read:  
"Where an established enterprise has obtained a business license for a legal person enterprise, and although the actual capital invested is inconsistent with the registered capital, the amount has reached the figure specified in Article 12, Item (7) of the Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Registration of Enterprise Legal Persons, it shall be deemed to have legal person status, and the establishing unit shall bear civil liability to the extent of the difference between the actual capital invested and the registered capital."  
4. Article 3 is revised to read:  
"Where an established enterprise has obtained a business license for a legal person enterprise, but the capital invested has not reached the figure specified in Article 12, Item (7) of the Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Registration of Enterprise Legal Persons, or it does not meet other conditions for being a legal person enterprise, it shall be deemed not to have legal person status, and its civil liability shall be borne by the establishing unit."  
5. Articles 4, 6, 8, 9, 14, and 16 are deleted.  
6. Article 7 is revised to read:  
"Where, at the time of dissolution of an established enterprise, the establishing unit or its competent authority issues a certifying document to the administrative authority for industry and commerce, voluntarily undertaking to bear the debts of the established enterprise, it shall bear civil liability for the debts of the established enterprise in accordance with its commitment."  
7. Article 13 is revised to read:  
"Where an established enterprise has raised funds from the public or from its internal employees and has not yet repaid them, during the distribution of bankruptcy property, such claims shall be satisfied in accordance with Article 113, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China."  
8. The sequence of articles shall be adjusted accordingly.

五、修改《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国公司法〉若干问题的规定（二）》  
1.将第二条修改为：  
“股东提起解散公司诉讼，同时又申请人民法院对公司进行清算的，人民法院对其提出的清算申请不予受理。人民法院可以告知原告，在人民法院判决解散公司后，依据民法典第七十条、公司法第一百八十三条和本规定第七条的规定，自行组织清算或者另行申请人民法院对公司进行清算。”  
2.将第七条修改为：  
“公司应当依照民法典第七十条、公司法第一百八十三条的规定，在解散事由出现之日起十五日内成立清算组，开始自行清算。  
有下列情形之一，债权人、公司股东、董事或其他利害关系人申请人民法院指定清算组进行清算的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）公司解散逾期不成立清算组进行清算的；  
（二）虽然成立清算组但故意拖延清算的；  
（三）违法清算可能严重损害债权人或者股东利益的。”  
3.将第九条修改为：  
“人民法院指定的清算组成员有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以根据债权人、公司股东、董事或其他利害关系人的申请，或者依职权更换清算组成员：  
（一）有违反法律或者行政法规的行为；  
（二）丧失执业能力或者民事行为能力；  
（三）有严重损害公司或者债权人利益的行为。”  
4.将第十五条修改为：  
“公司自行清算的，清算方案应当报股东会或者股东大会决议确认；人民法院组织清算的，清算方案应当报人民法院确认。未经确认的清算方案，清算组不得执行。  
执行未经确认的清算方案给公司或者债权人造成损失，公司、股东、董事、公司其他利害关系人或者债权人主张清算组成员承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。”  
5.将第二十一条修改为：  
“按照本规定第十八条和第二十条第一款的规定应当承担责任的有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东，以及公司的实际控制人为二人以上的，其中一人或者数人依法承担民事责任后，主张其他人员按照过错大小分担责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。”

**Article None** V. Revision of the "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China (II)"  
1. Article 2 is revised to read:  
"Where a shareholder initiates a lawsuit for dissolution of the company and simultaneously applies to the People's Court for liquidation of the company, the People's Court shall not accept the liquidation application. The People's Court may inform the plaintiff that, after the court renders a judgment dissolving the company, the plaintiff may, in accordance with Article 70 of the Civil Code, Article 183 of the Company Law, and Article 7 of these Provisions, organize liquidation on its own or separately apply to the People's Court for liquidation of the company."  
2. Article 7 is revised to read:  
"A company shall, in accordance with Article 70 of the Civil Code and Article 183 of the Company Law, establish a liquidation committee within fifteen days from the date on which the cause for dissolution arises and commence liquidation on its own.  
In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall accept an application filed by a creditor, a company shareholder, a director, or any other interested person for the People's Court to appoint a liquidation committee to conduct liquidation:  
(1) The company fails to establish a liquidation committee for liquidation within the prescribed period after dissolution;  
(2) Although a liquidation committee has been established, it deliberately delays the liquidation;  
(3) Illegal liquidation may cause serious damage to the interests of creditors or shareholders."  
3. Article 9 is revised to read:  
"Where a member of a liquidation committee appointed by the People's Court falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may replace the member of the liquidation committee upon the application of a creditor, a company shareholder, a director, or any other interested person, or ex officio:  
(1) Having committed an act violating laws or administrative regulations;  
(2) Having lost professional qualification or capacity for civil conduct;  
(3) Having committed an act seriously damaging the interests of the company or creditors."  
4. Article 15 is revised to read:  
"Where a company conducts liquidation on its own, the liquidation plan shall be submitted to the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders for resolution and confirmation; where a People's Court organizes liquidation, the liquidation plan shall be submitted to the People's Court for confirmation.
