# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于办理死刑案件审查判断证据若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2010-06-13  
**生效日期**：2010-07-01

> （2010年6月13日发布 自2010年7月1日起施行 法发〔2010〕20号） 为依法、公正、准确、慎重地办理死刑案件，惩罚犯罪，保障人权，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合司法实际，制定本规定。 一、一般规定 第一条 办理死刑案件，必须严格执行刑法和刑事诉讼法，切实做到事实清楚，证据确实、充分，程序合法，适用法律正确，确保案件质量。 第二条 认定案件事实，必须以证据为根据。 第三条 侦查人员、检察人员、审判人员应当严格遵守法定程序，全面、客观地收集、审查、核实和认定证据。 第四条 经过当庭出示、辨认、质证等法庭调查程序查证属实的证据，才能作为定罪量刑的根据。 第五条 办理死刑案件，对被告人犯罪事实的认定，必须达到证据确实、充分。 证据确实、充分是指： （一）定罪量刑的事实都有证据证明； （二）每一个定案的证据均已经法定程序查证属实； （三）证据与证据之间、证据与案件事实之间不存在矛盾或者矛盾得以合理排除； （四）共同犯罪案件中，被告人的地位、作用均已查清； （五）根据证据认定案件事实的过程符合逻辑和经验规则，由证据得出的结论为唯一结论。 办理死刑案件，对于以下事实的证明必须达到证据确实、充分：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 办理死刑案件，必须严格执行刑法和刑事诉讼法，切实做到事实清楚，证据确实、充分，程序合法，适用法律正确，确保案件质量。

**Article 1** In handling death penalty cases, the Criminal Law and the Criminal Procedure Law must be strictly implemented, and it must be effectively ensured that the facts are clear, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, the procedures are lawful, and the applicable law is correct, so as to guarantee the quality of the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 认定案件事实，必须以证据为根据。

**Article 2** The determination of case facts must be based on evidence.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 侦查人员、检察人员、审判人员应当严格遵守法定程序，全面、客观地收集、审查、核实和认定证据。

**Article 3** Investigators, procurators, and adjudicators shall strictly observe the statutory procedures and collect, examine, verify, and determine evidence comprehensively and objectively.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 经过当庭出示、辨认、质证等法庭调查程序查证属实的证据，才能作为定罪量刑的根据。

**Article 4** Only evidence that has been verified through court investigation procedures such as presentation in court, identification, and cross-examination may serve as the basis for conviction and sentencing.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 办理死刑案件，对被告人犯罪事实的认定，必须达到证据确实、充分。  
证据确实、充分是指：  
（一）定罪量刑的事实都有证据证明；  
（二）每一个定案的证据均已经法定程序查证属实；  
（三）证据与证据之间、证据与案件事实之间不存在矛盾或者矛盾得以合理排除；  
（四）共同犯罪案件中，被告人的地位、作用均已查清；  
（五）根据证据认定案件事实的过程符合逻辑和经验规则，由证据得出的结论为唯一结论。  
办理死刑案件，对于以下事实的证明必须达到证据确实、充分：  
（一）被指控的犯罪事实的发生；  
（二）被告人实施了犯罪行为与被告人实施犯罪行为的时间、地点、手段、后果以及其他情节；  
（三）影响被告人定罪的身份情况；  
（四）被告人有刑事责任能力；  
（五）被告人的罪过；  
（六）是否共同犯罪及被告人在共同犯罪中的地位、作用；  
（七）对被告人从重处罚的事实。  
二、证据的分类审查与认定  
1.物证、书证

**Article 5** In handling death penalty cases, the determination of the criminal facts of the defendant must reach the standard that the evidence is reliable and sufficient.  
Evidence being reliable and sufficient means:  
(1) All facts for conviction and sentencing are supported by evidence;  
(2) Each piece of evidence used for final decision has been verified as true through statutory procedures;  
(3) There is no contradiction between evidence and evidence, or between evidence and case facts, or any contradiction has been reasonably eliminated;  
(4) In joint crime cases, the status and role of the defendant have been clarified;  
(5) The process of determining case facts based on evidence conforms to the rules of logic and experience, and the conclusion drawn from the evidence is the sole conclusion.  
In handling death penalty cases, the proof of the following facts must reach the standard that the evidence is reliable and sufficient:  
(1) The occurrence of the alleged criminal facts;  
(2) The defendant committed the criminal act and the time, place, means, consequences, and other circumstances of the defendant's commission of the criminal act;  
(3) The identity status affecting the conviction of the defendant;  
(4) The defendant has criminal capacity;  
(5) The defendant's culpability;  
(6) Whether it is a joint crime and the status and role of the defendant in the joint crime;  
(7) Facts for severe punishment of the defendant.  
II. Categorized Examination and Determination of Evidence  
1. Physical Evidence and Documentary Evidence

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 对物证、书证应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）物证、书证是否为原物、原件，物证的照片、录像或者复制品及书证的副本、复制件与原物、原件是否相符；物证、书证是否经过辨认、鉴定；物证的照片、录像或者复制品和书证的副本、复制件是否由二人以上制作，有无制作人关于制作过程及原件、原物存放于何处的文字说明及签名。  
（二）物证、书证的收集程序、方式是否符合法律及有关规定；经勘验、检查、搜查提取、扣押的物证、书证，是否附有相关笔录或者清单；笔录或者清单是否有侦查人员、物品持有人、见证人签名，没有物品持有人签名的，是否注明原因；对物品的特征、数量、质量、名称等注明是否清楚。  
（三）物证、书证在收集、保管及鉴定过程中是否受到破坏或者改变。  
（四）物证、书证与案件事实有无关联。对现场遗留与犯罪有关的具备检验鉴定条件的血迹、指纹、毛发、体液等生物物证、痕迹、物品，是否通过DNA鉴定、指纹鉴定等鉴定方式与被告人或者被害人的相应生物检材、生物特征、物品等作同一认定。  
（五）与案件事实有关联的物证、书证是否全面收集。

**Article 6** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for physical evidence and documentary evidence:  
(1) Whether the physical evidence or documentary evidence is the original object or original document; whether the photographs, videos, or reproductions of the physical evidence and the copies or reproductions of the documentary evidence are consistent with the original object or original document; whether the physical evidence or documentary evidence has undergone identification or expert evaluation; whether the photographs, videos, or reproductions of physical evidence and the copies or reproductions of documentary evidence were made by at least two persons, and whether there are written explanations and signatures of the producers regarding the production process and the location of the original document or original object.  
(2) Whether the collection procedures and methods of physical evidence and documentary evidence comply with laws and relevant provisions; whether physical evidence and documentary evidence obtained through inspection, examination, search, extraction, or seizure are accompanied by relevant records or lists; whether the records or lists bear the signatures of the investigator, the holder of the item, and the witness; if the holder of the item has not signed, whether the reason is stated; whether the characteristics, quantity, quality, name, etc. of the item are clearly indicated.  
(3) Whether the physical evidence or documentary evidence has been damaged or altered during collection, storage, and expert evaluation.  
(4) Whether the physical evidence or documentary evidence is connected to the case facts. For biological physical evidence, traces, and items left at the scene related to the crime that possess conditions for testing and identification, such as bloodstains, fingerprints, hair, and bodily fluids, has an identical determination been made through DNA identification, fingerprint identification, or other identification methods with the corresponding biological samples, biological characteristics, or items of the defendant or the victim?  
(5) Whether all physical evidence and documentary evidence related to the case facts have been comprehensively collected.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 对在勘验、检查、搜查中发现与案件事实可能有关联的血迹、指纹、足迹、字迹、毛发、体液、人体组织等痕迹和物品应当提取而没有提取，应当检验而没有检验，导致案件事实存疑的，人民法院应当向人民检察院说明情况，人民检察院依法可以补充收集、调取证据，作出合理的说明或者退回侦查机关补充侦查，调取有关证据。

**Article 7** Where traces and items that may be related to the case facts, such as bloodstains, fingerprints, footprints, handwriting, hair, bodily fluids, and human tissues, are discovered during inspection, examination, or search but are not extracted when they should be, or are not examined when they should be, thereby causing doubts about the case facts, the People's Court shall explain the circumstances to the People's Procuratorate. The People's Procuratorate may, according to law, supplement the collection or obtain evidence, provide a reasonable explanation, or return the case to the investigative authority for supplementary investigation to obtain relevant evidence.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 据以定案的物证应当是原物。只有在原物不便搬运、不易保存或者依法应当由有关部门保管、处理或者依法应当返还时，才可以拍摄或者制作足以反映原物外形或者内容的照片、录像或者复制品。物证的照片、录像或者复制品，经与原物核实无误或者经鉴定证明为真实的，或者以其他方式确能证明其真实的，可以作为定案的根据。原物的照片、录像或者复制品，不能反映原物的外形和特征的，不能作为定案的根据。  
据以定案的书证应当是原件。只有在取得原件确有困难时，才可以使用副本或者复制件。书证的副本、复制件，经与原件核实无误或者经鉴定证明为真实的，或者以其他方式确能证明其真实的，可以作为定案的根据。书证有更改或者更改迹象不能作出合理解释的，书证的副本、复制件不能反映书证原件及其内容的，不能作为定案的根据。

**Article 8** Physical evidence used as the basis for final decision shall be the original object. Only when the original object is inconvenient to transport, is not easy to preserve, or should be kept or handled by relevant authorities according to law, or should be returned according to law, may photographs, videos, or reproductions that sufficiently reflect the appearance or content of the original object be taken or made. Photographs, videos, or reproductions of physical evidence, if verified as correct with the original object or proven to be authentic through expert evaluation, or otherwise reliably proven to be authentic, may serve as the basis for final decision. If photographs, videos, or reproductions of the original object cannot reflect the appearance and characteristics of the original object, they shall not serve as the basis for final decision.  
Documentary evidence used as the basis for final decision shall be the original document. Only when there is genuine difficulty in obtaining the original document may copies or reproductions be used. Copies or reproductions of documentary evidence, if verified as correct with the original document or proven to be authentic through expert evaluation, or otherwise reliably proven to be authentic, may serve as the basis for final decision. If a documentary document has alterations or signs of alteration that cannot be reasonably explained, or if copies or reproductions of the documentary evidence cannot reflect the original document and its content, they shall not serve as the basis for final decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 经勘验、检查、搜查提取、扣押的物证、书证，未附有勘验、检查笔录，搜查笔录，提取笔录，扣押清单，不能证明物证、书证来源的，不能作为定案的根据。  
物证、书证的收集程序、方式存在下列瑕疵，通过有关办案人员的补正或者作出合理解释的，可以采用：  
（一）收集调取的物证、书证，在勘验、检查笔录，搜查笔录，提取笔录，扣押清单上没有侦查人员、物品持有人、见证人签名或者物品特征、数量、质量、名称等注明不详的；  
（二）收集调取物证照片、录像或者复制品，书证的副本、复制件未注明与原件核对无异，无复制时间、无被收集、调取人（单位）签名（盖章）的；  
（三）物证照片、录像或者复制品，书证的副本、复制件没有制作人关于制作过程及原物、原件存放于何处的说明或者说明中无签名的；  
（四）物证、书证的收集程序、方式存在其他瑕疵的。  
对物证、书证的来源及收集过程有疑问，不能作出合理解释的，该物证、书证不能作为定案的根据。

**Article 9** Where physical evidence or documentary evidence obtained through inspection, examination, search, extraction, or seizure is not accompanied by inspection or examination records, search records, extraction records, or seizure lists, and the source of the physical evidence or documentary evidence cannot be proved, it shall not serve as the basis for final decision.  
If the collection procedures or methods of physical evidence or documentary evidence have the following defects, they may be adopted after rectification or reasonable explanation by the relevant case-handling personnel:  
(1) The collected physical evidence or documentary evidence has no signature of the investigator, holder of the item, or witness on the inspection or examination record, search record, extraction record, or seizure list, or the characteristics, quantity, quality, name, etc. of the item are not clearly indicated;  
(2) For collected photographs, videos, or reproductions of physical evidence, or copies or reproductions of documentary evidence, it is not indicated that they are identical to the original after verification, there is no reproduction time, and no signature (seal) of the person (unit) who collected or obtained them;  
(3) For photographs, videos, or reproductions of physical evidence, or copies or reproductions of documentary evidence, there is no explanation by the producer about the production process and the location of the original object or original document, or the explanation is unsigned;  
(4) Other defects exist in the collection procedures or methods of the physical evidence or documentary evidence.  
If there are doubts about the source and collection process of physical evidence or documentary evidence that cannot be reasonably explained, such physical evidence or documentary evidence shall not serve as the basis for final decision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 具备辨认条件的物证、书证应当交由当事人或者证人进行辨认，必要时应当进行鉴定。  
2.证人证言

**Article 10** Physical evidence and documentary evidence that can be identified shall be submitted to the parties or witnesses for identification, and where necessary, expert evaluation shall be conducted.  
2. Witness Testimony

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 对证人证言应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）证言的内容是否为证人直接感知。  
（二）证人作证时的年龄、认知水平、记忆能力和表达能力，生理上和精神上的状态是否影响作证。  
（三）证人与案件当事人、案件处理结果有无利害关系。  
（四）证言的取得程序、方式是否符合法律及有关规定：有无使用暴力、威胁、引诱、欺骗以及其他非法手段取证的情形；有无违反询问证人应当个别进行的规定；笔录是否经证人核对确认并签名（盖章）、捺指印；询问未成年证人，是否通知了其法定代理人到场，其法定代理人是否在场等。  
（五）证人证言之间以及与其他证据之间能否相互印证，有无矛盾。

**Article 11** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for witness testimony:  
(1) Whether the content of the testimony was directly perceived by the witness.  
(2) Whether the age, cognitive level, memory ability, and expression ability of the witness at the time of testifying, as well as their physiological and mental state, affect the testimony.  
(3) Whether the witness has any interest in the parties or the outcome of the case.  
(4) Whether the procedures and methods for obtaining the testimony comply with laws and relevant provisions: whether there is any use of violence, threats, inducement, deception, or other illegal means to obtain testimony; whether the requirement that witnesses be questioned individually has been violated; whether the record has been checked and confirmed by the witness and signed (sealed) or fingerprinted; when questioning a minor witness, whether the legal representative was notified to be present and whether the legal representative was actually present.  
(5) Whether the witness testimony and other evidence corroborate each other and whether there are contradictions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 以暴力、威胁等非法手段取得的证人证言，不能作为定案的根据。  
处于明显醉酒、麻醉品中毒或者精神药物麻醉状态，以致不能正确表达的证人所提供的证言，不能作为定案的根据。  
证人的猜测性、评论性、推断性的证言，不能作为证据使用，但根据一般生活经验判断符合事实的除外。

**Article 12** Witness testimony obtained by illegal means such as violence or threats shall not serve as the basis for final decision.  
Testimony provided by a witness who is in a state of obvious intoxication, narcotic poisoning, or mental drug anesthesia such that they cannot correctly express themselves shall not serve as the basis for final decision.  
Speculative, evaluative, or inferential testimony of a witness shall not be used as evidence, except where it is consistent with facts based on general life experience.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 具有下列情形之一的证人证言，不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）询问证人没有个别进行而取得的证言；  
（二）没有经证人核对确认并签名（盖章）、捺指印的书面证言；  
（三）询问聋哑人或者不通晓当地通用语言、文字的少数民族人员、外国人，应当提供翻译而未提供的。

**Article 13** In any of the following circumstances, witness testimony shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) Testimony obtained without questioning witnesses individually;  
(2) Written testimony that has not been checked and confirmed by the witness and signed (sealed) or fingerprinted;  
(3) When questioning a deaf-mute person or a person of an ethnic minority or a foreigner who does not understand the local commonly used language or script, where interpretation should have been provided but was not.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 证人证言的收集程序和方式有下列瑕疵，通过有关办案人员的补正或者作出合理解释的，可以采用：  
（一）没有填写询问人、记录人、法定代理人姓名或者询问的起止时间、地点的；  
（二）询问证人的地点不符合规定的；  
（三）询问笔录没有记录告知证人应当如实提供证言和有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证要负法律责任内容的；  
（四）询问笔录反映出在同一时间段内，同一询问人员询问不同证人的。

**Article 14** If the procedures and methods for collecting witness testimony have the following defects, they may be adopted after rectification or reasonable explanation by the relevant case-handling personnel:  
(1) The names of the questioner, recorder, or legal representative, or the start and end times and location of the questioning, are not filled in;  
(2) The location where the witness was questioned does not comply with regulations;  
(3) The questioning record does not record the notification that the witness shall truthfully provide testimony and that intentionally giving false testimony or concealing criminal evidence shall bear legal liability;  
(4) The questioning record reflects that the same questioner questioned different witnesses within the same time period.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 具有下列情形的证人，人民法院应当通知出庭作证；经依法通知不出庭作证证人的书面证言经质证无法确认的，不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人对证人证言有异议，该证人证言对定罪量刑有重大影响的；  
（二）人民法院认为其他应当出庭作证的。  
证人在法庭上的证言与其庭前证言相互矛盾，如果证人当庭能够对其翻证作出合理解释，并有相关证据印证的，应当采信庭审证言。  
对未出庭作证证人的书面证言，应当听取出庭检察人员、被告人及其辩护人的意见，并结合其他证据综合判断。未出庭作证证人的书面证言出现矛盾，不能排除矛盾且无证据印证的，不能作为定案的根据。

**Article 15** A witness under any of the following circumstances shall be notified by the People's Court to appear in court to testify; if the witness, after being notified according to law, fails to appear in court and the written testimony cannot be confirmed through cross-examination, such written testimony shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) The People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or the defender raises objections to the witness testimony, and the witness testimony has a significant impact on conviction and sentencing;  
(2) The People's Court considers that there are other circumstances where the witness should appear in court to testify.  
If the testimony of a witness in court contradicts their pre-court testimony, if the witness can reasonably explain their change of testimony in court and there is corroborating evidence, the testimony in court shall be adopted.  
For written testimony of a witness who does not appear in court, the opinions of the procurator present in court, the defendant, and the defender shall be heard, and a comprehensive judgment shall be made in combination with other evidence. If contradictions arise in the written testimony of a witness who does not appear in court, and the contradictions cannot be resolved and there is no corroborating evidence, such written testimony shall not serve as the basis for final decision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 证人作证，涉及国家秘密或者个人隐私的，应当保守秘密。  
证人出庭作证，必要时，人民法院可以采取限制公开证人信息、限制询问、遮蔽容貌、改变声音等保护性措施。  
3.被害人陈述

**Article 16** When a witness testifies, if state secrets or personal privacy are involved, the witness shall maintain confidentiality.  
When a witness appears in court to testify, if necessary, the People's Court may take protective measures such as restricting the disclosure of the witness's information, restricting questioning, concealing the witness's appearance, or altering the witness's voice.  
3. Victim's Statements

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 对被害人陈述的审查与认定适用前述关于证人证言的有关规定。  
4.被告人供述和辩解

**Article 17** The examination and determination of victim's statements shall apply the relevant provisions on witness testimony mentioned above.  
4. Defendant's Confessions and Justifications

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 对被告人供述和辩解应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）讯问的时间、地点、讯问人的身份等是否符合法律及有关规定，讯问被告人的侦查人员是否不少于二人，讯问被告人是否个别进行等。  
（二）讯问笔录的制作、修改是否符合法律及有关规定，讯问笔录是否注明讯问的起止时间和讯问地点，首次讯问时是否告知被告人申请回避、聘请律师等诉讼权利，被告人是否核对确认并签名（盖章）、捺指印，是否有不少于二人的讯问人签名等。  
（三）讯问聋哑人、少数民族人员、外国人时是否提供了通晓聋、哑手势的人员或者翻译人员，讯问未成年同案犯时，是否通知了其法定代理人到场，其法定代理人是否在场。  
（四）被告人的供述有无以刑讯逼供等非法手段获取的情形，必要时可以调取被告人进出看守所的健康检查记录、笔录。  
（五）被告人的供述是否前后一致，有无反复以及出现反复的原因；被告人的所有供述和辩解是否均已收集入卷；应当入卷的供述和辩解没有入卷的，是否出具了相关说明。  
（六）被告人的辩解内容是否符合案情和常理，有无矛盾。  
（七）被告人的供述和辩解与同案犯的供述和辩解以及其他证据能否相互印证，有无矛盾。  
对于上述内容，侦查机关随案移送有录音录像资料的，应当结合相关录音录像资料进行审查。

**Article 18** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for the defendant's confessions and justifications:  
(1) Whether the time, place, and identity of the interrogator comply with laws and relevant provisions; whether the number of investigators interrogating the defendant is not less than two; whether the defendant is interrogated individually.  
(2) Whether the preparation and modification of the interrogation record comply with laws and relevant provisions; whether the interrogation record indicates the start and end times and location of the interrogation; whether the defendant was informed of procedural rights such as the right to apply for withdrawal and the right to retain a lawyer at the first interrogation; whether the defendant checked and confirmed the record and signed (sealed) or fingerprinted it; whether there are signatures of at least two interrogators.  
(3) When interrogating a deaf-mute person, a person of an ethnic minority, or a foreigner, whether a person proficient in sign language or an interpreter was provided; when interrogating a minor co-defendant, whether the legal representative was notified to be present and whether the legal representative was actually present.  
(4) Whether the defendant's confession was obtained by illegal means such as extorting confessions by torture; where necessary, the health examination records and records of the defendant's entry and exit from the detention center may be obtained.  
(5) Whether the defendant's confessions are consistent over time, whether there are inconsistencies, and the reasons for such inconsistencies; whether all confessions and justifications of the defendant have been collected and placed in the case file; if confessions and justifications that should be in the file are not included, whether relevant explanations have been provided.  
(6) Whether the content of the defendant's justifications is consistent with the case facts and common sense, and whether there are contradictions.  
(7) Whether the defendant's confessions and justifications corroborate the confessions and justifications of co-defendants and other evidence, and whether there are contradictions.  
For the above content, if the investigative authority has submitted audio and video recordings along with the case, the examination shall be conducted in conjunction with the relevant audio and video recordings.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 采用刑讯逼供等非法手段取得的被告人供述，不能作为定案的根据。

**Article 19** The defendant's confessions obtained by illegal means such as extortion of confessions by torture shall not serve as the basis for final decision.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 具有下列情形之一的被告人供述，不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）讯问笔录没有经被告人核对确认并签名（盖章）、捺指印的；  
（二）讯问聋哑人、不通晓当地通用语言、文字的人员时，应当提供通晓聋、哑手势的人员或者翻译人员而未提供的。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, the defendant's confessions shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) The interrogation record has not been checked and confirmed by the defendant and signed (sealed) or fingerprinted;  
(2) When interrogating a deaf-mute person or a person who does not understand the local commonly used language or script, a person proficient in sign language or an interpreter should have been provided but was not.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 讯问笔录有下列瑕疵，通过有关办案人员的补正或者作出合理解释的，可以采用：  
（一）笔录填写的讯问时间、讯问人、记录人、法定代理人等有误或者存在矛盾的；  
（二）讯问人没有签名的；  
（三）首次讯问笔录没有记录告知被讯问人诉讼权利内容的。

**Article 21** If the interrogation record has the following defects, it may be adopted after rectification or reasonable explanation by the relevant case-handling personnel:  
(1) The interrogation time, interrogator, recorder, legal representative, etc. filled in the record are erroneous or contradictory;  
(2) The interrogator did not sign;  
(3) The first interrogation record does not record that the interrogated person was informed of their procedural rights.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 对被告人供述和辩解的审查，应当结合控辩双方提供的所有证据以及被告人本人的全部供述和辩解进行。  
被告人庭前供述一致，庭审中翻供，但被告人不能合理说明翻供理由或者其辩解与全案证据相矛盾，而庭前供述与其他证据能够相互印证的，可以采信被告人庭前供述。  
被告人庭前供述和辩解出现反复，但庭审中供认的，且庭审中的供述与其他证据能够印证的，可以采信庭审中的供述；被告人庭前供述和辩解出现反复，庭审中不供认，且无其他证据与庭前供述印证的，不能采信庭前供述。  
5.鉴定意见

**Article 22** The examination of the defendant's confessions and justifications shall be conducted in conjunction with all evidence provided by the prosecution and defense, as well as all of the defendant's own confessions and justifications.  
If the defendant's pre-court confessions are consistent but the defendant changes their statement in court, and the defendant cannot reasonably explain the reason for changing the statement or their justification contradicts all the evidence in the case, while the pre-court confessions and other evidence can corroborate each other, the defendant's pre-court confessions may be adopted.  
If the defendant's pre-court confessions and justifications are inconsistent, but the defendant confesses in court, and the confession in court can be corroborated by other evidence, the confession in court may be adopted; if the defendant's pre-court confessions and justifications are inconsistent, and the defendant does not confess in court, and there is no other evidence to corroborate the pre-court confessions, the pre-court confessions shall not be adopted.  
5. Expert Opinions

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 对鉴定意见应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）鉴定人是否存在应当回避而未回避的情形。  
（二）鉴定机构和鉴定人是否具有合法的资质。  
（三）鉴定程序是否符合法律及有关规定。  
（四）检材的来源、取得、保管、送检是否符合法律及有关规定，与相关提取笔录、扣押物品清单等记载的内容是否相符，检材是否充足、可靠。  
（五）鉴定的程序、方法、分析过程是否符合本专业的检验鉴定规程和技术方法要求。  
（六）鉴定意见的形式要件是否完备，是否注明提起鉴定的事由、鉴定委托人、鉴定机构、鉴定要求、鉴定过程、检验方法、鉴定文书的日期等相关内容，是否由鉴定机构加盖鉴定专用章并由鉴定人签名盖章。  
（七）鉴定意见是否明确。  
（八）鉴定意见与案件待证事实有无关联。  
（九）鉴定意见与其他证据之间是否有矛盾，鉴定意见与检验笔录及相关照片是否有矛盾。  
（十）鉴定意见是否依法及时告知相关人员，当事人对鉴定意见是否有异议。

**Article 23** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for expert opinions:  
(1) Whether there are circumstances where the expert should have withdrawn but did not.  
(2) Whether the expert institution and the expert have lawful qualifications.  
(3) Whether the expert evaluation procedure complies with laws and relevant provisions.  
(4) Whether the source, acquisition, storage, and submission of the sample comply with laws and relevant provisions; whether they are consistent with the contents recorded in relevant extraction records, seizure lists, etc.; whether the sample is sufficient and reliable.  
(5) Whether the evaluation procedure, method, and analysis process comply with the inspection and evaluation procedures and technical method requirements of the profession.  
(6) Whether the formal requirements of the expert opinion are complete; whether the reasons for initiating the evaluation, the evaluation client, the evaluation institution, the evaluation requirements, the evaluation process, the inspection method, the date of the evaluation document, and other relevant contents are indicated; whether the evaluation institution affixed its special evaluation seal and the expert signed and sealed.  
(7) Whether the expert opinion is clear.  
(8) Whether the expert opinion is related to the facts to be proved in the case.  
(9) Whether there are contradictions between the expert opinion and other evidence, or between the expert opinion and the inspection records and relevant photographs.  
(10) Whether the expert opinion was timely notified to the relevant persons according to law, and whether the parties have objections to the expert opinion.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 鉴定意见具有下列情形之一的，不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）鉴定机构不具备法定的资格和条件，或者鉴定事项超出本鉴定机构项目范围或者鉴定能力的；  
（二）鉴定人不具备法定的资格和条件、鉴定人不具有相关专业技术或者职称、鉴定人违反回避规定的；  
（三）鉴定程序、方法有错误的；  
（四）鉴定意见与证明对象没有关联的；  
（五）鉴定对象与送检材料、样本不一致的；  
（六）送检材料、样本来源不明或者确实被污染且不具备鉴定条件的；  
（七）违反有关鉴定特定标准的；  
（八）鉴定文书缺少签名、盖章的；  
（九）其他违反有关规定的情形。  
对鉴定意见有疑问的，人民法院应当依法通知鉴定人出庭作证或者由其出具相关说明，也可以依法补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。  
6.勘验、检查笔录

**Article 24** In any of the following circumstances, the expert opinion shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) The expert institution does not have statutory qualifications or conditions, or the evaluation matter exceeds the project scope or evaluation capability of the institution;  
(2) The expert does not have statutory qualifications or conditions, the expert does not have the relevant professional expertise or title, or the expert violates the withdrawal provisions;  
(3) There are errors in the evaluation procedure or method;  
(4) The expert opinion is not related to the object of proof;  
(5) The evaluation object is inconsistent with the submitted material or sample;  
(6) The source of the submitted material or sample is unknown, or it is indeed contaminated and does not meet the evaluation conditions;  
(7) The evaluation violates specific standards for such evaluations;  
(8) The evaluation document lacks a signature or seal;  
(9) Other circumstances violating relevant provisions.  
If there are doubts about the expert opinion, the People's Court shall, according to law, notify the expert to appear in court to testify or provide relevant explanations, and may also, according to law, conduct supplementary evaluation or re-evaluation.  
6. Inspection and Examination Records

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 对勘验、检查  
笔录应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）勘验、检查是否依法进行，笔录的制作是否符合法律及有关规定的要求，勘验、检查人员和见证人是否签名或者盖章等。  
（二）勘验、检查笔录的内容是否全面、详细、准确、规范：是否准确记录了提起勘验、检查的事由，勘验、检查的时间、地点，在场人员、现场方位、周围环境等情况；是否准确记载了现场、物品、人身、尸体等的位置、特征等详细情况以及勘验、检查、搜查的过程；文字记载与实物或者绘图、录像、照片是否相符；固定证据的形式、方法是否科学、规范；现场、物品、痕迹等是否被破坏或者伪造，是否是原始现场；人身特征、伤害情况、生理状况有无伪装或者变化等。  
（三）补充进行勘验、检查的，前后勘验、检查的情况是否有矛盾，是否说明了再次勘验、检查的原由。  
（四）勘验、检查笔录中记载的情况与被告人供述、被害人陈述、鉴定意见等其他证据能否印证，有无矛盾。

**Article 25** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for inspection and examination records:  
(1) Whether the inspection or examination was conducted according to law, whether the preparation of the record complies with legal and relevant requirements, and whether the inspectors, examiners, and witnesses signed or sealed.  
(2) Whether the content of the inspection or examination record is comprehensive, detailed, accurate, and standardized: whether it accurately records the reasons for initiating the inspection or examination, the time and place of the inspection or examination, the persons present, the location and surrounding environment, etc.; whether it accurately records the location, characteristics, and other details of the scene, items, persons, bodies, etc., and the process of inspection, examination, and search; whether the written description is consistent with the actual objects or drawings, videos, or photographs; whether the form and method of fixing evidence are scientific and standardized; whether the scene, items, traces, etc. have been damaged or forged, and whether it is the original scene; whether the physical characteristics, injuries, or physiological conditions have been disguised or changed.  
(3) If supplementary inspection or examination is conducted, whether there are contradictions between the previous and subsequent inspections or examinations, and whether the reason for the re-inspection or re-examination is explained.  
(4) Whether the circumstances recorded in the inspection or examination record can be corroborated by the defendant's confessions, victim's statements, expert opinions, and other evidence, and whether there are contradictions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 勘验、检查笔录存在明显不符合法律及有关规定的情形，并且不能作出合理解释或者说明的，不能作为证据使用。  
勘验、检查笔录存在勘验、检查没有见证人的，勘验、检查人员和见证人没有签名、盖章的，勘验、检查人员违反回避规定的等情形，应当结合案件其他证据，审查其真实性和关联性。  
7.视听资料

**Article 26** If an inspection or examination record obviously does not comply with laws and relevant provisions and cannot be reasonably explained or accounted for, it shall not be used as evidence.  
Where an inspection or examination record has circumstances such as the absence of witnesses at the inspection or examination, the lack of signatures or seals of the inspectors, examiners, and witnesses, or the inspectors or examiners violating withdrawal provisions, the authenticity and relevance shall be examined in conjunction with other evidence in the case.  
7. Audio-Visual Materials

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 对视听资料应当着重审查以下内容：  
（一）视听资料的来源是否合法，制作过程中当事人有无受到威胁、引诱等违反法律及有关规定的情形；  
（二）是否载明制作人或者持有人的身份，制作的时间、地点和条件以及制作方法；  
（三）是否为原件，有无复制及复制份数；调取的视听资料是复制件的，是否附有无法调取原件的原因、制作过程和原件存放地点的说明，是否有制作人和原视听资料持有人签名或者盖章；  
（四）内容和制作过程是否真实，有无经过剪辑、增加、删改、编辑等伪造、变造情形；  
（五）内容与案件事实有无关联性。  
对视听资料有疑问的，应当进行鉴定。  
对视听资料，应当结合案件其他证据，审查其真实性和关联性。

**Article 27** The following contents shall be emphatically examined for audio-visual materials:  
(1) Whether the source of the audio-visual material is lawful, and whether the parties were subjected to threats, inducements, or other acts violating laws and relevant provisions during the production process;  
(2) Whether the identity of the producer or holder, the time, place, and conditions of production, and the production method are indicated;  
(3) Whether it is the original, whether there are copies and the number of copies; if the obtained audio-visual material is a copy, whether it is accompanied by an explanation of the reasons why the original could not be obtained, the production process, and the location of the original, and whether the producer and the original holder of the audio-visual material signed or sealed;  
(4) Whether the content and production process are authentic, and whether there are any forgery or alteration situations such as editing, adding, deleting, or modifying;  
(5) Whether the content is related to the case facts.  
If there are doubts about the audio-visual material, an expert evaluation shall be conducted.  
For audio-visual materials, their authenticity and relevance shall be examined in conjunction with other evidence in the case.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 具有下列情形之一的视听资料，不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）视听资料经审查或者鉴定无法确定真伪的；  
（二）对视听资料的制作和取得的时间、地点、方式等有异议，不能作出合理解释或者提供必要证明的。  
8.其他规定

**Article 28** In any of the following circumstances, audio-visual materials shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) The authenticity of the audio-visual material cannot be determined after examination or expert evaluation;  
(2) There are objections to the time, place, method, etc. of the production and acquisition of the audio-visual material, and a reasonable explanation cannot be provided or necessary proof cannot be furnished.  
8. Other Provisions

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 对于电子邮件、电子数据交换、网上聊天记录、网络博客、手机短信、电子签名、域名等电子证据，应当主要审查以下内容：  
（一）该电子证据存储磁盘、存储光盘等可移动存储介质是否与打印件一并提交；  
（二）是否载明该电子证据形成的时间、地点、对象、制作人、制作过程及设备情况等；  
（三）制作、储存、传递、获得、收集、出示等程序和环节是否合法，取证人、制作人、持有人、见证人等是否签名或者盖章；  
（四）内容是否真实，有无剪裁、拼凑、篡改、添加等伪造、变造情形；  
（五）该电子证据与案件事实有无关联性。  
对电子证据有疑问的，应当进行鉴定。  
对电子证据，应当结合案件其他证据，审查其真实性和关联性。

**Article 29** For electronic evidence such as emails, electronic data interchange, online chat records, blogs, mobile phone text messages, electronic signatures, and domain names, the following contents shall be mainly examined:  
(1) Whether the storage media such as disks and CDs storing the electronic evidence are submitted together with the printed copy;  
(2) Whether the time, place, object, producer, production process, and equipment conditions of the electronic evidence formation are indicated;  
(3) Whether the procedures and links of production, storage, transmission, acquisition, collection, and presentation are lawful, and whether the evidence obtainer, producer, holder, witness, etc. have signed or sealed;  
(4) Whether the content is authentic and whether there are any forgery or alteration situations such as cutting, splicing, tampering, or adding;  
(5) Whether the electronic evidence is related to the case facts.  
If there are doubts about the electronic evidence, an expert evaluation shall be conducted.  
For electronic evidence, its authenticity and relevance shall be examined in conjunction with other evidence in the case.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 侦查机关组织的辨认，存在下列情形之一的，应当严格审查，不能确定其真实性的，辨认结果不能作为定案的根据：  
（一）辨认不是在侦查人员主持下进行的；  
（二）辨认前使辨认人见到辨认对象的；  
（三）辨认人的辨认活动没有个别进行的；  
（四）辨认对象没有混杂在具有类似特征的其他对象中，或者供辨认的对象数量不符合规定的；尸体、场所等特定辨认对象除外。  
（五）辨认中给辨认人明显暗示或者明显有指认嫌疑的。  
有下列情形之一的，通过有关办案人员的补正或者作出合理解释的，辨认结果可以作为证据使用：  
（一）主持辨认的侦查人员少于二人的；  
（二）没有向辨认人详细询问辨认对象的具体特征的；  
（三）对辨认经过和结果没有制作专门的规范的辨认笔录，或者辨认笔录没有侦查人员、辨认人、见证人的签名或者盖章的；  
（四）辨认记录过于简单，只有结果没有过程的；  
（五）案卷中只有辨认笔录，没有被辨认对象的照片、录像等资料，无法获悉辨认的真实情况的。

**Article 30** If an identification organized by an investigative authority has any of the following circumstances, it shall be strictly examined; if its authenticity cannot be determined, the identification result shall not serve as the basis for final decision:  
(1) The identification was not conducted under the auspices of investigators;  
(2) The identifier was allowed to see the identification object before the identification;  
(3) The identification activities of the identifier were not conducted individually;  
(4) The identification objects were not mixed with other objects having similar characteristics, or the number of objects for identification does not comply with regulations; except for specific identification objects such as corpses and locations.  
(5) Obvious suggestions were given to the identifier during the identification, or there was an obvious suspicion of pointing out.  
In any of the following circumstances, after rectification or reasonable explanation by the relevant case-handling personnel, the identification result may be used as evidence:  
(1) The number of investigators presiding over the identification was fewer than two;  
(2) The identifier was not questioned in detail about the specific characteristics of the identification object;  
(3) No special standardized identification record was made for the identification process and results, or the identification record was not signed or sealed by the investigators, identifier, or witnesses;  
(4) The identification record is too simple, only having results but no process;  
(5) Only the identification record is in the case file, without photographs, videos, or other materials of the identified objects, making it impossible to know the true circumstances of the identification.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 对侦查机关出具的破案经过等材料，应当审查是否有出具该说明材料的办案人、办案机关的签字或者盖章。  
对破案经过有疑问，或者对确定被告人有重大嫌疑的根据有疑问的，应当要求侦查机关补充说明。  
三、证据的综合审查和运用

**Article 31** For materials such as the case-solving process issued by the investigative authority, it shall be examined whether the case-handling personnel and the case-handling authority that issued the explanatory material have signed or sealed.  
If there are doubts about the case-solving process or the basis for determining that the defendant is a major suspect, the investigative authority shall be requested to provide supplementary explanations.  
III. Comprehensive Examination and Application of Evidence

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 对证据的证明力，应当结合案件的具体情况，从各证据与待证事实的关联程度、各证据之间的联系等方面进行审查判断。  
证据之间具有内在的联系，共同指向同一待证事实，且能合理排除矛盾的，才能作为定案的根据。

**Article 32** The probative value of evidence shall be examined and judged in light of the specific circumstances of the case, considering the degree of relevance of each piece of evidence to the facts to be proved and the connections between the pieces of evidence.  
Only when there is an inherent connection between pieces of evidence, all pointing to the same fact to be proved, and contradictions can be reasonably resolved, may they serve as the basis for final decision.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 没有直接证据证明犯罪行为系被告人实施，但同时符合下列条件的可以认定被告人有罪：  
（一）据以定案的间接证据已经查证属实；  
（二）据以定案的间接证据之间相互印证，不存在无法排除的矛盾和无法解释的疑问；  
（三）据以定案的间接证据已经形成完整的证明体系；  
（四）依据间接证据认定的案件事实，结论是唯一的，足以排除一切合理怀疑；  
（五）运用间接证据进行的推理符合逻辑和经验判断。  
根据间接证据定案的，判处死刑应当特别慎重。

**Article 33** If there is no direct evidence proving that the criminal act was committed by the defendant, but the following conditions are simultaneously met, the defendant may be found guilty:  
(1) The indirect evidence used for final decision has been verified as true;  
(2) The indirect evidence used for final decision corroborates each other, and there are no irresolvable contradictions or inexplicable doubts;  
(3) The indirect evidence used for final decision has formed a complete system of proof;  
(4) The conclusion of the case facts determined based on indirect evidence is the sole conclusion, sufficient to exclude all reasonable doubts;  
(5) The reasoning using indirect evidence conforms to logic and experience judgment.  
When final decision is based on indirect evidence, imposing the death penalty shall be particularly cautious.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 根据被告人的供述、指认提取到了隐蔽性很强的物证、书证，且与其他证明犯罪事实发生的证据互相印证，并排除串供、逼供、诱供等可能性的，可以认定有罪。

**Article 34** If, based on the defendant's confessions and identification, physical evidence or documentary evidence of a highly concealed nature is obtained, and such evidence corroborates other evidence proving the occurrence of the criminal act, and the possibility of collusion, extortion of confessions, or inducement is eliminated, the defendant may be found guilty.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 侦查机关依照有关规定采用特殊侦查措施所收集的物证、书证及其他证据材料，经法庭查证属实，可以作为定案的根据。  
法庭依法不公开特殊侦查措施的过程及方法。

**Article 35** Physical evidence, documentary evidence, and other evidentiary materials collected by investigative authorities using special investigative measures in accordance with relevant provisions may serve as the basis for final decision if they are verified as true in court.  
The court shall not disclose the process and methods of special investigative measures according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 在对被告人作出有罪认定后，人民法院认定被告人的量刑事实，除审查法定情节外，还应审查以下影响量刑的情节：  
（一）案件起因；  
（二）被害人有无过错及过错程度，是否对矛盾激化负有责任及责任大小；  
（三）被告人的近亲属是否协助抓获被告人；  
（四）被告人平时表现及有无悔罪态度；  
（五）被害人附带民事诉讼赔偿情况，被告人是否取得被害人或者被害人近亲属谅解；  
（六）其他影响量刑的情节。  
既有从轻、减轻处罚等情节，又有从重处罚等情节的，应当依法综合相关情节予以考虑。  
不能排除被告人具有从轻、减轻处罚等量刑情节的，判处死刑应当特别慎重。

**Article 36** After making a determination of guilt against the defendant, the People's Court, when determining the sentencing facts of the defendant, shall, in addition to examining statutory circumstances, also examine the following circumstances affecting sentencing:  
(1) The cause of the case;  
(2) Whether the victim was at fault and the degree of fault, and whether the victim bears responsibility for the escalation of the conflict and the extent of such responsibility;  
(3) Whether the defendant's close relatives assisted in arresting the defendant;  
(4) The defendant's usual behavior and whether the defendant shows remorse;  
(5) The situation of compensation in the victim's incidental civil action, and whether the defendant obtained forgiveness from the victim or the victim's close relatives;  
(6) Other circumstances affecting sentencing.  
If there are both circumstances for mitigated or reduced punishment and circumstances for severe punishment, the relevant circumstances shall be comprehensively considered according to law.  
If it cannot be excluded that the defendant has circumstances such as mitigated or reduced punishment, imposing the death penalty shall be particularly cautious.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 对于有下列情形的证据应当慎重使用，有其他证据印证的，可以采信：  
（一）生理上、精神上有缺陷的被害人、证人和被告人，在对案件事实的认知和表达上存在一定困难，但尚未丧失正确认知、正确表达能力而作的陈述、证言和供述；  
（二）与被告人有亲属关系或者其他密切关系的证人所作的对该被告人有利的证言，或者与被告人有利害冲突的证人所作的对该被告人不利的证言。

**Article 37** Evidence in the following circumstances shall be used cautiously; if there is corroborating evidence, it may be adopted:  
(1) Statements, testimonies, and confessions made by victims, witnesses, and defendants who have physiological or mental defects, which cause some difficulty in their cognition and expression of case facts, but who have not lost the ability to correctly cognize and express;  
(2) Testimony favorable to the defendant made by a witness who has a familial or other close relationship with the defendant, or testimony unfavorable to the defendant made by a witness who has a conflict of interest with the defendant.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 法庭对证据有疑问的，可以告知出庭检察人员、被告人及其辩护人补充证据或者作出说明；确有核实必要的，可以宣布休庭，对证据进行调查核实。法庭进行庭外调查时，必要时，可以通知出庭检察人员、辩护人到场。出庭检察人员、辩护人一方或者双方不到场的，法庭记录在案。  
人民检察院、辩护人补充的和法庭庭外调查核实取得的证据，法庭可以庭外征求出庭检察人员、辩护人的意见。双方意见不一致，有一方要求人民法院开庭进行调查的，人民法院应当开庭。

**Article 38** If the court has doubts about evidence, it may inform the procurator present in court, the defendant, and the defender to supplement evidence or provide explanations; if verification is truly necessary, the court may adjourn the hearing to investigate and verify the evidence. When the court conducts an out-of-court investigation, if necessary, it may notify the procurator present in court and the defender to be present. If one or both parties (the procurator present in court and the defender) are not present, the court shall record it in the file.  
For evidence supplemented by the People's Procuratorate or the defender, and evidence obtained through the court's out-of-court investigation and verification, the court may, out of court, seek the opinions of the procurator present in court and the defender. If the opinions of both parties are inconsistent and one party requests the court to open a hearing for investigation, the court shall open the hearing.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 被告人及其辩护人提出有自首的事实及理由，有关机关未予认定的，应当要求有关机关提供证明材料或者要求相关人员作证，并结合其他证据判断自首是否成立。  
被告人是否协助或者如何协助抓获同案犯的证明材料不全，导致无法认定被告人构成立功的，应当要求有关机关提供证明材料或者要求相关人员作证，并结合其他证据判断立功是否成立。  
被告人有检举揭发他人犯罪情形的，应当审查是否已经查证属实；尚未查证的，应当及时查证。  
被告人累犯的证明材料不全，应当要求有关机关提供证明材料。

**Article 39** If the defendant and the defender raise facts and reasons for voluntary surrender, but the relevant authority has not confirmed it, the relevant authority shall be requested to provide evidentiary materials or to request relevant persons to testify, and a judgment shall be made in conjunction with other evidence as to whether voluntary surrender is established.  
If the evidentiary materials on whether the defendant assisted or how the defendant assisted in arresting a co-defendant are incomplete, making it impossible to determine whether the defendant has performed meritorious service, the relevant authority shall be requested to provide evidentiary materials or to request relevant persons to testify, and a judgment shall be made in conjunction with other evidence as to whether meritorious service is established.  
If the defendant has reported others' criminal activities, it shall be examined whether such reports have been verified; if not yet verified, verification shall be conducted promptly.  
If the evidentiary materials on the defendant being a recidivist are incomplete, the relevant authority shall be requested to provide evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 审查被告人实施犯罪时是否已满十八周岁，一般应当以户籍证明为依据；对户籍证明有异议，并有经查证属实的出生证明文件、无利害关系人的证言等证据证明被告人不满十八周岁的，应认定被告人不满十八周岁；没有户籍证明以及出生证明文件的，应当根据人口普查登记、无利害关系人的证言等证据综合进行判断，必要时，可以进行骨龄鉴定，并将结果作为判断被告人年龄的参考。  
未排除证据之间的矛盾，无充分证据证明被告人实施被指控的犯罪时已满十八周岁且确实无法查明的，不能认定其已满十八周岁。

**Article 40** When examining whether the defendant had reached the age of eighteen at the time of committing the crime, a household registration certificate shall generally be used as the basis; if there is an objection to the household registration certificate and there is evidence such as a verified birth certificate or testimony from persons without interest that proves the defendant was under eighteen years old, the defendant shall be found to be under eighteen years old; if there is no household registration certificate or birth certificate, a comprehensive judgment shall be made based on evidence such as population census registration records and testimony from persons without interest; if necessary, a bone age assessment may be conducted, and the results shall be used as a reference for determining the defendant's age.  
If contradictions between evidence are not resolved, and there is insufficient evidence to prove that the defendant had reached the age of eighteen at the time of committing the alleged crime and it is indeed impossible to ascertain, the defendant shall not be found to have reached the age of eighteen.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 本规定自二O一O年七月一日起施行。

**Article 41** These Provisions shall come into force on July 1, 2010.
