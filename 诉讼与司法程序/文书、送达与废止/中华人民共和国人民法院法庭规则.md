### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为维护法庭秩序，保障审判活动的正常进行，根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》和其他有关法律的规定，制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the Organic Law of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws, in order to maintain courtroom order and ensure the normal conduct of adjudication activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院开庭审理案件时，合议庭的审判长或者独任审判的审判员主持法庭的审判活动，指挥司法警察维持法庭秩序。

**Article 2** When a People's Court holds a session to try a case, the presiding judge of the collegial panel or the sole judge shall preside over the adjudication activities of the court and direct the judicial police to maintain courtroom order.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　法庭正面应当悬挂国徽。

**Article 3** The national emblem shall be hung at the front of the courtroom.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　出庭的审判人员、书记员、公诉人或者抗诉人、司法警察应当按照规定着装；出庭的辩护人、诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人、勘验人、翻译人员和其他诉讼参与人应当衣着整洁。

**Article 4** The adjudicators, court clerks, public prosecutors or protestants, and judicial police who appear in court shall dress according to regulations; the defenders, litigation agents, witnesses, expert evaluators, inspectors, interpreters and other participants in proceedings who appear in court shall be neatly dressed.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　审判人员进入法庭和审判长或者独任审判员宣告法院判决时，全体人员应当起立。

**Article 5** When adjudicators enter the courtroom and when the presiding judge or the sole judge announces the court's judgment, all persons present shall stand up.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　审判人员应当严格按照法律规定的诉讼程序进行审判活动，保障诉讼参与人的诉讼权利。

**Article 6** Adjudicators shall strictly conduct adjudication activities in accordance with the litigation procedures prescribed by law, and shall safeguard the litigation rights of participants in proceedings.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　诉讼参与人应当遵守法庭规则，维护法庭秩序，不得喧哗、吵闹；发言、陈述和辩论，须经审判长或者独任审判员许可。

**Article 7** Participants in proceedings shall observe courtroom rules and maintain courtroom order, and shall not make noise or disturbances; any speeches, statements and debates shall be subject to the permission of the presiding judge or the sole judge.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　公开审理的案件，公民可以旁听；根据法庭场所和参加旁听人数等情况，需要时，持人民法院发出的旁听证进入法庭。  
下列人员不得旁听：  
（一）未成年人（经法院批准的除外）；  
（二）精神病人和醉酒的人；  
（三）其他不宜旁听的人；

**Article 8** Citizens may observe the trial of cases heard in public; when necessary, based on the courtroom premises and the number of observers, they shall enter the courtroom with an observer's permit issued by the People's Court.  
The following persons shall not observe:  
(1) Minors (unless approved by the court);  
(2) Persons with mental illness and persons who are intoxicated;  
(3) Other persons who are not suitable to observe;

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　旁听人员必须遵守下列纪律：  
（一）不得录音、录像和摄影；  
（二）不得随意走动和进入审判区；  
（三）不得发言、提问；  
（四）不得鼓掌、喧哗、哄闹和实施其他妨害审判活动的行为。

**Article 9** Observers must abide by the following discipline:  
(1) They shall not make audio recordings, video recordings, or take photographs;  
(2) They shall not walk around at will or enter the adjudication area;  
(3) They shall not speak or ask questions;  
(4) They shall not applaud, make noise, create disturbances, or engage in any other acts that impede adjudication activities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　新闻记者旁听应遵守本规则。未经审判长或者独任审判员许可，不得在庭审过程中录音、录像和摄影。

**Article 10** Journalists who observe shall comply with these Rules. Without the permission of the presiding judge or the sole judge, they shall not make audio recordings, video recordings, or take photographs during the court proceedings.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于违反法庭规则的人，审判长或者独任审判员可以口头警告、训诫，也可以没收录音、录像和摄影器材，责令退出法庭或者经院长批准予以罚款、拘留。

**Article 11** The presiding judge or the sole judge may give a verbal warning or admonition to any person who violates courtroom rules, and may also confiscate audio recording, video recording and photographic equipment, order them to leave the courtroom, or impose a fine or detention upon the approval of the president of the court.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对哄闹、冲击法庭，侮辱、诽谤、威胁、殴打审判人员等严重扰乱法庭秩序的人，依法追究刑事责任；  
情节较轻的，予以罚款、拘留。

**Article 12** Any person who makes disturbances, storms the courtroom, insults, slanders, threatens, or assaults adjudicators, or otherwise seriously disrupts courtroom order, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law;  
where the circumstances are relatively minor, a fine or detention shall be imposed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对违反法庭规则的人采取强制措施，由司法警察执行。

**Article 13** Compulsory measures against persons who violate courtroom rules shall be executed by the judicial police.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国人或者外国记者旁听，应当遵守本规则。

**Article 14** Foreign nationals or foreign journalists who observe shall comply with these Rules.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规则自一九九四年一月一日起施行，《中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则（试行）》同时废止。  
1993年12月1日

**Article 15** These Rules shall come into force as of January 1, 1994, and the Rules of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China on Courtroom Rules (for Trial Implementation) shall be abolished simultaneously.  
December 1, 1993
