# 最高人民法院关于修改《中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕7号  
**公布日期**：2015-12-21  
**生效日期**：2016-05-01

> 最高人民法院 《关于修改〈中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则〉的决定》已于2015年12月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1673次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年5月1日起施行。 2016年4月13日 最高人民法院 关于修改《中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则》的决定 法释〔2016〕7号 为了维护法庭安全，规范庭审秩序，保障诉讼参与人诉讼权利，方便公众旁听，促进司法公正，彰显司法权威，根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实际，现决定对《中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则》作如下修改： 一、将第一条修改为：“为了维护法庭安全和秩序，保障庭审活动正常进行，保障诉讼参与人依法行使诉讼权利，方便公众旁听，促进司法公正，彰显司法权威，根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关法律规定，制定本规则。” 二、删除第二条，将相关内容调整到第十七条、第二十一条。 三、将第三条改为第二条，修改为：“法庭是人民法院代表国家依法审判各类案件的专门场所。 “法庭正面上方应当悬挂国徽。” 四、将第四条改为第十二条，修改为：“出庭履行职务的人员，按照职业着装规定着装。但是，具有下列情形之一的，着正装： “（一）没有职业着装规定； “（二）侦查人员出庭作证； “（三）所在单位系案件当事人。 “非履行职务的出庭人员及旁听人员，应当文明着装。”

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条    为了维护法庭安全和秩序，保障庭审活动正常进行，保障诉讼参与人依法行使诉讼权利，方便公众旁听，促进司法公正，彰显司法权威，根据《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关法律规定，制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the Organic Law of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China, the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant laws, for the purposes of maintaining court security and order, ensuring the normal conduct of court proceedings, protecting the litigation participants' exercise of their litigation rights according to law, facilitating public observation, promoting judicial fairness, and upholding judicial authority.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条    法庭是人民法院代表国家依法审判各类案件的专门场所。  
法庭正面上方应当悬挂国徽。

**Article 2** A courtroom is a special venue where the People's Courts, on behalf of the State, adjudicate various types of cases according to law.  
The national emblem shall be hung above the front of the courtroom.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条    法庭分设审判活动区和旁听区，两区以栏杆等进行隔离。  
审理未成年人案件的法庭应当根据未成年人身心发展特点设置区域和席位。  
有新闻媒体旁听或报道庭审活动时，旁听区可以设置专门的媒体记者席。

**Article 3** A courtroom shall be divided into an adjudication activity area and an observation area, which shall be separated by railings or other means.  
Courtrooms for trials of cases involving minors shall have areas and seats arranged according to the physical and mental development characteristics of minors.  
Where news media observe or report court proceedings, a special media seating area may be set up in the observation area.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    刑事法庭可以配置同步视频作证室，供依法应当保护或其他确有保护必要的证人、鉴定人、被害人在庭审作证时使用。

**Article 4** Criminal courtrooms may be equipped with synchronous video testimony rooms for use by witnesses, expert evaluators, and victims who shall be protected according to law or for whom protection is indeed necessary when testifying in court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条    法庭应当设置残疾人无障碍设施；根据需要配备合议庭合议室，检察人员、律师及其他诉讼参与人休息室，被告人羁押室等附属场所。

**Article 5** Courtrooms shall be equipped with barrier-free facilities for persons with disabilities; and, as needed, shall be provided with ancillary premises such as a collegial panel deliberation room, rest rooms for procuratorial personnel, lawyers and other litigation participants, and a custodial room for the defendant.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进入法庭的人员应当出示有效身份证件，并接受人身及携带物品的安全检查。  
持有效工作证件和出庭通知履行职务的检察人员、律师可以通过专门通道进入法庭。需要安全检查的，人民法院对检察人员和律师平等对待。

**Article 6** Persons entering a courtroom shall present valid identity documents and undergo security checks of their persons and belongings.  
Procuratorial personnel and lawyers who hold valid work credentials and notice of appearance to perform their duties may enter the courtroom through a special passage. Where security checks are required, the People's Court shall treat procuratorial personnel and lawyers equally.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条    除经人民法院许可，需要在法庭上出示的证据外，下列物品不得携带进入法庭：  
（一）枪支、弹药、管制刀具以及其他具有杀伤力的器具；  
（二）易燃易爆物、疑似爆炸物；  
（三）放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性、强气味性物质以及传染病病原体；  
（四）液体及胶状、粉末状物品；  
（五）标语、条幅、传单；  
（六）其他可能危害法庭安全或妨害法庭秩序的物品。

**Article 7** Except for evidence that, with the permission of the People's Court, needs to be produced in court, the following items shall not be brought into the courtroom:  
(1) firearms, ammunition, controlled knives, and other lethal instruments;  
(2) flammable and explosive substances, suspected explosives;  
(3) radioactive, toxic, corrosive, or strong-odor substances, and infectious disease pathogens;  
(4) liquids, gels, and powders;  
(5) slogans, banners, and leaflets;  
(6) other items that may endanger court security or disrupt court order.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条    人民法院应当通过官方网站、电子显示屏、公告栏等向公众公开各法庭的编号、具体位置以及旁听席位数量等信息。

**Article 8** The People's Court shall make public to the public, through its official website, electronic display screens, bulletin boards, and other means, information such as the numbers, specific locations, and the number of observation seats of each courtroom.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条    公开的庭审活动，公民可以旁听。  
旁听席位不能满足需要时，人民法院可以根据申请的先后顺序或者通过抽签、摇号等方式发放旁听证，但应当优先安排当事人的近亲属或其他与案件有利害关系的人旁听。  
下列人员不得旁听：  
（一）证人、鉴定人以及准备出庭提出意见的有专门知识的人；  
（二）未获得人民法院批准的未成年人；  
（三）拒绝接受安全检查的人；  
（四）醉酒的人、精神病人或其他精神状态异常的人；  
（五）其他有可能危害法庭安全或妨害法庭秩序的人。  
依法有可能封存犯罪记录的公开庭审活动，任何单位或个人不得组织人员旁听。  
依法不公开的庭审活动，除法律另有规定外，任何人不得旁听。

**Article 9** Citizens may observe open court proceedings.  
Where observation seats are insufficient, the People's Court may issue observation passes based on the order of application or through methods such as drawing lots or ballot, but shall give priority to the near relatives of the parties or other persons with an interest in the case.  
The following persons shall not observe:  
(1) witnesses, expert evaluators, and persons with specialized knowledge who are preparing to appear in court to give opinions;  
(2) minors who have not obtained the approval of the People's Court;  
(3) persons who refuse to undergo security checks;  
(4) intoxicated persons, mentally ill persons, or persons with other abnormal mental conditions;  
(5) other persons who may endanger court security or disrupt court order.  
For open court proceedings where criminal records may be sealed according to law, no unit or individual shall organize persons to observe.  
For court proceedings that are not open according to law, no person shall observe, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条    人民法院应当对庭审活动进行全程录像或录音。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall make a full video or audio recording of court proceedings.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条    依法公开进行的庭审活动，具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以通过电视、互联网或其他公共媒体进行图文、音频、视频直播或录播：  
（一）公众关注度较高；  
（二）社会影响较大；  
（三）法治宣传教育意义较强。

**Article 11** Where an open court proceeding held according to law falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may conduct live or recorded broadcast in text, audio, or video through television, the Internet, or other public media:  
(1) relatively high public attention;  
(2) relatively great social impact;  
(3) relatively strong significance for legal education.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条    出庭履行职务的人员，按照职业着装规定着装。但是，具有下列情形之一的，着正装：  
（一）没有职业着装规定；  
（二）侦查人员出庭作证；  
（三）所在单位系案件当事人。  
非履行职务的出庭人员及旁听人员，应当文明着装。

**Article 12** Persons appearing in court to perform their duties shall dress according to the occupational dress code. However, under any of the following circumstances, they shall wear formal attire:  
(1) there is no occupational dress code;  
(2) investigation personnel appear in court to testify;  
(3) their employing unit is a party to the case.  
Non-duty persons appearing in court and observers shall dress in a civilized manner.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条    刑事在押被告人或上诉人出庭受审时，着正装或便装，不着监管机构的识别服。  
人民法院在庭审活动中不得对被告人或上诉人使用戒具，但认为其人身危险性大，可能危害法庭安全的除外。

**Article 13** When a criminal defendant in custody or an appellant appears in court for trial, he or she shall wear formal attire or casual clothes, and shall not wear the identification uniform of the custodial institution.  
The People's Court shall not use restraints on the defendant or appellant during court proceedings, unless it considers the person to be of great personal danger and likely to endanger court security.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条    庭审活动开始前，书记员应当宣布[本规则第十七条](#art-17)规定的法庭纪律。

**Article 14** Before court proceedings begin, the court clerk shall announce the courtroom discipline prescribed in Article 17 of these Rules.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条    审判人员进入法庭以及审判长或独任审判员宣告判决、裁定、决定时，全体人员应当起立。

**Article 15** All persons shall stand when adjudicative personnel enter the courtroom and when the presiding judge or the sole judge pronounces a judgment, ruling, or decision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条    人民法院开庭审判案件应当严格按照法律规定的诉讼程序进行。  
审判人员在庭审活动中应当平等对待诉讼各方。

**Article 16** When the People's Court opens a court session to try a case, it shall strictly follow the litigation procedures prescribed by law.  
Adjudicative personnel shall treat all parties to the action equally during court proceedings.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条    全体人员在庭审活动中应当服从审判长或独任审判员的指挥，尊重司法礼仪，遵守法庭纪律，不得实施下列行为：  
（一）鼓掌、喧哗；  
（二）吸烟、进食；  
（三）拨打或接听电话；  
（四）对庭审活动进行录音、录像、拍照或使用移动通信工具等传播庭审活动；  
（五）其他危害法庭安全或妨害法庭秩序的行为。  
检察人员、诉讼参与人发言或提问，应当经审判长或独任审判员许可。  
旁听人员不得进入审判活动区，不得随意站立、走动，不得发言和提问。  
媒体记者经许可实施第一款第四项规定的行为，应当在指定的时间及区域进行，不得影响或干扰庭审活动。

**Article 17** All persons shall obey the direction of the presiding judge or the sole judge during court proceedings, respect judicial etiquette, abide by courtroom discipline, and shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) applauding or making noise;  
(2) smoking or eating;  
(3) making or answering phone calls;  
(4) recording, videotaping, photographing court proceedings, or disseminating court proceedings using mobile communication tools or other means;  
(5) other acts that endanger court security or disrupt court order.  
Procuratorial personnel and litigation participants shall obtain permission from the presiding judge or the sole judge before speaking or asking questions.  
Observers shall not enter the adjudication activity area, shall not stand or move about at will, and shall not speak or ask questions.  
Media reporters who are permitted to engage in the acts specified in subparagraph (4) of paragraph 1 shall do so at the designated time and area, and shall not affect or interfere with court proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条    审判长或独任审判员主持庭审活动时，依照规定使用法槌。

**Article 18** When the presiding judge or the sole judge presides over court proceedings, he or she shall use the gavel in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条    审判长或独任审判员对违反法庭纪律的人员应当予以警告；对不听警告的，予以训诫；对训诫无效的，责令其退出法庭；对拒不退出法庭的，指令司法警察将其强行带出法庭。  
行为人违反[本规则第十七条](#art-17)第一款第四项规定的，人民法院可以暂扣其使用的设备及存储介质，删除相关内容。

**Article 19** The presiding judge or the sole judge shall give a warning to a person who violates courtroom discipline; if the person ignores the warning, he or she shall be reprimanded; if the reprimand is ineffective, the person shall be ordered to leave the courtroom; if the person refuses to leave, the judicial police shall be instructed to forcibly remove the person from the courtroom.  
Where a person violates the provisions of Article 17, paragraph 1, subparagraph (4) of these Rules, the People's Court may temporarily seize the equipment and storage media used by the person and delete the relevant content.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条    行为人实施下列行为之一，危及法庭安全或扰乱法庭秩序的，根据相关法律规定，予以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究其刑事责任：  
（一）非法携带枪支、弹药、管制刀具或者爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性物品以及传染病病原体进入法庭；  
（二）哄闹、冲击法庭；  
（三）侮辱、诽谤、威胁、殴打司法工作人员或诉讼参与人；  
（四）毁坏法庭设施，抢夺、损毁诉讼文书、证据；  
（五）其他危害法庭安全或扰乱法庭秩序的行为。

**Article 20** Where a person commits any of the following acts, endangering court security or disrupting court order, he or she shall be subjected to a fine or detention in accordance with relevant laws; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued according to law:  
(1) illegally bringing firearms, ammunition, controlled knives, or explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic, corrosive substances, or infectious disease pathogens into the courtroom;  
(2) creating a disturbance in or storming the courtroom;  
(3) insulting, slandering, threatening, or assaulting judicial personnel or litigation participants;  
(4) damaging court facilities, or seizing or destroying litigation documents or evidence;  
(5) other acts that endanger court security or disrupt court order.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条    司法警察依照审判长或独任审判员的指令维持法庭秩序。  
出现危及法庭内人员人身安全或者严重扰乱法庭秩序等紧急情况时，司法警察可以直接采取必要的处置措施。  
人民法院依法对违反法庭纪律的人采取的扣押物品、强行带出法庭以及罚款、拘留等强制措施，由司法警察执行。

**Article 21** Judicial police shall maintain courtroom order in accordance with the instructions of the presiding judge or the sole judge.  
Where an emergency endangering the personal safety of persons in the courtroom or seriously disrupting courtroom order arises, the judicial police may directly take necessary disposal measures.  
Compulsory measures taken by the People's Court according to law against persons who violate courtroom discipline, such as seizure of articles, forcible removal from the courtroom, fines, and detention, shall be executed by the judicial police.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条    人民检察院认为审判人员违反本规则的，可以在庭审活动结束后向人民法院提出处理建议。  
诉讼参与人、旁听人员认为审判人员、书记员、司法警察违反本规则的，可以在庭审活动结束后向人民法院反映。

**Article 22** Where the People's Procuratorate considers that adjudicative personnel have violated these Rules, it may propose handling suggestions to the People's Court after the court proceedings conclude.  
Litigation participants and observers who consider that adjudicative personnel, court clerks, or judicial police have violated these Rules may report to the People's Court after the court proceedings conclude.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条    检察人员违反本规则的，人民法院可以向人民检察院通报情况并提出处理建议。

**Article 23** Where procuratorial personnel violate these Rules, the People's Court may inform the People's Procuratorate of the situation and propose handling suggestions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条    律师违反本规则的，人民法院可以向司法行政机关及律师协会通报情况并提出处理建议。

**Article 24** Where a lawyer violates these Rules, the People's Court may inform the judicial administrative authority and the lawyers' association of the situation and propose handling suggestions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条    人民法院进行案件听证、国家赔偿案件质证、网络视频远程审理以及在法院以外的场所巡回审判等，参照适用本规则。

**Article 25** When the People's Court conducts case hearings, evidence hearings in state compensation cases, remote trials via online video, and circuit trials at venues other than courts, these Rules shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条    外国人、无国籍人旁听庭审活动，外国媒体记者报道庭审活动，应当遵守本规则。

**Article 26** Foreigners and stateless persons observing court proceedings, and foreign media reporters reporting court proceedings, shall comply with these Rules.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条    本规则自2016年5月1日起施行；最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释及规范性文件与本规则不一致的，以本规则为准。

**Article 27** These Rules shall come into force on May 1, 2016; if any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Rules, these Rules shall prevail.
