### 最高人民法院关于私房改造中典当双方都是被改造户的回赎案件应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于私房改造中典当双方都是被改造户的回赎  
  
案件应如何处理问题的批复  
  
法民〔1990〕6号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1990）鲁法民字第 4号《关于处理房屋典当回赎问题的请示》报告收悉。  
  
经研究，答复如下：  
  
在私房社会主义改造中，房屋典当关系的双方都属于私房被改造户，承典人的私房被全部改造，而将承典的房屋作为自住房留下的，不论房管部门何时明确作为自住房，均应视为承典房和私房合并纳入改造后所留下的自住房。参照国家房产管理局（65）国房局字第 105号《关于私房改造中处理典当房屋问题的意见》，此类房屋出典人要求回赎的，不予准许。  
  
1990年7月25日

**Article 1** Reply on How to Handle Cases of Redemption Where Both the Pawner and Pawnee Were Subject to the Transformation in Private Housing Transformation  
Fa Min [1990] No. 6  
Shandong Higher People's Court:  
Your court's Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning the Handling of House Pawn Redemption (Lu Fa Min Zi [1990] No. 4) has been received.  
Upon examination, the reply is as follows:  
Where, during the socialist transformation of private housing, both parties to a house pawn relationship were households subject to the transformation of private housing, and the pawnee's private housing was fully transformed, but the pawned house was retained as self-use housing, regardless of when the housing administration department specified it as self-use housing, it shall be deemed as the self-use housing retained after the pawned house and the private housing were combined and incorporated into the transformation. By reference to the Opinions on Issues Concerning the Handling of Pawned Houses in Private Housing Transformation issued by the State Administration of Housing (65) Guo Fang Ju Zi No. 105, where the pawner requests redemption of such a house, it shall not be permitted.  
July 25, 1990
