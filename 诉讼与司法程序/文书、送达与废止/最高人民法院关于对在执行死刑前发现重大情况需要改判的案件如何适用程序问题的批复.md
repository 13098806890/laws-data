### 最高人民法院关于对在执行死刑前发现重大情况需要改判的案件如何适用程序问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对在执行死刑前发现重大情况需要  
  
改判的案件如何适用程序问题的批复  
  
法释〔1999〕2号  
  
（1999年1月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第1042次会议通过，自1999年1月29日起施行。）  
  
甘肃省高级人民法院：  
  
你院甘高法〔1998〕97号《对在执行死刑前发现重大情况需要改判的案件适用何种程序的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对核准死刑的判决、裁定生效之后，执行死刑前发现有[刑事诉讼法第二百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-211)规定的情形，需要改判的案件，应当由有死刑核准权的人民法院适用审判监督程序依法改判或者指令下级人民法院再审。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Gansu Higher People's Court:  
  
We have received your Request on the Applicable Procedure for Cases Requiring a Changed Judgment When Material Circumstances Are Discovered Before Execution of Death Penalty (Gan Gao Fa [1998] No. 97). Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
After a judgment or ruling approving the death penalty takes effect, if, before execution of the death penalty, it is discovered that there exist circumstances as provided in Article 211 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the case requires a changed judgment, the People's Court with the authority to approve the death penalty shall apply the trial supervision procedure to change the judgment according to law, or order a lower People's Court to conduct a retrial.  
  
This is our reply.
