# 最高人民法院关于人民法院依法执行行政机关的行政处罚决定应用何种法律文书的问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法

### 最高人民法院关于人民法院依法执行行政机关的行政处罚决定应用何种法律文书的问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院依法执行行政机关的行政处罚决定  
  
应用何种法律文书的问题的批复  
  
一九八五年十一月六日法（经）复〔1985〕49号  
  
宁省高级人民法院：  
  
关于你院询问当事人对行政机关的行政处罚决定，在规定期限内不向人民院起诉又不履行，主管行政机关依法申请人民法院强制执行的，人民法院应何种法律文书的问题，我们研究后认为，依照民事诉讼法（试行）第一百七条的规定，人民法院接到主管行政机关的申请执行书后，应当了解案情。如认为处罚决定正确，则用《执行通知书》通知被执行人在指定的期限内履。逾期不履行的，强制执行。如果发现处罚决定确有错误，则不予执行，并告知主管行政机关。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of What Type of Legal Document the People's Court Shall Use When Enforcing an Administrative Penalty Decision of an Administrative Organ According to Law  
November 6, 1985 Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 49  
Higher People's Court of Ning Province:  
Regarding the question raised by your court concerning what type of legal document the People's Court shall use when a party neither files a prosecution with the People's Court within the prescribed time limit nor complies with an administrative penalty decision of an administrative organ, and the competent administrative organ applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution according to law, we have studied the matter and consider that, in accordance with the provisions of Article 107 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), after receiving the application for execution from the competent administrative organ, the People's Court shall examine the case. If it deems the penalty decision correct, it shall use a "Notice of Execution" to notify the person subject to execution to perform within the specified time limit. If the person fails to perform within the time limit, compulsory execution shall be carried out. If it finds that the penalty decision is indeed erroneous, it shall not enforce it and shall inform the competent administrative organ.
