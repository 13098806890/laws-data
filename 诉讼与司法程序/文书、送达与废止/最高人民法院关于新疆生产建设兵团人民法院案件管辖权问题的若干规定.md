# 最高人民法院关于新疆生产建设兵团人民法院案件管辖权问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕4号  
**公布日期**：2005-01-13  
**生效日期**：2005-06-06

> 《最高人民法院关于新疆生产建设兵团人民法院案件管辖权问题的若干规定》已于2005年1月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1340次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年6月6日起施行。 2005年5月24日 最高人民法院 关于新疆生产建设兵团人民法院 案件管辖权问题的若干规定 法释〔2005〕4号 根据《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于新疆维吾尔自治区生产建设兵团设置人民法院和人民检察院的决定》第三条的规定，对新疆生产建设兵团各级人民法院案件管辖权问题规定如下： 第一条 新疆生产建设兵团基层人民法院和中级人民法院分别行使地方基层人民法院和中级人民法院的案件管辖权，管辖兵团范围内的各类案件。 新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院管辖原应当由高级人民法院管辖的兵团范围内的第一审案件、上诉案件和其他案件，其判决和裁定是新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院的判决和裁定。但兵团各中级人民法院判处死刑（含死缓）的案件的上诉案件以及死刑复核案件由新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院管辖。 第二条 兵团人民检察院提起公诉的第一审刑事案件，由兵团人民法院管辖。 兵团人民法院对第一审刑事自诉案件、第二审刑事案件以及再审刑事案件的管辖，适用刑事诉讼法的有关规定。 第三条 兵团人民法院管辖以下民事案件： （一）垦区范围内发生的案件； （二）城区内发生的双方当事人均为兵团范围内的公民、法人或者其他组织的案件； （三）城区内发生的双方当事人一方为兵团范围内的公民、法人或者其他组织，且被告住所地在兵团工作区、生活区或者管理区内的案件。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 新疆生产建设兵团基层人民法院和中级人民法院分别行使地方基层人民法院和中级人民法院的案件管辖权，管辖兵团范围内的各类案件。  
新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院管辖原应当由高级人民法院管辖的兵团范围内的第一审案件、上诉案件和其他案件，其判决和裁定是新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院的判决和裁定。但兵团各中级人民法院判处死刑（含死缓）的案件的上诉案件以及死刑复核案件由新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院管辖。

**Article 1** The Primary People's Courts and Intermediate People's Courts of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall respectively exercise the jurisdiction over cases of local Primary People's Courts and Intermediate People's Courts, and shall have jurisdiction over all kinds of cases within the scope of the Corps.  
The Production and Construction Corps Division of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region shall have jurisdiction over first-instance cases, appellate cases and other cases within the scope of the Corps that otherwise should be under the jurisdiction of a Higher People's Court. Its judgments and rulings shall be the judgments and rulings of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region. However, the appellate cases and death penalty review cases of cases in which the Intermediate People's Courts of the Corps impose the death penalty (including death sentence with a two-year suspension of execution) shall be under the jurisdiction of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 兵团人民检察院提起公诉的第一审刑事案件，由兵团人民法院管辖。  
兵团人民法院对第一审刑事自诉案件、第二审刑事案件以及再审刑事案件的管辖，适用刑事诉讼法的有关规定。

**Article 2** First-instance criminal cases in which a public prosecution is initiated by the People's Procuratorate of the Corps shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the Corps.  
The jurisdiction of the People's Court of the Corps over first-instance criminal cases of private prosecution, second-instance criminal cases and criminal cases for retrial shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 兵团人民法院管辖以下民事案件：  
（一）垦区范围内发生的案件；  
（二）城区内发生的双方当事人均为兵团范围内的公民、法人或者其他组织的案件；  
（三）城区内发生的双方当事人一方为兵团范围内的公民、法人或者其他组织，且被告住所地在兵团工作区、生活区或者管理区内的案件。  
对符合协议管辖和专属管辖条件的案件，依照民事诉讼法的有关规定确定管辖权。

**Article 3** The People's Courts of the Corps shall have jurisdiction over the following civil cases:  
(1) Cases arising within the reclamation areas;  
(2) Cases arising within the urban areas where both parties are citizens, legal persons or other organizations within the scope of the Corps;  
(3) Cases arising within the urban areas where one party is a citizen, legal person or other organization within the scope of the Corps, and the domicile of the defendant is located in the work area, living area or management area of the Corps.  
For cases that meet the conditions for jurisdiction by agreement and exclusive jurisdiction, the jurisdiction shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 以兵团的行政机关作为被告的行政案件由该行政机关所在地的兵团人民法院管辖，其管辖权限依照行政诉讼法的规定办理。

**Article 4** Administrative cases in which an administrative organ of the Corps is the defendant shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the Corps in the place where the administrative organ is located, and the jurisdictional authority thereof shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 兵团人民法院管辖兵团范围内发生的涉外案件。新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院根据最高人民法院的有关规定确定管辖涉外案件的兵团法院。

**Article 5** The People's Courts of the Corps shall have jurisdiction over foreign-related cases arising within the scope of the Corps. The Production and Construction Corps Division of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Supreme People's Court, determine the courts of the Corps that shall have jurisdiction over foreign-related cases.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 兵团各级人民法院与新疆维吾尔自治区地方各级人民法院之间因管辖权发生争议的，由争议双方协商解决；协商不成的，报请新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院决定管辖。

**Article 6** Where a dispute over jurisdiction arises between the People's Courts of the Corps at various levels and the local People's Courts at various levels of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, the disputing parties shall resolve it through consultation; if consultation fails, the matter shall be submitted to the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region for a decision on jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院所管辖第一审案件的上诉法院是最高人民法院。

**Article 7** The appellate court for first-instance cases under the jurisdiction of the Production and Construction Corps Division of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region shall be the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 对于新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院审理再审案件所作出的判决、裁定，新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院不再进行再审。

**Article 8** For judgments and rulings rendered by the Production and Construction Corps Division of the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region in the trial of retrial cases, the Higher People's Court of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region shall not conduct a further retrial.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 本规定自发布之日起实施。人民法院关于兵团人民法院案件管辖的其他规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 9** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. Where other provisions of the People's Courts regarding the jurisdiction of cases of the People's Courts of the Corps are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
