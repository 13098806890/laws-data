### 最高人民法院关于人民法院在审判工作中能否采用人类白细胞抗原作亲子鉴定问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院在审判工作中  
  
能否采用人类白细胞抗原作亲子鉴定问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1987〕20号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院关于当事人要求作亲子鉴定的情况和处理意见的请示报告收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
关于人民法院在审判工作中能否采用人类白细胞抗原（HLA）作亲子关系鉴定的问题，根据近几年来审判实践中试用此项技术的经验，参考卫生部及上海市中心血站所提供的意见，同意你院采用此项技术进行亲子关系的鉴定。  
  
鉴于亲子鉴定关系到夫妻双方、子女和他人的人身关系和财产关系，是一项严肃的工作，因此，对要求作亲子关系鉴定的案件，应从保护妇女、儿童的合法权益，有利于增进团结和防止矛盾激化出发，区别情况，慎重对待。对于双方当事人同意作亲子鉴定的，一般应予准许；一方当事人要求作亲子鉴定的，或者子女已超过三周岁的，应视具体情况，从严掌握，对其中必须作亲子鉴定的，也要做好当事人及有关人员的思想工作。  
  
人民法院对于亲子关系的确认，要进行调查研究，尽力收集其他证据。对亲子鉴定结论，仅作为鉴别亲子关系的证据之一，一定要与本案其他证据相印证，综合分析，作出正确的判断。  
  
此复  
  
1987年6月15日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether Human Leukocyte Antigen May Be Used for Paternity Testing in Adjudication Work by People's Courts  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1987] No. 20  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
We have received your report requesting instructions on the circumstances and handling opinions regarding parties requesting paternity testing. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
  
With respect to the issue of whether human leukocyte antigen (HLA) may be used for paternity determination in the adjudication work of People's Courts, based on the experience of using this technology in adjudication practice over recent years, and with reference to the opinions provided by the Ministry of Health and the Shanghai Central Blood Station, we agree that your court may use this technology for paternity determination.  
  
Given that paternity testing concerns the personal and property relationships between spouses, children, and other persons, and is a serious matter, cases requiring paternity determination shall be handled with care by distinguishing different circumstances, starting from the protection of the legitimate rights and interests of women and children, and facilitating unity and preventing the intensification of conflicts. Where both parties agree to paternity testing, it shall generally be permitted; where one party requests paternity testing, or the child has exceeded three years of age, strict control shall be exercised based on the specific circumstances, and where paternity testing is absolutely necessary, ideological work shall also be properly done on the parties and relevant persons.  
  
A People's Court shall conduct investigation and research and make every effort to collect other evidence for the confirmation of paternity. The conclusion of paternity testing shall only serve as one of the pieces of evidence for identifying paternity, and it must be corroborated with other evidence in the case and comprehensively analyzed to reach a correct judgment.  
  
This reply is hereby given.  
June 15, 1987
