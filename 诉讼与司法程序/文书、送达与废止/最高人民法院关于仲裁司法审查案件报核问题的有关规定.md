# 最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕21号  
**公布日期**：2017-12-26  
**生效日期**：2018-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定》已于2017年11月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1727次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年1月1日起施行。 2017年12月26日 最高人民法院 关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定 法释〔2017〕21号 （2017年11月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1727次会议通过，自2018年1月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称仲裁司法审查案件，包括下列案件：  
（一）申请确认仲裁协议效力案件；  
（二）申请撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决案件；  
（三）申请执行我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决案件；  
（四）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区仲裁裁决案件；  
（五）申请承认和执行外国仲裁裁决案件；  
（六）其他仲裁司法审查案件。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, the cases of judicial review of arbitration shall include the following:  
(1) cases applying for confirmation of the validity of an arbitration agreement;  
(2) cases applying for setting aside an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China;  
(3) cases applying for enforcement of an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China;  
(4) cases applying for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award made in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region;  
(5) cases applying for recognition and enforcement of a foreign arbitral award; and  
(6) other cases of judicial review of arbitration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各中级人民法院或者专门人民法院办理涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，经审查拟认定仲裁协议无效，不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，不予认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区仲裁裁决，不予承认和执行外国仲裁裁决，应当向本辖区所属高级人民法院报核；高级人民法院经审查拟同意的，应当向最高人民法院报核。待最高人民法院审核后，方可依最高人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。  
各中级人民法院或者专门人民法院办理非涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，经审查拟认定仲裁协议无效，不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，应当向本辖区所属高级人民法院报核；待高级人民法院审核后，方可依高级人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。

**Article 2** Where an intermediate people's court or a special people's court handling a case of judicial review of arbitration involving foreign, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan elements, after review, intends to find the arbitration agreement invalid, refuse enforcement of or set aside an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China, or refuse recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award made in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region, or refuse recognition and enforcement of a foreign arbitral award, it shall report the case to the higher people's court in its jurisdiction for review. Where the higher people's court, after review, intends to approve the decision, it shall report the case to the Supreme People's Court for review. Only after the Supreme People's Court has examined the case may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the Supreme People's Court.  
Where an intermediate people's court or a special people's court handling a case of judicial review of arbitration that is non-foreign-related and non-Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan-related, after review, intends to find the arbitration agreement invalid, refuse enforcement of or set aside an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China, it shall report the case to the higher people's court in its jurisdiction for review. Only after the higher people's court has examined the case may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the higher people's court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[本规定第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的非涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，高级人民法院经审查拟同意中级人民法院或者专门人民法院认定仲裁协议无效，不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，在下列情形下，应当向最高人民法院报核，待最高人民法院审核后，方可依最高人民法院的审核意见作出裁定：  
（一）仲裁司法审查案件当事人住所地跨省级行政区域；  
（二）以违背社会公共利益为由不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决。

**Article 3** With respect to the non-foreign-related and non-Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan-related cases of judicial review of arbitration as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of these Provisions, where the higher people's court, after review, intends to approve the intermediate people's court's or the special people's court's decision to find the arbitration agreement invalid, refuse enforcement of or set aside an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China, in any of the following circumstances, it shall report the case to the Supreme People's Court for review, and only after the Supreme People's Court has examined the case may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the Supreme People's Court:  
(1) the domiciles of the parties to the case of judicial review of arbitration are located in different provincial-level administrative regions; or  
(2) refusal to enforce or setting aside of an arbitral award made by an arbitration institution in the mainland of China on the ground that it violates the public interest.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下级人民法院报请上级人民法院审核的案件，应当将书面报告和案件卷宗材料一并上报。书面报告应当写明审查意见及具体理由。

**Article 4** Where a lower people's court requests a higher people's court to review a case, it shall submit both a written report and the case files together. The written report shall state the review opinion and the specific reasons therefor.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　上级人民法院收到下级人民法院的报核申请后，认为案件相关事实不清的，可以询问当事人或者退回下级人民法院补充查明事实后再报。

**Article 5** After receiving an application for reporting for review from a lower people's court, if the higher people's court finds that the relevant facts of the case are unclear, it may question the parties or return the case to the lower people's court for supplementing the findings of facts and then report again.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　上级人民法院应当以复函的形式将审核意见答复下级人民法院。

**Article 6** The higher people's court shall reply to the lower people's court with its review opinion in the form of an official reply letter.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在民事诉讼案件中，对于人民法院因涉及仲裁协议效力而作出的不予受理、驳回起诉、管辖权异议的裁定，当事人不服提起上诉，第二审人民法院经审查拟认定仲裁协议不成立、无效、失效、内容不明确无法执行的，须按照[本规定第二条](#art-2)的规定逐级报核，待上级人民法院审核后，方可依上级人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。

**Article 7** In civil action cases, where a party disagrees with a ruling made by a people's court on non-acceptance, dismissal of the action, or objection to jurisdiction concerning the validity of an arbitration agreement and files an appeal, and the people's court of second instance, after review, intends to find that the arbitration agreement is not formed, null and void, invalid, or its contents are unclear and unenforceable, it shall report level by level for review in accordance with Article 2 of these Provisions, and only after the higher people's court has examined the case may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the higher people's court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定自2018年1月1日起施行，本院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 8** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2018. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
