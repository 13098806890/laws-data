### 最高人民法院关于企业或个人欠国家银行贷款逾期两年未还应当适用民法通则规定的诉讼时效问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于企业或个人欠国家银行贷款  
  
逾期两年未还应当适用民法通则规定  
  
的诉讼时效问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕1号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院豫法研〔1990〕23号请示收悉。关于企业或个人欠国家银行贷款逾期两年未还是否适用民法通则规定的诉讼时效问题，经研究，答复如下：  
  
国家各专业银行及其他金融机构系实行独立核算的经济实体。它们与借款的企业或公民之间的借贷关系，是平等主体之间的债权债务关系。国家各专业银行及其他金融机构向人民法院请求保护其追偿贷款权利的，应当适用民法通则关于诉讼时效的规定。确已超过诉讼时效期间，并且没有诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长诉讼时效期间情况的，人民法院应当判决驳回其诉讼请求。  
  
1993年2月22日

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the Provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law on Limitation of Action Shall Apply to Loans Owed by Enterprises or Individuals to State Banks That Have Been Overdue for Two Years  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 1  
The Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
Your Request for Instructions Yu Fa Yan [1990] No. 23 has been received. With respect to the issue of whether the provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law on limitation of action shall apply to loans owed by enterprises or individuals to state banks that have been overdue for two years, upon study, the reply is as follows:  
The various specialized state banks and other financial institutions are economic entities that practice independent accounting. The loan relationships between them and borrowing enterprises or citizens are creditor-debt relationships between equal subjects. The provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law on limitation of action shall apply when the various specialized state banks and other financial institutions request the People's Court to protect their right to recover loans. If the period of limitation of action has indeed been exceeded and there are no circumstances of suspension, interruption, or extension of the limitation of action, the People's Court shall render a judgment dismissing the claim.  
February 22, 1993
