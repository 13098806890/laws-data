### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计、技术秘密、计算机软件、垄断等专业技术性较强的知识产权案件时，可以指派技术调查官参与诉讼活动。

**Article 1** When the People's Court adjudicates intellectual property cases with strong technical expertise, such as patents, new plant varieties, integrated circuit layout designs, technical secrets, computer software, and monopoly, it may appoint technical investigation officers to participate in the litigation activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　技术调查官属于审判辅助人员。  
人民法院可以设置技术调查室，负责技术调查官的日常管理，指派技术调查官参与知识产权案件诉讼活动、提供技术咨询。

**Article 2** Technical investigation officers belong to adjudication support personnel.  
The People's Court may establish a technical investigation office, responsible for the daily management of technical investigation officers, appointing them to participate in intellectual property case litigation activities and provide technical consultation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　参与知识产权案件诉讼活动的技术调查官确定或者变更后，应当在三日内告知当事人，并依法告知当事人有权申请技术调查官回避。

**Article 3** After a technical investigation officer participating in the litigation activities of an intellectual property case is confirmed or changed, the party shall be notified within three days, and the party shall be informed according to law of the right to apply for the withdrawal of the technical investigation officer.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　技术调查官的回避，参照适用刑事诉讼法、民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法等有关其他人员回避的规定。

**Article 4** The withdrawal of a technical investigation officer shall be handled by reference to the provisions on the withdrawal of other personnel in the Criminal Procedure Law, the Civil Procedure Law, and the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在一个审判程序中参与过案件诉讼活动的技术调查官，不得再参与该案其他程序的诉讼活动。  
发回重审的案件，在一审法院作出裁判后又进入第二审程序的，原第二审程序中参与诉讼的技术调查官不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 5** A technical investigation officer who has participated in the litigation activities of a case in one adjudication procedure shall not participate in the litigation activities of other procedures of the same case.  
For a case remanded for retrial, if it enters the second instance procedure after the court of first instance renders a judgment, the technical investigation officer who participated in the litigation in the original second instance procedure shall not be subject to the restriction of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　参与知识产权案件诉讼活动的技术调查官就案件所涉技术问题履行下列职责：  
（一）对技术事实的争议焦点以及调查范围、顺序、方法等提出建议；  
（二）参与调查取证、勘验、保全；  
（三）参与询问、听证、庭前会议、开庭审理；  
（四）提出技术调查意见；  
（五）协助法官组织鉴定人、相关技术领域的专业人员提出意见；  
（六）列席合议庭评议等有关会议；  
（七）完成其他相关工作。

**Article 6** A technical investigation officer participating in the litigation activities of an intellectual property case shall perform the following duties regarding the technical issues involved in the case:  
(1) Propose suggestions on the focus of disputes over technical facts and the scope, sequence, methods, etc. of investigation;  
(2) Participate in investigation and evidence collection, inspection, and preservation;  
(3) Participate in inquiries, hearings, pre-trial conferences, and court trials;  
(4) Render technical investigation opinions;  
(5) Assist judges in organizing expert evaluators and professionals in relevant technical fields to present opinions;  
(6) Attend relevant meetings such as collegial panel deliberations;  
(7) Complete other related work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　技术调查官参与调查取证、勘验、保全的，应当事先查阅相关技术资料，就调查取证、勘验、保全的方法、步骤和注意事项等提出建议。

**Article 7** Where a technical investigation officer participates in investigation and evidence collection, inspection, or preservation, he or she shall review the relevant technical materials in advance and propose suggestions on the methods, steps, and matters needing attention for investigation and evidence collection, inspection, or preservation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　技术调查官参与询问、听证、庭前会议、开庭审理活动时，经法官同意，可以就案件所涉技术问题向当事人及其他诉讼参与人发问。  
技术调查官在法庭上的座位设在法官助理的左侧，书记员的座位设在法官助理的右侧。

**Article 8** When a technical investigation officer participates in inquiries, hearings, pre-trial conferences, or court trials, with the consent of the judge, he or she may ask questions to the parties and other participants in the proceedings regarding the technical issues involved in the case.  
The seat of the technical investigation officer in the courtroom shall be on the left side of the judge's assistant, and the seat of the court clerk shall be on the right side of the judge's assistant.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　技术调查官应当在案件评议前就案件所涉技术问题提出技术调查意见。  
技术调查意见由技术调查官独立出具并签名，不对外公开。

**Article 9** A technical investigation officer shall render a technical investigation opinion on the technical issues involved in the case before the case deliberation.  
The technical investigation opinion shall be independently issued and signed by the technical investigation officer, and shall not be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　技术调查官列席案件评议时，其提出的意见应当记入评议笔录，并由其签名。  
技术调查官对案件裁判结果不具有表决权。

**Article 10** When a technical investigation officer attends case deliberations, the opinions he or she expresses shall be recorded in the deliberation minutes and signed by him or her.  
A technical investigation officer shall not have the right to vote on the outcome of the case judgment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　技术调查官提出的技术调查意见可以作为合议庭认定技术事实的参考。  
合议庭对技术事实认定依法承担责任。

**Article 11** The technical investigation opinion rendered by a technical investigation officer may serve as a reference for the collegial panel in determining technical facts.  
The collegial panel shall bear responsibility according to law for the determination of technical facts.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　技术调查官参与知识产权案件诉讼活动的，应当在裁判文书上署名。技术调查官的署名位于法官助理之下、书记员之上。

**Article 12** Where a technical investigation officer participates in the litigation activities of an intellectual property case, he or she shall sign the judgment document. The signature of the technical investigation officer shall be placed below that of the judge's assistant and above that of the court clerk.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　技术调查官违反与审判工作有关的法律及相关规定，贪污受贿、徇私舞弊，故意出具虚假、误导或者重大遗漏的不实技术调查意见的，应当追究法律责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where a technical investigation officer violates the laws and relevant regulations related to adjudication work, engages in embezzlement or bribery, practices favoritism or engages in fraud, intentionally issues false, misleading, or materially incomplete technical investigation opinions, legal liability shall be pursued; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　根据案件审理需要，上级人民法院可以对本辖区内各级人民法院的技术调查官进行调派。  
人民法院审理[本规定第一条](#art-1)所称案件时，可以申请上级人民法院调派技术调查官参与诉讼活动。

**Article 14** Based on the needs of case adjudication, a higher People's Court may assign technical investigation officers from within its jurisdiction to various levels of People's Courts.  
When a People's Court adjudicates a case as referred to in Article 1 of these Provisions, it may apply to a higher People's Court to assign technical investigation officers to participate in the litigation activities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定自2019年5月1日起施行。本院以前发布的相关规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force as of May 1, 2019. Where any relevant provisions previously issued by this Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
