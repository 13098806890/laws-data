### 正文

一、关于人身安全保护令案件是否收取诉讼费的问题。同意你院倾向性意见，即向人民法院申请人身安全保护令，不收取诉讼费用。

**Article None** I. The issue of whether litigation fees shall be charged in personal safety protection order cases. This court agrees with your court's prevailing opinion that no litigation fees shall be charged for applying to the People's Court for a personal safety protection order.

二、关于申请人身安全保护令是否需要提供担保的问题。同意你院倾向性意见，即根据《中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法》请求人民法院作出人身安全保护令的，申请人不需要提供担保。

**Article None** II. The issue of whether security needs to be provided when applying for a personal safety protection order. This court agrees with your court's prevailing opinion that, when requesting the People's Court to issue a personal safety protection order in accordance with the Anti-Domestic Violence Law of the People's Republic of China, the applicant shall not need to provide security.

三、关于人身安全保护令案件适用程序等问题。人身安全保护令案件适用何种程序，反家庭暴力法中没有作出直接规定。人民法院可以比照特别程序进行审理。家事纠纷案件中的当事人向人民法院申请人身安全保护令的，由审理该案的审判组织作出是否发出人身安全保护令的裁定；如果人身安全保护令的申请人在接受其申请的人民法院并无正在进行的家事案件诉讼，由法官以独任审理的方式审理。至于是否需要就发出人身安全保护令问题听取被申请人的意见，则由承办法官视案件的具体情况决定。

**Article None** III. Issues concerning the applicable procedure for personal safety protection order cases, etc. The Anti-Domestic Violence Law does not directly stipulate which procedure shall apply to personal safety protection order cases. The People's Court may hear such cases by analogy with special procedures. Where a party in a family dispute case applies to the People's Court for a personal safety protection order, the adjudication organization hearing the case shall render a ruling on whether to issue the personal safety protection order; if the applicant for the personal safety protection order has no ongoing family case proceedings before the People's Court accepting the application, the judge shall hear the case sitting alone. As to whether it is necessary to hear the respondent's opinions on the issuance of the personal safety protection order, the presiding judge shall decide it based on the specific circumstances of the case.

四、关于复议问题。对于人身安全保护令的被申请人提出的复议申请和人身安全保护令的申请人就驳回裁定提出的复议申请，可以由原审判组织进行复议；人民法院认为必要的，也可以另行指定审判组织进行复议。  
此复。

**Article None** IV. The issue of reconsideration. A reconsideration application filed by the respondent against a personal safety protection order and a reconsideration application filed by the applicant against a dismissal ruling may be reviewed by the original adjudication organization; if the People's Court deems it necessary, it may also designate another adjudication organization to conduct the reconsideration.  
This is hereby replied.
