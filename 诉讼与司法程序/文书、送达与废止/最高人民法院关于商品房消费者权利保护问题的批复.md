# 最高人民法院关于商品房消费者权利保护问题的批复

**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕1号  
**公布日期**：2023-02-14  
**生效日期**：2023-04-20

---

### 正文

一、建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及其他债权之间的权利顺位关系，按照《最高人民法院关于审理建设工程施工合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第三十六条的规定处理。

**Article None** 1. The priority ranking among the construction project price priority right of compensation, mortgage, and other claims shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 36 of the Interpretation (I) of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Disputes over Construction Project Contracts.

二、商品房消费者以居住为目的购买房屋并已支付全部价款，主张其房屋交付请求权优先于建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及其他债权的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
只支付了部分价款的商品房消费者，在一审法庭辩论终结前已实际支付剩余价款的，可以适用前款规定。

**Article None** 2. Where a commodity housing consumer purchases a house for the purpose of residence and has paid the full price, and claims that his right to demand delivery of the house has priority over the construction project price priority right of compensation, mortgage, and other claims, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where a commodity housing consumer who has only paid part of the price has actually paid the remaining price before the conclusion of the court debate at first instance, the preceding paragraph may apply.

三、在房屋不能交付且无实际交付可能的情况下，商品房消费者主张价款返还请求权优先于建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及其他债权的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article None** 3. In the case where the house cannot be delivered and there is no actual possibility of delivery, where a commodity housing consumer claims that his right to demand refund of the price has priority over the construction project price priority right of compensation, mortgage, and other claims, the People's Court shall support such claim.
