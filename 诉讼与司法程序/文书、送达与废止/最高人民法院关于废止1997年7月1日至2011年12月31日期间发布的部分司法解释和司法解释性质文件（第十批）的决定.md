法释〔2013〕7号  
  
为适应形势发展变化，保证国家法律统一正确适用，根据有关法律规定和审判工作实际，最高人民法院会同有关部门，对1997年7月1日至2011年12月31日期间发布的司法解释和司法解释性质文件进行了集中清理。现决定废止1997年7月1日至2011年12月31日期间制发的81件司法解释和司法解释性质文件。废止的司法解释和司法解释性质文件从本决定施行之日起不再适用，但过去依据下列司法解释和司法解释性质文件对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。

**Article 1** February 26, 2013  
Supreme People's Court  
Decision on Abolishing Certain Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature Issued from July 1, 1997 to December 31, 2011 (Tenth Batch)  
Fa Shi [2013] No. 7  
To adapt to developments and changes in the situation, ensure the uniform and correct application of national laws, and in accordance with relevant legal provisions and the actual needs of judicial work, the Supreme People's Court, together with relevant departments, conducted a centralized review of the judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature issued between July 1, 1997 and December 31, 2011. It is hereby decided to abolish 81 judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature formulated and issued during the period from July 1, 1997 to December 31, 2011. The abolished judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall no longer apply from the date this Decision comes into force; however, judgments and rulings made in relevant cases based on the following judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall remain valid.
