### 最高人民法院关于继母与生父离婚后仍有权要求已与其形成抚养关系的继子女履行赡养义务的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于继母与生父离婚后仍有权要求  
  
已与其形成抚养关系的继子女履行赡养义务的批复  
  
（1986）民他字第 9号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 85）民监字 6号关于王淑梅诉李春景姐弟等人赡养费一案处理意见的请示报告收悉。  
  
据报告及所附材料，被申诉人王淑梅于一九五一年十二月与申诉人李春景之父李明心结婚时，李明心有前妻所生子女李春景等五人（均未成年）。在长期共同生活中，王淑梅对五个继子女都尽了一定的抚养教育义务，直至其成年并参加工作。一九八三年四月王淑梅与李明心离婚。一九八三年八月王淑梅向大连市西岗区人民法院起诉，要求继子女给付赡养费。一、二审法院判决认为，继子女李春景姐弟五人受过王淑梅的抚养教育，根据权利义务一致的原则，在王淑梅年老体弱，生活无来源的情况下，对王淑梅应履行赡养义务。李春景姐弟对判决不服，以王淑梅已与生父离婚，继母与继子女关系即消失为由，拒不承担对王淑梅的赡养义务，并向你院申诉。你院认为，王淑梅与李明心既已离婚，继子女与继母关系事实上已经消除，李春景姐弟不应再承担对王淑梅的赡养义务。  
  
经我们研究认为：王淑梅与李春景姐弟五人之间，既存在继母与继子女间的姻亲关系，又存在由于长期共同生活而形成的抚养关系。尽管继母王淑梅与生父李明心离婚，婚姻关系消失，但王淑梅与李春景姐弟等人之间已经形成的抚养关系不能消失。因此，有负担能力的李春景姐弟等人，对曾经长期抚养教育过他们的年老体弱、生活困难的王淑梅应尽赡养扶助的义务。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年三月二十一日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Official Reply on Whether a Stepmother, after Divorce from the Biological Father, Still Has the Right to Demand Alimony from Stepchildren with Whom She Has Formed a Support Relationship  
(1986) Min Ta Zi No. 9  
  
Higher People's Court of Liaoning Province:  
  
We have received your petition report, (85) Min Jian Zi No. 6, regarding the opinions on handling the case of Wang Shumei v. Li Chunjing and his siblings concerning alimony.  
  
According to the report and the attached materials, when the respondent Wang Shumei married Li Mingxin, the father of the petitioner Li Chunjing, in December 1951, Li Mingxin had five children (all minors) from his former wife, including Li Chunjing. During the long-term cohabitation, Wang Shumei fulfilled certain obligations of upbringing and education for all five stepchildren until they reached adulthood and started working. In April 1983, Wang Shumei divorced Li Mingxin. In August 1983, Wang Shumei filed a lawsuit with the Xigang District People's Court of Dalian City, demanding alimony from the stepchildren. The courts of first instance and second instance held that the stepchildren, Li Chunjing and his siblings, had received upbringing and education from Wang Shumei and, under the principle of consistency of rights and obligations, should perform the obligation of alimony for Wang Shumei, who was old, weak, and without a source of income. Li Chunjing and his siblings refused to accept the judgment, arguing that since Wang Shumei had divorced the biological father, the relationship between stepmother and stepchildren had terminated, and they should not assume the alimony obligation. They then filed a petition with your Court. Your Court considered that since Wang Shumei and Li Mingxin had divorced, the relationship between stepchildren and stepmother had in fact been extinguished, and Li Chunjing and his siblings should no longer bear the alimony obligation for Wang Shumei.  
  
Upon our study, we hold that: Between Wang Shumei and Li Chunjing and his five siblings, there exists both an affinity relationship of stepmother and stepchildren and a support relationship formed through long-term cohabitation. Although the stepmother Wang Shumei has divorced the biological father Li Mingxin, and the marital relationship has terminated, the support relationship already formed between Wang Shumei and Li Chunjing and his siblings cannot be terminated. Therefore, Li Chunjing and his siblings, who have the ability to bear the obligation, shall provide alimony and assistance to Wang Shumei, who once raised and educated them for a long period and is now old, weak, and in financial difficulty.  
  
This reply is hereby given.  
  
March 21, 1986
