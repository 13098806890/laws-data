# 中华人民共和国最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于公布失信被执行人名单信息的若干规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕7号  
**公布日期**：2017-01-16  
**生效日期**：2017-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于修改〈最高人民法院关于公布失信被执行人名单信息的若干规定〉的决定》已于2017年1月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1707次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年5月1日起施行。 2017年2月28日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于公布失信被执行人名单信息的若干规定》的决定 法释〔2017〕7号 根据最高人民法院审判委员会第1707次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于公布失信被执行人名单信息的若干规定》作如下修改： 一、将第一条修改为：“被执行人未履行生效法律文书确定的义务，并具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当将其纳入失信被执行人名单，依法对其进行信用惩戒： （一）有履行能力而拒不履行生效法律文书确定义务的； （二）以伪造证据、暴力、威胁等方法妨碍、抗拒执行的； （三）以虚假诉讼、虚假仲裁或者以隐匿、转移财产等方法规避执行的； （四）违反财产报告制度的； （五）违反限制消费令的； （六）无正当理由拒不履行执行和解协议的。” 二、增加一条，作为第二条：“被执行人具有本规定第一条第二项至第六项规定情形的，纳入失信被执行人名单的期限为二年。被执行人以暴力、威胁方法妨碍、抗拒执行情节严重或具有多项失信行为的，可以延长一至三年。 失信被执行人积极履行生效法律文书确定义务或主动纠正失信行为的，人民法院可以决定提前删除失信信息。”

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　被执行人未履行生效法律文书确定的义务，并具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当将其纳入失信被执行人名单，依法对其进行信用惩戒：  
（一）有履行能力而拒不履行生效法律文书确定义务的；  
（二）以伪造证据、暴力、威胁等方法妨碍、抗拒执行的；  
（三）以虚假诉讼、虚假仲裁或者以隐匿、转移财产等方法规避执行的；  
（四）违反财产报告制度的；  
（五）违反限制消费令的；  
（六）无正当理由拒不履行执行和解协议的。

**Article 1** Where a person subject to enforcement fails to perform an obligation specified in an effective legal document and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall include him or it in the list of judgment defaulters and impose credit sanctions on him or it according to law:  
(1) having the capability to perform but refusing to perform the obligation specified in the effective legal document;  
(2) obstructing or resisting enforcement by forging evidence, violence, threats, or other means;  
(3) evading enforcement by means of false litigation, false arbitration, or concealing or transferring property;  
(4) violating the property reporting system;  
(5) violating the order restricting consumption;  
(6) refusing to perform a settlement agreement reached during enforcement without justifiable grounds.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　被执行人具有[本规定第一条](#art-1)第二项至第六项规定情形的，纳入失信被执行人名单的期限为二年。被执行人以暴力、威胁方法妨碍、抗拒执行情节严重或具有多项失信行为的，可以延长一至三年。  
失信被执行人积极履行生效法律文书确定义务或主动纠正失信行为的，人民法院可以决定提前删除失信信息。

**Article 2** Where a person subject to enforcement falls under any of the circumstances specified in Items (2) to (6) of Article 1 of these Provisions, the period for being included in the list of judgment defaulters shall be two years. Where the person subject to enforcement obstructs or resists enforcement by violence or threats with serious circumstances or has multiple acts of dishonesty, the period may be extended by one to three years.  
Where a judgment defaulter actively performs the obligation specified in the effective legal document or voluntarily corrects the dishonest act, the People's Court may decide to delete the dishonest information before the expiration of the period.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院不得依据[本规定第一条](#art-1)第一项的规定将被执行人纳入失信被执行人名单：  
（一）提供了充分有效担保的；  
（二）已被采取查封、扣押、冻结等措施的财产足以清偿生效法律文书确定债务的；  
（三）被执行人履行顺序在后，对其依法不应强制执行的；  
（四）其他不属于有履行能力而拒不履行生效法律文书确定义务的情形。

**Article 3** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not include the person subject to enforcement in the list of judgment defaulters pursuant to the provisions of Item (1) of Article 1 of these Provisions:  
(1) having provided sufficient and effective guarantee;  
(2) the property that has been sealed up, seized, or frozen is sufficient to satisfy the debt specified in the effective legal document;  
(3) the person subject to enforcement has a later priority in performance and shall not be subject to compulsory enforcement according to law;  
(4) other circumstances that do not constitute having the capability to perform but refusing to perform the obligation specified in the effective legal document.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　被执行人为未成年人的，人民法院不得将其纳入失信被执行人名单。

**Article 4** Where the person subject to enforcement is a minor, the People's Court shall not include him or her in the list of judgment defaulters.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院向被执行人发出的执行通知中，应当载明有关纳入失信被执行人名单的风险提示等内容。  
申请执行人认为被执行人具有[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定情形之一的，可以向人民法院申请将其纳入失信被执行人名单。人民法院应当自收到申请之日起十五日内审查并作出决定。人民法院认为被执行人具有[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定情形之一的，也可以依职权决定将其纳入失信被执行人名单。  
人民法院决定将被执行人纳入失信被执行人名单的，应当制作决定书，决定书应当写明纳入失信被执行人名单的理由，有纳入期限的，应当写明纳入期限。决定书由院长签发，自作出之日起生效。决定书应当按照民事诉讼法规定的法律文书送达方式送达当事人。

**Article 5** The enforcement notice issued by the People's Court to the person subject to enforcement shall contain a risk warning concerning inclusion in the list of judgment defaulters and other information.  
Where the applicant for enforcement believes that the person subject to enforcement falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 1 of these Provisions, the applicant may apply to the People's Court for including him or it in the list of judgment defaulters. The People's Court shall examine the application and render a decision within fifteen days from the date of receipt. Where the People's Court believes that the person subject to enforcement falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 1 of these Provisions, it may also decide ex officio to include him or it in the list of judgment defaulters.  
Where the People's Court decides to include the person subject to enforcement in the list of judgment defaulters, it shall issue a written decision stating the reasons for inclusion; if there is an inclusion period, the period shall be stated. The written decision shall be signed by the president of the court and shall take effect on the date it is made. The written decision shall be served on the parties in accordance with the service methods for legal documents specified in the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　记载和公布的失信被执行人名单信息应当包括：  
（一）作为被执行人的法人或者其他组织的名称、统一社会信用代码（或组织机构代码）、法定代表人或者负责人姓名；  
（二）作为被执行人的自然人的姓名、性别、年龄、身份证号码；  
（三）生效法律文书确定的义务和被执行人的履行情况；  
（四）被执行人失信行为的具体情形；  
（五）执行依据的制作单位和文号、执行案号、立案时间、执行法院；  
（六）人民法院认为应当记载和公布的不涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的其他事项。

**Article 6** The information recorded and published in the list of judgment defaulters shall include:  
(1) the name, uniform social credit code (or organization code), and name of the legal representative or responsible person of a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement;  
(2) the name, gender, age, and identity card number of a natural person who is the person subject to enforcement;  
(3) the obligations specified in the effective legal document and the performance status of the person subject to enforcement;  
(4) the specific circumstances of the dishonest act of the person subject to enforcement;  
(5) the issuing authority and document number of the enforcement basis, the enforcement case number, the case-filing time, and the enforcing court;  
(6) other matters that the People's Court considers necessary to record and publish and that do not involve state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民法院应当将失信被执行人名单信息录入最高人民法院失信被执行人名单库，并通过该名单库统一向社会公布。  
各级人民法院可以根据各地实际情况，将失信被执行人名单通过报纸、广播、电视、网络、法院公告栏等其他方式予以公布，并可以采取新闻发布会或者其他方式对本院及辖区法院实施失信被执行人名单制度的情况定期向社会公布。

**Article 7** People's courts at all levels shall enter the information of the list of judgment defaulters into the database of the list of judgment defaulters of the Supreme People's Court and shall uniformly publish it to the public through that database.  
People's courts at all levels may, based on local actual conditions, publish the list of judgment defaulters through newspapers, radio, television, the Internet, court notice boards, or other means, and may regularly disclose to the public the implementation of the judgment defaulter list system by their own courts and courts within their jurisdiction through press conferences or other means.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院应当将失信被执行人名单信息，向政府相关部门、金融监管机构、金融机构、承担行政职能的事业单位及行业协会等通报，供相关单位依照法律、法规和有关规定，在政府采购、招标投标、行政审批、政府扶持、融资信贷、市场准入、资质认定等方面，对失信被执行人予以信用惩戒。  
人民法院应当将失信被执行人名单信息向征信机构通报，并由征信机构在其征信系统中记录。  
国家工作人员、人大代表、政协委员等被纳入失信被执行人名单的，人民法院应当将失信情况通报其所在单位和相关部门。  
国家机关、事业单位、国有企业等被纳入失信被执行人名单的，人民法院应当将失信情况通报其上级单位、主管部门或者履行出资人职责的机构。

**Article 8** The People's Court shall notify the information of the list of judgment defaulters to relevant government departments, financial regulatory authorities, financial institutions, public institutions with administrative functions, and industry associations, so that relevant entities may impose credit sanctions on judgment defaulters in accordance with laws, regulations, and relevant provisions in areas such as government procurement, tenders and bidding, administrative approval, government support, financing and credit, market access, and qualification recognition.  
The People's Court shall notify the information of the list of judgment defaulters to credit reference agencies, and the credit reference agencies shall record it in their credit reference systems.  
Where state functionaries, deputies to people's congresses, members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, or the like are included in the list of judgment defaulters, the People's Court shall notify the dishonesty situation to their work units and relevant departments.  
Where state organs, public institutions, state-owned enterprises, or the like are included in the list of judgment defaulters, the People's Court shall notify the dishonesty situation to their superior units, competent authorities, or the agencies performing the duties of the investor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　不应纳入失信被执行人名单的公民、法人或其他组织被纳入失信被执行人名单的，人民法院应当在三个工作日内撤销失信信息。  
记载和公布的失信信息不准确的，人民法院应当在三个工作日内更正失信信息。

**Article 9** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization that should not have been included in the list of judgment defaulters is included therein, the People's Court shall cancel the dishonest information within three working days.  
Where the recorded and published dishonest information is inaccurate, the People's Court shall correct the dishonest information within three working days.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当在三个工作日内删除失信信息：  
（一）被执行人已履行生效法律文书确定的义务或人民法院已执行完毕的；  
（二）当事人达成执行和解协议且已履行完毕的；  
（三）申请执行人书面申请删除失信信息，人民法院审查同意的；  
（四）终结本次执行程序后，通过网络执行查控系统查询被执行人财产两次以上，未发现有可供执行财产，且申请执行人或者其他人未提供有效财产线索的；  
（五）因审判监督或破产程序，人民法院依法裁定对失信被执行人中止执行的；  
（六）人民法院依法裁定不予执行的；  
（七）人民法院依法裁定终结执行的。  
有纳入期限的，不适用前款规定。纳入期限届满后三个工作日内，人民法院应当删除失信信息。  
依照本条第一款规定删除失信信息后，被执行人具有[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定情形之一的，人民法院可以重新将其纳入失信被执行人名单。  
依照本条第一款第三项规定删除失信信息后六个月内，申请执行人申请将该被执行人纳入失信被执行人名单的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall delete the dishonest information within three working days:  
(1) the person subject to enforcement has performed the obligation specified in the effective legal document or the People's Court has completed enforcement;  
(2) the parties have reached an enforcement settlement agreement and it has been fully performed;  
(3) the applicant for enforcement submits a written application for deletion of the dishonest information, and the People's Court approves it after examination;  
(4) after the termination of the current enforcement procedure, the property of the person subject to enforcement has been searched through the online enforcement investigation and control system at least twice without discovering any property available for enforcement, and the applicant for enforcement or any other person has not provided effective property clues;  
(5) due to adjudication supervision or bankruptcy proceedings, the People's Court has rendered an order according to law to suspend enforcement against the judgment defaulter;  
(6) the People's Court has rendered an order according to law not to enforce;  
(7) the People's Court has rendered an order according of law to terminate enforcement.  
Where there is an inclusion period, the preceding paragraph shall not apply. Within three working days after the expiration of the inclusion period, the People's Court shall delete the dishonest information.  
After the dishonest information is deleted in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article, if the person subject to enforcement falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 1 of these Provisions, the People's Court may include him or it again in the list of judgment defaulters.  
Within six months after the dishonest information is deleted in accordance with Item (3) of the first paragraph of this Article, if the applicant for enforcement applies to include the person subject to enforcement in the list of judgment defaulters, the People's Court shall not support such application.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被纳入失信被执行人名单的公民、法人或其他组织认为有下列情形之一的，可以向执行法院申请纠正：  
（一）不应将其纳入失信被执行人名单的；  
（二）记载和公布的失信信息不准确的；  
（三）失信信息应予删除的。

**Article 11** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization that has been included in the list of judgment defaulters believes that any of the following circumstances exists, it may apply to the enforcing court for correction:  
(1) it should not have been included in the list of judgment defaulters;  
(2) the recorded and published dishonest information is inaccurate;  
(3) the dishonest information shall be deleted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公民、法人或其他组织对被纳入失信被执行人名单申请纠正的，执行法院应当自收到书面纠正申请之日起十五日内审查，理由成立的，应当在三个工作日内纠正；理由不成立的，决定驳回。公民、法人或其他组织对驳回决定不服的，可以自决定书送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十五日内作出决定。  
复议期间，不停止原决定的执行。

**Article 12** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization applies for correction regarding its inclusion in the list of judgment defaulters, the enforcing court shall examine the written correction application within fifteen days from the date of receipt. If the grounds are valid, it shall make corrections within three working days; if the grounds are not valid, it shall decide to reject the application. If the citizen, legal person, or other organization is dissatisfied with the rejection decision, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the written decision. The People's Court at the next higher level shall render a decision within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application.  
During the reconsideration period, enforcement of the original decision shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院工作人员违反本规定公布、撤销、更正、删除失信信息的，参照有关规定追究责任。

**Article 13** Where personnel of the People's Court violate these Provisions in publishing, canceling, correcting, or deleting dishonest information, they shall be held accountable with reference to relevant provisions.
