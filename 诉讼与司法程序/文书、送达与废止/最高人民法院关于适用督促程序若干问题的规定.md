# 最高人民法院关于适用督促程序若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕2号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-13  
**生效日期**：2001-01-21

> （2000年11月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1137次会议通过，自2001年1月21日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕2号 为了在审判工作中正确适用督促程序，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》有关规定，现对适用督促程序处理案件的若干问题规定如下： 第一条 基层人民法院受理债权人依法申请支付令的案件，不受争议金额的限制。 第二条 共同债务人住所地、经常居住地不在同一基层人民法院辖区，各有关人民法院都有管辖权的，债权人可以向其中任何一个基层人民法院申请支付令；债权人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请支付令的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。 第三条 人民法院收到债权人的书面申请后，认为申请书不符合要求的，人民法院可以通知债权人限期补正。补正期间不计入民事诉讼法第一百九十条规定的期限。 第四条 对设有担保的债务案件主债务人发出的支付令，对担保人没有拘束力。债权人就担保关系单独提起诉讼的，支付令自行失效。 第五条 人民法院受理债权人的支付令申请后，经审理，有下列情况之一的，应当裁定驳回申请： （一）当事人不适格； （二）给付金钱或者汇票、本票、支票以及股票、债券、国库券、可转让的存款单等有价证券的证明文件没有约定逾期给付利息或者违约金、赔偿金，债权人坚持要求给付利息或者违约金、赔偿金； （三）债权人要求给付的金钱或者汇票、本票、支票以及股票、债券、国库券、可转让的存款单等有价证券属于违法所得； （四）债权人申请支付令之前已向人民法院申请诉前保全，或者申请支付令同时又要求诉前保全。 第六条 人民法院受理支付令申请后，债权人就同一债权关系又提起诉讼，或者人民法院发出支付令之日起三十日内无法送达债务人的，应当裁定终结督促程序。 第七条 债务人对债权债务关系没有异议，但对清偿能力、清偿期限、清偿方式等提出不同意见的，不影响支付令的效力。 第八条 债权人基于同一债权债务关系，向债务人提出多项支付请求，债务人仅就其中一项或几项请求提出异议的，不影响其他各项请求的效力。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 基层人民法院受理债权人依法申请支付令的案件，不受争议金额的限制。

**Article 1** A Primary People's Court shall accept a case where a creditor applies for an order for payment according to law, and shall not be limited by the amount in dispute.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 共同债务人住所地、经常居住地不在同一基层人民法院辖区，各有关人民法院都有管辖权的，债权人可以向其中任何一个基层人民法院申请支付令；债权人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请支付令的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Where the domicile or habitual residence of joint debtors are not within the jurisdiction of the same Primary People's Court, and each relevant People's Court has jurisdiction, the creditor may apply for an order for payment to any one of the Primary People's Courts; if the creditor applies for an order for payment to two or more People's Courts having jurisdiction, the People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 人民法院收到债权人的书面申请后，认为申请书不符合要求的，人民法院可以通知债权人限期补正。补正期间不计入[民事诉讼法第一百九十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-190)规定的期限。

**Article 3** After a People's Court receives a written application from a creditor, if it considers that the application does not meet the requirements, the People's Court may notify the creditor to make corrections within a specified time limit. The period for making corrections shall not be counted in the time limit prescribed in Article 190 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 对设有担保的债务案件主债务人发出的支付令，对担保人没有拘束力。债权人就担保关系单独提起诉讼的，支付令自行失效。

**Article 4** An order for payment issued against the principal debtor in a debt case with security shall have no binding force on the surety. If the creditor separately initiates a lawsuit on the security relationship, the order for payment shall automatically cease to have effect.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院受理债权人的支付令申请后，经审理，有下列情况之一的，应当裁定驳回申请：  
（一）当事人不适格；  
（二）给付金钱或者汇票、本票、支票以及股票、债券、国库券、可转让的存款单等有价证券的证明文件没有约定逾期给付利息或者违约金、赔偿金，债权人坚持要求给付利息或者违约金、赔偿金；  
（三）债权人要求给付的金钱或者汇票、本票、支票以及股票、债券、国库券、可转让的存款单等有价证券属于违法所得；  
（四）债权人申请支付令之前已向人民法院申请诉前保全，或者申请支付令同时又要求诉前保全。

**Article 5** After a People's Court accepts an application for an order for payment from a creditor, upon trial, if any of the following circumstances exists, it shall rule to dismiss the application:  
(1) the parties are not qualified;  
(2) the supporting documents for the payment of money or negotiable instruments such as bills of exchange, promissory notes, checks, and stocks, bonds, treasury bonds, transferable certificates of deposit, etc., do not stipulate overdue interest or liquidated damages or compensation, and the creditor insists on payment of interest or liquidated damages or compensation;  
(3) the money or negotiable instruments such as bills of exchange, promissory notes, checks, and stocks, bonds, treasury bonds, transferable certificates of deposit, etc., that the creditor requests to be paid are illegal gains;  
(4) before applying for an order for payment, the creditor has already applied to the People's Court for pre-litigation preservation, or, at the same time as applying for an order for payment, also requests pre-litigation preservation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院受理支付令申请后，债权人就同一债权关系又提起诉讼，或者人民法院发出支付令之日起三十日内无法送达债务人的，应当裁定终结督促程序。

**Article 6** After a People's Court accepts an application for an order for payment, if the creditor initiates another lawsuit on the same creditor's right, or if the order for payment cannot be served on the debtor within thirty days from the date the People's Court issues the order for payment, it shall rule to terminate the supervision procedure.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 债务人对债权债务关系没有异议，但对清偿能力、清偿期限、清偿方式等提出不同意见的，不影响支付令的效力。

**Article 7** If the debtor has no objection to the creditor-debtor relationship but raises different opinions on the ability to repay, the term for repayment, the method of repayment, etc., this shall not affect the validity of the order for payment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 债权人基于同一债权债务关系，向债务人提出多项支付请求，债务人仅就其中一项或几项请求提出异议的，不影响其他各项请求的效力。

**Article 8** Where a creditor, based on the same creditor-debtor relationship, makes multiple requests for payment to the debtor, and the debtor objects to only one or several of the requests, this shall not affect the validity of the other requests.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 债权人基于同一债权债务关系，就可分之债向多个债务人提出支付请求，多个债务人中的一人或几人提出异议的，不影响其他请求的效力。

**Article 9** Where a creditor, based on the same creditor-debtor relationship, makes a request for payment to multiple debtors on a divisible debt, and one or several of the multiple debtors object, this shall not affect the validity of the other requests.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院作出终结督促程序前，债务人请求撤回异议的，应当准许。

**Article 10** Before a People's Court makes a ruling to terminate the supervision procedure, if the debtor requests to withdraw the objection, the request shall be permitted.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院院长对本院已发生法律效力的支付令，发现确有错误，认为需要撤销的，应当提交审判委员会讨论决定后，裁定撤销支付令，驳回债权人的申请。

**Article 11** If the president of a People's Court discovers that an order for payment that has already taken legal effect by the same court contains a definite error and considers it necessary to revoke it, the matter shall be submitted to the judicial committee for discussion and decision, after which the court shall rule to revoke the order for payment and dismiss the creditor's application.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 最高人民法院有关适用督促程序的其他司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 12** If other judicial interpretations of the Supreme People's Court on the application of the supervision procedure are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
