# 最高人民法院关于经济纠纷案件当事人向受诉法院提出管辖权异议的期限问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1990〕10号  
**公布日期**：1990-08-05

---

### 正文

一、人民法院受理的第一审经济纠纷案件，当事人在法律规定的答辩期限内对法院的管辖权提出异议的，法院应当先就本院对该案有无管辖权问题进行审议；逾期提出的，法院不予审议。

**Article None** 1. In a first-instance economic dispute case accepted by a People's Court, if a party raises an objection to the court's jurisdiction within the statutory defense period, the court shall first consider whether it has jurisdiction over the case; if the objection is raised after the expiration of the period, the court shall not consider it.

二、当事人在法律规定的答辩期限内对法院的管辖权提出了异议，但是在法院就有无管辖权问题作出裁定前，又以书面或口头形式（须经法院记录在案并经本人签字）表示接受受诉法院管辖的，视为当事人自动放弃了异议。以后，当事人在诉讼中再行提出管辖异议的，法院不再审议。

**Article None** 2. If a party raises an objection to the court's jurisdiction within the statutory defense period but, before the court renders a ruling on whether it has jurisdiction, expresses acceptance of the jurisdiction of the court accepting the case in written or oral form (which shall be recorded by the court and signed by the party), it shall be deemed that the party has voluntarily waived the objection. Thereafter, if the party raises a jurisdictional objection again during the litigation, the court shall not consider it.

三、一、二审法院驳回管辖权异议的裁定发生法律效力后，当事人就法院的管辖权问题申诉的，不影响法院对案件进行审理。

**Article None** 3. After a ruling of the court of first instance or second instance rejecting a jurisdictional objection becomes legally effective, if a party petitions regarding the jurisdictional issue of the court, it shall not affect the court's adjudication of the case.

四、法院对案件作出的判决发生法律效力后，如果当事人对驳回管辖权异议的裁定和判决一并申诉的，法院经过复查，发现管辖虽有错误，但判决正确的，应当不再变动；如经复查，认为管辖和判决均确有错误，应按审判监督程序处理。经过再审或者提审，原判决和裁定均被撤销的，应将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院审理。  
1990年8月5日

**Article None** 4. After a judgment rendered by the court on a case becomes legally effective, if a party petitions against both the ruling that rejected the jurisdictional objection and the judgment together, the court, after review, finding that although the jurisdiction is erroneous, the judgment is correct, shall not modify it; if upon review, it finds that both the jurisdiction and the judgment are indeed erroneous, it shall handle the case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure. If, after retrial or trial by a higher court, both the original judgment and ruling are set aside, the case shall be transferred to a People's Court with jurisdiction for adjudication.  
August 5, 1990
