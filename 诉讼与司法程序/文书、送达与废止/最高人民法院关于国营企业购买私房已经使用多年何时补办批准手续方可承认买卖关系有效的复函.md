### 最高人民法院关于国营企业购买私房已经使用多年何时补办批准手续方可承认买卖关系有效的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于国营企业购买私房已经使用多年何时补办  
  
批准手续方可承认买卖关系有效的复函  
  
（1994）法民字第28号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于国营企业购买私房已经使用多年何时补办批准手续方可承认买卖关系有效的请示报告》收悉。经研究认为，处理此类案件，应本着实事求是的原则，根据案件的具体情况具体分析。如果当事人双方订立买卖协议时意思表示真实，买方已交付房款并使用房屋多年。在诉讼前或诉讼中补办了批准手续，经人民法院审查，补办的手续符合法律规定，依照我院（1985）法民字第14号批复的精神，可承认双方的买卖关系有效。  
  
1995年1月6日

**Article 1** No. 28 (1994) Fa Min Zi  
Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province:  
  
We have received your Request for Instructions on When Retroactive Approval Procedures Should Be Completed to Recognize the Validity of a Sales Relationship Where a State-Owned Enterprise Purchased a Private House and Has Used It for Many Years. Upon study, it is held that in handling such cases, the principle of seeking truth from facts shall be followed, and specific analysis shall be conducted based on the specific circumstances of each case. If, at the time of concluding the sales agreement, both parties' expression of intent was genuine, the buyer has paid the house price and used the house for many years, and the approval procedures were retroactively completed before or during the litigation, and upon examination by the People's Court, such retroactively completed procedures comply with legal provisions, then, in accordance with the spirit of the Reply No. 14 (1985) Fa Min Zi of our Court, the sales relationship between both parties may be recognized as valid.  
  
January 6, 1995
