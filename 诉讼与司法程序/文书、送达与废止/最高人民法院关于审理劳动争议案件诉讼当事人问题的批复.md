<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于审理劳动争议案件诉讼  
当事人问题的批复  
1988年10月19日      法（经）复〔1988〕50号  
陕西省高级人民法院：  
你院陕高法研〔1988〕43号“关于审理劳动争议案件诉讼当事人问题的请示”收悉，经研究答复如下：  
同意你院的意见。即：劳动争议当事人不服劳动争议仲裁委员会的仲裁决定，向人民法院起诉，争议的双方仍然是企业与职工。双方当事人在适用法律上和诉讼地位上是平等的。此类案件不是行政案件。人民法院在审理时，应以争议的双方为诉讼当事人，不应把劳动争议仲裁委员会列为被告或第三人。  
附：  
陕西省高级人民法院  
关于审理劳动争议案件诉讼当事人问题的请示  
1988年8月2日      陕高法研〔1988〕43号  
最高人民法院：  
当前我省法院在审理劳动争议案件中，由于一些法院对于劳动争议案件中的诉讼当事人问题有不同的认识，把劳动争议仲裁委员会作为被告传唤出庭应诉。陕西省劳动争议仲裁委员会对此向我院提出意见，要求明确解决。此问题经我们研究认为：劳动争议当事人双方的法律地位是平等的，一方或者双方当事人对于仲裁机关的仲裁决定不服时，都有权依法向人民法院起诉。起诉后其争议的主体没有变，仍是原企业法人与被处理的职工。人民法院在审理此类案件时，仍应以原争议双方为诉讼当事人，不应把劳动争议仲裁委员会列为被告人或者以第三人让其参加诉讼。  
妥否？请指示。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Issues Concerning the Parties to Proceedings in Trials of Labor Disputes  
October 19, 1988 Fa (Jing) Fu [1988] No. 50  
Higher People's Court of Shaanxi Province:  
Your Court's Shaan Gao Fa Yan [1988] No. 43 "Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning the Parties to Proceedings in Trials of Labor Disputes" has been received. After study, we reply as follows:  
We agree with your opinion. That is: where a party to a labor dispute, being dissatisfied with the arbitration award of the labor dispute arbitration committee, initiates a prosecution in the People's Court, the parties to the dispute shall remain the enterprise and the employee. The two parties shall be equal in the application of law and in their litigation status. Such cases are not administrative cases. When trying such a case, the People's Court shall treat both parties to the dispute as the parties to the proceedings, and shall not list the labor dispute arbitration committee as the defendant or a third party.  
Attachment:  
Higher People's Court of Shaanxi Province  
Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning the Parties to Proceedings in Trials of Labor Disputes  
August 2, 1988 Shaan Gao Fa Yan [1988] No. 43  
Supreme People's Court:  
Currently, when trying labor dispute cases, some courts in our province hold different understandings on the issue of parties to proceedings in labor dispute cases and have summoned the labor dispute arbitration committee as a defendant to appear in court and respond to the action. The Shaanxi Provincial Labor Dispute Arbitration Committee has submitted an opinion to our court on this matter, requesting a clear resolution. After our study, we believe that: the legal status of the two parties to a labor dispute is equal. Where one party or both parties are dissatisfied with the arbitration award of the arbitration authority, they all have the right to initiate a prosecution in the People's Court according to law. After the prosecution, the subject of the dispute shall not change, and shall remain the original enterprise legal person and the employee being disciplined. When trying such a case, the People's Court shall still treat the original parties to the dispute as the parties to the proceedings, and shall not list the labor dispute arbitration committee as the defendant or allow it to participate in the proceedings as a third party.  
Is this proper? Please advise.
