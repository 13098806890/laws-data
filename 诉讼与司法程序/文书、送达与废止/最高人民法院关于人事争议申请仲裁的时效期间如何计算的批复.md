四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于事业单位人事争议仲裁时效如何计算的请示》（川高法〔2012〕430号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
依据[《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》第二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法.md#art-27)第一款、第五十二条的规定，当事人自知道或者应当知道其权利被侵害之日起一年内申请仲裁，仲裁机构予以受理的，人民法院应予认可。

**Article 1** September 12, 2013  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Calculate the Limitation Period for Applying for Arbitration of Personnel Disputes  
Interpretation No. 23 [2013] of the Supreme People's Court  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan:  
Your Request on How to Calculate the Limitation Period for Arbitration of Personnel Disputes in Public Institutions (Chuan Gao Fa [2012] No. 430) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 27 and Article 52 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Mediation and Arbitration of Labor Disputes, if a party applies for arbitration within one year from the date on which it knows or should have known that its rights have been infringed, and the arbitration institution accepts the application, the People's Court shall uphold it.
