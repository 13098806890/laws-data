为进一步加强民事审判工作，依法保护当事人的合法权益，根据有关法律规定和审判实际需要，决定废止2007年底以前发布的27件司法解释（第七批）。废止的司法解释从公布之日起不再适用，但过去适用下列司法解释对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。  
  
予以废止的2007年底以前发布的有关司法解释目录（第七批）

**Article 1** December 18, 2008  
Supreme People's Court  
Regarding the Abolition of Judicial Interpretations Promulgated Before the End of 2007  
Decision on Relevant Judicial Interpretations (Seventh Batch)  
Fa Shi [2008] No. 15  
To further strengthen civil adjudication work and protect the lawful rights and interests of parties according to law, and in accordance with relevant legal provisions and the actual needs of adjudication, it is decided to abolish 27 judicial interpretations (Seventh Batch) promulgated before the end of 2007. The abolished judicial interpretations shall no longer apply from the date of promulgation, but judgments and rulings made in relevant cases by applying the following judicial interpretations in the past shall remain valid.
