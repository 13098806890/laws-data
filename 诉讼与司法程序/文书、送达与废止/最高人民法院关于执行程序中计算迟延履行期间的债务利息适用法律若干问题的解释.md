# 最高人民法院关于执行程序中计算迟延履行期间的债务利息适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕8号  
**公布日期**：2014-06-09  
**生效日期**：2014-08-01

> 最高人民法院《关于执行程序中计算迟延履行期间的债务利息适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2014年6月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1619次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年8月1日起施行。 2014年7月7日 最高人民法院 关于执行程序中计算迟延履行期间的债务利息 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2014〕8号 为规范执行程序中迟延履行期间债务利息的计算，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的规定，结合司法实践，制定本解释。 第一条   根据民事诉讼法第二百五十三条规定加倍计算之后的迟延履行期间的债务利息，包括迟延履行期间的一般债务利息和加倍部分债务利息。 迟延履行期间的一般债务利息，根据生效法律文书确定的方法计算；生效法律文书未确定给付该利息的，不予计算。 加倍部分债务利息的计算方法为：加倍部分债务利息=债务人尚未清偿的生效法律文书确定的除一般债务利息之外的金钱债务×日万分之一点七五×迟延履行期间。 第二条   加倍部分债务利息自生效法律文书确定的履行期间届满之日起计算；生效法律文书确定分期履行的，自每次履行期间届满之日起计算；生效法律文书未确定履行期间的，自法律文书生效之日起计算。 第三条   加倍部分债务利息计算至被执行人履行完毕之日；被执行人分次履行的，相应部分的加倍部分债务利息计算至每次履行完毕之日。 人民法院划拨、提取被执行人的存款、收入、股息、红利等财产的，相应部分的加倍部分债务利息计算至划拨、提取之日；人民法院对被执行人财产拍卖、变卖或者以物抵债的，计算至成交裁定或者抵债裁定生效之日；人民法院对被执行人财产通过其他方式变价的，计算至财产变价完成之日。 非因被执行人的申请，对生效法律文书审查而中止或者暂缓执行的期间及再审中止执行的期间，不计算加倍部分债务利息。 第四条   被执行人的财产不足以清偿全部债务的，应当先清偿生效法律文书确定的金钱债务，再清偿加倍部分债务利息，但当事人对清偿顺序另有约定的除外。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   根据[民事诉讼法第二百五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-253)规定加倍计算之后的迟延履行期间的债务利息，包括迟延履行期间的一般债务利息和加倍部分债务利息。  
迟延履行期间的一般债务利息，根据生效法律文书确定的方法计算；生效法律文书未确定给付该利息的，不予计算。  
加倍部分债务利息的计算方法为：加倍部分债务利息=债务人尚未清偿的生效法律文书确定的除一般债务利息之外的金钱债务×日万分之一点七五×迟延履行期间。

**Article 1** The debt interest during the period of delayed performance after double calculation under Article 253 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall include the general debt interest and the double portion of debt interest during the period of delayed performance.  
The general debt interest during the period of delayed performance shall be calculated according to the method determined by the effective legal document; if the effective legal document does not determine the payment of such interest, it shall not be calculated.  
The calculation method for the double portion of debt interest is: double portion of debt interest = monetary debt other than the general debt interest determined by the effective legal document that the debtor has not yet satisfied × 0.000175 per day × period of delayed performance.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   加倍部分债务利息自生效法律文书确定的履行期间届满之日起计算；生效法律文书确定分期履行的，自每次履行期间届满之日起计算；生效法律文书未确定履行期间的，自法律文书生效之日起计算。

**Article 2** The double portion of debt interest shall be calculated from the date on which the performance period determined by the effective legal document expires; if the effective legal document determines performance by installments, it shall be calculated from the date on which each performance period expires; if the effective legal document does not determine a performance period, it shall be calculated from the date on which the legal document takes effect.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   加倍部分债务利息计算至被执行人履行完毕之日；被执行人分次履行的，相应部分的加倍部分债务利息计算至每次履行完毕之日。  
人民法院划拨、提取被执行人的存款、收入、股息、红利等财产的，相应部分的加倍部分债务利息计算至划拨、提取之日；人民法院对被执行人财产拍卖、变卖或者以物抵债的，计算至成交裁定或者抵债裁定生效之日；人民法院对被执行人财产通过其他方式变价的，计算至财产变价完成之日。  
非因被执行人的申请，对生效法律文书审查而中止或者暂缓执行的期间及再审中止执行的期间，不计算加倍部分债务利息。

**Article 3** The double portion of debt interest shall be calculated until the date on which the person subject to enforcement fulfills the obligation; if the person subject to enforcement fulfills the obligation in installments, the double portion of debt interest for the corresponding part shall be calculated until the date on which each installment is fulfilled.  
Where the People's Court transfers or withdraws the deposits, income, dividends, bonus shares and other property of the person subject to enforcement, the double portion of debt interest for the corresponding part shall be calculated until the date of such transfer or withdrawal; where the People's Court auctions, sells by public auction, or transfers property in satisfaction of a debt of the person subject to enforcement, it shall be calculated until the date on which the ruling of transaction or the ruling of debt settlement takes effect; where the People's Court realizes the property of the person subject to enforcement through other means, it shall be calculated until the date on which the property realization is completed.  
The period during which the enforcement is suspended or stayed due to the review of an effective legal document not upon the application of the person subject to enforcement, and the period during which the enforcement is suspended due to a retrial, shall not be counted for the double portion of debt interest.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   被执行人的财产不足以清偿全部债务的，应当先清偿生效法律文书确定的金钱债务，再清偿加倍部分债务利息，但当事人对清偿顺序另有约定的除外。

**Article 4** Where the property of the person subject to enforcement is insufficient to satisfy all debts, the monetary debt determined by the effective legal document shall be satisfied first, and then the double portion of debt interest shall be satisfied, unless the parties have otherwise agreed on the order of satisfaction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   生效法律文书确定给付外币的，执行时以该种外币按日万分之一点七五计算加倍部分债务利息，但申请执行人主张以人民币计算的，人民法院应予准许。  
以人民币计算加倍部分债务利息的，应当先将生效法律文书确定的外币折算或者套算为人民币后再进行计算。  
外币折算或者套算为人民币的，按照加倍部分债务利息起算之日的中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构公布的人民币对该外币的中间价折合成人民币计算；中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构未公布汇率中间价的外币，按照该日境内银行人民币对该外币的中间价折算成人民币，或者该外币在境内银行、国际外汇市场对美元汇率，与人民币对美元汇率中间价进行套算。

**Article 5** Where the effective legal document determines the payment of foreign currency, the double portion of debt interest shall be calculated in such foreign currency at 0.000175 per day; provided that if the enforcing applicant claims calculation in RMB, the People's Court shall permit it.  
Where the double portion of debt interest is calculated in RMB, the foreign currency determined by the effective legal document shall first be converted or cross-calculated into RMB before calculation.  
Where the foreign currency is converted or cross-calculated into RMB, it shall be converted into RMB according to the central parity rate of RMB against such foreign currency published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or the institution authorized by the People's Bank of China on the date of the start of calculation of the double portion of debt interest; for foreign currencies for which the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or the institution authorized by the People's Bank of China has not published a central parity rate, it shall be converted into RMB according to the central parity rate of RMB against such foreign currency published by domestic banks on that date, or cross-calculated based on the exchange rate of such foreign currency against the US dollar in domestic banks or the international foreign exchange market and the central parity rate of RMB against the US dollar.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   执行回转程序中，原申请执行人迟延履行金钱给付义务的，应当按照本解释的规定承担加倍部分债务利息。

**Article 6** In the process of execution reversal, if the original enforcing applicant delays performance of the monetary payment obligation, it shall bear the double portion of debt interest in accordance with the provisions of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   本解释施行时尚未执行完毕部分的金钱债务，本解释施行前的迟延履行期间债务利息按照之前的规定计算；施行后的迟延履行期间债务利息按照本解释计算。  
本解释施行前本院发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 7** For the part of monetary debt that has not been fully enforced at the time when this Interpretation comes into force, the debt interest during the period of delayed performance before this Interpretation comes into force shall be calculated in accordance with the previous provisions; the debt interest during the period of delayed performance after coming into force shall be calculated in accordance with this Interpretation.  
If any judicial interpretation issued by this Court before the coming into force of this Interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
