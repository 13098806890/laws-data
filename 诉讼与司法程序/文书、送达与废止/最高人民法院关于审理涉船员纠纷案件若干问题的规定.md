# 最高人民法院关于审理涉船员纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕11号  
**公布日期**：2020-09-29  
**生效日期**：2020-09-29

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉船员纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2020年 6月 8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第 1803次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年9月29日起施行。 2020年9月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉船员纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2020〕11号 为正确审理涉船员纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》《中华人民共和国海商法》《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　船员与船舶所有人之间的劳动争议不涉及船员登船、在船工作、离船遣返，当事人直接向海事法院提起诉讼的，海事法院告知当事人依照《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》的规定处理。 第二条　船员与船舶所有人之间的劳务合同纠纷，当事人向原告住所地、合同签订地、船员登船港或者离船港所在地、被告住所地海事法院提起诉讼的，海事法院应予受理。 第三条　船员服务机构仅代理船员办理相关手续，或者仅为船员提供就业信息，且不属于劳务派遣情形，船员服务机构主张其与船员仅成立居间或委托合同关系的，应予支持。 第四条　船舶所有人以被挂靠单位的名义对外经营，船舶所有人未与船员签订书面劳动合同，其聘用的船员因工伤亡，船员主张被挂靠单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位的，应予支持。船舶所有人与船员成立劳动关系的除外。 第五条　与船员登船、在船工作、离船遣返无关的劳动争议提交劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁，仲裁庭根据船员的申请，就船员工资和其他劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或赔偿金裁决先予执行的，移送地方人民法院审查。 船员申请扣押船舶的，仲裁庭应将扣押船舶申请提交船籍港所在地或者船舶所在地的海事法院审查，或交地方人民法院委托船籍港所在地或者船舶所在地的海事法院审查。 第六条　具有船舶优先权的海事请求，船员未依照《中华人民共和国海商法》第二十八条的规定请求扣押产生船舶优先权的船舶，仅请求确认其在一定期限内对该产生船舶优先权的船舶享有优先权的，应予支持。 前款规定的期限自优先权产生之日起以一年为限。 第七条　具有船舶优先权的海事请求，船员未申请限制船舶继续营运，仅申请对船舶采取限制处分、限制抵押等保全措施的，应予支持。船员主张该保全措施构成《中华人民共和国海商法》第二十八条规定的船舶扣押的，不予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　船员与船舶所有人之间的劳动争议不涉及船员登船、在船工作、离船遣返，当事人直接向海事法院提起诉讼的，海事法院告知当事人依照《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》的规定处理。

**Article 1** Where a labor dispute between a seafarer and the shipowner does not involve the seafarer's boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship, and the party directly initiates a lawsuit in a maritime court, the maritime court shall inform the party that the dispute shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Mediation and Arbitration of Labor Disputes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　船员与船舶所有人之间的劳务合同纠纷，当事人向原告住所地、合同签订地、船员登船港或者离船港所在地、被告住所地海事法院提起诉讼的，海事法院应予受理。

**Article 2** Where a dispute over a service contract between a seafarer and the shipowner is initiated by a party in a maritime court of the place of the plaintiff's domicile, the place of contract conclusion, the port of the seafarer's boarding or discharge, or the place of the defendant's domicile, the maritime court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　船员服务机构仅代理船员办理相关手续，或者仅为船员提供就业信息，且不属于劳务派遣情形，船员服务机构主张其与船员仅成立居间或委托合同关系的，应予支持。

**Article 3** Where a seafarer service agency merely acts as an agent for the seafarer in handling relevant procedures or merely provides employment information to the seafarer, and the situation does not constitute labor dispatch, and the seafarer service agency claims that it has only established a brokerage or agency contract relationship with the seafarer, such claim shall be upheld.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶所有人以被挂靠单位的名义对外经营，船舶所有人未与船员签订书面劳动合同，其聘用的船员因工伤亡，船员主张被挂靠单位为承担工伤保险责任的单位的，应予支持。船舶所有人与船员成立劳动关系的除外。

**Article 4** Where a shipowner operates externally in the name of a entity under whose name the ship is operated, the shipowner fails to sign a written labor contract with the seafarer, and a seafarer employed by the shipowner suffers a work-related injury or death, and the seafarer claims that the entity under whose name the ship is operated shall bear the liability for work-related injury insurance, such claim shall be upheld. However, this shall not apply where a labor relationship is established between the shipowner and the seafarer.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　与船员登船、在船工作、离船遣返无关的劳动争议提交劳动争议仲裁委员会仲裁，仲裁庭根据船员的申请，就船员工资和其他劳动报酬、工伤医疗费、经济补偿或赔偿金裁决先予执行的，移送地方人民法院审查。  
船员申请扣押船舶的，仲裁庭应将扣押船舶申请提交船籍港所在地或者船舶所在地的海事法院审查，或交地方人民法院委托船籍港所在地或者船舶所在地的海事法院审查。

**Article 5** Where a labor dispute unrelated to the seafarer's boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship is submitted to a labor dispute arbitration commission for arbitration, and the arbitral tribunal, upon the seafarer's application, makes an award on advance execution concerning the seafarer's wages and other labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation or damages, the case shall be transferred to the local people's court for examination.  
Where a seafarer applies for the arrest of a ship, the arbitral tribunal shall submit the application for ship arrest to the maritime court of the place of the ship's port of registry or the place where the ship is located for examination, or refer it to the local people's court to entrust the maritime court of the place of the ship's port of registry or the place where the ship is located for examination.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　具有船舶优先权的海事请求，船员未依照[《中华人民共和国海商法》第二十八条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-28)的规定请求扣押产生船舶优先权的船舶，仅请求确认其在一定期限内对该产生船舶优先权的船舶享有优先权的，应予支持。  
前款规定的期限自优先权产生之日起以一年为限。

**Article 6** Where, in respect of a maritime claim secured by a maritime lien, the seafarer does not apply for the arrest of the ship giving rise to the maritime lien in accordance with Article 28 of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China, but only requests confirmation that he or she enjoys a lien on the ship giving rise to the maritime lien within a certain period, such request shall be upheld.  
The period specified in the preceding paragraph shall be limited to one year from the date on which the maritime lien arises.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　具有船舶优先权的海事请求，船员未申请限制船舶继续营运，仅申请对船舶采取限制处分、限制抵押等保全措施的，应予支持。船员主张该保全措施构成[《中华人民共和国海商法》第二十八条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-28)规定的船舶扣押的，不予支持。

**Article 7** Where, in respect of a maritime claim secured by a maritime lien, the seafarer does not apply for restricting the continued operation of the ship but only applies for preservation measures such as restricting the disposition of or mortgage on the ship, such application shall be upheld. Where the seafarer claims that such preservation measures constitute an arrest of the ship as provided for in Article 28 of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China, such claim shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　因登船、在船工作、离船遣返产生的下列工资、其他劳动报酬，船员主张船舶优先权的，应予支持：  
（一）正常工作时间的报酬或基本工资；  
（二）延长工作时间的加班工资，休息日、法定休假日加班工资；  
（三）在船服务期间的奖金、相关津贴和补贴，以及特殊情况下支付的工资等；  
（四）未按期支付上述款项产生的孳息。  
《中华人民共和国劳动法》和《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》中规定的相关经济补偿金、赔偿金，未依据[《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》第八十二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国劳动合同法.md#art-82)之规定签订书面劳动合同而应支付的双倍工资，以及因未按期支付本款规定的前述费用而产生的孳息，船员主张船舶优先权的，不予支持。

**Article 8** Where a seafarer claims a maritime lien for the following wages and other labor remuneration arising from boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship, such claim shall be upheld:  
(1) Remuneration for normal working hours or basic wages;  
(2) Overtime wages for extended working hours, overtime wages for rest days and statutory holidays;  
(3) Bonuses, relevant allowances and subsidies during the period of service onboard, and wages paid under special circumstances;  
(4) Accrued interest arising from the late payment of the above amounts.  
Where a seafarer claims a maritime lien for the relevant economic compensation or damages provided for in the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China and the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, the double wages payable for failure to sign a written labor contract in accordance with Article 82 of the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, and the accrued interest arising from the late payment of the aforementioned fees under this paragraph, such claim shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船员因登船、在船工作、离船遣返而产生的工资、其他劳动报酬、船员遣返费用、社会保险费用，船舶所有人未依约支付，第三方向船员垫付全部或部分费用，船员将相应的海事请求权转让给第三方，第三方就受让的海事请求权请求确认或行使船舶优先权的，应予支持。

**Article 9** Where a shipowner fails to pay the wages, other labor remuneration, repatriation expenses, and social insurance costs arising from the seafarer's boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship as agreed, and a third party advances all or part of such expenses to the seafarer, the seafarer assigns the corresponding maritime claim to the third party, and the third party requests confirmation of or exercise of a maritime lien in respect of the assigned maritime claim, such request shall be upheld.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船员境外工作期间被遗弃，或遭遇其他突发事件，船舶所有人或其财务担保人、船员外派机构未承担相应责任，船员请求财务担保人、船员外派机构从财务担保费用、海员外派备用金中先行支付紧急救助所需相关费用的，应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a seafarer is abandoned during overseas employment or encounters other emergencies, and the shipowner or its financial guarantor or the seafarer dispatching agency fails to bear the corresponding liability, and the seafarer requests the financial guarantor or the seafarer dispatching agency to pay in advance the relevant expenses necessary for emergency rescue from the financial guarantee fees or the seafarer dispatch reserve fund, such request shall be upheld.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于船员工资构成是否涵盖船员登船、在船工作、离船遣返期间的工作日加班工资、休息日加班工资、法定休假日加班工资，当事人有约定并主张依据约定确定双方加班工资的，应予支持。但约定标准低于法定最低工资标准的，不予支持。

**Article 11** Where the parties have an agreement on whether the composition of the seafarer's wages covers overtime wages for working days, rest days, and statutory holidays during the period of boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship, and the parties claim that the overtime wages of both parties shall be determined in accordance with such agreement, such claim shall be upheld. However, if the agreed standard is lower than the statutory minimum wage standard, such claim shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　标准工时制度下，船员就休息日加班主张加班工资，船舶所有人举证证明已做补休安排，不应按法定标准支付加班工资的，应予支持。综合计算工时工作制下，船员对综合计算周期内的工作时间总量超过标准工作时间总量的部分主张加班工资的，应予支持。  
船员就法定休假日加班主张加班工资，船舶所有人抗辩对法定休假日加班已做补休安排，不应支付法定休假日加班工资的，对船舶所有人的抗辩不予支持。双方另有约定的除外。

**Article 12** Under the standard working hours system, where a seafarer claims overtime wages for work performed on rest days, and the shipowner provides evidence proving that compensatory leave has been arranged, and claims that overtime wages shall not be paid in accordance with the statutory standard, such claim shall be upheld. Under the comprehensive working hours system, where a seafarer claims overtime wages for the part of the total working hours within the comprehensive calculation period that exceeds the total standard working hours, such claim shall be upheld.  
Where a seafarer claims overtime wages for work performed on statutory holidays, and the shipowner argues that compensatory leave has been arranged for work on statutory holidays and that overtime wages for statutory holidays shall not be paid, the shipowner's argument shall not be upheld. However, this shall not apply where the parties have agreed otherwise.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人对船员工资或其他劳动报酬的支付标准、支付方式未作约定或约定不明，当事人主张以同工种、同级别、同时期市场的平均标准确定的，应予支持。

**Article 13** Where the parties have not agreed on or have ambiguously agreed on the payment standard or method of the seafarer's wages or other labor remuneration, and a party claims that such standard or method shall be determined by reference to the average market standard for the same type of work, the same level, and the same period, such claim shall be upheld.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船员因受欺诈、受胁迫在禁渔期、禁渔区或使用禁用的工具、方法捕捞水产品，或者捕捞珍稀、濒危海洋生物，或者进行其他违法作业，对船员主张的登船、在船工作、离船遣返期间的船员工资、其他劳动报酬，应予支持。  
船舶所有人举证证明船员对违法作业自愿且明知的，对船员的上述请求不予支持。  
船舶所有人或者船员的行为应受行政处罚或涉嫌刑事犯罪的，依照相关法定程序处理。

**Article 14** Where a seafarer, due to being defrauded or coerced, engages in fishing aquatic products during a closed fishing season or in a closed fishing area, or uses prohibited tools or methods, or catches rare or endangered marine organisms, or engages in other illegal operations, the seafarer's claim for wages and other labor remuneration for the period of boarding of the ship, performance of work onboard, or repatriation and discharge from the ship shall be upheld.  
If the shipowner provides evidence proving that the seafarer voluntarily and knowingly participated in the illegal operations, the seafarer's aforementioned claims shall not be upheld.  
If the conduct of the shipowner or the seafarer is subject to administrative penalties or is suspected of constituting a criminal offence, it shall be handled in accordance with relevant statutory procedures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船员因劳务受到损害，船舶所有人举证证明船员自身存在过错，并请求判令船员自担相应责任的，对船舶所有人的抗辩予以支持。

**Article 15** Where a seafarer suffers damage due to the service, and the shipowner provides evidence proving that the seafarer is at fault, and requests the court to order the seafarer to bear the corresponding liability, the shipowner's defense shall be upheld.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　因第三人的原因遭受工伤，船员对第三人提起民事诉讼请求民事赔偿，第三人以船员已获得工伤保险待遇为由，抗辩其不应承担民事赔偿责任的，对第三人的抗辩不予支持。但船员已经获得医疗费用的，对船员关于医疗费用的诉讼请求不予支持。

**Article 16** Where a seafarer suffers a work-related injury due to a third party's act, and the seafarer initiates a civil action against the third party claiming civil compensation, and the third party argues that it shall not bear civil compensation liability on the ground that the seafarer has already obtained work-related injury insurance benefits, the third party's defense shall not be upheld. However, if the seafarer has already obtained medical expenses, the seafarer's claim for medical expenses shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　船员与船舶所有人之间的劳动合同具有涉外因素，当事人请求依照[《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法》第四十三条](../../民事与商事/外商与涉外/中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法.md#art-43)确定应适用的法律的，应予支持。  
船员与船舶所有人之间的劳务合同，当事人没有选择应适用的法律，当事人主张适用劳务派出地、船舶所有人主营业地、船旗国法律的，应予支持。  
船员与船员服务机构之间，以及船员服务机构与船舶所有人之间的居间或委托协议，当事人未选择应适用的法律，当事人主张适用与该合同有最密切联系的法律的，应予支持。

**Article 17** Where a labor contract between a seafarer and a shipowner contains a foreign-related element, and a party requests that the applicable law be determined in accordance with Article 43 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations, such request shall be upheld.  
Where the parties have not chosen the law applicable to a service contract between the seafarer and the shipowner, and a party claims that the law of the place of dispatch, the shipowner's principal place of business, or the flag State shall apply, such claim shall be upheld.  
Where the parties have not chosen the law applicable to a brokerage or agency agreement between the seafarer and a seafarer service agency, or between the seafarer service agency and the shipowner, and a party claims that the law having the closest connection with the contract shall apply, such claim shall be upheld.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定中的船舶所有人，包括光船承租人、船舶管理人、船舶经营人。

**Article 18** The term "shipowner" in these Provisions includes the bareboat charterer, ship manager, and ship operator.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall apply to cases that have not been finally adjudicated after these Provisions come into force; these Provisions shall not apply to cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, for which a party applies for a retrial or which are decided to be retried in accordance with the trial supervision procedure.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本院以前发布的规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 20** If any provisions previously issued by this Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定自2020年9月29日起实施。

**Article 21** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 29, 2020.
