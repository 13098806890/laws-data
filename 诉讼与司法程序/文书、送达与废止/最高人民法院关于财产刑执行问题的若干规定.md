# 最高人民法院关于财产刑执行问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕4号  
**公布日期**：2009-11-30  
**生效日期**：2010-06-01

> 最高人民法院《关于财产刑执行问题的若干规定》已于2009年11月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1478次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年6月1日起施行。 2010年2月10日 最高人民法院 关于财产刑执行问题的若干规定 法释〔2010〕4号 为完善财产刑的执行制度，规范财产刑的执行工作，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等法律规定，制定本规定。 第一条  财产刑由第一审人民法院负责裁判执行的机构执行。 被执行的财产在异地的，第一审人民法院可以委托财产所在地的同级人民法院代为执行。 第二条  第一审人民法院应当在本院作出的刑事判决、裁定生效后，或者收到上级人民法院生效的刑事判决、裁定后，对有关财产刑执行的法律文书立案执行。 第三条  对罚金的执行，被执行人在判决、裁定确定的期限内未足额缴纳的，人民法院应当在期满后强制缴纳。 对没收财产的执行，人民法院应当立即执行。 第四条  人民法院应当依法对被执行人的财产状况进行调查，发现有可供执行的财产，需要查封、扣押、冻结的，应当及时采取查封、扣押、冻结等强制执行措施。 第五条  执行财产刑时，案外人对被执行财产提出权属异议的，人民法院应当审查并参照民事诉讼法的有关规定处理。 第六条  被判处罚金或者没收财产，同时又承担刑事附带民事诉讼赔偿责任的被执行人，应当先履行对被害人的民事赔偿责任。 判处财产刑之前被执行人所负正当债务，应当偿还的，经债权人请求，先行予以偿还。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  财产刑由第一审人民法院负责裁判执行的机构执行。  
被执行的财产在异地的，第一审人民法院可以委托财产所在地的同级人民法院代为执行。

**Article 1** A pecuniary penalty shall be enforced by the organ responsible for the enforcement of judgments and rulings of the People's Court of first instance.  
Where the property to be enforced is located in another jurisdiction, the People's Court of first instance may entrust the People's Court at the same level in the place where the property is located to enforce on its behalf.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  第一审人民法院应当在本院作出的刑事判决、裁定生效后，或者收到上级人民法院生效的刑事判决、裁定后，对有关财产刑执行的法律文书立案执行。

**Article 2** The People's Court of first instance shall, after the criminal judgment or ruling made by it has taken effect, or after it has received an effective criminal judgment or ruling from a People's Court at a higher level, accept and enforce the legal documents relating to the enforcement of pecuniary penalties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  对罚金的执行，被执行人在判决、裁定确定的期限内未足额缴纳的，人民法院应当在期满后强制缴纳。  
对没收财产的执行，人民法院应当立即执行。

**Article 3** With respect to the enforcement of a fine, if the person subject to enforcement fails to pay in full within the time limit prescribed by the judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall enforce the payment compulsorily after the expiration of such time limit.  
With respect to the enforcement of confiscation of property, the People's Court shall enforce it immediately.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院应当依法对被执行人的财产状况进行调查，发现有可供执行的财产，需要查封、扣押、冻结的，应当及时采取查封、扣押、冻结等强制执行措施。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall, according to law, investigate the property status of the person subject to enforcement. If it discovers that there is property available for enforcement and needs to be sealed up, detained or frozen, it shall promptly take compulsory enforcement measures such as sealing up, detaining or freezing.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  执行财产刑时，案外人对被执行财产提出权属异议的，人民法院应当审查并参照民事诉讼法的有关规定处理。

**Article 5** When enforcing a pecuniary penalty, if a person other than a party raises an objection to the ownership of the property to be enforced, the People's Court shall examine the objection and handle it by reference to the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  被判处罚金或者没收财产，同时又承担刑事附带民事诉讼赔偿责任的被执行人，应当先履行对被害人的民事赔偿责任。  
判处财产刑之前被执行人所负正当债务，应当偿还的，经债权人请求，先行予以偿还。

**Article 6** Where a person subject to enforcement is sentenced to a fine or confiscation of property and also bears the liability for compensation in a criminal incidental civil action, he shall first perform the civil compensation liability to the victim.  
Where, before the imposition of the pecuniary penalty, the person subject to enforcement has incurred legitimate debts that shall be repaid, upon the request of the creditor, such debts shall be repaid first.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  执行的财产应当全部上缴国库。  
委托执行的，受托人民法院应当将执行情况连同上缴国库凭据送达委托人民法院；不能执行到位的，应当及时告知委托人民法院。

**Article 7** All property enforced shall be turned over to the State Treasury.  
Where the enforcement is entrusted, the entrusted People's Court shall deliver the enforcement situation together with the receipt of the turnover to the State Treasury to the entrusting People's Court; if the enforcement cannot be completed, it shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定中止执行；中止执行的原因消除后，恢复执行：  
（一）执行标的物系人民法院或者仲裁机构正在审理的案件争议标的物，需等待该案件审理完毕确定权属的；  
（二）案外人对执行标的物提出异议确有理由的；  
（三）其他应当中止执行的情形。  
被执行人没有全部缴纳罚金的，人民法院在任何时候发现被执行人有可供执行的财产，应当随时追缴。

**Article 8** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the enforcement; after the cause for suspension is eliminated, the enforcement shall be resumed:  
(1) The subject matter of enforcement is the subject matter of a dispute in a case being tried by a People's Court or an arbitration institution, and the ownership needs to be determined upon conclusion of the trial of that case;  
(2) The objection raised by a person other than a party to the subject matter of enforcement is indeed well-grounded;  
(3) Other circumstances under which enforcement shall be suspended.  
If the person subject to enforcement has not fully paid the fine, the People's Court shall, whenever it discovers that the person subject to enforcement has property available for enforcement, pursue such property at any time.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定终结执行：  
（一）据以执行的刑事判决、裁定被撤销的；  
（二）被执行人死亡或者被执行死刑，且无财产可供执行的；  
（三）被判处罚金的单位终止，且无财产可供执行的；  
（四）依照[刑法第五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-53)规定免除罚金的；  
（五）其他应当终结执行的情形。  
人民法院裁定终结执行后，发现被执行人有隐匿、转移财产情形的，应当追缴。

**Article 9** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the enforcement:  
(1) The criminal judgment or ruling on which the enforcement is based has been revoked;  
(2) The person subject to enforcement has died or has been executed by death penalty, and has no property available for enforcement;  
(3) The entity sentenced to a fine has been dissolved, and has no property available for enforcement;  
(4) The fine has been remitted in accordance with Article 53 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(5) Other circumstances under which enforcement shall be terminated.  
After the People's Court has ruled to terminate the enforcement, if it discovers that the person subject to enforcement has concealed or transferred property, it shall pursue such property.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  财产刑全部或者部分被撤销的，已经执行的财产应当全部或者部分返还被执行人；无法返还的，应予赔偿。

**Article 10** Where all or part of a pecuniary penalty is revoked, the property that has already been enforced shall be returned in whole or in part to the person subject to enforcement; if it cannot be returned, compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  因遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸缴纳罚金确有困难，被执行人向执行法院申请减少或者免除的，执行法院经审查认为符合法定减免条件的，应当在收到申请后一个月内依法作出裁定准予减免；认为不符合法定减免条件的，裁定驳回申请。

**Article 11** Where the person subject to enforcement has genuine difficulty in paying a fine due to an irresistible disaster, and applies to the enforcing court for reduction or remission, if the enforcing court, after examination, considers that the statutory conditions for reduction or remission are met, it shall, within one month from receipt of the application, make a ruling according to law to grant the reduction or remission; if it considers that the statutory conditions are not met, it shall rule to reject the application.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  人民法院办理财产刑执行案件，本规定没有规定的，参照适用民事执行的有关规定。

**Article 12** Where these Provisions do not provide for matters concerning the handling of cases of enforcement of pecuniary penalties by the People's Court, the relevant provisions on civil enforcement shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  此前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 13** Where any previously issued judicial interpretation is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
