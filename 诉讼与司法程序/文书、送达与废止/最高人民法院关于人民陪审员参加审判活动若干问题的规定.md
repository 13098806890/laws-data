# 最高人民法院关于人民陪审员参加审判活动若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕2号  
**公布日期**：2009-11-23  
**生效日期**：2010-01-14

> 最高人民法院《关于人民陪审员参加审判活动若干问题的规定》已于2009年11月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1477次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年1月14日起施行。 2010年1月12日 最高人民法院 关于人民陪审员参加审判活动若干问题的规定 法释〔2010〕2号 为依法保障和规范人民陪审员参加审判活动，根据《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于完善人民陪审员制度的决定》等法律的规定，结合审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  人民法院审判第一审刑事、民事、行政案件，属于下列情形之一的，由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭进行，适用简易程序审理的案件和法律另有规定的案件除外： （一）涉及群体利益的； （二）涉及公共利益的； （三）人民群众广泛关注的； （四）其他社会影响较大的。 第二条  第一审刑事案件被告人、民事案件原告或者被告、行政案件原告申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭进行。 人民法院征得前款规定的当事人同意由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭审判案件的，视为申请。 第三条  第一审人民法院决定适用普通程序审理案件后应当明确告知本规定第二条的当事人，在收到通知五日内有权申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判案件。 人民法院接到当事人在规定期限内提交的申请后，经审查符合本规定的，应当组成有人民陪审员参加的合议庭进行审判。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院审判第一审刑事、民事、行政案件，属于下列情形之一的，由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭进行，适用简易程序审理的案件和法律另有规定的案件除外：  
（一）涉及群体利益的；  
（二）涉及公共利益的；  
（三）人民群众广泛关注的；  
（四）其他社会影响较大的。

**Article 1** Where the People's Court adjudicates criminal, civil, or administrative cases of first instance that fall under any of the following circumstances, a collegial panel shall be formed jointly by people's assessors and judges, except for cases tried under summary procedures and cases otherwise provided by law:  
(1) involving group interests;  
(2) involving public interests;  
(3) attracting widespread public attention; or  
(4) having a relatively large social impact in other respects.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  第一审刑事案件被告人、民事案件原告或者被告、行政案件原告申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭进行。  
人民法院征得前款规定的当事人同意由人民陪审员和法官共同组成合议庭审判案件的，视为申请。

**Article 2** Where the defendant in a criminal case of first instance, the plaintiff or defendant in a civil case, or the plaintiff in an administrative case makes an application for a trial by a collegial panel that includes people's assessors, a collegial panel shall be formed jointly by people's assessors and judges.  
If the People's Court obtains the consent of the party as stipulated in the preceding paragraph to have the case tried by a collegial panel jointly formed by people's assessors and judges, it shall be deemed an application.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  第一审人民法院决定适用普通程序审理案件后应当明确告知[本规定第二条](#art-2)的当事人，在收到通知五日内有权申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判案件。  
人民法院接到当事人在规定期限内提交的申请后，经审查符合本规定的，应当组成有人民陪审员参加的合议庭进行审判。

**Article 3** After deciding to try a case under ordinary procedures, the People's Court of first instance shall clearly inform the parties set forth in Article 2 of these Provisions that they have the right to apply, within five days of receiving the notice, for a trial by a collegial panel that includes people's assessors.  
Upon receiving an application submitted by a party within the prescribed time limit, the People's Court shall, after examination and confirmation that the conditions set forth in these Provisions are met, form a collegial panel that includes people's assessors to conduct the trial.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院应当在开庭七日前采取电脑生成等方式，从人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定人民陪审员。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall, no later than seven days before the opening of the trial, randomly select and determine people's assessors from the list of people's assessors by methods such as computer generation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  特殊案件需要具有特定专业知识的人民陪审员参加审判的，人民法院可以在具有相应专业知识的人民陪审员范围内随机抽取。

**Article 5** Where special cases require the participation of people's assessors with specific professional knowledge in the trial, the People's Court may randomly select from among those people's assessors who possess such corresponding professional knowledge.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民陪审员确有正当理由不能参加审判活动，或者当事人申请其回避的理由经审查成立的，人民法院应当及时重新确定其他人选。

**Article 6** Where a people's assessor is unable to participate in adjudication activities due to justifiable reasons, or where the party's application for withdrawal of such assessor is found to be well-grounded upon examination, the People's Court shall promptly determine another person in lieu thereof.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  人民陪审员参加合议庭评议案件时，有权对事实认定、法律适用独立发表意见，并独立行使表决权。  
人民陪审员评议案件时应当围绕事实认定、法律适用充分发表意见并说明理由。

**Article 7** When a people's assessor participates in the deliberation of a case by the collegial panel, he or she has the right to independently express opinions on the determination of facts and the application of law, and to independently exercise the right to vote.  
When deliberating a case, a people's assessor shall fully express opinions and state reasons centering on the determination of facts and the application of law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  合议庭评议案件时，先由承办法官介绍案件涉及的相关法律、审查判断证据的有关规则，后由人民陪审员及合议庭其他成员充分发表意见，审判长最后发表意见并总结合议庭意见。

**Article 8** When the collegial panel deliberates a case, the presiding judge shall first introduce the relevant laws involved in the case and the rules for examining and evaluating evidence, then the people's assessor and other members of the collegial panel shall fully express their opinions, and the chief judge shall give the final opinion and summarize the collegial panel's opinion.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民陪审员同合议庭其他组成人员意见分歧，要求合议庭将案件提请院长决定是否提交审判委员会讨论决定的，应当说明理由；人民陪审员提出的要求及理由应当写入评议笔录。

**Article 9** Where a people's assessor disagrees with the other members of the collegial panel and requests that the collegial panel refer the case to the president for a decision on whether to submit it to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision, such assessor shall state the reasons; the request and reasons raised by the people's assessor shall be recorded in the deliberation minutes.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民陪审员应当认真阅读评议笔录，确认无误后签名；发现评议笔录与评议内容不一致的，应当要求更正后签名。  
人民陪审员应当审核裁判文书文稿并签名。

**Article 10** A people's assessor shall carefully read the deliberation minutes and sign them after confirming that they are correct; if any inconsistency is found between the deliberation minutes and the content of the deliberation, the assessor shall request corrections before signing.  
A people's assessor shall examine the draft of the adjudicative document and sign it.
