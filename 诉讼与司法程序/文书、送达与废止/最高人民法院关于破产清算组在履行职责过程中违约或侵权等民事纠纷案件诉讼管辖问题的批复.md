### 最高人民法院关于破产清算组在履行职责过程中违约或侵权等民事纠纷案件诉讼管辖问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>中华人民共和国最高人民法院  
  
公　　告  
  
《最高人民法院关于破产清算组在履行职责过程中违约或侵权等民事纠纷案件诉讼管辖问题的批复》已于2004年6月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1317次会议通过。现予公布，自2004年6月28日起施行。  
  
2004年6月21日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于破产清算组在履行职责过程中违约  
  
或侵权等民事纠纷案件诉讼管辖问题的批复  
  
法释〔2004〕5号  
  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鄂高法〔2003〕383号《关于破产清算组在履行职责过程中违约或侵权等民事纠纷案件诉讼管辖问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
企业被宣告破产后，清算组因履行清算职责对他人违约或者侵权引起的民事诉讼，发生在破产程序终结之前的，由受理破产案件的人民法院管辖，在破产程序中一并处理。  
  
此复

**Article 1** June 21, 2004  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Jurisdiction over Civil Action Cases Concerning Breach of Contract or Tort by the Bankruptcy Liquidation Group in the Course of Performing Its Duties  
Fa Shi [2004] No. 5  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
Your Request for Instruction on the Issue of Jurisdiction over Civil Action Cases Concerning Breach of Contract or Tort by the Bankruptcy Liquidation Group in the Course of Performing Its Duties (E Gao Fa [2003] No. 383) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a civil action arising from a breach of contract or tort committed by the liquidation group against another person in the course of performing its liquidation duties occurs before the conclusion of the bankruptcy proceedings, it shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy case, and shall be handled together in the bankruptcy proceedings.  
It is so replied.
