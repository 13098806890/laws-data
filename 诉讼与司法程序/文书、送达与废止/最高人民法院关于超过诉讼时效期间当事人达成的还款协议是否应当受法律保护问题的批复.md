**发布机关**：最高人民法院

### 最高人民法院关于超过诉讼时效期间当事人达成的还款协议是否应当受法律保护问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于超过诉讼时效期间当事人达成的还款协议  
  
是否应当受法律保护问题的批复  
  
法复〔1997〕4号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1996〕116号《关于超过诉讼时效期间达成的还款协议是否应受法律保护问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》第九十条规定的精神，对超过诉讼时效期间，当事人双方就原债务达成还款协议的，应当依法予以保护。  
  
此复。  
  
1997年4月16日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Regarding the Issue of Whether a Repayment Agreement Reached by Parties after the Limitation of Action Period  
Should Be Protected by Law - Reply  
Fa Fu [1997] No. 4  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
We have received your court's Chuan Gao Fa [1996] No. 116 "Request for Instructions on Whether a Repayment Agreement Reached after the Limitation of Action Period Should Be Protected by Law." Upon study, we reply as follows:  
According to the spirit of Article 90 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, where parties reach a repayment agreement on the original debt after the limitation of action period, such agreement shall be protected according to law.  
Thus replies.  
April 16, 1997
