# 最高人民法院关于内地与澳门特别行政区相互认可和执行仲裁裁决的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕17号  
**公布日期**：2007-10-30  
**生效日期**：2008-01-01

> 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区经协商，达成《关于内地与澳门特别行政区相互认可和执行仲裁裁决的安排》（以下简称《安排》），并于2007年10月30日签署。本《安排》已于2007年9月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1437次会议通过，现予公布。根据双方一致意见，本《安排》自2008年1月1日起实施。 2007年12月12日 关于内地与澳门特别行政区 相互认可和执行仲裁裁决的安排 法释〔2007〕17号 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，经最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区协商，现就内地与澳门特别行政区相互认可和执行仲裁裁决的有关事宜达成如下安排： 第一条  内地人民法院认可和执行澳门特别行政区仲裁机构及仲裁员按照澳门特别行政区仲裁法规在澳门作出的民商事仲裁裁决，澳门特别行政区法院认可和执行内地仲裁机构依据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》在内地作出的民商事仲裁裁决，适用本安排。 本安排没有规定的，适用认可和执行地的程序法律规定。 第二条  在内地或者澳门特别行政区作出的仲裁裁决，一方当事人不履行的，另一方当事人可以向被申请人住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地的有关法院申请认可和执行。 内地有权受理认可和执行仲裁裁决申请的法院为中级人民法院。两个或者两个以上中级人民法院均有管辖权的，当事人应当选择向其中一个中级人民法院提出申请。 澳门特别行政区有权受理认可仲裁裁决申请的法院为中级法院，有权执行的法院为初级法院。 第三条  被申请人的住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地分别在内地和澳门特别行政区的，申请人可以向一地法院提出认可和执行申请，也可以分别向两地法院提出申请。 当事人分别向两地法院提出申请的，两地法院都应当依法进行审查。予以认可的，采取查封、扣押或者冻结被执行人财产等执行措施。仲裁地法院应当先进行执行清偿；另一地法院在收到仲裁地法院关于经执行债权未获清偿情况的证明后，可以对申请人未获清偿的部分进行执行清偿。两地法院执行财产的总额，不得超过依据裁决和法律规定所确定的数额。 第四条  申请人向有关法院申请认可和执行仲裁裁决的，应当提交以下文件或者经公证的副本： （一）申请书；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  内地人民法院认可和执行澳门特别行政区仲裁机构及仲裁员按照澳门特别行政区仲裁法规在澳门作出的民商事仲裁裁决，澳门特别行政区法院认可和执行内地仲裁机构依据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》在内地作出的民商事仲裁裁决，适用本安排。  
本安排没有规定的，适用认可和执行地的程序法律规定。

**Article 1** The recognition and enforcement of civil and commercial arbitral awards made in the Macao Special Administrative Region by arbitration institutions and arbitrators of the Macao Special Administrative Region in accordance with the arbitration laws and regulations of the Macao Special Administrative Region by the People's Courts in the Mainland, and the recognition and enforcement of civil and commercial arbitral awards made in the Mainland by arbitration institutions of the Mainland in accordance with the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China by the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region, shall be governed by this Arrangement.  
If this Arrangement does not provide otherwise, the procedural laws and regulations of the place of recognition and enforcement shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  在内地或者澳门特别行政区作出的仲裁裁决，一方当事人不履行的，另一方当事人可以向被申请人住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地的有关法院申请认可和执行。  
内地有权受理认可和执行仲裁裁决申请的法院为中级人民法院。两个或者两个以上中级人民法院均有管辖权的，当事人应当选择向其中一个中级人民法院提出申请。  
澳门特别行政区有权受理认可仲裁裁决申请的法院为中级法院，有权执行的法院为初级法院。

**Article 2** If one party fails to comply with an arbitral award made in the Mainland or the Macao Special Administrative Region, the other party may apply to the relevant court at the domicile, habitual residence, or place of property of the respondent for recognition and enforcement.  
The court in the Mainland with jurisdiction to accept applications for recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards shall be the Intermediate People's Court. If two or more Intermediate People's Courts have jurisdiction, the party shall choose to file the application with one of them.  
In the Macao Special Administrative Region, the court with jurisdiction to accept applications for recognition of arbitral awards shall be the Intermediate Court, and the court with jurisdiction for enforcement shall be the Primary Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  被申请人的住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地分别在内地和澳门特别行政区的，申请人可以向一地法院提出认可和执行申请，也可以分别向两地法院提出申请。  
当事人分别向两地法院提出申请的，两地法院都应当依法进行审查。予以认可的，采取查封、扣押或者冻结被执行人财产等执行措施。仲裁地法院应当先进行执行清偿；另一地法院在收到仲裁地法院关于经执行债权未获清偿情况的证明后，可以对申请人未获清偿的部分进行执行清偿。两地法院执行财产的总额，不得超过依据裁决和法律规定所确定的数额。

**Article 3** If the respondent's domicile, habitual residence, or place of property are located in the Mainland and the Macao Special Administrative Region respectively, the applicant may file an application for recognition and enforcement with a court in one place, or may file applications with the courts in both places.  
If the party files applications with the courts in both places, both courts shall conduct review according to law. If recognition is granted, enforcement measures such as sealing up, distraining, or freezing the property of the person subject to enforcement shall be taken. The court at the place of arbitration shall first carry out enforcement and satisfaction; after receiving from the court at the place of arbitration a certificate of the circumstances that the debt has not been fully satisfied through enforcement, the court in the other place may carry out enforcement and satisfaction with respect to the part of the applicant's claim that has not been satisfied. The total amount of property enforced by the courts in both places shall not exceed the amount determined in accordance with the award and legal provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  申请人向有关法院申请认可和执行仲裁裁决的，应当提交以下文件或者经公证的副本：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）申请人身份证明；  
（三）仲裁协议；  
（四）仲裁裁决书或者仲裁调解书。  
上述文件没有中文文本的，申请人应当提交经正式证明的中文译本。

**Article 4** An applicant applying to the relevant court for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award shall submit the following documents or notarized copies:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) identity certificate of the applicant;  
(3) the arbitration agreement;  
(4) the arbitral award or the arbitral mediation document.  
If the above documents are not in the Chinese language, the applicant shall submit a formally certified Chinese translation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  申请书应当包括下列内容：  
（一）申请人或者被申请人为自然人的，应当载明其姓名及住所；为法人或者其他组织的，应当载明其名称及住所，以及其法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务和住所；申请人是外国籍法人或者其他组织的，应当提交相应的公证和认证材料；  
（二）请求认可和执行的仲裁裁决书或者仲裁调解书的案号或识别资料和生效日期；  
（三）申请认可和执行仲裁裁决的理由及具体请求，以及被申请人财产所在地、财产状况及该仲裁裁决的执行情况。

**Article 5** The written application shall include the following:  
(1) If the applicant or the respondent is a natural person, his or her name and domicile shall be indicated; if it is a legal person or any other organization, its name and domicile, as well as the name, position, and domicile of its legal representative or principal responsible person shall be indicated; if the applicant is a legal person or any other organization of foreign nationality, corresponding notarization and authentication materials shall be submitted;  
(2) the case number or identifying information and the effective date of the arbitral award or arbitral mediation document for which recognition and enforcement is requested;  
(3) the reasons for applying for recognition and enforcement of the arbitral award and the specific requests, as well as the location of the respondent's property, the status of the property, and the enforcement status of the arbitral award.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  申请人向有关法院申请认可和执行内地或者澳门特别行政区仲裁裁决的期限，依据认可和执行地的法律确定。

**Article 6** The time limit for an applicant to apply to the relevant court for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award of the Mainland or the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be determined in accordance with the law of the place of recognition and enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  对申请认可和执行的仲裁裁决，被申请人提出证据证明有下列情形之一的，经审查核实，有关法院可以裁定不予认可：  
（一）仲裁协议一方当事人依对其适用的法律在订立仲裁协议时属于无行为能力的；或者依当事人约定的准据法，或当事人没有约定适用的准据法而依仲裁地法律，该仲裁协议无效的；  
（二）被申请人未接到选任仲裁员或者进行仲裁程序的适当通知，或者因他故未能陈述意见的；  
（三）裁决所处理的争议不是提交仲裁的争议，或者不在仲裁协议范围之内；或者裁决载有超出当事人提交仲裁范围的事项的决定，但裁决中超出提交仲裁范围的事项的决定与提交仲裁事项的决定可以分开的，裁决中关于提交仲裁事项的决定部分可以予以认可；  
（四）仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁程序违反了当事人的约定，或者在当事人没有约定时与仲裁地的法律不符的；  
（五）裁决对当事人尚无约束力，或者业经仲裁地的法院撤销或者拒绝执行的。有关法院认定，依执行地法律，争议事项不能以仲裁解决的，不予认可和执行该裁决。内地法院认定在内地认可和执行该仲裁裁决违反内地法律的基本原则或者社会公共利益，澳门特别行政区法院认定在澳门特别行政区认可和执行该仲裁裁决违反澳门特别行政区法律的基本原则或者公共秩序，不予认可和执行该裁决。

**Article 7** Where, in respect of an arbitral award for which recognition and enforcement is applied, the respondent adduces evidence that there exists any of the following circumstances, upon examination and verification, the relevant court may rule to refuse recognition:  
(1) a party to the arbitration agreement was under incapacity under the law applicable to it at the time of concluding the arbitration agreement; or the arbitration agreement is invalid under the proper law agreed upon by the parties, or in the absence of an agreed proper law, under the law of the place of arbitration;  
(2) the respondent was not given proper notice of the appointment of an arbitrator or of the arbitral proceedings, or was otherwise unable to present its case;  
(3) the dispute dealt with in the award is not the dispute submitted to arbitration, or is not within the scope of the arbitration agreement; or the award contains decisions on matters beyond the scope of the dispute submitted by the parties to arbitration, provided that, if the decisions on matters beyond the scope of the dispute submitted to arbitration can be separated from the decisions on matters submitted to arbitration, the part of the award that contains decisions on matters submitted to arbitration may be recognized;  
(4) the composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitral procedure was not in accordance with the agreement of the parties, or, in the absence of such agreement, was not in accordance with the law of the place of arbitration;  
(5) the award has not yet become binding on the parties, or has been set aside or refused enforcement by a court of the place of arbitration. If the relevant court determines that, under the law of the place of enforcement, the subject matter of the dispute cannot be resolved by arbitration, it shall refuse recognition and enforcement of the award. If a Mainland court determines that recognition and enforcement of the arbitral award in the Mainland would violate the basic principles of Mainland law or the social public interest, or if a Macao Special Administrative Region court determines that recognition and enforcement of the arbitral award in the Macao Special Administrative Region would violate the basic principles of Macao Special Administrative Region law or public order, it shall refuse recognition and enforcement of the award.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  申请人依据本安排申请认可和执行仲裁裁决的，应当根据执行地法律的规定，交纳诉讼费用。

**Article 8** An applicant who applies for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award under this Arrangement shall pay litigation costs in accordance with the provisions of the law of the place of enforcement.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  一方当事人向一地法院申请执行仲裁裁决，另一方当事人向另一地法院申请撤销该仲裁裁决，被执行人申请中止执行且提供充分担保的，执行法院应当中止执行。  
根据经认可的撤销仲裁裁决的判决、裁定，执行法院应当终结执行程序；撤销仲裁裁决申请被驳回的，执行法院应当恢复执行。  
当事人申请中止执行的，应当向执行法院提供其他法院已经受理申请撤销仲裁裁决案件的法律文书。

**Article 9** Where one party applies to a court in one place for enforcement of an arbitral award, and the other party applies to a court in another place for setting aside the said arbitral award, if the person subject to enforcement applies for suspension of enforcement and provides sufficient security, the enforcement court shall suspend enforcement.  
If a judgment or ruling setting aside the arbitral award is recognized, the enforcement court shall terminate the enforcement proceedings; if the application for setting aside the arbitral award is dismissed, the enforcement court shall resume enforcement.  
If a party applies for suspension of enforcement, it shall provide to the enforcement court the legal documents evidencing that the other court has accepted the case for setting aside the arbitral award.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  受理申请的法院应当尽快审查认可和执行的请求，并作出裁定。

**Article 10** The court that accepts the application shall, as soon as possible, examine the request for recognition and enforcement and render a ruling.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  法院在受理认可和执行仲裁裁决申请之前或者之后，可以依当事人的申请，按照法院地法律规定，对被申请人的财产采取保全措施。

**Article 11** Before or after accepting an application for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award, the court may, upon the application of a party and in accordance with the provisions of the law of the court, adopt preservation measures against the property of the respondent.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  由一方有权限公共机构（包括公证员）作成的文书正本或者经公证的文书副本及译本，在适用本安排时，可以免除认证手续在对方使用。

**Article 12** An original document, or a notarized copy of a document and its translation, made by a competent public authority (including a notary public) of one side may, for the purposes of this Arrangement, be used in the other side without the need for authentication.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  本安排实施前，当事人提出的认可和执行仲裁裁决的请求，不适用本安排。  
自1999年12月20日至本安排实施前，澳门特别行政区仲裁机构及仲裁员作出的仲裁裁决，当事人向内地申请认可和执行的期限，自本安排实施之日起算。

**Article 13** Requests for recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards filed by a party before this Arrangement takes effect shall not be governed by this Arrangement.  
For arbitral awards made by the arbitration institutions and arbitrators of the Macao Special Administrative Region during the period from December 20, 1999 to the date before this Arrangement takes effect, the time limit for a party to apply to the Mainland for recognition and enforcement shall be calculated from the date on which this Arrangement takes effect.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  为执行本安排，最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院应当相互提供相关法律资料。  
最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院每年相互通报执行本安排的情况。

**Article 14** For the purpose of implementing this Arrangement, the Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall provide each other with relevant legal materials.  
The Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall exchange information annually on the implementation of this Arrangement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  本安排在执行过程中遇有问题或者需要修改的，由最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区协商解决。

**Article 15** If any issue arises during the implementation of this Arrangement or if amendments are required, it shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  本安排自2008年1月1日起实施。

**Article 16** This Arrangement shall come into effect as of January 1, 2008.
