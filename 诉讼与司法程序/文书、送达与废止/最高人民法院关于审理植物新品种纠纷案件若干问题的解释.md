# 最高人民法院关于审理植物新品种纠纷案件若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕5号  
**公布日期**：2001-02-05  
**生效日期**：2001-02-14

> （2000年12月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1154次会议通过　2001年2月5日最高人民法院公告公布　自2001年2月14日起施行） 为依法受理和审判植物新品种纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的有关规定，现就有关问题解释如下： 第一条　人民法院受理的植物新品种纠纷案件主要包括以下几类： （一）是否应当授予植物新品种权纠纷案件； （二）宣告授予的植物新品种权无效或者维持植物新品种权的纠纷案件； （三）授予品种权的植物新品种更名的纠纷案件； （四）实施强制许可的纠纷案件； （五）实施强制许可使用费的纠纷案件； （六）植物新品种申请权纠纷案件； （七）植物新品种权权利归属纠纷案件； （八）转让植物新品种申请权和转让植物新品种权的纠纷案件； （九）侵犯植物新品种权的纠纷案件； （十）不服省级以上农业、林业行政管理部门依据职权对侵犯植物新品种权处罚的纠纷案件； （十一）不服县级以上农业、林业行政管理部门依据职权对假冒授权品种处罚的纠纷案件。 第二条　人民法院在依法审查当事人涉及植物新品种权的起诉时，只要符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零八条、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第四十一条规定的民事案件或者行政案件的起诉条件，均应当依法予以受理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院受理的植物新品种纠纷案件主要包括以下几类：  
（一）是否应当授予植物新品种权纠纷案件；  
（二）宣告授予的植物新品种权无效或者维持植物新品种权的纠纷案件；  
（三）授予品种权的植物新品种更名的纠纷案件；  
（四）实施强制许可的纠纷案件；  
（五）实施强制许可使用费的纠纷案件；  
（六）植物新品种申请权纠纷案件；  
（七）植物新品种权权利归属纠纷案件；  
（八）转让植物新品种申请权和转让植物新品种权的纠纷案件；  
（九）侵犯植物新品种权的纠纷案件；  
（十）不服省级以上农业、林业行政管理部门依据职权对侵犯植物新品种权处罚的纠纷案件；  
（十一）不服县级以上农业、林业行政管理部门依据职权对假冒授权品种处罚的纠纷案件。

**Article 1** The dispute cases concerning new plant varieties accepted by the People's Court shall mainly include the following types:  
(1) Dispute cases on whether a new plant variety right shall be granted;  
(2) Dispute cases on declaring the granted new plant variety right invalid or maintaining the new plant variety right;  
(3) Dispute cases on renaming a new plant variety for which a variety right has been granted;  
(4) Dispute cases on implementing compulsory licensing;  
(5) Dispute cases on royalties for compulsory licensing;  
(6) Dispute cases on the right to apply for a new plant variety;  
(7) Dispute cases on the ownership of the right to new plant varieties;  
(8) Dispute cases on the transfer of the right to apply for a new plant variety and the transfer of the right to new plant varieties;  
(9) Dispute cases on infringement of the right to new plant varieties;  
(10) Dispute cases on refusal to accept penalties imposed by agricultural or forestry administrative departments at or above the provincial level, in accordance with their functions and powers, for infringement of the right to new plant varieties;  
(11) Dispute cases on refusal to accept penalties imposed by agricultural or forestry administrative departments at or above the county level, in accordance with their functions and powers, for counterfeiting authorized varieties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院在依法审查当事人涉及植物新品种权的起诉时，只要符合[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-108)、[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第四十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-41)规定的民事案件或者行政案件的起诉条件，均应当依法予以受理。

**Article 2** When the People's Court reviews a prosecution filed by a party concerning the right to new plant varieties according to law, as long as it meets the conditions for prosecution of a civil action as prescribed in Article 108 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China or an administrative action as prescribed in Article 41 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[本解释第一条](#art-1)所列第（一）至（五）类案件，由北京市第二中级人民法院作为第一审人民法院审理；第（六）至（十一）类案件，由各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在地和最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院作为第一审人民法院审理。

**Article 3** Cases of types (1) to (5) listed in Article 1 of this Interpretation shall be tried by the Beijing No. 2 Intermediate People's Court as the court of first instance; cases of types (6) to (11) shall be tried by the intermediate people's courts in the localities of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and those designated by the Supreme People's Court, as the courts of first instance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　以侵权行为地确定人民法院管辖的侵犯植物新品种权的民事案件，其所称的侵权行为地，是指未经品种权所有人许可，以商业目的生产、销售该授权植物新品种的繁殖材料的所在地，或者将该授权品种的繁殖材料重复使用于生产另一品种的繁殖材料的所在地。

**Article 4** In a civil action for infringement of the right to a new plant variety where the jurisdiction of the People's Court is determined by the place of the infringing act, the said place of the infringing act shall refer to the place where, without the permission of the owner of the variety right, the propagating material of the authorized new plant variety is produced or sold for commercial purposes, or the place where the propagating material of the authorized variety is repeatedly used to produce the propagating material of another variety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　关于是否应当授予植物新品种权的纠纷案件、宣告授予的植物新品种权无效或者维持植物新品种权的纠纷案件、授予品种权的植物新品种更名的纠纷案件，应当以行政主管机关植物新品种复审委员会为被告；关于实施强制许可的纠纷案件，应当以植物新品种审批机关为被告；关于强制许可使用费纠纷案件，应当根据原告所请求的事项和所起诉的当事人确定被告。

**Article 5** In a dispute case on whether a new plant variety right shall be granted, a dispute case on declaring the granted new plant variety right invalid or maintaining the new plant variety right, or a dispute case on renaming a new plant variety for which a variety right has been granted, the New Plant Variety Reexamination Committee of the competent administrative authority shall be the defendant; in a dispute case on implementing compulsory licensing, the new plant variety approval authority shall be the defendant; in a dispute case on royalties for compulsory licensing, the defendant shall be determined according to the matters requested by the plaintiff and the party against whom the prosecution is brought.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院审理侵犯植物新品种权纠纷案件，被告在答辩期间内向行政主管机关植物新品种复审委员会请求宣告该植物新品种权无效的，人民法院一般不中止诉讼。

**Article 6** Where, during the period for defense, the defendant requests the New Plant Variety Reexamination Committee of the competent administrative authority to declare the new plant variety right invalid in a dispute case on infringement of the right to a new plant variety being tried by the People's Court, the People's Court shall generally not suspend the action.
