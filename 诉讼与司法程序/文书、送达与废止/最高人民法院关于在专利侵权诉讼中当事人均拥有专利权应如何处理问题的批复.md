### 最高人民法院关于在专利侵权诉讼中当事人均拥有专利权应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于在专利侵权诉讼中当事人  
  
均拥有专利权应如何处理问题的批复  
  
（93）经他字第20号  
  
北京市高级人民法院：  
  
你院京高法（1992）143号关于《天津市东郊农牧场诉中国人民解放军3608工厂专利侵权上诉案》有关问题的请示收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
在专利侵权诉讼中，人民法院应当依据中国专利局授予的有效专利权作为法律保护的客体，审查其是否受到侵害。至于原告的专利权或者原、被告双方各自拥有的专利权是否真正符合专利性条件，应当由诉讼当事人通过撤销程序或者无效程序解决；诉讼当事人不向专利复审委员会请求撤销或者宣告对方专利权无效的，人民法院应当认定诉讼当事人拥有的专利权有效。  
  
对于相同或者类似产品，不同的人都拥有专利权的有以下三种情形：一是不同的发明人对该产品所作出的发明创造的发明点不同，他们的技术方案之间有本质区别；二是在后的专利技术是对在先的专利技术的改进或者改良，它比在先的专利技术更先进，但实施该技术有赖于实施前一项专利技术，因而它属于从属专利；三是因实用新型专利未经实质审查，前后两项实用新型专利的技术方案相同或者等同，后一项实用新型专利属于重复授权。  
  
人民法院在审理专利侵权纠纷案件时，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》规定的先申请原则，只要原告先于被告提出专利申请，则应当依据原告的专利权保护范围，审查被告制造的产品主要技术特征是否完全覆盖原告的专利保护范围。在一般情况下，前述第一种情形由于被告发明的技术方案同原告发明的技术方案有本质的区别，故被告不构成侵权。后两种情形或者被告为了实施其从属专利而未经在先专利权人的许可，实施了在先的专利技术；或者由于前后两项实用新型专利的技术方案相同或者等同，被告对后一项重复授权专利技术的实施，均构成对原告专利权的侵犯。因此，人民法院不应当仅以被告拥有专利权为由，不进行是否构成专利侵权的分析判断即驳回原告的诉讼请求，而应当分析被告拥有专利权的具体情况以及与原告专利权的关系，从而判定是否构成侵权。  
  
1993年8月16日

**Article 1** For identical or similar products, where different persons all own patent rights, there are the following three situations: First, different inventors have different inventive points in the inventions made for the product, and their technical solutions are essentially different from each other; Second, the later patent technology is an improvement or refinement of the earlier patent technology, which is more advanced than the earlier patent technology, but its implementation depends on the implementation of the earlier patent technology, thus it is a dependent patent; Third, because utility model patents are not subject to substantive examination, the technical solutions of the two utility model patents before and after are identical or equivalent, and the later utility model patent constitutes a repeated grant.  
When hearing a patent infringement dispute case, the People's Court shall, pursuant to the first-to-file principle as prescribed in the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, as long as the plaintiff files a patent application prior to the defendant, take the protection scope of the plaintiff's patent right as the basis to examine whether the main technical features of the product manufactured by the defendant fully cover the protection scope of the plaintiff's patent. Under normal circumstances, in the first situation mentioned above, since the technical solution invented by the defendant is essentially different from the technical solution invented by the plaintiff, the defendant does not constitute infringement. In the latter two situations, either the defendant, in order to implement its dependent patent, implements the earlier patent technology without obtaining permission from the earlier patentee; or because the technical solutions of the two utility model patents before and after are identical or equivalent, the defendant's implementation of the later repeatedly granted patent technology, both constitute infringement of the plaintiff's patent right. Therefore, the People's Court shall not, merely on the ground that the defendant holds a patent right, dismiss the plaintiff's claim without conducting analysis and judgment on whether patent infringement is constituted; instead, it shall analyze the specific circumstances of the defendant's patent right and its relationship with the plaintiff's patent right, so as to determine whether infringement is constituted.  
August 16, 1993
