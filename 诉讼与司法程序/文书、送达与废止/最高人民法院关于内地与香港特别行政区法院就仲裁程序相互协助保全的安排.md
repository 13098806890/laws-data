# 最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院就仲裁程序相互协助保全的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

> 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府经协商，达成《关于内地与香港特别行政区法院就仲裁程序相互协助保全的安排》（以下简称《安排》），并于2019年4 月2 日签署。本《安排》已于2019 年3 月25 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第  1763 次会议通过，现予公布。根据双方一致意见，本《安排》自2019 年10 月1 日起生效。 2019 年9 月26 日 最高人民法院 关于内地与香港特别行政区法院 就仲裁程序相互协助保全的安排 法释〔2019〕14 号 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府经协商，现就内地与香港特别行政区法院关于仲裁程序相互协助保全作出如下安排： 第一条     本安排所称“保全”，在内地包括财产保全、证据保全、行为保全；在香港特别行政区包括强制令以及其他临时措施，以在争议得以裁决之前维持现状或者恢复原状、采取行动防止目前或者即将对仲裁程序发生的危害或者损害，或者不采取可能造成这种危害或者损害的行动、保全资产或者保全对解决争议可能具有相关性和重要性的证据。 第二条     本安排所称“ 香港仲裁程序”，应当以香港特别行政区为仲裁地，并且由以下机构或者常设办事处管理： （一）在香港特别行政区设立或者总部设于香港特别行政区，并以香港特别行政区为主要管理地的仲裁机构； （二）中华人民共和国加入的政府间国际组织在香港特别行政区设立的争议解决机构或者常设办事处； （三）其他仲裁机构在香港特别行政区设立的争议解决机构或者常设办事处，且该争议解决机构或者常设办事处满足香港特别行政区政府订立的有关仲裁案件宗数以及标的金额等标准。 以上机构或者常设办事处的名单由香港特别行政区政府向最高人民法院提供， 并经双方确认。 第三条    香港仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以参照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国仲裁法》以及相关司法解释的规定，向被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地的内地中级人民法院申请保全。被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地在不同人民法院辖区的，应当选择向其中一个人民法院提出申请，不得分别向两个或者两个以上人民法院提出申请。 当事人在有关机构或者常设办事处受理仲裁申请后提出保全申请的，应当由该机构或者常设办事处转递其申请。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条     本安排所称“保全”，在内地包括财产保全、证据保全、行为保全；在香港特别行政区包括强制令以及其他临时措施，以在争议得以裁决之前维持现状或者恢复原状、采取行动防止目前或者即将对仲裁程序发生的危害或者损害，或者不采取可能造成这种危害或者损害的行动、保全资产或者保全对解决争议可能具有相关性和重要性的证据。

**Article 1** For the purpose of this Arrangement, "preservation" in the Mainland includes property preservation, evidence preservation, and preservation of conduct; in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, it includes injunctions and other interim measures for the purpose of maintaining or restoring the status quo pending the adjudication of the dispute, taking action to prevent any current or imminent harm or damage to the arbitration proceedings, or refraining from taking action that is likely to cause such harm or damage, preserving assets, or preserving evidence that may be relevant and material to the resolution of the dispute.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条     本安排所称“ 香港仲裁程序”，应当以香港特别行政区为仲裁地，并且由以下机构或者常设办事处管理：  
（一）在香港特别行政区设立或者总部设于香港特别行政区，并以香港特别行政区为主要管理地的仲裁机构；  
（二）中华人民共和国加入的政府间国际组织在香港特别行政区设立的争议解决机构或者常设办事处；  
（三）其他仲裁机构在香港特别行政区设立的争议解决机构或者常设办事处，且该争议解决机构或者常设办事处满足香港特别行政区政府订立的有关仲裁案件宗数以及标的金额等标准。  
以上机构或者常设办事处的名单由香港特别行政区政府向最高人民法院提供， 并经双方确认。

**Article 2** For the purpose of this Arrangement, a "Hong Kong arbitration proceeding" shall have the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region as the place of arbitration and shall be administered by any of the following institutions or permanent offices:  
(a) arbitration institutions established in or having their headquarters in, and with their principal place of management in, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(b) dispute resolution institutions or permanent offices established by intergovernmental international organizations to which the People's Republic of China has acceded in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(c) dispute resolution institutions or permanent offices established by other arbitration institutions in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, provided that such dispute resolution institution or permanent office meets the standards prescribed by the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region regarding the number of arbitration cases and the amount in dispute.  
The list of the above institutions or permanent offices shall be provided by the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to the Supreme People's Court and confirmed by both parties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条    香港仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以参照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国仲裁法》以及相关司法解释的规定，向被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地的内地中级人民法院申请保全。被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地在不同人民法院辖区的，应当选择向其中一个人民法院提出申请，不得分别向两个或者两个以上人民法院提出申请。  
当事人在有关机构或者常设办事处受理仲裁申请后提出保全申请的，应当由该机构或者常设办事处转递其申请。  
在有关机构或者常设办事处受理仲裁申请前提出保全申请，内地人民法院采取保全措施后三十日内未收到有关机构或者常设办事处提交的已受理仲裁案件的证明函件的，内地人民法院应当解除保全。

**Article 3** A party to a Hong Kong arbitration proceeding may, before the arbitration award is made, apply for preservation to the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the respondent has its domicile, or where the property or evidence is located, by reference to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China, and relevant judicial interpretations. If the places of the respondent's domicile, the location of the property or the evidence are within the jurisdiction of different People's Courts, the party shall choose to apply to one of such People's Courts and shall not apply to two or more People's Courts separately.  
Where a party files an application for preservation after the relevant institution or permanent office has accepted the arbitration application, the application shall be transmitted by such institution or permanent office.  
Where an application for preservation is filed before the relevant institution or permanent office has accepted the arbitration application, and the Mainland People's Court does not receive, within 30 days after taking preservation measures, a supporting letter from the relevant institution or permanent office confirming that the arbitration case has been accepted, the Mainland People's Court shall dissolve the preservation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    向内地人民法院申请保全的，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）保全申请书；  
（二）仲裁协议；  
（三）身份证明材料：申请人为自然人的，应当提交身份证件复印件；申请人为法人或者非法人组织的，应当提交注册登记证书的复印件以及法定代表人或者负责人的身份证件复印件；  
（四）在有关机构或者常设办事处受理仲裁案件后申请保全的，应当提交包含主要仲裁请求和所根据的事实与理由的仲裁申请文件以及相关证据材料、该机构或者常设办事处出具的已受理有关仲裁案件的证明函件；  
（五）内地人民法院要求的其他材料。  身份证明材料系在内地以外形成的，应当依据内地相关法律规定办理证明手续。  
向内地人民法院提交的文件没有中文文本的，应当提交准确的中文译本。

**Article 4** A party applying for preservation to a Mainland People's Court shall submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for preservation;  
(2) the arbitration agreement;  
(3) identity documents: where the applicant is a natural person, a copy of the identity document shall be submitted; where the applicant is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, a copy of the registration certificate and a copy of the identity document of the legal representative or the responsible person shall be submitted;  
(4) where the application for preservation is made after the relevant institution or permanent office has accepted the arbitration case, the arbitration application documents containing the main arbitration claims and the facts and grounds on which they are based, as well as relevant evidence materials, and a supporting letter issued by such institution or permanent office confirming acceptance of the arbitration case shall be submitted;  
(5) other materials required by the Mainland People's Court.  
Where identity documents are created outside the Mainland, the notarization procedures shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of the Mainland.  
Where the documents submitted to the Mainland People's Court are not in the Chinese language, an accurate Chinese translation shall be submitted.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条    保全申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况：当事人为自然人的，包括姓名、住所、身份证件信息、通 讯方式等；当事人为法人或者非法人组织的，包括法人或者非法人组织的名称、住所以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；  
（二）请求事项，包括申请保全财产的数额、申请行为保全的内容和期限等；  
（三）请求所依据的事实、理由和相关证据，包括关于情况紧急，如不立即保全将会使申请人合法权益受到难以弥补的损害或者将使仲裁裁决难以执行的说明等；  
（四）申请保全的财产、证据的明确信息或者具体线索；  
（五）用于提供担保的内地财产信息或者资信证明；  
（六）是否已在其他法院、有关机构或者常设办事处提出本安排所规定的申请和申请情况；  
（七）其他需要载明的事项。

**Article 5** The written application for preservation shall state the following:  
(1) basic information of the parties: where the party is a natural person, including name, domicile, identity document information, contact details, etc.; where the party is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, including the name, domicile of the legal person or unincorporated organization, and the name, position, domicile, identity document information, contact details of the legal representative or the principal responsible person, etc.;  
(2) the relief sought, including the amount of property for which preservation is sought, and the content and duration of the preservation of conduct sought, etc.;  
(3) the facts, grounds and relevant evidence on which the application is based, including an explanation that circumstances are urgent and that failure to preserve immediately will cause irreparable damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or render the arbitration award difficult to enforce, etc.;  
(4) clear information or specific leads regarding the property or evidence for which preservation is sought;  
(5) information on Mainland property or proof of creditworthiness to be used for providing security;  
(6) whether an application under this Arrangement has been made in any other court, relevant institution or permanent office and the status of such application;  
(7) other matters that need to be stated.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条    内地仲裁机构管理的仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以依据香港特别行政区《仲裁条例》《高等法院条例》，向香港特别行政区高等法院申请保全。

**Article 6** A party to an arbitration proceeding administered by a Mainland arbitration institution may, before the arbitration award is made, apply for preservation to the High Court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region pursuant to the Arbitration Ordinance and the High Court Ordinance of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条    向香港特别行政区法院申请保全的，应当依据香港特别行政区相关法律规定，提交申请、支持申请的誓章、附同的证物、论点纲要以及法庭命令的草拟本， 并应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况：当事人为自然人的，包括姓名、地址；当事人为法人或者非法人组织的，包括法人或者非法人组织的名称、地址以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、通讯方式等；  
（二）申请的事项和理由；  
（三）申请标的所在地以及情况；  
（四）被申请人就申请作出或者可能作出的回应以及说法；  
（五）可能会导致法庭不批准所寻求的保全，或者不在单方面申请的情况下批准该保全的事实；  
（六）申请人向香港特别行政区法院作出的承诺；  
（七）其他需要载明的事项。

**Article 7** A party applying for preservation to a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall, in accordance with the relevant laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, submit the application, an affidavit in support, any exhibits annexed thereto, a skeleton argument, and a draft of the court order, and shall state the following:  
(1) basic information of the parties: where the party is a natural person, including name and address; where the party is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, including the name and address of the legal person or unincorporated organization, and the name, position, and contact details of the legal representative or the principal responsible person, etc.;  
(2) the matters and grounds of the application;  
(3) the location and status of the subject matter of the application;  
(4) the respondent's response or likely response and submissions in relation to the application;  
(5) facts that might cause the court not to grant the preservation sought, or not to grant it on an ex parte basis;  
(6) the undertakings given by the applicant to the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(7) other matters that need to be stated.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条    被请求方法院应当尽快审查当事人的保全申请。内地人民法院可以要求申请人提供担保等，香港特别行政区法院可以要求申请人作出承诺、就费用提供保证等。  
经审查，当事人的保全申请符合被请求方法律规定的，被请求方法院应当作出保全裁定或者命令等。

**Article 8** The court of the requested party shall review the party's application for preservation as soon as possible. The Mainland People's Court may require the applicant to provide security, etc., and the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region may require the applicant to give undertakings, provide security for costs, etc.  
Upon review, if the party's application for preservation complies with the laws of the requested party, the court of the requested party shall make a preservation ruling or order, etc.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条    当事人对被请求方法院的裁定或者命令等不服的，按被请求方相关法律规定处理。

**Article 9** If a party is dissatisfied with a ruling or order, etc., of the court of the requested party, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of the requested party.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条    当事人申请保全的，应当依据被请求方有关诉讼收费的法律和规定交纳费用。

**Article 10** A party applying for preservation shall pay fees in accordance with the laws and regulations of the requested party on litigation fees.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条    本安排不减损内地和香港特别行政区的仲裁机构、仲裁庭、当事人依据对方法律享有的权利。

**Article 11** This Arrangement shall not derogate from the rights enjoyed by arbitration institutions, arbitral tribunals, and parties in the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region under the laws of the other party.
