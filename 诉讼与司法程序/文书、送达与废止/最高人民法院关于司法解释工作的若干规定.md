# 最高人民法院关于司法解释工作的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1997〕15号  
**公布日期**：1997-06-23

> 第三条　最高人民法院发布的司法解释，必须经审判委员会讨论通过。 第四条　最高人民法院制定并发布的司法解释，具有法律效力。 第五条　司法解释的立项，由最高人民法院各审判业务庭、室，根据审判工作中应用法律的问题，提出意见，经研究室协调后，分别报分管副院长批准。 最高人民法院审判委员会认为需要作出司法解释的，由有关审判业务庭、室直接立项。 司法解释立项后，送研究室备案。 第六条　司法解释的起草，由最高人民法院各审判业务庭、室负责。 第七条　经论证、修改的司法解释草案，送研究室协调提出意见后，由起草部门报请分管副院长审核。 第八条　司法解释经审判委员会讨论通过后，以最高人民法院公告的形式在《人民法院报》上公开发布，并下发各高级人民法院或地方各级人民法院、专门人民法院。 第九条　司法解释的形式分为“解释”、“规定”、“批复”三种。对于如何应用某一法律或者对某一类案件、某一类问题如何适用法律所作的规定，采用“解释”的形式。根据审判工作需要，对于审判工作提出的规范、意见，采用“规定”的形式。对于高级人民法院、解放军军事法院就审判工作中具体应用法律问题的请示所作的答复，采用“批复”的形式。 第十条　司法解释在首部写明司法解释的名称，××××年××月××日最高人民法院审判委员会第×××次会议通过；文件编号；如属“批复”，还应写明主送机关。在正文部分，写明司法解释的具体内容。 第十一条　司法解释以在《人民法院报》上公开发布的日期为生效时间，但司法解释专有规定的除外。 第十二条　司法解释在颁布了新的法律，或在原法律修改、废止，或者制定了新的司法解释后，不再具有法律效力。 第十三条　司法解释需要补充、修改或者废止的，由原起草司法解释的审判业务庭、室提出具体意见，按照本规定办理。 第十四条　司法解释与有关法律规定一并作为人民法院判决或者裁定的依据时，应当在司法文书中援引。 援引司法解释作为判决或者裁定的依据，应当先引用适用的法律条款，再引用适用的司法解释条款。

### 正文

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　最高人民法院制定并发布的司法解释，具有法律效力。

**Article 4** The judicial interpretations formulated and issued by the Supreme People's Court shall have legal effect.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　司法解释的立项，由最高人民法院各审判业务庭、室，根据审判工作中应用法律的问题，提出意见，经研究室协调后，分别报分管副院长批准。  
最高人民法院审判委员会认为需要作出司法解释的，由有关审判业务庭、室直接立项。  
司法解释立项后，送研究室备案。

**Article 5** The initiation of judicial interpretations shall be proposed by the various adjudication divisions and offices of the Supreme People's Court based on issues concerning the application of laws in adjudication work, and after coordination by the Research Office, shall be submitted to the respective vice presidents in charge for approval.  
Where the Adjudication Committee of the Supreme People's Court considers that a judicial interpretation is necessary, the relevant adjudication division or office shall directly initiate the project.  
After the initiation of a judicial interpretation project, it shall be submitted to the Research Office for record.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　司法解释的起草，由最高人民法院各审判业务庭、室负责。

**Article 6** The drafting of judicial interpretations shall be the responsibility of the various adjudication divisions and offices of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经论证、修改的司法解释草案，送研究室协调提出意见后，由起草部门报请分管副院长审核。

**Article 7** After the draft judicial interpretation has been demonstrated and revised, it shall be submitted to the Research Office for coordination and comments, and then the drafting department shall submit it to the vice president in charge for review.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　司法解释经审判委员会讨论通过后，以最高人民法院公告的形式在《人民法院报》上公开发布，并下发各高级人民法院或地方各级人民法院、专门人民法院。

**Article 8** After a judicial interpretation is discussed and adopted by the Adjudication Committee, it shall be publicly released in the form of an announcement of the Supreme People's Court in the People's Court Daily, and shall be distributed to each Higher People's Court or local people's courts at various levels and special people's courts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　司法解释的形式分为“解释”、“规定”、“批复”三种。对于如何应用某一法律或者对某一类案件、某一类问题如何适用法律所作的规定，采用“解释”的形式。根据审判工作需要，对于审判工作提出的规范、意见，采用“规定”的形式。对于高级人民法院、解放军军事法院就审判工作中具体应用法律问题的请示所作的答复，采用“批复”的形式。

**Article 9** The forms of judicial interpretations are divided into three types: "Interpretation", "Provisions", and "Reply". For provisions on how to apply a certain law or how to apply laws to a certain type of case or a certain type of issue, the form of "Interpretation" shall be adopted. For norms and opinions proposed for adjudication work based on the needs of adjudication work, the form of "Provisions" shall be adopted. For replies to requests from Higher People's Courts and the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army concerning specific issues in the application of laws in adjudication work, the form of "Reply" shall be adopted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　司法解释在首部写明司法解释的名称，××××年××月××日最高人民法院审判委员会第×××次会议通过；文件编号；如属“批复”，还应写明主送机关。在正文部分，写明司法解释的具体内容。

**Article 10** The heading of a judicial interpretation shall state the name of the judicial interpretation, the date of adoption by the XXth meeting of the Adjudication Committee of the Supreme People's Court on XX month XX day XXXX year; the document number; if it is a "Reply", the receiving authority shall also be stated. In the main body, the specific content of the judicial interpretation shall be stated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　司法解释以在《人民法院报》上公开发布的日期为生效时间，但司法解释专有规定的除外。

**Article 11** The date of public release in the People's Court Daily shall be the effective date of a judicial interpretation, unless otherwise provided by the judicial interpretation itself.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　司法解释在颁布了新的法律，或在原法律修改、废止，或者制定了新的司法解释后，不再具有法律效力。

**Article 12** A judicial interpretation shall no longer have legal effect after a new law is promulgated, or the original law is amended or abolished, or a new judicial interpretation is formulated.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　司法解释需要补充、修改或者废止的，由原起草司法解释的审判业务庭、室提出具体意见，按照本规定办理。

**Article 13** Where a judicial interpretation needs to be supplemented, amended, or abolished, the adjudication division or office that originally drafted the judicial interpretation shall propose specific opinions and handle the matter pursuant to these Provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　司法解释与有关法律规定一并作为人民法院判决或者裁定的依据时，应当在司法文书中援引。  
援引司法解释作为判决或者裁定的依据，应当先引用适用的法律条款，再引用适用的司法解释条款。

**Article 14** When a judicial interpretation, together with the relevant legal provisions, is used as the basis for a judgment or ruling of a People's Court, it shall be cited in the judicial documents.  
When citing a judicial interpretation as the basis for a judgment or ruling, the applicable legal provision shall be cited first, followed by the applicable provision of the judicial interpretation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　司法解释的清理、编纂由最高人民法院审判委员会决定，具体工作由研究室负责，各审判庭、室参加。

**Article 15** The review and compilation of judicial interpretations shall be decided by the Adjudication Committee of the Supreme People's Court. The specific work shall be the responsibility of the Research Office, with the participation of all adjudication divisions and offices.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　最高人民法院对地方各级人民法院和专门人民法院在审判工作中应用司法解释的情况实行监督。上级人民法院对下级人民法院在审判工作中应用司法解释的情况实行监督。

**Article 16** The Supreme People's Court shall supervise the application of judicial interpretations in the adjudication work of local people's courts at various levels and special people's courts. A higher people's court shall supervise the application of judicial interpretations in the adjudication work of lower people's courts.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定自一九九七年七月一日起施行。
