# 最高人民法院关于人民法院登记立案若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕8号  
**公布日期**：2015-04-13  
**生效日期**：2015-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院登记立案若干问题的规定》已于2015年4月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1647次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年5月1日起施行。 2015年4月15日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院登记立案若干问题的规定 法释〔2015〕8号 为保护公民、法人和其他组织依法行使诉权，实现人民法院依法、及时受理案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等法律规定，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院对依法应该受理的一审民事起诉、行政起诉和刑事自诉，实行立案登记制。 第二条　对起诉、自诉，人民法院应当一律接收诉状，出具书面凭证并注明收到日期。 对符合法律规定的起诉、自诉，人民法院应当当场予以登记立案。 对不符合法律规定的起诉、自诉，人民法院应当予以释明。 第三条　人民法院应当提供诉状样本，为当事人书写诉状提供示范和指引。 当事人书写诉状确有困难的，可以口头提出，由人民法院记入笔录。符合法律规定的，予以登记立案。 第四条　民事起诉状应当记明以下事项： （一）原告的姓名、性别、年龄、民族、职业、工作单位、住所、联系方式，法人或者其他组织的名称、住所和法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、联系方式； （二）被告的姓名、性别、工作单位、住所等信息，法人或者其他组织的名称、住所等信息；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院对依法应该受理的一审民事起诉、行政起诉和刑事自诉，实行立案登记制。

**Article 1** : The People's Court shall adopt the case-filing registration system for first-instance civil actions, administrative actions, and criminal private prosecutions that shall be accepted according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对起诉、自诉，人民法院应当一律接收诉状，出具书面凭证并注明收到日期。  
对符合法律规定的起诉、自诉，人民法院应当当场予以登记立案。  
对不符合法律规定的起诉、自诉，人民法院应当予以释明。

**Article 2** : The People's Court shall accept all petitions for prosecution or private prosecution, issue a written receipt and indicate the date of receipt.  
For prosecutions or private prosecutions that comply with the provisions of the law, the People's Court shall register and file the case on the spot.  
For prosecutions or private prosecutions that do not comply with the provisions of the law, the People's Court shall provide explanations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应当提供诉状样本，为当事人书写诉状提供示范和指引。  
当事人书写诉状确有困难的，可以口头提出，由人民法院记入笔录。符合法律规定的，予以登记立案。

**Article 3** : The People's Court shall provide sample petitions to provide demonstrations and guidance for parties to write petitions.  
If a party has genuine difficulty in writing a petition, he or she may submit it orally, and the People's Court shall record it in the transcripts. If it complies with the provisions of the law, the case shall be registered and filed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民事起诉状应当记明以下事项：  
（一）原告的姓名、性别、年龄、民族、职业、工作单位、住所、联系方式，法人或者其他组织的名称、住所和法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、联系方式；  
（二）被告的姓名、性别、工作单位、住所等信息，法人或者其他组织的名称、住所等信息；  
（三）诉讼请求和所根据的事实与理由；  
（四）证据和证据来源；  
（五）有证人的，载明证人姓名和住所。  
行政起诉状参照民事起诉状书写。

**Article 4** : A civil complaint shall state the following particulars:  
(1) The name, gender, age, ethnicity, occupation, work unit, domicile, and contact information of the plaintiff, or the name and domicile of a legal person or other organization and the name, position, and contact information of its legal representative or principal responsible person;  
(2) The name, gender, work unit, domicile, and other information of the defendant, or the name, domicile, and other information of a legal person or other organization;  
(3) The claims and the facts and grounds on which they are based;  
(4) The evidence and the sources of evidence;  
(5) If there are witnesses, the names and domiciles of the witnesses.  
An administrative complaint shall be written by reference to a civil complaint.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　刑事自诉状应当记明以下事项：  
（一）自诉人或者代为告诉人、被告人的姓名、性别、年龄、民族、文化程度、职业、工作单位、住址、联系方式；  
（二）被告人实施犯罪的时间、地点、手段、情节和危害后果等；  
（三）具体的诉讼请求；  
（四）致送的人民法院和具状时间；  
（五）证据的名称、来源等；  
（六）有证人的，载明证人的姓名、住所、联系方式等。

**Article 5** : A criminal private complaint shall state the following particulars:  
(1) The name, gender, age, ethnicity, education level, occupation, work unit, address, and contact information of the private prosecutor or the person filing the complaint on behalf of another, and of the defendant;  
(2) The time, place, means, circumstances, and harmful consequences of the crime committed by the defendant;  
(3) The specific claims;  
(4) The People's Court to which it is addressed and the date of filing;  
(5) The names, sources, etc. of the evidence;  
(6) If there are witnesses, the names, addresses, contact information, etc. of the witnesses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人提出起诉、自诉的，应当提交以下材料：  
（一）起诉人、自诉人是自然人的，提交身份证明复印件；起诉人、自诉人是法人或者其他组织的，提交营业执照或者组织机构代码证复印件、法定代表人或者主要负责人身份证明书；法人或者其他组织不能提供组织机构代码的，应当提供组织机构被注销的情况说明；  
（二）委托起诉或者代为告诉的，应当提交授权委托书、代理人身份证明、代为告诉人身份证明等相关材料；  
（三）具体明确的足以使被告或者被告人与他人相区别的姓名或者名称、住所等信息；  
（四）起诉状原本和与被告或者被告人及其他当事人人数相符的副本；  
（五）与诉请相关的证据或者证明材料。

**Article 6** : If a party brings a prosecution or private prosecution, he or she shall submit the following materials:  
(1) If the prosecutor or private prosecutor is a natural person, submit a copy of the identity certificate; if the prosecutor or private prosecutor is a legal person or other organization, submit a copy of the business license or organization code certificate, and the identity certificate of the legal representative or principal responsible person; if the legal person or other organization cannot provide the organization code, it shall provide a statement explaining that the organization has been cancelled;  
(2) If the prosecution is entrusted or the complaint is filed on behalf of another, the relevant materials such as the power of attorney, the identity certificate of the agent, and the identity certificate of the person filing the complaint on behalf of another shall be submitted;  
(3) Specific and clear information such as the name or title and domicile that is sufficient to distinguish the defendant or the accused from others;  
(4) The original complaint and copies corresponding to the number of defendants or accused persons and other parties;  
(5) Evidence or supporting materials related to the claims.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人提交的诉状和材料不符合要求的，人民法院应当一次性书面告知在指定期限内补正。  
当事人在指定期限内补正的，人民法院决定是否立案的期间，自收到补正材料之日起计算。  
当事人在指定期限内没有补正的，退回诉状并记录在册；坚持起诉、自诉的，裁定或者决定不予受理、不予立案。  
经补正仍不符合要求的，裁定或者决定不予受理、不予立案。

**Article 7** : If the petition and materials submitted by the party do not meet the requirements, the People's Court shall, in a one-time written notice, inform the party to make corrections within a specified period.  
If the party makes corrections within the specified period, the period for the People's Court to decide whether to file the case shall be calculated from the date of receipt of the corrected materials.  
If the party fails to make corrections within the specified period, the petition shall be returned and recorded; if the party insists on prosecution or private prosecution, an order or decision shall be made to reject the case or not to file it.  
If after corrections the materials still do not meet the requirements, an order or decision shall be made to reject the case or not to file it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对当事人提出的起诉、自诉，人民法院当场不能判定是否符合法律规定的，应当作出以下处理：  
（一）对民事、行政起诉，应当在收到起诉状之日起七日内决定是否立案；  
（二）对刑事自诉，应当在收到自诉状次日起十五日内决定是否立案；  
（三）对第三人撤销之诉，应当在收到起诉状之日起三十日内决定是否立案；  
（四）对执行异议之诉，应当在收到起诉状之日起十五日内决定是否立案。  
人民法院在法定期间内不能判定起诉、自诉是否符合法律规定的，应当先行立案。

**Article 8** : If the People's Court cannot determine on the spot whether a prosecution or private prosecution filed by a party complies with the provisions of the law, it shall handle it as follows:  
(1) For civil or administrative prosecutions, decide whether to file the case within seven days from the date of receipt of the complaint;  
(2) For criminal private prosecutions, decide whether to file the case within fifteen days from the day after the date of receipt of the private complaint;  
(3) For a third-party revocation action, decide whether to file the case within thirty days from the date of receipt of the complaint;  
(4) For an action regarding objections to enforcement, decide whether to file the case within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the complaint.  
If the People's Court cannot determine within the statutory period whether a prosecution or private prosecution complies with the provisions of the law, it shall file the case first.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院对起诉、自诉不予受理或者不予立案的，应当出具书面裁定或者决定，并载明理由。

**Article 9** : If the People's Court decides not to accept or not to file a prosecution or private prosecution, it shall issue a written order or decision and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院对下列起诉、自诉不予登记立案：  
（一）违法起诉或者不符合法律规定的；  
（二）涉及危害国家主权和领土完整的；  
（三）危害国家安全的；  
（四）破坏国家统一和民族团结的；  
（五）破坏国家宗教政策的；  
（六）所诉事项不属于人民法院主管的。

**Article 10** : The People's Court shall not register and file the following prosecutions or private prosecutions:  
(1) Those that are illegal prosecutions or do not comply with the provisions of the law;  
(2) Those involving harm to national sovereignty and territorial integrity;  
(3) Those endangering national security;  
(4) Those undermining national unity and ethnic solidarity;  
(5) Those undermining national religious policies;  
(6) Those matters complained of are not within the jurisdiction of the People's Court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　登记立案后，当事人未在法定期限内交纳诉讼费的，按撤诉处理，但符合法律规定的缓、减、免交诉讼费条件的除外。

**Article 11** : After the case is registered and filed, if the party fails to pay the litigation fee within the statutory period, the case shall be treated as withdrawn, except where the conditions for deferring, reducing, or exempting the litigation fee as provided by law are met.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　登记立案后，人民法院立案庭应当及时将案件移送审判庭审理。

**Article 12** : After the case is registered and filed, the filing division of the People's Court shall promptly transfer the case to the adjudication division for trial.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对立案工作中存在的不接收诉状、接收诉状后不出具书面凭证，不一次性告知当事人补正诉状内容，以及有案不立、拖延立案、干扰立案、既不立案又不作出裁定或者决定等违法违纪情形，当事人可以向受诉人民法院或者上级人民法院投诉。  
人民法院应当在受理投诉之日起十五日内，查明事实，并将情况反馈当事人。发现违法违纪行为的，依法依纪追究相关人员责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** : If, in the case-filing work, there are illegal or disciplinary circumstances such as refusing to accept petitions, failing to issue a written receipt after accepting a petition, failing to inform the party in a one-time manner to correct the content of the petition, as well as failing to file cases that should be filed, delaying case filing, interfering with case filing, or neither filing a case nor issuing an order or decision, the party may file a complaint with the People's Court handling the case or with the higher People's Court.  
The People's Court shall, within fifteen days from the date of accepting the complaint, find out the facts and provide feedback to the party. If illegal or disciplinary acts are found, the relevant personnel shall be investigated for responsibility according to law and discipline; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　为方便当事人行使诉权，人民法院提供网上立案、预约立案、巡回立案等诉讼服务。

**Article 14** : To facilitate parties in exercising their right of action, the People's Court shall provide litigation services such as online case filing, appointment-based case filing, and circuit case filing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院推动多元化纠纷解决机制建设，尊重当事人选择人民调解、行政调解、行业调解、仲裁等多种方式维护权益，化解纠纷。

**Article 15** : The People's Court shall promote the construction of a diversified dispute resolution mechanism, respecting the parties' choice of various methods such as people's mediation, administrative mediation, industry mediation, and arbitration to protect rights and interests and resolve disputes.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院依法维护登记立案秩序，推进诉讼诚信建设。对干扰立案秩序、虚假诉讼的，根据民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法有关规定予以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 16** : The People's Court shall maintain the order of case-filing registration according to law and promote the construction of litigation integrity. Those who disrupt the order of case filing or engage in fictitious litigation shall be fined or detained according to the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定的“起诉”，是指当事人提起民事、行政诉讼；“自诉”，是指当事人提起刑事自诉。

**Article 17** : For the purposes of these Provisions, "prosecution" refers to a party filing a civil or administrative action; "private prosecution" refers to a party filing a criminal private prosecution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　强制执行和国家赔偿申请登记立案工作，按照本规定执行。  
上诉、申请再审、刑事申诉、执行复议和国家赔偿申诉案件立案工作，不适用本规定。

**Article 18** : The registration and filing of applications for compulsory execution and state compensation shall be handled in accordance with these Provisions.  
The case-filing work for appeals, applications for retrial, criminal petitions, enforcement reconsideration, and complaints regarding state compensation shall not be governed by these Provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法庭登记立案工作，按照本规定执行。

**Article 19** : The registration and filing work of the people's tribunals shall be handled in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定自2015年5月1日起施行。以前有关立案的规定与本规定不一致的，按照本规定执行。

**Article 20** : These Provisions shall come into force on May 1, 2015. Where previous provisions on case filing are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
