# 最高人民法院关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-08-19  
**生效日期**：2020-08-20

> （2015年6月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1655次会议通过，根据2020年8月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1809次会议《关于修改〈关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定〉的决定》修正，该修正自2020年8月20日起施行） 为正确审理民间借贷纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》《中华人民共和国物权法》《中华人民共和国担保法》《中华人民共和国合同法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等相关法律之规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条  本规定所称的民间借贷，是指自然人、法人和非法人组织之间进行资金融通的行为。 经金融监管部门批准设立的从事贷款业务的金融机构及其分支机构，因发放贷款等相关金融业务引发的纠纷，不适用本规定。 第二条  出借人向人民法院提起民间借贷诉讼时，应当提供借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证以及其他能够证明借贷法律关系存在的证据。 当事人持有的借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证没有载明债权人，持有债权凭证的当事人提起民间借贷诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。被告对原告的债权人资格提出有事实依据的抗辩，人民法院经审查认为原告不具有债权人资格的，裁定驳回起诉。 第三条  借贷双方就合同履行地未约定或者约定不明确，事后未达成补充协议，按照合同相关条款或者交易习惯仍不能确定的，以接受货币一方所在地为合同履行地。 第四条  保证人为借款人提供连带责任保证，出借人仅起诉借款人的，人民法院可以不追加保证人为共同被告；出借人仅起诉保证人的，人民法院可以追加借款人为共同被告。 保证人为借款人提供一般保证，出借人仅起诉保证人的，人民法院应当追加借款人为共同被告；出借人仅起诉借款人的，人民法院可以不追加保证人为共同被告。 第五条  人民法院立案后，发现民间借贷行为本身涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的，应当裁定驳回起诉，并将涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料移送公安或者检察机关。 公安或者检察机关不予立案，或者立案侦查后撤销案件，或者检察机关作出不起诉决定，或者经人民法院生效判决认定不构成非法集资等犯罪，当事人又以同一事实向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。 第六条  人民法院立案后，发现与民间借贷纠纷案件虽有关联但不是同一事实的涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料的，人民法院应当继续审理民间借贷纠纷案件，并将涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料移送公安或者检察机关。 第七条  民间借贷纠纷的基本案件事实必须以刑事案件的审理结果为依据，而该刑事案件尚未审结的，人民法院应当裁定中止诉讼。 第八条  借款人涉嫌犯罪或者生效判决认定其有罪，出借人起诉请求担保人承担民事责任的，人民法院应予受理。 第九条  自然人之间的借款合同具有下列情形之一的，可以视为合同成立：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  本规定所称的民间借贷，是指自然人、法人和非法人组织之间进行资金融通的行为。  
经金融监管部门批准设立的从事贷款业务的金融机构及其分支机构，因发放贷款等相关金融业务引发的纠纷，不适用本规定。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, "private lending" refers to the conduct of financial accommodation among natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations.  
Disputes arising from the issuance of loans or other related financial activities by financial institutions and their branches that are approved to engage in lending business by the financial regulatory authority shall not be governed by these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  出借人向人民法院提起民间借贷诉讼时，应当提供借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证以及其他能够证明借贷法律关系存在的证据。  
当事人持有的借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证没有载明债权人，持有债权凭证的当事人提起民间借贷诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。被告对原告的债权人资格提出有事实依据的抗辩，人民法院经审查认为原告不具有债权人资格的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 2** When a lender initiates a private lending action in a People's Court, it shall provide debt instruments such as IOUs, receipts, and promissory notes, as well as other evidence that can prove the existence of the lending legal relationship.  
If the debt instrument held by a party does not specify the creditor, and the party holding the debt instrument initiates a private lending action, the People's Court shall accept the case. If the defendant raises a factually based defense against the plaintiff's qualification as a creditor, and after examination, the People's Court finds that the plaintiff does not have creditor status, it shall rule to dismiss the action.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  借贷双方就合同履行地未约定或者约定不明确，事后未达成补充协议，按照合同相关条款或者交易习惯仍不能确定的，以接受货币一方所在地为合同履行地。

**Article 3** If the parties to a loan have not agreed on the place of contract performance, or the agreement is unclear, and no supplementary agreement is reached afterwards, and the place still cannot be determined according to the relevant terms of the contract or trade usage, the place where the party receiving the currency is located shall be the place of contract performance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  保证人为借款人提供连带责任保证，出借人仅起诉借款人的，人民法院可以不追加保证人为共同被告；出借人仅起诉保证人的，人民法院可以追加借款人为共同被告。  
保证人为借款人提供一般保证，出借人仅起诉保证人的，人民法院应当追加借款人为共同被告；出借人仅起诉借款人的，人民法院可以不追加保证人为共同被告。

**Article 4** If the surety provides joint and several liability guarantee for the borrower, and the lender only sues the borrower, the People's Court may refrain from adding the surety as a co-defendant; if the lender only sues the surety, the People's Court may add the borrower as a co-defendant.  
If the surety provides ordinary guarantee for the borrower, and the lender only sues the surety, the People's Court shall add the borrower as a co-defendant; if the lender only sues the borrower, the People's Court may refrain from adding the surety as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院立案后，发现民间借贷行为本身涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的，应当裁定驳回起诉，并将涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料移送公安或者检察机关。  
公安或者检察机关不予立案，或者立案侦查后撤销案件，或者检察机关作出不起诉决定，或者经人民法院生效判决认定不构成非法集资等犯罪，当事人又以同一事实向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 5** After a People's Court has docketed a case, if it discovers that the private lending conduct itself is suspected of constituting illegal fundraising or other crimes, it shall rule to dismiss the action and transfer the leads and materials concerning the suspected illegal fundraising or other crimes to the public security or procuratorial authorities.  
If the public security or procuratorial authorities decide not to open a case, or after opening a case and investigation, they close the case, or the procuratorial authority makes a decision not to prosecute, or a valid judgment of the People's Court determines that it does not constitute illegal fundraising or other crimes, and the party again initiates an action in the People's Court based on the same facts, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院立案后，发现与民间借贷纠纷案件虽有关联但不是同一事实的涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料的，人民法院应当继续审理民间借贷纠纷案件，并将涉嫌非法集资等犯罪的线索、材料移送公安或者检察机关。

**Article 6** After a People's Court has docketed a case, if it discovers leads or materials concerning suspected illegal fundraising or other crimes that are related to but not identical to the facts of the private lending dispute, the People's Court shall continue to hear the private lending dispute case and transfer the leads and materials concerning the suspected illegal fundraising or other crimes to the public security or procuratorial authorities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  民间借贷纠纷的基本案件事实必须以刑事案件的审理结果为依据，而该刑事案件尚未审结的，人民法院应当裁定中止诉讼。

**Article 7** If the basic facts of a private lending dispute case must be determined based on the outcome of a criminal case, and such criminal case has not yet been concluded, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the litigation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  借款人涉嫌犯罪或者生效判决认定其有罪，出借人起诉请求担保人承担民事责任的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 8** If the borrower is suspected of a crime or a valid judgment finds the borrower guilty, and the lender sues requesting the surety to bear civil liability, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  自然人之间的借款合同具有下列情形之一的，可以视为合同成立：  
（一）以现金支付的，自借款人收到借款时；  
（二）以银行转账、网上电子汇款等形式支付的，自资金到达借款人账户时；  
（三）以票据交付的，自借款人依法取得票据权利时；  
（四）出借人将特定资金账户支配权授权给借款人的，自借款人取得对该账户实际支配权时；  
（五）出借人以与借款人约定的其他方式提供借款并实际履行完成时。

**Article 9** A loan contract between natural persons may be deemed to be formed under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) if payment is made in cash, from the time the borrower receives the loan;  
(2) if payment is made by bank transfer, online electronic remittance, or other similar means, from the time the funds reach the borrower's account;  
(3) if payment is made by negotiable instrument, from the time the borrower legally obtains the rights under the instrument;  
(4) if the lender authorizes the borrower to control a specific fund account, from the time the borrower obtains actual control over that account;  
(5) if the lender provides the loan in another manner agreed upon with the borrower and has actually performed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  除自然人之间的借款合同外，当事人主张民间借贷合同自合同成立时生效的，人民法院应予支持，但当事人另有约定或者法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 10** Except for loan contracts between natural persons, if a party claims that a private lending contract takes effect upon its formation, the People's Court shall uphold such claim, unless the parties have agreed otherwise or laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  法人之间、非法人组织之间以及它们相互之间为生产、经营需要订立的民间借贷合同，除存在《中华人民共和国合同法》第五十二条以及[本规定第十四条](#art-14)规定的情形外，当事人主张民间借贷合同有效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** For private lending contracts concluded between legal persons, between unincorporated organizations, or between them for the needs of production or business operations, except where the circumstances provided in Article 52 of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 14 of these Provisions exist, if a party claims that the private lending contract is valid, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  法人或者非法人组织在本单位内部通过借款形式向职工筹集资金，用于本单位生产、经营，且不存在《中华人民共和国合同法》第五十二条以及[本规定第十四条](#art-14)规定的情形，当事人主张民间借贷合同有效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** If a legal person or unincorporated organization raises funds from its employees within the unit in the form of loans for the unit's production or business operations, and the circumstances provided in Article 52 of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 14 of these Provisions do not exist, and a party claims that the private lending contract is valid, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  借款人或者出借人的借贷行为涉嫌犯罪，或者已经生效的裁判认定构成犯罪，当事人提起民事诉讼的，民间借贷合同并不当然无效。人民法院应当依据《中华人民共和国合同法》  
第五十二条以及[本规定第十四条](#art-14)之规定，认定民间借贷合同的效力。  
担保人以借款人或者出借人的借贷行为涉嫌犯罪或者已经生效的裁判认定构成犯罪为由，主张不承担民事责任的，人民法院应当依据民间借贷合同与担保合同的效力、当事人的过错程度，依法确定担保人的民事责任。

**Article 13** If the lending conduct of the borrower or lender is suspected of a crime, or has been determined by a valid judgment to constitute a crime, and a party initiates a civil action, the private lending contract shall not be automatically void. The People's Court shall determine the validity of the private lending contract in accordance with Article 52 of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China  
and Article 14 of these Provisions.  
If a surety claims that it shall not bear civil liability on the ground that the lending conduct of the borrower or lender is suspected of a crime or has been determined by a valid judgment to constitute a crime, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the validity of the private lending contract and the guarantee contract and the degree of fault of the parties, determine the surety's civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定民间借贷合同无效：  
（一）套取金融机构贷款转贷的；  
（二）以向其他营利法人借贷、向本单位职工集资，或者以向公众非法吸收存款等方式取得的资金转贷的；  
（三）未依法取得放贷资格的出借人，以营利为目的向社会不特定对象提供借款的；  
（四）出借人事先知道或者应当知道借款人借款用于违法犯罪活动仍然提供借款的；  
（五）违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的；  
（六）违背公序良俗的。

**Article 14** A private lending contract shall be determined void by the People's Court under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the lender obtains loans from financial institutions and re-lends them;  
(2) the lender re-lends funds obtained by borrowing from other for-profit legal persons, raising funds from the unit's employees, or illegally absorbing deposits from the public;  
(3) the lender, without legally obtaining lending qualifications, provides loans to unspecified persons in society for profit-making purposes;  
(4) the lender, knowing or having reason to know that the borrower will use the loan for illegal or criminal activities, still provides the loan;  
(5) it violates mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations;  
(6) it violates public order or good customs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  原告以借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证为依据提起民间借贷诉讼，被告依据基础法律关系提出抗辩或者反诉，并提供证据证明债权纠纷非民间借贷行为引起的，人民法院应当依据查明的案件事实，按照基础法律关系审理。  
当事人通过调解、和解或者清算达成的债权债务协议，不适用前款规定。

**Article 15** If the plaintiff initiates a private lending action based on debt instruments such as IOUs, receipts, and promissory notes, and the defendant raises a defense or counterclaim based on an underlying legal relationship and provides evidence to prove that the debt dispute is not caused by a private lending act, the People's Court shall, based on the ascertained facts of the case, hear the case in accordance with the underlying legal relationship.  
If the parties have reached a debt and credit agreement through mediation, conciliation, or liquidation, the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  原告仅依据借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证提起民间借贷诉讼，被告抗辩已经偿还借款的，被告应当对其主张提供证据证明。被告提供相应证据证明其主张后，原告仍应就借贷关系的存续承担举证责任。  
被告抗辩借贷行为尚未实际发生并能作出合理说明的，人民法院应当结合借贷金额、款项交付、当事人的经济能力、当地或者当事人之间的交易方式、交易习惯、当事人财产变动情况以及证人证言等事实和因素，综合判断查证借贷事实是否发生。

**Article 16** If the plaintiff initiates a private lending action solely on the basis of debt instruments such as IOUs, receipts, and promissory notes, and the defendant defends on the ground that the loan has been repaid, the defendant shall provide evidence to support its claim. After the defendant provides corresponding evidence to support its claim, the plaintiff shall still bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the existence of the lending relationship.  
If the defendant defends on the ground that the lending act has not actually occurred and can provide a reasonable explanation, the People's Court shall, taking into account such facts and factors as the loan amount, delivery of funds, economic capacity of the parties, local or inter-party transaction methods and trade usage, changes in the parties' property, and witness testimony, comprehensively determine and verify whether the lending fact has occurred.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  原告仅依据金融机构的转账凭证提起民间借贷诉讼，被告抗辩转账系偿还双方之前借款或者其他债务的，被告应当对其主张提供证据证明。被告提供相应证据证明其主张后，原告仍应就借贷关系的成立承担举证责任。

**Article 17** If the plaintiff initiates a private lending action solely on the basis of a transfer voucher from a financial institution, and the defendant defends on the ground that the transfer was for repayment of a previous loan or other debt between the parties, the defendant shall provide evidence to support its claim. After the defendant provides corresponding evidence to support its claim, the plaintiff shall still bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the formation of the lending relationship.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  依据《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第一百七十四条第二款之规定，负有举证责任的原告无正当理由拒不到庭，经审查现有证据无法确认借贷行为、借贷金额、支付方式等案件主要事实的，人民法院对原告主张的事实不予认定。

**Article 18** In accordance with Article 174, paragraph 2 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, if the plaintiff who bears the burden of adducing evidence refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, and upon examination the existing evidence cannot confirm the main facts of the case, such as the lending act, loan amount, and method of payment, the People's Court shall not recognize the facts claimed by the plaintiff.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  人民法院审理民间借贷纠纷案件时发现有下列情形之一的，应当严格审查借贷发生的原因、时间、地点、款项来源、交付方式、款项流向以及借贷双方的关系、经济状况等事实，综合判断是否属于虚假民事诉讼：  
（一）出借人明显不具备出借能力；  
（二）出借人起诉所依据的事实和理由明显不符合常理；  
（三）出借人不能提交债权凭证或者提交的债权凭证存在伪造的可能；  
（四）当事人双方在一定期限内多次参加民间借贷诉讼；  
（五）当事人无正当理由拒不到庭参加诉讼，委托代理人对借贷事实陈述不清或者陈述前后矛盾；  
（六）当事人双方对借贷事实的发生没有任何争议或者诉辩明显不符合常理；  
（七）借款人的配偶或者合伙人、案外人的其他债权人提出有事实依据的异议；  
（八）当事人在其他纠纷中存在低价转让财产的情形；  
（九）当事人不正当放弃权利；  
（十）其他可能存在虚假民间借贷诉讼的情形。

**Article 19** When hearing a private lending dispute case, if the People's Court discovers any of the following circumstances, it shall strictly examine such facts as the cause, time, and place of the loan; the source of funds; the method of delivery; the flow of funds; and the relationship and economic status of the parties to the loan, and comprehensively determine whether it is a false civil action:  
(1) the lender obviously does not have the capacity to lend;  
(2) the facts and reasons on which the lender bases its action are obviously inconsistent with common sense;  
(3) the lender cannot submit debt instruments, or the submitted debt instruments may be forged;  
(4) both parties have participated in private lending actions multiple times within a certain period;  
(5) a party refuses to appear in court for the action without justifiable reasons, and the authorized agent makes unclear or contradictory statements about the lending facts;  
(6) the parties have no dispute about the occurrence of the lending facts, or the claims and defenses are obviously inconsistent with common sense;  
(7) the borrower's spouse or partner, or another creditor of a person not involved in the case, raises a factually based objection;  
(8) a party has transferred property at a low price in other disputes;  
(9) a party improperly waives rights;  
(10) other circumstances that may indicate a false private lending action.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  经查明属于虚假民间借贷诉讼，原告申请撤诉的，人民法院不予准许，并应当依据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-112)之规定，判决驳回其请求。  
诉讼参与人或者其他人恶意制造、参与虚假诉讼，人民法院应当依据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)、第一百一十二条和第一百一十三条之规定，依法予以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，应当移送有管辖权的司法机关追究刑事责任。  
单位恶意制造、参与虚假诉讼的，人民法院应当对该单位进行罚款，并可以对其主要负责人或者直接责任人员予以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，应当移送有管辖权的司法机关追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** If after investigation it is determined that the action is a false private lending action, and the plaintiff applies to withdraw the action, the People's Court shall not permit it, and shall, in accordance with Article 112 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims.  
If a participant in the proceedings or any other person maliciously creates or participates in a false action, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Articles 111, 112, and 113 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, impose a fine or detention according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the matter shall be transferred to a judicial authority with jurisdiction to investigate criminal liability.  
If a unit maliciously creates or participates in a false action, the People's Court shall impose a fine on the unit, and may also impose a fine or detention on its principal responsible person or directly responsible personnel; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the matter shall be transferred to a judicial authority with jurisdiction to investigate criminal liability.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  他人在借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证或者借款合同上签名或者盖章，但是未表明其保证人身份或者承担保证责任，或者通过其他事实不能推定其为保证人，出借人请求其承担保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** If a person signs or affixes a seal on a debt instrument such as an IOU, receipt, or promissory note, or on a loan contract, but does not indicate his or her status as a surety or an assumption of suretyship liability, or if it cannot be inferred from other facts that the person is a surety, and the lender requests that person to assume suretyship liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  借贷双方通过网络贷款平台形成借贷关系，网络贷款平台的提供者仅提供媒介服务，当事人请求其承担担保责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
网络贷款平台的提供者通过网页、广告或者其他媒介明示或者有其他证据证明其为借贷提供担保，出借人请求网络贷款平台的提供者承担担保责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 22** If the parties to a loan form a lending relationship through an online lending platform, and the provider of the online lending platform only provides intermediary services, and a party requests it to assume suretyship liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.  
If the provider of the online lending platform expressly states through web pages, advertisements, or other media, or if there is other evidence proving that it provides a guarantee for the loan, and the lender requests the provider of the online lending platform to assume guarantee liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  法人的法定代表人或者非法人组织的负责人以单位名义与出借人签订民间借贷合同，有证据证明所借款项系法定代表人或者负责人个人使用，出借人请求将法定代表人或者负责人列为共同被告或者第三人的，人民法院应予准许。  
法人的法定代表人或者非法人组织的负责人以个人名义与出借人订立民间借贷合同，所借款项用于单位生产经营，出借人请求单位与个人共同承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** If the legal representative of a legal person or the responsible person of an unincorporated organization signs a private lending contract with a lender in the name of the unit, and there is evidence proving that the borrowed funds are used personally by the legal representative or responsible person, and the lender requests to list the legal representative or responsible person as a co-defendant or third party, the People's Court shall permit such request.  
If the legal representative of a legal person or the responsible person of an unincorporated organization enters into a private lending contract with a lender in his or her personal name, and the borrowed funds are used for the production or business operations of the unit, and the lender requests the unit and the individual to jointly bear liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  当事人以订立买卖合同作为民间借贷合同的担保，借款到期后借款人不能还款，出借人请求履行买卖合同的，人民法院应当按照民间借贷法律关系审理。当事人根据法庭审理情况变更诉讼请求的，人民法院应当准许。  
按照民间借贷法律关系审理作出的判决生效后，借款人不履行生效判决确定的金钱债务，出借人可以申请拍卖买卖合同标的物，以偿还债务。就拍卖所得的价款与应偿还借款本息之间的差额，借款人或者出借人有权主张返还或者补偿。

**Article 24** If the parties use the conclusion of a sales contract as a guarantee for a private lending contract, and after the loan becomes due, the borrower fails to repay, and the lender requests performance of the sales contract, the People's Court shall hear the case in accordance with the private lending legal relationship. If a party changes the claim in light of the court hearing, the People's Court shall permit it.  
After a judgment rendered in accordance with the private lending legal relationship takes effect, if the borrower fails to perform the monetary obligation under the effective judgment, the lender may apply for the auction of the subject matter of the sales contract to satisfy the debt. The borrower or the lender shall be entitled to claim a refund or compensation for the difference between the proceeds from the auction and the principal and interest that should be repaid.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  借贷双方没有约定利息，出借人主张支付利息的，人民法院不予支持。  
自然人之间借贷对利息约定不明，出借人主张支付利息的，人民法院不予支持。除自然人之间借贷的外，借贷双方对借贷利息约定不明，出借人主张利息的，人民法院应当结合民间借贷合同的内容，并根据当地或者当事人的交易方式、交易习惯、市场报价利率等因素确定利息。

**Article 25** If the parties to a loan have not agreed on interest, and the lender claims payment of interest, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.  
If a loan between natural persons has an unclear agreement on interest, and the lender claims payment of interest, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim. For loans other than those between natural persons, if the parties have an unclear agreement on interest, and the lender claims interest, the People's Court shall, taking into account the contents of the private lending contract and based on factors such as local or inter-party transaction methods, trade usage, and market quotation rate, determine the interest.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  出借人请求借款人按照合同约定利率支付利息的，人民法院应予支持，但是双方约定的利率超过合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍的除外。  
前款所称“一年期贷款市场报价利率”，是指中国人民银行授权全国银行间同业拆借中心自2019年8月20日起每月发布的一年期贷款市场报价利率。

**Article 26** If the lender requests the borrower to pay interest at the rate agreed in the contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request, unless the interest rate agreed by the parties exceeds four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the contract was formed.  
The “one-year loan prime rate” referred to in the preceding paragraph means the one-year loan prime rate published monthly by the National Interbank Funding Center as authorized by the People's Bank of China from August 20, 2019.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  借据、收据、欠条等债权凭证载明的借款金额，一般认定为本金。预先在本金中扣除利息的，人民法院应当将实际出借的金额认定为本金。

**Article 27** The loan amount stated in debt instruments such as IOUs, receipts, and promissory notes shall generally be recognized as the principal. If interest is deducted in advance from the principal, the People's Court shall recognize the amount actually lent as the principal.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  借贷双方对前期借款本息结算后将利息计入后期借款本金并重新出具债权凭证，如果前期利率没有超过合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍，重新出具的债权凭证载明的金额可认定为后期借款本金。超过部分的利息，不应认定为后期借款本金。  
按前款计算，借款人在借款期间届满后应当支付的本息之和，超过以最初借款本金与以最初借款本金为基数、以合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍计算的整个借款期间的利息之和的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** If the parties, after settling the principal and interest of a prior loan, add the interest to the principal of a subsequent loan and re-issue a debt instrument, and if the prior interest rate does not exceed four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the contract was formed, the amount stated in the newly issued debt instrument may be recognized as the principal of the subsequent loan. Interest in excess of that amount shall not be recognized as the principal of the subsequent loan.  
If, calculated in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the total of principal and interest that the borrower shall pay after the loan period expires exceeds the sum of the original principal and the interest calculated on the basis of the original principal at four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the contract was formed for the entire loan period, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim for such excess.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  借贷双方对逾期利率有约定的，从其约定，但是以不超过合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍为限。  
未约定逾期利率或者约定不明的，人民法院可以区分不同情况处理：  
（一）既未约定借期内利率，也未约定逾期利率，出借人主张借款人自逾期还款之日起承担逾期还款违约责任的，人民法院应予支持；  
（二）约定了借期内利率但是未约定逾期利率，出借人主张借款人自逾期还款之日起按照借期内利率支付资金占用期间利息的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 29** If the parties have agreed on an overdue interest rate, such agreement shall prevail, provided that it does not exceed four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the contract was formed.  
If there is no agreement on the overdue interest rate or the agreement is unclear, the People's Court may differentiate the situations and handle them as follows:  
(1) if neither the in-term interest rate nor the overdue interest rate is agreed, and the lender claims that the borrower shall bear liability for breach of contract due to overdue repayment from the date of overdue repayment, the People's Court shall uphold such claim;  
(2) if the in-term interest rate is agreed but the overdue interest rate is not agreed, and the lender claims that the borrower shall pay interest for the period of fund occupation from the date of overdue repayment at the in-term interest rate, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  出借人与借款人既约定了逾期利率，又约定了违约金或者其他费用，出借人可以选择主张逾期利息、违约金或者其他费用，也可以一并主张，但是总计超过合同成立时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍的部分，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 30** If the lender and the borrower have agreed on both an overdue interest rate and liquidated damages or other fees, the lender may choose to claim overdue interest, liquidated damages, or other fees, or may claim them together; however, if the total exceeds four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the contract was formed, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim for the excess part.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  借款人可以提前偿还借款，但是当事人另有约定的除外。  
借款人提前偿还借款并主张按照实际借款期限计算利息的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 31** The borrower may repay the loan in advance, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.  
If the borrower repays the loan in advance and claims that interest shall be calculated based on the actual loan period, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条  本规定施行后，人民法院新受理的一审民间借贷纠纷案件，适用本规定。  
借贷行为发生在2019年8月20日之前的，可参照原告起诉时一年期贷款市场报价利率四倍确定受保护的利率上限。  
本规定施行后，最高人民法院以前作出的相关司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 32** After these Provisions come into force, the People's Court shall apply these Provisions to newly accepted first-instance private lending dispute cases.  
If the lending act occurred before August 20, 2019, the protected interest rate cap may be determined by reference to four times the one-year loan prime rate at the time the plaintiff initiated the action.  
After these Provisions come into force, if any previous relevant judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
