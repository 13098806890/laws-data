# 最高人民法院关于人民法院相互办理委托事项的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕26号  
**公布日期**：1993-09-25

---

### 正文

一、人民法院在案件审理和执行过程中，根据需要，可以委托其他人民法院代为调查、送达、宣判和代为执行。

**Article None** 1. A People's Court, in the course of adjudication and enforcement of a case, may, as necessary, entrust another People's Court to conduct investigations, service of documents, pronouncement of judgments, and enforcement on its behalf.

二、被调查人或者被调查的事项在外地的，受理案件的人民法院可以委托当地基层人民法院代为调查。必要时也可以委托当地中级人民法院代为调查。

**Article None** 2. Where the person to be investigated or the matter to be investigated is located outside the locality, the People's Court accepting the case may entrust the local Primary People's Court to conduct the investigation on its behalf. When necessary, it may also entrust the local Intermediate People's Court to conduct the investigation.

三、委托外地人民法院代为调查，委托人民法院应当出具委托书，提出明确的调查事项和要求。必要时，应当简要介绍案情或者附具调查提纲。  
根据情况需要，受委托人民法院也可以主动作补充调查。

**Article None** 3. When entrusting an out-of-locality People's Court to conduct an investigation on its behalf, the entrusting People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment, specifying the matters and requirements for the investigation. When necessary, it shall briefly introduce the case or attach an investigation outline.  
Where circumstances so require, the entrusted People's Court may also proactively conduct supplementary investigations.

四、委托调查，受委托人民法院应当在收到委托书之日起三十日内完成。委托书中对完成委托调查的时间有特殊要求的，应当在要求的期限内完成。因故不能完成的，应当在上述期限内函告委托人民法院，说明情况。

**Article None** 4. For an entrusted investigation, the entrusted People's Court shall complete it within thirty days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment. If the letter of entrustment contains special requirements regarding the time for completing the entrusted investigation, it shall be completed within the required time limit. If it cannot be completed for any reason, the entrusted People's Court shall inform the entrusting People's Court in writing within the aforesaid time limit, stating the reasons.

五、人民法院在审理案件过程中，需要勘验现场的，一般不应委托其他人民法院代为调查。

**Article None** 5. If a People's Court needs to conduct an on-site inspection during the adjudication of a case, it shall generally not entrust another People's Court to conduct the investigation on its behalf.

六、受送达人在外地，或者虽在本地但由受理案件的人民法院直接送达有困难的，可以委托送达，由受理案件的人民法院将需要送达的诉讼文书交由受送达人住所地的基层人民法院代为送达。

**Article None** 6. Where the person to be served with documents is located outside the locality, or although within the locality but direct service by the People's Court accepting the case is difficult, service may be entrusted to the Primary People's Court of the place where the person to be served has his or her domicile, and the People's Court accepting the case shall deliver the litigation documents to be served to such Primary People's Court for service on its behalf.

七、委托送达，委托人民法院应当出具委托书，并附需要送达的诉讼文书和送达回证。委托人民法院对送达诉讼文书有特殊要求的，应当在委托书中说明。  
受委托送达的人民法院应当在收到委托书之日起七日内完成，并将送达回证寄回委托人民法院。因故无法送达的，应当在上述期限内函告委托人民法院。

**Article None** 7. For entrusted service, the entrusting People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment, and attach the litigation documents to be served and the certificate of service. If the entrusting People's Court has special requirements for the service of litigation documents, it shall state them in the letter of entrustment.  
The entrusted People's Court for service shall complete the service within seven days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment, and return the certificate of service to the entrusting People's Court. If service cannot be effected for any reason, it shall inform the entrusting People's Court in writing within the aforesaid time limit.

八、受送达人下落不明，或者诉讼文书需要由有关单位转交送达的，受理案件的人民法院不得委托其他人民法院代为送达。

**Article None** 8. Where the whereabouts of the person to be served with documents are unknown, or the litigation documents need to be served through a relevant entity for onward transmission, the People's Court accepting the case shall not entrust another People's Court to effect service on its behalf.

九、接受宣判的当事人在外地的，受理案件的人民法院可以委托当地基层人民法院代为宣判。明知接受宣判的当事人下落不明的，不应委托宣判。

**Article None** 9. Where a party who is to receive a judgment pronouncement is located outside the locality, the People's Court accepting the case may entrust the local Primary People's Court to pronounce the judgment on its behalf. If it is known that the whereabouts of the party who is to receive the judgment pronouncement are unknown, the entrustment for pronouncement shall not be made.

十、委托宣判，委托人民法院应当出具委托书和需要宣判的法律文书。委托人民法院对委托宣判事项有特殊要求的，应当在委托书中说明。  
受委托人民法院应当在收到委托书之日起七日内完成，并将宣判情况制作笔录；宣判后，及时将宣判笔录和送达回证寄回委托人民法院。

**Article None** 10. For entrustment of judgment pronouncement, the entrusting People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment and the legal documents to be pronounced. If the entrusting People's Court has special requirements for the matters of the entrusted pronouncement, it shall state them in the letter of entrustment.  
The entrusted People's Court shall complete the pronouncement within seven days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment, and make a record of the pronouncement; after the pronouncement, it shall promptly send the record of pronouncement and the certificate of service back to the entrusting People's Court.

十一、在执行中，被执行人、被执行的财产在外地的，负责执行的人民法院可以委托当地基层人民法院代为执行。

**Article None** 11. During enforcement, where the person subject to enforcement or the property subject to enforcement is located outside the locality, the People's Court responsible for enforcement may entrust the local Primary People's Court to carry out the enforcement on its behalf.

十二、委托执行，委托人民法院应当出具委托书和生效的法律文书。委托书应当提出明确的执行要求。有关需要说明的事项，应当另附函件。

**Article None** 12. For entrustment of enforcement, the entrusting People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment and the effective legal document. The letter of entrustment shall set forth clear enforcement requirements. Matters requiring explanation shall be attached in a separate letter.

十三、委托执行，受委托人民法院必须接受，并应当严格按照生效法律文书的规定和委托人民法院的要求执行，不对生效的法律文书进行审查。

**Article None** 13. For entrustment of enforcement, the entrusted People's Court must accept the entrustment, and shall strictly carry out the enforcement in accordance with the provisions of the effective legal document and the requirements of the entrusting People's Court, and shall not review the effective legal document.

十四、受委托人民法院必须在收到委托书和生效法律文书之日起十五日内开始执行，并在开始执行后，及时告知委托人民法院。  
对债务人履行债务的时间、期限和方式需要变通的，应当征得委托人民法院的同意。

**Article None** 14. The entrusted People's Court must commence enforcement within fifteen days from the date of receiving the letter of entrustment and the effective legal document, and shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court after commencement of enforcement.  
If the time, period, or method for the debtor to perform the debt needs to be adjusted, the consent of the entrusting People's Court shall be obtained.

十五、受委托人民法院在办理委托执行过程中，有权依法采取必要的执行措施。

**Article None** 15. The entrusted People's Court, in the process of handling the entrusted enforcement, has the right to take necessary enforcement measures according to law.

十六、在委托执行中，被执行人和申请人自行和解达成协议的，受委托人民法院应当及时将协议内容告知委托人民法院。  
一方当事人不履行和解协议或者翻悔的，对方当事人应当向委托人民法院申请恢复执行，并由委托人民法院通知受委托人民法院。

**Article None** 16. During entrusted enforcement, if the person subject to enforcement and the applicant reach a settlement agreement on their own, the entrusted People's Court shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court of the content of the agreement.  
If one party fails to perform the settlement agreement or reneges, the other party shall apply to the entrusting People's Court for resumption of enforcement, and the entrusting People's Court shall notify the entrusted People's Court accordingly.

十七、在委托执行中。被执行人申请提供担保，并经申请执行人同意的，受委托人民法院应当及时函告委托人民法院，由委托人民法院决定是否暂缓执行及暂缓执行的期限。

**Article None** 17. During entrusted enforcement, if the person subject to enforcement applies to provide a surety and the applicant for enforcement consents, the entrusted People's Court shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court in writing, and the entrusting People's Court shall decide whether to suspend enforcement and the period of such suspension.

十八、在委托执行中，案外人对执行标的提出异议的，受委托人民法院应当函告委托人民法院处理。

**Article None** 18. During entrusted enforcement, if a person other than a party raises an objection to the subject matter of enforcement, the entrusted People's Court shall inform the entrusting People's Court in writing for handling.

十九、受委托人民法院遇有需要中止执行或者终结执行的情形，应当及时函告委托人民法院，由委托人民法院作出裁定。受委托人民法院不得自行裁定中止执行或者终结执行。

**Article None** 19. If the entrusted People's Court encounters circumstances requiring suspension or termination of enforcement, it shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court in writing, and the entrusting People's Court shall make a ruling. The entrusted People's Court shall not make a ruling on its own to suspend or terminate enforcement.

二十、被执行人的财产不足以清偿所有申请人的债权，受委托人民法院应当在征得委托人民法院的同意后，严格按照规定的清偿顺序执行。

**Article None** 20. If the property of the person subject to enforcement is insufficient to satisfy the claims of all applicants, the entrusted People's Court shall, after obtaining the consent of the entrusting People's Court, strictly carry out enforcement in accordance with the prescribed order of distribution.

二十一、执行完毕后，受委托人民法院应当将执行结果及时函复委托人民法院；如果在三十日内还未执行完毕，也应当将执行情况函告委托人民法院。

**Article None** 21. After the enforcement is completed, the entrusted People's Court shall promptly reply in writing to the entrusting People's Court with the results of the enforcement; if the enforcement is not completed within thirty days, it shall also inform the entrusting People's Court in writing of the status of the enforcement.

二十二、委托人民法院自委托书和生效法律文书发送之日起三十日内，没有收到受委托人民法院关于开始执行的通知的，可以请求受委托人民法院的上一级人民法院指令受委托人民法院执行。  
受委托人民法院的上一级人民法院在接到委托人民法院的请求后，应当在五日内指令受委托人民法院执行，并将这一情况及时告知委托人民法院。  
委托人民法院自向受委托人民法院的上一级人民法院发出指令执行的请求之日起二十日内，没有收到该人民法院关于已经指令执行的通知的，可以再行向该人民法院的上级人民法院逐级请求。上级人民法院必须及时指令执行。

**Article None** 22. If the entrusting People's Court does not receive a notification from the entrusted People's Court regarding the commencement of enforcement within thirty days from the date of sending the letter of entrustment and the effective legal document, it may request the People's Court at the next higher level over the entrusted People's Court to direct the entrusted People's Court to enforce.  
The People's Court at the next higher level over the entrusted People's Court, upon receiving the request from the entrusting People's Court, shall, within five days, direct the entrusted People's Court to enforce, and shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court of this fact.  
If the entrusting People's Court does not receive a notification from such People's Court that it has directed enforcement within twenty days from the date of sending the request for direction of enforcement to the People's Court at the next higher level over the entrusted People's Court, it may again make a request level by level to the People's Court at a higher level over such People's Court. The higher-level People's Court must promptly direct enforcement.

二十三、委托执行，受委托人民法院的执行员应当将执行情况制作笔录，在执行完毕后，交由委托人民法院存档备查。

**Article None** 23. For entrusted enforcement, the enforcement officer of the entrusted People's Court shall make a record of the enforcement status, and after the enforcement is completed, deliver it to the entrusting People's Court for archiving and future reference.

二十四、在委托执行的执行过程中，受委托人民法院发现委托执行的法律文书有错误的，应当及时函请委托人民法院进行审查。在委托人民法院作出答复后，受委托人民法院如有异议，可以向委托人民法院的上一级人民法院反映，但不得停止执行。

**Article None** 24. During the process of entrusted enforcement, if the entrusted People's Court discovers an error in the legal document for entrusted enforcement, it shall promptly request the entrusting People's Court in writing to review it. After the entrusting People's Court gives its response, if the entrusted People's Court has any objection, it may report to the People's Court at the next higher level over the entrusting People's Court, but it shall not suspend enforcement.

二十五、办理委托执行，委托人民法院不应收取执行费，受委托人民法院也不得向委托人民法院收取费用。在执行中实际支出的费用，按照《人民法院诉讼收费办法》收取。

**Article None** 25. For handling entrusted enforcement, the entrusting People's Court shall not collect enforcement fees, and the entrusted People's Court shall not charge any fees to the entrusting People's Court. Expenses actually incurred in the enforcement shall be collected in accordance with the Measures for Litigation Fee Collection by People's Courts.

二十六、被执行人、被执行的财产在外地的，根据需要，人民法院可以直接到外地执行；要求当地人民法院协助执行的，当地人民法院应当积极配合，不得推拖、阻挠执行。

**Article None** 26. Where the person subject to enforcement or the property subject to enforcement is located outside the locality, the People's Court may, as necessary, go directly to the outside locality to enforce; if it requests the local People's Court to assist in enforcement, the local People's Court shall actively cooperate and shall not delay, obstruct, or impede enforcement.

二十七、办理委托调查、送达、宣判事项，由人民法院审判庭负责。办理委托执行事项，由人民法院执行庭负责；尚未设立执行庭的，由有关审判庭负责。

**Article None** 27. Matters concerning entrusted investigation, service, and judgment pronouncement shall be handled by the adjudication division of the People's Court. Matters concerning entrusted enforcement shall be handled by the enforcement division of the People's Court; if no enforcement division has been established, they shall be handled by the relevant adjudication division.

二十八、受委托人民法院办理委托事项，应当建立登记制度，登记内容包括：委托人民法院、委托时间、委托事项和办理结果等。

**Article None** 28. The entrusted People's Court shall establish a registration system for handling entrusted matters, with the content of the registration including: the entrusting People's Court, the time of entrustment, the entrusted matters, and the handling results, among others.

二十九、上级人民法院应当督促下级人民法院及时办理委托事项。受委托人民法院应当向上一级人民法院报告办理委托事项的情况。委托人民法院也可定期或不定期向受委托人民法院的上一级人民法院反映受委托人民法院办理委托事项的情况。

**Article None** 29. The higher-level People's Court shall urge the lower-level People's Court to handle entrusted matters in a timely manner. The entrusted People's Court shall report to the People's Court at the next higher level on the status of handling entrusted matters. The entrusting People's Court may also, periodically or from time to time, report to the People's Court at the next higher level over the entrusted People's Court on the status of handling entrusted matters by the entrusted People's Court.

三十、受委托人民法院要把办理委托事项作为考核干部的一项重要内容，纳入岗位责任制，同其他工作一样进行考核和奖惩。

**Article None** 30. The entrusted People's Court shall regard the handling of entrusted matters as an important element of cadre assessment, incorporate it into the post responsibility system, and conduct assessment, rewards and penalties in the same manner as for other work.

三十一、上级人民法院对下级人民法院不依法办理委托事项，造成重大影响或后果的，应当予以通报批评；情节严重的，追究相应的行政和法律责任。

**Article None** 31. Where a lower-level People's Court fails to handle entrusted matters according to law, causing significant impact or consequences, the higher-level People's Court shall circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, corresponding administrative and legal liabilities shall be pursued.  
September 25, 1993
