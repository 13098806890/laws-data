# 最高人民法院关于委托执行若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕11号  
**公布日期**：2011-05-03  
**生效日期**：2011-05-16

> 最高人民法院《关于委托执行若干问题的规定》已于2011年4月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1521次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年5月16日起施行。 2011年5月3日 最高人民法院 关于委托执行若干问题的规定 法释〔2011〕11号 （2011年4月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1521次会议通过　2011年5月3日最高人民法院公告公布　自2011年5月16日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　执行法院经调查发现被执行人在本辖区内已无财产可供执行，且在其他省、自治区、直辖市内有可供执行财产的，应当将案件委托异地的同级人民法院执行。  
执行案件中有三个以上被执行人或者三处以上被执行财产在本省、自治区、直辖市辖区以外，且分属不同异地的，执行法院根据案件具体情况，报经高级人民法院批准后可以异地执行。

**Article 1** Where, upon investigation, the enforcing court discovers that the person subject to enforcement has no property available for enforcement within its jurisdiction, but has property available for enforcement in another province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, it shall entrust the case to a People's Court of the same level in the different locality for enforcement.  
Where there are three or more persons subject to enforcement, or three or more properties subject to enforcement located outside the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and they are in different localities, the enforcing court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case and upon approval by the Higher People's Court, conduct enforcement in the different locality.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　案件委托执行后，受托法院应当依法立案，委托法院应当在收到受托法院的立案通知书后作委托结案处理。  
委托异地法院协助查询、冻结、查封、调查或者送达法律文书等有关事项的，受托法院不作为委托执行案件立案办理，但应当积极予以协助。

**Article 2** After a case is entrusted for enforcement, the entrusted court shall docket the case according to law, and the entrusting court shall, upon receipt of the notice of docketing from the entrusted court, handle the matter as concluded entrustment.  
Where matters such as inquiry, freezing, sealing, investigation, or service of legal documents are entrusted to a court in a different locality for assistance, the entrusted court shall not docket the matter as an entrusted enforcement case, but shall actively provide assistance.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　委托执行应当以执行标的物所在地或者执行行为实施地的同级人民法院为受托执行法院。有两处以上财产在异地的，可以委托主要财产所在地的人民法院执行。  
被执行人是现役军人或者军事单位的，可以委托对其有管辖权的军事法院执行。  
执行标的物是船舶的，可以委托有管辖权的海事法院执行。

**Article 3** For entrusted enforcement, the People's Court of the same level at the location of the subject matter of enforcement or the place where the enforcement act is implemented shall be the entrusted enforcement court. Where there are two or more properties in different localities, enforcement may be entrusted to the People's Court at the location of the principal property.  
Where the person subject to enforcement is an active serviceman or a military unit, enforcement may be entrusted to a military court that has jurisdiction over it.  
Where the subject matter of enforcement is a ship, enforcement may be entrusted to a maritime court that has jurisdiction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　委托执行案件应当由委托法院直接向受托法院办理委托手续，并层报各自所在的高级人民法院备案。  
事项委托应当以机要形式送达委托事项的相关手续，不需报高级人民法院备案。

**Article 4** For an entrusted enforcement case, the entrusting court shall directly handle the entrustment procedures with the entrusted court, and shall report level by level to their respective Higher People's Courts for filing.  
For entrustment of matters, the relevant procedures for the entrusted matters shall be served in a confidential form, and no filing with the Higher People's Court is required.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　案件委托执行时，委托法院应当提供下列材料：  
（一）委托执行函；  
（二）申请执行书和委托执行案件审批表；  
（三）据以执行的生效法律文书副本；  
（四）有关案件情况的材料或者说明，包括本辖区无财产的调查材料、财产保全情况、被执行人财产状况、生效法律文书的履行情况等；  
（五）申请执行人地址、联系电话；  
（六）被执行人身份证件或者营业执照复印件、地址、联系电话；  
（七）委托法院执行员和联系电话；  
（八）其他必要的案件材料等。

**Article 5** When a case is entrusted for enforcement, the entrusting court shall provide the following materials:  
(1) a letter of entrustment for enforcement;  
(2) the application for enforcement and the approval form for the entrusted enforcement case;  
(3) a copy of the effective legal document based on which enforcement is sought;  
(4) materials or explanations concerning the case, including investigation materials showing no property within its jurisdiction, the status of property preservation, the property status of the person subject to enforcement, the performance status of the effective legal document, etc.;  
(5) the address and contact telephone number of the applicant for enforcement;  
(6) copies of the identity document or business license of the person subject to enforcement, address, and contact telephone number;  
(7) the enforcement officer of the entrusting court and contact telephone number;  
(8) other necessary case materials, etc.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　委托执行时，委托法院应当将已经查封、扣押、冻结的被执行人的异地财产，一并移交受托法院处理，并在委托执行函中说明。  
委托执行后，委托法院对被执行人财产已经采取查封、扣押、冻结等措施的，视为受托法院的查封、扣押、冻结措施。受托法院需要继续查封、扣押、冻结，持委托执行函和立案通知书办理相关手续。续封续冻时，仍为原委托法院的查封冻结顺序。  
查封、扣押、冻结等措施的有效期限在移交受托法院时不足1个月的，委托法院应当先行续封或者续冻，再移交受托法院。

**Article 6** When entrusting enforcement, the entrusting court shall transfer to the entrusted court for handling all properties of the person subject to enforcement in different localities that have already been sealed, seized, or frozen, and shall indicate this in the letter of entrustment for enforcement.  
After entrustment of enforcement, any measures such as sealing, seizure, or freezing already taken by the entrusting court against the property of the person subject to enforcement shall be deemed as measures of the entrusted court. If the entrusted court needs to continue the sealing, seizure, or freezing, it shall handle the relevant procedures with the letter of entrustment for enforcement and the notice of docketing. When extending the sealing or freezing, the order of sealing or freezing of the original entrusting court shall still prevail.  
If the validity period of measures such as sealing, seizure, or freezing is less than one month when they are transferred to the entrusted court, the entrusting court shall first extend the sealing or freezing, and then transfer them to the entrusted court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　受托法院收到委托执行函后，应当在7日内予以立案，并及时将立案通知书通过委托法院送达申请执行人，同时将指定的承办人、联系电话等书面告知委托法院。  
委托法院收到上述通知书后，应当在7日内书面通知申请执行人案件已经委托执行，并告知申请执行人可以直接与受托法院联系执行相关事宜。

**Article 7** After receiving the letter of entrustment for enforcement, the entrusted court shall docket the case within seven days, and shall promptly serve the notice of docketing on the applicant for enforcement through the entrusting court, and at the same time inform the entrusting court in writing of the designated person handling the case, contact telephone number, etc.  
After receiving the aforesaid notice, the entrusting court shall, within seven days, notify the applicant for enforcement in writing that the case has been entrusted for enforcement, and inform the applicant for enforcement that it may directly contact the entrusted court regarding matters related to enforcement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　受托法院如发现委托执行的手续、材料不全，可以要求委托法院补办。委托法院应当在30日内完成补办事项，在上述期限内未完成的，应当作出书面说明。委托法院既不补办又不说明原因的，视为撤回委托，受托法院可以将委托材料退回委托法院。

**Article 8** If the entrusted court discovers that the procedures or materials for entrusted enforcement are incomplete, it may request the entrusting court to complete them. The entrusting court shall complete the supplementary matters within 30 days; if it fails to do so within the aforesaid period, it shall provide a written explanation. If the entrusting court neither completes the supplementary matters nor provides an explanation, the entrustment shall be deemed withdrawn, and the entrusted court may return the entrustment materials to the entrusting court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　受托法院退回委托的，应当层报所在辖区高级人民法院审批。高级人民法院同意退回后，受托法院应当在15日内将有关委托手续和案卷材料退回委托法院，并作出书面说明。  
委托执行案件退回后，受托法院已立案的，应当作销案处理。委托法院在案件退回原因消除之后可以再行委托。确因委托不当被退回的，委托法院应当决定撤销委托并恢复案件执行，报所在的高级人民法院备案。

**Article 9** If the entrusted court returns the entrustment, it shall report level by level to the Higher People's Court in its jurisdiction for examination and approval. After the Higher People's Court approves the return, the entrusted court shall, within 15 days, return the relevant entrustment procedures and case files to the entrusting court, and provide a written explanation.  
After an entrusted enforcement case is returned, if the entrusted court has already docketed the case, it shall handle the matter as case closure. The entrusting court may re-entrust after the cause for the return of the case is eliminated. If the case is returned due to improper entrustment, the entrusting court shall decide to revoke the entrustment and resume enforcement of the case, and report to its Higher People's Court for filing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　委托法院在案件委托执行后又发现有可供执行财产的，应当及时告知受托法院。受托法院发现被执行人在受托法院辖区外另有可供执行财产的，可以直接异地执行，一般不再行委托执行。根据情况确需再行委托的，应当按照委托执行案件的程序办理，并通知案件当事人。

**Article 10** If, after the case has been entrusted for enforcement, the entrusting court discovers further property available for enforcement, it shall promptly inform the entrusted court. If the entrusted court discovers that the person subject to enforcement has other property available for enforcement outside the jurisdiction of the entrusted court, it may directly conduct enforcement in the different locality, and generally shall not re-entrust for enforcement. If, based on the circumstances, re-entrustment is indeed necessary, it shall be handled in accordance with the procedures for entrusted enforcement cases, and the parties to the case shall be notified.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　受托法院未能在6个月内将受托案件执结的，申请执行人有权请求受托法院的上一级人民法院提级执行或者指定执行，上一级人民法院应当立案审查，发现受托法院无正当理由不予执行的，应当限期执行或者作出裁定提级执行或者指定执行。

**Article 11** If the entrusted court fails to conclude the entrusted case within six months, the applicant for enforcement shall have the right to request the People's Court at the next higher level above the entrusted court to elevate the case for enforcement or to designate enforcement. The People's Court at the next higher level shall docket the case for review, and if it finds that the entrusted court has no justifiable reason for non-enforcement, it shall order enforcement within a time limit or make a ruling to elevate the case for enforcement or designate enforcement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　执行法院赴异地执行案件时，应当持有其所在辖区高级人民法院的批准函件，但异地采取财产保全措施和查封、扣押、冻结等非处分性执行措施的除外。  
异地执行时，可以根据案件具体情况，请求当地法院协助执行，当地法院应当积极配合，保证执行人员的人身安全和执行装备、执行标的物不受侵害。

**Article 12** When the enforcing court goes to a different locality to enforce a case, it shall hold an approval letter from the Higher People's Court in its jurisdiction, except when taking measures such as property preservation and non-dispositive enforcement measures such as sealing, seizure, or freezing in the different locality.  
When conducting enforcement in a different locality, it may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, request the local court to assist with the enforcement. The local court shall actively cooperate to ensure the personal safety of the enforcement personnel and to protect the enforcement equipment and subject matter of enforcement from harm.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　高级人民法院应当对辖区内委托执行和异地执行工作实行统一管理和协调，履行以下职责：  
（一）统一管理跨省、自治区、直辖市辖区的委托和受托执行案件；  
（二）指导、检查、监督本辖区内的受托案件的执行情况；  
（三）协调本辖区内跨省、自治区、直辖市辖区的委托和受托执行争议案件；  
（四）承办需异地执行的有关案件的审批事项；  
（五）对下级法院报送的有关委托和受托执行案件中的相关问题提出指导性处理意见；  
（六）办理其他涉及委托执行工作的事项。

**Article 13** The Higher People's Court shall exercise unified management and coordination over the work of entrusted enforcement and enforcement in different localities within its jurisdiction, and shall perform the following duties:  
(1) uniformly manage cases of entrustment and entrusted enforcement that cross the jurisdictions of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) guide, inspect, and supervise the enforcement of entrusted cases within its jurisdiction;  
(3) coordinate disputes in cases of entrustment and entrusted enforcement that cross the jurisdictions of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government within its jurisdiction;  
(4) undertake the examination and approval of relevant cases requiring enforcement in different localities;  
(5) provide guiding opinions on relevant issues in cases of entrustment and entrusted enforcement submitted by lower courts;  
(6) handle other matters related to entrusted enforcement work.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定所称的异地是指本省、自治区、直辖市以外的区域。各省、自治区、直辖市内的委托执行，由各高级人民法院参照本规定，结合实际情况，制定具体办法。

**Article 14** For the purposes of these Provisions, "different locality" refers to an area outside the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the court is located. Entrusted enforcement within each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be governed by specific measures formulated by the respective Higher People's Courts by reference to these Provisions and in light of actual circumstances.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定施行之后，其他有关委托执行的司法解释不再适用。

**Article 15** After these Provisions come into force, other judicial interpretations concerning entrusted enforcement shall no longer apply.
