# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于死刑第二审案件开庭审理程序若干问题的规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕8号  
**公布日期**：2006-08-28  
**生效日期**：2006-09-25

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于死刑第二审案件开庭审理程序若干问题的规定（试行）》已于2006年8月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1398次会议、2006年9月11日由最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第60次会议通过，现予公布，自2006年9月25日起施行。 2006年9月21日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于死刑第二审案件开庭审理程序 若干问题的规定（试行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  第二审人民法院审理第一审判处死刑立即执行的被告人上诉、人民检察院抗诉的案件，应当依照法律和有关规定开庭审理。

**Article 1** The second instance People's Court shall, when hearing a case appealed by a defendant sentenced to death penalty with immediate execution in the first instance or protested by a People's Procuratorate, hold a hearing in accordance with the law and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  第二审人民法院审理第一审判处死刑缓期二年执行的被告人上诉的案件，有下列情形之一的，应当开庭审理：  
（一）被告人或者辩护人提出影响定罪量刑的新证据，需要开庭审理的；  
（二）具有[刑事诉讼法第一百八十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-187)规定的开庭审理情形的。  
人民检察院对第一审人民法院判处死刑缓期二年执行提出抗诉的案件，第二审人民法院应当开庭审理。

**Article 2** The second instance People's Court shall, when hearing a case appealed by a defendant sentenced to death penalty with a two-year reprieve in the first instance, hold a hearing in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The defendant or defense counsel presents new evidence affecting conviction and sentencing, requiring a hearing;  
(2) There exist circumstances for a hearing as provided in Article 187 of the Criminal Procedure Law.  
Where the People's Procuratorate protests a sentence of death penalty with a two-year reprieve rendered by the first instance People's Court, the second instance People's Court shall hold a hearing.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  被判处死刑的被告人提出上诉的案件，原审人民法院应当在上诉期满后三日以内将上诉状连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，同时将上诉状副本送交同级人民检察院和当事人。收到上诉状副本的人民检察院应当在三日以内将上诉状副本及有关材料报送上一级人民检察院。  
对第一审的死刑判决抗诉的案件，提出抗诉的人民检察院向原审人民法院提交抗诉书后，应当在三日以内将抗诉书副本及有关材料报送上一级人民检察院。原审人民法院应当在抗诉期满后三日以内将抗诉书连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，并将抗诉书副本送交当事人。

**Article 3** Where a defendant sentenced to death files an appeal, the original trial People's Court shall, within three days after the expiration of the appeal period, transfer the appeal petition together with the case file and evidence to the People's Court at the next higher level, and simultaneously serve a copy of the appeal petition to the People's Procuratorate at the same level and the parties concerned. The People's Procuratorate receiving the copy of the appeal petition shall, within three days, submit the copy of the appeal petition and relevant materials to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level.  
In the case of a protest against a death sentence of the first instance, the People's Procuratorate filing the protest shall, after submitting the protest to the original trial People's Court, within three days submit the copy of the protest and relevant materials to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level. The original trial People's Court shall, within three days after the expiration of the protest period, transfer the protest together with the case file and evidence to the People's Court at the next higher level, and serve a copy of the protest to the parties concerned.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  对死刑判决提出上诉的被告人，在上诉期满后第二审开庭前要求撤回上诉的，第二审人民法院应当进行审查。合议庭经过阅卷、讯问被告人、听取其他当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人的意见后，认为原判决事实清楚，适用法律正确，量刑适当的，不再开庭审理，裁定准许被告人撤回上诉；认为原判决事实不清，证据不足或者将无罪判为有罪，轻罪重判的，应当不准许撤回上诉，按照第二审程序开庭审理。

**Article 4** Where a defendant who appeals a death sentence requests to withdraw the appeal after the expiration of the appeal period but before the second instance hearing, the second instance People's Court shall review the request. After reviewing the case files, interrogating the defendant, and hearing the opinions of other parties, defense counsel, and litigation agents, if the collegial panel considers that the facts found in the original judgment are clear, the law applied is correct, and the sentence is appropriate, it shall no longer hold a hearing and shall render a ruling permitting the defendant to withdraw the appeal; if it considers that the facts in the original judgment are unclear, the evidence is insufficient, or an innocent person was convicted of a crime, or a lesser crime was punished with a heavier penalty, it shall not permit the withdrawal of the appeal and shall hold a hearing in accordance with the second instance procedure.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  第二审人民法院开庭审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，合议庭应当在开庭前对案卷材料进行全面审查，重点审查下列内容：  
（一）上诉、抗诉的理由及是否提出了新的事实和证据；  
（二）被告人供述、辩解的情况；  
（三）辩护人的意见以及原审人民法院采纳的情况；  
（四）原审判决认定的事实是否清楚，证据是否确实、充分；  
（五）原审判决适用法律是否正确，量刑是否适当；  
(六)在侦查、起诉及审判中，有无违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情形；  
(七)原审人民法院合议庭、审判委员会讨论的意见；  
（八）其他对定罪量刑有影响的内容。

**Article 5** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing on a death penalty appeal or protest case, the collegial panel shall, before the hearing, conduct a comprehensive review of the case materials, focusing on the following:  
(1) The grounds for appeal or protest and whether new facts or evidence are raised;  
(2) The circumstances of the defendant's statements and explanations;  
(3) The opinions of the defense counsel and the extent to which they were adopted by the original trial court;  
(4) Whether the facts found in the original judgment are clear, and the evidence is reliable and sufficient;  
(5) Whether the law applied in the original judgment is correct and the sentence is appropriate;  
(6) Whether there have been violations of procedural law during investigation, prosecution, and adjudication;  
(7) The opinions of the collegial panel and the adjudication committee of the original trial People's Court;  
(8) Other content affecting conviction and sentencing.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  第二审人民法院应当及时查明被判处死刑立即执行的被告人是否委托了辩护人。没有委托辩护人的，应当告知被告人可以自行委托辩护人或者通知法律援助机构指定承担法律援助义务的律师为其提供辩护。  
被告人拒绝人民法院指定的辩护人为其辩护，有正当理由的，人民法院应当准许，被告人可以另行委托辩护人。被告人没有委托辩护人的，人民法院应当为其另行指定辩护人。

**Article 6** The second instance People's Court shall, in a timely manner, ascertain whether a defendant sentenced to death with immediate execution has retained a defense counsel. If no defense counsel has been retained, the court shall notify the defendant that he may retain a defense counsel on his own or notify the legal aid authority to designate a lawyer who undertakes legal aid obligations to provide defense for him.  
If the defendant refuses the defense counsel designated by the People's Court for a legitimate reason, the court shall permit the refusal, and the defendant may retain another defense counsel. If the defendant has not retained a defense counsel, the court shall designate another defense counsel for him.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  第二审人民法院开庭审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，应当在开庭十日以前通知人民检察院查阅案卷。

**Article 7** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing on a death penalty appeal or protest case, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate to review the case files at least ten days before the hearing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民检察院办理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，应当在开庭前对案卷材料进行全面审查，重点围绕抗诉或者上诉的理由，审查第一审判决认定案件事实、适用法律是否正确，证据是否确实、充分，量刑是否适当，审判活动是否合法，并进行下列工作：  
（一）应当讯问被告人，听取被告人的上诉理由或者辩解；  
（二）必要时听取辩护人的意见；  
（三）核查主要证据，必要时询问证人；  
（四）对鉴定结论有疑问的，可以重新鉴定或者补充鉴定；  
（五）根据案件情况，可以听取被害人的意见。

**Article 8** When handling death penalty appeal or protest cases, the People's Procuratorate shall, before the hearing, conduct a comprehensive review of the case materials, focusing on the grounds for protest or appeal, examining whether the facts found in the first instance judgment are correct, whether the law applied is correct, whether the evidence is reliable and sufficient, whether the sentence is appropriate, and whether the adjudication activities are lawful, and shall carry out the following work:  
(1) Interrogate the defendant and listen to the defendant's reasons for appeal or defenses;  
(2) When necessary, listen to the opinions of the defense counsel;  
(3) Verify the main evidence and, when necessary, question witnesses;  
(4) If doubts exist about the expert conclusion, may conduct a new expert evaluation or supplementary evaluation;  
(5) Based on the circumstances of the case, may listen to the opinions of the victim.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  第二审人民法院开庭审理的死刑上诉、抗诉案件，同级人民检察院应当派员出庭。

**Article 9** For death penalty appeal or protest cases heard by the second instance People's Court, the People's Procuratorate at the same level shall send a representative to attend the hearing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  第二审人民法院开庭审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，人民检察院应当在开庭前拟定庭审中的讯问、询问、举证、质证、答辩提纲和出庭意见书等。

**Article 10** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing on a death penalty appeal or protest case, the People's Procuratorate shall, before the hearing, prepare outlines for questioning, interrogation, presentation of evidence, cross-examination, arguments, and the opinion letter for appearance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  第二审人民法院开庭审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，应当由审判员三人至五人组成合议庭，对于疑难、复杂、重大的死刑案件，应当由院长或者庭长担任审判长。

**Article 11** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing on a death penalty appeal or protest case, the collegial panel shall be composed of three to five judges. For difficult, complex, or major death penalty cases, the president or chief judge shall serve as the presiding judge.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  合议庭应当在开庭前查明有关情况并做好以下准备工作：  
（一）在第一审判决宣判后，被告人是否有检举、揭发行为需要查证核实的；  
（二）是否存在可能导致延期审理的情形；  
（三）必要时应当讯问被告人；  
（四）拟定庭审提纲，确定需要开庭审理的内容；  
（五）将开庭的时间、地点在开庭三日以前通知人民检察院；  
（六）通知人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人在开庭五日以前提供出庭作证的证人、鉴定人名单；  
（七）将传唤当事人和通知辩护人、证人、鉴定人和翻译人员的传票和通知书，在开庭三日以前送达；  
（八）人民检察院向第二审人民法院提交新证据的，第二审人民法院应当通知被告人的辩护律师或者经许可的其他辩护人在开庭前到人民法院查阅；被告人及其辩护人向第二审人民法院提交新证据的，第二审人民法院应当通知人民检察院在开庭前到人民法院查阅；  
（九）人民检察院在审查期间进行重新鉴定或者补充鉴定的，作出的鉴定应当及时提交人民法院，人民法院应当在开庭三日以前将鉴定结论告知当事人及其诉讼代理人、辩护人；被害人及其诉讼代理人或者被告人及其辩护人提出重新鉴定、补充鉴定要求并经第二审人民法院同意的，作出的鉴定应当及时提交人民法院，人民法院应当在开庭三日以前将鉴定结论告知对方当事人及其诉讼代理人、辩护人并通知人民检察院；  
（十）公开审判的案件，在开庭三日以前先期公布案由、被告人姓名、开庭时间和地点；  
（十一）其他准备工作。  
上述活动情形应当写入笔录，由审判人员和书记员签名。

**Article 12** The collegial panel shall, before the hearing, ascertain relevant circumstances and make the following preparatory work:  
(1) After the pronouncement of the first instance judgment, whether the defendant has engaged in reporting or disclosure behavior that needs verification;  
(2) Whether there exist circumstances that may lead to an adjournment of the hearing;  
(3) When necessary, interrogate the defendant;  
(4) Prepare a hearing outline and determine the content to be heard;  
(5) Notify the People's Procuratorate of the time and place of the hearing at least three days before the hearing;  
(6) Notify the People's Procuratorate and the defendant and his defense counsel to provide a list of witnesses and expert witnesses who will testify in court at least five days before the hearing;  
(7) Deliver the summons for the parties and the notice for the defense counsel, witnesses, expert witnesses, and interpreters at least three days before the hearing;  
(8) If the People's Procuratorate submits new evidence to the second instance People's Court, the court shall notify the defendant's defense lawyer or other permitted defense counsel to review it at the court before the hearing; if the defendant or his defense counsel submits new evidence to the second instance People's Court, the court shall notify the People's Procuratorate to review it at the court before the hearing;  
(9) If the People's Procuratorate conducts a new expert evaluation or supplementary evaluation during the review period, the evaluation made shall be promptly submitted to the court, and the court shall, at least three days before the hearing, inform the parties and their litigation agents and defense counsel of the expert conclusion; if the victim or his litigation agent, or the defendant or his defense counsel requests a new expert evaluation or supplementary evaluation and obtains consent from the second instance People's Court, the evaluation made shall be promptly submitted to the court, and the court shall, at least three days before the hearing, inform the opposing party and its litigation agents and defense counsel of the expert conclusion and notify the People's Procuratorate;  
(10) For cases heard in public, announce the cause of action, defendant's name, hearing time and place in advance at least three days before the hearing;  
(11) Other preparatory work.  
The above activities shall be recorded in the record, signed by the judge and the court clerk.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  第二审人民法院开庭审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件，具有下列情形之一的，应当通知证人、鉴定人、被害人出庭作证：  
（一）人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人对鉴定结论有异议、鉴定程序违反规定或者鉴定结论明显存在疑点的；  
（二）人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人对证人证言、被害人陈述有异议，该证人证言或者被害人陈述对定罪量刑有重大影响的；  
（三）合议庭认为其他有必要出庭作证的。

**Article 13** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing on a death penalty appeal or protest case, in any of the following circumstances, it shall notify witnesses, expert witnesses, and victims to testify in court:  
(1) The People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his defense counsel raises objections to the expert conclusion, the expert evaluation procedure violates regulations, or the expert conclusion is obviously doubtful;  
(2) The People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his defense counsel raises objections to the witness testimony or victim statement, and such testimony or statement has a significant impact on conviction and sentencing;  
(3) The collegial panel considers that other circumstances require testimony in court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  第二审人民法院应当全面审理死刑上诉、抗诉案件。但在开庭时，可以根据具体情况围绕人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人提出争议的问题和人民法院认为需要重点审查的问题进行：  
（一）审判长宣布开庭后，可以宣读原审判决书，也可以只宣读案由、主要事实、证据和判决主文等判决书的主要内容。法庭调查时，上诉案件由上诉人或者辩护人先宣读上诉状或者陈述上诉理由，抗诉案件由检察人员先宣读抗诉书；对于既有上诉又有抗诉的案件，先由检察人员宣读抗诉书，后由上诉人或者辩护人宣读上诉状或者陈述上诉理由。  
（二）法庭调查的重点是，对原审判决提出异议的事实、证据以及提交的新的证据等。对于人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人没有异议的事实、证据和情节，可以不在庭审时调查。  
（三）人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人对原审判决采纳的证据没有异议的，可以不再举证和质证。  
（四）法庭辩论时，抗诉的案件，由检察人员先发言；上诉的案件，由上诉人、辩护人先发言；既有抗诉又有上诉的案件，由检察人员先发言，并依次进行辩论。  
（五）对共同犯罪中没有判处死刑且没有提出上诉的被告人，人民检察院和辩护人在开庭前表示不需要进行讯问和质证的，可以不再传唤到庭。对没有被判处死刑的其他被告人的罪行，事实清楚的，可以不在庭审时审理。  
（六）对被告人所犯数罪中判处其他刑罚的犯罪，事实清楚且人民检察院、被告人及其辩护人没有异议的，可以不在庭审时审理。

**Article 14** The second instance People's Court shall fully hear death penalty appeal and protest cases. However, during the hearing, it may, based on specific circumstances, focus on the issues disputed by the People's Procuratorate, the defendant, and his defense counsel, and the issues the court considers necessary to examine:  
(1) After the presiding judge announces the opening of the hearing, the original judgment may be read, or only the main contents such as cause of action, main facts, evidence, and ruling may be read. During court investigation, for appeal cases, the appellant or defense counsel shall first read the appeal petition or state appeal reasons; for protest cases, the prosecutor shall first read the protest; for cases with both appeal and protest, the prosecutor shall first read the protest, then the appellant or defense counsel shall read the appeal petition or state appeal reasons.  
(2) The focus of court investigation is on facts, evidence, and new evidence that challenge the original judgment. Facts, evidence, and circumstances not disputed by the People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his defense counsel may not be investigated during the hearing.  
(3) If the People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his defense counsel have no objection to the evidence adopted in the original judgment, presentation of evidence and cross-examination may be omitted.  
(4) During court debate, for protest cases, the prosecutor shall speak first; for appeal cases, the appellant and defense counsel shall speak first; for cases with both protest and appeal, the prosecutor shall speak first, followed by debate in sequence.  
(5) For co-defendants who were not sentenced to death and did not appeal, if the People's Procuratorate and defense counsel indicate before the hearing that interrogation and cross-examination are not needed, they may not be summoned to court. For the crimes of other co-defendants not sentenced to death, if the facts are clear, they may not be tried during the hearing.  
(6) For other punishments imposed on the defendant for multiple crimes, if the facts are clear and the People's Procuratorate, the defendant, or his defense counsel have no objection, they may not be tried during the hearing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  在第二审程序中，检察人员或者辩护人发现证据出现重大变化，可能影响案件定罪量刑的，可以建议延期审理。

**Article 15** During the second instance procedure, if a prosecutor or defense counsel discovers significant changes in evidence that may affect conviction and sentencing, they may suggest an adjournment of the hearing.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  第二审人民法院应当在裁判文书中写明人民检察院的意见、被告人的辩解和辩护人的意见，以及是否采纳的情况并说明理由。

**Article 16** The second instance People's Court shall state in the adjudication document the opinions of the People's Procuratorate, the defenses of the defendant, and the opinions of the defense counsel, as well as whether they were adopted and the reasons.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  第二审人民法院作出判决、裁定后，当庭宣判的，应当在五日以内将判决书或者裁定书送达当事人、辩护人和同级人民检察院；定期宣判的，应当在宣判后立即送达。  
第二审人民法院可以委托第一审人民法院代为宣判，并向当事人送达第二审判决书或者裁定书。

**Article 17** After the second instance People's Court renders a judgment or ruling, if the judgment is pronounced in court, it shall serve the judgment or ruling on the parties, the defense counsel, and the People's Procuratorate at the same level within five days; if the judgment is pronounced at a scheduled later date, it shall be served immediately after pronouncement.  
The second instance People's Court may entrust the first instance People's Court to pronounce the judgment on its behalf and serve the second instance judgment or ruling to the parties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  在第二审程序中，出席法庭的检察人员发现法庭审判活动违反法律规定的诉讼程序，休庭后由人民检察院向人民法院提出纠正意见。

**Article 18** During the second instance procedure, if a prosecutor attending court discovers that the court trial activity violates the procedural law, after the adjournment, the People's Procuratorate shall submit corrective opinions to the court.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  死刑第二审案件开庭审理程序的其他事项，依照刑事诉讼法、司法解释和有关规定执行。

**Article 19** Other matters concerning the hearing procedure for second instance death penalty cases shall be implemented in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law, judicial interpretations, and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  本规定自发布之日起施行。本规定发布前的司法解释和有关规定，与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. Any judicial interpretations and relevant provisions promulgated before these Provisions that contradict these Provisions shall be subject to these Provisions.
