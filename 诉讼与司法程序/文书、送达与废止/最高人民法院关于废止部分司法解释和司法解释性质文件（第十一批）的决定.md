关于废止部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件（第十一批）的决定  
  
法释〔2015〕2号  
  
为适应形势发展变化，保证国家法律统一正确适用，根据经第十一届全国人民代表大会第五次会议《关于修改中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法的决定》第二次修正的《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，结合审判工作实际，最高人民法院对单独发布的有关刑事诉讼的司法解释和司法解释性质文件进行了清理。现决定废止11件司法解释和司法解释性质文件。废止的司法解释和司法解释性质文件从本决定施行之日起不再适用，但过去依据或参照下列司法解释和司法解释性质文件对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。  
  
最高人民法院决定废止的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件目录（第十一批）

**Article 1** January 12, 2015  
Supreme People's Court  
Decision on Abolishing Certain Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature (Eleventh Batch)  
Fa Shi [2015] No. 2  
In order to adapt to the development and changes of the situation, ensure the unified and correct application of national laws, and pursuant to the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China as amended for the second time by the Decision on Amending the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China adopted at the Fifth Session of the Eleventh National People's Congress, and in light of the actual circumstances of adjudication work, the Supreme People's Court has conducted a review of the judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature concerning criminal actions that were separately issued. It is hereby decided to abolish 11 judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature. The abolished judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall no longer apply from the date of coming into force of this Decision, but the judgments and rulings made in the past on relevant cases pursuant to or with reference to the following judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall remain valid.  
Catalogue of Certain Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature to Be Abolished by the Supreme People's Court (Eleventh Batch)
