# 关于处理自首和立功若干具体问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：法发〔2010〕60号  
**公布日期**：2010-12-22

> （2010年12月22日发布 法发〔2010〕60号） 为规范司法实践中对自首和立功制度的运用，更好地贯彻落实宽严相济刑事政策，根据刑法、刑事诉讼法和最高人民法院《关于处理自首和立功具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（以下简称《解释》）等规定，对自首和立功若干具体问题提出如下处理意见： 一、关于“自动投案”的具体认定 《解释》第一条第（一）项规定七种应当视为自动投案的情形，体现了犯罪嫌疑人投案的主动性和自愿性。根据《解释》第一条第（一）项的规定，犯罪嫌疑人具有以下情形之一的，也应当视为自动投案：1.犯罪后主动报案，虽未表明自己是作案人，但没有逃离现场，在司法机关询问时交代自己罪行的；2.明知他人报案而在现场等待，抓捕时无拒捕行为，供认犯罪事实的；3.在司法机关未确定犯罪嫌疑人，尚在一般性排查询问时主动交代自己罪行的；4.因特定违法行为被采取劳动教养、行政拘留、司法拘留、强制隔离戒毒等行政、司法强制措施期间，主动向执行机关交代尚未被掌握的犯罪行为的；5.其他符合立法本意，应当视为自动投案的情形。 罪行未被有关部门、司法机关发觉，仅因形迹可疑被盘问、教育后，主动交代了犯罪事实的，应当视为自动投案，但有关部门、司法机关在其身上、随身携带的物品、驾乘的交通工具等处发现与犯罪有关的物品的，不能认定为自动投案。 交通肇事后保护现场、抢救伤者，并向公安机关报告的，应认定为自动投案，构成自首的，因上述行为同时系犯罪嫌疑人的法定义务，对其是否从宽、从宽幅度要适当从严掌握。交通肇事逃逸后自动投案，如实供述自己罪行的，应认定为自首，但应依法以较重法定刑为基准，视情决定对其是否从宽处罚以及从宽处罚的幅度。 犯罪嫌疑人被亲友采用捆绑等手段送到司法机关，或者在亲友带领侦查人员前来抓捕时无拒捕行为，并如实供认犯罪事实的，虽然不能认定为自动投案，但可以参照法律对自首的有关规定酌情从轻处罚。 二、关于“如实供述自己的罪行”的具体认定 《解释》第一条第（二）项规定如实供述自己的罪行，除供述自己的主要犯罪事实外，还应包括姓名、年龄、职业、住址、前科等情况。犯罪嫌疑人供述的身份等情况与真实情况虽有差别，但不影响定罪量刑的，应认定为如实供述自己的罪行。犯罪嫌疑人自动投案后隐瞒自己的真实身份等情况，影响对其定罪量刑的，不能认定为如实供述自己的罪行。 犯罪嫌疑人多次实施同种罪行的，应当综合考虑已交代的犯罪事实与未交代的犯罪事实的危害程度，决定是否认定为如实供述主要犯罪事实。虽然投案后没有交代全部犯罪事实，但如实交代的犯罪情节重于未交代的犯罪情节，或者如实交代的犯罪数额多于未交代的犯罪数额，一般应认定为如实供述自己的主要犯罪事实。无法区分已交代的与未交代的犯罪情节的严重程度，或者已交代的犯罪数额与未交代的犯罪数额相当，一般不认定为如实供述自己的主要犯罪事实。 犯罪嫌疑人自动投案时虽然没有交代自己的主要犯罪事实，但在司法机关掌握其主要犯罪事实之前主动交代的，应认定为如实供述自己的罪行。 三、关于“司法机关还未掌握的本人其他罪行”和“不同种罪行”的具体认定 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在被采取强制措施期间，向司法机关主动如实供述本人的其他罪行，该罪行能否认定为司法机关已掌握，应根据不同情形区别对待。如果该罪行已被通缉，一般应以该司法机关是否在通缉令发布范围内作出判断，不在通缉令发布范围内的，应认定为还未掌握，在通缉令发布范围内的，应视为已掌握；如果该罪行已录入全国公安信息网络在逃人员信息数据库，应视为已掌握。如果该罪行未被通缉、也未录入全国公安信息网络在逃人员信息数据库，应以该司法机关是否已实际掌握该罪行为标准。 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在被采取强制措施期间如实供述本人其他罪行，该罪行与司法机关已掌握的罪行属同种罪行还是不同种罪行，一般应以罪名区分。虽然如实供述的其他罪行的罪名与司法机关已掌握犯罪的罪名不同，但如实供述的其他犯罪与司法机关已掌握的犯罪属选择性罪名或者在法律、事实上密切关联，如因受贿被采取强制措施后，又交代因受贿为他人谋取利益行为，构成滥用职权罪的，应认定为同种罪行。 四、关于立功线索来源的具体认定

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、关于“自动投案”的具体认定  
《解释》第一条第（一）项规定七种应当视为自动投案的情形，体现了犯罪嫌疑人投案的主动性和自愿性。根据《解释》第一条第（一）项的规定，犯罪嫌疑人具有以下情形之一的，也应当视为自动投案：1.犯罪后主动报案，虽未表明自己是作案人，但没有逃离现场，在司法机关询问时交代自己罪行的；2.明知他人报案而在现场等待，抓捕时无拒捕行为，供认犯罪事实的；3.在司法机关未确定犯罪嫌疑人，尚在一般性排查询问时主动交代自己罪行的；4.因特定违法行为被采取劳动教养、行政拘留、司法拘留、强制隔离戒毒等行政、司法强制措施期间，主动向执行机关交代尚未被掌握的犯罪行为的；5.其他符合立法本意，应当视为自动投案的情形。  
罪行未被有关部门、司法机关发觉，仅因形迹可疑被盘问、教育后，主动交代了犯罪事实的，应当视为自动投案，但有关部门、司法机关在其身上、随身携带的物品、驾乘的交通工具等处发现与犯罪有关的物品的，不能认定为自动投案。  
交通肇事后保护现场、抢救伤者，并向公安机关报告的，应认定为自动投案，构成自首的，因上述行为同时系犯罪嫌疑人的法定义务，对其是否从宽、从宽幅度要适当从严掌握。交通肇事逃逸后自动投案，如实供述自己罪行的，应认定为自首，但应依法以较重法定刑为基准，视情决定对其是否从宽处罚以及从宽处罚的幅度。  
犯罪嫌疑人被亲友采用捆绑等手段送到司法机关，或者在亲友带领侦查人员前来抓捕时无拒捕行为，并如实供认犯罪事实的，虽然不能认定为自动投案，但可以参照法律对自首的有关规定酌情从轻处罚。

**Article 1** I. Specific Determination of "Voluntary Surrender"  
Paragraph (1) of Article 1 of the Interpretation lists seven circumstances that shall be deemed as voluntary surrender, reflecting the initiative and voluntariness of the criminal suspect's surrender. According to Paragraph (1) of Article 1 of the Interpretation, a criminal suspect shall also be deemed to have voluntarily surrendered under any of the following circumstances: 1. After committing a crime, he/she voluntarily reports the case, and although not identifying himself/herself as the perpetrator, does not flee the scene and confesses his/her crime when questioned by the judicial authority; 2. Knowing that another person has reported the case, he/she waits at the scene, and when arrested, does not resist arrest and confesses the criminal facts; 3. Before the judicial authority has identified the criminal suspect and while still conducting general screening inquiries, he/she voluntarily confesses his/her crime; 4. During the period when administrative or judicial compulsory measures such as re-education through labor, administrative detention, judicial detention, or compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation are imposed for specific illegal acts, he/she voluntarily confesses to the executing authority any criminal act not yet discovered; 5. Other circumstances that, in line with the legislative intent, shall be deemed as voluntary surrender.  
Where a crime is not yet discovered by the relevant department or judicial authority, and the person voluntarily confesses the criminal facts after being questioned and educated solely because of suspicious behavior, it shall be deemed as voluntary surrender. However, if the relevant department or judicial authority finds articles related to the crime on the person, in the items he/she carries, or in the vehicle he/she drives or rides, such act shall not be deemed as voluntary surrender.  
After a traffic accident causing injuries, protecting the scene, rescuing the injured, and reporting to the public security organ shall be deemed as voluntary surrender. If it constitutes voluntary surrender, because the above acts are also the legal obligations of the criminal suspect, the determination of whether to grant leniency and the extent of leniency shall be strictly controlled. Where the person voluntarily surrenders after fleeing from a traffic accident and truthfully confesses his/her crime, it shall be deemed as voluntary surrender, but a heavier statutory penalty shall be taken as the benchmark according to law, and the decision on whether to grant lenient punishment and the extent thereof shall be made based on the circumstances.  
Where a criminal suspect is brought to the judicial authority by relatives or friends through means such as binding, or does not resist arrest when relatives or friends lead investigators to arrest him/her and truthfully confesses the criminal facts, although it cannot be deemed as voluntary surrender, a lighter punishment may be imposed discretionarily by reference to the legal provisions on voluntary surrender.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、关于“如实供述自己的罪行”的具体认定  
《解释》第一条第（二）项规定如实供述自己的罪行，除供述自己的主要犯罪事实外，还应包括姓名、年龄、职业、住址、前科等情况。犯罪嫌疑人供述的身份等情况与真实情况虽有差别，但不影响定罪量刑的，应认定为如实供述自己的罪行。犯罪嫌疑人自动投案后隐瞒自己的真实身份等情况，影响对其定罪量刑的，不能认定为如实供述自己的罪行。  
犯罪嫌疑人多次实施同种罪行的，应当综合考虑已交代的犯罪事实与未交代的犯罪事实的危害程度，决定是否认定为如实供述主要犯罪事实。虽然投案后没有交代全部犯罪事实，但如实交代的犯罪情节重于未交代的犯罪情节，或者如实交代的犯罪数额多于未交代的犯罪数额，一般应认定为如实供述自己的主要犯罪事实。无法区分已交代的与未交代的犯罪情节的严重程度，或者已交代的犯罪数额与未交代的犯罪数额相当，一般不认定为如实供述自己的主要犯罪事实。  
犯罪嫌疑人自动投案时虽然没有交代自己的主要犯罪事实，但在司法机关掌握其主要犯罪事实之前主动交代的，应认定为如实供述自己的罪行。

**Article 2** II. Specific Determination of "Truthfully Confessing One's Own Crime"  
Paragraph (2) of Article 1 of the Interpretation provides that confessing one's own crime truthfully shall, in addition to confessing the main criminal facts, also include information such as name, age, occupation, address, and criminal record. Where the identity information confessed by the criminal suspect differs from the true situation but does not affect the determination of guilt or the imposition of punishment, it shall be deemed as truthfully confessing one's own crime. Where a criminal suspect, after voluntary surrender, conceals his/her true identity or other information, thereby affecting the determination of guilt or punishment, it shall not be deemed as truthfully confessing one's own crime.  
Where a criminal suspect has committed the same type of crime multiple times, the harmfulness of the confessed criminal facts and the unconfessed criminal facts shall be comprehensively considered in deciding whether to deem it as truthfully confessing the main criminal facts. Although the person does not confess all criminal facts after surrender, if the criminal circumstances truthfully confessed are more serious than those not confessed, or the amount of crime truthfully confessed is larger than that not confessed, it shall generally be deemed as truthfully confessing the main criminal facts. Where it is impossible to distinguish the severity of the confessed criminal circumstances from the unconfessed ones, or the confessed criminal amount is equivalent to the unconfessed amount, it shall generally not be deemed as truthfully confessing the main criminal facts.  
Where a criminal suspect does not confess his/her main criminal facts at the time of voluntary surrender but voluntarily confesses them before the judicial authority has mastered the main criminal facts, it shall be deemed as truthfully confessing one's own crime.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、关于“司法机关还未掌握的本人其他罪行”和“不同种罪行”的具体认定  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在被采取强制措施期间，向司法机关主动如实供述本人的其他罪行，该罪行能否认定为司法机关已掌握，应根据不同情形区别对待。如果该罪行已被通缉，一般应以该司法机关是否在通缉令发布范围内作出判断，不在通缉令发布范围内的，应认定为还未掌握，在通缉令发布范围内的，应视为已掌握；如果该罪行已录入全国公安信息网络在逃人员信息数据库，应视为已掌握。如果该罪行未被通缉、也未录入全国公安信息网络在逃人员信息数据库，应以该司法机关是否已实际掌握该罪行为标准。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在被采取强制措施期间如实供述本人其他罪行，该罪行与司法机关已掌握的罪行属同种罪行还是不同种罪行，一般应以罪名区分。虽然如实供述的其他罪行的罪名与司法机关已掌握犯罪的罪名不同，但如实供述的其他犯罪与司法机关已掌握的犯罪属选择性罪名或者在法律、事实上密切关联，如因受贿被采取强制措施后，又交代因受贿为他人谋取利益行为，构成滥用职权罪的，应认定为同种罪行。

**Article 3** III. Regarding the Specific Determination of "Other Crimes of the Person Not Yet Known to the Judicial Authority" and "Different Types of Crimes"  
When a criminal suspect or defendant, during the period when compulsory measures are being taken against them, voluntarily and truthfully confesses their other crimes to the judicial authority, whether such crimes shall be deemed as already known to the judicial authority shall be determined on a case-by-case basis based on different circumstances. If the crime has been wanted, judgment shall generally be made based on whether the judicial authority falls within the scope of the wanted notice; if it does not fall within the scope of the wanted notice, it shall be deemed as not yet known; if it falls within the scope of the wanted notice, it shall be deemed as already known. If the crime has been entered into the national public security information network's fugitive information database, it shall be deemed as already known. If the crime has not been wanted and has not been entered into the national public security information network's fugitive information database, the standard shall be whether the judicial authority has actually known of the crime.  
When a criminal suspect or defendant, during the period when compulsory measures are being taken against them, truthfully confesses their other crimes, whether such crimes belong to the same type of crime as the crime already known by the judicial authority or a different type of crime shall generally be distinguished by the name of the crime. If the name of the other crime truthfully confessed is different from the name of the crime already known by the judicial authority, but the other crime truthfully confessed and the crime already known by the judicial authority are alternative charges or are closely related in law or fact, such as when a person, after being subjected to compulsory measures for accepting bribes, further confesses the act of seeking benefits for others through bribery, thereby constituting the crime of abuse of power, it shall be deemed as the same type of crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、关于立功线索来源的具体认定  
犯罪分子通过贿买、暴力、胁迫等非法手段，或者被羁押后与律师、亲友会见过程中违反监管规定，获取他人犯罪线索并“检举揭发”的，不能认定为有立功表现。  
犯罪分子将本人以往查办犯罪职务活动中掌握的，或者从负有查办犯罪、监管职责的国家工作人员处获取的他人犯罪线索予以检举揭发的，不能认定为有立功表现。  
犯罪分子亲友为使犯罪分子“立功”，向司法机关提供他人犯罪线索、协助抓捕犯罪嫌疑人的，不能认定为犯罪分子有立功表现。

**Article 4** IV. Specific Determination of the Source of Meritorious Service Clues  
Where a criminal obtains clues about others' crimes through illegal means such as bribery, violence, or coercion, or through violation of supervision regulations during meetings with lawyers, relatives, or friends while in custody, and then "reports and exposes" such clues, such act shall not be deemed as meritorious service.  
Where a criminal reports and exposes clues about others' crimes that he/she has obtained in the course of previous official duties investigating crimes, or obtained from state personnel responsible for investigating crimes or supervision duties, such act shall not be deemed as meritorious service.  
Where relatives or friends of a criminal provide clues about others' crimes to the judicial authority or assist in arresting criminal suspects for the purpose of enabling the criminal to "perform meritorious service", such act shall not be deemed as meritorious service by the criminal.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、关于“协助抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人”的具体认定  
犯罪分子具有下列行为之一，使司法机关抓获其他犯罪嫌疑人的，属于《解释》第五条规定的“协助司法机关抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人”：1.按照司法机关的安排，以打电话、发信息等方式将其他犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）约至指定地点的；2.按照司法机关的安排，当场指认、辨认其他犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）的；3.带领侦查人员抓获其他犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）的；4.提供司法机关尚未掌握的其他案件犯罪嫌疑人的联络方式、藏匿地址的，等等。  
犯罪分子提供同案犯姓名、住址、体貌特征等基本情况，或者提供犯罪前、犯罪中掌握、使用的同案犯联络方式、藏匿地址，司法机关据此抓捕同案犯的，不能认定为协助司法机关抓捕同案犯。

**Article 5** V. Specific Determination of "Assisting in the Arrest of Other Criminal Suspects"  
Where a criminal engages in any of the following acts, thereby enabling the judicial authority to arrest other criminal suspects, such act falls within the scope of "assisting the judicial authority in arresting other criminal suspects" as provided in Article 5 of the Interpretation: 1. Under the arrangement of the judicial authority, luring other criminal suspects (including accomplices) to a designated location by telephone, message, or other means; 2. Under the arrangement of the judicial authority, pointing out or identifying other criminal suspects (including accomplices) on the spot; 3. Leading investigators to arrest other criminal suspects (including accomplices); 4. Providing contact information or hiding places of criminal suspects in other cases that are not yet known to the judicial authority, etc.  
Where a criminal provides basic information such as the name, address, or physical characteristics of an accomplice, or provides contact information or hiding places of an accomplice that he/she knew or used before or during the crime, and the judicial authority arrests the accomplice based on such information, such act shall not be deemed as assisting the judicial authority in arresting the accomplice.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、关于立功线索的查证程序和具体认定  
被告人在一、二审审理期间检举揭发他人犯罪行为或者提供侦破其他案件的重要线索，人民法院经审查认为该线索内容具体、指向明确的，应及时移交有关人民检察院或者公安机关依法处理。  
侦查机关出具材料，表明在三个月内还不能查证并抓获被检举揭发的人，或者不能查实的，人民法院审理案件可不再等待查证结果。  
被告人检举揭发他人犯罪行为或者提供侦破其他案件的重要线索经查证不属实，又重复提供同一线索，且没有提出新的证据材料的，可以不再查证。  
根据被告人检举揭发破获的他人犯罪案件，如果已有审判结果，应当依据判决确认的事实认定是否查证属实；如果被检举揭发的他人犯罪案件尚未进入审判程序，可以依据侦查机关提供的书面查证情况认定是否查证属实。检举揭发的线索经查确有犯罪发生，或者确定了犯罪嫌疑人，可能构成重大立功，只是未能将犯罪嫌疑人抓获归案的，对可能判处死刑的被告人一般要留有余地，对其他被告人原则上应酌情从轻处罚。  
被告人检举揭发或者协助抓获的人的行为构成犯罪，但因法定事由不追究刑事责任、不起诉、终止审理的，不影响对被告人立功表现的认定；被告人检举揭发或者协助抓获的人的行为应判处无期徒刑以上刑罚，但因具有法定、酌定从宽情节，宣告刑为有期徒刑或者更轻刑罚的，不影响对被告人重大立功表现的认定。

**Article 6** VI. Verification Procedures and Specific Determination of Meritorious Service Clues  
Where a defendant reports and exposes another person's criminal act or provides important clues for solving other cases during the first-instance or second-instance trial, and the People's Court, upon examination, finds that such clues are specific and clearly directed, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the relevant People's Procuratorate or public security organ for handling according to law.  
Where the investigative authority issues materials indicating that within three months it is still unable to verify and arrest the reported person, or cannot ascertain the facts, the People's Court may, when adjudicating the case, no longer wait for the verification result.  
Where a defendant reports and exposes another person's criminal act or provides important clues for solving other cases, which are verified to be untrue, and then repeatedly provides the same clues without presenting new evidentiary materials, such clues may no longer be verified.  
Where another person's criminal case is solved based on the defendant's report and exposure, if there is already an adjudication result, whether the clues are verified as true shall be determined based on the facts confirmed by the judgment; if the reported other person's criminal case has not yet entered the trial procedure, whether the clues are verified as true may be determined based on the written verification materials provided by the investigative authority. Where the reported clues are verified to have indeed caused a crime to occur, or have identified a criminal suspect, and may constitute major meritorious service, but the criminal suspect has not yet been arrested, for a defendant who may be sentenced to death, discretion shall generally be reserved; for other defendants, a lighter punishment shall in principle be imposed discretionarily.  
Where the act of the person reported or assisted in arrest by the defendant constitutes a crime, but criminal liability is not investigated, prosecution is not initiated, or the case is terminated due to statutory reasons, this shall not affect the determination of the defendant's meritorious service; where the act of the person reported or assisted in arrest by the defendant shall be punishable by life imprisonment or higher, but due to statutory or discretionary leniency circumstances, the pronounced sentence is fixed-term imprisonment or a lighter penalty, this shall not affect the determination of the defendant's major meritorious service.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、关于自首、立功证据材料的审查  
人民法院审查的自首证据材料，应当包括被告人投案经过、有罪供述以及能够证明其投案情况的其他材料。投案经过的内容一般应包括被告人投案时间、地点、方式等。证据材料应加盖接受被告人投案的单位的印章，并有接受人员签名。  
人民法院审查的立功证据材料，一般应包括被告人检举揭发材料及证明其来源的材料、司法机关的调查核实材料、被检举揭发人的供述等。被检举揭发案件已立案、侦破，被检举揭发人被采取强制措施、公诉或者审判的，还应审查相关的法律文书。证据材料应加盖接收被告人检举揭发材料的单位的印章，并有接收人员签名。  
人民法院经审查认为证明被告人自首、立功的材料不规范、不全面的，应当由检察机关、侦查机关予以完善或者提供补充材料。  
上述证据材料在被告人被指控的犯罪一、二审审理时已形成的，应当经庭审质证。

**Article 7** VII. Review of Evidence Materials for Voluntary Surrender and Meritorious Service  
The evidence materials for voluntary surrender reviewed by the People's Court shall include the record of the defendant's surrender, confession of guilt, and other materials that can prove the circumstances of the surrender. The record of surrender shall generally include the time, place, and manner of the defendant's surrender. The evidence materials shall bear the seal of the unit that accepted the defendant's surrender and be signed by the person accepting the surrender.  
The evidence materials for meritorious service reviewed by the People's Court shall generally include the materials of the defendant's report and exposure and materials proving their source, the investigation and verification materials of the judicial authority, and the confession of the reported person, etc. If the reported case has been filed for investigation, solved, and the reported person has been subjected to compulsory measures, prosecuted, or tried, the relevant legal documents shall also be reviewed. The evidence materials shall bear the seal of the unit that received the defendant's report and exposure materials and be signed by the person receiving them.  
Where the People's Court, upon review, finds that the materials proving the defendant's voluntary surrender or meritorious service are not standard or complete, the procuratorial authority or the investigative authority shall improve or provide supplementary materials.  
The above evidence materials that have already been formed during the first-instance or second-instance trial of the crime of which the defendant is accused shall be subject to cross-examination in court.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、关于对自首、立功的被告人的处罚  
对具有自首、立功情节的被告人是否从宽处罚、从宽处罚的幅度，应当考虑其犯罪事实、犯罪性质、犯罪情节、危害后果、社会影响、被告人的主观恶性和人身危险性等。自首的还应考虑投案的主动性、供述的及时性和稳定性等。立功的还应考虑检举揭发罪行的轻重、被检举揭发的人可能或者已经被判处的刑罚、提供的线索对侦破案件或者协助抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人所起作用的大小等。  
具有自首或者立功情节的，一般应依法从轻、减轻处罚；犯罪情节较轻的，可以免除处罚。类似情况下，对具有自首情节的被告人的从宽幅度要适当宽于具有立功情节的被告人。  
虽然具有自首或者立功情节，但犯罪情节特别恶劣、犯罪后果特别严重、被告人主观恶性深、人身危险性大，或者在犯罪前即为规避法律、逃避处罚而准备自首、立功的，可以不从宽处罚。  
对于被告人具有自首、立功情节，同时又有累犯、毒品再犯等法定从重处罚情节的，既要考虑自首、立功的具体情节，又要考虑被告人的主观恶性、人身危险性等因素，综合分析判断，确定从宽或者从严处罚。累犯的前罪为非暴力犯罪的，一般可以从宽处罚，前罪为暴力犯罪或者前、后罪为同类犯罪的，可以不从宽处罚。  
在共同犯罪案件中，对具有自首、立功情节的被告人的处罚，应注意共同犯罪人以及首要分子、主犯、从犯之间的量刑平衡。犯罪集团的首要分子、共同犯罪的主犯检举揭发或者协助司法机关抓捕同案地位、作用较次的犯罪分子的，从宽处罚与否应当从严掌握，如果从轻处罚可能导致全案量刑失衡的，一般不从轻处罚；如果检举揭发或者协助司法机关抓捕的是其他案件中罪行同样严重的犯罪分子，一般应依法从宽处罚。对于犯罪集团的一般成员、共同犯罪的从犯立功的，特别是协助抓捕首要分子、主犯的，应当充分体现政策，依法从宽处罚。

**Article 8** VIII. Punishment of Defendants with Voluntary Surrender or Meritorious Service  
Whether to grant lenient punishment to a defendant who has the circumstance of voluntary surrender or meritorious service, and the extent of such leniency, shall be determined by considering factors such as the facts of the crime, the nature of the crime, the circumstances of the crime, the harmful consequences, the social impact, the subjective malignancy of the defendant, and the danger posed by the defendant to society. For voluntary surrender, the initiative of the surrender and the timeliness and stability of the confession shall also be considered. For meritorious service, the seriousness of the reported crime, the possible or already imposed punishment on the reported person, and the extent to which the provided clues contributed to solving the case or assisting in arresting other criminal suspects shall also be considered.  
Where a defendant has the circumstance of voluntary surrender or meritorious service, a lighter or mitigated punishment shall generally be imposed according to law; where the circumstances of the crime are relatively minor, the punishment may be exempted. Under similar circumstances, the extent of leniency for a defendant with voluntary surrender shall be appropriately broader than that for a defendant with meritorious service.  
Where a defendant has the circumstance of voluntary surrender or meritorious service, but the circumstances of the crime are particularly heinous, the consequences of the crime are particularly serious, the defendant has deep subjective malignancy, poses a high danger to society, or before committing the crime had prepared voluntary surrender or meritorious service to circumvent the law or avoid punishment, lenient punishment may not be granted.  
For a defendant who has both voluntary surrender or meritorious service and statutory circumstances for severe punishment such as recidivism or drug-related recidivism, both the specific circumstances of voluntary surrender or meritorious service and the factors such as the defendant's subjective malignancy and danger to society shall be considered, and a comprehensive analysis shall be conducted to determine whether to impose lenient or severe punishment. If the previous crime for recidivism is a non-violent crime, lenient punishment may generally be granted; if the previous crime is a violent crime or the previous and subsequent crimes are of the same type, lenient punishment may not be granted.  
In joint crime cases, when punishing a defendant with voluntary surrender or meritorious service, attention shall be paid to the sentencing balance among the joint offenders, including the principal culprit, principal offender, and accessory. Where the principal culprit of a criminal group or the principal offender of a joint crime reports and exposes or assists the judicial authority in arresting an accomplice of lesser status and role, the decision on whether to grant lenient punishment shall be strictly controlled; if a lighter punishment may lead to an imbalance in sentencing for the whole case, a lighter punishment shall generally not be imposed; if the person reported or assisted in arrest is a criminal in another case whose crime is equally serious, lenient punishment shall generally be granted according to law. For ordinary members of a criminal group or accessories in a joint crime who perform meritorious service, especially by assisting in arresting the principal culprit or principal offender, the policy shall be fully reflected, and lenient punishment shall be granted according to law.
