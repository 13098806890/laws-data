# 最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托提取证据的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕4号  
**公布日期**：2016-12-29

> 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区经协商，达成《关于内地与香港特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托提取证据的安排》（以下简称《安排》），并于2016年12月29日签署。本《安排》已于2016年10月31日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1697次会议通过，现予公布。根据双方一致意见，本《安排》自2017年3月1日起生效。 2017年2月27日 最高人民法院 关于内地与香港特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托提取证据的安排 法释〔2017〕4号 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区经协商，就民商事案件相互委托提取证据问题作出如下安排： 第一条　内地人民法院与香港特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托提取证据，适用本安排。 第二条　双方相互委托提取证据，须通过各自指定的联络机关进行。其中，内地指定各高级人民法院为联络机关；香港特别行政区指定香港特别行政区政府政务司司长办公室辖下行政署为联络机关。 最高人民法院可以直接通过香港特别行政区指定的联络机关委托提取证据。 第三条　受委托方的联络机关收到对方的委托书后，应当及时将委托书及所附相关材料转送相关法院或者其他机关办理，或者自行办理。 如果受委托方认为委托材料不符合本辖区相关法律规定，影响其完成受托事项，应当及时通知委托方修改、补充。委托方应当按照受委托方的要求予以修改、补充，或者重新出具委托书。 如果受委托方认为受托事项不属于本安排规定的委托事项范围，可以予以退回并说明原因。 第四条　委托书及所附相关材料应当以中文文本提出。没有中文文本的，应当提供中文译本。 第五条　委托方获得的证据材料只能用于委托书所述的相关诉讼。 第六条　内地人民法院根据本安排委托香港特别行政区法院提取证据的，请求协助的范围包括：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　内地人民法院与香港特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托提取证据，适用本安排。

**Article 1** For mutual requests between the People's Courts of the Mainland and the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for the taking of evidence in civil and commercial cases, this Arrangement shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　双方相互委托提取证据，须通过各自指定的联络机关进行。其中，内地指定各高级人民法院为联络机关；香港特别行政区指定香港特别行政区政府政务司司长办公室辖下行政署为联络机关。  
最高人民法院可以直接通过香港特别行政区指定的联络机关委托提取证据。

**Article 2** The two sides shall make mutual requests for the taking of evidence through their respective designated liaison authorities. The Mainland designates the Higher People's Courts as the liaison authorities; the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region designates the Administration Wing, under the Office of the Chief Secretary for Administration of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region as the liaison authority.  
The Supreme People's Court may directly make a request for the taking of evidence through the liaison authority designated by the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　受委托方的联络机关收到对方的委托书后，应当及时将委托书及所附相关材料转送相关法院或者其他机关办理，或者自行办理。  
如果受委托方认为委托材料不符合本辖区相关法律规定，影响其完成受托事项，应当及时通知委托方修改、补充。委托方应当按照受委托方的要求予以修改、补充，或者重新出具委托书。  
如果受委托方认为受托事项不属于本安排规定的委托事项范围，可以予以退回并说明原因。

**Article 3** After receiving the letter of request from the other party, the liaison authority of the requested party shall promptly forward the letter of request and the attached relevant materials to the relevant court or other authorities for handling, or handle the matter itself.  
If the requested party considers that the requested materials do not comply with the relevant legal provisions of its jurisdiction and affect its completion of the requested matter, it shall promptly notify the requesting party to make modifications or supplements. The requesting party shall make modifications or supplements in accordance with the requirements of the requested party, or issue a new letter of request.  
If the requested party considers that the requested matter is not within the scope of matters for request as provided for in this Arrangement, it may return the request and state the reasons.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　委托书及所附相关材料应当以中文文本提出。没有中文文本的，应当提供中文译本。

**Article 4** The letter of request and the attached relevant materials shall be submitted in the Chinese language. Where there is no Chinese version, a Chinese translation shall be provided.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　委托方获得的证据材料只能用于委托书所述的相关诉讼。

**Article 5** The evidence materials obtained by the requesting party may only be used for the relevant litigation stated in the letter of request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　内地人民法院根据本安排委托香港特别行政区法院提取证据的，请求协助的范围包括：  
（一）讯问证人；  
（二）取得文件；  
（三）检查、拍摄、保存、保管或扣留财产；  
（四）取得财产样品或对财产进行试验；  
（五）对人进行身体检验。  
香港特别行政区法院根据本安排委托内地人民法院提取证据的，请求协助的范围包括：  
（一）取得当事人的陈述及证人证言；  
（二）提供书证、物证、视听资料及电子数据；  
（三）勘验、鉴定。

**Article 6** Where a People's Court of the Mainland requests a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to take evidence under this Arrangement, the scope of assistance requested includes:  
(1) examining witnesses;  
(2) obtaining documents;  
(3) inspecting, photographing, preserving, keeping or detaining property;  
(4) obtaining samples of property or conducting experiments on property;  
(5) conducting physical examinations on persons.  
Where a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region requests a People's Court of the Mainland to take evidence under this Arrangement, the scope of assistance requested includes:  
(1) obtaining statements of parties and witness testimony;  
(2) providing documentary evidence, physical evidence, audio-visual materials and electronic data;  
(3) inspection and expert evaluation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　受委托方应当根据本辖区法律规定安排取证。  
委托方请求按照特殊方式提取证据的，如果受委托方认为不违反本辖区的法律规定，可以按照委托方请求的方式执行。  
如果委托方请求其司法人员、有关当事人及其诉讼代理人（法律代表）在受委托方取证时到场，以及参与录取证言的程序，受委托方可以按照其辖区内相关法律规定予以考虑批准。批准同意的，受委托方应当将取证时间、地点通知委托方联络机关。

**Article 7** The requested party shall arrange for the taking of evidence in accordance with the legal provisions of its jurisdiction.  
If the requesting party requests the taking of evidence in a special manner, and if the requested party considers that it does not violate the legal provisions of its jurisdiction, it may execute the request in the manner requested by the requesting party.  
If the requesting party requests that its judicial personnel, relevant parties and their litigation agents (legal representatives) be present when the requested party takes evidence, and participate in the process of taking testimony, the requested party may consider and approve the request in accordance with the relevant legal provisions of its jurisdiction. If approval is granted, the requested party shall notify the liaison authority of the requesting party of the time and place of taking evidence.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　内地人民法院委托香港特别行政区法院提取证据，应当提供加盖最高人民法院或者高级人民法院印章的委托书。香港特别行政区法院委托内地人民法院提取证据，应当提供加盖香港特别行政区高等法院印章的委托书。  
委托书或者所附相关材料应当写明：  
（一）出具委托书的法院名称和审理相关案件的法院名称；  
（二）与委托事项有关的当事人或者证人的姓名或者名称、地址及其他一切有助于联络及辨别其身份的信息；  
（三）要求提供的协助详情，包括但不限于：与委托事项有关的案件基本情况（包括案情摘要、涉及诉讼的性质及正在进行的审理程序等）；需向当事人或者证人取得的指明文件、物品及询（讯）问的事项或问题清单；需要委托提取有关证据的原因等；必要时，需陈明有关证据对诉讼的重要性及用来证实的事实及论点等；  
（四）是否需要采用特殊方式提取证据以及具体要求；  
（五）委托方的联络人及其联络信息；  
（六）有助执行委托事项的其他一切信息。

**Article 8** When a People's Court of the Mainland requests a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to take evidence, it shall provide a letter of request bearing the seal of the Supreme People's Court or the Higher People's Court. When a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region requests a People's Court of the Mainland to take evidence, it shall provide a letter of request bearing the seal of the High Court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
The letter of request or the attached relevant materials shall state:  
(1) the name of the court issuing the letter of request and the name of the court hearing the relevant case;  
(2) the name or designation, address and any other information that assists in contacting and identifying the parties or witnesses concerned in the requested matter;  
(3) details of the assistance requested, including but not limited to: basic information of the case relevant to the requested matter (including a summary of the case, the nature of the litigation involved and the ongoing proceedings, etc.); specific documents, objects and matters or lists of questions to be obtained from the parties or witnesses; the reasons why the relevant evidence is requested to be taken; where necessary, the importance of the evidence to the litigation and the facts and arguments to be proved, etc.;  
(4) whether special methods are required for taking evidence and the specific requirements;  
(5) the contact person of the requesting party and their contact information;  
(6) any other information that facilitates the execution of the requested matter.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　受委托方因执行受托事项产生的一般性开支，由受委托方承担。  
受委托方因执行受托事项产生的翻译费用、专家费用、鉴定费用、应委托方要求的特殊方式取证所产生的额外费用等非一般性开支，由委托方承担。  
如果受委托方认为执行受托事项或会引起非一般性开支，应先与委托方协商，以决定是否继续执行受托事项。

**Article 9** General expenses incurred by the requested party in executing the requested matter shall be borne by the requested party.  
Non-general expenses incurred by the requested party in executing the requested matter, such as translation fees, expert fees, appraisal fees, and additional expenses incurred from taking evidence in a special manner as requested by the requesting party, shall be borne by the requesting party.  
If the requested party considers that executing the requested matter may give rise to non-general expenses, it shall first consult with the requesting party to decide whether to continue executing the requested matter.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　受委托方应当尽量自收到委托书之日起六个月内完成受托事项。受委托方完成受托事项后，应当及时书面回复委托方。  
如果受委托方未能按委托方的请求完成受托事项，或者只能部分完成受托事项，应当向委托方书面说明原因，并按委托方指示及时退回委托书所附全部或者部分材料。  
如果证人根据受委托方的法律规定，拒绝提供证言时，受委托方应当以书面通知委托方，并按委托方指示退回委托书所附全部材料。

**Article 10** The requested party shall use its best efforts to complete the requested matter within six months from the date of receipt of the letter of request. After completing the requested matter, the requested party shall promptly reply in writing to the requesting party.  
If the requested party fails to complete the requested matter in accordance with the request of the requesting party, or can only partially complete the requested matter, it shall explain the reasons in writing to the requesting party, and promptly return all or part of the materials attached to the letter of request in accordance with the instructions of the requesting party.  
If a witness refuses to give testimony in accordance with the legal provisions of the requested party, the requested party shall notify the requesting party in writing, and return all materials attached to the letter of request in accordance with the instructions of the requesting party.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本安排在执行过程中遇有问题，或者本安排需要修改，应当通过最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府协商解决。

**Article 11** If any problem arises during the implementation of this Arrangement, or if this Arrangement needs to be amended, it shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本安排在内地由最高人民法院发布司法解释和香港特别行政区完成有关内部程序后，由双方公布生效日期。  
本安排适用于受委托方在本安排生效后收到的委托事项，但不影响双方根据现行法律考虑及执行在本安排生效前收到的委托事项。

**Article 12** This Arrangement shall, after the Supreme People's Court issues a judicial interpretation in the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region completes the relevant internal procedures, have its effective date announced by both sides.  
This Arrangement shall apply to requests received by the requested party after this Arrangement takes effect, but shall not affect the consideration and execution by the two sides under current laws of requests received before this Arrangement takes effect.
