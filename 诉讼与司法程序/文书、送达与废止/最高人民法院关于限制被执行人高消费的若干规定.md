# 最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕8号  
**公布日期**：2010-05-17  
**生效日期**：2010-10-01

> 最高人民法院《关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》已于2010年5月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1487次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年10月1日起施行。 2010年7月1日 最高人民法院 关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定 法释〔2010〕8号 为进一步加大执行力度，推动社会信用机制建设，最大限度保护申请执行人和被执行人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，结合人民法院民事执行工作的实践经验，制定本规定。 第一条  被执行人未按执行通知书指定的期间履行生效法律文书确定的给付义务的，人民法院可以限制其高消费。 第二条  人民法院决定采取限制高消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。 第三条  被执行人为自然人的，被限制高消费后，不得有以下以其财产支付费用的行为： （一）乘坐交通工具时，选择飞机、列车软卧、轮船二等以上舱位； （二）在星级以上宾馆、酒店、夜总会、高尔夫球场等场所进行高消费； （三）购买不动产或者新建、扩建、高档装修房屋； （四）租赁高档写字楼、宾馆、公寓等场所办公； （五）购买非经营必需车辆； （六）旅游、度假；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  被执行人未按执行通知书指定的期间履行生效法律文书确定的给付义务的，人民法院可以限制其高消费。

**Article 1** If the person subject to enforcement fails to perform the obligation of payment as determined by a legally effective legal document within the period specified in the enforcement notice, the People's Court may restrict his high consumption.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  人民法院决定采取限制高消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。

**Article 2** When the People's Court decides to adopt the measure of restricting high consumption, it shall consider factors such as whether the person subject to enforcement has acts of passive performance, evasion of enforcement, or resistance to enforcement, as well as the enforcement capacity of the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  被执行人为自然人的，被限制高消费后，不得有以下以其财产支付费用的行为：  
（一）乘坐交通工具时，选择飞机、列车软卧、轮船二等以上舱位；  
（二）在星级以上宾馆、酒店、夜总会、高尔夫球场等场所进行高消费；  
（三）购买不动产或者新建、扩建、高档装修房屋；  
（四）租赁高档写字楼、宾馆、公寓等场所办公；  
（五）购买非经营必需车辆；  
（六）旅游、度假；  
（七）子女就读高收费私立学校；  
（八）支付高额保费购买保险理财产品；  
（九）其他非生活和工作必需的高消费行为。  
被执行人为单位的，被限制高消费后，禁止被执行人及其法定代表人、主要负责人、影响债务履行的直接责任人员以单位财产实施本条第一款规定的行为。

**Article 3** If the person subject to enforcement is a natural person, after being restricted from high consumption, he shall not engage in the following acts of paying expenses with his property:  
(1) choosing first class or above on aircraft, soft sleeper on trains, second class or above on ships when taking transportation;  
(2) engaging in high consumption at places such as star-rated hotels, nightclubs, golf courses, etc.;  
(3) purchasing real estate, or newly building, expanding, or high-end decorating houses;  
(4) leasing high-end office buildings, hotels, apartments, etc. for office use;  
(5) purchasing vehicles not necessary for business operations;  
(6) traveling or vacationing;  
(7) enrolling children in high-fee private schools;  
(8) paying high premiums to purchase insurance and financial products;  
(9) other high consumption acts not necessary for daily life and work.  
If the person subject to enforcement is an entity, after being restricted from high consumption, the person subject to enforcement and its legal representative, principal responsible person, and directly responsible personnel who affect debt performance shall be prohibited from using the entity's property to carry out the acts specified in the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  限制高消费一般由申请执行人提出书面申请，经人民法院审查决定；必要时人民法院可以依职权决定。

**Article 4** Restriction on high consumption shall generally be applied for in writing by the applicant for enforcement, and be decided by the People's Court after review; when necessary, the People's Court may decide ex officio.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院决定限制高消费的，应当向被执行人发出限制高消费令。限制高消费令由人民法院院长签发。限制高消费令应当载明限制高消费的期间、项目、法律后果等内容。

**Article 5** If the People's Court decides to restrict high consumption, it shall issue a restriction order on high consumption to the person subject to enforcement. The restriction order on high consumption shall be signed by the president of the People's Court. The restriction order on high consumption shall specify the period, items, legal consequences, etc. of the restriction on high consumption.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院根据案件需要和被执行人的情况可以向有义务协助调查、执行的单位送达协助执行通知书，也可以在相关媒体上进行公告。

**Article 6** The People's Court may, based on the needs of the case and the circumstances of the person subject to enforcement, serve a notice of assistance in enforcement to units obligated to assist in investigation and enforcement, or may make a public announcement in relevant media.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  限制高消费令的公告费用由被执行人负担；申请执行人申请在媒体公告的，应当垫付公告费用。

**Article 7** The announcement expenses for the restriction order on high consumption shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement; if the applicant for enforcement applies for announcement in the media, the applicant shall advance the announcement expenses.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  被限制高消费的被执行人因生活或者经营必需而进行本规定禁止的消费活动的，应当向人民法院提出申请，获批准后方可进行。

**Article 8** If a person subject to enforcement who is restricted from high consumption needs to engage in consumption activities prohibited by these Provisions due to necessities of life or business operations, the person shall submit an application to the People's Court and may proceed only after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  在限制高消费期间，被执行人提供确实有效的担保或者经申请执行人同意的，人民法院可以解除限制高消费令；被执行人履行完毕生效法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院应当在[本规定第六条](#art-6)通知或者公告的范围内及时以通知或者公告解除限制高消费令。

**Article 9** During the period of restriction on high consumption, if the person subject to enforcement provides genuine and effective security or has the consent of the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court may lift the restriction order on high consumption; if the person subject to enforcement has fulfilled all obligations determined by the legally effective legal document, the People's Court shall promptly lift the restriction order on high consumption by notice or announcement within the scope of the notification or announcement under Article 6 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民法院应当设置举报电话或者邮箱，接受申请执行人和社会公众对被限制高消费的被执行人违反[本规定第三条](#art-3)的举报，并进行审查认定。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall set up a reporting telephone or mailbox to receive reports from the applicant for enforcement and the public regarding violations of Article 3 of these Provisions by a person subject to enforcement who is restricted from high consumption, and shall review and determine such reports.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  被执行人违反限制高消费令进行消费的行为属于拒不履行人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的行为，经查证属实的，依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的规定，予以拘留、罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，追究其刑事责任。  
有关单位在收到人民法院协助执行通知书后，仍允许被执行人高消费的，人民法院可以依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-103)的规定，追究其法律责任。

**Article 11** If the person subject to enforcement violates the restriction order on high consumption and engages in consumption, such act constitutes an act of refusing to perform a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect by the People's Court. If verified, the person shall be detained or fined in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued.  
If a relevant unit, after receiving the notice of assistance in enforcement from the People's Court, still allows the person subject to enforcement to engage in high consumption, the People's Court may pursue its legal liability in accordance with Article 103 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  本规定自2010年10月1日起施行。

**Article 12** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 2010.
