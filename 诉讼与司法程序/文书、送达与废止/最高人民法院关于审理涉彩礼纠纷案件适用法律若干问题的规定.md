# 最高人民法院关于审理涉彩礼纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕1号  
**公布日期**：2023-11-13  
**生效日期**：2024-02-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉彩礼纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2023年11月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1905次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年2月1日起施行。 2024年1月17日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉彩礼纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2024〕1号 为正确审理涉彩礼纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　以婚姻为目的依据习俗给付彩礼后，因要求返还产生的纠纷，适用本规定。 第二条　禁止借婚姻索取财物。一方以彩礼为名借婚姻索取财物，另一方要求返还的，人民法院应予支持。 第三条　人民法院在审理涉彩礼纠纷案件中，可以根据一方给付财物的目的，综合考虑双方当地习俗、给付的时间和方式、财物价值、给付人及接收人等事实，认定彩礼范围。 下列情形给付的财物，不属于彩礼： （一）一方在节日、生日等有特殊纪念意义时点给付的价值不大的礼物、礼金； （二）一方为表达或者增进感情的日常消费性支出； （三）其他价值不大的财物。 第四条　婚约财产纠纷中，婚约一方及其实际给付彩礼的父母可以作为共同原告；婚约另一方及其实际接收彩礼的父母可以作为共同被告。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　以婚姻为目的依据习俗给付彩礼后，因要求返还产生的纠纷，适用本规定。

**Article 1** Where a bride price is given pursuant to customs for the purpose of marriage, these Provisions shall apply to disputes arising from the request for its return.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　禁止借婚姻索取财物。一方以彩礼为名借婚姻索取财物，另一方要求返还的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** It shall be prohibited to demand property under the guise of marriage. Where one party demands property under the guise of marriage in the name of bride price, and the other party requests its return, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院在审理涉彩礼纠纷案件中，可以根据一方给付财物的目的，综合考虑双方当地习俗、给付的时间和方式、财物价值、给付人及接收人等事实，认定彩礼范围。  
下列情形给付的财物，不属于彩礼：  
（一）一方在节日、生日等有特殊纪念意义时点给付的价值不大的礼物、礼金；  
（二）一方为表达或者增进感情的日常消费性支出；  
（三）其他价值不大的财物。

**Article 3** In hearing cases involving bride price disputes, the People's Court may, based on the purpose of giving property by a party, comprehensively consider facts such as the local customs of both parties, the time and manner of giving, the value of the property, the giver and receiver, to determine the scope of bride price.  
  
Property given in the following circumstances shall not be considered bride price:  
(1) gifts or cash gifts of small value given by a party at special commemorative times such as festivals or birthdays;  
(2) daily consumption expenditures by a party for expressing or enhancing feelings;  
(3) other property of small value.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　婚约财产纠纷中，婚约一方及其实际给付彩礼的父母可以作为共同原告；婚约另一方及其实际接收彩礼的父母可以作为共同被告。  
离婚纠纷中，一方提出返还彩礼诉讼请求的，当事人仍为夫妻双方。

**Article 4** In a dispute over betrothal property, the party to the betrothal and his or her parents who actually gave the bride price may serve as co-plaintiffs; the other party to the betrothal and his or her parents who actually received the bride price may serve as co-defendants.  
  
In a divorce dispute, where a party raises a claim for the return of bride price, the parties shall still be the husband and wife.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　双方已办理结婚登记且共同生活，离婚时一方请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，人民法院一般不予支持。但是，如果共同生活时间较短且彩礼数额过高的，人民法院可以根据彩礼实际使用及嫁妆情况，综合考虑彩礼数额、共同生活及孕育情况、双方过错等事实，结合当地习俗，确定是否返还以及返还的具体比例。  
人民法院认定彩礼数额是否过高，应当综合考虑彩礼给付方所在地居民人均可支配收入、给付方家庭经济情况以及当地习俗等因素。

**Article 5** Where the parties have already registered their marriage and live together, and one party requests the return of bride price given pursuant to customs at the time of divorce, the People's Court shall generally not support such request. However, if the cohabitation period is relatively short and the bride price is excessively high, the People's Court may, based on the actual use of the bride price and the dowry, comprehensively consider facts such as the amount of bride price, the cohabitation and pregnancy conditions, the fault of both parties, and in light of local customs, determine whether to return and the specific proportion of return.  
  
In determining whether the amount of bride price is excessively high, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the per capita disposable income of the residents in the locality of the party giving the bride price, the financial situation of that party's family, and local customs.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　双方未办理结婚登记但已共同生活，一方请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，人民法院应当根据彩礼实际使用及嫁妆情况，综合考虑共同生活及孕育情况、双方过错等事实，结合当地习俗，确定是否返还以及返还的具体比例。

**Article 6** Where the parties have not registered their marriage but have lived together, and one party requests the return of bride price given pursuant to customs, the People's Court shall, based on the actual use of the bride price and the dowry, comprehensively consider facts such as the cohabitation and pregnancy conditions, the fault of both parties, and in light of local customs, determine whether to return and the specific proportion of return.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定自2024年2月1日起施行。  
本规定施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本规定。本规定施行前已经终审、施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 7** These Provisions shall come into force as of February 1, 2024.  
  
After these Provisions come into force, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been finally adjudicated by the People's Court shall be governed by these Provisions. Cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, and for which a party applies for a retrial or which are decided to be retried pursuant to the adjudication supervision procedure after these Provisions come into force, shall not be governed by these Provisions.
