# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于执行《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严禁卖淫嫖娼的决定》的若干问题的解答

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1992〕42号  
**公布日期**：1992-12-11

---

### 正文

一、《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严禁卖淫嫖娼的决定》规定了几个新罪名？  
《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严禁卖淫嫖娼的决定》（以下简称《决定》）规定了4个新罪名，即：组织他人卖淫罪（第一条第一款）；协助组织他人卖淫罪（第一条第二款）；介绍他人卖淫罪（第三条第一款）；传播性病罪（第五条第一款）。

**Article None** I. How many new crimes are prescribed by the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Strictly Prohibiting Prostitution and Whoring?  
The Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Strictly Prohibiting Prostitution and Whoring (hereinafter referred to as the "Decision") prescribes four new crimes, namely: the crime of organizing prostitution by others (Article 1, paragraph 1); the crime of assisting in organizing prostitution by others (Article 1, paragraph 2); the crime of introducing prostitution by others (Article 3, paragraph 1); and the crime of spreading venereal disease (Article 5, paragraph 1).

二、怎样认定组织他人卖淫罪？  
根据《决定》第一条第一款的规定，组织他人卖淫罪，是指以招募、雇佣、强迫、引诱、容留等手段，控制多人从事卖淫的行为。  
本罪的主体必须是卖淫的组织者，可以是几个人，也可以是一个人，关键要看其在卖淫活动中是否起组织者的作用。  
在组织他人卖淫的犯罪活动中，对被组织卖淫的人有强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫行为的，应当作为组织他人卖淫罪的量刑情节予以考虑，不实行数罪并罚。如果这些行为是对被组织者以外的其他人实施的，仍应当分别定罪，实行数罪并罚。

**Article None** II. How to determine the crime of organizing prostitution by others?  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 1, paragraph 1 of the Decision, the crime of organizing prostitution by others refers to the act of controlling multiple persons to engage in prostitution by means such as recruitment, employment, coercion, enticement, or harboring.  
The subject of this crime shall be the organizer of prostitution, which may be a group or an individual; the key is whether he or she plays the role of an organizer in the prostitution activities.  
In the criminal activity of organizing prostitution by others, if the organizer also commits acts of coercion, enticement, harboring, or introduction of prostitution against the persons being organized to engage in prostitution, such acts shall be considered as circumstances of sentencing for the crime of organizing prostitution by others, and no combined punishment for multiple crimes shall be applied. If such acts are committed against persons other than those being organized, they shall still be convicted separately and subjected to combined punishment for multiple crimes.

三、怎样认定协助组织他人卖淫罪？  
根据《决定》第一条第二款的规定，协助组织他人卖淫罪，是指在组织他人卖淫的共同犯罪中起帮助作用的行为。如充当保镖、打手、管帐人等。  
依照《决定》第一条第二款的规定，协助组织他人卖淫的行为，有具体的罪状和单独的法定刑，应当确定为独立的罪名，适用单独的法定刑处罚，不适用刑法总则第二十四条关于从犯的处罚原则。

**Article None** III. How to determine the crime of assisting in organizing prostitution by others?  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 1, paragraph 2 of the Decision, the crime of assisting in organizing prostitution by others refers to the act of playing an assisting role in a joint crime of organizing prostitution by others, such as acting as a bodyguard, thug, or bookkeeper.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 1, paragraph 2 of the Decision, the act of assisting in organizing prostitution by others has a specific description of the crime and an independent statutory penalty. It shall be determined as an independent crime and punished by applying an independent statutory penalty, and the principle of punishing accomplices under Article 24 of the General Provisions of the Criminal Law shall not apply.

四、怎样理解《决定》第二条第（三）项关于“强奸后迫使卖淫”的规定？  
《决定》第二条第（三）项规定的“强奸后迫使卖淫”，是指强奸行为与强迫他人卖淫的行为有联系，是强迫他人卖淫的法定从重情节。因此，只定强迫他人卖淫罪即可。如果强奸行为与强迫他人卖淫的行为之间没有联系，则应当分别定罪，实行并罚。

**Article None** IV. How to understand the provision of 'forcing prostitution after rape' in Article 2, item (3) of the Decision?  
The 'forcing prostitution after rape' prescribed in Article 2, item (3) of the Decision means that the rape act is connected with the act of forcing others to engage in prostitution, and it is a statutory aggravating circumstance of forcing others to engage in prostitution. Therefore, only the crime of forcing others to engage in prostitution shall be determined. If there is no connection between the rape act and the act of forcing others to engage in prostitution, they shall be convicted separately and subjected to combined punishment.

五、哪些是组织他人卖淫罪、强迫他人卖淫罪中“情节特别严重”的行为？  
《决定》第一条第一款规定的组织他人卖淫罪中的“情节特别严重”，主要是指组织他人卖淫的首要分子情节特别严重的；组织他人卖淫手段特别恶劣的；对被组织卖淫者造成特别严重后果的；组织多人多次卖淫具有极大的社会危害性的，等等。  
《决定》第二条规定的强迫他人卖淫罪中的“情节特别严重”，是指《决定》第二条所列四项情形中特别严重的情节。在具体执行中，不应在这四项情形之外扩大范围。

**Article None** V. What are the acts of 'particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of organizing prostitution by others and the crime of forcing others to engage in prostitution?  
The 'particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of organizing prostitution by others prescribed in Article 1, paragraph 1 of the Decision mainly refer to: the ringleader of organizing prostitution by others whose circumstances are particularly serious; the means of organizing prostitution by others are especially odious; particularly serious consequences are caused to the persons being organized to engage in prostitution; organizing multiple persons to engage in prostitution many times has great social harmfulness, etc.  
The 'particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of forcing others to engage in prostitution prescribed in Article 2 of the Decision refer to the particularly serious circumstances among the four situations listed in Article 2 of the Decision. In specific enforcement, the scope shall not be expanded beyond these four situations.

六、怎样认定引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫罪？  
引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫罪是一个选择性罪名。引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫这三种行为，不论是同时实施还是只实施其中一种行为，均构成本罪。如：介绍他人卖淫的，定介绍他人卖淫罪；兼有引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫三种行为的，定引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫罪，不实行数罪并罚。  
引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫是否以营利为目的，不影响本罪的成立。  
根据《决定》第三条第二款的规定，引诱不满十四岁的幼女卖淫的，依照《决定》第二条第（一）项关于强迫不满十四岁的幼女卖淫的规定处罚，定强迫他人卖淫罪。

**Article None** VI. How to determine the crime of enticing, harboring, or introducing prostitution by others?  
The crime of enticing, harboring, or introducing prostitution by others is a selective charge. The three acts of enticing, harboring, or introducing others to engage in prostitution, whether committed simultaneously or only one of them, all constitute this crime. For example, if one introduces prostitution by others, the charge is the crime of introducing prostitution by others; if one combines the three acts of enticing, harboring, and introducing prostitution by others, the charge is the crime of enticing, harboring, and introducing prostitution by others, and no combined punishment for multiple crimes shall be applied.  
Whether the enticement, harboring, or introduction of others to engage in prostitution is for the purpose of profit shall not affect the establishment of this crime.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 3, paragraph 2 of the Decision, if one entices a young girl under the age of fourteen to engage in prostitution, he or she shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 2, item (1) of the Decision on forcing a young girl under the age of fourteen to engage in prostitution, and the charge shall be the crime of forcing others to engage in prostitution.

七、哪些是引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫罪中“情节严重”的行为？  
引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫，情节严重的，一般有以下几种情形：  
（一）多次引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫的；  
（二）引诱、容留、介绍多人卖淫的；  
（三）引诱、容留、介绍明知是有严重性病的人卖淫的；  
（四）容留、介绍不满十四岁的幼女卖淫的；  
（五）引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫具有其他严重情节的。

**Article None** VII. What are the acts of 'serious circumstances' in the crime of enticing, harboring, or introducing prostitution by others?  
Where the circumstances of enticing, harboring, or introducing others to engage in prostitution are serious, the following situations generally exist:  
(1) repeatedly enticing, harboring, or introducing others to engage in prostitution;  
(2) enticing, harboring, or introducing multiple persons to engage in prostitution;  
(3) enticing, harboring, or introducing a person known to have a serious venereal disease to engage in prostitution;  
(4) harboring or introducing a young girl under the age of fourteen to engage in prostitution;  
(5) other serious circumstances in enticing, harboring, or introducing others to engage in prostitution.

八、怎样认定传播性病罪？  
根据《决定》第五条第一款的规定，传播性病罪，是指明知自己患有梅毒、淋病等严重性病而进行卖淫嫖娼的行为。  
（一）本罪属特殊主体，即已满十六岁，具有刑事责任能力，且患有梅毒、淋病等严重性病的人。中国公民和外国人均可成为本罪的主体。  
（二）必须实施了卖淫、嫖娼的行为。至于实际是否已造成他人染上性病的结果，不影响本罪的成立。行为人通过其他方式（如通奸等）将性病传播给他人的，不构成本罪。  
（三）具备以下情形之一的，可以认定为“明知”：  
1、有证据证明曾到医院就医，被诊断为患有严重性病的；  
2、根据本人的知识和经验，能够知道自己患有严重性病的；  
3、通过其他方法能够证明被告人是“明知”的。

**Article None** VIII. How to determine the crime of spreading venereal disease?  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 5, paragraph 1 of the Decision, the crime of spreading venereal disease refers to the act of engaging in prostitution or whoring while knowing that one is suffering from serious venereal diseases such as syphilis or gonorrhea.  
(1) This crime is subject to a special subject, that is, a person who has reached the age of sixteen, has criminal liability, and is suffering from serious venereal diseases such as syphilis or gonorrhea. Both Chinese citizens and foreigners may become the subject of this crime.  
(2) The actor must have committed acts of prostitution or whoring. Whether the act actually causes others to contract venereal disease or not shall not affect the establishment of this crime. Where the actor transmits venereal disease to others through other means (such as adultery), this crime shall not be constituted.  
(3) Where any of the following circumstances exists, the actor may be determined to have 'knowledge':  
1. there is evidence that the actor has been to a hospital for medical treatment and was diagnosed as suffering from a serious venereal disease;  
2. based on the actor's own knowledge and experience, he or she could know that he or she suffers from a serious venereal disease;  
3. it can be proved by other methods that the defendant had 'knowledge'.

九、对《决定》中提到的“他人”、“多人”、“多次”应当怎样理解？  
（一）组织、协助组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫中的“他人”，主要是指女人，也包括男人。  
（二）《决定》和本解答中的“多人”、“多次”的“多”，是指“三”以上的数（含本数）

**Article None** IX. How shall the terms 'others', 'multiple persons', and 'many times' mentioned in the Decision be understood?  
(1) The term 'others' in organizing, assisting in organizing, forcing, enticing, harboring, or introducing others to engage in prostitution mainly refers to women, but also includes men.  
(2) The term 'multiple' in 'multiple persons' and 'many times' in the Decision and this interpretation refers to a number of 'three' or more (including the number itself).

十、如何理解《决定》的时效问题？  
（一）对在《决定》公布施行后发生的案件，依照《决定》的规定办理。对在《决定》公布施行前发生、公布施行后尚未处理或者正在处理的案件，依照刑法第九条规定的原则办理。  
（二）本解答发布后，对应当按照《决定》处理的案件，适用本解答。《决定》公布施行前已处理的案件和本解答发布前已按《决定》处理的案件，不再适用本解答。  
（三）鉴于《决定》对刑法第一百四十条和第一百六十九条以及《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严惩严重危害社会治安的犯罪分子的决定》第一条第6项的规定已进行修改、补充，对《决定》公布施行后依照《决定》处理的案件，在诉讼文书中不再引用上述有关条文。

**Article None** X. How to understand the issue of effectivity of the Decision?  
(1) Cases occurring after the Decision is promulgated and comes into force shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Decision. Cases occurring before the Decision is promulgated and comes into force but which have not yet been handled or are being handled after its promulgation and coming into force shall be handled in accordance with the principles prescribed in Article 9 of the Criminal Law.  
(2) After this interpretation is issued, cases that shall be handled in accordance with the Decision shall be subject to this interpretation. Cases that have already been handled before the Decision is promulgated and comes into force and cases that have already been handled in accordance with the Decision before this interpretation is issued shall no longer be subject to this interpretation.  
(3) In view of the fact that the Decision has amended and supplemented the provisions of Articles 140 and 169 of the Criminal Law and Article 1, item 6 of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Severely Punishing Criminals Who Seriously Endanger Public Security, for cases handled in accordance with the Decision after its promulgation and coming into force, the above-mentioned relevant provisions shall no longer be cited in litigation documents.  
December 11, 1992
