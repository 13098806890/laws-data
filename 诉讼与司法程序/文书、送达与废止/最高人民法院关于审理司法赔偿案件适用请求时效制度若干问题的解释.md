# 最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕2号  
**公布日期**：2023-04-03  
**生效日期**：2023-06-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释》已于2023年4月3日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1883次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年6月1日起施行。 2023年5月23日 最高人民法院 关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度 若干问题的解释 法释〔2023〕2号 为正确适用国家赔偿请求时效制度的规定，保障赔偿请求人的合法权益，依照《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》的规定，结合司法赔偿审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　赔偿请求人向赔偿义务机关提出赔偿请求的时效期间为两年，自其知道或者应当知道国家机关及其工作人员行使职权时的行为侵犯其人身权、财产权之日起计算。 赔偿请求人知道上述侵权行为时，相关诉讼程序或者执行程序尚未终结的，请求时效期间自该诉讼程序或者执行程序终结之日起计算，但是本解释有特别规定的除外。 第二条　赔偿请求人以人身权受到侵犯为由，依照国家赔偿法第十七条第一项、第二项、第三项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其收到决定撤销案件、终止侦查、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪等终止追究刑事责任或者再审改判无罪的法律文书之日起计算。 办案机关未作出终止追究刑事责任的法律文书，但是符合《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第二条规定情形，赔偿请求人申请赔偿的，依法应当受理。 第三条　赔偿请求人以人身权受到侵犯为由，依照国家赔偿法第十七条第四项、第五项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其知道或者应当知道损害结果之日起计算；损害结果当时不能确定的，自损害结果确定之日起计算。 第四条　赔偿请求人以财产权受到侵犯为由，依照国家赔偿法第十八条第一项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其收到刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书之日起计算，但是刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结之后办案机关对涉案财物尚未处理完毕的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人知道或者应当知道其财产权受到侵犯之日起计算。 办案机关未作出刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书，但是符合《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第三条规定情形，赔偿请求人申请赔偿的，依法应当受理。 赔偿请求人以财产权受到侵犯为由，依照国家赔偿法第十八条第二项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人收到生效再审刑事裁判文书之日起计算。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　赔偿请求人向赔偿义务机关提出赔偿请求的时效期间为两年，自其知道或者应当知道国家机关及其工作人员行使职权时的行为侵犯其人身权、财产权之日起计算。  
赔偿请求人知道上述侵权行为时，相关诉讼程序或者执行程序尚未终结的，请求时效期间自该诉讼程序或者执行程序终结之日起计算，但是本解释有特别规定的除外。

**Article 1** The limitation period for a compensation claimant to file a claim for compensation with the compensation obligor shall be two years, commencing from the date when the claimant knows or should have known that the act of a state organ or its staff in exercising their functions infringes upon his/her personal rights or property rights.  
If, when the compensation claimant knows of the said infringement, the relevant litigation proceedings or enforcement proceedings have not yet been concluded, the limitation period shall commence from the date of conclusion of such litigation proceedings or enforcement proceedings, unless otherwise specifically provided in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　赔偿请求人以人身权受到侵犯为由，依照[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)第一项、第二项、第三项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其收到决定撤销案件、终止侦查、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪等终止追究刑事责任或者再审改判无罪的法律文书之日起计算。  
办案机关未作出终止追究刑事责任的法律文书，但是符合[《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第二条](../../刑事/检察院/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-2)规定情形，赔偿请求人申请赔偿的，依法应当受理。

**Article 2** Where a compensation claimant files a claim for compensation on the ground that his/her personal rights have been infringed, in accordance with the provisions of subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 17 of the State Compensation Law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the claimant receives the legal document deciding to drop the case, terminate investigation, decide not to prosecute, or acquit by judgment, or other legal document terminating criminal liability or acquitting by retrial.  
If the case-handling organ has not issued a legal document terminating criminal liability, but the circumstances meet the provisions of Article 2 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in Handling Criminal Compensation Cases, and the compensation claimant files a claim for compensation, such claim shall be accepted according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿请求人以人身权受到侵犯为由，依照[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)第四项、第五项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其知道或者应当知道损害结果之日起计算；损害结果当时不能确定的，自损害结果确定之日起计算。

**Article 3** Where a compensation claimant files a claim for compensation on the ground that his/her personal rights have been infringed, in accordance with the provisions of subparagraphs (4) and (5) of Article 17 of the State Compensation Law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the claimant knows or should have known of the damage result; if the damage result cannot be determined at that time, the period shall commence from the date when the damage result is determined.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　赔偿请求人以财产权受到侵犯为由，依照[国家赔偿法第十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-18)第一项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自其收到刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书之日起计算，但是刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结之后办案机关对涉案财物尚未处理完毕的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人知道或者应当知道其财产权受到侵犯之日起计算。  
办案机关未作出刑事诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书，但是符合[《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第三条](../../刑事/检察院/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-3)规定情形，赔偿请求人申请赔偿的，依法应当受理。  
赔偿请求人以财产权受到侵犯为由，依照[国家赔偿法第十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-18)第二项规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人收到生效再审刑事裁判文书之日起计算。

**Article 4** Where a compensation claimant files a claim for compensation on the ground that his/her property rights have been infringed, in accordance with the provisions of subparagraph (1) of Article 18 of the State Compensation Law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the claimant receives the legal document concluding the criminal proceedings or enforcement proceedings. However, if after the conclusion of the criminal proceedings or enforcement proceedings, the case-handling organ has not yet completed the handling of the property involved in the case, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the compensation claimant knows or should have known that his/her property rights are infringed.  
If the case-handling organ has not issued a legal document concluding the criminal proceedings or enforcement proceedings, but the circumstances meet the provisions of Article 3 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in Handling Criminal Compensation Cases, and the compensation claimant files a claim for compensation, such claim shall be accepted according to law.  
Where a compensation claimant files a claim for compensation on the ground that his/her property rights have been infringed, in accordance with the provisions of subparagraph (2) of Article 18 of the State Compensation Law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the compensation claimant receives the effective criminal judgment or order of retrial.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　赔偿请求人以人身权或者财产权受到侵犯为由，依照[国家赔偿法第三十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-38)规定申请赔偿的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人收到民事、行政诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书之日起计算，但是下列情形除外：  
（一）罚款、拘留等强制措施已被依法撤销的，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人收到撤销决定之日起计算；  
（二）在民事、行政诉讼过程中，有殴打、虐待或者唆使、放纵他人殴打、虐待等行为，以及违法使用武器、警械，造成公民人身损害的，请求时效期间的计算适用[本解释第三条](#art-3)的规定。  
人民法院未作出民事、行政诉讼程序或者执行程序终结的法律文书，请求时效期间自赔偿请求人知道或者应当知道其人身权或者财产权受到侵犯之日起计算。

**Article 5** Where a compensation claimant files a claim for compensation on the ground that his/her personal rights or property rights have been infringed, in accordance with the provisions of Article 38 of the State Compensation Law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the compensation claimant receives the legal document concluding the civil or administrative proceedings or enforcement proceedings, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) If compulsory measures such as fines or detention have been revoked according to law, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the compensation claimant receives the decision to revoke them;  
(2) If during civil or administrative proceedings, there have been actions such as beating, maltreatment, or instigating or indulging others to beat or maltreat, as well as illegal use of weapons or police instruments, causing personal injury to a citizen, the calculation of the limitation period shall apply the provisions of Article 3 of this Interpretation.  
If the People's Court has not issued a legal document concluding the civil or administrative proceedings or enforcement proceedings, the limitation period shall commence from the date when the compensation claimant knows or should have known that his/her personal rights or property rights are infringed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　依照[国家赔偿法第三十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-39)第一款规定，赔偿请求人被羁押等限制人身自由的期间，不计算在请求时效期间内。  
赔偿请求人依照法律法规规定的程序向相关机关申请确认职权行为违法或者寻求救济的期间，不计算在请求时效期间内，但是相关机关已经明确告知赔偿请求人应当依法申请国家赔偿的除外。

**Article 6** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 39 of the State Compensation Law, the period during which the compensation claimant is in custody or otherwise has his/her personal freedom restricted shall not be counted within the limitation period.  
The period during which the compensation claimant applies to the relevant organ for confirmation of the illegality of the exercise of functions or seeks relief in accordance with the procedures prescribed by laws and regulations shall not be counted within the limitation period, unless the relevant organ has expressly informed the compensation claimant that he/she shall apply for state compensation according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　依照[国家赔偿法第三十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-39)第二款规定，在请求时效期间的最后六个月内，赔偿请求人因下列障碍之一，不能行使请求权的，请求时效中止：  
（一）不可抗力；  
（二）无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人没有法定代理人，或者法定代理人死亡、丧失民事行为能力、丧失代理权；  
（三）其他导致不能行使请求权的障碍。  
自中止时效的原因消除之日起满六个月，请求时效期间届满。

**Article 7** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 39 of the State Compensation Law, if within the last six months of the limitation period, the compensation claimant is unable to exercise the right to claim due to one of the following obstacles, the limitation period shall be suspended:  
(1) Force majeure;  
(2) The person with no or limited capacity for civil conduct has no statutory agent, or the statutory agent has died, lost capacity for civil conduct, or lost the power of agency;  
(3) Other obstacles that prevent the exercise of the right to claim.  
Upon the expiration of six months from the date of removal of the cause for suspension, the limitation period shall expire.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　请求时效期间届满的，赔偿义务机关可以提出不予赔偿的抗辩。  
请求时效期间届满，赔偿义务机关同意赔偿或者予以赔偿后，又以请求时效期间届满为由提出抗辩或者要求赔偿请求人返还赔偿金的，人民法院赔偿委员会不予支持。

**Article 8** If the limitation period has expired, the compensation obligor may raise a defense of non-compensation.  
If, after the limitation period has expired, the compensation obligor agrees to compensate or has made compensation, and then raises a defense on the ground that the limitation period has expired or requests the compensation claimant to return the compensation amount, the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　赔偿义务机关以请求时效期间届满为由抗辩，应当在人民法院赔偿委员会作出国家赔偿决定前提出。  
赔偿义务机关未按前款规定提出抗辩，又以请求时效期间届满为由申诉的，人民法院赔偿委员会不予支持。

**Article 9** The compensation obligor that raises a defense on the ground that the limitation period has expired shall do so before the Compensation Committee of the People's Court renders a decision on state compensation.  
If the compensation obligor fails to raise the defense in accordance with the preceding paragraph and then files a petition on the ground that the limitation period has expired, the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院赔偿委员会审理国家赔偿案件，不得主动适用请求时效的规定。

**Article 10** When hearing a state compensation case, the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall not proactively apply the provisions on the limitation period.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　请求时效期间起算的当日不计入，自下一日开始计算。  
请求时效期间按照年、月计算，到期月的对应日为期间的最后一日；没有对应日的，月末日为期间的最后一日。  
请求时效期间的最后一日是法定休假日的，以法定休假日结束的次日为期间的最后一日。

**Article 11** The day on which the limitation period commences shall not be counted; it shall be counted from the next day.  
If the limitation period is calculated by year or month, the corresponding day of the expiration month shall be the last day of the period; if there is no corresponding day, the last day of that month shall be the last day of the period.  
If the last day of the limitation period is a statutory holiday, the day after the end of the statutory holiday shall be the last day of the period.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2023年6月1日起施行。本解释施行后，案件尚在审理的，适用本解释；对本解释施行前已经作出生效赔偿决定的案件进行再审，不适用本解释。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force as of June 1, 2023. After this Interpretation comes into force, cases still under trial shall be governed by this Interpretation. For cases in which an effective compensation decision has been made before this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall not apply when such cases are retried.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本院之前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 13** Where any previous judicial interpretation issued by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
