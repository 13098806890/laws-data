# 最高人民法院关于审理涉执行司法赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕3号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-20  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉执行司法赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2021年12月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1857次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年3月1日起施行。 2022年2月8日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉执行司法赔偿案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2022〕3号 为正确审理涉执行司法赔偿案件，保障公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》等法律规定，结合人民法院国家赔偿审判和执行工作实际，制定本解释。 第一条　人民法院在执行判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书过程中，错误采取财产调查、控制、处置、交付、分配等执行措施或者罚款、拘留等强制措施，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益并造成损害，受害人依照国家赔偿法第三十八条规定申请赔偿的，适用本解释。 第二条　公民、法人和其他组织认为有下列错误执行行为造成损害申请赔偿的，人民法院应当依法受理： （一）执行未生效法律文书，或者明显超出生效法律文书确定的数额和范围执行的； （二）发现被执行人有可供执行的财产，但故意拖延执行、不执行，或者应当依法恢复执行而不恢复的； （三）违法执行案外人财产，或者违法将案件执行款物交付给其他当事人、案外人的； （四）对抵押、质押、留置、保留所有权等财产采取执行措施，未依法保护上述权利人优先受偿权等合法权益的； （五）对其他人民法院已经依法采取保全或者执行措施的财产违法执行的； （六）对执行中查封、扣押、冻结的财产故意不履行或者怠于履行监管职责的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院在执行判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书过程中，错误采取财产调查、控制、处置、交付、分配等执行措施或者罚款、拘留等强制措施，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益并造成损害，受害人依照[国家赔偿法第三十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-38)规定申请赔偿的，适用本解释。

**Article 1** Where a People's Court, in the course of enforcing a judgment, ruling, or other effective legal document, erroneously adopts enforcement measures such as property investigation, control, disposition, delivery, or distribution, or compulsory measures such as a fine or detention, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization and causes damage, and the victim applies for compensation in accordance with Article 38 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公民、法人和其他组织认为有下列错误执行行为造成损害申请赔偿的，人民法院应当依法受理：  
（一）执行未生效法律文书，或者明显超出生效法律文书确定的数额和范围执行的；  
（二）发现被执行人有可供执行的财产，但故意拖延执行、不执行，或者应当依法恢复执行而不恢复的；  
（三）违法执行案外人财产，或者违法将案件执行款物交付给其他当事人、案外人的；  
（四）对抵押、质押、留置、保留所有权等财产采取执行措施，未依法保护上述权利人优先受偿权等合法权益的；  
（五）对其他人民法院已经依法采取保全或者执行措施的财产违法执行的；  
（六）对执行中查封、扣押、冻结的财产故意不履行或者怠于履行监管职责的；  
（七）对不宜长期保存或者易贬值的财产采取执行措施，未及时处理或者违法处理的；  
（八）违法拍卖、变卖、以物抵债，或者依法应当评估而未评估，依法应当拍卖而未拍卖的；  
（九）违法撤销拍卖、变卖或者以物抵债的；  
（十）违法采取纳入失信被执行人名单、限制消费、限制出境等措施的；  
（十一）因违法或者过错采取执行措施或者强制措施的其他行为。

**Article 2** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization believes that any of the following erroneous enforcement acts have caused damage and applies for compensation, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) enforcing an ineffective legal document, or enforcing beyond the amount and scope clearly determined in the effective legal document;  
(2) discovering that the party subject to enforcement has property available for enforcement but intentionally delaying enforcement, failing to enforce, or failing to resume enforcement that should be resumed according to law;  
(3) illegally enforcing the property of a person not involved in the case, or illegally delivering the enforcement funds or property to another party or a person not involved in the case;  
(4) adopting enforcement measures against property that is mortgaged, pledged, subject to a lien, or retained ownership, without protecting according to law the prior right to satisfaction of such property holders and other lawful rights and interests;  
(5) illegally enforcing property that has been legally preserved or enforced by another People's Court;  
(6) intentionally failing to fulfill or neglecting the supervision duty over property sealed up, detained, or frozen during enforcement;  
(7) adopting enforcement measures against property that is not suitable for long-term storage or is prone to depreciation, without timely handling or handling it illegally;  
(8) illegally auctioning, selling off, or offsetting debt by property, or failing to appraise when appraisal should be conducted according to law, or failing to auction when auction should be conducted according to law;  
(9) illegally revoking an auction, sale-off, or offsetting of debt by property;  
(10) illegally adopting measures such as inclusion in the list of dishonest judgment debtors, restriction on consumption, or restriction on exit;  
(11) any other act of adopting enforcement measures or compulsory measures that is illegal or faulty.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　原债权人转让债权的，其基于债权申请国家赔偿的权利随之转移，但根据债权性质、当事人约定或者法律规定不得转让的除外。

**Article 3** Where the original creditor assigns a claim, the right to apply for state compensation based on that claim shall be transferred accordingly, except where the claim is not assignable by its nature, by agreement of the parties, or by law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院将查封、扣押、冻结等事项委托其他人民法院执行的，公民、法人和其他组织认为错误执行行为造成损害申请赔偿的，委托法院为赔偿义务机关。

**Article 4** Where a People's Court entrusts another People's Court with matters such as sealing up, detaining, or freezing for enforcement, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization believes that the erroneous enforcement act has caused damage and applies for compensation, the entrusting court shall be the compensation obligor.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民、法人和其他组织申请错误执行赔偿，应当在执行程序终结后提出，终结前提出的不予受理。但有下列情形之一，且无法在相关诉讼或者执行程序中予以补救的除外：  
（一）罚款、拘留等强制措施已被依法撤销，或者实施过程中造成人身损害的；  
（二）被执行的财产经诉讼程序依法确认不属于被执行人，或者人民法院生效法律文书已确认执行行为违法的；  
（三）自立案执行之日起超过五年，且已裁定终结本次执行程序，被执行人已无可供执行财产的；  
（四）在执行程序终结前可以申请赔偿的其他情形。  
赔偿请求人依据前款规定，在执行程序终结后申请赔偿的，该执行程序期间不计入赔偿请求时效。

**Article 5** A citizen, legal person, or other organization applying for compensation for erroneous enforcement shall make the application after the enforcement procedure has concluded. An application made before conclusion shall not be accepted, except under any of the following circumstances where the damage cannot be remedied in the relevant litigation or enforcement procedure:  
(1) compulsory measures such as a fine or detention have been revoked according to law, or personal injury has been caused in the course of implementation;  
(2) the enforced property has been confirmed by litigation procedure according to law as not belonging to the party subject to enforcement, or an effective legal document of the People's Court has confirmed the illegality of the enforcement act;  
(3) more than five years have passed since the case was docketed for enforcement, the current enforcement procedure has been ruled to be concluded, and the party subject to enforcement has no property available for enforcement;  
(4) any other circumstance under which an application for compensation may be made before the conclusion of the enforcement procedure.  
Where a compensation claimant applies for compensation after the conclusion of the enforcement procedure in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the period of such enforcement procedure shall not be included in the limitation period for claiming compensation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公民、法人和其他组织在执行异议、复议或者执行监督程序审查期间，就相关执行措施或者强制措施申请赔偿的，人民法院不予受理，已经受理的予以驳回，并告知其在上述程序终结后可以依照[本解释第五条](#art-5)的规定依法提出赔偿申请。  
公民、法人和其他组织在执行程序中未就相关执行措施、强制措施提出异议、申请复议或者申请执行监督，不影响其依法申请赔偿的权利。

**Article 6** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization applies for compensation in relation to relevant enforcement measures or compulsory measures during the review period of an enforcement objection, reconsideration, or enforcement supervision procedure, the People's Court shall not accept the application; if already accepted, it shall be dismissed, and the applicant shall be informed that after the conclusion of the above-mentioned procedures, it may file a compensation application according to law in accordance with Article 5 of this Interpretation.  
The failure of a citizen, legal person, or other organization to raise an objection, apply for reconsideration, or apply for enforcement supervision in respect of relevant enforcement measures or compulsory measures during the enforcement procedure shall not affect its right to apply for compensation according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经执行异议、复议或者执行监督程序作出的生效法律文书，对执行行为是否合法已有认定的，该生效法律文书可以作为人民法院赔偿委员会认定执行行为合法性的根据。  
赔偿请求人对执行行为的合法性提出相反主张，且提供相应证据予以证明的，人民法院赔偿委员会应当对执行行为进行合法性审查并作出认定。

**Article 7** Where an effective legal document made through the enforcement objection, reconsideration, or enforcement supervision procedure has made a determination on whether the enforcement act is lawful, such effective legal document may serve as the basis for the compensation committee of the People's Court to determine the legality of the enforcement act.  
Where the compensation claimant raises a contrary assertion regarding the legality of the enforcement act and provides corresponding evidence to prove it, the compensation committee of the People's Court shall conduct a legality review of the enforcement act and make a determination.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　根据当时有效的执行依据或者依法认定的基本事实作出的执行行为，不因下列情形而认定为错误执行：  
（一）采取执行措施或者强制措施后，据以执行的判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书被撤销或者变更的；  
（二）被执行人足以对抗执行的实体事由，系在执行措施完成后发生或者被依法确认的；  
（三）案外人对执行标的享有足以排除执行的实体权利，系在执行措施完成后经法定程序确认的；  
（四）人民法院作出准予执行行政行为的裁定并实施后，该行政行为被依法变更、撤销、确认违法或者确认无效的；  
（五）根据财产登记采取执行措施后，该登记被依法确认错误的；  
（六）执行依据或者基本事实嗣后改变的其他情形。

**Article 8** An enforcement act made on the basis of the enforcement basis in effect at the time or the basic facts legally determined shall not be deemed an erroneous enforcement due to any of the following circumstances:  
(1) after the adoption of enforcement measures or compulsory measures, the judgment, ruling, or other effective legal document on which the enforcement was based is revoked or modified;  
(2) the substantive grounds that entitle the party subject to enforcement to oppose enforcement occur or are legally confirmed after the completion of the enforcement measures;  
(3) the substantive right of a person not involved in the case to exclude enforcement of the subject matter is confirmed through legal procedures after the completion of the enforcement measures;  
(4) after the People's Court makes a ruling to permit enforcement of an administrative act and implements it, such administrative act is legally modified, revoked, confirmed illegal, or confirmed void;  
(5) after adopting enforcement measures based on property registration, such registration is legally confirmed to be erroneous;  
(6) any other circumstance where the enforcement basis or basic facts subsequently change.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　赔偿请求人应当对其主张的损害负举证责任。但因人民法院未列清单、列举不详等过错致使赔偿请求人无法就损害举证的，应当由人民法院对上述事实承担举证责任。  
双方主张损害的价值无法认定的，应当由负有举证责任的一方申请鉴定。负有举证责任的一方拒绝申请鉴定的，由其承担不利的法律后果；无法鉴定的，人民法院赔偿委员会应当结合双方的主张和在案证据，运用逻辑推理、日常生活经验等进行判断。

**Article 9** The compensation claimant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the damage claimed. However, if due to faults of the People's Court, such as failure to list an inventory or insufficient enumeration, the compensation claimant is unable to adduce evidence of the damage, the People's Court shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the above facts.  
Where the value of the damage claimed by both parties cannot be determined, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall apply for expert evaluation. If the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence refuses to apply for expert evaluation, it shall bear the adverse legal consequences; if expert evaluation is not possible, the compensation committee of the People's Court shall make a judgment based on the claims of both parties and the evidence on file, using logical reasoning and the common experience of daily life.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被执行人因财产权被侵犯依照[本解释第五条](#art-5)第一款规定申请赔偿，其债务尚未清偿的，获得的赔偿金应当首先用于清偿其债务。

**Article 10** Where the party subject to enforcement applies for compensation in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 5 of this Interpretation due to infringement of property rights, and its debts have not yet been satisfied, the compensation received shall first be used to satisfy its debts.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因错误执行取得不当利益且无法返还的，人民法院承担赔偿责任后，可以依据赔偿决定向取得不当利益的人追偿。  
因错误执行致使生效法律文书无法执行，申请执行人获得国家赔偿后申请继续执行的，不予支持。人民法院承担赔偿责任后，可以依据赔偿决定向被执行人追偿。

**Article 11** Where a person obtains unjustified benefits due to erroneous enforcement and cannot return them, after bearing compensation liability, the People's Court may, based on the compensation decision, seek recourse against the person who obtained the unjustified benefits.  
Where due to erroneous enforcement an effective legal document cannot be enforced, and the enforcement applicant who obtains state compensation applies for continued enforcement, such application shall not be supported. After bearing compensation liability, the People's Court may, based on the compensation decision, seek recourse against the party subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在执行过程中，因保管人或者第三人的行为侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益并造成损害的，应当由保管人或者第三人承担责任。但人民法院未尽监管职责的，应当在其能够防止或者制止损害发生、扩大的范围内承担相应的赔偿责任，并可以依据赔偿决定向保管人或者第三人追偿。

**Article 12** Where during the enforcement process, the acts of a custodian or a third party infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization and cause damage, the custodian or the third party shall bear liability. However, if the People's Court fails to fulfill its supervision duty, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability to the extent that it could have prevented or stopped the occurrence or aggravation of the damage, and may, based on the compensation decision, seek recourse against the custodian or the third party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　属于下列情形之一的，人民法院不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）申请执行人提供财产线索错误的；  
（二）执行措施系根据依法提供的担保而采取或者解除的；  
（三）人民法院工作人员实施与行使职权无关的个人行为的；  
（四）评估或者拍卖机构实施违法行为造成损害的；  
（五）因不可抗力、正当防卫或者紧急避险造成损害的；  
（六）依法不应由人民法院承担赔偿责任的其他情形。  
前款情形中，人民法院有错误执行行为的，应当根据其在损害发生过程和结果中所起的作用承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall not bear compensation liability in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the enforcement applicant provides incorrect property leads;  
(2) the enforcement measures are adopted or lifted based on security provided according to law;  
(3) a staff member of the People's Court engages in personal conduct unrelated to the exercise of functions and powers;  
(4) damage caused by an appraisal or auction institution through illegal acts;  
(5) damage caused by force majeure, justifiable defense, or act of rescue;  
(6) any other circumstance where the People's Court should not bear compensation liability according to law.  
In the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, if the People's Court has committed an erroneous enforcement act, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability based on the role it played in the occurrence and outcome of the damage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　错误执行造成公民、法人和其他组织利息、租金等实际损失的，适用[国家赔偿法第三十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-36)第八项的规定予以赔偿。

**Article 14** Where erroneous enforcement causes actual losses such as interest or rent to a citizen, legal person, or other organization, compensation shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of Item 8 of Article 36 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　侵犯公民、法人和其他组织的财产权，按照错误执行行为发生时的市场价格不足以弥补受害人损失或者该价格无法确定的，可以采用下列方式计算损失：  
（一）按照错误执行行为发生时的市场价格计算财产损失并支付利息，利息计算期间从错误执行行为实施之日起至赔偿决定作出之日止；  
（二）错误执行行为发生时的市场价格无法确定，或者因时间跨度长、市场价格波动大等因素按照错误执行行为发生时的市场价格计算显失公平的，可以参照赔偿决定作出时同类财产市场价格计算；  
（三）其他合理方式。

**Article 15** Where the property rights of a citizen, legal person, or other organization are infringed, and the market price at the time of the erroneous enforcement act is insufficient to compensate the victim's loss or such price cannot be determined, the loss may be calculated by the following methods:  
(1) calculating the property loss at the market price at the time of the erroneous enforcement act and paying interest, with the interest calculation period running from the date of the erroneous enforcement act to the date of the compensation decision;  
(2) if the market price at the time of the erroneous enforcement act cannot be determined, or if calculating at such price would be manifestly unfair due to factors such as a long time span or substantial market price fluctuations, the calculation may be made by reference to the market price of similar property at the time of the compensation decision;  
(3) any other reasonable method.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　错误执行造成受害人停产停业的，下列损失属于停产停业期间必要的经常性费用开支：  
（一）必要留守职工工资；  
（二）必须缴纳的税款、社会保险费；  
（三）应当缴纳的水电费、保管费、仓储费、承包费；  
（四）合理的房屋场地租金、设备租金、设备折旧费；  
（五）维系停产停业期间运营所需的其他基本开支。  
错误执行生产设备、用于营运的运输工具，致使受害人丧失唯一生活来源的，按照其实际损失予以赔偿。

**Article 16** Where erroneous enforcement causes the victim to suspend production or business operations, the following losses shall be considered necessary recurring expenses during the period of suspension:  
(1) wages of essential remaining employees;  
(2) taxes and social insurance premiums that must be paid;  
(3) utility fees, custody fees, warehousing fees, and contracting fees that shall be paid;  
(4) reasonable rent for premises and equipment, and equipment depreciation costs;  
(5) other basic expenses required to maintain operations during the period of suspension.  
Where erroneous enforcement of production equipment or means of transport used for operations causes the victim to lose the sole source of livelihood, compensation shall be made according to the actual loss.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　错误执行侵犯债权的，赔偿范围一般应当以债权标的额为限。债权受让人申请赔偿的，赔偿范围以其受让债权时支付的对价为限。

**Article 17** Where erroneous enforcement infringes upon a claim, the scope of compensation shall generally be limited to the amount of the claim. Where an assignee of a claim applies for compensation, the scope of compensation shall be limited to the consideration paid at the time of the assignment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违法采取保全措施的案件进入执行程序后，公民、法人和其他组织申请赔偿的，应当作为错误执行案件予以立案审查。

**Article 18** Where a case in which preservation measures were illegally taken enters the enforcement procedure, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization applies for compensation, it shall be docketed and examined as a case of erroneous enforcement.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　审理违法采取妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全、先予执行赔偿案件，可以参照适用本解释。

**Article 19** Where trying a compensation case involving illegal adoption of compulsory measures to hinder litigation, preservation, or advance execution, this Interpretation may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本解释自2022年3月1日起施行。施行前本院公布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 20** This Interpretation shall come into force as of March 1, 2022. Where any judicial interpretation previously published by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
