# 最高人民法院关于行政申请再审案件立案程序的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕18号  
**公布日期**：2016-11-21  
**生效日期**：2018-01-01

> 最高人民法院《关于行政申请再审案件立案程序的规定》已于2016年11月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1700次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年1月1日起施行。 2017年10月13日 最高人民法院 关于行政申请再审案件立案程序的规定 法释〔2017〕18号 为依法保障当事人申请再审权利，规范人民法院行政申请再审案件立案工作，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关规定，结合审判工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　再审申请应当符合以下条件： （一）再审申请人是生效裁判文书列明的当事人，或者其他因不能归责于本人的事由未被裁判文书列为当事人，但与行政行为有利害关系的公民、法人或者其他组织； （二）受理再审申请的法院是作出生效裁判的上一级人民法院； （三）申请再审的裁判属于行政诉讼法第九十条规定的生效裁判； （四）申请再审的事由属于行政诉讼法第九十一条规定的情形。 第二条　申请再审，有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予立案： （一）再审申请被驳回后再次提出申请的； （二）对再审判决、裁定提出申请的； （三）在人民检察院对当事人的申请作出不予提出检察建议或者抗诉决定后又提出申请的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　再审申请应当符合以下条件：  
（一）再审申请人是生效裁判文书列明的当事人，或者其他因不能归责于本人的事由未被裁判文书列为当事人，但与行政行为有利害关系的公民、法人或者其他组织；  
（二）受理再审申请的法院是作出生效裁判的上一级人民法院；  
（三）申请再审的裁判属于[行政诉讼法第九十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-90)规定的生效裁判；  
（四）申请再审的事由属于[行政诉讼法第九十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-91)规定的情形。

**Article 1** An application for retrial shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The applicant for retrial is a party listed in the effective judgment document, or any other citizen, legal person or other organization that, although not listed as a party in the judgment document due to reasons not attributable to itself, has an interest in the administrative action;  
(2) The court accepting the application for retrial is the people's court at the next higher level than the one that rendered the effective judgment;  
(3) The judgment for which retrial is applied falls within the scope of effective judgments as provided in Article 90 of the Administrative Procedure Law;  
(4) The grounds for applying for retrial fall within the circumstances provided in Article 91 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　申请再审，有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予立案：  
（一）再审申请被驳回后再次提出申请的；  
（二）对再审判决、裁定提出申请的；  
（三）在人民检察院对当事人的申请作出不予提出检察建议或者抗诉决定后又提出申请的；  
前款第一项、第二项规定情形，人民法院应当告知当事人可以向人民检察院申请检察建议或者抗诉。

**Article 2** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not docket an application for retrial:  
(1) The application is filed again after the previous application for retrial has been rejected;  
(2) The application is filed against a retrial judgment or ruling;  
(3) The application is filed after the People's Procuratorate has made a decision not to issue a procuratorial suggestion or not to lodge a protest in response to the party's application.  
For the circumstances specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall inform the party that it may apply to the People's Procuratorate for a procuratorial suggestion or protest.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　委托他人代为申请再审的，诉讼代理人应为下列人员：  
（一）律师、基层法律服务工作者；  
（二）当事人的近亲属或者工作人员；  
（三）当事人所在社区、单位以及有关社会团体推荐的公民。

**Article 3** Where a person is entrusted to apply for retrial on behalf of another, the litigation agent shall be one of the following persons:  
(1) A lawyer or a primary-level legal service worker;  
(2) A close relative or staff member of the party;  
(3) A citizen recommended by the community, the entity of the party, or a relevant social organization.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请再审，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）再审申请书，并按照被申请人及原审其他当事人的人数提交副本；  
（二）再审申请人是自然人的，应当提交身份证明复印件；再审申请人是法人或者其他组织的，应当提交营业执照复印件、组织机构代码证书复印件、法定代表人或者主要负责人身份证明；法人或者其他组织不能提供组织机构代码证书复印件的，应当提交情况说明；  
（三）委托他人代为申请再审的，应当提交授权委托书和代理人身份证明；  
（四）原审判决书、裁定书、调解书，或者与原件核对无异的复印件；  
（五）法律、法规规定需要提交的其他材料。

**Article 4** An application for retrial shall be filed with the following materials:  
(1) A written application for retrial, together with copies thereof in the number equal to the number of the respondent and other parties in the original trial;  
(2) If the applicant for retrial is a natural person, a photocopy of identity documents shall be submitted; if the applicant for retrial is a legal person or other organization, a photocopy of the business license, a photocopy of the organization code certificate, and the identity certificate of the legal representative or principal responsible person shall be submitted; if the legal person or other organization cannot provide a photocopy of the organization code certificate, a statement of explanation shall be submitted;  
(3) If a person is entrusted to apply for retrial on behalf of another, a power of attorney and the identity certificate of the agent shall be submitted;  
(4) The original judgment, ruling, or mediation document of the original trial, or photocopies verified to be identical with the originals;  
(5) Other materials required by laws and regulations to be submitted.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人申请再审，一般还应提交下列材料：  
（一）一审起诉状复印件、二审上诉状复印件；  
（二）在原审诉讼过程中提交的主要证据材料；  
（三）支持再审申请事由和再审请求的证据材料；  
（四）行政机关作出相关行政行为的证据材料；  
（五）其向行政机关提出申请，但行政机关不作为的相关证据材料；  
（六）认为需要提交的其他材料。

**Article 5** Where a party applies for retrial, he or it shall generally also submit the following materials:  
(1) A photocopy of the complaint of the first instance and a photocopy of the statement of appeal of the second instance;  
(2) The main evidentiary materials submitted during the original trial proceedings;  
(3) Evidentiary materials supporting the grounds for the application for retrial and the claims for retrial;  
(4) Evidentiary materials relating to the administrative action taken by the administrative organ;  
(5) Relevant evidentiary materials showing that a request was made to the administrative organ but the administrative organ failed to act;  
(6) Other materials that the party considers necessary to submit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　再审申请人提交再审申请书等材料时，应当填写送达地址确认书，并可同时附上相关材料的电子文本。

**Article 6** When submitting the application for retrial and other materials, the applicant for retrial shall fill in a confirmation of service address, and may at the same time attach the electronic text of the relevant materials.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　再审申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）再审申请人、被申请人及原审其他当事人的基本情况。当事人是自然人的，应列明姓名、性别、出生日期、民族、住址及有效联系电话、通讯地址；当事人是法人或者其他组织的，应列明名称、住所地和法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务及有效联系电话、通讯地址；  
（二）原审人民法院的名称，原审判决、裁定或者调解书的案号；  
（三）具体的再审请求；  
（四）申请再审的具体法定事由及事实、理由；  
（五）受理再审申请的人民法院名称；  
（六）再审申请人的签名、捺印或者盖章；  
（七）递交再审申请书的日期。

**Article 7** The written application for retrial shall specify the following particulars:  
(1) The basic information of the applicant for retrial, the respondent, and other parties in the original trial. If the party is a natural person, the name, gender, date of birth, ethnicity, address, valid contact telephone number, and correspondence address shall be stated; if the party is a legal person or other organization, the name, domicile, and the name, position, valid contact telephone number, and correspondence address of the legal representative or principal responsible person shall be stated;  
(2) The name of the original trial people's court and the case number of the original judgment, ruling, or mediation document;  
(3) The specific claims for retrial;  
(4) The specific statutory grounds for applying for retrial and the facts and reasons;  
(5) The name of the people's court accepting the application for retrial;  
(6) The signature, fingerprint, or seal of the applicant for retrial;  
(7) The date on which the written application for retrial is submitted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　再审申请人提交的再审申请书等材料符合上述要求的，人民法院应当出具《诉讼材料收取清单》，注明收到材料日期，并加盖专用收件章。《诉讼材料收取清单》一式两份，一份由人民法院入卷，一份由再审申请人留存。

**Article 8** Where the application for retrial and other materials submitted by the applicant for retrial comply with the above requirements, the People's Court shall issue a Receipt List of Litigation Materials, indicating the date on which the materials were received, and affix the special receipt seal. The Receipt List of Litigation Materials shall be made in duplicate, one copy to be placed in the file by the People's Court and one copy to be retained by the applicant for retrial.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　再审申请人提出的再审申请不符合本规定的，人民法院应当当场告知再审申请人。  
再审申请人提交的再审申请书等材料不符合要求的，人民法院应当将材料退回再审申请人，并一次性全面告知其在指定的合理期限内予以补正。再审申请人无正当理由逾期不予补正且仍坚持申请再审的，人民法院应当裁定驳回其再审申请。  
人民法院不得因再审申请人未提交[本规定第五条](#art-5)规定的相关材料，认定其提交的材料不符合要求。

**Article 9** If the application for retrial submitted by the applicant for retrial does not comply with these Provisions, the People's Court shall inform the applicant for retrial on the spot.  
If the application for retrial and other materials submitted by the applicant for retrial do not comply with the requirements, the People's Court shall return the materials to the applicant for retrial and fully inform it at one time of the corrections to be made within a specified reasonable period. If the applicant for retrial fails to make corrections within the specified period without justifiable reasons and still insists on applying for retrial, the People's Court shall rule to reject the application for retrial.  
The People's Court shall not deem the materials submitted by the applicant for retrial as not meeting the requirements solely because the applicant for retrial fails to submit the relevant materials specified in Article 5 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对符合上述条件的再审申请，人民法院应当及时立案，并应自收到符合条件的再审申请书等材料之日起五日内向再审申请人发送受理通知书，同时向被申请人及原审其他当事人发送应诉通知书、再审申请书副本及送达地址确认书。  
因通讯地址不详等原因，受理通知书、应诉通知书、再审申请书副本等材料未送达当事人的，不影响案件的审查。  
被申请人可以在收到再审申请书副本之日起十五日内向人民法院提出书面答辩意见，被申请人未提出书面答辩意见的，不影响人民法院审查。

**Article 10** For an application for retrial that meets the above conditions, the People's Court shall promptly docket the case and, within five days from the date of receipt of the qualified application for retrial and other materials, shall send a notice of acceptance to the applicant for retrial, and at the same time shall send a notice of response, a copy of the application for retrial, and a confirmation of service address to the respondent and other parties in the original trial.  
If the notice of acceptance, notice of response, copy of the application for retrial, and other materials are not served on the party due to reasons such as an inaccurate correspondence address, the examination of the case shall not be affected.  
The respondent may submit a written statement of defense to the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the copy of the application for retrial; the failure of the respondent to submit a written statement of defense shall not affect the examination by the People's Court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　再审申请人向原审人民法院申请再审或者越级申请再审的，原审人民法院或者有关上级人民法院应当告知其向作出生效裁判的人民法院的上一级法院提出。

**Article 11** If the applicant for retrial files an application for retrial with the original trial people's court or bypasses the intermediate level to file an application, the original trial people's court or the relevant higher people's court shall inform it to make the application to the court at the next higher level above the people's court that rendered the effective judgment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人申请再审，应当在判决、裁定、调解书发生法律效力后六个月内提出。  
申请再审期间为人民法院向当事人送达裁判文书之日起至再审申请人向上一级人民法院申请再审之日止。  
申请再审期间为不变期间，不适用中止、中断、延长的规定。  
再审申请人对2015年5月1日行政诉讼法实施前已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书申请再审的，人民法院依据最高人民法院《关于执行〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》第七十三条规定的2年确定申请再审的期间，但该期间在2015年10月31日尚未届满的，截止至2015年10月31日。

**Article 12** A party applying for retrial shall file the application within six months after the judgment, ruling, or mediation document takes legal effect.  
The period for applying for retrial runs from the date on which the People's Court serves the judgment document on the party to the date on which the applicant for retrial files the application with the people's court at the next higher level.  
The period for applying for retrial is a definite period and is not subject to suspension, interruption, or extension.  
If an applicant for retrial applies for retrial of a judgment, ruling, or mediation document that has already taken legal effect before the implementation of the Administrative Procedure Law on May 1, 2015, the People's Court shall determine the period for applying for retrial as two years in accordance with Article 73 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Implementation of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; provided, however, that if such period has not yet expired by October 31, 2015, it shall expire on October 31, 2015.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院认为再审申请不符合法定申请再审期间要求的，应当告知再审申请人。  
再审申请人认为未超过法定期间的，人民法院可以要求其在十日内提交生效裁判文书的送达回证复印件或其他能够证明裁判文书实际生效日期的相应证据材料。再审申请人拒不提交上述证明材料或逾期未提交，或者提交的证据材料不足以证明申请再审未超过法定期间的，人民法院裁定驳回再审申请。

**Article 13** If the People's Court considers that the application for retrial does not meet the statutory requirement concerning the period for applying for retrial, it shall inform the applicant for retrial.  
If the applicant for retrial considers that the statutory period has not been exceeded, the People's Court may require it to submit, within ten days, a photocopy of the service receipt of the effective judgment document or other corresponding evidentiary materials that can prove the actual date on which the judgment document took effect. If the applicant for retrial refuses to submit the aforesaid evidentiary materials or fails to submit them within the time limit, or if the submitted evidentiary materials are insufficient to prove that the application for retrial has not exceeded the statutory period, the People's Court shall rule to reject the application for retrial.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　再审申请人申请撤回再审申请，尚未立案的，人民法院退回已提交材料并记录在册；已经立案的，人民法院裁定是否准许撤回再审申请。人民法院准许撤回再审申请或者按撤回再审申请处理后，再审申请人再次申请再审的，人民法院不予立案，但有[行政诉讼法第九十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-91)第二项、第三项、第七项、第八项规定等情形，自知道或者应当知道之日起六个月内提出的除外。

**Article 14** If the applicant for retrial applies to withdraw the application for retrial and the case has not yet been docketed, the People's Court shall return the submitted materials and record the matter in the register; if the case has already been docketed, the People's Court shall rule on whether to permit the withdrawal of the application for retrial. After the People's Court permits the withdrawal of the application for retrial or treats the matter as a withdrawal of the application for retrial, if the applicant for retrial again applies for retrial, the People's Court shall not docket the case, unless the circumstances specified in items (2), (3), (7), and (8) of Article 91 of the Administrative Procedure Law exist and the application is filed within six months from the date on which the applicant knew or should have known of such circumstances.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定自2018年1月1日起施行，最高人民法院以前发布的有关规定与本规定不符的，按照本规定执行。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force on January 1, 2018. In the event of any inconsistency between these Provisions and the relevant provisions previously issued by the Supreme People's Court, these Provisions shall prevail.
