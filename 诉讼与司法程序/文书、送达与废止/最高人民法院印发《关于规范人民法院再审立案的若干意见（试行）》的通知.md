# 最高人民法院印发《关于规范人民法院再审立案的若干意见（试行）》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔2002〕13号  
**公布日期**：2002-09-10  
**生效日期**：2002-11-01

> 《最高人民法院关于规范人民法院再审立案的若干意见（试行）》已经最高人民法院审判委员会第1230次会议讨论通过，现印发给你们试行，请认真遵照执行。 附： 最高人民法院 关于规范人民法院再审立案的 若干意见（试行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　各级人民法院、专门人民法院对本院或者上级人民法院对下级人民法院作出的终审裁判，经复查认为符合再审立案条件的，应当决定或裁定再审。  
人民检察院依照法律规定对人民法院作出的终审裁判提出抗诉的，应当再审立案。

**Article 1** People's courts at all levels and special people's courts shall, upon review of a final judgment or ruling rendered by themselves or by a higher people's court on a lower people's court, decide or order a retrial if it is deemed to meet the conditions for case filing for retrial.  
Where the People's Procuratorate files a protest against a final judgment or ruling rendered by a people's court in accordance with the law, the case shall be filed for retrial.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地方各级人民法院、专门人民法院负责下列案件的再审立案：  
（一）本院作出的终审裁判，符合再审立案条件的；  
（二）下一级人民法院复查驳回或者再审改判，符合再审立案条件的；  
（三）上级人民法院指令再审的；  
（四）人民检察院依法提出抗诉的。

**Article 2** Local people's courts at all levels and special people's courts shall be responsible for the case filing for retrial of the following cases:  
(1) A final judgment or ruling rendered by themselves that meets the conditions for case filing for retrial;  
(2) A case where a lower people's court has rejected the petition upon review or altered the judgment upon retrial, and the case meets the conditions for case filing for retrial;  
(3) A case where a higher people's court orders a retrial;  
(4) A case where the People's Procuratorate files a protest according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　最高人民法院负责下列案件的再审立案：  
（一）本院作出的终审裁判，符合再审立案条件的；  
（二）高级人民法院复查驳回或者再审改判，符合再审立案条件的；  
（三）最高人民检察院依法提出抗诉的；  
（四）最高人民法院认为应由自己再审的。

**Article 3** The Supreme People's Court shall be responsible for the case filing for retrial of the following cases:  
(1) A final judgment or ruling rendered by itself that meets the conditions for case filing for retrial;  
(2) A case where a Higher People's Court has rejected the petition upon review or altered the judgment upon retrial, and the case meets the conditions for case filing for retrial;  
(3) A case where the Supreme People's Procuratorate files a protest according to law;  
(4) A case which the Supreme People's Court considers should be retried by itself.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　上级人民法院对下级人民法院作出的终审裁判，认为确有必要的，可以直接立案复查，经复查认为符合再审立案条件的，可以决定或裁定再审。

**Article 4** Where a higher people's court deems it truly necessary with respect to a final judgment or ruling rendered by a lower people's court, it may directly file the case for review, and if upon review it finds that the conditions for case filing for retrial are met, it may decide or order a retrial.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　再审申请人或申诉人向人民法院申请再审或申诉，应当提交以下材料：  
（一）再审申请书或申诉状，应当载明当事人的基本情况、申请再审或申诉的事实与理由；  
（二）原一、二审判决书、裁定书等法律文书，经过人民法院复查或再审的，应当附有驳回通知书、再审判决书或裁定书；  
（三）以有新的证据证明原裁判认定的事实确有错误为由申请再审或申诉的，应当同时附有证据目录、证人名单和主要证据复印件或者照片；需要人民法院调查取证的，应当附有证据线索。  
申请再审或申诉不符合前款规定的，人民法院不予审查。

**Article 5** An applicant for retrial or a petitioner who applies to a people's court for retrial or files a petition shall submit the following materials:  
(1) A written application for retrial or a petition, which shall state the basic information of the parties and the facts and grounds for the application for retrial or the petition;  
(2) The original judgment or ruling of first instance or second instance and other legal documents; if the case has been reviewed or retried by a people's court, the notice of dismissal or the judgment or ruling of retrial shall be attached;  
(3) If the application for retrial or the petition is based on the existence of new evidence proving that the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling are indeed erroneous, a list of evidence, a list of witnesses, and copies or photographs of the main evidence shall be attached; if it is necessary for the people's court to investigate and collect evidence, the clues to evidence shall be attached.  
If the application for retrial or the petition does not comply with the preceding paragraph, the people's court shall not review it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请再审或申诉一般由终审人民法院审查处理。  
上一级人民法院对未经终审人民法院审查处理的申请再审或申诉，一般交终审人民法院审查；对经终审人民法院审查处理后仍坚持申请再审或申诉的，应当受理。  
对未经终审人民法院及其上一级人民法院审查处理，直接向上级人民法院申请再审或申诉的，上级人民法院应当交下一级人民法院处理。

**Article 6** An application for retrial or a petition shall generally be reviewed and handled by the people's court that rendered the final judgment.  
If an application for retrial or a petition has not been reviewed and handled by the people's court that rendered the final judgment, the next higher people's court shall generally refer it to the court that rendered the final judgment for review; if the applicant for retrial or the petitioner still persists in the application for retrial or the petition after review and handling by the court that rendered the final judgment, the next higher people's court shall accept it.  
If an application for retrial or a petition is directly submitted to a higher people's court without being reviewed and handled by the people's court that rendered the final judgment or its next higher people's court, the higher people's court shall refer it to the next lower people's court for handling.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对终审刑事裁判的申诉，具备下列情形之一的，人民法院应当决定再审：  
（一）有审判时未收集到的或者未被采信的证据，可能推翻原定罪量刑的；  
（二）主要证据不充分或者不具有证明力的；  
（三）原裁判的主要事实依据被依法变更或撤销的；  
（四）据以定罪量刑的主要证据自相矛盾的；  
（五）引用法律条文错误或者违反[刑法第十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-12)的规定适用失效法律的；  
（六）违反法律关于溯及力规定的；  
（七）量刑明显不当的；  
（八）审判程序不合法，影响案件公正裁判的；  
（九）审判人员在审理案件时索贿受贿、徇私舞弊并导致枉法裁判的。

**Article 7** For a petition against a final criminal judgment or ruling, the people's court shall decide to conduct a retrial if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) There is evidence that was not collected or was not admitted during the trial, which may overturn the original conviction and sentencing;  
(2) The main evidence is insufficient or lacks probative force;  
(3) The main factual basis of the original judgment or ruling has been altered or revoked according to law;  
(4) The main evidence on which the conviction and sentencing were based is self-contradictory;  
(5) The citation of legal provisions is erroneous, or the law that has ceased to have effect is applied in violation of the provisions of Article 12 of the Criminal Law;  
(6) The provisions of the law concerning retroactive effect are violated;  
(7) The sentencing is manifestly inappropriate;  
(8) The trial procedure is illegal, affecting the fair adjudication of the case;  
(9) The judicial officer engaged in soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, and thereby caused an unjust judgment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对终审民事裁判、调解的再审申请，具备下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定再审：  
（一）有再审申请人以前不知道或举证不能的证据，可能推翻原裁判的；  
（二）主要证据不充分或者不具有证明力的；  
（三）原裁判的主要事实依据被依法变更或撤销的；  
（四）就同一法律事实或同一法律关系，存在两个相互矛盾的生效法律文书，再审申请人对后一生效法律文书提出再审申请的；  
（五）引用法律条文错误或者适用失效、尚未生效法律的；  
（六）违反法律关于溯及力规定的；  
（七）调解协议明显违反自愿原则，内容违反法律或者损害国家利益、公共利益和他人利益的；  
（八）审判程序不合法，影响案件公正裁判的；  
（九）审判人员在审理案件时索贿受贿、徇私舞弊并导致枉法裁判的。

**Article 8** For an application for retrial against a final civil judgment, ruling, or mediation, the people's court shall order a retrial if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) There is evidence that the applicant for retrial was previously unaware of or could not adduce, which may overturn the original judgment or ruling;  
(2) The main evidence is insufficient or lacks probative force;  
(3) The main factual basis of the original judgment or ruling has been altered or revoked according to law;  
(4) There are two conflicting effective legal instruments concerning the same legal fact or the same legal relationship, and the applicant for retrial files an application for retrial against the later effective legal instrument;  
(5) The citation of legal provisions is erroneous, or a law that has ceased to have effect or has not yet taken effect is applied;  
(6) The provisions of the law concerning retroactive effect are violated;  
(7) The mediation agreement manifestly violates the principle of voluntariness, its content violates the law, or harms state interests, public interests, or the interests of others;  
(8) The trial procedure is illegal, affecting the fair adjudication of the case;  
(9) The judicial officer engaged in soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, and thereby caused an unjust judgment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对终审行政裁判的申诉，具备下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定再审：  
（一）依法应当受理而不予受理或驳回起诉的；  
（二）有新的证据可能改变原裁判的；  
（三）主要证据不充分或不具有证明力的；  
（四）原裁判的主要事实依据被依法变更或撤销的；  
（五）引用法律条文错误或者适用失效、尚未生效法律的；  
（六）违反法律关于溯及力规定的；  
（七）行政赔偿调解协议违反自愿原则，内容违反法律或损害国家利益、公共利益和他人利益的；  
（八）审判程序不合法，影响案件公正裁判的；  
（九）审判人员在审理案件时索贿受贿、徇私舞弊并导致枉法裁判的。

**Article 9** For a petition against a final administrative judgment or ruling, the people's court shall order a retrial if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) The case should have been accepted according to law but was not accepted or the prosecution was dismissed;  
(2) There is new evidence that may change the original judgment or ruling;  
(3) The main evidence is insufficient or lacks probative force;  
(4) The main factual basis of the original judgment or ruling has been altered or revoked according to law;  
(5) The citation of legal provisions is erroneous, or a law that has ceased to have effect or has not yet taken effect is applied;  
(6) The provisions of the law concerning retroactive effect are violated;  
(7) The administrative compensation mediation agreement violates the principle of voluntariness, its content violates the law, or harms state interests, public interests, or the interests of others;  
(8) The trial procedure is illegal, affecting the fair adjudication of the case;  
(9) The judicial officer engaged in soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, and thereby caused an unjust judgment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院对刑事案件的申诉人在刑罚执行完毕后两年内提出的申诉，应当受理；超过两年提出申诉，具有下列情形之一的，应当受理：  
（一）可能对原审被告人宣告无罪的；  
（二）原审被告人在本条规定的期限内向人民法院提出申诉，人民法院未受理的；  
（三）属于疑难、复杂、重大案件的。  
不符合前款规定的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 10** A people's court shall accept a petition filed by a petitioner in a criminal case within two years after the execution of the criminal penalty is completed; if the petition is filed after more than two years, it shall be accepted if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) The original defendant may be declared not guilty;  
(2) The original defendant filed a petition with the people's court within the time limit specified in this Article, but the people's court did not accept it;  
(3) The case is of a doubtful, complicated, or major nature.  
If the preceding paragraph is not complied with, the people's court shall not accept the petition.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院对刑事附带民事案件中仅就民事部分提出申诉的，一般不予再审立案。但有证据证明民事部分明显失当且原审被告人有赔偿能力的除外。

**Article 11** For a petition in a criminal case with a civil action attached that is filed only against the civil part, a people's court shall generally not file the case for retrial. However, this shall not apply where there is evidence proving that the civil part is manifestly improper and the original defendant has the capacity to make compensation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院对民事、行政案件的再审申请人或申诉人超过两年提出再审申请或申诉的，不予受理。

**Article 12** A people's court shall not accept an application for retrial or a petition filed by an applicant for retrial or a petitioner in a civil or administrative case after more than two years.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院对不符合法定主体资格的再审申请或申诉，不予受理。

**Article 13** A people's court shall not accept an application for retrial or a petition filed by a person who does not meet the statutory qualification as a subject.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院对下列民事案件的再审申请不予受理：  
（一）人民法院依照督促程序、公示催告程序和破产还债程序审理的案件；  
（二）人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决和裁定不予执行仲裁裁决的案件；  
（三）人民法院判决、调解解除婚姻关系的案件，但当事人就财产分割问题申请再审的除外。

**Article 14** : The People's Court shall not accept applications for retrial of the following civil cases:  
(1) Cases tried by the People's Court in accordance with the summary procedure for a payment order, the procedure for public summons for exhortation, and the bankruptcy and debt repayment procedure;  
(2) Cases where the People's Court has rendered a ruling to set aside an arbitration award or a ruling to refuse enforcement of an arbitration award;  
(3) Cases where the People's Court has rendered a judgment or mediation to dissolve the marital relationship, except where a party applies for retrial concerning the division of property.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　上级人民法院对经终审法院的上一级人民法院依照审判监督程序审理后维持原判或者经两级人民法院依照审判监督程序复查均驳回的申请再审或申诉案件，一般不予受理。  
但再审申请人或申诉人提出新的理由，且符合[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-204)、[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)、[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第六十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-62)及本规定第七、八、九条规定条件的，以及刑事案件的原审被告人可能被宣告无罪的除外。

**Article 15** A higher people's court shall generally not accept an application for retrial or a petition where the next higher people's court of the court that rendered the final judgment has, after trial under the trial supervision procedure, affirmed the original judgment, or where the courts at two levels have both rejected the application upon review under the trial supervision procedure.  
However, this shall not apply where the applicant for retrial or the petitioner raises new grounds and meets the requirements under Article 204 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 179 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 62 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and Articles 7, 8, and 9 of these Provisions, as well as where the original defendant in a criminal case may be declared not guilty.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　最高人民法院再审裁判或者复查驳回的案件，再审申请人或申诉人仍不服提出再审申请或申诉的，不予受理。

**Article 16** Where an applicant for retrial or a petitioner is still dissatisfied with a case that has been retried or rejected upon review by the Supreme People's Court and files an application for retrial or a petition, the Supreme People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本意见自2002年11月1日起施行。以前有关再审立案的规定与本意见不一致的，按本意见执行。

**Article 17** These Opinions shall come into force as of November 1, 2002. Where previous provisions on case filing for retrial are inconsistent with these Opinions, these Opinions shall prevail.
