# 最高人民法院关于人民法院庭审录音录像的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕5号  
**公布日期**：2017-01-25  
**生效日期**：2017-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院庭审录音录像的若干规定》已于2017年1月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1708次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年3月1日起施行。 2017年2月22日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院庭审录音录像的若干规定 法释〔2017〕5号 为保障诉讼参与人诉讼权利，规范庭审活动，提高庭审效率，深化司法公开，促进司法公正，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院开庭审判案件，应当对庭审活动进行全程录音录像。 第二条　人民法院应当在法庭内配备固定或者移动的录音录像设备。 有条件的人民法院可以在法庭安装使用智能语音识别同步转换文字系统。 第三条　庭审录音录像应当自宣布开庭时开始，至闭庭时结束。除下列情形外，庭审录音录像不得人为中断： （一）休庭； （二）公开庭审中的不公开举证、质证活动； （三）不宜录制的调解活动。 负责录音录像的人员应当对录音录像的起止时间、有无中断等情况进行记录并附卷。 第四条　人民法院应当采取叠加同步录制时间或者其他措施保证庭审录音录像的真实和完整。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院开庭审判案件，应当对庭审活动进行全程录音录像。

**Article 1** : When a People's Court opens a court session to adjudicate a case, it shall make a full audio and video recording of the court proceedings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院应当在法庭内配备固定或者移动的录音录像设备。  
有条件的人民法院可以在法庭安装使用智能语音识别同步转换文字系统。

**Article 2** : A People's Court shall be equipped with fixed or mobile audio and video recording equipment in the courtroom.  
A People's Court with the necessary conditions may install and use an intelligent speech recognition system for synchronous conversion into text in the courtroom.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　庭审录音录像应当自宣布开庭时开始，至闭庭时结束。除下列情形外，庭审录音录像不得人为中断：  
（一）休庭；  
（二）公开庭审中的不公开举证、质证活动；  
（三）不宜录制的调解活动。  
负责录音录像的人员应当对录音录像的起止时间、有无中断等情况进行记录并附卷。

**Article 3** : The audio and video recording of a court session shall commence when the court session is declared open and end when the court session is adjourned. Except for the following circumstances, the audio and video recording of a court session shall not be manually interrupted:  
(1) during a recess;  
(2) non-public presentation of evidence and cross-examination during a public trial;  
(3) mediation activities that are not suitable for recording.  
The person responsible for the recording shall record the start and end times of the recording, whether there are any interruptions, and other relevant information, and attach such records to the case file.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当采取叠加同步录制时间或者其他措施保证庭审录音录像的真实和完整。  
因设备故障或技术原因导致录音录像不真实、不完整的，负责录音录像的人员应当作出书面说明，经审判长或独任审判员审核签字后附卷。

**Article 4** : A People's Court shall adopt measures such as superimposing the synchronized recording time or other measures to ensure the authenticity and integrity of the audio and video recording of the court session.  
If the audio and video recording is not authentic or complete due to equipment malfunction or technical reasons, the person responsible for the recording shall make a written explanation, which shall be attached to the case file after being reviewed and signed by the presiding judge or the sole adjudicator.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院应当使用专门设备在线或离线存储、备份庭审录音录像。因设备故障等原因导致不符合技术标准的录音录像，应当一并存储。  
庭审录音录像的归档，按照人民法院电子诉讼档案管理规定执行。

**Article 5** : A People's Court shall use specialized equipment to store and back up the audio and video recording of the court session online or offline. Audio and video recordings that do not meet the technical standards due to equipment malfunction or other reasons shall also be stored together.  
The archiving of audio and video recordings of court sessions shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions on the management of electronic litigation records of the People's Court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院通过使用智能语音识别系统同步转换生成的庭审文字记录，经审判人员、书记员、诉讼参与人核对签字后，作为法庭笔录管理和使用。

**Article 6** : The written records of the court session generated by the People's Court through the use of an intelligent speech recognition system for synchronous conversion, after being checked and signed by the adjudicators, court clerks, and litigation participants, shall be managed and used as the court record.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　诉讼参与人对法庭笔录有异议并申请补正的，书记员可以播放庭审录音录像进行核对、补正；不予补正的，应当将申请记录在案。

**Article 7** : If a litigation participant raises an objection to the court record and requests a correction, the court clerk may play the audio and video recording of the court session to check and make corrections; if the correction is not made, the request shall be recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　适用简易程序审理民事案件的庭审录音录像，经当事人同意的，可以替代法庭笔录。

**Article 8** : For civil cases tried under summary procedure, the audio and video recording of the court session, with the consent of the parties, may replace the court record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院应当将替代法庭笔录的庭审录音录像同步保存在服务器或者刻录成光盘，并由当事人和其他诉讼参与人对其完整性校验值签字或者采取其他方法进行确认。

**Article 9** : The People's Court shall save the audio and video recording of the court session that replaces the court record synchronously on a server or burn it onto a CD, and the parties and other litigation participants shall sign the integrity check value or take other methods to confirm it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院应当通过审判流程信息公开平台、诉讼服务平台以及其他便民诉讼服务平台，为当事人、辩护律师、诉讼代理人等依法查阅庭审录音录像提供便利。  
对提供查阅的录音录像，人民法院应当设置必要的安全防范措施。

**Article 10** : A People's Court shall, through the trial procedure information disclosure platform, litigation service platform, and other convenient litigation service platforms, facilitate the access to the audio and video recording of the court session by the parties, defense lawyers, litigation agents, etc., in accordance with law.  
For the audio and video recordings provided for access, the People's Court shall set up necessary security measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人、辩护律师、诉讼代理人等可以依照规定复制录音或者誊录庭审录音录像，必要时人民法院应当配备相应设施。

**Article 11** : The parties, defense lawyers, litigation agents, etc., may, in accordance with the provisions, copy the audio or transcribe the audio and video recording of the court session. When necessary, the People's Court shall provide corresponding facilities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院可以播放依法公开审理案件的庭审录音录像。

**Article 12** : A People's Court may play the audio and video recording of cases tried openly in accordance with law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　诉讼参与人、旁听人员违反法庭纪律或者有关法律规定，危害法庭安全、扰乱法庭秩序的，人民法院可以通过庭审录音录像进行调查核实，并将其作为追究法律责任的证据。

**Article 13** : If litigation participants or observers violate courtroom discipline or relevant legal provisions, endanger courtroom safety, or disrupt courtroom order, the People's Court may investigate and verify through the audio and video recording of the court session and use it as evidence for pursuing legal liability.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民检察院、诉讼参与人认为庭审活动不规范或者违反法律规定的，人民法院应当结合庭审录音录像进行调查核实。

**Article 14** : If the People's Procuratorate or litigation participants consider that the court proceedings are not standard or violate legal provisions, the People's Court shall conduct investigation and verification in conjunction with the audio and video recording of the court session.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　未经人民法院许可，任何人不得对庭审活动进行录音录像，不得对庭审录音录像进行拍录、复制、删除和迁移。  
行为人实施前款行为的，依照规定追究其相应责任。

**Article 15** : Without the permission of the People's Court, no one may make an audio or video recording of the court proceedings, nor may they photograph, record, copy, delete, or transfer the audio and video recording of the court session.  
If a person commits the acts mentioned in the preceding paragraph, their corresponding liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私等庭审活动的录制，以及对庭审录音录像的存储、查阅、复制、誊录等，应当符合保密管理等相关规定。

**Article 16** : The recording of court proceedings involving state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, etc., as well as the storage, access, copying, transcription, etc., of the audio and video recording of the court session, shall comply with relevant provisions such as confidentiality management.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　庭审录音录像涉及的相关技术保障、技术标准和技术规范，由最高人民法院另行制定。

**Article 17** : The relevant technical support, technical standards, and technical specifications concerning the audio and video recording of court sessions shall be separately formulated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院从事其他审判活动或者进行执行、听证、接访等活动需要进行录音录像的，参照本规定执行。

**Article 18** : If a People's Court needs to make audio and video recordings for other judicial activities or for activities such as enforcement, hearings, or receiving visitors, these Provisions shall be followed by reference.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定自2017年3月1日起施行。最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释及规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 19** : These Provisions shall come into force as of March 1, 2017. If any judicial interpretations and normative documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
