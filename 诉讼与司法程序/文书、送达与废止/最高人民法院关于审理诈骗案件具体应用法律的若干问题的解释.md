# 最高人民法院关于审理诈骗案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕32号  
**公布日期**：1996-12-16

---

### 正文

一、根据《刑法》第一百五十一条和第一百五十二条的规定，诈骗公私财物数额较大的，构成诈骗罪。  
个人诈骗公私财物2000元以上的，属于“数额较大”；个人诈骗公私财物3万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”。  
个人诈骗公私财物20万元以上的，属于诈骗数额特别巨大。诈骗数额特别巨大是认定诈骗犯罪“情节特别严重”的一个重要内容，但不是唯一情节。诈骗数额在10万元以上，又具有下列情形之一的，也应认定为“情节特别严重”：  
（1）诈骗集团的首要分子或者共同诈骗犯罪中情节严重的主犯；  
（2）惯犯或者流窜作案危害严重的；  
（3）诈骗法人、其他组织或者个人急需的生产资料，严重影响生产或者造成其他严重损失的；  
（4）诈骗救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、救济、医疗款物，造成严重后果的；  
（5）挥霍诈骗的财物，致使诈骗的财物无法返还的；  
（6）使用诈骗的财物进行违法犯罪活动的；  
（7）曾因诈骗受过刑事处罚的；  
（8）导致被害人死亡、精神失常或者其他严重后果的；  
（9）具有其他严重情节的。  
单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员以单位名义实施诈骗行为，诈骗所得归单位所有，数额在5万至10万元以上的，应当依照《刑法》第一百五十一条的规定追究上述人员的刑事责任；数额在20万至30万元以上的，依照《刑法》第一百五十二条的规定追究上述人员的刑事责任。  
对共同诈骗犯罪，应当以行为人参与共同诈骗的数额认定其犯罪数额，并结合行为人在共同犯罪中的地位、作用和非法所得数额等情节依法处罚。  
已经着手实行诈骗行为，只是由于行为人意志以外的原因而未获取财物的，是诈骗未遂。诈骗未遂，情节严重的，也应当定罪并依法处罚。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在“2000元至4000元”、“3万元至5万元”的幅度内，分别确定本地区执行的个人诈骗“数额较大”、“数额巨大”，以及单位实施诈骗，追究有关人员刑事责任，参照本条第四款规定的数额，确定适用《刑法》第一百五十一条或者第一百五十二条的具体数额标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article None** I. According to the provisions of Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law, whoever defrauds public or private property in a relatively large amount shall constitute the crime of fraud.  
Where an individual defrauds public or private property of 2,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where an individual defrauds public or private property of 30,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount".  
Where an individual defrauds public or private property of 200,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a particularly huge amount of fraud. A particularly huge amount of fraud is an important element for determining that the circumstances of a fraud crime are "especially serious", but it is not the only element. Where the amount of fraud is 100,000 yuan or more and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall also be determined as "especially serious circumstances":  
(1) The ringleader of a fraud group or a principal offender with serious circumstances in a joint fraud crime;  
(2) A habitual offender or a person who commits crimes by roaming and causes serious harm;  
(3) Defrauding means of production urgently needed by a legal person, other organization, or individual, seriously affecting production or causing other serious losses;  
(4) Defrauding funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, social relief, or medical treatment, causing serious consequences;  
(5) Squandering the defrauded property, rendering it impossible to return;  
(6) Using the defrauded property to engage in illegal or criminal activities;  
(7) Having previously received criminal punishment for fraud;  
(8) Causing the death, mental disorder, or other serious consequences of the victim;  
(9) Having other serious circumstances.  
Where the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel of a unit, in the name of the unit, commit fraudulent acts, and the proceeds of fraud belong to the unit, if the amount is 50,000 to 100,000 yuan or more, the criminal liability of the above-mentioned personnel shall be investigated according to the provisions of Article 151 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is 200,000 to 300,000 yuan or more, the criminal liability of the above-mentioned personnel shall be investigated according to the provisions of Article 152 of the Criminal Law.  
For a joint fraud crime, the amount of the crime shall be determined based on the amount in which the actor participated in the joint fraud, and the punishment shall be imposed according to law in light of the actor's position, role, and amount of illegal gains in the joint crime.  
Where the fraudulent act has already been initiated but no property is obtained due to reasons beyond the actor's will, it shall constitute an attempted fraud. An attempted fraud with serious circumstances shall also be convicted and punished according to law.  
The Higher People's Courts of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the economic development of their respective regions and taking into account the public security situation, respectively determine the specific amount standards for "relatively large amount" and "huge amount" of individual fraud implemented in their regions, as well as the specific amount standards for applying Article 151 or Article 152 of the Criminal Law for unit fraud and investigating the criminal liability of relevant persons by reference to the amount specified in paragraph 4 of this Article, within the ranges of "2,000 to 4,000 yuan" and "30,000 to 50,000 yuan", and shall report to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.

二、根据《刑法》第一百五十一条和第一百五十二条的规定，利用经济合同诈骗他人财物数额较大的，构成诈骗罪。  
利用经济合同进行诈骗的，诈骗数额应当以行为人实际骗取的数额认定，合同标的数额可以作为量刑情节予以考虑。  
行为人具有下列情形之一的，应认定其行为属于以非法占有为目的，利用经济合同进行诈骗：  
（一）明知没有履行合同的能力或者有效的担保，采取下列欺骗手段与他人签订合同，骗取财物数额较大并造成较大损失的：  
1、虚构主体；  
2、冒用他人名义；  
3、使用伪造、变造或者无效的单据、介绍信、印章或者其他证明文件的；  
4、隐瞒真相，使用明知不能兑现的票据或者其他结算凭证作为合同履行担保的；  
5、隐瞒真相，使用明知不符合担保条件的抵押物、债权文书等作为合同履行担保的；  
6、使用其他欺骗手段使对方交付款、物的。  
（二）合同签订后携带对方当事人交付的货物、货款、预付款或者定金、保证金等担保合同履行的财产逃跑的；  
（三）挥霍对方当事人交付的货物、货款、预付款或者定金、保证金等担保合同履行的财产，致使上述款物无法返还的；  
（四）使用对方当事人交付的货物、货款、预付款或者定金、保证金等担保合同履行的财产进行违法犯罪活动，致使上述款物无法返还的；  
（五）隐匿合同货物、货款、预付款或者定金、保证金等担保合同履行的财产，拒不返还的；  
（六）合同签订后，以支付部分货款，开始履行合同为诱饵，骗取全部货物后，在合同规定的期限内或者双方另行约定的付款期限内，无正当理由拒不支付其余货款的。

**Article None** II. According to the provisions of Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law, whoever uses an economic contract to defraud another person of property in a relatively large amount shall constitute the crime of fraud.  
Where fraud is committed by using an economic contract, the amount of fraud shall be determined based on the amount actually defrauded by the actor, and the amount of the contract subject matter may be considered as a circumstance for sentencing.  
Where an actor has any of the following circumstances, his act shall be determined as using an economic contract to commit fraud for the purpose of illegal possession:  
(1) Knowing that he has no ability to perform the contract or no valid guarantee, he uses the following deceptive means to enter into a contract with another person, defrauding property in a relatively large amount and causing relatively large losses:  
1. Fabricating a subject;  
2. Using another person's name without authorization;  
3. Using forged, altered, or invalid documents, letters of introduction, seals, or other certification documents;  
4. Concealing the truth and using instruments or other settlement vouchers known to be non-negotiable as guarantees for contract performance;  
5. Concealing the truth and using collateral, debt instruments, etc., known not to meet the guarantee conditions as guarantees for contract performance;  
6. Using other deceptive means to cause the other party to deliver money or goods.  
(2) After the contract is signed, absconding with the goods,货款, prepayment, deposit, bond, or other property delivered by the other party to guarantee contract performance;  
(3) Squandering the goods,货款, prepayment, deposit, bond, or other property delivered by the other party to guarantee contract performance, rendering the aforesaid money and property unable to be returned;  
(4) Using the goods,货款, prepayment, deposit, bond, or other property delivered by the other party to guarantee contract performance to engage in illegal or criminal activities, rendering the aforesaid money and property unable to be returned;  
(5) Concealing the contractual goods,货款, prepayment, deposit, bond, or other property to guarantee contract performance and refusing to return it;  
(6) After the contract is signed, using partial payment of货款 and commencement of contract performance as a lure to defraud all the goods, then without justifiable reasons refusing to pay the remaining货款 within the period stipulated in the contract or within the payment period otherwise agreed by both parties.

三、根据《决定》第八条规定，以非法占有为目的，使用诈骗方法非法集资的，构成集资诈骗罪。  
“诈骗方法”是指行为人采取虚构集资用途，以虚假的证明文件和高回报率为诱饵，骗取集资款的手段。  
“非法集资”是指法人、其他组织或者个人，未经有权机关批准，向社会公众募集资金的行为。行为人实施《决定》第八条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定其行为属于“以非法占有为目的，使用诈骗方法非法集资”：  
（1）携带集资款逃跑的；  
（2）挥霍集资款，致使集资款无法返还的；  
（3）使用集资款进行违法犯罪活动，致使集资款无法返还的；  
（4）具有其他欺诈行为，拒不返还集资款，或者致使集资款无法返还的。  
个人进行集资诈骗数额在20万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；个人进行集资诈骗数额在100万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
单位进行集资诈骗数额在50万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；单位进行集资诈骗数额在250万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。

**Article None** III. According to the provisions of Article 8 of the Decision, whoever illegally raises funds by fraudulent means for the purpose of illegal possession shall constitute the crime of fund-raising fraud.  
"Fraudulent means" refers to the methods by which an actor fabricates the purpose of fund-raising and uses false certification documents and high rates of return as bait to defraud funds.  
"Illegal fund-raising" refers to the act of a legal person, other organization, or individual, without approval from the competent authority, raising funds from the public. Where an actor commits the acts specified in Article 8 of the Decision and has any of the following circumstances, his act shall be determined as "illegally raising funds by fraudulent means for the purpose of illegal possession":  
(1) Absconding with the raised funds;  
(2) Squandering the raised funds, rendering them unable to be returned;  
(3) Using the raised funds to engage in illegal or criminal activities, rendering them unable to be returned;  
(4) Having other fraudulent acts and refusing to return the raised funds, or causing them to be unable to be returned.  
Where an individual commits fund-raising fraud with an amount of 200,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where an individual commits fund-raising fraud with an amount of 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Where a unit commits fund-raising fraud with an amount of 500,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where a unit commits fund-raising fraud with an amount of 2,500,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".

四、根据《决定》第十条规定，以非法占有为目的，诈骗银行或者其他金融机构的贷款，数额较大的，构成贷款诈骗罪。  
《决定》第十条规定的“其他严重情节”是指：  
（1）为骗取贷款，向银行或者金融机构的工作人员行贿，数额较大的；  
（2）挥霍贷款，或者用贷款进行违法活动，致使贷款到期无法偿还的；  
（3）隐匿贷款去向，贷款期限届满后，拒不偿还的；  
（4）提供虚假的担保申请贷款，贷款期限届满后，拒不偿还的；  
（5）假冒他人名义申请贷款，贷款期限届满后，拒不偿还的。  
《决定》第十条规定的“其他特别严重情节”是指：  
（1）为骗取贷款，向银行或者金融机构的工作人员行贿，数额巨大的；  
（2）携带贷款逃跑的；  
（3）使用贷款进行犯罪活动的。  
个人进行贷款诈骗数额在1万元以上的，属于“数额较大”；个人进行贷款诈骗数额在5万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；个人进行贷款诈骗数额在20万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。

**Article None** IV. According to the provisions of Article 10 of the Decision, whoever defrauds a bank or other financial institution of a loan for the purpose of illegal possession, in a relatively large amount, shall constitute the crime of loan fraud.  
"Other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 10 of the Decision refer to:  
(1) Bribing a staff member of a bank or financial institution to obtain a loan, in a relatively large amount;  
(2) Squandering the loan or using it for illegal activities, rendering the loan unable to be repaid when due;  
(3) Concealing the whereabouts of the loan and refusing to repay it after the loan term expires;  
(4) Providing false guarantee to apply for a loan and refusing to repay it after the loan term expires;  
(5) Applying for a loan under another person's name and refusing to repay it after the loan term expires.  
"Other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 10 of the Decision refer to:  
(1) Bribing a staff member of a bank or financial institution to obtain a loan, in a huge amount;  
(2) Absconding with the loan;  
(3) Using the loan to engage in criminal activities.  
Where an individual commits loan fraud with an amount of 10,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where an individual commits loan fraud with an amount of 50,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where an individual commits loan fraud with an amount of 200,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".

五、根据《决定》第十二条规定，利用金融票据进行诈骗活动，数额较大的，构成票据诈骗罪。  
个人进行票据诈骗数额在5000元以上的，属于“数额较大”；个人进行票据诈骗数额在5万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；个人进行票据诈骗数额在10万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
单位进行票据诈骗数额在10万元以上的，属于“数额较大”；单位进行票据诈骗数额在30万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；单位进行票据诈骗数额在100万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
使用伪造、变造的委托收款凭证、汇款凭证、银行存单等其他银行结算凭证进行诈骗，数额较大的，以票据诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article None** V. According to the provisions of Article 12 of the Decision, whoever uses financial instruments to engage in fraudulent activities, in a relatively large amount, shall constitute the crime of instrument fraud.  
Where an individual commits instrument fraud with an amount of 5,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where an individual commits instrument fraud with an amount of 50,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where an individual commits instrument fraud with an amount of 100,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Where a unit commits instrument fraud with an amount of 100,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where a unit commits instrument fraud with an amount of 300,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where a unit commits instrument fraud with an amount of 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Whoever uses forged or altered documents for collection on commission, remittance vouchers, bank deposit certificates, or other bank settlement vouchers to commit fraud, in a relatively large amount, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of instrument fraud.

六、根据《决定》第十三条规定，利用信用证进行诈骗活动的，构成信用证诈骗罪。  
个人进行信用证诈骗数额在10万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；个人进行信用证诈骗数额在50万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
单位进行信用证诈骗数额在50万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；单位进行信用证诈骗数额在250万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。

**Article None** VI. According to the provisions of Article 13 of the Decision, whoever uses a letter of credit to engage in fraudulent activities shall constitute the crime of letter of credit fraud.  
Where an individual commits letter of credit fraud with an amount of 100,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where an individual commits letter of credit fraud with an amount of 500,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Where a unit commits letter of credit fraud with an amount of 500,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where a unit commits letter of credit fraud with an amount of 2,500,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".

七、根据《决定》第十四条规定，利用信用卡进行诈骗活动，数额较大的，构成信用卡诈骗罪。  
行为人实施《决定》第十四条第一款（一）、（二）、（三）项规定的行为，诈骗数额在5千元以上的，属于“数额较大”；诈骗数额在5万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；诈骗数额在20万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
“恶意透支”是指持卡人以非法占有为目的，或者明知无力偿还，透支数额超过信用卡准许透支的数额较大，逃避追查，或者自收到发卡银行催收通知之日起3个月内仍不归还的行为。恶意透支5千元以上的，属于“数额较大”；恶意透支5万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；恶意透支20万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
持卡人在银行交纳保证金的，其恶意透支数额以超出保证金的数额计算。

**Article None** VII. According to the provisions of Article 14 of the Decision, whoever uses a credit card to engage in fraudulent activities, in a relatively large amount, shall constitute the crime of credit card fraud.  
Where an actor commits the acts specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), and (3) of the first paragraph of Article 14 of the Decision, if the amount of fraud is 5,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; if the amount of fraud is 50,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; if the amount of fraud is 200,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
"Malicious overdraft" refers to the act of a cardholder, for the purpose of illegal possession, or knowing that he is unable to repay, exceeding the credit limit by a relatively large amount, evading investigation, or failing to repay within three months from the date of receiving the collection notice from the issuing bank. A malicious overdraft of 5,000 yuan or more shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; a malicious overdraft of 50,000 yuan or more shall constitute a "huge amount"; a malicious overdraft of 200,000 yuan or more shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Where a cardholder has deposited a guarantee with the bank, the amount of his malicious overdraft shall be calculated as the amount exceeding the guarantee.

八、根据《决定》第十六条规定，进行保险诈骗活动，数额较大的，构成保险诈骗罪。  
个人进行保险诈骗数额在1万元以上的，属于“数额较大”；个人进行保险诈骗数额在5万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；个人进行保险诈骗数额在20万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。  
单位进行保险诈骗数额在5万元以上的，属于“数额较大”；单位进行保险诈骗数额在25万元以上的，属于“数额巨大”；单位进行保险诈骗数额在100万元以上的，属于“数额特别巨大”。

**Article None** VIII. According to the provisions of Article 16 of the Decision, whoever engages in insurance fraud activities, in a relatively large amount, shall constitute the crime of insurance fraud.  
Where an individual commits insurance fraud with an amount of 10,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where an individual commits insurance fraud with an amount of 50,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where an individual commits insurance fraud with an amount of 200,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".  
Where a unit commits insurance fraud with an amount of 50,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "relatively large amount"; where a unit commits insurance fraud with an amount of 250,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "huge amount"; where a unit commits insurance fraud with an amount of 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall constitute a "particularly huge amount".

九、对于多次进行诈骗，并以后次诈骗财物归还前次诈骗财物，在计算诈骗数额时，应当将案发前已经归还的数额扣除，按实际未归还的数额认定，量刑时可将多次行骗的数额作为从重情节予以考虑。

**Article None** IX. Where a person commits fraud multiple times and uses the property defrauded in a later fraud to repay the property defrauded in a previous fraud, when calculating the amount of fraud, the amount already repaid before the case is discovered shall be deducted, and the amount actually not repaid shall be determined. When sentencing, the total amount of multiple frauds may be considered as an aggravating circumstance.

十、行为人进行诈骗犯罪活动，案发后扣押、冻结在案的财物及其孳息，如果权属明确的，应当发还给被害人；如果权属不明确的，可按被害人被骗款物占扣押、冻结在案的财物及其孳息总额的比例发还被害人；如果能够确定扣押、冻结在案的财物及其孳息不属于已查明的被害人所有，但又无法发还未查明被害人的，应当依法上缴国库。

**Article None** X. Where an actor engages in criminal fraud activities, and after the case is discovered, the property and its fruits seized or frozen in the case, if the ownership is clear, shall be returned to the victim; if the ownership is unclear, it may be returned to the victim in proportion to the amount of money or property defrauded from the victim out of the total amount of the property and its fruits seized or frozen in the case; if it can be determined that the property and its fruits seized or frozen in the case do not belong to any identified victim, but cannot be returned to unidentified victims, it shall be turned over to the State treasury according to law.

十一、行为人将诈骗财物已用于归还个人欠款、货款或者其他经济活动的，如果对方明知是诈骗财物而收取，属恶意取得，应当一律予以追缴；如确属善意取得，则不再追缴。

**Article None** XI. Where the actor has used the defrauded property to repay personal debts,货款, or other economic activities, if the other party knowingly receives the defrauded property, it shall constitute malicious acquisition, and the property shall be recovered in all cases; if it is indeed acquired in good faith, it shall no longer be recovered.

十二、本解释中使用的货币数额是指人民币的数额。审理具体案件涉及外币的，应当依照行为发生时国家外汇管理局公布的外汇牌价折算成人民币。

**Article None** XII. The monetary amounts used in this Interpretation refer to amounts in Renminbi. When hearing a specific case involving foreign currency, it shall be converted into Renminbi according to the foreign exchange rate published by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange at the time of the act.

十三、本解释所称“以上”包括本数在内。

**Article None** XIII. The term "or more" as used in this Interpretation includes the figure itself.

十四、本解释自公布之日起生效。
