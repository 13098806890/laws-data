# 关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕11号  
**公布日期**：2015-06-02  
**生效日期**：2016-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定》已于2015年6月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1653次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年5月1日起施行。 2016年4月27日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的 大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定 法释〔2016〕11号 为落实《海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议》，保障接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑工作顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关法律，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民（以下简称被判刑人）回大陆服刑案件（以下简称接收被判刑人案件），应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，秉持人道和互惠原则，不得违反社会公共利益。 第二条　接收被判刑人案件由最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。 第三条　申请机关向人民法院申请接收被判刑人回大陆服刑，应当同时提交以下材料： （一）申请机关制作的接收被判刑人申请书，其中应当载明： 1.台湾地区法院认定的被判刑人实施的犯罪行为及判决依据的具体条文内容； 2.该行为在大陆依据刑法也构成犯罪、相应的刑法条文、罪名及该行为未进入大陆刑事诉讼程序的说明； 3.建议转换的具体刑罚； 4.其他需要说明的事项。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民（以下简称被判刑人）回大陆服刑案件（以下简称接收被判刑人案件），应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，秉持人道和互惠原则，不得违反社会公共利益。

**Article 1** When handling cases of accepting mainland residents serving sentences in the Taiwan region (hereinafter referred to as sentenced persons) to serve their sentences on the mainland (hereinafter referred to as cases of accepting sentenced persons), the People's Court shall adhere to the One-China principle, observe the fundamental principles of national laws, uphold the principles of humanity and reciprocity, and shall not violate social public interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　接收被判刑人案件由最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Cases of accepting sentenced persons shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court designated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请机关向人民法院申请接收被判刑人回大陆服刑，应当同时提交以下材料：  
（一）申请机关制作的接收被判刑人申请书，其中应当载明：  
1.台湾地区法院认定的被判刑人实施的犯罪行为及判决依据的具体条文内容；  
2.该行为在大陆依据刑法也构成犯罪、相应的刑法条文、罪名及该行为未进入大陆刑事诉讼程序的说明；  
3.建议转换的具体刑罚；  
4.其他需要说明的事项。  
（二）被判刑人系大陆居民的身份证明；  
（三）台湾地区法院对被判刑人定罪处刑的裁判文书、生效证明和执行文书；  
（四）被判刑人或其法定代理人申请或者同意回大陆服刑的书面意见，且法定代理人与被判刑人的意思表示一致；  
（五）被判刑人或其法定代理人所作的关于被判刑人在台湾地区接受公正审判的权利已获得保障的书面声明；  
（六）两岸有关业务主管部门均同意被判刑人回大陆服刑的书面意见；  
（七）台湾地区业务主管部门出具的有关刑罚执行情况的说明，包括被判刑人交付执行前的羁押期、已服刑期、剩余刑期，被判刑人服刑期间的表现、退赃退赔情况，被判刑人的健康状况、疾病与治疗情况；  
（八）根据案件具体情况需要提交的其他材料。  
申请机关提交材料齐全的，人民法院应当在七日内立案。提交材料不全的，应当通知申请机关在十五日内补送，至迟不能超过两个月；逾期未补送的，不予立案，并于七日内书面告知申请机关。

**Article 3** When applying to the People's Court for accepting a sentenced person to serve his or her sentence on the mainland, the applying authority shall simultaneously submit the following materials:  
(1) The written application for accepting the sentenced person prepared by the applying authority, which shall set forth:  
1. The criminal act committed by the sentenced person as determined by the court of the Taiwan region and the specific provisions of the law on which the judgment is based;  
2. An explanation that such act also constitutes a crime under the Criminal Law on the mainland, the corresponding provisions of the Criminal Law, the罪名 (charge), and that such act has not entered into the criminal proceedings on the mainland;  
3. The specific penalty recommended for conversion;  
4. Other matters that need to be explained.  
(2) The identity certificate proving that the sentenced person is a mainland resident;  
(3) The judgment document, certificate of taking effect, and enforcement document of the court of the Taiwan region convicting and sentencing the sentenced person;  
(4) The written opinion of the sentenced person or his or her statutory agent applying for or consenting to serving the sentence on the mainland, and the意思表示 (expression of intent) of the statutory agent is consistent with that of the sentenced person;  
(5) A written statement made by the sentenced person or his or her statutory agent regarding the guarantee of the sentenced person's right to a fair trial in the Taiwan region;  
(6) The written opinions from the competent authorities on both sides of the strait that both agree to the sentenced person's return to the mainland to serve the sentence;  
(7) An explanation issued by the competent authority of the Taiwan region on the status of the execution of the penalty, including the period of custody before delivery for execution, the period of sentence already served, the remaining term of sentence, the sentenced person's behavior during sentence, the restitution and return of illicit gains, and the sentenced person's health condition, illness, and treatment;  
(8) Other materials that need to be submitted based on the specific circumstances of the case.  
If the materials submitted by the applying authority are complete, the People's Court shall docket the case within seven days. If the materials are incomplete, the court shall notify the applying authority to supplement them within fifteen days, and such supplementation shall in no case exceed two months; if the authority fails to supplement within the time limit, the case shall not be docketed, and the court shall notify the applying authority in writing within seven days.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当组成合议庭审理接收被判刑人案件。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall form a collegial panel to hear cases of accepting sentenced persons.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院应当在立案后一个月内就是否准予接收被判刑人作出裁定，情况复杂、特殊的，可以延长一个月。  
人民法院裁定准予接收的，应当依据台湾地区法院判决认定的事实并参考其所定罪名，根据刑法就相同或者最相似犯罪行为规定的法定刑，按照下列原则对台湾地区法院确定的无期徒刑或者有期徒刑予以转换：  
（一）原判处刑罚未超过刑法规定的最高刑，包括原判处刑罚低于刑法规定的最低刑的，以原判处刑罚作为转换后的刑罚；  
（二）原判处刑罚超过刑法规定的最高刑的，以刑法规定的最高刑作为转换后的刑罚；  
（三）转换后的刑罚不附加适用剥夺政治权利。  
前款所称的最高刑，如台湾地区法院认定的事实依据刑法应当认定为一个犯罪的，是指刑法对该犯罪规定的最高刑；如应当认定为多个犯罪的，是指刑法对数罪并罚规定的最高刑。  
对人民法院立案前，台湾地区有关业务主管部门对被判刑人在服刑期间作出的减轻刑罚决定，人民法院应当一并予以转换，并就最终应当执行的刑罚作出裁定。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall, within one month after docketing the case, render a ruling on whether to permit the acceptance of the sentenced person; if the circumstances are complex or special, the period may be extended by one month.  
If the People's Court rules to permit acceptance, it shall, based on the facts determined by the judgment of the court of the Taiwan region and with reference to the charge determined thereby, and in accordance with the statutory sentence prescribed by the Criminal Law for the same or the most similar criminal act, convert the life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment determined by the court of the Taiwan region according to the following principles:  
(1) If the originally imposed penalty does not exceed the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, including where the originally imposed penalty is lower than the minimum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, the originally imposed penalty shall be taken as the converted penalty;  
(2) If the originally imposed penalty exceeds the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law shall be taken as the converted penalty;  
(3) The converted penalty shall not be imposed with an additional penalty of deprivation of political rights.  
The "maximum penalty" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means, if the facts determined by the court of the Taiwan region should, under the Criminal Law, be determined as a single crime, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law for that crime; if such facts should be determined as multiple crimes, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law for concurrent punishment for multiple crimes.  
With respect to any decision to reduce the penalty of the sentenced person made during the service of the sentence by the competent authority of the Taiwan region before the People's Court dockets the case, the People's Court shall convert it accordingly and render a ruling on the final penalty to be executed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被判刑人被接收回大陆服刑前被实际羁押的期间，应当以一日折抵转换后的刑期一日。

**Article 6** Any period of actual custody of the sentenced person before being accepted back to the mainland to serve the sentence shall be offset against the converted term of imprisonment on a one-for-one basis (one day of custody offsetting one day of imprisonment).

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被判刑人被接收回大陆前已在台湾地区被假释或保外就医的，或者被判刑人或其法定代理人在申请或者同意回大陆服刑的书面意见中同时申请暂予监外执行的，人民法院应当根据刑法、刑事诉讼法的规定一并审查，并作出是否假释或者暂予监外执行的决定。

**Article 7** If, before being accepted back to the mainland, the sentenced person has been granted parole or released on medical parole in the Taiwan region, or if the sentenced person or his or her statutory agent, in the written opinion applying for or consenting to return to the mainland to serve the sentence, also applies for temporary service of sentence outside prison, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law and the Criminal Procedure Law, conduct a joint review and render a decision on whether to grant parole or temporary service of sentence outside prison.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院作出裁定后，应当在七日内送达申请机关。裁定一经送达，立即生效。

**Article 8** After the People's Court renders a ruling, it shall serve it on the applying authority within seven days. The ruling shall take effect immediately upon service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被判刑人回大陆服刑后，有关减刑、假释、暂予监外执行、赦免等事项，适用刑法、刑事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 9** After the sentenced person returns to the mainland to serve the sentence, matters such as commutation of punishment, parole, temporary service of sentence outside prison, and pardon shall be governed by the provisions of the Criminal Law, the Criminal Procedure Law, and relevant judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被判刑人回大陆服刑后，对其在台湾地区已被判处刑罚的行为，人民法院不再审理。

**Article 10** After the sentenced person returns to the mainland to serve the sentence, the People's Court shall not try the act for which the sentenced person has already been sentenced to punishment in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定自2016年5月1日起施行。

**Article 11** These Provisions shall come into force on May 1, 2016.
