# 最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕17号  
**公布日期**：2022-06-07  
**生效日期**：2022-08-01

> 《最高人民法院关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2022年6月7日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1870次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年8月1日起施行。 2022年7月14日 最高人民法院 关于办理人身安全保护令案件适用法律 若干问题的规定 法释〔2022〕17号 为正确办理人身安全保护令案件，及时保护家庭暴力受害人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　当事人因遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力的现实危险，依照反家庭暴力法向人民法院申请人身安全保护令的，人民法院应当受理。 向人民法院申请人身安全保护令，不以提起离婚等民事诉讼为条件。 第二条　当事人因年老、残疾、重病等原因无法申请人身安全保护令，其近亲属、公安机关、民政部门、妇女联合会、居民委员会、村民委员会、残疾人联合会、依法设立的老年人组织、救助管理机构等，根据当事人意愿，依照反家庭暴力法第二十三条规定代为申请的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第三条　家庭成员之间以冻饿或者经常性侮辱、诽谤、威胁、跟踪、骚扰等方式实施的身体或者精神侵害行为，应当认定为反家庭暴力法第二条规定的“家庭暴力”。 第四条　反家庭暴力法第三十七条规定的“家庭成员以外共同生活的人”一般包括共同生活的儿媳、女婿、公婆、岳父母以及其他有监护、扶养、寄养等关系的人。 第五条　当事人及其代理人对因客观原因不能自行收集的证据，申请人民法院调查收集，符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十四条第一款规定情形的，人民法院应当调查收集。 人民法院经审查，认为办理案件需要的证据符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十六条规定的，应当调查收集。 第六条　人身安全保护令案件中，人民法院根据相关证据，认为申请人遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力现实危险的事实存在较大可能性的，可以依法作出人身安全保护令。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人因遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力的现实危险，依照反家庭暴力法向人民法院申请人身安全保护令的，人民法院应当受理。  
向人民法院申请人身安全保护令，不以提起离婚等民事诉讼为条件。

**Article 1** Where a party, having suffered domestic violence or facing a real risk of domestic violence, applies to a People's Court for a personal safety protection order in accordance with the Anti-Domestic Violence Law, the People's Court shall accept the application.  
An application to a People's Court for a personal safety protection order shall not be conditional on the filing of a civil action such as divorce.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人因年老、残疾、重病等原因无法申请人身安全保护令，其近亲属、公安机关、民政部门、妇女联合会、居民委员会、村民委员会、残疾人联合会、依法设立的老年人组织、救助管理机构等，根据当事人意愿，依照[反家庭暴力法第二十三条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法.md#art-23)规定代为申请的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 2** Where a party is unable to apply for a personal safety protection order due to old age, disability, serious illness or other reasons, and his or her close relatives, public security organs, civil affairs departments, women's federations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, disabled persons' federations, lawfully established organizations for the elderly, relief management institutions, etc., apply on behalf of the party in accordance with the party's wishes and pursuant to Article 23 of the Anti-Domestic Violence Law, the People's Court shall accept the application according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　家庭成员之间以冻饿或者经常性侮辱、诽谤、威胁、跟踪、骚扰等方式实施的身体或者精神侵害行为，应当认定为[反家庭暴力法第二条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法.md#art-2)规定的“家庭暴力”。

**Article 3** Physical or mental harming acts committed among family members by means of freezing or starving, or habitual insult, defamation, threat, stalking, harassment, etc., shall be identified as "domestic violence" as stipulated in Article 2 of the Anti-Domestic Violence Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[反家庭暴力法第三十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法.md#art-37)规定的“家庭成员以外共同生活的人”一般包括共同生活的儿媳、女婿、公婆、岳父母以及其他有监护、扶养、寄养等关系的人。

**Article 4** The "persons living together with a family member" as stipulated in Article 37 of the Anti-Domestic Violence Law shall generally include a daughter-in-law, son-in-law, parents-in-law (paternal and maternal), and other persons who have a relationship of guardianship, support, foster care, etc., living together.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人及其代理人对因客观原因不能自行收集的证据，申请人民法院调查收集，符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十四条第一款规定情形的，人民法院应当调查收集。  
人民法院经审查，认为办理案件需要的证据符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十六条规定的，应当调查收集。

**Article 5** Where a party or his or her agent applies to the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence that cannot be collected by the party on his or her own due to objective reasons, and the circumstances meet the requirements of Paragraph 1, Article 94 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall investigate and collect such evidence.  
Where, upon review, the People's Court considers that the evidence required for handling the case meets the provisions of Article 96 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall investigate and collect such evidence.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人身安全保护令案件中，人民法院根据相关证据，认为申请人遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力现实危险的事实存在较大可能性的，可以依法作出人身安全保护令。  
前款所称“相关证据”包括：  
（一）当事人的陈述；  
（二）公安机关出具的家庭暴力告诫书、行政处罚决定书；  
（三）公安机关的出警记录、讯问笔录、询问笔录、接警记录、报警回执等；  
（四）被申请人曾出具的悔过书或者保证书等；  
（五）记录家庭暴力发生或者解决过程等的视听资料；  
（六）被申请人与申请人或者其近亲属之间的电话录音、短信、即时通讯信息、电子邮件等；  
（七）医疗机构的诊疗记录；  
（八）申请人或者被申请人所在单位、民政部门、居民委员会、村民委员会、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会、未成年人保护组织、依法设立的老年人组织、救助管理机构、反家暴社会公益机构等单位收到投诉、反映或者求助的记录；  
（九）未成年子女提供的与其年龄、智力相适应的证言或者亲友、邻居等其他证人证言；  
（十）伤情鉴定意见；  
（十一）其他能够证明申请人遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力现实危险的证据。

**Article 6** In a personal safety protection order case, where the People's Court, based on relevant evidence, considers that there is a relatively high probability that the applicant has suffered domestic violence or faces a real risk of domestic violence, it may issue a personal safety protection order according to law.  
The "relevant evidence" mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes:  
(1) statements of the parties;  
(2) domestic violence admonition letters and administrative penalty decisions issued by public security organs;  
(3) records of police dispatches, interrogation transcripts, inquiry transcripts, call-receiving records, alarm receipts, etc., of public security organs;  
(4) any letter of repentance or letter of guarantee previously issued by the respondent;  
(5) audio-visual materials recording the occurrence or resolution process of domestic violence;  
(6) telephone recordings, text messages, instant messaging information, emails, etc., between the respondent and the applicant or his or her close relatives;  
(7) medical diagnosis and treatment records;  
(8) records of complaints, reports, or requests for assistance received by the applicant's or respondent's employer, civil affairs departments, residents' committees, villagers' committees, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, organizations for the protection of minors, lawfully established organizations for the elderly, relief management institutions, anti-domestic violence public welfare organizations, etc.;  
(9) testimony provided by a minor child that is commensurate with his or her age and intelligence, or testimony of other witnesses such as relatives, friends, or neighbors;  
(10) injury evaluation opinions;  
(11) other evidence capable of proving that the applicant has suffered domestic violence or faces a real risk of domestic violence.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院可以通过在线诉讼平台、电话、短信、即时通讯工具、电子邮件等简便方式询问被申请人。被申请人未发表意见的，不影响人民法院依法作出人身安全保护令。

**Article 7** A People's Court may question the respondent through simple methods such as an online litigation platform, telephone, text message, instant messaging tool, or email. The failure of the respondent to express an opinion shall not affect the People's Court's issuance of a personal safety protection order according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被申请人认可存在家庭暴力行为，但辩称申请人有过错的，不影响人民法院依法作出人身安全保护令。

**Article 8** Where the respondent acknowledges the existence of domestic violence but argues that the applicant was at fault, this shall not affect the People's Court's issuance of a personal safety protection order according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　离婚等案件中，当事人仅以人民法院曾作出人身安全保护令为由，主张存在家庭暴力事实的，人民法院应当根据《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第一百零八条的规定，综合认定是否存在该事实。

**Article 9** In cases such as divorce, where a party claims the existence of domestic violence solely on the ground that the People's Court has previously issued a personal safety protection order, the People's Court shall make a comprehensive determination of whether such fact exists in accordance with Article 108 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[反家庭暴力法第二十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法.md#art-29)第四项规定的“保护申请人人身安全的其他措施”可以包括下列措施：  
（一）禁止被申请人以电话、短信、即时通讯工具、电子邮件等方式侮辱、诽谤、威胁申请人及其相关近亲属；  
（二）禁止被申请人在申请人及其相关近亲属的住所、学校、工作单位等经常出入场所的一定范围内从事可能影响申请人及其相关近亲属正常生活、学习、工作的活动。

**Article 10** The "other measures to protect the personal safety of the applicant" stipulated in Item (4) of Article 29 of the Anti-Domestic Violence Law may include the following measures:  
(1) prohibiting the respondent from insulting, defaming, or threatening the applicant and his or her close relatives by means such as telephone, text message, instant messaging tool, or email;  
(2) prohibiting the respondent from engaging in activities within a certain range of the applicant's and his or her close relatives' usual places such as residence, school, or workplace that may affect the normal life, study, or work of the applicant and his or her close relatives.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　离婚案件中，判决不准离婚或者调解和好后，被申请人违反人身安全保护令实施家庭暴力的，可以认定为[民事诉讼法第一百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-127)第七项规定的“新情况、新理由”。

**Article 11** In a divorce case, where, after a judgment dismissing the divorce claim or reconciliation through mediation, the respondent violates a personal safety protection order by committing domestic violence, this may be identified as a "new circumstance or new ground" as stipulated in Item (7) of Article 127 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被申请人违反人身安全保护令，符合[《中华人民共和国刑法》第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)规定的，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪定罪处罚；同时构成其他犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定处理。

**Article 12** Where a respondent violates a personal safety protection order and the violation meets the provisions of Article 313 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or order; if the act also constitutes another crime, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定自2022年8月1日起施行。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of August 1, 2022.
