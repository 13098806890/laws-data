# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国担保法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕44号  
**公布日期**：2000-09-29  
**生效日期**：2000-12-13

> （2000年9月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第1133次会议通过，自2000年12月13日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕44号 为了正确适用《中华人民共和国担保法》（以下简称担保法），结合审判实践经验，对人民法院审理担保纠纷案件适用法律问题作出如下解释。 一、关于总则部分的解释 第一条　当事人对由民事关系产生的债权，在不违反法律、法规强制性规定的情况下，以担保法规定的方式设定担保的，可以认定为有效。 第二条　反担保人可以是债务人，也可以是债务人之外的其他人。 反担保方式可以是债务人提供的抵押或者质押，也可以是其他人提供的保证、抵押或者质押。 第三条　国家机关和以公益为目的的事业单位、社会团体违反法律规定提供担保的，担保合同无效。因此给债权人造成损失的，应当根据担保法第五条第二款的规定处理。 第四条　董事、经理违反《中华人民共和国公司法》第六十条的规定，以公司资产为本公司的股东或者其他个人债务提供担保的，担保合同无效。除债权人知道或者应当知道的外，债务人、担保人应当对债权人的损失承担连带赔偿责任。 第五条　以法律、法规禁止流通的财产或者不可转让的财产设定担保的，担保合同无效。 以法律、法规限制流通的财产设定担保的，在实现债权时，人民法院应当按照有关法律、法规的规定对该财产进行处理。 第六条　有下列情形之一的，对外担保合同无效： （一）未经国家有关主管部门批准或者登记对外担保的； （二）未经国家有关主管部门批准或者登记，为境外机构向境内债权人提供担保的； （三）为外商投资企业注册资本、外商投资企业中的外方投资部分的对外债务提供担保的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人对由民事关系产生的债权，在不违反法律、法规强制性规定的情况下，以担保法规定的方式设定担保的，可以认定为有效。

**Article 1** Where a party creates a security interest in a creditor's right arising from a civil relationship in the manner prescribed by the Security Law, without violating the mandatory provisions of laws or regulations, such creation may be deemed valid.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　反担保人可以是债务人，也可以是债务人之外的其他人。  
反担保方式可以是债务人提供的抵押或者质押，也可以是其他人提供的保证、抵押或者质押。

**Article 2** A counter-guarantor may be the debtor, or may be a person other than the debtor.  
The method of counter-guaranty may be a mortgage or pledge provided by the debtor, or may be a suretyship, mortgage or pledge provided by another person.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家机关和以公益为目的的事业单位、社会团体违反法律规定提供担保的，担保合同无效。因此给债权人造成损失的，应当根据担保法第五条第二款的规定处理。

**Article 3** Where a state organ, or a public institution or social organization for public welfare purposes, provides a guaranty in violation of legal provisions, the guaranty contract shall be null and void. Any loss caused to the creditor thereby shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of Article 5, paragraph 2 of the Security Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　董事、经理违反[《中华人民共和国公司法》第六十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-60)的规定，以公司资产为本公司的股东或者其他个人债务提供担保的，担保合同无效。除债权人知道或者应当知道的外，债务人、担保人应当对债权人的损失承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 4** Where a director or manager violates the provisions of Article 60 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China by providing a guaranty with the company's assets for the debts of the company's shareholders or other individuals, the guaranty contract shall be null and void. Unless the creditor knew or should have known, the debtor and the guarantor shall bear joint and several compensation liability for the losses of the creditor.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　以法律、法规禁止流通的财产或者不可转让的财产设定担保的，担保合同无效。  
以法律、法规限制流通的财产设定担保的，在实现债权时，人民法院应当按照有关法律、法规的规定对该财产进行处理。

**Article 5** Where a security interest is created with property the circulation of which is prohibited by laws or regulations, or with non-transferable property, the guaranty contract shall be null and void.  
Where a security interest is created with property the circulation of which is restricted by laws or regulations, when the creditor's right is realized, the People's Court shall dispose of such property in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有下列情形之一的，对外担保合同无效：  
（一）未经国家有关主管部门批准或者登记对外担保的；  
（二）未经国家有关主管部门批准或者登记，为境外机构向境内债权人提供担保的；  
（三）为外商投资企业注册资本、外商投资企业中的外方投资部分的对外债务提供担保的；  
（四）无权经营外汇担保业务的金融机构、无外汇收入的非金融性质的企业法人提供外汇担保的；  
（五）主合同变更或者债权人将对外担保合同项下的权利转让，未经担保人同意和国家有关主管部门批准的，担保人不再承担担保责任。但法律、法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** A foreign guaranty contract shall be null and void under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The foreign guaranty is undertaken without approval or registration by the relevant competent state department;  
(2) A guaranty is provided to a domestic creditor for an overseas institution without approval or registration by the relevant competent state department;  
(3) A guaranty is provided for the registered capital of a foreign-invested enterprise or for the foreign-investment portion of a foreign-invested enterprise;  
(4) A financial institution without the authority to engage in foreign exchange guaranty business, or a non-financial enterprise legal person without foreign exchange income, provides a foreign exchange guaranty;  
(5) Where the principal contract is amended or the creditor assigns the rights under the foreign guaranty contract without the consent of the guarantor and the approval of the relevant competent state department, the guarantor shall no longer bear the guaranty liability, unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　主合同有效而担保合同无效，债权人无过错的，担保人与债务人对主合同债权人的经济损失，承担连带赔偿责任；债权人、担保人有过错的，担保人承担民事责任的部分，不应超过债务人不能清偿部分的二分之一。

**Article 7** Where the principal contract is valid but the guaranty contract is null and void, if the creditor is not at fault, the guarantor and the debtor shall bear joint and several compensation liability for the economic losses of the creditor under the principal contract; if the creditor and the guarantor are at fault, the portion of civil liability borne by the guarantor shall not exceed one-half of the portion that the debtor is unable to pay.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　主合同无效而导致担保合同无效，担保人无过错的，担保人不承担民事责任；担保人有过错的，担保人承担民事责任的部分，不应超过债务人不能清偿部分的三分之一。

**Article 8** Where the principal contract is null and void, thereby rendering the guaranty contract null and void, if the guarantor is not at fault, the guarantor shall not bear civil liability; if the guarantor is at fault, the portion of civil liability borne by the guarantor shall not exceed one-third of the portion that the debtor is unable to pay.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　担保人因无效担保合同向债权人承担赔偿责任后，可以向债务人追偿，或者在承担赔偿责任的范围内，要求有过错的反担保人承担赔偿责任。  
担保人可以根据承担赔偿责任的事实对债务人或者反担保人另行提起诉讼。

**Article 9** After the guarantor has borne compensation liability to the creditor due to a null and void guaranty contract, he may seek recourse against the debtor, or may, within the scope of the compensation liability borne, require the counter-guarantor at fault to bear compensation liability.  
The guarantor may, based on the fact of having borne compensation liability, file a separate action against the debtor or the counter-guarantor.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　主合同解除后，担保人对债务人应当承担的民事责任仍应承担担保责任。但是，担保合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 10** After the principal contract is rescinded, the guarantor shall still bear the guaranty liability for the civil liability that the debtor shall bear, unless otherwise agreed in the guaranty contract.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　法人或者其他组织的法定代表人、负责人超越权限订立的担保合同，除相对人知道或者应当知道其超越权限的以外，该代表行为有效。

**Article 11** Where the legal representative or responsible person of a legal person or other organization concludes a guaranty contract in excess of his authority, such representative act shall be valid, unless the counterpart knew or should have known of such excess of authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人约定的或者登记部门要求登记的担保期间，对担保物权的存续不具有法律约束力。  
担保物权所担保的债权的诉讼时效结束后，担保权人在诉讼时效结束后的二年内行使担保物权的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
二、关于保证部分的解释

**Article 12** The security period agreed upon by the parties or required to be registered by the registration authority shall have no legally binding effect on the existence of the security interest.  
After the limitation of action for the creditor's right secured by the security interest expires, if the security holder exercises the security interest within two years after the expiration of the limitation of action, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
II. Interpretation of the Part concerning Suretyship

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　保证合同中约定保证人代为履行非金钱债务的，如果保证人不能实际代为履行，对债权人因此造成的损失，保证人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 13** Where a suretyship contract stipulates that the surety shall perform a non-monetary obligation on behalf of the debtor, if the surety cannot actually perform on behalf of the debtor, the surety shall bear compensation liability for the losses caused to the creditor thereby.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　不具有完全代偿能力的法人、其他组织或者自然人，以保证人身份订立保证合同后，又以自己没有代偿能力要求免除保证责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where a legal person, other organization or natural person that does not have full capacity to perform on behalf of the debtor concludes a suretyship contract as a surety, and subsequently claims exemption from suretyship liability on the ground that it does not have such capacity, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　担保法第七条规定的其他组织主要包括：  
（一）依法登记领取营业执照的独资企业、合伙企业；  
（二）依法登记领取营业执照的联营企业；  
（三）依法登记领取营业执照的中外合作经营企业；  
（四）经民政部门核准登记的社会团体；  
（五）经核准登记领取营业执照的乡镇、街道、村办企业。

**Article 15** The “other organizations” as stipulated in Article 7 of the Security Law mainly include:  
(1) Sole proprietorships and partnerships that have been registered according to law and obtained business licenses;  
(2) Joint-operation enterprises that have been registered according to law and obtained business licenses;  
(3) Chinese-foreign cooperative joint ventures that have been registered according to law and obtained business licenses;  
(4) Social organizations that have been approved and registered by the civil affairs department;  
(5) Township, sub-district, and village enterprises that have been approved and registered and obtained business licenses.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事经营活动的事业单位、社会团体为保证人的，如无其他导致保证合同无效的情况，其所签定的保证合同应当认定为有效。

**Article 16** Where a public institution or social organization engaged in business activities acts as a surety, if there are no other circumstances rendering the suretyship contract null and void, the suretyship contract concluded by it shall be deemed valid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业法人的分支机构未经法人书面授权提供保证的，保证合同无效。因此给债权人造成损失的，应当根据担保法第五条第二款的规定处理。  
企业法人的分支机构经法人书面授权提供保证的，如果法人的书面授权范围不明，法人的分支机构应当对保证合同约定的全部债务承担保证责任。  
企业法人的分支机构经营管理的财产不足以承担保证责任的，由企业法人承担民事责任。  
企业法人的分支机构提供的保证无效后应当承担赔偿责任的，由分支机构经营管理的财产承担。企业法人有过错的，按照担保法第二十九条的规定处理。

**Article 17** Where a branch of an enterprise legal person provides a suretyship without the written authorization of the legal person, the suretyship contract shall be null and void. Any loss caused to the creditor thereby shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of Article 5, paragraph 2 of the Security Law.  
Where a branch of an enterprise legal person provides a suretyship with the written authorization of the legal person, if the scope of the written authorization is ambiguous, the branch of the enterprise legal person shall bear suretyship liability for all debts stipulated in the suretyship contract.  
Where the property managed and operated by the branch of the enterprise legal person is insufficient to bear the suretyship liability, the enterprise legal person shall bear the civil liability.  
Where the suretyship provided by the branch of the enterprise legal person is null and void and the branch shall bear compensation liability, such liability shall be satisfied out of the property managed and operated by the branch. If the enterprise legal person is at fault, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of Article 29 of the Security Law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业法人的职能部门提供保证的，保证合同无效。债权人知道或者应当知道保证人为企业法人的职能部门的，因此造成的损失由债权人自行承担。  
债权人不知保证人为企业法人的职能部门，因此造成的损失，可以参照担保法第五条第二款的规定和第二十九条的规定处理。

**Article 18** Where a functional department of an enterprise legal person provides a suretyship, the suretyship contract shall be null and void. If the creditor knew or should have known that the surety was a functional department of an enterprise legal person, the losses caused thereby shall be borne by the creditor itself.  
If the creditor did not know that the surety was a functional department of an enterprise legal person, the losses caused thereby may be dealt with by reference to the provisions of Article 5, paragraph 2 and Article 29 of the Security Law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　两个以上保证人对同一债务同时或者分别提供保证时，各保证人与债权人没有约定保证份额的，应当认定为连带共同保证。  
连带共同保证的保证人以其相互之间约定各自承担的份额对抗债权人的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 19** Where two or more sureties provide suretyship for the same debt simultaneously or separately, if there is no agreement between each surety and the creditor on the share of suretyship, it shall be deemed as joint and several co-suretyship.  
Where a surety in a joint and several co-suretyship sets up against the creditor the shares each shall bear as agreed among themselves, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　连带共同保证的债务人在主合同规定的债务履行期届满没有履行债务的，债权人可以要求债务人履行债务，也可以要求任何一个保证人承担全部保证责任。  
连带共同保证的保证人承担保证责任后，向债务人不能追偿的部分，由各连带保证人按其内部约定的比例分担。没有约定的，平均分担。

**Article 20** Where the debtor under a joint and several co-suretyship fails to perform its debt upon expiry of the performance period stipulated in the principal contract, the creditor may demand the debtor to perform the debt, or may demand any one of the sureties to bear all the suretyship liability.  
After a surety under a joint and several co-suretyship has borne the suretyship liability, the portion that cannot be recovered from the debtor shall be apportioned among the joint and several sureties according to the proportion agreed upon internally. If there is no agreement, it shall be apportioned equally.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　按份共同保证的保证人按照保证合同约定的保证份额承担保证责任后，在其履行保证责任的范围内对债务人行使追偿权。

**Article 21** After a surety under a several co-suretyship bears the suretyship liability in accordance with the suretyship share stipulated in the suretyship contract, it shall exercise the right of recourse against the debtor within the scope of its performance of the suretyship liability.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　第三人单方以书面形式向债权人出具担保书，债权人接受且未提出异议的，保证合同成立。  
主合同中虽然没有保证条款，但是，保证人在主合同上以保证人的身份签字或者盖章的，保证合同成立。

**Article 22** Where a third party unilaterally issues a guaranty letter in writing to the creditor, and the creditor accepts it without raising any objection, the suretyship contract is established.  
Where the principal contract does not contain a suretyship clause, but the surety signs or seals the principal contract in the capacity of a surety, the suretyship contract is established.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　最高额保证合同的不特定债权确定后，保证人应当对在最高债权额限度内就一定期间连续发生的债权余额承担保证责任。

**Article 23** After the unspecified creditor's rights under a maximum-amount suretyship contract are determined, the surety shall bear suretyship liability for the balance of creditor's rights successively arising within a certain period within the limit of the maximum amount of creditor's rights.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　一般保证的保证人在主债权履行期间届满后，向债权人提供了债务人可供执行财产的真实情况的，债权人放弃或者怠于行使权利致使该财产不能被执行，保证人可以请求人民法院在其提供可供执行财产的实际价值范围内免除保证责任。

**Article 24** Where, after the expiration of the performance period of the principal creditor's right, the surety under a general suretyship provides the creditor with true information about the debtor's property available for enforcement, and the creditor abandons or neglects to exercise its rights, resulting in the property being unenforceable, the surety may request the People's Court to be exempted from suretyship liability within the actual value of the property made available for enforcement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　担保法第十七条第三款第（一）项规定的债权人要求债务人履行债务发生的重大困难情形，包括债务人下落不明、移居境外，且无财产可供执行。

**Article 25** The circumstances of major difficulty in the creditor's demand for the debtor to perform the debt as provided in Article 17, paragraph 3, item (1) of the Security Law include where the debtor's whereabouts are unknown, or the debtor has moved abroad, and there is no property available for enforcement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　第三人向债权人保证监督支付专款专用的，在履行了监督支付专款专用的义务后，不再承担责任。未尽监督义务造成资金流失的，应当对流失的资金承担补充赔偿责任。

**Article 26** Where a third party guarantees to the creditor that it will supervise the payment of designated funds for designated purposes, after it has performed the obligation of supervising the payment of designated funds for designated purposes, it shall no longer bear liability. If it fails to fulfill the supervision obligation, resulting in the loss of funds, it shall bear supplementary compensation liability for the lost funds.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　保证人对债务人的注册资金提供保证的，债务人的实际投资与注册资金不符，或者抽逃转移注册资金的，保证人在注册资金不足或者抽逃转移注册资金的范围内承担连带保证责任。

**Article 27** Where a surety provides a suretyship for the registered capital of the debtor, if the debtor's actual investment does not match the registered capital, or if the debtor withdraws or transfers the registered capital, the surety shall bear joint and several suretyship liability within the scope of the insufficiency of registered capital or the amount withdrawn or transferred.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　保证期间，债权人依法将主债权转让给第三人的，保证债权同时转让，保证人在原保证担保的范围内对受让人承担保证责任。但是保证人与债权人事先约定仅对特定的债权人承担保证责任或者禁止债权转让的，保证人不再承担保证责任。

**Article 28** During the suretyship period, if the creditor lawfully assigns the principal creditor's right to a third party, the suretyship creditor's right shall be assigned simultaneously, and the surety shall bear suretyship liability to the assignee within the scope of the original suretyship. However, if the surety and the creditor have previously agreed that the surety shall bear suretyship liability only to a specific creditor or that the assignment of the creditor's right is prohibited, the surety shall no longer bear suretyship liability.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　保证期间，债权人许可债务人转让部分债务未经保证人书面同意的，保证人对未经其同意转让部分的债务，不再承担保证责任。但是，保证人仍应当对未转让部分的债务承担保证责任。

**Article 29** During the suretyship period, if the creditor permits the debtor to assign part of the debt without the written consent of the surety, the surety shall no longer bear suretyship liability for the part of the debt assigned without its consent. However, the surety shall still bear suretyship liability for the part of the debt not assigned.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　保证期间，债权人与债务人对主合同数量、价款、币种、利率等内容作了变动，未经保证人同意的，如果减轻债务人的债务的，保证人仍应当对变更后的合同承担保证责任；如果加重债务人的债务的，保证人对加重的部分不承担保证责任。  
债权人与债务人对主合同履行期限作了变动，未经保证人书面同意的，保证期间为原合同约定的或者法律规定的期间。  
债权人与债务人协议变动主合同内容，但并未实际履行的，保证人仍应当承担保证责任。

**Article 30** During the suretyship period, if the creditor and the debtor modify the content of the principal contract, such as the quantity, price, currency, interest rate, etc., without the consent of the surety, if the debtor's debt is reduced, the surety shall still bear suretyship liability for the modified contract; if the debtor's debt is increased, the surety shall not bear suretyship liability for the increased portion.  
If the creditor and the debtor modify the performance period of the principal contract without the written consent of the surety, the suretyship period shall be the period as agreed in the original contract or as provided by law.  
If the creditor and the debtor agree to modify the content of the principal contract but do not actually perform it, the surety shall still bear suretyship liability.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　保证期间不因任何事由发生中断、中止、延长的法律后果。

**Article 31** The suretyship period shall not be subject to the legal consequences of suspension, interruption, or extension for any reason.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　保证合同约定的保证期间早于或者等于主债务履行期限的，视为没有约定，保证期间为主债务履行期届满之日起六个月。  
保证合同约定保证人承担保证责任直至主债务本息还清时为止等类似内容的，视为约定不明，保证期间为主债务履行期届满之日起二年。

**Article 32** If the suretyship period agreed in the suretyship contract is earlier than or equal to the performance period of the principal debt, it shall be deemed as no agreement, and the suretyship period shall be six months from the date of expiry of the performance period of the principal debt.  
If the suretyship contract stipulates that the surety shall bear suretyship liability until the principal debt and interest are fully paid off, or contains similar content, it shall be deemed as unclear agreement, and the suretyship period shall be two years from the date of expiry of the performance period of the principal debt.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　主合同对主债务履行期限没有约定或者约定不明的，保证期间自债权人要求债务人履行义务的宽限期届满之日起计算。

**Article 33** Where the principal contract does not stipulate or does not clearly stipulate the performance period of the principal debt, the suretyship period shall commence from the date of expiry of the grace period granted by the creditor to the debtor for performing the obligation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　一般保证的债权人在保证期间届满前对债务人提起诉讼或者申请仲裁的，从判决或者仲裁裁决生效之日起，开始计算保证合同的诉讼时效。  
连带责任保证的债权人在保证期间届满前要求保证人承担保证责任的，从债权人要求保证人承担保证责任之日起，开始计算保证合同的诉讼时效。

**Article 34** Where the creditor under a general suretyship files an action against the debtor or applies for arbitration before the expiry of the suretyship period, the limitation of action for the suretyship contract shall commence from the date on which the judgment or arbitration award takes effect.  
Where the creditor under a suretyship with joint and several liability demands the surety to bear suretyship liability before the expiry of the suretyship period, the limitation of action for the suretyship contract shall commence from the date on which the creditor demands the surety to bear suretyship liability.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　保证人对已经超过诉讼时效期间的债务承担保证责任或者提供保证的，又以超过诉讼时效为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 35** If a surety bears suretyship liability or provides a suretyship for a debt for which the limitation of action has already expired, and subsequently raises a defense on the ground that the limitation of action has expired, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　一般保证中，主债务诉讼时效中断，保证债务诉讼时效中断；连带责任保证中，主债务诉讼时效中断，保证债务诉讼时效不中断。  
一般保证和连带责任保证中，主债务诉讼时效中止的，保证债务的诉讼时效同时中止。

**Article 36** In a general suretyship, if the limitation of action for the principal debt is interrupted, the limitation of action for the surety debt shall be interrupted; in a suretyship with joint and several liability, if the limitation of action for the principal debt is interrupted, the limitation of action for the surety debt shall not be interrupted.  
In both a general suretyship and a suretyship with joint and several liability, if the limitation of action for the principal debt is suspended, the limitation of action for the surety debt shall be suspended simultaneously.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　最高额保证合同对保证期间没有约定或者约定不明的，如最高额保证合同约定有保证人清偿债务期限的，保证期间为清偿期限届满之日起六个月。没有约定债务清偿期限的，保证期间自最高额保证终止之日或自债权人收到保证人终止保证合同的书面通知到达之日起六个月。

**Article 37** Where the maximum-amount suretyship contract does not stipulate or does not clearly stipulate the suretyship period, if the maximum-amount suretyship contract stipulates a period for the surety to pay off the debt, the suretyship period shall be six months from the date of expiry of such payment period. If there is no such stipulation on the debt payment period, the suretyship period shall be six months from the date of termination of the maximum-amount suretyship or from the date on which the creditor receives the surety's written notice of termination of the suretyship contract.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　同一债权既有保证又有第三人提供物的担保的，债权人可以请求保证人或者物的担保人承担担保责任。当事人对保证担保的范围或者物的担保的范围没有约定或者约定不明的，承担了担保责任的担保人，可以向债务人追偿，也可以要求其他担保人清偿其应当分担的份额。  
同一债权既有保证又有物的担保的，物的担保合同被确认无效或者被撤销，或者担保物因不可抗力的原因灭失而没有代位物的，保证人仍应当按合同的约定或者法律的规定承担保证责任。  
债权人在主合同履行期届满后怠于行使担保物权，致使担保物的价值减少或者毁损、灭失的，视为债权人放弃部分或者全部物的担保。保证人在债权人放弃权利的范围内减轻或者免除保证责任。

**Article 38** Where the same creditor's right has both a suretyship and a property guaranty provided by a third party, the creditor may request either the surety or the property guarantor to bear the guaranty liability. If the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the scope of the suretyship or the property guaranty, the guarantor who has borne the guaranty liability may seek recourse against the debtor, or may demand that the other guarantor pay off the share it shall bear.  
Where the same creditor's right has both a suretyship and a property guaranty, if the property guaranty contract is confirmed null and void or is revoked, or if the guaranty property is lost due to force majeure and there is no subrogated thing, the surety shall still bear the suretyship liability in accordance with the contract or the provisions of law.  
If, after the expiration of the performance period of the principal contract, the creditor fails to exercise the security interest, causing the value of the guaranty property to be diminished or the property to be damaged or lost, the creditor shall be deemed to have waived part or all of the property guaranty. The surety shall be relieved of or have its suretyship liability reduced within the scope of the rights waived by the creditor.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　主合同当事人双方协议以新贷偿还旧贷，除保证人知道或者应当知道的外，保证人不承担民事责任。  
新贷与旧贷系同一保证人的，不适用前款的规定。

**Article 39** Where the parties to the principal contract agree to repay an old loan with a new loan, unless the surety knew or should have known, the surety shall not bear civil liability.  
If the new loan and the old loan have the same surety, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　主合同债务人采取欺诈、胁迫等手段，使保证人在违背真实意思的情况下提供保证的，债权人知道或者应当知道欺诈、胁迫事实的，按照担保法第三十条的规定处理。

**Article 40** Where the debtor under the principal contract, by means of fraud, coercion, or the like, causes the surety to provide a suretyship against its true intention, if the creditor knew or should have known of the facts of fraud or coercion, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the Security Law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　债务人与保证人共同欺骗债权人，订立主合同和保证合同的，债权人可以请求人民法院予以撤销。因此给债权人造成损失的，由保证人与债务人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 41** Where the debtor and the surety jointly deceive the creditor into concluding the principal contract and the suretyship contract, the creditor may request the People's Court to revoke them. Any loss caused to the creditor thereby shall be borne by the surety and the debtor with joint and several compensation liability.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　人民法院判决保证人承担保证责任或者赔偿责任的，应当在判决书主文中明确保证人享有担保法第三十一条规定的权利。判决书中未予明确追偿权的，保证人只能按照承担责任的事实，另行提起诉讼。  
保证人对债务人行使追偿权的诉讼时效，自保证人向债权人承担责任之日起开始计算。

**Article 42** Where the People's Court adjudges that a surety shall bear suretyship liability or compensation liability, it shall specify in the main text of the judgment that the surety enjoys the rights provided in Article 31 of the Security Law. If the judgment does not specify the right of recourse, the surety may only file a separate action based on the fact of having borne liability.  
The limitation of action for the surety to exercise the right of recourse against the debtor shall commence from the date on which the surety bears liability to the creditor.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　保证人自行履行保证责任时，其实际清偿额大于主债权范围的，保证人只能在主债权范围内对债务人行使追偿权。

**Article 43** Where a surety performs the suretyship liability on its own, if the actual amount paid exceeds the scope of the principal creditor's right, the surety may only exercise the right of recourse against the debtor within the scope of the principal creditor's right.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　保证期间，人民法院受理债务人破产案件的，债权人既可以向人民法院申报债权，也可以向保证人主张权利。  
债权人申报债权后在破产程序中未受清偿的部分，保证人仍应当承担保证责任。债权人要求保证人承担保证责任的，应当在破产程序终结后六个月内提出。

**Article 44** During the suretyship period, if the People's Court accepts a bankruptcy case of the debtor, the creditor may either file a claim with the People's Court or assert its rights against the surety.  
For the portion of the creditor's claim that is not satisfied after being filed in the bankruptcy proceedings, the surety shall still bear suretyship liability. If the creditor requires the surety to bear suretyship liability, it shall make such demand within six months after the conclusion of the bankruptcy proceedings.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　债权人知道或者应当知道债务人破产，既未申报债权也未通知保证人，致使保证人不能预先行使追偿权的，保证人在该债权在破产程序中可能受偿的范围内免除保证责任。

**Article 45** Where the creditor knows or should have known of the debtor's bankruptcy but neither files a claim nor notifies the surety, thereby preventing the surety from exercising the right of recourse in advance, the surety shall be exempted from suretyship liability within the scope of the amount that the creditor's right might have been satisfied in the bankruptcy proceedings.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　人民法院受理债务人破产案件后，债权人未申报债权的，各连带共同保证的保证人应当作为一个主体申报债权，预先行使追偿权。  
三、关于抵押部分的解释

**Article 46** After the People's Court accepts a bankruptcy case of the debtor, if the creditor does not file a claim, all sureties under a joint and several co-suretyship shall file a claim as a single entity and pre-exercise the right of recourse.  
III. Interpretation of the Part concerning Mortgage

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　以依法获准尚未建造的或者正在建造中的房屋或者其他建筑物抵押的，当事人办理了抵押物登记，人民法院可以认定抵押有效。

**Article 47** Where a mortgage is created on a house or other building that has been lawfully approved but not yet constructed or is under construction, if the parties have registered the mortgaged property, the People's Court may deem the mortgage valid.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　以法定程序确认为违法、违章的建筑物抵押的，抵押无效。

**Article 48** Where a mortgage is created on a building that has been confirmed as illegal or in violation of regulations through statutory procedures, the mortgage shall be invalid.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　以尚未办理权属证书的财产抵押的，在第一审法庭辩论终结前能够提供权利证书或者补办登记手续的，可以认定抵押有效。  
当事人未办理抵押物登记手续的，不得对抗第三人。

**Article 49** Where a mortgage is created on property for which the title certificate has not yet been obtained, if the title certificate can be provided or the registration procedures can be completed before the end of the court debate in the first instance, the mortgage may be deemed valid.  
If the parties have not gone through the registration procedures for the mortgaged property, it shall not be effective against third parties.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　以担保法第三十四条第一款所列财产一并抵押的，抵押财产的范围应当以登记的财产为准。抵押财产的价值在抵押权实现时予以确定。

**Article 50** Where the properties listed in Article 34, paragraph 1 of the Security Law are mortgaged together, the scope of the mortgaged property shall be determined by the registered property. The value of the mortgaged property shall be determined at the time of realization of the mortgage.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　抵押人所担保的债权超出其抵押物价值的，超出的部分不具有优先受偿的效力。

**Article 51** Where the creditor's right secured by the mortgagor exceeds the value of the mortgaged property, the exceeding portion shall not have the effect of priority in satisfaction.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人以农作物和与其尚未分离的土地使用权同时抵押的，土地使用权部分的抵押无效。

**Article 52** Where the parties simultaneously mortgage crops and the land-use right that has not yet been separated therefrom, the mortgage of the land-use right portion shall be invalid.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　学校、幼儿园、医院等以公益为目的的事业单位、社会团体，以其教育设施、医疗卫生设施和其他社会公益设施以外的财产为自身债务设定抵押的，人民法院可以认定抵押有效。

**Article 53** Where a public institution or social organization for public welfare purposes, such as a school, kindergarten, or hospital, creates a mortgage with property other than its educational facilities, medical and health facilities, and other public welfare facilities for its own debts, the People's Court may deem the mortgage valid.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　按份共有人以其共有财产中享有的份额设定抵押的，抵押有效。  
共同共有人以其共有财产设定抵押，未经其他共有人的同意，抵押无效。但是，其他共有人知道或者应当知道而未提出异议的视为同意，抵押有效。

**Article 54** Where a co-owner in shares creates a mortgage on the share he owns in the co-owned property, the mortgage shall be valid.  
Where a joint co-owner creates a mortgage on the co-owned property without the consent of the other co-owners, the mortgage shall be invalid. However, if the other co-owners knew or should have known but did not raise any objection, it shall be deemed as consent, and the mortgage shall be valid.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　已经设定抵押的财产被采取查封、扣押等财产保全或者执行措施的，不影响抵押权的效力。

**Article 55** Where property that has already been mortgaged is subjected to property preservation or enforcement measures such as seizure or attachment, the validity of the mortgage shall not be affected.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　抵押合同对被担保的主债权种类、抵押财产没有约定或者约定不明，根据主合同和抵押合同不能补正或者无法推定的，抵押不成立。  
法律规定登记生效的抵押合同签订后，抵押人违背诚实信用原则拒绝办理抵押登记致使债权人受到损失的，抵押人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 56** Where the mortgage contract does not stipulate or does not clearly stipulate the type of the principal creditor's right secured or the mortgaged property, and such deficiency cannot be remedied or inferred from the principal contract and the mortgage contract, the mortgage shall not be established.  
After a mortgage contract that becomes effective upon registration as provided by law is signed, if the mortgagor, in violation of the principle of good faith, refuses to go through the mortgage registration, thereby causing losses to the creditor, the mortgagor shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　当事人在抵押合同中约定，债务履行期届满抵押权人未受清偿时，抵押物的所有权转移为债权人所有的内容无效。该内容的无效不影响抵押合同其他部分内容的效力。  
债务履行期届满后抵押权人未受清偿时，抵押权人和抵押人可以协议以抵押物折价取得抵押物。但是，损害顺序在后的担保物权人和其他债权人利益的，人民法院可以适用合同法第七十四条、第七十五条的有关规定。

**Article 57** Where the parties stipulate in the mortgage contract that if the mortgagee is not satisfied upon expiration of the debt performance period, the ownership of the mortgaged property shall be transferred to the creditor, such stipulation shall be null and void. The invalidity of such stipulation shall not affect the validity of the other parts of the mortgage contract.  
Where the mortgagee is not satisfied upon expiration of the debt performance period, the mortgagee and the mortgagor may agree to acquire the mortgaged property by converting it into value. However, if the interests of subsequent security interest holders or other creditors are prejudiced, the People's Court may apply the relevant provisions of Articles 74 and 75 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　当事人同一天在不同的法定登记部门办理抵押物登记的，视为顺序相同。  
因登记部门的原因致使抵押物进行连续登记的，抵押物第一次登记的日期，视为抵押登记的日期，并依此确定抵押权的顺序。

**Article 58** Where the parties register the mortgaged property with different statutory registration authorities on the same day, the registration shall be deemed to have the same rank.  
Where the mortgaged property is registered consecutively due to reasons attributable to the registration authority, the date of the first registration of the mortgaged property shall be deemed the date of mortgage registration, and the rank of the mortgage shall be determined accordingly.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　当事人办理抵押物登记手续时，因登记部门的原因致使其无法办理抵押物登记，抵押人向债权人交付权利凭证的，可以认定债权人对该财产有优先受偿权。但是，未办理抵押物登记的，不得对抗第三人。

**Article 59** Where, when the parties go through the registration procedures for the mortgaged property, they are unable to complete the registration due to reasons attributable to the registration authority, if the mortgagor delivers the title certificate to the creditor, the creditor may be deemed to have the right of priority in satisfaction over such property. However, if the mortgaged property is not registered, it shall not be effective against third parties.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　以担保法第四十二条第（二）项规定的不动产抵押的，县级以上地方人民政府对登记部门未作规定，当事人在土地管理部门或者房产管理部门办理了抵押物登记手续，人民法院可以确认其登记的效力。

**Article 60** Where a mortgage is created on the immovable property specified in Article 42, item (2) of the Security Law, and the local people's government at or above the county level has not stipulated the registration authority, if the parties have gone through the registration procedures for the mortgaged property with the land administration department or the real estate management department, the People's Court may confirm the validity of such registration.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　抵押物登记记载的内容与抵押合同约定的内容不一致的，以登记记载的内容为准。

**Article 61** Where the content recorded in the registration of the mortgaged property is inconsistent with the content stipulated in the mortgage contract, the content recorded in the registration shall prevail.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　抵押物因附合、混合或者加工使抵押物的所有权为第三人所有的，抵押权的效力及于补偿金；抵押物所有人为附合物、混合物或者加工物的所有人的，抵押权的效力及于附合物、混合物或者加工物；第三人与抵押物所有人为附合物、混合物或者加工物的共有人的，抵押权的效力及于抵押人对共有物享有的份额。

**Article 62** Where the ownership of the mortgaged property becomes vested in a third party due to accession, mixture, or processing, the effect of the mortgage shall extend to the compensation; where the owner of the mortgaged property becomes the owner of the accession, mixture, or processed thing, the effect of the mortgage shall extend to the accession, mixture, or processed thing; where the third party and the owner of the mortgaged property become co-owners of the accession, mixture, or processed thing, the effect of the mortgage shall extend to the share of the mortgagor in the co-owned thing.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　抵押权设定前为抵押物的从物的，抵押权的效力及于抵押物的从物。但是，抵押物与其从物为两个以上的人分别所有时，抵押权的效力不及于抵押物的从物。

**Article 63** Where an accessory becomes an accessory to the mortgaged property before the establishment of the mortgage, the effect of the mortgage shall extend to such accessory. However, where the mortgaged property and its accessory are respectively owned by two or more persons, the effect of the mortgage shall not extend to the accessory of the mortgaged property.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　债务履行期届满，债务人不履行债务致使抵押物被人民法院依法扣押的，自扣押之日起抵押权人收取的由抵押物分离的天然孳息和法定孳息，按照下列顺序清偿：  
（一）收取孳息的费用；  
（二）主债权的利息；  
（三）主债权。

**Article 64** Where, upon expiration of the period for performance of the obligation, the debtor fails to perform the obligation, causing the mortgaged property to be lawfully seized by the People's Court, the natural fruits and statutory fruits derived from the mortgaged property and collected by the mortgagee from the date of seizure shall be applied to satisfy the claim in the following order:  
(1) expenses for collecting the fruits;  
(2) interest on the principal claim;  
(3) the principal claim.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　抵押人将已出租的财产抵押的，抵押权实现后，租赁合同在有效期内对抵押物的受让人继续有效。

**Article 65** Where the mortgagor mortgages a property that has been leased, after the enforcement of the mortgage, the lease contract shall remain valid against the transferee of the mortgaged property during its term of validity.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　抵押人将已抵押的财产出租的，抵押权实现后，租赁合同对受让人不具有约束力。  
抵押人将已抵押的财产出租时，如果抵押人未书面告知承租人该财产已抵押的，抵押人对出租抵押物造成承租人的损失承担赔偿责任；如果抵押人已书面告知承租人该财产已抵押的，抵押权实现造成承租人的损失，由承租人自己承担。

**Article 66** Where the mortgagor leases out a property that has been mortgaged, after the enforcement of the mortgage, the lease contract shall not be binding on the transferee.  
Where the mortgagor leases out a property that has been mortgaged, if the mortgagor fails to notify the lessee in writing that the property has been mortgaged, the mortgagor shall bear compensation liability for the losses caused to the lessee by the leasing of the mortgaged property; if the mortgagor has notified the lessee in writing that the property has been mortgaged, any loss caused to the lessee by the enforcement of the mortgage shall be borne by the lessee itself.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　抵押权存续期间，抵押人转让抵押物未通知抵押权人或者未告知受让人的，如果抵押物已经登记的，抵押权人仍可以行使抵押权；取得抵押物所有权的受让人，可以代替债务人清偿其全部债务，使抵押权消灭。受让人清偿债务后可以向抵押人追偿。  
如果抵押物未经登记的，抵押权不得对抗受让人，因此给抵押权人造成损失的，由抵押人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 67** Where, during the period of existence of the mortgage, the mortgagor transfers the mortgaged property without notifying the mortgagee or informing the transferee, if the mortgaged property has been registered, the mortgagee may still exercise the mortgage; the transferee who has acquired the ownership of the mortgaged property may discharge all the debts of the debtor in place of the debtor, thereby extinguishing the mortgage. After discharging the debts, the transferee may claim recourse against the mortgagor.  
If the mortgaged property has not been registered, the mortgage shall not be asserted against the transferee. If losses are thereby caused to the mortgagee, the mortgagor shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　抵押物依法被继承或者赠与的，抵押权不受影响。

**Article 68** Where the mortgaged property is succeeded to or donated according to law, the mortgage shall not be affected.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　债务人有多个普通债权人的，在清偿债务时，债务人与其中一个债权人恶意串通，将其全部或者部分财产抵押给该债权人，因此丧失了履行其他债务的能力，损害了其他债权人的合法权益，受损害的其他债权人可以请求人民法院撤销该抵押行为。

**Article 69** Where a debtor has multiple ordinary creditors, if, when discharging debts, the debtor maliciously colludes with one of the creditors to mortgage all or part of its property to that creditor, thereby losing the capacity to perform other debts and harming the lawful rights and interests of other creditors, the other creditors who have suffered harm may request the People's Court to revoke such mortgage.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　抵押人的行为足以使抵押物价值减少的，抵押权人请求抵押人恢复原状或提供担保遭到拒绝时，抵押权人可以请求债务人履行债务，也可以请求提前行使抵押权。

**Article 70** Where the mortgagor's conduct is sufficient to cause a decrease in the value of the mortgaged property, and the mortgagee's request for the mortgagor to restore the original condition or provide security is refused, the mortgagee may request the debtor to perform the obligation, or may request to exercise the mortgage before maturity.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　主债权未受全部清偿的，抵押权人可以就抵押物的全部行使其抵押权。  
抵押物被分割或者部分转让的，抵押权人可以就分割或者转让后的抵押物行使抵押权。

**Article 71** Where the principal claim is not fully satisfied, the mortgagee may exercise its mortgage over the entire mortgaged property.  
Where the mortgaged property is partitioned or partially transferred, the mortgagee may exercise the mortgage over the partitioned or transferred mortgaged property.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　主债权被分割或者部分转让的，各债权人可以就其享有的债权份额行使抵押权。  
主债务被分割或者部分转让的，抵押人仍以其抵押物担保数个债务人履行债务。但是，第三人提供抵押的，债权人许可债务人转让债务未经抵押人书面同意的，抵押人对未经其同意转让的债务，不再承担担保责任。

**Article 72** Where the principal claim is partitioned or partially transferred, each creditor may exercise the mortgage to the extent of the creditor's share of the claim.  
Where the principal obligation is partitioned or partially transferred, the mortgagor shall still secure the performance of the obligations by the several debtors with its mortgaged property. However, where a third party provides the mortgage, if the creditor permits the debtor to transfer the obligation without the written consent of the mortgagor, the mortgagor shall no longer bear the security liability for the obligation transferred without its consent.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　抵押物折价或者拍卖、变卖该抵押物的价款低于抵押权设定时约定价值的，应当按照抵押物实现的价值进行清偿。不足清偿的剩余部分，由债务人清偿。

**Article 73** Where the price obtained from converting the mortgaged property into money, or from auctioning or selling off the mortgaged property, is lower than the value agreed upon at the time of establishment of the mortgage, the claim shall be satisfied according to the realized value of the mortgaged property. Any remaining part not fully satisfied shall be discharged by the debtor.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　抵押物折价或者拍卖、变卖所得的价款，当事人没有约定的，按下列顺序清偿：  
（一）实现抵押权的费用；  
（二）主债权的利息；  
（三）主债权。

**Article 74** Where the parties have not agreed otherwise, the proceeds from converting the mortgaged property into money, or from auctioning or selling off the mortgaged property, shall be applied to satisfy the claim in the following order:  
(1) expenses for enforcing the mortgage;  
(2) interest on the principal claim;  
(3) the principal claim.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　同一债权有两个以上抵押人的，债权人放弃债务人提供的抵押担保的，其他抵押人可以请求人民法院减轻或者免除其应当承担的担保责任。  
同一债权有两个以上抵押人的，当事人对其提供的抵押财产所担保的债权份额或者顺序没有约定或者约定不明的，抵押权人可以就其中任一或者各个财产行使抵押权。  
抵押人承担担保责任后，可以向债务人追偿，也可以要求其他抵押人清偿其应当承担的份额。

**Article 75** Where there are two or more mortgagors for the same claim, if the creditor abandons the mortgage security provided by the debtor, the other mortgagors may request the People's Court to reduce or remit the security liability that they shall bear.  
Where there are two or more mortgagors for the same claim, and the parties have not agreed or the agreement is unclear on the share of the claim or the order secured by the mortgaged property they provide, the mortgagee may exercise the mortgage over any one or each of such properties.  
After bearing the security liability, the mortgagor may claim recourse against the debtor, or may require the other mortgagors to discharge the shares they shall bear.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　同一动产向两个以上债权人抵押的，当事人未办理抵押物登记，实现抵押权时，各抵押权人按照债权比例受偿。

**Article 76** Where the same chattel is mortgaged to two or more creditors, and the parties have not registered the mortgage, when the mortgage is enforced, each mortgagee shall be satisfied in proportion to its claim.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　同一财产向两个以上债权人抵押的，顺序在先的抵押权与该财产的所有权归属一人时，该财产的所有权人可以以其抵押权对抗顺序在后的抵押权。

**Article 77** Where the same property is mortgaged to two or more creditors, if the mortgage with a prior ranking and the ownership of such property vest in the same person, the owner of the property may assert its mortgage against the mortgage with a subsequent ranking.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　同一财产向两个以上债权人抵押的，顺序在后的抵押权所担保的债权先到期的，抵押权人只能就抵押物价值超出顺序在先的抵押担保债权的部分受偿。  
顺序在先的抵押权所担保的债权先到期的，抵押权实现后的剩余价款应予提存，留待清偿顺序在后的抵押担保债权。

**Article 78** Where the same property is mortgaged to two or more creditors, if the claim secured by the mortgage with a subsequent ranking falls due first, the mortgagee may only be satisfied out of the value of the mortgaged property exceeding the claim secured by the prior ranking mortgage.  
If the claim secured by the mortgage with a prior ranking falls due first, the remaining proceeds after enforcement of the mortgage shall be deposited to be available for satisfying the claim secured by the subsequent ranking mortgage.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　同一财产法定登记的抵押权与质权并存时，抵押权人优先于质权人受偿。  
同一财产抵押权与留置权并存时，留置权人优先于抵押权人受偿。

**Article 79** Where a legally registered mortgage and a pledge coexist on the same property, the mortgagee shall be satisfied in preference to the pledgee.  
Where a mortgage and a lien coexist on the same property, the lienholder shall be satisfied in preference to the mortgagee.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　在抵押物灭失、毁损或者被征用的情况下，抵押权人可以就该抵押物的保险金、赔偿金或者补偿金优先受偿。  
抵押物灭失、毁损或者被征用的情况下，抵押权所担保的债权未届清偿期的，抵押权人可以请求人民法院对保险金、赔偿金或补偿金等采取保全措施。

**Article 80** In the event of destruction, damage, or expropriation of the mortgaged property, the mortgagee may be satisfied in preference out of the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity for the mortgaged property.  
In the event of destruction, damage, or expropriation of the mortgaged property, if the claim secured by the mortgage has not yet fallen due, the mortgagee may request the People's Court to adopt preservation measures with respect to the insurance proceeds, compensation, or indemnity, etc.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　最高额抵押权所担保的债权范围，不包括抵押物因财产保全或者执行程序被查封后或债务人、抵押人破产后发生的债权。

**Article 81** The scope of claims secured by a maximum amount mortgage shall not include claims arising after the mortgaged property is sealed up due to property preservation or enforcement proceedings, or after the debtor or mortgagor becomes bankrupt.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　当事人对最高额抵押合同的最高限额、最高额抵押期间进行变更，以其变更对抗顺序在后的抵押权人的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 82** Where the parties modify the maximum amount or the period of a maximum amount mortgage contract, and assert such modification against a mortgagee with a subsequent ranking, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　最高额抵押权所担保的不特定债权，在特定后，债权已届清偿期的，最高额抵押权人可以根据普通抵押权的规定行使其抵押权。  
抵押权人实现最高额抵押权时，如果实际发生的债权余额高于最高限额的，以最高限额为限，超过部分不具有优先受偿的效力；如果实际发生的债权余额低于最高限额的，以实际发生的债权余额为限对抵押物优先受偿。  
四、关于质押部分的解释  
（一）动产质押

**Article 83** Where the unspecified claims secured by a maximum amount mortgage become specified and have fallen due, the maximum amount mortgagee may exercise its mortgage in accordance with the provisions on ordinary mortgages.  
Where the mortgagee enforces the maximum amount mortgage, if the actual balance of claims is higher than the maximum amount, the maximum amount shall be the limit, and the excess shall not have the effect of preferred satisfaction; if the actual balance of claims is lower than the maximum amount, the mortgagee shall be satisfied in preference out of the mortgaged property to the extent of the actual balance of claims.  
IV. Interpretation on the Part concerning Pledge  
(1) Pledge of Chattels

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　出质人以其不具有所有权但合法占有的动产出质的，不知出质人无处分权的质权人行使质权后，因此给动产所有人造成损失的，由出质人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 84** Where the pledgor pledges a chattel over which it does not have ownership but which it lawfully possesses, and the pledgee, who does not know that the pledgor has no right of disposal, exercises the pledge, if losses are thereby caused to the owner of the chattel, the pledgor shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　债务人或者第三人将其金钱以特户、封金、保证金等形式特定化后，移交债权人占有作为债权的担保，债务人不履行债务时，债权人可以以该金钱优先受偿。

**Article 85** Where a debtor or a third party specificizes its money in the form of a special account, sealed money, deposit, or the like, and delivers it to the creditor for possession as security for the claim, if the debtor fails to perform the obligation, the creditor may be satisfied in preference out of such money.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　债务人或者第三人未按质押合同约定的时间移交质物的，因此给质权人造成损失的，出质人应当根据其过错承担赔偿责任。

**Article 86** Where the debtor or third party fails to deliver the pledged property at the time agreed in the pledge contract, thereby causing losses to the pledgee, the pledgor shall bear compensation liability according to its fault.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　出质人代质权人占有质物的，质押合同不生效；质权人将质物返还于出质人后，以其质权对抗第三人的，人民法院不予支持。  
因不可归责于质权人的事由而丧失对质物的占有，质权人可以向不当占有人请求停止侵害、恢复原状、返还质物。

**Article 87** If the pledgor possesses the pledged property on behalf of the pledgee, the pledge contract shall not take effect; if the pledgee, after returning the pledged property to the pledgor, asserts its pledge against a third party, the People's Court shall not support it.  
Where the pledgee loses possession of the pledged property due to a cause not attributable to it, the pledgee may request the wrongful possessor to cease the infringement, restore the original condition, and return the pledged property.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　出质人以间接占有的财产出质的，质押合同自书面通知送达占有人时视为移交。占有人收到出质通知后，仍接受出质人的指示处分出质财产的，该行为无效。

**Article 88** Where the pledgor pledges property in its indirect possession, the pledge contract shall be deemed to have been delivered when the written notice is served on the possessor. If, after receiving the notice of pledge, the possessor still accepts the pledgor's instructions to dispose of the pledged property, such act shall be void.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　质押合同中对质押的财产约定不明，或者约定的出质财产与实际移交的财产不一致的，以实际交付占有的财产为准。

**Article 89** If, in a pledge contract, the pledged property is not clearly agreed upon, or the agreed pledged property is inconsistent with the property actually delivered, the property actually delivered into possession shall prevail.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　质物有隐蔽瑕疵造成质权人其他财产损害的，应由出质人承担赔偿责任。但是，质权人在质物移交时明知质物有瑕疵而予以接受的除外。

**Article 90** If the pledged property has latent defects causing damage to other property of the pledgee, the pledgor shall bear compensation liability. However, this shall not apply if the pledgee, at the time of delivery of the pledged property, knew of the defects and accepted it.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　动产质权的效力及于质物的从物。但是，从物未随同质物移交质权人占有的，质权的效力不及于从物。

**Article 91** The effect of a chattel pledge extends to the accessories of the pledged property. However, if the accessories are not delivered to the pledgee for possession together with the pledged property, the effect of the pledge shall not extend to the accessories.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　按照担保法第六十九条的规定将质物提存的，质物提存费用由质权人负担；出质人提前清偿债权的，应当扣除未到期部分的利息。

**Article 92** Where the pledged property is deposited in accordance with Article 69 of the Security Law, the deposit expenses shall be borne by the pledgee; if the pledgor discharges the claim before maturity, the interest on the unmatured portion shall be deducted.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　质权人在质权存续期间，未经出质人同意，擅自使用、出租、处分质物，因此给出质人造成损失的，由质权人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 93** Where, during the period of existence of the pledge, the pledgee, without the consent of the pledgor, uses, leases, or disposes of the pledged property without authorization, thereby causing losses to the pledgor, the pledgee shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　质权人在质权存续期间，为担保自己的债务，经出质人同意，以其所占有的质物为第三人设定质权的，应当在原质权所担保的债权范围之内，超过的部分不具有优先受偿的效力。转质权的效力优于原质权。  
质权人在质权存续期间，未经出质人同意，为担保自己的债务，在其所占有的质物上为第三人设定质权的无效。质权人对因转质而发生的损害承担赔偿责任。

**Article 94** Where, during the period of existence of the pledge, the pledgee, with the consent of the pledgor, creates a pledge over the pledged property in its possession for a third party to secure its own debts, such creation shall be within the scope of the claim secured by the original pledge, and any excess shall not have the effect of preferred satisfaction. The sub-pledge shall have priority over the original pledge.  
Where, during the period of existence of the pledge, the pledgee, without the consent of the pledgor, creates a pledge over the pledged property in its possession for a third party to secure its own debts, such creation shall be void. The pledgee shall bear compensation liability for any damage caused by the sub-pledge.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　债务履行期届满质权人未受清偿的，质权人可以继续留置质物，并以质物的全部行使权利。出质人清偿所担保的债权后，质权人应当返还质物。  
债务履行期届满，出质人请求质权人及时行使权利，而质权人怠于行使权利致使质物价格下跌的，由此造成的损失，质权人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 95** Where the pledgee has not been satisfied upon expiration of the period for performance of the obligation, the pledgee may continue to retain the pledged property and exercise its rights over the entire pledged property. After the pledgor discharges the secured claim, the pledgee shall return the pledged property.  
Where, upon expiration of the period for performance of the obligation, the pledgor requests the pledgee to exercise its rights in a timely manner, but the pledgee neglects to exercise its rights, causing the price of the pledged property to fall, the pledgee shall bear compensation liability for the losses thereby incurred.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　[本解释第五十七条](#art-57)、第六十二条、第六十四条、第七十一条、第七十二条、第七十三条、第七十四条、第八十条之规定，适用于动产质押。  
（二）权利质押

**Article 96** The provisions of Articles 57, 62, 64, 71, 72, 73, 74, and 80 of this Interpretation shall apply to the pledge of chattels.  
(2) Pledge of Rights

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　以公路桥梁、公路隧道或者公路渡口等不动产收益权出质的，按照担保法第七十五条第（四）项的规定处理。

**Article 97** Where the right to collect income from immovable properties such as highway bridges, highway tunnels, or highway ferries is pledged, it shall be handled in accordance with Article 75(4) of the Security Law.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　以汇票、支票、本票出质，出质人与质权人没有背书记载“质押”字样，以票据出质对抗善意第三人的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 98** Where a bill of exchange, cheque, or promissory note is pledged, if the pledgor and the pledgee have not endorsed the words “pledge” on the back, and the pledge is asserted against a bona fide third party, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　以公司债券出质的，出质人与质权人没有背书记载“质押”字样，以债券出质对抗公司和第三人的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 99** Where a corporate bond is pledged, if the pledgor and the pledgee have not endorsed the words “pledge” on the back, and the pledge is asserted against the company or a third party, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　以存款单出质的，签发银行核押后又受理挂失并造成存款流失的，应当承担民事责任。

**Article 100** Where a certificate of deposit is pledged, if the issuing bank, after verifying the pledge, accepts a report of loss and causes loss of the deposit, it shall bear civil liability.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　以票据、债券、存款单、仓单、提单出质的，质权人再转让或者质押的无效。

**Article 101** Where instruments, bonds, certificates of deposit, warehouse receipts, or bills of lading are pledged, any further transfer or pledge by the pledgee shall be void.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　以载明兑现或者提货日期的汇票、支票、本票、债券、存款单、仓单、提单出质的，其兑现或者提货日期后于债务履行期的，质权人只能在兑现或者提货日期届满时兑现款项或者提取货物。

**Article 102** Where a bill of exchange, cheque, promissory note, bond, certificate of deposit, warehouse receipt, or bill of lading that specifies a date for payment or delivery of goods is pledged, if the date for payment or delivery of goods is later than the period for performance of the obligation, the pledgee may only collect the money or take delivery of the goods upon the expiration of the date for payment or delivery of goods.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　以股份有限公司的股份出质的，适用《中华人民共和国公司法》有关股份转让的规定。  
以上市公司的股份出质的，质押合同自股份出质向证券登记机构办理出质登记之日起生效。  
以非上市公司的股份出质的，质押合同自股份出质记载于股东名册之日起生效。

**Article 103** Where shares of a company limited by shares are pledged, the provisions on share transfer in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.  
Where shares of a listed company are pledged, the pledge contract shall take effect from the date the pledge of shares is registered with the securities registration institution.  
Where shares of a non-listed company are pledged, the pledge contract shall take effect from the date the pledge of shares is recorded in the register of shareholders.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　以依法可以转让的股份、股票出质的，质权的效力及于股份、股票的法定孳息。

**Article 104** Where shares or stocks that may be transferred according to law are pledged, the effect of the pledge shall extend to the statutory fruits of such shares or stocks.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　以依法可以转让的商标专用权，专利权、著作权中的财产权出质的，出质人未经质权人同意而转让或者许可他人使用已出质权利的，应当认定为无效。因此给质权人或者第三人造成损失的，由出质人承担民事责任。

**Article 105** Where the right to exclusive use of a trademark, patent right, or property rights in copyright that may be transferred according to law are pledged, if the pledgor, without the consent of the pledgee, transfers or licenses others to use the pledged rights, such act shall be deemed void. If losses are thereby caused to the pledgee or a third party, the pledgor shall bear civil liability.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　质权人向出质人、出质债权的债务人行使质权时，出质人、出质债权的债务人拒绝的，质权人可以起诉出质人和出质债权的债务人，也可以单独起诉出质债权的债务人。  
五、关于留置部分的解释

**Article 106** Where the pledgee exercises the pledge against the pledgor or the debtor of the pledged claim, and the pledgor or the debtor of the pledged claim refuses, the pledgee may bring a prosecution against the pledgor and the debtor of the pledged claim, or may separately bring a prosecution against the debtor of the pledged claim.  
V. Interpretation on the Part concerning Lien

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　当事人在合同中约定排除留置权，债务履行期届满，债权人行使留置权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 107** Where the parties agree in the contract to exclude the lien, and upon expiration of the period for performance of the obligation, the creditor exercises the lien, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　债权人合法占有债务人交付的动产时，不知债务人无处分该动产的权利，债权人可以按照担保法第八十二条的规定行使留置权。

**Article 108** Where a creditor lawfully possesses a chattel delivered by the debtor, and does not know that the debtor has no right to dispose of the chattel, the creditor may exercise a lien in accordance with Article 82 of the Security Law.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　债权人的债权已届清偿期，债权人对动产的占有与其债权的发生有牵连关系，债权人可以留置其所占有的动产。

**Article 109** Where the creditor's claim has fallen due, and the creditor's possession of the chattel is related to the occurrence of the claim, the creditor may retain the chattel in its possession.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　留置权人在债权未受全部清偿前，留置物为不可分物的，留置权人可以就其留置物的全部行使留置权。

**Article 110** Where the retained property is an indivisible thing, the lienholder may exercise the lien over the entire retained property before the claim is fully satisfied.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　债权人行使留置权与其承担的义务或者合同的特殊约定相抵触的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 111** Where the creditor's exercise of the lien is inconsistent with its obligations or a special agreement in the contract, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　债权人的债权未届清偿期，其交付占有标的物的义务已届履行期的，不能行使留置权。但是，债权人能够证明债务人无支付能力的除外。

**Article 112** Where the creditor's claim has not fallen due but its obligation to deliver the possessed subject matter has become performable, it may not exercise the lien. However, this shall not apply if the creditor can prove that the debtor is unable to pay.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　债权人未按担保法第八十七条规定的期限通知债务人履行义务，直接变价处分留置物的，应当对此造成的损失承担赔偿责任。债权人与债务人按照担保法第八十七条的规定在合同中约定宽限期的，债权人可以不经通知，直接行使留置权。

**Article 113** Where a creditor fails to notify the debtor to perform its obligation within the period prescribed in Article 87 of the Security Law and directly disposes of the retained property by converting it into money, the creditor shall bear compensation liability for the losses thereby incurred. If the creditor and the debtor have agreed on a grace period in the contract in accordance with Article 87 of the Security Law, the creditor may directly exercise the lien without giving notice.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　[本解释第六十四条](#art-64)、第八十条、第八十七条、第九十一条、第九十三条的规定，适用于留置。  
六、关于定金部分的解释

**Article 114** The provisions of Articles 64, 80, 87, 91, and 93 of this Interpretation shall apply to liens.  
VI. Interpretation on the Part concerning Deposit

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　当事人约定以交付定金作为订立主合同担保的，给付定金的一方拒绝订立主合同的，无权要求返还定金；收受定金的一方拒绝订立合同的，应当双倍返还定金。

**Article 115** Where the parties agree to deliver a deposit as security for the conclusion of a main contract, if the party paying the deposit refuses to conclude the main contract, it shall not be entitled to demand the return of the deposit; if the party receiving the deposit refuses to conclude the contract, it shall return the deposit twice the amount.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　当事人约定以交付定金作为主合同成立或者生效要件的，给付定金的一方未支付定金，但主合同已经履行或者已经履行主要部分的，不影响主合同的成立或者生效。

**Article 116** Where the parties agree that the delivery of a deposit shall be a condition for the formation or taking effect of the main contract, if the party paying the deposit fails to pay the deposit, but the main contract has been performed in whole or in substantial part, the formation or taking effect of the main contract shall not be affected.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　定金交付后，交付定金的一方可以按照合同的约定以丧失定金为代价而解除主合同，收受定金的一方可以双倍返还定金为代价而解除主合同。对解除主合同后责任的处理，适用《中华人民共和国合同法》的规定。

**Article 117** After the deposit is delivered, the party paying the deposit may, at the cost of forfeiting the deposit, rescind the main contract in accordance with the contract; the party receiving the deposit may, at the cost of returning twice the amount of the deposit, rescind the main contract. The handling of liability after rescission of the main contract shall be governed by the provisions of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　当事人交付留置金、担保金、保证金、订约金、押金或者订金等，但没有约定定金性质的，当事人主张定金权利的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 118** Where a party delivers a retention money, guarantee money, deposit, earnest money, security deposit, or booking deposit, but the parties have not agreed that it shall have the nature of a deposit, if a party claims the rights of a deposit, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　实际交付的定金数额多于或者少于约定数额，视为变更定金合同；收受定金一方提出异议并拒绝接受定金的，定金合同不生效。

**Article 119** Where the amount of deposit actually delivered exceeds or is less than the agreed amount, the deposit contract shall be deemed to have been modified; if the party receiving the deposit raises an objection and refuses to accept the deposit, the deposit contract shall not take effect.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　因当事人一方迟延履行或者其他违约行为，致使合同目的不能实现，可以适用定金罚则。但法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。  
当事人一方不完全履行合同的，应当按照未履行部分所占合同约定内容的比例，适用定金罚则。

**Article 120** Where the purpose of the contract cannot be realized due to delay in performance or other breach of contract by one party, the deposit penalty rule may apply. However, this shall not apply if the law provides otherwise or the parties have agreed otherwise.  
Where one party does not fully perform the contract, the deposit penalty rule shall apply in proportion to the part of the contract not performed.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　当事人约定的定金数额超过主合同标的额百分之二十的，超过的部分，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 121** Where the deposit amount agreed by the parties exceeds 20% of the subject matter amount of the main contract, the excess shall not be supported by the People's Court.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　因不可抗力、意外事件致使主合同不能履行的，不适用定金罚则。因合同关系以外第三人的过错，致使主合同不能履行的，适用定金罚则。受定金处罚的一方当事人，可以依法向第三人追偿。  
七、关于其他问题的解释

**Article 122** Where the main contract cannot be performed due to force majeure or an accident, the deposit penalty rule shall not apply. Where the main contract cannot be performed due to the fault of a third party outside the contractual relationship, the deposit penalty rule shall apply. The party subject to the deposit penalty may claim recourse against the third party according to law.  
VII. Interpretation on Other Issues

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　同一债权上数个担保物权并存时，债权人放弃债务人提供的物的担保的，其他担保人在其放弃权利的范围内减轻或者免除担保责任。

**Article 123** Where several security interests coexist on the same claim, if the creditor abandons the security interest in property provided by the debtor, the other sureties shall have their security liability reduced or exempted to the extent of the rights abandoned.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　企业法人的分支机构为他人提供保证的，人民法院在审理保证纠纷案件中可以将该企业法人作为共同被告参加诉讼。但是商业银行、保险公司的分支机构提供保证的除外。

**Article 124** Where a branch of an enterprise legal person provides a suretyship for another person, the People's Court may, in adjudicating a suretyship dispute, join the enterprise legal person as a co-defendant in the proceedings. However, this shall not apply where a branch of a commercial bank or an insurance company provides the suretyship.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　一般保证的债权人向债务人和保证人一并提起诉讼的，人民法院可以将债务人和保证人列为共同被告参加诉讼。但是，应当在判决书中明确在对债务人财产依法强制执行后仍不能履行债务时，由保证人承担保证责任。

**Article 125** Where, in a general suretyship, the creditor brings a prosecution against both the debtor and the surety, the People's Court may list the debtor and the surety as co-defendants to participate in the proceedings. However, it shall be specified in the judgment that the surety shall bear the suretyship liability only after compulsory enforcement against the debtor's property according to law still fails to satisfy the obligation.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　连带责任保证的债权人可以将债务人或者保证人作为被告提起诉讼，也可以将债务人和保证人作为共同被告提起诉讼。

**Article 126** In a suretyship with joint and several liability, the creditor may bring a prosecution against either the debtor or the surety as the defendant, or may bring a prosecution against both the debtor and the surety as co-defendants.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　债务人对债权人提起诉讼，债权人提起反诉的，保证人可以作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 127** Where the debtor brings a prosecution against the creditor, and the creditor files a counterclaim, the surety may participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　债权人向人民法院请求行使担保物权时，债务人和担保人应当作为共同被告参加诉讼。  
同一债权既有保证又有物的担保的，当事人发生纠纷提起诉讼的，债务人与保证人、抵押人或者出质人可以作为共同被告参加诉讼。

**Article 128** Where a creditor requests the People's Court to exercise a security interest, the debtor and the guarantor shall participate in the proceedings as co-defendants.  
Where the same claim is secured by both a suretyship and a security interest in property, and a dispute arises and a prosecution is brought, the debtor, the surety, the mortgagor, or the pledgor may participate in the proceedings as co-defendants.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　主合同和担保合同发生纠纷提起诉讼的，应当根据主合同确定案件管辖。担保人承担连带责任的担保合同发生纠纷，债权人向担保人主张权利的，应当由担保人住所地的法院管辖。  
主合同和担保合同选择管辖的法院不一致的，应当根据主合同确定案件管辖。

**Article 129** Where a dispute arises over a main contract and a guarantee contract and a prosecution is brought, jurisdiction over the case shall be determined based on the main contract. Where a dispute arises over a guarantee contract under which the guarantor bears joint and several liability, and the creditor asserts rights against the guarantor, the court of the place of domicile of the guarantor shall have jurisdiction.  
If the main contract and the guarantee contract select different courts for jurisdiction, the jurisdiction over the case shall be determined based on the main contract.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　在主合同纠纷案件中，对担保合同未经审判，人民法院不应当依据对主合同当事人所作出的判决或者裁定，直接执行担保人的财产。

**Article 130** In a case concerning a dispute over a main contract, if the guarantee contract has not been adjudicated, the People's Court shall not directly enforce the property of the guarantor based on the judgment or ruling rendered against the parties to the main contract.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　本解释所称“不能清偿”指对债务人的存款、现金、有价证券、成品、半成品、原材料、交通工具等可以执行的动产和其他方便执行的财产执行完毕后，债务仍未能得到清偿的状态。

**Article 131** The term “unable to satisfy” as used in this Interpretation refers to the state where, after enforcement has been carried out against the debtor's deposits, cash, negotiable securities, finished goods, semi-finished goods, raw materials, means of transportation, and other executable chattels and other property convenient for enforcement, the obligation still remains unsatisfied.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　在案件审理或者执行程序中，当事人提供财产担保的，人民法院应当对该财产的权属证书予以扣押，同时向有关部门发出协助执行通知书，要求其在规定的时间内不予办理担保财产的转移手续。

**Article 132** Where, during the adjudication or enforcement proceedings, a party provides property as security, the People's Court shall seize the title certificate of such property and simultaneously issue a notice of assistance in enforcement to the relevant authority, requiring it not to handle the transfer procedures of the secured property within the specified time.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　担保法施行以前发生的担保行为，适用担保行为发生时的法律、法规和有关司法解释。  
担保法施行以后因担保行为发生的纠纷案件，在本解释公布施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。  
担保法施行以后因担保行为发生的纠纷案件，在本解释公布施行后尚在一审或二审阶段的，适用担保法和本解释。

**Article 133** A security act that occurred before the Security Law came into force shall be governed by the laws, regulations, and relevant judicial interpretations in effect at the time the security act occurred.  
For a dispute case arising from a security act after the Security Law came into force, if a final adjudication has been made before this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, and a party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided under the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.  
For a dispute case arising from a security act after the Security Law came into force, if it is still at the stage of first instance or second instance after this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Security Law and this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　最高人民法院在担保法施行以前作出的有关担保问题的司法解释，与担保法和本解释相抵触的，不再适用。

**Article 134** Judicial interpretations on guarantee issues issued by the Supreme People's Court prior to the coming into force of the Guarantee Law that contradict the Guarantee Law or this Interpretation shall no longer apply.
