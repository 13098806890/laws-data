# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理人身损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕14号  
**公布日期**：2022-02-15  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于审理人身损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2022年2月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1864次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年5月1日起施行。 2022年4月24日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理人身损害赔偿 案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2022〕14号 最高人民法院审判委员会第1864次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于审理人身损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》作如下修改： 一、第十二条修改为：“残疾赔偿金根据受害人丧失劳动能力程度或者伤残等级，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入标准，自定残之日起按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。 “受害人因伤致残但实际收入没有减少，或者伤残等级较轻但造成职业妨害严重影响其劳动就业的，可以对残疾赔偿金作相应调整。” 二、第十五条修改为：“死亡赔偿金按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入标准，按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。” 三、第十七条修改为：“被扶养人生活费根据扶养人丧失劳动能力程度，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均消费支出标准计算。被扶养人为未成年人的，计算至十八周岁；被扶养人无劳动能力又无其他生活来源的，计算二十年。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。 “被扶养人是指受害人依法应当承担扶养义务的未成年人或者丧失劳动能力又无其他生活来源的成年近亲属。被扶养人还有其他扶养人的，赔偿义务人只赔偿受害人依法应当负担的部分。被扶养人有数人的，年赔偿总额累计不超过上一年度城镇居民人均消费支出额。” 四、第十八条修改为：“赔偿权利人举证证明其住所地或者经常居住地城镇居民人均可支配收入高于受诉法院所在地标准的，残疾赔偿金或者死亡赔偿金可以按照其住所地或者经常居住地的相关标准计算。 “被扶养人生活费的相关计算标准，依照前款原则确定。” 五、第二十二条修改为：“本解释所称‘城镇居民人均可支配收入’‘城镇居民人均消费支出’‘职工平均工资’，按照政府统计部门公布的各省、自治区、直辖市以及经济特区和计划单列市上一年度相关统计数据确定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因生命、身体、健康遭受侵害，赔偿权利人起诉请求赔偿义务人赔偿物质损害和精神损害的，人民法院应予受理。  
本条所称“赔偿权利人”，是指因侵权行为或者其他致害原因直接遭受人身损害的受害人以及死亡受害人的近亲属。  
本条所称“赔偿义务人”，是指因自己或者他人的侵权行为以及其他致害原因依法应当承担民事责任的自然人、法人或者非法人组织。

**Article 1** Where a compensation obligee initiates a prosecution requesting a compensation obligor to compensate for material damage and mental damage due to infringement of life, body, or health, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
The term "compensation obligee" as used in this Article refers to the victim who has directly suffered personal injury due to the infringement or any other cause of damage, and the close relatives of the deceased victim.  
The term "compensation obligor" as used in this Article refers to a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that shall bear civil liability according to law due to its own or another person's infringement and any other cause of damage.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　赔偿权利人起诉部分共同侵权人的，人民法院应当追加其他共同侵权人作为共同被告。赔偿权利人在诉讼中放弃对部分共同侵权人的诉讼请求的，其他共同侵权人对被放弃诉讼请求的被告应当承担的赔偿份额不承担连带责任。责任范围难以确定的，推定各共同侵权人承担同等责任。  
人民法院应当将放弃诉讼请求的法律后果告知赔偿权利人，并将放弃诉讼请求的情况在法律文书中叙明。

**Article 2** Where a compensation obligee initiates a prosecution against some joint infringers, the People's Court shall add the other joint infringers as co-defendants. Where the compensation obligee waives the claims against some joint infringers during the litigation, the other joint infringers shall not bear joint and several liability for the compensation share that the defendant against whom the claim is waived shall bear. If the scope of liability is difficult to determine, it shall be presumed that each joint infringer bears equal liability.  
The People's Court shall inform the compensation obligee of the legal consequences of waiving the claim, and shall state the circumstances of the waiver of the claim in the legal instruments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法应当参加工伤保险统筹的用人单位的劳动者，因工伤事故遭受人身损害，劳动者或者其近亲属向人民法院起诉请求用人单位承担民事赔偿责任的，告知其按《工伤保险条例》的规定处理。  
因用人单位以外的第三人侵权造成劳动者人身损害，赔偿权利人请求第三人承担民事赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where a worker of an employer that shall participate in the overall planning of work-related injury insurance according to law suffers personal injury due to a work-related injury accident, and the worker or his close relatives initiate a prosecution to the People's Court requesting the employer to bear civil compensation liability, they shall be informed that the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Regulation on Work-related Injury Insurance.  
Where a third party other than the employer causes personal injury to a worker, and the compensation obligee requests the third party to bear civil compensation liability, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人，在从事帮工活动中致人损害的，被帮工人应当承担赔偿责任。被帮工人承担赔偿责任后向有故意或者重大过失的帮工人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 4** Where a volunteer worker providing labor without compensation causes damage to another person during the volunteer work, the beneficiary of the volunteer work shall bear compensation liability. Where the beneficiary of the volunteer work, after bearing compensation liability, seeks recourse against the volunteer worker who acted intentionally or with gross negligence, the People's Court shall support it. Where the beneficiary of the volunteer work explicitly refuses the volunteer work, he shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　无偿提供劳务的帮工人因帮工活动遭受人身损害的，根据帮工人和被帮工人各自的过错承担相应的责任；被帮工人明确拒绝帮工的，被帮工人不承担赔偿责任，但可以在受益范围内予以适当补偿。  
帮工人在帮工活动中因第三人的行为遭受人身损害的，有权请求第三人承担赔偿责任，也有权请求被帮工人予以适当补偿。被帮工人补偿后，可以向第三人追偿。

**Article 5** Where a volunteer worker providing labor without compensation suffers personal injury due to the volunteer work, the volunteer worker and the beneficiary of the volunteer work shall bear corresponding liability based on their respective fault; where the beneficiary of the volunteer work explicitly refuses the volunteer work, the beneficiary shall not bear compensation liability, but may provide appropriate compensation within the scope of benefits received.  
Where a volunteer worker suffers personal injury in the volunteer work due to the act of a third party, he shall have the right to request the third party to bear compensation liability, and shall also have the right to request the beneficiary of the volunteer work to provide appropriate compensation. After the beneficiary of the volunteer work provides compensation, he may seek recourse against the third party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　医疗费根据医疗机构出具的医药费、住院费等收款凭证，结合病历和诊断证明等相关证据确定。赔偿义务人对治疗的必要性和合理性有异议的，应当承担相应的举证责任。  
医疗费的赔偿数额，按照一审法庭辩论终结前实际发生的数额确定。器官功能恢复训练所必要的康复费、适当的整容费以及其他后续治疗费，赔偿权利人可以待实际发生后另行起诉。但根据医疗证明或者鉴定结论确定必然发生的费用，可以与已经发生的医疗费一并予以赔偿。

**Article 6** Medical expenses shall be determined based on the payment receipts for medicine, hospitalization, etc. issued by the medical institution, in combination with relevant evidence such as medical records and diagnostic certificates. Where the compensation obligor has objections to the necessity and reasonableness of the treatment, he shall bear the corresponding burden of adducing evidence.  
The amount of compensation for medical expenses shall be determined based on the amount actually incurred before the conclusion of the debate in the first-instance court. For necessary rehabilitation expenses for organ function recovery training, appropriate plastic surgery expenses, and other follow-up treatment expenses, the compensation obligee may bring a separate prosecution after they are actually incurred. However, expenses that are determined to be inevitably incurred based on medical certificates or expert evaluation conclusions may be compensated together with the medical expenses already incurred.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　误工费根据受害人的误工时间和收入状况确定。  
误工时间根据受害人接受治疗的医疗机构出具的证明确定。受害人因伤致残持续误工的，误工时间可以计算至定残日前一天。  
受害人有固定收入的，误工费按照实际减少的收入计算。受害人无固定收入的，按照其最近三年的平均收入计算；受害人不能举证证明其最近三年的平均收入状况的，可以参照受诉法院所在地相同或者相近行业上一年度职工的平均工资计算。

**Article 7** Lost work time compensation shall be determined based on the victim's lost work time and income status.  
The lost work time shall be determined based on the certificate issued by the medical institution where the victim receives treatment. Where the victim continues to lose work time due to disability caused by injury, the lost work time may be calculated up to the day before the disability assessment.  
Where the victim has fixed income, the lost work time compensation shall be calculated based on the income actually reduced. Where the victim has no fixed income, it shall be calculated based on the average income of the last three years; where the victim cannot prove the average income status of the last three years, it may be calculated by reference to the average wage of employees in the same or similar industry in the previous year in the locality of the court accepting the case.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　护理费根据护理人员的收入状况和护理人数、护理期限确定。  
护理人员有收入的，参照误工费的规定计算；护理人员没有收入或者雇佣护工的，参照当地护工从事同等级别护理的劳务报酬标准计算。护理人员原则上为一人，但医疗机构或者鉴定机构有明确意见的，可以参照确定护理人员人数。  
护理期限应计算至受害人恢复生活自理能力时止。受害人因残疾不能恢复生活自理能力的，可以根据其年龄、健康状况等因素确定合理的护理期限，但最长不超过二十年。  
受害人定残后的护理，应当根据其护理依赖程度并结合配制残疾辅助器具的情况确定护理级别。

**Article 8** Nursing expenses shall be determined based on the income status of the nursing personnel, the number of nursing personnel, and the nursing period.  
Where the nursing personnel have income, the expenses shall be calculated by reference to the provisions on lost work time compensation; where the nursing personnel have no income or a hired caregiver is employed, the expenses shall be calculated based on the labor remuneration standard for caregivers providing the same level of care in the locality. The nursing personnel shall in principle be one person, but if the medical institution or the expert evaluation institution has a clear opinion, the number of nursing personnel may be determined by reference to that opinion.  
The nursing period shall be calculated up to the time when the victim recovers the ability to take care of himself. Where the victim cannot recover the ability to take care of himself due to disability, a reasonable nursing period may be determined based on factors such as his age and health condition, but shall not exceed twenty years.  
After the victim is assessed for disability, the nursing grade shall be determined based on the degree of his nursing dependency and in combination with the circumstances of fitting of assistive devices for disability.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　交通费根据受害人及其必要的陪护人员因就医或者转院治疗实际发生的费用计算。交通费应当以正式票据为凭；有关凭据应当与就医地点、时间、人数、次数相符合。

**Article 9** Transportation expenses shall be calculated based on the expenses actually incurred by the victim and his necessary accompanying personnel for medical treatment or transfer to another hospital for treatment. Transportation expenses shall be evidenced by formal receipts; the relevant receipts shall correspond to the place, time, number of persons, and number of times of medical treatment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　住院伙食补助费可以参照当地国家机关一般工作人员的出差伙食补助标准予以确定。  
受害人确有必要到外地治疗，因客观原因不能住院，受害人本人及其陪护人员实际发生的住宿费和伙食费，其合理部分应予赔偿。

**Article 10** Hospitalization food subsidy may be determined by reference to the standard of travel food subsidy for general staff of local state organs.  
Where it is truly necessary for the victim to go to another place for treatment but cannot be hospitalized due to objective reasons, the actual accommodation expenses and food expenses incurred by the victim himself and his accompanying personnel shall be compensated for their reasonable portion.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　营养费根据受害人伤残情况参照医疗机构的意见确定。

**Article 11** Nutrition expenses shall be determined based on the victim's disability circumstances by reference to the opinion of the medical institution.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　残疾赔偿金根据受害人丧失劳动能力程度或者伤残等级，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入标准，自定残之日起按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。  
受害人因伤致残但实际收入没有减少，或者伤残等级较轻但造成职业妨害严重影响其劳动就业的，可以对残疾赔偿金作相应调整。

**Article 12** Disability compensation shall be calculated based on the degree of loss of working ability or the level of disability of the victim, in accordance with the standard of per capita disposable income of urban residents in the previous year in the locality of the court accepting the case, for twenty years from the date of disability assessment. However, for those over 60 years of age, the number of years shall be reduced by one year for each additional year of age; for those over 75 years of age, it shall be calculated as five years.  
Where the victim becomes disabled due to injury but his actual income does not decrease, or where the disability level is relatively minor but causes occupational hindrance seriously affecting his employment, the disability compensation may be adjusted accordingly.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　残疾辅助器具费按照普通适用器具的合理费用标准计算。伤情有特殊需要的，可以参照辅助器具配制机构的意见确定相应的合理费用标准。  
辅助器具的更换周期和赔偿期限参照配制机构的意见确定。

**Article 13** Expenses for assistive devices for disability shall be calculated based on the standard of reasonable costs for ordinary applicable devices. Where the injury condition has special needs, the corresponding reasonable cost standard may be determined by reference to the opinion of the assistive device fitting institution.  
The replacement cycle and compensation period of assistive devices shall be determined by reference to the opinion of the fitting institution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　丧葬费按照受诉法院所在地上一年度职工月平均工资标准，以六个月总额计算。

**Article 14** Funeral expenses shall be calculated based on the standard of average monthly wage of employees in the previous year in the locality of the court accepting the case, for a total of six months.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　死亡赔偿金按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均可支配收入标准，按二十年计算。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。

**Article 15** Death compensation shall be calculated based on the standard of per capita disposable income of urban residents in the previous year in the locality of the court accepting the case, for twenty years. However, for those over 60 years of age, the number of years shall be reduced by one year for each additional year of age; for those over 75 years of age, it shall be calculated as five years.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　被扶养人生活费计入残疾赔偿金或者死亡赔偿金。

**Article 16** Living expenses of dependents shall be included in the disability compensation or death compensation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　被扶养人生活费根据扶养人丧失劳动能力程度，按照受诉法院所在地上一年度城镇居民人均消费支出标准计算。被扶养人为未成年人的，计算至十八周岁；被扶养人无劳动能力又无其他生活来源的，计算二十年。但六十周岁以上的，年龄每增加一岁减少一年；七十五周岁以上的，按五年计算。  
被扶养人是指受害人依法应当承担扶养义务的未成年人或者丧失劳动能力又无其他生活来源的成年近亲属。被扶养人还有其他扶养人的，赔偿义务人只赔偿受害人依法应当负担的部分。被扶养人有数人的，年赔偿总额累计不超过上一年度城镇居民人均消费支出额。

**Article 17** Living expenses of dependents shall be calculated based on the degree of loss of working ability of the supporter, in accordance with the standard of per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents in the previous year in the locality of the court accepting the case. Where the dependent is a minor, it shall be calculated until the age of 18; where the dependent has no working ability and no other source of living, it shall be calculated for 20 years. However, for those over 60 years of age, the number of years shall be reduced by one year for each additional year of age; for those over 75 years of age, it shall be calculated as five years.  
The term "dependent" refers to a minor to whom the victim shall bear the obligation of maintenance according to law, or an adult close relative who has lost working ability and has no other source of living. Where the dependent has other supporters, the compensation obligor shall only compensate the part that the victim shall bear according to law. Where there are multiple dependents, the total annual compensation amount shall not exceed the per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents in the previous year.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　赔偿权利人举证证明其住所地或者经常居住地城镇居民人均可支配收入高于受诉法院所在地标准的，残疾赔偿金或者死亡赔偿金可以按照其住所地或者经常居住地的相关标准计算。  
被扶养人生活费的相关计算标准，依照前款原则确定。

**Article 18** Where the compensation obligee proves by evidence that the per capita disposable income of urban residents in his domicile or habitual residence is higher than the standard in the locality of the court accepting the case, the disability compensation or death compensation may be calculated in accordance with the relevant standard in his domicile or habitual residence.  
The relevant calculation standard for living expenses of dependents shall be determined in accordance with the principle of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　超过确定的护理期限、辅助器具费给付年限或者残疾赔偿金给付年限，赔偿权利人向人民法院起诉请求继续给付护理费、辅助器具费或者残疾赔偿金的，人民法院应予受理。赔偿权利人确需继续护理、配制辅助器具，或者没有劳动能力和生活来源的，人民法院应当判令赔偿义务人继续给付相关费用五至十年。

**Article 19** Where the determined nursing period, payment period for assistive device expenses, or payment period for disability compensation expires, and the compensation obligee initiates a prosecution to the People's Court requesting continued payment of nursing expenses, assistive device expenses, or disability compensation, the People's Court shall accept the case. Where the compensation obligee truly needs continued nursing, fitting of assistive devices, or has no working ability and source of living, the People's Court shall order the compensation obligor to continue paying the relevant expenses for five to ten years.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　赔偿义务人请求以定期金方式给付残疾赔偿金、辅助器具费的，应当提供相应的担保。人民法院可以根据赔偿义务人的给付能力和提供担保的情况，确定以定期金方式给付相关费用。但是，一审法庭辩论终结前已经发生的费用、死亡赔偿金以及精神损害抚慰金，应当一次性给付。

**Article 20** Where the compensation obligor requests to pay disability compensation and assistive device expenses by way of a periodic payment, he shall provide corresponding security. The People's Court may, based on the compensation obligor's payment ability and the provision of security, determine to pay the relevant expenses by way of a periodic payment. However, expenses already incurred before the conclusion of the debate in the first-instance court, death compensation, and solatium for mental damage shall be paid in a lump sum.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院应当在法律文书中明确定期金的给付时间、方式以及每期给付标准。执行期间有关统计数据发生变化的，给付金额应当适时进行相应调整。  
定期金按照赔偿权利人的实际生存年限给付，不受本解释有关赔偿期限的限制。

**Article 21** The People's Court shall specify in the legal instruments the payment time, method, and standard for each periodic payment. Where the relevant statistical data change during the enforcement period, the payment amount shall be adjusted accordingly in a timely manner.  
The periodic payment shall be paid based on the actual life span of the compensation obligee, and shall not be subject to the limitation of the compensation period under this Interpretation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本解释所称“城镇居民人均可支配收入”“城镇居民人均消费支出”“职工平均工资”，按照政府统计部门公布的各省、自治区、直辖市以及经济特区和计划单列市上一年度相关统计数据确定。  
“上一年度”，是指一审法庭辩论终结时的上一统计年度。

**Article 22** The terms "per capita disposable income of urban residents", "per capita consumption expenditure of urban residents", and "average wage of employees" as used in this Interpretation shall be determined in accordance with the relevant statistical data of the previous year announced by the government statistical departments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as special economic zones and cities separately listed in the state plan.  
The term "previous year" refers to the previous statistical year at the time of conclusion of the debate in the first-instance court.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　精神损害抚慰金适用《最高人民法院关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释》予以确定。

**Article 23** Solatium for mental damage shall be determined by applying the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Determination of Liability for Mental Damage in Civil Torts.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本解释自2022年5月1日起施行。施行后发生的侵权行为引起的人身损害赔偿案件适用本解释。  
本院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 24** This Interpretation shall come into force as of May 1, 2022. Cases of compensation for personal injury caused by infringements occurring after its coming into force shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
