# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理仲裁裁决执行案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕5号  
**公布日期**：2018-01-05  
**生效日期**：2018-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理仲裁裁决执行案件若干问题的规定》已于2018年1月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1730次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年3月1日起施行。 2018年2月22日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理仲裁裁决执行案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕5号 为了规范人民法院办理仲裁裁决执行案件，依法保护当事人、案外人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国仲裁法》等法律规定，结合人民法院执行工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称的仲裁裁决执行案件，是指当事人申请人民法院执行仲裁机构依据仲裁法作出的仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书的案件。 第二条　当事人对仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书申请执行的，由被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地的中级人民法院管辖。 符合下列条件的，经上级人民法院批准，中级人民法院可以参照民事诉讼法第三十八条的规定指定基层人民法院管辖： （一）执行标的额符合基层人民法院一审民商事案件级别管辖受理范围； （二）被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地在被指定的基层人民法院辖区内。 被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件申请不予执行的，负责执行的中级人民法院应当另行立案审查处理；执行案件已指定基层人民法院管辖的，应当于收到不予执行申请后三日内移送原执行法院另行立案审查处理。 第三条　仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书执行内容具有下列情形之一导致无法执行的，人民法院可以裁定驳回执行申请；导致部分无法执行的，可以裁定驳回该部分的执行申请；导致部分无法执行且该部分与其他部分不可分的，可以裁定驳回执行申请。 （一）权利义务主体不明确；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称的仲裁裁决执行案件，是指当事人申请人民法院执行仲裁机构依据仲裁法作出的仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书的案件。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, a case of enforcement of an arbitral award refers to a case where a party applies to a People's Court for enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement made by an arbitration institution in accordance with the Arbitration Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人对仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书申请执行的，由被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地的中级人民法院管辖。  
符合下列条件的，经上级人民法院批准，中级人民法院可以参照[民事诉讼法第三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-38)的规定指定基层人民法院管辖：  
（一）执行标的额符合基层人民法院一审民商事案件级别管辖受理范围；  
（二）被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地在被指定的基层人民法院辖区内。  
被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件申请不予执行的，负责执行的中级人民法院应当另行立案审查处理；执行案件已指定基层人民法院管辖的，应当于收到不予执行申请后三日内移送原执行法院另行立案审查处理。

**Article 2** Where a party applies for enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement made by an arbitration institution, the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to enforcement or at the location of the property subject to enforcement shall have jurisdiction.  
Where the following conditions are met, with the approval of a superior People's Court, an Intermediate People's Court may, by reference to Article 38 of the Civil Procedure Law, designate a Primary People's Court to have jurisdiction:  
(1) The amount of the subject matter of enforcement falls within the scope of the level-based jurisdiction for first-instance civil and commercial cases of the Primary People's Court;  
(2) The domicile of the person subject to enforcement or the location of the property subject to enforcement is within the jurisdiction of the designated Primary People's Court.  
Where the person subject to enforcement or a person other than a party applies for refusal of enforcement of the arbitral award, the Intermediate People's Court responsible for enforcement shall separately open a case for review and disposition; if the enforcement case has been designated to a Primary People's Court, it shall transfer the case to the original enforcement court within three days after receipt of the application for refusal of enforcement for separate case opening, review, and disposition.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书执行内容具有下列情形之一导致无法执行的，人民法院可以裁定驳回执行申请；导致部分无法执行的，可以裁定驳回该部分的执行申请；导致部分无法执行且该部分与其他部分不可分的，可以裁定驳回执行申请。  
（一）权利义务主体不明确；  
（二）金钱给付具体数额不明确或者计算方法不明确导致无法计算出具体数额；  
（三）交付的特定物不明确或者无法确定；  
（四）行为履行的标准、对象、范围不明确。  
仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书仅确定继续履行合同，但对继续履行的权利义务，以及履行的方式、期限等具体内容不明确，导致无法执行的，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 3** Where the enforcement content of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement has any of the following circumstances rendering enforcement impossible, the People's Court may rule to dismiss the enforcement application; where it renders partial enforcement impossible, it may rule to dismiss the enforcement application for that part; where it renders a part impossible to enforce and that part is inseparable from other parts, it may rule to dismiss the entire enforcement application.  
(1) The subject of rights and obligations is unclear;  
(2) The specific amount of monetary payment is unclear or the method of calculation is unclear, making it impossible to calculate the specific amount;  
(3) The specific thing to be delivered is unclear or cannot be determined;  
(4) The standard, object, or scope of performance of an act is unclear.  
Where an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement only determines that a contract shall be further performed, but the rights and obligations of further performance, as well as the specific content such as the method and time limit of performance, are unclear, rendering enforcement impossible, it shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对仲裁裁决主文或者仲裁调解书中的文字、计算错误以及仲裁庭已经认定但在裁决主文中遗漏的事项，可以补正或说明的，人民法院应当书面告知仲裁庭补正或说明，或者向仲裁机构调阅仲裁案卷查明。仲裁庭不补正也不说明，且人民法院调阅仲裁案卷后执行内容仍然不明确具体无法执行的，可以裁定驳回执行申请。

**Article 4** Where there are clerical or computational errors in the main text of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, or matters that have been determined by the arbitral tribunal but omitted from the main text of the award, which may be corrected or clarified, the People's Court shall notify the arbitral tribunal in writing to make corrections or clarifications, or shall consult the arbitration case file from the arbitration institution for verification. Where the arbitral tribunal neither makes corrections nor provides clarifications, and after the People's Court consults the arbitration case file, the enforcement content remains unclear and specific such that enforcement is impossible, the People's Court may rule to dismiss the enforcement application.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　申请执行人对人民法院依照[本规定第三条](#art-3)、第四条作出的驳回执行申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。

**Article 5** Where an applicant for enforcement is dissatisfied with a ruling made by a People's Court in accordance with Articles 3 and 4 of these Provisions to dismiss the enforcement application, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书确定交付的特定物确已毁损或者灭失的，依照《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第四百九十四条的规定处理。

**Article 6** Where the specific thing determined to be delivered in an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement has indeed been damaged or lost, it shall be handled in accordance with Article 494 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被执行人申请撤销仲裁裁决并已由人民法院受理的，或者被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件提出不予执行申请并提供适当担保的，执行法院应当裁定中止执行。中止执行期间，人民法院应当停止处分性措施，但申请执行人提供充分、有效的担保请求继续执行的除外；执行标的查封、扣押、冻结期限届满前，人民法院可以根据当事人申请或者依职权办理续行查封、扣押、冻结手续。  
申请撤销仲裁裁决、不予执行仲裁裁决案件司法审查期间，当事人、案外人申请对已查封、扣押、冻结之外的财产采取保全措施的，负责审查的人民法院参照[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)的规定处理。司法审查后仍需继续执行的，保全措施自动转为执行中的查封、扣押、冻结措施；采取保全措施的人民法院与执行法院不一致的，应当将保全手续移送执行法院，保全裁定视为执行法院作出的裁定。

**Article 7** Where the person subject to enforcement applies to set aside an arbitral award and such application has been accepted by a People's Court, or the person subject to enforcement or a person other than a party files an application for refusal of enforcement of the arbitral award and provides appropriate security, the enforcement court shall rule to suspend enforcement. During the period of suspension of enforcement, the People's Court shall cease any dispositive measures, unless the applicant for enforcement provides sufficient and effective security and requests continued enforcement; before the expiration of the term of seizure, impoundment, or freezing of the subject matter of enforcement, the People's Court may, upon application by a party or ex officio, handle the extension of seizure, impoundment, or freezing procedures.  
During the period of judicial review of an application to set aside an arbitral award or refuse enforcement of an arbitral award, where a party or a person other than a party applies for preservation measures against property other than that already seized, impounded, or frozen, the People's Court responsible for the review shall handle it by reference to Article 100 of the Civil Procedure Law. If continued enforcement is still required after judicial review, the preservation measures shall automatically be converted into enforcement measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing; if the People's Court that took the preservation measures is different from the enforcement court, it shall transfer the preservation documents to the enforcement court, and the preservation ruling shall be deemed as a ruling made by the enforcement court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被执行人向人民法院申请不予执行仲裁裁决的，应当在执行通知书送达之日起十五日内提出书面申请；有[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第四、六项规定情形且执行程序尚未终结的，应当自知道或者应当知道有关事实或案件之日起十五日内提出书面申请。  
本条前款规定期限届满前，被执行人已向有管辖权的人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决且已被受理的，自人民法院驳回撤销仲裁裁决申请的裁判文书生效之日起重新计算期限。

**Article 8** Where the person subject to enforcement applies to a People's Court for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, it shall submit a written application within fifteen days from the date of service of the enforcement notice; where the circumstances provided in Article 237(2)(4) and (6) of the Civil Procedure Law exist and the enforcement procedure has not yet concluded, it shall submit a written application within fifteen days from the date it knows or should have known the relevant facts or case.  
If, before the expiration of the time limit set forth in the preceding paragraph of this Article, the person subject to enforcement has applied to a People's Court with jurisdiction to set aside the arbitral award and such application has been accepted, the time limit shall be recalculated from the date of effectiveness of the judicial document dismissing the application to set aside the arbitral award.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　案外人向人民法院申请不予执行仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书的，应当提交申请书以及证明其请求成立的证据材料，并符合下列条件：  
（一）有证据证明仲裁案件当事人恶意申请仲裁或者虚假仲裁，损害其合法权益；  
（二）案外人主张的合法权益所涉及的执行标的尚未执行终结；  
（三）自知道或者应当知道人民法院对该标的采取执行措施之日起三十日内提出。

**Article 9** Where a person other than a party applies to a People's Court for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, it shall submit an application and evidentiary materials proving the validity of its claim, and shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) There is evidence proving that the parties to the arbitration case maliciously applied for arbitration or engaged in sham arbitration, harming its lawful rights and interests;  
(2) The enforcement subject matter involved in the lawful rights and interests claimed by the person other than a party has not yet concluded enforcement;  
(3) The application is made within thirty days from the date it knows or should have known that the People's Court took enforcement measures against the subject matter.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被执行人申请不予执行仲裁裁决，对同一仲裁裁决的多个不予执行事由应当一并提出。不予执行仲裁裁决申请被裁定驳回后，再次提出申请的，人民法院不予审查，但有新证据证明存在[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第四、六项规定情形的除外。

**Article 10** Where the person subject to enforcement applies for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, multiple grounds for refusal of enforcement against the same arbitral award shall be raised together. After an application for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award has been dismissed by a ruling, any subsequent application shall not be reviewed by the People's Court, unless there is new evidence proving the existence of the circumstances provided in Article 237(2)(4) and (6) of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院对不予执行仲裁裁决案件应当组成合议庭围绕被执行人申请的事由、案外人的申请进行审查；对被执行人没有申请的事由不予审查，但仲裁裁决可能违背社会公共利益的除外。  
被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件申请不予执行的，人民法院应当进行询问；被执行人在询问终结前提出其他不予执行事由的，应当一并审查。人民法院审查时，认为必要的，可以要求仲裁庭作出说明，或者向仲裁机构调阅仲裁案卷。

**Article 11** A People's Court shall form a collegial panel to review a case of refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, focusing on the grounds raised by the person subject to enforcement and the application of the person other than a party; it shall not review grounds not raised by the person subject to enforcement, unless the arbitral award may contravene public interests.  
Where the person subject to enforcement or a person other than a party applies for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, the People's Court shall hold an inquiry; if the person subject to enforcement raises other grounds for refusal of enforcement before the conclusion of the inquiry, such grounds shall be reviewed together. When reviewing, if the People's Court deems it necessary, it may request the arbitral tribunal to make clarifications, or may consult the arbitration case file from the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院对不予执行仲裁裁决案件的审查，应当在立案之日起两个月内审查完毕并作出裁定；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长一个月。

**Article 12** A People's Court shall complete the review of a case of refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award within two months from the date of case filing and shall make a ruling; if there are special circumstances requiring an extension, with the approval of the president of the court, the period may be extended by one month.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下列情形经人民法院审查属实的，应当认定为[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第二项规定的“裁决的事项不属于仲裁协议的范围或者仲裁机构无权仲裁的”情形：  
（一）裁决的事项超出仲裁协议约定的范围；  
（二）裁决的事项属于依照法律规定或者当事人选择的仲裁规则规定的不可仲裁事项；  
（三）裁决内容超出当事人仲裁请求的范围；  
（四）作出裁决的仲裁机构非仲裁协议所约定。

**Article 13** Where the following circumstances are verified by a People's Court, they shall be determined as the circumstance under Article 237(2)(2) of the Civil Procedure Law that "the matters decided by the award are beyond the scope of the arbitration agreement or the arbitration institution has no power to arbitrate":  
(1) The matters decided by the award exceed the scope agreed in the arbitration agreement;  
(2) The matters decided by the award are matters that are not arbitrable under the law or the arbitration rules selected by the parties;  
(3) The content of the award exceeds the scope of the parties' arbitration claims;  
(4) The arbitration institution that made the award is not the one agreed upon in the arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　违反仲裁法规定的仲裁程序、当事人选择的仲裁规则或者当事人对仲裁程序的特别约定，可能影响案件公正裁决，经人民法院审查属实的，应当认定为[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第三项规定的“仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁的程序违反法定程序的”情形。  
当事人主张未按照仲裁法或仲裁规则规定的方式送达法律文书导致其未能参与仲裁，或者仲裁员根据仲裁法或仲裁规则的规定应当回避而未回避，可能影响公正裁决，经审查属实的，人民法院应当支持；仲裁庭按照仲裁法或仲裁规则以及当事人约定的方式送达仲裁法律文书，当事人主张不符合民事诉讼法有关送达规定的，人民法院不予支持。  
适用的仲裁程序或仲裁规则经特别提示，当事人知道或者应当知道法定仲裁程序或选择的仲裁规则未被遵守，但仍然参加或者继续参加仲裁程序且未提出异议，在仲裁裁决作出之后以违反法定程序为由申请不予执行仲裁裁决的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where the arbitration procedure prescribed by the Arbitration Law, the arbitration rules selected by the parties, or the special agreements of the parties on the arbitration procedure are violated, which may affect the fair adjudication of the case, and this is verified by the People's Court, it shall be determined as the circumstance under Article 237(2)(3) of the Civil Procedure Law that "the composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitration procedure violates statutory procedures".  
Where a party claims that the failure to serve legal documents in the manner prescribed by the Arbitration Law or the arbitration rules caused its inability to participate in the arbitration, or that an arbitrator should have withdrawn under the Arbitration Law or the arbitration rules but did not withdraw, which may affect fair adjudication, and such claims are verified after review, the People's Court shall uphold them; where the arbitral tribunal served arbitration legal documents in accordance with the Arbitration Law or the arbitration rules and the agreement of the parties, and a party claims that this does not comply with the service provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall not uphold such claims.  
Where the applicable arbitration procedure or arbitration rules have been specifically brought to the attention of the parties, and the parties knew or should have known that the statutory arbitration procedure or the selected arbitration rules were not complied with, but nevertheless participated in or continued to participate in the arbitration procedure without raising an objection, and then after the arbitral award is made, apply for refusal of enforcement of the arbitral award on the ground of violation of statutory procedures, the People's Court shall not uphold such application.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　符合下列条件的，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第四项规定的“裁决所根据的证据是伪造的”情形：  
（一）该证据已被仲裁裁决采信；  
（二）该证据属于认定案件基本事实的主要证据；  
（三）该证据经查明确属通过捏造、变造、提供虚假证明等非法方式形成或者获取，违反证据的客观性、关联性、合法性要求。

**Article 15** Where the following conditions are met, the People's Court shall determine it as the circumstance under Article 237(2)(4) of the Civil Procedure Law that "the evidence on which the award is based is forged":  
(1) The evidence has been admitted in the arbitral award;  
(2) The evidence is a principal piece of evidence for determining the basic facts of the case;  
(3) The evidence is verified to have been formed or obtained through illegal means such as fabrication, alteration, or provision of false certifications, violating the requirements of objectivity, relevance, and legality of evidence.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　符合下列条件的，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第二百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-237)第二款第五项规定的“对方当事人向仲裁机构隐瞒了足以影响公正裁决的证据的”情形：  
（一）该证据属于认定案件基本事实的主要证据；  
（二）该证据仅为对方当事人掌握，但未向仲裁庭提交；  
（三）仲裁过程中知悉存在该证据，且要求对方当事人出示或者请求仲裁庭责令其提交，但对方当事人无正当理由未予出示或者提交。  
当事人一方在仲裁过程中隐瞒己方掌握的证据，仲裁裁决作出后以己方所隐瞒的证据足以影响公正裁决为由申请不予执行仲裁裁决的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 16** Where the following conditions are met, the People's Court shall determine it as the circumstance under Article 237(2)(5) of the Civil Procedure Law that "the other party to the arbitration concealed from the arbitration institution evidence sufficient to affect a fair award":  
(1) The evidence is a principal piece of evidence for determining the basic facts of the case;  
(2) The evidence is only in the possession of the other party but was not submitted to the arbitral tribunal;  
(3) During the arbitration process, the existence of such evidence was known, and the other party was requested to produce it or the arbitral tribunal was requested to order its submission, but the other party failed to produce or submit it without justifiable reasons.  
Where a party conceals evidence in its own possession during the arbitration process and, after the arbitral award is made, applies for refusal of enforcement on the ground that the concealed evidence is sufficient to affect a fair award, the People's Court shall not uphold such application.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　被执行人申请不予执行仲裁调解书或者根据当事人之间的和解协议、调解协议作出的仲裁裁决，人民法院不予支持，但该仲裁调解书或者仲裁裁决违背社会公共利益的除外。

**Article 17** Where the person subject to enforcement applies for refusal of enforcement of an arbitration mediation statement or an arbitral award made based on a settlement agreement or mediation agreement between the parties, the People's Court shall not uphold such application, unless the arbitration mediation statement or the arbitral award contravenes public interests.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　案外人根据[本规定第九条](#art-9)申请不予执行仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书，符合下列条件的，人民法院应当支持：  
（一）案外人系权利或者利益的主体；  
（二）案外人主张的权利或者利益合法、真实；  
（三）仲裁案件当事人之间存在虚构法律关系，捏造案件事实的情形；  
（四）仲裁裁决主文或者仲裁调解书处理当事人民事权利义务的结果部分或者全部错误，损害案外人合法权益。

**Article 18** Where a person other than a party applies for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement pursuant to Article 9 of these Provisions and meets the following conditions, the People's Court shall uphold the application:  
(1) The person other than a party is the subject of the rights or interests;  
(2) The rights or interests claimed by the person other than a party are lawful and genuine;  
(3) There is a circumstance of a fabricated legal relationship or fabricated case facts between the parties to the arbitration case;  
(4) The main text of the arbitral award or the arbitration mediation statement handles the civil rights and obligations of the parties partially or entirely incorrectly, harming the lawful rights and interests of the person other than a party.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件逾期申请不予执行的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，应当裁定驳回不予执行申请。  
被执行人、案外人对仲裁裁决执行案件申请不予执行，经审查理由成立的，人民法院应当裁定不予执行；理由不成立的，应当裁定驳回不予执行申请。

**Article 19** Where the person subject to enforcement or a person other than a party applies late for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the application; if it has already been accepted, it shall rule to dismiss the application for refusal of enforcement.  
Where the person subject to enforcement or a person other than a party applies for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, and after review, if the grounds are established, the People's Court shall rule to refuse enforcement; if the grounds are not established, it shall rule to dismiss the application for refusal of enforcement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人向人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决被驳回后，又在执行程序中以相同事由提出不予执行申请的，人民法院不予支持；当事人向人民法院申请不予执行被驳回后，又以相同事由申请撤销仲裁裁决的，人民法院不予支持。  
在不予执行仲裁裁决案件审查期间，当事人向有管辖权的人民法院提出撤销仲裁裁决申请并被受理的，人民法院应当裁定中止对不予执行申请的审查；仲裁裁决被撤销或者决定重新仲裁的，人民法院应当裁定终结执行，并终结对不予执行申请的审查；撤销仲裁裁决申请被驳回或者申请执行人撤回撤销仲裁裁决申请的，人民法院应当恢复对不予执行申请的审查；被执行人撤回撤销仲裁裁决申请的，人民法院应当裁定终结对不予执行申请的审查，但案外人申请不予执行仲裁裁决的除外。

**Article 20** Where a party's application to a People's Court to set aside an arbitral award has been dismissed, and then it raises an application for refusal of enforcement on the same ground in the enforcement procedure, the People's Court shall not uphold it; where a party's application for refusal of enforcement has been dismissed, and then it applies to set aside the arbitral award on the same ground, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
During the period of review of a case of refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, where a party files an application to set aside the arbitral award with a People's Court having jurisdiction and such application is accepted, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the review of the application for refusal of enforcement; where the arbitral award is set aside or a decision to re-arbitrate is made, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the enforcement and terminate the review of the application for refusal of enforcement; where the application to set aside the arbitral award is dismissed or the applicant for enforcement withdraws the application to set aside the arbitral award, the People's Court shall resume the review of the application for refusal of enforcement; where the person subject to enforcement withdraws the application to set aside the arbitral award, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the review of the application for refusal of enforcement, unless a person other than a party applies for refusal of enforcement of the arbitral award.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院裁定驳回撤销仲裁裁决申请或者驳回不予执行仲裁裁决、仲裁调解书申请的，执行法院应当恢复执行。  
人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决或者基于被执行人申请裁定不予执行仲裁裁决，原被执行人申请执行回转或者解除强制执行措施的，人民法院应当支持。原申请执行人对已履行或者被人民法院强制执行的款物申请保全的，人民法院应当依法准许；原申请执行人在人民法院采取保全措施之日起三十日内，未根据双方达成的书面仲裁协议重新申请仲裁或者向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当裁定解除保全。  
人民法院基于案外人申请裁定不予执行仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书，案外人申请执行回转或者解除强制执行措施的，人民法院应当支持。

**Article 21** Where a People's Court rules to dismiss the application to set aside an arbitral award or to dismiss the application for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, the enforcement court shall resume enforcement.  
Where a People's Court rules to set aside an arbitral award or, based on an application of the person subject to enforcement, rules to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award, and the original person subject to enforcement applies for execution reversal or discharge of compulsory enforcement measures, the People's Court shall uphold it. Where the original applicant for enforcement applies for preservation of the funds or property that have been performed or enforced by the People's Court, the People's Court shall grant it according to law; if the original applicant for enforcement fails to re-apply for arbitration or file a lawsuit with a People's Court based on the written arbitration agreement reached by both parties within thirty days from the date the People's Court takes preservation measures, the People's Court shall rule to discharge the preservation.  
Where a People's Court, based on an application of a person other than a party, rules to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, and the person other than a party applies for execution reversal or discharge of compulsory enforcement measures, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院裁定不予执行仲裁裁决、驳回或者不予受理不予执行仲裁裁决申请后，当事人对该裁定提出执行异议或者申请复议的，人民法院不予受理。  
人民法院裁定不予执行仲裁裁决的，当事人可以根据双方达成的书面仲裁协议重新申请仲裁，也可以向人民法院起诉。  
人民法院基于案外人申请裁定不予执行仲裁裁决或者仲裁调解书，当事人不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议；人民法院裁定驳回或者不予受理案外人提出的不予执行仲裁裁决、仲裁调解书申请，案外人不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。

**Article 22** After a People's Court rules to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award, or to dismiss or not accept an application for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award, if a party raises an enforcement objection or applies for reconsideration against such ruling, the People's Court shall not accept it.  
Where a People's Court rules to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award, a party may re-apply for arbitration based on the written arbitration agreement reached by both parties, or may file a lawsuit with a People's Court.  
Where a People's Court, based on an application of a person other than a party, rules to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, and a party is dissatisfied, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling; where a People's Court rules to dismiss or not accept an application of a person other than a party for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award or an arbitration mediation statement, and the person other than a party is dissatisfied, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　[本规定第八条](#art-8)、第九条关于对仲裁裁决执行案件申请不予执行的期限自本规定施行之日起重新计算。

**Article 23** The time limit for applying for refusal of enforcement of an arbitral award as provided in Articles 8 and 9 of these Provisions shall be recalculated from the date these Provisions come into force.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本规定自2018年3月1日起施行，最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
本规定施行前已经执行终结的执行案件，不适用本规定；本规定施行后尚未执行终结的执行案件，适用本规定。

**Article 24** These Provisions shall come into force as of March 1, 2018. Where judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
Enforcement cases that have been concluded before these Provisions come into force shall not be subject to these Provisions; enforcement cases that have not been concluded after these Provisions come into force shall be subject to these Provisions.
