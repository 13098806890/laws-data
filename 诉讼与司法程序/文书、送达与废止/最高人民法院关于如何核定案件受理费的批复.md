### 最高人民法院关于如何核定案件受理费的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于如何核定案件受理费的批复  
  
法（司）复〔 1988〕2号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院辽法（经）请〔 1987〕22号《关于如何核定案件受理费的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
《民事诉讼收费办法（试行）》第三条第二款规定： “财产案件受理费，按起诉的请求数额计算，请求数额与实际不符的，由人民法院核定。”这里所指的 “请求数额与实际不符”，是指当事人提起诉讼时请求的争议数额同法院审理后认定的争议数额不符。从请示报告看，双方当事人所签订的加工承揽合同为有效合同，并已履行了一部分。上诉人提出了继续履行合同的请求，被上诉人也提出了不能继续履行的答辩，可见，上诉人提出的请求数额，即是双方实际争议的数额，不存在 “请求数额与实际不符”的问题。因此，此案的受理费，应按上诉人的请求数额即该合同中未履行部分进行计算收取。  
  
此复  
  
1988年 1月6日

**Article 1** Reply on How to Verify Case Acceptance Fees  
Fa (Si) Fu [1988] No. 2  
Higher People's Court of Liaoning Province:  
Your court's Liao Fa (Jing) Qing [1987] No. 22 "Request for Instructions on How to Verify Case Acceptance Fees" has been received. Upon review, the reply is as follows:  
Paragraph 3 of Article 2 of the Measures for the Collection of Litigation Fees in Civil Actions (Trial Implementation) provides: "The acceptance fee for property cases shall be calculated based on the amount claimed in the prosecution; if the claimed amount is inconsistent with the actual amount, it shall be verified by the People's Court." The term "the claimed amount is inconsistent with the actual amount" referred to herein means that the amount in dispute claimed by the party when filing the action is inconsistent with the amount in dispute determined by the court after trial. From the request report, the processing and fabrication contract signed by the two parties is a valid contract and has been partially performed. The appellant made a request for continued performance of the contract, and the respondent raised a defense that it could not continue performance. It can be seen that the amount claimed by the appellant is precisely the amount actually in dispute between the two parties, and there is no issue of "the claimed amount is inconsistent with the actual amount." Therefore, the acceptance fee for this case shall be calculated and collected based on the amount claimed by the appellant, i.e., the unperformed portion of the contract.  
This is our reply.  
January 6, 1988
