### 最高人民法院关于对分家析产的房屋再立遗嘱变更产权，其遗嘱是否有效的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对分家析产的房屋再立遗嘱  
  
变更产权，其遗嘱是否有效的批复  
  
（1985）民他字第12号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（85）川法民字第3号《关于处理张家定、张家铭、张家慧诉张士国房屋产权纠纷一案的请示》收悉。关于建国前已经析产确权，能否再予重新分割或立遗嘱继承等问题，经研究答复如下：张家定之祖父张文卿（张士国之父）于一九四八年将其家中自有房宅，除自己居住的一处外，其余四处均分给四个儿子。建国后由人民政府颁发了产权证。一九五三年张文卿召开有镇政府干部参加的家庭会议，经协商，重新调整各自分得的房产，以清偿分家前的债务，立了经镇政府认可的“房屋分管字据”，均无异议。一九五五年张文卿夫妇将调整给二儿媳的房产，又立遗嘱由四子张士国“继承”。一九六六年巫溪县人民法院按“遗嘱”作了调解。二儿媳的女儿张家定等人不服，提起申诉。据上，我们认为，对张家在一九四八年析产后，经财产所有人共同协商，于一九五三年分家时达成的各自管业且已执行多年的房产协议，应予以维护。张文卿夫妇于一九五五年所立“遗嘱”无效。  
  
此复  
  
一九八五年十一月二十八日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a Will Changing Property Rights over a House Already Partitioned Is Valid  
(1985) Min Ta Zi No. 12  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your Request No. (85) Chuan Fa Min Zi No. 3 "Request for Instructions on Handling the Case of Zhang Jiading, Zhang Jiaming, and Zhang Jiahui v. Zhang Shiguo Concerning a House Property Rights Dispute" has been received. Regarding the issues of whether property that has already been partitioned and confirmed before the founding of the People's Republic of China may be re-divided or made the subject of a will for succession, upon study, the reply is as follows: Zhang Jiading's grandfather, Zhang Wenqing (father of Zhang Shiguo), in 1948, divided all of his self-owned houses, except for one residence where he himself lived, among his four sons. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, the people's government issued property certificates. In 1953, Zhang Wenqing convened a family meeting attended by a town government official, and through negotiation, the property previously allocated to each was readjusted to repay debts incurred before the division, and a "House Division Document" recognized by the town government was executed, with no objections from anyone. In 1955, Zhang Wenqing and his wife made a will bequeathing the property that had been readjusted to the second daughter-in-law to the fourth son, Zhang Shiguo, for "succession." In 1966, the Wuxi County People's Court made a mediation in accordance with the "will." Zhang Jiading and others, daughters of the second daughter-in-law, refused to accept this and filed a petition. Based on the above, we are of the opinion that the agreement on the respective management of property reached in 1953, after consultation among all property owners following the 1948 property partition and which has been executed for many years, shall be upheld. The "will" made by Zhang Wenqing and his wife in 1955 is void.  
It is so replied.  
November 28, 1985
