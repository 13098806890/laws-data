### 最高人民法院关于人民法院依法有权查询、冻结和扣划邮政储蓄存款问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院依法有权查询、冻结和扣划  
  
邮政储蓄存款问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕1号  
  
福建省高级人民法院：  
  
你院闽高法〔1995〕118号《关于人民法院查询、冻结邮政存款的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第六十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-65)的规定，人民法院有权向包括邮政企业的有关单位调查取证，有关单位不得拒绝。  
  
[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-103)、第二百二十一条和第二百二十二条中的“其他有储蓄业务的单位”，包括办理邮政储蓄业务的邮政企业。人民法院为财产保全、先予执行或者执行已经发生法律效力的法律文书，有权查询、冻结、扣划邮政企业办理的邮政储蓄存款；有关的邮政企业依法应当协助人民法院查询、冻结和扣划。  
  
1996年2月29日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
On the Issue of the People's Court's Right to Query, Freeze and Transfer According to Law  
Reply on the Issue of Postal Savings Deposits  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 1  
Higher People's Court of Fujian Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on the People's Court's Query and Freeze of Postal Deposits (Min Gao Fa [1995] No. 118). Upon study, we reply as follows:  
In accordance with Article 65 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall have the right to investigate and collect evidence from relevant units, including postal enterprises, and such units shall not refuse.  
"Other units with savings business" as mentioned in Articles 103, 221 and 222 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall include postal enterprises that handle postal savings business. The People's Court, for the purpose of property preservation, advance execution, or enforcement of legally effective legal documents, shall have the right to query, freeze, and transfer postal savings deposits handled by postal enterprises; the relevant postal enterprises shall, according to law, assist the People's Court in querying, freezing, and transferring such deposits.  
February 29, 1996
