# 最高人民法院关于审理网络消费纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕8号  
**公布日期**：2022-02-15  
**生效日期**：2022-03-15

> 《最高人民法院关于审理网络消费纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（一）》已于2022年2月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1864次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年3月15日起施行。 2022年3月1日 最高人民法院 关于审理网络消费纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的规定（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　电子商务经营者提供的格式条款有以下内容的，人民法院应当依法认定无效：  
（一）收货人签收商品即视为认可商品质量符合约定；  
（二）电子商务平台经营者依法应承担的责任一概由平台内经营者承担；  
（三）电子商务经营者享有单方解释权或者最终解释权；  
（四）排除或者限制消费者依法投诉、举报、请求调解、申请仲裁、提起诉讼的权利；  
（五）其他排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除电子商务经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理的内容。

**Article 1** Where a standard term provided by an e-commerce operator contains any of the following contents, the People's Court shall determine it as null and void according to law:  
(1) deeming that the receipt of the goods by the consignee constitutes acceptance of the quality of the goods as conforming to the agreement;  
(2) all liabilities that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear according to law shall be borne by the operator on the platform;  
(3) the e-commerce operator enjoys the right of unilateral interpretation or final interpretation;  
(4) excluding or restricting the consumer's rights to complain, report, request mediation, apply for arbitration, or initiate prosecution according to law;  
(5) other contents that exclude or restrict the consumer's rights, reduce or exempt the e-commerce operator's liabilities, or increase the consumer's liabilities, which are unfair or unreasonable to the consumer.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　电子商务经营者就[消费者权益保护法第二十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-25)第一款规定的四项除外商品做出七日内无理由退货承诺，消费者主张电子商务经营者应当遵守其承诺的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where an e-commerce operator makes a commitment to accept unconditional returns within seven days for the four categories of excluded goods as specified in paragraph 1 of Article 25 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, and the consumer claims that the e-commerce operator shall abide by its commitment, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　消费者因检查商品的必要对商品进行拆封查验且不影响商品完好，电子商务经营者以商品已拆封为由主张不适用[消费者权益保护法第二十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-25)规定的无理由退货制度的，人民法院不予支持，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 3** Where a consumer unpacks and inspects the goods out of necessity for examination and does not affect the integrity of the goods, and the e-commerce operator claims that the unconditional return system as specified in Article 25 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests is inapplicable on the ground that the goods have been unpacked, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　电子商务平台经营者以标记自营业务方式或者虽未标记自营但实际开展自营业务所销售的商品或者提供的服务损害消费者合法权益，消费者主张电子商务平台经营者承担商品销售者或者服务提供者责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
电子商务平台经营者虽非实际开展自营业务，但其所作标识等足以误导消费者使消费者相信系电子商务平台经营者自营，消费者主张电子商务平台经营者承担商品销售者或者服务提供者责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** Where an e-commerce platform operator sells goods or provides services in a manner marked as self-operated, or actually carries out self-operated business though not marked as such, and such goods or services harm the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and the consumer claims that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear the liability of a seller of goods or a service provider, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where an e-commerce platform operator does not actually carry out self-operated business, but its markings, etc., are sufficient to mislead the consumer into believing that the business is self-operated by the e-commerce platform operator, and the consumer claims that the e-commerce platform operator shall bear the liability of a seller of goods or a service provider, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　平台内经营者出售商品或者提供服务过程中，其工作人员引导消费者通过交易平台提供的支付方式以外的方式进行支付，消费者主张平台内经营者承担商品销售者或者服务提供者责任，平台内经营者以未经过交易平台支付为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where, in the process of selling goods or providing services by an operator on the platform, its staff guides the consumer to make payment in a manner other than the payment method provided by the trading platform, and the consumer claims that the operator on the platform shall bear the liability of a seller of goods or a service provider, and the operator on the platform defends on the ground that the payment was not made through the trading platform, the People's Court shall not uphold such defense.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　注册网络经营账号开设网络店铺的平台内经营者，通过协议等方式将网络账号及店铺转让给其他经营者，但未依法进行相关经营主体信息变更公示，实际经营者的经营活动给消费者造成损害，消费者主张注册经营者、实际经营者承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where an operator on the platform that registers an online business account and opens an online store transfers the online account and store to another operator through an agreement, etc., but fails to publicly disclose the change of information on the relevant business subject according to law, and the business activities of the actual operator cause damage to a consumer, and the consumer claims that the registered operator and the actual operator shall bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　消费者在二手商品网络交易平台购买商品受到损害，人民法院综合销售者出售商品的性质、来源、数量、价格、频率、是否有其他销售渠道、收入等情况，能够认定销售者系从事商业经营活动，消费者主张销售者依据消费者权益保护法承担经营者责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a consumer suffers damage from purchasing goods on a second-hand goods online trading platform, and the People's Court, after comprehensively considering the nature, source, quantity, price, frequency, whether there are other sales channels, income, and other circumstances of the seller's sale of goods, is able to determine that the seller is engaged in commercial business activities, and the consumer claims that the seller shall bear the liability of an operator under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　电子商务经营者在促销活动中提供的奖品、赠品或者消费者换购的商品给消费者造成损害，消费者主张电子商务经营者承担赔偿责任，电子商务经营者以奖品、赠品属于免费提供或者商品属于换购为由主张免责的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** Where prizes, gifts, or goods exchanged by a consumer provided by an e-commerce operator in promotional activities cause damage to the consumer, and the consumer claims that the e-commerce operator shall bear compensation liability, and the e-commerce operator claims exemption from liability on the ground that the prizes or gifts are provided free of charge or the goods are exchanged, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　电子商务经营者与他人签订的以虚构交易、虚构点击量、编造用户评价等方式进行虚假宣传的合同，人民法院应当依法认定无效。

**Article 9** Where an e-commerce operator enters into a contract with another person for false publicity by means of fabricating transactions, fabricating click rates, fabricating user reviews, etc., the People's Court shall determine the contract as null and void according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　平台内经营者销售商品或者提供服务损害消费者合法权益，其向消费者承诺的赔偿标准高于相关法定赔偿标准，消费者主张平台内经营者按照承诺赔偿的，人民法院应依法予以支持。

**Article 10** Where an operator on the platform sells goods or provides services that harm the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and its compensation standard committed to the consumer is higher than the relevant statutory compensation standard, and the consumer claims that the operator on the platform shall compensate according to the commitment, the People's Court shall uphold such claim according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　平台内经营者开设网络直播间销售商品，其工作人员在网络直播中因虚假宣传等给消费者造成损害，消费者主张平台内经营者承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where an operator on the platform sets up an online live-streaming room to sell goods, and its staff, due to false publicity or the like in the online live-streaming, causes damage to a consumer, and the consumer claims that the operator on the platform shall bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　消费者因在网络直播间点击购买商品合法权益受到损害，直播间运营者不能证明已经以足以使消费者辨别的方式标明其并非销售者并标明实际销售者的，消费者主张直播间运营者承担商品销售者责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
直播间运营者能够证明已经尽到前款所列标明义务的，人民法院应当综合交易外观、直播间运营者与经营者的约定、与经营者的合作模式、交易过程以及消费者认知等因素予以认定。

**Article 12** Where a consumer's lawful rights and interests are harmed due to clicking to purchase goods in an online live-streaming room, and the operator of the live-streaming room cannot prove that it has, in a manner sufficient for the consumer to discern, indicated that it is not the seller and indicated the actual seller, and the consumer claims that the operator of the live-streaming room shall bear the liability of a seller of goods, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where the operator of the live-streaming room can prove that it has fulfilled the indication obligations as listed in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall determine comprehensively by taking into account factors such as the transaction appearance, the agreement between the operator of the live-streaming room and the business operator, the cooperation mode with the business operator, the transaction process, and the consumer's perception.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　网络直播营销平台经营者通过网络直播方式开展自营业务销售商品，消费者主张其承担商品销售者责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where an operator of an online live-streaming marketing platform conducts self-operated business to sell goods through online live-streaming, and a consumer claims that it shall bear the liability of a seller of goods, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　网络直播间销售商品损害消费者合法权益，网络直播营销平台经营者不能提供直播间运营者的真实姓名、名称、地址和有效联系方式的，消费者依据[消费者权益保护法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-44)规定向网络直播营销平台经营者请求赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。网络直播营销平台经营者承担责任后，向直播间运营者追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where the sale of goods in an online live-streaming room harms the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and the operator of the online live-streaming marketing platform cannot provide the true name, title, address, and valid contact information of the operator of the live-streaming room, and the consumer requests compensation from the operator of the online live-streaming marketing platform pursuant to Article 44 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, the People's Court shall uphold such claim. After the operator of the online live-streaming marketing platform bears liability, if it seeks recourse against the operator of the live-streaming room, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　网络直播营销平台经营者对依法需取得食品经营许可的网络直播间的食品经营资质未尽到法定审核义务，使消费者的合法权益受到损害，消费者依据[食品安全法第一百三十一条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-131)等规定主张网络直播营销平台经营者与直播间运营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where an operator of an online live-streaming marketing platform fails to fulfill its statutory review obligation regarding the food business qualifications of an online live-streaming room that is required by law to obtain a food business license, thereby causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and the consumer claims that the operator of the online live-streaming marketing platform and the operator of the live-streaming room shall bear joint and several liability pursuant to Article 131 of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and other provisions, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　网络直播营销平台经营者知道或者应当知道网络直播间销售的商品不符合保障人身、财产安全的要求，或者有其他侵害消费者合法权益行为，未采取必要措施，消费者依据[电子商务法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国电子商务法.md#art-38)等规定主张网络直播营销平台经营者与直播间运营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where an operator of an online live-streaming marketing platform knows or should have known that the goods sold in an online live-streaming room do not meet the requirements for the protection of personal and property safety, or there are other acts infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and fails to take necessary measures, and the consumer claims that the operator of the online live-streaming marketing platform and the operator of the live-streaming room shall bear joint and several liability pursuant to Article 38 of the E-Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China and other provisions, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　直播间运营者知道或者应当知道经营者提供的商品不符合保障人身、财产安全的要求，或者有其他侵害消费者合法权益行为，仍为其推广，给消费者造成损害，消费者依据[民法典第一千一百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1168)等规定主张直播间运营者与提供该商品的经营者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** Where an operator of a live-streaming room knows or should have known that the goods provided by a business operator do not meet the requirements for the protection of personal and property safety, or there are other acts infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and still promotes such goods, thereby causing damage to a consumer, and the consumer claims that the operator of the live-streaming room and the business operator providing the goods shall bear joint and several liability pursuant to Article 1168 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other provisions, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　网络餐饮服务平台经营者违反[食品安全法第六十二条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-62)和第一百三十一条规定，未对入网餐饮服务提供者进行实名登记、审查许可证，或者未履行报告、停止提供网络交易平台服务等义务，使消费者的合法权益受到损害，消费者主张网络餐饮服务平台经营者与入网餐饮服务提供者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 18** Where an operator of an online catering service platform violates Articles 62 and 131 of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China by failing to conduct real-name registration, review the license of the online catering service provider, or failing to fulfill the obligations of reporting, suspending the provision of online trading platform services, etc., thereby causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and the consumer claims that the operator of the online catering service platform and the online catering service provider shall bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　入网餐饮服务提供者所经营食品损害消费者合法权益，消费者主张入网餐饮服务提供者承担经营者责任，入网餐饮服务提供者以订单系委托他人加工制作为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 19** Where the food operated by an online catering service provider harms the lawful rights and interests of a consumer, and the consumer claims that the online catering service provider shall bear the liability of an operator, and the online catering service provider defends on the ground that the order was entrusted to another person for processing and production, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定自2022年3月15日起施行。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force on March 15, 2022.
