# 最高人民法院关于申请执行工商仲裁机构法律文书中的被执行人已撤销如何处理问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1990〕17号  
**公布日期**：1990-11-14

---

### 正文

一、当事人根据民事诉讼法（试行）第一百六十七条和第一百六十九条的规定向人民法院申请执行工商仲裁机构的裁决书或调解书，人民法院在接受申请时发现被申请执行人已撤销的，法院不予受理。应当告知申请执行人可向原仲裁机构申请变更义务承担人，待变更后再向人民法院申请执行。

**Article None** 1. Where a party applies to the People's Court for enforcement of an award or mediation agreement of an industrial and commercial arbitration institution in accordance with Articles 167 and 169 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), and the People's Court discovers, upon accepting the application, that the person against whom enforcement is sought has been dissolved, the People's Court shall not accept the application. It shall inform the applicant that the applicant may apply to the original arbitration institution for a change of the person bearing the obligation, and after such change, may apply to the People's Court for enforcement.

二、人民法院在执行工商仲裁机构的裁决书或调解书过程中，发现被执行人已撤销的，应当裁定中止执行，并告知申请执行人向原仲裁机构申请变更义务承担人。如果仲裁机构依法变更了义务承担人，申请执行人可向人民法院申请恢复执行。  
1990年11月14日

**Article None** 2. Where, in the course of enforcing an award or mediation agreement of an industrial and commercial arbitration institution, the People's Court discovers that the person subject to enforcement has been dissolved, it shall issue a ruling to suspend enforcement and inform the applicant to apply to the original arbitration institution for a change of the person bearing the obligation. If the arbitration institution changes the person bearing the obligation according to law, the applicant may apply to the People's Court for resumption of enforcement.  
November 14, 1990
