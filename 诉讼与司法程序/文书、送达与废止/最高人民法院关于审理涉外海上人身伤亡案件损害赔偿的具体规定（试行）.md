# 最高人民法院关于审理涉外海上人身伤亡案件损害赔偿的具体规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1992〕16号  
**公布日期**：1991-11-08

> （1991年11月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第 521次会议通过） 法发〔1992〕16号 为了正确及时地审理涉外海上人身伤亡损害赔偿案件，保护当事人的合法权益，依据《中华人民共和国民法通则》有关规定，结合我国海事审判实践，参照国际习惯作法，特作如下具体规定： 一、涉外海上人身伤亡损害赔偿案件，是指案件的主体、客体和法律事实具有涉外因素的，在海上（含通海水域）和港口作业过程中因受害人的生命、健康受到侵害所引起的海事赔偿案件伤残者本人、死亡者遗属均有权依法向有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼，请求侵权人赔偿损失根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第十五条规定，伤亡者所在单位可以支持伤残者及死亡者遗属向法院起诉。 二、责任的承担 除法律另有规定者外，损害的发生完全是因一方的过错造成的，由该过错方承担全部责任；互有过错的，按过错程度比例分别承担责任；过错程度比例难以确定的，由各自平均承担责任。 二人以上共同侵权造成他人损害，侵害人承担连带责任。 船舶所有人、经营人、承租人、救助人等的受雇人员在执行职务过程中造成第三者伤亡的，出船舶所有人、经营人、承租人或救助人承担赔偿责任。 三、伤残赔偿范围 （一）收入损失。是指根据伤残者受伤致残之前的实际收入水平计算的收入损失。因受伤、致残丧失劳动能力者，按受伤、致残之前的实际收入的全额赔偿；因受伤、致残丧失部分劳动能力者，按受伤、致残前后的实际收入的差额赔偿。 （二）医疗、护理费。医疗费包括挂号费、检查诊断费、治疗医药费、住院费等；护理费包括住院期间必需陪护人的合理费用和出院后生活不能自理所雇请的护理人的费用。 （三）安抚费。是指对受伤致残者的精神损失所给子的补偿。可按伤势轻重、伤痛情况、残废程度，并考虑其年龄、职业等因素作一次性的赔付。 （四）其他必要的费用。包括运送伤残人员的交通、食宿之合理费用、伤愈前的营养费、补救性治疗（整容、镶牙等）费、残疾用具（假肢、代步车等）费、医疗期间陪住家属的交通费、食宿费等合理支出。 四、死亡赔偿范围和计算公式 （一）收入损失。是指根据死者生前的综合收入水平计算的收入损失。收入损失=（年收入一年个人生活费）×死亡时起至退休的年数+退休收入×10

### 正文

一、涉外海上人身伤亡损害赔偿案件，是指案件的主体、客体和法律事实具有涉外因素的，在海上（含通海水域）和港口作业过程中因受害人的生命、健康受到侵害所引起的海事赔偿案件伤残者本人、死亡者遗属均有权依法向有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼，请求侵权人赔偿损失根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第十五条规定，伤亡者所在单位可以支持伤残者及死亡者遗属向法院起诉。

**Article None** 1. Foreign-related maritime personal injury or death compensation cases refer to maritime compensation cases where the subject, object, and legal facts of the case have foreign-related elements, and which arise from infringement upon the life or health of the victim during operations at sea (including navigable waters) and in ports. The injured person themselves or the dependents of the deceased person are entitled to file a civil action with the competent maritime court according to law, requesting the infringer to compensate for losses. Pursuant to Article 15 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the unit to which the injured or deceased person belongs may support the injured person or the dependents of the deceased person in bringing a lawsuit to the court.

二、责任的承担  
除法律另有规定者外，损害的发生完全是因一方的过错造成的，由该过错方承担全部责任；互有过错的，按过错程度比例分别承担责任；过错程度比例难以确定的，由各自平均承担责任。  
二人以上共同侵权造成他人损害，侵害人承担连带责任。  
船舶所有人、经营人、承租人、救助人等的受雇人员在执行职务过程中造成第三者伤亡的，出船舶所有人、经营人、承租人或救助人承担赔偿责任。

**Article None** 2. Assumption of liability  
Except as otherwise provided by law, where damage is entirely caused by the fault of one party, that party shall bear full liability; if both parties are at fault, each shall bear liability in proportion to the degree of fault; if the degree of fault is difficult to determine, each shall bear liability equally.  
Where two or more persons jointly infringe and cause damage to others, the infringers shall bear joint and several liability.  
Where an employee of the shipowner, operator, charterer, salvor, or other person causes personal injury or death to a third party in the course of performing duties, the shipowner, operator, charterer, or salvor shall bear compensation liability.

三、伤残赔偿范围  
（一）收入损失。是指根据伤残者受伤致残之前的实际收入水平计算的收入损失。因受伤、致残丧失劳动能力者，按受伤、致残之前的实际收入的全额赔偿；因受伤、致残丧失部分劳动能力者，按受伤、致残前后的实际收入的差额赔偿。  
（二）医疗、护理费。医疗费包括挂号费、检查诊断费、治疗医药费、住院费等；护理费包括住院期间必需陪护人的合理费用和出院后生活不能自理所雇请的护理人的费用。  
（三）安抚费。是指对受伤致残者的精神损失所给子的补偿。可按伤势轻重、伤痛情况、残废程度，并考虑其年龄、职业等因素作一次性的赔付。  
（四）其他必要的费用。包括运送伤残人员的交通、食宿之合理费用、伤愈前的营养费、补救性治疗（整容、镶牙等）费、残疾用具（假肢、代步车等）费、医疗期间陪住家属的交通费、食宿费等合理支出。

**Article None** 3. Scope of compensation for injury and disability  
(1) Income loss. Refers to the loss of income calculated based on the actual income level of the injured or disabled person before the injury or disability. For those who lose the ability to work due to injury or disability, compensation shall be made for the full amount of the actual income before the injury or disability; for those who lose partial ability to work due to injury or disability, compensation shall be made for the difference in actual income before and after the injury or disability.  
(2) Medical and nursing expenses. Medical expenses include registration fees, examination and diagnosis fees, treatment and medication fees, hospitalization fees, etc.; nursing expenses include reasonable expenses for necessary caregivers during hospitalization and expenses for hired caregivers after discharge when the person cannot take care of themselves.  
(3) Solatium. Refers to compensation for the mental loss of the injured or disabled person. It may be paid in a lump sum based on the severity of the injury, the pain and suffering, the degree of disability, and taking into account factors such as the person's age and occupation.  
(4) Other necessary expenses. Include reasonable expenses for transportation and accommodation for the injured or disabled person, nutritional expenses before recovery, remedial treatment (cosmetic surgery, dentures, etc.), expenses for disability aids (prosthetics, mobility aids, etc.), and reasonable expenses such as transportation and accommodation for family members staying with the patient during medical treatment.

四、死亡赔偿范围和计算公式  
（一）收入损失。是指根据死者生前的综合收入水平计算的收入损失。收入损失=（年收入一年个人生活费）×死亡时起至退休的年数+退休收入×10  
死者年个人生活费占年收入的25%—30%。  
（二）医疗、护理费〔具体内容参见前条第（二）项〕。  
（三）安抚费。是指对死者遗属的精神损失所给予的补偿。  
（四）丧葬费。包括运尸、火化、骨灰盒和一期骨灰存放费等合理支出。但以死者生前六个月的收入总额为限。  
（五）其他必要的费用。包括寻找尸体、遗属的交通、食宿及误工等合理费用。

**Article None** 4. Scope and calculation formula for death compensation  
(1) Income loss. Refers to the loss of income calculated based on the deceased's comprehensive income level during their lifetime. Income loss = (annual income - annual personal living expenses) × number of years from the time of death to retirement + retirement income × 10.  
The deceased's annual personal living expenses account for 25% to 30% of annual income.  
(2) Medical and nursing expenses (see item (2) of the preceding article for details).  
(3) Solatium. Refers to compensation for the mental loss of the deceased's dependents.  
(4) Funeral expenses. Include reasonable expenses such as transportation of the body, cremation, urn, and initial storage of ashes. However, the total shall not exceed six months of the deceased's pre-death income.  
(5) Other necessary expenses. Include reasonable expenses such as searching for the body, transportation and accommodation for dependents, and loss of work.

五、受伤者的收入损失，计算到伤愈为止；致残者的收入损失，计算到70岁；死亡者的收入损失，计算到70岁。  
70岁以上致残或死亡的，其计算收入损失的年限不足5年者，按5年计算，并予以一次性赔付（综合考虑利率及物价上涨因素）。

**Article None** 5. For injured persons, income loss shall be calculated up to the time of recovery; for disabled persons, income loss shall be calculated up to age 70; for deceased persons, income loss shall be calculated up to age 70.  
For persons who become disabled or die at age 70 or above, if the number of years for calculating income loss is less than five years, such number shall be calculated as five years, and compensation shall be paid in a lump sum (taking into account interest rates and price increases).

六、伤亡者本人无固定工资收入的，其收入损失可比照同岗位、同工种、同职务的人员工资标准，或按其所在地区正常年度内的收入计算。  
伤亡者为待业人员及其他无固定工资收入的，按其所在地的平均生活水平计算。伤亡者为成年人的，可参照本款以18岁为起点计算。  
伤亡者为我国公民的，其对外索赔的标准，可参照我国有关部门制定的对外索赔工资标准处理。

**Article None** 6. Where the injured or deceased person has no fixed wage income, their income loss may be calculated by reference to the wage standards of persons in the same position, same type of work, or same post, or according to the income in the normal year in the area where they are located.  
Where the injured or deceased person is an unemployed person or another person without fixed wage income, the calculation shall be based on the average living standard of their location. Where the injured or deceased person is an adult, reference may be made to this paragraph starting from age 18.  
Where the injured or deceased person is a Chinese citizen, the standard for claiming compensation abroad may be handled by reference to the wage standards for foreign claims formulated by the relevant Chinese authorities.

七、海上人身伤亡损害赔偿的最高限额为每人80万元人民币。

**Article None** 7. The maximum limit of compensation for maritime personal injury or death shall be RMB 800,000 per person.

八、赔偿费应赔付给死者遗属、伤残者本人。伤亡者所在单位或者其他单位或个人为处理伤亡事故所垫付的费用，应从赔偿费中返还。

**Article None** 8. Compensation shall be paid to the dependents of the deceased or to the injured or disabled person themselves. Expenses advanced by the unit to which the injured or deceased person belongs or by other units or individuals for handling the injury or death accident shall be reimbursed from the compensation.

九、当事人双方国籍相同或者在同一国家有住所的，可以适用当事人本国法律或者住所地法律。

**Article None** 9. Where both parties have the same nationality or have their domicile in the same country, the law of the country of the parties' nationality or the law of their place of domicile may apply.

十、本规定自一九九二年七月一日起试行。  
1992年5月16日

**Article None** 10. These Provisions shall be implemented on a trial basis from July 1, 1992.
