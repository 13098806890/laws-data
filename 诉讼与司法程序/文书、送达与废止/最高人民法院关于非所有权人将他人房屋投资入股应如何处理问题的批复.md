### 最高人民法院关于非所有权人将他人房屋投资入股应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于非所有权人将他人房屋投资入股  
  
应如何处理问题的批复  
  
（1986）民他字第29号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（86）川法民示字第6号《关于非所有权人将他人房屋投资入股案件如何处理的请示报告》收悉。  
  
据你院调查了解，曹桂芳在铜梁县平滩街上有铺面房计67.65平方米。1964年其侄女曹碧玉擅自将该铺面房折价120元投资入股，并领取股息，直至“文化大革命”中断。该房现由铜梁县供销社平滩区综合商店使用。1979年曹桂芳得知此情况后，向当地政府申请落实房屋产权。1983年5月，平滩区区公所决定将铺面房退还给曹桂芳，因县供销社对该决定坚持异议，房未退成。曹桂芳遂向铜梁县人民法院起诉，请求保护自有房屋的所有权。  
  
经研究，我们同意你院审判委员会对本案的处理意见，即曹碧玉擅自将曹桂芳的房屋入股是一种侵权行为，非产权人的入股属无效的民事行为，人民法院应依法保护曹桂芳的房屋所有权。  
  
此复  
  
1987年2月23日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Regarding the Investment of Another Person's House as Shares by a Non-owner  
Reply on How to Handle the Issue  
(1986) Min Ta Zi No. 29  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions (No. (86) Chuan Fa Min Shi Zi No. 6) on How to Handle the Case of Investment of Another Person's House as Shares by a Non-owner.  
According to your investigation, Cao Guifang owns a shop house at Pingtan Street, Tongliang County, with a total area of 67.65 square meters.  
In 1964, her niece Cao Biyu, without authorization, invested the shop house as shares at a valuation of 120 yuan and received dividends, which continued until the 'Cultural Revolution' interrupted it.  
The house is currently used by the Pingtan District Comprehensive Store of the Tongliang County Supply and Marketing Cooperative.  
In 1979, Cao Guifang learned of this and applied to the local government for the confirmation of the house property rights.  
In May 1983, the Pingtan District Office decided to return the shop house to Cao Guifang, but the county Supply and Marketing Cooperative insisted on its objection, and the house was not returned.  
Cao Guifang then brought a lawsuit to the People's Court of Tongliang County, requesting protection of the ownership of her own house.  
After study, we agree with the opinion of the Adjudication Committee of your court regarding the handling of this case, namely, that Cao Biyu's unauthorized investment of Cao Guifang's house as shares constitutes an infringement, and the investment by a non-owner is an invalid civil act. The People's Court shall protect Cao Guifang's ownership of the house according to law.  
This is the reply.  
February 23, 1987
