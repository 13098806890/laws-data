# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕21号  
**公布日期**：2021-11-15  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定〉的决定》已于2021年11月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1850次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月24日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件 报核问题的有关规定》的决定 法释〔2021〕21号 根据审判实践需要，经最高人民法院审判委员会第1850次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于仲裁司法审查案件报核问题的有关规定》作如下修改： 一、将第三条修改为：“本规定第二条第二款规定的非涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，高级人民法院经审查，拟同意中级人民法院或者专门人民法院以违背社会公共利益为由不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决的，应当向最高人民法院报核，待最高人民法院审核后，方可依最高人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。” 二、增加一条作为第四条：“依据本规定第二条第二款由高级人民法院审核的案件，高级人民法院应当在作出审核意见之日起十五日内向最高人民法院报备。” 三、原第四条作为第五条。 四、原第五条作为第六条。 五、原第六条作为第七条。 六、原第七条作为第八条。 七、原第八条作为第九条。 本决定自2022年1月1日起施行。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称仲裁司法审查案件，包括下列案件：  
（一）申请确认仲裁协议效力案件；  
（二）申请撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决案件；  
（三）申请执行我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决案件；  
（四）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区仲裁裁决案件；  
（五）申请承认和执行外国仲裁裁决案件；  
（六）其他仲裁司法审查案件。

**Article 1** : For the purpose of these Provisions, cases of judicial review of arbitration shall include the following cases:  
(1) cases applying for confirmation of the validity of an arbitration agreement;  
(2) cases applying for setting aside an arbitral award rendered by an arbitration institution in Mainland China;  
(3) cases applying for enforcement of an arbitral award rendered by an arbitration institution in Mainland China;  
(4) cases applying for recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan Region;  
(5) cases applying for recognition and enforcement of foreign arbitral awards;  
(6) other cases of judicial review of arbitration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各中级人民法院或者专门人民法院办理涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，经审查拟认定仲裁协议无效，不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，不予认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区仲裁裁决，不予承认和执行外国仲裁裁决，应当向本辖区所属高级人民法院报核；高级人民法院经审查拟同意的，应当向最高人民法院报核。待最高人民法院审核后，方可依最高人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。  
各中级人民法院或者专门人民法院办理非涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，经审查拟认定仲裁协议无效，不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，应当向本辖区所属高级人民法院报核；待高级人民法院审核后，方可依高级人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。

**Article 2** : Where any Intermediate People's Court or Special People's Court, when handling a case of judicial review of arbitration involving foreign, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan elements, after review, intends to determine that the arbitration agreement is null and void, to refuse enforcement or set aside an arbitral award rendered by an arbitration institution in Mainland China, to refuse recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan Region, or to refuse recognition and enforcement of a foreign arbitral award, it shall submit a report to the Higher People's Court in its jurisdiction for review; where the Higher People's Court, after review, intends to approve such determination, it shall submit a report to the Supreme People's Court for review. Only after the Supreme People's Court has reviewed the matter may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the Supreme People's Court.  
Where any Intermediate People's Court or Special People's Court, when handling a case of judicial review of arbitration that does not involve foreign, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan elements, after review, intends to determine that the arbitration agreement is null and void, to refuse enforcement or set aside an arbitral award rendered by an arbitration institution in Mainland China, it shall submit a report to the Higher People's Court in its jurisdiction for review; only after the Higher People's Court has reviewed the matter may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[本规定第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的非涉外涉港澳台仲裁司法审查案件，高级人民法院经审查，拟同意中级人民法院或者专门人民法院以违背社会公共利益为由不予执行或者撤销我国内地仲裁机构的仲裁裁决的，应当向最高人民法院报核，待最高人民法院审核后，方可依最高人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。

**Article 3** : With respect to the cases of judicial review of arbitration that do not involve foreign, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan elements as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of these Provisions, if the Higher People's Court, after review, intends to approve the Intermediate People's Court or Special People's Court's decision to refuse enforcement or set aside an arbitral award rendered by an arbitration institution in Mainland China on the ground that it violates the public interest, it shall submit a report to the Supreme People's Court for review; only after the Supreme People's Court has reviewed the matter may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　依据[本规定第二条](#art-2)第二款由高级人民法院审核的案件，高级人民法院应当在作出审核意见之日起十五日内向最高人民法院报备。

**Article 4** : For cases reviewed by the Higher People's Court pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 2 of these Provisions, the Higher People's Court shall, within fifteen days from the date of rendering the review opinion, report to the Supreme People's Court for record.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下级人民法院报请上级人民法院审核的案件，应当将书面报告和案件卷宗材料一并上报。书面报告应当写明审查意见及具体理由。

**Article 5** : Where a lower people's court submits a case to the higher people's court for review, it shall submit the written report together with the case file materials. The written report shall state the review opinion and the specific reasons therefor.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　上级人民法院收到下级人民法院的报核申请后，认为案件相关事实不清的，可以询问当事人或者退回下级人民法院补充查明事实后再报。

**Article 6** : After receiving the application for review from the lower people's court, if the higher people's court finds that the relevant facts of the case are not clear, it may inquire of the parties or remand the case to the lower people's court for supplementary fact-finding before resubmission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　上级人民法院应当以复函的形式将审核意见答复下级人民法院。

**Article 7** : The higher people's court shall reply to the lower people's court with its review opinion in the form of a letter of reply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在民事诉讼案件中，对于人民法院因涉及仲裁协议效力而作出的不予受理、驳回起诉、管辖权异议的裁定，当事人不服提起上诉，第二审人民法院经审查拟认定仲裁协议不成立、无效、失效、内容不明确无法执行的，须按照[本规定第二条](#art-2)的规定逐级报核，待上级人民法院审核后，方可依上级人民法院的审核意见作出裁定。

**Article 8** : In a civil action, where a people's court has rendered a ruling on non-acceptance, dismissal of the action, or objection to jurisdiction on grounds relating to the validity of an arbitration agreement, and a party dissatisfied therewith files an appeal, if the court of second instance, after review, intends to determine that the arbitration agreement is not established, null and void, or ceased to have effect, or that its content is unclear and unenforceable, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 2 of these Provisions, submit a report level by level for review; only after the higher people's court has reviewed the matter may a ruling be made in accordance with the review opinion of the higher people's court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本规定自2018年1月1日起施行，本院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 9** : These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2018. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
