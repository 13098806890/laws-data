# 人民检察院检察建议工作规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2019-02-26  
**生效日期**：2019-02-26

> 《人民检察院检察建议工作规定》已于2018年12月25日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第十二次会议通过，现予以公布，自公布之日起施行。 最 高 人 民 检 察 院 2019年2月26日 人民检察院检察建议工作规定 （2018年12月25日最高人民检察院 第十三届检察委员会第十二次会议通过）

---

### 第一章 总 则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  为了进一步加强和规范检察建议工作，确保检察建议的质量和效果，充分发挥检察建议的作用，根据《中华人民共和国人民检察院组织法》等法律规定，结合检察工作实际，制定本规定。

**Article 1** For the purposes of further strengthening and regulating the work of procuratorial suggestions, ensuring the quality and effectiveness of procuratorial suggestions, and giving full play to the role of procuratorial suggestions, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Organic Law of the People's Procuratorates of the People's Republic of China and other laws and regulations, and in light of the actual work of procuratorial authorities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  检察建议是人民检察院依法履行法律监督职责，参与社会治理，维护司法公正，促进依法行政，预防和减少违法犯罪，保护国家利益和社会公共利益，维护个人和组织合法权益，保障法律统一正确实施的重要方式。

**Article 2** A procuratorial suggestion is an important means by which a People's Procuratorate performs its duty of legal supervision according to law, participates in social governance, safeguards judicial justice, promotes law-based administration, prevents and reduces illegal and criminal acts, protects national interests and social public interests, upholds the lawful rights and interests of individuals and organizations, and ensures the uniform and correct implementation of laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民检察院可以直接向本院所办理案件的涉案单位、本级有关主管机关以及其他有关单位提出检察建议。  
需要向涉案单位以外的上级有关主管机关提出检察建议的，应当层报被建议单位的同级人民检察院决定并提出检察建议，或者由办理案件的人民检察院制作检察建议书后，报被建议单位的同级人民检察院审核并转送被建议单位。  
需要向下级有关单位提出检察建议的，应当指令对应的下级人民检察院提出检察建议。  
需要向异地有关单位提出检察建议的，应当征求被建议单位所在地同级人民检察院意见。被建议单位所在地同级人民检察院提出不同意见，办理案件的人民检察院坚持认为应当提出检察建议的，层报共同的上级人民检察院决定。

**Article 3** A People's Procuratorate may directly make a procuratorial suggestion to the entity involved in the case handled by the same People's Procuratorate, the relevant competent authority at the same level, or other relevant entities.  
If a procuratorial suggestion needs to be made to a higher-level competent authority other than the entity involved, the matter shall be reported level by level to the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the suggested entity for a decision and the issuance of the procuratorial suggestion, or the People's Procuratorate handling the case shall prepare a procuratorial suggestion letter, submit it to the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the suggested entity for review and forwarding to the suggested entity.  
If a procuratorial suggestion needs to be made to a lower-level relevant entity, the corresponding lower-level People's Procuratorate shall be instructed to make the procuratorial suggestion.  
If a procuratorial suggestion needs to be made to a relevant entity in another locality, the opinion of the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the suggested entity's location shall be solicited. If the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the suggested entity's location raises a different opinion, and the People's Procuratorate handling the case insists that a procuratorial suggestion should be made, the matter shall be reported level by level to the common higher-level People's Procuratorate for a decision.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  提出检察建议，应当立足检察职能，结合司法办案工作，坚持严格依法、准确及时、必要审慎、注重实效的原则。

**Article 4** When making a procuratorial suggestion, a People's Procuratorate shall base itself on procuratorial functions, integrate it with judicial case-handling work, and adhere to the principles of strict compliance with law, accuracy and timeliness, necessity and prudence, and emphasis on practical results.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  检察建议主要包括以下类型：  
（一）再审检察建议；  
（二）纠正违法检察建议；  
（三）公益诉讼检察建议；  
（四）社会治理检察建议；  
（五）其他检察建议。

**Article 5** Procuratorial suggestions mainly include the following types:  
(1) Retrial procuratorial suggestions;  
(2) Procuratorial suggestions for correction of illegalities;  
(3) Procuratorial suggestions for public interest litigation;  
(4) Procuratorial suggestions for social governance;  
(5) Other procuratorial suggestions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  检察建议应当由检察官办案组或者检察官办理。

**Article 6** A procuratorial suggestion shall be handled by a prosecutor case-handling team or a prosecutor.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  制发检察建议应当在统一业务应用系统中进行，实行以院名义统一编号、统一签发、全程留痕、全程监督。

**Article 7** The formulation and issuance of a procuratorial suggestion shall be conducted in the unified business application system, with unified numbering and unified signing in the name of the People's Procuratorate, leaving traces throughout the process and subjecting the entire process to supervision.

### 第二章 适用范围

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民检察院发现同级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定具有法律规定的应当再审情形的，或者发现调解书损害国家利益、社会公共利益的，可以向同级人民法院提出再审检察建议。

**Article 8** If a People's Procuratorate discovers that a legally effective judgment or ruling of a People's Court at the same level falls under the circumstances for which a retrial shall be conducted as prescribed by law, or that a mediation document harms national interests or social public interests, the People's Procuratorate may make a retrial procuratorial suggestion to the People's Court at the same level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民检察院在履行对诉讼活动的法律监督职责中发现有关执法、司法机关具有下列情形之一的，可以向有关执法、司法机关提出纠正违法检察建议：  
（一）人民法院审判人员在民事、行政审判活动中存在违法行为的；  
（二）人民法院在执行生效民事、行政判决、裁定、决定或者调解书、支付令、仲裁裁决书、公证债权文书等法律文书过程中存在违法执行、不执行、怠于执行等行为，或者有其他重大隐患的；  
（三）人民检察院办理行政诉讼监督案件或者执行监督案件，发现行政机关有违反法律规定、可能影响人民法院公正审理和执行的行为的；  
（四）公安机关、人民法院、监狱、社区矫正机构、强制医疗执行机构等在刑事诉讼活动中或者执行人民法院生效刑事判决、裁定、决定等法律文书过程中存在普遍性、倾向性违法问题，或者有其他重大隐患，需要引起重视予以解决的；  
（五）诉讼活动中其他需要以检察建议形式纠正违法的情形。

**Article 9** If a People's Procuratorate, in the performance of its duty of legal supervision over litigation activities, discovers that any relevant law enforcement or judicial organ falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Procuratorate may make a procuratorial suggestion for correction of illegalities to the relevant law enforcement or judicial organ:  
(1) The adjudicators of a People's Court have engaged in illegal acts in civil or administrative adjudication activities;  
(2) A People's Court, in the process of enforcing effective civil or administrative judgments, rulings, decisions, or mediation documents, payment orders, arbitration awards, notarized creditor's rights documents, or other legal instruments, has engaged in illegal enforcement, non-enforcement, slack enforcement, or other acts, or there are other major hidden dangers;  
(3) A People's Procuratorate, in handling administrative litigation supervision cases or enforcement supervision cases, discovers that an administrative organ has engaged in acts that violate legal provisions and may affect the fair adjudication or enforcement by the People's Court;  
(4) Public security organs, People's Courts, prisons, community correction institutions, or compulsory medical treatment enforcement institutions, in criminal litigation activities or in the process of enforcing effective criminal judgments, rulings, decisions, or other legal instruments of the People's Court, have widespread or tendentious illegal problems, or have other major hidden dangers that need attention and resolution;  
(5) Other circumstances in litigation activities where it is necessary to correct illegalities in the form of a procuratorial suggestion.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民检察院在履行职责中发现生态环境和资源保护、食品药品安全、国有财产保护、国有土地使用权出让等领域负有监督管理职责的行政机关违法行使职权或者不作为，致使国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害，符合法律规定的公益诉讼条件的，应当按照公益诉讼案件办理程序向行政机关提出督促依法履职的检察建议。

**Article 10** If a People's Procuratorate, in performing its duties, discovers that an administrative organ responsible for supervision and administration in areas such as ecological environment and resource protection, food and drug safety, state-owned property protection, or state-owned land use rights transfer has illegally exercised its functions or failed to act, thereby infringing upon national interests or social public interests, and the conditions for public interest litigation prescribed by law are met, the People's Procuratorate shall, in accordance with the procedures for handling public interest litigation cases, make a procuratorial suggestion to the administrative organ urging it to perform its duties according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民检察院在办理案件中发现社会治理工作存在下列情形之一的，可以向有关单位和部门提出改进工作、完善治理的检察建议：  
（一）涉案单位在预防违法犯罪方面制度不健全、不落实，管理不完善，存在违法犯罪隐患，需要及时消除的；  
（二）一定时期某类违法犯罪案件多发、频发，或者已发生的案件暴露出明显的管理监督漏洞，需要督促行业主管部门加强和改进管理监督工作的；  
（三）涉及一定群体的民间纠纷问题突出，可能导致发生群体性事件或者恶性案件，需要督促相关部门完善风险预警防范措施，加强调解疏导工作的；  
（四）相关单位或者部门不依法及时履行职责，致使个人或者组织合法权益受到损害或者存在损害危险，需要及时整改消除的；  
（五）需要给予有关涉案人员、责任人员或者组织行政处罚、政务处分、行业惩戒，或者需要追究有关责任人员的司法责任的;  
（六）其他需要提出检察建议的情形。

**Article 11** If a People's Procuratorate, in handling cases, discovers that social governance work falls under any of the following circumstances, it may make a procuratorial suggestion to the relevant entity or department for improvement of work and enhancement of governance:  
(1) The entity involved has an unsound or unimplemented system for preventing illegal and criminal acts, has inadequate management, and has hidden dangers of illegal and criminal acts that need to be eliminated in a timely manner;  
(2) Certain types of illegal and criminal cases have occurred frequently or repeatedly over a certain period, or the cases that have occurred reveal obvious loopholes in management and supervision, requiring the competent industry department to be urged to strengthen and improve management and supervision work;  
(3) Civil disputes involving a certain group are prominent, which may lead to mass incidents or vicious cases, requiring the relevant departments to be urged to improve risk warning and prevention measures and strengthen mediation and guidance work;  
(4) The relevant entity or department fails to perform its duties in a timely manner according to law, causing damage or danger of damage to the lawful rights and interests of individuals or organizations, requiring timely rectification and elimination;  
(5) It is necessary to impose administrative penalties, disciplinary sanctions, or industry sanctions on the relevant persons involved, responsible persons, or organizations, or to pursue the judicial liability of the relevant responsible persons;  
(6) Other circumstances where a procuratorial suggestion is necessary.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  对执法、司法机关在诉讼活动中的违法情形，以及需要对被不起诉人给予行政处罚、处分或者需要没收其违法所得，法律、司法解释和其他有关规范性文件明确规定应当发出纠正违法通知书、检察意见书的，依照相关规定执行。

**Article 12** Where laws, judicial interpretations, and other relevant regulatory documents explicitly provide that a notice for correction of illegalities or a procuratorial opinion letter shall be issued for the illegal circumstances of law enforcement or judicial organs in litigation activities, or where it is necessary to impose administrative penalties or sanctions on a person not subject to prosecution or to confiscate his or her illegal gains, the relevant provisions shall be followed.

### 第三章 调查办理和督促落实

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  检察官在履行职责中发现有应当依照本规定提出检察建议情形的，应当报经检察长决定，对相关事项进行调查核实，做到事实清楚、准确。

**Article 13** If a prosecutor discovers, in the performance of his or her duties, circumstances where a procuratorial suggestion shall be made in accordance with these Provisions, the prosecutor shall report to the Chief Prosecutor for a decision, and investigate and verify the relevant matters to ensure that the facts are clear and accurate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  检察官可以采取以下措施进行调查核实：  
（一）查询、调取、复制相关证据材料；  
（二）向当事人、有关知情人员或者其他相关人员了解情况；  
（三）听取被建议单位意见；  
（四）咨询专业人员、相关部门或者行业协会等对专门问题的意见；  
（五）委托鉴定、评估、审计；  
（六）现场走访、查验；  
（七）查明事实所需要采取的其他措施。  
进行调查核实，不得采取限制人身自由和查封、扣押、冻结财产等强制性措施。

**Article 14** A prosecutor may take the following measures for investigation and verification:  
(1) Searching for, retrieving, and duplicating relevant evidence materials;  
(2) Learning about the situation from the parties, relevant informed persons, or other relevant persons;  
(3) Hearing the opinions of the suggested entity;  
(4) Consulting professionals, relevant departments, or industry associations on specialized issues;  
(5) Commissioning expert evaluations, appraisals, or audits;  
(6) Conducting on-site visits and inspections;  
(7) Other measures necessary to ascertain the facts.  
When conducting investigation and verification, no compulsory measures such as restricting personal freedom or sealing, seizing, or freezing property shall be taken.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  检察官一般应当在检察长作出决定后两个月以内完成检察建议事项的调查核实。情况紧急的，应当及时办结。  
检察官调查核实完毕，应当制作调查终结报告，写明调查过程和认定的事实与证据，提出处理意见。认为需要提出检察建议的，应当起草检察建议书，一并报送检察长，由检察长或者检察委员会讨论决定是否提出检察建议。  
经调查核实，查明相关单位不存在需要纠正或者整改的违法事实或者重大隐患，决定不提出检察建议的，检察官应当将调查终结报告连同相关材料订卷存档。

**Article 15** A prosecutor shall generally complete the investigation and verification of matters concerning a procuratorial suggestion within two months after the Chief Prosecutor makes a decision. In urgent circumstances, the matter shall be concluded in a timely manner.  
After completing the investigation and verification, the prosecutor shall prepare an investigation completion report, stating the investigation process, the facts and evidence determined, and proposing handling opinions. If it is considered necessary to make a procuratorial suggestion, the prosecutor shall draft a procuratorial suggestion letter and submit it together with the report to the Chief Prosecutor, who shall decide or submit the matter to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision on whether to make the procuratorial suggestion.  
If, after investigation and verification, it is determined that the relevant entity does not have any illegal facts or major hidden dangers requiring correction or rectification, and a decision is made not to make a procuratorial suggestion, the prosecutor shall file and archive the investigation completion report together with relevant materials.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  检察建议书要阐明相关的事实和依据，提出的建议应当符合法律、法规及其他有关规定，明确具体、说理充分、论证严谨、语言简洁、有操作性。  
检察建议书一般包括以下内容:  
案件或者问题的来源；  
依法认定的案件事实或者经调查核实的事实及其证据；  
（三）存在的违法情形或者应当消除的隐患；  
（四）建议的具体内容及所依据的法律、法规和有关文件等的规定；  
（五）被建议单位提出异议的期限；  
（六）被建议单位书面回复落实情况的期限；  
（七）其他需要说明的事项。

**Article 16** A procuratorial suggestion letter shall set forth the relevant facts and grounds, and the suggestion made shall comply with laws, regulations, and other relevant provisions, and shall be specific, well-reasoned, rigorous in argument, concise in language, and operable.  
A procuratorial suggestion letter shall generally include the following:  
(1) The source of the case or the issue;  
(2) The facts of the case determined according to law or the facts and evidence after investigation and verification;  
(3) The existing illegal circumstances or hidden dangers to be eliminated;  
(4) The specific content of the suggestion and the legal, regulatory, and relevant document provisions on which it is based;  
(5) The time limit for the suggested entity to raise objections;  
(6) The time limit for the suggested entity to provide a written reply on the implementation status;  
(7) Other matters to be explained.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  检察官依据[本规定第十一条](#art-11)的规定起草的检察建议书，报送检察长前，应当送本院负责法律政策研究的部门对检察建议的必要性、合法性、说理性等进行审核。  
检察建议书正式发出前，可以征求被建议单位的意见。

**Article 17** A procuratorial suggestion letter drafted by a prosecutor in accordance with Article 11 of these Provisions shall, before being submitted to the Chief Prosecutor, be sent to the department of the same People's Procuratorate responsible for legal policy research for review of the necessity, legality, and reasoning of the procuratorial suggestion.  
Before the procuratorial suggestion letter is formally issued, the opinions of the suggested entity may be solicited.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  检察建议书应当以人民检察院的名义送达有关单位。送达检察建议书，可以书面送达，也可以现场宣告送达。  
宣告送达检察建议书应当商被建议单位同意，可以在人民检察院、被建议单位或者其他适宜场所进行，由检察官向被建议单位负责人当面宣读检察建议书并进行示证、说理，听取被建议单位负责人意见。必要时，可以邀请人大代表、政协委员或者特约检察员、人民监督员等第三方人员参加。

**Article 18** A procuratorial suggestion letter shall be served on the relevant entity in the name of the People's Procuratorate. The service of a procuratorial suggestion letter may be made in writing or by on-site announcement.  
The announcement and service of a procuratorial suggestion letter shall be subject to the consent of the suggested entity, and may be conducted at the People's Procuratorate, the suggested entity, or another suitable place. The prosecutor shall read the procuratorial suggestion letter in person to the responsible person of the suggested entity, present evidence and reasoning, and hear the opinions of the responsible person of the suggested entity. Where necessary, third-party personnel such as deputies to the People's Congress, members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, specially invited procurators, or people's supervisors may be invited to participate.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  人民检察院提出检察建议，除另有规定外，应当要求被建议单位自收到检察建议书之日起两个月以内作出相应处理，并书面回复人民检察院。因情况紧急需要被建议单位尽快处理的，可以根据实际情况确定相应的回复期限。

**Article 19** When a People's Procuratorate makes a procuratorial suggestion, unless otherwise provided, it shall require the suggested entity to make corresponding handling within two months from the date of receipt of the procuratorial suggestion letter and to reply in writing to the People's Procuratorate. If urgent circumstances require the suggested entity to handle the matter as soon as possible, the corresponding reply time limit may be determined based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  涉及事项社会影响大、群众关注度高、违法情形具有典型性、所涉问题应当引起有关部门重视的检察建议书，可以抄送同级党委、人大、政府、纪检监察机关或者被建议单位的上级机关、行政主管部门以及行业自律组织等。

**Article 20** A procuratorial suggestion letter involving matters with significant social impact, high public concern, typical illegal circumstances, or issues that should draw the attention of relevant departments may be copied to the Party committee, People's Congress, government, or discipline inspection and supervision organs at the same level, or to the superior organ, administrative competent department, or industry self-regulatory organization of the suggested entity.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  发出的检察建议书，应当于五日内报上一级人民检察院对口业务部门和负责法律政策研究的部门备案。

**Article 21** A procuratorial suggestion letter that has been issued shall be submitted within five days to the corresponding business department and the department responsible for legal policy research of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for archival purposes.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  检察长认为本院发出的检察建议书确有不当的，应当决定变更或者撤回，并及时通知有关单位，说明理由。  
上级人民检察院认为下级人民检察院发出的检察建议书确有不当的，应当指令下级人民检察院变更或者撤回，并及时通知有关单位，说明理由。

**Article 22** If the Chief Prosecutor considers that a procuratorial suggestion letter issued by the same People's Procuratorate is indeed inappropriate, the Chief Prosecutor shall decide to amend or withdraw it, and shall promptly notify the relevant entity, stating the reasons.  
If a higher-level People's Procuratorate considers that a procuratorial suggestion letter issued by a lower-level People's Procuratorate is indeed inappropriate, the higher-level People's Procuratorate shall instruct the lower-level People's Procuratorate to amend or withdraw it, and shall promptly notify the relevant entity, stating the reasons.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  被建议单位对检察建议提出异议的，检察官应当立即进行复核。经复核，异议成立的，应当报经检察长或者检察委员会讨论决定后，及时对检察建议书作出修改或者撤回检察建议书；异议不成立的，应当报经检察长同意后，向被建议单位说明理由。

**Article 23** If the suggested entity raises an objection to a procuratorial suggestion, the prosecutor shall immediately conduct a review. If, after the review, the objection is found to be justified, the matter shall be reported to the Chief Prosecutor or the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision, and then the procuratorial suggestion letter shall be amended or withdrawn in a timely manner; if the objection is found to be unjustified, the matter shall be reported to the Chief Prosecutor for approval, and then the reasons shall be explained to the suggested entity.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  人民检察院应当积极督促和支持配合被建议单位落实检察建议。督促落实工作由原承办检察官办理，可以采取询问、走访、不定期会商、召开联席会议等方式，并制作笔录或者工作记录。

**Article 24** A People's Procuratorate shall actively urge, support, and cooperate with the suggested entity in implementing the procuratorial suggestion. The work of urging implementation shall be handled by the original prosecutor handling the case, who may adopt methods such as inquiries, visits, irregular consultations, or joint meetings, and shall make records or work notes.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  被建议单位在规定期限内经督促无正当理由不予整改或者整改不到位的，经检察长决定，可以将相关情况报告上级人民检察院，通报被建议单位的上级机关、行政主管部门或者行业自律组织等，必要时可以报告同级党委、人大，通报同级政府、纪检监察机关。符合提起公益诉讼条件的，依法提起公益诉讼。

**Article 25** If, after being urged, the suggested entity fails to rectify within the prescribed time limit without justifiable reasons, or the rectification is inadequate, the relevant circumstances may, upon the decision of the Chief Prosecutor, be reported to the higher-level People's Procuratorate, and be communicated to the superior organ, administrative competent department, or industry self-regulatory organization of the suggested entity. Where necessary, it may be reported to the Party committee or People's Congress at the same level, and communicated to the government or discipline inspection and supervision organs at the same level. If the conditions for filing a public interest lawsuit are met, a public interest lawsuit shall be filed according to law.

### 第四章 监督管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  各级人民检察院检察委员会应当定期对本院制发的检察建议的落实效果进行评估。

**Article 26** The Procuratorial Committee of a People's Procuratorate at each level shall periodically evaluate the implementation effects of the procuratorial suggestions issued by the same People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  人民检察院案件管理部门负责检察建议的流程监控和分类统计，定期组织对检察建议进行质量评查，对检察建议工作情况进行综合分析。

**Article 27** The case management department of a People's Procuratorate shall be responsible for the process monitoring and classification statistics of procuratorial suggestions, periodically organize quality evaluations of procuratorial suggestions, and conduct comprehensive analyses of the status of procuratorial suggestion work.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  人民检察院应当将制发检察建议的质量和效果纳入检察官履职绩效考核。

**Article 28** A People's Procuratorate shall incorporate the quality and effectiveness of formulating and issuing procuratorial suggestions into the performance assessment of prosecutors in the performance of their duties.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  上级人民检察院应当加强对下级人民检察院开展检察建议工作的指导，及时通报情况，帮助解决检察建议工作中的问题。

**Article 29** A higher-level People's Procuratorate shall strengthen its guidance to lower-level People's Procuratorates in carrying out the work of procuratorial suggestions, promptly communicate information, and help solve problems in the work of procuratorial suggestions.

### 第五章 附 则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  法律、司法解释和其他有关规范性文件对再审检察建议、纠正违法检察建议和公益诉讼检察建议的办理有规定的，依照其规定办理；没有规定的，参照本规定办理。

**Article 30** Where laws, judicial interpretations, and other relevant regulatory documents have provisions on the handling of retrial procuratorial suggestions, procuratorial suggestions for correction of illegalities, and procuratorial suggestions for public interest litigation, such provisions shall prevail; where there are no such provisions, these Provisions shall be referred to.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  本规定由最高人民检察院负责解释。

**Article 31** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Supreme People's Procuratorate.
