# 最高人民法院关于涉及担保纠纷案件的司法解释的适用和保证责任方式认定问题的批复

### 正文

一、最高人民法院法发〔1994〕8号《关于审理经济合同纠纷案件有关保证的若干间题的规定》，适用于该规定施行后发生的担保纠纷案件和该规定施行前发生的尚未审结的第一审、第二审担保纠纷案件。该规定施行前判决、裁定已经发生法律效力的担保纠纷案件，进行再审的，不适用该《规定》。《中华人民共和国担保法》生效后发生的担保行为和担保纠纷，适用担保法和担保法相关司法解释的规定。

**Article None** I. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning Guarantee in Cases of Disputes over Economic Contracts (Fa Fa [1994] No. 8) shall apply to guarantee dispute cases that occur after such Provisions come into force and to guarantee dispute cases of first instance and second instance that occur before such Provisions come into force but have not yet been concluded. For guarantee dispute cases where judgments or rulings have already taken legal effect before such Provisions come into force and are subject to retrial, such Provisions shall not apply. For guaranty acts and guarantee disputes that occur after the Guarantee Law of the People's Republic of China takes effect, the Guarantee Law and the provisions of the relevant judicial interpretations of the Guarantee Law shall apply.

二、担保法生效之前订立的保证合同中对保证责任方式没有约定或者约定不明的，应当认定为一般保证。保证合同中明确约定保证人在债务人不能履行债务时始承担保证责任的，视为一般保证。保证合同中明确约定保证人在被保证人不履行债务时承担保证责任，且根据当事人订立合同的本意推定不出为一般保证责任的，视为连带责任保证。  
在本批复施行前，判决、裁定己经发生法律效力的担保纠纷案件，当事人申请再审或者按审到监督程序决定再审的，不适用本批复。  
此复  
2002年11月23日

**Article None** II. Where a guaranty contract concluded before the Guarantee Law took effect has no agreement on or an unclear agreement on the mode of the guarantor's liability, it shall be deemed as general suretyship. If the guaranty contract explicitly stipulates that the guarantor shall assume the guaranty liability only when the debtor is unable to perform the debt, it shall be regarded as general suretyship. If the guaranty contract explicitly stipulates that the guarantor shall assume the guaranty liability when the guaranteed person fails to perform the debt, and it cannot be presumed as general suretyship liability according to the true intention of the parties when concluding the contract, it shall be regarded as joint and several suretyship.  
  
Before this Reply comes into force, for guarantee dispute cases where judgments or rulings have already taken legal effect, if any party applies for retrial or a decision is made to retry the case according to the trial supervision procedure, this Reply shall not apply.  
  
This is our reply.  
  
November 23, 2002
