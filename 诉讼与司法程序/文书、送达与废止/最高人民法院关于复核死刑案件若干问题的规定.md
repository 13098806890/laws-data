# 最高人民法院关于复核死刑案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕4号  
**公布日期**：2007-01-22  
**生效日期**：2007-02-28

> 《最高人民法院关于复核死刑案件若干问题的规定》已于2007年1月22日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1414次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年2月28日起施行。 2007年2月27日 最高人民法院 关于复核死刑案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2007〕4号 为确保死刑案件审判质量，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，结合审判实践，现就最高人民法院复核死刑案件的若干问题规定如下： 第一条  最高人民法院复核死刑案件，应当作出核准的裁定、判决，或者作出不予核准的裁定。 第二条  原判认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当、诉讼程序合法的，裁定予以核准。 原判判处被告人死刑并无不当，但具体认定的某一事实或者引用的法律条款等不完全准确、规范的，可以在纠正后作出核准死刑的判决或者裁定。 第三条  最高人民法院复核后认为原判认定事实不清、证据不足的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。 第四条  最高人民法院复核后认为原判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。 第五条  最高人民法院复核后认为原审人民法院违反法定诉讼程序，可能影响公正审判的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。 第六条  数罪并罚案件，一人有两罪以上被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，认为其中部分犯罪的死刑裁判认定事实不清、证据不足的，对全案裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判；认为其中部分犯罪的死刑裁判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，可以改判并对其他应当判处死刑的犯罪作出核准死刑的判决。 第七条  一案中两名以上被告人被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，认为其中部分被告人的死刑裁判认定事实不清、证据不足的，对全案裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判；认为其中部分被告人的死刑裁判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，可以改判并对其他应当判处死刑的被告人作出核准死刑的判决。 第八条  最高人民法院裁定不予核准死刑的，根据案件具体情形可以发回第二审人民法院或者第一审人民法院重新审判。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  最高人民法院复核死刑案件，应当作出核准的裁定、判决，或者作出不予核准的裁定。

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court, when reviewing a death penalty case, shall make a ruling or judgment to approve, or make a ruling not to approve.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  原判认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当、诉讼程序合法的，裁定予以核准。  
原判判处被告人死刑并无不当，但具体认定的某一事实或者引用的法律条款等不完全准确、规范的，可以在纠正后作出核准死刑的判决或者裁定。

**Article 2** Where the facts found and the application of law in the original judgment are correct, the sentence is appropriate, and the legal procedure is lawful, a ruling shall be made to approve.  
Where the original judgment sentences the defendant to death without impropriety, but a specific fact found or a legal provision cited is not fully accurate or standard, a judgment or ruling approving the death penalty may be made after correction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  最高人民法院复核后认为原判认定事实不清、证据不足的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。

**Article 3** Where the Supreme People's Court, after review, finds that the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it shall make a ruling not to approve, and shall vacate the original judgment and remand for retrial.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  最高人民法院复核后认为原判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。

**Article 4** Where the Supreme People's Court, after review, finds that the facts in the original judgment are correct, but according to law the death penalty should not be imposed, it shall make a ruling not to approve, and shall vacate the original judgment and remand for retrial.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  最高人民法院复核后认为原审人民法院违反法定诉讼程序，可能影响公正审判的，裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。

**Article 5** Where the Supreme People's Court, after review, finds that the original trial People's Court violated the statutory procedure and it may affect a fair trial, it shall make a ruling not to approve, and shall vacate the original judgment and remand for retrial.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  数罪并罚案件，一人有两罪以上被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，认为其中部分犯罪的死刑裁判认定事实不清、证据不足的，对全案裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判；认为其中部分犯罪的死刑裁判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，可以改判并对其他应当判处死刑的犯罪作出核准死刑的判决。

**Article 6** In a case of concurrent punishment for multiple crimes, where a person is sentenced to death for two or more crimes, after review by the Supreme People's Court, if it finds that the death judgment for some of the crimes has unclear facts or insufficient evidence, it shall make a ruling not to approve the entire case, and shall vacate the original judgment and remand for retrial; if it finds that the death judgment for some of the crimes has correct facts, but according to law the death penalty should not be imposed, it may amend the judgment and make a judgment approving the death penalty for other crimes for which the death penalty is to be imposed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  一案中两名以上被告人被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，认为其中部分被告人的死刑裁判认定事实不清、证据不足的，对全案裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判；认为其中部分被告人的死刑裁判认定事实正确，但依法不应当判处死刑的，可以改判并对其他应当判处死刑的被告人作出核准死刑的判决。

**Article 7** Where two or more defendants are sentenced to death in the same case, after review by the Supreme People's Court, if it finds that the death judgment for some of the defendants has unclear facts or insufficient evidence, it shall make a ruling not to approve the entire case, and shall vacate the original judgment and remand for retrial; if it finds that the death judgment for some of the defendants has correct facts, but according to law the death penalty should not be imposed, it may amend the judgment and make a judgment approving the death penalty for other defendants for whom the death penalty is to be imposed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  最高人民法院裁定不予核准死刑的，根据案件具体情形可以发回第二审人民法院或者第一审人民法院重新审判。  
高级人民法院依照复核程序审理后报请最高人民法院核准死刑的案件，最高人民法院裁定不予核准死刑，发回高级人民法院重新审判的，高级人民法院可以提审或者发回第一审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 8** Where the Supreme People's Court makes a ruling not to approve the death penalty, it may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, remand to the second-instance People's Court or the first-instance People's Court for retrial.  
For a case that the Higher People's Court has tried according to the review procedure and then submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval of the death penalty, where the Supreme People's Court makes a ruling not to approve the death penalty and remands to the Higher People's Court for retrial, the Higher People's Court may either bring the case up for trial itself or remand to the first-instance People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  发回第二审人民法院重新审判的案件，第二审人民法院可以直接改判；必须通过开庭审理查清事实、核实证据的，或者必须通过开庭审理纠正原审程序违法的，应当开庭审理。

**Article 9** For a case remanded to the second-instance People's Court for retrial, the second-instance People's Court may directly amend the judgment; if it is necessary to open a court session to clarify facts or verify evidence, or to correct procedural violations in the original trial, a court session shall be opened.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  发回第一审人民法院重新审判的案件，第一审人民法院应当开庭审理。

**Article 10** For a case remanded to the first-instance People's Court for retrial, the first-instance People's Court shall open a court session.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  依照[本规定第三条](#art-3)、第五条、第六条、第七条发回重新审判的案件，原审人民法院应当另行组成合议庭进行审理。

**Article 11** For a case remanded for retrial in accordance with Articles 3, 5, 6, or 7 of these Provisions, the original trial People's Court shall form a new collegial panel to conduct the trial.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  最高人民法院依照本规定核准或者不予核准死刑的，裁判文书应当引用相关法律和司法解释条文，并说明理由。

**Article 12** Where the Supreme People's Court approves or does not approve the death penalty in accordance with these Provisions, the written judgment shall cite the relevant legal and judicial interpretation provisions and provide reasoning.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  本规定自发布之日起施行。  
本规定发布前的有关司法解释，与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.  
Where any relevant judicial interpretation issued before the promulgation of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
