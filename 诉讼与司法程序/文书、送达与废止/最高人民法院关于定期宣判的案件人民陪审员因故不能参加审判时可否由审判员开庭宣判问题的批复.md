# 最高人民法院关于定期宣判的案件人民陪审员因故不能参加审判时可否由审判员开庭宣判问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1981-08-04  
**生效日期**：1981-08-04

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于定期宣判的案件人民陪审员因故不能参加  
宣判时可否由审判员开庭宣判问题的批复  
1981年8月4日      〔81〕法研字第23号  
山东省高级人民法院：  
你院〔81〕鲁法研字第10号请示报告收悉。关于定期宣判的案件人民陪审员因故不能参加宣判可否由审判员开庭宣判的问题，本院1957年2月15日法研字第3417号批复曾规定：“定期宣判的案件，人民陪审员因故不能参加宣判，在不改变原来评议时所作的决定的情况下，可以由原来审判本案的审判员独自开庭宣判；判决书上仍应署审判本案的审判员和人民陪审员的姓名。”我们认为，现在仍可按照这一规定办理，即：当合议庭组成人员中某一人民陪审员因故不能参加宣判时，可由审判员和其他人民陪审员开庭宣判；人民陪审员都因故不能参加宣判时，可由审判员独自开庭宣判。判决书仍应由合议庭全体组成人员署名。  
此复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether a Judge May Pronounce Judgment in Court When a People's Assessor Is Unable to Attend the Pronouncement of a Judgment at a Fixed Date  
August 4, 1981 〔81〕Fa Yan Zi No. 23  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions No. 10 〔81〕Lu Fa Yan Zi. Regarding the issue of whether a judge may preside over the court session to pronounce judgment when a people's assessor is unable to attend the pronouncement of a judgment at a fixed date, this Court's Reply No. 3417 Fa Yan Zi of February 15, 1957 once provided: "For a case where judgment is pronounced at a fixed date, if a people's assessor is unable to attend the pronouncement, the judge who originally tried the case may preside over the court session alone to pronounce the judgment, provided that the decision made during the original deliberation is not altered; the judgment shall still bear the names of both the judge and the people's assessor who tried the case." We are of the opinion that this provision may still be followed, that is: when one of the people's assessors in the collegial panel is unable to attend the pronouncement due to some reason, the judge and the other people's assessors may preside over the court session to pronounce the judgment; when all the people's assessors are unable to attend the pronouncement due to some reason, the judge may preside over the court session alone to pronounce the judgment. The judgment shall still be signed by all members of the collegial panel.  
This reply.
