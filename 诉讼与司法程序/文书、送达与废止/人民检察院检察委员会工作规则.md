### 第一章 总 则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  为了规范人民检察院检察委员会工作，保障检察委员会依法履行职责，根据《中华人民共和国人民检察院组织法》《中华人民共和国检察官法》等有关法律规定，结合检察工作实际，制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the Organic Law of the People's Procuratorates of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Procurators, and other relevant laws, and in light of the actual work of procuratorial work, for the purpose of regulating the work of the Procuratorial Committee of the People's Procuratorate and ensuring that the Procuratorial Committee performs its duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  检察委员会是人民检察院的办案组织和重大业务工作议事决策机构。

**Article 2** The Procuratorial Committee is the case-handling organization of the People's Procuratorate and the deliberative and decision-making body for major operational work.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  检察委员会讨论决定案件和事项实行民主集中制。

**Article 3** The Procuratorial Committee shall practice democratic centralism in discussing and deciding on cases and matters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  检察委员会履行下列职能：  
（一）讨论决定重大、疑难、复杂案件；  
（二）总结检察工作经验；  
（三）讨论决定有关检察工作的其他重大问题。

**Article 4** The Procuratorial Committee shall perform the following functions:  
(1) Discussing and deciding on major, difficult, and complex cases;  
(2) Summarizing procuratorial work experience;  
(3) Discussing and deciding on other major issues concerning procuratorial work.

### 第二章 组成人员

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  检察委员会由检察长、副检察长和若干资深检察官组成，成员应当为单数，并设专职委员。  
检察委员会委员依照法律规定任免。

**Article 5** The Procuratorial Committee shall be composed of the Chief Procurator, Deputy Chief Procurators, and a certain number of senior public procurators, and the number of members shall be an odd number, and there shall be full-time members.  
Members of the Procuratorial Committee shall be appointed and removed in accordance with the provisions of law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  担任检察委员会委员的资深检察官应当具备以下条件：  
（一）最高人民检察院应当为一级高级检察官以上等级的检察官；  
（二）省级人民检察院应当为三级高级检察官以上等级的检察官；  
（三）设区的市级人民检察院应当为一级检察官以上等级的检察官；  
（四）基层人民检察院应当为三级检察官以上等级的检察官。

**Article 6** Senior public procurators serving as members of the Procuratorial Committee shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) For the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the public procurator shall be at the level of First-class Senior Public Procurator or above;  
(2) For a provincial people's procuratorate, the public procurator shall be at the level of Third-class Senior Public Procurator or above;  
(3) For a people's procuratorate of a city divided into districts, the public procurator shall be at the level of First-class Public Procurator or above;  
(4) For a primary people's procuratorate, the public procurator shall be at the level of Third-class Public Procurator or above.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  检察委员会委员履行下列职责：  
（一）审阅检察委员会讨论的案件材料和事项材料，发表意见，参加表决；  
（二）受检察长或者检察委员会指派，对检察委员会决定的落实进行督促检查；  
（三）参加检察委员会集体学习；  
（四）应当履行的其他职责。  
检察委员会委员应当遵守检察委员会各项规章制度。

**Article 7** Members of the Procuratorial Committee shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Reviewing case materials and matter materials discussed by the Procuratorial Committee, expressing opinions, and participating in voting;  
(2) Being assigned by the Chief Procurator or the Procuratorial Committee to supervise and inspect the implementation of decisions of the Procuratorial Committee;  
(3) Participating in collective learning of the Procuratorial Committee;  
(4) Other duties that shall be performed.  
Members of the Procuratorial Committee shall comply with the rules and regulations of the Procuratorial Committee.

### 第三章 讨论决定的案件和事项范围

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民检察院办理下列案件，应当提交检察委员会讨论决定：  
（一）涉及国家重大利益和严重影响社会稳定的案件；  
（二）拟层报最高人民检察院核准追诉或者核准按照缺席审判程序提起公诉的案件；  
（三）拟提请或者提出抗诉的重大、疑难、复杂案件；  
（四）拟向上级人民检察院请示的案件；  
（五）对检察委员会原决定进行复议的案件；  
（六）其他重大、疑难、复杂案件。  
检察委员会讨论案件，办案检察官对其汇报的事实负责，检察委员会委员对本人发表的意见和表决负责。

**Article 8** A People's Procuratorate shall, when handling the following cases, submit them to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision:  
(1) Cases involving major national interests and seriously affecting social stability;  
(2) Cases for which it is intended to report level by level to the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval of prosecution or approval to initiate a public prosecution under the trial by default procedure;  
(3) Major, difficult, and complex cases for which it is intended to request or make a protest;  
(4) Cases for which it is intended to request instructions from the people's procuratorate at a higher level;  
(5) Cases for reviewing the original decision of the Procuratorial Committee;  
(6) Other major, difficult, and complex cases.  
When the Procuratorial Committee discusses a case, the handling public procurator shall be responsible for the facts reported, and the members of the Procuratorial Committee shall be responsible for the opinions expressed and the votes cast.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民检察院办理下列事项，应当提交检察委员会讨论决定：  
（一）在检察工作中贯彻执行党中央关于全面依法治国重大战略部署和国家法律、政策的重大问题；  
（二）贯彻执行本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会决议的重要措施，拟提交本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会的工作报告；  
（三）最高人民检察院对属于检察工作中具体应用法律的问题进行解释，发布指导性案例；  
（四）围绕刑事、民事、行政、公益诉讼检察业务工作遇到的重大情况、重要问题，总结办案经验教训，研究对策措施；  
（五）对检察委员会原决定进行复议的事项；  
（六）本级人民检察院检察长、公安机关负责人的回避；  
（七）拟向上一级人民检察院请示或者报告的重大事项；  
（八）其他重大事项。

**Article 9** A People's Procuratorate shall, when handling the following matters, submit them to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision:  
(1) Major issues in implementing the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China's major strategic plan for comprehensively governing the country according to law and national laws and policies in procuratorial work;  
(2) Important measures for implementing the resolutions of the People's Congress and its Standing Committee at the corresponding level, and the work reports to be submitted to the People's Congress and its Standing Committee at the corresponding level;  
(3) The Supreme People's Procuratorate's interpretation of issues concerning the specific application of law in procuratorial work and issuance of guiding cases;  
(4) Summarizing lessons from case handling experience and studying countermeasures around major situations and important issues encountered in criminal, civil, administrative, and public interest litigation procuratorial work;  
(5) Matters for reviewing the original decision of the Procuratorial Committee;  
(6) The recusal of the Chief Procurator of the people's procuratorate at the corresponding level and the head of the public security organ;  
(7) Major matters intended to request instructions from or report to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level;  
(8) Other major matters.

### 第四章 会议制度

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  检察委员会实行例会制，定期开会。必要时，可以提前或者推迟召开会议。

**Article 10** The Procuratorial Committee shall adopt a regular meeting system and hold meetings periodically. When necessary, meetings may be held earlier or postponed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  检察委员会召开会议，应当有全体委员半数以上出席。  
检察委员会委员因特殊原因不能出席会议的，应当向检察长或者主持会议的副检察长请假，并告知检察委员会办事机构。

**Article 11** When the Procuratorial Committee holds a meeting, more than half of all members shall be present.  
If a member of the Procuratorial Committee cannot attend the meeting due to special reasons, the member shall ask for leave from the Chief Procurator or the Deputy Chief Procurator presiding over the meeting and inform the working body of the Procuratorial Committee.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 对于拟提交检察委员会讨论决定的案件和事项，办案检察官和事项承办人应当制作报告，经其所在内设机构负责人审核后报检察长决定。

**Article 12** For cases and matters to be submitted to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision, the handling public procurator and the person undertaking the matter shall prepare a report, which shall be verified by the head of the internal institution to which they belong and then submitted to the Chief Procurator for decision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 检察长决定将案件和事项提交检察委员会讨论的，检察委员会办事机构应当对办案检察官或者事项承办人移送的案件材料和事项材料进行审核，认为案件、事项及其报告的内容和形式不符合相关规定或者欠缺有关材料的，应当提出意见，由办案检察官或者事项承办人修改、补充。办事机构可以对案件和事项涉及的法律问题提出意见，供办案检察官或者事项承办人参考，并作为附件编入会议材料。

**Article 13** Where the Chief Procurator decides to submit a case or matter to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion, the working body of the Procuratorial Committee shall review the case materials and matter materials transferred by the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter. If it considers that the content or form of the case, matter, or report does not comply with relevant provisions or that relevant materials are lacking, it shall propose opinions, and the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter shall amend or supplement them. The working body may propose opinions on the legal issues involved in the case or matter for reference by the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter, and compile them as attachments into the meeting materials.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 检察委员会办事机构应当根据会议排期或者案件、事项紧迫程度，提出会议议程建议，报检察长决定。除特殊情况外，一般应当在会议召开三日前将会议通知、议程和案件材料或者事项材料等分送检察委员会委员、列席会议的人员和办案检察官或者事项承办人。

**Article 14** The working body of the Procuratorial Committee shall, based on the meeting schedule or the urgency of the case or matter, propose a meeting agenda and submit it to the Chief Procurator for decision. Except under special circumstances, the meeting notice, agenda, and case materials or matter materials shall generally be distributed to the members of the Procuratorial Committee, persons attending the meeting as observers, and the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter three days before the meeting.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  检察委员会委员接到会议通知和议程后，应当认真审阅案件材料或者事项材料，准备意见，按时出席会议。

**Article 15** After receiving the meeting notice and agenda, members of the Procuratorial Committee shall carefully review the case materials or matter materials, prepare opinions, and attend the meeting on time.

### 第五章 会议程序

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  检察委员会会议由检察长主持。检察长因故不能出席的，应当委托一名副检察长主持；出现检察长职位空缺等不能委托情形的，由分管人民检察院日常工作的副检察长主持。

**Article 16** The meeting of the Procuratorial Committee shall be presided over by the Chief Procurator. If the Chief Procurator cannot attend due to reasons, the Chief Procurator shall delegate a Deputy Chief Procurator to preside; if there is a vacancy in the position of Chief Procurator or other circumstances where delegation is not possible, the Deputy Chief Procurator in charge of the daily work of the People's Procuratorate shall preside.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  检察委员会讨论决定案件和事项，按照以下程序进行：  
（一）办案检察官或者事项承办人汇报，其所在内设机构负责人可以补充说明情况；  
（二）检察委员会委员提问；  
（三）检察委员会委员发表意见。顺序一般为：委员、专职委员、担任副检察长的委员、主持会议的委员。必要时，主持人可以请有关列席人员发表意见；  
（四）主持人总结讨论情况；  
（五）表决。

**Article 17** The Procuratorial Committee shall follow the following procedures when discussing and deciding on cases and matters:  
(1) The handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter makes a report, and the head of the internal institution to which they belong may supplement the explanation of the situation;  
(2) Members of the Procuratorial Committee ask questions;  
(3) Members of the Procuratorial Committee express opinions. The order is generally: members, full-time members, members who are Deputy Chief Procurators, and the member presiding over the meeting. When necessary, the presiding person may ask relevant observers to express opinions;  
(4) The presiding person summarizes the discussion;  
(5) Voting.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  检察委员会委员应当围绕讨论决定的案件和事项发表意见，提出明确的观点，并说明理由和依据。

**Article 18** Members of the Procuratorial Committee shall express opinions around the cases and matters under discussion and decision, present clear viewpoints, and state the reasons and basis.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  检察长、副检察长、检察委员会专职委员作为主办检察官或者独任检察官承办的案件或者事项提交检察委员会讨论的，应当履行办案检察官或者事项承办人和检察委员会委员的双重职责。

**Article 19** Where the Chief Procurator, Deputy Chief Procurator, or full-time member of the Procuratorial Committee, as the presiding public procurator or sole public procurator, undertakes a case or matter that is submitted to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion, they shall perform the dual duties of the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter and of a member of the Procuratorial Committee.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  对于提交讨论决定的案件和事项，检察委员会应当在讨论后进行表决。认为需要补充相关情况和材料的，可以责成办案检察官或者事项承办人补充相关情况和材料后，重新提交检察委员会讨论决定。

**Article 20** For cases and matters submitted for discussion and decision, the Procuratorial Committee shall conduct a vote after discussion. If it considers that relevant circumstances and materials need to be supplemented, it may instruct the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter to supplement the relevant circumstances and materials and resubmit them to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  检察委员会表决案件和事项，除分别依照[本规则第二十二条](#art-22)或者第二十三条的规定办理外，应当按照全体委员过半数的意见作出决定。少数委员的意见应当记录在卷。  
表决结果由会议主持人当场宣布。

**Article 21** When the Procuratorial Committee votes on cases and matters, except where handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 22 or Article 23 of these Rules respectively, it shall make a decision based on the opinions of more than half of all members. The opinions of the minority members shall be recorded in the file.  
The voting result shall be announced on the spot by the person presiding over the meeting.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  地方各级人民检察院检察长不同意本院检察委员会全体委员过半数的意见，属于办理案件的，可以报请上一级人民检察院决定；属于重大事项的，可以报请上一级人民检察院或者本级人民代表大会常务委员会决定。报请本级人民代表大会常务委员会决定的，应当同时抄报上一级人民检察院。

**Article 22** Where the Chief Procurator of a local people's procuratorate at any level disagrees with the opinions of more than half of all members of the Procuratorial Committee of that procuratorate, if it concerns a case, the matter may be submitted to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for decision; if it concerns a major matter, it may be submitted to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level or the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for decision. If it is submitted to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for decision, it shall also be reported to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for filing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  地方各级人民检察院检察委员会表决案件和事项，没有一种意见超过全体委员半数，如果全体委员出席会议的，应当报请上一级人民检察院决定。如果部分委员出席会议的， 应当书面征求未出席会议委员的意见。征求意见后，应当按照全体委员过半数的意见作出决定，或者依照[本规则第二十二条](#art-22)的规定办理；仍没有一种意见超过全体委员半数的，应当报请上一级人民检察院决定。

**Article 23** When the Procuratorial Committee of a local people's procuratorate at any level votes on cases and matters, if no opinion exceeds half of all members, if all members are present at the meeting, the matter shall be submitted to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for decision. If only some members are present, the opinions of members not present at the meeting shall be solicited in writing. After soliciting opinions, a decision shall be made based on the opinions of more than half of all members, or handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 22 of these Rules; if still no opinion exceeds half of all members, the matter shall be submitted to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for decision.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  受委托主持会议的副检察长应当在会后将会议讨论情况和表决结果及时报告检察长。报告检察长后，依照[本规则第二十一条](#art-21)的规定办理。

**Article 24** The Deputy Chief Procurator entrusted to preside over the meeting shall promptly report the discussion and voting results to the Chief Procurator after the meeting. After reporting to the Chief Procurator, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of these Rules.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  检察委员会讨论决定案件和事项，副检察长、检察委员会委员具有应当回避的情形的，应当申请回避并由检察长决定；本人没有申请回避的，检察长应当决定其回避。  
检察长的回避依照[本规则第九条](#art-9)的规定办理。

**Article 25** When the Procuratorial Committee discusses and decides on cases and matters, if a Deputy Chief Procurator or a member of the Procuratorial Committee has circumstances that shall cause recusal, they shall apply for recusal and the Chief Procurator shall decide; if they do not apply for recusal, the Chief Procurator shall decide on their recusal.  
The recusal of the Chief Procurator shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these Rules.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  检察委员会召开会议，经检察长决定，不担任检察委员会委员的院领导和有关内设机构负责人可以列席会议；必要时，可以决定其他有关人员列席会议。

**Article 26** When the Procuratorial Committee holds a meeting, with the decision of the Chief Procurator, leaders of the procuratorate who are not members of the Procuratorial Committee and heads of relevant internal institutions may attend the meeting as observers; when necessary, other relevant persons may be decided to attend the meeting as observers.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  检察委员会讨论和决定的情况，由检察委员会办事机构进行录音录像并如实记录，经检察长审批后存档。检察委员会委员不得要求或者自行在会议记录上修改已发表的意见和观点。  
任何人未经检察长批准，不得查阅、抄录、复制检察委员会会议记录；办案检察官或者事项承办人查阅、抄录、复制检察委员会委员关于所办案件和事项的具体意见除外。

**Article 27** The situation of discussion and decision of the Procuratorial Committee shall be audio and video recorded and truthfully recorded by the working body of the Procuratorial Committee, and archived after approval by the Chief Procurator. Members of the Procuratorial Committee shall not request or themselves modify the opinions and viewpoints already expressed in the meeting record.  
No person shall inspect, transcribe, or reproduce the meeting record of the Procuratorial Committee without the approval of the Chief Procurator; except that the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter may inspect, transcribe, or reproduce the specific opinions of the members of the Procuratorial Committee on the case or matter they handle.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  检察委员会讨论案件和事项，检察委员会办事机构应当制作会议纪要和检察委员会决定事项通知书。纪要经检察长或者主持会议的副检察长审批后分送委员，并报上一级人民检察院检察委员会办事机构备案。检察委员会决定事项通知书发办案检察官或者事项承办人和有关内设机构执行。  
检察委员会的决定需要有关下级人民检察院执行的，应当以人民检察院名义作出书面决定。  
检察委员会会议纪要应当按照相关规定存档。

**Article 28** When the Procuratorial Committee discusses cases and matters, the working body of the Procuratorial Committee shall produce meeting minutes and a notice of matters decided by the Procuratorial Committee. The minutes, after approval by the Chief Procurator or the Deputy Chief Procurator presiding over the meeting, shall be distributed to the members and reported to the working body of the Procuratorial Committee of the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for filing. The notice of matters decided by the Procuratorial Committee shall be issued to the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter and the relevant internal institutions for enforcement.  
If a decision of the Procuratorial Committee requires enforcement by a relevant lower-level people's procuratorate, a written decision shall be made in the name of the People's Procuratorate.  
The meeting minutes of the Procuratorial Committee shall be archived in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第六章 决定的执行和督办

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  检察委员会的决定，办案检察官或者事项承办人和有关内设机构、下级人民检察院应当及时执行。  
检察委员会原则通过但提出完善意见的司法解释、规范性文件、工作经验总结等事项，承办内设机构应当根据意见进行修改。修改情况应当书面报告检察长。

**Article 29** The decisions of the Procuratorial Committee shall be promptly enforced by the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter, the relevant internal institutions, and the lower-level people's procuratorates.  
For matters such as judicial interpretations, normative documents, and summaries of work experience that are adopted in principle by the Procuratorial Committee but with suggestions for improvement, the undertaking internal institution shall make amendments based on the suggestions. The amendments shall be reported in writing to the Chief Procurator.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  办案检察官或者事项承办人和有关内设机构因特殊原因无法及时执行检察委员会决定或者在执行完毕前出现新情况的，应当立即书面报告检察长。  
下级人民检察院因特殊原因无法及时执行上级人民检察院检察委员会决定或者执行完毕前出现新情况的，应当立即书面报告上级人民检察院。

**Article 30** Where the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter and the relevant internal institution cannot timely enforce a decision of the Procuratorial Committee due to special reasons, or new circumstances arise before enforcement is completed, they shall immediately report in writing to the Chief Procurator.  
Where a lower-level people's procuratorate cannot timely enforce a decision of the Procuratorial Committee of a higher-level people's procuratorate due to special reasons, or new circumstances arise before enforcement is completed, it shall immediately report in writing to the higher-level people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  下级人民检察院不同意上级人民检察院检察委员会决定的，可以向上级人民检察院书面报告，但是不能停止对该决定的执行。  
上级人民检察院有关内设机构应当对下级人民检察院书面报告进行审查并提出意见，报检察长决定。检察长决定提交检察委员会复议的，可以通知下级人民检察院暂停执行原决定，并在接到报告后的一个月以内召开检察委员会会议进行复议。经复议认为原决定确有错误或者出现新情况的，应当作出新的决定；认为原决定正确的，应当作出维持的决定。经复议作出的决定，下级人民检察院应当执行。

**Article 31** Where a lower-level people's procuratorate disagrees with a decision of the Procuratorial Committee of a higher-level people's procuratorate, it may report in writing to the higher-level people's procuratorate, but shall not suspend the enforcement of the decision.  
The relevant internal institution of the higher-level people's procuratorate shall review the written report of the lower-level people's procuratorate, propose opinions, and submit them to the Chief Procurator for decision. If the Chief Procurator decides to submit the matter to the Procuratorial Committee for review, the higher-level people's procuratorate may notify the lower-level people's procuratorate to suspend the enforcement of the original decision, and shall convene a meeting of the Procuratorial Committee for review within one month after receiving the report. If upon review it is considered that the original decision is indeed erroneous or that new circumstances have arisen, a new decision shall be made; if the original decision is considered correct, a decision to maintain it shall be made. The decision made upon review shall be enforced by the lower-level people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条  办案检察官或者事项承办人应当在执行检察委员会决定完毕后五日以内填写《检察委员会决定事项执行情况反馈表》，连同反映执行情况、案件办理情况的相关材料，经所在内设机构负责人审核后送检察委员会办事机构。  
对于上级人民检察院检察委员会作出的决定，有关下级人民检察院应当在执行完毕后五日以内将反映执行情况、案件办理情况的相关材料，报送上级人民检察院。

**Article 32** The handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter shall, within five days after completing the enforcement of a decision of the Procuratorial Committee, fill in the 'Feedback Form on the Enforcement of Matters Decided by the Procuratorial Committee' and, together with relevant materials reflecting the enforcement and case handling, submit them to the working body of the Procuratorial Committee after verification by the head of the internal institution to which they belong.  
For a decision made by the Procuratorial Committee of a higher-level people's procuratorate, the relevant lower-level people's procuratorate shall, within five days after completing enforcement, submit relevant materials reflecting the enforcement and case handling to the higher-level people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条  检察委员会办事机构应当及时了解办案检察官或者事项承办人和有关内设机构、下级人民检察院执行检察委员会决定的情况，必要时进行督办，重要情况及时报告检察长。

**Article 33** The working body of the Procuratorial Committee shall promptly understand the situation of enforcement of decisions of the Procuratorial Committee by the handling public procurator or the person undertaking the matter, the relevant internal institutions, and lower-level people's procuratorates, and when necessary, supervise enforcement, and report important situations to the Chief Procurator in a timely manner.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条  对故意拖延、拒不执行检察委员会决定的，应当按照有关规定追究责任人员的法律、纪律责任。

**Article 34** Where a person intentionally delays or refuses to enforce a decision of the Procuratorial Committee, the legal and disciplinary responsibilities of the responsible person shall be pursued in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第七章 办事机构

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条  人民检察院设立检察委员会办事机构，负责检察委员会日常工作。

**Article 35** A People's Procuratorate shall establish a working body of the Procuratorial Committee, which shall be responsible for the daily work of the Procuratorial Committee.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条  检察委员会办事机构履行下列职责：  
（一）对拟提交检察委员会讨论的案件材料和事项材料是否规范进行审核；  
（二）对拟提交检察委员会讨论的案件和事项涉及的法律问题提出意见；  
（三）承担检察委员会会务工作和检察长列席同级人民法院审判委员会会议相关工作；  
（四）对检察委员会决定进行督办并向检察长和检察委员会报告；  
（五）对下一级人民检察院报送备案的检察委员会会议纪要进行审查并向检察委员会报告；  
（六）对以人民检察院名义制发的司法解释、规范性文件、司法政策文件和指导性案例等，在印发前进行法律审核；  
（七）检察长或者检察委员会交办的其他工作。

**Article 36** The working body of the Procuratorial Committee shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Review whether the case materials and matter materials to be submitted to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion are standardized;  
(2) Propose opinions on legal issues involved in cases and matters to be submitted to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion;  
(3) Undertake the meeting affairs of the Procuratorial Committee and work related to the Chief Procurator's attendance at the meeting of the Adjudication Committee of the People's Court at the corresponding level;  
(4) Supervise the enforcement of decisions of the Procuratorial Committee and report to the Chief Procurator and the Procuratorial Committee;  
(5) Review the meeting minutes of the Procuratorial Committee submitted for filing by the people's procuratorate at the next lower level and report to the Procuratorial Committee;  
(6) Conduct legal review before issuance of judicial interpretations, normative documents, judicial policy documents, and guiding cases issued in the name of the People's Procuratorate;  
(7) Other work assigned by the Chief Procurator or the Procuratorial Committee.

### 第八章 附 则

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条  检察委员会讨论决定的案件和事项，其提交、讨论、表决、作出决定、执行和督办等均在统一业务应用系统中进行，全程留痕。

**Article 37** The submission, discussion, voting, decision-making, enforcement, and supervision of cases and matters discussed and decided by the Procuratorial Committee shall all be conducted in a unified business application system, with full traceability.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条  出席、列席检察委员会会议的人员，对检察委员会讨论的内容和情况应当保密。

**Article 38** Persons attending or observing meetings of the Procuratorial Committee shall keep confidential the content and circumstances of the discussions of the Procuratorial Committee.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条  检察委员会及其委员的司法责任的认定和追究，适用《中华人民共和国人民检察院组织法》《中华人民共和国检察官法》和检察官惩戒相关规定。

**Article 39** The determination and pursuit of judicial responsibility of the Procuratorial Committee and its members shall be governed by the Organic Law of the People's Procuratorates of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Procurators, and relevant provisions on disciplinary action against public procurators.
