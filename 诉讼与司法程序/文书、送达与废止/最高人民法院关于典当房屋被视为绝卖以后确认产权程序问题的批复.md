### 最高人民法院关于典当房屋被视为绝卖以后确认产权程序问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于典当房屋被视为绝卖以后确认产权程序问题的批复  
  
（1989）法民字第17号  
  
山西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1989）晋法民报字第 3号《关于典当房屋被视为绝卖以后产权确认问题的请示》收悉。经研究，现答复如下：  
  
出典的房屋已超过规定的回赎期限，承典人提起确认房屋产权归己的诉讼时，人民法院应分别情况适用不同程序：对出典人或者其继承人提出异议的，应按照民事诉讼法（试行）规定的普通程序或简易程序进行审理；对出典人或者其继承人无异议的，或者出典人已经死亡又无继承人的，可以比照民事诉讼法（试行）特别程序的有关规定进行审理。经审理，如确认房屋产权归承典人所有，承典人即可持法院判决书向房管部门中请办理产权登记手续。  
  
1989年7月24日

**Article 1** Reply on the Procedure for Confirming Property Rights after a Pawned House Is Deemed a Permanent Sale  
(1989) Fa Min Zi No. 17  
Shanxi Higher People's Court:  
Your Request on the Confirmation of Property Rights after a Pawned House Is Deemed a Permanent Sale ((1989) Jin Fa Min Bao Zi No. 3) has been received. Upon examination, the reply is as follows:  
Where a pawned house has exceeded the prescribed redemption period, and the pledgee initiates an action to confirm that the property right of the house belongs to him, the People's Court shall apply different procedures according to the circumstances: if the pawner or his heir raises an objection, the case shall be tried in accordance with the ordinary procedure or summary procedure provided for in the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation); if the pawner or his heir raises no objection, or the pawner has died and has no heir, the case may be tried by reference to the relevant provisions on special procedure in the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation). Upon trial, if the property right of the house is confirmed to belong to the pledgee, the pledgee may, with the court judgment, apply to the housing administration department for registration of property rights.  
July 24, 1989
