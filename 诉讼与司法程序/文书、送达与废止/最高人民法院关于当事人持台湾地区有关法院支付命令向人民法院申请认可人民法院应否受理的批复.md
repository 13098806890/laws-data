### 最高人民法院关于当事人持台湾地区有关法院支付命令向人民法院申请认可人民法院应否受理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于当事人持台湾地区有关法院支付命令  
  
向人民法院申请认可人民法院应否受理的批复  
  
（2001年3月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1053次会议通过，自2001年4月27日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕13号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院粤高法立〔2000〕30号《关于当事人持台湾地区有关法院支付命令向人民法院申请认可，人民法院应否受理的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
人民法院对当事人持台湾地区有关法院支付命令及其确定证明书申请其认可的，可比照我院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的规定》予以受理。

**Article 1** Judicial Interpretation No. 13 [2001]  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
The petition submitted by your court, No. Yue Gao Fa Li [2000] 30, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether a People's Court Shall Accept an Application for Recognition Filed by a Party Holding a Payment Order Issued by a Relevant Court in the Taiwan Region" has been received and noted. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a party holds a payment order issued by a relevant court in the Taiwan Region and its certificate of confirmation and applies to a People's Court for recognition, the People's Court may accept the application by reference to the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Recognition of Civil Judgments Rendered by Relevant Courts in the Taiwan Region.
