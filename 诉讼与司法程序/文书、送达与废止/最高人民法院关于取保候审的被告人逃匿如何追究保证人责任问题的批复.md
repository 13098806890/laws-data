# 最高人民法院关于取保候审的被告人逃匿如何追究保证人责任问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1989〕4号  
**公布日期**：1989-07-03

---

### 正文

一、根据案件事实，取保候审的被告人确系犯罪分子，如保证人与被告人串通，协助被告人逃匿，视其情节，已构成犯罪的，可根据刑法第一百六十二条规定的窝藏罪，追究保证人的刑事责任。

**Article None** 1. If, based on the facts of the case, the defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial is indeed a criminal, and the surety colludes with the defendant to assist the defendant in absconding, and the circumstances are such that a crime has been constituted, the surety may be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the crime of harboring as prescribed in Article 162 of the Criminal Law.

二、在刑事附带民事诉讼中，取保候审的被告人逃匿，如保证人明知其逃匿下落，但拒绝提供被告人去处或者拒绝将被告人找回，以及与被告人串通，协助被告人逃匿的，可由保证人承担应由被告人承担的民事赔偿责任。  
1989年7月3日

**Article None** 2. In a criminal incidental civil action, where the defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial absconds, if the surety clearly knows the whereabouts of the absconding defendant but refuses to provide the defendant's location or refuses to bring the defendant back, or colludes with the defendant to assist the defendant in absconding, the surety may bear the civil compensation liability that should have been borne by the defendant.  
July 3, 1989
