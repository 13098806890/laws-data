### 最高人民法院关于在土改前已分家析产的房屋，土改时误登在一人名下的产权仍归双方各自所有的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于在土改前已分家析产的房屋，土改时误登在  
  
一人名下的产权仍归双方各自所有的批复  
  
（1985）法民字第21号  
  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鄂法（85）民行字第29号关于胡秋英与王惠珍房产纠纷一案的请示报告收悉。  
  
据报告称，胡秋英、王惠珍二人的公爹杨元臣原有祖遗房四间（正房三间、厨房一间），由胡、王各住一半，后来又经过了扩建和分家，双方仍各分住一半。土改时将双方所住房屋五间，全部填写在王惠珍的土地证上，但依然各自管业。一九八三年因胡秋英要拆房，与王惠珍发生争执，经公社、大队调解，双方达成协会，事后王惠珍反悔，以有土地证为凭，五间房屋应归她所有为由，向法院起诉。  
  
经我们研究认为：胡秋英与王惠珍所住房屋均系公爹杨元臣的祖遗房产，双方各分住一半，土改前分家时已经明确各自产权，几十年来从未发生争执，当地群众、双方亲属和双方当事人均承认各有一半产权。根据胡秋英和王惠珍双方长期居住管业的历史事实，双方讼争房屋应归胡秋英所有。  
  
一九八五年十二月二十七日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Property Rights of Houses That Were Divided and Separated Before the Land Reform but Were Mistakenly Registered Under One Person's Name During the Land Reform Still Belonging to Both Parties Respectively  
(1985) Min Fa Zi No. 21  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions Report No. E-Fa (85) Min Xing Zi No. 29 concerning the housing dispute between Hu Qiuying and Wang Huizhen.  
According to the report, Hu Qiuying and Wang Huizhen's father-in-law Yang Yuanchen originally had four ancestral rooms (three main rooms and one kitchen), with Hu and Wang each occupying half. Later, after expansion and division of the family property, the two parties still each occupied half. During the land reform, the five rooms occupied by both parties were all recorded on Wang Huizhen's land certificate, but the parties continued to manage their respective portions. In 1983, because Hu Qiuying intended to dismantle the house, a dispute arose with Wang Huizhen. After mediation by the commune and the production brigade, the two parties reached an agreement. Subsequently, Wang Huizhen reneged and filed a lawsuit with the court, claiming that, based on the land certificate, the five rooms should belong to her.  
Upon our study, we hold that: The houses occupied by Hu Qiuying and Wang Huizhen are all ancestral properties of their father-in-law Yang Yuanchen, with each party occupying half. At the time of the division of the family property before the land reform, the respective property rights had already been clearly defined. No dispute had ever arisen for decades. Local residents, relatives of both parties, and the parties themselves all acknowledge that each party owns half of the property rights. Based on the historical fact that Hu Qiuying and Wang Huizhen have long resided in and managed their respective portions, the disputed houses shall belong to Hu Qiuying.  
December 27, 1985
