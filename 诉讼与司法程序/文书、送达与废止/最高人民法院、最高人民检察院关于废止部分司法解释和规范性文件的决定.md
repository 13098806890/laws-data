关于废止部分司法解释和规范性文件的决定  
  
法释〔2010〕17号  
  
为适应形势发展变化，保证国家法律统一正确适用，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院会同有关部门，对2008年底以前制发的司法解释和规范性文件进行了集中清理。现决定将已实际废止或者不再适用的37件司法解释和规范性文件予以明令废止。  
  
最高人民法院、最高人民检察院决定废止的部分司法解释和规范性文件目录（37件）

**Article 1** December 13, 2010  
Supreme People's Court Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Decision on Abolishing Certain Judicial Interpretations and Normative Documents  
Fa Shi [2010] No. 17  
In order to adapt to changes in circumstances and ensure the uniform and correct application of national laws, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate, together with relevant departments, conducted a centralized review of judicial interpretations and normative documents issued before the end of 2008. It is hereby decided to expressly abolish 37 judicial interpretations and normative documents that have been actually abolished or are no longer applicable.
