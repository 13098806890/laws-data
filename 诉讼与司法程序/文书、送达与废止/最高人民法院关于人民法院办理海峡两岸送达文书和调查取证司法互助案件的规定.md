# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书和调查取证司法互助案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕15号  
**公布日期**：2010-12-16  
**生效日期**：2011-06-25

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书和调查取证司法互助案件的规定》已于2010年12月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1506次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年6月25日起施行。 2011年6月14日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书和 调查取证司法互助案件的规定 法释〔2011〕15号 为落实《海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议》（以下简称协议），进一步推动海峡两岸司法互助业务的开展，确保协议中涉及人民法院有关送达文书和调查取证司法互助工作事项的顺利实施，结合各级人民法院开展海峡两岸司法互助工作实践，制定本规定。 一、总  则 第一条  人民法院依照协议，办理海峡两岸民事、刑事、行政诉讼案件中的送达文书和调查取证司法互助业务，适用本规定。 第二条  人民法院应当在法定职权范围内办理海峡两岸司法互助业务。 人民法院办理海峡两岸司法互助业务，应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，不得违反社会公共利益。 二、职责分工 第三条  人民法院和台湾地区业务主管部门通过各自指定的协议联络人，建立办理海峡两岸司法互助业务的直接联络渠道。 第四条  最高人民法院是与台湾地区业务主管部门就海峡两岸司法互助业务进行联络的一级窗口。最高人民法院台湾司法事务办公室主任是最高人民法院指定的协议联络人。 最高人民法院负责：就协议中涉及人民法院的工作事项与台湾地区业务主管部门开展磋商、协调和交流；指导、监督、组织、协调地方各级人民法院办理海峡两岸司法互助业务；就海峡两岸调查取证司法互助业务与台湾地区业务主管部门直接联络，并在必要时具体办理调查取证司法互助案件；及时将本院和台湾地区业务主管部门指定的协议联络人的姓名、联络方式及变动情况等工作信息通报高级人民法院。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院依照协议，办理海峡两岸民事、刑事、行政诉讼案件中的送达文书和调查取证司法互助业务，适用本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to the handling by the People's Courts of judicial mutual assistance matters involving service of documents and investigation and evidence collection in civil, criminal, and administrative action cases between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait in accordance with the Agreement.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  人民法院应当在法定职权范围内办理海峡两岸司法互助业务。  
人民法院办理海峡两岸司法互助业务，应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，不得违反社会公共利益。  
二、职责分工

**Article 2** The People's Courts shall handle cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters within the scope of their statutory functions and powers.  
In handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters, the People's Courts shall adhere to the One-China principle, comply with the fundamental principles of national laws, and shall not violate social public interests.  
II. Division of Duties and Responsibilities

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院和台湾地区业务主管部门通过各自指定的协议联络人，建立办理海峡两岸司法互助业务的直接联络渠道。

**Article 3** The People's Courts and the competent authorities in the Taiwan region shall establish direct channels of contact for handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters through their respective designated Agreement Liaison Persons.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  最高人民法院是与台湾地区业务主管部门就海峡两岸司法互助业务进行联络的一级窗口。最高人民法院台湾司法事务办公室主任是最高人民法院指定的协议联络人。  
最高人民法院负责：就协议中涉及人民法院的工作事项与台湾地区业务主管部门开展磋商、协调和交流；指导、监督、组织、协调地方各级人民法院办理海峡两岸司法互助业务；就海峡两岸调查取证司法互助业务与台湾地区业务主管部门直接联络，并在必要时具体办理调查取证司法互助案件；及时将本院和台湾地区业务主管部门指定的协议联络人的姓名、联络方式及变动情况等工作信息通报高级人民法院。

**Article 4** The Supreme People's Court shall be the primary window for contacting the competent authorities in the Taiwan region regarding cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters. The Director of the Taiwan Judicial Affairs Office of the Supreme People's Court shall be the Agreement Liaison Person designated by the Supreme People's Court.  
The Supreme People's Court shall be responsible for: conducting consultations, coordination, and exchanges with the competent authorities in the Taiwan region on work matters involving the People's Courts under the Agreement; guiding, supervising, organizing, and coordinating the handling of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters by local People's Courts at various levels; directly contacting the competent authorities in the Taiwan region regarding cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving investigation and evidence collection, and, where necessary, specifically handling such judicial mutual assistance cases; and promptly notifying the Higher People's Courts of the names, contact methods, and changes thereof of the Agreement Liaison Persons designated by itself and by the competent authorities in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  最高人民法院授权高级人民法院就办理海峡两岸送达文书司法互助案件，建立与台湾地区业务主管部门联络的二级窗口。高级人民法院应当指定专人作为经最高人民法院授权的二级联络窗口联络人。  
高级人民法院负责：指导、监督、组织、协调本辖区人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书和调查取证司法互助业务；就办理海峡两岸送达文书司法互助案件与台湾地区业务主管部门直接联络，并在必要时具体办理送达文书和调查取证司法互助案件；登记、统计本辖区人民法院办理的海峡两岸送达文书司法互助案件；定期向最高人民法院报告本辖区人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书司法互助业务情况；及时将本院联络人的姓名、联络方式及变动情况报告最高人民法院，同时通报台湾地区联络人和下级人民法院。

**Article 5** The Supreme People's Court authorizes the Higher People's Courts to establish secondary windows for contacting the competent authorities in the Taiwan region for handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases involving service of documents. A Higher People's Court shall designate a specific person as the liaison person for the secondary contact window authorized by the Supreme People's Court.  
A Higher People's Court shall be responsible for: guiding, supervising, organizing, and coordinating the handling of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving service of documents and investigation and evidence collection by the People's Courts within its jurisdiction; directly contacting the competent authorities in the Taiwan region for handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases involving service of documents, and, where necessary, specifically handling judicial mutual assistance cases involving service of documents and investigation and evidence collection; registering and compiling statistics on cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases involving service of documents handled by the People's Courts within its jurisdiction; periodically reporting to the Supreme People's Court on the handling of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving service of documents by the People's Courts within its jurisdiction; and promptly reporting the name, contact method, and changes thereof of its own liaison person to the Supreme People's Court, while simultaneously notifying the Taiwan region liaison person and the lower-level People's Courts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  中级人民法院和基层人民法院应当指定专人负责海峡两岸司法互助业务。  
中级人民法院和基层人民法院负责：具体办理海峡两岸送达文书和调查取证司法互助案件；定期向高级人民法院层报本院办理海峡两岸送达文书司法互助业务情况；及时将本院海峡两岸司法互助业务负责人员的姓名、联络方式及变动情况层报高级人民法院。  
三、送达文书司法互助

**Article 6** The Intermediate People's Courts and the Primary People's Courts shall designate specific persons to be responsible for cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters.  
The Intermediate People's Courts and the Primary People's Courts shall be responsible for: specifically handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases involving service of documents and investigation and evidence collection; periodically reporting to the Higher People's Court, level by level, on the handling of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving service of documents by their respective courts; and promptly reporting, level by level, to the Higher People's Court the names, contact methods, and changes thereof of the persons in charge of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters in their respective courts.  
III. Judicial Mutual Assistance in Service of Documents

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  人民法院向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达民事和行政诉讼司法文书，可以采用下列方式：  
（一）受送达人居住在大陆的，直接送达。受送达人是自然人，本人不在的，可以交其同住成年家属签收；受送达人是法人或者其他组织的，应当由法人的法定代表人、其他组织的主要负责人或者该法人、其他组织负责收件的人签收。  
受送达人不在大陆居住，但送达时在大陆的，可以直接送达。  
（二）受送达人在大陆有诉讼代理人的，向诉讼代理人送达。但受送达人在授权委托书中明确表明其诉讼代理人无权代为接收的除外。  
（三）受送达人有指定代收人的，向代收人送达。  
（四）受送达人在大陆有代表机构、分支机构、业务代办人的，向其代表机构或者经受送达人明确授权接受送达的分支机构、业务代办人送达。  
（五）通过协议确定的海峡两岸司法互助方式，请求台湾地区送达。  
（六）受送达人在台湾地区的地址明确的，可以邮寄送达。  
（七）有明确的传真号码、电子信箱地址的，可以通过传真、电子邮件方式向受送达人送达。  
采用上述方式均不能送达或者台湾地区当事人下落不明的，可以公告送达。  
人民法院需要向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达刑事司法文书，可以通过协议确定的海峡两岸司法互助方式，请求台湾地区送达。

**Article 7** When serving judicial documents in civil and administrative actions on a party whose domicile is in the Taiwan region, the People's Courts may use the following methods:  
(1) If the person on whom service is to be made resides in the Mainland, service shall be effected directly. If the person on whom service is to be made is a natural person and is not present, the documents may be delivered to and signed for by an adult family member living with him or her; if the person on whom service is to be made is a legal person or any other organization, the documents shall be signed for by the legal representative of the legal person, the principal responsible person of the other organization, or the person in charge of receiving documents of such legal person or other organization.  
If the person on whom service is to be made does not reside in the Mainland but is in the Mainland at the time of service, service may be effected directly.  
(2) If the person on whom service is to be made has a litigation agent in the Mainland, service may be effected on the litigation agent, unless the person clearly states in the power of attorney that the litigation agent has no authority to receive service on his or her behalf.  
(3) If the person on whom service is to be made has designated a person to receive documents, service may be effected on that designated person.  
(4) If the person on whom service is to be made has a representative office, branch office, or business agent in the Mainland, service may be effected on the representative office, or on the branch office or business agent expressly authorized by the person to receive service.  
(5) Service may be requested to be effected through the Taiwan region by means of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance as determined in the Agreement.  
(6) If the address of the person on whom service is to be made in the Taiwan region is clear, service may be effected by mail.  
(7) If the fax number or e-mail address is clear, service may be effected on the person by fax or e-mail.  
If service cannot be effected by any of the above methods, or the party in the Taiwan region is missing, service may be effected by public announcement.  
If the People's Court needs to serve criminal judicial documents on a party whose domicile is in the Taiwan region, it may request service through the Taiwan region by means of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance as determined in the Agreement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院协助台湾地区法院送达司法文书，应当采用民事诉讼法、刑事诉讼法、行政诉讼法等法律和相关司法解释规定的送达方式，并应当尽可能采用直接送达方式，但不采用公告送达方式。

**Article 8** When assisting courts in the Taiwan region in serving judicial documents, the People's Courts shall adopt the methods of service prescribed by the Civil Procedure Law, the Criminal Procedure Law, the Administrative Procedure Law, and other relevant laws and judicial interpretations, and shall, to the extent possible, use the method of direct service, but shall not use the method of service by public announcement.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院协助台湾地区送达司法文书，应当充分负责，及时努力送达。

**Article 9** When assisting in the service of judicial documents for the Taiwan region, the People's Courts shall be fully responsible and make timely and diligent efforts to effect service.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  审理案件的人民法院需要台湾地区协助送达司法文书的，应当填写《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达文书请求书》附录部分，连同需要送达的司法文书，一式二份，及时送交高级人民法院。  
需要台湾地区协助送达的司法文书中有指定开庭日期等类似期限的，一般应当为协助送达程序预留不少于六个月的时间。

**Article 10** If the People's Court hearing a case needs the Taiwan region to assist in serving judicial documents, it shall fill in the appendix part of the "Request for Service of Documents under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and, together with the judicial documents to be served, submit two copies to the Higher People's Court in a timely manner.  
If the judicial document for which assistance in service is requested from the Taiwan region contains a specified court session date or similar time limit, generally no less than six months shall be reserved for the assistance service procedure.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  高级人民法院收到本院或者下级人民法院《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达文书请求书》附录部分和需要送达的司法文书后，应当在七个工作日内完成审查。经审查认为可以请求台湾地区协助送达的，高级人民法院联络人应当填写《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达文书请求书》正文部分，连同附录部分和需要送达的司法文书，立即寄送台湾地区联络人；经审查认为欠缺相关材料、内容或者认为不需要请求台湾地区协助送达的，应当立即告知提出请求的人民法院补充相关材料、内容或者在说明理由后将材料退回。

**Article 11** Upon receiving the appendix part of the "Request for Service of Documents under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and the judicial documents to be served from its own court or a lower-level People's Court, the Higher People's Court shall complete the review within seven working days. If, upon review, it finds that the Taiwan region may be requested to assist in service, the liaison person of the Higher People's Court shall fill in the main body part of the "Request for Service of Documents under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and, together with the appendix part and the judicial documents to be served, immediately send them to the liaison person in the Taiwan region; if, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking, or that it is unnecessary to request assistance in service from the Taiwan region, it shall immediately notify the requesting People's Court to supplement the relevant materials or content, or return the materials after stating the reasons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  台湾地区成功送达并将送达证明材料寄送高级人民法院联络人，或者未能成功送达并将相关材料送还，同时出具理由说明给高级人民法院联络人的，高级人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内，完成审查并转送提出请求的人民法院。经审查认为欠缺相关材料或者内容的，高级人民法院联络人应当立即与台湾地区联络人联络并请求补充相关材料或者内容。  
自高级人民法院联络人向台湾地区寄送有关司法文书之日起满四个月，如果未能收到送达证明材料或者说明文件，且根据各种情况不足以认定已经送达的，视为不能按照协议确定的海峡两岸司法互助方式送达。

**Article 12** If the Taiwan region successfully effects service and sends the service certificate to the liaison person of the Higher People's Court, or fails to effect service and returns the relevant materials together with a statement of reasons to the liaison person of the Higher People's Court, the Higher People's Court shall, within seven working days of receipt, complete the review and transmit them to the requesting People's Court. If, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking, the liaison person of the Higher People's Court shall immediately contact the liaison person in the Taiwan region and request supplementation of the relevant materials or content.  
If, four months after the liaison person of the Higher People's Court sent the relevant judicial documents to the Taiwan region, no service certificate or explanatory document has been received, and based on all circumstances it is insufficient to conclude that service has been effected, service shall be deemed impossible by means of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance as determined in the Agreement.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  台湾地区请求人民法院协助送达台湾地区法院的司法文书并通过其联络人将请求书和相关司法文书寄送高级人民法院联络人的，高级人民法院应当在七个工作日内完成审查。经审查认为可以协助送达的，应当立即转送有关下级人民法院送达或者由本院送达；经审查认为欠缺相关材料、内容或者认为不宜协助送达的，高级人民法院联络人应当立即向台湾地区联络人说明情况并告知其补充相关材料、内容或者将材料送还。  
具体办理送达文书司法互助案件的人民法院应当在收到高级人民法院转送的材料之日起五个工作日内，以“协助台湾地区送达民事（刑事、行政诉讼）司法文书”案由立案，指定专人办理，并应当自立案之日起十五日内完成协助送达，最迟不得超过两个月。  
收到台湾地区送达文书请求时，司法文书中指定的开庭日期或者其他期限逾期的，人民法院亦应予以送达，同时高级人民法院联络人应当及时向台湾地区联络人说明情况。

**Article 13** If the Taiwan region requests the People's Court to assist in serving judicial documents of a court in the Taiwan region and sends the request letter and relevant judicial documents to the liaison person of the Higher People's Court through its liaison person, the Higher People's Court shall complete the review within seven working days. If, upon review, it finds that assistance in service may be provided, it shall immediately transmit the documents to the relevant lower-level People's Court for service or effect service by itself; if, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking or that assistance in service is not appropriate, the liaison person of the Higher People's Court shall immediately explain the situation to the liaison person in the Taiwan region and inform them to supplement the relevant materials or content, or return the materials.  
The People's Court that specifically handles the judicial mutual assistance case for service of documents shall, within five working days of receiving the materials transmitted by the Higher People's Court, file the case under the cause of action "Assisting the Taiwan Region in Serving Judicial Documents of Civil (Criminal, Administrative) Actions," designate a specific person to handle it, and shall complete the assistance in service within fifteen days from the date of filing the case, which shall in no case exceed two months.  
If, at the time of receiving the request for service of documents from the Taiwan region, the court session date or other time limit specified in the judicial document has already expired, the People's Court shall still effect service, and the liaison person of the Higher People's Court shall promptly explain the situation to the liaison person in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  具体办理送达文书司法互助案件的人民法院成功送达的，应当由送达人在《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达回证》上签名或者盖章，并在成功送达之日起七个工作日内将送达回证送交高级人民法院；未能成功送达的，应当由送达人在《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达回证》上注明未能成功送达的原因并签名或者盖章，在确认不能送达之日起七个工作日内，将该送达回证和未能成功送达的司法文书送交高级人民法院。  
高级人民法院应当在收到前款所述送达回证之日起七个工作日内完成审查，由高级人民法院联络人在前述送达回证上签名或者盖章，同时出具《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉送达文书回复书》，连同该送达回证和未能成功送达的司法文书，立即寄送台湾地区联络人。  
四、调查取证司法互助

**Article 14** If the People's Court that specifically handles the judicial mutual assistance case for service of documents has successfully effected service, the server shall sign or affix his/her seal on the "Acknowledgment of Service under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and, within seven working days from the date of successful service, deliver the acknowledgment of service to the Higher People's Court; if service has not been successfully effected, the server shall indicate on the "Acknowledgment of Service under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" the reasons for the failure to effect service and sign or affix his/her seal, and within seven working days from the date of confirming the impossibility of service, deliver the acknowledgment of service and the judicial documents that were not successfully served to the Higher People's Court.  
The Higher People's Court shall complete the review within seven working days from receipt of the acknowledgment of service referred to in the preceding paragraph, and the liaison person of the Higher People's Court shall sign or affix his/her seal on the said acknowledgment of service, and at the same time issue a "Reply on Service of Documents under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement," which, together with the acknowledgment of service and the judicial documents that were not successfully served, shall be immediately sent to the liaison person in the Taiwan region.  
IV. Judicial Mutual Assistance in Investigation and Evidence Collection

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  人民法院办理海峡两岸调查取证司法互助业务，限于与台湾地区法院相互协助调取与诉讼有关的证据，包括取得证言及陈述；提供书证、物证及视听资料；确定关系人所在地或者确认其身份、前科等情况；进行勘验、检查、扣押、鉴定和查询等。

**Article 15** The handling by the People's Courts of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving investigation and evidence collection shall be limited to mutual assistance with courts in the Taiwan region in obtaining evidence related to litigation, including obtaining testimony and statements; providing documentary evidence, physical evidence, and audio-visual materials; determining the whereabouts of interested persons or confirming their identity, criminal record, and the like; and conducting inspection, examination, seizure, expert evaluation, and inquiry, among others.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  人民法院协助台湾地区法院调查取证，应当采用民事诉讼法、刑事诉讼法、行政诉讼法等法律和相关司法解释规定的方式。  
在不违反法律和相关规定、不损害社会公共利益、不妨碍正在进行的诉讼程序的前提下，人民法院应当尽力协助调查取证，并尽可能依照台湾地区请求的内容和形式予以协助。  
台湾地区调查取证请求书所述的犯罪事实，依照大陆法律规定不认为涉嫌犯罪的，人民法院不予协助，但有重大社会危害并经双方业务主管部门同意予以个案协助的除外。台湾地区请求促使大陆居民至台湾地区作证，但未作出非经大陆主管部门同意不得追诉其进入台湾地区之前任何行为的书面声明的，人民法院可以不予协助。

**Article 16** When assisting courts in the Taiwan region in investigation and evidence collection, the People's Courts shall adopt the methods prescribed by the Civil Procedure Law, the Criminal Procedure Law, the Administrative Procedure Law, and other relevant laws and judicial interpretations.  
Provided that there is no violation of laws and relevant regulations, no damage to social public interests, and no hindrance to ongoing proceedings, the People's Courts shall make every effort to assist in investigation and evidence collection, and shall, to the extent possible, provide assistance in accordance with the content and form requested by the Taiwan region.  
If the criminal facts described in the request for investigation and evidence collection from the Taiwan region are not deemed to be suspected crimes under the laws of the Mainland, the People's Court shall not provide assistance, unless the case involves major social harm and the competent authorities of both sides agree to provide case-specific assistance. If the Taiwan region requests to facilitate a Mainland resident's travel to the Taiwan region to testify, but has not made a written declaration that no prosecution shall be initiated for any act committed before the resident's entry into the Taiwan region without the consent of the competent Mainland authorities, the People's Court may decline to provide assistance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  审理案件的人民法院需要台湾地区协助调查取证的，应当填写《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉调查取证请求书》附录部分，连同相关材料，一式三份，及时送交高级人民法院。  
高级人民法院应当在收到前款所述材料之日起七个工作日内完成初步审查，并将审查意见和《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉调查取证请求书》附录部分及相关材料，一式二份，立即转送最高人民法院。

**Article 17** If the People's Court hearing a case needs the Taiwan region to assist in investigation and evidence collection, it shall fill in the appendix part of the "Request for Investigation and Evidence Collection under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and, together with the relevant materials, submit three copies to the Higher People's Court in a timely manner.  
The Higher People's Court shall, within seven working days from receipt of the materials referred to in the preceding paragraph, complete the preliminary review, and immediately transmit the review opinions and two copies of the appendix part of the "Request for Investigation and Evidence Collection under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and the relevant materials to the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  最高人民法院收到高级人民法院转送的《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉调查取证请求书》附录部分和相关材料以及高级人民法院审查意见后，应当在七个工作日内完成最终审查。经审查认为可以请求台湾地区协助调查取证的，最高人民法院联络人应当填写《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉调查取证请求书》正文部分，连同附录部分和相关材料，立即寄送台湾地区联络人；经审查认为欠缺相关材料、内容或者认为不需要请求台湾地区协助调查取证的，应当立即通过高级人民法院告知提出请求的人民法院补充相关材料、内容或者在说明理由后将材料退回。

**Article 18** Upon receiving the appendix part of the "Request for Investigation and Evidence Collection under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and the relevant materials transmitted by the Higher People's Court, together with the review opinions of the Higher People's Court, the Supreme People's Court shall complete the final review within seven working days. If, upon review, it finds that the Taiwan region may be requested to assist in investigation and evidence collection, the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court shall fill in the main body part of the "Request for Investigation and Evidence Collection under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement" and, together with the appendix part and the relevant materials, immediately send them to the liaison person in the Taiwan region; if, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking, or that it is unnecessary to request assistance in investigation and evidence collection from the Taiwan region, it shall immediately, through the Higher People's Court, notify the requesting People's Court to supplement the relevant materials or content, or return the materials after stating the reasons.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  台湾地区成功调查取证并将取得的证据材料寄送最高人民法院联络人，或者未能成功调查取证并将相关材料送还，同时出具理由说明给最高人民法院联络人的，最高人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内完成审查并转送高级人民法院，高级人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内转送提出请求的人民法院。经审查认为欠缺相关材料或者内容的，最高人民法院联络人应当立即与台湾地区联络人联络并请求补充相关材料或者内容。

**Article 19** If the Taiwan region successfully conducts investigation and evidence collection and sends the obtained evidence materials to the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court, or fails to do so and returns the relevant materials together with a statement of reasons to the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Court shall, within seven working days of receipt, complete the review and transmit them to the Higher People's Court, and the Higher People's Court shall, within seven working days of receipt, transmit them to the requesting People's Court. If, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking, the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court shall immediately contact the liaison person in the Taiwan region and request supplementation of the relevant materials or content.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  台湾地区请求人民法院协助台湾地区法院调查取证并通过其联络人将请求书和相关材料寄送最高人民法院联络人的，最高人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内完成审查。经审查认为可以协助调查取证的，应当立即转送有关高级人民法院或者由本院办理，高级人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内转送有关下级人民法院办理或者由本院办理；经审查认为欠缺相关材料、内容或者认为不宜协助调查取证的，最高人民法院联络人应当立即向台湾地区联络人说明情况并告知其补充相关材料、内容或者将材料送还。  
具体办理调查取证司法互助案件的人民法院应当在收到高级人民法院转送的材料之日起五个工作日内，以“协助台湾地区民事（刑事、行政诉讼）调查取证”案由立案，指定专人办理，并应当自立案之日起一个月内完成协助调查取证，最迟不得超过三个月。因故不能在期限届满前完成的，应当提前函告高级人民法院，并由高级人民法院转报最高人民法院。

**Article 20** If the Taiwan region requests the People's Court to assist a court in the Taiwan region in investigation and evidence collection and sends the request letter and relevant materials to the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court through its liaison person, the Supreme People's Court shall complete the review within seven working days of receipt. If, upon review, it finds that assistance in investigation and evidence collection may be provided, it shall immediately transmit the materials to the relevant Higher People's Court or handle the matter by itself; the Higher People's Court shall, within seven working days of receipt, transmit the materials to the relevant lower-level People's Court for handling or handle the matter by itself; if, upon review, it finds that relevant materials or content are lacking or that assistance in investigation and evidence collection is not appropriate, the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court shall immediately explain the situation to the liaison person in the Taiwan region and inform them to supplement the relevant materials or content, or return the materials.  
The People's Court that specifically handles the judicial mutual assistance case for investigation and evidence collection shall, within five working days of receiving the materials transmitted by the Higher People's Court, file the case under the cause of action "Assisting the Taiwan Region in Investigation and Evidence Collection of Civil (Criminal, Administrative) Actions," designate a specific person to handle it, and shall complete the assistance in investigation and evidence collection within one month from the date of filing the case, which shall in no case exceed three months. If for any reason it cannot be completed before the deadline expires, the court shall notify the Higher People's Court in writing in advance, and the Higher People's Court shall report to the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  具体办理调查取证司法互助案件的人民法院成功调查取证的，应当在完成调查取证之日起七个工作日内将取得的证据材料一式三份，连同台湾地区提供的材料，并在必要时附具情况说明，送交高级人民法院；未能成功调查取证的，应当出具说明函一式三份，连同台湾地区提供的材料，在确认不能成功调查取证之日起七个工作日内送交高级人民法院。  
高级人民法院应当在收到前款所述材料之日起七个工作日内完成初步审查，并将审查意见和前述取得的证据材料或者说明函等，一式二份，连同台湾地区提供的材料，立即转送最高人民法院。  
最高人民法院应当在收到之日起七个工作日内完成最终审查，由最高人民法院联络人出具《〈海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议〉调查取证回复书》，必要时连同相关材料，立即寄送台湾地区联络人。  
证据材料不适宜复制或者难以取得备份的，可不按本条第一款和第二款的规定提供备份材料。  
五、附  则

**Article 21** If the People's Court that specifically handles the judicial mutual assistance case for investigation and evidence collection has successfully conducted the investigation and evidence collection, it shall, within seven working days from the date of completion, deliver three copies of the obtained evidence materials, together with the materials provided by the Taiwan region and, where necessary, an explanatory note, to the Higher People's Court; if the investigation and evidence collection has not been successfully conducted, it shall issue an explanatory letter in three copies, together with the materials provided by the Taiwan region, and deliver them to the Higher People's Court within seven working days from the date of confirming the impossibility of successful investigation and evidence collection.  
The Higher People's Court shall, within seven working days from receipt of the materials referred to in the preceding paragraph, complete the preliminary review, and immediately transmit two copies of the review opinions and the said obtained evidence materials or explanatory letter, together with the materials provided by the Taiwan region, to the Supreme People's Court.  
The Supreme People's Court shall, within seven working days from receipt, complete the final review, and the liaison person of the Supreme People's Court shall issue a "Reply on Investigation and Evidence Collection under the Cross-Strait Joint Crime Prevention and Judicial Mutual Assistance Agreement," which, together with the relevant materials where necessary, shall be immediately sent to the liaison person in the Taiwan region.  
If the evidence materials are unsuitable for reproduction or it is difficult to obtain copies, the provision of copies as required by paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of this Article may be dispensed with.  
V. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  人民法院对于台湾地区请求协助所提供的和执行请求所取得的相关资料应当予以保密。但依据请求目的使用的除外。

**Article 22** The People's Courts shall keep confidential the relevant materials provided by the Taiwan region for the purpose of assistance and those obtained in the execution of the request, unless they are used in accordance with the purpose of the request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  人民法院应当依据请求书载明的目的使用台湾地区协助提供的资料。但最高人民法院和台湾地区业务主管部门另有商定的除外。

**Article 23** The People's Courts shall use the materials provided with the assistance of the Taiwan region in accordance with the purpose stated in the request letter, unless otherwise agreed between the Supreme People's Court and the competent authorities in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  对于依照协议和本规定从台湾地区获得的证据和司法文书等材料，不需要办理公证、认证等形式证明。

**Article 24** For evidence, judicial documents, and other materials obtained from the Taiwan region in accordance with the Agreement and these Provisions, no notarization, authentication, or other forms of certification shall be required.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  人民法院办理海峡两岸司法互助业务，应当使用统一、规范的文书样式。

**Article 25** When handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters, the People's Courts shall use uniform and standardized document formats.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  对于执行台湾地区的请求所发生的费用，由有关人民法院负担。但下列费用应当由台湾地区业务主管部门负责支付：  
（一）鉴定费用；  
（二）翻译费用和誊写费用；  
（三）为台湾地区提供协助的证人和鉴定人，因前往、停留、离开台湾地区所发生的费用；  
（四）其他经最高人民法院和台湾地区业务主管部门商定的费用。

**Article 26** The expenses incurred in executing a request from the Taiwan region shall be borne by the relevant People's Court. However, the following expenses shall be paid by the competent authorities in the Taiwan region:  
(1) costs of expert evaluation;  
(2) translation costs and copying costs;  
(3) expenses incurred by witnesses and expert evaluators who provide assistance to the Taiwan region for traveling to, staying in, and leaving the Taiwan region; and  
(4) other expenses agreed upon between the Supreme People's Court and the competent authorities in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  人民法院在办理海峡两岸司法互助案件中收到、取得、制作的各种文件和材料，应当以原件或者复制件形式，作为诉讼档案保存。

**Article 27** All documents and materials received, obtained, or produced by the People's Courts in handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases shall be preserved in the form of originals or copies as litigation archives.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  最高人民法院审理的案件需要请求台湾地区协助送达司法文书和调查取证的，参照本规定由本院自行办理。  
专门人民法院办理海峡两岸送达文书和调查取证司法互助业务，参照本规定执行。

**Article 28** If a case being tried by the Supreme People's Court requires a request to the Taiwan region for assistance in serving judicial documents or investigation and evidence collection, the Supreme People's Court shall handle it by itself with reference to these Provisions.  
Specialized People's Courts handling cross-strait judicial mutual assistance matters involving service of documents and investigation and evidence collection shall implement these Provisions by reference.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  办理海峡两岸司法互助案件和执行本规定的情况，应当纳入对有关人民法院及相关工作人员的工作绩效考核和案件质量评查范围。

**Article 29** The handling of cross-strait judicial mutual assistance cases and the implementation of these Provisions shall be incorporated into the scope of performance assessment and case quality evaluation for the relevant People's Courts and their relevant staff.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  此前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 30** Where any previously issued judicial interpretation is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
