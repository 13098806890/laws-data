### 最高人民法院关于对一方当事人下落不明未满两年的离婚案件是否受理和公告送达问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于对一方当事人下落不明未满两年的离婚案件  
  
是否受理和公告送达问题的批复  
  
（1989）法民字第20号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院辽法（88）民请字第 1号请示收悉，以研究，现答复如下：  
  
对于夫妻一方下落不明，另一方诉至法院，只要求离婚，不申请宣告下落不明人死亡的案件，不论下落不明人出走时间的长短，法院均应受理，并应依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》规定的普通程序进行审理；法律文书的送达依照民事诉讼法（试行）第七十五条的规定进行。  
  
1989年8月22日

**Article 1** Reply on Whether to Accept and Serve by Publication a Divorce Case Where One Party's Whereabouts Are Unknown for Less than Two Years  
(1989) Fa Min Zi No. 20  
Liaoning Higher People's Court:  
Your request for guidance, Liao Fa (88) Min Qing Zi No. 1, has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
For a case in which one spouse's whereabouts are unknown and the other spouse files a lawsuit with the court seeking only a divorce without applying for a declaration of death of the missing person, the court shall accept the case regardless of the duration of the missing person's absence, and shall conduct the trial in accordance with the ordinary procedures prescribed by the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation); service of legal documents shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 75 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation).  
August 22, 1989
