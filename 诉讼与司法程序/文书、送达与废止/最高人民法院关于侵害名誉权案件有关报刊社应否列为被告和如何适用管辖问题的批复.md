**分类**：司法解释

### 最高人民法院关于侵害名誉权案件有关报刊社应否列为被告和如何适用管辖问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于侵害名誉权案件有关报刊社  
  
应否列为被告和如何适用管辖问题的批复  
  
法（民）复〔 1988〕11号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于侵害名誉权案件有关报刊社应否列为被告和如何适用管辖等问题的请示报告》收悉。  
  
经我们研究认为：报刊社对要发表的稿件，应负责审查核实。发表后侵害了公民的名誉权，作者和报刊社都有责任，可将报刊社与作者列为共同被告。  
  
关于这类案件的管辖问题，可分别情况处理：如果原告只对作者起诉的，由作者户籍所在地的基层人民法院管辖，受诉法院可追加报刊社为被告；如果原告只对报刊社起诉的，由该报刊社所在地的基层人民法院管辖，受诉法院可追加作者为被告；如果原告把作者和报刊社作为共同被告起诉的，一般由报刊社所在地的基层人民法院管辖为宜。  
  
此复。  
  
1988年 1月15日

**Article 1** Reply concerning Whether a Newspaper Office  
Shall Be Listed as a Defendant and How to Apply Jurisdiction in Defamation Cases  
Fa (Min) Fu [1988] No. 11  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
We have received your 'Request for Instructions concerning Whether a Newspaper Office Shall Be Listed as a Defendant and How to Apply Jurisdiction in Defamation Cases'.  
Upon our study, we are of the opinion that: A newspaper office shall be responsible for reviewing and verifying the manuscripts to be published. If after publication the reputation rights of a citizen are infringed, both the author and the newspaper office bear liability, and they may be listed as co-defendants.  
Regarding the jurisdiction of such cases, they may be handled according to the following circumstances: If the plaintiff only prosecutes the author, the Primary People's Court of the author's domicile shall have jurisdiction, and the court hearing the case may add the newspaper office as a defendant; if the plaintiff only prosecutes the newspaper office, the Primary People's Court of the newspaper office's location shall have jurisdiction, and the court hearing the case may add the author as a defendant; if the plaintiff prosecutes both the author and the newspaper office as co-defendants, the Primary People's Court of the newspaper office's location shall have jurisdiction as a general rule.  
This is our reply.  
January 15, 1988
