**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1987-03-31

### 最高人民法院关于地方各级人民法院不应制定司法解释性文件的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于地方各级人民法院  
  
不应制定司法解释性文件的批复  
  
（1987）民他字第10号  
  
广西壮族自治区高级人民法院：  
  
你院报来和刊载于《审判工作探索》上的“关于处理房屋、宅基地案件贯彻执行有关政策法律若干问题的意见”均已收到。  
  
经研究，我们认为：你院下发的上述具有司法解释性的文件，地方各级法院均不应制定。对审判实践中遇到一些具体问题，建议你们在调查研究的基础上，可写一些经验总结性的文章，供审判人员办案时参考，或者召开一定范围的会议，总结交流经验。另外，“意见”中有一些条款的规定明显与现行政策法律相悖。如在“改建或重建他人房屋之产权问题”中规定的：“……无约定的，可确认为房主与改建、重建者共有”,“未经房主同意，擅自将房屋进行重大改建或重建，房主明知而不提出异议，或者改建、重建后房主知道，在一年内不提出异议的，改建或重建后的产权，可确认为房主与改建、重建者共有”等等，均属不当。  
  
此复  
  
1987年3月31日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on People's Courts at All Levels  
Shall Not Formulate Judicial Interpretation Documents  
(1987) Min Ta Zi No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region:  
We have received both the document submitted by your court and the “Suggestions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of Relevant Policies and Laws in Handling Cases of Houses and Homesteads” published in “Adjudication Work Exploration”.  
Upon study, we believe that local people's courts at all levels shall not formulate the above-mentioned judicial interpretation documents issued by your court. For specific issues encountered in adjudication practice, it is suggested that, on the basis of investigation and research, you may write some experience-summarizing articles for reference by adjudicators when handling cases, or hold meetings within a certain scope to summarize and exchange experiences. In addition, some provisions in the “Suggestions” are clearly inconsistent with current policies and laws. For example, in the section “On the Property Right Issues in Altering or Rebuilding Others’ Houses,” it is provided: “…where there is no agreement, it may be confirmed as co-ownership between the house owner and the person who altered or rebuilt the house”; “Where a house owner, without consent, arbitrarily carries out major alterations or rebuilding of the house, and the house owner, knowing this, raises no objection, or where the house owner, after knowing of the alteration or rebuilding, fails to raise any objection within one year, the property right after alteration or rebuilding may be confirmed as co-ownership between the house owner and the person who altered or rebuilt the house,” etc., all of which are inappropriate.  
This is the reply.  
March 31, 1987
