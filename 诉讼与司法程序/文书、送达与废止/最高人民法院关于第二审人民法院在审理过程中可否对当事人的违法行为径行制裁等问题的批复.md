# 最高人民法院关于第二审人民法院在审理过程中可否对当事人的违法行为径行制裁等问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法经〔1990〕45号  
**公布日期**：1990-07-25

---

### 正文

一、第二审人民法院在审理案件过程中，认为当事人有违法行为应予依法制裁而原审人民法院未予制裁的，可以径行予以民事制裁。

**Article None** 1. If the People's Court of second instance, during the trial of a case, finds that a party has committed an illegal act which shall be sanctioned according to law but the People's Court of first instance did not impose such sanction, it may directly impose civil sanctions.

二、当事人不服人民法院民事制裁决定而向上一级人民法院申请复议的，该上级人民法院无论维持、变更或者撤销原决定，均应制作民事制裁决定书。

**Article None** 2. If a party refuses to accept a civil sanction decision of the People's Court and applies for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level, such higher People's Court shall, whether it upholds, modifies or revokes the original decision, issue a written civil sanction decision.

三、人民法院复议期间，被制裁人请求撤回复议申请的，经过审查，应当采取通知的形式，准予撤回申请或者驳回其请求。  
1990年7月25日

**Article None** 3. During the period of reconsideration by the People's Court, if the sanctioned person requests to withdraw the reconsideration application, after review, the People's Court shall, by means of a notice, grant the withdrawal of the application or reject the request.  
July 25, 1990
