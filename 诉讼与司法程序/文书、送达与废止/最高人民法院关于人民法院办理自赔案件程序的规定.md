# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理自赔案件程序的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕19号  
**公布日期**：2013-04-01  
**生效日期**：2013-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理自赔案件程序的规定》已于2013年4月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1573次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年9月1日起施行。 2013年7月26日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理自赔案件程序的规定 法释〔2013〕19号 根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》，结合人民法院国家赔偿工作实际，对人民法院办理自赔案件的程序作如下规定： 第一条   本规定所称自赔案件，是指人民法院办理的本院作为赔偿义务机关的国家赔偿案件。 第二条   基层人民法院国家赔偿小组、中级以上人民法院赔偿委员会负责办理本院的自赔案件。 第三条   人民法院对赔偿请求人提出的赔偿申请，根据《最高人民法院关于国家赔偿案件立案工作的规定》予以审查立案。 第四条   人民法院办理自赔案件，应当指定一名审判员承办。 负责承办的审判员应当查清事实并提出处理意见，经国家赔偿小组或者赔偿委员会讨论后，报请院长决定。重大、疑难案件由院长提交院长办公会议讨论决定。 第五条   参与办理自赔案件的审判人员是赔偿请求人或其代理人的近亲属，与本案有利害关系，或者有其他关系，可能影响案件公正办理的，应当主动回避。 赔偿请求人认为参与办理自赔案件的审判人员有前款规定情形的，有权以书面或者口头方式申请其回避。 以上规定，适用于书记员、翻译人员、鉴定人、勘验人。 第六条   赔偿请求人申请回避，应当在人民法院作出赔偿决定前提出。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   本规定所称自赔案件，是指人民法院办理的本院作为赔偿义务机关的国家赔偿案件。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, the term “self-handled compensation cases” refers to state compensation cases handled by a People’s Court in which it serves as the compensatory authority.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   基层人民法院国家赔偿小组、中级以上人民法院赔偿委员会负责办理本院的自赔案件。

**Article 2** The state compensation panel of a Primary People’s Court and the compensation committee of an Intermediate People’s Court or higher shall be responsible for handling the self-handled compensation cases of the said People’s Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   人民法院对赔偿请求人提出的赔偿申请，根据《最高人民法院关于国家赔偿案件立案工作的规定》予以审查立案。

**Article 3** A People’s Court shall examine and accept a compensation application submitted by a compensation claimant in accordance with the Provisions of the Supreme People’s Court on Case-filing for State Compensation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   人民法院办理自赔案件，应当指定一名审判员承办。  
负责承办的审判员应当查清事实并提出处理意见，经国家赔偿小组或者赔偿委员会讨论后，报请院长决定。重大、疑难案件由院长提交院长办公会议讨论决定。

**Article 4** When handling a self-handled compensation case, a People’s Court shall designate a judge to undertake the case.  
The judge responsible for the case shall ascertain the facts and provide a proposed decision, which shall be submitted to the president for approval after discussion by the state compensation panel or the compensation committee. Major or difficult cases shall be submitted by the president to the president’s executive meeting for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   参与办理自赔案件的审判人员是赔偿请求人或其代理人的近亲属，与本案有利害关系，或者有其他关系，可能影响案件公正办理的，应当主动回避。  
赔偿请求人认为参与办理自赔案件的审判人员有前款规定情形的，有权以书面或者口头方式申请其回避。  
以上规定，适用于书记员、翻译人员、鉴定人、勘验人。

**Article 5** Any judicial personnel participating in the handling of a self-handled compensation case who is a close relative of the compensation claimant or the claimant’s representative, has an interest in the case, or has any other relationship that may affect the impartial handling of the case shall voluntarily withdraw.  
Where the compensation claimant believes that judicial personnel participating in the handling of the self-handled compensation case fall under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the claimant shall have the right to apply for their withdrawal in writing or orally.  
The above provisions shall apply to court clerks, interpreters, expert evaluators, and inspectors.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   赔偿请求人申请回避，应当在人民法院作出赔偿决定前提出。  
人民法院应当自赔偿请求人申请回避之日起三日内作出书面决定。赔偿请求人对决定不服的，可以申请复议一次。人民法院对复议申请，应当在三日内做出复议决定，并通知复议申请人。复议期间，被申请回避的人员不停止案件办理工作。  
审判人员的回避，由院长决定；其他人员的回避，由国家赔偿小组负责人或者赔偿委员会主任决定。

**Article 6** An application for withdrawal by a compensation claimant shall be made before the People’s Court renders a compensation decision.  
The People’s Court shall make a written decision within three days from the date of the compensation claimant’s application for withdrawal. If the compensation claimant disagrees with the decision, the claimant may apply for one review. The People’s Court shall make a review decision within three days of receiving the review application and notify the review applicant. During the review period, the person subject to the withdrawal application shall not cease case handling.  
The withdrawal of a judicial person shall be decided by the president; the withdrawal of other personnel shall be decided by the head of the state compensation panel or the chairperson of the compensation committee.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   人民法院应当全面审查案件，充分听取赔偿请求人的意见。必要时可以调取原审判、执行案卷，可以向原案件承办部门或有关人员调查、核实情况。听取意见、调查核实情况，应当制作笔录。  
案件争议较大，或者案情疑难、复杂的，人民法院可以组织赔偿请求人、原案件承办人以及其他相关人员举行听证。听证情况应当制作笔录。

**Article 7** A People’s Court shall conduct a comprehensive review of the case and fully hear the opinions of the compensation claimant. Where necessary, the court may retrieve the original case files of trial or enforcement and may investigate or verify the circumstances with the original case-handling department or relevant personnel. Records shall be made of hearing opinions, investigations, and verifications.  
Where the case involves substantial disputes or is complicated or difficult, the People’s Court may organize a hearing with the compensation claimant, the original case-handler, and other relevant persons. Records shall be made of the hearing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   人民法院可以与赔偿请求人就赔偿方式、赔偿项目和赔偿数额在法律规定的范围内进行协商。协商应当遵循自愿、合法的原则。协商情况应当制作笔录。  
经协商达成协议的，人民法院应当制作国家赔偿决定书。协商不成的，人民法院应当依法及时作出决定。

**Article 8** A People’s Court may negotiate with the compensation claimant on the method, items, and amount of compensation within the scope provided by law. Negotiation shall follow the principles of voluntariness and legality. Records shall be made of the negotiation.  
If an agreement is reached through negotiation, the People’s Court shall issue a state compensation decision. If negotiation fails, the People’s Court shall make a decision promptly according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   人民法院作出决定前，赔偿请求人撤回赔偿申请的，人民法院应当准许。  
赔偿请求人撤回赔偿申请后，在[国家赔偿法第三十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-39)规定的时效内又申请赔偿，并有证据证明其撤回申请确属违背真实意思表示或者有其他正当理由的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 9** Where a compensation claimant withdraws the compensation application before the People’s Court renders a decision, the People’s Court shall permit the withdrawal.  
Where the compensation claimant, after withdrawing the compensation application, applies again for compensation within the limitation period prescribed in Article 39 of the State Compensation Law and provides evidence proving that the withdrawal was indeed contrary to the claimant’s true intention or that there are other justifiable reasons, the People’s Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当决定中止办理：  
（一）作为赔偿请求人的公民死亡，需要等待其继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属表明是否参加赔偿案件处理的；  
（二）作为赔偿请求人的公民丧失行为能力，尚未确定法定代理人的；  
（三）作为赔偿请求人的法人或者其他组织终止，尚未确定权利承受人的；  
（四）赔偿请求人因不可抗力或者其他障碍，在法定期限内不能参加赔偿案件处理的；  
（五）宣告无罪的案件，人民法院决定再审或者人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的。  
中止办理的原因消除后，人民法院应当及时恢复办理，并通知赔偿请求人。

**Article 10** In any of the following circumstances, a People’s Court shall decide to suspend the handling:  
(1) Where the citizen who is the compensation claimant dies and it is necessary to wait for the heirs and other dependents to indicate whether they wish to participate in the handling of the compensation case;  
(2) Where the citizen who is the compensation claimant loses capacity and no legal representative has been determined;  
(3) Where the legal person or other organization that is the compensation claimant terminates and no rights successor has been determined;  
(4) Where the compensation claimant cannot participate in the handling of the compensation case within the statutory period due to force majeure or other obstacles;  
(5) Where the People’s Court decides to retry a case in which a verdict of not guilty was entered, or the People’s Procuratorate lodges a protest in accordance with the trial supervision procedure.  
After the cause of suspension is eliminated, the People’s Court shall promptly resume the handling and notify the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当决定终结办理：  
（一）作为赔偿请求人的公民死亡，没有继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属，或者其继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属放弃要求赔偿权利的；  
（二）作为赔偿请求人的法人或者其他组织终止后，其权利承受人放弃要求赔偿权利的；  
（三）赔偿请求人据以申请赔偿的撤销案件决定、不起诉决定或者宣告无罪的判决被撤销的。

**Article 11** In any of the following circumstances, a People’s Court shall decide to terminate the handling:  
(1) Where the citizen who is the compensation claimant dies and there is no heir or other dependent, or the heir or other dependent waives the right to claim compensation;  
(2) Where the legal person or other organization that is the compensation claimant terminates and its rights successor waives the right to claim compensation;  
(3) Where the decision to dismiss a case, the decision not to prosecute, or the verdict of not guilty on which the compensation claimant relied for the application for compensation is revoked.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   人民法院应当自收到赔偿申请之日起两个月内作出是否赔偿的决定,并制作国家赔偿决定书。  
申请人向人民法院申请委托鉴定、评估的，鉴定、评估期间不计入办理期限。

**Article 12** A People’s Court shall, within two months from the date of receiving the compensation application, make a decision on whether to grant compensation and issue a state compensation decision.  
Where the applicant applies to the People’s Court for entrusting an expert evaluation or appraisal, the period for such evaluation or appraisal shall not be counted in the handling period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   国家赔偿决定书应当载明以下事项：  
（一）赔偿请求人的基本情况；  
（二）申请事项及理由；  
（三）决定的事实理由及法律依据；  
（四）决定内容；  
（五）申请上一级人民法院赔偿委员会作出赔偿决定的期间和上一级人民法院名称。

**Article 13** A state compensation decision shall specify the following:  
(1) Basic information of the compensation claimant;  
(2) Application matters and reasons;  
(3) Facts, reasons, and legal basis for the decision;  
(4) Decision content;  
(5) The period for applying to the compensation committee of the higher People’s Court for a compensation decision and the name of the higher People’s Court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   人民法院决定赔偿或不予赔偿的，应当自作出决定之日起十日内将国家赔偿决定书送达赔偿请求人。

**Article 14** Where a People’s Court decides to grant or deny compensation, it shall serve the state compensation decision on the compensation claimant within ten days from the date of the decision.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   赔偿请求人依据[国家赔偿法第三十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-37)第二款的规定向人民法院申请支付赔偿金的，应当递交申请书，并提交以下材料：  
（一）赔偿请求人的身份证明；  
（二）生效的国家赔偿决定书。  
赔偿请求人当面递交申请支付材料的，人民法院应当出具收讫凭证。赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以口头申请，人民法院应当记入笔录，由赔偿请求人签名、捺印或者盖章。

**Article 15** Where a compensation claimant applies to a People’s Court for payment of compensation in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 37 of the State Compensation Law, the claimant shall submit a written application and the following materials:  
(1) Identity certificate of the compensation claimant;  
(2) Effective state compensation decision.  
Where the compensation claimant submits the application for payment in person, the People’s Court shall issue a receipt. If the compensation claimant has difficulty in writing the application, the claimant may make an oral application, and the People’s Court shall record it in the record, which shall be signed, fingerprinted, or sealed by the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   申请支付材料真实、有效、完整的，人民法院应当受理，并书面通知赔偿请求人。人民法院受理后，应当自收到支付申请之日起七日内，依照预算管理权限向有关财政部门提出支付申请。  
申请支付材料不完整的，人民法院应当当场或者在三个工作日内一次性告知赔偿请求人需要补正的全部材料。收到支付申请的时间自人民法院收到补正材料之日起计算。  
申请支付材料虚假、无效，人民法院决定不予受理的，应当在三个工作日内书面通知赔偿请求人并说明理由。

**Article 16** Where the application materials for payment are truthful, valid, and complete, the People’s Court shall accept the application and notify the compensation claimant in writing. After acceptance, the People’s Court shall, within seven days from the date of receiving the payment application, submit a payment application to the relevant financial department in accordance with budget management authority.  
Where the application materials for payment are incomplete, the People’s Court shall, on the spot or within three working days, inform the compensation claimant of all materials to be supplemented in a single notice. The date of receipt of the payment application shall be calculated from the date on which the People’s Court receives the supplemented materials.  
Where the application materials for payment are false or invalid and the People’s Court decides not to accept the application, it shall notify the compensation claimant in writing with reasons within three working days.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   赔偿请求人对人民法院不予受理申请支付的通知有异议的，可以自收到通知之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复核。上一级人民法院应当自收到复核申请之日起五个工作日内作出复核决定，并在作出复核决定之日起三个工作日内送达赔偿请求人。

**Article 17** Where a compensation claimant raises an objection to the notice of the People’s Court on not accepting the application for payment, the claimant may apply to the higher People’s Court for review within ten days from the date of receiving the notice. The higher People’s Court shall make a review decision within five working days from the date of receiving the review application and serve the decision on the compensation claimant within three working days from the date of making the review decision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条   财政部门告知人民法院申请支付材料不符合要求的，人民法院应当自接到通知之日起五个工作日内按照要求提交补正材料。  
需要赔偿请求人补正材料的，人民法院应当及时通知赔偿请求人。

**Article 18** Where the financial department informs the People’s Court that the application materials for payment do not meet the requirements, the People’s Court shall, within five working days from the date of receiving the notice, submit the supplemented materials as required.  
Where materials need to be supplemented by the compensation claimant, the People’s Court shall promptly notify the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条   财政部门告知人民法院已支付国家赔偿费用的，人民法院应当及时通知赔偿请求人。

**Article 19** Where the financial department informs the People’s Court that the state compensation expenses have been paid, the People’s Court shall promptly notify the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条   本规定自2013年9月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前本院发布的司法解释，与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force on September 1, 2013.  
Where any judicial interpretation issued by the Supreme People’s Court before the entry into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
