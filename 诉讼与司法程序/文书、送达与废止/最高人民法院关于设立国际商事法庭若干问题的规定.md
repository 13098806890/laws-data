# 最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕11号  
**公布日期**：2018-06-27  
**生效日期**：2018-07-01

> 《最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定》已于2018年6月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1743次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年7月1日起施行。 2018年6月27日 最高人民法院 关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕11号 （2018年6月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1743次会议通过，自2018年7月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　最高人民法院设立国际商事法庭。国际商事法庭是最高人民法院的常设审判机构。

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court establishes an International Commercial Court. The International Commercial Court is a permanent adjudication institution of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国际商事法庭受理下列案件：  
（一）当事人依照[民事诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-34)的规定协议选择最高人民法院管辖且标的额为人民币3亿元以上的第一审国际商事案件；  
（二）高级人民法院对其所管辖的第一审国际商事案件，认为需要由最高人民法院审理并获准许的；  
（三）在全国有重大影响的第一审国际商事案件；  
（四）依照[本规定第十四条](#art-14)申请仲裁保全、申请撤销或者执行国际商事仲裁裁决的；  
（五）最高人民法院认为应当由国际商事法庭审理的其他国际商事案件。

**Article 2** The International Commercial Court shall accept the following cases:  
(1) First-instance international commercial cases where the parties have agreed by agreement to submit to the jurisdiction of the Supreme People's Court in accordance with Article 34 of the Civil Procedure Law and the value of the subject matter is not less than RMB 300 million;  
(2) First-instance international commercial cases under the jurisdiction of a higher people's court which the higher people's court considers need to be adjudicated by the Supreme People's Court and permission has been granted;  
(3) First-instance international commercial cases that have a major nationwide impact;  
(4) Applications for arbitration preservation, applications for setting aside or enforcing international commercial arbitration awards made in accordance with Article 14 of these Provisions;  
(5) Other international commercial cases that the Supreme People's Court considers shall be adjudicated by the International Commercial Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　具有下列情形之一的商事案件，可以认定为本规定所称的国际商事案件：  
（一）当事人一方或者双方是外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织的；  
（二）当事人一方或者双方的经常居所地在中华人民共和国领域外的；  
（三）标的物在中华人民共和国领域外的；  
（四）产生、变更或者消灭商事关系的法律事实发生在中华人民共和国领域外的。

**Article 3** Commercial cases that have any of the following circumstances may be identified as international commercial cases as referred to in these Provisions:  
(1) One or both parties are foreigners, stateless persons, foreign enterprises or organizations;  
(2) The habitual residence of one or both parties is outside the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) The subject matter is located outside the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) The legal facts that create, change or extinguish a commercial relationship occur outside the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国际商事法庭法官由最高人民法院在具有丰富审判工作经验，熟悉国际条约、国际惯例以及国际贸易投资实务，能够同时熟练运用中文和英文作为工作语言的资深法官中选任。

**Article 4** The judges of the International Commercial Court shall be selected by the Supreme People's Court from among senior judges who have rich trial work experience, are familiar with international treaties, international practices and international trade and investment practice, and can proficiently use both Chinese and English as working languages.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国际商事法庭审理案件，由三名或者三名以上法官组成合议庭。  
合议庭评议案件，实行少数服从多数的原则。少数意见可以在裁判文书中载明。

**Article 5** The International Commercial Court shall adjudicate cases by a collegial panel composed of three or more judges.  
When the collegial panel deliberates on a case, the principle of the minority shall be subordinate to the majority. The dissenting opinion may be recorded in the judgment document.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国际商事法庭作出的保全裁定，可以指定下级人民法院执行。

**Article 6** Preservation rulings made by the International Commercial Court may designate a lower people's court for enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国际商事法庭审理案件，依照《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法》的规定确定争议适用的实体法律。  
当事人依照法律规定选择适用法律的，应当适用当事人选择的法律。

**Article 7** When adjudicating a case, the International Commercial Court shall determine the substantive law applicable to the dispute in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations.  
If the parties choose to apply a law in accordance with legal provisions, the law chosen by the parties shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国际商事法庭审理案件应当适用域外法律时，可以通过下列途径查明：  
（一）由当事人提供；  
（二）由中外法律专家提供；  
（三）由法律查明服务机构提供；  
（四）由国际商事专家委员提供；  
（五）由与我国订立司法协助协定的缔约对方的中央机关提供；  
（六）由我国驻该国使领馆提供；  
（七）由该国驻我国使馆提供；  
（八）其他合理途径。  
通过上述途径提供的域外法律资料以及专家意见，应当依照法律规定在法庭上出示，并充分听取各方当事人的意见。

**Article 8** When the International Commercial Court needs to apply foreign law in adjudicating a case, it may ascertain the foreign law through the following channels:  
(1) Provided by the parties;  
(2) Provided by Chinese or foreign legal experts;  
(3) Provided by legal ascertainment service institutions;  
(4) Provided by members of the International Commercial Expert Committee;  
(5) Provided by the central authority of the other contracting party that has concluded a judicial assistance agreement with China;  
(6) Provided by Chinese embassies or consulates in that country;  
(7) Provided by the embassy of that country in China;  
(8) Other reasonable channels.  
Foreign legal materials and expert opinions provided through the above channels shall be presented in court in accordance with legal provisions and the opinions of all parties shall be fully heard.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人向国际商事法庭提交的证据材料系在中华人民共和国领域外形成的，不论是否已办理公证、认证或者其他证明手续，均应当在法庭上质证。  
当事人提交的证据材料系英文且经对方当事人同意的，可以不提交中文翻译件。

**Article 9** Where evidence materials submitted by a party to the International Commercial Court are formed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, regardless of whether notarization, legalization or other certification procedures have been completed, they shall be subject to cross-examination in court.  
If the evidence materials submitted by a party are in English and the other party agrees, Chinese translations may not be submitted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国际商事法庭调查收集证据以及组织质证，可以采用视听传输技术及其他信息网络方式。

**Article 10** The International Commercial Court may use audio-visual transmission technology and other information network methods for investigating and collecting evidence and organizing cross-examination.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　最高人民法院组建国际商事专家委员会，并选定符合条件的国际商事调解机构、国际商事仲裁机构与国际商事法庭共同构建调解、仲裁、诉讼有机衔接的纠纷解决平台，形成“一站式”国际商事纠纷解决机制。  
国际商事法庭支持当事人通过调解、仲裁、诉讼有机衔接的纠纷解决平台，选择其认为适宜的方式解决国际商事纠纷。

**Article 11** The Supreme People's Court shall establish an International Commercial Expert Committee and select qualified international commercial mediation institutions and international commercial arbitration institutions to work together with the International Commercial Court to build a dispute resolution platform that integrates mediation, arbitration, and litigation, forming a "one-stop" international commercial dispute resolution mechanism.  
The International Commercial Court shall support parties in resolving international commercial disputes through an integrated dispute resolution platform of mediation, arbitration, and litigation, choosing the method they consider appropriate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国际商事法庭在受理案件后七日内，经当事人同意，可以委托国际商事专家委员会成员或者国际商事调解机构调解。

**Article 12** Within seven days after accepting a case, the International Commercial Court may, with the consent of the parties, entrust members of the International Commercial Expert Committee or an international commercial mediation institution to mediate.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经国际商事专家委员会成员或者国际商事调解机构主持调解，当事人达成调解协议的，国际商事法庭可以依照法律规定制发调解书；当事人要求发给判决书的，可以依协议的内容制作判决书送达当事人。

**Article 13** If the parties reach a mediation agreement under the mediation of members of the International Commercial Expert Committee or an international commercial mediation institution, the International Commercial Court may issue a mediation letter in accordance with legal provisions; if the parties request a judgment, a judgment may be made based on the content of the agreement and served on the parties.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人协议选择[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一款规定的国际商事仲裁机构仲裁的，可以在申请仲裁前或者仲裁程序开始后，向国际商事法庭申请证据、财产或者行为保全。  
当事人向国际商事法庭申请撤销或者执行[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一款规定的国际商事仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决的，国际商事法庭依照民事诉讼法等相关法律规定进行审查。

**Article 14** If the parties agree by agreement to arbitrate with an international commercial arbitration institution specified in paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Provisions, they may apply to the International Commercial Court for preservation of evidence, property or conduct before applying for arbitration or after the commencement of the arbitration proceedings.  
If a party applies to the International Commercial Court for setting aside or enforcing an arbitration award made by an international commercial arbitration institution specified in paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Provisions, the International Commercial Court shall review it in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and other relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国际商事法庭作出的判决、裁定，是发生法律效力的判决、裁定。  
国际商事法庭作出的调解书，经双方当事人签收后，即具有与判决同等的法律效力。

**Article 15** Judgments and rulings made by the International Commercial Court are legally effective judgments and rulings.  
Mediation letters made by the International Commercial Court, upon being signed and received by both parties, shall have the same legal effect as a judgment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人对国际商事法庭作出的已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定和调解书，可以依照民事诉讼法的规定向最高人民法院本部申请再审。  
最高人民法院本部受理前款规定的申请再审案件以及再审案件，均应当另行组成合议庭。

**Article 16** A party may apply to the Supreme People's Court headquarters for a retrial of legally effective judgments, rulings and mediation letters made by the International Commercial Court in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law.  
The Supreme People's Court headquarters shall form a separate collegial panel for accepting applications for retrial and retrial cases as referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国际商事法庭作出的发生法律效力的判决、裁定和调解书，当事人可以向国际商事法庭申请执行。

**Article 17** For legally effective judgments, rulings and mediation letters made by the International Commercial Court, the party may apply to the International Commercial Court for enforcement.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国际商事法庭通过电子诉讼服务平台、审判流程信息公开平台以及其他诉讼服务平台为诉讼参与人提供诉讼便利，并支持通过网络方式立案、缴费、阅卷、证据交换、送达、开庭等。

**Article 18** The International Commercial Court shall provide litigation convenience to participants in proceedings through electronic litigation service platforms, trial procedure information disclosure platforms and other litigation service platforms, and support online methods for case filing, fee payment, case file review, evidence exchange, service, and court hearings, among others.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定自2018年7月1日起施行。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall come into force on July 1, 2018.
