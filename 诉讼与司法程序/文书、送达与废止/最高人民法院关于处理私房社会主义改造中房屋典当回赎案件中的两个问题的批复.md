# 最高人民法院关于处理私房社会主义改造中房屋典当回赎案件中的两个问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1988〕37号  
**公布日期**：1988-09-08

---

### 正文

一、人民法院处理有关私房社会主义改造中的房屋典当回赎案件，仍应参照国家房产管理局（65）国房局字105号《关于私房改造中处理典当房屋问题的意见》办理；但此类房屋典当问题比较复杂，政策性强，各地在执行中可根据具体情况，区别对待。如出典的房屋在改造前未回赎，改造中产权已转移给国家，改造后不准回赎；如出典的房屋没有被改造，产权未转移给国家，应按国房局字105号文件中关于“今后对于出典房屋一般仍应按照典当关系处理”①的规定，和最高人民法院（84）法办字第112号《关于贯彻执行民事政策法律若干问题的意见》第（58）条的规定，以及最高人民法院近几年来对此条规定所作的解释处理。

**Article None** 1. When handling cases concerning the redemption of pawned houses in the socialist transformation of private houses, the People's Court shall still refer to the "Opinions on Handling Issues of Pawned Houses in the Reform of Private Houses" (Document No. 105 (65) Guo Fang Ju Zi of the State Administration of Housing) for handling; however, such issues of house pawns are relatively complex and involve strong policy considerations. Local courts may, in the course of enforcement, handle them differently according to specific circumstances. If the pawned house was not redeemed before the transformation and the title was transferred to the State during the transformation, redemption shall not be allowed after the transformation; if the pawned house was not transformed and the title was not transferred to the State, the case shall be handled in accordance with the provision in Document No. 105 (65) Guo Fang Ju Zi that "from now on, pawned houses shall generally still be handled according to the pawn relationship"①, the provision of Article (58) of the "Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of Civil Policies and Laws" (Document No. 112 (84) Fa Ban Zi of the Supreme People's Court), and the interpretations of this Article given by the Supreme People's Court in recent years.

二、最高人民法院（84）法办字第112号《关于贯彻执行民事政策法律若干问题的意见》第（58）条第二款规定：“典期届满逾期十年或典期未载明期限经过三十年未赎的，原则上应视为绝卖。”人民法院处理未被改造的出典房屋纠纷案件，对于已超过上述规定回赎期限的，应确认房屋产权为承典人所有；对于未超过规定回赎期限的，应准予出典人回赎。  
此复  
1988年9月8日  
①该文件第一条第一项规定：出典房屋不同于出阻房屋，如果将出典房屋纳入改造，名义上改的是出典人，实际改了承典人。因此，今后对于出典房屋一般仍应按照典当关系处理。

**Article None** 2. The second paragraph of Article (58) of the "Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of Civil Policies and Laws" (Document No. 112 (84) Fa Ban Zi of the Supreme People's Court) stipulates: "If the pawn period expires and it has been overdue for ten years, or if the pawn period is not specified and thirty years have elapsed without redemption, it shall in principle be deemed an absolute sale." When handling dispute cases involving pawned houses that have not been transformed, if the redemption period specified above has been exceeded, the People's Court shall confirm that the title to the house belongs to the pawnee; if the redemption period has not been exceeded, the court shall permit the pawner to redeem the house.  
This is a reply.  
September 8, 1988  
① Item 1 of Article 1 of that document stipulates: Pawned houses are different from houses that are rented out. If a pawned house is included in the transformation, nominally it is the pawner who is transformed, but in reality it is the pawnee who is transformed. Therefore, from now on, pawned houses shall generally still be handled according to the pawn relationship.
