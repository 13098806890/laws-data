# 最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行当事人协议管辖的民商事案件判决的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕9号  
**公布日期**：2006-07-14

> 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区经协商，达成《关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行当事人协议管辖的民商事案件判决的安排》（以下简称《安排》），并于2006年7月14日签署。《安排》已于2006年6月12日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1390次会议通过，现予公布。根据双方一致意见，本《安排》自2008年8月1日起生效。 2008年7月3日 最高人民法院 关于内地与香港特别行政区法院 相互认可和执行当事人协议管辖的 民商事案件判决的安排 法释〔2008〕9号 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府经协商，现就当事人协议管辖的民商事案件判决的认可和执行问题作出如下安排： 第一条  内地人民法院和香港特别行政区法院在具有书面管辖协议的民商事案件中作出的须支付款项的具有执行力的终审判决，当事人可以根据本安排向内地人民法院或者香港特别行政区法院申请认可和执行。 第二条  本安排所称“具有执行力的终审判决”: （一）在内地是指： 1.最高人民法院的判决； 2.高级人民法院、中级人民法院以及经授权管辖第一审涉外、涉港澳台民商事案件的基层人民法院（名单附后）依法不准上诉或者已经超过法定期限没有上诉的第一审判决，第二审判决和依照审判监督程序由上一级人民法院提审后作出的生效判决。 （二）在香港特别行政区是指终审法院、高等法院上诉法庭及原讼法庭和区域法院作出的生效判决。 本安排所称判决，在内地包括判决书、裁定书、调解书、支付令；在香港特别行政区包括判决书、命令和诉讼费评定证明书。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  内地人民法院和香港特别行政区法院在具有书面管辖协议的民商事案件中作出的须支付款项的具有执行力的终审判决，当事人可以根据本安排向内地人民法院或者香港特别行政区法院申请认可和执行。

**Article 1** First Article For final judgments with enforcement force requiring payment of a sum of money rendered by the People's Courts of the Mainland and the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in civil and commercial cases with a written jurisdiction agreement, the parties may, in accordance with this Arrangement, apply to the People's Courts of the Mainland or the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for recognition and enforcement.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  本安排所称“具有执行力的终审判决”:  
（一）在内地是指：  
1.最高人民法院的判决；  
2.高级人民法院、中级人民法院以及经授权管辖第一审涉外、涉港澳台民商事案件的基层人民法院（名单附后）依法不准上诉或者已经超过法定期限没有上诉的第一审判决，第二审判决和依照审判监督程序由上一级人民法院提审后作出的生效判决。  
（二）在香港特别行政区是指终审法院、高等法院上诉法庭及原讼法庭和区域法院作出的生效判决。  
本安排所称判决，在内地包括判决书、裁定书、调解书、支付令；在香港特别行政区包括判决书、命令和诉讼费评定证明书。  
当事人向香港特别行政区法院申请认可和执行判决后，内地人民法院对该案件依法再审的，由作出生效判决的上一级人民法院提审。

**Article 2** Second Article "Final judgments with enforcement force" as referred to in this Arrangement mean:  
(1) In the Mainland:  
1. Judgments of the Supreme People's Court;  
2. Judgments of Higher People's Courts, Intermediate People's Courts, and Primary People's Courts authorized to have jurisdiction over first-instance foreign-related, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan-related civil and commercial cases (the list annexed hereto) from which no appeal is allowed according to law or where no appeal has been filed within the statutory time limit, judgments of second instance, and effective judgments rendered by the people's court at the next higher level upon retrial in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision.  
(2) In the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region: effective judgments rendered by the Court of Final Appeal, the Court of Appeal and the Court of First Instance of the High Court, and the District Court.  
The term "judgment" as used in this Arrangement includes judgments, written orders, mediation statements, and payment orders in the Mainland; and includes judgments, orders, and cost assessment certificates in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
If, after a party applies to a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, the Mainland People's Court retries the case according to law, the people's court at the next higher level that rendered the effective judgment shall conduct the retrial.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  本安排所称“书面管辖协议”，是指当事人为解决与特定法律关系有关的已经发生或者可能发生的争议，自本安排生效之日起，以书面形式明确约定内地人民法院或者香港特别行政区法院具有唯一管辖权的协议。  
本条所称“特定法律关系”，是指当事人之间的民商事合同，不包括雇佣合同以及自然人因个人消费、家庭事宜或者其他非商业目的而作为协议一方的合同。  
本条所称“书面形式”是指合同书、信件和数据电文（包括电报、电传、传真、电子数据交换和电子邮件）等可以有形地表现所载内容、可以调取以备日后查用的形式。  
书面管辖协议可以由一份或者多份书面形式组成。  
除非合同另有规定，合同中的管辖协议条款独立存在，合同的变更、解除、终止或者无效，不影响管辖协议条款的效力。

**Article 3** Third Article "Written jurisdiction agreement" as referred to in this Arrangement means an agreement in which the parties expressly agree, in writing, that the People's Court of the Mainland or the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall have exclusive jurisdiction, from the date this Arrangement takes effect, for the purpose of resolving disputes that have arisen or may arise in connection with a specific legal relationship.  
The term "specific legal relationship" as used in this Article means a civil or commercial contract between the parties, excluding employment contracts and contracts where a natural person is a party for personal consumption, family matters, or other non-commercial purposes.  
The term "written form" as used in this Article means a form such as a contract instrument, letters, and data messages (including telegrams, telex, facsimile, electronic data interchange, and e-mail) that can tangibly represent the content contained therein and can be retrieved for future reference.  
A written jurisdiction agreement may consist of one or more written forms.  
Unless otherwise provided in the contract, the jurisdiction agreement clause in the contract shall exist independently, and any amendment, rescission, termination, or invalidity of the contract shall not affect the validity of the jurisdiction agreement clause.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  申请认可和执行符合本安排规定的民商事判决，在内地向被申请人住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地的中级人民法院提出，在香港特别行政区向香港特别行政区高等法院提出。

**Article 4** Fourth Article An application for recognition and enforcement of a civil or commercial judgment that meets the requirements of this Arrangement shall be filed with the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the respondent has its domicile, habitual residence, or property in the Mainland, and with the High Court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  被申请人住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地在内地不同的中级人民法院辖区的，申请人应当选择向其中一个人民法院提出认可和执行的申请，不得分别向两个或者两个以上人民法院提出申请。  
被申请人的住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地，既在内地又在香港特别行政区的，申请人可以同时分别向两地法院提出申请，两地法院分别执行判决的总额，不得超过判决确定的数额。已经部分或者全部执行判决的法院应当根据对方法院的要求提供已执行判决的情况。

**Article 5** Fifth Article If the place where the respondent has its domicile, habitual residence, or property is within the jurisdiction of different Intermediate People's Courts in the Mainland, the applicant shall choose to file the application for recognition and enforcement with one of those people's courts, and shall not file applications with two or more people's courts separately.  
If the place where the respondent has its domicile, habitual residence, or property is both in the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the applicant may file applications with the courts of both places at the same time, and the total amount enforced by the courts of the two places separately shall not exceed the amount determined in the judgment. The court that has already partially or fully enforced the judgment shall provide information on the enforcement of the judgment at the request of the other court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  申请人向有关法院申请认可和执行判决的，应当提交以下文件：  
（一）请求认可和执行的申请书；  
（二）经作出终审判决的法院盖章的判决书副本；  
（三）作出终审判决的法院出具的证明书，证明该判决属于本安排第二条所指的终审判决，在判决作出地可以执行；  
（四）身份证明材料：  
1.申请人为自然人的，应当提交身份证或者经公证的身份证复印件；  
2.申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应当提交经公证的法人或者其他组织注册登记证书的复印件；  
3.申请人是外国籍法人或者其他组织的，应当提交相应的公证和认证材料。  
向内地人民法院提交的文件没有中文文本的，申请人应当提交证明无误的中文译本。  
执行地法院对于本条所规定的法院出具的证明书，无需另行要求公证。

**Article 6** Sixth Article An applicant applying to the relevant court for recognition and enforcement of a judgment shall submit the following documents:  
(1) A written application for recognition and enforcement;  
(2) A copy of the judgment sealed by the court that rendered the final judgment;  
(3) A certificate issued by the court that rendered the final judgment, certifying that the judgment is a final judgment as referred to in Article 2 of this Arrangement and is enforceable in the place where it was rendered;  
(4) Identity documents:  
1. If the applicant is a natural person, it shall submit an identity card or a notarized copy of the identity card;  
2. If the applicant is a legal person or other organization, it shall submit a notarized copy of the registration certificate of the legal person or other organization;  
3. If the applicant is a foreign legal person or other organization, it shall submit corresponding notarization and certification materials.  
If the documents submitted to the Mainland People's Court are not in the Chinese language, the applicant shall submit a Chinese translation that is certified to be accurate.  
The enforcement court shall not require notarization for the certificate issued by the court as specified in this Article.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  请求认可和执行申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人为自然人的，其姓名、住所；当事人为法人或者其他组织的，法人或者其他组织的名称、住所以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务和住所；  
（二）申请执行的理由与请求的内容，被申请人的财产所在地以及财产状况；  
（三）判决是否在原审法院地申请执行以及已执行的情况。

**Article 7** Seventh Article The written application for recognition and enforcement shall state the following:  
(1) If the party is a natural person, its name and domicile; if the party is a legal person or other organization, the name and domicile of the legal person or other organization, and the name, title, and domicile of its legal representative or principal responsible person;  
(2) The reasons for applying for enforcement and the content of the request, the location of the respondent's property and its property status;  
(3) Whether the judgment has been applied for enforcement in the place of the original trial court and the status of any enforcement already carried out.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  “申请人申请认可和执行内地人民法院或者香港特别行政区法院判决的程序，依据执行地法律的规定。本安排另有规定的除外。  
申请人申请认可和执行的期间为二年。  
前款规定的期间，内地判决到香港特别行政区申请执行的，从判决规定履行期间的最后一日起计算，判决规定分期履行的，从规定的每次履行期间的最后一日起计算，判决未规定履行期间的，从判决生效之日起计算；香港特别行政区判决到内地申请执行的，从判决可强制执行之日起计算，该日为判决上注明的判决日期，判决对履行期间另有规定的，从规定的履行期间届满后开始计算。

**Article 8** Eighth Article The procedure for an applicant to apply for recognition and enforcement of a judgment of a Mainland People's Court or a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall be governed by the law of the place of enforcement, unless otherwise provided in this Arrangement.  
The time limit for an applicant to apply for recognition and enforcement shall be two years.  
With respect to the time limit provided in the preceding paragraph, where a Mainland judgment is to be enforced in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the time limit shall be calculated from the last day of the performance period specified in the judgment; if the judgment provides for performance by installments, from the last day of each specified performance period; if the judgment does not specify a performance period, from the date on which the judgment takes effect. Where a judgment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is to be enforced in the Mainland, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the judgment becomes enforceable, which shall be the date of the judgment as indicated on the judgment; if the judgment otherwise specifies a performance period, the time limit shall start from the expiry of the specified performance period.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  对申请认可和执行的判决，原审判决中的债务人提供证据证明有下列情形之一的，受理申请的法院经审查核实，应当裁定不予认可和执行：  
（一）根据当事人协议选择的原审法院地的法律，管辖协议属于无效。但选择法院已经判定该管辖协议为有效的除外；  
（二）判决已获完全履行；  
（三）根据执行地的法律，执行地法院对该案享有专属管辖权；  
（四）根据原审法院地的法律，未曾出庭的败诉一方当事人未经合法传唤或者虽经合法传唤但未获依法律规定的答辩时间。但原审法院根据其法律或者有关规定公告送达的，不属于上述情形；  
（五）判决是以欺诈方法取得的；  
（六）执行地法院就相同诉讼请求作出判决，或者外国、境外地区法院就相同诉讼请求作出判决，或者有关仲裁机构作出仲裁裁决，已经为执行地法院所认可或者执行的。  
内地人民法院认为在内地执行香港特别行政区法院判决违反内地社会公共利益，或者香港特别行政区法院认为在香港特别行政区执行内地人民法院判决违反香港特别行政区公共政策的，不予认可和执行。

**Article 9** Ninth Article With respect to a judgment for which recognition and enforcement is applied, if the debtor under the original judgment provides evidence proving any of the following circumstances, the court accepting the application shall, upon examination and verification, rule to refuse recognition and enforcement:  
(1) The jurisdiction agreement is invalid according to the law of the place of the original trial court chosen by the parties, unless the chosen court has already determined that the jurisdiction agreement is valid;  
(2) The judgment has been fully performed;  
(3) According to the law of the place of enforcement, the enforcement court has exclusive jurisdiction over the case;  
(4) The losing party that did not appear in court was not lawfully summoned, or although lawfully summoned, was not granted the time for defense as provided by law, except where the original trial court served by public notice according to its law or relevant provisions;  
(5) The judgment was obtained by fraud;  
(6) A judgment on the same claim has been rendered by the enforcement court, or a judgment on the same claim has been rendered by a foreign or regional court, or an arbitration award has been made by a relevant arbitration institution, and such judgment or award has already been recognized or enforced by the enforcement court.  
If a Mainland People's Court considers that enforcement of a judgment of a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in the Mainland would violate the social public interest of the Mainland, or a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region considers that enforcement of a judgment of a Mainland People's Court in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region would violate the public policy of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, recognition and enforcement shall be refused.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  对于香港特别行政区法院作出的判决，判决确定的债务人已经提出上诉，或者上诉程序尚未完结的，内地人民法院审查核实后，可以中止认可和执行程序。经上诉，维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。  
内地地方人民法院就已经作出的判决按照审判监督程序作出提审裁定，或者最高人民法院作出提起再审裁定的，香港特别行政区法院审查核实后，可以中止认可和执行程序。再审判决维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；再审判决完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。

**Article 10** Tenth Article For a judgment rendered by a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, if the debtor specified in the judgment has filed an appeal, or the appeal procedure has not yet been concluded, the Mainland People's Court may, after examination and verification, suspend the recognition and enforcement procedure. If upon appeal, the original judgment is maintained in whole or in part, the recognition and enforcement procedure shall be resumed; if the original judgment is completely modified, the recognition and enforcement procedure shall be terminated.  
If a local Mainland People's Court makes a ruling to conduct a retrial in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision with respect to an already rendered judgment, or the Supreme People's Court makes a ruling to initiate a retrial, the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region may, after examination and verification, suspend the recognition and enforcement procedure. If the retrial judgment maintains the original judgment in whole or in part, the recognition and enforcement procedure shall be resumed; if the retrial judgment completely modifies the original judgment, the recognition and enforcement procedure shall be terminated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  根据本安排而获认可的判决与执行地法院的判决效力相同。

**Article 11** Eleventh Article A judgment recognized in accordance with this Arrangement shall have the same legal effect as a judgment of the enforcement court.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  当事人对认可和执行与否的裁定不服的，在内地可以向上一级人民法院申请复议，在香港特别行政区可以根据其法律规定提出上诉。

**Article 12** Twelfth Article If a party is dissatisfied with a ruling on whether to recognize and enforce, it may, in the Mainland, apply for reconsideration to the people's court at the next higher level, and in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, may file an appeal in accordance with its laws.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  在法院受理当事人申请认可和执行判决期间，当事人依相同事实再行提起诉讼的，法院不予受理。  
已获认可和执行的判决，当事人依相同事实再行提起诉讼的，法院不予受理。  
对于根据本安排第九条不予认可和执行的判决，申请人不得再行提起认可和执行的申请，但是可以按照执行地的法律依相同案件事实向执行地法院提起诉讼。

**Article 13** Thirteenth Article During the period when the court has accepted a party's application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, if the party initiates another lawsuit based on the same facts, the court shall not accept it.  
If a judgment has already been recognized and enforced, and the party initiates another lawsuit based on the same facts, the court shall not accept it.  
With respect to a judgment for which recognition and enforcement is refused in accordance with Article 9 of this Arrangement, the applicant shall not file another application for recognition and enforcement, but may initiate a lawsuit with the enforcement court based on the same case facts in accordance with the law of the place of enforcement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  法院受理认可和执行判决的申请之前或者之后，可以按照执行地法律关于财产保全或者禁制资产转移的规定，根据申请人的申请，对被申请人的财产采取保全或强制措施。

**Article 14** Fourteenth Article Before or after accepting an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, the court may, in accordance with the provisions of the law of the place of enforcement on property preservation or prohibition of asset transfer, take preservation or compulsory measures against the respondent's property upon the applicant's application.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  当事人向有关法院申请执行判决，应当根据执行地有关诉讼收费的法律和规定交纳执行费或者法院费用。

**Article 15** Fifteenth Article A party applying to the relevant court for enforcement of a judgment shall pay enforcement fees or court costs in accordance with the laws and regulations on litigation fees of the place of enforcement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行的标的范围，除判决确定的数额外，还包括根据该判决须支付的利息、经法院核定的律师费以及诉讼费，但不包括税收和罚款。  
在香港特别行政区诉讼费是指经法官或者司法常务官在诉讼费评定证明书中核定或者命令支付的诉讼费用。

**Article 16** Sixteenth Article The scope of the subject matter of mutual recognition and enforcement between the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region courts shall, in addition to the amount determined in the judgment, include interest payable under the judgment, lawyer's fees approved by the court, and litigation costs, but shall not include taxes and fines.  
Litigation costs in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region refer to litigation expenses approved or ordered to be paid by a judge or the Registrar in a cost assessment certificate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  内地与香港特别行政区法院自本安排生效之日（含本日）起作出的判决，适用本安排。

**Article 17** Seventeenth Article This Arrangement shall apply to judgments rendered by the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region on or after the date on which this Arrangement takes effect (including that date).
