2017年9月22日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于废止部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件(第十二批)的决定  
  
法释〔2017〕17号  
  
为适应形势发展变化，保证国家法律统一正确适用，根据经第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议《关于修改中华人民共和国行政诉讼法的决定》修改的《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的规定，结合审判工作实际，最高人民法院对1988年至2013年期间单独发布的有关行政诉讼的司法解释和司法解释性质文件进行了清理。现决定废止15件司法解释和司法解释性质文件。废止的司法解释和司法解释性质文件从本决定施行之日起不再适用，但过去依据或参照下列司法解释和司法解释性质文件对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。当事人对废止决定公布前的行政行为不服，在决定公布后提起行政诉讼或者进入再审程序的，除相关司法解释和司法解释性质文件与当时有效的法律相抵触外，人民法院可依据相关司法解释和司法解释性质文件作出裁判。  
  
最高人民法院  
  
决定废止的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件目录(第十二批)

**Article 1** September 22, 2017  
Supreme People's Court  
Decision on Abolishing Certain Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature (Twelfth Batch)  
Fa Shi [2017] No. 17  
In order to adapt to the development and changes of the situation and ensure the uniform and correct application of national laws, pursuant to the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China as amended by the Decision on Amending the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China adopted at the Eleventh Session of the Standing Committee of the Twelfth National People's Congress, and taking into account the actual adjudication work, the Supreme People's Court has reviewed the judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature concerning administrative actions separately issued between 1988 and 2013. It is hereby decided to abolish 15 items of judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature. The abolished judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall no longer apply from the date this Decision comes into force; however, judgments and rulings previously made on relevant cases in accordance with or by reference to the following judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall remain valid. Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative act taken before the announcement of the abolition decision and initiates an administrative action or enters into a retrial procedure after the announcement of the decision, unless the relevant judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature conflict with the laws effective at that time, the People's Court may make a judgment or ruling based on the relevant judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature.  
Supreme People's Court  
Directory of Certain Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature to Be Abolished (Twelfth Batch)
