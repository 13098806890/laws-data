### 最高人民法院关于当事人因对不予执行仲裁裁决的裁定不服而申请再审人民法院不予受理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于当事人因对不予执行仲裁裁决的裁定不服  
  
而申请再审人民法院不予受理的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕8号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1995〕198号《关于当事人认为人民法院对仲裁裁决作出的不予执行的裁定有错误而申请再审，人民法院应否受理的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)的规定，人民法院对仲裁裁决依法裁定不予执行，当事人不服而申请再审的，没有法律依据，人民法院不予受理。  
  
1996年6月26日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the People's Court Shall Accept a Party's Application for Retrial on the Ground of Dissatisfaction with a Ruling Refusing Enforcement of an Arbitral Award  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 8  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your court's Chuan Gao Fa [1995] No. 198, the "Request for Instructions on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept an Application for Retrial Where a Party Believes That the People's Court's Ruling Refusing Enforcement of an Arbitral Award Is Erroneous," has been received. Upon review, the reply is as follows:  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 217 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where a People's Court has lawfully ruled to refuse enforcement of an arbitral award, and a party, being dissatisfied, applies for a retrial, there is no legal basis for such application, and the People's Court shall not accept it.  
June 26, 1996
