# 最高人民法院关于一审判决宣告无罪的公诉案件如何适用法律问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1989〕9号  
**公布日期**：1989-11-04

---

### 正文

一、对被告人有违法行为，但情节显著轻微，危害不大，不认为是犯罪的，可在宣告无罪判决的法律文书中，同时引用刑法第十条和刑事诉讼法第十一条第一项的规定作为法律根据。

**Article None** 1. Where a defendant has committed an illegal act, but the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great such that it is not considered a crime, the provisions of Article 10 of the Criminal Law and Article 11, item 1 of the Criminal Procedure Law may be cited simultaneously as the legal basis in the legal document pronouncing a judgment of acquittal.

二、对被告人没有违法行为，可在判决书中说明事实、理由后，直接宣告无罪。

**Article None** 2. Where a defendant has committed no illegal act, a judgment of acquittal may be pronounced directly after stating the facts and reasons in the judgment document.

三、对于因主要事实不清、证据不足，经多次退查后，检察院仍未查清犯罪事实，法院自己调查也无法查证清楚，不能认定被告人有罪的，可在判决书中说明情况后，直接宣告无罪；如根据已查清的犯罪事实和证据，认为虽已构成犯罪，但不需要判处刑罚的，可以根据刑事诉讼法第一百零八条的规定，要求人民检察院撤回起诉，也可以根据刑法第三十二条的规定，依法作出判决。  
1989年11月4日

**Article None** 3. Where, due to unclear main facts and insufficient evidence, after multiple returns for investigation, the People's Procuratorate still has not clarified the criminal facts, and the court's own investigation cannot verify them either, so that the defendant cannot be found guilty, a judgment of acquittal may be pronounced directly after stating the circumstances in the judgment document. If, based on the clarified criminal facts and evidence, it is considered that although a crime has been constituted, a criminal punishment is not necessary, the court may request the People's Procuratorate to withdraw the prosecution according to the provisions of Article 108 of the Criminal Procedure Law, or may render a judgment according to law in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of the Criminal Law.  
November 4, 1989
