# 最高人民法院关于商标法修改决定施行后商标案件管辖和法律适用问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕4号  
**公布日期**：2014-03-25  
**生效日期**：2014-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于商标法修改决定施行后商标案件管辖和法律适用问题的解释》已于2014年2月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1606次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年5月1日起施行。 2014年3月25日 最高人民法院 关于商标法修改决定施行后商标案件管辖和 法律适用问题的解释 法释〔2014〕4号 为正确审理商标案件，根据2013年8月30日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国商标法〉的决定》和重新公布的《中华人民共和国商标法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定，就人民法院审理商标案件有关管辖和法律适用等问题，制定本解释。 第一条　人民法院受理以下商标案件： 1.不服国务院工商行政管理部门商标评审委员会（以下简称商标评审委员会）作出的复审决定或者裁定的行政案件； 2.不服工商行政管理部门作出的有关商标的其他具体行政行为的案件； 3.商标权权属纠纷案件； 4.侵害商标专用权纠纷案件； 5.确认不侵害商标专用权纠纷案件； 6.商标权转让合同纠纷案件； 7.商标使用许可合同纠纷案件；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院受理以下商标案件：  
1.不服国务院工商行政管理部门商标评审委员会（以下简称商标评审委员会）作出的复审决定或者裁定的行政案件；  
2.不服工商行政管理部门作出的有关商标的其他具体行政行为的案件；  
3.商标权权属纠纷案件；  
4.侵害商标专用权纠纷案件；  
5.确认不侵害商标专用权纠纷案件；  
6.商标权转让合同纠纷案件；  
7.商标使用许可合同纠纷案件；  
8.商标代理合同纠纷案件；  
9.申请诉前停止侵害商标专用权案件；  
10.因申请停止侵害商标专用权损害责任案件；  
11.因商标纠纷申请诉前财产保全案件；  
12.因商标纠纷申请诉前证据保全案件；  
13.其他商标案件。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall accept the following trademark cases:  
1. Administrative cases against the review decision or adjudication made by the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board of the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board);  
2. Cases against other specific administrative acts concerning trademarks made by the administrative department for industry and commerce;  
3. Cases concerning disputes over trademark ownership;  
4. Cases concerning disputes over infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
5. Cases concerning disputes over confirmation of non-infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
6. Cases concerning disputes over trademark assignment contracts;  
7. Cases concerning disputes over trademark licensing contracts;  
8. Cases concerning disputes over trademark agency contracts;  
9. Cases concerning an application for a preliminary injunction to stop infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
10. Cases concerning liability for damages arising from an application for a preliminary injunction to stop infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
11. Cases concerning an application for pre-litigation property preservation due to trademark disputes;  
12. Cases concerning an application for pre-litigation evidence preservation due to trademark disputes;  
13. Other trademark cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　不服商标评审委员会作出的复审决定或者裁定的行政案件及国家工商行政管理总局商标局（以下简称商标局）作出的有关商标的具体行政行为案件，由北京市有关中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Administrative cases against the review decision or adjudication made by the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, and cases against the specific administrative acts concerning trademarks made by the Trademark Office of the State Administration for Industry and Commerce (hereinafter referred to as the Trademark Office), shall be under the jurisdiction of the relevant Intermediate People's Court of Beijing Municipality.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　第一审商标民事案件，由中级以上人民法院及最高人民法院指定的基层人民法院管辖。  
涉及对驰名商标保护的民事、行政案件，由省、自治区人民政府所在地市、计划单列市、直辖市辖区中级人民法院及最高人民法院指定的其他中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** First-instance civil trademark cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate people's courts and above and the primary people's courts designated by the Supreme People's Court.  
Civil and administrative cases involving the protection of well-known trademarks shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate people's courts in the localities of the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions, in cities under separate state planning, in districts under the jurisdiction of municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other intermediate people's courts designated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在工商行政管理部门查处侵害商标权行为过程中，当事人就相关商标提起商标权权属或者侵害商标专用权民事诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 4** Where, in the course of investigation and punishment of trademark infringement by the administrative department for industry and commerce, a party brings a civil action concerning trademark ownership or infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark in respect of the relevant trademark, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对于在商标法修改决定施行前提出的商标注册及续展申请，商标局于决定施行后作出对该商标申请不予受理或者不予续展的决定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院审查时适用修改后的商标法。  
对于在商标法修改决定施行前提出的商标异议申请，商标局于决定施行后作出对该异议不予受理的决定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院审查时适用修改前的商标法。

**Article 5** For a trademark registration or renewal application filed before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the Trademark Office makes a decision after the coming into force of the Decision to reject the application or not to renew the trademark, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall apply the amended Trademark Law in its review.  
For a trademark opposition application filed before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the Trademark Office makes a decision after the coming into force of the Decision not to accept the opposition, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall apply the pre-amendment Trademark Law in its review.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对于在商标法修改决定施行前当事人就尚未核准注册的商标申请复审，商标评审委员会于决定施行后作出复审决定或者裁定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院审查时适用修改后的商标法。  
对于在商标法修改决定施行前受理的商标复审申请，商标评审委员会于决定施行后作出核准注册决定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；商标评审委员会于决定施行后作出不予核准注册决定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院审查相关诉权和主体资格问题时，适用修改前的商标法。

**Article 6** For a review application filed by a party before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law in respect of a trademark not yet approved for registration, if the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a review decision or adjudication after the coming into force of the Decision, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall apply the amended Trademark Law in its review.  
For a trademark review application accepted before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a decision to approve the registration after the coming into force of the Decision, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a decision not to approve the registration after the coming into force of the Decision, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall, when reviewing issues relating to the right of action and the qualification of the subject, apply the pre-amendment Trademark Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对于在商标法修改决定施行前已经核准注册的商标，商标评审委员会于决定施行前受理、在决定施行后作出复审决定或者裁定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院审查相关程序问题适用修改后的商标法，审查实体问题适用修改前的商标法。

**Article 7** For a trademark that has been approved for registration before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board accepts a review case before the coming into force of the Decision and makes a review decision or adjudication after the coming into force of the Decision, and a party initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall apply the amended Trademark Law in reviewing procedural issues and the pre-amendment Trademark Law in reviewing substantive issues.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对于在商标法修改决定施行前受理的相关商标案件，商标局、商标评审委员会于决定施行后作出决定或者裁定，当事人提起行政诉讼的，人民法院认定该决定或者裁定是否符合商标法有关审查时限规定时，应当从修改决定施行之日起计算该审查时限。

**Article 8** For relevant trademark cases accepted before the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a decision or adjudication after the coming into force of the Decision, and a party initiates an administrative action, when determining whether such decision or adjudication complies with the provisions of the Trademark Law concerning the time limit for examination, the People's Court shall calculate such time limit from the date on which the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law comes into force.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　除本解释另行规定外，商标法修改决定施行后人民法院受理的商标民事案件，涉及该决定施行前发生的行为的，适用修改前商标法的规定；涉及该决定施行前发生，持续到该决定施行后的行为的，适用修改后商标法的规定。

**Article 9** Unless otherwise provided in this Interpretation, for civil trademark cases accepted by the People's Court after the coming into force of the Decision on Amending the Trademark Law, if the case involves acts that occurred before the coming into force of the Decision, the provisions of the pre-amendment Trademark Law shall apply; if the case involves acts that occurred before the coming into force of the Decision and continued after the coming into force of the Decision, the provisions of the amended Trademark Law shall apply.
