# 最高人民法院关于缓刑考验期内表现好的罪犯可否缩减其缓刑考验期限的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

---

### 最高人民法院关于缓刑考验期内表现好的罪犯可否缩减其缓刑考验期限的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于缓刑考验期内表现好的罪犯  
  
可否缩减其缓刑考验期限的批复  
  
一九八五年五月九日法（研）复〔1985〕27号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院（85 ）沪高法办字第30号《关于缓刑犯可否减刑的请示报告》收悉。经研究，我们认为，运用缓刑必须符合[刑法第六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-67)、第六十八条、第七十条的规定，缓刑不能离开原判刑罚独立存在，因此，对缓刑考验期限单独缩减没有法律依据。被判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑、宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，如果在缓刑考验期间确有突出的悔改表现或者立功表现，可以参照[刑法第七十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-71)的规定，对原判刑罚予以减刑，同时相应地缩减其缓刑考验期限。减刑后的刑期不能少于原判刑期的二分之一，减刑后相应缩减的缓刑考验期限不能低于减刑后的刑期，而且判拘役的缓刑的考验期限不能少于一个月，判有期徒刑的缓刑的考验期限不能少于一年。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether the Probation Period May Be Shortened for Offenders Who Perform Well During the Probation Period  
May 9, 1985 Fa (Yan) Fu [1985] No. 27  
  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
We have received your court's (85) Hu Gao Fa Ban Zi No. 30 "Request for Instructions on Whether Probationers May Have Their Sentences Commuted". Upon study, we are of the opinion that the application of probation must comply with the provisions of Articles 67, 68, and 70 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and that probation cannot exist independently from the original sentence. Therefore, there is no legal basis for independently shortening the probation period. For a criminal who is sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years and is placed on probation, if during the probation period the offender demonstrates outstanding repentance or meritorious service, the court may, by reference to the provisions of Article 71 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, grant a commutation of the original sentence and correspondingly shorten the probation period. The term of imprisonment after commutation shall not be less than one-half of the original term, and the probation period as shortened correspondingly shall not be less than the term of imprisonment after commutation. Moreover, for probation in cases of criminal detention, the probation period shall not be less than one month; for probation in cases of fixed-term imprisonment, the probation period shall not be less than one year.  
This is our reply.
