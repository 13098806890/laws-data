### 最高人民法院关于在经济纠纷案件执行过程中，自愿达成和解协议后，一方当事人不履行或者翻悔，可否按原生效法律文书执行问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院批复  
  
关于在经济纠纷案件执行过程中，当事人  
  
自愿达成和解协议后，一方当事人不履行或者翻悔，  
  
可否按原生效法律文书执行问题的批复  
  
法（经）复（1989）8号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（89）粤法经行字第 55号请示收悉。关于在经济纠纷案件执行过程中，当事人自愿达成和解协议后，一方当事人不履行该协议或者翻悔的，可否申请按原生效的法律文书执行的问题，经研究复如下：  
  
在经济纠纷案件执行过程中，当事人自行和解达成协议，是当事人对自己权利的处分，只要确系出于自愿，且不违反法律或者损害国家、集体、第三人的合法权益，人民法院应按民事诉讼法（试行）第一百八十一条的规定处理。如果一方当事人不履行和解协议或者翻悔，对方当事人申请执行的，人民法院应按原生效的法律文书执行。但是和解协议已经履行完毕，当事人又申请按原生效法律文书执行的，人民法院不予准许。  
  
1989年9月16日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether, after the Parties Voluntarily Reach a Settlement Agreement during the Enforcement of an Economic Dispute Case, the Original Effective Legal Instrument May Be Enforced If One Party Fails to Perform or Retracts  
Fa (Jing) Fu (1989) No. 8  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your request No. (89) Yue Fa Jing Xing Zi No. 55. Regarding the issue of whether, after the parties voluntarily reach a settlement agreement during the enforcement of an economic dispute case, one party fails to perform the agreement or retracts, the other party may apply for enforcement in accordance with the original effective legal instrument, our reply after study is as follows:  
During the enforcement of an economic dispute case, where the parties voluntarily enter into a settlement agreement, such agreement constitutes the parties' disposition of their own rights. As long as it is truly voluntary and does not violate the law or harm the lawful rights and interests of the state, the collective, or a third party, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 181 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation). If one party fails to perform the settlement agreement or retracts, and the other party applies for enforcement, the People's Court shall enforce the case in accordance with the original effective legal instrument. However, if the settlement agreement has been fully performed and a party subsequently applies for enforcement in accordance with the original effective legal instrument, the People's Court shall not grant such application.  
September 16, 1989
