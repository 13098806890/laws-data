# 最高人民法院关于审理扰乱电信市场管理秩序案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕12号  
**公布日期**：2000-04-28  
**生效日期**：2000-05-24

> （2000年4月28日最高人民法院审判委员会第1113次会议通过，自2000年5月24日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕12号 为依法惩处扰乱电信市场管理秩序的犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 违反国家规定，采取租用国际专线、私设转接设备或者其他方法，擅自经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务进行营利活动，扰乱电信市场管理秩序，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百二十五条第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。 第二条 实施本解释第一条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节严重”： （一）经营去话业务数额在一百万元以上的； （二）经营来话业务造成电信资费损失数额在一百万元以上的。 具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节特别严重”： （一）经营去话业务数额在五百万元以上的； （二）经营来话业务造成电信资费损失数额在五百万元以上的。 第三条 实施本解释第一条规定的行为，经营数额或者造成电信资费损失数额接近非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”的数额起点标准，并具有下列情形之一的，可以分别认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”： （一）两年内因非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务行为受过行政处罚两次以上的； （二）因非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务行为造成其他严重后果的。 第四条 单位实施本解释第一条规定的行为构成犯罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照本解释第二条、第三条的规定处罚。 第五条 违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自占用频率，非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务进行营利活动，同时构成非法经营罪和刑法第二百八十八条规定的扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 违反国家规定，采取租用国际专线、私设转接设备或者其他方法，擅自经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务进行营利活动，扰乱电信市场管理秩序，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Whoever, in violation of state regulations, engages in profit-making activities by leasing international private lines, installing transfer equipment without authorization, or by other means, to operate international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan without authorization, thereby disrupting the management order of the telecommunications market, if the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operation in accordance with the provisions of subparagraph (4) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节严重”：  
（一）经营去话业务数额在一百万元以上的；  
（二）经营来话业务造成电信资费损失数额在一百万元以上的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节特别严重”：  
（一）经营去话业务数额在五百万元以上的；  
（二）经营来话业务造成电信资费损失数额在五百万元以上的。

**Article 2** Where any person commits the act prescribed in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be considered as "serious circumstances" of illegal business operation:  
(1) the amount of outbound call service operations reaches RMB 1,000,000 or more;  
(2) the amount of losses in telecommunications charges caused by inbound call service operations reaches RMB 1,000,000 or more.  
Falling under any of the following circumstances shall be considered as "particularly serious circumstances" of illegal business operation:  
(1) the amount of outbound call service operations reaches RMB 5,000,000 or more;  
(2) the amount of losses in telecommunications charges caused by inbound call service operations reaches RMB 5,000,000 or more.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，经营数额或者造成电信资费损失数额接近非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”的数额起点标准，并具有下列情形之一的，可以分别认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”：  
（一）两年内因非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务行为受过行政处罚两次以上的；  
（二）因非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务行为造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 3** Where any person commits the act prescribed in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and the amount of operations or the amount of losses in telecommunications charges caused is close to the threshold amount for "serious circumstances" or "particularly serious circumstances" of illegal business operation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as "serious circumstances" or "particularly serious circumstances" of illegal business operation respectively:  
(1) having been subjected to administrative penalties twice or more within two years for illegally operating international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan;  
(2) having caused other serious consequences due to illegally operating international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 单位实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为构成犯罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照[本解释第二条](#art-2)、第三条的规定处罚。

**Article 4** Where a unit commits the act prescribed in Article 1 of this Interpretation and constitutes a crime, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Articles 2 and 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自占用频率，非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务进行营利活动，同时构成非法经营罪和[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)规定的扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person, in violation of state regulations, establishes or uses a radio station (transmitting station) without authorization, or occupies frequencies without authorization, and engages in profit-making activities by illegally operating international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, and such act concurrently constitutes the crime of illegal business operation and the crime of disrupting the order of radio communications management as prescribed in Article 288 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国有电信企业的工作人员，由于严重不负责任或者滥用职权，造成国有电信企业破产或者严重损失，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第一百六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-168)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where an employee of a state-owned telecommunications enterprise, due to serious irresponsibility or abuse of power, causes bankruptcy or severe losses of the state-owned telecommunications enterprise, thereby causing major losses to the interests of the state, the employee shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 168 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 将电信卡非法充值后使用，造成电信资费损失数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person illegally adds value to a telecommunications card and then uses it, causing a relatively large amount of losses in telecommunications charges, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft in accordance with the provisions of Article 264 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 盗用他人公共信息网络上网帐号、密码上网，造成他人电信资费损失数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Where a person steals another person's account number and password for accessing the public information network and uses them to go online, causing a relatively large amount of losses in telecommunications charges to others, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft in accordance with the provisions of Article 264 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 以虚假、冒用的身份证件办理入网手续并使用移动电话，造成电信资费损失数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)的规定，以诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person uses false or fraudulently used identity documents to go through the procedures for network access and uses a mobile phone, causing a relatively large amount of losses in telecommunications charges, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud in accordance with the provisions of Article 266 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 本解释所称“经营去话业务数额”，是指以行为人非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务的总时长（分钟数）乘以行为人每分钟收取的用户使用费所得的数额。  
本解释所称“电信资费损失数额”，是指以行为人非法经营国际电信业务或者涉港澳台电信业务的总时长（分钟数）乘以在合法电信业务中我国应当得到的每分钟国际结算价格所得的数额。

**Article 10** The term "amount of outbound call service operations" as used in this Interpretation refers to the amount obtained by multiplying the total duration (in minutes) of the illegal operation of international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan by the user usage fee per minute charged by the actor.  
The term "amount of losses in telecommunications charges" as used in this Interpretation refers to the amount obtained by multiplying the total duration (in minutes) of the illegal operation of international telecommunications services or telecommunications services involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan by the international settlement price per minute that China should have received in legitimate telecommunications services.
