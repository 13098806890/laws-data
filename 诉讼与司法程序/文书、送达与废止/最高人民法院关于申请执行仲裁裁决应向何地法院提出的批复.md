**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

### 最高人民法院关于申请执行仲裁裁决应向何地法院提出的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于申请执行仲裁裁决应向何地法院提出的批复  
  
一九八五年一月十七日          法（研）复〔1985〕5号  
  
天津市高级人民法院：  
  
你院一九八四年十二月二十四日津高法（1984）第95号请示收悉。关于民事诉讼法（试行）第一百六十七条“仲裁机构的裁决发生法律效力后，一方当事人不履行的，对方当事人可以向有管辖权的人民法院申请执行”中所说的“有管辖权的人民法院”应如何理解的问题，我们同意你院的意见，即应向被执行人户籍所在地（或被执行单位所在地）的人民法院或者被执行财产所在地的人民法院申请执行；被执行人的户籍所在地与居所地不一致的，可向被执行人居所地的人民法院申请执行。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Official Reply on the People's Court to Which Application for Enforcement of an Arbitral Award Shall Be Filed  
January 17, 1985 Fa (Yan) Fu [1985] No. 5  
Higher People's Court of Tianjin:  
We have received your Request No. 95 of December 24, 1984 (Jin Gao Fa [1984] No. 95). Concerning the understanding of the phrase "the people's court with competent jurisdiction" as referred to in Article 167 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), which provides that "after the award of the arbitration institution has become legally effective, if one party fails to perform it, the other party may apply to the people's court with competent jurisdiction for enforcement," we agree with your opinion, namely, an application for enforcement shall be filed with the people's court at the domicile (or the location of the unit) of the person subject to enforcement, or with the people's court at the place where the property subject to enforcement is located; if the domicile of the person subject to enforcement is inconsistent with his place of residence, an application for enforcement may be filed with the people's court at the place of residence of the person subject to enforcement.  
This reply is hereby given.
