# 最高人民法院关于诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为和保全证据适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕2号  
**公布日期**：2001-12-25  
**生效日期**：2002-01-22

> （2001年12月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1203次会议通过，自2002年1月22日起施行） 法释〔2002〕2号 为切实保护商标注册人和利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国商标法》（以下简称商标法）、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》》（以下简称民事诉讼法）的有关规定，现就有关诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为和保全证据适用法律问题解释如下： 第一条 根据商标法第五十七条、第五十八条的规定，商标注册人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院提出诉前责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的申请。 提出申请的利害关系人，包括商标使用许可合同的被许可人、注册商标财产权利的合法继承人。注册商标使用许可合同被许可人中，独占使用许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他使用许可合同的被许可人在商标注册人不申请的情况下，可以提出申请。 第二条 诉前责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的申请，应当向侵权行为地或者被申请人住所地对商标案件有管辖权的人民法院提出。 第三条 商标注册人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请，应当递交书面申请状。申请状应当载明：（一）当事人及其基本情况；（二）申请的具体内容、范围；（三）申请的理由，包括有关行为如不及时制止，将会使商标注册人或者利害关系人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的具体说明。 商标注册人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出诉前保全证据的申请，应当递交书面申请状。申请状应当载明：（一）当事人及其基本情况；（二）申请保全证据的具体内容、范围、所在地点；（三）请求保全的证据能够证明的对象；（四）申请的理由，包括证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得，且当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能自行收集的具体说明。 第四条 申请人提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请时，应当提交下列证据： （一）商标注册人应当提交商标注册证，利害关系人应当提交商标使用许可合同、在商标局备案的材料及商标注册证复印件；排他使用许可合同的被许可人单独提出申请的，应当提交商标注册人放弃申请的证据材料；注册商标财产权利的继承人应当提交已经继承或者正在继承的证据材料。 （二）证明被申请人正在实施或者即将实施侵犯注册商标专用权的行为的证据，包括被控侵权商品。 第五条 人民法院作出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的裁定事项，应当限于商标注册人或者利害关系人申请的范围。 第六条 申请人提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请时应当提供担保。 申请人申请诉前保全证据可能涉及被申请人财产损失的，人民法院可以责令申请人提供相应的担保。 申请人提供保证、抵押等形式的担保合理、有效的，人民法院应当准许。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 根据[商标法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-57)、第五十八条的规定，商标注册人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院提出诉前责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的申请。  
提出申请的利害关系人，包括商标使用许可合同的被许可人、注册商标财产权利的合法继承人。注册商标使用许可合同被许可人中，独占使用许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他使用许可合同的被许可人在商标注册人不申请的情况下，可以提出申请。

**Article 1** In accordance with the provisions of Article 57 and Article 58 of the Trademark Law of the People's Republic of China, the trademark registrant or an interested party may file an application with the People's Court for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark or for preservation of evidence.  
The interested party filing the application shall include the licensee under a trademark licensing contract and the lawful successor to the property rights in the registered trademark. Among the licensees under a registered trademark licensing contract, the licensee under an exclusive licensing contract may file the application alone with the People's Court; the licensee under a sole licensing contract may file the application if the trademark registrant fails to do so.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 诉前责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的申请，应当向侵权行为地或者被申请人住所地对商标案件有管辖权的人民法院提出。

**Article 2** An application for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark or for preservation of evidence shall be filed with the People's Court that has jurisdiction over trademark cases at the place where the infringing act is committed or where the respondent has its domicile.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 商标注册人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请，应当递交书面申请状。申请状应当载明：（一）当事人及其基本情况；（二）申请的具体内容、范围；（三）申请的理由，包括有关行为如不及时制止，将会使商标注册人或者利害关系人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的具体说明。  
商标注册人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出诉前保全证据的申请，应当递交书面申请状。申请状应当载明：（一）当事人及其基本情况；（二）申请保全证据的具体内容、范围、所在地点；（三）请求保全的证据能够证明的对象；（四）申请的理由，包括证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得，且当事人及其诉讼代理人因客观原因不能自行收集的具体说明。

**Article 3** Where the trademark registrant or an interested party files an application with the People's Court for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, it shall submit a written application. The application shall state: (1) the party and its basic information; (2) the specific content and scope of the application; (3) the reasons for the application, including a specific explanation that if the relevant act is not promptly stopped, the lawful rights and interests of the trademark registrant or the interested party shall suffer irreparable harm.  
Where the trademark registrant or an interested party files an application with the People's Court for pre-litigation preservation of evidence, it shall submit a written application. The application shall state: (1) the party and its basic information; (2) the specific content, scope, and location of the evidence to be preserved; (3) the object that the evidence sought to be preserved can prove; (4) the reasons for the application, including a specific explanation that the evidence may be lost or become difficult to obtain later, and that the party and its litigation agent cannot collect it on their own due to objective reasons.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 申请人提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请时，应当提交下列证据：  
（一）商标注册人应当提交商标注册证，利害关系人应当提交商标使用许可合同、在商标局备案的材料及商标注册证复印件；排他使用许可合同的被许可人单独提出申请的，应当提交商标注册人放弃申请的证据材料；注册商标财产权利的继承人应当提交已经继承或者正在继承的证据材料。  
（二）证明被申请人正在实施或者即将实施侵犯注册商标专用权的行为的证据，包括被控侵权商品。

**Article 4** When filing an application for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, the applicant shall submit the following evidence:  
(1) The trademark registrant shall submit the trademark registration certificate; the interested party shall submit the trademark licensing contract, materials filed with the Trademark Office, and a copy of the trademark registration certificate; where the licensee under a sole licensing contract files the application alone, it shall submit evidence materials that the trademark registrant has abandoned the application; the successor to the property rights in the registered trademark shall submit evidence materials that the succession has taken place or is taking place.  
(2) Evidence proving that the respondent is committing or is about to commit the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, including the allegedly infringing goods.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院作出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据的裁定事项，应当限于商标注册人或者利害关系人申请的范围。

**Article 5** The matters ruled by the People's Court for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark or for preservation of evidence shall be limited to the scope of the application filed by the trademark registrant or the interested party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 申请人提出诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请时应当提供担保。  
申请人申请诉前保全证据可能涉及被申请人财产损失的，人民法院可以责令申请人提供相应的担保。  
申请人提供保证、抵押等形式的担保合理、有效的，人民法院应当准许。  
申请人不提供担保的，驳回申请。  
人民法院确定担保的范围时，应当考虑责令停止有关行为所涉及的商品销售收益，以及合理的仓储、保管等费用，停止有关行为可能造成的合理损失等。

**Article 6** When filing an application for a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, the applicant shall provide security.  
Where the applicant's application for pre-litigation preservation of evidence may involve property losses of the respondent, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide corresponding security.  
Where the security provided by the applicant in the form of a guarantee, mortgage, or other form is reasonable and effective, the People's Court shall permit it.  
If the applicant fails to provide security, the application shall be rejected.  
When determining the scope of security, the People's Court shall consider the revenue from the sale of goods involved in the ordered cessation of the relevant act, as well as reasonable costs such as storage and custody, and the reasonable losses that may be caused by the cessation of the relevant act.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 在执行停止有关行为裁定过程中，被申请人可能因采取该项措施造成更大损失的，人民法院可以责令申请人追加相应的担保。申请人不追加担保的，可以解除有关停止措施。

**Article 7** During the enforcement of a ruling ordering the cessation of the relevant act, if the respondent may suffer greater losses due to the adoption of such measure, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide additional corresponding security. If the applicant fails to provide additional security, the relevant cessation measure may be lifted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为裁定所采取的措施，不因被申请人提供担保而解除，但申请人同意的除外。

**Article 8** The measures taken under a ruling to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark shall not be lifted because the respondent provides security, unless the applicant consents.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院接受商标注册人或者利害关系人提出责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的申请后，经审查符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)的，应当在四十八小时内作出书面裁定；裁定责令被申请人停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为的，应当立即开始执行。  
人民法院作出诉前责令停止有关行为的裁定，应当及时通知被申请人，至迟不得超过五日。

**Article 9** After accepting an application filed by the trademark registrant or an interested party for an order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, the People's Court shall, upon examination and if it complies with Article 4 of these Provisions, make a written ruling within forty-eight hours; where the ruling orders the respondent to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, enforcement shall begin immediately.  
Where the People's Court makes a ruling on a pre-litigation order to cease the relevant act, it shall promptly notify the respondent, no later than five days.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 当事人对诉前责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定之日起十日内申请复议一次。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 10** Where a party is dissatisfied with a ruling on a pre-litigation order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, it may apply for reconsideration once within ten days from the date of receipt of the ruling. The enforcement of the ruling shall not be suspended during the reconsideration period.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院对当事人提出的复议申请应当从以下方面进行审查：  
（一）被申请人正在实施或者即将实施的行为是否侵犯注册商标专用权；  
（二）不采取有关措施，是否会给申请人合法权益造成难以弥补的损害；  
（三）申请人提供担保的情况；  
（四）责令被申请人停止有关行为是否损害社会公共利益。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall examine a reconsideration application filed by a party from the following aspects:  
(1) Whether the act that the respondent is committing or is about to commit infringes the exclusive right to use a registered trademark;  
(2) Whether the failure to adopt the relevant measure shall cause irreparable harm to the applicant's lawful rights and interests;  
(3) The circumstances of the security provided by the applicant;  
(4) Whether ordering the respondent to cease the relevant act damages the public interest.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 商标注册人或者利害关系人在人民法院采取停止有关行为或者保全证据的措施后十五日内不起诉的，人民法院应当解除裁定采取的措施。

**Article 12** If the trademark registrant or an interested party fails to file a prosecution within fifteen days after the People's Court adopts measures to cease the relevant act or to preserve evidence, the People's Court shall lift the measures taken under the ruling.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 申请人不起诉或者申请错误造成被申请人损失的，被申请人可以向有管辖权的人民法院起诉请求申请人赔偿，也可以在商标注册人或者利害关系人提起的侵犯注册商标专用权的诉讼中提出损害赔偿请求，人民法院可以一并处理。

**Article 13** Where the applicant fails to file a prosecution or the application is erroneous and causes losses to the respondent, the respondent may file a prosecution with the People's Court having jurisdiction for compensation from the applicant, or may claim damages in the lawsuit for infringement of the exclusive right to use a registered trademark brought by the trademark registrant or the interested party. The People's Court may handle them together.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为裁定的效力，一般应维持到终审法律文书生效时止。  
人民法院也可以根据案情，确定停止有关行为的具体期限；期限届满时，根据当事人的请求及追加担保的情况，可以作出继续停止有关行为的裁定。

**Article 14** The effect of a ruling to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark shall generally be maintained until the final legal instrument takes effect.  
The People's Court may also, based on the circumstances of the case, determine a specific period for the cessation of the relevant act; upon expiration of the period, a ruling may be made to continue the cessation of the relevant act based on the request of the party and the circumstances of additional security.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 被申请人违反人民法院责令停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为或者保全证据裁定的，依照[民事诉讼法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)规定处理。

**Article 15** Where a respondent violates a ruling of the People's Court ordering the cessation of the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark or the preservation of evidence, it shall be handled in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 商标注册人或者利害关系人向人民法院提起商标侵权诉讼时或者诉讼中，提出先行停止侵犯注册商标专用权请求的，人民法院可以先行作出裁定。前款规定涉及的有关申请、证据提交、担保的确定、裁定的执行和复议等事项，参照本司法解释有关规定办理。

**Article 16** Where the trademark registrant or an interested party, at the time of or during a trademark infringement lawsuit filed with the People's Court, requests a preliminary order to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, the People's Court may make a preliminary ruling. Matters concerning the relevant application, submission of evidence, determination of security, enforcement of the ruling, and reconsideration as provided in the preceding paragraph shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of this judicial interpretation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为和保全证据的案件，申请人应当按照《人民法院诉讼收费办法》及其补充规定缴纳费用。

**Article 17** For cases concerning pre-litigation orders to cease the act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark and pre-litigation preservation of evidence, the applicant shall pay fees in accordance with the Measures for Litigation Fee Payment of the People's Court and its supplementary provisions.
