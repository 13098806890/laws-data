# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕1号  
**公布日期**：2014-01-09  
**生效日期**：2014-01-24

> 最高人民法院《关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年12月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1601次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年1月24日起施行。 2014年1月9日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷 调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2014〕1号 为正确审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件，根据《中华人民共和国农村土地承包法》、《中华人民共和国农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合民事审判实践，就审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律的若干问题，制定本解释。 第一条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会根据农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法第十八条规定，以超过申请仲裁的时效期间为由驳回申请后，当事人就同一纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。 第二条　当事人在收到农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的裁决书之日起三十日后或者签收农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的调解书后，就同一纠纷向人民法院提起诉讼的，裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。 第三条　当事人在收到农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的裁决书之日起三十日内，向人民法院提起诉讼，请求撤销仲裁裁决的，人民法院应当告知当事人就原纠纷提起诉讼。 第四条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会依法向人民法院提交当事人财产保全申请的，申请财产保全的当事人为申请人。 农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当提交下列材料： （一）财产保全申请书； （二）农村土地承包仲裁委员会发出的受理案件通知书； （三）申请人的身份证明；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会根据[农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法第十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法.md#art-18)规定，以超过申请仲裁的时效期间为由驳回申请后，当事人就同一纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** After the rural land contract arbitration committee rejects an application on the ground that the time limit for applying for arbitration has been exceeded in accordance with Article 18 of the Law on Mediation and Arbitration of Rural Land Contract Disputes, if a party files a lawsuit regarding the same dispute, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人在收到农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的裁决书之日起三十日后或者签收农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的调解书后，就同一纠纷向人民法院提起诉讼的，裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 2** If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the same dispute after thirty days from the date of receipt of the award made by the rural land contract arbitration committee or after signing and receiving the mediation statement made by the rural land contract arbitration committee, the court shall rule to not accept the case; if already accepted, it shall rule to dismiss the lawsuit.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人在收到农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的裁决书之日起三十日内，向人民法院提起诉讼，请求撤销仲裁裁决的，人民法院应当告知当事人就原纠纷提起诉讼。

**Article 3** If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court within thirty days from the date of receipt of the award made by the rural land contract arbitration committee, requesting revocation of the arbitration award, the People's Court shall inform the party to file a lawsuit regarding the original dispute.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会依法向人民法院提交当事人财产保全申请的，申请财产保全的当事人为申请人。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当提交下列材料：  
（一）财产保全申请书；  
（二）农村土地承包仲裁委员会发出的受理案件通知书；  
（三）申请人的身份证明；  
（四）申请保全财产的具体情况。  
人民法院采取保全措施，可以责令申请人提供担保，申请人不提供担保的，裁定驳回申请。

**Article 4** If the rural land contract arbitration committee submits a property preservation application of a party to the People's Court according to law, the party applying for property preservation shall be the applicant.  
The rural land contract arbitration committee shall submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for property preservation;  
(2) the notice of case acceptance issued by the rural land contract arbitration committee;  
(3) the identity certificate of the applicant;  
(4) specific information of the property to be preserved.  
When the People's Court adopts preservation measures, it may order the applicant to provide security; if the applicant fails to provide security, it shall rule to dismiss the application.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院对农村土地承包仲裁委员会提交的财产保全申请材料，应当进行审查。符合前条规定的，应予受理；申请材料不齐全或不符合规定的，人民法院应当告知农村土地承包仲裁委员会需要补齐的内容。  
人民法院决定受理的，应当于三日内向当事人送达受理通知书并告知农村土地承包仲裁委员会。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall review the property preservation application materials submitted by the rural land contract arbitration committee. If they meet the requirements of the preceding article, it shall accept the case; if the application materials are incomplete or non-compliant, the People's Court shall inform the rural land contract arbitration committee of the content to be supplemented.  
If the People's Court decides to accept the case, it shall serve a notice of acceptance to the parties and inform the rural land contract arbitration committee within three days.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院受理财产保全申请后，应当在十日内作出裁定。因特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长五日。  
人民法院接受申请后，对情况紧急的，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定采取保全措施的，应当立即开始执行。

**Article 6** After accepting an application for property preservation, the People's Court shall issue a ruling within ten days. If an extension is required due to special circumstances, with the approval of the president of this court, it may be extended by five days.  
After accepting the application, if the situation is urgent, the People's Court must issue a ruling within forty-eight hours; if the ruling adopts preservation measures, it shall immediately commence enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁中采取的财产保全措施，在申请保全的当事人依法提起诉讼后，自动转为诉讼中的财产保全措施，并适用最高人民法院《关于人民法院民事执行中查封、扣押、冻结财产的规定》第二十九条关于查封、扣押、冻结期限的规定。

**Article 7** The property preservation measures taken in the arbitration of rural land contract disputes shall automatically be converted into property preservation measures in litigation after the party applying for preservation files a lawsuit according to law, and shall apply the provisions of Article 29 of the Supreme People's Court's Provisions on the Seizure, Attachment, and Freezing of Property in Civil Enforcement of the People's Courts regarding the time limits for seizure, attachment, and freezing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会依法向人民法院提交当事人证据保全申请的，应当提供下列材料：  
（一）证据保全申请书；  
（二）农村土地承包仲裁委员会发出的受理案件通知书；  
（三）申请人的身份证明；  
（四）申请保全证据的具体情况。  
对证据保全的具体程序事项，适用本解释第五、六、七条关于财产保全的规定。

**Article 8** If the rural land contract arbitration committee submits an application for evidence preservation of a party to the People's Court according to law, it shall provide the following materials:  
(1) a written application for evidence preservation;  
(2) a notice of case acceptance issued by the rural land contract arbitration committee;  
(3) the identity certificate of the applicant;  
(4) specific information of the evidence to be preserved.  
For the specific procedural matters of evidence preservation, the provisions of Articles 5, 6, and 7 of this Interpretation on property preservation shall apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出先行裁定后，一方当事人依法向被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地基层人民法院申请执行的，人民法院应予受理和执行。  
申请执行先行裁定的，应当提供以下材料：  
（一）申请执行书；  
（二）农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的先行裁定书；  
（三）申请执行人的身份证明；  
（四）申请执行人提供的担保情况；  
（五）其他应当提交的文件或证件。

**Article 9** After the rural land contract arbitration committee issues an advance ruling, if one party applies to the Primary People's Court at the domicile of the party against whom enforcement is sought or at the location of the property to be enforced for enforcement according to law, the People's Court shall accept and enforce it.  
To apply for enforcement of an advance ruling, the following materials shall be provided:  
(1) a written application for enforcement;  
(2) the advance ruling issued by the rural land contract arbitration committee;  
(3) the identity certificate of the applicant for enforcement;  
(4) the security provided by the applicant for enforcement;  
(5) other documents or certificates that shall be submitted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人根据[农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法.md#art-49)规定，向人民法院申请执行调解书、裁决书，符合最高人民法院《关于人民法院执行工作若干问题的规定（试行）》第十八条规定条件的，人民法院应予受理和执行。

**Article 10** If a party applies to the People's Court for enforcement of a mediation statement or an award in accordance with Article 49 of the Law on Mediation and Arbitration of Rural Land Contract Disputes, and meets the conditions specified in Article 18 of the Supreme People's Court's Provisions on Certain Issues Concerning the Enforcement Work of the People's Courts (for Trial Implementation), the People's Court shall accept and enforce it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人因不服农村土地承包仲裁委员会作出的仲裁裁决向人民法院提起诉讼的，起诉期从其收到裁决书的次日起计算。

**Article 11** If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court because it is dissatisfied with the arbitration award made by the rural land contract arbitration committee, the limitation period for prosecution shall be calculated from the day after the party receives the award.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释施行后，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本解释规定。本解释施行前已经作出生效裁判的案件，本解释施行后依法再审的，不适用本解释规定。

**Article 12** After this Interpretation comes into force, cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been finally adjudicated by the People's Court shall apply the provisions of this Interpretation. Cases for which an effective judgment has already been made before this Interpretation comes into force and which are retried according to law after this Interpretation comes into force shall not apply the provisions of this Interpretation.
