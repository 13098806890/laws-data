### 最高人民法院关于购销合同标的物掺杂使假引起的诉讼如何确定诉讼时效的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院关于购销合同标的物掺杂使假  
  
引起的诉讼如何确定诉讼时效的复函  
  
法函〔1992〕10号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院〔1991〕经上字第 4号《关于审理购销合同纠纷案件中标的物掺杂使假是否受诉讼时效期间约束问题的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
因购销合同的标的物掺杂使假引起的纠纷，应当适用《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百三十五条规定，即“向人民法院请求保护民事权利的诉讼时效期间为二年”。  
  
1992年1月16日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court on How to Determine the Limitation of Action for a Lawsuit Arising from Adulteration and Falsification of the Subject Matter of a Purchase and Sale Contract  
Fa Han [1992] No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Liaoning Province:  
  
Your court's [1991] Jing Shang Zi No. 4, the "Report for Instructions on the Question of Whether Adulteration and Falsification of the Subject Matter in Cases of Disputes over Purchase and Sale Contracts is Subject to the Limitation of Action Period," has been received. Upon deliberation, the reply is as follows:  
  
Disputes arising from the adulteration and falsification of the subject matter of a purchase and sale contract shall be governed by the provisions of Article 135 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, namely, "The limitation of action for applying to a people's court for protection of civil rights shall be two years."  
  
January 16, 1992
