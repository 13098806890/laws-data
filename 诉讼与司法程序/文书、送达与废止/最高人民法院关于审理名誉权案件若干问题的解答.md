# 最高人民法院关于审理名誉权案件若干问题的解答

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕15号  
**公布日期**：1993-08-07

> （最高人民法院审判委员会 1993年 6月15日第 579次会议讨论通过） 法发〔1993〕15号 各地人民法院在审理名誉权案件中，提出一些如何适用法律的问题，现解答如下： 一、问：人民法院对当事人关于名誉权纠纷的起诉应如何进行审查？ 答：人民法院收到有关名誉权纠纷的起诉时，应按照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法）第一百零八条的规定进行审查，符合条件的，应予受理。对不符合起诉条件的，应裁定不予受理；对缺乏侵权事实坚持起诉的，应裁定驳回起诉。 二、问：当事人在公共场所受到侮辱、诽谤，经公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》（以下简称治安管理处罚条例）处理后，又向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院是否受理？ 答：当事人在公共场所受到侮辱、诽谤，以名誉权受侵害为由提起民事诉讼的，无论是否经公安机关依照治安管理处罚条例处理，人民法院均应依法审查，符合受理条件的，应予受理。 三、问：当事人提起名誉权诉讼后，以同一事实和理由又要求追究被告人的刑事责任的，应如何处理？ 答：当事人提起名誉权诉讼后，以同一事实和理由又要求追究被告刑事责任的，应中止民事诉讼，待刑事案件审结后，根据不同情况分别处理：对于犯罪情节轻微，没有给予被告人刑事处罚的，或者刑事自诉已由原告撤回或者被驳回的，应恢复民事诉讼；对于民事诉讼请求已在刑事附带民事诉讼中解决的，应终结民事案件的审理。 四、问：名誉权案件如何确定管辖？ 答：名誉权案件，适用民事诉讼法第二十九条的规定，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括侵权行为实施地和侵权结果发生地。 五、问：死者名誉受到损害，哪些人可以作为原告提起民事诉讼？ 答：死者名誉受到损害的，其近亲属有权向人民法院起诉。近亲属包括：配偶、父母、子女、兄弟姐妹、祖父母、外祖父母、孙子女、外孙子女。 六、问：因新闻报道或者其他作品引起的名誉权纠纷，如何确定被告？ 答：因新闻报道或其他作品发生的名誉权纠纷，应根据原告的起诉确定被告。只诉作者的，列作者为被告；只诉新闻出版单位的，列新闻出版单位为被告；对作者和新闻出版单位都提起诉讼的，将作者和新闻出版单位均列为被告，但作者与新闻出版单位为隶属关系，作品系作者履行职务所形成的，只列单位为被告。

### 正文

一、问：人民法院对当事人关于名誉权纠纷的起诉应如何进行审查？  
答：人民法院收到有关名誉权纠纷的起诉时，应按照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法）第一百零八条的规定进行审查，符合条件的，应予受理。对不符合起诉条件的，应裁定不予受理；对缺乏侵权事实坚持起诉的，应裁定驳回起诉。

**Article None** 1. Q: How shall a People's Court review a prosecution filed by a party concerning a dispute over the right to reputation?  
A: Upon receipt of a prosecution concerning a dispute over the right to reputation, the People's Court shall conduct a review in accordance with the provisions of Article 108 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Procedure Law). If the conditions are met, the case shall be accepted. If the conditions for prosecution are not met, a ruling shall be made not to accept the case; if the party insists on prosecution despite the lack of facts constituting infringement, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the prosecution.

二、问：当事人在公共场所受到侮辱、诽谤，经公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》（以下简称治安管理处罚条例）处理后，又向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院是否受理？  
答：当事人在公共场所受到侮辱、诽谤，以名誉权受侵害为由提起民事诉讼的，无论是否经公安机关依照治安管理处罚条例处理，人民法院均应依法审查，符合受理条件的，应予受理。

**Article None** 2. Q: If a party is insulted or defamed in a public place, and after being handled by a public security organ in accordance with the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Regulations on Public Security Penalties), the party subsequently initiates a civil action with a People's Court, shall the People's Court accept the case?  
A: Where a party is insulted or defamed in a public place and initiates a civil action on the ground that his or her right to reputation has been infringed, regardless of whether the public security organ has handled the matter in accordance with the Regulations on Public Security Penalties, the People's Court shall conduct a review according to law and, if the conditions for acceptance are met, shall accept the case.

三、问：当事人提起名誉权诉讼后，以同一事实和理由又要求追究被告人的刑事责任的，应如何处理？  
答：当事人提起名誉权诉讼后，以同一事实和理由又要求追究被告刑事责任的，应中止民事诉讼，待刑事案件审结后，根据不同情况分别处理：对于犯罪情节轻微，没有给予被告人刑事处罚的，或者刑事自诉已由原告撤回或者被驳回的，应恢复民事诉讼；对于民事诉讼请求已在刑事附带民事诉讼中解决的，应终结民事案件的审理。

**Article None** 3. Q: If, after initiating an action on the right to reputation, a party also demands that the defendant be investigated for criminal liability based on the same facts and reasons, how shall the matter be handled?  
A: If, after initiating an action on the right to reputation, a party also demands that the defendant be investigated for criminal liability based on the same facts and reasons, the civil action shall be suspended. After the conclusion of the criminal case, the matter shall be handled separately according to different circumstances: if the crime is minor and no criminal penalty is imposed on the defendant, or if the private criminal prosecution has been withdrawn by the plaintiff or dismissed, the civil action shall be resumed; if the civil claims have already been resolved in the incidental civil action attached to the criminal case, the trial of the civil case shall be terminated.

四、问：名誉权案件如何确定管辖？  
答：名誉权案件，适用民事诉讼法第二十九条的规定，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括侵权行为实施地和侵权结果发生地。

**Article None** 4. Q: How shall jurisdiction be determined for cases concerning the right to reputation?  
A: For cases concerning the right to reputation, the provisions of Article 29 of the Civil Procedure Law shall apply, and the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the tortious act was committed or where the defendant has his or her domicile. The place where the tortious act was committed includes the place where the tortious act was carried out and the place where the harmful result of the tort occurred.

五、问：死者名誉受到损害，哪些人可以作为原告提起民事诉讼？  
答：死者名誉受到损害的，其近亲属有权向人民法院起诉。近亲属包括：配偶、父母、子女、兄弟姐妹、祖父母、外祖父母、孙子女、外孙子女。

**Article None** 5. Q: If the reputation of a deceased person is harmed, which persons may initiate a civil action as plaintiffs?  
A: If the reputation of a deceased person is harmed, his or her close relatives shall have the right to file a prosecution with a People's Court. Close relatives include: spouses, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, maternal grandparents, grandchildren, and maternal grandchildren.

六、问：因新闻报道或者其他作品引起的名誉权纠纷，如何确定被告？  
答：因新闻报道或其他作品发生的名誉权纠纷，应根据原告的起诉确定被告。只诉作者的，列作者为被告；只诉新闻出版单位的，列新闻出版单位为被告；对作者和新闻出版单位都提起诉讼的，将作者和新闻出版单位均列为被告，但作者与新闻出版单位为隶属关系，作品系作者履行职务所形成的，只列单位为被告。

**Article None** 6. Q: How shall the defendant be determined in a dispute over the right to reputation arising from news reports or other works?  
A: For a dispute over the right to reputation arising from news reports or other works, the defendant shall be determined according to the plaintiff's prosecution. If the plaintiff sues only the author, the author shall be listed as the defendant; if the plaintiff sues only the news publishing unit, the news publishing unit shall be listed as the defendant; if the plaintiff sues both the author and the news publishing unit, both the author and the news publishing unit shall be listed as defendants. However, if the author is in a subordinate relationship with the news publishing unit and the work is formed by the author in the performance of his or her duties, only the unit shall be listed as the defendant.

七、问：侵害名誉权责任应如何认定？  
答：是否构成侵害名誉权的责任，应当根据受害人确有名誉被损害的事实、行为人行为违法、违法行为与损害后果之间有因果关系、行为人主观上有过错来认定。  
以书面或者口头形式侮辱或者诽谤他人，损害他人名誉的，应认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
对未经他人同意，擅自公布他人的隐私材料或者以书面、口头形式宣扬他人隐私，致他人名誉受到损害的，按照侵害他人名誉权处理。  
因新闻报道严重失实，致他人名誉受到损害的，应按照侵害他人名誉权处理。

**Article None** 7. Q: How shall liability for infringement of the right to reputation be determined?  
A: Whether liability for infringement of the right to reputation is constituted shall be determined on the basis of: the fact that the victim's reputation has indeed been harmed, the illegality of the actor's conduct, the causal relationship between the illegal conduct and the harmful result, and the subjective fault of the actor.  
Insulting or defaming another person in written or oral form, thereby harming his or her reputation, shall be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.  
Disclosing another person's private materials without his or her consent, or publicizing another person's privacy in written or oral form, thereby causing harm to his or her reputation, shall be dealt with as infringement of the right to reputation.  
If news reports are seriously inaccurate, thereby causing harm to another person's reputation, this shall be dealt with as infringement of the right to reputation.

八、问：因撰写、发表批评文章引起的名誉权纠纷，应如何认定是否构成侵权？  
答：因撰写、发表批评文章引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院应根据不同情况处理：  
文章反映的问题基本真实，没有侮辱他人人格的内容的，不应认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
文章反映的问题虽基本属实，但有侮辱他人人格的内容，使他人名誉受到损害的，应认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
文章的基本内容失实，使他人名誉受到损害的，应认定为侵害他人名誉权。

**Article None** 8. Q: How shall it be determined whether infringement is constituted in a dispute over the right to reputation arising from the writing or publication of critical articles?  
A: For a dispute over the right to reputation arising from the writing or publication of critical articles, the People's Court shall handle the matter according to different circumstances:  
If the issues reflected in the article are basically true and the article does not contain content that insults the personality of another person, it shall not be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.  
If the issues reflected in the article are basically true but the article contains content that insults the personality of another person, thereby harming his or her reputation, it shall be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.  
If the basic content of the article is false, thereby harming the reputation of another person, it shall be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.

九、问：因文学作品引起的名誉权纠纷，应如何认定是否构成侵权？  
答：撰写、发表文学作品，不是以生活中特定的人为描写对象，仅是作品的情节与生活中某人的情况相似，不应认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
描写真人真事的文学作品，对特定人进行侮辱、诽谤或者披露隐私损害其名誉的；或者虽未写明真实姓各和住址，但事实是以特定人或者特定人的特定事实为描写对象，文中有侮辱诽谤或者披露隐私的内容，致其名誉受到损害的，应认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
编辑出版单位在作品已被认定为侵害他人名誉权或者被告知明显属于侵害他人名誉权后，应刊登声明消除影响或者采取其他补救措施；拒不刊登声明，不采取其他补救措施，或者继续刊登、出版侵权作品的，应认定为侵权。

**Article None** 9. Q: How shall it be determined whether infringement is constituted in a dispute over the right to reputation arising from literary works?  
A: If a literary work is written or published not with a specific person in real life as the object of description, and only the plot of the work is similar to the situation of a certain person in real life, it shall not be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.  
If a literary work describing real persons and real events insults, defames, or discloses the privacy of a specific person, thereby harming his or her reputation; or if, although the true name and address are not stated, the work in fact takes a specific person or specific facts of a specific person as the object of description and contains content insulting, defaming, or disclosing privacy, thereby causing harm to his or her reputation, it shall be determined as infringement of the right to reputation.  
After a work has been determined to infringe the right to reputation of another person, or after the editing and publishing unit has been informed that the work obviously infringes the right to reputation of another person, the editing and publishing unit shall publish a statement to eliminate the adverse effects or take other remedial measures. If it refuses to publish the statement, refuses to take other remedial measures, or continues to publish or issue the infringing work, this shall be determined as infringement.

十、问：侵害名誉权的责任承担形式如何掌握？  
答：人民法院依照《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百二十条和第一百三十四条的规定，可以责令侵权人停止侵害、恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失。  
恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉可以书面或者口头的方式进行，内容须事先经人民法院审查。  
恢复名誉、消除影响的范围，一般应与侵权所造成不良影响的范围相当。  
公民、法人因名誉权受到侵害要求赔偿的，侵权人应赔偿侵权行为造成的经济损失；公民并提出精神损害赔偿要求的，人民法院可根据侵权人的过错程度、侵权行为的具体情节、给受害人造成精神损害的后果等情况酌定。

**Article None** 10. Q: How shall the forms of liability for infringement of the right to reputation be applied?  
A: In accordance with the provisions of Articles 120 and 134 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court may order the infringer to cease the infringement, restore the reputation, eliminate the adverse effects, make an apology, and compensate for losses.  
Restoration of reputation, elimination of adverse effects, and making an apology may be done in written or oral form, and the content shall be reviewed in advance by the People's Court.  
The scope of restoration of reputation and elimination of adverse effects shall generally be commensurate with the scope of the adverse effects caused by the infringement.  
If a citizen or legal person, whose right to reputation has been infringed, claims compensation, the infringer shall compensate for the economic losses caused by the infringement. If the citizen also claims compensation for mental damages, the People's Court may consider factors such as the degree of fault of the infringer, the specific circumstances of the infringement, and the consequences of mental harm caused to the victim, and determine the amount at its discretion.

十一、问：侵权人不执行生效判决，不为对方恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉的，应如何处理？  
答：侵权人拒不执行生效判决，不为对方恢复名誉、消除影响的，人民法院可以采取公告、登报等方式，将判决的主要内容和有关情况公布于众，费用由被执行人负担，并可依照民事诉讼法第一百零二条第六项的规定处理。  
1993年8月7日

**Article None** 11. Q: If the infringer refuses to execute an effective judgment and fails to restore the reputation of the other party, eliminate the adverse effects, or make an apology, how shall the matter be handled?  
A: If the infringer refuses to execute an effective judgment and fails to restore the reputation of the other party or eliminate the adverse effects, the People's Court may adopt methods such as issuing a public announcement or publishing the judgment in a newspaper to publicize the main contents of the judgment and the relevant circumstances. The costs shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement, and the matter may be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 102(6) of the Civil Procedure Law.  
August 7, 1993
