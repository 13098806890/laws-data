# 最高人民法院关于内地与澳门特别行政区就仲裁程序相互协助保全的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕7号  
**公布日期**：2022-02-15  
**生效日期**：2022-03-25

> 《最高人民法院关于内地与澳门特别行政区就仲裁程序相互协助保全的安排》已于2022年2月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1864次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年3月25日起施行。 2022年2月24日 最高人民法院 关于内地与澳门特别行政区就仲裁程序 相互协助保全的安排 法释〔2022〕7号 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，经最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区协商，现就内地与澳门特别行政区关于仲裁程序相互协助保全作出如下安排。 第一条　本安排所称“保全”，在内地包括财产保全、证据保全、行为保全；在澳门特别行政区包括为确保受威胁的权利得以实现而采取的保存或者预行措施。 第二条　按照澳门特别行政区仲裁法规向澳门特别行政区仲裁机构提起民商事仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以参照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国仲裁法》以及相关司法解释的规定，向被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地的内地中级人民法院申请保全。被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地在不同人民法院辖区的，应当选择向其中一个人民法院提出申请，不得分别向两个或者两个以上人民法院提出申请。 在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件前申请保全，内地人民法院采取保全措施后三十日内未收到仲裁机构已受理仲裁案件的证明函件的，内地人民法院应当解除保全。 第三条　向内地人民法院申请保全的，应当提交下列材料： （一）保全申请书； （二）仲裁协议； （三）身份证明材料：申请人为自然人的，应当提交身份证件复印件；申请人为法人或者非法人组织的，应当提交注册登记证书的复印件以及法定代表人或者负责人的身份证件复印件； （四）在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件后申请保全的，应当提交包含主要仲裁请求和所根据的事实与理由的仲裁申请文件以及相关证据材料、仲裁机构出具的已受理有关仲裁案件的证明函件；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本安排所称“保全”，在内地包括财产保全、证据保全、行为保全；在澳门特别行政区包括为确保受威胁的权利得以实现而采取的保存或者预行措施。

**Article 1** : "Preservation" as referred to in this Arrangement includes property preservation, evidence preservation, and conduct preservation in the Mainland; in the Macao Special Administrative Region, it includes preservation or provisional measures taken to ensure the realization of threatened rights.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　按照澳门特别行政区仲裁法规向澳门特别行政区仲裁机构提起民商事仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以参照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国仲裁法》以及相关司法解释的规定，向被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地的内地中级人民法院申请保全。被申请人住所地、财产所在地或者证据所在地在不同人民法院辖区的，应当选择向其中一个人民法院提出申请，不得分别向两个或者两个以上人民法院提出申请。  
在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件前申请保全，内地人民法院采取保全措施后三十日内未收到仲裁机构已受理仲裁案件的证明函件的，内地人民法院应当解除保全。

**Article 2** : A party that initiates civil or commercial arbitration proceedings with an arbitration institution in the Macao Special Administrative Region in accordance with the arbitration laws and regulations of the Macao Special Administrative Region may, before the arbitration award is made, apply to the Intermediate People's Court of the Mainland at the place where the respondent has its domicile, where the property is located, or where the evidence is located for preservation by reference to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China, and relevant judicial interpretations. If the respondent's domicile, property location, or evidence location is within the jurisdiction of different people's courts, the application shall be filed with one of those people's courts, and shall not be filed separately with two or more people's courts.  
If an application for preservation is made before the arbitration institution accepts the arbitration case, and the Mainland People's Court does not receive a certificate letter confirming that the arbitration institution has accepted the arbitration case within thirty days after taking preservation measures, the Mainland People's Court shall dissolve the preservation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　向内地人民法院申请保全的，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）保全申请书；  
（二）仲裁协议；  
（三）身份证明材料：申请人为自然人的，应当提交身份证件复印件；申请人为法人或者非法人组织的，应当提交注册登记证书的复印件以及法定代表人或者负责人的身份证件复印件；  
（四）在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件后申请保全的，应当提交包含主要仲裁请求和所根据的事实与理由的仲裁申请文件以及相关证据材料、仲裁机构出具的已受理有关仲裁案件的证明函件；  
（五）内地人民法院要求的其他材料。  
身份证明材料系在内地以外形成的，应当依据内地相关法律规定办理证明手续。  
向内地人民法院提交的文件没有中文文本的，应当提交中文译本。

**Article 3** : An application for preservation to a Mainland People's Court shall submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for preservation;  
(2) the arbitration agreement;  
(3) identity documents: if the applicant is a natural person, a copy of the identity document shall be submitted; if the applicant is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, copies of the registration certificate and the identity documents of the legal representative or responsible person shall be submitted;  
(4) if the application for preservation is made after the arbitration institution has accepted the arbitration case, the arbitration application documents containing the main arbitration claims and the facts and grounds on which they are based, as well as relevant evidentiary materials, and a certificate letter issued by the arbitration institution confirming that it has accepted the arbitration case shall be submitted;  
(5) other materials required by the Mainland People's Court.  
If the identity documents are issued outside the Mainland, the certification formalities shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations of the Mainland.  
If the documents submitted to the Mainland People's Court are not in Chinese, a Chinese translation shall be submitted.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　向内地人民法院提交的保全申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况：当事人为自然人的，包括姓名、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；当事人为法人或者非法人组织的，包括法人或者非法人组织的名称、住所以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；  
（二）请求事项，包括申请保全财产的数额、申请行为保全的内容和期限等；  
（三）请求所依据的事实、理由和相关证据，包括关于情况紧急，如不立即保全将会使申请人合法权益受到难以弥补的损害或者将使仲裁裁决难以执行的说明等；  
（四）申请保全的财产、证据的明确信息或者具体线索；  
（五）用于提供担保的内地财产信息或者资信证明；  
（六）是否已提出其他保全申请以及保全情况；  
（七）其他需要载明的事项。

**Article 4** : A written application for preservation submitted to a Mainland People's Court shall state the following matters:  
(1) basic information of the parties: if the party is a natural person, including the name, domicile, identity document information, means of communication, etc.; if the party is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, including the name, domicile of the legal person or unincorporated organization, and the name, position, domicile, identity document information, means of communication of the legal representative or principal responsible person, etc.;  
(2) the claims, including the amount of property for which preservation is sought, the content and duration of the conduct preservation sought, etc.;  
(3) the facts, grounds, and relevant evidence on which the claims are based, including an explanation of urgent circumstances such that failure to preserve immediately would cause irreparable damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or would make it difficult to enforce the arbitration award, etc.;  
(4) clear information or specific clues regarding the property or evidence for which preservation is sought;  
(5) information on property in the Mainland or creditworthiness certificates for providing security;  
(6) whether any other preservation applications have been made and the status of such preservation;  
(7) other matters that need to be stated.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》向内地仲裁机构提起民商事仲裁程序的当事人，在仲裁裁决作出前，可以根据澳门特别行政区法律规定，向澳门特别行政区初级法院申请保全。  
在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件前申请保全的，申请人应当在澳门特别行政区法律规定的期间内，采取开展仲裁程序的必要措施，否则该保全措施失效。申请人应当将已作出必要措施及作出日期的证明送交澳门特别行政区法院。

**Article 5** : A party that initiates civil or commercial arbitration proceedings with an arbitration institution in the Mainland in accordance with the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China may, before the arbitration award is made, apply to the Court of First Instance of the Macao Special Administrative Region for preservation in accordance with the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
If an application for preservation is made before the arbitration institution accepts the arbitration case, the applicant shall, within the time limit prescribed by the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region, take necessary measures to initiate the arbitration proceedings; otherwise, the preservation measure shall cease to have effect. The applicant shall submit to the court of the Macao Special Administrative Region proof that the necessary measures have been taken and the date on which they were taken.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　向澳门特别行政区法院申请保全的，须附同下列资料：  
（一）仲裁协议；  
（二）申请人或者被申请人为自然人的，应当载明其姓名以及住所；为法人或者非法人组织的，应当载明其名称、住所以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务和住所；  
（三）请求的详细资料，尤其包括请求所依据的事实和法律理由、申请标的的情况、财产的详细资料、须保全的金额、申请行为保全的详细内容和期限以及附同相关证据，证明权利受威胁以及解释恐防受侵害的理由；  
（四）在仲裁机构受理仲裁案件后申请保全的，应当提交该仲裁机构出具的已受理有关仲裁案件的证明；  
（五）是否已提出其他保全申请以及保全情况；  
（六）法院要求的其他资料。  
如向法院提交的文件并非使用澳门特别行政区的其中一种正式语文，则申请人应当提交其中一种正式语文的译本。

**Article 6** : An application for preservation to a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be accompanied by the following materials:  
(1) the arbitration agreement;  
(2) if the applicant or the respondent is a natural person, the name and domicile thereof shall be stated; if it is a legal person or an unincorporated organization, the name, domicile thereof, and the name, position, and domicile of the legal representative or principal responsible person shall be stated;  
(3) detailed information on the claim, in particular the facts and legal grounds on which the claim is based, the subject matter of the application, details of the property, the amount to be preserved, the detailed content and duration of the conduct preservation sought, together with relevant evidence to prove that the right is threatened and to explain the grounds for fearing infringement;  
(4) if the application for preservation is made after the arbitration institution has accepted the arbitration case, a certificate issued by the arbitration institution confirming that it has accepted the arbitration case shall be submitted;  
(5) whether any other preservation applications have been made and the status of such preservation;  
(6) other materials required by the court.  
If the documents submitted to the court are not in one of the official languages of the Macao Special Administrative Region, the applicant shall submit a translation into one of the official languages.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被请求方法院应当尽快审查当事人的保全申请，可以按照被请求方法律规定要求申请人提供担保。  
经审查，当事人的保全申请符合被请求方法律规定的，被请求方法院应当作出保全裁定。

**Article 7** : The court of the requested party shall examine the party's application for preservation as soon as possible, and may require the applicant to provide security in accordance with the laws of the requested party.  
Upon examination, if the party's application for preservation complies with the laws of the requested party, the court of the requested party shall render a ruling on preservation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人对被请求方法院的裁定不服的，按被请求方相关法律规定处理。

**Article 8** : If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling of the court of the requested party, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws of the requested party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人申请保全的，应当根据被请求方法律的规定交纳费用。

**Article 9** : A party applying for preservation shall pay fees in accordance with the laws of the requested party.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本安排不减损内地和澳门特别行政区的仲裁机构、仲裁庭、仲裁员、当事人依据对方法律享有的权利。

**Article 10** : This Arrangement shall not prejudice the rights enjoyed by the arbitration institutions, arbitral tribunals, arbitrators, and parties of the Mainland and the Macao Special Administrative Region under the laws of the other party.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本安排在执行过程中遇有问题或者需要修改的，由最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区协商解决。

**Article 11** : Any problems encountered during the implementation of this Arrangement or the need for amendment shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本安排自2022年3月25日起施行。

**Article 12** : This Arrangement shall come into force as of March 25, 2022.
