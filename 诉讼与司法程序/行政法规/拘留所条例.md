# 拘留所条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2012-02-23  
**生效日期**：2012-04-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范拘留所的设置和管理，惩戒和教育被拘留人，保护被拘留人的合法权益，根据有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws for the purposes of regulating the establishment and management of detention centers, disciplining and educating detainees, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of detainees.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对下列人员的拘留在拘留所执行：  
(一)被公安机关、国家安全机关依法给予拘留行政处罚的人；  
(二)被人民法院依法决定拘留的人。

**Article 2** Detention of the following persons shall be executed in a detention center:  
(1) persons who are subject to detention as an administrative penalty lawfully imposed by a public security organ or a national security authority;  
(2) persons whose detention is lawfully decided by a People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　拘留所应当依法保障被拘留人的人身安全和合法权益，不得侮辱、体罚、虐待被拘留人或者指使、纵容他人侮辱、体罚、虐待被拘留人。  
被拘留人应当遵守法律、行政法规和拘留所的管理规定，服从管理，接受教育。

**Article 3** A detention center shall, according to law, protect the personal safety and lawful rights and interests of detainees, and shall not insult, physically punish, maltreat detainees, or instigate or connive at others to insult, physically punish, or maltreat detainees.  
Detainees shall abide by the laws, administrative regulations, and the management rules of the detention center, submit to management, and receive education.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院公安部门主管全国拘留所的管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府公安机关主管本行政区域拘留所的管理工作。

**Article 4** The public security department under the State Council shall be in charge of the management of detention centers nationwide. The public security organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the management of detention centers within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　拘留所

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上地方人民政府根据需要设置拘留所。拘留所的设置和撤销，由县级以上地方人民政府公安机关提出意见，按照规定的权限和程序审批。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish detention centers as needed. The establishment and dissolution of a detention center shall be proposed by the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall be examined and approved in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　拘留所应当按照规定的建设标准，设置拘留区、行政办公区等功能区域。

**Article 6** A detention center shall, in accordance with the prescribed construction standards, set up functional areas such as a detention area and an administrative office area.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拘留所依照规定配备武器、警械，配备交通、通讯、技术防范、医疗和消防等装备和设施。

**Article 7** A detention center shall, in accordance with the provisions, be equipped with weapons and police instruments, and with equipment and facilities for transportation, communication, technical prevention, medical treatment, firefighting, etc.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　拘留所所需经费列入本级人民政府财政预算。

**Article 8** The funds needed by a detention center shall be included in the fiscal budget of the people's government at the same level.

### 第三章　拘留

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　拘留所应当凭拘留决定机关的拘留决定文书及时收拘被拘留人。需要异地收拘的，拘留决定机关应当出具相关法律文书和需要异地收拘的书面说明，并经异地拘留所主管公安机关批准。

**Article 9** A detention center shall promptly detain a person upon the written decision on detention issued by the organ deciding the detention. If detention in a different place is required, the organ deciding the detention shall issue the relevant legal documents and a written explanation of the need for detention in a different place, and shall obtain the approval of the public security organ in charge of the detention center in that different place.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　拘留所收拘被拘留人，应当告知被拘留人依法享有的权利和应当遵守的规定。  
拘留所收拘被拘留人后，拘留决定机关应当及时通知被拘留人家属。

**Article 10** When detaining a detainee, a detention center shall inform the detainee of the rights that he or she enjoys according to law and the rules that he or she shall observe.  
After a detainee is detained in a detention center, the organ deciding the detention shall promptly notify the detainee's family members.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　拘留所收拘被拘留人，应当对被拘留人的人身和携带的物品进行检查。被拘留人的非生活必需品及现金由拘留所登记并统一保管。检查发现的违禁品和其他与案件有关的物品应当移交拘留决定机关依法处理。  
对女性被拘留人的人身检查应当由女性人民警察进行。

**Article 11** When detaining a detainee, a detention center shall conduct a search of the detainee's person and the articles he or she carries. Non-necessities of everyday life and cash belonging to the detainee shall be registered and kept in unified custody by the detention center. Contraband and other articles related to the case found during the search shall be handed over to the organ deciding the detention for handling according to law.  
A body search of a female detainee shall be conducted by female people's police officers.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　拘留所发现被拘留人可能被错误拘留的，应当通知拘留决定机关，拘留决定机关应当在24小时内作出处理决定；对依照[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第二十一条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-21)的规定不应当被执行拘留的，拘留所不予收拘，并通知拘留决定机关。

**Article 12** If a detention center discovers that a detainee may have been wrongfully detained, it shall notify the organ deciding the detention, and the organ deciding the detention shall make a decision on handling within 24 hours; if, in accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments, the person shall not be subjected to detention, the detention center shall not detain him or her and shall notify the organ deciding the detention.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拘留所发现被拘留人吸食、注射毒品成瘾的，应当给予必要的戒毒治疗，并提请拘留所的主管公安机关对被拘留人依法作出社区戒毒或者强制隔离戒毒的决定。

**Article 13** If a detention center discovers that a detainee is addicted to drug inhalation or injection, the detention center shall provide necessary drug rehabilitation treatment and shall request the public security organ in charge of the detention center to make a decision on community drug rehabilitation or compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation against the detainee according to law.

### 第四章　管理教育

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　拘留所应当建立值班巡视制度和突发事件应急机制。值班巡视人员应当严守岗位，发现问题及时报告并妥善处理。  
拘留所应当安装监控录像设备，对被拘留人进行安全监控。

**Article 14** A detention center shall establish a duty patrol system and an emergency response mechanism. The personnel on duty patrol shall strictly keep their posts, and shall timely report and properly handle any problems discovered.  
A detention center shall install surveillance video equipment to carry out safety monitoring of detainees.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　拘留所应当根据被拘留人的性别、是否成年以及其他管理的需要，对被拘留人实行分别拘押和管理。  
对女性被拘留人的直接管理应当由女性人民警察进行。

**Article 15** A detention center shall, based on the gender, whether the detainee is an adult, and other management needs, carry out separate detention and management of detainees.  
Direct management of female detainees shall be conducted by female people's police officers.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　拘留所应当建立被拘留人管理档案。

**Article 16** A detention center shall establish management files for detainees.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　拘留所应当按照规定的标准为被拘留人提供饮食，并尊重被拘留人的民族饮食习惯。

**Article 17** A detention center shall provide food and drink for detainees in accordance with the prescribed standards, and shall respect the ethnic dietary habits of detainees.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　拘留所应当建立医疗卫生防疫制度，做好防病、防疫、治疗工作。  
拘留所对患病的被拘留人应当及时治疗。被拘留人患病需要出所治疗的，由拘留所所长批准，并派人民警察管理；被拘留人患有传染病需要隔离治疗的，拘留所应当采取隔离治疗措施。  
被拘留人病情严重的，拘留所应当立即采取急救措施并通知被拘留人的亲属。

**Article 18** A detention center shall establish a medical and health epidemic prevention system, and shall carry out disease prevention, epidemic prevention, and treatment work well.  
A detention center shall provide timely treatment for detainees who are ill. If a detainee is ill and needs to leave the detention center for treatment, the director of the detention center shall approve it, and people's police officers shall be assigned to manage the detainee; if a detainee has an infectious disease and needs isolation treatment, the detention center shall take isolation treatment measures.  
If a detainee is seriously ill, the detention center shall immediately take first aid measures and notify the detainee's relatives.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　拘留所发现被拘留人有下列情形之一的，应当建议拘留决定机关作出停止执行拘留的决定：  
(一)患有精神病或者患有传染病需要隔离治疗的；  
(二)病情严重可能危及生命安全的。

**Article 19** If a detention center discovers that a detainee is in any of the following circumstances, it shall suggest that the organ deciding the detention make a decision to suspend the execution of detention:  
(1) suffering from mental illness or suffering from an infectious disease requiring isolation treatment;  
(2) the illness is serious and may endanger life safety.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　为被拘留人提供的拘留期间生活必需品应当由拘留所检查登记后转交被拘留人。非生活必需品，拘留所不予接收。

**Article 20** Necessities of everyday life provided for detainees during detention shall be inspected and registered by the detention center before being handed over to the detainees. Non-necessities of everyday life shall not be accepted by the detention center.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　拘留所应当对被拘留人进行法律、道德等教育，组织被拘留人开展适当的文体活动。  
拘留所应当保证被拘留人每日不少于2小时的拘室外活动时间。  
拘留所不得强迫被拘留人从事生产劳动。

**Article 21** A detention center shall provide education on law, morality, etc. to detainees, and organize detainees to engage in appropriate recreational and sports activities.  
A detention center shall ensure that detainees have no less than two hours of out-of-cell activity time each day.  
A detention center shall not compel detainees to engage in productive labor.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被拘留人检举、揭发违法犯罪行为经查证属实或者被拘留人制止违法犯罪行为的，拘留所应当予以表扬。

**Article 22** If a detainee reports or exposes illegal or criminal acts which are verified to be true, or if a detainee stops illegal or criminal acts, the detention center shall give commendation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被拘留人有下列违法行为之一的，拘留所可以予以训诫、责令具结悔过或者使用警械：  
(一)哄闹、打架斗殴的；  
(二)殴打、欺侮他人的；  
(三)故意损毁拘留所财物或者他人财物的；  
(四)预谋或者实施逃跑的；  
(五)严重违反管理的其他行为。  
拘留所人民警察对被拘留人使用警械应当经拘留所所长批准，并遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 23** If a detainee commits any of the following illegal acts, the detention center may give a reprimand, order a written pledge of repentance, or use police instruments:  
(1) creating disturbances, engaging in fights or affrays;  
(2) assaulting or bullying others;  
(3) intentionally damaging property of the detention center or others' property;  
(4) planning or carrying out an escape;  
(5) other acts that seriously violate management.  
The use of police instruments against a detainee by people's police officers of the detention center shall be approved by the director of the detention center, and shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被拘留人在拘留期间有新的违法犯罪嫌疑的，拘留所应当报告拘留所的主管公安机关处理；拘留所发现被拘留人收拘前有其他违法犯罪嫌疑的，应当通知拘留决定机关或者报告拘留所的主管公安机关处理。

**Article 24** If a detainee is suspected of committing new illegal or criminal acts during detention, the detention center shall report to the public security organ in charge of the detention center for handling; if the detention center discovers that a detainee was suspected of other illegal or criminal acts before detention, it shall notify the organ deciding the detention or report to the public security organ in charge of the detention center for handling.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　拘留所保障被拘留人在拘留期间的通信权利，被拘留人与他人的来往信件不受检查和扣押。被拘留人应当遵守拘留所的通信管理规定。

**Article 25** A detention center shall guarantee the right of a detainee to correspondence during detention, and incoming and outgoing letters between the detainee and others shall not be inspected or detained. Detainees shall comply with the detention center's management rules on correspondence.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　拘留所保障被拘留人在拘留期间的会见权利。被拘留人应当遵守拘留所的会见管理规定。  
会见被拘留人应当持有效身份证件按照规定的时间在拘留所的会见区进行。  
被拘留人委托的律师会见被拘留人还应当持律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和委托书或者法律援助公函。

**Article 26** A detention center shall guarantee the right of a detainee to visits during detention. Detainees shall comply with the detention center's management rules on visits.  
Visits to a detainee shall be conducted in the visiting area of the detention center with valid identity documents and at the prescribed times.  
A lawyer entrusted by a detainee who visits the detainee shall also hold a lawyer's practicing certificate, a certificate from the law firm, and a power of attorney or a legal aid letter.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被拘留人遇有参加升学考试、子女出生或者近亲属病危、死亡等情形的，被拘留人或者其近亲属可以提出请假出所的申请。  
请假出所的申请由拘留所提出审核意见，报拘留决定机关决定是否批准。拘留决定机关应当在被拘留人或者其近亲属提出申请的12小时内作出是否准予请假出所的决定。  
被拘留人请假出所的时间不计入拘留期限。

**Article 27** If a detainee encounters circumstances such as taking an entrance examination, the birth of his or her child, or the critical illness or death of a close relative, the detainee or his or her close relative may submit an application for leave outside the detention center.  
The application for leave outside the detention center shall be reviewed by the detention center to provide an opinion, and shall be submitted to the organ deciding the detention for decision on whether to approve it. The organ deciding the detention shall, within 12 hours of the submission of the application by the detainee or his or her close relative, make a decision on whether to grant leave outside the detention center.  
The period during which a detainee is on leave outside the detention center shall not be counted toward the detention term.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　被拘留人或者其近亲属提出请假出所申请的，应当向拘留决定机关提出担保人或者交纳保证金。有关担保人和保证金的管理按照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的有关规定执行。  
被拘留人请假出所不归的，由拘留决定机关负责带回拘留所执行拘留。

**Article 28** If a detainee or his or her close relative submits an application for leave outside the detention center, a guarantor shall be provided or a deposit shall be paid to the organ deciding the detention. The management of the guarantor and deposit shall be subject to the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments.  
If a detainee on leave outside the detention center fails to return, the organ deciding the detention shall be responsible for bringing the detainee back to the detention center for execution of detention.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　被拘留人提出举报、控告，申请行政复议，提起行政诉讼或者申请暂缓执行拘留的，拘留所应当在24小时内将有关材料转送有关机关，不得检查或者扣押。

**Article 29** If a detainee makes a report or accusation, applies for administrative reconsideration, initiates an administrative action, or applies for suspension of execution of detention, the detention center shall, within 24 hours, transfer the relevant materials to the relevant authorities, and shall not inspect or detain them.

### 第五章　解除拘留

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被拘留人拘留期满，拘留所应当按时解除拘留，发给解除拘留证明书，并返还代为保管的财物。

**Article 30** When a detainee's detention term expires, the detention center shall timely release the detainee, issue a certificate of release from detention, and return the property and money kept on behalf of the detainee.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被拘留人在解除拘留时有下列情形之一的，拘留所应当向有关机关或者单位移交被拘留人：  
(一)依法被决定驱逐出境、遣送出境的；  
(二)依法被决定执行刑事强制措施的；  
(三)依法被决定社区戒毒、强制隔离戒毒的；  
(四)依法被决定采取强制性教育矫治措施的。

**Article 31** If a detainee is in any of the following circumstances upon release from detention, the detention center shall hand over the detainee to the relevant authorities or units:  
(1) the detainee is lawfully decided to be expelled from the country or deported;  
(2) the detainee is lawfully decided to be subject to execution of criminal compulsory measures;  
(3) the detainee is lawfully decided to be subject to community drug rehabilitation or compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation;  
(4) the detainee is lawfully decided to be subject to compulsory educational corrective measures.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　执行拘留的时间以日为单位计算，从收拘当日到第2日为1日。

**Article 32** The period of execution of detention shall be calculated in days, with the day from the day of detention to the next day counting as one day.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家安全机关设置的拘留所，由国家安全机关依照本条例的规定进行管理。

**Article 33** Detention centers established by national security authorities shall be managed by the national security authorities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　被公安机关依法给予行政强制措施性质拘留的人在拘留所执行拘押，应当与[本条例第二条](#art-2)规定的被拘留人分别拘押，具体管理办法由国务院公安部门参照本条例规定。

**Article 34** Persons who are lawfully subject to detention as a compulsory administrative measure by a public security organ and are held in custody in a detention center shall be detained separately from the detainees specified in Article 2 of these Regulations, and the specific management measures shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2012年4月1日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force on April 1, 2012.
