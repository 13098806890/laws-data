# 森林采伐更新管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1987年8月25日国务院批准　1987年9月10日林业部发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为合理采伐森林，及时更新采伐迹地，恢复和扩大森林资源，根据《中华人民共和国森林法》(以下简称森林法)及有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　森林采伐更新要贯彻“以营林为基础，普遍护林，大力造林，采育结合，永续利用”的林业建设方针，执行森林经营方案，实行限额采伐，发挥森林的生态效益、经济效益和社会效益。 第三条　全民、集体所有的森林、林木和个人所有的林木采伐更新，必须遵守本办法。 第二章　森林采伐 第四条　森林采伐，包括主伐、抚育采伐、更新采伐和低产林改造。 第五条　采伐林木按照森林法实施条例第三十条规定，申请林木采伐许可证时，除提交其他必备的文件外，国营企业事业单位和部队还应当提交有关主管部门核定的年度木材生产计划；农村集体、个人还应当提交基层林业站核定的年度采伐指标。上年度进行采伐的，应当提交上年度的更新验收合格证。 第六条　林木采伐许可证的核发，按森林法及其实施条例的有关规定办理。授权核发林木采伐许可证，应当有书面文件。被授权核发林木采伐许可证的单位，应当配备熟悉业务的人员，并受授权单位监督。 国营林业局、国营林场根据林木采伐许可证、伐区设计文件和年度木材生产计划，向其基层经营单位拨交伐区，发给国有林林木采伐作业证。作业证格式由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门制定。 第七条　对用材林的成熟林和过熟林实行主伐。主要树种的主伐年龄，按《用材林主要树种主伐年龄表》的规定执行。定向培育的森林以及表内未列入树种的主伐年龄，由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门规定。 第八条　用材林的主伐方式为择伐、皆伐和渐伐。 中幼龄树木多的复层异龄林，应当实行择伐。择伐强度不得大于伐前林木蓄积量的40%，伐后林分郁闭度应当保留在0.5以上。伐后容易引起林木风倒、自然枯死的林分，择伐强度应当适当降低。两次择伐的间隔期不得少于一个龄级期。 成过熟单层林、中幼龄树木少的异龄林，应当实行皆伐。皆伐面积一次不得超过5公顷，坡度平缓、土壤肥沃、容易更新的林分，可以扩大到20公顷。在采伐带、采伐块之间，应当保留相当于皆伐面积的林带、林块。对保留的林带、林块，待采伐迹地上更新的幼树生长稳定后方可采伐。皆伐后依靠天然更新的，每公顷应当保留适当数量的单株或者群状母树。 天然更新能力强的成过熟单层林，应当实行渐伐。全部采伐更新过程不得超过一个龄级期。上层林木郁闭度较小，林内幼苗、幼树株数已经达到更新标准的，可进行二次渐伐，第一次采伐林木蓄积量的50%；上层林木郁闭度较大，林内幼苗、幼树株数达不到更新标准的，可进行三次渐伐，第一次采伐林木蓄积量的30%，第二次采伐保留林木蓄积的50%，第三次采伐应当在林内更新起来的幼树接近或者达到郁闭状态时进行。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为合理采伐森林，及时更新采伐迹地，恢复和扩大森林资源，根据《中华人民共和国森林法》(以下简称森林法)及有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of rationally harvesting forests, promptly regenerating harvested areas, and restoring and expanding forest resources, in accordance with the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Forest Law) and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　森林采伐更新要贯彻“以营林为基础，普遍护林，大力造林，采育结合，永续利用”的林业建设方针，执行森林经营方案，实行限额采伐，发挥森林的生态效益、经济效益和社会效益。

**Article 2** The harvesting and regeneration of forests shall implement the forestry construction policy of "taking forest management as the foundation, universally protecting forests, vigorously afforesting, combining harvesting with cultivation, and sustainable utilization," follow the forest management plan, practice quota-based harvesting, and bring into play the ecological, economic, and social benefits of forests.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　全民、集体所有的森林、林木和个人所有的林木采伐更新，必须遵守本办法。

**Article 3** The harvesting and regeneration of forests and trees owned by the whole people or collectives, and trees owned by individuals, must comply with these Measures.

### 第二章　森林采伐

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　森林采伐，包括主伐、抚育采伐、更新采伐和低产林改造。

**Article 4** Forest harvesting includes final cutting, tending cutting, regeneration cutting, and improvement of low-yield forests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　采伐林木按照[森林法实施条例第三十条](../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国森林法实施条例.md#art-30)规定，申请林木采伐许可证时，除提交其他必备的文件外，国营企业事业单位和部队还应当提交有关主管部门核定的年度木材生产计划；农村集体、个人还应当提交基层林业站核定的年度采伐指标。上年度进行采伐的，应当提交上年度的更新验收合格证。

**Article 5** When applying for a forest tree felling license in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the Regulations on the Implementation of the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China, in addition to submitting other necessary documents, state-owned enterprises, institutions, and military units shall also submit the annual timber production plan approved by the competent authority; rural collectives and individuals shall also submit the annual felling quota approved by the basic-level forestry station. If felling was conducted in the previous year, the regeneration acceptance certificate for the previous year shall be submitted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　林木采伐许可证的核发，按森林法及其实施条例的有关规定办理。授权核发林木采伐许可证，应当有书面文件。被授权核发林木采伐许可证的单位，应当配备熟悉业务的人员，并受授权单位监督。  
国营林业局、国营林场根据林木采伐许可证、伐区设计文件和年度木材生产计划，向其基层经营单位拨交伐区，发给国有林林木采伐作业证。作业证格式由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门制定。

**Article 6** The issuance of forest tree felling licenses shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Forest Law and its implementing regulations. Authorization to issue forest tree felling licenses shall be in writing. The unit authorized to issue forest tree felling licenses shall be staffed with personnel familiar with the work and shall be subject to the supervision of the authorizing unit.  
State-owned forestry bureaus and state-owned forest farms shall, on the basis of the forest tree felling license, the cutting area design documents, and the annual timber production plan, allocate cutting areas to their local operating units and issue state-owned forest tree felling operation certificates. The format of the operation certificate shall be prescribed by the competent forestry authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对用材林的成熟林和过熟林实行主伐。主要树种的主伐年龄，按《用材林主要树种主伐年龄表》的规定执行。定向培育的森林以及表内未列入树种的主伐年龄，由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门规定。

**Article 7** Final cutting shall be carried out on mature and over-mature timber forests. The final cutting ages for major tree species shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the "Table of Final Cutting Ages for Major Tree Species in Timber Forests." The final cutting ages for forests under target-oriented cultivation and for tree species not listed in the table shall be prescribed by the competent forestry authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　用材林的主伐方式为择伐、皆伐和渐伐。  
中幼龄树木多的复层异龄林，应当实行择伐。择伐强度不得大于伐前林木蓄积量的40%，伐后林分郁闭度应当保留在0.5以上。伐后容易引起林木风倒、自然枯死的林分，择伐强度应当适当降低。两次择伐的间隔期不得少于一个龄级期。  
成过熟单层林、中幼龄树木少的异龄林，应当实行皆伐。皆伐面积一次不得超过5公顷，坡度平缓、土壤肥沃、容易更新的林分，可以扩大到20公顷。在采伐带、采伐块之间，应当保留相当于皆伐面积的林带、林块。对保留的林带、林块，待采伐迹地上更新的幼树生长稳定后方可采伐。皆伐后依靠天然更新的，每公顷应当保留适当数量的单株或者群状母树。  
天然更新能力强的成过熟单层林，应当实行渐伐。全部采伐更新过程不得超过一个龄级期。上层林木郁闭度较小，林内幼苗、幼树株数已经达到更新标准的，可进行二次渐伐，第一次采伐林木蓄积量的50%；上层林木郁闭度较大，林内幼苗、幼树株数达不到更新标准的，可进行三次渐伐，第一次采伐林木蓄积量的30%，第二次采伐保留林木蓄积的50%，第三次采伐应当在林内更新起来的幼树接近或者达到郁闭状态时进行。  
毛竹林采伐后每公顷应当保留的健壮母竹，不得少于2000株。

**Article 8** The methods of final cutting for timber forests are selection cutting, clear cutting, and shelterwood cutting.  
For multi-storied uneven-aged forests with many young and middle-aged trees, selection cutting shall be practiced. The intensity of selection cutting shall not be greater than 40% of the standing volume before cutting, and the stand canopy density after cutting shall remain above 0.5. For stands where windthrow or natural dieback is likely to occur after cutting, the intensity of selection cutting shall be appropriately reduced. The interval between two selection cuttings shall not be less than one age class period.  
For mature and over-mature single-storied forests and uneven-aged forests with few young and middle-aged trees, clear cutting shall be practiced. The area of one clear cutting shall not exceed 5 hectares, but for stands with gentle slopes, fertile soil, and easy regeneration, it may be expanded to 20 hectares. Between cutting strips or cutting blocks, forest strips or blocks equivalent to the clear-cut area shall be retained. The retained forest strips or blocks shall not be cut until the young trees regenerated on the clear-cut area have become stable. If natural regeneration is relied upon after clear cutting, an appropriate number of individual or group seed trees per hectare shall be retained.  
For mature and over-mature single-storied forests with strong natural regeneration capacity, shelterwood cutting shall be practiced. The entire process of cutting and regeneration shall not exceed one age class period. If the canopy density of the overstory trees is low and the number of seedlings and saplings in the forest has reached the regeneration standard, a two-cut shelterwood cutting may be conducted, with the first cut removing 50% of the standing volume; if the canopy density of the overstory trees is high and the number of seedlings and saplings in the forest has not reached the regeneration standard, a three-cut shelterwood cutting may be conducted, with the first cut removing 30% of the standing volume, the second cut removing 50% of the remaining standing volume, and the third cut to be carried out when the regenerated young trees in the forest are approaching or have reached the canopy closure state.  
For Moso bamboo forests, the number of healthy mother bamboos retained per hectare after cutting shall not be less than 2,000.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对下列森林只准进行抚育和更新采伐：  
(一)大型水库、湖泊周围山脊以内和平地150米以内的森林，干渠的护岸林。  
(二)大江、大河两岸150米以内，以及大江、大河主要支流两岸50米以内的森林；在此范围内有山脊的，以第一层山脊为界。  
(三)铁路两侧各100米、公路干线两侧各50米以内的森林；在此范围内有山脊的，以第一层山脊为界。  
(四)高山森林分布上限以下150米至200米以内的森林。  
(五)生长在坡陡和岩石裸露地方的森林。

**Article 9** For the following forests, only tending and regeneration cutting is permitted:  
(1) Forests within the ridges surrounding large reservoirs and lakes and within 150 meters of flat land, and the bank protection forests of main canals.  
(2) Forests within 150 meters on both sides of major rivers and within 50 meters on both sides of major tributaries of major rivers; where there is a ridge within this range, the first ridge shall serve as the boundary.  
(3) Forests within 100 meters on both sides of railways and within 50 meters on both sides of main highways; where there is a ridge within this range, the first ridge shall serve as the boundary.  
(4) Forests within 150 to 200 meters below the upper limit of alpine forest distribution.  
(5) Forests growing on steep slopes and exposed rock areas.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　防护林和特种用途林中的国防林、母树林、环境保护林、风景林的更新采伐技术规程，由林业部会同有关部门制定。  
薪炭林、经济林的采伐技术规程，由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门制定。

**Article 10** The technical regulations for regeneration cutting of shelter forests and special-purpose forests, including national defense forests, seed production forests, environmental protection forests, and scenic forests, shall be formulated by the Ministry of Forestry in conjunction with relevant departments.  
The technical regulations for cutting of fuelwood forests and economic forests shall be formulated by the competent forestry authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　幼龄林、中龄林的抚育采伐，包括透光抚育、生长抚育、综合抚育；低产林的改造，包括局部改造和全面改造，其具体办法按照林业部发布的有关技术规程执行。

**Article 11** Tending cutting of young and middle-aged forests includes light tending, growth tending, and comprehensive tending; improvement of low-yield forests includes partial improvement and comprehensive improvement, and the specific methods shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant technical regulations issued by the Ministry of Forestry.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国营林业局和国营、集体林场的采伐作业，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)按林木采伐许可证和伐区设计进行采伐，不得越界采伐或者遗弃应当采伐的林木。  
(二)择伐和渐伐作业实行采伐木挂号，先伐除病腐木、风折木、枯立木以及影响目的树种生长和无生长前途的树木，保留生长健壮、经济价值高的树木。  
(三)控制树倒方向，固定集材道，保护幼苗、幼树、母树和其他保留树木。依靠天然更新的，伐后林地上幼苗、幼树株数保存率应当达到60%以上。  
(四)采伐的木材长度2米以上，小头直径不小于8厘米的，全部运出利用；伐根高度不得超过10厘米。  
(五)伐区内的采伐剩余物和藤条、灌木，在不影响森林更新的原则下，采取保留、利用、火烧、堆集或者截短散铺方法清理。  
(六)对容易引起水土冲刷的集材主道，应当采取防护措施。  
其他单位和个人的采伐作业，参照上述规定执行。

**Article 12** The cutting operations of state-owned forestry bureaus and state-owned and collective forest farms shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Cutting shall be carried out in accordance with the forest tree felling license and the cutting area design, and no cross-boundary cutting or abandonment of trees that should be cut shall be allowed.  
(2) In selection cutting and shelterwood cutting operations, trees to be cut shall be marked in advance, and diseased, rotten, wind-broken, dead standing trees, as well as trees that affect the growth of target species or have no growth potential, shall be removed first, while healthy and economically valuable trees shall be retained.  
(3) The direction of tree fall shall be controlled, skid trails shall be fixed, and seedlings, saplings, mother trees, and other retained trees shall be protected. Where natural regeneration is relied upon, the preservation rate of seedlings and saplings on the cutover land after cutting shall reach over 60%.  
(4) All timber with a length of 2 meters or more and a small-end diameter of not less than 8 centimeters shall be transported out for utilization; the height of stumps shall not exceed 10 centimeters.  
(5) Cutting residues, vines, and shrubs within the cutting area shall be cleared by methods such as retention, utilization, burning, piling, or cutting and scattering, provided that forest regeneration is not affected.  
(6) Protective measures shall be taken for main skid trails that are prone to causing soil erosion.  
The cutting operations of other units and individuals shall be carried out with reference to the above provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　森林采伐后，核发林木采伐许可证的部门应当对采伐作业质量组织检查验收，签发采伐作业质量验收证明。验收证明格式由省、自治区、直辖市林业主管部门制定。

**Article 13** After forest felling, the department that issued the forest tree felling license shall organize an inspection and acceptance of the quality of cutting operations and issue a certificate of acceptance of cutting operation quality. The format of the acceptance certificate shall be prescribed by the competent forestry authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　森林更新

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　采伐林木的单位和个人，应当按照优先发展人工更新，人工更新、人工促进天然更新、天然更新相结合的原则，在采伐后的当年或者次年内必须完成更新造林任务。

**Article 14** The units and individuals that fell forest trees shall, in accordance with the principle of giving priority to artificial regeneration and combining artificial regeneration, artificially promoted natural regeneration, and natural regeneration, complete the regeneration and afforestation task within the year of felling or the following year.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　更新质量必须达到以下标准：  
(一)人工更新，当年成活率应当不低于85%，3年后保存率应当不低于80%。  
(二)人工促进天然更新，补植、补播后的成活率和保存率达到人工更新的标准；天然下种前整地的，达到本条第三项规定的天然更新标准。  
(三)天然更新，每公顷皆伐迹地应当保留健壮目的树种幼树不少于3000株或者幼苗不少于6000株，更新均匀度应当不低于60%。择伐、渐伐迹地的更新质量，达到[本办法第八条](#art-8)第二款、第四款规定的标准。

**Article 15** The quality of regeneration must meet the following standards:  
(1) For artificial regeneration, the survival rate in the current year shall be not less than 85%, and the preservation rate after three years shall be not less than 80%.  
(2) For artificially promoted natural regeneration, the survival rate and preservation rate after supplementary planting or seeding shall meet the standards for artificial regeneration; where site preparation is conducted before natural seeding, the natural regeneration standards specified in paragraph (3) of this Article shall be met.  
(3) For natural regeneration, each hectare of clear-cut land shall retain not less than 3,000 healthy target species saplings or not less than 6,000 seedlings, and the regeneration uniformity shall be not less than 60%. The regeneration quality of selection cutting and shelterwood cutting sites shall meet the standards specified in paragraphs 2 and 4 of Article 8 of these Measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　未更新的旧采伐迹地、火烧迹地、林中空地、水湿地等宜林荒山荒地，应当由森林经营单位制定规划，限期完成更新造林。

**Article 16** For old cutover lands, burned areas, forest gaps, waterlogged lands, and other barren mountains and wastelands suitable for afforestation that have not been regenerated, the forest management units shall formulate plans and complete regeneration and afforestation within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人工更新和造林应当执行林业部发布的有关造林规程，做到适地适树、细致整地、良种壮苗、密度合理、精心栽植、适时抚育。在立地条件好的地方，应当培育速生丰产林。

**Article 17** Artificial regeneration and afforestation shall implement the relevant afforestation regulations issued by the Ministry of Forestry, ensuring site-species matching, meticulous site preparation, improved varieties and strong seedlings, reasonable density, careful planting, and timely tending. In places with good site conditions, fast-growing and high-yield forests shall be cultivated.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　森林更新后，核发林木采伐许可证的部门应当组织更新单位对更新面积和质量进行检查验收，核发更新验收合格证。

**Article 18** After forest regeneration, the department that issued the forest tree felling license shall organize the regeneration units to inspect and accept the regeneration area and quality, and issue a regeneration acceptance certificate.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列行为之一的，依照[森林法第三十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国森林法.md#art-39)和森林法实施条例的有关规定处罚：  
(一)国营企业事业单位和集体所有制单位未取得林木采伐许可证，擅自采伐林木的，或者年木材产量超过采伐许可证规定数量5%的；  
(二)国营企业事业单位不按批准的采伐设计文件进行采伐作业的面积占批准的作业面积5%以上的；  
集体所有制单位按照林木采伐许可证的规定进行采伐时，不符合采伐质量要求的作业面积占批准的作业面积5%以上的；  
(三)个人未取得林木采伐许可证，擅自采伐林木的，或者违反林木采伐许可证规定的采伐数量、地点、方式、树种，采伐的林木超过半立方米的。

**Article 19** Any of the following acts shall be punished in accordance with Article 39 of the Forest Law and the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Implementation of the Forest Law:  
(1) A state-owned enterprise, institution, or collectively-owned unit fells trees without obtaining a forest tree felling license, or the annual timber output exceeds the amount specified in the felling license by 5%;  
(2) A state-owned enterprise or institution carries out cutting operations not in accordance with the approved cutting design documents, and the area of such operations accounts for 5% or more of the approved operation area;  
When a collectively-owned unit carries out cutting in accordance with the provisions of the forest tree felling license, the area of operations that fails to meet the cutting quality requirements accounts for 5% or more of the approved operation area;  
(3) An individual fells trees without obtaining a forest tree felling license, or violates the felling quantity, location, method, or tree species specified in the forest tree felling license, and the volume of trees felled exceeds 0.5 cubic meters.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　盗伐、滥伐林木数量较大，不便计算补种株数的，可按盗伐、滥伐木材数量折算面积，并根据[森林法第三十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国森林法.md#art-39)规定的处罚原则，责令限期营造相应面积的新林。

**Article 20** Where the volume of trees illegally cut or excessively cut is relatively large and it is inconvenient to calculate the number of trees to be replanted, the area may be converted on the basis of the volume of illegally cut or excessively cut timber, and, in accordance with the punishment principles specified in Article 39 of the Forest Law, the person responsible shall be ordered to afforest an area of new forest corresponding to that area within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　无证采伐或者超过林木采伐许可证规定数量的木材，应当从下年度木材生产计划或者采伐指标中扣除。

**Article 21** Timber cut without a license or in excess of the amount specified in the forest tree felling license shall be deducted from the timber production plan or felling quota of the following year.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国营企业事业单位和集体所有制单位有下列行为之一，自检查之日起1个月内未纠正的，发放林木采伐许可证的部门有权收缴林木采伐许可证，中止其采伐，直到纠正为止：  
(一)未按规定清理伐区的；  
(二)在采伐迹地上遗弃木材，每公顷超过半立方米的；  
(三)对容易引起水土冲刷的集材主道，未采取防护措施的。

**Article 22** If a state-owned enterprise, institution, or collectively-owned unit commits any of the following acts and fails to rectify them within one month from the date of inspection, the department that issued the forest tree felling license shall have the power to withdraw the forest tree felling license and suspend its cutting operations until rectification is made:  
(1) Failing to clean up the cutting area as required;  
(2) Abandoning timber on the cutover land in excess of 0.5 cubic meters per hectare;  
(3) Failing to take protective measures for main skid trails that are prone to causing soil erosion.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　采伐林木的单位和个人违反[本办法第十四条](#art-14)、第十五条规定的，依照[森林法第四十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国森林法.md#art-45)和森林法实施条例的有关规定处理。

**Article 23** Units and individuals that fell forest trees in violation of the provisions of Articles 14 and 15 of these Measures shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 45 of the Forest Law and the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Implementation of the Forest Law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　采伐林木的单位违反本办法有关规定的，对其主要负责人和直接责任人员，由所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分。

**Article 24** Where a unit that fells forest trees violates the relevant provisions of these Measures, its principal responsible person and directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions by the unit or the competent authority at a higher level.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对国营企业事业单位所处罚款，从其自有资金或预算包干结余经费中开支。

**Article 25** Fines imposed on state-owned enterprises and institutions shall be paid from their own funds or from the surplus funds of the budget lump-sum.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本办法由林业部负责解释。

**Article 26** These Measures shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Forestry.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 27** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
