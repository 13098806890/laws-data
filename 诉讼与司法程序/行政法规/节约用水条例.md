# 节约用水条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-09  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> 第二条　本条例所称节约用水（以下简称节水），是指通过加强用水管理、转变用水方式，采取技术上可行、经济上合理的措施，降低水资源消耗、减少水资源损失、防止水资源浪费，合理、有效利用水资源的活动。 第三条　节水工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，遵循统筹规划、综合施策、因地制宜、分类指导的原则，坚持总量控制、科学配置、高效利用，坚持约束和激励相结合，建立政府主导、各方协同、市场调节、公众参与的节水机制。 第四条　国家厉行节水，坚持和落实节水优先方针，深入实施国家节水行动，全面建设节水型社会。 任何单位和个人都应当依法履行节水义务。 第五条　国家建立水资源刚性约束制度，坚持以水定城、以水定地、以水定人、以水定产，优化国土空间开发保护格局，促进人口和城市科学合理布局，构建与水资源承载能力相适应的现代产业体系。 第六条　县级以上人民政府应当将节水工作纳入国民经济和社会发展有关规划、年度计划，加强对节水工作的组织领导，完善并推动落实节水政策和保障措施，统筹研究和协调解决节水工作中的重大问题。 第七条　国务院水行政主管部门负责全国节水工作。国务院住房城乡建设主管部门按照职责分工指导城市节水工作。国务院发展改革、工业和信息化、农业农村、自然资源、市场监督管理、科技、教育、机关事务管理等主管部门按照职责分工做好节水有关工作。 县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工做好节水工作。 第八条　国家完善鼓励和支持节水产业发展、科技创新的政策措施，加强节水科技创新能力建设和产业化应用，强化科技创新对促进节水的支撑作用。 第九条　国家加强节水宣传教育和科学普及，提升全民节水意识和节水技能，促进形成自觉节水的社会共识和良好风尚。 国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当组织开展多种形式的节水宣传教育和知识普及活动。 新闻媒体应当开展节水公益宣传，对浪费水资源的行为进行舆论监督。 第二章　用水管理 第十条　国务院有关部门按照职责分工，根据国民经济和社会发展规划、全国水资源战略规划编制全国节水规划。县级以上地方人民政府根据经济社会发展需要、水资源状况和上级节水规划，组织编制本行政区域的节水规划。 节水规划应当包括水资源状况评价、节水潜力分析、节水目标、主要任务和措施等内容。

### 正文

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　节水工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，遵循统筹规划、综合施策、因地制宜、分类指导的原则，坚持总量控制、科学配置、高效利用，坚持约束和激励相结合，建立政府主导、各方协同、市场调节、公众参与的节水机制。

**Article 3** Water conservation work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security, follow the principles of overall planning, comprehensive measures, adaptation to local conditions, and classified guidance, adhere to total quantity control, scientific allocation, and efficient utilization, adhere to the combination of constraints and incentives, and establish a water conservation mechanism featuring government leadership, multi-party coordination, market regulation, and public participation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家厉行节水，坚持和落实节水优先方针，深入实施国家节水行动，全面建设节水型社会。  
任何单位和个人都应当依法履行节水义务。

**Article 4** The State strictly practices water conservation, adheres to and implements the policy of giving priority to water conservation, deeply implements the national water conservation action, and comprehensively builds a water-saving society.  
Any unit or individual shall, according to law, fulfill the obligation of water conservation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立水资源刚性约束制度，坚持以水定城、以水定地、以水定人、以水定产，优化国土空间开发保护格局，促进人口和城市科学合理布局，构建与水资源承载能力相适应的现代产业体系。

**Article 5** The State establishes a rigid constraint system for water resources, adheres to determining urban development, land use, population, and industrial production based on water availability, optimizes the pattern of territorial space development and protection, promotes a scientific and reasonable layout of population and cities, and builds a modern industrial system compatible with the carrying capacity of water resources.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当将节水工作纳入国民经济和社会发展有关规划、年度计划，加强对节水工作的组织领导，完善并推动落实节水政策和保障措施，统筹研究和协调解决节水工作中的重大问题。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate water conservation work into relevant plans and annual plans for national economic and social development, strengthen organizational leadership over water conservation work, improve and promote the implementation of water conservation policies and safeguard measures, and conduct overall research and coordinate to resolve major issues in water conservation work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院水行政主管部门负责全国节水工作。国务院住房城乡建设主管部门按照职责分工指导城市节水工作。国务院发展改革、工业和信息化、农业农村、自然资源、市场监督管理、科技、教育、机关事务管理等主管部门按照职责分工做好节水有关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工做好节水工作。

**Article 7** The water administrative department of the State Council is responsible for national water conservation work. The housing and urban-rural construction department of the State Council, according to its division of responsibilities, guides urban water conservation work. The competent departments of the State Council for development and reform, industry and information technology, agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, market regulation, science and technology, education, and government offices administration, among others, shall, according to their division of responsibilities, properly carry out work related to water conservation.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their division of responsibilities, properly carry out water conservation work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家完善鼓励和支持节水产业发展、科技创新的政策措施，加强节水科技创新能力建设和产业化应用，强化科技创新对促进节水的支撑作用。

**Article 8** The State improves policies and measures that encourage and support the development of the water conservation industry and scientific and technological innovation, strengthens the construction of scientific and technological innovation capabilities for water conservation and their industrial application, and reinforces the supporting role of scientific and technological innovation in promoting water conservation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家加强节水宣传教育和科学普及，提升全民节水意识和节水技能，促进形成自觉节水的社会共识和良好风尚。  
国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当组织开展多种形式的节水宣传教育和知识普及活动。  
新闻媒体应当开展节水公益宣传，对浪费水资源的行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 9** The State strengthens water conservation publicity, education, and scientific popularization, enhances the water conservation awareness and skills of the whole population, and promotes the formation of a social consensus and good practice of conscious water conservation.  
The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, the people's governments of townships and towns, and sub-district offices shall organize and carry out various forms of water conservation publicity, education, and knowledge popularization activities.  
News media shall conduct public service publicity on water conservation and exercise public opinion supervision over acts of water waste.

### 第二章　用水管理

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院有关部门按照职责分工，根据国民经济和社会发展规划、全国水资源战略规划编制全国节水规划。县级以上地方人民政府根据经济社会发展需要、水资源状况和上级节水规划，组织编制本行政区域的节水规划。  
节水规划应当包括水资源状况评价、节水潜力分析、节水目标、主要任务和措施等内容。

**Article 10** The relevant departments of the State Council, according to their division of responsibilities, shall formulate a national water conservation plan based on the national economic and social development plan and the national water resources strategic plan. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the formulation of water conservation plans for their respective administrative regions based on the needs of economic and social development, the state of water resources, and the higher-level water conservation plan.  
A water conservation plan shall include contents such as evaluation of water resources status, analysis of water conservation potential, water conservation targets, main tasks, and measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院水行政、标准化主管部门组织制定全国主要农作物、重点工业产品和服务业等的用水定额（以下称国家用水定额）。组织制定国家用水定额，应当征求国务院有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的意见。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际需要，可以制定严于国家用水定额的地方用水定额；国家用水定额未作规定的，可以补充制定地方用水定额。地方用水定额由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关行业主管部门提出，经同级水行政、标准化主管部门审核同意后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公布，并报国务院水行政、标准化主管部门备案。  
用水定额应当根据经济社会发展水平、水资源状况、产业结构变化和技术进步等情况适时修订。

**Article 11** The water administrative department and the standardization department of the State Council shall organize the formulation of water use quotas for major crops nationwide, key industrial products, and the service industry (hereinafter referred to as national water use quotas). When organizing the formulation of national water use quotas, opinions shall be solicited from the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on actual needs, formulate local water use quotas that are stricter than the national water use quotas; where the national water use quotas have not provided for certain items, they may supplement with local water use quotas. Local water use quotas shall be proposed by the relevant competent industry departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and after review and approval by the water administrative department and the standardization department at the same level, shall be published by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and reported to the water administrative department and the standardization department of the State Council for record.  
Water use quotas shall be revised in a timely manner based on the level of economic and social development, the state of water resources, changes in industrial structure, and technological progress.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同有关部门，根据用水定额、经济技术条件以及水量分配方案、地下水控制指标等确定的可供本行政区域使用的水量，制定本行政区域年度用水计划，对年度用水实行总量控制。

**Article 12** The water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, formulate an annual water use plan for its administrative region based on the water use quotas, economic and technical conditions, and the volume of water available for use in that administrative region as determined by water allocation plans, groundwater control indicators, etc., and implement total quantity control over annual water use.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对用水达到一定规模的单位实行计划用水管理。  
用水单位的用水计划应当根据用水定额、本行政区域年度用水计划制定。对直接取用地下水、地表水的用水单位，用水计划由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者相应流域管理机构制定；对使用城市公共供水的用水单位，用水计划由城市节水主管部门会同城市供水主管部门制定。  
用水单位计划用水管理的具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 13** The State implements planned water use management for units whose water use reaches a certain scale.  
The water use plan of a water-using unit shall be formulated based on water use quotas and the annual water use plan of its administrative region. For units that directly draw groundwater or surface water, the water use plan shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the corresponding river basin management agency; for units that use urban public water supply, the water use plan shall be formulated by the urban water conservation department in conjunction with the urban water supply department.  
The specific measures for planned water use management of water-using units shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　用水应当计量。对不同水源、不同用途的水应当分别计量。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强农业灌溉用水计量设施建设。水资源严重短缺地区、地下水超采地区应当限期建设农业灌溉用水计量设施。农业灌溉用水暂不具备计量条件的，可以采用以电折水等间接方式进行计量。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、擅自移动用水计量设施，不得干扰用水计量。

**Article 14** Water use shall be metered. Water from different sources and for different purposes shall be metered separately.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of metering facilities for agricultural irrigation water. In areas with severe water shortage or areas with over-extraction of groundwater, metering facilities for agricultural irrigation water shall be constructed within a specified time limit. Where conditions for metering agricultural irrigation water are temporarily not available, indirect metering methods such as converting electricity into water may be used.  
No unit or individual may occupy, damage, or move water use metering facilities without authorization, or interfere with water metering.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　用水实行计量收费。国家建立促进节水的水价体系，完善与经济社会发展水平、水资源状况、用水定额、供水成本、用水户承受能力和节水要求等相适应的水价形成机制。  
城镇居民生活用水和具备条件的农村居民生活用水实行阶梯水价，非居民用水实行超定额（超计划）累进加价。  
农业水价应当依法统筹供水成本、水资源稀缺程度和农业用水户承受能力等因素合理制定，原则上不低于工程运行维护成本。对具备条件的农业灌溉用水，推进实行超定额累进加价。  
再生水、海水淡化水的水价在地方人民政府统筹协调下由供需双方协商确定。

**Article 15** Water use shall be subject to metered charging. The State establishes a water pricing system that promotes water conservation, and improves the water price formation mechanism that is compatible with the level of economic and social development, the state of water resources, water use quotas, water supply costs, the affordability of water users, and water conservation requirements.  
For domestic water use of urban residents and domestic water use of rural residents who meet the conditions, a tiered water price shall be implemented; for non-residential water use, a progressive surcharge for exceeding the quota (plan) shall be implemented.  
Agricultural water prices shall be reasonably determined according to law by taking into account factors such as water supply costs, the degree of water resource scarcity, and the affordability of agricultural water users, and shall, in principle, not be lower than the operation and maintenance costs of the project. For agricultural irrigation water that meets the conditions, the implementation of a progressive surcharge for exceeding the quota shall be promoted.  
The water prices for reclaimed water and desalinated seawater shall be determined through consultation between suppliers and demanders under the overall coordination of local people's governments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　水资源严重短缺地区、地下水超采地区应当严格控制高耗水产业项目建设，禁止新建并限期淘汰不符合国家产业政策的高耗水产业项目。

**Article 16** In areas with severe water shortage or areas with over-extraction of groundwater, the construction of projects in high water-consumption industries shall be strictly controlled, and the construction of new high water-consumption industry projects that do not conform to the national industrial policy shall be prohibited, and such projects shall be phased out within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对节水潜力大、使用面广的用水产品实行水效标识管理，并逐步淘汰水效等级较低的用水产品。水效标识管理办法由国务院发展改革主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 17** The State implements water efficiency labeling management for water-using products with great water conservation potential and wide usage, and gradually eliminates water-using products with low water efficiency grades. The measures for the administration of water efficiency labeling shall be formulated by the competent department of development and reform of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家鼓励对节水产品实施质量认证，通过认证的节水产品可以按照规定使用认证标志。认证基本规范、认证规则由国务院认证认可监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 18** The State encourages quality certification for water conservation products. Water conservation products that have passed certification may use the certification mark in accordance with the regulations. The basic certification norms and certification rules shall be formulated by the certification and accreditation regulatory department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　新建、改建、扩建建设项目，建设单位应当根据工程建设内容制定节水措施方案，配套建设节水设施。节水设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。节水设施建设投资纳入建设项目总投资。

**Article 19** For new construction, reconstruction, or expansion projects, the construction unit shall, based on the project content, formulate a water conservation measures plan and construct supporting water conservation facilities. Water conservation facilities shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project. The investment in the construction of water conservation facilities shall be included in the total investment of the construction project.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家逐步淘汰落后的、耗水量高的技术、工艺、设备和产品，具体名录由国务院发展改革主管部门会同国务院工业和信息化、水行政、住房城乡建设等有关部门制定并公布。  
禁止生产、销售列入前款规定名录的技术、工艺、设备和产品。从事生产经营活动的使用者应当限期停止使用列入前款规定名录的技术、工艺、设备和产品。

**Article 20** The State gradually eliminates outdated technologies, processes, equipment, and products with high water consumption. The specific catalog shall be formulated and published by the competent department of development and reform of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council such as industry and information technology, water administration, and housing and urban-rural construction.  
It is prohibited to produce or sell the technologies, processes, equipment, and products listed in the catalog specified in the preceding paragraph. Users engaged in production and business activities shall cease using the technologies, processes, equipment, and products listed in the catalog specified in the preceding paragraph within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家建立健全节水标准体系。  
国务院有关部门依法组织制定并适时修订有关节水的国家标准、行业标准。  
国家鼓励有关社会团体、企业依法制定严于国家标准、行业标准的节水团体标准、企业标准。

**Article 21** The State establishes and improves the water conservation standard system.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, according to law, organize the formulation and timely revision of national standards and industry standards related to water conservation.  
The State encourages relevant social organizations and enterprises to formulate, according to law, water conservation group standards and enterprise standards that are stricter than national standards and industry standards.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院有关部门依法建立节水统计调查制度，定期公布节水统计信息。

**Article 22** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, according to law, establish a water conservation statistical investigation system and regularly publish water conservation statistical information.

### 第三章　节水措施

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当根据经济社会发展水平和水资源状况，引导农业生产经营主体合理调整种植养殖结构和农业用水结构，积极发展节水型农业，因地制宜发展旱作农业。  
国家对水资源短缺地区发展节水型农业给予重点扶持。

**Article 23** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, based on the level of economic and social development and the state of water resources, guide agricultural production and operation entities to reasonably adjust the planting and breeding structure and the agricultural water use structure, actively develop water-saving agriculture, and develop dryland agriculture according to local conditions.  
The State provides key support to areas with water resource shortage for the development of water-saving agriculture.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家支持耐旱农作物新品种以及土壤保墒、水肥一体化、养殖废水资源化利用等种植业、养殖业节水技术的研究和推广。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当组织开展节水农业试验示范和技术培训，指导农业生产经营主体使用节水技术。

**Article 24** The State supports the research and promotion of new drought-resistant crop varieties and water-saving technologies in planting and breeding industries, such as soil moisture conservation, integrated water and fertilizer management, and resource utilization of breeding wastewater.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall organize and carry out water-saving agriculture experimental demonstrations and technical training, and guide agricultural production and operation entities in using water-saving technologies.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家发展节水灌溉，推广喷灌、微灌、管道输水灌溉、渠道防渗输水灌溉、集雨补灌等节水灌溉技术，提高灌溉用水效率。水资源短缺地区、地下水超采地区应当优先发展节水灌溉。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当支持和推动节水灌溉工程设施建设。新建灌溉工程设施应当符合节水灌溉工程技术标准。已经建成的灌溉工程设施不符合节水灌溉工程技术标准的，应当限期进行节水改造。

**Article 25** The State develops water-saving irrigation, promotes water-saving irrigation technologies such as sprinkler irrigation, micro-irrigation, pipeline water conveyance irrigation, canal seepage control irrigation, and rainwater harvesting supplementary irrigation, and improves irrigation water use efficiency. In areas with water resource shortage or areas with over-extraction of groundwater, priority shall be given to developing water-saving irrigation.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall support and promote the construction of water-saving irrigation engineering facilities. Newly built irrigation engineering facilities shall meet the technical standards for water-saving irrigation. Where existing irrigation engineering facilities do not meet the technical standards for water-saving irrigation, water-saving renovation shall be carried out within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家加快推进农村生活节水。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强农村生活供水设施以及配套管网建设和改造，推广使用生活节水器具。

**Article 26** The State accelerates the promotion of water conservation in rural domestic use.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall strengthen the construction and renovation of rural domestic water supply facilities and supporting pipe networks, and promote the use of domestic water-saving appliances.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　工业企业应当加强内部用水管理，建立节水管理制度，采用分质供水、高效冷却和洗涤、循环用水、废水处理回用等先进、适用节水技术、工艺和设备，降低单位产品（产值）耗水量，提高水资源重复利用率。高耗水工业企业用水水平超过用水定额的，应当限期进行节水改造。  
工业企业的生产设备冷却水、空调冷却水、锅炉冷凝水应当回收利用。高耗水工业企业应当逐步推广废水深度处理回用技术措施。

**Article 27** Industrial enterprises shall strengthen internal water use management, establish water conservation management systems, adopt advanced and applicable water-saving technologies, processes, and equipment such as quality-based water supply, efficient cooling and washing, recirculating water use, and wastewater treatment and reuse, reduce water consumption per unit of product (output value), and increase the rate of water resource reuse. Where the water use level of a high water-consumption industrial enterprise exceeds the water use quota, it shall carry out water-saving renovation within a specified time limit.  
The cooling water for production equipment, air conditioning cooling water, and boiler condensate water of industrial enterprises shall be recovered and reused. High water-consumption industrial enterprises shall gradually promote technical measures for advanced wastewater treatment and reuse.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　新建、改建、扩建工业企业集聚的各类开发区、园区等（以下统称工业集聚区）应当统筹建设供水、排水、废水处理及循环利用设施，推动企业间串联用水、分质用水，实现一水多用和循环利用。  
国家鼓励已经建成的工业集聚区开展以节水为重点内容的绿色高质量转型升级和循环化改造，加快节水及水循环利用设施建设。

**Article 28** For new construction, reconstruction, or expansion of various development zones, parks, etc. where industrial enterprises are concentrated (hereinafter collectively referred to as industrial agglomeration areas), water supply, drainage, wastewater treatment, and recycling facilities shall be constructed in a coordinated manner, promoting series water use and quality-based water use among enterprises to achieve multiple uses and recycling of water.  
The State encourages established industrial agglomeration areas to carry out green high-quality transformation and upgrading and circular transformation with water conservation as the key content, and accelerate the construction of water conservation and water recycling facilities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对城市建成区内生产、生活、生态用水的统筹，将节水要求落实到城市规划、建设、治理的各个环节，全面推进节水型城市建设。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the coordination of water use for production, domestic use, and ecology within built-up urban areas, implement water conservation requirements in all aspects of urban planning, construction, and governance, and comprehensively promote the construction of water-saving cities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公共供水企业和自建用水管网设施的单位应当加强供水、用水管网设施运行和维护管理，建立供水、用水管网设施漏损控制体系，采取措施控制水的漏损。超出供水管网设施漏损控制国家标准的漏水损失，不得计入公共供水企业定价成本。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当加强对公共供水管网设施运行的监督管理，支持和推动老旧供水管网设施改造。

**Article 30** Public water supply enterprises and units with self-built water supply pipe network facilities shall strengthen the operation and maintenance management of water supply and use pipe network facilities, establish a leakage control system for water supply and use pipe network facilities, and take measures to control water leakage. Leakage losses exceeding the national standard for leakage control of water supply pipe network facilities shall not be included in the pricing cost of public water supply enterprises.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and management of the operation of public water supply pipe network facilities, and support and promote the renovation of old water supply pipe network facilities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家把节水作为推广绿色建筑的重要内容，推动降低建筑运行水耗。  
新建、改建、扩建公共建筑应当使用节水器具。

**Article 31** The State regards water conservation as an important part of promoting green buildings, and promotes the reduction of water consumption in building operation.  
Newly built, reconstructed, or expanded public buildings shall use water-saving appliances.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公共机构应当发挥节水表率作用，建立健全节水管理制度，率先采用先进的节水技术、工艺、设备和产品，开展节水改造，积极建设节水型单位。

**Article 32** Public institutions shall play an exemplary role in water conservation, establish and improve water conservation management systems, take the lead in adopting advanced water-saving technologies, processes, equipment, and products, carry out water-saving renovations, and actively build water-saving units.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　城镇园林绿化应当提高用水效率。  
水资源短缺地区城镇园林绿化应当优先选用适合本地区的节水耐旱型植被，采用喷灌、微灌等节水灌溉方式。  
水资源短缺地区应当严格控制人造河湖等景观用水。

**Article 33** Urban landscaping and greening shall improve water use efficiency.  
In areas with water resource shortage, urban landscaping and greening shall prioritize the selection of water-saving and drought-tolerant vegetation suitable for the region, and adopt water-saving irrigation methods such as sprinkler irrigation and micro-irrigation.  
In areas with water resource shortage, water use for landscape such as man-made rivers and lakes shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据水资源状况，将再生水、集蓄雨水、海水及海水淡化水、矿坑（井）水、微咸水等非常规水纳入水资源统一配置。  
水资源短缺地区县级以上地方人民政府应当制定非常规水利用计划，提高非常规水利用比例，对具备使用非常规水条件但未合理使用的建设项目，不得批准其新增取水许可。

**Article 34** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the state of water resources, incorporate unconventional water sources such as reclaimed water, collected rainwater, seawater and desalinated seawater, mine (well) water, and brackish water into the unified allocation of water resources.  
In areas with water resource shortage, local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate plans for the utilization of unconventional water sources, increase the proportion of unconventional water use, and shall not approve new water withdrawal permits for construction projects that have conditions to use unconventional water sources but fail to use them reasonably.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹规划、建设污水资源化利用基础设施，促进污水资源化利用。  
城市绿化、道路清扫、车辆冲洗、建筑施工以及生态景观等用水，应当优先使用符合标准要求的再生水。

**Article 35** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall plans and construct infrastructure for wastewater resource utilization, and promote wastewater resource utilization.  
Water use for urban greening, road cleaning, vehicle washing, construction, and ecological landscaping shall prioritize the use of reclaimed water that meets the standard requirements.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当推进海绵城市建设，提高雨水资源化利用水平。  
开展城市新区建设、旧城区改造和市政基础设施建设等，应当按照海绵城市建设要求，因地制宜规划、建设雨水滞渗、净化、利用和调蓄设施。

**Article 36** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall promote the construction of sponge cities and improve the level of rainwater resource utilization.  
When carrying out construction of new urban areas, renovation of old urban areas, and construction of municipal infrastructure, facilities for rainwater retention and infiltration, purification, utilization, and storage shall be planned and built according to local conditions in accordance with the requirements of sponge city construction.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　沿海地区应当积极开发利用海水资源。  
沿海或者海岛淡水资源短缺地区新建、改建、扩建工业企业项目应当优先使用海水淡化水。具备条件的，可以将海水淡化水作为市政新增供水以及应急备用水源。

**Article 37** Coastal areas shall actively develop and utilize seawater resources.  
In coastal areas or islands with freshwater resource shortage, newly built, reconstructed, or expanded industrial enterprise projects shall give priority to using desalinated seawater. Where conditions permit, desalinated seawater may be used as new municipal water supply and emergency backup water source.

### 第四章　保障和监督

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当健全与节水成效、农业水价水平、财力状况相匹配的农业用水精准补贴机制和节水奖励机制。  
对符合条件的节水项目，按照国家有关规定给予补助。

**Article 38** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a sound precise subsidy mechanism for agricultural water use and a water conservation reward mechanism that are matched with the effectiveness of water conservation, the level of agricultural water prices, and the financial situation.  
Water conservation projects that meet the conditions shall be granted subsidies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家鼓励金融机构提供多种形式的节水金融服务，引导金融机构加大对节水项目的融资支持力度。  
国家鼓励和引导社会资本按照市场化原则依法参与节水项目建设和运营，保护其合法权益。

**Article 39** The State encourages financial institutions to provide various forms of financial services for water conservation, and guides financial institutions to increase financing support for water conservation projects.  
The State encourages and guides social capital to participate in the construction and operation of water conservation projects in accordance with market principles and according to law, and protects their legitimate rights and interests.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家鼓励发展社会化、专业化、规范化的节水服务产业，支持节水服务机构创新节水服务模式，开展节水咨询、设计、检测、计量、技术改造、运行管理、产品认证等服务，引导和推动节水服务机构与用水单位或者个人签订节水管理合同，提供节水服务并以节水效益分享等方式获得合理收益。  
国家鼓励农村集体经济组织、农民专业合作社、农民用水合作组织以及其他专业化服务组织参与农业节水服务。

**Article 40** The State encourages the development of a socialized, specialized, and standardized water conservation service industry, supports water conservation service institutions in innovating water conservation service models, and carrying out services such as water conservation consultation, design, testing, metering, technical transformation, operation management, and product certification, and guides and promotes water conservation service institutions to sign water conservation management contracts with water-using units or individuals, provide water conservation services, and obtain reasonable income through methods such as sharing water conservation benefits.  
The State encourages rural collective economic organizations, specialized farmers' cooperatives, farmers' water use cooperative organizations, and other specialized service organizations to participate in agricultural water conservation services.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家培育和规范水权市场，支持开展多种形式的水权交易，健全水权交易系统，引导开展集中交易，完善水权交易规则，并逐步将水权交易纳入公共资源交易平台体系。

**Article 41** The State cultivates and regulates the water rights market, supports the development of various forms of water rights trading, improves the water rights trading system, guides the conduct of centralized trading, refines the water rights trading rules, and gradually incorporates water rights trading into the public resource trading platform system.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对节水成绩显著的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 42** Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in water conservation shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府水行政、住房城乡建设、市场监督管理等主管部门应当按照职责分工，加强对用水活动的监督检查，依法查处违法行为。  
有关部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入现场开展检查，调查了解有关情况；  
（二）要求被检查单位或者个人就节水有关问题作出说明；  
（三）要求被检查单位或者个人提供有关文件、资料，进行查阅或者复制；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。  
监督检查人员在履行监督检查职责时，应当主动出示执法证件。被检查单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 43** The water administrative, housing and urban-rural construction, market regulation, and other competent departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their division of responsibilities, strengthen the supervision and inspection of water use activities, and investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law.  
When performing their supervision and inspection duties, the relevant departments shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the site to conduct inspections and investigate and understand the relevant circumstances;  
(2) Require the unit or individual under inspection to provide explanations on matters related to water conservation;  
(3) Require the unit or individual under inspection to provide relevant documents and materials for review or copying;  
(4) Other measures provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
Supervision and inspection personnel shall actively present their law enforcement credentials when performing their supervision and inspection duties. The unit or individual under inspection shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　对浪费水资源的行为，任何单位和个人有权向有关部门举报，接到举报的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 44** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report acts of wasting water resources to the relevant departments, and the department that receives the report shall handle it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家实行节水责任制和节水考核评价制度，将节水目标完成情况纳入对地方人民政府及其负责人考核范围。

**Article 45** The State implements a water conservation responsibility system and a water conservation assessment and evaluation system, and incorporates the completion of water conservation targets into the scope of assessment of local people's governments and their responsible persons.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　侵占、损毁、擅自移动用水计量设施，或者干扰用水计量的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政、住房城乡建设主管部门或者流域管理机构责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 46** Whoever occupies, damages, or moves water metering facilities without authorization, or interferes with water metering, shall be ordered by the water administrative department, the housing and urban-rural construction department of a local people's government at or above the county level, or the river basin management agency to cease the illegal act, take remedial measures within a specified time limit, and be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　建设项目的节水设施没有建成或者没有达到国家规定的要求，擅自投入使用的，以及生产、销售或者在生产经营中使用国家明令淘汰的落后的、耗水量高的技术、工艺、设备和产品的，依照《中华人民共和国水法》有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 47** Where the water conservation facilities of a construction project are not constructed or do not meet the requirements stipulated by the State and are put into use without authorization, and where technologies, processes, equipment, and products that are outdated and have high water consumption and are explicitly eliminated by the State are produced, sold, or used in production and business operations, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　高耗水工业企业用水水平超过用水定额，未在规定的期限内进行节水改造的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令改正，可以处10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，采取限制用水措施或者吊销其取水许可证。

**Article 48** Where a high water-consumption industrial enterprise whose water use level exceeds the water use quota fails to carry out water-saving renovation within the specified time limit, the water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, measures to restrict water use shall be taken or its water withdrawal permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　工业企业的生产设备冷却水、空调冷却水、锅炉冷凝水未回收利用的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 49** Where the cooling water for production equipment, air conditioning cooling water, and boiler condensate water of an industrial enterprise are not recovered and reused, the water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门的工作人员在节水工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 50** Where staff members of people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in water conservation work, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security administrative penalty by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自2024年5月1日起施行。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2024.
