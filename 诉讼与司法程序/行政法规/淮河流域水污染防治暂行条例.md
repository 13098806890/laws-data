# 淮河流域水污染防治暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1995-08-08

> (1995年8月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第183号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强淮河流域水污染防治，保护和改善水质，保障人体健康和人民生活、生产用水，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于淮河流域的河流、湖泊、水库、渠道等地表水体的污染防治。 第三条　淮河流域水污染防治的目标：1997年实现全流域工业污染源达标排放；2000年淮河流域各主要河段、湖泊、水库的水质达到淮河流域水污染防治规划的要求，实现淮河水体变清。 第四条　淮河流域水资源保护领导小组(以下简称领导小组)，负责协调、解决有关淮河流域水资源保护和水污染防治的重大问题，监督、检查淮河流域水污染防治工作，并行使国务院授予的其他职权。 领导小组办公室设在淮河流域水资源保护局。 第五条　河南、安徽、江苏、山东四省(以下简称四省)人民政府各对本省淮河流域水环境质量负责，必须采取措施确保本省淮河流域水污染防治目标的实现。 四省人民政府应当将淮河流域水污染治理任务分解到有关市(地)、县，签订目标责任书，限期完成，并将该项工作作为考核有关干部政绩的重要内容。 第六条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府，应当定期向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告本行政区域内淮河流域水污染防治工作进展情况。 第七条　国家对淮河流域水污染防治实行优惠、扶持政策。 第八条　四省人民政府应当妥善做好淮河流域关、停企业的职工安置工作。 第九条　国家对淮河流域实行水污染物排放总量(以下简称排污总量)控制制度。 第十条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门会同国务院计划部门、水行政主管部门商四省人民政府，根据淮河流域水污染防治目标，拟订淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，经由领导小组报国务院批准后执行。 第十一条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府，根据上级人民政府制定的淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，组织制定本行政区域内淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，并纳入本行政区域的国民经济和社会发展中长期规划和年度计划。 第十二条　淮河流域排污总量控制计划，应当包括确定的排污总量控制区域、排污总量、排污削减量和削减时限要求，以及应当实行重点排污控制的区域和重点排污控制区域外的重点排污单位名单等内容。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强淮河流域水污染防治，保护和改善水质，保障人体健康和人民生活、生产用水，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the prevention and control of water pollution in the Huaihe River Basin, to protect and improve water quality, and to safeguard human health and water use for people's livelihood and production.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于淮河流域的河流、湖泊、水库、渠道等地表水体的污染防治。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the prevention and control of pollution in surface water bodies such as rivers, lakes, reservoirs, and channels in the Huaihe River Basin.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　淮河流域水污染防治的目标：1997年实现全流域工业污染源达标排放；2000年淮河流域各主要河段、湖泊、水库的水质达到淮河流域水污染防治规划的要求，实现淮河水体变清。

**Article 3** The objectives of water pollution prevention and control in the Huaihe River Basin are: to achieve discharge standards for all industrial pollution sources in the entire basin by 1997; by 2000, the water quality of major river sections, lakes, and reservoirs in the Huaihe River Basin shall meet the requirements of the Huaihe River Basin Water Pollution Prevention and Control Plan, and the water bodies of the Huaihe River shall become clear.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　淮河流域水资源保护领导小组(以下简称领导小组)，负责协调、解决有关淮河流域水资源保护和水污染防治的重大问题，监督、检查淮河流域水污染防治工作，并行使国务院授予的其他职权。  
领导小组办公室设在淮河流域水资源保护局。

**Article 4** The Leading Group for the Protection of Water Resources in the Huaihe River Basin (hereinafter referred to as the Leading Group) shall be responsible for coordinating and resolving major issues concerning the protection of water resources and the prevention and control of water pollution in the Huaihe River Basin, supervising and inspecting the work of water pollution prevention and control in the Huaihe River Basin, and exercising other functions and powers conferred by the State Council.  
The office of the Leading Group shall be located in the Huaihe River Basin Water Resources Protection Bureau.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　河南、安徽、江苏、山东四省(以下简称四省)人民政府各对本省淮河流域水环境质量负责，必须采取措施确保本省淮河流域水污染防治目标的实现。  
四省人民政府应当将淮河流域水污染治理任务分解到有关市(地)、县，签订目标责任书，限期完成，并将该项工作作为考核有关干部政绩的重要内容。

**Article 5** The people's governments of the four provinces of Henan, Anhui, Jiangsu, and Shandong (hereinafter referred to as the four provinces) shall each be responsible for the water environment quality of the Huaihe River Basin within their respective provinces and must take measures to ensure the achievement of the objectives for water pollution prevention and control in the Huaihe River Basin within their respective provinces.  
The people's governments of the four provinces shall decompose the tasks of water pollution control in the Huaihe River Basin to relevant cities (prefectures) and counties, sign target responsibility documents, complete them within a prescribed time limit, and regard this work as an important element in assessing the performance of relevant officials.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府，应当定期向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告本行政区域内淮河流域水污染防治工作进展情况。

**Article 6** Local people's governments at or above the county level in the Huaihe River Basin shall regularly report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level on the progress of water pollution prevention and control work in the Huaihe River Basin within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对淮河流域水污染防治实行优惠、扶持政策。

**Article 7** The State shall implement preferential and supportive policies for the prevention and control of water pollution in the Huaihe River Basin.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　四省人民政府应当妥善做好淮河流域关、停企业的职工安置工作。

**Article 8** The people's governments of the four provinces shall properly arrange for the placement of employees of enterprises that are closed or suspended in the Huaihe River Basin.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对淮河流域实行水污染物排放总量(以下简称排污总量)控制制度。

**Article 9** The State shall implement a system of total discharge control of water pollutants (hereinafter referred to as total discharge control) in the Huaihe River Basin.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门会同国务院计划部门、水行政主管部门商四省人民政府，根据淮河流域水污染防治目标，拟订淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，经由领导小组报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 10** The administrative department in charge of environmental protection under the State Council shall, together with the planning department and the administrative department in charge of water resources under the State Council and after consulting the people's governments of the four provinces, formulate the Huaihe River Basin water pollution prevention and control plan and the total discharge control plan based on the objectives of water pollution prevention and control in the Huaihe River Basin, and submit them through the Leading Group to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府，根据上级人民政府制定的淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，组织制定本行政区域内淮河流域水污染防治规划和排污总量控制计划，并纳入本行政区域的国民经济和社会发展中长期规划和年度计划。

**Article 11** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Huai River Basin shall, in accordance with the Huai River Basin water pollution prevention plans and the total discharge volume control plans formulated by the people's governments at higher levels, organize the formulation of the Huai River Basin water pollution prevention plans and the total discharge volume control plans within their respective administrative regions, and incorporate them into the medium- and long-term plans and annual plans for national economic and social development of their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　淮河流域排污总量控制计划，应当包括确定的排污总量控制区域、排污总量、排污削减量和削减时限要求，以及应当实行重点排污控制的区域和重点排污控制区域外的重点排污单位名单等内容。

**Article 12** The total discharge volume control plan for the Huai River Basin shall include the designated total discharge volume control zones, the total discharge volume, the discharge reduction amount and the time limit for reduction, as well as the zones where key discharge control shall be implemented and the list of key discharge units outside the key discharge control zones.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　向淮河流域水体排污的企业事业单位和个体工商户(以下简称排污单位)，凡纳入排污总量控制的，由环境保护行政主管部门商同级有关行业主管部门，根据排污总量控制计划、建设项目环境影响报告书和排污申报量，确定其排污总量控制指标。  
排污单位的排污总量控制指标的削减量以及削减时限要求，由下达指标的环境保护行政主管部门根据本级人民政府的规定，商同级有关行业主管部门核定。  
超过排污总量控制指标排污的，由有关县级以上地方人民政府责令限期治理。

**Article 13** For enterprises, institutions and individual industrial and commercial households that discharge pollutants into the waters of the Huai River Basin (hereinafter referred to as discharge units) and are included in the total discharge volume control, the competent administrative department of environmental protection shall, in consultation with the relevant competent department of the same level, determine their total discharge volume control indicators in accordance with the total discharge volume control plan, the environmental impact report of the construction project and the declared discharge volume.  
The reduction amount and the time limit for reduction of the total discharge volume control indicators for discharge units shall be verified by the competent administrative department of environmental protection that issued the indicators, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government at the same level, in consultation with the relevant competent department of the same level.  
Where a unit discharges pollutants exceeding the total discharge volume control indicators, the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to achieve compliance within a specified period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在淮河流域排污总量控制计划确定的重点排污控制区域内的排污单位和重点排污控制区域外的重点排污单位，必须按照国家有关规定申请领取排污许可证，并在排污口安装污水排放计量器具。

**Article 14** Discharge units within the key discharge control zones as determined in the total discharge volume control plan for the Huai River Basin and key discharge units outside the key discharge control zones must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, apply for and obtain pollutant discharge permits, and install wastewater discharge metering devices at their discharge outlets.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门商国务院水行政主管部门，根据淮河流域排污总量控制计划以及四省的经济技术条件，制定淮河流域省界水质标准，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 15** The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall, in consultation with the competent administrative department of water resources under the State Council, formulate the water quality standards for the provincial boundaries of the Huai River Basin in accordance with the total discharge volume control plan for the Huai River Basin and the economic and technological conditions of the four provinces, and shall submit them to the State Council for approval before they come into force.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　淮河流域水资源保护局负责监测四省省界水质，并将监测结果及时报领导小组。

**Article 16** The Huai River Basin Water Resources Protection Bureau shall be responsible for monitoring the water quality at the provincial boundaries of the four provinces, and shall promptly report the monitoring results to the Leading Group.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　淮河流域重点排污单位超标排放水污染物的，责令限期治理。  
市、县或者市、县以下人民政府管辖的企业事业单位的限期治理，由有关市、县人民政府决定。中央或者省级人民政府管辖的企业事业单位的限期治理，由省级人民政府决定。  
限期治理的重点排污单位名单，由国务院环境保护行政主管部门商四省人民政府拟订，经领导小组审核同意后公布。

**Article 17** Where a key discharge unit in the Huai River Basin discharges water pollutants exceeding the standards, it shall be ordered to achieve compliance within a specified period.  
The order for compliance within a specified period for enterprises and institutions under the jurisdiction of a city, county, or a people's government below the county level shall be decided by the relevant city or county people's government. The order for compliance within a specified period for enterprises and institutions under the jurisdiction of the central government or a provincial people's government shall be decided by the provincial people's government.  
The list of key discharge units subject to compliance within a specified period shall be drafted by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council in consultation with the people's governments of the four provinces, and shall be published after review and approval by the Leading Group.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　自1998年1月1日起，禁止一切工业企业向淮河流域水体超标排放水污染物。

**Article 18** As of January 1, 1998, all industrial enterprises shall be prohibited from discharging water pollutants into the waters of the Huai River Basin in excess of the standards.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　淮河流域排污单位必须采取措施按期完成污染治理任务，保证水污染物的排放符合国家制定的和地方制定的排放标准；持有排污许可证的单位应当保证其排污总量不超过排污许可证规定的排污总量控制指标。  
未按期完成污染治理任务的排污单位，应当集中资金尽快完成治理任务；完成治理任务前，不得建设扩大生产规模的项目。

**Article 19** Discharge units in the Huai River Basin must take measures to complete the pollution control tasks within the prescribed time limit, and ensure that the discharge of water pollutants complies with the national and local discharge standards; units holding pollutant discharge permits shall ensure that their total discharge volume does not exceed the total discharge volume control indicators specified in the pollutant discharge permits.  
Discharge units that fail to complete the pollution control tasks within the prescribed time limit shall concentrate their funds to complete the tasks as soon as possible; before the completion of the control tasks, they shall not construct projects that expand the scale of production.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门征收的排污费，必须按照国家有关规定，全部用于污染治理，不得挪作他用。  
审计部门应当对排污费的使用情况依法进行审计，并由四省人民政府审计部门将审计结果报领导小组。

**Article 20** The pollutant discharge fees collected by the competent administrative departments of environmental protection of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Huai River Basin must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be entirely used for pollution control and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.  
The audit departments shall audit the use of the pollutant discharge fees according to law, and the audit departments of the people's governments of the four provinces shall report the audit results to the Leading Group.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在淮河流域河流、湖泊、水库、渠道等管理范围内设置或者扩大排污口的，必须依法报经水行政主管部门同意。

**Article 21** To establish or expand a sewage outlet within the management scope of rivers, lakes, reservoirs, canals, etc. in the Huaihe River Basin, approval from the water administrative department shall be obtained according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止在淮河流域新建化学制浆造纸企业。  
禁止在淮河流域新建制革、化工、印染、电镀、酿造等污染严重的小型企业。  
严格限制在淮河流域新建前款所列大中型项目或者其他污染严重的项目；建设该类项目的，必须事先征得有关省人民政府环境保护行政主管部门的同意，并报国务院环境保护行政主管部门备案。  
禁止和严格限制的产业、产品名录，由国务院环境保护行政主管部门商国务院有关行业主管部门拟订，经领导小组审核同意，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 22** It is prohibited to construct new chemical pulp and paper enterprises in the Huaihe River Basin.  
It is prohibited to construct new small-scale enterprises with heavy pollution such as tanning, chemical, printing and dyeing, electroplating, and brewing in the Huaihe River Basin.  
Strict restrictions shall be imposed on the construction of large and medium-sized projects listed in the preceding paragraph or other projects with heavy pollution in the Huaihe River Basin; for the construction of such projects, consent shall be obtained in advance from the environmental protection administrative department of the relevant provincial people's government, and the matter shall be reported to the environmental protection administrative department of the State Council for record.  
The catalogue of industries and products that are prohibited or strictly restricted shall be formulated by the environmental protection administrative department of the State Council in consultation with the relevant competent departments of the State Council, examined and approved by the Leading Group, and submitted to the State Council for approval before being promulgated and implemented.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门审批向水体排放污染物的建设项目的环境影响报告书时，不得突破本行政区域排污总量控制指标。

**Article 23** When examining and approving the environmental impact report of a construction project that discharges pollutants into water bodies in the Huaihe River Basin, the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall not exceed the total pollutant discharge control quota for its administrative region.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　淮河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照淮河流域水污染防治规划的要求，建设城镇污水集中处理设施。

**Article 24** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Huaihe River Basin shall, in accordance with the requirements of the plan for the prevention and control of water pollution in the Huaihe River Basin, construct centralized urban sewage treatment facilities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　淮河流域水闸应当在保证防汛、抗旱的前提下，兼顾上游下游水质，制定防污调控方案，避免闸控河道蓄积的污水集中下泄。  
领导小组确定的重要水闸，由淮河水利委员会会同有关省人民政府水行政主管部门制定防污调控方案，报领导小组批准后施行。

**Article 25** Sluices in the Huaihe River Basin shall, on the premise of ensuring flood control and drought relief, take into account the water quality of both upstream and downstream, formulate anti-pollution regulation plans, and avoid the concentrated discharge of accumulated sewage in the sluice-controlled rivers.  
For important sluices determined by the Leading Group, the Huaihe River Water Conservancy Commission shall, jointly with the water administrative departments of the relevant provincial people's governments, formulate anti-pollution regulation plans, which shall be submitted to the Leading Group for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　领导小组办公室应当组织四省人民政府环境保护行政主管部门、水行政主管部门等采取下列措施，开展枯水期水污染联合防治工作：  
(一)加强对主要河道、湖泊、水库的水质、水情的动态监测，并及时通报监测资料；  
(二)根据枯水期的水环境最大容量，商四省人民政府环境保护行政主管部门规定各省枯水期污染源限排总量，由四省人民政府环境保护行政主管部门逐级分解到排污单位，使其按照枯水期污染源限排方案限量排污；  
(三)根据水闸防污调控方案，调度水闸。

**Article 26** The office of the Leading Group shall organize the environmental protection administrative departments and water administrative departments of the people's governments of the four provinces to take the following measures for joint prevention and control of water pollution during the dry season:  
(1) Strengthen the dynamic monitoring of water quality and water regime of major rivers, lakes, and reservoirs, and promptly notify the monitoring data;  
(2) Based on the maximum environmental capacity of water bodies during the dry season, consult with the environmental protection administrative departments of the people's governments of the four provinces to specify the total amount of pollutant discharge limits for each province during the dry season, and the environmental protection administrative departments of the people's governments of the four provinces shall break down the limits level by level to pollutant-discharging units, so that they discharge pollutants in limited quantities in accordance with the pollutant discharge limit plan for the dry season;  
(3) Dispatch sluices in accordance with the anti-pollution regulation plans for sluices.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　淮河流域发生水污染事故时，必须及时向环境保护行政主管部门报告。环境保护行政主管部门应当在接到事故报告时起24小时内，向本级人民政府、上级环境保护行政主管部门和领导小组办公室报告，并向相邻上游和下游的环境保护行政主管部门、水行政主管部门通报。当地人民政府应当采取应急措施，消除或者减轻污染危害。

**Article 27** When a water pollution accident occurs in the Huaihe River Basin, the party concerned shall promptly report to the environmental protection administrative department. The environmental protection administrative department shall, within 24 hours of receiving the accident report, report to the local people's government, the higher-level environmental protection administrative department, and the office of the Leading Group, and shall notify the environmental protection administrative departments and water administrative departments of the adjacent upstream and downstream areas. The local people's government shall take emergency measures to eliminate or mitigate the pollution damage.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　淮河流域省际水污染纠纷，由领导小组办公室进行调查、监测，提出解决方案，报领导小组协调处理。

**Article 28** Inter-provincial water pollution disputes in the Huaihe River Basin shall be investigated and monitored by the office of the Leading Group, which shall propose solutions and submit them to the Leading Group for coordinated handling.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　领导小组办公室根据领导小组的授权，可以组织四省人民政府环境保护行政主管部门、水行政主管部门等检查淮河流域水污染防治工作。被检查单位必须如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。

**Article 29** The office of the Leading Group may, as authorized by the Leading Group, organize the environmental protection administrative departments and water administrative departments of the people's governments of the four provinces to inspect the work of prevention and control of water pollution in the Huaihe River Basin. The units being inspected shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide necessary materials.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　排污单位有下列情形之一的，由有关县级以上人民政府责令关闭或者停业：  
(一)造成严重污染，又没有治理价值的；  
(二)自1998年1月1日起，工业企业仍然超标排污的。

**Article 30** Where a pollutant-discharging unit falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall order its closure or cessation of business:  
(1) It has caused serious pollution and has no treatment value;  
(2) As of January 1, 1998, industrial enterprises still discharge pollutants in excess of the standards.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在限期治理期限内，未完成治理任务的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令限量排污，可以处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由有关县级以上人民政府责令关闭或者停业。

**Article 31** Where a party fails to complete the remediation task within the time limit for remediation, the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to limit its discharge of pollutants and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant people's government at or above the county level shall order it to close down or suspend business.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　擅自在河流、湖泊、水库、渠道管理范围内设置或者扩大排污口的，由有关县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门或者水行政主管部门责令纠正，可以处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 32** Where a party, without authorization, installs or expands a discharge outlet within the management scope of a river, lake, reservoir or canal, the relevant environmental protection administrative department or water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to rectify the situation and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　自本条例施行之日起，新建化学制浆造纸企业和制革、化工、印染、电镀、酿造等污染严重的小型企业或者未经批准建设属于严格限制的项目的，由有关县级人民政府责令停止建设或者关闭，环境保护行政主管部门可以处20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 33** From the date these Regulations come into force, if a new chemical pulping and papermaking enterprise, or a small enterprise with serious pollution such as tanning, chemical, printing and dyeing, electroplating, brewing, etc., is built, or a project that is strictly restricted is constructed without approval, the relevant county people's government shall order it to stop construction or close down, and the environmental protection administrative department may impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　环境保护行政主管部门超过本行政区域的排污总量控制指标，批准建设项目环境影响报告书的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** Where an environmental protection administrative department approves an environmental impact report of a construction project in excess of the total discharge control quota of its administrative region, the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反枯水期污染源限排方案超量排污的，由有关县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令纠正，可以处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由有关县级以上人民政府责令关闭或者停业；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 35** Where a party violates the plan for restricted discharge of pollution sources during the dry season and discharges pollutants in excessive amounts, the relevant environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to rectify the situation and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant people's government at or above the county level shall order it to close down or suspend business; the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例规定的责令企业事业单位停止建设或者停业、关闭，由作出限期治理决定的人民政府决定；责令中央管辖的企业事业单位停止建设或者停业、关闭，须报国务院批准。

**Article 36** The order to stop construction or suspend business or close down an enterprise or institution as provided in these Regulations shall be decided by the people's government that made the time-limit remediation decision; an order to stop construction or suspend business or close down an enterprise or institution under the jurisdiction of the central government shall be subject to approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级人民政府环境保护行政主管部门或者水行政主管部门决定的罚款额，以不超过1万元为限；超过1万元的，应当报上一级环境保护行政主管部门或者水行政主管部门批准。  
设区的市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门决定的罚款额，以不超过5万元为限；超过5万元的，应当报上一级环境保护行政主管部门批准。

**Article 37** The amount of a fine decided by the environmental protection administrative department or the water administrative department of a county people's government shall not exceed 10,000 yuan; if it exceeds 10,000 yuan, approval from the environmental protection administrative department or water administrative department at the next higher level shall be obtained.  
The amount of a fine decided by the environmental protection administrative department of a people's government of a city divided into districts shall not exceed 50,000 yuan; if it exceeds 50,000 yuan, approval from the environmental protection administrative department at the next higher level shall be obtained.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反水闸防污调控方案调度水闸的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令纠正；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 38** Where a party operates a sluice gate in violation of the anti-pollution regulation plan for sluice gates, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to rectify the situation; the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　因发生水污染事故，造成重大经济损失或者人员伤亡，负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where a water pollution accident causes significant economic losses or casualties, and the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons whose acts constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　拒绝、阻碍承担本条例规定职责的国家工作人员依法执行职务，违反治安管理的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where a party refuses or obstructs a state functionary who assumes responsibilities under these Regulations from performing his duties according to law, and such act violates public security administration, the party shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　承担本条例规定职责的国家工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守，或者拒不履行义务，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 41** State functionaries who assume duties prescribed in these Regulations and abuse their power, engage in malpractices for personal gain, neglect their duties, or refuse to perform their obligations, where such acts constitute a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; where the acts do not constitute a crime, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　四省人民政府可以根据本条例分别制定实施办法。

**Article 42** The people's governments of the four provinces may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate implementing measures respectively.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例自1995年8月8日起施行。

**Article 43** These Regulations shall come into force on August 8, 1995.
