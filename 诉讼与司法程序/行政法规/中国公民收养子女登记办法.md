# 中国公民收养子女登记办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

> （1999年5月12日国务院批准　1999年5月25日民政部令第14号发布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2023年7月20日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一条　为了规范收养登记行为，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本办法。 第二条　中国公民在中国境内收养子女或者协议解除收养关系的，应当依照本办法的规定办理登记。 办理收养登记的机关是县级人民政府民政部门。 第三条　收养登记工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，遵循最有利于被收养人的原则，保障被收养人和收养人的合法权益。 第四条　收养社会福利机构抚养的查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童和孤儿的，在社会福利机构所在地的收养登记机关办理登记。 收养非社会福利机构抚养的查找不到生父母的弃婴和儿童的，在弃婴和儿童发现地的收养登记机关办理登记。 收养生父母有特殊困难无力抚养的子女或者由监护人监护的孤儿的，在被收养人生父母或者监护人常住户口所在地（组织作监护人的，在该组织所在地）的收养登记机关办理登记。 收养三代以内同辈旁系血亲的子女，以及继父或者继母收养继子女的，在被收养人生父或者生母常住户口所在地的收养登记机关办理登记。 第五条　收养关系当事人应当亲自到收养登记机关办理成立收养关系的登记手续。 夫妻共同收养子女的，应当共同到收养登记机关办理登记手续；一方因故不能亲自前往的，应当书面委托另一方办理登记手续，委托书应当经过村民委员会或者居民委员会证明或者经过公证。 第六条　收养人应当向收养登记机关提交收养申请书和下列证件、证明材料： （一）收养人的居民户口簿和居民身份证； （二）由收养人所在单位或者村民委员会、居民委员会出具的本人婚姻状况和抚养教育被收养人的能力等情况的证明，以及收养人出具的子女情况声明； （三）县级以上医疗机构出具的未患有在医学上认为不应当收养子女的疾病的身体健康检查证明。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范收养登记行为，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Code) for the purpose of regulating adoption registration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国公民在中国境内收养子女或者协议解除收养关系的，应当依照本办法的规定办理登记。  
办理收养登记的机关是县级人民政府民政部门。

**Article 2** Chinese citizens who adopt a child within the territory of China or dissolve an adoption relationship by agreement shall register in accordance with these Measures.  
The organ handling adoption registration shall be the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　收养登记工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，遵循最有利于被收养人的原则，保障被收养人和收养人的合法权益。

**Article 3** The adoption registration work shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, follow the principle of the best interests of the adopted person, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of both the adopted person and the adopter.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　收养社会福利机构抚养的查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童和孤儿的，在社会福利机构所在地的收养登记机关办理登记。  
收养非社会福利机构抚养的查找不到生父母的弃婴和儿童的，在弃婴和儿童发现地的收养登记机关办理登记。  
收养生父母有特殊困难无力抚养的子女或者由监护人监护的孤儿的，在被收养人生父母或者监护人常住户口所在地（组织作监护人的，在该组织所在地）的收养登记机关办理登记。  
收养三代以内同辈旁系血亲的子女，以及继父或者继母收养继子女的，在被收养人生父或者生母常住户口所在地的收养登记机关办理登记。

**Article 4** Where an abandoned infant, child, or orphan whose biological parents cannot be found and who is raised by a social welfare institution is adopted, registration shall be handled at the adoption registration organ in the locality where the social welfare institution is located.  
Where an abandoned infant or child whose biological parents cannot be found and who is not raised by a social welfare institution is adopted, registration shall be handled at the adoption registration organ in the locality where the abandoned infant or child was discovered.  
Where a child whose biological parents have special difficulties and are unable to raise the child, or an orphan under guardianship is adopted, registration shall be handled at the adoption registration organ in the locality of the permanent residence of the biological parents or guardian of the adopted person (where an organization acts as the guardian, in the locality of the organization).  
Where a child of collateral relatives by blood within three generations is adopted, or a stepfather or stepmother adopts a stepchild, registration shall be handled at the adoption registration organ in the locality of the permanent residence of the biological father or biological mother of the adopted person.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　收养关系当事人应当亲自到收养登记机关办理成立收养关系的登记手续。  
夫妻共同收养子女的，应当共同到收养登记机关办理登记手续；一方因故不能亲自前往的，应当书面委托另一方办理登记手续，委托书应当经过村民委员会或者居民委员会证明或者经过公证。

**Article 5** The parties to an adoption relationship shall appear in person at the adoption registration organ to go through the registration formalities for establishing the adoption relationship.  
Where a husband and wife jointly adopt a child, they shall jointly go to the adoption registration organ to go through the registration formalities; if one party cannot appear in person due to a reason, he or she shall entrust the other party in writing to go through the registration formalities, and the power of attorney shall be certified by the villagers' committee or residents' committee or be notarized.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　收养人应当向收养登记机关提交收养申请书和下列证件、证明材料：  
（一）收养人的居民户口簿和居民身份证；  
（二）由收养人所在单位或者村民委员会、居民委员会出具的本人婚姻状况和抚养教育被收养人的能力等情况的证明，以及收养人出具的子女情况声明；  
（三）县级以上医疗机构出具的未患有在医学上认为不应当收养子女的疾病的身体健康检查证明。  
收养查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童的，并应当提交收养人经常居住地卫生健康主管部门出具的收养人生育情况证明；其中收养非社会福利机构抚养的查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童的，收养人应当提交下列证明材料：  
（一）收养人经常居住地卫生健康主管部门出具的收养人生育情况证明；  
（二）公安机关出具的捡拾弃婴、儿童报案的证明。  
收养继子女的，可以只提交居民户口簿、居民身份证和收养人与被收养人生父或者生母结婚的证明。  
对收养人出具的子女情况声明，登记机关可以进行调查核实。

**Article 6** An adopter shall submit to the adoption registration organ an adoption application and the following certificates and evidentiary materials:  
(1) The adopter's residence booklet and resident identity card;  
(2) Certificates issued by the adopter's work unit or by the villagers' committee or residents' committee, evidencing the adopter's marital status and ability to raise and educate the adopted person, as well as a statement on the adopter's own children;  
(3) A physical health examination certificate issued by a medical institution at or above the county level, certifying that the adopter does not suffer from any disease which is medically considered to disqualify the person from adopting a child.  
Where the adoption involves an abandoned infant or child whose biological parents cannot be found, the adopter shall also submit a certificate from the health authority of the adopter's habitual residence evidencing the adopter's fertility status; among such cases, where the abandoned infant or child is not raised by a social welfare institution, the adopter shall submit the following evidentiary materials:  
(1) A certificate from the health authority of the adopter's habitual residence evidencing the adopter's fertility status;  
(2) A certificate issued by the public security organ evidencing the report of finding the abandoned infant or child.  
Where the adoption involves a stepchild, the adopter may only submit the residence booklet, resident identity card, and a certificate of marriage between the adopter and the biological father or biological mother of the adopted person.  
The registration organ may investigate and verify the statement on the adopter's own children submitted by the adopter.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　送养人应当向收养登记机关提交下列证件和证明材料：  
（一）送养人的居民户口簿和居民身份证（组织作监护人的，提交其负责人的身份证件）；  
（二）民法典规定送养时应当征得其他有抚养义务的人同意的，并提交其他有抚养义务的人同意送养的书面意见。  
社会福利机构为送养人的，并应当提交弃婴、儿童进入社会福利机构的原始记录，公安机关出具的捡拾弃婴、儿童报案的证明，或者孤儿的生父母死亡或者宣告死亡的证明。  
监护人为送养人的，并应当提交实际承担监护责任的证明，孤儿的父母死亡或者宣告死亡的证明，或者被收养人生父母无完全民事行为能力并对被收养人有严重危害的证明。  
生父母为送养人，有特殊困难无力抚养子女的，还应当提交送养人有特殊困难的声明；因丧偶或者一方下落不明由单方送养的，还应当提交配偶死亡或者下落不明的证明。对送养人有特殊困难的声明，登记机关可以进行调查核实；子女由三代以内同辈旁系血亲收养的，还应当提交公安机关出具的或者经过公证的与收养人有亲属关系的证明。  
被收养人是残疾儿童的，并应当提交县级以上医疗机构出具的该儿童的残疾证明。

**Article 7** The person placing out the child for adoption shall submit to the adoption registration organ the following certificates and evidentiary materials:  
(1) The residence booklet and resident identity card of the person placing out the child (where an organization acts as guardian, the identity document of its responsible person shall be submitted);  
(2) Where the Civil Code requires that consent of other persons with the obligation to support be obtained when placing out a child, the written consent of such other persons to the placement shall also be submitted.  
Where a social welfare institution places out a child, it shall also submit the original records of the abandoned infant or child entering the social welfare institution, a certificate issued by the public security organ evidencing the report of finding the abandoned infant or child, or a certificate of death or declaration of death of the orphan's biological parents.  
Where a guardian places out a child, the guardian shall also submit evidence that the guardian actually bears the guardianship responsibility, a certificate of death or declaration of death of the orphan's parents, or a certificate that the biological parents of the adopted person lack full civil capacity and pose a serious danger to the adopted person.  
Where biological parents place out a child due to special difficulties rendering them unable to raise the child, they shall also submit a statement of special difficulties; where a single parent places out a child due to the spouse's death or disappearance, a certificate of the spouse's death or disappearance shall also be submitted. The registration organ may investigate and verify the statement of special difficulties of the person placing out the child; where the child is adopted by a collateral relative by blood within three generations, a certificate issued by the public security organ or notarized evidencing the kinship relationship with the adopter shall also be submitted.  
Where the adopted person is a disabled child, a disability certificate for the child issued by a medical institution at or above the county level shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　收养登记机关收到收养登记申请书及有关材料后，应当自次日起30日内进行审查。对符合民法典规定条件的，为当事人办理收养登记，发给收养登记证，收养关系自登记之日起成立；对不符合民法典规定条件的，不予登记，并对当事人说明理由。  
收养查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童的，收养登记机关应当在登记前公告查找其生父母；自公告之日起满60日，弃婴、儿童的生父母或者其他监护人未认领的，视为查找不到生父母的弃婴、儿童。公告期间不计算在登记办理期限内。

**Article 8** After receiving the adoption registration application and relevant materials, the adoption registration organ shall conduct a review within 30 days from the next day. For those meeting the conditions prescribed by the Civil Code, the organ shall handle the adoption registration for the parties, issue the adoption registration certificate, and the adoption relationship shall be established from the date of registration; for those not meeting the conditions prescribed by the Civil Code, the organ shall not register and shall explain the reasons to the parties.  
Where an abandoned infant or child whose biological parents cannot be found is adopted, the adoption registration organ shall, before registration, make a public announcement to search for the biological parents; if after 60 days from the date of the announcement, the biological parents or other guardians of the abandoned infant or child do not claim the child, the child shall be deemed to be an abandoned infant or child whose biological parents cannot be found. The announcement period shall not be counted in the registration processing period.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　收养关系成立后，需要为被收养人办理户口登记或者迁移手续的，由收养人持收养登记证到户口登记机关按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 9** After the adoption relationship is established, if it is necessary to go through the formalities of household registration or relocation for the adopted person, the adopter shall, with the adoption registration certificate, go to the household registration authority to handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　收养关系当事人协议解除收养关系的，应当持居民户口簿、居民身份证、收养登记证和解除收养关系的书面协议，共同到被收养人常住户口所在地的收养登记机关办理解除收养关系登记。

**Article 10** Where the parties to an adoption relationship agree to dissolve the adoption relationship, they shall, with their residence booklets, resident identity cards, the adoption registration certificate, and a written agreement on the dissolution of the adoption relationship, jointly go to the adoption registration organ in the locality of the permanent residence of the adopted person to go through the registration for dissolution of the adoption relationship.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　收养登记机关收到解除收养关系登记申请书及有关材料后，应当自次日起30日内进行审查；对符合民法典规定的，为当事人办理解除收养关系的登记，收回收养登记证，发给解除收养关系证明。

**Article 11** The adoption registration authority shall, within 30 days from the day after receiving the application for deregistration of the adoption relationship and the relevant materials, conduct a review; for those that meet the provisions of the Civil Code, it shall handle the registration of deregistration of the adoption relationship, retrieve the adoption registration certificate, and issue a certificate of deregistration of the adoption relationship.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　为收养关系当事人出具证明材料的组织，应当如实出具有关证明材料。出具虚假证明材料的，由收养登记机关没收虚假证明材料，并建议有关组织对直接责任人员给予批评教育，或者依法给予行政处分、纪律处分。

**Article 12** An organization that issues evidentiary materials for the parties to an adoption relationship shall truthfully issue the relevant evidentiary materials. Where false evidentiary materials are issued, the adoption registration authority shall confiscate the false evidentiary materials and recommend that the relevant organization provide criticism and education to the directly responsible person, or impose administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　收养关系当事人弄虚作假骗取收养登记的，收养关系无效，由收养登记机关撤销登记，收缴收养登记证。

**Article 13** Where a party to an adoption relationship obtains adoption registration by fraud, the adoption relationship shall be invalid, and the adoption registration authority shall revoke the registration and confiscate the adoption registration certificate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本办法规定的收养登记证、解除收养关系证明的式样，由国务院民政部门制订。

**Article 14** The format of the adoption registration certificate and the certificate of deregistration of the adoption relationship as provided in these Measures shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　华侨以及居住在香港、澳门、台湾地区的中国公民在内地收养子女的，申请办理收养登记的管辖以及所需要出具的证件和证明材料，按照国务院民政部门的有关规定执行。

**Article 15** Where overseas Chinese and Chinese citizens residing in Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan adopt children in the mainland, the jurisdiction for applying for adoption registration and the required certificates and evidentiary materials shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 16** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
