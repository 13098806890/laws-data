# 中华人民共和国保障措施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2004-03-31  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

> (2001年11月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第330号公布　根据2004年3月31日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国保障措施条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进对外贸易健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　进口产品数量增加，并对生产同类产品或者直接竞争产品的国内产业造成严重损害或者严重损害威胁(以下除特别指明外，统称损害)的，依照本条例的规定进行调查，采取保障措施。 第二章　调查 第三条　与国内产业有关的自然人、法人或者其他组织(以下统称申请人)，可以依照本条例的规定，向商务部提出采取保障措施的书面申请。 商务部应当及时对申请人的申请进行审查，决定立案调查或者不立案调查。 第四条　商务部没有收到采取保障措施的书面申请，但有充分证据认为国内产业因进口产品数量增加而受到损害的，可以决定立案调查。 第五条　立案调查的决定，由商务部予以公告。 商务部应当将立案调查的决定及时通知世界贸易组织保障措施委员会(以下简称保障措施委员会)。 第六条　对进口产品数量增加及损害的调查和确定，由商务部负责；其中，涉及农产品的保障措施国内产业损害调查，由商务部会同农业部进行。 第七条　进口产品数量增加，是指进口产品数量的绝对增加或者与国内生产相比的相对增加。 第八条　在确定进口产品数量增加对国内产业造成的损害时，应当审查下列相关因素： (一)进口产品的绝对和相对增长率与增长量； (二)增加的进口产品在国内市场中所占的份额；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进对外贸易健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of promoting the healthy development of foreign trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进口产品数量增加，并对生产同类产品或者直接竞争产品的国内产业造成严重损害或者严重损害威胁(以下除特别指明外，统称损害)的，依照本条例的规定进行调查，采取保障措施。

**Article 2** Where an increase in the quantity of imported products causes or threatens to cause serious injury to a domestic industry producing like or directly competitive products (hereinafter collectively referred to as injury, unless otherwise specified), an investigation shall be conducted and safeguard measures shall be taken in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

### 第二章　调查

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　与国内产业有关的自然人、法人或者其他组织(以下统称申请人)，可以依照本条例的规定，向商务部提出采取保障措施的书面申请。  
商务部应当及时对申请人的申请进行审查，决定立案调查或者不立案调查。

**Article 3** Any natural person, legal person or other organization concerned with the domestic industry (hereinafter collectively referred to as the applicant) may submit a written application for taking safeguard measures to the Ministry of Commerce in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall promptly examine the application of the applicant and decide to initiate an investigation or not to initiate an investigation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　商务部没有收到采取保障措施的书面申请，但有充分证据认为国内产业因进口产品数量增加而受到损害的，可以决定立案调查。

**Article 4** Where the Ministry of Commerce has not received any written application for taking safeguard measures but has sufficient evidence to believe that the domestic industry is suffering injury due to an increase in the quantity of imported products, it may decide to initiate an investigation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　立案调查的决定，由商务部予以公告。  
商务部应当将立案调查的决定及时通知世界贸易组织保障措施委员会(以下简称保障措施委员会)。

**Article 5** The decision to initiate an investigation shall be announced by the Ministry of Commerce.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall promptly notify the World Trade Organization Committee on Safeguards (hereinafter referred to as the Committee on Safeguards) of the decision to initiate an investigation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对进口产品数量增加及损害的调查和确定，由商务部负责；其中，涉及农产品的保障措施国内产业损害调查，由商务部会同农业部进行。

**Article 6** The investigation and determination of the increase in the quantity of imported products and the injury caused thereby shall be the responsibility of the Ministry of Commerce; provided that, where agricultural products are involved, the investigation of injury to the domestic industry for the purpose of safeguard measures shall be conducted by the Ministry of Commerce jointly with the Ministry of Agriculture.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　进口产品数量增加，是指进口产品数量的绝对增加或者与国内生产相比的相对增加。

**Article 7** An increase in the quantity of imported products refers to an absolute increase in the quantity of imported products or a relative increase compared with domestic production.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在确定进口产品数量增加对国内产业造成的损害时，应当审查下列相关因素：  
(一)进口产品的绝对和相对增长率与增长量；  
(二)增加的进口产品在国内市场中所占的份额；  
(三)进口产品对国内产业的影响，包括对国内产业在产量、销售水平、市场份额、生产率、设备利用率、利润与亏损、就业等方面的影响；  
(四)造成国内产业损害的其他因素。  
对严重损害威胁的确定，应当依据事实，不能仅依据指控、推测或者极小的可能性。  
在确定进口产品数量增加对国内产业造成的损害时，不得将进口增加以外的因素对国内产业造成的损害归因于进口增加。

**Article 8** In determining the injury caused to the domestic industry by an increase in the quantity of imported products, the following relevant factors shall be examined:  
(1) the absolute and relative rate and amount of increase in imported products;  
(2) the share of the domestic market taken by the increased imports;  
(3) the impact of imports on the domestic industry, including impact on output, sales level, market share, productivity, capacity utilization, profits and losses, employment, etc. of the domestic industry;  
(4) other factors causing injury to the domestic industry.  
The determination of a threat of serious injury shall be based on facts and not merely on allegation, conjecture or remote possibility.  
In determining the injury caused to the domestic industry by an increase in the quantity of imported products, the injury caused to the domestic industry by factors other than increased imports shall not be attributed to increased imports.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在调查期间，商务部应当及时公布对案情的详细分析和审查的相关因素等。

**Article 9** During the investigation period, the Ministry of Commerce shall timely publish a detailed analysis of the case and the relevant factors examined.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国内产业，是指中华人民共和国国内同类产品或者直接竞争产品的全部生产者，或者其总产量占国内同类产品或者直接竞争产品全部总产量的主要部分的生产者。

**Article 10** Domestic industry refers to the domestic producers as a whole of the like or directly competitive products within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or those producers whose collective output of the like or directly competitive products constitutes a major proportion of the total domestic production of those products.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　商务部应当根据客观的事实和证据，确定进口产品数量增加与国内产业的损害之间是否存在因果关系。

**Article 11** The Ministry of Commerce shall determine, based on objective facts and evidence, whether there is a causal relationship between the increase in the quantity of imported products and the injury to the domestic industry.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　商务部应当为进口经营者、出口经营者和其他利害关系方提供陈述意见和论据的机会。  
调查可以采用调查问卷的方式，也可以采用听证会或者其他方式。

**Article 12** The Ministry of Commerce shall provide opportunities for importers, exporters and other interested parties to present their views and arguments.  
The investigation may be conducted by means of questionnaires, hearings or other methods.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　调查中获得的有关资料，资料提供方认为需要保密的，商务部可以按保密资料处理。  
保密申请有理由的，应当对资料提供方提供的资料按保密资料处理，同时要求资料提供方提供一份非保密的该资料概要。  
按保密资料处理的资料，未经资料提供方同意，不得泄露。

**Article 13** Where the provider of relevant information obtained during investigation considers that such information requires confidential treatment, the Ministry of Commerce may treat it as confidential information.  
If the application for confidential treatment is justified, the information provided by the provider shall be treated as confidential information, and the provider shall be requested to provide a non-confidential summary thereof.  
Information treated as confidential information shall not be disclosed without the consent of the provider.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　进口产品数量增加、损害的调查结果及其理由的说明，由商务部予以公布。  
商务部应当将调查结果及有关情况及时通知保障措施委员会。

**Article 14** The findings of the investigation on the increase in the quantity of imported products and injury, together with a statement of reasons therefor, shall be published by the Ministry of Commerce.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall promptly notify the Committee on Safeguards of the findings and relevant information.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　商务部根据调查结果，可以作出初裁决定，也可以直接作出终裁决定，并予以公告。

**Article 15** The Ministry of Commerce may, based on the results of the investigation, make a preliminary determination, or may directly make a final determination, and shall announce the same.

### 第三章　保障措施

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有明确证据表明进口产品数量增加，在不采取临时保障措施将对国内产业造成难以补救的损害的紧急情况下，可以作出初裁决定，并采取临时保障措施。  
临时保障措施采取提高关税的形式。

**Article 16** Where there is clear evidence that the quantity of imported products has increased, and in critical circumstances where delay would cause damage to the domestic industry which would be difficult to repair, a preliminary determination may be made and provisional safeguard measures may be applied.  
Provisional safeguard measures shall take the form of tariff increases.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　采取临时保障措施，由商务部提出建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告。海关自公告规定实施之日起执行。  
在采取临时保障措施前，商务部应当将有关情况通知保障措施委员会。

**Article 17** For the application of provisional safeguard measures, the Ministry of Commerce shall make a recommendation, the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendation of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the same. The Customs shall enforce the measures from the date of implementation specified in the announcement.  
Before applying provisional safeguard measures, the Ministry of Commerce shall notify the Committee on Safeguards of the relevant information.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　临时保障措施的实施期限，自临时保障措施决定公告规定实施之日起，不超过200天。

**Article 18** The period of application of provisional safeguard measures shall not exceed 200 days from the date of implementation specified in the announcement of the decision on provisional safeguard measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　终裁决定确定进口产品数量增加，并由此对国内产业造成损害的，可以采取保障措施。实施保障措施应当符合公共利益。  
保障措施可以采取提高关税、数量限制等形式。

**Article 19** Where the final determination finds that the quantity of imported products has increased and thereby causes injury to the domestic industry, safeguard measures may be applied. The application of safeguard measures shall be in the public interest.  
Safeguard measures may take the form of tariff increases, quantitative restrictions, etc.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　保障措施采取提高关税形式的，由商务部提出建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告；采取数量限制形式的，由商务部作出决定并予以公告。海关自公告规定实施之日起执行。  
商务部应当将采取保障措施的决定及有关情况及时通知保障措施委员会。

**Article 20** Where safeguard measures take the form of tariff increases, the Ministry of Commerce shall make a recommendation, the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendation of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the same; where they take the form of quantitative restrictions, the Ministry of Commerce shall make a decision and announce the same. The Customs shall enforce the measures from the date of implementation specified in the announcement.  
The Ministry of Commerce shall promptly notify the Committee on Safeguards of the decision to apply safeguard measures and relevant information.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　采取数量限制措施的，限制后的进口量不得低于最近3个有代表性年度的平均进口量；但是，有正当理由表明为防止或者补救严重损害而有必要采取不同水平的数量限制措施的除外。  
采取数量限制措施，需要在有关出口国(地区)或者原产国(地区)之间进行数量分配的，商务部可以与有关出口国(地区)或者原产国(地区)就数量的分配进行磋商。

**Article 21** Where quantitative restriction measures are applied, the quantity of imports after restriction shall not be less than the average quantity of imports in the most recent three representative years, unless there is proper justification that a different level of quantitative restriction is necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury.  
Where quantitative restriction measures are applied and the allocation of quantities among exporting or producing countries (regions) concerned is required, the Ministry of Commerce may consult with the exporting or producing countries (regions) concerned on the allocation of quantities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　保障措施应当针对正在进口的产品实施，不区分产品来源国(地区)。

**Article 22** Safeguard measures shall be applied to a product being imported irrespective of its source country (region).

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　采取保障措施应当限于防止、补救严重损害并便利调整国内产业所必要的范围内。

**Article 23** Safeguard measures shall be applied only to the extent necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury and to facilitate adjustment of the domestic industry.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在采取保障措施前，商务部应当为与有关产品的出口经营者有实质利益的国家(地区)政府提供磋商的充分机会。

**Article 24** Before applying safeguard measures, the Ministry of Commerce shall provide adequate opportunity for consultations with the governments of countries (regions) having a substantial interest as exporters of the product concerned.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　终裁决定确定不采取保障措施的，已征收的临时关税应当予以退还。

**Article 25** Where the final determination decides not to apply safeguard measures, the provisional customs duties that have been collected shall be refunded.

### 第四章　保障措施的期限与复审

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　保障措施的实施期限不超过4年。  
符合下列条件的，保障措施的实施期限可以适当延长：  
(一)按照本条例规定的程序确定保障措施对于防止或者补救严重损害仍然有必要；  
(二)有证据表明相关国内产业正在进行调整；  
(三)已经履行有关对外通知、磋商的义务；  
(四)延长后的措施不严于延长前的措施。  
一项保障措施的实施期限及其延长期限，最长不超过10年。

**Article 26** The period of application of safeguard measures shall not exceed 4 years.  
The period of application of safeguard measures may be extended if the following conditions are met:  
(1) it is determined in accordance with the procedures prescribed in these Regulations that the safeguard measures remain necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury;  
(2) there is evidence that the relevant domestic industry is adjusting;  
(3) the relevant obligations regarding external notification and consultations have been fulfilled; and  
(4) the extended measures are not more restrictive than the measures before extension.  
The total period of application of a safeguard measure, including the period of initial application and any extension thereof, shall not exceed 10 years.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　保障措施实施期限超过1年的，应当在实施期间内按固定时间间隔逐步放宽。

**Article 27** Where the period of application of safeguard measures exceeds 1 year, the measures shall be progressively liberalized at regular intervals during the period of application.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　保障措施实施期限超过3年的，商务部应当在实施期间内对该项措施进行中期复审。  
复审的内容包括保障措施对国内产业的影响、国内产业的调整情况等。

**Article 28** Where the period of application of safeguard measures exceeds 3 years, the Ministry of Commerce shall conduct a mid-term review of the measures during the period of application.  
The review shall cover, inter alia, the impact of the safeguard measures on the domestic industry and the adjustment progress of the domestic industry.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　保障措施属于提高关税的，商务部应当根据复审结果，依照本条例的规定，提出保留、取消或者加快放宽提高关税措施的建议，国务院关税税则委员会根据商务部的建议作出决定，由商务部予以公告；保障措施属于数量限制或者其他形式的，商务部应当根据复审结果，依照本条例的规定，作出保留、取消或者加快放宽数量限制措施的决定并予以公告。

**Article 29** Where the safeguard measures are in the form of tariff increases, the Ministry of Commerce shall, based on the results of the review, make recommendations on maintaining, eliminating or accelerating the liberalization of the tariff increase measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council shall make a decision based on the recommendations of the Ministry of Commerce, and the Ministry of Commerce shall announce the same; where the safeguard measures are in the form of quantitative restrictions or other forms, the Ministry of Commerce shall, based on the results of the review, make a decision on maintaining, eliminating or accelerating the liberalization of the quantitative restriction measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and announce the same.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对同一进口产品再次采取保障措施的，与前次采取保障措施的时间间隔应当不短于前次采取保障措施的实施期限，并且至少为2年。  
符合下列条件的，对一产品实施的期限为180天或者少于180天的保障措施，不受前款限制：  
(一)自对该进口产品实施保障措施之日起，已经超过1年；  
(二)自实施该保障措施之日起5年内，未对同一产品实施2次以上保障措施。

**Article 30** Where safeguard measures are applied again to the same imported product, the time interval between the new application and the previous application shall not be shorter than the period of application of the previous safeguard measures and shall be at least 2 years.  
Safeguard measures applied to a product for a period of 180 days or less shall not be subject to the preceding paragraph if the following conditions are met:  
(1) at least 1 year has elapsed since the date of application of the safeguard measures to the imported product; and  
(2) no safeguard measure has been applied to the same product more than twice in the 5-year period commencing on the date of application of such safeguard measures.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何国家(地区)对中华人民共和国的出口产品采取歧视性保障措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对该国家(地区)采取相应的措施。

**Article 31** Where any country (region) applies discriminatory safeguard measures against export products of the People's Republic of China, the People's Republic of China may, in light of the actual circumstances, take corresponding measures against that country (region).

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　商务部负责与保障措施有关的对外磋商、通知和争端解决事宜。

**Article 32** The Ministry of Commerce shall be responsible for external consultations, notifications and dispute settlement matters relating to safeguard measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　商务部可以根据本条例制定具体实施办法。

**Article 33** The Ministry of Commerce may formulate specific implementing rules in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
