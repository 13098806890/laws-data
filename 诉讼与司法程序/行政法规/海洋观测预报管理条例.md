# 海洋观测预报管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

> （2012年3月1日中华人民共和国国务院令第615号公布　根据2023年7月20日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强海洋观测预报管理，规范海洋观测预报活动，防御和减轻海洋灾害，为经济建设、国防建设和社会发展服务，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋观测预报活动，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　海洋观测预报事业是基础性公益事业。国务院和沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当将海洋观测预报事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算。 第四条　国务院海洋主管部门主管全国海洋观测预报工作。 国务院海洋主管部门的海区派出机构依照本条例和国务院海洋主管部门规定的权限，负责所管辖海域的海洋观测预报监督管理。 沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门主管本行政区毗邻海域的海洋观测预报工作。 第五条　国家鼓励、支持海洋观测预报科学技术的研究，推广先进的技术和设备，培养海洋观测预报人才，促进海洋观测预报业务水平的提高。 对在海洋观测预报工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　海洋观测网的规划、建设与保护 第六条　国务院海洋主管部门负责编制全国海洋观测网规划。编制全国海洋观测网规划应当征求国务院有关部门和有关军事机关的意见，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准后实施。 沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门应当根据全国海洋观测网规划和本行政区毗邻海域的实际情况，编制本省、自治区、直辖市的海洋观测网规划，在征求本级人民政府有关部门的意见后，报本级人民政府批准实施，并报国务院海洋主管部门备案。 修改海洋观测网规划，应当按照规划编制程序报原批准机关批准。 第七条　编制海洋观测网规划，应当坚持统筹兼顾、突出重点、合理布局的原则，避免重复建设，保障国防安全。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海洋观测预报管理，规范海洋观测预报活动，防御和减轻海洋灾害，为经济建设、国防建设和社会发展服务，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the management of marine observation and forecasting, regulating marine observation and forecasting activities, preventing and mitigating marine disasters, and serving the economic construction, national defence construction, and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋观测预报活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any entity or individual that engages in marine observation and forecasting activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海洋观测预报事业是基础性公益事业。国务院和沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当将海洋观测预报事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算。

**Article 3** The marine observation and forecasting undertaking is a basic public welfare undertaking. The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall incorporate the marine observation and forecasting undertaking into the national economic and social development plans of their respective levels, and the necessary funds shall be included in the fiscal budgets of their respective levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院海洋主管部门主管全国海洋观测预报工作。  
国务院海洋主管部门的海区派出机构依照本条例和国务院海洋主管部门规定的权限，负责所管辖海域的海洋观测预报监督管理。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门主管本行政区毗邻海域的海洋观测预报工作。

**Article 4** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the marine observation and forecasting work nationwide.  
The regional offices of the marine administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the powers prescribed by the marine administrative department of the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of marine observation and forecasting in the sea areas under their jurisdiction.  
The marine administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the marine observation and forecasting work in the sea areas adjacent to their respective administrative divisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励、支持海洋观测预报科学技术的研究，推广先进的技术和设备，培养海洋观测预报人才，促进海洋观测预报业务水平的提高。  
对在海洋观测预报工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 5** The State encourages and supports research on the science and technology of marine observation and forecasting, promotes advanced technologies and equipment, cultivates talents for marine observation and forecasting, and promotes the improvement of the level of marine observation and forecasting services.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in marine observation and forecasting work shall be commended and rewarded.

### 第二章　海洋观测网的规划、建设与保护

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院海洋主管部门负责编制全国海洋观测网规划。编制全国海洋观测网规划应当征求国务院有关部门和有关军事机关的意见，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准后实施。  
沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门应当根据全国海洋观测网规划和本行政区毗邻海域的实际情况，编制本省、自治区、直辖市的海洋观测网规划，在征求本级人民政府有关部门的意见后，报本级人民政府批准实施，并报国务院海洋主管部门备案。  
修改海洋观测网规划，应当按照规划编制程序报原批准机关批准。

**Article 6** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the national marine observation network plan. The national marine observation network plan shall be formulated after soliciting the opinions of the relevant departments of the State Council and relevant military authorities, and shall be implemented upon approval by the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council.  
The marine administrative departments of the coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national marine observation network plan and the actual conditions of the sea areas adjacent to their respective administrative divisions, formulate the marine observation network plans of their respective provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. Such plans shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval and implementation after soliciting the opinions of the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, and shall be reported to the marine administrative department of the State Council for recordation.  
Any modification to a marine observation network plan shall be subject to the approval of the original approving authority in accordance with the planning formulation procedures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　编制海洋观测网规划，应当坚持统筹兼顾、突出重点、合理布局的原则，避免重复建设，保障国防安全。  
编制海洋观测网规划，应当将沿海城市和人口密集区、产业园区、滨海重大工程所在区、海洋灾害易发区和海上其他重要区域作为规划的重点。

**Article 7** In formulating a marine observation network plan, the principles of overall planning, highlighting key points, and rational layout shall be adhered to, duplication of construction shall be avoided, and national defence security shall be ensured.  
In formulating a marine observation network plan, the coastal cities and densely populated areas, industrial parks, areas where major coastal projects are located, areas prone to marine disasters, and other important offshore areas shall be taken as the key areas of the plan.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海洋观测网规划主要包括规划目标、海洋观测网体系构成、海洋观测站（点）总体布局及设施建设、保障措施等内容。

**Article 8** A marine observation network plan mainly includes such contents as the planning objectives, the composition of the marine observation network system, the overall layout and facility construction of marine observation stations (sites), and safeguard measures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　海洋观测网的建设应当符合海洋观测网规划，并按照国家固定资产投资项目建设程序组织实施。  
海洋观测站（点）的建设应当符合国家有关标准和技术要求，保证建设质量。

**Article 9** The construction of a marine observation network shall conform to the marine observation network plan, and shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the construction procedures for fixed-asset investment projects of the State.  
The construction of marine observation stations (sites) shall conform to the relevant national standards and technical requirements, and the construction quality shall be ensured.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门负责基本海洋观测站（点）的设立和调整。  
有关主管部门因水利、气象、航运等管理需要设立、调整有关观测站（点）开展海洋观测的，应当事先征求有关海洋主管部门的意见。  
其他单位或者个人因生产、科研等活动需要设立、调整海洋观测站（点）的，应当按照国务院海洋主管部门的规定，报有关海洋主管部门备案。

**Article 10** The marine administrative department of the State Council and the marine administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the establishment and adjustment of basic marine observation stations (sites).  
Where the relevant competent departments need to establish or adjust observation stations (sites) for marine observation due to the needs of water conservancy, meteorology, shipping or other management, they shall solicit the opinions of the relevant marine administrative departments in advance.  
Where other units or individuals need to establish or adjust marine observation stations (sites) for production, scientific research or other activities, they shall report to the relevant marine administrative department for recordation in accordance with the provisions of the marine administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海洋观测站（点）及其设施受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁损或者擅自移动。

**Article 11** Ocean observation stations (points) and their facilities shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual shall occupy, damage, or move them without authorization.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院海洋主管部门、沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门，应当商本级人民政府有关部门按照管理权限和国家有关标准划定基本海洋观测站（点）的海洋观测环境保护范围，予以公告，并根据需要在保护范围边界设立标志。  
禁止在海洋观测环境保护范围内进行下列活动：  
（一）设置障碍物、围填海；  
（二）设置影响海洋观测的高频电磁辐射装置；  
（三）影响海洋观测的矿产资源勘探开发、捕捞作业、水产养殖、倾倒废弃物、爆破等活动；  
（四）可能对海洋观测产生危害的其他活动。

**Article 12** The State Council's ocean administrative department and the ocean administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, after consulting with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and in accordance with the jurisdiction and relevant national standards, delimit the ocean observation environmental protection zones for basic ocean observation stations (points), make a public announcement thereof, and set up signs at the boundaries of the protection zones as needed.  
The following activities are prohibited within the ocean observation environmental protection zones:  
(1) setting up obstacles and reclamation;  
(2) installing high-frequency electromagnetic radiation devices affecting ocean observation;  
(3) activities such as mineral resource exploration and exploitation, fishing operations, aquaculture, dumping of waste, and blasting that affect ocean observation; and  
(4) other activities that may cause harm to ocean observation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　新建、改建、扩建建设工程，应当避免对海洋观测站（点）及其设施、观测环境造成危害；确实无法避免的，建设单位应当按照原负责或者批准设立、调整该海洋观测站（点）的主管部门的要求，在开工建设前采取增建抗干扰设施或者新建海洋观测站（点）等措施，所需费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 13** Construction of new, renovation, or expansion projects shall avoid causing harm to ocean observation stations (points) and their facilities and the observation environment; if it is truly unavoidable, the construction unit shall, in accordance with the requirements of the competent department originally responsible for establishing or approving the establishment or adjustment of the ocean observation station (point), take measures such as building additional anti-interference facilities or establishing new ocean observation stations (points) before the commencement of construction, and the necessary expenses shall be borne by the construction unit.

### 第三章　海洋观测与资料的汇交使用

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事海洋观测活动应当遵守国家海洋观测技术标准、规范和规程。  
从事海洋观测活动的单位应当建立质量保证体系和计量管理体系，加强对海洋观测资料获取和传输的质量控制，保证海洋观测资料的真实性、准确性和完整性。

**Article 14** Activities engaged in ocean observation shall comply with national ocean observation technical standards, specifications, and procedures.  
Units engaged in ocean observation activities shall establish a quality assurance system and a metrology management system, strengthen quality control over the acquisition and transmission of ocean observation data, and ensure the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of ocean observation data.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海洋观测使用的仪器设备应当符合国家有关产品标准、规范和海洋观测技术要求。  
海洋观测计量器具应当依法经计量检定合格。未经检定、检定不合格或者超过检定周期的计量器具，不得用于海洋观测。对不具备检定条件的海洋观测计量器具，应当通过校准保证量值溯源。

**Article 15** Instruments and equipment used for ocean observation shall conform to relevant national product standards, specifications, and technical requirements for ocean observation.  
Ocean observation metrological instruments shall, according to law, pass metrological verification. Instruments that have not been verified, have failed verification, or have exceeded the verification period shall not be used for ocean observation. For ocean observation metrological instruments that do not meet the conditions for verification, calibration shall be used to ensure traceability of values.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家建立海上船舶、平台志愿观测制度。  
承担志愿观测的船舶、平台所需要的海洋观测仪器设备由海洋主管部门负责购置、安装和维修；船舶、平台的所有权人或者使用权人应当予以配合，并承担日常管护责任。

**Article 16** The State establishes a voluntary observation system for ships and platforms at sea.  
The ocean observation instruments and equipment required for ships and platforms undertaking voluntary observation shall be purchased, installed, and maintained by the ocean administrative department; the owner or user of the ship or platform shall cooperate and shall bear the responsibility for daily management and maintenance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事海洋观测活动的单位应当按照国务院海洋主管部门的规定，将获取的海洋观测资料向有关海洋主管部门统一汇交。  
国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门应当妥善存储、保管海洋观测资料，并根据经济建设和社会发展需要对海洋观测资料进行加工整理，建立海洋观测资料数据库，实行资料共享。  
海洋观测资料的汇交、存储、保管、共享和使用应当遵守保守国家秘密法律、法规的规定。

**Article 17** Units engaged in ocean observation activities shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council's ocean administrative department, uniformly submit the ocean observation data obtained to the relevant ocean administrative department.  
The State Council's ocean administrative department and the ocean administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall properly store and preserve ocean observation data, and shall process and organize the ocean observation data according to the needs of economic construction and social development, establish a database of ocean observation data, and implement data sharing.  
The submission, storage, preservation, sharing, and use of ocean observation data shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on the protection of state secrets.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家机关决策和防灾减灾、国防建设、公共安全等公益事业需要使用海洋观测资料的，国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门应当无偿提供。

**Article 18** If state organs need to use ocean observation data for decision-making, disaster prevention and mitigation, national defense construction, public security, and other public welfare undertakings, the State Council's ocean administrative department and the ocean administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall provide it free of charge.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国际组织、外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋观测活动，依照《中华人民共和国涉外海洋科学研究管理规定》的规定执行。  
国际组织、外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋观测活动，应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法规，不得危害中华人民共和国的国家安全。

**Article 19** International organizations, foreign organizations, or individuals engaged in ocean observation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Foreign-Related Marine Scientific Research.  
International organizations, foreign organizations, or individuals engaged in ocean observation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall abide by the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China and shall not endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　任何单位和个人不得擅自向国际组织、外国的组织或者个人提供属于国家秘密的海洋观测资料和成果；确需提供的，应当报国务院海洋主管部门或者沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门批准；有关海洋主管部门在批准前，应当征求本级人民政府有关部门的意见，其中涉及军事秘密的，还应当征得有关军事机关的同意。

**Article 20** No unit or individual shall provide ocean observation data and results that are state secrets to international organizations, foreign organizations, or individuals without authorization; if provision is truly necessary, approval shall be obtained from the State Council's ocean administrative department or the ocean administrative department of the coastal provincial, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; before granting approval, the relevant ocean administrative department shall seek opinions from the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, and if military secrets are involved, it shall also obtain consent from the relevant military authorities.

### 第四章　海洋预报

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构应当根据海洋观测资料，分析、预测海洋状况变化趋势及其影响，及时制作海洋预报和海洋灾害警报，做好海洋预报工作。  
国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构应当适时进行海洋预报和海洋灾害警报会商，提高海洋预报和海洋灾害警报的准确性、及时性。

**Article 21** The marine forecasting institutions affiliated with the oceanic administration under the State Council and the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, based on marine observation data, analyze and forecast the trends and impacts of marine condition changes, produce marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings in a timely manner, and properly carry out marine forecasting work.  
The marine forecasting institutions affiliated with the oceanic administration under the State Council and the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall conduct consultations on marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings at appropriate times to improve the accuracy and timeliness of marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　海洋预报和海洋灾害警报由国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构按照职责向公众统一发布。其他任何单位和个人不得向公众发布海洋预报和海洋灾害警报。

**Article 22** Marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings shall be uniformly issued to the public by the marine forecasting institutions affiliated with the oceanic administration under the State Council and the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas in accordance with their respective functions. No other unit or individual shall issue marine forecasts or marine disaster warnings to the public.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院有关部门、沿海地方各级人民政府和沿海县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当根据海洋预报机构提供的海洋灾害警报信息采取必要措施，并根据防御海洋灾害的需要，启动相应的海洋灾害应急预案，避免或者减轻海洋灾害。

**Article 23** The relevant departments under the State Council, the local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas, and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall take necessary measures based on the marine disaster warning information provided by the marine forecasting institutions, and shall activate the corresponding emergency plans for marine disasters in accordance with the needs of defending against marine disasters, so as to avoid or mitigate marine disasters.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府指定的当地广播、电视和报纸等媒体应当安排固定的时段或者版面，及时刊播海洋预报和海洋灾害警报。  
广播、电视等媒体改变海洋预报播发时段的，应当事先与有关海洋主管部门协商一致，但是因特殊需要，广播电视行政部门要求改变播发时段的除外。对国计民生可能产生重大影响的海洋灾害警报，应当及时增播或者插播。

**Article 24** The media such as local radio, television, and newspapers designated by the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall arrange fixed time slots or columns to publish and broadcast marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings in a timely manner.  
Where radio, television, or other media change the broadcast time slot for marine forecasts, they shall reach consensus in advance with the relevant oceanic administration, except where, due to special needs, the radio and television administrative department requires a change of the broadcast time slot. For marine disaster warnings that may have a significant impact on the national economy and people's livelihood, additional or inserted broadcasts shall be made in a timely manner.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　广播、电视和报纸等媒体刊播海洋预报和海洋灾害警报，应当使用国务院海洋主管部门和沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构提供的信息，并明示海洋预报机构的名称。

**Article 25** When publishing and broadcasting marine forecasts and marine disaster warnings, the media such as radio, television, and newspapers shall use the information provided by the marine forecasting institutions affiliated with the oceanic administration under the State Council and the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas, and shall clearly indicate the names of the marine forecasting institutions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当建立和完善海洋灾害信息发布平台，根据海洋灾害防御需要，在沿海交通枢纽、公共活动场所等人口密集区和海洋灾害易发区建立海洋灾害警报信息接收和播发设施。

**Article 26** The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall establish and improve the marine disaster information release platform, and, in accordance with the needs of marine disaster defense, shall establish facilities for receiving and broadcasting marine disaster warning information in densely populated areas such as coastal transportation hubs and public activity venues, as well as in areas prone to marine disasters.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院海洋主管部门和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门应当根据海洋灾害分析统计结果，商本级人民政府有关部门提出确定海洋灾害重点防御区的意见，报本级人民政府批准后公布。  
在海洋灾害重点防御区内设立产业园区、进行重大项目建设的，应当在项目可行性论证阶段，进行海洋灾害风险评估，预测和评估海啸、风暴潮等海洋灾害的影响。

**Article 27** The oceanic administration under the State Council and the oceanic administrations of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in coastal areas shall, based on the results of analysis and statistics of marine disasters, consult with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level to propose opinions on determining key defense areas for marine disasters, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval before promulgation.  
Where industrial parks are established or major projects are constructed within key defense areas for marine disasters, a marine disaster risk assessment shall be conducted during the project feasibility study phase to forecast and assess the impact of marine disasters such as tsunamis and storm surges.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院海洋主管部门负责组织海平面变化和影响气候变化的重大海洋现象的预测和评估，并及时公布预测意见和评估结果。  
沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门应当根据海洋灾害防御需要，对沿海警戒潮位进行核定，报本级人民政府批准后公布。

**Article 28** The oceanic administration under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the forecast and assessment of sea level change and major marine phenomena affecting climate change, and shall promptly publish the forecast opinions and assessment results.  
The oceanic administrations of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in coastal areas shall, in accordance with the needs of marine disaster defense, verify the coastal warning water levels and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval before promulgation.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院海洋主管部门及其海区派出机构、沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门，不依法作出行政许可或者办理批准文件，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处，或者有其他未依照本条例规定履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Where the oceanic administration under the State Council and its regional offices, or the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas, fail to grant administrative licenses or handle approval documents according to law, discover illegal acts or receive reports of illegal acts but fail to investigate and handle them, or have other acts of failing to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be imposed sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院海洋主管部门及其海区派出机构、沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构瞒报、谎报或者由于玩忽职守导致重大漏报、错报、迟报海洋灾害警报的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where the marine forecasting institutions affiliated with the oceanic administration under the State Council and its regional offices, or the oceanic administrations of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas, conceal, falsely report, or, due to dereliction of duty, cause material omission, mistake, or delay in reporting marine disaster warnings, the superior authority or supervisory organ shall order them to make a correction; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be imposed sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons commit a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　设立、调整海洋观测站（点）未按照规定备案的，由有关海洋主管部门责令限期改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；不符合海洋观测网规划的，责令限期拆除；逾期不拆除的，依法实施强制拆除，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 31** Where the establishment or adjustment of an ocean observation station (site) is not filed for record in accordance with the relevant provisions, the relevant competent marine department shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; where the station (site) does not comply with the ocean observation network plan, the said department shall order its removal within a time limit; if it is not removed within the time limit, compulsory removal shall be implemented according to law, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不恢复原状或者不采取其他补救措施的，依法强制执行；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）侵占、毁损或者擅自移动海洋观测站（点）及其设施的；  
（二）在海洋观测环境保护范围内进行危害海洋观测活动的。

**Article 32** Where any person violates these Regulations and commits one of the following acts, the relevant competent marine department shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition or adoption of other remedial measures within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the person fails to restore the original condition or adopt other remedial measures within the time limit, the said department shall take compulsory enforcement according to law; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Encroaching upon, damaging or moving without authorization an ocean observation station (site) or its facilities;  
(2) Carrying out activities within the scope of the ocean observation environment protection that endanger ocean observation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
（一）不遵守国家海洋观测技术标准、规范或者规程的；  
（二）使用不符合国家有关产品标准、规范或者海洋观测技术要求的海洋观测仪器设备的；  
（三）使用未经检定、检定不合格或者超过检定周期的海洋观测计量器具的。  
违反本条第一款第二项、第三项规定的，责令限期更换有关海洋观测仪器设备、海洋观测计量器具。

**Article 33** Where any person violates these Regulations and commits one of the following acts, the relevant competent department shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; if the person fails to rectify within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Failing to comply with the national technical standards, specifications or procedures for ocean observation;  
(2) Using ocean observation instruments and equipment that do not conform to the relevant national product standards, specifications or ocean observation technical requirements;  
(3) Using ocean observation measuring instruments that have not been verified, have been verified as unqualified, or have exceeded the verification period.  
Where the provisions of Item (2) or (3) of the first paragraph are violated, the said department shall order the replacement of the relevant ocean observation instruments, equipment and measuring instruments within a time limit.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事海洋观测活动的单位未按照规定汇交海洋观测资料的，由负责接收海洋观测资料的海洋主管部门责令限期汇交；逾期不汇交的，责令停止海洋观测活动，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Where a unit engaged in ocean observation activities fails to submit ocean observation data in accordance with the relevant provisions, the competent marine department responsible for receiving ocean observation data shall order submission within a time limit; if the unit fails to submit within the time limit, the said department shall order cessation of the ocean observation activities and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　单位或者个人未经批准，向国际组织、外国的组织或者个人提供属于国家秘密的海洋观测资料或者成果的，由有关海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where any unit or individual, without approval, provides ocean observation data or results that are State secrets to an international organization, foreign organization or individual, the relevant competent marine department shall order cessation of the illegal act; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定发布海洋预报或者海洋灾害警报的，由有关海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where any person releases an ocean forecast or ocean disaster warning in violation of these Regulations, the relevant competent marine department shall order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　广播、电视、报纸等媒体有下列行为之一的，由有关主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依照本条例规定刊播海洋预报、海洋灾害警报的；  
（二）未及时增播或者插播对国计民生可能产生重大影响的海洋灾害警报的；  
（三）刊播海洋预报、海洋灾害警报，未使用海洋主管部门所属的海洋预报机构提供的信息的。

**Article 37** Where any media such as broadcasting, television, or newspaper commits one of the following acts, the relevant competent department shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to publish or broadcast an ocean forecast or ocean disaster warning in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) Failing to timely add or insert an ocean disaster warning that may have a significant impact on the national economy and people's livelihood;  
(3) Publishing or broadcasting an ocean forecast or ocean disaster warning without using the information provided by the ocean forecasting institution affiliated with the competent marine department.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
（一）海洋观测，是指以掌握、描述海洋状况为目的，对潮汐、盐度、海温、海浪、海流、海冰、海啸波等进行的观察测量活动，以及对相关数据采集、传输、分析和评价的活动。  
（二）海洋预报，是指对潮汐、盐度、海温、海浪、海流、海冰、海啸、风暴潮、海平面变化、海岸侵蚀、咸潮入侵等海洋状况和海洋现象开展的预测和信息发布的活动。  
（三）海洋观测站（点），是指为获取海洋观测资料，在海洋、海岛和海岸设立的海洋观测场所。  
（四）海洋观测设施，是指海洋观测站（点）所使用的观测站房、雷达站房、观测平台、观测井、观测船、浮标、潜标、海床基、观测标志、仪器设备、通信线路等及附属设施。  
（五）海洋观测环境，是指为保证海洋观测活动正常进行，以海洋观测站（点）为中心，以获取连续、准确和具有代表性的海洋观测数据为目标所必需的最小立体空间。

**Article 38** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) 'Ocean observation' means the activities of observing and measuring tides, salinity, sea temperature, waves, ocean currents, sea ice, tsunami waves, etc., for the purpose of understanding and describing ocean conditions, as well as the activities of collecting, transmitting, analyzing and evaluating relevant data.  
(2) 'Ocean forecast' means the activities of predicting and releasing information on ocean conditions and phenomena such as tides, salinity, sea temperature, waves, ocean currents, sea ice, tsunamis, storm surges, sea level change, coastal erosion, saltwater intrusion, etc.  
(3) 'Ocean observation station (site)' means the place established in the ocean, on an island or on the coast for obtaining ocean observation data.  
(4) 'Ocean observation facilities' means the observation station buildings, radar station buildings, observation platforms, observation wells, observation vessels, buoys, submerged buoys, seabed bases, observation marks, instruments and equipment, communication lines, etc., used by an ocean observation station (site), as well as ancillary facilities.  
(5) 'Ocean observation environment' means the minimum three-dimensional space centered on an ocean observation station (site) that is necessary to ensure the normal conduct of ocean observation activities and to obtain continuous, accurate and representative ocean observation data.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中国人民解放军的海洋观测预报工作，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。  
海洋环境监测及监测信息的发布，依照有关法律、法规和国家规定执行。

**Article 39** The ocean observation and forecast work of the People's Liberation Army shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
The release of marine environment monitoring and monitoring information shall be carried out in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and state provisions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自2012年6月1日起施行。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2012.
