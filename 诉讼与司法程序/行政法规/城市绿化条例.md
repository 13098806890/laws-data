# 城市绿化条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：1992-08-01

> (1992年6月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第100号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进城市绿化事业的发展，改善生态环境，美化生活环境，增进人民身心健康，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在城市规划区内种植和养护树木花草等城市绿化的规划、建设、保护和管理。 第三条　城市人民政府应当把城市绿化建设纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。 第四条　国家鼓励和加强城市绿化的科学研究，推广先进技术，提高城市绿化的科学技术和艺术水平。 第五条　城市中的单位和有劳动能力的公民，应当依照国家有关规定履行植树或者其他绿化义务。 第六条　对在城市绿化工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予表彰和奖励。 第七条　国务院设立全国绿化委员会，统一组织领导全国城乡绿化工作，其办公室设在国务院林业行政主管部门。 国务院城市建设行政主管部门和国务院林业行政主管部门等，按照国务院规定的职权划分，负责全国城市绿化工作。 地方绿化管理体制，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地实际情况规定。 城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门主管本行政区域内城市规划区的城市绿化工作。 在城市规划区内，有关法律、法规规定由林业行政主管部门等管理的绿化工作，依照有关法律、法规执行。 第二章　规划和建设 第八条　城市人民政府应当组织城市规划行政主管部门和城市绿化行政主管部门等共同编制城市绿化规划，并纳入城市总体规划。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进城市绿化事业的发展，改善生态环境，美化生活环境，增进人民身心健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of promoting the development of urban greening, improving the ecological environment, beautifying the living environment, and enhancing the physical and mental health of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在城市规划区内种植和养护树木花草等城市绿化的规划、建设、保护和管理。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the planning, construction, protection and management of urban greening, such as planting and maintaining trees and flowers, within urban planning areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市人民政府应当把城市绿化建设纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 3** The people's government of a city shall incorporate urban greening construction into the national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励和加强城市绿化的科学研究，推广先进技术，提高城市绿化的科学技术和艺术水平。

**Article 4** The State shall encourage and strengthen scientific research on urban greening, promote advanced technologies, and improve the scientific and artistic level of urban greening.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　城市中的单位和有劳动能力的公民，应当依照国家有关规定履行植树或者其他绿化义务。

**Article 5** Units and citizens with labor capacity in cities shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, perform the obligation of tree planting or other greening activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对在城市绿化工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 6** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in urban greening work shall be commended and rewarded by the people's government.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院设立全国绿化委员会，统一组织领导全国城乡绿化工作，其办公室设在国务院林业行政主管部门。  
国务院城市建设行政主管部门和国务院林业行政主管部门等，按照国务院规定的职权划分，负责全国城市绿化工作。  
地方绿化管理体制，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地实际情况规定。  
城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门主管本行政区域内城市规划区的城市绿化工作。  
在城市规划区内，有关法律、法规规定由林业行政主管部门等管理的绿化工作，依照有关法律、法规执行。

**Article 7** The State Council shall establish the National Greening Committee to uniformly organize and lead the national urban and rural greening work, and its office shall be located in the competent administrative department for forestry under the State Council.  
The competent administrative department for urban construction under the State Council and the competent administrative department for forestry under the State Council, among others, shall be responsible for the national urban greening work in accordance with the division of functions and powers prescribed by the State Council.  
The local greening management system shall be prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government according to the actual local conditions.  
The competent administrative department for urban greening under the people's government of a city shall be in charge of the urban greening work within the urban planning area of its administrative region.  
Within the urban planning area, the greening work that is managed by the competent administrative department for forestry, among others, as provided by relevant laws and regulations, shall be implemented in accordance with such laws and regulations.

### 第二章　规划和建设

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　城市人民政府应当组织城市规划行政主管部门和城市绿化行政主管部门等共同编制城市绿化规划，并纳入城市总体规划。

**Article 8** The people's government of a city shall organize the competent administrative department for urban planning and the competent administrative department for urban greening, among others, to jointly compile the urban greening plan, and incorporate it into the overall urban plan.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市绿化规划应当从实际出发，根据城市发展需要，合理安排同城市人口和城市面积相适应的城市绿化用地面积。  
城市人均公共绿地面积和绿化覆盖率等规划指标，由国务院城市建设行政主管部门根据不同城市的性质、规模和自然条件等实际情况规定。

**Article 9** The urban greening plan shall proceed from reality, rationally arrange the urban greening land area commensurate with the urban population and urban area according to the needs of urban development.  
The planning indicators such as the per capita public green area and greening coverage rate of the city shall be prescribed by the competent administrative department for urban construction under the State Council according to the actual conditions of different cities such as their nature, scale, and natural conditions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　城市绿化规划应当根据当地的特点，利用原有的地形、地貌、水体、植被和历史文化遗址等自然、人文条件，以方便群众为原则，合理设置公共绿地、居住区绿地、防护绿地、生产绿地和风景林地等。

**Article 10** The urban greening plan shall, based on local characteristics, make use of the original natural and cultural conditions such as topography, landforms, water bodies, vegetation, and historical and cultural sites, and, on the principle of convenience for the public, rationally set up public green spaces, residential green spaces, protective green spaces, production green spaces, and scenic forest lands, among others.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城市绿化工程的设计，应当委托持有相应资格证书的设计单位承担。  
工程建设项目的附属绿化工程设计方案，按照基本建设程序审批时，必须有城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门参加审查。  
建设单位必须按照批准的设计方案进行施工。设计方案确需改变时，须经原批准机关审批。

**Article 11** The design of urban greening projects shall be undertaken by a design unit holding corresponding qualification certificates.  
When the design plan for auxiliary greening works of construction projects is reviewed and approved in accordance with the capital construction procedures, the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city must participate in the examination.  
The construction unit must carry out the construction in accordance with the approved design plan. Where the design plan truly needs to be changed, it shall be subject to the approval of the original approving authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　城市绿化工程的设计，应当借鉴国内外先进经验，体现民族风格和地方特色。城市公共绿地和居住区绿地的建设，应当以植物造景为主，选用适合当地自然条件的树木花草，并适当配置泉、石、雕塑等景物。

**Article 12** The design of urban greening projects shall draw on advanced experience at home and abroad and reflect ethnic styles and local characteristics. The construction of urban public green spaces and residential area green spaces shall focus on plant landscaping, select trees, flowers, and grasses suitable for local natural conditions, and appropriately configure springs, rocks, sculptures, and other scenery.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　城市绿化规划应当因地制宜地规划不同类型的防护绿地。各有关单位应当依照国家有关规定，负责本单位管界内防护绿地的绿化建设。

**Article 13** Urban greening planning shall plan different types of protective green spaces according to local conditions. Each relevant unit shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be responsible for the greening construction of protective green spaces within its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　单位附属绿地的绿化规划和建设，由该单位自行负责，城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门应当监督检查，并给予技术指导。

**Article 14** The greening planning and construction of subsidiary green spaces of units shall be the responsibility of the unit itself, and the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city shall supervise and inspect and provide technical guidance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市苗圃、草圃、花圃等生产绿地的建设，应当适应城市绿化建设的需要。

**Article 15** The construction of production green spaces such as urban nurseries, grass nurseries, and flower nurseries shall meet the needs of urban greening construction.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城市新建、扩建、改建工程项目和开发住宅区项目，需要绿化的，其基本建设投资中应当包括配套的绿化建设投资，并统一安排绿化工程施工，在规定的期限内完成绿化任务。

**Article 16** For urban projects of new construction, expansion, or renovation and housing development projects that require greening, the capital construction investment shall include the investment for supporting greening construction, and the greening project construction shall be arranged in a unified manner, and the greening task shall be completed within the prescribed time limit.

### 第三章　保护和管理

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市的公共绿地、风景林地、防护绿地、行道树及干道绿化带的绿化，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门管理；各单位管界内的防护绿地的绿化，由该单位按照国家有关规定管理；单位自建的公园和单位附属绿地的绿化，由该单位管理；居住区绿地的绿化，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门根据实际情况确定的单位管理；城市苗圃、草圃和花圃等，由其经营单位管理。

**Article 17** The greening of urban public green spaces, scenic forest land, protective green spaces, street trees, and green strips along main roads shall be managed by the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city; the greening of protective green spaces within the jurisdiction of each unit shall be managed by that unit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; the greening of parks built by units and subsidiary green spaces of units shall be managed by that unit; the greening of residential area green spaces shall be managed by the unit determined by the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city in light of the actual situation; and urban nurseries, grass nurseries, and flower nurseries shall be managed by their operating units.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　任何单位和个人都不得擅自改变城市绿化规划用地性质或者破坏绿化规划用地的地形、地貌、水体和植被。

**Article 18** No unit or individual may, without authorization, change the nature of the land designated for urban greening planning or damage the terrain, landforms, water bodies, and vegetation of the land designated for greening planning.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位和个人都不得擅自占用城市绿化用地；占用的城市绿化用地，应当限期归还。  
因建设或者其他特殊需要临时占用城市绿化用地，须经城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门同意，并按照有关规定办理临时用地手续。

**Article 19** No unit or individual may, without authorization, occupy land designated for urban greening; land designated for urban greening that has been occupied shall be returned within a specified time limit.  
Where temporary occupation of land designated for urban greening is necessary due to construction or other special needs, it shall be subject to the consent of the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city, and the procedures for temporary land use shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　任何单位和个人都不得损坏城市树木花草和绿化设施。  
砍伐城市树木，必须经城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门批准，并按照国家有关规定补植树木或者采取其他补救措施。

**Article 20** No unit or individual may damage urban trees, flowers, grasses, or greening facilities.  
Cutting down urban trees must be approved by the urban greening administrative department of the people's government of the city, and the trees shall be replanted or other remedial measures shall be taken in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在城市的公共绿地内开设商业、服务摊点的，应当持工商行政管理部门批准的营业执照，在公共绿地管理单位指定的地点从事经营活动，并遵守公共绿地和工商行政管理的规定。

**Article 21** For establishing commercial or service stalls within urban public green spaces, the party shall hold a business license approved by the administrative department for industry and commerce, conduct business activities at locations designated by the public green space management unit, and comply with the provisions on public green spaces and industrial and commercial administration.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　城市的绿地管理单位，应当建立、健全管理制度，保持树木花草繁茂及绿化设施完好。

**Article 22** Urban green space management units shall establish and improve management systems, maintain the flourishing of trees, flowers, and grass, and keep greening facilities in good condition.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　为保证管线的安全使用需要修剪树木时，应当按照兼顾管线安全使用和树木正常生长的原则进行修剪。承担修剪费用的办法，由城市人民政府规定。  
因不可抗力致使树木倾斜危及管线安全时，管线管理单位可以先行扶正或者砍伐树木，但是，应当及时报告城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门和绿地管理单位。

**Article 23** When trees need to be pruned to ensure the safe use of pipelines, pruning shall be carried out in accordance with the principle of taking into account both the safe use of pipelines and the normal growth of trees. The method for bearing the pruning costs shall be prescribed by the people's government of the city.  
When trees tilt due to force majeure and endanger pipeline safety, the pipeline management unit may straighten or cut down the trees first, but shall promptly report to the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government and the green space management unit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　百年以上树龄的树木，稀有、珍贵树木，具有历史价值或者重要纪念意义的树木，均属古树名木。  
对城市古树名木实行统一管理，分别养护。城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门，应当建立古树名木的档案和标志，划定保护范围，加强养护管理。在单位管界内或者私人庭院内的古树名木，由该单位或者居民负责养护，城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门负责监督和技术指导。  
严禁砍伐或者迁移古树名木。因特殊需要迁移古树名木，必须经城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门审查同意，并报同级或者上级人民政府批准。

**Article 24** Trees aged over one hundred years, rare or precious trees, and trees of historical value or important commemorative significance are all classified as ancient and famous trees.  
Unified management and separate maintenance shall be implemented for urban ancient and famous trees. The urban greening administrative department of the city people's government shall establish archives and signs for ancient and famous trees, designate protection areas, and strengthen maintenance management. Ancient and famous trees within the boundaries of a unit or within private courtyards shall be maintained by that unit or resident, and the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government shall be responsible for supervision and technical guidance.  
Cutting down or relocating ancient and famous trees is strictly prohibited. Where relocation of ancient and famous trees is necessary due to special circumstances, it must be examined and approved by the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government, and reported to the people's government at the same level or a higher level for approval.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　工程建设项目的附属绿化工程设计方案，未经批准或者未按照批准的设计方案施工的，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门责令停止施工、限期改正或者采取其他补救措施。

**Article 25** Where the design plan for auxiliary greening works of an engineering construction project is not approved or the construction is not carried out in accordance with the approved design plan, the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government shall order the cessation of construction, correction within a time limit, or the adoption of other remedial measures.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门或者其授权的单位责令停止侵害，可以并处罚款；造成损失的，应当负赔偿责任；应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)损坏城市树木花草的；  
(二)擅自砍伐城市树木的；  
(三)砍伐、擅自迁移古树名木或者因养护不善致使古树名木受到损伤或者死亡的；  
(四)损坏城市绿化设施的。

**Article 26** Any person who violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall, by the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government or its authorized unit, be ordered to cease the infringement, and may also be imposed a fine; if losses are caused, liability for compensation shall be borne; if a penalty for public security administration should be imposed, it shall be penalized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) damaging urban trees, flowers, or grass;  
(2) cutting down urban trees without authorization;  
(3) cutting down or relocating ancient and famous trees without authorization, or causing damage to or death of ancient and famous trees due to poor maintenance;  
(4) damaging urban greening facilities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　未经同意擅自占用城市绿化用地的，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门责令限期退还、恢复原状，可以并处罚款；造成损失的，应当负赔偿责任。

**Article 27** Where a person occupies urban greening land without authorization and without consent, the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government shall order the return of the land within a time limit and restoration to its original condition, and may also impose a fine; if losses are caused, liability for compensation shall be borne.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对不服从公共绿地管理单位管理的商业、服务摊点，由城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门或者其授权的单位给予警告，可以并处罚款；情节严重的，可以提请工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 28** For commercial or service stalls that do not comply with the management of the public green space management unit, the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government or its authorized unit shall give a warning and may also impose a fine; if the circumstances are serious, it may request the administrative department for industry and commerce to revoke the business license.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对违反本条例的直接责任人员或者单位负责人，可以由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** The person directly responsible or the person in charge of the unit who violates these Regulations may be given administrative sanctions by his or her unit or the competent authority at a higher level; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城市人民政府城市绿化行政主管部门和城市绿地管理单位的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where functionaries of the urban greening administrative department of the city people's government or the urban green space management unit neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, administrative sanctions shall be imposed by their unit or the competent authority at a higher level; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对行政处罚不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定之日起15日内向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以直接向人民法院起诉。逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。  
对治安管理处罚不服的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定执行。

**Article 31** Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the penalty decision; if the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, file a prosecution with the People's Court. The party may also directly file a prosecution with the People's Court. If the party fails to apply for reconsideration within the time limit, fails to file a prosecution with the People's Court, and also fails to perform the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.  
  
Where a party is dissatisfied with a public security administrative penalty, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依照本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 32** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例自1992年8月1日起施行。

**Article 33** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 1992.
