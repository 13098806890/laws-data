### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了明确经营者集中的申报标准，根据《中华人民共和国反垄断法》，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Anti-Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of specifying the filing standards for concentration of undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　经营者集中是指下列情形：  
（一）经营者合并；  
（二）经营者通过取得股权或者资产的方式取得对其他经营者的控制权；  
（三）经营者通过合同等方式取得对其他经营者的控制权或者能够对其他经营者施加决定性影响。

**Article 2** Concentration of undertakings refers to any of the following circumstances:  
(1) merger of undertakings;  
(2) acquisition of control over other undertakings by an undertaking through acquiring equity or assets;  
(3) acquisition of control over other undertakings or being able to exert decisive influence on other undertakings by an undertaking through contracts or other means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经营者集中达到下列标准之一的，经营者应当事先向国务院反垄断执法机构申报，未申报的不得实施集中：  
（一）参与集中的所有经营者上一会计年度在全球范围内的营业额合计超过120亿元人民币，并且其中至少两个经营者上一会计年度在中国境内的营业额均超过8亿元人民币；  
（二）参与集中的所有经营者上一会计年度在中国境内的营业额合计超过40亿元人民币，并且其中至少两个经营者上一会计年度在中国境内的营业额均超过8亿元人民币。  
营业额的计算，应当考虑银行、保险、证券、期货等特殊行业、领域的实际情况，具体办法由国务院反垄断执法机构会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 3** Where a concentration of undertakings meets any of the following thresholds, the undertakings shall file with the anti-monopoly enforcement agency of the State Council in advance, and no concentration shall be implemented without such filing:  
(1) the total worldwide turnover of all undertakings participating in the concentration in the preceding accounting year exceeds RMB 12 billion, and at least two of such undertakings each have a turnover of more than RMB 800 million within the territory of China in the preceding accounting year;  
(2) the total turnover within the territory of China of all undertakings participating in the concentration in the preceding accounting year exceeds RMB 4 billion, and at least two of such undertakings each have a turnover of more than RMB 800 million within the territory of China in the preceding accounting year.  
For the calculation of turnover, the actual circumstances of special industries and fields such as banking, insurance, securities, and futures shall be taken into account, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the anti-monopoly enforcement agency of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经营者集中未达到[本规定第三条](#art-3)规定的申报标准，但有证据证明该经营者集中具有或者可能具有排除、限制竞争效果的，国务院反垄断执法机构可以要求经营者申报。

**Article 4** If a concentration of undertakings does not meet the filing thresholds specified in Article 3 of these Provisions, but there is evidence that the concentration has or may have the effect of eliminating or restricting competition, the anti-monopoly enforcement agency of the State Council may require the undertakings to file.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营者未依照[本规定第三条](#art-3)和第四条规定进行申报的，国务院反垄断执法机构应当依法进行调查。

**Article 5** Where an undertaking fails to file in accordance with the provisions of Articles 3 and 4 of these Provisions, the anti-monopoly enforcement agency of the State Council shall conduct an investigation according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院反垄断执法机构应当根据经济发展情况，对本规定确定的申报标准的实施情况进行评估。

**Article 6** The anti-monopoly enforcement agency of the State Council shall evaluate the implementation of the filing standards specified in these Provisions in light of the economic development.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 7** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
