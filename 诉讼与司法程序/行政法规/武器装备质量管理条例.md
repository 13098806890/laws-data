# 武器装备质量管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2010-09-30  
**生效日期**：2010-11-01

> (2010年9月30日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第582号公布　自2010年11月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对武器装备质量的监督管理，提高武器装备质量水平，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》和《中华人民共和国产品质量法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称武器装备，是指实施和保障军事行动的武器、武器系统和军事技术器材。 武器装备以及用于武器装备的计算机软件、专用元器件、配套产品、原材料的质量管理，适用本条例。 第三条　武器装备质量管理的基本任务是依照有关法律、法规，对武器装备质量特性的形成、保持和恢复等过程实施控制和监督，保证武器装备性能满足规定或者预期要求。 第四条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当建立健全质量管理体系，对其承担的武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修任务实行有效的质量管理，确保武器装备质量符合要求。 第五条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修应当执行军用标准以及其他满足武器装备质量要求的国家标准、行业标准和企业标准；鼓励采用适用的国际标准和国外先进标准。 武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当依照计量法律、法规和其他有关规定，实施计量保障和监督，确保武器装备和检测设备的量值准确和计量单位统一。 第六条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当建立武器装备质量信息系统和信息交流制度，及时记录、收集、分析、上报、反馈、交流武器装备的质量信息，实现质量信息资源共享，并确保质量信息安全，做好保密工作。 第七条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门和中国人民解放军总装备部(以下简称总装备部)，在各自的职责范围内负责武器装备质量的监督管理工作。 第八条　国家鼓励采用先进的科学技术和管理方法提高武器装备质量，并对保证和提高武器装备质量作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　论证质量管理 第九条　武器装备论证质量管理的任务是保证论证科学、合理、可行，论证结果满足作战任务需求。 军队有关装备部门组织武器装备的论证，并对武器装备论证质量负责。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对武器装备质量的监督管理，提高武器装备质量水平，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》和《中华人民共和国产品质量法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and administration of the quality of weaponry and equipment, improving the quality level of weaponry and equipment, in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China and the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称武器装备，是指实施和保障军事行动的武器、武器系统和军事技术器材。  
武器装备以及用于武器装备的计算机软件、专用元器件、配套产品、原材料的质量管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** Weaponry and equipment as used in these Regulations means weapons, weapon systems and military technical equipment that implement and support military operations.  
The quality administration of weaponry and equipment and of computer software, special components, supporting products and raw materials used for weaponry and equipment shall be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　武器装备质量管理的基本任务是依照有关法律、法规，对武器装备质量特性的形成、保持和恢复等过程实施控制和监督，保证武器装备性能满足规定或者预期要求。

**Article 3** The basic task of quality management of weaponry and equipment shall be to implement control and supervision over processes such as the formation, maintenance and restoration of quality characteristics of weaponry and equipment in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, and to ensure that the performance of weaponry and equipment meets specified or anticipated requirements.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当建立健全质量管理体系，对其承担的武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修任务实行有效的质量管理，确保武器装备质量符合要求。

**Article 4** Units that conduct demonstration, development, production, testing and maintenance of weaponry and equipment shall establish a sound quality management system, implement effective quality management for the tasks of demonstration, development, production, testing and maintenance of weaponry and equipment they undertake, and ensure that the quality of weaponry and equipment meets the requirements.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修应当执行军用标准以及其他满足武器装备质量要求的国家标准、行业标准和企业标准；鼓励采用适用的国际标准和国外先进标准。  
武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当依照计量法律、法规和其他有关规定，实施计量保障和监督，确保武器装备和检测设备的量值准确和计量单位统一。

**Article 5** Demonstration, development, production, testing and maintenance of weaponry and equipment shall implement military standards and other national standards, industrial standards and enterprise standards that meet the quality requirements of weaponry and equipment; the adoption of applicable international standards and advanced foreign standards shall be encouraged.  
Units that develop, produce, test and maintain weaponry and equipment shall, in accordance with metrology laws, regulations and other relevant provisions, implement measurement assurance and supervision to ensure the accuracy of quantity values of weaponry and equipment and testing equipment and the uniformity of measurement units.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　武器装备论证、研制、生产、试验和维修单位应当建立武器装备质量信息系统和信息交流制度，及时记录、收集、分析、上报、反馈、交流武器装备的质量信息，实现质量信息资源共享，并确保质量信息安全，做好保密工作。

**Article 6** Units that conduct demonstration, development, production, testing and maintenance of weaponry and equipment shall establish a weaponry and equipment quality information system and an information exchange system, promptly record, collect, analyze, report, feedback and exchange quality information of weaponry and equipment, realize the sharing of quality information resources, and ensure the security of quality information and maintain secrecy.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门和中国人民解放军总装备部(以下简称总装备部)，在各自的职责范围内负责武器装备质量的监督管理工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense of the State Council, the relevant departments of the State Council and the General Armament Department of the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the General Armament Department) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality of weaponry and equipment within their respective duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励采用先进的科学技术和管理方法提高武器装备质量，并对保证和提高武器装备质量作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 8** The State encourages the adoption of advanced science and technology and management methods to improve the quality of weaponry and equipment, and shall commend and reward units and individuals that make outstanding contributions to ensuring and improving the quality of weaponry and equipment.

### 第二章　论证质量管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　武器装备论证质量管理的任务是保证论证科学、合理、可行，论证结果满足作战任务需求。  
军队有关装备部门组织武器装备的论证，并对武器装备论证质量负责。

**Article 9** The task of quality management in demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall be to ensure that the demonstration is scientific, reasonable and feasible, and that the demonstration results meet the operational mission requirements.  
The relevant equipment departments of the armed forces shall organize the demonstration of weaponry and equipment and shall be responsible for the quality of the demonstration of weaponry and equipment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　武器装备论证单位应当制定并执行论证工作程序和规范，实施论证过程的质量管理。

**Article 10** Units that conduct demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall formulate and implement demonstration work procedures and specifications, and implement quality management of the demonstration process.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　武器装备论证单位应当根据论证任务需求，统筹考虑武器装备性能(含功能特性、可靠性、维修性、保障性、测试性和安全性等，下同)、研制进度和费用，提出相互协调的武器装备性能的定性定量要求、质量保证要求和保障要求。

**Article 11** Units that conduct demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall, based on the requirements of the demonstration task, comprehensively consider weaponry and equipment performance (including functional characteristics, reliability, maintainability, supportability, testability and safety, hereinafter the same), development schedule and costs, and propose qualitative and quantitative requirements for weaponry and equipment performance, quality assurance requirements and support requirements that are coordinated with each other.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　武器装备论证单位应当征求作战、训练、运输等部门和武器装备研制、生产、试验、使用、维修等单位的意见，确认各种需求和约束条件，并在论证结果中落实。

**Article 12** Units that conduct demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall solicit opinions from departments of operations, training, transportation and units of development, production, testing, use and maintenance of weaponry and equipment, confirm various requirements and constraint conditions, and implement them in the demonstration results.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　武器装备论证单位应当对论证结果进行风险分析，提出降低或者控制风险的措施。武器装备研制总体方案应当优先选用成熟技术，对采用的新技术和关键技术，应当经过试验或者验证。

**Article 13** Units that conduct demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall conduct risk analysis on the demonstration results and propose measures to reduce or control risks. The overall scheme for weaponry and equipment development shall give priority to mature technologies; new technologies and key technologies adopted shall be tested or verified.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　武器装备论证单位应当拟制多种备选的武器装备研制总体方案，并提出优选方案。

**Article 14** Units that conduct demonstration of weaponry and equipment shall prepare multiple alternative overall schemes for weaponry and equipment development and propose preferred schemes.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军队有关装备部门应当按照规定的程序，组织作战、训练、运输等部门和武器装备研制、生产、试验、使用、维修等单位对武器装备论证结果进行评审。

**Article 15** The relevant equipment departments of the armed forces shall, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, organize the departments of operations, training, transportation and the units of development, production, testing, use and maintenance of weaponry and equipment to review the demonstration results of weaponry and equipment.

### 第三章　研制、生产与试验质量管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　武器装备研制、生产与试验质量管理的任务是保证武器装备质量符合研制总要求和合同要求。  
武器装备研制、生产单位对其研制、生产的武器装备质量负责；武器装备试验单位对其承担的武器装备试验结论的正确性和准确性负责。  
中央管理的企业对所属单位承担的武器装备研制、生产质量实施监督管理。

**Article 16** The task of quality management in development, production and testing of weaponry and equipment shall be to ensure that the quality of weaponry and equipment meets the overall development requirements and contract requirements.  
Units that develop and produce weaponry and equipment shall be responsible for the quality of the weaponry and equipment they develop and produce; units that test weaponry and equipment shall be responsible for the correctness and accuracy of the test conclusions they undertake.  
Centrally managed enterprises shall implement supervision and administration of the quality of development and production of weaponry and equipment undertaken by their subordinate units.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　订立武器装备研制、生产合同应当明确规定武器装备的性能指标、质量保证要求、依据的标准、验收准则和方法以及合同双方的质量责任。

**Article 17** When concluding contracts for the development or production of weaponry and equipment, the performance indicators, quality assurance requirements, standards to be followed, acceptance criteria and methods, and the quality responsibilities of both parties to the contract shall be clearly specified.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　武器装备研制、生产涉及若干单位的，其质量保证工作由任务总体单位或者总承包单位负责组织。

**Article 18** Where the development or production of weaponry and equipment involves several units, the quality assurance work shall be organized by the overall task unit or the prime contractor unit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当根据合同要求和研制、生产程序制定武器装备研制、生产项目质量计划，并将其纳入研制、生产和条件保障计划。

**Article 19** Units that develop or produce weaponry and equipment shall, based on contract requirements and development/production procedures, formulate quality plans for weaponry and equipment development or production projects, and incorporate them into the development, production and condition assurance plans.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当运用可靠性、维修性、保障性、测试性和安全性等工程技术方法，优化武器装备的设计方案和保障方案。

**Article 20** Units that develop or produce weaponry and equipment shall use engineering technology methods such as reliability, maintainability, supportability, testability and safety to optimize the design scheme and support scheme of weaponry and equipment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　武器装备研制单位应当在满足武器装备研制总要求和合同要求的前提下，优先采用成熟技术和通用化、系列化、组合化的产品。  
武器装备研制单位对设计方案采用的新技术、新材料、新工艺应当进行充分的论证、试验和鉴定，并按照规定履行审批手续。

**Article 21** Weapons and equipment development units shall, under the premise of meeting the overall requirements for weapons and equipment development and contractual requirements, give priority to adopting mature technologies and products that are universal, serialized, and modular.  
Weapons and equipment development units shall conduct sufficient demonstration, testing, and expert evaluation of new technologies, new materials, and new processes adopted in the design plan, and shall go through the approval procedures according to regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　武器装备研制单位应当对计算机软件开发实施工程化管理，对影响武器装备性能和安全的计算机软件进行独立的测试和评价。

**Article 22** Weapons and equipment development units shall implement engineering management for computer software development, and shall conduct independent testing and evaluation of computer software that affects the performance and safety of weapons and equipment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当对武器装备的研制、生产过程严格实施技术状态管理。更改技术状态应当按照规定履行审批手续；对可能影响武器装备性能和合同要求的技术状态的更改，应当充分论证和验证，并经原审批部门批准。

**Article 23** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall strictly implement technical state management during the development and production process of weapons and equipment. Changes to the technical state shall go through approval procedures according to regulations; changes to the technical state that may affect the performance of weapons and equipment and contractual requirements shall be fully demonstrated and verified, and shall be approved by the original approving department.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当严格执行设计评审、工艺评审和产品质量评审制度。对技术复杂、质量要求高的产品，应当进行可靠性、维修性、保障性、测试性和安全性以及计算机软件、元器件、原材料等专题评审。

**Article 24** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall strictly implement the systems of design review, process review, and product quality review. For products with complex technology and high quality requirements, special reviews on reliability, maintainability, supportability, testability, and safety, as well as on computer software, components, and raw materials, shall be conducted.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军队有关装备部门应当按照武器装备研制程序，组织转阶段审查，确认达到规定的质量要求后，方可批准转入下一研制阶段。

**Article 25** The relevant equipment departments of the military shall organize phase-transition reviews in accordance with the weapons and equipment development procedure, and may approve the transition to the next development phase only after confirming that the specified quality requirements have been met.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当实行图样和技术资料的校对、审核、批准的审签制度，工艺和质量会签制度以及标准化审查制度。

**Article 26** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall implement a review and signature system for the proofreading, review, and approval of drawings and technical documents, a process and quality co-signature system, and a standardization review system.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当对产品的关键件或者关键特性、重要件或者重要特性、关键工序、特种工艺编制质量控制文件，并对关键件、重要件进行首件鉴定。

**Article 27** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall prepare quality control documents for key parts or key characteristics, important parts or important characteristics, key processes, and special processes, and shall conduct first-article expert evaluation for key parts and important parts.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　武器装备研制、生产和试验单位应当建立故障的报告、分析和纠正措施系统。对武器装备研制、生产和试验过程中出现的故障，应当及时采取纠正和预防措施。

**Article 28** Weapons and equipment development, production, and test units shall establish a system for fault reporting, analysis, and corrective actions. For faults occurring during the development, production, and test process of weapons and equipment, corrective and preventive measures shall be taken in a timely manner.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　武器装备研制单位组织实施研制试验，应当编制试验大纲或者试验方案，明确试验质量保证要求，对试验过程进行质量控制。

**Article 29** Weapons and equipment development units that organize and implement development tests shall prepare a test outline or test plan, specify quality assurance requirements for the test, and conduct quality control on the test process.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　承担武器装备定型试验的单位应当根据武器装备定型有关规定，拟制试验大纲，明确试验项目质量要求以及保障条件，对试验过程进行质量控制，保证试验数据真实、准确和试验结论完整、正确。  
试验单位所用的试验装备及其配套的检测设备应当符合使用要求，并依法定期进行检定、校准，保持完好的技术状态；对一次性使用的试验装备，应当进行试验前的检定、校准。

**Article 30** Units undertaking finalization tests of weapons and equipment shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions on the finalization of weapons and equipment, draft a test outline, specify quality requirements for test items and support conditions, conduct quality control on the test process, and ensure that the test data are true and accurate and the test conclusions are complete and correct.  
The test equipment and its supporting testing equipment used by the test unit shall meet the usage requirements, and shall be verified and calibrated periodically according to law to maintain a sound technical state; for single-use test equipment, verification and calibration shall be carried out before the test.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　提交武器装备设计定型审查的图样、技术资料应当正确、完整，试验报告的数据应当全面、准确，结论明确。

**Article 31** The drawings and technical documents submitted for review of the design finalization of weapons and equipment shall be correct and complete; the data in the test reports shall be comprehensive and accurate, and the conclusions shall be clear.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　提交武器装备生产定型审查的图样、技术资料应当符合规定要求；试验报告和部队试用报告的数据应当全面、准确，结论明确。

**Article 32** The drawings and technical documents submitted for review of the production finalization of weapons and equipment shall meet the specified requirements; the data in the test reports and troop trial reports shall be comprehensive and accurate, and the conclusions shall be clear.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当对其外购、外协产品的质量负责，对采购过程实施严格控制，对供应单位的质量保证能力进行评定和跟踪，并编制合格供应单位名录。未经检验合格的外购、外协产品，不得投入使用。

**Article 33** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall be responsible for the quality of their outsourced and subcontracted products, shall implement strict control over the procurement process, shall evaluate and track the quality assurance capabilities of suppliers, and shall compile a list of qualified suppliers. Outsourced and subcontracted products that have not been inspected and found qualified shall not be put into use.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　武器装备的生产应当符合下列要求：  
(一)工艺文件和质量控制文件经审查批准；  
(二)制造、测量、试验设备和工艺装置依法经检定或者测试合格；  
(三)元器件、原材料、外协件、成品件经检验合格；  
(四)工作环境符合规定要求；  
(五)操作人员经培训并考核合格；  
(六)法律、法规规定的其他要求。

**Article 34** The production of weapons and equipment shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) Process documents and quality control documents have been reviewed and approved;  
(2) Manufacturing, measuring, and test equipment and process devices have been verified or tested as qualified according to law;  
(3) Components, raw materials, subcontracted parts, and finished parts have been inspected and found qualified;  
(4) The working environment meets the specified requirements;  
(5) Operators have been trained and assessed as qualified;  
(6) Other requirements as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当建立产品批次管理制度和产品标识制度，严格实行工艺流程控制，保证产品质量原始记录的真实和完整。

**Article 35** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall establish a product batch management system and a product identification system, strictly implement process flow control, and ensure the authenticity and completeness of original records of product quality.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当按照标准和程序要求进行进货检验、工序检验和最终产品检验；对首件产品应当进行规定的检验；对实行军检的项目，应当按照规定提交军队派驻的军事代表(以下简称军事代表)检验。

**Article 36** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall conduct incoming inspection, process inspection, and final product inspection in accordance with standards and procedures; shall conduct the specified inspection on the first article; and for items subject to military inspection, shall submit them to the military representatives stationed by the military (hereinafter referred to as military representatives) for inspection according to regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当建立不合格产品处置制度。

**Article 37** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall establish a disposition system for nonconforming products.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　武器装备研制、生产单位应当运用统计技术，分析工序能力，改进过程质量控制，保证产品质量的一致性和稳定性。

**Article 38** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall apply statistical techniques to analyze process capability, improve process quality control, and ensure the consistency and stability of product quality.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　武器装备研制、生产单位交付的武器装备及其配套的设备、备件和技术资料应当经检验合格；交付的技术资料应当满足使用单位对武器装备的使用和维修要求。新型武器装备交付前，武器装备研制、生产单位还应当完成对使用和维修单位的技术培训。  
军事代表应当按照合同和验收技术要求对交付的武器装备及其配套的设备、备件和技术资料进行检验、验收，并监督新型武器装备使用和维修技术培训的实施。

**Article 39** Weapons and equipment delivered by weapons and equipment development and production units, along with their supporting equipment, spare parts, and technical documents, shall be inspected and found qualified; the delivered technical documents shall meet the usage and maintenance requirements of the user unit. Before the delivery of new types of weapons and equipment, the weapons and equipment development and production units shall also complete technical training for the user and maintenance units.  
Military representatives shall inspect and accept the delivered weapons and equipment and their supporting equipment, spare parts, and technical documents in accordance with the contract and acceptance technical requirements, and shall supervise the implementation of technical training for the use and maintenance of new types of weapons and equipment.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　武器装备研制、生产单位对暂停生产的武器装备图样和技术资料应当按照规定归档并妥善保管，不得擅自销毁。

**Article 40** Weapons and equipment development and production units shall archive and properly keep the drawings and technical documents of weapons and equipment whose production has been suspended according to regulations, and shall not destroy them without authorization.

### 第四章　维修质量管理

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　武器装备维修质量管理的任务是保持和恢复武器装备性能。  
武器装备维修单位对武器装备维修质量负责。

**Article 41** The task of quality management for the maintenance of weapon equipment is to maintain and restore the performance of weapon equipment.  
The unit maintaining weapon equipment shall be responsible for the quality of the maintenance of weapon equipment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　武器装备维修单位应当落实质量责任制，严格执行各项规章制度，如实记录武器装备维修质量状态，及时报告发现的质量问题。

**Article 42** The unit maintaining weapon equipment shall implement the quality responsibility system, strictly execute all rules and regulations, truthfully record the quality status of weapon equipment maintenance, and promptly report any quality problems discovered.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军队有关装备部门应当定期组织武器装备质量评估，将武器装备质量问题及时反馈武器装备研制、生产、维修单位，并督促其采取纠正措施。

**Article 43** The relevant equipment departments of the armed forces shall organize quality evaluations of weapon equipment on a regular basis, promptly feed back the quality problems of weapon equipment to the units developing, producing and maintaining weapon equipment, and urge them to take corrective actions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　武器装备研制、生产和维修单位发现武器装备存在质量缺陷的，应当及时、主动通报军队有关装备部门及有关单位，采取纠正措施，解决武器装备质量问题，防止类似质量缺陷重复发生。

**Article 44** Where a unit developing, producing or maintaining weapon equipment discovers a quality defect in weapon equipment, it shall promptly and proactively notify the relevant equipment department of the armed forces and the relevant units, take corrective actions, resolve the quality problems of the weapon equipment, and prevent the recurrence of similar quality defects.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　武器装备研制、生产和维修单位应当建立健全售(修)后服务保障机制，依据合同组织武器装备售(修)后技术服务，及时解决武器装备交付后出现的质量问题，协助武器装备使用单位培训技术骨干，并对武器装备的退役和报废工作提供技术支持。  
部队执行作战和重大任务时，武器装备研制、生产和维修单位应当依照法律、法规的要求组织伴随保障和应急维修保障，协助部队保持、恢复武器装备的质量水平。

**Article 45** The units developing, producing and maintaining weapon equipment shall establish and improve the after-sale (after-maintenance) service support mechanism, organize technical services after the sale (maintenance) of weapon equipment pursuant to the contract, promptly solve quality problems arising after the delivery of weapon equipment, assist the units using weapon equipment in training technical backbone personnel, and provide technical support for the retirement and scrapping of weapon equipment.  
When the armed forces are performing combat or major tasks, the units developing, producing and maintaining weapon equipment shall, in accordance with the requirements of laws and regulations, organize accompanying support and emergency maintenance support, and assist the armed forces in maintaining and restoring the quality level of weapon equipment.

### 第五章　质量监督

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门和总装备部联合组织对承担武器装备研制、生产、维修任务单位的质量管理体系实施认证，对用于武器装备的通用零(部)件、重要元器件和原材料实施认证。  
国务院国防科技工业主管部门和总装备部在各自的职责范围内，组织对武器装备测试和校准试验室实施认可，对质量专业人员实施资格管理。  
未通过质量管理体系认证的单位，不得承担武器装备研制、生产、维修任务。

**Article 46** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the General Armament Department shall jointly organize the certification of the quality management system of the units undertaking the tasks of developing, producing and maintaining weapon equipment, and organize the certification of common parts (components), important components and raw materials used for weapon equipment.  
The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the General Armament Department shall, within their respective functions and duties, organize the accreditation of testing and calibration laboratories for weapon equipment, and administer the qualification of quality professionals.  
Any unit that has not passed the certification of its quality management system may not undertake the tasks of developing, producing or maintaining weapon equipment.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　军工产品定型工作机构应当按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定，全面考核新型武器装备质量，确认其达到武器装备研制总要求和规定标准的质量要求。

**Article 47** The finalization work institution for military products shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, comprehensively examine the quality of new weapon equipment, and confirm that it meets the overall requirements for weapon equipment development and the quality requirements of the prescribed standards.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　军事代表依照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定和武器装备合同要求，对武器装备研制、生产、维修的质量和质量管理工作实施监督。

**Article 48** Military representatives shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission and the requirements of weapon equipment contracts, exercise supervision over the quality and quality management of the development, production and maintenance of weapon equipment.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门、总装备部会同国务院有关部门查处武器装备研制、生产、维修过程中制造、销售和使用假冒伪劣产品的违法行为。省级人民政府及其有关部门应当积极配合查处工作。

**Article 49** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, the General Armament Department, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall investigate and deal with illegal acts of manufacturing, selling and using counterfeit and shoddy products in the process of developing, producing and maintaining weapon equipment. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government and their relevant departments shall actively cooperate with the investigation and handling work.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　武器装备研制、生产、试验、使用和维修过程中发生质量事故时，有关单位应当及时向上级主管部门报告，不得隐瞒不报、谎报或者延误报告。负责武器装备质量监督管理的部门对重大质量事故应当及时调查处理。

**Article 50** When a quality accident occurs in the process of developing, producing, testing, using or maintaining weapon equipment, the relevant unit shall promptly report to the competent higher authority, and shall not conceal, make false reports or delay the report. The department responsible for the supervision and administration of weapon equipment quality shall promptly investigate and handle major quality accidents.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例的行为，有权向负责武器装备质量监督管理的部门以及其他有关部门举报。

**Article 51** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of weapon equipment quality or other relevant departments any act violating these Regulations.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，在武器装备论证工作中弄虚作假，或者违反武器装备论证工作程序，造成严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照有关规定给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, practices fraud in the demonstration work of weapon equipment, or violates the working procedures for weapon equipment demonstration, thereby causing serious consequences, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚；属于军队的武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位，由军队有关部门按照有关规定处理：  
(一)因管理不善、工作失职，导致发生武器装备重大质量事故的；  
(二)对武器装备重大质量事故隐瞒不报、谎报或者延误报告，造成严重后果的；  
(三)在武器装备试验中出具虚假试验数据，造成严重后果的；  
(四)将不合格的武器装备交付部队使用的。  
前款规定的违法行为情节严重的，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门和军队有关部门依法取消其武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修的资格；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; if it is a unit of the armed forces that develops, produces, tests or maintains weapon equipment, the relevant departments of the armed forces shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions:  
(1) causing a major quality accident of weapon equipment due to poor management or dereliction of duty;  
(2) concealing, making false reports or delaying the report of a major quality accident of weapon equipment, thereby causing serious consequences;  
(3) issuing false test data in the testing of weapon equipment, thereby causing serious consequences;  
(4) delivering unqualified weapon equipment to the armed forces for use.  
Where the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph is of a serious nature, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the relevant departments of the armed forces shall cancel its qualification for developing, producing, testing and maintaining weapon equipment according to law; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，泄露武器装备质量信息秘密的，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》等有关法律、法规的规定处罚；属于军队的武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位，由军队有关部门按照有关规定处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, divulges the secrets of weapon equipment quality information, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and other relevant laws and regulations; if it is a unit of the armed forces that develops, produces, tests or maintains weapon equipment, the relevant departments of the armed forces shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，阻碍、干扰武器装备质量监督管理工作，情节严重的，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚；属于军队的武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位，由军队有关部门按照有关规定处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, obstructs or interferes with the supervision and administration of weapon equipment quality, if the circumstances are serious, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; if it is a unit of the armed forces that develops, produces, tests or maintains weapon equipment, the relevant departments of the armed forces shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，为武器装备研制、生产、试验和维修单位提供元器件、原材料以及其他产品，以次充好、以假充真的，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门依照《中华人民共和国产品质量法》等有关法律、法规的规定处罚；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, provides components, raw materials or other products to units developing, producing, testing or maintaining weapon equipment, passing off inferior ones as superior ones or passing off fakes as genuine ones, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　武器装备质量检验机构、认证机构与武器装备研制、生产单位恶意串通，弄虚作假，或者伪造检验、认证结果，出具虚假证明的，取消其检验、认证资格，并由国务院国防科技工业主管部门、国务院有关部门依照《中华人民共和国认证认可条例》的有关规定处罚；属于军队的武器装备质量检验机构、认证机构，由军队有关部门按照有关规定处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where a quality inspection institution or certification institution for weapon equipment maliciously colludes with a unit developing or producing weapon equipment, practices fraud, or forges inspection or certification results, or issues false certificates, its inspection or certification qualification shall be revoked, and the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Certification and Accreditation; if it is a quality inspection institution or certification institution of the armed forces, the relevant departments of the armed forces shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　武器装备质量监督管理人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由所在单位或者上级主管部门依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Where a supervisor or administrator of weapon equipment quality neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, the unit to which he belongs or the competent authority at a higher level shall impose sanctions on him according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　武器装备预先研究、专项工程的质量管理工作，参照本条例执行。

**Article 59** The quality management of the pre-research of weapon equipment and special projects shall be implemented with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　中国人民武装警察部队和民兵的武器装备质量管理工作，参照本条例执行。

**Article 60** The quality management of weapon equipment of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and the militia shall be implemented with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本条例自2010年11月1日起施行。1987年5月25日国务院、中央军事委员会批准，1987年6月5日国防科工委发布的《军工产品质量管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 61** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2010. The Regulations on the Administration of the Quality of Military Products, approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission on May 25, 1987, and promulgated by the Commission on Science, Technology, and Industry for National Defense on June 5, 1987, shall be abolished simultaneously.
