# 中华人民共和国飞行基本规则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2007-10-18

> （2000年7月24日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第288号公布　根据2001年7月27日《国务院、中央军委关于修改〈中华人民共和国飞行基本规则〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2007年10月18日《国务院、中央军委关于修改〈中华人民共和国飞行基本规则〉的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总则 第一条　为了维护国家领空主权，规范中华人民共和国境内的飞行活动，保障飞行活动安全有秩序地进行，制定本规则。　　第二条　凡辖有航空器的单位、个人和与飞行有关的人员及其飞行活动，必须遵守本规则。　　第三条　国家对境内所有飞行实行统一的飞行管制。　　第四条　国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会领导全国的飞行管制工作。　　第五条　航空单位的负责人对本单位遵守本规则负责。机长对本空勤组成员遵守本规则负责。　　第六条　各航空单位在组织与实施飞行中，应当协调配合，通报有关情况。　　第七条　组织与实施飞行，应当按照飞行预先准备、飞行直接准备、飞行实施和飞行讲评等阶段进行。飞行阶段的具体内容和要求，由各航空管理部门自行规定。　　第八条　与飞行有关的所有单位、人员负有保证飞行安全的责任，必须遵守有关规章制度，积极采取预防事故的措施，保证飞行安全。　　经过批准的飞行，有关的机场和部门应当认真做好组织指挥和勤务保障工作。　　第九条　飞行人员在飞行中，必须服从指挥，严格遵守纪律和操作规程，正确处置空中情况。遇到特殊情况，民用航空器的机长，为保证民用航空器及其所载人员的安全，有权对民用航空器作出处置；非民用航空器的机长（或者单座航空器飞行员，下同）在不能请示时，对于航空器的处置有最后决定权。　　第十条　各航空管理部门制定与飞行有关的规范，应当符合本规则的规定。 第二章　空域管理 第十一条　空域管理应当维护国家安全，兼顾民用、军用航空的需要和公众利益，统一规划，合理、充分、有效地利用空域。　　第十二条　空域的划设应当考虑国家安全、飞行需要、飞行管制能力和通信、导航、雷达设施建设以及机场分布、环境保护等因素。　　空域通常划分为机场飞行空域、航路、航线、空中禁区、空中限制区和空中危险区等。空域管理和飞行任务需要的，可以划设空中走廊、空中放油区和临时飞行空域。　　第十三条　空域的划设、调整，应当按照国家有关规定履行审批、备案手续。　　第十四条　机场飞行空域应当划设在航路和空中走廊以外。仪表（云中）飞行空域的边界距离航路、空中走廊以及其他空域的边界，均不得小于10公里。　　机场飞行空域通常包括驾驶术（特技、编队、仪表）飞行空域、科研试飞飞行空域、射击飞行空域、低空飞行空域、超低空飞行空域、海上飞行空域、夜间飞行空域和等待空域等。　　等待空域通常划设在导航台上空；飞行活动频繁的机场，可以在机场附近上空划设。等待空域的最低高度层，距离地面最高障碍物的真实高度不得小于600米。8400米以下，每隔300米为一个等待高度层；8400米至8900米隔500米为一个等待高度层；8900米至12500米，每隔300米为一个等待高度层；12500米以上，每隔600米为一个等待高度层。　　机场飞行空域的划设，由驻机场航空单位提出方案，报所在地区的中国人民解放军军级航空单位或者军区空军批准。　　相邻机场之间飞行空域可以相互调整使用。　　第十五条　航路分为国际航路和国内航路。　　航路的宽度为20公里，其中心线两侧各10公里；航路的某一段受到条件限制的，可以减少宽度，但不得小于8公里。航路还应当确定上限和下限。　　第十六条　航线分为固定航线和临时航线。　　临时航线通常不得与航路、固定航线交叉或者通过飞行频繁的机场上空。　　第十七条　国家重要的政治、经济、军事目标上空，可以划设空中禁区、临时空中禁区。　　未按照国家有关规定经特别批准，任何航空器不得飞入空中禁区和临时空中禁区。　　第十八条　位于航路、航线附近的军事要地、兵器试验场上空和航空兵部队、飞行院校等航空单位的机场飞行空域，可以划设空中限制区。根据需要还可以在其他地区上空划设临时空中限制区。　　在规定时限内，未经飞行管制部门许可的航空器，不得飞入空中限制区或者临时空中限制区。　　第十九条　位于机场、航路、航线附近的对空射击场或者发射场等，根据其射向、射高、范围，可以在上空划设空中危险区或者临时空中危险区。　　在规定时限内，禁止无关航空器飞入空中危险区或者临时空中危险区。　　第二十条　空中禁区、空中限制区、空中危险区的划设、变更或者撤消，应当根据需要公布。　　第二十一条　空中走廊通常划设在机场密集的大、中城市附近地区上空。　　空中走廊的划设应当明确走向、宽度和飞行高度，并兼顾航空器进离场的便利。　　空中走廊的宽度通常为10公里，其中心线两侧各5公里。受条件限制的，其宽度不得小于8公里。　　第二十二条　空中放油区的划设，按照国家有关规定执行。　　第二十三条　临时飞行空域的划设，由申请使用空域的航空单位提出方案，经有关飞行管制部门划定，并通报有关单位。　　国（边）境线至我方一侧10公里之间地带上空禁止划设临时飞行空域。通用航空飞行特殊需要时，经所在地大军区批准后由有关飞行管制部门划设。　　第二十四条　在机场区域内必须严格执行国家有关保护机场净空的规定，禁止在机场附近修建影响飞行安全的射击靶场、建筑物、构筑物、架空线路等障碍物体。　　在机场及其按照国家规定划定的净空保护区域以外，对可能影响飞行安全的高大建筑物或者设施，应当按照国家有关规定设置飞行障碍灯和标志，并使其保持正常状态。　　第二十五条　在距离航路边界30公里以内的地带，禁止修建影响飞行安全的射击靶场和其他设施。　　在前款规定地带以外修建固定或者临时靶场，应当按照国家有关规定获得批准。靶场射击或者发射的方向、航空器进入目标的方向不得与航路交叉。　　第二十六条　修建各种固定对空射击场或者炮兵射击靶场，必须报国务院、中央军事委员会批准。设立临时性靶场和射击点，经有关飞行管制部门同意后，由设立单位报所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和大军区审查批准。　　固定或者临时性的对空射击场、发射场、炮兵射击靶场、射击点的管理单位，应当负责与所在地区飞行管制部门建立有效的通信联络，并制定协同通报制度；在射击或者发射时，应当进行对空观察，确保飞行安全。　　第二十七条　升放无人驾驶航空自由气球或者可能影响飞行安全的系留气球，须经有关飞行管制部门批准。具体管理办法由国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会会同国务院民用航空主管部门、中国人民解放军空军拟定，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准实施。 第三章　飞行管制 第二十八条　中华人民共和国境内的飞行管制，由中国人民解放军空军统一组织实施，各有关飞行管制部门按照各自的职责分工提供空中交通管制服务。　　第二十九条　飞行管制的基本任务是：　　（一）监督航空器严格按照批准的计划飞行，维护飞行秩序，禁止未经批准的航空器擅自飞行；　　（二）禁止未经批准的航空器飞入空中禁区、临时空中禁区或者飞出、飞入国（边）境；　　（三）防止航空器与航空器、航空器与地面障碍物相撞；　　（四）防止地面对空兵器或者对空装置误射航空器。　　第三十条　在中华人民共和国境内，按照飞行管制责任划分为：飞行管制区、飞行管制分区、机场飞行管制区。　　航路、航线地带和民用机场区域设置高空管制区、中低空管制区、终端（进近）管制区、机场塔台管制区。　　在中华人民共和国境内、毗连区、专属经济区及其毗连的公海的上空划分若干飞行情报区。　　第三十一条　各类管制区的划设，应当按照国家有关规定审批。　　第三十二条　各类管制区的飞行管制，由有关飞行管制部门按照职责分工实施。　　第三十三条　中华人民共和国境内特定地区以及执行特殊任务的飞行，应当执行特种飞行管制规定。　　第三十四条　担负飞行管制任务的航空管理部门及航空单位，应当按照各自的职责权限，根据本规则制定飞行管制的具体实施办法。　　相关飞行管制部门之间，应当制定协同制度。　　第三十五条　所有飞行必须预先提出申请，经批准后方可实施。　　获准飞出或者飞入中华人民共和国领空的航空器，实施飞出或者飞入中华人民共和国领空的飞行和各飞行管制区间的飞行，必须经中国人民解放军空军批准；飞行管制区内飞行管制分区间的飞行，经负责该管制区飞行管制的部门批准；飞行管制分区内的飞行，经负责该分区飞行管制的部门批准。　　民用航空的班期飞行，按照规定的航路、航线和班期时刻表进行；民用航空的不定期运输飞行，由国务院民用航空主管部门批准，报中国人民解放军空军备案；涉及其他航空管理部门的，还应当报其他航空管理部门备案。　　第三十六条　战斗飞行按照战斗命令执行，飞机起飞前或者起飞后必须及时通报飞行管制部门。　　第三十七条　对未经批准而起飞或者升空的航空器，有关单位必须迅速查明情况，采取必要措施，直至强迫其降落。　　第三十八条　转场航空器的起飞，机场区域内、外飞行的开始和结束，均应当遵守预定的时间；需要提前或者推迟起飞时间的，应当经上一级飞行管制部门的许可。　　转场航空器超过预定起飞时间一小时仍未起飞，又未申请延期的，其原飞行申请失效。　　第三十九条　组织与实施通用航空飞行活动，必须按照有关规定履行报批手续，并向当地飞行管制部门提出飞行申请。飞行申请的内容包括：任务性质、航空器型别、飞行范围、起止时间、飞行高度和飞行条件等。各航空单位应当按照批准的飞行计划组织实施。　　第四十条　航空器飞入相邻管制区前，飞行管制部门之间应当进行管制移交。管制移交应当按照程序管制或者雷达管制的有关规定实施。　　第四十一条　在中华人民共和国领空飞行的航空器，必须标明明显的识别标志，禁止无识别标志的航空器飞行。　　无识别标志的航空器因特殊情况需要飞行的，必须经中国人民解放军空军批准。　　航空器的识别标志，必须按照国家有关规定获得批准。　　第四十二条　空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员（含飞行管制员，下同）应当按照国家有关规定，经过专门培训、考核，取得执照、证书后，方可上岗工作。 第四章　机场区域内飞行 第四十三条　机场区域是指机场和为该机场划定的一定范围的设置各种飞行空域的空间。　　机场区域应当根据机场周围的地形，使用该机场的航空器的型别和任务性质，邻近机场的位置和跑道方向，机场附近的国（边）境、空中禁区、对空射击场或者发射场、航路和空中走廊的位置，以及公众利益和安全保障等因素划定。　　相邻机场距离过近的，可以合划一个机场区域。　　机场区域的界线通常与机场飞行（塔台）管制区的界线相同。　　第四十四条　机场区域内飞行，应当遵守机场使用细则。　　机场使用细则的制定、审批和备案，按照国家有关规定执行。　　第四十五条　飞行人员飞行时，必须按照规定携带必备的资料、文书和证件。　　第四十六条　飞行准备以及保障飞行的准备工作，必须在飞行开始前完成。在各项准备和天气情况符合飞行要求时，飞行方可开始。　　接受转场飞行航空器降落的机场，必须在航空器到达机场30分钟以前，做好保障降落的各项准备工作。　　第四十七条　昼间飞行，在航空器起飞、降落前，水平能见度小于2公里的，应当打开机场全部障碍标志灯；水平能见度小于1公里的，起飞时还应当打开跑道灯，着陆时还应当打开航空器着陆方向（着陆的反航向）上保障飞行的全部灯光。　　第四十八条　飞行人员自起飞前开车起到着陆后关车止，必须同空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员保持无线电通信联络，并且严格遵守通信纪律。　　未配备无线电通信设备或者通信设备发生故障的航空器，按照本规则附件一的规定进行联络。　　第四十九条　飞行员开车滑行，必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可。滑行或者牵引时，应当遵守下列规定：　　（一）按照规定的或者空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员指定的路线滑行或者牵引。　　（二）滑行速度应当按照相应航空器的飞行手册或者飞行员驾驶守则执行；在障碍物附近滑行，速度不得超过每小时15公里。　　（三）航空器对头相遇，应当各自靠右侧滑行，并且保持必要的安全间隔；航空器交叉相遇，飞行员从座舱左侧看到另一架航空器时应当停止滑行，主动避让。　　（四）两架以上航空器跟进滑行，后航空器不得超越前航空器，后航空器与前航空器的距离，不得小于50米。　　（五）夜间滑行或者牵引，应当打开航空器上的航行灯。　　（六）直升机可以用1米至10米高度的飞行代替滑行。　　水上航空器在滑行或者牵引中，与船只对头或者交叉相遇，应当按照航空器滑行或者牵引时相遇的避让方法避让。　　第五十条　通常情况下，准备起飞的航空器，在起落航线第四转弯后无其他航空器进入着陆时，经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，方可滑进跑道；跑道上无障碍物，方准起飞。　　航空器起飞、着陆时，后航空器应当与前航空器保持规定的安全间隔。　　第五十一条　机场的起落航线通常为左航线；若因地形、城市等条件的限制，或者为避免同邻近机场的起落航线交叉，也可以为右航线；起落航线的飞行高度，通常为300米至500米。　　进行起落航线飞行时，禁止超越同型航空器；各航空器之间的距离，一般应当保持在1500米以上；经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，速度大的航空器可以在第三转弯前超越速度小的航空器，超越时应当从前航空器的外侧超越，其间隔不得小于200米。除必须立即降落的航空器外，任何航空器不得从内侧超越前航空器。　　加入起落航线飞行必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，并且应当顺沿航线加入，不得横向截入。　　第五十二条　航空器起飞后在机场区域内上升或者降落前在机场区域内下降，必须按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示进行。　　航空器飞离机场加入航路、航线和脱离航路、航线飞向机场，应当按照该机场使用细则或者进离场程序规定的航线和高度上升或者下降。　　第五十三条　相邻机场的穿云上升航线、穿云下降航线互有交叉，飞行发生矛盾时，由负责该地区飞行管制的部门调整。　　第五十四条　航空器进行空域飞行时，应当按照规定的航线（航向）、高度、次序进入空域或者脱离空域，并且保持在规定的空域和高度范围内飞行。　　除等待空域外，一个飞行空域，在同一个时间内，只允许安排一至三批航空器飞行。各批航空器飞行活动的高度范围之间，通常应当保持2000米以上的高度差。　　第五十五条　目视飞行时，飞行人员必须加强空中观察。航空器应当与云保持一定的水平距离和垂直距离。　　机长对目视飞行的安全负直接责任。　　第五十六条　航空器进入着陆，应当经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可；不具备着陆条件的，不得勉强着陆。　　航空器着陆后，应当迅速脱离跑道。　　第五十七条　飞行人员在复杂气象条件下按仪表飞行，必须同时具备下列条件：　　（一）飞行人员掌握复杂气象飞行技术；　　（二）航空器配备有完好的航行设备和无线电通信设备。　　第五十八条　复杂气象条件下进入机场区域的飞行，必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可。空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员允许航空器飞入机场区域时，应当及时向飞行员通报下列情况：　　（一）进入的飞行高度；　　（二）机场区域内有关的飞行情况；　　（三）水平能见度或者跑道视程、天气现象和机场上空的云底高度，地面和穿云高度上的风向、风速，场面气压或者修正海平面气压，或者零点高度，以及地面大气温度；　　（四）仪表进场或者穿云方法和着陆航向。　　第五十九条　航空器在等待空域内，必须保持在规定的等待高度层并且按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员指示的方法飞行，未经许可，不得自行改变。　　在等待空域内等待降落的航空器，应当按照规定的顺序降落。特殊情况下，经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，方可优先降落。　　第六十条　航空器穿云下降必须按照该机场的仪表进近图或者穿云图进行。当下降到规定的最低高度或者决断高度仍不能以目视进行着陆时，应当立即停止下降，并且按照规定的航向上升至安全高度。　　航空器因故不能在该机场降落的，空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员或者航空公司签派员及其代理人，应当立即通知备降机场准备接受航空器降落，同时指示航空器飞往备降机场的航向、飞行高度和通知备降机场的天气情况。在飞行人员同备降机场沟通无线电联络并且报告在备降机场着陆已有保障以前，空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员或者航空公司签派员及其代理人应当继续与该航空器保持联络。　　第六十一条　航空器飞临降落机场时，机场的天气情况低于机长飞行的最低气象条件，且航空器无法飞往备降机场的，空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员应当采取一切措施，指挥航空器安全降落。　　第六十二条　飞机在空中拖曳滑翔机时，拖曳飞机同滑翔机应当视为一个航空器。滑翔机飞行员应当服从拖曳飞机飞行员的指挥。　　滑翔机在空中脱离拖曳，必须在规定的高度上进行，并且经拖曳飞机飞行员同意，但紧急情况除外。　　第六十三条　机场区域内飞行的开始和结束的时间，其他任务飞行的航空器在该机场起飞和降落的时间，均应当及时报告上级飞行管制部门。　　相邻机场应当互相主动通报有关的飞行情况。 第五章　航路和航线飞行 第六十四条　航空器使用航路和航线，应当经负责该航路和航线的飞行管制部门同意。　　第六十五条　航路和固定航线地带应当设置必要的监视和导航设备。　　沿航路和固定航线应当有备降机场。备降机场应当有必备的设备和良好的通信、导航、气象保障。　　军用机场作为民用航空器的固定备降机场或者民用机场作为军用航空器的固定备降机场，应当按照国家有关规定经过批准。　　第六十六条　穿越航路和航线的飞行，应当明确穿越的地段、高度和时间，穿越时还应当保证与航路和航线飞行的航空器有规定的飞行间隔。　　第六十七条　飞行任务书是许可飞行人员进行转场飞行和民用航空飞行的基本文件。飞行任务书由驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人签发。　　在飞行任务书中，应当明确飞行任务、起飞时间、航线、高度、允许机长飞行的最低气象条件以及其他有关事项。　　第六十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行前，驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人应当亲自或者指定专人对飞行人员的飞行准备情况进行检查。飞行准备质量符合要求时，方可执行飞行任务。　　第六十九条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器的起飞，应当根据飞行人员和航空器的准备情况，起飞机场、降落机场和备降机场的准备情况以及天气情况等确定；有下列情况之一的，不得起飞：　　（一）空勤组成员不齐，或者由于技术、健康等原因不适于飞行的；　　（二）飞行人员尚未完成飞行准备、飞行准备质量不符合要求、驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人未批准飞行的；　　（三）飞行人员未携带飞行任务书、飞行气象文件及其他必备飞行文件的；　　（四）飞行人员未校对本次飞行所需的航行、通信、导航资料和仪表进近图或者穿云图的；　　（五）航空器或者航空器上的设备有故障可能影响飞行安全，或者民用航空器设备低于最低设备清单规定，或者军用航空器经机长确认可能影响本次飞行安全的；　　（六）航空器表面的冰、霜、雪未除净的；　　（七）航空器上的装载和乘载不符合规定的；　　（八）航空器未按规定携带备用燃料的；　　（九）天气情况低于机长飞行的最低气象条件，以及天气情况危及本次飞行安全的。　　第七十条　飞行人员在飞行中必须遵守有关的飞行规则和飞行任务书中的各项规定，服从飞行指挥，准确实施领航，保持规定的航行诸元，注意观察空中情况，按照规定及时报告航空器位置、飞行情况和天气情况，特别是危险天气现象及其发展情况。　　第七十一条　目视飞行时，航空器应当按照下列规定避让：　　（一）在同一高度上对头相遇，应当各自向右避让，并保持500米以上的间隔；　　（二）在同一高度上交叉相遇，飞行员从座舱左侧看到另一架航空器时应当下降高度，从座舱右侧看到另一架航空器时应当上升高度；　　（三）在同一高度上超越前航空器，应当从前航空器右侧超越，并保持500米以上的间隔；　　（四）单机应当主动避让编队或者拖曳飞机，有动力装置的航空器应当主动避让无动力装置的航空器，战斗机应当主动避让运输机。　　第七十二条　在与航路、固定航线交叉或者靠近的临时航线飞行时，飞行人员应当加强对空中的观察，防止与航路飞行的航空器相撞。当临时航线与航路、固定航线交叉时，水平能见度大于8公里的，应当按照规定的飞行高度通过；在云中飞行或者水平能见度小于8公里的，应当按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示通过。在靠近航路的航线上飞行时，应当与航路的边界保持规定的安全间隔。　　第七十三条　未配备复杂气象飞行设备的航空器，机长应当按照规定的飞行最低气象条件，在安全高度以上进行目视飞行，防止飞入云中。　　第七十四条　当天气情况不低于机长飞行的最低气象条件时，机长方可在300米以下进行目视飞行，飞行时航空器距离云层底部不得小于50米。　　第七十五条　航空器沿航路和固定航线飞行通过中途机场100至50公里前，除有协议的外，飞行人员应当向该机场的空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员报告预计通过的时间和高度。中途机场的空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员必须指挥在本机场区域内飞行的航空器避让过往航空器，保证其安全通过；无特殊原因，不得改变过往航空器的航线和高度。　　航空器在临时航线飞行通过中途机场时，应当按照规定的航线和高度通过，或者按照该机场空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示通过。　　第七十六条　飞行中，飞行人员与地面联络中断，可以停止执行飞行任务，返回原机场或者飞往就近的备降机场降落。当保持原高度飞向备降机场符合飞行高度层配备规定时，仍保持原高度飞行；当保持原高度飞向备降机场不符合飞行高度层配备规定时，应当下降到下一层高度飞向备降机场；因飞行安全高度所限不能下降到下一层高度的，应当上升至上一层高度飞向备降机场。　　第七十七条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，在起飞前或者在中途机场降落后需要继续飞行的，机长或者其代理人必须到机场飞行管制部门办理飞行手续，校对有关资料，经批准后方可起飞；航空器降落后需要连续起飞的，必须事先经中途机场飞行管制部门的许可。　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器降落后，机长或者其代理人必须到机场飞行管制部门或者航空公司报告飞行情况和航路、航线天气情况，送交飞行任务书和飞行天气报告表。　　未经批准而降落在非预定机场的航空器，必须由驻该机场航空单位的负责人向上级报告，经批准后方可起飞。　　第七十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器到达预定机场后，其各项保障工作由驻该机场的有关部门按照规定或者协议负责。 第六章　飞行间隔 第七十九条　飞行间隔是为了防止飞行冲突，保证飞行安全，提高飞行空间和时间利用率所规定的航空器之间应当保持的最小安全距离。飞行间隔包括垂直间隔和水平间隔。水平间隔分为纵向间隔和横向间隔。　　机长必须按照规定的飞行间隔飞行，需要改变时，应当经飞行管制部门许可。　　第八十条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的垂直间隔，按照飞行高度层配备。飞行高度层按照以下标准划分：　　（一）真航线角在0度至179度范围内，高度由900米至8100米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度由8900米至12500米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在12500米以上，每隔1200米为一个高度层。　　（二）真航线角在180度至359度范围内，高度由600米至8400米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度由9200米至12200米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在13100米以上，每隔1200米为一个高度层。　　（三）飞行高度层应当根据标准大气压条件下假定海平面计算。真航线角应当从航线起点和转弯点量取。　　飞行高度层的具体配备标准见本规则附件二。　　第八十一条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的水平间隔，由中国人民解放军空军会同国务院民用航空主管部门拟定，报国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会批准。　　第八十二条　飞行的安全高度是避免航空器与地面障碍物相撞的最低飞行高度。　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的安全高度，在高原和山区应当高出航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内最高标高600米；在其他地区应当高出航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内最高标高400米。　　受性能限制的航空器，其航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的安全高度，由有关航空管理部门另行规定。　　第八十三条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，在航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内的最高标高不超过100米，大气压力不低于1000百帕（750毫米水银柱）的，允许在600米的高度层内飞行；当最高标高超过100米，大气压力低于1000百帕（750毫米水银柱）的，飞行最低的高度层必须相应提高，保证飞行的真实高度不低于安全高度。　　第八十四条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的高度层，由批准本次飞行的负责人，通过飞行管制部门具体配备。　　飞行高度层应当根据飞行任务的性质、航空器性能、飞行区域以及航线的地形、天气和飞行情况等配备。　　第八十五条　在同一条航路、航线有数架（数批）航空器同时飞行并且互有影响的，应当分别将每架（每批）航空器配备在不同的高度层内；不能配备在不同高度层的，可以允许数架（数批）航空器在同一条航路、航线、同一个高度层内飞行，但是各架（各批）航空器之间应当保持规定的纵向间隔。　　第八十六条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器起飞前，应当将场面气压的数值调整到航空器上气压高度表的固定指标，使气压高度表的指针指到零的位置。　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器起飞后，在未规定过渡高度或者过渡高的机场上升到距该机场道面600米高度时，应当将航空器上气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标，然后再继续上升到规定的飞行高度层；规定有过渡高度或者过渡高的机场，在上升至过渡高度或者过渡高时，应当将气压高度表调整到标准海平面气压值。　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，进入降落机场区域并下降至该机场过渡高度层时，或者根据空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员的指示，将机场场面气压的数值调整到航空器上气压高度表的固定指标。　　仅供民用航空器起降的机场，可以修正海平面气压值为航空器气压高度表拨正值。　　提供外国航空器起降的机场，可以向外国航空器提供机场修正海平面气压值。　　军用、民用航空器在同一机场同时飞行的，必须统一航空器上气压高度表拨正时机。　　第八十七条　在高原机场起飞前，航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压数值的，应当将气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标（此时所指示的高度为假定零点高度），然后起飞和上升到规定的飞行高度。　　在高原机场降落时，航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压数值的，应当按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员通知的假定零点高度进行着陆。航空器上有两个气压高度表的，应当将其中一个气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标，而将另一个气压高度表以修正的海平面气压值调整到固定指标。　　在高原、山区飞行，必须注意航空器上气压高度表与无线电高度表配合使用。　　第八十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行时，因航空器故障、积冰、绕飞雷雨区等原因需要改变飞行高度层的，机长应当向飞行管制部门报告原因和当时航空器的准确位置，请求另行配备飞行高度层。飞行管制部门允许航空器改变飞行高度层时，必须明确改变的高度层以及改变高度层的地段和时间。　　遇有紧急情况，飞行安全受到威胁时，机长可以决定改变原配备的飞行高度层，但必须立即报告飞行管制部门，并对该决定负责。改变高度层的方法是：从航空器飞行的方向向右转30度，并以此航向飞行20公里，再左转平行原航线上升或者下降到新的高度层，然后转回原航线。 第七章　飞行指挥 第八十九条　组织实施飞行指挥应当根据本规则和有关规定进行，做到正确、及时和不间断。　　第九十条　飞行指挥员必须切实履行职责，维护机场、空中秩序和飞行纪律，并做到：　　（一）了解飞行任务、飞行计划、飞行人员的技术水平及健康状况、航空器性能和机载设备，以及各项保障工作情况；　　（二）掌握飞行动态，了解天气变化，及时向飞行人员通知有关的空中情况和指挥其准确地按照计划飞行；　　（三）当空中情况发生变化时，及时采取措施，正确处置。　　第九十一条　飞行指挥必须按照下列调配原则安排飞行次序：　　（一）一切飞行让战斗飞行；　　（二）其他飞行让专机飞行和重要任务飞行；　　（三）国内一般任务飞行让班期飞行；　　（四）训练飞行让任务飞行；　　（五）场内飞行让场外飞行；　　（六）场内、场外飞行让转场飞行。　　第九十二条　在飞行期间，所有参加飞行和保障飞行的人员，必须服从飞行指挥员的指挥。　　第九十三条　驻在同一机场的军用航空器和民用航空器同时飞行时，必须实施统一指挥。军用航空单位派出飞行指挥员，民用航空单位派出飞行副指挥员。　　飞行副指挥员负责向飞行指挥员报告民用航空器的航行诸元和有关飞行情况，并且按照飞行指挥员的指示，对民用航空器实施指挥。　　第九十四条　执行不同任务的航空器或者不同型别的航空器，在同一机场同时飞行的，应当根据具体情况安排优先起飞和降落的顺序。　　对执行紧急或者重要任务的航空器，班期飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，速度大的航空器，应当允许优先起飞；对有故障的航空器，剩余油量少的航空器，执行紧急或者重要任务的航空器，班期飞行和航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，应当允许优先降落。　　第九十五条　飞行指挥用无线电实施。指挥用语应当简短、明确、易懂、规范。　　未配备无线电通信设备的航空器，无线电受干扰或者无线电通信设备发生故障的航空器，按照本规则附件一的规定实施指挥。　　第九十六条　现用机场应当设飞行管制室、起飞线塔台（指挥塔台）或者机场管制塔台，其位置应当有良好的视界，可观察到机场、净空地带以及航空器飞行和航空器在机场上的活动。　　机场飞行管制室、起飞线塔台（指挥塔台）或者机场管制塔台，应当配备指挥和保障飞行的通信设备、雷达显示设备或者雷达标图以及其他有关设备和必要的文件图表等。　　第九十七条　作战飞行的指挥，按照中国人民解放军有关规定执行。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国家领空主权，规范中华人民共和国境内的飞行活动，保障飞行活动安全有秩序地进行，制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated for the purposes of safeguarding the sovereignty of the national airspace, regulating flight activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and ensuring that flight activities are conducted safely and in an orderly manner.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡辖有航空器的单位、个人和与飞行有关的人员及其飞行活动，必须遵守本规则。

**Article 2** All entities and individuals that possess aircraft, as well as persons involved in flight and their flight activities, must comply with these Rules.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对境内所有飞行实行统一的飞行管制。

**Article 3** The State shall exercise unified flight control over all flights within the territory.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会领导全国的飞行管制工作。

**Article 4** The Air Traffic Control Commission of the State Council and the Central Military Commission shall lead the national flight control work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　航空单位的负责人对本单位遵守本规则负责。机长对本空勤组成员遵守本规则负责。

**Article 5** The person in charge of an aviation unit shall be responsible for the unit's compliance with these Rules. The captain of an aircraft shall be responsible for the compliance of the crew members of that aircraft with these Rules.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各航空单位在组织与实施飞行中，应当协调配合，通报有关情况。

**Article 6** In organizing and implementing flights, each aviation unit shall coordinate and cooperate, and communicate relevant information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　组织与实施飞行，应当按照飞行预先准备、飞行直接准备、飞行实施和飞行讲评等阶段进行。飞行阶段的具体内容和要求，由各航空管理部门自行规定。

**Article 7** The organization and implementation of flights shall be conducted in phases such as pre-flight preparation, direct flight preparation, flight execution, and flight debriefing. The specific contents and requirements of each flight phase shall be prescribed by the respective aviation management departments.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　与飞行有关的所有单位、人员负有保证飞行安全的责任，必须遵守有关规章制度，积极采取预防事故的措施，保证飞行安全。  
　　经过批准的飞行，有关的机场和部门应当认真做好组织指挥和勤务保障工作。

**Article 8** All units and personnel related to flight shall bear the responsibility of ensuring flight safety, must comply with relevant rules and regulations, and actively take measures to prevent accidents to ensure flight safety.  
For approved flights, the relevant airports and departments shall properly carry out organization, command, and service support work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　飞行人员在飞行中，必须服从指挥，严格遵守纪律和操作规程，正确处置空中情况。遇到特殊情况，民用航空器的机长，为保证民用航空器及其所载人员的安全，有权对民用航空器作出处置；非民用航空器的机长（或者单座航空器飞行员，下同）在不能请示时，对于航空器的处置有最后决定权。

**Article 9** During flight, flight personnel must obey commands, strictly observe discipline and operating procedures, and properly handle airborne situations. In case of special circumstances, the captain of a civil aircraft is entitled to take actions regarding the civil aircraft to ensure the safety of the civil aircraft and persons on board; the captain of a non-civil aircraft (or a pilot of a single-seat aircraft, the same below), when unable to seek instructions, shall have the final decision on the disposition of the aircraft.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各航空管理部门制定与飞行有关的规范，应当符合本规则的规定。

**Article 10** The norms related to flight formulated by various aviation management departments shall comply with the provisions of these Rules.

### 第二章　空域管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　空域管理应当维护国家安全，兼顾民用、军用航空的需要和公众利益，统一规划，合理、充分、有效地利用空域。

**Article 11** Airspace management shall safeguard national security, take into account the needs of civil and military aviation and the public interest, and conduct unified planning to use airspace in a reasonable, full, and effective manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　空域的划设应当考虑国家安全、飞行需要、飞行管制能力和通信、导航、雷达设施建设以及机场分布、环境保护等因素。  
　　空域通常划分为机场飞行空域、航路、航线、空中禁区、空中限制区和空中危险区等。空域管理和飞行任务需要的，可以划设空中走廊、空中放油区和临时飞行空域。

**Article 12** The designation of airspace shall take into account factors such as national security, flight needs, flight control capabilities, construction of communication, navigation and radar facilities, distribution of airports, and environmental protection.  
Airspace is normally divided into airport flight airspace, airways, air routes, prohibited areas, restricted areas, and danger areas. Where necessary for airspace management or flight missions, air corridors, air fuel jettisoning areas, and temporary flight airspace may be designated.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　空域的划设、调整，应当按照国家有关规定履行审批、备案手续。

**Article 13** The designation and adjustment of airspace shall go through the procedures of approval and filing according to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　机场飞行空域应当划设在航路和空中走廊以外。仪表（云中）飞行空域的边界距离航路、空中走廊以及其他空域的边界，均不得小于10公里。  
　　机场飞行空域通常包括驾驶术（特技、编队、仪表）飞行空域、科研试飞飞行空域、射击飞行空域、低空飞行空域、超低空飞行空域、海上飞行空域、夜间飞行空域和等待空域等。  
　　等待空域通常划设在导航台上空；飞行活动频繁的机场，可以在机场附近上空划设。等待空域的最低高度层，距离地面最高障碍物的真实高度不得小于600米。8400米以下，每隔300米为一个等待高度层；8400米至8900米隔500米为一个等待高度层；8900米至12500米，每隔300米为一个等待高度层；12500米以上，每隔600米为一个等待高度层。  
　　机场飞行空域的划设，由驻机场航空单位提出方案，报所在地区的中国人民解放军军级航空单位或者军区空军批准。  
　　相邻机场之间飞行空域可以相互调整使用。

**Article 14** Airport flight airspace shall be designated outside airways and air corridors. The boundary of instrument (in-cloud) flight airspace shall not be less than 10 kilometers from the boundaries of airways, air corridors, and other airspace.  
Airport flight airspace normally includes flight airspace for piloting techniques (aerobatics, formation, instrument), scientific research test flight airspace, gunnery flight airspace, low-altitude flight airspace, ultra-low-altitude flight airspace, maritime flight airspace, night flight airspace, and holding airspace, etc.  
Holding airspace is normally designated over navigation stations; for airports with frequent flight activities, it may be designated in the vicinity of the airport. The minimum altitude level of holding airspace shall not be less than 600 meters above the highest obstacle on the ground. Below 8,400 meters, every 300 meters constitutes a holding altitude level; from 8,400 meters to 8,900 meters, every 500 meters constitutes a holding altitude level; from 8,900 meters to 12,500 meters, every 300 meters constitutes a holding altitude level; above 12,500 meters, every 600 meters constitutes a holding altitude level.  
The designation of airport flight airspace shall be proposed by the aviation unit stationed at the airport and reported to the relevant corps-level aviation unit of the People's Liberation Army or the air force of the military region for approval.  
The flight airspace between adjacent airports may be adjusted and used mutually.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　航路分为国际航路和国内航路。  
　　航路的宽度为20公里，其中心线两侧各10公里；航路的某一段受到条件限制的，可以减少宽度，但不得小于8公里。航路还应当确定上限和下限。

**Article 15** Airways are divided into international airways and domestic airways.  
The width of an airway shall be 20 kilometers, with 10 kilometers on each side of its center line; where a section of the airway is limited by conditions, the width may be reduced, but shall not be less than 8 kilometers. The upper and lower limits of the airway shall also be determined.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　航线分为固定航线和临时航线。  
　　临时航线通常不得与航路、固定航线交叉或者通过飞行频繁的机场上空。

**Article 16** Air routes are divided into fixed air routes and temporary air routes.  
Temporary air routes shall normally not cross airways or fixed air routes, nor pass over airports with frequent flight activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家重要的政治、经济、军事目标上空，可以划设空中禁区、临时空中禁区。  
　　未按照国家有关规定经特别批准，任何航空器不得飞入空中禁区和临时空中禁区。

**Article 17** Over important political, economic, and military targets of the State, prohibited areas and temporary prohibited areas may be designated.  
Without special approval according to the relevant provisions of the State, no aircraft may fly into prohibited areas or temporary prohibited areas.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　位于航路、航线附近的军事要地、兵器试验场上空和航空兵部队、飞行院校等航空单位的机场飞行空域，可以划设空中限制区。根据需要还可以在其他地区上空划设临时空中限制区。  
　　在规定时限内，未经飞行管制部门许可的航空器，不得飞入空中限制区或者临时空中限制区。

**Article 18** Over military important places near airways or air routes, weapon test ranges, and airport flight airspace of aviation units such as aviation troops and flight academies, restricted areas may be designated. Temporary restricted areas may also be designated over other areas as needed.  
Within the specified time limit, without permission from the flight control department, no aircraft may fly into restricted areas or temporary restricted areas.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　位于机场、航路、航线附近的对空射击场或者发射场等，根据其射向、射高、范围，可以在上空划设空中危险区或者临时空中危险区。  
　　在规定时限内，禁止无关航空器飞入空中危险区或者临时空中危险区。

**Article 19** For anti-aircraft shooting ranges or launching sites near airports, airways, or air routes, based on their direction, height, and range, danger areas or temporary danger areas may be designated in the airspace above.  
Within the specified time limit, no unrelated aircraft may fly into danger areas or temporary danger areas.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　空中禁区、空中限制区、空中危险区的划设、变更或者撤消，应当根据需要公布。

**Article 20** The designation, alteration, or cancellation of prohibited areas, restricted areas, and danger areas shall be announced as needed.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　空中走廊通常划设在机场密集的大、中城市附近地区上空。  
　　空中走廊的划设应当明确走向、宽度和飞行高度，并兼顾航空器进离场的便利。  
　　空中走廊的宽度通常为10公里，其中心线两侧各5公里。受条件限制的，其宽度不得小于8公里。

**Article 21** Air corridors are normally designated over the vicinity of large and medium-sized cities with dense airport distribution.  
The designation of air corridors shall specify the direction, width, and flight altitude, and shall take into account the convenience of aircraft approach and departure.  
The width of an air corridor is normally 10 kilometers, with 5 kilometers on each side of its center line. Where limited by conditions, the width shall not be less than 8 kilometers.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　空中放油区的划设，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 22** The designation of air fuel jettisoning areas shall be carried out according to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　临时飞行空域的划设，由申请使用空域的航空单位提出方案，经有关飞行管制部门划定，并通报有关单位。  
　　国（边）境线至我方一侧10公里之间地带上空禁止划设临时飞行空域。通用航空飞行特殊需要时，经所在地大军区批准后由有关飞行管制部门划设。

**Article 23** The designation of temporary flight airspace shall be proposed by the aviation unit applying for the use of the airspace, delineated by the relevant flight control department, and notified to the relevant units.  
The designation of temporary flight airspace over the belt between the national border (frontier) line and 10 kilometers on our side is prohibited. When special needs exist for general aviation flights, it may be designated by the relevant flight control department after approval by the local major military command.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在机场区域内必须严格执行国家有关保护机场净空的规定，禁止在机场附近修建影响飞行安全的射击靶场、建筑物、构筑物、架空线路等障碍物体。  
　　在机场及其按照国家规定划定的净空保护区域以外，对可能影响飞行安全的高大建筑物或者设施，应当按照国家有关规定设置飞行障碍灯和标志，并使其保持正常状态。

**Article 24** Within the airport area, the State's provisions on protecting airport clearance must be strictly implemented. It is prohibited to construct shooting ranges, buildings, structures, overhead lines, and other obstacles that affect flight safety near the airport.  
Outside the airport and its designated clearance protection area according to State provisions, for tall buildings or facilities that may affect flight safety, flight obstacle lights and markings shall be installed according to the relevant provisions of the State, and they shall be maintained in normal condition.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在距离航路边界30公里以内的地带，禁止修建影响飞行安全的射击靶场和其他设施。  
　　在前款规定地带以外修建固定或者临时靶场，应当按照国家有关规定获得批准。靶场射击或者发射的方向、航空器进入目标的方向不得与航路交叉。

**Article 25** Within the belt of 30 kilometers from the boundary of an airway, it is prohibited to construct shooting ranges and other facilities that affect flight safety.  
The construction of fixed or temporary ranges outside the belt specified in the preceding paragraph shall obtain approval according to the relevant provisions of the State. The direction of firing or launching of the range and the direction of aircraft entering the target shall not cross the airway.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　修建各种固定对空射击场或者炮兵射击靶场，必须报国务院、中央军事委员会批准。设立临时性靶场和射击点，经有关飞行管制部门同意后，由设立单位报所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和大军区审查批准。  
　　固定或者临时性的对空射击场、发射场、炮兵射击靶场、射击点的管理单位，应当负责与所在地区飞行管制部门建立有效的通信联络，并制定协同通报制度；在射击或者发射时，应当进行对空观察，确保飞行安全。

**Article 26** The construction of all fixed anti-aircraft shooting ranges or artillery firing ranges must be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval. For the establishment of temporary ranges and firing points, after obtaining the consent of the relevant flight control department, the establishing unit shall report to the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the major military command for review and approval.  
The management units of fixed or temporary anti-aircraft shooting ranges, launching sites, artillery firing ranges, and firing points shall be responsible for establishing effective communication links with the local flight control department and formulating a coordination and notification system; when firing or launching occurs, they shall conduct air observation to ensure flight safety.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　升放无人驾驶航空自由气球或者可能影响飞行安全的系留气球，须经有关飞行管制部门批准。具体管理办法由国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会会同国务院民用航空主管部门、中国人民解放军空军拟定，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准实施。

**Article 27** The release of unmanned free balloons or tethered balloons that may affect flight safety shall be approved by the relevant flight control department. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the Air Traffic Control Commission of the State Council and the Central Military Commission together with the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council and the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army, and shall be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval before implementation.

### 第三章　飞行管制

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中华人民共和国境内的飞行管制，由中国人民解放军空军统一组织实施，各有关飞行管制部门按照各自的职责分工提供空中交通管制服务。

**Article 28** The flight control within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be uniformly organized and implemented by the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army. The relevant flight control departments shall provide air traffic control services according to their respective responsibilities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　飞行管制的基本任务是：  
　　（一）监督航空器严格按照批准的计划飞行，维护飞行秩序，禁止未经批准的航空器擅自飞行；  
　　（二）禁止未经批准的航空器飞入空中禁区、临时空中禁区或者飞出、飞入国（边）境；  
　　（三）防止航空器与航空器、航空器与地面障碍物相撞；  
　　（四）防止地面对空兵器或者对空装置误射航空器。

**Article 29** The basic tasks of flight control are:  
(1) Supervise aircraft to fly strictly according to the approved plans, maintain flight order, and prohibit unauthorized aircraft from flying without approval;  
(2) Prohibit unauthorized aircraft from flying into prohibited areas or temporary prohibited areas, or from flying out of or into the national border (frontier);  
(3) Prevent collisions between aircraft, and between aircraft and ground obstacles;  
(4) Prevent ground anti-aircraft weapons or devices from mistakenly firing at aircraft.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在中华人民共和国境内，按照飞行管制责任划分为：飞行管制区、飞行管制分区、机场飞行管制区。  
　　航路、航线地带和民用机场区域设置高空管制区、中低空管制区、终端（进近）管制区、机场塔台管制区。  
　　在中华人民共和国境内、毗连区、专属经济区及其毗连的公海的上空划分若干飞行情报区。

**Article 30** Within the territory of the People's Republic of China, according to flight control responsibilities, it is divided into: flight control areas, flight control sub-areas, and airport flight control areas.  
In airway and air route zones and civil airport areas, high-altitude control areas, medium-low-altitude control areas, terminal (approach) control areas, and airport tower control areas shall be established.  
Over the territory, contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone, and the adjacent high seas of the People's Republic of China, several flight information regions shall be divided.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各类管制区的划设，应当按照国家有关规定审批。

**Article 31** The designation of various types of control areas shall be subject to approval according to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各类管制区的飞行管制，由有关飞行管制部门按照职责分工实施。

**Article 32** The flight control of various types of control areas shall be implemented by the relevant flight control departments according to their respective responsibilities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中华人民共和国境内特定地区以及执行特殊任务的飞行，应当执行特种飞行管制规定。

**Article 33** Flights in specific areas within the territory of the People's Republic of China and flights performing special tasks shall comply with special flight control regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　担负飞行管制任务的航空管理部门及航空单位，应当按照各自的职责权限，根据本规则制定飞行管制的具体实施办法。  
　　相关飞行管制部门之间，应当制定协同制度。

**Article 34** The aviation management departments and aviation units charged with flight control tasks shall, according to their respective responsibilities and authorities, formulate specific implementation measures for flight control based on these Rules.  
Relevant flight control departments shall formulate coordination systems among themselves.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　所有飞行必须预先提出申请，经批准后方可实施。  
　　获准飞出或者飞入中华人民共和国领空的航空器，实施飞出或者飞入中华人民共和国领空的飞行和各飞行管制区间的飞行，必须经中国人民解放军空军批准；飞行管制区内飞行管制分区间的飞行，经负责该管制区飞行管制的部门批准；飞行管制分区内的飞行，经负责该分区飞行管制的部门批准。  
　　民用航空的班期飞行，按照规定的航路、航线和班期时刻表进行；民用航空的不定期运输飞行，由国务院民用航空主管部门批准，报中国人民解放军空军备案；涉及其他航空管理部门的，还应当报其他航空管理部门备案。

**Article 35** All flights must apply in advance and may only be implemented after approval.  
For aircraft that have been approved to fly out of or into the airspace of the People's Republic of China, flights to implement such exit or entry and flights between flight control areas must be approved by the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army; flights between flight control sub-areas within a flight control area shall be approved by the department responsible for flight control in that area; flights within a flight control sub-area shall be approved by the department responsible for flight control in that sub-area.  
Scheduled flights of civil aviation shall be conducted according to the prescribed airways, air routes, and schedules; non-scheduled transport flights of civil aviation shall be approved by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council and reported to the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army for filing; where other aviation management departments are involved, they shall also be reported to those other aviation management departments for filing.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　战斗飞行按照战斗命令执行，飞机起飞前或者起飞后必须及时通报飞行管制部门。

**Article 36** Combat flights shall be executed according to combat orders. Before taking off or after taking off, aircraft must promptly notify the flight control department.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对未经批准而起飞或者升空的航空器，有关单位必须迅速查明情况，采取必要措施，直至强迫其降落。

**Article 37** For aircraft that take off or ascend without approval, the relevant units must promptly investigate the situation and take necessary measures, up to forcing them to land.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　转场航空器的起飞，机场区域内、外飞行的开始和结束，均应当遵守预定的时间；需要提前或者推迟起飞时间的，应当经上一级飞行管制部门的许可。  
　　转场航空器超过预定起飞时间一小时仍未起飞，又未申请延期的，其原飞行申请失效。

**Article 38** The takeoff of transit aircraft, and the start and end of flights within and outside the airport area, shall comply with the scheduled time; where the takeoff time needs to be advanced or postponed, approval from the superior flight control department shall be obtained.  
If a transit aircraft has not taken off one hour after the scheduled takeoff time and has not applied for an extension, its original flight application shall cease to have effect.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　组织与实施通用航空飞行活动，必须按照有关规定履行报批手续，并向当地飞行管制部门提出飞行申请。飞行申请的内容包括：任务性质、航空器型别、飞行范围、起止时间、飞行高度和飞行条件等。各航空单位应当按照批准的飞行计划组织实施。

**Article 39** For organizing and implementing general aviation flight activities, the procedures for reporting and approval must be completed according to relevant provisions, and a flight application shall be submitted to the local flight control department. The contents of the flight application include: nature of the mission, type of aircraft, scope of flight, start and end times, flight altitude, and flight conditions, etc. Each aviation unit shall organize and implement according to the approved flight plan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　航空器飞入相邻管制区前，飞行管制部门之间应当进行管制移交。管制移交应当按照程序管制或者雷达管制的有关规定实施。

**Article 40** Before an aircraft enters an adjacent control area, the flight control departments shall conduct control handover. The control handover shall be implemented according to the relevant provisions of procedural control or radar control.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在中华人民共和国领空飞行的航空器，必须标明明显的识别标志，禁止无识别标志的航空器飞行。  
　　无识别标志的航空器因特殊情况需要飞行的，必须经中国人民解放军空军批准。  
　　航空器的识别标志，必须按照国家有关规定获得批准。

**Article 41** Aircraft flying in the airspace of the People's Republic of China must bear clear identification marks. Aircraft without identification marks are prohibited from flying.  
If an aircraft without identification marks needs to fly due to special circumstances, approval from the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army must be obtained.  
The identification marks of aircraft must be approved according to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员（含飞行管制员，下同）应当按照国家有关规定，经过专门培训、考核，取得执照、证书后，方可上岗工作。

**Article 42** Air traffic controllers and flight commanders (including flight controllers, the same below) shall, according to the relevant provisions of the State, undergo specialized training and assessment, and obtain licenses and certificates before they may work.

### 第四章　机场区域内飞行

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　机场区域是指机场和为该机场划定的一定范围的设置各种飞行空域的空间。  
　　机场区域应当根据机场周围的地形，使用该机场的航空器的型别和任务性质，邻近机场的位置和跑道方向，机场附近的国（边）境、空中禁区、对空射击场或者发射场、航路和空中走廊的位置，以及公众利益和安全保障等因素划定。  
　　相邻机场距离过近的，可以合划一个机场区域。  
　　机场区域的界线通常与机场飞行（塔台）管制区的界线相同。

**Article 43** The airport area refers to the airport and the space of a certain scope designated for that airport where various flight airspaces are established.  
The airport area shall be designated based on factors such as the terrain around the airport, the types and mission natures of aircraft using the airport, the positions and runway directions of neighboring airports, the national border (frontier), prohibited areas, anti-aircraft shooting ranges or launching sites, airways and air corridors near the airport, as well as public interest and safety protection.  
Where adjacent airports are too close, one airport area may be combined for them.  
The boundaries of the airport area are normally the same as the boundaries of the airport flight (tower) control area.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　机场区域内飞行，应当遵守机场使用细则。  
　　机场使用细则的制定、审批和备案，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 44** Flights within the airport area shall comply with the airport operation instructions.  
The formulation, approval, and filing of the airport operation instructions shall be carried out according to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　飞行人员飞行时，必须按照规定携带必备的资料、文书和证件。

**Article 45** When flying, flight personnel must carry the necessary materials, documents, and certificates as prescribed.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　飞行准备以及保障飞行的准备工作，必须在飞行开始前完成。在各项准备和天气情况符合飞行要求时，飞行方可开始。  
　　接受转场飞行航空器降落的机场，必须在航空器到达机场30分钟以前，做好保障降落的各项准备工作。

**Article 46** Flight preparation and the preparation work for supporting flights must be completed before the flight begins. When all preparations and weather conditions meet flight requirements, the flight may commence.  
An airport accepting the landing of a transit aircraft must complete all preparatory work for ensuring the landing at least 30 minutes before the aircraft arrives at the airport.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　昼间飞行，在航空器起飞、降落前，水平能见度小于2公里的，应当打开机场全部障碍标志灯；水平能见度小于1公里的，起飞时还应当打开跑道灯，着陆时还应当打开航空器着陆方向（着陆的反航向）上保障飞行的全部灯光。

**Article 47** For daytime flights, when the horizontal visibility is less than 2 kilometers before takeoff or landing of an aircraft, all obstacle warning lights of the airport shall be turned on; when the horizontal visibility is less than 1 kilometer, for takeoff, the runway lights shall also be turned on, and for landing, all lights ensuring flight in the aircraft's landing direction (the opposite direction of landing) shall also be turned on.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　飞行人员自起飞前开车起到着陆后关车止，必须同空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员保持无线电通信联络，并且严格遵守通信纪律。  
　　未配备无线电通信设备或者通信设备发生故障的航空器，按照本规则附件一的规定进行联络。

**Article 48** Flight personnel, from engine start before takeoff to engine shutdown after landing, must maintain radio communication contact with the air traffic controller or flight commander, and strictly observe communication discipline.  
For aircraft not equipped with radio communication equipment or with malfunctioning communication equipment, contact shall be made according to the provisions of Appendix 1 of these Rules.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　飞行员开车滑行，必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可。滑行或者牵引时，应当遵守下列规定：  
　　（一）按照规定的或者空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员指定的路线滑行或者牵引。  
　　（二）滑行速度应当按照相应航空器的飞行手册或者飞行员驾驶守则执行；在障碍物附近滑行，速度不得超过每小时15公里。  
　　（三）航空器对头相遇，应当各自靠右侧滑行，并且保持必要的安全间隔；航空器交叉相遇，飞行员从座舱左侧看到另一架航空器时应当停止滑行，主动避让。  
　　（四）两架以上航空器跟进滑行，后航空器不得超越前航空器，后航空器与前航空器的距离，不得小于50米。  
　　（五）夜间滑行或者牵引，应当打开航空器上的航行灯。  
　　（六）直升机可以用1米至10米高度的飞行代替滑行。  
　　水上航空器在滑行或者牵引中，与船只对头或者交叉相遇，应当按照航空器滑行或者牵引时相遇的避让方法避让。

**Article 49** A pilot must obtain permission from the air traffic controller or flight commander before starting the engine and taxiing. When taxiing or being towed, the following provisions shall be complied with:  
(1) Taxi or be towed along the prescribed route or the route designated by the air traffic controller or flight commander.  
(2) The taxiing speed shall be according to the relevant aircraft flight manual or pilot's operating procedures; when taxiing near obstacles, the speed shall not exceed 15 kilometers per hour.  
(3) When aircraft meet head-on, each shall taxi to the right and maintain the necessary safety interval; when aircraft cross, if the pilot sees another aircraft from the left side of the cockpit, he shall stop taxiing and give way actively.  
(4) When two or more aircraft are taxiing in succession, the following aircraft shall not overtake the preceding aircraft, and the distance between the following aircraft and the preceding aircraft shall not be less than 50 meters.  
(5) When taxiing or being towed at night, the navigation lights of the aircraft shall be turned on.  
(6) Helicopters may replace taxiing by flying at an altitude of 1 to 10 meters.  
Water aircraft, when taxiing or being towed and meeting a vessel head-on or at a crossing, shall give way according to the avoidance methods for aircraft meeting while taxiing or being towed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　通常情况下，准备起飞的航空器，在起落航线第四转弯后无其他航空器进入着陆时，经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，方可滑进跑道；跑道上无障碍物，方准起飞。  
　　航空器起飞、着陆时，后航空器应当与前航空器保持规定的安全间隔。

**Article 50** Under normal circumstances, an aircraft ready for takeoff, after the fourth turn of the traffic pattern and when no other aircraft is entering for landing, may taxi onto the runway only with permission from the air traffic controller or flight commander; only when there are no obstacles on the runway may takeoff be permitted.  
During takeoff and landing of aircraft, the following aircraft shall maintain the prescribed safety interval from the preceding aircraft.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　机场的起落航线通常为左航线；若因地形、城市等条件的限制，或者为避免同邻近机场的起落航线交叉，也可以为右航线；起落航线的飞行高度，通常为300米至500米。  
　　进行起落航线飞行时，禁止超越同型航空器；各航空器之间的距离，一般应当保持在1500米以上；经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，速度大的航空器可以在第三转弯前超越速度小的航空器，超越时应当从前航空器的外侧超越，其间隔不得小于200米。除必须立即降落的航空器外，任何航空器不得从内侧超越前航空器。  
　　加入起落航线飞行必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，并且应当顺沿航线加入，不得横向截入。

**Article 51** The takeoff and landing pattern of an airport shall normally be a left pattern; however, if restricted by terrain, city, or other conditions, or to avoid crossing the pattern of a neighboring airport, it may also be a right pattern. The flight altitude in the takeoff and landing pattern shall normally be 300 meters to 500 meters.  
  
When conducting pattern flights, it is prohibited to overtake an aircraft of the same type; the distance between aircraft shall generally be maintained at not less than 1,500 meters; with the permission of the air traffic controller or flight commander, an aircraft with higher speed may overtake an aircraft with lower speed before the third turn, and when overtaking, it shall overtake from the outside of the preceding aircraft with an interval of not less than 200 meters. Except for aircraft that must land immediately, no aircraft may overtake the preceding aircraft from the inside.  
  
Joining the pattern flight must be permitted by the air traffic controller or flight commander, and the aircraft shall join along the pattern and shall not cut in laterally.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　航空器起飞后在机场区域内上升或者降落前在机场区域内下降，必须按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示进行。  
　　航空器飞离机场加入航路、航线和脱离航路、航线飞向机场，应当按照该机场使用细则或者进离场程序规定的航线和高度上升或者下降。

**Article 52** When ascending within the airport area after takeoff or descending within the airport area before landing, the aircraft must follow the instructions of the air traffic controller or flight commander.  
  
When departing from the airport to join an airway or route, or when leaving an airway or route to fly towards the airport, the aircraft shall ascend or descend in accordance with the routes and altitudes specified in the airport operating procedures or arrival and departure procedures.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　相邻机场的穿云上升航线、穿云下降航线互有交叉，飞行发生矛盾时，由负责该地区飞行管制的部门调整。

**Article 53** When the instrument climb routes and instrument descent routes of neighboring airports intersect, and conflicts arise during flight, the department responsible for flight control in that area shall make adjustments.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　航空器进行空域飞行时，应当按照规定的航线（航向）、高度、次序进入空域或者脱离空域，并且保持在规定的空域和高度范围内飞行。  
　　除等待空域外，一个飞行空域，在同一个时间内，只允许安排一至三批航空器飞行。各批航空器飞行活动的高度范围之间，通常应当保持2000米以上的高度差。

**Article 54** When conducting flight in an airspace, the aircraft shall enter or leave the airspace in accordance with the specified route (heading), altitude, and sequence, and shall remain within the specified airspace and altitude range.  
  
Except for holding airspace, within a single flight airspace, only one to three groups of aircraft may be permitted to operate at the same time. The altitude ranges of flight activities of different groups of aircraft shall normally maintain a height difference of not less than 2,000 meters.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　目视飞行时，飞行人员必须加强空中观察。航空器应当与云保持一定的水平距离和垂直距离。  
　　机长对目视飞行的安全负直接责任。

**Article 55** When conducting visual flight, flight personnel must enhance aerial observation. The aircraft shall maintain a certain horizontal and vertical distance from clouds.  
  
The pilot-in-command shall bear direct responsibility for the safety of visual flight.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　航空器进入着陆，应当经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可；不具备着陆条件的，不得勉强着陆。  
　　航空器着陆后，应当迅速脱离跑道。

**Article 56** An aircraft approaching for landing shall be subject to permission from the air traffic controller or flight commander; if the conditions for landing are not met, the aircraft shall not attempt to land forcibly.  
  
After landing, the aircraft shall quickly vacate the runway.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　飞行人员在复杂气象条件下按仪表飞行，必须同时具备下列条件：  
　　（一）飞行人员掌握复杂气象飞行技术；  
　　（二）航空器配备有完好的航行设备和无线电通信设备。

**Article 57** When flight personnel conduct instrument flight under complex meteorological conditions, the following conditions must be satisfied simultaneously:  
  
(1) The flight personnel have mastered the techniques for flying under complex meteorological conditions; and  
(2) The aircraft is equipped with serviceable navigation equipment and radio communication equipment.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　复杂气象条件下进入机场区域的飞行，必须经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可。空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员允许航空器飞入机场区域时，应当及时向飞行员通报下列情况：  
　　（一）进入的飞行高度；  
　　（二）机场区域内有关的飞行情况；  
　　（三）水平能见度或者跑道视程、天气现象和机场上空的云底高度，地面和穿云高度上的风向、风速，场面气压或者修正海平面气压，或者零点高度，以及地面大气温度；  
　　（四）仪表进场或者穿云方法和着陆航向。

**Article 58** Flights entering the airport area under complex meteorological conditions must be permitted by the air traffic controller or flight commander. When the air traffic controller or flight commander permits an aircraft to fly into the airport area, they shall promptly inform the pilot of the following information:  
  
(1) The entry flight altitude;  
(2) Relevant flight conditions within the airport area;  
(3) Horizontal visibility or runway visual range, weather phenomena and the cloud base height above the airport, wind direction and wind speed at ground level and at instrument altitude, QFE or QNH, or zero-point altitude, and ground air temperature; and  
(4) Instrument approach or instrument departure method and landing heading.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　航空器在等待空域内，必须保持在规定的等待高度层并且按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员指示的方法飞行，未经许可，不得自行改变。  
　　在等待空域内等待降落的航空器，应当按照规定的顺序降落。特殊情况下，经空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员许可，方可优先降落。

**Article 59** Within the holding airspace, the aircraft must maintain the specified holding altitude level and fly in accordance with the method instructed by the air traffic controller or flight commander, and shall not change it without permission.  
  
Aircraft holding within the holding airspace awaiting landing shall land in the specified order. Under special circumstances, only with the permission of the air traffic controller or flight commander may an aircraft land with priority.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　航空器穿云下降必须按照该机场的仪表进近图或者穿云图进行。当下降到规定的最低高度或者决断高度仍不能以目视进行着陆时，应当立即停止下降，并且按照规定的航向上升至安全高度。  
　　航空器因故不能在该机场降落的，空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员或者航空公司签派员及其代理人，应当立即通知备降机场准备接受航空器降落，同时指示航空器飞往备降机场的航向、飞行高度和通知备降机场的天气情况。在飞行人员同备降机场沟通无线电联络并且报告在备降机场着陆已有保障以前，空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员或者航空公司签派员及其代理人应当继续与该航空器保持联络。

**Article 60** The instrument descent of an aircraft must be conducted in accordance with the instrument approach chart or instrument descent chart of the airport. When the aircraft descends to the specified minimum altitude or decision altitude and still cannot make a visual landing, it shall immediately stop descending and ascend to the safe altitude in accordance with the specified heading.  
  
If the aircraft cannot land at that airport due to any reason, the air traffic controller, flight commander, or airline dispatcher and their agent shall immediately notify the alternate airport to prepare for the aircraft's landing, and at the same time instruct the heading and flight altitude for the aircraft to fly to the alternate airport and notify it of the weather conditions at the alternate airport. Before the flight personnel establish radio contact with the alternate airport and report that landing at the alternate airport is assured, the air traffic controller, flight commander, or airline dispatcher and their agent shall continue to maintain communication with the aircraft.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　航空器飞临降落机场时，机场的天气情况低于机长飞行的最低气象条件，且航空器无法飞往备降机场的，空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员应当采取一切措施，指挥航空器安全降落。

**Article 61** When an aircraft arrives at the landing airport and the weather conditions at the airport are below the minimum meteorological conditions for the pilot-in-command's flight, and the aircraft is unable to proceed to an alternate airport, the air traffic controller or flight commander shall take all measures to direct the aircraft to land safely.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　飞机在空中拖曳滑翔机时，拖曳飞机同滑翔机应当视为一个航空器。滑翔机飞行员应当服从拖曳飞机飞行员的指挥。  
　　滑翔机在空中脱离拖曳，必须在规定的高度上进行，并且经拖曳飞机飞行员同意，但紧急情况除外。

**Article 62** When an airplane tows a glider in the air, the towing airplane and the glider shall be considered as one aircraft. The glider pilot shall obey the command of the towing airplane pilot.  
  
The glider shall release from the tow in the air only at the specified altitude and with the consent of the towing airplane pilot, except in an emergency.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　机场区域内飞行的开始和结束的时间，其他任务飞行的航空器在该机场起飞和降落的时间，均应当及时报告上级飞行管制部门。  
　　相邻机场应当互相主动通报有关的飞行情况。

**Article 63** The start and end times of flights within the airport area, and the takeoff and landing times of aircraft engaged in other mission flights at that airport, shall all be promptly reported to the superior flight control department.  
  
Neighboring airports shall proactively notify each other of relevant flight situations.

### 第五章　航路和航线飞行

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　航空器使用航路和航线，应当经负责该航路和航线的飞行管制部门同意。

**Article 64** The use of an airway or route by an aircraft shall be subject to the consent of the flight control department responsible for that airway or route.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　航路和固定航线地带应当设置必要的监视和导航设备。  
　　沿航路和固定航线应当有备降机场。备降机场应当有必备的设备和良好的通信、导航、气象保障。  
　　军用机场作为民用航空器的固定备降机场或者民用机场作为军用航空器的固定备降机场，应当按照国家有关规定经过批准。

**Article 65** Airway and fixed route areas shall be equipped with necessary surveillance and navigation equipment.  
  
There shall be alternate airports along the airway and fixed route. Alternate airports shall have the necessary equipment and reliable communication, navigation, and meteorological support.  
  
If a military airport is used as a fixed alternate airport for civil aircraft, or a civil airport as a fixed alternate airport for military aircraft, approval shall be obtained in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　穿越航路和航线的飞行，应当明确穿越的地段、高度和时间，穿越时还应当保证与航路和航线飞行的航空器有规定的飞行间隔。

**Article 66** For flights crossing an airway or route, the crossing segment, altitude, and time shall be clearly specified, and when crossing, the prescribed flight separation from aircraft flying on the airway or route shall be ensured.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　飞行任务书是许可飞行人员进行转场飞行和民用航空飞行的基本文件。飞行任务书由驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人签发。  
　　在飞行任务书中，应当明确飞行任务、起飞时间、航线、高度、允许机长飞行的最低气象条件以及其他有关事项。

**Article 67** The flight assignment document is the basic document authorizing flight personnel to conduct cross-country flights and civil aviation flights. The flight assignment document shall be issued by the person in charge of the aviation unit stationed at the airport or the airline.  
  
The flight assignment document shall specify the flight mission, takeoff time, route, altitude, minimum meteorological conditions permitted for the pilot-in-command to fly, and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行前，驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人应当亲自或者指定专人对飞行人员的飞行准备情况进行检查。飞行准备质量符合要求时，方可执行飞行任务。

**Article 68** Before a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight, the person in charge of the aviation unit stationed at the airport or the airline shall, in person or designate a specialist, inspect the flight preparation of the flight personnel. Only when the quality of flight preparation meets the requirements may the flight mission be executed.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器的起飞，应当根据飞行人员和航空器的准备情况，起飞机场、降落机场和备降机场的准备情况以及天气情况等确定；有下列情况之一的，不得起飞：  
　　（一）空勤组成员不齐，或者由于技术、健康等原因不适于飞行的；  
　　（二）飞行人员尚未完成飞行准备、飞行准备质量不符合要求、驻机场航空单位或者航空公司的负责人未批准飞行的；  
　　（三）飞行人员未携带飞行任务书、飞行气象文件及其他必备飞行文件的；  
　　（四）飞行人员未校对本次飞行所需的航行、通信、导航资料和仪表进近图或者穿云图的；  
　　（五）航空器或者航空器上的设备有故障可能影响飞行安全，或者民用航空器设备低于最低设备清单规定，或者军用航空器经机长确认可能影响本次飞行安全的；  
　　（六）航空器表面的冰、霜、雪未除净的；  
　　（七）航空器上的装载和乘载不符合规定的；  
　　（八）航空器未按规定携带备用燃料的；  
　　（九）天气情况低于机长飞行的最低气象条件，以及天气情况危及本次飞行安全的。

**Article 69** The takeoff of an aircraft for a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight shall be determined based on the readiness of the flight personnel and the aircraft, the readiness of the departure airport, landing airport, and alternate airport, and the weather conditions; takeoff shall not be permitted under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The flight crew is incomplete, or any member is unfit for flight due to technical or health reasons;  
(2) The flight personnel have not completed flight preparation, the quality of flight preparation does not meet the requirements, or the person in charge of the aviation unit stationed at the airport or the airline has not approved the flight;  
(3) The flight personnel do not carry the flight assignment document, flight meteorological documents, or other necessary flight documents;  
(4) The flight personnel have not checked the navigation, communication, and navigation data and the instrument approach chart or instrument descent chart required for this flight;  
(5) The aircraft or its equipment has a malfunction that may affect flight safety, or civil aircraft equipment is below the minimum equipment list requirements, or for military aircraft, the pilot-in-command confirms that it may affect the safety of this flight;  
(6) Ice, frost, or snow on the aircraft surface has not been removed;  
(7) The loading and passenger loading of the aircraft do not comply with regulations;  
(8) The aircraft does not carry reserve fuel as required; or  
(9) The weather conditions are below the minimum meteorological conditions for the pilot-in-command's flight, or the weather conditions endanger the safety of this flight.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　飞行人员在飞行中必须遵守有关的飞行规则和飞行任务书中的各项规定，服从飞行指挥，准确实施领航，保持规定的航行诸元，注意观察空中情况，按照规定及时报告航空器位置、飞行情况和天气情况，特别是危险天气现象及其发展情况。

**Article 70** During flight, flight personnel must comply with the relevant flight rules and the provisions of the flight assignment document, obey flight command, accurately conduct navigation, maintain the specified navigational parameters, observe the aerial situation, and promptly report the aircraft's position, flight conditions, and weather conditions in accordance with regulations, especially hazardous weather phenomena and their development.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　目视飞行时，航空器应当按照下列规定避让：  
　　（一）在同一高度上对头相遇，应当各自向右避让，并保持500米以上的间隔；  
　　（二）在同一高度上交叉相遇，飞行员从座舱左侧看到另一架航空器时应当下降高度，从座舱右侧看到另一架航空器时应当上升高度；  
　　（三）在同一高度上超越前航空器，应当从前航空器右侧超越，并保持500米以上的间隔；  
　　（四）单机应当主动避让编队或者拖曳飞机，有动力装置的航空器应当主动避让无动力装置的航空器，战斗机应当主动避让运输机。

**Article 71** When conducting visual flight, aircraft shall yield as follows:  
(1) When meeting head-on at the same altitude, both aircraft shall turn right to avoid each other and maintain an interval of not less than 500 meters;  
(2) When crossing at the same altitude, if the pilot sees another aircraft on the left side of the cockpit, the pilot shall descend altitude; if seen on the right side, the pilot shall ascend altitude;  
(3) When overtaking a preceding aircraft at the same altitude, the aircraft shall overtake from the right side of the preceding aircraft and maintain an interval of not less than 500 meters; and  
(4) A single aircraft shall actively give way to a formation or towing aircraft, power-driven aircraft shall actively give way to non-power-driven aircraft, and fighter aircraft shall actively give way to transport aircraft.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　在与航路、固定航线交叉或者靠近的临时航线飞行时，飞行人员应当加强对空中的观察，防止与航路飞行的航空器相撞。当临时航线与航路、固定航线交叉时，水平能见度大于8公里的，应当按照规定的飞行高度通过；在云中飞行或者水平能见度小于8公里的，应当按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示通过。在靠近航路的航线上飞行时，应当与航路的边界保持规定的安全间隔。

**Article 72** When flying on a temporary route that crosses or is close to an airway or fixed route, flight personnel shall strengthen aerial observation to prevent collision with aircraft operating on the airway. When the temporary route crosses an airway or fixed route, if horizontal visibility is greater than 8 kilometers, the crossing shall be made at the specified flight altitude; when flying in clouds or when horizontal visibility is less than 8 kilometers, the crossing shall be made in accordance with the instructions of the air traffic controller or flight commander. When flying on a route close to an airway, the prescribed safety interval from the boundary of the airway shall be maintained.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　未配备复杂气象飞行设备的航空器，机长应当按照规定的飞行最低气象条件，在安全高度以上进行目视飞行，防止飞入云中。

**Article 73** For an aircraft not equipped with complex meteorological flight equipment, the pilot-in-command shall conduct visual flight at or above the safe altitude in accordance with the prescribed minimum meteorological conditions for flight, and shall avoid flying into clouds.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　当天气情况不低于机长飞行的最低气象条件时，机长方可在300米以下进行目视飞行，飞行时航空器距离云层底部不得小于50米。

**Article 74** Only when the weather conditions are not lower than the minimum meteorological conditions for the pilot-in-command's flight may the pilot-in-command conduct visual flight below 300 meters, and during such flight, the distance of the aircraft from the cloud base shall not be less than 50 meters.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　航空器沿航路和固定航线飞行通过中途机场100至50公里前，除有协议的外，飞行人员应当向该机场的空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员报告预计通过的时间和高度。中途机场的空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员必须指挥在本机场区域内飞行的航空器避让过往航空器，保证其安全通过；无特殊原因，不得改变过往航空器的航线和高度。  
　　航空器在临时航线飞行通过中途机场时，应当按照规定的航线和高度通过，或者按照该机场空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的指示通过。

**Article 75** When an aircraft flying along an airway or fixed route passes through a midway airport at a distance of 100 to 50 kilometers, the flight personnel shall report the expected time and altitude of passage to the air traffic controller or flight commander of that airport, unless otherwise agreed. The air traffic controller or flight commander of the midway airport must direct aircraft flying within the airport area to give way to the passing aircraft and ensure its safe passage; without special reasons, the route and altitude of the passing aircraft shall not be changed.  
  
When an aircraft flying on a temporary route passes through a midway airport, it shall pass in accordance with the specified route and altitude, or in accordance with the instructions of the air traffic controller or flight commander of that airport.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　飞行中，飞行人员与地面联络中断，可以停止执行飞行任务，返回原机场或者飞往就近的备降机场降落。当保持原高度飞向备降机场符合飞行高度层配备规定时，仍保持原高度飞行；当保持原高度飞向备降机场不符合飞行高度层配备规定时，应当下降到下一层高度飞向备降机场；因飞行安全高度所限不能下降到下一层高度的，应当上升至上一层高度飞向备降机场。

**Article 76** During flight, if contact with the ground is interrupted, the flight personnel may stop executing the flight mission and return to the original airport or proceed to the nearest alternate airport for landing. If maintaining the original altitude to fly to the alternate airport complies with the flight level allocation regulations, the aircraft shall continue flying at the original altitude; if maintaining the original altitude to fly to the alternate airport does not comply with the flight level allocation regulations, the aircraft shall descend to the next lower level to fly to the alternate airport; if descending to the next lower level is not possible due to flight safety altitude limitations, the aircraft shall ascend to the next higher level to fly to the alternate airport.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，在起飞前或者在中途机场降落后需要继续飞行的，机长或者其代理人必须到机场飞行管制部门办理飞行手续，校对有关资料，经批准后方可起飞；航空器降落后需要连续起飞的，必须事先经中途机场飞行管制部门的许可。  
　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器降落后，机长或者其代理人必须到机场飞行管制部门或者航空公司报告飞行情况和航路、航线天气情况，送交飞行任务书和飞行天气报告表。  
　　未经批准而降落在非预定机场的航空器，必须由驻该机场航空单位的负责人向上级报告，经批准后方可起飞。

**Article 77** Before takeoff or when needing to continue flying after landing at a midway airport, the pilot-in-command or their agent of an aircraft conducting a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight must go through flight procedures at the airport flight control department, verify relevant data, and may take off only after approval; if the aircraft needs to make a continuous takeoff after landing, prior permission from the midway airport's flight control department must be obtained.  
  
After landing, the pilot-in-command or their agent of an aircraft conducting a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight must report to the airport flight control department or the airline the flight conditions and the weather conditions on the airway or route, and submit the flight assignment document and the flight weather report form.  
  
An aircraft that lands at an unintended airport without approval must be reported by the person in charge of the aviation unit stationed at that airport to the higher authority, and may take off only after approval.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器到达预定机场后，其各项保障工作由驻该机场的有关部门按照规定或者协议负责。

**Article 78** After an aircraft conducting a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight arrives at the scheduled airport, the relevant departments stationed at that airport shall be responsible for all support work in accordance with regulations or agreements.

### 第六章　飞行间隔

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　飞行间隔是为了防止飞行冲突，保证飞行安全，提高飞行空间和时间利用率所规定的航空器之间应当保持的最小安全距离。飞行间隔包括垂直间隔和水平间隔。水平间隔分为纵向间隔和横向间隔。  
　　机长必须按照规定的飞行间隔飞行，需要改变时，应当经飞行管制部门许可。

**Article 79** Flight separation refers to the minimum safe distance that shall be maintained between aircraft, as prescribed to prevent flight conflicts, ensure flight safety, and improve the utilization of flight space and time. Flight separation includes vertical separation and horizontal separation. Horizontal separation is divided into longitudinal separation and lateral separation.  
  
The pilot-in-command must fly in accordance with the prescribed flight separation; if a change is needed, permission from the flight control department shall be obtained.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的垂直间隔，按照飞行高度层配备。飞行高度层按照以下标准划分：  
　　（一）真航线角在0度至179度范围内，高度由900米至8100米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度由8900米至12500米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在12500米以上，每隔1200米为一个高度层。  
　　（二）真航线角在180度至359度范围内，高度由600米至8400米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度由9200米至12200米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在13100米以上，每隔1200米为一个高度层。  
　　（三）飞行高度层应当根据标准大气压条件下假定海平面计算。真航线角应当从航线起点和转弯点量取。  
　　飞行高度层的具体配备标准见本规则附件二。

**Article 80** The vertical separation for flights on an airway, route, or cross-country flights shall be allocated according to flight levels. Flight levels shall be divided according to the following criteria:  
(1) For true track angles from 0 degrees to 179 degrees, altitudes from 900 meters to 8,100 meters, every 600 meters constitutes a flight level; altitudes from 8,900 meters to 12,500 meters, every 600 meters constitutes a flight level; altitudes above 12,500 meters, every 1,200 meters constitutes a flight level.  
(2) For true track angles from 180 degrees to 359 degrees, altitudes from 600 meters to 8,400 meters, every 600 meters constitutes a flight level; altitudes from 9,200 meters to 12,200 meters, every 600 meters constitutes a flight level; altitudes above 13,100 meters, every 1,200 meters constitutes a flight level.  
(3) Flight levels shall be calculated based on the assumed sea level under standard atmospheric pressure. The true track angle shall be measured from the starting point and turning point of the route.  
The specific allocation standards for flight levels are provided in Appendix 2 of these Rules.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的水平间隔，由中国人民解放军空军会同国务院民用航空主管部门拟定，报国务院、中央军事委员会空中交通管制委员会批准。

**Article 81** The horizontal separation for flights on an airway, route, or cross-country flights shall be formulated by the Air Force of the Chinese People's Liberation Army jointly with the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, and reported to the Air Traffic Control Commission of the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　飞行的安全高度是避免航空器与地面障碍物相撞的最低飞行高度。  
　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的安全高度，在高原和山区应当高出航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内最高标高600米；在其他地区应当高出航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内最高标高400米。  
　　受性能限制的航空器，其航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的安全高度，由有关航空管理部门另行规定。

**Article 82** The safe altitude for flight is the minimum flight altitude to avoid collision of the aircraft with ground obstacles.  
  
The safe altitude for flights on an airway, route, or cross-country flights shall, in plateau and mountainous areas, be 600 meters above the highest elevation within 25 kilometers on each side of the airway centerline or route; in other areas, it shall be 400 meters above the highest elevation within 25 kilometers on each side of the airway centerline or route.  
  
For aircraft with performance limitations, the safe altitude for flights on an airway, route, or cross-country flights shall be separately prescribed by the relevant aviation management department.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，在航路中心线、航线两侧各25公里以内的最高标高不超过100米，大气压力不低于1000百帕（750毫米水银柱）的，允许在600米的高度层内飞行；当最高标高超过100米，大气压力低于1000百帕（750毫米水银柱）的，飞行最低的高度层必须相应提高，保证飞行的真实高度不低于安全高度。

**Article 83** For aircraft conducting flights on an airway, route, or cross-country flights, if the highest elevation within 25 kilometers on each side of the airway centerline or route does not exceed 100 meters, and the atmospheric pressure is not lower than 1,000 hPa (750 mm Hg), flight within the 600-meter flight level is permitted; when the highest elevation exceeds 100 meters, or the atmospheric pressure is lower than 1,000 hPa (750 mm Hg), the minimum flight level must be raised accordingly to ensure that the true altitude of flight is not lower than the safe altitude.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的高度层，由批准本次飞行的负责人，通过飞行管制部门具体配备。  
　　飞行高度层应当根据飞行任务的性质、航空器性能、飞行区域以及航线的地形、天气和飞行情况等配备。

**Article 84** The flight level for a flight on an airway, route, or cross-country flight shall be specifically allocated by the person approving the flight through the flight control department.  
  
Flight levels shall be allocated based on the nature of the flight mission, the performance of the aircraft, the flight area, and the terrain, weather, and flight conditions of the route.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　在同一条航路、航线有数架（数批）航空器同时飞行并且互有影响的，应当分别将每架（每批）航空器配备在不同的高度层内；不能配备在不同高度层的，可以允许数架（数批）航空器在同一条航路、航线、同一个高度层内飞行，但是各架（各批）航空器之间应当保持规定的纵向间隔。

**Article 85** If several (several groups of) aircraft are flying simultaneously on the same airway or route and affect each other, each (each group of) aircraft shall be allocated to different flight levels; if allocation to different flight levels is not possible, several (several groups of) aircraft may be permitted to fly on the same airway or route at the same flight level, provided that the prescribed longitudinal separation is maintained between each (each group of) aircraft.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器起飞前，应当将场面气压的数值调整到航空器上气压高度表的固定指标，使气压高度表的指针指到零的位置。  
　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器起飞后，在未规定过渡高度或者过渡高的机场上升到距该机场道面600米高度时，应当将航空器上气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标，然后再继续上升到规定的飞行高度层；规定有过渡高度或者过渡高的机场，在上升至过渡高度或者过渡高时，应当将气压高度表调整到标准海平面气压值。  
　　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，进入降落机场区域并下降至该机场过渡高度层时，或者根据空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员的指示，将机场场面气压的数值调整到航空器上气压高度表的固定指标。  
　　仅供民用航空器起降的机场，可以修正海平面气压值为航空器气压高度表拨正值。  
　　提供外国航空器起降的机场，可以向外国航空器提供机场修正海平面气压值。  
　　军用、民用航空器在同一机场同时飞行的，必须统一航空器上气压高度表拨正时机。

**Article 86** Before takeoff of an aircraft conducting a flight on an airway, route, or cross-country flight, the QFE value shall be set to the fixed index of the aircraft's barometric altimeter so that the altimeter pointer indicates zero.  
  
After takeoff, when climbing to 600 meters above the airport surface at an airport where no transition altitude or transition height is specified, the aircraft shall set the standard sea level pressure value of the barometric altimeter to the fixed index, and then continue climbing to the specified flight level; at an airport where a transition altitude or transition height is specified, when climbing to the transition altitude or transition height, the barometric altimeter shall be adjusted to the standard sea level pressure value.  
  
When entering the landing airport area and descending to the transition level of that airport, or in accordance with the instructions of the air traffic controller or flight commander, the QFE value shall be set to the fixed index of the aircraft's barometric altimeter.  
  
For airports used only for civil aircraft takeoffs and landings, the QNH value may be used as the barometric altimeter setting for aircraft.  
  
For airports serving foreign aircraft, the airport may provide the QNH value to foreign aircraft.  
  
When military and civil aircraft operate simultaneously at the same airport, the timing of barometric altimeter settings on the aircraft must be unified.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　在高原机场起飞前，航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压数值的，应当将气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标（此时所指示的高度为假定零点高度），然后起飞和上升到规定的飞行高度。  
　　在高原机场降落时，航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压数值的，应当按照空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员通知的假定零点高度进行着陆。航空器上有两个气压高度表的，应当将其中一个气压高度表的标准海平面气压值调整到固定指标，而将另一个气压高度表以修正的海平面气压值调整到固定指标。  
　　在高原、山区飞行，必须注意航空器上气压高度表与无线电高度表配合使用。

**Article 87** Before takeoff at a plateau airport, if the barometric scale of the aircraft's barometric altimeter cannot be adjusted to the airport's QFE value, the standard sea level pressure value of the barometric altimeter shall be set to the fixed index (the altitude indicated at this time is the assumed zero-point altitude), and then takeoff and climb to the specified flight altitude shall be carried out.  
  
When landing at a plateau airport, if the barometric scale of the aircraft's barometric altimeter cannot be adjusted to the airport's QFE value, landing shall be conducted in accordance with the assumed zero-point altitude notified by the air traffic controller or flight commander. If the aircraft has two barometric altimeters, one shall have its standard sea level pressure value set to the fixed index, and the other shall have its corrected sea level pressure value set to the fixed index.  
  
When flying in plateau and mountainous areas, attention must be paid to the coordinated use of the aircraft's barometric altimeter and radio altimeter.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行时，因航空器故障、积冰、绕飞雷雨区等原因需要改变飞行高度层的，机长应当向飞行管制部门报告原因和当时航空器的准确位置，请求另行配备飞行高度层。飞行管制部门允许航空器改变飞行高度层时，必须明确改变的高度层以及改变高度层的地段和时间。  
　　遇有紧急情况，飞行安全受到威胁时，机长可以决定改变原配备的飞行高度层，但必须立即报告飞行管制部门，并对该决定负责。改变高度层的方法是：从航空器飞行的方向向右转30度，并以此航向飞行20公里，再左转平行原航线上升或者下降到新的高度层，然后转回原航线。

**Article 88** When conducting a flight on an airway, route, or a cross-country flight, if a change of flight level is necessary due to reasons such as aircraft malfunction, icing, or avoiding thunderstorms, the pilot-in-command shall report the reason and the exact current position of the aircraft to the flight control department and request a different flight level allocation. When the flight control department permits the aircraft to change its flight level, it must specify the new level and the segment and time for the change.  
  
In an emergency where flight safety is threatened, the pilot-in-command may decide to change the originally allocated flight level, but must immediately report to the flight control department and assume responsibility for that decision. The method for changing the flight level is: turn right 30 degrees from the aircraft's direction of flight, fly for 20 kilometers on that heading, then turn left to parallel the original route and ascend or descend to the new flight level, then return to the original route.

### 第七章　飞行指挥

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　组织实施飞行指挥应当根据本规则和有关规定进行，做到正确、及时和不间断。

**Article 89** The organization and implementation of flight command shall be carried out in accordance with these Rules and relevant regulations, and shall be correct, timely, and continuous.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　飞行指挥员必须切实履行职责，维护机场、空中秩序和飞行纪律，并做到：  
　　（一）了解飞行任务、飞行计划、飞行人员的技术水平及健康状况、航空器性能和机载设备，以及各项保障工作情况；  
　　（二）掌握飞行动态，了解天气变化，及时向飞行人员通知有关的空中情况和指挥其准确地按照计划飞行；  
　　（三）当空中情况发生变化时，及时采取措施，正确处置。

**Article 90** The flight commander must earnestly perform their duties, maintain order at the airport, in the air, and flight discipline, and shall:  
(1) Be aware of the flight mission, flight plan, the technical level and health status of flight personnel, the performance of the aircraft and onboard equipment, and the status of all support operations;  
(2) Keep track of flight dynamics, be aware of weather changes, promptly notify flight personnel of relevant air situations and direct them to fly accurately in accordance with the plan; and  
(3) Take timely measures and handle correctly when the air situation changes.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　飞行指挥必须按照下列调配原则安排飞行次序：  
　　（一）一切飞行让战斗飞行；  
　　（二）其他飞行让专机飞行和重要任务飞行；  
　　（三）国内一般任务飞行让班期飞行；  
　　（四）训练飞行让任务飞行；  
　　（五）场内飞行让场外飞行；  
　　（六）场内、场外飞行让转场飞行。

**Article 91** Flight command shall arrange the order of flights in accordance with the following allocation principles:  
(1) All flights shall yield to combat flights;  
(2) Other flights shall yield to special aircraft flights and flights for important missions;  
(3) General domestic mission flights shall yield to scheduled flights;  
(4) Training flights shall yield to mission flights;  
(5) In-field flights shall yield to out-of-field flights; and  
(6) In-field and out-of-field flights shall yield to cross-country flights.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　在飞行期间，所有参加飞行和保障飞行的人员，必须服从飞行指挥员的指挥。

**Article 92** During flight operations, all personnel participating in the flight and flight support must obey the command of the flight commander.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　驻在同一机场的军用航空器和民用航空器同时飞行时，必须实施统一指挥。军用航空单位派出飞行指挥员，民用航空单位派出飞行副指挥员。  
　　飞行副指挥员负责向飞行指挥员报告民用航空器的航行诸元和有关飞行情况，并且按照飞行指挥员的指示，对民用航空器实施指挥。

**Article 93** When military aircraft and civil aircraft stationed at the same airport operate simultaneously, unified command must be implemented. The military aviation unit shall dispatch a flight commander, and the civil aviation unit shall dispatch a deputy flight commander.  
  
The deputy flight commander shall be responsible for reporting the navigational parameters and relevant flight conditions of civil aircraft to the flight commander, and shall exercise command over civil aircraft in accordance with the instructions of the flight commander.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　执行不同任务的航空器或者不同型别的航空器，在同一机场同时飞行的，应当根据具体情况安排优先起飞和降落的顺序。  
　　对执行紧急或者重要任务的航空器，班期飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，速度大的航空器，应当允许优先起飞；对有故障的航空器，剩余油量少的航空器，执行紧急或者重要任务的航空器，班期飞行和航路、航线飞行或者转场飞行的航空器，应当允许优先降落。

**Article 94** When aircraft performing different missions or of different types operate simultaneously at the same airport, the priority order for takeoff and landing shall be arranged according to specific circumstances.  
  
Aircraft on emergency or important missions, scheduled or cross-country flights, and aircraft with higher speed shall be given priority for takeoff; aircraft with malfunctions, low fuel reserves, on emergency or important missions, scheduled flights, flights on airways or routes, or cross-country flights shall be given priority for landing.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　飞行指挥用无线电实施。指挥用语应当简短、明确、易懂、规范。  
　　未配备无线电通信设备的航空器，无线电受干扰或者无线电通信设备发生故障的航空器，按照本规则附件一的规定实施指挥。

**Article 95** Flight command shall be conducted by radio. Command language shall be brief, clear, understandable, and standard.  
  
For aircraft not equipped with radio communication equipment, or whose radio is subject to interference or whose radio communication equipment is malfunctioning, command shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Appendix 1 of these Rules.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　现用机场应当设飞行管制室、起飞线塔台（指挥塔台）或者机场管制塔台，其位置应当有良好的视界，可观察到机场、净空地带以及航空器飞行和航空器在机场上的活动。  
　　机场飞行管制室、起飞线塔台（指挥塔台）或者机场管制塔台，应当配备指挥和保障飞行的通信设备、雷达显示设备或者雷达标图以及其他有关设备和必要的文件图表等。

**Article 96** Operational airports shall have a flight control office, a takeoff line tower (control tower), or an airport control tower, which shall be positioned with a good view to observe the airport, clearance areas, and aircraft flight and aircraft activity on the airport.  
  
The airport flight control office, takeoff line tower (control tower), or airport control tower shall be equipped with communication equipment for command and flight support, radar display equipment or radar plotting, and other relevant equipment and necessary documents and charts.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　作战飞行的指挥，按照中国人民解放军有关规定执行。

**Article 97** The command of combat flights shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

### 第八章　飞行中特殊情况的处置

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　飞行中的特殊情况，是指突然发生的危及飞行安全的情况。  
　　对飞行中特殊情况的处置，应当根据情况的性质、飞行条件和可供进行处置的时间来确定。飞行中各种特殊情况的处置办法，由各航空管理部门规定。

**Article 98** In-flight emergencies refer to situations that suddenly occur and endanger flight safety.  
  
The handling of in-flight emergencies shall be determined based on the nature of the situation, flight conditions, and the time available for intervention. The procedures for handling various in-flight emergencies shall be prescribed by each aviation management department.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　飞行人员、空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员和各类保障飞行的人员，对飞行中特殊情况的处置必须预有准备。飞行人员应当及时察觉飞行中出现特殊情况的各种征兆，熟练掌握在各种特殊情况下的操作程序和紧急处置方法；空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员，应当熟知在不同的飞行条件下特殊情况的指挥措施和组织援救遇险航空器的方法；各类保障飞行的人员在任何情况下都应当恪尽职守，使各种保障设施经常处于良好状态，随时能为飞行人员、空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员正确处置特殊情况提供有利条件。

**Article 99** Flight personnel, air traffic controllers, flight commanders, and all flight support personnel must be prepared in advance for handling in-flight emergencies. Flight personnel shall promptly detect various signs of an emergency during flight, and skillfully master the operational procedures and emergency handling methods under various emergency conditions; air traffic controllers or flight commanders shall be thoroughly familiar with command measures under different flight conditions and methods for organizing rescue of aircraft in distress; all flight support personnel shall, under all circumstances, diligently perform their duties, keep all support facilities in good condition at all times, and be ready to provide favorable conditions for flight personnel, air traffic controllers, and flight commanders to correctly handle emergencies.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　飞行中发生特殊情况，机长必须在保证航空器上人员生命安全的前提下，积极采取措施保全航空器。时间允许的，机长应当及时向空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员报告所发生的情况和准备采取的措施，并且按照其指示行动。  
　　空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员应当根据空中具体情况，及时采取正确措施指挥航空器。

**Article 100** When an emergency occurs during flight, the pilot-in-command must, on the premise of ensuring the safety of persons on board the aircraft, actively take measures to preserve the aircraft. If time permits, the pilot-in-command shall promptly report the situation and the measures to be taken to the air traffic controller or flight commander, and act in accordance with their instructions.  
  
The air traffic controller or flight commander shall, based on the specific air situation, promptly take correct measures to direct the aircraft.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　在飞行中遇到严重危及航空器和人员安全的情况时，飞行人员应当利用一切手段，重复发出规定的遇险信号。其他航空器飞行人员在飞行中收到遇险信号，应当暂时停止使用无线电发信，必要时协助遇险航空器重复发出遇险信号。  
　　空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员在收到航空器发出的遇险信号后，应当迅速查明遇险航空器的位置和险情性质，立即采取措施，并报告上级。

**Article 101** When encountering a situation that seriously endangers the safety of the aircraft and persons on board during flight, flight personnel shall use all means to repeatedly transmit the prescribed distress signal. Other aircraft flight personnel who receive a distress signal during flight shall temporarily cease using radio transmission and, when necessary, assist the aircraft in distress to repeatedly transmit the distress signal.  
After receiving a distress signal transmitted by an aircraft, an air traffic controller or flight commander shall promptly ascertain the position of the aircraft in distress and the nature of the danger, immediately take measures, and report to the superior.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　军用航空器遇险时，有关部门应当及时报告当地政府和驻军。当地政府和驻军应当立即组织搜寻援救。在海上搜寻援救遇险航空器时，还应当报告国家海上搜寻援救组织和附近的海上搜寻援救组织，国家海上搜寻援救组织和附近的海上搜寻援救组织应当迅速进行搜寻和援救。  
　　民用航空器遇险时，搜寻援救活动的组织与实施按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 102** When a military aircraft is in distress, the relevant departments shall promptly report to the local government and the stationed troops. The local government and the stationed troops shall immediately organize search and rescue. When conducting search and rescue for an aircraft in distress at sea, they shall also report to the national maritime search and rescue organization and the nearby maritime search and rescue organizations, and the national maritime search and rescue organization and the nearby maritime search and rescue organizations shall promptly carry out search and rescue.  
When a civil aircraft is in distress, the organization and implementation of search and rescue activities shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　航空器在中华人民共和国境外遇险时，应当使用国际通用的遇险信号和频率。在海上飞行遇险时，设备允许的，还应当使用500千赫频率发出遇险信号。

**Article 103** When an aircraft is in distress outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall use internationally recognized distress signals and frequencies. When in distress during flight over the sea, if the equipment permits, it shall also transmit a distress signal using the frequency of 500 kilohertz.

### 第九章　通信、导航、雷达、气象和航行情报保障

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　通信、导航、雷达、气象和航行情报保障部门应当明确任务，认真履行职责，密切协同，周密组织与实施飞行保障工作。

**Article 104** The departments responsible for communications, navigation, radar, meteorology, and aeronautical information services shall define their tasks, earnestly perform their duties, closely coordinate, and meticulously organize and implement flight support work.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　各种通信、导航设备必须经常处于良好状态，主要设备应当配有备份，保证通信、导航的可靠性和稳定性。  
　　有关部门应当加强对航空通信、导航无线电频率的管理和保护。任何单位或者个人使用的无线电台和其他仪器、装置，不得妨碍航空无线电专用频率的正常使用。  
　　航路、航线地空通信、导航设备的增设、撤除或者变更，应当经中国人民解放军空军或者国务院民用航空主管部门同意。其中中波导航台和军用、民用航空共用的地空通信、导航设备的撤除还须经使用各方协商同意。

**Article 105** All types of communications and navigation equipment must be maintained in a good condition at all times, and main equipment shall be provided with backups to ensure the reliability and stability of communications and navigation.  
The relevant departments shall strengthen the management and protection of aeronautical communications and navigation radio frequencies. Radio stations and other instruments and devices used by any unit or individual shall not interfere with the normal use of aeronautical radio exclusive frequencies.  
The addition, removal, or modification of air-ground communications and navigation equipment on air routes and airways shall be subject to the consent of the Air Force of the People's Liberation Army or the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council. The removal of medium wave beacons and air-ground communications and navigation equipment shared by military and civil aviation shall also require the agreement of all parties using them after consultation.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　飞行实施过程中，飞行人员、空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员应当按照通信、导航保障规定，正确使用通信、导航设备。

**Article 106** During the execution of flight, flight personnel, air traffic controllers, and flight commanders shall use communications and navigation equipment correctly in accordance with the provisions on communications and navigation support.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　雷达保障部门应当对中华人民共和国境内的所有飞行提供保障。  
　　雷达设备应当经常处于良好状态，保证雷达工作的可靠性和稳定性。  
　　雷达保障工作，应当按照管制区域或者雷达责任区组织实施。

**Article 107** The radar support department shall provide support for all flights within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Radar equipment shall be maintained in a good condition at all times to ensure the reliability and stability of radar operations.  
Radar support work shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the control area or radar responsibility area.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　雷达保障应当做到：  
　　（一）及时、准确、连续地测定和通报空中航空器的位置；  
　　（二）严密监视航空器按照预定的航路、航线、飞行空域和高度飞行，及时发现和报知航空器偏离航路、航线、改变飞行高度和超出飞行空域的情况；  
　　（三）当获知空中有迷航、遇险的航空器时，应当组织有关雷达重点观察，迅速判明迷航、遇险的航空器及有关情况；  
　　（四）当飞行区域天气不稳定时，应当根据空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员的要求，及时组织雷达探测天气。

**Article 108** Radar support shall achieve the following:  
(1) Timely, accurate, and continuous determination and notification of the position of aircraft in the air;  
(2) Close monitoring of aircraft flying in accordance with the predetermined air route, airway, flight airspace, and altitude, and timely detection and reporting of any deviation from the air route or airway, change of flight altitude, or exceeding of flight airspace;  
(3) When learning of an aircraft that is lost or in distress in the air, organizing relevant radar for focused observation, and promptly determining the identity of the lost or distressed aircraft and related circumstances;  
(4) When the weather in the flight area is unstable, organizing radar for weather detection in a timely manner at the request of an air traffic controller or flight commander.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　飞行的气象保障工作由航空气象保障部门负责。  
　　航空气象保障部门必须严密组织气象保障，及时、准确地提供天气预报、天气实况，及时发布重要气象情报、危险天气警报和通报；必要时可以提出派遣航空器侦察天气和利用探测设备探测天气的建议。  
　　有关单位应当优先传递重要气象情报、危险天气警报和通报。  
　　机场的气象台，应当根据空中交通管制员或者飞行指挥员下达的任务，对在本机场起飞、降落的航空器，实施气象保障；兼负飞行管制分区（区域）飞行管制任务部门的机场气象台，还应当负责本区内转场飞行的气象保障。  
　　国家和各省、自治区、直辖市气象部门应当根据航空单位的申请，提供必要的气象情报。

**Article 109** The meteorological support for flight shall be the responsibility of the aeronautical meteorological support department.  
The aeronautical meteorological support department must strictly organize meteorological support, provide weather forecasts and actual weather conditions timely and accurately, and issue important meteorological information, hazardous weather warnings, and bulletins in a timely manner; when necessary, it may propose dispatching aircraft for weather reconnaissance and using detection equipment for weather detection.  
The relevant units shall give priority to the transmission of important meteorological information, hazardous weather warnings, and bulletins.  
The meteorological station at an airport shall provide meteorological support for aircraft taking off and landing at that airport according to the tasks assigned by the air traffic controller or flight commander; the meteorological station at an airport that also undertakes flight control tasks in a flight control sub-area (area) shall also be responsible for meteorological support for ferry flights within that area.  
The national and provincial, autonomous region, and municipal meteorological departments shall provide necessary meteorological information upon application by aviation units.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　飞行气象保障的组织与实施，应当按照各航空单位的有关规定执行。  
　　飞行保障任务涉及两个以上无隶属关系的气象部门时，应当按照有关协同规定组织实施。

**Article 110** The organization and implementation of flight meteorological support shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of each aviation unit.  
When a flight support task involves two or more meteorological departments that are not subordinate to each other, it shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the relevant coordination provisions.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　航行情报部门，应当提供保证飞行安全、正常和效率所需要的各种航行情报资料。  
　　有关单位应当主动配合，密切协作，及时提供航行情报，保证航行资料及时、准确和完整。

**Article 111** The aeronautical information service department shall provide all kinds of aeronautical information data necessary to ensure flight safety, normality, and efficiency.  
The relevant units shall actively cooperate and closely coordinate to provide aeronautical information in a timely manner, ensuring the timeliness, accuracy, and completeness of aeronautical data.

### 第十章　对外国航空器的特别规定

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　外国航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国领空，或者在中华人民共和国境内飞行、停留，必须按照中华人民共和国的有关规定获得批准。

**Article 112** A foreign aircraft that flies into or out of the territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China, or flies or stays within the territory of the People's Republic of China, must obtain approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　外国航空器在中华人民共和国境内的航路、航线飞行时，由中华人民共和国国务院民用航空主管部门负责提供空中交通管制服务。

**Article 113** When a foreign aircraft flies on air routes or airways within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council of the People's Republic of China shall be responsible for providing air traffic control services.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　外国航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国领空，必须按照规定的航路飞入或者飞出。飞入或者飞出领空前20至15分钟，其机组必须向中华人民共和国有关空中交通管制部门报告，并取得飞入或者飞出领空的许可；未经许可，不得飞入或者飞出。

**Article 114** A foreign aircraft flying into or out of the territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China must enter or exit via the prescribed air routes. Twenty to fifteen minutes before entering or leaving the territorial airspace, its crew must report to the relevant air traffic control department of the People's Republic of China and obtain permission for entering or leaving the territorial airspace; without permission, it shall not enter or leave.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　未经批准擅自飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国领空的外国民用航空器，中华人民共和国有关机关有权采取必要措施，令其在指定的机场降落。  
　　在中华人民共和国境内飞行、停留的外国民用航空器违反本规则规定的，由中华人民共和国有关空中交通管制部门采取措施，令其纠正。情节严重的，有关部门可以采取必要措施，直至迫使其在指定机场降落。

**Article 115** If a foreign civil aircraft flies into or out of the territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China without authorization and without approval, the relevant authorities of the People's Republic of China shall have the right to take necessary measures to order it to land at a designated airport.  
If a foreign civil aircraft flying or staying within the territory of the People's Republic of China violates the provisions of these Rules, the relevant air traffic control department of the People's Republic of China shall take measures to order it to correct the violation. If the circumstances are serious, the relevant department may take necessary measures, up to forcing it to land at a designated airport.

### 第十一章　法律责任

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　违反本规则规定，《中华人民共和国民用航空法》及有关法规对其处罚有明确规定的，从其规定；无明确规定的，适用本章规定。

**Article 116** If a violation of the provisions of these Rules is clearly subject to penalties under the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant regulations, those provisions shall apply; if there is no clear provision, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　未按本规则规定履行审批、备案或者其他手续的，由有关部门按照职责分工责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 117** Where a party fails to go through the procedures of approval, recordation, or other formalities as required by these Rules, the relevant department shall, according to the division of responsibilities, order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　飞行人员未按本规则规定履行职责的，由有关部门依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；情节严重的，依法给予吊扣执照一个月至六个月的处罚，或者责令停飞一个月至三个月；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 118** Where flight personnel fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of these Rules, the relevant department shall, according to law, impose administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions; if the circumstances are serious, a penalty of suspending or revoking their license for one to six months shall be imposed according to law, or they shall be ordered to cease flying for one to three months; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　空中交通管制员、飞行指挥员未按本规则规定履行职责的，由有关部门视情节给予批评教育、警告、记过、降职或者取消资格，免除职务的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 119** Where an air traffic controller or flight commander fails to perform his duties in accordance with the provisions of these Rules, the relevant department shall, depending on the circumstances, impose punishments such as criticism and education, warning, demerit, demotion, or disqualification and removal from office; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　飞行保障部门及其人员未按本规则规定履行职责的，由有关航空管理部门视情节给予通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员或者其他责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 120** Where a flight support department or its personnel fails to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of these Rules, the relevant aviation management department shall, depending on the circumstances, give a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge or other liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　中华人民共和国航空器在本国领海以外毗连区、专属经济区和公海上空飞行，中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本规则有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 121** When an aircraft of the People's Republic of China flies over the contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone, and high seas beyond the territorial sea, and the provisions of an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China differ from those of these Rules, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply; however, the provisions on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations shall be excepted.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　拦截违反本规则的航空器所使用的信号和被拦截的航空器回答的信号，按照本规则附件三的规定执行。

**Article 122** The signals used for intercepting an aircraft that violates these Rules and the signals to be answered by the intercepted aircraft shall be performed in accordance with the provisions of Appendix 3 to these Rules.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　本规则下列用语的含义：  
　　航空单位，是指拥有航空器并从事航空飞行活动的机关或者单位，包括航空运输公司、飞行俱乐部、飞行部队、飞行院校等。  
　　航空管理部门，是指对从事飞行活动的航空单位具有管理职能的机关或者单位，包括中国民用航空总局、国家体育总局、航空工业集团公司，中国人民解放军海军、空军、总参谋部陆航局等。  
　　过渡高度，是指一个特定的修正海平面气压高度。在此高度及其以下，航空器的垂直位置按修正海平面气压高度表示。  
　　过渡高，是指一个特定的场面气压高。在此高及其以下，航空器的垂直位置按场面气压高表示。  
　　过渡高度层，是指在过渡高度之上的最低可用飞行高度层。  
　　终端管制区，是指设在一个或者几个主要机场附近的空中交通服务航路汇合处的管制区。

**Article 123** For the purposes of these Rules, the following terms shall have the meanings specified:  
"Aviation unit" means an organ or unit that owns aircraft and engages in aviation flight activities, including air transport companies, flight clubs, flight units, flight academies, etc.  
"Aviation management department" means an organ or unit that has the function of managing aviation units engaged in flight activities, including the Civil Aviation Administration of China, the General Administration of Sport of China, the Aviation Industry Corporation of China, the Navy and Air Force of the People's Liberation Army, the Army Aviation Bureau of the General Staff, etc.  
"Transition altitude" means a specific altitude related to the corrected mean sea level pressure. At and below this altitude, the vertical position of an aircraft is expressed in terms of the corrected mean sea level pressure altitude.  
"Transition height" means a specific height related to the aerodrome pressure (QFE). At and below this height, the vertical position of an aircraft is expressed in terms of the aerodrome pressure height.  
"Transition level" means the lowest usable flight level above the transition altitude.  
"Terminal control area" means a control area established at the convergence of air traffic service routes near one or more major airports.
