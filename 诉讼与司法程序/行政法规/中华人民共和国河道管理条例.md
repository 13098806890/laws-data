### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强河道管理，保障防洪安全，发挥江河湖泊的综合效益，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the "Water Law of the People's Republic of China" for the purposes of strengthening river management, ensuring flood control safety, and giving full play to the comprehensive benefits of rivers and lakes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国领域内的河道(包括湖泊、人工水道、行洪区、蓄洪区、滞洪区)。  
河道内的航道，同时适用《中华人民共和国航道管理条例》。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to rivers (including lakes, artificial waterways, flood passage areas, flood storage areas, and detention basins) within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Waterways within rivers shall also be governed by the "Regulations on the Administration of Waterways of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　开发利用江河湖泊水资源和防治水害，应当全面规划、统筹兼顾、综合利用、讲求效益，服从防洪的总体安排，促进各项事业的发展。

**Article 3** The development and utilization of water resources of rivers and lakes and the prevention and control of flood disasters shall be subject to comprehensive planning, overall consideration, comprehensive utilization, emphasis on benefits, compliance with the overall arrangements for flood control, and promotion of the development of various undertakings.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院水利行政主管部门是全国河道的主管机关。  
各省、自治区、直辖市的水利行政主管部门是该行政区域的河道主管机关。

**Article 4** The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall be the competent authority for rivers nationwide.  
The competent water administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be the competent authorities for rivers in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对河道实行按水系统一管理和分级管理相结合的原则。  
长江、黄河、淮河、海河、珠江、松花江、辽河等大江大河的主要河段，跨省、自治区、直辖市的重要河段，省、自治区、直辖市之间的边界河道以及国境边界河道，由国家授权的江河流域管理机构实施管理，或者由上述江河所在省、自治区、直辖市的河道主管机关根据流域统一规划实施管理。其他河道由省、自治区、直辖市或者市、县的河道主管机关实施管理。

**Article 5** The State shall apply the principle of combining unified management by river system with management at different levels for rivers.  
The major reaches of large rivers such as the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Huaihe River, Haihe River, Pearl River, Songhua River, and Liaohe River, the important reaches spanning provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, boundary rivers between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and boundary rivers along national borders shall be managed by river basin management agencies authorized by the State, or by the competent river authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government where the aforesaid rivers are located, in accordance with the unified basin plan. Other rivers shall be managed by the competent river authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, or cities and counties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　河道划分等级。河道等级标准由国务院水利行政主管部门制定。

**Article 6** Rivers shall be classified into grades. The standards for river grades shall be formulated by the competent water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　河道防汛和清障工作实行地方人民政府行政首长负责制。

**Article 7** The responsibility system under the administrative head of the local people's government shall be implemented for river flood prevention and obstacle removal work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府河道主管机关以及河道监理人员，必须按照国家法律、法规，加强河道管理，执行供水计划和防洪调度命令，维护水工程和人民生命财产安全。

**Article 8** The competent river authorities of people's governments at all levels and river supervision personnel must, in accordance with national laws and regulations, strengthen river management, implement water supply plans and flood control dispatch orders, and protect water projects and the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　一切单位和个人都有保护河道堤防安全和参加防汛抢险的义务。

**Article 9** All entities and individuals shall have the obligation to protect the safety of river dikes and to participate in flood prevention and emergency rescue.

### 第二章　河道整治与建设

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　河道的整治与建设，应当服从流域综合规划，符合国家规定的防洪标准、通航标准和其他有关技术要求，维护堤防安全，保持河势稳定和行洪、航运通畅。

**Article 10** The regulation and construction of rivers shall be subject to the comprehensive basin plan, comply with the national standards for flood control, navigation, and other relevant technical requirements, maintain the safety of dikes, preserve river regime stability, and ensure smooth flood passage and navigation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　修建开发水利、防治水害、整治河道的各类工程和跨河、穿河、穿堤、临河的桥梁、码头、道路、渡口、管道、缆线等建筑物及设施，建设单位必须按照河道管理权限，将工程建设方案报送河道主管机关审查同意。未经河道主管机关审查同意的，建设单位不得开工建设。  
建设项目经批准后，建设单位应当将施工安排告知河道主管机关。

**Article 11** For the construction of various projects for water development, flood disaster prevention and control, and river regulation, as well as buildings and facilities such as bridges, wharves, roads, ferries, pipelines, and cables that cross, penetrate, or adjoin rivers or dikes, the construction entity must, in accordance with the river management authority, submit the project construction plan to the competent river authority for review and approval. Without review and approval by the competent river authority, the construction entity shall not commence construction.  
After the construction project is approved, the construction entity shall notify the competent river authority of the construction arrangements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　修建桥梁、码头和其他设施，必须按照国家规定的防洪标准所确定的河宽进行，不得缩窄行洪通道。  
桥梁和栈桥的梁底必须高于设计洪水位，并按照防洪和航运的要求，留有一定的超高。设计洪水位由河道主管机关根据防洪规划确定。  
跨越河道的管道、线路的净空高度必须符合防洪和航运的要求。

**Article 12** The construction of bridges, wharves, and other facilities must be carried out in accordance with the river width determined by the national flood control standards, and shall not narrow the flood passage channel.  
The soffit of bridges and trestles must be higher than the design flood level and shall retain a certain clearance height in accordance with flood control and navigation requirements. The design flood level shall be determined by the competent river authority according to the flood control plan.  
The clearance height of pipelines and lines crossing rivers must comply with flood control and navigation requirements.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　交通部门进行航道整治，应当符合防洪安全要求，并事先征求河道主管机关对有关设计和计划的意见。  
水利部门进行河道整治，涉及航道的，应当兼顾航运的需要，并事先征求交通部门对有关设计和计划的意见。  
在国家规定可以流放竹木的河流和重要的渔业水域进行河道、航道整治，建设单位应当兼顾竹木水运和渔业发展的需要，并事先将有关设计和计划送同级林业、渔业主管部门征求意见。

**Article 13** When the transportation department conducts waterway regulation, it shall comply with flood control safety requirements and shall seek in advance the opinions of the competent river authority on the relevant designs and plans.  
When the water conservancy department conducts river regulation involving waterways, it shall take into account the needs of navigation and shall seek in advance the opinions of the transportation department on the relevant designs and plans.  
When river or waterway regulation is carried out in rivers where bamboo and timber may be floated as prescribed by the State and in important fishing waters, the construction entity shall take into account the needs of bamboo and timber water transport and fishery development, and shall submit the relevant designs and plans to the competent forestry and fishery departments at the same level for comments in advance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　堤防上已修建的涵闸、泵站和埋设的穿堤管道、缆线等建筑物及设施，河道主管机关应当定期检查，对不符合工程安全要求的，限期改建。  
在堤防上新建前款所指建筑物及设施，应当服从河道主管机关的安全管理。

**Article 14** The competent river authority shall conduct regular inspections of the culvert gates, pumping stations, and buried through-dike pipelines, cables, and other buildings and facilities that have been built on dikes, and shall order the reconstruction within a time limit of those that do not meet the engineering safety requirements.  
The construction of new buildings and facilities referred to in the preceding paragraph on dikes shall be subject to the safety management of the competent river authority.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　确需利用堤顶或者戗台兼做公路的，须经县级以上地方人民政府河道主管机关批准。堤身和堤顶公路的管理和维护办法，由河道主管机关商交通部门制定。

**Article 15** Where it is truly necessary to use the dike crest or berm concurrently as a road, approval must be obtained from the competent river authority of the local people's government at or above the county level. The measures for the management and maintenance of the dike body and the road on the dike crest shall be formulated by the competent river authority in consultation with the transportation department.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城镇建设和发展不得占用河道滩地。城镇规划的临河界限，由河道主管机关会同城镇规划等有关部门确定。沿河城镇在编制和审查城镇规划时，应当事先征求河道主管机关的意见。

**Article 16** Urban construction and development shall not occupy river floodplain land. The riverfront boundaries of urban planning shall be determined by the competent river authority jointly with the urban planning and other relevant departments. When formulating and reviewing urban plans, towns along rivers shall seek in advance the opinions of the competent river authority.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　河道岸线的利用和建设，应当服从河道整治规划和航道整治规划。计划部门在审批利用河道岸线的建设项目时，应当事先征求河道主管机关的意见。  
河道岸线的界限，由河道主管机关会同交通等有关部门报县级以上地方人民政府划定。

**Article 17** The utilization and construction of river shoreline shall be subject to the river regulation plan and waterway regulation plan. When examining and approving construction projects that utilize river shoreline, the planning department shall seek in advance the opinions of the competent river authority.  
The boundaries of river shoreline shall be submitted by the competent river authority jointly with the transportation and other relevant departments to the local people's government at or above the county level for demarcation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　河道清淤和加固堤防取土以及按照防洪规划进行河道整治需要占用的土地，由当地人民政府调剂解决。  
因修建水库、整治河道所增加的可利用土地，属于国家所有，可以由县级以上人民政府用于移民安置和河道整治工程。

**Article 18** Land required for river dredging, dike reinforcement by soil borrowing, and river regulation according to the flood control plan shall be resolved by the local people's government through adjustment.  
Usable land increased as a result of reservoir construction or river regulation shall be owned by the State and may be used by the people's government at or above the county level for resettlement and river regulation projects.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　省、自治区、直辖市以河道为边界的，在河道两岸外侧各10公里之内，以及跨省、自治区、直辖市的河道，未经有关各方达成协议或者国务院水利行政主管部门批准，禁止单方面修建排水、阻水、引水、蓄水工程以及河道整治工程。

**Article 19** For rivers that serve as boundaries between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, within 10 kilometers on each side of the river banks, and for rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the unilateral construction of drainage, water-blocking, water diversion, water storage projects, and river regulation projects is prohibited without an agreement reached by all parties concerned or approval from the competent water administrative department of the State Council.

### 第三章　河道保护

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有堤防的河道，其管理范围为两岸堤防之间的水域、沙洲、滩地(包括可耕地)、行洪区，两岸堤防及护堤地。  
无堤防的河道，其管理范围根据历史最高洪水位或者设计洪水位确定。  
河道的具体管理范围，由县级以上地方人民政府负责划定。

**Article 20** For rivers with dikes, the management scope shall be the waters, sandbars, floodplains (including arable land), flood passage areas between the dikes on both banks, as well as the dikes themselves and the dike protection areas.  
For rivers without dikes, the management scope shall be determined based on the historical highest flood level or the design flood level.  
The specific management scope of rivers shall be demarcated by the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在河道管理范围内，水域和土地的利用应当符合江河行洪、输水和航运的要求；滩地的利用，应当由河道主管机关会同土地管理等有关部门制定规划，报县级以上地方人民政府批准后实施。

**Article 21** Within the river management scope, the utilization of waters and land shall comply with the requirements of river flood passage, water conveyance, and navigation; the utilization of floodplains shall be subject to a plan formulated by the competent river authority jointly with the land administration and other relevant departments, and shall be implemented after approval by the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止损毁堤防、护岸、闸坝等水工程建筑物和防汛设施、水文监测和测量设施、河岸地质监测设施以及通信照明等设施。  
在防汛抢险期间，无关人员和车辆不得上堤。  
因降雨雪等造成堤顶泥泞期间，禁止车辆通行，但防汛抢险车辆除外。

**Article 22** It is prohibited to damage water engineering structures such as dikes, revetments, sluice dams, and flood control facilities, hydrological monitoring and surveying facilities, riverbank geological monitoring facilities, and communication and lighting facilities.  
During the period of flood prevention and emergency rescue, unrelated persons and vehicles shall not ascend the dike.  
During periods when the dike crest becomes muddy due to rain or snow, vehicles are prohibited from passing, except for flood prevention and emergency rescue vehicles.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止非管理人员操作河道上的涵闸闸门，禁止任何组织和个人干扰河道管理单位的正常工作。

**Article 23** It is prohibited for non-management personnel to operate culvert gates on rivers, and it is prohibited for any organization or individual to interfere with the normal work of river management units.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在河道管理范围内，禁止修建围堤、阻水渠道、阻水道路；种植高杆农作物、芦苇、杞柳、荻柴和树木(堤防防护林除外)；设置拦河渔具；弃置矿渣、石渣、煤灰、泥土、垃圾等。  
在堤防和护堤地，禁止建房、放牧、开渠、打井、挖窖、葬坟、晒粮、存放物料、开采地下资源、进行考古发掘以及开展集市贸易活动。

**Article 24** Within the river management scope, it is prohibited to build enclosure dikes, water-blocking channels, or water-blocking roads; to plant tall-stalk crops, reeds, willow shrubs, thatch, or trees (except for dike protection forests); to set up fishing gear blocking the river; or to dump mineral waste, stone slag, coal ash, soil, garbage, etc.  
On dikes and dike protection areas, it is prohibited to build houses, graze livestock, dig ditches, dig wells, dig cellars, bury graves, dry grain, store materials, exploit underground resources, conduct archaeological excavations, or hold market fair activities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在河道管理范围内进行下列活动，必须报经河道主管机关批准；涉及其他部门的，由河道主管机关会同有关部门批准：  
(一)采砂、取土、淘金、弃置砂石或者淤泥；  
(二)爆破、钻探、挖筑鱼塘；  
(三)在河道滩地存放物料、修建厂房或者其他建筑设施；  
(四)在河道滩地开采地下资源及进行考古发掘。

**Article 25** The following activities within the river management scope must be reported to the competent river authority for approval; if other departments are involved, the competent river authority shall approve jointly with the relevant departments:  
(1) sand extraction, soil borrowing, gold panning, and dumping of sand, gravel, or silt;  
(2) blasting, drilling, and excavating fish ponds;  
(3) storing materials, constructing factory buildings or other structures on river floodplains;  
(4) exploiting underground resources and conducting archaeological excavations on river floodplains.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　根据堤防的重要程度、堤基土质条件等，河道主管机关报经县级以上人民政府批准，可以在河道管理范围的相连地域划定堤防安全保护区。在堤防安全保护区内，禁止进行打井、钻探、爆破、挖筑鱼塘、采石、取土等危害堤防安全的活动。

**Article 26** Based on the importance of the dike and the soil conditions of the dike foundation, the competent river authority may, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government at or above the county level, demarcate dike safety protection zones in the adjacent areas of the river management scope. Within the dike safety protection zones, activities such as digging wells, drilling, blasting, excavating fish ponds, quarrying, and soil borrowing that endanger dike safety are prohibited.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　禁止围湖造田。已经围垦的，应当按照国家规定的防洪标准进行治理，逐步退田还湖。湖泊的开发利用规划必须经河道主管机关审查同意。  
禁止围垦河流，确需围垦的，必须经过科学论证，并经省级以上人民政府批准。

**Article 27** It is prohibited to reclaim land by enclosing lakes. Where reclamation has already been carried out, it shall be regulated in accordance with the national flood control standards, and the land shall be gradually returned to the lake. The development and utilization plan for a lake must be reviewed and approved by the competent river authority.  
It is prohibited to reclaim land by enclosing rivers. Where reclamation is truly necessary, it must undergo scientific论证 and be approved by the people's government at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　加强河道滩地、堤防和河岸的水土保持工作，防止水土流失、河道淤积。

**Article 28** Soil and water conservation work on river floodplains, dikes, and riverbanks shall be strengthened to prevent soil erosion and river siltation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　江河的故道、旧堤、原有工程设施等，不得擅自填堵、占用或者拆毁。

**Article 29** Ancient river courses, old dikes, and existing engineering facilities of rivers shall not be filled, blocked, occupied, or demolished without authorization.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　护堤护岸林木，由河道管理单位组织营造和管理，其他任何单位和个人不得侵占、砍伐或者破坏。  
河道管理单位对护堤护岸林木进行抚育和更新性质的采伐及用于防汛抢险的采伐，根据国家有关规定免交育林基金。

**Article 30** Trees for dike and bank protection shall be planted and managed by the river management unit; no other entity or individual may encroach upon, fell, or damage them.  
When the river management unit carries out tending and regeneration felling of dike and bank protection trees, as well as felling for flood prevention and emergency rescue, it shall be exempted from paying the afforestation fund in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在为保证堤岸安全需要限制航速的河段，河道主管机关应当会同交通部门设立限制航速的标志，通行的船舶不得超速行驶。  
在汛期，船舶的行驶和停靠必须遵守防汛指挥部的规定。

**Article 31** In river reaches where speed limits are necessary to ensure the safety of dikes and banks, the competent river authority shall, jointly with the transportation department, set up speed limit signs; vessels passing through shall not exceed the speed limit.  
During the flood season, vessels' navigation and berthing must comply with the regulations of the flood control headquarters.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　山区河道有山体滑坡、崩岸、泥石流等自然灾害的河段，河道主管机关应当会同地质、交通等部门加强监测。在上述河段，禁止从事开山采石、采矿、开荒等危及山体稳定的活动。

**Article 32** For river reaches in mountainous areas where natural disasters such as landslides, bank collapses, and debris flows occur, the competent river authority shall, jointly with the geological, transportation, and other departments, strengthen monitoring. In the aforesaid river reaches, activities such as mountain quarrying, mining, and land reclamation that endanger mountain stability are prohibited.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在河道中流放竹木，不得影响行洪、航运和水工程安全，并服从当地河道主管机关的安全管理。  
在汛期，河道主管机关有权对河道上的竹木和其他漂流物进行紧急处置。

**Article 33** The floating of bamboo and timber in rivers shall not affect flood passage, navigation, or the safety of water projects, and shall be subject to the safety management of the local competent river authority.  
During the flood season, the competent river authority shall have the right to take emergency measures concerning bamboo, timber, and other floating objects on the river.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　向河道、湖泊排污的排污口的设置和扩大，排污单位在向环境保护部门申报之前，应当征得河道主管机关的同意。

**Article 34** For the installation and expansion of sewage outlets discharging into rivers and lakes, the discharging entity shall obtain the consent of the competent river authority before reporting to the environmental protection department.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在河道管理范围内，禁止堆放、倾倒、掩埋、排放污染水体的物体。禁止在河道内清洗装贮过油类或者有毒污染物的车辆、容器。  
河道主管机关应当开展河道水质监测工作，协同环境保护部门对水污染防治实施监督管理。

**Article 35** Within the river management scope, it is prohibited to pile up, dump, bury, or discharge objects that pollute water bodies. It is prohibited to wash in rivers vehicles or containers that have stored oil or toxic pollutants.  
The competent river authority shall carry out river water quality monitoring and shall cooperate with the environmental protection department in the supervision and management of water pollution prevention and control.

### 第四章　河道清障

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对河道管理范围内的阻水障碍物，按照“谁设障，谁清除”的原则，由河道主管机关提出清障计划和实施方案，由防汛指挥部责令设障者在规定的期限内清除。逾期不清除的，由防汛指挥部组织强行清除，并由设障者负担全部清障费用。

**Article 36** For water-blocking obstacles within the river management scope, following the principle of "the party that creates the obstacle shall be responsible for its removal," the competent river authority shall propose an obstacle removal plan and implementation scheme, and the flood control headquarters shall order the party that created the obstacle to remove it within a specified time limit. If the obstacle is not removed within the time limit, the flood control headquarters shall organize its forcible removal, and the party that created the obstacle shall bear all removal costs.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对壅水、阻水严重的桥梁、引道、码头和其他跨河工程设施，根据国家规定的防洪标准，由河道主管机关提出意见并报经人民政府批准，责成原建设单位在规定的期限内改建或者拆除。汛期影响防洪安全的，必须服从防汛指挥部的紧急处理决定。

**Article 37** For bridges, approach roads, wharves, and other river-crossing engineering facilities that cause serious backwater or water obstruction, in accordance with the national flood control standards, the competent river authority shall give its opinions and report to the people's government for approval, and shall order the original construction entity to renovate or demolish them within a specified time limit. If flood control safety is affected during the flood season, the emergency treatment decisions of the flood control headquarters must be complied with.

### 第五章　经费

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　河道堤防的防汛岁修费，按照分级管理的原则，分别由中央财政和地方财政负担，列入中央和地方年度财政预算。

**Article 38** The annual flood control maintenance expenses for river dikes shall be borne by the central finance and local finance respectively according to the principle of management at different levels, and shall be included in the central and local annual financial budgets.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　受益范围明确的堤防、护岸、水闸、圩垸、海塘和排涝工程设施，河道主管机关可以向受益的工商企业等单位和农户收取河道工程修建维护管理费，其标准应当根据工程修建和维护管理费用确定。收费的具体标准和计收办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 39** For dikes, revetments, sluices, polders, seawalls, and drainage engineering facilities with clearly defined benefit scopes, the competent river authority may collect river engineering construction, maintenance, and management fees from benefiting entities such as industrial and commercial enterprises and farming households. The fee rates shall be determined based on the construction and maintenance management costs. The specific fee rates and collection methods shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　在河道管理范围内采砂、取土、淘金，必须按照经批准的范围和作业方式进行，并向河道主管机关缴纳管理费。收费的标准和计收办法由国务院水利行政主管部门会同国务院财政主管部门制定。

**Article 40** Sand extraction, soil borrowing, and gold panning within the river management scope must be carried out in accordance with the approved scope and operation methods, and the management fee shall be paid to the competent river authority. The fee standards and collection methods shall be formulated by the competent water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the competent financial department of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何单位和个人，凡对堤防、护岸和其他水工程设施造成损坏或者造成河道淤积的，由责任者负责修复、清淤或者承担维修费用。

**Article 41** Any entity or individual that causes damage to dikes, revetments, or other water engineering facilities, or causes river siltation, shall be responsible for repair, dredging, or bearing the maintenance costs.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　河道主管机关收取的各项费用，用于河道堤防工程的建设、管理、维修和设施的更新改造。结余资金可以连年结转使用，任何部门不得截取或者挪用。

**Article 42** All fees collected by the competent river authority shall be used for the construction, management, maintenance, and renovation of facilities of river dike projects. Surplus funds may be carried forward for use in subsequent years, and no department may intercept or misappropriate them.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　河道两岸的城镇和农村，当地县级以上人民政府可以在汛期组织堤防保护区域内的单位和个人义务出工，对河道堤防工程进行维修和加固。

**Article 43** For towns and villages on both sides of rivers, the local people's government at or above the county level may, during the flood season, organize entities and individuals within the dike protection area to perform voluntary labor for the maintenance and reinforcement of river dike projects.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，县级以上地方人民政府河道主管机关除责令其纠正违法行为、采取补救措施外，可以并处警告、罚款、没收非法所得；对有关责任人员，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)在河道管理范围内弃置、堆放阻碍行洪物体的；种植阻碍行洪的林木或者高秆植物的；修建围堤、阻水渠道、阻水道路的；  
(二)在堤防、护堤地建房、放牧、开渠、打井、挖窖、葬坟、晒粮、存放物料、开采地下资源、进行考古发掘以及开展集市贸易活动的；  
(三)未经批准或者不按照国家规定的防洪标准、工程安全标准整治河道或者修建水工程建筑物和其他设施的；  
(四)未经批准或者不按照河道主管机关的规定在河道管理范围内采砂、取土、淘金、弃置砂石或者淤泥、爆破、钻探、挖筑鱼塘的；  
(五)未经批准在河道滩地存放物料、修建厂房或者其他建筑设施，以及开采地下资源或者进行考古发掘的；  
(六)违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)的规定，围垦湖泊、河流的；  
(七)擅自砍伐护堤护岸林木的；  
(八)汛期违反防汛指挥部的规定或者指令的。

**Article 44** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Regulations, the competent river authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in addition to ordering the correction of the illegal act and the adoption of remedial measures, also impose a warning, a fine, or confiscation of illegal income; for the responsible persons, disciplinary sanctions shall be given by their units or superior competent authorities; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) dumping or piling up objects that obstruct flood passage within the river management scope; planting trees or tall-stalk plants that obstruct flood passage; building enclosure dikes, water-blocking channels, or water-blocking roads;  
(2) building houses, grazing livestock, digging ditches, digging wells, digging cellars, burying graves, drying grain, storing materials, exploiting underground resources, conducting archaeological excavations, or holding market fair activities on dikes or dike protection areas;  
(3) regulating rivers or constructing water engineering structures and other facilities without approval or not in accordance with the national flood control standards and engineering safety standards;  
(4) extracting sand, borrowing soil, panning gold, dumping sand, gravel, or silt, blasting, drilling, or excavating fish ponds within the river management scope without approval or not in accordance with the regulations of the competent river authority;  
(5) storing materials, constructing factory buildings or other structures, exploiting underground resources, or conducting archaeological excavations on river floodplains without approval;  
(6) reclaiming lakes or rivers by enclosure in violation of the provisions of Article 27 of these Regulations;  
(7) felling dike and bank protection trees without authorization;  
(8) violating the regulations or orders of the flood control headquarters during the flood season.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，县级以上地方人民政府河道主管机关除责令其纠正违法行为、赔偿损失、采取补救措施外，可以并处警告、罚款；应当给予治安管理处罚的，按照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)损毁堤防、护岸、闸坝、水工程建筑物，损毁防汛设施、水文监测和测量设施、河岸地质监测设施以及通信照明等设施；  
(二)在堤防安全保护区内进行打井、钻探、爆破、挖筑鱼塘、采石、取土等危害堤防安全的活动的；  
(三)非管理人员操作河道上的涵闸闸门或者干扰河道管理单位正常工作的。

**Article 45** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Regulations, the competent river authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in addition to ordering the correction of the illegal act, compensation for losses, and the adoption of remedial measures, also impose a warning or a fine; if a public security penalty should be imposed, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the "Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China"; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) damaging dikes, revetments, sluice dams, water engineering structures, flood control facilities, hydrological monitoring and surveying facilities, riverbank geological monitoring facilities, and communication and lighting facilities;  
(2) engaging in activities such as digging wells, drilling, blasting, excavating fish ponds, quarrying, and soil borrowing within dike safety protection zones that endanger dike safety;  
(3) non-management personnel operating culvert gates on rivers or interfering with the normal work of river management units.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议，对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起15日内，向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，直接向人民法院起诉。当事人逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。对治安管理处罚不服的，按照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定办理。

**Article 46** Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the decision; where the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, institute prosecution in a People's Court. The party may also, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, directly institute prosecution in a People's Court. Where the party neither applies for reconsideration nor institutes prosecution within the time limit and does not perform the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision shall apply to a People's Court for compulsory execution. Where a party is dissatisfied with a public security penalty, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the "Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对违反本条例规定，造成国家、集体、个人经济损失的，受害方可以请求县级以上河道主管机关处理。受害方也可以直接向人民法院起诉。  
当事人对河道主管机关的处理决定不服的，可以在接到通知之日起，15日内向人民法院起诉。

**Article 47** Where a violation of these Regulations causes economic losses to the State, a collective, or an individual, the injured party may request the competent river authority at or above the county level to handle the matter. The injured party may also directly institute prosecution in a People's Court.  
Where a party is dissatisfied with a handling decision made by the competent river authority, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice, institute prosecution in a People's Court.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　河道主管机关的工作人员以及河道监理人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；对公共财产、国家和人民利益造成重大损失的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where staff members of the competent river authority or river supervision personnel neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed by their units or superior competent authorities; if significant losses are caused to public property or the interests of the State or the people, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，可以根据本条例的规定，结合本地区的实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 49** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and in light of the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate implementation measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例由国务院水利行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the competent water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 51** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
