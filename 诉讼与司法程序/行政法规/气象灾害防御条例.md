# 气象灾害防御条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2010-04-01

> (2010年1月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第570号公布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强气象灾害的防御，避免、减轻气象灾害造成的损失，保障人民生命财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国气象法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内从事气象灾害防御活动的，应当遵守本条例。 本条例所称气象灾害，是指台风、暴雨(雪)、寒潮、大风(沙尘暴)、低温、高温、干旱、雷电、冰雹、霜冻和大雾等所造成的灾害。 水旱灾害、地质灾害、海洋灾害、森林草原火灾等因气象因素引发的衍生、次生灾害的防御工作，适用有关法律、行政法规的规定。 第三条　气象灾害防御工作实行以人为本、科学防御、部门联动、社会参与的原则。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对气象灾害防御工作的组织、领导和协调，将气象灾害的防御纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算。 第五条　国务院气象主管机构和国务院有关部门应当按照职责分工，共同做好全国气象灾害防御工作。 地方各级气象主管机构和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，共同做好本行政区域的气象灾害防御工作。 第六条　气象灾害防御工作涉及两个以上行政区域的，有关地方人民政府、有关部门应当建立联防制度，加强信息沟通和监督检查。 第七条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当采取多种形式，向社会宣传普及气象灾害防御知识，提高公众的防灾减灾意识和能力。 学校应当把气象灾害防御知识纳入有关课程和课外教育内容，培养和提高学生的气象灾害防范意识和自救互救能力。教育、气象等部门应当对学校开展的气象灾害防御教育进行指导和监督。 第八条　国家鼓励开展气象灾害防御的科学技术研究，支持气象灾害防御先进技术的推广和应用，加强国际合作与交流，提高气象灾害防御的科技水平。 第九条　公民、法人和其他组织有义务参与气象灾害防御工作，在气象灾害发生后开展自救互救。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强气象灾害的防御，避免、减轻气象灾害造成的损失，保障人民生命财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国气象法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Meteorology Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the prevention of meteorological disasters, avoiding and mitigating losses caused by meteorological disasters, and ensuring the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内从事气象灾害防御活动的，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称气象灾害，是指台风、暴雨(雪)、寒潮、大风(沙尘暴)、低温、高温、干旱、雷电、冰雹、霜冻和大雾等所造成的灾害。  
水旱灾害、地质灾害、海洋灾害、森林草原火灾等因气象因素引发的衍生、次生灾害的防御工作，适用有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in meteorological disaster prevention activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, meteorological disasters refer to disasters caused by typhoon, rainstorm (snowstorm), cold wave, strong wind (sandstorm), low temperature, high temperature, drought, thunder and lightning, hail, frost, dense fog, and the like.  
The prevention of derivative and secondary disasters triggered by meteorological factors, such as flood and drought disasters, geological disasters, marine disasters, and forest and grassland fires, shall be governed by the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　气象灾害防御工作实行以人为本、科学防御、部门联动、社会参与的原则。

**Article 3** The principle of people-oriented approach, scientific prevention, interdepartmental coordination, and social participation shall be implemented in meteorological disaster prevention work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对气象灾害防御工作的组织、领导和协调，将气象灾害的防御纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the organization, leadership, and coordination of meteorological disaster prevention work, incorporate meteorological disaster prevention into their respective national economic and social development plans, and include the necessary funds in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院气象主管机构和国务院有关部门应当按照职责分工，共同做好全国气象灾害防御工作。  
地方各级气象主管机构和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，共同做好本行政区域的气象灾害防御工作。

**Article 5** The competent meteorological department of the State Council and the relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, jointly conduct the national meteorological disaster prevention work.  
Local meteorological competent authorities at all levels and the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, jointly conduct the meteorological disaster prevention work within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　气象灾害防御工作涉及两个以上行政区域的，有关地方人民政府、有关部门应当建立联防制度，加强信息沟通和监督检查。

**Article 6** Where meteorological disaster prevention work involves two or more administrative regions, the relevant local people's governments and relevant departments shall establish a joint prevention system, strengthen information communication, and enhance supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当采取多种形式，向社会宣传普及气象灾害防御知识，提高公众的防灾减灾意识和能力。  
学校应当把气象灾害防御知识纳入有关课程和课外教育内容，培养和提高学生的气象灾害防范意识和自救互救能力。教育、气象等部门应当对学校开展的气象灾害防御教育进行指导和监督。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall adopt various forms to disseminate and popularize knowledge on meteorological disaster prevention to the public, and enhance the public's awareness and capability of disaster prevention and mitigation.  
Schools shall incorporate knowledge on meteorological disaster prevention into relevant curricula and extracurricular education, cultivate and improve students' awareness of meteorological disaster prevention and their ability of self-rescue and mutual rescue. The education department, meteorological department, and other relevant departments shall provide guidance and supervision over the meteorological disaster prevention education conducted by schools.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励开展气象灾害防御的科学技术研究，支持气象灾害防御先进技术的推广和应用，加强国际合作与交流，提高气象灾害防御的科技水平。

**Article 8** The State encourages scientific and technological research on meteorological disaster prevention, supports the dissemination and application of advanced technologies for meteorological disaster prevention, strengthens international cooperation and exchange, and improves the scientific and technological level of meteorological disaster prevention.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公民、法人和其他组织有义务参与气象灾害防御工作，在气象灾害发生后开展自救互救。  
对在气象灾害防御工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the obligation to participate in meteorological disaster prevention work and to conduct self-rescue and mutual rescue after the occurrence of a meteorological disaster.  
Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in meteorological disaster prevention work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　预防

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当组织气象等有关部门对本行政区域内发生的气象灾害的种类、次数、强度和造成的损失等情况开展气象灾害普查，建立气象灾害数据库，按照气象灾害的种类进行气象灾害风险评估，并根据气象灾害分布情况和气象灾害风险评估结果，划定气象灾害风险区域。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the meteorological and other relevant departments to conduct a general survey of meteorological disasters, including the types, frequencies, intensities, and losses caused by meteorological disasters that have occurred within their respective administrative regions, establish a meteorological disaster database, carry out meteorological disaster risk assessment by type of meteorological disaster, and, based on the distribution of meteorological disasters and the results of meteorological disaster risk assessment, delimit meteorological disaster risk areas.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院气象主管机构应当会同国务院有关部门，根据气象灾害风险评估结果和气象灾害风险区域，编制国家气象灾害防御规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门，根据上一级人民政府的气象灾害防御规划，结合本地气象灾害特点，编制本行政区域的气象灾害防御规划。

**Article 11** The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a national meteorological disaster prevention plan based on the results of meteorological disaster risk assessment and meteorological disaster risk areas, and shall organize the implementation of the plan after submitting it to the State Council for approval.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to formulate meteorological disaster prevention plans for their respective administrative regions based on the meteorological disaster prevention plan of the people's government at the next higher level and in light of the characteristics of local meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　气象灾害防御规划应当包括气象灾害发生发展规律和现状、防御原则和目标、易发区和易发时段、防御设施建设和管理以及防御措施等内容。

**Article 12** A meteorological disaster prevention plan shall include the patterns and current status of the occurrence and development of meteorological disasters, prevention principles and objectives, high-incidence areas and periods, construction and management of prevention facilities, and prevention measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当按照气象灾害防御规划，加强气象灾害防御设施建设，做好气象灾害防御工作。

**Article 13** The relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the meteorological disaster prevention plan, strengthen the construction of meteorological disaster prevention facilities and conduct meteorological disaster prevention work properly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院有关部门制定电力、通信等基础设施的工程建设标准，应当考虑气象灾害的影响。

**Article 14** When formulating engineering construction standards for infrastructure such as electric power and communications, the relevant departments of the State Council shall take into account the impact of meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院气象主管机构应当会同国务院有关部门，根据气象灾害防御需要，编制国家气象灾害应急预案，报国务院批准。  
县级以上地方人民政府、有关部门应当根据气象灾害防御规划，结合本地气象灾害的特点和可能造成的危害，组织制定本行政区域的气象灾害应急预案，报上一级人民政府、有关部门备案。

**Article 15** The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a national emergency response plan for meteorological disasters based on the needs of meteorological disaster prevention, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the meteorological disaster prevention plan and in light of the characteristics of local meteorological disasters and their potential hazards, organize the formulation of emergency response plans for meteorological disasters within their respective administrative regions, and submit them to the people's government at the next higher level and relevant departments for recordation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　气象灾害应急预案应当包括应急预案启动标准、应急组织指挥体系与职责、预防与预警机制、应急处置措施和保障措施等内容。

**Article 16** An emergency response plan for meteorological disasters shall include the criteria for activating the emergency response plan, the emergency organization and command system and its responsibilities, prevention and early warning mechanisms, emergency response measures, and safeguard measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方各级人民政府应当根据本地气象灾害特点，组织开展气象灾害应急演练，提高应急救援能力。居民委员会、村民委员会、企业事业单位应当协助本地人民政府做好气象灾害防御知识的宣传和气象灾害应急演练工作。

**Article 17** Local people's governments at all levels shall, based on the characteristics of local meteorological disasters, organize emergency drills for meteorological disasters to improve emergency rescue capabilities. Residents' committees, villagers' committees, enterprises, and public institutions shall assist the local people's government in disseminating knowledge on meteorological disaster prevention and conducting emergency drills for meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　大风(沙尘暴)、龙卷风多发区域的地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当加强防护林和紧急避难场所等建设，并定期组织开展建(构)筑物防风避险的监督检查。  
台风多发区域的地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当加强海塘、堤防、避风港、防护林、避风锚地、紧急避难场所等建设，并根据台风情况做好人员转移等准备工作。

**Article 18** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments in areas prone to strong wind (sandstorm) and tornadoes shall strengthen the construction of shelterbelts and emergency shelters, and regularly organize supervision and inspection of wind prevention and hazard avoidance of buildings (structures).  
Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments in areas prone to typhoons shall strengthen the construction of sea walls, embankments, storm shelters, shelterbelts, storm anchorages, emergency shelters, etc., and make preparations such as personnel evacuation based on typhoon conditions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门和单位应当根据本地降雨情况，定期组织开展各种排水设施检查，及时疏通河道和排水管网，加固病险水库，加强对地质灾害易发区和堤防等重要险段的巡查。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, and entities shall, based on local rainfall conditions, regularly organize inspections of various drainage facilities, promptly dredge river courses and drainage pipe networks, reinforce dangerous reservoirs, and strengthen patrols of geological disaster-prone areas and important dangerous sections such as embankments.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门和单位应当根据本地降雪、冰冻发生情况，加强电力、通信线路的巡查，做好交通疏导、积雪(冰)清除、线路维护等准备工作。  
有关单位和个人应当根据本地降雪情况，做好危旧房屋加固、粮草储备、牲畜转移等准备工作。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, and entities shall, based on local snowfall and freezing conditions, strengthen patrols of power and communication lines, and make preparations such as traffic diversion, snow (ice) removal, and line maintenance.  
Relevant entities and individuals shall, based on local snowfall conditions, make preparations such as reinforcing dilapidated buildings, storing grain and fodder, and relocating livestock.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门和单位应当在高温来临前做好供电、供水和防暑医药供应的准备工作，并合理调整工作时间。

**Article 21** Local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, and entities shall, before the onset of high temperatures, make preparations for the supply of electricity, water, and heatstroke prevention medicines, and reasonably adjust working hours.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　大雾、霾多发区域的地方各级人民政府、有关部门和单位应当加强对机场、港口、高速公路、航道、渔场等重要场所和交通要道的大雾、霾的监测设施建设，做好交通疏导、调度和防护等准备工作。

**Article 22** Local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, and entities in areas prone to dense fog and haze shall strengthen the construction of monitoring facilities for dense fog and haze at important places and traffic arteries such as airports, ports, expressways, waterways, and fishing grounds, and make preparations for traffic diversion, dispatching, and protection.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各类建(构)筑物、场所和设施安装雷电防护装置应当符合国家有关防雷标准的规定。新建、改建、扩建建(构)筑物、场所和设施的雷电防护装置应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。  
新建、改建、扩建建设工程雷电防护装置的设计、施工，可以由取得相应建设、公路、水路、铁路、民航、水利、电力、核电、通信等专业工程设计、施工资质的单位承担。  
油库、气库、弹药库、化学品仓库和烟花爆竹、石化等易燃易爆建设工程和场所，雷电易发区内的矿区、旅游景点或者投入使用的建(构)筑物、设施等需要单独安装雷电防护装置的场所，以及雷电风险高且没有防雷标准规范、需要进行特殊论证的大型项目，其雷电防护装置的设计审核和竣工验收由县级以上地方气象主管机构负责。未经设计审核或者设计审核不合格的，不得施工；未经竣工验收或者竣工验收不合格的，不得交付使用。  
房屋建筑、市政基础设施、公路、水路、铁路、民航、水利、电力、核电、通信等建设工程的主管部门，负责相应领域内建设工程的防雷管理。

**Article 23** The installation of lightning protection devices on all types of buildings (structures), premises, and facilities shall comply with the provisions of the national lightning protection standards. Lightning protection devices for newly built, renovated, or expanded buildings (structures), premises, and facilities shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.  
The design and construction of lightning protection devices for newly built, renovated, or expanded construction projects may be undertaken by entities that have obtained the corresponding qualifications for professional engineering design and construction in fields such as construction, highway, waterway, railway, civil aviation, water conservancy, electric power, nuclear power, and communications.  
For oil depots, gas depots, ammunition depots, chemical warehouses, fireworks and firecrackers, petrochemical and other flammable and explosive construction projects and premises, mining areas within lightning-prone areas, tourist attractions, or buildings (structures) and facilities that are put into use and need to install lightning protection devices separately, as well as large-scale projects with high lightning risk and no lightning protection standards or specifications that require special demonstration, the design review and completion acceptance of their lightning protection devices shall be the responsibility of the local meteorological competent authorities at or above the county level. If the design has not been reviewed or fails the design review, construction shall not be carried out; if the completion acceptance has not been carried out or fails, the project shall not be delivered for use.  
The competent departments for construction projects such as housing construction, municipal infrastructure, highway, waterway, railway, civil aviation, water conservancy, electric power, nuclear power, and communications shall be responsible for the lightning protection management of construction projects within their respective fields.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事雷电防护装置检测的单位应当具备下列条件，取得国务院气象主管机构或者省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构颁发的资质证：  
(一)有法人资格；  
(二)有固定的办公场所和必要的设备、设施；  
(三)有相应的专业技术人员；  
(四)有完备的技术和质量管理制度；  
(五)国务院气象主管机构规定的其他条件。  
从事电力、通信雷电防护装置检测的单位的资质证由国务院气象主管机构和国务院电力或者国务院通信主管部门共同颁发。

**Article 24** A unit engaged in the testing of lightning protection devices shall meet the following conditions and obtain a qualification certificate issued by the competent meteorological department of the State Council or by the meteorological competent authority of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having a fixed office premises and necessary equipment and facilities;  
(3) having corresponding professional and technical personnel;  
(4) having complete technical and quality management systems; and  
(5) other conditions prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the State Council.  
The qualification certificate for units engaged in the testing of lightning protection devices for electric power and communications shall be jointly issued by the competent meteorological department of the State Council and the competent department of electric power or the competent department of communications of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门应当根据本地气象灾害发生情况，加强农村地区气象灾害预防、监测、信息传播等基础设施建设，采取综合措施，做好农村气象灾害防御工作。

**Article 25** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall, based on the occurrence of local meteorological disasters, strengthen the construction of infrastructure for meteorological disaster prevention, monitoring, and information dissemination in rural areas, adopt comprehensive measures, and conduct rural meteorological disaster prevention work properly.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级气象主管机构应当在本级人民政府的领导和协调下，根据实际情况组织开展人工影响天气工作，减轻气象灾害的影响。

**Article 26** Meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall, under the leadership and coordination of the people's government at the same level, organize and carry out weather modification work based on actual conditions to mitigate the impact of meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门在国家重大建设工程、重大区域性经济开发项目和大型太阳能、风能等气候资源开发利用项目以及城乡规划编制中，应当统筹考虑气候可行性和气象灾害的风险性，避免、减轻气象灾害的影响。

**Article 27** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in national major construction projects, major regional economic development projects, large-scale solar energy, wind energy and other climate resource development and utilization projects, as well as in the formulation of urban and rural plans, take into comprehensive consideration climatic feasibility and the risk of meteorological disasters, so as to avoid and mitigate the impact of meteorological disasters.

### 第三章　监测、预报和预警

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据气象灾害防御的需要，建设应急移动气象灾害监测设施，健全应急监测队伍，完善气象灾害监测体系。  
县级以上人民政府应当整合完善气象灾害监测信息网络，实现信息资源共享。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of meteorological disaster prevention, construct emergency mobile meteorological disaster monitoring facilities, improve emergency monitoring teams, and perfect the meteorological disaster monitoring system.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall integrate and improve the meteorological disaster monitoring information network to achieve information resource sharing.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级气象主管机构及其所属的气象台站应当完善灾害性天气的预报系统，提高灾害性天气预报、警报的准确率和时效性。  
各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站、其他有关部门所属的气象台站和与灾害性天气监测、预报有关的单位应当根据气象灾害防御的需要，按照职责开展灾害性天气的监测工作，并及时向气象主管机构和有关灾害防御、救助部门提供雨情、水情、风情、旱情等监测信息。  
各级气象主管机构应当根据气象灾害防御的需要组织开展跨地区、跨部门的气象灾害联合监测，并将人口密集区、农业主产区、地质灾害易发区域、重要江河流域、森林、草原、渔场作为气象灾害监测的重点区域。

**Article 29** The meteorological competent authorities at all levels and their subordinate meteorological stations shall improve the forecasting system for hazardous weather and enhance the accuracy and timeliness of hazardous weather forecasts and warnings.  
Meteorological stations subordinate to meteorological competent authorities at all levels, meteorological stations subordinate to other relevant departments, and entities related to hazardous weather monitoring and forecasting shall, in accordance with the needs of meteorological disaster prevention and within their respective functions, carry out hazardous weather monitoring and promptly provide monitoring information on rainfall, water conditions, wind conditions, drought conditions, etc. to the meteorological competent authorities and relevant disaster prevention and relief departments.  
Meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall, based on the needs of meteorological disaster prevention, organize cross-regional and cross-departmental joint monitoring of meteorological disasters, and designate densely populated areas, major agricultural production areas, geological disaster-prone areas, important river basins, forests, grasslands, and fishing grounds as key areas for meteorological disaster monitoring.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当按照职责向社会统一发布灾害性天气警报和气象灾害预警信号，并及时向有关灾害防御、救助部门通报；其他组织和个人不得向社会发布灾害性天气警报和气象灾害预警信号。  
气象灾害预警信号的种类和级别，由国务院气象主管机构规定。

**Article 30** Meteorological stations subordinate to meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall, in accordance with their functions, uniformly issue hazardous weather warnings and meteorological disaster early warning signals to the public, and promptly notify the relevant disaster prevention and relief departments; no other organization or individual may issue hazardous weather warnings or meteorological disaster early warning signals to the public.  
The types and levels of meteorological disaster early warning signals shall be prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　广播、电视、报纸、电信等媒体应当及时向社会播发或者刊登当地气象主管机构所属的气象台站提供的适时灾害性天气警报、气象灾害预警信号，并根据当地气象台站的要求及时增播、插播或者刊登。

**Article 31** Media such as radio, television, newspapers, and telecommunications shall promptly broadcast or publish the timely hazardous weather warnings and meteorological disaster early warning signals provided by the meteorological station subordinate to the local meteorological competent authority, and shall, upon the request of the local meteorological station, increase, interrupt, or publish them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立和完善气象灾害预警信息发布系统，并根据气象灾害防御的需要，在交通枢纽、公共活动场所等人口密集区域和气象灾害易发区域建立灾害性天气警报、气象灾害预警信号接收和播发设施，并保证设施的正常运转。  
乡(镇)人民政府、街道办事处应当确定人员，协助气象主管机构、民政部门开展气象灾害防御知识宣传、应急联络、信息传递、灾害报告和灾情调查等工作。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the meteorological disaster early warning information release system, and, based on the needs of meteorological disaster prevention, establish facilities for receiving and broadcasting hazardous weather warnings and meteorological disaster early warning signals in densely populated areas such as transportation hubs and public activity venues, as well as in areas prone to meteorological disasters, and shall ensure the normal operation of such facilities.  
Town (township) people's governments and sub-district offices shall designate personnel to assist the meteorological competent authorities and civil affairs departments in conducting activities such as the dissemination of knowledge on meteorological disaster prevention, emergency liaison, information transmission, disaster reporting, and disaster investigation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　各级气象主管机构应当做好太阳风暴、地球空间暴等空间天气灾害的监测、预报和预警工作。

**Article 33** Meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall conduct monitoring, forecasting, and early warning of space weather disasters such as solar storms and geomagnetic storms.

### 第四章　应急处置

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当及时向本级人民政府和有关部门报告灾害性天气预报、警报情况和气象灾害预警信息。  
县级以上地方人民政府、有关部门应当根据灾害性天气警报、气象灾害预警信号和气象灾害应急预案启动标准，及时作出启动相应应急预案的决定，向社会公布，并报告上一级人民政府；必要时，可以越级上报，并向当地驻军和可能受到危害的毗邻地区的人民政府通报。  
发生跨省、自治区、直辖市大范围的气象灾害，并造成较大危害时，由国务院决定启动国家气象灾害应急预案。

**Article 34** Meteorological stations subordinate to meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall promptly report to the people's government at the same level and relevant departments on the situation of hazardous weather forecasts, warnings, and meteorological disaster early warning information.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall, based on hazardous weather warnings, meteorological disaster early warning signals, and the criteria for activating the meteorological disaster emergency response plan, promptly make a decision to activate the corresponding emergency response plan, announce it to the public, and report it to the people's government at the next higher level; when necessary, they may report directly to a higher level and notify the local military units and the people's governments of adjacent areas that may be affected.  
When a large-scale meteorological disaster occurs across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government and causes major harm, the State Council shall decide to activate the national meteorological disaster emergency response plan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据灾害性天气影响范围、强度，将可能造成人员伤亡或者重大财产损失的区域临时确定为气象灾害危险区，并及时予以公告。

**Article 35** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the scope and intensity of the impact of hazardous weather, temporarily designate areas that may cause casualties or significant property losses as meteorological disaster danger zones, and promptly make a public announcement thereof.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府、有关部门应当根据气象灾害发生情况，依照《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定及时采取应急处置措施；情况紧急时，及时动员、组织受到灾害威胁的人员转移、疏散，开展自救互救。  
对当地人民政府、有关部门采取的气象灾害应急处置措施，任何单位和个人应当配合实施，不得妨碍气象灾害救助活动。

**Article 36** Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall, based on the occurrence of meteorological disasters, take emergency response measures in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China; in case of emergency, promptly mobilize and organize persons threatened by the disaster to evacuate and disperse, and carry out self-rescue and mutual rescue.  
Any entity or individual shall cooperate in the implementation of the meteorological disaster emergency response measures taken by the local people's government and relevant departments, and shall not obstruct meteorological disaster relief activities.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　气象灾害应急预案启动后，各级气象主管机构应当组织所属的气象台站加强对气象灾害的监测和评估，启用应急移动气象灾害监测设施，开展现场气象服务，及时向本级人民政府、有关部门报告灾害性天气实况、变化趋势和评估结果，为本级人民政府组织防御气象灾害提供决策依据。

**Article 37** After the meteorological disaster emergency response plan is activated, meteorological competent authorities at all levels shall organize their subordinate meteorological stations to strengthen the monitoring and assessment of meteorological disasters, activate emergency mobile meteorological disaster monitoring facilities, provide on-site meteorological services, and promptly report to the people's government at the same level and relevant departments on the actual situation, changing trends, and assessment results of hazardous weather, so as to provide a decision-making basis for the people's government at the same level to organize the prevention of meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照各自职责，做好相应的应急工作。  
民政部门应当设置避难场所和救济物资供应点，开展受灾群众救助工作，并按照规定职责核查灾情、发布灾情信息。  
卫生主管部门应当组织医疗救治、卫生防疫等卫生应急工作。  
交通运输、铁路等部门应当优先运送救灾物资、设备、药物、食品，及时抢修被毁的道路交通设施。  
住房城乡建设部门应当保障供水、供气、供热等市政公用设施的安全运行。  
电力、通信主管部门应当组织做好电力、通信应急保障工作。  
国土资源部门应当组织开展地质灾害监测、预防工作。  
农业主管部门应当组织开展农业抗灾救灾和农业生产技术指导工作。  
水利主管部门应当统筹协调主要河流、水库的水量调度，组织开展防汛抗旱工作。  
公安部门应当负责灾区的社会治安和道路交通秩序维护工作，协助组织灾区群众进行紧急转移。

**Article 38** The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, conduct the corresponding emergency work properly.  
The civil affairs department shall set up shelters and relief supply points, carry out relief work for affected people, and, in accordance with its prescribed duties, verify disaster situations and release disaster information.  
The competent health department shall organize medical treatment, sanitation and epidemic prevention, and other health emergency work.  
The departments of transport and railway shall give priority to the transportation of disaster relief materials, equipment, medicine, and food, and promptly repair damaged road and traffic facilities.  
The department of housing and urban-rural development shall ensure the safe operation of municipal public facilities such as water supply, gas supply, and heat supply.  
The competent departments of electric power and communications shall organize and conduct emergency support work for electric power and communications.  
The department of land and resources shall organize and carry out geological disaster monitoring and prevention work.  
The competent agricultural department shall organize agricultural disaster resistance and relief work and provide guidance on agricultural production techniques.  
The competent water conservancy department shall coordinate the water quantity dispatch of major rivers and reservoirs, and organize flood control and drought relief work.  
The public security department shall be responsible for maintaining public order and road traffic order in the disaster area, and assist in organizing the emergency evacuation of the masses in the disaster area.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　气象、水利、国土资源、农业、林业、海洋等部门应当根据气象灾害发生的情况，加强对气象因素引发的衍生、次生灾害的联合监测，并根据相应的应急预案，做好各项应急处置工作。

**Article 39** The departments of meteorology, water conservancy, land and resources, agriculture, forestry, and oceans shall, based on the occurrence of meteorological disasters, strengthen joint monitoring of derivative and secondary disasters triggered by meteorological factors, and, in accordance with the corresponding emergency response plans, conduct all emergency response work properly.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　广播、电视、报纸、电信等媒体应当及时、准确地向社会传播气象灾害的发生、发展和应急处置情况。

**Article 40** Media such as radio, television, newspapers, and telecommunications shall promptly and accurately disseminate to the public information on the occurrence, development, and emergency response of meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当根据气象主管机构提供的灾害性天气发生、发展趋势信息以及灾情发展情况，按照有关规定适时调整气象灾害级别或者作出解除气象灾害应急措施的决定。

**Article 41** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, based on the information on the occurrence and development trends of hazardous weather provided by the meteorological competent authority and the development of the disaster situation, adjust the level of the meteorological disaster in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions or make a decision to lift the meteorological disaster emergency measures.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　气象灾害应急处置工作结束后，地方各级人民政府应当组织有关部门对气象灾害造成的损失进行调查，制定恢复重建计划，并向上一级人民政府报告。

**Article 42** After the completion of the meteorological disaster emergency response work, local people's governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments to investigate the losses caused by the meteorological disaster, formulate a recovery and reconstruction plan, and report to the people's government at the next higher level.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，地方各级人民政府、各级气象主管机构和其他有关部门及其工作人员，有下列行为之一的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照规定编制气象灾害防御规划或者气象灾害应急预案的；  
(二)未按照规定采取气象灾害预防措施的；  
(三)向不符合条件的单位颁发雷电防护装置检测资质证的；  
(四)隐瞒、谎报或者由于玩忽职守导致重大漏报、错报灾害性天气警报、气象灾害预警信号的；  
(五)未及时采取气象灾害应急措施的；  
(六)不依法履行职责的其他行为。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, local people's governments at all levels, meteorological competent authorities at all levels, other relevant departments, and their staff members commit any of the following acts, the superior organ or the supervisory organ shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to formulate a meteorological disaster prevention plan or a meteorological disaster emergency response plan in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to take meteorological disaster prevention measures in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) issuing a qualification certificate for lightning protection device testing to a unit that does not meet the conditions;  
(4) concealing, making false reports, or, due to dereliction of duty, causing major omissions or errors in hazardous weather warnings or meteorological disaster early warning signals;  
(5) failing to take meteorological disaster emergency measures in a timely manner; or  
(6) other acts of failing to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府或者有关部门责令改正；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照规定采取气象灾害预防措施的；  
(二)不服从所在地人民政府及其有关部门发布的气象灾害应急处置决定、命令，或者不配合实施其依法采取的气象灾害应急措施的。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the local people's government at or above the county level or the relevant department shall order the person to make corrections; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to take meteorological disaster prevention measures in accordance with the provisions; or  
(2) disobeying the decisions or orders on meteorological disaster emergency response issued by the local people's government and its relevant departments, or failing to cooperate in the implementation of the meteorological disaster emergency measures lawfully taken by them.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上气象主管机构或者其他有关部门按照权限责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)无资质或者超越资质许可范围从事雷电防护装置检测的；  
(二)在雷电防护装置设计、施工、检测中弄虚作假的；  
(三)违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第三款的规定，雷电防护装置未经设计审核或者设计审核不合格施工的，未经竣工验收或者竣工验收不合格交付使用的。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the meteorological competent authority at or above the county level or other relevant department shall, within its authority, order the cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) engaging in the testing of lightning protection devices without qualification or beyond the scope permitted by the qualification;  
(2) practicing fraud in the design, construction, or testing of lightning protection devices; or  
(3) violating the provisions of the third paragraph of Article 23 of these Regulations by constructing a lightning protection device without design review or where the design review fails, or delivering it for use without completion acceptance or where the completion acceptance fails.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上气象主管机构责令改正，给予警告，可以处5万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚：  
(一)擅自向社会发布灾害性天气警报、气象灾害预警信号的；  
(二)广播、电视、报纸、电信等媒体未按照要求播发、刊登灾害性天气警报和气象灾害预警信号的；  
(三)传播虚假的或者通过非法渠道获取的灾害性天气信息和气象灾害灾情的。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the meteorological competent authority at or above the county level shall order the person to make corrections, give a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law:  
(1) issuing hazardous weather warnings or meteorological disaster early warning signals to the public without authorization;  
(2) radio, television, newspaper, telecommunications, or other media failing to broadcast or publish hazardous weather warnings or meteorological disaster early warning signals as required; or  
(3) disseminating false hazardous weather information or meteorological disaster situations, or disseminating such information obtained through illegal channels.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　中国人民解放军的气象灾害防御活动，按照中央军事委员会的规定执行。

**Article 47** The meteorological disaster prevention activities of the Chinese People's Liberation Army shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2010年4月1日起施行。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 2010.
