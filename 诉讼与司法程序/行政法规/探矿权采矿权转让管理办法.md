# 探矿权采矿权转让管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29

> (1998年2月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第242号发布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强对探矿权、采矿权转让的管理，保护探矿权人、采矿权人的合法权益，促进矿业发展，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本办法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域转让依法取得的探矿权、采矿权的，必须遵守本办法。 第三条　除按照下列规定可以转让外，探矿权、采矿权不得转让： (一)探矿权人有权在划定的勘查作业区内进行规定的勘查作业，有权优先取得勘查作业区内矿产资源的采矿权。探矿权人在完成规定的最低勘查投入后，经依法批准，可以将探矿权转让他人。 (二)已经取得采矿权的矿山企业，因企业合并、分立，与他人合资、合作经营，或者因企业资产出售以及有其他变更企业资产产权的情形，需要变更采矿权主体的，经依法批准，可以将采矿权转让他人采矿。 第四条　国务院地质矿产主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门是探矿权、采矿权转让的审批管理机关。 国务院地质矿产主管部门负责由其审批发证的探矿权、采矿权转让的审批。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门负责本条第二款规定以外的探矿权、采矿权转让的审批。 第五条　转让探矿权，应当具备下列条件： (一)自颁发勘查许可证之日起满2年，或者在勘查作业区内发现可供进一步勘查或者开采的矿产资源； (二)完成规定的最低勘查投入； (三)探矿权属无争议； (四)按照国家有关规定已经缴纳探矿权使用费、探矿权价款； (五)国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的其他条件。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对探矿权、采矿权转让的管理，保护探矿权人、采矿权人的合法权益，促进矿业发展，根据《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the administration of the transfer of exploration rights and mining rights, protecting the lawful rights and interests of exploration right holders and mining right holders, and promoting the development of the mining industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域转让依法取得的探矿权、采矿权的，必须遵守本办法。

**Article 2** Any transfer of exploration rights or mining rights lawfully obtained within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction must comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　除按照下列规定可以转让外，探矿权、采矿权不得转让：  
(一)探矿权人有权在划定的勘查作业区内进行规定的勘查作业，有权优先取得勘查作业区内矿产资源的采矿权。探矿权人在完成规定的最低勘查投入后，经依法批准，可以将探矿权转让他人。  
(二)已经取得采矿权的矿山企业，因企业合并、分立，与他人合资、合作经营，或者因企业资产出售以及有其他变更企业资产产权的情形，需要变更采矿权主体的，经依法批准，可以将采矿权转让他人采矿。

**Article 3** Except for transfers that may be made in accordance with the following provisions, exploration rights and mining rights shall not be transferred:  
(1) An exploration right holder has the right to carry out prescribed exploration operations within the designated exploration area and has the priority right to obtain the mining rights for the mineral resources in the exploration area. After completing the prescribed minimum exploration input, the exploration right holder may, subject to approval according to law, transfer the exploration right to another person.  
(2) A mining enterprise that has already obtained mining rights may, due to merger, division, joint venture, cooperative operation with others, or due to the sale of enterprise assets or other circumstances involving changes in the property rights of enterprise assets, if it is necessary to change the subject of the mining rights, may, subject to approval according to law, transfer the mining rights to another person for mining.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院地质矿产主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门是探矿权、采矿权转让的审批管理机关。  
国务院地质矿产主管部门负责由其审批发证的探矿权、采矿权转让的审批。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门负责本条第二款规定以外的探矿权、采矿权转让的审批。

**Article 4** The competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and the competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be the authorities for examination and approval of the transfer of exploration rights and mining rights.  
The competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall be responsible for the examination and approval of the transfer of exploration rights and mining rights for which it issues exploration permits or mining permits.  
The competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the examination and approval of the transfer of exploration rights and mining rights other than those specified in the second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　转让探矿权，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)自颁发勘查许可证之日起满2年，或者在勘查作业区内发现可供进一步勘查或者开采的矿产资源；  
(二)完成规定的最低勘查投入；  
(三)探矿权属无争议；  
(四)按照国家有关规定已经缴纳探矿权使用费、探矿权价款；  
(五)国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 5** The transfer of an exploration right shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Two years have elapsed since the date of issuance of the exploration permit, or mineral resources that are available for further exploration or exploitation have been discovered within the exploration area;  
(2) The prescribed minimum exploration input has been completed;  
(3) There is no dispute over the ownership of the exploration right;  
(4) The exploration right usage fee and the exploration right price have been paid in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　转让采矿权，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)矿山企业投入采矿生产满1年；  
(二)采矿权属无争议；  
(三)按照国家有关规定已经缴纳采矿权使用费、采矿权价款、矿产资源补偿费和资源税；  
(四)国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的其他条件。  
国有矿山企业在申请转让采矿权前，应当征得矿山企业主管部门的同意。

**Article 6** The transfer of a mining right shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The mining enterprise has been engaged in mining production for at least one year;  
(2) There is no dispute over the ownership of the mining right;  
(3) The mining right usage fee, the mining right price, the mineral resources compensation fee, and the resource tax have been paid in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.  
Before applying for the transfer of a mining right, a State-owned mining enterprise shall obtain the consent of the competent department in charge of the mining enterprise.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　探矿权或者采矿权转让的受让人，应当符合《矿产资源勘查区块登记管理办法》或者《矿产资源开采登记管理办法》规定的有关探矿权申请人或者采矿权申请人的条件。

**Article 7** The transferee of an exploration right or mining right shall meet the conditions for an applicant for an exploration right or an applicant for a mining right as prescribed in the Measures for the Registration of Exploration Blocks of Mineral Resources or the Measures for the Registration of Mining of Mineral Resources.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　探矿权人或者采矿权人在申请转让探矿权或者采矿权时，应当向审批管理机关提交下列资料：  
(一)转让申请书；  
(二)转让人与受让人签订的转让合同；  
(三)受让人资质条件的证明文件；  
(四)转让人具备[本办法第五条](#art-5)或者第六条规定的转让条件的证明；  
(五)矿产资源勘查或者开采情况的报告；  
(六)审批管理机关要求提交的其他有关资料。  
国有矿山企业转让采矿权时，还应当提交有关主管部门同意转让采矿权的批准文件。

**Article 8** When applying for the transfer of an exploration right or mining right, the exploration right holder or mining right holder shall submit the following materials to the examination and approval authority:  
(1) An application for transfer;  
(2) A transfer contract signed by the transferor and the transferee;  
(3) Documents certifying the eligibility of the transferee;  
(4) Evidence that the transferor satisfies the conditions for transfer as provided in Article 5 or Article 6 of these Measures;  
(5) A report on the exploration or mining of mineral resources;  
(6) Other relevant materials as required by the examination and approval authority.  
When transferring a mining right, a State-owned mining enterprise shall also submit the approval document issued by the competent department in charge consenting to the transfer of the mining right.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　转让国家出资勘查所形成的探矿权、采矿权的，必须进行评估。  
国家出资勘查形成的探矿权、采矿权价款，由具有矿业权评估资质的评估机构进行评估；评估报告报探矿权、采矿权登记管理机关备案。

**Article 9** The transfer of exploration rights or mining rights formed through exploration funded by the State must be subject to appraisal.  
The price of exploration rights or mining rights formed through exploration funded by the State shall be appraised by an appraisal institution with the qualification for mining rights appraisal; the appraisal report shall be submitted to the registration authority for exploration rights and mining rights for archival filing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请转让探矿权、采矿权的，审批管理机关应当自收到转让申请之日起40日内，作出准予转让或者不准转让的决定，并通知转让人和受让人。  
准予转让的，转让人和受让人应当自收到批准转让通知之日起60日内，到原发证机关办理变更登记手续；受让人按照国家规定缴纳有关费用后，领取勘查许可证或者采矿许可证，成为探矿权人或者采矿权人。  
批准转让的，转让合同自批准之日起生效。  
不准转让的，审批管理机关应当说明理由。

**Article 10** Where an application is made for the transfer of an exploration right or mining right, the examination and approval authority shall, within 40 days from the date of receipt of the application for transfer, make a decision on whether to approve the transfer or not, and shall notify the transferor and the transferee.  
If the transfer is approved, the transferor and the transferee shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the notice of approval of transfer, go through the modification registration procedures with the original issuing authority; the transferee shall, after paying the relevant fees in accordance with State provisions, obtain the exploration permit or mining permit and become the exploration right holder or mining right holder.  
Where a transfer is approved, the transfer contract shall take effect from the date of approval.  
If the transfer is not approved, the examination and approval authority shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　审批管理机关批准转让探矿权、采矿权后，应当及时通知原发证机关。

**Article 11** After approving the transfer of an exploration right or mining right, the examination and approval authority shall promptly notify the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　探矿权、采矿权转让后，探矿权人、采矿权人的权利、义务随之转移。

**Article 12** After the transfer of an exploration right or mining right, the rights and obligations of the exploration right holder or mining right holder shall be transferred accordingly.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　探矿权、采矿权转让后，勘查许可证、采矿许可证的有效期限，为原勘查许可证、采矿许可证的有效期减去已经进行勘查、采矿的年限的剩余期限。

**Article 13** After the transfer of an exploration right or mining right, the term of validity of the exploration permit or mining permit shall be the remaining period after deducting the years that have already been spent on exploration or mining from the term of validity of the original exploration permit or mining permit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　未经审批管理机关批准，擅自转让探矿权、采矿权的，由登记管理机关责令改正，没收违法所得，处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销勘查许可证、采矿许可证。

**Article 14** Where anyone, without approval from the examination and approval authority, transfers an exploration right or mining right without authorization, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the exploration permit or mining permit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反[本办法第三条](#art-3)第(二)项的规定，以承包等方式擅自将采矿权转给他人进行采矿的，由县级以上人民政府负责地质矿产管理工作的部门按照国务院地质矿产主管部门规定的权限，责令改正，没收违法所得，处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销采矿许可证。

**Article 15** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 3(2) of these Measures, transfers a mining right to another person for mining by means of contracting or other means without authorization, the department in charge of geology and mineral resources administration under the people's government at or above the county level shall, within the limits of authority prescribed by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council, order it to make corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the mining permit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审批管理机关工作人员徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 16** Where any staff member of the examination and approval authority engages in favoritism, abuses power, or neglects duty, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　探矿权转让申请书、采矿权转让申请书的格式，由国务院地质矿产主管部门统一制定。

**Article 17** The forms of the application for transfer of exploration rights and the application for transfer of mining rights shall be uniformly formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 18** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of issuance.
