# 诉讼费用交纳办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2006-12-19  
**生效日期**：2007-04-01

> (2006年12月8日国务院第159次常务会议通过　2006年12月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第481号公布　自2007年4月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》(以下简称民事诉讼法)和《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》(以下简称行政诉讼法)的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　当事人进行民事诉讼、行政诉讼，应当依照本办法交纳诉讼费用。 本办法规定可以不交纳或者免予交纳诉讼费用的除外。 第三条　在诉讼过程中不得违反本办法规定的范围和标准向当事人收取费用。 第四条　国家对交纳诉讼费用确有困难的当事人提供司法救助，保障其依法行使诉讼权利，维护其合法权益。 第五条　外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织在人民法院进行诉讼，适用本办法。 外国法院对中华人民共和国公民、法人或者其他组织，与其本国公民、法人或者其他组织在诉讼费用交纳上实行差别对待的，按照对等原则处理。 第二章　诉讼费用交纳范围 第六条　当事人应当向人民法院交纳的诉讼费用包括： (一)案件受理费； (二)申请费； (三)证人、鉴定人、翻译人员、理算人员在人民法院指定日期出庭发生的交通费、住宿费、生活费和误工补贴。 第七条　案件受理费包括：

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》(以下简称民事诉讼法)和《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》(以下简称行政诉讼法)的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Procedure Law) and the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Administrative Procedure Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人进行民事诉讼、行政诉讼，应当依照本办法交纳诉讼费用。  
本办法规定可以不交纳或者免予交纳诉讼费用的除外。

**Article 2** A party who engages in a civil action or an administrative action shall pay litigation costs in accordance with these Measures.  
Except as otherwise provided in these Measures that litigation costs may not be paid or shall be exempted.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在诉讼过程中不得违反本办法规定的范围和标准向当事人收取费用。

**Article 3** In the course of litigation, no fees shall be collected from a party in violation of the scope and standards prescribed in these Measures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对交纳诉讼费用确有困难的当事人提供司法救助，保障其依法行使诉讼权利，维护其合法权益。

**Article 4** The State shall provide judicial assistance to a party who has genuine difficulty in paying litigation costs, ensuring that it may exercise its litigation rights according to law and safeguarding its lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织在人民法院进行诉讼，适用本办法。  
外国法院对中华人民共和国公民、法人或者其他组织，与其本国公民、法人或者其他组织在诉讼费用交纳上实行差别对待的，按照对等原则处理。

**Article 5** These Measures shall apply to a foreigner, a stateless person, a foreign enterprise or organization that conducts litigation in a People's Court.  
If a foreign court implements differential treatment with respect to the payment of litigation costs against a citizen, legal person or other organization of the People's Republic of China as compared with its own citizens, legal persons or other organizations, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the principle of reciprocity.

### 第二章　诉讼费用交纳范围

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人应当向人民法院交纳的诉讼费用包括：  
(一)案件受理费；  
(二)申请费；  
(三)证人、鉴定人、翻译人员、理算人员在人民法院指定日期出庭发生的交通费、住宿费、生活费和误工补贴。

**Article 6** The litigation costs that a party shall pay to the People's Court include:  
(1) case acceptance fees;  
(2) application fees;  
(3) transportation expenses, accommodation expenses, living expenses and subsidies for loss of work incurred by witnesses, expert evaluators, interpreters and adjusters who appear in court on the date designated by the People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　案件受理费包括：  
(一)第一审案件受理费；  
(二)第二审案件受理费；  
(三)再审案件中，依照本办法规定需要交纳的案件受理费。

**Article 7** Case acceptance fees include:  
(1) case acceptance fees for first instance cases;  
(2) case acceptance fees for second instance cases;  
(3) case acceptance fees that shall be paid in retrial cases in accordance with the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下列案件不交纳案件受理费：  
(一)依照民事诉讼法规定的特别程序审理的案件；  
(二)裁定不予受理、驳回起诉、驳回上诉的案件；  
(三)对不予受理、驳回起诉和管辖权异议裁定不服，提起上诉的案件；  
(四)行政赔偿案件。

**Article 8** No case acceptance fee shall be paid for the following cases:  
(1) cases tried in accordance with the special procedures prescribed in the Civil Procedure Law;  
(2) cases where a ruling is made not to accept the case, to dismiss the prosecution, or to dismiss the appeal;  
(3) cases where an appeal is filed against a ruling on non-acceptance, dismissal of prosecution, or objection to jurisdiction;  
(4) administrative compensation cases.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　根据民事诉讼法和行政诉讼法规定的审判监督程序审理的案件，当事人不交纳案件受理费。但是，下列情形除外：  
(一)当事人有新的证据，足以推翻原判决、裁定，向人民法院申请再审，人民法院经审查决定再审的案件；  
(二)当事人对人民法院第一审判决或者裁定未提出上诉，第一审判决、裁定或者调解书发生法律效力后又申请再审，人民法院经审查决定再审的案件。

**Article 9** For cases tried under the procedure for trial supervision prescribed in the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law, a party shall not pay a case acceptance fee, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) a case where a party has new evidence sufficient to overturn the original judgment or ruling, applies to the People's Court for a retrial, and the People's Court, after examination, decides to conduct a retrial;  
(2) a case where a party did not appeal against the first instance judgment or ruling of the People's Court, but applies for a retrial after the first instance judgment, ruling or mediation statement has taken legal effect, and the People's Court, after examination, decides to conduct a retrial.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人依法向人民法院申请下列事项，应当交纳申请费：  
(一)申请执行人民法院发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书，仲裁机构依法作出的裁决和调解书，公证机构依法赋予强制执行效力的债权文书；  
(二)申请保全措施；  
(三)申请支付令；  
(四)申请公示催告；  
(五)申请撤销仲裁裁决或者认定仲裁协议效力；  
(六)申请破产；  
(七)申请海事强制令、共同海损理算、设立海事赔偿责任限制基金、海事债权登记、船舶优先权催告；  
(八)申请承认和执行外国法院判决、裁定和国外仲裁机构裁决。

**Article 10** A party who applies to the People's Court according to law for any of the following matters shall pay an application fee:  
(1) application for enforcement of a legally effective judgment, ruling or mediation statement of the People's Court, an award or mediation statement lawfully made by an arbitral institution, or a notarized creditor's document to which a notarial institution has lawfully given compulsory enforcement effect;  
(2) application for preservation measures;  
(3) application for a payment order;  
(4) application for public summons for exhortation;  
(5) application for setting aside an arbitral award or affirming the validity of an arbitration agreement;  
(6) application for bankruptcy;  
(7) application for a maritime injunction, general average adjustment, establishment of a maritime liability limitation fund, registration of maritime claims, or a warning call on maritime liens;  
(8) application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment or ruling of a foreign court or an award of a foreign arbitral institution.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　证人、鉴定人、翻译人员、理算人员在人民法院指定日期出庭发生的交通费、住宿费、生活费和误工补贴，由人民法院按照国家规定标准代为收取。  
当事人复制案件卷宗材料和法律文书应当按实际成本向人民法院交纳工本费。

**Article 11** The transportation expenses, accommodation expenses, living expenses and subsidies for loss of work incurred by witnesses, expert evaluators, interpreters and adjusters who appear in court on the date designated by the People's Court shall be collected by the People's Court on their behalf in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State.  
A party who duplicates case files and legal documents shall pay the production costs to the People's Court based on the actual cost.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　诉讼过程中因鉴定、公告、勘验、翻译、评估、拍卖、变卖、仓储、保管、运输、船舶监管等发生的依法应当由当事人负担的费用，人民法院根据谁主张、谁负担的原则，决定由当事人直接支付给有关机构或者单位，人民法院不得代收代付。  
人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-11)第三款规定提供当地民族通用语言、文字翻译的，不收取费用。

**Article 12** For expenses incurred during the litigation process due to expert evaluation, public announcement, inspection, translation, appraisal, auction, sale, storage, custody, transportation, ship supervision, etc., which shall be borne by a party according to law, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the principle that the party who asserts bears the cost, decide that the party shall directly pay such expenses to the relevant institution or unit, and the People's Court shall not collect or pay on behalf of the party.  
Where the People's Court provides translation in the common local ethnic language or script in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 11 of the Civil Procedure Law, no fee shall be charged.

### 第三章　诉讼费用交纳标准

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　案件受理费分别按照下列标准交纳：  
(一)财产案件根据诉讼请求的金额或者价额，按照下列比例分段累计交纳：  
1．不超过1万元的，每件交纳50元；  
2．超过1万元至10万元的部分，按照2.5%交纳；  
3．超过10万元至20万元的部分，按照2%交纳；  
4．超过20万元至50万元的部分，按照1.5%交纳；  
5．超过50万元至100万元的部分，按照1%交纳；  
6．超过100万元至200万元的部分，按照0.9%交纳；  
7．超过200万元至500万元的部分，按照0.8%交纳；  
8．超过500万元至1000万元的部分，按照0.7%交纳；  
9．超过1000万元至2000万元的部分，按照0.6%交纳；  
10．超过2000万元的部分，按照0.5%交纳。  
(二)非财产案件按照下列标准交纳：  
1．离婚案件每件交纳50元至300元。涉及财产分割，财产总额不超过20万元的，不另行交纳；超过20万元的部分，按照0.5%交纳。  
2．侵害姓名权、名称权、肖像权、名誉权、荣誉权以及其他人格权的案件，每件交纳100元至500元。涉及损害赔偿，赔偿金额不超过5万元的，不另行交纳；超过5万元至10万元的部分，按照1%交纳；超过10万元的部分，按照0.5%交纳。  
3．其他非财产案件每件交纳50元至100元。  
(三)知识产权民事案件，没有争议金额或者价额的，每件交纳500元至1000元；有争议金额或者价额的，按照财产案件的标准交纳。  
(四)劳动争议案件每件交纳10元。  
(五)行政案件按照下列标准交纳：  
1．商标、专利、海事行政案件每件交纳100元；  
2．其他行政案件每件交纳50元。  
(六)当事人提出案件管辖权异议，异议不成立的，每件交纳50元至100元。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以结合本地实际情况在本条第(二)项、第(三)项、第(六)项规定的幅度内制定具体交纳标准。

**Article 13** Case acceptance fees shall be paid respectively in accordance with the following standards:  
(1) For property cases, the fee shall be paid cumulatively in segments according to the following percentages based on the amount or value of the claim:  
1. Not exceeding 10,000 yuan, 50 yuan per case;  
2. The portion exceeding 10,000 yuan up to 100,000 yuan shall be paid at 2.5%;  
3. The portion exceeding 100,000 yuan up to 200,000 yuan shall be paid at 2%;  
4. The portion exceeding 200,000 yuan up to 500,000 yuan shall be paid at 1.5%;  
5. The portion exceeding 500,000 yuan up to 1,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 1%;  
6. The portion exceeding 1,000,000 yuan up to 2,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.9%;  
7. The portion exceeding 2,000,000 yuan up to 5,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.8%;  
8. The portion exceeding 5,000,000 yuan up to 10,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.7%;  
9. The portion exceeding 10,000,000 yuan up to 20,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.6%;  
10. The portion exceeding 20,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.5%.  
(2) For non-property cases, the fee shall be paid in accordance with the following standards:  
1. For divorce cases, 50 yuan to 300 yuan per case. Where property division is involved and the total property does not exceed 200,000 yuan, no additional fee shall be paid; for the portion exceeding 200,000 yuan, the fee shall be paid at 0.5%.  
2. For cases involving infringement of the right to name, right to appellation, right to portrait, right to reputation, right to honor, and other personality rights, 100 yuan to 500 yuan per case. Where compensation for damages is involved and the amount of compensation does not exceed 50,000 yuan, no additional fee shall be paid; for the portion exceeding 50,000 yuan up to 100,000 yuan, the fee shall be paid at 1%; for the portion exceeding 100,000 yuan, the fee shall be paid at 0.5%.  
3. For other non-property cases, 50 yuan to 100 yuan per case.  
(3) For civil intellectual property cases, if there is no disputed amount or value, 500 yuan to 1,000 yuan per case; if there is a disputed amount or value, the fee shall be paid in accordance with the standards for property cases.  
(4) For labor dispute cases, 10 yuan per case.  
(5) For administrative cases, the fee shall be paid in accordance with the following standards:  
1. For trademark, patent, and maritime administrative cases, 100 yuan per case;  
2. For other administrative cases, 50 yuan per case.  
(6) Where a party raises an objection to the jurisdiction of a case and the objection is not sustained, 50 yuan to 100 yuan per case.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, taking into account the local实际情况, formulate specific payment standards within the ranges prescribed in subparagraphs (2), (3) and (6) of this Article.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请费分别按照下列标准交纳：  
(一)依法向人民法院申请执行人民法院发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书，仲裁机构依法作出的裁决和调解书，公证机关依法赋予强制执行效力的债权文书，申请承认和执行外国法院判决、裁定以及国外仲裁机构裁决的，按照下列标准交纳：  
1．没有执行金额或者价额的，每件交纳50元至500元。  
2．执行金额或者价额不超过1万元的，每件交纳50元；超过1万元至50万元的部分，按照1.5%交纳；超过50万元至500万元的部分，按照1%交纳；超过500万元至1000万元的部分，按照0.5%交纳；超过1000万元的部分，按照0.1%交纳。  
3．符合[民事诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-55)第四款规定，未参加登记的权利人向人民法院提起诉讼的，按照本项规定的标准交纳申请费，不再交纳案件受理费。  
(二)申请保全措施的，根据实际保全的财产数额按照下列标准交纳：  
财产数额不超过1000元或者不涉及财产数额的，每件交纳30元；超过1000元至10万元的部分，按照1%交纳；超过10万元的部分，按照0.5%交纳。但是，当事人申请保全措施交纳的费用最多不超过5000元。  
(三)依法申请支付令的，比照财产案件受理费标准的1/3交纳。  
(四)依法申请公示催告的，每件交纳100元。  
(五)申请撤销仲裁裁决或者认定仲裁协议效力的，每件交纳400元。  
(六)破产案件依据破产财产总额计算，按照财产案件受理费标准减半交纳，但是，最高不超过30万元。  
(七)海事案件的申请费按照下列标准交纳：  
1．申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，每件交纳1000元至1万元；  
2．申请海事强制令的，每件交纳1000元至5000元；  
3．申请船舶优先权催告的，每件交纳1000元至5000元；  
4．申请海事债权登记的，每件交纳1000元；  
5．申请共同海损理算的，每件交纳1000元。

**Article 14** Application fees shall be paid respectively in accordance with the following standards:  
(1) For applications filed according to law to a People's Court for enforcement of a legally effective judgment, ruling or mediation statement of the People's Court, an award or mediation statement lawfully made by an arbitral institution, or a notarized creditor's document to which a notarial authority has lawfully given compulsory enforcement effect, and for applications for recognition and enforcement of a judgment or ruling of a foreign court or an award of a foreign arbitral institution, the fee shall be paid in accordance with the following standards:  
1. If there is no amount or value for enforcement, 50 yuan to 500 yuan per case.  
2. If the amount or value for enforcement does not exceed 10,000 yuan, 50 yuan per case; the portion exceeding 10,000 yuan up to 500,000 yuan shall be paid at 1.5%; the portion exceeding 500,000 yuan up to 5,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 1%; the portion exceeding 5,000,000 yuan up to 10,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.5%; the portion exceeding 10,000,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.1%.  
3. Where, in compliance with paragraph 4 of Article 55 of the Civil Procedure Law, a right holder who has not participated in the registration institutes an action in the People's Court, the application fee shall be paid in accordance with the standards prescribed in this subparagraph, and no case acceptance fee shall be paid.  
(2) For applications for preservation measures, the fee shall be paid in accordance with the following standards based on the actual amount of property preserved:  
If the amount of property does not exceed 1,000 yuan or if no property amount is involved, 30 yuan per case; the portion exceeding 1,000 yuan up to 100,000 yuan shall be paid at 1%; the portion exceeding 100,000 yuan shall be paid at 0.5%. However, the fee paid by a party for applying for preservation measures shall not exceed 5,000 yuan at most.  
(3) For applications for a payment order according to law, the fee shall be paid by reference to one-third of the standard for case acceptance fees for property cases.  
(4) For applications for public summons for exhortation according to law, 100 yuan per case.  
(5) For applications for setting aside an arbitral award or affirming the validity of an arbitration agreement, 400 yuan per case.  
(6) For bankruptcy cases, the fee shall be calculated based on the total amount of bankruptcy property and shall be paid at half the standard for case acceptance fees for property cases, but shall not exceed 300,000 yuan at most.  
(7) Application fees for maritime cases shall be paid in accordance with the following standards:  
1. For applications for establishment of a maritime liability limitation fund, 1,000 yuan to 10,000 yuan per case;  
2. For applications for a maritime injunction, 1,000 yuan to 5,000 yuan per case;  
3. For applications for a warning call on maritime liens, 1,000 yuan to 5,000 yuan per case;  
4. For applications for registration of maritime claims, 1,000 yuan per case;  
5. For applications for general average adjustment, 1,000 yuan per case.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　以调解方式结案或者当事人申请撤诉的，减半交纳案件受理费。

**Article 15** Where a case is concluded by mediation or a party applies for withdrawal of the action, the case acceptance fee shall be paid at half the rate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　适用简易程序审理的案件减半交纳案件受理费。

**Article 16** For cases tried under the summary procedure, the case acceptance fee shall be paid at half the rate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对财产案件提起上诉的，按照不服一审判决部分的上诉请求数额交纳案件受理费。

**Article 17** Where an appeal is filed against a property case, the case acceptance fee shall be paid according to the amount of the appellate claim that does not accept the part of the first instance judgment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被告提起反诉、有独立请求权的第三人提出与本案有关的诉讼请求，人民法院决定合并审理的，分别减半交纳案件受理费。

**Article 18** Where the defendant files a counterclaim or a third party with independent claims raises a claim related to the case, and the People's Court decides to consolidate the trials, the case acceptance fees shall be paid respectively at half the rate.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　依照[本办法第九条](#art-9)规定需要交纳案件受理费的再审案件，按照不服原判决部分的再审请求数额交纳案件受理费。

**Article 19** For a retrial case in which a case acceptance fee shall be paid in accordance with Article 9 of these Measures, the case acceptance fee shall be paid according to the amount of the retrial claim that does not accept the part of the original judgment.

### 第四章　诉讼费用的交纳和退还

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　案件受理费由原告、有独立请求权的第三人、上诉人预交。被告提起反诉，依照本办法规定需要交纳案件受理费的，由被告预交。追索劳动报酬的案件可以不预交案件受理费。  
申请费由申请人预交。但是，[本办法第十条](#art-10)第(一)项、第(六)项规定的申请费不由申请人预交，执行申请费执行后交纳，破产申请费清算后交纳。  
[本办法第十一条](#art-11)规定的费用，待实际发生后交纳。

**Article 20** The case acceptance fee shall be paid in advance by the plaintiff, the third party with independent claims, or the appellant. Where the defendant files a counterclaim and a case acceptance fee shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, the defendant shall pay such fee in advance. For cases involving claims for labor remuneration, the case acceptance fee may not be paid in advance.  
The application fee shall be paid in advance by the applicant. However, the application fees prescribed in subparagraphs (1) and (6) of Article 10 of these Measures shall not be paid in advance by the applicant; the application fee for enforcement shall be paid after enforcement, and the application fee for bankruptcy shall be paid after liquidation.  
The fees prescribed in Article 11 of these Measures shall be paid after they are actually incurred.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人在诉讼中变更诉讼请求数额，案件受理费依照下列规定处理：  
(一)当事人增加诉讼请求数额的，按照增加后的诉讼请求数额计算补交；  
(二)当事人在法庭调查终结前提出减少诉讼请求数额的，按照减少后的诉讼请求数额计算退还。

**Article 21** Where a party changes the amount of its claims in an action, the case acceptance fee shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If a party increases the amount of its claims, the party shall make up the difference by paying additional fees calculated based on the increased amount of claims;  
(2) If a party, before the conclusion of court investigation, proposes to reduce the amount of its claims, the party shall have the fees refunded calculated based on the reduced amount of claims.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　原告自接到人民法院交纳诉讼费用通知次日起7日内交纳案件受理费；反诉案件由提起反诉的当事人自提起反诉次日起7日内交纳案件受理费。  
上诉案件的案件受理费由上诉人向人民法院提交上诉状时预交。双方当事人都提起上诉的，分别预交。上诉人在上诉期内未预交诉讼费用的，人民法院应当通知其在7日内预交。  
申请费由申请人在提出申请时或者在人民法院指定的期限内预交。  
当事人逾期不交纳诉讼费用又未提出司法救助申请，或者申请司法救助未获批准，在人民法院指定期限内仍未交纳诉讼费用的，由人民法院依照有关规定处理。

**Article 22** A plaintiff shall pay the case acceptance fee within seven days from the day following the date of receipt of the notice on payment of litigation costs from the People's Court; in a counterclaim case, the party filing the counterclaim shall pay the case acceptance fee within seven days from the day following the date of filing the counterclaim.  
The case acceptance fee for an appeal case shall be prepaid by the appellant when submitting the appeal petition to the People's Court. Where both parties file an appeal, they shall each prepay the fee. If an appellant fails to prepay the litigation costs within the appeal period, the People's Court shall notify the appellant to prepay within seven days.  
The application fee shall be prepaid by the applicant at the time of filing the application or within the period designated by the People's Court.  
Where a party fails to pay litigation costs on time and does not apply for judicial assistance, or the application for judicial assistance is not approved, and the party still fails to pay the litigation costs within the period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　依照[本办法第九条](#art-9)规定需要交纳案件受理费的再审案件，由申请再审的当事人预交。双方当事人都申请再审的，分别预交。

**Article 23** For a retrial case that requires payment of a case acceptance fee in accordance with Article 9 of these Measures, the fee shall be prepaid by the party applying for retrial. Where both parties apply for retrial, they shall each prepay the fee.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　依照[民事诉讼法第三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-36)、第三十七条、第三十八条、第三十九条规定移送、移交的案件，原受理人民法院应当将当事人预交的诉讼费用随案移交接收案件的人民法院。

**Article 24** For a case transferred or referred in accordance with Articles 36, 37, 38, and 39 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court that originally accepted the case shall transfer the litigation costs prepaid by the party together with the case file to the People's Court that receives the case.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民法院审理民事案件过程中发现涉嫌刑事犯罪并将案件移送有关部门处理的，当事人交纳的案件受理费予以退还；移送后民事案件需要继续审理的，当事人已交纳的案件受理费不予退还。

**Article 25** Where, during the trial of a civil case, the People's Court finds that criminal offenses are suspected and transfers the case to the relevant authorities for handling, the case acceptance fee paid by the party shall be refunded; if the civil case needs to continue to be tried after the transfer, the case acceptance fee already paid by the party shall not be refunded.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中止诉讼、中止执行的案件，已交纳的案件受理费、申请费不予退还。中止诉讼、中止执行的原因消除，恢复诉讼、执行的，不再交纳案件受理费、申请费。

**Article 26** For a case where the action or enforcement is suspended, the case acceptance fee and application fee already paid shall not be refunded. Where the cause for suspension of action or enforcement is eliminated and the action or enforcement is resumed, no case acceptance fee or application fee shall be paid.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　第二审人民法院决定将案件发回重审的，应当退还上诉人已交纳的第二审案件受理费。  
第一审人民法院裁定不予受理或者驳回起诉的，应当退还当事人已交纳的案件受理费；当事人对第一审人民法院不予受理、驳回起诉的裁定提起上诉，第二审人民法院维持第一审人民法院作出的裁定的，第一审人民法院应当退还当事人已交纳的案件受理费。

**Article 27** Where the People's Court of second instance decides to remand the case for retrial, it shall refund the case acceptance fee of second instance already paid by the appellant.  
Where the People's Court of first instance rules to dismiss the case or reject the complaint, it shall refund the case acceptance fee already paid by the party; where a party appeals against the ruling of the People's Court of first instance to dismiss the case or reject the complaint, and the People's Court of second instance sustains the ruling made by the People's Court of first instance, the People's Court of first instance shall refund the case acceptance fee already paid by the party.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　依照[民事诉讼法第一百三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-137)规定终结诉讼的案件，依照本办法规定已交纳的案件受理费不予退还。

**Article 28** For a case where the action is terminated in accordance with Article 137 of the Civil Procedure Law, the case acceptance fee already paid in accordance with these Measures shall not be refunded.

### 第五章　诉讼费用的负担

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　诉讼费用由败诉方负担，胜诉方自愿承担的除外。  
部分胜诉、部分败诉的，人民法院根据案件的具体情况决定当事人各自负担的诉讼费用数额。  
共同诉讼当事人败诉的，人民法院根据其对诉讼标的的利害关系，决定当事人各自负担的诉讼费用数额。

**Article 29** The litigation costs shall be borne by the losing party, unless the winning party voluntarily bears them.  
Where a party prevails in part and loses in part, the People's Court shall determine the amount of litigation costs to be borne by each party based on the specific circumstances of the case.  
Where a party in a joint action loses the case, the People's Court shall determine the amount of litigation costs to be borne by each party based on their respective interest in the subject matter of the action.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　第二审人民法院改变第一审人民法院作出的判决、裁定的，应当相应变更第一审人民法院对诉讼费用负担的决定。

**Article 30** Where the People's Court of second instance modifies the judgment or ruling made by the People's Court of first instance, it shall correspondingly modify the decision of the People's Court of first instance on the allocation of litigation costs.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　经人民法院调解达成协议的案件，诉讼费用的负担由双方当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由人民法院决定。

**Article 31** For a case where an agreement is reached through mediation by the People's Court, the allocation of litigation costs shall be resolved through consultation between the parties; if consultation fails, the People's Court shall decide.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依照[本办法第九条](#art-9)第(一)项、第(二)项的规定应当交纳案件受理费的再审案件，诉讼费用由申请再审的当事人负担；双方当事人都申请再审的，诉讼费用依照[本办法第二十九条](#art-29)的规定负担。原审诉讼费用的负担由人民法院根据诉讼费用负担原则重新确定。

**Article 32** For a retrial case for which a case acceptance fee shall be paid in accordance with subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 9 of these Measures, the litigation costs shall be borne by the party applying for retrial; where both parties apply for retrial, the litigation costs shall be allocated in accordance with Article 29 of these Measures. The allocation of litigation costs of the original trial shall be re-determined by the People's Court in accordance with the principles of allocation of litigation costs.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　离婚案件诉讼费用的负担由双方当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由人民法院决定。

**Article 33** For a divorce case, the allocation of litigation costs shall be resolved through consultation between the parties; if consultation fails, the People's Court shall decide.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　民事案件的原告或者上诉人申请撤诉，人民法院裁定准许的，案件受理费由原告或者上诉人负担。  
行政案件的被告改变或者撤销具体行政行为，原告申请撤诉，人民法院裁定准许的，案件受理费由被告负担。

**Article 34** Where the plaintiff or appellant in a civil case applies for withdrawal of the action, and the People's Court rules to permit it, the case acceptance fee shall be borne by the plaintiff or appellant.  
Where the defendant in an administrative case modifies or revokes the specific administrative act, and the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the action, and the People's Court rules to permit it, the case acceptance fee shall be borne by the defendant.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人在法庭调查终结后提出减少诉讼请求数额的，减少请求数额部分的案件受理费由变更诉讼请求的当事人负担。

**Article 35** Where a party proposes to reduce the amount of its claims after the conclusion of court investigation, the case acceptance fee for the portion of the reduced claims shall be borne by the party that changes the claims.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　债务人对督促程序未提出异议的，申请费由债务人负担。债务人对督促程序提出异议致使督促程序终结的，申请费由申请人负担；申请人另行起诉的，可以将申请费列入诉讼请求。

**Article 36** Where the debtor raises no objection to the supervision procedure, the application fee shall be borne by the debtor. Where the debtor raises an objection to the supervision procedure, thereby causing the termination of the supervision procedure, the application fee shall be borne by the applicant; if the applicant separately files an action, the applicant may include the application fee in the claims.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公示催告的申请费由申请人负担。

**Article 37** The application fee for public notice of exhortation shall be borne by the applicant.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　[本办法第十条](#art-10)第(一)项、第(八)项规定的申请费由被执行人负担。  
执行中当事人达成和解协议的，申请费的负担由双方当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由人民法院决定。  
[本办法第十条](#art-10)第(二)项规定的申请费由申请人负担，申请人提起诉讼的，可以将该申请费列入诉讼请求。  
[本办法第十条](#art-10)第(五)项规定的申请费，由人民法院依照[本办法第二十九条](#art-29)规定决定申请费的负担。

**Article 38** The application fee specified in subparagraphs (1) and (8) of Article 10 of these Measures shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement.  
Where the parties reach a settlement agreement during enforcement, the allocation of the application fee shall be resolved through consultation between the parties; if consultation fails, the People's Court shall decide.  
The application fee specified in subparagraph (2) of Article 10 of these Measures shall be borne by the applicant; if the applicant files an action, the applicant may include the application fee in the claims.  
The application fee specified in subparagraph (5) of Article 10 of these Measures shall be allocated by the People's Court in accordance with Article 29 of these Measures.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　海事案件中的有关诉讼费用依照下列规定负担：  
(一)诉前申请海事请求保全、海事强制令的，申请费由申请人负担；申请人就有关海事请求提起诉讼的，可将上述费用列入诉讼请求；  
(二)诉前申请海事证据保全的，申请费由申请人负担；  
(三)诉讼中拍卖、变卖被扣押船舶、船载货物、船用燃油、船用物料发生的合理费用，由申请人预付，从拍卖、变卖价款中先行扣除，退还申请人；  
(四)申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金、申请债权登记与受偿、申请船舶优先权催告案件的申请费，由申请人负担；  
(五)设立海事赔偿责任限制基金、船舶优先权催告程序中的公告费用由申请人负担。

**Article 39** The relevant litigation costs in maritime cases shall be borne in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For a pre-litigation application for maritime claim preservation or a maritime injunction, the application fee shall be borne by the applicant; if the applicant files an action regarding the relevant maritime claim, the applicant may include the above fees in the claims;  
(2) For a pre-litigation application for maritime evidence preservation, the application fee shall be borne by the applicant;  
(3) Reasonable expenses incurred during litigation for the auction or sale of arrested vessels, cargo on board, vessel fuel, or vessel supplies shall be advanced by the applicant, deducted from the auction or sale proceeds, and refunded to the applicant;  
(4) The application fee for applying for the establishment of a maritime liability limitation fund, applying for registration of claims and participation in distribution, or applying for public notice of maritime liens shall be borne by the applicant;  
(5) The publication expenses in the procedure for establishing a maritime liability limitation fund or public notice of maritime liens shall be borne by the applicant.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人因自身原因未能在举证期限内举证，在二审或者再审期间提出新的证据致使诉讼费用增加的，增加的诉讼费用由该当事人负担。

**Article 40** Where a party fails to present evidence within the time limit for evidence submission due to its own reasons, and presents new evidence during the second instance or retrial, thereby causing an increase in litigation costs, the increased litigation costs shall be borne by that party.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　依照特别程序审理案件的公告费，由起诉人或者申请人负担。

**Article 41** The announcement fees for cases tried under special procedures shall be borne by the plaintiff or the applicant.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　依法向人民法院申请破产的，诉讼费用依照有关法律规定从破产财产中拨付。

**Article 42** Where an application for bankruptcy is filed with a People's Court according to law, the litigation costs shall be allocated from the bankruptcy property in accordance with relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　当事人不得单独对人民法院关于诉讼费用的决定提起上诉。  
当事人单独对人民法院关于诉讼费用的决定有异议的，可以向作出决定的人民法院院长申请复核。复核决定应当自收到当事人申请之日起15日内作出。  
当事人对人民法院决定诉讼费用的计算有异议的，可以向作出决定的人民法院请求复核。计算确有错误的，作出决定的人民法院应当予以更正。

**Article 43** A party shall not separately file an appeal against a People's Court's decision on litigation costs.  
If a party has an objection to a People's Court's decision on litigation costs separately, the party may apply to the president of the People's Court that made the decision for a review. A review decision shall be made within 15 days from the date of receipt of the party's application.  
If a party has an objection to the calculation of litigation costs as determined by a People's Court, the party may request a review from the People's Court that made the decision. If the calculation is indeed erroneous, the People's Court that made the decision shall correct it.

### 第六章　司法救助

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人交纳诉讼费用确有困难的，可以依照本办法向人民法院申请缓交、减交或者免交诉讼费用的司法救助。  
诉讼费用的免交只适用于自然人。

**Article 44** If a party truly has difficulty paying litigation costs, the party may, in accordance with these Measures, apply to the People's Court for judicial assistance by deferring, reducing, or exempting the payment of litigation costs.  
Exemption from litigation costs shall only apply to natural persons.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　当事人申请司法救助，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当准予免交诉讼费用：  
(一)残疾人无固定生活来源的；  
(二)追索赡养费、扶养费、抚育费、抚恤金的；  
(三)最低生活保障对象、农村特困定期救济对象、农村五保供养对象或者领取失业保险金人员，无其他收入的；  
(四)因见义勇为或者为保护社会公共利益致使自身合法权益受到损害，本人或者其近亲属请求赔偿或者补偿的；  
(五)确实需要免交的其他情形。

**Article 45** If a party applies for judicial assistance and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant exemption from litigation costs:  
(1) The disabled person has no stable source of income;  
(2) Claiming alimony, maintenance, child-rearing expenses, or pensions;  
(3) Persons receiving minimum subsistence allowance, persons receiving regular rural poverty relief, persons receiving rural five-guarantee support, or persons receiving unemployment insurance benefits, with no other income;  
(4) Where one's own legitimate rights and interests are damaged due to acting courageously for a just cause or protecting the public interest, and the person or their close relatives claim compensation or indemnification;  
(5) Other circumstances where exemption is truly necessary.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　当事人申请司法救助，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当准予减交诉讼费用：  
(一)因自然灾害等不可抗力造成生活困难，正在接受社会救济，或者家庭生产经营难以为继的；  
(二)属于国家规定的优抚、安置对象的；  
(三)社会福利机构和救助管理站；  
(四)确实需要减交的其他情形。  
人民法院准予减交诉讼费用的，减交比例不得低于30%。

**Article 46** If a party applies for judicial assistance and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant a reduction of litigation costs:  
(1) Living difficulties caused by force majeure such as natural disasters, and currently receiving social relief, or household production and operation are unsustainable;  
(2) Persons who are entitled to preferential treatment and resettlement under state provisions;  
(3) Social welfare institutions and relief management stations;  
(4) Other circumstances where reduction is truly necessary.  
Where the People's Court grants a reduction of litigation costs, the reduction ratio shall not be less than 30%.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　当事人申请司法救助，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当准予缓交诉讼费用：  
(一)追索社会保险金、经济补偿金的；  
(二)海上事故、交通事故、医疗事故、工伤事故、产品质量事故或者其他人身伤害事故的受害人请求赔偿的；  
(三)正在接受有关部门法律援助的；  
(四)确实需要缓交的其他情形。

**Article 47** If a party applies for judicial assistance and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant a deferral of litigation costs:  
(1) Claiming social insurance benefits and economic compensation;  
(2) Victims of maritime accidents, traffic accidents, medical accidents, work-related injury accidents, product quality accidents, or other personal injury accidents claim compensation;  
(3) Currently receiving legal aid from relevant authorities;  
(4) Other circumstances where deferral is truly necessary.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　当事人申请司法救助，应当在起诉或者上诉时提交书面申请、足以证明其确有经济困难的证明材料以及其他相关证明材料。  
因生活困难或者追索基本生活费用申请免交、减交诉讼费用的，还应当提供本人及其家庭经济状况符合当地民政、劳动保障等部门规定的公民经济困难标准的证明。  
人民法院对当事人的司法救助申请不予批准的，应当向当事人书面说明理由。

**Article 48** When a party applies for judicial assistance, the party shall submit a written application, evidentiary materials sufficient to prove genuine economic difficulties, and other relevant evidentiary materials at the time of prosecution or appeal.  
If applying for exemption or reduction of litigation costs due to living difficulties or claiming basic living expenses, the party shall also provide proof that the economic conditions of the party and their family meet the citizen economic hardship standards prescribed by local civil affairs, labor and social security departments and other relevant authorities.  
If the People's Court does not approve a party's application for judicial assistance, it shall provide a written explanation to the party.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　当事人申请缓交诉讼费用经审查符合[本办法第四十七条](#art-47)规定的，人民法院应当在决定立案之前作出准予缓交的决定。

**Article 49** If, upon review, a party's application for deferral of litigation costs conforms to the provisions of Article 47 of these Measures, the People's Court shall make a decision to grant the deferral before deciding to accept the case.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民法院对一方当事人提供司法救助，对方当事人败诉的，诉讼费用由对方当事人负担；对方当事人胜诉的，可以视申请司法救助的当事人的经济状况决定其减交、免交诉讼费用。

**Article 50** If the People's Court provides judicial assistance to one party and the opposing party loses the case, the litigation costs shall be borne by the opposing party; if the opposing party wins the case, the court may decide to reduce or exempt the litigation costs of the party who applied for judicial assistance based on that party's economic conditions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民法院准予当事人减交、免交诉讼费用的，应当在法律文书中载明。

**Article 51** Where the People's Court grants a reduction or exemption of litigation costs to a party, it shall record such in the legal documents.

### 第七章　诉讼费用的管理和监督

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　诉讼费用的交纳和收取制度应当公示。人民法院收取诉讼费用按照其财务隶属关系使用国务院财政部门或者省级人民政府财政部门印制的财政票据。案件受理费、申请费全额上缴财政，纳入预算，实行收支两条线管理。  
人民法院收取诉讼费用应当向当事人开具缴费凭证，当事人持缴费凭证到指定代理银行交费。依法应当向当事人退费的，人民法院应当按照国家有关规定办理。诉讼费用缴库和退费的具体办法由国务院财政部门商最高人民法院另行制定。  
在边远、水上、交通不便地区，基层巡回法庭当场审理案件，当事人提出向指定代理银行交纳诉讼费用确有困难的，基层巡回法庭可以当场收取诉讼费用，并向当事人出具省级人民政府财政部门印制的财政票据；不出具省级人民政府财政部门印制的财政票据的，当事人有权拒绝交纳。

**Article 52** The system for payment and collection of litigation costs shall be made public. A People's Court shall, in accordance with its financial subordination, use financial receipts printed by the finance department of the State Council or the finance department of a provincial people's government for collecting litigation costs. Case acceptance fees and application fees shall be fully turned over to the treasury, incorporated into the budget, and managed through separate revenue and expenditure channels.  
Where a People's Court collects litigation costs, it shall issue a payment voucher to the party, and the party shall pay the fees at the designated agent bank with the payment voucher. Where fees are to be refunded to a party according to law, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant state provisions. The specific methods for depositing litigation costs into the treasury and refunding fees shall be separately formulated by the finance department of the State Council in consultation with the Supreme People's Court.  
In remote areas, on water, or in areas with inconvenient transportation, if a grassroots circuit tribunal hears a case on the spot and a party claims that it is truly difficult to pay litigation costs at the designated agent bank, the grassroots circuit tribunal may collect the litigation costs on the spot and issue to the party a financial receipt printed by the finance department of the provincial people's government; if no such financial receipt is issued, the party shall have the right to refuse payment.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　案件审结后，人民法院应当将诉讼费用的详细清单和当事人应当负担的数额书面通知当事人，同时在判决书、裁定书或者调解书中写明当事人各方应当负担的数额。  
需要向当事人退还诉讼费用的，人民法院应当自法律文书生效之日起15日内退还有关当事人。

**Article 53** After a case is concluded, the People's Court shall notify the parties in writing of the detailed list of litigation costs and the amounts to be borne by the parties, and shall also specify in the judgment, ruling, or mediation document the amounts to be borne by each party.  
If litigation costs are to be refunded to a party, the People's Court shall refund the relevant party within 15 days from the date the legal document takes effect.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　价格主管部门、财政部门按照收费管理的职责分工，对诉讼费用进行管理和监督；对违反本办法规定的乱收费行为，依照法律、法规和国务院相关规定予以查处。

**Article 54** The price administrative authorities and finance departments shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities for fee management, manage and supervise litigation costs; any act of illegal fee collection in violation of the provisions of these Measures shall be investigated and punished in accordance with laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State Council.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　诉讼费用以人民币为计算单位。以外币为计算单位的，依照人民法院决定受理案件之日国家公布的汇率换算成人民币计算交纳；上诉案件和申请再审案件的诉讼费用，按照第一审人民法院决定受理案件之日国家公布的汇率换算。

**Article 55** Litigation costs shall be denominated in Renminbi. If denominated in foreign currency, they shall be converted into Renminbi for payment at the exchange rate published by the state on the date on which the People's Court decides to accept the case; for appeal cases and cases applying for retrial, the litigation costs shall be converted at the exchange rate published by the state on the date on which the People's Court of first instance decides to accept the case.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本办法自2007年4月1日起施行。

**Article 56** These Measures shall come into force as of April 1, 2007.
