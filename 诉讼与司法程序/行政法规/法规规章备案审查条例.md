# 法规规章备案审查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2024-08-30  
**生效日期**：2024-11-01

> 第四条　法规、规章公布后，应当自公布之日起30日内，依照下列规定报送备案： （一）地方性法规、自治州和自治县的自治条例和单行条例由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会报国务院备案； （二）部门规章由国务院部门报国务院备案，两个或者两个以上部门联合制定的规章，由主办的部门报国务院备案； （三）省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规章由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报国务院备案； （四）设区的市、自治州的人民政府规章由设区的市、自治州的人民政府报国务院备案，同时报省、自治区人民政府备案； （五）经济特区法规由经济特区所在地的省、市的人民代表大会常务委员会报国务院备案，浦东新区法规由上海市人民代表大会常务委员会报国务院备案，海南自由贸易港法规由海南省人民代表大会常务委员会报国务院备案。 第五条　国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府应当依法履行规章备案职责，加强对规章备案工作的组织领导。 国务院部门法制机构，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市、自治州的人民政府法制机构，具体负责本部门、本地方的规章备案工作。 第六条　国务院备案审查工作机构依照本条例的规定负责国务院的法规、规章备案工作，履行备案审查监督职责，健全备案审查工作制度，每年向国务院报告上一年法规、规章备案审查工作情况。 第七条　国务院备案审查工作机构应当通过备案审查衔接联动机制加强与其他机关备案审查工作机构的联系，在双重备案联动、移交处理、征求意见、会商协调、信息共享、能力提升等方面加强协作配合。 第八条　依照本条例报送国务院备案的法规、规章，径送国务院备案审查工作机构。 报送法规备案，按照全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于法规备案的有关规定执行。 报送规章备案，应当提交备案报告、规章文本和说明，并按照规定的格式装订成册，一式三份。 报送法规、规章备案，应当同时报送法规、规章的电子文本。 第九条　报送法规、规章备案，符合本条例第二条和第八条第二款、第三款、第四款规定的，国务院备案审查工作机构予以备案登记；不符合第二条规定的，不予备案登记；符合第二条规定但不符合第八条第二款、第三款、第四款规定的，暂缓办理备案登记。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府应当依法履行规章备案职责，加强对规章备案工作的组织领导。  
国务院部门法制机构，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和设区的市、自治州的人民政府法制机构，具体负责本部门、本地方的规章备案工作。

**Article 5** The departments of the State Council, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and of cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures shall perform their duties for the recordation of rules according to law, and shall strengthen the organizational leadership of the work on recordation of rules.  
The legal affairs institutions of the departments of the State Council, and the legal affairs institutions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and of cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures, shall be specifically responsible for the recordation of rules within their respective departments and localities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院备案审查工作机构依照本条例的规定负责国务院的法规、规章备案工作，履行备案审查监督职责，健全备案审查工作制度，每年向国务院报告上一年法规、规章备案审查工作情况。

**Article 6** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall be responsible for the recordation of regulations and rules of the State Council in accordance with these Regulations, perform the duties of supervision over recordation review, improve the working system for recordation review, and report to the State Council each year on the work of recordation review of regulations and rules of the preceding year.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院备案审查工作机构应当通过备案审查衔接联动机制加强与其他机关备案审查工作机构的联系，在双重备案联动、移交处理、征求意见、会商协调、信息共享、能力提升等方面加强协作配合。

**Article 7** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall strengthen its contacts with the recordation review institutions of other organs through a linkage mechanism for recordation review, and shall strengthen cooperation and coordination in such aspects as dual-recordation linkage, transfer for handling, solicitation of opinions, consultation and coordination, information sharing, and capacity building.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依照本条例报送国务院备案的法规、规章，径送国务院备案审查工作机构。  
报送法规备案，按照全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于法规备案的有关规定执行。  
报送规章备案，应当提交备案报告、规章文本和说明，并按照规定的格式装订成册，一式三份。  
报送法规、规章备案，应当同时报送法规、规章的电子文本。

**Article 8** Regulations and rules submitted to the State Council for recordation in accordance with these Regulations shall be submitted directly to the recordation review institution of the State Council.  
The submission of regulations for recordation shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the recordation of regulations.  
For the submission of rules for recordation, a recordation report, the text of the rules and an explanation shall be submitted, and shall be bound in book form in the prescribed format, in triplicate.  
When submitting regulations and rules for recordation, electronic texts of the regulations and rules shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　报送法规、规章备案，符合本条例第二条和第八条第二款、第三款、第四款规定的，国务院备案审查工作机构予以备案登记；不符合第二条规定的，不予备案登记；符合第二条规定但不符合第八条第二款、第三款、第四款规定的，暂缓办理备案登记。  
暂缓办理备案登记的，由国务院备案审查工作机构通知制定机关补充报送备案或者重新报送备案；制定机关应当自收到通知之日起15日内按照要求补充或者重新报送备案；补充或者重新报送备案符合规定的，予以备案登记。

**Article 9** Where the submission of regulations and rules for recordation complies with the provisions of Article 2 and Article 8, paragraphs 2, 3 and 4 of these Regulations, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall register them; if it does not comply with Article 2, it shall not register them; if it complies with Article 2 but does not comply with Article 8, paragraphs 2, 3 and 4, the registration shall be suspended.  
Where registration is suspended, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall notify the enacting authority to submit supplementary materials or re-submit the regulations or rules for recordation; the enacting authority shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notification, supplement the submission or re-submit the regulations or rules for recordation as required; if the supplementary or re-submission complies with the provisions, registration shall be carried out.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经备案登记的法规、规章，由国务院备案审查工作机构按月公布目录。  
编辑出版法规、规章汇编的范围，应当以公布的法规、规章目录为准。

**Article 10** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall publish a directory of registered regulations and rules on a monthly basis.  
The scope for compiling and publishing a collection of regulations and rules shall be based on the published directory of regulations and rules.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院备案审查工作机构对报送备案的法规、规章进行主动审查，并可以根据需要进行专项审查。主动审查一般应当在审查程序启动后2个月内完成；情况疑难复杂的，为保证审查质量，可以适当延长审查期限。  
国务院备案审查工作机构发现法规、规章存在涉及其他机关备案审查工作职责范围的共性问题的，可以与其他机关备案审查工作机构开展联合调研或者联合审查，共同研究提出审查意见和建议。

**Article 11** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall conduct proactive review of regulations and rules submitted for recordation, and may carry out special review as necessary. Proactive review shall generally be completed within two months after the initiation of the review procedure; in complex or difficult cases, the review period may be appropriately extended to ensure the quality of review.  
Where the recordation review institution of the State Council finds that regulations and rules involve common issues falling within the scope of duties of other organs' recordation review institutions, it may conduct joint research or joint review with such other organs' recordation review institutions, and jointly study and propose review opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家机关、社会组织、企业事业单位、公民认为地方性法规同行政法规相抵触的，或者认为规章以及国务院各部门、省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府发布的其他具有普遍约束力的行政决定、命令同法律、行政法规相抵触的，可以向国务院书面提出审查建议，由国务院备案审查工作机构研究并提出处理意见，按照规定程序处理。

**Article 12** Where a state organ, social organization, enterprise, institution, or citizen considers that a local regulation is inconsistent with an administrative regulation, or considers that a rule or any other administrative decision or order of a universally binding nature issued by a department of the State Council or by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture is inconsistent with a law or an administrative regulation, they may submit a written review recommendation to the State Council, which shall be studied by the recordation review institution of the State Council, which shall propose treatment opinions and handle the matter in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院备案审查工作机构对报送国务院备案的法规、规章，就下列事项进行审查：  
（一）是否符合党中央、国务院的重大决策部署和国家重大改革方向；  
（二）是否超越权限；  
（三）下位法是否违反上位法的规定；  
（四）地方性法规与部门规章之间或者不同规章之间对同一事项的规定不一致，是否应当改变或者撤销一方的或者双方的规定；  
（五）规章的规定是否适当，规定的措施是否符合立法目的和实际情况；  
（六）是否违背法定程序。

**Article 13** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall review regulations and rules submitted to the State Council for recordation in respect of the following matters:  
(1) Whether they conform to the major decisions and arrangements of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council and to the direction of major national reforms;  
(2) Whether they exceed the limits of authority;  
(3) Whether a lower-level legal norm violates the provisions of a higher-level legal norm;  
(4) Whether, where the provisions of a local regulation and a departmental rule, or of different rules, on the same matter are inconsistent, provisions of one party or both parties should be changed or annulled;  
(5) Whether the provisions of a rule are appropriate, and whether the measures provided for conform to the legislative purpose and the actual circumstances;  
(6) Whether the statutory procedure has been violated.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院备案审查工作机构审查法规、规章时，认为需要有关的国务院部门或者地方人民政府提出意见的，有关的机关应当在规定期限内回复；认为需要法规、规章的制定机关说明有关情况的，有关的制定机关应当在规定期限内予以说明。

**Article 14** When examining regulations and rules, if the recordation review institution of the State Council considers that the relevant department of the State Council or local people's government needs to provide its opinions, the relevant organ shall reply within the prescribed time limit; if it considers that the enacting authority of the regulation or rules needs to provide explanations on the relevant circumstances, the relevant enacting authority shall provide explanations within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院备案审查工作机构审查法规、规章时，可以通过座谈会、论证会、听证会、委托研究、实地调研等方式，听取国家机关、社会组织、企业事业单位、专家学者以及利益相关方的意见，并注重发挥备案审查专家委员会的作用。

**Article 15** When examining regulations and rules, the recordation review institution of the State Council may, by means of symposiums, demonstration meetings, hearings, commissioned research, field investigations, etc., solicit opinions from state organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, experts, scholars, and interested parties, and shall give due consideration to the role of the expert committee on recordation review.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经审查，地方性法规同行政法规相抵触的，由国务院备案审查工作机构移送全国人民代表大会常务委员会工作机构研究处理；必要时由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会处理。

**Article 16** Upon review, where a local regulation is inconsistent with an administrative regulation, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall transfer the matter to the working institution of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for study and handling; when necessary, the State Council shall submit the matter to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for handling.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方性法规与部门规章之间对同一事项的规定不一致的，由国务院备案审查工作机构提出处理意见，报国务院依照《中华人民共和国立法法》的有关规定处理。

**Article 17** Where the provisions of a local regulation and a departmental rule on the same matter are inconsistent, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall propose treatment opinions and report them to the State Council for handling in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Legislation Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经审查，认为规章应当予以纠正的，国务院备案审查工作机构可以通过与制定机关沟通、提出书面审查意见等方式，建议制定机关及时修改或者废止；或者由国务院备案审查工作机构提出处理意见报国务院决定，并通知制定机关。

**Article 18** Upon review, where it is considered that a rule should be corrected, the recordation review institution of the State Council may, through communication with the enacting authority, issuing a written review opinion, or other means, suggest that the enacting authority promptly amend or abolish the rule; or the recordation review institution of the State Council shall propose treatment opinions and report them to the State Council for decision, and shall notify the enacting authority.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　部门规章之间、部门规章与地方政府规章之间对同一事项的规定不一致的，由国务院备案审查工作机构进行协调；经协调不能取得一致意见的，由国务院备案审查工作机构提出处理意见报国务院决定，并通知制定机关。

**Article 19** Where the provisions of departmental rules, or of departmental rules and local government rules, on the same matter are inconsistent, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall conduct coordination; if no consensus can be reached through coordination, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall propose treatment opinions and report them to the State Council for decision, and shall notify the enacting authorities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对《规章制定程序条例》规定的无效规章，国务院备案审查工作机构不予备案，并通知制定机关。  
规章在制定技术上存在问题的，国务院备案审查工作机构可以向制定机关提出处理意见，由制定机关自行处理。

**Article 20** For rules that are invalid as prescribed in the Regulations on Procedures for the Formulation of Rules, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall not record them and shall notify the enacting authority.  
Where a rule has defects in drafting techniques, the recordation review institution of the State Council may propose treatment opinions to the enacting authority, and the enacting authority shall handle the matter on its own.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　规章的制定机关应当自接到[本条例第十八条](#art-18)、第十九条、第二十条规定的通知之日起30日内，将处理情况报国务院备案审查工作机构。

**Article 21** The enacting authority of a rule shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notification provided for in Articles 18, 19 and 20 of these Regulations, report the handling status to the recordation review institution of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　法规、规章的制定机关应当于每年1月31日前将上一年所制定的法规、规章目录报国务院备案审查工作机构。

**Article 22** The enacting authorities of regulations and rules shall, before January 31 of each year, submit the directory of regulations and rules enacted in the preceding year to the recordation review institution of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对于不报送规章备案或者不按时报送规章备案的，由国务院备案审查工作机构通知制定机关，限期报送；逾期仍不报送的，给予通报，并责令限期改正。

**Article 23** Where a rule is not submitted for recordation or is not submitted on time, the recordation review institution of the State Council shall notify the enacting authority and require it to submit within a specified time limit; if it still fails to submit after the expiration of the time limit, a notice of criticism shall be given, and the enacting authority shall be ordered to rectify within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院备案审查工作机构应当加强对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府法制机构规章备案审查工作的联系和指导，通过培训、案例指导等方式，推动省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提高规章备案审查工作能力和质量。

**Article 24** The recordation review institution of the State Council shall strengthen contacts and guidance with the legal affairs institutions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government regarding their work on recordation review of rules, and shall, through training, case guidance, and other means, promote the improvement of their capacity and quality in the work of recordation review of rules.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当依法加强对下级行政机关发布的规章和其他具有普遍约束力的行政决定、命令的监督，依照本条例的有关规定，建立相关的备案审查制度，维护社会主义法制的统一，保证法律、法规的正确实施。

**Article 25** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to law, strengthen supervision over rules and other administrative decisions and orders of a universally binding nature issued by lower-level administrative organs, and shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, establish relevant recordation review systems, maintain the unity of the socialist legal system, and ensure the correct implementation of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2024年11月1日起施行。2001年12月14日国务院发布的《法规规章备案条例》同时废止。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2024. The Regulations on the Recordation of Regulations and Rules promulgated by the State Council on December 14, 2001 shall be abolished simultaneously.
