# 实施《中华人民共和国反外国制裁法》的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2025-03-23  
**生效日期**：2025-03-23

> 第五条　国务院有关部门作出采取反制措施的决定，应当明确反制措施的适用对象、具体反制措施、施行日期等。 第六条　反外国制裁法第六条第一项中的不予签发签证、不准入境、注销签证或者驱逐出境，由国务院外交、国家移民管理等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。 第七条　反外国制裁法第六条第二项中的查封、扣押、冻结，由国务院公安、财政、自然资源、交通运输、海关、市场监督管理、金融管理、知识产权等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。 反外国制裁法第六条第二项中的其他各类财产，包括现金、票据、银行存款、有价证券、基金份额、股权、知识产权、应收账款等财产和财产权利。 第八条　反外国制裁法第六条第三项中的禁止或者限制我国境内的组织、个人与其进行有关交易、合作等活动，包括但不限于教育、科技、法律服务、环保、经贸、文化、旅游、卫生、体育领域的活动，由国务院教育、科技、司法行政、生态环境、商务、文化和旅游、卫生健康、体育行政等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。 第九条　反外国制裁法第六条第四项中的其他必要措施，包括但不限于禁止或者限制从事与我国有关的进出口活动，禁止或者限制在我国境内投资，禁止向其出口相关物项，禁止或者限制向其提供数据、个人信息，取消或者限制其相关人员在我国境内工作许可、停留或者居留资格，处以罚款。 第十条　国务院外交、商务、发展改革、司法行政等部门按照各自职责和任务分工，负责承担反外国制裁工作协调机制相关工作。国务院有关部门加强对反制措施确定和实施的协同配合和信息共享。 第十一条　国务院有关部门作出采取、暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施决定的，应当通过其官方网站等途径发布并及时更新。 第十二条　反制措施需要国务院其他部门实施的，作出采取、暂停、变更或者取消反制措施决定的国务院有关部门应当按照相关程序，将反制措施的决定通报负责实施的国务院有关部门。 收到反制措施决定的国务院有关部门，应当依照职责分工实施。 第十三条　国务院有关部门对不依法执行反制措施的，有权责令改正，禁止或者限制其从事政府采购、招标投标以及有关货物、技术的进出口或者国际服务贸易等活动，禁止或者限制其从境外接收或者向境外提供数据、个人信息，禁止或者限制其出境、在我国境内停留居留等。 第十四条　采取反制措施的决定公布后，被采取反制措施的组织、个人可以向作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门申请暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施，申请时应当提供其改正行为、采取措施消除行为后果等方面的事实和理由。 第十五条　作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门可以根据实际情况组织评估反制措施的执行情况和效果。 作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门根据评估结果或者根据对被采取反制措施的组织、个人申请事实和理由的审查情况，可以暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施。 第十六条　采取反制措施的决定公布后，有关组织、个人在特殊情况下确需与被采取反制措施的组织、个人进行被禁止或者限制的相关活动的，应当向作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门提供相应的事实和理由，经同意可以与被采取反制措施的组织、个人进行相关活动。

### 正文

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[反外国制裁法第六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反外国制裁法.md#art-6)第一项中的不予签发签证、不准入境、注销签证或者驱逐出境，由国务院外交、国家移民管理等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。

**Article 6** The matters set forth in subparagraph (1) of Article 6 of the Law on Countering Foreign Sanctions, including refusal to issue a visa, denial of entry, cancellation of a visa, or deportation, shall be implemented by the relevant departments of the State Council, including the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the National Immigration Administration, in accordance with their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[反外国制裁法第六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反外国制裁法.md#art-6)第二项中的查封、扣押、冻结，由国务院公安、财政、自然资源、交通运输、海关、市场监督管理、金融管理、知识产权等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。  
[反外国制裁法第六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反外国制裁法.md#art-6)第二项中的其他各类财产，包括现金、票据、银行存款、有价证券、基金份额、股权、知识产权、应收账款等财产和财产权利。

**Article 7** The seizure, impoundment, and freezing set forth in subparagraph (2) of Article 6 of the Law on Countering Foreign Sanctions shall be implemented by the relevant departments of the State Council, including public security, finance, natural resources, transportation, customs, market regulation, financial management, and intellectual property, in accordance with their respective functions and powers.  
  
Other types of property set forth in subparagraph (2) of Article 6 of the Law on Countering Foreign Sanctions include cash, negotiable instruments, bank deposits, securities, fund shares, equity, intellectual property, accounts receivable, and other property and property rights.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[反外国制裁法第六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反外国制裁法.md#art-6)第三项中的禁止或者限制我国境内的组织、个人与其进行有关交易、合作等活动，包括但不限于教育、科技、法律服务、环保、经贸、文化、旅游、卫生、体育领域的活动，由国务院教育、科技、司法行政、生态环境、商务、文化和旅游、卫生健康、体育行政等有关部门，依照职责权限实施。

**Article 8** The prohibition or restriction of organizations and individuals within the territory of China from engaging in relevant transactions, cooperation, and other activities with persons subject to countermeasures, as set forth in subparagraph (3) of Article 6 of the Law on Countering Foreign Sanctions, which includes but is not limited to activities in the fields of education, science and technology, legal services, environmental protection, economy and trade, culture, tourism, health, and sports, shall be implemented by the relevant departments of the State Council, including education, science and technology, judicial administration, ecological environment, commerce, culture and tourism, health, and sports administration, in accordance with their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[反外国制裁法第六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反外国制裁法.md#art-6)第四项中的其他必要措施，包括但不限于禁止或者限制从事与我国有关的进出口活动，禁止或者限制在我国境内投资，禁止向其出口相关物项，禁止或者限制向其提供数据、个人信息，取消或者限制其相关人员在我国境内工作许可、停留或者居留资格，处以罚款。

**Article 9** The other necessary measures set forth in subparagraph (4) of Article 6 of the Law on Countering Foreign Sanctions include but are not limited to prohibiting or restricting engagement in import or export activities related to China, prohibiting or restricting investment within the territory of China, prohibiting the export of relevant items to such persons, prohibiting or restricting the provision of data or personal information to such persons, revoking or restricting the work permit, stay or residence qualification of relevant personnel within the territory of China, and imposing fines.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院外交、商务、发展改革、司法行政等部门按照各自职责和任务分工，负责承担反外国制裁工作协调机制相关工作。国务院有关部门加强对反制措施确定和实施的协同配合和信息共享。

**Article 10** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Commerce, the National Development and Reform Commission, the Ministry of Justice, and other departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and division of tasks, be responsible for undertaking the work related to the coordination mechanism for countering foreign sanctions. The relevant departments of the State Council shall strengthen coordination, collaboration, and information sharing in the determination and implementation of countermeasures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院有关部门作出采取、暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施决定的，应当通过其官方网站等途径发布并及时更新。

**Article 11** Where the relevant department of the State Council decides to adopt, suspend, modify, or revoke a relevant countermeasure, it shall publish the decision through its official website or other channels and update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　反制措施需要国务院其他部门实施的，作出采取、暂停、变更或者取消反制措施决定的国务院有关部门应当按照相关程序，将反制措施的决定通报负责实施的国务院有关部门。  
收到反制措施决定的国务院有关部门，应当依照职责分工实施。

**Article 12** Where the implementation of a countermeasure requires action by other departments of the State Council, the relevant department of the State Council that decides to adopt, suspend, modify, or revoke the countermeasure shall, in accordance with relevant procedures, notify the decision on the countermeasure to the relevant department of the State Council responsible for implementation.  
  
The relevant department of the State Council that receives the decision on the countermeasure shall implement it in accordance with its division of functions and powers.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院有关部门对不依法执行反制措施的，有权责令改正，禁止或者限制其从事政府采购、招标投标以及有关货物、技术的进出口或者国际服务贸易等活动，禁止或者限制其从境外接收或者向境外提供数据、个人信息，禁止或者限制其出境、在我国境内停留居留等。

**Article 13** Where an entity fails to implement countermeasures according to law, the relevant department of the State Council shall have the authority to order rectification, prohibit or restrict it from engaging in government procurement, tendering and bidding, and activities related to the import or export of goods and technologies or international trade in services, prohibit or restrict it from receiving from abroad or providing abroad data or personal information, and prohibit or restrict its personnel from exiting the country, staying or residing within the territory of China, among other measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　采取反制措施的决定公布后，被采取反制措施的组织、个人可以向作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门申请暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施，申请时应当提供其改正行为、采取措施消除行为后果等方面的事实和理由。

**Article 14** After the decision to adopt countermeasures is published, the organization or individual subject to the countermeasures may apply to the relevant department of the State Council that made the decision to adopt the countermeasures for suspending, modifying, or revoking the relevant countermeasures. When making the application, it shall provide facts and reasons regarding its corrective actions and measures taken to eliminate the consequences of its actions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门可以根据实际情况组织评估反制措施的执行情况和效果。  
作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门根据评估结果或者根据对被采取反制措施的组织、个人申请事实和理由的审查情况，可以暂停、变更或者取消有关反制措施。

**Article 15** The relevant department of the State Council that decides to adopt countermeasures may, based on the actual circumstances, organize an evaluation of the implementation and effectiveness of the countermeasures.  
  
The relevant department of the State Council that decides to adopt countermeasures may, based on the evaluation results or on the review of the facts and reasons provided in the application by the organization or individual subject to the countermeasures, suspend, modify, or revoke the relevant countermeasures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　采取反制措施的决定公布后，有关组织、个人在特殊情况下确需与被采取反制措施的组织、个人进行被禁止或者限制的相关活动的，应当向作出采取反制措施决定的国务院有关部门提供相应的事实和理由，经同意可以与被采取反制措施的组织、个人进行相关活动。

**Article 16** After the decision to take countermeasures is announced, if an organization or individual, under special circumstances, truly needs to engage in prohibited or restricted activities with the organization or individual subject to countermeasures, it shall provide relevant facts and reasons to the relevant department of the State Council that made the decision to take countermeasures, and with approval, may engage in such activities with the organization or individual subject to countermeasures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对执行或者协助执行外国国家对我国公民、组织采取的歧视性限制措施的，国务院有关部门有权进行约谈，责令改正，采取相应处理措施。

**Article 17** With respect to the implementation of or assistance in implementing discriminatory restrictive measures taken by a foreign state against Chinese citizens or organizations, the relevant department of the State Council shall have the authority to conduct interviews, order rectification, and take corresponding measures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　任何组织和个人执行或者协助执行外国国家对我国公民、组织采取的歧视性限制措施，侵害我国公民、组织合法权益的，我国公民、组织有权依法向人民法院提起诉讼，要求停止侵害、赔偿损失。

**Article 18** Where any organization or individual implements or assists in implementing discriminatory restrictive measures taken by a foreign state against Chinese citizens or organizations, thereby infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of Chinese citizens or organizations, such Chinese citizens or organizations shall have the right to initiate a prosecution with the People's Court according to law, demanding cessation of the infringement and compensation for losses.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国国家、组织或者个人通过推动、实施诉讼等手段危害我国主权、安全、发展利益的，国务院有关部门有权决定将参与诉讼和判决执行等活动的上述主体及与其相关的组织、个人列入反制清单，采取限制入境，查封、扣押、冻结在我国境内的财产，禁止或者限制与其进行有关交易、合作等反制措施，并保留采取强制执行财产以及其他更严厉反制措施的权利。  
任何组织和个人均不得执行或者协助执行前款外国国家、组织或者个人推动、实施的诉讼所作出的判决。

**Article 19** Where a foreign state, organization, or individual jeopardizes China's sovereignty, security, or development interests by promoting or initiating litigation or other means, the relevant department of the State Council shall have the authority to decide to include the aforementioned subjects that participate in the litigation and enforcement of judgments, as well as organizations and individuals related to them, in the countermeasure list, and take such countermeasures as restricting entry, sealing up, seizing, or freezing property within China's territory, prohibiting or restricting relevant transactions or cooperation with them, and shall retain the right to take compulsory enforcement of property and other more stringent countermeasures.  
No organization or individual shall implement or assist in implementing any judgment rendered in the litigation promoted or initiated by the foreign state, organization, or individual as referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　鼓励支持律师事务所、公证机构等专业服务机构为反外国制裁提供法律服务，包括协助相关组织、个人为执行反制措施实施风险控制管理，代理我国公民、组织就相关组织、个人因执行或者协助执行外国国家歧视性限制措施侵害合法权益向人民法院提起诉讼，办理相关公证业务等。

**Article 20** Law firms, notarization institutions, and other professional service institutions are encouraged and supported to provide legal services for countering foreign sanctions, including assisting relevant organizations and individuals in implementing risk control management for executing countermeasures, representing Chinese citizens and organizations in initiating prosecutions with the People's Court regarding the infringement of lawful rights and interests caused by the implementation of or assistance in implementing discriminatory restrictive measures of foreign states by relevant organizations or individuals, handling relevant notarization business, and so on.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在实施反外国制裁法和本规定过程中，涉及司法协助相关工作的，由国务院司法行政部门会同主管机关依照我国有关法律、缔结或者参加的国际条约办理。

**Article 21** In the process of implementing the Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law and these Provisions, matters involving judicial assistance shall be handled by the judicial administration department of the State Council together with the competent authorities in accordance with relevant Chinese laws and international treaties concluded or acceded to by China.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
