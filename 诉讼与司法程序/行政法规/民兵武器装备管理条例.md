# 民兵武器装备管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1995年6月3日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第178号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强民兵武器装备管理，保障民兵完成作战、执勤、训练等项任务，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称民兵武器装备，是指配备给民兵使用和储存的武器、弹药和军事技术器材。 第三条　民兵武器装备管理工作的基本任务是保证民兵武器装备经常处于良好的技术状态，防止发生丢失、被盗等事故，确保安全，保障民兵能随时用于执行任务。 第四条　全国的民兵武器装备管理工作在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，由中国人民解放军总参谋部(以下简称总参谋部)主管。 军区、省军区(含卫戍区、警备区，下同)、军分区(含警备区，下同)、县(含自治县、不设区的市、市辖区，下同)人民武装部和乡(含民族乡、镇，下同)人民武装部、企业事业单位人民武装部，负责本地区或者本单位的民兵武器装备管理工作。 第五条　地方各级人民政府必须加强对民兵武器装备管理工作的领导，督促有关单位做好民兵武器装备管理工作。 地方各级人民政府有关部门，应当协助军事机关做好民兵武器装备管理工作，解决有关问题。 企业事业单位应当按照当地人民政府和本地区军事机关的要求，把民兵武器装备管理工作纳入管理计划，做好各项工作。 第六条　民兵武器装备管理，应当贯彻艰苦奋斗、勤俭建军的方针，实行管理科学化、制度化，管好现有武器装备，立足于民兵使用现有武器装备完成各项任务。 第二章　职责与分工 第七条　军区、省军区、军分区、县人民武装部、乡人民武装部、企业事业单位人民武装部管理民兵武器装备，履行下列职责： (一)根据本条例和上级军事机关有关民兵武器装备管理的规定，制定本地区或者本单位民兵武器装备管理的规章制度； (二)组织、督促所属单位和人员执行民兵武器装备管理法规和规章制度，建立和保持良好的管理秩序；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强民兵武器装备管理，保障民兵完成作战、执勤、训练等项任务，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of militia weapons and equipment and to ensure that the militia may accomplish tasks such as combat, duty, and training.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称民兵武器装备，是指配备给民兵使用和储存的武器、弹药和军事技术器材。

**Article 2** "Militia weapons and equipment" as used in these Regulations refers to weapons, ammunition, and military technical equipment allocated to the militia for use and storage.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民兵武器装备管理工作的基本任务是保证民兵武器装备经常处于良好的技术状态，防止发生丢失、被盗等事故，确保安全，保障民兵能随时用于执行任务。

**Article 3** The basic task of militia weapons and equipment management is to ensure that militia weapons and equipment are constantly in good technical condition, prevent accidents such as loss or theft, ensure safety, and guarantee that the militia may be used at any time to perform tasks.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　全国的民兵武器装备管理工作在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，由中国人民解放军总参谋部(以下简称总参谋部)主管。  
军区、省军区(含卫戍区、警备区，下同)、军分区(含警备区，下同)、县(含自治县、不设区的市、市辖区，下同)人民武装部和乡(含民族乡、镇，下同)人民武装部、企业事业单位人民武装部，负责本地区或者本单位的民兵武器装备管理工作。

**Article 4** The nationwide management of militia weapons and equipment shall be under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and shall be in charge of the General Staff of the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the General Staff).  
The military region, provincial military command (including garrison commands and 警备区, similarly hereinafter), military sub-command (including 警备区, similarly hereinafter), the people's armed forces department of the county (including autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts, similarly hereinafter), the people's armed forces department of the township (including ethnic townships and towns, similarly hereinafter), and the people's armed forces departments of enterprises and institutions shall be responsible for the management of militia weapons and equipment within their respective regions or units.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民政府必须加强对民兵武器装备管理工作的领导，督促有关单位做好民兵武器装备管理工作。  
地方各级人民政府有关部门，应当协助军事机关做好民兵武器装备管理工作，解决有关问题。  
企业事业单位应当按照当地人民政府和本地区军事机关的要求，把民兵武器装备管理工作纳入管理计划，做好各项工作。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at all levels must strengthen leadership over the management of militia weapons and equipment and urge relevant units to properly manage militia weapons and equipment.  
Relevant departments of local people's governments at all levels shall assist military organs in properly managing militia weapons and equipment and resolve relevant issues.  
Enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and the military organs of the region, incorporate the management of militia weapons and equipment into their management plans and perform all tasks well.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民兵武器装备管理，应当贯彻艰苦奋斗、勤俭建军的方针，实行管理科学化、制度化，管好现有武器装备，立足于民兵使用现有武器装备完成各项任务。

**Article 6** The management of militia weapons and equipment shall implement the policy of hard work and building the army through diligence and frugality, adopt scientific and institutionalized management, properly manage existing weapons and equipment, and be based on the militia using existing weapons and equipment to accomplish various tasks.

### 第二章　职责与分工

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军区、省军区、军分区、县人民武装部、乡人民武装部、企业事业单位人民武装部管理民兵武器装备，履行下列职责：  
(一)根据本条例和上级军事机关有关民兵武器装备管理的规定，制定本地区或者本单位民兵武器装备管理的规章制度；  
(二)组织、督促所属单位和人员执行民兵武器装备管理法规和规章制度，建立和保持良好的管理秩序；  
(三)选配和培训民兵武器装备看管人员和技术人员；  
(四)教育民兵武器装备的看管人员和使用人员管好用好武器装备；  
(五)做好民兵武器装备的安全和防止事故工作；  
(六)掌握民兵武器装备管理情况，及时报告并解决管理中的问题；  
(七)完成上级军事机关赋予的与民兵武器装备管理有关的其他工作。

**Article 7** The military region, provincial military command, military sub-command, the people's armed forces department of the county, the people's armed forces department of the township, and the people's armed forces departments of enterprises and institutions, in managing militia weapons and equipment, shall perform the following duties:  
(1) formulate rules and regulations for the management of militia weapons and equipment within their respective regions or units in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of the superior military organs on the management of militia weapons and equipment;  
(2) organize and supervise the subordinate units and personnel to implement the laws, regulations, rules, and regulations on the management of militia weapons and equipment, and establish and maintain a good management order;  
(3) select and train custodians and technical personnel for militia weapons and equipment;  
(4) educate the custodians and users of militia weapons and equipment to properly manage and use the weapons and equipment;  
(5) ensure the safety of militia weapons and equipment and prevent accidents;  
(6) keep abreast of the management status of militia weapons and equipment, and promptly report and resolve problems in management;  
(7) complete other tasks related to the management of militia weapons and equipment assigned by the superior military organs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　民兵武器装备的看管人员和使用人员应当履行下列职责：  
(一)遵守民兵武器装备管理法规和规章制度；  
(二)熟悉民兵武器装备性能，做到会使用、会保养、会检查、会排除一般故障；  
(三)保守民兵武器装备秘密；  
(四)做好民兵武器装备的安全和防止事故工作；  
(五)看管和使用民兵武器装备的其他有关职责。

**Article 8** The custodians and users of militia weapons and equipment shall perform the following duties:  
(1) abide by the laws, regulations, rules, and regulations on the management of militia weapons and equipment;  
(2) be familiar with the performance of militia weapons and equipment, and be able to use, maintain, inspect, and troubleshoot common malfunctions;  
(3) keep the secrets of militia weapons and equipment;  
(4) ensure the safety of militia weapons and equipment and prevent accidents;  
(5) other relevant duties regarding the custody and use of militia weapons and equipment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民兵武器装备的配备、补充、调整、动用、封存等组织计划工作，由军事机关司令部门负责。

**Article 9** The organizational and planning work for the allocation, replenishment, adjustment, use, and sealing of militia weapons and equipment shall be the responsibility of the command departments of military organs.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民兵武器装备的储存保管、技术鉴定、维护修理等技术管理工作，按照职责分工，由军事机关的司令部门或者装备技术部门负责。

**Article 10** The technical management work such as storage, technical evaluation, maintenance, and repair of militia weapons and equipment shall be the responsibility of the command departments or equipment technical departments of military organs, according to the division of duties.

### 第三章　配备与补充

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　民兵武器装备的配备与补充，由总参谋部统一规划。军区、省军区、军分区和县人民武装部，根据上级的规划，制定本地区的配备与补充计划，并组织实施。

**Article 11** The allocation and replenishment of militia weapons and equipment shall be uniformly planned by the General Staff. The military region, provincial military command, military sub-command, and the people's armed forces department of the county shall formulate plans for allocation and replenishment for their respective regions based on the superior plans, and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民兵武器装备的配备，应当根据基干民兵的组建计划和战备、执勤、训练等项任务的需要，做到保障重点，合理布局。

**Article 12** The allocation of militia weapons and equipment shall be carried out according to the formation plan of primary militia and the needs of tasks such as combat readiness, duty, and training, ensuring the priority of key areas and reasonable deployment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民兵配属部队执行作战、支前任务所需的武器装备，由县人民武装部配发；到达部队后，由所在部队按照损耗补充。

**Article 13** The weapons and equipment required for militia attached to troops to perform combat or support frontline tasks shall be issued by the people's armed forces department of the county; after arriving at the troops, they shall be replenished by the troops according to consumption.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民兵武器装备的调整，按照管辖范围，分别由县人民武装部、军分区、省军区、军区批准；超出管辖范围的，由上级军事机关批准；调出民兵系统的，由总参谋部批准。

**Article 14** The adjustment of militia weapons and equipment shall be approved by the people's armed forces department of the county, military sub-command, provincial military command, or military region respectively according to the scope of jurisdiction; if beyond the scope of jurisdiction, it shall be approved by the superior military organ; if transferred out of the militia system, it shall be approved by the General Staff.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民兵武器装备的制造、装配、接收、购置，必须经总参谋部批准。

**Article 15** The manufacture, assembly, acceptance, and purchase of militia weapons and equipment must be approved by the General Staff.

### 第四章　保管与使用

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民兵武器装备的保管，应当符合技术和战备、安全的要求，建立健全值班、交接、登记、检查、保养等制度，做到无丢失、无损坏、无锈蚀、无霉烂变质。  
武器、弹药应当分开存放。

**Article 16** The storage of militia weapons and equipment shall meet technical, combat readiness, and safety requirements, and establish and improve systems such as duty, handover, registration, inspection, and maintenance, so as to achieve no loss, no damage, no rust, and no mildew deterioration.  
Weapons and ammunition shall be stored separately.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民兵武器装备，应当集中在县以上民兵武器装备仓库保管；因战备、值勤的需要，经省军区批准，可以由乡人民武装部、企业事业单位或者民兵值勤点保管。  
配备给乡、企业事业单位的高射机枪和火炮，由乡人民武装部、企业事业单位保管。

**Article 17** Militia weapons and equipment shall be stored in centralized warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at or above the county level; when necessary for combat readiness or duty, with the approval of the provincial military command, they may be stored by the township people's armed forces department, enterprises and institutions, or militia duty points.  
Anti-aircraft machine guns and artillery allocated to townships, enterprises, and institutions shall be stored by the township people's armed forces department, enterprises, and institutions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　省军区、军分区民兵武器装备仓库的管理，除依照本条例执行外，并应当执行中国人民解放军军械仓库业务管理的有关规定；县以下民兵武器装备仓库的管理，除依照本条例执行外，并应当执行上级军事机关的有关规定。

**Article 18** The management of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at the provincial military command and military sub-command levels shall, in addition to these Regulations, also comply with the relevant provisions on the operational management of ordnance warehouses of the People's Liberation Army; the management of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at or below the county level shall, in addition to these Regulations, also comply with the relevant provisions of the superior military organs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保管民兵武器装备的乡人民武装部、企业事业单位必须有牢固的库房、枪柜(箱、架)和可靠的安全设施，配备专职看管人员。

**Article 19** The township people's armed forces departments, enterprises, and institutions that store militia weapons and equipment must have solid warehouses, gun cabinets (boxes, racks), and reliable safety facilities, and be equipped with full-time custodians.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民兵武器装备仓库是国家的军事设施，地方各级人民政府和军事机关应当依照《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》做好保护工作。

**Article 20** Warehouses for militia weapons and equipment are military installations of the State. Local people's governments at all levels and military organs shall, in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Military Installations, properly protect them.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　掌握武器装备的民兵和民兵武器装备仓库的看管人员，应当由人民武装部门按照有关规定审查批准，并报上一级军事机关备案。

**Article 21** The militiamen who hold weapons and equipment and the custodians of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment shall be examined and approved by the people's armed forces departments in accordance with relevant provisions, and be reported to the superior military organ for record.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　省军区、军分区和县、乡人民武装部民兵武器装备仓库的新建、扩建和改建，应当纳入地方基本建设计划统筹安排，所需经费由同级人民政府解决。企业事业单位民兵武器装备仓库的修建和改建所需的经费，按照国家有关规定解决。  
省军区、军分区民兵武器装备仓库的职工工资、公务事业费和福利费等，从国防费中开支；县民兵武器装备仓库的维修费、业务费和职工工资等，按照国家的有关规定执行。

**Article 22** The construction, expansion, and renovation of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment of the provincial military command, military sub-command, and the people's armed forces departments of counties and townships shall be incorporated into the local capital construction plan for overall arrangement, and the required funds shall be solved by the people's government at the same level. The funds needed for the construction and renovation of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment of enterprises and institutions shall be solved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The wages, public service expenses, and welfare expenses of staff of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at the provincial military command and military sub-command levels shall be disbursed from national defense funds; the maintenance expenses, business expenses, and wages of staff of warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at the county level shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　平时启用封存的民兵武器装备，应当经过批准。启用简易封存的民兵武器装备，由军分区以上军事机关批准；启用新品和长期封存的民兵武器装备，由省军区以上军事机关批准。

**Article 23** The activation of sealed militia weapons and equipment during peacetime shall be approved. The activation of simply sealed militia weapons and equipment shall be approved by the military organ at or above the military sub-command level; the activation of new products and long-term sealed militia weapons and equipment shall be approved by the military organ at or above the provincial military command level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　高等院校学生军事训练用的教练枪，应当按照规定经过批准，由当地县人民武装部提供，由院校负责保管。  
学生军事训练用的教练枪，必须经过技术处理，使其不能用于实弹射击。

**Article 24** Training rifles used for military training of students in institutions of higher education shall be approved in accordance with regulations, provided by the local county people's armed forces department, and kept under the custody of the institution.  
Training rifles used for student military training must undergo technical treatment so that they cannot be used for live firing.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　高等院校、高级中学和相当于高级中学的学校学生军事训练所需的实弹射击用枪，由当地县人民武装部提供并负责管理。

**Article 25** Rifles for live firing required for military training of students in institutions of higher education, senior high schools, and schools equivalent to senior high schools shall be provided and managed by the local county people's armed forces department.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民兵配合部队执行任务或者配合公安机关维护社会治安，需要动用民兵武器装备时，应当按照有关规定执行。

**Article 26** When the militia is required to use weapons and equipment to cooperate with troops in performing tasks or to assist public security organs in maintaining public order, it shall be done in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民兵武器装备，不得擅自借出。因执勤、训练需要借用配发给民兵或者民兵组织的武器装备的，必须报经县人民武装部批准。借用县以上民兵武器装备仓库保管的民兵武器装备，必须报上一级军事机关批准。

**Article 27** Militia weapons and equipment shall not be lent out without authorization. If it is necessary to borrow weapons and equipment allocated to the militia or militia organizations due to duty or training needs, it must be reported to and approved by the county people's armed forces department. To borrow militia weapons and equipment stored in warehouses at or above the county level, it must be reported to and approved by the superior military organ.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　保管与使用民兵武器、弹药的，必须遵守下列规定：  
(一)不准随意射击、投掷；  
(二)不准用与武器非配用的弹药射击；  
(三)不准持武器、弹药打闹；  
(四)不准随意拆卸武器、弹药和改变其性能；  
(五)不准擅自借出武器、弹药；  
(六)不准擅自动用武器、弹药打猎；  
(七)不准擅自携带武器、弹药；  
(八)不准动用武器、弹药参加械斗和参与处理民间纠纷。

**Article 28** Those who store and use militia weapons and ammunition must comply with the following provisions:  
(1) not to fire or throw arbitrarily;  
(2) not to fire with ammunition not matching the weapon;  
(3) not to play with weapons or ammunition;  
(4) not to disassemble weapons or ammunition arbitrarily or alter their performance;  
(5) not to lend out weapons or ammunition without authorization;  
(6) not to use weapons or ammunition for hunting without authorization;  
(7) not to carry weapons or ammunition without authorization;  
(8) not to use weapons or ammunition to participate in armed fights or handle civil disputes.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　因执行任务需要，按照规定配发给个人的民兵武器、弹药，实行持枪证和持枪通行证制度。持枪证和持枪通行证式样及使用办法，由总参谋部规定。

**Article 29** For militia weapons and ammunition allocated to individuals according to regulations due to task requirements, a system of firearm permits and firearm passes shall be implemented. The format and usage methods of firearm permits and firearm passes shall be prescribed by the General Staff.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民兵弹药的使用，应当执行用旧存新、用零存整的原则。军事训练、武器修理、试验等剩余的弹药，必须交回县以上民兵武器装备仓库保管，列入本年度装备实力统计，任何单位或者个人不得私自留存。

**Article 30** The use of militia ammunition shall follow the principle of using old stock and storing new, and using scattered and storing whole. The remaining ammunition from military training, weapon repair, tests, etc., must be returned to warehouses for militia weapons and equipment at or above the county level for storage, and be included in the annual equipment strength statistics. No unit or individual may retain it privately.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　民兵、学生军事训练所需弹药，由总参谋部规定标准和下达指标，逐级进行分配。

**Article 31** The ammunition required for military training of militia and students shall have standards prescribed and targets assigned by the General Staff, and be distributed level by level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经中央军事委员会或者总参谋部批准，民兵为外国人进行军事表演所需弹药，由省军区拨给。

**Article 32** With the approval of the Central Military Commission or the General Staff, the ammunition required for militia to conduct military performances for foreigners shall be allocated by the provincial military command.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　修理、试验民兵武器和进行试验、化验所需要的弹药，按照中国人民解放军有关标准执行，由省军区装备技术部批准拨给；未设装备技术部的，由司令部批准拨给。

**Article 33** The ammunition required for repairing, testing militia weapons and for conducting tests and chemical analyses shall be handled in accordance with the relevant standards of the People's Liberation Army, and shall be approved and allocated by the equipment technical department of the provincial military command; where no equipment technical department is established, it shall be approved and allocated by the command department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　严禁挪用、出租、交换民兵武器装备。  
未经中央军事委员会或者总参谋部批准，不得馈赠、出售民兵武器装备。

**Article 34** It is strictly prohibited to misappropriate, rent out, or exchange militia weapons and equipment.  
Without the approval of the Central Military Commission or the General Staff, militia weapons and equipment shall not be given as gifts or sold.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　未经总参谋部批准，不得动用民兵武器装备从事生产经营活动。

**Article 35** Without the approval of the General Staff, militia weapons and equipment shall not be used to engage in production and business activities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　发生民兵武器装备丢失、被盗等事故时，应当立即向当地军事机关和人民政府报告，并迅速处理。  
军事机关必须及时逐级上报总参谋部。

**Article 36** When an accident such as loss or theft of militia weapons and equipment occurs, it shall be immediately reported to the local military organ and the people's government, and promptly handled.  
The military organ must promptly report to the General Staff level by level.

### 第五章　修理与报废

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县人民武装部负责修理其管理的民兵武器装备，企业事业单位负责修理其保管的民兵武器装备；无力修理的，由军分区、省军区、军区修械所(厂)修理。其中，弹药的修理，由省军区民兵武器装备仓库负责；无力修理的，由军区司令部门安排修理。  
民兵武器装备维修所需的经费，从民兵事业费的装备管理维修费中开支。

**Article 37** The county people's armed forces department shall be responsible for repairing the militia weapons and equipment under its management; enterprises and institutions shall be responsible for repairing the militia weapons and equipment under their custody. If they are unable to repair, the repair shall be done by the repair workshops (factories) of the military sub-command, provincial military command, or military region. Among them, the repair of ammunition shall be the responsibility of the provincial military command warehouse for militia weapons and equipment; if unable to repair, the repair shall be arranged by the command department of the military region.  
The funds required for the maintenance of militia weapons and equipment shall be disbursed from the equipment management and maintenance fees of the militia undertaking expenses.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　军分区、省军区修械所负责修理民兵武器装备和军分区、省军区直属分队的武器装备。其职工工资、公务事业费和福利费等从国防费中开支。

**Article 38** The repair workshops of the military sub-command and the provincial military command shall be responsible for repairing the weapons and equipment of the militia and of the units directly under the military sub-command and provincial military command. The wages, public service expenses, and welfare expenses of their staff shall be disbursed from national defense funds.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　民兵武器装备的分级和转级，按照中国人民解放军有关规定执行。

**Article 39** The classification and reclassification of militia weapons and equipment shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民兵武器装备的报废，应当经过批准。报废的批准和处理权限，由总参谋部规定。  
民兵武器装备的报废处理规则，按照中国人民解放军有关规定执行。  
有重要历史意义的民兵武器装备，应当妥善保管，不得自行处理。

**Article 40** The scrapping of militia weapons and equipment shall be approved. The authority for approving and handling scrapping shall be prescribed by the General Staff.  
The rules for handling scrapped militia weapons and equipment shall be in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Liberation Army.  
Militia weapons and equipment of significant historical value shall be properly stored and shall not be disposed of on own authority.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　严禁将民兵武器装备管理维修费、民兵武器装备维修材料或者备件挪作他用。

**Article 41** It is strictly prohibited to misappropriate the management and maintenance funds, maintenance materials, or spare parts of militia weapons and equipment for other purposes.

### 第六章　奖励与惩处

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　符合下列条件之一的单位和个人，由人民政府、军事机关给予奖励：  
(一)同抢劫、盗窃、破坏民兵武器装备以及其他危害民兵武器装备的行为进行斗争的；  
(二)在危险事故中抢救或者保护民兵武器装备，或者避免危险事故发生的；  
(三)长期在基层从事民兵武器装备管理工作或者在民兵武器装备维修等项工作中，完成任务出色的；  
(四)在民兵武器装备管理工作中从事危险作业，圆满完成任务的；  
(五)在民兵武器装备管理工作中严格执行各项规章制度，成绩突出的。

**Article 42** Units and individuals that meet one of the following conditions shall be rewarded by the people's government or military organ:  
(1) fighting against robbery, theft, destruction of militia weapons and equipment, or other acts harmful to militia weapons and equipment;  
(2) rescuing or protecting militia weapons and equipment in dangerous accidents, or preventing dangerous accidents;  
(3) having long been engaged in primary-level management of militia weapons and equipment or in maintenance of militia weapons and equipment, and having accomplished tasks with outstanding results;  
(4) having engaged in dangerous operations in the management of militia weapons and equipment and accomplished tasks successfully;  
(5) having strictly implemented various rules and regulations in the management of militia weapons and equipment with outstanding achievements.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列行为之一的，依法给予行政处分；属于违反治安管理行为的，依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)私藏、盗窃、抢劫、破坏民兵武器装备，或者利用民兵武器装备进行违法活动的；  
(二)擅自制造、装配、接收、购置民兵武器装备或者擅自挪用、出租、交换、馈赠、出售、携带、留存、动用、借出民兵武器装备的；  
(三)挪用民兵装备管理维修费、武器装备维修材料或者备件的；  
(四)玩忽职守，致使民兵武器装备丢失、被盗或者损坏、锈蚀、霉烂变质，影响使用的；  
(五)违反民兵武器装备操作规程和使用规定，造成后果的；  
(六)在民兵武器装备受到抢劫、盗窃、破坏时，不采取制止和保护措施，致使武器装备遭受损失的；  
(七)对民兵武器装备事故隐瞒不报的；  
(八)有违反本条例的其他行为的。

**Article 43** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the person shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) privately possessing, stealing, robbing, or destroying militia weapons and equipment, or using militia weapons and equipment to carry out illegal activities;  
(2) manufacturing, assembling, accepting, or purchasing militia weapons and equipment without authorization, or misappropriating, renting out, exchanging, giving as gifts, selling, carrying, retaining, using, or lending out militia weapons and equipment without authorization;  
(3) misappropriating the management and maintenance funds, maintenance materials, or spare parts of militia weapons and equipment;  
(4) neglecting duties, causing loss, theft, damage, rust, or mildew deterioration of militia weapons and equipment affecting their use;  
(5) violating the operating procedures and usage regulations of militia weapons and equipment, causing consequences;  
(6) failing to take measures to stop and protect militia weapons and equipment when they are being robbed, stolen, or destroyed, resulting in loss;  
(7) concealing and not reporting accidents of militia weapons and equipment;  
(8) other acts violating these Regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　有[本条例第四十三条](#art-43)所列行为之一的单位，除对主管负责人员和直接责任人员给予行政处分、行政处罚或者依法追究刑事责任外，应当对该单位给予通报批评，并限期改正。

**Article 44** A unit that commits any of the acts listed in Article 43 of these Regulations shall, in addition to imposing administrative sanctions, administrative penalties, or investigating criminal liability according to law on the person in charge and the person directly responsible, be given a notice of criticism and be ordered to make corrections within a time limit.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　民兵通信装备、工兵装备、防化装备的管理办法，由总参谋部根据本条例制定。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和省军区可以根据本条例，制定本地区民兵武器装备管理的具体办法。

**Article 45** The management measures for militia communication equipment, engineer equipment, and chemical defense equipment shall be formulated by the General Staff in accordance with these Regulations.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the provincial military commands may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate specific measures for the management of militia weapons and equipment within their respective regions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
