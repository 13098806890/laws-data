# 法律援助条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2003-07-21  
**生效日期**：2003-09-01

> (2003年7月16日国务院第15次常务会议通过　2003年7月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第385号公布　自2003年9月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障经济困难的公民获得必要的法律服务，促进和规范法律援助工作，制定本条例。 第二条　符合本条例规定的公民，可以依照本条例获得法律咨询、代理、刑事辩护等无偿法律服务。 第三条　法律援助是政府的责任，县级以上人民政府应当采取积极措施推动法律援助工作，为法律援助提供财政支持，保障法律援助事业与经济、社会协调发展。 法律援助经费应当专款专用，接受财政、审计部门的监督。 第四条　国务院司法行政部门监督管理全国的法律援助工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府司法行政部门监督管理本行政区域的法律援助工作。 中华全国律师协会和地方律师协会应当按照律师协会章程对依据本条例实施的法律援助工作予以协助。 第五条　直辖市、设区的市或者县级人民政府司法行政部门根据需要确定本行政区域的法律援助机构。 法律援助机构负责受理、审查法律援助申请，指派或者安排人员为符合本条例规定的公民提供法律援助。 第六条　律师应当依照律师法和本条例的规定履行法律援助义务，为受援人提供符合标准的法律服务，依法维护受援人的合法权益，接受律师协会和司法行政部门的监督。 第七条　国家鼓励社会对法律援助活动提供捐助。 第八条　国家支持和鼓励社会团体、事业单位等社会组织利用自身资源为经济困难的公民提供法律援助。 第九条　对在法律援助工作中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，有关的人民政府、司法行政部门应当给予表彰、奖励。 第二章　法律援助范围

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障经济困难的公民获得必要的法律服务，促进和规范法律援助工作，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure that citizens in financial difficulty obtain necessary legal services, and to promote and regulate legal aid work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　符合本条例规定的公民，可以依照本条例获得法律咨询、代理、刑事辩护等无偿法律服务。

**Article 2** Citizens who meet the requirements of these Regulations may obtain free legal services such as legal consultation, representation, and criminal defense in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　法律援助是政府的责任，县级以上人民政府应当采取积极措施推动法律援助工作，为法律援助提供财政支持，保障法律援助事业与经济、社会协调发展。  
法律援助经费应当专款专用，接受财政、审计部门的监督。

**Article 3** Legal aid is the responsibility of the government. People's governments at or above the county level shall take active measures to promote legal aid work, provide financial support for legal aid, and ensure the coordinated development of legal aid undertakings with the economy and society.  
Legal aid funds shall be used for designated purposes only and shall be subject to supervision by the financial and audit departments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院司法行政部门监督管理全国的法律援助工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府司法行政部门监督管理本行政区域的法律援助工作。  
中华全国律师协会和地方律师协会应当按照律师协会章程对依据本条例实施的法律援助工作予以协助。

**Article 4** The judicial administration department of the State Council shall supervise and administer the national legal aid work. The judicial administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and administer the legal aid work within their respective administrative regions.  
The All China Lawyers Association and local lawyers associations shall, in accordance with the articles of association of lawyers associations, assist in the legal aid work implemented pursuant to these Regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　直辖市、设区的市或者县级人民政府司法行政部门根据需要确定本行政区域的法律援助机构。  
法律援助机构负责受理、审查法律援助申请，指派或者安排人员为符合本条例规定的公民提供法律援助。

**Article 5** The judicial administration departments of municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, or county people's governments shall determine legal aid institutions within their respective administrative regions as needed.  
Legal aid institutions shall be responsible for accepting and examining legal aid applications, and appointing or arranging personnel to provide legal aid to citizens who meet the requirements of these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　律师应当依照律师法和本条例的规定履行法律援助义务，为受援人提供符合标准的法律服务，依法维护受援人的合法权益，接受律师协会和司法行政部门的监督。

**Article 6** Lawyers shall perform their legal aid obligations in accordance with the Lawyers Law and these Regulations, provide legal services meeting the standards to recipients, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of recipients according to law, and accept supervision from lawyers associations and judicial administration departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励社会对法律援助活动提供捐助。

**Article 7** The State encourages society to make donations to legal aid activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家支持和鼓励社会团体、事业单位等社会组织利用自身资源为经济困难的公民提供法律援助。

**Article 8** The State supports and encourages social organizations such as social groups and public institutions to use their own resources to provide legal aid to citizens in financial difficulty.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在法律援助工作中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，有关的人民政府、司法行政部门应当给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 9** Relevant people's governments and judicial administration departments shall commend and reward organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in legal aid work.

### 第二章　法律援助范围

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公民对下列需要代理的事项，因经济困难没有委托代理人的，可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助：  
(一)依法请求国家赔偿的；  
(二)请求给予社会保险待遇或者最低生活保障待遇的；  
(三)请求发给抚恤金、救济金的；  
(四)请求给付赡养费、抚养费、扶养费的；  
(五)请求支付劳动报酬的；  
(六)主张因见义勇为行为产生的民事权益的。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以对前款规定以外的法律援助事项作出补充规定。  
公民可以就本条第一款、第二款规定的事项向法律援助机构申请法律咨询。

**Article 10** Citizens who have not authorized any agent due to financial difficulty for the following matters requiring representation may apply to legal aid institutions for legal aid:  
(1) claiming state compensation according to law;  
(2) claiming social insurance benefits or minimum subsistence allowance benefits;  
(3) claiming pensions or relief funds;  
(4) claiming alimony, child support, or support for family members;  
(5) claiming labor remuneration;  
(6) asserting civil rights and interests arising from acts of bravery.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may make supplementary provisions on legal aid matters other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.  
Citizens may apply to legal aid institutions for legal consultation on the matters specified in the first and second paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　刑事诉讼中有下列情形之一的，公民可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助：  
(一)犯罪嫌疑人在被侦查机关第一次讯问后或者采取强制措施之日起，因经济困难没有聘请律师的；  
(二)公诉案件中的被害人及其法定代理人或者近亲属，自案件移送审查起诉之日起，因经济困难没有委托诉讼代理人的；  
(三)自诉案件的自诉人及其法定代理人，自案件被人民法院受理之日起，因经济困难没有委托诉讼代理人的。

**Article 11** In a criminal action, a citizen may apply to a legal aid institution for legal aid under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) a criminal suspect, from the time of the first interrogation by an investigation organ or the date on which compulsory measures are taken, has not engaged a lawyer due to financial difficulty;  
(2) a victim in a public prosecution case, and his or her legal representative or near relative, from the date the case is transferred for examination and prosecution, has not authorized a litigation agent due to financial difficulty;  
(3) a private prosecutor in a private prosecution case, and his or her legal representative, from the date the case is accepted by a People's Court, has not authorized a litigation agent due to financial difficulty.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公诉人出庭公诉的案件，被告人因经济困难或者其他原因没有委托辩护人，人民法院为被告人指定辩护时，法律援助机构应当提供法律援助。  
被告人是盲、聋、哑人或者未成年人而没有委托辩护人的，或者被告人可能被判处死刑而没有委托辩护人的，人民法院为被告人指定辩护时，法律援助机构应当提供法律援助，无须对被告人进行经济状况的审查。

**Article 12** In a case where a public prosecutor appears in court for public prosecution, if the defendant has not entrusted a defender due to financial difficulty or other reasons, and the People's Court designates a defender for the defendant, the legal aid institution shall provide legal aid.  
If the defendant is blind, deaf, mute, or a minor and has not entrusted a defender, or if the defendant may be sentenced to death and has not entrusted a defender, when the People's Court designates a defender for the defendant, the legal aid institution shall provide legal aid without conducting an examination of the defendant's financial status.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本条例所称公民经济困难的标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本行政区域经济发展状况和法律援助事业的需要规定。  
申请人住所地的经济困难标准与受理申请的法律援助机构所在地的经济困难标准不一致的，按照受理申请的法律援助机构所在地的经济困难标准执行。

**Article 13** The standard for a citizen's financial difficulty as mentioned in these Regulations shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on the economic development of their respective administrative regions and the needs of legal aid undertakings.  
If the financial difficulty standard at the applicant's place of domicile is inconsistent with that at the place where the legal aid institution accepting the application is located, the standard at the place where the legal aid institution accepting the application is located shall apply.

### 第三章　法律援助申请和审查

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公民就[本条例第十条](#art-10)所列事项申请法律援助，应当按照下列规定提出：  
(一)请求国家赔偿的，向赔偿义务机关所在地的法律援助机构提出申请；  
(二)请求给予社会保险待遇、最低生活保障待遇或者请求发给抚恤金、救济金的，向提供社会保险待遇、最低生活保障待遇或者发给抚恤金、救济金的义务机关所在地的法律援助机构提出申请；  
(三)请求给付赡养费、抚养费、扶养费的，向给付赡养费、抚养费、扶养费的义务人住所地的法律援助机构提出申请；  
(四)请求支付劳动报酬的，向支付劳动报酬的义务人住所地的法律援助机构提出申请；  
(五)主张因见义勇为行为产生的民事权益的，向被请求人住所地的法律援助机构提出申请。

**Article 14** Citizens applying for legal aid on the matters listed in Article 10 of these Regulations shall submit their applications in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for claiming state compensation, the application shall be submitted to the legal aid institution at the place where the compensating organ is located;  
(2) for claiming social insurance benefits, minimum subsistence allowance benefits, or claiming pensions or relief funds, the application shall be submitted to the legal aid institution at the place where the obligor providing such benefits or funds is located;  
(3) for claiming alimony, child support, or support for family members, the application shall be submitted to the legal aid institution at the place of domicile of the obligor liable for payment;  
(4) for claiming labor remuneration, the application shall be submitted to the legal aid institution at the place of domicile of the obligor liable for payment;  
(5) for asserting civil rights and interests arising from acts of bravery, the application shall be submitted to the legal aid institution at the place of domicile of the person claimed against.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[本条例第十一条](#art-11)所列人员申请法律援助的，应当向审理案件的人民法院所在地的法律援助机构提出申请。被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人的申请由看守所在24小时内转交法律援助机构，申请法律援助所需提交的有关证件、证明材料由看守所通知申请人的法定代理人或者近亲属协助提供。

**Article 15** Persons listed in Article 11 of these Regulations applying for legal aid shall submit their applications to the legal aid institution at the place where the People's Court trying the case is located. The application of a criminal suspect in custody shall be forwarded by the detention house to the legal aid institution within 24 hours. The relevant certificates and evidentiary materials required for the application for legal aid shall be provided with the assistance of the applicant's legal representative or near relative as notified by the detention house.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，由其法定代理人代为提出申请。  
无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人与其法定代理人之间发生诉讼或者因其他利益纠纷需要法律援助的，由与该争议事项无利害关系的其他法定代理人代为提出申请。

**Article 16** If the applicant is a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, the application shall be submitted by his or her legal representative on his or her behalf.  
If a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct has a lawsuit or other interest dispute with his or her legal representative and needs legal aid, the application shall be submitted on his or her behalf by another legal representative who has no interest in the disputed matter.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公民申请代理、刑事辩护的法律援助应当提交下列证件、证明材料：  
(一)身份证或者其他有效的身份证明，代理申请人还应当提交有代理权的证明；  
(二)经济困难的证明；  
(三)与所申请法律援助事项有关的案件材料。  
申请应当采用书面形式，填写申请表；以书面形式提出申请确有困难的，可以口头申请，由法律援助机构工作人员或者代为转交申请的有关机构工作人员作书面记录。

**Article 17** Citizens applying for legal aid for representation or criminal defense shall submit the following certificates and evidentiary materials:  
(1) an identity card or other valid identity certificate, and the applicant acting through an agent shall also submit proof of the authority to act;  
(2) proof of financial difficulty;  
(3) case materials related to the matter for which legal aid is applied.  
The application shall be made in writing and an application form shall be filled out; if it is truly difficult to make the application in writing, an oral application may be made, and a written record shall be made by the legal aid institution staff or the staff of the relevant institution forwarding the application.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　法律援助机构收到法律援助申请后，应当进行审查；认为申请人提交的证件、证明材料不齐全的，可以要求申请人作出必要的补充或者说明，申请人未按要求作出补充或者说明的，视为撤销申请；认为申请人提交的证件、证明材料需要查证的，由法律援助机构向有关机关、单位查证。  
对符合法律援助条件的，法律援助机构应当及时决定提供法律援助；对不符合法律援助条件的，应当书面告知申请人理由。

**Article 18** A legal aid institution shall, after receiving an application for legal aid, examine it; if it considers that the certificates and evidentiary materials submitted by the applicant are incomplete, it may require the applicant to make necessary supplements or explanations; if the applicant fails to make supplements or explanations as required, the application shall be deemed withdrawn; if it considers that the certificates and evidentiary materials submitted by the applicant need verification, the legal aid institution shall verify with the relevant authorities or units.  
For those meeting the conditions for legal aid, the legal aid institution shall promptly decide to provide legal aid; for those not meeting the conditions, it shall inform the applicant in writing of the reasons.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人对法律援助机构作出的不符合法律援助条件的通知有异议的，可以向确定该法律援助机构的司法行政部门提出，司法行政部门应当在收到异议之日起5个工作日内进行审查，经审查认为申请人符合法律援助条件的，应当以书面形式责令法律援助机构及时对该申请人提供法律援助。

**Article 19** If an applicant has an objection to the notice from a legal aid institution that he or she does not meet the conditions for legal aid, the applicant may raise the objection with the judicial administration department that established the legal aid institution. The judicial administration department shall conduct an examination within 5 working days from the date of receiving the objection. If upon examination it considers that the applicant meets the conditions for legal aid, it shall order the legal aid institution in writing to provide legal aid to the applicant promptly.

### 第四章　法律援助实施

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　由人民法院指定辩护的案件，人民法院在开庭10日前将指定辩护通知书和起诉书副本或者判决书副本送交其所在地的法律援助机构；人民法院不在其所在地审判的，可以将指定辩护通知书和起诉书副本或者判决书副本送交审判地的法律援助机构。

**Article 20** For cases where the People's Court designates a defender, the People's Court shall, 10 days before the court session, deliver the notice of designated defense and a copy of the indictment or a copy of the judgment to the legal aid institution at its location; if the People's Court does not adjudicate at its location, it may deliver the notice of designated defense and a copy of the indictment or a copy of the judgment to the legal aid institution at the place of adjudication.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　法律援助机构可以指派律师事务所安排律师或者安排本机构的工作人员办理法律援助案件；也可以根据其他社会组织的要求，安排其所属人员办理法律援助案件。对人民法院指定辩护的案件，法律援助机构应当在开庭3日前将确定的承办人员名单回复作出指定的人民法院。

**Article 21** A legal aid institution may assign a law firm to arrange a lawyer, or arrange staff members of its own institution, to handle legal aid cases; it may also, upon request of other social organizations, arrange personnel affiliated with such organizations to handle legal aid cases. For cases where the People's Court designates a defender, the legal aid institution shall, 3 days before the court session, reply to the People's Court that made the designation with the list of the determined personnel handling the case.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　办理法律援助案件的人员，应当遵守职业道德和执业纪律，提供法律援助不得收取任何财物。

**Article 22** Persons handling legal aid cases shall observe professional ethics and practice discipline, and shall not accept any property or money for providing legal aid.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　办理法律援助案件的人员遇有下列情形之一的，应当向法律援助机构报告，法律援助机构经审查核实的，应当终止该项法律援助：  
(一)受援人的经济收入状况发生变化，不再符合法律援助条件的；  
(二)案件终止审理或者已被撤销的；  
(三)受援人又自行委托律师或者其他代理人的；  
(四)受援人要求终止法律援助的。

**Article 23** Persons handling legal aid cases shall report to the legal aid institution when encountering any of the following circumstances, and the legal aid institution, upon verification, shall terminate the legal aid:  
(1) the recipient's economic income status has changed and no longer meets the conditions for legal aid;  
(2) the case has been terminated or has been withdrawn;  
(3) the recipient has on his or her own entrusted a lawyer or other agent;  
(4) the recipient requests termination of legal aid.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　受指派办理法律援助案件的律师或者接受安排办理法律援助案件的社会组织人员在案件结案时，应当向法律援助机构提交有关的法律文书副本或者复印件以及结案报告等材料。  
法律援助机构收到前款规定的结案材料后，应当向受指派办理法律援助案件的律师或者接受安排办理法律援助案件的社会组织人员支付法律援助办案补贴。  
法律援助办案补贴的标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门会同同级财政部门，根据当地经济发展水平，参考法律援助机构办理各类法律援助案件的平均成本等因素核定，并可以根据需要调整。

**Article 24** Lawyers assigned to handle legal aid cases or personnel of social organizations arranged to handle legal aid cases shall, upon conclusion of the case, submit to the legal aid institution copies or photocopies of relevant legal instruments and the case closing report, among other materials.  
After receiving the case closing materials specified in the preceding paragraph, the legal aid institution shall pay a case handling subsidy to the lawyer assigned to handle the legal aid case or the personnel of the social organization arranged to handle the legal aid case.  
The standard for legal aid case handling subsidies shall be determined by the judicial administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the finance department at the same level, based on the local economic development level, with reference to factors such as the average cost of handling various types of legal aid cases by legal aid institutions, and may be adjusted as needed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　法律援助机构对公民申请的法律咨询服务，应当即时办理；复杂疑难的，可以预约择时办理。

**Article 25** Legal aid institutions shall handle promptly legal consultation services applied for by citizens; for complex or difficult matters, they may handle them by appointment at a scheduled time.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　法律援助机构及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员以及其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分：  
(一)为不符合法律援助条件的人员提供法律援助，或者拒绝为符合法律援助条件的人员提供法律援助的；  
(二)办理法律援助案件收取财物的；  
(三)从事有偿法律服务的；  
(四)侵占、私分、挪用法律援助经费的。  
办理法律援助案件收取的财物，由司法行政部门责令退还；从事有偿法律服务的违法所得，由司法行政部门予以没收；侵占、私分、挪用法律援助经费的，由司法行政部门责令追回，情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where a legal aid institution or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) providing legal aid to persons who do not meet the conditions for legal aid, or refusing to provide legal aid to persons who meet the conditions for legal aid;  
(2) accepting property or money for handling legal aid cases;  
(3) engaging in paid legal services;  
(4) embezzling, privately dividing, or misappropriating legal aid funds.  
Any property or money accepted for handling legal aid cases shall be ordered by the judicial administration department to be returned; any illegal gains from engaging in paid legal services shall be confiscated by the judicial administration department; for embezzling, privately dividing, or misappropriating legal aid funds, the judicial administration department shall order recovery, and if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　律师事务所拒绝法律援助机构的指派，不安排本所律师办理法律援助案件的，由司法行政部门给予警告、责令改正；情节严重的，给予1个月以上3个月以下停业整顿的处罚。

**Article 27** If a law firm refuses the assignment by a legal aid institution and fails to arrange for a lawyer of the firm to handle a legal aid case, the judicial administration department shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a penalty of suspension of operations for rectification for a period of not less than one month nor more than three months.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　律师有下列情形之一的，由司法行政部门给予警告、责令改正；情节严重的，给予1个月以上3个月以下停止执业的处罚：  
(一)无正当理由拒绝接受、擅自终止法律援助案件的；  
(二)办理法律援助案件收取财物的。  
有前款第(二)项违法行为的，由司法行政部门责令退还违法所得的财物，可以并处所收财物价值1倍以上3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 28** Where a lawyer commits any of the following acts, the judicial administration department shall give him or her a warning and order him or her to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a penalty of suspension of practice for a period of not less than one month nor more than three months:  
(1) refusing to accept or unilaterally terminating a legal aid case without justifiable reasons;  
(2) accepting property or money for handling a legal aid case.  
If the lawyer commits the illegal act specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph, the judicial administration department shall order the return of the illegally obtained property or money, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time nor more than three times the value of the property or money accepted.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　律师办理法律援助案件违反职业道德和执业纪律的，按照律师法的规定予以处罚。

**Article 29** If a lawyer violates professional ethics and practice discipline when handling a legal aid case, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Lawyers Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　司法行政部门工作人员在法律援助的监督管理工作中，有滥用职权、玩忽职守行为的，依法给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** If a staff member of a judicial administration department, in the supervision and administration of legal aid, commits an act of abuse of power or dereliction of duty, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自2003年9月1日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2003.
