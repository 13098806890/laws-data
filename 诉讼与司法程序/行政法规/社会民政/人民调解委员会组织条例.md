# 人民调解委员会组织条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1989-06-16  
**生效日期**：1989-06-17

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强人民调解委员会的建设，及时调解民间纠纷，增进人民团结，维护社会安定，以利于社会主义现代化建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the construction of people's mediation committees, timely mediating civil disputes, enhancing unity among the people, maintaining social stability, and facilitating socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民调解委员会是村民委员会和居民委员会下设的调解民间纠纷的群众性组织，在基层人民政府和基层人民法院指导下进行工作。  
基层人民政府及其派出机关指导人民调解委员会的日常工作由司法助理员负责。

**Article 2** A people's mediation committee is a mass organization established under a villagers committee or a residents committee to mediate civil disputes, and shall carry out its work under the guidance of the primary people's government and the primary people's court.  
The judicial assistant shall be responsible for guiding the daily work of the people's mediation committee by the primary people's government and its dispatched offices.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民调解委员会由委员三至九人组成，设主任一人，必要时可以设副主任。  
人民调解委员会委员除由村民委员会成员或者居民委员会成员兼任的以外由群众选举产生，每三年改选一次，可以连选连任。  
多民族居住地区的人民调解委员会中，应当有人数较少的民族的成员。  
人民调解委员会委员不能任职时，由原选举单位补选。  
人民调解委员会委员严重失职或者违法乱纪的，由原选举单位撤换。

**Article 3** A people's mediation committee shall be composed of three to nine members, with one director, and may have one or more deputy directors if necessary.  
The members of a people's mediation committee, except for those concurrently held by members of the villagers committee or residents committee, shall be elected by the masses, and shall be re-elected every three years, and may be re-elected consecutively.  
In a people's mediation committee in a multi-ethnic area, there shall be members from minority ethnic groups.  
When a member of a people's mediation committee is unable to hold office, a by-election shall be held by the original electoral unit.  
If a member of a people's mediation committee is seriously derelict of duty or engages in illegal activities, the original electoral unit shall remove him.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　为人公正，联系群众，热心人民调解工作，并有一定法律知识和政策水平的成年公民，可以当选为人民调解委员会委员。

**Article 4** Any adult citizen who is fair-minded, maintains close ties with the masses, is enthusiastic about people's mediation work, and has a certain knowledge of law and policy may be elected as a member of a people's mediation committee.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民调解委员会的任务为调解民间纠纷，并通过调解工作宣传法律、法规、规章和政策，教育公民遵纪守法，尊重社会公德。  
人民调解委员会应当向村民委员会或者居民委员会反映民间纠纷和调解工作的情况。

**Article 5** The task of a people's mediation committee is to mediate civil disputes, and through mediation work, to publicize laws, regulations, rules and policies, and to educate citizens to abide by the law and respect social ethics.  
A people's mediation committee shall report to the villagers committee or the residents committee on the situation of civil disputes and mediation work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民调解委员会的调解工作应当遵守以下原则：  
(一)依据法律、法规、规章和政策进行调解，法律、法规、规章和政策没有明确规定的，依据社会公德进行调解；  
(二)在双方当事人自愿平等的基础上进行调解；  
(三)尊重当事人的诉讼权利，不得因未经调解或者调解不成而阻止当事人向人民法院起诉。

**Article 6** The mediation work of a people's mediation committee shall observe the following principles:  
(1) Mediation shall be conducted pursuant to laws, regulations, rules and policies; where there is no explicit provision in laws, regulations, rules or policies, mediation shall be conducted pursuant to social ethics;  
(2) Mediation shall be conducted on the basis of the voluntary and equal will of both parties;  
(3) The litigation rights of the parties shall be respected, and no party shall be prevented from instituting a prosecution in a people's court on the ground that mediation has not been conducted or has failed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民调解委员会根据当事人的申请及时调解纠纷；当事人没有申请的，也可以主动调解。  
人民调解委员会调解纠纷可以由委员一人或数人进行；跨地区、跨单位的纠纷，可以由有关的各方调解组织共同调解。  
人民调解委员会调解纠纷，可以邀请有关单位和个人参加，被邀请的单位和个人应当给予支持。

**Article 7** A people's mediation committee shall promptly mediate disputes upon the application of the parties; it may also mediate on its own initiative if no application is made.  
Mediation of disputes by a people's mediation committee may be conducted by one or more members; disputes involving multiple areas or units may be mediated jointly by the relevant mediation organizations.  
When mediating a dispute, a people's mediation committee may invite relevant units and individuals to participate, and the invited units and individuals shall provide support.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民调解委员会调解纠纷，应当在查明事实、分清是非的基础上，充分说理，耐心疏导，消除隔阂，帮助当事人达成协议。  
调解纠纷应当进行登记，制作笔录，根据需要或者当事人的请求，可以制作调解协议书。调解协议书应当有双方当事人和调解人员的签名，并加盖人民调解委员会的印章。

**Article 8** When mediating a dispute, a people's mediation committee shall, on the basis of ascertaining the facts and distinguishing right from wrong, fully reason, patiently guide, eliminate estrangement, and help the parties reach an agreement.  
Mediation of a dispute shall be recorded, and transcripts shall be made; a mediation agreement may be prepared as needed or at the request of the parties. The mediation agreement shall bear the signatures of both parties and the mediator, and shall be affixed with the seal of the people's mediation committee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民调解委员会主持下达成的调解协议，当事人应当履行。  
经过调解，当事人未达成协议或者达成协议后又反悔的，任何一方可以请求基层人民政府处理，也可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 9** A mediation agreement reached under the auspices of a people's mediation committee shall be performed by the parties.  
If, after mediation, the parties fail to reach an agreement or renege after reaching an agreement, either party may request the primary people's government to handle the matter, or may institute a prosecution in a people's court.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　基层人民政府对于人民调解委员会主持下达成的调解协议，符合法律、法规、规章和政策的，应当予以支持；违背法律、法规、规章和政策的，应当予以纠正。

**Article 10** The primary people's government shall support a mediation agreement reached under the auspices of a people's mediation committee if it conforms to laws, regulations, rules and policies; if it violates laws, regulations, rules or policies, the primary people's government shall correct it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民调解委员会调解民间纠纷不收费。

**Article 11** A people's mediation committee shall not charge any fee for mediating civil disputes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民调解委员会委员必须遵守以下纪律：  
(一)不得徇私舞弊；  
(二)不得对当事人压制、打击报复；  
(三)不得侮辱、处罚当事人；  
(四)不得泄露当事人的隐私；  
(五)不得吃请受礼。

**Article 12** Members of a people's mediation committee must observe the following discipline:  
(1) They shall not engage in fraudulent practices for personal gain;  
(2) They shall not suppress or retaliate against the parties;  
(3) They shall not insult or punish the parties;  
(4) They shall not divulge the privacy of the parties;  
(5) They shall not accept invitations to meals or gifts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府对成绩显著的人民调解委员会和调解委员应当予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 13** People's governments at all levels shall commend and reward people's mediation committees and mediators that have achieved outstanding results.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对人民调解委员会委员，根据情况可以给予适当补贴。  
人民调解委员会的工作经费和调解委员的补贴经费，由村民委员会或者居民委员会解决。

**Article 14** Appropriate subsidies may be given to members of a people's mediation committee according to circumstances.  
The working expenses of a people's mediation committee and the subsidies for mediators shall be borne by the villagers committee or the residents committee.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业、事业单位根据需要设立的人民调解委员会，参照本条例执行。

**Article 15** People's mediation committees established by enterprises and institutions as needed shall be governed by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例由司法部负责解释。

**Article 16** The Ministry of Justice shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1954年3月22日原中央人民政府政务院公布的《人民调解委员会暂行组织通则》同时废止。

**Article 17** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisional General Rules for the Organization of People's Mediation Committees promulgated by the former Central People's Government Administration Council on March 22, 1954 shall be abolished simultaneously.
