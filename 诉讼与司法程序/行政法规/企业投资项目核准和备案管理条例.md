# 企业投资项目核准和备案管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2016-11-30  
**生效日期**：2017-02-01

> (2016年10月8日国务院第149次常务会议通过　2016年11月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第673号公布　自2017年2月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范政府对企业投资项目的核准和备案行为，加快转变政府的投资管理职能，落实企业投资自主权，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称企业投资项目(以下简称项目)，是指企业在中国境内投资建设的固定资产投资项目。 第三条　对关系国家安全、涉及全国重大生产力布局、战略性资源开发和重大公共利益等项目，实行核准管理。具体项目范围以及核准机关、核准权限依照政府核准的投资项目目录执行。政府核准的投资项目目录由国务院投资主管部门会同国务院有关部门提出，报国务院批准后实施，并适时调整。国务院另有规定的，依照其规定。 对前款规定以外的项目，实行备案管理。除国务院另有规定的，实行备案管理的项目按照属地原则备案，备案机关及其权限由省、自治区、直辖市和计划单列市人民政府规定。 第四条　除涉及国家秘密的项目外，项目核准、备案通过国家建立的项目在线监管平台(以下简称在线平台)办理。 核准机关、备案机关以及其他有关部门统一使用在线平台生成的项目代码办理相关手续。 国务院投资主管部门会同有关部门制定在线平台管理办法。 第五条　核准机关、备案机关应当通过在线平台列明与项目有关的产业政策，公开项目核准的办理流程、办理时限等，并为企业提供相关咨询服务。 第六条　企业办理项目核准手续，应当向核准机关提交项目申请书；由国务院核准的项目，向国务院投资主管部门提交项目申请书。项目申请书应当包括下列内容： (一)企业基本情况； (二)项目情况，包括项目名称、建设地点、建设规模、建设内容等； (三)项目利用资源情况分析以及对生态环境的影响分析； (四)项目对经济和社会的影响分析。 企业应当对项目申请书内容的真实性负责。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范政府对企业投资项目的核准和备案行为，加快转变政府的投资管理职能，落实企业投资自主权，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate the government's approval and filing of enterprise investment projects, accelerate the transformation of the government's investment management functions, and implement the enterprise's autonomy in investment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称企业投资项目(以下简称项目)，是指企业在中国境内投资建设的固定资产投资项目。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term "enterprise investment project" (hereinafter referred to as "project") means a fixed asset investment project invested and constructed by an enterprise within the territory of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对关系国家安全、涉及全国重大生产力布局、战略性资源开发和重大公共利益等项目，实行核准管理。具体项目范围以及核准机关、核准权限依照政府核准的投资项目目录执行。政府核准的投资项目目录由国务院投资主管部门会同国务院有关部门提出，报国务院批准后实施，并适时调整。国务院另有规定的，依照其规定。  
对前款规定以外的项目，实行备案管理。除国务院另有规定的，实行备案管理的项目按照属地原则备案，备案机关及其权限由省、自治区、直辖市和计划单列市人民政府规定。

**Article 3** Approval management shall be implemented for projects that relate to national security, involve the national major productivity layout, strategic resource development, and major public interests. The specific project scope, as well as the approving authority and the scope of approval, shall be implemented in accordance with the Catalogue of Government-Approved Investment Projects. The Catalogue of Government-Approved Investment Projects shall be proposed by the investment department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, reported to the State Council for approval before implementation, and adjusted as appropriate. Where the State Council provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.  
For projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, record-filing management shall be implemented. Except as otherwise provided by the State Council, projects subject to record-filing management shall be filed in accordance with the territorial principle, and the filing authority and its authority shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities under separate state planning.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　除涉及国家秘密的项目外，项目核准、备案通过国家建立的项目在线监管平台(以下简称在线平台)办理。  
核准机关、备案机关以及其他有关部门统一使用在线平台生成的项目代码办理相关手续。  
国务院投资主管部门会同有关部门制定在线平台管理办法。

**Article 4** Except for projects involving state secrets, project approval and record-filing shall be handled through the project online supervision platform (hereinafter referred to as the online platform) established by the State.  
The approving authority, the filing authority, and other relevant departments shall uniformly use the project code generated by the online platform to handle relevant procedures.  
The investment department under the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, formulate the measures for the administration of the online platform.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　核准机关、备案机关应当通过在线平台列明与项目有关的产业政策，公开项目核准的办理流程、办理时限等，并为企业提供相关咨询服务。

**Article 5** The approving authority and the filing authority shall, through the online platform, list the industrial policies related to the project, disclose the processing flow and time limit for project approval, and provide relevant consulting services to enterprises.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业办理项目核准手续，应当向核准机关提交项目申请书；由国务院核准的项目，向国务院投资主管部门提交项目申请书。项目申请书应当包括下列内容：  
(一)企业基本情况；  
(二)项目情况，包括项目名称、建设地点、建设规模、建设内容等；  
(三)项目利用资源情况分析以及对生态环境的影响分析；  
(四)项目对经济和社会的影响分析。  
企业应当对项目申请书内容的真实性负责。  
法律、行政法规规定办理相关手续作为项目核准前置条件的，企业应当提交已经办理相关手续的证明文件。

**Article 6** To go through the procedures of project approval, an enterprise shall submit a project application to the approving authority; for a project to be approved by the State Council, the enterprise shall submit a project application to the investment department under the State Council. The project application shall include the following:  
(1) the basic information of the enterprise;  
(2) the project information, including the project name, construction location, construction scale, construction content, etc.;  
(3) an analysis of the utilization of resources by the project and the impact on the ecological environment;  
(4) an analysis of the impact of the project on the economy and society.  
The enterprise shall be responsible for the authenticity of the contents of the project application.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide that the handling of relevant procedures shall be a prerequisite for project approval, the enterprise shall submit supporting documents that the relevant procedures have been handled.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　项目申请书由企业自主组织编制，任何单位和个人不得强制企业委托中介服务机构编制项目申请书。  
核准机关应当制定并公布项目申请书示范文本，明确项目申请书编制要求。

**Article 7** The project application shall be independently prepared by the enterprise, and no entity or individual may compel the enterprise to entrust an intermediary service agency to prepare the project application.  
The approving authority shall formulate and publish a model text for the project application, specifying the requirements for preparing the project application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　由国务院有关部门核准的项目，企业可以通过项目所在地省、自治区、直辖市和计划单列市人民政府有关部门(以下称地方人民政府有关部门)转送项目申请书，地方人民政府有关部门应当自收到项目申请书之日起5个工作日内转送核准机关。  
由国务院核准的项目，企业通过地方人民政府有关部门转送项目申请书的，地方人民政府有关部门应当在前款规定的期限内将项目申请书转送国务院投资主管部门，由国务院投资主管部门审核后报国务院核准。

**Article 8** For a project to be approved by a relevant department of the State Council, the enterprise may transmit the project application through the relevant department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or city under separate state planning where the project is located (hereinafter referred to as the relevant department of the local people's government), and the relevant department of the local people's government shall, within 5 working days from the date of receiving the project application, transmit it to the approving authority.  
For a project to be approved by the State Council, if the enterprise transmits the project application through the relevant department of the local people's government, the relevant department of the local people's government shall, within the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph, transmit the project application to the investment department under the State Council, which shall, after review, report it to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　核准机关应当从下列方面对项目进行审查：  
(一)是否危害经济安全、社会安全、生态安全等国家安全；  
(二)是否符合相关发展建设规划、技术标准和产业政策；  
(三)是否合理开发并有效利用资源；  
(四)是否对重大公共利益产生不利影响。  
项目涉及有关部门或者项目所在地地方人民政府职责的，核准机关应当书面征求其意见，被征求意见单位应当及时书面回复。  
核准机关委托中介服务机构对项目进行评估的，应当明确评估重点；除项目情况复杂的，评估时限不得超过30个工作日。评估费用由核准机关承担。

**Article 9** The approving authority shall review the project from the following aspects:  
(1) whether it endangers economic security, social security, ecological security, and other national security;  
(2) whether it complies with relevant development and construction plans, technical standards, and industrial policies;  
(3) whether resources are rationally developed and effectively utilized;  
(4) whether it has an adverse effect on major public interests.  
Where the project involves the responsibilities of relevant departments or the local people's government of the project location, the approving authority shall solicit their opinions in writing, and the consulted units shall promptly reply in writing.  
Where the approving authority entrusts an intermediary service agency to evaluate the project, it shall specify the evaluation focus; except where the project is complex, the evaluation time limit shall not exceed 30 working days. The evaluation fee shall be borne by the approving authority.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　核准机关应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，作出是否予以核准的决定；项目情况复杂或者需要征求有关单位意见的，经本机关主要负责人批准，可以延长核准期限，但延长的期限不得超过40个工作日。核准机关委托中介服务机构对项目进行评估的，评估时间不计入核准期限。  
核准机关对项目予以核准的，应当向企业出具核准文件；不予核准的，应当书面通知企业并说明理由。由国务院核准的项目，由国务院投资主管部门根据国务院的决定向企业出具核准文件或者不予核准的书面通知。

**Article 10** The approving authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to approve the project; if the project is complex or opinions from relevant units are required, the approval period may be extended with the approval of the principal responsible person of the authority, but the extension shall not exceed 40 working days. Where the approving authority entrusts an intermediary service agency to evaluate the project, the evaluation time shall not be counted in the approval period.  
Where the approving authority approves the project, it shall issue an approval document to the enterprise; if it does not approve, it shall notify the enterprise in writing and state the reasons. For a project approved by the State Council, the investment department under the State Council shall, based on the decision of the State Council, issue an approval document or a written notice of disapproval to the enterprise.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业拟变更已核准项目的建设地点，或者拟对建设规模、建设内容等作较大变更的，应当向核准机关提出变更申请。核准机关应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，作出是否同意变更的书面决定。

**Article 11** If an enterprise intends to change the construction site of a project that has been approved, or intends to make significant changes to the construction scale, construction content, etc., it shall submit an application for change to the approval authority. The approval authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a written decision on whether to approve the change.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　项目自核准机关作出予以核准决定或者同意变更决定之日起2年内未开工建设，需要延期开工建设的，企业应当在2年期限届满的30个工作日前，向核准机关申请延期开工建设。核准机关应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，作出是否同意延期开工建设的决定。开工建设只能延期一次，期限最长不得超过1年。国家对项目延期开工建设另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 12** If a project has not commenced construction within two years from the date of the approval decision made by the approval authority or the decision approving the change, and an extension of the construction commencement is needed, the enterprise shall, within 30 working days before the expiry of the two-year period, apply to the approval authority for an extension of construction commencement. The approval authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to approve the extension of construction commencement. Construction commencement may be extended only once, and the extension period shall not exceed one year. Where the State has other provisions on the extension of project construction commencement, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　实行备案管理的项目，企业应当在开工建设前通过在线平台将下列信息告知备案机关：  
(一)企业基本情况；  
(二)项目名称、建设地点、建设规模、建设内容；  
(三)项目总投资额；  
(四)项目符合产业政策的声明。  
企业应当对备案项目信息的真实性负责。  
备案机关收到本条第一款规定的全部信息即为备案；企业告知的信息不齐全的，备案机关应当指导企业补正。  
企业需要备案证明的，可以要求备案机关出具或者通过在线平台自行打印。

**Article 13** For a project subject to recordation management, the enterprise shall, before commencing construction, notify the recordation authority of the following information through the online platform:  
(1) basic information of the enterprise;  
(2) project name, construction site, construction scale, and construction content;  
(3) total investment amount of the project;  
(4) a statement that the project complies with the industrial policy.  
The enterprise shall be responsible for the truthfulness of the project recordation information.  
The recordation authority's receipt of all the information specified in the first paragraph of this Article shall constitute recordation; if the information notified by the enterprise is incomplete, the recordation authority shall guide the enterprise to supplement and correct it.  
If the enterprise needs a recordation certificate, it may request the recordation authority to issue such certificate or print it by itself through the online platform.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　已备案项目信息发生较大变更的，企业应当及时告知备案机关。

**Article 14** If there is a significant change in the information of a project that has been recorded, the enterprise shall promptly notify the recordation authority.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　备案机关发现已备案项目属于产业政策禁止投资建设或者实行核准管理的，应当及时告知企业予以纠正或者依法办理核准手续，并通知有关部门。

**Article 15** If the recordation authority discovers that a recorded project falls within the scope of investment prohibited by industrial policy or is subject to approval management, it shall promptly notify the enterprise to make corrections or to go through the approval formalities according to law, and shall inform the relevant departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　核准机关、备案机关以及依法对项目负有监督管理职责的其他有关部门应当加强事中事后监管，按照谁审批谁监管、谁主管谁监管的原则，落实监管责任，采取在线监测、现场核查等方式，加强对项目实施的监督检查。  
企业应当通过在线平台如实报送项目开工建设、建设进度、竣工的基本信息。

**Article 16** The approval authority, the recordation authority, and other relevant departments responsible for supervision and management of projects according to law shall strengthen interim and ex-post supervision, implement supervision responsibilities in accordance with the principle of "who approves shall supervise, who is in charge shall supervise," and strengthen the supervision and inspection of project implementation by means such as online monitoring and on-site verification.  
The enterprise shall truthfully report the basic information on project construction commencement, construction progress, and completion through the online platform.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　核准机关、备案机关以及依法对项目负有监督管理职责的其他有关部门应当建立项目信息共享机制，通过在线平台实现信息共享。  
企业在项目核准、备案以及项目实施中的违法行为及其处理信息，通过国家社会信用信息平台向社会公示。

**Article 17** The approval authority, the recordation authority, and other relevant departments responsible for supervision and management of projects according to law shall establish a project information sharing mechanism and achieve information sharing through the online platform.  
Information on the illegal acts of the enterprise in project approval, recordation, and project implementation, as well as the handling information, shall be disclosed to the public through the National Social Credit Information Platform.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　实行核准管理的项目，企业未依照本条例规定办理核准手续开工建设或者未按照核准的建设地点、建设规模、建设内容等进行建设的，由核准机关责令停止建设或者责令停产，对企业处项目总投资额1‰以上5‰以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款，属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分。  
以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得项目核准文件，尚未开工建设的，由核准机关撤销核准文件，处项目总投资额1‰以上5‰以下的罚款；已经开工建设的，依照前款规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** For a project subject to approval management, if the enterprise commences construction without going through the approval formalities in accordance with these Regulations, or constructs without following the approved construction site, construction scale, construction content, etc., the approval authority shall order the cessation of construction or production, impose a fine of not less than 1‰ but not more than 5‰ of the total investment amount of the project on the enterprise; and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons. If such persons are state functionaries, they shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where the project approval document is obtained by fraudulent, bribery or other illegitimate means, and construction has not yet commenced, the approval authority shall revoke the approval document and impose a fine of not less than 1‰ but not more than 5‰ of the total investment amount of the project; if construction has already commenced, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　实行备案管理的项目，企业未依照本条例规定将项目信息或者已备案项目的信息变更情况告知备案机关，或者向备案机关提供虚假信息的，由备案机关责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 19** For a project subject to recordation management, if the enterprise fails to notify the recordation authority of the project information or changes to the information of a recorded project in accordance with these Regulations, or provides false information to the recordation authority, the recordation authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业投资建设产业政策禁止投资建设项目的，由县级以上人民政府投资主管部门责令停止建设或者责令停产并恢复原状，对企业处项目总投资额5‰以上10‰以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分。法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 20** If an enterprise invests in and constructs a project that is prohibited for investment by industrial policy, the competent investment department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of construction or production and restoration to the original state, impose a fine of not less than 5‰ but not more than 10‰ of the total investment amount of the project on the enterprise; and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons. If such persons are state functionaries, they shall be given sanctions according to law. Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　核准机关、备案机关及其工作人员在项目核准、备案工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where the approval authority, the filing authority or their staff neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in project approval or filing, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　事业单位、社会团体等非企业组织在中国境内投资建设的固定资产投资项目适用本条例，但通过预算安排的固定资产投资项目除外。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall apply to fixed asset investment projects invested and constructed by non-enterprise organizations such as public institutions and social organizations within the territory of China, except for fixed asset investment projects arranged through budgets.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国防科技工业企业在中国境内投资建设的固定资产投资项目核准和备案管理办法，由国务院国防科技工业管理部门根据本条例的原则另行制定。

**Article 23** The measures for the administration of approval and filing of fixed asset investment projects invested and constructed by national defense science, technology and industry enterprises within the territory of China shall be separately formulated by the national defense science, technology and industry administration department of the State Council in accordance with the principles of these Regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自2017年2月1日起施行。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2017.
