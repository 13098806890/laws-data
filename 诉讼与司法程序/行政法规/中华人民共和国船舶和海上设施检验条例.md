# 中华人民共和国船舶和海上设施检验条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保证船舶、海上设施和船运货物集装箱具备安全航行、安全作业的技术条件，保障人民生命财产的安全和防止水域环境污染，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of ensuring that ships, offshore installations and freight containers have the technical conditions for safe navigation and safe operations, safeguarding the safety of people's lives and property, and preventing environmental pollution of waters.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于：  
(一)在中华人民共和国登记或者将在中华人民共和国登记的船舶(以下简称中国籍船舶)；  
(二)根据本条例或者国家有关规定申请检验的外国籍船舶；  
(三)在中华人民共和国沿海水域内设置或者将在中华人民共和国沿海水域内设置的海上设施(以下简称海上设施)；  
(四)在中华人民共和国登记的企业法人所拥有的船运货物集装箱(以下简称集装箱)。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to:  
(1) ships registered or to be registered in the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as Chinese ships);  
(2) foreign ships applying for survey in accordance with these Regulations or the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) offshore installations established or to be established in the coastal waters of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as offshore installations);  
(4) freight containers owned by enterprise legal persons registered in the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as containers).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国船舶检验局(以下简称船检局)是依照本条例规定实施各项检验工作的主管机构。  
经国务院交通主管部门批准，船检局可以在主要港口和工业区设置船舶检验机构。  
经国务院交通主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门可以在所辖港口设置地方船舶检验机构。

**Article 3** The Register of Shipping of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Ship Inspection Bureau) shall be the competent authority for carrying out various survey work in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
With the approval of the competent transport department of the State Council, the Ship Inspection Bureau may establish ship survey organizations in major ports and industrial areas.  
With the approval of the competent transport department of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the competent transport departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may establish local ship survey organizations in the ports under their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国船级社是社会团体性质的船舶检验机构，承办国内外船舶、海上设施和集装箱的入级检验、鉴证检验和公证检验业务；经船检局授权，可以代行法定检验。

**Article 4** The China Classification Society shall be a ship survey organization of a social group nature, undertaking the classification survey, certification survey and notarial survey of ships, offshore installations and containers at home and abroad; upon authorization by the Ship Inspection Bureau, it may exercise statutory survey on its behalf.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　实施本条例规定的各项检验，应当贯彻安全第一、质量第一的原则，鼓励新技术的开发和应用。

**Article 5** In implementing the various surveys provided for in these Regulations, the principles of safety first and quality first shall be observed, and the development and application of new technologies shall be encouraged.

### 第二章　船舶检验

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶检验分别由下列机构实施：  
(一)船检局设置的船舶检验机构；  
(二)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通主管部门设置的地方船舶检验机构；  
(三)船检局委托、指定或者认可的检验机构。  
前款所列机构，以下统称船舶检验机构。

**Article 6** Ship survey shall be carried out respectively by the following organizations:  
(1) ship survey organizations established by the Ship Inspection Bureau;  
(2) local ship survey organizations established by the competent transport departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) survey organizations entrusted, designated or recognized by the Ship Inspection Bureau.  
The organizations listed in the preceding paragraph shall hereinafter be collectively referred to as ship survey organizations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国籍船舶的所有人或者经营人，必须向船舶检验机构申请下列检验：  
(一)建造或者改建船舶时，申请建造检验；  
(二)营运中的船舶，申请定期检验；  
(三)由外国籍船舶改为中国籍船舶的，申请初次检验。

**Article 7** The owner or operator of a Chinese ship must apply to a ship survey organization for the following surveys:  
(1) construction survey when constructing or rebuilding a ship;  
(2) periodical survey for a ship in service;  
(3) initial survey when a foreign ship is changed into a Chinese ship.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中国籍船舶所使用的有关海上交通安全的和防止水域环境污染的重要设备、部件和材料，须经船舶检验机构按照有关规定检验。

**Article 8** Important equipment, components and materials used on Chinese ships relating to maritime traffic safety and prevention of environmental pollution of waters must be surveyed by a ship survey organization in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国籍船舶须由船舶检验机构测定总吨位和净吨位，核定载重线和乘客定额。

**Article 9** The gross tonnage and net tonnage of a Chinese ship must be measured by a ship survey organization, and the load line and passenger quota must be approved.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在中国沿海水域从事钻探、开发作业的外国籍钻井船、移动式平台的所有人或者经营人，必须向船检局设置或者指定的船舶检验机构申请下列检验：  
(一)作业前检验；  
(二)作业期间的定期检验。

**Article 10** The owner or operator of a foreign drilling ship or mobile platform engaged in drilling and development operations in the coastal waters of China must apply to a ship survey organization established or designated by the Ship Inspection Bureau for the following surveys:  
(1) pre-operation survey;  
(2) periodic survey during operation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中国沿海水域内的移动式平台、浮船坞和其他大型设施进行拖带航行，起拖前必须向船检局设置的或者指定的船舶检验机构申请拖航检验。

**Article 11** A mobile platform, floating dock or other large installation within the coastal waters of China, before being towed, must apply to a ship survey organization established or designated by the Ship Inspection Bureau for a towing survey.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中国籍船舶有下列情形之一的，船舶所有人或者经营人必须向船舶检验机构申请临时检验：  
(一)因发生事故，影响船舶适航性能的；  
(二)改变船舶证书所限定的用途或者航区的；  
(三)船舶检验机构签发的证书失效的；  
(四)海上交通安全或者环境保护主管机关责成检验的。  
在中国港口内的外国籍船舶，有前款(一)、(四)项所列情形之一的，必须向船检局设置或者指定的船舶检验机构申请临时检验。

**Article 12** Where a Chinese ship falls under any of the following circumstances, the owner or operator must apply to a ship survey organization for a special survey:  
(1) an accident has occurred that affects the ship's seaworthiness;  
(2) the use or navigation area restricted by the ship's certificate has been changed;  
(3) the certificate issued by the ship survey organization has ceased to have effect;  
(4) the competent authority for maritime traffic safety or environmental protection has ordered a survey.  
Where a foreign ship in a Chinese port falls under the circumstances specified in items (1) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, it must apply to a ship survey organization established or designated by the Ship Inspection Bureau for a special survey.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下列中国籍船舶，必须向中国船级社申请入级检验：  
(一)从事国际航行的船舶；  
(二)在海上航行的乘客定额一百人以上的客船；  
(三)载重量一千吨以上的油船；  
(四)滚装船、液化气体运输船和散装化学品运输船；  
(五)船舶所有人或者经营人要求入级的其他船舶。

**Article 13** The following Chinese ships must apply to the China Classification Society for classification survey:  
(1) ships engaged in international voyages;  
(2) passenger ships with a passenger quota of 100 or more sailing at sea;  
(3) oil tankers with a deadweight of 1,000 tons or more;  
(4) ro-ro ships, liquefied gas carriers and bulk chemical carriers;  
(5) other ships for which the owner or operator requires classification.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶经检验合格后，船舶检验机构应当按照规定签发相应的检验证书。

**Article 14** After a ship has been surveyed and found satisfactory, the ship survey organization shall issue the corresponding survey certificate in accordance with the provisions.

### 第三章　海上设施检验

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海上设施的所有人或者经营人，必须向船检局设置或者指定的船舶检验机构申请下列检验，但是[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定的除外：  
(一)建造或者改建海上设施时，申请建造检验；  
(二)使用中的海上设施，申请定期检验；  
(三)因发生事故影响海上设施安全性能的，申请临时检验；  
(四)海上交通安全或者环境保护主管机关责成检验的，申请临时检验。

**Article 15** The owner or operator of an offshore installation must apply to a ship survey organization established or designated by the Ship Inspection Bureau for the following surveys, except as provided for in Article 31 of these Regulations:  
(1) construction survey when constructing or rebuilding the offshore installation;  
(2) periodical survey for an offshore installation in service;  
(3) special survey where an accident has occurred affecting the safety performance of the offshore installation;  
(4) special survey where the competent authority for maritime traffic safety or environmental protection has ordered a survey.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海上设施经检验合格后，船舶检验机构应当按照规定签发相应的检验证书。

**Article 16** After an offshore installation has been surveyed and found satisfactory, the ship survey organization shall issue the corresponding survey certificate in accordance with the provisions.

### 第四章　集装箱检验

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　集装箱的所有人或者经营人，必须向船检局设置或者指定的船舶检验机构申请下列检验：  
(一)制造集装箱时，申请制造检验；  
(二)使用中的集装箱，申请定期检验。

**Article 17** The owner or operator of a container must apply to a ship survey organization established or designated by the Ship Inspection Bureau for the following surveys:  
(1) manufacturing survey when manufacturing the container;  
(2) periodical survey for a container in service.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　集装箱经检验合格后，船舶检验机构应当按照规定签发相应的检验证书。

**Article 18** After a container has been surveyed and found satisfactory, the ship survey organization shall issue the corresponding survey certificate in accordance with the provisions.

### 第五章　检验管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　船舶、海上设施、集装箱的检验制度和技术规范，除[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定的外，由船检局制订，经国务院交通主管部门批准后公布施行。

**Article 19** The survey systems and technical specifications for ships, offshore installations and containers shall be formulated by the Ship Inspection Bureau, except as provided for in Article 31 of these Regulations, and shall be published and come into force after approval by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　船舶检验机构的检验人员，必须具备相应的专业知识和检验技能，并经考核合格。

**Article 20** Surveyors of a ship survey organization must have the corresponding professional knowledge and survey skills and have passed the examination.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　检验人员执行检验任务或者对事故进行技术分析调查时，有关单位应当提供必要的条件。

**Article 21** When surveyors carry out survey tasks or conduct technical analysis and investigation of accidents, the relevant units shall provide the necessary conditions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　船舶检验机构实施检验，按照规定收取费用。收费办法由国务院交通主管部门会同国务院物价主管部门、国务院财政主管部门制定。

**Article 22** A ship survey organization that carries out a survey shall collect fees in accordance with the provisions. The fee collection measures shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the competent pricing department of the State Council and the competent financial department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人对船舶检验机构的检验结论有异议的，可以向上一级检验机构申请复验；对复验结论仍有异议的，可以向船检局提出再复验，由船检局组织技术专家组进行检验、评议，作出最终结论。

**Article 23** Where a party disagrees with the survey conclusion of a ship survey organization, it may apply to the higher-level survey organization for re-survey; if it still disagrees with the re-survey conclusion, it may apply to the Ship Inspection Bureau for a further re-survey, and the Ship Inspection Bureau shall organize a technical expert panel to carry out a survey and evaluation and make a final conclusion.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何单位和个人不得涂改、伪造检验证书，不得擅自更改船舶检验机构勘划的船舶载重线。

**Article 24** No unit or individual may alter or forge a survey certificate, nor may they arbitrarily change the load line of a ship marked by a ship survey organization.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　关于外国船舶检验机构在中国境内设置常驻代表机构或者派驻检验人员的管理办法，由国务院交通主管部门制定。

**Article 25** The administrative measures for foreign ship survey organizations to establish permanent representative offices or station surveyors within the territory of China shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　涂改检验证书、擅自更改船舶载重线或者以欺骗行为获取检验证书的，船检局或者其委托的检验机构有权撤销已签发的相应证书，并可以责令改正或者补办有关手续。

**Article 26** Where anyone alters a survey certificate, arbitrarily changes a ship's load line, or obtains a survey certificate by fraudulent means, the Ship Inspection Bureau or the survey organization entrusted by it shall have the right to revoke the corresponding certificate already issued and may order the person to make corrections or go through the relevant procedures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　伪造船舶检验证书或者擅自更改船舶载重线的，由有关行政主管机关给予通报批评，并可以处以相当于相应的检验费一倍至五倍的罚款；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where anyone forges a ship survey certificate or arbitrarily changes a ship's load line, the relevant competent administrative authority shall give a circulating notice of criticism and may impose a fine of one to five times the corresponding survey fee; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　船舶检验机构的检验人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、严重失职的，由所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分或者撤销其检验资格；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where a surveyor of a ship survey organization abuses his power, engages in malpractice for personal gain, neglects his duty, or is seriously derelict in his duty, the unit to which he belongs or the higher authority shall give him an administrative sanction or revoke his qualification as a surveyor; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例下列用语的定义：  
(一)船舶，是指各类排水或者非排水船、艇、水上飞机、潜水器和移动式平台。  
(二)海上设施，是指水上水下各种固定或者浮动建筑、装置和固定平台。  
(三)沿海水域，是指中华人民共和国沿海的港口、内水和领海以及国家管辖的一切其他海域。

**Article 29** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "Ship" means any type of displacement or non-displacement vessel, craft, seaplane, submersible and mobile platform.  
(2) "Offshore installation" means any fixed or floating construction, device and fixed platform above or under water.  
(3) "Coastal waters" means the ports, internal waters and territorial sea along the coast of the People's Republic of China, as well as all other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the State.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　除从事国际航行的渔业辅助船舶依照本条例进行检验外，其他渔业船舶的检验，由国务院交通运输主管部门按照相关渔业船舶检验的行政法规执行。

**Article 30** Except for fishery auxiliary ships engaged in international voyages which shall be surveyed in accordance with these Regulations, the survey of other fishing vessels shall be carried out by the competent transport department of the State Council in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations on the survey of fishing vessels.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　海上设施中的海上石油天然气生产设施的检验，由国务院石油主管部门会同国务院交通主管部门另行规定。

**Article 31** The survey of offshore oil and gas production facilities among offshore installations shall be separately prescribed by the competent petroleum department of the State Council jointly with the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　下列船舶不适用本条例：  
(一)军用舰艇、公安船艇和体育运动船艇；  
(二)按照船舶登记规定，不需要登记的船舶。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall not apply to the following ships:  
(1) military vessels, public security vessels and sports vessels;  
(2) ships that are not required to be registered under the provisions on ship registration.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 33** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
