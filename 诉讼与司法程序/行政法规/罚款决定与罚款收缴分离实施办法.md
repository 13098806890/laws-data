# 罚款决定与罚款收缴分离实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1997-11-17  
**生效日期**：1998-01-01

> (1997年11月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第235号发布　自1998年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为了实施罚款决定与罚款收缴分离，加强对罚款收缴活动的监督，保证罚款及时上缴国库，根据《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》(以下简称行政处罚法)的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　罚款的收取、缴纳及相关活动，适用本办法。 第三条　作出罚款决定的行政机关应当与收缴罚款的机构分离；但是，依照行政处罚法的规定可以当场收缴罚款的除外。 第四条　罚款必须全部上缴国库，任何行政机关、组织或者个人不得以任何形式截留、私分或者变相私分。 行政机关执法所需经费的拨付，按照国家有关规定执行。 第五条　经中国人民银行批准有代理收付款项业务的商业银行、信用合作社(以下简称代收机构)，可以开办代收罚款的业务。 具体代收机构由县级以上地方人民政府组织本级财政部门、中国人民银行当地分支机构和依法具有行政处罚权的行政机关共同研究，统一确定。海关、外汇管理等实行垂直领导的依法具有行政处罚权的行政机关作出罚款决定的，具体代收机构由财政部、中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门确定。依法具有行政处罚权的国务院有关部门作出罚款决定的，具体代收机构由财政部、中国人民银行确定。 代收机构应当具备足够的代收网点，以方便当事人缴纳罚款。 第六条　行政机关应当依照本办法和国家有关规定，同代收机构签订代收罚款协议。 代收罚款协议应当包括下列事项： (一)行政机关、代收机构名称； (二)具体代收网点； (三)代收机构上缴罚款的预算科目、预算级次； (四)代收机构告知行政机关代收罚款情况的方式、期限；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了实施罚款决定与罚款收缴分离，加强对罚款收缴活动的监督，保证罚款及时上缴国库，根据《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》(以下简称行政处罚法)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Administrative Penalty Law) for the purpose of implementing the separation of the decision on fines and the collection of fines, strengthening the supervision over the collection of fines, and ensuring that fines are turned over to the State Treasury in a timely manner.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　罚款的收取、缴纳及相关活动，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the collection, payment, and related activities of fines.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　作出罚款决定的行政机关应当与收缴罚款的机构分离；但是，依照行政处罚法的规定可以当场收缴罚款的除外。

**Article 3** The administrative organ that makes the decision on a fine shall be separated from the agency that collects the fine; however, this shall not apply to the cases where the fine may be collected on the spot in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　罚款必须全部上缴国库，任何行政机关、组织或者个人不得以任何形式截留、私分或者变相私分。  
行政机关执法所需经费的拨付，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 4** All fines must be turned over to the State Treasury, and no administrative organ, organization or individual may, in any form, retain, privately divide, or covertly divide the fines.  
The allocation of funds needed for law enforcement by administrative organs shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经中国人民银行批准有代理收付款项业务的商业银行、信用合作社(以下简称代收机构)，可以开办代收罚款的业务。  
具体代收机构由县级以上地方人民政府组织本级财政部门、中国人民银行当地分支机构和依法具有行政处罚权的行政机关共同研究，统一确定。海关、外汇管理等实行垂直领导的依法具有行政处罚权的行政机关作出罚款决定的，具体代收机构由财政部、中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门确定。依法具有行政处罚权的国务院有关部门作出罚款决定的，具体代收机构由财政部、中国人民银行确定。  
代收机构应当具备足够的代收网点，以方便当事人缴纳罚款。

**Article 5** Commercial banks and credit cooperatives that have been approved by the People's Bank of China to handle agency collection and payment services (hereinafter referred to as collection agencies) may commence the business of collecting fines on behalf.  
The specific collection agencies shall be uniformly determined by the local people's government at or above the county level through joint consideration with the financial department at the same level, the local branch of the People's Bank of China, and the administrative organs that have the power to impose administrative penalties according to law. Where an administrative organ that has the power to impose administrative penalties according to law and operates under vertical leadership, such as the customs and foreign exchange administration, makes a decision on a fine, the specific collection agency shall be determined by the Ministry of Finance and the People's Bank of China jointly with the relevant department of the State Council. Where a relevant department of the State Council that has the power to impose administrative penalties according to law makes a decision on a fine, the specific collection agency shall be determined by the Ministry of Finance and the People's Bank of China.  
A collection agency shall have sufficient collection outlets to facilitate the payment of fines by the parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行政机关应当依照本办法和国家有关规定，同代收机构签订代收罚款协议。  
代收罚款协议应当包括下列事项：  
(一)行政机关、代收机构名称；  
(二)具体代收网点；  
(三)代收机构上缴罚款的预算科目、预算级次；  
(四)代收机构告知行政机关代收罚款情况的方式、期限；  
(五)需要明确的其他事项。  
自代收罚款协议签订之日起15日内，行政机关应当将代收罚款协议报上一级行政机关和同级财政部门备案；代收机构应当将代收罚款协议报中国人民银行或者其当地分支机构备案。

**Article 6** An administrative organ shall, in accordance with these Measures and the relevant provisions of the State, conclude an agreement on the agency collection of fines with a collection agency.  
The agreement on the agency collection of fines shall include the following items:  
(1) the names of the administrative organ and the collection agency;  
(2) specific collection outlets;  
(3) the budget classification and budget level for turning over the fines by the collection agency;  
(4) the method and time limit for the collection agency to inform the administrative organ of the collection status of fines;  
(5) other matters that need to be clarified.  
Within 15 days from the date of signing the agreement on the agency collection of fines, the administrative organ shall report the agreement to the administrative organ at the next higher level and the financial department at the same level for record; the collection agency shall report the agreement to the People's Bank of China or its local branch for record.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　行政机关作出罚款决定的行政处罚决定书应当载明代收机构的名称、地址和当事人应当缴纳罚款的数额、期限等，并明确对当事人逾期缴纳罚款是否加处罚款。  
当事人应当按照行政处罚决定书确定的罚款数额、期限，到指定的代收机构缴纳罚款。

**Article 7** The written decision on administrative penalty made by an administrative organ imposing a fine shall state the name and address of the collection agency, the amount and time limit for the payment of the fine by the party, and shall specify whether an additional fine shall be imposed on the party for overdue payment of the fine.  
The party shall, in accordance with the amount and time limit for the fine as determined in the written decision on administrative penalty, go to the designated collection agency to pay the fine.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　代收机构代收罚款，应当向当事人出具罚款收据。  
罚款收据的格式和印制，由财政部规定。

**Article 8** When collecting a fine on behalf, the collection agency shall issue a receipt for the fine to the party.  
The format and printing of the receipt for the fine shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人逾期缴纳罚款，行政处罚决定书明确需要加处罚款的，代收机构应当按照行政处罚决定书加收罚款。  
当事人对加收罚款有异议的，应当先缴纳罚款和加收的罚款，再依法向作出行政处罚决定的行政机关申请复议。

**Article 9** Where a party pays the fine beyond the time limit and the written decision on administrative penalty specifies that an additional fine shall be imposed, the collection agency shall collect an additional fine in accordance with the written decision on administrative penalty.  
Where a party objects to the additional fine, it shall first pay the fine and the additional fine, and then apply for administrative reconsideration to the administrative organ that made the decision on administrative penalty according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　代收机构应当按照代收罚款协议规定的方式、期限，将当事人的姓名或者名称、缴纳罚款的数额、时间等情况书面告知作出行政处罚决定的行政机关。

**Article 10** The collection agency shall, in accordance with the method and time limit provided in the agreement on the agency collection of fines, inform the administrative organ that made the decision on administrative penalty in writing of the party's name, the amount of the fine paid, the time of payment, and other relevant information.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　代收机构应当按照行政处罚法和国家有关规定，将代收的罚款直接上缴国库。

**Article 11** The collection agency shall, in accordance with the Administrative Penalty Law and the relevant provisions of the State, directly turn over the collected fines to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国库应当按照《中华人民共和国国家金库条例》的规定，定期同财政部门和行政机关对账，以保证收受的罚款和上缴国库的罚款数额一致。

**Article 12** The State Treasury shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the State Treasury, regularly reconcile accounts with the financial departments and administrative organs to ensure that the amount of fines received and the amount of fines turned over to the State Treasury are consistent.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　代收机构应当在代收网点、营业时间、服务设施、缴款手续等方面为当事人缴纳罚款提供方便。

**Article 13** The collection agency shall facilitate the payment of fines by the parties in terms of collection outlets, business hours, service facilities, payment procedures, and other aspects.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　财政部门应当向代收机构支付手续费，具体标准由财政部制定。

**Article 14** The financial department shall pay service fees to the collection agency; the specific standards shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织和依法受委托的组织依法作出的罚款决定与罚款收缴，适用本办法。

**Article 15** These Measures shall apply to the decisions on fines and the collection of fines made according to law by organizations authorized by laws and regulations to manage public affairs and by organizations entrusted according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法由财政部会同中国人民银行组织实施。

**Article 16** These Measures shall be organized and implemented by the Ministry of Finance jointly with the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自1998年1月1日起施行。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force as of January 1, 1998.
