# 中华人民共和国出境入境边防检查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1995-07-20  
**生效日期**：1995-09-01

> (1995年7月6日国务院第34次常务会议通过　1995年7月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第182号发布　自1995年9月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为维护中华人民共和国的主权、安全和社会秩序，便利出境、入境的人员和交通运输工具的通行，制定本条例。 第二条　出境、入境边防检查工作由公安部主管。 第三条　中华人民共和国在对外开放的港口、航空港、车站和边境通道等口岸设立出境入境边防检查站(以下简称边防检查站)。 第四条　边防检查站为维护国家主权、安全和社会秩序，履行下列职责： (一)对出境、入境的人员及其行李物品、交通运输工具及其载运的货物实施边防检查； (二)按照国家有关规定对出境、入境的交通运输工具进行监护； (三)对口岸的限定区域进行警戒，维护出境、入境秩序； (四)执行主管机关赋予的和其他法律、行政法规规定的任务。 第五条　出境、入境的人员和交通运输工具，必须经对外开放的口岸或者主管机关特许的地点通行，接受边防检查、监护和管理。 出境、入境的人员，必须遵守中华人民共和国的法律、行政法规。 第六条　边防检查人员必须依法执行公务。 任何组织和个人不得妨碍边防检查人员依法执行公务。 第二章　人员的检查和管理

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为维护中华人民共和国的主权、安全和社会秩序，便利出境、入境的人员和交通运输工具的通行，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of safeguarding the sovereignty, security and social order of the People's Republic of China and facilitating the passage of persons and means of transport exiting or entering the country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　出境、入境边防检查工作由公安部主管。

**Article 2** The work of exit and entry border inspection shall be under the charge of the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国在对外开放的港口、航空港、车站和边境通道等口岸设立出境入境边防检查站(以下简称边防检查站)。

**Article 3** The People's Republic of China shall set up exit and entry border inspection stations (hereinafter referred to as border inspection stations) at such ports as ports, airports, railway stations and border passages open to foreign countries.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　边防检查站为维护国家主权、安全和社会秩序，履行下列职责：  
(一)对出境、入境的人员及其行李物品、交通运输工具及其载运的货物实施边防检查；  
(二)按照国家有关规定对出境、入境的交通运输工具进行监护；  
(三)对口岸的限定区域进行警戒，维护出境、入境秩序；  
(四)执行主管机关赋予的和其他法律、行政法规规定的任务。

**Article 4** For the purpose of safeguarding the sovereignty, security and social order of the State, a border inspection station shall perform the following duties:  
(1) to carry out border inspection on persons exiting or entering the country and their luggage and articles, and on means of transport exiting or entering the country and the goods they carry;  
(2) to guard means of transport exiting or entering the country in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) to keep watch over the restricted areas of ports and maintain the order of exit and entry;  
(4) to carry out other tasks assigned by the competent authorities and prescribed by other laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　出境、入境的人员和交通运输工具，必须经对外开放的口岸或者主管机关特许的地点通行，接受边防检查、监护和管理。  
出境、入境的人员，必须遵守中华人民共和国的法律、行政法规。

**Article 5** Persons and means of transport exiting or entering the country must pass through ports open to foreign countries or other places specially permitted by the competent authorities, and shall undergo border inspection, guard and control.  
Persons exiting or entering the country must abide by the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　边防检查人员必须依法执行公务。  
任何组织和个人不得妨碍边防检查人员依法执行公务。

**Article 6** Border inspection officers must perform their duties according to law.  
No organization or individual may obstruct border inspection officers from performing their duties according to law.

### 第二章　人员的检查和管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　出境、入境的人员必须按照规定填写出境、入境登记卡，向边防检查站交验本人的有效护照或者其他出境、入境证件(以下简称出境、入境证件)，经查验核准后，方可出境、入境。

**Article 7** Persons exiting or entering the country must, in accordance with the provisions, fill in exit or entry registration cards and submit to the border inspection station their valid passports or other exit or entry certificates (hereinafter referred to as exit or entry certificates) for examination and verification; only after verification may they exit or enter the country.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出境、入境的人员有下列情形之一的，边防检查站有权阻止其出境、入境：  
(一)未持出境、入境证件的；  
(二)持用无效出境、入境证件的；  
(三)持用他人出境、入境证件的；  
(四)持用伪造或者涂改的出境、入境证件的；  
(五)拒绝接受边防检查的；  
(六)未在限定口岸通行的；  
(七)国务院公安部门、国家安全部门通知不准出境、入境的；  
(八)法律、行政法规规定不准出境、入境的。  
出境、入境的人员有前款第(三)项、第(四)项或者中国公民有前款第(七)项、第(八)项所列情形之一的，边防检查站可以扣留或者收缴其出境、入境证件。

**Article 8** The border inspection station shall have the power to prevent a person from exiting or entering the country under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) not holding an exit or entry certificate;  
(2) holding an invalid exit or entry certificate;  
(3) holding another person's exit or entry certificate;  
(4) holding a forged or altered exit or entry certificate;  
(5) refusing to undergo border inspection;  
(6) not passing through the designated port;  
(7) having been notified by the public security department or the state security department of the State Council as not permitted to exit or enter the country;  
(8) being not permitted to exit or enter the country by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a person exiting or entering the country falls under subparagraph (3) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, or a Chinese citizen falls under subparagraph (7) or (8) of the preceding paragraph, the border inspection station may detain or confiscate the exit or entry certificate thereof.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对交通运输工具的随行服务员工出境、入境的边防检查、管理，适用本条例的规定。但是，中华人民共和国与有关国家或者地区订有协议的，按照协议办理。

**Article 9** The provisions of these Regulations shall apply to the border inspection and control of accompanying service crew members of means of transport exiting or entering the country. However, where an agreement has been concluded between the People's Republic of China and the country or region concerned, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　抵达中华人民共和国口岸的船舶的外国籍船员及其随行家属和香港、澳门、台湾船员及其随行家属，要求在港口城市登陆、住宿的，应当由船长或者其代理人向边防检查站申请办理登陆、住宿手续。  
经批准登陆、住宿的船员及其随行家属，必须按照规定的时间返回船舶。登陆后有违法行为，尚未构成犯罪的，责令立即返回船舶，并不得再次登陆。  
从事国际航行船舶上的中国船员，凭本人的出境、入境证件登陆、住宿。

**Article 10** Where foreign crew members of a vessel arriving at a port of the People's Republic of China and their accompanying family members, and crew members from Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan and their accompanying family members, need to land or lodge in a port city, the captain or his agent shall apply to the border inspection station for the formalities of landing or lodging.  
Crew members and their accompanying family members who have been permitted to land or lodge must return to the vessel within the prescribed time limit. If, after landing, they commit any illegal act which does not constitute a crime, they shall be ordered to return to the vessel immediately and shall not be allowed to land again.  
Chinese crew members on vessels engaged in international navigation may land or lodge with their own exit or entry certificates.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请登陆的人员有[本条例第八条](#art-8)所列情形之一的，边防检查站有权拒绝其登陆。

**Article 11** Where a person applying for landing falls under any of the circumstances listed in Article 8 of these Regulations, the border inspection station shall have the power to refuse the landing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　上下外国船舶的人员，必须向边防检查人员交验出境、入境证件或者其他规定的证件，经许可后，方可上船、下船。口岸检查、检验单位的人员需要登船执行公务的，应当着制服并出示证件。

**Article 12** Persons boarding or leaving a foreign vessel must submit their exit or entry certificates or other prescribed certificates to border inspection officers, and may board or leave the vessel only after obtaining permission. Personnel of port inspection and quarantine units who need to board a vessel to perform official duties shall wear uniforms and produce their certificates.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国与毗邻国家(地区)接壤地区的双方公务人员、边境居民临时出境、入境的边防检查，双方订有协议的，按照协议执行；没有协议的，适用本条例的规定。  
毗邻国家的边境居民按照协议临时入境的，限于在协议规定范围内活动；需要到协议规定范围以外活动的，应当事先办理入境手续。

**Article 13** Where border inspection for the temporary exit or entry of public servants and border residents of the contiguous areas between the People's Republic of China and neighboring countries (regions) is concerned, if an agreement has been concluded between the two sides, it shall be executed in accordance with the agreement; if no agreement has been concluded, the provisions of these Regulations shall apply.  
Border residents of neighboring countries who temporarily enter the country under an agreement shall confine their activities within the scope prescribed by the agreement; if they need to engage in activities beyond the said scope, they shall go through entry formalities in advance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　边防检查站认为必要时，可以对出境、入境的人员进行人身检查。人身检查应当由两名与受检查人同性别的边防检查人员进行。

**Article 14** When the border inspection station deems it necessary, it may conduct a body search on persons exiting or entering the country. A body search shall be conducted by two border inspection officers of the same sex as the person being searched.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　出境、入境的人员有下列情形之一的，边防检查站有权限制其活动范围，进行调查或者移送有关机关处理：  
(一)有持用他人出境、入境证件嫌疑的；  
(二)有持用伪造或者涂改的出境、入境证件嫌疑的；  
(三)国务院公安部门、国家安全部门和省、自治区、直辖市公安机关、国家安全机关通知有犯罪嫌疑的；  
(四)有危害国家安全、利益和社会秩序嫌疑的。

**Article 15** The border inspection station shall have the power to restrict the scope of activities of a person exiting or entering the country, to conduct an investigation or to transfer the case to the relevant organ for handling, under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) being suspected of holding another person's exit or entry certificate;  
(2) being suspected of holding a forged or altered exit or entry certificate;  
(3) having been notified by the public security department or the state security department of the State Council or by the public security organ or state security organ of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government as being suspected of committing a crime;  
(4) being suspected of endangering the security, interests or social order of the State.

### 第三章　交通运输工具的检查和监护

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出境、入境的交通运输工具离、抵口岸时，必须接受边防检查。对交通运输工具的入境检查，在最先抵达的口岸进行；出境检查，在最后离开的口岸进行。在特殊情况下，经主管机关批准，对交通运输工具的入境、出境检查，也可以在特许的地点进行。

**Article 16** Means of transport exiting or entering the country must undergo border inspection when they leave or arrive at a port. Entry inspection of means of transport shall be conducted at the port of first arrival; exit inspection shall be conducted at the port of final departure. Under special circumstances, with the approval of the competent authority, entry or exit inspection of means of transport may also be conducted at other specially permitted places.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　交通运输工具的负责人或者有关交通运输部门，应当事先将出境、入境的船舶、航空器、火车离、抵口岸的时间、停留地点和载运人员、货物情况，向有关的边防检查站报告。  
交通运输工具抵达口岸时，船长、机长或者其代理人必须向边防检查站申报员工和旅客的名单；列车长及其他交通运输工具的负责人必须申报员工和旅客的人数。

**Article 17** The person in charge of the means of transport or the relevant transport department shall, in advance, report to the relevant border inspection station the time of departure or arrival of the vessel, aircraft or train, the place of stay and the information on the persons and goods carried.  
When a means of transport arrives at a port, the captain, the pilot or his agent must submit to the border inspection station the list of crew and passengers; the train conductor and the person in charge of other means of transport must submit the number of crew and passengers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对交通运输工具实施边防检查时，其负责人或者代理人应当到场协助边防检查人员进行检查。

**Article 18** When border inspection is conducted on a means of transport, the person in charge thereof or his agent shall be present to assist the border inspection officers in the inspection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　出境、入境的交通运输工具在中国境内必须按照规定的路线、航线行驶。外国船舶未经许可不得在非对外开放的港口停靠。  
出境的交通运输工具自出境检查后到出境前，入境的交通运输工具自入境后到入境检查前，未经边防检查站许可，不得上下人员、装卸物品。

**Article 19** Means of transport exiting or entering the country must, within the territory of China, travel along the prescribed routes or shipping lines. Foreign vessels shall not, without permission, berth at ports not open to foreign countries.  
From the completion of exit inspection to the departure of a means of transport exiting the country, and from the arrival of a means of transport entering the country to the commencement of entry inspection, persons shall not board or leave the means of transport and articles shall not be loaded or unloaded without the permission of the border inspection station.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中国船舶需要搭靠外国船舶的，应当由船长或者其代理人向边防检查站申请办理搭靠手续；未办理手续的，不得擅自搭靠。

**Article 20** Where a Chinese vessel needs to lie alongside a foreign vessel, the captain or his agent shall apply to the border inspection station for the formalities of lying alongside; if the formalities have not been completed, it shall not lie alongside without authorization.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　边防检查站对处于下列情形之一的出境、入境交通运输工具，有权进行监护：  
(一)离、抵口岸的火车、外国船舶和中国客船在出境检查后到出境前、入境后到入境检查前和检查期间；  
(二)火车及其他机动车辆在国(边)界线距边防检查站较远的区域内行驶期间；  
(三)外国船舶在中国内河航行期间；  
(四)边防检查站认为有必要进行监护的其他情形。

**Article 21** The border inspection station shall have the power to guard means of transport exiting or entering the country under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) trains, foreign vessels and Chinese passenger vessels leaving or arriving at ports, during the period from exit inspection to departure, from arrival to entry inspection, and during the inspection;  
(2) trains and other motor vehicles during the period of traveling within an area far from the border inspection station near the national (border) line;  
(3) foreign vessels during the period of navigating in the inland waters of China;  
(4) other circumstances where the border inspection station deems it necessary to carry out guard.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对随交通运输工具执行监护职务的边防检查人员，交通运输工具的负责人应当提供必要的办公、生活条件。  
被监护的交通运输工具和上下该交通运输工具的人员应当服从监护人员的检查。

**Article 22** The person in charge of the means of transport shall provide necessary working and living conditions for the border inspection officers performing guard duties on board the means of transport.  
The means of transport being guarded and the persons boarding or leaving such means of transport shall submit to the inspection by the guarding officers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　未实行监护措施的交通运输工具，其负责人应当自行管理，保证该交通运输工具和员工遵守本条例的规定。

**Article 23** Where no guard measures are implemented on a means of transport, the person in charge thereof shall manage it by himself and ensure that the means of transport and the crew comply with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　发现出境、入境的交通运输工具载运不准出境、入境人员，偷越国(边)境人员及未持有效出境、入境证件的人员的，交通运输工具负责人应当负责将其遣返，并承担由此发生的一切费用。

**Article 24** Where a means of transport exiting or entering the country is found to carry persons who are not permitted to exit or enter the country, persons who secretly cross the national (border) line, or persons who do not hold valid exit or entry certificates, the person in charge of the means of transport shall be responsible for repatriating them and shall bear all expenses incurred therefrom.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出境、入境的交通运输工具有下列情形之一的，边防检查站有权推迟或者阻止其出境、入境：  
(一)离、抵口岸时，未经边防检查站同意，擅自出境、入境的；  
(二)拒绝接受边防检查、监护的；  
(三)被认为载有危害国家安全、利益和社会秩序的人员或者物品的；  
(四)被认为载有非法出境、入境人员的；  
(五)拒不执行边防检查站依法作出的处罚或者处理决定的；  
(六)未经批准擅自改变出境、入境口岸的。  
边防检查站在前款所列情形消失后，对有关交通运输工具应当立即放行。

**Article 25** The border inspection station shall have the power to postpone or prevent the exit or entry of a means of transport under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) leaving or arriving at a port, it exits or enters the country without the consent of the border inspection station;  
(2) refusing to undergo border inspection or guard;  
(3) being considered to carry persons or articles endangering the security, interests or social order of the State;  
(4) being considered to carry persons illegally exiting or entering the country;  
(5) refusing to execute the punishment or handling decision lawfully made by the border inspection station;  
(6) changing the port of exit or entry without authorization.  
After the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph disappear, the border inspection station shall immediately release the relevant means of transport.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　出境、入境的船舶、航空器，由于不可预见的紧急情况或者不可抗拒的原因，驶入对外开放口岸以外地区的，必须立即向附近的边防检查站或者当地公安机关报告并接受检查和监护；在驶入原因消失后，必须立即按照通知的时间和路线离去。

**Article 26** Where a vessel or aircraft exiting or entering the country, due to an unforeseeable emergency or force majeure, enters an area other than a port open to foreign countries, it must immediately report to the nearby border inspection station or the local public security organ and undergo inspection and guard; after the cause for entering the said area disappears, it must immediately depart in accordance with the notified time and route.

### 第四章　行李物品、货物的检查

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　边防检查站根据维护国家安全和社会秩序的需要，可以对出境、入境人员携带的行李物品和交通运输工具载运的货物进行重点检查。

**Article 27** The border inspection station may, as required by the need to maintain national security and social order, conduct focused inspection on the luggage and articles carried by persons exiting or entering the country and on the goods carried by means of transport.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　出境、入境的人员和交通运输工具不得携带、载运法律、行政法规规定的危害国家安全和社会秩序的违禁物品；携带、载运违禁物品的，边防检查站应当扣留违禁物品，对携带人、载运违禁物品的交通运输工具负责人依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 28** Persons and means of transport exiting or entering the country shall not carry or transport prohibited articles that endanger national security and social order as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations; if they carry or transport such prohibited articles, the border inspection station shall confiscate the prohibited articles and deal with the person carrying them or the person in charge of the means of transport carrying them in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　任何人不得非法携带属于国家秘密的文件、资料和其他物品出境；非法携带属于国家秘密的文件、资料和其他物品的，边防检查站应当予以收缴，对携带人依照有关法律、行政法规规定处理。

**Article 29** No person may illegally carry documents, materials or other articles classified as State secrets out of the country; if any person illegally carries documents, materials or other articles classified as State secrets, the border inspection station shall confiscate them and deal with the person carrying them in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　出境、入境的人员携带或者托运枪支、弹药，必须遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定，向边防检查站办理携带或者托运手续；未经许可，不得携带、托运枪支、弹药出境、入境。

**Article 30** Persons exiting or entering the country who carry or consign firearms or ammunition must abide by the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations and shall go through the formalities for carrying or consigning with the border inspection station; without permission, they shall not carry or consign firearms or ammunition out of or into the country.

### 第五章　处罚

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对违反本条例规定的处罚，由边防检查站执行。

**Article 31** The penalties for violating the provisions of these Regulations shall be executed by the border inspection station.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　出境、入境的人员有下列情形之一的，处以500元以上2000元以下的罚款或者依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处以拘留：  
(一)未持出境、入境证件的；  
(二)持用无效出境、入境证件的；  
(三)持用他人出境、入境证件的；  
(四)持用伪造或者涂改的出境、入境证件的。

**Article 32** A person exiting or entering the country who falls under any of the following circumstances shall be fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan, or shall be detained in accordance with the relevant laws or administrative regulations:  
(1) not holding an exit or entry certificate;  
(2) holding an invalid exit or entry certificate;  
(3) holding another person's exit or entry certificate;  
(4) holding a forged or altered exit or entry certificate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　协助他人非法出境、入境，情节轻微尚不构成犯罪的，处以2000元以上10000元以下的罚款；有非法所得的，没收非法所得。

**Article 33** Whoever assists another person in illegally exiting or entering the country, if the circumstances are minor and do not constitute a crime, shall be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　未经批准携带或者托运枪支、弹药出境、入境的，没收其枪支、弹药，并处以1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Whoever carries or consigns firearms or ammunition out of or into the country without approval shall have the firearms or ammunition confiscated and shall be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有下列情形之一的，处以警告或者500元以下罚款：  
(一)未经批准进入口岸的限定区域或者进入后不服从管理，扰乱口岸管理秩序的；  
(二)污辱边防检查人员的；  
(三)未经批准或者未按照规定登陆、住宿的。

**Article 35** Under any of the following circumstances, a warning shall be given or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) entering the restricted area of a port without approval or, after entering, refusing to obey management and disturbing the order of port control;  
(2) insulting a border inspection officer;  
(3) landing or lodging without approval or not in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　出境、入境的交通运输工具载运不准出境、入境人员，偷越国(边)境人员及未持有效出境、入境证件的人员出境、入境的，对其负责人按每载运一人处以5000元以上10000元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where a means of transport exiting or entering the country carries persons who are not permitted to exit or enter the country, persons who secretly cross the national (border) line, or persons who do not hold valid exit or entry certificates out of or into the country, the person in charge thereof shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan for each person so carried.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　交通运输工具有下列情形之一的，对其负责人处以10000元以上30000元以下的罚款：  
(一)离、抵口岸时，未经边防检查站同意，擅自出境、入境的；  
(二)未按照规定向边防检查站申报员工、旅客和货物情况的，或者拒绝协助检查的；  
(三)交通运输工具在入境后到入境检查前、出境检查后到出境前，未经边防检查站许可，上下人员、装卸物品的。

**Article 37** Where a means of transport falls under any of the following circumstances, the person in charge thereof shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) leaving or arriving at a port, it exits or enters the country without the consent of the border inspection station;  
(2) failing to report the information on crew, passengers and goods to the border inspection station in accordance with the provisions, or refusing to assist in inspection;  
(3) after the means of transport enters the country but before entry inspection, or after exit inspection but before departure, persons board or leave the means of transport or articles are loaded or unloaded without the permission of the border inspection station.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　交通运输工具有下列情形之一的，对其负责人给予警告并处500元以上5000元以下的罚款：  
(一)出境、入境的交通运输工具在中国境内不按照规定的路线行驶的；  
(二)外国船舶未经许可停靠在非对外开放港口的；  
(三)中国船舶未经批准擅自搭靠外国籍船舶的。

**Article 38** Where a means of transport falls under any of the following circumstances, the person in charge thereof shall be given a warning and concurrently fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan:  
(1) a means of transport exiting or entering the country does not travel along the prescribed route within the territory of China;  
(2) a foreign vessel berths at a port not open to foreign countries without permission;  
(3) a Chinese vessel lies alongside a foreign vessel without approval.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　出境、入境的船舶、航空器，由于不可预见的紧急情况或者不可抗拒的原因，驶入对外开放口岸对外地区，没有正当理由不向附近边防检查站或者当地公安机关报告的；或者在驶入原因消失后，没有按照通知的时间和路线离去的，对其负责人处以10000元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a vessel or aircraft exiting or entering the country, due to an unforeseeable emergency or force majeure, enters an area other than a port open to foreign countries and, without justifiable reason, fails to report to the nearby border inspection station or the local public security organ, or after the cause for entering the said area disappears, fails to depart in accordance with the notified time and route, the person in charge thereof shall be fined not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　边防检查站执行罚没款处罚，应当向被处罚人出具收据。罚没款应当按照规定上缴国库。

**Article 40** When a border inspection station imposes a penalty of fine or confiscation, it shall issue a receipt to the person being penalized. The fines and confiscated money shall be turned over to the State treasury in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　出境、入境的人员违反本条例的规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where a person exiting or entering the country violates the provisions of these Regulations and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　被处罚人对边防检查站作出的处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定书之日起15日内，向边防检查站所在地的县级公安机关申请复议；有关县级公安机关应当自接到复议申请书之日起15日内作出复议决定；被处罚人对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定书之日起15日内，向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 42** If a person subject to a penalty is dissatisfied with the decision on penalty made by the border inspection station, he may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision on penalty, apply for reconsideration to the public security organ at the county level in the place where the border inspection station is located; the relevant public security organ at the county level shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration, make a reconsideration decision; if the person subject to a penalty is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, he may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, bring a lawsuit in the People's Court.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对享有外交特权与豁免权的外国人入境、出境的边防检查，法律有特殊规定的，从其规定。

**Article 43** Where laws have special provisions on the border inspection of entry or exit of foreigners enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunities, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　外国对中华人民共和国公民和交通运输工具入境、过境、出境的检查和管理有特别规定的，边防检查站可以根据主管机关的决定采取相应的措施。

**Article 44** Where a foreign country has special provisions on the inspection and control of the entry, transit or exit of citizens and means of transport of the People's Republic of China, the border inspection station may, in accordance with the decision of the competent authority, take corresponding measures.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对往返香港、澳门、台湾的中华人民共和国公民和交通运输工具的边防检查，适用本条例的规定；法律、行政法规有专门规定的，从其规定。

**Article 45** The provisions of these Regulations shall apply to the border inspection of citizens of the People's Republic of China and means of transport traveling to and from Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan; where laws or administrative regulations have special provisions, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
“出境、入境的人员”，是指一切离开、进入或者通过中华人民共和国国(边)境的中国籍、外国籍和无国籍人；  
“出境、入境的交通运输工具”，是指一切离开、进入或者通过中华人民共和国国(边)境的船舶、航空器、火车和机动车辆、非机动车辆以及驮畜；  
“员工”，是指出境、入境的船舶、航空器、火车和机动车辆的负责人、驾驶员、服务员和其他工作人员。

**Article 46** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms mean:  
"Persons exiting or entering the country" means all persons of Chinese nationality, foreign nationality or stateless persons who leave, enter or pass through the national (border) territory of the People's Republic of China;  
"Means of transport exiting or entering the country" means all vessels, aircraft, trains, motor vehicles, non-motor vehicles and pack animals which leave, enter or pass through the national (border) territory of the People's Republic of China;  
"Crew" means the persons in charge, pilots, attendants and other staff members of vessels, aircraft, trains and motor vehicles exiting or entering the country.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自1995年9月1日起施行。1952年7月29日中央人民政府政务院批准实施的《出入国境治安检查暂行条例》和1965年4月30日国务院发布的《边防检查条例》同时废止。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 1995. The Provisional Regulations on the Security Inspection of Exit and Entry (approved by the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government and implemented on July 29, 1952) and the Regulations on Border Inspection (promulgated by the State Council on April 30, 1965) shall be abolished simultaneously.
