# 政府督查工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2020-12-26  
**生效日期**：2021-02-01

> 政府督查机构根据党中央、国务院重大决策部署，上级和本级人民政府重要工作部署，以及掌握的线索，可以提出督查工作建议，经本级人民政府行政首长批准后，确定督查事项。 第十二条　政府督查可以采取以下方式： （一）要求督查对象自查、说明情况； （二）听取督查对象汇报； （三）开展检查、访谈、暗访； （四）组织座谈、听证、统计、评估； （五）调阅、复制与督查事项有关的资料； （六）通过信函、电话、媒体等渠道收集线索； （七）约谈督查对象负责人或者相关责任人； （八）运用现代信息技术手段开展“互联网+督查”。 第十三条　政府督查工作需要协助的，有关行政机关应当在职权范围内予以协助。 第十四条　县级以上人民政府可以组织开展综合督查、专项督查、事件调查、日常督办、线索核查等政府督查工作。 第十五条　开展政府督查工作应当制定督查方案，明确督查内容、对象和范围；应当严格控制督查频次和时限，科学运用督查方式，严肃督查纪律，提前培训督查人员。 政府督查工作应当严格执行督查方案，不得随意扩大督查范围、变更督查对象和内容，不得干预督查对象的正常工作，严禁重复督查、多头督查、越权督查。 第十六条　县级以上人民政府在政府督查工作结束后应当作出督查结论。与督查对象有关的督查结论应当向督查对象反馈。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　政府督查可以采取以下方式：  
（一）要求督查对象自查、说明情况；  
（二）听取督查对象汇报；  
（三）开展检查、访谈、暗访；  
（四）组织座谈、听证、统计、评估；  
（五）调阅、复制与督查事项有关的资料；  
（六）通过信函、电话、媒体等渠道收集线索；  
（七）约谈督查对象负责人或者相关责任人；  
（八）运用现代信息技术手段开展“互联网+督查”。

**Article 12** : Government supervision may adopt the following methods:  
(1) Requiring the supervisee to conduct self-inspection and explain the situation;  
(2) Listening to reports from the supervisee;  
(3) Conducting inspections, interviews, and unannounced visits;  
(4) Organizing seminars, hearings, statistics, and evaluations;  
(5) Accessing and copying materials related to the supervision matters;  
(6) Collecting clues through letters, telephone, media, and other channels;  
(7) Interviewing the person in charge of the supervisee or relevant responsible persons;  
(8) Using modern information technology means to carry out 'Internet+ supervision'.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　政府督查工作需要协助的，有关行政机关应当在职权范围内予以协助。

**Article 13** : Where a government supervision work requires assistance, the relevant administrative organs shall provide assistance within the scope of their functions and powers.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府可以组织开展综合督查、专项督查、事件调查、日常督办、线索核查等政府督查工作。

**Article 14** : People's governments at or above the county level may organize and carry out government supervision work such as comprehensive supervision, special supervision, incident investigation, daily supervision, and clue verification.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　开展政府督查工作应当制定督查方案，明确督查内容、对象和范围；应当严格控制督查频次和时限，科学运用督查方式，严肃督查纪律，提前培训督查人员。  
政府督查工作应当严格执行督查方案，不得随意扩大督查范围、变更督查对象和内容，不得干预督查对象的正常工作，严禁重复督查、多头督查、越权督查。

**Article 15** : To carry out government supervision work, a supervision plan shall be formulated, specifying the supervision content, targets, and scope; the frequency and time limit of supervision shall be strictly controlled, supervision methods shall be scientifically applied, supervision discipline shall be strictly observed, and supervision personnel shall be trained in advance.  
Government supervision work shall strictly implement the supervision plan, and shall not arbitrarily expand the supervision scope, change the supervision targets and content, or interfere with the normal work of the supervisee. Repeated supervision, multi-track supervision, and ultra vires supervision are strictly prohibited.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府在政府督查工作结束后应当作出督查结论。与督查对象有关的督查结论应当向督查对象反馈。  
督查结论应当事实清楚，证据充分，客观公正。

**Article 16** : After the completion of government supervision work, the people's government at or above the county level shall make a supervision conclusion. The supervision conclusion related to the supervisee shall be fed back to the supervisee.  
The supervision conclusion shall be clear in facts, sufficient in evidence, and objective and fair.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　督查对象对督查结论有异议的，可以自收到该督查结论之日起30日内，向作出该督查结论的人民政府申请复核。收到申请的人民政府应当在30日内作出复核决定。参与作出督查结论的工作人员在复核中应当回避。

**Article 17** : Where the supervisee has objections to the supervision conclusion, it may apply for review to the people's government that made the supervision conclusion within 30 days from the date of receipt of the supervision conclusion. The people's government that receives the application shall make a review decision within 30 days. The staff members who participated in making the supervision conclusion shall recuse themselves from the review.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对于督查结论中要求整改的事项，督查对象应当按要求整改。政府督查机构可以根据工作需要，对整改情况进行核查。

**Article 18** : For matters requiring rectification as stated in the supervision conclusion, the supervisee shall make rectification as required. The government supervision agency may, based on work needs, verify the rectification situation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　政府督查机构可以根据督查结论，提出改变或者撤销本级或者下级人民政府及其所属部门不适当的决定、命令等规范性文件的建议，报本级人民政府或者本级人民政府行政首长。

**Article 19** : The government supervision agency may, based on the supervision conclusion, propose suggestions to change or revoke inappropriate decisions, orders, and other regulatory documents of the people's government at the same level or at a lower level and their subordinate departments, and report them to the people's government at the same level or the chief executive of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　政府督查机构可以针对督查结论中反映的突出问题开展调查研究，真实准确地向本级人民政府或者本级人民政府行政首长报告调查研究情况。

**Article 20** : The government supervision agency may, targeting the prominent issues reflected in the supervision conclusion, carry out investigation and research, and truthfully and accurately report the investigation and research situation to the people's government at the same level or the chief executive of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　政府督查机构可以根据督查结论或者整改核查结果，提出对督查对象依法依规进行表扬、激励、批评等建议，经本级人民政府或者本级人民政府行政首长批准后组织实施。  
政府督查机构可以根据督查结论或者整改核查结果，提出对督查对象依法依规追究责任的建议，经本级人民政府或者本级人民政府行政首长批准后，交有权机关调查处理。

**Article 21** : The government supervision agency may, based on the supervision conclusion or the rectification verification results, propose suggestions to commend, incentivize, or criticize the supervisee according to laws and regulations, and organize implementation after approval by the people's government at the same level or the chief executive of the people's government at the same level.  
The government supervision agency may, based on the supervision conclusion or the rectification verification results, propose suggestions to investigate the responsibility of the supervisee according to laws and regulations, and after approval by the people's government at the same level or the chief executive of the people's government at the same level, refer the matter to the competent authority for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　政府督查应当加强与行政执法监督、备案审查监督等的协调衔接。

**Article 22** Government supervision shall strengthen coordination and alignment with the supervision of administrative law enforcement, supervision over the review of records, and other types of supervision.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　督查工作中发现公职人员涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务违法或者职务犯罪的问题线索，政府督查机构应当移送监察机关，由监察机关依法调查处置；发现涉嫌其他犯罪的问题线索，移送司法机关依法处理。

**Article 23** Where leads indicating suspicion of duty-related violations of law or duty-related crimes, such as embezzlement and bribery, dereliction of duty, or malfeasance, are discovered in the course of supervision, the government supervision authority shall transfer them to the supervisory authority, which shall investigate and handle them according to law; where leads indicating suspicion of other crimes are discovered, they shall be transferred to the judicial authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　政府督查机构及督查人员违反本条例规定，滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守的，泄露督查过程中所知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的，或者违反廉政规定的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法依规给予处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where a government supervision authority or its supervisors violate the provisions of these Regulations by abusing power, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, or neglecting their duties; disclose state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy learned in the course of supervision; or violate integrity regulations, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be dealt with according to laws and regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　督查对象及其工作人员不得阻碍督查工作，不得隐瞒实情、弄虚作假，不得伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据。有上述情形的，由政府督查机构责令改正；情节严重的，依法依规追究责任。

**Article 25** The supervised parties and their staff shall not obstruct the supervision work, shall not conceal the true situation or practice fraud, and shall not forge, conceal, or destroy evidence. If any of the above circumstances occur, the government supervision authority shall order corrections; if the circumstances are serious, liability shall be pursued according to laws and regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对督查人员或者提供线索、反映情况的单位和个人进行威胁、打击、报复、陷害的，依法依规追究责任。

**Article 26** Whoever subjects supervisors or units or individuals that provide leads or report information to intimidation, attack, retaliation, or framing shall be pursued for liability according to laws and regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府及其所属部门依照有关法律法规开展的其他监督检查，按照有关法律法规规定执行。

**Article 27** Other supervisory inspections carried out by people's governments at or above the county level and their subordinate departments in accordance with relevant laws and regulations shall be implemented according to the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自2021年2月1日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2021.
