# 地震监测管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （2004年6月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第409号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强对地震监测活动的管理，提高地震监测能力，根据《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于地震监测台网的规划、建设和管理以及地震监测设施和地震观测环境的保护。 第三条　地震监测工作是服务于经济建设、国防建设和社会发展的公益事业。 县级以上人民政府应当将地震监测工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划。 第四条　国家对地震监测台网实行统一规划，分级、分类管理。 第五条　国务院地震工作主管部门负责全国地震监测的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，负责本行政区域内地震监测的监督管理工作。 第六条　国家鼓励、支持地震监测的科学研究，推广应用先进的地震监测技术，开展地震监测的国际合作与交流。 有关地方人民政府应当支持少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和海岛的地震监测台网的建设和运行。 第七条　外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测活动，必须与中华人民共和国有关部门或者单位合作进行，并经国务院地震工作主管部门批准。 从事前款规定的活动，必须遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规的规定，并不得涉及国家秘密和危害国家安全。 第二章　地震监测台网的规划和建设 第八条　全国地震监测台网，由国家地震监测台网、省级地震监测台网和市、县地震监测台网组成。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对地震监测活动的管理，提高地震监测能力，根据《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Earthquake Prevention and Disaster Reduction Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the management of earthquake monitoring activities and improving earthquake monitoring capabilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于地震监测台网的规划、建设和管理以及地震监测设施和地震观测环境的保护。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the planning, construction and management of earthquake monitoring networks as well as the protection of earthquake monitoring facilities and seismic observation environment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地震监测工作是服务于经济建设、国防建设和社会发展的公益事业。  
县级以上人民政府应当将地震监测工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 3** Earthquake monitoring work is a public welfare undertaking serving economic construction, national defense construction and social development.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate earthquake monitoring work into the national economic and social development plans at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对地震监测台网实行统一规划，分级、分类管理。

**Article 4** The State shall adopt unified planning and hierarchical and categorical management for earthquake monitoring networks.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院地震工作主管部门负责全国地震监测的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，负责本行政区域内地震监测的监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of earthquake monitoring nationwide.  
The departments or institutions for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of earthquake monitoring within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励、支持地震监测的科学研究，推广应用先进的地震监测技术，开展地震监测的国际合作与交流。  
有关地方人民政府应当支持少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和海岛的地震监测台网的建设和运行。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on earthquake monitoring, promote the application of advanced earthquake monitoring technologies, and carry out international cooperation and exchange in earthquake monitoring.  
The relevant local people's governments shall support the construction and operation of earthquake monitoring networks in ethnic minority areas, remote poverty-stricken areas and islands.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测活动，必须与中华人民共和国有关部门或者单位合作进行，并经国务院地震工作主管部门批准。  
从事前款规定的活动，必须遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规的规定，并不得涉及国家秘密和危害国家安全。

**Article 7** Foreign organizations or individuals that engage in earthquake monitoring activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China must cooperate with the relevant departments or units of the People's Republic of China and must obtain approval from the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.  
Those who engage in the activities prescribed in the preceding paragraph must comply with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China, and shall not involve State secrets or endanger national security.

### 第二章　地震监测台网的规划和建设

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　全国地震监测台网，由国家地震监测台网、省级地震监测台网和市、县地震监测台网组成。  
专用地震监测台网和有关单位、个人建设的社会地震监测台站（点）是全国地震监测台网的补充。

**Article 8** The national earthquake monitoring network shall consist of the national earthquake monitoring network, the provincial earthquake monitoring networks and the municipal and county earthquake monitoring networks.  
Special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks and social earthquake monitoring stations (points) constructed by relevant units and individuals shall be supplements to the national earthquake monitoring network.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　编制地震监测台网规划，应当坚持布局合理、资源共享的原则，并与土地利用总体规划和城乡规划相协调。

**Article 9** The formulation of plans for earthquake monitoring networks shall adhere to the principles of reasonable layout and resource sharing, and shall be coordinated with the overall land use plan and urban and rural plans.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　全国地震监测台网总体规划和国家地震监测台网规划，由国务院地震工作主管部门根据全国地震监测预报方案商国务院有关部门制定，并负责组织实施。  
省级地震监测台网规划，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，根据全国地震监测台网总体规划和本行政区域地震监测预报方案制定，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
市、县地震监测台网规划，由市、县人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，根据省级地震监测台网规划制定，报本级人民政府批准后实施。

**Article 10** The overall plan for the national earthquake monitoring network and the plan for the national earthquake monitoring network shall be formulated by the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council on the basis of the national earthquake monitoring and prediction scheme, and the said department shall be responsible for organizing their implementation.  
The plan for a provincial earthquake monitoring network shall be formulated by the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government on the basis of the overall plan for the national earthquake monitoring network and the earthquake monitoring and prediction scheme for the administrative region, and shall be implemented after approval by the people's government at the corresponding level.  
The plan for a municipal or county earthquake monitoring network shall be formulated by the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of a city or county on the basis of the plan for the provincial earthquake monitoring network, and shall be implemented after approval by the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省级地震监测台网规划和市、县地震监测台网规划需要变更的，应当报原批准机关批准。

**Article 11** Where the plan for a provincial earthquake monitoring network or the plan for a municipal or county earthquake monitoring network needs to be modified, it shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全国地震监测台网和专用地震监测台网的建设，应当遵守法律、法规和国家有关标准，符合国家规定的固定资产投资项目建设程序，保证台网建设质量。  
全国地震监测台网的建设，应当依法实行招投标。

**Article 12** The construction of the national earthquake monitoring network and special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks shall comply with laws, regulations and relevant national standards, conform to the procedures for fixed asset investment project construction prescribed by the State, and ensure the quality of network construction.  
The construction of the national earthquake monitoring network shall adopt bidding according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设全国地震监测台网和专用地震监测台网，应当按照国务院地震工作主管部门的规定，采用符合国家标准、行业标准或者有关地震监测的技术要求的设备和软件。

**Article 13** The construction of the national earthquake monitoring network and special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council, adopt equipment and software that conform to national standards, industrial standards or relevant technical requirements for earthquake monitoring.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　下列建设工程应当建设专用地震监测台网：  
（一）坝高100米以上、库容5亿立方米以上，且可能诱发5级以上地震的水库；  
（二）受地震破坏后可能引发严重次生灾害的油田、矿山、石油化工等重大建设工程。

**Article 14** The following construction projects shall construct special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks:  
(1) reservoirs with a dam height of 100 meters or more, a storage capacity of 500 million cubic meters or more, and which may induce earthquakes of magnitude 5 or above;  
(2) major construction projects such as oil fields, mines, and petrochemical projects which may cause serious secondary disasters after being damaged by an earthquake.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　核电站、水库大坝、特大桥梁、发射塔等重大建设工程应当按照国家有关规定，设置强震动监测设施。

**Article 15** Major construction projects such as nuclear power plants, reservoir dams, extra-large bridges, and launch towers shall install strong motion monitoring facilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建设单位应当将专用地震监测台网、强震动监测设施的建设情况，报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构备案。

**Article 16** The construction unit shall report the construction progress of the special-purpose earthquake monitoring network and the strong motion monitoring facilities to the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the project is located for archival filing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励利用废弃的油井、矿井和人防工程进行地震监测。  
利用废弃的油井、矿井和人防工程进行地震监测的，应当采取相应的安全保障措施。

**Article 17** The State encourages the use of abandoned oil wells, mines and civil air defense works for earthquake monitoring.  
Where abandoned oil wells, mines and civil air defense works are used for earthquake monitoring, corresponding safety measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　全国地震监测台网的建设资金和运行经费，按照事权和财权相统一的原则，由中央和地方财政承担。  
专用地震监测台网、强震动监测设施的建设资金和运行经费，由建设单位承担。

**Article 18** The construction funds and operation expenses of the national earthquake monitoring network shall be borne by the central and local finance in accordance with the principle of unifying powers and financial resources.  
The construction funds and operation expenses of special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks and strong motion monitoring facilities shall be borne by the construction unit.

### 第三章　地震监测台网的管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　全国地震监测台网正式运行后，不得擅自中止或者终止；确需中止或者终止的，国家地震监测台网和省级地震监测台网必须经国务院地震工作主管部门批准，市、县地震监测台网必须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构批准，并报国务院地震工作主管部门备案。  
专用地震监测台网中止或者终止运行的，应当报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构备案。

**Article 19** After the national earthquake monitoring network is formally put into operation, it shall not be suspended or terminated without authorization; if it is truly necessary to suspend or terminate, the national earthquake monitoring network and the provincial earthquake monitoring network must be approved by the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council, and the municipal and county earthquake monitoring networks must be approved by the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council for archival filing.  
Where a special-purpose earthquake monitoring network is suspended or terminated, it shall be reported to the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for archival filing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当对专用地震监测台网和社会地震监测台站（点）的运行予以指导。

**Article 20** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or institutions for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide guidance on the operation of special-purpose earthquake monitoring networks and social earthquake monitoring stations (points).

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当为全国地震监测台网的运行提供必要的通信、交通、水、电等条件保障。  
全国地震监测台网、专用地震监测台网的运行受到影响时，当地人民政府应当组织有关部门采取紧急措施，尽快恢复地震监测台网的正常运行。

**Article 21** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary conditions such as communications, transportation, water and electricity for the operation of the national earthquake monitoring network.  
When the operation of the national earthquake monitoring network or a special-purpose earthquake monitoring network is affected, the local people's government shall organize the relevant departments to take emergency measures to restore the normal operation of the earthquake monitoring network as soon as possible.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　检测、传递、分析、处理、存贮、报送地震监测信息的单位，应当保证地震监测信息的安全和质量。

**Article 22** Units that detect, transmit, analyze, process, store and report earthquake monitoring information shall ensure the safety and quality of the earthquake monitoring information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　专用地震监测台网和强震动监测设施的管理单位，应当将地震监测信息及时报送所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构。

**Article 23** The management unit of a special-purpose earthquake monitoring network or strong motion monitoring facilities shall promptly report the earthquake monitoring information to the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当加强对从事地震监测工作人员的业务培训，提高其专业技术水平。

**Article 24** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or institutions for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the professional training of personnel engaged in earthquake monitoring and improve their professional and technical level.

### 第四章　地震监测设施和地震观测环境的保护

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家依法保护地震监测设施和地震观测环境。  
地震监测设施所在地的市、县人民政府应当加强对地震监测设施和地震观测环境的保护工作。  
任何单位和个人都有依法保护地震监测设施和地震观测环境的义务，对危害、破坏地震监测设施和地震观测环境的行为有权举报。

**Article 25** The State shall protect earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment according to law.  
The people's governments of cities and counties where earthquake monitoring facilities are located shall strengthen the protection of earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment.  
All units and individuals have the obligation to protect earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment according to law, and have the right to report acts that endanger or damage earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　禁止占用、拆除、损坏下列地震监测设施：  
（一）地震监测仪器、设备和装置；  
（二）供地震监测使用的山洞、观测井（泉）；  
（三）地震监测台网中心、中继站、遥测点的用房；  
（四）地震监测标志；  
（五）地震监测专用无线通信频段、信道和通信设施；  
（六）用于地震监测的供电、供水设施。

**Article 26** It shall be prohibited to occupy, dismantle or damage the following earthquake monitoring facilities:  
(1) earthquake monitoring instruments, equipment and devices;  
(2) caves and observation wells (springs) used for earthquake monitoring;  
(3) buildings of earthquake monitoring network centers, relay stations and telemetry points;  
(4) earthquake monitoring markers;  
(5) radio communication frequency bands, channels and communication facilities dedicated to earthquake monitoring;  
(6) power supply and water supply facilities used for earthquake monitoring.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　地震观测环境应当按照地震监测设施周围不能有影响其工作效能的干扰源的要求划定保护范围。具体保护范围，由县级以上人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构会同其他有关部门，按照国家有关标准规定的最小距离划定。  
国家有关标准对地震监测设施保护的最小距离尚未作出规定的，由县级以上人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构会同其他有关部门，按照国家有关标准规定的测试方法、计算公式等，通过现场实测确定。

**Article 27** The protection scope of the seismic observation environment shall be delimited in accordance with the requirement that there shall be no interference sources affecting the working effectiveness around the earthquake monitoring facilities. The specific protection scope shall be delimited by the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government at or above the county level jointly with other relevant departments, in accordance with the minimum distance prescribed by the relevant national standards.  
Where the relevant national standards have not yet prescribed the minimum distance for the protection of earthquake monitoring facilities, the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with other relevant departments, determine it through on-site measurements in accordance with the testing methods, calculation formulas, etc. prescribed by the relevant national standards.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　除依法从事[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)、第三十三条规定的建设活动外，禁止在已划定的地震观测环境保护范围内从事下列活动：  
（一）爆破、采矿、采石、钻井、抽水、注水；  
（二）在测震观测环境保护范围内设置无线信号发射装置、进行振动作业和往复机械运动；  
（三）在电磁观测环境保护范围内铺设金属管线、电力电缆线路、堆放磁性物品和设置高频电磁辐射装置；  
（四）在地形变观测环境保护范围内进行振动作业；  
（五）在地下流体观测环境保护范围内堆积和填埋垃圾、进行污水处理；  
（六）在观测线和观测标志周围设置障碍物或者擅自移动地震观测标志。

**Article 28** Except for construction activities carried out in accordance with the provisions of Articles 32 and 33 of these Regulations, it shall be prohibited to engage in the following activities within the delimited protection scope of the seismic observation environment:  
(1) blasting, mining, quarrying, drilling, pumping water, injecting water;  
(2) installing wireless signal transmitting devices, carrying out vibration operations and reciprocating mechanical movements within the protection scope of the seismometric observation environment;  
(3) laying metal pipelines, power cable lines, stacking magnetic articles and installing high-frequency electromagnetic radiation devices within the protection scope of the electromagnetic observation environment;  
(4) carrying out vibration operations within the protection scope of the crustal deformation observation environment;  
(5) piling and burying garbage, and carrying out sewage treatment within the protection scope of the underground fluid observation environment;  
(6) setting up obstacles around observation lines and observation markers, or moving earthquake observation markers without authorization.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当会同有关部门在地震监测设施附近设立保护标志，标明地震监测设施和地震观测环境保护的要求。

**Article 29** The departments or institutions for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the relevant departments, set up protection markers near earthquake monitoring facilities, indicating the requirements for the protection of earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当将本行政区域内的地震监测设施的分布地点及其保护范围，报告当地人民政府，并通报同级公安机关和国土资源、城乡规划、测绘等部门。

**Article 30** The departments or institutions for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall report the distribution locations and protection scopes of earthquake monitoring facilities within their respective administrative regions to the local people's government, and shall notify the public security organ at the corresponding level and the departments of land and resources, urban and rural planning, surveying and mapping, etc.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　土地利用总体规划和城乡规划应当考虑保护地震监测设施和地震观测环境的需要。

**Article 31** The overall land use plan and urban and rural plans shall take into consideration the need to protect earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，应当遵循国家有关测震、电磁、形变、流体等地震观测环境保护的标准，避免对地震监测设施和地震观测环境造成危害。对在地震观测环境保护范围内的建设工程项目，县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门在核发选址意见书时，应当事先征求同级人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的意见；负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构应当在10日内反馈意见。

**Article 32** The construction of new projects, or the expansion or renovation of existing projects, shall comply with the national standards for the protection of seismic observation environments, such as those for seismometry, electromagnetism, crustal deformation and underground fluids, so as to avoid causing harm to earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment. For construction project sites within the protection scope of the seismic observation environment, the competent department of urban and rural planning under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, when issuing the site selection opinion, solicit in advance the opinions of the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the people's government at the same level; the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs shall provide feedback within 10 days.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　建设国家重点工程，确实无法避免对地震监测设施和地震观测环境造成破坏的，建设单位应当按照县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的要求，增建抗干扰设施或者新建地震监测设施后，方可进行建设。  
需要新建地震监测设施的，县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，可以要求新建地震监测设施正常运行1年以后，再拆除原地震监测设施。  
本条第一款、第二款规定的措施所需费用，由建设单位承担。

**Article 33** Where the construction of a national key project truly cannot avoid causing damage to earthquake monitoring facilities and the seismic observation environment, the construction unit shall, in accordance with the requirements of the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's government at or above the county level, construct additional anti-interference facilities or new earthquake monitoring facilities before proceeding with the construction.  
Where new earthquake monitoring facilities need to be constructed, the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's government at or above the county level may require that the new earthquake monitoring facilities be in normal operation for one year before the original earthquake monitoring facilities are dismantled.  
The expenses required for the measures specified in Paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be borne by the construction unit.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例的规定，国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的工作人员，不履行监督管理职责，发现违法行为不予查处或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为，构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 34** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any staff member of the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or of a department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under a local people's government at or above the county level fails to perform their supervision and administration duties, fails to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered, or commits any other act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed on the persons in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令改正，并要求采取相应的补救措施，对主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分：  
（一）未按照有关法律、法规和国家有关标准进行地震监测台网建设的；  
（二）未按照国务院地震工作主管部门的规定采用地震监测设备和软件的；  
（三）擅自中止或者终止地震监测台网运行的。

**Article 35** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and require the adoption of corresponding remedial measures, and administrative sanctions shall be imposed on the persons in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law:  
(1) failing to construct an earthquake monitoring network in accordance with the relevant laws, regulations and national standards;  
(2) failing to adopt earthquake monitoring equipment and software in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council;  
(3) suspending or terminating the operation of an earthquake monitoring network without authorization.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十八条所列行为之一的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令停止违法行为，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施。  
单位有前款所列违法行为，情节严重的，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；个人有前款所列违法行为，情节严重的，处2000元以下的罚款。构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 36** Where any of the acts listed in Articles 26 and 28 of these Regulations is committed, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition or adoption of other remedial measures.  
Where a unit commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; where an individual commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例的规定，建设单位从事建设活动时，未按照要求增建抗干扰设施或者新建地震监测设施，对地震监测设施或者地震观测环境造成破坏的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令改正，限期恢复原状或者采取相应的补救措施；情节严重的，依照[《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》第八十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国防震减灾法.md#art-85)的规定处以罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 37** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction unit, when engaging in construction activities, fails to construct additional anti-interference facilities or new earthquake monitoring facilities as required, thereby causing damage to earthquake monitoring facilities or the seismic observation environment, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the department or institution for managing earthquake affairs under the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and require restoration to the original condition or adoption of corresponding remedial measures within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 85 of the Earthquake Prevention and Disaster Reduction Law of the People's Republic of China; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例的规定，外国的组织或者个人未经批准，擅自在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域进行地震监测活动的，由国务院地震工作主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收监测成果和监测设施，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a foreign organization or individual, without approval, engages in earthquake monitoring activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China or other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the monitoring results and monitoring facilities, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　火山监测的管理，参照本条例执行。

**Article 39** The management of volcano monitoring shall be implemented by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自2004年9月1日起施行。1994年1月10日国务院发布的《地震监测设施和地震观测环境保护条例》同时废止。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2004. The Regulations on the Protection of Earthquake Monitoring Facilities and Seismic Observation Environment promulgated by the State Council on January 10, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
