# 信息网络传播权保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2013-01-30  
**生效日期**：2006-07-01

> (2006年5月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第468号公布　根据2013年1月30日《国务院关于修改〈信息网络传播权保护条例〉的决定》修订) 第一条　为保护著作权人、表演者、录音录像制作者(以下统称权利人)的信息网络传播权，鼓励有益于社会主义精神文明、物质文明建设的作品的创作和传播，根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下简称著作权法)，制定本条例。 第二条　权利人享有的信息网络传播权受著作权法和本条例保护。除法律、行政法规另有规定的外，任何组织或者个人将他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品通过信息网络向公众提供，应当取得权利人许可，并支付报酬。 第三条　依法禁止提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品，不受本条例保护。 权利人行使信息网络传播权，不得违反宪法和法律、行政法规，不得损害公共利益。 第四条　为了保护信息网络传播权，权利人可以采取技术措施。 任何组织或者个人不得故意避开或者破坏技术措施，不得故意制造、进口或者向公众提供主要用于避开或者破坏技术措施的装置或者部件，不得故意为他人避开或者破坏技术措施提供技术服务。但是，法律、行政法规规定可以避开的除外。 第五条　未经权利人许可，任何组织或者个人不得进行下列行为： (一)故意删除或者改变通过信息网络向公众提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品的权利管理电子信息，但由于技术上的原因无法避免删除或者改变的除外； (二)通过信息网络向公众提供明知或者应知未经权利人许可被删除或者改变权利管理电子信息的作品、表演、录音录像制品。 第六条　通过信息网络提供他人作品，属于下列情形的，可以不经著作权人许可，不向其支付报酬： (一)为介绍、评论某一作品或者说明某一问题，在向公众提供的作品中适当引用已经发表的作品； (二)为报道时事新闻，在向公众提供的作品中不可避免地再现或者引用已经发表的作品； (三)为学校课堂教学或者科学研究，向少数教学、科研人员提供少量已经发表的作品； (四)国家机关为执行公务，在合理范围内向公众提供已经发表的作品；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保护著作权人、表演者、录音录像制作者(以下统称权利人)的信息网络传播权，鼓励有益于社会主义精神文明、物质文明建设的作品的创作和传播，根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下简称著作权法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Copyright Law) for the purpose of protecting the right of communication through information network of copyright owners, performers, and producers of sound recordings and video recordings (hereinafter collectively referred to as rights holders), and encouraging the creation and dissemination of works that are conducive to the construction of socialist spiritual civilization and material civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　权利人享有的信息网络传播权受著作权法和本条例保护。除法律、行政法规另有规定的外，任何组织或者个人将他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品通过信息网络向公众提供，应当取得权利人许可，并支付报酬。

**Article 2** The right of communication through information network enjoyed by rights holders shall be protected by the Copyright Law and these Regulations. Unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations, any organization or individual that provides another person's work, performance, or sound recording or video recording to the public through an information network shall obtain permission from the rights holder and pay remuneration.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法禁止提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品，不受本条例保护。  
权利人行使信息网络传播权，不得违反宪法和法律、行政法规，不得损害公共利益。

**Article 3** Works, performances, and sound recordings or video recordings the provision of which is prohibited according to law shall not be protected by these Regulations.  
When exercising the right of communication through information network, rights holders shall not violate the Constitution and laws and administrative regulations, and shall not harm the public interest.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　为了保护信息网络传播权，权利人可以采取技术措施。  
任何组织或者个人不得故意避开或者破坏技术措施，不得故意制造、进口或者向公众提供主要用于避开或者破坏技术措施的装置或者部件，不得故意为他人避开或者破坏技术措施提供技术服务。但是，法律、行政法规规定可以避开的除外。

**Article 4** For the purpose of protecting the right of communication through information network, rights holders may take technological measures.  
No organization or individual shall intentionally circumvent or destroy technological measures, shall not intentionally manufacture, import, or provide to the public devices or components primarily used for circumventing or destroying technological measures, and shall not intentionally provide technical services for others to circumvent or destroy technological measures, except where circumvention is permitted by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　未经权利人许可，任何组织或者个人不得进行下列行为：  
(一)故意删除或者改变通过信息网络向公众提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品的权利管理电子信息，但由于技术上的原因无法避免删除或者改变的除外；  
(二)通过信息网络向公众提供明知或者应知未经权利人许可被删除或者改变权利管理电子信息的作品、表演、录音录像制品。

**Article 5** Without the permission of the rights holder, no organization or individual shall engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) intentionally deleting or altering any electronic information on rights management of a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording provided to the public through an information network, except where the deletion or alteration is unavoidable due to technical reasons;  
(2) providing to the public through an information network any work, performance, or sound recording or video recording with the electronic information on rights management deleted or altered without the permission of the rights holder, knowing or having reason to know that such deletion or alteration is unauthorized.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　通过信息网络提供他人作品，属于下列情形的，可以不经著作权人许可，不向其支付报酬：  
(一)为介绍、评论某一作品或者说明某一问题，在向公众提供的作品中适当引用已经发表的作品；  
(二)为报道时事新闻，在向公众提供的作品中不可避免地再现或者引用已经发表的作品；  
(三)为学校课堂教学或者科学研究，向少数教学、科研人员提供少量已经发表的作品；  
(四)国家机关为执行公务，在合理范围内向公众提供已经发表的作品；  
(五)将中国公民、法人或者其他组织已经发表的、以汉语言文字创作的作品翻译成的少数民族语言文字作品，向中国境内少数民族提供；  
(六)不以营利为目的，以盲人能够感知的独特方式向盲人提供已经发表的文字作品；  
(七)向公众提供在信息网络上已经发表的关于政治、经济问题的时事性文章；  
(八)向公众提供在公众集会上发表的讲话。

**Article 6** Where a work of another person is provided through an information network under any of the following circumstances, permission may not be obtained from the copyright owner and remuneration may not be paid:  
(1) appropriately quoting a published work in the work provided to the public for the purpose of introducing or commenting on a certain work, or explaining a certain issue;  
(2) inevitably reproducing or quoting a published work in the work provided to the public for the purpose of reporting current events and news;  
(3) providing a small amount of published works to a small number of teaching or scientific research personnel for the purpose of classroom teaching or scientific research in schools;  
(4) providing published works to the public within a reasonable scope by a state organ for the purpose of performing official duties;  
(5) translating works created in Chinese language and published by Chinese citizens, legal persons, or other organizations into works in minority ethnic group languages, and providing them to ethnic minorities within the territory of China;  
(6) providing published written works to blind persons in a unique manner perceivable by them, without profit-making purpose;  
(7) providing to the public current affairs articles on political or economic issues already published on information networks;  
(8) providing to the public speeches delivered at public gatherings.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　图书馆、档案馆、纪念馆、博物馆、美术馆等可以不经著作权人许可，通过信息网络向本馆馆舍内服务对象提供本馆收藏的合法出版的数字作品和依法为陈列或者保存版本的需要以数字化形式复制的作品，不向其支付报酬，但不得直接或者间接获得经济利益。当事人另有约定的除外。  
前款规定的为陈列或者保存版本需要以数字化形式复制的作品，应当是已经损毁或者濒临损毁、丢失或者失窃，或者其存储格式已经过时，并且在市场上无法购买或者只能以明显高于标定的价格购买的作品。

**Article 7** Libraries, archives, memorial halls, museums, art galleries, etc. may, without permission from the copyright owner, provide through an information network within the premises of their buildings to service objects the lawfully published digital works collected by them and the works reproduced in digital form according to law for the needs of display or preservation of versions, and may not pay remuneration therefor, provided that no economic benefits shall be obtained directly or indirectly, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
The works reproduced in digital form for the needs of display or preservation of versions as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be works that have been damaged or are on the verge of damage, lost or stolen, or whose storage format has become obsolete, and that cannot be purchased on the market or can only be purchased at a price significantly higher than the marked price.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　为通过信息网络实施九年制义务教育或者国家教育规划，可以不经著作权人许可，使用其已经发表作品的片断或者短小的文字作品、音乐作品或者单幅的美术作品、摄影作品制作课件，由制作课件或者依法取得课件的远程教育机构通过信息网络向注册学生提供，但应当向著作权人支付报酬。

**Article 8** For the purpose of implementing nine-year compulsory education or national education plans through an information network, fragments of a published work, short written works, musical works, or single pieces of fine art works or photographic works may be used to produce courseware without permission from the copyright owner, and the courseware may be provided by the distance education institution that produced or lawfully obtained the courseware to enrolled students through an information network, provided that remuneration shall be paid to the copyright owner.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　为扶助贫困，通过信息网络向农村地区的公众免费提供中国公民、法人或者其他组织已经发表的种植养殖、防病治病、防灾减灾等与扶助贫困有关的作品和适应基本文化需求的作品，网络服务提供者应当在提供前公告拟提供的作品及其作者、拟支付报酬的标准。自公告之日起30日内，著作权人不同意提供的，网络服务提供者不得提供其作品；自公告之日起满30日，著作权人没有异议的，网络服务提供者可以提供其作品，并按照公告的标准向著作权人支付报酬。网络服务提供者提供著作权人的作品后，著作权人不同意提供的，网络服务提供者应当立即删除著作权人的作品，并按照公告的标准向著作权人支付提供作品期间的报酬。  
依照前款规定提供作品的，不得直接或者间接获得经济利益。

**Article 9** For the purpose of supporting poverty alleviation, where works related to poverty alleviation such as planting, breeding, disease prevention and treatment, disaster prevention and mitigation, and works meeting basic cultural needs that have been published by Chinese citizens, legal persons, or other organizations are provided free of charge to the public in rural areas through an information network, the network service provider shall, before provision, announce the works to be provided and their authors, and the standard of remuneration to be paid. Where, within 30 days from the date of announcement, the copyright owner does not consent to the provision, the network service provider shall not provide that work; where, upon expiry of 30 days from the date of announcement, the copyright owner raises no objection, the network service provider may provide that work and shall pay remuneration to the copyright owner in accordance with the announced standard. Where, after the network service provider has provided the work of the copyright owner, the copyright owner does not consent to the provision, the network service provider shall immediately delete the work of the copyright owner and pay remuneration to the copyright owner in accordance with the announced standard for the period during which the work was provided.  
Where works are provided in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, no economic benefits shall be obtained directly or indirectly.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依照本条例规定不经著作权人许可、通过信息网络向公众提供其作品的，还应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)除[本条例第六条](#art-6)第一项至第六项、第七条规定的情形外，不得提供作者事先声明不许提供的作品；  
(二)指明作品的名称和作者的姓名(名称)；  
(三)依照本条例规定支付报酬；  
(四)采取技术措施，防止[本条例第七条](#art-7)、第八条、第九条规定的服务对象以外的其他人获得著作权人的作品，并防止[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的服务对象的复制行为对著作权人利益造成实质性损害；  
(五)不得侵犯著作权人依法享有的其他权利。

**Article 10** Where a work is provided to the public through an information network without permission from the copyright owner in accordance with these Regulations, the following provisions shall also be complied with:  
(1) except for the circumstances specified in Items (1) to (6) of Article 6 and Article 7 of these Regulations, no work shall be provided that the author has declared in advance not to be provided;  
(2) the title of the work and the name (or title) of the author shall be indicated;  
(3) remuneration shall be paid in accordance with these Regulations;  
(4) technological measures shall be taken to prevent persons other than the service objects specified in Articles 7, 8, and 9 of these Regulations from obtaining the work of the copyright owner, and to prevent the reproduction by the service objects specified in Article 7 of these Regulations from causing substantial harm to the interests of the copyright owner;  
(5) no other rights lawfully enjoyed by the copyright owner shall be infringed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　通过信息网络提供他人表演、录音录像制品的，应当遵守[本条例第六条](#art-6)至第十条的规定。

**Article 11** Where a performance or sound recording or video recording of another person is provided through an information network, the provisions of Articles 6 to 10 of these Regulations shall be complied with.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　属于下列情形的，可以避开技术措施，但不得向他人提供避开技术措施的技术、装置或者部件，不得侵犯权利人依法享有的其他权利：  
(一)为学校课堂教学或者科学研究，通过信息网络向少数教学、科研人员提供已经发表的作品、表演、录音录像制品，而该作品、表演、录音录像制品只能通过信息网络获取；  
(二)不以营利为目的，通过信息网络以盲人能够感知的独特方式向盲人提供已经发表的文字作品，而该作品只能通过信息网络获取；  
(三)国家机关依照行政、司法程序执行公务；  
(四)在信息网络上对计算机及其系统或者网络的安全性能进行测试。

**Article 12** Technological measures may be circumvented under any of the following circumstances, provided that no technology, device, or component for circumventing technological measures shall be provided to others, and no other rights lawfully enjoyed by the rights holder shall be infringed:  
(1) providing published works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings to a small number of teaching or scientific research personnel through an information network for the purpose of classroom teaching or scientific research in schools, where such works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings can only be obtained through an information network;  
(2) providing published written works to blind persons through an information network in a unique manner perceivable by them, without profit-making purpose, where such works can only be obtained through an information network;  
(3) a state organ performing official duties in accordance with administrative or judicial procedures;  
(4) testing the security performance of a computer and its system or network on an information network.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　著作权行政管理部门为了查处侵犯信息网络传播权的行为，可以要求网络服务提供者提供涉嫌侵权的服务对象的姓名(名称)、联系方式、网络地址等资料。

**Article 13** For the purpose of investigating and punishing acts infringing the right of communication through information network, the copyright administrative department may require a network service provider to provide materials such as the name (or title), contact information, and network address of the service object suspected of infringement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对提供信息存储空间或者提供搜索、链接服务的网络服务提供者，权利人认为其服务所涉及的作品、表演、录音录像制品，侵犯自己的信息网络传播权或者被删除、改变了自己的权利管理电子信息的，可以向该网络服务提供者提交书面通知，要求网络服务提供者删除该作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者断开与该作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接。通知书应当包含下列内容：  
(一)权利人的姓名(名称)、联系方式和地址；  
(二)要求删除或者断开链接的侵权作品、表演、录音录像制品的名称和网络地址；  
(三)构成侵权的初步证明材料。  
权利人应当对通知书的真实性负责。

**Article 14** With respect to a network service provider that provides information storage space or provides search or link services, if a rights holder believes that a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording involved in its service infringes its right of communication through information network or that its electronic information on rights management has been deleted or altered, it may submit a written notice to the network service provider, requesting the network service provider to delete that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording or to disconnect the link to that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording. The notice shall contain the following:  
(1) the name (or title), contact information, and address of the rights holder;  
(2) the title and network address of the allegedly infringing work, performance, or sound recording or video recording requested to be deleted or to which the link is to be disconnected;  
(3) preliminary materials evidencing the infringement.  
The rights holder shall be responsible for the authenticity of the notice.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　网络服务提供者接到权利人的通知书后，应当立即删除涉嫌侵权的作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者断开与涉嫌侵权的作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接，并同时将通知书转送提供作品、表演、录音录像制品的服务对象；服务对象网络地址不明、无法转送的，应当将通知书的内容同时在信息网络上公告。

**Article 15** After receiving the notice from the rights holder, the network service provider shall immediately delete the allegedly infringing work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, or disconnect the link to the allegedly infringing work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, and shall at the same time forward the notice to the service object that provided such work, performance, or sound recording or video recording; where the network address of the service object is unknown and the notice cannot be forwarded, the content of the notice shall be simultaneously announced on the information network.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　服务对象接到网络服务提供者转送的通知书后，认为其提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品未侵犯他人权利的，可以向网络服务提供者提交书面说明，要求恢复被删除的作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者恢复与被断开的作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接。书面说明应当包含下列内容：  
(一)服务对象的姓名(名称)、联系方式和地址；  
(二)要求恢复的作品、表演、录音录像制品的名称和网络地址；  
(三)不构成侵权的初步证明材料。  
服务对象应当对书面说明的真实性负责。

**Article 16** After receiving the notice forwarded by the network service provider, if the service object believes that the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording provided by it does not infringe another person's rights, it may submit a written statement to the network service provider, requesting the restoration of the deleted work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, or the restoration of the link to the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording that has been disconnected. The written statement shall contain the following:  
(1) the name (or title), contact information, and address of the service object;  
(2) the title and network address of the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording requested to be restored;  
(3) preliminary materials evidencing that no infringement is constituted.  
The service object shall be responsible for the authenticity of the written statement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　网络服务提供者接到服务对象的书面说明后，应当立即恢复被删除的作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者可以恢复与被断开的作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接，同时将服务对象的书面说明转送权利人。权利人不得再通知网络服务提供者删除该作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者断开与该作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接。

**Article 17** After receiving the written statement from the service object, the network service provider shall immediately restore the deleted work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, or may restore the link to the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording that has been disconnected, and shall at the same time forward the written statement of the service object to the rights holder. The rights holder shall not again notify the network service provider to delete that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording or to disconnect the link to that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列侵权行为之一的，根据情况承担停止侵害、消除影响、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失等民事责任；同时损害公共利益的，可以由著作权行政管理部门责令停止侵权行为，没收违法所得，非法经营额5万元以上的，可处非法经营额1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有非法经营额或者非法经营额5万元以下的，根据情节轻重，可处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，著作权行政管理部门可以没收主要用于提供网络服务的计算机等设备；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)通过信息网络擅自向公众提供他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品的；  
(二)故意避开或者破坏技术措施的；  
(三)故意删除或者改变通过信息网络向公众提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品的权利管理电子信息，或者通过信息网络向公众提供明知或者应知未经权利人许可而被删除或者改变权利管理电子信息的作品、表演、录音录像制品的；  
(四)为扶助贫困通过信息网络向农村地区提供作品、表演、录音录像制品超过规定范围，或者未按照公告的标准支付报酬，或者在权利人不同意提供其作品、表演、录音录像制品后未立即删除的；  
(五)通过信息网络提供他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品，未指明作品、表演、录音录像制品的名称或者作者、表演者、录音录像制作者的姓名(名称)，或者未支付报酬，或者未依照本条例规定采取技术措施防止服务对象以外的其他人获得他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者未防止服务对象的复制行为对权利人利益造成实质性损害的。

**Article 18** Where any of the following acts of infringement is committed in violation of these Regulations, civil liability such as cessation of infringement, elimination of effects, making of an apology, and compensation for losses shall be borne according to the circumstances; where public interests are also harmed, the copyright administrative department may order the cessation of the infringing act, confiscate the illegal gains, and where the illegal business turnover is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal business turnover may be imposed; where there is no illegal business turnover or the illegal business turnover is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may be imposed according to the seriousness of the circumstances; where the circumstances are serious, the copyright administrative department may confiscate computers and other equipment mainly used for providing network services; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) providing the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording of another person to the public through an information network without authorization;  
(2) intentionally circumventing or destroying technological measures;  
(3) intentionally deleting or altering the electronic information on rights management of a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording provided to the public through an information network, or providing to the public through an information network a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording with the electronic information on rights management deleted or altered without the permission of the rights holder, knowing or having reason to know that such deletion or alteration is unauthorized;  
(4) providing works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings to rural areas through an information network for the purpose of supporting poverty alleviation beyond the prescribed scope, or failing to pay remuneration in accordance with the announced standard, or failing to immediately delete after the copyright owner does not consent to the provision of its work, performance, or sound recording or video recording;  
(5) providing the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording of another person through an information network without indicating the title of the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording or the name (or title) of the author, performer, or producer of sound recording or video recording, or failing to pay remuneration, or failing to take technological measures in accordance with these Regulations to prevent persons other than the service objects from obtaining the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording of another person, or failing to prevent the reproduction by the service objects from causing substantial harm to the interests of the rights holder.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由著作权行政管理部门予以警告，没收违法所得，没收主要用于避开、破坏技术措施的装置或者部件；情节严重的，可以没收主要用于提供网络服务的计算机等设备；非法经营额5万元以上的，可处非法经营额1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有非法经营额或者非法经营额5万元以下的，根据情节轻重，可处25万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)故意制造、进口或者向他人提供主要用于避开、破坏技术措施的装置或者部件，或者故意为他人避开或者破坏技术措施提供技术服务的；  
(二)通过信息网络提供他人的作品、表演、录音录像制品，获得经济利益的；  
(三)为扶助贫困通过信息网络向农村地区提供作品、表演、录音录像制品，未在提供前公告作品、表演、录音录像制品的名称和作者、表演者、录音录像制作者的姓名(名称)以及报酬标准的。

**Article 19** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of these Regulations, the copyright administrative department shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and confiscate the devices or components mainly used for circumventing or destroying technological measures; where the circumstances are serious, computers and other equipment mainly used for providing network services may be confiscated; where the illegal business turnover is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal business turnover may be imposed; where there is no illegal business turnover or the illegal business turnover is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may be imposed according to the seriousness of the circumstances; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) intentionally manufacturing, importing, or providing to others devices or components mainly used for circumventing or destroying technological measures, or intentionally providing technical services for others to circumvent or destroy technological measures;  
(2) providing the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording of another person through an information network and obtaining economic benefits;  
(3) providing works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings to rural areas through an information network for the purpose of supporting poverty alleviation, without announcing before provision the titles of the works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings and the names (or titles) of the authors, performers, or producers of sound recordings or video recordings, and the standard of remuneration.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　网络服务提供者根据服务对象的指令提供网络自动接入服务，或者对服务对象提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品提供自动传输服务，并具备下列条件的，不承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未选择并且未改变所传输的作品、表演、录音录像制品；  
(二)向指定的服务对象提供该作品、表演、录音录像制品，并防止指定的服务对象以外的其他人获得。

**Article 20** A network service provider that provides network automatic access services according to the instructions of a service object, or provides automatic transmission services for the works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings provided by the service object, and meets the following conditions, shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) it has not selected and has not altered the transmitted work, performance, or sound recording or video recording;  
(2) it provides that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording to the designated service object and prevents persons other than the designated service object from obtaining it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　网络服务提供者为提高网络传输效率，自动存储从其他网络服务提供者获得的作品、表演、录音录像制品，根据技术安排自动向服务对象提供，并具备下列条件的，不承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未改变自动存储的作品、表演、录音录像制品；  
(二)不影响提供作品、表演、录音录像制品的原网络服务提供者掌握服务对象获取该作品、表演、录音录像制品的情况；  
(三)在原网络服务提供者修改、删除或者屏蔽该作品、表演、录音录像制品时，根据技术安排自动予以修改、删除或者屏蔽。

**Article 21** A network service provider that, for the purpose of improving network transmission efficiency, automatically stores works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings obtained from other network service providers and automatically provides them to service objects according to technical arrangements, and meets the following conditions, shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) it has not altered the automatically stored work, performance, or sound recording or video recording;  
(2) it does not affect the ability of the original network service provider that provided the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording to control the situation of service objects obtaining that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording;  
(3) when the original network service provider modifies, deletes, or blocks that work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, it automatically modifies, deletes, or blocks it according to the technical arrangements.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　网络服务提供者为服务对象提供信息存储空间，供服务对象通过信息网络向公众提供作品、表演、录音录像制品，并具备下列条件的，不承担赔偿责任：  
(一)明确标示该信息存储空间是为服务对象所提供，并公开网络服务提供者的名称、联系人、网络地址；  
(二)未改变服务对象所提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品；  
(三)不知道也没有合理的理由应当知道服务对象提供的作品、表演、录音录像制品侵权；  
(四)未从服务对象提供作品、表演、录音录像制品中直接获得经济利益；  
(五)在接到权利人的通知书后，根据本条例规定删除权利人认为侵权的作品、表演、录音录像制品。

**Article 22** A network service provider that provides information storage space for service objects for the service objects to provide works, performances, or sound recordings or video recordings to the public through an information network, and meets the following conditions, shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) it clearly indicates that the information storage space is provided for the service object, and makes public the name, contact person, and network address of the network service provider;  
(2) it has not altered the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording provided by the service object;  
(3) it does not know and has no reasonable grounds to know that the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording provided by the service object infringes rights;  
(4) it has not obtained direct economic benefits from the service object's provision of the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording;  
(5) upon receiving the notice from the rights holder, it deletes the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording that the rights holder believes to be infringing in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　网络服务提供者为服务对象提供搜索或者链接服务，在接到权利人的通知书后，根据本条例规定断开与侵权的作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接的，不承担赔偿责任；但是，明知或者应知所链接的作品、表演、录音录像制品侵权的，应当承担共同侵权责任。

**Article 23** A network service provider that provides search or link services for service objects, and upon receiving a notice from a rights holder, disconnects the link to an infringing work, performance, or sound recording or video recording in accordance with these Regulations, shall not bear compensation liability; however, if it knows or has reason to know that the linked work, performance, or sound recording or video recording is infringing, it shall bear joint infringement liability.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因权利人的通知导致网络服务提供者错误删除作品、表演、录音录像制品，或者错误断开与作品、表演、录音录像制品的链接，给服务对象造成损失的，权利人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 24** Where, due to the notice of a rights holder, a network service provider erroneously deletes a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, or erroneously disconnects a link to a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording, thereby causing losses to the service object, the rights holder shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　网络服务提供者无正当理由拒绝提供或者拖延提供涉嫌侵权的服务对象的姓名(名称)、联系方式、网络地址等资料的，由著作权行政管理部门予以警告；情节严重的，没收主要用于提供网络服务的计算机等设备。

**Article 25** Where a network service provider, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide or delays in providing materials such as the name (or title), contact information, and network address of the service object suspected of infringement, the copyright administrative department shall give it a warning; where the circumstances are serious, computers and other equipment mainly used for providing network services shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
信息网络传播权，是指以有线或者无线方式向公众提供作品、表演或者录音录像制品，使公众可以在其个人选定的时间和地点获得作品、表演或者录音录像制品的权利。  
技术措施，是指用于防止、限制未经权利人许可浏览、欣赏作品、表演、录音录像制品的或者通过信息网络向公众提供作品、表演、录音录像制品的有效技术、装置或者部件。  
权利管理电子信息，是指说明作品及其作者、表演及其表演者、录音录像制品及其制作者的信息，作品、表演、录音录像制品权利人的信息和使用条件的信息，以及表示上述信息的数字或者代码。

**Article 26** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
'Right of communication through information network' means the right to provide a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording to the public by wire or wireless means, enabling the public to obtain such work, performance, or sound recording or video recording at a time and place individually chosen by them.  
'Technological measures' means effective technologies, devices, or components used to prevent or restrict the browsing or appreciation of a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording without the permission of the rights holder, or to prevent or restrict the provision of a work, performance, or sound recording or video recording to the public through an information network.  
'Electronic information on rights management' means information that identifies the work and its author, the performance and its performer, the sound recording or video recording and its producer, information of the rights holder of the work, performance, or sound recording or video recording and conditions of use, as well as numbers or codes that represent such information.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2006年7月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2006.
