# 机关事务管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2012-06-28  
**生效日期**：2012-10-01

> (2012年6月13日国务院第208次常务会议通过　2012年6月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第621号公布　自2012年10月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强机关事务管理，规范机关事务工作，保障机关正常运行，降低机关运行成本，建设节约型机关，制定本条例。 第二条　各级人民政府及其部门的机关事务管理活动适用本条例。 第三条　县级以上人民政府应当推进本级政府机关事务的统一管理，建立健全管理制度和标准，统筹配置资源。 政府各部门应当对本部门的机关事务实行集中管理，执行机关事务管理制度和标准。 第四条　国务院机关事务主管部门负责拟订有关机关事务管理的规章制度，指导下级政府公务用车、公务接待、公共机构节约能源资源等工作，主管中央国家机关的机关事务工作。 县级以上地方人民政府机关事务主管部门指导下级政府有关机关事务工作，主管本级政府的机关事务工作。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对本级政府各部门和下级政府的机关事务工作的监督检查，及时纠正违法违纪行为。 县级以上人民政府发展改革、财政、审计、监察等部门和机关事务主管部门应当根据职责分工，依照有关法律、法规的规定，加强对机关运行经费、资产和服务管理工作的监督检查；接到对违反机关事务管理制度、标准行为的举报，应当及时依法调查处理。 第六条　机关事务工作应当遵循保障公务、厉行节约、务实高效、公开透明的原则。 第七条　各级人民政府应当依照国家有关政府信息公开的规定建立健全机关运行经费公开制度，定期公布公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费等机关运行经费的预算和决算情况。 第八条　各级人民政府应当推进机关后勤服务、公务用车和公务接待服务等工作的社会化改革，建立健全相关管理制度。 第二章　经费管理 第九条　各级人民政府及其部门应当加强机关运行经费管理，提高资金使用效益。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强机关事务管理，规范机关事务工作，保障机关正常运行，降低机关运行成本，建设节约型机关，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of government affairs, regulate government affairs work, ensure the normal operation of government offices, reduce operating costs, and build resource-efficient offices.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府及其部门的机关事务管理活动适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the government affairs management activities of people's governments at all levels and their departments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当推进本级政府机关事务的统一管理，建立健全管理制度和标准，统筹配置资源。  
政府各部门应当对本部门的机关事务实行集中管理，执行机关事务管理制度和标准。

**Article 3** People's governments at or above the county level shall promote the unified management of government affairs of the government at the same level, establish and improve management systems and standards, and allocate resources in a coordinated manner.  
All government departments shall implement centralized management of the government affairs of their own departments and implement the government affairs management systems and standards.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院机关事务主管部门负责拟订有关机关事务管理的规章制度，指导下级政府公务用车、公务接待、公共机构节约能源资源等工作，主管中央国家机关的机关事务工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府机关事务主管部门指导下级政府有关机关事务工作，主管本级政府的机关事务工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of government affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for drafting rules and regulations on government affairs management, guiding the work of subordinate governments on official vehicles, official receptions, and energy and resource conservation in public institutions, and shall take charge of the government affairs work of central state organs.  
The competent department of government affairs of a local people's government at or above the county level shall guide the relevant government affairs work of subordinate governments, and shall take charge of the government affairs work of the government at the same level.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对本级政府各部门和下级政府的机关事务工作的监督检查，及时纠正违法违纪行为。  
县级以上人民政府发展改革、财政、审计、监察等部门和机关事务主管部门应当根据职责分工，依照有关法律、法规的规定，加强对机关运行经费、资产和服务管理工作的监督检查；接到对违反机关事务管理制度、标准行为的举报，应当及时依法调查处理。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the government affairs work of the departments of the government at the same level and of subordinate governments, and shall timely correct illegal or disciplinary violations.  
The development and reform, finance, audit, supervision and other departments and the competent department of government affairs of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, and in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, strengthen supervision and inspection over the management of operating funds, assets and services of government offices; upon receiving reports of acts violating the government affairs management systems and standards, they shall promptly investigate and handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　机关事务工作应当遵循保障公务、厉行节约、务实高效、公开透明的原则。

**Article 6** Government affairs work shall follow the principles of ensuring official business, practicing strict economy, being pragmatic and efficient, and being open and transparent.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当依照国家有关政府信息公开的规定建立健全机关运行经费公开制度，定期公布公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费等机关运行经费的预算和决算情况。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the provisions of the state on disclosure of government information, establish and improve the system for disclosing government office operating funds, and shall periodically disclose the budget and final accounts of government office operating funds, such as official reception expenses, official vehicle purchase and operation expenses, and expenses for official overseas travel.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府应当推进机关后勤服务、公务用车和公务接待服务等工作的社会化改革，建立健全相关管理制度。

**Article 8** People's governments at all levels shall promote the socialization reform of government office logistics services, official vehicle services and official reception services, and establish and improve relevant management systems.

### 第二章　经费管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民政府及其部门应当加强机关运行经费管理，提高资金使用效益。  
本条例所称机关运行经费，是指为保障机关运行用于购买货物和服务的各项资金。

**Article 9** People's governments at all levels and their departments shall strengthen the management of government office operating funds and increase the efficiency of fund utilization.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "government office operating funds" means the funds used for purchasing goods and services to ensure the operation of government offices.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府机关事务主管部门应当根据机关运行的基本需求，结合机关事务管理实际，制定实物定额和服务标准。  
县级以上人民政府财政部门应当根据实物定额和服务标准，参考有关货物和服务的市场价格，组织制定机关运行经费预算支出定额标准和有关开支标准。

**Article 10** The competent department of government affairs of a people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the basic needs for government office operation and taking into account the actual conditions of government affairs management, formulate quotas for physical goods and service standards.  
The finance department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the quotas for physical goods and service standards, and with reference to the market prices of relevant goods and services, organize the formulation of quota standards for budget expenditures on government office operating funds and related expenditure standards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府财政部门应当根据预算支出定额标准，结合本级政府各部门的工作职责、性质和特点，按照总额控制、从严从紧的原则，采取定员定额方式编制机关运行经费预算。

**Article 11** The finance department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the standard quota for budget expenditures, take into account the functions, nature and characteristics of the work of each department of the government at the same level, and, under the principles of total amount control and strict and tight management, prepare the budget for the operating expenses of the government offices by way of fixed personnel and fixed quotas.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府应当将公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费纳入预算管理，严格控制公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费在机关运行经费预算总额中的规模和比例。  
政府各部门应当根据工作需要和机关运行经费预算制定公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费支出计划，不得挪用其他预算资金用于公务接待、公务用车购置和运行或者因公出国(境)。

**Article 12** A people's government at or above the county level shall include the expenses for official hospitality, the expenses for procurement and operation of official vehicles, and the expenses for official overseas travel (including travel to Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan) into budgetary management, and strictly control the scale and proportion of such expenses in the total budget for the operating expenses of government offices.  
Each department of a government shall, based on the work needs and the budget for the operating expenses of government offices, formulate expenditure plans for the expenses for official hospitality, the expenses for procurement and operation of official vehicles, and the expenses for official overseas travel, and shall not misappropriate other budgetary funds for official hospitality, procurement and operation of official vehicles, or official overseas travel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府机关事务主管部门按照规定，结合本级政府机关事务管理实际情况，统一组织实施本级政府机关的办公用房建设和维修、公务用车配备更新、后勤服务等事务的，经费管理按照国家预算管理规定执行。

**Article 13** Where the department in charge of government office affairs of a people's government at or above the county level, in accordance with the provisions and taking into account the actual circumstances of the management of government office affairs of the government at the same level, uniformly organizes and implements the construction and maintenance of office buildings, the allocation and renewal of official vehicles, and the logistics services of the government offices of the government at the same level, the financial management thereof shall be carried out in accordance with the state provisions on budget management.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　政府各部门应当依照有关政府采购的法律、法规和规定采购机关运行所需货物和服务；需要招标投标的，应当遵守有关招标投标的法律、法规和规定。  
政府各部门应当采购经济适用的货物，不得采购奢侈品、超标准的服务或者购建豪华办公用房。

**Article 14** Each department of a government shall, in accordance with the laws, regulations and provisions on government procurement, procure the goods and services needed for the operation of the government offices; where tenders and bids are required, it shall comply with the laws, regulations and provisions on tenders and bids.  
Each department of a government shall procure economical and appropriate goods, and shall not procure luxury goods, services exceeding the standards, or construct or purchase luxury office buildings.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　政府各部门采购纳入集中采购目录由政府集中采购机构采购的项目，不得违反规定自行采购或者以化整为零等方式规避政府集中采购。  
政府集中采购机构应当建立健全管理制度，缩短采购周期，提高采购效率，降低采购成本，保证采购质量。政府集中采购货物和服务的价格应当低于相同货物和服务的市场平均价格。

**Article 15** Where a department of a government procures items that are included in the centralized procurement catalog and shall be procured by the government centralized procurement institution, it shall not, in violation of the provisions, procure by itself or evade the government centralized procurement by means such as breaking down the items into smaller ones.  
The government centralized procurement institution shall establish and improve the management system, shorten the procurement cycle, improve procurement efficiency, reduce procurement costs, and ensure procurement quality. The prices of goods and services procured through government centralized procurement shall be lower than the average market prices of the same goods and services.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全机关运行经费支出统计报告和绩效考评制度，组织开展机关运行成本统计、分析、评价等工作。

**Article 16** A people's government at or above the county level shall establish and improve a system for statistical reporting of the expenses on the operation of the government offices and a system for performance assessment, and organize and carry out the work of statistics, analysis, evaluation, etc. of the operating costs of the government offices.

### 第三章　资产管理

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府机关事务主管部门按照职责分工，制定和组织实施机关资产管理的具体制度，并接受财政等有关部门的指导和监督。

**Article 17** The department in charge of government office affairs of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to the division of duties, formulate and organize the implementation of specific systems for the management of government office assets, and accept the guidance and supervision of the finance department and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当根据有关机关资产管理的规定、经济社会发展水平、节能环保要求和机关运行的基本需求，结合机关事务管理实际，分类制定机关资产配置标准，确定资产数量、价格、性能和最低使用年限。政府各部门应当根据机关资产配置标准编制本部门的资产配置计划。

**Article 18** A people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions on the management of government office assets, the level of economic and social development, the requirements for energy conservation and environmental protection, and the basic needs for the operation of the government offices, and taking into account the actual circumstances of the management of government office affairs, formulate by category the standards for the allocation of government office assets, and determine the quantity, price, performance and minimum service life of the assets. Each department of a government shall, according to the standards for the allocation of government office assets, prepare the asset allocation plan of its own department.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　政府各部门应当完善机关资产使用管理制度，建立健全资产账卡和使用档案，定期清查盘点，保证资产安全完整，提高使用效益。  
政府各部门的闲置资产应当由本级政府统一调剂使用或者采取公开拍卖等方式处置，处置收益应当上缴国库。

**Article 19** Each department of a government shall improve the system for the use and management of government office assets, establish and maintain asset accounts, cards and use archives, conduct regular inventory checks, ensure the safety and integrity of the assets, and improve the efficiency of use.  
The idle assets of each department of a government shall be uniformly reallocated for use by the government at the same level or disposed of by means such as public auction, and the proceeds from the disposition shall be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府应当对本级政府机关用地实行统一管理。城镇总体规划、详细规划应当统筹考虑政府机关用地布局和空间安排的需要。  
县级以上人民政府机关事务主管部门应当统筹安排机关用地，集约节约利用土地。  
对政府机关新增用地需求，县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当严格审核，并依照有关土地管理的法律、法规和规定办理用地手续。

**Article 20** A people's government at or above the county level shall implement unified management of the land used by the government offices at the same level. The overall urban plan and detailed plan shall take into overall consideration the needs for the layout and spatial arrangement of the land for government offices.  
The department in charge of government office affairs of a people's government at or above the county level shall make overall arrangements for the land for government offices, and use the land intensively and economically.  
With respect to the newly increased demand for land for government offices, the department of land and resources of a people's government at or above the county level shall conduct strict examination and handle the land use procedures in accordance with the laws, regulations and provisions on land management.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全机关办公用房管理制度，对本级政府机关办公用房实行统一调配、统一权属登记；具备条件的，可以对本级政府机关办公用房实行统一建设。  
政府各部门办公用房的建设和维修应当严格执行政府机关办公用房建设、维修标准，符合简朴实用、节能环保、安全保密等要求；办公用房的使用和维护应当严格执行政府机关办公用房物业服务标准。

**Article 21** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the management system for government office buildings, and implement unified allocation and unified ownership registration for government office buildings at the corresponding level; where conditions permit, they may implement unified construction of government office buildings at the corresponding level.  
The construction and maintenance of office buildings of various government departments shall strictly comply with the standards for construction and maintenance of government office buildings, and meet the requirements of simplicity and practicality, energy conservation and environmental protection, safety and confidentiality, etc.; the use and maintenance of office buildings shall strictly comply with the property service standards for government office buildings.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　政府各部门超过核定面积的办公用房，因办公用房新建、调整和机构撤销腾退的办公用房，应当由本级政府及时收回，统一调剂使用。  
各级人民政府及其部门的工作人员退休或者调离的，其办公用房应当由原单位及时收回，调剂使用。

**Article 22** Office buildings of various government departments that exceed the approved area, and office buildings vacated due to new construction, adjustment, or dissolution of institutions, shall be taken back by the government at the corresponding level in a timely manner and uniformly allocated for use.  
Where staff members of people's governments at various levels and their departments retire or are transferred, their office buildings shall be taken back by the original unit in a timely manner and allocated for use.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　政府各部门不得出租、出借办公用房或者改变办公用房使用功能；未经本级人民政府批准，不得租用办公用房。

**Article 23** Various government departments shall not lease or lend office buildings, or change the use function of office buildings; without approval of the people's government at the corresponding level, they shall not rent office buildings.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院机关事务主管部门会同有关部门拟订公务用车配备使用管理办法，定期发布政府公务用车选用车型目录，负责中央国家机关公务用车管理工作。执法执勤类公务用车配备使用管理的具体规定，由国务院财政部门会同有关部门制定。  
县级以上地方人民政府公务用车主管部门负责本级政府公务用车管理工作，指导和监督下级政府公务用车管理工作。

**Article 24** The competent department of government office administration under the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, formulate management measures for the allocation and use of official vehicles, regularly publish a catalogue of selected vehicle models for government official vehicles, and be responsible for the management of official vehicles of central state organs. Specific provisions on the management of allocation and use of official vehicles for law enforcement and duty purposes shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.  
The competent departments for official vehicles of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management of official vehicles of the governments at the corresponding level, and guide and supervise the management of official vehicles of governments at lower levels.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　政府各部门应当严格执行公务用车编制和配备标准，建立健全公务用车配备更新管理制度，不得超编制、超标准配备公务用车或者超标准租用车辆，不得为公务用车增加高档配置或者豪华内饰，不得借用、占用下级单位和其他单位的车辆，不得接受企业事业单位和个人捐赠的车辆。

**Article 25** Various government departments shall strictly implement the establishment and allocation standards for official vehicles, establish and improve the management system for allocation and renewal of official vehicles, and shall not allocate official vehicles beyond the establishment or standards, or rent vehicles beyond the standards, shall not add high-grade configurations or luxurious interiors to official vehicles, shall not borrow or occupy vehicles of lower-level units or other units, and shall not accept vehicles donated by enterprises, public institutions, or individuals.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　政府各部门应当对公务用车实行集中管理、统一调度，并建立健全公务用车使用登记和统计报告制度。  
政府各部门应当对公务用车的油耗和维修保养费用实行单车核算。

**Article 26** Various government departments shall implement centralized management and unified dispatch of official vehicles, and establish and improve the registration and statistical reporting system for the use of official vehicles.  
Various government departments shall implement per-vehicle accounting for fuel consumption and maintenance costs of official vehicles.

### 第四章　服务管理

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府机关事务主管部门应当制定统一的机关后勤服务管理制度，确定机关后勤服务项目和标准，加强对本级政府各部门后勤服务工作的指导和监督，合理配置和节约使用后勤服务资源。  
政府各部门应当建立健全本部门后勤服务管理制度，不得超出规定的项目和标准提供后勤服务。

**Article 27** The competent departments of government office administration of people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate unified management systems for government logistics services, determine the items and standards of government logistics services, strengthen guidance and supervision over the logistics service work of various departments of the governments at the corresponding level, and rationally allocate and economically use logistics service resources.  
Various government departments shall establish and improve their own logistics service management systems, and shall not provide logistics services beyond the prescribed items and standards.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级人民政府应当按照简化礼仪、务实节俭的原则管理和规范公务接待工作。  
国务院机关事务主管部门负责拟订政府机关公务接待的相关制度和中央国家机关公务接待标准。县级以上地方人民政府应当结合本地实际，确定公务接待的范围和标准。政府各部门和公务接待管理机构应当严格执行公务接待制度和标准。  
县级以上地方人民政府公务接待管理机构负责管理本级政府公务接待工作，指导下级政府公务接待工作。

**Article 28** People's governments at various levels shall manage and regulate official reception work in accordance with the principles of simplifying formalities, being practical and thrifty.  
The competent department of government office administration under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating relevant systems for government official reception and the standards for official reception of central state organs. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of local realities, determine the scope and standards of official reception. Various government departments and official reception management institutions shall strictly implement the systems and standards for official reception.  
The official reception management institutions of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for managing the official reception work of the governments at the corresponding level, and guide the official reception work of governments at lower levels.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府及其部门应当加强会议管理，控制会议数量、规模和会期，充分利用机关内部场所和电视电话、网络视频等方式召开会议，节省会议开支。

**Article 29** People's governments at various levels and their departments shall strengthen meeting management, control the number, scale, and duration of meetings, and make full use of internal meeting venues as well as teleconferencing, video conferencing, and other means to hold meetings, so as to save meeting expenses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　政府各部门应当执行有关因公出国(境)的规定，对本部门工作人员因公出国(境)的事由、内容、必要性和日程安排进行审查，控制因公出国(境)团组和人员数量、在国(境)外停留时间，不得安排与本部门业务工作无关的考察和培训。

**Article 30** Various government departments shall implement the provisions concerning going abroad (or leaving the country) on official business, examine the reasons, content, necessity, and itinerary arrangements for their staff members going abroad (or leaving the country) on official business, control the number of groups and personnel going abroad (or leaving the country) on official business and the duration of stay abroad (or outside the country), and shall not arrange inspections or trainings unrelated to the department's business work.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，接到对违反机关事务管理制度、标准行为的举报不及时依法调查处理的，由上级机关责令改正；情节严重的，由任免机关或者监察机关对责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 31** Where, in violation of these Regulations, having received a report on acts violating the management system and standards of government offices, one fails to promptly investigate and handle it according to law, the superior organ shall order rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the appointing and dismissing organ or the supervisory organ shall impose a sanction on the responsible person according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由上级机关责令改正，并由任免机关或者监察机关对责任人员给予警告处分；情节较重的，给予记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分：  
(一)超预算、超标准开支公务接待费、公务用车购置和运行费、因公出国(境)费，或者挪用其他预算资金用于公务接待、公务用车购置和运行、因公出国(境)的；  
(二)采购奢侈品、超标准的服务或者购建豪华办公用房的；  
(三)出租、出借办公用房，改变办公用房使用功能，或者擅自租用办公用房的；  
(四)超编制、超标准配备公务用车或者超标准租用车辆，或者为公务用车增加高档配置、豪华内饰，或者借用、占用下级单位、其他单位车辆，或者接受企业事业单位、个人捐赠车辆的；  
(五)超出规定的项目或者标准提供后勤服务的；  
(六)安排与本部门业务工作无关的出国(境)考察或者培训的。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following circumstances occurs, the superior organ shall order rectification, and the appointing and dismissing organ or the supervisory organ shall impose a disciplinary warning on the responsible person; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, demotion or removal from office shall be imposed:  
(1) spending official reception expenses, expenses for purchase and operation of official vehicles, and expenses for official overseas travel beyond the budget or standard, or misappropriating other budget funds for official reception, purchase and operation of official vehicles, or official overseas travel;  
(2) procuring luxury goods, services beyond the standard, or constructing or purchasing luxurious office premises;  
(3) leasing or lending office premises, changing the use function of office premises, or leasing office premises without authorization;  
(4) allocating official vehicles beyond the authorized number or standard, or renting vehicles beyond the standard, or adding high-end configurations or luxurious interiors to official vehicles, or borrowing or occupying vehicles of subordinate or other units, or accepting donated vehicles from enterprises, public institutions, or individuals;  
(5) providing logistical services beyond the prescribed items or standards;  
(6) arranging overseas inspection or training unrelated to the business work of the department.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　机关事务管理人员在机关事务管理活动中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者贪污受贿的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where a functionary of government offices administration abuses his power, neglects his duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or commits embezzlement or bribery in the administration of government offices activities, a sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　其他国家机关和有关人民团体的机关事务管理活动，参照本条例执行。

**Article 34** The administration of government offices activities of other State organs and relevant people's organizations shall be implemented with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2012年10月1日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2012.
