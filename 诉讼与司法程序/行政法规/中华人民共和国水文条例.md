# 中华人民共和国水文条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> (2007年4月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第496号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强水文管理，规范水文工作，为开发、利用、节约、保护水资源和防灾减灾服务，促进经济社会的可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国水法》和《中华人民共和国防洪法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事水文站网规划与建设，水文监测与预报，水资源调查评价，水文监测资料汇交、保管与使用，水文设施与水文监测环境的保护等活动，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　水文事业是国民经济和社会发展的基础性公益事业。县级以上人民政府应当将水文事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算，保障水文监测工作的正常开展，充分发挥水文工作在政府决策、经济社会发展和社会公众服务中的作用。 县级以上人民政府应当关心和支持少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和艰苦地区水文基础设施的建设和运行。 第四条　国务院水行政主管部门主管全国的水文工作，其直属的水文机构具体负责组织实施管理工作。 国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构(以下简称流域管理机构)，在所管辖范围内按照法律、本条例规定和国务院水行政主管部门规定的权限，组织实施管理有关水文工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的水文工作，其直属的水文机构接受上级业务主管部门的指导，并在当地人民政府的领导下具体负责组织实施管理工作。 第五条　国家鼓励和支持水文科学技术的研究、推广和应用，保护水文科技成果，培养水文科技人才，加强水文国际合作与交流。 第六条　县级以上人民政府对在水文工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第七条　外国组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域内从事水文活动的，应当经国务院水行政主管部门会同有关部门批准，并遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法规；在中华人民共和国与邻国交界的跨界河流上从事水文活动的，应当遵守中华人民共和国与相关国家缔结的有关条约、协定。 第二章　规划与建设 第八条　国务院水行政主管部门负责编制全国水文事业发展规划，在征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准实施。 流域管理机构根据全国水文事业发展规划编制流域水文事业发展规划，报国务院水行政主管部门批准实施。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强水文管理，规范水文工作，为开发、利用、节约、保护水资源和防灾减灾服务，促进经济社会的可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国水法》和《中华人民共和国防洪法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China and the Flood Control Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening hydrological management, standardizing hydrological work, serving the development, utilization, conservation, and protection of water resources as well as disaster prevention and mitigation, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事水文站网规划与建设，水文监测与预报，水资源调查评价，水文监测资料汇交、保管与使用，水文设施与水文监测环境的保护等活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in activities such as planning and construction of hydrological station networks, hydrological monitoring and forecasting, water resources survey and evaluation, compilation, storage and use of hydrological monitoring data, and protection of hydrological facilities and hydrological monitoring environment within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　水文事业是国民经济和社会发展的基础性公益事业。县级以上人民政府应当将水文事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算，保障水文监测工作的正常开展，充分发挥水文工作在政府决策、经济社会发展和社会公众服务中的作用。  
县级以上人民政府应当关心和支持少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和艰苦地区水文基础设施的建设和运行。

**Article 3** Hydrological undertakings are basic public welfare undertakings for national economic and social development. People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate hydrological undertakings into their plans for national economic and social development, include the required funds in their fiscal budgets, ensure the normal operation of hydrological monitoring work, and give full play to the role of hydrological work in government decision-making, economic and social development, and public services.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall pay attention to and support the construction and operation of hydrological infrastructure in minority areas, remote poverty-stricken areas, and areas with harsh conditions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院水行政主管部门主管全国的水文工作，其直属的水文机构具体负责组织实施管理工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构(以下简称流域管理机构)，在所管辖范围内按照法律、本条例规定和国务院水行政主管部门规定的权限，组织实施管理有关水文工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的水文工作，其直属的水文机构接受上级业务主管部门的指导，并在当地人民政府的领导下具体负责组织实施管理工作。

**Article 4** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for hydrological work nationwide, and its directly subordinate hydrological agency shall be specifically responsible for organizing and implementing management work.  
The basin management agencies established by the water administrative department of the State Council in the important rivers and lakes designated by the State (hereinafter referred to as basin management agencies) shall, within their respective jurisdictions, organize and implement the management of relevant hydrological work in accordance with laws, these Regulations, and the authority prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for hydrological work within their respective administrative regions, and their directly subordinate hydrological agencies shall accept the guidance of the superior competent departments and, under the leadership of the local people's government, be specifically responsible for organizing and implementing management work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持水文科学技术的研究、推广和应用，保护水文科技成果，培养水文科技人才，加强水文国际合作与交流。

**Article 5** The State encourages and supports research, promotion, and application of hydrological science and technology, protects hydrological scientific and technological achievements, cultivates hydrological technical personnel, and strengthens international cooperation and exchanges in hydrology.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府对在水文工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall commend and reward, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to hydrological work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域内从事水文活动的，应当经国务院水行政主管部门会同有关部门批准，并遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法规；在中华人民共和国与邻国交界的跨界河流上从事水文活动的，应当遵守中华人民共和国与相关国家缔结的有关条约、协定。

**Article 7** Foreign organizations or individuals engaging in hydrological activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall obtain approval from the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments, and shall comply with the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China; when engaging in hydrological activities on transboundary rivers at the border between the People's Republic of China and neighboring countries, they shall comply with the relevant treaties and agreements concluded between the People's Republic of China and the relevant countries.

### 第二章　规划与建设

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院水行政主管部门负责编制全国水文事业发展规划，在征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准实施。  
流域管理机构根据全国水文事业发展规划编制流域水文事业发展规划，报国务院水行政主管部门批准实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门根据全国水文事业发展规划和流域水文事业发展规划编制本行政区域的水文事业发展规划，报本级人民政府批准实施，并报国务院水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 8** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the national hydrological undertaking development plan, which shall be submitted to the State Council or its authorized department for approval and implementation after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council.  
Basin management agencies shall, based on the national hydrological undertaking development plan, formulate basin hydrological undertaking development plans, which shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval and implementation.  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national hydrological undertaking development plan and basin hydrological undertaking development plans, formulate hydrological undertaking development plans for their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval and implementation, and shall be reported to the water administrative department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　水文事业发展规划是开展水文工作的依据。修改水文事业发展规划，应当按照规划编制程序经原批准机关批准。

**Article 9** The hydrological undertaking development plan is the basis for carrying out hydrological work. Any revision to the hydrological undertaking development plan shall be approved by the original approving authority in accordance with the planning formulation procedures.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　水文事业发展规划主要包括水文事业发展目标、水文站网建设、水文监测和情报预报设施建设、水文信息网络和业务系统建设以及保障措施等内容。

**Article 10** The hydrological undertaking development plan mainly includes contents such as the development objectives of hydrological undertakings, construction of hydrological station networks, construction of hydrological monitoring and information forecasting facilities, construction of hydrological information networks and business systems, and safeguard measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家对水文站网建设实行统一规划。水文站网建设应当坚持流域与区域相结合、区域服从流域，布局合理、防止重复，兼顾当前和长远需要的原则。

**Article 11** The State implements unified planning for the construction of hydrological station networks. The construction of hydrological station networks shall adhere to the principles of combining basins and regions with regions subordinating to basins, rational layout and prevention of duplication, and balancing current and long-term needs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　水文站网的建设应当依据水文事业发展规划，按照国家固定资产投资项目建设程序组织实施。  
为国家水利、水电等基础工程设施提供服务的水文站网的建设和运行管理经费，应当分别纳入工程建设概算和运行管理经费。  
本条例所称水文站网，是指在流域或者区域内，由适当数量的各类水文测站构成的水文监测资料收集系统。

**Article 12** The construction of hydrological station networks shall be carried out in accordance with the hydrological undertaking development plan and in accordance with the procedures for the construction of State fixed-asset investment projects.  
The funds for the construction and operation management of hydrological station networks that provide services for national water conservancy, hydropower and other basic engineering facilities shall be included separately in the project budget estimates and operation management funds.  
"Hydrological station network" as used in these Regulations means a system for collecting hydrological monitoring data composed of an appropriate number of various types of hydrological monitoring stations in a basin or region.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对水文测站实行分类分级管理。  
水文测站分为国家基本水文测站和专用水文测站。国家基本水文测站分为国家重要水文测站和一般水文测站。

**Article 13** The State implements classified and graded management of hydrological monitoring stations.  
Hydrological monitoring stations are divided into national basic hydrological monitoring stations and special-purpose hydrological monitoring stations. National basic hydrological monitoring stations are divided into national important hydrological monitoring stations and ordinary hydrological monitoring stations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家重要水文测站和流域管理机构管理的一般水文测站的设立和调整，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构报国务院水行政主管部门直属水文机构批准。其他一般水文测站的设立和调整，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门批准，报国务院水行政主管部门直属水文机构备案。

**Article 14** The establishment and adjustment of national important hydrological monitoring stations and ordinary hydrological monitoring stations managed by basin management agencies shall be approved by the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the State Council upon submission by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the basin management agency. The establishment and adjustment of other ordinary hydrological monitoring stations shall be approved by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　设立专用水文测站，不得与国家基本水文测站重复；在国家基本水文测站覆盖的区域，确需设立专用水文测站的，应当按照管理权限报流域管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门直属水文机构批准。其中，因交通、航运、环境保护等需要设立专用水文测站的，有关主管部门批准前，应当征求流域管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门直属水文机构的意见。  
撤销专用水文测站，应当报原批准机关批准。

**Article 15** The establishment of special-purpose hydrological monitoring stations shall not duplicate national basic hydrological monitoring stations; if it is truly necessary to establish a special-purpose hydrological monitoring station in an area covered by national basic hydrological monitoring stations, approval shall be obtained from the basin management agency or the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the management authority. Among these, if a special-purpose hydrological monitoring station is to be established due to needs of transportation, navigation, environmental protection, etc., the relevant competent department shall, before approval, seek opinions from the basin management agency or the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The revocation of a special-purpose hydrological monitoring station shall be approved by the original approving authority.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专用水文测站和从事水文活动的其他单位，应当接受水行政主管部门直属水文机构的行业管理。

**Article 16** Special-purpose hydrological monitoring stations and other units engaged in hydrological activities shall accept the industry management of the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门管理的水文测站，对流域水资源管理和防灾减灾有重大作用的，业务上应当同时接受流域管理机构的指导和监督。

**Article 17** Hydrological monitoring stations managed by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have a significant role in basin water resources management and disaster prevention and mitigation shall, in their operations, simultaneously accept the guidance and supervision of the basin management agency.

### 第三章　监测与预报

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事水文监测活动应当遵守国家水文技术标准、规范和规程，保证监测质量。未经批准，不得中止水文监测。  
国家水文技术标准、规范和规程，由国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院标准化行政主管部门制定。

**Article 18** Persons engaged in hydrological monitoring activities shall comply with national hydrological technical standards, norms, and procedures to ensure monitoring quality. Without approval, hydrological monitoring shall not be suspended.  
National hydrological technical standards, norms, and procedures shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the standardization administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　水文监测所使用的专用技术装备应当符合国务院水行政主管部门规定的技术要求。  
水文监测所使用的计量器具应当依法经检定合格。水文监测所使用的计量器具的检定规程，由国务院水行政主管部门制定，报国务院计量行政主管部门备案。

**Article 19** The special technical equipment used for hydrological monitoring shall meet the technical requirements prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The measuring instruments used for hydrological monitoring shall be verified as qualified according to law. The verification procedures for measuring instruments used for hydrological monitoring shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council and reported to the metrological administrative department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　水文机构应当加强水资源的动态监测工作，发现被监测水体的水量、水质等情况发生变化可能危及用水安全的，应当加强跟踪监测和调查，及时将监测、调查情况和处理建议报所在地人民政府及其水行政主管部门；发现水质变化，可能发生突发性水体污染事件的，应当及时将监测、调查情况报所在地人民政府水行政主管部门和环境保护行政主管部门。  
有关单位和个人对水资源动态监测工作应当予以配合。

**Article 20** Hydrological agencies shall strengthen the dynamic monitoring of water resources. If changes in the volume or quality of the monitored water body are found that may endanger water safety, they shall strengthen follow-up monitoring and investigation, and promptly report the monitoring and investigation situations and handling suggestions to the local people's government and its water administrative department; if water quality changes are found that may lead to sudden water pollution incidents, they shall promptly report the monitoring and investigation situations to the local people's government's water administrative department and environmental protection administrative department.  
Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the dynamic monitoring of water resources.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承担水文情报预报任务的水文测站，应当及时、准确地向县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构和水行政主管部门报告有关水文情报预报。

**Article 21** Hydrological monitoring stations undertaking hydrological information forecasting tasks shall promptly and accurately report relevant hydrological information forecasts to the flood control and drought relief headquarters and the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　水文情报预报由县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构、水行政主管部门或者水文机构按照规定权限向社会统一发布。禁止任何其他单位和个人向社会发布水文情报预报。  
广播、电视、报纸和网络等新闻媒体，应当按照国家有关规定和防汛抗旱要求，及时播发、刊登水文情报预报，并标明发布机构和发布时间。

**Article 22** Hydrological information forecasts shall be uniformly released to the public by the flood control and drought relief headquarters, the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, or the hydrological agencies in accordance with the prescribed authority. No other unit or individual may release hydrological information forecasts to the public.  
News media such as radio, television, newspapers, and the Internet shall promptly broadcast or publish hydrological information forecasts in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the requirements of flood control and drought relief, and shall indicate the issuing authority and the time of issuance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　信息产业部门应当根据水文工作的需要，按照国家有关规定提供通信保障。

**Article 23** The information industry department shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide communication support according to the needs of hydrological work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当根据经济社会的发展要求，会同有关部门组织相关单位开展水资源调查评价工作。  
从事水文、水资源调查评价的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有法人资格和固定的工作场所；  
(二)具有与所从事水文活动相适应的专业技术人员；  
(三)具有与所从事水文活动相适应的专业技术装备；  
(四)具有健全的管理制度；  
(五)符合国务院水行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 24** The water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the requirements of economic and social development, organize relevant units to carry out water resources survey and evaluation in conjunction with the relevant departments.  
Units engaged in hydrological and water resources survey and evaluation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the status of a legal person and a fixed workplace;  
(2) having professional technical personnel commensurate with the hydrological activities engaged in;  
(3) having professional technical equipment commensurate with the hydrological activities engaged in;  
(4) having a sound management system;  
(5) meeting other conditions prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.

### 第四章　资料的汇交保管与使用

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家对水文监测资料实行统一汇交制度。从事地表水和地下水资源、水量、水质监测的单位以及其他从事水文监测的单位，应当按照资料管理权限向有关水文机构汇交监测资料。  
重要地下水源地、超采区的地下水资源监测资料和重要引(退)水口、在江河和湖泊设置的排污口、重要断面的监测资料，由从事水文监测的单位向流域管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门直属水文机构汇交。  
取用水工程的取(退)水、蓄(泄)水资料，由取用水工程管理单位向工程所在地水文机构汇交。

**Article 25** The State implements a unified compilation system for hydrological monitoring data. Units engaged in monitoring surface water and groundwater resources, water volume, and water quality, as well as other units engaged in hydrological monitoring, shall compile monitoring data to the relevant hydrological agencies in accordance with the data management authority.  
Monitoring data on groundwater resources in important groundwater sources and overexploited areas, and monitoring data on important water intake (discharge) outlets, sewage outlets installed in rivers and lakes, and important cross-sections, shall be compiled by units engaged in hydrological monitoring to the basin management agency or the directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Data on water intake (discharge) and water storage (release) of water intake and use projects shall be compiled by the management unit of the water intake and use project to the hydrological agency at the project location.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立水文监测资料共享制度。水文机构应当妥善存储和保管水文监测资料，根据国民经济建设和社会发展需要对水文监测资料进行加工整理形成水文监测成果，予以刊印。国务院水行政主管部门直属的水文机构应当建立国家水文数据库。  
基本水文监测资料应当依法公开，水文监测资料属于国家秘密的，对其密级的确定、变更、解密以及对资料的使用、管理，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 26** The State establishes a sharing system for hydrological monitoring data. Hydrological agencies shall properly store and keep hydrological monitoring data, process and organize the data to form hydrological monitoring results for publication according to the needs of national economic construction and social development. The directly subordinate hydrological agency of the water administrative department of the State Council shall establish a national hydrological database.  
Basic hydrological monitoring data shall be made public according to law; if hydrological monitoring data constitute State secrets, the determination, change, declassification of their classification level, and the use and management of the data shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　编制重要规划、进行重点项目建设和水资源管理等使用的水文监测资料应当完整、可靠、一致。

**Article 27** Hydrological monitoring data used for preparing important plans, constructing key projects, and managing water resources shall be complete, reliable, and consistent.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家机关决策和防灾减灾、国防建设、公共安全、环境保护等公益事业需要使用水文监测资料和成果的，应当无偿提供。  
除前款规定的情形外，需要使用水文监测资料和成果的，按照国家有关规定收取费用，并实行收支两条线管理。  
因经营性活动需要提供水文专项咨询服务的，当事人双方应当签订有偿服务合同，明确双方的权利和义务。

**Article 28** Where State organs need to use hydrological monitoring data and results for decision-making and for public welfare undertakings such as disaster prevention and mitigation, national defense construction, public security, and environmental protection, such data and results shall be provided free of charge.  
Except for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, where it is necessary to use hydrological monitoring data and results, fees shall be charged in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the system of separate revenue and expenditure management shall be implemented.  
Where it is necessary to provide specialized hydrological consulting services for business activities, the parties shall enter into a paid service contract specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.

### 第五章　设施与监测环境保护

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家依法保护水文监测设施。任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁坏、擅自移动或者擅自使用水文监测设施，不得干扰水文监测。  
国家基本水文测站因不可抗力遭受破坏的，所在地人民政府和有关水行政主管部门应当采取措施，组织力量修复，确保其正常运行。

**Article 29** The State protects hydrological monitoring facilities according to law. No unit or individual may occupy, damage, move without authorization, or use without authorization hydrological monitoring facilities, or interfere with hydrological monitoring.  
If a national basic hydrological monitoring station is damaged due to force majeure, the local people's government and the relevant water administrative department shall take measures to organize its repair and ensure its normal operation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未经批准，任何单位和个人不得迁移国家基本水文测站；因重大工程建设确需迁移的，建设单位应当在建设项目立项前，报请对该站有管理权限的水行政主管部门批准，所需费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 30** Without approval, no unit or individual may relocate a national basic hydrological monitoring station; if relocation is truly necessary due to major engineering construction, the construction unit shall, before the project is established, obtain approval from the water administrative department with jurisdiction over the station, and the costs shall be borne by the construction unit.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家依法保护水文监测环境。县级人民政府应当按照国务院水行政主管部门确定的标准划定水文监测环境保护范围，并在保护范围边界设立地面标志。  
任何单位和个人都有保护水文监测环境的义务。

**Article 31** The State protects the hydrological monitoring environment according to law. People's governments at the county level shall, in accordance with the standards determined by the water administrative department of the State Council, delineate the protection scope for the hydrological monitoring environment, and set up ground markers at the boundaries of the protection scope.  
All units and individuals have the obligation to protect the hydrological monitoring environment.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　禁止在水文监测环境保护范围内从事下列活动：  
(一)种植高秆作物、堆放物料、修建建筑物、停靠船只；  
(二)取土、挖砂、采石、淘金、爆破和倾倒废弃物；  
(三)在监测断面取水、排污或者在过河设备、气象观测场、监测断面的上空架设线路；  
(四)其他对水文监测有影响的活动。

**Article 32** The following activities are prohibited within the protection scope of the hydrological monitoring environment:  
(1) planting tall-stalk crops, piling up materials, constructing buildings, or berthing ships;  
(2) soil extraction, sand excavation, stone quarrying, gold panning, blasting, or dumping waste;  
(3) taking water or discharging pollutants at monitoring cross-sections, or erecting lines above river-crossing equipment, meteorological observation fields, or monitoring cross-sections;  
(4) other activities that may affect hydrological monitoring.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在国家基本水文测站上下游建设影响水文监测的工程，建设单位应当采取相应措施，在征得对该站有管理权限的水行政主管部门同意后方可建设。因工程建设致使水文测站改建的，所需费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 33** Where a project that may affect hydrological monitoring is to be constructed upstream or downstream of a national basic hydrological monitoring station, the construction unit shall take corresponding measures and may construct only after obtaining consent from the water administrative department with jurisdiction over the station. If the project necessitates the reconstruction of the hydrological monitoring station, the costs shall be borne by the construction unit.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在通航河道中或者桥上进行水文监测作业时，应当依法设置警示标志。

**Article 34** When carrying out hydrological monitoring operations in navigable rivers or on bridges, warning signs shall be set up according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　水文机构依法取得的无线电频率使用权和通信线路使用权受国家保护。任何单位和个人不得挤占、干扰水文机构使用的无线电频率，不得破坏水文机构使用的通信线路。

**Article 35** The rights to use radio frequencies and communication lines legally obtained by hydrological agencies are protected by the State. No unit or individual may crowd or interfere with the radio frequencies or damage the communication lines used by hydrological agencies.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)错报水文监测信息造成严重经济损失的；  
(二)汛期漏报、迟报水文监测信息的；  
(三)擅自发布水文情报预报的；  
(四)丢失、毁坏、伪造水文监测资料的；  
(五)擅自转让、转借水文监测资料的；  
(六)不依法履行职责的其他行为。

**Article 36** Where anyone violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) reporting wrong hydrological monitoring information causing serious economic losses;  
(2) omitting or delaying the reporting of hydrological monitoring information during flood season;  
(3) releasing hydrological information forecasts without authorization;  
(4) losing, destroying, or forging hydrological monitoring data;  
(5) transferring or lending hydrological monitoring data without authorization;  
(6) other acts of failing to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　未经批准擅自设立水文测站或者未经同意擅自在国家基本水文测站上下游建设影响水文监测的工程的，责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施，补办有关手续；无法采取补救措施、逾期不补办或者补办未被批准的，责令限期拆除违法建筑物；逾期不拆除的，强行拆除，所需费用由违法单位或者个人承担。

**Article 37** Where a hydrological monitoring station is established without approval, or a project affecting hydrological monitoring is constructed upstream or downstream of a national basic hydrological monitoring station without consent, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, take remedial measures within a time limit, and go through the relevant procedures; if remedial measures cannot be taken, or if the procedures are not completed within the time limit or the application for completion is not approved, the violator shall be ordered to dismantle the illegal structures within a time limit; if the structures are not dismantled within the time limit, they shall be forcibly dismantled, and the costs shall be borne by the illegal unit or individual.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　不符合[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)规定的条件从事水文活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 38** Where anyone engages in hydrological activities without meeting the conditions specified in Article 24 of these Regulations, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，使用不符合规定的水文专用技术装备和水文计量器具的，责令限期改正。

**Article 39** Where anyone violates these Regulations by using hydrological special technical equipment or hydrological measuring instruments that do not conform to the provisions, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，责令停止违法行为，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款：  
(一)拒不汇交水文监测资料的；  
(二)非法向社会传播水文情报预报，造成严重经济损失和不良影响的。

**Article 40** Where anyone violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) refusing to compile hydrological monitoring data;  
(2) illegally disseminating hydrological information forecasts to the public, causing serious economic losses and adverse effects.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，侵占、毁坏水文监测设施或者未经批准擅自移动、擅自使用水文监测设施的，责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以处5万元以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where anyone violates these Regulations by occupying, damaging, or moving or using hydrological monitoring facilities without authorization, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, restore the original state or take other remedial measures within a time limit, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security management, a public security penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反[本条例规定，从事本条例第三十二条](#art-32)所列活动的，责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以处1万元以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where anyone violates these Regulations by engaging in any of the activities listed in Article 32 of these Regulations, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, restore the original state or take other remedial measures within a time limit, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security management, a public security penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权决定。

**Article 43** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level or the basin management agencies according to their functions and powers.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例中下列用语的含义是：  
水文监测，是指通过水文站网对江河、湖泊、渠道、水库的水位、流量、水质、水温、泥沙、冰情、水下地形和地下水资源，以及降水量、蒸发量、墒情、风暴潮等实施监测，并进行分析和计算的活动。  
水文测站，是指为收集水文监测资料在江河、湖泊、渠道、水库和流域内设立的各种水文观测场所的总称。  
国家基本水文测站，是指为公益目的统一规划设立的对江河、湖泊、渠道、水库和流域基本水文要素进行长期连续观测的水文测站。  
国家重要水文测站，是指对防灾减灾或者对流域和区域水资源管理等有重要作用的基本水文测站。  
专用水文测站，是指为特定目的设立的水文测站。  
基本水文监测资料，是指由国家基本水文测站监测并经过整编后的资料。  
水文情报预报，是指对江河、湖泊、渠道、水库和其他水体的水文要素实时情况的报告和未来情况的预告。  
水文监测设施，是指水文站房、水文缆道、测船、测船码头、监测场地、监测井、监测标志、专用道路、仪器设备、水文通信设施以及附属设施等。  
水文监测环境，是指为确保监测到准确水文信息所必需的区域构成的立体空间。

**Article 44** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings assigned to them:  
"Hydrological monitoring" means the activities of monitoring, analyzing, and calculating water level, flow, water quality, water temperature, sediment, ice conditions, underwater topography, and groundwater resources of rivers, lakes, channels, and reservoirs, as well as precipitation, evaporation, soil moisture, storm surge, etc., through the hydrological station network.  
"Hydrological monitoring station" means the general term for various hydrological observation sites established in rivers, lakes, channels, reservoirs, and basins for collecting hydrological monitoring data.  
"National basic hydrological monitoring station" means a hydrological monitoring station established under unified planning for public welfare purposes to conduct long-term continuous observation of basic hydrological elements in rivers, lakes, channels, reservoirs, and basins.  
"National important hydrological monitoring station" means a basic hydrological monitoring station that plays an important role in disaster prevention and mitigation or in basin and regional water resources management.  
"Special-purpose hydrological monitoring station" means a hydrological monitoring station established for a specific purpose.  
"Basic hydrological monitoring data" means the data monitored by national basic hydrological monitoring stations and compiled after processing.  
"Hydrological information forecast" means the report on real-time conditions and the forecast on future conditions of hydrological elements of rivers, lakes, channels, reservoirs, and other water bodies.  
"Hydrological monitoring facilities" means hydrological station buildings, hydrological cableways, survey boats, survey boat docks, monitoring sites, monitoring wells, monitoring markers, special roads, instruments and equipment, hydrological communication facilities, and auxiliary facilities.  
"Hydrological monitoring environment" means the three-dimensional space composed of the area necessary to ensure the acquisition of accurate hydrological information.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中国人民解放军的水文工作，按照中央军事委员会的规定执行。

**Article 45** The hydrological work of the People's Liberation Army shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自2007年6月1日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2007.
