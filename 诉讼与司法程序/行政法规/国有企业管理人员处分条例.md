### 正文

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国有企业管理人员处分工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持党管干部原则，加强国有企业管理人员队伍建设，推动国有企业高质量发展。

**Article 3** The disciplinary sanction work for managers of state-owned enterprises shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the principle of the Party managing cadres, strengthen the construction of the team of managers of state-owned enterprises, and promote the high-quality development of state-owned enterprises.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　任免机关、单位加强对国有企业管理人员的教育、管理、监督。给予国有企业管理人员处分，应当坚持公正公平，集体讨论决定；坚持宽严相济，惩戒与教育相结合；坚持法治原则，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，依法保障国有企业管理人员以及相关人员的合法权益。

**Article 4** The appointing and dismissing authorities and units shall strengthen the education, management, and supervision of managers of state-owned enterprises. When imposing disciplinary sanctions on managers of state-owned enterprises, they shall adhere to the principles of impartiality and fairness, making decisions through collective discussion; adhere to the combination of leniency and severity, integrating punishment with education; adhere to the principle of the rule of law, based on facts and taking law as the criterion, and protect the lawful rights and interests of managers of state-owned enterprises and relevant personnel according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　履行出资人职责的机构或者有干部管理权限的部门依照法律、法规和国家有关规定，指导国有企业整合优化监督资源，推动出资人监督与纪检监察监督、巡视监督、审计监督、财会监督、社会监督等相衔接，健全协同高效的监督机制，建立互相配合、互相制约的内部监督管理制度，增强对国有企业及其管理人员监督的系统性、针对性、有效性。

**Article 5** The institution performing the duties of an investor or the department with the authority over cadre management shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and relevant state provisions, guide state-owned enterprises in integrating and optimizing supervision resources, promote the convergence of investor supervision with disciplinary inspection and supervision, inspection supervision, audit supervision, financial accounting supervision, and social supervision, establish a sound and efficient supervision mechanism, establish an internal supervision and management system featuring mutual coordination and mutual restraint, and enhance the systemic, targeted, and effective supervision of state-owned enterprises and their managers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　给予国有企业管理人员处分，应当事实清楚、证据确凿、定性准确、处理恰当、程序合法、手续完备，与其违法行为的性质、情节、危害程度相适应。

**Article 6** When imposing disciplinary sanctions on managers of state-owned enterprises, it shall be ensured that the facts are clear, the evidence is conclusive, the characterization is accurate, the handling is appropriate, the procedures are lawful, the formalities are complete, and the sanctions are commensurate with the nature, circumstances, and degree of harm of their illegal acts.

### 第二章　处分的种类和适用

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　处分的种类为：  
（一）警告；  
（二）记过；  
（三）记大过；  
（四）降级；  
（五）撤职；  
（六）开除。

**Article 7** The types of disciplinary sanctions are:  
(1) warning;  
(2) demerit;  
(3) serious demerit;  
(4) demotion;  
(5) removal from office;  
(6) dismissal.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　处分的期间为：  
（一）警告，6个月；  
（二）记过，12个月；  
（三）记大过，18个月；  
（四）降级、撤职，24个月。  
处分决定自作出之日起生效，处分期自处分决定生效之日起计算。

**Article 8** The duration of disciplinary sanctions is:  
(1) warning, 6 months;  
(2) demerit, 12 months;  
(3) serious demerit, 18 months;  
(4) demotion and removal from office, 24 months.  
The sanction decision shall take effect from the date it is made, and the sanction period shall be calculated from the date the sanction decision takes effect.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国有企业管理人员同时有两个以上需要给予处分的违法行为的，应当分别确定其处分。应当给予的处分种类不同的，执行其中最重的处分；应当给予撤职以下多个相同种类处分的，可以在一个处分期以上、多个处分期之和以下确定处分期，但是最长不得超过48个月。

**Article 9** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise has two or more illegal acts for which sanctions need to be imposed, the sanctions for each shall be determined separately. If the types of sanctions to be imposed are different, the most severe among them shall be executed; if multiple sanctions of the same type below removal from office are to be imposed, the sanction period may be determined within a range of not less than one sanction period and not more than the sum of multiple sanction periods, but the maximum period shall not exceed 48 months.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国有企业实施违法行为或者国有企业管理人员集体作出的决定违法，应当追究法律责任的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员中的国有企业管理人员给予处分。  
国有企业管理人员2人以上共同违法，需要给予处分的，按照各自应当承担的责任，分别给予相应的处分。

**Article 10** Where a state-owned enterprise commits an illegal act or a collective decision made by managers of a state-owned enterprise is illegal, and legal liability shall be pursued, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed on the managers of the state-owned enterprise among the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who bear responsibility.  
Where two or more managers of a state-owned enterprise jointly commit an illegal act and sanctions need to be imposed, corresponding sanctions shall be imposed separately according to the respective responsibilities they should bear.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国有企业管理人员有下列情形之一的，可以从轻或者减轻给予处分：  
（一）主动交代本人应当受到处分的违法行为；  
（二）配合调查，如实说明本人违法事实；  
（三）检举他人违法行为，经查证属实；  
（四）主动采取措施，有效避免、挽回损失或者消除不良影响；  
（五）在共同违法行为中起次要或者辅助作用；  
（六）主动上交或者退赔违法所得；  
（七）属于推进国有企业改革中因缺乏经验、先行先试出现的失误错误；  
（八）法律、法规规定的其他从轻或者减轻情节。  
从轻给予处分，是指在本条例规定的违法行为应当受到的处分幅度以内，给予较轻的处分。  
减轻给予处分，是指在本条例规定的违法行为应当受到的处分幅度以外，减轻一档给予处分。

**Article 11** Where managers of state-owned enterprises fall under any of the following circumstances, a lighter or mitigated sanction may be imposed:  
(1) voluntarily confessing their own illegal acts for which they should be sanctioned;  
(2) cooperating with the investigation and truthfully explaining the facts of their own illegal acts;  
(3) reporting others' illegal acts, which are verified to be true;  
(4) taking the initiative to take measures to effectively avoid, recover losses, or eliminate adverse effects;  
(5) playing a secondary or auxiliary role in a joint illegal act;  
(6) voluntarily surrendering or refunding illegal gains;  
(7) errors made due to lack of experience or pilot exploration in promoting the reform of state-owned enterprises;  
(8) other circumstances of lighter or mitigated sanction as stipulated by laws and regulations.  
Lighter sanction refers to imposing a relatively lighter sanction within the range of sanctions that should be imposed for the illegal act stipulated in these Regulations.  
Mitigated sanction refers to imposing a sanction one grade lower outside the range of sanctions that should be imposed for the illegal act stipulated in these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国有企业管理人员违法行为情节轻微，且具有[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第一款规定情形之一的，可以对其进行谈话提醒、批评教育、责令检查或者予以诫勉，免予或者不予处分。  
国有企业管理人员因不明真相被裹挟或者被胁迫参与违法活动，经批评教育后确有悔改表现的，可以减轻、免予或者不予处分。

**Article 12** Where the illegal act of a manager of a state-owned enterprise is minor and falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Regulations, the enterprise may conduct conversation reminders, criticism and education, order a check, or give an admonition, and may waive or not impose a sanction.  
Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise, being unaware of the truth, is involved or coerced into participating in an illegal act, and after criticism and education shows genuine repentance, the sanction may be mitigated, waived, or not imposed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国有企业管理人员有下列情形之一的，应当从重给予处分：  
（一）在处分期内再次故意违法，应当受到处分；  
（二）阻止他人检举、提供证据；  
（三）串供或者伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据；  
（四）包庇同案人员；  
（五）胁迫、唆使他人实施违法行为；  
（六）拒不上交或者退赔违法所得；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他从重情节。  
从重给予处分，是指在本条例规定的违法行为应当受到的处分幅度以内，给予较重的处分。

**Article 13** Where managers of state-owned enterprises fall under any of the following circumstances, a heavier sanction shall be imposed:  
(1) committing intentional illegal acts again during the sanction period and should be sanctioned;  
(2) preventing others from reporting or providing evidence;  
(3) colluding to provide consistent statements or falsifying, concealing, or destroying evidence;  
(4) covering up accomplices;  
(5) coercing or instigating others to commit illegal acts;  
(6) refusing to surrender or refund illegal gains;  
(7) other circumstances of heavier sanction as stipulated by laws and regulations.  
Heavier sanction refers to imposing a relatively heavier sanction within the range of sanctions that should be imposed for the illegal act stipulated in these Regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国有企业管理人员在处分期内，不得晋升职务、岗位等级和职称；其中，被记过、记大过、降级、撤职的，不得晋升薪酬待遇等级。被撤职的，降低职务或者岗位等级，同时降低薪酬待遇。被开除的，用人单位依法解除劳动合同。

**Article 14** During the sanction period, a manager of a state-owned enterprise shall not be promoted in position, rank, or professional title; where the sanction is demerit, serious demerit, demotion, or removal from office, they shall not be promoted in salary and remuneration grade. Where removed from office, the position or rank shall be lowered, and the salary and remuneration shall be reduced correspondingly. Where dismissed, the employing unit shall terminate the labor contract according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国有企业管理人员违法取得的财物和用于违法行为的本人财物，除依法应当由有关机关没收、追缴或者责令退赔的外，应当退还原所有人或者原持有人。  
国有企业管理人员因违法行为获得的职务、职级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、待遇、资格、学历、学位、荣誉、奖励等其他利益，任免机关、单位应当予以纠正或者建议有关机关、单位、组织按规定予以纠正。

**Article 15** Property illegally obtained by a manager of a state-owned enterprise and the personal property used for the illegal act shall, except for those that should be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be refunded by the relevant authorities according to law, be returned to the original owner or holder.  
For other benefits such as positions, ranks, levels, posts, employee grades, professional titles, benefits, qualifications, academic degrees, honorific titles, and rewards obtained by a manager of a state-owned enterprise due to illegal acts, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall correct them or suggest that the relevant authority, unit, or organization correct them according to regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　已经退休的国有企业管理人员退休前或者退休后有违法行为应当受到处分的，不再作出处分决定，但是可以对其立案调查；依法应当给予降级、撤职、开除处分的，应当按照规定相应调整其享受的待遇，对其违法取得的财物和用于违法行为的本人财物依照[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定处理。

**Article 16** Where a retired manager of a state-owned enterprise commits illegal acts before or after retirement for which sanctions should be imposed, no sanction decision shall be made, but an investigation may be initiated; where demotion, removal from office, or dismissal should be imposed according to law, the benefits they enjoy shall be adjusted correspondingly according to regulations, and the property illegally obtained and the personal property used for the illegal act shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 15 of these Regulations.

### 第三章　违法行为及其适用的处分

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第二十八条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-28)的规定，予以记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）散布有损坚持和完善社会主义基本经济制度的言论；  
（二）拒不执行或者变相不执行国有企业改革发展和党的建设有关决策部署；  
（三）在对外经济合作、对外援助、对外交流等工作中损害国家安全和国家利益。  
公开发表反对宪法确立的国家指导思想，反对中国共产党领导，反对社会主义制度，反对改革开放的文章、演说、宣言、声明等的，予以开除。

**Article 17** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, they shall, in accordance with Article 28 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a demerit or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) disseminating remarks that harm adhering to and improving the basic socialist economic system;  
(2) refusing to implement or covertly refusing to implement relevant decisions and deployments of the reform and development of state-owned enterprises and Party building;  
(3) harming national security and national interests in work such as foreign economic cooperation, foreign aid, and foreign exchanges.  
Where a person publicly publishes articles, speeches, declarations, or statements opposing the state guiding ideology established by the Constitution, opposing the leadership of the Communist Party of China, opposing the socialist system, or opposing reform and opening up, they shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-30)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职：  
（一）违反规定的决策程序、职责权限决定国有企业重大决策事项、重要人事任免事项、重大项目安排事项、大额度资金运作事项；  
（二）故意规避、干涉、破坏集体决策，个人或者少数人决定国有企业重大决策事项、重要人事任免事项、重大项目安排事项、大额度资金运作事项；  
（三）拒不执行或者擅自改变国有企业党委（组）会、股东（大）会、董事会、职工代表大会等集体依法作出的重大决定；  
（四）拒不执行或者变相不执行、拖延执行履行出资人职责的机构、行业管理部门等有关部门依法作出的决定。

**Article 18** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, they shall, in accordance with Article 30 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are serious, a demotion or removal from office:  
(1) deciding on major decision-making matters, important personnel appointment and removal matters, major project arrangement matters, or large-sum fund operation matters of the state-owned enterprise in violation of the prescribed decision-making procedures and functional authority;  
(2) intentionally evading, interfering with, or undermining collective decision-making, or individually or by a few persons deciding on major decision-making matters, important personnel appointment and removal matters, major project arrangement matters, or large-sum fund operation matters of the state-owned enterprise;  
(3) refusing to implement or arbitrarily changing major decisions made collectively by the Party committee (group), shareholders' meeting (general meeting), board of directors, workers' congress, etc. of the state-owned enterprise according to law;  
(4) refusing to implement, covertly refusing to implement, or delaying the implementation of decisions made by the institution performing the duties of an investor, the industry administrative department, or other relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十三条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-33)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）利用职务上的便利，侵吞、窃取、骗取或者以其他手段非法占有、挪用本企业以及关联企业的财物、客户资产等；  
（二）利用职务上的便利，索取他人财物或者非法收受他人财物，为他人谋取利益；  
（三）为谋取不正当利益，向国家机关、国家出资企业、事业单位、人民团体，或者向国家工作人员、企业或者其他单位的工作人员，外国公职人员、国际公共组织官员行贿；  
（四）利用职权或者职务上的影响，违反规定在企业关系国有资产出资人权益的重大事项以及工程建设、资产处置、出版发行、招标投标等活动中为本人或者他人谋取私利；  
（五）纵容、默许特定关系人利用本人职权或者职务上的影响，在企业关系国有资产出资人权益的重大事项以及企业经营管理活动中谋取私利；  
（六）违反规定，以单位名义将国有资产集体私分给个人。  
拒不纠正特定关系人违反规定任职、兼职或者从事经营活动，且不服从职务调整的，予以撤职。

**Article 19** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, they shall, in accordance with Article 33 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) taking advantage of their position to misappropriate, steal, defraud, or otherwise unlawfully occupy or misappropriate the property, client assets, etc. of the enterprise or its affiliated enterprises;  
(2) taking advantage of their position to demand property from others or illegally accept property from others, seeking benefits for others;  
(3) to seek improper benefits, bribing state organs, state-invested enterprises, public institutions, people's organizations, or state functionaries, staff of enterprises or other units, foreign public officials, or officials of international public organizations;  
(4) taking advantage of their position or influence to seek personal gains for themselves or others in major matters concerning the rights and interests of state-owned capital investors, as well as in activities such as project construction, asset disposal, publication and distribution, bidding and tendering, in violation of regulations;  
(5) indulging or acquiescing to specific persons taking advantage of their position or influence to seek personal gains in major matters concerning the rights and interests of state-owned capital investors or in the operation and management activities of the enterprise;  
(6) in violation of regulations, using the name of the enterprise to collectively distribute state-owned assets to individuals.  
Where a person refuses to correct a specific person's violation of regulations in holding office, taking part-time positions, or engaging in business activities, and does not comply with position adjustments, they shall be removed from office.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-35)的规定，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职：  
（一）超提工资总额或者超发工资，或者在工资总额之外以津贴、补贴、奖金等其他形式设定和发放工资性收入；  
（二）未实行工资总额预算管理，或者未按规定履行工资总额备案或者核准程序；  
（三）违反规定，自定薪酬、奖励、津贴、补贴和其他福利性货币收入；  
（四）在培训活动、办公用房、公务用车、业务招待、差旅费用等方面超过规定的标准、范围；  
（五）公款旅游或者以学习培训、考察调研、职工疗养等名义变相公款旅游。

**Article 20** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, in accordance with Article 35 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, a demotion or removal from office:  
(1) exceeding the total payroll or overpaying wages, or setting and distributing wage income in other forms such as allowances, subsidies, and bonuses outside the total payroll;  
(2) failing to implement total payroll budget management, or failing to go through the filing or approval procedures for total payroll according to regulations;  
(3) in violation of regulations, self-determining salary, rewards, allowances, subsidies, and other monetary welfare income;  
(4) exceeding the prescribed standards and scope in training activities, office space, official vehicles, business entertainment, travel expenses, etc.;  
(5) taking official trips for tourism or covertly taking official trips for tourism under the guise of learning and training, investigation and research, staff recuperation, etc.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十六条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-36)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）违反规定，个人经商办企业、拥有非上市公司（企业）股份或者证券、从事有偿中介活动、在国（境）外注册公司或者进行投资入股等营利性活动；  
（二）利用职务上的便利，为他人经营与所任职企业同类经营的企业；  
（三）违反规定，未经批准在本企业所出资企业或者其他企业、事业单位、社会组织、中介机构、国际组织等兼任职务；  
（四）经批准兼职，但是违反规定领取薪酬或者获取其他收入；  
（五）利用企业内幕信息或者其他未公开的信息、商业秘密、无形资产等谋取私利。

**Article 21** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, they shall, in accordance with Article 36 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) in violation of regulations, engaging in profit-making activities such as operating a business, holding shares or securities in non-listed companies (enterprises), engaging in paid intermediary activities, registering a company abroad or making investments and shareholdings;  
(2) taking advantage of their position to operate a business of the same type as the enterprise they work for on behalf of others;  
(3) in violation of regulations, holding concurrent positions in the enterprise's invested enterprises or other enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, intermediary agencies, international organizations, etc. without approval;  
(4) holding concurrent positions with approval but receiving salary or obtaining other income in violation of regulations;  
(5) using inside information or other undisclosed information, trade secrets, intangible assets, etc. of the enterprise to seek personal gains.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国有企业管理人员在履行提供社会公共服务职责过程中，侵犯服务对象合法权益或者社会公共利益，被监管机构查实并提出处分建议的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十八条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-38)的规定，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节特别严重的，予以开除。

**Article 22** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise, in the course of performing public service duties, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the service recipients or the public interest, and the regulatory authority confirms and proposes a sanction recommendation, they shall, in accordance with Article 38 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit if the circumstances are relatively serious; a demotion or removal from office if the circumstances are serious; and dismissal if the circumstances are particularly serious.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一，造成国有资产损失或者其他严重不良后果的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-39)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）截留、占用、挪用或者拖欠应当上缴国库的预算收入；  
（二）违反规定，不履行或者不正确履行经营投资职责；  
（三）违反规定，进行关联交易，开展融资性贸易、虚假交易、虚假合资、挂靠经营等活动；  
（四）在国家规定期限内不办理或者不如实办理企业国有资产产权登记，或者伪造、涂改、出租、出借、出售国有资产产权登记证（表）；  
（五）拒不提供有关信息资料或者编制虚假数据信息，致使国有企业绩效评价结果失真；  
（六）掩饰企业真实状况，不如实向会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等中介服务机构提供有关情况和资料，或者与会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等中介服务机构串通作假。

**Article 23** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, causing losses to state-owned assets or other serious adverse consequences, they shall, in accordance with Article 39 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) intercepting, occupying, misappropriating, or defaulting on budget revenues that should be turned over to the state treasury;  
(2) in violation of regulations, failing to perform or incorrectly performing duties in operation and investment;  
(3) in violation of regulations, engaging in related-party transactions, carrying out financing-type trade, fictitious transactions, fictitious joint ventures, affiliated operations, etc.;  
(4) failing to handle or truthfully handle the registration of state-owned assets of the enterprise within the time limit prescribed by the state, or forging, altering, renting, lending, or selling the registration certificate (form) of state-owned assets;  
(5) refusing to provide relevant information and materials or fabricating false data and information, leading to distorted performance evaluation results of state-owned enterprises;  
(6) concealing the true situation of the enterprise, failing to truthfully provide relevant information and materials to intermediary service institutions such as accounting firms, law firms, and asset appraisal institutions, or colluding with such institutions to commit fraud.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-39)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）洗钱或者参与洗钱；  
（二）吸收客户资金不入账，非法吸收公众存款或者变相吸收公众存款，违反规定参与或者变相参与民间借贷；  
（三）违反规定发放贷款或者对贷款本金减免、停息、减息、缓息、免息、展期等，进行呆账核销，处置不良资产；  
（四）违反规定出具金融票证、提供担保，对违法票据予以承兑、付款或者保证；  
（五）违背受托义务，擅自运用客户资金或者其他委托、信托的资产；  
（六）伪造、变造货币、贵金属、金融票证或者国家发行的有价证券；  
（七）伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借金融机构经营许可证或者批准文件，未经批准擅自设立金融机构、发行股票或者债券；  
（八）编造并且传播影响证券、期货交易的虚假信息，操纵证券、期货市场，提供虚假信息或者伪造、变造、销毁交易记录，诱骗投资者买卖证券、期货合约；  
（九）进行虚假理赔或者参与保险诈骗活动；  
（十）窃取、收买或者非法提供他人信用卡信息及其他公民个人信息资料。

**Article 24** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, they shall, in accordance with Article 39 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) money laundering or participating in money laundering;  
(2) accepting customer funds without recording them in accounts, illegally absorbing public deposits or covertly absorbing public deposits, or in violation of regulations, participating or covertly participating in private lending;  
(3) in violation of regulations, issuing loans or granting exemptions, deferrals, interest reductions, interest deferrals, interest exemptions, extensions, etc. on loan principal, writing off non-performing loans, or disposing of non-performing assets;  
(4) in violation of regulations, issuing financial instruments, providing guarantees, or accepting, paying, or guaranteeing illegal negotiable instruments;  
(5) breaching fiduciary duties by arbitrarily using customer funds or other entrusted or trust assets;  
(6) forging or altering currency, precious metals, financial instruments, or securities issued by the state;  
(7) forging, altering, transferring, renting, or lending the financial institution operating license or approval document, or establishing financial institutions, issuing stocks or bonds without approval;  
(8) fabricating and spreading false information affecting securities or futures trading, manipulating securities or futures markets, providing false information or forging, altering, or destroying transaction records to induce investors to buy or sell securities or futures contracts;  
(9) conducting fraudulent claims or participating in insurance fraud activities;  
(10) stealing, purchasing, or illegally providing others' credit card information or other personal information data.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国有企业管理人员有下列行为之一，造成不良后果或者影响的，依据[公职人员政务处分法第三十九条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-39)的规定，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）泄露企业内幕信息或者商业秘密；  
（二）伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借行政许可证件、资质证明文件，或者出租、出借国有企业名称或者企业名称中的字号；  
（三）违反规定，举借或者变相举借地方政府债务；  
（四）在中华人民共和国境外违反规定造成重大工程质量问题、引起重大劳务纠纷或者其他严重后果；  
（五）不履行或者不依法履行安全生产管理职责，导致发生生产安全事故；  
（六）在工作中有敷衍应付、推诿扯皮，或者片面理解、机械执行党和国家路线方针政策、重大决策部署等形式主义、官僚主义行为；  
（七）拒绝、阻挠、拖延依法开展的出资人监督、审计监督、财会监督工作，或者对出资人监督、审计监督、财会监督发现的问题拒不整改、推诿敷衍、虚假整改；  
（八）不依法提供有关信息、报送有关报告或者履行信息披露义务，或者配合其他主体从事违法违规行为；  
（九）不履行法定职责或者违法行使职权，侵犯劳动者合法权益；  
（十）违反规定，拒绝或者延迟支付中小企业款项、农民工工资等；  
（十一）授意、指使、强令、纵容、包庇下属人员违反法律法规规定。

**Article 25** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise commits any of the following acts, causing adverse consequences or effects, they shall, in accordance with Article 39 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, be given a warning, demerit, or serious demerit; where the circumstances are relatively serious, a demotion or removal from office; where the circumstances are serious, dismissal:  
(1) disclosing inside information or trade secrets of the enterprise;  
(2) forging, altering, transferring, renting, or lending administrative license certificates or qualification certificates, or renting or lending the name of a state-owned enterprise or the font of its name;  
(3) in violation of regulations, incurring or covertly incurring local government debts;  
(4) in violation of regulations outside the People's Republic of China, causing major project quality problems, major labor disputes, or other serious consequences;  
(5) failing to perform or not performing safety production management duties according to law, leading to production safety accidents;  
(6) engaging in formalism or bureaucratism behaviors such as perfunctory responses, buck-passing, or one-sided understanding and mechanical implementation of the Party's and state's lines, policies, and major decisions and deployments in work;  
(7) refusing, obstructing, or delaying the lawfully conducted supervision by investors, audit supervision, or financial accounting supervision, or refusing to rectify, shirking, or falsely rectifying problems discovered by such supervision;  
(8) failing to provide relevant information, submit relevant reports, or perform information disclosure obligations according to law, or cooperating with other entities in committing illegal or irregular acts;  
(9) failing to perform statutory duties or abusing power, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of workers;  
(10) in violation of regulations, refusing or delaying payment of amounts due to small and medium-sized enterprises, wages of migrant workers, etc.;  
(11) instigating, instructing, coercing, indulging, or covering up subordinates' violations of laws and regulations.

### 第四章　处分的程序

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　任免机关、单位按照干部管理权限对有公职人员政务处分法和本条例规定违法行为的国有企业管理人员依法给予处分，保障国有企业管理人员以及相关人员的合法权益。  
任免机关、单位应当结合国有企业的组织形式、组织机构等实际情况，明确承担国有企业管理人员处分工作的内设部门或者机构（以下称承办部门）及其职责权限、运行机制等。

**Article 26** The appointing and dismissing authorities and units shall, in accordance with the authority over cadre management, impose sanctions on managers of state-owned enterprises who have engaged in illegal acts stipulated in the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials and these Regulations, and protect the lawful rights and interests of managers of state-owned enterprises and relevant personnel.  
The appointing and dismissing authorities and units shall, in light of the organizational form and structure of state-owned enterprises, specify the internal departments or institutions (hereinafter referred to as undertaking departments) responsible for the sanction work for managers of state-owned enterprises, as well as their functional authority and operating mechanisms.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对涉嫌违法的国有企业管理人员进行调查、处理，应当由2名以上工作人员进行，按照下列程序办理：  
（一）经任免机关、单位负责人同意，由承办部门对需要调查处理的问题线索进行初步核实；  
（二）经初步核实，承办部门认为该国有企业管理人员涉嫌违反公职人员政务处分法和本条例规定，需要进一步查证的，经任免机关、单位主要负责人批准同意后立案，书面告知被调查的国有企业管理人员本人（以下称被调查人）及其所在单位，并向有管理权限的监察机关通报；  
（三）承办部门负责对被调查人的违法行为作进一步调查，收集、查证有关证据材料，向有关单位和人员了解情况，并形成书面调查报告，向任免机关、单位负责人报告，有关单位和个人应当如实提供情况；  
（四）承办部门将调查认定的事实以及拟给予处分的依据告知被调查人，听取其陈述和申辩，并对其提出的事实、理由和证据进行核实，记录在案，被调查人提出的事实、理由和证据成立的，应予采纳；  
（五）承办部门经审查提出处理建议，按程序报任免机关、单位领导成员集体讨论，作出对被调查人给予处分、免予处分、不予处分或者撤销案件的决定，并向有管理权限的监察机关通报；  
（六）任免机关、单位应当自本条第一款第五项决定作出之日起1个月以内，将处分、免予处分、不予处分或者撤销案件的决定以书面形式通知被调查人及其所在单位，并在一定范围内宣布，涉及国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的，按照国家有关规定办理；  
（七）承办部门应当将处分有关决定及执行材料归入被调查人本人档案，同时汇集有关材料形成该处分案件的工作档案。  
严禁以威胁、引诱、欺骗等非法方式收集证据。以非法方式收集的证据不得作为给予处分的依据。不得因被调查人的申辩而加重处分。

**Article 27** The investigation and handling of managers of state-owned enterprises suspected of illegal acts shall be conducted by two or more staff members, and shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) Upon approval by the responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, the undertaking department shall conduct a preliminary verification of the leads requiring investigation and handling;  
(2) After preliminary verification, if the undertaking department believes that the manager of the state-owned enterprise is suspected of violating the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials and these Regulations and further investigation is needed, upon approval by the principal responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, a case shall be filed, the investigated manager (hereinafter referred to as the investigated person) and their unit shall be notified in writing, and the matter shall be reported to the supervisory authority with jurisdiction;  
(3) The undertaking department shall be responsible for further investigating the illegal acts of the investigated person, collecting and verifying relevant evidence materials, inquiring about the situation with relevant units and personnel, and forming a written investigation report to be reported to the responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, and relevant units and individuals shall truthfully provide information;  
(4) The undertaking department shall inform the investigated person of the facts established by the investigation and the basis for the proposed sanction, hear their statements and defenses, verify the facts, reasons, and evidence they put forward, and record them; if the facts, reasons, and evidence put forward by the investigated person are established, they shall be adopted;  
(5) The undertaking department shall, upon review, propose a handling suggestion, report it to the leadership members of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit for collective discussion according to the procedure, and make a decision on imposing a sanction, waiving a sanction, not imposing a sanction, or dismissing the case against the investigated person, and shall inform the supervisory authority with jurisdiction;  
(6) The appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall, within one month from the date of the decision under item (5) of this paragraph, notify the investigated person and their unit in writing of the decision on sanction, waiver of sanction, non-imposition of sanction, or dismissal of the case, and announce it within a certain scope; where state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy are involved, the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations;  
(7) The undertaking department shall place the relevant sanction decision and execution materials into the personal file of the investigated person, and at the same time compile relevant materials to form a work file for the sanction case.  
It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence by illegal means such as threats, inducements, or deception. Evidence collected by illegal means shall not be used as the basis for imposing a sanction. The sanction shall not be increased due to the defense of the investigated person.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　重大违法案件调查过程中，确有需要的，可以商请有管理权限的监察机关提供必要支持。  
违法情形复杂、涉及面广或者造成重大影响，由任免机关、单位调查核实存在困难的，经任免机关、单位负责人同意，可以商请有管理权限的监察机关处理。

**Article 28** During the investigation of major illegal cases, where truly necessary, the supervisory authority with jurisdiction may be consulted to provide necessary support.  
Where the illegal case is complex, involves a wide scope, or causes significant impact, and it is difficult for the appointing and dismissing authority or unit to investigate and verify, upon approval by the responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, the matter may be referred to the supervisory authority with jurisdiction for handling.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　给予国有企业管理人员处分，应当自立案之日起6个月内作出决定；案情复杂或者遇有其他特殊情形的，经任免机关、单位主要负责人批准可以适当延长，但是延长期限不得超过6个月。

**Article 29** The decision to impose a sanction on a manager of a state-owned enterprise shall be made within six months from the date of filing the case; where the case is complex or there are other special circumstances, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, the period may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed six months.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　决定给予处分的，应当制作处分决定书。  
处分决定书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）受到处分的国有企业管理人员（以下称被处分人）的姓名、工作单位和职务；  
（二）违法事实和证据；  
（三）处分的种类和依据；  
（四）不服处分决定，申请复核、申诉的途径和期限；  
（五）作出处分决定的机关、单位名称和日期。  
处分决定书应当盖有作出决定的机关、单位印章。

**Article 30** Where a decision to impose a sanction is made, a written sanction decision shall be prepared.  
The sanction decision shall state the following:  
(1) the name, work unit, and position of the manager of the state-owned enterprise who has been sanctioned (hereinafter referred to as the sanctioned person);  
(2) the facts and evidence of the illegal act;  
(3) the type and basis of the sanction;  
(4) the channels and time limit for applying for review and appeal if dissatisfied with the sanction decision;  
(5) the name and date of the authority or unit that made the sanction decision.  
The sanction decision shall bear the seal of the authority or unit that made the decision.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　参与国有企业管理人员违法案件调查、处理的人员有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，被调查人、检举人以及其他有关人员可以要求其回避：  
（一）是被调查人或者检举人的近亲属；  
（二）担任过本案的证人；  
（三）本人或者其近亲属与调查的案件有利害关系；  
（四）可能影响案件公正调查、处理的其他情形。  
任免机关、单位主要负责人的回避，由上一级机关、单位负责人决定；其他参与违法案件调查、处理人员的回避，由任免机关、单位负责人决定。  
任免机关、单位发现参与处分工作的人员有应当回避情形的，可以直接决定该人员回避。

**Article 31** Where personnel involved in the investigation and handling of illegal cases of managers of state-owned enterprises fall under any of the following circumstances, they shall recuse themselves, and the investigated person, the reporter, and other relevant persons may request their recusal:  
(1) being a close relative of the investigated person or the reporter;  
(2) having served as a witness in the case;  
(3) having an interest relationship with the case under investigation, themselves or their close relatives;  
(4) other circumstances that may affect the impartial investigation and handling of the case.  
The recusal of the principal responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall be decided by the responsible person of the superior authority or unit; the recusal of other personnel involved in the investigation and handling of the illegal case shall be decided by the responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit.  
Where the appointing and dismissing authority or unit discovers that a person involved in the sanction work falls under a circumstance requiring recusal, it may directly decide on that person's recusal.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国有企业管理人员被依法追究刑事责任的，任免机关、单位应当根据司法机关的生效判决、裁定、决定及其认定的事实和情节，依法给予处分。  
国有企业管理人员依法受到行政处罚，应当给予处分的，任免机关、单位可以根据生效的行政处罚决定认定的事实和情节，经核实后依法给予处分。  
任免机关、单位根据本条第一款、第二款规定作出处分决定后，司法机关、行政机关依法改变原生效判决、裁定、决定等，对原处分决定产生影响的，任免机关、单位应当根据改变后的判决、裁定、决定等重新作出相应处理。

**Article 32** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise is investigated for criminal liability according to law, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall, based on the effective judgment, ruling, or decision of the judicial authority and the facts and circumstances established therein, impose a sanction according to law.  
Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise has been subjected to an administrative penalty according to law and a sanction should be imposed, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit may, based on the facts and circumstances established in the effective administrative penalty decision, impose a sanction according to law after verification.  
Where, after the appointing and dismissing authority or unit has made a sanction decision in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, the judicial authority or administrative authority legally changes the original effective judgment, ruling, or decision, and such change affects the original sanction decision, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall make corresponding new handling based on the changed judgment, ruling, or decision.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　任免机关、单位对担任各级人民代表大会代表或者中国人民政治协商会议各级委员会委员的国有企业管理人员给予处分的，应当向有关的人民代表大会常务委员会，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团或者中国人民政治协商会议委员会常务委员会通报。

**Article 33** Where the appointing and dismissing authority or unit imposes a sanction on a manager of a state-owned enterprise who serves as a deputy to a people's congress at any level or a member of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference committee at any level, it shall notify the relevant standing committee of the people's congress, the presidium of the township, ethnic township, or town people's congress, or the standing committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference committee.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国有企业管理人员涉嫌违法，已经被立案调查，不宜继续履行职责的，任免机关、单位可以决定暂停其履行职务。国有企业管理人员在被立案调查期间，未经决定立案的任免机关、单位同意，不得出境、辞去公职；其任免机关、单位以及上级机关、单位不得对其交流、晋升、奖励或者办理退休手续。

**Article 34** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise is suspected of an illegal act and has been placed under investigation, and it is inappropriate for them to continue performing their duties, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit may decide to suspend them from performing their duties. During the period of investigation, without the consent of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit that decided to file the case, the manager under investigation shall not leave the country or resign from public office; their appointing and dismissing authority or unit and the superior authority or unit shall not arrange transfer, promotion, award, or retirement procedures for them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　调查中发现国有企业管理人员因依法履行职责遭受不实举报、诬告陷害、侮辱诽谤，造成不良影响的，任免机关、单位应当按照规定及时澄清事实，恢复名誉，消除不良影响。

**Article 35** Where, during an investigation, it is found that a manager of a state-owned enterprise has suffered adverse effects due to false reports, false accusations, insults, or defamation arising from their lawful performance of duties, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall promptly clarify the facts, restore their reputation, and eliminate the adverse effects according to regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国有企业管理人员受到降级、撤职、开除处分的，应当在处分决定作出后1个月内，由相应人事部门等按照管理权限办理岗位、职务、工资和其他有关待遇等变更手续，并依法变更或者解除劳动合同；特殊情况下，经任免机关、单位主要负责人批准可以适当延长办理期限，但是最长不得超过6个月。

**Article 36** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise is subjected to demotion, removal from office, or dismissal, the relevant personnel department shall, within one month after the sanction decision is made, handle the procedures for changing their post, position, salary, and other related benefits in accordance with the management authority, and modify or terminate the labor contract according to law; under special circumstances, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the appointing and dismissing authority or unit, the processing period may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed six months.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国有企业管理人员受到开除以外的处分，在受处分期间有悔改表现，并且没有再出现应当给予处分的违法情形的，处分期满后自动解除处分。  
处分解除后，考核以及晋升职务、职级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、薪酬待遇等级等不再受原处分影响。但是，受到降级、撤职处分的，不恢复受处分前的职务、职级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、薪酬待遇等级等。  
任免机关、单位应当按照国家有关规定正确对待、合理使用受处分的国有企业管理人员，坚持尊重激励与监督约束并重，营造干事创业的良好环境。

**Article 37** Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise receives a sanction other than dismissal and shows repentance during the sanction period, and no illegal conduct warranting a sanction reoccurs, the sanction shall be automatically lifted upon the expiration of the sanction period.  
After the sanction is lifted, assessments, and promotions in positions, ranks, levels, posts, employee grades, professional titles, and salary and remuneration grades shall no longer be affected by the original sanction. However, where demotion or removal from office has been imposed, the position, rank, level, post, employee grade, professional title, salary and remuneration grade, etc. before the sanction shall not be restored.  
The appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall correctly treat and reasonably use the sanctioned manager of a state-owned enterprise in accordance with relevant state regulations, adhere to the principle of equal emphasis on respect, encouragement, supervision, and restraint, and create a favorable environment for entrepreneurship.

### 第五章　复核、申诉

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　被处分人对处分决定不服的，可以自收到处分决定书之日起1个月内，向作出处分决定的任免机关、单位（以下称原处分决定单位）申请复核。原处分决定单位应当自接到复核申请后1个月以内作出复核决定。  
被处分人因不可抗拒的事由或者其他正当理由耽误复核申请期限的，在障碍消除后的10个工作日内，可以申请顺延期限；是否准许，由原处分决定单位决定。

**Article 38** Where a sanctioned person is dissatisfied with a sanction decision, they may, within one month from the date of receiving the written sanction decision, apply for review to the appointing and dismissing authority or unit that made the decision (hereinafter referred to as the original sanction decision unit). The original sanction decision unit shall make a review decision within one month from the date of receiving the review application.  
Where the sanctioned person delays the review application period due to force majeure or other legitimate reasons, they may apply for an extension within ten working days after the obstacle is removed; whether to grant the extension shall be decided by the original sanction decision unit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　被处分人对复核决定仍不服的，可以自收到复核决定之日起1个月内按照管理权限向上一级机关、单位申诉。受理申诉的机关、单位（以下称申诉机关）应当自受理之日起2个月以内作出处理决定；案情复杂的，可以适当延长，但是延长期限最多不超过1个月。  
被处分人因不可抗拒的事由或者其他正当理由耽误申诉申请期限的，在障碍消除后的10个工作日内，可以申请顺延期限；是否准许，由申诉机关决定。

**Article 39** Where the sanctioned person is still dissatisfied with the review decision, they may, within one month from the date of receiving the review decision, appeal to the superior authority or unit in accordance with the management authority. The authority or unit that accepts the appeal (hereinafter referred to as the appeal authority) shall make a handling decision within two months from the date of acceptance; where the case is complex, the period may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed one month.  
Where the sanctioned person delays the appeal application period due to force majeure or other legitimate reasons, they may apply for an extension within ten working days after the obstacle is removed; whether to grant the extension shall be decided by the appeal authority.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　原处分决定单位接到复核申请、申诉机关受理申诉后，相关承办部门应当成立工作组，调阅原案材料，必要时可以进行调查，收集、查证有关证据材料，向有关单位和人员了解情况。工作组应当集体研究，提出办理意见，按程序报原处分决定单位、申诉机关领导成员集体讨论作出复核、申诉决定，并向有管理权限的监察机关通报。复核、申诉决定应当自作出之日起1个月以内以书面形式通知被处分人及其所在单位，并在一定范围内宣布；涉及国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的，按照国家有关规定办理。  
复核、申诉期间，不停止原处分决定的执行。  
国有企业管理人员不因提出复核、申诉而被加重处分。  
坚持复核、申诉与原案调查相分离，原案调查、承办人员不得参与复核、申诉。

**Article 40** After the original sanction decision unit receives the review application or the appeal authority accepts the appeal, the relevant undertaking department shall set up a working group to review the original case materials, and if necessary, conduct investigations, collect and verify relevant evidence materials, and inquire about the situation with relevant units and personnel. The working group shall conduct collective research, propose handling opinions, and submit them to the leadership members of the original sanction decision unit or the appeal authority for collective discussion according to the procedure to make a review or appeal decision, and shall inform the supervisory authority with jurisdiction. The review or appeal decision shall be notified in writing to the sanctioned person and their unit within one month from the date it is made, and announced within a certain scope; where state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy are involved, the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
During the review or appeal period, the execution of the original sanction decision shall not be suspended.  
A manager of a state-owned enterprise shall not be subjected to a heavier sanction due to raising a review or appeal.  
The review and appeal shall be separated from the original case investigation; the original case investigators and handling personnel shall not participate in the review or appeal.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任免机关、单位发现本机关、本单位或者下级机关、单位作出的处分决定确有错误的，应当及时予以纠正或者责令下级机关、单位及时予以纠正。  
监察机关发现任免机关、单位应当给予处分而未给予，或者给予的处分违法、不当，依法提出监察建议的，任免机关、单位应当采纳并将执行情况函告监察机关，不采纳的应当说明理由。

**Article 41** Where the appointing and dismissing authority or unit discovers that a sanction decision made by itself or a subordinate authority or unit is indeed erroneous, it shall promptly correct it or order the subordinate authority or unit to promptly correct it.  
Where a supervisory authority discovers that the appointing and dismissing authority or unit should have imposed a sanction but did not, or that the imposed sanction is illegal or improper, and proposes a supervisory recommendation according to law, the appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall adopt it and inform the supervisory authority in writing of the implementation; if it does not adopt it, reasons shall be provided.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有下列情形之一的，原处分决定单位、申诉机关应当撤销原处分决定，重新作出决定或者由申诉机关责令原处分决定单位重新作出决定：  
（一）处分所依据的违法事实不清或者证据不足；  
（二）违反本条例规定的程序，影响案件公正处理；  
（三）超越职权或者滥用职权作出处分决定。

**Article 42** Under any of the following circumstances, the original sanction decision unit or the appeal authority shall revoke the original sanction decision and make a new decision, or the appeal authority shall order the original sanction decision unit to make a new decision:  
(1) the illegal facts on which the sanction is based are unclear or the evidence is insufficient;  
(2) the procedures stipulated in these Regulations are violated, affecting the fair handling of the case;  
(3) the sanction decision is made by exceeding authority or abusing power.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列情形之一的，原处分决定单位、申诉机关应当变更原处分决定，或者由申诉机关责令原处分决定单位予以变更：  
（一）适用法律、法规确有错误；  
（二）对违法行为的情节认定确有错误；  
（三）处分不当。

**Article 43** Under any of the following circumstances, the original sanction decision unit or the appeal authority shall modify the original sanction decision, or the appeal authority shall order the original sanction decision unit to modify it:  
(1) the application of laws or regulations is indeed erroneous;  
(2) the determination of the circumstances of the illegal act is indeed erroneous;  
(3) the sanction is inappropriate.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　原处分决定单位、申诉机关认为处分决定认定事实清楚，适用法律正确的，应当予以维持。

**Article 44** Where the original sanction decision unit or the appeal authority believes that the facts determined in the sanction decision are clear and the law applied is correct, it shall uphold the decision.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国有企业管理人员的处分决定被变更，需要调整该国有企业管理人员的职务、岗位等级、薪酬待遇等级等的，应当按照规定予以调整。国有企业管理人员的处分决定被撤销，需要恢复该国有企业管理人员的职务、岗位等级、薪酬待遇等级等的，应当按照原职务和岗位等级安排相应的职务和岗位，并在原处分决定公布范围内为其恢复名誉。  
国有企业管理人员因有[本条例第四十二条](#art-42)、第四十三条规定情形被撤销处分或者减轻处分的，应当结合其实际履职、业绩贡献等情况对其薪酬待遇受到的损失予以适当补偿。  
维持、变更、撤销处分的决定应当在作出后1个月内按照[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)第一款第六项规定予以送达、宣布，并存入被处分人本人档案。

**Article 45** Where a sanction decision for a manager of a state-owned enterprise is modified and the position, post grade, salary and remuneration grade, etc. of the manager need to be adjusted, the adjustment shall be made according to regulations. Where a sanction decision for a manager of a state-owned enterprise is revoked and the position, post grade, salary and remuneration grade, etc. need to be restored, the corresponding position and post shall be arranged according to the original position and post grade, and their reputation shall be restored within the scope of the original announcement of the sanction decision.  
Where a manager of a state-owned enterprise has the sanction revoked or mitigated due to circumstances specified in Articles 42 and 43 of these Regulations, appropriate compensation shall be made for the losses in their salary and remuneration, taking into account their actual performance of duties, contribution, etc.  
The decision to uphold, modify, or revoke the sanction shall be served and announced within one month after being made in accordance with Article 27, paragraph 1, item (6) of these Regulations, and shall be placed in the personal file of the sanctioned person.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　任免机关、单位及其工作人员在国有企业管理人员处分工作中有[公职人员政务处分法第六十一条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-61)、第六十三条规定情形的，依据公职人员政务处分法的规定对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员给予处理。

**Article 46** Where the appointing and dismissing authority or unit or its staff members have the circumstances specified in Articles 61 and 63 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials in the sanction work for managers of state-owned enterprises, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有关机关、单位、组织或者人员拒不执行处分决定或者有[公职人员政务处分法第六十二条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-62)规定情形的，由其上级机关、主管部门、履行出资人职责的机构或者任免机关、单位依据公职人员政务处分法的规定给予处理。

**Article 47** Where relevant authorities, units, organizations, or personnel refuse to execute a sanction decision or have the circumstances specified in Article 62 of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials, the superior authority, competent department, institution performing the duties of an investor, or appointing and dismissing authority or unit shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Administrative Sanctions for Public Officials.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　相关单位或者个人利用举报等方式歪曲捏造事实，诬告陷害国有企业管理人员的，应当依法承担法律责任。

**Article 48** Where relevant units or individuals use reporting or other means to distort facts, fabricate allegations, and falsely accuse managers of state-owned enterprises, they shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家对违法的金融、文化国有企业管理人员追究责任另有规定的，同时适用。

**Article 50** Where the state has other provisions on investigating the liability of managers of illegal financial or cultural state-owned enterprises, such provisions shall also apply.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例施行前，已经结案的案件如果需要复核、申诉，适用当时的规定。尚未结案的案件，如果行为发生时的规定不认为是违法的，适用当时的规定；如果行为发生时的规定认为是违法的，依照当时的规定处理，但是如果本条例不认为是违法或者根据本条例处理较轻的，适用本条例。

**Article 51** For cases that have been concluded before these Regulations come into force, if review or appeal is required, the provisions in force at that time shall apply. For cases that have not yet been concluded, if the provisions in force at the time of the act did not consider it illegal, the provisions in force at that time shall apply; if the provisions in force at the time of the act considered it illegal, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions in force at that time, but if these Regulations do not consider it illegal or the punishment under these Regulations is lighter, these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自2024年9月1日起施行。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2024.
