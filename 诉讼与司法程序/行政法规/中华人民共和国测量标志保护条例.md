# 中华人民共和国测量标志保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1997-01-01

> (1996年9月4日中华人民共和国国务院令第203号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强测量标志的保护和管理，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国领域内和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域设置的测量标志。 第三条　测量标志属于国家所有，是国家经济建设和科学研究的基础设施。 第四条　本条例所称测量标志，是指： (一)建设在地上、地下或者建筑物上的各种等级的三角点、基线点、导线点、军用控制点、重力点、天文点、水准点的木质觇标、钢质觇标和标石标志，全球卫星定位控制点，以及用于地形测图、工程测量和形变测量的固定标志和海底大地点设施等永久性测量标志； (二)测量中正在使用的临时性测量标志。 第五条　国务院测绘行政主管部门主管全国的测量标志保护工作。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责管理本部门专用的测量标志保护工作。 县级以上地方人民政府管理测绘工作的部门负责本行政区域内的测量标志保护工作。 军队测绘主管部门负责管理军事部门测量标志保护工作，并按照国务院、中央军事委员会规定的职责分工负责管理海洋基础测量标志保护工作。 第六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对测量标志保护工作的领导，增强公民依法保护测量标志的意识。 乡级人民政府应当做好本行政区域内的测量标志保护管理工作。 第七条　对在保护永久性测量标志工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。 第八条　建设永久性测量标志，应当符合下列要求： (一)使用国家规定的测绘基准和测绘标准；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强测量标志的保护和管理，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the protection and management of surveying markers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国领域内和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域设置的测量标志。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to surveying markers established within the territory of the People's Republic of China and in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　测量标志属于国家所有，是国家经济建设和科学研究的基础设施。

**Article 3** Surveying markers shall be owned by the State and shall be infrastructure for national economic construction and scientific research.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本条例所称测量标志，是指：  
(一)建设在地上、地下或者建筑物上的各种等级的三角点、基线点、导线点、军用控制点、重力点、天文点、水准点的木质觇标、钢质觇标和标石标志，全球卫星定位控制点，以及用于地形测图、工程测量和形变测量的固定标志和海底大地点设施等永久性测量标志；  
(二)测量中正在使用的临时性测量标志。

**Article 4** For the purposes of these Regulations, "surveying markers" means:  
(1) permanent surveying markers such as wooden survey markers, steel survey markers, and benchmark markers of various orders at triangulation points, baseline points, traverse points, military control points, gravity points, astronomical points, and leveling points constructed on land, underground, or on buildings, global satellite positioning control points, as well as fixed markers for topographic mapping, engineering surveying, and deformation measurement, and seabed geodetic facilities; and  
(2) temporary surveying markers being used in surveying.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院测绘行政主管部门主管全国的测量标志保护工作。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责管理本部门专用的测量标志保护工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府管理测绘工作的部门负责本行政区域内的测量标志保护工作。  
军队测绘主管部门负责管理军事部门测量标志保护工作，并按照国务院、中央军事委员会规定的职责分工负责管理海洋基础测量标志保护工作。

**Article 5** The competent surveying and mapping administrative department of the State Council shall take charge of the protection of surveying markers nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the protection of surveying markers exclusively used by their respective departments.  
The department administering surveying and mapping work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the protection of surveying markers within its administrative area.  
The competent surveying and mapping department of the military shall be responsible for the protection of military surveying markers and, in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, for the protection of marine basic surveying markers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对测量标志保护工作的领导，增强公民依法保护测量标志的意识。  
乡级人民政府应当做好本行政区域内的测量标志保护管理工作。

**Article 6** The people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over the protection of surveying markers and enhance citizens' awareness of protecting surveying markers according to law.  
The people's government at the township level shall properly carry out the protection and management of surveying markers within its administrative area.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在保护永久性测量标志工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in protecting permanent surveying markers shall be given rewards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　建设永久性测量标志，应当符合下列要求：  
(一)使用国家规定的测绘基准和测绘标准；  
(二)选择有利于测量标志长期保护和管理的点位；  
(三)符合法律、法规规定的其他要求。

**Article 8** Construction of permanent surveying markers shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) using the surveying and mapping datums and surveying and mapping standards prescribed by the State;  
(2) selecting locations conducive to long-term protection and management of surveying markers; and  
(3) complying with other requirements prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设置永久性测量标志的，应当对永久性测量标志设立明显标记；设置基础性测量标志的，还应当设立由国务院测绘行政主管部门统一监制的专门标牌。

**Article 9** Where permanent surveying markers are established, conspicuous marks shall be set up for them; where basic surveying markers are established, special signboards uniformly produced under the supervision of the competent surveying and mapping administrative department of the State Council shall also be set up.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建设永久性测量标志需要占用土地的，地面标志占用土地的范围为36平方米—100平方米，地下标志占用土地的范围为16平方米—36平方米。

**Article 10** Where the construction of permanent surveying markers requires land occupation, the area of land occupied by surface markers shall range from 36 square meters to 100 square meters, and the area of land occupied by underground markers shall range from 16 square meters to 36 square meters.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设置永久性测量标志，需要依法使用土地或者在建筑物上建设永久性测量标志的，有关单位和个人不得干扰和阻挠。

**Article 11** Where the establishment of permanent surveying markers requires the use of land according to law or the construction of permanent surveying markers on buildings, the relevant units and individuals shall not interfere with or obstruct such activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对测量标志实行义务保管制度。  
设置永久性测量标志的部门应当将永久性测量标志委托测量标志设置地的有关单位或者人员负责保管，签订测量标志委托保管书，明确委托方和被委托方的权利和义务，并由委托方将委托保管书抄送乡级人民政府和县级以上地方人民政府管理测绘工作的部门备案。

**Article 12** The State shall implement a system of obligatory custody for surveying markers.  
The department establishing permanent surveying markers shall entrust the permanent surveying markers to the relevant units or personnel at the place where they are established for custody, sign a surveying marker entrustment custody contract, specify the rights and obligations of the entrusting party and the entrusted party, and the entrusting party shall send a copy of the entrustment custody contract to the people's government at the township level and the department administering surveying and mapping work of the local people's government at or above the county level for archival filing.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　负责保管测量标志的单位和人员，应当对其所保管的测量标志经常进行检查；发现测量标志有被移动或者损毁的情况时，应当及时报告当地乡级人民政府，并由乡级人民政府报告县级以上地方人民政府管理测绘工作的部门。

**Article 13** The units and personnel responsible for the custody of surveying markers shall regularly inspect the surveying markers under their custody; upon discovering that a surveying marker has been moved or damaged, they shall promptly report to the local people's government at the township level, and the people's government at the township level shall report to the department administering surveying and mapping work of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　负责保管测量标志的单位和人员有权制止、检举和控告移动、损毁、盗窃测量标志的行为，任何单位或者个人不得阻止和打击报复。

**Article 14** The units and personnel responsible for the custody of surveying markers shall have the right to stop, report, and accuse acts of moving, damaging, or stealing surveying markers, and no unit or individual shall obstruct or retaliate against them.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对测量标志实行有偿使用；但是，使用测量标志从事军事测绘任务的除外。测量标志有偿使用的收入应当用于测量标志的维护、维修，不得挪作他用。具体办法由国务院测绘行政主管部门会同国务院物价行政主管部门规定。

**Article 15** The State shall implement a system of compensated use for surveying markers, except where surveying markers are used for military surveying and mapping tasks. The income from compensated use of surveying markers shall be used for the maintenance and repair of surveying markers and shall not be diverted to other purposes. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent surveying and mapping administrative department of the State Council jointly with the competent pricing administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　测绘人员使用永久性测量标志，应当持有测绘工作证件，并接受县级以上人民政府管理测绘工作的部门的监督和负责保管测量标志的单位和人员的查询，确保测量标志完好。

**Article 16** Personnel engaged in surveying and mapping who use permanent surveying markers shall hold surveying and mapping work certificates, and shall accept the supervision of the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government at or above the county level and inquiries from the units and personnel responsible for the custody of surveying markers, and shall ensure the integrity of surveying markers.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　测量标志保护工作应当执行维修规划和计划。  
全国测量标志维修规划，由国务院测绘行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理测绘工作的部门应当组织同级有关部门，根据全国测量标志维修规划，制定本行政区域内的测量标志维修计划，并组织协调有关部门和单位统一实施。

**Article 17** The protection of surveying markers shall implement maintenance plans and programs.  
The national maintenance plan for surveying markers shall be formulated by the competent surveying and mapping administrative department of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council.  
The department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the relevant departments at the same level to formulate maintenance programs for surveying markers within its administrative area in accordance with the national maintenance plan, and shall organize and coordinate the relevant departments and units to implement them uniformly.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　设置永久性测量标志的部门应当按照国家有关的测量标志维修规程，对永久性测量标志定期组织维修，保证测量标志正常使用。

**Article 18** The department establishing permanent surveying markers shall, in accordance with the relevant State regulations for surveying marker maintenance, periodically organize maintenance of permanent surveying markers to ensure the normal use of surveying markers.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　进行工程建设，应当避开永久性测量标志；确实无法避开，需要拆迁永久性测量标志或者使永久性测量标志失去使用效能的，工程建设单位应当履行下列批准手续：  
(一)拆迁基础性测量标志或者使基础性测量标志失去使用效能的，由国务院测绘行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理测绘工作的部门批准；  
(二)拆迁部门专用的永久性测量标志或者使部门专用的永久性测量标志失去使用效能的，应当经设置测量标志的部门同意，并经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理测绘工作的部门批准。  
拆迁永久性测量标志，还应当通知负责保管测量标志的有关单位和人员。

**Article 19** In carrying out engineering construction, permanent surveying markers shall be avoided; where it is truly unavoidable and it is necessary to relocate a permanent surveying marker or cause a permanent surveying marker to lose its functionality, the engineering construction unit shall fulfill the following approval procedures:  
(1) where a basic surveying marker is relocated or loses functionality, approval shall be obtained from the competent surveying and mapping administrative department of the State Council or the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(2) where a permanent surveying marker exclusively used by a department is relocated or loses functionality, consent shall be obtained from the department that established the surveying marker, and approval shall be obtained from the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
When a permanent surveying marker is relocated, the relevant units and personnel responsible for the custody of the surveying marker shall also be notified.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经批准拆迁基础性测量标志或者使基础性测量标志失去使用效能的，工程建设单位应当按照国家有关规定向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理测绘工作的部门支付迁建费用。  
经批准拆迁部门专用的测量标志或者使部门专用的测量标志失去使用效能的，工程建设单位应当按照国家有关规定向设置测量标志的部门支付迁建费用；设置部门专用的测量标志的部门查找不到的，工程建设单位应当按照国家有关规定向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理测绘工作的部门支付迁建费用。

**Article 20** Where approval is obtained to relocate a basic surveying marker or cause it to lose functionality, the engineering construction unit shall pay relocation and reconstruction fees to the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where approval is obtained to relocate a surveying marker exclusively used by a department or cause it to lose functionality, the engineering construction unit shall pay relocation and reconstruction fees to the department that established the surveying marker in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; where the department that established the surveying marker cannot be found, the engineering construction unit shall pay relocation and reconstruction fees to the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　永久性测量标志的重建工作，由收取测量标志迁建费用的部门组织实施。

**Article 21** The reconstruction of permanent surveying markers shall be organized and implemented by the department that has collected the relocation and reconstruction fees for the surveying markers.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　测量标志受国家保护，禁止下列有损测量标志安全和使测量标志失去使用效能的行为：  
(一)损毁或者擅自移动地下或者地上的永久性测量标志以及使用中的临时性测量标志的；  
(二)在测量标志占地范围内烧荒、耕作、取土、挖沙或者侵占永久性测量标志用地的；  
(三)在距永久性测量标志50米范围内采石、爆破、射击、架设高压电线的；  
(四)在测量标志的占地范围内，建设影响测量标志使用效能的建筑物的；  
(五)在测量标志上架设通讯设施、设置观望台、搭帐篷、拴牲畜或者设置其他有可能损毁测量标志的附着物的；  
(六)擅自拆除设有测量标志的建筑物或者拆除建筑物上的测量标志的；  
(七)其他有损测量标志安全和使用效能的。

**Article 22** Surveying markers shall be protected by the State. The following acts that damage the safety of surveying markers or cause them to lose functionality are prohibited:  
(1) destroying or unauthorizedly moving permanent surveying markers underground or on the surface, or temporary surveying markers in use;  
(2) burning-off, farming, digging earth, extracting sand, or encroaching on the land of permanent surveying markers within the land occupation areas of surveying markers;  
(3) quarrying, blasting, shooting, or erecting high-voltage power lines within 50 meters of permanent surveying markers;  
(4) constructing buildings that affect the functionality of surveying markers within the land occupation areas of surveying markers;  
(5) installing communication facilities, setting up observation platforms, putting up tents, tethering livestock, or placing other attachments that may damage surveying markers on the surveying markers;  
(6) unauthorizedly demolishing buildings with surveying markers or removing surveying markers from buildings; and  
(7) other acts damaging the safety or functionality of surveying markers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)禁止的行为之一，或者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府管理测绘工作的部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并可以根据情节处以5万元以下的罚款；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)干扰或者阻挠测量标志建设单位依法使用土地或者在建筑物上建设永久性测量标志的；  
(二)工程建设单位未经批准擅自拆迁永久性测量标志或者使永久性测量标志失去使用效能的，或者拒绝按照国家有关规定支付迁建费用的；  
(三)违反测绘操作规程进行测绘，使永久性测量标志受到损坏的；  
(四)无证使用永久性测量标志并且拒绝县级以上人民政府管理测绘工作的部门监督和负责保管测量标志的单位和人员查询的。

**Article 23** Whoever commits any of the acts prohibited in Article 22 of these Regulations, or commits any of the following acts, shall be ordered by the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a time limit, be given a warning, and may, depending on the circumstances, be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) interfering with or obstructing the construction unit of surveying markers in using land according to law or constructing permanent surveying markers on buildings;  
(2) the engineering construction unit unauthorizedly relocating a permanent surveying marker or causing it to lose functionality without approval, or refusing to pay relocation and reconstruction fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) causing damage to a permanent surveying marker in the course of surveying and mapping in violation of surveying and mapping operation rules; or  
(4) using a permanent surveying marker without a certificate and refusing the supervision of the department administering surveying and mapping work of the people's government at or above the county level and inquiries from the units and personnel responsible for the custody of the surveying marker.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　管理测绘工作的部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 24** Where staff members of the department administering surveying and mapping work neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations warrants a public security administration penalty, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自1997年1月1日起施行。1984年1月7日国务院发布的《测量标志保护条例》同时废止。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1997. The Regulations on the Protection of Surveying Markers promulgated by the State Council on January 7, 1984 shall be abolished simultaneously.
