# 国务院关于高级专家离休退休若干问题的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1983-09-12  
**生效日期**：1983-09-12

> (1983年9月12日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行) 为了充分发挥高级专家的作用，为社会主义建设事业多作贡献，并有利于新生力量的成长和队伍的更新，特制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称高级专家，系指：正副教授、正副研究员、高级工程师、高级农艺师、正副主任医师、正副编审、正副译审、正副研究馆员、高级经济师、高级统计师、高级会计师、特级记者、高级记者、高级工艺美术师，以及文艺六级以上的专家。 第二条　高级专家离休退休年龄，一般应按国家统一规定执行。对其中少数高级专家，确因工作需要，身体能够坚持正常工作，征得本人同意，经下述机关批准，其离休退休年龄可以适当延长： 副教授、副研究员以及相当这一级职称的高级专家，经所在单位报请上一级主管机关批准，可以适当延长离休退休年龄，但最长不超过六十五周岁； 教授、研究员以及相当这一级职称的高级专家，经所在单位报请省、市、自治区人民政府或中央、国家机关的部委批准，可以延长离休退休年龄，但最长不超过七十周岁； 学术上造诣高深、在国内外有重大影响的杰出高级专家，经国务院批准，可以暂缓离休退休，继续从事研究或著述工作。 第三条　延长离休退休年龄的高级专家中，担任行政领导职务或管理职务的，在达到国家统一规定的离休退休年龄时，应当免去其行政领导职务或管理职务，使他们集中精力继续从事科学技术或文化艺术等工作。特殊情况经过任免机关批准的除外。 第四条　高级专家离休退休的待遇，按国家统一规定办理。符合以下情况的，退休费标准可以适当提高： (一)有重大贡献的高级专家，经省、市、自治区人民政府或中央、国家机关的部委批准，其退休费标准可以酌情提高5%—15%。提高标准后的退休费，不得超过本人原标准工资。 (二)建国后从国外或者从香港、澳门、台湾回来定居工作的高级专家，其退休费均按建国后参加革命工作退休干部的最高标准发给。其中有重大贡献的，再按本条(一)项规定提高退休费。 第五条　高级专家离休退休后，如身体尚好，可以接受部门或单位的聘请，担任科学技术或文化艺术顾问，也可以直接承担业务工作。聘请离休退休高级专家应签订聘请合同，聘请条件和受聘人员待遇按国家有关规定办理。 第六条　各单位和各部门要认真执行国务院关于干部离休退休后政治、生活待遇的有关规定，对离休退休的高级专家应体贴关怀，热情爱护；要积极主动地帮助他们继续进行科学研究、资料整理、著书立说等工作，为他们的业务活动提供必要的方便。 第七条　本规定自发布之日起施行。本规定发布以前，已经离休退休的高级专家，不再复职。 第八条　本规定由劳动人事部负责解释。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称高级专家，系指：正副教授、正副研究员、高级工程师、高级农艺师、正副主任医师、正副编审、正副译审、正副研究馆员、高级经济师、高级统计师、高级会计师、特级记者、高级记者、高级工艺美术师，以及文艺六级以上的专家。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/劳动就业/国务院关于高级专家退休问题的补充规定.md#art-1" title="被《国务院关于高级专家退休问题的补充规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 1** The term "senior experts" as used in these Provisions means: full and associate professors, full and associate researchers, senior engineers, senior agronomists, full and associate chief physicians, full and associate senior editors, full and associate senior translators, full and associate senior librarians, senior economists, senior statisticians, senior accountants, special-grade journalists, senior journalists, senior industrial artists, and experts at or above Grade 6 in literature and arts.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　高级专家离休退休年龄，一般应按国家统一规定执行。对其中少数高级专家，确因工作需要，身体能够坚持正常工作，征得本人同意，经下述机关批准，其离休退休年龄可以适当延长：  
副教授、副研究员以及相当这一级职称的高级专家，经所在单位报请上一级主管机关批准，可以适当延长离休退休年龄，但最长不超过六十五周岁；  
教授、研究员以及相当这一级职称的高级专家，经所在单位报请省、市、自治区人民政府或中央、国家机关的部委批准，可以延长离休退休年龄，但最长不超过七十周岁；  
学术上造诣高深、在国内外有重大影响的杰出高级专家，经国务院批准，可以暂缓离休退休，继续从事研究或著述工作。

**Article 2** The retirement age for senior experts shall generally be implemented in accordance with uniform state provisions. For a small number of senior experts who are indeed required by work and whose physical condition allows them to persist in normal work, subject to their consent and approval by the following authorities, their retirement age may be appropriately extended:  
Associate professors, associate researchers, and senior experts with equivalent titles may, upon approval by the next higher competent authority submitted by their employing unit, have their retirement age appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed sixty-five years of age.  
Professors, researchers, and senior experts with equivalent titles may, upon approval by the people's government of a province, municipality directly under the Central Government, or autonomous region, or by a ministry or commission of the Central Government or State organs, have their retirement age extended, but the extension shall not exceed seventy years of age.  
Outstanding senior experts of profound academic attainments and significant influence at home or abroad may, upon approval by the State Council, postpone retirement and continue to engage in research or writing.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　延长离休退休年龄的高级专家中，担任行政领导职务或管理职务的，在达到国家统一规定的离休退休年龄时，应当免去其行政领导职务或管理职务，使他们集中精力继续从事科学技术或文化艺术等工作。特殊情况经过任免机关批准的除外。

**Article 3** Among senior experts whose retirement age is extended, those who hold administrative leadership or management positions shall, upon reaching the uniform state retirement age, be relieved of their administrative leadership or management positions, so that they may concentrate on continuing to engage in scientific, technological, cultural or artistic work. This shall not apply where special circumstances are approved by the authority responsible for appointment and removal.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　高级专家离休退休的待遇，按国家统一规定办理。符合以下情况的，退休费标准可以适当提高：  
(一)有重大贡献的高级专家，经省、市、自治区人民政府或中央、国家机关的部委批准，其退休费标准可以酌情提高5%—15%。提高标准后的退休费，不得超过本人原标准工资。  
(二)建国后从国外或者从香港、澳门、台湾回来定居工作的高级专家，其退休费均按建国后参加革命工作退休干部的最高标准发给。其中有重大贡献的，再按本条(一)项规定提高退休费。

**Article 4** The retirement benefits for senior experts shall be handled in accordance with uniform state provisions. Under the following circumstances, the standard of retirement pension may be appropriately increased:  
(1) For senior experts with major contributions, upon approval by the people's government of a province, municipality directly under the Central Government, or autonomous region, or by a ministry or commission of the Central Government or State organs, their retirement pension standard may be increased by 5% to 15% as appropriate. The retirement pension after the increase shall not exceed the original standard wage of the person.  
(2) For senior experts who, after the founding of the People's Republic of China, returned from abroad or from Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan to settle and work, their retirement pension shall be granted at the highest standard applicable to retired cadres who participated in revolutionary work after the founding of the People's Republic of China. If any of them have made major contributions, their retirement pension shall be further increased in accordance with paragraph (1) of this Article.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　高级专家离休退休后，如身体尚好，可以接受部门或单位的聘请，担任科学技术或文化艺术顾问，也可以直接承担业务工作。聘请离休退休高级专家应签订聘请合同，聘请条件和受聘人员待遇按国家有关规定办理。

**Article 5** After retirement, if in good health, senior experts may accept engagement by departments or units to serve as scientific, technological, cultural or art consultants, or may directly undertake operational work. Engagements of retired senior experts shall be formalized by a contract, and the terms of engagement and the treatment of the engaged personnel shall be handled in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各单位和各部门要认真执行国务院关于干部离休退休后政治、生活待遇的有关规定，对离休退休的高级专家应体贴关怀，热情爱护；要积极主动地帮助他们继续进行科学研究、资料整理、著书立说等工作，为他们的业务活动提供必要的方便。

**Article 6** All units and departments shall conscientiously implement the relevant provisions of the State Council concerning the political and livelihood treatment of retired cadres, and shall show consideration and care for retired senior experts with warmth and solicitude; they shall proactively assist them in continuing scientific research, data collation, book writing, and other work, and provide necessary conveniences for their professional activities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定自发布之日起施行。本规定发布以前，已经离休退休的高级专家，不再复职。

**Article 7** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of issuance. Senior experts who have already retired before the issuance of these Provisions shall not be reinstated.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本规定由劳动人事部负责解释。

**Article 8** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Labour and Personnel.
