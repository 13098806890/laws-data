# 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2003-06-20  
**生效日期**：2003-08-01

> (2003年6月18日国务院第12次常务会议通过　2003年6月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第381号公布　自2003年8月1日起施行) 第一条　为了对在城市生活无着的流浪、乞讨人员(以下简称流浪乞讨人员)实行救助，保障其基本生活权益，完善社会救助制度，制定本办法。 第二条　县级以上城市人民政府应当根据需要设立流浪乞讨人员救助站。救助站对流浪乞讨人员的救助是一项临时性社会救助措施。 第三条　县级以上城市人民政府应当采取积极措施及时救助流浪乞讨人员，并应当将救助工作所需经费列入财政预算，予以保障。 国家鼓励、支持社会组织和个人救助流浪乞讨人员。 第四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门负责流浪乞讨人员的救助工作，并对救助站进行指导、监督。 公安、卫生、交通、铁道、城管等部门应当在各自的职责范围内做好相关工作。 第五条　公安机关和其他有关行政机关的工作人员在执行职务时发现流浪乞讨人员的，应当告知其向救助站求助；对其中的残疾人、未成年人、老年人和行动不便的其他人员，还应当引导、护送到救助站。 第六条　向救助站求助的流浪乞讨人员，应当如实提供本人的姓名等基本情况并将随身携带物品在救助站登记，向救助站提出求助需求。 救助站对属于救助对象的求助人员，应当及时提供救助，不得拒绝；对不属于救助对象的求助人员，应当说明不予救助的理由。 第七条　救助站应当根据受助人员的需要提供下列救助： (一)提供符合食品卫生要求的食物； (二)提供符合基本条件的住处； (三)对在站内突发急病的，及时送医院救治； (四)帮助与其亲属或者所在单位联系；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了对在城市生活无着的流浪、乞讨人员(以下简称流浪乞讨人员)实行救助，保障其基本生活权益，完善社会救助制度，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of providing assistance to vagrants and beggars (hereinafter referred to as vagrants and beggars) who have no means of living in cities, safeguarding their basic living rights and interests, and improving the social assistance system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上城市人民政府应当根据需要设立流浪乞讨人员救助站。救助站对流浪乞讨人员的救助是一项临时性社会救助措施。

**Article 2** The people's governments of cities at or above the county level shall establish assistance stations for vagrants and beggars as needed. The assistance provided by assistance stations to vagrants and beggars is a temporary social assistance measure.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上城市人民政府应当采取积极措施及时救助流浪乞讨人员，并应当将救助工作所需经费列入财政预算，予以保障。  
国家鼓励、支持社会组织和个人救助流浪乞讨人员。

**Article 3** The people's governments of cities at or above the county level shall take active measures to promptly provide assistance to vagrants and beggars, and shall include the funds required for assistance work in the fiscal budget to ensure such funding.  
The State encourages and supports social organizations and individuals to provide assistance to vagrants and beggars.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门负责流浪乞讨人员的救助工作，并对救助站进行指导、监督。  
公安、卫生、交通、铁道、城管等部门应当在各自的职责范围内做好相关工作。

**Article 4** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the assistance work for vagrants and beggars, and shall provide guidance and supervision over assistance stations.  
The public security, health, transportation, railway, and urban management departments shall perform relevant work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公安机关和其他有关行政机关的工作人员在执行职务时发现流浪乞讨人员的，应当告知其向救助站求助；对其中的残疾人、未成年人、老年人和行动不便的其他人员，还应当引导、护送到救助站。

**Article 5** Where staff members of public security organs and other relevant administrative organs discover vagrants and beggars while performing their duties, they shall inform such persons to seek assistance from assistance stations; and with respect to disabled persons, minors, elderly persons, and other persons with impaired mobility among them, such staff members shall also guide and escort them to assistance stations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　向救助站求助的流浪乞讨人员，应当如实提供本人的姓名等基本情况并将随身携带物品在救助站登记，向救助站提出求助需求。  
救助站对属于救助对象的求助人员，应当及时提供救助，不得拒绝；对不属于救助对象的求助人员，应当说明不予救助的理由。

**Article 6** Vagrants and beggars who seek assistance at an assistance station shall truthfully provide their basic information such as their names and register the items they carry with them at the assistance station, and shall state their need for assistance to the assistance station.  
With respect to persons seeking assistance who fall within the scope of assistance targets, the assistance station shall promptly provide assistance and shall not refuse them; with respect to persons seeking assistance who do not fall within the scope of assistance targets, the assistance station shall explain the reasons for not providing assistance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　救助站应当根据受助人员的需要提供下列救助：  
(一)提供符合食品卫生要求的食物；  
(二)提供符合基本条件的住处；  
(三)对在站内突发急病的，及时送医院救治；  
(四)帮助与其亲属或者所在单位联系；  
(五)对没有交通费返回其住所地或者所在单位的，提供乘车凭证。

**Article 7** An assistance station shall, based on the needs of the recipients, provide the following assistance:  
(1) providing food that meets the requirements of food hygiene;  
(2) providing accommodation that meets basic conditions;  
(3) promptly sending to hospital for medical treatment those who suffer from sudden acute illnesses within the station;  
(4) helping them to contact their relatives or their employing entities;  
(5) providing travel vouchers to those who have no means to pay for transportation to return to their place of domicile or their employing entities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　救助站为受助人员提供的住处，应当按性别分室住宿，女性受助人员应当由女性工作人员管理。

**Article 8** The accommodation provided by an assistance station for recipients shall be arranged in separate rooms according to gender; female recipients shall be managed by female staff members.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　救助站应当保障受助人员在站内的人身安全和随身携带物品的安全，维护站内秩序。

**Article 9** An assistance station shall ensure the personal safety of recipients within the station and the safety of the items they carry with them, and shall maintain order within the station.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　救助站不得向受助人员、其亲属或者所在单位收取费用，不得以任何借口组织受助人员从事生产劳动。

**Article 10** An assistance station shall not charge fees to recipients, their relatives, or their employing entities, and shall not organize recipients to engage in productive labor under any pretext.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　救助站应当劝导受助人员返回其住所地或者所在单位，不得限制受助人员离开救助站。救助站对受助的残疾人、未成年人、老年人应当给予照顾；对查明住址的，及时通知其亲属或者所在单位领回；对无家可归的，由其户籍所在地人民政府妥善安置。

**Article 11** An assistance station shall advise recipients to return to their place of domicile or their employing entities, and shall not restrict recipients from leaving the assistance station. The assistance station shall provide appropriate care to disabled persons, minors, and elderly persons among recipients; for those whose addresses have been ascertained, it shall promptly notify their relatives or employing entities to take them back; for those who are homeless, the people's government of their place of household registration shall make proper arrangements for them.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　受助人员住所地的县级人民政府应当采取措施，帮助受助人员解决生产、生活困难，教育遗弃残疾人、未成年人、老年人的近亲属或者其他监护人履行抚养、赡养义务。

**Article 12** The people's government at the county level of the place of domicile of recipients shall take measures to help recipients solve production and living difficulties, and shall educate the close relatives or other guardians who have abandoned disabled persons, minors, or elderly persons to fulfill their obligations to raise or support them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　救助站应当建立、健全站内管理的各项制度，实行规范化管理。

**Article 13** An assistance station shall establish and improve various systems for station management and implement standardized management.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门应当加强对救助站工作人员的教育、培训和监督。  
救助站工作人员应当自觉遵守国家的法律法规、政策和有关规章制度，不准拘禁或者变相拘禁受助人员；不准打骂、体罚、虐待受助人员或者唆使他人打骂、体罚、虐待受助人员；不准敲诈、勒索、侵吞受助人员的财物；不准克扣受助人员的生活供应品；不准扣压受助人员的证件、申诉控告材料；不准任用受助人员担任管理工作；不准使用受助人员为工作人员干私活；不准调戏妇女。  
违反前款规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 14** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the education, training, and supervision of the staff members of assistance stations.  
Staff members of assistance stations shall conscientiously abide by the national laws, regulations, policies, and relevant rules and systems. They shall not detain or detain recipients in disguised form; shall not beat, scold, physically punish, or maltreat recipients or instigate others to do so; shall not extort, blackmail, or embezzle the property of recipients; shall not misappropriate the daily necessities supplied to recipients; shall not withhold the identification documents, petition or complaint materials of recipients; shall not assign recipients to management work; shall not use recipients to do private errands for staff members; and shall not molest women.  
Where the provisions of the preceding paragraph are violated and such violation constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; where it does not constitute a crime, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　救助站不履行救助职责的，求助人员可以向当地民政部门举报；民政部门经查证属实的，应当责令救助站及时提供救助，并对直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 15** Where an assistance station fails to perform its duty of assistance, the person seeking assistance may report the matter to the local civil affairs department; where the civil affairs department verifies the report to be true, it shall order the assistance station to promptly provide assistance and impose disciplinary sanctions on the directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　受助人员应当遵守法律法规。受助人员违反法律法规的，应当依法处理。  
受助人员应当遵守救助站的各项规章制度。

**Article 16** Recipients shall abide by laws and regulations. Where recipients violate laws and regulations, they shall be dealt with according to law.  
Recipients shall abide by the various rules and systems of the assistance station.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法的实施细则由国务院民政部门制定。

**Article 17** The detailed rules for the implementation of these Measures shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法自2003年8月1日起施行。1982年5月12日国务院发布的《城市流浪乞讨人员收容遣送办法》同时废止。

**Article 18** These Measures shall come into force on August 1, 2003. The Measures for the Shelter and Repatriation of Urban Vagrants and Beggars promulgated by the State Council on May 12, 1982 shall be abolished simultaneously.
