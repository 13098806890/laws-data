# 中华人民共和国考古涉外工作管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06

> (1990年12月31日国务院批准　1991年2月22日国家文物局令第1号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一条　为了加强考古涉外工作管理，保护我国的古代文化遗产，促进我国与外国的考古学术交流，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法适用于在中国境内陆地、内水和领海以及由中国管辖的其他海域，中国有关单位(以下简称中方)同外国组织和国际组织(以下简称外方)所进行的考古调查、勘探、发掘和与之有关的研究、科技保护及其他活动。 第三条　任何外国组织、国际组织在中国境内进行考古调查、勘探、发掘，都应当采取与中国合作的形式。 第四条　国家文物局统一管理全国考古涉外工作。 第五条　本办法下列用语的含义是： (一)考古调查是指以获取考古资料为目的，对古文化遗址、古墓葬、古建筑、石窟寺和其他地下、水下文物进行的考古记录和收集文物、自然标本等活动； (二)考古勘探是指为了解地下、水下历史文化遗存的性质、结构、范围等基本情况而进行的探测活动； (三)考古发掘是指以获取考古资料为目的，对古文化遗址、古墓葬和其他地下、水下文物进行的科学揭露、考古记录和收集文物、自然标本等活动； (四)考古记录是指系统的文字描述、测量、绘图、拓印、照相、拍摄电影和录像活动； (五)自然标本是指考古调查、勘探、发掘中所获取的自然遗存物。 第六条　中外合作进行考古调查、勘探、发掘活动，应当遵守下列原则： (一)合作双方共同实施考古调查、勘探、发掘项目，并组成联合考古队，由中方专家主持全面工作； (二)合作双方应当在中国境内共同整理考古调查、勘探、发掘所获取的资料并编写报告。报告由合作双方共同署名，中方有权优先发表； (三)合作考古调查、勘探、发掘活动所获取的文物、自然标本以及考古记录的原始资料，均归中国所有，并确保其安全；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强考古涉外工作管理，保护我国的古代文化遗产，促进我国与外国的考古学术交流，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of foreign-related archaeological work, protecting the ancient cultural relics of China, and promoting archaeological academic exchanges between China and foreign countries.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法适用于在中国境内陆地、内水和领海以及由中国管辖的其他海域，中国有关单位(以下简称中方)同外国组织和国际组织(以下简称外方)所进行的考古调查、勘探、发掘和与之有关的研究、科技保护及其他活动。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to archaeological surveys, explorations, excavations, and related research, scientific and technological protection, and other activities carried out in the land territory, internal waters, territorial sea, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of China by relevant Chinese entities (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese party) jointly with foreign organizations and international organizations (hereinafter referred to as the foreign party).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　任何外国组织、国际组织在中国境内进行考古调查、勘探、发掘，都应当采取与中国合作的形式。

**Article 3** Any foreign organization or international organization that conducts archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations within the territory of China shall adopt the form of cooperation with the Chinese party.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家文物局统一管理全国考古涉外工作。

**Article 4** The State Administration of Cultural Heritage shall uniformly administer the foreign-related archaeological work nationwide.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本办法下列用语的含义是：  
(一)考古调查是指以获取考古资料为目的，对古文化遗址、古墓葬、古建筑、石窟寺和其他地下、水下文物进行的考古记录和收集文物、自然标本等活动；  
(二)考古勘探是指为了解地下、水下历史文化遗存的性质、结构、范围等基本情况而进行的探测活动；  
(三)考古发掘是指以获取考古资料为目的，对古文化遗址、古墓葬和其他地下、水下文物进行的科学揭露、考古记录和收集文物、自然标本等活动；  
(四)考古记录是指系统的文字描述、测量、绘图、拓印、照相、拍摄电影和录像活动；  
(五)自然标本是指考古调查、勘探、发掘中所获取的自然遗存物。

**Article 5** For the purposes of these Measures, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Archaeological survey" means activities such as archaeological recording and collection of cultural relics and natural specimens conducted on ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, ancient buildings, cave temples, and other underground or underwater cultural relics for the purpose of obtaining archaeological data;  
(2) "Archaeological exploration" means detection activities carried out to ascertain the basic conditions such as the nature, structure, and extent of underground or underwater historical and cultural remains;  
(3) "Archaeological excavation" means activities such as scientific uncovering, archaeological recording, and collection of cultural relics and natural specimens conducted on ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, and other underground or underwater cultural relics for the purpose of obtaining archaeological data;  
(4) "Archaeological recording" means systematic activities of textual description, measurement, drawing, rubbing, photography, filming, and video recording;  
(5) "Natural specimens" means natural remains obtained during archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中外合作进行考古调查、勘探、发掘活动，应当遵守下列原则：  
(一)合作双方共同实施考古调查、勘探、发掘项目，并组成联合考古队，由中方专家主持全面工作；  
(二)合作双方应当在中国境内共同整理考古调查、勘探、发掘所获取的资料并编写报告。报告由合作双方共同署名，中方有权优先发表；  
(三)合作考古调查、勘探、发掘活动所获取的文物、自然标本以及考古记录的原始资料，均归中国所有，并确保其安全；  
(四)合作双方都应当遵守中国的法律、法规和规章。

**Article 6** Chinese-foreign cooperative archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations shall comply with the following principles:  
(1) The two cooperating parties shall jointly implement the archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation project and form a joint archaeological team, with the Chinese experts taking charge of the overall work;  
(2) The two cooperating parties shall jointly compile the data obtained from the archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation and prepare reports within the territory of China. The reports shall be jointly signed by both parties, and the Chinese party shall have the right of priority publication;  
(3) The cultural relics, natural specimens, and original archaeological records obtained from the cooperative archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation shall all belong to China, and their safety shall be ensured;  
(4) Both cooperating parties shall abide by the laws, regulations, and rules of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外方申请与中方合作进行考古调查、勘探、发掘时，应当按照下列规定向国家文物局提出书面申请：  
(一)合作意向；  
(二)对象、范围和目的；  
(三)组队方案；  
(四)工作步骤和文物的安全、技术保护措施等；  
(五)经费、设备的来源及管理方式；  
(六)意外事故的处理及风险承担。

**Article 7** When applying to cooperate with the Chinese party in conducting archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations, the foreign party shall submit a written application to the State Administration of Cultural Heritage in accordance with the following requirements:  
(1) Intention of cooperation;  
(2) Object, scope, and purpose;  
(3) Team composition plan;  
(4) Work procedures and measures for the safety and technical protection of cultural relics;  
(5) Sources and management of funds and equipment;  
(6) Handling of accidents and risk bearing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请合作考古调查、勘探、发掘的项目应当同时具备下列条件：  
(一)有利于促进中国文物保护和考古学研究，有利于促进国际文化学术交流；  
(二)中方已有一定的工作基础和研究成果，有从事该课题方向研究的专家；  
(三)外方应当是专业考古研究机构，有从事该课题方向或者相近方向研究的专家，并具有一定的实际考古工作经历；  
(四)有可靠的措施使发掘后的文物得到保护。

**Article 8** A project applying for cooperative archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations shall simultaneously meet the following conditions:  
(1) It shall be conducive to promoting the protection of Chinese cultural relics and archaeological research, and conducive to promoting international cultural and academic exchanges;  
(2) The Chinese party shall already have a certain work basis and research achievements, and have experts engaging in research in the relevant subject direction;  
(3) The foreign party shall be a professional archaeological research institution, having experts engaging in research in the relevant subject direction or a similar direction, and possessing some practical archaeological work experience;  
(4) There shall be reliable measures to ensure the protection of cultural relics after excavation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家文物局会同中国社会科学院对外方的申请进行初步审查后，由国家文物局按照国家有关规定送请国防、外交、公安、国家安全等有关部门审查，经审查合格的，由国家文物局报请国务院特别许可。

**Article 9** After the State Administration of Cultural Heritage, together with the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, conducts a preliminary review of the foreign party's application, the State Administration of Cultural Heritage shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, submit it to the relevant departments such as national defense, foreign affairs, public security, and state security for review. If the application is found to be qualified upon review, the State Administration of Cultural Heritage shall report it to the State Council for special permission.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　合作考古调查、勘探、发掘项目获得国务院特别许可的，合作双方应当就批准的合作项目的具体事宜签订协议书。

**Article 10** Where a cooperative archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation project has obtained the special permission of the State Council, the two cooperating parties shall sign an agreement on the specific matters of the approved cooperative project.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　合作考古调查、勘探、发掘的文物或者自然标本需要送到中国境外进行分析化验或者技术鉴定的，应当报经国家文物局批准。化验、鉴定完毕后，除测试损耗外，原标本应当全部运回中国境内。

**Article 11** Where cultural relics or natural specimens obtained from cooperative archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations need to be sent outside China for analysis, testing, or technical evaluation, approval from the State Administration of Cultural Heritage shall be obtained. After the analysis, testing, or evaluation is completed, all original specimens, except for those consumed in the testing, shall be transported back to the territory of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国留学人员(含本科生、研究生和进修生)以及外国研究学者在中国学习、研究考古学的批准期限在1年以上者，可以随同学习所在单位参加中方单独或者中外合作进行的考古调查、勘探、发掘活动。但须由其学习、研究所在单位征得考古调查、勘探、发掘单位的同意后，报国家文物局批准。

**Article 12** Foreign students (including undergraduates, postgraduates, and advanced students) and foreign research scholars whose approved period of studying or researching archaeology in China is more than one year may participate, together with the entity they are studying or researching with, in archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations conducted solely by the Chinese party or through Chinese-foreign cooperation. However, the entity where they study or conduct research shall first obtain the consent of the entity conducting the archaeological survey, exploration, or excavation, and then report to the State Administration of Cultural Heritage for approval.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国公民、外国组织和国际组织在中国境内参观尚未公开接待参观者的文物点，在开放地区的，需由文物点所在地的管理单位或者接待参观者的中央国家机关及其直属单位，在参观一个月以前向文物点所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政管理部门申报参观计划，经批准后方可进行；在未开放地区的，需由文物点所在地的管理单位或者接待参观者的中央国家机关及其直属单位，在参观一个月以前向文物点所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政管理部门申报参观计划，经批准并按照有关涉外工作管理规定向有关部门办理手续后方可进行。  
参观正在进行工作的考古发掘现场，接待单位须征求主持发掘单位的意见，经考古发掘现场所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物行政管理部门批准后方可进行。  
外国公民、外国组织和国际组织在参观过程中不得收集任何文物、自然标本和进行考古记录。

**Article 13** Where foreign citizens, foreign organizations, or international organizations wish to visit relics sites within the territory of China that are not yet open to visitors, if the site is in an open area, the management unit of the site or the central state organ that receives visitors and its directly subordinate units shall submit a visit plan to the administrative department for cultural relics under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the site is located at least one month before the visit, and the visit may proceed only after approval; if the site is in a closed area, the management unit of the site or the central state organ that receives visitors and its directly subordinate units shall submit a visit plan to the administrative department for cultural relics under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the site is located at least one month before the visit, and the visit may proceed only after approval and after completing the relevant procedures with the competent departments in accordance with the relevant regulations on foreign affairs.  
Where visiting an archaeological excavation site that is currently being worked, the receiving entity shall solicit the opinion of the entity in charge of the excavation, and the visit may proceed only after approval by the administrative department for cultural relics under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the archaeological excavation site is located.  
Foreign citizens, foreign organizations, and international organizations shall not collect any cultural relics or natural specimens or conduct archaeological recording during the visit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家文物局可以对合作考古调查、勘探、发掘工作实施检查，对工作质量达不到《田野考古工作规程》或者其他有关技术规范的要求的，责令暂停作业，限期改正。

**Article 14** The State Administration of Cultural Heritage may conduct inspections on the cooperative archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations. If the quality of the work fails to meet the requirements of the "Regulations on Field Archaeological Work" or other relevant technical specifications, it shall order the suspension of operations and require rectification within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反[本办法第六条](#art-6)、第七条、第八条、第十条、第十一条的规定，根据情节轻重，由国家文物局给予警告、暂停作业、撤销项目、罚款1000元至1万元、没收其非法所得文物或者责令赔偿损失。

**Article 15** Whoever violates the provisions of Articles 6, 7, 8, 10, or 11 of these Measures shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given a warning, ordered to suspend operations, have the project revoked, be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan, have the illegally obtained cultural relics confiscated, or be ordered to compensate for losses by the State Administration of Cultural Heritage.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　违反[本办法第十二条](#art-12)的规定，擅自接收外国留学人员、研究学者参加考古调查、勘探、发掘活动或者延长其工作期限的，国家文物局可以给予警告或者暂停该接收单位的团体考古发掘资格。

**Article 16** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 12 of these Measures by accepting foreign students or research scholars to participate in archaeological surveys, explorations, or excavations without authorization or by extending their work period shall be given a warning, or the State Administration of Cultural Heritage may suspend the group archaeological excavation qualification of the accepting entity.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国公民、外国组织和国际组织违反[本办法第十三条](#art-13)的规定，擅自参观文物点或者擅自收集文物、自然标本、进行考古记录的，文物行政管理部门可以停止其参观，没收其收集的文物、自然标本和考古记录。

**Article 17** Where foreign citizens, foreign organizations, or international organizations violate the provisions of Article 13 of these Measures by visiting relics sites without authorization or by collecting cultural relics, natural specimens, or conducting archaeological recording without authorization, the administrative department for cultural relics may stop their visit and confiscate the cultural relics, natural specimens, and archaeological records they have collected.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本办法的规定，构成违反治安管理的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where a violation of the provisions of these Measures constitutes a violation of public security administration, the violator shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the "Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security"; if the violation constitutes a crime, the violator shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　台湾、香港、澳门地区的考古团体与大陆合作进行考古调查、勘探、发掘，可以参照本办法执行。

**Article 19** The archaeological groups from the Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao regions may, with reference to these Measures, conduct cooperative archaeological surveys, explorations, and excavations with the mainland.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　文物研究、科技保护涉外工作的管理办法，由国家文物局根据本办法的原则制定。

**Article 20** The administrative measures for foreign-related work in cultural relic research and scientific and technological protection shall be formulated by the State Administration of Cultural Heritage in accordance with the principles of these Measures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本办法由国家文物局负责解释。

**Article 21** These Measures shall be interpreted by the State Administration of Cultural Heritage.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
