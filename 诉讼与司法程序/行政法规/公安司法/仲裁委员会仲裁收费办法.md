# 仲裁委员会仲裁收费办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1995-07-28  
**生效日期**：1995-09-01

> (1995年7月28日国务院发布　自1995年9月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范仲裁委员会的仲裁收费，制定本办法。 第二条　当事人申请仲裁，应当按照本办法的规定向仲裁委员会交纳仲裁费用，仲裁费用包括案件受理费和案件处理费。 第三条　案件受理费用于给付仲裁员报酬、维持仲裁委员会正常运转的必要开支。 第四条　申请人应当自收到仲裁委员会受理通知书之日起15日内，按照仲裁案件受理费表的规定预交案件受理费。被申请人在提出反请求的同时，应当按照仲裁案件受理费表的规定预交案件受理费。 仲裁案件受理费的具体标准由仲裁委员会在仲裁案件受理费表规定的幅度内确定，并报仲裁委员会所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府物价管理部门核准。 第五条　仲裁案件受理费表中的争议金额，以申请人请求的数额为准；请求的数额与实际争议金额不一致的，以实际争议金额为准。 申请仲裁时争议金额未确定的，由仲裁委员会根据争议所涉及权益的具体情况确定预先收取的案件受理费数额。 第六条　当事人预交案件受理费确有困难的，由当事人提出申请，经仲裁委员会批准，可以缓交。 当事人在本办法第四条第一款规定的期限内不预交案件受理费，又不提出缓交申请的，视为撤回仲裁申请。 第七条　案件处理费包括： (一)仲裁员因办理仲裁案件出差、开庭而支出的食宿费、交通费及其他合理费用； (二)证人、鉴定人、翻译人员等因出庭而支出的食宿费、交通费、误工补贴； (三)咨询、鉴定、勘验、翻译等费用； (四)复制、送达案件材料、文书的费用；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范仲裁委员会的仲裁收费，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to regulate the arbitration fees charged by arbitration commissions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人申请仲裁，应当按照本办法的规定向仲裁委员会交纳仲裁费用，仲裁费用包括案件受理费和案件处理费。

**Article 2** When a party applies for arbitration, it shall pay arbitration fees to the arbitration commission in accordance with these Measures. Arbitration fees include case acceptance fees and case handling fees.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　案件受理费用于给付仲裁员报酬、维持仲裁委员会正常运转的必要开支。

**Article 3** Case acceptance fees shall be used for paying remuneration to arbitrators and for necessary expenses to maintain the normal operation of the arbitration commission.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请人应当自收到仲裁委员会受理通知书之日起15日内，按照仲裁案件受理费表的规定预交案件受理费。被申请人在提出反请求的同时，应当按照仲裁案件受理费表的规定预交案件受理费。  
仲裁案件受理费的具体标准由仲裁委员会在仲裁案件受理费表规定的幅度内确定，并报仲裁委员会所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府物价管理部门核准。

**Article 4** The applicant shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of acceptance of the arbitration case from the arbitration commission, prepay the case acceptance fee in accordance with the Arbitration Case Acceptance Fee Schedule. When the respondent files a counterclaim, it shall also prepay the case acceptance fee in accordance with the Arbitration Case Acceptance Fee Schedule.  
The specific standards for case acceptance fees shall be determined by the arbitration commission within the range specified in the Arbitration Case Acceptance Fee Schedule, and shall be submitted to the price administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the arbitration commission is located for approval.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　仲裁案件受理费表中的争议金额，以申请人请求的数额为准；请求的数额与实际争议金额不一致的，以实际争议金额为准。  
申请仲裁时争议金额未确定的，由仲裁委员会根据争议所涉及权益的具体情况确定预先收取的案件受理费数额。

**Article 5** The amount in dispute in the Arbitration Case Acceptance Fee Schedule shall be based on the amount claimed by the applicant; if the claimed amount is inconsistent with the actual amount in dispute, the actual amount in dispute shall prevail.  
If the amount in dispute is not determined when applying for arbitration, the arbitration commission shall determine the amount of case acceptance fee to be collected in advance based on the specific circumstances of the rights and interests involved in the dispute.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人预交案件受理费确有困难的，由当事人提出申请，经仲裁委员会批准，可以缓交。  
当事人在[本办法第四条](#art-4)第一款规定的期限内不预交案件受理费，又不提出缓交申请的，视为撤回仲裁申请。

**Article 6** If a party genuinely has difficulty in prepaying the case acceptance fee, the party may submit an application and, with the approval of the arbitration commission, may defer payment.  
If a party fails to prepay the case acceptance fee within the time limit specified in paragraph 1 of Article 4 of these Measures and does not apply for deferment, it shall be deemed to have withdrawn the arbitration application.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　案件处理费包括：  
(一)仲裁员因办理仲裁案件出差、开庭而支出的食宿费、交通费及其他合理费用；  
(二)证人、鉴定人、翻译人员等因出庭而支出的食宿费、交通费、误工补贴；  
(三)咨询、鉴定、勘验、翻译等费用；  
(四)复制、送达案件材料、文书的费用；  
(五)其他应当由当事人承担的合理费用。  
本条款第(二)、(三)项规定的案件处理费，由提出申请的一方当事人预付。

**Article 7** Case handling fees include:  
(1) accommodation, meals, transportation, and other reasonable expenses incurred by arbitrators for travel and hearings in handling arbitration cases;  
(2) accommodation, meals, transportation, and compensation for lost working time incurred by witnesses, expert evaluators, interpreters, etc. for appearing in court;  
(3) fees for consultation, expert evaluation, inspection, translation, etc.;  
(4) fees for copying and serving case materials and documents;  
(5) other reasonable expenses that shall be borne by the parties.  
The case handling fees specified in items (2) and (3) of this Article shall be prepaid by the party making the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　案件处理费的收费标准按照国家有关规定执行；国家没有规定的，按照合理的实际支出收取。

**Article 8** The charging standards for case handling fees shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; where there are no such provisions, the fees shall be collected based on reasonable actual expenses.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　仲裁费用原则上由败诉的当事人承担；当事人部分胜诉、部分败诉的，由仲裁庭根据当事人各方责任大小确定其各自应当承担的仲裁费用的比例。当事人自行和解或者经仲裁庭调解结案的，当事人可以协商确定各自承担的仲裁费用的比例。  
仲裁庭应当在调解书或者裁决书中写明双方当事人最终应当支付的仲裁费用金额。

**Article 9** Arbitration fees shall in principle be borne by the losing party; if a party partially wins and partially loses, the arbitral tribunal shall determine the proportion of arbitration fees to be borne by each party based on their respective responsibilities. If the parties reach a settlement on their own or the case is concluded through mediation by the arbitral tribunal, the parties may negotiate and determine the proportion of arbitration fees to be borne by each party.  
The arbitral tribunal shall specify in the mediation statement or arbitral award the amount of arbitration fees that both parties shall ultimately pay.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依照[仲裁法第六十一条](../../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-61)的规定，仲裁庭同意重新仲裁的，仲裁委员会不得再行收取案件受理费。  
仲裁庭依法对裁决书中的文字、计算错误或者仲裁庭已经裁决但在裁决书中遗漏的事项作出补正，不得收费。

**Article 10** In accordance with Article 61 of the Arbitration Law, if the arbitral tribunal agrees to re-arbitration, the arbitration commission shall not collect the case acceptance fee again.  
If the arbitral tribunal makes corrections according to law to clerical or computational errors in the arbitral award or matters that have been decided but omitted from the arbitral award, no fee shall be charged.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请人经书面通知，无正当理由不到庭或者未经仲裁庭许可中途退庭，可以视为撤回仲裁申请，案件受理费、处理费不予退回。

**Article 11** If the applicant, after written notice, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons or leaves the hearing without the permission of the arbitral tribunal, it may be deemed to have withdrawn the arbitration application, and the case acceptance fee and handling fee shall not be refunded.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　仲裁委员会受理仲裁申请后，仲裁庭组成前，申请人撤回仲裁申请，或者当事人自行达成和解协议并撤回仲裁申请的，案件受理费应当全部退回。  
仲裁庭组成后，申请人撤回仲裁申请或者当事人自行达成和解协议并撤回仲裁申请的，应当根据实际情况酌情退回部分案件受理费。

**Article 12** After the arbitration commission accepts the arbitration application but before the formation of the arbitral tribunal, if the applicant withdraws the arbitration application, or the parties reach a settlement agreement on their own and withdraw the arbitration application, the case acceptance fee shall be fully refunded.  
After the formation of the arbitral tribunal, if the applicant withdraws the arbitration application or the parties reach a settlement agreement on their own and withdraw the arbitration application, part of the case acceptance fee shall be refunded as appropriate based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[本办法第五条](#art-5)、第十二条的规定同样适用于被申请人提出反请求的情形。

**Article 13** The provisions of Articles 5 and 12 of these Measures shall apply mutatis mutandis to the case where the respondent files a counterclaim.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　仲裁委员会收取仲裁案件受理费，应当使用省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门统一印制的收费票据，并按照国家有关规定，建立、健全财务核算制度，加强财务、收支管理，接受财政、审计、税务、物价等部门的监督。

**Article 14** When collecting arbitration case acceptance fees, the arbitration commission shall use fee receipts uniformly printed by the finance department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish and improve the financial accounting system, strengthen financial and revenue-expenditure management, and accept supervision from departments of finance, audit, taxation, pricing, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本办法自1995年9月1日起施行。
