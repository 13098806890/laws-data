# 仲裁委员会登记暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1995-07-28  
**生效日期**：1995-09-01

> (1995年7月28日国务院发布　自1995年9月1日起施行) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》(以下简称仲裁法)，制定本办法。 第二条　仲裁委员会的登记机关是省、自治区、直辖市的司法行政部门。 第三条　仲裁委员会可以在直辖市和省、自治区人民政府所在地的市设立，也可以根据需要在其他设区的市设立，不按行政区划层层设立。 设立仲裁委员会，应当向登记机关办理设立登记；未经设立登记的，仲裁裁决不具有法律效力。 办理设立登记，应当向登记机关提交下列文件： (一)设立仲裁委员会申请书； (二)组建仲裁委员会的市的人民政府设立仲裁委员会的文件； (三)仲裁委员会章程； (四)必要的经费证明； (五)仲裁委员会住所证明； (六)聘任的仲裁委员会组成人员的聘书副本； (七)拟聘任的仲裁员名册。 第四条　登记机关应当在收到本办法第三条第三款规定的文件之日起10日内，对符合设立条件的仲裁委员会予以设立登记，并发给登记证书；对符合设立条件，但所提供的文件不符合本办法第三条第三款规定的，在要求补正后予以登记；对不符合本办法第三条第一款规定的，不予登记。 第五条　仲裁委员会变更住所、组成人员，应当在变更后的10日内向登记机关备案，并向登记机关提交与变更事项有关的文件。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》(以下简称仲裁法)，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Arbitration Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　仲裁委员会的登记机关是省、自治区、直辖市的司法行政部门。

**Article 2** The registration authority for an arbitration commission shall be the judicial administrative department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　仲裁委员会可以在直辖市和省、自治区人民政府所在地的市设立，也可以根据需要在其他设区的市设立，不按行政区划层层设立。  
设立仲裁委员会，应当向登记机关办理设立登记；未经设立登记的，仲裁裁决不具有法律效力。  
办理设立登记，应当向登记机关提交下列文件：  
(一)设立仲裁委员会申请书；  
(二)组建仲裁委员会的市的人民政府设立仲裁委员会的文件；  
(三)仲裁委员会章程；  
(四)必要的经费证明；  
(五)仲裁委员会住所证明；  
(六)聘任的仲裁委员会组成人员的聘书副本；  
(七)拟聘任的仲裁员名册。

**Article 3** Arbitration commissions may be established in municipalities directly under the Central Government and in cities that are the seats of the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions, and may also be established in other cities divided into districts as needed. They shall not be established at each level of administrative divisions.  
An arbitration commission shall apply to the registration authority for establishment registration when it is established; without establishment registration, an arbitral award shall not have legal effect.  
To apply for establishment registration, the following documents shall be submitted to the registration authority:  
(1) a written application for establishment of an arbitration commission;  
(2) the document issued by the people's government of the city establishing the arbitration commission;  
(3) the charter of the arbitration commission;  
(4) the necessary proof of funding;  
(5) the proof of domicile of the arbitration commission;  
(6) the duplicate letters of appointment of the appointed members of the arbitration commission;  
(7) the name list of the proposed arbitrators.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　登记机关应当在收到[本办法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的文件之日起10日内，对符合设立条件的仲裁委员会予以设立登记，并发给登记证书；对符合设立条件，但所提供的文件不符合[本办法第三条](#art-3)第三款规定的，在要求补正后予以登记；对不符合[本办法第三条](#art-3)第一款规定的，不予登记。

**Article 4** The registration authority shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the documents specified in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of these Measures, grant establishment registration to an arbitration commission that meets the conditions for establishment and issue a registration certificate; for those that meet the conditions for establishment but the provided documents do not comply with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 3 of these Measures, the registration authority shall grant registration after requiring supplementation; for those that do not comply with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of these Measures, the registration authority shall not grant registration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　仲裁委员会变更住所、组成人员，应当在变更后的10日内向登记机关备案，并向登记机关提交与变更事项有关的文件。

**Article 5** An arbitration commission that changes its domicile or members shall, within 10 days after such change, file a record with the registration authority and submit to the registration authority the documents related to the change.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　仲裁委员会决议终止的，应当向登记机关办理注销登记。  
仲裁委员会办理注销登记，应当向登记机关提交下列文件或者证书：  
(一)注销登记申请书；  
(二)组建仲裁委员会的市的人民政府同意注销该仲裁委员会的文件；  
(三)有关机关确认的清算报告；  
(四)仲裁委员会登记证书。

**Article 6** Where an arbitration commission resolves to terminate, it shall apply to the registration authority for cancellation of registration.  
To apply for cancellation of registration, an arbitration commission shall submit the following documents or certificates to the registration authority:  
(1) a written application for cancellation of registration;  
(2) the document issued by the people's government of the city that established the arbitration commission, consenting to the cancellation of the arbitration commission;  
(3) the liquidation report confirmed by the relevant authority;  
(4) the registration certificate of the arbitration commission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　登记机关应当自收到[本办法第六条](#art-6)第二款规定的文件、证书之日起10日内，对符合终止条件的仲裁委员会予以注销登记，收回仲裁委员会登记证书。

**Article 7** The registration authority shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the documents and certificates specified in paragraph 2 of Article 6 of these Measures, grant cancellation of registration to an arbitration commission that meets the conditions for termination and withdraw the registration certificate of the arbitration commission.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　登记机关对仲裁委员会的设立登记、注销登记，自作出登记之日起生效，予以公告，并报国务院司法行政部门备案。  
仲裁委员会登记证书，由国务院司法行政部门负责印制。

**Article 8** The registration authority's establishment registration and cancellation registration of an arbitration commission shall take effect from the date on which the registration is made, and shall be publicly announced and reported to the judicial administrative department of the State Council for archival purposes.  
The registration certificate of an arbitration commission shall be printed by the judicial administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　仲裁法施行前在直辖市和省、自治区人民政府所在地的市以及其他设区的市设立的仲裁机构，应当依照仲裁法和国务院的有关规定重新组建，并依照本办法申请设立登记；未重新组建的，自仲裁法施行之日起届满1年时终止。  
仲裁法施行前设立的不符合仲裁法规定的其他仲裁机构，自仲裁法施行之日起终止。

**Article 9** Arbitration institutions established in municipalities directly under the Central Government and in cities that are the seats of the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions, as well as other cities divided into districts, prior to the coming into force of the Arbitration Law, shall be reorganized in accordance with the Arbitration Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council, and shall apply for establishment registration in accordance with these Measures; those that are not reorganized shall terminate upon the expiry of one year from the date of the coming into force of the Arbitration Law.  
Other arbitration institutions that were established prior to the coming into force of the Arbitration Law and do not comply with the provisions of the Arbitration Law shall terminate on the date of the coming into force of the Arbitration Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本办法自1995年9月1日起施行。

**Article 10** These Measures shall come into force as of September 1, 1995.
