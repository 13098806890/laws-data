### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对古生物化石的保护，促进古生物化石的科学研究和合理利用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the protection of paleontological fossils, promoting scientific research and rational utilization of paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事古生物化石发掘、收藏等活动以及古生物化石进出境，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称古生物化石，是指地质历史时期形成并赋存于地层中的动物和植物的实体化石及其遗迹化石。  
古猿、古人类化石以及与人类活动有关的第四纪古脊椎动物化石的保护依照国家文物保护的有关规定执行。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in activities such as excavation and collection of paleontological fossils, as well as the entry and exit of paleontological fossils, within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "paleontological fossils" refer to the body fossils and trace fossils of animals and plants that were formed in geological history periods and are preserved in strata.  
The protection of fossil apes, fossil humans, and Quaternary fossil vertebrates related to human activities shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions on the protection of cultural relics of the State.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域遗存的古生物化石属于国家所有。  
国有的博物馆、科学研究单位、高等院校和其他收藏单位收藏的古生物化石，以及单位和个人捐赠给国家的古生物化石属于国家所有，不因其收藏单位的终止或者变更而改变其所有权。

**Article 3** Paleontological fossils remaining in the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be owned by the State.  
Paleontological fossils collected by state-owned museums, scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education and other collection entities, as well as those donated to the State by entities or individuals, shall be owned by the State, and their ownership shall not be changed due to the termination or alteration of the collection entity.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对古生物化石实行分类管理、重点保护、科研优先、合理利用的原则。

**Article 4** The State shall adhere to the principles of classified management, prioritized protection, scientific research priority, and rational utilization with respect to paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院自然资源主管部门主管全国古生物化石保护工作。县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门主管本行政区域古生物化石保护工作。  
县级以上人民政府公安、市场监督管理等部门按照各自的职责负责古生物化石保护的有关工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall be in charge of the protection of paleontological fossils nationwide. The competent departments of natural resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the protection of paleontological fossils within their respective administrative regions.  
The public security departments, market supervision and administration departments, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant work concerning the protection of paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院自然资源主管部门负责组织成立国家古生物化石专家委员会。国家古生物化石专家委员会由国务院有关部门和中国古生物学会推荐的专家组成，承担重点保护古生物化石名录的拟定、国家级古生物化石自然保护区建立的咨询、古生物化石发掘申请的评审、重点保护古生物化石进出境的鉴定等工作，具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 6** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the establishment of the National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts. The National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts shall be composed of experts recommended by the relevant departments of the State Council and the Paleontological Society of China, and shall undertake the following tasks: formulation of the list of key protected paleontological fossils, consultation on the establishment of national paleontological fossil nature reserves, evaluation of applications for paleontological fossil excavation, and expert evaluation of the entry and exit of key protected paleontological fossils. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　按照在生物进化以及生物分类上的重要程度，将古生物化石划分为重点保护古生物化石和一般保护古生物化石。  
具有重要科学研究价值或者数量稀少的下列古生物化石，应当列为重点保护古生物化石：  
(一)已经命名的古生物化石种属的模式标本；  
(二)保存完整或者较完整的古脊椎动物实体化石；  
(三)大型的或者集中分布的高等植物化石、无脊椎动物化石和古脊椎动物的足迹等遗迹化石；  
(四)国务院自然资源主管部门确定的其他需要重点保护的古生物化石。  
重点保护古生物化石名录由国家古生物化石专家委员会拟定，由国务院自然资源主管部门批准并公布。

**Article 7** Paleontological fossils shall be classified into key protected paleontological fossils and general protected paleontological fossils based on their importance in biological evolution and biological taxonomy.  
The following paleontological fossils with significant scientific research value or rarity shall be listed as key protected paleontological fossils:  
(1) type specimens of named paleontological fossil species or genera;  
(2) intact or relatively intact body fossils of ancient vertebrates;  
(3) large or concentrated fossil plants of higher plants, invertebrate fossils, and trace fossils such as footprints of ancient vertebrates;  
(4) other paleontological fossils determined by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council that need key protection.  
The list of key protected paleontological fossils shall be formulated by the National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts and approved and published by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　重点保护古生物化石集中的区域，应当建立国家级古生物化石自然保护区；一般保护古生物化石集中的区域，同时该区域已经发现重点保护古生物化石的，应当建立地方级古生物化石自然保护区。建立古生物化石自然保护区的程序，依照《中华人民共和国自然保护区条例》的规定执行。  
建立国家级古生物化石自然保护区，应当征求国家古生物化石专家委员会的意见。

**Article 8** National paleontological fossil nature reserves shall be established in areas where key protected paleontological fossils are concentrated; local paleontological fossil nature reserves shall be established in areas where general protected paleontological fossils are concentrated and where key protected paleontological fossils have been found. The procedures for establishing paleontological fossil nature reserves shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Nature Reserves.  
When establishing a national paleontological fossil nature reserve, the opinions of the National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts shall be solicited.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对古生物化石保护工作的领导，将古生物化石保护工作所需经费列入本级财政预算。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门开展古生物化石保护知识的宣传教育，增强公众保护古生物化石的意识，并按照国家有关规定对在古生物化石保护工作中做出突出成绩的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over the protection of paleontological fossils, and shall include the funds required for the protection of paleontological fossils in their respective fiscal budgets.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to carry out publicity and education on the knowledge of paleontological fossil protection, enhance public awareness of paleontological fossil protection, and, in accordance with relevant State regulations, reward entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection of paleontological fossils.

### 第二章　古生物化石发掘

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　因科学研究、教学、科学普及或者对古生物化石进行抢救性保护等需要，方可发掘古生物化石。发掘古生物化石的，应当符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第二款规定的条件，并依照本条例的规定取得批准。  
本条例所称发掘，是指有一定工作面，使用机械或者其他动力工具挖掘古生物化石的活动。

**Article 10** Paleontological fossils may be excavated only for the purposes of scientific research, teaching, science popularization, or salvage protection of paleontological fossils. Any person that excavates paleontological fossils shall satisfy the conditions specified in paragraph 2 of Article 11 of these Regulations and obtain approval in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "excavation" refers to the activity of excavating paleontological fossils using mechanical or other power tools with a certain working face.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在国家级古生物化石自然保护区内发掘古生物化石，或者在其他区域发掘重点保护古生物化石的，应当向国务院自然资源主管部门提出申请并取得批准；在国家级古生物化石自然保护区外发掘一般保护古生物化石的，应当向古生物化石所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请并取得批准。  
申请发掘古生物化石的单位应当符合下列条件，并在提出申请时提交其符合下列条件的证明材料以及发掘项目概况、发掘方案、发掘标本保存方案和发掘区自然生态条件恢复方案：  
(一)有3名以上拥有古生物专业或者相关专业技术职称，并有3年以上古生物化石发掘经历的技术人员(其中至少有1名技术人员具有古生物专业高级职称并作为发掘活动的领队)；  
(二)有符合古生物化石发掘需要的设施、设备；  
(三)有与古生物化石保护相适应的处理技术和工艺；  
(四)有符合古生物化石保管需要的设施、设备和场所。

**Article 11** Any person that excavates paleontological fossils within a national paleontological fossil nature reserve or excavates key protected paleontological fossils in other areas shall file an application with the competent department of natural resources under the State Council and obtain approval; any person that excavates general protected paleontological fossils outside a national paleontological fossil nature reserve shall file an application with the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council where the paleontological fossils are located and obtain approval.  
An entity applying to excavate paleontological fossils shall satisfy the following conditions and, when filing the application, shall submit evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the following conditions, as well as an overview of the excavation project, an excavation plan, a plan for preserving the excavated specimens, and a plan for restoring the natural ecological conditions of the excavation area:  
(1) having more than three technicians who possess professional or relevant technical titles in paleontology and have more than three years of experience in paleontological fossil excavation (with at least one technician having a senior professional title in paleontology and serving as the leader of the excavation activity);  
(2) having facilities and equipment suitable for paleontological fossil excavation;  
(3) having treatment techniques and processes compatible with the protection of paleontological fossils;  
(4) having facilities, equipment, and premises suitable for the storage of paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起3个工作日内将申请材料送国家古生物化石专家委员会。国家古生物化石专家委员会应当自收到申请材料之日起10个工作日内出具书面评审意见。评审意见应当作为是否批准古生物化石发掘的重要依据。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内完成审查，对申请单位符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第二款规定条件，同时古生物化石发掘方案、发掘标本保存方案和发掘区自然生态条件恢复方案切实可行的，予以批准；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
国务院自然资源主管部门批准古生物化石发掘申请前，应当征求古生物化石所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门的意见；批准发掘申请后，应当将批准发掘古生物化石的情况通报古生物化石所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门。

**Article 12** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall, within 3 working days from the date of accepting an application, submit the application materials to the National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts. The National Committee of Paleontological Fossil Experts shall, within 10 working days from the date of receiving the application materials, issue a written evaluation opinion. The evaluation opinion shall serve as an important basis for whether or not to approve the excavation of paleontological fossils.  
The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall complete the review within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, and shall grant approval if the applicant entity satisfies the conditions specified in paragraph 2 of Article 11 of these Regulations and if the paleontological fossil excavation plan, the plan for preserving the excavated specimens, and the plan for restoring the natural ecological conditions of the excavation area are feasible; if the conditions are not met, the applicant entity shall be notified in writing with reasons stated.  
Before approving an application for paleontological fossil excavation, the competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall solicit the opinions of the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council where the paleontological fossils are located; after approving the excavation application, it shall notify the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council where the paleontological fossils are located of the approved excavation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门受理古生物化石发掘申请的，应当依照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第二款规定的期限和要求进行审查、批准，并听取古生物专家的意见。

**Article 13** Where the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council accepts an application for paleontological fossil excavation, it shall conduct review and approval in accordance with the time limit and requirements specified in paragraph 2 of Article 12 of these Regulations, and shall listen to the opinions of paleontological experts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发掘古生物化石的单位，应当按照批准的发掘方案进行发掘；确需改变发掘方案的，应当报原批准发掘的自然资源主管部门批准。

**Article 14** An entity that excavates paleontological fossils shall carry out excavation in accordance with the approved excavation plan; if it is truly necessary to change the excavation plan, it shall report to the competent department of natural resources that originally approved the excavation for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发掘古生物化石的单位，应当自发掘或者科学研究、教学等活动结束之日起30日内，对发掘的古生物化石登记造册，作出相应的描述与标注，并移交给批准发掘的自然资源主管部门指定的符合条件的收藏单位收藏。

**Article 15** An entity that excavates paleontological fossils shall, within 30 days from the date of completion of excavation or activities such as scientific research or teaching, register the excavated paleontological fossils in a ledger, make corresponding descriptions and markings, and deliver them to a qualified collection entity designated by the competent department of natural resources that approved the excavation for storage.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　进行区域地质调查或者科学研究机构、高等院校等因科学研究、教学需要零星采集古生物化石标本的，不需要申请批准，但是，应当在采集活动开始前将采集时间、采集地点、采集数量等情况书面告知古生物化石所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门。采集的古生物化石的收藏应当遵守本条例的规定。  
本条例所称零星采集，是指使用手持非机械工具在地表挖掘极少量古生物化石，同时不对地表和其他资源造成影响的活动。

**Article 16** Where regional geological surveys are conducted, or where scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, and others need to sporadically collect paleontological fossil specimens for scientific research or teaching purposes, no application for approval is required. However, prior to the commencement of the collection activity, the collector shall inform the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council where the paleontological fossils are located in writing of the collection time, collection location, and quantity collected. The storage of the collected paleontological fossils shall comply with the provisions of these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "sporadic collection" refers to the activity of using hand-held non-mechanical tools to excavate a very small number of paleontological fossils on the surface without causing any impact on the surface or other resources.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国人、外国组织因中外合作进行科学研究需要，方可在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域发掘古生物化石。发掘古生物化石的，应当经国务院自然资源主管部门批准，采取与符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第二款规定条件的中方单位合作的方式进行，并遵守本条例有关古生物化石发掘、收藏、进出境的规定。

**Article 17** Foreign nationals or foreign organizations may excavate paleontological fossils within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China only when necessary for Sino-foreign cooperative scientific research. Any person that excavates paleontological fossils shall obtain approval from the competent department of natural resources under the State Council, shall carry out the excavation through cooperation with a Chinese entity that satisfies the conditions specified in paragraph 2 of Article 11 of these Regulations, and shall comply with the provisions of these Regulations concerning the excavation, storage, and entry and exit of paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　单位和个人在生产、建设等活动中发现古生物化石的，应当保护好现场，并立即报告所在地县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门接到报告后，应当在24小时内赶赴现场，并在7日内提出处理意见。确有必要的，可以报请当地人民政府通知公安机关协助保护现场。发现重点保护古生物化石的，应当逐级上报至国务院自然资源主管部门，由国务院自然资源主管部门提出处理意见。  
生产、建设等活动中发现的古生物化石需要进行抢救性发掘的，由提出处理意见的自然资源主管部门组织符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)第二款规定条件的单位发掘。

**Article 18** Where entities or individuals discover paleontological fossils during production, construction, or other activities, they shall protect the site and immediately report to the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
Upon receiving the report, the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall arrive at the site within 24 hours and issue a treatment opinion within 7 days. If truly necessary, they may request the local people's government to notify the public security organ to assist in protecting the site. Where key protected paleontological fossils are discovered, the matter shall be reported level by level up to the competent department of natural resources under the State Council, which shall issue a treatment opinion.  
Where paleontological fossils discovered during production, construction, or other activities require salvage excavation, the competent department of natural resources that issued the treatment opinion shall organize entities that satisfy the conditions specified in paragraph 2 of Article 11 of these Regulations to carry out the excavation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强对古生物化石发掘活动的监督检查，发现未经依法批准擅自发掘古生物化石，或者不按照批准的发掘方案发掘古生物化石的，应当依法予以处理。

**Article 19** The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of paleontological fossil excavation activities; if they discover any unauthorized excavation of paleontological fossils without approval according to law, or excavation that does not comply with the approved excavation plan, they shall handle it according to law.

### 第三章　古生物化石收藏

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　古生物化石的收藏单位，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)有固定的馆址、专用展室、相应面积的藏品保管场所；  
(二)有相应数量的拥有相关研究成果的古生物专业或者相关专业的技术人员；  
(三)有防止古生物化石自然损毁的技术、工艺和设备；  
(四)有完备的防火、防盗等设施、设备和完善的安全保卫等管理制度；  
(五)有维持正常运转所需的经费。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强对古生物化石收藏单位的管理和监督检查。

**Article 20** A paleontological fossil collection entity shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) having a fixed site, dedicated exhibition rooms, and storage premises of appropriate area for the collections;  
(2) having a corresponding number of technical personnel who possess relevant research achievements in paleontology or related disciplines;  
(3) having techniques, processes, and equipment to prevent natural damage to paleontological fossils;  
(4) having complete fire prevention, anti-theft, and other facilities and equipment, and comprehensive management systems for security protection, etc.;  
(5) having funds necessary for normal operation.  
The competent departments of natural resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management and supervision and inspection of paleontological fossil collection entities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院自然资源主管部门负责建立全国的重点保护古生物化石档案和数据库。县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门负责建立本行政区域的重点保护古生物化石档案和数据库。  
收藏单位应当建立本单位收藏的古生物化石档案，并如实对收藏的古生物化石作出描述与标注。

**Article 21** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be responsible for establishing the archives and database of nationally protected paleontological fossils. The competent departments of natural resources of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for establishing the archives and database of the key protected paleontological fossils within their respective administrative regions.  
The collection entity shall establish archives of the paleontological fossils in its collection, and shall truthfully describe and label the collected paleontological fossils.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励单位和个人将其收藏的重点保护古生物化石捐赠给符合条件的收藏单位收藏。  
除收藏单位之间转让、交换、赠与其收藏的重点保护古生物化石外，其他任何单位和个人不得买卖重点保护古生物化石。买卖一般保护古生物化石的，应当在县级以上地方人民政府指定的场所进行。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 22** The State encourages entities and individuals to donate the key protected paleontological fossils in their collection to eligible collection entities for collection.  
Except for the transfer, exchange, or donation of key protected paleontological fossils among collection entities, no other entity or individual may buy or sell key protected paleontological fossils. The buying and selling of generally protected paleontological fossils shall be conducted at a site designated by the local people's government at or above the county level. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国有收藏单位不得将其收藏的重点保护古生物化石转让、交换、赠与给非国有收藏单位或者个人。  
任何单位和个人不得将其收藏的重点保护古生物化石转让、交换、赠与、质押给外国人或者外国组织。

**Article 23** A state-owned collection entity shall not transfer, exchange, or donate the key protected paleontological fossils in its collection to a non-state-owned collection entity or individual.  
No entity or individual may transfer, exchange, donate, or pledge the key protected paleontological fossils in its collection to any foreigner or foreign organization.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　收藏单位之间转让、交换、赠与其收藏的重点保护古生物化石的，应当在事后向国务院自然资源主管部门备案。具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 24** Where a collection entity transfers, exchanges, or donates the key protected paleontological fossils in its collection to another collection entity, it shall file it for record with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council afterwards. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公安、市场监督管理、海关等部门应当对依法没收的古生物化石登记造册、妥善保管，并在结案后30个工作日内移交给同级自然资源主管部门。接受移交的自然资源主管部门应当出具接收凭证，并将接收的古生物化石交符合条件的收藏单位收藏。  
国有收藏单位不再收藏的一般保护古生物化石，应当按照国务院自然资源主管部门的规定处理。

**Article 25** The public security, market supervision and management, customs and other departments shall register and keep in good custody the paleontological fossils confiscated according to law, and shall transfer them to the competent department of natural resources at the same level within 30 working days after the case is closed. The competent department of natural resources that accepts the transfer shall issue a receipt and deliver the received paleontological fossils to a qualified collection entity for collection.  
The generally protected paleontological fossils no longer collected by a state-owned collection entity shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of natural resources of the State Council.

### 第四章　古生物化石进出境

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　未命名的古生物化石不得出境。  
重点保护古生物化石符合下列条件之一，经国务院自然资源主管部门批准，方可出境：  
(一)因科学研究需要与国外有关研究机构进行合作的；  
(二)因科学、文化交流需要在境外进行展览的。  
一般保护古生物化石经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门批准，方可出境。

**Article 26** Unnamed paleontological fossils may not be taken out of the country.  
Key protected paleontological fossils may be taken out of the country only after approval by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council if they meet one of the following conditions:  
(1) Cooperation with foreign research institutions is needed for scientific research;  
(2) Exhibition abroad is needed for scientific or cultural exchange.  
Generally protected paleontological fossils may be taken out of the country only after approval by the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请古生物化石出境的，应当向国务院自然资源主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门提出出境申请，并提交出境古生物化石的清单和照片。出境申请应当包括申请人的基本情况和古生物化石的出境地点、出境目的、出境时间等内容。  
申请重点保护古生物化石出境的，申请人还应当提供外方合作单位的基本情况和合作科学研究合同或者展览合同，以及古生物化石的应急保护预案、保护措施、保险证明等材料。

**Article 27** Any applicant applying to take paleontological fossils out of the country shall submit an exit application to the competent department of natural resources of the State Council or the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and submit a list and photos of the paleontological fossils to be taken out. The exit application shall include the basic information of the applicant and the place of exit, purpose of exit, time of exit, etc.  
For an application to take key protected paleontological fossils out of the country, the applicant shall also provide the basic information of the foreign cooperating entity, the scientific research cooperation contract or exhibition contract, and the emergency protection plan, protection measures, insurance certificates, etc.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　申请重点保护古生物化石出境的，国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起3个工作日内将申请材料送国家古生物化石专家委员会。国家古生物化石专家委员会应当自收到申请材料之日起10个工作日内对申请出境的重点保护古生物化石进行鉴定，确认古生物化石的种属、数量和完好程度，并出具书面鉴定意见。鉴定意见应当作为是否批准重点保护古生物化石出境的重要依据。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，符合规定条件的，作出批准出境的决定；不符合规定条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 28** For an application to take key protected paleontological fossils out of the country, the competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, within 3 working days from the date of acceptance, deliver the application materials to the National Expert Committee on Paleontological Fossils. The National Expert Committee on Paleontological Fossils shall, within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the application materials, conduct an expert evaluation on the key protected paleontological fossils to be taken out of the country, confirm the species, quantity, and degree of intactness, and issue a written expert evaluation opinion. The evaluation opinion shall serve as an important basis for whether to approve the exit of key protected paleontological fossils.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall complete the examination within 20 working days from the date of acceptance, and if the prescribed conditions are met, it shall make a decision to approve the exit; if not, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　申请一般保护古生物化石出境的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，同意出境的，作出批准出境的决定；不同意出境的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 29** For an application to take generally protected paleontological fossils out of the country, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the examination within 20 working days from the date of acceptance. If it approves the exit, it shall make a decision to approve the exit; if not, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　古生物化石出境批准文件的有效期为90日；超过有效期出境的，应当重新提出出境申请。  
重点古生物化石在境外停留的期限一般不超过6个月；因特殊情况确需延长境外停留时间的，应当在境外停留期限届满60日前向国务院自然资源主管部门申请延期。延长期限最长不超过6个月。

**Article 30** The validity period of the approval document for taking paleontological fossils out of the country shall be 90 days; if the fossils are taken out after the expiry of the validity period, a new exit application shall be filed.  
The period of stay of key paleontological fossils abroad shall generally not exceed 6 months; if it is truly necessary to extend the stay abroad due to special circumstances, an application for extension shall be filed with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council 60 days before the expiry of the stay period. The extension period shall not exceed 6 months at most.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　经批准出境的重点保护古生物化石出境后进境的，申请人应当自办结进境海关手续之日起5日内向国务院自然资源主管部门申请进境核查。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起3个工作日内将申请材料送国家古生物化石专家委员会。国家古生物化石专家委员会应当自收到申请材料之日起5个工作日内对出境后进境的重点保护古生物化石进行鉴定，并出具书面鉴定意见。鉴定意见应当作为重点保护古生物化石进境核查结论的重要依据。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当自受理申请之日起15个工作日内完成核查，作出核查结论；对确认为非原出境重点保护古生物化石的，责令申请人追回原出境重点保护古生物化石。

**Article 31** Where key protected paleontological fossils that have been approved for exit are later brought into the country, the applicant shall, within 5 days from the date of completing the customs entry procedures, apply to the competent department of natural resources of the State Council for entry verification.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, within 3 working days from the date of acceptance, deliver the application materials to the National Expert Committee on Paleontological Fossils. The National Expert Committee on Paleontological Fossils shall, within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the application materials, conduct an expert evaluation on the key protected paleontological fossils that were taken out and then brought back in, and issue a written expert evaluation opinion. The evaluation opinion shall serve as an important basis for the conclusion of entry verification of key protected paleontological fossils.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall complete the verification within 15 working days from the date of acceptance and make a verification conclusion; if it confirms that the fossils are not the original key protected paleontological fossils that were taken out, it shall order the applicant to recover the original key protected paleontological fossils that were taken out.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　境外古生物化石临时进境的，应当交由海关加封，由境内有关单位或者个人自办结进境海关手续之日起5日内向国务院自然资源主管部门申请核查、登记。国务院自然资源主管部门核查海关封志完好无损的，逐件进行拍照、登记。  
临时进境的古生物化石进境后出境的，由境内有关单位或者个人向国务院自然资源主管部门申请核查。国务院自然资源主管部门应当依照[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)第二款规定的程序，自受理申请之日起15个工作日内完成核查，对确认为原临时进境的古生物化石的，批准出境。  
境内单位或者个人从境外取得的古生物化石进境的，应当向海关申报，按照海关管理的有关规定办理进境手续。

**Article 32** Where paleontological fossils from abroad are temporarily brought into the country, they shall be sealed by the customs, and the relevant entity or individual within the country shall, within 5 days from the date of completing the customs entry procedures, apply to the competent department of natural resources of the State Council for verification and registration. If the competent department of natural resources of the State Council verifies that the customs seal is intact, it shall take photos and register each piece.  
Where paleontological fossils temporarily brought into the country are later to be taken out again, the relevant entity or individual within the country shall apply to the competent department of natural resources of the State Council for verification. The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, in accordance with the procedures prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 31 of these Regulations, complete the verification within 15 working days from the date of acceptance, and if it confirms that they are the original temporarily imported paleontological fossils, it shall approve the exit.  
Where an entity or individual within the country brings paleontological fossils obtained abroad into the country, it shall declare to the customs and go through the entry formalities in accordance with the relevant customs administration provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　运送、邮寄、携带古生物化石出境的，应当如实向海关申报，并向海关提交国务院自然资源主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门的出境批准文件。  
对有理由怀疑属于古生物化石的物品出境的，海关可以要求有关单位或者个人向国务院自然资源主管部门或者出境口岸所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府自然资源主管部门申请办理是否属于古生物化石的证明文件。

**Article 33** Where paleontological fossils are transported, mailed, or carried out of the country, a truthful declaration shall be made to the customs, and the customs shall be submitted with the exit approval document issued by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council or the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where there are reasonable grounds to suspect that an item being taken out of the country is a paleontological fossil, the customs may require the relevant entity or individual to apply to the competent department of natural resources of the State Council or the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the exit port for a certificate confirming whether it is a paleontological fossil.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家对违法出境的古生物化石有权进行追索。  
国务院自然资源主管部门代表国家具体负责追索工作。国务院外交、公安、海关等部门应当配合国务院自然资源主管部门做好违法出境古生物化石的追索工作。

**Article 34** The State has the right to recover the paleontological fossils that have been taken out of the country illegally.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be specifically responsible for the recovery work on behalf of the State. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the public security, customs and other departments under the State Council shall cooperate with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council in carrying out the recovery of illegally exported paleontological fossils.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例规定批准古生物化石发掘的；  
(二)未依照本条例规定批准古生物化石出境的；  
(三)发现违反本条例规定的行为不予查处，或者接到举报不依法处理的；  
(四)其他不依法履行监督管理职责的行为。

**Article 35** Where the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level or its staff members commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to approve the excavation of paleontological fossils in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Failing to approve the exit of paleontological fossils in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Failing to investigate and deal with acts violating these Regulations upon discovery, or failing to handle reports according to law;  
(4) Other acts of failing to perform supervisory and administrative duties according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　单位或者个人有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令停止发掘，限期改正，没收发掘的古生物化石，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经批准发掘古生物化石的；  
(二)未按照批准的发掘方案发掘古生物化石的。  
有前款第(二)项行为，情节严重的，由批准古生物化石发掘的自然资源主管部门撤销批准发掘的决定。

**Article 36** Where any entity or individual commits any of the following acts, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease excavation, rectify within a time limit, confiscate the excavated paleontological fossils, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Excavating paleontological fossils without approval;  
(2) Excavating paleontological fossils not in accordance with the approved excavation plan.  
Where the act under subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph is serious, the competent department of natural resources that approved the excavation shall revoke the decision to approve the excavation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　古生物化石发掘单位未按照规定移交发掘的古生物化石的，由批准古生物化石发掘的自然资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正，或者造成古生物化石损毁的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where the entity excavating paleontological fossils fails to hand over the excavated paleontological fossils in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of natural resources that approved the excavation shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, or causes damage to the paleontological fossils, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　古生物化石收藏单位不符合收藏条件收藏古生物化石的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；已严重影响其收藏的重点保护古生物化石安全的，由国务院自然资源主管部门指定符合条件的收藏单位代为收藏，代为收藏的费用由原收藏单位承担。

**Article 38** Where a paleontological fossil collection entity collects paleontological fossils without meeting the collection conditions, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the safety of the key protected paleontological fossils in its collection has been seriously affected, the competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall designate a qualified collection entity to collect them on behalf of the original entity, and the cost of the collection on behalf shall be borne by the original collection entity.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　古生物化石收藏单位未按照规定建立本单位收藏的古生物化石档案的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，没收有关古生物化石，并处2万元的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a paleontological fossil collection entity fails to establish archives of the paleontological fossils in its collection in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the relevant paleontological fossils shall be confiscated, and a fine of 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　单位或者个人违反规定买卖重点保护古生物化石的，由市场监督管理部门责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where any entity or individual violates the provisions by buying or selling key protected paleontological fossils, the market supervision and management department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国有收藏单位将其收藏的重点保护古生物化石违法转让、交换、赠与给非国有收藏单位或者个人的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门对国有收藏单位处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Where a state-owned collection unit illegally transfers, exchanges, or gives the key protected paleontological fossils in its collection to a non-state-owned collection unit or individual, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the state-owned collection unit, and shall give sanctions according to law to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　单位或者个人将其收藏的重点保护古生物化石转让、交换、赠与、质押给外国人或者外国组织的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期追回，对个人处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对单位处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a unit or individual transfers, exchanges, gives, or pledges the key protected paleontological fossils in its or his/her collection to a foreigner or foreign organization, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the recovery within a time limit, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the individual, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on the unit; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　单位或者个人未取得批准运送、邮寄、携带古生物化石出境的，由海关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where a unit or individual transports, mails, or carries paleontological fossils out of the country without obtaining approval, the Customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门、其他有关部门的工作人员，或者国有的博物馆、科学研究单位、高等院校、其他收藏单位以及发掘单位的工作人员，利用职务上的便利，将国有古生物化石非法占为己有的，依法给予处分，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门追回非法占有的古生物化石；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where a staff member of the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level, or of other relevant departments, or a staff member of a state-owned museum, scientific research institution, institution of higher education, other collection unit or excavation unit, takes advantage of his/her position to illegally possess state-owned paleontological fossils as his/her own, sanctions shall be given according to law, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall recover the illegally possessed paleontological fossils; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2011年1月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2011.
