# 中华人民共和国对外国籍船舶管理规则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1979-09-18

> (国务院批准　1979年9月18日交通部发布) 总则 第一条　为维护中华人民共和国的主权，维持港口和沿海水域的秩序，保证航行安全，防止水域污染，特制定本规则。 第二条　在中华人民共和国港口和沿海水域航行的外国籍船舶(以下简称船舶)应遵守本规则以及中华人民共和国一切有关法令、规章和规定。中华人民共和国政府设置在港口的港务监督认为有必要对船舶进行检查时，船舶应接受检查。 本规则所称沿海水域是指属于中华人民共和国的内水和领海以及国家规定的管辖水域。 第一章　进出港和航行 第三条　船长或船舶所有人应在船舶预定到达港口一星期之前，通过外轮代理公司填具规定的表报，向港务监督办理进口申请批准手续，并在到达港口之前二十四小时(航程不足二十四小时的，在驶离前一港口时)，将预计到港时间，前、后吃水等情况通过外轮代理公司向港务监督报告。如预计到港时间有变化，应随时报告。船舶在航行途中，因遇险、发生故障、船员或旅客患急病等特殊情况，需临时进港或返航，应事先向港务监督报告。 第四条　船舶进出港口或在港内航行、移泊，必须由港务监督指派引航员引航。有关引航的具体事项，应按照中华人民共和国交通部颁发的《海港引航工作规定》办理。 第五条　船舶抵港后，应即呈报进口报告书及其它有关表报，同时交验船舶证书及有关文书，并接受检查。船舶出港前，应呈报出口报告书及其它有关表报，经检查发给出口许可证后，才可出口。 第六条　船舶上的武器、弹药，应在船舶抵港后由港务监督予以封存。无线电报发射机、无线电话发射机、火箭信号、火焰信号、信号枪，只有在危急情况下才可以使用，但在使用后必须向港务监督报告。 第七条　港内禁止射击、游泳、钓鱼、鸣放鞭炮或焰火以及其它危及港口安全秩序的行为。 第八条　船舶有下列情况之一者，港务监督有权在一定期间内禁止其出港或令其停航、改航、返航： 一、处于不适航状态； 二、违反中华人民共和国的法律或规章； 三、发生海损事故；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为维护中华人民共和国的主权，维持港口和沿海水域的秩序，保证航行安全，防止水域污染，特制定本规则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated to safeguard the sovereignty of the People's Republic of China, maintain order in ports and coastal waters, ensure navigation safety, and prevent water pollution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国港口和沿海水域航行的外国籍船舶(以下简称船舶)应遵守本规则以及中华人民共和国一切有关法令、规章和规定。中华人民共和国政府设置在港口的港务监督认为有必要对船舶进行检查时，船舶应接受检查。  
本规则所称沿海水域是指属于中华人民共和国的内水和领海以及国家规定的管辖水域。

**Article 2** Foreign vessels (hereinafter referred to as vessels) navigating in the ports and coastal waters of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Rules and all relevant laws, regulations, and provisions of the People's Republic of China. When the Harbour Superintendency Administration established by the Government of the People's Republic of China at the port deems it necessary to inspect a vessel, the vessel shall submit to the inspection.  
The term "coastal waters" in these Rules means the internal waters and territorial sea belonging to the People's Republic of China, as well as the jurisdictional waters prescribed by the State.

### 第一章　进出港和航行

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　船长或船舶所有人应在船舶预定到达港口一星期之前，通过外轮代理公司填具规定的表报，向港务监督办理进口申请批准手续，并在到达港口之前二十四小时(航程不足二十四小时的，在驶离前一港口时)，将预计到港时间，前、后吃水等情况通过外轮代理公司向港务监督报告。如预计到港时间有变化，应随时报告。船舶在航行途中，因遇险、发生故障、船员或旅客患急病等特殊情况，需临时进港或返航，应事先向港务监督报告。

**Article 3** The master or the owner of a vessel shall, one week before the vessel's scheduled arrival at the port, complete the prescribed forms through a ship-chartering agency and apply to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for entry approval. Twenty-four hours before arrival at the port (or, if the voyage is less than twenty-four hours, at the time of departure from the previous port), the vessel shall report the estimated time of arrival, forward and aft drafts, and other relevant information through the ship-chartering agency to the Harbour Superintendency Administration. If there is any change in the estimated time of arrival, it shall be reported promptly. If, during the voyage, a vessel needs to temporarily enter a port or return to port due to special circumstances such as distress, breakdown, or acute illness of crew members or passengers, it shall report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration in advance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶进出港口或在港内航行、移泊，必须由港务监督指派引航员引航。有关引航的具体事项，应按照中华人民共和国交通部颁发的《海港引航工作规定》办理。

**Article 4** Vessels entering or leaving a port or navigating or shifting berths within a port must be piloted by a pilot assigned by the Harbour Superintendency Administration. Specific matters concerning pilotage shall be handled in accordance with the Provisions on Harbour Pilotage issued by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　船舶抵港后，应即呈报进口报告书及其它有关表报，同时交验船舶证书及有关文书，并接受检查。船舶出港前，应呈报出口报告书及其它有关表报，经检查发给出口许可证后，才可出口。

**Article 5** Upon arrival at the port, a vessel shall promptly submit an entry report and other relevant forms, and at the same time present the vessel's certificates and relevant documents for inspection and undergo examination. Before departure from the port, the vessel shall submit a clearance report and other relevant forms, and may depart only after an inspection and the issuance of a clearance permit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶上的武器、弹药，应在船舶抵港后由港务监督予以封存。无线电报发射机、无线电话发射机、火箭信号、火焰信号、信号枪，只有在危急情况下才可以使用，但在使用后必须向港务监督报告。

**Article 6** Weapons and ammunition on board a vessel shall be sealed up by the Harbour Superintendency Administration after the vessel's arrival at the port. Radiotelegraph transmitters, radiotelephone transmitters, rocket signals, flare signals, and signal guns may be used only in emergency situations, but must be reported to the Harbour Superintendency Administration after use.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　港内禁止射击、游泳、钓鱼、鸣放鞭炮或焰火以及其它危及港口安全秩序的行为。

**Article 7** In ports, shooting, swimming, fishing, setting off firecrackers or fireworks, and other acts that endanger the safety and order of the port are prohibited.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶有下列情况之一者，港务监督有权在一定期间内禁止其出港或令其停航、改航、返航：  
一、处于不适航状态；  
二、违反中华人民共和国的法律或规章；  
三、发生海损事故；  
四、未缴付应承担的款项，又未提供适当担保者；  
五、其它需要禁止航行的情况。

**Article 8** The Harbour Superintendency Administration shall have the power to prohibit a vessel from leaving the port or order it to stop, alter course, or return to port within a certain period if the vessel is in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It is in an unseaworthy condition;  
(2) It violates the laws or regulations of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) It has been involved in a maritime casualty;  
(4) It has not paid the amounts due and has not provided appropriate security;  
(5) Other circumstances requiring a prohibition of navigation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　航行在中华人民共和国港口和沿海水域的船舶，不得进行危害中华人民共和国安全和权益的活动，并应遵守有关海峡、水道、航线和禁航区的规定。

**Article 9** Vessels navigating in the ports and coastal waters of the People's Republic of China shall not engage in activities that endanger the security and rights of the People's Republic of China, and shall comply with the provisions concerning straits, waterways, routes, and prohibited navigation areas.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船舶在港内不得以危及其它船舶和港口设施安全的速度航行。

**Article 10** Vessels in ports shall not navigate at speeds that endanger the safety of other vessels or port facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶附属的艇(筏)，除了救生以外，不准在港内航行。

**Article 11** Boats (rafts) attached to vessels shall not navigate within the port except for life-saving purposes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　船舶在港内航行、移泊时，船上的艇(筏)、吊货杆和舷梯等，不得伸出舷外。

**Article 12** When a vessel is navigating or shifting berths within the port, the boats (rafts), cargo booms, gangways, and the like on board shall not project over the side.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　需要进入中华人民共和国对外轮开放的港口避风或临时停泊的船舶应向港务监督申请批准，申请内容包括：船名、呼号、国籍、船公司名称、出发港、目的港、船位、航速、吃水、船体颜色、烟囱颜色和标志，并应在指定的地点避风。  
船舶如需在中华人民共和国对外轮开放的港口以外的地点避风或临时停泊，除办理上述申请批准手续外，还应遵守下列规定：  
一、及时向就近的港务监督报告抛锚时间、位置和驶离时间；  
二、遵守当地有关部门的规定，接受检查和询问，并听从指挥；  
三、未经当地有关部门批准，船上人员不得登陆，不得装卸货物。

**Article 13** Vessels that need to enter a port open to foreign vessels of the People's Republic of China for shelter or temporary anchorage shall apply to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for approval. The application shall include: the vessel's name, call sign, nationality, name of the shipping company, port of departure, port of destination, position, speed, draft, hull colour, funnel colour and markings, and shall take shelter at a designated location.  
If a vessel needs to take shelter or anchor temporarily at a location other than a port open to foreign vessels of the People's Republic of China, in addition to completing the above application and approval procedures, the following provisions shall also be complied with:  
(1) Promptly report to the nearest Harbour Superintendency Administration the time and position of anchoring and the time of departure;  
(2) Comply with the provisions of the relevant local authorities, accept inspection and inquiry, and follow instructions;  
(3) Without approval from the local authorities, persons on board shall not go ashore, and cargo shall not be loaded or discharged.

### 第二章　停泊

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶在港内停泊，必须留有足以保证船舶安全操纵的船员值班，遇有台风警报等紧急情况，全体船员必须立即回船采取防范、应急等措施。

**Article 14** When a vessel is at anchor or berthed in the port, it must keep a crew on watch sufficient to ensure safe manoeuvring of the vessel. In the event of an emergency such as a typhoon warning, all crew members must immediately return on board and take preventive and emergency measures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船舶在船员、旅客和其他人员上下之处设置的舷梯必须稳固，并有栏杆或攀索，软梯必须牢固安全，夜间应有足够的照明。

**Article 15** Gangways placed by a vessel at points where crew members, passengers, and other persons embark or disembark must be secure and provided with handrails or manropes; rope ladders must be strong and safe; and adequate lighting shall be provided at night.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　船舶需要活车时，必须注意尾部周围环境，在不危及其他船舶和港口设施安全的情况下才可进行。

**Article 16** When a vessel needs to turn its propeller, it must pay attention to the surrounding environment astern and may do so only if it does not endanger the safety of other vessels or port facilities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　停泊在港内的船舶，其两舷可能影响其他船舶、码头或人员上下的出水口必须加盖复罩。

**Article 17** Vessels at anchor or berthed in the port must cover the overboard discharges on both sides that may affect other vessels, docks, or the embarkation and disembarkation of persons.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　船舶的灯光不得影响其他船舶的航行安全，船上射向航道的强灯光，应予以遮蔽。

**Article 18** The lights of a vessel shall not affect the navigation safety of other vessels. Strong lights on board directed towards the fairway shall be screened.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　船舶对装卸操作应提供安全良好的条件，装卸设备应具有合格证书，保持良好的技术状态。

**Article 19** A vessel shall provide safe and good conditions for cargo handling operations. The cargo handling equipment shall have valid certificates and be maintained in good technical condition.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　船舶进行下列事项，应事先向港务监督申请批准：  
一、拆修锅炉、主机、锚机、舵机、电台；  
二、试航、试车；  
三、放艇(筏)进行救生演习；  
四、烧焊(进船厂修理的除外)，或者在甲板上明火作业；  
五、悬挂彩灯。

**Article 20** A vessel shall apply to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for approval in advance before carrying out the following matters:  
(1) Dismantling or repairing boilers, main engines, windlasses, steering gears, or radio stations;  
(2) Trial trips or engine tests;  
(3) Launching boats (rafts) for lifeboat drills;  
(4) Welding (except when carried out in a shipyard for repairs) or performing open-flame work on deck;  
(5) Hanging coloured lights.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　船舶熏蒸，应采取严密的安全措施，并应悬挂港口规定的信号。

**Article 21** Fumigation on board a vessel shall be carried out with strict safety measures, and the signals prescribed by the port shall be displayed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　为了维护港口和船舶的安全，需要在港内的船舶移泊或提前、推迟开航，船舶应遵守港务监督的决定。

**Article 22** Where it is necessary for vessels in the port to shift berths or to advance or postpone departure for the safety of the port and vessels, the vessels shall comply with the decisions of the Harbour Superintendency Administration.

### 第三章　信号和通讯

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　船舶在中华人民共和国港口和沿海水域航行、停泊，白天应悬挂船籍国的国旗，进出港口和移泊应加挂船名呼号旗和港口规定的有关信号。

**Article 23** Vessels navigating or at anchor in the ports and coastal waters of the People's Republic of China shall display the national flag of the vessel's registry during the daytime, and when entering or leaving the port or shifting berths, shall additionally display the vessel's name and call sign flag and the relevant signals prescribed by the port.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　船舶在进出港口和锚泊时，应注意港口信号台的呼号和信号，在使用视觉信号时，应遵守中华人民共和国沿海港口信号规定。沿海港口未曾规定的信号，应依照《国际信号规则》办理。

**Article 24** When entering or leaving a port or at anchor, vessels shall pay attention to the call signs and signals of the port signal station. When using visual signals, they shall comply with the provisions on coastal port signals of the People's Republic of China. Signals not prescribed by the coastal ports shall be handled in accordance with the International Code of Signals.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　船舶在港内除因航行安全必须外，不得随意鸣放声号。需要试笛时，应事先向港务监督报告。

**Article 25** Vessels in ports shall not sound whistle signals unnecessarily except when required for navigation safety. If it is necessary to test the whistle, the Harbour Superintendency Administration shall be notified in advance.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　船舶在港内使用甚高频无线电话，应遵守中华人民共和国交通部颁发的《关于外轮使用甚高频无线电话暂行办法》。

**Article 26** The use of VHF radiotelephones by vessels in ports shall comply with the Interim Measures on the Use of VHF Radiotelephones by Foreign Vessels issued by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　危险货物

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　船舶装卸、载运危险货物，应悬挂规定的信号，遵守有关危险货物运输管理的规定，采取必要的安全措施，特别是性能相抵触的货物，不许混装，严禁爆炸物品与发火物、易燃物品装载于同一舱内。

**Article 27** When loading, discharging, or carrying dangerous goods, vessels shall display the prescribed signals, comply with the regulations on the administration of dangerous goods transport, and take necessary safety measures. In particular, goods with incompatible properties shall not be mixed, and it is strictly prohibited to load explosives together with igniters or flammable articles in the same compartment.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　船舶载运爆炸物品、剧毒物品、放射性物品、压缩气体和液化气体、氧化剂、自燃物品、遇水燃烧物品、易燃液体、易燃固体和酸性腐蚀物品等一级烈性危险货物，应详细列具品名、性质、包装数量和装载位置，并且附具危险货物性质说明书，在预定到达港口三天之前，通过外轮代理公司向港务监督申请办理签证，经许可后才可进港、起卸或者过境。出口船舶载运上述危险货物，应在开始装载的三天以前，申请办理签证，经许可后才可装运。

**Article 28** Vessels carrying Class A highly dangerous goods, such as explosives, highly toxic substances, radioactive substances, compressed gases and liquefied gases, oxidizers, spontaneously combustible substances, substances that become combustible on contact with water, flammable liquids, flammable solids, and acidic corrosive substances, shall list in detail the names, properties, packaging quantities, and stowage positions, and attach a description of the properties of the dangerous goods. An application for endorsement shall be submitted to the Harbour Superintendency Administration through a ship-chartering agency at least three days before the scheduled arrival at the port, and the vessel may enter the port, discharge, or transit only after obtaining permission. For outbound vessels carrying the above dangerous goods, an application for endorsement shall be made at least three days before the commencement of loading, and loading may proceed only after permission is granted.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　船舶申请签发装运出口危险货物安全装载证明书，应在开始装载三天之前向港务监督提出书面申请，写明危险货物的品名、性质、包装、数量、装载位置(并且附具货物装载图)、中途港和目的港等事项并在港务监督指定的泊位进行装载。

**Article 29** A vessel applying for the issuance of a certificate of safe stowage for outbound dangerous goods shall submit a written application to the Harbour Superintendency Administration at least three days before the commencement of loading, stating the names, properties, packaging, quantities, stowage positions (with a stowage plan attached), intermediate ports, and port of destination, and shall load at the berth designated by the Harbour Superintendency Administration.

### 第五章　航道保护

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　船舶航行应遵守航行规定，维护航行秩序，如船舶发生意外事故有沉没危险时，应立即向港务监督报告，并尽力采取有效措施，驶离航道，避免妨碍交通和危及其他船舶。如果船舶已经沉没，船方应及时在沉没地点设置临时信号标志。

**Article 30** Vessels shall comply with navigation regulations and maintain navigational order. If a vessel is involved in an accident and is in danger of sinking, it shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration and use its best efforts to take effective measures to move out of the fairway, so as to avoid obstructing traffic and endangering other vessels. If the vessel has already sunk, the shipowner shall promptly set up temporary signal marks at the location of the sinking.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对沉没在港口或沿海水域的船舶或其他物体的打捞，均按照《中华人民共和国打捞沉船、沉物管理办法》办理。港务监督可视具体情况，通知沉船沉物所有人限期打捞清除，或立即组织打捞或解体清除，全部责任和费用应由沉船沉物所有人承担。

**Article 31** The salvage of vessels or other objects sunk in ports or coastal waters shall be handled in accordance with the Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of the Salvage of Sunken Vessels and Sunken Objects. The Harbour Superintendency Administration may, in light of the specific circumstances, notify the owner of the sunken vessel or object to salvage and remove it within a time limit, or promptly organize salvage or dismantling and removal. All liability and expenses shall be borne by the owner of the sunken vessel or object.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　船舶发现或捞获沉、浮物体，应报告或送交港务监督处理，由港务监督酌情给予奖励。

**Article 32** A vessel that discovers or recovers sunken or floating objects shall report or deliver them to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for disposal, and the Harbour Superintendency Administration may, as it deems appropriate, give a reward.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　船舶在港内需要倾倒垃圾等废弃物，应显示港口规定的信号招用垃圾船(车)。

**Article 33** If a vessel in port needs to discharge garbage or other waste, it shall display the signals prescribed by the port to summon a garbage boat (truck).

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　船舶应爱护航道设备和助航标志，如损坏了助航标志、港口建筑或其他设施，应立即向港务监督报告，并应负责恢复原状或偿付恢复原状所需费用。

**Article 34** Vessels shall take care of fairway equipment and aids to navigation. If aids to navigation, port buildings, or other facilities are damaged, the vessel shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration and shall be liable for restoring them to their original condition or reimbursing the cost of restoration.

### 第六章　防止污染

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在中华人民共和国的港口和沿海水域，禁止船舶任意排放油类、油性混合物，以及其他有害的污染物质和废弃物。

**Article 35** In the ports and coastal waters of the People's Republic of China, the discharge of oil, oily mixtures, and other harmful pollutants and wastes by vessels is prohibited.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　船舶排放压舱水、洗舱水、舱底水，必须向港务监督申请批准。如果船舶来自有疫情的港口，应经过卫生检疫机关卫生处理。装运危险货物和其他有害污染物船舱的污水、洗舱水，应经有关卫生部门鉴定合格后，方可在指定地点排放。

**Article 36** Vessels must apply to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for approval before discharging ballast water, tank washing water, or bilge water. If the vessel comes from a port where an epidemic exists, it shall undergo sanitary treatment by the health quarantine authorities. Wastewater and tank washing water from compartments carrying dangerous goods or other harmful pollutants may be discharged at designated locations only after being tested and found up to standard by the relevant health authorities.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　凡油轮和使用燃油的船舶，应备有油类记录簿，并且按照记录簿各项规定及时如实记载。

**Article 37** All oil tankers and vessels using fuel oil shall keep an oil record book and make timely and truthful entries in accordance with the provisions of the record book.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　如船舶在港口和沿海水域发生污染事故，应将经过情况分别记入油类记录簿和航海日志，并立即向港务监督报告，同时必须采取有效的措施防止扩散。如需采用化学剂处理，应提供化学成份说明书，向港务监督申请批准。

**Article 38** If a pollution accident occurs involving a vessel in ports or coastal waters, the circumstances shall be recorded in the oil record book and the deck log book respectively, and the Harbour Superintendency Administration shall be immediately notified. At the same time, effective measures must be taken to prevent the spread of pollution. If chemical agents are to be used for treatment, a description of the chemical composition shall be provided, and approval shall be obtained from the Harbour Superintendency Administration.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本章未列事项，按照中华人民共和国有关防止水域污染的规定办理。

**Article 39** Matters not covered in this Chapter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the People's Republic of China on the prevention of water pollution.

### 第七章　消防和救助

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　严禁在货舱以及易于引起船舶火警的场所吸烟和弄火。

**Article 40** Smoking and the use of open flames are strictly prohibited in cargo holds and other places on board where they may cause a fire on the vessel.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　船舶加油和油船装卸作业，应采取严密的防火安全措施。

**Article 41** Strict fire prevention safety measures shall be taken during bunkering operations and tanker loading and unloading operations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　船舶在港内进行烧焊等工程，应事先清理周围环境，采取严密防范措施，配备消防设备，并且在施工前后进行检查。对油舱及其邻近部位还必须卸完全部油料，清除残油，彻底通风，排除其内部易燃气体，并取得合格证明之后，才可以烧焊。

**Article 42** When a vessel carries out welding or similar work in the port, it shall first clean the surrounding area, take strict preventive measures, equip itself with fire-fighting apparatus, and conduct inspections before and after the work. For oil tanks and adjacent parts, all oil must be completely discharged, residual oil removed, thorough ventilation carried out to expel flammable gases inside, and a certificate of fitness obtained, only then may welding be carried out.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　船舶失火或发生海难，应立即将出事地点和本船吨位、吃水、载货、受损和需要某种援助等情况报告港务监督。

**Article 43** In the event of a fire or marine casualty on board a vessel, the vessel shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration the location of the incident, the vessel's tonnage, draft, cargo, damage sustained, and the type of assistance required.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　港务监督在必要时，可动员和指挥在港内或沿海水域的船舶参加救助遇难船舶，在不影响本船安全的情况下，被动员的船舶有责任尽力救助。

**Article 44** The Harbour Superintendency Administration may, when necessary, mobilise and direct vessels in the port or coastal waters to participate in the rescue of a vessel in distress. The mobilised vessel shall have the duty to use its best efforts to render assistance, provided that this does not endanger its own safety.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　港务监督或救助部门的负责人员到达现场，遇难船舶的船长应立即报告失事情况及已经采取的措施，提供救助必需的资料和方便，并且可以提出有关救助的建议。港务监督为维护安全秩序作出的决定，有关方面必须遵守。

**Article 45** When the responsible person of the Harbour Superintendency Administration or the rescue department arrives at the scene, the master of the vessel in distress shall immediately report the circumstances of the casualty and the measures already taken, provide the information and facilities necessary for rescue, and may put forward suggestions regarding the rescue. Decisions made by the Harbour Superintendency Administration to maintain safety and order must be complied with by the parties concerned.

### 第八章　海损事故

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　船舶发生海损事故，应尽速用电报或无线电话向港务监督报出扼要报告。在港区以外发出的海损事故，船长应在船舶进入第一港口四十八小时内，向港务监督递交海损事故报告书；在港区内发生的海损事故，船长应在二十四小时内向港务监督递交海损事故报告书。

**Article 46** When a vessel is involved in a maritime casualty, it shall, as soon as possible, report a brief account to the Harbour Superintendency Administration by telegram or radiotelephone. For a maritime casualty occurring outside a harbour area, the master shall submit a written report on the maritime casualty to the Harbour Superintendency Administration within forty-eight hours after the vessel enters the first port. For a maritime casualty occurring within a harbour area, the master shall submit the written report within twenty-four hours.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　船舶在中华人民共和国港口和沿海水域造成人命、财产损害事故时，应积极救助受害的船舶和人员，及时向港务监督报告，并接受调查和处理。如果肇事者见危不救，隐匿逃遁，将从严处理。

**Article 47** When a vessel in the ports and coastal waters of the People's Republic of China causes an accident involving loss of life or damage to property, it shall actively rescue the affected vessel and persons, promptly report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration, and submit to investigation and handling. If the perpetrator fails to render assistance when seeing danger, or conceals or flees, the case shall be dealt with severely.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　船舶发生船员死亡事故，应立即向港务监督报告。在港内由于船方或港方人员的过失，造成对方损害或伤亡事故等，应保留现场，双方都应及时向港务监督报告。如果发生纠纷，当事的任何一方，都可以向港务监督申请调查处理，涉及刑事范围的，由中华人民共和国司法机关处理。

**Article 48** In the event of a death of a crew member on board a vessel, the vessel shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration. In the port, if damage or casualties are caused to the other party due to the fault of the shipowner or port personnel, the scene shall be preserved, and both parties shall promptly report to the Harbour Superintendency Administration. If a dispute arises, either party may apply to the Harbour Superintendency Administration for investigation and handling. Matters involving criminal offences shall be handled by the judicial organs of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本章未列事项，按照中华人民共和国交通部颁发的《海损事故调查和处理规则》办理。

**Article 49** Matters not covered in this Chapter shall be handled in accordance with the Rules for the Investigation and Handling of Maritime Accidents issued by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China.

### 第九章　违章处罚

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　凡违反本规则以及中华人民共和国一切有关法令、规章和规定者，港务监督得按其性质、情节分别给予警告、罚款等处分，性质恶劣、情节严重者移交司法机关处理。

**Article 50** Any person who violates these Rules or any relevant laws, regulations, and provisions of the People's Republic of China may be given sanctions such as a warning or a fine by the Harbour Superintendency Administration according to the nature and circumstances of the violation. Those whose violations are of a serious nature or involve grave circumstances shall be referred to the judicial authorities for handling.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　受处分者如果对所受处分不服，可以在接到通告的次日起十五日之内，向中华人民共和国港务监督局提出申诉。但在没有变更决定之前，原处分仍为有效。

**Article 51** A person subjected to a disciplinary sanction who disagrees with the sanction may file a petition with the Bureau of Harbor Superintendency of the People's Republic of China within fifteen days from the day following receipt of the notice. However, the original sanction shall remain effective until a decision on the change is made.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　关于船舶避碰，本规则和中华人民共和国其他有关规定中未列事项，依照中华人民共和国施行的《国际海上避碰规则》办理。

**Article 52** For matters not covered by these Rules or other relevant regulations of the People's Republic of China concerning collision avoidance of vessels, the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea as implemented by the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本规则由中华人民共和国国务院批准施行，中华人民共和国交通部一九五七年三月十二日颁发的《中华人民共和国对外国籍船舶进出港口管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 53** These Rules are approved and shall come into force by the State Council of the People's Republic of China. The Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Administration of Entry and Departure of Foreign Vessels at Ports promulgated by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China on March 12, 1957 shall be abolished simultaneously.
