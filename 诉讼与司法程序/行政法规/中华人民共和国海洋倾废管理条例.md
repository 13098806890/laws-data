# 中华人民共和国海洋倾废管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：1985-04-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为实施《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，严格控制向海洋倾倒废弃物，防止对海洋环境的污染损害，保持生态平衡，保护海洋资源，促进海洋事业的发展，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of implementing the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, strictly controlling the dumping of wastes into the sea, preventing pollution damage to the marine environment, maintaining ecological balance, protecting marine resources, and promoting the development of marine undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例中的“倾倒”，是指利用船舶、航空器、平台及其他载运工具，向海洋处置废弃物和其他物质；向海洋弃置船舶、航空器、平台和其他海上人工构造物，以及向海洋处置由于海底矿物资源的勘探开发及与勘探开发相关的海上加工所产生的废弃物和其他物质。  
“倾倒”不包括船舶、航空器及其他载运工具和设施正常操作产生的废弃物的排放。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "dumping" means the disposal of wastes and other substances into the sea by means of vessels, aircraft, platforms and other means of transport; the abandonment of vessels, aircraft, platforms and other man-made structures at sea; and the disposal into the sea of wastes and other substances arising from the exploration and development of seabed mineral resources and offshore processing related to such exploration and development.  
"Dumping" does not include the discharge of wastes generated during the normal operation of vessels, aircraft, other means of transport and installations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于：  
一、向中华人民共和国的内海、领海、大陆架和其他管辖海域倾倒废弃物和其他物质；  
二、为倾倒的目的，在中华人民共和国陆地或港口装载废弃物和其他物质；  
三、为倾倒的目的，经中华人民共和国的内海、领海及其他管辖海域运送废弃物和其他物质；  
四、在中华人民共和国管辖海域焚烧处置废弃物和其他物质。  
海洋石油勘探开发过程中产生的废弃物，按照《中华人民共和国海洋石油勘探开发环境保护管理条例》的规定处理。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to:  
1. the dumping of wastes and other substances into the inland seas, territorial seas, continental shelf and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China;  
2. the loading of wastes and other substances on land or in ports of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of dumping;  
3. the transportation of wastes and other substances through the inland seas, territorial seas and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of dumping; and  
4. the incineration and disposal of wastes and other substances in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
Wastes generated during the exploration and development of offshore oil shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Environmental Protection in the Exploration and Development of Offshore Oil.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海洋倾倒废弃物的主管部门是中华人民共和国国家海洋局及其派出机构(简称“主管部门”，下同)。

**Article 4** The competent authority for the dumping of wastes into the sea shall be the State Oceanic Administration of the People's Republic of China and its dispatched agencies (hereinafter referred to as the "competent authority").

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海洋倾倒区由主管部门商同有关部门，按科学、合理、安全和经济的原则划出，报国务院批准确定。

**Article 5** Ocean dumping areas shall be designated by the competent authority in consultation with the relevant departments in accordance with the principles of scientific, reasonable, safe and economical operation, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and determination.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　需要向海洋倾倒废弃物的单位，应事先向主管部门提出申请，按规定的格式填报倾倒废弃物申请书，并附报废弃物特性和成分检验单。  
主管部门在接到申请书之日起两个月内予以审批。对同意倾倒者应发给废弃物倾倒许可证。  
任何单位和船舶、航空器、平台及其他载运工具，未依法经主管部门批准，不得向海洋倾倒废弃物。

**Article 6** Any unit that needs to dump wastes into the sea shall, in advance, submit an application to the competent authority, fill in an application form for dumping wastes in the prescribed format, and attach a certificate of inspection of the characteristics and composition of the wastes.  
The competent authority shall, within two months from the date of receiving the application, make a decision on approval. For those approved to dump, a waste dumping permit shall be issued.  
No unit, vessel, aircraft, platform or other means of transport may dump wastes into the sea without having been approved by the competent authority according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国的废弃物不得运至中华人民共和国管辖海域进行倾倒，包括弃置船舶、航空器、平台和其他海上人工构造物。违者，主管部门可责令其限期治理，支付清除污染费，赔偿损失，并处以罚款。  
在中华人民共和国管辖海域以外倾倒废弃物，造成中华人民共和国管辖海域污染损害的，按[本条例第十七条](#art-17)规定处理。

**Article 7** No foreign wastes may be transported into the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China for dumping, including the abandonment of vessels, aircraft, platforms and other man-made structures at sea. In case of violation, the competent authority may order the violator to take remedial action within a time limit, pay the costs of pollution elimination, compensate for losses, and impose a fine.  
Where the dumping of wastes outside the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China causes pollution damage to the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　为倾倒的目的，经过中华人民共和国管辖海域运送废弃物的任何船舶及其他载运工具，应当在进入中华人民共和国管辖海域15天之前，通报主管部门，同时报告进入中华人民共和国管辖海域的时间、航线、以及废弃物的名称、数量及成分。

**Article 8** Any vessel or other means of transport that transports wastes through the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of dumping shall notify the competent authority 15 days before entering the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, and shall at the same time report the time and route of entry into the said sea areas, as well as the name, quantity and composition of the wastes.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国籍船舶、平台在中华人民共和国管辖海域，由于海底矿物资源的勘探开发及与勘探开发相关的海上加工所产生的废弃物和其他物质需要向海洋倾倒的，应按规定程序报经主管部门批准。

**Article 9** Where foreign-flagged vessels or platforms in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China need to dump into the sea wastes and other substances generated from the exploration and development of seabed mineral resources and offshore processing related to such exploration and development, they shall submit an application to the competent authority for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　倾倒许可证应注明倾倒单位、有效期限和废弃物的数量、种类、倾倒方法等事项。  
签发许可证应根据本条例的有关规定严格控制。主管部门根据海洋生态环境的变化和科学技术的发展，可以更换或撤销许可证。

**Article 10** A dumping permit shall specify the dumping unit, the period of validity, and the quantity, type and method of dumping of the wastes, among other matters.  
The issuance of a permit shall be strictly controlled in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations. The competent authority may, based on changes in the marine ecological environment and the development of science and technology, replace or revoke a permit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　废弃物根据其毒性、有害物质含量和对海洋环境的影响等因素，分为三类。其分类标准，由主管部门制定。主管部门可根据海洋生态环境的变化，科学技术的发展，以及海洋环境保护的需要，对附件进行修订。  
一、禁止倾倒附件一所列的废弃物及其他物质(见附件一)。当出现紧急情况，在陆地上处置会严重危及人民健康时，经国家海洋局批准，获得紧急许可证，可到指定的区域按规定的方法倾倒。  
二、倾倒附件二所列的废弃物(见附件二)，应当事先获得特别许可证。  
三、倾倒未列入附件一和附件二的低毒或无毒的废弃物，应当事先获得普通许可证。

**Article 11** Wastes shall be classified into three categories according to their toxicity, content of hazardous substances, and impact on the marine environment, among other factors. The criteria for such classification shall be formulated by the competent authority. The competent authority may, based on changes in the marine ecological environment, the development of science and technology, and the needs of marine environmental protection, revise the Annexes.  
1. It is prohibited to dump the wastes and other substances listed in Annex I (see Annex I). In the event of an emergency situation where disposal on land would seriously endanger public health, upon approval by the State Oceanic Administration and after obtaining an emergency permit, dumping may be carried out in a designated area by the prescribed method.  
2. Dumping of wastes listed in Annex II (see Annex II) shall require a special permit obtained in advance.  
3. Dumping of low-toxicity or non-toxic wastes not listed in Annex I or Annex II shall require an ordinary permit obtained in advance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　获准向海洋倾倒废弃物的单位在废弃物装载时，应通知主管部门予以核实。  
核实工作按许可证所载的事项进行。主管部门如发现实际装载与许可证所注明内容不符，应责令停止装运；情节严重的，应中止或吊销许可证。

**Article 12** A unit that has been approved to dump wastes into the sea shall, at the time of loading the wastes, notify the competent authority for verification.  
The verification shall be carried out in accordance with the matters stated in the permit. If the competent authority finds that the actual loading does not conform to the contents specified in the permit, it shall order the loading to be stopped; if the circumstances are serious, it shall suspend or revoke the permit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　主管部门应对海洋倾倒活动进行监视和监督，必要时可派员随航。倾倒单位应为随航公务人员提供方便。

**Article 13** The competent authority shall monitor and supervise the dumping activities at sea, and may, when necessary, send personnel to accompany the voyage. The dumping unit shall provide convenience for the accompanying public servants.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　获准向海洋倾倒废弃物的单位，应当按许可证注明的期限和条件，到指定的区域进行倾倒，如实地详细填写倾倒情况记录表，并按许可证注明的要求，将记录表报送主管部门。倾倒废弃物的船舶、航空器、平台和其他载运工具应有明显标志和信号，并在航行日志上详细记录倾倒情况。

**Article 14** A unit that has been approved to dump wastes into the sea shall dump the wastes in the designated area within the time limit and under the conditions specified in the permit, truthfully and in detail fill in a record sheet of the dumping operations, and submit the record sheet to the competent authority in accordance with the requirements specified in the permit. Vessels, aircraft, platforms and other means of transport dumping wastes shall have conspicuous markings and signals, and shall record in detail the dumping situation in the logbook.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　倾倒废弃物的船舶、航空器、平台和其他载运工具，凡属[《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》第八十九条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-89)、第九十一条规定的情形，可免于承担赔偿责任。  
为紧急避险或救助人命，未按许可证规定的条件和区域进行倾倒时，应尽力避免或减轻因倾倒而造成的污染损害，并在事后尽快向主管部门报告。倾倒单位和紧急避险或救助人命的受益者，应对由此所造成的污染损害进行补偿。  
由于第三者的过失造成污染损害的，倾倒单位应向主管部门提出确凿证据，经主管部门确认后责令第三者承担赔偿责任。  
在海上航行和作业的船舶、航空器、平台和其他载运工具，因不可抗拒的原因而弃置时，其所有人应向主管部门和就近的港务监督报告，并尽快打捞清理。

**Article 15** Vessels, aircraft, platforms and other means of transport dumping wastes that fall under the circumstances specified in Articles 89 and 91 of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China may be exempted from compensation liability.  
Where dumping is carried out not in accordance with the conditions and area specified in the permit due to an act of rescue or to save human life, all efforts shall be made to avoid or mitigate the pollution damage caused by the dumping, and a report shall be submitted to the competent authority as soon as possible after the event. The dumping unit and the beneficiaries of the act of rescue or saving of human life shall compensate for the pollution damage caused thereby.  
Where pollution damage is caused by the fault of a third party, the dumping unit shall provide conclusive evidence to the competent authority; after confirmation by the competent authority, the third party shall be ordered to bear compensation liability.  
Where a vessel, aircraft, platform or other means of transport navigating or operating at sea is abandoned due to force majeure, its owner shall report to the competent authority and the nearest harbor superintendency, and shall salvage and clean up as soon as possible.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　主管部门对海洋倾倒区应定期进行监测，加强管理，避免对渔业资源和其他海上活动造成有害影响。当发现倾倒区不宜继续倾倒时，主管部门可决定予以封闭。

**Article 16** The competent authority shall conduct regular monitoring of ocean dumping areas, strengthen management, and avoid causing adverse effects on fishery resources and other maritime activities. When it is found that a dumping area is no longer suitable for continued dumping, the competent authority may decide to close it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对违反本条例，造成海洋环境污染损害的，主管部门可责令其限期治理，支付清除污染费，向受害方赔偿由此所造成的损失，并视情节轻重和污染损害的程度，处以警告或人民币10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 17** Anyone who violates these Regulations and causes pollution damage to the marine environment may be ordered by the competent authority to take remedial action within a time limit, pay the costs of pollution elimination, compensate the injured party for the losses caused thereby, and, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances and the degree of pollution damage, be given a warning or imposed a fine of not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　要求赔偿损失的单位和个人，应尽快向主管部门提出污染损害索赔报告书。报告书应包括：受污染损害的时间、地点、范围、对象、损失清单，技术鉴定和公证证明，并尽可能提供有关原始单据和照片等。

**Article 18** Any unit or individual claiming compensation for losses shall, as soon as possible, submit a report on the claim for pollution damage to the competent authority. The report shall include: the time, location, scope, and target of the pollution damage, a list of losses, technical appraisal and notarization certificates, and, to the extent possible, relevant original documents and photographs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　受托清除污染的单位在作业结束后，应尽快向主管部门提交索取清除污染费用报告书。报告书应包括：清除污染的时间、地点，投入的人力、机具、船只，清除材料的数量、单价、计算方法，组织清除的管理费、交通费及其他有关费用，清除效果及其情况，其他有关证据和证明材料。

**Article 19** A unit entrusted with pollution elimination shall, after the completion of the operation, submit as soon as possible a report on the claim for the costs of pollution elimination to the competent authority. The report shall include: the time and location of the pollution elimination, the manpower, machinery and vessels deployed, the quantity, unit price and calculation method of the elimination materials, the management fees, transportation fees and other relevant expenses for organizing the elimination, the results and circumstances of the elimination, and other relevant evidence and supporting materials.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对违法行为的处罚标准如下：  
一、凡有下列行为之一者，处以警告或人民币2000元以下的罚款：  
(一)伪造废弃物检验单的；  
(二)不按[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定填报倾倒情况记录表的；  
(三)在[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的情况下，未及时向主管部门和港务监督报告的。  
二、凡实际装载与许可证所注明内容不符，情节严重的，除中止或吊销许可证外，还可处以人民币2000元以上5000元以下的罚款。  
三、凡未按[本条例第十二条](#art-12)规定通知主管部门核实而擅自进行倾倒的，可处以人民币5000元以上2万元以下的罚款。  
四、凡有下列行为之一者，可处以人民币2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未经批准向海洋倾倒废弃物的；  
(二)不按批准的条件和区域进行倾倒的，但[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的情况不在此限。

**Article 20** The standards for penalties for illegal acts are as follows:  
1. Anyone who commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning or imposed a fine of not more than RMB 2,000:  
(1) forging a waste inspection certificate;  
(2) failing to fill in the record sheet of dumping operations in accordance with Article 14 of these Regulations; or  
(3) failing to report promptly to the competent authority and the harbor superintendency in the circumstances specified in Article 15 of these Regulations.  
2. Where the actual loading does not conform to the contents specified in the permit and the circumstances are serious, in addition to the suspension or revocation of the permit, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 but not more than RMB 5,000 may also be imposed.  
3. Anyone who engages in dumping without notifying the competent authority for verification as required by Article 12 of these Regulations may be imposed a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 20,000.  
4. Anyone who commits any of the following acts may be imposed a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000:  
(1) dumping wastes into the sea without approval; or  
(2) dumping not in accordance with the approved conditions and area, except in the circumstances specified in Article 15 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对违反本条例，造成或可能造成海洋环境污染损害的直接责任人，主管部门可处以警告或者罚款，也可以并处。  
对于违反本条例，污染损害海洋环境造成重大财产损失或致人伤亡的直接责任人，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** The competent authority may impose a warning or a fine, or both, on the directly responsible persons who violate these Regulations and cause or may cause pollution damage to the marine environment.  
For directly responsible persons who violate these Regulations, pollute and damage the marine environment, causing major property losses or casualties, the judicial authorities shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人对主管部门的处罚决定不服的，可以在收到处罚通知书之日起15日内，向人民法院起诉；期满不起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由主管部门申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 22** If a party disagrees with a penalty decision made by the competent authority, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, file a lawsuit with the People's Court. If no lawsuit is filed within the time limit and the penalty decision is not complied with, the competent authority shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对违反本条例，造成海洋环境污染损害的行为，主动检举、揭发，积极提供证据，或采取有效措施减少污染损害有成绩的个人，应给予表扬或奖励。

**Article 23** Any individual who proactively reports and exposes acts that violate these Regulations and cause pollution damage to the marine environment, actively provides evidence, or takes effective measures to reduce pollution damage with achievements, shall be given commendation or reward.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自1985年4月1日起施行。  
附件1  
禁止倾倒的物质  
一、含有机卤素化合物、汞及汞化合物、镉及镉化合物的废弃物，但微含量的或能在海水中迅速转化为无害物质的除外。  
二、强放射性废弃物及其他强放射性物质。

四、渔网、绳索、塑料制品及其他能在海面漂浮或在水中悬浮，严重妨碍航行、捕鱼及其他活动或危害海洋生物的人工合成物质。  
五、含有本附件第一、二项所列物质的阴沟污泥和疏浚物。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 1985.  
Annex 1  
Substances Prohibited from Dumping  
1. Wastes containing organic halogen compounds, mercury and mercury compounds, cadmium and cadmium compounds, except those with trace amounts or those that can be rapidly converted into harmless substances in seawater.  
2. High-level radioactive wastes and other highly radioactive substances.  
3. Crude oil and its wastes, petroleum refining products, residual oils, and mixtures containing such substances.  
4. Fishing nets, ropes, plastic products and other synthetic substances that float on the sea surface or remain suspended in water, seriously hindering navigation, fishing and other activities or endangering marine organisms.  
5. Sewage sludge and dredged materials containing the substances listed in items 1 and 2 of this Annex.  
Annex 2  
Substances Requiring a Special Permit for Dumping  
1. Wastes containing large quantities of the following substances:  
(1) arsenic and its compounds;  
(2) lead and its compounds;  
(3) copper and its compounds;  
(4) zinc and its compounds;  
(5) organic silicon compounds;  
(6) cyanides;  
(7) fluorides;  
(8) beryllium, chromium, nickel, vanadium and their compounds;  
(9) pesticides not listed in Annex 1 and their by-products.  
But excluding those that are harmless or that can be rapidly converted into harmless substances in seawater.  
2. Wastes containing low-level radioactive substances.  
3. Containers, scrap metal and other bulky wastes that are likely to sink to the seabed and may seriously obstruct fishing and navigation.  
4. Sewage sludge and dredged materials containing the substances listed in items 1 and 2 of this Annex.
