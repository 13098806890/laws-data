# 制止牟取暴利的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1995年1月11日国务院批准　1995年1月25日国家计划委员会令第4号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制止牟取暴利，保护消费者的合法权益，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于对国民经济和社会发展有重大影响的和与居民生活有密切关系的商品和服务(以下简称商品和服务)。 前款规定的商品和服务的项目，由国务院价格主管部门按照国务院的有关规定公布、调整；其中实行国家定价的，按照国家定价和国务院有关价格管理的规定执行。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地方的实际情况，在国务院价格主管部门公布的商品和服务的项目的基础上，决定适当增加与本地方居民生活有密切关系的商品和服务的项目，予以公布，并报国务院价格主管部门备案。 第三条　在中华人民共和国境内从事生产经营和提供有偿服务的公民、法人和其他组织(以下简称生产经营者)必须遵守本规定。 第四条　生产经营者的价格行为应当遵循公开、公平和诚实信用的原则，执行国家关于商品和服务实行明码标价的规定。 第五条　商品的价格和服务的收费标准(以下统称价格)，应当符合下列要求： (一)某一商品或者服务的价格水平不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的市场平均价格的合理幅度； (二)某一商品或者服务的差价率不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的平均差价率的合理幅度； (三)某一商品或者服务的利润率不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的平均利润率的合理幅度。但是，生产经营者通过改善经营管理，运用新技术，降低成本，提高效益而实现的利润率除外。 第六条　商品或者服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率以其社会平均成本为基础测定。 商品或者服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率的合理幅度，按照其与国民经济和社会发展的关系或者与居民生活的密切程度，市场供求状况和不同行业、不同环节、不同商品或者服务的特点规定。 第七条　商品和服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率及其合理幅度，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府价格管理部门会同有关业务主管部门，按照国务院价格主管部门的规定测定和规定，并予以公布；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格管理部门也可以根据需要，授权市、县人民政府价格管理部门测定和规定与居民生活有密切关系的部分商品和服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率及其合理幅度，并予以公布。 有关业务主管部门和生产经营者应当配合价格管理部门的测定工作。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制止牟取暴利，保护消费者的合法权益，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of maintaining the order of the socialist market economy, stopping the pursuit of excessive profits, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of consumers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于对国民经济和社会发展有重大影响的和与居民生活有密切关系的商品和服务(以下简称商品和服务)。  
前款规定的商品和服务的项目，由国务院价格主管部门按照国务院的有关规定公布、调整；其中实行国家定价的，按照国家定价和国务院有关价格管理的规定执行。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地方的实际情况，在国务院价格主管部门公布的商品和服务的项目的基础上，决定适当增加与本地方居民生活有密切关系的商品和服务的项目，予以公布，并报国务院价格主管部门备案。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to goods and services that have a significant impact on the national economy and social development and that are closely related to the lives of residents (hereinafter referred to as goods and services).  
The items of goods and services specified in the preceding paragraph shall be announced and adjusted by the competent department of price under the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council; among them, those subject to government pricing shall be implemented in accordance with the government prices and the relevant provisions of the State Council on price management.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the actual conditions of their respective localities and on the basis of the items of goods and services announced by the competent department of price under the State Council, decide to appropriately increase the items of goods and services that are closely related to the lives of local residents, announce them, and report them to the competent department of price under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国境内从事生产经营和提供有偿服务的公民、法人和其他组织(以下简称生产经营者)必须遵守本规定。

**Article 3** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations that engage in production and operation activities and provide paid services within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as production and business operators) must abide by these Provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产经营者的价格行为应当遵循公开、公平和诚实信用的原则，执行国家关于商品和服务实行明码标价的规定。

**Article 4** The pricing conduct of production and business operators shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, and good faith, and shall implement the state provisions on clearly marking prices for goods and services.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　商品的价格和服务的收费标准(以下统称价格)，应当符合下列要求：  
(一)某一商品或者服务的价格水平不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的市场平均价格的合理幅度；  
(二)某一商品或者服务的差价率不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的平均差价率的合理幅度；  
(三)某一商品或者服务的利润率不超过同一地区、同一期间、同一档次、同种商品或者服务的平均利润率的合理幅度。但是，生产经营者通过改善经营管理，运用新技术，降低成本，提高效益而实现的利润率除外。

**Article 5** The prices of goods and the fee standards for services (hereinafter collectively referred to as prices) shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) The price level of a certain good or service shall not exceed the reasonable range of the average market price of the same type of good or service in the same region, during the same period, and at the same grade;  
(2) The price difference rate of a certain good or service shall not exceed the reasonable range of the average price difference rate of the same type of good or service in the same region, during the same period, and at the same grade;  
(3) The profit rate of a certain good or service shall not exceed the reasonable range of the average profit rate of the same type of good or service in the same region, during the same period, and at the same grade. However, this shall not apply to the profit rate achieved by production and business operators through improving operation and management, adopting new technologies, reducing costs, and increasing efficiency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商品或者服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率以其社会平均成本为基础测定。  
商品或者服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率的合理幅度，按照其与国民经济和社会发展的关系或者与居民生活的密切程度，市场供求状况和不同行业、不同环节、不同商品或者服务的特点规定。

**Article 6** The average market price, average price difference rate, and average profit rate of goods or services shall be determined on the basis of their average social costs.  
The reasonable ranges of the average market price, average price difference rate, and average profit rate of goods or services shall be prescribed in accordance with their relationship to the national economy and social development or their degree of closeness to residents' lives, the supply and demand conditions in the market, and the characteristics of different industries, different links, and different goods or services.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　商品和服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率及其合理幅度，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府价格管理部门会同有关业务主管部门，按照国务院价格主管部门的规定测定和规定，并予以公布；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格管理部门也可以根据需要，授权市、县人民政府价格管理部门测定和规定与居民生活有密切关系的部分商品和服务的市场平均价格、平均差价率、平均利润率及其合理幅度，并予以公布。  
有关业务主管部门和生产经营者应当配合价格管理部门的测定工作。

**Article 7** The average market price, average price difference rate, average profit rate, and their reasonable ranges for goods and services shall be determined and prescribed by the competent department of price under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, jointly with the relevant competent business departments, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of price under the State Council, and shall be announced; the competent department of price under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, as needed, authorize the competent department of price under the people's government of a city or county to determine and prescribe the average market price, average price difference rate, average profit rate, and their reasonable ranges for some goods and services closely related to residents' lives, and to announce them.  
The relevant competent business departments and production and business operators shall cooperate with the determination work of the competent departments of price.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产经营者不得违反本规定，以下列手段非法牟利：  
(一)不按照规定明码标价或者在明码标示的价格之外索要高价；  
(二)谎称削价让利，或者以虚假的优惠价、折扣价、处理价、最低价以及其他虚假的价格信息，进行价格欺诈；  
(三)生产经营者之间或者行业组织之间相互串通，哄抬价格；  
(四)违反公平、自愿原则，强迫交易对方接受高价；  
(五)采取其他价格欺诈手段。

**Article 8** No production and business operator shall, in violation of these Provisions, seek illegal profits by the following means:  
(1) failing to clearly mark prices as required, or demanding high prices beyond the prices clearly marked;  
(2) falsely claiming price reductions or profit concessions, or engaging in price fraud by using false preferential prices, discount prices, clearance prices, minimum prices, or other false price information;  
(3) colluding among production and business operators or among industry organizations to drive up prices;  
(4) violating the principles of fairness and voluntariness by forcing the other party to a transaction to accept high prices;  
(5) adopting other fraudulent pricing means.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　价格监督检查人员依法执行职务，生产经营者不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 9** While price supervision and inspection personnel are performing their duties according to law, production and business operators shall not refuse to cooperate or obstruct them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对生产经营者牟取暴利的行为，任何单位和个人都有权向价格监督检查机构投诉或者举报。  
价格监督检查机构受理投诉或者收到举报后，应当及时调查核实情况，依照本规定予以处理，并根据情况对举报者给予奖励。

**Article 10** With respect to any act of seeking excessive profits by a production and business operator, any unit or individual shall have the right to lodge a complaint or report with the price supervision and inspection agency.  
After accepting a complaint or receiving a report, the price supervision and inspection agency shall timely investigate and verify the facts, handle the matter in accordance with these Provisions, and, depending on the circumstances, give a reward to the person who made the report.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　违反[本规定第五条](#art-5)规定的，由价格监督检查机构责令改正；拒不改正的，予以警告、没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 11** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 5 of these Provisions shall be ordered by the price supervision and inspection agency to make corrections; if the party refuses to make corrections, a warning shall be given, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　违反[本规定第八条](#art-8)规定的，由价格监督检查机构予以警告，责令其向遭受损害的一方退还违法所得，违法所得不能退还的，予以没收，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 12** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 8 of these Provisions shall be given a warning by the price supervision and inspection agency, and ordered to return the illegal gains to the party that suffered damage; if the illegal gains cannot be returned, they shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　以暴力、威胁方法阻碍价格监督检查人员依法执行职务的，依法追究刑事责任；拒绝、阻碍价格监督检查人员依法执行职务，未使用暴力、威胁方法，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 13** Whoever obstructs price supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law by means of violence or threats shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; whoever refuses to cooperate with or obstructs price supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law without using violence or threats, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　价格监督检查人员玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者包庇、纵容牟取暴利的行为的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** Where price supervision and inspection personnel neglect their duties, engage in malpractices for personal gain, or shield or connive at acts of seeking excessive profits, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　工商行政管理、审计、财政、税务、公安、技术监督等部门，应当在各自的职责范围内，配合价格监督检查机构查处牟取暴利的行为。

**Article 15** The departments of industry and commerce administration, audit, finance, taxation, public security, and technical supervision shall, within their respective scopes of duties, cooperate with the price supervision and inspection agencies in investigating and punishing acts of seeking excessive profits.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，可以结合本地方的实际情况，根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 16** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual conditions of their respective localities, formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定由国务院价格主管部门组织实施。

**Article 17** These Provisions shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of price under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 18** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
