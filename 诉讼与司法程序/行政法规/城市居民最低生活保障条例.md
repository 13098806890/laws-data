# 城市居民最低生活保障条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：1999-09-28  
**生效日期**：1999-10-01

> (1999年9月28日国务院第21次常务会议通过　1999年9月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第271号发布　自1999年10月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范城市居民最低生活保障制度，保障城市居民基本生活，制定本条例。 第二条　持有非农业户口的城市居民，凡共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地城市居民最低生活保障标准的，均有从当地人民政府获得基本生活物质帮助的权利。 前款所称收入，是指共同生活的家庭成员的全部货币收入和实物收入，包括法定赡养人、扶养人或者抚养人应当给付的赡养费、扶养费或者抚养费，不包括优抚对象按照国家规定享受的抚恤金、补助金。 第三条　城市居民最低生活保障制度遵循保障城市居民基本生活的原则，坚持国家保障与社会帮扶相结合、鼓励劳动自救的方针。 第四条　城市居民最低生活保障制度实行地方各级人民政府负责制。县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门具体负责本行政区域内城市居民最低生活保障的管理工作；财政部门按照规定落实城市居民最低生活保障资金；统计、物价、审计、劳动保障和人事等部门分工负责，在各自的职责范围内负责城市居民最低生活保障的有关工作。 县级人民政府民政部门以及街道办事处和镇人民政府(以下统称管理审批机关)负责城市居民最低生活保障的具体管理审批工作。 居民委员会根据管理审批机关的委托，可以承担城市居民最低生活保障的日常管理、服务工作。 国务院民政部门负责全国城市居民最低生活保障的管理工作。 第五条　城市居民最低生活保障所需资金，由地方人民政府列入财政预算，纳入社会救济专项资金支出项目，专项管理，专款专用。 国家鼓励社会组织和个人为城市居民最低生活保障提供捐赠、资助；所提供的捐赠资助，全部纳入当地城市居民最低生活保障资金。 第六条　城市居民最低生活保障标准，按照当地维持城市居民基本生活所必需的衣、食、住费用，并适当考虑水电燃煤(燃气)费用以及未成年人的义务教育费用确定。 直辖市、设区的市的城市居民最低生活保障标准，由市人民政府民政部门会同财政、统计、物价等部门制定，报本级人民政府批准并公布执行；县(县级市)的城市居民最低生活保障标准，由县(县级市)人民政府民政部门会同财政、统计、物价等部门制定，报本级人民政府批准并报上一级人民政府备案后公布执行。 城市居民最低生活保障标准需要提高时，依照前两款的规定重新核定。 第七条　申请享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇，由户主向户籍所在地的街道办事处或者镇人民政府提出书面申请，并出具有关证明材料，填写《城市居民最低生活保障待遇审批表》。城市居民最低生活保障待遇，由其所在地的街道办事处或者镇人民政府初审，并将有关材料和初审意见报送县级人民政府民政部门审批。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范城市居民最低生活保障制度，保障城市居民基本生活，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating the system of subsistence allowances for urban residents and ensuring the basic livelihood of urban residents.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　持有非农业户口的城市居民，凡共同生活的家庭成员人均收入低于当地城市居民最低生活保障标准的，均有从当地人民政府获得基本生活物质帮助的权利。  
前款所称收入，是指共同生活的家庭成员的全部货币收入和实物收入，包括法定赡养人、扶养人或者抚养人应当给付的赡养费、扶养费或者抚养费，不包括优抚对象按照国家规定享受的抚恤金、补助金。

**Article 2** Urban residents with non-agricultural household registration whose per capita income of family members living together is lower than the local subsistence allowance standard for urban residents shall have the right to obtain basic living material assistance from the local people's government.  
The income mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the total monetary income and in-kind income of the family members living together, including the alimony, support, or child support that shall be paid by the statutory alimony providers, support providers, or child support providers, but excluding the pension and subsidy enjoyed by entitled groups according to state regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市居民最低生活保障制度遵循保障城市居民基本生活的原则，坚持国家保障与社会帮扶相结合、鼓励劳动自救的方针。

**Article 3** The system of subsistence allowances for urban residents shall follow the principle of ensuring the basic livelihood of urban residents, adhere to the policy of combining state guarantee with social assistance, and encourage self-help through labor.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　城市居民最低生活保障制度实行地方各级人民政府负责制。县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门具体负责本行政区域内城市居民最低生活保障的管理工作；财政部门按照规定落实城市居民最低生活保障资金；统计、物价、审计、劳动保障和人事等部门分工负责，在各自的职责范围内负责城市居民最低生活保障的有关工作。  
县级人民政府民政部门以及街道办事处和镇人民政府(以下统称管理审批机关)负责城市居民最低生活保障的具体管理审批工作。  
居民委员会根据管理审批机关的委托，可以承担城市居民最低生活保障的日常管理、服务工作。  
国务院民政部门负责全国城市居民最低生活保障的管理工作。

**Article 4** The system of subsistence allowances for urban residents shall implement the responsibility system of local people's governments at all levels. The civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be specifically responsible for the administration of subsistence allowances for urban residents within their respective administrative areas; the financial departments shall, in accordance with regulations, ensure the allocation of funds for subsistence allowances for urban residents; the statistics, price, audit, labor security, and personnel departments shall be responsible for the relevant work of subsistence allowances for urban residents within their respective duties.  
The civil affairs departments of county people's governments, as well as sub-district offices and town people's governments (hereinafter collectively referred to as the administrative and approval authorities), shall be responsible for the specific administration and approval of subsistence allowances for urban residents.  
Residents committees may, upon entrustment by the administrative and approval authorities, undertake the daily administration and service work of subsistence allowances for urban residents.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of subsistence allowances for urban residents nationwide.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　城市居民最低生活保障所需资金，由地方人民政府列入财政预算，纳入社会救济专项资金支出项目，专项管理，专款专用。  
国家鼓励社会组织和个人为城市居民最低生活保障提供捐赠、资助；所提供的捐赠资助，全部纳入当地城市居民最低生活保障资金。

**Article 5** The funds needed for subsistence allowances for urban residents shall be included in the fiscal budget by local people's governments, incorporated into the special fund expenditure items for social relief, and subject to special management and earmarked use.  
The state encourages social organizations and individuals to provide donations and assistance for subsistence allowances for urban residents; all such donations and assistance shall be fully included in the local funds for subsistence allowances for urban residents.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　城市居民最低生活保障标准，按照当地维持城市居民基本生活所必需的衣、食、住费用，并适当考虑水电燃煤(燃气)费用以及未成年人的义务教育费用确定。  
直辖市、设区的市的城市居民最低生活保障标准，由市人民政府民政部门会同财政、统计、物价等部门制定，报本级人民政府批准并公布执行；县(县级市)的城市居民最低生活保障标准，由县(县级市)人民政府民政部门会同财政、统计、物价等部门制定，报本级人民政府批准并报上一级人民政府备案后公布执行。  
城市居民最低生活保障标准需要提高时，依照前两款的规定重新核定。

**Article 6** The subsistence allowance standard for urban residents shall be determined based on the expenses for clothing, food, and housing necessary to maintain the basic livelihood of local urban residents, taking into account the expenses for water, electricity, coal (gas), and the compulsory education expenses for minors.  
The subsistence allowance standard for urban residents in municipalities directly under the Central Government and cities divided into districts shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the municipal people's government in conjunction with the financial, statistics, price and other departments, reported to the people's government at the same level for approval and published for implementation; the subsistence allowance standard for urban residents in counties (county-level cities) shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the county (county-level city) people's government in conjunction with the financial, statistics, price and other departments, reported to the people's government at the same level for approval and reported to the people's government at the next higher level for record, and then published for implementation.  
When the subsistence allowance standard for urban residents needs to be raised, it shall be re-determined in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇，由户主向户籍所在地的街道办事处或者镇人民政府提出书面申请，并出具有关证明材料，填写《城市居民最低生活保障待遇审批表》。城市居民最低生活保障待遇，由其所在地的街道办事处或者镇人民政府初审，并将有关材料和初审意见报送县级人民政府民政部门审批。  
管理审批机关为审批城市居民最低生活保障待遇的需要，可以通过入户调查、邻里访问以及信函索证等方式对申请人的家庭经济状况和实际生活水平进行调查核实。申请人及有关单位、组织或者个人应当接受调查，如实提供有关情况。

**Article 7** To apply for the enjoyment of subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, the head of the household shall submit a written application to the sub-district office or town people's government at the place of household registration, provide relevant supporting materials, and fill in the "Approval Form for Subsistence Allowance Benefits for Urban Residents". The subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents shall be preliminarily examined by the sub-district office or town people's government at the place of residence, and the relevant materials and preliminary examination opinions shall be submitted to the civil affairs department of the county people's government for approval.  
For the purpose of approving subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, the administrative and approval authorities may investigate and verify the economic status and actual living standard of the applicant's family through methods such as household surveys, neighborhood visits, and correspondence requested. The applicant and relevant units, organizations or individuals shall accept the investigation and truthfully provide relevant information.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级人民政府民政部门经审查，对符合享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇条件的家庭，应当区分下列不同情况批准其享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇：  
(一)对无生活来源、无劳动能力又无法定赡养人、扶养人或者抚养人的城市居民，批准其按照当地城市居民最低生活保障标准全额享受；  
(二)对尚有一定收入的城市居民，批准其按照家庭人均收入低于当地城市居民最低生活保障标准的差额享受。  
县级人民政府民政部门经审查，对不符合享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇条件的，应当书面通知申请人，并说明理由。  
管理审批机关应当自接到申请人提出申请之日起的30日内办结审批手续。  
城市居民最低生活保障待遇由管理审批机关以货币形式按月发放；必要时，也可以给付实物。

**Article 8** Upon examination, the civil affairs department of the county people's government shall approve the following categories of families that meet the conditions for enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, and distinguish among the following circumstances to grant them subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents:  
(1) For urban residents who have no source of income, no capacity for work, and have no statutory alimony provider, support provider, or child support provider, approve them to receive the full amount according to the local subsistence allowance standard for urban residents;  
(2) For urban residents who still have some income, approve them to receive the difference based on the per capita income of the family being lower than the local subsistence allowance standard for urban residents.  
Upon examination, the civil affairs department of the county people's government shall, if a family does not meet the conditions for enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The administrative and approval authorities shall complete the approval procedures within 30 days from the date of receiving the application from the applicant.  
The subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents shall be disbursed monthly by the administrative and approval authorities in monetary form; when necessary, they may also be provided in kind.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对经批准享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的城市居民，由管理审批机关采取适当形式以户为单位予以公布，接受群众监督。任何人对不符合法定条件而享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的，都有权向管理审批机关提出意见；管理审批机关经核查，对情况属实的，应当予以纠正。

**Article 9** The administrative and approval authorities shall, in an appropriate manner, publish the urban residents who have been approved to enjoy subsistence allowance benefits on a household basis, to accept public supervision. Any person who finds that a person enjoys subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents without meeting the statutory conditions shall have the right to submit comments to the administrative and approval authorities; upon verification, the administrative and approval authorities shall correct the situation if it is true.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的城市居民家庭人均收入情况发生变化的，应当及时通过居民委员会告知管理审批机关，办理停发、减发或者增发城市居民最低生活保障待遇的手续。  
管理审批机关应当对享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的城市居民的家庭收入情况定期进行核查。  
在就业年龄内有劳动能力但尚未就业的城市居民，在享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇期间，应当参加其所在的居民委员会组织的公益性社区服务劳动。

**Article 10** Where there is a change in the per capita income of a family of urban residents enjoying subsistence allowance benefits, they shall promptly inform the administrative and approval authorities through the residents committee, and go through the procedures for suspending, reducing, or increasing the subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents.  
The administrative and approval authorities shall regularly verify the family income of urban residents enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents.  
Urban residents who are of working age and have the capacity to work but are not yet employed shall, during the period of enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, participate in the public welfare community service labor organized by the residents committee to which they belong.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地方各级人民政府及其有关部门，应当对享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的城市居民在就业、从事个体经营等方面给予必要的扶持和照顾。

**Article 11** Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall provide necessary support and care to urban residents enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents in terms of employment, self-employment, etc.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　财政部门、审计部门依法监督城市居民最低生活保障资金的使用情况。

**Article 12** The financial departments and audit departments shall supervise the use of funds for subsistence allowances for urban residents according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事城市居民最低生活保障管理审批工作的人员有下列行为之一的，给予批评教育，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对符合享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇条件的家庭拒不签署同意享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇意见的，或者对不符合享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇条件的家庭故意签署同意享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇意见的；  
(二)玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者贪污、挪用、扣压、拖欠城市居民最低生活保障款物的。

**Article 13** Personnel engaged in the administration and approval of subsistence allowances for urban residents who commit any of the following acts shall be given criticism and education, and shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing to sign opinions agreeing to the enjoyment of subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents for families that meet the conditions, or deliberately signing opinions agreeing to the enjoyment of subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents for families that do not meet the conditions;  
(2) Dereliction of duty, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, or embezzling, misappropriating, withholding, or defaulting on the funds and materials for subsistence allowances for urban residents.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的城市居民有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府民政部门给予批评教育或者警告，追回其冒领的城市居民最低生活保障款物；情节恶劣的，处冒领金额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款：  
(一)采取虚报、隐瞒、伪造等手段，骗取享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的；  
(二)在享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇期间家庭收入情况好转，不按规定告知管理审批机关，继续享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇的。

**Article 14** Urban residents enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents who commit any of the following acts shall be given criticism and education or a warning by the civil affairs department of the county people's government, and the wrongfully obtained funds and materials for subsistence allowances for urban residents shall be recovered; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount wrongfully obtained shall be imposed:  
(1) Obtaining the enjoyment of subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents by means of false reporting, concealment, forgery, etc.;  
(2) Failing to inform the administrative and approval authorities as required when the family income improves during the period of enjoying subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents, and continuing to enjoy subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市居民对县级人民政府民政部门作出的不批准享受城市居民最低生活保障待遇或者减发、停发城市居民最低生活保障款物的决定或者给予的行政处罚不服的，可以依法申请行政复议；对复议决定仍不服的，可以依法提起行政诉讼。

**Article 15** Urban residents who are not satisfied with the decision made by the civil affairs department of the county people's government to disapprove the enjoyment of subsistence allowance benefits for urban residents or to reduce or suspend the disbursement of funds and materials for subsistence allowances for urban residents, or with the administrative penalty imposed, may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law; if still not satisfied with the reconsideration decision, they may initiate an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例，结合本行政区域城市居民最低生活保障工作的实际情况，规定实施的办法和步骤。

**Article 16** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Regulations and in light of the actual circumstances of the subsistence allowance work for urban residents in their respective administrative areas, formulate measures and steps for implementation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本条例自1999年10月1日起施行。

**Article 17** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1999.
