### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范民办非企业单位的登记管理，保障民办非企业单位的合法权益，促进社会主义物质文明、精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating the registration administration of private non-enterprise units, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of private non-enterprise units, and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称民办非企业单位，是指企业事业单位、社会团体和其他社会力量以及公民个人利用非国有资产举办的，从事非营利性社会服务活动的社会组织。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'private non-enterprise units' refer to social organizations organized by enterprises, institutions, social groups, and other social forces as well as individual citizens using non-state-owned assets, engaged in non-profit social service activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　成立民办非企业单位，应当经其业务主管单位审查同意，并依照本条例的规定登记。

**Article 3** The establishment of a private non-enterprise unit shall be subject to examination and consent by its competent business unit, and shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民办非企业单位应当遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策，不得反对宪法确定的基本原则，不得危害国家的统一、安全和民族的团结，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利益以及其他社会组织和公民的合法权益，不得违背社会道德风尚。  
民办非企业单位不得从事营利性经营活动。

**Article 4** Private non-enterprise units shall abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, and state policies; shall not oppose the basic principles established by the Constitution; shall not endanger the unity, security, and ethnic solidarity of the state; shall not harm state interests, public interests, and the legitimate rights and interests of other social organizations and citizens; and shall not violate social moral standards.  
Private non-enterprise units shall not engage in for-profit business activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院民政部门和县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门是本级人民政府的民办非企业单位登记管理机关(以下简称登记管理机关)。  
国务院有关部门和县级以上地方各级人民政府的有关部门、国务院或者县级以上地方各级人民政府授权的组织，是有关行业、业务范围内民办非企业单位的业务主管单位(以下简称业务主管单位)。  
法律、行政法规对民办非企业单位的监督管理另有规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 5** The civil affairs department of the State Council and the civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be the registration administration organs for private non-enterprise units of the people's governments at the corresponding levels (hereinafter referred to as the registration administration organs).  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, as well as organizations authorized by the State Council or local people's governments at or above the county level, shall be the competent business units for private non-enterprise units within the relevant industries and business scopes (hereinafter referred to as competent business units).  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the supervision and administration of private non-enterprise units, the provisions of such laws and administrative regulations shall prevail.

### 第二章　管辖

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　登记管理机关负责同级业务主管单位审查同意的民办非企业单位的登记管理。

**Article 6** The registration administration organ shall be responsible for the registration administration of private non-enterprise units that have been examined and consented to by the competent business unit at the same level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　登记管理机关、业务主管单位与其管辖的民办非企业单位的住所不在一地的，可以委托民办非企业单位住所地的登记管理机关、业务主管单位负责委托范围内的监督管理工作。

**Article 7** Where the domicile of a registration administration organ or a competent business unit is not in the same place as that of the private non-enterprise unit under its jurisdiction, it may entrust the registration administration organ or competent business unit in the place of the domicile of the private non-enterprise unit to be responsible for the supervision and administration work within the scope of entrustment.

### 第三章　登记

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请登记民办非企业单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)经业务主管单位审查同意；  
(二)有规范的名称、必要的组织机构；  
(三)有与其业务活动相适应的从业人员；  
(四)有与其业务活动相适应的合法财产；  
(五)有必要的场所。  
民办非企业单位的名称应当符合国务院民政部门的规定，不得冠以“中国”、“全国”、“中华”等字样。

**Article 8** To apply for registration of a private non-enterprise unit, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Having passed the examination and consent of the competent business unit;  
(2) Having a standard name and necessary organizational structure;  
(3) Having employees who are suitable for its business activities;  
(4) Having lawful property that is suitable for its business activities;  
(5) Having necessary premises.  
The name of a private non-enterprise unit shall comply with the provisions of the civil affairs department of the State Council, and shall not be prefixed with words such as 'China', 'National', 'Zhonghua', etc.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请民办非企业单位登记，举办者应当向登记管理机关提交下列文件：  
(一)登记申请书；  
(二)业务主管单位的批准文件；  
(三)场所使用权证明；  
(四)验资报告；  
(五)拟任负责人的基本情况、身份证明；  
(六)章程草案。

**Article 9** To apply for registration of a private non-enterprise unit, the sponsor(s) shall submit the following documents to the registration administration organ:  
(1) An application for registration;  
(2) The document of approval from the competent business unit;  
(3) A certificate of the right to use the premises;  
(4) A capital verification report;  
(5) Basic information and identity certificate of the proposed responsible person;  
(6) A draft of the articles of association.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民办非企业单位的章程应当包括下列事项：  
(一)名称、住所；  
(二)宗旨和业务范围；  
(三)组织管理制度；  
(四)法定代表人或者负责人的产生、罢免的程序；  
(五)资产管理和使用的原则；  
(六)章程的修改程序；  
(七)终止程序和终止后资产的处理；  
(八)需要由章程规定的其他事项。

**Article 10** The articles of association of a private non-enterprise unit shall include the following matters:  
(1) Name and domicile;  
(2) Purpose and business scope;  
(3) Organizational management system;  
(4) Procedures for the election and removal of the legal representative or responsible person;  
(5) Principles for asset management and use;  
(6) Procedure for amending the articles of association;  
(7) Procedure for termination and disposal of assets after termination;  
(8) Other matters that need to be prescribed by the articles of association.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　登记管理机关应当自收到成立登记申请的全部有效文件之日起60日内作出准予登记或者不予登记的决定。  
有下列情形之一的，登记管理机关不予登记，并向申请人说明理由：  
(一)有根据证明申请登记的民办非企业单位的宗旨、业务范围不符合[本条例第四条](#art-4)规定的；  
(二)在申请成立时弄虚作假的；  
(三)在同一行政区域内已有业务范围相同或者相似的民办非企业单位，没有必要成立的；  
(四)拟任负责人正在或者曾经受到剥夺政治权利的刑事处罚，或者不具有完全民事行为能力的；  
(五) 有法律、行政法规禁止的其他情形的。

**Article 11** The registration administration organ shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving all valid documents for the application for establishment registration, make a decision on whether to grant registration or not.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, the registration administration organ shall not grant registration and shall explain the reasons to the applicant:  
(1) There is evidence proving that the purpose or business scope of the private non-enterprise unit applying for registration does not conform to the provisions of Article 4 of these Regulations;  
(2) Fraud is practiced in the application for establishment;  
(3) There is already a private non-enterprise unit with the same or similar business scope within the same administrative region, and there is no need for establishment;  
(4) The proposed responsible person is currently or has been subject to criminal punishment of deprivation of political rights, or does not have full capacity for civil conduct;  
(5) There are other circumstances prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　准予登记的民办非企业单位，由登记管理机关登记民办非企业单位的名称、住所、宗旨和业务范围、法定代表人或者负责人、开办资金、业务主管单位，并根据其依法承担民事责任的不同方式，分别发给《民办非企业单位(法人)登记证书》、《民办非企业单位(合伙)登记证书》、《民办非企业单位(个体)登记证书》。  
依照法律、其他行政法规规定，经有关主管部门依法审核或者登记，已经取得相应的执业许可证书的民办非企业单位，登记管理机关应当简化登记手续，凭有关主管部门出具的执业许可证明文件，发给相应的民办非企业单位登记证书。

**Article 12** For a private non-enterprise unit that is granted registration, the registration administration organ shall register its name, domicile, purpose and business scope, legal representative or responsible person, start-up capital, and competent business unit, and shall, according to the different ways in which it bears civil liability according to law, respectively issue the 'Registration Certificate for Private Non-enterprise Unit (Legal Person)', 'Registration Certificate for Private Non-enterprise Unit (Partnership)', or 'Registration Certificate for Private Non-enterprise Unit (Individual)'.  
Where, in accordance with laws or other administrative regulations, a private non-enterprise unit has passed the examination or registration by the relevant competent department according to law and has obtained the corresponding practice license certificate, the registration administration organ shall simplify the registration procedures and, based on the practice license certificate issued by the relevant competent department, issue the corresponding registration certificate for the private non-enterprise unit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民办非企业单位不得设立分支机构。

**Article 13** Private non-enterprise units shall not establish branch offices.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民办非企业单位凭登记证书申请刻制印章，开立银行帐户。民办非企业单位应当将印章式样、银行帐号报登记管理机关备案。

**Article 14** A private non-enterprise unit shall, with its registration certificate, apply for the engraving of seals and the opening of bank accounts. The private non-enterprise unit shall report the seal impressions and bank account numbers to the registration administration organ for archival purposes.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民办非企业单位的登记事项需要变更的，应当自业务主管单位审查同意之日起30日内，向登记管理机关申请变更登记。  
民办非企业单位修改章程，应当自业务主管单位审查同意之日起30日内，报登记管理机关核准。

**Article 15** Where the registration items of a private non-enterprise unit need to be changed, it shall, within 30 days from the date of examination and consent by the competent business unit, apply to the registration administration organ for change registration.  
Where a private non-enterprise unit amends its articles of association, it shall, within 30 days from the date of examination and consent by the competent business unit, submit them to the registration administration organ for verification and approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民办非企业单位自行解散的，分立、合并的，或者由于其他原因需要注销登记的，应当向登记管理机关办理注销登记。  
民办非企业单位在办理注销登记前，应当在业务主管单位和其他有关机关的指导下，成立清算组织，完成清算工作。清算期间，民办非企业单位不得开展清算以外的活动。

**Article 16** Where a private non-enterprise unit dissolves itself, splits, merges, or needs to cancel registration due to other reasons, it shall handle the cancellation registration with the registration administration organ.  
Before handling the cancellation registration, the private non-enterprise unit shall, under the guidance of the competent business unit and other relevant organs, establish a liquidation organization and complete the liquidation work. During the liquidation period, the private non-enterprise unit shall not engage in any activities other than those related to liquidation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民办非企业单位法定代表人或者负责人应当自完成清算之日起15日内，向登记管理机关办理注销登记。办理注销登记，须提交注销登记申请书、业务主管单位的审查文件和清算报告。  
登记管理机关准予注销登记的，发给注销证明文件，收缴登记证书、印章和财务凭证。

**Article 17** The legal representative or responsible person of a private non-enterprise unit shall, within 15 days from the date of completion of liquidation, handle the cancellation registration with the registration administration organ. To handle the cancellation registration, an application for cancellation registration, the examination document of the competent business unit, and the liquidation report shall be submitted.  
Where the registration administration organ approves the cancellation registration, it shall issue a cancellation certificate and collect the registration certificate, seals, and financial documents.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民办非企业单位成立、注销以及变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者负责人，由登记管理机关予以公告。

**Article 18** The establishment, cancellation, and change of name, domicile, legal representative or responsible person of a private non-enterprise unit shall be announced by the registration administration organ.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　登记管理机关履行下列监督管理职责：  
(一)负责民办非企业单位的成立、变更、注销登记；  
(二)对民办非企业单位实施年度检查；  
(三)对民办非企业单位违反本条例的问题进行监督检查，对民办非企业单位违反本条例的行为给予行政处罚。

**Article 19** The registration administration organ shall perform the following supervision and administration duties:  
(1) Being responsible for the registration of establishment, change, and cancellation of private non-enterprise units;  
(2) Carrying out annual inspection of private non-enterprise units;  
(3) Supervising and inspecting issues concerning private non-enterprise units' violation of these Regulations, and imposing administrative penalties for acts violating these Regulations by private non-enterprise units.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　业务主管单位履行下列监督管理职责：  
(一)负责民办非企业单位成立、变更、注销登记前的审查；  
(二)监督、指导民办非企业单位遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策，按照章程开展活动；  
(三)负责民办非企业单位年度检查的初审；  
(四)协助登记管理机关和其他有关部门查处民办非企业单位的违法行为；  
(五)会同有关机关指导民办非企业单位的清算事宜。  
业务主管单位履行前款规定的职责，不得向民办非企业单位收取费用。

**Article 20** The competent business unit shall perform the following supervision and administration duties:  
(1) Being responsible for the examination before registration of establishment, change, and cancellation of private non-enterprise units;  
(2) Supervising and guiding private non-enterprise units to abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, and state policies, and to carry out activities in accordance with the articles of association;  
(3) Being responsible for the preliminary examination of the annual inspection of private non-enterprise units;  
(4) Assisting the registration administration organ and other relevant departments in investigating and dealing with illegal acts of private non-enterprise units;  
(5) Consulting with relevant organs to guide the liquidation matters of private non-enterprise units.  
When performing the duties prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the competent business unit shall not charge any fees to private non-enterprise units.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民办非企业单位的资产来源必须合法，任何单位和个人不得侵占、私分或者挪用民办非企业单位的资产。  
民办非企业单位开展章程规定的活动，按照国家有关规定取得的合法收入，必须用于章程规定的业务活动。  
民办非企业单位接受捐赠、资助，必须符合章程规定的宗旨和业务范围，必须根据与捐赠人、资助人约定的期限、方式和合法用途使用。民办非企业单位应当向业务主管单位报告接受、使用捐赠、资助的有关情况，并应当将有关情况以适当方式向社会公布。

**Article 21** The sources of assets of a private non-enterprise unit must be legal, and no unit or individual may encroach upon, privately divide, or misappropriate the assets of a private non-enterprise unit.  
The lawful income obtained by a private non-enterprise unit from carrying out activities prescribed in its articles of association in accordance with the relevant state provisions must be used for the business activities prescribed in the articles of association.  
Donations and subsidies accepted by a private non-enterprise unit must conform to the purpose and business scope prescribed in its articles of association, and must be used in accordance with the time limit, method, and lawful purpose agreed upon with the donor or subsidizer. The private non-enterprise unit shall report to the competent business unit the relevant information on the acceptance and use of donations and subsidies, and shall make the relevant information public to the society in an appropriate manner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民办非企业单位必须执行国家规定的财务管理制度，接受财政部门的监督；资产来源属于国家资助或者社会捐赠、资助的，还应当接受审计机关的监督。  
民办非企业单位变更法定代表人或者负责人，登记管理机关、业务主管单位应当组织对其进行财务审计。

**Article 22** A private non-enterprise unit must implement the financial management system prescribed by the state and accept supervision by the financial department; where the sources of assets involve state subsidies or social donations or subsidies, it shall also accept supervision by the audit authority.  
Where a private non-enterprise unit changes its legal representative or responsible person, the registration administration organ and the competent business unit shall organize a financial audit thereof.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民办非企业单位应当于每年3月31日前向业务主管单位报送上一年度的工作报告，经业务主管单位初审同意后，于5月31日前报送登记管理机关，接受年度检查。工作报告内容包括：本民办非企业单位遵守法律法规和国家政策的情况、依照本条例履行登记手续的情况、按照章程开展活动的情况、人员和机构变动的情况以及财务管理的情况。  
对于依照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第二款的规定发给登记证书的民办非企业单位，登记管理机关对其应当简化年度检查的内容。

**Article 23** A private non-enterprise unit shall, before March 31 each year, submit a work report for the previous year to the competent business unit, and after preliminary examination and consent by the competent business unit, submit it to the registration administration organ before May 31 for annual inspection. The work report shall include: the compliance of the private non-enterprise unit with laws, regulations, and state policies; the performance of registration procedures in accordance with these Regulations; the carrying out of activities in accordance with the articles of association; changes in personnel and institutions; and financial management.  
For a private non-enterprise unit to which a registration certificate is issued in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 12 of these Regulations, the registration administration organ shall simplify the contents of the annual inspection.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民办非企业单位在申请登记时弄虚作假，骗取登记的，或者业务主管单位撤销批准的，由登记管理机关予以撤销登记。

**Article 24** Where a private non-enterprise unit practices fraud in applying for registration and obtains registration by deception, or where the competent business unit revokes its approval, the registration administration organ shall revoke the registration.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民办非企业单位有下列情形之一的，由登记管理机关予以警告，责令改正，可以限期停止活动；情节严重的，予以撤销登记；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)涂改、出租、出借民办非企业单位登记证书，或者出租、出借民办非企业单位印章的；  
(二)超出其章程规定的宗旨和业务范围进行活动的；  
(三)拒不接受或者不按照规定接受监督检查的；  
(四)不按照规定办理变更登记的；  
(五)设立分支机构的；  
(六)从事营利性的经营活动的；  
(七)侵占、私分、挪用民办非企业单位的资产或者所接受的捐赠、资助的；  
(八)违反国家有关规定收取费用、筹集资金或者接受使用捐赠、资助的。  
前款规定的行为有违法经营额或者违法所得的，予以没收，可以并处违法经营额1倍以上3倍以下或者违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 25** Where a private non-enterprise unit has any of the following circumstances, the registration administration organ shall give it a warning, order it to make corrections, and may order it to suspend activities for a certain period; if the circumstances are serious, its registration shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Altering, leasing, or lending the registration certificate of the private non-enterprise unit, or leasing or lending the seal of the private non-enterprise unit;  
(2) Carrying out activities beyond the purpose and business scope prescribed in its articles of association;  
(3) Refusing to accept or failing to accept supervision and inspection in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) Failing to handle change registration in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Establishing branch offices;  
(6) Engaging in for-profit business activities;  
(7) Encroaching upon, privately dividing, or misappropriating the assets of the private non-enterprise unit or the donations and subsidies it has accepted;  
(8) Charging fees, raising funds, or accepting or using donations or subsidies in violation of relevant state provisions.  
If the act specified in the preceding paragraph involves illegal business turnover or illegal gains, the illegal turnover or illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal business turnover, or not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains, may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民办非企业单位的活动违反其他法律、法规的，由有关国家机关依法处理；有关国家机关认为应当撤销登记的，由登记管理机关撤销登记。

**Article 26** Where the activities of a private non-enterprise unit violate other laws or regulations, the relevant state organ shall handle the matter according to law; if the relevant state organ considers that the registration shall be revoked, the registration administration organ shall revoke the registration.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　未经登记，擅自以民办非企业单位名义进行活动的，或者被撤销登记的民办非企业单位继续以民办非企业单位名义进行活动的，由登记管理机关予以取缔，没收非法财产；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 27** Where any unit or individual, without registration, engages in activities in the name of a private non-enterprise unit, or where a private non-enterprise unit whose registration has been revoked continues to engage in activities in the name of a private non-enterprise unit, the registration administration organ shall ban the unit or individual, confiscate illegal property; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民办非企业单位被限期停止活动的，由登记管理机关封存其登记证书、印章和财务凭证。  
民办非企业单位被撤销登记的，由登记管理机关收缴登记证书和印章。

**Article 28** Where a private non-enterprise unit is ordered to suspend activities for a certain period, the registration administration organ shall seal up its registration certificate, seals, and financial documents.  
Where a private non-enterprise unit's registration is revoked, the registration administration organ shall collect its registration certificate and seals.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　登记管理机关、业务主管单位的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 29** Where a staff member of a registration administration organ or a competent business unit abuses his power, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or neglects his duty, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民办非企业单位登记证书的式样由国务院民政部门制定。  
对民办非企业单位进行年度检查不得收取费用。

**Article 30** The form of the registration certificate for private non-enterprise units shall be prescribed by the civil affairs department of the State Council.  
No fees may be charged for conducting annual inspection of private non-enterprise units.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例施行前已经成立的民办非企业单位，应当自本条例实施之日起1年内依照本条例有关规定申请登记。

**Article 31** Private non-enterprise units established before these Regulations come into force shall, within one year from the date of implementation of these Regulations, apply for registration in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
