### 最高人民法院关于人民法院审理经济行政案件不应进行调解的通知

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院审理经济行政案件  
  
不应进行调解的通知  
  
一九八五年十一月六日法（经）发〔1985〕25号  
  
全国地方各级人民法院、铁路运输各级法院、各海事法院：  
  
近几年国家制定的一些经济法律、法规大都有当事人如果对主管行政机关所作的行政处罚决定或者其他行政处理决定不服，可以在一定期限内向人民法院起诉的规定。这种行政案件的原告是受到行政处罚或者其他行政处理的企业事业单位、其他组织或者个人，被告是行使国家管理职权的主管行政机关。人民法院审理这种行政案件，不同于解决原、被告之间的民事权利义务关系问题，而是要以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，审查和确认主管行政机关依据职权所作的行政处罚决定或者其他行政处理决定是否合法、正确。因此，人民法院不应进行调解，而应在查明情况的基础上作出公正的判决：如果主管行政机关所作的行政处罚决定或者其他行政处理决定正确、合法，应当驳回原告的起诉；如果主管行政机关的行政处罚决定或者其他行政处理决定在认定事实、适用法律方面确有错误，应当予以撤销或者变更。  
  
特此通知

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Notice on People's Courts Not Conducting Mediation in Economic Administrative Cases  
November 6, 1985, Fa (Jing) Fa [1985] No. 25  
To all local People's Courts at various levels, all Railway Transport Courts, and all Maritime Courts:  
  
In recent years, many economic laws and regulations enacted by the state include provisions that if a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty decision or other administrative disposition made by the competent administrative organ, the party may file a prosecution with a People's Court within a certain period. The plaintiff in such an administrative case is an enterprise, institution, other organization, or individual that has received an administrative penalty or other administrative disposition, and the defendant is the competent administrative organ exercising state administrative powers. When a People's Court hears such an administrative case, it is different from resolving civil rights and obligations between the plaintiff and the defendant. Instead, it shall, based on facts and taking law as the criterion, examine and confirm whether the administrative penalty decision or other administrative disposition made by the competent administrative organ pursuant to its authority is lawful and correct. Therefore, a People's Court shall not conduct mediation but shall make a fair judgment on the basis of ascertaining the facts: if the administrative penalty decision or other administrative disposition made by the competent administrative organ is correct and lawful, the court shall dismiss the plaintiff's prosecution; if the administrative penalty decision or other administrative disposition made by the competent administrative organ is indeed erroneous in finding facts or applying law, the court shall revoke or modify it.  
  
Hereby notified
