# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕9号  
**公布日期**：2015-04-20  
**生效日期**：2015-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》已于2015年4月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1648次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年5月1日起施行。 2015年4月22日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕9号 为正确适用第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议决定修改的《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》，结合人民法院行政审判工作实际，现就有关条款的适用问题解释如下： 第一条　人民法院对符合起诉条件的案件应当立案，依法保障当事人行使诉讼权利。 对当事人依法提起的诉讼，人民法院应当根据行政诉讼法第五十一条的规定，一律接收起诉状。能够判断符合起诉条件的，应当当场登记立案；当场不能判断是否符合起诉条件的，应当在接收起诉状后七日内决定是否立案；七日内仍不能作出判断的，应当先予立案。 起诉状内容或者材料欠缺的，人民法院应当一次性全面告知当事人需要补正的内容、补充的材料及期限。在指定期限内补正并符合起诉条件的，应当登记立案。当事人拒绝补正或者经补正仍不符合起诉条件的，裁定不予立案，并载明不予立案的理由。 当事人对不予立案裁定不服的，可以提起上诉。 第二条　行政诉讼法第四十九条第三项规定的“有具体的诉讼请求”是指： （一）请求判决撤销或者变更行政行为； （二）请求判决行政机关履行法定职责或者给付义务； （三）请求判决确认行政行为违法； （四）请求判决确认行政行为无效；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院对符合起诉条件的案件应当立案，依法保障当事人行使诉讼权利。  
对当事人依法提起的诉讼，人民法院应当根据[行政诉讼法第五十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-51)的规定，一律接收起诉状。能够判断符合起诉条件的，应当当场登记立案；当场不能判断是否符合起诉条件的，应当在接收起诉状后七日内决定是否立案；七日内仍不能作出判断的，应当先予立案。  
起诉状内容或者材料欠缺的，人民法院应当一次性全面告知当事人需要补正的内容、补充的材料及期限。在指定期限内补正并符合起诉条件的，应当登记立案。当事人拒绝补正或者经补正仍不符合起诉条件的，裁定不予立案，并载明不予立案的理由。  
当事人对不予立案裁定不服的，可以提起上诉。

**Article 1** A People's Court shall docket a case that meets the conditions for prosecution and shall protect the party's exercise of litigation rights according to law.  
For a lawsuit filed by a party according to law, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 51 of the Administrative Procedure Law, accept the statement of complaint in all cases. If it can determine that the conditions for prosecution are met, it shall register and docket the case on the spot; if it cannot determine whether the conditions for prosecution are met on the spot, it shall decide whether to docket the case within seven days after accepting the statement of complaint; if it still cannot make a determination within seven days, it shall first docket the case.  
If the contents of the statement of complaint or the materials are incomplete, the People's Court shall inform the party in one go of all the contents to be corrected, materials to be supplemented, and the time limit. If the party makes corrections within the specified time limit and the conditions for prosecution are met, the case shall be registered and docketed. If the party refuses to make corrections or the conditions for prosecution are still not met after corrections, a ruling shall be made not to docket the case, stating the reasons for not docketing.  
If a party refuses to accept the ruling not to docket the case, it may file an appeal.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第三项规定的“有具体的诉讼请求”是指：  
（一）请求判决撤销或者变更行政行为；  
（二）请求判决行政机关履行法定职责或者给付义务；  
（三）请求判决确认行政行为违法；  
（四）请求判决确认行政行为无效；  
（五）请求判决行政机关予以赔偿或者补偿；  
（六）请求解决行政协议争议；  
（七）请求一并审查规章以下规范性文件；  
（八）请求一并解决相关民事争议；  
（九）其他诉讼请求。  
当事人未能正确表达诉讼请求的，人民法院应当予以释明。

**Article 2** The term "specific claims" as specified in Item (3) of Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law means:  
(1) requesting a judgment to revoke or modify an administrative act;  
(2) requesting a judgment that an administrative organ perform its statutory duties or payment obligations;  
(3) requesting a judgment to confirm that an administrative act is illegal;  
(4) requesting a judgment to confirm that an administrative act is invalid;  
(5) requesting a judgment that an administrative organ provide compensation or indemnity;  
(6) requesting the resolution of a dispute over an administrative agreement;  
(7) requesting a concurrent review of regulatory documents below the level of rules;  
(8) requesting a concurrent resolution of relevant civil disputes;  
(9) other claims.  
If a party fails to correctly express its claims, the People's Court shall provide clarification.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　有下列情形之一，已经立案的，应当裁定驳回起诉：  
（一）不符合[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)规定的；  
（二）超过法定起诉期限且无正当理由的；  
（三）错列被告且拒绝变更的；  
（四）未按照法律规定由法定代理人、指定代理人、代表人为诉讼行为的；  
（五）未按照法律、法规规定先向行政机关申请复议的；  
（六）重复起诉的；  
（七）撤回起诉后无正当理由再行起诉的；  
（八）行政行为对其合法权益明显不产生实际影响的；  
（九）诉讼标的已为生效裁判所羁束的；  
（十）不符合其他法定起诉条件的。  
人民法院经过阅卷、调查和询问当事人，认为不需要开庭审理的，可以径行裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 3** In any of the following circumstances, if the case has already been docketed, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the prosecution:  
(1) failing to meet the requirements of Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law;  
(2) exceeding the statutory time limit for prosecution without justifiable reasons;  
(3) incorrectly listing the defendant and refusing to make a change;  
(4) failing to conduct litigation acts by a statutory agent, designated agent, or representative as required by law;  
(5) failing to first apply to the administrative organ for reconsideration as required by laws or regulations;  
(6) instituting a duplicate prosecution;  
(7) instituting a new prosecution without justifiable reasons after withdrawing the original prosecution;  
(8) the administrative act manifestly has no actual impact on the party's lawful rights and interests;  
(9) the subject matter of the lawsuit is already bound by an effective judgment or ruling;  
(10) failing to meet other statutory conditions for prosecution.  
If the People's Court, after reviewing the case files, conducting investigation, and questioning the party, considers that a court session is unnecessary, it may directly make a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公民、法人或者其他组织依照[行政诉讼法第四十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-47)第一款的规定，对行政机关不履行法定职责提起诉讼的，应当在行政机关履行法定职责期限届满之日起六个月内提出。

**Article 4** If a citizen, legal person, or other organization, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 47 of the Administrative Procedure Law, initiates a lawsuit against an administrative organ for failing to perform its statutory duties, such lawsuit shall be filed within six months from the date on which the time limit for the administrative organ to perform its statutory duties expires.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的“行政机关负责人”，包括行政机关的正职和副职负责人。行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，可以另行委托一至二名诉讼代理人。

**Article 5** The term "head of an administrative organ" as specified in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the principal and deputy heads of the administrative organ. If the head of an administrative organ appears in court to respond to the lawsuit, he or she may additionally entrust one or two litigation agents.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[行政诉讼法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-26)第二款规定的“复议机关决定维持原行政行为”，包括复议机关驳回复议申请或者复议请求的情形，但以复议申请不符合受理条件为由驳回的除外。  
[行政诉讼法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-26)第二款规定的“复议机关改变原行政行为”，是指复议机关改变原行政行为的处理结果。

**Article 6** The term "the reconsideration organ decides to uphold the original administrative act" as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the circumstances where the reconsideration organ dismisses the reconsideration application or reconsideration request, except where the dismissal is on the ground that the reconsideration application does not meet the acceptance conditions.  
The term "the reconsideration organ alters the original administrative act" as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the Administrative Procedure Law means that the reconsideration organ changes the outcome of the original administrative act.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　复议机关决定维持原行政行为的，作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关是共同被告。原告只起诉作出原行政行为的行政机关或者复议机关的，人民法院应当告知原告追加被告。原告不同意追加的，人民法院应当将另一机关列为共同被告。

**Article 7** If the reconsideration organ decides to uphold the original administrative act, the administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ shall be co-defendants. If the plaintiff only sues the administrative organ that made the original administrative act or the reconsideration organ, the People's Court shall inform the plaintiff to add the other organ as a defendant. If the plaintiff does not agree to add it, the People's Court shall list the other organ as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关为共同被告的，以作出原行政行为的行政机关确定案件的级别管辖。

**Article 8** If the administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ are co-defendants, the level of jurisdiction over the case shall be determined based on the administrative organ that made the original administrative act.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　复议机关决定维持原行政行为的，人民法院应当在审查原行政行为合法性的同时，一并审查复议程序的合法性。  
作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关对原行政行为合法性共同承担举证责任，可以由其中一个机关实施举证行为。复议机关对复议程序的合法性承担举证责任。

**Article 9** If the reconsideration organ decides to uphold the original administrative act, the People's Court shall, while reviewing the legality of the original administrative act, concurrently review the legality of the reconsideration procedure.  
The administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ shall jointly bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the legality of the original administrative act, and one of the organs may carry out the acts of adducing evidence. The reconsideration organ shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the legality of the reconsideration procedure.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院对原行政行为作出判决的同时，应当对复议决定一并作出相应判决。  
人民法院判决撤销原行政行为和复议决定的，可以判决作出原行政行为的行政机关重新作出行政行为。  
人民法院判决作出原行政行为的行政机关履行法定职责或者给付义务的，应当同时判决撤销复议决定。  
原行政行为合法、复议决定违反法定程序的，应当判决确认复议决定违法，同时判决驳回原告针对原行政行为的诉讼请求。  
原行政行为被撤销、确认违法或者无效，给原告造成损失的，应当由作出原行政行为的行政机关承担赔偿责任；因复议程序违法给原告造成损失的，由复议机关承担赔偿责任。

**Article 10** When the People's Court renders a judgment on the original administrative act, it shall simultaneously render a corresponding judgment on the reconsideration decision.  
If the People's Court renders a judgment to revoke both the original administrative act and the reconsideration decision, it may render a judgment ordering the administrative organ that made the original administrative act to make a new administrative act.  
If the People's Court renders a judgment ordering the administrative organ that made the original administrative act to perform its statutory duties or payment obligations, it shall simultaneously render a judgment revoking the reconsideration decision.  
If the original administrative act is lawful but the reconsideration decision violates statutory procedures, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming that the reconsideration decision is illegal and simultaneously render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims against the original administrative act.  
If the original administrative act is revoked, confirmed illegal, or invalid, causing losses to the plaintiff, the administrative organ that made the original administrative act shall bear compensation liability; if losses are caused to the plaintiff due to the illegality of the reconsideration procedure, the reconsideration organ shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政机关为实现公共利益或者行政管理目标，在法定职责范围内，与公民、法人或者其他组织协商订立的具有行政法上权利义务内容的协议，属于[行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第一款第十一项规定的行政协议。  
公民、法人或者其他组织就下列行政协议提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理：  
（一）政府特许经营协议；  
（二）土地、房屋等征收征用补偿协议；  
（三）其他行政协议。

**Article 11** An agreement concluded through consultation between an administrative organ and a citizen, legal person, or other organization, within the scope of its statutory duties, for the purpose of realizing public interest or administrative management objectives, and containing rights and obligations under administrative law, falls within the administrative agreement specified in Item (11) of paragraph 1 of Article 12 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If a citizen, legal person, or other organization files an administrative lawsuit regarding the following administrative agreements, the People's Court shall accept it according to law:  
(1) government concession agreements;  
(2) compensation agreements for expropriation or requisition of land, houses, etc.;  
(3) other administrative agreements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关不依法履行、未按照约定履行协议提起诉讼的，参照民事法律规范关于诉讼时效的规定；对行政机关单方变更、解除协议等行为提起诉讼的，适用行政诉讼法及其司法解释关于起诉期限的规定。

**Article 12** If a citizen, legal person, or other organization initiates a lawsuit against an administrative organ for failing to perform an agreement according to law or failing to perform an agreement as agreed, reference shall be made to the provisions of civil legal rules on limitation of action; if a lawsuit is initiated against an administrative organ's unilateral modification or rescission of an agreement, the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law and its judicial interpretations on the time limit for prosecution shall apply.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对行政协议提起诉讼的案件，适用行政诉讼法及其司法解释的规定确定管辖法院。

**Article 13** For a case in which a lawsuit is filed regarding an administrative agreement, the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law and its judicial interpretations shall apply to determine the competent court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院审查行政机关是否依法履行、按照约定履行协议或者单方变更、解除协议是否合法，在适用行政法律规范的同时，可以适用不违反行政法和行政诉讼法强制性规定的民事法律规范。

**Article 14** When the People's Court reviews whether an administrative organ has performed an agreement according to law or as agreed, or whether its unilateral modification or rescission of an agreement is lawful, it may, while applying administrative legal norms, apply civil legal norms that do not violate the mandatory provisions of the administrative law and the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　原告主张被告不依法履行、未按照约定履行协议或者单方变更、解除协议违法，理由成立的，人民法院可以根据原告的诉讼请求判决确认协议有效、判决被告继续履行协议，并明确继续履行的具体内容；被告无法继续履行或者继续履行已无实际意义的，判决被告采取相应的补救措施；给原告造成损失的，判决被告予以赔偿。  
原告请求解除协议或者确认协议无效，理由成立的，判决解除协议或者确认协议无效，并根据合同法等相关法律规定作出处理。  
被告因公共利益需要或者其他法定理由单方变更、解除协议，给原告造成损失的，判决被告予以补偿。

**Article 15** If the plaintiff claims that the defendant has failed to perform the agreement according to law or as agreed, or that the unilateral modification or rescission of the agreement by the defendant is illegal, and the reasons are established, the People's Court may, based on the plaintiff's claims, render a judgment confirming the validity of the agreement, ordering the defendant to continue to perform the agreement, and specifying the specific content of continued performance; if the defendant is unable to continue performance or continued performance no longer has practical significance, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to take corresponding remedial measures; if losses are caused to the plaintiff, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to make compensation.  
If the plaintiff requests rescission of the agreement or confirmation of the invalidity of the agreement, and the reasons are established, the People's Court shall render a judgment to rescind the agreement or confirm its invalidity, and make disposition in accordance with the Contract Law and other relevant legal provisions.  
If the defendant unilaterally modifies or rescinds the agreement due to public interest needs or other statutory reasons, causing losses to the plaintiff, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to provide indemnity.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对行政机关不依法履行、未按照约定履行协议提起诉讼的，诉讼费用准用民事案件交纳标准；对行政机关单方变更、解除协议等行为提起诉讼的，诉讼费用适用行政案件交纳标准。

**Article 16** For a lawsuit initiated against an administrative organ for failing to perform an agreement according to law or failing to perform an agreement as agreed, litigation costs shall be applied by reference to the payment standards for civil cases; for a lawsuit initiated against an administrative organ's unilateral modification or rescission of an agreement, litigation costs shall apply the payment standards for administrative cases.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公民、法人或者其他组织请求一并审理[行政诉讼法第六十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-61)规定的相关民事争议，应当在第一审开庭审理前提出；有正当理由的，也可以在法庭调查中提出。  
有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当作出不予准许一并审理民事争议的决定，并告知当事人可以依法通过其他渠道主张权利：  
（一）法律规定应当由行政机关先行处理的；  
（二）违反民事诉讼法专属管辖规定或者协议管辖约定的；  
（三）已经申请仲裁或者提起民事诉讼的；  
（四）其他不宜一并审理的民事争议。  
对不予准许的决定可以申请复议一次。

**Article 17** If a citizen, legal person, or other organization requests a concurrent adjudication of the relevant civil disputes specified in Article 61 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the request shall be raised before the first-instance court session; if there are justifiable reasons, it may also be raised during the court investigation.  
In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall make a decision not to permit the concurrent adjudication of the civil disputes and inform the party that it may assert its rights through other channels according to law:  
(1) where the law provides that the matter shall be handled first by the administrative organ;  
(2) where it violates the provisions on exclusive jurisdiction or agreement on contractual jurisdiction under the Civil Procedure Law;  
(3) where an arbitration has already been applied for or a civil lawsuit has already been initiated;  
(4) other civil disputes that are not suitable for concurrent adjudication.  
An application for reconsideration may be filed once against the decision not to permit concurrent adjudication.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院在行政诉讼中一并审理相关民事争议的，民事争议应当单独立案，由同一审判组织审理。  
审理行政机关对民事争议所作裁决的案件，一并审理民事争议的，不另行立案。

**Article 18** If the People's Court concurrently adjudicates relevant civil disputes in an administrative action, the civil disputes shall be separately docketed and tried by the same adjudication organization.  
If, in a case involving the adjudication made by an administrative organ on a civil dispute, the civil dispute is concurrently adjudicated, no separate docketing shall be made.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院一并审理相关民事争议，适用民事法律规范的相关规定，法律另有规定的除外。  
当事人在调解中对民事权益的处分，不能作为审查被诉行政行为合法性的根据。  
行政争议和民事争议应当分别裁判。当事人仅对行政裁判或者民事裁判提出上诉的，未上诉的裁判在上诉期满后即发生法律效力。第一审人民法院应当将全部案卷一并移送第二审人民法院，由行政审判庭审理。第二审人民法院发现未上诉的生效裁判确有错误的，应当按照审判监督程序再审。

**Article 19** If the People's Court concurrently adjudicates relevant civil disputes, the relevant provisions of civil legal rules shall apply, unless the law provides otherwise.  
The disposition of civil rights and interests by a party in mediation shall not be used as a basis for reviewing the legality of the alleged administrative act.  
The administrative dispute and the civil dispute shall be adjudicated separately. If a party only appeals against the administrative judgment or the civil judgment, the judgment that is not appealed shall become legally effective upon expiration of the appeal period. The first-instance People's Court shall transfer all case files to the second-instance People's Court, and the administrative adjudication division shall try the case. If the second-instance People's Court finds that the effective judgment that has not been appealed is indeed erroneous, it shall retry the case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公民、法人或者其他组织请求人民法院一并审查[行政诉讼法第五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-53)规定的规范性文件，应当在第一审开庭审理前提出；有正当理由的，也可以在法庭调查中提出。

**Article 20** If a citizen, legal person, or other organization requests the People's Court to concurrently review the regulatory documents specified in Article 53 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the request shall be raised before the first-instance court session; if there are justifiable reasons, it may also be raised during the court investigation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　规范性文件不合法的，人民法院不作为认定行政行为合法的依据，并在裁判理由中予以阐明。作出生效裁判的人民法院应当向规范性文件的制定机关提出处理建议，并可以抄送制定机关的同级人民政府或者上一级行政机关。

**Article 21** If a regulatory document is illegal, the People's Court shall not take it as the basis for determining the legality of an administrative act, and shall clarify this in the reasons for the judgment. The People's Court that renders the effective judgment shall propose handling suggestions to the authority that formulated the regulatory document, and may send a copy to the people's government at the same level as the formulating authority or the administrative organ at the next higher level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　原告请求被告履行法定职责的理由成立，被告违法拒绝履行或者无正当理由逾期不予答复的，人民法院可以根据[行政诉讼法第七十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-72)的规定，判决被告在一定期限内依法履行原告请求的法定职责；尚需被告调查或者裁量的，应当判决被告针对原告的请求重新作出处理。

**Article 22** If the reasons for the plaintiff's request that the defendant perform its statutory duties are established, and the defendant illegally refuses to perform or fails to respond within the time limit without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may, in accordance with Article 72 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment ordering the defendant to perform the statutory duties requested by the plaintiff within a certain time limit according to law; if the defendant still needs to investigate or exercise discretion, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to make a new disposition in response to the plaintiff's request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　原告申请被告依法履行支付抚恤金、最低生活保障待遇或者社会保险待遇等给付义务的理由成立，被告依法负有给付义务而拒绝或者拖延履行义务且无正当理由的，人民法院可以根据[行政诉讼法第七十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-73)的规定，判决被告在一定期限内履行相应的给付义务。

**Article 23** If the reasons for the plaintiff's application that the defendant perform payment obligations such as paying pensions, minimum living allowances, or social insurance benefits according to law are established, and the defendant is legally obligated to make such payments but refuses or delays performance without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may, in accordance with Article 73 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment ordering the defendant to perform the corresponding payment obligations within a certain time limit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人向上一级人民法院申请再审，应当在判决、裁定或者调解书发生法律效力后六个月内提出。有下列情形之一的，自知道或者应当知道之日起六个月内提出：  
（一）有新的证据，足以推翻原判决、裁定的；  
（二）原判决、裁定认定事实的主要证据是伪造的；  
（三）据以作出原判决、裁定的法律文书被撤销或者变更的；  
（四）审判人员审理该案件时有贪污受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁判行为的。

**Article 24** A party applying for a retrial to the People's Court at the next higher level shall file the application within six months after the judgment, ruling, or mediation document takes legal effect. In any of the following circumstances, the application shall be filed within six months from the date on which the party knows or should have known:  
(1) there is new evidence sufficient to overturn the original judgment or ruling;  
(2) the main evidence on which the facts were found in the original judgment or ruling is forged;  
(3) the legal document on which the original judgment or ruling was based has been revoked or modified;  
(4) the adjudicators who tried the case committed acts of embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, or perversion of the law in making the judgment.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一的，当事人可以向人民检察院申请抗诉或者检察建议：  
（一）人民法院驳回再审申请的；  
（二）人民法院逾期未对再审申请作出裁定的；  
（三）再审判决、裁定有明显错误的。  
人民法院基于抗诉或者检察建议作出再审判决、裁定后，当事人申请再审的，人民法院不予立案。

**Article 25** In any of the following circumstances, a party may apply to the People's Procuratorate for a protest or procuratorial recommendation:  
(1) the People's Court has dismissed the application for retrial;  
(2) the People's Court has failed to make a ruling on the application for retrial within the prescribed time limit;  
(3) the judgment or ruling made upon retrial has obvious errors.  
After the People's Court renders a judgment or ruling upon retrial based on a protest or procuratorial recommendation, if the party applies for retrial, the People's Court shall not docket the case.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　2015年5月1日前起诉期限尚未届满的，适用修改后的行政诉讼法关于起诉期限的规定。  
2015年5月1日前尚未审结案件的审理期限，适用修改前的行政诉讼法关于审理期限的规定。依照修改前的行政诉讼法已经完成的程序事项，仍然有效。  
对2015年5月1日前发生法律效力的判决、裁定或者行政赔偿调解书不服申请再审，或者人民法院依照审判监督程序再审的，程序性规定适用修改后的行政诉讼法的规定。

**Article 26** If the time limit for prosecution has not yet expired before May 1, 2015, the provisions of the amended Administrative Procedure Law on the time limit for prosecution shall apply.  
For cases that have not been concluded before May 1, 2015, the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law before amendment on the time limit for trial shall apply. Procedural matters already completed in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law before amendment shall remain valid.  
If an application for retrial is filed against a judgment, ruling, or administrative compensation mediation document that took legal effect before May 1, 2015, or if the People's Court conducts a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the procedural provisions of the amended Administrative Procedure Law shall apply.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 27** If any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
