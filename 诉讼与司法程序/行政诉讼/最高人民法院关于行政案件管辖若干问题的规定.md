# 最高人民法院关于行政案件管辖若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕1号  
**公布日期**：2007-12-17  
**生效日期**：2008-02-01

> 《最高人民法院关于行政案件管辖若干问题的规定》已于2007年12月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1441次会议通过，现予公布，自2008年2月1日起施行。 2008年1月14日 最高人民法院 关于行政案件管辖若干问题的规定 法释〔2008〕1号 为保证人民法院依法公正审理行政案件，切实保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，维护和监督行政机关依法行使职权，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》制定本规定。 第一条  有下列情形之一的，属于行政诉讼法第十四条第（三）项规定的应当由中级人民法院管辖的第一审行政案件： （一）被告为县级以上人民政府的案件，但以县级人民政府名义办理不动产物权登记的案件可以除外； （二）社会影响重大的共同诉讼、集团诉讼案件； （三）重大涉外或者涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的案件； （四）其他重大、复杂的案件。 第二条  当事人以案件重大复杂为由或者认为有管辖权的基层人民法院不宜行使管辖权，直接向中级人民法院起诉，中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在7日内分别作出以下处理： （一）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖； （二）决定自己审理； （三）书面告知当事人向有管辖权的基层人民法院起诉。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-14)第（三）项规定的应当由中级人民法院管辖的第一审行政案件：  
（一）被告为县级以上人民政府的案件，但以县级人民政府名义办理不动产物权登记的案件可以除外；  
（二）社会影响重大的共同诉讼、集团诉讼案件；  
（三）重大涉外或者涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的案件；  
（四）其他重大、复杂的案件。

**Article 1** Under any of the following circumstances, the case shall be an administrative action of first instance that shall be under the jurisdiction of an Intermediate People's Court as provided in Article 14, item (3) of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) A case where the defendant is a people's government at or above the county level, except for a case where the registration of a real right in immovable property is handled in the name of a people's government at the county level;  
(2) A joint action or class action with significant social impact;  
(3) A case involving foreign interests, or involving the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region;  
(4) Any other case of major complexity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  当事人以案件重大复杂为由或者认为有管辖权的基层人民法院不宜行使管辖权，直接向中级人民法院起诉，中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在7日内分别作出以下处理：  
（一）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖；  
（二）决定自己审理；  
（三）书面告知当事人向有管辖权的基层人民法院起诉。

**Article 2** Where a party directly initiates a prosecution before an Intermediate People's Court on the ground that the case is of major complexity or that the Primary People's Court with jurisdiction is not suitable to exercise jurisdiction, the Intermediate People's Court shall, depending on the circumstances, make one of the following dispositions within 7 days:  
(1) Designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to exercise jurisdiction;  
(2) Decide to hear the case by itself;  
(3) Notify the party in writing to initiate a prosecution before the Primary People's Court with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  当事人向有管辖权的基层人民法院起诉，受诉人民法院在7日内未立案也未作出裁定，当事人向中级人民法院起诉，中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在7日内分别作出以下处理：  
（一）要求有管辖权的基层人民法院依法处理；  
（二）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖；  
（三）决定自己审理。

**Article 3** Where a party initiates a prosecution before the Primary People's Court with jurisdiction, the court receiving the prosecution neither opens a case nor makes a ruling within 7 days, and the party then initiates a prosecution before the Intermediate People's Court, the Intermediate People's Court shall, depending on the circumstances, make one of the following dispositions within 7 days:  
(1) Request the Primary People's Court with jurisdiction to handle the case according to law;  
(2) Designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to exercise jurisdiction;  
(3) Decide to hear the case by itself.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  基层人民法院对其管辖的第一审行政案件，认为需要由中级人民法院审理或者指定管辖的，可以报请中级人民法院决定。中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在7日内分别作出以下处理：  
（一）决定自己审理；  
（二）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖；  
（三）决定由报请的人民法院审理。

**Article 4** Where a Primary People's Court, with respect to an administrative action of first instance under its jurisdiction, considers that the case needs to be heard by an Intermediate People's Court or to be subject to designated jurisdiction, it may report to the Intermediate People's Court for a decision. The Intermediate People's Court shall, depending on the circumstances, make one of the following dispositions within 7 days:  
(1) Decide to hear the case by itself;  
(2) Designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to exercise jurisdiction;  
(3) Decide to have the case heard by the Primary People's Court that made the report.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  中级人民法院对基层人民法院管辖的第一审行政案件，根据案件情况，可以决定自己审理，也可以指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖。

**Article 5** With respect to an administrative action of first instance under the jurisdiction of a Primary People's Court, the Intermediate People's Court may, based on the circumstances of the case, decide to hear the case by itself, or may designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to exercise jurisdiction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  指定管辖裁定应当分别送达被指定管辖的人民法院及案件当事人。[本规定第四条](#art-4)的指定管辖裁定还应当送达报请的人民法院。

**Article 6** The ruling on designated jurisdiction shall be served on the designated People's Court and the parties to the case respectively. The ruling on designated jurisdiction under Article 4 of these Provisions shall also be served on the People's Court that made the report.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  对指定管辖裁定有异议的，不适用管辖异议的规定。

**Article 7** Where an objection is raised against a ruling on designated jurisdiction, the provisions on challenge to jurisdiction shall not apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  执行本规定的审理期限，提级管辖从决定之日起计算；指定管辖或者决定由报请的人民法院审理的，从收到指定管辖裁定或者决定之日起计算。

**Article 8** The time limit for trial under these Provisions shall be calculated, in the case of elevation of jurisdiction, from the date of the decision; in the case of designated jurisdiction or a decision to have the case heard by the Primary People's Court that made the report, from the date of receipt of the ruling on designated jurisdiction or the decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  中级人民法院和高级人民法院管辖的第一审行政案件需要由上一级人民法院审理或者指定管辖的，参照本规定。

**Article 9** Where an administrative action of first instance under the jurisdiction of an Intermediate People's Court or a Higher People's Court needs to be heard by a People's Court at the next higher level or to be subject to designated jurisdiction, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  本规定施行前已经立案的不适用本规定。本院以前所作的司法解释及规范性文件，凡与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 10** These Provisions shall not apply to cases that have already been docketed before these Provisions come into force. Where any previous judicial interpretation or normative document of this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall govern.
