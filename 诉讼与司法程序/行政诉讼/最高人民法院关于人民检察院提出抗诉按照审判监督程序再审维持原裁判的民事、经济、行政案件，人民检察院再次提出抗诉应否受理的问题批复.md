# 最高人民法院关于人民检察院提出抗诉按照审判监督程序再审维持原裁判的民事、经济、行政案件，人民检察院再次提出抗诉应否受理的问题批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于人民检察院提出抗诉按照审判监督程序  
再审维持原裁判的民事、经济、行政案件，  
人民检察院再次提出抗诉应否  
受理问题的批复  
1995年10月6日      法复〔1995〕7号  
四川省高级人民法院：  
你院关于人民检察院提出抗诉，人民法院按照审判监督程序再审维持原裁判的民事、经济、行政案件，人民检察院再次提出抗诉，人民法院应否受理的请示收悉。经研究，同意你院意见，即上级人民检察院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的民事、经济、行政案件提出抗诉的，无论是同级人民法院再审还是指令下级人民法院再审，凡作出维持原裁判的判决、裁定后，原提出抗诉的人民检察院再次提出抗诉的，人民法院不予受理；原提出抗诉的人民检察院的上级人民检察院提出抗诉的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning the Issue of Whether a People's Court Shall Accept a Second Protest Lodged by a People's Procuratorate in Civil, Economic, and Administrative Cases Where the Original Judgment Has Been Sustained in Retrial Under the Adjudication Supervision Procedure  
October 6, 1995 Fa Fu [1995] No. 7  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your request concerning the issue of whether a People's Court shall accept a second protest lodged by a People's Procuratorate in civil, economic, and administrative cases where the People's Court sustains the original judgment in retrial under the adjudication supervision procedure after a protest has been lodged by the People's Procuratorate has been received. Upon review, we agree with your opinion that, where a higher People's Procuratorate lodges a protest against a civil, economic, or administrative case in which the judgment or ruling of a lower People's Court has already taken legal effect, regardless of whether the retrial is conducted by the People's Court at the same level or by a lower People's Court as directed, and after a judgment or ruling sustaining the original judgment or ruling is rendered, if the People's Procuratorate that originally lodged the protest lodges a second protest, the People's Court shall not accept it; if a People's Procuratorate at a higher level than the one that originally lodged the protest lodges a protest, the People's Court shall accept it.
