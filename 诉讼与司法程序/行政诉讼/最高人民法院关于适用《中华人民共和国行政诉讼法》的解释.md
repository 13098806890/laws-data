# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕1号  
**公布日期**：2017-11-13  
**生效日期**：2018-02-08

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉的解释》已于2017年11月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1726次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年2月8日起施行。 2018年2月6日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的解释 法释〔2018〕1号 为正确适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法），结合人民法院行政审判工作实际，制定本解释。 一、受案范围 第一条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关及其工作人员的行政行为不服，依法提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。 下列行为不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围： （一）公安、国家安全等机关依照刑事诉讼法的明确授权实施的行为； （二）调解行为以及法律规定的仲裁行为； （三）行政指导行为； （四）驳回当事人对行政行为提起申诉的重复处理行为； （五）行政机关作出的不产生外部法律效力的行为； （六）行政机关为作出行政行为而实施的准备、论证、研究、层报、咨询等过程性行为；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关及其工作人员的行政行为不服，依法提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。  
下列行为不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围：  
（一）公安、国家安全等机关依照刑事诉讼法的明确授权实施的行为；  
（二）调解行为以及法律规定的仲裁行为；  
（三）行政指导行为；  
（四）驳回当事人对行政行为提起申诉的重复处理行为；  
（五）行政机关作出的不产生外部法律效力的行为；  
（六）行政机关为作出行政行为而实施的准备、论证、研究、层报、咨询等过程性行为；  
（七）行政机关根据人民法院的生效裁判、协助执行通知书作出的执行行为，但行政机关扩大执行范围或者采取违法方式实施的除外；  
（八）上级行政机关基于内部层级监督关系对下级行政机关作出的听取报告、执法检查、督促履责等行为；  
（九）行政机关针对信访事项作出的登记、受理、交办、转送、复查、复核意见等行为；  
（十）对公民、法人或者其他组织权利义务不产生实际影响的行为。

**Article 1** If a citizen, legal person, or other organization refuses to accept an administrative act of an administrative agency or its staff member and initiates a lawsuit according to law, it shall fall within the scope of administrative actions accepted by the People's Court.  
The following acts shall not fall within the scope of administrative actions accepted by the People's Court:  
(1) Acts carried out by public security, national security, and other authorities in accordance with the explicit authorization of the Criminal Procedure Law;  
(2) Mediation acts and arbitration acts as prescribed by law;  
(3) Administrative guidance acts;  
(4) Repeated disposition acts that reject a party's petition against an administrative act;  
(5) Acts of an administrative agency that do not produce external legal effect;  
(6) Procedural acts such as preparation, demonstration, research, hierarchical reporting, consultation, etc., carried out by an administrative agency for the purpose of making an administrative act;  
(7) Enforcement acts carried out by an administrative agency in accordance with an effective judgment or a notice to assist in enforcement of the People's Court, except where the administrative agency expands the scope of enforcement or carries out enforcement by illegal means;  
(8) Acts such as hearing reports, conducting law enforcement inspections, and supervising performance of duties carried out by a higher-level administrative agency to a lower-level administrative agency based on an internal hierarchical supervision relationship;  
(9) Acts such as registration, acceptance, assignment, forwarding, review, and re-examination opinions made by an administrative agency in response to petition matters;  
(10) Acts that have no actual impact on the rights and obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[行政诉讼法第十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-13)第一项规定的“国家行为”，是指国务院、中央军事委员会、国防部、外交部等根据宪法和法律的授权，以国家的名义实施的有关国防和外交事务的行为，以及经宪法和法律授权的国家机关宣布紧急状态等行为。  
[行政诉讼法第十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-13)第二项规定的“具有普遍约束力的决定、命令”，是指行政机关针对不特定对象发布的能反复适用的规范性文件。  
[行政诉讼法第十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-13)第三项规定的“对行政机关工作人员的奖惩、任免等决定”，是指行政机关作出的涉及行政机关工作人员公务员权利义务的决定。  
[行政诉讼法第十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-13)第四项规定的“法律规定由行政机关最终裁决的行政行为”中的“法律”，是指全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定、通过的规范性文件。  
二、管辖

**Article 2** The "acts of state" as specified in item (1) of Article 13 of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to acts concerning national defense and foreign affairs carried out by the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the Ministry of National Defense, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, etc., in the name of the state in accordance with the authorization of the Constitution and laws, as well as acts such as the declaration of a state of emergency by state organs authorized by the Constitution and laws.  
The "decisions and orders with general binding force" as specified in item (2) of Article 13 of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to normative documents issued by administrative agencies to unspecified objects that may be repeatedly applied.  
The "decisions on rewards, punishments, appointments, and removals of staff members of administrative agencies" as specified in item (3) of Article 13 of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to decisions made by administrative agencies concerning the rights and obligations of civil servants who are staff members of administrative agencies.  
The "law" in "administrative acts for which the law provides that the administrative agency shall make the final adjudication" as specified in item (4) of Article 13 of the Administrative Procedure Law refers to normative documents enacted and adopted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee.  
II. Jurisdiction

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民法院行政审判庭审理行政案件和审查行政机关申请执行其行政行为的案件。  
专门人民法院、人民法庭不审理行政案件，也不审查和执行行政机关申请执行其行政行为的案件。铁路运输法院等专门人民法院审理行政案件，应当执行[行政诉讼法第十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-18)第二款的规定。

**Article 3** The administrative divisions of the People's Courts at all levels shall adjudicate administrative cases and examine cases where an administrative agency applies for enforcement of its administrative acts.  
Specialized People's Courts and People's Tribunal shall not adjudicate administrative cases, nor shall they examine or enforce cases where an administrative agency applies for enforcement of its administrative acts. Where specialized People's Courts such as railway transport courts adjudicate administrative cases, they shall implement the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 18 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　立案后，受诉人民法院的管辖权不受当事人住所地改变、追加被告等事实和法律状态变更的影响。

**Article 4** After a case is docketed, the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepts the case shall not be affected by changes in the facts or legal status such as a change in the party's domicile or the addition of a defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-15)第三项规定的“本辖区内重大、复杂的案件”：  
（一）社会影响重大的共同诉讼案件；  
（二）涉外或者涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的案件；  
（三）其他重大、复杂案件。

**Article 5** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a "major or complex case within its jurisdiction" as specified in item (3) of Article 15 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) A joint action case with significant social impact;  
(2) A case involving foreign elements or involving the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region;  
(3) Other major or complex cases.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人以案件重大复杂为由，认为有管辖权的基层人民法院不宜行使管辖权或者根据[行政诉讼法第五十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-52)的规定，向中级人民法院起诉，中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在七日内分别作出以下处理：  
（一）决定自行审理；  
（二）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖；  
（三）书面告知当事人向有管辖权的基层人民法院起诉。

**Article 6** Where a party, on the ground that the case is major or complex, believes that the Primary People's Court with jurisdiction is not suitable to exercise jurisdiction, or initiates a lawsuit with an Intermediate People's Court in accordance with Article 52 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the Intermediate People's Court shall, within seven days, make one of the following dispositions according to the circumstances:  
(1) Decide to adjudicate the case itself;  
(2) Designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to have jurisdiction;  
(3) Inform the party in writing to initiate a lawsuit with the Primary People's Court having jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　基层人民法院对其管辖的第一审行政案件，认为需要由中级人民法院审理或者指定管辖的，可以报请中级人民法院决定。中级人民法院应当根据不同情况在七日内分别作出以下处理：  
（一）决定自行审理；  
（二）指定本辖区其他基层人民法院管辖；  
（三）决定由报请的人民法院审理。

**Article 7** Where a Primary People's Court, regarding an administrative case of first instance within its jurisdiction, believes that the case needs to be adjudicated by an Intermediate People's Court or designated for jurisdiction, it may report to the Intermediate People's Court for a decision. The Intermediate People's Court shall, within seven days, make one of the following dispositions according to the circumstances:  
(1) Decide to adjudicate the case itself;  
(2) Designate another Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to have jurisdiction;  
(3) Decide that the case shall be adjudicated by the Primary People's Court that made the report.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[行政诉讼法第十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-19)规定的“原告所在地”，包括原告的户籍所在地、经常居住地和被限制人身自由地。  
对行政机关基于同一事实，既采取限制公民人身自由的行政强制措施，又采取其他行政强制措施或者行政处罚不服的，由被告所在地或者原告所在地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 8** The "place where the plaintiff is located" as provided in Article 19 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the plaintiff's domicile, habitual residence, and the place where the plaintiff's personal freedom is restricted.  
Where a party refuses to accept both the administrative compulsory measure restricting a citizen's personal freedom and other administrative compulsory measures or administrative penalties taken by an administrative agency based on the same fact, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the place where the defendant is located or the place where the plaintiff is located.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[行政诉讼法第二十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-20)规定的“因不动产提起的行政诉讼”是指因行政行为导致不动产物权变动而提起的诉讼。  
不动产已登记的，以不动产登记簿记载的所在地为不动产所在地；不动产未登记的，以不动产实际所在地为不动产所在地。

**Article 9** The "administrative action initiated with respect to immovable property" as provided in Article 20 of the Administrative Procedure Law refers to an action initiated due to a change in the real right of immovable property caused by an administrative act.  
If the immovable property is registered, the place of the immovable property shall be the place recorded in the immovable property register; if the immovable property is not registered, the place of the immovable property shall be the actual location of the immovable property.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院受理案件后，被告提出管辖异议的，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十五日内提出。  
对当事人提出的管辖异议，人民法院应当进行审查。异议成立的，裁定将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院；异议不成立的，裁定驳回。  
人民法院对管辖异议审查后确定有管辖权的，不因当事人增加或者变更诉讼请求等改变管辖，但违反级别管辖、专属管辖规定的除外。

**Article 10** After the People's Court accepts a case, if the defendant raises an objection to jurisdiction, it shall do so within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the copy of the statement of complaint.  
The People's Court shall examine any objection to jurisdiction raised by a party. If the objection is sustained, it shall rule to transfer the case to a People's Court with jurisdiction; if the objection is not sustained, it shall rule to reject it.  
Where the People's Court, after examining the objection to jurisdiction, determines that it has jurisdiction, the jurisdiction shall not be changed due to a party's addition or modification of claims, except where the provisions on level jurisdiction or exclusive jurisdiction are violated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予审查：  
（一）人民法院发回重审或者按第一审程序再审的案件，当事人提出管辖异议的；  
（二）当事人在第一审程序中未按照法律规定的期限和形式提出管辖异议，在第二审程序中提出的。  
三、诉讼参加人

**Article 11** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not examine the objection:  
(1) In a case that is remanded for retrial or retried under the procedure of first instance by the People's Court, the party raises an objection to jurisdiction;  
(2) The party fails to raise an objection to jurisdiction within the time limit and in the form prescribed by law in the first instance procedure, but raises it in the second instance procedure.  
III. Participants in Litigation

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-25)第一款规定的“与行政行为有利害关系”：  
（一）被诉的行政行为涉及其相邻权或者公平竞争权的；  
（二）在行政复议等行政程序中被追加为第三人的；  
（三）要求行政机关依法追究加害人法律责任的；  
（四）撤销或者变更行政行为涉及其合法权益的；  
（五）为维护自身合法权益向行政机关投诉，具有处理投诉职责的行政机关作出或者未作出处理的；  
（六）其他与行政行为有利害关系的情形。

**Article 12** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute "having an interest in the administrative act" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 25 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) The administrative act complained of concerns the party's adjacent right or fair competition right;  
(2) The party is added as a third party in administrative reconsideration or other administrative procedures;  
(3) The party requests the administrative agency to investigate the legal liability of the injuring party according to law;  
(4) The revocation or modification of the administrative act concerns the party's lawful rights and interests;  
(5) The party files a complaint with an administrative agency to protect its own lawful rights and interests, and the administrative agency with the duty to handle the complaint either makes a disposition or fails to do so;  
(6) Other circumstances where the party has an interest in the administrative act.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　债权人以行政机关对债务人所作的行政行为损害债权实现为由提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当告知其就民事争议提起民事诉讼，但行政机关作出行政行为时依法应予保护或者应予考虑的除外。

**Article 13** Where a creditor initiates an administrative action on the ground that an administrative act of an administrative agency against a debtor impairs the realization of the creditor's claim, the People's Court shall inform the creditor to initiate a civil action regarding the civil dispute, except where the administrative agency should have protected or taken into account the creditor's interest according to law when making the administrative act.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　[行政诉讼法第二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-25)第二款规定的“近亲属”，包括配偶、父母、子女、兄弟姐妹、祖父母、外祖父母、孙子女、外孙子女和其他具有扶养、赡养关系的亲属。  
公民因被限制人身自由而不能提起诉讼的，其近亲属可以依其口头或者书面委托以该公民的名义提起诉讼。近亲属起诉时无法与被限制人身自由的公民取得联系，近亲属可以先行起诉，并在诉讼中补充提交委托证明。

**Article 14** The "close relatives" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 25 of the Administrative Procedure Law include spouse, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, maternal grandparents, grandchildren, and other relatives with a relationship of support or maintenance.  
Where a citizen is unable to initiate a lawsuit because his or her personal freedom is restricted, his or her close relatives may initiate a lawsuit in the name of the citizen on the basis of an oral or written authorization. If the close relatives are unable to contact the citizen whose personal freedom is restricted at the time of initiating the lawsuit, they may initiate the lawsuit first and submit the authorization certificate later during the litigation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　合伙企业向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当以核准登记的字号为原告。未依法登记领取营业执照的个人合伙的全体合伙人为共同原告；全体合伙人可以推选代表人，被推选的代表人，应当由全体合伙人出具推选书。  
个体工商户向人民法院提起诉讼的，以营业执照上登记的经营者为原告。有字号的，以营业执照上登记的字号为原告，并应当注明该字号经营者的基本信息。

**Article 15** Where a partnership enterprise initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court, it shall take the name approved and registered as the plaintiff. For an individual partnership that has not been registered and obtained a business license according to law, all partners shall be co-plaintiffs; all partners may select a representative, and the selected representative shall be issued a letter of selection by all partners.  
Where an individual industrial or commercial household initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court, the operator registered on the business license shall be the plaintiff. If there is a trade name, the trade name registered on the business license shall be the plaintiff, and the basic information of the operator of such trade name shall be indicated.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　股份制企业的股东大会、股东会、董事会等认为行政机关作出的行政行为侵犯企业经营自主权的，可以企业名义提起诉讼。  
联营企业、中外合资或者合作企业的联营、合资、合作各方，认为联营、合资、合作企业权益或者自己一方合法权益受行政行为侵害的，可以自己的名义提起诉讼。  
非国有企业被行政机关注销、撤销、合并、强令兼并、出售、分立或者改变企业隶属关系的，该企业或者其法定代表人可以提起诉讼。

**Article 16** Where the shareholders' general meeting, shareholders' meeting, board of directors, etc., of a joint-stock enterprise believe that an administrative act of an administrative agency infringes upon the enterprise's autonomy in operation, they may initiate a lawsuit in the name of the enterprise.  
Where the parties to a joint venture, Chinese-foreign equity joint venture, or Chinese-foreign contractual joint venture believe that the rights and interests of the joint venture, equity joint venture, or contractual joint venture, or their own lawful rights and interests are infringed upon by an administrative act, they may initiate a lawsuit in their own names.  
Where a non-state-owned enterprise is cancelled, revoked, merged, forcibly merged, sold, divided, or its subordinate relationship is changed by an administrative agency, the enterprise or its legal representative may initiate a lawsuit.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　事业单位、社会团体、基金会、社会服务机构等非营利法人的出资人、设立人认为行政行为损害法人合法权益的，可以自己的名义提起诉讼。

**Article 17** Where an investor or founder of a non-profit legal person such as a public institution, social organization, foundation, or social service institution believes that an administrative act damages the lawful rights and interests of the legal person, it may initiate a lawsuit in its own name.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　业主委员会对于行政机关作出的涉及业主共有利益的行政行为，可以自己的名义提起诉讼。  
业主委员会不起诉的，专有部分占建筑物总面积过半数或者占总户数过半数的业主可以提起诉讼。

**Article 18** The owners' committee may initiate a lawsuit in its own name against an administrative act of an administrative agency concerning the common interests of the owners.  
If the owners' committee does not initiate a lawsuit, the owners whose exclusive parts account for more than half of the total building area or who account for more than half of the total number of households may initiate a lawsuit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人不服经上级行政机关批准的行政行为，向人民法院提起诉讼的，以在对外发生法律效力的文书上署名的机关为被告。

**Article 19** Where a party refuses to accept an administrative act approved by a higher-level administrative agency and initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court, the agency that signed the document producing legal effect externally shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行政机关组建并赋予行政管理职能但不具有独立承担法律责任能力的机构，以自己的名义作出行政行为，当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以组建该机构的行政机关为被告。  
法律、法规或者规章授权行使行政职权的行政机关内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织，超出法定授权范围实施行政行为，当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以实施该行为的机构或者组织为被告。  
没有法律、法规或者规章规定，行政机关授权其内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织行使行政职权的，属于[行政诉讼法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-26)规定的委托。当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以该行政机关为被告。

**Article 20** Where an administrative agency establishes a body with administrative management functions but without the capacity to independently bear legal liability, and such body makes an administrative act in its own name, and a party refuses to accept it and initiates a lawsuit, the administrative agency that established the body shall be the defendant.  
Where an internal organ, dispatched organ, or other organization of an administrative agency, which is authorized by laws, regulations, or rules to exercise administrative functions, carries out an administrative act beyond the scope of statutory authorization, and a party refuses to accept it and initiates a lawsuit, the organ or organization that carried out the act shall be the defendant.  
Where there is no provision in any law, regulation, or rule, and an administrative agency authorizes its internal organ, dispatched organ, or other organization to exercise administrative functions, such authorization shall constitute an entrustment as provided in Article 26 of the Administrative Procedure Law. Where a party refuses to accept the act and initiates a lawsuit, the administrative agency that made the entrustment shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人对由国务院、省级人民政府批准设立的开发区管理机构作出的行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以该开发区管理机构为被告；对由国务院、省级人民政府批准设立的开发区管理机构所属职能部门作出的行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以其职能部门为被告；对其他开发区管理机构所属职能部门作出的行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以开发区管理机构为被告；开发区管理机构没有行政主体资格的，以设立该机构的地方人民政府为被告。

**Article 21** Where a party refuses to accept an administrative act made by the management body of a development zone approved and established by the State Council or the provincial-level people's government and initiates a lawsuit, such management body of the development zone shall be the defendant; where a party refuses to accept an administrative act made by a functional department subordinate to the management body of a development zone approved and established by the State Council or the provincial-level people's government and initiates a lawsuit, such functional department shall be the defendant; where a party refuses to accept an administrative act made by a functional department subordinate to the management body of another development zone and initiates a lawsuit, the management body of the development zone shall be the defendant; if the management body of the development zone does not have the status of an administrative subject, the local people's government that established such body shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[行政诉讼法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-26)第二款规定的“复议机关改变原行政行为”，是指复议机关改变原行政行为的处理结果。复议机关改变原行政行为所认定的主要事实和证据、改变原行政行为所适用的规范依据，但未改变原行政行为处理结果的，视为复议机关维持原行政行为。  
复议机关确认原行政行为无效，属于改变原行政行为。  
复议机关确认原行政行为违法，属于改变原行政行为，但复议机关以违反法定程序为由确认原行政行为违法的除外。

**Article 22** The "reconsideration agency altering the original administrative act" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the Administrative Procedure Law refers to the reconsideration agency changing the outcome of the original administrative act. Where the reconsideration agency changes the main facts and evidence found in the original administrative act, or changes the normative basis applied in the original administrative act, but does not change the outcome of the original administrative act, it shall be deemed that the reconsideration agency upholds the original administrative act.  
Where the reconsideration agency confirms that the original administrative act is void, it shall constitute an alteration of the original administrative act.  
Where the reconsideration agency confirms that the original administrative act is illegal, it shall constitute an alteration of the original administrative act, except where the reconsideration agency confirms the illegality on the ground that the original administrative act violated statutory procedures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　行政机关被撤销或者职权变更，没有继续行使其职权的行政机关的，以其所属的人民政府为被告；实行垂直领导的，以垂直领导的上一级行政机关为被告。

**Article 23** Where an administrative agency is abolished or its functions and powers are changed, and there is no administrative agency that continues to exercise its functions and powers, the people's government to which it belongs shall be the defendant; where vertical leadership is implemented, the administrative agency at the next higher level under vertical leadership shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人对村民委员会或者居民委员会依据法律、法规、规章的授权履行行政管理职责的行为不服提起诉讼的，以村民委员会或者居民委员会为被告。  
当事人对村民委员会、居民委员会受行政机关委托作出的行为不服提起诉讼的，以委托的行政机关为被告。  
当事人对高等学校等事业单位以及律师协会、注册会计师协会等行业协会依据法律、法规、规章的授权实施的行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以该事业单位、行业协会为被告。  
当事人对高等学校等事业单位以及律师协会、注册会计师协会等行业协会受行政机关委托作出的行为不服提起诉讼的，以委托的行政机关为被告。

**Article 24** Where a party refuses to accept an act of a villagers' committee or residents' committee in performing administrative management duties pursuant to authorization by laws, regulations, or rules, and initiates a lawsuit, the villagers' committee or residents' committee shall be the defendant.  
Where a party refuses to accept an act of a villagers' committee or residents' committee made under entrustment by an administrative agency, and initiates a lawsuit, the entrusting administrative agency shall be the defendant.  
Where a party refuses to accept an administrative act implemented by a public institution such as a higher education institution, or a trade association such as a lawyers' association or a certified public accountants' association, pursuant to authorization by laws, regulations, or rules, and initiates a lawsuit, such public institution or trade association shall be the defendant.  
Where a party refuses to accept an act of a public institution such as a higher education institution, or a trade association such as a lawyers' association or a certified public accountants' association, made under entrustment by an administrative agency, and initiates a lawsuit, the entrusting administrative agency shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　市、县级人民政府确定的房屋征收部门组织实施房屋征收与补偿工作过程中作出行政行为，被征收人不服提起诉讼的，以房屋征收部门为被告。  
征收实施单位受房屋征收部门委托，在委托范围内从事的行为，被征收人不服提起诉讼的，应当以房屋征收部门为被告。

**Article 25** Where the department for house expropriation designated by the people's government at the city or county level makes an administrative act in the course of organizing the implementation of house expropriation and compensation, and the person whose house is expropriated refuses to accept it and initiates a lawsuit, the department for house expropriation shall be the defendant.  
Where an entity implementing the expropriation, entrusted by the department for house expropriation, engages in acts within the scope of entrustment, and the person whose house is expropriated refuses to accept such acts and initiates a lawsuit, the department for house expropriation shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　原告所起诉的被告不适格，人民法院应当告知原告变更被告；原告不同意变更的，裁定驳回起诉。  
应当追加被告而原告不同意追加的，人民法院应当通知其以第三人的身份参加诉讼，但行政复议机关作共同被告的除外。

**Article 26** Where the defendant sued by the plaintiff is not the proper defendant, the People's Court shall inform the plaintiff to change the defendant; if the plaintiff does not agree to change, the court shall rule to reject the complaint.  
Where a defendant should be added but the plaintiff does not agree to add it, the People's Court shall notify the plaintiff to participate in the proceedings as a third party, except where a reconsideration agency acts as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　必须共同进行诉讼的当事人没有参加诉讼的，人民法院应当依法通知其参加；当事人也可以向人民法院申请参加。  
人民法院应当对当事人提出的申请进行审查，申请理由不成立的，裁定驳回；申请理由成立的，书面通知其参加诉讼。  
前款所称的必须共同进行诉讼，是指按照[行政诉讼法第二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-27)的规定，当事人一方或者双方为两人以上，因同一行政行为发生行政争议，人民法院必须合并审理的诉讼。

**Article 27** Where a party who must participate in joint litigation fails to participate in the proceedings, the People's Court shall notify it to participate according to law; a party may also apply to the People's Court for participation.  
The People's Court shall examine the application filed by a party. If the grounds for the application are not established, it shall rule to reject it; if the grounds are established, it shall notify the party in writing to participate in the proceedings.  
The "must participate in joint litigation" referred to in the preceding paragraph means that, in accordance with Article 27 of the Administrative Procedure Law, one or both parties consist of two or more persons, and an administrative dispute arises from the same administrative act, and the People's Court shall consolidate the trials.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民法院追加共同诉讼的当事人时，应当通知其他当事人。应当追加的原告，已明确表示放弃实体权利的，可不予追加；既不愿意参加诉讼，又不放弃实体权利的，应追加为第三人，其不参加诉讼，不能阻碍人民法院对案件的审理和裁判。

**Article 28** When the People's Court adds a party to joint litigation, it shall notify the other parties. If a plaintiff who should be added has clearly indicated the waiver of substantive rights, it may not be added; if the party is unwilling to participate in the proceedings but does not waive substantive rights, it shall be added as a third party, and its failure to participate in the proceedings shall not prevent the People's Court from hearing and adjudicating the case.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[行政诉讼法第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-28)规定的“人数众多”，一般指十人以上。  
根据[行政诉讼法第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-28)的规定，当事人一方人数众多的，由当事人推选代表人。当事人推选不出的，可以由人民法院在起诉的当事人中指定代表人。  
[行政诉讼法第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-28)规定的代表人为二至五人。代表人可以委托一至二人作为诉讼代理人。

**Article 29** The "numerous persons" as provided in Article 28 of the Administrative Procedure Law generally refers to ten or more persons.  
In accordance with Article 28 of the Administrative Procedure Law, where there are numerous persons on one side, the parties shall select a representative. If the parties fail to select one, the People's Court may designate a representative from among the parties who initiated the lawsuit.  
The representative as provided in Article 28 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall be two to five persons. The representative may entrust one or two persons as litigation agents.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　行政机关的同一行政行为涉及两个以上利害关系人，其中一部分利害关系人对行政行为不服提起诉讼，人民法院应当通知没有起诉的其他利害关系人作为第三人参加诉讼。  
与行政案件处理结果有利害关系的第三人，可以申请参加诉讼，或者由人民法院通知其参加诉讼。人民法院判决其承担义务或者减损其权益的第三人，有权提出上诉或者申请再审。  
[行政诉讼法第二十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-29)规定的第三人，因不能归责于本人的事由未参加诉讼，但有证据证明发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书损害其合法权益的，可以依照[行政诉讼法第九十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-90)的规定，自知道或者应当知道其合法权益受到损害之日起六个月内，向上一级人民法院申请再审。

**Article 30** Where the same administrative act of an administrative agency involves two or more interested persons, and some of them refuse to accept the administrative act and initiate a lawsuit, the People's Court shall notify the other interested persons who have not sued to participate in the proceedings as third parties.  
A third party who has an interest in the outcome of the administrative case may apply to participate in the proceedings, or the People's Court may notify it to participate. A third party whom the People's Court orders to assume obligations or whose rights or interests are diminished has the right to appeal or apply for retrial.  
Where a third party as provided in Article 29 of the Administrative Procedure Law fails to participate in the proceedings for reasons not attributable to itself, but has evidence proving that the effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document damages its lawful rights and interests, it may, in accordance with Article 90 of the Administrative Procedure Law, apply for retrial with the People's Court at the next higher level within six months from the date when it knows or should know that its lawful rights and interests are damaged.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人委托诉讼代理人，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。委托书应当载明委托事项和具体权限。公民在特殊情况下无法书面委托的，也可以由他人代书，并由自己捺印等方式确认，人民法院应当核实并记录在卷；被诉行政机关或者其他有义务协助的机关拒绝人民法院向被限制人身自由的公民核实的，视为委托成立。当事人解除或者变更委托的，应当书面报告人民法院。

**Article 31** Where a party appoints a litigation agent, it shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the appointing party. The power of attorney shall state the matters entrusted and the specific scope of authority. If a citizen is unable to make a written appointment under special circumstances, the appointment may be made in writing by another person on his or her behalf, and confirmed by the citizen's fingerprint or other means, and the People's Court shall verify and record it in the case file; if the administrative agency sued or any other agency obliged to assist refuses to allow the People's Court to verify with the citizen whose personal freedom is restricted, the appointment shall be deemed to have been established. Where a party rescinds or changes the appointment, it shall report in writing to the People's Court.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依照[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第二款第二项规定，与当事人有合法劳动人事关系的职工，可以当事人工作人员的名义作为诉讼代理人。以当事人的工作人员身份参加诉讼活动，应当提交以下证据之一加以证明：  
（一）缴纳社会保险记录凭证；  
（二）领取工资凭证；  
（三）其他能够证明其为当事人工作人员身份的证据。

**Article 32** In accordance with item (2) of paragraph 2 of Article 31 of the Administrative Procedure Law, an employee who has a lawful labor or personnel relationship with a party may act as a litigation agent in the capacity of a staff member of the party. To participate in litigation activities in the capacity of a staff member of a party, one of the following pieces of evidence shall be submitted for proof:  
(1) Certificate of payment of social insurance contributions;  
(2) Certificate of receiving wages;  
(3) Other evidence that can prove the person's status as a staff member of the party.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第二款第三项规定，有关社会团体推荐公民担任诉讼代理人的，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）社会团体属于依法登记设立或者依法免予登记设立的非营利性法人组织；  
（二）被代理人属于该社会团体的成员，或者当事人一方住所地位于该社会团体的活动地域；  
（三）代理事务属于该社会团体章程载明的业务范围；  
（四）被推荐的公民是该社会团体的负责人或者与该社会团体有合法劳动人事关系的工作人员。  
专利代理人经中华全国专利代理人协会推荐，可以在专利行政案件中担任诉讼代理人。  
四、证据

**Article 33** In accordance with item (3) of paragraph 2 of Article 31 of the Administrative Procedure Law, where a relevant social organization recommends a citizen to act as a litigation agent, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) The social organization is a non-profit legal person organization established through registration or exempted from registration according to law;  
(2) The principal is a member of such social organization, or the domicile of one party is located within the activity area of such social organization;  
(3) The agency matters fall within the scope of business stipulated in the articles of association of such social organization;  
(4) The recommended citizen is a responsible person of such social organization or a staff member having a lawful labor or personnel relationship with such social organization.  
A patent agent recommended by the All-China Patent Agents Association may act as a litigation agent in patent administrative cases.  
IV. Evidence

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　根据[行政诉讼法第三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-36)第一款的规定，被告申请延期提供证据的，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十五日内以书面方式向人民法院提出。人民法院准许延期提供的，被告应当在正当事由消除后十五日内提供证据。逾期提供的，视为被诉行政行为没有相应的证据。

**Article 34** In accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 36 of the Administrative Procedure Law, if the defendant applies for an extension of time to provide evidence, it shall submit a written application to the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the copy of the statement of complaint. If the People's Court grants the extension, the defendant shall provide the evidence within fifteen days after the legitimate reason ceases to exist. If the evidence is provided beyond the time limit, it shall be deemed that the administrative act complained of has no corresponding evidence.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　原告或者第三人应当在开庭审理前或者人民法院指定的交换证据清单之日提供证据。因正当事由申请延期提供证据的，经人民法院准许，可以在法庭调查中提供。逾期提供证据的，人民法院应当责令其说明理由；拒不说明理由或者理由不成立的，视为放弃举证权利。  
原告或者第三人在第一审程序中无正当事由未提供而在第二审程序中提供的证据，人民法院不予接纳。

**Article 35** The plaintiff or a third party shall provide evidence before the hearing or on the date designated by the People's Court for exchange of evidence lists. If an application for extension of time to provide evidence is made due to a legitimate reason, and the People's Court grants the extension, the evidence may be provided during the court investigation. If evidence is provided beyond the time limit, the People's Court shall order the party to state the reasons; if the party refuses to state the reasons or the reasons are not established, it shall be deemed that the party waives the right of adducing evidence.  
Where the plaintiff or a third party fails to provide evidence in the first instance procedure without a legitimate reason, and provides it in the second instance procedure, the People's Court shall not admit it.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　当事人申请延长举证期限，应当在举证期限届满前向人民法院提出书面申请。  
申请理由成立的，人民法院应当准许，适当延长举证期限，并通知其他当事人。申请理由不成立的，人民法院不予准许，并通知申请人。

**Article 36** Where a party applies for extension of the time limit for adducing evidence, it shall submit a written application to the People's Court before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence.  
If the grounds for the application are established, the People's Court shall grant the application, appropriately extend the time limit for adducing evidence, and notify the other parties. If the grounds for the application are not established, the People's Court shall not grant the application and shall notify the applicant.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　根据[行政诉讼法第三十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-39)的规定，对当事人无争议，但涉及国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实，人民法院可以责令当事人提供或者补充有关证据。

**Article 37** In accordance with Article 39 of the Administrative Procedure Law, regarding facts that are not disputed by the parties but involve national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court may order the parties to provide or supplement relevant evidence.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对于案情比较复杂或者证据数量较多的案件，人民法院可以组织当事人在开庭前向对方出示或者交换证据，并将交换证据清单的情况记录在卷。  
当事人在庭前证据交换过程中没有争议并记录在卷的证据，经审判人员在庭审中说明后，可以作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 38** For cases with relatively complex facts or a large number of evidence, the People's Court may organize the parties to present or exchange evidence with each other before the hearing, and record the circumstances of the exchange of evidence lists in the case file.  
Evidence that is not disputed by the parties during the pre-trial evidence exchange and is recorded in the case file may, after being explained by the trial judge during the court hearing, serve as the basis for finding the facts of the case.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　当事人申请调查收集证据，但该证据与待证事实无关联、对证明待证事实无意义或者其他无调查收集必要的，人民法院不予准许。

**Article 39** Where a party applies for investigation and collection of evidence, but such evidence is not relevant to the facts to be proved, is meaningless for proving the facts to be proved, or is otherwise unnecessary to investigate and collect, the People's Court shall not grant the application.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民法院在证人出庭作证前应当告知其如实作证的义务以及作伪证的法律后果。  
证人因履行出庭作证义务而支出的交通、住宿、就餐等必要费用以及误工损失，由败诉一方当事人承担。

**Article 40** : Before a witness testifies in court, the People's Court shall inform the witness of the obligation to testify truthfully and the legal consequences of giving false testimony.  
The necessary expenses such as transportation, accommodation, meals, and loss of wages incurred by the witness in fulfilling the obligation to testify in court shall be borne by the losing party.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列情形之一，原告或者第三人要求相关行政执法人员出庭说明的，人民法院可以准许：  
（一）对现场笔录的合法性或者真实性有异议的；  
（二）对扣押财产的品种或者数量有异议的；  
（三）对检验的物品取样或者保管有异议的；  
（四）对行政执法人员身份的合法性有异议的；  
（五）需要出庭说明的其他情形。

**Article 41** : In any of the following circumstances, if the plaintiff or the third party requests the relevant administrative law enforcement officer to appear in court to give explanations, the People's Court may permit such request:  
(1) Where there is an objection to the legality or authenticity of the on-site record;  
(2) Where there is an objection to the type or quantity of the seized property;  
(3) Where there is an objection to the sampling or custody of the inspected items;  
(4) Where there is an objection to the legality of the identity of the administrative law enforcement officer;  
(5) Other circumstances requiring appearance in court to give explanations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　能够反映案件真实情况、与待证事实相关联、来源和形式符合法律规定的证据，应当作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 42** : Evidence that can reflect the true circumstances of the case, is relevant to the facts to be proved, and whose source and form comply with legal provisions shall serve as the basis for determining the facts of the case.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第四十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-43)第三款规定的“以非法手段取得的证据”：  
（一）严重违反法定程序收集的证据材料；  
（二）以违反法律强制性规定的手段获取且侵害他人合法权益的证据材料；  
（三）以利诱、欺诈、胁迫、暴力等手段获取的证据材料。

**Article 43** : Any of the following circumstances constitutes "evidence obtained by illegal means" as stipulated in paragraph 3 of Article 43 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) Evidence materials collected by seriously violating statutory procedures;  
(2) Evidence materials obtained by means that violate mandatory provisions of the law and infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(3) Evidence materials obtained by means such as inducement, fraud, coercion, or violence.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　人民法院认为有必要的，可以要求当事人本人或者行政机关执法人员到庭，就案件有关事实接受询问。在询问之前，可以要求其签署保证书。  
保证书应当载明据实陈述、如有虚假陈述愿意接受处罚等内容。当事人或者行政机关执法人员应当在保证书上签名或者捺印。  
负有举证责任的当事人拒绝到庭、拒绝接受询问或者拒绝签署保证书，待证事实又欠缺其他证据加以佐证的，人民法院对其主张的事实不予认定。

**Article 44** : If the People's Court deems it necessary, it may require the party in person or the administrative organ's law enforcement officer to appear in court to accept inquiries regarding the relevant facts of the case. Before the inquiry, the court may require them to sign an affidavit.  
The affidavit shall state that the testimony will be truthful and that the affiant is willing to accept punishment for any false testimony. The party or the administrative organ's law enforcement officer shall sign or affix a fingerprint on the affidavit.  
If the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence refuses to appear in court, refuses to accept inquiries, or refuses to sign the affidavit, and there is a lack of other evidence to corroborate the facts to be proved, the People's Court shall not recognize the facts claimed by such party.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　被告有证据证明其在行政程序中依照法定程序要求原告或者第三人提供证据，原告或者第三人依法应当提供而没有提供，在诉讼程序中提供的证据，人民法院一般不予采纳。

**Article 45** : Where the defendant has evidence proving that during the administrative procedure it requested the plaintiff or the third party to provide evidence in accordance with statutory procedures, and the plaintiff or the third party should have provided such evidence according to law but failed to do so, the People's Court shall generally not admit the evidence provided by the plaintiff or the third party during the litigation proceedings.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　原告或者第三人确有证据证明被告持有的证据对原告或者第三人有利的，可以在开庭审理前书面申请人民法院责令行政机关提交。  
申请理由成立的，人民法院应当责令行政机关提交，因提交证据所产生的费用，由申请人预付。行政机关无正当理由拒不提交的，人民法院可以推定原告或者第三人基于该证据主张的事实成立。  
持有证据的当事人以妨碍对方当事人使用为目的，毁灭有关证据或者实施其他致使证据不能使用行为的，人民法院可以推定对方当事人基于该证据主张的事实成立，并可依照[行政诉讼法第五十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-59)规定处理。

**Article 46** : Where the plaintiff or the third party has concrete evidence proving that the evidence held by the defendant is favorable to the plaintiff or the third party, they may, before the commencement of the court hearing, submit a written application to the People's Court requesting that the court order the administrative organ to submit such evidence.  
If the grounds for the application are established, the People's Court shall order the administrative organ to submit the evidence, and the expenses incurred in submitting the evidence shall be prepaid by the applicant. If the administrative organ refuses to submit without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may presume that the facts claimed by the plaintiff or the third party based on such evidence are established.  
If a party holding evidence destroys the relevant evidence or takes other actions rendering the evidence unusable for the purpose of obstructing the opposing party's use thereof, the People's Court may presume that the facts claimed by the opposing party based on such evidence are established, and may handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 59 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　根据[行政诉讼法第三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-38)第二款的规定，在行政赔偿、补偿案件中，因被告的原因导致原告无法就损害情况举证的，应当由被告就该损害情况承担举证责任。  
对于各方主张损失的价值无法认定的，应当由负有举证责任的一方当事人申请鉴定，但法律、法规、规章规定行政机关在作出行政行为时依法应当评估或者鉴定的除外；负有举证责任的当事人拒绝申请鉴定的，由其承担不利的法律后果。  
当事人的损失因客观原因无法鉴定的，人民法院应当结合当事人的主张和在案证据，遵循法官职业道德，运用逻辑推理和生活经验、生活常识等，酌情确定赔偿数额。  
五、期间、送达

**Article 47** : Pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 38 of the Administrative Procedure Law, in cases concerning administrative compensation or indemnity, if the plaintiff is unable to adduce evidence regarding the damage due to the defendant's fault, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding such damage.  
If the value of the losses claimed by each party cannot be determined, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall apply for an expert evaluation, except where laws, regulations, or rules stipulate that the administrative organ shall conduct an appraisal or evaluation according to law when making the administrative act; if the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence refuses to apply for an expert evaluation, it shall bear the adverse legal consequences.  
If the party's losses cannot be appraised due to objective reasons, the People's Court shall, taking into account the party's claims and the evidence on record, follow the professional ethics of judges, and apply logical reasoning, life experience, and common sense to determine the amount of compensation as it deems appropriate.  
V. Time Periods and Service

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　期间包括法定期间和人民法院指定的期间。  
期间以时、日、月、年计算。期间开始的时和日，不计算在期间内。  
期间届满的最后一日是节假日的，以节假日后的第一日为期间届满的日期。  
期间不包括在途时间，诉讼文书在期满前交邮的，视为在期限内发送。

**Article 48** : Time periods include statutory time periods and time periods designated by the People's Court.  
Time periods shall be calculated in hours, days, months, and years. The hour and day from which a time period begins shall not be counted as part of the time period.  
If the last day of a time period falls on a holiday, the day after the holiday shall be deemed the last day of the time period.  
Time periods shall not include travel time; if litigation documents are posted before the expiry of the time period, they shall be deemed to have been sent within the time period.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　[行政诉讼法第五十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-51)第二款规定的立案期限，因起诉状内容欠缺或者有其他错误通知原告限期补正的，从补正后递交人民法院的次日起算。由上级人民法院转交下级人民法院立案的案件，从受诉人民法院收到起诉状的次日起算。

**Article 49** : For the case-filing period stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 51 of the Administrative Procedure Law, if the plaintiff is notified to make corrections within a specified time limit due to deficiencies in the content of the complaint or other errors, the period shall be calculated from the day after the corrected complaint is submitted to the People's Court. For cases transferred by a higher People's Court to a lower People's Court for filing, the period shall be calculated from the day after the receiving People's Court receives the complaint.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　[行政诉讼法第八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-81)、第八十三条、第八十八条规定的审理期限，是指从立案之日起至裁判宣告、调解书送达之日止的期间，但公告期间、鉴定期间、调解期间、中止诉讼期间、审理当事人提出的管辖异议以及处理人民法院之间的管辖争议期间不应计算在内。  
再审案件按照第一审程序或者第二审程序审理的，适用[行政诉讼法第八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-81)、第八十八条规定的审理期限。审理期限自再审立案的次日起算。  
基层人民法院申请延长审理期限，应当直接报请高级人民法院批准，同时报中级人民法院备案。

**Article 50** : The trial period specified in Articles 81, 83, and 88 of the Administrative Procedure Law refers to the period from the date of case filing to the date of pronouncement of judgment or service of the mediation document. However, periods for public announcement, expert evaluation, mediation, suspension of litigation, hearing the parties' objections to jurisdiction, and handling jurisdictional disputes between People's Courts shall not be counted.  
If a retrial case is tried in accordance with the procedures of first instance or second instance, the trial periods specified in Articles 81 and 88 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall apply. The trial period shall be calculated from the day after the filing of the retrial case.  
If a Primary People's Court applies for an extension of the trial period, it shall directly submit the request to the Higher People's Court for approval, and simultaneously report it to the Intermediate People's Court for record.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民法院可以要求当事人签署送达地址确认书，当事人确认的送达地址为人民法院法律文书的送达地址。  
当事人同意电子送达的，应当提供并确认传真号、电子信箱等电子送达地址。  
当事人送达地址发生变更的，应当及时书面告知受理案件的人民法院；未及时告知的，人民法院按原地址送达，视为依法送达。  
人民法院可以通过国家邮政机构以法院专递方式进行送达。

**Article 51** : The People's Court may require the parties to sign a service address confirmation form; the service address confirmed by the parties shall be the service address for the legal documents of the People's Court.  
If the parties agree to electronic service, they shall provide and confirm an electronic service address such as a fax number or email address.  
If the service address of a party changes, such party shall promptly notify the People's Court accepting the case in writing; if such party fails to notify promptly, the People's Court shall serve at the original address, and such service shall be deemed to have been made according to law.  
The People's Court may effect service through the national postal service by court express mail.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　人民法院可以在当事人住所地以外向当事人直接送达诉讼文书。当事人拒绝签署送达回证的，采用拍照、录像等方式记录送达过程即视为送达。审判人员、书记员应当在送达回证上注明送达情况并签名。  
六、起诉与受理

**Article 52** : The People's Court may directly serve litigation documents on the parties at a location other than the party's domicile. If the party refuses to sign the service receipt, recording the service process by means such as photographing or video recording shall be deemed as service. The judicial personnel and court clerk shall note the service circumstances on the service receipt and sign it.  
VI. Filing and Acceptance

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　人民法院对符合起诉条件的案件应当立案，依法保障当事人行使诉讼权利。  
对当事人依法提起的诉讼，人民法院应当根据[行政诉讼法第五十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-51)的规定接收起诉状。能够判断符合起诉条件的，应当当场登记立案；当场不能判断是否符合起诉条件的，应当在接收起诉状后七日内决定是否立案；七日内仍不能作出判断的，应当先予立案。

**Article 53** : The People's Court shall file cases that meet the conditions for filing, and shall protect the parties' exercise of litigation rights according to law.  
For a lawsuit initiated by a party according to law, the People's Court shall accept the complaint in accordance with Article 51 of the Administrative Procedure Law. If it can determine that the conditions for filing are met, it shall register and file the case on the spot; if it cannot determine on the spot whether the conditions for filing are met, it shall decide whether to file the case within seven days after receiving the complaint; if it still cannot make a determination within seven days, it shall file the case first.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依照[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)的规定，公民、法人或者其他组织提起诉讼时应当提交以下起诉材料：  
（一）原告的身份证明材料以及有效联系方式；  
（二）被诉行政行为或者不作为存在的材料；  
（三）原告与被诉行政行为具有利害关系的材料；  
（四）人民法院认为需要提交的其他材料。  
由法定代理人或者委托代理人代为起诉的，还应当在起诉状中写明或者在口头起诉时向人民法院说明法定代理人或者委托代理人的基本情况，并提交法定代理人或者委托代理人的身份证明和代理权限证明等材料。

**Article 54** : Pursuant to Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law, a citizen, legal person, or other organization shall submit the following filing materials when initiating a lawsuit:  
(1) Materials proving the plaintiff's identity and valid contact information;  
(2) Materials demonstrating the existence of the administrative act or omission complained of;  
(3) Materials demonstrating that the plaintiff has an interest in the administrative act complained of;  
(4) Other materials deemed necessary by the People's Court.  
If a statutory agent or authorized agent initiates the lawsuit on behalf of the plaintiff, the basic information of such agent shall also be stated in the complaint or explained to the People's Court during oral filing, and materials such as the identity certificate and certificate of agency authority of the agent shall be submitted.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　依照[行政诉讼法第五十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-51)的规定，人民法院应当就起诉状内容和材料是否完备以及是否符合行政诉讼法规定的起诉条件进行审查。  
起诉状内容或者材料欠缺的，人民法院应当给予指导和释明，并一次性全面告知当事人需要补正的内容、补充的材料及期限。在指定期限内补正并符合起诉条件的，应当登记立案。当事人拒绝补正或者经补正仍不符合起诉条件的，退回诉状并记录在册；坚持起诉的，裁定不予立案，并载明不予立案的理由。

**Article 55** : Pursuant to Article 51 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court shall examine whether the content and materials of the complaint are complete and whether they meet the conditions for filing stipulated in the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If the content or materials of the complaint are deficient, the People's Court shall provide guidance and explanations, and shall inform the party of the items to be corrected, materials to be supplemented, and the time limit for doing so in a single comprehensive notice. If corrections are made within the specified time limit and the conditions for filing are met, the case shall be registered and filed. If the party refuses to make corrections or the corrections still do not meet the conditions for filing, the complaint shall be returned and recorded; if the party insists on filing, a ruling shall be made not to accept the case, stating the reasons for not accepting it.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　法律、法规规定应当先申请复议，公民、法人或者其他组织未申请复议直接提起诉讼的，人民法院裁定不予立案。  
依照[行政诉讼法第四十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-45)的规定，复议机关不受理复议申请或者在法定期限内不作出复议决定，公民、法人或者其他组织不服，依法向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法立案。

**Article 56** : Where laws or regulations provide that an application for reconsideration must first be made, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization directly initiates a lawsuit without applying for reconsideration, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the case.  
Pursuant to Article 45 of the Administrative Procedure Law, if the reconsideration organ does not accept the reconsideration application or fails to make a reconsideration decision within the statutory period, and the citizen, legal person, or other organization, being dissatisfied, initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　法律、法规未规定行政复议为提起行政诉讼必经程序，公民、法人或者其他组织既提起诉讼又申请行政复议的，由先立案的机关管辖；同时立案的，由公民、法人或者其他组织选择。公民、法人或者其他组织已经申请行政复议，在法定复议期间内又向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院裁定不予立案。

**Article 57** : Where laws or regulations do not provide that administrative reconsideration is a prerequisite for initiating an administrative lawsuit, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization both initiates a lawsuit and applies for administrative reconsideration, the authority that first accepts the case shall have jurisdiction; if the cases are accepted simultaneously, the citizen, legal person, or other organization shall make the choice. If a citizen, legal person, or other organization has already applied for administrative reconsideration and subsequently initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court during the statutory reconsideration period, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the case.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　法律、法规未规定行政复议为提起行政诉讼必经程序，公民、法人或者其他组织向复议机关申请行政复议后，又经复议机关同意撤回复议申请，在法定起诉期限内对原行政行为提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法立案。

**Article 58** : Where laws or regulations do not provide that administrative reconsideration is a prerequisite for initiating an administrative lawsuit, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization, after applying to the reconsideration organ for administrative reconsideration, withdraws the reconsideration application with the consent of the reconsideration organ, and initiates a lawsuit against the original administrative act within the statutory period for filing a lawsuit, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　公民、法人或者其他组织向复议机关申请行政复议后，复议机关作出维持决定的，应当以复议机关和原行为机关为共同被告，并以复议决定送达时间确定起诉期限。

**Article 59** : Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization has applied to the reconsideration organ for administrative reconsideration, and the reconsideration organ makes a decision affirming the original act, the reconsideration organ and the original organ that made the act shall be named as co-defendants, and the period for filing a lawsuit shall be determined by the time of service of the reconsideration decision.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　人民法院裁定准许原告撤诉后，原告以同一事实和理由重新起诉的，人民法院不予立案。  
准予撤诉的裁定确有错误，原告申请再审的，人民法院应当通过审判监督程序撤销原准予撤诉的裁定，重新对案件进行审理。

**Article 60** : After the People's Court rules to permit the plaintiff to withdraw the lawsuit, if the plaintiff files a new lawsuit based on the same facts and reasons, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
If the ruling permitting the withdrawal is indeed erroneous, and the plaintiff applies for a retrial, the People's Court shall, through the adjudication supervision procedure, revoke the original ruling permitting the withdrawal and retry the case.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　原告或者上诉人未按规定的期限预交案件受理费，又不提出缓交、减交、免交申请，或者提出申请未获批准的，按自动撤诉处理。在按撤诉处理后，原告或者上诉人在法定期限内再次起诉或者上诉，并依法解决诉讼费预交问题的，人民法院应予立案。

**Article 61** : If the plaintiff or appellant fails to prepay the case acceptance fee within the prescribed time limit, and does not apply for deferred payment, reduced payment, or exemption, or if the application is not approved, the case shall be treated as automatically withdrawn. After the case is treated as withdrawn, if the plaintiff or appellant files a new lawsuit or appeal within the statutory period and resolves the issue of prepayment of litigation fees according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　人民法院判决撤销行政机关的行政行为后，公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关重新作出的行政行为不服向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法立案。

**Article 62** : After the People's Court renders a judgment annulling an administrative act of an administrative organ, if a citizen, legal person, or other organization, being dissatisfied with the new administrative act made by the administrative organ, initiates a lawsuit with the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　行政机关作出行政行为时，没有制作或者没有送达法律文书，公民、法人或者其他组织只要能证明行政行为存在，并在法定期限内起诉的，人民法院应当依法立案。

**Article 63** : If an administrative organ, when making an administrative act, fails to produce or serve a legal document, as long as a citizen, legal person, or other organization can prove the existence of such administrative act and files a lawsuit within the statutory period, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　行政机关作出行政行为时，未告知公民、法人或者其他组织起诉期限的，起诉期限从公民、法人或者其他组织知道或者应当知道起诉期限之日起计算，但从知道或者应当知道行政行为内容之日起最长不得超过一年。  
复议决定未告知公民、法人或者其他组织起诉期限的，适用前款规定。

**Article 64** : If an administrative organ, when making an administrative act, fails to inform a citizen, legal person, or other organization of the period for filing a lawsuit, such period shall be calculated from the date on which the citizen, legal person, or other organization knows or should have known the period for filing a lawsuit, but shall not exceed one year from the date on which they know or should have known the content of the administrative act.  
If a reconsideration decision fails to inform a citizen, legal person, or other organization of the period for filing a lawsuit, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　公民、法人或者其他组织不知道行政机关作出的行政行为内容的，其起诉期限从知道或者应当知道该行政行为内容之日起计算，但最长不得超过[行政诉讼法第四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-46)第二款规定的起诉期限。

**Article 65** : If a citizen, legal person, or other organization does not know the content of an administrative act made by an administrative organ, the period for filing a lawsuit shall be calculated from the date on which they know or should have known the content of such administrative act, but shall not exceed the period for filing a lawsuit as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 46 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　公民、法人或者其他组织依照[行政诉讼法第四十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-47)第一款的规定，对行政机关不履行法定职责提起诉讼的，应当在行政机关履行法定职责期限届满之日起六个月内提出。

**Article 66** : Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization initiates a lawsuit against an administrative organ's failure to perform its statutory duties in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 47 of the Administrative Procedure Law, such lawsuit shall be filed within six months from the date on which the period for the administrative organ to perform its statutory duties expires.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　原告提供被告的名称等信息足以使被告与其他行政机关相区别的，可以认定为[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第二项规定的“有明确的被告”。  
起诉状列写被告信息不足以认定明确的被告的，人民法院可以告知原告补正；原告补正后仍不能确定明确的被告的，人民法院裁定不予立案。

**Article 67** : If the plaintiff provides the name of the defendant and other information sufficient to distinguish the defendant from other administrative organs, it may be deemed as having "a clear defendant" as stipulated in item (2) of Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If the information about the defendant listed in the complaint is insufficient to identify a clear defendant, the People's Court may inform the plaintiff to make corrections; if a clear defendant still cannot be determined after the plaintiff makes corrections, the People's Court shall rule not to accept the case.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第三项规定的“有具体的诉讼请求”是指：  
（一）请求判决撤销或者变更行政行为；  
（二）请求判决行政机关履行特定法定职责或者给付义务；  
（三）请求判决确认行政行为违法；  
（四）请求判决确认行政行为无效；  
（五）请求判决行政机关予以赔偿或者补偿；  
（六）请求解决行政协议争议；  
（七）请求一并审查规章以下规范性文件；  
（八）请求一并解决相关民事争议；  
（九）其他诉讼请求。  
当事人单独或者一并提起行政赔偿、补偿诉讼的，应当有具体的赔偿、补偿事项以及数额；请求一并审查规章以下规范性文件的，应当提供明确的文件名称或者审查对象；请求一并解决相关民事争议的，应当有具体的民事诉讼请求。  
当事人未能正确表达诉讼请求的，人民法院应当要求其明确诉讼请求。

**Article 68** : "Specific litigation claims" as stipulated in item (3) of Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law means:  
(1) A claim for a judgment to annul or modify an administrative act;  
(2) A claim for a judgment ordering the administrative organ to perform a specific statutory duty or payment obligation;  
(3) A claim for a judgment confirming that an administrative act is illegal;  
(4) A claim for a judgment confirming that an administrative act is void;  
(5) A claim for a judgment ordering the administrative organ to provide compensation or indemnity;  
(6) A claim to resolve an administrative agreement dispute;  
(7) A claim for a combined review of regulatory documents below the level of rules;  
(8) A claim for a combined resolution of related civil disputes;  
(9) Other litigation claims.  
If a party initiates an administrative compensation or indemnity lawsuit separately or jointly, there shall be specific items and amounts of compensation or indemnity; if a party requests a combined review of regulatory documents below the level of rules, it shall provide a clear name of the document or the subject of review; if a party requests a combined resolution of related civil disputes, there shall be specific civil litigation claims.  
If a party fails to correctly express its litigation claims, the People's Court shall require it to specify the claims.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有下列情形之一，已经立案的，应当裁定驳回起诉：  
（一）不符合[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)规定的；  
（二）超过法定起诉期限且无[行政诉讼法第四十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-48)规定情形的；  
（三）错列被告且拒绝变更的；  
（四）未按照法律规定由法定代理人、指定代理人、代表人为诉讼行为的；  
（五）未按照法律、法规规定先向行政机关申请复议的；  
（六）重复起诉的；  
（七）撤回起诉后无正当理由再行起诉的；  
（八）行政行为对其合法权益明显不产生实际影响的；  
（九）诉讼标的已为生效裁判或者调解书所羁束的；  
（十）其他不符合法定起诉条件的情形。  
前款所列情形可以补正或者更正的，人民法院应当指定期间责令补正或者更正；在指定期间已经补正或者更正的，应当依法审理。  
人民法院经过阅卷、调查或者询问当事人，认为不需要开庭审理的，可以迳行裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 69** : In any of the following circumstances, if a case has already been filed, a ruling shall be made to dismiss the lawsuit:  
(1) The conditions specified in Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law are not met;  
(2) The statutory period for filing a lawsuit has been exceeded and the circumstances provided in Article 48 of the Administrative Procedure Law do not exist;  
(3) The defendant is incorrectly named and the party refuses to make a correction;  
(4) Litigation acts are not carried out by a statutory agent, designated agent, or representative according to law;  
(5) The party fails to first apply to the administrative organ for reconsideration as required by laws or regulations;  
(6) Duplicate lawsuits are filed;  
(7) After withdrawing the lawsuit, the party files a new lawsuit without justifiable reasons;  
(8) The administrative act obviously has no actual impact on the party's lawful rights and interests;  
(9) The subject matter of the lawsuit is already bound by an effective judgment or mediation document;  
(10) Other circumstances where the statutory conditions for filing a lawsuit are not met.  
If the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph can be remedied or corrected, the People's Court shall specify a time limit and order the party to remedy or correct them; if the party has remedied or corrected them within the specified time limit, the case shall be tried according to law.  
If the People's Court, after reviewing the files, conducting investigations, or questioning the parties, considers that a court hearing is unnecessary, it may directly issue a ruling to dismiss the lawsuit.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　起诉状副本送达被告后，原告提出新的诉讼请求的，人民法院不予准许，但有正当理由的除外。  
七、审理与判决

**Article 70** : After a copy of the complaint has been served on the defendant, if the plaintiff raises new litigation claims, the People's Court shall not permit such claims, except where there are justifiable reasons.  
VII. Trial and Judgment

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　人民法院适用普通程序审理案件，应当在开庭三日前用传票传唤当事人。对证人、鉴定人、勘验人、翻译人员，应当用通知书通知其到庭。当事人或者其他诉讼参与人在外地的，应当留有必要的在途时间。

**Article 71** : When the People's Court tries a case under ordinary procedure, it shall summon the parties by subpoena at least three days before the court session. It shall notify witnesses, expert evaluators, inspectors, and interpreters to appear in court by a notice. If the parties or other participants in the proceedings are located in another place, necessary travel time shall be reserved.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　有下列情形之一的，可以延期开庭审理：  
（一）应当到庭的当事人和其他诉讼参与人有正当理由没有到庭的；  
（二）当事人临时提出回避申请且无法及时作出决定的；  
（三）需要通知新的证人到庭，调取新的证据，重新鉴定、勘验，或者需要补充调查的；  
（四）其他应当延期的情形。

**Article 72** : A court session may be adjourned under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) A party or other participant in the proceedings who should appear in court fails to appear for justifiable reasons;  
(2) A party makes a temporary application for withdrawal and a decision cannot be made promptly;  
(3) It is necessary to notify a new witness to appear in court, obtain new evidence, conduct a new expert evaluation or inspection, or supplement an investigation;  
(4) Other circumstances where an adjournment is necessary.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　根据[行政诉讼法第二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-27)的规定，有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以决定合并审理：  
（一）两个以上行政机关分别对同一事实作出行政行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（二）行政机关就同一事实对若干公民、法人或者其他组织分别作出行政行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服分别向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（三）在诉讼过程中，被告对原告作出新的行政行为，原告不服向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（四）人民法院认为可以合并审理的其他情形。

**Article 73** : Pursuant to Article 27 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court may decide to consolidate the trial of cases under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Two or more administrative organs separately make administrative acts regarding the same fact, and citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, being dissatisfied, initiate lawsuits with the same People's Court;  
(2) An administrative organ makes separate administrative acts regarding the same fact against several citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, and such citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, being dissatisfied, separately initiate lawsuits with the same People's Court;  
(3) During the proceedings, the defendant makes a new administrative act against the plaintiff, and the plaintiff, being dissatisfied, initiates a lawsuit with the same People's Court;  
(4) Other circumstances where the People's Court considers consolidation to be appropriate.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　当事人申请回避，应当说明理由，在案件开始审理时提出；回避事由在案件开始审理后知道的，应当在法庭辩论终结前提出。  
被申请回避的人员，在人民法院作出是否回避的决定前，应当暂停参与本案的工作，但案件需要采取紧急措施的除外。  
对当事人提出的回避申请，人民法院应当在三日内以口头或者书面形式作出决定。对当事人提出的明显不属于法定回避事由的申请，法庭可以依法当庭驳回。  
申请人对驳回回避申请决定不服的，可以向作出决定的人民法院申请复议一次。复议期间，被申请回避的人员不停止参与本案的工作。对申请人的复议申请，人民法院应当在三日内作出复议决定，并通知复议申请人。

**Article 74** : A party applying for withdrawal shall state the reasons and raise the application at the commencement of the trial; if the grounds for withdrawal become known after the trial has commenced, the application shall be raised before the conclusion of court debate.  
The person applied for withdrawal shall suspend participation in the work of the case until the People's Court makes a decision on whether to grant the withdrawal, except where urgent measures need to be taken in the case.  
The People's Court shall make a decision orally or in writing within three days on the withdrawal application raised by the party. For an application that is clearly not based on statutory grounds for withdrawal, the court may orally reject it on the spot according to law.  
If the applicant is dissatisfied with the decision to reject the withdrawal application, it may apply once for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the decision. During the reconsideration period, the person applied for withdrawal shall not cease to participate in the work of the case. The People's Court shall make a reconsideration decision within three days regarding the applicant's reconsideration application and notify the reconsideration applicant.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　在一个审判程序中参与过本案审判工作的审判人员，不得再参与该案其他程序的审判。  
发回重审的案件，在一审法院作出裁判后又进入第二审程序的，原第二审程序中合议庭组成人员不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 75** : Judicial personnel who have participated in the trial of a case in one trial procedure shall not participate in the trial of the same case in another procedure.  
For a case remanded for retrial, if the case enters the second instance procedure again after the first-instance court renders a judgment, the members of the original collegial panel in the second instance procedure shall not be subject to the restriction of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　人民法院对于因一方当事人的行为或者其他原因，可能使行政行为或者人民法院生效裁判不能或者难以执行的案件，根据对方当事人的申请，可以裁定对其财产进行保全、责令其作出一定行为或者禁止其作出一定行为；当事人没有提出申请的，人民法院在必要时也可以裁定采取上述保全措施。  
人民法院采取保全措施，可以责令申请人提供担保；申请人不提供担保的，裁定驳回申请。  
人民法院接受申请后，对情况紧急的，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定采取保全措施的，应当立即开始执行。  
当事人对保全的裁定不服的，可以申请复议；复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 76** : For a case where, due to the conduct of one party or other reasons, an administrative act or an effective judgment of the People's Court may be or become difficult to enforce, the People's Court may, upon application of the opposing party, issue a ruling to preserve the property of the party, order it to perform a certain act, or prohibit it from performing a certain act; if no party makes an application, the People's Court may, when necessary, also issue a ruling to take the above preservation measures.  
When taking preservation measures, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide a guarantee; if the applicant fails to provide a guarantee, the court shall rule to reject the application.  
After accepting an application, if the circumstances are urgent, the People's Court must make a ruling within forty-eight hours; if the ruling is to take preservation measures, execution shall commence immediately.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling on preservation, it may apply for reconsideration; during the reconsideration period, execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　利害关系人因情况紧急，不立即申请保全将会使其合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的，可以在提起诉讼前向被保全财产所在地、被申请人住所地或者对案件有管辖权的人民法院申请采取保全措施。申请人应当提供担保，不提供担保的，裁定驳回申请。  
人民法院接受申请后，必须在四十八小时内作出裁定；裁定采取保全措施的，应当立即开始执行。  
申请人在人民法院采取保全措施后三十日内不依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应当解除保全。  
当事人对保全的裁定不服的，可以申请复议；复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 77** : If, due to urgent circumstances, an interested person would suffer irreparable harm to its lawful rights and interests if preservation is not applied for immediately, it may, before initiating a lawsuit, apply to the People's Court at the location of the property to be preserved, the domicile of the respondent, or the court having jurisdiction over the case for preservation measures. The applicant shall provide a guarantee; if no guarantee is provided, the court shall rule to reject the application.  
After accepting the application, the People's Court must make a ruling within forty-eight hours; if the ruling is to take preservation measures, execution shall commence immediately.  
If the applicant fails to initiate a lawsuit according to law within thirty days after the People's Court takes preservation measures, the People's Court shall lift the preservation.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling on preservation, it may apply for reconsideration; during the reconsideration period, execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　保全限于请求的范围，或者与本案有关的财物。  
财产保全采取查封、扣押、冻结或者法律规定的其他方法。人民法院保全财产后，应当立即通知被保全人。  
财产已被查封、冻结的，不得重复查封、冻结。  
涉及财产的案件，被申请人提供担保的，人民法院应当裁定解除保全。  
申请有错误的，申请人应当赔偿被申请人因保全所遭受的损失。

**Article 78** : Preservation shall be limited to the scope of the claim or to the property relevant to the case.  
Property preservation shall be effected by means such as sealing up, distraining, freezing, or other methods provided by law. After preserving the property, the People's Court shall promptly notify the person whose property is preserved.  
If property has already been sealed up or frozen, it shall not be sealed up or frozen repeatedly.  
In cases involving property, if the respondent provides a guarantee, the People's Court shall rule to lift the preservation.  
If the application is erroneous, the applicant shall compensate the respondent for the losses suffered due to the preservation.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　原告或者上诉人申请撤诉，人民法院裁定不予准许的，原告或者上诉人经传票传唤无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，人民法院可以缺席判决。  
第三人经传票传唤无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，不发生阻止案件审理的效果。  
根据[行政诉讼法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-58)的规定，被告经传票传唤无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，人民法院可以按期开庭或者继续开庭审理，对到庭的当事人诉讼请求、双方的诉辩理由以及已经提交的证据及其他诉讼材料进行审理后，依法缺席判决。

**Article 79** Where a plaintiff or appellant applies to withdraw the action and the People's Court rules not to permit the withdrawal, if the plaintiff or appellant, having been summoned by service of a subpoena, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the courtroom midway without the permission of the court, the People's Court may enter a default judgment.  
Where a third party, having been summoned by service of a subpoena, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the courtroom midway without the permission of the court, this shall not have the effect of preventing the case from being tried.  
Pursuant to the provisions of Article 58 of the Administrative Procedure Law, if the defendant, having been summoned by service of a subpoena, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the courtroom midway without the permission of the court, the People's Court may hear the case on the scheduled date or continue the hearing, and after hearing the claims of the parties present, the arguments of both sides, as well as the evidence and other litigation materials already submitted, shall enter a default judgment according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　原告或者上诉人在庭审中明确拒绝陈述或者以其他方式拒绝陈述，导致庭审无法进行，经法庭释明法律后果后仍不陈述意见的，视为放弃陈述权利，由其承担不利的法律后果。  
当事人申请撤诉或者依法可以按撤诉处理的案件，当事人有违反法律的行为需要依法处理的，人民法院可以不准许撤诉或者不按撤诉处理。  
法庭辩论终结后原告申请撤诉，人民法院可以准许，但涉及到国家利益和社会公共利益的除外。

**Article 80** Where a plaintiff or appellant expressly refuses to make statements during the court hearing or otherwise refuses to make statements, thereby rendering the hearing impossible, and after the court has explained the legal consequences, the party still fails to state opinions, the party shall be deemed to have waived the right to make statements, and shall bear the adverse legal consequences.  
Where a party applies to withdraw the action, or the case may be treated as withdrawal according to law, but the party has committed an act violating the law that requires handling according to law, the People's Court may refuse to permit the withdrawal or refuse to treat the case as withdrawal.  
After the conclusion of court debate, if the plaintiff applies to withdraw the action, the People's Court may permit the withdrawal, except where the national interest or social public interest is involved.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　被告在一审期间改变被诉行政行为的，应当书面告知人民法院。  
原告或者第三人对改变后的行政行为不服提起诉讼的，人民法院应当就改变后的行政行为进行审理。  
被告改变原违法行政行为，原告仍要求确认原行政行为违法的，人民法院应当依法作出确认判决。  
原告起诉被告不作为，在诉讼中被告作出行政行为，原告不撤诉的，人民法院应当就不作为依法作出确认判决。

**Article 81** If the defendant changes the challenged administrative act during the first instance, it shall notify the People's Court in writing.  
If the plaintiff or a third party is dissatisfied with the changed administrative act and files a lawsuit, the People's Court shall try the changed administrative act.  
If the defendant changes the original illegal administrative act, but the plaintiff still requests a declaration that the original administrative act is illegal, the People's Court shall render a declaratory judgment according to law.  
If the plaintiff sues the defendant for nonfeasance, and during the litigation the defendant performs an administrative act, but the plaintiff does not withdraw the action, the People's Court shall render a declaratory judgment on the nonfeasance according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　当事人之间恶意串通，企图通过诉讼等方式侵害国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的，人民法院应当裁定驳回起诉或者判决驳回其请求，并根据情节轻重予以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Where parties collude maliciously, attempting to infringe upon the national interest, social public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others through litigation or other means, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the action or render a judgment dismissing their claims, and shall impose a fine or detention depending on the severity of the circumstances; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　[行政诉讼法第五十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-59)规定的罚款、拘留可以单独适用，也可以合并适用。  
对同一妨害行政诉讼行为的罚款、拘留不得连续适用。发生新的妨害行政诉讼行为的，人民法院可以重新予以罚款、拘留。

**Article 83** The fine or detention prescribed in Article 59 of the Administrative Procedure Law may be applied independently or in combination.  
A fine or detention for the same act obstructing an administrative action shall not be applied consecutively. If a new act obstructing an administrative action occurs, the People's Court may impose a fine or detention anew.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　人民法院审理[行政诉讼法第六十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-60)第一款规定的行政案件，认为法律关系明确、事实清楚，在征得当事人双方同意后，可以迳行调解。

**Article 84** Where the People's Court, when trying an administrative case prescribed in Article 60, paragraph 1 of the Administrative Procedure Law, considers that the legal relationship is clear and the facts are evident, it may proceed directly with mediation after obtaining the consent of both parties.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　调解达成协议，人民法院应当制作调解书。调解书应当写明诉讼请求、案件的事实和调解结果。  
调解书由审判人员、书记员署名，加盖人民法院印章，送达双方当事人。  
调解书经双方当事人签收后，即具有法律效力。调解书生效日期根据最后收到调解书的当事人签收的日期确定。

**Article 85** Where an agreement is reached through mediation, the People's Court shall prepare a mediation paper. The mediation paper shall state the claims, the facts of the case, and the mediation result.  
The mediation paper shall be signed by the adjudicators and the court clerk, sealed with the seal of the People's Court, and served on both parties.  
The mediation paper shall become legally effective upon receipt and signature by both parties. The effective date of the mediation paper shall be determined according to the date of receipt and signature by the party who last receives the mediation paper.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　人民法院审理行政案件，调解过程不公开，但当事人同意公开的除外。  
经人民法院准许，第三人可以参加调解。人民法院认为有必要的，可以通知第三人参加调解。  
调解协议内容不公开，但为保护国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益，人民法院认为确有必要公开的除外。  
当事人一方或者双方不愿调解、调解未达成协议的，人民法院应当及时判决。  
当事人自行和解或者调解达成协议后，请求人民法院按照和解协议或者调解协议的内容制作判决书的，人民法院不予准许。

**Article 86** When the People's Court tries an administrative case, the mediation process shall not be made public, except where the parties agree to make it public.  
With the permission of the People's Court, a third party may participate in the mediation. If the People's Court considers it necessary, it may notify a third party to participate in the mediation.  
The content of the mediation agreement shall not be made public, except where, for the purpose of protecting the national interest, social public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court considers it truly necessary to make it public.  
If one or both parties are unwilling to mediate, or mediation fails to reach an agreement, the People's Court shall render a judgment in a timely manner.  
After the parties reach a settlement or a mediation agreement, if they request the People's Court to prepare a judgment in accordance with the content of the settlement agreement or mediation agreement, the People's Court shall not permit it.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　在诉讼过程中，有下列情形之一的，中止诉讼：  
（一）原告死亡，须等待其近亲属表明是否参加诉讼的；  
（二）原告丧失诉讼行为能力，尚未确定法定代理人的；  
（三）作为一方当事人的行政机关、法人或者其他组织终止，尚未确定权利义务承受人的；  
（四）一方当事人因不可抗力的事由不能参加诉讼的；  
（五）案件涉及法律适用问题，需要送请有权机关作出解释或者确认的；  
（六）案件的审判须以相关民事、刑事或者其他行政案件的审理结果为依据，而相关案件尚未审结的；  
（七）其他应当中止诉讼的情形。  
中止诉讼的原因消除后，恢复诉讼。

**Article 87** During the litigation process, the action shall be suspended under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The plaintiff dies, and it is necessary to await the plaintiff's close relatives to indicate whether they will participate in the action;  
(2) The plaintiff loses capacity for litigation, and no legal representative has yet been designated;  
(3) The administrative organ, legal person, or other organization that is a party is dissolved, and no successor of rights and obligations has yet been determined;  
(4) One party is unable to participate in the action due to force majeure;  
(5) The case involves issues of legal application that need to be submitted to an competent authority for interpretation or confirmation;  
(6) The adjudication of the case must be based on the outcome of a related civil, criminal, or other administrative case, and such related case has not yet been concluded;  
(7) Other circumstances where the action should be suspended.  
After the cause for suspension is eliminated, the action shall be resumed.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　在诉讼过程中，有下列情形之一的，终结诉讼：  
（一）原告死亡，没有近亲属或者近亲属放弃诉讼权利的；  
（二）作为原告的法人或者其他组织终止后，其权利义务的承受人放弃诉讼权利的。  
因[本解释第八十七条](#art-87)第一款第一、二、三项原因中止诉讼满九十日仍无人继续诉讼的，裁定终结诉讼，但有特殊情况的除外。

**Article 88** During the litigation process, the action shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The plaintiff dies, and there is no close relative, or the close relatives waive the right to litigate;  
(2) After the legal person or other organization that is the plaintiff is dissolved, the successor of its rights and obligations waives the right to litigate.  
Where the action has been suspended for a full ninety days due to the reasons specified in items (1), (2), or (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 87 of this Interpretation and no one continues the action, the action shall be terminated by ruling, except under special circumstances.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　复议决定改变原行政行为错误，人民法院判决撤销复议决定时，可以一并责令复议机关重新作出复议决定或者判决恢复原行政行为的法律效力。

**Article 89** Where a reconsideration decision changes the original administrative act erroneously, and the People's Court renders a judgment to revoke the reconsideration decision, it may, at the same time, order the reconsideration organ to make a new reconsideration decision or render a judgment to restore the legal effect of the original administrative act.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　人民法院判决被告重新作出行政行为，被告重新作出的行政行为与原行政行为的结果相同，但主要事实或者主要理由有改变的，不属于[行政诉讼法第七十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-71)规定的情形。  
人民法院以违反法定程序为由，判决撤销被诉行政行为的，行政机关重新作出行政行为不受[行政诉讼法第七十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-71)规定的限制。  
行政机关以同一事实和理由重新作出与原行政行为基本相同的行政行为，人民法院应当根据[行政诉讼法第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-70)、第七十一条的规定判决撤销或者部分撤销，并根据[行政诉讼法第九十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-96)的规定处理。

**Article 90** Where the People's Court renders a judgment ordering the defendant to make a new administrative act, and the new administrative act made by the defendant has the same result as the original administrative act, but with changes in the main facts or main reasons, this shall not fall under the circumstances prescribed in Article 71 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
Where the People's Court renders a judgment to revoke the challenged administrative act on the ground of violation of statutory procedures, the administrative organ's making of a new administrative act shall not be restricted by the provisions of Article 71 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If the administrative organ, based on the same facts and reasons, makes a new administrative act that is substantially identical to the original administrative act, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 70 and 71 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment to revoke or partially revoke it, and shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 96 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　原告请求被告履行法定职责的理由成立，被告违法拒绝履行或者无正当理由逾期不予答复的，人民法院可以根据[行政诉讼法第七十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-72)的规定，判决被告在一定期限内依法履行原告请求的法定职责；尚需被告调查或者裁量的，应当判决被告针对原告的请求重新作出处理。

**Article 91** Where the plaintiff's reasons for requesting the defendant to perform its statutory duty are well-founded, and the defendant illegally refuses to perform or fails to give a reply within the time limit without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 72 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment ordering the defendant to perform the statutory duty requested by the plaintiff within a certain period according to law; if further investigation or discretion by the defendant is still required, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to make a new disposition in response to the plaintiff's request.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　原告申请被告依法履行支付抚恤金、最低生活保障待遇或者社会保险待遇等给付义务的理由成立，被告依法负有给付义务而拒绝或者拖延履行义务的，人民法院可以根据[行政诉讼法第七十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-73)的规定，判决被告在一定期限内履行相应的给付义务。

**Article 92** Where the plaintiff's reasons for applying for the defendant to perform its payment obligations such as paying pensions, minimum living allowances, or social insurance benefits according to law are well-founded, and the defendant, having a legal obligation to make such payments, refuses or delays the performance of the obligation, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 73 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment ordering the defendant to perform the corresponding payment obligation within a certain period.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　原告请求被告履行法定职责或者依法履行支付抚恤金、最低生活保障待遇或者社会保险待遇等给付义务，原告未先向行政机关提出申请的，人民法院裁定驳回起诉。  
人民法院经审理认为原告所请求履行的法定职责或者给付义务明显不属于行政机关权限范围的，可以裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 93** Where the plaintiff requests the defendant to perform its statutory duty or to perform payment obligations such as paying pensions, minimum living allowances, or social insurance benefits according to law, but the plaintiff has not first filed an application with the administrative organ, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the action.  
Where the People's Court, upon trial, considers that the statutory duty or payment obligation requested by the plaintiff is manifestly not within the scope of the administrative organ's authority, it may rule to dismiss the action.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　公民、法人或者其他组织起诉请求撤销行政行为，人民法院经审查认为行政行为无效的，应当作出确认无效的判决。  
公民、法人或者其他组织起诉请求确认行政行为无效，人民法院审查认为行政行为不属于无效情形，经释明，原告请求撤销行政行为的，应当继续审理并依法作出相应判决；原告请求撤销行政行为但超过法定起诉期限的，裁定驳回起诉；原告拒绝变更诉讼请求的，判决驳回其诉讼请求。

**Article 94** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization brings an action requesting the revocation of an administrative act, and the People's Court, upon review, considers that the administrative act is void, it shall render a declaratory judgment of voidness.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization brings an action requesting a declaration that an administrative act is void, and the People's Court, upon review, considers that the administrative act does not fall under the circumstances of voidness, after explanation, if the plaintiff requests revocation of the administrative act, the People's Court shall continue the trial and render a corresponding judgment according to law; if the plaintiff requests revocation of the administrative act but exceeds the statutory limitation period for bringing an action, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the action; if the plaintiff refuses to change the claims, the People's Court shall render a judgment dismissing the claims.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　人民法院经审理认为被诉行政行为违法或者无效，可能给原告造成损失，经释明，原告请求一并解决行政赔偿争议的，人民法院可以就赔偿事项进行调解；调解不成的，应当一并判决。人民法院也可以告知其就赔偿事项另行提起诉讼。

**Article 95** Where the People's Court, upon trial, considers that the challenged administrative act is illegal or void and may cause losses to the plaintiff, after explanation, if the plaintiff requests to resolve the administrative compensation dispute together, the People's Court may conduct mediation on the compensation matter; if mediation fails, it shall render a judgment together. The People's Court may also inform the plaintiff to file a separate lawsuit on the compensation matter.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　有下列情形之一，且对原告依法享有的听证、陈述、申辩等重要程序性权利不产生实质损害的，属于[行政诉讼法第七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-74)第一款第二项规定的“程序轻微违法”：  
（一）处理期限轻微违法；  
（二）通知、送达等程序轻微违法；  
（三）其他程序轻微违法的情形。

**Article 96** Any of the following circumstances, which does not cause substantial harm to the important procedural rights of the plaintiff, such as the right to a hearing, the right to make statements, and the right to defend, shall fall under the "minor procedural violation" as prescribed in Article 74, paragraph 1, item (2) of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) Minor violation of the processing time limit;  
(2) Minor violation of procedures such as notification and service;  
(3) Other circumstances of minor procedural violation.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　原告或者第三人的损失系由其自身过错和行政机关的违法行政行为共同造成的，人民法院应当依据各方行为与损害结果之间有无因果关系以及在损害发生和结果中作用力的大小，确定行政机关相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 97** Where the losses of the plaintiff or a third party are jointly caused by the party's own fault and the illegal administrative act of the administrative organ, the People's Court shall, based on whether there is a causal relationship between each party's act and the damage result, and the degree of contribution of each act in the occurrence and outcome of the damage, determine the corresponding compensation liability of the administrative organ.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　因行政机关不履行、拖延履行法定职责，致使公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益遭受损害的，人民法院应当判决行政机关承担行政赔偿责任。在确定赔偿数额时，应当考虑该不履行、拖延履行法定职责的行为在损害发生过程和结果中所起的作用等因素。

**Article 98** Where, due to the administrative organ's failure to perform or delay in performing its statutory duty, the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization suffer damage, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the administrative organ to bear administrative compensation liability. When determining the amount of compensation, factors such as the role of the act of failing to perform or delaying the performance of the statutory duty in the process and outcome of the damage shall be considered.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第七十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-75)规定的“重大且明显违法”：  
（一）行政行为实施主体不具有行政主体资格；  
（二）减损权利或者增加义务的行政行为没有法律规范依据；  
（三）行政行为的内容客观上不可能实施；  
（四）其他重大且明显违法的情形。

**Article 99** Any of the following circumstances shall fall under the "major and obvious illegality" as prescribed in Article 75 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) The subject implementing the administrative act does not have the qualification of an administrative subject;  
(2) The administrative act that derogates from rights or increases obligations has no legal basis;  
(3) The content of the administrative act is objectively impossible to implement;  
(4) Other circumstances of major and obvious illegality.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　人民法院审理行政案件，适用最高人民法院司法解释的，应当在裁判文书中援引。  
人民法院审理行政案件，可以在裁判文书中引用合法有效的规章及其他规范性文件。

**Article 100** Where the People's Court, when trying an administrative case, applies a judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court, it shall cite it in the adjudicatory document.  
When trying an administrative case, the People's Court may cite legally effective rules and other regulatory documents in the adjudicatory document.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　裁定适用于下列范围：  
（一）不予立案；  
（二）驳回起诉；  
（三）管辖异议；  
（四）终结诉讼；  
（五）中止诉讼；  
（六）移送或者指定管辖；  
（七）诉讼期间停止行政行为的执行或者驳回停止执行的申请；  
（八）财产保全；  
（九）先予执行；  
（十）准许或者不准许撤诉；  
（十一）补正裁判文书中的笔误；  
（十二）中止或者终结执行；  
（十三）提审、指令再审或者发回重审；  
（十四）准许或者不准许执行行政机关的行政行为；  
（十五）其他需要裁定的事项。  
对第一、二、三项裁定，当事人可以上诉。  
裁定书应当写明裁定结果和作出该裁定的理由。裁定书由审判人员、书记员署名，加盖人民法院印章。口头裁定的，记入笔录。

**Article 101** Rulings shall apply to the following scope:  
(1) Refusal to docket a case;  
(2) Dismissal of an action;  
(3) Objection to jurisdiction;  
(4) Termination of an action;  
(5) Suspension of an action;  
(6) Transfer or designation of jurisdiction;  
(7) Suspension of the execution of an administrative act during litigation or rejection of an application for suspension of execution;  
(8) Property preservation;  
(9) Advance execution;  
(10) Permission or refusal to permit withdrawal of an action;  
(11) Correction of clerical errors in adjudicatory documents;  
(12) Suspension or termination of enforcement;  
(13) Removal for trial, designation of retrial, or remand for retrial;  
(14) Permission or refusal to permit enforcement of an administrative act of an administrative organ;  
(15) Other matters requiring a ruling.  
A party may appeal against the rulings under items (1), (2), and (3).  
The written ruling shall state the ruling result and the reasons for making the ruling. The written ruling shall be signed by the adjudicators and the court clerk and sealed with the seal of the People's Court. An oral ruling shall be recorded in the笔录.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　[行政诉讼法第八十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-82)规定的行政案件中的“事实清楚”，是指当事人对争议的事实陈述基本一致，并能提供相应的证据，无须人民法院调查收集证据即可查明事实；“权利义务关系明确”，是指行政法律关系中权利和义务能够明确区分；“争议不大”，是指当事人对行政行为的合法性、责任承担等没有实质分歧。

**Article 102** The term "facts are evident" in the administrative cases prescribed in Article 82 of the Administrative Procedure Law means that the parties' statements on the disputed facts are basically consistent and they can provide corresponding evidence, so that the facts can be ascertained without the need for the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence; "the rights and obligations relationship is clear" means that the rights and obligations in the administrative legal relationship can be clearly distinguished; "the dispute is not significant" means that the parties have no substantial disagreement on the legality of the administrative act, the assumption of liability, etc.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　适用简易程序审理的行政案件，人民法院可以用口头通知、电话、短信、传真、电子邮件等简便方式传唤当事人、通知证人、送达裁判文书以外的诉讼文书。  
以简便方式送达的开庭通知，未经当事人确认或者没有其他证据证明当事人已经收到的，人民法院不得缺席判决。

**Article 103** For administrative cases tried under summary procedures, the People's Court may summon the parties, notify witnesses, and serve litigation documents other than adjudicatory documents by simple means such as oral notification, telephone, text message, fax, or email.  
If a notice of hearing served by simple means has not been confirmed by the party or there is no other evidence proving that the party has received it, the People's Court shall not enter a default judgment.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　适用简易程序案件的举证期限由人民法院确定，也可以由当事人协商一致并经人民法院准许，但不得超过十五日。被告要求书面答辩的，人民法院可以确定合理的答辩期间。  
人民法院应当将举证期限和开庭日期告知双方当事人，并向当事人说明逾期举证以及拒不到庭的法律后果，由双方当事人在笔录和开庭传票的送达回证上签名或者捺印。  
当事人双方均表示同意立即开庭或者缩短举证期限、答辩期间的，人民法院可以立即开庭审理或者确定近期开庭。

**Article 104** For cases subject to summary procedures, the time limit for adducing evidence shall be determined by the People's Court, or may be agreed upon by the parties through consultation and approved by the People's Court, but shall not exceed fifteen days. If the defendant requests to submit a written defense, the People's Court may determine a reasonable period for defense.  
The People's Court shall inform both parties of the time limit for adducing evidence and the date of the hearing, and shall explain to the parties the legal consequences of adducing evidence beyond the time limit and failing to appear in court. Both parties shall sign or affix their fingerprints on the笔录 and the acknowledgment of service of the subpoena.  
If both parties express agreement to start the hearing immediately or to shorten the time limit for adducing evidence or the period for defense, the People's Court may commence the hearing immediately or set a hearing date in the near future.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　人民法院发现案情复杂，需要转为普通程序审理的，应当在审理期限届满前作出裁定并将合议庭组成人员及相关事项书面通知双方当事人。  
案件转为普通程序审理的，审理期限自人民法院立案之日起计算。

**Article 105** Where the People's Court finds that the case is complex and needs to be tried under ordinary procedures, it shall make a ruling before the expiration of the trial period and shall notify both parties in writing of the composition of the collegiate bench and relevant matters.  
Where a case is switched to ordinary procedures for trial, the trial period shall be calculated from the date of docketing by the People's Court.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　当事人就已经提起诉讼的事项在诉讼过程中或者裁判生效后再次起诉，同时具有下列情形的，构成重复起诉：  
（一）后诉与前诉的当事人相同；  
（二）后诉与前诉的诉讼标的相同；  
（三）后诉与前诉的诉讼请求相同，或者后诉的诉讼请求被前诉裁判所包含。

**Article 106** Where a party files another action on the same matter already brought to court, either during the litigation process or after the adjudicatory document takes effect, and the following circumstances exist simultaneously, it shall constitute a duplicate action:  
(1) The parties in the subsequent action and the prior action are identical;  
(2) The subject matter of the subsequent action and the prior action is identical;  
(3) The claims in the subsequent action and the prior action are identical, or the claims in the subsequent action are encompassed by the adjudicatory document of the prior action.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　第一审人民法院作出判决和裁定后，当事人均提起上诉的，上诉各方均为上诉人。  
诉讼当事人中的一部分人提出上诉，没有提出上诉的对方当事人为被上诉人，其他当事人依原审诉讼地位列明。

**Article 107** Where, after the People's Court of first instance renders a judgment or ruling, all parties file an appeal, each appealing party shall be an appellant.  
Where only some of the parties to the action appeal, the opposing party that did not appeal shall be the respondent, and the other parties shall be listed according to their original litigation status.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　当事人提出上诉，应当按照其他当事人或者诉讼代表人的人数提出上诉状副本。  
原审人民法院收到上诉状，应当在五日内将上诉状副本发送其他当事人，对方当事人应当在收到上诉状副本之日起十五日内提出答辩状。  
原审人民法院应当在收到答辩状之日起五日内将副本发送上诉人。对方当事人不提出答辩状的，不影响人民法院审理。  
原审人民法院收到上诉状、答辩状，应当在五日内连同全部案卷和证据，报送第二审人民法院；已经预收的诉讼费用，一并报送。

**Article 108** When a party files an appeal, it shall submit copies of the statement of appeal according to the number of other parties or the representatives of the litigation.  
Upon receipt of the statement of appeal, the People's Court of first instance shall, within five days, send copies of the statement of appeal to the other parties, and the opposing party shall submit a statement of defense within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the copy of the statement of appeal.  
The People's Court of first instance shall, within five days from receipt of the statement of defense, send a copy to the appellant. Failure of the opposing party to submit a statement of defense shall not affect the trial by the People's Court.  
Upon receipt of the statement of appeal and the statement of defense, the People's Court of first instance shall, within five days, transmit them together with the entire case file and evidence to the People's Court of second instance; any litigation costs already collected shall also be transmitted.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　第二审人民法院经审理认为原审人民法院不予立案或者驳回起诉的裁定确有错误且当事人的起诉符合起诉条件的，应当裁定撤销原审人民法院的裁定，指令原审人民法院依法立案或者继续审理。  
第二审人民法院裁定发回原审人民法院重新审理的行政案件，原审人民法院应当另行组成合议庭进行审理。  
原审判决遗漏了必须参加诉讼的当事人或者诉讼请求的，第二审人民法院应当裁定撤销原审判决，发回重审。  
原审判决遗漏行政赔偿请求，第二审人民法院经审查认为依法不应当予以赔偿的，应当判决驳回行政赔偿请求。  
原审判决遗漏行政赔偿请求，第二审人民法院经审理认为依法应当予以赔偿的，在确认被诉行政行为违法的同时，可以就行政赔偿问题进行调解；调解不成的，应当就行政赔偿部分发回重审。  
当事人在第二审期间提出行政赔偿请求的，第二审人民法院可以进行调解；调解不成的，应当告知当事人另行起诉。

**Article 109** Where the People's Court of second instance, upon trial, considers that the ruling of the People's Court of first instance refusing to docket the case or dismissing the action is indeed erroneous and the party's action meets the conditions for filing, it shall rule to revoke the ruling of the People's Court of first instance and instruct the People's Court of first instance to docket the case according to law or continue the trial.  
Where the People's Court of second instance rules to remand an administrative case to the People's Court of first instance for retrial, the People's Court of first instance shall form a new collegiate bench for trial.  
Where the judgment of first instance omits a party that must participate in the litigation or omits a claim, the People's Court of second instance shall rule to revoke the judgment of first instance and remand the case for retrial.  
Where the judgment of first instance omits a claim for administrative compensation, and the People's Court of second instance, upon review, considers that compensation should not be awarded according to law, it shall render a judgment dismissing the claim for administrative compensation.  
Where the judgment of first instance omits a claim for administrative compensation, and the People's Court of second instance, upon trial, considers that compensation should be awarded according to law, while confirming that the challenged administrative act is illegal, it may conduct mediation on the issue of administrative compensation; if mediation fails, it shall remand the part concerning administrative compensation for retrial.  
Where a party raises a claim for administrative compensation during the second instance, the People's Court of second instance may conduct mediation; if mediation fails, it shall inform the party to file a separate action.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　当事人向上一级人民法院申请再审，应当在判决、裁定或者调解书发生法律效力后六个月内提出。有下列情形之一的，自知道或者应当知道之日起六个月内提出：  
（一）有新的证据，足以推翻原判决、裁定的；  
（二）原判决、裁定认定事实的主要证据是伪造的；  
（三）据以作出原判决、裁定的法律文书被撤销或者变更的；  
（四）审判人员审理该案件时有贪污受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁判行为的。

**Article 110** A party applying for retrial to the People's Court at the next higher level shall file the application within six months after the judgment, ruling, or mediation paper takes legal effect. Under any of the following circumstances, the application shall be filed within six months from the date the party knows or should have known:  
(1) There is new evidence sufficient to overturn the original judgment or ruling;  
(2) The main evidence on which the facts were found in the original judgment or ruling was forged;  
(3) The legal document based on which the original judgment or ruling was made has been revoked or changed;  
(4) The adjudicators committed acts of embezzlement or bribery, engaged in favoritism or fraud, or perverted the law in the adjudication of the case.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　当事人申请再审的，应当提交再审申请书等材料。人民法院认为有必要的，可以自收到再审申请书之日起五日内将再审申请书副本发送对方当事人。对方当事人应当自收到再审申请书副本之日起十五日内提交书面意见。人民法院可以要求申请人和对方当事人补充有关材料，询问有关事项。

**Article 111** Where a party applies for retrial, it shall submit the application for retrial and other materials. If the People's Court considers it necessary, it may, within five days from receipt of the application for retrial, send a copy of the application to the opposing party. The opposing party shall submit written opinions within fifteen days from receipt of the copy of the application for retrial. The People's Court may request the applicant and the opposing party to supplement relevant materials and inquire about relevant matters.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　人民法院应当自再审申请案件立案之日起六个月内审查，有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。

**Article 112** The People's Court shall review the case of an application for retrial within six months from the date of docketing the case. If extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it shall be approved by the president of the court.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　人民法院根据审查再审申请案件的需要决定是否询问当事人；新的证据可能推翻原判决、裁定的，人民法院应当询问当事人。

**Article 113** The People's Court shall decide whether to question the parties based on the needs of reviewing the application for retrial; if new evidence may overturn the original judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall question the parties.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　审查再审申请期间，被申请人及原审其他当事人依法提出再审申请的，人民法院应当将其列为再审申请人，对其再审事由一并审查，审查期限重新计算。经审查，其中一方再审申请人主张的再审事由成立的，应当裁定再审。各方再审申请人主张的再审事由均不成立的，一并裁定驳回再审申请。

**Article 114** During the period of reviewing an application for retrial, if the respondent and other parties in the original trial also file an application for retrial according to law, the People's Court shall list them as applicants for retrial and shall review their grounds for retrial together; the review period shall be recalculated. Upon review, if the grounds for retrial asserted by any one of the applicants for retrial are established, the People's Court shall rule to grant retrial. If the grounds for retrial asserted by all applicants for retrial are not established, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss all applications for retrial together.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　审查再审申请期间，再审申请人申请人民法院委托鉴定、勘验的，人民法院不予准许。  
审查再审申请期间，再审申请人撤回再审申请的，是否准许，由人民法院裁定。  
再审申请人经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不接受询问的，按撤回再审申请处理。  
人民法院准许撤回再审申请或者按撤回再审申请处理后，再审申请人再次申请再审的，不予立案，但有[行政诉讼法第九十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-91)第二项、第三项、第七项、第八项规定情形，自知道或者应当知道之日起六个月内提出的除外。

**Article 115** During the period of reviewing an application for retrial, if the applicant for retrial applies to the People's Court to commission an expert evaluation or inspection, the People's Court shall not permit it.  
During the period of reviewing an application for retrial, if the applicant for retrial withdraws the application for retrial, whether to permit the withdrawal shall be decided by the People's Court by ruling.  
If the applicant for retrial, having been summoned by service of a subpoena, refuses to submit to questioning without justifiable reasons, the application shall be treated as withdrawn.  
After the People's Court permits the withdrawal of the application for retrial or treats it as withdrawn, if the applicant for retrial applies for retrial again, the court shall not docket the case, except where the circumstances fall under items (2), (3), (7), or (8) of Article 91 of the Administrative Procedure Law and the application is filed within six months from the date the party knows or should have known.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　当事人主张的再审事由成立，且符合行政诉讼法和本解释规定的申请再审条件的，人民法院应当裁定再审。  
当事人主张的再审事由不成立，或者当事人申请再审超过法定申请再审期限、超出法定再审事由范围等不符合行政诉讼法和本解释规定的申请再审条件的，人民法院应当裁定驳回再审申请。

**Article 116** If the grounds for retrial asserted by a party are established and meet the conditions for applying for retrial prescribed in the Administrative Procedure Law and this Interpretation, the People's Court shall rule to grant retrial.  
If the grounds for retrial asserted by a party are not established, or the party's application for retrial exceeds the statutory time limit for applying for retrial, exceeds the statutory scope of grounds for retrial, or otherwise does not meet the conditions for applying for retrial prescribed in the Administrative Procedure Law and this Interpretation, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the application for retrial.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　有下列情形之一的，当事人可以向人民检察院申请抗诉或者检察建议：  
（一）人民法院驳回再审申请的；  
（二）人民法院逾期未对再审申请作出裁定的；  
（三）再审判决、裁定有明显错误的。  
人民法院基于抗诉或者检察建议作出再审判决、裁定后，当事人申请再审的，人民法院不予立案。

**Article 117** Under any of the following circumstances, a party may apply to the People's Procuratorate for a protest or a procuratorial recommendation:  
(1) The People's Court dismisses the application for retrial;  
(2) The People's Court fails to make a ruling on the application for retrial within the time limit;  
(3) The retrial judgment or ruling contains obvious errors.  
After the People's Court renders a retrial judgment or ruling based on a protest or a procuratorial recommendation, if a party applies for retrial, the People's Court shall not docket the case.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，裁定中止原判决、裁定、调解书的执行，但支付抚恤金、最低生活保障费或者社会保险待遇的案件，可以不中止执行。  
上级人民法院决定提审或者指令下级人民法院再审的，应当作出裁定，裁定应当写明中止原判决的执行；情况紧急的，可以将中止执行的裁定口头通知负责执行的人民法院或者作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院，但应当在口头通知后十日内发出裁定书。

**Article 118** For a case decided to be retried under the trial supervision procedure, the execution of the original judgment, ruling, or mediation agreement shall be suspended by ruling, except that in cases involving payment of pensions, minimum living allowances, or social insurance benefits, the execution may not be suspended.  
Where a higher people's court decides to bring a case up for trial or instructs a lower people's court to conduct a retrial, it shall render a ruling, and the ruling shall specify the suspension of execution of the original judgment; in an emergency, the ruling to suspend execution may be notified orally to the people's court responsible for execution or the people's court that rendered the effective judgment or ruling, but the written ruling shall be issued within ten days after the oral notification.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的案件，发生法律效力的判决、裁定是由第一审法院作出的，按照第一审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定，当事人可以上诉；发生法律效力的判决、裁定是由第二审法院作出的，按照第二审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定，是发生法律效力的判决、裁定；上级人民法院按照审判监督程序提审的，按照第二审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定是发生法律效力的判决、裁定。  
人民法院审理再审案件，应当另行组成合议庭。

**Article 119** For a case retried by a people's court under the trial supervision procedure, if the legally effective judgment or ruling was made by the court of first instance, the case shall be tried under the procedure of first instance, and the parties may appeal against the judgment or ruling rendered; if the legally effective judgment or ruling was made by the court of second instance, the case shall be tried under the procedure of second instance, and the judgment or ruling rendered shall be legally effective; if a higher people's court brings a case up for trial under the trial supervision procedure, the case shall be tried under the procedure of second instance, and the judgment or ruling rendered shall be legally effective.  
When a people's court tries a retrial case, it shall form a collegial panel anew.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　人民法院审理再审案件应当围绕再审请求和被诉行政行为合法性进行。当事人的再审请求超出原审诉讼请求，符合另案诉讼条件的，告知当事人可以另行起诉。  
被申请人及原审其他当事人在庭审辩论结束前提出的再审请求，符合本解释规定的申请期限的，人民法院应当一并审理。  
人民法院经再审，发现已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益的，应当一并审理。

**Article 120** When a people's court tries a retrial case, it shall focus on the retrial request and the legality of the administrative act under suit. If a party's retrial request exceeds the original claims in the original trial and meets the conditions for a separate action, the party shall be informed that it may file a separate action.  
Where the respondent and other parties in the original trial propose retrial requests before the end of the court debate and such requests comply with the application period prescribed in this Interpretation, the people's court shall try them together.  
If, through retrial, the people's court finds that a legally effective judgment or ruling has harmed national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, it shall try such matters together.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　再审审理期间，有下列情形之一的，裁定终结再审程序：  
（一）再审申请人在再审期间撤回再审请求，人民法院准许的；  
（二）再审申请人经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭，按撤回再审请求处理的；  
（三）人民检察院撤回抗诉的；  
（四）其他应当终结再审程序的情形。  
因人民检察院提出抗诉裁定再审的案件，申请抗诉的当事人有前款规定的情形，且不损害国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的，人民法院裁定终结再审程序。  
再审程序终结后，人民法院裁定中止执行的原生效判决自动恢复执行。

**Article 121** During the retrial proceedings, in any of the following circumstances, the retrial procedure shall be terminated by ruling:  
(1) the applicant for retrial withdraws the retrial request during the retrial period and the people's court approves the withdrawal;  
(2) the applicant for retrial, having been summoned by subpoena, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the court mid-session without the court's permission, and the case is treated as withdrawal of the retrial request;  
(3) the people's procuratorate withdraws the protest;  
(4) other circumstances under which the retrial procedure shall be terminated.  
For a case ordered to be retried due to a protest lodged by the people's procuratorate, if the party who applied for the protest has a circumstance specified in the preceding paragraph and such circumstance does not harm national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the people's court shall terminate the retrial procedure by ruling.  
After the retrial procedure is terminated, the original effective judgment whose execution was suspended by the people's court's ruling shall automatically resume execution.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　人民法院审理再审案件，认为原生效判决、裁定确有错误，在撤销原生效判决或者裁定的同时，可以对生效判决、裁定的内容作出相应裁判，也可以裁定撤销生效判决或者裁定，发回作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院重新审理。

**Article 122** When a people's court tries a retrial case and finds that the original effective judgment or ruling is indeed erroneous, it may, while revoking the original effective judgment or ruling, render a corresponding adjudication on the content of the effective judgment or ruling, or may rule to revoke the effective judgment or ruling and remand the case to the people's court that rendered the effective judgment or ruling for retrial.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　人民法院审理二审案件和再审案件，对原审法院立案、不予立案或者驳回起诉错误的，应当分别情况作如下处理：  
（一）第一审人民法院作出实体判决后，第二审人民法院认为不应当立案的，在撤销第一审人民法院判决的同时，可以迳行驳回起诉；  
（二）第二审人民法院维持第一审人民法院不予立案裁定错误的，再审法院应当撤销第一审、第二审人民法院裁定，指令第一审人民法院受理；  
（三）第二审人民法院维持第一审人民法院驳回起诉裁定错误的，再审法院应当撤销第一审、第二审人民法院裁定，指令第一审人民法院审理。

**Article 123** When a people's court tries a case on appeal or a retrial case, if the court of original trial erroneously docketed the case, refused to docket the case, or dismissed the prosecution, it shall handle the matter as follows according to the circumstances:  
(1) after the court of first instance renders a judgment on the merits, if the court of second instance considers that the case should not have been docketed, it may, while revoking the judgment of the court of first instance, directly dismiss the prosecution;  
(2) if the court of second instance erroneously upholds the ruling of the court of first instance refusing to docket the case, the retrial court shall revoke the rulings of the court of first instance and the court of second instance and instruct the court of first instance to accept the case;  
(3) if the court of second instance erroneously upholds the ruling of the court of first instance dismissing the prosecution, the retrial court shall revoke the rulings of the court of first instance and the court of second instance and instruct the court of first instance to try the case.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，接受抗诉的人民法院应当自收到抗诉书之日起三十日内作出再审的裁定；有[行政诉讼法第九十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-91)第二、三项规定情形之一的，可以指令下一级人民法院再审，但经该下一级人民法院再审过的除外。  
人民法院在审查抗诉材料期间，当事人之间已经达成和解协议的，人民法院可以建议人民检察院撤回抗诉。

**Article 124** For a case in which the people's procuratorate has lodged a protest, the people's court that accepts the protest shall, within thirty days from the date of receiving the protest letter, render a ruling on retrial; if there is a circumstance specified in Items (2) or (3) of Article 91 of the Administrative Procedure Law, it may instruct the next lower people's court to conduct a retrial, except where such next lower people's court has already conducted a retrial.  
During the period when the people's court is reviewing the protest materials, if the parties have reached a settlement agreement, the people's court may suggest that the people's procuratorate withdraw the protest.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，人民法院再审开庭时，应当在开庭三日前通知人民检察院派员出庭。

**Article 125** For a case in which the people's procuratorate has lodged a protest, when the people's court opens a retrial hearing, it shall notify the people's procuratorate to send personnel to appear in court three days before the hearing.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　人民法院收到再审检察建议后，应当组成合议庭，在三个月内进行审查，发现原判决、裁定、调解书确有错误，需要再审的，依照[行政诉讼法第九十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-92)规定裁定再审，并通知当事人；经审查，决定不予再审的，应当书面回复人民检察院。

**Article 126** After receiving a procuratorial recommendation for retrial, the people's court shall form a collegial panel and, within three months, review it. If it finds that the original judgment, ruling, or mediation agreement is indeed erroneous and a retrial is necessary, it shall rule to retry the case in accordance with Article 92 of the Administrative Procedure Law and notify the parties; if, after review, it decides not to retry the case, it shall reply in writing to the people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　人民法院审理因人民检察院抗诉或者检察建议裁定再审的案件，不受此前已经作出的驳回当事人再审申请裁定的限制。  
八、行政机关负责人出庭应诉

**Article 127** When a people's court tries a case ordered to be retried due to a protest or procuratorial recommendation from the people's procuratorate, it shall not be bound by a previous ruling that dismissed the party's application for retrial.  
VIII. Appearance of Responsible Persons of Administrative Organs in Court to Respond to Lawsuits

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的行政机关负责人，包括行政机关的正职、副职负责人以及其他参与分管的负责人。  
行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，可以另行委托一至二名诉讼代理人。行政机关负责人不能出庭的，应当委托行政机关相应的工作人员出庭，不得仅委托律师出庭。

**Article 128** The "responsible person of an administrative organ" as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the principal and deputy responsible persons of the administrative organ as well as other responsible persons involved in the division of labor.  
Where a responsible person of an administrative organ appears in court to respond to a lawsuit, it may separately authorize one or two litigation agents. If the responsible person of the administrative organ is unable to appear in court, it shall authorize the corresponding staff member of the administrative organ to appear in court, and shall not authorize only a lawyer to appear in court.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　涉及重大公共利益、社会高度关注或者可能引发群体性事件等案件以及人民法院书面建议行政机关负责人出庭的案件，被诉行政机关负责人应当出庭。  
被诉行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，应当在当事人及其诉讼代理人基本情况、案件由来部分予以列明。  
行政机关负责人有正当理由不能出庭应诉的，应当向人民法院提交情况说明，并加盖行政机关印章或者由该机关主要负责人签字认可。  
行政机关拒绝说明理由的，不发生阻止案件审理的效果，人民法院可以向监察机关、上一级行政机关提出司法建议。

**Article 129** In cases involving major public interests, high social concern, or possibly triggering mass incidents, as well as cases where the people's court has made a written suggestion that the responsible person of the administrative organ appear in court, the responsible person of the administrative organ under suit shall appear in court.  
Where the responsible person of the administrative organ under suit appears in court to respond to a lawsuit, it shall be specified in the part concerning the basic information of the parties and their litigation agents and the origin of the case.  
If the responsible person of the administrative organ has justifiable reasons for being unable to appear in court to respond to a lawsuit, it shall submit a statement of explanation to the people's court, affixed with the seal of the administrative organ or signed for approval by the principal responsible person of the organ.  
If the administrative organ refuses to provide an explanation, it shall not have the effect of blocking the trial of the case, and the people's court may issue a judicial suggestion to the supervision organ or the administrative organ at the next higher level.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的“行政机关相应的工作人员”，包括该行政机关具有国家行政编制身份的工作人员以及其他依法履行公职的人员。  
被诉行政行为是地方人民政府作出的，地方人民政府法制工作机构的工作人员，以及被诉行政行为具体承办机关工作人员，可以视为被诉人民政府相应的工作人员。

**Article 130** The "corresponding staff members of the administrative organ" as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes staff members of the administrative organ who hold national administrative staffing status and other personnel who perform public duties according to law.  
Where the administrative act under suit is made by a local people's government, staff members of the legal affairs working body of the local people's government and staff members of the organ that specifically handles the administrative act under suit may be regarded as corresponding staff members of the people's government under suit.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，应当向人民法院提交能够证明该行政机关负责人职务的材料。  
行政机关委托相应的工作人员出庭应诉的，应当向人民法院提交加盖行政机关印章的授权委托书，并载明工作人员的姓名、职务和代理权限。

**Article 131** Where a responsible person of an administrative organ appears in court to respond to a lawsuit, it shall submit to the people's court materials that can prove the position of such responsible person.  
Where an administrative organ authorizes the corresponding staff member to appear in court to respond to a lawsuit, it shall submit to the people's court a power of attorney affixed with the seal of the administrative organ, specifying the name, position, and scope of authority of the staff member.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　行政机关负责人和行政机关相应的工作人员均不出庭，仅委托律师出庭的或者人民法院书面建议行政机关负责人出庭应诉，行政机关负责人不出庭应诉的，人民法院应当记录在案和在裁判文书中载明，并可以建议有关机关依法作出处理。  
九、复议机关作共同被告

**Article 132** Where neither the responsible person of the administrative organ nor the corresponding staff member of the administrative organ appears in court, and only a lawyer is authorized to appear in court, or where the people's court makes a written suggestion that the responsible person of the administrative organ appear in court to respond to a lawsuit but the responsible person fails to do so, the people's court shall record it in the case file and state it in the adjudication document, and may suggest that the relevant organ handle the matter according to law.  
IX. Reconsideration Organ as Co-Defendant

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　[行政诉讼法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-26)第二款规定的“复议机关决定维持原行政行为”，包括复议机关驳回复议申请或者复议请求的情形，但以复议申请不符合受理条件为由驳回的除外。

**Article 133** The "decision of the reconsideration organ to uphold the original administrative act" as specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the circumstances where the reconsideration organ dismisses the reconsideration application or the reconsideration request, except where the dismissal is on the ground that the reconsideration application does not meet the acceptance conditions.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　复议机关决定维持原行政行为的，作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关是共同被告。原告只起诉作出原行政行为的行政机关或者复议机关的，人民法院应当告知原告追加被告。原告不同意追加的，人民法院应当将另一机关列为共同被告。  
行政复议决定既有维持原行政行为内容，又有改变原行政行为内容或者不予受理申请内容的，作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关为共同被告。  
复议机关作共同被告的案件，以作出原行政行为的行政机关确定案件的级别管辖。

**Article 134** Where a reconsideration organ decides to uphold the original administrative act, the administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ shall be co-defendants. If the plaintiff only sues the administrative organ that made the original administrative act or the reconsideration organ, the people's court shall inform the plaintiff to add a defendant. If the plaintiff does not agree to add, the people's court shall list the other organ as a co-defendant.  
Where a reconsideration decision contains both content upholding the original administrative act and content altering the original administrative act or content refusing to accept an application, the administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ shall be co-defendants.  
For a case where the reconsideration organ acts as a co-defendant, the level jurisdiction of the case shall be determined by the administrative organ that made the original administrative act.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　复议机关决定维持原行政行为的，人民法院应当在审查原行政行为合法性的同时，一并审查复议决定的合法性。  
作出原行政行为的行政机关和复议机关对原行政行为合法性共同承担举证责任，可以由其中一个机关实施举证行为。复议机关对复议决定的合法性承担举证责任。  
复议机关作共同被告的案件，复议机关在复议程序中依法收集和补充的证据，可以作为人民法院认定复议决定和原行政行为合法的依据。

**Article 135** Where a reconsideration organ decides to uphold the original administrative act, the people's court shall, while reviewing the legality of the original administrative act, jointly review the legality of the reconsideration decision.  
The administrative organ that made the original administrative act and the reconsideration organ shall jointly bear the burden of adducing evidence for the legality of the original administrative act, and one of them may carry out the act of adducing evidence. The reconsideration organ shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the legality of the reconsideration decision.  
For a case where the reconsideration organ acts as a co-defendant, the evidence collected and supplemented by the reconsideration organ according to law during the reconsideration procedure may serve as the basis for the people's court to determine the legality of the reconsideration decision and the original administrative act.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　人民法院对原行政行为作出判决的同时，应当对复议决定一并作出相应判决。  
人民法院依职权追加作出原行政行为的行政机关或者复议机关为共同被告的，对原行政行为或者复议决定可以作出相应判决。  
人民法院判决撤销原行政行为和复议决定的，可以判决作出原行政行为的行政机关重新作出行政行为。  
人民法院判决作出原行政行为的行政机关履行法定职责或者给付义务的，应当同时判决撤销复议决定。  
原行政行为合法、复议决定违法的，人民法院可以判决撤销复议决定或者确认复议决定违法，同时判决驳回原告针对原行政行为的诉讼请求。  
原行政行为被撤销、确认违法或者无效，给原告造成损失的，应当由作出原行政行为的行政机关承担赔偿责任；因复议决定加重损害的，由复议机关对加重部分承担赔偿责任。  
原行政行为不符合复议或者诉讼受案范围等受理条件，复议机关作出维持决定的，人民法院应当裁定一并驳回对原行政行为和复议决定的起诉。  
十、相关民事争议的一并审理

**Article 136** When the people's court renders a judgment on the original administrative act, it shall simultaneously render a corresponding judgment on the reconsideration decision.  
Where the people's court ex officio adds the administrative organ that made the original administrative act or the reconsideration organ as a co-defendant, it may render a corresponding judgment on the original administrative act or the reconsideration decision.  
Where the people's court revokes both the original administrative act and the reconsideration decision, it may order the administrative organ that made the original administrative act to make a new administrative act.  
Where the people's court orders the administrative organ that made the original administrative act to perform its statutory duty or payment obligation, it shall simultaneously revoke the reconsideration decision.  
If the original administrative act is legal but the reconsideration decision is illegal, the people's court may revoke the reconsideration decision or confirm the illegality of the reconsideration decision, while simultaneously dismissing the plaintiff's claim against the original administrative act.  
If the original administrative act is revoked, confirmed illegal, or invalid, causing losses to the plaintiff, the administrative organ that made the original administrative act shall bear compensation liability; if the damage is aggravated by the reconsideration decision, the reconsideration organ shall bear compensation liability for the aggravated part.  
If the original administrative act does not meet the acceptance conditions such as the scope of administrative reconsideration or litigation, and the reconsideration organ makes a decision to uphold it, the people's court shall render a ruling to dismiss the prosecution against both the original administrative act and the reconsideration decision.  
X. Joint Trial of Related Civil Disputes

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　公民、法人或者其他组织请求一并审理[行政诉讼法第六十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-61)规定的相关民事争议，应当在第一审开庭审理前提出；有正当理由的，也可以在法庭调查中提出。

**Article 137** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization requests the joint trial of related civil disputes as specified in Article 61 of the Administrative Procedure Law, such request shall be made before the opening of the first-instance court hearing; with justifiable reasons, it may also be made during the court investigation.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　人民法院决定在行政诉讼中一并审理相关民事争议，或者案件当事人一致同意相关民事争议在行政诉讼中一并解决，人民法院准许的，由受理行政案件的人民法院管辖。  
公民、法人或者其他组织请求一并审理相关民事争议，人民法院经审查发现行政案件已经超过起诉期限，民事案件尚未立案的，告知当事人另行提起民事诉讼；民事案件已经立案的，由原审判组织继续审理。  
人民法院在审理行政案件中发现民事争议为解决行政争议的基础，当事人没有请求人民法院一并审理相关民事争议的，人民法院应当告知当事人依法申请一并解决民事争议。当事人就民事争议另行提起民事诉讼并已立案的，人民法院应当中止行政诉讼的审理。民事争议处理期间不计算在行政诉讼审理期限内。

**Article 138** Where the people's court decides to jointly try related civil disputes in an administrative action, or the parties to the case unanimously agree that the related civil disputes be resolved together in the administrative action and the people's court approves, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the people's court that accepted the administrative case.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization requests the joint trial of related civil disputes, and the people's court, upon review, finds that the administrative case has exceeded the statute of limitations for prosecution and the civil case has not yet been docketed, it shall inform the parties to file a separate civil action; if the civil case has already been docketed, the original adjudication organization shall continue to try it.  
Where, during the trial of an administrative case, the people's court finds that a civil dispute is the basis for resolving the administrative dispute, and the parties have not requested the people's court to jointly try the related civil disputes, the people's court shall inform the parties to apply for jointly resolving the civil dispute according to law. If the parties file a separate civil action for the civil dispute and it has been docketed, the people's court shall suspend the trial of the administrative action. The period for handling the civil dispute shall not be counted in the trial period of the administrative action.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当作出不予准许一并审理民事争议的决定，并告知当事人可以依法通过其他渠道主张权利：  
（一）法律规定应当由行政机关先行处理的；  
（二）违反民事诉讼法专属管辖规定或者协议管辖约定的；  
（三）约定仲裁或者已经提起民事诉讼的；  
（四）其他不宜一并审理民事争议的情形。  
对不予准许的决定可以申请复议一次。

**Article 139** In any of the following circumstances, the people's court shall make a decision not to permit the joint trial of civil disputes, and inform the parties that they may claim their rights through other channels according to law:  
(1) the law provides that the administrative organ shall handle the matter first;  
(2) it violates the provisions on exclusive jurisdiction or agreement on jurisdiction under the Civil Procedure Law;  
(3) arbitration has been agreed upon or a civil action has already been instituted;  
(4) other circumstances under which the joint trial of civil disputes is not appropriate.  
An application for reconsideration may be filed once against the decision not to permit.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　人民法院在行政诉讼中一并审理相关民事争议的，民事争议应当单独立案，由同一审判组织审理。  
人民法院审理行政机关对民事争议所作裁决的案件，一并审理民事争议的，不另行立案。

**Article 140** Where the people's court jointly tries related civil disputes in an administrative action, the civil disputes shall be separately docketed and tried by the same adjudication organization.  
Where the people's court tries a case involving an administrative organ's adjudication on a civil dispute and jointly tries the civil dispute, no separate docketing is required.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　人民法院一并审理相关民事争议，适用民事法律规范的相关规定，法律另有规定的除外。  
当事人在调解中对民事权益的处分，不能作为审查被诉行政行为合法性的根据。

**Article 141** Where the people's court jointly tries related civil disputes, it shall apply the relevant provisions of civil legal norms, except as otherwise provided by law.  
The disposition of civil rights and interests by the parties during mediation shall not serve as the basis for reviewing the legality of the administrative act under suit.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　对行政争议和民事争议应当分别裁判。  
当事人仅对行政裁判或者民事裁判提出上诉的，未上诉的裁判在上诉期满后即发生法律效力。第一审人民法院应当将全部案卷一并移送第二审人民法院，由行政审判庭审理。第二审人民法院发现未上诉的生效裁判确有错误的，应当按照审判监督程序再审。

**Article 142** The administrative dispute and the civil dispute shall be adjudicated separately.  
If a party files an appeal only against the administrative judgment or the civil judgment, the judgment not appealed shall become legally effective upon the expiration of the appeal period. The court of first instance shall transfer all case files to the court of second instance, and the administrative division shall try the case. If the court of second instance finds that the effective judgment not appealed is indeed erroneous, it shall conduct a retrial under the trial supervision procedure.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　行政诉讼原告在宣判前申请撤诉的，是否准许由人民法院裁定。人民法院裁定准许行政诉讼原告撤诉，但其对已经提起的一并审理相关民事争议不撤诉的，人民法院应当继续审理。

**Article 143** Where the plaintiff in an administrative action applies to withdraw the lawsuit before the judgment is pronounced, the people's court shall rule on whether to permit it. If the people's court permits the plaintiff to withdraw the administrative action, but the plaintiff does not withdraw the related civil disputes that have been filed for joint trial, the people's court shall continue to try them.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　人民法院一并审理相关民事争议，应当按行政案件、民事案件的标准分别收取诉讼费用。  
十一、规范性文件的一并审查

**Article 144** Where the people's court jointly tries related civil disputes, it shall collect litigation fees separately according to the standards for administrative cases and civil cases.  
XI. Joint Review of Normative Documents

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　公民、法人或者其他组织在对行政行为提起诉讼时一并请求对所依据的规范性文件审查的，由行政行为案件管辖法院一并审查。

**Article 145** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization, when filing a lawsuit against an administrative act, simultaneously requests the review of the normative document on which it is based, the court with jurisdiction over the administrative act case shall conduct a joint review.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　公民、法人或者其他组织请求人民法院一并审查[行政诉讼法第五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-53)规定的规范性文件，应当在第一审开庭审理前提出；有正当理由的，也可以在法庭调查中提出。

**Article 146** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization requests the people's court to jointly review a normative document as specified in Article 53 of the Administrative Procedure Law, such request shall be made before the opening of the first-instance court hearing; with justifiable reasons, it may also be made during the court investigation.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　人民法院在对规范性文件审查过程中，发现规范性文件可能不合法的，应当听取规范性文件制定机关的意见。  
制定机关申请出庭陈述意见的，人民法院应当准许。  
行政机关未陈述意见或者未提供相关证明材料的，不能阻止人民法院对规范性文件进行审查。

**Article 147** During the review of a normative document, if the people's court finds that the normative document may be illegal, it shall hear the opinions of the authority that enacted the normative document.  
If the enacting authority applies to appear in court to state its opinions, the people's court shall permit it.  
If the administrative organ fails to state its opinions or fails to provide relevant evidentiary materials, it shall not prevent the people's court from conducting the review of the normative document.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　人民法院对规范性文件进行一并审查时，可以从规范性文件制定机关是否超越权限或者违反法定程序、作出行政行为所依据的条款以及相关条款等方面进行。  
有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第六十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-64)规定的“规范性文件不合法”：  
（一）超越制定机关的法定职权或者超越法律、法规、规章的授权范围的；  
（二）与法律、法规、规章等上位法的规定相抵触的；  
（三）没有法律、法规、规章依据，违法增加公民、法人和其他组织义务或者减损公民、法人和其他组织合法权益的；  
（四）未履行法定批准程序、公开发布程序，严重违反制定程序的；  
（五）其他违反法律、法规以及规章规定的情形。

**Article 148** When conducting a joint review of a normative document, the people's court may review it from aspects such as whether the enacting authority exceeded its statutory powers or violated statutory procedures, and the provisions on which the administrative act is based and related provisions.  
Any of the following circumstances constitutes a "normative document that is illegal" as specified in Article 64 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) exceeding the statutory functions and powers of the enacting authority or exceeding the scope of authorization by laws, regulations, or rules;  
(2) conflicting with the provisions of superior legal norms such as laws, regulations, or rules;  
(3) without any basis in laws, regulations, or rules, illegally increasing the obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, or impairing the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations;  
(4) failing to undergo the statutory approval procedures and public release procedures, and seriously violating the enactment procedures;  
(5) other circumstances violating the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　人民法院经审查认为行政行为所依据的规范性文件合法的，应当作为认定行政行为合法的依据；经审查认为规范性文件不合法的，不作为人民法院认定行政行为合法的依据，并在裁判理由中予以阐明。作出生效裁判的人民法院应当向规范性文件的制定机关提出处理建议，并可以抄送制定机关的同级人民政府、上一级行政机关、监察机关以及规范性文件的备案机关。  
规范性文件不合法的，人民法院可以在裁判生效之日起三个月内，向规范性文件制定机关提出修改或者废止该规范性文件的司法建议。  
规范性文件由多个部门联合制定的，人民法院可以向该规范性文件的主办机关或者共同上一级行政机关发送司法建议。  
接收司法建议的行政机关应当在收到司法建议之日起六十日内予以书面答复。情况紧急的，人民法院可以建议制定机关或者其上一级行政机关立即停止执行该规范性文件。

**Article 149** If, upon review, the people's court finds that the normative document on which the administrative act is based is legal, it shall serve as the basis for determining the legality of the administrative act; if, upon review, it finds that the normative document is illegal, it shall not serve as the basis for the people's court to determine the legality of the administrative act, and shall be explained in the reasoning of the adjudication. The people's court that rendered the effective judgment shall make a handling suggestion to the authority that enacted the normative document, and may send a copy to the people's government at the same level as the enacting authority, the administrative organ at the next higher level, the supervision organ, and the filing authority of the normative document.  
If the normative document is illegal, the people's court may, within three months from the date the judgment takes effect, issue a judicial suggestion to the enacting authority to amend or abolish the normative document.  
If the normative document is jointly enacted by multiple departments, the people's court may send the judicial suggestion to the leading authority of the normative document or the common higher-level administrative organ.  
The administrative organ receiving the judicial suggestion shall give a written reply within sixty days from the date of receiving the judicial suggestion. In an emergency, the people's court may suggest that the enacting authority or its higher-level administrative organ immediately cease the implementation of the normative document.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　人民法院认为规范性文件不合法的，应当在裁判生效后报送上一级人民法院进行备案。涉及国务院部门、省级行政机关制定的规范性文件，司法建议还应当分别层报最高人民法院、高级人民法院备案。

**Article 150** Where the people's court considers a normative document to be illegal, it shall, after the judgment takes effect, report it to the next higher people's court for filing. For normative documents enacted by departments of the State Council or administrative organs at the provincial level, the judicial suggestion shall also be reported level by level to the Supreme People's Court or the Higher People's Court for filing.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　各级人民法院院长对本院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，发现规范性文件合法性认定错误，认为需要再审的，应当提交审判委员会讨论。  
最高人民法院对地方各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，发现规范性文件合法性认定错误的，有权提审或者指令下级人民法院再审。  
十二、执行

**Article 151** If the president of a people's court at any level finds an error in the determination of the legality of a normative document in a legally effective judgment or ruling of that court and considers a retrial necessary, the president shall submit it to the adjudication committee for discussion.  
If the Supreme People's Court finds an error in the determination of the legality of a normative document in a legally effective judgment or ruling of a local people's court at any level, or if a higher people's court finds such an error in a legally effective judgment or ruling of a lower people's court, it has the power to bring the case up for trial or to instruct the lower people's court to conduct a retrial.  
XII. Execution

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　对发生法律效力的行政判决书、行政裁定书、行政赔偿判决书和行政调解书，负有义务的一方当事人拒绝履行的，对方当事人可以依法申请人民法院强制执行。  
人民法院判决行政机关履行行政赔偿、行政补偿或者其他行政给付义务，行政机关拒不履行的，对方当事人可以依法向法院申请强制执行。

**Article 152** For a legally effective administrative judgment, administrative ruling, administrative compensation judgment, or administrative mediation agreement, if the party bearing obligations refuses to perform, the other party may apply to the people's court for compulsory execution according to law.  
If the people's court orders an administrative organ to perform administrative compensation, administrative compensation, or other administrative payment obligations, and the administrative organ refuses to perform, the other party may apply to the people's court for compulsory execution according to law.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　申请执行的期限为二年。申请执行时效的中止、中断，适用法律有关规定。  
申请执行的期限从法律文书规定的履行期间最后一日起计算；法律文书规定分期履行的，从规定的每次履行期间的最后一日起计算；法律文书中没有规定履行期限的，从该法律文书送达当事人之日起计算。  
逾期申请的，除有正当理由外，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 153** The time limit for applying for execution is two years. The suspension or interruption of the time limit for applying for execution shall be governed by the relevant provisions of law.  
The time limit for applying for execution shall be calculated from the last day of the performance period specified in the legal instrument; if the legal instrument provides for performance by installments, it shall be calculated from the last day of each specified performance period; if no performance period is specified in the legal instrument, it shall be calculated from the date the legal instrument is served on the parties.  
For an application made after the expiration of the time limit, the people's court shall not accept it, except where there are justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　发生法律效力的行政判决书、行政裁定书、行政赔偿判决书和行政调解书，由第一审人民法院执行。  
第一审人民法院认为情况特殊，需要由第二审人民法院执行的，可以报请第二审人民法院执行；第二审人民法院可以决定由其执行，也可以决定由第一审人民法院执行。

**Article 154** A legally effective administrative judgment, administrative ruling, administrative compensation judgment, or administrative mediation agreement shall be executed by the court of first instance.  
If the court of first instance considers that the circumstances are special and execution by the court of second instance is necessary, it may request the court of second instance to execute; the court of second instance may decide to execute by itself or decide that the court of first instance shall execute.

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　行政机关根据[行政诉讼法第九十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-97)的规定申请执行其行政行为，应当具备以下条件：  
（一）行政行为依法可以由人民法院执行；  
（二）行政行为已经生效并具有可执行内容；  
（三）申请人是作出该行政行为的行政机关或者法律、法规、规章授权的组织；  
（四）被申请人是该行政行为所确定的义务人；  
（五）被申请人在行政行为确定的期限内或者行政机关催告期限内未履行义务；  
（六）申请人在法定期限内提出申请；  
（七）被申请执行的行政案件属于受理执行申请的人民法院管辖。  
行政机关申请人民法院执行，应当提交[行政强制法第五十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政强制法.md#art-55)规定的相关材料。  
人民法院对符合条件的申请，应当在五日内立案受理，并通知申请人；对不符合条件的申请，应当裁定不予受理。行政机关对不予受理裁定有异议，在十五日内向上一级人民法院申请复议的，上一级人民法院应当在收到复议申请之日起十五日内作出裁定。

**Article 155** When an administrative agency applies to the People's Court for enforcement of its administrative act in accordance with Article 97 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) The administrative act may be enforceable by the People's Court according to law;  
(2) The administrative act has taken effect and has enforceable content;  
(3) The applicant is the administrative agency that made the administrative act or an organization authorized by laws, regulations, or rules;  
(4) The respondent is the obligor determined by the administrative act;  
(5) The respondent has failed to perform the obligations within the time limit specified in the administrative act or within the time limit urged by the administrative agency;  
(6) The applicant has filed the application within the statutory time limit;  
(7) The administrative case subject to enforcement falls under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepts the enforcement application.  
When an administrative agency applies to the People's Court for enforcement, it shall submit the relevant materials as specified in Article 55 of the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The People's Court shall, within five days, docket and accept an application that meets the conditions and shall notify the applicant; for an application that does not meet the conditions, it shall rule to refuse acceptance. If the administrative agency objects to the ruling of refusal to accept and applies for reconsideration to the people's court at the next higher level within fifteen days, the people's court at the next higher level shall make a ruling within fifteen days from the date of receiving the reconsideration application.

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条　没有强制执行权的行政机关申请人民法院强制执行其行政行为，应当自被执行人的法定起诉期限届满之日起三个月内提出。逾期申请的，除有正当理由外，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 156** An administrative agency without compulsory enforcement authority that applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement of its administrative act shall file the application within three months from the date on which the statutory period for the person subject to enforcement to institute an action expires. If the application is filed beyond the time limit, the People's Court shall not accept it unless there are justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条　行政机关申请人民法院强制执行其行政行为的，由申请人所在地的基层人民法院受理；执行对象为不动产的，由不动产所在地的基层人民法院受理。  
基层人民法院认为执行确有困难的，可以报请上级人民法院执行；上级人民法院可以决定由其执行，也可以决定由下级人民法院执行。

**Article 157** Where an administrative agency applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement of its administrative act, the case shall be accepted by the primary people's court at the location of the applicant; if the subject matter of enforcement is immovable property, it shall be accepted by the primary people's court at the location of the immovable property.  
If a primary people's court finds that enforcement is indeed difficult, it may report to the superior people's court for enforcement; the superior people's court may decide to enforce it by itself or may decide to have it enforced by a lower people's court.

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条　行政机关根据法律的授权对平等主体之间民事争议作出裁决后，当事人在法定期限内不起诉又不履行，作出裁决的行政机关在申请执行的期限内未申请人民法院强制执行的，生效行政裁决确定的权利人或者其继承人、权利承受人在六个月内可以申请人民法院强制执行。  
享有权利的公民、法人或者其他组织申请人民法院强制执行生效行政裁决，参照行政机关申请人民法院强制执行行政行为的规定。

**Article 158** After an administrative agency, pursuant to the authorization of a law, makes an adjudication on a civil dispute between equal parties, if the parties neither institute an action within the statutory time limit nor perform the adjudication, and the administrative agency that made the adjudication fails to apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement within the period for applying for enforcement, the obligee determined by the effective administrative adjudication, or its heir or successor in interest may, within six months, apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization that enjoys rights applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement of an effective administrative adjudication, the provisions governing an administrative agency's application to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement of an administrative act shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条　行政机关或者行政行为确定的权利人申请人民法院强制执行前，有充分理由认为被执行人可能逃避执行的，可以申请人民法院采取财产保全措施。后者申请强制执行的，应当提供相应的财产担保。

**Article 159** Before an administrative agency or the obligee determined by an administrative act applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement, if there are sufficient grounds to believe that the person subject to enforcement may evade enforcement, it may apply to the People's Court to take property preservation measures. Where the latter applies for compulsory enforcement, it shall provide corresponding property guarantees.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条　人民法院受理行政机关申请执行其行政行为的案件后，应当在七日内由行政审判庭对行政行为的合法性进行审查，并作出是否准予执行的裁定。  
人民法院在作出裁定前发现行政行为明显违法并损害被执行人合法权益的，应当听取被执行人和行政机关的意见，并自受理之日起三十日内作出是否准予执行的裁定。  
需要采取强制执行措施的，由本院负责强制执行非诉行政行为的机构执行。

**Article 160** After the People's Court accepts a case where an administrative agency applies for enforcement of its administrative act, the administrative adjudication division shall, within seven days, examine the legality of the administrative act and make a ruling on whether to grant enforcement.  
If, before making the ruling, the People's Court finds that the administrative act is manifestly illegal and infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the person subject to enforcement, it shall hear the opinions of the person subject to enforcement and the administrative agency, and shall make a ruling on whether to grant enforcement within thirty days from the date of acceptance.  
Where compulsory enforcement measures are necessary, the institution of the court responsible for compulsory enforcement of non-litigation administrative acts shall carry out the enforcement.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条　被申请执行的行政行为有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不准予执行：  
（一）实施主体不具有行政主体资格的；  
（二）明显缺乏事实根据的；  
（三）明显缺乏法律、法规依据的；  
（四）其他明显违法并损害被执行人合法权益的情形。  
行政机关对不准予执行的裁定有异议，在十五日内向上一级人民法院申请复议的，上一级人民法院应当在收到复议申请之日起三十日内作出裁定。  
十三、附则

**Article 161** Where the administrative act applied for enforcement falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule to disallow enforcement:  
(1) The implementing entity does not have the status of an administrative subject;  
(2) The act is manifestly lacking in factual basis;  
(3) The act is manifestly lacking in legal or regulatory basis;  
(4) Other circumstances where the act is manifestly illegal and infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the person subject to enforcement.  
If the administrative agency objects to the ruling disallowing enforcement and applies for reconsideration to the people's court at the next higher level within fifteen days, the people's court at the next higher level shall make a ruling within thirty days from the date of receiving the reconsideration application.  
13. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条　公民、法人或者其他组织对2015年5月1日之前作出的行政行为提起诉讼，请求确认行政行为无效的，人民法院不予立案。

**Article 162** Where a citizen, legal person, or other organization institutes an action against an administrative act made before May 1, 2015, and requests confirmation that the administrative act is void, the People's Court shall not docket the case.

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条　本解释自2018年2月8日起施行。  
本解释施行后，《最高人民法院关于执行〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》（法释〔2000〕8号）、《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》（法释〔2015〕9号）同时废止。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 163** This Interpretation shall come into force on February 8, 2018.  
Upon the coming into force of this Interpretation, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (Fa Shi [2000] No. 8) and the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (Fa Shi [2015] No. 9) shall be abolished simultaneously. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, it shall no longer apply.
