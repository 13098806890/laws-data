### 最高人民法院关于国有资产产权管理行政案件管辖问题的解释

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于国有资产产权管理行政案件管辖问题的解释  
  
（2001年1月10日最高人民法院审判委员会第1156次会议通过，自2001年2月21日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕6号  
  
为了正确适用[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-17)、第十九条的规定，现对国有资产产权管理行政案件的管辖问题作出如下解释：  
  
当事人因国有资产产权界定行为提起行政诉讼的，应当根据不同情况确定管辖法院。产权界定行为直接针对不动产作出的，由不动产所在地人民法院管辖。产权界定行为针对包含不动产在内的整体产权作出的，由最初作出产权界定的行政机关所在地人民法院管辖；经过复议的案件，复议机关改变原产权界定行为的，也可以由复议机关所在地人民法院管辖。

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2001] No. 6  
For the purpose of correctly applying the provisions of Articles 17 and 19 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the following interpretation is hereby given on the jurisdiction over administrative cases concerning the management of state-owned property rights:  
Where a party initiates an administrative action due to an act of definition of state-owned property rights, the competent People's Court shall be determined according to different circumstances. If the act of definition of property rights is directly aimed at immovable property, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the immovable property is located. If the act of definition of property rights is aimed at the entire property including immovable property, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the administrative organ that initially performed the definition of property rights is located; in the case of a reconsidered case, if the reconsideration organ changed the original act of definition of property rights, the case may also be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the reconsideration organ is located.
