# 最高人民法院关于民事、行政诉讼中司法赔偿若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕27号  
**公布日期**：2000-09-14  
**生效日期**：2000-09-21

> （2000年9月14日最高人民法院审判委员会第1130次会议通过，自2000年9月21日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕27号 根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》（以下简称国家赔偿法）以及有关法律规定，现就审理民事、行政诉讼中司法赔偿案件具体适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 根据国家赔偿法第三十一条的规定，人民法院在民事、行政诉讼过程中，违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全措施或者对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益造成损害的，依法应由国家承担赔偿责任。 第二条 违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施，是指下列行为： （一）对没有实施妨害诉讼行为的人或者没有证据证明实施妨害诉讼的人采取司法拘留、罚款措施的； （二）超过法律规定期限实施司法拘留的； （三）对同一妨害诉讼行为重复采取罚款、司法拘留措施的； （四）超过法律规定金额实施罚款的； （五）违反法律规定的其他情形。 第三条 违法采取保全措施，是指人民法院依职权采取的下列行为： （一）依法不应当采取保全措施而采取保全措施或者依法不应当解除保全措施而解除保全措施的； （二）保全案外人财产的，但案外人对案件当事人负有到期债务的情形除外； （三）明显超过申请人申请保全数额或者保全范围的； （四）对查封、扣押的财物不履行监管职责，严重不负责任，造成毁损、灭失的，但依法交由有关单位、个人负责保管的情形除外；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 根据[国家赔偿法第三十一条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-31)的规定，人民法院在民事、行政诉讼过程中，违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全措施或者对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益造成损害的，依法应由国家承担赔偿责任。

**Article 1** In accordance with the provisions of Article 31 of the State Compensation Law, where in the course of civil or administrative litigation, a People's Court illegally adopts compulsory measures against acts obstructing litigation, takes preservative measures, or erroneously enforces judgments, rulings or other effective legal documents, thus infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons or other organizations and causing damage, the State shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施，是指下列行为：  
（一）对没有实施妨害诉讼行为的人或者没有证据证明实施妨害诉讼的人采取司法拘留、罚款措施的；  
（二）超过法律规定期限实施司法拘留的；  
（三）对同一妨害诉讼行为重复采取罚款、司法拘留措施的；  
（四）超过法律规定金额实施罚款的；  
（五）违反法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 2** Illegal adoption of compulsory measures against acts obstructing litigation shall refer to the following acts:  
(1) Imposing judicial detention or a fine on a person who has not committed an act obstructing litigation or for whom there is no evidence proving the commission of an act obstructing litigation;  
(2) Imposing judicial detention beyond the time limit prescribed by law;  
(3) Imposing a fine or judicial detention repeatedly for the same act obstructing litigation;  
(4) Imposing a fine exceeding the amount prescribed by law;  
(5) Other circumstances that violate the provisions of laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 违法采取保全措施，是指人民法院依职权采取的下列行为：  
（一）依法不应当采取保全措施而采取保全措施或者依法不应当解除保全措施而解除保全措施的；  
（二）保全案外人财产的，但案外人对案件当事人负有到期债务的情形除外；  
（三）明显超过申请人申请保全数额或者保全范围的；  
（四）对查封、扣押的财物不履行监管职责，严重不负责任，造成毁损、灭失的，但依法交由有关单位、个人负责保管的情形除外；  
（五）变卖财产未由合法评估机构估价，或者应当拍卖而未依法拍卖，强行将财物变卖给他人的；  
（六）违反法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 3** Illegal adoption of preservative measures shall refer to the following acts taken by a People's Court ex officio:  
(1) Taking preservative measures where preservative measures should not have been taken according to law, or dissolving preservative measures where preservative measures should not have been dissolved according to law;  
(2) Preserving the property of a person not a party to the case, except where such person has a matured debt owed to a party to the case;  
(3) Obviously exceeding the amount or scope of preservative measures applied for by the applicant;  
(4) Failing to perform supervisory duties over sealed-up or distrained property, with serious irresponsibility, resulting in damage or loss, except where the property has been lawfully delivered to the relevant unit or individual for safekeeping;  
(5) Selling off property without appraisal by a legitimate appraisal institution, or forcibly selling off property to others when it should have been sold by auction without lawful auction;  
(6) Other circumstances that violate the provisions of laws.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，是指对已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、民事制裁决定、调解、支付令、仲裁裁决、具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书以及行政处罚、处理决定等执行错误。包括下列行为：  
（一）执行尚未发生法律效力的判决、裁定、民事制裁决定等法律文书的；  
（二）违反法律规定先予执行的；  
（三）违法执行案外人财产且无法执行回转的；  
（四）明显超过申请数额、范围执行且无法执行回转的；  
（五）执行过程中，对查封、扣押的财产不履行监管职责，严重不负责任，造成财物毁损、灭失的；  
（六）执行过程中，变卖财物未由合法评估机构估价，或者应当拍卖而未依法拍卖，强行将财物变卖给他人的；  
（七）违反法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 4** Erroneous enforcement of judgments, rulings or other effective legal documents shall refer to erroneous enforcement of judgments, rulings, civil sanction decisions, mediation, payment orders, arbitration awards, notarized creditor's rights documents with compulsory enforcement effect, as well as administrative penalties, handling decisions, etc. that have come into legal effect. It includes the following acts:  
(1) Enforcing legal documents such as judgments, rulings, civil sanction decisions that have not yet come into legal effect;  
(2) Conducting advance execution in violation of legal provisions;  
(3) Illegally enforcing property of a person not a party to the case and unable to reverse the enforcement;  
(4) Obviously exceeding the amount or scope of the application for enforcement and unable to reverse the enforcement;  
(5) During the enforcement process, failing to perform supervisory duties over sealed-up or distrained property, with serious irresponsibility, resulting in damage or loss of property;  
(6) During the enforcement process, selling off property without appraisal by a legitimate appraisal institution, or forcibly selling off property to others when it should have been sold by auction without lawful auction;  
(7) Other circumstances that violate the provisions of laws.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院及其工作人员在民事、行政诉讼或者执行过程中，以殴打或者唆使他人以殴打等暴力行为，或者违法使用武器、警械，造成公民身体伤害、死亡的，应当比照[国家赔偿法第十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-15)第（四）项、第（五）项规定予以赔偿。

**Article 5** Where a People's Court and its staff, in the course of civil or administrative litigation or enforcement, cause bodily injury or death to a citizen by beating or instigating others to use violence such as beating, or by illegally using weapons or police instruments, compensation shall be paid by reference to the provisions of Items (4) and (5) of Article 15 of the State Compensation Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院及其工作人员在民事、行政诉讼或者执行过程中，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)至第五条规定情形，造成损害的，应当承担直接损失的赔偿责任。  
因多种原因造成的损害，只赔偿因违法侵权行为所造成的直接损失。

**Article 6** Where a People's Court and its staff, in the course of civil or administrative litigation or enforcement, have the circumstances specified in Articles 2 through 5 of this Interpretation and cause damage, they shall bear the compensation liability for direct losses.  
Where damage is caused by multiple reasons, only the direct losses caused by the illegal infringing act shall be compensated.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 根据[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)、第三十一条的规定，具有下列情形之一的，国家不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）因申请人申请保全有错误造成损害的；  
（二）因申请人提供的执行标的物有错误造成损害的；  
（三）人民法院工作人员与行使职权无关的个人行为；  
（四）属于[民事诉讼法第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-214)规定情形的；  
（五）被保全人、被执行人，或者人民法院依法指定的保管人员违法动用、隐匿、毁损、转移、变卖人民法院已经保全的财产的；  
（六）因不可抗力造成损害后果的；  
（七）依法不应由国家承担赔偿责任的其他情形。

**Article 7** In accordance with the provisions of Articles 17 and 31 of the State Compensation Law, the State shall not bear compensation liability under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Damage caused by an error in the applicant's application for preservative measures;  
(2) Damage caused by an error in the subject matter of enforcement provided by the applicant;  
(3) Personal conduct of staff of the People's Court unrelated to the exercise of their functions;  
(4) Circumstances falling under the provisions of Article 214 of the Civil Procedure Law;  
(5) The preserved person, the person subject to enforcement, or the custodian lawfully designated by the People's Court illegally uses, conceals, damages, transfers, or sells off property that has been preserved by the People's Court;  
(6) Damage caused by force majeure;  
(7) Other circumstances for which the State should not bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 申请民事、行政诉讼中司法赔偿的，违法行使职权的行为应当先经依法确认。  
申请确认的，应当先向侵权的人民法院提出。  
人民法院应自受理确认申请之日起两个月内依照相应程序作出裁决或相关的决定。  
申请人对确认裁定或者决定不服或者侵权的人民法院逾期不予确认的，申请人可以向其上一级人民法院申诉。

**Article 8** Where an application is made for judicial compensation in civil or administrative litigation, the illegal exercise of functions shall first be confirmed according to law.  
An application for confirmation shall first be submitted to the People's Court that committed the infringement.  
The People's Court shall, within two months from the date of accepting the application for confirmation, make a ruling or relevant decision in accordance with the corresponding procedures.  
If the applicant refuses to accept the confirmation ruling or decision, or if the infringing People's Court fails to make confirmation within the time limit, the applicant may petition to the People's Court at the next higher level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 未经依法确认直接向人民法院赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定的，人民法院赔偿委员会不予受理。

**Article 9** Where an application is made directly to the Compensation Committee of a People's Court for a compensation decision without confirmation according to law, the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 经依法确认有[本解释第二条](#art-2)至第五条规定情形之一的，赔偿请求人可依法向侵权的人民法院提出赔偿申请，人民法院应当受理。人民法院逾期不作决定的，赔偿请求人可以向其上一级人民法院赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定。

**Article 10** Where any circumstance specified in Articles 2 through 5 of this Interpretation has been confirmed according to law, the claimant for compensation may, according to law, submit an application for compensation to the infringing People's Court, and the People's Court shall accept the application. If the People's Court fails to make a decision within the time limit, the claimant for compensation may apply to the Compensation Committee of the People's Court at the next higher level for a compensation decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 民事、行政诉讼中司法赔偿的赔偿方式主要为支付赔偿金。包括：支付侵犯人身自由权、生命健康权的赔偿金；财产损坏的，赔偿修复所需费用；财产灭失的，按侵权行为发生时当地市场价格予以赔偿；财产已拍卖的，给付拍卖所得的价款；财产已变卖的，按合法评估机构的估价赔偿；造成其他损害的，赔偿直接损失。  
能够返还财产或者恢复原状的，予以返还财产或者恢复原状。包括：解除查封、扣押、冻结；返还财产、恢复原状；退还罚款、罚没财物。

**Article 11** The method of compensation for judicial compensation in civil or administrative litigation shall mainly be payment of compensation money. This includes: payment of compensation for infringement of the right to personal freedom and the right to life and health; where property is damaged, compensation for the cost of repair; where property is lost, compensation according to the local market price at the time of the infringing act; where property has been auctioned, payment of the proceeds from the auction; where property has been sold off, compensation according to the appraisal by a legitimate appraisal institution; where other damage is caused, compensation for direct losses.  
Where it is possible to return the property or restore the original condition, the property shall be returned or the original condition restored. This includes: lifting sealing, distraint, and freezing; returning property and restoring original condition; refunding fines and confiscated property.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 [国家赔偿法第二十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-28)第（七）项规定的直接损失包括下列情形：  
（一）保全、执行过程中造成财物灭失、毁损、霉变、腐烂等损坏的；  
（二）违法使用保全、执行的财物造成损坏的；  
（三）保全的财产系国家批准的金融机构贷款的，当事人应支付的该贷款借贷状态下的贷款利息。执行上述款项的，贷款本金及当事人应支付的该贷款借贷状态下的贷款利息；  
（四）保全、执行造成停产停业的，停产停业期间的职工工资、税金、水电费等必要的经常性费用；  
（五）法律规定的其他直接损失。

**Article 12** The direct losses specified in Item (7) of Article 28 of the State Compensation Law shall include the following circumstances:  
(1) Loss, damage, mildew, rot, or other deterioration of property during preservation or enforcement;  
(2) Damage caused by illegal use of property during preservation or enforcement;  
(3) Where the preserved property is a loan from a financial institution approved by the State, the loan interest payable by the party under the loan borrowing status. Where the above-mentioned funds are enforced, the loan principal and the loan interest payable by the party under the loan borrowing status;  
(4) Where preservation or enforcement causes suspension of production or business, the necessary recurring expenses such as wages of employees, taxes, water and electricity charges during the period of suspension;  
(5) Other direct losses prescribed by law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 违法采取司法拘留措施的，按[国家赔偿法第二十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-26)规定予以赔偿。  
造成受害人名誉权、荣誉权损害的，按照[国家赔偿法第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)规定，在侵权行为影响的范围内，为受害人消除影响、恢复名誉、赔礼道歉。

**Article 13** Where judicial detention is illegally imposed, compensation shall be paid in accordance with Article 26 of the State Compensation Law.  
Where damage is caused to the victim's right to reputation or right to honor, the victim's adverse effects shall be eliminated, reputation shall be restored, and an apology shall be made within the scope of influence of the infringing act in accordance with Article 30 of the State Compensation Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 人民法院赔偿委员会在审理侦查、检察、监狱管理机关及其工作人员违法行使职权侵犯公民财产权造成损害的赔偿案件时，可参照本解释的有关规定办理。

**Article 14** When the Compensation Committee of a People's Court tries a compensation case involving damage caused by illegal exercise of functions by investigation, procuratorial, or prison administration organs or their staff infringing upon citizens' property rights, it may handle the case by reference to the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.
