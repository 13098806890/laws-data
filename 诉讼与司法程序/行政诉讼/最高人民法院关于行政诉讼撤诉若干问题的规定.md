# 最高人民法院关于行政诉讼撤诉若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕2号  
**公布日期**：2007-12-17  
**生效日期**：2008-02-01

> 《最高人民法院关于行政诉讼撤诉若干问题的规定》已于2007年12月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1441次会议通过，现予公布，自2008年2月1日起施行。 2008年1月14日 最高人民法院 关于行政诉讼撤诉若干问题的规定 法释〔2008〕2号 为妥善化解行政争议，依法审查行政诉讼中行政机关改变被诉具体行政行为及当事人申请撤诉的行为，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》制定本规定。 第一条  人民法院经审查认为被诉具体行政行为违法或者不当，可以在宣告判决或者裁定前，建议被告改变其所作的具体行政行为。 第二条  被告改变被诉具体行政行为，原告申请撤诉，符合下列条件的，人民法院应当裁定准许： （一）申请撤诉是当事人真实意思表示； （二）被告改变被诉具体行政行为，不违反法律、法规的禁止性规定，不超越或者放弃职权，不损害公共利益和他人合法权益； （三）被告已经改变或者决定改变被诉具体行政行为，并书面告知人民法院； （四）第三人无异议。 第三条  有下列情形之一的，属于行政诉讼法第五十一条规定的“被告改变其所作的具体行政行为”： （一）改变被诉具体行政行为所认定的主要事实和证据； （二）改变被诉具体行政行为所适用的规范依据且对定性产生影响；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院经审查认为被诉具体行政行为违法或者不当，可以在宣告判决或者裁定前，建议被告改变其所作的具体行政行为。

**Article 1** If a People's Court finds, upon review, that the specific administrative action under litigation is illegal or improper, it may, before pronouncing a judgment or ruling, suggest that the defendant change the specific administrative action it has taken.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  被告改变被诉具体行政行为，原告申请撤诉，符合下列条件的，人民法院应当裁定准许：  
（一）申请撤诉是当事人真实意思表示；  
（二）被告改变被诉具体行政行为，不违反法律、法规的禁止性规定，不超越或者放弃职权，不损害公共利益和他人合法权益；  
（三）被告已经改变或者决定改变被诉具体行政行为，并书面告知人民法院；  
（四）第三人无异议。

**Article 2** If the defendant changes the specific administrative action under litigation and the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the action, meeting the following conditions, the People's Court shall make a ruling to permit the withdrawal:  
(1) the application for withdrawal reflects the true intention of the party;  
(2) the defendant's change of the specific administrative action under litigation does not violate any prohibitive provisions of laws or regulations, does not exceed or abandon its functions and powers, and does not harm the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(3) the defendant has changed or decided to change the specific administrative action under litigation and has notified the People's Court in writing; and  
(4) the third party has no objection.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第五十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-51)规定的“被告改变其所作的具体行政行为”：  
（一）改变被诉具体行政行为所认定的主要事实和证据；  
（二）改变被诉具体行政行为所适用的规范依据且对定性产生影响；  
（三）撤销、部分撤销或者变更被诉具体行政行为处理结果。

**Article 3** Any of the following circumstances shall fall within the scope of "the defendant changes the specific administrative action it has taken" as provided in Article 51 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) changing the main facts and evidence determined in the specific administrative action under litigation;  
(2) changing the normative basis applied in the specific administrative action under litigation, which affects the characterization; or  
(3) revoking, partially revoking, or altering the result of the specific administrative action under litigation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  有下列情形之一的，可以视为“被告改变其所作的具体行政行为”：  
（一）根据原告的请求依法履行法定职责；  
（二）采取相应的补救、补偿等措施；  
（三）在行政裁决案件中，书面认可原告与第三人达成的和解。

**Article 4** Any of the following circumstances may be deemed as "the defendant changes the specific administrative action it has taken":  
(1) performing statutory duties according to law at the request of the plaintiff;  
(2) taking corresponding remedial, compensatory, or other measures; or  
(3) in an administrative adjudication case, acknowledging in writing the settlement reached between the plaintiff and the third party.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  被告改变被诉具体行政行为，原告申请撤诉，有履行内容且履行完毕的，人民法院可以裁定准许撤诉；不能即时或者一次性履行的，人民法院可以裁定准许撤诉，也可以裁定中止审理。

**Article 5** If the defendant changes the specific administrative action under litigation and the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the action, and there is content to be performed and it has been fully performed, the People's Court may make a ruling to permit withdrawal. If the performance cannot be made immediately or in a lump sum, the People's Court may make a ruling to permit withdrawal, or may make a ruling to suspend the hearing.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  准许撤诉裁定可以载明被告改变被诉具体行政行为的主要内容及履行情况，并可以根据案件具体情况，在裁定理由中明确被诉具体行政行为全部或者部分不再执行。

**Article 6** The ruling permitting withdrawal may specify the main content of the defendant's change of the specific administrative action under litigation and the performance thereof, and may, according to the specific circumstances of the case, clarify in the reasons of the ruling that all or part of the specific administrative action under litigation shall no longer be enforced.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  申请撤诉不符合法定条件，或者被告改变被诉具体行政行为后当事人不撤诉的，人民法院应当及时作出裁判。

**Article 7** If the application for withdrawal does not meet the statutory conditions, or if the party does not withdraw the action after the defendant changes the specific administrative action under litigation, the People's Court shall render a judgment or ruling in a timely manner.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  第二审或者再审期间行政机关改变被诉具体行政行为，当事人申请撤回上诉或者再审申请的，参照本规定。  
准许撤回上诉或者再审申请的裁定可以载明行政机关改变被诉具体行政行为的主要内容及履行情况，并可以根据案件具体情况，在裁定理由中明确被诉具体行政行为或者原裁判全部或者部分不再执行。

**Article 8** During the second instance or retrial, if the administrative organ changes the specific administrative action under litigation and the party applies for withdrawal of the appeal or the application for retrial, reference shall be made to these Provisions.  
The ruling permitting withdrawal of the appeal or the application for retrial may specify the main content of the administrative organ's change of the specific administrative action under litigation and the performance thereof, and may, according to the specific circumstances of the case, clarify in the reasons of the ruling that all or part of the specific administrative action under litigation or the original judgment or ruling shall no longer be enforced.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  本院以前所作的司法解释及规范性文件，凡与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 9** Any previous judicial interpretations and normative documents of this Court that are inconsistent with these Provisions shall be governed by these Provisions.
