# 最高人民法院关于行政诉讼证据若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕21号  
**公布日期**：2002-06-04  
**生效日期**：2002-10-01

> （2002年6月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1224次会议通过，自2002年10月1日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕21号 为准确认定案件事实，公正、及时地审理行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法）等有关法律规定，结合行政审判实际，制定本规定。 一、举证责任分配和举证期限 第一条 根据行政诉讼法第三十二条和第四十三条的规定，被告对作出的具体行政行为负有举证责任，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十日内，提供据以作出被诉具体行政行为的全部证据和所依据的规范性文件。被告不提供或者无正当理由逾期提供证据的，视为被诉具体行政行为没有相应的证据。 被告因不可抗力或者客观上不能控制的其他正当事由，不能在前款规定的期限内提供证据的，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十日内向人民法院提出延期提供证据的书面申请。人民法院准许延期提供的，被告应当在正当事由消除后十日内提供证据。逾期提供的，视为被诉具体行政行为没有相应的证据。 第二条 原告或者第三人提出其在行政程序中没有提出的反驳理由或者证据的，经人民法院准许，被告可以在第一审程序中补充相应的证据。 第三条 根据行政诉讼法第三十三条的规定，在诉讼过程中，被告及其诉讼代理人不得自行向原告和证人收集证据。 第四条 公民、法人或者其他组织向人民法院起诉时，应当提供其符合起诉条件的相应的证据材料。 在起诉被告不作为的案件中，原告应当提供其在行政程序中曾经提出申请的证据材料。但有下列情形的除外： （一）被告应当依职权主动履行法定职责的； （二）原告因被告受理申请的登记制度不完备等正当事由不能提供相关证据材料并能够作出合理说明的。 被告认为原告起诉超过法定期限的，由被告承担举证责任。 第五条 在行政赔偿诉讼中，原告应当对被诉具体行政行为造成损害的事实提供证据。 第六条 原告可以提供证明被诉具体行政行为违法的证据。原告提供的证据不成立的，不免除被告对被诉具体行政行为合法性的举证责任。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-32)和第四十三条的规定，被告对作出的具体行政行为负有举证责任，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十日内，提供据以作出被诉具体行政行为的全部证据和所依据的规范性文件。被告不提供或者无正当理由逾期提供证据的，视为被诉具体行政行为没有相应的证据。  
被告因不可抗力或者客观上不能控制的其他正当事由，不能在前款规定的期限内提供证据的，应当在收到起诉状副本之日起十日内向人民法院提出延期提供证据的书面申请。人民法院准许延期提供的，被告应当在正当事由消除后十日内提供证据。逾期提供的，视为被诉具体行政行为没有相应的证据。

**Article 1** Pursuant to the provisions of Article 32 and Article 43 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the specific administrative act it has performed, and shall, within ten days from the date of receipt of the copy of the bill of prosecution, provide all the evidence on the basis of which the specific administrative act under prosecution was performed and the normative documents relied upon. If the defendant fails to provide evidence or provides evidence beyond the time limit without justifiable reasons, the specific administrative act under prosecution shall be deemed to have no corresponding evidence.  
If the defendant cannot provide evidence within the time limit prescribed in the preceding paragraph due to force majeure or other justifiable reasons objectively beyond its control, it shall, within ten days from the date of receipt of the copy of the bill of prosecution, submit a written application to the People's Court for an extension of time to provide evidence. Where the People's Court permits the extension, the defendant shall provide the evidence within ten days after the justifiable reasons disappear. If the evidence is provided beyond the extended time limit, the specific administrative act under prosecution shall be deemed to have no corresponding evidence.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 原告或者第三人提出其在行政程序中没有提出的反驳理由或者证据的，经人民法院准许，被告可以在第一审程序中补充相应的证据。

**Article 2** Where the plaintiff or a third party raises rebuttal reasons or evidence that were not raised in the administrative procedure, upon permission of the People's Court, the defendant may supplement corresponding evidence during the first instance proceedings.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-33)的规定，在诉讼过程中，被告及其诉讼代理人不得自行向原告和证人收集证据。

**Article 3** Pursuant to the provision of Article 33 of the Administrative Procedure Law, during the litigation process, the defendant and its litigation agent shall not collect evidence from the plaintiff and witnesses on their own.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 公民、法人或者其他组织向人民法院起诉时，应当提供其符合起诉条件的相应的证据材料。  
在起诉被告不作为的案件中，原告应当提供其在行政程序中曾经提出申请的证据材料。但有下列情形的除外：  
（一）被告应当依职权主动履行法定职责的；  
（二）原告因被告受理申请的登记制度不完备等正当事由不能提供相关证据材料并能够作出合理说明的。  
被告认为原告起诉超过法定期限的，由被告承担举证责任。

**Article 4** When a citizen, legal person, or other organization prosecutes before the People's Court, it shall provide corresponding evidential materials showing that it meets the conditions for prosecution.  
In a case where the prosecution is against a defendant's omission, the plaintiff shall provide evidential materials showing that it once filed an application in the administrative procedure, except under the following circumstances:  
(1) the defendant shall proactively perform its legal duties ex officio; or  
(2) the plaintiff cannot provide relevant evidential materials due to justifiable reasons such as the incomplete registration system of the defendant for accepting applications, and is able to make a reasonable explanation.  
If the defendant considers that the plaintiff's prosecution exceeds the statutory time limit, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 在行政赔偿诉讼中，原告应当对被诉具体行政行为造成损害的事实提供证据。

**Article 5** In an administrative compensation action, the plaintiff shall provide evidence for the fact that damage was caused by the specific administrative act under prosecution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 原告可以提供证明被诉具体行政行为违法的证据。原告提供的证据不成立的，不免除被告对被诉具体行政行为合法性的举证责任。

**Article 6** The plaintiff may provide evidence proving that the specific administrative act under prosecution is illegal. If the evidence provided by the plaintiff is not established, the defendant's burden of adducing evidence concerning the legality of the specific administrative act under prosecution shall not be relieved.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 原告或者第三人应当在开庭审理前或者人民法院指定的交换证据之日提供证据。因正当事由申请延期提供证据的，经人民法院准许，可以在法庭调查中提供。逾期提供证据的，视为放弃举证权利。  
原告或者第三人在第一审程序中无正当事由未提供而在第二审程序中提供的证据，人民法院不予接纳。

**Article 7** The plaintiff or a third party shall provide evidence before the court session or on the date designated by the People's Court for evidence exchange. If applying for an extension of time to provide evidence due to justifiable reasons, upon permission of the People's Court, the evidence may be provided during court investigation. If evidence is provided beyond the time limit, the right to adduce evidence shall be deemed waived.  
Where the plaintiff or a third party fails to provide evidence without justifiable reasons in the first instance proceedings but provides it in the second instance proceedings, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院向当事人送达受理案件通知书或者应诉通知书时，应当告知其举证范围、举证期限和逾期提供证据的法律后果，并告知因正当事由不能按期提供证据时应当提出延期提供证据的申请。

**Article 8** When the People's Court serves on a party the notice of acceptance of the case or the notice of responding to the action, it shall inform the party of the scope of adducing evidence, the time limit for adducing evidence, and the legal consequences of providing evidence beyond the time limit, and shall also inform the party that if it cannot provide evidence on time due to justifiable reasons, it shall file an application for an extension of time to provide evidence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-34)第一款的规定，人民法院有权要求当事人提供或者补充证据。  
对当事人无争议，但涉及国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实，人民法院可以责令当事人提供或者补充有关证据。  
二、提供证据的要求

**Article 9** Pursuant to the provision of Article 34, paragraph 1 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court has the authority to require the parties to provide or supplement evidence.  
For facts that are undisputed by the parties but involve national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court may order the parties to provide or supplement relevant evidence.  
II. Requirements for Providing Evidence

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（一）项的规定，当事人向人民法院提供书证的，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）提供书证的原件，原本、正本和副本均属于书证的原件。提供原件确有困难的，可以提供与原件核对无误的复印件、照片、节录本；  
（二）提供由有关部门保管的书证原件的复制件、影印件或者抄录件的，应当注明出处，经该部门核对无异后加盖其印章；  
（三）提供报表、图纸、会计帐册、专业技术资料、科技文献等书证的，应当附有说明材料；  
（四）被告提供的被诉具体行政行为所依据的询问、陈述、谈话类笔录，应当有行政执法人员、被询问人、陈述人、谈话人签名或者盖章。  
法律、法规、司法解释和规章对书证的制作形式另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 10** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (1) of the Administrative Procedure Law, where a party provides documentary evidence to the People's Court, it shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) The original of the documentary evidence shall be provided. The original manuscript, the certified copy, and the duplicate all constitute the original of the documentary evidence. If it is truly difficult to provide the original, a photocopy, photograph, or excerpt that has been verified to be identical with the original may be provided;  
(2) Where a reproduction, photocopy, or transcribed copy of the original documentary evidence kept by a relevant department is provided, the source shall be indicated, and after the department has verified that it is identical, its seal shall be affixed;  
(3) Where documentary evidence such as reports, drawings, accounting books, professional technical data, or scientific and technological documents is provided, explanatory materials shall be attached;  
(4) Records of inquiries, statements, or conversations relied upon by the specific administrative act under prosecution and provided by the defendant shall bear the signatures or seals of the administrative law enforcement personnel, the person inquired, the person making the statement, and the person having the conversation.  
Where laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, or rules have other provisions on the form of documentary evidence, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（二）项的规定，当事人向人民法院提供物证的，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）提供原物。提供原物确有困难的，可以提供与原物核对无误的复制件或者证明该物证的照片、录像等其他证据；  
（二）原物为数量较多的种类物的，提供其中的一部分。

**Article 11** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (2) of the Administrative Procedure Law, where a party provides physical evidence to the People's Court, it shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) The original object shall be provided. If it is truly difficult to provide the original object, a reproduction verified to be identical with the original object, or other evidence such as photographs or video recordings proving the physical evidence may be provided;  
(2) If the original object is a large quantity of fungible items, a portion thereof may be provided.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（三）项的规定，当事人向人民法院提供计算机数据或者录音、录像等视听资料的，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）提供有关资料的原始载体。提供原始载体确有困难的，可以提供复制件；  
（二）注明制作方法、制作时间、制作人和证明对象等；  
（三）声音资料应当附有该声音内容的文字记录。

**Article 12** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (3) of the Administrative Procedure Law, where a party provides audio-visual materials such as computer data, audio recordings, or video recordings to the People's Court, it shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) The original carrier of the relevant materials shall be provided. If it is truly difficult to provide the original carrier, a reproduction may be provided;  
(2) The method of production, time of production, producer, and object to be proved shall be specified;  
(3) Audio materials shall be accompanied by a written transcript of the audio content.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（四）项的规定，当事人向人民法院提供证人证言的，应当符合下列要求：  
（一）写明证人的姓名、年龄、性别、职业、住址等基本情况；  
（二）有证人的签名，不能签名的，应当以盖章等方式证明；  
（三）注明出具日期；  
（四）附有居民身份证复印件等证明证人身份的文件。

**Article 13** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (4) of the Administrative Procedure Law, where a party provides witness testimony to the People's Court, it shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) The basic information of the witness such as name, age, gender, occupation, and address shall be stated;  
(2) The witness shall affix his/her signature; if the witness cannot sign, it shall be proved by affixing a seal or other means;  
(3) The date of issuance shall be specified;  
(4) Documents proving the identity of the witness, such as a copy of the resident identity card, shall be attached.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（六）项的规定，被告向人民法院提供的在行政程序中采用的鉴定结论，应当载明委托人和委托鉴定的事项、向鉴定部门提交的相关材料、鉴定的依据和使用的科学技术手段、鉴定部门和鉴定人鉴定资格的说明，并应有鉴定人的签名和鉴定部门的盖章。通过分析获得的鉴定结论，应当说明分析过程。

**Article 14** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (6) of the Administrative Procedure Law, an expert evaluation conclusion adopted in the administrative procedure and provided by the defendant to the People's Court shall specify the client and the matters commissioned for evaluation, the relevant materials submitted to the evaluation department, the basis for evaluation and the scientific and technical means used, and a statement of the evaluation qualifications of the evaluation department and the evaluator, and shall bear the signature of the evaluator and the seal of the evaluation department. If the evaluation conclusion is obtained through analysis, the analysis process shall be explained.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-31)第一款第（七）项的规定，被告向人民法院提供的现场笔录，应当载明时间、地点和事件等内容，并由执法人员和当事人签名。当事人拒绝签名或者不能签名的，应当注明原因。有其他人在现场的，可由其他人签名。  
法律、法规和规章对现场笔录的制作形式另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 15** Pursuant to the provision of Article 31, paragraph 1, subparagraph (7) of the Administrative Procedure Law, a record of an on-site inspection provided by the defendant to the People's Court shall specify the time, place, and event, and shall be signed by the law enforcement personnel and the party. If a party refuses to sign or cannot sign, the reason shall be indicated. If there are other persons present at the scene, the record may be signed by other persons.  
Where laws, regulations, or rules have other provisions on the form of making records of on-site inspections, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 当事人向人民法院提供的在中华人民共和国领域外形成的证据，应当说明来源，经所在国公证机关证明，并经中华人民共和国驻该国使领馆认证，或者履行中华人民共和国与证据所在国订立的有关条约中规定的证明手续。  
当事人提供的在中华人民共和国香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区内形成的证据，应当具有按照有关规定办理的证明手续。

**Article 16** Evidence formed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China and provided by a party to the People's Court shall indicate the source, be notarized by a notarial authority in the country where it was formed, and be authenticated by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country, or the certification formalities prescribed in the relevant treaty concluded between the People's Republic of China and the country where the evidence is located shall be fulfilled.  
Evidence formed in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, or Taiwan region of the People's Republic of China and provided by a party shall bear certification formalities handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 当事人向人民法院提供外文书证或者外国语视听资料的，应当附有由具有翻译资质的机构翻译的或者其他翻译准确的中文译本，由翻译机构盖章或者翻译人员签名。

**Article 17** Where a party provides documentary evidence in a foreign language or audio-visual materials in a foreign language to the People's Court, it shall attach a Chinese translation translated by an institution with translation qualifications or otherwise accurately translated, and the translation institution shall affix its seal or the translator shall sign.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 证据涉及国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的，提供人应当作出明确标注，并向法庭说明，法庭予以审查确认。

**Article 18** Where evidence involves State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy, the provider shall clearly mark it and explain it to the court, and the court shall examine and confirm it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 当事人应当对其提交的证据材料分类编号，对证据材料的来源、证明对象和内容作简要说明，签名或者盖章，注明提交日期。

**Article 19** A party shall classify and number the evidential materials it submits, give a brief explanation of the source, object to be proved, and content of the evidential materials, affix its signature or seal, and specify the date of submission.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 人民法院收到当事人提交的证据材料，应当出具收据，注明证据的名称、份数、页数、件数、种类等以及收到的时间，由经办人员签名或者盖章。

**Article 20** Upon receiving evidential materials submitted by a party, the People's Court shall issue a receipt specifying the name, number of copies, number of pages, number of items, type, and time of receipt of the evidence, and the handling personnel shall affix their signatures or seals.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 对于案情比较复杂或者证据数量较多的案件，人民法院可以组织当事人在开庭前向对方出示或者交换证据，并将交换证据的情况记录在卷。  
三、调取和保全证据

**Article 21** For cases that are relatively complicated in facts or involve a large amount of evidence, the People's Court may organize the parties to present or exchange evidence with the opposing party before the court session, and shall record the circumstances of the evidence exchange in the case file.  
III. Obtaining and Preserving Evidence

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 根据[行政诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-34)第二款的规定，有下列情形之一的，人民法院有权向有关行政机关以及其他组织、公民调取证据：  
（一）涉及国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实认定的；  
（二）涉及依职权追加当事人、中止诉讼、终结诉讼、回避等程序性事项的。

**Article 22** Pursuant to the provision of Article 34, paragraph 2 of the Administrative Procedure Law, under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court has the authority to obtain evidence from relevant administrative organs, other organizations, or citizens:  
(1) It involves the determination of facts concerning national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others; or  
(2) It involves procedural matters such as the ex officio addition of parties, suspension of the action, termination of the action, or withdrawal.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 原告或者第三人不能自行收集，但能够提供确切线索的，可以申请人民法院调取下列证据材料：  
（一）由国家有关部门保存而须由人民法院调取的证据材料；  
（二）涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的证据材料；  
（三）确因客观原因不能自行收集的其他证据材料。  
人民法院不得为证明被诉具体行政行为的合法性，调取被告在作出具体行政行为时未收集的证据。

**Article 23** Where the plaintiff or a third party cannot collect evidence on its own but can provide definite leads, it may apply to the People's Court for obtaining the following evidential materials:  
(1) Evidential materials kept by relevant State authorities that need to be obtained by the People's Court;  
(2) Evidential materials involving State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy; or  
(3) Other evidential materials that truly cannot be collected on its own due to objective reasons.  
The People's Court shall not, for the purpose of proving the legality of the specific administrative act under prosecution, obtain evidence that the defendant did not collect when performing the specific administrative act.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 当事人申请人民法院调取证据的，应当在举证期限内提交调取证据申请书。  
调取证据申请书应当写明下列内容：  
（一）证据持有人的姓名或者名称、住址等基本情况；  
（二）拟调取证据的内容；  
（三）申请调取证据的原因及其要证明的案件事实。

**Article 24** Where a party applies to the People's Court for obtaining evidence, it shall submit a written application for obtaining evidence within the time limit for adducing evidence.  
The written application for obtaining evidence shall state the following:  
(1) The name and address and other basic information of the holder of the evidence;  
(2) The content of the evidence to be obtained; and  
(3) The reasons for applying to obtain the evidence and the facts of the case to be proved.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 人民法院对当事人调取证据的申请，经审查符合调取证据条件的，应当及时决定调取；不符合调取证据条件的，应当向当事人或者其诉讼代理人送达通知书，说明不准许调取的理由。当事人及其诉讼代理人可以在收到通知书之日起三日内向受理申请的人民法院书面申请复议一次。人民法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出答复。  
人民法院根据当事人申请，经调取未能取得相应证据的，应当告知申请人并说明原因。

**Article 25** Where the People's Court, after examining a party's application for obtaining evidence, finds that it meets the conditions for obtaining evidence, it shall promptly decide to obtain the evidence; if it does not meet the conditions, it shall serve a notice on the party or its litigation agent, stating the reasons for not permitting the obtaining. The party and its litigation agent may, within three days from the date of receipt of the notice, file one written application for reconsideration with the People's Court that accepted the application. The People's Court shall make a reply within five days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.  
Where the People's Court, after obtaining evidence pursuant to a party's application, fails to obtain the corresponding evidence, it shall inform the applicant and explain the reasons.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 人民法院需要调取的证据在异地的，可以书面委托证据所在地人民法院调取。受托人民法院应当在收到委托书后，按照委托要求及时完成调取证据工作，送交委托人民法院。受托人民法院不能完成委托内容的，应当告知委托的人民法院并说明原因。

**Article 26** Where the evidence that the People's Court needs to obtain is located in another place, it may entrust in writing the People's Court in the place where the evidence is located to obtain the evidence. The entrusted People's Court shall, after receiving the letter of entrustment, promptly complete the work of obtaining evidence in accordance with the requirements of the entrustment and deliver it to the entrusting People's Court. If the entrusted People's Court cannot complete the entrusted task, it shall inform the entrusting People's Court and explain the reasons.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 当事人根据[行政诉讼法第三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-36)的规定向人民法院申请保全证据的，应当在举证期限届满前以书面形式提出，并说明证据的名称和地点、保全的内容和范围、申请保全的理由等事项。  
当事人申请保全证据的，人民法院可以要求其提供相应的担保。  
法律、司法解释规定诉前保全证据的，依照其规定办理。

**Article 27** Where a party applies to the People's Court for preservation of evidence pursuant to Article 36 of the Administrative Procedure Law, it shall submit the application in writing before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence, and shall state the name and location of the evidence, the content and scope of preservation, and the reasons for applying for preservation.  
Where a party applies for preservation of evidence, the People's Court may require it to provide corresponding security.  
If laws or judicial interpretations provide for pre-litigation preservation of evidence, such provisions shall be followed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 人民法院依照[行政诉讼法第三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-36)规定保全证据的，可以根据具体情况，采取查封、扣押、拍照、录音、录像、复制、鉴定、勘验、制作询问笔录等保全措施。  
人民法院保全证据时，可以要求当事人或者其诉讼代理人到场。

**Article 28** Where the People's Court preserves evidence in accordance with Article 36 of the Administrative Procedure Law, it may, based on the specific circumstances, take preservation measures such as sealing up, distraining, photographing, audio recording, video recording, copying, evaluation, inspection, or making records of inquiries.  
When the People's Court preserves evidence, it may require the party or its litigation agent to be present.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 原告或者第三人有证据或者有正当理由表明被告据以认定案件事实的鉴定结论可能有错误，在举证期限内书面申请重新鉴定的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 29** Where the plaintiff or a third party has evidence or justifiable reasons indicating that the evaluation conclusion on which the defendant bases its determination of the case facts may be erroneous, and submits a written application for re-evaluation within the time limit for adducing evidence, the People's Court shall grant permission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 当事人对人民法院委托的鉴定部门作出的鉴定结论有异议申请重新鉴定，提出证据证明存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应予准许：  
（一）鉴定部门或者鉴定人不具有相应的鉴定资格的；  
（二）鉴定程序严重违法的；  
（三）鉴定结论明显依据不足的；  
（四）经过质证不能作为证据使用的其他情形。  
对有缺陷的鉴定结论，可以通过补充鉴定、重新质证或者补充质证等方式解决。

**Article 30** Where a party challenges an evaluation conclusion made by an evaluation department entrusted by the People's Court and applies for re-evaluation, and provides evidence proving the existence of any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant permission:  
(1) The evaluation department or the evaluator does not have the corresponding evaluation qualifications;  
(2) The evaluation procedure is seriously illegal;  
(3) The evaluation conclusion is clearly insufficient in its basis; or  
(4) Other circumstances under which the evaluation conclusion cannot be used as evidence after cross-examination.  
For a defective evaluation conclusion, it may be resolved by means of supplementary evaluation, renewed cross-examination, or supplementary cross-examination.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 对需要鉴定的事项负有举证责任的当事人，在举证期限内无正当理由不提出鉴定申请、不预交鉴定费用或者拒不提供相关材料，致使对案件争议的事实无法通过鉴定结论予以认定的，应当对该事实承担举证不能的法律后果。

**Article 31** Where a party bearing the burden of adducing evidence for a matter requiring evaluation fails, within the time limit for adducing evidence and without justifiable reasons, to file an application for evaluation, to prepay the evaluation fees, or to provide relevant materials, thereby rendering it impossible to determine the disputed facts of the case through an evaluation conclusion, the party shall bear the legal consequences of being unable to adduce evidence for such facts.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 人民法院对委托或者指定的鉴定部门出具的鉴定书，应当审查是否具有下列内容：  
（一）鉴定的内容；  
（二）鉴定时提交的相关材料；  
（三）鉴定的依据和使用的科学技术手段；  
（四）鉴定的过程；  
（五）明确的鉴定结论；  
（六）鉴定部门和鉴定人鉴定资格的说明；  
（七）鉴定人及鉴定部门签名盖章。  
前款内容欠缺或者鉴定结论不明确的，人民法院可以要求鉴定部门予以说明、补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。

**Article 32** The People's Court shall examine whether the evaluation report issued by an evaluation department entrusted or designated by it contains the following:  
(1) The content of the evaluation;  
(2) The relevant materials submitted at the time of evaluation;  
(3) The basis for evaluation and the scientific and technical means used;  
(4) The evaluation process;  
(5) A clear evaluation conclusion;  
(6) A statement of the evaluation qualifications of the evaluation department and the evaluator; and  
(7) The signatures and seals of the evaluator and the evaluation department.  
If the content mentioned in the preceding paragraph is incomplete or the evaluation conclusion is unclear, the People's Court may require the evaluation department to provide explanations, make supplementary evaluation, or conduct a new evaluation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 人民法院可以依当事人申请或者依职权勘验现场。  
勘验现场时，勘验人必须出示人民法院的证件，并邀请当地基层组织或者当事人所在单位派人参加。当事人或其成年亲属应当到场，拒不到场的，不影响勘验的进行，但应当在勘验笔录中说明情况。

**Article 33** The People's Court may inspect the scene upon a party's application or ex officio.  
When inspecting the scene, the inspector must present the credentials of the People's Court and shall invite the local grassroots organization or the unit where the party works to send persons to participate. The party or its adult relatives shall be present; if they refuse to be present, it shall not affect the conduct of the inspection, but the situation shall be noted in the inspection record.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 审判人员应当制作勘验笔录，记载勘验的时间、地点、勘验人、在场人、勘验的经过和结果，由勘验人、当事人、在场人签名。  
勘验现场时绘制的现场图，应当注明绘制的时间、方位、绘制人姓名和身份等内容。  
当事人对勘验结论有异议的，可以在举证期限内申请重新勘验，是否准许由人民法院决定。  
四、证据的对质辨认和核实

**Article 34** The judicial personnel shall make an inspection record, recording the time, place, inspector, persons present, and the process and results of the inspection, which shall be signed by the inspector, the party, and the persons present.  
A scene diagram drawn during the inspection of the scene shall specify the time, orientation, and name and identity of the drafter.  
If a party objects to the inspection conclusion, it may apply for a re-inspection within the time limit for adducing evidence; whether to permit it shall be decided by the People's Court.  
IV. Confrontation, Identification, and Verification of Evidence

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 证据应当在法庭上出示，并经庭审质证。未经庭审质证的证据，不能作为定案的依据。  
当事人在庭前证据交换过程中没有争议并记录在卷的证据，经审判人员在庭审中说明后，可以作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 35** Evidence shall be presented in court and cross-examined during the trial. Evidence that has not been cross-examined during the trial shall not be used as the basis for deciding the case.  
Evidence that is undisputed by the parties during the pre-trial evidence exchange and recorded in the case file may, after being explained by the judicial personnel during the trial, be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 经合法传唤，因被告无正当理由拒不到庭而需要依法缺席判决的，被告提供的证据不能作为定案的依据，但当事人在庭前交换证据中没有争议的证据除外。

**Article 36** Where, after lawful summons, a defendant refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, and a default judgment needs to be rendered according to law, the evidence provided by the defendant shall not be used as the basis for deciding the case, except for evidence that is undisputed by the parties during the pre-trial evidence exchange.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 涉及国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私或者法律规定的其他应当保密的证据，不得在开庭时公开质证。

**Article 37** Evidence involving State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or other evidence that shall be kept confidential as provided by law shall not be publicly cross-examined during the court session.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 当事人申请人民法院调取的证据，由申请调取证据的当事人在庭审中出示，并由当事人质证。  
人民法院依职权调取的证据，由法庭出示，并可就调取该证据的情况进行说明，听取当事人意见。

**Article 38** Evidence obtained by the People's Court upon a party's application shall be presented in court by the party that applied for obtaining the evidence, and shall be cross-examined by the parties.  
Evidence obtained by the People's Court ex officio shall be presented by the court, and the court may explain the circumstances under which the evidence was obtained and hear the opinions of the parties.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 当事人应当围绕证据的关联性、合法性和真实性，针对证据有无证明效力以及证明效力大小，进行质证。  
经法庭准许，当事人及其代理人可以就证据问题相互发问，也可以向证人、鉴定人或者勘验人发问。  
当事人及其代理人相互发问，或者向证人、鉴定人、勘验人发问时，发问的内容应当与案件事实有关联，不得采用引诱、威胁、侮辱等语言或者方式。

**Article 39** The parties shall cross-examine evidence with respect to its relevance, legality, and authenticity, and on whether the evidence has probative value and the extent of such probative value.  
With the permission of the court, the parties and their agents may question each other on evidentiary issues, and may also question witnesses, evaluators, or inspectors.  
When the parties and their agents question each other or question witnesses, evaluators, or inspectors, the content of the questions shall be relevant to the facts of the case, and language or methods such as inducement, threats, or insults shall not be used.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 对书证、物证和视听资料进行质证时，当事人应当出示证据的原件或者原物。但有下列情况之一的除外：  
（一）出示原件或者原物确有困难并经法庭准许可以出示复制件或者复制品；  
（二）原件或者原物已不存在，可以出示证明复制件、复制品与原件、原物一致的其他证据。  
视听资料应当当庭播放或者显示，并由当事人进行质证。

**Article 40** When cross-examining documentary evidence, physical evidence, and audio-visual materials, the parties shall present the original of the evidence or the original object, except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) If it is truly difficult to present the original or the original object and the court permits, a reproduction or replica may be presented;  
(2) If the original or the original object no longer exists, other evidence proving that the reproduction or replica is consistent with the original or original object may be presented.  
Audio-visual materials shall be played or displayed in court and cross-examined by the parties.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 凡是知道案件事实的人，都有出庭作证的义务。有下列情形之一的，经人民法院准许。当事人可以提交书面证言：  
（一）当事人在行政程序或者庭前证据交换中对证人证言无异议的；  
（二）证人因年迈体弱或者行动不便无法出庭的；  
（三）证人因路途遥远、交通不便无法出庭的；  
（四）证人因自然灾害等不可抗力或者其他意外事件无法出庭的；  
（五）证人因其他特殊原因确实无法出庭的。

**Article 41** Any person who knows the facts of a case has the obligation to testify in court. Under any of the following circumstances, with the permission of the People's Court, a party may submit a written testimony:  
(1) The parties have no objection to the witness testimony during the administrative procedure or the pre-trial evidence exchange;  
(2) The witness is unable to appear in court due to old age, infirmity, or mobility difficulties;  
(3) The witness is unable to appear in court due to long distance or inconvenient transportation;  
(4) The witness is unable to appear in court due to force majeure such as natural disasters or other unexpected events; or  
(5) The witness is truly unable to appear in court due to other special reasons.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 不能正确表达意志的人不能作证。  
根据当事人申请，人民法院可以就证人能否正确表达意志进行审查或者交由有关部门鉴定。必要时，人民法院也可以依职权交由有关部门鉴定。

**Article 42** A person who cannot correctly express his/her will shall not testify.  
Upon a party's application, the People's Court may examine whether a witness can correctly express his/her will or assign the matter to a relevant department for evaluation. When necessary, the People's Court may also ex officio assign the matter to a relevant department for evaluation.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条 当事人申请证人出庭作证的，应当在举证期限届满前提出，并经人民法院许可。人民法院准许证人出庭作证的，应当在开庭审理前通知证人出庭作证。  
当事人在庭审过程中要求证人出庭作证的，法庭可以根据审理案件的具体情况，决定是否准许以及是否延期审理。

**Article 43** Where a party applies for a witness to testify in court, the application shall be made before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence and shall be subject to the permission of the People's Court. Where the People's Court permits a witness to testify in court, it shall notify the witness to testify in court before the court session.  
If a party requests a witness to testify in court during the trial, the court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, decide whether to permit it and whether to adjourn the trial.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条 有下列情形之一，原告或者第三人可以要求相关行政执法人员作为证人出庭作证：  
（一）对现场笔录的合法性或者真实性有异议的；  
（二）对扣押财产的品种或者数量有异议的；  
（三）对检验的物品取样或者保管有异议的；  
（四）对行政执法人员的身份的合法性有异议的；  
（五）需要出庭作证的其他情形。

**Article 44** Under any of the following circumstances, the plaintiff or a third party may request the relevant administrative law enforcement personnel to testify in court as witnesses:  
(1) There is an objection to the legality or authenticity of the record of on-site inspection;  
(2) There is an objection to the type or quantity of the property seized;  
(3) There is an objection to the sampling or storage of the inspected items;  
(4) There is an objection to the legality of the identity of the administrative law enforcement personnel; or  
(5) Other circumstances requiring testimony in court.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条 证人出庭作证时，应当出示证明其身份的证件。法庭应当告知其诚实作证的法律义务和作伪证的法律责任。  
出庭作证的证人不得旁听案件的审理。法庭询问证人时，其他证人不得在场，但组织证人对质的除外。

**Article 45** When a witness testifies in court, he/she shall present identification documents. The court shall inform the witness of the legal obligation to testify truthfully and the legal liability for giving false testimony.  
A witness testifying in court shall not attend the hearing of the case. When the court examines a witness, other witnesses shall not be present, except when the court organizes confrontation among witnesses.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条 证人应当陈述其亲历的具体事实。证人根据其经历所作的判断、推测或者评论，不能作为定案的依据。

**Article 46** A witness shall state the specific facts he/she personally experienced. Judgments, inferences, or comments made by a witness based on his/her experience shall not be used as the basis for deciding the case.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条 当事人要求鉴定人出庭接受询问的，鉴定人应当出庭。鉴定人因正当事由不能出庭的，经法庭准许，可以不出庭，由当事人对其书面鉴定结论进行质证。  
鉴定人不能出庭的正当事由，参照[本规定第四十一条](#art-41)的规定。  
对于出庭接受询问的鉴定人，法庭应当核实其身份、与当事人及案件的关系，并告知鉴定人如实说明鉴定情况的法律义务和故意作虚假说明的法律责任。

**Article 47** Where a party requests an evaluator to appear in court to be questioned, the evaluator shall appear in court. If the evaluator cannot appear in court due to justifiable reasons, with the permission of the court, he/she may not appear in court, and the parties shall cross-examine his/her written evaluation conclusion.  
The justifiable reasons for an evaluator's inability to appear in court shall be governed by mutatis mutandis the provisions of Article 41 of these Provisions.  
For an evaluator appearing in court to be questioned, the court shall verify his/her identity and relationship with the parties and the case, and shall inform the evaluator of the legal obligation to truthfully explain the evaluation situation and the legal liability for knowingly making false explanations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条 对被诉具体行政行为涉及的专门性问题，当事人可以向法庭申请由专业人员出庭进行说明，法庭也可以通知专业人员出庭说明。必要时，法庭可以组织专业人员进行对质。  
当事人对出庭的专业人员是否具备相应专业知识、学历、资历等专业资格等有异议的，可以进行询问。由法庭决定其是否可以作为专业人员出庭。  
专业人员可以对鉴定人进行询问。

**Article 48** For specialized issues involved in the specific administrative act under prosecution, a party may apply to the court for a professional to appear in court to explain, and the court may also notify a professional to appear in court to explain. When necessary, the court may organize confrontation among professionals.  
If a party objects to whether the professional appearing in court possesses corresponding professional knowledge, educational background, qualifications, or other professional credentials, it may question the professional. The court shall decide whether the person may appear in court as a professional.  
A professional may question the evaluator.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条 法庭在质证过程中，对与案件没有关联的证据材料，应予排除并说明理由。  
法庭在质证过程中，准许当事人补充证据的，对补充的证据仍应进行质证。  
法庭对经过庭审质证的证据，除确有必要外，一般不再进行质证。

**Article 49** During the process of cross-examination, the court shall exclude and state the reasons for any evidence materials that are not relevant to the case.  
During the process of cross-examination, if the court permits a party to supplement evidence, the supplementary evidence shall still be subject to cross-examination.  
Except where truly necessary, the court shall generally not conduct further cross-examination of evidence that has undergone cross-examination during trial.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条 在第二审程序中，对当事人依法提供的新的证据，法庭应当进行质证；当事人对第一审认定的证据仍有争议的，法庭也应当进行质证。

**Article 50** In the second instance proceedings, the court shall conduct cross-examination of new evidence provided by a party according to law; if a party still disputes the evidence admitted in the first instance, the court shall also conduct cross-examination.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条 按照审判监督程序审理的案件，对当事人依法提供的新的证据，法庭应当进行质证；因原判决、裁定认定事实的证据不足而提起再审所涉及的主要证据，法庭也应当进行质证。

**Article 51** In cases tried under the procedure for trial supervision, the court shall conduct cross-examination of new evidence provided by a party according to law; the court shall also conduct cross-examination of the main evidence involved in the retrial initiated because the evidence for the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling was insufficient.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条 [本规定第五十条](#art-50)和第五十一条中的“新的证据”是指以下证据：  
（一）在一审程序中应当准予延期提供而未获准许的证据；  
（二）当事人在一审程序中依法申请调取而未获准许或者未取得，人民法院在第二审程序中调取的证据；  
（三）原告或者第三人提供的在举证期限届满后发现的证据。  
五、证据的审核认定

**Article 52** The term "new evidence" in Articles 50 and 51 of these Provisions refers to the following evidence:  
(1) evidence for which an extension for provision should have been granted in the first instance proceedings but was not granted;  
(2) evidence that a party applied for collection according to law in the first instance proceedings but was not granted or obtained, and that was collected by the People's Court in the second instance proceedings;  
(3) evidence provided by the plaintiff or a third party that was discovered after the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence.  
V. Examination and Determination of Evidence

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条 人民法院裁判行政案件，应当以证据证明的案件事实为依据。

**Article 53** In adjudicating administrative cases, the People's Court shall take the facts of the case proved by evidence as the basis.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条 法庭应当对经过庭审质证的证据和无需质证的证据进行逐一审查和对全部证据综合审查，遵循法官职业道德，运用逻辑推理和生活经验，进行全面、客观和公正地分析判断，确定证据材料与案件事实之间的证明关系，排除不具有关联性的证据材料，准确认定案件事实。

**Article 54** The court shall conduct an individual examination of evidence that has undergone cross-examination and evidence that does not require cross-examination, and a comprehensive examination of all evidence; shall abide by judicial ethics, apply logical reasoning and life experience, conduct a comprehensive, objective, and fair analysis and judgment, determine the probative relationship between the evidence materials and the facts of the case, exclude evidence materials that are not relevant, and accurately determine the facts of the case.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条 法庭应当根据案件的具体情况，从以下方面审查证据的合法性：  
（一）证据是否符合法定形式；  
（二）证据的取得是否符合法律、法规、司法解释和规章的要求；  
（三）是否有影响证据效力的其他违法情形。

**Article 55** The court shall examine the legality of evidence in light of the specific circumstances of the case from the following aspects:  
(1) whether the evidence conforms to the statutory form;  
(2) whether the obtaining of evidence conforms to the requirements of laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, and rules;  
(3) whether there are other illegal circumstances affecting the validity of evidence.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条 法庭应当根据案件的具体情况，从以下方面审查证据的真实性：  
（一）证据形成的原因；  
（二）发现证据时的客观环境；  
（三）证据是否为原件、原物，复制件、复制品与原件、原物是否相符；  
（四）提供证据的人或者证人与当事人是否具有利害关系；  
（五）影响证据真实性的其他因素。

**Article 56** The court shall examine the authenticity of evidence in light of the specific circumstances of the case from the following aspects:  
(1) the reason for the formation of the evidence;  
(2) the objective environment when the evidence was discovered;  
(3) whether the evidence is an original or the original object, and whether the copies or reproductions are consistent with the original or original object;  
(4) whether the person providing the evidence or the witness has an interest in the party;  
(5) other factors affecting the authenticity of the evidence.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条 下列证据材料不能作为定案依据：  
（一）严重违反法定程序收集的证据材料；  
（二）以偷拍、偷录、窃听等手段获取侵害他人合法权益的证据材料；  
（三）以利诱、欺诈、胁迫、暴力等不正当手段获取的证据材料；  
（四）当事人无正当事由超出举证期限提供的证据材料；  
（五）在中华人民共和国领域以外或者在中华人民共和国香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区形成的未办理法定证明手续的证据材料；  
（六）当事人无正当理由拒不提供原件、原物，又无其他证据印证，且对方当事人不予认可的证据的复制件或者复制品；  
（七）被当事人或者他人进行技术处理而无法辨明真伪的证据材料；  
（八）不能正确表达意志的证人提供的证言；  
（九）不具备合法性和真实性的其他证据材料。

**Article 57** The following evidence materials shall not be used as the basis for deciding a case:  
(1) evidence materials collected in serious violation of statutory procedures;  
(2) evidence materials obtained by means such as surreptitious photography, surreptitious recording, or wiretapping that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(3) evidence materials obtained by improper means such as inducement, fraud, coercion, or violence;  
(4) evidence materials provided by a party beyond the time limit for adducing evidence without legitimate reasons;  
(5) evidence materials formed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China or in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region of the People's Republic of China that have not undergone statutory certification procedures;  
(6) copies or reproductions of evidence that the party refuses to provide the original or original object without justifiable reasons, and there is no other evidence to corroborate, and the opposing party does not accept;  
(7) evidence materials that have been technically processed by a party or another person and cannot be distinguished as genuine or false;  
(8) testimony given by a witness who cannot correctly express intent;  
(9) other evidence materials that lack legality and authenticity.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条 以违反法律禁止性规定或者侵犯他人合法权益的方法取得的证据，不能作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 58** Evidence obtained by methods that violate prohibitive provisions of law or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others shall not be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条 被告在行政程序中依照法定程序要求原告提供证据，原告依法应当提供而拒不提供，在诉讼程序中提供的证据，人民法院一般不予采纳。

**Article 59** Where the defendant requests the plaintiff to provide evidence in accordance with statutory procedures during the administrative process, and the plaintiff, who should have provided evidence according to law, refuses to provide it, and then provides the evidence during the litigation proceedings, the People's Court shall generally not adopt such evidence.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条 下列证据不能作为认定被诉具体行政行为合法的依据：  
（一）被告及其诉讼代理人在作出具体行政行为后或者在诉讼程序中自行收集的证据；  
（二）被告在行政程序中非法剥夺公民、法人或者其他组织依法享有的陈述、申辩或者听证权利所采用的证据；  
（三）原告或者第三人在诉讼程序中提供的、被告在行政程序中未作为具体行政行为依据的证据。

**Article 60** The following evidence shall not be used as the basis for determining the legality of the specific administrative act under challenge:  
(1) evidence collected by the defendant or its litigation agent after making the specific administrative act or during the litigation proceedings;  
(2) evidence adopted by the defendant during the administrative process by illegally depriving a citizen, legal person, or other organization of the right to make statements, to defend, or to a hearing that they are entitled to according to law;  
(3) evidence that is provided by the plaintiff or a third party during the litigation proceedings that the defendant did not use as the basis for the specific administrative act during the administrative process.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条 复议机关在复议程序中收集和补充的证据，或者作出原具体行政行为的行政机关在复议程序中未向复议机关提交的证据，不能作为人民法院认定原具体行政行为合法的依据。

**Article 61** Evidence collected and supplemented by the reconsideration authority during the reconsideration process, or evidence that the administrative agency that made the original specific administrative act did not submit to the reconsideration authority during the reconsideration process, shall not be used as the basis for the People's Court to determine the legality of the original specific administrative act.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条 对被告在行政程序中采纳的鉴定结论，原告或者第三人提出证据证明有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予采纳：  
（一）鉴定人不具备鉴定资格；  
（二）鉴定程序严重违法；  
（三）鉴定结论错误、不明确或者内容不完整。

**Article 62** With respect to expert evaluation conclusions adopted by the defendant during the administrative process, if the plaintiff or a third party provides evidence proving that any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall not adopt them:  
(1) the expert lacks the qualifications for expert evaluation;  
(2) the expert evaluation procedure is seriously illegal;  
(3) the expert evaluation conclusion is erroneous, unclear, or incomplete in content.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条 证明同一事实的数个证据，其证明效力一般可以按照下列情形分别认定：  
（一）国家机关以及其他职能部门依职权制作的公文文书优于其他书证；  
（二）鉴定结论、现场笔录、勘验笔录、档案材料以及经过公证或者登记的书证优于其他书证、视听资料和证人证言；  
（三）原件、原物优于复制件、复制品；  
（四）法定鉴定部门的鉴定结论优于其他鉴定部门的鉴定结论；  
（五）法庭主持勘验所制作的勘验笔录优于其他部门主持勘验所制作的勘验笔录；  
（六）原始证据优于传来证据；  
（七）其他证人证言优于与当事人有亲属关系或者其他密切关系的证人提供的对该当事人有利的证言；  
（八）出庭作证的证人证言优于未出庭作证的证人证言；  
（九）数个种类不同、内容一致的证据优于一个孤立的证据。

**Article 63** For several pieces of evidence proving the same fact, their probative weight may generally be determined respectively according to the following circumstances:  
(1) official documents made by state organs and other functional departments in the exercise of their functions and powers shall prevail over other documentary evidence;  
(2) expert evaluation conclusions, on-site records, inspection records, archival materials, and documentary evidence that has been notarized or registered shall prevail over other documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and witness testimony;  
(3) originals and original objects shall prevail over copies and reproductions;  
(4) expert evaluation conclusions of statutory expert evaluation departments shall prevail over expert evaluation conclusions of other expert evaluation departments;  
(5) inspection records made by the court in conducting inspection shall prevail over inspection records made by other departments in conducting inspection;  
(6) original evidence shall prevail over derivative evidence;  
(7) other witness testimony shall prevail over testimony favorable to a party given by a witness who has a familial relationship or other close relationship with that party;  
(8) witness testimony given in court shall prevail over witness testimony given without appearing in court;  
(9) several pieces of evidence of different types with consistent content shall prevail over a single piece of isolated evidence.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条 以有形载体固定或者显示的电子数据交换、电子邮件以及其他数据资料，其制作情况和真实性经对方当事人确认，或者以公证等其他有效方式予以证明的，与原件具有同等的证明效力。

**Article 64** Electronic data interchange, electronic mail, and other data materials that are fixed or displayed on a tangible carrier, if their production and authenticity are confirmed by the opposing party, or are proved by notarization or other effective means, shall have the same probative weight as originals.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条 在庭审中一方当事人或者其代理人在代理权限范围内对另一方当事人陈述的案件事实明确表示认可的，人民法院可以对该事实予以认定。但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 65** If during trial one party or its agent, within the scope of the agency authority, explicitly recognizes the facts of the case stated by the other party, the People's Court may admit such facts. This does not apply if there is sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条 在行政赔偿诉讼中，人民法院主持调解时当事人为达成调解协议而对案件事实的认可，不得在其后的诉讼中作为对其不利的证据。

**Article 66** In administrative compensation litigation, when the People's Court conducts mediation, the recognition by a party of the facts of the case for the purpose of reaching a mediation agreement shall not be used as evidence adverse to that party in subsequent litigation.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条 在不受外力影响的情况下，一方当事人提供的证据，对方当事人明确表示认可的，可以认定该证据的证明效力；对方当事人予以否认，但不能提供充分的证据进行反驳的，可以综合全案情况审查认定该证据的证明效力。

**Article 67** Where there is no external influence, if the opposing party explicitly recognizes the evidence provided by one party, the probative weight of such evidence may be determined; if the opposing party denies it but cannot provide sufficient evidence to rebut, the probative weight of the evidence may be examined and determined in light of the overall circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条 下列事实法庭可以直接认定：  
（一）众所周知的事实；  
（二）自然规律及定理；  
（三）按照法律规定推定的事实；  
（四）已经依法证明的事实；  
（五）根据日常生活经验法则推定的事实。  
前款（一）、（三）、（四）、（五）项，当事人有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 68** The court may directly determine the following facts:  
(1) facts that are well known;  
(2) natural laws and theorems;  
(3) facts presumed in accordance with legal provisions;  
(4) facts that have been proved according to law;  
(5) facts presumed according to the rules of daily life experience.  
For items (1), (3), (4), and (5) of the preceding paragraph, this does not apply if the party has sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条 原告确有证据证明被告持有的证据对原告有利，被告无正当事由拒不提供的，可以推定原告的主张成立。

**Article 69** If the plaintiff has evidence proving that the evidence held by the defendant is favorable to the plaintiff, and the defendant refuses to provide it without justifiable reasons, it may be presumed that the plaintiff's claim is established.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条 生效的人民法院裁判文书或者仲裁机构裁决文书确认的事实，可以作为定案依据。但是如果发现裁判文书或者裁决文书认定的事实有重大问题的，应当中止诉讼，通过法定程序予以纠正后恢复诉讼。

**Article 70** Facts confirmed by effective judgment documents of the People's Court or award documents of arbitration institutions may be used as the basis for deciding a case. However, if it is found that there is a major problem with the facts determined in the judgment document or award document, the litigation shall be suspended, and after correction through statutory procedures, the litigation shall be resumed.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条 下列证据不能单独作为定案依据：  
（一）未成年人所作的与其年龄和智力状况不相适应的证言；  
（二）与一方当事人有亲属关系或者其他密切关系的证人所作的对该当事人有利的证言，或者与一方当事人有不利关系的证人所作的对该当事人不利的证言；  
（三）应当出庭作证而无正当理由不出庭作证的证人证言；  
（四）难以识别是否经过修改的视听资料；  
（五）无法与原件、原物核对的复制件或者复制品；  
（六）经一方当事人或者他人改动，对方当事人不予认可的证据材料；  
（七）其他不能单独作为定案依据的证据材料。

**Article 71** The following evidence shall not be used alone as the basis for deciding a case:  
(1) testimony given by a minor that is inconsistent with his age and intellectual capacity;  
(2) testimony favorable to a party given by a witness who has a familial relationship or other close relationship with that party, or testimony unfavorable to a party given by a witness who has an adverse relationship with that party;  
(3) witness testimony of a witness who should have appeared in court to testify but did not appear without justifiable reasons;  
(4) audio-visual materials that are difficult to determine whether they have been altered;  
(5) copies or reproductions that cannot be verified against the original or original object;  
(6) evidence materials that have been altered by a party or another person and are not accepted by the opposing party;  
(7) other evidence materials that cannot be used alone as the basis for deciding a case.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条 庭审中经过质证的证据，能够当庭认定的，应当当庭认定；不能当庭认定的，应当在合议庭合议时认定。  
人民法院应当在裁判文书中阐明证据是否采纳的理由。

**Article 72** Evidence that has undergone cross-examination during trial shall be determined at trial if it can be determined; if it cannot be determined at trial, it shall be determined during the deliberation of the collegial panel.  
The People's Court shall explain the reasons for admitting or not admitting evidence in the judgment document.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条 法庭发现当庭认定的证据有误，可以按照下列方式纠正：  
（一）庭审结束前发现错误的，应当重新进行认定；  
（二）庭审结束后宣判前发现错误的，在裁判文书中予以更正并说明理由，也可以再次开庭予以认定；  
（三）有新的证据材料可能推翻已认定的证据的，应当再次开庭予以认定。  
六、附则

**Article 73** If the court discovers an error in the evidence determined at trial, it may correct the error in the following manners:  
(1) if the error is discovered before the end of the trial, it shall make a new determination;  
(2) if the error is discovered after the end of the trial but before the announcement of judgment, it shall make a correction in the judgment document and state the reasons, and may also open a new trial to make a determination;  
(3) if there is new evidence material that may overturn the determined evidence, it shall open a new trial to make a determination.  
VI. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条 证人、鉴定人及其近亲属的人身和财产安全受法律保护。  
人民法院应当对证人、鉴定人的住址和联系方式予以保密。

**Article 74** The personal and property safety of witnesses, experts, and their close relatives shall be protected by law.  
The People's Court shall keep confidential the addresses and contact information of witnesses and experts.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条 证人、鉴定人因出庭作证或者接受询问而支出的合理费用，由提供证人、鉴定人的一方当事人先行支付，由败诉一方当事人承担。

**Article 75** The reasonable expenses incurred by witnesses and experts for appearing in court to testify or to be questioned shall be paid in advance by the party who provides the witness or expert, and shall be borne by the losing party.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条 证人、鉴定人作伪证的，依照[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第一款第（二）项的规定追究其法律责任。

**Article 76** If a witness or expert gives false testimony, their legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of Article 49, paragraph 1, subparagraph (2) of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条 诉讼参与人或者其他人有对审判人员或者证人、鉴定人、勘验人及其近亲属实施威胁、侮辱、殴打、骚扰或者打击报复等妨碍行政诉讼行为的，依照[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第一款第（三）项、第（五）项或者第（六）项的规定追究其法律责任。

**Article 77** If a litigation participant or another person commits acts that obstruct administrative litigation, such as threatening, insulting, beating, harassing, or retaliating against an adjudicator, witness, expert, inspector, or their close relatives, their legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of Article 49, paragraph 1, subparagraphs (3), (5), or (6) of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条 对应当协助调取证据的单位和个人，无正当理由拒不履行协助义务的，依照[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第一款第（五）项的规定追究其法律责任。

**Article 78** If a unit or individual that should assist in the collection of evidence refuses to perform the assistance obligation without justifiable reasons, their legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of Article 49, paragraph 1, subparagraph (5) of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条 本院以前有关行政诉讼的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 79** If previous judicial interpretations of this Court concerning administrative litigation are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条 本规定自2002年10月1日起施行。2002年10月1日尚未审结的一审、二审和再审行政案件不适用本规定。  
本规定施行前已经审结的行政案件，当事人以违反本规定为由申请再审的，人民法院不予支持。  
本规定施行后按照审判监督程序决定再审的行政案件，适用本规定。  
2002年7月24日

**Article 80** These Provisions shall come into force on October 1, 2002. These Provisions shall not apply to administrative cases of first instance, second instance, and retrial that have not been finally adjudicated by October 1, 2002.  
For administrative cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, if a party applies for retrial on the ground of violation of these Provisions, the People's Court shall not support such application.  
For administrative cases for which retrial is decided under the procedure for trial supervision after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall apply.
