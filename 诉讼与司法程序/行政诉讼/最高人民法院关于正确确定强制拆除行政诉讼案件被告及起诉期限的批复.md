### 最高人民法院关于正确确定强制拆除行政诉讼案件被告及起诉期限的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>中华人民共和国最高人民法院  
  
公　告  
  
《最高人民法院关于正确确定强制拆除行政诉讼案件被告及起诉期限的批复》已于2024年6月3日经最高人民法院审判委员会第1921次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年8月8日起施行。  
  
2024年8月7日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于正确确定强制拆除行政诉讼案件  
  
被告及起诉期限的批复  
  
法释〔2024〕8号  
  
宁夏回族自治区高级人民法院：  
  
你院关于如何理解[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-46)规定的“知道或者应当知道作出行政行为”与《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉的解释》第六十四条中“知道或者应当知道行政行为内容”等的请示收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
公民、法人或者其他组织对强制拆除其建筑物或者其他设施不服提起诉讼的，以作出强制拆除决定的行政机关为被告；没有强制拆除决定书的，以具体实施强制拆除行为的行政机关为被告；未收到强制拆除决定书，实施强制拆除行为的主体不明确的，可以以现有证据初步证明实施强制拆除行为的行政机关为被告。人民法院在案件审理过程中，认为原告起诉的被告不适格且能够确定适格被告的，应当告知原告变更被告；原告拒绝变更的，应当裁定驳回起诉。人民法院经审查各方当事人提供的证据或者依职权调查后，仍不能确定适格被告的，可以依据[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第六十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-66)的规定，视情将有关材料移送有关机关调查并裁定中止诉讼。  
  
[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-46)规定的六个月起诉期限与《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉的解释》第六十四条规定的一年起诉期限，应当从公民、法人或者其他组织知道或者应当知道行政行为内容且知道或者应当知道该行为实施主体之日起计算。被告主张原告自知道或者应当知道行政行为内容及实施主体之日起已经超过法定起诉期限的，应当承担举证责任。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** August 7, 2024  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Correctly Determining the Defendant and the Prosecution Period in Compulsory Demolition Administrative Litigation Cases  
Fa Shi [2024] No. 8  
Higher People's Court of Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region:  
Your request regarding how to understand the provisions of Article 46 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China concerning "knowing or should know that an administrative act has been taken" and Article 64 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China concerning "knowing or should know the content of an administrative act" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a citizen, legal person or other organization initiates an administrative action against the compulsory demolition of its building or other facility, the administrative authority that made the compulsory demolition decision shall be the defendant; if there is no written compulsory demolition decision, the administrative authority that actually carried out the compulsory demolition shall be the defendant; if no written compulsory demolition decision has been received and the subject that actually carried out the compulsory demolition is unclear, the administrative authority that existing evidence preliminarily proves as having carried out the compulsory demolition may be the defendant. In the course of hearing the case, if the People's Court finds that the defendant sued by the plaintiff is not the proper defendant and can determine the proper defendant, it shall inform the plaintiff to change the defendant; if the plaintiff refuses to change, it shall rule to dismiss the prosecution. After reviewing the evidence provided by the parties or conducting an investigation ex officio, if the People's Court still cannot determine the proper defendant, it may, pursuant to Article 66 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, transfer the relevant materials to the relevant authorities for investigation depending on the circumstances and rule to suspend the proceedings.  
The six-month prosecution period provided in Article 46 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the one-year prosecution period provided in Article 64 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall commence from the date on which the citizen, legal person or other organization knows or should know the content of the administrative act and knows or should know the subject that implemented the act. If the defendant claims that the plaintiff has exceeded the statutory prosecution period since the date on which it knew or should know the content of the administrative act and its implementing subject, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
This is the reply.
