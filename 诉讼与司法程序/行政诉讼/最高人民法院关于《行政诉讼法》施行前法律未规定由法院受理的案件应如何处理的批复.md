> 关于《行政诉讼法》施行前法律未规定由 法院受理的案件应如何处理的批复 1993年2月15日      〔1992〕民他字第10号 河南省高级人民法院： 你院豫法（告）请〔1991〕38号《关于不服工商行政机关的查封、划拨通知书能否按民事或行政侵权案件受理的请示》收悉。 经我院审判委员会讨论，同意你院请示报告中的第二种意见，即：开封市工商局1988年对开封市曹门经销部作出冻结划拨酒款通知书，并以“白条”为收据提走其1653件川曲酒替开封市豫川副食品联营公司冲抵货款的行为，是行政侵权行为，但案发在行政诉讼法施行之前，当时的法律没有规定法院受理此类案件，因此，人民法院不能受理。曹门经销部应向有关行政机关申请解决。

**Article 1** Reply on How to Handle Cases Accepted by the People's Court  
February 15, 1993 [1992] Min Ta Zi No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
We have received your Request No. 38 [1991] Yu Fa (Gao) Qing, titled 'Request on Whether a Notice of Seizure and Transfer by an Administrative Authority for Industry and Commerce Can Be Accepted as a Civil or Administrative Infringement Case'.  
After discussion by our Adjudication Committee, we agree with the second opinion in your request report, namely: The act of the Kaifeng Municipal Bureau of Industry and Commerce in 1988 of issuing a notice to freeze and transfer liquor funds of the Kaifeng Caomen Distribution Department, and using an informal receipt to take away 1,653 pieces of Sichuan Qu liquor to offset the debts for the Kaifeng Yuchuan Non-staple Food Joint Venture Company, constitutes an administrative infringement. However, the incident occurred before the Administrative Procedure Law came into force, and the laws then in force did not provide that the People's Court could accept such cases. Therefore, the People's Court shall not accept it. The Caomen Distribution Department shall apply to the relevant administrative authority for resolution.
