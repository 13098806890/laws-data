# 最高人民法院行政审判庭关于公安机关未具法定立案搜查手续对公民进行住宅人身搜查被搜查人提起诉讼人民法院可否按行政案件受理问题的电话答复

**发文字号**：川法研〔1991〕22号

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院行政审判庭  
关于公安机关未具法定立案搜查手续对公民  
进行住宅人身搜查被搜查人提起诉讼  
人民法院可否按行政案件受理  
问题的电话答复  
（1991年6月18日）  
四川省高级人民法院：  
你院川法研〔1991〕22号请示收悉。经研究，同意请示中第一种意见。公安机关在侦破刑事案件中，对公民的住宅、人身进行搜查，属于刑事侦查措施。对于刑事侦查措施不服提起诉讼的，不属于行政诉讼调整范围。如果公安机关在采取上述措施时违反法定程序，可以向该公安机关或其上级机关及有关部门反映解决，人民法院不应作为行政案件受理。  
附：  
四川省高级人民法院  
关于公安机关未具法定立案搜查手续对公民  
进行住宅人身搜查，被搜查人提起诉讼  
人民法院可否按行政案件受理的请示  
1991年5月6日      川法研〔1991〕22号  
最高人民法院：  
我省有的公安机关主要的是公安派出所，接到群众和单位被盗的报案后，没有立案，也未具有合法搜查手续，在没有掌握任何证据的情况下，即对作案怀疑对象的住宅、人身进行搜查。被搜查人以公安机关非法搜查侵犯住宅、人身权利为由向法院提起诉讼，要求公安机关恢复名誉，消除影响。对此类诉讼，法院可否按行政案件受理，我们在讨论中有两种意见：  
第一种意见认为，这种搜查住宅、人身的行为，虽不符法定手续，但仍属刑事侦查措施，不属行政诉讼法调整的范围，应告知被搜查人向其上级公安机关反映解决，或向检察机关提出控告，人民法院不应按行政案件受理。  
第二种意见认为，刑事侦查措施应当按照刑事诉讼法的规定采取，公安机关既未依法立案，又未具备合法搜查手续，即对公民的住宅、人身进行搜查，侵犯了公民的人身权利，根据[行政诉讼法第十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-11)第八项的规定，行政机关及其工作人员在执行职务中侵犯了公民的人身权、财产权，应属行政案件的受案范围，人民法院应按行政案件受理。在判决方式上，可宣告公安机关搜查违反刑事诉讼法的规定，造成对公民名誉权的损害，责令公安机关赔礼道歉，恢复名誉，消除影响。如果搜查中造成公民人身伤害和财产损失的，应按行政侵权赔偿一并审理。  
我们倾向于第一种意见，当否，请批复。

**Article 1** Telephone Reply of the Administrative Adjudication Tribunal of the Supreme People's Court  
on the Issue of Whether a People's Court May Accept as an Administrative Case a Lawsuit Filed by a Person Searched  
When a Public Security Organ Searches a Citizen's Residence or Person  
Without Having the Statutory Case-Filing and Search Warrant  
(June 18, 1991)  
  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your Request No. Chuan Fa Yan [1991] 22 has been received. Upon study, we concur with the first opinion in the request. Where a public security organ searches a citizen's residence or person during the investigation of a criminal case, such action constitutes a criminal investigation measure. A lawsuit challenging a criminal investigation measure does not fall within the scope of administrative litigation. If the public security organ violates statutory procedures when taking the aforesaid measures, the matter may be reported to that public security organ, its superior organ, or the relevant departments for resolution; the People's Court shall not accept it as an administrative case.  
  
Attachment:  
Request of the Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province  
on the Issue of Whether a People's Court May Accept as an Administrative Case a Lawsuit Filed by a Person Searched  
When a Public Security Organ Searches a Citizen's Residence or Person  
Without Having the Statutory Case-Filing and Search Warrant  
  
May 6, 1991 Chuan Fa Yan [1991] No. 22  
  
Supreme People's Court:  
In our Province, some public security organs, mainly police stations, after receiving reports of theft from citizens or units, have, without filing a case or having a lawful search warrant, and in the absence of any evidence, searched the residence and person of a suspect. The person searched has filed a lawsuit in court against the public security organ on the ground that the illegal search violated the rights of residence and person, demanding restoration of reputation and elimination of adverse effects. Regarding such lawsuits, whether the court may accept them as administrative cases, we have two opinions in our discussion:  
  
The first opinion holds that although the search of residence and person does not comply with statutory procedures, it is still a criminal investigation measure and does not fall within the scope of regulation of the Administrative Procedure Law. The person searched shall be informed to report the matter to the superior public security organ for resolution, or to file a complaint with the procuratorial organ; the People's Court shall not accept it as an administrative case.  
  
The second opinion holds that criminal investigation measures shall be taken in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law. Since the public security organ has neither filed a case according to law nor possessed a lawful search warrant when searching a citizen's residence and person, it has violated the citizen's personal rights. According to Article 11, Item 8 of the Administrative Procedure Law, where an administrative organ or its staff member infringes upon a citizen's personal rights or property rights in the performance of duties, such matter shall fall within the scope of acceptance of administrative cases, and the People's Court shall accept it as an administrative case. In terms of judgment, the court may declare that the search by the public security organ violates the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law, causes damage to the citizen's right to reputation, and order the public security organ to make an apology, restore reputation, and eliminate adverse effects. If personal injury or property loss is caused during the search, it shall be tried together as an administrative tort compensation case.  
  
We incline toward the first opinion. Is this correct? Please issue your approval.
