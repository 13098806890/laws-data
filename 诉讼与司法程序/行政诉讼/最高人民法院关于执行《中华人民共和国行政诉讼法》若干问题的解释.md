# 最高人民法院关于执行《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕8号  
**公布日期**：1999-11-24  
**生效日期**：2000-03-10

> （1999年11月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1088次会议通过，自2000年3月10日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕8号 为正确理解和适用《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法），现结合行政审判工作实际，对执行行政诉讼法的若干问题作出如下解释： 一、受案范围 第一条 公民、法人或者其他组织对具有国家行政职权的机关和组织及其工作人员的行政行为不服，依法提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。 公民、法人或者其他组织对下列行为不服提起诉讼的，不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围： （一）行政诉讼法第十二条规定的行为； （二）公安、国家安全等机关依照刑事诉讼法的明确授权实施的行为； （三）调解行为以及法律规定的仲裁行为； （四）不具有强制力的行政指导行为； （五）驳回当事人对行政行为提起申诉的重复处理行为； （六）对公民、法人或者其他组织权利义务不产生实际影响的行为。 第二条 行政诉讼法第十二条第（一）项规定的国家行为，是指国务院、中央军事委员会、国防部、外交部等根据宪法和法律的授权，以国家的名义实施的有关国防和外交事务的行为，以及经宪法和法律授权的国家机关宣布紧急状态、实施戒严和总动员等行为。 第三条 行政诉讼法第十二条第（二）项规定的“具有普遍约束力的决定、命令”，是指行政机关针对不特定对象发布的能反复适用的行政规范性文件。 第四条 行政诉讼法第十二条第（三）项规定的“对行政机关工作人员的奖惩、任免等决定”，是指行政机关作出的涉及该行政机关公务员权利义务的决定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 公民、法人或者其他组织对具有国家行政职权的机关和组织及其工作人员的行政行为不服，依法提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。  
公民、法人或者其他组织对下列行为不服提起诉讼的，不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围：  
（一）[行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)规定的行为；  
（二）公安、国家安全等机关依照刑事诉讼法的明确授权实施的行为；  
（三）调解行为以及法律规定的仲裁行为；  
（四）不具有强制力的行政指导行为；  
（五）驳回当事人对行政行为提起申诉的重复处理行为；  
（六）对公民、法人或者其他组织权利义务不产生实际影响的行为。

**Article 1** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept an administrative act of an organ or organization with State administrative functions or of their staff members and initiates administrative proceedings according to law, such proceedings shall be within the scope of acceptance of administrative actions by the People's Courts.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept any of the following acts and initiates administrative proceedings, such proceedings shall not be within the scope of acceptance of administrative actions by the People's Courts:  
(1) acts specified in Article 12 of the Administrative Procedure Law;  
(2) acts performed by public security, State security, and other authorities in accordance with the explicit authorization of the Criminal Procedure Law;  
(3) mediation acts and arbitration acts as provided by law;  
(4) administrative guidance acts that are not binding;  
(5) repeated acts that reject a party's petition against an administrative act; and  
(6) acts that have no actual effect on the rights or obligations of a citizen, legal person, or any other organization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 [行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第（一）项规定的国家行为，是指国务院、中央军事委员会、国防部、外交部等根据宪法和法律的授权，以国家的名义实施的有关国防和外交事务的行为，以及经宪法和法律授权的国家机关宣布紧急状态、实施戒严和总动员等行为。

**Article 2** The State acts specified in Article 12, paragraph (1) of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to acts concerning national defense and foreign affairs performed in the name of the State by the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the Ministry of National Defense, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and other authorities in accordance with the authorization of the Constitution and laws, as well as acts such as the declaration of a state of emergency, the imposition of martial law, and general mobilization by State organs authorized by the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 [行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第（二）项规定的“具有普遍约束力的决定、命令”，是指行政机关针对不特定对象发布的能反复适用的行政规范性文件。

**Article 3** The "decisions and orders with general binding force" specified in Article 12, paragraph (2) of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to administrative regulatory documents issued by administrative organs that are directed at non-specific persons and may be applied repeatedly.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 [行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第（三）项规定的“对行政机关工作人员的奖惩、任免等决定”，是指行政机关作出的涉及该行政机关公务员权利义务的决定。

**Article 4** The "decisions on rewards, punishments, appointments, and removals of administrative organ staff" specified in Article 12, paragraph (3) of the Administrative Procedure Law refer to decisions made by administrative organs concerning the rights and obligations of civil servants of that administrative organ.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 [行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第（四）项规定的“法律规定由行政机关最终裁决的具体行政行为”中的“法律”，是指全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定、通过的规范性文件。  
二、管辖

**Article 5** The term "laws" in the phrase "specific administrative acts ultimately adjudicated by administrative organs as provided by law" specified in Article 12, paragraph (4) of the Administrative Procedure Law refers to the regulatory documents formulated and adopted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee.  
II. Jurisdiction

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 各级人民法院行政审判庭审理行政案件和审查行政机关申请执行其具体行政行为的案件。  
专门人民法院、人民法庭不审理行政案件，也不审查和执行行政机关申请执行其具体行政行为的案件。

**Article 6** The administrative divisions of the People's Courts at all levels shall try administrative cases and examine cases in which administrative organs apply for the enforcement of their specific administrative acts.  
Specialized People's Courts and people's tribunals shall not try administrative cases, nor shall they examine or enforce cases in which administrative organs apply for the enforcement of their specific administrative acts.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 复议决定有下列情形之一的，属于行政诉讼法规定的“改变原具体行政行为”：  
（一）改变原具体行政行为所认定的主要事实和证据的；  
（二）改变原具体行政行为所适用的规范依据且对定性产生影响的；  
（三）撤销、部分撤销或者变更原具体行政行为处理结果的。

**Article 7** Any of the following circumstances in a reconsideration decision shall constitute "alteration of the original specific administrative act" as provided for in the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) altering the main facts and evidence determined in the original specific administrative act;  
(2) altering the normative bases applied in the original specific administrative act and affecting the characterization; or  
(3) revoking, partially revoking, or changing the result of the original specific administrative act.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-14)第（三）项规定的“本辖区内重大、复杂的案件”：  
（一）被告为县级以上人民政府，且基层人民法院不适宜审理的案件；  
（二）社会影响重大的共同诉讼、集团诉讼案件；  
（三）重大涉外或者涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的案件；  
（四）其他重大、复杂案件。

**Article 8** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a "major and complex case within the jurisdiction" as specified in Article 14, paragraph (3) of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) a case where the defendant is a People's Government at or above the county level and the Primary People's Court is not suitable for trying the case;  
(2) a case of joint action or class action with significant social impact;  
(3) a major case involving foreign elements or involving the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region; or  
(4) other major and complex cases.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 [行政诉讼法第十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-18)规定的“原告所在地”，包括原告的户籍所在地、经常居住地和被限制人身自由地。  
行政机关基于同一事实既对人身又对财产实施行政处罚或者采取行政强制措施的，被限制人身自由的公民、被扣押或者没收财产的公民、法人或者其他组织对上述行为均不服的，既可以向被告所在地人民法院提起诉讼，也可以向原告所在地人民法院提起诉讼，受诉人民法院可一并管辖。

**Article 9** The "place where the plaintiff is located" specified in Article 18 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the place of the plaintiff's domicile, habitual residence, and the place where the plaintiff is restricted in personal freedom.  
Where an administrative organ, based on the same facts, imposes administrative penalties or adopts compulsory measures against both the person and property of a citizen, and the citizen whose personal freedom is restricted or the citizen, legal person, or any other organization whose property is seized or confiscated refuses to accept the above acts, such person may initiate administrative proceedings either in the People's Court of the place where the defendant is located or in the People's Court of the place where the plaintiff is located, and the People's Court accepting the case may exercise jurisdiction over all such claims.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 当事人提出管辖异议，应当在接到人民法院应诉通知之日起10日内以书面形式提出。  
对当事人提出的管辖异议，人民法院应当进行审查。异议成立的，裁定将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院；异议不成立的，裁定驳回。  
三、诉讼参加人

**Article 10** Where a party raises an objection to jurisdiction, it shall do so in writing within ten days from the date of receipt of the notice to respond to the lawsuit from the People's Court.  
Where a party raises an objection to jurisdiction, the People's Court shall examine it. If the objection is sustained, the People's Court shall make a ruling to transfer the case to the People's Court with jurisdiction; if the objection is not sustained, the People's Court shall make a ruling to reject it.  
III. Litigants

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 [行政诉讼法第二十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-24)规定的“近亲属”，包括配偶、父母、子女、兄弟姐妹、祖父母、外祖父母、孙子女、外孙子女和其他具有扶养、赡养关系的亲属。  
公民因被限制人身自由而不能提起诉讼的，其近亲属可以依其口头或者书面委托以该公民的名义提起诉讼。

**Article 11** The "close relatives" specified in Article 24 of the Administrative Procedure Law include spouses, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, maternal grandparents, grandchildren, and other relatives who have a relationship of support or maintenance.  
Where a citizen is unable to initiate administrative proceedings due to restriction of personal freedom, his or her close relatives may initiate proceedings in the name of such citizen upon oral or written authorization by the citizen.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 与具体行政行为有法律上利害关系的公民、法人或者其他组织对该行为不服的，可以依法提起行政诉讼。

**Article 12** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization having a legal interest in a specific administrative act refuses to accept such act, such person may initiate administrative proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 有下列情形之一的，公民、法人或者其他组织可以依法提起行政诉讼：  
（一）被诉的具体行政行为涉及其相邻权或者公平竞争权的；  
（二）与被诉的行政复议决定有法律上利害关系或者在复议程序中被追加为第三人的；  
（三）要求主管行政机关依法追究加害人法律责任的；  
（四）与撤销或者变更具体行政行为有法律上利害关系的。

**Article 13** In any of the following circumstances, a citizen, legal person, or any other organization may initiate administrative proceedings according to law:  
(1) the specific administrative act being sued involves his or her right of neighbor or right to fair competition;  
(2) having a legal interest in the administrative reconsideration decision being sued, or having been joined as a third party in the reconsideration procedure;  
(3) requesting the competent administrative organ to investigate the legal liability of the injuring party according to law; or  
(4) having a legal interest in the revocation or alteration of the specific administrative act.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 合伙企业向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当以核准登记的字号为原告，由执行合伙企业事务的合伙人作诉讼代表人；其他合伙组织提起诉讼的，合伙人为共同原告。  
不具备法人资格的其他组织向人民法院提起诉讼的，由该组织的主要负责人作诉讼代表人；没有主要负责人的，可以由推选的负责人作诉讼代表人。  
同案原告为5人以上，应当推选1至5名诉讼代表人参加诉讼；在指定期限内未选定的，人民法院可以依职权指定。

**Article 14** Where a partnership enterprise initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court, the enterprise shall be named as the plaintiff under its approved and registered name, with the partner managing the affairs of the partnership enterprise serving as the litigation representative; where any other partnership organization initiates administrative proceedings, all partners shall be joint plaintiffs.  
Where any other organization without legal person status initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court, the principal person in charge of such organization shall serve as the litigation representative; if there is no principal person in charge, the organization may elect a person in charge to serve as the litigation representative.  
Where there are five or more plaintiffs in the same case, the plaintiffs shall elect one to five litigation representatives to participate in the litigation; if no such election is made within the specified period, the People's Court may designate the representatives ex officio.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 联营企业、中外合资或者合作企业的联营、合资、合作各方，认为联营、合资、合作企业权益或者自己一方合法权益受具体行政行为侵害的，均可以自已的名义提起诉讼。

**Article 15** Where any party to a joint-operation enterprise, a Chinese-foreign equity joint venture, or a Chinese-foreign contractual joint venture considers that the rights and interests of the joint-operation, joint venture, or cooperative enterprise, or the legal rights and interests of its own side have been infringed upon by a specific administrative act, such party may initiate administrative proceedings in its own name.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 农村土地承包人等土地使用权人对行政机关处分其使用的农村集体所有土地的行为不服，可以自己的名义提起诉讼。

**Article 16** Where any rural land contractor or other holder of land-use rights refuses to accept an act by an administrative organ disposing of the collectively-owned rural land that such person uses, such person may initiate administrative proceedings in his or her own name.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 非国有企业被行政机关注销、撤销、合并、强令兼并、出售、分立或者改变企业隶属关系的，该企业或者其法定代表人可以提起诉讼。

**Article 17** Where a non-State-owned enterprise is cancelled, dissolved, merged, forced to merge, sold, divided, or its subordination relationship is changed by an administrative organ, such enterprise or its legal representative may initiate administrative proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 股份制企业的股东大会、股东代表大会、董事会等认为行政机关作出的具体行政行为侵犯企业经营自主权的，可以企业名义提起诉讼。

**Article 18** Where the shareholders' meeting, the shareholders' general meeting, the board of directors, or any other organ of a joint-stock enterprise considers that a specific administrative act made by an administrative organ infringes upon the enterprise's operational autonomy, such enterprise may initiate administrative proceedings in the name of the enterprise.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 当事人不服经上级行政机关批准的具体行政行为，向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当以在对外发生法律效力的文书上署名的机关为被告。

**Article 19** Where a party refuses to accept a specific administrative act that has been approved by a higher administrative organ and initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court, the organ that signed the document with external legal effect shall be named as the defendant.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 行政机关组建并赋予行政管理职能但不具有独立承担法律责任能力的机构，以自己的名义作出具体行政行为，当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以组建该机构的行政机关为被告。  
行政机关的内设机构或者派出机构在没有法律、法规或者规章授权的情况下，以自己的名义作出具体行政行为，当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以该行政机关为被告。  
法律、法规或者规章授权行使行政职权的行政机关内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织，超出法定授权范围实施行政行为，当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以实施该行为的机构或者组织为被告。

**Article 20** Where an organ established by an administrative organ and endowed with administrative functions but lacking the capacity to independently bear legal liability makes a specific administrative act in its own name, and a party refuses to accept such act and initiates administrative proceedings, the administrative organ that established such organ shall be named as the defendant.  
Where an internal organ or dispatched organ of an administrative organ, without authorization from laws, regulations, or rules, makes a specific administrative act in its own name, and a party refuses to accept such act and initiates administrative proceedings, such administrative organ shall be named as the defendant.  
Where an internal organ, dispatched organ, or any other organization of an administrative organ that is authorized by laws, regulations, or rules to exercise administrative functions performs an administrative act beyond the scope of statutory authorization, and a party refuses to accept such act and initiates administrative proceedings, the organ or organization that performed such act shall be named as the defendant.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 行政机关在没有法律、法规或者规章规定的情况下，授权其内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织行使行政职权的，应当视为委托。当事人不服提起诉讼的，应当以该行政机关为被告。

**Article 21** Where an administrative organ, in the absence of provisions of laws, regulations, or rules, authorizes its internal organ, dispatched organ, or any other organization to exercise administrative functions, such authorization shall be deemed as a delegation. Where a party refuses to accept the act and initiates administrative proceedings, such administrative organ shall be named as the defendant.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 复议机关在法定期间内不作复议决定，当事人对原具体行政行为不服提起诉讼的，应当以作出原具体行政行为的行政机关为被告；当事人对复议机关不作为不服提起诉讼的，应当以复议机关为被告。

**Article 22** Where the reconsideration organ fails to make a reconsideration decision within the statutory period, and a party refuses to accept the original specific administrative act and initiates administrative proceedings, the administrative organ that made the original specific administrative act shall be named as the defendant; where a party refuses to accept the inaction of the reconsideration organ and initiates administrative proceedings, the reconsideration organ shall be named as the defendant.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 原告所起诉的被告不适格，人民法院应当告知原告变更被告；原告不同意变更的，裁定驳回起诉。  
应当追加被告而原告不同意追加的，人民法院应当通知其以第三人的身份参加诉讼。

**Article 23** Where the defendant sued by the plaintiff is not the proper defendant, the People's Court shall inform the plaintiff to substitute the defendant; if the plaintiff refuses to substitute, the People's Court shall make a ruling to dismiss the action.  
Where a defendant should be joined but the plaintiff refuses to join such defendant, the People's Court shall notify the person to participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 行政机关的同一具体行政行为涉及两个以上利害关系人，其中一部分利害关系人对具体行政行为不服提起诉讼，人民法院应当通知没有起诉的其他利害关系人作为第三人参加诉讼。  
第三人有权提出与本案有关的诉讼主张，对人民法院的一审判决不服，有权提起上诉。

**Article 24** Where the same specific administrative act of an administrative organ involves two or more interested persons, and some of the interested persons refuse to accept the specific administrative act and initiate administrative proceedings, the People's Court shall notify the other interested persons who have not initiated proceedings to participate in the proceedings as third parties.  
A third party shall have the right to raise claims relating to the case and the right to appeal against a judgment of first instance rendered by the People's Court.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 当事人委托诉讼代理人，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。委托书应当载明委托事项和具体权限。公民在特殊情况下无法书面委托的，也可以口头委托。口头委托的，人民法院应当核实并记录在卷；被诉机关或者其他有义务协助的机关拒绝人民法院向被限制人身自由的公民核实的，视为委托成立。当事人解除或者变更委托的，应当书面报告人民法院，由人民法院通知其他当事人。  
四、证据

**Article 25** Where a party appoints a litigation agent, the party shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal. The power of attorney shall state the matters entrusted and the specific powers granted. Where a citizen is unable to make a written appointment under special circumstances, the appointment may be made orally. If the appointment is made orally, the People's Court shall verify and record it in the case file; if the sued organ or other organ obliged to assist refuses to assist the People's Court in verifying the matter with the citizen whose personal freedom is restricted, the appointment shall be deemed established. Where a party rescinds or modifies an appointment, the party shall report in writing to the People's Court, which shall notify the other parties.  
IV. Evidence

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 在行政诉讼中，被告对其作出的具体行政行为承担举证责任。  
被告应当在收到起诉状副本之日起10日内提交答辩状，并提供作出具体行政行为时的证据、依据；被告不提供或者无正当理由逾期提供的，应当认定该具体行政行为没有证据、依据。

**Article 26** In administrative proceedings, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the specific administrative act it made.  
The defendant shall submit a statement of defense within ten days from the date of receipt of the copy of the complaint and provide the evidence and bases on which the specific administrative act was made; if the defendant fails to provide evidence or provides it after the time limit without justification, it shall be determined that the specific administrative act is without evidence or basis.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 原告对下列事项承担举证责任：  
（一）证明起诉符合法定条件，但被告认为原告起诉超过起诉期限的除外；  
（二）在起诉被告不作为的案件中，证明其提出申请的事实；  
（三）在一并提起的行政赔偿诉讼中，证明因受被诉行为侵害而造成损失的事实；  
（四）其他应当由原告承担举证责任的事项。

**Article 27** The plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the following matters:  
(1) proving that the action meets the statutory conditions for initiation, except where the defendant contends that the plaintiff's action exceeds the statutory limitation period for initiation;  
(2) in a case where the defendant's inaction is sued, proving the fact that the plaintiff filed an application;  
(3) in an administrative compensation action brought concurrently, proving the fact of losses caused by the infringement of the act being sued; and  
(4) other matters for which the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 有下列情形之一的，被告经人民法院准许可以补充相关的证据：  
（一）被告在作出具体行政行为时已经收集证据，但因不可抗力等正当事由不能提供的；  
（二）原告或者第三人在诉讼过程中，提出了其在被告实施行政行为过程中没有提出的反驳理由或者证据的。

**Article 28** In any of the following circumstances, the defendant may, with the permission of the People's Court, supplement relevant evidence:  
(1) the defendant had already collected evidence at the time of making the specific administrative act but is unable to provide it due to force majeure or other justifiable reasons; or  
(2) during the proceedings, the plaintiff or a third party raises rebuttal arguments or evidence that were not raised during the defendant's implementation of the administrative act.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 有下列情形之一的，人民法院有权调取证据：  
（一）原告或者第三人及其诉讼代理人提供了证据线索，但无法自行收集而申请人民法院调取的；  
（二）当事人应当提供而无法提供原件或者原物的。

**Article 29** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall have the authority to collect evidence:  
(1) where the plaintiff or a third party, or their litigation agents, have provided clues to evidence but are unable to collect it by themselves and apply to the People's Court for collection; or  
(2) where a party should provide but is unable to provide the original document or object.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 下列证据不能作为认定被诉具体行政行为合法的根据：  
（一）被告及其诉讼代理人在作出具体行政行为后自行收集的证据；  
（二）被告严重违反法定程序收集的其他证据。

**Article 30** The following evidence shall not be used as the basis for determining the legality of the specific administrative act being sued:  
(1) evidence collected by the defendant or its litigation agent on its own after the specific administrative act was made; and  
(2) other evidence collected by the defendant in serious violation of statutory procedures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 未经法庭质证的证据不能作为人民法院裁判的根据。  
复议机关在复议过程中收集和补充的证据，不能作为人民法院维持原具体行政行为的根据。  
被告在二审过程中向法庭提交在一审过程中没有提交的证据，不能作为二审法院撤销或者变更一审裁判的根据。  
五、起诉与受理

**Article 31** Evidence that has not been cross-examined in court shall not be used as the basis for the People's Court's judgment.  
Evidence collected and supplemented by the reconsideration organ during the reconsideration process shall not be used as the basis for the People's Court to sustain the original specific administrative act.  
Evidence submitted by the defendant to the court during the second instance that was not submitted during the first instance shall not be used as the basis for the court of second instance to revoke or modify the judgment or ruling of the first instance.  
V. Initiation and Acceptance of Actions

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 人民法院应当组成合议庭对原告的起诉进行审查。符合起诉条件的，应当在7日内立案；不符合起诉条件的，应当在7日内裁定不予受理。  
7日内不能决定是否受理的，应当先予受理；受理后经审查不符合起诉条件的，裁定驳回起诉。  
受诉人民法院在7日内既不立案，又不作出裁定的，起诉人可以向上一级人民法院申诉或者起诉。上一级人民法院认为符合受理条件的，应予受理；受理后可以移交或者指定下级人民法院审理，也可以自行审理。  
前三款规定的期限，从受诉人民法院收到起诉状之日起计算；因起诉状内容欠缺而责令原告补正的，从人民法院收到补正材料之日起计算。

**Article 32** The People's Court shall form a collegial panel to examine the plaintiff's complaint. If the action meets the conditions for initiation, the court shall docket the case within seven days; if the action does not meet the conditions for initiation, the court shall make a ruling of non-acceptance within seven days.  
If the court cannot decide whether to accept the case within seven days, it shall accept the case provisionally; if upon examination after acceptance it finds that the conditions for initiation are not met, the court shall make a ruling to dismiss the action.  
If the People's Court that receives the complaint neither dockets the case nor makes a ruling within seven days, the complainant may petition or initiate proceedings in the People's Court at the next higher level. If the People's Court at the next higher level considers that the conditions for acceptance are met, it shall accept the case; after acceptance, it may transfer the case to or designate a lower People's Court for trial, or it may try the case itself.  
The time limits specified in the preceding three paragraphs shall be calculated from the date on which the People's Court receiving the complaint receives the complaint; if the plaintiff is required to supplement the complaint due to deficiencies in its contents, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the People's Court receives the supplemented materials.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 法律、法规规定应当先申请复议，公民、法人或者其他组织未申请复议直接提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。  
复议机关不受理复议申请或者在法定期限内不作出复议决定，公民、法人或者其他组织不服，依法向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 33** Where laws or regulations provide that an application for administrative reconsideration shall first be made, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization directly initiates administrative proceedings without applying for reconsideration, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where the reconsideration organ refuses to accept the reconsideration application or fails to make a reconsideration decision within the statutory period, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept this and initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 法律、法规未规定行政复议为提起行政诉讼必经程序，公民、法人或者其他组织既提起诉讼又申请行政复议的，由先受理的机关管辖；同时受理的，由公民、法人或者其他组织选择。公民、法人或者其他组织已经申请行政复议，在法定复议期间内又向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 34** Where laws or regulations do not provide that administrative reconsideration is a prerequisite for initiating administrative proceedings, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization both initiates administrative proceedings and applies for administrative reconsideration, the organ that first accepts the matter shall have jurisdiction; if both are accepted at the same time, the citizen, legal person, or any other organization may choose. Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization has already applied for administrative reconsideration and initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court within the statutory period for reconsideration, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 法律、法规未规定行政复议为提起行政诉讼必经程序，公民、法人或者其他组织向复议机关申请行政复议后，又经复议机关同意撤回复议申请，在法定起诉期限内对原具体行政行为提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 35** Where laws or regulations do not provide that administrative reconsideration is a prerequisite for initiating administrative proceedings, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization, after applying for administrative reconsideration to the reconsideration organ, withdraws the reconsideration application with the consent of the reconsideration organ and initiates administrative proceedings against the original specific administrative act within the statutory period for initiation, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 人民法院裁定准许原告撤诉后，原告以同一事实和理由重新起诉的，人民法院不予受理。  
准予撤诉的裁定确有错误，原告申请再审的，人民法院应当通过审判监督程序撤销原准予撤诉的裁定，重新对案件进行审理。

**Article 36** After the People's Court makes a ruling permitting the plaintiff to withdraw the action, if the plaintiff initiates a new action based on the same facts and reasons, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where the ruling permitting withdrawal is indeed erroneous and the plaintiff applies for retrial, the People's Court shall, through the trial supervision procedure, revoke the original ruling permitting withdrawal and retry the case.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 原告或者上诉人未按规定的期限预交案件受理费，又不提出缓交、减交、免交申请，或者提出申请未获批准的，按自动撤诉处理。在按撤诉处理后，原告或者上诉人在法定期限内再次起诉或者上诉，并依法解决诉讼费预交问题的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 37** Where the plaintiff or appellant fails to prepay the case acceptance fee within the specified time limit, and neither applies for deferred payment, reduction, or exemption, nor obtains approval for such an application, the action shall be treated as withdrawn automatically. After being treated as withdrawn, if the plaintiff or appellant initiates a new action or appeals again within the statutory time limit and resolves the prepayment of litigation fees according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 人民法院判决撤销行政机关的具体行政行为后，公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关重新作出的具体行政行为不服向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 38** After the People's Court renders a judgment revoking a specific administrative act of an administrative organ, if a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept the new specific administrative act made by the administrative organ and initiates proceedings in the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 公民、法人或者其他组织申请行政机关履行法定职责，行政机关在接到申请之日起60日内不履行的，公民、法人或者其他组织向人民法院提起诉讼，人民法院应当依法受理。法律、法规、规章和其他规范性文件对行政机关履行职责的期限另有规定的，从其规定。  
公民、法人或者其他组织在紧急情况下请求行政机关履行保护其人身权、财产权的法定职责，行政机关不履行的，起诉期间不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 39** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization applies to an administrative organ to perform its statutory duties, and the administrative organ fails to perform within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, if the citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates administrative proceedings in the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law. If laws, regulations, rules, or other regulatory documents provide otherwise for the time limit for an administrative organ to perform its duties, such provisions shall prevail.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization requests an administrative organ to perform its statutory duty of protecting his, her, or its right to the person or property in an emergency, and the administrative organ fails to perform, the time limit for initiation of proceedings shall not be subject to the restriction of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 行政机关作出具体行政行为时，没有制作或者没有送达法律文书，公民、法人或者其他组织不服向人民法院起诉的，只要能证明具体行政行为存在，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 40** Where an administrative organ, when making a specific administrative act, fails to prepare or serve a legal document, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept the act and initiates proceedings in the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law if the existence of the specific administrative act can be proved.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 行政机关作出具体行政行为时，未告知公民、法人或者其他组织诉权或者起诉期限的，起诉期限从公民、法人或者其他组织知道或者应当知道诉权或者起诉期限之日起计算，但从知道或者应当知道具体行政行为内容之日起最长不得超过2年。  
复议决定未告知公民、法人或者其他组织诉权或者法定起诉期限的，适用前款规定。

**Article 41** Where an administrative organ, when making a specific administrative act, fails to inform a citizen, legal person, or any other organization of the right to sue or the time limit for initiation, the time limit for initiation shall be calculated from the date on which the citizen, legal person, or any other organization knows or should have known of the right to sue or the time limit for initiation, but the maximum period from the date on which such person knows or should have known of the content of the specific administrative act shall not exceed two years.  
Where a reconsideration decision fails to inform a citizen, legal person, or any other organization of the right to sue or the statutory time limit for initiation, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 公民、法人或者其他组织不知道行政机关作出的具体行政行为内容的，其起诉期限从知道或者应当知道该具体行政行为内容之日起计算。对涉及不动产的具体行政行为从作出之日起超过20年、其他具体行政行为从作出之日起超过5年提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 42** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization does not know the content of a specific administrative act made by an administrative organ, the time limit for initiation of proceedings shall be calculated from the date on which such person knows or should have known the content of the specific administrative act. If an action against a specific administrative act involving immovable property is initiated more than 20 years from the date of the act, or an action against any other specific administrative act is initiated more than five years from the date of the act, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条 由于不属于起诉人自身的原因超过起诉期限的，被耽误的时间不计算在起诉期间内。因人身自由受到限制而不能提起诉讼的，被限制人身自由的时间不计算在起诉期间内。  
六、审理与判决

**Article 43** Where the time limit for initiation is exceeded due to reasons not attributable to the complainant, the period of delay shall not be counted in the time limit for initiation. Where an action cannot be initiated due to restriction of personal freedom, the period during which personal freedom is restricted shall not be counted in the time limit for initiation.  
VI. Trial and Judgment

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条 有下列情形之一的，应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉：  
（一）请求事项不属于行政审判权限范围的；  
（二）起诉人无原告诉讼主体资格的；  
（三）起诉人错列被告且拒绝变更的；  
（四）法律规定必须由法定或者指定代理人、代表人为诉讼行为，未由法定或者指定代理人、代表人为诉讼行为的，  
（五）由诉讼代理人代为起诉，其代理不符合法定要求的；  
（六）起诉超过法定期限且无正当理由的；  
（七）法律、法规规定行政复议为提起诉讼必经程序而未申请复议的；  
（八）起诉人重复起诉的；  
（九）已撤回起诉，无正当理由再行起诉的，  
（十）诉讼标的为生效判决的效力所羁束的；  
（十一）起诉不具备其他法定要件的。  
前款所列情形可以补正或者更正的，人民法院应当指定期间责令补正或者更正；在指定期间已经补正或者更正的，应当依法受理。

**Article 44** In any of the following circumstances, a ruling of non-acceptance shall be made; if the case has already been accepted, a ruling to dismiss the action shall be made:  
(1) the claims are not within the scope of administrative adjudication authority;  
(2) the complainant has no qualification as a plaintiff;  
(3) the complainant incorrectly lists the defendant and refuses to correct it;  
(4) where a legal representative or designated agent or representative must perform procedural acts as provided by law, and no such legal representative, designated agent, or representative performs the acts;  
(5) where a litigation agent initiates the action on behalf of the party but the agency does not meet the statutory requirements;  
(6) the action is initiated beyond the statutory time limit without justification;  
(7) where laws or regulations provide that administrative reconsideration is a prerequisite for initiating proceedings but no application for reconsideration has been made;  
(8) the complainant files a duplicate action;  
(9) the action has been withdrawn and a new action is initiated without justification;  
(10) the object of the action is bound by the force of an effective judgment; or  
(11) the action lacks other statutory conditions.  
If the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph can be supplemented or corrected, the People's Court shall specify a time limit and order the supplementation or correction; if supplementation or correction has been made within the specified time limit, the court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条 起诉状副本送达被告后，原告提出新的诉讼请求的，人民法院不予准许，但有正当理由的除外。

**Article 45** Where, after the copy of the complaint is served on the defendant, the plaintiff raises new claims, the People's Court shall not grant permission, except where there is a justified reason.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条 有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以决定合并审理：  
（一）两个以上行政机关分别依据不同的法律、法规对同一事实作出具体行政行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（二）行政机关就同一事实对若干公民、法人或者其他组织分别作出具体行政行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服分别向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（三）在诉讼过程中，被告对原告作出新的具体行政行为，原告不服向同一人民法院起诉的；  
（四）人民法院认为可以合并审理的其他情形。

**Article 46** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may decide to consolidate the trial:  
(1) two or more administrative organs, based on different laws or regulations, make specific administrative acts concerning the same facts, and citizens, legal persons, or any other organizations, refusing to accept such acts, initiate actions against all in the same People's Court;  
(2) an administrative organ makes separate specific administrative acts concerning the same facts against several citizens, legal persons, or any other organizations, and such persons, refusing to accept the acts, initiate separate actions in the same People's Court;  
(3) during the proceedings, the defendant makes a new specific administrative act against the plaintiff, and the plaintiff, refusing to accept it, initiates an action in the same People's Court; or  
(4) other circumstances that the People's Court considers may be consolidated for trial.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条 当事人申请回避，应当说明理由，在案件开始审理时提出；回避事由在案件开始审理后知道的，应当在法庭辩论终结前提出。  
被申请回避的人员，在人民法院作出是否回避的决定前，应当暂停参与本案的工作，但案件需要采取紧急措施的除外。  
对当事人提出的回避申请，人民法院应当在3日内以口头或者书面形式作出决定。  
申请人对驳回回避申请决定不服的，可以向作出决定的人民法院申请复议一次。复议期间，被申请回避的人员不停止参与本案的工作。对申请人的复议申请，人民法院应当在3日内作出复议决定，并通知复议申请人。

**Article 47** Where a party applies for withdrawal, it shall state the reasons and raise the application at the commencement of the trial; if the grounds for withdrawal become known after the trial has commenced, the application shall be raised before the conclusion of court debate.  
The person whose withdrawal is applied for shall suspend participation in the work of the case pending the decision of the People's Court on whether to grant the withdrawal, except where urgent measures are required for the case.  
The People's Court shall make a decision within three days in oral or written form on the application for withdrawal raised by the party.  
If the applicant is dissatisfied with the decision to reject the application for withdrawal, it may apply once for reconsideration to the People's Court that made the decision. During the period of reconsideration, the person whose withdrawal is applied for shall not cease participating in the work of the case. The People's Court shall make a reconsideration decision within three days upon receipt of the reconsideration application and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条 人民法院对于因一方当事人的行为或者其他原因，可能使具体行政行为或者人民法院生效裁判不能或者难以执行的案件，可以根据对方当事人的申请作出财产保全的裁定；当事人没有提出申请的，人民法院在必要时也可以依法采取财产保全措施。  
人民法院审理起诉行政机关没有依法发给抚恤金、社会保险金、最低生活保障费等案件，可以根据原告的申请，依法书面裁定先予执行。  
当事人对财产保全或者先予执行的裁定不服的，可以申请复议。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 48** With respect to a case where, due to the conduct of one party or any other reason, the specific administrative act or the effective judgment or ruling of the People's Court may be impossible or difficult to enforce, the People's Court may, upon application of the other party, render a ruling on property preservation; if the party fails to make such an application, the People's Court may, when necessary, take property preservation measures according to law.  
Where a People's Court tries a case in which a lawsuit is brought against an administrative organ for failing to pay pensions, social insurance benefits, minimum subsistence allowances, etc. according to law, it may, upon the plaintiff's application, render a written ruling on advance execution according to law.  
If a party is dissatisfied with a ruling on property preservation or advance execution, it may apply for reconsideration. The execution of the ruling shall not be suspended during the period of reconsideration.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条 原告或者上诉人经合法传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，可以按撤诉处理。  
原告或者上诉人申请撤诉，人民法院裁定不予准许的，原告或者上诉人经合法传唤无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可而中途退庭的，人民法院可以缺席判决。  
第三人经合法传唤无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，不影响案件的审理。

**Article 49** If the plaintiff or appellant, after being legally summoned, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the court halfway without permission of the court, the case may be treated as withdrawal of the complaint.  
If the plaintiff or appellant applies to withdraw the complaint but the People's Court rules to disallow it, and the plaintiff or appellant, after being legally summoned, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the court halfway without permission of the court, the People's Court may render a default judgment.  
If a third party, after being legally summoned, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the court halfway without permission of the court, this shall not affect the trial of the case.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条 被告在一审期间改变被诉具体行政行为的，应当书面告知人民法院。  
原告或者第三人对改变后的行为不服提起诉讼的，人民法院应当就改变后的具体行政行为进行审理。  
被告改变原具体行政行为，原告不撤诉，人民法院经审查认为原具体行政行为违法的，应当作出确认其违法的判决；认为原具体行政行为合法的，应当判决驳回原告的诉讼请求。  
原告起诉被告不作为，在诉讼中被告作出具体行政行为，原告不撤诉的，参照上述规定处理。

**Article 50** If the defendant changes the sued specific administrative act during the first instance, it shall notify the People's Court in writing.  
If the plaintiff or a third party is dissatisfied with the changed act and institutes a lawsuit, the People's Court shall try the changed specific administrative act.  
If the defendant changes the original specific administrative act but the plaintiff does not withdraw the complaint, and the People's Court, upon examination, finds that the original specific administrative act is illegal, it shall render a judgment confirming its illegality; if it finds that the original specific administrative act is legal, it shall render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims.  
If the plaintiff brings a lawsuit against the defendant for nonfeasance, and during the litigation the defendant performs a specific administrative act, but the plaintiff does not withdraw the complaint, the matter shall be handled with reference to the provisions above.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条 在诉讼过程中，有下列情形之一的，中止诉讼：  
（一）原告死亡，须等待其近亲属表明是否参加诉讼的；  
（二）原告丧失诉讼行为能力，尚未确定法定代理人的；  
（三）作为一方当事人的行政机关、法人或者其他组织终止，尚未确定权利义务承受人的；  
（四）一方当事人因不可抗力的事由不能参加诉讼的；  
（五）案件涉及法律适用问题，需要送请有权机关作出解释或者确认的；  
（六）案件的审判须以相关民事、刑事或者其他行政案件的审理结果为依据，而相关案件尚未审结的；  
（七）其他应当中止诉讼的情形。  
中止诉讼的原因消除后，恢复诉讼。

**Article 51** During the litigation process, the lawsuit shall be suspended under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The plaintiff dies, and it is necessary to wait for his close relatives to indicate whether to participate in the lawsuit;  
(2) The plaintiff loses his capacity to engage in litigation, and no legal representative has been designated;  
(3) The administrative organ, legal person or other organization that is a party terminates, and no person has been designated to succeed to its rights and obligations;  
(4) One party is unable to participate in the lawsuit due to force majeure;  
(5) The case involves a question of application of law that needs to be submitted to an authority with power for interpretation or confirmation;  
(6) The adjudication of the case must be based on the outcome of the trial of a related civil, criminal or other administrative case, and the related case has not yet been concluded;  
(7) Other circumstances under which the lawsuit shall be suspended.  
After the cause for suspension is eliminated, the lawsuit shall be resumed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条 在诉讼过程中，有下列情形之一的，终结诉讼：  
（一）原告死亡，没有近亲属或者近亲属放弃诉讼权利的；  
（二）作为原告的法人或者其他组织终止后，其权利义务的承受人放弃诉讼权利的。  
因[本解释第五十一条](#art-51)第一款第（一）、（二）、（三）项原因中止诉讼满90日仍无人继续诉讼的，裁定终结诉讼，但有特殊情况的除外。

**Article 52** During the litigation process, the lawsuit shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The plaintiff dies, and there are no close relatives, or the close relatives waive the right to litigate;  
(2) After the legal person or other organization that is the plaintiff terminates, the person succeeding to its rights and obligations waives the right to litigate.  
If the lawsuit is suspended for 90 days due to the causes specified in subparagraphs (1), (2) or (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 51 of this Interpretation and no one continues the lawsuit, a ruling shall be made to terminate the lawsuit, except where there are special circumstances.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条 复议决定维持原具体行政行为的，人民法院判决撤销原具体行政行为，复议决定自然无效。  
复议决定改变原具体行政行为错误，人民法院判决撤销复议决定时，应当责令复议机关重新作出复议决定。

**Article 53** If a reconsideration decision upholds the original specific administrative act, and the People's Court renders a judgment to annul the original specific administrative act, the reconsideration decision shall become null and void automatically.  
If a reconsideration decision changes the original specific administrative act erroneously, and the People's Court renders a judgment to annul the reconsideration decision, it shall order the reconsideration authority to make a new reconsideration decision.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条 人民法院判决被告重新作出具体行政行为，被告重新作出的具体行政行为与原具体行政行为的结果相同，但主要事实或者主要理由有改变的，不属于[行政诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-55)规定的情形。  
人民法院以违反法定程序为由，判决撤销被诉具体行政行为的，行政机关重新作出具体行政行为不受[行政诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-55)规定的限制。  
行政机关以同一事实和理由重新作出与原具体行政行为基本相同的具体行政行为，人民法院应当根据[行政诉讼法第五十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-54)第（二）项、第五十五条的规定判决撤销或者部分撤销，并根据[行政诉讼法第六十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-65)第三款的规定处理。

**Article 54** Where the People's Court renders a judgment requiring the defendant to make a new specific administrative act, and the new specific administrative act made by the defendant has the same result as the original specific administrative act but the main facts or main reasons have been changed, this shall not fall within the circumstances provided for in Article 55 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If the People's Court renders a judgment to annul the sued specific administrative act on the ground that it violates statutory procedures, the administrative organ's making of a new specific administrative act shall not be subject to the restriction provided for in Article 55 of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
If the administrative organ makes a new specific administrative act that is substantially identical to the original specific administrative act based on the same facts and reasons, the People's Court shall render a judgment to annul or partially annul it in accordance with Article 54(2) and Article 55 of the Administrative Procedure Law, and shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 65(3) of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条 人民法院审理行政案件不得加重对原告的处罚，但利害关系人同为原告的除外。  
人民法院审理行政案件不得对行政机关未予处罚的人直接给予行政处罚。

**Article 55** When trying an administrative case, the People's Court shall not aggravate the penalty imposed on the plaintiff, except where an interested person is also a plaintiff.  
When trying an administrative case, the People's Court shall not directly impose an administrative penalty on a person whom the administrative organ has not penalized.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条 有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当判决驳回原告的诉讼请求：  
（一）起诉被告不作为理由不能成立的；  
（二）被诉具体行政行为合法但存在合理性问题的；  
（三）被诉具体行政行为合法，但因法律、政策变化需要变更或者废止的；  
（四）其他应当判决驳回诉讼请求的情形。

**Article 56** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall render a judgment to dismiss the plaintiff's claims:  
(1) The reasons for suing the defendant for nonfeasance are not established;  
(2) The sued specific administrative act is legal but there is a question of reasonableness;  
(3) The sued specific administrative act is legal, but it needs to be changed or abolished due to changes in laws or policies;  
(4) Other circumstances under which the claims shall be dismissed by judgment.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条 人民法院认为被诉具体行政行为合法，但不适宜判决维持或者驳回诉讼请求的，可以作出确认其合法或者有效的判决。  
有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当作出确认被诉具体行政行为违法或者无效的判决：  
（一）被告不履行法定职责，但判决责令其履行法定职责已无实际意义的；  
（二）被诉具体行政行为违法，但不具有可撤销内容的；  
（三）被诉具体行政行为依法不成立或者无效的。

**Article 57** If the People's Court considers that the sued specific administrative act is legal but it is not appropriate to render a judgment of affirmation or to dismiss the claims, it may render a judgment confirming its legality or validity.  
Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming that the sued specific administrative act is illegal or invalid:  
(1) The defendant fails to perform its statutory duties, but a judgment ordering it to perform such duties is no longer of practical significance;  
(2) The sued specific administrative act is illegal but its content is not revocable;  
(3) The sued specific administrative act is not established or is invalid according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条 被诉具体行政行为违法，但撤销该具体行政行为将会给国家利益或者公共利益造成重大损失的，人民法院应当作出确认被诉具体行政行为违法的判决，并责令被诉行政机关采取相应的补救措施；造成损害的，依法判决承担赔偿责任。

**Article 58** If the sued specific administrative act is illegal but annulling it would cause significant damage to the national interest or the public interest, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming that the sued specific administrative act is illegal, and shall order the sued administrative organ to take corresponding remedial measures; if damage has been caused, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条 根据[行政诉讼法第五十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-54)第（二）项规定判决撤销违法的被诉具体行政行为，将会给国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益造成损失的，人民法院在判决撤销的同时，可以分别采取以下方式处理：  
（一）判决被告重新作出具体行政行为；  
（二）责令被诉行政机关采取相应的补救措施；  
（三）向被告和有关机关提出司法建议；  
（四）发现违法犯罪行为的，建议有权机关依法处理。

**Article 59** If, in rendering a judgment to annul an illegal sued specific administrative act in accordance with Article 54(2) of the Administrative Procedure Law, it will cause damage to the national interest, the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court may, while rendering the annulment judgment, adopt the following methods as appropriate:  
(1) Render a judgment requiring the defendant to make a new specific administrative act;  
(2) Order the sued administrative organ to take corresponding remedial measures;  
(3) Submit a judicial recommendation to the defendant and relevant authorities;  
(4) If any illegal or criminal act is discovered, recommend that the authority with power handle it according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条 人民法院判决被告重新作出具体行政行为，如不及时重新作出具体行政行为，将会给国家利益、公共利益或者当事人利益造成损失的，可以限定重新作出具体行政行为的期限。  
人民法院判决被告履行法定职责，应当指定履行的期限，因情况特殊难于确定期限的除外。

**Article 60** If the People's Court renders a judgment requiring the defendant to make a new specific administrative act, and if a failure to promptly make the new specific administrative act would cause damage to the national interest, the public interest or the interests of the parties, it may set a time limit for making the new specific administrative act.  
If the People's Court renders a judgment requiring the defendant to perform its statutory duties, it shall specify a time limit for performance, except where it is difficult to determine a time limit due to special circumstances.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条 被告对平等主体之间民事争议所作的裁决违法，民事争议当事人要求人民法院一并解决相关民事争议的，人民法院可以一并审理。

**Article 61** If the ruling made by the defendant on a civil dispute between subjects with equal status is illegal, and the parties to the civil dispute request the People's Court to resolve the relevant civil dispute simultaneously, the People's Court may try them together.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条 人民法院审理行政案件，适用最高人民法院司法解释的，应当在裁判文书中援引。  
人民法院审理行政案件，可以在裁判文书中引用合法有效的规章及其他规范性文件。

**Article 62** When trying an administrative case, if the People's Court applies the judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court, it shall cite it in the judgment document.  
When trying an administrative case, the People's Court may cite lawful and effective rules and other normative documents in the judgment document.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条 裁定适用于下列范围：  
（一）不予受理；  
（二）驳回起诉；  
（三）管辖异议；  
（四）终结诉讼；  
（五）中止诉讼；  
（六）移送或者指定管辖；  
（七）诉讼期间停止具体行政行为的执行或者驳回停止执行的申请；  
（八）财产保全；  
（九）先予执行；  
（十）准许或者不准许撤诉；  
（十一）补正裁判文书中的笔误；  
（十二）中止或者终结执行；  
（十三）提审、指令再审或者发回重审，  
（十四）准许或者不准许执行行政机关的具体行政行为；  
（十五）其他需要裁定的事项。  
对第（一）、（二）、（三）项裁定，当事人可以上诉。

**Article 63** Rulings shall apply to the following scope:  
(1) Refusal to accept a case;  
(2) Dismissal of a lawsuit;  
(3) Objection to jurisdiction;  
(4) Termination of a lawsuit;  
(5) Suspension of a lawsuit;  
(6) Transfer or designation of jurisdiction;  
(7) Suspension of the execution of a specific administrative act during the litigation or dismissal of the application for suspension of execution;  
(8) Property preservation;  
(9) Advance execution;  
(10) Permission or denial of withdrawal of the complaint;  
(11) Correction of clerical errors in judgment documents;  
(12) Suspension or termination of execution;  
(13) Initiation of retrial, instruction for retrial, or remand for retrial;  
(14) Permission or denial of execution of a specific administrative act of an administrative organ;  
(15) Other matters requiring rulings.  
Parties may appeal against the rulings specified in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3).

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条 [行政诉讼法第五十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-57)、第六十条规定的审限，是指从立案之日起至裁判宣告之日止的期间。鉴定、处理管辖争议或者异议以及中止诉讼的时间不计算在内。

**Article 64** The trial period provided for in Articles 57 and 60 of the Administrative Procedure Law means the period from the date of case filing to the date of pronouncement of judgment or ruling. The time spent on expert evaluation, handling of jurisdictional disputes or objections, and suspension of the lawsuit shall not be counted.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条 第一审人民法院作出判决和裁定后，当事人均提起上诉的，上诉各方均为上诉人。  
诉讼当事人中的一部分人提出上诉，没有提出上诉的对方当事人为被上诉人，其他当事人依原审诉讼地位列明。

**Article 65** After the People's Court of first instance renders a judgment or ruling, if all parties file an appeal, each appealing party shall be an appellant.  
If only some of the parties to the lawsuit file an appeal, the opposing parties who have not filed an appeal shall be the respondents, and the other parties shall be listed according to their litigation status in the original instance.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条 当事人提出上诉，应当按照其他当事人或者诉讼代表人的人数提出上诉状副本。  
原审人民法院收到上诉状，应当在5日内将上诉状副本送达其他当事人，对方当事人应当在收到上诉状副本之日起10日内提出答辩状。  
原审人民法院应当在收到答辩状之日起5日内将副本送达当事人。  
原审人民法院收到上诉状、答辩状，应当在5日内连同全部案卷和证据，报送第二审人民法院。已经预收诉讼费用的，一并报送。

**Article 66** When filing an appeal, the party shall submit copies of the statement of appeal equal to the number of the other parties or the representatives of action.  
Upon receipt of the statement of appeal, the People's Court of original instance shall, within five days, serve copies of the statement of appeal on the other parties, and the opposing party shall file a written defense within ten days from the date of receipt of the copy of the statement of appeal.  
The People's Court of original instance shall, within five days from the date of receipt of the written defense, serve copies thereof on the parties.  
Upon receipt of the statement of appeal and the written defense, the People's Court of original instance shall, within five days, forward them together with all case files and evidence to the People's Court of second instance. If litigation costs have been prepaid, they shall be forwarded together.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条 第二审人民法院审理上诉案件，应当对原审人民法院的裁判和被诉具体行政行为是否合法进行全面审查。  
当事人对原审人民法院认定的事实有争议的，或者第二审人民法院认为原审人民法院认定事实不清楚的，第二审人民法院应当开庭审理。

**Article 67** When trying an appealed case, the People's Court of second instance shall conduct a full review of the judgment or ruling of the People's Court of original instance and the legality of the sued specific administrative act.  
If the parties dispute the facts found by the People's Court of original instance, or if the People's Court of second instance considers that the facts found by the People's Court of original instance are unclear, the People's Court of second instance shall hold a court session for trial.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条 第二审人民法院经审理认为原审人民法院不予受理或者驳回起诉的裁定确有错误，且起诉符合法定条件的，应当裁定撤销原审人民法院的裁定，指令原审人民法院依法立案受理或者继续审理。

**Article 68** If, upon trial, the People's Court of second instance considers that the ruling of the People's Court of original instance to refuse to accept the case or dismiss the lawsuit is indeed erroneous, and that the lawsuit meets the statutory conditions, it shall render a ruling to annul the ruling of the People's Court of original instance, and instruct the People's Court of original instance to docket the case for trial or continue the trial according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条 第二审人民法院裁定发回原审人民法院重新审理的行政案件，原审人民法院应当另行组成合议庭进行审理。

**Article 69** If the People's Court of second instance renders a ruling remanding an administrative case to the People's Court of original instance for retrial, the People's Court of original instance shall form a new collegial panel for trial.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条 第二审人民法院审理上诉案件，需要改变原审判决的，应当同时对被诉具体行政行为作出判决。

**Article 70** When trying an appealed case, if the People's Court of second instance needs to change the judgment of original instance, it shall simultaneously render a judgment on the sued specific administrative act.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条 原审判决遗漏了必须参加诉讼的当事人或者诉讼请求的，第二审人民法院应当裁定撤销原审判决，发回重审。  
原审判决遗漏行政赔偿请求，第二审人民法院经审查认为依法不应当予以赔偿的，应当判决驳回行政赔偿请求。  
原审判决遗漏行政赔偿请求，第二审人民法院经审理认为依法应当予以赔偿的，在确认被诉具体行政行为违法的同时，可以就行政赔偿问题进行调解；调解不成的，应当就行政赔偿部分发回重审。  
当事人在第二审期间提出行政赔偿请求的，第二审人民法院可以进行调解；调解不成的，应当告知当事人另行起诉。

**Article 71** If the judgment of original instance omits a party that must participate in the lawsuit or a claim, the People's Court of second instance shall render a ruling to annul the original judgment and remand for retrial.  
If the judgment of original instance omits a claim for administrative compensation, and upon review the People's Court of second instance considers that compensation shall not be awarded according to law, it shall render a judgment dismissing the claim for administrative compensation.  
If the judgment of original instance omits a claim for administrative compensation, and upon trial the People's Court of second instance considers that compensation shall be awarded according to law, it may, while confirming that the sued specific administrative act is illegal, conduct mediation on the issue of administrative compensation; if mediation fails, it shall remand the part concerning administrative compensation for retrial.  
If a party raises a claim for administrative compensation during the second instance, the People's Court of second instance may conduct mediation; if mediation fails, it shall inform the party to file a separate lawsuit.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条 有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第六十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-63)规定的“违反法律、法规规定”：  
（一）原判决、裁定认定的事实主要证据不足；  
（二）原判决、裁定适用法律、法规确有错误；  
（三）违反法定程序，可能影响案件正确裁判；  
（四）其他违反法律、法规的情形。

**Article 72** Any of the following circumstances shall fall within the meaning of "violating the provisions of laws or regulations" as provided for in Article 63 of the Administrative Procedure Law:  
(1) The facts found in the original judgment or ruling lack main evidence;  
(2) There is a definite error in the application of laws or regulations in the original judgment or ruling;  
(3) The statutory procedure is violated, which may affect the correct adjudication of the case;  
(4) Other circumstances violating laws or regulations.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条 当事人申请再审，应当在判决、裁定发生法律效力后2年内提出。  
当事人对已经发生法律效力的行政赔偿调解书，提出证据证明调解违反自愿原则或者调解协议的内容违反法律规定的，可以在2年内申请再审。

**Article 73** A party applying for retrial shall file the application within two years after the judgment or ruling becomes legally effective.  
If a party presents evidence proving that a legally effective mediation agreement for administrative compensation was reached in violation of the principle of voluntariness or that its content violates the provisions of law, it may apply for retrial within two years.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条 人民法院接到当事人的再审申请后，经审查，符合再审条件的，应当立案并及时通知各方当事人；不符合再审条件的，予以驳回。

**Article 74** Upon receipt of a party's application for retrial, the People's Court shall, after examination, docket the case and promptly notify all parties if the conditions for retrial are satisfied; if the conditions are not satisfied, it shall reject the application.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条 对人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的案件，人民法院应当再审。  
人民法院开庭审理抗诉案件时，应当通知人民检察院派员出庭。

**Article 75** With respect to a case in which the People's Procuratorate files a protest in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, the People's Court shall conduct a retrial.  
When the People's Court holds a court session to try a protested case, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate to send personnel to appear in court.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条 人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的案件，发生法律效力的判决、裁定是由第一审人民法院作出的，按照第一审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定，当事人可以上诉；发生法律效力的判决、裁定是由第二审人民法院作出的，按照第二审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定是发生法律效力的判决、裁定；上级人民法院按照审判监督程序提审的，按照第二审程序审理，所作的判决、裁定是发生法律效力的判决、裁定。  
人民法院审理再审案件，应当另行组成合议庭。

**Article 76** For a case retried by a People's Court in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, if the legally effective judgment or ruling was made by a People's Court of first instance, it shall be tried in accordance with the procedure of first instance, and the parties may appeal against the judgment or ruling rendered; if the legally effective judgment or ruling was made by a People's Court of second instance, it shall be tried in accordance with the procedure of second instance, and the judgment or ruling rendered shall be a legally effective judgment or ruling; if a higher People's Court initiates a retrial in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, it shall be tried in accordance with the procedure of second instance, and the judgment or ruling rendered shall be a legally effective judgment or ruling.  
When trying a retrial case, the People's Court shall form a new collegial panel.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条 按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，应当裁定中止原判决的执行；裁定由院长署名，加盖人民法院印章。  
上级人民法院决定提审或者指令下级人民法院再审的，应当作出裁定，裁定应当写明中止原判决的执行；情况紧急的，可以将中止执行的裁定口头通知负责执行的人民法院或者作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院，但应当在口头通知后10日内发出裁定书。

**Article 77** For a case decided to be retried in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, a ruling shall be made to suspend the execution of the original judgment; the ruling shall be signed by the president and affixed with the seal of the People's Court.  
If a higher People's Court decides to initiate a retrial itself or instruct a lower People's Court to conduct a retrial, it shall make a ruling, and the ruling shall specify the suspension of the execution of the original judgment; in emergency circumstances, the ruling to suspend execution may be notified orally to the People's Court responsible for enforcement or the People's Court that made the effective judgment or ruling, but the written ruling shall be issued within ten days after the oral notification.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条 人民法院审理再审案件，认为原生效判决、裁定确有错误，在撤销原生效判决或者裁定的同时，可以对生效判决、裁定的内容作出相应裁判，也可以裁定撤销生效判决或者裁定，发回作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院重新审判。

**Article 78** If a People's Court, when trying a retrial case, considers that the original effective judgment or ruling is indeed erroneous, it may, while annulling the original effective judgment or ruling, render a corresponding judgment or ruling on the content of the effective judgment or ruling, or may render a ruling to annul the effective judgment or ruling and remand the case to the People's Court that made the effective judgment or ruling for retrial.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条 人民法院审理二审案件和再审案件，对原审法院受理、不予受理或者驳回起诉错误的，应当分别情况作如下处理：  
（一）第一审人民法院作出实体判决后，第二审人民法院认为不应当受理的，在撤销第一审人民法院判决的同时，可以发回重审，也可以迳行驳回起诉；  
（二）第二审人民法院维持第一审人民法院不予受理裁定错误的，再审法院应当撤销第一审、第二审人民法院裁定，指令第一审人民法院受理；  
（三）第二审人民法院维持第一审人民法院驳回起诉裁定错误的，再审法院应当撤销第一审、第二审人民法院裁定，指令第一审人民法院审理。

**Article 79** Where a People's Court, when trying a case of second instance or a retrial case, finds that the court of original instance was erroneous in accepting, refusing to accept, or dismissing the lawsuit, it shall handle the matter as follows according to the specific circumstances:  
(1) If the People's Court of first instance renders a substantive judgment, and the People's Court of second instance considers that the case should not have been accepted, it may, while annulling the judgment of the People's Court of first instance, remand the case for retrial or directly dismiss the lawsuit;  
(2) If the People's Court of second instance erroneously upholds the People's Court of first instance's ruling to refuse to accept the case, the retrial court shall annul the rulings of both the People's Court of first instance and the People's Court of second instance, and instruct the People's Court of first instance to accept the case;  
(3) If the People's Court of second instance erroneously upholds the People's Court of first instance's ruling to dismiss the lawsuit, the retrial court shall annul the rulings of both the People's Court of first instance and the People's Court of second instance, and instruct the People's Court of first instance to try the case.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条 人民法院审理再审案件，发现生效裁判有下列情形之一的，应当裁定发回作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院重新审理：  
（一）审理本案的审判人员、书记员应当回避而未回避的；  
（二）依法应当开庭审理而未经开庭即作出判决的；  
（三）未经合法传唤当事人而缺席判决的；  
（四）遗漏必须参加诉讼的当事人的；  
（五）对与本案有关的诉讼请求未予裁判的；  
（六）其他违反法定程序可能影响案件正确裁判的。

**Article 80** Where a People's Court, when trying a retrial case, discovers that the effective judgment or ruling has any of the following circumstances, it shall render a ruling to remand the case to the People's Court that made the effective judgment or ruling for retrial:  
(1) The judges or court clerks who tried the case should have withdrawn but failed to do so;  
(2) A judgment was made without a court session when a court session should have been held according to law;  
(3) A default judgment was made without legally summoning the parties;  
(4) A party that must participate in the lawsuit was omitted;  
(5) Claims related to the case were not adjudicated;  
(6) Other violations of statutory procedures that may affect the correct adjudication of the case.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条 再审案件按照第一审程序审理的，适用[行政诉讼法第五十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-57)规定的审理期限。  
再审案件按照第二审程序审理的，适用[行政诉讼法第六十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-60)规定的审理期限。

**Article 81** If a retrial case is tried in accordance with the procedure of first instance, the trial period provided for in Article 57 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall apply.  
If a retrial case is tried in accordance with the procedure of second instance, the trial period provided for in Article 60 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall apply.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条 基层人民法院申请延长审理期限，应当直接报请高级人民法院批准，同时报中级人民法院备案。  
七、执行

**Article 82** If a Primary People's Court applies for an extension of the trial period, it shall directly submit a request to the Higher People's Court for approval, and shall simultaneously report to the Intermediate People's Court for record.  
VII. Enforcement

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条 对发生法律效力的行政判决书、行政裁定书、行政赔偿判决书和行政赔偿调解书，负有义务的一方当事人拒绝履行的，对方当事人可以：现依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 83** If a party that bears obligations under a legally effective administrative judgment, administrative ruling, administrative compensation judgment or administrative compensation mediation agreement refuses to perform, the other party may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution according to law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条 申请人是公民的，申请执行生效的行政判决书、行政裁定书、行政赔偿判决书和行政赔偿调解书的期限为1年，申请人是行政机关、法人或者其他组织的为180日。  
申请执行的期限从法律文书规定的履行期间最后一日起计算；法律文书中没有规定履行期限的，从该法律文书送达当事人之日起计算。  
逾期申请的，除有正当理由外，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 84** If the applicant is a citizen, the time limit for applying for execution of an effective administrative judgment, administrative ruling, administrative compensation judgment or administrative compensation mediation agreement shall be one year; if the applicant is an administrative organ, legal person or other organization, the time limit shall be 180 days.  
The time limit for applying for execution shall be calculated from the last day of the performance period specified in the legal document; if no performance period is specified in the legal document, it shall be calculated from the date on which the legal document is served on the parties.  
If an application is filed beyond the time limit, the People's Court shall not accept it, except where there are justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条 发生法律效力的行政判决书、行政裁定书、行政赔偿判决书和行政赔偿调解书，由第一审人民法院执行。  
第一审人民法院认为情况特殊需要由第二审人民法院执行的，可以报请第二审人民法院执行；第二审人民法院可以决定由其执行，也可以决定由第一审人民法院执行。

**Article 85** A legally effective administrative judgment, administrative ruling, administrative compensation judgment or administrative compensation mediation agreement shall be enforced by the People's Court of first instance.  
If the People's Court of first instance considers that due to special circumstances it is necessary for the People's Court of second instance to enforce the document, it may submit a request to the People's Court of second instance for enforcement; the People's Court of second instance may decide to enforce it by itself or may decide that the People's Court of first instance shall enforce it.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条 行政机关根据[行政诉讼法第六十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-66)的规定申请执行其具体行政行为，应当具备以下条件：  
（一）具体行政行为依法可以由人民法院执行；  
（二）具体行政行为已经生效并具有可执行内容；  
（三）申请人是作出该具体行政行为的行政机关或者法律、法规、规章授权的组织；  
（四）被申请人是该具体行政行为所确定的义务人；  
（五）被申请人在具体行政行为确定的期限内或者行政机关另行指定的期限内未履行义务；  
（六）申请人在法定期限内提出申请；  
（七）被申请执行的行政案件属于受理申请执行的人民法院管辖。  
人民法院对符合条件的申请，应当立案受理，并通知申请人；对不符合条件的申请，应当裁定不予受理。

**Article 86** An administrative organ applying for execution of its specific administrative act in accordance with Article 66 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The specific administrative act may be enforced by a People's Court according to law;  
(2) The specific administrative act has taken effect and has enforceable content;  
(3) The applicant is the administrative organ that made the specific administrative act or an organization authorized by laws, regulations or rules;  
(4) The respondent is the obligor determined in the specific administrative act;  
(5) The respondent has failed to perform the obligation within the time limit specified in the specific administrative act or within another time limit specified by the administrative organ;  
(6) The applicant files the application within the statutory time limit;  
(7) The administrative case subject to the application for execution falls within the jurisdiction of the People's Court accepting the application.  
The People's Court shall docket and accept the application that meets the conditions, and shall notify the applicant; for an application that does not meet the conditions, it shall render a ruling of refusal to accept.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条 法律、法规没有赋予行政机关强制执行权，行政机关申请人民法院强制执行的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
法律、法规规定既可以由行政机关依法强制执行，也可以申请人民法院强制执行，行政机关申请人民法院强制执行的，人民法院可以依法受理。

**Article 87** If the laws or regulations do not grant the administrative organ the power to enforce execution, and the administrative organ applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution, the People's Court shall accept the application according to law.  
If the laws or regulations provide that the administrative organ may enforce execution according to law or may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution, and the administrative organ applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution, the People's Court may accept the application according to law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条 行政机关申请人民法院强制执行其具体行政行为，应当自被执行人的法定起诉期限届满之日起180日内提出。逾期申请的。除有正当理由外，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 88** An administrative organ applying to the People's Court for compulsory execution of its specific administrative act shall file the application within 180 days from the date on which the statutory time limit for the person subject to enforcement to institute an action expires. If the application is filed beyond the time limit, the People's Court shall not accept it, except where there are justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条 行政机关申请人民法院强制执行其具体行政行为，由申请人所在地的基层人民法院受理；执行对象为不动产的，由不动产所在地的基层人民法院受理。基层人民法院认为执行确有困难的，可以报请上级人民法院执行；上级人民法院可以决定由其执行，也可以决定由下级人民法院执行。

**Article 89** Where an administrative organ applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution of its specific administrative act, the case shall be accepted by the Primary People's Court in the locality of the applicant; if the object of execution is immovable property, the case shall be accepted by the Primary People's Court in the locality of the immovable property. If the Primary People's Court considers execution truly difficult, it may report to a higher People's Court for execution; the higher People's Court may decide to execute by itself, or may decide to have a lower People's Court execute.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条 行政机关根据法律的授权对平等主体之间民事争议作出裁决后，当事人在法定期限内不起诉又不履行，作出裁决的行政机关在申请执行的期限内未申请人民法院强制执行的，生效具体行政行为确定的权利人或者其继承人、权利承受人在90日内可以申请人民法院强制执行。  
享有权利的公民、法人或者其他组织申请人民法院强制执行具体行政行为，参照行政机关申请人民法院强制执行具体行政行为的规定。

**Article 90** After an administrative organ, pursuant to the authorization of law, makes an adjudication on a civil dispute between equal subjects, if the parties neither file a lawsuit nor perform within the statutory time limit, and the administrative organ that made the adjudication fails to apply to a People's Court for compulsory execution within the time limit for applying for execution, the right holder determined by the effective specific administrative act or his successor or assignee of rights may, within 90 days, apply to a People's Court for compulsory execution.  
Where a citizen, legal person or other organization enjoying the right applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution of a specific administrative act, the provisions on an administrative organ applying to a People's Court for compulsory execution of its specific administrative act shall be referred to.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条 行政机关申请人民法院强制执行其具体行政行为，应当提交申请执行书、据以执行的行政法律文书、证明该具体行政行为合法的材料和被执行人财产状况以及其他必须提交的材料。  
享有权利的公民、法人或者其他组织申请人民法院强制执行的，人民法院应当向作出裁决的行政机关调取有关材料。

**Article 91** Where an administrative organ applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution of its specific administrative act, it shall submit a written application for execution, the administrative legal instrument on which the execution is based, materials proving the legality of the specific administrative act, the property status of the person subject to execution, and other materials that must be submitted.  
Where a citizen, legal person or other organization enjoying the right applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution, the People's Court shall obtain relevant materials from the administrative organ that made the adjudication.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条 行政机关或者具体行政行为确定的权利人申请人民法院强制执行前，有充分理由认为被执行人可能逃避执行的，可以申请人民法院采取财产保全措施。后者申请强制执行的，应当提供相应的财产担保。

**Article 92** Before an administrative organ or the right holder determined by a specific administrative act applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution, if there is sufficient reason to believe that the person subject to execution may evade execution, the applicant may apply to the People's Court for adopting property preservation measures. Where the latter applies for compulsory execution, it shall provide corresponding property guarantee.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条 人民法院受理行政机关申请执行其具体行政行为的案件后，应当在30日内由行政审判庭组成合议庭对具体行政行为的合法性进行审查，并就是否准予强制执行作出裁定；需要采取强制执行措施的，由本院负责强制执行非诉行政行为的机构执行。

**Article 93** After a People's Court accepts a case where an administrative organ applies for execution of its specific administrative act, it shall, within 30 days, form a collegial panel in the administrative adjudication division to examine the legality of the specific administrative act, and render a ruling on whether to grant compulsory execution; if compulsory execution measures need to be taken, the organ of the People's Court responsible for compulsory execution of non-litigation administrative acts shall execute.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条 在诉讼过程中，被告或者具体行政行为确定的权利人申请人民法院强制执行被诉具体行政行为，人民法院不予执行，但不及时执行可能给国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益造成不可弥补的损失的，人民法院可以先予执行。后者申请强制执行的，应当提供相应的财产担保。

**Article 94** During litigation, if the defendant or the right holder determined by the specific administrative act applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution of the specific administrative act under litigation, the People's Court shall not execute, unless failure to execute in time may cause irreparable damage to national interests, public interests or the lawful rights and interests of others, in which case the People's Court may render advance execution. Where the latter applies for compulsory execution, it shall provide corresponding property guarantee.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条 被申请执行的具体行政行为有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不准予执行：  
（一）明显缺乏事实根据的；  
（二）明显缺乏法律依据的；  
（三）其他明显违法并损害被执行人合法权益的。

**Article 95** Where the specific administrative act applied for execution falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule that execution shall not be granted:  
(1) It obviously lacks factual basis;  
(2) It obviously lacks legal basis;  
(3) There are other obvious illegalities that damage the lawful rights and interests of the person subject to execution.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条 行政机关拒绝履行人民法院生效判决、裁定的，人民法院可以依照[行政诉讼法第六十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-65)第三款的规定处理，并可以参照[民事诉讼法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的有关规定，对主要负责人或者直接责任人员予以罚款处罚。  
八、其他

**Article 96** Where an administrative organ refuses to perform an effective judgment or ruling of a People's Court, the People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 65, paragraph 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law, and may, by referring to the relevant provisions of Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law, impose a fine on the principal responsible person or the directly responsible person.  
VIII. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条 人民法院审理行政案件，除依照行政诉讼法和本解释外，可以参照民事诉讼的有关规定。

**Article 97** When a People's Court hears an administrative case, in addition to applying the Administrative Procedure Law and this Interpretation, it may refer to the relevant provisions of civil actions.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条 本解释自发布之日起施行，最高人民法院《关于贯彻执行〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的意见（试行）》同时废止；最高人民法院以前所作的司法解释以及与有关机关联合发布的规范性文件，凡与本解释不一致的，按本解释执行。

**Article 98** This Interpretation shall come into force as of the date of promulgation, and the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Implementation of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) shall be abolished simultaneously; any judicial interpretations previously made by the Supreme People's Court and any normative documents jointly issued with relevant authorities that are inconsistent with this Interpretation shall be executed in accordance with this Interpretation.
