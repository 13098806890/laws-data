# 最高人民法院关于审理人民法院国家赔偿确认案件若干问题的规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕10号  
**公布日期**：2004-05-18  
**生效日期**：2004-10-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理人民法院国家赔偿确认案件若干问题的规定（试行）》已于2004年5月18日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1315次会议通过。现予公布，自2004年10月1日起施行。 2004年8月10日 最高人民法院 关于审理人民法院国家赔偿确认案件 若干问题的规定（试行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 公民、法人或者其他组织认为人民法院及其工作人员的职务行为侵犯其合法权益提起国家赔偿请求的，除[本规定第五条](#art-5)规定的情形外，应当依法先行申请确认。

**Article 1** Where a citizen, legal person or other organization claims that an official act of a People's Court or its staff has infringed upon their lawful rights and interests and files a state compensation claim, unless the circumstances fall under those specified in Article 5 of these Provisions, they shall first apply for confirmation according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 公民、法人或者其他组织认为人民法院及其工作人员违法行使职权，申请确认的是确认申请人。  
申请确认由作出司法行为的人民法院受理，但申请确认基层人民法院司法行为违法的案件，由中级人民法院受理。

**Article 2** A citizen, legal person or other organization that claims that a People's Court or its staff has illegally exercised their functions and powers and applies for confirmation shall be the confirmation applicant.  
The application for confirmation shall be accepted by the People's Court that made the judicial act; provided that an application for confirmation that the judicial act of a Primary People's Court was illegal shall be accepted by an Intermediate People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 具备下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）确认申请人应当具有[《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》第十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-18)规定的国家赔偿请求人资格；  
（二）有具体的确认请求和损害事实、理由；  
（三）确认申请人申请确认应当在司法行为发生或者知道、应当知道司法行为发生之日起两年内提出；  
（四）属于受理确认申请的人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** Where the following conditions are met, the case shall be docketed:  
(1) The confirmation applicant shall have the qualifications of a state compensation claimant as provided in Article 18 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) There are specific confirmation claims and facts of damage and reasons;  
(3) The confirmation applicant shall file the application for confirmation within two years from the date on which the judicial act occurred or the date the applicant knew or should have known that the judicial act occurred;  
(4) The case falls within the jurisdiction of the People's Court that accepts the confirmation application.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 具有下列情形之一的确认申请，不予受理：  
（一）依法应当通过审判监督程序提出申诉或者申请再审的；  
（二）申请事项属于司法机关已经立案正在查处的；  
（三）人民法院工作人员的行为与行使职权无关的；  
（四）属于[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-214)规定情形的；  
（五）依法不属于确认范围的其他情形。

**Article 4** A confirmation application in any of the following circumstances shall not be accepted:  
(1) Where a petition or an application for retrial shall be filed through the adjudication supervision procedure according to law;  
(2) Where the matter applied for falls under a case that has been docketed and is being investigated by a judicial organ;  
(3) Where the act of a People's Court staff is unrelated to the exercise of functions and powers;  
(4) Where the circumstances fall under those specified in Article 214 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(5) Other circumstances that do not fall within the scope of confirmation according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院作出的下列情形之一的判决、裁定、决定，属于依法确认，当事人可以根据该判决、裁定、决定提出国家赔偿申请：  
（一）逮捕决定已经依法撤销的，但[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-15)规定的情形除外；  
（二）判决宣告无罪并已发生法律效力的；  
（三）实施了[国家赔偿法第十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-15)第（四）、（五）项规定的行为责任人员已被依法追究的；  
（四）实施了[国家赔偿法第十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-16)第（一）项规定行为，并已依法作出撤销决定的；  
（五）依法撤销违法司法拘留、罚款、财产保全、执行裁定、决定的；  
（六）对违法行为予以纠正的其他情形。

**Article 5** A judgment, ruling or decision rendered by a People's Court in any of the following circumstances shall be deemed a confirmation according to law, and the party may file a state compensation application based on such judgment, ruling or decision:  
(1) An arrest decision has been revoked according to law, except for the circumstances specified in Article 15 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) A judgment of acquittal has been rendered and has become legally effective;  
(3) The persons responsible for the acts specified in Item (4) and (5) of Article 15 of the State Compensation Law have been held accountable according to law;  
(4) An act specified in Item (1) of Article 16 of the State Compensation Law has been committed, and a decision to revoke it has been made according to law;  
(5) An illegal judicial detention, fine, property preservation, enforcement ruling or decision has been revoked according to law;  
(6) Other circumstances in which an illegal act has been corrected.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院应当在收到确认申请之日起七日内决定是否立案。  
审查立案时，发现缺少相关证据的，可以通知确认申请人七日内予以补充。

**Article 6** A People's Court shall decide whether to docket the case within seven days from the date of receipt of the confirmation application.  
When reviewing for docketing, if it discovers that relevant evidence is lacking, it may notify the confirmation applicant to supplement it within seven days.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 确认申请人对不予受理决定不服的，可以在收到不予受理决定书之日起十五日内，向上一级人民法院申请复议。  
上一级人民法院应当在收到复议申请之日起三十日内作出是否受理的决定。

**Article 7** Where a confirmation applicant refuses to accept a decision not to accept the case, they may apply for review to the next higher People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the written decision not to accept.  
The next higher People's Court shall make a decision on whether to accept the case within thirty days from the date of receipt of the review application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院审理确认案件应当组成合议庭。

**Article 8** A People's Court shall form a collegial panel to hear a confirmation case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院审理确认案件，应当审查以下内容：  
（一）被申请确认的损害事实是否存在；  
（二）人民法院原作出司法行为的理由及依据；  
（三）人民法院原行使职权的行为是否符合法定程序、原行使的职权适用法律是否正确；  
（四）其他需要审查的内容。

**Article 9** When hearing a confirmation case, a People's Court shall examine the following:  
(1) Whether the facts of damage for which confirmation is sought exist;  
(2) The reasons and basis for the People's Court's original judicial act;  
(3) Whether the People's Court's original exercise of functions and powers complied with statutory procedures and whether the law applied in the original exercise of functions and powers was correct;  
(4) Other matters that need to be examined.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院审理确认案件可以进行书面审理，根据案件的具体情况可以进行听证。是否听证由合议庭决定。

**Article 10** A People's Court may conduct documentary examination in hearing a confirmation case, and may conduct a hearing based on the specific circumstances of the case. Whether to hold a hearing shall be decided by the collegial panel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 被申请确认的案件在原审判、执行过程中，具有下列情形之一的，应当确认违法：  
（一）人民法院决定逮捕的犯罪嫌疑人没有犯罪事实或者事实不清、证据不足，释放后，未依法撤销逮捕决定的；  
（二）查封、扣押、冻结、追缴与刑事案件无关的合法财产，并造成损害的；  
（三）违反法律规定对没有实施妨害诉讼行为的人、被执行人、协助执行人等，采取或者重复采取拘传、拘留、罚款等强制措施，且未依法撤销的；  
（四）司法拘留超过法律规定或者决定书确定的期限的；  
（五）超过法定金额实施司法罚款的；  
（六）违反法律规定采取或者解除保全措施，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（七）超标的查封、扣押、冻结、变卖或者执行确认申请人可分割的财产，给申请人造成损害的；  
（八）违反法律规定，重复查封、扣押、冻结确认申请人财产，给申请人造成损害的；  
（九）对查封、扣押的财物故意不履行监管职责，发生灭失或者其他严重后果，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（十）对已经发现的被执行人的财产，故意拖延执行或者不执行，导致被执行的财产流失，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（十一）对应当恢复执行的案件不予恢复执行，导致被执行的财产流失，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（十二）没有法律依据将案件执行款物执行给其他当事人或者案外人，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（十三）违法查封、扣押、执行案外人财产，给案外人造成损害的；  
（十四）对依法应当拍卖的财产未拍卖，强行将财产变卖或者以物抵债，给确认申请人造成损害的；  
（十五）违反法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 11** Where a case subject to confirmation has any of the following circumstances in the original adjudication or enforcement process, it shall be confirmed as illegal:  
(1) A criminal suspect whose arrest was decided by a People's Court has no criminal facts or the facts are unclear or evidence is insufficient, and after release, the arrest decision has not been revoked according to law;  
(2) Lawful property unrelated to the criminal case has been sealed up, seized, frozen, or recovered, causing damage;  
(3) In violation of legal provisions, compulsory measures such as compulsory summons, detention, or fine have been taken or repeatedly taken against a person who has not committed an act obstructing litigation, a judgment debtor, an assistance enforcer, etc., and have not been revoked according to law;  
(4) Judicial detention exceeds the period provided by law or specified in the written decision;  
(5) A judicial fine exceeding the statutory amount has been imposed;  
(6) Preservation measures have been taken or lifted in violation of legal provisions, causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(7) The property of the confirmation applicant that is divisible has been sealed up, seized, frozen, sold off, or enforced beyond the scope of the claim, causing damage to the applicant;  
(8) In violation of legal provisions, the property of the confirmation applicant has been repeatedly sealed up, seized, or frozen, causing damage to the applicant;  
(9) The supervisory duty over sealed up or seized property has been intentionally neglected, resulting in loss or other serious consequences, causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(10) The enforcement of the discovered property of the judgment debtor has been intentionally delayed or not carried out, causing the property subject to enforcement to be lost, thereby causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(11) A case that should be resumed for enforcement has not been resumed, causing the property subject to enforcement to be lost, thereby causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(12) Without legal basis, the property or money subject to enforcement in the case has been enforced to another party or a person not involved in the case, causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(13) The property of a person not involved in the case has been illegally sealed up, seized, or enforced, causing damage to such person;  
(14) Property that should be auctioned according to law has not been auctioned, and the property has been forcibly sold off or used to offset the debt, causing damage to the confirmation applicant;  
(15) Other circumstances that violate legal provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院确认或者不予确认违法行使职权的，应当制作裁定书。确认违法的，应同时撤销原违法裁决。  
人民法院对本院司法行为是否违法作出的裁定书由院长署名；上级人民法院对下级人民法院司法行为是否违法作出的裁定书由合议庭署名。

**Article 12** Where a People's Court confirms or does not confirm that functions and powers have been illegally exercised, it shall issue a written ruling. If it confirms the illegality, it shall at the same time revoke the original illegal decision.  
A written ruling made by a People's Court on whether its own judicial act is illegal shall be signed by the president of the court; a written ruling made by a higher People's Court on whether a lower People's Court's judicial act is illegal shall be signed by the collegial panel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 人民法院审理确认案件，应当自送达受理通知书之日起六个月内作出裁定。需要延长期限的，报请本院院长批准可以延期三个月。

**Article 13** A People's Court shall render a ruling within six months from the date of service of the notice of acceptance of the case. If an extension is necessary, it may be extended by up to three months upon approval by the president of the court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 确认申请人对人民法院受理确认申请后，超过审理期限未作出裁决的，可以在期满后三十日内向上一级人民法院提出书面申诉。  
上一级人民法院应当在收到确认申诉书之日起三个月内指令下级人民法院限期作出裁定或者自行审理。自行审理需要延长期限的，报请本院院长批准可以延期三个月。

**Article 14** Where a People's Court fails to render a ruling within the trial period after accepting a confirmation application, the confirmation applicant may file a written petition with the next higher People's Court within thirty days after the expiration of the period.  
The next higher People's Court shall, within three months from the date of receipt of the written confirmation petition, order the lower People's Court to render a ruling within a specified period or hear the case itself. If an extension is necessary for hearing the case itself, it may be extended by up to three months upon approval by the president of that court.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 上级人民法院审理确认案件举行听证的，下级人民法院应当参加听证。  
确认申请人无正当理由不参加听证的，视为撤回确认申请。

**Article 15** Where a higher People's Court holds a hearing in hearing a confirmation case, the lower People's Court shall participate in the hearing.  
If the confirmation applicant fails to participate in the hearing without justifiable reasons, the application for confirmation shall be deemed withdrawn.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 原作出司法行为的人民法院有义务对其行为的合法性作出说明。

**Article 16** The People's Court that originally made the judicial act has the obligation to explain the legality of its act.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 确认申请人对人民法院作出的不予确认违法的裁定不服，可以在收到裁定书之日起三十日内向上一级人民法院提出申诉。  
上一级人民法院应当在收到确认申诉书之日起三个月内作出确认或者不予确认的裁定。需要延长期限的，报请本院院长批准可以延期三个月。

**Article 17** Where a confirmation applicant refuses to accept a ruling made by a People's Court that does not confirm the illegality, they may file a petition with the next higher People's Court within thirty days from the date of receipt of the written ruling.  
The next higher People's Court shall render a ruling confirming or not confirming the illegality within three months from the date of receipt of the written confirmation petition. If an extension is necessary, it may be extended by up to three months upon approval by the president of that court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 最高人民法院对各级人民法院、上级人民法院对下级人民法院作出的确认裁定认为确有错误的，可以直接作出确认，也可以指令下级人民法院或者其他同级人民法院重新确认。

**Article 18** Where the Supreme People's Court considers that a confirmation ruling made by a People's Court at any level, or a higher People's Court considers that a confirmation ruling made by a lower People's Court is indeed erroneous, it may directly make a confirmation or instruct the lower People's Court or another People's Court at the same level to make a new confirmation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 本规定发布前的司法解释，与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。

**Article 19** Where any judicial interpretation issued before the issuance of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 本规定自2004年10月1日起施行。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 2004.
