# 最高人民法院关于国家赔偿案件立案工作的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕1号  
**公布日期**：2011-12-26  
**生效日期**：2012-02-15

> 最高人民法院《关于国家赔偿案件立案工作的规定》己于2011年12月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1537次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年2月15日起施行。 2012年1月13日 最高人民法院 关于国家赔偿案件立案工作的规定 法释〔2012〕1号 为保障公民、法人和其他组织依法行使请求国家赔偿的权利，保证人民法院及时、准确审查受理国家赔偿案件，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》及有关法律规定，现就人民法院国家赔偿案件立案工作规定如下： 第一条 本规定所称国家赔偿案件，是指国家赔偿法第十七条、第十八条、第二十一条、第三十八条规定的下列案件： （一）违反刑事诉讼法的规定对公民采取拘留措施的，或者依照刑事诉讼法规定的条件和程序对公民采取拘留措施，但是拘留时间超过刑事诉讼法规定的时限，其后决定撤销案件、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪终止追究刑事责任的； （二）对公民采取逮捕措施后，决定撤销案件、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪终止追究刑事责任的； （三）二审改判无罪，以及二审发回重审后作无罪处理的； （四）依照审判监督程序再审改判无罪，原判刑罚已经执行的； （五）刑讯逼供或者以殴打、虐待等行为或者唆使、放纵他人以殴打、虐待等行为造成公民身体伤害或者死亡的； （六）违法使用武器、警械造成公民身体伤害或者死亡的； （七）在刑事诉讼过程中违法对财产采取查封、扣押、冻结、追缴等措施的； （八）依照审判监督程序再审改判无罪，原判罚金、没收财产已经执行的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 本规定所称国家赔偿案件，是指[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)、第十八条、第二十一条、第三十八条规定的下列案件：  
（一）违反刑事诉讼法的规定对公民采取拘留措施的，或者依照刑事诉讼法规定的条件和程序对公民采取拘留措施，但是拘留时间超过刑事诉讼法规定的时限，其后决定撤销案件、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪终止追究刑事责任的；  
（二）对公民采取逮捕措施后，决定撤销案件、不起诉或者判决宣告无罪终止追究刑事责任的；  
（三）二审改判无罪，以及二审发回重审后作无罪处理的；  
（四）依照审判监督程序再审改判无罪，原判刑罚已经执行的；  
（五）刑讯逼供或者以殴打、虐待等行为或者唆使、放纵他人以殴打、虐待等行为造成公民身体伤害或者死亡的；  
（六）违法使用武器、警械造成公民身体伤害或者死亡的；  
（七）在刑事诉讼过程中违法对财产采取查封、扣押、冻结、追缴等措施的；  
（八）依照审判监督程序再审改判无罪，原判罚金、没收财产已经执行的；  
（九）在民事诉讼、行政诉讼过程中，违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全措施或者对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，造成损害的。

**Article 1** For the purpose of these Provisions, "state compensation cases" refer to the following cases as prescribed in Articles 17, 18, 21 and 38 of the State Compensation Law:  
(1) Where compulsory detention measures are taken against a citizen in violation of the Criminal Procedure Law, or where compulsory detention measures are taken against a citizen in accordance with the conditions and procedures prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law but the detention period exceeds the time limit prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law, and the case is subsequently dismissed, or a decision is made not to prosecute, or a judgment of acquittal is rendered, thereby terminating the investigation of criminal liability;  
(2) Where after arrest measures are taken against a citizen, the case is dismissed, or a decision is made not to prosecute, or a judgment of acquittal is rendered, thereby terminating the investigation of criminal liability;  
(3) Where the judgment is changed to acquittal upon second instance, or where a judgment of acquittal is rendered after retrial upon remand by the court of second instance;  
(4) Where the judgment is changed to acquittal upon retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, and the original sentence has been executed;  
(5) Where extorting confessions by torture, or committing acts such as battery or abuse, or instigating or tolerating others to commit such acts, causes bodily injury or death to a citizen;  
(6) Where illegally using weapons or police instruments causes bodily injury or death to a citizen;  
(7) Where during criminal proceedings, measures such as sealing up, distraining, freezing or recovery of property are illegally taken;  
(8) Where the judgment is changed to acquittal upon retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, and the original sentence of fine or confiscation of property has been executed;  
(9) Where during civil action or administrative action, compulsory measures against obstructions to the proceedings, preservation measures are illegally taken, or errors occur in the enforcement of judgments, rulings or other effective legal instruments, causing damage.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 赔偿请求人向作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院提出赔偿申请，或者依照[国家赔偿法第二十四条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-24)、第二十五条的规定向人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请的，收到申请的人民法院根据本规定予以审查立案。

**Article 2** Where a compensation claimant files a compensation application with a People's Court that is the compensation obligor, or files a compensation application with a compensation committee of a People's Court in accordance with Articles 24 and 25 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application shall examine and register the case in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 赔偿请求人当面递交赔偿申请的，收到申请的人民法院应当依照[国家赔偿法第十二条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-12)的规定，当场出具加盖本院专用印章并注明收讫日期的书面凭证。  
赔偿请求人以邮寄等形式提出赔偿申请的，收到申请的人民法院应当及时登记审查。  
申请材料不齐全的，收到申请的人民法院应当在五日内一次性告知赔偿请求人需要补正的全部内容。收到申请的时间自人民法院收到补正材料之日起计算。

**Article 3** Where a compensation claimant submits a compensation application in person, the People's Court that receives the application shall, in accordance with Article 12 of the State Compensation Law, issue on the spot a written receipt bearing the special seal of that court and indicating the date of receipt.  
Where a compensation claimant submits a compensation application by mail or other means, the People's Court that receives the application shall promptly register and examine it.  
If the application materials are incomplete, the People's Court that receives the application shall, within five days, inform the compensation claimant of all the contents that need to be supplemented in a single notice. The time of receipt of the application shall be calculated from the date on which the People's Court receives the supplemented materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 赔偿请求人向作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院经审查认为其申请符合下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）赔偿请求人具备法律规定的主体资格；  
（二）本院是赔偿义务机关；  
（三）有具体的申请事项和理由；  
（四）属于[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定的情形。

**Article 4** Where a compensation claimant files a compensation application with a People's Court that is the compensation obligor, the People's Court that receives the application shall, upon examination, register the case if it finds that the application satisfies the following conditions:  
(1) The compensation claimant has the subject qualification prescribed by law;  
(2) That court is the compensation obligor;  
(3) There are specific application matters and reasons;  
(4) The case falls within the circumstances prescribed in Article 1 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 赔偿请求人对作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院作出的是否赔偿的决定不服，依照[国家赔偿法第二十四条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-24)的规定向其上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院经审查认为其申请符合下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）有赔偿义务机关作出的是否赔偿的决定书；  
（二）符合法律规定的请求期间，因不可抗力或者其他障碍未能在法定期间行使请求权的情形除外。

**Article 5** Where a compensation claimant is dissatisfied with the decision on whether to compensate made by the People's Court that is the compensation obligor, and files a compensation application with the compensation committee of the People's Court at the next higher level in accordance with Article 24 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application shall, upon examination, register the case if it finds that the application satisfies the following conditions:  
(1) There is a written decision on whether to compensate made by the compensation obligor;  
(2) The application is within the time limit for claims prescribed by law, except where the claimant is unable to exercise the right to claim within the statutory time limit due to force majeure or other obstacles.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院逾期未作出是否赔偿的决定，赔偿请求人依照[国家赔偿法第二十四条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-24)的规定向其上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院经审查认为其申请符合下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）赔偿请求人具备法律规定的主体资格；  
（二）被申请的赔偿义务机关是法律规定的赔偿义务机关；  
（三）有具体的申请事项和理由；  
（四）属于[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定的情形；  
（五）有赔偿义务机关已经收到赔偿申请的收讫凭证或者相应证据；  
（六）符合法律规定的请求期间，因不可抗力或者其他障碍未能在法定期间行使请求权的情形除外。

**Article 6** Where the People's Court that is the compensation obligor fails to make a decision on whether to compensate within the time limit, and the compensation claimant files a compensation application with the compensation committee of the People's Court at the next higher level in accordance with Article 24 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application shall, upon examination, register the case if it finds that the application satisfies the following conditions:  
(1) The compensation claimant has the subject qualification prescribed by law;  
(2) The compensation obligor applied against is the compensation obligor prescribed by law;  
(3) There are specific application matters and reasons;  
(4) The case falls within the circumstances prescribed in Article 1 of these Provisions;  
(5) There is a receipt or corresponding evidence showing that the compensation obligor has received the compensation application;  
(6) The application is within the time limit for claims prescribed by law, except where the claimant is unable to exercise the right to claim within the statutory time limit due to force majeure or other obstacles.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 赔偿请求人对行使侦查、检察职权的机关以及看守所、监狱管理机关作出的决定不服，经向其上一级机关申请复议，对复议机关的复议决定仍不服，依照[国家赔偿法第二十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-25)的规定向复议机关所在地的同级人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院经审查认为其申请符合下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）有复议机关的复议决定书；  
（二）符合法律规定的请求期间，因不可抗力或者其他障碍未能在法定期间行使请求权的情形除外。

**Article 7** Where a compensation claimant is dissatisfied with a decision made by an organ exercising investigatory or procuratorial functions, or by a detention center or prison administration organ, and after applying for reconsideration to the next higher organ, is still dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, and files a compensation application with the compensation committee of the People's Court at the same level as the place where the reconsideration organ is located in accordance with Article 25 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application shall, upon examination, register the case if it finds that the application satisfies the following conditions:  
(1) There is a written reconsideration decision issued by the reconsideration organ;  
(2) The application is within the time limit for claims prescribed by law, except where the claimant is unable to exercise the right to claim within the statutory time limit due to force majeure or other obstacles.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 复议机关逾期未作出复议决定，赔偿请求人依照[国家赔偿法第二十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-25)的规定向复议机关所在地的同级人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院经审查认为其申请符合下列条件的，应予立案：  
（一）赔偿请求人具备法律规定的主体资格；  
（二）被申请的赔偿义务机关、复议机关是法律规定的赔偿义务机关、复议机关；  
（三）有具体的申请事项和理由；  
（四）属于[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定的情形；  
（五）有赔偿义务机关、复议机关已经收到赔偿申请的收讫凭证或者相应证据；  
（六）符合法律规定的请求期间，因不可抗力或者其他障碍未能在法定期间行使请求权的情形除外。

**Article 8** Where the reconsideration organ fails to make a reconsideration decision within the time limit, and the compensation claimant files a compensation application with the compensation committee of the People's Court at the same level as the place where the reconsideration organ is located in accordance with Article 25 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application shall, upon examination, register the case if it finds that the application satisfies the following conditions:  
(1) The compensation claimant has the subject qualification prescribed by law;  
(2) The compensation obligor and reconsideration organ applied against are the compensation obligor and reconsideration organ prescribed by law;  
(3) There are specific application matters and reasons;  
(4) The case falls within the circumstances prescribed in Article 1 of these Provisions;  
(5) There is a receipt or corresponding evidence showing that the compensation obligor and reconsideration organ have received the compensation application;  
(6) The application is within the time limit for claims prescribed by law, except where the claimant is unable to exercise the right to claim within the statutory time limit due to force majeure or other obstacles.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院应当在收到申请之日起七日内决定是否立案。  
决定立案的，人民法院应当在立案之日起五日内向赔偿请求人送达受理案件通知书。属于人民法院赔偿委员会审理的国家赔偿案件，还应当同时向赔偿义务机关、复议机关送达受理案件通知书、国家赔偿申请书或者《申请赔偿登记表》副本。  
经审查不符合立案条件的，人民法院应当在七日内作出不予受理决定，并应当在作出决定之日起十日内送达赔偿请求人。

**Article 9** A People's Court shall decide whether to register a case within seven days from the date of receiving the application.  
If it decides to register the case, the People's Court shall, within five days from the date of registration, serve a notice of case acceptance on the compensation claimant. If the state compensation case is to be tried by the compensation committee of the People's Court, it shall also serve simultaneously on the compensation obligor and the reconsideration organ the notice of case acceptance, and a copy of the state compensation application or the "Application for Compensation Registration Form".  
If, upon examination, the conditions for registration are not met, the People's Court shall, within seven days, make a decision not to accept the case, and shall serve it on the compensation claimant within ten days from the date of making the decision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 赔偿请求人对复议机关或者作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院作出的决定不予受理的文书不服，依照[国家赔偿法第二十四条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-24)、第二十五条的规定向人民法院赔偿委员会提出赔偿申请，收到申请的人民法院可以依照[本规定第六条](#art-6)、第八条予以审查立案。  
经审查认为原不予受理错误的，人民法院赔偿委员会可以直接审查并作出决定，必要时也可以交由复议机关或者作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院作出决定。

**Article 10** Where a compensation claimant is dissatisfied with a written decision not to accept the case made by the reconsideration organ or the People's Court that is the compensation obligor, and files a compensation application with the compensation committee of a People's Court in accordance with Articles 24 and 25 of the State Compensation Law, the People's Court that receives the application may examine and register the case in accordance with Articles 6 and 8 of these Provisions.  
If, upon examination, it is found that the original decision not to accept the case was erroneous, the compensation committee of the People's Court may directly examine and make a decision, or, when necessary, may refer the matter to the reconsideration organ or the People's Court that is the compensation obligor to make a decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 自本规定施行之日起，最高人民法院《关于刑事赔偿和非刑事司法赔偿案件立案工作的暂行规定（试行）》即行废止；本规定施行前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 11** As of the date on which these Provisions come into force, the Interim Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Registration of Criminal Compensation and Non-Criminal Judicial Compensation Cases (for Trial Implementation) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any judicial interpretation issued by this Court before these Provisions come into force is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
