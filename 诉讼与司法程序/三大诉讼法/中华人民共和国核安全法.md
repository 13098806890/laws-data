### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障核安全，预防与应对核事故，安全利用核能，保护公众和从业人员的安全与健康，保护生态环境，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to ensure nuclear safety, prevent and respond to nuclear accidents, safely utilize nuclear energy, protect the safety and health of the public and personnel, protect the ecological environment, and promote sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域内，对核设施、核材料及相关放射性废物采取充分的预防、保护、缓解和监管等安全措施，防止由于技术原因、人为原因或者自然灾害造成核事故，最大限度减轻核事故情况下的放射性后果的活动，适用本法。  
核设施，是指：  
（一）核电厂、核热电厂、核供汽供热厂等核动力厂及装置；  
（二）核动力厂以外的研究堆、实验堆、临界装置等其他反应堆；  
（三）核燃料生产、加工、贮存和后处理设施等核燃料循环设施；  
（四）放射性废物的处理、贮存、处置设施。  
核材料，是指：  
（一）铀－235材料及其制品；  
（二）铀－233材料及其制品；  
（三）钚－239材料及其制品；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他需要管制的核材料。  
放射性废物，是指核设施运行、退役产生的，含有放射性核素或者被放射性核素污染，其浓度或者比活度大于国家确定的清洁解控水平，预期不再使用的废弃物。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities that take adequate preventive, protective, mitigation, and regulatory safety measures for nuclear facilities, nuclear materials, and related radioactive waste within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, to prevent nuclear accidents caused by technical reasons, human factors, or natural disasters, and to minimize the radioactive consequences in the event of a nuclear accident.  
Nuclear facilities means:  
(1) nuclear power plants and installations such as nuclear power plants, nuclear heating plants, and nuclear steam supply and heating plants;  
(2) other reactors such as research reactors, experimental reactors, and critical assemblies other than nuclear power plants;  
(3) nuclear fuel cycle facilities such as facilities for the production, processing, storage, and reprocessing of nuclear fuel;  
(4) facilities for the treatment, storage, and disposal of radioactive waste.  
Nuclear materials means:  
(1) uranium-235 materials and products thereof;  
(2) uranium-233 materials and products thereof;  
(3) plutonium-239 materials and products thereof;  
(4) other nuclear materials subject to control as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
Radioactive waste means waste that is produced from the operation and decommissioning of nuclear facilities, which contains or is contaminated with radionuclides and whose concentration or specific activity is greater than the clearance levels determined by the State, and is expected not to be used further.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家坚持理性、协调、并进的核安全观，加强核安全能力建设，保障核事业健康发展。

**Article 3** The State adheres to a rational, coordinated, and progressive nuclear safety concept, strengthens the building of nuclear safety capabilities, and ensures the healthy development of the nuclear industry.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事核事业必须遵循确保安全的方针。  
核安全工作必须坚持安全第一、预防为主、责任明确、严格管理、纵深防御、独立监管、全面保障的原则。

**Article 4** Any person engaged in the nuclear industry must follow the policy of ensuring safety. Nuclear safety work must adhere to the principles of safety first, prevention foremost, clear responsibilities, strict management, defense in depth, independent supervision, and comprehensive assurance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　核设施营运单位对核安全负全面责任。  
为核设施营运单位提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位，应当负相应责任。

**Article 5** Nuclear facility operating entities shall bear full responsibility for nuclear safety. Entities that provide equipment, engineering, and services to nuclear facility operating entities shall bear corresponding responsibility.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院核安全监督管理部门负责核安全的监督管理。  
国务院核工业主管部门、能源主管部门和其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的核安全管理工作。  
国家建立核安全工作协调机制，统筹协调有关部门推进相关工作。

**Article 6** The nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of nuclear safety. The nuclear industry competent authority, the energy competent authority, and other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant nuclear safety management within their respective responsibilities. The State shall establish a nuclear safety work coordination mechanism to coordinate relevant departments in advancing related work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院核安全监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门编制国家核安全规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。

**Article 7** The nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate the national nuclear safety plan, and after submitting it to the State Council for approval, shall organize its implementation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家坚持从高从严建立核安全标准体系。  
国务院有关部门按照职责分工制定核安全标准。核安全标准是强制执行的标准。  
核安全标准应当根据经济社会发展和科技进步适时修改。

**Article 8** The State shall establish a nuclear safety standard system with high and strict standards. The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate nuclear safety standards according to their respective responsibilities. Nuclear safety standards are mandatory standards. Nuclear safety standards shall be amended in a timely manner in light of economic and social development and scientific and technological progress.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家制定核安全政策，加强核安全文化建设。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门、核工业主管部门和能源主管部门应当建立培育核安全文化的机制。  
核设施营运单位和为其提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位应当积极培育和建设核安全文化，将核安全文化融入生产、经营、科研和管理的各个环节。

**Article 9** The State shall formulate nuclear safety policies and strengthen the building of nuclear safety culture. The nuclear safety supervision and administration department, the nuclear industry competent authority, and the energy competent authority of the State Council shall establish mechanisms for cultivating nuclear safety culture. Nuclear facility operating entities and entities that provide equipment, engineering, and services to them shall actively cultivate and build nuclear safety culture, and integrate nuclear safety culture into all aspects of production, operation, scientific research, and management.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持核安全相关科学技术的研究、开发和利用，加强知识产权保护，注重核安全人才的培养。  
国务院有关部门应当在相关科研规划中安排与核设施、核材料安全和辐射环境监测、评估相关的关键技术研究专项，推广先进、可靠的核安全技术。  
核设施营运单位和为其提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位、与核安全有关的科研机构等单位，应当持续开发先进、可靠的核安全技术，充分利用先进的科学技术成果，提高核安全水平。  
国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府及其有关部门对在科技创新中做出重要贡献的单位和个人，按照有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** The State shall encourage and support the research, development, and utilization of science and technology related to nuclear safety, strengthen intellectual property protection, and attach importance to the cultivation of nuclear safety personnel. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in relevant scientific research plans, arrange special research on key technologies related to the safety of nuclear facilities and nuclear materials and the monitoring and assessment of radiation environment, and promote advanced and reliable nuclear safety technologies. Nuclear facility operating entities, entities that provide equipment, engineering, and services to them, and scientific research institutions related to nuclear safety shall continuously develop advanced and reliable nuclear safety technologies, fully utilize advanced scientific and technological achievements, and improve the level of nuclear safety. The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, commend and reward units and individuals that have made significant contributions to scientific and technological innovation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人不得危害核设施、核材料安全。  
公民、法人和其他组织依法享有获取核安全信息的权利，受到核损害的，有依法获得赔偿的权利。

**Article 11** No unit or individual shall endanger the safety of nuclear facilities or nuclear materials. Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations have the right to obtain nuclear safety information in accordance with law, and if they suffer nuclear damage, have the right to obtain compensation in accordance with law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家加强对核设施、核材料的安全保卫工作。  
核设施营运单位应当建立和完善安全保卫制度，采取安全保卫措施，防范对核设施、核材料的破坏、损害和盗窃。

**Article 12** The State shall strengthen the security of nuclear facilities and nuclear materials. Nuclear facility operating entities shall establish and improve security systems and adopt security measures to prevent damage, harm, and theft of nuclear facilities and nuclear materials.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家组织开展与核安全有关的国际交流与合作，完善核安全国际合作机制，防范和应对核恐怖主义威胁，履行中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际公约所规定的义务。

**Article 13** The State shall organize international exchanges and cooperation related to nuclear safety, improve the international cooperation mechanism for nuclear safety, prevent and respond to nuclear terrorism threats, and fulfill the obligations stipulated in international conventions concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

### 第二章　核设施安全

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对核设施的选址、建设进行统筹规划，科学论证，合理布局。  
国家根据核设施的性质和风险程度等因素，对核设施实行分类管理。

**Article 14** The State shall make overall planning, scientific demonstration, and rational layout for the siting and construction of nuclear facilities. The State shall implement classified management of nuclear facilities based on factors such as their nature and risk level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　核设施营运单位应当具备保障核设施安全运行的能力，并符合下列条件：  
（一）有满足核安全要求的组织管理体系和质量保证、安全管理、岗位责任等制度；  
（二）有规定数量、合格的专业技术人员和管理人员；  
（三）具备与核设施安全相适应的安全评价、资源配置和财务能力；  
（四）具备必要的核安全技术支撑和持续改进能力；  
（五）具备应急响应能力和核损害赔偿财务保障能力；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 15** Nuclear facility operating entities shall possess the capability to ensure the safe operation of nuclear facilities and meet the following conditions:  
(1) have an organizational management system and systems for quality assurance, safety management, post responsibility, etc., that meet nuclear safety requirements;  
(2) have the prescribed number of qualified professional technical personnel and management personnel;  
(3) possess safety assessment, resource allocation, and financial capabilities appropriate to the safety of nuclear facilities;  
(4) possess the necessary technical support for nuclear safety and the capability for continuous improvement;  
(5) possess emergency response capability and financial security capability for nuclear damage compensation;  
(6) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　核设施营运单位应当依照法律、行政法规和标准的要求，设置核设施纵深防御体系，有效防范技术原因、人为原因和自然灾害造成的威胁，确保核设施安全。  
核设施营运单位应当对核设施进行定期安全评价，并接受国务院核安全监督管理部门的审查。

**Article 16** Nuclear facility operating entities shall, in accordance with the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, and standards, establish a defense-in-depth system for nuclear facilities to effectively prevent threats caused by technical reasons, human factors, and natural disasters, and ensure the safety of nuclear facilities. Nuclear facility operating entities shall conduct periodic safety assessments of nuclear facilities and accept review by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　核设施营运单位和为其提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位应当建立并实施质量保证体系，有效保证设备、工程和服务等的质量，确保设备的性能满足核安全标准的要求，工程和服务等满足核安全相关要求。

**Article 17** Nuclear facility operating entities and entities that provide equipment, engineering, and services to them shall establish and implement a quality assurance system to effectively guarantee the quality of equipment, engineering, and services, ensuring that the performance of equipment meets the requirements of nuclear safety standards, and that engineering and services meet relevant nuclear safety requirements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　核设施营运单位应当严格控制辐射照射，确保有关人员免受超过国家规定剂量限值的辐射照射，确保辐射照射保持在合理、可行和尽可能低的水平。

**Article 18** Nuclear facility operating entities shall strictly control radiation exposure, ensure that relevant persons are free from radiation exposure exceeding the dose limits prescribed by the State, and ensure that radiation exposure is maintained at a level that is reasonable, achievable, and as low as possible.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　核设施营运单位应当对核设施周围环境中所含的放射性核素的种类、浓度以及核设施流出物中的放射性核素总量实施监测，并定期向国务院环境保护主管部门和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门报告监测结果。

**Article 19** Nuclear facility operating entities shall monitor the types and concentrations of radionuclides in the environment around nuclear facilities and the total amount of radionuclides in effluents from nuclear facilities, and shall periodically report the monitoring results to the competent environmental protection department of the State Council and the competent environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the facility is located.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　核设施营运单位应当按照国家有关规定，制定培训计划，对从业人员进行核安全教育和技能培训并进行考核。  
核设施营运单位应当为从业人员提供相应的劳动防护和职业健康检查，保障从业人员的安全和健康。

**Article 20** Nuclear facility operating entities shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, formulate training plans, provide nuclear safety education and skill training to personnel, and conduct assessments. Nuclear facility operating entities shall provide corresponding labor protection and occupational health examinations to personnel, and ensure the safety and health of personnel.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当对国家规划确定的核动力厂等重要核设施的厂址予以保护，在规划期内不得变更厂址用途。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当在核动力厂等重要核设施周围划定规划限制区，经国务院核安全监督管理部门同意后实施。  
禁止在规划限制区内建设可能威胁核设施安全的易燃、易爆、腐蚀性物品的生产、贮存设施以及人口密集场所。

**Article 21** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall protect the sites of important nuclear facilities such as nuclear power plants identified in the national plan, and shall not change the use of the sites within the planning period. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall delimit a planning restricted zone around important nuclear facilities such as nuclear power plants, and shall implement it after obtaining consent from the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council. It is prohibited to construct production and storage facilities for flammable, explosive, or corrosive substances that may threaten the safety of nuclear facilities, as well as densely populated areas, within the planning restricted zone.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家建立核设施安全许可制度。  
核设施营运单位进行核设施选址、建造、运行、退役等活动，应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门申请许可。  
核设施营运单位要求变更许可文件规定条件的，应当报国务院核安全监督管理部门批准。

**Article 22** The State shall establish a nuclear facility safety licensing system. Nuclear facility operating entities that engage in activities such as siting, construction, operation, and decommissioning of nuclear facilities shall apply for a license from the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council. If a nuclear facility operating entity requests to change the conditions specified in the license document, it shall submit the request to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　核设施营运单位应当对地质、地震、气象、水文、环境和人口分布等因素进行科学评估，在满足核安全技术评价要求的前提下，向国务院核安全监督管理部门提交核设施选址安全分析报告，经审查符合核安全要求后，取得核设施场址选择审查意见书。

**Article 23** Nuclear facility operating entities shall conduct a scientific assessment of factors such as geology, seismology, meteorology, hydrology, environment, and population distribution, and on the premise of meeting the requirements of nuclear safety technical evaluation, submit a safety analysis report for the nuclear facility site to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, and after the review confirms that it meets nuclear safety requirements, obtain a review opinion on the selection of the nuclear facility site.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　核设施设计应当符合核安全标准，采用科学合理的构筑物、系统和设备参数与技术要求，提供多样保护和多重屏障，确保核设施运行可靠、稳定和便于操作，满足核安全要求。

**Article 24** The design of nuclear facilities shall comply with nuclear safety standards, adopt scientific and reasonable parameters and technical requirements for structures, systems, and equipment, provide diverse protection and multiple barriers, ensure that the operation of nuclear facilities is reliable, stable, and easy to operate, and meet nuclear safety requirements.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　核设施建造前，核设施营运单位应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门提出建造申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）核设施建造申请书；  
（二）初步安全分析报告；  
（三）环境影响评价文件；  
（四）质量保证文件；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他材料。

**Article 25** Before the construction of a nuclear facility, the nuclear facility operating entity shall submit a construction application to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, and submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for construction of the nuclear facility;  
(2) a preliminary safety analysis report;  
(3) environmental impact assessment documents;  
(4) quality assurance documents;  
(5) other materials as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　核设施营运单位取得核设施建造许可证后，应当确保核设施整体性能满足核安全标准的要求。  
核设施建造许可证的有效期不得超过十年。有效期届满，需要延期建造的，应当报国务院核安全监督管理部门审查批准。但是，有下列情形之一且经评估不存在安全风险的除外：  
（一）国家政策或者行为导致核设施延期建造；  
（二）用于科学研究的核设施；  
（三）用于工程示范的核设施；  
（四）用于乏燃料后处理的核设施。  
核设施建造完成后应当进行调试，验证其是否满足设计的核安全要求。

**Article 26** After obtaining the nuclear facility construction license, the nuclear facility operating entity shall ensure that the overall performance of the nuclear facility meets the requirements of nuclear safety standards. The validity period of the nuclear facility construction license shall not exceed ten years. If, upon expiration of the validity period, extension of construction is needed, it shall be reported to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council for review and approval. However, except under any of the following circumstances and after assessment that there is no safety risk:  
(1) the delayed construction of the nuclear facility is caused by national policies or actions;  
(2) the nuclear facility is used for scientific research;  
(3) the nuclear facility is used for engineering demonstration;  
(4) the nuclear facility is used for spent fuel reprocessing.  
After the construction of the nuclear facility is completed, it shall undergo commissioning to verify whether it meets the designed nuclear safety requirements.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　核设施首次装投料前，核设施营运单位应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门提出运行申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）核设施运行申请书；  
（二）最终安全分析报告；  
（三）质量保证文件；  
（四）应急预案；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他材料。  
核设施营运单位取得核设施运行许可证后，应当按照许可证的规定运行。  
核设施运行许可证的有效期为设计寿期。在有效期内，国务院核安全监督管理部门可以根据法律、行政法规和新的核安全标准的要求，对许可证规定的事项作出合理调整。  
核设施营运单位调整下列事项的，应当报国务院核安全监督管理部门批准：  
（一）作为颁发运行许可证依据的重要构筑物、系统和设备；  
（二）运行限值和条件；  
（三）国务院核安全监督管理部门批准的与核安全有关的程序和其他文件。

**Article 27** Before the first loading of nuclear fuel into a nuclear facility, the nuclear facility operating entity shall submit an operation application to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, and submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for operation of the nuclear facility;  
(2) a final safety analysis report;  
(3) quality assurance documents;  
(4) emergency response plan;  
(5) other materials as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
After obtaining the nuclear facility operation license, the nuclear facility operating entity shall operate in accordance with the provisions of the license. The validity period of the nuclear facility operation license shall be the design lifetime. During the validity period, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council may, in accordance with the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, and new nuclear safety standards, make reasonable adjustments to the matters specified in the license. Where a nuclear facility operating entity adjusts any of the following matters, it shall report to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council for approval:  
(1) important structures, systems, and equipment that served as the basis for issuing the operation license;  
(2) operational limits and conditions;  
(3) procedures and other documents related to nuclear safety approved by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　核设施运行许可证有效期届满需要继续运行的，核设施营运单位应当于有效期届满前五年，向国务院核安全监督管理部门提出延期申请，并对其是否符合核安全标准进行论证、验证，经审查批准后，方可继续运行。

**Article 28** If the nuclear facility operation license expires and continued operation is needed, the nuclear facility operating entity shall, five years before the expiration of the validity period, submit an application for extension to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, and demonstrate and verify whether it meets the nuclear safety standards, and may continue operation only after the review and approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　核设施终止运行后，核设施营运单位应当采取安全的方式进行停闭管理，保证停闭期间的安全，确保退役所需的基本功能、技术人员和文件。

**Article 29** After a nuclear facility ceases operation, the nuclear facility operating entity shall manage the shutdown in a safe manner, ensure safety during the shutdown period, and ensure the basic functions, technical personnel, and documents needed for decommissioning.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　核设施退役前，核设施营运单位应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门提出退役申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）核设施退役申请书；  
（二）安全分析报告；  
（三）环境影响评价文件；  
（四）质量保证文件；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他材料。  
核设施退役时，核设施营运单位应当按照合理、可行和尽可能低的原则处理、处置核设施场址的放射性物质，将构筑物、系统和设备的放射性水平降低至满足标准的要求。  
核设施退役后，核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门应当对核设施场址及其周围环境中所含的放射性核素的种类和浓度组织监测。

**Article 30** Before decommissioning a nuclear facility, the nuclear facility operating entity shall submit a decommissioning application to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, and submit the following materials:  
(1) a written application for decommissioning of the nuclear facility;  
(2) a safety analysis report;  
(3) environmental impact assessment documents;  
(4) quality assurance documents;  
(5) other materials as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
When decommissioning a nuclear facility, the nuclear facility operating entity shall treat and dispose of radioactive substances on the nuclear facility site in accordance with the principles of reasonableness, achievability, and as low as reasonably possible, and reduce the radioactivity levels of structures, systems, and equipment to meet the requirements of the standards. After decommissioning of the nuclear facility, the competent environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nuclear facility is located shall organize monitoring of the types and concentrations of radionuclides in the nuclear facility site and its surrounding environment.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　进口核设施，应当满足中华人民共和国有关核安全法律、行政法规和标准的要求，并报国务院核安全监督管理部门审查批准。  
出口核设施，应当遵守中华人民共和国有关核设施出口管制的规定。

**Article 31** Imported nuclear facilities shall meet the requirements of the relevant nuclear safety laws, administrative regulations, and standards of the People's Republic of China, and shall be submitted to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council for review and approval. Exported nuclear facilities shall comply with the provisions of the People's Republic of China on the control of nuclear facility exports.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院核安全监督管理部门应当依照法定条件和程序，对核设施安全许可申请组织安全技术审查，满足核安全要求的，在技术审查完成之日起二十日内，依法作出准予许可的决定。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门审批核设施建造、运行许可申请时，应当向国务院有关部门和核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府征询意见，被征询意见的单位应当在三个月内给予答复。

**Article 32** The nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the statutory conditions and procedures, organize a safety technical review of the nuclear facility safety license application. If the nuclear safety requirements are met, it shall, within twenty days from the date of completion of the technical review, make a decision to grant the license in accordance with law. When examining and approving the application for a nuclear facility construction or operation license, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall seek opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nuclear facility is located, and the unit from which opinions are sought shall give a reply within three months.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院核安全监督管理部门组织安全技术审查时，应当委托与许可申请单位没有利益关系的技术支持单位进行技术审评。受委托的技术支持单位应当对其技术评价结论的真实性、准确性负责。

**Article 33** When organizing a safety technical review, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall entrust a technical support unit that has no conflict of interest with the license applicant to conduct a technical assessment. The entrusted technical support unit shall be responsible for the truthfulness and accuracy of its technical evaluation conclusions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院核安全监督管理部门成立核安全专家委员会，为核安全决策提供咨询意见。  
制定核安全规划和标准，进行核设施重大安全问题技术决策，应当咨询核安全专家委员会的意见。

**Article 34** The nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall establish a nuclear safety expert committee to provide advisory opinions for nuclear safety decision-making. When formulating nuclear safety plans and standards, and making technical decisions on major nuclear facility safety issues, the opinions of the nuclear safety expert committee shall be consulted.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家建立核设施营运单位核安全报告制度，具体办法由国务院有关部门制定。  
国务院有关部门应当建立核安全经验反馈制度，并及时处理核安全报告信息，实现信息共享。  
核设施营运单位应当建立核安全经验反馈体系。

**Article 35** The State shall establish a nuclear safety reporting system for nuclear facility operating entities, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council. The relevant departments of the State Council shall establish a nuclear safety experience feedback system, promptly process nuclear safety report information, and achieve information sharing. Nuclear facility operating entities shall establish a nuclear safety experience feedback system.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　为核设施提供核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验服务的单位，应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门申请许可。境外机构为境内核设施提供核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验服务的，应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门申请注册。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门依法对进口的核安全设备进行安全检验。

**Article 36** Units providing services of design, manufacture, installation, and non-destructive testing of nuclear safety equipment for nuclear facilities shall apply for a license from the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council. If an overseas institution provides services of design, manufacture, installation, and non-destructive testing of nuclear safety equipment for nuclear facilities within the territory, it shall apply for registration with the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council. The nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with law, conduct safety inspection of imported nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　核设施操纵人员以及核安全设备焊接人员、无损检验人员等特种工艺人员应当按照国家规定取得相应资格证书。  
核设施营运单位以及核安全设备制造、安装和无损检验单位应当聘用取得相应资格证书的人员从事与核设施安全专业技术有关的工作。

**Article 37** Nuclear facility operators and special process personnel such as welders and non-destructive testers for nuclear safety equipment shall obtain corresponding qualification certificates in accordance with state regulations. Nuclear facility operating entities and units engaged in manufacture, installation, and non-destructive testing of nuclear safety equipment shall employ persons who have obtained corresponding qualification certificates to engage in work related to the professional technology of nuclear facility safety.

### 第三章　核材料和放射性废物安全

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　核设施营运单位和其他有关单位持有核材料，应当按照规定的条件依法取得许可，并采取下列措施，防止核材料被盗、破坏、丢失、非法转让和使用，保障核材料的安全与合法利用：  
（一）建立专职机构或者指定专人保管核材料；  
（二）建立核材料衡算制度，保持核材料收支平衡；  
（三）建立与核材料保护等级相适应的实物保护系统；  
（四）建立信息保密制度，采取保密措施；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 38** Nuclear facility operating entities and other relevant units that possess nuclear materials shall, in accordance with the prescribed conditions, obtain a license in accordance with law, and adopt the following measures to prevent nuclear materials from being stolen, damaged, lost, illegally transferred, and used, and to ensure the safety and legitimate utilization of nuclear materials:  
(1) establish a specialized institution or designate a specific person to keep nuclear materials;  
(2) establish a nuclear material accounting system to maintain a balance of receipts and expenditures of nuclear materials;  
(3) establish a physical protection system commensurate with the level of protection of nuclear materials;  
(4) establish an information confidentiality system and adopt confidentiality measures;  
(5) other measures as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　产生、贮存、运输、后处理乏燃料的单位应当采取措施确保乏燃料的安全，并对持有的乏燃料承担核安全责任。

**Article 39** Units that generate, store, transport, and reprocess spent fuel shall take measures to ensure the safety of spent fuel, and shall bear nuclear safety responsibility for the spent fuel in their possession.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　放射性废物应当实行分类处置。  
低、中水平放射性废物在国家规定的符合核安全要求的场所实行近地表或者中等深度处置。  
高水平放射性废物实行集中深地质处置，由国务院指定的单位专营。

**Article 40** Radioactive waste shall be subject to classified disposal. Low- and intermediate-level radioactive waste shall be subject to near-surface or intermediate-depth disposal in sites designated by the State that meet nuclear safety requirements. High-level radioactive waste shall be subject to centralized deep geological disposal, and shall be exclusively operated by a unit designated by the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　核设施营运单位、放射性废物处理处置单位应当对放射性废物进行减量化、无害化处理、处置，确保永久安全。

**Article 41** Nuclear facility operating entities and radioactive waste treatment and disposal units shall conduct minimization, harmlessness treatment and disposal of radioactive waste to ensure permanent safety.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院核工业主管部门会同国务院有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制低、中水平放射性废物处置场所的选址规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
国务院核工业主管部门会同国务院有关部门编制高水平放射性废物处置场所的选址规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
放射性废物处置场所的建设应当与核能发展的要求相适应。

**Article 42** The nuclear industry competent authority of the State Council, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall formulate siting plans for low- and intermediate-level radioactive waste disposal sites, and after submitting them to the State Council for approval, shall organize their implementation. The nuclear industry competent authority of the State Council, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate siting plans for high-level radioactive waste disposal sites, and after submitting them to the State Council for approval, shall organize their implementation. The construction of radioactive waste disposal sites shall be commensurate with the requirements of nuclear energy development.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立放射性废物管理许可制度。  
专门从事放射性废物处理、贮存、处置的单位，应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门申请许可。  
核设施营运单位利用与核设施配套建设的处理、贮存设施，处理、贮存本单位产生的放射性废物的，无需申请许可。

**Article 43** The State shall establish a radioactive waste management licensing system. Units that specialize in the treatment, storage, and disposal of radioactive waste shall apply for a license from the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council. If a nuclear facility operating entity uses treatment and storage facilities built in support of the nuclear facility to treat and store radioactive waste generated by itself, it is not required to apply for a license.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　核设施营运单位应当对其产生的放射性固体废物和不能经净化排放的放射性废液进行处理，使其转变为稳定的、标准化的固体废物后，及时送交放射性废物处置单位处置。  
核设施营运单位应当对其产生的放射性废气进行处理，达到国家放射性污染防治标准后，方可排放。

**Article 44** Nuclear facility operating entities shall treat the radioactive solid waste and radioactive liquid waste that cannot be purified and discharged generated by them, transform them into stable and standardized solid waste, and then promptly deliver them to a radioactive waste disposal unit for disposal. Nuclear facility operating entities shall treat the radioactive gas generated by them, and may discharge it only after it meets the national radioactive pollution prevention and control standards.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　放射性废物处置单位应当按照国家放射性污染防治标准的要求，对其接收的放射性废物进行处置。  
放射性废物处置单位应当建立放射性废物处置情况记录档案，如实记录处置的放射性废物的来源、数量、特征、存放位置等与处置活动有关的事项。记录档案应当永久保存。

**Article 45** Radioactive waste disposal units shall dispose of the radioactive waste they receive in accordance with the requirements of national radioactive pollution prevention and control standards. Radioactive waste disposal units shall establish record files of radioactive waste disposal, truthfully recording the source, quantity, characteristics, storage location, and other matters related to the disposal activities of the disposed radioactive waste. The record files shall be permanently preserved.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家建立放射性废物处置设施关闭制度。  
放射性废物处置设施有下列情形之一的，应当依法办理关闭手续，并在划定的区域设置永久性标记：  
（一）设计服役期届满；  
（二）处置的放射性废物已经达到设计容量；  
（三）所在地区的地质构造或者水文地质等条件发生重大变化，不适宜继续处置放射性废物；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他需要关闭的情形。

**Article 46** The State shall establish a closure system for radioactive waste disposal facilities. If a radioactive waste disposal facility is under any of the following circumstances, closure formalities shall be gone through in accordance with law, and permanent markers shall be set up in the delineated area:  
(1) the design service life has expired;  
(2) the disposed radioactive waste has reached the design capacity;  
(3) the geological structure or hydrogeological conditions of the area have undergone significant changes, making it unsuitable for continued disposal of radioactive waste;  
(4) other circumstances requiring closure as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　放射性废物处置设施关闭前，放射性废物处置单位应当编制放射性废物处置设施关闭安全监护计划，报国务院核安全监督管理部门批准。  
安全监护计划应当包括下列主要内容：  
（一）安全监护责任人及其责任；  
（二）安全监护费用；  
（三）安全监护措施；  
（四）安全监护期限。  
放射性废物处置设施关闭后，放射性废物处置单位应当按照经批准的安全监护计划进行安全监护；经国务院核安全监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门批准后，将其交由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府进行监护管理。

**Article 47** Before the closure of a radioactive waste disposal facility, the radioactive waste disposal unit shall formulate a safety monitoring plan for the closure of the radioactive waste disposal facility, and submit it to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council for approval. The safety monitoring plan shall include the following main contents:  
(1) the safety monitoring responsible person and their responsibilities;  
(2) the safety monitoring costs;  
(3) the safety monitoring measures;  
(4) the safety monitoring period.  
After the closure of the radioactive waste disposal facility, the radioactive waste disposal unit shall conduct safety monitoring in accordance with the approved safety monitoring plan; after obtaining approval from the nuclear safety supervision and administration department of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council, it shall transfer the facility to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for monitoring and management.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　核设施营运单位应当按照国家规定缴纳乏燃料处理处置费用，列入生产成本。  
核设施营运单位应当预提核设施退役费用、放射性废物处置费用，列入投资概算、生产成本，专门用于核设施退役、放射性废物处置。具体办法由国务院财政部门、价格主管部门会同国务院核安全监督管理部门、核工业主管部门和能源主管部门制定。

**Article 48** Nuclear facility operating entities shall pay the costs for spent fuel treatment and disposal in accordance with state regulations, and include them in production costs. Nuclear facility operating entities shall accrue costs for nuclear facility decommissioning and radioactive waste disposal, include them in investment estimates and production costs, and use them exclusively for nuclear facility decommissioning and radioactive waste disposal. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department and the competent price department of the State Council, together with the nuclear safety supervision and administration department, the nuclear industry competent authority, and the energy competent authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家对核材料、放射性废物的运输实行分类管理，采取有效措施，保障运输安全。

**Article 49** The State shall implement classified management for the transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste, and adopt effective measures to ensure transport safety.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家保障核材料、放射性废物的公路、铁路、水路等运输，国务院有关部门应当加强对公路、铁路、水路等运输的管理，制定具体的保障措施。

**Article 50** The State shall ensure the transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste by road, railway, waterway, etc. The relevant departments of the State Council shall strengthen the management of transport by road, railway, waterway, etc., and formulate specific safeguard measures.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院核工业主管部门负责协调乏燃料运输管理活动，监督有关保密措施。  
公安机关对核材料、放射性废物道路运输的实物保护实施监督，依法处理可能危及核材料、放射性废物安全运输的事故。通过道路运输核材料、放射性废物的，应当报启运地县级以上人民政府公安机关按照规定权限批准；其中，运输乏燃料或者高水平放射性废物的，应当报国务院公安部门批准。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门负责批准核材料、放射性废物运输包装容器的许可申请。

**Article 51** The nuclear industry authority under the State Council shall be responsible for coordinating the management of spent fuel transport activities and supervising relevant confidentiality measures.  
The public security organ shall supervise the physical protection of nuclear materials and radioactive waste during road transport, and handle incidents that may endanger the safe transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste according to law. Where nuclear materials or radioactive waste are transported by road, the consignor shall report to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level or above at the place of departure for approval in accordance with the prescribed authority; where spent fuel or high-level radioactive waste is transported, the consignor shall report to the public security department under the State Council for approval.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for approving applications for licenses for packaging containers for the transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　核材料、放射性废物的托运人应当在运输中采取有效的辐射防护和安全保卫措施，对运输中的核安全负责。  
乏燃料、高水平放射性废物的托运人应当向国务院核安全监督管理部门提交有关核安全分析报告，经审查批准后方可开展运输活动。  
核材料、放射性废物的承运人应当依法取得国家规定的运输资质。

**Article 52** The consignor of nuclear materials or radioactive waste shall take effective radiation protection and safety security measures during transport, and shall be responsible for nuclear safety during transport.  
For spent fuel or high-level radioactive waste, the consignor shall submit a nuclear safety analysis report to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council, and may only commence transport activities after the report has been reviewed and approved.  
The carrier of nuclear materials or radioactive waste shall obtain the relevant transport qualifications prescribed by the State according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　通过公路、铁路、水路等运输核材料、放射性废物，本法没有规定的，适用相关法律、行政法规和规章关于放射性物品运输、危险货物运输的规定。

**Article 53** Where this Law has no provisions on the transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste by highway, railway, waterway, or other means, the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and rules on the transport of radioactive articles and dangerous goods shall apply.

### 第四章　核事故应急

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家设立核事故应急协调委员会，组织、协调全国的核事故应急管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际需要设立核事故应急协调委员会，组织、协调本行政区域内的核事故应急管理工作。

**Article 54** The State shall establish a nuclear accident emergency coordination committee to organize and coordinate the national nuclear accident emergency management.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may establish nuclear accident emergency coordination committees based on actual needs to organize and coordinate the nuclear accident emergency management within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院核工业主管部门承担国家核事故应急协调委员会日常工作，牵头制定国家核事故应急预案，经国务院批准后组织实施。国家核事故应急协调委员会成员单位根据国家核事故应急预案部署，制定本单位核事故应急预案，报国务院核工业主管部门备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门承担核事故应急协调委员会的日常工作，负责制定本行政区域内场外核事故应急预案，报国家核事故应急协调委员会审批后组织实施。  
核设施营运单位负责制定本单位场内核事故应急预案，报国务院核工业主管部门、能源主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门备案。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队按照国务院、中央军事委员会的规定，制定本系统支援地方的核事故应急工作预案，报国务院核工业主管部门备案。  
应急预案制定单位应当根据实际需要和情势变化，适时修订应急预案。

**Article 55** The nuclear industry authority under the State Council shall undertake the daily work of the national nuclear accident emergency coordination committee, take the lead in formulating the national nuclear accident emergency plan, and organize its implementation after approval by the State Council. Member units of the national nuclear accident emergency coordination committee shall formulate their own nuclear accident emergency plans in accordance with the deployment of the national nuclear accident emergency plan and report them to the nuclear industry authority under the State Council for recordation.  
The department designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall undertake the daily work of the nuclear accident emergency coordination committee, be responsible for formulating the off-site nuclear accident emergency plan within the administrative region, and organize its implementation after approval by the national nuclear accident emergency coordination committee.  
The nuclear facility operating entity shall be responsible for formulating the on-site nuclear accident emergency plan of the entity and report it to the nuclear industry authority under the State Council, the energy authority, and the department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.  
The People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall formulate their own nuclear accident emergency work plans for supporting local areas in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and report them to the nuclear industry authority under the State Council for recordation.  
The formulating entity of an emergency plan shall revise the emergency plan in a timely manner based on actual needs and changes in circumstances.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　核设施营运单位应当按照应急预案，配备应急设备，开展应急工作人员培训和演练，做好应急准备。  
核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门，应当开展核事故应急知识普及活动，按照应急预案组织有关企业、事业单位和社区开展核事故应急演练。

**Article 56** A nuclear facility operating entity shall equip emergency equipment in accordance with the emergency plan, carry out training and drills for emergency staff, and make emergency preparations.  
The department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nuclear facility is located shall carry out activities to popularize knowledge on nuclear accident emergencies, and organize emergency drills for relevant enterprises, institutions, and communities in accordance with the emergency plan.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立核事故应急准备金制度，保障核事故应急准备与响应工作所需经费。核事故应急准备金管理办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 57** The State shall establish a nuclear accident emergency reserve fund system to guarantee the funds needed for nuclear accident emergency preparedness and response. The measures for the administration of nuclear accident emergency reserve funds shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家对核事故应急实行分级管理。  
发生核事故时，核设施营运单位应当按照应急预案的要求开展应急响应，减轻事故后果，并立即向国务院核工业主管部门、核安全监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门报告核设施状况，根据需要提出场外应急响应行动建议。

**Article 58** The State shall implement graded management for nuclear accident emergencies.  
When a nuclear accident occurs, the nuclear facility operating entity shall carry out emergency response in accordance with the requirements of the emergency plan to mitigate the consequences of the accident, and immediately report the status of the nuclear facility to the nuclear industry authority under the State Council, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department, and the department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and propose off-site emergency response action recommendations as needed.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家核事故应急协调委员会按照国家核事故应急预案部署，组织协调国务院有关部门、地方人民政府、核设施营运单位实施核事故应急救援工作。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队按照国务院、中央军事委员会的规定，实施核事故应急救援工作。  
核设施营运单位应当按照核事故应急救援工作的要求，实施应急响应支援。

**Article 59** The national nuclear accident emergency coordination committee shall, in accordance with the deployment of the national nuclear accident emergency plan, organize and coordinate the relevant departments of the State Council, local people's governments, and nuclear facility operating entities to carry out nuclear accident emergency rescue work.  
The People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, carry out nuclear accident emergency rescue work.  
The nuclear facility operating entity shall implement emergency response support in accordance with the requirements of the nuclear accident emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院核工业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门负责发布核事故应急信息。  
国家核事故应急协调委员会统筹协调核事故应急国际通报和国际救援工作。

**Article 60** The nuclear industry authority under the State Council or the department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for releasing nuclear accident emergency information.  
The national nuclear accident emergency coordination committee shall coordinate international notifications and international rescue work for nuclear accidents.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　各级人民政府及其有关部门、核设施营运单位等应当按照国务院有关规定和授权，组织开展核事故后的恢复行动、损失评估等工作。  
核事故的调查处理，由国务院或者其授权的部门负责实施。  
核事故场外应急行动的调查处理，由国务院或者其指定的机构负责实施。

**Article 61** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments, nuclear facility operating entities, etc., shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions and authorizations of the State Council, organize and carry out recovery actions, loss assessment, etc., after a nuclear accident.  
The investigation and handling of nuclear accidents shall be implemented by the State Council or its authorized departments.  
The investigation and handling of off-site emergency actions for nuclear accidents shall be implemented by the State Council or its designated organs.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　核材料、放射性废物运输的应急应当纳入所经省、自治区、直辖市场外核事故应急预案或者辐射应急预案。发生核事故时，由事故发生地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责应急响应。

**Article 62** The emergency response for the transport of nuclear materials and radioactive waste shall be incorporated into the off-site nuclear accident emergency plan or radiation emergency plan of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government traversed. When a nuclear accident occurs, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the accident occurs shall be responsible for emergency response.

### 第五章　信息公开和公众参与

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国务院有关部门及核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门应当在各自职责范围内依法公开核安全相关信息。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门应当依法公开与核安全有关的行政许可，以及核安全有关活动的安全监督检查报告、总体安全状况、辐射环境质量和核事故等信息。  
国务院应当定期向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告核安全情况。

**Article 63** The relevant departments of the State Council and the departments designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where nuclear facilities are located shall, within their respective functions and duties, disclose nuclear safety-related information according to law.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall disclose according to law information on administrative licenses related to nuclear safety, as well as safety supervision and inspection reports, overall safety status, radiation environment quality, and nuclear accidents related to nuclear safety activities.  
The State Council shall regularly report the nuclear safety situation to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　核设施营运单位应当公开本单位核安全管理制度和相关文件、核设施安全状况、流出物和周围环境辐射监测数据、年度核安全报告等信息。具体办法由国务院核安全监督管理部门制定。

**Article 64** A nuclear facility operating entity shall disclose information on its nuclear safety management system and relevant documents, nuclear facility safety status, effluent and surrounding environment radiation monitoring data, annual nuclear safety reports, etc. Specific measures shall be formulated by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对依法公开的核安全信息，应当通过政府公告、网站以及其他便于公众知晓的方式，及时向社会公开。  
公民、法人和其他组织，可以依法向国务院核安全监督管理部门和核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门申请获取核安全相关信息。

**Article 65** Information on nuclear safety disclosed according to law shall be disclosed to the public in a timely manner through government announcements, websites, and other means that are convenient for the public to access.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations may, according to law, apply to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and the department designated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nuclear facility is located for access to nuclear safety-related information.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　核设施营运单位应当就涉及公众利益的重大核安全事项通过问卷调查、听证会、论证会、座谈会，或者采取其他形式征求利益相关方的意见，并以适当形式反馈。  
核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当就影响公众利益的重大核安全事项举行听证会、论证会、座谈会，或者采取其他形式征求利益相关方的意见，并以适当形式反馈。

**Article 66** A nuclear facility operating entity shall solicit opinions from interested parties on major nuclear safety matters involving public interests through questionnaire surveys, hearings, demonstration meetings, symposiums, or other forms, and provide feedback in an appropriate manner.  
The people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nuclear facility is located shall hold hearings, demonstration meetings, symposiums, or take other forms to solicit opinions from interested parties on major nuclear safety matters affecting public interests, and provide feedback in an appropriate manner.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　核设施营运单位应当采取下列措施，开展核安全宣传活动：  
（一）在保证核设施安全的前提下，对公众有序开放核设施；  
（二）与学校合作，开展对学生的核安全知识教育活动；  
（三）建设核安全宣传场所，印制和发放核安全宣传材料；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 67** A nuclear facility operating entity shall take the following measures to carry out nuclear safety publicity activities:  
(1) Opening nuclear facilities to the public in an orderly manner on the premise of ensuring the safety of nuclear facilities;  
(2) Cooperating with schools to carry out nuclear safety knowledge education activities for students;  
(3) Building nuclear safety publicity venues, printing and distributing nuclear safety publicity materials;  
(4) Other measures provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　公民、法人和其他组织有权对存在核安全隐患或者违反核安全法律、行政法规的行为，向国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门举报。  
公民、法人和其他组织不得编造、散布核安全虚假信息。

**Article 68** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the right to report to the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments any acts that have nuclear safety hazards or violate nuclear safety laws and administrative regulations.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall not fabricate or disseminate false nuclear safety information.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　涉及国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息的政府信息公开，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 69** The disclosure of government information involving state secrets, trade secrets, and personal information shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国家建立核安全监督检查制度。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门应当对从事核安全活动的单位遵守核安全法律、行政法规、规章和标准的情况进行监督检查。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门可以在核设施集中的地区设立派出机构。国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其派出机构应当向核设施建造、运行、退役等现场派遣监督检查人员，进行核安全监督检查。

**Article 70** The State shall establish a nuclear safety supervision and inspection system.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and other relevant departments shall supervise and inspect the compliance of units engaged in nuclear safety activities with nuclear safety laws, administrative regulations, rules, and standards.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council may establish dispatched agencies in areas where nuclear facilities are concentrated. The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or its dispatched agencies shall dispatch supervision and inspection personnel to sites such as nuclear facility construction, operation, and decommissioning to carry out nuclear safety supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国务院核安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门应当加强核安全监管能力建设，提高核安全监管水平。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门应当组织开展核安全监管技术研究开发，保持与核安全监督管理相适应的技术评价能力。

**Article 71** The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and other relevant departments shall strengthen the construction of nuclear safety supervision capacity and improve the level of nuclear safety supervision.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall organize and carry out research and development of nuclear safety supervision technologies, and maintain technical evaluation capabilities commensurate with nuclear safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国务院核安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门进行核安全监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入现场进行监测、检查或者核查；  
（二）调阅相关文件、资料和记录；  
（三）向有关人员调查、了解情况；  
（四）发现问题的，现场要求整改。  
国务院核安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门应当将监督检查情况形成报告，建立档案。

**Article 72** When carrying out nuclear safety supervision and inspection, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and other relevant departments shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Entering the site for monitoring, inspection, or verification;  
(2) Consulting relevant documents, materials, and records;  
(3) Investigating and understanding the situation from relevant personnel;  
(4) If problems are found, requiring rectification on the spot.  
The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and other relevant departments shall form reports on the supervision and inspection situation and establish archives.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　对国务院核安全监督管理部门和其他有关部门依法进行的监督检查，从事核安全活动的单位应当予以配合，如实说明情况，提供必要资料，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 73** Units engaged in nuclear safety activities shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted according to law by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council and other relevant departments, truthfully explain the situation, provide necessary information, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　核安全监督检查人员应当忠于职守，勤勉尽责，秉公执法。  
核安全监督检查人员应当具备与监督检查活动相应的专业知识和业务能力，并定期接受培训。  
核安全监督检查人员执行监督检查任务，应当出示有效证件，对获知的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息，应当依法予以保密。

**Article 74** Nuclear safety supervision and inspection personnel shall be loyal to their duties, diligent and responsible, and enforce the law impartially.  
Nuclear safety supervision and inspection personnel shall possess the professional knowledge and operational capabilities corresponding to the supervision and inspection activities, and shall receive training regularly.  
When carrying out supervision and inspection tasks, nuclear safety supervision and inspection personnel shall present valid identification, and shall keep confidential according to law any state secrets, trade secrets, and personal information they become aware of.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门未依法对许可申请进行审批的；  
（二）国务院有关部门或者核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府指定的部门未依法公开核安全相关信息的；  
（三）核设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府未就影响公众利益的重大核安全事项征求利益相关方意见的；  
（四）国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门未将监督检查情况形成报告，或者未建立档案的；  
（五）核安全监督检查人员执行监督检查任务，未出示有效证件，或者对获知的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人信息未依法予以保密的；  
（六）国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 75** In violation of the provisions of this Law, in any of the following circumstances, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments fail to examine and approve license applications according to law;  
(2) The relevant departments of the State Council or the departments designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where nuclear facilities are located fail to disclose nuclear safety-related information according to law;  
(3) The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where nuclear facilities are located fail to solicit opinions from interested parties on major nuclear safety matters affecting public interests;  
(4) The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments fail to form reports on the supervision and inspection situation, or fail to establish archives;  
(5) When carrying out supervision and inspection tasks, nuclear safety supervision and inspection personnel fail to present valid identification, or fail to keep confidential according to law any state secrets, trade secrets, or personal information they become aware of;  
(6) The nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments, and the relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, have other acts of abusing power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本法规定，危害核设施、核材料安全，或者编造、散布核安全虚假信息，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 76** In violation of the provisions of this Law, endangering the safety of nuclear facilities or nuclear materials, or fabricating or disseminating false nuclear safety information, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose public security administration penalties according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停止建设或者停产整顿：  
（一）核设施营运单位未设置核设施纵深防御体系的；  
（二）核设施营运单位或者为其提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位未建立或者未实施质量保证体系的；  
（三）核设施营运单位未按照要求控制辐射照射剂量的；  
（四）核设施营运单位未建立核安全经验反馈体系的；  
（五）核设施营运单位未就涉及公众利益的重大核安全事项征求利益相关方意见的。

**Article 77** In violation of the provisions of this Law, in any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments shall order rectification and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the entity refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend construction or suspend production for rectification:  
(1) The nuclear facility operating entity fails to establish a defense in depth system for the nuclear facility;  
(2) The nuclear facility operating entity or a unit providing equipment, engineering, and services to it fails to establish or implement a quality assurance system;  
(3) The nuclear facility operating entity fails to control radiation exposure doses as required;  
(4) The nuclear facility operating entity fails to establish a nuclear safety experience feedback system;  
(5) The nuclear facility operating entity fails to solicit opinions from interested parties on major nuclear safety matters involving public interests.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，在规划限制区内建设可能威胁核设施安全的易燃、易爆、腐蚀性物品的生产、贮存设施或者人口密集场所的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令限期拆除，恢复原状，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 78** In violation of the provisions of this Law, constructing production or storage facilities for flammable, explosive, or corrosive articles that may threaten the safety of nuclear facilities, or constructing densely populated places within the planned restricted area, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order dismantling within a specified time limit and restoration to the original condition, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，处一百万元以上五百万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停止建设或者停产整顿；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；造成环境污染的，责令限期采取治理措施消除污染，逾期不采取措施的，指定有能力的单位代为履行，所需费用由污染者承担；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未经许可，从事核设施建造、运行或者退役等活动的；  
（二）未经许可，变更许可文件规定条件的；  
（三）核设施运行许可证有效期届满，未经审查批准，继续运行核设施的；  
（四）未经审查批准，进口核设施的。

**Article 79** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity has any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan; if the entity refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend construction or suspend production for rectification; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if environmental pollution is caused, the entity shall be ordered to take treatment measures within a specified time limit to eliminate the pollution, and if it fails to take measures within the time limit, a competent unit shall be designated to perform on its behalf, with the costs borne by the polluter; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) Engaging in activities such as construction, operation, or decommissioning of a nuclear facility without a license;  
(2) Changing the conditions specified in the license document without permission;  
(3) Continuing to operate a nuclear facility after the validity period of the nuclear facility operation license expires without review and approval;  
(4) Importing a nuclear facility without review and approval.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；造成环境污染的，责令限期采取治理措施消除污染，逾期不采取措施的，指定有能力的单位代为履行，所需费用由污染者承担：  
（一）未对核设施进行定期安全评价，或者不接受国务院核安全监督管理部门审查的；  
（二）核设施终止运行后，未采取安全方式进行停闭管理，或者未确保退役所需的基本功能、技术人员和文件的；  
（三）核设施退役时，未将构筑物、系统或者设备的放射性水平降低至满足标准的要求的；  
（四）未将产生的放射性固体废物或者不能经净化排放的放射性废液转变为稳定的、标准化的固体废物，及时送交放射性废物处置单位处置的；  
（五）未对产生的放射性废气进行处理，或者未达到国家放射性污染防治标准排放的。

**Article 80** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity has any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if environmental pollution is caused, the entity shall be ordered to take treatment measures within a specified time limit to eliminate the pollution, and if it fails to take measures within the time limit, a competent unit shall be designated to perform on its behalf, with the costs borne by the polluter:  
(1) Failing to conduct periodic safety assessments of the nuclear facility, or refusing to accept review by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council;  
(2) After the nuclear facility ceases operation, failing to manage its shutdown in a safe manner, or failing to ensure the basic functions, technical personnel, and documents required for decommissioning;  
(3) Upon decommissioning of the nuclear facility, failing to reduce the radiation levels of structures, systems, or equipment to meet the requirements of the standards;  
(4) Failing to convert the generated solid radioactive waste or radioactive liquid waste that cannot be purified and discharged into stable, standardized solid waste, and promptly deliver it to a radioactive waste disposal unit for disposal;  
(5) Failing to treat the generated radioactive exhaust gas, or discharging it without meeting the national radioactive pollution prevention and control standards.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位未对核设施周围环境中所含的放射性核素的种类、浓度或者核设施流出物中的放射性核素总量实施监测，或者未按照规定报告监测结果的，由国务院环境保护主管部门或者所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 81** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity fails to monitor the types and concentrations of radionuclides in the environment surrounding the nuclear facility or the total amount of radionuclides in the effluent of the nuclear facility, or fails to report the monitoring results as required, the environmental protection authority under the State Council or the environmental protection authority of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the facility is located shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，受委托的技术支持单位出具虚假技术评价结论的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 82** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where an entrusted technical support unit issues a false technical evaluation conclusion, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未经许可，为核设施提供核安全设备设计、制造、安装或者无损检验服务的；  
（二）未经注册，境外机构为境内核设施提供核安全设备设计、制造、安装或者无损检验服务的。

**Article 83** In violation of the provisions of this Law, in any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) Providing nuclear safety equipment design, manufacturing, installation, or non-destructive testing services for a nuclear facility without a license;  
(2) An overseas institution providing nuclear safety equipment design, manufacturing, installation, or non-destructive testing services for a domestic nuclear facility without registration.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位或者核安全设备制造、安装、无损检验单位聘用未取得相应资格证书的人员从事与核设施安全专业技术有关的工作的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 84** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity or a nuclear safety equipment manufacturing, installation, or non-destructive testing unit employs persons who have not obtained the corresponding qualification certificates to engage in work related to nuclear facility safety professional technology, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the entity refuses to rectify, its license shall be suspended or revoked, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，未经许可持有核材料的，由国务院核工业主管部门没收非法持有的核材料，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 85** In violation of the provisions of this Law, holding nuclear materials without a license, the nuclear industry authority under the State Council shall confiscate the illegally held nuclear materials and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；造成环境污染的，责令限期采取治理措施消除污染，逾期不采取措施的，指定有能力的单位代为履行，所需费用由污染者承担：  
（一）未经许可，从事放射性废物处理、贮存、处置活动的；  
（二）未建立放射性废物处置情况记录档案，未如实记录与处置活动有关的事项，或者未永久保存记录档案的；  
（三）对应当关闭的放射性废物处置设施，未依法办理关闭手续的；  
（四）关闭放射性废物处置设施，未在划定的区域设置永久性标记的；  
（五）未编制放射性废物处置设施关闭安全监护计划的；  
（六）放射性废物处置设施关闭后，未按照经批准的安全监护计划进行安全监护的。

**Article 86** In violation of the provisions of this Law, in any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if environmental pollution is caused, the entity shall be ordered to take treatment measures within a specified time limit to eliminate the pollution, and if it fails to take measures within the time limit, a competent unit shall be designated to perform on its behalf, with the costs borne by the polluter:  
(1) Engaging in activities of treatment, storage, or disposal of radioactive waste without a license;  
(2) Failing to establish records of radioactive waste disposal situations, failing to truthfully record matters related to disposal activities, or failing to permanently preserve records;  
(3) Failing to handle closure procedures for a radioactive waste disposal facility that should be closed according to law;  
(4) Upon closure of a radioactive waste disposal facility, failing to set up permanent markers in the designated area;  
(5) Failing to prepare a safety monitoring plan for the closure of a radioactive waste disposal facility;  
(6) After the closure of a radioactive waste disposal facility, failing to carry out safety monitoring in accordance with the approved safety monitoring plan.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位有下列情形之一的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定制定场内核事故应急预案的；  
（二）未按照应急预案配备应急设备，未开展应急工作人员培训或者演练的；  
（三）未按照核事故应急救援工作的要求，实施应急响应支援的。

**Article 87** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity has any of the following circumstances, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) Failing to formulate an on-site nuclear accident emergency plan as required;  
(2) Failing to equip emergency equipment in accordance with the emergency plan, failing to conduct training or drills for emergency staff;  
(3) Failing to implement emergency response support in accordance with the requirements of the nuclear accident emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，核设施营运单位未按照规定公开相关信息的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 88** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a nuclear facility operating entity fails to disclose relevant information as required, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council shall order rectification; if the entity refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　违反本法规定，对国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门依法进行的监督检查，从事核安全活动的单位拒绝、阻挠的，由国务院核安全监督管理部门或者其他有关部门责令改正，可以处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，暂扣或者吊销其许可证；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 89** In violation of the provisions of this Law, where a unit engaged in nuclear safety activities refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection conducted according to law by the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments, the nuclear safety supervision and administration department under the State Council or other relevant departments shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the entity refuses to rectify, its license shall be suspended or revoked; if a violation of public security administration is constituted, the public security organ shall impose public security administration penalties according to law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　因核事故造成他人人身伤亡、财产损失或者环境损害的，核设施营运单位应当按照国家核损害责任制度承担赔偿责任，但能够证明损害是因战争、武装冲突、暴乱等情形造成的除外。  
为核设施营运单位提供设备、工程以及服务等的单位不承担核损害赔偿责任。核设施营运单位与其有约定的，在承担赔偿责任后，可以按照约定追偿。  
核设施营运单位应当通过投保责任保险、参加互助机制等方式，作出适当的财务保证安排，确保能够及时、有效履行核损害赔偿责任。

**Article 90** Where a nuclear accident causes personal injury or death, property loss, or environmental damage to others, the nuclear facility operating entity shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the national nuclear damage liability system, except where it can prove that the damage is caused by war, armed conflict, riot, or similar circumstances.  
A unit providing equipment, engineering, and services to a nuclear facility operating entity shall not bear nuclear damage compensation liability. If the nuclear facility operating entity has an agreement with such unit, it may seek recourse in accordance with the agreement after bearing compensation liability.  
The nuclear facility operating entity shall make appropriate financial guarantee arrangements, such as purchasing liability insurance or participating in mutual assistance mechanisms, to ensure that it can perform its nuclear damage compensation liability promptly and effectively.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 91** In violation of the provisions of this Law, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　军工、军事核安全，由国务院、中央军事委员会依照本法规定的原则另行规定。

**Article 92** Nuclear safety for defense industry and military purposes shall be separately prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with the principles set forth in this Law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
核事故，是指核设施内的核燃料、放射性产物、放射性废物或者运入运出核设施的核材料所发生的放射性、毒害性、爆炸性或者其他危害性事故，或者一系列事故。  
纵深防御，是指通过设定一系列递进并且独立的防护、缓解措施或者实物屏障，防止核事故发生，减轻核事故后果。  
核设施营运单位，是指在中华人民共和国境内，申请或者持有核设施安全许可证，可以经营和运行核设施的单位。  
核安全设备，是指在核设施中使用的执行核安全功能的设备，包括核安全机械设备和核安全电气设备。  
乏燃料，是指在反应堆堆芯内受过辐照并从堆芯永久卸出的核燃料。  
停闭，是指核设施已经停止运行，并且不再启动。  
退役，是指采取去污、拆除和清除等措施，使核设施不再使用的场所或者设备的辐射剂量满足国家相关标准的要求。  
经验反馈，是指对核设施的事件、质量问题和良好实践等信息进行收集、筛选、评价、分析、处理和分发，总结推广良好实践经验，防止类似事件和问题重复发生。  
托运人，是指在中华人民共和国境内，申请将托运货物提交运输并获得批准的单位。

**Article 93** The meanings of the following terms in this Law:  
Nuclear accident means an accident or series of accidents involving radioactive, toxic, explosive, or other hazardous properties of nuclear fuel, radioactive products, radioactive waste within a nuclear facility, or nuclear materials transported into or out of a nuclear facility.  
Defense in depth means preventing nuclear accidents and mitigating the consequences of nuclear accidents by establishing a series of progressive and independent protective, mitigation measures or physical barriers.  
Nuclear facility operating entity means a unit within the territory of the People's Republic of China that applies for or holds a nuclear facility safety license and can operate and run a nuclear facility.  
Nuclear safety equipment means equipment used in nuclear facilities that performs nuclear safety functions, including nuclear safety mechanical equipment and nuclear safety electrical equipment.  
Spent fuel means nuclear fuel that has been irradiated in the reactor core and permanently removed from the core.  
Shutdown means that a nuclear facility has ceased operation and will not be restarted.  
Decommissioning means taking measures such as decontamination, dismantling, and removal to reduce the radiation dose of places or equipment no longer used by the nuclear facility to meet the requirements of the relevant national standards.  
Experience feedback means collecting, screening, evaluating, analyzing, processing, and distributing information on incidents, quality issues, and good practices of nuclear facilities, summarizing and promoting good practices, and preventing the recurrence of similar incidents and problems.  
Consignor means a unit within the territory of the People's Republic of China that applies to submit consigned goods for transport and obtains approval.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　本法自2018年1月1日起施行。

**Article 94** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2018.
