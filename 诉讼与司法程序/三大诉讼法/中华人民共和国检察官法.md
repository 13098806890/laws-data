# 中华人民共和国检察官法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-23

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了全面推进高素质检察官队伍建设，加强对检察官的管理和监督，维护检察官合法权益，保障人民检察院依法独立行使检察权，保障检察官依法履行职责，保障司法公正，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of comprehensively advancing the building of a high-quality team of prosecutors, strengthening the management and supervision of prosecutors, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of prosecutors, ensuring that the People's Procuratorates independently exercise the procuratorial power according to law, ensuring that prosecutors perform their duties according to law, and ensuring judicial justice.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　检察官是依法行使国家检察权的检察人员，包括最高人民检察院、地方各级人民检察院和军事检察院等专门人民检察院的检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员。

**Article 2** Prosecutors are procuratorial personnel who exercise the state's procuratorial power according to law, including the chief procurators, deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators of the Supreme People's Procuratorate, local People's Procuratorates at various levels, and special People's Procuratorates such as the Military Procuratorate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　检察官必须忠实执行宪法和法律，维护社会公平正义，全心全意为人民服务。

**Article 3** Prosecutors shall faithfully implement the Constitution and laws, uphold social fairness and justice, and serve the people wholeheartedly.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　检察官应当勤勉尽责，清正廉明，恪守职业道德。

**Article 4** Prosecutors shall be diligent and responsible, honest and clean, and strictly abide by professional ethics.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　检察官履行职责，应当以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，秉持客观公正的立场。  
检察官办理刑事案件，应当严格坚持罪刑法定原则，尊重和保障人权，既要追诉犯罪，也要保障无罪的人不受刑事追究。

**Article 5** In performing their duties, prosecutors shall base on facts and take law as the criterion, and uphold an objective and impartial stance.  
In handling criminal cases, prosecutors shall strictly adhere to the principle of legality, respect and safeguard human rights, and shall both prosecute crimes and ensure that innocent persons are not subject to criminal investigation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　检察官依法履行职责，受法律保护，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 6** Prosecutors shall perform their duties according to law, and shall be protected by law from interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

### 第二章　检察官的职责、义务和权利

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　检察官的职责：  
（一）对法律规定由人民检察院直接受理的刑事案件进行侦查；  
（二）对刑事案件进行审查逮捕、审查起诉，代表国家进行公诉；  
（三）开展公益诉讼工作；  
（四）开展对刑事、民事、行政诉讼活动的监督工作；  
（五）法律规定的其他职责。  
检察官对其职权范围内就案件作出的决定负责。

**Article 7** The duties of prosecutors:  
(1) Investigating criminal cases that are directly accepted by the People's Procuratorate as provided for by law;  
(2) Reviewing arrests and reviewing prosecutions in criminal cases, and initiating public prosecutions on behalf of the state;  
(3) Carrying out public interest litigation work;  
(4) Carrying out supervision over criminal, civil, and administrative litigation activities;  
(5) Other duties as provided for by law.  
Prosecutors shall be responsible for the decisions made on cases within their authority.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员除履行检察职责外，还应当履行与其职务相适应的职责。

**Article 8** In addition to performing procuratorial duties, the chief procurators, deputy chief procurators, and members of the procuratorial committee of a People's Procuratorate shall also perform duties commensurate with their positions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　检察官在检察长领导下开展工作，重大办案事项由检察长决定。检察长可以将部分职权委托检察官行使，可以授权检察官签发法律文书。

**Article 9** Prosecutors shall carry out their work under the leadership of the chief procurator, and major case-handling matters shall be decided by the chief procurator. The chief procurator may entrust part of his or her authority to prosecutors for exercise and may authorize prosecutors to issue legal documents.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　检察官应当履行下列义务：  
（一）严格遵守宪法和法律；  
（二）秉公办案，不得徇私枉法；  
（三）依法保障当事人和其他诉讼参与人的诉讼权利；  
（四）维护国家利益、社会公共利益，维护个人和组织的合法权益；  
（五）保守国家秘密和检察工作秘密，对履行职责中知悉的商业秘密和个人隐私予以保密；  
（六）依法接受法律监督和人民群众监督；  
（七）通过依法办理案件以案释法，增强全民法治观念，推进法治社会建设；  
（八）法律规定的其他义务。

**Article 10** Prosecutors shall fulfill the following obligations:  
(1) Strictly abide by the Constitution and laws;  
(2) Handle cases impartially, and shall not engage in any favoritism or malpractice;  
(3) Protect the litigation rights of the parties and other participants in proceedings according to law;  
(4) Safeguard the interests of the state and the public interest, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of individuals and organizations;  
(5) Keep state secrets and procuratorial work secrets, and keep confidential the trade secrets and personal privacy learned in the performance of their duties;  
(6) Accept legal supervision and supervision by the people according to law;  
(7) Explain the law through case handling in accordance with the law, enhance the awareness of the rule of law among the whole people, and promote the construction of a law-based society;  
(8) Other obligations as provided for by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　检察官享有下列权利：  
（一）履行检察官职责应当具有的职权和工作条件；  
（二）非因法定事由、非经法定程序，不被调离、免职、降职、辞退或者处分；  
（三）履行检察官职责应当享有的职业保障和福利待遇；  
（四）人身、财产和住所安全受法律保护；  
（五）提出申诉或者控告；  
（六）法律规定的其他权利。

**Article 11** Prosecutors shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) The powers and working conditions necessary for performing the duties of a prosecutor;  
(2) Not to be transferred, removed, demoted, discharged, or sanctioned without statutory reasons and statutory procedures;  
(3) The professional security and welfare benefits that should be enjoyed in performing the duties of a prosecutor;  
(4) The security of their person, property, and residence protected by law;  
(5) To lodge a petition or complaint;  
(6) Other rights as provided for by law.

### 第三章　检察官的条件和遴选

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　担任检察官必须具备下列条件：  
（一）具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）拥护中华人民共和国宪法，拥护中国共产党领导和社会主义制度；  
（三）具有良好的政治、业务素质和道德品行；  
（四）具有正常履行职责的身体条件；  
（五）具备普通高等学校法学类本科学历并获得学士及以上学位；或者普通高等学校非法学类本科及以上学历并获得法律硕士、法学硕士及以上学位；或者普通高等学校非法学类本科及以上学历，获得其他相应学位，并具有法律专业知识；  
（六）从事法律工作满五年。其中获得法律硕士、法学硕士学位，或者获得法学博士学位的，从事法律工作的年限可以分别放宽至四年、三年；  
（七）初任检察官应当通过国家统一法律职业资格考试取得法律职业资格。  
适用前款第五项规定的学历条件确有困难的地方，经最高人民检察院审核确定，在一定期限内，可以将担任检察官的学历条件放宽为高等学校本科毕业。

**Article 12** The following conditions must be met to serve as a prosecutor:  
(1) Having the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Upholding the Constitution of the People's Republic of China, the leadership of the Communist Party of China, and the socialist system;  
(3) Having good political, professional, and moral qualities;  
(4) Having the physical condition to normally perform their duties;  
(5) Holding a bachelor's degree in law from a regular institution of higher education and having been awarded a bachelor's degree or above; or holding a bachelor's degree or above in a non-law field from a regular institution of higher education and having been awarded a master's degree in law or a master's degree in legal science or above; or holding a bachelor's degree or above in a non-law field from a regular institution of higher education, having been awarded another corresponding degree, and possessing legal professional knowledge;  
(6) Having engaged in legal work for not less than five years; provided that for those who have obtained a master's degree in law or a master's degree in legal science, or a doctoral degree in legal science, the number of years of legal work may be reduced to four years and three years, respectively;  
(7) A first-instance prosecutor shall have passed the national uniform legal professional qualification examination and obtained legal professional qualifications.  
Where it is truly difficult to apply the educational background conditions specified in subparagraph (5) of the preceding paragraph, upon review and determination by the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the educational background requirement for serving as a prosecutor may be relaxed to graduation from a regular institution of higher education with a bachelor's degree within a certain period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下列人员不得担任检察官：  
（一）因犯罪受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）被开除公职的；  
（三）被吊销律师、公证员执业证书或者被仲裁委员会除名的；  
（四）有法律规定的其他情形的。

**Article 13** The following persons shall not serve as prosecutors:  
(1) Persons who have been subjected to criminal punishment for a crime;  
(2) Persons who have been discharged from public employment;  
(3) Persons whose lawyer's or notary's practice certificates have been revoked or who have been expelled from an arbitration commission;  
(4) Persons having other circumstances provided for by law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　初任检察官采用考试、考核的办法，按照德才兼备的标准，从具备检察官条件的人员中择优提出人选。  
人民检察院的检察长应当具有法学专业知识和法律职业经历。副检察长、检察委员会委员应当从检察官、法官或者其他具备检察官条件的人员中产生。

**Article 14** For first-instance prosecutors, the method of examination and assessment shall be adopted, and candidates shall be selected on the basis of merit from among persons who meet the conditions for being a prosecutor.  
The chief procurator of a People's Procuratorate shall have legal professional knowledge and legal professional experience. Deputy chief procurators and members of the procuratorial committee shall be selected from among prosecutors, judges, or other persons who meet the conditions for being a prosecutor.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民检察院可以根据检察工作需要，从律师或者法学教学、研究人员等从事法律职业的人员中公开选拔检察官。  
除应当具备检察官任职条件外，参加公开选拔的律师应当实际执业不少于五年，执业经验丰富，从业声誉良好，参加公开选拔的法学教学、研究人员应当具有中级以上职称，从事教学、研究工作五年以上，有突出研究能力和相应研究成果。

**Article 15** A People's Procuratorate may, based on the needs of procuratorial work, openly select prosecutors from among lawyers or legal teaching and research personnel or other persons engaged in legal professions.  
In addition to meeting the qualifications for being a prosecutor, a lawyer participating in the open selection shall have practiced law for not less than five years, have rich practice experience, and have a good professional reputation; a legal teaching or research personnel participating in the open selection shall have an intermediate or higher professional title, have engaged in teaching or research for more than five years, and have outstanding research capabilities and corresponding research achievements.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市设立检察官遴选委员会，负责初任检察官人选专业能力的审核。  
省级检察官遴选委员会的组成人员应当包括地方各级人民检察院检察官代表、其他从事法律职业的人员和有关方面代表，其中检察官代表不少于三分之一。  
省级检察官遴选委员会的日常工作由省级人民检察院的内设职能部门承担。  
遴选最高人民检察院检察官应当设立最高人民检察院检察官遴选委员会，负责检察官人选专业能力的审核。

**Article 16** Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish a Prosecutor Selection Committee, responsible for reviewing the professional competence of candidates for first-instance prosecutors.  
The members of the provincial-level Prosecutor Selection Committee shall include representatives of prosecutors from local People's Procuratorates at various levels, other persons engaged in legal professions, and representatives from relevant parties, among which the representatives of prosecutors shall account for not less than one-third.  
The routine work of the provincial-level Prosecutor Selection Committee shall be undertaken by the internal functional departments of the provincial People's Procuratorate.  
For selecting prosecutors of the Supreme People's Procuratorate, a Prosecutor Selection Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be established, responsible for reviewing the professional competence of candidates.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　初任检察官一般到基层人民检察院任职。上级人民检察院检察官一般逐级遴选；最高人民检察院和省级人民检察院检察官可以从下两级人民检察院遴选。参加上级人民检察院遴选的检察官应当在下级人民检察院担任检察官一定年限，并具有遴选职位相关工作经历。

**Article 17** First-instance prosecutors shall generally take office at a grassroots People's Procuratorate. Prosecutors of a People's Procuratorate at a higher level shall generally be selected through progressive selection; prosecutors of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and provincial-level People's Procuratorates may be selected from People's Procuratorates at the next two lower levels. A prosecutor participating in the selection of a higher-level People's Procuratorate shall have served as a prosecutor at a lower-level People's Procuratorate for a certain number of years and have relevant work experience for the selected position.

### 第四章　检察官的任免

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　检察官的任免，依照宪法和法律规定的任免权限和程序办理。  
最高人民检察院检察长由全国人民代表大会选举和罢免，副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，由检察长提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
地方各级人民检察院检察长由本级人民代表大会选举和罢免，副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，由检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
地方各级人民检察院检察长的任免，须报上一级人民检察院检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院分院检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，由省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
省级人民检察院和设区的市级人民检察院依法设立作为派出机构的人民检察院的检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，由派出的人民检察院检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
新疆生产建设兵团各级人民检察院、专门人民检察院的检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，依照全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关规定任免。

**Article 18** The appointment and removal of prosecutors shall be handled in accordance with the authority and procedures provided for by the Constitution and laws.  
The Chief Procurator of the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be elected and removed by the National People's Congress; the Deputy Chief Procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the Chief Procurator.  
The chief procurator of a local People's Procuratorate at any level shall be elected and removed by the People's Congress at the corresponding level; the deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the chief procurator.  
The appointment and removal of the chief procurator of a local People's Procuratorate at any level shall be reported to the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level, who shall submit it to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for approval.  
The chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators of a branch of a People's Procuratorate in a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators of a People's Procuratorate established as a dispatched agency by a provincial-level People's Procuratorate or a People's Procuratorate of a city divided into districts according to law shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the chief procurator of the dispatching People's Procuratorate.  
The chief procurators, deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committees, and procurators of the People's Procuratorates at all levels of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps and special People's Procuratorates shall be appointed and removed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　检察官在依照法定程序产生后，在就职时应当公开进行宪法宣誓。

**Article 19** After being produced in accordance with statutory procedures, prosecutors shall take a public oath to the Constitution at the time of assuming office.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　检察官有下列情形之一的，应当依法提请免除其检察官职务：  
（一）丧失中华人民共和国国籍的；  
（二）调出所任职人民检察院的；  
（三）职务变动不需要保留检察官职务的，或者本人申请免除检察官职务经批准的；  
（四）经考核不能胜任检察官职务的；  
（五）因健康原因长期不能履行职务的；  
（六）退休的；  
（七）辞职或者依法应当予以辞退的；  
（八）因违纪违法不宜继续任职的。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, a prosecutor shall be submitted for removal from the procuratorial office according to law:  
(1) Loss of the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Transfer out of the People's Procuratorate in which he or she holds office;  
(3) Change of position that does not require retention of the procuratorial office, or application for removal from the procuratorial office approved;  
(4) Deemed incompetent for the procuratorial office after assessment;  
(5) Inability to perform duties for a long period due to health reasons;  
(6) Retirement;  
(7) Resignation or discharge according to law;  
(8) Being unfit to continue holding office due to violation of discipline or law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对于不具备本法规定条件或者违反法定程序被选举为人民检察院检察长的，上一级人民检察院检察长有权提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会不批准。

**Article 21** Where a person who does not meet the conditions provided for in this Law or is elected as chief procurator of a People's Procuratorate in violation of statutory procedures, the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall have the right to submit the matter to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for disapproval.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　发现违反本法规定的条件任命检察官的，任命机关应当撤销该项任命；上级人民检察院发现下级人民检察院检察官的任命违反本法规定的条件的，应当要求下级人民检察院依法提请任命机关撤销该项任命。

**Article 22** Where it is discovered that the appointment of a prosecutor violates the conditions provided for in this Law, the appointing authority shall revoke the appointment; where a People's Procuratorate at a higher level discovers that the appointment of a prosecutor by a lower-level People's Procuratorate violates the conditions provided for in this Law, it shall require the lower-level People's Procuratorate to submit the matter to the appointing authority for revocation of the appointment according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　检察官不得兼任人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员，不得兼任行政机关、监察机关、审判机关的职务，不得兼任企业或者其他营利性组织、事业单位的职务，不得兼任律师、仲裁员和公证员。

**Article 23** A prosecutor shall not concurrently serve as a member of the Standing Committee of a People's Congress, hold a position in an administrative organ, a supervision organ, or an adjudicative organ, hold a position in an enterprise or other for-profit organization or a public institution, or concurrently serve as a lawyer, an arbitrator, or a notary.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　检察官之间有夫妻关系、直系血亲关系、三代以内旁系血亲以及近姻亲关系的，不得同时担任下列职务：  
（一）同一人民检察院的检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员；  
（二）同一人民检察院的检察长、副检察长和检察员；  
（三）同一业务部门的检察员；  
（四）上下相邻两级人民检察院的检察长、副检察长。

**Article 24** Where prosecutors are related by marriage, direct blood relation, collateral blood relation within three generations, or close marriage relation, they shall not concurrently hold the following positions:  
(1) Chief procurator, deputy chief procurator, or member of the procuratorial committee of the same People's Procuratorate;  
(2) Chief procurator, deputy chief procurator, and procurator of the same People's Procuratorate;  
(3) Procurators of the same business department;  
(4) Chief procurator or deputy chief procurator of two adjacent levels of People's Procuratorates.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　检察官的配偶、父母、子女有下列情形之一的，检察官应当实行任职回避：  
（一）担任该检察官所任职人民检察院辖区内律师事务所的合伙人或者设立人的；  
（二）在该检察官所任职人民检察院辖区内以律师身份担任诉讼代理人、辩护人，或者为诉讼案件当事人提供其他有偿法律服务的。

**Article 25** Where the spouse, parents, or children of a prosecutor fall under any of the following circumstances, the prosecutor shall implement post avoidance:  
(1) Serving as a partner or founder of a law firm within the jurisdiction of the People's Procuratorate where the prosecutor holds office;  
(2) Acting as a litigation agent or defender in the capacity of a lawyer, or providing other paid legal services to a party to a litigation case within the jurisdiction of the People's Procuratorate where the prosecutor holds office.

### 第五章　检察官的管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　检察官实行员额制管理。检察官员额根据案件数量、经济社会发展情况、人口数量和人民检察院层级等因素确定，在省、自治区、直辖市内实行总量控制、动态管理，优先考虑基层人民检察院和案件数量多的人民检察院办案需要。  
检察官员额出现空缺的，应当按照程序及时补充。  
最高人民检察院检察官员额由最高人民检察院商有关部门确定。

**Article 26** Prosecutors shall be managed under a fixed-post system. The fixed number of prosecutors shall be determined based on factors such as the number of cases, economic and social development, population size, and the level of the People's Procuratorate, and shall be subject to total control and dynamic management within provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, giving priority to the case-handling needs of grassroots People's Procuratorates and People's Procuratorates with a high caseload.  
Where vacancies arise in the fixed number of prosecutors, they shall be filled promptly in accordance with procedures.  
The fixed number of prosecutors in the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be determined by the Supreme People's Procuratorate in consultation with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　检察官实行单独职务序列管理。  
检察官等级分为十二级，依次为首席大检察官、一级大检察官、二级大检察官、一级高级检察官、二级高级检察官、三级高级检察官、四级高级检察官、一级检察官、二级检察官、三级检察官、四级检察官、五级检察官。

**Article 27** Prosecutors shall be managed under a separate rank sequence.  
The ranks of prosecutors are divided into twelve levels, in order: Chief Grand Prosecutor, First-Grade Grand Prosecutor, Second-Grade Grand Prosecutor, First-Grade Senior Prosecutor, Second-Grade Senior Prosecutor, Third-Grade Senior Prosecutor, Fourth-Grade Senior Prosecutor, First-Grade Prosecutor, Second-Grade Prosecutor, Third-Grade Prosecutor, Fourth-Grade Prosecutor, and Fifth-Grade Prosecutor.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　最高人民检察院检察长为首席大检察官。

**Article 28** The Chief Procurator of the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be the Chief Grand Prosecutor.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　检察官等级的确定，以检察官德才表现、业务水平、检察工作实绩和工作年限等为依据。  
检察官等级晋升采取按期晋升和择优选升相结合的方式，特别优秀或者工作特殊需要的一线办案岗位检察官可以特别选升。

**Article 29** The determination of the rank of a prosecutor shall be based on the prosecutor's moral and professional performance, professional competence, actual achievements in procuratorial work, and length of service.  
Promotion of the rank of a prosecutor shall be carried out by combining regular promotion and selective promotion; prosecutors in frontline case-handling positions who are particularly outstanding or have special work needs may be specially selected for promotion.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　检察官的等级设置、确定和晋升的具体办法，由国家另行规定。

**Article 30** The specific measures for the establishment, determination, and promotion of the ranks of prosecutors shall be separately formulated by the state.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　初任检察官实行统一职前培训制度。

**Article 31** A unified pre-service training system shall be implemented for first-instance prosecutors.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对检察官应当有计划地进行政治、理论和业务培训。  
检察官的培训应当理论联系实际、按需施教、讲求实效。

**Article 32** Political, theoretical, and professional training shall be carried out for prosecutors in a planned manner.  
The training of prosecutors shall integrate theory with practice, be conducted according to needs, and emphasize practical results.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　检察官培训情况，作为检察官任职、等级晋升的依据之一。

**Article 33** The training status of a prosecutor shall serve as one of the bases for the prosecutor's appointment and rank promotion.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　检察官培训机构按照有关规定承担培训检察官的任务。

**Article 34** Prosecutor training institutions shall undertake the task of training prosecutors in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　检察官申请辞职，应当由本人书面提出，经批准后，依照法律规定的程序免除其职务。

**Article 35** A prosecutor applying for resignation shall submit a written application in person, and after approval, his or her position shall be removed in accordance with the procedures provided for by law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　辞退检察官应当依照法律规定的程序免除其职务。  
辞退检察官应当按照管理权限决定。辞退决定应当以书面形式通知被辞退的检察官，并列明作出决定的理由和依据。

**Article 36** Discharge of a prosecutor shall be carried out by removing his or her position in accordance with the procedures provided for by law.  
The decision to discharge a prosecutor shall be made in accordance with the management authority. The discharge decision shall be notified in writing to the discharged prosecutor and shall specify the reasons and basis for the decision.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　检察官从人民检察院离任后两年内，不得以律师身份担任诉讼代理人或者辩护人。  
检察官从人民检察院离任后，不得担任原任职检察院办理案件的诉讼代理人或者辩护人，但是作为当事人的监护人或者近亲属代理诉讼或者进行辩护的除外。  
检察官被开除后，不得担任诉讼代理人或者辩护人，但是作为当事人的监护人或者近亲属代理诉讼或者进行辩护的除外。

**Article 37** Within two years after leaving a People's Procuratorate, a prosecutor shall not act as a litigation agent or defender in the capacity of a lawyer.  
After leaving a People's Procuratorate, a prosecutor shall not act as a litigation agent or defender in a case handled by the People's Procuratorate in which he or she previously held office, except where acting as a guardian or close relative of a party to represent the party in litigation or to defend.  
After being expelled, a prosecutor shall not act as a litigation agent or defender, except where acting as a guardian or close relative of a party to represent the party in litigation or to defend.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　检察官因工作需要，经单位选派或者批准，可以在高等学校、科研院所协助开展实践性教学、研究工作，并遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 38** A prosecutor may, due to work needs and upon selection or approval by the unit, assist in practical teaching and research work in institutions of higher education or scientific research institutes, and shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第六章　检察官的考核、奖励和惩戒

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民检察院设立检察官考评委员会，负责对本院检察官的考核工作。

**Article 39** A People's Procuratorate shall establish a Prosecutor Evaluation Committee, responsible for the assessment of the prosecutors of that procuratorate.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　检察官考评委员会的组成人员为五至九人。  
检察官考评委员会主任由本院检察长担任。

**Article 40** The Prosecutor Evaluation Committee shall consist of five to nine members.  
The director of the Prosecutor Evaluation Committee shall be the chief procurator of that procuratorate.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对检察官的考核，应当全面、客观、公正，实行平时考核和年度考核相结合。

**Article 41** The assessment of prosecutors shall be comprehensive, objective, and impartial, and shall combine routine assessment with annual assessment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对检察官的考核内容包括：检察工作实绩、职业道德、专业水平、工作能力、工作作风。重点考核检察工作实绩。

**Article 42** The content of the assessment of prosecutors includes: actual achievements in procuratorial work, professional ethics, professional competence, work ability, and work style. The focus shall be on the actual achievements in procuratorial work.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　年度考核结果分为优秀、称职、基本称职和不称职四个等次。  
考核结果作为调整检察官等级、工资以及检察官奖惩、免职、降职、辞退的依据。

**Article 43** The results of annual assessment shall be divided into four grades: excellent, competent, basically competent, and incompetent.  
The assessment results shall serve as the basis for adjusting the rank and salary of a prosecutor, as well as for rewards, punishment, removal, demotion, and discharge of a prosecutor.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　考核结果以书面形式通知检察官本人。检察官对考核结果如果有异议，可以申请复核。

**Article 44** The assessment results shall be notified to the prosecutor in writing. If a prosecutor has objections to the assessment results, he or she may apply for a review.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　检察官在检察工作中有显著成绩和贡献的，或者有其他突出事迹的，应当给予奖励。

**Article 45** Prosecutors who have made remarkable achievements and contributions in procuratorial work, or who have other outstanding deeds, shall be given rewards.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　检察官有下列表现之一的，应当给予奖励：  
（一）公正司法，成绩显著的；  
（二）总结检察实践经验成果突出，对检察工作有指导作用的；  
（三）在办理重大案件、处理突发事件和承担专项重要工作中，做出显著成绩和贡献的；  
（四）对检察工作提出改革建议被采纳，效果显著的；  
（五）提出检察建议被采纳或者开展法治宣传、解决各类纠纷，效果显著的；  
（六）有其他功绩的。  
检察官的奖励按照有关规定办理。

**Article 46** A prosecutor who has any of the following performances shall be given a reward:  
(1) Adjudicating impartially with remarkable results;  
(2) Having outstanding achievements in summarizing procuratorial practice experience, which has a guiding role in procuratorial work;  
(3) Making remarkable achievements and contributions in handling major cases, dealing with emergencies, and undertaking special important tasks;  
(4) Proposing reform suggestions for procuratorial work that are adopted with remarkable results;  
(5) Proposing procuratorial suggestions that are adopted or carrying out legal publicity and resolving various disputes with remarkable results;  
(6) Having other meritorious deeds.  
Rewards for prosecutors shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　检察官有下列行为之一的，应当给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）贪污受贿、徇私枉法、刑讯逼供的；  
（二）隐瞒、伪造、变造、故意损毁证据、案件材料的；  
（三）泄露国家秘密、检察工作秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的；  
（四）故意违反法律法规办理案件的；  
（五）因重大过失导致案件错误并造成严重后果的；  
（六）拖延办案，贻误工作的；  
（七）利用职权为自己或者他人谋取私利的；  
（八）接受当事人及其代理人利益输送，或者违反有关规定会见当事人及其代理人的；  
（九）违反有关规定从事或者参与营利性活动，在企业或者其他营利性组织中兼任职务的；  
（十）有其他违纪违法行为的。  
检察官的处分按照有关规定办理。

**Article 47** A prosecutor who commits any of the following acts shall be given a sanction; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Engaging in bribery, favoritism, or extorting confessions by torture;  
(2) Concealing, falsifying, altering, or intentionally destroying evidence or case materials;  
(3) Disclosing state secrets, procuratorial work secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy;  
(4) Intentionally handling cases in violation of laws and regulations;  
(5) Causing case errors due to gross negligence and causing serious consequences;  
(6) Delaying case handling and causing work delays;  
(7) Using authority to seek personal gains for oneself or others;  
(8) Accepting benefits from parties or their agents, or meeting with parties or their agents in violation of relevant provisions;  
(9) Engaging in or participating in for-profit activities in violation of relevant provisions, or concurrently holding a position in an enterprise or other for-profit organization;  
(10) Having other acts in violation of discipline or law.  
Sanctions for prosecutors shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　检察官涉嫌违纪违法，已经被立案调查、侦查，不宜继续履行职责的，按照管理权限和规定的程序暂时停止其履行职务。

**Article 48** Where a prosecutor is suspected of violating discipline or law and has been placed on file for investigation or investigation, and is unfit to continue performing duties, his or her duties shall be temporarily suspended in accordance with the management authority and prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　最高人民检察院和省、自治区、直辖市设立检察官惩戒委员会，负责从专业角度审查认定检察官是否存在[本法第四十七条](#art-47)第四项、第五项规定的违反检察职责的行为，提出构成故意违反职责、存在重大过失、存在一般过失或者没有违反职责等审查意见。检察官惩戒委员会提出审查意见后，人民检察院依照有关规定作出是否予以惩戒的决定，并给予相应处理。  
检察官惩戒委员会由检察官代表、其他从事法律职业的人员和有关方面代表组成，其中检察官代表不少于半数。  
最高人民检察院检察官惩戒委员会、省级检察官惩戒委员会的日常工作，由相关人民检察院的内设职能部门承担。

**Article 49** The Supreme People's Procuratorate and provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish a Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee, responsible for reviewing and determining from a professional perspective whether a prosecutor has committed acts in violation of procuratorial duties as specified in subparagraphs (4) and (5) of Article 47 of this Law, and for issuing review opinions such as intentional violation of duties, gross negligence, ordinary negligence, or no violation of duties. After the Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee issues a review opinion, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision on whether to impose a sanction in accordance with the relevant provisions and give corresponding treatment.  
The Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee shall be composed of representatives of prosecutors, other persons engaged in legal professions, and representatives from relevant parties, among which the representatives of prosecutors shall account for not less than half.  
The routine work of the Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the provincial-level Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee shall be undertaken by the internal functional departments of the relevant People's Procuratorates.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　检察官惩戒委员会审议惩戒事项时，当事检察官有权申请有关人员回避，有权进行陈述、举证、辩解。

**Article 50** When the Prosecutor Disciplinary Committee deliberates on disciplinary matters, the prosecutor concerned shall have the right to apply for the withdrawal of relevant personnel, and the right to make statements, present evidence, and offer explanations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　检察官惩戒委员会作出的审查意见应当送达当事检察官。当事检察官对审查意见有异议的，可以向惩戒委员会提出，惩戒委员会应当对异议及其理由进行审查，作出决定。

**Article 51** The review opinion issued by the Procurator Discipline Committee shall be served on the procurator concerned. Where the procurator concerned has an objection to the review opinion, he or she may raise it to the Discipline Committee, and the Discipline Committee shall review the objection and its reasons and make a decision.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　检察官惩戒委员会审议惩戒事项的具体程序，由最高人民检察院商有关部门确定。

**Article 52** The specific procedures for the Procurator Discipline Committee to deliberate disciplinary matters shall be determined by the Supreme People's Procuratorate in consultation with relevant departments.

### 第七章　检察官的职业保障

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　人民检察院设立检察官权益保障委员会，维护检察官合法权益，保障检察官依法履行职责。

**Article 53** A people's procuratorate shall establish a Procurator Rights Protection Committee to safeguard the lawful rights and interests of procurators and ensure that procurators perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　除下列情形外，不得将检察官调离检察业务岗位：  
（一）按规定需要任职回避的；  
（二）按规定实行任职交流的；  
（三）因机构调整、撤销、合并或者缩减编制员额需要调整工作的；  
（四）因违纪违法不适合在检察业务岗位工作的；  
（五）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 54** A procurator shall not be transferred from a procuratorial operational post except under the following circumstances:  
(1) The procurator is required to avoid service by appointment according to regulations;  
(2) The procurator is subject to appointment exchange according to regulations;  
(3) The procurator's position needs to be adjusted due to institutional restructuring, dissolution, merger, or reduction of authorized staffing;  
(4) The procurator is unsuitable for the procuratorial operational post due to violation of discipline or law;  
(5) Other circumstances as provided by law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　任何单位或者个人不得要求检察官从事超出法定职责范围的事务。  
对任何干涉检察官办理案件的行为，检察官有权拒绝并予以全面如实记录和报告；有违纪违法情形的，由有关机关根据情节轻重追究有关责任人员、行为人的责任。

**Article 55** No unit or individual may require a procurator to engage in matters beyond the scope of statutory duties.  
For any conduct that interferes with a procurator's handling of a case, the procurator shall have the right to refuse it and shall comprehensively and truthfully record and report it; where there is a violation of discipline or law, the relevant organ shall, based on the severity of the circumstances, investigate the liability of the responsible persons and the actor.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　检察官的职业尊严和人身安全受法律保护。  
任何单位和个人不得对检察官及其近亲属打击报复。  
对检察官及其近亲属实施报复陷害、侮辱诽谤、暴力侵害、威胁恐吓、滋事骚扰等违法犯罪行为的，应当依法从严惩治。

**Article 56** The professional dignity and personal safety of a procurator shall be protected by law.  
No unit or individual may retaliate against a procurator or his or her close relatives.  
Where any illegal or criminal acts such as retaliation, framing, insult, defamation, violent assault, threat, intimidation, harassment, or disturbance are committed against a procurator or his or her close relatives, the perpetrator shall be severely punished according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　检察官因依法履行职责遭受不实举报、诬告陷害、侮辱诽谤，致使名誉受到损害的，人民检察院应当会同有关部门及时澄清事实，消除不良影响，并依法追究相关单位或者个人的责任。

**Article 57** Where a procurator suffers damage to reputation due to false reports, false accusations, frame-ups, insults, or defamation while performing duties according to law, the people's procuratorate shall, together with the relevant departments, promptly clarify the facts, eliminate adverse effects, and investigate the liability of the relevant unit or individual according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　检察官因依法履行职责，本人及其近亲属人身安全面临危险的，人民检察院、公安机关应当对检察官及其近亲属采取人身保护、禁止特定人员接触等必要保护措施。

**Article 58** Where a procurator, due to performing duties according to law, faces danger to the personal safety of himself or herself and his or her close relatives, the people's procuratorate and the public security organ shall take necessary protective measures such as personal protection and prohibiting specific persons from approaching the procurator and his or her close relatives.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　检察官实行与其职责相适应的工资制度，按照检察官等级享有国家规定的工资待遇，并建立与公务员工资同步调整机制。  
检察官的工资制度，根据检察工作特点，由国家另行规定。

**Article 59** A salary system commensurate with the procurator's duties shall be implemented. A procurator shall enjoy the salary treatment prescribed by the state according to the procurator grade, and a mechanism for synchronous adjustment with the salary of civil servants shall be established.  
The salary system for procurators shall be separately prescribed by the state in light of the characteristics of procuratorial work.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　检察官实行定期增资制度。  
经年度考核确定为优秀、称职的，可以按照规定晋升工资档次。

**Article 60** A system of regular salary increases shall be implemented for procurators.  
Where a procurator is evaluated as excellent or competent in the annual assessment, he or she may be promoted in salary grade according to regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　检察官享受国家规定的津贴、补贴、奖金、保险和福利待遇。

**Article 61** A procurator shall enjoy the allowances, subsidies, bonuses, insurance, and welfare benefits prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　检察官因公致残的，享受国家规定的伤残待遇。检察官因公牺牲、因公死亡或者病故的，其亲属享受国家规定的抚恤和优待。

**Article 62** Where a procurator becomes disabled in the line of duty, he or she shall enjoy the disability benefits prescribed by the state. Where a procurator dies in the line of duty or dies of illness, his or her relatives shall enjoy the pensions and preferential treatments prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　检察官的退休制度，根据检察工作特点，由国家另行规定。

**Article 63** The retirement system for procurators shall be separately prescribed by the state in light of the characteristics of procuratorial work.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　检察官退休后，享受国家规定的养老金和其他待遇。

**Article 64** After retirement, a procurator shall enjoy the pension and other benefits prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对于国家机关及其工作人员侵犯[本法第十一条](#art-11)规定的检察官权利的行为，检察官有权提出控告。

**Article 65** Where a state organ or its staff infringes upon the rights of a procurator as provided in Article 11 of this Law, the procurator may lodge a complaint.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对检察官处分或者人事处理错误的，应当及时予以纠正；造成名誉损害的，应当恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉；造成经济损失的，应当赔偿。对打击报复的直接责任人员，应当依法追究其责任。

**Article 66** Where a sanction or personnel action against a procurator is erroneous, it shall be promptly corrected; where reputational damage is caused, the reputation shall be restored, adverse effects eliminated, and an apology made; where economic losses are caused, compensation shall be made. The persons directly responsible for retaliation shall be investigated for liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家对初任检察官实行统一法律职业资格考试制度，由国务院司法行政部门商最高人民检察院等有关部门组织实施。

**Article 67** The state shall implement a unified legal professional qualification examination system for first-time procurators, which shall be organized and implemented by the judicial administrative department of the State Council in consultation with the Supreme People's Procuratorate and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　人民检察院的检察官助理在检察官指导下负责审查案件材料、草拟法律文书等检察辅助事务。  
人民检察院应当加强检察官助理队伍建设，为检察官遴选储备人才。

**Article 68** Procurator assistants of a people's procuratorate shall, under the guidance of procurators, be responsible for procuratorial auxiliary matters such as reviewing case materials and drafting legal documents.  
The people's procuratorate shall strengthen the building of the procurator assistant team to reserve talents for the selection of procurators.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有关检察官的权利、义务和管理制度，本法已有规定的，适用本法的规定；本法未作规定的，适用公务员管理的相关法律法规。

**Article 69** Where this Law has provisions on the rights, obligations, and management system of procurators, the provisions of this Law shall apply; where this Law has no provisions, the relevant laws and regulations on the management of civil servants shall apply.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本法自2019年10月1日起施行。

**Article 70** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2019.
