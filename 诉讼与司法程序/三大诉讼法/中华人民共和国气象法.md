### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展气象事业，规范气象工作，准确、及时地发布气象预报，防御气象灾害，合理开发利用和保护气候资源，为经济建设、国防建设、社会发展和人民生活提供气象服务，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of developing meteorological undertakings, regulating meteorological work, issuing meteorological forecasts accurately and timely, preventing and mitigating meteorological disasters, rationally developing, utilizing and protecting climatic resources, and providing meteorological services for economic construction, national defense, social development and people's livelihood.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事气象探测、预报、服务和气象灾害防御、气候资源利用、气象科学技术研究等活动，应当遵守本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall be observed in engaging in meteorological observation, forecasting, services and meteorological disaster prevention, climatic resource utilization, meteorological scientific and technological research and other activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　气象事业是经济建设、国防建设、社会发展和人民生活的基础性公益事业，气象工作应当把公益性气象服务放在首位。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强对气象工作的领导和协调，将气象事业纳入中央和地方同级国民经济和社会发展计划及财政预算，以保障其充分发挥为社会公众、政府决策和经济发展服务的功能。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据当地社会经济发展的需要所建设的地方气象事业项目，其投资主要由本级财政承担。  
气象台站在确保公益性气象无偿服务的前提下，可以依法开展气象有偿服务。

**Article 3** Meteorological undertakings are basic public welfare undertakings for economic construction, national defense, social development and people's livelihood. Meteorological work shall give priority to public welfare meteorological services.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership and coordination over meteorological work, and incorporate meteorological undertakings into the national economic and social development plans and fiscal budgets at the corresponding levels of the central and local governments, so as to ensure that they fully play their role in serving the public, government decision-making and economic development.  
For local meteorological projects constructed by the local people's governments at or above the county level according to the needs of local socio-economic development, the investment shall be mainly borne by the fiscal budget at the corresponding level.  
Meteorological stations and offices may, on the premise of ensuring free public welfare meteorological services, lawfully provide fee-based meteorological services.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县、市气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当主要为农业生产服务，及时主动提供保障当地农业生产所需的公益性气象信息服务。

**Article 4** Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to the competent meteorological departments at the county and city levels shall mainly serve agricultural production, and shall provide in a timely and proactive manner public welfare meteorological information services necessary for ensuring local agricultural production.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院气象主管机构负责全国的气象工作。地方各级气象主管机构在上级气象主管机构和本级人民政府的领导下，负责本行政区域内的气象工作。  
国务院其他有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门所属的气象台站，应当接受同级气象主管机构对其气象工作的指导、监督和行业管理。

**Article 5** The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall be responsible for meteorological work nationwide. Local competent meteorological departments at all levels shall, under the leadership of the competent meteorological department at the next higher level and the people's government at the corresponding level, be responsible for meteorological work within their respective administrative areas.  
Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to other relevant departments of the State Council and other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall accept the guidance, supervision and sectoral management by the competent meteorological department at the corresponding level over their meteorological work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事气象业务活动，应当遵守国家制定的气象技术标准、规范和规程。

**Article 6** Those engaged in meteorological operational activities shall comply with the meteorological technical standards, specifications and procedures formulated by the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和支持气象科学技术研究、气象科学知识普及，培养气象人才，推广先进的气象科学技术，保护气象科技成果，加强国际气象合作与交流，发展气象信息产业，提高气象工作水平。  
各级人民政府应当关心和支持少数民族地区、边远贫困地区、艰苦地区和海岛的气象台站的建设和运行。  
对在气象工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on meteorology, popularization of meteorological scientific knowledge, cultivation of meteorological talents, dissemination of advanced meteorological science and technology, protection of meteorological scientific and technological achievements, enhancement of international meteorological cooperation and exchanges, development of meteorological information industry, and improvement of the level of meteorological work.  
People's governments at all levels shall show concern for and support the construction and operation of meteorological stations and offices in minority nationality areas, remote and poverty-stricken areas, harsh areas and islands.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to meteorological work shall be given awards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国的组织和个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事气象活动，必须经国务院气象主管机构会同有关部门批准。

**Article 8** Foreign organizations and individuals engaging in meteorological activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China must obtain approval from the competent meteorological department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

### 第二章　气象设施的建设与管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院气象主管机构应当组织有关部门编制气象探测设施、气象信息专用传输设施、大型气象专用技术装备等重要气象设施的建设规划，报国务院批准后实施。气象设施建设规划的调整、修改，必须报国务院批准。  
编制气象设施建设规划，应当遵循合理布局、有效利用、兼顾当前与长远需要的原则，避免重复建设。

**Article 9** The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall organize the relevant departments to compile construction plans for important meteorological facilities such as meteorological observation facilities, dedicated meteorological information transmission facilities, and large-scale specialized meteorological technical equipment, and shall submit such plans to the State Council for approval before implementation. Any adjustment or modification to the construction plans for meteorological facilities must be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
In compiling construction plans for meteorological facilities, the principles of rational layout, effective utilization, and balancing current and long-term needs shall be followed, and duplicate construction shall be avoided.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　重要气象设施建设项目应当符合重要气象设施建设规划要求，并在项目建议书和可行性研究报告批准前，征求国务院气象主管机构或者省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构的意见。

**Article 10** Construction projects for important meteorological facilities shall meet the requirements of the construction plans for important meteorological facilities, and opinions shall be solicited from the competent meteorological department of the State Council or the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government before the project proposal and feasibility study report are approved.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家依法保护气象设施，任何组织或者个人不得侵占、损毁或者擅自移动气象设施。  
气象设施因不可抗力遭受破坏时，当地人民政府应当采取紧急措施，组织力量修复，确保气象设施正常运行。

**Article 11** The State protects meteorological facilities according to law. No organization or individual may occupy, damage or without authorization remove meteorological facilities.  
When meteorological facilities are damaged due to force majeure, the local people's government shall take emergency measures and organize forces to repair them so as to ensure the normal operation of meteorological facilities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　未经依法批准，任何组织或者个人不得迁移气象台站；确因实施城市规划或者国家重点工程建设，需要迁移国家基准气候站、基本气象站的，应当报经国务院气象主管机构批准；需要迁移其他气象台站的，应当报经省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构批准。迁建费用由建设单位承担。

**Article 12** No organization or individual may relocate a meteorological station or office without approval according to law. Where it is truly necessary to relocate a national reference climatic station or basic meteorological station due to the implementation of urban planning or national key engineering construction, approval shall be obtained from the competent meteorological department of the State Council; where it is necessary to relocate other meteorological stations or offices, approval shall be obtained from the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. The costs of relocation and reconstruction shall be borne by the construction unit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　气象专用技术装备应当符合国务院气象主管机构规定的技术要求，并经国务院气象主管机构审查合格；未经审查或者审查不合格的，不得在气象业务中使用。

**Article 13** Specialized technical equipment for meteorology shall meet the technical requirements prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the State Council, and shall pass the examination by the competent meteorological department of the State Council; those that have not been examined or have failed the examination shall not be used in meteorological operations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　气象计量器具应当依照《中华人民共和国计量法》的有关规定，经气象计量检定机构检定。未经检定、检定不合格或者超过检定有效期的气象计量器具，不得使用。  
国务院气象主管机构和省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构可以根据需要建立气象计量标准器具，其各项最高计量标准器具依照《中华人民共和国计量法》的规定，经考核合格后，方可使用。

**Article 14** Meteorological measuring instruments shall be verified by meteorological measurement verification institutions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China. Meteorological measuring instruments that have not been verified, have failed verification, or have exceeded the validity period of verification shall not be used.  
The competent meteorological department of the State Council and the competent meteorological departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, as needed, establish meteorological measurement standards, and their highest measurement standards may be used only after passing examination in accordance with the provisions of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第三章　气象探测

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站，应当按照国务院气象主管机构的规定，进行气象探测并向有关气象主管机构汇交气象探测资料。未经上级气象主管机构批准，不得中止气象探测。  
国务院气象主管机构及有关地方气象主管机构应当按照国家规定适时发布基本气象探测资料。

**Article 15** Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to competent meteorological departments at all levels shall conduct meteorological observation and submit meteorological observation data to the relevant competent meteorological departments in accordance with the provisions of the competent meteorological department of the State Council. Without approval from the competent meteorological department at the next higher level, meteorological observation shall not be suspended.  
The competent meteorological department of the State Council and the relevant local competent meteorological departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, issue basic meteorological observation data in a timely manner.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院其他有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门所属的气象台站及其他从事气象探测的组织和个人，应当按照国家有关规定向国务院气象主管机构或者省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构汇交所获得的气象探测资料。  
各级气象主管机构应当按照气象资料共享、共用的原则，根据国家有关规定，与其他从事气象工作的机构交换有关气象信息资料。

**Article 16** Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to other relevant departments of the State Council and other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as other organizations and individuals engaged in meteorological observation, shall submit the meteorological observation data obtained to the competent meteorological department of the State Council or the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Competent meteorological departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the principle of sharing and common use of meteorological data and in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, exchange relevant meteorological information and data with other institutions engaged in meteorological work.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在中华人民共和国内水、领海和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域的海上钻井平台和具有中华人民共和国国籍的在国际航线上飞行的航空器、远洋航行的船舶，应当按照国家有关规定进行气象探测并报告气象探测信息。

**Article 17** Offshore drilling platforms in the internal waters, territorial sea and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, as well as aircraft flying on international routes and ocean-going vessels of the nationality of the People's Republic of China, shall conduct meteorological observation and report meteorological observation information in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　基本气象探测资料以外的气象探测资料需要保密的，其密级的确定、变更和解密以及使用，依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》的规定执行。

**Article 18** Where meteorological observation data other than basic meteorological observation data need to be kept confidential, the determination, alteration, declassification and use of their classification shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家依法保护气象探测环境，任何组织和个人都有保护气象探测环境的义务。

**Article 19** The State protects the meteorological observation environment according to law. All organizations and individuals have the obligation to protect the meteorological observation environment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　禁止下列危害气象探测环境的行为：  
（一）在气象探测环境保护范围内设置障碍物、进行爆破和采石；  
（二）在气象探测环境保护范围内设置影响气象探测设施工作效能的高频电磁辐射装置；  
（三）在气象探测环境保护范围内从事其他影响气象探测的行为。  
气象探测环境保护范围的划定标准由国务院气象主管机构规定。各级人民政府应当按照法定标准划定气象探测环境的保护范围，并纳入城市规划或者村庄和集镇规划。

**Article 20** The following acts endangering the meteorological observation environment are prohibited:  
(1) Setting up obstacles, conducting blasting and quarrying within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment;  
(2) Installing high-frequency electromagnetic radiation devices that affect the working efficiency of meteorological observation facilities within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment;  
(3) Engaging in other acts affecting meteorological observation within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment.  
The standards for delimiting the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment shall be prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the State Council. People's governments at all levels shall delimit the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment in accordance with the statutory standards, and incorporate it into urban planning or village and town planning.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，应当避免危害气象探测环境；确实无法避免的，建设单位应当事先征得省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构的同意，并采取相应的措施后，方可建设。

**Article 21** The construction, expansion or reconstruction of projects shall avoid endangering the meteorological observation environment; where it is truly unavoidable, the construction unit shall obtain the consent of the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in advance, and may proceed with construction only after taking corresponding measures.

### 第四章　气象预报与灾害性天气警报

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对公众气象预报和灾害性天气警报实行统一发布制度。  
各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当按照职责向社会发布公众气象预报和灾害性天气警报，并根据天气变化情况及时补充或者订正。其他任何组织或者个人不得向社会发布公众气象预报和灾害性天气警报。  
国务院其他有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门所属的气象台站，可以发布供本系统使用的专项气象预报。  
各级气象主管机构及其所属的气象台站应当提高公众气象预报和灾害性天气警报的准确性、及时性和服务水平。

**Article 22** The State applies a unified issuance system for public meteorological forecasts and warnings of severe weather.  
Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to competent meteorological departments at all levels shall, in accordance with their functions, issue public meteorological forecasts and warnings of severe weather to the public, and shall make timely supplements or corrections in light of changes in the weather. No other organization or individual may issue public meteorological forecasts or warnings of severe weather to the public.  
Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to other relevant departments of the State Council and other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may issue special meteorological forecasts for use within their own systems.  
Competent meteorological departments at all levels and the meteorological stations and offices subordinate to them shall improve the accuracy, timeliness and service level of public meteorological forecasts and warnings of severe weather.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当根据需要，发布农业气象预报、城市环境气象预报、火险气象等级预报等专业气象预报，并配合军事气象部门进行国防建设所需的气象服务工作。

**Article 23** Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to competent meteorological departments at all levels shall, as needed, issue specialized meteorological forecasts such as agricultural meteorological forecasts, urban environmental meteorological forecasts, and fire risk meteorological grade forecasts, and shall cooperate with military meteorological departments in carrying out meteorological services required for national defense construction.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级广播、电视台站和省级人民政府指定的报纸，应当安排专门的时间或者版面，每天播发或者刊登公众气象预报或者灾害性天气警报。  
各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当保证其制作的气象预报节目的质量。  
广播、电视播出单位改变气象预报节目播发时间安排的，应当事先征得有关气象台站的同意；对国计民生可能产生重大影响的灾害性天气警报和补充、订正的气象预报，应当及时增播或者插播。

**Article 24** Radio and television stations at all levels and newspapers designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall arrange special time slots or pages to broadcast or publish public meteorological forecasts or warnings of severe weather every day.  
Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to competent meteorological departments at all levels shall ensure the quality of the meteorological forecast programs they produce.  
Where radio and television broadcasting units change the broadcast time arrangements for meteorological forecast programs, they shall obtain the consent of the relevant meteorological station or office in advance; for warnings of severe weather that may have a significant impact on the national economy and people's livelihood, as well as supplements or corrections of meteorological forecasts, such broadcasts shall be added or inserted in a timely manner.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　广播、电视、报纸、电信等媒体向社会传播气象预报和灾害性天气警报，必须使用气象主管机构所属的气象台站提供的适时气象信息，并标明发布时间和气象台站的名称。通过传播气象信息获得的收益，应当提取一部分支持气象事业的发展。

**Article 25** Media such as radio, television, newspapers and telecommunications, when disseminating meteorological forecasts and warnings of severe weather to the public, must use the timely meteorological information provided by the meteorological stations or offices subordinate to the competent meteorological departments, and shall indicate the time of issuance and the name of the meteorological station or office. A portion of the proceeds obtained from disseminating meteorological information shall be allocated to support the development of meteorological undertakings.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　信息产业部门应当与气象主管机构密切配合，确保气象通信畅通，准确、及时地传递气象情报、气象预报和灾害性天气警报。  
气象无线电专用频道和信道受国家保护，任何组织或者个人不得挤占和干扰。

**Article 26** The information industry departments shall cooperate closely with the competent meteorological departments to ensure the smoothness of meteorological communications and the accurate and timely transmission of meteorological information, meteorological forecasts and warnings of severe weather.  
Dedicated meteorological radio frequencies and channels are protected by the State. No organization or individual may preempt or interfere with them.

### 第五章　气象灾害防御

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府应当加强气象灾害监测、预警系统建设，组织有关部门编制气象灾害防御规划，并采取有效措施，提高防御气象灾害的能力。有关组织和个人应当服从人民政府的指挥和安排，做好气象灾害防御工作。

**Article 27** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of meteorological disaster monitoring and early warning systems, organize the relevant departments to compile meteorological disaster prevention plans, and take effective measures to improve the capability of preventing and mitigating meteorological disasters. Relevant organizations and individuals shall follow the command and arrangements of the people's governments and carry out meteorological disaster prevention work well.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级气象主管机构应当组织对重大灾害性天气的跨地区、跨部门的联合监测、预报工作，及时提出气象灾害防御措施，并对重大气象灾害作出评估，为本级人民政府组织防御气象灾害提供决策依据。  
各级气象主管机构所属的气象台站应当加强对可能影响当地的灾害性天气的监测和预报，并及时报告有关气象主管机构。其他有关部门所属的气象台站和与灾害性天气监测、预报有关的单位应当及时向气象主管机构提供监测、预报气象灾害所需要的气象探测信息和有关的水情、风暴潮等监测信息。

**Article 28** Competent meteorological departments at all levels shall organize cross-regional and cross-departmental joint monitoring and forecasting of major severe weather, propose meteorological disaster prevention measures in a timely manner, and make assessments of major meteorological disasters, so as to provide decision-making basis for the people's government at the corresponding level in organizing the prevention of meteorological disasters.  
Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to competent meteorological departments at all levels shall strengthen the monitoring and forecasting of severe weather that may affect the local area, and report to the relevant competent meteorological departments in a timely manner. Meteorological stations and offices subordinate to other relevant departments and units related to the monitoring and forecasting of severe weather shall, in a timely manner, provide the competent meteorological departments with the meteorological observation information needed for monitoring and forecasting meteorological disasters, as well as relevant monitoring information on water regimes, storm surges, etc.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据防御气象灾害的需要，制定气象灾害防御方案，并根据气象主管机构提供的气象信息，组织实施气象灾害防御方案，避免或者减轻气象灾害。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to the needs of preventing and mitigating meteorological disasters, formulate meteorological disaster prevention plans, and organize the implementation of such plans based on the meteorological information provided by the competent meteorological departments, so as to avoid or mitigate meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对人工影响天气工作的领导，并根据实际情况，有组织、有计划地开展人工影响天气工作。  
国务院气象主管机构应当加强对全国人工影响天气工作的管理和指导。地方各级气象主管机构应当制定人工影响天气作业方案，并在本级人民政府的领导和协调下，管理、指导和组织实施人工影响天气作业。有关部门应当按照职责分工，配合气象主管机构做好人工影响天气的有关工作。  
实施人工影响天气作业的组织必须具备省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构规定的条件，并使用符合国务院气象主管机构要求的技术标准的作业设备，遵守作业规范。

**Article 30** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over weather modification work, and shall carry out weather modification work in an organized and planned manner based on actual conditions.  
The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall strengthen the management and guidance of nationwide weather modification work. Local competent meteorological departments at all levels shall formulate weather modification operation plans, and under the leadership and coordination of the people's government at the corresponding level, manage, guide and organize the implementation of weather modification operations. Relevant departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, cooperate with the competent meteorological departments in carrying out relevant work on weather modification.  
Organizations performing weather modification operations must meet the conditions prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall use operational equipment that meets the technical standards required by the competent meteorological department of the State Council, and comply with the operational specifications.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级气象主管机构应当加强对雷电灾害防御工作的组织管理，并会同有关部门指导对可能遭受雷击的建筑物、构筑物和其他设施安装的雷电灾害防护装置的检测工作。  
安装的雷电灾害防护装置应当符合国务院气象主管机构规定的使用要求。

**Article 31** Competent meteorological departments at all levels shall strengthen the organization and management of lightning disaster prevention work, and shall, jointly with relevant departments, guide the testing of lightning disaster protection devices installed on buildings, structures and other facilities that may be struck by lightning.  
The installed lightning disaster protection devices shall meet the usage requirements prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the State Council.

### 第六章　气候资源开发利用和保护

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院气象主管机构负责全国气候资源的综合调查、区划工作，组织进行气候监测、分析、评价，并对可能引起气候恶化的大气成分进行监测，定期发布全国气候状况公报。

**Article 32** The competent meteorological department of the State Council shall be responsible for the comprehensive survey and regionalization of climatic resources nationwide, organize climate monitoring, analysis and evaluation, monitor atmospheric components that may cause climate deterioration, and periodically issue a national climate status bulletin.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本地区气候资源的特点，对气候资源开发利用的方向和保护的重点作出规划。  
地方各级气象主管机构应当根据本级人民政府的规划，向本级人民政府和同级有关部门提出利用、保护气候资源和推广应用气候资源区划等成果的建议。

**Article 33** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the characteristics of climatic resources in their respective areas, make plans for the direction of development and utilization of climatic resources and the focus of protection.  
Local competent meteorological departments at all levels shall, in accordance with the plans of the people's governments at the corresponding levels, submit to the people's governments at the corresponding levels and the relevant departments at the same level suggestions on utilizing and protecting climatic resources and on promoting and applying the achievements of climatic resource regionalization.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级气象主管机构应当组织对城市规划、国家重点建设工程、重大区域性经济开发项目和大型太阳能、风能等气候资源开发利用项目进行气候可行性论证。  
具有大气环境影响评价资质的单位进行工程建设项目大气环境影响评价时，应当使用符合国家气象技术标准的气象资料。

**Article 34** Competent meteorological departments at all levels shall organize climate feasibility demonstration for urban planning, national key construction projects, major regional economic development projects, and large-scale solar energy, wind energy and other climatic resource development and utilization projects.  
Units qualified for atmospheric environmental impact assessment shall, when conducting atmospheric environmental impact assessment for engineering construction projects, use meteorological data that meet the national meteorological technical standards.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关气象主管机构按照权限责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以并处五万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）侵占、损毁或者未经批准擅自移动气象设施的；  
（二）在气象探测环境保护范围内从事危害气象探测环境活动的。  
在气象探测环境保护范围内，违法批准占用土地的，或者非法占用土地新建建筑物或者其他设施的，依照《中华人民共和国城乡规划法》或者《中华人民共和国土地管理法》的有关规定处罚。

**Article 35** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the relevant competent meteorological department, within its authority, to cease the illegal act, restore the original state within a time limit or take other remedial measures, and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; where losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Occupying, damaging or removing meteorological facilities without approval;  
(2) Engaging in activities endangering the meteorological observation environment within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment.  
Where within the protection scope of the meteorological observation environment, land is illegally approved for occupation, or land is illegally occupied for constructing new buildings or other facilities, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Urban and Rural Planning Law of the People's Republic of China or the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本法规定，使用不符合技术要求的气象专用技术装备，造成危害的，由有关气象主管机构按照权限责令改正，给予警告，可以并处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, uses specialized technical equipment for meteorology that does not meet the technical requirements and causes harm, shall be ordered by the relevant competent meteorological department, within its authority, to make corrections, given a warning, and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本法规定，安装不符合使用要求的雷电灾害防护装置的，由有关气象主管机构责令改正，给予警告。使用不符合使用要求的雷电灾害防护装置给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 37** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, installs a lightning disaster protection device that does not meet the usage requirements shall be ordered by the relevant competent meteorological department to make corrections and given a warning. Where the use of a lightning disaster protection device that does not meet the usage requirements causes losses to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关气象主管机构按照权限责令改正，给予警告，可以并处五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）非法向社会发布公众气象预报、灾害性天气警报的；  
（二）广播、电视、报纸、电信等媒体向社会传播公众气象预报、灾害性天气警报，不使用气象主管机构所属的气象台站提供的适时气象信息的；  
（三）从事大气环境影响评价的单位进行工程建设项目大气环境影响评价时，使用的气象资料不符合国家气象技术标准的。

**Article 38** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the relevant competent meteorological department, within its authority, to make corrections, given a warning, and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) Illegally issuing public meteorological forecasts or warnings of severe weather to the public;  
(2) Media such as radio, television, newspapers or telecommunications, when disseminating public meteorological forecasts or warnings of severe weather to the public, fail to use the timely meteorological information provided by the meteorological stations or offices subordinate to the competent meteorological departments;  
(3) A unit engaged in atmospheric environmental impact assessment, when conducting atmospheric environmental impact assessment for an engineering construction project, uses meteorological data that do not meet the national meteorological technical standards.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本法规定，不具备省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构规定的条件实施人工影响天气作业的，或者实施人工影响天气作业使用不符合国务院气象主管机构要求的技术标准的作业设备的，由有关气象主管机构按照权限责令改正，给予警告，可以并处十万元以下的罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, conducts weather modification operations without meeting the conditions prescribed by the competent meteorological department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, or uses operational equipment that does not meet the technical standards required by the competent meteorological department of the State Council when conducting weather modification operations, shall be ordered by the relevant competent meteorological department, within its authority, to make corrections, given a warning, and may also be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; where losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　各级气象主管机构及其所属气象台站的工作人员由于玩忽职守，导致重大漏报、错报公众气象预报、灾害性天气警报，以及丢失或者毁坏原始气象探测资料、伪造气象资料等事故的，依法给予行政处分；致使国家利益和人民生命财产遭受重大损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where staff members of competent meteorological departments at all levels or the meteorological stations and offices subordinate to them, due to dereliction of duty, cause major omissions or errors in public meteorological forecasts or warnings of severe weather, or cause accidents such as loss or destruction of original meteorological observation data, or falsification of meteorological data, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; where such acts cause major losses to the interests of the State and the life and property of the people and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本法中下列用语的含义是：  
（一）气象设施，是指气象探测设施、气象信息专用传输设施、大型气象专用技术装备等。  
（二）气象探测，是指利用科技手段对大气和近地层的大气物理过程、现象及其化学性质等进行的系统观察和测量。  
（三）气象探测环境，是指为避开各种干扰保证气象探测设施准确获得气象探测信息所必需的最小距离构成的环境空间。  
（四）气象灾害，是指台风、暴雨（雪）、寒潮、大风（沙尘暴）、低温、高温、干旱、雷电、冰雹、霜冻和大雾等所造成的灾害。  
（五）人工影响天气，是指为避免或者减轻气象灾害，合理利用气候资源，在适当条件下通过科技手段对局部大气的物理、化学过程进行人工影响，实现增雨雪、防雹、消雨、消雾、防霜等目的的活动。

**Article 41** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings specified below:  
(1) "Meteorological facilities" means meteorological observation facilities, dedicated meteorological information transmission facilities, large-scale specialized meteorological technical equipment, etc.  
(2) "Meteorological observation" means systematic observation and measurement of the atmosphere and near-surface atmospheric physical processes, phenomena and their chemical properties using scientific and technological means.  
(3) "Meteorological observation environment" means the environmental space constituted by the minimum distance necessary to avoid various interferences and ensure that meteorological observation facilities accurately obtain meteorological observation information.  
(4) "Meteorological disasters" means disasters caused by typhoons, rainstorms (snowstorms), cold waves, strong winds (sandstorms), low temperatures, high temperatures, droughts, lightning, hailstorms, frost, heavy fog, etc.  
(5) "Weather modification" means activities carried out to avoid or mitigate meteorological disasters, rationally utilize climatic resources, and artificially influence the physical and chemical processes of the local atmosphere through scientific and technological means under appropriate conditions, so as to achieve the purposes of increasing precipitation and snow, preventing hail, dispersing rain and fog, and preventing frost.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　气象台站和其他开展气象有偿服务的单位，从事气象有偿服务的范围、项目、收费等具体管理办法，由国务院依据本法规定。

**Article 42** The specific administrative measures for the scope, items, charging and other matters concerning the provision of fee-based meteorological services by meteorological stations and offices and other units providing fee-based meteorological services shall be formulated by the State Council in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　中国人民解放军气象工作的管理办法，由中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 43** The administrative measures for the meteorological work of the Chinese People's Liberation Army shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关气象活动的国际条约与本法有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 44** Where an international treaty on meteorological activities concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions different from those of this Law, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply, except for the clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本法自2000年1月1日起施行。1994年8月18日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国气象条例》同时废止。

**Article 45** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2000. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Meteorology promulgated by the State Council on August 18, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
