# 中华人民共和国矿山安全法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1992年11月7日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　矿山建设的安全保障 第三章　矿山开采的安全保障 第四章　矿山企业的安全管理 第五章　矿山安全的监督和管理 第六章　矿山事故处理 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障矿山生产安全，防止矿山事故，保护矿山职工人身安全，促进采矿业的发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事矿产资源开采活动，必须遵守本法。 第三条　矿山企业必须具有保障安全生产的设施，建立、健全安全管理制度，采取有效措施改善职工劳动条件，加强矿山安全管理工作，保证安全生产。 第四条　国务院劳动行政主管部门对全国矿山安全工作实施统一监督。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障矿山生产安全，防止矿山事故，保护矿山职工人身安全，促进采矿业的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to ensure safety in mine production, prevent mine accidents, protect the personal safety of mine workers, and promote the development of the mining industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事矿产资源开采活动，必须遵守本法。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in mineral resource extraction activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China must comply with this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　矿山企业必须具有保障安全生产的设施，建立、健全安全管理制度，采取有效措施改善职工劳动条件，加强矿山安全管理工作，保证安全生产。

**Article 3** Mine enterprises must have facilities to guarantee safe production, establish and perfect safety management systems, adopt effective measures to improve workers' working conditions, strengthen mine safety management, and ensure safe production.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院劳动行政主管部门对全国矿山安全工作实施统一监督。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府劳动行政主管部门对本行政区域内的矿山安全工作实施统一监督。  
县级以上人民政府管理矿山企业的主管部门对矿山安全工作进行管理。

**Article 4** The administrative department for labour under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision over the national mine safety work.  
The administrative departments for labour of local people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise unified supervision over the mine safety work within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent departments of people's governments at or above the county level in charge of mine enterprises shall administer the mine safety work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励矿山安全科学技术研究，推广先进技术，改进安全设施，提高矿山安全生产水平。

**Article 5** The State encourages scientific and technological research on mine safety, promotes advanced technologies, improves safety facilities, and enhances the level of safe production in mines.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对坚持矿山安全生产，防止矿山事故，参加矿山抢险救护，进行矿山安全科学技术研究等方面取得显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 6** Units and individuals that have achieved remarkable results in persisting in safe production in mines, preventing mine accidents, participating in mine rescue and emergency response, and conducting scientific and technological research on mine safety shall be rewarded.

### 第二章　矿山建设的安全保障

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　矿山建设工程的安全设施必须和主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入生产和使用。

**Article 7** The safety facilities of a mine construction project must be designed, constructed, and put into production and use simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　矿山建设工程的设计文件，必须符合矿山安全规程和行业技术规范，并按照国家规定经管理矿山企业的主管部门批准；不符合矿山安全规程和行业技术规范的，不得批准。  
矿山建设工程安全设施的设计必须有劳动行政主管部门参加审查。  
矿山安全规程和行业技术规范，由国务院管理矿山企业的主管部门制定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例.md#art-7" title="被《中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The design documents of a mine construction project must conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications, and shall be approved by the competent department in charge of mine enterprises as prescribed by the State; if they do not conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications, they shall not be approved.  
The design of the safety facilities of a mine construction project must be examined with the participation of the administrative department for labour.  
The mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications shall be formulated by the competent department under the State Council in charge of mine enterprises.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　矿山设计下列项目必须符合矿山安全规程和行业技术规范：  
（一）矿井的通风系统和供风量、风质、风速；  
（二）露天矿的边坡角和台阶的宽度、高度；  
（三）供电系统；  
（四）提升、运输系统；  
（五）防水、排水系统和防火、灭火系统；  
（六）防瓦斯系统和防尘系统；  
（七）有关矿山安全的其他项目。

**Article 9** The following items in a mine design must conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications:  
(1) the ventilation system and the air supply volume, air quality, and air speed of the mine;  
(2) the slope angle of open-pit mines and the width and height of benches;  
(3) the power supply system;  
(4) the hoisting and transportation systems;  
(5) the waterproof and drainage systems and the fire prevention and extinguishing systems;  
(6) the gas prevention system and the dust prevention system;  
(7) other items related to mine safety.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　每个矿井必须有两个以上能行人的安全出口，出口之间的直线水平距离必须符合矿山安全规程和行业技术规范。

**Article 10** Each mine must have at least two accessible safety exits, and the straight-line horizontal distance between the exits must conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　矿山必须有与外界相通的、符合安全要求的运输和通讯设施。

**Article 11** A mine must have transportation and communication facilities that are connected with the outside and meet safety requirements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　矿山建设工程必须按照管理矿山企业的主管部门批准的设计文件施工。  
矿山建设工程安全设施竣工后，由管理矿山企业的主管部门验收，并须有劳动行政主管部门参加；不符合矿山安全规程和行业技术规范的，不得验收，不得投入生产。

**Article 12** A mine construction project must be constructed in accordance with the design documents approved by the competent department in charge of mine enterprises.  
After the completion of the safety facilities of a mine construction project, they shall be accepted by the competent department in charge of mine enterprises with the participation of the administrative department for labour; if they do not conform to the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications, they shall not be accepted and shall not be put into production.

### 第三章　矿山开采的安全保障

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　矿山开采必须具备保障安全生产的条件，执行开采不同矿种的矿山安全规程和行业技术规范。

**Article 13** Mining operations must meet the conditions for ensuring safe production and comply with the mine safety regulations and industry technical specifications applicable to different types of minerals.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　矿山设计规定保留的矿柱、岩柱，在规定的期限内，应当予以保护，不得开采或者毁坏。

**Article 14** Pillars and rock pillars reserved as specified in the mine design shall be protected within the specified period and shall not be mined or destroyed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　矿山使用的有特殊安全要求的设备、器材、防护用品和安全检测仪器，必须符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准；不符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准的，不得使用。

**Article 15** Equipment, materials, protective articles, and safety testing instruments with special safety requirements used in mines must conform to national safety standards or industry safety standards; if they do not conform to national safety standards or industry safety standards, they shall not be used.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　矿山企业必须对机电设备及其防护装置、安全检测仪器，定期检查、维修，保证使用安全。

**Article 16** Mine enterprises must regularly inspect and maintain mechanical and electrical equipment and their protective devices, as well as safety testing instruments, to ensure safe use.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　矿山企业必须对作业场所中的有毒有害物质和井下空气含氧量进行检测，保证符合安全要求。

**Article 17** Mine enterprises must detect toxic and hazardous substances in the workplace and the oxygen content in the underground air to ensure compliance with safety requirements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　矿山企业必须对下列危害安全的事故隐患采取预防措施：  
（一）冒顶、片帮、边坡滑落和地表塌陷；  
（二）瓦斯爆炸、煤尘爆炸；  
（三）冲击地压、瓦斯突出、井喷；  
（四）地面和井下的火灾、水害；  
（五）爆破器材和爆破作业发生的危害；  
（六）粉尘、有毒有害气体、放射性物质和其他有害物质引起的危害；  
（七）其他危害。

**Article 18** Mine enterprises must take preventive measures against the following hazards to safety:  
(1) roof falls, rib spalling, slope sliding, and surface subsidence;  
(2) gas explosions and coal dust explosions;  
(3) rock bursts, gas outbursts, and blowouts;  
(4) surface and underground fires and water hazards;  
(5) hazards arising from blasting materials and blasting operations;  
(6) hazards caused by dust, toxic and harmful gases, radioactive substances, and other harmful substances;  
(7) other hazards.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　矿山企业对使用机械、电气设备，排土场、矸石山、尾矿库与矿山闭坑后可能引起的危害，应当采取预防措施。

**Article 19** Mine enterprises shall take preventive measures against hazards that may be caused by the use of machinery and electrical equipment, waste dumps, waste rock piles, tailings ponds, and the possible consequences after mine closure.

### 第四章　矿山企业的安全管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　矿山企业必须建立、健全安全生产责任制。  
矿长对本企业的安全生产工作负责。

**Article 20** Mine enterprises must establish and perfect a responsibility system for safe production.  
The mine manager shall be responsible for the safe production work of the enterprise.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　矿长应当定期向职工代表大会或者职工大会报告安全生产工作，发挥职工代表大会的监督作用。

**Article 21** The mine manager shall regularly report on safe production work to the workers' congress or the general meeting of workers, and shall play the supervisory role of the workers' congress.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　矿山企业职工必须遵守有关矿山安全的法律、法规和企业规章制度。  
矿山企业职工有权对危害安全的行为，提出批评、检举和控告。

**Article 22** Workers in mine enterprises must comply with the laws, regulations, and enterprise rules and regulations concerning mine safety.  
Workers in mine enterprises shall have the right to criticize, report, and accuse acts that endanger safety.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　矿山企业工会依法维护职工生产安全的合法权益，组织职工对矿山安全工作进行监督。

**Article 23** The trade union of a mine enterprise shall, according to law, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of workers in production safety and organize workers to supervise the mine safety work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　矿山企业违反有关安全的法律、法规，工会有权要求企业行政方面或者有关部门认真处理。  
矿山企业召开讨论有关安全生产的会议，应当有工会代表参加，工会有权提出意见和建议。

**Article 24** If a mine enterprise violates the laws and regulations concerning safety, the trade union shall have the right to demand that the administrative side of the enterprise or the relevant department deal with the matter seriously.  
When a mine enterprise holds a meeting to discuss matters concerning safe production, trade union representatives shall participate, and the trade union shall have the right to put forward opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　矿山企业工会发现企业行政方面违章指挥、强令工人冒险作业或者生产过程中发现明显重大事故隐患和职业危害，有权提出解决的建议；发现危及职工生命安全的情况时，有权向矿山企业行政方面建议组织职工撤离危险现场，矿山企业行政方面必须及时作出处理决定。

**Article 25** Where the trade union of a mine enterprise discovers that the administrative side of the enterprise directs operations in violation of regulations, compels workers to work under risk, or finds obvious major hidden dangers of accidents and occupational hazards during the production process, it shall have the right to propose solutions; when it finds circumstances that endanger the life safety of workers, it shall have the right to suggest to the administrative side of the mine enterprise that workers be evacuated from the dangerous site, and the administrative side of the mine enterprise must promptly make a decision on handling the matter.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　矿山企业必须对职工进行安全教育、培训；未经安全教育、培训的，不得上岗作业。  
矿山企业安全生产的特种作业人员必须接受专门培训，经考核合格取得操作资格证书的，方可上岗作业。

**Article 26** Mine enterprises must provide safety education and training to workers; those who have not received safety education and training shall not be assigned to work.  
Workers engaged in special types of operations for safe production in mine enterprises must undergo special training, and may only take up their posts after passing the examination and obtaining a qualification certificate for operations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　矿长必须经过考核，具备安全专业知识，具有领导安全生产和处理矿山事故的能力。  
矿山企业安全工作人员必须具备必要的安全专业知识和矿山安全工作经验。

**Article 27** The mine manager must pass an examination, possess professional knowledge of safety, and have the ability to lead safe production and handle mine accidents.  
Safety management personnel of mine enterprises must possess the necessary professional knowledge of safety and experience in mine safety work.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　矿山企业必须向职工发放保障安全生产所需的劳动防护用品。

**Article 28** Mine enterprises must distribute to workers the labour protection articles necessary to ensure safe production.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　矿山企业不得录用未成年人从事矿山井下劳动。  
矿山企业对女职工按照国家规定实行特殊劳动保护，不得分配女职工从事矿山井下劳动。

**Article 29** Mine enterprises shall not employ minors to work underground in mines.  
Mine enterprises shall provide special labour protection for female workers as prescribed by the State, and shall not assign female workers to work underground in mines.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　矿山企业必须制定矿山事故防范措施，并组织落实。

**Article 30** Mine enterprises must formulate measures for preventing mine accidents and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　矿山企业应当建立由专职或者兼职人员组成的救护和医疗急救组织，配备必要的装备、器材和药物。

**Article 31** Mine enterprises shall establish rescue and medical emergency organizations composed of full-time or part-time personnel, and be equipped with necessary equipment, materials, and medicines.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　矿山企业必须从矿产品销售额中按照国家规定提取安全技术措施专项费用。安全技术措施专项费用必须全部用于改善矿山安全生产条件，不得挪作他用。

**Article 32** Mine enterprises must, in accordance with State provisions, allocate special funds for safety technical measures from the sales revenue of mineral products. The special funds for safety technical measures must be fully used to improve mine safe production conditions and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

### 第五章　矿山安全的监督和管理

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上各级人民政府劳动行政主管部门对矿山安全工作行使下列监督职责：  
（一）检查矿山企业和管理矿山企业的主管部门贯彻执行矿山安全法律、法规的情况；  
（二）参加矿山建设工程安全设施的设计审查和竣工验收；  
（三）检查矿山劳动条件和安全状况；  
（四）检查矿山企业职工安全教育、培训工作；  
（五）监督矿山企业提取和使用安全技术措施专项费用的情况；  
（六）参加并监督矿山事故的调查和处理；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他监督职责。

**Article 33** The administrative departments for labour of people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise the following supervisory duties over mine safety work:  
(1) inspecting the implementation of mine safety laws and regulations by mine enterprises and the competent departments in charge of mine enterprises;  
(2) participating in the design review and completion acceptance of safety facilities of mine construction projects;  
(3) inspecting mine working conditions and safety status;  
(4) inspecting the safety education and training of workers in mine enterprises;  
(5) supervising the allocation and use of special funds for safety technical measures by mine enterprises;  
(6) participating in and supervising the investigation and handling of mine accidents;  
(7) other supervisory duties as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府管理矿山企业的主管部门对矿山安全工作行使下列管理职责：  
（一）检查矿山企业贯彻执行矿山安全法律、法规的情况；  
（二）审查批准矿山建设工程安全设施的设计；  
（三）负责矿山建设工程安全设施的竣工验收；  
（四）组织矿长和矿山企业安全工作人员的培训工作；  
（五）调查和处理重大矿山事故；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他管理职责。

**Article 34** The competent departments of people's governments at or above the county level in charge of mine enterprises shall exercise the following management duties over mine safety work:  
(1) inspecting the implementation of mine safety laws and regulations by mine enterprises;  
(2) examining and approving the design of safety facilities of mine construction projects;  
(3) being responsible for the completion acceptance of safety facilities of mine construction projects;  
(4) organizing the training of mine managers and safety management personnel of mine enterprises;  
(5) investigating and handling major mine accidents;  
(6) other management duties as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　劳动行政主管部门的矿山安全监督人员有权进入矿山企业，在现场检查安全状况；发现有危及职工安全的紧急险情时，应当要求矿山企业立即处理。

**Article 35** The mine safety supervision personnel of the administrative department for labour shall have the right to enter mine enterprises and inspect the safety conditions on site; when they find emergency dangers endangering the safety of workers, they shall demand that the mine enterprise deal with them immediately.

### 第六章　矿山事故处理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　发生矿山事故，矿山企业必须立即组织抢救，防止事故扩大，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，对伤亡事故必须立即如实报告劳动行政主管部门和管理矿山企业的主管部门。

**Article 36** When a mine accident occurs, the mine enterprise must immediately organize rescue, prevent the accident from escalating, reduce casualties and property losses, and must promptly and truthfully report the casualty accident to the administrative department for labour and the competent department in charge of mine enterprises.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　发生一般矿山事故，由矿山企业负责调查和处理。  
发生重大矿山事故，由政府及其有关部门、工会和矿山企业按照行政法规的规定进行调查和处理。

**Article 37** General mine accidents shall be investigated and handled by the mine enterprise.  
Major mine accidents shall be investigated and handled by the government and its relevant departments, the trade union, and the mine enterprise in accordance with the provisions of administrative regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　矿山企业对矿山事故中伤亡的职工按照国家规定给予抚恤或者补偿。

**Article 38** Mine enterprises shall, in accordance with State provisions, provide pensions or compensation to workers killed or injured in mine accidents.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　矿山事故发生后，应当尽快消除现场危险，查明事故原因，提出防范措施。现场危险消除后，方可恢复生产。

**Article 39** After a mine accident, the dangers at the scene shall be eliminated as soon as possible, the cause of the accident shall be ascertained, and preventive measures shall be proposed. Production may be resumed only after the dangers at the scene have been eliminated.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由劳动行政主管部门责令改正，可以并处罚款；情节严重的，提请县级以上人民政府决定责令停产整顿；对主管人员和直接责任人员由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分：  
（一）未对职工进行安全教育、培训，分配职工上岗作业的；  
（二）使用不符合国家安全标准或者行业安全标准的设备、器材、防护用品、安全检测仪器的；  
（三）未按照规定提取或者使用安全技术措施专项费用的；  
（四）拒绝矿山安全监督人员现场检查或者在被检查时隐瞒事故隐患、不如实反映情况的；  
（五）未按照规定及时、如实报告矿山事故的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例.md#art-52" title="被《中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 40** Any person who violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to rectify by the administrative department for labour, and may also be fined; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be submitted to the people's government at or above the county level for a decision to order suspension of production for rectification; the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions by their unit or the competent authority at a higher level:  
(1) failing to provide safety education and training to workers and assigning workers to work;  
(2) using equipment, materials, protective articles, or safety testing instruments that do not conform to national safety standards or industry safety standards;  
(3) failing to allocate or use special funds for safety technical measures as prescribed;  
(4) refusing on-site inspection by mine safety supervision personnel, or concealing hidden dangers of accidents or failing to truthfully report the situation during inspection;  
(5) failing to timely and truthfully report mine accidents as prescribed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　矿长不具备安全专业知识的，安全生产的特种作业人员未取得操作资格证书上岗作业的，由劳动行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，提请县级以上人民政府决定责令停产，调整配备合格人员后，方可恢复生产。

**Article 41** If the mine manager does not possess professional knowledge of safety, or if workers engaged in special types of operations for safe production take up their posts without obtaining qualification certificates for operations, the administrative department for labour shall order rectification within a specified time limit; if they fail to rectify within the time limit, the matter shall be submitted to the people's government at or above the county level for a decision to order suspension of production, and production may be resumed only after qualified personnel have been assigned.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　矿山建设工程安全设施的设计未经批准擅自施工的，由管理矿山企业的主管部门责令停止施工；拒不执行的，由管理矿山企业的主管部门提请县级以上人民政府决定由有关主管部门吊销其采矿许可证和营业执照。

**Article 42** If construction of a mine construction project is carried out without approval of the design of safety facilities, the competent department in charge of mine enterprises shall order the construction to cease; if the order is not complied with, the competent department in charge of mine enterprises shall submit the matter to the people's government at or above the county level for a decision that the relevant competent authority shall revoke its mining license and business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　矿山建设工程的安全设施未经验收或者验收不合格擅自投入生产的，由劳动行政主管部门会同管理矿山企业的主管部门责令停止生产，并由劳动行政主管部门处以罚款；拒不停止生产的，由劳动行政主管部门提请县级以上人民政府决定由有关主管部门吊销其采矿许可证和营业执照。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国矿山安全法实施条例》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 43** If the safety facilities of a mine construction project are put into production without acceptance or after failing acceptance, the administrative department for labour, jointly with the competent department in charge of mine enterprises, shall order production to cease, and the administrative department for labour shall impose a fine; if production is not ceased, the administrative department for labour shall submit the matter to the people's government at or above the county level for a decision that the relevant competent authority shall revoke its mining license and business license.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　已经投入生产的矿山企业，不具备安全生产条件而强行开采的，由劳动行政主管部门会同管理矿山企业的主管部门责令限期改进；逾期仍不具备安全生产条件的，由劳动行政主管部门提请县级以上人民政府决定责令停产整顿或者由有关主管部门吊销其采矿许可证和营业执照。

**Article 44** If a mine enterprise already put into production forcibly mines without meeting safe production conditions, the administrative department for labour, jointly with the competent department in charge of mine enterprises, shall order rectification within a specified time limit; if it still does not meet safe production conditions after the time limit, the administrative department for labour shall submit the matter to the people's government at or above the county level for a decision to order suspension of production for rectification or that the relevant competent authority shall revoke its mining license and business license.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚决定通知之日起十五日内向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；当事人也可以在接到处罚决定通知之日起十五日内直接向人民法院起诉。  
复议机关应当在接到复议申请之日起六十日内作出复议决定。当事人对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。复议机关逾期不作出复议决定的，当事人可以在复议期满之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。  
当事人逾期不申请复议也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 45** If a party is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receiving the notice of the penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the penalty decision; the party may also, within fifteen days from the date of receiving the notice of the penalty decision, directly file a prosecution with the People's Court.  
The reconsideration authority shall, within sixty days from the date of receiving the application for reconsideration, make a reconsideration decision. If the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receiving the reconsideration decision, file a prosecution with the People's Court. If the reconsideration authority fails to make a reconsideration decision within the time limit, the party may, within fifteen days from the expiry of the reconsideration period, file a prosecution with the People's Court.  
If the party neither applies for reconsideration nor files a prosecution with the People's Court within the time limit, nor complies with the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　矿山企业主管人员违章指挥、强令工人冒险作业，因而发生重大伤亡事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** If a person in charge of a mine enterprise directs operations in violation of regulations or compels workers to work under risk, thereby causing a major casualty accident, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　矿山企业主管人员对矿山事故隐患不采取措施，因而发生重大伤亡事故的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** If a person in charge of a mine enterprise fails to take measures against hidden dangers of mine accidents, thereby causing a major casualty accident, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　矿山安全监督人员和安全管理人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。

**Article 48** If mine safety supervision personnel and safety management personnel abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院劳动行政主管部门根据本法制定实施条例，报国务院批准施行。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法和本地区的实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 49** The administrative department for labour under the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementing regulations, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before coming into force.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate implementing measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自1993年5月1日起施行。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 1993.
