**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2009-06-27  
**生效日期**：2010-01-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了公正、及时解决农村土地承包经营纠纷，维护当事人的合法权益，促进农村经济发展和社会稳定，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of resolving rural land contract management disputes fairly and promptly, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the parties, and promoting rural economic development and social stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　农村土地承包经营纠纷调解和仲裁，适用本法。  
农村土地承包经营纠纷包括：  
（一）因订立、履行、变更、解除和终止农村土地承包合同发生的纠纷；  
（二）因农村土地承包经营权转包、出租、互换、转让、入股等流转发生的纠纷；  
（三）因收回、调整承包地发生的纠纷；  
（四）因确认农村土地承包经营权发生的纠纷；  
（五）因侵害农村土地承包经营权发生的纠纷；  
（六）法律、法规规定的其他农村土地承包经营纠纷。  
因征收集体所有的土地及其补偿发生的纠纷，不属于农村土地承包仲裁委员会的受理范围，可以通过行政复议或者诉讼等方式解决。

**Article 2** Mediation and arbitration of rural land contract management disputes shall be governed by this Law.  
Rural land contract management disputes include:  
(1) disputes arising from the conclusion, performance, modification, rescission and termination of rural land contract contracts;  
(2) disputes arising from the circulation of rural land contract management rights such as subcontracting, leasing, exchange, transfer, and contribution of shares;  
(3) disputes arising from the recovery or adjustment of contracted land;  
(4) disputes arising from the confirmation of rural land contract management rights;  
(5) disputes arising from the infringement of rural land contract management rights;  
(6) other rural land contract management disputes as provided by laws and regulations.  
Disputes arising from the expropriation of collectively owned land and its compensation shall not fall within the scope of acceptance of the rural land contract arbitration committee, and may be resolved through administrative reconsideration or litigation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　发生农村土地承包经营纠纷的，当事人可以自行和解，也可以请求村民委员会、乡（镇）人民政府等调解。

**Article 3** In the event of a rural land contract management dispute, the parties may settle the dispute on their own, or may request mediation by the villagers committee, the township (town) people's government, or other entities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人和解、调解不成或者不愿和解、调解的，可以向农村土地承包仲裁委员会申请仲裁，也可以直接向人民法院起诉。

**Article 4** If the parties fail to reach a settlement or mediation, or are unwilling to settle or mediate, they may apply for arbitration to the rural land contract arbitration committee, or may directly initiate prosecution in the People's Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　农村土地承包经营纠纷调解和仲裁，应当公开、公平、公正，便民高效，根据事实，符合法律，尊重社会公德。

**Article 5** Mediation and arbitration of rural land contract management disputes shall be open, fair, and just, convenient and efficient, based on facts, in conformity with the law, and respectful of social ethics.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对农村土地承包经营纠纷调解和仲裁工作的指导。  
县级以上人民政府农村土地承包管理部门及其他有关部门应当依照职责分工，支持有关调解组织和农村土地承包仲裁委员会依法开展工作。

**Article 6** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen guidance on mediation and arbitration of rural land contract management disputes.  
The rural land contract management departments of people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, support relevant mediation organizations and rural land contract arbitration committees in carrying out their work according to law.

### 第二章　调解

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　村民委员会、乡（镇）人民政府应当加强农村土地承包经营纠纷的调解工作，帮助当事人达成协议解决纠纷。

**Article 7** The villagers committee and the township (town) people's government shall strengthen mediation work for rural land contract management disputes, and assist the parties in reaching an agreement to resolve the dispute.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人申请农村土地承包经营纠纷调解可以书面申请，也可以口头申请。口头申请的，由村民委员会或者乡（镇）人民政府当场记录申请人的基本情况、申请调解的纠纷事项、理由和时间。

**Article 8** A party applying for mediation of a rural land contract management dispute may apply in writing, or may make an oral application. For an oral application, the villagers committee or the township (town) people's government shall record on the spot the basic information of the applicant, the matters of the dispute for which mediation is sought, the reasons, and the time.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　调解农村土地承包经营纠纷，村民委员会或者乡（镇）人民政府应当充分听取当事人对事实和理由的陈述，讲解有关法律以及国家政策，耐心疏导，帮助当事人达成协议。

**Article 9** When mediating a rural land contract management dispute, the villagers committee or the township (town) people's government shall fully hear the parties' statements on the facts and reasons, explain relevant laws and national policies, patiently counsel, and assist the parties in reaching an agreement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经调解达成协议的，村民委员会或者乡（镇）人民政府应当制作调解协议书。  
调解协议书由双方当事人签名、盖章或者按指印，经调解人员签名并加盖调解组织印章后生效。

**Article 10** If an agreement is reached through mediation, the villagers committee or the township (town) people's government shall produce a mediation agreement.  
The mediation agreement shall be signed, sealed or fingerprinted by both parties, and shall take effect after being signed by the mediators and sealed with the seal of the mediation organization.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　仲裁庭对农村土地承包经营纠纷应当进行调解。调解达成协议的，仲裁庭应当制作调解书；调解不成的，应当及时作出裁决。  
调解书应当写明仲裁请求和当事人协议的结果。调解书由仲裁员签名，加盖农村土地承包仲裁委员会印章，送达双方当事人。  
调解书经双方当事人签收后，即发生法律效力。在调解书签收前当事人反悔的，仲裁庭应当及时作出裁决。

**Article 11** The arbitral tribunal shall conduct mediation for rural land contract management disputes. If an agreement is reached through mediation, the arbitral tribunal shall produce a mediation document; if mediation fails, it shall promptly render an award.  
The mediation document shall state the arbitration claims and the result of the parties' agreement. The mediation document shall be signed by the arbitrators, sealed with the seal of the rural land contract arbitration committee, and served to both parties.  
The mediation document shall take effect upon receipt and signature by both parties. If a party retracts before receipt and signature of the mediation document, the arbitral tribunal shall promptly render an award.

### 第三章　仲裁

#### 第一节　仲裁委员会和仲裁员

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会，根据解决农村土地承包经营纠纷的实际需要设立。农村土地承包仲裁委员会可以在县和不设区的市设立，也可以在设区的市或者其市辖区设立。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会在当地人民政府指导下设立。设立农村土地承包仲裁委员会的，其日常工作由当地农村土地承包管理部门承担。

**Article 12** Rural land contract arbitration committees shall be established based on the actual need for resolving rural land contract management disputes. Rural land contract arbitration committees may be established in counties and cities without districts, and may also be established in cities with districts or their districts.  
Rural land contract arbitration committees shall be established under the guidance of the local people's government. Where a rural land contract arbitration committee is established, its daily work shall be undertaken by the local rural land contract management department.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会由当地人民政府及其有关部门代表、有关人民团体代表、农村集体经济组织代表、农民代表和法律、经济等相关专业人员兼任组成，其中农民代表和法律、经济等相关专业人员不得少于组成人员的二分之一。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会设主任一人、副主任一至二人和委员若干人。主任、副主任由全体组成人员选举产生。

**Article 13** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall be composed of concurrent members including representatives of the local people's government and its relevant departments, representatives of relevant people's organizations, representatives of rural collective economic organizations, representatives of farmers, and relevant professionals in law and economics, among whom the representatives of farmers and relevant professionals in law and economics shall not be less than one-half of the total members.  
A rural land contract arbitration committee shall have one chairperson, one to two vice chairpersons, and several members. The chairperson and vice chairpersons shall be elected by all members.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会依法履行下列职责：  
（一）聘任、解聘仲裁员；  
（二）受理仲裁申请；  
（三）监督仲裁活动。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当依照本法制定章程，对其组成人员的产生方式及任期、议事规则等作出规定。

**Article 14** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall perform the following duties according to law:  
(1) appointing and removing arbitrators;  
(2) accepting applications for arbitration;  
(3) supervising arbitration activities.  
A rural land contract arbitration committee shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate its charter to provide for the method of selection and term of office of its members, rules of procedure, and other matters.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当从公道正派的人员中聘任仲裁员。  
仲裁员应当符合下列条件之一：  
（一）从事农村土地承包管理工作满五年；  
（二）从事法律工作或者人民调解工作满五年；  
（三）在当地威信较高，并熟悉农村土地承包法律以及国家政策的居民。

**Article 15** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall appoint arbitrators from among persons who are fair and upright.  
An arbitrator shall meet one of the following conditions:  
(1) having been engaged in rural land contract management work for at least five years;  
(2) having been engaged in legal work or people's mediation work for at least five years;  
(3) being a resident with high prestige in the locality and familiar with rural land contract laws and national policies.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当对仲裁员进行农村土地承包法律以及国家政策的培训。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农村土地承包管理部门应当制定仲裁员培训计划，加强对仲裁员培训工作的组织和指导。

**Article 16** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall provide training to arbitrators on rural land contract laws and national policies.  
The rural land contract management departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate training plans for arbitrators and strengthen the organization and guidance of arbitrator training.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会组成人员、仲裁员应当依法履行职责，遵守农村土地承包仲裁委员会章程和仲裁规则，不得索贿受贿、徇私舞弊，不得侵害当事人的合法权益。  
仲裁员有索贿受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁决以及接受当事人请客送礼等违法违纪行为的，农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当将其除名；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
县级以上地方人民政府及有关部门应当受理对农村土地承包仲裁委员会组成人员、仲裁员违法违纪行为的投诉和举报，并依法组织查处。

**Article 17** Members and arbitrators of a rural land contract arbitration committee shall perform their duties according to law, abide by the charter and arbitration rules of the rural land contract arbitration committee, and shall not solicit or accept bribes, engage in graft, or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the parties.  
If an arbitrator engages in illegal or disciplinary acts such as soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in graft, rendering a judgment perverting the law, or accepting treats or gifts from the parties, the rural land contract arbitration committee shall remove him; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall accept complaints and reports concerning illegal or disciplinary acts by members and arbitrators of the rural land contract arbitration committee, and shall organize investigation and handling according to law.

#### 第二节　申请和受理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农村土地承包经营纠纷申请仲裁的时效期间为二年，自当事人知道或者应当知道其权利被侵害之日起计算。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** The limitation period for applying for arbitration of a rural land contract management dispute shall be two years, counting from the date on which the party knows or should have known that his rights have been infringed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁的申请人、被申请人为当事人。家庭承包的，可以由农户代表人参加仲裁。当事人一方人数众多的，可以推选代表人参加仲裁。  
与案件处理结果有利害关系的，可以申请作为第三人参加仲裁，或者由农村土地承包仲裁委员会通知其参加仲裁。  
当事人、第三人可以委托代理人参加仲裁。

**Article 19** The applicant and respondent in arbitration of a rural land contract management dispute shall be the parties. For household contracts, the representative of the rural household may participate in arbitration. If one party has a large number of persons, they may elect representatives to participate in arbitration.  
A person who has an interest in the result of the case may apply to participate in arbitration as a third party, or may be notified by the rural land contract arbitration committee to participate in arbitration.  
The parties and third parties may authorize agents to participate in arbitration.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁应当符合下列条件：  
（一）申请人与纠纷有直接的利害关系；  
（二）有明确的被申请人；  
（三）有具体的仲裁请求和事实、理由；  
（四）属于农村土地承包仲裁委员会的受理范围。

**Article 20** An application for arbitration of a rural land contract management dispute shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the applicant has a direct interest in the dispute;  
(2) there is a definite respondent;  
(3) there are specific arbitration claims and facts and grounds;  
(4) the dispute falls within the scope of acceptance of the rural land contract arbitration committee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人申请仲裁，应当向纠纷涉及的土地所在地的农村土地承包仲裁委员会递交仲裁申请书。仲裁申请书可以邮寄或者委托他人代交。仲裁申请书应当载明申请人和被申请人的基本情况，仲裁请求和所根据的事实、理由，并提供相应的证据和证据来源。  
书面申请确有困难的，可以口头申请，由农村土地承包仲裁委员会记入笔录，经申请人核实后由其签名、盖章或者按指印。

**Article 21** A party applying for arbitration shall submit an arbitration application to the rural land contract arbitration committee of the place where the land involved in the dispute is located. The arbitration application may be sent by mail or entrusted to another person for submission. The arbitration application shall state the basic information of the applicant and the respondent, the arbitration claims and the facts and grounds on which they are based, and provide corresponding evidence and sources of evidence.  
If it is truly difficult to make a written application, an oral application may be made, and the rural land contract arbitration committee shall record it in the minutes, which shall be signed, sealed or fingerprinted by the applicant after verification.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当对仲裁申请予以审查，认为符合[本法第二十条](#art-20)规定的，应当受理。有下列情形之一的，不予受理；已受理的，终止仲裁程序：  
（一）不符合申请条件；  
（二）人民法院已受理该纠纷；  
（三）法律规定该纠纷应当由其他机构处理；  
（四）对该纠纷已有生效的判决、裁定、仲裁裁决、行政处理决定等。

**Article 22** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall examine the application for arbitration. If it finds that the application meets the conditions provided in Article 20 of this Law, it shall accept the application. If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall not accept the application; if already accepted, it shall terminate the arbitration proceedings:  
(1) the application does not meet the conditions;  
(2) the People's Court has already accepted the dispute;  
(3) the law provides that the dispute shall be handled by another institution;  
(4) there is already a legally effective judgment, ruling, arbitration award, administrative decision, etc. regarding the dispute.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会决定受理的，应当自收到仲裁申请之日起五个工作日内，将受理通知书、仲裁规则和仲裁员名册送达申请人；决定不予受理或者终止仲裁程序的，应当自收到仲裁申请或者发现终止仲裁程序情形之日起五个工作日内书面通知申请人，并说明理由。

**Article 23** If a rural land contract arbitration committee decides to accept the application, it shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the application, serve the acceptance notice, the arbitration rules, and the list of arbitrators to the applicant; if it decides not to accept the application or to terminate the arbitration proceedings, it shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the application or discovery of the grounds for termination, notify the applicant in writing, stating the reasons.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当自受理仲裁申请之日起五个工作日内，将受理通知书、仲裁申请书副本、仲裁规则和仲裁员名册送达被申请人。

**Article 24** A rural land contract arbitration committee shall, within five working days from the date of acceptance of the arbitration application, serve the acceptance notice, a copy of the arbitration application, the arbitration rules, and the list of arbitrators to the respondent.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被申请人应当自收到仲裁申请书副本之日起十日内向农村土地承包仲裁委员会提交答辩书；书面答辩确有困难的，可以口头答辩，由农村土地承包仲裁委员会记入笔录，经被申请人核实后由其签名、盖章或者按指印。农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当自收到答辩书之日起五个工作日内将答辩书副本送达申请人。被申请人未答辩的，不影响仲裁程序的进行。

**Article 25** The respondent shall, within ten days from the date of receipt of the copy of the arbitration application, submit a written statement of defense to the rural land contract arbitration committee; if it is truly difficult to submit a written defense, an oral defense may be made, and the rural land contract arbitration committee shall record it in the minutes, which shall be signed, sealed or fingerprinted by the respondent after verification. The rural land contract arbitration committee shall, within five working days from the date of receipt of the statement of defense, serve a copy of the statement of defense to the applicant. Failure of the respondent to submit a statement of defense shall not affect the conduct of the arbitration proceedings.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　一方当事人因另一方当事人的行为或者其他原因，可能使裁决不能执行或者难以执行的，可以申请财产保全。  
当事人申请财产保全的，农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当将当事人的申请提交被申请人住所地或者财产所在地的基层人民法院。  
申请有错误的，申请人应当赔偿被申请人因财产保全所遭受的损失。

**Article 26** A party may apply for property preservation if, due to the conduct of the other party or other reasons, the award may become unenforceable or difficult to enforce.  
If a party applies for property preservation, the rural land contract arbitration committee shall submit the party's application to the Primary People's Court of the place where the respondent has its domicile or where the property is located.  
If the application is erroneous, the applicant shall compensate the respondent for losses suffered due to the property preservation.

#### 第三节　仲裁庭的组成

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　仲裁庭由三名仲裁员组成，首席仲裁员由当事人共同选定，其他二名仲裁员由当事人各自选定；当事人不能选定的，由农村土地承包仲裁委员会主任指定。  
事实清楚、权利义务关系明确、争议不大的农村土地承包经营纠纷，经双方当事人同意，可以由一名仲裁员仲裁。仲裁员由当事人共同选定或者由农村土地承包仲裁委员会主任指定。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当自仲裁庭组成之日起二个工作日内将仲裁庭组成情况通知当事人。

**Article 27** An arbitral tribunal shall be composed of three arbitrators. The presiding arbitrator shall be jointly selected by the parties, and the other two arbitrators shall be selected by the parties respectively; if the parties fail to select, they shall be appointed by the chairperson of the rural land contract arbitration committee.  
For a rural land contract management dispute where the facts are clear, the rights and obligations are definite, and the dispute is minor, with the consent of both parties, the arbitration may be conducted by a single arbitrator. The arbitrator shall be jointly selected by the parties or appointed by the chairperson of the rural land contract arbitration committee.  
The rural land contract arbitration committee shall, within two working days from the date of formation of the arbitral tribunal, notify the parties of the composition of the arbitral tribunal.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　仲裁员有下列情形之一的，必须回避，当事人也有权以口头或者书面方式申请其回避：  
（一）是本案当事人或者当事人、代理人的近亲属；  
（二）与本案有利害关系；  
（三）与本案当事人、代理人有其他关系，可能影响公正仲裁；  
（四）私自会见当事人、代理人，或者接受当事人、代理人的请客送礼。  
当事人提出回避申请，应当说明理由，在首次开庭前提出。回避事由在首次开庭后知道的，可以在最后一次开庭终结前提出。

**Article 28** An arbitrator must withdraw under any of the following circumstances, and the parties also have the right to apply for his withdrawal orally or in writing:  
(1) he is a party to the case or a close relative of a party or his agent;  
(2) he has an interest in the case;  
(3) he has other relationships with a party or his agent that may affect impartial arbitration;  
(4) he privately meets with a party or his agent, or accepts treats or gifts from a party or his agent.  
A party applying for withdrawal shall state the reasons and submit the application before the first session. If the grounds for withdrawal become known after the first session, the application may be submitted before the conclusion of the last session.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农村土地承包仲裁委员会对回避申请应当及时作出决定，以口头或者书面方式通知当事人，并说明理由。  
仲裁员是否回避，由农村土地承包仲裁委员会主任决定；农村土地承包仲裁委员会主任担任仲裁员时，由农村土地承包仲裁委员会集体决定。  
仲裁员因回避或者其他原因不能履行职责的，应当依照本法规定重新选定或者指定仲裁员。

**Article 29** The rural land contract arbitration committee shall promptly make a decision on an application for withdrawal, notify the parties orally or in writing, and state the reasons.  
Whether an arbitrator shall withdraw shall be decided by the chairperson of the rural land contract arbitration committee; if the chairperson serves as an arbitrator, the decision shall be made collectively by the rural land contract arbitration committee.  
If an arbitrator is unable to perform his duties due to withdrawal or other reasons, a new arbitrator shall be selected or appointed in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### 第四节　开庭和裁决

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁应当开庭进行。  
开庭可以在纠纷涉及的土地所在地的乡（镇）或者村进行，也可以在农村土地承包仲裁委员会所在地进行。当事人双方要求在乡（镇）或者村开庭的，应当在该乡（镇）或者村开庭。  
开庭应当公开，但涉及国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私以及当事人约定不公开的除外。

**Article 30** Arbitration of rural land contract management disputes shall be conducted in open session.  
The session may be held in the township (town) or village where the land involved in the dispute is located, or at the seat of the rural land contract arbitration committee. If both parties request to hold the session in a township (town) or village, the session shall be held in that township (town) or village.  
The session shall be held publicly, except where state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy are involved, or where the parties agree not to hold it publicly.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　仲裁庭应当在开庭五个工作日前将开庭的时间、地点通知当事人和其他仲裁参与人。  
当事人有正当理由的，可以向仲裁庭请求变更开庭的时间、地点。是否变更，由仲裁庭决定。

**Article 31** The arbitral tribunal shall notify the parties and other arbitration participants of the time and place of the session five working days before the session.  
If a party has a valid reason, it may request the arbitral tribunal to change the time or place of the session. Whether to change shall be decided by the arbitral tribunal.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人申请仲裁后，可以自行和解。达成和解协议的，可以请求仲裁庭根据和解协议作出裁决书，也可以撤回仲裁申请。

**Article 32** After applying for arbitration, the parties may reach a settlement on their own. If a settlement agreement is reached, they may request the arbitral tribunal to render an award based on the settlement agreement, or they may withdraw the arbitration application.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　申请人可以放弃或者变更仲裁请求。被申请人可以承认或者反驳仲裁请求，有权提出反请求。

**Article 33** The applicant may waive or modify the arbitration claims. The respondent may admit or rebut the arbitration claims, and has the right to make counterclaims.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　仲裁庭作出裁决前，申请人撤回仲裁申请的，除被申请人提出反请求的外，仲裁庭应当终止仲裁。

**Article 34** If the applicant withdraws the arbitration application before the arbitral tribunal renders an award, the arbitral tribunal shall terminate the arbitration, except where the respondent has made a counterclaim.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　申请人经书面通知，无正当理由不到庭或者未经仲裁庭许可中途退庭的，可以视为撤回仲裁申请。  
被申请人经书面通知，无正当理由不到庭或者未经仲裁庭许可中途退庭的，可以缺席裁决。

**Article 35** If the applicant, after being notified in writing, fails to appear before the arbitral tribunal without just cause or leaves the session midway without the permission of the arbitral tribunal, the application may be deemed to be withdrawn.  
If the respondent, after being notified in writing, fails to appear before the arbitral tribunal without just cause or leaves the session midway without the permission of the arbitral tribunal, a default award may be made.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　当事人在开庭过程中有权发表意见、陈述事实和理由、提供证据、进行质证和辩论。对不通晓当地通用语言文字的当事人，农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当为其提供翻译。

**Article 36** During the session, the parties shall have the right to present their opinions, state facts and grounds, provide evidence, conduct cross-examination, and debate. For a party who does not have a command of the commonly used local language, the rural land contract arbitration committee shall provide interpretation for him.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人应当对自己的主张提供证据。与纠纷有关的证据由作为当事人一方的发包方等掌握管理的，该当事人应当在仲裁庭指定的期限内提供，逾期不提供的，应当承担不利后果。

**Article 37** Each party shall provide evidence for its own claims. If evidence related to the dispute is in the possession or under the management of the party that grants the contract, that party shall provide it within the time limit specified by the arbitral tribunal; if it fails to provide it within the time limit, it shall bear adverse consequences.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　仲裁庭认为有必要收集的证据，可以自行收集。

**Article 38** The arbitral tribunal may collect evidence on its own if it deems it necessary.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　仲裁庭对专门性问题认为需要鉴定的，可以交由当事人约定的鉴定机构鉴定；当事人没有约定的，由仲裁庭指定的鉴定机构鉴定。  
根据当事人的请求或者仲裁庭的要求，鉴定机构应当派鉴定人参加开庭。当事人经仲裁庭许可，可以向鉴定人提问。

**Article 39** If the arbitral tribunal considers that a specialized issue requires expert evaluation, it may submit the matter to an evaluation institution agreed upon by the parties; if the parties have not agreed, it shall be submitted to an evaluation institution designated by the arbitral tribunal.  
At the request of a party or upon the requirement of the arbitral tribunal, the evaluation institution shall send an evaluator to attend the session. With the permission of the arbitral tribunal, the parties may ask questions to the evaluator.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　证据应当在开庭时出示，但涉及国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私的证据不得在公开开庭时出示。  
仲裁庭应当依照仲裁规则的规定开庭，给予双方当事人平等陈述、辩论的机会，并组织当事人进行质证。  
经仲裁庭查证属实的证据，应当作为认定事实的根据。

**Article 40** Evidence shall be presented during the session, except that evidence involving state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy shall not be presented during a public session.  
The arbitral tribunal shall conduct the session in accordance with the arbitration rules, provide both parties with equal opportunities to make statements and debate, and organize the parties for cross-examination.  
Evidence verified by the arbitral tribunal shall serve as the basis for finding facts.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，当事人可以申请证据保全。当事人申请证据保全的，农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当将当事人的申请提交证据所在地的基层人民法院。

**Article 41** Where evidence may be destroyed or become difficult to obtain later, a party may apply for evidence preservation. If a party applies for evidence preservation, the rural land contract arbitration committee shall submit the party's application to the Primary People's Court of the place where the evidence is located.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对权利义务关系明确的纠纷，经当事人申请，仲裁庭可以先行裁定维持现状、恢复农业生产以及停止取土、占地等行为。  
一方当事人不履行先行裁定的，另一方当事人可以向人民法院申请执行，但应当提供相应的担保。

**Article 42** For a dispute where the rights and obligations are clear, upon application of a party, the arbitral tribunal may make an interim ruling to maintain the status quo, restore agricultural production, and stop activities such as soil extraction and land occupation.  
If one party fails to comply with the interim ruling, the other party may apply to the People's Court for enforcement, but shall provide corresponding security.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　仲裁庭应当将开庭情况记入笔录，由仲裁员、记录人员、当事人和其他仲裁参与人签名、盖章或者按指印。  
当事人和其他仲裁参与人认为对自己陈述的记录有遗漏或者差错的，有权申请补正。如果不予补正，应当记录该申请。

**Article 43** The arbitral tribunal shall record the proceedings of the session in the minutes, which shall be signed, sealed or fingerprinted by the arbitrators, the recorder, the parties, and other arbitration participants.  
If a party or other arbitration participant believes that the record of its statements contains omissions or errors, it has the right to apply for corrections. If the corrections are not made, such application shall be recorded.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　仲裁庭应当根据认定的事实和法律以及国家政策作出裁决并制作裁决书。  
裁决应当按照多数仲裁员的意见作出，少数仲裁员的不同意见可以记入笔录。仲裁庭不能形成多数意见时，裁决应当按照首席仲裁员的意见作出。

**Article 44** The arbitral tribunal shall render an award and produce a written award based on the facts found and the law and national policies.  
The award shall be made in accordance with the opinions of the majority of the arbitrators; the differing opinions of a minority of the arbitrators may be recorded in the minutes. If the arbitral tribunal cannot form a majority opinion, the award shall be made in accordance with the opinion of the presiding arbitrator.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　裁决书应当写明仲裁请求、争议事实、裁决理由、裁决结果、裁决日期以及当事人不服仲裁裁决的起诉权利、期限，由仲裁员签名，加盖农村土地承包仲裁委员会印章。  
农村土地承包仲裁委员会应当在裁决作出之日起三个工作日内将裁决书送达当事人，并告知当事人不服仲裁裁决的起诉权利、期限。

**Article 45** The written award shall state the arbitration claims, the facts of the dispute, the reasons for the award, the result of the award, the date of the award, and the right and time limit for the parties to prosecute if they are dissatisfied with the arbitration award. It shall be signed by the arbitrators and sealed with the seal of the rural land contract arbitration committee.  
The rural land contract arbitration committee shall, within three working days from the date of making the award, serve the written award to the parties and inform them of the right and time limit for prosecution if they are dissatisfied with the arbitration award.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　仲裁庭依法独立履行职责，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 46** The arbitral tribunal shall independently perform its duties according to law and shall not be interfered with by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　仲裁农村土地承包经营纠纷，应当自受理仲裁申请之日起六十日内结束；案情复杂需要延长的，经农村土地承包仲裁委员会主任批准可以延长，并书面通知当事人，但延长期限不得超过三十日。

**Article 47** Arbitration of a rural land contract management dispute shall be concluded within sixty days from the date of acceptance of the arbitration application; if the case is complex and needs extension, it may be extended with the approval of the chairperson of the rural land contract arbitration committee, and the parties shall be notified in writing, but the extension shall not exceed thirty days.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　当事人不服仲裁裁决的，可以自收到裁决书之日起三十日内向人民法院起诉。逾期不起诉的，裁决书即发生法律效力。

**Article 48** If a party is dissatisfied with the arbitration award, it may prosecute in the People's Court within thirty days from the date of receipt of the written award. If no prosecution is filed within that time limit, the written award shall take effect.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　当事人对发生法律效力的调解书、裁决书，应当依照规定的期限履行。一方当事人逾期不履行的，另一方当事人可以向被申请人住所地或者财产所在地的基层人民法院申请执行。受理申请的人民法院应当依法执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 49** The parties shall perform the legally effective mediation document or written award within the prescribed time limit. If one party fails to perform within the time limit, the other party may apply for enforcement to the Primary People's Court of the place where the respondent has its domicile or where the property is located. The People's Court that accepts the application shall enforce according to law.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法所称农村土地，是指农民集体所有和国家所有依法由农民集体使用的耕地、林地、草地，以及其他依法用于农业的土地。

**Article 50** For the purposes of this Law, 'rural land' means cultivated land, forest land, grassland owned by peasant collectives or owned by the state and lawfully used by peasant collectives, and other land lawfully used for agriculture.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁规则和农村土地承包仲裁委员会示范章程，由国务院农业、林业行政主管部门依照本法规定共同制定。

**Article 51** The arbitration rules for rural land contract management disputes and the model charter for rural land contract arbitration committees shall be jointly formulated by the administrative departments of agriculture and forestry under the State Council in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁不得向当事人收取费用，仲裁工作经费纳入财政预算予以保障。

**Article 52** No fees shall be charged to the parties for arbitration of rural land contract management disputes, and the expenses for arbitration work shall be included in the fiscal budget and guaranteed.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本法自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2010.
