### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全基层群众自治制度，由村民依法办理自己的事情，发展基层民主，维护村民的合法权益，促进乡村全面振兴，推进基层治理体系和治理能力现代化，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of improving the system of primary-level self-governance by the masses, enabling villagers to handle their own affairs according to law, developing democracy at the primary level, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of villagers, promoting the comprehensive revitalization of rural areas, and modernizing the governance system and governance capacity at the primary level.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　村民委员会是村民自我管理、自我服务、自我教育、自我监督的基层群众性自治组织，实行民主选举、民主协商、民主决策、民主管理、民主监督。  
村民委员会办理本村的公共事务和公益事业，调解民间纠纷，协助维护社会治安，向人民政府反映村民的意见、要求和提出建议。  
村民委员会向村民会议、村民代表会议负责并报告工作。

**Article 2** A villagers committee is a primary-level mass self-governance organization in which villagers practice self-management, self-service, self-education, and self-supervision, and shall practice democratic elections, democratic consultations, democratic decision-making, democratic management, and democratic supervision.  
The villagers committee shall handle the public affairs and public welfare undertakings of the village, mediate civil disputes, assist in maintaining public security, reflect villagers' opinions and demands to the people's government, and make suggestions.  
The villagers committee shall be responsible for and report on its work to the villagers assembly and the villagers representative assembly.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　村民委员会根据村民居住状况、人口多少，按照便于村民自治，有利于经济发展和基层治理的原则设立。  
村民委员会的设立、撤销、范围调整，由乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府提出，经村民会议讨论同意，报县级人民政府批准。  
村民委员会可以根据村民居住状况、集体土地所有权关系等分设若干村民小组。

**Article 3** A villagers committee shall be established based on the living conditions of the villagers, the size of the population, and in accordance with the principles of facilitating villagers' self-governance and being conducive to economic development and primary-level governance.  
The establishment, dissolution, or scope adjustment of a villagers committee shall be proposed by the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, discussed and approved by the villagers assembly, and reported to the people's government at the county level for approval.  
A villagers committee may, based on factors such as the living conditions of the villagers and the ownership of collective land, establish several villagers groups.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　村民委员会工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持和发展全过程人民民主，坚持自治、法治、德治相结合。  
中国共产党在农村的基层组织，按照中国共产党章程进行工作，领导和支持村民委员会行使职权；依照宪法和法律，支持和保障村民开展自治活动、直接行使民主权利。

**Article 4** The work of the villagers committee shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, uphold and develop whole-process people's democracy, and integrate self-governance, rule of law, and rule of virtue.  
The grassroots organizations of the Communist Party of China in rural areas shall carry out their work in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China, lead and support the villagers committee in exercising its functions and powers; and, in accordance with the Constitution and laws, support and guarantee that villagers carry out self-governance activities and directly exercise democratic rights.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央和地方基层群众自治指导监督部门负责指导和监督农村基层群众自治工作。

**Article 5** The central and local departments for guidance and supervision of primary-level mass self-governance shall be responsible for guiding and supervising the work of rural primary-level mass self-governance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府对村民委员会的工作给予指导、支持和帮助，但是不得干预依法属于村民自治范围内的事项。  
村民委员会协助乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府开展工作。

**Article 6** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall provide guidance, support, and assistance to the work of the villagers committee, but shall not interfere with matters that fall within the scope of villagers' self-governance according to law.  
The villagers committee shall assist the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town in carrying out its work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在村民自治工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the work of villagers' self-governance shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　村民委员会的组成和职责

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　村民委员会由主任、副主任和委员共三至七人组成。  
村民委员会成员中，应当有妇女成员。村民委员会成员实行近亲属回避。  
村民委员会主任可以由村党组织负责人通过法定程序担任。村民委员会成员和村党组织领导班子成员可以交叉任职。

**Article 8** A villagers committee shall be composed of three to seven members, including a director, deputy directors, and members.  
Among the members of the villagers committee, there shall be female members. Members of the villagers committee shall practice avoidance of close relatives.  
The director of the villagers committee may be concurrently held by the head of the village Party organization through statutory procedures. Members of the villagers committee and members of the leading body of the village Party organization may hold concurrent posts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　村民委员会根据需要设人民调解、治安保卫、公共卫生、人居环境、老年人和妇女儿童工作等委员会。村民委员会成员可以兼任下属委员会的成员。人口少的村的村民委员会可以不设下属委员会，由村民委员会成员分工负责有关工作。

**Article 9** A villagers committee may, as needed, set up committees for people's mediation, public security, public health, human settlement environment, work concerning the elderly, women, and children, among others. Members of the villagers committee may concurrently serve as members of the subordinate committees. In a village with a small population, the villagers committee may not set up subordinate committees, and its members shall be responsible for the relevant work through division of duties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　村民委员会应当支持和组织村民依法发展各种形式的合作经济和其他经济，承担本村生产的服务和协调工作，促进农村生产建设和经济发展。  
村民委员会依照法律规定，引导村民合理利用自然资源，保护和改善生态环境，推进美丽乡村建设。  
村民委员会应当尊重并支持农村集体经济组织依法独立进行经济活动的自主权，维护以家庭承包经营为基础、统分结合的双层经营体制，保障农村集体经济组织和村民、承包经营户、联户或者合伙的财产权和其他合法权益。未设立农村集体经济组织的，村民委员会、村民小组可以依法代行农村集体经济组织的职能。

**Article 10** The villagers committee shall support and organize villagers to develop various forms of cooperative economy and other economies according to law, undertake the service and coordination work for the production of the village, and promote rural production construction and economic development.  
In accordance with the provisions of laws, the villagers committee shall guide villagers to rationally utilize natural resources, protect and improve the ecological environment, and promote the construction of beautiful villages.  
The villagers committee shall respect and support the autonomy of rural collective economic organizations to independently carry out economic activities according to law, uphold the two-tier management system based on household contract management with the combination of unified and separate management, and protect the property rights and other legitimate rights and interests of rural collective economic organizations and villagers, contracted households, joint households or partnerships. Where no rural collective economic organization is established, the villagers committee or the villagers group may, according to law, exercise the functions of a rural collective economic organization on its behalf.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　村民委员会应当宣传宪法、法律、法规，宣传党和国家的政策，支持和推动村民履行法律规定的义务，维护村民的合法权益，支持和引导村民践行社会主义核心价值观，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，发展文化教育，普及科技知识，促进男女平等和家庭和睦，关心关爱老年人、留守儿童、残疾人和困难村民，增强团结互助，推进移风易俗，培育文明乡风。  
村民委员会应当协助组织村民参与群防群治，协助处理信访事项和协调化解矛盾纠纷，协助做好社区矫正工作和刑满释放人员帮教工作，协助做好突发事件应对工作。  
村民委员会应当支持和引导社会组织、社会工作者、志愿者等社会力量参与乡村治理和服务。  
多民族村民居住的村，村民委员会应当支持和引导各民族村民增进团结、互相尊重、互相帮助。

**Article 11** The villagers committee shall publicize the Constitution, laws, and regulations, publicize the policies of the Party and the State, support and facilitate villagers to fulfill the obligations prescribed by laws, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of villagers, support and guide villagers to practice the core socialist values, forge a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, develop culture and education, popularize scientific and technological knowledge, promote gender equality and family harmony, care for the elderly, left-behind children, disabled persons, and villagers in difficulty, enhance solidarity and mutual assistance, promote the transformation of outdated customs, and cultivate civilized rural customs.  
The villagers committee shall assist in organizing villagers to participate in mass prevention and mass governance, assist in handling petition matters and mediating and resolving disputes, assist in doing a good job in community correction and helping persons released after serving their sentences, and assist in doing a good job in responding to emergencies.  
The villagers committee shall support and guide social organizations, social workers, volunteers, and other social forces to participate in rural governance and services.  
In a village inhabited by multiple ethnic groups, the villagers committee shall support and guide villagers of all ethnic groups to enhance unity, respect each other, and help each other.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　村民委员会及其成员应当遵守宪法、法律、法规，遵守党和国家的政策，遵守并组织实施村民自治章程、村规民约，执行村民会议、村民代表会议的决定、决议，办事公道，廉洁奉公，热心为村民服务，接受村民监督。

**Article 12** The villagers committee and its members shall abide by the Constitution, laws, and regulations, abide by the policies of the Party and the State, abide by and organize the implementation of the villagers' self-governance charter and village rules and conventions, implement the decisions and resolutions of the villagers assembly and the villagers representative assembly, act fairly, be honest in performing their duties, be enthusiastic in serving the villagers, and accept the supervision of the villagers.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　村民委员会具有基层群众性自治组织法人资格，村民委员会主任为法定代表人。村民委员会可以从事为履行职能所需要的民事活动，但是不得提供担保。

**Article 13** A villagers committee shall have the legal person status of a primary-level mass self-governance organization, and the director of the villagers committee shall be the legal representative. The villagers committee may engage in civil activities necessary for the performance of its functions, but shall not provide guarantees.

### 第三章　村民委员会的选举

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　村民委员会主任、副主任和委员，由村民直接选举产生。任何组织或者个人不得指定、委派或者撤换村民委员会成员。  
村民委员会每届任期五年，届满应当及时举行换届选举。村民委员会成员可以连选连任。

**Article 14** The director, deputy directors, and members of the villagers committee shall be directly elected by the villagers. No organization or individual shall designate, appoint, or remove members of the villagers committee.  
The villagers committee shall have a term of five years, and a re-election shall be held in a timely manner upon the expiration of the term. Members of the villagers committee may be re-elected.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　村民委员会的选举，由村民选举委员会主持。  
村民选举委员会由主任、副主任和委员组成，由村民会议、村民代表会议或者各村民小组会议推选产生。  
村民选举委员会成员或者其近亲属被提名为村民委员会成员候选人的，应当退出村民选举委员会。  
村民选举委员会成员退出村民选举委员会或者因其他原因出缺的，按照原推选结果依次递补，也可以另行推选。

**Article 15** The election of a villagers committee shall be presided over by a villagers electoral committee.  
The villagers electoral committee shall be composed of a director, deputy directors, and members, and shall be elected by the villagers assembly, the villagers representative assembly, or the meetings of each villagers group.  
Where a member of the villagers electoral committee or his or her close relative is nominated as a candidate for membership of the villagers committee, the member shall withdraw from the villagers electoral committee.  
Where a member of the villagers electoral committee withdraws from the villagers electoral committee or becomes vacant due to other reasons, a replacement shall be made in order according to the original election results, or a new election may be held.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　年满十八周岁的村民，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、家庭出身、宗教信仰、教育程度、财产状况、居住期限，都有选举权和被选举权；但是，依照法律被剥夺政治权利的人除外。  
村民委员会选举前，应当对下列人员进行登记，列入参加选举的村民名单：  
（一）户籍在本村并且在本村居住的村民；  
（二）户籍在本村，不在本村居住，本人表示参加选举的村民；  
（三）户籍不在本村，在本村居住或者从事村务工作一年以上，本人申请参加选举，并且经村民会议或者村民代表会议同意参加选举的公民。  
已在户籍所在村或者居住村登记参加选举的村民，不得再参加其他地方村民委员会的选举。

**Article 16** Villagers who have reached the age of 18 shall have the right to vote and stand for election, regardless of ethnicity, race, sex, occupation, family background, religious belief, educational level, property status, or length of residence; however, persons who have been deprived of political rights according to law shall be excluded.  
Before the election of a villagers committee, the following persons shall be registered and included in the list of villagers participating in the election:  
(1) Villagers whose household registration is in the village and who reside in the village;  
(2) Villagers whose household registration is in the village but who do not reside in the village, and who have indicated their intention to participate in the election;  
(3) Citizens whose household registration is not in the village but who have resided in the village or engaged in village affairs for more than one year, who have applied to participate in the election, and whose participation has been approved by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly.  
Villagers who have been registered to participate in the election in the village of their household registration or the village of residence shall not participate in the election of the villagers committee of any other place.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　登记参加选举的村民名单应当在选举日的二十日前由村民选举委员会公布。  
对登记参加选举的村民名单有异议的，应当自名单公布之日起五日内向村民选举委员会申诉，村民选举委员会应当自收到申诉之日起三日内作出处理决定，并公布处理结果。

**Article 17** The list of villagers registered to participate in the election shall be published by the villagers electoral committee twenty days before the election day.  
Any person who has an objection to the list of villagers registered to participate in the election shall file a petition with the villagers electoral committee within five days from the date of publication of the list, and the villagers electoral committee shall make a decision on the handling within three days from the date of receipt of the petition and publish the handling result.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　村民委员会成员候选人由村党组织或者登记参加选举的村民提名推荐。提名推荐候选人，应当从全体村民利益出发，推荐拥护中国共产党的领导、奉公守法、品行良好、公道正派、热心公益、具有一定文化水平和工作能力的村民为候选人。被开除中国共产党党籍，因犯罪受过刑事处罚，利用宗族或者黑恶势力从事非法活动，组织或者参加非法宗教活动或者邪教活动的，不得作为候选人。候选人的名额应当多于应选名额。村民选举委员会应当组织候选人与村民见面，由候选人介绍履行职责的设想，回答村民提出的问题。  
选举村民委员会，有登记参加选举的村民过半数投票，选举有效；候选人获得参加投票的村民过半数的选票，始得当选。当选人数不足应选名额的，不足的名额另行选举。另行选举的，第一次投票未当选的人员得票多的为候选人，候选人以得票多的当选，但是所得票数不得少于已投选票总数的三分之一。  
选举实行无记名投票、公开计票的方法，选举结果应当当场公布。选举时，应当设立秘密写票处。  
登记参加选举的村民，选举期间外出不能参加投票的，可以书面委托本村有选举权的近亲属或者其他村民代为投票。每一村民接受委托不得超过三人。候选人不得接受委托投票。村民选举委员会应当公布委托人和受委托人的名单。  
具体选举办法由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会规定。

**Article 18** Candidates for members of the villagers committee shall be nominated and recommended by the village Party organization or by villagers registered to participate in the election. When nominating and recommending candidates, the interests of all villagers shall be taken as the starting point, and villagers who support the leadership of the Communist Party of China, abide by the law, have good conduct, are fair and upright, are enthusiastic about public welfare, and have a certain level of education and work ability shall be recommended as candidates. Persons who have been expelled from the Communist Party of China, have been subject to criminal punishment for crimes, have used clan or black and evil forces to engage in illegal activities, or have organized or participated in illegal religious activities or cult activities shall not serve as candidates. The number of candidates shall be greater than the number of persons to be elected. The villagers electoral committee shall organize meetings between candidates and villagers, at which candidates shall introduce their visions for fulfilling their duties and answer questions raised by villagers.  
In the election of a villagers committee, the election shall be valid if more than half of the villagers registered to participate in the election cast their votes; a candidate shall be elected only if he or she receives more than half of the votes cast by the villagers who participate in the voting. If the number of persons elected is less than the number to be elected, a supplementary election shall be held for the shortfall. In the supplementary election, the persons who have received the most votes among those who were not elected in the first round of voting shall be the candidates, and the candidates shall be elected on the basis of receiving the most votes, provided that the number of votes received shall not be less than one-third of the total number of votes cast.  
The election shall be conducted by secret ballot and open counting of votes, and the election results shall be announced on the spot. During the election, a secret ballot booth shall be set up.  
Villagers registered to participate in the election who are unable to vote due to being away during the election period may entrust in writing a close relative who has the right to vote in the village or another villager to vote on their behalf. No villager may accept entrustment from more than three persons. Candidates shall not accept entrustment to vote. The villagers electoral committee shall publish the list of the principals and the entrusted persons.  
The specific election procedures shall be prescribed by the standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本村五分之一以上有选举权的村民或者三分之一以上的村民代表联名，可以提出罢免村民委员会成员的要求，并说明要求罢免的理由。被提出罢免的村民委员会成员有权提出申辩意见。  
罢免村民委员会成员，须有登记参加选举的村民过半数投票，并须经投票的村民过半数通过。

**Article 19** A request to recall a member of the villagers committee may be made jointly by more than one-fifth of the villagers with the right to vote in the village or more than one-third of the villagers' representatives, stating the reasons for the recall. The member of the villagers committee who is subject to the recall shall have the right to present a defense.  
To recall a member of the villagers committee, more than half of the villagers registered to participate in the election shall cast their votes, and the recall shall be approved by more than half of the villagers who have voted.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　村民委员会成员可以向村民委员会提出辞职申请，其职务自村民会议或者村民代表会议审议通过之日起终止。

**Article 20** A member of the villagers committee may submit a resignation application to the villagers committee, and his or her position shall terminate from the date on which the resignation is considered and approved by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　以暴力、威胁、欺骗、贿赂、伪造选票、虚报选举票数等不正当手段当选村民委员会成员的，当选无效。  
对以暴力、威胁、欺骗、贿赂、伪造选票、虚报选举票数等不正当手段，妨害村民行使选举权、被选举权，破坏村民委员会选举的行为，村民有权向乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会和人民政府或者县级人民代表大会常务委员会和人民政府及其有关主管部门举报，由乡级或者县级人民政府负责调查并依法处理。

**Article 21** Any person who is elected as a member of the villagers committee by improper means such as violence, threats, deception, bribery, forging ballots, or falsely reporting the number of votes cast shall have his or her election declared invalid.  
With respect to acts that interfere with villagers' exercise of the right to vote and stand for election and undermine the election of the villagers committee by improper means such as violence, threats, deception, bribery, forging ballots, or falsely reporting the number of votes cast, villagers shall have the right to report to the people's congress and the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, or to the standing committee of the people's congress at the county level and the people's government at the county level and their relevant competent departments. The people's government at the township level or the county level shall be responsible for investigating and handling the matter according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　村民委员会成员丧失行为能力或者被判处刑罚的，其职务自行终止。

**Article 22** Where a member of the villagers committee loses capacity for civil conduct or is sentenced to criminal punishment, his or her position shall be automatically terminated.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　村民委员会成员出缺，可以由村民会议或者村民代表会议进行补选。补选程序参照[本法第十八条](#art-18)的规定办理。补选的村民委员会成员的任期到本届村民委员会任期届满时止。

**Article 23** Where a vacancy arises among the members of the villagers committee, a by-election may be conducted by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly. The procedures for the by-election shall be carried out by reference to the provisions of Article 18 of this Law. The term of office of a member elected in the by-election shall end upon the expiration of the term of office of the current villagers committee.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　村民委员会应当自新一届村民委员会产生之日起十日内完成工作移交。工作移交由村民选举委员会主持，由乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府监督。

**Article 24** The villagers committee shall complete the handover of work within ten days from the date on which the new term of the villagers committee is formed. The handover of work shall be presided over by the villagers electoral committee and supervised by the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town.

### 第四章　村民会议和村民代表会议

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　村民会议由本村十八周岁以上的村民组成。  
村民会议由村民委员会召集。有十分之一以上的村民或者三分之一以上的村民代表提议，应当召集村民会议。召集村民会议，应当提前十日通知村民；遇有特殊情况的，可以临时通知村民。  
村民会议每年至少召开一次。

**Article 25** The villagers assembly shall be composed of villagers who have reached the age of 18 in the village.  
The villagers assembly shall be convened by the villagers committee. Where more than one-tenth of the villagers or more than one-third of the villagers' representatives propose, the villagers assembly shall be convened. When convening the villagers assembly, the villagers shall be notified ten days in advance; in special circumstances, the villagers may be notified on an ad hoc basis.  
The villagers assembly shall be held at least once a year.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　召开村民会议，应当有本村十八周岁以上村民的过半数，或者本村三分之二以上的户的代表参加，村民会议所作决定应当经到会人员的过半数通过。法律对召开村民会议及作出决定另有规定的，依照其规定。  
召开村民会议，根据需要可以邀请驻本村的企业、事业单位和群众组织派代表列席。

**Article 26** To convene a villagers assembly, more than half of the villagers in the village who have reached the age of 18, or representatives of more than two-thirds of the households in the village, shall be present; decisions made by the villagers assembly shall be approved by more than half of the persons present. Where laws provide otherwise for convening the villagers assembly and making decisions, those provisions shall prevail.  
When convening the villagers assembly, representatives of enterprises, public institutions, and mass organizations located in the village may be invited to attend as non-voting participants as needed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　村民会议审议村民委员会的年度工作报告，评议村民委员会成员的工作；有权撤销或者变更村民委员会不适当的决定；有权撤销或者变更村民代表会议不适当的决定。  
村民会议可以授权村民代表会议审议村民委员会的年度工作报告，评议村民委员会成员的工作，撤销或者变更村民委员会不适当的决定。

**Article 27** The villagers assembly shall review the annual work report of the villagers committee and appraise the work of the members of the villagers committee; it shall have the power to annul or modify inappropriate decisions of the villagers committee; it shall have the power to annul or modify inappropriate decisions of the villagers representative assembly.  
The villagers assembly may authorize the villagers representative assembly to review the annual work report of the villagers committee, appraise the work of the members of the villagers committee, and annul or modify inappropriate decisions of the villagers committee.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　涉及村民利益的下列事项，经村民会议讨论决定方可办理：  
（一）涉及村庄规划、建设等乡村建设重要事项；  
（二）本村享受误工补贴的人员及补贴标准；  
（三）从村集体经济所得资金和物资的使用；  
（四）本村公益事业的兴办和筹资筹劳方案及建设承包方案；  
（五）本村重点帮扶人员、帮扶方案；  
（六）法律、法规规定或者村民会议认为应当由村民会议讨论决定的涉及村民利益的其他事项。  
村民会议可以授权村民代表会议讨论决定前款规定的事项。  
需由村民会议或者村民代表会议决定的重要事项，应当先经村党组织研究讨论。  
村民委员会依法代行农村集体经济组织职能的，讨论决定有关集体财产和成员权益的事项，参照适用《中华人民共和国农村集体经济组织法》的相关规定。

**Article 28** The following matters involving the interests of villagers may be handled only after being discussed and decided by the villagers assembly:  
(1) Important matters concerning rural construction such as village planning and construction;  
(2) Persons entitled to work-failure subsidies in the village and the standards for such subsidies;  
(3) The use of funds and materials derived from the village collective economy;  
(4) The initiation of public welfare undertakings in the village and the plans for raising funds and labor and the plans for construction contracting;  
(5) The key assistance recipients and assistance plans in the village;  
(6) Other matters involving the interests of villagers that are prescribed by laws and regulations or that the villagers assembly considers should be discussed and decided by the villagers assembly.  
The villagers assembly may authorize the villagers representative assembly to discuss and decide the matters specified in the preceding paragraph.  
Important matters that need to be decided by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly shall be first studied and discussed by the village Party organization.  
Where the villagers committee exercises the functions of a rural collective economic organization according to law, the provisions of the Rural Collective Economic Organization Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply mutatis mutandis to the discussion and decision of matters concerning collective property and members' rights and interests.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人数较多或者居住分散的村，可以设立村民代表会议，讨论决定村民会议授权的事项。村民代表会议由村民委员会成员和村民代表组成，村民代表应当占村民代表会议组成人员的五分之四以上，妇女村民代表应当占村民代表会议组成人员的三分之一以上。  
村民代表由村民按每五户至十五户推选一人，或者由各村民小组推选若干人。村民代表的任期与村民委员会的任期相同。村民代表可以连选连任。  
村民代表应当向其推选户或者村民小组负责，接受村民监督。

**Article 29** In a village with a relatively large population or scattered residences, a villagers representative assembly may be established to discuss and decide matters authorized by the villagers assembly. The villagers representative assembly shall be composed of members of the villagers committee and villagers' representatives. Villagers' representatives shall account for no less than four-fifths of the members of the villagers representative assembly, and female villagers' representatives shall account for no less than one-third of the members of the villagers representative assembly.  
Villagers' representatives shall be elected by the villagers on the basis of one representative for every five to fifteen households, or by each villagers group to elect a certain number of representatives. The term of office of villagers' representatives shall be the same as that of the villagers committee. Villagers' representatives may be re-elected.  
Villagers' representatives shall be responsible to the households or villagers group that elected them and shall accept the supervision of the villagers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　村民代表会议由村民委员会召集。村民代表会议每季度召开一次。有五分之一以上的村民代表提议，应当召集村民代表会议。  
村民代表会议有三分之二以上的组成人员参加方可召开，所作决定应当经到会人员的过半数同意。

**Article 30** The villagers representative assembly shall be convened by the villagers committee. The villagers representative assembly shall be held once every quarter. Where more than one-fifth of the villagers' representatives propose, the villagers representative assembly shall be convened.  
The villagers representative assembly may be held only if more than two-thirds of its members are present, and decisions made shall be approved by more than half of the persons present.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　村民会议可以制定和修改村民自治章程、村规民约，并报乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府备案。  
村民自治章程、村规民约以及村民会议或者村民代表会议的决定不得与宪法、法律、法规相抵触，不得违背公序良俗，不得有侵犯村民的人身权利、民主权利和财产权利的内容。  
村民自治章程、村规民约以及村民会议或者村民代表会议的决定违反前款规定的，由乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府责令改正。

**Article 31** The villagers assembly may formulate and amend the villagers' self-governance charter and village rules and conventions, and shall report them to the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town for recordation.  
The villagers' self-governance charter, village rules and conventions, and decisions of the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly shall not contravene the Constitution, laws, or regulations, shall not violate public order and good customs, and shall not contain content that infringes upon the personal rights, democratic rights, and property rights of villagers.  
Where the villagers' self-governance charter, village rules and conventions, or decisions of the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly violate the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall order correction.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　村民小组在村民委员会的组织下开展活动。村民小组会议讨论决定本村民小组的重要事项。  
召开村民小组会议，应当有本村民小组十八周岁以上的村民三分之二以上，或者本村民小组三分之二以上的户的代表参加，所作决定应当经到会人员的过半数同意。  
村民小组组长由村民小组会议推选。村民小组组长任期与村民委员会的任期相同，可以连选连任。  
村民小组代行农村集体经济组织职能的，由村民小组会议依照有关法律的规定讨论决定属于村民小组的集体所有的土地、企业和其他财产的经营管理以及公益事项的办理，所作决定及实施情况应当及时向本村民小组的村民公布。

**Article 32** A villagers group shall carry out activities under the organization of the villagers committee. A villagers group meeting shall discuss and decide important matters of the villagers group.  
To convene a villagers group meeting, more than two-thirds of the villagers in the group who have reached the age of 18, or representatives of more than two-thirds of the households in the group, shall be present, and decisions made shall be approved by more than half of the persons present.  
The head of a villagers group shall be elected by the villagers group meeting. The term of office of the head of a villagers group shall be the same as that of the villagers committee, and he or she may be re-elected.  
Where a villagers group exercises the functions of a rural collective economic organization, the villagers group meeting shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, discuss and decide on the management of land, enterprises, and other property collectively owned by the villagers group, as well as the handling of public welfare matters. The decisions made and the implementation situation shall be promptly published to the villagers of the group.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对涉及村民切身利益的公共事务、公益事业以及村民反映的实际困难和矛盾纠纷，村民委员会应当组织村民及其他利益相关方开展协商。  
协商可以采取议事会、听证会、恳谈会等多种形式，根据需要邀请人民政府有关部门，乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府，群团组织和人民代表大会代表、政治协商会议委员会委员等参加。涉及农业科技、工程建设等专业性较强的事项，可以邀请相关专业人员参加。  
对协商确定的事项，村民委员会应当及时组织实施或者监督落实；需要提交村民会议或者村民代表会议的，应当召集会议讨论决定。

**Article 33** With respect to public affairs, public welfare undertakings that involve the vital interests of villagers, as well as actual difficulties and disputes reflected by villagers, the villagers committee shall organize villagers and other stakeholders to conduct consultations.  
Consultations may take various forms such as council meetings, hearings, and symposiums, and, as needed, relevant departments of the people's government, the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, mass organizations, deputies to the people's congress, and members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference may be invited to participate. Matters involving a high level of expertise such as agricultural science and technology and engineering construction may be addressed by inviting relevant professionals to participate.  
With respect to matters determined through consultation, the villagers committee shall organize implementation or supervise implementation in a timely manner; where the matter needs to be submitted to the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly, a meeting shall be convened to discuss and decide.

### 第五章　民主管理和民主监督

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　村民委员会应当实行少数服从多数的民主决策机制和公开透明的工作原则，建立健全各种工作制度。

**Article 34** The villagers committee shall practice a democratic decision-making mechanism of the minority being subordinate to the majority and the work principle of openness and transparency, and shall establish and improve various work systems.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　村民委员会实行村务公开制度。  
村民委员会应当及时公布下列事项，接受村民的监督：  
（一）[本法第二十七条](#art-27)、第二十八条规定的由村民会议、村民代表会议讨论决定的事项及其实施情况；  
（二）政府拨付和接受社会捐赠的救灾救助、补贴补助等资金、物资的管理使用情况；  
（三）村民委员会协助人民政府开展工作的情况；  
（四）村民委员会组织协商确定的事项及其落实情况；  
（五）涉及本村村民利益，村民普遍关心的其他事项。  
前款规定事项中，一般事项至少每季度公布一次；财务往来较多的，财务收支情况应当每月公布一次；涉及村民利益的重大事项应当随时公布。  
村民委员会应当设立村务公开栏，可以采用现代信息技术进行村务公开。公布的期限不得少于十五日。村民委员会应当保证公布事项的真实性，并接受村民的查询。

**Article 35** The villagers committee shall practice a system of openness of village affairs.  
The villagers committee shall promptly publish the following matters for the supervision of villagers:  
(1) The matters specified in Articles 27 and 28 of this Law that are discussed and decided by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly, and the implementation thereof;  
(2) The management and use of funds and materials such as disaster relief, assistance, subsidies, and grants allocated by the government and received from social donations;  
(3) The situation of the villagers committee assisting the people's government in carrying out its work;  
(4) The matters determined through consultation organized by the villagers committee and the implementation thereof;  
(5) Other matters that involve the interests of the villagers and are of general concern to the villagers.  
Among the matters specified in the preceding paragraph, general matters shall be published at least once every quarter; where there are frequent financial transactions, the financial revenue and expenditure shall be published once a month; major matters involving the interests of villagers shall be published at any time.  
The villagers committee shall set up a bulletin board for the openness of village affairs, and may use modern information technology for the openness of village affairs. The publication period shall not be less than fifteen days. The villagers committee shall ensure the authenticity of the published matters and accept inquiries from villagers.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　村民委员会不及时公布应当公布的事项或者公布的事项不真实的，村民有权向乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府或者县级人民政府及其有关主管部门反映，有关人民政府或者主管部门应当负责调查核实，责令依法公布；经查证确有违法行为的，有关人员应当依法承担责任。

**Article 36** Where the villagers committee fails to publish in a timely manner matters that should be published or publishes matters that are untrue, villagers shall have the right to report to the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, or to the people's government at the county level and its relevant competent departments. The relevant people's government or competent department shall be responsible for investigating and verifying the matter and ordering publication according to law; if there is indeed any illegal act after verification, the relevant persons shall bear liability according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　村应当建立村务监督委员会或者其他形式的村务监督机构，负责村民民主理财，监督村务公开等制度的落实，其成员由村民会议或者村民代表会议在村民中推选产生，其中应有具备财会、管理知识的人员。村民委员会成员及其近亲属不得担任村务监督机构成员。村务监督机构的任期与村民委员会的任期相同。  
村务监督机构向村民会议和村民代表会议负责，其成员可以列席村民委员会会议和村民代表会议，参与各项协商活动。  
村务监督机构在履行职责过程中发现有侵害群众利益等违纪违法行为的，应当向乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府或者县级人民政府和监察机关反映。

**Article 37** A village shall establish a village affairs supervision committee or other forms of village affairs supervision bodies, which shall be responsible for villagers' democratic financial management and for supervising the implementation of systems such as openness of village affairs. Its members shall be elected by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly from among the villagers, and there shall be persons with knowledge of finance, accounting, and management. The members of the villagers committee and their close relatives shall not serve as members of the village affairs supervision body. The term of office of the village affairs supervision body shall be the same as that of the villagers committee.  
The village affairs supervision body shall be responsible to the villagers assembly and the villagers representative assembly, and its members may attend meetings of the villagers committee and the villagers representative assembly as non-voting participants and participate in various consultation activities.  
Where, in the course of performing its duties, the village affairs supervision body discovers any violation of discipline or law that infringes upon the interests of the masses, it shall report to the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, or to the people's government at the county level and the supervisory organ.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　村民委员会成员以及由村民或者村集体承担误工补贴的聘用人员，应当接受村民会议或者村民代表会议对其履行职责情况的民主评议。民主评议每年至少进行一次，由村务监督机构主持。  
村民委员会成员连续两次被评议不称职的，其职务终止。

**Article 38** Members of the villagers committee and hired personnel whose work-failure subsidies are borne by the villagers or the village collective shall accept democratic appraisal by the villagers assembly or the villagers representative assembly of their performance of duties. Democratic appraisal shall be conducted at least once a year, and shall be presided over by the village affairs supervision body.  
Where a member of the villagers committee is appraised as incompetent for two consecutive times, his or her position shall be terminated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　村民委员会和村务监督机构应当建立村务档案。村务档案包括：选举文件和选票，会议记录，公益设施基本资料，基本建设资料等。村务档案应当真实、准确、完整和规范。

**Article 39** The villagers committee and the village affairs supervision body shall establish village affairs archives. Village affairs archives shall include: election documents and ballots, meeting minutes, basic data of public welfare facilities, basic construction data, etc. The village affairs archives shall be authentic, accurate, complete, and standardized.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　村民委员会成员实行任期和离任经济责任审计，审计包括下列事项：  
（一）本村财务收支情况；  
（二）本村债权债务情况；  
（三）政府拨付和接受社会捐赠的资金、物资管理使用情况；  
（四）本村公益事业建设项目招标投标情况；  
（五）本村资金管理使用情况；  
（六）本村五分之一以上的村民要求审计的其他事项。  
村民委员会成员的任期和离任经济责任审计，由县级人民政府农业农村部门、财政部门或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府负责组织，审计结果应当公布，其中离任经济责任审计结果应当在下一届村民委员会选举之前公布。

**Article 40** Members of the villagers committee shall undergo term and departure economic responsibility audits, which shall include the following matters:  
(1) The financial revenue and expenditure of the village;  
(2) The claims and debts of the village;  
(3) The management and use of funds and materials allocated by the government and received from social donations;  
(4) The bidding and tendering of public welfare construction projects in the village;  
(5) The management and use of funds in the village;  
(6) Other matters for which more than one-fifth of the villagers in the village request an audit.  
The term and departure economic responsibility audits of members of the villagers committee shall be organized by the agricultural and rural department or the finance department of the people's government at the county level, or by the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town. The audit results shall be published, and the departure economic responsibility audit results shall be published before the election of the next term of the villagers committee.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　村民委员会或者村民委员会成员作出的决定侵害村民合法权益的，受侵害的村民可以申请人民法院予以撤销，责任人依法承担法律责任。  
村民委员会不依照法律、法规的规定履行法定义务的，由乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府责令改正。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府干预依法属于村民自治范围事项的，由上一级人民政府责令改正。

**Article 41** Where a decision made by the villagers committee or a member of the villagers committee infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of a villager, the aggrieved villager may apply to the People's Court for annulment, and the responsible person shall bear legal liability according to law.  
Where the villagers committee fails to perform its legal obligations in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall order correction.  
Where the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town interferes with matters that fall within the scope of villagers' self-governance according to law, the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction.

### 第六章　村民委员会工作的保障

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　村民委员会日常运转经费、成员基本报酬标准由县级以上人民政府规定，并由县级人民政府给予适当补助。  
村民委员会办理本村公益事业所需的经费，由村民会议通过筹资筹劳解决；经费确有困难的，由地方人民政府给予适当支持。  
村集体经济收入应当适当用于村公共事务和公益事业、村民委员会运转保障以及误工人员补贴。

**Article 42** The standards for daily operation funds of the villagers committee and the basic remuneration of its members shall be prescribed by the people's government at or above the county level, and the people's government at the county level shall provide appropriate subsidies.  
The funds required for the villagers committee to handle public welfare undertakings in the village shall be raised through fund-raising and labor contribution by the villagers assembly; where there is genuine difficulty in funds, the local people's government shall provide appropriate support.  
The income of the village collective economy shall be appropriately used for village public affairs and public welfare undertakings, the guarantee of the operation of the villagers committee, and subsidies for persons who miss work.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府对村民委员会协助政府开展工作应当提供必要的经费和条件。  
地方各级人民政府应当建立健全整治形式主义为基层减负长效机制，有关部门委托村民委员会协助开展工作的事项，应当符合法律、法规规定，所需经费由委托部门承担。

**Article 43** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall provide necessary funds and conditions for the villagers committee to assist the government in carrying out its work.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall establish and improve a long-term mechanism for combating formalism and reducing burdens at the grassroots level. Matters on which relevant departments entrust the villagers committee to assist in carrying out work shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations, and the necessary funds shall be borne by the entrusting department.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　村级综合服务设施建设应当纳入相关规划。县级以上地方人民政府应当对村级综合服务设施建设给予支持。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当对乡村治理信息化建设进行统筹规划和实施，鼓励和支持村民委员会运用现代信息技术服务村民。

**Article 44** The construction of comprehensive service facilities at the village level shall be included in relevant plans. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide support for the construction of comprehensive service facilities at the village level.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall plans and implementation for the informatization construction of rural governance, and encourage and support the villagers committee to use modern information technology to serve the villagers.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府或者县级人民政府应当为村民委员会成员以及其他村务工作者提供培训，帮助其提升政治素质、法治意识、政策水平和服务能力。

**Article 45** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town or the people's government at the county level shall provide training for the members of the villagers committee and other village affairs workers, helping them to improve their political quality, awareness of the rule of law, policy level, and service capacity.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　不设区的市、市辖区、街道以及开发区、林区、垦区等设立村民委员会的，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 46** Where a city not divided into districts, a municipal district, a subdistrict, or a development zone, forest area, or reclamation area establishes a villagers committee, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　驻在农村的机关、团体、部队、国有及国有控股企业、事业单位及其人员不参加村民委员会组织，但应当通过多种形式参与乡村建设，并遵守有关村规民约。  
村民委员会、村民会议或者村民代表会议讨论决定与前款规定的单位有关的事项，应当与其协商。

**Article 47** State organs, organizations, military units, state-owned and state-controlled enterprises, public institutions, and their personnel stationed in rural areas shall not participate in the organization of the villagers committee, but shall participate in rural construction through various forms and abide by the relevant village rules and conventions.  
Where the villagers committee, the villagers assembly, or the villagers representative assembly discusses and decides matters related to the units specified in the preceding paragraph, they shall consult with such units.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地方各级人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会在本行政区域内保证本法的实施，保障村民依法行使自治权利。

**Article 48** Local people's congresses at all levels and the standing committees of local people's congresses at or above the county level shall ensure the implementation of this Law within their respective administrative regions and guarantee that villagers exercise their self-governance rights according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会根据本法，结合本行政区域的实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 49** The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with this Law and in light of the actual circumstances of their respective administrative regions, formulate implementing measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force as of the date of its promulgation.
