# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》审判监督程序若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕14号  
**公布日期**：2008-11-25  
**生效日期**：2008-12-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉审判监督程序若干问题的解释》已于2008年11月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1453次会议通过，现予公布，自2008年12月1日起施行。 2008年11月25日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》 审判监督程序若干问题的解释 法释〔2008〕14号 （2008年11月10日最高人民法院审判委员会第1453次会议通过　2008年11月25日最高人民法院公告公布　自2008年12月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人在[民事诉讼法第一百八十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-184)规定的期限内，以[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)所列明的再审事由，向原审人民法院的上一级人民法院申请再审的，上一级人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where a party applies for a retrial to the people's court at the next higher level of the original trial court within the time limit prescribed in Article 184 of the Civil Procedure Law on the grounds for retrial specified in Article 179 of the Civil Procedure Law, the people's court at the next higher level shall accept the case in accordance with law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[民事诉讼法第一百八十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-184)规定的申请再审期间不适用中止、中断和延长的规定。

**Article 2** The provisions on suspension, interruption, and extension shall not apply to the time limit for applying for a retrial as prescribed in Article 184 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人申请再审，应当向人民法院提交再审申请书，并按照对方当事人人数提出副本。  
人民法院应当审查再审申请书是否载明下列事项：  
（一）申请再审人与对方当事人的姓名、住所及有效联系方式等基本情况；法人或其他组织的名称、住所和法定代表人或主要负责人的姓名、职务及有效联系方式等基本情况；  
（二）原审人民法院的名称，原判决、裁定、调解文书案号；  
（三）申请再审的法定情形及具体事实、理由；  
（四）具体的再审请求。

**Article 3** A party who applies for a retrial shall submit a written application for retrial to the people's court and serve copies thereof according to the number of the opposite party.  
The people's court shall examine whether the application for retrial contains the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the applicant and the opposite party, such as names, addresses, and valid means of contact; for a legal person or any other organization, its name, address, and the name, position, and valid means of contact of its legal representative or principal responsible person;  
(2) The name of the original trial people's court, and the case number of the original judgment, ruling, or mediation document;  
(3) The statutory circumstances for applying for a retrial and the specific facts and reasons;  
(4) The specific claims for retrial.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人申请再审，应当向人民法院提交已经发生法律效力的判决书、裁定书、调解书，身份证明及相关证据材料。

**Article 4** A party who applies for a retrial shall submit to the people's court the legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document, identity certificate, and relevant evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　案外人对原判决、裁定、调解书确定的执行标的物主张权利，且无法提起新的诉讼解决争议的，可以在判决、裁定、调解书发生法律效力后二年内，或者自知道或应当知道利益被损害之日起三个月内，向作出原判决、裁定、调解书的人民法院的上一级人民法院申请再审。  
在执行过程中，案外人对执行标的提出书面异议的，按照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)的规定处理。

**Article 5** A person other than a party who claims rights over the subject matter of enforcement determined by the original judgment, ruling, or mediation document and is unable to file a new lawsuit to resolve the dispute may apply for a retrial to the people's court at the next higher level of the people's court that rendered the original judgment, ruling, or mediation document within two years after the judgment, ruling, or mediation document becomes legally effective, or within three months from the date when he knows or should have known that his interests are harmed.  
During the enforcement process, if a person other than a party raises a written objection to the subject matter of enforcement, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请再审人提交的再审申请书或者其他材料不符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)、第四条的规定，或者有人身攻击等内容，可能引起矛盾激化的，人民法院应当要求申请再审人补充或改正。

**Article 6** Where the application for retrial or other materials submitted by the applicant for retrial do not comply with the provisions of Articles 3 and 4 of this Interpretation, or contain personal attacks or other content that may intensify conflicts, the people's court shall require the applicant to supplement or correct them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院应当自收到符合条件的再审申请书等材料后五日内完成向申请再审人发送受理通知书等受理登记手续，并向对方当事人发送受理通知书及再审申请书副本。

**Article 7** The people's court shall, within five days after receiving the qualified application for retrial and other materials, complete the acceptance registration formalities such as sending a notice of acceptance to the applicant, and send a notice of acceptance and a copy of the application for retrial to the opposite party.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院受理再审申请后，应当组成合议庭予以审查。

**Article 8** After accepting an application for retrial, the people's court shall form a collegial panel to conduct an examination.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院对再审申请的审查，应当围绕再审事由是否成立进行。

**Article 9** The examination of an application for retrial by the people's court shall focus on whether the grounds for retrial are established.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请再审人提交下列证据之一的，人民法院可以认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（一）项规定的“新的证据”：  
（一）原审庭审结束前已客观存在庭审结束后新发现的证据；  
（二）原审庭审结束前已经发现，但因客观原因无法取得或在规定的期限内不能提供的证据；  
（三）原审庭审结束后原作出鉴定结论、勘验笔录者重新鉴定、勘验，推翻原结论的证据。  
当事人在原审中提供的主要证据，原审未予质证、认证，但足以推翻原判决、裁定的，应当视为新的证据。

**Article 10** Where the applicant for retrial submits any of the following evidence, the people's court may deem it as 'new evidence' as provided in Article 179(1)(i) of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) Evidence that objectively existed before the conclusion of the original trial proceedings but was discovered after the conclusion thereof;  
(2) Evidence that was discovered before the conclusion of the original trial proceedings but could not be obtained for objective reasons or could not be provided within the prescribed time limit;  
(3) Evidence that, after the conclusion of the original trial proceedings, the original expert evaluator or inspection record maker re-conducted the expert evaluation or inspection and overturned the original conclusion.  
Where the main evidence provided by a party in the original trial was not cross-examined or authenticated in the original trial, but is sufficient to overturn the original judgment or ruling, it shall be regarded as new evidence.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对原判决、裁定的结果有实质影响、用以确定当事人主体资格、案件性质、具体权利义务和民事责任等主要内容所依据的事实，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（二）项规定的“基本事实”。

**Article 11** The facts that have a substantive impact on the outcome of the original judgment or ruling and are used to determine the main content such as the parties' qualifications, nature of the case, specific rights and obligations, and civil liability shall be deemed by the people's court as 'basic facts' as provided in Article 179(1)(ii) of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（五）项规定的“对审理案件需要的证据”，是指人民法院认定案件基本事实所必需的证据。

**Article 12** The 'evidence necessary for the adjudication of the case' as provided in Article 179(1)(v) of the Civil Procedure Law refers to the evidence necessary for the people's court to determine the basic facts of the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　原判决、裁定适用法律、法规或司法解释有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（六）项规定的“适用法律确有错误”：  
（一）适用的法律与案件性质明显不符的；  
（二）确定民事责任明显违背当事人约定或者法律规定的；  
（三）适用已经失效或尚未施行的法律的；  
（四）违反法律溯及力规定的；  
（五）违反法律适用规则的；  
（六）明显违背立法本意的。

**Article 13** Where the original judgment or ruling falls under any of the following circumstances in applying laws, regulations, or judicial interpretations, the people's court shall deem it as 'definite error in applying the law' as provided in Article 179(1)(vi) of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) The applied law is clearly inconsistent with the nature of the case;  
(2) The determination of civil liability clearly violates the agreement of the parties or the provisions of the law;  
(3) The application of a law that has ceased to have effect or has not yet come into force;  
(4) Violation of provisions on the retroactive effect of laws;  
(5) Violation of rules on the application of laws;  
(6) Clear departure from the legislative intent.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　违反专属管辖、专门管辖规定以及其他严重违法行使管辖权的，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（七）项规定的“管辖错误”。

**Article 14** Violations of the provisions on exclusive jurisdiction or specialized jurisdiction, or other serious illegal exercise of jurisdiction, shall be deemed by the people's court as 'error in jurisdiction' as provided in Article 179(1)(vii) of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　原审开庭过程中审判人员不允许当事人行使辩论权利，或者以不送达起诉状副本或上诉状副本等其他方式，致使当事人无法行使辩论权利的，人民法院应当认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（十）项规定的“剥夺当事人辩论权利”。但依法缺席审理，依法径行判决、裁定的除外。

**Article 15** Where, during the original trial, the adjudicators do not allow the party to exercise the right to debate, or by other means such as not serving a copy of the statement of claim or copy of the appeal petition, cause the party to be unable to exercise the right to debate, the people's court shall deem it as 'deprivation of the party's right to debate' as provided in Article 179(1)(x) of the Civil Procedure Law, except where the trial was conducted by default or a judgment or ruling was rendered directly in accordance with law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　原判决、裁定对基本事实和案件性质的认定系根据其他法律文书作出，而上述其他法律文书被撤销或变更的，人民法院可以认定为[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（十三）项规定的情形。

**Article 16** Where the original judgment or ruling's determination of basic facts and the nature of the case is based on other legal documents, and such other legal documents are revoked or modified, the people's court may deem it as the circumstance provided in Article 179(1)(xiii) of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第二款规定的“违反法定程序可能影响案件正确判决、裁定的情形”，是指除[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（四）项以及第（七）项至第（十二）项之外的其他违反法定程序，可能导致案件裁判结果错误的情形。

**Article 17** The 'circumstances where violation of statutory procedures may affect the correct judgment or ruling of the case' as provided in Article 179, paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law refers to circumstances other than those under Article 179(1)(iv) and (vii) to (xii) of the Civil Procedure Law that violate statutory procedures and may lead to erroneous adjudication results.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第二款规定的“审判人员在审理该案件时有贪污受贿，徇私舞弊，枉法裁判行为”，是指该行为已经相关刑事法律文书或者纪律处分决定确认的情形。

**Article 18** The 'acts of embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, or perversion of justice by the adjudicators in the trial of the case' as provided in Article 179, paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law refers to circumstances where such acts have been confirmed by relevant criminal legal documents or disciplinary decision.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院经审查再审申请书等材料，认为申请再审事由成立的，应当径行裁定再审。  
当事人申请再审超过[民事诉讼法第一百八十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-184)规定的期限，或者超出[民事诉讼法第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)所列明的再审事由范围的，人民法院应当裁定驳回再审申请。

**Article 19** Where the people's court, after examining the application for retrial and other materials, considers that the grounds for retrial are established, it shall directly make a ruling for retrial.  
Where the party's application for retrial exceeds the time limit prescribed in Article 184 of the Civil Procedure Law, or goes beyond the scope of grounds for retrial listed in Article 179 of the Civil Procedure Law, the people's court shall make a ruling to reject the application for retrial.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院认为仅审查再审申请书等材料难以作出裁定的，应当调阅原审卷宗予以审查。

**Article 20** Where the people's court considers that it is difficult to make a ruling by only examining the application for retrial and other materials, it shall retrieve the original trial files for examination.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院可以根据案情需要决定是否询问当事人。  
以有新的证据足以推翻原判决、裁定为由申请再审的，人民法院应当询问当事人。

**Article 21** The people's court may decide whether to interrogate the parties based on the needs of the case.  
Where an application for retrial is made on the ground that there is new evidence sufficient to overturn the original judgment or ruling, the people's court shall interrogate the parties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在审查再审申请过程中，对方当事人也申请再审的，人民法院应当将其列为申请再审人，对其提出的再审申请一并审查。

**Article 22** Where, during the examination of an application for retrial, the opposite party also applies for a retrial, the people's court shall list it as an applicant for retrial and examine its application for retrial together.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　申请再审人在案件审查期间申请撤回再审申请的，是否准许，由人民法院裁定。  
申请再审人经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不接受询问，可以裁定按撤回再审申请处理。

**Article 23** Where the applicant for retrial applies to withdraw the application for retrial during the examination period, the people's court shall rule on whether to permit it.  
Where the applicant for retrial, after being summoned by subpoena, refuses without justifiable grounds to submit to interrogation, the people's court may rule that the application for retrial is deemed withdrawn.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民法院经审查认为申请再审事由不成立的，应当裁定驳回再审申请。  
驳回再审申请的裁定一经送达，即发生法律效力。

**Article 24** Where the people's court, after examination, considers that the grounds for retrial are not established, it shall make a ruling to reject the application for retrial.  
A ruling rejecting the application for retrial becomes legally effective upon service.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以裁定终结审查：  
（一）申请再审人死亡或者终止，无权利义务承受人或者权利义务承受人声明放弃再审申请的；  
（二）在给付之诉中，负有给付义务的被申请人死亡或者终止，无可供执行的财产，也没有应当承担义务的人的；  
（三）当事人达成执行和解协议且已履行完毕的，但当事人在执行和解协议中声明不放弃申请再审权利的除外；  
（四）当事人之间的争议可以另案解决的。

**Article 25** Under any of the following circumstances, the people's court may rule to terminate the examination:  
(1) The applicant for retrial dies or is dissolved, and there is no person succeeding to the rights and obligations or the person succeeding declares to waive the application for retrial;  
(2) In an action for payment, the respondent obliged to make payment dies or is dissolved, and there is no property available for enforcement nor any person to assume the obligation;  
(3) The parties have reached an enforcement settlement agreement and have fully performed it, except where the parties declare in the settlement agreement that they do not waive the right to apply for retrial;  
(4) The dispute between the parties may be resolved in a separate action.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民法院审查再审申请期间，人民检察院对该案提出抗诉的，人民法院应依照[民事诉讼法第一百八十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-188)的规定裁定再审。申请再审人提出的具体再审请求应纳入审理范围。

**Article 26** Where, during the period when the people's court is examining the application for retrial, the People's Procuratorate files a protest against the case, the people's court shall make a ruling for retrial in accordance with Article 188 of the Civil Procedure Law. The specific claims for retrial raised by the applicant for retrial shall be included in the scope of trial.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　上一级人民法院经审查认为申请再审事由成立的，一般由本院提审。最高人民法院、高级人民法院也可以指定与原审人民法院同级的其他人民法院再审，或者指令原审人民法院再审。

**Article 27** Where the people's court at the next higher level, after examination, considers that the grounds for retrial are established, it shall generally bring the case up for trial by itself. The Supreme People's Court and a Higher People's Court may also designate another people's court at the same level as the original trial court to conduct the retrial, or instruct the original trial court to conduct the retrial.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　上一级人民法院可以根据案件的影响程度以及案件参与人等情况，决定是否指定再审。需要指定再审的，应当考虑便利当事人行使诉讼权利以及便利人民法院审理等因素。  
接受指定再审的人民法院，应当按照[民事诉讼法第一百八十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-186)第一款规定的程序审理。

**Article 28** The people's court at the next higher level may decide whether to designate a retrial based on the degree of influence of the case and the participants in the case. If it is necessary to designate a retrial, consideration shall be given to factors such as facilitating the parties' exercise of litigation rights and facilitating the people's court's trial.  
The people's court that accepts the designation for retrial shall conduct the trial in accordance with the procedure prescribed in Article 186(1) of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有下列情形之一的，不得指令原审人民法院再审：  
（一）原审人民法院对该案无管辖权的；  
（二）审判人员在审理该案件时有贪污受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁判行为的；  
（三）原判决、裁定系经原审人民法院审判委员会讨论作出的；  
（四）其他不宜指令原审人民法院再审的。

**Article 29** Under any of the following circumstances, the original trial court shall not be instructed to conduct the retrial:  
(1) The original trial court has no jurisdiction over the case;  
(2) The adjudicators committed acts of embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, or perversion of justice in the trial of the case;  
(3) The original judgment or ruling was made after discussion by the adjudication committee of the original trial court;  
(4) Other circumstances where it is inappropriate to instruct the original trial court to conduct the retrial.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　当事人未申请再审、人民检察院未抗诉的案件，人民法院发现原判决、裁定、调解协议有损害国家利益、社会公共利益等确有错误情形的，应当依照[民事诉讼法第一百七十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-177)的规定提起再审。

**Article 30** For a case where the parties have not applied for retrial and the People's Procuratorate has not filed a protest, if the people's court discovers that the original judgment, ruling, or mediation agreement has definite errors such as harming national interests or public interests, it shall initiate a retrial in accordance with Article 177 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第一百八十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-186)的规定，按照第一审程序或者第二审程序审理再审案件。  
人民法院审理再审案件应当开庭审理。但按照第二审程序审理的，双方当事人已经其他方式充分表达意见，且书面同意不开庭审理的除外。

**Article 31** The people's court shall, in accordance with Article 186 of the Civil Procedure Law, try a retrial case under the procedure of first instance or the procedure of second instance.  
The people's court shall hold a hearing in trying a retrial case, except where, in a retrial conducted under the procedure of second instance, both parties have fully expressed their opinions through other means and have agreed in writing that a hearing is not necessary.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院开庭审理再审案件，应分别不同情形进行：  
（一）因当事人申请裁定再审的，先由申请再审人陈述再审请求及理由，后由被申请人答辩及其他原审当事人发表意见；  
（二）因人民检察院抗诉裁定再审的，先由抗诉机关宣读抗诉书，再由申请抗诉的当事人陈述，后由被申请人答辩及其他原审当事人发表意见；  
（三）人民法院依职权裁定再审的，当事人按照其在原审中的诉讼地位依次发表意见。

**Article 32** When the people's court holds a hearing in a retrial case, the trial shall be conducted in different manners according to the circumstances:  
(1) If the ruling for retrial is made upon the party's application, the applicant for retrial shall first state the claims and reasons for retrial, followed by the respondent's reply and opinions from other original trial parties;  
(2) If the ruling for retrial is made upon a protest by the People's Procuratorate, the protesting authority shall first read out the protest document, followed by the statement of the party who applied for the protest, and then the respondent's reply and opinions from other original trial parties;  
(3) If the ruling for retrial is made ex officio by the people's court, the parties shall state their opinions in turn according to their litigation status in the original trial.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院应当在具体的再审请求范围内或在抗诉支持当事人请求的范围内审理再审案件。当事人超出原审范围增加、变更诉讼请求的，不属于再审审理范围。但涉及国家利益、社会公共利益，或者当事人在原审诉讼中已经依法要求增加、变更诉讼请求，原审未予审理且客观上不能形成其他诉讼的除外。  
经再审裁定撤销原判决，发回重审后，当事人增加诉讼请求的，人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第一百二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-126)的规定处理。

**Article 33** The people's court shall try a retrial case within the scope of the specific claims for retrial or within the scope of the protest supporting the party's claims. Where a party adds or modifies claims beyond the scope of the original trial, such claims shall not fall within the scope of the retrial trial, except where they involve national interests or public interests, or where the party had already requested the addition or modification of claims in accordance with law in the original trial, but the original trial did not adjudicate them and it is objectively impossible to form another action.  
After the original judgment is revoked by the ruling for retrial and the case is remanded for retrial, if a party adds claims, the people's court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 126 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　申请再审人在再审期间撤回再审申请的，是否准许由人民法院裁定。裁定准许的，应终结再审程序。申请再审人经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，可以裁定按自动撤回再审申请处理。  
人民检察院抗诉再审的案件，申请抗诉的当事人有前款规定的情形，且不损害国家利益、社会公共利益或第三人利益的，人民法院应当裁定终结再审程序；人民检察院撤回抗诉的，应当准予。  
终结再审程序的，恢复原判决的执行。

**Article 34** Where the applicant for retrial withdraws the application for retrial during the retrial, the people's court shall rule on whether to permit it. If the withdrawal is permitted, the retrial proceedings shall be terminated. Where the applicant for retrial fails to appear in court without justifiable grounds after being summoned by subpoena, or leaves the court midway without permission, the people's court may rule that the application for retrial is deemed automatically withdrawn.  
For a case subject to retrial upon a protest by the People's Procuratorate, if the party who applied for the protest falls under the circumstances prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and such circumstances do not harm national interests, public interests, or the interests of a third party, the people's court shall rule to terminate the retrial proceedings; if the People's Procuratorate withdraws its protest, the withdrawal shall be permitted.  
Where the retrial proceedings are terminated, the enforcement of the original judgment shall be resumed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　按照第一审程序审理再审案件时，一审原告申请撤回起诉的，是否准许由人民法院裁定。裁定准许的，应当同时裁定撤销原判决、裁定、调解书。

**Article 35** When a retrial case is tried under the procedure of first instance, if the plaintiff of the first instance applies to withdraw the prosecution, the people's court shall rule on whether to permit it. If the withdrawal is permitted, the people's court shall at the same time rule to revoke the original judgment, ruling, or mediation document.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　当事人在再审审理中经调解达成协议的，人民法院应当制作调解书。调解书经各方当事人签收后，即具有法律效力，原判决、裁定视为被撤销。

**Article 36** Where parties reach an agreement through mediation during the retrial trial, the people's court shall prepare a mediation document. The mediation document shall become legally effective upon receipt by all parties, and the original judgment or ruling shall be deemed to have been revoked.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民法院经再审审理认为，原判决、裁定认定事实清楚、适用法律正确的，应予维持；原判决、裁定在认定事实、适用法律、阐述理由方面虽有瑕疵，但裁判结果正确的，人民法院应在再审判决、裁定中纠正上述瑕疵后予以维持。

**Article 37** Where the people's court, after the retrial trial, considers that the original judgment or ruling has clear findings of facts and correct application of law, it shall affirm the same; where the original judgment or ruling has defects in findings of fact, application of law, or reasoning, but the result of the adjudication is correct, the people's court shall affirm the same after correcting the above defects in the retrial judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　人民法院按照第二审程序审理再审案件，发现原判决认定事实错误或者认定事实不清的，应当在查清事实后改判。但原审人民法院便于查清事实，化解纠纷的，可以裁定撤销原判决，发回重审；原审程序遗漏必须参加诉讼的当事人且无法达成调解协议，以及其他违反法定程序不宜在再审程序中直接作出实体处理的，应当裁定撤销原判决，发回重审。

**Article 38** Where the people's court tries a retrial case under the procedure of second instance and finds that the original judgment has erroneous findings or unclear findings of fact, it shall amend the judgment after ascertaining the facts. However, if the original trial court can more conveniently ascertain the facts and resolve the dispute, it may rule to revoke the original judgment and remand for retrial; where the original trial procedure omitted a party that must participate in the litigation and no mediation agreement can be reached, or there are other violations of statutory procedures that make it inappropriate to directly make an substantive decision in the retrial procedure, the people's court shall rule to revoke the original judgment and remand for retrial.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　新的证据证明原判决、裁定确有错误的，人民法院应予改判。  
申请再审人或者申请抗诉的当事人提出新的证据致使再审改判，被申请人等当事人因申请再审人或者申请抗诉的当事人的过错未能在原审程序中及时举证，请求补偿其增加的差旅、误工等诉讼费用的，人民法院应当支持；请求赔偿其由此扩大的直接损失，可以另行提起诉讼解决。

**Article 39** Where new evidence proves that the original judgment or ruling is indeed erroneous, the people's court shall amend the judgment.  
Where the applicant for retrial or the party applying for a protest submits new evidence that leads to an amendment of the judgment on retrial, and the respondent or other parties, due to the fault of the applicant for retrial or the party applying for the protest, failed to timely produce evidence in the original trial procedure, and request compensation for the increased litigation costs such as travel and loss of working time, the people's court shall support such request; if they request compensation for the direct losses thereby expanded, they may file a separate lawsuit to resolve the matter.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民法院以调解方式审结的案件裁定再审后，经审理发现申请再审人提出的调解违反自愿原则的事由不成立，且调解协议的内容不违反法律强制性规定的，应当裁定驳回再审申请，并恢复原调解书的执行。

**Article 40** Where, after a case concluded by mediation by the people's court is ruled for retrial, upon trial it is found that the ground raised by the applicant for retrial that the mediation violated the principle of voluntariness is not established, and the content of the mediation agreement does not violate mandatory provisions of the law, the people's court shall rule to reject the application for retrial and resume the enforcement of the original mediation document.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　民事再审案件的当事人应为原审案件的当事人。原审案件当事人死亡或者终止的，其权利义务承受人可以申请再审并参加再审诉讼。

**Article 41** The parties to a civil retrial case shall be the parties to the original trial case. Where a party to the original trial case dies or is dissolved, the person succeeding to the rights and obligations may apply for retrial and participate in the retrial proceedings.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　因案外人申请人民法院裁定再审的，人民法院经审理认为案外人应为必要的共同诉讼当事人，在按第一审程序再审时，应追加其为当事人，作出新的判决；在按第二审程序再审时，经调解不能达成协议的，应撤销原判，发回重审，重审时应追加案外人为当事人。  
案外人不是必要的共同诉讼当事人的，仅审理其对原判决提出异议部分的合法性，并应根据审理情况作出撤销原判决相关判项或者驳回再审请求的判决；撤销原判决相关判项的，应当告知案外人以及原审当事人可以提起新的诉讼解决相关争议。

**Article 42** Where the people's court adjudicates a retrial upon the application of a person other than a party, if the people's court upon trial considers that the person other than a party shall be a necessary co-litigant, when retrying under the procedure of first instance, it shall add that person as a party and render a new judgment; when retrying under the procedure of second instance, if no mediation agreement can be reached, it shall revoke the original judgment and remand for retrial, and shall add the person other than a party as a party in the retrial.  
If the person other than a party is not a necessary co-litigant, the court shall only examine the legality of the objections raised by that person against the original judgment, and based on the trial, render a judgment either revoking the relevant adjudication items of the original judgment or rejecting the request for retrial; if it revokes the relevant adjudication items of the original judgment, it shall inform the person other than a party and the original trial parties that they may file a new lawsuit to resolve the relevant dispute.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。本解释未作规定的，按照以前的规定执行。

**Article 43** Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail. Matters not provided for in this Interpretation shall be implemented in accordance with the previous provisions.
