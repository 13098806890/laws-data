# 最高人民法院关于民事审判监督程序严格依法适用指令再审和发回重审若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕7号  
**公布日期**：2015-02-02  
**生效日期**：2015-03-15

> 最高人民法院《关于民事审判监督程序严格依法适用指令再审和发回重审若干问题的规定》已于2015年2月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1643次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年3月15日起施行。 2015年2月16日 最高人民法院 关于民事审判监督程序严格依法适用指令再审和发回重审若干问题的规定 法释〔2015〕7号 为了及时有效维护各方当事人的合法权益，维护司法公正，进一步规范民事案件指令再审和再审发回重审，提高审判监督质量和效率，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》，结合审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条　上级人民法院应当严格依照民事诉讼法第二百条等规定审查当事人的再审申请，符合法定条件的，裁定再审。不得因指令再审而降低再审启动标准，也不得因当事人反复申诉将依法不应当再审的案件指令下级人民法院再审。 第二条　因当事人申请裁定再审的案件一般应当由裁定再审的人民法院审理。有下列情形之一的，最高人民法院、高级人民法院可以指令原审人民法院再审： （一）依据民事诉讼法第二百条第（四）项、第（五）项或者第（九）项裁定再审的； （二）发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书是由第一审法院作出的； （三）当事人一方人数众多或者当事人双方为公民的； （四）经审判委员会讨论决定的其他情形。 人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，由接受抗诉的人民法院审理，具有民事诉讼法第二百条第（一）至第（五）项规定情形之一的，可以指令原审人民法院再审。 人民法院依据民事诉讼法第一百九十八条第二款裁定再审的，应当提审。 第三条　虽然符合本规定第二条可以指令再审的条件，但有下列情形之一的，应当提审：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　上级人民法院应当严格依照[民事诉讼法第二百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-200)等规定审查当事人的再审申请，符合法定条件的，裁定再审。不得因指令再审而降低再审启动标准，也不得因当事人反复申诉将依法不应当再审的案件指令下级人民法院再审。

**Article 1** A higher people's court shall strictly examine a party's application for retrial in accordance with Article 200 of the Civil Procedure Law and other provisions, and if the statutory conditions are met, shall rule to initiate a retrial. It shall not lower the threshold for initiating a retrial due to an order for retrial, nor shall it order a lower people's court to retry a case that should not be retried according to law due to repeated petitions by the party.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因当事人申请裁定再审的案件一般应当由裁定再审的人民法院审理。有下列情形之一的，最高人民法院、高级人民法院可以指令原审人民法院再审：  
（一）依据[民事诉讼法第二百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-200)第（四）项、第（五）项或者第（九）项裁定再审的；  
（二）发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书是由第一审法院作出的；  
（三）当事人一方人数众多或者当事人双方为公民的；  
（四）经审判委员会讨论决定的其他情形。  
人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，由接受抗诉的人民法院审理，具有[民事诉讼法第二百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-200)第（一）至第（五）项规定情形之一的，可以指令原审人民法院再审。  
人民法院依据[民事诉讼法第一百九十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-198)第二款裁定再审的，应当提审。

**Article 2** A case where a retrial is ordered upon a party's application shall generally be adjudicated by the people's court that ordered the retrial. Under any of the following circumstances, the Supreme People's Court or a higher people's court may order the original trial people's court to conduct a retrial:  
(1) the retrial is ordered pursuant to subparagraph (4), (5) or (9) of Article 200 of the Civil Procedure Law;  
(2) the legally effective judgment, ruling or mediation statement was made by the court of first instance;  
(3) one party consists of a large number of persons, or both parties are citizens;  
(4) other circumstances decided by the adjudication committee after discussion.  
For a case protested by a people's procuratorate, it shall be adjudicated by the people's court that accepted the protest. If any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1) to (5) of Article 200 of the Civil Procedure Law exist, the court may order the original trial people's court to conduct a retrial.  
If a people's court orders a retrial pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 198 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall bring the case up for trial.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　虽然符合[本规定第二条](#art-2)可以指令再审的条件，但有下列情形之一的，应当提审：  
（一）原判决、裁定系经原审人民法院再审审理后作出的；  
（二）原判决、裁定系经原审人民法院审判委员会讨论作出的；  
（三）原审审判人员在审理该案件时有贪污受贿，徇私舞弊，枉法裁判行为的；  
（四）原审人民法院对该案无再审管辖权的；  
（五）需要统一法律适用或裁量权行使标准的；  
（六）其他不宜指令原审人民法院再审的情形。

**Article 3** Although the conditions for ordering a retrial under Article 2 of these Provisions are met, under any of the following circumstances, the case shall be brought up for trial:  
(1) the original judgment or ruling was made after a retrial by the original trial people's court;  
(2) the original judgment or ruling was made after discussion by the adjudication committee of the original trial people's court;  
(3) the adjudicators of the original trial engaged in embezzlement, bribery, favoritism or perversion of the law in adjudicating the case;  
(4) the original trial people's court does not have jurisdiction over the retrial of the case;  
(5) it is necessary to unify the application of law or the standard for exercising discretion;  
(6) other circumstances where it is inappropriate to order the original trial people's court to conduct a retrial.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院按照第二审程序审理再审案件，发现原判决认定基本事实不清的，一般应当通过庭审认定事实后依法作出判决。但原审人民法院未对基本事实进行过审理的，可以裁定撤销原判决，发回重审。原判决认定事实错误的，上级人民法院不得以基本事实不清为由裁定发回重审。

**Article 4** When a people's court tries a retrial case under the procedure of second instance and finds that the basic facts found in the original judgment are unclear, it shall generally make a judgment according to law after finding the facts through trial. However, if the original trial people's court has not tried the basic facts, the court may rule to vacate the original judgment and remand the case for retrial. If the original judgment has erroneous findings of facts, the higher people's court shall not rule to remand the case for retrial on the ground that the basic facts are unclear.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院按照第二审程序审理再审案件，发现第一审人民法院有下列严重违反法定程序情形之一的，可以依照[民事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-170)第一款第（四）项的规定，裁定撤销原判决，发回第一审人民法院重审：  
（一）原判决遗漏必须参加诉讼的当事人的；  
（二）无诉讼行为能力人未经法定代理人代为诉讼,或者应当参加诉讼的当事人，因不能归责于本人或者其诉讼代理人的事由，未参加诉讼的；  
（三）未经合法传唤缺席判决，或者违反法律规定剥夺当事人辩论权利的；  
（四）审判组织的组成不合法或者依法应当回避的审判人员没有回避的；  
（五）原判决、裁定遗漏诉讼请求的。

**Article 5** When a people's court tries a retrial case under the procedure of second instance and finds that the court of first instance has committed any of the following serious violations of statutory procedure, it may, in accordance with subparagraph (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 170 of the Civil Procedure Law, rule to vacate the original judgment and remand the case to the court of first instance for retrial:  
(1) the original judgment omits a party who must participate in the proceedings;  
(2) a person without capacity for litigation is not represented by a legal agent, or a party who should participate in the proceedings fails to participate due to reasons not attributable to himself or his litigation agent;  
(3) a default judgment is rendered without lawful summons, or a party's right to debate is deprived in violation of legal provisions;  
(4) the composition of the adjudicatory organization is illegal, or an adjudicator who should withdraw according to law does not withdraw;  
(5) the original judgment or ruling omits a claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　上级人民法院裁定指令再审、发回重审的，应当在裁定书中阐明指令再审或者发回重审的具体理由。

**Article 6** If a higher people's court rules to order a retrial or to remand a case for retrial, it shall state the specific reasons for ordering the retrial or remanding for retrial in the ruling.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　再审案件应当围绕申请人的再审请求进行审理和裁判。对方当事人在再审庭审辩论终结前也提出再审请求的，应一并审理和裁判。当事人的再审请求超出原审诉讼请求的不予审理，构成另案诉讼的应告知当事人可以提起新的诉讼。

**Article 7** A retrial case shall be tried and adjudicated around the retrial claims of the applicant. If the opposing party also raises retrial claims before the conclusion of the debate in the retrial hearing, such claims shall be tried and adjudicated together. Retrial claims of a party that exceed the claims of the original trial shall not be tried; if they constitute a separate action, the party shall be informed that it may file a new lawsuit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　再审发回重审的案件,应当围绕当事人原诉讼请求进行审理。当事人申请变更、增加诉讼请求和提出反诉的，按照最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第二百五十二条的规定审查决定是否准许。当事人变更其在原审中的诉讼主张、质证及辩论意见的，应说明理由并提交相应的证据，理由不成立或证据不充分的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** A case remanded for retrial after a retrial shall be tried around the original claims of the parties. If a party applies to change or add claims or to file a counterclaim, the court shall examine and decide whether to permit it in accordance with Article 252 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. If a party changes its litigation assertions, evidence challenges, or debate opinions from the original trial, it shall state the reasons and submit corresponding evidence; if the reasons are not established or the evidence is insufficient, the people's court shall not support them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民法院对民事案件指令再审和再审发回重审的审判行为，应当严格遵守本规定。违反本规定的，应当依照相关规定追究有关人员的责任。

**Article 9** People's courts at all levels shall strictly comply with these Provisions in their judicial acts of ordering retrial of civil cases and remanding retrial cases for retrial. Anyone who violates these Provisions shall be held accountable in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 10** Any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court that is inconsistent with these Provisions shall no longer apply.
