### 最高人民法院关于审理刑事附带民事诉讼案件有关问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于审理刑事附带民事诉讼案件  
  
有关问题的批复  
  
（2000年11月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1142次会议通过，自2000年12月9日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕40号  
  
吉林省高级人民法院：  
  
你院吉高法〔2000〕46号《关于刑事附带民事诉讼案件中有关问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
第二审人民法院审理对附带民事诉讼部分提出上诉的案件，原告一方要求增加赔偿数额，第二审人民法院可以依法进行调解。调解未达成协议或者调解书送达前一方反悔的，第二审人民法院应当依照刑事诉讼法、民事诉讼法的有关规定作出判决或者裁定。  
  
根据《最高人民法院关于执行〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》第一百条的规定，对于附带民事诉讼当事人提出先予执行申请的，人民法院应当依照民事诉讼法的有关规定，裁定先予执行或者驳回申请。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2000] No. 40  
Higher People's Court of Jilin Province:  
Your Court's Ji Gao Fa [2000] No. 46 "Request for Instructions on Relevant Issues in Cases of Civil Actions Collateral to Criminal Proceedings" has been received. Upon deliberation, the reply is as follows:  
When the People's Court of second instance hears a case where an appeal is filed against the part concerning the civil action collateral to criminal proceedings, and the plaintiff requests an increase in the amount of compensation, the People's Court of second instance may conduct mediation according to law. If mediation fails to reach an agreement or either party retracts before the service of the mediation statement, the People's Court of second instance shall render a judgment or ruling in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and the Civil Procedure Law.  
According to Article 100 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Implementation of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where a party to a civil action collateral to criminal proceedings files an application for advance execution, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, rule to grant advance execution or dismiss the application.  
Hereby reply.
