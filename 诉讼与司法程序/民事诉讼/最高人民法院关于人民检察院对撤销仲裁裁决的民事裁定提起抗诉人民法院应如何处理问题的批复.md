### 最高人民法院关于人民检察院对撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定提起抗诉人民法院应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民检察院对撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定  
  
提起抗诉人民法院应如何处理问题的批复  
  
（2000年6月30日最高人民法院审判委员会第1121次会议通过，自2000年7月15日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕17号  
  
陕西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院陕高法〔1999〕183号《关于下级法院撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定确有错误，检察机关抗诉应如何处理的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
检察机关对发生法律效力的撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定提起抗诉，没有法律依据，人民法院不予受理。依照[《中华人民共和国仲裁法》第九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-9)的规定，仲裁裁决被人民法院依法撤销后，当事人可以重新达成仲裁协议申请仲裁，也可以向人民法院提起诉讼。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Judicial Interpretation No. 17 [2000]  
Higher People's Court of Shaanxi Province:  
We have received your court's request No. Shaan Gao Fa [1999] No. 183, titled 'Request for Instructions on How to Handle a Protest Filed by a Procuratorial Organ When a Civil Ruling of a Lower Court Setting Aside an Arbitration Award Is Definitely Erroneous'. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
A protest filed by a procuratorial organ against a legally effective civil ruling setting aside an arbitration award has no legal basis, and the People's Court shall not accept such a protest. In accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China, after an arbitration award is set aside by the People's Court according to law, the parties may reach a new arbitration agreement and apply for arbitration, or may institute proceedings in the People's Court.  
This is our reply.
