### 最高人民法院关于对被监禁或被劳动教养的人提起的民事诉讼如何确定案件管辖问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>中华人民共和国最高人民法院  
  
公    告  
  
最高人民法院《关于对被监禁或被劳动教养的人提起的民事诉讼如何确定案件管辖问题的批复》已于2010年11月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1503次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年12月15日起施行。  
  
2010年12月9日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于对被监禁或被劳动教养的人提起的民事诉讼  
  
如何确定案件管辖问题的批复  
  
法释〔2010〕16号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于对被监禁的人提起的诉讼如何确定案件管辖问题的请示》〔（2010）鲁立函字第10号〕收悉。经研究，答复如下：根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-23)、最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第8条规定，对被监禁或被劳动教养的人提起的诉讼，原告没有被监禁或被劳动教养的，由原告住所地人民法院管辖。原告也被监禁或被劳动教养的，由被告原住所地人民法院管辖；被告被监禁或被劳动教养一年以上的，由被告被监禁地或被劳动教养地人民法院管辖。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** December 9, 2010  
Supreme People's Court  
Regarding Civil Actions Brought Against Persons Imprisoned or Subjected to Re-education through Labor  
Reply on How to Determine the Jurisdiction of Cases  
Fa Shi [2010] No. 16  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your submitted Request on How to Determine the Jurisdiction of Actions Brought Against Imprisoned Persons [(2010) Lu Li Han Zi No. 10] has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows: According to Article 23 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 8 of the Supreme People's Court's Opinions on Several Issues concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, for an action brought against a person imprisoned or subjected to re-education through labor, if the plaintiff is not imprisoned or subjected to re-education through labor, the People's Court at the plaintiff's domicile shall have jurisdiction. If the plaintiff is also imprisoned or subjected to re-education through labor, the People's Court at the defendant's original domicile shall have jurisdiction; if the defendant has been imprisoned or subjected to re-education through labor for more than one year, the People's Court at the place of imprisonment or re-education through labor of the defendant shall have jurisdiction.  
Hereby reply.
