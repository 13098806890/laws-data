# 最高人民法院关于在经济审判工作中严格执行《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕29号  
**公布日期**：1994-12-22

> 7、各高级人民法院就本省、自治区、直辖市作出的关于案件级别管辖的规定，应当报送最高人民法院批准。未经批准的，不能作为级别管辖的依据；已经批准公布实施的，应当认真执行，不得随意更改。 8、地方各级人民法院不得自行作出地域管辖的规定，已作规定的，一律无效。 二、关于无独立请求权的第三人 9、受诉人民法院对与原被告双方争议的诉讼标的无直接牵连和不负有返还或者赔偿等义务的人，以及与原告或被告约定仲裁或有约定管辖的案外人，或者专属管辖案件的一方当事人，均不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。 10、人民法院在审理产品质量纠纷案件中，对原被告之间法律关系以外的人，证据已证明其已经提供了合同约定或者符合法律规定的产品的，或者案件中的当事人未在规定的质量异议期内提出异议的，或者作为收货方已经认可该产品质量的，不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。 11、人民法院对已经履行了义务，或者依法取得了一方当事人的财产，并支付了相应对价的原被告之间法律关系以外的人，不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。 三、关于财产保全和先予执行 12、人民法院采取诉前财产保全，必须由申请人提供相当于请求保全数额的担保。担保的条件，依法律规定；法律未作规定的，由人民法院审查决定。 13、人民法院对财产采取诉讼保全措施，一般应当由当事人提交符合法定条件的申请。只有在诉讼争议的财产有毁损、灭失等危险，或者有证据表明被申请人可能采取隐匿、转移、出卖其财产的，人民法院方可依职权裁定采取财产保全措施。 14、人民法院采取财产保全措施时，保全的范围应当限于当事人争议的财产，或者被告的财产。对案外人的财产不得采取保全措施，对案外人善意取得的与案件有关的财产，一般也不得采取财产保全措施；被申请人提供相应数额并有可供执行的财产作担保的，采取措施的人民法院应当及时解除财产保全。 15、人民法院对有偿还能力的企业法人，一般不得采取查封、冻结的保全措施。已采取查封、冻结保全措施的，如该企业法人提供了可供执行的财产担保，或者可以采取其他方式保全的，应当及时予以解封、解冻。 16、人民法院先予执行的裁定，应当由当事人提出书面申请，并经开庭审理后作出。在管辖权尚未确定的情况下，不得裁定先予执行。 17、人民法院对当事人申请先予执行的案件，只有在案件的基本事实清楚，当事人间的权利义务关系明确，被申请人负有给付、返还或者赔偿义务，先予执行的财产为申请人生产、生活所急需，不先予执行会造成更大损失的情况下，才能采取先予执行的措施。 18、人民法院采取先予执行措施后，申请先予执行的当事人申请撤诉的，人民法院应当及时通知对方当事人、第三人或有关的案外人。在接到通知至准予撤诉的裁定送达前，对方当事人、第三人及有关的案外人，对撤诉提出异议的，应当裁定驳回撤诉申请。 19、受诉人民法院院长或者上级人民法院发现采取财产保全或者先予执行措施确有错误的，应当按照审判监督程序立即纠正。因申请错误造成被申请人损失的，由申请人予以赔偿；因人民法院依职权采取保全措施错误造成损失的，由人民法院依法予以赔偿。

### 正文

二、关于无独立请求权的第三人  
9、受诉人民法院对与原被告双方争议的诉讼标的无直接牵连和不负有返还或者赔偿等义务的人，以及与原告或被告约定仲裁或有约定管辖的案外人，或者专属管辖案件的一方当事人，均不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。  
10、人民法院在审理产品质量纠纷案件中，对原被告之间法律关系以外的人，证据已证明其已经提供了合同约定或者符合法律规定的产品的，或者案件中的当事人未在规定的质量异议期内提出异议的，或者作为收货方已经认可该产品质量的，不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。  
11、人民法院对已经履行了义务，或者依法取得了一方当事人的财产，并支付了相应对价的原被告之间法律关系以外的人，不得作为无独立请求权的第三人通知其参加诉讼。

**Article None** II. Third Party Without Independent Claims  
9. A People's Court shall not notify any person who has no direct connection with the subject matter of the dispute between the plaintiff and the defendant and who is not obligated to make restitution or pay damages, nor any person outside the case who has agreed with the plaintiff or defendant to arbitrate or has agreed on jurisdiction, nor any party to a case under exclusive jurisdiction, to participate in the proceedings as a third party without independent claims.  
10. When hearing a case involving a product quality dispute, a People's Court shall not notify any person outside the legal relationship between the plaintiff and defendant to participate in the proceedings as a third party without independent claims if the evidence has proved that such person has already provided a product that conforms to the contract or meets legal requirements, or if the party in the case has not raised any objection within the prescribed quality objection period, or if the consignee has already accepted the product quality.  
11. A People's Court shall not notify any person outside the legal relationship between the plaintiff and defendant who has already performed the obligations, or has lawfully acquired property from one party and paid the corresponding consideration, to participate in the proceedings as a third party without independent claims.

三、关于财产保全和先予执行  
12、人民法院采取诉前财产保全，必须由申请人提供相当于请求保全数额的担保。担保的条件，依法律规定；法律未作规定的，由人民法院审查决定。  
13、人民法院对财产采取诉讼保全措施，一般应当由当事人提交符合法定条件的申请。只有在诉讼争议的财产有毁损、灭失等危险，或者有证据表明被申请人可能采取隐匿、转移、出卖其财产的，人民法院方可依职权裁定采取财产保全措施。  
14、人民法院采取财产保全措施时，保全的范围应当限于当事人争议的财产，或者被告的财产。对案外人的财产不得采取保全措施，对案外人善意取得的与案件有关的财产，一般也不得采取财产保全措施；被申请人提供相应数额并有可供执行的财产作担保的，采取措施的人民法院应当及时解除财产保全。  
15、人民法院对有偿还能力的企业法人，一般不得采取查封、冻结的保全措施。已采取查封、冻结保全措施的，如该企业法人提供了可供执行的财产担保，或者可以采取其他方式保全的，应当及时予以解封、解冻。  
16、人民法院先予执行的裁定，应当由当事人提出书面申请，并经开庭审理后作出。在管辖权尚未确定的情况下，不得裁定先予执行。  
17、人民法院对当事人申请先予执行的案件，只有在案件的基本事实清楚，当事人间的权利义务关系明确，被申请人负有给付、返还或者赔偿义务，先予执行的财产为申请人生产、生活所急需，不先予执行会造成更大损失的情况下，才能采取先予执行的措施。  
18、人民法院采取先予执行措施后，申请先予执行的当事人申请撤诉的，人民法院应当及时通知对方当事人、第三人或有关的案外人。在接到通知至准予撤诉的裁定送达前，对方当事人、第三人及有关的案外人，对撤诉提出异议的，应当裁定驳回撤诉申请。  
19、受诉人民法院院长或者上级人民法院发现采取财产保全或者先予执行措施确有错误的，应当按照审判监督程序立即纠正。因申请错误造成被申请人损失的，由申请人予以赔偿；因人民法院依职权采取保全措施错误造成损失的，由人民法院依法予以赔偿。

**Article None** III. Property Preservation and Advance Execution  
12. To adopt pre-litigation property preservation, a People's Court must require the applicant to provide security equivalent to the amount for which preservation is requested. The conditions for security shall be as provided by law; if not provided by law, the People's Court shall examine and decide.  
13. When adopting litigation preservation measures against property, a People's Court shall generally require a party to submit an application that meets the statutory conditions. Only when the property subject to the dispute is in danger of damage, loss, or the like, or there is evidence that the respondent may conceal, transfer, or sell the property, may the People's Court, ex officio, rule to adopt property preservation measures.  
14. When adopting property preservation measures, the scope of preservation shall be limited to the property in dispute between the parties or the property of the defendant. Preservation measures shall not be adopted against the property of a person outside the case, and generally shall not be adopted against property acquired by a person outside the case in good faith and related to the case. If the respondent provides security of a corresponding amount with property subject to enforcement, the People's Court that adopted the measures shall promptly lift the property preservation.  
15. A People's Court shall generally not adopt preservation measures such as seizure or freezing against an enterprise legal person with capacity to repay. If such preservation measures have already been adopted, and the enterprise legal person provides property security subject to enforcement, or if other methods of preservation may be adopted, the Court shall promptly lift the seizure or freeze.  
16. A ruling on advance execution by a People's Court shall be made upon a written application by a party and after a court hearing. Where jurisdiction has not yet been determined, no ruling on advance execution shall be made.  
17. A People's Court may adopt advance execution measures only when the basic facts of the case are clear, the rights and obligations between the parties are definite, the respondent has an obligation to make payment, restitution, or compensation, the property subject to advance execution is urgently needed for the applicant's production or livelihood, and failure to grant advance execution would cause greater losses.  
18. After a People's Court adopts advance execution measures, if the party applying for advance execution requests withdrawal of the lawsuit, the People's Court shall promptly notify the other party, third party, or relevant person outside the case. If, between the time of receiving the notice and the service of the ruling granting withdrawal, the other party, third party, or relevant person outside the case raises an objection to the withdrawal, the Court shall rule to dismiss the withdrawal application.  
19. If the president of the People's Court hearing the case or a higher People's Court finds that the adoption of property preservation or advance execution measures is indeed erroneous, it shall promptly correct the error in accordance with the adjudication supervision procedure. If losses are caused to the respondent due to the applicant's error, the applicant shall compensate; if losses are caused due to the People's Court's ex officio adoption of erroneous preservation measures, the People's Court shall compensate according to law.

四、关于办案期限  
20、上级人民法院决定调卷审查的案件，下级人民法院应当在接到上级人民法院调卷函后的十五日内将全部案卷报送上级人民法院。如有特殊情况不能按期报送的，应当及时报告上级人民法院。  
21、上级人民法院发函要求下级人民法院对已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定进行审查的，下级人民法院应当在收函之日起三个月内向上级人民法院报送审查结果或者审查情况。  
22、上级人民法院必要时可以在调卷函或要求审查的函件中提出暂缓执行的意见，有关人民法院对此如有异议，应当及时报告上级人民法院。上级人民法院接到下级人民法院审查报告后，应当在一个月内作出调卷决定或者通知恢复执行。上级人民法院决定调卷的案件，应当在收到案卷后三个月内向下级人民法院发出中止执行的裁定或恢复执行的通知。  
23、最高人民法院对地方各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，按照审判监督程序，决定提审或者指令再审的，提审或者再审的人民法院除有特殊情况外，适用第一审程序的，应当在六个月内结案；适用第二审程序的，应当在三个月内结案。决定提审的，办案期限自裁定提审的次日起计算。指令再审的，自下级人民法院接到指令再审的裁定的次日起计算。上级人民法院指令再审的案件，下级人民法院在该案审结后，应当将裁判结果报上级人民法院。

**Article None** IV. Time Limits for Handling Cases  
20. Where a higher People's Court decides to transfer the case files for review, the lower People's Court shall, within fifteen days of receiving the letter requesting the transfer of files, submit all case files to the higher People's Court. If special circumstances prevent timely submission, it shall promptly report to the higher People's Court.  
21. Where a higher People's Court sends a letter requesting a lower People's Court to review a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect, the lower People's Court shall, within three months of receiving the letter, submit the review results or review status to the higher People's Court.  
22. Where necessary, a higher People's Court may propose a stay of enforcement in the letter requesting the transfer of files or the letter requesting review. If the relevant People's Court has an objection, it shall promptly report to the higher People's Court. After receiving the review report from the lower People's Court, the higher People's Court shall, within one month, decide to transfer the files or notify the resumption of enforcement. For cases in which the higher People's Court decides to transfer the files, it shall, within three months of receiving the case files, issue a ruling to suspend enforcement or a notice to resume enforcement to the lower People's Court.  
23. Where the Supreme People's Court decides to bring up for trial or to remand for retrial, in accordance with the adjudication supervision procedure, a judgment or ruling of a local People's Court at any level that has already taken legal effect, or where a higher People's Court so decides with respect to a lower People's Court, the People's Court conducting the trial or retrial shall, except in special circumstances, conclude the case within six months if the first instance procedure applies, or within three months if the second instance procedure applies. Where a decision is made to bring up for trial, the time limit for handling the case shall be counted from the day following the date of the ruling to bring up for trial. Where a retrial is directed, the time limit shall be counted from the day following the date on which the lower People's Court receives the ruling directing the retrial. For a case in which a higher People's Court directs a retrial, the lower People's Court shall, after concluding the case, report the adjudication result to the higher People's Court.
