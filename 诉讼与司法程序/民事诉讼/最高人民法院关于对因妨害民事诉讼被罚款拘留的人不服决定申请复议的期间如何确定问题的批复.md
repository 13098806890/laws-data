### 最高人民法院关于对因妨害民事诉讼被罚款拘留的人不服决定申请复议的期间如何确定问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对因妨害民事诉讼被罚款拘留的人  
  
不服决定申请复议的期间如何确定问题的批复  
  
（93）法民字第7号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于对因妨害民事诉讼被罚款拘留的人不服决定申请复议的期间如何确定问题的请示》收悉。经研究，同意你院意见，即不服人民法院作出的罚款、拘留决定的人，可在接到决定书之次日起三日内，向作出决定的人民法院提出书面申请，要求上一级人民法院复议，或直接向上一级人民法院申请复议。对提出书面申请有困难的，可以口头申请。当事人的口头申请，应当记入笔录，由当事人签名或者盖章。  
  
1993年2月23日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Regarding Persons Fined or Detained for Obstructing a Civil Action  
Who Are Dissatisfied with the Decision and Apply for Review, the Reply on How to Determine the Period  
(93) Fa Min Zi No. 7  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your court's Request for Instructions on the Determination of the Period for Applying for Review by Persons Fined or Detained for Obstructing a Civil Action Who Are Dissatisfied with the Decision has been received. After study, we agree with your opinion that a person who is dissatisfied with the decision of a People's Court to impose a fine or detention may, within three days from the day following receipt of the decision, submit a written application to the People's Court that made the decision, requesting a review by the People's Court at the next higher level, or directly apply to the People's Court at the next higher level for review. If a party has difficulty in submitting a written application, he may make an oral application. The oral application of the party shall be recorded in the transcript and signed or sealed by the party.  
February 23, 1993
