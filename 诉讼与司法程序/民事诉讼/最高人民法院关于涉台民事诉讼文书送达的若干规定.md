# 最高人民法院关于涉台民事诉讼文书送达的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕4号  
**公布日期**：2008-04-23  
**生效日期**：2008-04-23

> 最高人民法院《关于涉台民事诉讼文书送达的若干规定》已由最高人民法院审判委员会第1421次会议通过。现予公布，自2008年4月23日起施行。 2008年4月17日 最高人民法院 关于涉台民事诉讼文书送达的若干规定 法释〔2008〕4号 为维护涉台民事案件当事人的合法权益，保障涉台民事案件诉讼活动的顺利进行，促进海峡两岸人员往来和交流，根据民事诉讼法的有关规定，制定本规定。 第一条  人民法院审理涉台民事案件向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，以及人民法院接受台湾地区有关法院的委托代为向住所地在大陆的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，适用本规定。 涉台民事诉讼文书送达事务的处理，应当遵守一个中国原则和法律的基本原则，不违反社会公共利益。 第二条  人民法院送达或者代为送达的民事诉讼文书包括：起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、授权委托书、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证以及与民事诉讼有关的其他文书。 第三条  人民法院向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，可以采用下列方式： （一）受送达人居住在大陆的，直接送达。受送达人是自然人，本人不在的，可以交其同住成年家属签收；受送达人是法人或者其他组织的，应当由法人的法定代表人、其他组织的主要负责人或者该法人、组织负责收件的人签收； 受送达人不在大陆居住，但送达时在大陆的，可以直接送达； （二）受送达人在大陆有诉讼代理人的，向诉讼代理人送达。受送达人在授权委托书中明确表明其诉讼代理人无权代为接收的除外； （三）受送达人有指定代收人的，向代收人送达； （四）受送达人在大陆有代表机构、分支机构、业务代办人的，向其代表机构或者经受送达人明确授权接受送达的分支机构、业务代办人送达；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院审理涉台民事案件向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，以及人民法院接受台湾地区有关法院的委托代为向住所地在大陆的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，适用本规定。  
涉台民事诉讼文书送达事务的处理，应当遵守一个中国原则和法律的基本原则，不违反社会公共利益。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to the service of civil action documents by a People's Court on a party whose domicile is in the Taiwan region in the trial of civil actions involving Taiwan, and to the service of civil action documents by a People's Court on behalf of a relevant court in the Taiwan region on a party whose domicile is in the Mainland.  
The handling of service of civil action documents involving Taiwan shall comply with the One-China principle and the basic principles of law, and shall not violate social public interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  人民法院送达或者代为送达的民事诉讼文书包括：起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、授权委托书、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证以及与民事诉讼有关的其他文书。

**Article 2** The civil action documents served or served on behalf of the People's Court include: copies of the statement of prosecution, copies of the statement of appeal, copies of the statement of counterclaim, copies of the statement of defense, power of attorney, summons, written judgment, written mediation, written ruling, payment order, written decision, notice, certificate, service certificate, and other documents related to civil action.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院向住所地在台湾地区的当事人送达民事诉讼文书，可以采用下列方式：  
（一）受送达人居住在大陆的，直接送达。受送达人是自然人，本人不在的，可以交其同住成年家属签收；受送达人是法人或者其他组织的，应当由法人的法定代表人、其他组织的主要负责人或者该法人、组织负责收件的人签收；  
受送达人不在大陆居住，但送达时在大陆的，可以直接送达；  
（二）受送达人在大陆有诉讼代理人的，向诉讼代理人送达。受送达人在授权委托书中明确表明其诉讼代理人无权代为接收的除外；  
（三）受送达人有指定代收人的，向代收人送达；  
（四）受送达人在大陆有代表机构、分支机构、业务代办人的，向其代表机构或者经受送达人明确授权接受送达的分支机构、业务代办人送达；  
（五）受送达人在台湾地区的地址明确的，可以邮寄送达；  
（六）有明确的传真号码、电子信箱地址的，可以通过传真、电子邮件方式向受送达人送达；  
（七）按照两岸认可的其他途径送达。  
采用上述方式不能送达或者台湾地区的当事人下落不明的，公告送达。

**Article 3** A People's Court may serve civil action documents on a party whose domicile is in the Taiwan region by the following methods:  
(1) If the person on whom the document is to be served resides in the Mainland, service shall be made directly. If the person on whom the document is to be served is a natural person and is not present, the document may be signed for by an adult family member living with him; if the person on whom the document is to be served is a legal person or any other organization, it shall be signed for by the legal representative of the legal person, the principal person in charge of the other organization, or the person responsible for receiving documents of the legal person or organization;  
If the person on whom the document is to be served does not reside in the Mainland but is in the Mainland at the time of service, service may be made directly;  
(2) If the person on whom the document is to be served has a litigation agent in the Mainland, service may be made on the litigation agent, except where the person on whom the document is to be served expressly indicates in the power of attorney that the litigation agent is not authorized to receive service on his behalf;  
(3) If the person on whom the document is to be served has a designated consignee, service may be made on the consignee;  
(4) If the person on whom the document is to be served has a representative office, branch, or business agent in the Mainland, service may be made on such representative office, or on the branch or business agent expressly authorized by the person on whom the document is to be served to accept service;  
(5) If the address of the person on whom the document is to be served in the Taiwan region is clear, service may be made by mail;  
(6) If there is a clear fax number or email address, service may be made on the person on whom the document is to be served by fax or email;  
(7) Service may be made by other means recognized by both sides.  
If service cannot be made by the above methods or if the party in the Taiwan region is not to be found, service shall be made by public announcement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  采用[本规定第三条](#art-3)第一款第（一）、（二）、（三）、（四）项方式送达的，由受送达人、诉讼代理人或者有权接受送达的人在送达回证上签收或者盖章，即为送达；拒绝签收或者盖章的，可以依法留置送达。

**Article 4** Where service is made by the methods specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), and (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of these Provisions, the person on whom the document is to be served, the litigation agent, or the person authorized to accept service shall sign or affix his seal on the service certificate, which shall be deemed service; if the person refuses to sign or affix his seal, service may be made by leaving the document at the place of service according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  采用[本规定第三条](#art-3)第一款第（五）项方式送达的，应当附有送达回证。受送达人未在送达回证上签收但在邮件回执上签收的，视为送达，签收日期为送达日期。  
自邮寄之日起满三个月，如果未能收到送达与否的证明文件，且根据各种情况不足以认定已经送达的，视为未送达。

**Article 5** Where service is made by the method specified in subparagraph (5) of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of these Provisions, a service certificate shall be attached. If the person on whom the document is to be served does not sign the service certificate but signs the mail receipt, it shall be deemed service, and the date of signing shall be the date of service.  
If, three months after the date of mailing, no proof of service or non-service has been received and it cannot be determined that service has been made based on the circumstances, it shall be deemed that service has not been made.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  采用[本规定第三条](#art-3)第一款第（六）项方式送达的，应当注明人民法院的传真号码或者电子信箱地址，并要求受送达人在收到传真件或者电子邮件后及时予以回复。以能够确认受送达人收悉的日期为送达日期。

**Article 6** Where service is made by the method specified in subparagraph (6) of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of these Provisions, the People's Court's fax number or email address shall be indicated, and the person on whom the document is to be served shall be requested to reply promptly upon receipt of the fax or email. The date on which receipt by the person on whom the document is to be served can be confirmed shall be the date of service.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  采用[本规定第三条](#art-3)第一款第（七）项方式送达的，应当由有关的高级人民法院出具盖有本院印章的委托函。委托函应当写明案件各方当事人的姓名或者名称、案由、案号；受送达人姓名或者名称、受送达人的详细地址以及需送达的文书种类。

**Article 7** Where service is made by the method specified in subparagraph (7) of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of these Provisions, the relevant Higher People's Court shall issue a letter of entrustment affixed with the seal of that court. The letter of entrustment shall set forth the names of all parties to the case, the cause of action, and the case number; the name of the person on whom the document is to be served, the detailed address of the person on whom the document is to be served, and the types of documents to be served.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  采用公告方式送达的，公告内容应当在境内外公开发行的报刊或者权威网站上刊登。  
公告送达的，自公告之日起满三个月，即视为送达。

**Article 8** Where service is made by public announcement, the content of the announcement shall be published in newspapers distributed both domestically and abroad or on authoritative websites.  
For service by public announcement, upon the expiration of three months from the date of announcement, it shall be deemed service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院按照两岸认可的有关途径代为送达台湾地区法院的民事诉讼文书的，应当有台湾地区有关法院的委托函。  
人民法院收到台湾地区有关法院的委托函后，经审查符合条件的，应当在收到委托函之日起两个月内完成送达。  
民事诉讼文书中确定的出庭日期或者其他期限逾期的，受委托的人民法院亦应予送达。

**Article 9** Where a People's Court serves civil action documents of a court in the Taiwan region on its behalf through relevant channels recognized by both sides, there shall be a letter of entrustment from the relevant court in the Taiwan region.  
After receiving the letter of entrustment from the relevant court in the Taiwan region, and upon examination if it meets the requirements, the People's Court shall complete service within two months from the date of receipt of the letter of entrustment.  
If the date of appearance or other time limit determined in the civil action documents has expired, the entrusted People's Court shall still serve the documents.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民法院按照委托函中的受送达人姓名或者名称、地址不能送达的，应当附函写明情况，将委托送达的民事诉讼文书退回。  
完成送达的送达回证以及未完成送达的委托材料，可以按照原途径退回。

**Article 10** If the People's Court cannot serve the documents in accordance with the name and address of the person on whom the document is to be served in the letter of entrustment, it shall attach a letter explaining the circumstances and return the civil action documents entrusted for service.  
The service certificate for completed service and the entrusted materials for uncompleted service may be returned through the original channels.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  受委托的人民法院对台湾地区有关法院委托送达的民事诉讼文书的内容和后果不负法律责任。

**Article 11** The entrusted People's Court shall not bear legal liability for the content and consequences of the civil action documents entrusted for service by the relevant court in the Taiwan region.
