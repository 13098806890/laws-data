### 最高人民法院关于如何理解《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第31条第2款的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于如何理解《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事  
  
诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第31条第2款的批复  
  
法释〔1998〕5号  
  
（1998年4月2日最高人民法院审判委员会第 970次会议通过，自1998年4月25日起施行）  
  
山西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院晋高法〔1996〕148号《关于对最高人民法院〈关于适用中华人民共和国民事诉讼法若干问题的意见〉第31条第2款如何理解的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第31条第2款的规定是指：在人民法院采取诉前财产保全后，申请人起诉的，应当向有管辖权的人民法院提起。采取诉前财产保全的人民法院对该案有管辖权的，应当依法受理；没有管辖权的，应当及时将采取诉前财产保全的全部材料移送有管辖权的受诉人民法院。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Shanxi Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on How to Understand Paragraph 2 of Article 31 of the Supreme People's Court's Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (Jin Gao Fa [1996] No. 148). Upon review, our reply is as follows:  
The provision in Paragraph 2 of Article 31 of the Supreme People's Court's Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means: after a People's Court has taken property preservation before prosecution, if the applicant files a prosecution, it shall be filed with the People's Court that has jurisdiction. If the People's Court that took the pre-litigation property preservation has jurisdiction over the case, it shall accept the case according to law; if it does not have jurisdiction, it shall promptly transfer all materials related to the pre-litigation property preservation to the competent People's Court that is to hear the case.  
This is our reply.
