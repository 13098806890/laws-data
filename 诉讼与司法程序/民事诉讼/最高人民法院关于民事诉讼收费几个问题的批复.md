# 最高人民法院关于民事诉讼收费几个问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1986〕22号

---

### 正文

一、关于第二审人民法院审理上诉案件，在进行调解时，第一审的诉讼费如何负担的问题。根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第一百五十三条的规定，第二审人民法院审理上诉案件，经过调解达成协议的，第一审人民法院的判决即视为撤销，其中关于诉讼费的负担部分也应视为撤销。因此，在第二审调解过程中，对第一审和第二审的诉讼费的负担，应由双方当事人协商，达成协议的应在调解书中写明。

**Article None** 1. Regarding the issue of how the litigation fees of the first instance shall be borne when the People's Court of second instance conducts mediation in hearing an appeal case. According to Article 153 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation), where the People's Court of second instance hears an appeal case and reaches an agreement through mediation, the judgment of the People's Court of first instance shall be deemed revoked, and the part concerning the bearing of litigation fees shall also be deemed revoked. Therefore, during the mediation process of the second instance, the bearing of litigation fees for the first and second instances shall be negotiated by both parties, and if an agreement is reached, it shall be specified in the mediation document.

二、关于两个以上诉讼标的的案件如何收取案件费的问题。原告在同一案件中提出了两个以上诉讼请求，人民法院在受理后，要对不同的诉讼请求分别进行审理。而且，对其中一个诉讼请求审理的结果，并不影响对其它诉讼请求的审理，所以，在处理这类案件时，应按不同的诉讼标的分别计算，合并收取受理费。

**Article None** 2. Regarding the issue of how to collect case fees in cases with more than two subject matters of action. The plaintiff has raised more than two claims in the same case. After accepting the case, the People's Court shall adjudicate the different claims separately. Moreover, the outcome of adjudication of one claim shall not affect the adjudication of other claims. Therefore, when handling such cases, the acceptance fees shall be calculated separately according to different subject matters of action and collected in aggregate.

三、关于第二审人民法院对第一审人民法院收取诉讼费有误应否纠正的问题。第二审人民法院在审理上诉案件时，发现第一审人民法院对案件实体部分的处理正确，但收取诉讼费有误，应当在终审判决中予以纠正。  
此复  
一九八六年六月二十一日

**Article None** 3. Regarding the issue of whether the People's Court of second instance shall rectify the error in the collection of litigation fees by the People's Court of first instance. When hearing an appeal case, if the People's Court of second instance finds that the People's Court of first instance correctly handled the substantive part of the case but erroneously collected litigation fees, it shall make corrections in the final judgment.
