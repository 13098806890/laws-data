### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为维护海事诉讼当事人的诉讼权利，保证人民法院查明事实，分清责任，正确适用法律，及时审理海事案件，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of safeguarding the litigation rights of the parties to maritime proceedings, ensuring that the People's Courts ascertain the facts, distinguish responsibilities, correctly apply the law, and timely adjudicate maritime cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内进行海事诉讼，适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和本法。本法有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 2** Maritime proceedings conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and this Law. Where this Law provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约与《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和本法对涉外海事诉讼有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定，但中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 3** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and this Law with respect to foreign-related maritime proceedings, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply, except those articles to which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海事法院受理当事人因海事侵权纠纷、海商合同纠纷以及法律规定的其他海事纠纷提起的诉讼。

**Article 4** The maritime courts shall accept actions instituted by parties arising from maritime tort disputes, maritime commercial contract disputes, and other maritime disputes as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海事法院及其所在地的高级人民法院和最高人民法院审理海事案件的，适用本法。

**Article 5** Where the maritime courts, the Higher People's Courts located in the same places as the maritime courts, and the Supreme People's Court adjudicate maritime cases, this Law shall apply.

### 第二章　管辖

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海事诉讼的地域管辖，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定。  
下列海事诉讼的地域管辖，依照以下规定：  
（一）因海事侵权行为提起的诉讼，除依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-29)至第三十一条的规定以外，还可以由船籍港所在地海事法院管辖；  
（二）因海上运输合同纠纷提起的诉讼，除依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-28)的规定以外，还可以由转运港所在地海事法院管辖；  
（三）因海船租用合同纠纷提起的诉讼，由交船港、还船港、船籍港所在地、被告住所地海事法院管辖；  
（四）因海上保赔合同纠纷提起的诉讼，由保赔标的物所在地、事故发生地、被告住所地海事法院管辖；  
（五）因海船的船员劳务合同纠纷提起的诉讼，由原告住所地、合同签订地、船员登船港或者离船港所在地、被告住所地海事法院管辖；  
（六）因海事担保纠纷提起的诉讼，由担保物所在地、被告住所地海事法院管辖；因船舶抵押纠纷提起的诉讼，还可以由船籍港所在地海事法院管辖；  
（七）因海船的船舶所有权、占有权、使用权、优先权纠纷提起的诉讼，由船舶所在地、船籍港所在地、被告住所地海事法院管辖。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第3条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第5条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第6条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第7条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 6** The territorial jurisdiction over maritime proceedings shall be subject to the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The territorial jurisdiction over the following maritime proceedings shall be determined in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) An action instituted due to a maritime tort may, in addition to being under the jurisdiction of a maritime court in accordance with Articles 29 to 31 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, also be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the ship's port of registry;  
(2) An action instituted due to a dispute over a maritime transport contract may, in addition to being under the jurisdiction of a maritime court in accordance with Article 28 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, also be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the port of transshipment;  
(3) An action instituted due to a dispute over a charter party for a seagoing ship shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the port of delivery, the port of redelivery, the port of registry, or the domicile of the defendant;  
(4) An action instituted due to a dispute over a marine protection and indemnity contract shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the subject matter of the protection and indemnity, the place where the accident occurred, or the domicile of the defendant;  
(5) An action instituted due to a dispute over a crew member's labor service contract for a seagoing ship shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the domicile of the plaintiff, the place where the contract was concluded, the place of the port of embarkation or disembarkation of the crew member, or the domicile of the defendant;  
(6) An action instituted due to a dispute over maritime security shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place where the security is situated or the domicile of the defendant; an action instituted due to a dispute over a ship mortgage may also be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the ship's port of registry;  
(7) An action instituted due to a dispute over the ownership, right of possession, right of use, or maritime lien of a seagoing ship shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place where the ship is located, the place of the ship's port of registry, or the domicile of the defendant.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列海事诉讼，由本条规定的海事法院专属管辖：  
（一）因沿海港口作业纠纷提起的诉讼，由港口所在地海事法院管辖；  
（二）因船舶排放、泄漏、倾倒油类或者其他有害物质，海上生产、作业或者拆船、修船作业造成海域污染损害提起的诉讼，由污染发生地、损害结果地或者采取预防污染措施地海事法院管辖；  
（三）因在中华人民共和国领域和有管辖权的海域履行的海洋勘探开发合同纠纷提起的诉讼，由合同履行地海事法院管辖。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-12" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第12条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 7** The following maritime proceedings shall be under the exclusive jurisdiction of the maritime courts as specified in this Article:  
(1) An action instituted due to a dispute over operations in a coastal port shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of the port;  
(2) An action instituted due to pollution damage caused to sea areas by the discharge, leakage, or dumping of oil or other harmful substances from a ship, or by maritime production, operations, or ship-breaking or ship-repairing operations, shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place where the pollution occurred, the place where the damage resulted, or the place where measures were taken to prevent pollution;  
(3) An action instituted due to a dispute over an ocean exploration and development contract performed within the territory of the People's Republic of China or within the sea areas under its jurisdiction shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court located at the place of performance of the contract.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海事纠纷的当事人都是外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织，当事人书面协议选择中华人民共和国海事法院管辖的，即使与纠纷有实际联系的地点不在中华人民共和国领域内，中华人民共和国海事法院对该纠纷也具有管辖权。

**Article 8** Where all parties to a maritime dispute are foreigners, stateless persons, foreign enterprises, or organizations, and the parties have agreed in writing to submit to the jurisdiction of a maritime court of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court of the People's Republic of China shall have jurisdiction over the dispute even if the place actually connected with the dispute is not within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人申请认定海上财产无主的，向财产所在地海事法院提出；申请因海上事故宣告死亡的，向处理海事事故主管机关所在地或者受理相关海事案件的海事法院提出。

**Article 9** Where a party applies for a declaration that maritime property is ownerless, the application shall be filed with the maritime court located at the place where the property is situated; where a party applies for a declaration of death due to a maritime accident, the application shall be filed with the maritime court located at the place where the competent authority handling the maritime accident is situated or with the maritime court that has accepted the relevant maritime case.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海事法院与地方人民法院之间因管辖权发生争议，由争议双方协商解决；协商解决不了的，报请他们的共同上级人民法院指定管辖。

**Article 10** Where a dispute over jurisdiction arises between a maritime court and a local People's Court, the two parties to the dispute shall resolve it through consultation; if consultation fails, the matter shall be referred to their common superior People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人申请执行海事仲裁裁决，申请承认和执行外国法院判决、裁定以及国外海事仲裁裁决的，向被执行的财产所在地或者被执行人住所地海事法院提出。被执行的财产所在地或者被执行人住所地没有海事法院的，向被执行的财产所在地或者被执行人住所地的中级人民法院提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** Where a party applies for enforcement of a maritime arbitral award, or for recognition and enforcement of a judgment or ruling of a foreign court or a foreign maritime arbitral award, the application shall be filed with the maritime court located at the place where the property to be enforced is situated or where the person against whom enforcement is sought has his domicile. If there is no maritime court at the place where the property to be enforced is situated or where the person against whom enforcement is sought has his domicile, the application shall be filed with the Intermediate People's Court at such place.

### 第三章　海事请求保全

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海事请求保全是指海事法院根据海事请求人的申请，为保障其海事请求的实现，对被请求人的财产所采取的强制措施。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** Maritime claim preservation refers to the compulsory measures taken by a maritime court against the property of the person against whom a claim is made, upon the application of the maritime claimant, to secure the realization of the maritime claim.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人在起诉前申请海事请求保全，应当向被保全的财产所在地海事法院提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 13** A party applying for maritime claim preservation before prosecution shall submit the application to the maritime court of the place where the property to be preserved is located.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海事请求保全不受当事人之间关于该海事请求的诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的约束。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 14** Maritime claim preservation shall not be subject to any agreement on litigation jurisdiction or arbitration agreement between the parties concerning the maritime claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海事请求人申请海事请求保全，应当向海事法院提交书面申请。申请书应当载明海事请求事项、申请理由、保全的标的物以及要求提供担保的数额，并附有关证据。

**Article 15** A maritime claimant applying for maritime claim preservation shall submit a written application to the maritime court. The application shall state the subject matter of the maritime claim, the grounds for the application, the object of preservation and the amount of security required, and shall be accompanied by relevant evidence.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　海事法院受理海事请求保全申请，可以责令海事请求人提供担保。海事请求人不提供的，驳回其申请。

**Article 16** Upon accepting an application for maritime claim preservation, a maritime court may order the maritime claimant to provide security. If the maritime claimant fails to provide security, the court shall reject the application.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海事法院接受申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出裁定。裁定采取海事请求保全措施的，应当立即执行；对不符合海事请求保全条件的，裁定驳回其申请。  
当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起五日内申请复议一次。海事法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出复议决定。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。  
利害关系人对海事请求保全提出异议，海事法院经审查，认为理由成立的，应当解除对其财产的保全。

**Article 17** After accepting an application, a maritime court shall make a ruling within forty-eight hours. If the ruling grants the maritime claim preservation measures, it shall be executed immediately; if the conditions for maritime claim preservation are not met, the court shall rule to reject the application.  
A party dissatisfied with the ruling may apply for one reconsideration within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within five days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, execution of the ruling shall not be suspended.  
If an interested person raises an objection to the maritime claim preservation, and the maritime court, upon examination, finds the reasons to be well-founded, it shall release the preservation of its property.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被请求人提供担保，或者当事人有正当理由申请解除海事请求保全的，海事法院应当及时解除保全。  
海事请求人在本法规定的期间内，未提起诉讼或者未按照仲裁协议申请仲裁的，海事法院应当及时解除保全或者返还担保。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-27" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** If the person against whom a claim is made provides security, or a party applies for release of the maritime claim preservation on justifiable grounds, the maritime court shall promptly release the preservation.  
If the maritime claimant fails to institute an action or apply for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration agreement within the time limit prescribed by this Law, the maritime court shall promptly release the preservation or return the security.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海事请求保全执行后，有关海事纠纷未进入诉讼或者仲裁程序的，当事人就该海事请求，可以向采取海事请求保全的海事法院或者其他有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼，但当事人之间订有诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的除外。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 19** After the execution of the maritime claim preservation, if the relevant maritime dispute has not entered into litigation or arbitration proceedings, a party may, in respect of the maritime claim, institute an action in the maritime court that took the maritime claim preservation or another maritime court with jurisdiction, unless there is an agreement on litigation jurisdiction or an arbitration agreement between the parties.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海事请求人申请海事请求保全错误的，应当赔偿被请求人或者利害关系人因此所遭受的损失。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 20** If a maritime claimant applies for maritime claim preservation erroneously, it shall compensate the person against whom the claim is made or the interested person for the losses sustained thereby.

#### 第二节　船舶的扣押与拍卖

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　下列海事请求，可以申请扣押船舶：  
（一）船舶营运造成的财产灭失或者损坏；  
（二）与船舶营运直接有关的人身伤亡；  
（三）海难救助；  
（四）船舶对环境、海岸或者有关利益方造成的损害或者损害威胁；为预防、减少或者消除此种损害而采取的措施；为此种损害而支付的赔偿；为恢复环境而实际采取或者准备采取的合理措施的费用；第三方因此种损害而蒙受或者可能蒙受的损失；以及与本项所指的性质类似的损害、费用或者损失；  
（五）与起浮、清除、回收或者摧毁沉船、残骸、搁浅船、被弃船或者使其无害有关的费用，包括与起浮、清除、回收或者摧毁仍在或者曾在该船上的物件或者使其无害的费用，以及与维护放弃的船舶和维持其船员有关的费用；  
（六）船舶的使用或者租用的协议；  
（七）货物运输或者旅客运输的协议；  
（八）船载货物（包括行李）或者与其有关的灭失或者损坏；  
（九）共同海损；  
（十）拖航；  
（十一）引航；  
（十二）为船舶营运、管理、维护、维修提供物资或者服务；  
（十三）船舶的建造、改建、修理、改装或者装备；  
（十四）港口、运河、码头、港湾以及其他水道规费和费用；  
（十五）船员的工资和其他款项，包括应当为船员支付的遣返费和社会保险费；  
（十六）为船舶或者船舶所有人支付的费用；  
（十七）船舶所有人或者光船承租人应当支付或者他人为其支付的船舶保险费（包括互保会费）；  
（十八）船舶所有人或者光船承租人应当支付的或者他人为其支付的与船舶有关的佣金、经纪费或者代理费；  
（十九）有关船舶所有权或者占有的纠纷；  
（二十）船舶共有人之间有关船舶的使用或者收益的纠纷；  
（二十一）船舶抵押权或者同样性质的权利；  
（二十二）因船舶买卖合同产生的纠纷。

**Article 21** The following maritime claims may give rise to an application for the arrest of a ship:  
(1) loss or damage caused by the operation of the ship;  
(2) loss of life or personal injury occurring in direct connection with the operation of the ship;  
(3) salvage operations;  
(4) damage or threat of damage caused by the ship to the environment, the coastline or related interests; measures taken to prevent, minimize or remove such damage; compensation for such damage; costs of reasonable measures actually taken or to be taken to restore the environment; loss or likely loss suffered by third parties as a result of such damage; and damage, costs or loss of a similar nature to those referred to in this subparagraph;  
(5) costs relating to the raising, removal, recovery or destruction of a ship which is sunk, wrecked, stranded or abandoned, including anything that is or has been on board such ship, and costs relating to the maintenance of an abandoned ship and the maintenance of its crew;  
(6) any agreement relating to the use or hire of the ship;  
(7) any agreement relating to the carriage of goods or passengers;  
(8) loss or damage to or in connection with goods (including luggage) carried on board the ship;  
(9) general average;  
(10) towage;  
(11) pilotage;  
(12) supplies or services rendered for the operation, management, maintenance or repair of the ship;  
(13) the construction, reconstruction, repair, conversion or equipping of the ship;  
(14) port, canal, dock, harbor and other waterway dues and charges;  
(15) wages and other sums due to the master and crew, including repatriation costs and social insurance contributions payable on their behalf;  
(16) disbursements incurred on behalf of the ship or its owner;  
(17) insurance premiums (including mutual insurance calls) in respect of the ship, payable by or on behalf of the shipowner or bareboat charterer;  
(18) commissions, brokerages or agency fees in respect of the ship, payable by or on behalf of the shipowner or bareboat charterer;  
(19) any dispute relating to the ownership or possession of the ship;  
(20) any dispute between co-owners of the ship concerning the use or earnings of the ship;  
(21) a mortgage or a charge of a similar nature on the ship;  
(22) any dispute arising out of a contract for the sale of the ship.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　非因[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定的海事请求不得申请扣押船舶，但为执行判决、仲裁裁决以及其他法律文书的除外。

**Article 22** No ship may be arrested for a maritime claim other than those specified in Article 21 of this Law, except for the purpose of enforcing a judgment, an arbitral award or other legal instruments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有下列情形之一的，海事法院可以扣押当事船舶：  
（一）船舶所有人对海事请求负有责任，并且在实施扣押时是该船的所有人；  
（二）船舶的光船承租人对海事请求负有责任，并且在实施扣押时是该船的光船承租人或者所有人；  
（三）具有船舶抵押权或者同样性质的权利的海事请求；  
（四）有关船舶所有权或者占有的海事请求；  
（五）具有船舶优先权的海事请求。  
海事法院可以扣押对海事请求负有责任的船舶所有人、光船承租人、定期租船人或者航次租船人在实施扣押时所有的其他船舶，但与船舶所有权或者占有有关的请求除外。  
从事军事、政府公务的船舶不得被扣押。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** A maritime court may arrest the ship in respect of which the maritime claim arose in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the owner of the ship is liable for the maritime claim and, at the time of the arrest, is the owner of the ship;  
(2) the bareboat charterer of the ship is liable for the maritime claim and, at the time of the arrest, is the bareboat charterer or owner of the ship;  
(3) the maritime claim is secured by a mortgage or a charge of a similar nature on the ship;  
(4) the maritime claim relates to the ownership or possession of the ship;  
(5) the maritime claim is subject to a maritime lien on the ship.  
A maritime court may arrest any other ship owned by the person who is liable for the maritime claim and is, at the time of the arrest, the owner, bareboat charterer, time charterer or voyage charterer of that ship, except for claims relating to the ownership or possession of the ship.  
Ships engaged in military or governmental public service shall not be arrested.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海事请求人不得因同一海事请求申请扣押已被扣押过的船舶，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）被请求人未提供充分的担保；  
（二）担保人有可能不能全部或者部分履行担保义务；  
（三）海事请求人因合理的原因同意释放被扣押的船舶或者返还已提供的担保；或者不能通过合理措施阻止释放被扣押的船舶或者返还已提供的担保。

**Article 24** A maritime claimant may not apply for the arrest of a ship that has already been arrested for the same maritime claim, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the person against whom the claim is made has not provided sufficient security;  
(2) the guarantor may be unable to perform all or part of the guarantee obligations;  
(3) the maritime claimant has consented to the release of the arrested ship or the return of the security provided for reasonable reasons, or is unable to prevent the release of the arrested ship or the return of the security through reasonable measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　海事请求人申请扣押当事船舶，不能立即查明被请求人名称的，不影响申请的提出。

**Article 25** If a maritime claimant applying for the arrest of the ship in respect of which the claim arose cannot immediately ascertain the name of the person against whom the claim is made, this shall not affect the submission of the application.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海事法院在发布或者解除扣押船舶命令的同时，可以向有关部门发出协助执行通知书，通知书应当载明协助执行的范围和内容，有关部门有义务协助执行。海事法院认为必要，可以直接派员登轮监护。

**Article 26** When issuing or lifting an order for the arrest of a ship, a maritime court may at the same time issue a notice of assistance in execution to the relevant authorities, which shall specify the scope and content of the assistance, and the relevant authorities shall have the obligation to assist in the execution. If the maritime court deems it necessary, it may directly send personnel to board the ship for supervision.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　海事法院裁定对船舶实施保全后，经海事请求人同意，可以采取限制船舶处分或者抵押等方式允许该船舶继续营运。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-29" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** After a maritime court rules to implement preservation against a ship, with the consent of the maritime claimant, it may allow the ship to continue its operations by means such as restricting the disposal or mortgage of the ship.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海事请求保全扣押船舶的期限为三十日。  
海事请求人在三十日内提起诉讼或者申请仲裁以及在诉讼或者仲裁过程中申请扣押船舶的，扣押船舶不受前款规定期限的限制。

**Article 28** The period for arrest of a ship in maritime claim preservation shall be thirty days.  
If the maritime claimant institutes an action or applies for arbitration within thirty days, or applies for the arrest of the ship during the litigation or arbitration proceedings, the arrest of the ship shall not be subject to the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　船舶扣押期间届满，被请求人不提供担保，而且船舶不宜继续扣押的，海事请求人可以在提起诉讼或者申请仲裁后，向扣押船舶的海事法院申请拍卖船舶。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 29** If, upon the expiry of the ship arrest period, the person against whom the claim is made fails to provide security and the ship is no longer suitable for continued arrest, the maritime claimant may, after instituting an action or applying for arbitration, apply to the maritime court that ordered the arrest for the auction of the ship.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　海事法院收到拍卖船舶的申请后，应当进行审查，作出准予或者不准予拍卖船舶的裁定。  
当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起五日内申请复议一次。海事法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出复议决定。复议期间停止裁定的执行。

**Article 30** Upon receipt of an application for the auction of a ship, the maritime court shall examine it and make a ruling granting or denying the auction.  
A party dissatisfied with the ruling may apply for one reconsideration within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within five days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, the execution of the ruling shall be suspended.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　海事请求人提交拍卖船舶申请后，又申请终止拍卖的，是否准许由海事法院裁定。海事法院裁定终止拍卖船舶的，为准备拍卖船舶所发生的费用由海事请求人承担。

**Article 31** After submitting an application for the auction of a ship, if the maritime claimant subsequently applies for the termination of the auction, whether to grant such termination shall be determined by the maritime court by ruling. If the maritime court rules to terminate the auction of the ship, the expenses incurred in preparing for the auction shall be borne by the maritime claimant.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　海事法院裁定拍卖船舶，应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告。拍卖外籍船舶的，应当通过对外发行的报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告。  
公告包括以下内容：  
（一）被拍卖船舶的名称和国籍；  
（二）拍卖船舶的理由和依据；  
（三）拍卖船舶委员会的组成；  
（四）拍卖船舶的时间和地点；  
（五）被拍卖船舶的展示时间和地点；  
（六）参加竞买应当办理的手续；  
（七）办理债权登记事项；  
（八）需要公告的其他事项。  
拍卖船舶的公告期间不少于三十日。

**Article 32** The maritime court that rules to auction a ship shall publish an announcement through newspapers or other news media. If a foreign ship is to be auctioned, the announcement shall be published through newspapers or other news media distributed abroad.  
The announcement shall include the following:  
(1) the name and nationality of the ship to be auctioned;  
(2) the reasons and basis for auctioning the ship;  
(3) the composition of the ship auction committee;  
(4) the time and place of the ship auction;  
(5) the time and place for display of the ship to be auctioned;  
(6) the procedures to be completed for participating in the bidding;  
(7) matters for registration of claims;  
(8) other matters requiring announcement.  
The period for announcement of the ship auction shall be not less than thirty days.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　海事法院应当在拍卖船舶三十日前，向被拍卖船舶登记国的登记机关和已知的船舶优先权人、抵押权人和船舶所有人发出通知。  
通知内容包括被拍卖船舶的名称、拍卖船舶的时间和地点、拍卖船舶的理由和依据以及债权登记等。  
通知方式包括书面方式和能够确认收悉的其他适当方式。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-34" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第34条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** The maritime court shall, thirty days before the auction of the ship, send a notice to the registration authority of the country of registration of the ship to be auctioned and to the known maritime lien holders, mortgagees, and shipowners.  
The content of the notice shall include the name of the ship to be auctioned, the time and place of the ship auction, the reasons and basis for auctioning the ship, and the registration of claims, etc.  
The method of notice shall include written form and other appropriate means capable of confirming receipt.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　拍卖船舶由拍卖船舶委员会实施。拍卖船舶委员会由海事法院指定的本院执行人员和聘请的拍卖师、验船师三人或者五人组成。  
拍卖船舶委员会组织对船舶鉴定、估价；组织和主持拍卖；与竞买人签订拍卖成交确认书；办理船舶移交手续。  
拍卖船舶委员会对海事法院负责，受海事法院监督。

**Article 34** The auction of a ship shall be carried out by the ship auction committee. The ship auction committee shall be composed of three or five members, consisting of execution personnel of the maritime court designated by the maritime court and hired auctioneers and ship surveyors.  
The ship auction committee shall organize the expert evaluation and appraisal of the ship; organize and preside over the auction; sign the auction confirmation with the winning bidder; and handle the ship delivery procedures.  
The ship auction committee shall be responsible to the maritime court and shall be subject to the supervision of the maritime court.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　竞买人应当在规定的期限内向拍卖船舶委员会登记。登记时应当交验本人、企业法定代表人或者其他组织负责人身份证明和委托代理人的授权委托书，并交纳一定数额的买船保证金。

**Article 35** Bidders shall register with the ship auction committee within the prescribed time limit. When registering, they shall submit their own identity certificate, the identity certificate of the legal representative of the enterprise or the responsible person of other organization, and the power of attorney of the authorized agent, and shall pay a certain amount of deposit for ship purchase.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　拍卖船舶委员会应当在拍卖船舶前，展示被拍卖船舶，并提供察看被拍卖船舶的条件和有关资料。

**Article 36** The ship auction committee shall, before the auction of the ship, display the ship to be auctioned and provide conditions for inspecting the ship to be auctioned and relevant materials.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　买受人在签署拍卖成交确认书后，应当立即交付不低于百分之二十的船舶价款，其余价款在成交之日起七日内付清，但拍卖船舶委员会与买受人另有约定的除外。

**Article 37** After signing the auction confirmation, the buyer shall immediately deliver not less than twenty percent of the ship price, and the remaining price shall be paid in full within seven days from the date of transaction, unless otherwise agreed between the ship auction committee and the buyer.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　买受人付清全部价款后，原船舶所有人应当在指定的期限内于船舶停泊地以船舶现状向买受人移交船舶。拍卖船舶委员会组织和监督船舶的移交，并在船舶移交后与买受人签署船舶移交完毕确认书。  
移交船舶完毕，海事法院发布解除扣押船舶命令。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-35" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第35条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** After the buyer pays the full price, the original shipowner shall deliver the ship to the buyer at the ship's berthing place in its current condition within the specified time limit. The ship auction committee shall organize and supervise the delivery of the ship, and after the delivery, shall sign a confirmation of completion of ship delivery with the buyer.  
Upon completion of the ship delivery, the maritime court shall issue an order to release the ship from arrest.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　船舶移交后，海事法院应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告，公布船舶已经公开拍卖并移交给买受人。

**Article 39** After the ship is delivered, the maritime court shall publish an announcement through newspapers or other news media, stating that the ship has been publicly auctioned and delivered to the buyer.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　买受人接收船舶后，应当持拍卖成交确认书和有关材料，向船舶登记机关办理船舶所有权登记手续。原船舶所有人应当向原船舶登记机关办理船舶所有权注销登记。原船舶所有人不办理船舶所有权注销登记的，不影响船舶所有权的转让。

**Article 40** After accepting the ship, the buyer shall, holding the auction confirmation and relevant materials, handle the formalities for registration of ship ownership with the ship registration authority. The original shipowner shall handle the formalities for cancellation of registration of ship ownership with the original ship registration authority. If the original shipowner fails to handle the cancellation of registration of ship ownership, the transfer of ship ownership shall not be affected.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　竞买人之间恶意串通的，拍卖无效。参与恶意串通的竞买人应当承担拍卖船舶费用并赔偿有关损失。海事法院可以对参与恶意串通的竞买人处最高应价百分之十以上百分之三十以下的罚款。

**Article 41** If bidders collude maliciously, the auction shall be invalid. The bidders participating in malicious collusion shall bear the expenses of the ship auction and compensate for relevant losses. The maritime court may impose a fine of not less than ten percent and not more than thirty percent of the highest bid on the bidders participating in malicious collusion.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　除本节规定的以外，拍卖适用《中华人民共和国拍卖法》的有关规定。

**Article 42** Except as provided in this Section, the auction shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Auction Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　执行程序中拍卖被扣押船舶清偿债务的，可以参照本节有关规定。

**Article 43** In the enforcement procedure, if a ship under arrest is auctioned to settle debts, the relevant provisions of this Section may be referred to.

#### 第三节　船载货物的扣押与拍卖

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　海事请求人为保障其海事请求的实现，可以申请扣押船载货物。  
申请扣押的船载货物，应当属于被请求人所有。

**Article 44** A maritime claimant may, to secure the realization of its maritime claim, apply for the arrest of cargo on board a ship.  
The cargo on board a ship for which arrest is applied for shall belong to the respondent.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　海事请求人申请扣押船载货物的价值，应当与其债权数额相当。

**Article 45** The value of the cargo on board a ship for which the maritime claimant applies for arrest shall be commensurate with the amount of its claim.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　海事请求保全扣押船载货物的期限为十五日。  
海事请求人在十五日内提起诉讼或者申请仲裁以及在诉讼或者仲裁过程中申请扣押船载货物的，扣押船载货物不受前款规定期限的限制。

**Article 46** The period for arrest of cargo on board a ship for maritime claim preservation shall be fifteen days.  
If the maritime claimant files a lawsuit or applies for arbitration within the fifteen days and applies for the arrest of cargo on board a ship during the lawsuit or arbitration, the arrest of cargo on board a ship shall not be limited by the time limit prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　船载货物扣押期间届满，被请求人不提供担保，而且货物不宜继续扣押的，海事请求人可以在提起诉讼或者申请仲裁后，向扣押船载货物的海事法院申请拍卖货物。  
对无法保管、不易保管或者保管费用可能超过其价值的物品，海事请求人可以申请提前拍卖。

**Article 47** If the period of arrest of cargo on board a ship expires, the respondent fails to provide security, and the cargo is not suitable for continued arrest, the maritime claimant may, after filing a lawsuit or applying for arbitration, apply to the maritime court that arrested the cargo for the auction of the cargo.  
For items that cannot be stored, are not easy to store, or whose storage costs may exceed their value, the maritime claimant may apply for an early auction.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　海事法院收到拍卖船载货物的申请后，应当进行审查，在七日内作出准予或者不准予拍卖船载货物的裁定。  
当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起五日内申请复议一次。海事法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出复议决定。复议期间停止裁定的执行。

**Article 48** After receiving an application for auction of cargo on board a ship, the maritime court shall examine the application and make a ruling on whether to approve or disapprove the auction within seven days.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling, it may apply for one reconsideration within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within five days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration. The execution of the ruling shall be suspended during the reconsideration period.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　拍卖船载货物由海事法院指定的本院执行人员和聘请的拍卖师组成的拍卖组织实施，或者由海事法院委托的机构实施。  
拍卖船载货物，本节没有规定的，参照本章第二节拍卖船舶的有关规定。

**Article 49** The auction of cargo on board a ship shall be carried out by an auction organization composed of execution personnel of the maritime court designated by the maritime court and hired auctioneers, or by an institution entrusted by the maritime court.  
For the auction of cargo on board a ship, matters not provided for in this Section shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions on auction of ships in Section 2 of this Chapter.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　海事请求人对与海事请求有关的船用燃油、船用物料申请海事请求保全，适用本节规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-38" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第38条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 50** If a maritime claimant applies for maritime claim preservation for marine fuel and marine stores related to the maritime claim, the provisions of this Section shall apply.

### 第四章　海事强制令

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　海事强制令是指海事法院根据海事请求人的申请，为使其合法权益免受侵害，责令被请求人作为或者不作为的强制措施。

**Article 51** A maritime injunction refers to a compulsory measure ordered by a maritime court, upon the application of a maritime claimant, to order the respondent to perform or refrain from performing a certain act, so as to prevent infringement upon the lawful rights and interests of the claimant.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人在起诉前申请海事强制令，应当向海事纠纷发生地海事法院提出。

**Article 52** Where a party applies for a maritime injunction before instituting an action, the application shall be filed with the maritime court located at the place where the maritime dispute arose.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　海事强制令不受当事人之间关于该海事请求的诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的约束。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-41" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第41条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 53** A maritime injunction shall not be subject to any agreement on jurisdiction for litigation or arbitration between the parties concerning the maritime claim.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　海事请求人申请海事强制令，应当向海事法院提交书面申请。申请书应当载明申请理由，并附有关证据。

**Article 54** A maritime claimant applying for a maritime injunction shall submit a written application to the maritime court. The application shall state the reasons for the application and be accompanied by relevant evidence.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　海事法院受理海事强制令申请，可以责令海事请求人提供担保。海事请求人不提供的，驳回其申请。

**Article 55** Upon accepting an application for a maritime injunction, the maritime court may order the maritime claimant to provide security. If the maritime claimant fails to provide security, the court shall reject the application.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　作出海事强制令，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）请求人有具体的海事请求；  
（二）需要纠正被请求人违反法律规定或者合同约定的行为；  
（三）情况紧急，不立即作出海事强制令将造成损害或者使损害扩大。

**Article 56** The issuance of a maritime injunction shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The claimant has a specific maritime claim;  
(2) There is a need to remedy an act of the respondent that violates the provisions of the law or the terms of a contract;  
(3) The circumstances are urgent, and failure to issue the maritime injunction immediately would cause damage or aggravate the damage.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　海事法院接受申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出裁定。裁定作出海事强制令的，应当立即执行；对不符合海事强制令条件的，裁定驳回其申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-42" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 57** After accepting an application, the maritime court shall make a ruling within forty-eight hours. If the ruling grants the maritime injunction, the court shall enforce it immediately; if the conditions for a maritime injunction are not met, the court shall rule to reject the application.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起五日内申请复议一次。海事法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出复议决定。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。  
利害关系人对海事强制令提出异议，海事法院经审查，认为理由成立的，应当裁定撤销海事强制令。

**Article 58** If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling, it may apply for a reconsideration once within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within five days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration. Execution of the ruling shall not be suspended during the period of reconsideration.  
If an interested person raises an objection to the maritime injunction and the maritime court, upon examination, finds the grounds to be valid, the court shall rule to revoke the maritime injunction.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　被请求人拒不执行海事强制令的，海事法院可以根据情节轻重处以罚款、拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
对个人的罚款金额，为一千元以上三万元以下。对单位的罚款金额，为三万元以上十万元以下。  
拘留的期限，为十五日以下。

**Article 59** If the respondent refuses to comply with the maritime injunction, the maritime court may, depending on the gravity of the circumstances, impose a fine or detention; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.  
The amount of fine imposed on an individual shall be not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan. The amount of fine imposed on a unit shall be not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.  
The term of detention shall be not more than fifteen days.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　海事请求人申请海事强制令错误的，应当赔偿被请求人或者利害关系人因此所遭受的损失。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-46" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第46条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 60** Where a maritime claimant applies for a maritime injunction wrongfully, the maritime claimant shall compensate the person against whom the application is made or the interested person for the losses thus sustained.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　海事强制令执行后，有关海事纠纷未进入诉讼或者仲裁程序的，当事人就该海事请求，可以向作出海事强制令的海事法院或者其他有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼，但当事人之间订有诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的除外。

**Article 61** After a maritime injunction is enforced, if the relevant maritime dispute has not been submitted to litigation or arbitration proceedings, the party concerned may, in respect of the maritime claim, institute an action in the maritime court that issued the maritime injunction or in another maritime court having jurisdiction, unless the parties have concluded an agreement on litigation jurisdiction or an arbitration agreement.

### 第五章　海事证据保全

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　海事证据保全是指海事法院根据海事请求人的申请，对有关海事请求的证据予以提取、保存或者封存的强制措施。

**Article 62** Maritime evidence preservation means the compulsory measures taken by a maritime court, upon the application of a maritime claimant, to extract, preserve or seal up the evidence relating to a maritime claim.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　当事人在起诉前申请海事证据保全，应当向被保全的证据所在地海事法院提出。

**Article 63** Where a party applies for maritime evidence preservation before instituting an action, the application shall be submitted to the maritime court in the place where the evidence to be preserved is located.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　海事证据保全不受当事人之间关于该海事请求的诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的约束。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-47" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 64** Maritime evidence preservation shall not be bound by any agreement on litigation jurisdiction or arbitration agreement between the parties in respect of the maritime claim.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　海事请求人申请海事证据保全，应当向海事法院提交书面申请。申请书应当载明请求保全的证据、该证据与海事请求的联系、申请理由。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-48" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 65** A maritime claimant applying for maritime evidence preservation shall submit a written application to the maritime court. The application shall state the evidence to be preserved, the connection between such evidence and the maritime claim, and the reasons for the application.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　海事法院受理海事证据保全申请，可以责令海事请求人提供担保。海事请求人不提供的，驳回其申请。

**Article 66** Upon accepting an application for maritime evidence preservation, the maritime court may order the maritime claimant to provide security. If the maritime claimant fails to provide security, the maritime court shall reject the application.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　采取海事证据保全，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）请求人是海事请求的当事人；  
（二）请求保全的证据对该海事请求具有证明作用；  
（三）被请求人是与请求保全的证据有关的人；  
（四）情况紧急，不立即采取证据保全就会使该海事请求的证据灭失或者难以取得。

**Article 67** The following conditions shall be met for taking maritime evidence preservation:  
(1) The applicant is a party to the maritime claim;  
(2) The evidence requested to be preserved is probative of the maritime claim;  
(3) The person against whom the application is made is a person related to the evidence requested to be preserved; and  
(4) The circumstances are urgent, and if evidence preservation is not taken immediately, the evidence relating to the maritime claim will be destroyed or become difficult to obtain.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　海事法院接受申请后，应当在四十八小时内作出裁定。裁定采取海事证据保全措施的，应当立即执行；对不符合海事证据保全条件的，裁定驳回其申请。

**Article 68** After accepting an application, the maritime court shall make a ruling within forty-eight hours. If the ruling decides to take maritime evidence preservation measures, it shall be enforced immediately; if the conditions for maritime evidence preservation are not met, the court shall rule to reject the application.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起五日内申请复议一次。海事法院应当在收到复议申请之日起五日内作出复议决定。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。被请求人申请复议的理由成立的，应当将保全的证据返还被请求人。  
利害关系人对海事证据保全提出异议，海事法院经审查，认为理由成立的，应当裁定撤销海事证据保全；已经执行的，应当将与利害关系人有关的证据返还利害关系人。

**Article 69** If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling, the party may apply for one reconsideration within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within five days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration. Enforcement of the ruling shall not be suspended during the period of reconsideration. If the reasons for reconsideration raised by the person against whom the application is made are sustained, the preserved evidence shall be returned to that person.  
If an interested person raises an objection to the maritime evidence preservation, and the maritime court, upon examination, finds the reasons sustained, it shall rule to cancel the maritime evidence preservation; if the measures have already been enforced, the evidence relating to the interested person shall be returned to that person.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　海事法院进行海事证据保全，根据具体情况，可以对证据予以封存，也可以提取复制件、副本，或者进行拍照、录相，制作节录本、调查笔录等。确有必要的，也可以提取证据原件。

**Article 70** When carrying out maritime evidence preservation, the maritime court may, depending on the specific circumstances, seal up the evidence, or extract copies, duplicates, or take photographs, video recordings, make excerpts, investigation records, etc. If truly necessary, the original evidence may also be extracted.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　海事请求人申请海事证据保全错误的，应当赔偿被请求人或者利害关系人因此所遭受的损失。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-51" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 71** Where a maritime claimant applies for maritime evidence preservation wrongfully, the maritime claimant shall compensate the person against whom the application is made or the interested person for the losses thus sustained.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　海事证据保全后，有关海事纠纷未进入诉讼或者仲裁程序的，当事人就该海事请求，可以向采取证据保全的海事法院或者其他有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼，但当事人之间订有诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的除外。

**Article 72** After maritime evidence preservation, if the relevant maritime dispute has not been submitted to litigation or arbitration proceedings, the party concerned may, in respect of the maritime claim, institute an action in the maritime court that took the evidence preservation measures or in another maritime court having jurisdiction, unless the parties have concluded an agreement on litigation jurisdiction or an arbitration agreement.

### 第六章　海事担保

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　海事担保包括本法规定的海事请求保全、海事强制令、海事证据保全等程序中所涉及的担保。  
担保的方式为提供现金或者保证、设置抵押或者质押。

**Article 73** Maritime security includes the security involved in the procedures of maritime claim preservation, maritime injunction, maritime evidence preservation, etc. as provided in this Law.  
The methods of providing security are to provide cash or a guaranty, or to create a mortgage or pledge.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　海事请求人的担保应当提交给海事法院；被请求人的担保可以提交给海事法院，也可以提供给海事请求人。

**Article 74** The security of a maritime claimant shall be submitted to the maritime court; the security of the person against whom the application is made may be submitted to the maritime court or provided to the maritime claimant.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　海事请求人提供的担保，其方式、数额由海事法院决定。被请求人提供的担保，其方式、数额由海事请求人和被请求人协商；协商不成的，由海事法院决定。

**Article 75** The method and amount of the security provided by a maritime claimant shall be determined by the maritime court. The method and amount of the security provided by the person against whom the application is made shall be negotiated between the maritime claimant and that person; if negotiation fails, the maritime court shall make the determination.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　海事请求人要求被请求人就海事请求保全提供担保的数额，应当与其债权数额相当，但不得超过被保全的财产价值。  
海事请求人提供担保的数额，应当相当于因其申请可能给被请求人造成的损失。具体数额由海事法院决定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 76** The amount of security requested by a maritime claimant from the person against whom the application is made for maritime claim preservation shall be commensurate with the amount of the claim, but shall not exceed the value of the preserved property.  
The amount of security provided by the maritime claimant shall be equivalent to the losses that may be caused to the person against whom the application is made due to the application. The specific amount shall be determined by the maritime court.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　担保提供后，提供担保的人有正当理由的，可以向海事法院申请减少、变更或者取消该担保。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-52" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 77** After the security has been provided, if the person providing the security has legitimate reasons, that person may apply to the maritime court for reduction, modification or cancellation of such security.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　海事请求人请求担保的数额过高，造成被请求人损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 78** Where the amount of security requested by a maritime claimant is excessively high, causing losses to the person against whom the application is made, the maritime claimant shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　设立海事赔偿责任限制基金和先予执行等程序所涉及的担保，可以参照本章规定。

**Article 79** For the security involved in procedures such as constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims and advance execution, the provisions of this Chapter may be referred to.

### 第七章　送达

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　海事诉讼法律文书的送达，适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，还可以采用下列方式：  
（一）向受送达人委托的诉讼代理人送达；  
（二）向受送达人在中华人民共和国领域内设立的代表机构、分支机构或者业务代办人送达；  
（三）通过能够确认收悉的其他适当方式送达。  
有关扣押船舶的法律文书也可以向当事船舶的船长送达。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-55" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 80** The service of legal documents in maritime litigation shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and the following methods may also be used:  
(1) service on the litigation agent authorized by the person on whom the document is to be served;  
(2) service on the representative office, branch office or business agent established by the person on whom the document is to be served within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) service by any other appropriate means by which the receipt may be confirmed.  
Legal documents concerning the arrest of a ship may also be served on the master of the ship concerned.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　有义务接受法律文书的人拒绝签收，送达人在送达回证上记明情况，经送达人、见证人签名或者盖章，将法律文书留在其住所或者办公处所的，视为送达。

**Article 81** If a person who is obliged to accept a legal document refuses to sign for its receipt, and the server notes the fact on the service receipt and, after affixing signature or seal by the server and a witness, leaves the legal document at the residence or office of such person, the service shall be deemed to have been made.

### 第八章　审判程序

#### 第一节　审理船舶碰撞案件的规定

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　原告在起诉时、被告在答辩时，应当如实填写《海事事故调查表》。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-56" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第56条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 82** When prosecuting, the plaintiff, and when responding, the defendant shall truthfully fill in the "Maritime Accident Investigation Form".

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　海事法院向当事人送达起诉状或者答辩状时，不附送有关证据材料。

**Article 83** When serving the statement of prosecution or defense on the parties, the maritime court shall not enclose relevant evidence materials.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　当事人应当在开庭审理前完成举证。当事人完成举证并向海事法院出具完成举证说明书后，可以申请查阅有关船舶碰撞的事实证据材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-56" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第56条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 84** The parties shall complete the adduction of evidence before the hearing. After the parties have completed the adduction of evidence and submitted a statement of completion of adduction of evidence to the maritime court, they may apply to inspect the evidence materials concerning the facts of the ship collision.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　当事人不能推翻其在《海事事故调查表》中的陈述和已经完成的举证，但有新的证据，并有充分的理由说明该证据不能在举证期间内提交的除外。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-59" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 85** A party may not overturn his statement in the Maritime Accident Investigation Form and the evidence already adduced, unless new evidence is obtained and there are sufficient reasons to explain that such evidence could not be submitted within the time limit for adduction of evidence.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　船舶检验、估价应当由国家授权或者其他具有专业资格的机构或者个人承担。非经国家授权或者未取得专业资格的机构或者个人所作的检验或者估价结论，海事法院不予采纳。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-60" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第60条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 86** Ship inspection and appraisal shall be undertaken by institutions or individuals authorized by the State or otherwise having professional qualifications. The maritime court shall not adopt inspection or appraisal conclusions made by institutions or individuals not authorized by the State or without professional qualifications.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　海事法院审理船舶碰撞案件，应当在立案后一年内审结。有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。

**Article 87** The maritime court shall conclude the trial of a ship collision case within one year after placing the case on file. If an extension is required due to special circumstances, the president of the court shall approve it.

#### 第二节　审理共同海损案件的规定

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　当事人就共同海损的纠纷，可以协议委托理算机构理算，也可以直接向海事法院提起诉讼。海事法院受理未经理算的共同海损纠纷，可以委托理算机构理算。

**Article 88** The parties may, by agreement, entrust an adjustment institution to adjust a dispute over general average, or may directly institute an action in a maritime court. Where a maritime court accepts a dispute over general average that has not been adjusted, it may entrust an adjustment institution to adjust it.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　理算机构作出的共同海损理算报告，当事人没有提出异议的，可以作为分摊责任的依据；当事人提出异议的，由海事法院决定是否采纳。

**Article 89** Where a party raises no objection to the general average adjustment report made by the adjustment institution, it may be used as the basis for apportioning liability; where a party raises an objection, the maritime court shall decide whether to adopt it.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　当事人可以不受因同一海损事故提起的共同海损诉讼程序的影响，就非共同海损损失向责任人提起诉讼。

**Article 90** A party may institute an action against the liable person for a loss not constituting general average, without being affected by the proceedings of the general average action instituted due to the same average accident.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　当事人就同一海损事故向受理共同海损案件的海事法院提起非共同海损的诉讼，以及对共同海损分摊向责任人提起追偿诉讼的，海事法院可以合并审理。

**Article 91** Where a party institutes an action not constituting general average in a maritime court that accepts a general average case against the same average accident, and also institutes an action for recourse against the liable person for general average contribution, the maritime court may consolidate the trial.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　海事法院审理共同海损案件，应当在立案后一年内审结。有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-64" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第64条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 92** The maritime court shall conclude the trial of a general average case within one year after placing the case on file. If an extension is required due to special circumstances, the president of the court shall approve it.

#### 第三节　海上保险人行使代位请求赔偿权利的规定

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　因第三人造成保险事故，保险人向被保险人支付保险赔偿后，在保险赔偿范围内可以代位行使被保险人对第三人请求赔偿的权利。

**Article 93** Where a third party causes an insured event, after the insurer pays insurance indemnity to the insured, it may, within the scope of the insurance indemnity, subrogate the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　保险人行使代位请求赔偿权利时，被保险人未向造成保险事故的第三人提起诉讼的，保险人应当以自己的名义向该第三人提起诉讼。

**Article 94** Where the insured has not instituted an action against the third party that caused the insured event when the insurer exercises the right to subrogated claim for compensation, the insurer shall institute an action against the third party in its own name.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　保险人行使代位请求赔偿权利时，被保险人已经向造成保险事故的第三人提起诉讼的，保险人可以向受理该案的法院提出变更当事人的请求，代位行使被保险人对第三人请求赔偿的权利。  
被保险人取得的保险赔偿不能弥补第三人造成的全部损失的，保险人和被保险人可以作为共同原告向第三人请求赔偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-65" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第65条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-66" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第66条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-67" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第67条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 95** Where the insured has already instituted an action against the third party that caused the insured event when the insurer exercises the right to subrogated claim for compensation, the insurer may apply to the court accepting the case for a change of the party to subrogate the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party.  
If the insurance indemnity obtained by the insured cannot fully compensate all losses caused by the third party, the insurer and the insured may act as co-plaintiffs to claim compensation from the third party.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　保险人依照[本法第九十四条](#art-94)、第九十五条的规定提起诉讼或者申请参加诉讼的，应当向受理该案的海事法院提交保险人支付保险赔偿的凭证，以及参加诉讼应当提交的其他文件。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-68" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第68条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 96** Where an insurer institutes an action or applies to participate in proceedings in accordance with Article 94 and Article 95 of this Law, it shall submit to the maritime court accepting the case the certificates of payment of insurance indemnity by the insurer, and other documents that shall be submitted for participation in the proceedings.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　对船舶造成油污损害的赔偿请求，受损害人可以向造成油污损害的船舶所有人提出，也可以直接向承担船舶所有人油污损害责任的保险人或者提供财务保证的其他人提出。  
油污损害责任的保险人或者提供财务保证的其他人被起诉的，有权要求造成油污损害的船舶所有人参加诉讼。

**Article 97** For a claim for compensation for oil pollution damage caused by a ship, the injured person may make the claim against the shipowner that caused the oil pollution damage, or may directly make the claim against the insurer that bears the shipowner's oil pollution damage liability or any other person that provides financial security.  
If the insurer that bears the oil pollution damage liability or any other person that provides financial security is sued, it shall have the right to demand that the shipowner that caused the oil pollution damage participate in the proceedings.

#### 第四节　简易程序、督促程序和公示催告程序

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　海事法院审理事实清楚、权利义务关系明确、争议不大的简单的海事案件，可以适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》简易程序的规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于海事法院可否适用小额诉讼程序问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于海事法院可否适用小额诉讼程序问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 98** Where a maritime court tries a simple maritime case with clear facts, definite rights and obligations, and minor disputes, it may apply the provisions on summary procedure of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　债权人基于海事事由请求债务人给付金钱或者有价证券，符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》有关规定的，可以向有管辖权的海事法院申请支付令。  
债务人是外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织，但在中华人民共和国领域内有住所、代表机构或者分支机构并能够送达支付令的，债权人可以向有管辖权的海事法院申请支付令。

**Article 99** Where a creditor requests the debtor to pay money or marketable securities based on a maritime cause and meets the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it may apply to the maritime court with jurisdiction for an order of payment.  
If the debtor is a foreigner, stateless person, foreign enterprise or organization, but has a domicile, representative office or branch within the territory of the People's Republic of China and the order of payment can be served, the creditor may apply to the maritime court with jurisdiction for an order of payment.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　提单等提货凭证持有人，因提货凭证失控或者灭失，可以向货物所在地海事法院申请公示催告。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-70" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第70条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-71" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第71条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 100** Where the holder of a bill of lading or other document of title loses control of such document or such document is lost or destroyed, he may apply to the maritime court at the place where the goods are located for a public summons.

### 第九章　设立海事赔偿责任限制基金程序

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　船舶所有人、承租人、经营人、救助人、保险人在发生海事事故后，依法申请责任限制的，可以向海事法院申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金。  
船舶造成油污损害的，船舶所有人及其责任保险人或者提供财务保证的其他人为取得法律规定的责任限制的权利，应当向海事法院设立油污损害的海事赔偿责任限制基金。  
设立责任限制基金的申请可以在起诉前或者诉讼中提出，但最迟应当在一审判决作出前提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-79" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第79条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 101** After a maritime accident occurs, the shipowner, charterer, operator, salvor or insurer may, if applying for limitation of liability according to law, file an application with the maritime court for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability.  
Where a ship causes oil pollution damage, the shipowner, the liability insurer or any other person providing financial security shall, in order to obtain the right to limit liability as provided by law, constitute a limitation fund for maritime claims liability for oil pollution damage with the maritime court.  
An application for constitution of a limitation fund may be filed before the prosecution or during the litigation, but shall be filed no later than the rendering of the judgment at first instance.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　当事人在起诉前申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，应当向事故发生地、合同履行地或者船舶扣押地海事法院提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-80" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第80条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 102** If a party applies for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability before prosecution, the application shall be submitted to the maritime court of the place where the accident occurred, the place of performance of the contract, or the place where the ship was arrested.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　设立海事赔偿责任限制基金，不受当事人之间关于诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的约束。

**Article 103** The constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability shall not be subject to any agreement between the parties on litigation jurisdiction or arbitration.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　申请人向海事法院申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金，应当提交书面申请。申请书应当载明申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的数额、理由，以及已知的利害关系人的名称、地址和通讯方法，并附有关证据。

**Article 104** An applicant who applies to the maritime court for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability shall submit a written application. The application shall specify the amount of and the reasons for the application for constitution of the limitation fund for maritime claims liability, as well as the names, addresses and means of communication of the known interested parties, and shall be accompanied by relevant evidence.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　海事法院受理设立海事赔偿责任限制基金申请后，应当在七日内向已知的利害关系人发出通知，同时通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告。  
通知和公告包括下列内容：  
（一）申请人的名称；  
（二）申请的事实和理由；  
（三）设立海事赔偿责任限制基金事项；  
（四）办理债权登记事项；  
（五）需要告知的其他事项。

**Article 105** After accepting an application for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability, the maritime court shall, within seven days, give notice to the known interested parties, and shall simultaneously publish an announcement through newspapers or other news media.  
The notice and announcement shall include the following contents:  
(1) the name of the applicant;  
(2) the facts and reasons for the application;  
(3) the matter of constitution of the limitation fund for maritime claims liability;  
(4) the matter of registration of claims;  
(5) any other matters that need to be notified.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　利害关系人对申请人申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金有异议的，应当在收到通知之日起七日内或者未收到通知的在公告之日起三十日内，以书面形式向海事法院提出。  
海事法院收到利害关系人提出的书面异议后，应当进行审查，在十五日内作出裁定。异议成立的，裁定驳回申请人的申请；异议不成立的，裁定准予申请人设立海事赔偿责任限制基金。  
当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起七日内提起上诉。第二审人民法院应当在收到上诉状之日起十五日内作出裁定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第22条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-83" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第83条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 106** If an interested party raises an objection to the applicant's application for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability, it shall file the objection in writing with the maritime court within seven days from the date of receipt of the notice, or within thirty days from the date of the announcement if no notice is received.  
After receiving the written objection filed by the interested party, the maritime court shall examine it and shall make an order within fifteen days. If the objection is upheld, the order shall reject the applicant's application; if the objection is not upheld, the order shall approve the applicant's constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the order, it may file an appeal within seven days from the date of receipt of the written order. The people's court of second instance shall make an order within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the appeal petition.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　利害关系人在规定的期间内没有提出异议的，海事法院裁定准予申请人设立海事赔偿责任限制基金。

**Article 107** If no interested party raises an objection within the prescribed period, the maritime court shall make an order approving the applicant's constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　准予申请人设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的裁定生效后，申请人应当在海事法院设立海事赔偿责任限制基金。  
设立海事赔偿责任限制基金可以提供现金，也可以提供经海事法院认可的担保。  
海事赔偿责任限制基金的数额，为海事赔偿责任限额和自事故发生之日起至基金设立之日止的利息。以担保方式设立基金的，担保数额为基金数额及其在基金设立期间的利息。  
以现金设立基金的，基金到达海事法院指定帐户之日为基金设立之日。以担保设立基金的，海事法院接受担保之日为基金设立之日。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-85" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第85条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 108** After the order approving the applicant's constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability takes effect, the applicant shall constitute a limitation fund for maritime claims liability with the maritime court.  
The limitation fund for maritime claims liability may be constituted in cash or by providing a security approved by the maritime court.  
The amount of the limitation fund for maritime claims liability shall be the limitation amount for maritime claims liability plus the interest from the date of the accident to the date of constitution of the fund. If the fund is constituted by way of security, the amount of security shall be the amount of the fund plus the interest during the period of constitution of the fund.  
If the fund is constituted in cash, the date on which the cash arrives at the account designated by the maritime court shall be the date of constitution of the fund. If the fund is constituted by way of security, the date on which the maritime court accepts the security shall be the date of constitution of the fund.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　设立海事赔偿责任限制基金以后，当事人就有关海事纠纷应当向设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的海事法院提起诉讼，但当事人之间订有诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的除外。

**Article 109** After the constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability, the parties shall, with respect to the relevant maritime dispute, bring an action in the maritime court where the limitation fund for maritime claims liability is constituted, unless the parties have concluded an agreement on litigation jurisdiction or an arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　申请人申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金错误的，应当赔偿利害关系人因此所遭受的损失。

**Article 110** If an applicant applies for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability wrongfully, the applicant shall compensate the interested party for the losses caused thereby.

### 第十章　债权登记与受偿程序

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　海事法院裁定强制拍卖船舶的公告发布后，债权人应当在公告期间，就与被拍卖船舶有关的债权申请登记。公告期间届满不登记的，视为放弃在本次拍卖船舶价款中受偿的权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-87" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第87条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第16条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 111** After the announcement of the maritime court's order for compulsory auction of a ship is made, creditors shall, within the period of the announcement, apply for registration of claims related to the ship to be auctioned. If no registration is made upon expiration of the announcement period, it shall be deemed that the right to be satisfied from the proceeds of the auction of the ship has been waived.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　海事法院受理设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的公告发布后，债权人应当在公告期间就与特定场合发生的海事事故有关的债权申请登记。公告期间届满不登记的，视为放弃债权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 112** After the announcement of acceptance of an application for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims liability is made by the maritime court, creditors shall, within the period of the announcement, apply for registration of claims related to the maritime accident occurring on a specific occasion. If no registration is made upon expiration of the announcement period, it shall be deemed that the claims have been waived.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　债权人向海事法院申请登记债权的，应当提交书面申请，并提供有关债权证据。  
债权证据，包括证明债权的具有法律效力的判决书、裁定书、调解书、仲裁裁决书和公证债权文书，以及其他证明具有海事请求的证据材料。

**Article 113** A creditor who applies to the maritime court for registration of claims shall submit a written application and provide relevant evidence of claims.  
Evidence of claims includes legally effective judgments, orders, mediation statements, arbitration awards and notarized documents of claims, as well as other evidentiary materials proving the existence of maritime claims.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　海事法院应当对债权人的申请进行审查，对提供债权证据的，裁定准予登记；对不提供债权证据的，裁定驳回申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-17" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第17条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 114** The maritime court shall examine the creditor's application. If evidence of claims is provided, it shall make an order approving the registration; if no evidence of claims is provided, it shall make an order rejecting the application.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　债权人提供证明债权的判决书、裁定书、调解书、仲裁裁决书或者公证债权文书的，海事法院经审查认定上述文书真实合法的，裁定予以确认。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-88" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第88条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 115** If a creditor provides a judgment, order, mediation statement, arbitration award or notarized document of claims proving the claims, and the maritime court, after examination, determines that the above documents are authentic and lawful, it shall make an order confirming them.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　债权人提供其他海事请求证据的，应当在办理债权登记以后，在受理债权登记的海事法院提起确权诉讼。当事人之间有仲裁协议的，应当及时申请仲裁。  
海事法院对确权诉讼作出的判决、裁定具有法律效力，当事人不得提起上诉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-90" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第90条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第20条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第21条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 116** If a creditor provides other evidence of maritime claims, the creditor shall, after completing the registration of claims, file an action for confirmation of rights in the maritime court that accepted the registration of claims. If the parties have an arbitration agreement, they shall promptly apply for arbitration.  
The judgments and orders made by the maritime court in an action for confirmation of rights shall be legally effective, and the parties may not appeal.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　海事法院审理并确认债权后，应当向债权人发出债权人会议通知书，组织召开债权人会议。

**Article 117** After hearing and confirming the claims, the maritime court shall issue a notice of creditors' meeting to the creditors and shall convene and hold a creditors' meeting.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　债权人会议可以协商提出船舶价款或者海事赔偿责任限制基金的分配方案，签订受偿协议。  
受偿协议经海事法院裁定认可，具有法律效力。  
债权人会议协商不成的，由海事法院依照《中华人民共和国海商法》以及其他有关法律规定的受偿顺序，裁定船舶价款或者海事赔偿责任限制基金的分配方案。

**Article 118** The creditors' meeting may negotiate and propose a distribution plan of the proceeds of the sale of the ship or the limitation fund for maritime claims liability, and conclude an agreement on satisfaction.  
An agreement on satisfaction, after being confirmed by an order of the maritime court, shall have legal effect.  
If no agreement is reached at the creditors' meeting, the maritime court shall, in accordance with the order of satisfaction prescribed by the Maritime Code of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, make an order on the distribution plan of the proceeds of the sale of the ship or the limitation fund for maritime claims liability.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　拍卖船舶所得价款及其利息，或者海事赔偿责任限制基金及其利息，应当一并予以分配。  
分配船舶价款时，应当由责任人承担的诉讼费用，为保存、拍卖船舶和分配船舶价款产生的费用，以及为债权人的共同利益支付的其他费用，应当从船舶价款中先行拨付。  
清偿债务后的余款，应当退还船舶原所有人或者海事赔偿责任限制基金设立人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-91" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第91条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》第22条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 119** The proceeds from the auction of a ship and the interest thereon, or the limitation fund for maritime claims and the interest thereon, shall be distributed together.  
When distributing the proceeds from the auction of a ship, the litigation costs to be borne by the person liable, the expenses incurred for preserving and auctioning the ship and distributing the proceeds, and any other expenses paid for the common benefit of the creditors shall be paid in advance from the proceeds of the ship.  
The balance after the debts are discharged shall be returned to the original owner of the ship or to the person who established the limitation fund for maritime claims.

### 第十一章　船舶优先权催告程序

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　船舶转让时，受让人可以向海事法院申请船舶优先权催告，催促船舶优先权人及时主张权利，消灭该船舶附有的船舶优先权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释.md#art-93" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释》第93条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 120** When a ship is transferred, the transferee may apply to the maritime court for a maritime lien publicity notice to urge the holder of a maritime lien to timely claim the right, so as to extinguish the maritime lien attached to the ship.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　受让人申请船舶优先权催告的，应当向转让船舶交付地或者受让人住所地海事法院提出。

**Article 121** Where a transferee applies for a maritime lien publicity notice, the application shall be filed with the maritime court at the place of delivery of the transferred ship or at the place of the transferee's domicile.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　申请船舶优先权催告，应当向海事法院提交申请书、船舶转让合同、船舶技术资料等文件。申请书应当载明船舶的名称、申请船舶优先权催告的事实和理由。

**Article 122** To apply for a maritime lien publicity notice, the applicant shall submit to the maritime court a written application, the ship transfer contract, the ship technical documents, and other documents. The written application shall state the name of the ship, the facts and reasons for applying for the maritime lien publicity notice.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　海事法院在收到申请书以及有关文件后，应当进行审查，在七日内作出准予或者不准予申请的裁定。  
受让人对裁定不服的，可以申请复议一次。

**Article 123** After receiving the written application and relevant documents, the maritime court shall examine them and, within seven days, make an order granting or denying the application.  
If the transferee refuses to accept the order, it may apply for reconsideration once.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　海事法院在准予申请的裁定生效后，应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告，催促船舶优先权人在催告期间主张船舶优先权。  
船舶优先权催告期间为六十日。

**Article 124** After the order granting the application takes effect, the maritime court shall publish a public notice through newspapers or other news media to urge the holder of a maritime lien to claim the maritime lien within the publicity notice period.  
The period of the maritime lien publicity notice shall be sixty days.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　船舶优先权催告期间，船舶优先权人主张权利的，应当在海事法院办理登记；不主张权利的，视为放弃船舶优先权。

**Article 125** During the period of the maritime lien publicity notice, if a holder of a maritime lien claims the right, it shall register with the maritime court; if it does not claim the right, it shall be deemed to have waived the maritime lien.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　船舶优先权催告期间届满，无人主张船舶优先权的，海事法院应当根据当事人的申请作出判决，宣告该转让船舶不附有船舶优先权。判决内容应当公告。

**Article 126** If, upon the expiration of the period of the maritime lien publicity notice, no one claims the maritime lien, the maritime court shall, upon the application of the party, render a judgment declaring that the transferred ship is not encumbered with a maritime lien. The content of the judgment shall be publicly announced.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　本法自2000年7月1日起施行。

**Article 127** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2000.
