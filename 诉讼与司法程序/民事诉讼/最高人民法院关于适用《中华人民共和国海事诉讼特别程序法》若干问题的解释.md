# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕3号  
**公布日期**：2002-12-03  
**生效日期**：2003-02-01

> （2002年12月3日最高人民法院审判委员会第1259次会议通过，自2003年2月1日起施行。） 法释〔2003〕3号 为了依法正确审理海事案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》的规定以及海事审判的实践，对人民法院适用海事诉讼特别程序法的若干问题作出如下解释。 一、关于管辖 第一条 在海上或者通海水域发生的与船舶或者运输、生产、作业相关的海事侵权纠纷、海商合同纠纷，以及法律或者相关司法解释规定的其他海事纠纷案件由海事法院及其上级人民法院专门管辖。 第二条 涉外海事侵权纠纷案件和海上运输合同纠纷案件的管辖，适用民事诉讼法第二十五章的规定；民事诉讼法第二十五章没有规定的，适用海事诉讼特别程序法第六条第二款（一）、（二）项的规定和民事诉讼法的其他有关规定。 第三条 海事诉讼特别程序法第六条规定的海船指适合航行于海上或者通海水域的船舶。 第四条 海事诉讼特别程序法第六条第二款（一）项规定的船籍港指被告船舶的船籍港。被告船舶的船籍港不在中华人民共和国领域内，原告船舶的船籍港在中华人民共和国领域内的，由原告船舶的船籍港所在地的海事法院管辖。 第五条 海事诉讼特别程序法第六条第二款（二）项规定的起运港、转运港和到达港指合同约定的或者实际履行的起运港、转运港和到达港。合同约定的起运港、转运港和到达港与实际履行的起运港、转运港和到达港不一致的，以实际履行的地点确定案件管辖。 第六条 海事诉讼特别程序法第六条第二款（四）项的保赔标的物所在地指保赔船舶的所在地。 第七条 海事诉讼特别程序法第六条第二款（七）项规定的船舶所在地指起诉时船舶的停泊地或者船舶被扣押地。 第八条 因船员劳务合同纠纷直接向海事法院提起的诉讼，海事法院应当受理。 第九条 因海难救助费用提起的诉讼，除依照民事诉讼法第三十二条的规定确定管辖外，还可以由被救助的船舶以外的其他获救财产所在地的海事法院管辖。 第十条 与船舶担保或者船舶优先权有关的借款合同纠纷，由被告住所地、合同履行地、船舶的船籍港、船舶所在地的海事法院管辖。 第十一条 海事诉讼特别程序法第七条（三）项规定的有管辖权的海域指中华人民共和国的毗连区、专属经济区、大陆架以及有管辖权的其他海域。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 在海上或者通海水域发生的与船舶或者运输、生产、作业相关的海事侵权纠纷、海商合同纠纷，以及法律或者相关司法解释规定的其他海事纠纷案件由海事法院及其上级人民法院专门管辖。

**Article 1** Cases of maritime tort disputes, maritime commercial contract disputes, and other maritime dispute cases as provided by law or relevant judicial interpretations, which occur on the sea or navigable waters and are related to ships or transportation, production, or operations, shall be under the special jurisdiction of the maritime court and its superior people's court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 涉外海事侵权纠纷案件和海上运输合同纠纷案件的管辖，适用民事诉讼法第二十五章的规定；民事诉讼法第二十五章没有规定的，适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)第二款（一）、（二）项的规定和民事诉讼法的其他有关规定。

**Article 2** The jurisdiction over cases of foreign-related maritime tort disputes and disputes over maritime transportation contracts shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter 25 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; if Chapter 25 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China has no provisions, the provisions of Article 6, Paragraph 2, Items (1) and (2) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)规定的海船指适合航行于海上或者通海水域的船舶。

**Article 3** The "seagoing vessel" as referred to in Article 6 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means a vessel suitable for navigation on the sea or navigable waters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)第二款（一）项规定的船籍港指被告船舶的船籍港。被告船舶的船籍港不在中华人民共和国领域内，原告船舶的船籍港在中华人民共和国领域内的，由原告船舶的船籍港所在地的海事法院管辖。

**Article 4** The "port of registry" as referred to in Article 6, Paragraph 2, Item (1) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the port of registry of the defendant's vessel. If the port of registry of the defendant's vessel is not within the territory of the People's Republic of China and the port of registry of the plaintiff's vessel is within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court of the place where the port of registry of the plaintiff's vessel is located shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)第二款（二）项规定的起运港、转运港和到达港指合同约定的或者实际履行的起运港、转运港和到达港。合同约定的起运港、转运港和到达港与实际履行的起运港、转运港和到达港不一致的，以实际履行的地点确定案件管辖。

**Article 5** The port of departure, port of transshipment, and port of destination as referred to in Article 6, Paragraph 2, Item (2) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China mean the port of departure, port of transshipment, and port of destination as agreed in the contract or as actually performed. If the port of departure, port of transshipment, and port of destination as agreed in the contract are inconsistent with those actually performed, the jurisdiction over the case shall be determined on the basis of the place of actual performance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)第二款（四）项的保赔标的物所在地指保赔船舶的所在地。

**Article 6** The location of the subject matter of protection and indemnity as referred to in Article 6, Paragraph 2, Item (4) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the location of the vessel under protection and indemnity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-6)第二款（七）项规定的船舶所在地指起诉时船舶的停泊地或者船舶被扣押地。

**Article 7** The location of the vessel as referred to in Article 6, Paragraph 2, Item (7) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the place where the vessel is moored at the time of prosecution or the place where the vessel is arrested.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 因船员劳务合同纠纷直接向海事法院提起的诉讼，海事法院应当受理。

**Article 8** Where an action is directly brought to a maritime court due to a dispute over a crew labor service contract, the maritime court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 因海难救助费用提起的诉讼，除依照[民事诉讼法第三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-32)的规定确定管辖外，还可以由被救助的船舶以外的其他获救财产所在地的海事法院管辖。

**Article 9** In addition to determining jurisdiction in accordance with Article 32 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, an action brought for salvage remuneration may also be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court of the place where the salved property other than the salved vessel is located.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 与船舶担保或者船舶优先权有关的借款合同纠纷，由被告住所地、合同履行地、船舶的船籍港、船舶所在地的海事法院管辖。

**Article 10** A dispute over a loan contract relating to a ship mortgage or maritime lien shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court of the place of domicile of the defendant, the place of performance of the contract, the port of registry of the vessel, or the location of the vessel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-7)（三）项规定的有管辖权的海域指中华人民共和国的毗连区、专属经济区、大陆架以及有管辖权的其他海域。

**Article 11** The sea areas under jurisdiction as referred to in Article 7, Item (3) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China mean the contiguous zone, the exclusive economic zone, the continental shelf, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-7)（三）项规定的合同履行地指合同的实际履行地；合同未实际履行的，为合同约定的履行地。

**Article 12** The place of performance of the contract as referred to in Article 7, Item (3) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the place of actual performance of the contract; if the contract is not actually performed, it means the place of performance as agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 当事人根据[海事诉讼特别程序法第十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-11)的规定申请执行海事仲裁裁决，申请承认和执行国外海事仲裁裁决的，由被执行的财产所在地或者被执行人住所地的海事法院管辖；被执行的财产为船舶的，无论该船舶是否在海事法院管辖区域范围内，均由海事法院管辖。船舶所在地没有海事法院的，由就近的海事法院管辖。  
前款所称财产所在地和被执行人住所地是指海事法院行使管辖权的地域。

**Article 13** Where a party applies for enforcement of a maritime arbitral award or for recognition and enforcement of a foreign maritime arbitral award in accordance with Article 11 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court of the place where the property to be enforced is located or the place of domicile of the person against whom enforcement is sought shall have jurisdiction; if the property to be enforced is a vessel, the maritime court shall have jurisdiction regardless of whether the vessel is within the jurisdictional area of a maritime court. If there is no maritime court at the place where the vessel is located, the nearest maritime court shall have jurisdiction.  
The "place where the property is located" and the "place of domicile of the person against whom enforcement is sought" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refer to the area in which the maritime court exercises jurisdiction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 认定海事仲裁协议效力案件，由被申请人住所地、合同履行地或者约定的仲裁机构所在地的海事法院管辖。

**Article 14** A case concerning the determination of the validity of a maritime arbitration agreement shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court of the place of domicile of the respondent, the place of performance of the contract, or the place where the agreed arbitration institution is located.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 除海事法院及其上级人民法院外，地方人民法院对当事人提出的船舶保全申请应不予受理；地方人民法院为执行生效法律文书需要扣押和拍卖船舶的，应当委托船籍港所在地或者船舶所在地的海事法院执行。

**Article 15** Except for maritime courts and their superior people's courts, a local people's court shall not accept an application for preservation of a vessel filed by a party; if a local people's court needs to arrest and auction a vessel for the purpose of enforcing a legally effective legal instrument, it shall entrust the maritime court of the place of the port of registry or the place where the vessel is located with the enforcement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 两个以上海事法院都有管辖权的诉讼，原告可以向其中一个海事法院起诉；原告向两个以上有管辖权的海事法院起诉的，由最先立案的海事法院管辖。

**Article 16** Where two or more maritime courts have jurisdiction over an action, the plaintiff may bring the action in any one of those maritime courts; if the plaintiff brings the action in two or more maritime courts having jurisdiction, the maritime court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 海事法院之间因管辖权发生争议，由争议双方协商解决；协商解决不了的，报请最高人民法院指定管辖。  
二、关于海事请求保全

**Article 17** Where a dispute over jurisdiction arises between maritime courts, it shall be resolved through consultation between the disputing parties; if consultation fails, the matter shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.  
II. Preservation of Maritime Claims

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-12)规定的被请求人的财产包括船舶、船载货物、船用燃油以及船用物料。对其他财产的海事请求保全适用民事诉讼法有关财产保全的规定。

**Article 18** The property of the respondent as referred to in Article 12 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China includes vessels, cargo aboard vessels, ship fuel, and ship stores. For the preservation of maritime claims in respect of other property, the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China on property preservation shall apply.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 海事诉讼特别程序法规定的船载货物指处于承运人掌管之下，尚未装船或者已经装载于船上以及已经卸载的货物。

**Article 19** The "cargo aboard a vessel" as provided in the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means cargo that is under the control of the carrier, whether not yet loaded on board, already loaded on board, or already discharged.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-13)规定的被保全的财产所在地指船舶的所在地或者货物的所在地。当事人在诉讼前对已经卸载但在承运人掌管之下的货物申请海事请求保全，如果货物所在地不在海事法院管辖区域的，可以向卸货港所在地的海事法院提出，也可以向货物所在地的地方人民法院提出。

**Article 20** The place where the preserved property is located as referred to in Article 13 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the location of the vessel or the location of the cargo. If a party applies for preservation of a maritime claim before action in respect of cargo that has been discharged but is still under the control of the carrier, and the place where the cargo is located is not within the jurisdictional area of a maritime court, the application may be filed with the maritime court of the place of the port of discharge or with the local people's court of the place where the cargo is located.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 诉讼或者仲裁前申请海事请求保全适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-14)的规定。外国法院已受理相关海事案件或者有关纠纷已经提交仲裁，但涉案财产在中华人民共和国领域内，当事人向财产所在地的海事法院提出海事请求保全申请的，海事法院应当受理。

**Article 21** An application for preservation of a maritime claim before action or arbitration shall be governed by Article 14 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. Where a relevant maritime case has already been accepted by a foreign court or a relevant dispute has been submitted for arbitration, but the property involved is within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and a party files an application for preservation of a maritime claim with the maritime court of the place where the property is located, the maritime court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 利害关系人对海事法院作出的海事请求保全裁定提出异议，经审查认为理由不成立的，应当书面通知利害关系人。

**Article 22** Where an interested person raises an objection to a ruling on preservation of a maritime claim made by a maritime court, and upon examination, the maritime court finds the grounds to be untenable, it shall notify the interested person in writing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 被请求人或者利害关系人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第二十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-20)的规定要求海事请求人赔偿损失，向采取海事请求保全措施的海事法院提起诉讼的，海事法院应当受理。

**Article 23** Where the respondent or an interested person claims compensation for losses from the claimant of the maritime claim in accordance with Article 20 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and brings an action in the maritime court that adopted the preservation measure, the maritime court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 申请扣押船舶错误造成的损失，包括因船舶被扣押在停泊期间产生的各项维持费用与支出、船舶被扣押造成的船期损失和被申请人为使船舶解除扣押而提供担保所支出的费用。

**Article 24** Losses caused by wrongful application for arrest of a vessel include: various maintenance costs and expenses incurred during the period when the vessel is under arrest, losses of voyage time caused by the arrest, and expenses incurred by the respondent in providing security for the release of the vessel.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 海事请求保全扣押船舶超过三十日、扣押货物或者其他财产超过十五日，海事请求人未提起诉讼或者未按照仲裁协议申请仲裁的，海事法院应当及时解除保全或者返还担保。  
海事请求人未在期限内提起诉讼或者申请仲裁，但海事请求人和被请求人协议进行和解或者协议约定了担保期限的，海事法院可以根据海事请求人的申请，裁定认可该协议。

**Article 25** If the arrest of a vessel for preservation of a maritime claim exceeds thirty days, or the arrest of cargo or other property exceeds fifteen days, and the claimant of the maritime claim has not brought an action or applied for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration agreement, the maritime court shall promptly release the preservation or return the security.  
If the claimant of the maritime claim has not brought an action or applied for arbitration within the time limit, but the claimant and the respondent have agreed to settle the dispute through negotiation or have agreed upon a period for the security, the maritime court may, upon the application of the claimant, render a ruling approving the agreement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 申请人为申请扣押船舶提供限额担保，在扣押船舶期限届满时，未按照海事法院的通知追加担保的，海事法院可以解除扣押。

**Article 26** If an applicant provides a limited amount of security for the application to arrest a vessel, and upon expiration of the period of arrest, fails to provide additional security as notified by the maritime court, the maritime court may release the arrest.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-18)第二款、第七十四条规定的提供给海事请求人的担保，除被请求人和海事请求人有约定的外，海事请求人应当返还；海事请求人不返还担保的，该担保至海事请求保全期间届满之次日失效。

**Article 27** The security provided to the claimant of a maritime claim as referred to in Article 18, Paragraph 2 and Article 74 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall be returned by the claimant unless otherwise agreed between the respondent and the claimant; if the claimant fails to return the security, the security shall cease to be effective on the day following the expiration of the period of preservation of the maritime claim.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 船舶被扣押期间产生的各项维持费用和支出，应当作为为债权人共同利益支出的费用，从拍卖船舶的价款中优先拨付。

**Article 28** All maintenance costs and expenses incurred during the period of arrest of a vessel shall be treated as expenses disbursed for the common benefit of creditors and shall be preferentially paid from the proceeds of the auction of the vessel.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 海事法院根据[海事诉讼特别程序法第二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-27)的规定准许已经实施保全的船舶继续营运的，一般仅限于航行于国内航线上的船舶完成本航次。

**Article 29** Where a maritime court permits a vessel that has been subjected to preservation to continue operation in accordance with Article 27 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, such permission shall generally be limited to allowing a vessel operating on domestic routes to complete its current voyage.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 申请扣押船舶的海事请求人在提起诉讼或者申请仲裁后，不申请拍卖被扣押船舶的，海事法院可以根据被申请人的申请拍卖船舶。拍卖所得价款由海事法院提存。

**Article 30** If the claimant of a maritime claim who has applied for arrest of a vessel, after bringing an action or applying for arbitration, does not apply for auction of the arrested vessel, the maritime court may, upon the application of the respondent, auction the vessel. The proceeds from the auction shall be deposited with the maritime court.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 海事法院裁定拍卖船舶，应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体连续公告三日。

**Article 31** Where a maritime court decides by ruling to auction a vessel, it shall make a public announcement through newspapers or other news media for three consecutive days.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 利害关系人请求终止拍卖被扣押船舶的，是否准许，海事法院应当作出裁定；海事法院裁定终止拍卖船舶的，为准备拍卖船舶所发生的费用由利害关系人承担。

**Article 32** Where an interested person requests termination of an auction of an arrested vessel, the maritime court shall render a ruling on whether to grant the request; if the maritime court rules to terminate the auction, the costs incurred in preparing for the auction shall be borne by the interested person.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 拍卖船舶申请人或者利害关系人申请终止拍卖船舶的，应当在公告确定的拍卖船舶日期届满七日前提出。

**Article 33** An applicant for the auction of a vessel or an interested person who applies for termination of the auction shall file the application at least seven days before the date of the auction as determined in the public announcement.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 海事请求人和被请求人应当按照海事法院的要求提供[海事诉讼特别程序法第三十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-33)规定的已知的船舶优先权人、抵押权人和船舶所有人的有关确切情况。

**Article 34** The claimant of a maritime claim and the respondent shall, as required by the maritime court, provide accurate information concerning the known maritime lien holders, mortgagees, and shipowners as referred to in Article 33 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-38)规定的船舶现状指船舶展示时的状况。船舶交接时的状况与船舶展示时的状况经评估确有明显差别的，船舶价款应当作适当的扣减，但属于正常损耗或者消耗的燃油不在此限。

**Article 35** The current condition of the vessel as referred to in Article 38 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the condition of the vessel at the time of display. If, after appraisal, there is a significant difference between the condition of the vessel at the time of delivery and the condition at the time of display, the vessel price shall be appropriately reduced, except for normal wear and tear or fuel consumed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 海事请求人申请扣押船载货物的价值应当与其请求的债权数额相当，但船载货物为不可分割的财产除外。

**Article 36** The value of cargo aboard a vessel for which the claimant of a maritime claim applies for arrest shall be commensurate with the amount of the claim, except where the cargo aboard the vessel is indivisible property.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 拍卖的船舶移交后，海事法院应当及时通知相关的船舶登记机关。

**Article 37** After the auctioned vessel is delivered, the maritime court shall promptly notify the relevant vessel registration authority.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 海事请求人申请扣押船用燃油、物料的，除适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第五十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-50)的规定外，还可以适用海事诉讼特别程序法第三章第一节的规定。

**Article 38** Where a claimant of a maritime claim applies for arrest of ship fuel or stores, in addition to the application of Article 50 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of Section 1 of Chapter 3 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China may also apply.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 二十总吨以下小型船艇的扣押和拍卖，可以依照民事诉讼法规定的扣押和拍卖程序进行。

**Article 39** The arrest and auction of small vessels under 20 gross tons may be carried out in accordance with the arrest and auction procedures provided by the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 申请人依据[《中华人民共和国海商法》第八十八条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-88)规定申请拍卖留置的货物的，参照海事诉讼特别程序法关于拍卖船载货物的规定执行。  
三、关于海事强制令

**Article 40** Where an applicant applies for auction of cargo subject to a lien in accordance with Article 88 of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China on auction of cargo aboard a vessel shall be applied mutatis mutandis.  
III. Maritime Injunction

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 诉讼或者仲裁前申请海事强制令的，适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-53)的规定。  
外国法院已受理相关海事案件或者有关纠纷已经提交仲裁的，当事人向中华人民共和国的海事法院提出海事强制令申请，并向法院提供可以执行海事强制令的相关证据的，海事法院应当受理。

**Article 41** An application for a maritime injunction before litigation or arbitration shall be governed by Article 53 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a relevant maritime case has already been accepted by a foreign court or a relevant dispute has been submitted for arbitration, and a party files an application for a maritime injunction with a maritime court of the People's Republic of China and provides the court with relevant evidence that the maritime injunction can be enforced, the maritime court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 海事法院根据[海事诉讼特别程序法第五十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-57)规定，准予申请人海事强制令申请的，应当制作民事裁定书并发布海事强制令。

**Article 42** Where a maritime court grants an applicant's application for a maritime injunction in accordance with Article 57 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall prepare a civil ruling and issue a maritime injunction.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条 海事强制令由海事法院执行。被申请人、其他相关单位或者个人不履行海事强制令的，海事法院应当依据民事诉讼法的有关规定强制执行。

**Article 43** A maritime injunction shall be enforced by the maritime court. If the respondent, other relevant entities, or individuals fail to comply with the maritime injunction, the maritime court shall enforce it compulsorily in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条 利害关系人对海事法院作出海事强制令的民事裁定提出异议，海事法院经审查认为理由不成立的，应当书面通知利害关系人。

**Article 44** Where an interested person raises an objection to the civil ruling of the maritime court on a maritime injunction, and upon examination, the maritime court finds the grounds to be untenable, it shall notify the interested person in writing.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条 海事强制令发布后十五日内，被请求人未提出异议，也未就相关的海事纠纷提起诉讼或者申请仲裁的，海事法院可以应中请人的请求，返还其提供的担保。

**Article 45** If, within fifteen days after the issuance of a maritime injunction, the respondent does not raise an objection and does not bring an action or apply for arbitration in relation to the relevant maritime dispute, the maritime court may, upon the request of the applicant, return the security provided by the applicant.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条 被请求人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第六十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-60)的规定要求海事请求人赔偿损失的，由发布海事强制令的海事法院受理。  
四、关于海事证据保全

**Article 46** Where the respondent claims compensation for losses from the claimant of the maritime claim in accordance with Article 60 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court that issued the maritime injunction shall have jurisdiction over the case.  
IV. Preservation of Maritime Evidence

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条 诉讼前申请海事证据保全，适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第六十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-64)的规定。  
外国法院已受理相关海事案件或者有关纠纷已经提交仲裁，当事人向中华人民共和国的海事法院提出海事证据保全申请，并提供被保全的证据在中华人民共和国领域内的相关证据的，海事法院应当受理。

**Article 47** An application for preservation of maritime evidence before action shall be governed by Article 64 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a relevant maritime case has already been accepted by a foreign court or a relevant dispute has been submitted for arbitration, and a party files an application for preservation of maritime evidence with a maritime court of the People's Republic of China and provides relevant evidence that the evidence to be preserved is within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条 海事请求人申请海事证据保全，申请书除应当依照[海事诉讼特别程序法第六十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-65)的规定载明相应内容外，还应当载明证据收集、调取的有关线索。

**Article 48** When a claimant of a maritime claim applies for preservation of maritime evidence, the application shall, in addition to stating the corresponding contents in accordance with Article 65 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, also state the relevant clues for the collection and retrieval of evidence.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条 海事请求人在采取海事证据保全的海事法院提起诉讼后，可以申请复制保全的证据材料；相关海事纠纷在中华人民共和国领域内的其他海事法院或者仲裁机构受理的，受诉法院或者仲裁机构应海事请求人的申请可以申请复制保全的证据材料。

**Article 49** After the claimant of a maritime claim brings an action in the maritime court that carried out the preservation of maritime evidence, the claimant may apply for copies of the preserved evidence materials; if the relevant maritime dispute is accepted by another maritime court or arbitration institution within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the court accepting the case or the arbitration institution may, upon the application of the claimant of the maritime claim, apply for copies of the preserved evidence materials.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条 利害关系人对海事法院作出的海事证据保全裁定提出异议，海事法院经审查认为理由不成立的，应当书面通知利害关系人。

**Article 50** Where an interested person raises an objection to a ruling on preservation of maritime evidence made by a maritime court, and upon examination, the maritime court finds the grounds to be untenable, it shall notify the interested person in writing.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条 被请求人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第七十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-71)的规定要求海事请求人赔偿损失的，由采取海事证据保全的海事法院受理。  
五、关于海事担保

**Article 51** Where the respondent claims compensation for losses from the claimant of the maritime claim in accordance with Article 71 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court that carried out the preservation of maritime evidence shall have jurisdiction over the case.  
V. Maritime Security

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第七十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-77)规定的正当理由指：  
（1）海事请求人请求担保的数额过高；  
（2）被请求人已采取其他有效的担保方式；  
（3）海事请求人的请求权消灭。  
六、关于送达

**Article 52** The "legitimate reasons" as referred to in Article 77 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China mean:  
(1) The amount of security requested by the claimant of the maritime claim is excessively high;  
(2) The respondent has already adopted other effective forms of security;  
(3) The right of claim of the claimant of the maritime claim has been extinguished.  
VI. Service

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条 有关海事强制令、海事证据保全的法律文书可以向当事船舶的船长送达。

**Article 53** Legal documents concerning maritime injunctions and preservation of maritime evidence may be served on the master of the vessel involved.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条 应当向被告送达的开庭传票等法律文书，可以向被扣押的被告船舶的船长送达，但船长作为原告的除外。

**Article 54** Legal documents, such as summons for trial, that shall be served on the defendant may be served on the master of the defendant's arrested vessel, except where the master is the plaintiff.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-80)第一款（三）项规定的其他适当方式包括传真、电子邮件（包括受送达人的专门网址）等送达方式。  
通过以上方式送达的，应确认受送达人确已收悉  
七、关于审判程序

**Article 55** The "other appropriate means" as referred to in Article 80, Paragraph 1, Item (3) of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China include service by facsimile, email (including the special website of the person to be served), and other means.  
Where service is effected by the above means, it shall be confirmed that the person to be served has actually received the documents.  
VII. Adjudication Procedure

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第八十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-84)规定的当事人应当在开庭审理前完成举证的内容，包括当事人按照[海事诉讼特别程序法第八十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-82)的规定填写《海事事故调查表》和提交有关船舶碰撞的事实证据材料。  
前款规定的证据材料，当事人应当在一审开庭前向海事法院提供。

**Article 56** The content of the parties' obligation to complete the presentation of evidence before trial as provided in Article 84 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China includes the parties' filling of the "Maritime Accident Investigation Form" and submission of factual evidence materials concerning the vessel collision in accordance with Article 82 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The evidence materials as referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be provided by the parties to the maritime court before the first-instance trial.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条 《海事事故调查表》属于当事人对发生船舶碰撞基本事实的陈述。经对方当事人认可或者经法院查证属实，可以作为认定事实的依据。

**Article 57** The "Maritime Accident Investigation Form" constitutes a statement by a party of the basic facts of a vessel collision. It may serve as a basis for determining the facts if it is admitted by the opposing party or verified as true by the court.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条 有关船舶碰撞的事实证据材料指涉及船舶碰撞的经过、碰撞原因等方面的证据材料。  
有关船舶碰撞的事实证据材料，在各方当事人完成举证后进行交换。当事人在完成举证前向法院申请查阅有关船舶碰撞的事实证据材料的，海事法院应予驳回。

**Article 58** The factual evidence materials concerning a vessel collision refer to evidence materials involving the course of the collision, the cause of the collision, and other aspects.  
Factual evidence materials concerning a vessel collision shall be exchanged after all parties have completed the presentation of evidence. If a party applies to the court for inspection of factual evidence materials concerning a vessel collision before completing the presentation of evidence, the maritime court shall reject the application.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第八十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-85)规定的新的证据指非当事人所持有，在开庭前尚未掌握或者不能获得，因而在开庭前不能举证的证据。

**Article 59** The "new evidence" as referred to in Article 85 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means evidence that is not in the possession of the party, and which was not available or accessible before the trial, and therefore could not be produced before the trial.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条 因船舶碰撞以外的海事海商案件需要进行船舶检验或者估价的，适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第八十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-86)的规定。

**Article 60** Where a vessel survey or appraisal is required in a maritime case other than a vessel collision case, the provisions of Article 86 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条 依据[《中华人民共和国海商法》第一百七十条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-170)的规定提起的诉讼和因船舶触碰造成损害提起的诉讼，参照海事诉讼特别程序法关于审理船舶碰撞案件的有关规定审理。

**Article 61** Actions brought in accordance with Article 170 of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China and actions brought for damages caused by vessel contact shall be tried with reference to the relevant provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China on the trial of vessel collision cases.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条 未经理算的共同海损纠纷诉至海事法院的，海事法院应责令当事人自行委托共同海损理算。确有必要由海事法院委托理算的，由当事人提出申请，委托理算的费用由主张共同海损的当事人垫付。

**Article 62** Where a dispute over general average not yet adjusted is brought before a maritime court, the maritime court shall order the parties to entrust a general average adjustment on their own. If it is truly necessary for the maritime court to entrust the adjustment, the parties shall file an application, and the costs of the entrusted adjustment shall be advanced by the party claiming general average.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条 当事人对共同海损理算报告提出异议，经海事法院审查异议成立，需要补充理算或者重新理算的，应当由原委托人通知理算人进行理算。原委托人不通知理算的，海事法院可以通知理算人重新理算，有关费用由异议人垫付；异议人拒绝垫付费用的，视为撤销异议。

**Article 63** Where a party raises an objection to a general average adjustment report, and upon examination by the maritime court, the objection is found to be justified, and a supplementary adjustment or a new adjustment is necessary, the original principal shall notify the adjuster to carry out the adjustment. If the original principal fails to notify the adjuster, the maritime court may notify the adjuster to carry out a new adjustment, and the relevant costs shall be advanced by the objecting party; if the objecting party refuses to advance the costs, the objection shall be deemed withdrawn.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条 因与共同海损纠纷有关的非共同海损损失向责任人提起的诉讼，适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第九十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-92)规定的审限。

**Article 64** An action brought against the person liable for non-general average losses related to a general average dispute shall be subject to the time limit for trial as provided in Article 92 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条 保险人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第九十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-95)规定行使代位请求赔偿权利，应当以自己的名义进行；以他人名义提起诉讼的，海事法院应不予受理或者驳回起诉。

**Article 65** Where an insurer exercises the right of subrogation for compensation in accordance with Article 95 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall do so in its own name; if it brings an action in the name of another person, the maritime court shall not accept the case or shall dismiss the action.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条 保险人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第九十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-95)的规定请求变更当事人或者请求作为共同原告参加诉讼的，海事法院应当予以审查并作出是否准予的裁定。当事人对裁定不服的，可以提起上诉。

**Article 66** Where an insurer requests a change of party or requests to participate in the proceedings as a co-plaintiff in accordance with Article 95 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court shall examine the request and render a ruling on whether to grant it. If a party is dissatisfied with the ruling, it may appeal.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条 保险人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第九十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-95)的规定参加诉讼的，被保险人依此前进行的诉讼行为所取得的财产保全或者通过扣押取得的担保权益等，在保险人的代位请求赔偿权利范围内对保险人有效。被保险人因自身过错产生的责任，保险人不予承担。

**Article 67** Where an insurer participates in the proceedings in accordance with Article 95 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the property preservation or security interests obtained by the insured through prior litigation acts or through arrest, etc., shall be valid in favor of the insurer within the scope of the insurer's right of subrogation for compensation. The insurer shall not assume any liability arising from the negligence of the insured.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第九十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-96)规定的支付保险赔偿的凭证指赔偿金收据、银行支付单据或者其他支付凭证。仅有被保险人出具的权利转让书但不能出具实际支付证明的，不能作为保险人取得代位请求赔偿权利的事实依据。

**Article 68** The certificate of payment of insurance compensation as referred to in Article 96 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the receipt for compensation, bank payment document, or other payment instrument. If the insured only issues a subrogation receipt but cannot produce actual payment evidence, it shall not serve as factual basis for the insurer to acquire the right of subrogation for compensation.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条 海事法院根据油污损害的保险人或者提供财务保证的其他人的请求，可以通知船舶所有人作为无独立请求权的第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 69** A maritime court may, upon the request of the insurer for oil pollution damage or another person providing financial security, notify the shipowner to participate in the proceedings as a third party with no independent claim.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-100)规定的失控指提单或者其他提货凭证被盗、遗失。

**Article 70** The term "lost control" as referred to in Article 100 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means that the bill of lading or other document of title to the goods is stolen or lost.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条 申请人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-100)的规定向海事法院申请公示催告的，应当递交申请书。申请书应当载明：提单等提货凭证的种类、编号、货物品名、数量、承运人、托运人、收货人、承运船舶名称、航次以及背书情况和申请的理由、事实等。有副本的应当附有单证的副本。

**Article 71** Where an applicant applies to a maritime court for a public summons for exhortation in accordance with Article 100 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the applicant shall submit a written application. The application shall state: the type and number of the bill of lading or other document of title to the goods, the name and quantity of the goods, the carrier, the shipper, the consignee, the name and voyage number of the carrying vessel, the endorsement status, and the reasons and facts for the application, etc. If there is a duplicate, a copy of the document shall be attached.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条 海事法院决定受理公示催告申请的，应当同时通知承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人停止交付货物，并于三日内发出公告，敦促利害关系人申报权利。公示催告的期间由海事法院根据情况决定，但不得少于三十日。

**Article 72** Where a maritime court decides to accept an application for a public summons for exhortation, it shall simultaneously notify the carrier, the carrier's agent, or the custodian of the goods to stop delivery of the goods, and shall issue a public announcement within three days, urging interested persons to claim their rights. The period of the public summons for exhortation shall be determined by the maritime court based on the circumstances, but shall not be less than thirty days.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条 承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人收到海事法院停止交付货物的通知后，应当停止交付，至公示催告程序终结。

**Article 73** Upon receipt of the notice from the maritime court to stop delivery of the goods, the carrier, the carrier's agent, or the custodian of the goods shall stop delivery until the conclusion of the public summons for exhortation procedure.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条 公示催告期间，转让提单的行为无效；有关货物的存储保管费用及风险由申请人承担。

**Article 74** During the period of the public summons for exhortation, any transfer of the bill of lading shall be invalid; the costs of storage and custody of the relevant goods and the risks thereof shall be borne by the applicant.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条 公示催告期间，国家重点建设项目待安装、施工、生产的货物，救灾物资，或者货物本身属性不宜长期保管以及季节性货物，在申请人提供充分可靠担保的情况下，海事法院可以依据申请人的申请作出由申请人提取货物的裁定。  
承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人收到海事法院准予提取货物的裁定后，应当依据裁定的指令将货物交付给指定的人。

**Article 75** During the period of the public summons for exhortation, for goods to be installed, constructed, or produced for key national construction projects, disaster relief materials, or goods whose nature makes them unsuitable for long-term storage, or seasonal goods, the maritime court may, upon the applicant's application and provided that the applicant provides sufficient and reliable security, render a ruling allowing the applicant to take delivery of the goods.  
After receiving the ruling of the maritime court granting permission to take delivery of the goods, the carrier, the carrier's agent, or the custodian of the goods shall deliver the goods to the designated person in accordance with the instructions in the ruling.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条 公示催告期间，利害关系人可以向海事法院申报权利。海事法院收到利害关系人的申报后，应当裁定终结公示催告程序，并通知申请人和承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人。  
申请人、申报人可以就有关纠纷向海事法院提起诉讼。

**Article 76** During the period of public notice for assertion of claims, an interested party may claim its rights with the maritime court. After receiving the claim from the interested party, the maritime court shall render an order to terminate the public notice procedure for assertion of claims, and shall notify the applicant, the carrier, the carrier's agent or the cargo custodian.  
The applicant or the claimant may institute an action with the maritime court in respect of the relevant disputes.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条 公示催告期间无人申报的，海事法院应当根据申请人的申请作出判决，宣告提单或者有关提货凭证无效。判决内容应当公告，并通知承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人。自判决公告之日起，申请人有权请求承运人、承运人的代理人或者货物保管人交付货物。

**Article 77** If no claim is made during the period of public notice for assertion of claims, the maritime court shall, upon the application of the applicant, render a judgment declaring the bill of lading or relevant document for taking delivery of the goods null and void. The content of the judgment shall be announced, and shall be notified to the carrier, the carrier's agent or the cargo custodian. From the date of announcement of the judgment, the applicant shall have the right to demand the carrier, the carrier's agent or the cargo custodian to deliver the goods.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条 利害关系人因正当理由不能在公示催告期间向海事法院申报的，自知道或者应当知道判决公告之日起一年内，可以向作出判决的海事法院起诉。  
八、关于设立海事赔偿责任限制基金程序

**Article 78** If an interested party, for justifiable reasons, is unable to make a claim with the maritime court during the period of public notice for assertion of claims, it may, within one year from the date when it knows or should have known the announcement of the judgment, institute an action with the maritime court that rendered the judgment.  
VIII. Procedures for Constitution of Limitation Fund for Maritime Claims

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-101)规定的船舶所有人指有关船舶证书上载明的船舶所有人。

**Article 79** The shipowner as referred to in Article 101 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the shipowner registered on the relevant ship certificate.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条 海事事故发生在中华人民共和国领域外的，船舶发生事故后进入中华人民共和国领域内的第一到达港视为[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-102)规定的事故发生地。

**Article 80** Where a maritime accident occurs outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, the first port of arrival within the territory of the People's Republic of China after the accident shall be deemed the place where the accident occurred as prescribed in Article 102 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条 当事人在诉讼中申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，应当向受理相关海事纠纷案件的海事法院提出，但当事人之间订有有效诉讼管辖协议或者仲裁协议的除外。

**Article 81** Where a party applies for constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims in the course of litigation, the application shall be filed with the maritime court that accepts the relevant maritime dispute case, unless the parties have concluded a valid litigation jurisdiction agreement or arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条 设立海事赔偿责任限制基金应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体连续公告三日。如果涉及的船舶是可以航行于国际航线的，应当通过对外发行的报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告。

**Article 82** The constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims shall be announced through newspapers or other news media for three consecutive days. If the vessel involved is capable of navigating on international routes, the announcement shall be published through newspapers or other news media distributed overseas.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条 利害关系人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-106)的规定对申请人设立海事赔偿责任限制基金提出异议的，海事法院应当对设立基金申请人的主体资格、事故所涉及的债权性质和申请设立基金的数额进行审查。

**Article 83** Where an interested party raises an objection to the applicant's constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims in accordance with Article 106 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court shall examine the applicant's legal personality for constituting the fund, the nature of the claims involved in the accident, and the amount of the fund applied for.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条 准予申请人设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的裁定生效后，申请人应当在三日内在海事法院设立海事赔偿责任限制基金。申请人逾期未设立基金的，按自动撤回申请处理。

**Article 84** After the order approving the applicant's constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims takes effect, the applicant shall constitute the limitation fund for maritime claims with the maritime court within three days. If the applicant fails to constitute the fund within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn automatically.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-108)规定的担保指中华人民共和国境内的银行或者其他金融机构所出具的担保。

**Article 85** The security as referred to in Article 108 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the security issued by a bank or other financial institution within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条 设立海事赔偿责任限制基金后，向基金提出请求的任何人，不得就该项索赔对设立或以其名义设立基金的人的任何其他财产，行使任何权利。  
九、关于债权登记与受偿程序

**Article 86** After the constitution of a limitation fund for maritime claims, no person who makes a claim against the fund may exercise any right against any other property of the person constituting the fund or on whose behalf the fund is constituted in respect of such claim.  
IX. Procedures for Registration and Satisfaction of Claims

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-111)规定的与被拍卖船舶有关的债权指与被拍卖船舶有关的海事债权。

**Article 87** The claims relating to the ship to be auctioned as referred to in Article 111 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China mean the maritime claims relating to the ship to be auctioned.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-115)规定的判决书、裁定书、调解书和仲裁裁决书指我国国内的判决书、裁定书、调解书和仲裁裁决书。对于债权人提供的国外的判决书、裁定书、调解书和仲裁裁决书，适用[民事诉讼法第二百六十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-268)和第二百六十九条规定的程序审查。

**Article 88** The judgments, orders, mediation statements and arbitration awards as referred to in Article 115 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China mean the domestic judgments, orders, mediation statements and arbitration awards of China. For foreign judgments, orders, mediation statements and arbitration awards provided by a creditor, the procedures for examination as prescribed in Articles 268 and 269 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条 在债权登记前，债权人已向受理债权登记的海事法院以外的海事法院起诉的，受理案件的海事法院应当将案件移送至登记债权的海事法院一并审理，但案件已经进人二审的除外。

**Article 89** Where, before the registration of claims, a creditor has instituted an action with a maritime court other than the one accepting the registration of claims, the maritime court accepting the case shall transfer the case to the maritime court where the claims are registered for consolidated trial, unless the case has already entered the second instance.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条 债权人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-116)规定向受理债权登记的海事法院提起确权诉讼的，应当在办理债权登记后七日内提起。

**Article 90** Where a creditor institutes an action for confirmation of rights with the maritime court accepting the registration of claims in accordance with Article 116 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, such action shall be instituted within seven days after the registration of the claim.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-119)第二款规定的三项费用按顺序拨付。  
十、关于船舶优先权催告程序

**Article 91** The three types of expenses as prescribed in the second paragraph of Article 119 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall be paid in order.  
X. Procedures for Public Notice Calling for Maritime Liens

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条 船舶转让合同订立后船舶实际交付前，受让人即可申请船舶优先权催告。  
受让人不能提供原船舶证书的，不影响船舶优先权催告申请的提出。

**Article 92** After the conclusion of a ship transfer contract but before the actual delivery of the ship, the transferee may apply for a public notice calling for maritime liens.  
The fact that the transferee cannot provide the original ship certificate shall not affect the filing of an application for a public notice calling for maritime liens.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条 [海事诉讼特别程序法第一百二十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-120)规定的受让人指船舶转让中的买方和有买船意向的人，但受让人申请海事法院作出除权判决时，必须提交其已经实际受让船舶的证据。

**Article 93** The transferee as referred to in Article 120 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China means the buyer in a ship transfer and a person intending to purchase a ship; provided that when the transferee applies to the maritime court for a judgment discharging rights, it must submit evidence that it has actually taken over the ship.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条 船舶受让人对不准予船舶优先权催告申请的裁定提出复议的，海事法院应当在七日内作出复议决定。

**Article 94** Where a ship transferee applies for reconsideration of an order disallowing the application for a public notice calling for maritime liens, the maritime court shall make a reconsideration decision within seven days.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条 海事法院准予船舶优先权催告申请的裁定生效后，应当通过报纸或者其他新闻媒体连续公告三日。优先权催告的船舶为可以航行于国际航线的，应当通过对外发行的报纸或者其他新闻媒体发布公告。

**Article 95** After the order of the maritime court approving the application for a public notice calling for maritime liens takes effect, it shall be announced through newspapers or other news media for three consecutive days. If the ship subject to the public notice calling for maritime liens is capable of navigating on international routes, the announcement shall be published through newspapers or other news media distributed overseas.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条 利害关系人在船舶优先权催告期间提出优先权主张的，海事法院应当裁定优先权催告程序终结。  
十一、其他

**Article 96** If an interested party asserts a maritime lien during the period of the public notice calling for maritime liens, the maritime court shall order the termination of the public notice calling for maritime liens.  
XI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条 在中华人民共和国领域内进行海事诉讼，适用海事诉讼特别程序法的规定。海事诉讼特别程序法没有规定的，适用民事诉讼法的有关规定。

**Article 97** Maritime litigation conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. Matters not provided for in the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.
