# 最高人民法院关于审理民事案件适用诉讼时效制度若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕11号  
**公布日期**：2008-08-21  
**生效日期**：2008-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理民事案件适用诉讼时效制度若干问题的规定》已于2008年8月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1450次会议通过，现予公布，自2008年9月1日起施行。 2008年8月21日 最高人民法院 关于审理民事案件适用诉讼 时效制度若干问题的规定 法释〔2008〕11号 （2008年8月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1450次会议通过　2008年8月21日最高人民法院公告公布　自2008年9月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人可以对债权请求权提出诉讼时效抗辩，但对下列债权请求权提出诉讼时效抗辩的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）支付存款本金及利息请求权；  
（二）兑付国债、金融债券以及向不特定对象发行的企业债券本息请求权；  
（三）基于投资关系产生的缴付出资请求权；  
（四）其他依法不适用诉讼时效规定的债权请求权。

**Article 1** A party may raise a limitation of action defense against a claim for creditor's rights, but if a party raises such a defense against any of the following claims for creditor's rights, the People's Court shall not support it:  
(1) a claim for payment of principal and interest on deposits;  
(2) a claim for payment of principal and interest on treasury bonds, financial bonds, and corporate bonds issued to unspecified persons;  
(3) a claim for contribution of capital arising from an investment relationship;  
(4) other claims for creditor's rights to which the provisions on limitation of action are not applicable according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人违反法律规定，约定延长或者缩短诉讼时效期间、预先放弃诉讼时效利益的，人民法院不予认可。

**Article 2** Where a party violates the provisions of law and agrees to extend or shorten the limitation period, or waives in advance the benefits of limitation of action, the People's Court shall not recognize such agreement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人未提出诉讼时效抗辩，人民法院不应对诉讼时效问题进行释明及主动适用诉讼时效的规定进行裁判。

**Article 3** If a party has not raised a limitation of action defense, the People's Court shall not interpret the issue of limitation of action or proactively apply the provisions on limitation of action to adjudicate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人在一审期间未提出诉讼时效抗辩，在二审期间提出的，人民法院不予支持，但其基于新的证据能够证明对方当事人的请求权已过诉讼时效期间的情形除外。  
当事人未按照前款规定提出诉讼时效抗辩，以诉讼时效期间届满为由申请再审或者提出再审抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** If a party fails to raise a limitation of action defense during the first instance, but raises it during the second instance, the People's Court shall not support it, except where the party can prove based on new evidence that the opposing party's claim has exceeded the limitation period.  
If a party fails to raise a limitation of action defense in accordance with the preceding paragraph, and applies for retrial or raises a retrial defense on the ground that the limitation period has expired, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人约定同一债务分期履行的，诉讼时效期间从最后一期履行期限届满之日起计算。

**Article 5** Where the parties agree that the same obligation shall be performed in installments, the limitation period shall run from the date on which the performance period for the last installment expires.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　未约定履行期限的合同，依照合同法第六十一条、第六十二条的规定，可以确定履行期限的，诉讼时效期间从履行期限届满之日起计算；不能确定履行期限的，诉讼时效期间从债权人要求债务人履行义务的宽限期届满之日起计算，但债务人在债权人第一次向其主张权利之时明确表示不履行义务的，诉讼时效期间从债务人明确表示不履行义务之日起计算。

**Article 6** For a contract without an agreed performance period, if the performance period can be determined in accordance with Articles 61 and 62 of the Contract Law, the limitation period shall run from the date on which the performance period expires; if the performance period cannot be determined, the limitation period shall run from the date on which the grace period set by the creditor for the debtor to perform the obligation expires, except that where the debtor expressly indicates non-performance at the time of the creditor's first demand for performance, the limitation period shall run from the date on which the debtor expressly indicates non-performance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　享有撤销权的当事人一方请求撤销合同的，应适用合同法第五十五条关于一年除斥期间的规定。对方当事人对撤销合同请求权提出诉讼时效抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
合同被撤销，返还财产、赔偿损失请求权的诉讼时效期间从合同被撤销之日起计算。

**Article 7** Where a party entitled to the right of revocation requests revocation of a contract, the provisions of Article 55 of the Contract Law concerning the one-year preclusive period shall apply. If the opposing party raises a limitation of action defense against the claim for revocation of the contract, the People's Court shall not support it.  
Where a contract is revoked, the limitation period for the claim for restitution of property and compensation for losses shall run from the date on which the contract is revoked.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　返还不当得利请求权的诉讼时效期间，从当事人一方知道或者应当知道不当得利事实及对方当事人之日起计算。

**Article 8** The limitation period for a claim for restitution of unjust enrichment shall run from the date on which a party knows or should have known the facts of unjust enrichment and the opposing party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　管理人因无因管理行为产生的给付必要管理费用、赔偿损失请求权的诉讼时效期间，从无因管理行为结束并且管理人知道或者应当知道本人之日起计算。  
本人因不当无因管理行为产生的赔偿损失请求权的诉讼时效期间，从其知道或者应当知道管理人及损害事实之日起计算。

**Article 9** The limitation period for a manager's claim arising from negotiorum gestio for payment of necessary management expenses and compensation for losses shall run from the date on which the negotiorum gestio ends and the manager knows or should have known the principal.  
The limitation period for the principal's claim for compensation for losses arising from improper negotiorum gestio shall run from the date on which the principal knows or should have known the manager and the fact of damage.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为民法通则第一百四十条规定的“当事人一方提出要求”，产生诉讼时效中断的效力：  
（一）当事人一方直接向对方当事人送交主张权利文书，对方当事人在文书上签字、盖章或者虽未签字、盖章但能够以其他方式证明该文书到达对方当事人的；  
（二）当事人一方以发送信件或者数据电文方式主张权利，信件或者数据电文到达或者应当到达对方当事人的；  
（三）当事人一方为金融机构，依照法律规定或者当事人约定从对方当事人账户中扣收欠款本息的；  
（四）当事人一方下落不明，对方当事人在国家级或者下落不明的当事人一方住所地的省级有影响的媒体上刊登具有主张权利内容的公告的，但法律和司法解释另有特别规定的，适用其规定。  
前款第（一）项情形中，对方当事人为法人或者其他组织的，签收人可以是其法定代表人、主要负责人、负责收发信件的部门或者被授权主体；对方当事人为自然人的，签收人可以是自然人本人、同住的具有完全行为能力的亲属或者被授权主体。

**Article 10** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "a party makes a demand" as provided in Article 140 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, which shall have the effect of interrupting the limitation of action:  
(1) a party directly delivers a document asserting its rights to the opposing party, and the opposing party signs or affixes its seal on the document, or, although not signing or affixing its seal, it can be proved by other means that the document has reached the opposing party;  
(2) a party asserts its rights by sending a letter or data message, and the letter or data message reaches or should have reached the opposing party;  
(3) a party is a financial institution and, in accordance with legal provisions or the agreement of the parties, deducts overdue principal and interest from the opposing party's account;  
(4) where a party's whereabouts are unknown, the opposing party publishes an announcement containing the content of asserting rights in a national-level media or in a provincial-level influential media in the place of domicile of the missing party, except where laws and judicial interpretations provide otherwise, in which case such provisions shall apply.  
In the circumstance under subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph, if the opposing party is a legal person or other organization, the recipient may be its legal representative, principal person in charge, the department responsible for receiving and dispatching correspondence, or an authorized person; if the opposing party is a natural person, the recipient may be the natural person himself, a family member living with him who has full capacity for civil conduct, or an authorized person.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　权利人对同一债权中的部分债权主张权利，诉讼时效中断的效力及于剩余债权，但权利人明确表示放弃剩余债权的情形除外。

**Article 11** Where a right holder asserts a right over part of a creditor's right in the same obligation, the effect of interruption of the limitation of action shall extend to the remainder of the creditor's right, except where the right holder expressly waives the remainder of the creditor's right.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人一方向人民法院提交起诉状或者口头起诉的，诉讼时效从提交起诉状或者口头起诉之日起中断。

**Article 12** Where a party submits a complaint to the People's Court or files an oral complaint, the limitation of action shall be interrupted from the date of submission of the complaint or oral complaint.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下列事项之一，人民法院应当认定与提起诉讼具有同等诉讼时效中断的效力：  
（一）申请仲裁；  
（二）申请支付令；  
（三）申请破产、申报破产债权；  
（四）为主张权利而申请宣告义务人失踪或死亡；  
（五）申请诉前财产保全、诉前临时禁令等诉前措施；  
（六）申请强制执行；  
（七）申请追加当事人或者被通知参加诉讼；  
（八）在诉讼中主张抵消；  
（九）其他与提起诉讼具有同等诉讼时效中断效力的事项。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall deem any of the following matters as having the same effect of interrupting the limitation of action as the initiation of a lawsuit:  
(1) applying for arbitration;  
(2) applying for a payment order;  
(3) applying for bankruptcy or declaring creditor's rights in bankruptcy;  
(4) applying for a declaration that the obligor is missing or dead for the purpose of asserting a right;  
(5) applying for pre-litigation property preservation, pre-litigation temporary injunction, or other pre-litigation measures;  
(6) applying for compulsory execution;  
(7) applying for the addition of a party or being notified to participate in a lawsuit;  
(8) asserting a set-off in a lawsuit;  
(9) other matters having the same effect of interrupting the limitation of action as the initiation of a lawsuit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　权利人向人民调解委员会以及其他依法有权解决相关民事纠纷的国家机关、事业单位、社会团体等社会组织提出保护相应民事权利的请求，诉讼时效从提出请求之日起中断。

**Article 14** Where a right holder makes a request for protection of the relevant civil right to the People's Mediation Committee or other state organs, institutions, social organizations, or other social organizations that are legally authorized to resolve relevant civil disputes, the limitation of action shall be interrupted from the date on which the request is made.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　权利人向公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院报案或者控告，请求保护其民事权利的，诉讼时效从其报案或者控告之日起中断。  
上述机关决定不立案、撤销案件、不起诉的，诉讼时效期间从权利人知道或者应当知道不立案、撤销案件或者不起诉之日起重新计算；刑事案件进入审理阶段，诉讼时效期间从刑事裁判文书生效之日起重新计算。

**Article 15** Where a right holder reports a case to or lodges a complaint with the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate, or the People's Court, requesting protection of its civil rights, the limitation of action shall be interrupted from the date of such report or complaint.  
If the aforesaid organ decides not to file a case, to withdraw the case, or not to prosecute, the limitation period shall be recalculated from the date on which the right holder knows or should have known of such decision; if the criminal case enters the trial stage, the limitation period shall be recalculated from the date on which the criminal judgment or order takes effect.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　义务人作出分期履行、部分履行、提供担保、请求延期履行、制定清偿债务计划等承诺或者行为的，应当认定为民法通则第一百四十条规定的当事人一方“同意履行义务”。

**Article 16** Where an obligor makes a commitment or engages in conduct such as installment performance, partial performance, providing a guarantee, requesting extension of performance, or formulating a debt repayment plan, it shall be deemed that the obligor has "agreed to perform the obligation" as provided in Article 140 of the General Principles of the Civil Law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于连带债权人中的一人发生诉讼时效中断效力的事由，应当认定对其他连带债权人也发生诉讼时效中断的效力。  
对于连带债务人中的一人发生诉讼时效中断效力的事由，应当认定对其他连带债务人也发生诉讼时效中断的效力。

**Article 17** Where a cause of interruption of the limitation of action occurs with respect to one of the joint creditors, it shall be deemed that the interruption of the limitation of action also occurs with respect to the other joint creditors.  
Where a cause of interruption of the limitation of action occurs with respect to one of the joint debtors, it shall be deemed that the interruption of the limitation of action also occurs with respect to the other joint debtors.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　债权人提起代位权诉讼的，应当认定对债权人的债权和债务人的债权均发生诉讼时效中断的效力。

**Article 18** Where a creditor initiates a subrogation lawsuit, it shall be deemed that the interruption of the limitation of action occurs with respect to both the creditor's creditor's right and the debtor's creditor's right.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　债权转让的，应当认定诉讼时效从债权转让通知到达债务人之日起中断。  
债务承担情形下，构成原债务人对债务承认的，应当认定诉讼时效从债务承担意思表示到达债权人之日起中断。

**Article 19** Where a creditor's right is assigned, it shall be deemed that the limitation of action is interrupted from the date on which the notice of assignment reaches the debtor.  
In the case of assumption of debt, if the original debtor acknowledges the debt, it shall be deemed that the limitation of action is interrupted from the date on which the expression of intention to assume the debt reaches the creditor.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，应当认定为民法通则第一百三十九条规定的“其他障碍”，诉讼时效中止：  
（一）权利被侵害的无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人没有法定代理人，或者法定代理人死亡、丧失代理权、丧失行为能力；  
（二）继承开始后未确定继承人或者遗产管理人；  
（三）权利人被义务人或者其他人控制无法主张权利；  
（四）其他导致权利人不能主张权利的客观情形。

**Article 20** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "other obstacles" as provided in Article 139 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and the limitation of action shall be suspended:  
(1) a person with no capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct whose rights have been infringed has no legal agent, or the legal agent dies, loses the power of agency, or loses capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) after the commencement of succession, the successor or estate administrator has not been determined;  
(3) the right holder is controlled by the obligor or another person and is unable to assert its rights;  
(4) other objective circumstances preventing the right holder from asserting its rights.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　主债务诉讼时效期间届满，保证人享有主债务人的诉讼时效抗辩权。  
保证人未主张前述诉讼时效抗辩权，承担保证责任后向主债务人行使追偿权的，人民法院不予支持，但主债务人同意给付的情形除外。

**Article 21** Where the limitation period for the principal obligation has expired, the guarantor shall enjoy the limitation of action defense right of the principal debtor.  
If the guarantor fails to assert the aforesaid limitation of action defense right and, after assuming the suretyship liability, exercises the right of recourse against the principal debtor, the People's Court shall not support it, except where the principal debtor agrees to make payment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　诉讼时效期间届满，当事人一方向对方当事人作出同意履行义务的意思表示或者自愿履行义务后，又以诉讼时效期间届满为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 22** Where the limitation period has expired, and a party makes a manifestation of intention to agree to perform the obligation to the opposing party or voluntarily performs the obligation, the People's Court shall not support a subsequent defense based on the expiration of the limitation period.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定施行后，案件尚在一审或者二审阶段的，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审的案件，人民法院进行再审时，不适用本规定。

**Article 23** After these Provisions come into force, if a case is still in the first instance or second instance stage, these Provisions shall apply; for cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, when the People's Court conducts a retrial, these Provisions shall not apply.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本规定施行前本院作出的有关司法解释与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。

**Article 24** Where any relevant judicial interpretation made by this Court before these Provisions come into force conflicts with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
