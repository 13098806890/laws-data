### 最高人民法院关于上级人民法院发现下级人民法院对没有严重妨害民事诉讼行为的当事人采取的强制措施能否纠正问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于上级人民法院发现下级人民法院  
  
对没有严重妨害民事诉讼行为的当事人  
  
采取的强制措施能否纠正问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔 1986〕 14号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院苏法民〔 1985〕137号请示收悉。关于上级人民法院发现下级人民法院对没有严重妨害民事诉讼行为的当事人采取的拘留强制措施，能否纠正和纠正的方法问题，经我们研究认为：根据人民法院组织法关于下级人民法院的审判工作受上级人民法院监督的规定，上级人民法院如果发现下级人民法院采取拘留强制措施不当时，应及时予以纠正。纠正的方法，可以口头或者书面通知下级人民法院纠正，也可以使用决定书，撤销下级人民法院的拘留决定，解除强制措施。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年四月二日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a Higher People's Court May Correct the Compulsory Measures Taken by a Lower People's Court Against a Party Who Has Not Committed a Grave Act of Obstructing Civil Action  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1986] No. 14  
Higher People's Court of Jiangsu Province:  
Your petition, Su Fa Min [1985] No. 137, has been received. With respect to the question of whether a higher people's court may correct the detention compulsory measure taken by a lower people's court against a party who has not committed a grave act of obstructing civil action, and the methods of correction, our study concludes as follows: In accordance with the provision of the Organic Law of the People's Courts that the adjudicative work of a lower people's court shall be subject to the supervision of the higher people's court, if a higher people's court finds that the detention compulsory measure taken by a lower people's court is improper, it shall promptly correct it. The methods of correction may be to notify the lower people's court to correct orally or in writing, or to use a written decision to revoke the detention decision of the lower people's court and lift the compulsory measure.  
This is herewith replied.  
April 2, 1986
