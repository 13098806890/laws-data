### 最高人民法院关于第二审人民法院因追加、更换当事人发回重审的民事裁定书上，应如何列当事人问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于第二审人民法院因追加、更换当事人发回重审  
  
的民事裁定书上，应如何列当事人问题的批复  
  
法民〔1990〕8号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鲁法（经）函（ 1990）19号《关于在二审追加当事人后调解不成发回重审的民事裁定书上，是否列上被追加的当事人问题的请示报告》收悉。经研究，我们认为：  
  
第二审人民法院审理需要追加或更换当事人的案件，如调解不成，应发回重审。在发回重审的民事裁定书上，不应列被追加或更换的当事人。  
  
1990年4月14日

**Article 1** Reply on the Issue of How to List Parties in a Civil Ruling on Remand for Retrial  
by the Second Instance People's Court Due to Addition or Replacement of Parties  
Fa Min [1990] No. 8  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your court's Lu Fa (Jing) Han (1990) No. 19, titled 'Request for Instructions on Whether to List the Added Parties in a Civil Ruling on Remand for Retrial After Mediation Fails Following Addition of Parties in Second Instance,' has been received. Upon study, we are of the opinion that:  
When the second instance People's Court hears a case requiring addition or replacement of parties, if mediation fails, the case shall be remanded for retrial. In the civil ruling on remand for retrial, the added or replaced parties shall not be listed.  
April 14, 1990
