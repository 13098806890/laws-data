# 最高人民法院关于人民法院已生效的法律文书是否适用民事诉讼法（试行）第一百六十九条规定的申请执行期限等问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1988-08-15

---

### 正文

一、人民法院已生效的法律文书，当事人一方拒绝履行的，对方当事人可以向人民法院申请执行，其申请执行的期限适用民事诉讼法（试行）第一百六十九条的规定。

**Article None** 1. Where a party refuses to comply with a legally effective legal document of the People's Court, the other party may apply to the People's Court for enforcement, and the time limit for such application shall be governed by the provisions of Article 169 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation).

二、人民法院已生效的具有给付赡养费、扶养费、抚育费内容的法律文书，含有财产执行内容的刑事法律文书，以及审判员认为其他确属应当移送执行的法律文书，当事人一方拒绝履行的，审判员应当移送执行。除此之外的法律文书，人民法院在送达或者宣判时，应当告知当事人有关申请执行问题的法律规定。  
此复  
1988年8月15日

**Article None** 2. Where a party refuses to comply with a legally effective legal document of the People's Court that contains provisions for payment of alimony, support allowance, or child-rearing expenses, a criminal legal document that contains property enforcement contents, and other legal documents that the adjudicator considers should indeed be transferred for enforcement, the adjudicator shall transfer for enforcement. For legal documents other than the above, when serving the document or pronouncing the judgment, the People's Court shall inform the party of the legal provisions concerning the application for enforcement.  
This reply  
August 15, 1988
