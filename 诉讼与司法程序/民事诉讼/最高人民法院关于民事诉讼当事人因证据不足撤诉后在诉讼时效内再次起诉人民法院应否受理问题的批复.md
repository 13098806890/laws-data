### 最高人民法院关于民事诉讼当事人因证据不足撤诉后在诉讼时效内再次起诉人民法院应否受理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院批复  
  
关于民事诉讼当事人因证据不足  
  
撤诉后在诉讼时效内再次起诉  
  
人民法院应否受理问题的批复  
  
法（民）复〔1990〕3号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院（89）沪高民他字第5号关于张珠英诉彭绍安债务纠纷案撤诉后能否再立案受理的请示报告收悉。  
  
经研究，我们同意你院审判委员会的意见。原告张珠英以暂因证据不足为由申请撤诉，在第一审人民法院裁定准许其撤诉后，张珠英在诉讼时效期间内又提出新的证据再行起诉，人民法院应予受理。  
  
1990年3月10日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court  
on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept a Case Where a Party in a Civil Action  
Withdraws the Prosecution Due to Insufficient Evidence  
and Subsequently Files a New Action Within the Limitation of Action  
Fa (Min) Fu [1990] No. 3  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
Your request for instructions, (89) Hu Gao Min Ta Zi No. 5, regarding whether a new case may be filed after withdrawal in the debt dispute case of Zhang Zhuying v. Peng Shaoan, has been received.  
Upon study, we agree with the opinion of the Adjudication Committee of your court. Where the plaintiff Zhang Zhuying filed an application to withdraw the prosecution on the ground of temporarily insufficient evidence, and after the first-instance People's Court rendered a ruling permitting such withdrawal, Zhang Zhuying submits new evidence and files a new action within the period of limitation of action, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
March 10, 1990
