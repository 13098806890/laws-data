### 最高人民法院关于对被判处死刑的被告人未提出上诉、共同犯罪的部分被告人或者附带民事诉讼原告人提出上诉的案件应适用何种程序审理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>中华人民共和国最高人民法院  
  
公    告  
  
最高人民法院《关于对被判处死刑的被告人未提出上诉、共同犯罪的部分被告人或者附带民事诉讼原告人提出上诉的案件应适用何种程序审理的批复》已于2010年3月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1485次会议通过。现予公布，自2010年4月1日起施行。  
  
2010年3月17日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于对被判处死刑的被告人  
  
未提出上诉、共同犯罪的部分被告人或者  
  
附带民事诉讼原告人提出上诉的案件  
  
应适用何种程序审理的批复  
  
法释〔2010〕6号  
  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院，新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院：  
  
近来，有的高级人民法院请示，对于中级人民法院一审判处死刑的案件，被判处死刑的被告人未提出上诉，但共同犯罪的部分被告人或者附带民事诉讼原告人提出上诉的，应当适用何种程序审理。经研究，批复如下：  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第一百八十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-186)的规定，中级人民法院一审判处死刑的案件，被判处死刑的被告人未提出上诉，共同犯罪的其他被告人提出上诉的，高级人民法院应当适用第二审程序对全案进行审查，并对涉及死刑之罪的事实和适用法律依法开庭审理，一并处理。  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-200)第一款的规定，中级人民法院一审判处死刑的案件，被判处死刑的被告人未提出上诉，仅附带民事诉讼原告人提出上诉的，高级人民法院应当适用第二审程序对附带民事诉讼依法审理，并由同一审判组织对未提出上诉的被告人的死刑判决进行复核，作出是否同意判处死刑的裁判。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** March 17, 2010  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Procedure for Adjudicating Cases Where a Defendant Sentenced to Death Does Not Appeal but Some Co-defendants or the Plaintiff in Incidental Civil Action Appeal  
Fa Shi [2010] No. 6  
Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army; and the Production and Construction Corps Branch of the Higher People's Court of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region:  
  
Recently, some Higher People's Courts have requested instructions on the procedure that shall be applied for the adjudication of cases in which the Intermediate People's Court imposed the death penalty at first instance, and the defendant sentenced to death does not appeal, but some co-defendants or the plaintiff in the incidental civil action have appealed. After study, the reply is as follows:  
  
According to the provisions of Article 186 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where, in a case in which the Intermediate People's Court imposed the death penalty at first instance, the defendant sentenced to death does not appeal, but other co-defendants appeal, the Higher People's Court shall apply the procedure of second instance to review the entire case, and shall conduct a trial in court according to law on the facts and application of law concerning the capital crime, and dispose of the case together.  
  
According to the provisions of Article 200, paragraph 1, of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where, in a case in which the Intermediate People's Court imposed the death penalty at first instance, the defendant sentenced to death does not appeal, but only the plaintiff in the incidental civil action appeals, the Higher People's Court shall apply the procedure of second instance to adjudicate the incidental civil action according to law, and the same adjudication organization shall review the death penalty judgment against the defendant who did not appeal, and render a ruling on whether to approve the death penalty.  
  
This reply is hereby given.
