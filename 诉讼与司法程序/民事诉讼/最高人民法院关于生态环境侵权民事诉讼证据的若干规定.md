# 最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权民事诉讼证据的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕6号  
**公布日期**：2023-04-17  
**生效日期**：2023-09-01

> 《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权民事诉讼证据的若干规定》已于2023年4月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1885次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年9月1日起施行。 2023年8月14日 最高人民法院 关于生态环境侵权民事诉讼证据的若干规定 法释〔2023〕6号 为保证人民法院正确认定案件事实，公正、及时审理生态环境侵权责任纠纷案件，保障和便利当事人依法行使诉讼权利，保护生态环境，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等有关法律规定，结合生态环境侵权民事案件审判经验和实际情况，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院审理环境污染责任纠纷案件、生态破坏责任纠纷案件和生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件，适用本规定。 生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件，包括环境污染民事公益诉讼案件、生态破坏民事公益诉讼案件和生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件。 第二条　环境污染责任纠纷案件、生态破坏责任纠纷案件的原告应当就以下事实承担举证责任： （一）被告实施了污染环境或者破坏生态的行为； （二）原告人身、财产受到损害或者有遭受损害的危险。 第三条　生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件的原告应当就以下事实承担举证责任： （一）被告实施了污染环境或者破坏生态的行为，且该行为违反国家规定； （二）生态环境受到损害或者有遭受损害的重大风险。 第四条　原告请求被告就其污染环境、破坏生态行为支付人身、财产损害赔偿费用，或者支付民法典第一千二百三十五条规定的损失、费用的，应当就其主张的损失、费用的数额承担举证责任。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理环境污染责任纠纷案件、生态破坏责任纠纷案件和生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件，适用本规定。  
生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件，包括环境污染民事公益诉讼案件、生态破坏民事公益诉讼案件和生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to the People's Court when hearing disputes over liability for environmental pollution, disputes over liability for ecological damage, and civil public interest litigation for ecological and environmental protection.  
Civil public interest litigation for ecological and environmental protection includes civil public interest litigation for environmental pollution, civil public interest litigation for ecological damage, and litigation for compensation for ecological and environmental damage.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　环境污染责任纠纷案件、生态破坏责任纠纷案件的原告应当就以下事实承担举证责任：  
（一）被告实施了污染环境或者破坏生态的行为；  
（二）原告人身、财产受到损害或者有遭受损害的危险。

**Article 2** The plaintiff in a dispute over liability for environmental pollution or a dispute over liability for ecological damage shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the following facts:  
(1) the defendant has committed an act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology;  
(2) the plaintiff's person or property has been harmed or is at risk of being harmed.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件的原告应当就以下事实承担举证责任：  
（一）被告实施了污染环境或者破坏生态的行为，且该行为违反国家规定；  
（二）生态环境受到损害或者有遭受损害的重大风险。

**Article 3** The plaintiff in a civil public interest litigation case for ecological and environmental protection shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the following facts:  
(1) the defendant has committed an act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and such act violates national regulations;  
(2) the ecology and environment have been harmed or are at a significant risk of being harmed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　原告请求被告就其污染环境、破坏生态行为支付人身、财产损害赔偿费用，或者支付[民法典第一千二百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1235)规定的损失、费用的，应当就其主张的损失、费用的数额承担举证责任。

**Article 4** Where the plaintiff requests the defendant to pay compensation for damage to person or property for the act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, or to pay the losses and expenses specified in Article 1235 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the amount of the losses and expenses claimed.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　原告起诉请求被告承担环境污染、生态破坏责任的，应当提供被告行为与损害之间具有关联性的证据。  
人民法院应当根据当事人提交的证据，结合污染环境、破坏生态的行为方式、污染物的性质、环境介质的类型、生态因素的特征、时间顺序、空间距离等因素，综合判断被告行为与损害之间的关联性是否成立。

**Article 5** Where the plaintiff initiates a prosecution requesting the defendant to bear liability for environmental pollution or ecological damage, the plaintiff shall provide evidence that there is a correlation between the defendant's act and the damage.  
The People's Court shall, based on the evidence submitted by the parties and taking into account factors such as the manner of the act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, the nature of the pollutants, the type of environmental media, the characteristics of ecological factors, the chronological order, and the spatial distance, comprehensively determine whether the correlation between the defendant's act and the damage is established.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被告应当就其行为与损害之间不存在因果关系承担举证责任。  
被告主张不承担责任或者减轻责任的，应当就法律规定的不承担责任或者减轻责任的情形承担举证责任。

**Article 6** The defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that there is no causal relationship between its act and the damage.  
Where the defendant claims that it shall not bear liability or that its liability shall be mitigated, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the circumstances of non-liability or mitigation of liability as provided by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被告证明其排放的污染物、释放的生态因素、产生的生态影响未到达损害发生地，或者其行为在损害发生后才实施且未加重损害后果，或者存在其行为不可能导致损害发生的其他情形的，人民法院应当认定被告行为与损害之间不存在因果关系。

**Article 7** Where the defendant proves that the pollutants it discharged, the ecological factors it released, or the ecological impacts it generated did not reach the place where the damage occurred, or that its act was carried out only after the damage occurred and did not aggravate the consequences of the damage, or that there are other circumstances in which its act could not possibly have caused the damage, the People's Court shall find that there is no causal relationship between the defendant's act and the damage.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对于发生法律效力的刑事裁判、行政裁判因未达到证明标准未予认定的事实，在因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为提起的生态环境侵权民事诉讼中，人民法院根据有关事实和证据确信待证事实的存在具有高度可能性的，应当认定该事实存在。

**Article 8** For facts not found in a legally effective criminal judgment or administrative judgment because they did not meet the standard of proof, in a civil action for ecological and environmental tort filed for the same act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, if the People's Court is convinced, based on the relevant facts and evidence, that the existence of the facts to be proved has a high degree of probability, it shall find such facts to exist.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对于人民法院在生态环境保护民事公益诉讼生效裁判中确认的基本事实，当事人在因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为提起的人身、财产损害赔偿诉讼中无需举证证明，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 9** For the basic facts confirmed in a legally effective judgment of the People's Court in a civil public interest litigation for ecological and environmental protection, the parties in a lawsuit for compensation for damage to person or property filed for the same act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology need not adduce evidence to prove them, unless there is sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于可能损害国家利益、社会公共利益的事实，双方当事人未主张或者无争议，人民法院认为可能影响裁判结果的，可以责令当事人提供有关证据。  
前款规定的证据，当事人申请人民法院调查收集，符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第九十四条规定情形的，人民法院应当准许；人民法院认为有必要的，可以依职权调查收集。

**Article 10** For facts that may harm national interests or social and public interests, where neither party has asserted them or there is no dispute between the parties, and the People's Court considers that they may affect the outcome of the judgment, it may order the parties to provide relevant evidence.  
For the evidence specified in the preceding paragraph, if a party applies to the People's Court to investigate and collect it and the circumstances meet the provisions of Article 94 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall grant permission; if the People's Court considers it necessary, it may investigate and collect it ex officio.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　实行环境资源案件集中管辖的法院，可以委托侵权行为实施地、侵权结果发生地、被告住所地等人民法院调查收集证据。受委托法院应当在收到委托函次日起三十日内完成委托事项，并将调查收集的证据及有关笔录移送委托法院。  
受委托法院未能完成委托事项的，应当向委托法院书面告知有关情况及未能完成的原因。

**Article 11** A court that exercises centralized jurisdiction over environmental and resource cases may entrust the People's Court at the place where the tort was committed, the place where the consequences of the tort occurred, or the domicile of the defendant, among others, to investigate and collect evidence. The entrusted court shall complete the entrusted matters within thirty days from the day following the receipt of the letter of entrustment, and shall transfer the evidence investigated and collected and the relevant records to the entrusting court.  
If the entrusted court fails to complete the entrusted matters, it shall inform the entrusting court in writing of the relevant circumstances and the reasons for the failure.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人或者利害关系人申请保全环境污染、生态破坏相关证据的，人民法院应当结合下列因素进行审查，确定是否采取保全措施：  
（一）证据灭失或者以后难以取得的可能性；  
（二）证据对证明待证事实有无必要；  
（三）申请人自行收集证据是否存在困难；  
（四）有必要采取证据保全措施的其他因素。

**Article 12** Where a party or an interested person applies for the preservation of evidence related to environmental pollution or ecological damage, the People's Court shall examine the following factors to determine whether to adopt preservation measures:  
(1) the possibility that the evidence may be lost or difficult to obtain in the future;  
(2) whether the evidence is necessary to prove the facts to be proved;  
(3) whether the applicant has difficulty in collecting the evidence by itself;  
(4) other factors justifying the adoption of evidence preservation measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在符合证据保全目的的情况下，人民法院应当选择对证据持有人利益影响最小的保全措施，尽量减少对保全标的物价值的损害和对证据持有人生产、生活的影响。  
确需采取查封、扣押等限制保全标的物使用的保全措施的，人民法院应当及时组织当事人对保全的证据进行质证。

**Article 13** Where the purpose of evidence preservation can be achieved, the People's Court shall choose preservation measures that have the least impact on the interests of the evidence holder, and shall minimize any damage to the value of the subject matter of preservation and any impact on the production and daily life of the evidence holder.  
If it is truly necessary to adopt preservation measures that restrict the use of the subject matter of preservation, such as sealing up or distraining, the People's Court shall promptly organize the parties to conduct cross-examination of the preserved evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院调查收集、保全或者勘验涉及环境污染、生态破坏专门性问题的证据，应当遵守相关技术规范。必要时，可以通知鉴定人到场，或者邀请负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门派员协助。

**Article 14** When investigating, collecting, preserving, or inspecting evidence involving specialized issues of environmental pollution or ecological damage, the People's Court shall comply with relevant technical norms. Where necessary, it may notify an expert evaluator to be present, or invite the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection to send personnel to assist.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人向人民法院提交证据后申请撤回该证据，或者声明不以该证据证明案件事实的，不影响其他当事人援引该证据证明案件事实以及人民法院对该证据进行审查认定。  
当事人放弃使用人民法院依其申请调查收集或者保全的证据的，按照前款规定处理。

**Article 15** Where a party, after submitting evidence to the People's Court, applies to withdraw such evidence, or declares that it will not use such evidence to prove the facts of the case, this shall not affect the right of other parties to invoke such evidence to prove the facts of the case, nor shall it affect the People's Court's examination and determination of such evidence.  
If a party abandons the use of evidence that the People's Court has investigated, collected, or preserved upon its application, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对于查明环境污染、生态破坏案件事实的专门性问题，人民法院经审查认为有必要的，应当根据当事人的申请或者依职权委托具有相应资格的机构、人员出具鉴定意见。

**Article 16** For specialized issues that are relevant to ascertaining the facts of a case concerning environmental pollution or ecological damage, if the People's Court, upon examination, considers it necessary, it shall, upon the application of a party or ex officio, entrust a qualified institution or person to provide an expert evaluation opinion.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于法律适用、当事人责任划分等非专门性问题，或者虽然属于专门性问题，但可以通过法庭调查、勘验等其他方式查明的，人民法院不予委托鉴定。

**Article 17** For non-specialized issues such as the application of law and the apportionment of liability among parties, or for issues that, although specialized, can be ascertained through other means such as court investigation or inspection, the People's Court shall not entrust an expert evaluation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　鉴定人需要邀请其他机构、人员完成部分鉴定事项的，应当向人民法院提出申请。  
人民法院经审查认为确有必要的，在听取双方当事人意见后，可以准许，并告知鉴定人对最终鉴定意见承担法律责任；主要鉴定事项由其他机构、人员实施的，人民法院不予准许。

**Article 18** Where an expert evaluator needs to invite another institution or person to complete part of the evaluation matters, it shall apply to the People's Court.  
If the People's Court, upon examination, considers it truly necessary, after hearing the opinions of both parties, it may grant permission and shall inform the expert evaluator that it shall bear legal liability for the final evaluation opinion; if the main evaluation matters are to be carried out by another institution or person, the People's Court shall not grant permission.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　未经人民法院准许，鉴定人邀请其他机构、人员完成部分鉴定事项的，鉴定意见不得作为认定案件事实的根据。  
前款情形，当事人申请退还鉴定费用的，人民法院应当在三日内作出裁定，责令鉴定人退还；拒不退还的，由人民法院依法执行。

**Article 19** Where an expert evaluator, without the permission of the People's Court, invites another institution or person to complete part of the evaluation matters, the evaluation opinion shall not be used as a basis for finding the facts of the case.  
In the circumstance of the preceding paragraph, if a party applies for the refund of the evaluation fees, the People's Court shall, within three days, render a ruling ordering the expert evaluator to refund; if the expert evaluator refuses to refund, the People's Court shall enforce it according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　鉴定人提供虚假鉴定意见的，该鉴定意见不得作为认定案件事实的根据。人民法院可以依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定进行处理。  
鉴定事项由其他机构、人员完成，其他机构、人员提供虚假鉴定意见的，按照前款规定处理。

**Article 20** Where an expert evaluator provides a false evaluation opinion, such opinion shall not be used as a basis for finding the facts of the case. The People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with Article 114 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the evaluation matters are completed by another institution or person and such institution or person provides a false evaluation opinion, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　因没有鉴定标准、成熟的鉴定方法、相应资格的鉴定人等原因无法进行鉴定，或者鉴定周期过长、费用过高的，人民法院可以结合案件有关事实、当事人申请的有专门知识的人的意见和其他证据，对涉及专门性问题的事实作出认定。

**Article 21** Where an expert evaluation cannot be carried out because there is no evaluation standard, mature evaluation method, or qualified expert evaluator, or because the evaluation cycle is too long or the cost is too high, the People's Court may, by taking into account the relevant facts of the case, the opinions of the persons with specialized knowledge applied by the parties, and other evidence, make a determination on the facts involving specialized issues.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人申请有专门知识的人出庭，就鉴定意见或者污染物认定、损害结果、因果关系、生态环境修复方案、生态环境修复费用、生态环境受到损害至修复完成期间服务功能丧失导致的损失、生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失等专业问题提出意见的，人民法院可以准许。  
对方当事人以有专门知识的人不具备相应资格为由提出异议的，人民法院对该异议不予支持。

**Article 22** Where a party applies for a person with specialized knowledge to appear in court to provide opinions on the expert evaluation opinion or on professional issues such as the identification of pollutants, the result of damage, the causal relationship, the plan for ecological and environmental restoration, the cost of ecological and environmental restoration, the loss caused by the loss of service functions during the period from the occurrence of ecological and environmental damage to the completion of restoration, and the loss caused by permanent damage to ecological and environmental functions, the People's Court may grant permission.  
If the opposing party raises an objection on the ground that the person with specialized knowledge does not have the corresponding qualifications, the People's Court shall not support such objection.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人就环境污染、生态破坏的专门性问题自行委托有关机构、人员出具的意见，人民法院应当结合本案的其他证据，审查确定能否作为认定案件事实的根据。  
对方当事人对该意见有异议的，人民法院应当告知提供意见的当事人可以申请出具意见的机构或者人员出庭陈述意见；未出庭的，该意见不得作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 23** Where a party entrusts a relevant institution or person to provide an opinion on specialized issues concerning environmental pollution or ecological damage, the People's Court shall, in light of other evidence in the case, examine and determine whether such opinion may serve as a basis for finding the facts of the case.  
If the opposing party has an objection to such opinion, the People's Court shall inform the party providing the opinion that it may apply for the institution or person that provided the opinion to appear in court to state the opinion; if such institution or person does not appear in court, the opinion shall not be used as a basis for finding the facts of the case.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门在其职权范围内制作的处罚决定等文书所记载的事项推定为真实，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。  
人民法院认为有必要的，可以依职权对上述文书的真实性进行调查核实。

**Article 24** Matters recorded in documents such as punishment decisions made by the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection within the scope of its functions and powers shall be presumed to be true, unless there is sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.  
If the People's Court considers it necessary, it may ex officio investigate and verify the authenticity of the aforementioned documents.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门及其所属或者委托的监测机构在行政执法过程中收集的监测数据、形成的事件调查报告、检验检测报告、评估报告等材料，以及公安机关单独或者会同负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门提取样品进行检测获取的数据，经当事人质证，可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 25** The monitoring data collected, the incident investigation reports, inspection and testing reports, assessment reports, and other materials formed during the administrative law enforcement process by the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection and its affiliated or entrusted monitoring institutions, as well as the data obtained by the public security organ alone or jointly with the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection by extracting samples for testing, may, after being cross-examined by the parties, serve as a basis for finding the facts of the case.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对于证明环境污染、生态破坏案件事实有重要意义的书面文件、数据信息或者录音、录像等证据在对方当事人控制之下的，承担举证责任的当事人可以根据《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第一百一十二条的规定，书面申请人民法院责令对方当事人提交。

**Article 26** Where written documents, data information, or audio or video recordings and other evidence that are important for proving the facts of a case concerning environmental pollution or ecological damage are under the control of the opposing party, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence may, in accordance with Article 112 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, apply in writing to the People's Court to order the opposing party to submit them.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　承担举证责任的当事人申请人民法院责令对方当事人提交证据的，应当提供有关证据的名称、主要内容、制作人、制作时间或者其他可以将有关证据特定化的信息。根据申请人提供的信息不能使证据特定化的，人民法院不予准许。  
人民法院应当结合申请人是否参与证据形成过程、是否接触过该证据等因素，综合判断其提供的信息是否达到证据特定化的要求。

**Article 27** Where the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence applies to the People's Court to order the opposing party to submit evidence, it shall provide the name, main content, preparer, time of preparation, or other information that can make the relevant evidence specific. If the evidence cannot be made specific based on the information provided by the applicant, the People's Court shall not grant permission.  
The People's Court shall, taking into account factors such as whether the applicant participated in the formation process of the evidence and whether it has had access to the evidence, comprehensively determine whether the information provided meets the requirement of specificity of the evidence.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　承担举证责任的当事人申请人民法院责令对方当事人提交证据的，应当提出证据由对方当事人控制的依据。对方当事人否认控制有关证据的，人民法院应当根据法律规定、当事人约定、交易习惯等因素，结合案件的事实、证据作出判断。  
有关证据虽未由对方当事人直接持有，但在其控制范围之内，其获取不存在客观障碍的，人民法院应当认定有关证据由其控制。

**Article 28** Where the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence applies to the People's Court to order the opposing party to submit evidence, it shall provide the basis that such evidence is under the control of the opposing party. If the opposing party denies controlling the relevant evidence, the People's Court shall, in accordance with factors such as legal provisions, the agreement between the parties, and trade practices, and in light of the facts and evidence of the case, make a determination.  
If the relevant evidence, although not directly held by the opposing party, is within its control and there is no objective obstacle to its acquisition, the People's Court shall find that the relevant evidence is under the control of the opposing party.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法律、法规、规章规定当事人应当披露或者持有的关于其排放的主要污染物名称、排放方式、排放浓度和总量、超标排放情况、防治污染设施的建设和运行情况、生态环境开发利用情况、生态环境违法信息等环境信息，属于[《最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定》第四十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定.md#art-47)第一款第三项规定的“对方当事人依照法律规定有权查阅、获取的书证”。

**Article 29** Environmental information that laws, regulations, and rules stipulate that a party shall disclose or possess, such as the names of the main pollutants it discharges, the discharge methods, the discharge concentration and total amount, the situation of excessive discharge, the construction and operation of pollution prevention facilities, the development and utilization of the ecology and environment, and information on violations of ecological and environmental laws, shall fall within the category of 'documentary evidence that the opposing party has the right to consult and obtain according to legal provisions' as specified in Article 47, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Evidence in Civil Litigation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在环境污染责任纠纷、生态破坏责任纠纷案件中，损害事实成立，但人身、财产损害赔偿数额难以确定的，人民法院可以结合侵权行为对原告造成损害的程度、被告因侵权行为获得的利益以及过错程度等因素，并可以参考负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门的意见等，合理确定。

**Article 30** In cases of disputes over liability for environmental pollution or disputes over liability for ecological damage, where the fact of damage is established but the amount of compensation for damage to person or property is difficult to determine, the People's Court may reasonably determine the amount by taking into account factors such as the degree of damage caused to the plaintiff by the tortious act, the benefits obtained by the defendant from the tortious act, and the degree of fault, and may also refer to the opinions of the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection, among others.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在生态环境保护民事公益诉讼案件中，损害事实成立，但生态环境修复费用、生态环境受到损害至修复完成期间服务功能丧失导致的损失、生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失等数额难以确定的，人民法院可以根据污染环境、破坏生态的范围和程度等已查明的案件事实，结合生态环境及其要素的稀缺性、生态环境恢复的难易程度、防治污染设备的运行成本、被告因侵权行为获得的利益以及过错程度等因素，并可以参考负有环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门的意见等，合理确定。

**Article 31** In cases of civil public interest litigation for ecological and environmental protection, where the fact of damage is established but the amounts such as the costs of ecological and environmental restoration, the losses caused by the loss of service functions during the period from the occurrence of ecological and environmental damage to the completion of restoration, and the losses caused by permanent damage to ecological and environmental functions are difficult to determine, the People's Court may reasonably determine the amounts based on the ascertained facts of the case, such as the scope and degree of the environmental pollution or ecological damage, and taking into account factors such as the scarcity of the ecology and environment and their elements, the difficulty of ecological and environmental restoration, the operating costs of pollution prevention equipment, the benefits obtained by the defendant from the tortious act, and the degree of fault, and may also refer to the opinions of the department responsible for supervision and administration of environmental and resource protection, among others.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本规定未作规定的，适用《最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定》。

**Article 32** Matters not provided for in these Provisions shall be governed by the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Evidence in Civil Litigation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院审理人民检察院提起的环境污染民事公益诉讼案件、生态破坏民事公益诉讼案件，参照适用本规定。

**Article 33** When the People's Court hears a civil public interest litigation case for environmental pollution or a civil public interest litigation case for ecological damage initiated by the People's Procuratorate, it shall apply these Provisions mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本规定自2023年9月1日起施行。  
本规定公布施行后，最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 34** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 1, 2023.  
After these Provisions are promulgated and come into force, where judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, they shall no longer apply.
