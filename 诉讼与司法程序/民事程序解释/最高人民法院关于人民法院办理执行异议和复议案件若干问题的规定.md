# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕10号  
**公布日期**：2015-05-05  
**生效日期**：2015-05-05

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》已于2014年12月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1638次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年5月5日起施行。 2015年5月5日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2015〕10号 为了规范人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件，维护当事人、利害关系人和案外人的合法权益，根据民事诉讼法等法律规定，结合人民法院执行工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　异议人提出执行异议或者复议申请人申请复议，应当向人民法院提交申请书。申请书应当载明具体的异议或者复议请求、事实、理由等内容，并附下列材料： （一）异议人或者复议申请人的身份证明； （二）相关证据材料； （三）送达地址和联系方式。 第二条　执行异议符合民事诉讼法第二百二十五条或者第二百二十七条规定条件的，人民法院应当在三日内立案，并在立案后三日内通知异议人和相关当事人。不符合受理条件的，裁定不予受理；立案后发现不符合受理条件的，裁定驳回申请。 执行异议申请材料不齐备的，人民法院应当一次性告知异议人在三日内补足，逾期未补足的，不予受理。 异议人对不予受理或者驳回申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院审查后认为符合受理条件的，应当裁定撤销原裁定，指令执行法院立案或者对执行异议进行审查。 第三条　执行法院收到执行异议后三日内既不立案又不作出不予受理裁定，或者受理后无正当理由超过法定期限不作出异议裁定的，异议人可以向上一级人民法院提出异议。上一级人民法院审查后认为理由成立的，应当指令执行法院在三日内立案或者在十五日内作出异议裁定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　异议人提出执行异议或者复议申请人申请复议，应当向人民法院提交申请书。申请书应当载明具体的异议或者复议请求、事实、理由等内容，并附下列材料：  
（一）异议人或者复议申请人的身份证明；  
（二）相关证据材料；  
（三）送达地址和联系方式。

**Article 1** Where an dissenter raises an enforcement objection or a person applying for reconsideration applies for reconsideration, he or she shall submit a written application to the People's Court. The written application shall specify the specific objection or reconsideration request, facts, reasons, and other contents, and shall be accompanied by the following materials:  
(1) The identity certificate of the dissenter or the person applying for reconsideration;  
(2) Relevant evidentiary materials;  
(3) Service address and contact information.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　执行异议符合[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)或者第二百二十七条规定条件的，人民法院应当在三日内立案，并在立案后三日内通知异议人和相关当事人。不符合受理条件的，裁定不予受理；立案后发现不符合受理条件的，裁定驳回申请。  
执行异议申请材料不齐备的，人民法院应当一次性告知异议人在三日内补足，逾期未补足的，不予受理。  
异议人对不予受理或者驳回申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院审查后认为符合受理条件的，应当裁定撤销原裁定，指令执行法院立案或者对执行异议进行审查。

**Article 2** Where an enforcement objection meets the conditions prescribed in Article 225 or Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court shall docket the case within three days and notify the dissenter and relevant parties within three days after docketing. Where the conditions for acceptance are not met, a ruling shall be made not to accept the case; where it is discovered after docketing that the conditions for acceptance are not met, a ruling shall be made to reject the application.  
Where the materials submitted for the enforcement objection are incomplete, the People's Court shall inform the dissenter at one time to supplement them within three days; if they are not supplemented within the time limit, the case shall not be accepted.  
Where the dissenter refuses to accept the ruling of non-acceptance or rejection of the application, he or she may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. Upon review, if the People's Court at the next higher level considers that the conditions for acceptance are met, it shall make a ruling to revoke the original ruling and instruct the enforcing court to docket the case or to examine the enforcement objection.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　执行法院收到执行异议后三日内既不立案又不作出不予受理裁定，或者受理后无正当理由超过法定期限不作出异议裁定的，异议人可以向上一级人民法院提出异议。上一级人民法院审查后认为理由成立的，应当指令执行法院在三日内立案或者在十五日内作出异议裁定。

**Article 3** Where the enforcing court, within three days after receiving an enforcement objection, neither dockets the case nor makes a ruling of non-acceptance, or after acceptance, fails to make an objection ruling beyond the statutory time limit without justifiable reasons, the dissenter may raise an objection to the People's Court at the next higher level. Upon review, if the People's Court at the next higher level considers that the reasons are established, it shall instruct the enforcing court to docket the case within three days or to make an objection ruling within fifteen days.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　执行案件被指定执行、提级执行、委托执行后，当事人、利害关系人对原执行法院的执行行为提出异议的，由提出异议时负责该案件执行的人民法院审查处理；受指定或者受委托的人民法院是原执行法院的下级人民法院的，仍由原执行法院审查处理。  
执行案件被指定执行、提级执行、委托执行后，案外人对原执行法院的执行标的提出异议的，参照前款规定处理。

**Article 4** After an enforcement case has been designated for enforcement, escalated for enforcement, or entrusted for enforcement, where a party or an interested person raises an objection to the enforcement act of the original enforcing court, the case shall be examined and handled by the People's Court responsible for the enforcement of the case at the time the objection is raised; where the designated or entrusted People's Court is a lower-level court of the original enforcing court, the case shall still be examined and handled by the original enforcing court.  
After an enforcement case has been designated for enforcement, escalated for enforcement, or entrusted for enforcement, where a person other than a party raises an objection to the subject matter of enforcement of the original enforcing court, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的，当事人以外的公民、法人和其他组织，可以作为利害关系人提出执行行为异议：  
（一）认为人民法院的执行行为违法，妨碍其轮候查封、扣押、冻结的债权受偿的；  
（二）认为人民法院的拍卖措施违法，妨碍其参与公平竞价的；  
（三）认为人民法院的拍卖、变卖或者以物抵债措施违法，侵害其对执行标的的优先购买权的；  
（四）认为人民法院要求协助执行的事项超出其协助范围或者违反法律规定的；  
（五）认为其他合法权益受到人民法院违法执行行为侵害的。

**Article 5** Under any of the following circumstances, citizens, legal persons, or other organizations other than the parties may, as interested persons, raise an objection to an enforcement act:  
(1) Where a person considers that an enforcement act of the People's Court is illegal and hinders the satisfaction of his or her claim that is subject to a subsequent seal-up, seizure, or freezing;  
(2) Where a person considers that the auction measure of the People's Court is illegal and hinders his or her participation in fair bidding;  
(3) Where a person considers that the auction, sale, or debt satisfaction by property measure of the People's Court is illegal and infringes upon his or her preemptive right to purchase the subject matter of enforcement;  
(4) Where a person considers that the matters for which the People's Court requests assistance in enforcement exceed the scope of his or her assistance or violate legal provisions;  
(5) Where a person considers that other lawful rights and interests are infringed upon by an illegal enforcement act of the People's Court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人、利害关系人依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定提出异议的，应当在执行程序终结之前提出，但对终结执行措施提出异议的除外。  
案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定提出异议的，应当在异议指向的执行标的执行终结之前提出；执行标的由当事人受让的，应当在执行程序终结之前提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院关于审理执行异议之诉案件适用法律问题的解释.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理执行异议之诉案件适用法律问题的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>

**Article 6** Where a party or an interested person raises an objection in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law, he or she shall do so before the conclusion of the enforcement proceedings, except where the objection is raised against measures for concluding enforcement.  
Where a person other than a party raises an objection in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law, he or she shall do so before the conclusion of the enforcement of the subject matter of enforcement to which the objection is directed; where the subject matter of enforcement is transferred to a party, the objection shall be raised before the conclusion of the enforcement proceedings.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人、利害关系人认为执行过程中或者执行保全、先予执行裁定过程中的下列行为违法提出异议的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定进行审查：  
（一）查封、扣押、冻结、拍卖、变卖、以物抵债、暂缓执行、中止执行、终结执行等执行措施；  
（二）执行的期间、顺序等应当遵守的法定程序；  
（三）人民法院作出的侵害当事人、利害关系人合法权益的其他行为。  
被执行人以债权消灭、丧失强制执行效力等执行依据生效之后的实体事由提出排除执行异议的，人民法院应当参照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定进行审查。  
除[本规定第十九条](#art-19)规定的情形外，被执行人以执行依据生效之前的实体事由提出排除执行异议的，人民法院应当告知其依法申请再审或者通过其他程序解决。

**Article 7** Where a party or an interested person raises an objection on the grounds that any of the following acts during the enforcement process or during the process of enforcing preservation or advance execution rulings is illegal, the People's Court shall conduct an examination in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) Enforcement measures such as seal-up, seizure, freezing, auction, sale, debt satisfaction by property, suspension of enforcement, stay of enforcement, and conclusion of enforcement;  
(2) Legal procedures that shall be followed concerning the time limits and order of enforcement;  
(3) Other acts made by the People's Court that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of a party or an interested person.  
Where a person subject to enforcement raises an objection to exclude enforcement on the basis of substantive matters that arise after the enforcement basis takes effect, such as the extinction of a claim or the loss of enforceability, the People's Court shall conduct an examination by referring to Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
Except for the circumstances specified in Article 19 of these Provisions, where a person subject to enforcement raises an objection to exclude enforcement on the basis of substantive matters that arise before the enforcement basis takes effect, the People's Court shall inform him or her to apply for a retrial according to law or to resolve the matter through other procedures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　案外人基于实体权利既对执行标的提出排除执行异议又作为利害关系人提出执行行为异议的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定进行审查。  
案外人既基于实体权利对执行标的提出排除执行异议又作为利害关系人提出与实体权利无关的执行行为异议的，人民法院应当分别依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)和第二百二十五条规定进行审查。

**Article 8** Where a person other than a party, based on substantive rights, both raises an objection to exclude enforcement against the subject matter of enforcement and, as an interested person, raises an objection to an enforcement act, the People's Court shall conduct an examination in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
Where a person other than a party, based on substantive rights, raises an objection to exclude enforcement against the subject matter of enforcement and, as an interested person, raises an objection to an enforcement act that is unrelated to substantive rights, the People's Court shall conduct examinations separately in accordance with Article 227 and Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law respectively.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被限制出境的人认为对其限制出境错误的，可以自收到限制出境决定之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十五日内作出决定。复议期间，不停止原决定的执行。

**Article 9** Where a person subject to exit restriction considers that the exit restriction against him or her is erroneous, he or she may, within ten days from the date of receipt of the decision on exit restriction, apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level. The People's Court at the next higher level shall make a decision within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, the enforcement of the original decision shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人不服驳回不予执行公证债权文书申请的裁定的，可以自收到裁定之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起三十日内审查，理由成立的，裁定撤销原裁定，不予执行该公证债权文书；理由不成立的，裁定驳回复议申请。复议期间，不停止执行。

**Article 10** Where a party refuses to accept a ruling rejecting an application for non-enforcement of a notarized instrument of creditor's rights, he or she may, within ten days from the date of receipt of the ruling, apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level. The People's Court at the next higher level shall conduct a review within thirty days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application; if the reasons are established, it shall make a ruling to revoke the original ruling and not to enforce the notarized instrument of creditor's rights; if the reasons are not established, it shall make a ruling to reject the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, enforcement shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院审查执行异议或者复议案件，应当依法组成合议庭。  
指令重新审查的执行异议案件，应当另行组成合议庭。  
办理执行实施案件的人员不得参与相关执行异议和复议案件的审查。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall form a collegial panel according to law when examining an enforcement objection or reconsideration case.  
For an enforcement objection case designated for re-examination, a different collegial panel shall be formed.  
Personnel handling enforcement implementation cases shall not participate in the examination of related enforcement objection and reconsideration cases.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院对执行异议和复议案件实行书面审查。案情复杂、争议较大的，应当进行听证。

**Article 12** The People's Court shall conduct a written examination of enforcement objection and reconsideration cases. Where the facts of a case are complex or the disputes are substantial, a hearing shall be held.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　执行异议、复议案件审查期间，异议人、复议申请人申请撤回异议、复议申请的，是否准许由人民法院裁定。

**Article 13** During the examination period of an enforcement objection or reconsideration case, where the dissenter or the person applying for reconsideration applies to withdraw the objection or reconsideration application, the People's Court shall decide by ruling whether to permit such withdrawal.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　异议人或者复议申请人经合法传唤，无正当理由拒不参加听证，或者未经法庭许可中途退出听证，致使人民法院无法查清相关事实的，由其自行承担不利后果。

**Article 14** Where a dissenter or a person applying for reconsideration, after being lawfully summoned, refuses to attend the hearing without justifiable reasons, or withdraws from the hearing midway without the permission of the court, resulting in the People's Court's inability to ascertain the relevant facts, he or she shall bear the adverse consequences.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人、利害关系人对同一执行行为有多个异议事由，但未在异议审查过程中一并提出，撤回异议或者被裁定驳回异议后，再次就该执行行为提出异议的，人民法院不予受理。  
案外人撤回异议或者被裁定驳回异议后，再次就同一执行标的提出异议的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 15** Where a party or an interested person has multiple grounds for objection against the same enforcement act but fails to raise them all together during the objection examination process, and after withdrawing the objection or having the objection rejected by ruling, raises another objection against the same enforcement act, the People's Court shall not accept it.  
Where a person other than a party withdraws an objection or has the objection rejected by ruling, and then raises another objection against the same subject matter of enforcement, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定作出裁定时，应当告知相关权利人申请复议的权利和期限。  
人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定作出裁定时，应当告知相关权利人提起执行异议之诉的权利和期限。  
人民法院作出其他裁定和决定时，法律、司法解释规定了相关权利人申请复议的权利和期限的，应当进行告知。

**Article 16** When the People's Court makes a ruling in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall inform the relevant right holder of the right to apply for reconsideration and the time limit for doing so.  
When the People's Court makes a ruling in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall inform the relevant right holder of the right to file an enforcement objection lawsuit and the time limit for doing so.  
When the People's Court makes other rulings and decisions, if the law or judicial interpretations provide for the relevant right holder's right to apply for reconsideration and the time limit, the court shall so inform.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院对执行行为异议，应当按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）异议不成立的，裁定驳回异议；  
（二）异议成立的，裁定撤销相关执行行为；  
（三）异议部分成立的，裁定变更相关执行行为；  
（四）异议成立或者部分成立，但执行行为无撤销、变更内容的，裁定异议成立或者相应部分异议成立。

**Article 17** The People's Court shall handle an objection to an enforcement act separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the objection is not established, a ruling shall be made to reject the objection;  
(2) Where the objection is established, a ruling shall be made to revoke the relevant enforcement act;  
(3) Where the objection is partially established, a ruling shall be made to modify the relevant enforcement act;  
(4) Where the objection is established or partially established, but the enforcement act has no content subject to revocation or modification, a ruling shall be made to uphold the objection or the corresponding part of the objection.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　执行过程中，第三人因书面承诺自愿代被执行人偿还债务而被追加为被执行人后，无正当理由反悔并提出异议的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 18** During the enforcement process, where a third party is added as a person subject to enforcement due to a written undertaking to voluntarily repay the debt on behalf of the person subject to enforcement, and later, without justifiable reasons, reneges on the undertaking and raises an objection, the People's Court shall not support such objection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人互负到期债务，被执行人请求抵销，请求抵销的债务符合下列情形的，除依照法律规定或者按照债务性质不得抵销的以外，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）已经生效法律文书确定或者经申请执行人认可；  
（二）与被执行人所负债务的标的物种类、品质相同。

**Article 19** Where parties have mutual debts that are due, and the person subject to enforcement requests setoff, if the debts for which setoff is requested meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request, except where setoff is not permitted according to law or by the nature of the debts:  
(1) The debts have been confirmed by an effective legal instrument or have been recognized by the person applying for enforcement;  
(2) The debts are of the same type and quality as the subject matter of the debts owed by the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　金钱债权执行中，符合下列情形之一，被执行人以执行标的系本人及所扶养家属维持生活必需的居住房屋为由提出异议的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）对被执行人有扶养义务的人名下有其他能够维持生活必需的居住房屋的；  
（二）执行依据生效后，被执行人为逃避债务转让其名下其他房屋的；  
（三）申请执行人按照当地廉租住房保障面积标准为被执行人及所扶养家属提供居住房屋，或者同意参照当地房屋租赁市场平均租金标准从该房屋的变价款中扣除五至八年租金的。  
执行依据确定被执行人交付居住的房屋，自执行通知送达之日起，已经给予三个月的宽限期，被执行人以该房屋系本人及所扶养家属维持生活的必需品为由提出异议的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** During the enforcement of monetary claims, where any of the following circumstances exists and the person subject to enforcement raises an objection on the grounds that the subject matter of enforcement is a residential house necessary for the subsistence of himself or herself and his or her dependents, the People's Court shall not support the objection:  
(1) Another person who has a duty to support the person subject to enforcement owns another residential house that can sustain the necessary subsistence;  
(2) After the enforcement basis takes effect, the person subject to enforcement transfers other houses under his or her name to evade debts;  
(3) The person applying for enforcement provides a residential house for the person subject to enforcement and his or her dependents in accordance with the local standard for low-rent housing security area, or agrees to deduct five to eight years' rent from the proceeds of the sale of the house in reference to the local average market rental standard.  
Where the enforcement basis requires the person subject to enforcement to deliver a residential house for occupation, and a grace period of three months has been given from the date of service of the enforcement notice, and the person subject to enforcement raises an objection on the grounds that the house is necessary for the subsistence of himself or herself and his or her dependents, the People's Court shall not support the objection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人、利害关系人提出异议请求撤销拍卖，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）竞买人之间、竞买人与拍卖机构之间恶意串通，损害当事人或者其他竞买人利益的；  
（二）买受人不具备法律规定的竞买资格的；  
（三）违法限制竞买人参加竞买或者对不同的竞买人规定不同竞买条件的；  
（四）未按照法律、司法解释的规定对拍卖标的物进行公告的；  
（五）其他严重违反拍卖程序且损害当事人或者竞买人利益的情形。  
当事人、利害关系人请求撤销变卖的，参照前款规定处理。

**Article 21** Where a party or an interested person raises an objection requesting the revocation of an auction, and any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) The bidders or the bidders and the auction institution have maliciously colluded to harm the interests of a party or other bidders;  
(2) The buyer does not have the bidding qualification as prescribed by law;  
(3) Bidders are illegally restricted from participating in the bidding, or different bidding conditions are prescribed for different bidders;  
(4) The announcement of the auction subject matter is not made in accordance with the provisions of laws or judicial interpretations;  
(5) Other circumstances that seriously violate the auction procedures and harm the interests of a party or a bidder.  
Where a party or an interested person requests the revocation of a sale, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公证债权文书对主债务和担保债务同时赋予强制执行效力的，人民法院应予执行；仅对主债务赋予强制执行效力未涉及担保债务的，对担保债务的执行申请不予受理；仅对担保债务赋予强制执行效力未涉及主债务的，对主债务的执行申请不予受理。  
人民法院受理担保债务的执行申请后，被执行人仅以担保合同不属于赋予强制执行效力的公证债权文书范围为由申请不予执行的，不予支持。

**Article 22** Where a notarized instrument of creditor's rights confers enforceability on both the principal debt and the secured debt, the People's Court shall enforce it; where it confers enforceability only on the principal debt without involving the secured debt, the application for enforcement of the secured debt shall not be accepted; where it confers enforceability only on the secured debt without involving the principal debt, the application for enforcement of the principal debt shall not be accepted.  
After the People's Court accepts an application for enforcement of a secured debt, where the person subject to enforcement applies for non-enforcement solely on the grounds that the guarantee contract does not fall within the scope of notarized instruments of creditor's rights that are conferred enforceability, such application shall not be supported.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　上一级人民法院对不服异议裁定的复议申请审查后，应当按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）异议裁定认定事实清楚，适用法律正确，结果应予维持的，裁定驳回复议申请，维持异议裁定；  
（二）异议裁定认定事实错误，或者适用法律错误，结果应予纠正的，裁定撤销或者变更异议裁定；  
（三）异议裁定认定基本事实不清、证据不足的，裁定撤销异议裁定，发回作出裁定的人民法院重新审查，或者查清事实后作出相应裁定；  
（四）异议裁定遗漏异议请求或者存在其他严重违反法定程序的情形，裁定撤销异议裁定，发回作出裁定的人民法院重新审查；  
（五）异议裁定对应当适用[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定审查处理的异议，错误适用[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)规定审查处理的，裁定撤销异议裁定，发回作出裁定的人民法院重新作出裁定。  
除依照本条第一款第三、四、五项发回重新审查或者重新作出裁定的情形外，裁定撤销或者变更异议裁定且执行行为可撤销、变更的，应当同时撤销或者变更该裁定维持的执行行为。  
人民法院对发回重新审查的案件作出裁定后，当事人、利害关系人申请复议的，上一级人民法院复议后不得再次发回重新审查。

**Article 23** After the People's Court at the next higher level examines a reconsideration application against an objection ruling, it shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the objection ruling has clear findings of fact, correct application of law, and the result should be upheld, a ruling shall be made to reject the reconsideration application and uphold the objection ruling;  
(2) Where the objection ruling has erroneous findings of fact, or erroneous application of law, and the result should be corrected, a ruling shall be made to revoke or modify the objection ruling;  
(3) Where the objection ruling has unclear findings of basic facts or insufficient evidence, a ruling shall be made to revoke the objection ruling and remand it to the People's Court that made the ruling for re-examination, or to make a corresponding ruling after ascertaining the facts;  
(4) Where the objection ruling omits any objection request or has other serious violations of statutory procedures, a ruling shall be made to revoke the objection ruling and remand it to the People's Court that made the ruling for re-examination;  
(5) Where the objection ruling erroneously examines and handles an objection that should be examined and handled in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law by applying Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law, a ruling shall be made to revoke the objection ruling and remand it to the People's Court that made the ruling for a new ruling.  
Except for circumstances where the matter is remanded for re-examination or a new ruling under items (3), (4), and (5) of the first paragraph, where a ruling revokes or modifies the objection ruling and the enforcement act is revocable or modifiable, the enforcement act upheld by that ruling shall be revoked or modified at the same time.  
After the People's Court makes a ruling on a case remanded for re-examination, where a party or an interested person applies for reconsideration, the People's Court at the next higher level shall not remand the case for re-examination again after reconsideration.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对案外人提出的排除执行异议，人民法院应当审查下列内容：  
（一）案外人是否系权利人；  
（二）该权利的合法性与真实性；  
（三）该权利能否排除执行。

**Article 24** For an objection to exclude enforcement raised by a person other than a party, the People's Court shall examine the following contents:  
(1) Whether the person other than a party is the right holder;  
(2) The legality and authenticity of such right;  
(3) Whether such right can exclude enforcement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对案外人的异议，人民法院应当按照下列标准判断其是否系权利人：  
（一）已登记的不动产，按照不动产登记簿判断；未登记的建筑物、构筑物及其附属设施，按照土地使用权登记簿、建设工程规划许可、施工许可等相关证据判断；  
（二）已登记的机动车、船舶、航空器等特定动产，按照相关管理部门的登记判断；未登记的特定动产和其他动产，按照实际占有情况判断；  
（三）银行存款和存管在金融机构的有价证券，按照金融机构和登记结算机构登记的账户名称判断；有价证券由具备合法经营资质的托管机构名义持有的，按照该机构登记的实际投资人账户名称判断；  
（四）股权按照工商行政管理机关的登记和企业信用信息公示系统公示的信息判断；  
（五）其他财产和权利，有登记的，按照登记机构的登记判断；无登记的，按照合同等证明财产权属或者权利人的证据判断。  
案外人依据另案生效法律文书提出排除执行异议，该法律文书认定的执行标的权利人与依照前款规定得出的判断不一致的，依照[本规定第二十六条](#art-26)规定处理。

**Article 25** For an objection raised by a person other than a party, the People's Court shall determine whether he or she is the right holder according to the following standards:  
(1) For registered immovable property, determination shall be made according to the immovable property register; for unregistered buildings, structures, and their ancillary facilities, determination shall be made according to relevant evidence such as the land use rights register, construction project planning permit, and construction permit;  
(2) For registered specific movables such as motor vehicles, ships, and aircraft, determination shall be made according to the registration of the relevant administrative department; for unregistered specific movables and other movables, determination shall be made according to actual possession;  
(3) For bank deposits and securities deposited with financial institutions, determination shall be made according to the account names registered with the financial institutions and the registration and settlement institutions; for securities nominally held by a custodian institution with lawful business qualifications, determination shall be made according to the account name of the actual investor registered by such institution;  
(4) For equity interests, determination shall be made according to the registration of the administrative authority for industry and commerce and the information published on the enterprise credit information publicity system;  
(5) For other property and rights, if registered, determination shall be made according to the registration of the registration authority; if not registered, determination shall be made according to evidence such as contracts that prove property ownership or the right holder.  
Where a person other than a party raises an objection to exclude enforcement based on an effective legal instrument from another case, and the right holder of the subject matter of enforcement determined by such legal instrument is inconsistent with the determination made under the preceding paragraph, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 26 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　金钱债权执行中，案外人依据执行标的被查封、扣押、冻结前作出的另案生效法律文书提出排除执行异议，人民法院应当按照下列情形，分别处理：  
（一）该法律文书系就案外人与被执行人之间的权属纠纷以及租赁、借用、保管等不以转移财产权属为目的的合同纠纷，判决、裁决执行标的归属于案外人或者向其返还执行标的且其权利能够排除执行的，应予支持；  
（二）该法律文书系就案外人与被执行人之间除前项所列合同之外的债权纠纷，判决、裁决执行标的归属于案外人或者向其交付、返还执行标的的，不予支持。  
（三）该法律文书系案外人受让执行标的的拍卖、变卖成交裁定或者以物抵债裁定且其权利能够排除执行的，应予支持。  
金钱债权执行中，案外人依据执行标的被查封、扣押、冻结后作出的另案生效法律文书提出排除执行异议的，人民法院不予支持。  
非金钱债权执行中，案外人依据另案生效法律文书提出排除执行异议，该法律文书对执行标的权属作出不同认定的，人民法院应当告知案外人依法申请再审或者通过其他程序解决。  
申请执行人或者案外人不服人民法院依照本条第一、二款规定作出的裁定，可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)规定提起执行异议之诉。

**Article 26** During the enforcement of monetary claims, where a person other than a party raises an objection to exclude enforcement based on an effective legal instrument from another case made before the seal-up, seizure, or freezing of the subject matter of enforcement, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the legal instrument is a judgment or ruling regarding a dispute over ownership between the person other than a party and the person subject to enforcement, or a contractual dispute such as lease, borrowing, or custody that does not aim at transferring property ownership, and it adjudicates or rules that the subject matter of enforcement belongs to the person other than a party or shall be returned to him or her, and his or her right can exclude enforcement, the objection shall be supported;  
(2) Where the legal instrument is a judgment or ruling regarding a dispute over claims other than the contracts listed in the preceding item between the person other than a party and the person subject to enforcement, and it adjudicates or rules that the subject matter of enforcement belongs to the person other than a party or shall be delivered or returned to him or her, the objection shall not be supported.  
(3) Where the legal instrument is a ruling confirming the acceptance of a bid in an auction or sale, or a ruling on debt satisfaction by property, by which the person other than a party acquires the subject matter of enforcement, and his or her right can exclude enforcement, the objection shall be supported.  
During the enforcement of monetary claims, where a person other than a party raises an objection to exclude enforcement based on an effective legal instrument from another case made after the seal-up, seizure, or freezing of the subject matter of enforcement, the People's Court shall not support the objection.  
During the enforcement of non-monetary claims, where a person other than a party raises an objection to exclude enforcement based on an effective legal instrument from another case, and such legal instrument makes a different determination on the ownership of the subject matter of enforcement, the People's Court shall inform the person other than a party to apply for a retrial according to law or to resolve the matter through other procedures.  
Where the person applying for enforcement or the person other than a party refuses to accept the ruling made by the People's Court in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, he or she may file an enforcement objection lawsuit in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请执行人对执行标的依法享有对抗案外人的担保物权等优先受偿权，人民法院对案外人提出的排除执行异议不予支持，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 27** Where the person applying for enforcement lawfully enjoys a security interest or other priority right to satisfaction against the subject matter of enforcement that can be asserted against a person other than a party, the People's Court shall not support the objection to exclude enforcement raised by the person other than a party, unless otherwise provided by laws or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　金钱债权执行中，买受人对登记在被执行人名下的不动产提出异议，符合下列情形且其权利能够排除执行的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）在人民法院查封之前已签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；  
（二）在人民法院查封之前已合法占有该不动产；  
（三）已支付全部价款，或者已按照合同约定支付部分价款且将剩余价款按照人民法院的要求交付执行；  
（四）非因买受人自身原因未办理过户登记。

**Article 28** During the enforcement of monetary claims, where a buyer raises an objection against immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and the following conditions are met and his or her right can exclude enforcement, the People's Court shall support the objection:  
(1) A lawful and valid written sales contract was concluded before the seal-up by the People's Court;  
(2) The buyer had lawfully taken possession of the immovable property before the seal-up by the People's Court;  
(3) The full purchase price has been paid, or part of the purchase price has been paid in accordance with the contract and the remaining price has been delivered for enforcement as required by the People's Court;  
(4) The failure to register the transfer of ownership is not attributable to the buyer itself.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　金钱债权执行中，买受人对登记在被执行的房地产开发企业名下的商品房提出异议，符合下列情形且其权利能够排除执行的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）在人民法院查封之前已签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；  
（二）所购商品房系用于居住且买受人名下无其他用于居住的房屋；  
（三）已支付的价款超过合同约定总价款的百分之五十。

**Article 29** During the enforcement of monetary claims, where a buyer raises an objection against a commercial housing unit registered under the name of the real estate development enterprise that is the person subject to enforcement, and the following conditions are met and his or her right can exclude enforcement, the People's Court shall support the objection:  
(1) A lawful and valid written sales contract was concluded before the seal-up by the People's Court;  
(2) The purchased commercial housing unit is used for residence, and the buyer has no other housing for residence under his or her name;  
(3) The paid price exceeds fifty percent of the total contract price.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　金钱债权执行中，对被查封的办理了受让物权预告登记的不动产，受让人提出停止处分异议的，人民法院应予支持；符合物权登记条件，受让人提出排除执行异议的，应予支持。

**Article 30** During the enforcement of monetary claims, where a transferee raises an objection to stop the disposition of sealed-up immovable property for which a advance notice registration of the transfer of real right has been made, the People's Court shall support the objection; where the conditions for real right registration are met and the transferee raises an objection to exclude enforcement, the objection shall be supported.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　承租人请求在租赁期内阻止向受让人移交占有被执行的不动产，在人民法院查封之前已签订合法有效的书面租赁合同并占有使用该不动产的，人民法院应予支持。  
承租人与被执行人恶意串通，以明显不合理的低价承租被执行的不动产或者伪造交付租金证据的，对其提出的阻止移交占有的请求，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 31** Where a lessee requests, during the lease term, to prevent the transfer of possession of the immovable property of the person subject to enforcement to the transferee, and the lessee had concluded a lawful and valid written lease contract and had taken possession and used the immovable property before the seal-up by the People's Court, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where the lessee and the person subject to enforcement maliciously collude to lease the immovable property of the person subject to enforcement at a manifestly unreasonable low price or forge evidence of rent payment, the People's Court shall not support the request to prevent the transfer of possession raised by the lessee.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本规定施行后尚未审查终结的执行异议和复议案件，适用本规定。本规定施行前已经审查终结的执行异议和复议案件，人民法院依法提起执行监督程序的，不适用本规定。

**Article 32** Where enforcement objection and reconsideration cases have not been finally examined after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall apply. Where enforcement objection and reconsideration cases have been finally examined before these Provisions come into force, and the People's Court initiates enforcement supervision procedures according to law, these Provisions shall not apply.
