# 最高人民法院关于内地与澳门特别行政区关于相互认可和执行民商事判决的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕2号  
**公布日期**：2006-03-21  
**生效日期**：2006-04-01

> 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区经协商，达成《内地与澳门特别行政区关于相互认可和执行民商事判决的安排》（以下简称《安排》），并于2006年2月28日签署。本《安排》已于2006年2月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1378次会议通过，现予公布。根据双方一致意见，本《安排》自2006年4月1日起生效。 2006年3月21日 最高人民法院 关于内地与澳门特别行政区关于相互认可 和执行民商事判决的安排 法释〔2006〕2号 （2006年2月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1378次会议通过　2006年3月21日最高人民法院公告公布　自2006年4月1日起生效）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　内地与澳门特别行政区民商事案件（在内地包括劳动争议案件，在澳门特别行政区包括劳动民事案件）判决的相互认可和执行，适用本安排。  
本安排亦适用于刑事案件中有关民事损害赔偿的判决、裁定。  
本安排不适用于行政案件。

**Article 1** This Arrangement shall apply to the mutual recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial cases between the Mainland and the Macao Special Administrative Region (including labor dispute cases in the Mainland and labor civil cases in the Macao Special Administrative Region).  
This Arrangement shall also apply to judgments and rulings concerning civil damages in criminal cases.  
This Arrangement shall not apply to administrative cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本安排所称“判决”，在内地包括：判决、裁定、决定、调解书、支付令；在澳门特别行政区包括：裁判、判决、确认和解的裁定、法官的决定或者批示。  
本安排所称“被请求方”，指内地或者澳门特别行政区双方中，受理认可和执行判决申请的一方。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Arrangement, "judgment" in the Mainland includes: judgments, rulings, decisions, mediation statements, and payment orders; in the Macao Special Administrative Region includes: judgments, decisions, rulings confirming settlements, and judges' decisions or instructions.  
For the purposes of this Arrangement, "requested party" means the party, between the Mainland and the Macao Special Administrative Region, that accepts the application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　一方法院作出的具有给付内容的生效判决，当事人可以向对方有管辖权的法院申请认可和执行。  
没有给付内容，或者不需要执行，但需要通过司法程序予以认可的判决，当事人可以向对方法院单独申请认可，也可以直接以该判决作为证据在对方法院的诉讼程序中使用。

**Article 3** Where a court of one party has rendered an effective judgment with payment content, a party may apply to the competent court of the other party for recognition and enforcement.  
Where a judgment has no payment content or does not require enforcement but needs to be recognized through judicial procedures, a party may apply separately to the court of the other party for recognition, or may directly use such judgment as evidence in the proceedings before the court of the other party.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　内地有权受理认可和执行判决申请的法院为被申请人住所地、经常居住地或者财产所在地的中级人民法院。两个或者两个以上中级人民法院均有管辖权的，申请人应当选择向其中一个中级人民法院提出申请。  
澳门特别行政区有权受理认可判决申请的法院为中级法院，有权执行的法院为初级法院。

**Article 4** The courts in the Mainland competent to accept applications for recognition and enforcement of judgments shall be the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the respondent has its domicile, habitual residence, or property. Where two or more Intermediate People's Courts have jurisdiction, the applicant shall choose one of them to submit the application.  
The court in the Macao Special Administrative Region competent to accept applications for recognition of judgments shall be the Intermediate Court, and the court competent to execute shall be the Primary Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　被申请人在内地和澳门特别行政区均有可供执行财产的，申请人可以向一地法院提出执行申请。  
申请人向一地法院提出执行申请的同时，可以向另一地法院申请查封、扣押或者冻结被执行人的财产。待一地法院执行完毕后，可以根据该地法院出具的执行情况证明，就不足部分向另一地法院申请采取处分财产的执行措施。  
两地法院执行财产的总额，不得超过依据判决和法律规定所确定的数额。

**Article 5** Where the respondent has property available for enforcement in both the Mainland and the Macao Special Administrative Region, the applicant may file an application for enforcement with the court of one place.  
While filing an application for enforcement with the court of one place, the applicant may apply to the court of the other place for sealing up, seizing, or freezing the property of the person subject to enforcement. After the court of one place has completed enforcement, the applicant may, based on the enforcement certificate issued by that court, apply to the court of the other place for enforcement measures involving disposition of property for the unsatisfied portion.  
The total amount of property enforced by the courts of the two places shall not exceed the amount determined according to the judgment and the provisions of law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　请求认可和执行判决的申请书，应当载明下列事项：  
（一）申请人或者被申请人为自然人的，应当载明其姓名及住所；为法人或者其他组织的，应当载明其名称及住所，以及其法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务和住所；  
（二）请求认可和执行的判决的案号和判决日期；  
（三）请求认可和执行判决的理由、标的，以及该判决在判决作出地法院的执行情况。

**Article 6** An application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment shall specify the following particulars:  
(1) if the applicant or the respondent is a natural person, the applicant's or respondent's name and domicile shall be specified; if it is a legal person or any other organization, its name and domicile, and the name, position, and domicile of its legal representative or principal responsible person shall be specified;  
(2) the case number and date of the judgment sought to be recognized and enforced;  
(3) the reasons and subject matter of the application for recognition and enforcement, and the status of enforcement of the judgment in the court that rendered the judgment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请书应当附生效判决书副本，或者经作出生效判决的法院盖章的证明书，同时应当附作出生效判决的法院或者有权限机构出具的证明下列事项的相关文件：  
（一）传唤属依法作出，但判决书已经证明的除外；  
（二）无诉讼行为能力人依法得到代理，但判决书已经证明的除外；  
（三）根据判决作出地的法律，判决已经送达当事人，并已生效；  
（四）申请人为法人的，应当提供法人营业执照副本或者法人登记证明书；  
（五）判决作出地法院发出的执行情况证明。  
如被请求方法院认为已充分了解有关事项时，可以免除提交相关文件。  
被请求方法院对当事人提供的判决书的真实性有疑问时，可以请求作出生效判决的法院予以确认。

**Article 7** The application shall be accompanied by a copy of the effective judgment or a certificate sealed by the court that rendered the effective judgment, and shall also be accompanied by relevant documents issued by the court that rendered the effective judgment or a competent authority proving the following:  
(1) the summons was issued in accordance with law, unless proven by the judgment itself;  
(2) a person without capacity to engage in litigation was represented in accordance with law, unless proven by the judgment itself;  
(3) the judgment has been served on the parties and has become effective according to the law of the place where the judgment was rendered;  
(4) if the applicant is a legal person, a copy of the business license of the legal person or a certificate of registration of the legal person shall be provided;  
(5) a certificate of enforcement status issued by the court of the place where the judgment was rendered.  
If the requested court considers that the relevant matters are sufficiently known, it may exempt the submission of the relevant documents.  
If the requested court has doubts about the authenticity of the judgment provided by the party, it may request the court that rendered the effective judgment to confirm it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请书应当用中文制作。所附司法文书及其相关文件未用中文制作的，应当提供中文译本。其中法院判决书未用中文制作的，应当提供由法院出具的中文译本。

**Article 8** The application shall be made in the Chinese language. If the attached judicial documents and related documents are not made in the Chinese language, a Chinese translation shall be provided. If the court judgment is not made in the Chinese language, a Chinese translation issued by the court shall be provided.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　法院收到申请人请求认可和执行判决的申请后，应当将申请书送达被申请人。  
被申请人有权提出答辩。

**Article 9** After receiving an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, the court shall serve the application on the respondent.  
The respondent shall be entitled to submit a defense.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被请求方法院应当尽快审查认可和执行的请求，并作出裁定。

**Article 10** The requested court shall review the request for recognition and enforcement as soon as possible and render a ruling.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被请求方法院经审查核实存在下列情形之一的，裁定不予认可：  
（一）根据被请求方的法律，判决所确认的事项属被请求方法院专属管辖；  
（二）在被请求方法院已存在相同诉讼，该诉讼先于待认可判决的诉讼提起，且被请求方法院具有管辖权；  
（三）被请求方法院已认可或者执行被请求方法院以外的法院或仲裁机构就相同诉讼作出的判决或仲裁裁决；  
（四）根据判决作出地的法律规定，败诉的当事人未得到合法传唤，或者无诉讼行为能力人未依法得到代理；  
（五）根据判决作出地的法律规定，申请认可和执行的判决尚未发生法律效力，或者因再审被裁定中止执行；  
（六）在内地认可和执行判决将违反内地法律的基本原则或者社会公共利益；在澳门特别行政区认可和执行判决将违反澳门特别行政区法律的基本原则或者公共秩序。

**Article 11** The requested court shall, upon examination and verification, rule not to recognize if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) according to the law of the requested party, the matters affirmed by the judgment fall within the exclusive jurisdiction of the court of the requested party;  
(2) there is already a pending action before the court of the requested party, which was initiated earlier than the action for the judgment to be recognized, and the court of the requested party has jurisdiction;  
(3) the court of the requested party has already recognized or enforced a judgment or arbitral award made by a court or arbitral institution other than the court of the requested party in the same action;  
(4) according to the law of the place where the judgment was rendered, the losing party was not legally summoned, or a person without capacity to engage in litigation was not legally represented;  
(5) according to the law of the place where the judgment was rendered, the judgment sought to be recognized and enforced has not yet become legally effective, or its enforcement has been suspended by a ruling due to retrial;  
(6) recognizing and enforcing the judgment in the Mainland would violate the fundamental principles of the laws of the Mainland or social public interests; recognizing and enforcing the judgment in the Macao Special Administrative Region would violate the fundamental principles of the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region or public order.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　法院就认可和执行判决的请求作出裁定后，应当及时送达。  
当事人对认可与否的裁定不服的，在内地可以向上一级人民法院提请复议，在澳门特别行政区可以根据其法律规定提起上诉；对执行中作出的裁定不服的，可以根据被请求方法律的规定，向上级法院寻求救济。

**Article 12** After the court renders a ruling on the request for recognition and enforcement, it shall serve the ruling in a timely manner.  
If a party disagrees with the ruling on whether to recognize, the party may, in the Mainland, apply for reconsideration to the next higher People's Court, and in the Macao Special Administrative Region, file an appeal according to its law; if a party disagrees with a ruling made during enforcement, the party may seek relief from a higher court according to the law of the requested party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经裁定予以认可的判决，与被请求方法院的判决具有同等效力。判决有给付内容的，当事人可以向该方有管辖权的法院申请执行。

**Article 13** A judgment that has been ruled to be recognized shall have the same effect as a judgment of the requested court. If the judgment has payment content, the party may apply to the competent court of that place for enforcement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被请求方法院不能对判决所确认的所有请求予以认可和执行时，可以认可和执行其中的部分请求。

**Article 14** Where the requested court cannot recognize and enforce all claims affirmed by the judgment, it may recognize and enforce part of them.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　法院受理认可和执行判决的申请之前或者之后，可以按照被请求方法律关于财产保全的规定，根据申请人的申请，对被申请人的财产采取保全措施。

**Article 15** Before or after accepting an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, the court may, in accordance with the provisions of the law of the requested party on property preservation and upon the application of the applicant, take preservation measures against the respondent's property.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在被请求方法院受理认可和执行判决的申请期间，或者判决已获认可和执行，当事人再行提起相同诉讼的，被请求方法院不予受理。

**Article 16** If, during the period when the requested court has accepted an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, or after the judgment has been recognized and enforced, a party initiates the same action again, the requested court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于根据本安排第十一条第（一）、（四）、（六）项不予认可的判决，申请人不得再行提起认可和执行的申请。但根据被请求方的法律，被请求方法院有管辖权的，当事人可以就相同案件事实向当地法院另行提起诉讼。  
本安排第十一条第（五）项所指的判决，在不予认可的情形消除后，申请人可以再行提起认可和执行的申请。

**Article 17** For a judgment that is not recognized under Article 11(1), (4), or (6) of this Arrangement, the applicant shall not file another application for recognition and enforcement. However, if the court of the requested party has jurisdiction according to the law of the requested party, the party may bring a separate action before the local court on the same facts.  
For a judgment referred to in Article 11(5) of this Arrangement, after the circumstances causing non-recognition have been eliminated, the applicant may file another application for recognition and enforcement.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　为适用本安排，由一方有权限公共机构（包括公证员）作成或者公证的文书正本、副本及译本，免除任何认证手续而可以在对方使用。

**Article 18** For the purposes of this Arrangement, the originals, copies, and translations of documents made or notarized by a competent public authority (including notaries) of one party shall be exempted from any authentication formalities and may be used in the other party.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人依据本安排申请认可和执行判决，应当根据被请求方法律规定，交纳诉讼费用、执行费用。  
申请人在生效判决作出地获准缓交、减交、免交诉讼费用的，在被请求方法院申请认可和执行判决时，应当享有同等待遇。

**Article 19** An applicant who applies for recognition and enforcement of a judgment under this Arrangement shall pay litigation fees and enforcement fees according to the provisions of the law of the requested party.  
If the applicant is permitted in the place where the effective judgment was rendered to postpone, reduce, or be exempted from paying litigation fees, the applicant shall enjoy the same treatment when applying for recognition and enforcement of the judgment in the requested court.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对民商事判决的认可和执行，除本安排有规定的以外，适用被请求方的法律规定。

**Article 20** The recognition and enforcement of civil and commercial judgments shall be governed by the law of the requested party, except as otherwise provided in this Arrangement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本安排生效前提出的认可和执行请求，不适用本安排。  
两地法院自1999年12月20日以后至本安排生效前作出的判决，当事人未向对方法院申请认可和执行，或者对方法院拒绝受理的，仍可以于本安排生效后提出申请。  
澳门特别行政区法院在上述期间内作出的判决，当事人向内地人民法院申请认可和执行的期限，自本安排生效之日起重新计算。

**Article 21** A request for recognition and enforcement made before the effective date of this Arrangement shall not be governed by this Arrangement.  
For judgments rendered by the courts of the two places after December 20, 1999 but before the effective date of this Arrangement, if a party has not applied to the court of the other party for recognition and enforcement, or the court of the other party has refused to accept, the party may still file an application after the effective date of this Arrangement.  
For a judgment rendered by a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region during the above period, the time limit for a party to apply to a People's Court in the Mainland for recognition and enforcement shall be recalculated from the effective date of this Arrangement.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本安排在执行过程中遇有问题或者需要修改，应当由最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区协商解决。

**Article 22** If any problem arises during the implementation of this Arrangement or modification is necessary, it shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　为执行本安排，最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院应当相互提供相关法律资料。  
最高人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院每年相互通报执行本安排的情况。

**Article 23** For the implementation of this Arrangement, the Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall provide each other with relevant legal materials.  
The Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall annually inform each other of the situation regarding the implementation of this Arrangement.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本安排自2006年4月1日起生效。

**Article 24** This Arrangement shall take effect as of April 1, 2006.
