# 最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕1号  
**公布日期**：2015-01-06  
**生效日期**：2015-01-07

> 最高人民法院《关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2014年12月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1631次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年1月7日起施行。 2015年1月6日 最高人民法院 关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕1号 为正确审理环境民事公益诉讼案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》、《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　法律规定的机关和有关组织依据民事诉讼法第五十五条、环境保护法第五十八条等法律的规定，对已经损害社会公共利益或者具有损害社会公共利益重大风险的污染环境、破坏生态的行为提起诉讼，符合民事诉讼法第一百一十九条第二项、第三项、第四项规定的，人民法院应予受理。 第二条　依照法律、法规的规定，在设区的市级以上人民政府民政部门登记的社会团体、民办非企业单位以及基金会等，可以认定为环境保护法第五十八条规定的社会组织。 第三条　设区的市，自治州、盟、地区，不设区的地级市，直辖市的区以上人民政府民政部门，可以认定为环境保护法第五十八条规定的“设区的市级以上人民政府民政部门”。 第四条　社会组织章程确定的宗旨和主要业务范围是维护社会公共利益，且从事环境保护公益活动的，可以认定为环境保护法第五十八条规定的“专门从事环境保护公益活动”。 社会组织提起的诉讼所涉及的社会公共利益，应与其宗旨和业务范围具有关联性。 第五条　社会组织在提起诉讼前五年内未因从事业务活动违反法律、法规的规定受过行政、刑事处罚的，可以认定为环境保护法第五十八条规定的“无违法记录”。 第六条　第一审环境民事公益诉讼案件由污染环境、破坏生态行为发生地、损害结果地或者被告住所地的中级以上人民法院管辖。 中级人民法院认为确有必要的，可以在报请高级人民法院批准后，裁定将本院管辖的第一审环境民事公益诉讼案件交由基层人民法院审理。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　法律规定的机关和有关组织依据[民事诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-55)、[环境保护法第五十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-58)等法律的规定，对已经损害社会公共利益或者具有损害社会公共利益重大风险的污染环境、破坏生态的行为提起诉讼，符合[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)第二项、第三项、第四项规定的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** If the authorities and relevant organizations as provided by law, in accordance with the provisions of Article 55 of the Civil Procedure Law, Article 58 of the Environmental Protection Law, and other laws, initiate a lawsuit against acts of environmental pollution or ecological damage that have harmed the public interest or pose a significant risk of harming the public interest, and if the provisions of items (2), (3), and (4) of Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law are met, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依照法律、法规的规定，在设区的市级以上人民政府民政部门登记的社会团体、民办非企业单位以及基金会等，可以认定为[环境保护法第五十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-58)规定的社会组织。

**Article 2** Social groups, private non-enterprise units, and foundations registered with the civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the level of a city with districts, in accordance with laws and regulations, may be recognized as social organizations as provided in Article 58 of the Environmental Protection Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　设区的市，自治州、盟、地区，不设区的地级市，直辖市的区以上人民政府民政部门，可以认定为[环境保护法第五十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-58)规定的“设区的市级以上人民政府民政部门”。

**Article 3** The civil affairs departments of the people's governments of cities with districts, autonomous prefectures, leagues, prefectures, prefecture-level cities without districts, and districts of municipalities directly under the Central Government may be recognized as the 'civil affairs departments of the people's governments at or above the level of a city with districts' as provided in Article 58 of the Environmental Protection Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　社会组织章程确定的宗旨和主要业务范围是维护社会公共利益，且从事环境保护公益活动的，可以认定为[环境保护法第五十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-58)规定的“专门从事环境保护公益活动”。  
社会组织提起的诉讼所涉及的社会公共利益，应与其宗旨和业务范围具有关联性。

**Article 4** If the purpose and main business scope as determined in the bylaws of a social organization are to safeguard the public interest, and it engages in environmental protection public welfare activities, it may be recognized as 'specially engaging in environmental protection public welfare activities' as provided in Article 58 of the Environmental Protection Law.  
The public interest involved in the lawsuit initiated by the social organization shall be related to its purpose and business scope.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　社会组织在提起诉讼前五年内未因从事业务活动违反法律、法规的规定受过行政、刑事处罚的，可以认定为[环境保护法第五十八条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-58)规定的“无违法记录”。

**Article 5** If a social organization has not received administrative or criminal punishment for violating laws or regulations in its business activities within the five years prior to initiating the lawsuit, it may be recognized as having 'no illegal record' as provided in Article 58 of the Environmental Protection Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　第一审环境民事公益诉讼案件由污染环境、破坏生态行为发生地、损害结果地或者被告住所地的中级以上人民法院管辖。  
中级人民法院认为确有必要的，可以在报请高级人民法院批准后，裁定将本院管辖的第一审环境民事公益诉讼案件交由基层人民法院审理。  
同一原告或者不同原告对同一污染环境、破坏生态行为分别向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院提起环境民事公益诉讼的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖，必要时由共同上级人民法院指定管辖。

**Article 6** First-instance environmental civil public interest litigation cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate people's courts or above at the place where the act of environmental pollution or ecological damage occurred, the place where the damage result occurred, or the domicile of the defendant.  
If an intermediate people's court deems it truly necessary, it may, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the higher people's court, rule to assign the first-instance environmental civil public interest litigation case under its jurisdiction to a primary people's court for trial.  
If the same plaintiff or different plaintiffs initiate environmental civil public interest litigation against the same act of environmental pollution or ecological damage before two or more people's courts with jurisdiction, the people's court that first files the case shall have jurisdiction; if necessary, the common superior people's court shall designate jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经最高人民法院批准，高级人民法院可以根据本辖区环境和生态保护的实际情况，在辖区内确定部分中级人民法院受理第一审环境民事公益诉讼案件。  
中级人民法院管辖环境民事公益诉讼案件的区域由高级人民法院确定。

**Article 7** With the approval of the Supreme People's Court, the higher people's court may, based on the actual conditions of environmental and ecological protection in its jurisdiction, designate some intermediate people's courts within its jurisdiction to accept first-instance environmental civil public interest litigation cases.  
The regions over which intermediate people's courts have jurisdiction for environmental civil public interest litigation cases shall be determined by the higher people's court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　提起环境民事公益诉讼应当提交下列材料：  
（一）符合[民事诉讼法第一百二十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-121)规定的起诉状，并按照被告人数提出副本；  
（二）被告的行为已经损害社会公共利益或者具有损害社会公共利益重大风险的初步证明材料；  
（三）社会组织提起诉讼的，应当提交社会组织登记证书、章程、起诉前连续五年的年度工作报告书或者年检报告书，以及由其法定代表人或者负责人签字并加盖公章的无违法记录的声明。

**Article 8** Initiating an environmental civil public interest litigation shall submit the following materials:  
(1) A complaint that complies with Article 121 of the Civil Procedure Law, and copies shall be submitted according to the number of defendants;  
(2) Preliminary evidentiary materials showing that the defendant's act has harmed the public interest or poses a significant risk of harming the public interest;  
(3) If initiated by a social organization, the social organization shall submit its registration certificate, bylaws, annual work reports or annual inspection reports for the five consecutive years prior to initiating the lawsuit, and a declaration signed by its legal representative or responsible person and affixed with its official seal stating that it has no illegal record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院认为原告提出的诉讼请求不足以保护社会公共利益的，可以向其释明变更或者增加停止侵害、恢复原状等诉讼请求。

**Article 9** If the People's Court considers that the claims proposed by the plaintiff are insufficient to protect the public interest, it may explain to the plaintiff that it may change or add claims such as cessation of infringement and restoration to the original condition.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院受理环境民事公益诉讼后，应当在立案之日起五日内将起诉状副本发送被告，并公告案件受理情况。  
有权提起诉讼的其他机关和社会组织在公告之日起三十日内申请参加诉讼，经审查符合法定条件的，人民法院应当将其列为共同原告；逾期申请的，不予准许。  
公民、法人和其他组织以人身、财产受到损害为由申请参加诉讼的，告知其另行起诉。

**Article 10** After the People's Court accepts an environmental civil public interest litigation, it shall, within five days from the date of filing the case, send a copy of the complaint to the defendant and announce the acceptance of the case.  
If other authorities and social organizations that have the right to initiate a lawsuit apply to participate in the litigation within thirty days from the date of the announcement, and upon examination they meet the statutory conditions, the People's Court shall list them as co-plaintiffs; if the application is overdue, it shall not be permitted.  
If citizens, legal persons, or other organizations apply to participate in the litigation on the grounds that their personal or property rights have been harmed, they shall be informed to initiate a separate lawsuit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　检察机关、负有环境保护监督管理职责的部门及其他机关、社会组织、企业事业单位依据[民事诉讼法第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-15)的规定，可以通过提供法律咨询、提交书面意见、协助调查取证等方式支持社会组织依法提起环境民事公益诉讼。

**Article 11** Procuratorial organs, departments with environmental protection supervision and management responsibilities, and other authorities, social organizations, enterprises, and institutions may, in accordance with Article 15 of the Civil Procedure Law, support social organizations in lawfully initiating environmental civil public interest litigation by providing legal consultation, submitting written opinions, assisting in investigation and evidence collection, and other means.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院受理环境民事公益诉讼后，应当在十日内告知对被告行为负有环境保护监督管理职责的部门。

**Article 12** After the People's Court accepts an environmental civil public interest litigation, it shall, within ten days, inform the department with environmental protection supervision and management responsibilities over the defendant's act.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　原告请求被告提供其排放的主要污染物名称、排放方式、排放浓度和总量、超标排放情况以及防治污染设施的建设和运行情况等环境信息，法律、法规、规章规定被告应当持有或者有证据证明被告持有而拒不提供，如果原告主张相关事实不利于被告的，人民法院可以推定该主张成立。

**Article 13** If the plaintiff requests the defendant to provide environmental information such as the names of main pollutants discharged, discharge methods, discharge concentrations and total amounts, circumstances of excessive discharge, and the construction and operation of pollution prevention facilities, and if laws, regulations, or rules provide that the defendant shall possess such information or there is evidence proving that the defendant possesses it but the defendant refuses to provide it, and if the plaintiff asserts facts unfavorable to the defendant, the People's Court may presume that the assertion is established.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件需要的证据，人民法院认为必要的，应当调查收集。  
对于应当由原告承担举证责任且为维护社会公共利益所必要的专门性问题，人民法院可以委托具备资格的鉴定人进行鉴定。

**Article 14** For evidence needed for trial of an environmental civil public interest litigation case, if the People's Court considers it necessary, it shall investigate and collect the evidence.  
For specialized issues that shall bear the burden of adducing evidence by the plaintiff and are necessary for safeguarding the public interest, the People's Court may entrust qualified expert evaluators to conduct expert evaluation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人申请通知有专门知识的人出庭，就鉴定人作出的鉴定意见或者就因果关系、生态环境修复方式、生态环境修复费用以及生态环境受到损害至恢复原状期间服务功能的损失等专门性问题提出意见的，人民法院可以准许。  
前款规定的专家意见经质证，可以作为认定事实的根据。

**Article 15** If a party applies to notify a person with specialized knowledge to appear in court to present opinions on the expert opinion made by the expert evaluator or on specialized issues such as causation, ecological environment restoration methods, ecological environment restoration costs, and loss of service functions during the period from when the ecological environment is harmed to restoration to the original condition, the People's Court may permit it.  
The expert opinions provided in the preceding paragraph, after cross-examination, may serve as the basis for finding facts.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　原告在诉讼过程中承认的对己方不利的事实和认可的证据，人民法院认为损害社会公共利益的，应当不予确认。

**Article 16** If the plaintiff admits facts unfavorable to itself or recognizes evidence during the litigation process, and the People's Court considers that it harms the public interest, it shall not confirm such facts or evidence.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　环境民事公益诉讼案件审理过程中，被告以反诉方式提出诉讼请求的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 17** During the trial of an environmental civil public interest litigation case, if the defendant raises claims by way of a counterclaim, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对污染环境、破坏生态，已经损害社会公共利益或者具有损害社会公共利益重大风险的行为，原告可以请求被告承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、恢复原状、赔偿损失、赔礼道歉等民事责任。

**Article 18** For acts of environmental pollution or ecological damage that have harmed the public interest or pose a significant risk of harming the public interest, the plaintiff may request the defendant to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement, removal of obstruction, elimination of danger, restoration to the original condition, compensation for losses, and apology.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　原告为防止生态环境损害的发生和扩大，请求被告停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险的，人民法院可以依法予以支持。  
原告为停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险采取合理预防、处置措施而发生的费用，请求被告承担的，人民法院可以依法予以支持。

**Article 19** If the plaintiff requests the defendant to cease infringement, remove obstruction, or eliminate danger in order to prevent the occurrence or expansion of ecological environment damage, the People's Court may support the request according to law.  
If the plaintiff requests the defendant to bear the expenses incurred by the plaintiff for taking reasonable prevention and disposal measures to cease infringement, remove obstruction, or eliminate danger, the People's Court may support the request according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　原告请求恢复原状的，人民法院可以依法判决被告将生态环境修复到损害发生之前的状态和功能。无法完全修复的，可以准许采用替代性修复方式。  
人民法院可以在判决被告修复生态环境的同时，确定被告不履行修复义务时应承担的生态环境修复费用；也可以直接判决被告承担生态环境修复费用。  
生态环境修复费用包括制定、实施修复方案的费用和监测、监管等费用。

**Article 20** If the plaintiff requests restoration to the original condition, the People's Court may, according to law, order the defendant to restore the ecological environment to its state and function before the damage occurred. If complete restoration is impossible, alternative restoration methods may be permitted.  
The People's Court may, while ordering the defendant to restore the ecological environment, determine the ecological environment restoration costs that the defendant shall bear if it fails to perform the restoration obligation; or it may directly order the defendant to bear the ecological environment restoration costs.  
Ecological environment restoration costs include expenses for formulating and implementing restoration plans, as well as expenses for monitoring and supervision.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　原告请求被告赔偿生态环境受到损害至恢复原状期间服务功能损失的，人民法院可以依法予以支持。

**Article 21** If the plaintiff requests the defendant to compensate for the loss of service functions during the period from when the ecological environment is harmed to restoration to the original condition, the People's Court may support the request according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　原告请求被告承担检验、鉴定费用，合理的律师费以及为诉讼支出的其他合理费用的，人民法院可以依法予以支持。

**Article 22** If the plaintiff requests the defendant to bear inspection and expert evaluation costs, reasonable attorney's fees, and other reasonable expenses incurred for litigation, the People's Court may support the request according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生态环境修复费用难以确定或者确定具体数额所需鉴定费用明显过高的，人民法院可以结合污染环境、破坏生态的范围和程度、生态环境的稀缺性、生态环境恢复的难易程度、防治污染设备的运行成本、被告因侵害行为所获得的利益以及过错程度等因素，并可以参考负有环境保护监督管理职责的部门的意见、专家意见等，予以合理确定。

**Article 23** If ecological environment restoration costs are difficult to determine or the expert evaluation costs required to determine the specific amount are obviously excessive, the People's Court may, by taking into account factors such as the scope and degree of environmental pollution or ecological damage, the scarcity of the ecological environment, the difficulty of ecological environment restoration, the operation costs of pollution prevention equipment, the benefits gained by the defendant from the infringing act and the degree of fault, and by referring to opinions of departments with environmental protection supervision and management responsibilities, expert opinions, etc., reasonably determine the costs.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民法院判决被告承担的生态环境修复费用、生态环境受到损害至恢复原状期间服务功能损失等款项，应当用于修复被损害的生态环境。  
其他环境民事公益诉讼中败诉原告所需承担的调查取证、专家咨询、检验、鉴定等必要费用，可以酌情从上述款项中支付。

**Article 24** The sums such as ecological environment restoration costs and compensation for loss of service functions during the period from when the ecological environment is harmed to restoration to the original condition that the People's Court orders the defendant to bear shall be used to restore the damaged ecological environment.  
The necessary expenses such as investigation and evidence collection, expert consultation, inspection, and expert evaluation that the losing plaintiff in other environmental civil public interest litigation needs to bear may be paid from the aforesaid sums as appropriate.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　环境民事公益诉讼当事人达成调解协议或者自行达成和解协议后，人民法院应当将协议内容公告，公告期间不少于三十日。  
公告期满后，人民法院审查认为调解协议或者和解协议的内容不损害社会公共利益的，应当出具调解书。当事人以达成和解协议为由申请撤诉的，不予准许。  
调解书应当写明诉讼请求、案件的基本事实和协议内容，并应当公开。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 25** After the parties to an environmental civil public interest litigation reach a mediation agreement or voluntarily reach a settlement agreement, the People's Court shall announce the content of the agreement, and the announcement period shall be no less than thirty days.  
After the announcement period expires, if the People's Court, upon review, considers that the content of the mediation agreement or settlement agreement does not harm the public interest, it shall issue a mediation letter. If a party applies to withdraw the lawsuit on the grounds of reaching a settlement agreement, it shall not be permitted.  
The mediation letter shall set forth the claims, basic facts of the case, and the content of the agreement, and shall be made public.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　负有环境保护监督管理职责的部门依法履行监管职责而使原告诉讼请求全部实现，原告申请撤诉的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 26** If the department with environmental protection supervision and management responsibilities lawfully performs its supervisory duties, thereby realizing all of the plaintiff's claims, and the plaintiff applies to withdraw the lawsuit, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　法庭辩论终结后，原告申请撤诉的，人民法院不予准许，但[本解释第二十六条](#art-26)规定的情形除外。

**Article 27** After the conclusion of court debate, if the plaintiff applies to withdraw the lawsuit, the People's Court shall not permit it, except for the circumstances provided in Article 26 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　环境民事公益诉讼案件的裁判生效后，有权提起诉讼的其他机关和社会组织就同一污染环境、破坏生态行为另行起诉，有下列情形之一的，人民法院应予受理：  
（一）前案原告的起诉被裁定驳回的；  
（二）前案原告申请撤诉被裁定准许的，但[本解释第二十六条](#art-26)规定的情形除外。  
环境民事公益诉讼案件的裁判生效后，有证据证明存在前案审理时未发现的损害，有权提起诉讼的机关和社会组织另行起诉的，人民法院应予受理。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 28** After a judgment in an environmental civil public interest litigation case becomes effective, if other authorities and social organizations that have the right to initiate a lawsuit separately file a lawsuit against the same act of environmental pollution or ecological damage, and any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) The previous plaintiff's prosecution was dismissed by ruling;  
(2) The previous plaintiff's application to withdraw the lawsuit was permitted by ruling, except for the circumstances provided in Article 26 of this Interpretation.  
After a judgment in an environmental civil public interest litigation case becomes effective, if there is evidence proving that damage not discovered during the trial of the previous case exists, and an authority or social organization that has the right to initiate a lawsuit separately files a lawsuit, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法律规定的机关和社会组织提起环境民事公益诉讼的，不影响因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为受到人身、财产损害的公民、法人和其他组织依据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)的规定提起诉讼。

**Article 29** The initiation of an environmental civil public interest litigation by the authorities and social organizations as provided by law shall not affect the right of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations that have suffered personal or property damage from the same act of environmental pollution or ecological damage to initiate a lawsuit in accordance with Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　已为环境民事公益诉讼生效裁判认定的事实，因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为依据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定提起诉讼的原告、被告均无需举证证明，但原告对该事实有异议并有相反证据足以推翻的除外。  
对于环境民事公益诉讼生效裁判就被告是否存在法律规定的不承担责任或者减轻责任的情形、行为与损害之间是否存在因果关系、被告承担责任的大小等所作的认定，因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为依据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定提起诉讼的原告主张适用的，人民法院应予支持，但被告有相反证据足以推翻的除外。被告主张直接适用对其有利的认定的，人民法院不予支持，被告仍应举证证明。

**Article 30** Facts that have been found by an effective judgment in an environmental civil public interest litigation shall not require proof by the plaintiff or defendant in a lawsuit initiated in accordance with Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law regarding the same act of environmental pollution or ecological damage, unless the plaintiff disputes such facts and has sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.  
Regarding the findings in an effective judgment of an environmental civil public interest litigation as to whether the defendant has circumstances for non-liability or reduced liability as provided by law, whether there is causation between the act and the damage, and the degree of liability of the defendant, if the plaintiff in a lawsuit initiated in accordance with Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law regarding the same act of environmental pollution or ecological damage claims application of such findings, the People's Court shall support it, unless the defendant has sufficient contrary evidence to overturn the findings. If the defendant claims direct application of findings favorable to it, the People's Court shall not support it, and the defendant shall still bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被告因污染环境、破坏生态在环境民事公益诉讼和其他民事诉讼中均承担责任，其财产不足以履行全部义务的，应当先履行其他民事诉讼生效裁判所确定的义务，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 31** If a defendant bears liability in both environmental civil public interest litigation and other civil lawsuits due to environmental pollution or ecological damage, and its property is insufficient to satisfy all obligations, it shall first perform the obligations determined by the effective judgments in other civil lawsuits, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　发生法律效力的环境民事公益诉讼案件的裁判，需要采取强制执行措施的，应当移送执行。

**Article 32** If compulsory enforcement measures are necessary for the effective judgment in an environmental civil public interest litigation case, it shall be transferred for enforcement.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　原告交纳诉讼费用确有困难，依法申请缓交的，人民法院应予准许。  
败诉或者部分败诉的原告申请减交或者免交诉讼费用的，人民法院应当依照《诉讼费用交纳办法》的规定，视原告的经济状况和案件的审理情况决定是否准许。

**Article 33** If the plaintiff truly has difficulty paying the litigation costs and lawfully applies for deferred payment, the People's Court shall permit it.  
If the plaintiff that loses the case or partially loses the case applies for reduction or exemption of litigation costs, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Payment of Litigation Costs, decide whether to permit it based on the plaintiff's financial situation and the trial circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　社会组织有通过诉讼违法收受财物等牟取经济利益行为的，人民法院可以根据情节轻重依法收缴其非法所得、予以罚款；涉嫌犯罪的，依法移送有关机关处理。  
社会组织通过诉讼牟取经济利益的，人民法院应当向登记管理机关或者有关机关发送司法建议，由其依法处理。

**Article 34** If a social organization engages in acts of illegally accepting property or other acts to seek economic benefits through litigation, the People's Court may, depending on the gravity of the circumstances, confiscate its illegal gains according to law and impose a fine; if a crime is suspected, it shall be transferred to the relevant authorities for handling according to law.  
If a social organization seeks economic benefits through litigation, the People's Court shall send a judicial recommendation to the registration management authority or relevant authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本解释施行前最高人民法院发布的司法解释和规范性文件，与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 35** If the judicial interpretations and normative documents issued by the Supreme People's Court before this Interpretation comes into force are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
