# 最高人民法院关于适用简易程序审理民事案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕15号  
**公布日期**：2003-09-10  
**生效日期**：2003-12-01

> （2003年7月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1280次会议通过　2003年9月10日最高人民法院公告公布　自2003年12月1日起施行） 为保障和方便当事人依法行使诉讼权利，保证人民法院公正、及时审理民事案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，结合民事审判经验和实际情况，制定本规定。 一、适用范围 第一条　基层人民法院根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百四十二条规定审理简单的民事案件，适用本规定，但有下列情形之一的案件除外： （一）起诉时被告下落不明的； （二）发回重审的； （三）共同诉讼中一方或者双方当事人人数众多的； （四）法律规定应当适用特别程序、审判监督程序、督促程序、公示催告程序和企业法人破产还债程序的； （五）人民法院认为不宜适用简易程序进行审理的。 第二条　基层人民法院适用第一审普通程序审理的民事案件，当事人各方自愿选择适用简易程序，经人民法院审查同意的，可以适用简易程序进行审理。 人民法院不得违反当事人自愿原则，将普通程序转为简易程序。 第三条　当事人就适用简易程序提出异议，人民法院认为异议成立的，或者人民法院在审理过程中发现不宜适用简易程序的，应当将案件转入普通程序审理。 二、起诉与答辩 第四条　原告本人不能书写起诉状，委托他人代写起诉状确有困难的，可以口头起诉。 原告口头起诉的，人民法院应当将当事人的基本情况、联系方式、诉讼请求、事实及理由予以准确记录，将相关证据予以登记。人民法院应当将上述记录和登记的内容向原告当面宣读，原告认为无误后应当签名或者捺印。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　基层人民法院根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百四十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-142)规定审理简单的民事案件，适用本规定，但有下列情形之一的案件除外：  
（一）起诉时被告下落不明的；  
（二）发回重审的；  
（三）共同诉讼中一方或者双方当事人人数众多的；  
（四）法律规定应当适用特别程序、审判监督程序、督促程序、公示催告程序和企业法人破产还债程序的；  
（五）人民法院认为不宜适用简易程序进行审理的。

**Article 1** A Primary People's Court that hears simple civil cases pursuant to Article 142 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply these Provisions, except for cases in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the whereabouts of the defendant is unknown at the time of prosecution;  
(2) the case is remanded for retrial;  
(3) one party or both parties in a joint action involve a large number of persons;  
(4) laws provide that special procedures, trial supervision procedures, supervision procedures, public summons and exhortation procedures, or enterprise legal person bankruptcy and debt repayment procedures shall be applicable; or  
(5) the People's Court considers that application of summary procedures is inappropriate.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　基层人民法院适用第一审普通程序审理的民事案件，当事人各方自愿选择适用简易程序，经人民法院审查同意的，可以适用简易程序进行审理。  
人民法院不得违反当事人自愿原则，将普通程序转为简易程序。

**Article 2** Where a civil case is heard by a Primary People's Court under ordinary procedures of first instance, if all parties voluntarily elect to apply summary procedures and the People's Court, upon examination, consents, the summary procedures may be applied for the hearing.  
The People's Court shall not convert the ordinary procedures into summary procedures in violation of the principle of the parties' voluntariness.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人就适用简易程序提出异议，人民法院认为异议成立的，或者人民法院在审理过程中发现不宜适用简易程序的，应当将案件转入普通程序审理。  
二、起诉与答辩

**Article 3** If a party raises an objection to the application of summary procedures and the People's Court finds the objection to be well-founded, or if during the hearing the People's Court discovers that the application of summary procedures is inappropriate, it shall transfer the case to ordinary procedures for hearing.  
II. Prosecution and Defense

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　原告本人不能书写起诉状，委托他人代写起诉状确有困难的，可以口头起诉。  
原告口头起诉的，人民法院应当将当事人的基本情况、联系方式、诉讼请求、事实及理由予以准确记录，将相关证据予以登记。人民法院应当将上述记录和登记的内容向原告当面宣读，原告认为无误后应当签名或者捺印。

**Article 4** If the plaintiff cannot personally write a statement of complaint and has genuine difficulty in entrusting another person to do so, the plaintiff may make an oral prosecution.  
Where the plaintiff makes an oral prosecution, the People's Court shall accurately record the basic information of the parties, their contact information, claims, facts and grounds, and shall register relevant evidence. The People's Court shall read the aforesaid records and registrations to the plaintiff in the plaintiff's presence. After the plaintiff confirms them as correct, the plaintiff shall sign or affix a thumbprint.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人应当在起诉或者答辩时向人民法院提供自己准确的送达地址、收件人、电话号码等其他联系方式，并签名或者捺印确认。  
送达地址应当写明受送达人住所地的邮政编码和详细地址；受送达人是有固定职业的自然人的，其从业的场所可以视为送达地址。

**Article 5** The parties shall, at the time of prosecution or defense, provide the People's Court with their accurate service addresses, recipients, telephone numbers and other contact information, and shall sign or affix a thumbprint to confirm.  
The service address shall include the postal code and detailed address of the residence of the person to be served; if the person to be served is a natural person with a fixed occupation, the place where that person works may be deemed to be the service address.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　原告起诉后，人民法院可以采取捎口信、电话、传真、电子邮件等简便方式随时传唤双方当事人、证人。

**Article 6** After the plaintiff brings a lawsuit, the People's Court may, by simple means such as leaving messages, telephone, fax, electronic mail, etc., summon the parties and witnesses at any time.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　双方当事人到庭后，被告同意口头答辩的，人民法院可以当即开庭审理；被告要求书面答辩的，人民法院应当将提交答辩状的期限和开庭的具体日期告知各方当事人，并向当事人说明逾期举证以及拒不到庭的法律后果，由各方当事人在笔录和开庭传票的送达回证上签名或者捺印。

**Article 7** After both parties appear in court, if the defendant agrees to an oral defense, the People's Court may conduct the hearing immediately; if the defendant requests a written defense, the People's Court shall inform each party of the time limit for submitting the defense and the specific date of the hearing, and shall explain to the parties the legal consequences of overdue evidence submission and failure to appear in court, and each party shall sign or affix a thumbprint on the record and the receipt of service of the summons.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院按照原告提供的被告的送达地址或者其他联系方式无法通知被告应诉的，应当按以下情况分别处理：  
（一）原告提供了被告准确的送达地址，但人民法院无法向被告直接送达或者留置送达应诉通知书的，应当将案件转入普通程序审理；  
（二）原告不能提供被告准确的送达地址，人民法院经查证后仍不能确定被告送达地址的，可以被告不明确为由裁定驳回原告起诉。

**Article 8** If the People's Court is unable to notify the defendant to appear in court using the defendant's service address or other contact information provided by the plaintiff, it shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the plaintiff has provided an accurate service address of the defendant but the People's Court cannot directly serve the notice of appearance on the defendant or serve it by leaving the document at the defendant's address, the case shall be transferred to ordinary procedures for hearing;  
(2) if the plaintiff cannot provide an accurate service address of the defendant and after investigation the People's Court still cannot ascertain the defendant's service address, it may dismiss the plaintiff's action on the ground that the defendant is not clearly identified.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被告到庭后拒绝提供自己的送达地址和联系方式的，人民法院应当告知其拒不提供送达地址的后果；经人民法院告知后被告仍然拒不提供的，按下列方式处理：  
（一）被告是自然人的，以其户籍登记中的住所地或者经常居住地为送达地址；  
（二）被告是法人或者其他组织的，应当以其工商登记或者其他依法登记、备案中的住所地为送达地址。  
人民法院应当将上述告知的内容记入笔录。

**Article 9** If, after appearing in court, the defendant refuses to provide its own service address and contact information, the People's Court shall inform it of the consequences of such refusal; if after being informed by the People's Court the defendant still refuses to provide such information, the matter shall be handled in the following manner:  
(1) if the defendant is a natural person, the service address shall be the residence recorded in its household registration or its habitual residence;  
(2) if the defendant is a legal person or other organization, the service address shall be its domicile as registered with industry and commerce authorities or as otherwise registered or filed according to law.  
The People's Court shall record the content of the aforesaid information in the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　因当事人自己提供的送达地址不准确、送达地址变更未及时告知人民法院，或者当事人拒不提供自己的送达地址而导致诉讼文书未能被当事人实际接收的，按下列方式处理：  
（一）邮寄送达的，以邮件回执上注明的退回之日视为送达之日；  
（二）直接送达的，送达人当场在送达回证上记明情况之日视为送达之日。  
上述内容，人民法院应当在原告起诉和被告答辩时以书面或者口头方式告知当事人。

**Article 10** Where, due to the service address provided by a party being inaccurate, or the party failing to timely inform the People's Court of a change of service address, or a party refusing to provide its own service address, a litigation document is not actually received by the party, the matter shall be handled in the following manner:  
(1) if served by mail, the date of return indicated on the mail receipt shall be deemed the date of service;  
(2) if served directly, the date on which the server makes a note on the service receipt shall be deemed the date of service.  
The People's Court shall inform the parties of the above content in writing or orally at the time of the plaintiff's prosecution and the defendant's defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　受送达的自然人以及他的同住成年家属拒绝签收诉讼文书的，或者法人、其他组织负责收件的人拒绝签收诉讼文书的，送达人应当依据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-79)的规定邀请有关基层组织或者所在单位的代表到场见证，被邀请的人不愿到场见证的，送达人应当在送达回证上记明拒收事由、时间和地点以及被邀请人不愿到场见证的情形，将诉讼文书留在受送达人的住所或者从业场所，即视为送达。  
受送达人的同住成年家属或者法人、其他组织负责收件的人是同一案件中另一方当事人的，不适用前款规定。  
三、审理前的准备

**Article 11** If the natural person to be served or the adult family member living with that person refuses to sign for the litigation document, or if the person responsible for receiving documents of a legal person or other organization refuses to sign for the litigation document, the server shall, pursuant to Article 79 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, invite a representative of the relevant grassroots organization or the unit to be present as a witness. If the invited person is unwilling to be present as a witness, the server shall record on the service receipt the reason for refusal, the time and place, and the fact that the invited person is unwilling to be present as a witness, and shall leave the litigation document at the residence or place of business of the person to be served, which shall be deemed service.  
If the adult family member of the person to be served living with that person or the person responsible for receiving documents of a legal person or other organization is the other party in the same case, the preceding paragraph shall not apply.  
III. Preparation Before Hearing

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　适用简易程序审理的民事案件，当事人及其诉讼代理人申请人民法院调查收集证据和申请证人出庭作证，应当在举证期限届满前提出，但其提出申请的期限不受[《最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定》第十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定.md#art-19)第一款、第五十四条第一款的限制。

**Article 12** In a civil case heard under summary procedures, a party or its litigation agent applying to the People's Court for investigation and collection of evidence or for a witness to appear in court to testify shall make the application before the expiry of the time limit for producing evidence; however, the time limit for filing such an application shall not be subject to the limitations of Article 19, Paragraph 1 and Article 54, Paragraph 1 of the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Evidence in Civil Action.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人一方或者双方就适用简易程序提出异议后，人民法院应当进行审查，并按下列情形分别处理：  
（一）异议成立的，应当将案件转入普通程序审理，并将合议庭的组成人员及相关事项以书面形式通知双方当事人；  
（二）异议不成立的，口头告知双方当事人，并将上述内容记入笔录。  
转入普通程序审理的民事案件的审理期限自人民法院立案的次日起开始计算。

**Article 13** After one party or both parties raise an objection to the application of summary procedures, the People's Court shall examine the objection and handle it separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the objection is well-founded, the case shall be transferred to ordinary procedures for hearing, and both parties shall be notified in writing of the composition of the collegial panel and related matters;  
(2) if the objection is not well-founded, both parties shall be notified orally, and the above content shall be recorded in the record.  
The trial period for a civil case transferred to ordinary procedures for hearing shall be calculated from the day after the People's Court opens the case.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　下列民事案件，人民法院在开庭审理时应当先行调解：  
（一）婚姻家庭纠纷和继承纠纷；  
（二）劳务合同纠纷；  
（三）交通事故和工伤事故引起的权利义务关系较为明确的损害赔偿纠纷；  
（四）宅基地和相邻关系纠纷；  
（五）合伙协议纠纷；  
（六）诉讼标的额较小的纠纷。  
但是根据案件的性质和当事人的实际情况不能调解或者显然没有调解必要的除外。

**Article 14** In the following civil cases, the People's Court shall conduct mediation first when opening the hearing:  
(1) disputes over marriage and family relations and succession;  
(2) disputes over service contracts;  
(3) compensation disputes arising from traffic accidents and work-related injuries where the rights and obligations are relatively clear;  
(4) disputes over homesteads and neighboring relations;  
(5) disputes over partnership agreements;  
(6) disputes involving a small amount in litigation.  
However, this does not apply if mediation is impossible or obviously unnecessary due to the nature of the case and the actual circumstances of the parties.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　调解达成协议并经审判人员审核后，双方当事人同意该调解协议经双方签名或者捺印生效的，该调解协议自双方签名或者捺印之日起发生法律效力。当事人要求摘录或者复制该调解协议的，应予准许。  
调解协议符合前款规定的，人民法院应当另行制作民事调解书。调解协议生效后一方拒不履行的，另一方可以持民事调解书申请强制执行。

**Article 15** After a mediation agreement is reached and examined by the adjudicators, if both parties agree that the mediation agreement shall become effective upon the signing or thumbprint-affixing of both parties, the mediation agreement shall become legally effective from the date of such signing or thumbprint-affixing. If a party requests to extract or copy the mediation agreement, the request shall be allowed.  
If the mediation agreement complies with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall separately prepare a civil mediation paper. If one party refuses to perform after the mediation agreement takes effect, the other party may apply for compulsory execution by presenting the civil mediation paper.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院可以当庭告知当事人到人民法院领取民事调解书的具体日期，也可以在当事人达成调解协议的次日起十日内将民事调解书发送给当事人。

**Article 16** The People's Court may notify the parties in court of the specific date for collecting the civil mediation paper from the court, or may send the civil mediation paper to the parties within ten days from the day after the date on which the parties reach the mediation agreement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人以民事调解书与调解协议的原意不一致为由提出异议，人民法院审查后认为异议成立的，应当根据调解协议裁定补正民事调解书的相关内容。  
四、开庭审理

**Article 17** If a party raises an objection on the ground that the civil mediation paper is inconsistent with the original intention of the mediation agreement, and after examination the People's Court finds the objection to be well-founded, it shall, in accordance with the mediation agreement, make a ruling to rectify the relevant content of the civil mediation paper.  
IV. Hearing

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　以捎口信、电话、传真、电子邮件等形式发送的开庭通知，未经当事人确认或者没有其他证据足以证明当事人已经收到的，人民法院不得将其作为按撤诉处理和缺席判决的根据。

**Article 18** If a hearing notice sent by means such as leaving messages, telephone, fax, electronic mail, etc., is not confirmed by the party or there is no other evidence sufficient to prove that the party has received it, the People's Court shall not take it as the basis for treating the case as a withdrawal of prosecution or for making a default judgment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　开庭前已经书面或者口头告知当事人诉讼权利义务，或者当事人各方均委托律师代理诉讼的，审判人员除告知当事人申请回避的权利外，可以不再告知当事人其他的诉讼权利义务。

**Article 19** If the litigation rights and obligations have been informed to the parties in writing or orally before the hearing, or if all parties have entrusted lawyers to act as litigation agents, the adjudicators may, apart from informing the parties of their right to apply for withdrawal, no longer inform the parties of other litigation rights and obligations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对没有委托律师代理诉讼的当事人，审判人员应当对回避、自认、举证责任等相关内容向其作必要的解释或者说明，并在庭审过程中适当提示当事人正确行使诉讼权利、履行诉讼义务，指导当事人进行正常的诉讼活动。

**Article 20** For a party who has not entrusted a lawyer to act as a litigation agent, the adjudicators shall provide necessary explanations or clarifications on matters such as withdrawal, admission, and burden of adducing evidence, and shall appropriately prompt the party during the court proceedings to correctly exercise its litigation rights and perform its litigation obligations, guiding the party to conduct normal litigation activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　开庭时，审判人员可以根据当事人的诉讼请求和答辩意见归纳出争议焦点，经当事人确认后，由当事人围绕争议焦点举证、质证和辩论。  
当事人对案件事实无争议的，审判人员可以在听取当事人就适用法律方面的辩论意见后径行判决、裁定。

**Article 21** At the hearing, the adjudicators may summarize the points of contention based on the parties' claims and defense opinions, and after the parties confirm them, the parties shall adduce evidence, cross-examine evidence, and debate around the points of contention.  
If the parties have no dispute over the facts of the case, the adjudicators may directly render a judgment or ruling after hearing the parties' arguments on the application of law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人双方同时到基层人民法院请求解决简单的民事纠纷，但未协商举证期限，或者被告一方经简便方式传唤到庭的，当事人在开庭审理时要求当庭举证的，应予准许；当事人当庭举证有困难的，举证的期限由当事人协商决定，但最长不得超过十五日；协商不成的，由人民法院决定。

**Article 22** If both parties simultaneously appear before the Primary People's Court to request resolution of a simple civil dispute but have not agreed on a time limit for evidence production, or if the defendant is summoned to appear in court by simple means, and a party requests to produce evidence at the hearing, the request shall be allowed; if a party has difficulty producing evidence at the hearing, the time limit for evidence production shall be decided by agreement between the parties, but shall not exceed fifteen days at most; if no agreement is reached, it shall be decided by the People's Court.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　适用简易程序审理的民事案件，应当一次开庭审结，但人民法院认为确有必要再次开庭的除外。

**Article 23** A civil case heard under summary procedures shall be concluded in one hearing, except where the People's Court finds it truly necessary to hold another hearing.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　书记员应当将适用简易程序审理民事案件的全部活动记入笔录。对于下列事项，应当详细记载：  
（一）审判人员关于当事人诉讼权利义务的告知、争议焦点的概括、证据的认定和裁判的宣告等重大事项；  
（二）当事人申请回避、自认、撤诉、和解等重大事项；  
（三）当事人当庭陈述的与其诉讼权利直接相关的其他事项。

**Article 24** The court clerk shall record all activities of the civil case heard under summary procedures in the record. The following matters shall be recorded in detail:  
(1) significant matters such as the adjudicators' informing the parties of their litigation rights and obligations, summarizing the points of contention, appraising evidence, and announcing the judgment;  
(2) significant matters such as the parties' applications for withdrawal, admissions, withdrawal of prosecution, and settlements;  
(3) other matters stated by the parties in court that are directly related to their litigation rights.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　庭审结束时，审判人员可以根据案件的审理情况对争议焦点和当事人各方举证、质证和辩论的情况进行简要总结，并就是否同意调解征询当事人的意见。

**Article 25** At the conclusion of the hearing, the adjudicators may, based on the trial situation, briefly summarize the points of contention and the parties' evidence production, cross-examination, and debate, and shall solicit the parties' opinions on whether they agree to mediation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　审判人员在审理过程中发现案情复杂需要转为普通程序的，应当在审限届满前及时作出决定，并书面通知当事人。  
五、宣判与送达

**Article 26** If, during the hearing, the adjudicators discover that the case is complex and needs to be transferred to ordinary procedures, they shall make a decision promptly before the expiry of the trial period and notify the parties in writing.  
V. Pronouncement of Judgment and Service

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　适用简易程序审理的民事案件，除人民法院认为不宜当庭宣判的以外，应当当庭宣判。

**Article 27** In a civil case heard under summary procedures, unless the People's Court considers it inappropriate to pronounce the judgment in court, the judgment shall be pronounced in court.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当庭宣判的案件，除当事人当庭要求邮寄送达的以外，人民法院应当告知当事人或者诉讼代理人领取裁判文书的期间和地点以及逾期不领取的法律后果。上述情况，应当记入笔录。  
人民法院已经告知当事人领取裁判文书的期间和地点的，当事人在指定期间内领取裁判文书之日即为送达之日；当事人在指定期间内未领取的，指定领取裁判文书期间届满之日即为送达之日，当事人的上诉期从人民法院指定领取裁判文书期间届满之日的次日起开始计算。

**Article 28** In a case where the judgment is pronounced in court, except where a party requests service by mail at the time of pronouncement, the People's Court shall inform the party or its litigation agent of the time limit and place for collecting the judgment document, as well as the legal consequences of failing to collect it within the time limit. The above information shall be recorded in the record.  
If the People's Court has informed the party of the time limit and place for collecting the judgment document, the day on which the party collects it within the designated period shall be the date of service; if the party fails to collect it within the designated period, the day on which the designated period expires shall be the date of service, and the appeal period for the party shall run from the day after the day on which the People's Court-designated period for collecting the judgment document expires.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　当事人因交通不便或者其他原因要求邮寄送达裁判文书的，人民法院可以按照当事人自己提供的送达地址邮寄送达。  
人民法院根据当事人自己提供的送达地址邮寄送达的，邮件回执上注明收到或者退回之日即为送达之日，当事人的上诉期从邮件回执上注明收到或者退回之日的次日起开始计算。

**Article 29** If a party requests that the judgment document be served by mail due to transportation inconvenience or other reasons, the People's Court may serve it by mail at the service address provided by the party itself.  
If the People's Court serves by mail at the service address provided by the party itself, the date of receipt or return indicated on the mail receipt shall be the date of service, and the appeal period for the party shall run from the day after the date of receipt or return indicated on the mail receipt.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　原告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，可以按撤诉处理；被告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，人民法院可以根据原告的诉讼请求及双方已经提交给法庭的证据材料缺席判决。  
按撤诉处理或者缺席判决的，人民法院可以按照当事人自己提供的送达地址将裁判文书送达给未到庭的当事人。

**Article 30** If the plaintiff, having been summoned by a summons, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons or leaves the court without permission during the trial, the case may be treated as a withdrawal of prosecution; if the defendant, having been summoned by a summons, fails to appear in court without justifiable reasons or leaves the court without permission during the trial, the People's Court may make a default judgment based on the plaintiff's claims and the evidence materials already submitted by both parties to the court.  
Where the case is treated as a withdrawal of prosecution or a default judgment is made, the People's Court may serve the judgment document on the party who failed to appear in court at the service address provided by that party.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　定期宣判的案件，定期宣判之日即为送达之日，当事人的上诉期自定期宣判的次日起开始计算。当事人在定期宣判的日期无正当理由未到庭的，不影响该裁判上诉期间的计算。  
当事人确有正当理由不能到庭，并在定期宣判前已经告知人民法院的，人民法院可以按照当事人自己提供的送达地址将裁判文书送达给未到庭的当事人。

**Article 31** In a case where judgment is pronounced on a scheduled date, the scheduled date of pronouncement shall be the date of service, and the appeal period for the party shall run from the day after the scheduled pronouncement. A party's failure to appear in court on the scheduled date of pronouncement without justifiable reasons shall not affect the calculation of the appeal period for that judgment.  
If a party has justifiable reasons for being unable to appear in court and has informed the People's Court before the scheduled pronouncement, the People's Court may serve the judgment document on the party who failed to appear in court at the service address provided by that party.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　适用简易程序审理的民事案件，有下列情形之一的，人民法院在制作裁判文书时对认定事实或者判决理由部分可以适当简化：  
（一）当事人达成调解协议并需要制作民事调解书的；  
（二）一方当事人在诉讼过程中明确表示承认对方全部诉讼请求或者部分诉讼请求的；  
（三）当事人对案件事实没有争议或者争议不大的；  
（四）涉及个人隐私或者商业秘密的案件，当事人一方要求简化裁判文书中的相关内容，人民法院认为理由正当的；  
（五）当事人双方一致同意简化裁判文书的。  
六、其　　他

**Article 32** In a civil case heard under summary procedures, if any of the following circumstances exist, the People's Court may appropriately simplify the part concerning the findings of fact or the reasons for the judgment when preparing the judgment document:  
(1) the parties have reached a mediation agreement and a civil mediation paper needs to be prepared;  
(2) a party has expressly admitted all or part of the other party's claims during the litigation;  
(3) the parties have no dispute or have little dispute over the facts of the case;  
(4) the case involves personal privacy or trade secrets, and one party requests simplification of the relevant content of the judgment document, and the People's Court finds the reason to be legitimate;  
(5) both parties unanimously agree to simplify the judgment document.  
VI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本院已经公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 33** If any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本规定自2003年12月1日起施行。2003年12月1日以后受理的民事案件，适用本规定。

**Article 34** These Provisions shall come into force on December 1, 2003. Civil cases accepted after December 1, 2003 shall be governed by these Provisions.
