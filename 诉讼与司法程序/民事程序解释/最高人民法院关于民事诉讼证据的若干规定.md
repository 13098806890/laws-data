# 最高人民法院关于民事诉讼证据的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕33号  
**公布日期**：2001-12-21  
**生效日期**：2002-04-01

> （2001年12月6日最高人民法院审判委员会第1201次会议通过　2001年12月21日最高人民法院公告公布　自2002年4月1日起施行） 为保证人民法院正确认定案件事实，公正、及时审理民事案件，保障和便利当事人依法行使诉讼权利，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称《民事诉讼法》）等有关法律的规定，结合民事审判经验和实际情况，制定本规定。 一、当事人举证 第一条　原告向人民法院起诉或者被告提出反诉，应当附有符合起诉条件的相应的证据材料。 第二条　当事人对自己提出的诉讼请求所依据的事实或者反驳对方诉讼请求所依据的事实有责任提供证据加以证明。 没有证据或者证据不足以证明当事人的事实主张的，由负有举证责任的当事人承担不利后果。 第三条　人民法院应当向当事人说明举证的要求及法律后果，促使当事人在合理期限内积极、全面、正确、诚实地完成举证。 当事人因客观原因不能自行收集的证据，可申请人民法院调查收集。 第四条　下列侵权诉讼，按照以下规定承担举证责任： （一）因新产品制造方法发明专利引起的专利侵权诉讼，由制造同样产品的单位或者个人对其产品制造方法不同于专利方法承担举证责任； （二）高度危险作业致人损害的侵权诉讼，由加害人就受害人故意造成损害的事实承担举证责任； （三）因环境污染引起的损害赔偿诉讼，由加害人就法律规定的免责事由及其行为与损害结果之间不存在因果关系承担举证责任； （四）建筑物或者其他设施以及建筑物上的搁置物、悬挂物发生倒塌、脱落、坠落致人损害的侵权诉讼，由所有人或者管理人对其无过错承担举证责任； （五）饲养动物致人损害的侵权诉讼，由动物饲养人或者管理人就受害人有过错或者第三人有过错承担举证责任； （六）因缺陷产品致人损害的侵权诉讼，由产品的生产者就法律规定的免责事由承担举证责任；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　原告向人民法院起诉或者被告提出反诉，应当附有符合起诉条件的相应的证据材料。

**Article 1** Where a plaintiff prosecutes a People's Court or a defendant files a counterclaim, the relevant evidentiary materials that meet the conditions for prosecution shall be attached.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人对自己提出的诉讼请求所依据的事实或者反驳对方诉讼请求所依据的事实有责任提供证据加以证明。  
没有证据或者证据不足以证明当事人的事实主张的，由负有举证责任的当事人承担不利后果。

**Article 2** A party shall have the responsibility to provide evidence to prove the facts on which its own claim is based or the facts on which it rebuts the opposing party's claim.  
Where there is no evidence or the evidence is insufficient to prove the party's factual allegations, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall bear adverse consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应当向当事人说明举证的要求及法律后果，促使当事人在合理期限内积极、全面、正确、诚实地完成举证。  
当事人因客观原因不能自行收集的证据，可申请人民法院调查收集。

**Article 3** A People's Court shall explain to the parties the requirements for adducing evidence and the legal consequences, so as to urge the parties to actively, comprehensively, correctly and honestly complete the adduction of evidence within a reasonable time limit.  
Where a party cannot collect evidence on its own due to objective reasons, it may apply to the People's Court to investigate and collect such evidence.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下列侵权诉讼，按照以下规定承担举证责任：  
（一）因新产品制造方法发明专利引起的专利侵权诉讼，由制造同样产品的单位或者个人对其产品制造方法不同于专利方法承担举证责任；  
（二）高度危险作业致人损害的侵权诉讼，由加害人就受害人故意造成损害的事实承担举证责任；  
（三）因环境污染引起的损害赔偿诉讼，由加害人就法律规定的免责事由及其行为与损害结果之间不存在因果关系承担举证责任；  
（四）建筑物或者其他设施以及建筑物上的搁置物、悬挂物发生倒塌、脱落、坠落致人损害的侵权诉讼，由所有人或者管理人对其无过错承担举证责任；  
（五）饲养动物致人损害的侵权诉讼，由动物饲养人或者管理人就受害人有过错或者第三人有过错承担举证责任；  
（六）因缺陷产品致人损害的侵权诉讼，由产品的生产者就法律规定的免责事由承担举证责任；  
（七）因共同危险行为致人损害的侵权诉讼，由实施危险行为的人就其行为与损害结果之间不存在因果关系承担举证责任；  
（八）因医疗行为引起的侵权诉讼，由医疗机构就医疗行为与损害结果之间不存在因果关系及不存在医疗过错承担举证责任。  
有关法律对侵权诉讼的举证责任有特殊规定的，从其规定。

**Article 4** The following tort litigation shall bear the burden of adducing evidence in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For a patent infringement action arising from a method patent for manufacturing a new product, the entity or individual that manufactures the same product shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that its product manufacturing method is different from the patented method;  
(2) For an infringement action for damage caused by ultra-hazardous operations, the infringer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the victim intentionally caused the damage;  
(3) For an action for damages arising from environmental pollution, the infringer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to the exemption provided by law and that there is no causal relationship between its act and the damage;  
(4) For an infringement action for damage caused by collapse, detachment, or fall of a building or other facility, or of an object placed or suspended on a building, the owner or manager shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that it is not at fault;  
(5) For an infringement action for damage caused by a domesticated animal, the keeper or manager of the animal shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the victim or a third party was at fault;  
(6) For an infringement action for damage caused by a defective product, the manufacturer of the product shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to the exemption provided by law;  
(7) For an infringement action for damage caused by joint dangerous acts, the person who performed the dangerous act shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that there is no causal relationship between its act and the damage;  
(8) For an infringement action arising from medical treatment, the medical institution shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that there is no causal relationship between the medical treatment and the damage and that there is no medical fault.  
Where the relevant law has special provisions on the burden of adducing evidence in tort litigation, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在合同纠纷案件中，主张合同关系成立并生效的一方当事人对合同订立和生效的事实承担举证责任；主张合同关系变更、解除、终止、撤销的一方当事人对引起合同关系变动的事实承担举证责任。  
对合同是否履行发生争议的，由负有履行义务的当事人承担举证责任。  
对代理权发生争议的，由主张有代理权一方当事人承担举证责任。

**Article 5** In a contract dispute case, the party asserting that the contractual relationship is established and has taken effect shall bear the burden of adducing evidence on the facts of contract formation and effectiveness; the party asserting modification, rescission, termination, or revocation of the contractual relationship shall bear the burden of adducing evidence on the facts that cause the change in the contractual relationship.  
Where a dispute arises over whether the contract has been performed, the party that has the obligation to perform shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
Where a dispute arises over the authority of agency, the party asserting the existence of agency authority shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在劳动争议纠纷案件中，因用人单位作出开除、除名、辞退、解除劳动合同、减少劳动报酬、计算劳动者工作年限等决定而发生劳动争议的，由用人单位负举证责任。

**Article 6** In a labor dispute case, where a labor dispute arises from a decision made by the employer such as dismissal, removal, discharge, termination of labor contract, reduction of labor remuneration, or calculation of the worker's length of service, the employer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在法律没有具体规定，依本规定及其他司法解释无法确定举证责任承担时，人民法院可以根据公平原则和诚实信用原则，综合当事人举证能力等因素确定举证责任的承担。

**Article 7** Where there are no specific provisions in the law, and the burden of adducing evidence cannot be determined pursuant to these Provisions or other judicial interpretations, the People's Court may, in accordance with the principles of fairness and good faith, take into account factors such as the parties' ability to adduce evidence, determine the allocation of the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　诉讼过程中，一方当事人对另一方当事人陈述的案件事实明确表示承认的，另一方当事人无需举证。但涉及身份关系的案件除外。  
对一方当事人陈述的事实，另一方当事人既未表示承认也未否认，经审判人员充分说明并询问后，其仍不明确表示肯定或者否定的，视为对该项事实的承认。  
当事人委托代理人参加诉讼的，代理人的承认视为当事人的承认。但未经特别授权的代理人对事实的承认直接导致承认对方诉讼请求的除外；当事人在场但对其代理人的承认不作否认表示的，视为当事人的承认。  
当事人在法庭辩论终结前撤回承认并经对方当事人同意，或者有充分证据证明其承认行为是在受胁迫或者重大误解情况下作出且与事实不符的，不能免除对方当事人的举证责任。

**Article 8** In the course of litigation, where a party expressly admits the facts of the case stated by the opposing party, the opposing party shall not be required to adduce evidence. However, this shall not apply to cases involving personal relations.  
Where the opposing party neither admits nor denies the facts stated by a party, and after the adjudicators fully explain and inquire, it still does not clearly indicate affirmation or denial, such silence shall be deemed as an admission of the relevant facts.  
Where a party appoints an authorized agent to participate in litigation, the admission by the agent shall be deemed as the admission by the party. However, this shall not apply where an agent without special authorization directly admits the facts, resulting in the admission of the opposing party's claim; where the party is present but does not deny the admission made by its agent, such admission shall be deemed as the party's own admission.  
Where a party withdraws an admission before the conclusion of court debate and with the consent of the opposing party, or where there is sufficient evidence to prove that the admission was made under duress or a material misunderstanding and does not conform to the facts, the opposing party's burden of adducing evidence shall not be exempted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列事实，当事人无需举证证明：  
（一）众所周知的事实；  
（二）自然规律及定理；  
（三）根据法律规定或者已知事实和日常生活经验法则，能推定出的另一事实；  
（四）已为人民法院发生法律效力的裁判所确认的事实；  
（五）已为仲裁机构的生效裁决所确认的事实；  
（六）已为有效公证文书所证明的事实。  
前款（一）、（三）、（四）、（五）、（六）项，当事人有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 9** A party shall not be required to adduce evidence to prove the following facts:  
(1) facts that are well-known;  
(2) natural laws and theorems;  
(3) other facts that can be inferred from legal provisions, known facts, or rules of daily life experience;  
(4) facts that have been confirmed by a legally effective judgment of a People's Court;  
(5) facts that have been confirmed by an effective award of an arbitration institution;  
(6) facts that have been proved by a valid notarized document.  
With respect to the facts listed in items (1), (3), (4), (5), and (6) of the preceding paragraph, unless the party has contrary evidence sufficient to rebut them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人向人民法院提供证据，应当提供原件或者原物。如需自己保存证据原件、原物或者提供原件、原物确有困难的，可以提供经人民法院核对无异的复制件或者复制品。

**Article 10** Where a party provides evidence to a People's Court, it shall provide the original or the object itself. If it is necessary for the party to keep the original or the object itself, or if it is truly difficult to provide the original or the object itself, a copy or replica verified as identical by the People's Court may be provided.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人向人民法院提供的证据系在中华人民共和国领域外形成的，该证据应当经所在国公证机关予以证明，并经中华人民共和国驻该国使领馆予以认证，或者履行中华人民共和国与该所在国订立的有关条约中规定的证明手续。  
当事人向人民法院提供的证据是在香港、澳门、台湾地区形成的，应当履行相关的证明手续。

**Article 11** Where the evidence provided by a party to a People's Court is formed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, such evidence shall be notarized by a notarial authority in the country where it is formed, and shall be authenticated by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country, or shall undergo the certification procedures provided for in the relevant treaty concluded between the People's Republic of China and that country.  
Where the evidence provided by a party to a People's Court is formed in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region, the relevant certification procedures shall be performed.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人向人民法院提供外文书证或者外文说明资料，应当附有中文译本。

**Article 12** Where a party provides to a People's Court documentary evidence or explanatory materials in a foreign language, a Chinese translation shall be attached.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对双方当事人无争议但涉及国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实，人民法院可以责令当事人提供有关证据。

**Article 13** With respect to facts that are not disputed between the parties but that involve the national interest, social public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the People's Court may order the parties to provide relevant evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人应当对其提交的证据材料逐一分类编号，对证据材料的来源、证明对象和内容作简要说明，签名盖章，注明提交日期，并依照对方当事人人数提出副本。  
人民法院收到当事人提交的证据材料，应当出具收据，注明证据的名称、份数和页数以及收到的时间，由经办人员签名或者盖章。  
二、人民法院调查收集证据

**Article 14** A party shall number and classify the evidentiary materials it submits one by one, briefly explain the source, object of proof, and content of the evidentiary materials, affix its signature or seal, indicate the submission date, and provide copies in accordance with the number of opposing parties.  
Upon receiving the evidentiary materials submitted by a party, the People's Court shall issue a receipt indicating the name, number of copies, number of pages, and the time of receipt, and the handling personnel shall affix their signatures or seals.  
II. Investigation and Collection of Evidence by the People's Court

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[《民事诉讼法》第六十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-64)规定的“人民法院认为审理案件需要的证据”，是指以下情形：  
（一）涉及可能有损国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实；  
（二）涉及依职权追加当事人、中止诉讼、终结诉讼、回避等与实体争议无关的程序事项。

**Article 15** "Evidence that the People's Court considers necessary for the trial of a case" as prescribed in Article 64 of the Civil Procedure Law refers to the following circumstances:  
(1) facts that may involve impairment of the national interest, social public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(2) procedural matters that are not related to the substantive dispute, such as the addition of parties ex officio, suspension of litigation, termination of litigation, and withdrawal.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　除[本规定第十五条](#art-15)规定的情形外，人民法院调查收集证据，应当依当事人的申请进行。

**Article 16** Except for the circumstances provided for in Article 15 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall investigate and collect evidence upon the application of a party.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　符合下列条件之一的，当事人及其诉讼代理人可以申请人民法院调查收集证据：  
（一）申请调查收集的证据属于国家有关部门保存并须人民法院依职权调取的档案材料；  
（二）涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的材料；  
（三）当事人及其诉讼代理人确因客观原因不能自行收集的其他材料。

**Article 17** If any of the following conditions are met, a party and its litigation agent may apply to the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence:  
(1) the evidence applied for investigation and collection is archival material held by a state authority and must be retrieved by the People's Court ex officio;  
(2) the material involves state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy;  
(3) other material that the party and its litigation agent are truly unable to collect on their own due to objective reasons.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人及其诉讼代理人申请人民法院调查收集证据，应当提交书面申请。申请书应当载明被调查人的姓名或者单位名称、住所地等基本情况、所要调查收集的证据的内容、需要由人民法院调查收集证据的原因及其要证明的事实。

**Article 18** Where a party and its litigation agent apply to the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence, a written application shall be submitted. The application shall state the basic information such as the name or entity name and domicile of the person to be investigated, the content of the evidence to be investigated and collected, the reasons why the People's Court is required to investigate and collect such evidence, and the facts to be proved.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人及其诉讼代理人申请人民法院调查收集证据，不得迟于举证期限届满前7日。  
人民法院对当事人及其诉讼代理人的申请不予准许的，应当向当事人或其诉讼代理人送达通知书。当事人及其诉讼代理人可以在收到通知书的次日起3日内向受理申请的人民法院书面申请复议一次。人民法院应当在收到复议申请之日起5日内作出答复。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院关于适用简易程序审理民事案件的若干规定.md#art-12" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用简易程序审理民事案件的若干规定》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 19** A party and its litigation agent shall apply to the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence no later than seven days before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence.  
Where the People's Court does not grant the application of the party or its litigation agent, it shall serve a notice on the party or its litigation agent. The party or its litigation agent may, within three days from the day following receipt of the notice, submit a written application for reconsideration to the People's Court that accepted the application. The People's Court shall render a response within five days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　调查人员调查收集的书证，可以是原件，也可以是经核对无误的副本或者复制件。是副本或者复制件的，应当在调查笔录中说明来源和取证情况。

**Article 20** Documentary evidence investigated and collected by the investigators may be the original, or may be a copy or duplicate that has been checked and verified. If it is a copy or duplicate, the source and the circumstances of obtaining the evidence shall be explained in the investigation record.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　调查人员调查收集的物证应当是原物。被调查人提供原物确有困难的，可以提供复制品或者照片。提供复制品或者照片的，应当在调查笔录中说明取证情况。

**Article 21** Physical evidence collected by investigators shall be the original. If the person under investigation has genuine difficulty in providing the original, a duplicate or photograph may be provided. Where a duplicate or photograph is provided, the circumstances of the evidence collection shall be stated in the investigation record.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　调查人员调查收集计算机数据或者录音、录像等视听资料的，应当要求被调查人提供有关资料的原始载体。提供原始载体确有困难的，可以提供复制件。提供复制件的，调查人员应当在调查笔录中说明其来源和制作经过。

**Article 22** Where investigators investigate and collect computer data or audio-visual materials such as audio and video recordings, they shall require the person under investigation to provide the original medium of such materials. If the person has genuine difficulty in providing the original medium, a copy may be provided. Where a copy is provided, the investigators shall state in the investigation record the source and the process of making the copy.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人依据[《民事诉讼法》第七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-74)的规定向人民法院申请保全证据，不得迟于举证期限届满前7日。  
当事人申请保全证据的，人民法院可以要求其提供相应的担保。  
法律、司法解释规定诉前保全证据的，依照其规定办理。

**Article 23** A party applying to the People's Court for preservation of evidence pursuant to Article 74 of the Civil Procedure Law shall do so no later than seven (7) days before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence.  
Where a party applies for preservation of evidence, the People's Court may require the party to provide corresponding security.  
Where the law or judicial interpretations provide for preservation of evidence before prosecution, such provisions shall be followed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民法院进行证据保全，可以根据具体情况，采取查封、扣押、拍照、录音、录像、复制、鉴定、勘验、制作笔录等方法。  
人民法院进行证据保全，可以要求当事人或者诉讼代理人到场。

**Article 24** When preserving evidence, the People's Court may, depending on the specific circumstances, take such measures as sealing, impounding, photographing, audio recording, video recording, copying, expert evaluation, inspection, and making records.  
When preserving evidence, the People's Court may require the parties or litigation agents to be present.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人申请鉴定，应当在举证期限内提出。符合[本规定第二十七条](#art-27)规定的情形，当事人申请重新鉴定的除外。  
对需要鉴定的事项负有举证责任的当事人，在人民法院指定的期限内无正当理由不提出鉴定申请或者不预交鉴定费用或者拒不提供相关材料，致使对案件争议的事实无法通过鉴定结论予以认定的，应当对该事实承担举证不能的法律后果。

**Article 25** A party applying for expert evaluation shall do so within the time limit for adducing evidence, except where the party applies for a new expert evaluation under the circumstances specified in Article 27 of these Provisions.  
Where a party bearing the burden of adducing evidence for a matter requiring expert evaluation fails, without justifiable reasons, to apply for expert evaluation, or to prepay the evaluation fee, or to provide relevant materials within the time limit designated by the People's Court, thereby causing the disputed facts of the case to be unascertainable through the expert evaluation conclusion, that party shall bear the legal consequences of failure to adduce evidence.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人申请鉴定经人民法院同意后，由双方当事人协商确定有鉴定资格的鉴定机构、鉴定人员，协商不成的，由人民法院指定。

**Article 26** After a party's application for expert evaluation is approved by the People's Court, the parties shall negotiate to designate a qualified evaluation institution and evaluator. If negotiation fails, the People's Court shall designate them.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　当事人对人民法院委托的鉴定部门作出的鉴定结论有异议申请重新鉴定，提出证据证明存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应予准许：  
（一）鉴定机构或者鉴定人员不具备相关的鉴定资格的；  
（二）鉴定程序严重违法的；  
（三）鉴定结论明显依据不足的；  
（四）经过质证认定不能作为证据使用的其他情形。  
对有缺陷的鉴定结论，可以通过补充鉴定、重新质证或者补充质证等方法解决的，不予重新鉴定。

**Article 27** Where a party objects to an expert evaluation conclusion made by an evaluation department entrusted by the People's Court and applies for a new expert evaluation, and adduces evidence to prove the existence of any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant the application:  
(1) The evaluation institution or evaluator lacks the relevant evaluation qualifications;  
(2) The evaluation procedure is seriously illegal;  
(3) The evaluation conclusion is clearly insufficient in basis;  
(4) Other circumstances where the evaluation conclusion cannot be used as evidence after cross-examination.  
If a defective evaluation conclusion may be resolved through supplementary evaluation, re-cross-examination, or supplementary cross-examination, a new evaluation shall not be granted.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　一方当事人自行委托有关部门作出的鉴定结论，另一方当事人有证据足以反驳并申请重新鉴定的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 28** Where one party independently entrusts a relevant department to make an expert evaluation conclusion, and the other party has evidence sufficient to refute it and applies for a new expert evaluation, the People's Court shall grant the application.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审判人员对鉴定人出具的鉴定书，应当审查是否具有下列内容：  
（一）委托人姓名或者名称、委托鉴定的内容；  
（二）委托鉴定的材料；  
（三）鉴定的依据及使用的科学技术手段；  
（四）对鉴定过程的说明；  
（五）明确的鉴定结论；  
（六）对鉴定人鉴定资格的说明；  
（七）鉴定人员及鉴定机构签名盖章。

**Article 29** The adjudicators shall examine whether the expert evaluation report issued by the evaluator contains the following:  
(1) The name of the client and the content of the entrusted evaluation;  
(2) The materials for the entrusted evaluation;  
(3) The basis of the evaluation and the scientific or technical methods used;  
(4) A description of the evaluation process;  
(5) A clear evaluation conclusion;  
(6) A statement of the evaluator's qualifications;  
(7) The signatures and seals of the evaluator(s) and the evaluation institution.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院勘验物证或者现场，应当制作笔录，记录勘验的时间、地点、勘验人、在场人、勘验的经过、结果，由勘验人、在场人签名或者盖章。对于绘制的现场图应当注明绘制的时间、方位、测绘人姓名、身份等内容。

**Article 30** When the People's Court inspects physical evidence or a scene, it shall make a record stating the time, place, inspector, persons present, the process and results of the inspection, and such record shall be signed or sealed by the inspector and the persons present. For any sketch of the scene drawn, the time of drawing, orientation, and the name and identity of the person who made the drawing shall be noted.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　摘录有关单位制作的与案件事实相关的文件、材料，应当注明出处，并加盖制作单位或者保管单位的印章，摘录人和其他调查人员应当在摘录件上签名或者盖章。  
摘录文件、材料应当保持内容相应的完整性，不得断章取义。  
三、举证时限与证据交换

**Article 31** Excerpts from documents and materials made by relevant units and related to the facts of the case shall indicate the source and be stamped with the seal of the unit that made or preserved them. The person making the excerpts and other investigators shall sign or seal the excerpts.  
Excerpts of documents and materials shall maintain the corresponding integrity of the content and shall not be taken out of context.  
III. Time Limit for Adducing Evidence and Exchange of Evidence

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被告应当在答辩期届满前提出书面答辩，阐明其对原告诉讼请求及所依据的事实和理由的意见。

**Article 32** The defendant shall submit a written defense before the expiration of the period for filing a defense, setting out its opinions on the plaintiff's claims and the facts and reasons on which they are based.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院应当在送达案件受理通知书和应诉通知书的同时向当事人送达举证通知书。举证通知书应当载明举证责任的分配原则与要求、可以向人民法院申请调查取证的情形、人民法院根据案件情况指定的举证期限以及逾期提供证据的法律后果。  
举证期限可以由当事人协商一致，并经人民法院认可。  
由人民法院指定举证期限的，指定的期限不得少于30日，自当事人收到案件受理通知书和应诉通知书的次日起计算。

**Article 33** The People's Court shall, at the same time as serving the notice of case acceptance and the notice of responding to the action, serve the notice of adducing evidence on the parties. The notice of adducing evidence shall state the principles and requirements for the allocation of the burden of adducing evidence, the circumstances under which a party may apply to the People's Court to investigate and collect evidence, the time limit for adducing evidence designated by the People's Court based on the circumstances of the case, and the legal consequences of providing evidence beyond the time limit.  
The time limit for adducing evidence may be agreed upon by the parties through consultation and shall be approved by the People's Court.  
Where the time limit for adducing evidence is designated by the People's Court, the designated period shall be no less than thirty (30) days, calculated from the day following the date on which the parties receive the notice of case acceptance and the notice of responding to the action.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　当事人应当在举证期限内向人民法院提交证据材料，当事人在举证期限内不提交的，视为放弃举证权利。  
对于当事人逾期提交的证据材料，人民法院审理时不组织质证。但对方当事人同意质证的除外。  
当事人增加、变更诉讼请求或者提起反诉的，应当在举证期限届满前提出。

**Article 34** A party shall submit evidentiary materials to the People's Court within the time limit for adducing evidence. If a party fails to submit evidence within the time limit, it shall be deemed to have waived the right to adduce evidence.  
For evidentiary materials submitted by a party beyond the time limit, the People's Court shall not organize cross-examination during trial, unless the opposing party agrees to cross-examination.  
If a party adds or changes its claims or files a counterclaim, it shall do so before the expiration of the time limit for adducing evidence.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　诉讼过程中，当事人主张的法律关系的性质或者民事行为的效力与人民法院根据案件事实作出的认定不一致的，不受[本规定第三十四条](#art-34)规定的限制，人民法院应当告知当事人可以变更诉讼请求。  
当事人变更诉讼请求的，人民法院应当重新指定举证期限。

**Article 35** During the proceedings, if the nature of the legal relationship claimed by a party or the validity of a civil act is inconsistent with the determination made by the People's Court based on the facts of the case, the restrictions of Article 34 of these Provisions shall not apply, and the People's Court shall inform the party that it may change its claims.  
Where a party changes its claims, the People's Court shall re-designate a time limit for adducing evidence.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　当事人在举证期限内提交证据材料确有困难的，应当在举证期限内向人民法院申请延期举证，经人民法院准许，可以适当延长举证期限。当事人在延长的举证期限内提交证据材料仍有困难的，可以再次提出延期申请，是否准许由人民法院决定。

**Article 36** If a party has genuine difficulty in submitting evidentiary materials within the time limit for adducing evidence, it shall apply to the People's Court for an extension within the time limit. If approved by the People's Court, the time limit may be appropriately extended. If the party still has difficulty in submitting evidentiary materials within the extended time limit, it may file another application for extension, and the People's Court shall decide whether to grant it.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　经当事人申请，人民法院可以组织当事人在开庭审理前交换证据。  
人民法院对于证据较多或者复杂疑难的案件，应当组织当事人在答辩期届满后、开庭审理前交换证据。

**Article 37** Upon application by the parties, the People's Court may organize the parties to exchange evidence before the court hearing.  
For cases with a large amount of evidence or that are complex and difficult, the People's Court shall organize the parties to exchange evidence after the expiration of the defense period and before the court hearing.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　交换证据的时间可以由当事人协商一致并经人民法院认可，也可以由人民法院指定。  
人民法院组织当事人交换证据的，交换证据之日举证期限届满。当事人申请延期举证经人民法院准许的，证据交换日相应顺延。

**Article 38** The time for exchanging evidence may be agreed upon by the parties through consultation and approved by the People's Court, or designated by the People's Court.  
Where the People's Court organizes the parties to exchange evidence, the time limit for adducing evidence expires on the date of the evidence exchange. If a party's application for an extension of the time limit for adducing evidence is approved by the People's Court, the date of evidence exchange shall be postponed accordingly.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　证据交换应当在审判人员的主持下进行。  
在证据交换的过程中，审判人员对当事人无异议的事实、证据应当记录在卷；对有异议的证据，按照需要证明的事实分类记录在卷，并记载异议的理由。通过证据交换，确定双方当事人争议的主要问题。

**Article 39** The exchange of evidence shall be conducted under the auspices of adjudicators.  
During the evidence exchange, the adjudicators shall record in the file the facts and evidence on which the parties have no objection; for evidence on which objection exists, it shall be recorded in the file classified according to the facts to be proved, and the reasons for the objection shall be stated. Through the evidence exchange, the main issues in dispute between the parties shall be identified.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人收到对方交换的证据后提出反驳并提出新证据的，人民法院应当通知当事人在指定的时间进行交换。  
证据交换一般不超过两次。但重大、疑难和案情特别复杂的案件，人民法院认为确有必要再次进行证据交换的除外。

**Article 40** Where a party, after receiving the evidence exchanged by the opposing party, raises a rebuttal and submits new evidence, the People's Court shall notify the parties to exchange evidence at a designated time.  
The exchange of evidence shall generally not exceed two times, except for cases that are significant, difficult, or particularly complex in facts, where the People's Court considers it truly necessary to conduct another exchange of evidence.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　[《民事诉讼法》第一百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“新的证据”，是指以下情形：  
（一）一审程序中的新的证据包括：当事人在一审举证期限届满后新发现的证据；当事人确因客观原因无法在举证期限内提供，经人民法院准许，在延长的期限内仍无法提供的证据。  
（二）二审程序中的新的证据包括：一审庭审结束后新发现的证据；当事人在一审举证期限届满前申请人民法院调查取证未获准许，二审法院经审查认为应当准许并依当事人申请调取的证据。

**Article 41** The "new evidence" as provided in Article 125, Paragraph 1 of the Civil Procedure Law refers to the following circumstances:  
(1) New evidence in first instance proceedings includes: evidence newly discovered by a party after the expiration of the first instance burden of adducing evidence period; evidence that a party is truly unable to provide within the burden of adducing evidence period due to objective reasons, and after permission by the People's Court, still cannot provide within the extended period.  
(2) New evidence in second instance proceedings includes: evidence newly discovered after the conclusion of the first instance trial; evidence that a party applied to the People's Court for investigation and evidence collection before the expiration of the first instance burden of adducing evidence period but was not permitted, and the second instance court, upon review, considers that permission should be granted and collects the evidence upon the party's application.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　当事人在一审程序中提供新的证据的，应当在一审开庭前或者开庭审理时提出。  
当事人在二审程序中提供新的证据的，应当在二审开庭前或者开庭审理时提出；二审不需要开庭审理的，应当在人民法院指定的期限内提出。

**Article 42** Where a party provides new evidence in first instance proceedings, it shall be submitted before the first instance trial or during the trial.  
Where a party provides new evidence in second instance proceedings, it shall be submitted before the second instance trial or during the trial; if the second instance does not require a trial, it shall be submitted within the time limit designated by the People's Court.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　当事人举证期限届满后提供的证据不是新的证据的，人民法院不予采纳。  
当事人经人民法院准许延期举证，但因客观原因未能在准许的期限内提供，且不审理该证据可能导致裁判明显不公的，其提供的证据可视为新的证据。

**Article 43** Where the evidence provided by a party after the expiration of the burden of adducing evidence period is not new evidence, the People's Court shall not admit it.  
Where a party is permitted by the People's Court to extend the time for adducing evidence but, due to objective reasons, is unable to provide the evidence within the permitted period, and failure to hear such evidence may lead to a clearly unjust judgment, the evidence provided may be deemed as new evidence.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　[《民事诉讼法》第一百七十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-179)第一款第（一）项规定的“新的证据”，是指原审庭审结束后新发现的证据。  
当事人在再审程序中提供新的证据的，应当在申请再审时提出。

**Article 44** The "new evidence" as provided in Article 179, Paragraph 1, Item (1) of the Civil Procedure Law refers to evidence newly discovered after the conclusion of the original trial.  
Where a party provides new evidence in retrial proceedings, it shall be submitted at the time of applying for retrial.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　一方当事人提出新的证据的，人民法院应当通知对方当事人在合理期限内提出意见或者举证。

**Article 45** Where one party submits new evidence, the People's Court shall notify the other party to present opinions or adduce evidence within a reasonable time limit.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　由于当事人的原因未能在指定期限内举证，致使案件在二审或者再审期间因提出新的证据被人民法院发回重审或者改判的，原审裁判不属于错误裁判案件。一方当事人请求提出新的证据的另一方当事人负担由此增加的差旅、误工、证人出庭作证、诉讼等合理费用以及由此扩大的直接损失，人民法院应予支持。  
四、质　　证

**Article 46** Where a party fails to adduce evidence within the specified time limit due to reasons attributable to the party, causing the case to be remanded for retrial or the judgment to be changed by the People's Court during the second instance or retrial due to the submission of new evidence, the original trial judgment shall not be deemed as an erroneous judgment. Where one party requests that the other party that submitted new evidence bear the reasonable expenses thereby increased, such as travel, lost work time, witness appearance for testimony, litigation costs, and the direct losses thereby expanded, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
IV. Examination of Evidence

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　证据应当在法庭上出示，由当事人质证。未经质证的证据，不能作为认定案件事实的依据。  
当事人在证据交换过程中认可并记录在卷的证据，经审判人员在庭审中说明后，可以作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 47** Evidence shall be presented in court and examined by the parties. Evidence that has not been examined shall not be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case.  
Where a party acknowledges evidence during the evidence exchange process and such acknowledgment is recorded in the case file, such evidence may be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case after the judicial personnel explain it during the trial.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　涉及国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私或者法律规定的其他应当保密的证据，不得在开庭时公开质证。

**Article 48** Evidence involving State secrets, trade secrets, private personal matters, or other evidence that shall be kept confidential as provided by law shall not be publicly examined at trial.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对书证、物证、视听资料进行质证时，当事人有权要求出示证据的原件或者原物。但有下列情况之一的除外：  
（一）出示原件或者原物确有困难并经人民法院准许出示复制件或者复制品的；  
（二）原件或者原物已不存在，但有证据证明复制件、复制品与原件或原物一致的。

**Article 49** When examining documentary evidence, physical evidence, or audio-visual materials, a party has the right to request the presentation of the original document or object. Except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where there is genuine difficulty in presenting the original document or object and the People's Court permits the presentation of a replica or copy;  
(2) Where the original document or object no longer exists, but there is evidence proving that the replica or copy is consistent with the original document or object.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　质证时，当事人应当围绕证据的真实性、关联性、合法性，针对证据证明力有无以及证明力大小，进行质疑、说明与辩驳。

**Article 50** When examining evidence, the parties shall focus on the authenticity, relevance, and legality of the evidence, and shall challenge, explain, and refute the existence and extent of the probative force of the evidence.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　质证按下列顺序进行：  
（一）原告出示证据，被告、第三人与原告进行质证；  
（二）被告出示证据，原告、第三人与被告进行质证；  
（三）第三人出示证据，原告、被告与第三人进行质证。  
人民法院依照当事人申请调查收集的证据，作为提出申请的一方当事人提供的证据。  
人民法院依照职权调查收集的证据应当在庭审时出示，听取当事人意见，并可就调查收集该证据的情况予以说明。

**Article 51** Examination of evidence shall proceed in the following order:  
(1) The plaintiff presents evidence, and the defendant and third party examine the evidence with the plaintiff;  
(2) The defendant presents evidence, and the plaintiff and third party examine the evidence with the defendant;  
(3) The third party presents evidence, and the plaintiff and defendant examine the evidence with the third party.  
Evidence investigated and collected by the People's Court upon a party's application shall be treated as evidence provided by the party that applied.  
Evidence investigated and collected by the People's Court on its own authority shall be presented at trial, and the opinions of the parties shall be heard; the court may explain the circumstances of the investigation and collection of such evidence.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　案件有两个以上独立的诉讼请求的，当事人可以逐个出示证据进行质证。

**Article 52** Where a case has two or more independent claims, the parties may present evidence one by one for examination.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　不能正确表达意志的人，不能作为证人。  
待证事实与其年龄、智力状况或者精神健康状况相适应的无民事行为能力人和限制民事行为能力人，可以作为证人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/民法典/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》合同编通则若干问题的解释.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民法典》合同编通则若干问题的解释》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 53** A person who cannot correctly express his will shall not serve as a witness.  
A person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity whose age, mental condition, or mental health status is appropriate to the facts to be proved may serve as a witness.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　当事人申请证人出庭作证，应当在举证期限届满10日前提出，并经人民法院许可。  
人民法院对当事人的申请予以准许的，应当在开庭审理前通知证人出庭作证，并告知其应当如实作证及作伪证的法律后果。  
证人因出庭作证而支出的合理费用，由提供证人的一方当事人先行支付，由败诉一方当事人承担。

**Article 54** A party applying for a witness to appear in court to testify shall submit the application ten days before the expiration of the burden of adducing evidence period, and shall obtain permission from the People's Court.  
Where the People's Court grants the party's application, it shall notify the witness to appear in court to testify before the trial, and shall inform the witness of the obligation to testify truthfully and the legal consequences of giving false testimony.  
The reasonable expenses incurred by a witness for appearing in court to testify shall be initially paid by the party that provides the witness, and shall ultimately be borne by the losing party.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　证人应当出庭作证，接受当事人的质询。  
证人在人民法院组织双方当事人交换证据时出席陈述证言的，可视为出庭作证。

**Article 55** A witness shall appear in court to testify and accept questioning by the parties.  
Where a witness attends and gives testimony when the People's Court organizes the parties to exchange evidence, such attendance shall be deemed as appearing in court to testify.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　[《民事诉讼法》第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-70)规定的“证人确有困难不能出庭”，是指有下列情形：  
（一）年迈体弱或者行动不便无法出庭的；  
（二）特殊岗位确实无法离开的；  
（三）路途特别遥远，交通不便难以出庭的；  
（四）因自然灾害等不可抗力的原因无法出庭的；  
（五）其他无法出庭的特殊情况。  
前款情形，经人民法院许可，证人可以提交书面证言或者视听资料或者通过双向视听传输技术手段作证。

**Article 56** The phrase "a witness who truly has difficulty appearing in court" as provided in Article 70 of the Civil Procedure Law refers to the following circumstances:  
(1) Being advanced in age, weak in health, or having mobility difficulties, making it impossible to appear in court;  
(2) Working in a special post that genuinely cannot be left;  
(3) The distance being particularly far and transportation inconvenient, making it difficult to appear in court;  
(4) Being unable to appear in court due to force majeure such as natural disasters;  
(5) Other special circumstances making it impossible to appear in court.  
In the circumstances of the preceding paragraph, with the permission of the People's Court, the witness may submit written testimony or audio-visual materials, or may testify through two-way audio-visual transmission technology.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　出庭作证的证人应当客观陈述其亲身感知的事实。证人为聋哑人的，可以其他表达方式作证。  
证人作证时，不得使用猜测、推断或者评论性的语言。

**Article 57** A witness appearing in court shall objectively state the facts he personally perceived. If the witness is a deaf-mute, he may testify by other means of expression.  
When testifying, a witness shall not use language that is speculative, inferential, or opinionated.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　审判人员和当事人可以对证人进行询问。证人不得旁听法庭审理；询问证人时，其他证人不得在场。人民法院认为有必要的，可以让证人进行对质。

**Article 58** Judicial personnel and the parties may question witnesses. Witnesses shall not sit in on court hearings; when a witness is being questioned, other witnesses shall not be present. If the People's Court deems it necessary, it may have witnesses confront each other.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　鉴定人应当出庭接受当事人质询。  
鉴定人确因特殊原因无法出庭的，经人民法院准许，可以书面答复当事人的质询。

**Article 59** An expert evaluator shall appear in court to accept questioning by the parties.  
If an expert evaluator is truly unable to appear in court due to special reasons, with the permission of the People's Court, he may respond in writing to the parties' questions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　经法庭许可，当事人可以向证人、鉴定人、勘验人发问。  
询问证人、鉴定人、勘验人不得使用威胁、侮辱及不适当引导证人的言语和方式。

**Article 60** With the permission of the court, a party may pose questions to witnesses, expert evaluators, and inspectors.  
When questioning witnesses, expert evaluators, or inspectors, threatening, insulting, or improperly leading words or methods shall not be used.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　当事人可以向人民法院申请由1至2名具有专门知识的人员出庭就案件的专门性问题进行说明。人民法院准许其申请的，有关费用由提出申请的当事人负担。  
审判人员和当事人可以对出庭的具有专门知识的人员进行询问。  
经人民法院准许，可以由当事人各自申请的具有专门知识的人员就有关案件中的问题进行对质。  
具有专门知识的人员可以对鉴定人进行询问。

**Article 61** A party may apply to the People's Court for one or two persons with specialized knowledge to appear in court to explain the specialized issues of the case. Where the People's Court approves the application, the relevant expenses shall be borne by the party making the application.  
The adjudicators and the parties may interrogate the persons with specialized knowledge who appear in court.  
With the approval of the People's Court, the persons with specialized knowledge respectively engaged by the parties may confront each other on issues in the case.  
The persons with specialized knowledge may interrogate the expert evaluator.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　法庭应当将当事人的质证情况记入笔录，并由当事人核对后签名或者盖章。  
五、证据的审核认定

**Article 62** The court shall record the parties' examination of evidence in the transcript, and the parties shall sign or affix their seals after verification.  
V. Examination and Determination of Evidence

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民法院应当以证据能够证明的案件事实为依据依法作出裁判。

**Article 63** The People's Court shall render a judgment according to law based on the facts of the case that can be proven by the evidence.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　审判人员应当依照法定程序，全面、客观地审核证据，依据法律的规定，遵循法官职业道德，运用逻辑推理和日常生活经验，对证据有无证明力和证明力大小独立进行判断，并公开判断的理由和结果。

**Article 64** Adjudicators shall examine evidence comprehensively and objectively in accordance with statutory procedures, pursuant to the provisions of law, abide by judicial ethics, and independently determine whether the evidence is probative and the weight of such evidence by applying logical reasoning and everyday life experience, and shall disclose the reasons for and results of their determination.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　审判人员对单一证据可以从下列方面进行审核认定：  
（一）证据是否原件、原物，复印件、复制品与原件、原物是否相符；  
（二）证据与本案事实是否相关；  
（三）证据的形式、来源是否符合法律规定；  
（四）证据的内容是否真实；  
（五）证人或者提供证据的人，与当事人有无利害关系。

**Article 65** Adjudicators may examine and determine a single item of evidence from the following aspects:  
(1) Whether the evidence is the original document or original object, and whether copies or reproductions correspond to the original document or original object;  
(2) Whether the evidence is relevant to the facts of the case;  
(3) Whether the form and source of the evidence comply with the provisions of law;  
(4) Whether the content of the evidence is authentic;  
(5) Whether the witness or the person providing the evidence has an interest in any party.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　审判人员对案件的全部证据，应当从各证据与案件事实的关联程度、各证据之间的联系等方面进行综合审查判断。

**Article 66** Adjudicators shall comprehensively examine and determine all evidence in a case from aspects such as the degree of relevance of each item of evidence to the facts of the case and the connections among the items of evidence.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在诉讼中，当事人为达成调解协议或者和解的目的作出妥协所涉及的对案件事实的认可，不得在其后的诉讼中作为对其不利的证据。

**Article 67** In litigation, an admission of case facts made by a party as a compromise for the purpose of reaching a mediation agreement or settlement shall not be used as evidence adverse to that party in subsequent litigation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　以侵害他人合法权益或者违反法律禁止性规定的方法取得的证据，不能作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 68** Evidence obtained by infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of another person or by violating the prohibitive provisions of law shall not be used as the basis for determining the facts of a case.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　下列证据不能单独作为认定案件事实的依据：  
（一）未成年人所作的与其年龄和智力状况不相当的证言；  
（二）与一方当事人或者其代理人有利害关系的证人出具的证言；  
（三）存有疑点的视听资料；  
（四）无法与原件、原物核对的复印件、复制品；  
（五）无正当理由未出庭作证的证人证言。

**Article 69** The following evidence shall not be used alone as the basis for determining the facts of a case:  
(1) Testimony given by a minor that is not commensurate with his or her age and mental capacity;  
(2) Testimony given by a witness who has an interest in a party or his or her representative;  
(3) Audio-visual materials that contain doubts;  
(4) Copies or reproductions that cannot be verified against the original document or original object;  
(5) Witness testimony given by a witness who, without justifiable reasons, fails to appear in court to testify.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　一方当事人提出的下列证据，对方当事人提出异议但没有足以反驳的相反证据的，人民法院应当确认其证明力：  
（一）书证原件或者与书证原件核对无误的复印件、照片、副本、节录本；  
（二）物证原物或者与物证原物核对无误的复制件、照片、录像资料等；  
（三）有其他证据佐证并以合法手段取得的、无疑点的视听资料或者与视听资料核对无误的复制件；  
（四）一方当事人申请人民法院依照法定程序制作的对物证或者现场的勘验笔录。

**Article 70** Where a party presents the following evidence and the opposing party raises an objection but fails to present sufficient contrary evidence to rebut it, the People's Court shall affirm its probative value:  
(1) The original documentary evidence, or copies, photographs, duplicates, or excerpts that have been verified to be error-free against the original documentary evidence;  
(2) The original physical evidence, or reproductions, photographs, video recordings, etc., that have been verified to be error-free against the original physical evidence;  
(3) Audio-visual materials supported by other evidence and obtained by lawful means without doubts, or reproductions verified to be error-free against the audio-visual materials;  
(4) An inspection record of physical evidence or a scene produced by the People's Court upon application by a party in accordance with statutory procedures.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　人民法院委托鉴定部门作出的鉴定结论，当事人没有足以反驳的相反证据和理由的，可以认定其证明力。

**Article 71** Where a party has no sufficient contrary evidence and reasons to rebut an expert conclusion made by an expert evaluation department commissioned by the People's Court, the probative value of such expert conclusion may be affirmed.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　一方当事人提出的证据，另一方当事人认可或者提出的相反证据不足以反驳的，人民法院可以确认其证明力。  
一方当事人提出的证据，另一方当事人有异议并提出反驳证据，对方当事人对反驳证据认可的，可以确认反驳证据的证明力。

**Article 72** Where evidence presented by a party is admitted by the other party, or the contrary evidence presented by the other party is insufficient to rebut it, the People's Court may affirm its probative value.  
Where evidence is presented by one party, the other party objects and presents rebuttal evidence, and the first party admits the rebuttal evidence, the probative value of the rebuttal evidence may be affirmed.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　双方当事人对同一事实分别举出相反的证据，但都没有足够的依据否定对方证据的，人民法院应当结合案件情况，判断一方提供证据的证明力是否明显大于另一方提供证据的证明力，并对证明力较大的证据予以确认。  
因证据的证明力无法判断导致争议事实难以认定的，人民法院应当依据举证责任分配的规则作出裁判。

**Article 73** Where both parties present contrary evidence on the same fact but neither has sufficient grounds to negate the other's evidence, the People's Court shall, in light of the circumstances of the case, determine whether the probative value of the evidence provided by one party is significantly greater than that of the evidence provided by the other party, and shall affirm the evidence with the greater probative value.  
Where the disputed facts are difficult to determine because the probative value of the evidence cannot be assessed, the People's Court shall render a judgment in accordance with the rules on allocation of the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　诉讼过程中，当事人在起诉状、答辩状、陈述及其委托代理人的代理词中承认的对己方不利的事实和认可的证据，人民法院应当予以确认，但当事人反悔并有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 74** During litigation, the People's Court shall affirm the facts adverse to a party and the evidence admitted by that party in its statement of complaint, statement of defense, oral statements, or the representation statement of its authorized agent, unless the party withdraws its admission and presents sufficient contrary evidence to overturn it.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　有证据证明一方当事人持有证据无正当理由拒不提供，如果对方当事人主张该证据的内容不利于证据持有人，可以推定该主张成立。

**Article 75** Where there is evidence that a party holds evidence but refuses to provide it without justifiable reason, if the opposing party claims that the content of such evidence is adverse to the holder of the evidence, the claim may be presumed to be established.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　当事人对自己的主张，只有本人陈述而不能提出其他相关证据的，其主张不予支持。但对方当事人认可的除外。

**Article 76** Where a party only makes its own statement on its claim but fails to present any other relevant evidence, its claim shall not be upheld, unless the opposing party admits the claim.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　人民法院就数个证据对同一事实的证明力，可以依照下列原则认定：  
（一）国家机关、社会团体依职权制作的公文书证的证明力一般大于其他书证；  
（二）物证、档案、鉴定结论、勘验笔录或者经过公证、登记的书证，其证明力一般大于其他书证、视听资料和证人证言；  
（三）原始证据的证明力一般大于传来证据；  
（四）直接证据的证明力一般大于间接证据；  
（五）证人提供的对与其有亲属或者其他密切关系的当事人有利的证言，其证明力一般小于其他证人证言。

**Article 77** With respect to the probative value of several items of evidence regarding the same fact, the People's Court may determine it in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) The probative value of official documentary evidence made by state organs or social organizations in the exercise of their functions and powers shall generally be greater than that of other documentary evidence;  
(2) Physical evidence, archives, expert conclusions, inspection records, or documentary evidence that has been notarized or registered shall generally have greater probative value than other documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and witness testimony;  
(3) The probative value of original evidence shall generally be greater than that of derivative evidence;  
(4) The probative value of direct evidence shall generally be greater than that of circumstantial evidence;  
(5) The probative value of testimony given by a witness in favor of a party with whom the witness has a family relationship or other close relationship shall generally be less than that of testimony given by other witnesses.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　人民法院认定证人证言，可以通过对证人的智力状况、品德、知识、经验、法律意识和专业技能等的综合分析作出判断。

**Article 78** In determining witness testimony, the People's Court may make a judgment by conducting a comprehensive analysis of the witness's mental capacity, moral character, knowledge, experience, legal awareness, professional skills, and other factors.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　人民法院应当在裁判文书中阐明证据是否采纳的理由。  
对当事人无争议的证据，是否采纳的理由可以不在裁判文书中表述。  
六、其　　他

**Article 79** The People's Court shall state in its adjudicative documents the reasons for whether the evidence is admitted.  
For evidence on which the parties have no dispute, the reasons for admission or non-admission may be omitted from the adjudicative documents.  
VI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　对证人、鉴定人、勘验人的合法权益依法予以保护。  
当事人或者其他诉讼参与人伪造、毁灭证据，提供假证据，阻止证人作证，指使、贿买、胁迫他人作伪证，或者对证人、鉴定人、勘验人打击报复的，依照[《民事诉讼法》第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的规定处理。

**Article 80** The lawful rights and interests of witnesses, expert evaluators, and inspectors shall be protected according to law.  
Where a party or any other participant in the proceedings forges or destroys evidence, provides false evidence, prevents a witness from testifying, instigates, bribes, or coerces another person to give false testimony, or retaliates against a witness, expert evaluator, or inspector, the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　人民法院适用简易程序审理案件，不受[本解释中第三十二条](#art-32)、第三十三条第三款和第七十九条规定的限制。

**Article 81** A People's Court that tries a case under summary procedure shall not be bound by the provisions of Article 32, paragraph 3 of Article 33, and Article 79 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本院过去的司法解释，与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 82** If any previous judicial interpretation of this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本规定自2002年4月1日起施行。2002年4月1日尚未审结的一审、二审和再审民事案件不适用本规定。  
本规定施行前已经审理终结的民事案件，当事人以违反本规定为由申请再审的，人民法院不予支持。  
本规定施行后受理的再审民事案件，人民法院依据[《民事诉讼法》第一百八十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-184)的规定进行审理的，适用本规定。

**Article 83** These Provisions shall come into force as of April 1, 2002. These Provisions shall not apply to civil cases of first instance, second instance, or retrial that have not been concluded by April 1, 2002.  
Where a party applies for retrial of a civil case that has been concluded before these Provisions come into force on the ground that these Provisions are violated, the People's Court shall not sustain such application.  
Where a People's Court tries a retrial civil case accepted after these Provisions come into force pursuant to the provisions of Article 184 of the Civil Procedure Law, these Provisions shall apply.
