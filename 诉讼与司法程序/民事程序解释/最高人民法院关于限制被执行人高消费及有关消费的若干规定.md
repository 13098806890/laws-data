# 最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费及有关消费的若干规定

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　被执行人未按执行通知书指定的期间履行生效法律文书确定的给付义务的，人民法院可以采取限制消费措施，限制其高消费及非生活或者经营必需的有关消费。  
纳入失信被执行人名单的被执行人，人民法院应当对其采取限制消费措施。

**Article 1** Where the person subject to enforcement fails to perform the payment obligation confirmed by the effective legal instrument within the period specified in the enforcement notice, the People's Court may take measures to restrict consumption, restricting their high consumption and related consumption not necessary for life or business operations.  
For a person subject to enforcement who is included in the list of dishonest persons subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall take measures to restrict consumption against them.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。

**Article 2** When deciding to take measures to restrict consumption, the People's Court shall consider factors such as whether the person subject to enforcement has engaged in passive performance, evasion of enforcement, or resistance to enforcement, as well as the performance ability of the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被执行人为自然人的，被采取限制消费措施后，不得有以下高消费及非生活和工作必需的消费行为：  
（一）乘坐交通工具时，选择飞机、列车软卧、轮船二等以上舱位；  
（二）在星级以上宾馆、酒店、夜总会、高尔夫球场等场所进行高消费；  
（三）购买不动产或者新建、扩建、高档装修房屋；  
（四）租赁高档写字楼、宾馆、公寓等场所办公；  
（五）购买非经营必需车辆；  
（六）旅游、度假；  
（七）子女就读高收费私立学校；  
（八）支付高额保费购买保险理财产品；  
（九）乘坐G字头动车组列车全部座位、其他动车组列车一等以上座位等其他非生活和工作必需的消费行为。  
被执行人为单位的，被采取限制消费措施后，被执行人及其法定代表人、主要负责人、影响债务履行的直接责任人员、实际控制人不得实施前款规定的行为。因私消费以个人财产实施前款规定行为的，可以向执行法院提出申请。执行法院审查属实的，应予准许。

**Article 3** Where the person subject to enforcement is a natural person, after being subject to consumption restriction measures, they shall not engage in the following acts of high consumption and consumption not necessary for life or work:  
(1) When taking public transport, choosing aircraft, soft sleeper trains, second-class cabins and above on ships;  
(2) Engaging in high consumption at places such as star-rated hotels, restaurants, nightclubs, and golf courses;  
(3) Purchasing real estate or constructing, expanding, or high-end renovating houses;  
(4) Renting high-end office buildings, hotels, apartments, or other places for work;  
(5) Purchasing vehicles not necessary for business operations;  
(6) Traveling or vacationing;  
(7) Enrolling children in high-tuition private schools;  
(8) Purchasing insurance and financial products with high premiums;  
(9) Taking any seats on G-series high-speed trains, or first-class seats and above on other high-speed trains, and other consumption acts not necessary for life or work.  
  
Where the person subject to enforcement is an entity, after being subject to consumption restriction measures, the person subject to enforcement and its legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible person affecting debt performance, and actual controller shall not engage in the acts specified in the preceding paragraph. If a person engages in the acts specified in the preceding paragraph for personal consumption using personal property, they may submit an application to the enforcement court. Where the enforcement court verifies the application as true, it shall permit such acts.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　限制消费措施一般由申请执行人提出书面申请，经人民法院审查决定；必要时人民法院可以依职权决定。

**Article 4** Measures to restrict consumption shall generally be applied for in writing by the application executor, and decided upon by the People's Court after review; where necessary, the People's Court may decide ex officio.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，应当向被执行人发出限制消费令。限制消费令由人民法院院长签发。限制消费令应当载明限制消费的期间、项目、法律后果等内容。

**Article 5** Where the People's Court decides to take measures to restrict consumption, it shall issue a consumption restriction order to the person subject to enforcement. The consumption restriction order shall be signed by the president of the People's Court. The consumption restriction order shall specify the period, items, and legal consequences of the consumption restriction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，可以根据案件需要和被执行人的情况向有义务协助调查、执行的单位送达协助执行通知书，也可以在相关媒体上进行公告。

**Article 6** Where the People's Court decides to take measures to restrict consumption, it may, based on the needs of the case and the circumstances of the person subject to enforcement, serve a notice of assistance in enforcement on units that have the obligation to assist in investigation and enforcement, or may also publish announcements in relevant media.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　限制消费令的公告费用由被执行人负担；申请执行人申请在媒体公告的，应当垫付公告费用。

**Article 7** The cost of publishing the consumption restriction order shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement; where the application executor applies for publication in the media, they shall advance the publication costs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被限制消费的被执行人因生活或者经营必需而进行本规定禁止的消费活动的，应当向人民法院提出申请，获批准后方可进行。

**Article 8** Where a person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restriction needs to engage in consumption activities prohibited by these Provisions due to necessity for life or business operations, they shall submit an application to the People's Court, and may only engage in such activities after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在限制消费期间，被执行人提供确实有效的担保或者经申请执行人同意的，人民法院可以解除限制消费令；被执行人履行完毕生效法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院应当在[本规定第六条](#art-6)通知或者公告的范围内及时以通知或者公告解除限制消费令。

**Article 9** During the period of consumption restriction, if the person subject to enforcement provides effective and sufficient guarantee or obtains the consent of the application executor, the People's Court may lift the consumption restriction order; if the person subject to enforcement has fully performed the obligations confirmed by the effective legal instrument, the People's Court shall, within the scope of the notice or announcement under Article 6 of these Provisions, promptly lift the consumption restriction order by notice or announcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院应当设置举报电话或者邮箱，接受申请执行人和社会公众对被限制消费的被执行人违反[本规定第三条](#art-3)的举报，并进行审查认定。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall set up a tip line or email to receive reports from the application executor and the public regarding violations of Article 3 of these Provisions by a person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restriction, and shall review and confirm such reports.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被执行人违反限制消费令进行消费的行为属于拒不履行人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的行为，经查证属实的，依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定，予以拘留、罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，追究其刑事责任。  
有关单位在收到人民法院协助执行通知书后，仍允许被执行人进行高消费及非生活或者经营必需的有关消费的，人民法院可以依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定，追究其法律责任。

**Article 11** The act of a person subject to enforcement consuming in violation of a consumption restriction order constitutes an act of refusing to perform a legally effective judgment or ruling of the People's Court. If verified, the person shall be subject to detention or fine in accordance with Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued.  
Where a relevant unit, after receiving a notice of assistance in enforcement from the People's Court, still allows the person subject to enforcement to engage in high consumption or related consumption not necessary for life or business operations, the People's Court may pursue legal liability in accordance with Article 114 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.
