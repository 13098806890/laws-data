<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
对《当事人对人民法院强制执行生效具体  
行政行为的案件提出申诉人民法院应如何  
受理和处理的请示》的答复  
1995年8月22日      法行〔1995〕12号  
吉林省高级人民法院：  
你院《关于当事人对人民法院强制执行生效具体行政行为的案件提出申诉人民法院应如何受理和处理的请示》收悉。经研究认为：公民、法人和其他组织认为人民法院强制执行生效的具体行政行为违法，侵犯其合法权益，向人民法院提出申诉，人民法院可以作为申诉进行审查。人民法院的全部执行活动合法，而生效具体行政行为违法的，应转送作出具体行政行为的行政机关依法处理，并通知申诉人同该行政机关直接联系；人民法院采取的强制措施等违法，造成损害的，应依照国家赔偿法的有关规定办理。

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court  
on the Petition Submitted by a Party in a Case Where the People's Court Enforces a Specific Administrative Act that Has Taken Effect: How the People's Court Shall Accept and Handle  
August 22, 1995 Fa Xing [1995] No. 12  
Higher People's Court of Jilin Province:  
The Request for Instructions from your court titled "Request for Instructions on How the People's Court Shall Accept and Handle a Petition Submitted by a Party in a Case Where the People's Court Enforces a Specific Administrative Act that Has Taken Effect" has been received. Upon study, it is held that: where a citizen, legal person, or other organization considers that a specific administrative act enforced by the People's Court which has taken effect is illegal, infringing upon its lawful rights and interests, and submits a petition to the People's Court, the People's Court may examine it as a petition. If all enforcement activities of the People's Court are lawful, but the specific administrative act that has taken effect is illegal, the matter shall be transferred to the administrative organ that made the specific administrative act for handling according to law, and the petitioner shall be notified to contact the administrative organ directly; if compulsory measures taken by the People's Court are illegal and cause damage, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Compensation Law.
