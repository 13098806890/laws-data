# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕6号  
**公布日期**：2014-05-16  
**生效日期**：2014-06-06

> 最高人民法院《关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定》已于2014年4月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1614次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年6月6日起施行。 2014年5月16日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事 案件的若干规定 法释〔2014〕6号 为正确审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件，维护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国公证法》、《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人依照公证法第四十三条规定向人民法院起诉请求民事赔偿的，应当以公证机构为被告，人民法院应作为侵权责任纠纷案件受理。 第二条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人起诉请求变更、撤销公证书或者确认公证书无效的，人民法院不予受理，告知其依照公证法第三十九条规定可以向出具公证书的公证机构提出复查。 第三条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人对公证书所公证的民事权利义务有争议的，可以依照公证法第四十条规定就该争议向人民法院提起民事诉讼。 当事人、公证事项的利害关系人对具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书的民事权利义务有争议直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院依法不予受理。但是，公证债权文书被人民法院裁定不予执行的除外。 第四条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人提供证据证明公证机构及其公证员在公证活动中具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定公证机构有过错： （一）为不真实、不合法的事项出具公证书的； （二）毁损、篡改公证书或者公证档案的； （三）泄露在执业活动中知悉的商业秘密或者个人隐私的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人依照[公证法第四十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国公证法.md#art-43)规定向人民法院起诉请求民事赔偿的，应当以公证机构为被告，人民法院应作为侵权责任纠纷案件受理。

**Article 1** Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter institutes a prosecution in a People's Court for civil compensation in accordance with Article 43 of the Notarization Law, the notarial office shall be named as the defendant, and the People's Court shall accept the case as a dispute over tort liability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人起诉请求变更、撤销公证书或者确认公证书无效的，人民法院不予受理，告知其依照[公证法第三十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国公证法.md#art-39)规定可以向出具公证书的公证机构提出复查。

**Article 2** Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter institutes a prosecution requesting modification, revocation, or confirmation of invalidity of a notarial certificate, the People's Court shall not accept the case, and shall inform the person that, in accordance with Article 39 of the Notarization Law, a review may be requested from the notarial office that issued the notarial certificate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人对公证书所公证的民事权利义务有争议的，可以依照[公证法第四十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国公证法.md#art-40)规定就该争议向人民法院提起民事诉讼。  
当事人、公证事项的利害关系人对具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书的民事权利义务有争议直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院依法不予受理。但是，公证债权文书被人民法院裁定不予执行的除外。

**Article 3** Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter disputes the civil rights and obligations certified by a notarial certificate, a civil action may be instituted in the People's Court concerning such dispute in accordance with Article 40 of the Notarization Law.  
Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter directly institutes a civil action in the People's Court disputing the civil rights and obligations under a notarized creditor's document with compulsory enforcement effect, the People's Court shall not accept the case according to law, unless the notarized creditor's document has been ruled by the People's Court not to be enforced.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人提供证据证明公证机构及其公证员在公证活动中具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定公证机构有过错：  
（一）为不真实、不合法的事项出具公证书的；  
（二）毁损、篡改公证书或者公证档案的；  
（三）泄露在执业活动中知悉的商业秘密或者个人隐私的；  
（四）违反公证程序、办证规则以及国务院司法行政部门制定的行业规范出具公证书的；  
（五）公证机构在公证过程中未尽到充分的审查、核实义务，致使公证书错误或者不真实的；  
（六）对存在错误的公证书，经当事人、公证事项的利害关系人申请仍不予纠正或者补正的；  
（七）其他违反法律、法规、国务院司法行政部门强制性规定的情形。

**Article 4** Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter provides evidence proving that a notarial office or its notary has any of the following circumstances in notarization activities, the People's Court shall find that the notarial office is at fault:  
(1) issuing a notarial certificate for an untrue or unlawful matter;  
(2) damaging or altering a notarial certificate or notarial archives;  
(3) disclosing a trade secret or personal privacy learned in the course of practicing;  
(4) issuing a notarial certificate in violation of notarization procedures, certificate-issuing rules, or industry norms formulated by the judicial administration department of the State Council;  
(5) failing to fulfill the obligation of adequate review and verification during notarization, thereby causing the notarial certificate to be erroneous or untrue;  
(6) failing to correct or remedy an erroneous notarial certificate despite the application of the party or interested person of the notarized matter; or  
(7) other circumstances that violate laws, regulations, or compulsory provisions of the judicial administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人提供虚假证明材料申请公证致使公证书错误造成他人损失的，当事人应当承担赔偿责任。公证机构依法尽到审查、核实义务的，不承担赔偿责任；未依法尽到审查、核实义务的，应当承担与其过错相应的补充赔偿责任；明知公证证明的材料虚假或者与当事人恶意串通的，承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 5** Where a party applies for notarization by providing false supporting materials, thereby causing the notarial certificate to be erroneous and resulting in losses to another person, the party shall bear compensation liability. If the notarial office has fulfilled its obligation of review and verification according to law, it shall not bear compensation liability; if it fails to fulfill such obligation according to law, it shall bear supplementary compensation liability corresponding to its fault; if it knowingly knows that the materials certified are false or maliciously colludes with the party, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人明知公证机构所出具的公证书不真实、不合法而仍然使用造成自己损失，请求公证机构承担赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 6** Where a party or an interested person of a notarized matter, while knowing that the notarial certificate issued by the notarial office is untrue or unlawful, still uses it and thereby causes losses to itself, and requests the notarial office to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定施行后，涉及公证活动的民事案件尚未终审的，适用本规定；本规定施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本规定。

**Article 7** After these Provisions come into force, civil cases involving notarization activities that have not yet been finally adjudicated shall be governed by these Provisions; where a case has already been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, and a party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided according to the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.
