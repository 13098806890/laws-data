# 最高人民法院关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕26号  
**公布日期**：2001-08-27  
**生效日期**：2001-09-15

> （2001年8月7日最高人民法院审判委员会第1186次会议通过　2001年8月27日最高人民法院公告公布　自2001年9月15日起施行） 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区代表经协商，现就内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据问题规定如下： 一、一般规定 第一条　内地人民法院与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件（在内地包括劳动争议案件，在澳门特别行政区包括民事劳工案件）相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，均适用本安排。 第二条　双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，均须通过各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院进行。最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院可以直接相互委托送达和调取证据。 本安排在执行过程中遇有问题，应当通过最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院协商解决。 第三条　各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院相互收到对方法院的委托书后，应当立即将委托书及所附司法文书和相关文件转送根据其本辖区法律规定有权完成该受托事项的法院。 如果受委托方法院认为委托书不符合本安排规定，影响其完成受托事项时，应当及时通知委托方法院，并说明对委托书的异议。必要时可以要求委托方法院补充材料。 第四条　委托书应当以中文文本提出。所附司法文书及其他相关文件没有中文文本的，应当提供中文译本。 第五条　委托方法院应当在合理的期限内提出委托请求，以保证受委托方法院收到委托书后，及时完成受托事项。 受委托方法院应优先处理受托事项。完成受托事项的期限，送达文书最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起两个月，调取证据最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起三个月。 第六条　受委托方法院应当根据本辖区法律规定执行受托事项。委托方法院请求按照特殊方式执行委托事项的，如果受委托方法院认为不违反本辖区的法律规定，可以按照其特殊方式执行。 第七条　委托方法院无须支付受委托方法院在送达司法文书或调取证据时发生的费用或税项。但受委托方法院根据其本辖区法律规定，有权在调取证据时，要求委托方法院预付鉴定人、证人、翻译人员的费用，以及因采用委托方法院在委托书中请求以特殊方式送达司法文书或调取证据所产生的费用。 第八条　受委托方法院收到委托书后，不得以其本辖区法律规定对委托方法院审理的该民商事案件享有专属管辖权或不承认对该请求事项提起诉讼的权利为由，不予执行受托事项。 受委托方法院在执行受托事项时，如果该事项不属于法院职权范围，或者内地人民法院认为在内地执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或社会公共利益，或者澳门特别行政区法院认为在澳门特别行政区执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或公共秩序的，可以不予执行，但应当及时向委托方法院书面说明不予执行的原因。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　内地人民法院与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件（在内地包括劳动争议案件，在澳门特别行政区包括民事劳工案件）相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，均适用本安排。

**Article 1** This Arrangement shall apply to the mutual entrustment by the Mainland People's Courts and the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region for the service of judicial documents and the taking of evidence in civil and commercial cases (including labor dispute cases in the Mainland and civil labor cases in the Macao Special Administrative Region).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，均须通过各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院进行。最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院可以直接相互委托送达和调取证据。  
本安排在执行过程中遇有问题，应当通过最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院协商解决。

**Article 2** Mutual entrustments by the two sides for the service of judicial documents and the taking of evidence shall be conducted through the Higher People's Courts and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region. The Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region may directly entrust each other to serve documents and take evidence.  
Where problems arise during the implementation of this Arrangement, they shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院相互收到对方法院的委托书后，应当立即将委托书及所附司法文书和相关文件转送根据其本辖区法律规定有权完成该受托事项的法院。  
如果受委托方法院认为委托书不符合本安排规定，影响其完成受托事项时，应当及时通知委托方法院，并说明对委托书的异议。必要时可以要求委托方法院补充材料。

**Article 3** Upon receiving the letter of request from the court of the other side, each Higher People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall promptly forward the letter of request together with the attached judicial documents and related documents to the court that has the authority to complete the entrusted matter under the laws of its own jurisdiction.  
Where the requested court considers that the letter of request does not comply with the provisions of this Arrangement and thus affects its ability to complete the entrusted matter, it shall promptly notify the requesting court and state its objections to the letter of request. When necessary, it may request the requesting court to provide supplementary materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　委托书应当以中文文本提出。所附司法文书及其他相关文件没有中文文本的，应当提供中文译本。

**Article 4** The letter of request shall be submitted in the Chinese language. If the attached judicial documents and other related documents are not in the Chinese language, a Chinese translation shall be provided.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　委托方法院应当在合理的期限内提出委托请求，以保证受委托方法院收到委托书后，及时完成受托事项。  
受委托方法院应优先处理受托事项。完成受托事项的期限，送达文书最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起两个月，调取证据最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起三个月。

**Article 5** The requesting court shall make the request within a reasonable period to ensure that, after receiving the letter of request, the requested court can complete the entrusted matter in a timely manner.  
The requested court shall give priority to the handling of the entrusted matter. The time limit for completing the entrusted matter shall not exceed two months from the date of receipt of the letter of request for service of documents, and shall not exceed three months from the date of receipt of the letter of request for taking of evidence.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　受委托方法院应当根据本辖区法律规定执行受托事项。委托方法院请求按照特殊方式执行委托事项的，如果受委托方法院认为不违反本辖区的法律规定，可以按照其特殊方式执行。

**Article 6** The requested court shall execute the entrusted matter in accordance with the laws of its own jurisdiction. Where the requesting court requests that the entrusted matter be executed in a special manner, the requested court may execute it in such special manner if it considers that doing so does not violate the laws of its own jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　委托方法院无须支付受委托方法院在送达司法文书或调取证据时发生的费用或税项。但受委托方法院根据其本辖区法律规定，有权在调取证据时，要求委托方法院预付鉴定人、证人、翻译人员的费用，以及因采用委托方法院在委托书中请求以特殊方式送达司法文书或调取证据所产生的费用。

**Article 7** The requesting court shall not be required to pay any fees or taxes incurred by the requested court in the service of judicial documents or the taking of evidence. However, the requested court, in accordance with the laws of its own jurisdiction, shall have the right, when taking evidence, to request the requesting court to advance the expenses of expert evaluators, witnesses, and interpreters, as well as the expenses incurred by adopting the special manner requested by the requesting court in the letter of request for serving judicial documents or taking evidence.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　受委托方法院收到委托书后，不得以其本辖区法律规定对委托方法院审理的该民商事案件享有专属管辖权或不承认对该请求事项提起诉讼的权利为由，不予执行受托事项。  
受委托方法院在执行受托事项时，如果该事项不属于法院职权范围，或者内地人民法院认为在内地执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或社会公共利益，或者澳门特别行政区法院认为在澳门特别行政区执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或公共秩序的，可以不予执行，但应当及时向委托方法院书面说明不予执行的原因。  
二、司法文书的送达

**Article 8** After receiving the letter of request, the requested court shall not refuse to execute the entrusted matter on the ground that the laws of its own jurisdiction grant exclusive jurisdiction over the civil or commercial case being heard by the requesting court or do not recognize the right to bring an action in respect of the requested matter.  
Where, in executing the entrusted matter, the matter is not within the functions and powers of the court, or where a Mainland People's Court considers that executing the entrusted matter in the Mainland would violate its fundamental legal principles or social public interests, or where a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region considers that executing the entrusted matter in the Macao Special Administrative Region would violate its fundamental legal principles or public order, the requested court may refuse to execute it, but shall promptly inform the requesting court in writing of the reasons for the refusal.  
II. Service of Judicial Documents

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　委托方法院请求送达司法文书，须出具盖有其印章的委托书，并在委托书中说明委托机关的名称、受送达人的姓名或者名称、详细地址及案件性质。如果执行方法院请求按特殊方式送达或者有特别注意的事项的，应当在委托书中注明。

**Article 9** When requesting the service of judicial documents, the requesting court shall issue a letter of request bearing its seal, and shall state in the letter of request the name of the requesting authority, the name of the person on whom service is to be made, the detailed address, and the nature of the case. If the requesting court requests service in a special manner or has matters requiring special attention, it shall indicate this in the letter of request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　委托书及所附司法文书和其他相关文件一式两份，受送达人为两人以上的，每人一式两份。

**Article 10** The letter of request and the attached judicial documents and other related documents shall be in duplicate. Where there are two or more persons on whom service is to be made, each person shall be provided with duplicate copies.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　完成司法文书送达事项后，内地人民法院应当出具送达回证；澳门特别行政区法院应当出具送达证明书。出具的送达回证和送达证明书，应当注明送达的方法、地点和日期，及司法文书接收人的身份，并加盖法院印章。  
受委托方法院无法送达的，应当在送达回证或者送达证明书上注明妨碍送达的原因、拒收事由和日期，并及时退回委托书及所附全部文件。

**Article 11** After the service of judicial documents has been completed, the Mainland People's Court shall issue a certificate of service; the court of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall issue a proof of service. The certificate of service or proof of service issued shall indicate the method, place, and date of service, as well as the identity of the recipient of the judicial documents, and shall be affixed with the court seal.  
If the requested court is unable to effect service, it shall state on the certificate of service or proof of service the reasons preventing service, the grounds for refusal and the date thereof, and shall promptly return the letter of request and all attached documents.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　不论委托方法院司法文书中确定的出庭日期或者期限是否已过，受委托方法院均应送达。

**Article 12** The requested court shall effect service regardless of whether the date or time limit for appearance specified in the judicial documents of the requesting court has already passed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　受委托方法院对委托方法院委托送达的司法文书和所附相关文件的内容和后果不负法律责任。

**Article 13** The requested court shall not bear legal liability for the content and consequences of the judicial documents and related documents entrusted for service by the requesting court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本安排中的司法文书在内地包括：起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、授权委托书、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证以及其他司法文书和所附相关文件；在澳门特别行政区包括：起诉状复本、答辩状复本、反诉状复本、上诉状复本、陈述书、申辩书、声明异议书、反驳书、申请书、撤诉书、认诺书、和解书、财产目录、财产分割表、和解建议书、债权人协议书、传唤书、通知书、法官批示、命令状、法庭许可令状、判决书、合议庭裁判书、送达证明书以及其他司法文书和所附相关文件。  
三、调取证据

**Article 14** For the purposes of this Arrangement, judicial documents in the Mainland include: copies of the statement of prosecution, copies of the statement of appeal, copies of the counterclaim, copies of the statement of defense, powers of attorney, summons, judgments, mediation statements, rulings, payment orders, decisions, notices, certificates, certificates of service, and other judicial documents and related documents; in the Macao Special Administrative Region, they include: duplicates of the statement of prosecution, duplicates of the statement of defense, duplicates of the counterclaim, duplicates of the statement of appeal, statements of case, statements of defense, statements of objection, statements of reply, applications, statements of withdrawal, acknowledgments, settlement agreements, inventories of property, property distribution schedules, settlement proposals, creditors' agreements, summons, notices, judicial orders, orders, court permits, judgments, collegiate bench rulings, proofs of service, and other judicial documents and related documents.  
III. Taking of Evidence

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　委托方法院请求调取的证据只能是用于与诉讼有关的证据。

**Article 15** The evidence that the requesting court requests to be taken shall only be evidence that is relevant to the proceedings.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　双方相互委托代为调取证据的委托书应当写明：  
（一）委托法院的名称；  
（二）当事人及其诉讼代理人的姓名、地址，及其他一切有助于辨别其身份的情况；  
（三）委托调取证据的原因，以及委托调取证据的具体事项；  
（四）被调查人的姓名、地址，及其他一切有助于辨别其身份的情况，以及需要向其提出的问题；  
（五）调取证据需采用的特殊方式；  
（六）有助于执行该委托的其他一切情况。

**Article 16** The letter of request for the mutual entrustment of the taking of evidence between the two sides shall state:  
(1) The name of the requesting court;  
(2) The names and addresses of the parties and their litigation agents, and any other information helpful to identify them;  
(3) The reasons for requesting the taking of evidence and the specific matters for which evidence is requested;  
(4) The name and address of the person to be examined, and any other information helpful to identify them, as well as the questions to be put to them;  
(5) The special manner, if any, in which the evidence is to be taken;  
(6) Any other information helpful to the execution of the request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　代为调取证据的范围包括：代为询问当事人、证人和鉴定人，代为进行鉴定和司法勘验，调取其他与诉讼有关的证据。

**Article 17** The scope of the taking of evidence on behalf of the requesting court includes: examining parties, witnesses, and expert evaluators on behalf of the requesting court; conducting expert evaluations and judicial inspections on behalf of the requesting court; and taking other evidence relevant to the proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　如委托方法院提出要求，受委托方法院应当将取证的时间、地点通知委托方法院，以便有关当事人及其诉讼代理人能够出席。

**Article 18** If the requesting court so requests, the requested court shall notify the requesting court of the time and place for the taking of evidence, so that the relevant parties and their litigation agents may be present.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　受委托方法院在执行委托调取证据时，根据委托方法院的请求，可以允许委托方法院派司法人员出席。必要时，经受委托方允许，委托方法院的司法人员可以向证人、鉴定人等发问。

**Article 19** When executing a request for the taking of evidence, the requested court may, upon the request of the requesting court, permit judicial personnel of the requesting court to be present. Where necessary, subject to the permission of the requested party, judicial personnel of the requesting court may put questions to witnesses, expert evaluators, and others.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　受委托方法院完成委托调取证据的事项后，应当向委托方法院书面说明。  
如果未能按委托方法院的请求全部或部分完成调取证据事项，受委托方法院应当向委托方法院书面说明妨碍调取证据的原因，并及时退回委托书及所附全部文件。  
如果当事人、证人根据受委托方的法律规定，拒绝作证或推辞提供证言时，受委托方法院应当以书面通知委托方法院，并退回委托书及所附全部文件。

**Article 20** After completing the entrusted matter of taking evidence, the requested court shall inform the requesting court in writing.  
If the requested court is unable to complete the taking of evidence in whole or in part as requested by the requesting court, it shall inform the requesting court in writing of the reasons preventing the taking of evidence and shall promptly return the letter of request together with all attached documents.  
Where a party or witness refuses to testify or declines to give testimony in accordance with the laws of the requested party, the requested court shall notify the requesting court in writing and return the letter of request together with all attached documents.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　受委托方法院可以根据委托方法院的请求，并经证人、鉴定人同意，协助安排其辖区的证人、鉴定人到对方辖区出庭作证。  
证人、鉴定人在委托方地域内逗留期间，不得因在其离开受委托方地域之前，在委托方境内所实施的行为或针对他所作的裁决而被刑事起诉、羁押，或者为履行刑罚或者其他处罚而被剥夺财产或者扣留身份证件，或者以任何方式对其人身自由加以限制。  
证人、鉴定人完成所需诉讼行为，且可自由离开委托方地域后，在委托方境内逗留超过7天，或者已离开委托方地域又自行返回时，前款所指的豁免即行终止。  
证人、鉴定人到委托方法院出庭而导致的费用及补偿，由委托方法院预付。  
该条所指出庭作证人员，在澳门特别行政区还包括当事人。

**Article 21** The requested court may, upon the request of the requesting court and with the consent of the witnesses and expert evaluators, assist in arranging for witnesses and expert evaluators within its jurisdiction to appear and testify in the other party's jurisdiction.  
During the period a witness or expert evaluator stays in the territory of the requesting party, they shall not be subject to criminal prosecution or custody, nor shall they be deprived of property or have their identity documents withheld for the purpose of executing a penalty or other punishment, nor shall their personal freedom be restricted in any way, on account of any act committed or any ruling made against them in the territory of the requesting party prior to their departure from the territory of the requested party.  
The immunity referred to in the preceding paragraph shall cease when the witness or expert evaluator, having completed the required procedural acts and being free to leave the territory of the requesting party, remains in that territory for more than seven days, or having left that territory, voluntarily returns to it.  
The expenses and compensation arising from the appearance of witnesses and expert evaluators before the requesting court shall be advanced by the requesting court.  
The persons referred to in this Article who appear to testify shall, in the Macao Special Administrative Region, also include parties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　受委托方法院取证时，被调查的当事人、证人、鉴定人等的代理人可以出席。  
四、附　　则

**Article 22** When the requested court takes evidence, the agents of the parties, witnesses, expert evaluators, or other persons being examined may be present.  
IV. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　受委托方法院可以根据委托方法院的请求代为查询并提供本辖区的有关法律。

**Article 23** The requested court may, upon the request of the requesting court, make inquiries and provide information on the relevant laws of its own jurisdiction.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　如果本安排需要修改，应当通过最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区代表协商解决。

**Article 24** If this Arrangement needs to be amended, it shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the representatives of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本安排自2001年9月15日起生效。

**Article 25** This Arrangement shall take effect as of September 15, 2001.
