（2018年12月25日  最高人民检察院  
第十三届检察委员会第十二次会议通过）  
北京市人民检察院：  
你院《关于认定累犯如何确定刑罚执行完毕以后五年以内起始日期的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)第一款规定的“刑罚执行完毕”，是指刑罚执行到期应予释放之日。认定累犯，确定刑罚执行完毕以后“五年以内”的起始日期，应当从刑满释放之日起计算。  
此复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
December 28, 2018  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Reply on How to Determine the Starting Date of the “Five-Year” Period After the Completion of Punishment Execution for the Recognition of Recidivism  
(Adopted at the 12th Session of the 13th Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on December 25, 2018)  
Beijing Municipal People's Procuratorate:  
Your Request for Instructions on How to Determine the Starting Date of the Five-Year Period After the Completion of Punishment Execution for the Recognition of Recidivism has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
The term “completion of punishment execution” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 65 of the Criminal Law refers to the date on which the punishment is due to be completed and the person shall be released. When determining the starting date of the “five-year” period after the completion of punishment execution for the recognition of recidivism, the calculation shall commence from the date of release upon completion of the sentence.  
This reply is hereby given.
