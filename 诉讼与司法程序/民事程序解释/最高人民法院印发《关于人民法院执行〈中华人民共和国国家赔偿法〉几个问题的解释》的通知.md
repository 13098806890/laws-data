# 最高人民法院印发《关于人民法院执行〈中华人民共和国国家赔偿法〉几个问题的解释》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕15号  
**公布日期**：1996-05-06  
**生效日期**：1996-05-06

> （最高人民法院审判委员会第811次会议讨论通过） 一、根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》（以下简称赔偿法）第十七条第（二）项、第（三）项的规定，依照刑法第十四条、第十五条规定不负刑事责任的人和依照刑事诉讼法第十五条规定不追究刑事责任的人被羁押，国家不承担赔偿责任。但是对起诉后经人民法院判处拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑和死刑并已执行的上列人员，有权依法取得赔偿。判决确定前被羁押的日期依法不予赔偿。 二、依照赔偿法第三十一条的规定，人民法院在民事诉讼、行政诉讼过程中，违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全措施或者对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，造成损害，具有以下情形之一的，适用刑事赔偿程序予以赔偿： （一）错误实施司法拘留、罚款的； （二）实施赔偿法第十五条第（四）项、第（五）项规定行为的； （三）实施赔偿法第十六条第（一）项规定行为的。 人民法院审理的民事、经济、行政案件发生错判并已执行，依法应当执行回转的，或者当事人申请财产保全、先予执行，申请有错误造成财产损失依法应由申请人赔偿的，国家不承担赔偿责任。 三、公民、法人和其他组织申请人民法院依照赔偿法规定予以赔偿的案件，应当经过依法确认。未经依法确认的，赔偿请求人应当要求有关人民法院予以确认。被要求的人民法院由有关审判庭负责办理依法确认事宜，并应以人民法院的名义答复赔偿请求人。被要求的人民法院不予确认的，赔偿请求人有权申诉。 四、根据赔偿法第二十六条、第二十七条的规定，人民法院判处管制、有期徒刑缓刑、剥夺政治权利等刑罚的人被依法改判无罪的，国家不承担赔偿责任，但是，赔偿请求人在判决生效前被羁押的，依法有权取得赔偿。 五、根据赔偿法第十九条第四款“再审改判无罪的，作出原生效判决的人民法院为赔偿义务机关”的规定，原一审人民法院作出判决后，被告人没有上诉，人民检察院没有抗诉，判决发生法律效力的，原一审人民法院为赔偿义务机关；被告人上诉或者人民检察院抗诉，原二审人民法院维持一审判决或者对一审人民法院判决予以改判的，原二审人民法院为赔偿义务机关。 六、赔偿法第二十六条关于“侵犯公民人身自由的，每日的赔偿金按照国家上年度职工日平均工资计算”中规定的上年度，应为赔偿义务机关、复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会作出赔偿决定时的上年度；复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会决定维持原赔偿决定的，按作出原赔偿决定时的上年度执行。 国家上年度职工日平均工资数额，应当以职工年平均工资除以全年法定工作日数的方法计算。年平均工资以国家统计局公布的数字为准。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》（以下简称赔偿法）第十七条第（二）项、第（三）项的规定，依照[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)、第十五条规定不负刑事责任的人和依照[刑事诉讼法第十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-15)规定不追究刑事责任的人被羁押，国家不承担赔偿责任。但是对起诉后经人民法院判处拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑和死刑并已执行的上列人员，有权依法取得赔偿。判决确定前被羁押的日期依法不予赔偿。

**Article 1** 1. According to the provisions of Items (2) and (3) of Article 17 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Compensation Law), if a person who shall not bear criminal liability in accordance with Articles 14 and 15 of the Criminal Law or a person whose criminal liability shall not be investigated in accordance with Article 15 of the Criminal Procedure Law is held in custody, the state shall not bear compensation liability. However, the above-mentioned persons, after prosecution, who have been sentenced by the People's Court to criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, life imprisonment or death penalty and whose sentences have been executed, shall have the right to obtain compensation according to law. The period of custody before the judgment is determined shall not be compensated according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、依照赔偿法第三十一条的规定，人民法院在民事诉讼、行政诉讼过程中，违法采取对妨害诉讼的强制措施、保全措施或者对判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书执行错误，造成损害，具有以下情形之一的，适用刑事赔偿程序予以赔偿：  
（一）错误实施司法拘留、罚款的；  
（二）实施赔偿法第十五条第（四）项、第（五）项规定行为的；  
（三）实施赔偿法第十六条第（一）项规定行为的。  
人民法院审理的民事、经济、行政案件发生错判并已执行，依法应当执行回转的，或者当事人申请财产保全、先予执行，申请有错误造成财产损失依法应由申请人赔偿的，国家不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 2** 2. In accordance with Article 31 of the Compensation Law, if, during the process of a civil action or administrative action, the People's Court illegally adopts compulsory measures against obstruction of proceedings, preservation measures, or commits errors in the enforcement of judgments, rulings, or other legally effective documents, causing damage, and any of the following circumstances exists, compensation shall be made through the criminal compensation procedure:  
(1) erroneous imposition of judicial detention or fines;  
(2) carrying out of acts specified in Items (4) and (5) of Article 15 of the Compensation Law;  
(3) carrying out of acts specified in Item (1) of Article 16 of the Compensation Law.  
Where a civil, economic or administrative case adjudicated by the People's Court results in an erroneous judgment that has been enforced, and restitution shall be made according to law, or where a party applies for property preservation or advance execution and the application is erroneous, causing property losses, and the applicant shall bear compensation liability according to law, the state shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、公民、法人和其他组织申请人民法院依照赔偿法规定予以赔偿的案件，应当经过依法确认。未经依法确认的，赔偿请求人应当要求有关人民法院予以确认。被要求的人民法院由有关审判庭负责办理依法确认事宜，并应以人民法院的名义答复赔偿请求人。被要求的人民法院不予确认的，赔偿请求人有权申诉。

**Article 3** 3. Cases in which citizens, legal persons or other organizations apply to the People's Court for compensation in accordance with the provisions of the Compensation Law shall undergo confirmation according to law. If no confirmation has been made according to law, the claimant for compensation shall request the relevant People's Court to make confirmation. The requested People's Court shall have the relevant adjudication division handle the matter of confirmation according to law, and shall respond to the claimant for compensation in the name of the People's Court. If the requested People's Court refuses to make confirmation, the claimant for compensation shall have the right to petition.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、根据赔偿法第二十六条、第二十七条的规定，人民法院判处管制、有期徒刑缓刑、剥夺政治权利等刑罚的人被依法改判无罪的，国家不承担赔偿责任，但是，赔偿请求人在判决生效前被羁押的，依法有权取得赔偿。

**Article 4** 4. According to Articles 26 and 27 of the Compensation Law, where a person sentenced by the People's Court to such punishments as public surveillance, probation for fixed-term imprisonment, or deprivation of political rights is acquitted upon retrial according to law, the state shall not bear compensation liability. However, if the claimant for compensation was held in custody before the judgment took effect, he/she shall have the right to obtain compensation according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、根据赔偿法第十九条第四款“再审改判无罪的，作出原生效判决的人民法院为赔偿义务机关”的规定，原一审人民法院作出判决后，被告人没有上诉，人民检察院没有抗诉，判决发生法律效力的，原一审人民法院为赔偿义务机关；被告人上诉或者人民检察院抗诉，原二审人民法院维持一审判决或者对一审人民法院判决予以改判的，原二审人民法院为赔偿义务机关。

**Article 5** 5. According to the provision of Paragraph 4 of Article 19 of the Compensation Law, which states, "If the guilty verdict is reversed upon retrial, the People's Court that rendered the original effective judgment shall be the compensatory obligor," where after the original first-instance People's Court renders a judgment, the defendant does not appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not protest, and the judgment becomes legally effective, the original first-instance People's Court shall be the compensatory obligor; where the defendant appeals or the People's Procuratorate protests, and the original second-instance People's Court affirms the first-instance judgment or modifies the judgment of the first-instance People's Court, the original second-instance People's Court shall be the compensatory obligor.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、赔偿法第二十六条关于“侵犯公民人身自由的，每日的赔偿金按照国家上年度职工日平均工资计算”中规定的上年度，应为赔偿义务机关、复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会作出赔偿决定时的上年度；复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会决定维持原赔偿决定的，按作出原赔偿决定时的上年度执行。  
国家上年度职工日平均工资数额，应当以职工年平均工资除以全年法定工作日数的方法计算。年平均工资以国家统计局公布的数字为准。

**Article 6** 6. The "previous year" as provided in Article 26 of the Compensation Law, which states, "Where a citizen's personal freedom is infringed, the daily compensation amount shall be calculated on the basis of the average daily wage of employees in the state in the previous year," shall refer to the previous year at the time when the compensatory obligor, the reconsideration authority, or the Compensation Committee of the People's Court makes the compensation decision; if the reconsideration authority or the Compensation Committee of the People's Court decides to uphold the original compensation decision, the previous year at the time of the original compensation decision shall apply.  
The average daily wage of employees in the state in the previous year shall be calculated by dividing the average annual wage by the number of legal working days in the entire year. The average annual wage shall be based on the figures published by the National Bureau of Statistics.
