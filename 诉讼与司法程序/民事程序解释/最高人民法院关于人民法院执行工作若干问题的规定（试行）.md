# 最高人民法院关于人民法院执行工作若干问题的规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕15号  
**公布日期**：1998-07-08  
**生效日期**：1998-07-18

> （1998年6月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第992次会议通过　1998年7月8日最高人民法院公告公布　自1998年7月18日起施行） 为了保证在执行程序中正确适用法律，及时有效地执行生效法律文书，维护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法）等有关法律的规定，结合人民法院执行工作的实践经验，现对人民法院执行工作若干问题作如下规定。 一、执行机构及其职责 1.人民法院根据需要，依据有关法律的规定，设立执行机构，专门负责执行工作。 2.执行机构负责执行下列生效法律文书： （1）人民法院民事、行政判决、裁定、调解书，民事制裁决定、支付令，以及刑事附带民事判决、裁定、调解书； （2）依法应由人民法院执行的行政处罚决定、行政处理决定； （3）我国仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决和调解书，人民法院依据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》有关规定作出的财产保全和证据保全裁定； （4）公证机关依法赋予强制执行效力的关于追偿债款、物品的债权文书； （5）经人民法院裁定承认其效力的外国法院作出的判决、裁定，以及国外仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决； （6）法律规定由人民法院执行的其他法律文书。 3.人民法院在审理民事、行政案件中作出的财产保全和先予执行裁定，由审理案件的审判庭负责执行。 4.人民法庭审结的案件，由人民法庭负责执行。其中复杂、疑难或被执行人不在本法院辖区的案件，由执行机构负责执行。 5.执行程序中重大事项的办理，应由3名以上执行员讨论，并报经院长批准。 6.依据民事诉讼法第二百一十七条或第二百六十条的规定对仲裁裁决是否有不予执行事由进行审查的，应组成合议庭进行。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、执行机构及其职责  
1.人民法院根据需要，依据有关法律的规定，设立执行机构，专门负责执行工作。  
2.执行机构负责执行下列生效法律文书：  
（1）人民法院民事、行政判决、裁定、调解书，民事制裁决定、支付令，以及刑事附带民事判决、裁定、调解书；  
（2）依法应由人民法院执行的行政处罚决定、行政处理决定；  
（3）我国仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决和调解书，人民法院依据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》有关规定作出的财产保全和证据保全裁定；  
（4）公证机关依法赋予强制执行效力的关于追偿债款、物品的债权文书；  
（5）经人民法院裁定承认其效力的外国法院作出的判决、裁定，以及国外仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决；  
（6）法律规定由人民法院执行的其他法律文书。  
3.人民法院在审理民事、行政案件中作出的财产保全和先予执行裁定，由审理案件的审判庭负责执行。  
4.人民法庭审结的案件，由人民法庭负责执行。其中复杂、疑难或被执行人不在本法院辖区的案件，由执行机构负责执行。  
5.执行程序中重大事项的办理，应由3名以上执行员讨论，并报经院长批准。  
6.依据[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)或第二百六十条的规定对仲裁裁决是否有不予执行事由进行审查的，应组成合议庭进行。  
7.执行机构应配备必要的交通工具、通讯设备、音像设备和警械用具等，以保障及时有效地履行职责。  
8.执行人员执行公务时，应向有关人员出示工作证和执行公务证，并按规定着装。必要时应由司法警察参加。  
执行公务证由最高人民法院统一制发。  
9.上级人民法院执行机构负责本院对下级人民法院执行工作的监督、指导和协调。

**Article 1** I. Enforcement Organs and Their Functions  
1. A People's Court shall, according to the needs and pursuant to the relevant provisions of laws, establish an enforcement organ to be specially responsible for enforcement work.  
2. The enforcement organ shall be responsible for enforcing the following effective legal instruments:  
(1) Civil and administrative judgments, rulings, and mediation papers of the People's Court, civil sanction decisions, payment orders, as well as criminal incidental civil judgments, rulings, and mediation papers;  
(2) Administrative penalty decisions and administrative disposition decisions that shall be enforced by the People's Court according to law;  
(3) Arbitration awards and mediation papers made by arbitration institutions of China, and rulings on property preservation and evidence preservation made by the People's Court pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) Notarial documents concerning the recovery of debts and articles that have been granted compulsory enforcement effect by a notary authority according to law;  
(5) Judgments and rulings of foreign courts whose validity has been recognized by a ruling of the People's Court, and arbitration awards made by foreign arbitration institutions;  
(6) Other legal instruments that shall be enforced by the People's Court as provided by law.  
3. Rulings on property preservation and advance execution made by the People's Court in the course of trying civil or administrative cases shall be enforced by the trial division that tries the case.  
4. Cases concluded by a people's tribunal shall be enforced by that people's tribunal. Among them, cases that are complex, difficult, or where the person subject to enforcement is not within the jurisdiction of that court shall be enforced by the enforcement organ.  
5. Handling of major matters in the enforcement procedure shall be discussed by three or more enforcement officers and shall be submitted to the president of the court for approval.  
6. Where a review is conducted on whether an arbitration award has grounds for non-enforcement pursuant to Article 217 or Article 260 of the Civil Procedure Law, a collegial panel shall be formed to conduct the review.  
7. The enforcement organ shall be equipped with necessary means of transport, communication equipment, audio-video equipment, police implements, etc., to ensure timely and effective performance of duties.  
8. When carrying out official duties, an enforcement officer shall show his/her work ID card and enforcement official card to the relevant persons, and shall dress according to regulations. When necessary, judicial police shall participate.  
The enforcement official card shall be uniformly issued by the Supreme People's Court.  
9. The enforcement organ of a higher People's Court shall be responsible for the supervision, guidance, and coordination of the enforcement work of the lower People's Courts by that court.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、执行管辖  
10.仲裁机构作出的国内仲裁裁决、公证机关依法赋予强制执行效力的公证债权文书，由被执行人住所地或被执行的财产所在地人民法院执行。  
前款案件的级别管辖，参照各地法院受理诉讼案件的级别管辖的规定确定。  
11.在国内仲裁过程中，当事人申请财产保全，经仲裁机构提交人民法院的，由被申请人住所地或被申请保全的财产所在地的基层人民法院裁定并执行；申请证据保全的，由证据所在地的基层人民法院裁定并执行。  
12.在涉外仲裁过程中，当事人申请财产保全，经仲裁机构提交人民法院的，由被申请人住所地或被申请保全的财产所在地的中级人民法院裁定并执行；申请证据保全的，由证据所在地的中级人民法院裁定并执行。  
13.专利管理机关依法作出的处理决定和处罚决定，由被执行人住所地或财产所在地的省、自治区、直辖市有权受理专利纠纷案件的中级人民法院执行。  
14.国务院各部门、各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和海关依照法律、法规作出的处理决定和处罚决定，由被执行人住所地或财产所在地的中级人民法院执行。  
15.两个以上人民法院都有管辖权的，当事人可以向其中一个人民法院申请执行；当事人向两个以上人民法院申请执行的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。  
16.人民法院之间因执行管辖权发生争议的，由双方协商解决；协商不成的，报请双方共同的上级人民法院指定管辖。  
17.基层人民法院和中级人民法院管辖的执行案件，因特殊情况需要由上级人民法院执行的，可以报请上级人民法院执行。

**Article 2** II. Jurisdiction over Execution  
10. Domestic arbitration awards made by arbitration institutions and notarized documents of creditor's rights to which a notary office has legally given compulsory execution effect shall be executed by the People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to execution or where the property subject to execution is located.  
The level jurisdiction over the cases in the preceding paragraph shall be determined by reference to the provisions on level jurisdiction over litigation cases accepted by courts in various regions.  
11. During domestic arbitration, if a party applies for property preservation and the arbitration institution submits it to the People's Court, the Primary People's Court at the domicile of the respondent or where the property to be preserved is located shall make a ruling and execute it; if applying for evidence preservation, the Primary People's Court at the location of the evidence shall make a ruling and execute it.  
12. During foreign-related arbitration, if a party applies for property preservation and the arbitration institution submits it to the People's Court, the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the respondent or where the property to be preserved is located shall make a ruling and execute it; if applying for evidence preservation, the Intermediate People's Court at the location of the evidence shall make a ruling and execute it.  
13. Treatment decisions and penalty decisions lawfully made by patent administrative authorities shall be executed by the Intermediate People's Court in the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the person subject to execution is domiciled or where the property is located, which has the authority to accept patent dispute cases.  
14. Treatment decisions and penalty decisions made by various departments of the State Council, people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and customs in accordance with laws and regulations shall be executed by the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to execution or where the property is located.  
15. If two or more People's Courts all have jurisdiction, a party may apply for execution to one of them; if a party applies for execution to two or more People's Courts, the court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.  
16. If a dispute arises between People's Courts over execution jurisdiction, the parties shall resolve it through negotiation; if negotiation fails, they shall report to their common higher People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.  
17. For execution cases under the jurisdiction of Primary People's Courts and Intermediate People's Courts, if execution by a higher People's Court is necessary due to special circumstances, they may report to the higher People's Court for execution.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、执行的申请和移送  
18.人民法院受理执行案件应当符合下列条件：  
（1）申请或移送执行的法律文书已经生效；  
（2）申请执行人是生效法律文书确定的权利人或其继承人、权利承受人；  
（3）申请执行人在法定期限内提出申请；  
（4）申请执行的法律文书有给付内容，且执行标的和被执行人明确；  
（5）义务人在生效法律文书确定的期限内未履行义务；  
（6）属于受申请执行的人民法院管辖。  
人民法院对符合上述条件的申请，应当在7日内予以立案；不符合上述条件之一的，应当在7日内裁定不予受理。  
19.生效法律文书的执行，一般应当由当事人依法提出申请。  
发生法律效力的具有给付赡养费、扶养费、抚育费内容的法律文书、民事制裁决定书，以及刑事附带民事判决、裁定、调解书，由审判庭移送执行机构执行。  
20.申请执行，应向人民法院提交下列文件和证件：  
（1）申请执行书。申请执行书中应当写明申请执行的理由、事项、执行标的，以及申请执行人所了解的被执行人的财产状况。  
申请执行人书写申请执行书确有困难的，可以口头提出申请。人民法院接待人员对口头申请应当制作笔录，由申请执行人签字或盖章。  
外国一方当事人申请执行的，应当提交中文申请执行书。当事人所在国与我国缔结或共同参加的司法协助条约有特别规定的，按照条约规定办理。  
（2）生效法律文书副本。  
（3）申请执行人的身份证明。公民个人申请的，应当出示居民身份证；法人申请的，应当提交法人营业执照副本和法定代表人身份证明；其他组织申请的，应当提交营业执照副本和主要负责人身份证明。  
（4）继承人或权利承受人申请执行的，应当提交继承或承受权利的证明文件。  
（5）其他应当提交的文件或证件。  
21.申请执行仲裁机构的仲裁裁决，应当向人民法院提交有仲裁条款的合同书或仲裁协议书。  
申请执行国外仲裁机构的仲裁裁决的，应当提交经我国驻外使领馆认证或我国公证机关公证的仲裁裁决书中文本。  
22.申请执行人可以委托代理人代为申请执行。委托代理的，应当向人民法院提交经委托人签字或盖章的授权委托书，写明委托事项和代理人的权限。  
委托代理人代为放弃、变更民事权利，或代为进行执行和解，或代为收取执行款项的，应当有委托人的特别授权。  
23.申请人民法院强制执行，应当按照人民法院诉讼收费办法的规定缴纳申请执行的费用。

**Article 3** III. Application and Transfer for Enforcement  
18. A People's Court shall accept an enforcement case subject to the following conditions:  
(1) The legal instrument for which enforcement is applied or transferred has taken effect;  
(2) The applicant for enforcement is the right holder determined in the effective legal instrument, or his/her heir or successor;  
(3) The applicant for enforcement has filed the application within the statutory time limit;  
(4) The legal instrument for which enforcement is applied contains a content of payment, and the subject matter of enforcement and the person subject to enforcement are definite;  
(5) The obligor has not performed the obligation within the time limit determined in the effective legal instrument;  
(6) The case is under the jurisdiction of the People's Court to which the application for enforcement is made.  
A People's Court shall, within seven days, docket an application that meets the above conditions; if any of the above conditions is not met, it shall, within seven days, rule to reject the application.  
19. Enforcement of an effective legal instrument shall generally be initiated by the party by applying according to law.  
Legal instruments that have taken legal effect and contain content on payment of alimony, support, or maintenance, civil sanction decisions, and criminal incidental civil judgments, rulings, and mediation papers shall be transferred by the trial division to the enforcement organ for enforcement.  
20. To apply for enforcement, the following documents and certificates shall be submitted to the People's Court:  
(1) Application for enforcement. The application for enforcement shall state the reasons for enforcement, the matters, the subject matter of enforcement, and the property status of the person subject to enforcement known to the applicant.  
If the applicant for enforcement has genuine difficulty in writing the application for enforcement, an oral application may be made. The reception personnel of the People's Court shall make a record of the oral application, which shall be signed or sealed by the applicant.  
If a foreign party applies for enforcement, a Chinese version of the application for enforcement shall be submitted. If there are special provisions in the judicial assistance treaty concluded between the country where the party is located and China or to which both are parties, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the treaty.  
(2) A copy of the effective legal instrument.  
(3) Identity certificate of the applicant for enforcement. If an individual citizen applies, a resident identity card shall be presented; if a legal person applies, a copy of the business license of the legal person and the identity certificate of its legal representative shall be submitted; if any other organization applies, a copy of its business license and the identity certificate of its principal responsible person shall be submitted.  
(4) If an heir or successor applies for enforcement, a certificate document of inheritance or succession of rights shall be submitted.  
(5) Other documents or certificates that shall be submitted.  
21. To apply for enforcement of an arbitration award made by an arbitration institution, a contract containing an arbitration clause or an arbitration agreement shall be submitted to the People's Court.  
To apply for enforcement of an arbitration award made by a foreign arbitration institution, a Chinese version of the arbitration award that has been certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate abroad or notarized by a Chinese notary authority shall be submitted.  
22. An applicant for enforcement may entrust an agent to apply for enforcement on his/her behalf. If an agent is entrusted, a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal shall be submitted to the People's Court, specifying the matters entrusted and the scope of authority of the agent.  
If the authorized agent is to abandon or modify civil rights on behalf of the principal, or to conduct enforcement settlement on behalf of the principal, or to collect enforcement payments on behalf of the principal, a special authorization from the principal shall be required.  
23. To apply for compulsory enforcement by the People's Court, the fee for applying for enforcement shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of the Measures on Litigation Fees of the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、执行前的准备和对被执行人财产状况的查明  
24.人民法院决定受理执行案件后，应当在3日内向被执行人发出执行通知书，责令其在指定的期间内履行生效法律文书确定的义务，并承担[民事诉讼法第二百三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-232)规定的迟延履行期间的债务利息或迟延履行金。  
25.执行通知书的送达，适用民事诉讼法关于送达的规定。  
26.被执行人未按执行通知书指定的期间履行生效法律文书确定的义务的，应当及时采取执行措施。  
在执行通知书指定的期限内，被执行人转移、隐匿、变卖、毁损财产的，应当立即采取执行措施。  
人民法院采取执行措施，应当制作裁定书，送达被执行人。  
27.人民法院执行非诉讼生效法律文书，必要时可向制作生效法律文书的机构调取卷宗材料。  
28.申请执行人应当向人民法院提供其所了解的被执行人的财产状况或线索。被执行人必须如实向人民法院报告其财产状况。  
人民法院在执行中有权向被执行人、有关机关、社会团体、企业事业单位或公民个人，调查了解被执行人的财产状况，对调查所需的材料可以进行复制、抄录或拍照，但应当依法保密。  
29.为查明被执行人的财产状况和履行义务的能力，可以传唤被执行人或被执行人的法定代表人或负责人到人民法院接受询问。  
30.被执行人拒绝按人民法院的要求提供其有关财产状况的证据材料的，人民法院可以按照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)的规定进行搜查。  
31.人民法院依法搜查时，对被执行人可能存放隐匿的财物及有关证据材料的处所、箱柜等，经责令被执行人开启而拒不配合的，可以强制开启。

**Article 4** IV. Preparation Before Execution and Investigation of the Property Status of the Person Subject to Execution  
24. After deciding to accept an execution case, the People's Court shall, within three days, issue a notice of execution to the person subject to execution, ordering him to perform the obligations specified in the effective legal document within the designated period and bear the interest on delayed performance or the delayed performance fee as provided in Article 232 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
25. The service of the notice of execution shall be governed by the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law on service.  
26. If the person subject to execution fails to perform the obligations specified in the effective legal document within the period designated by the notice of execution, execution measures shall be taken promptly.  
If, within the period designated by the notice of execution, the person subject to execution transfers, conceals, sells, or damages property, execution measures shall be taken immediately.  
When the People's Court takes execution measures, it shall make a ruling and serve it on the person subject to execution.  
27. When executing an effective non-litigation legal document, the People's Court may, if necessary, request the institution that made the effective legal document to provide case file materials.  
28. The applicant for execution shall provide the People's Court with information or clues about the property status of the person subject to execution that he knows. The person subject to execution must truthfully report his property status to the People's Court.  
During execution, the People's Court has the authority to investigate and ascertain the property status of the person subject to execution from the person subject to execution, relevant authorities, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, or individual citizens, and may copy, transcribe, or photograph materials necessary for the investigation, but shall maintain confidentiality in accordance with law.  
29. To ascertain the property status of the person subject to execution and his ability to perform obligations, the person subject to execution or his legal representative or responsible person may be summoned to the People's Court for questioning.  
30. If the person subject to execution refuses to provide evidence materials concerning his property status as required by the People's Court, the People's Court may conduct a search in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
31. When conducting a search in accordance with law, the People's Court may forcibly open places, cabinets, etc., where the person subject to execution may have concealed property or relevant evidence materials, if the person subject to execution fails to cooperate when ordered to open them.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、金钱给付的执行  
32.查询、冻结、划拨被执行人在银行（含其分理处、营业所和储蓄所）、非银行金融机构、其他有储蓄业务的单位（以下简称金融机构）的存款，依照中国人民银行、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部《关于查询、冻结、扣划企业事业单位、机关、团体银行存款的通知》的规定办理。  
33.金融机构擅自解冻被人民法院冻结的款项，致冻结款项被转移的，人民法院有权责令其限期追回已转移的款项。在限期内未能追回的，应当裁定该金融机构在转移的款项范围内以自己的财产向申请执行人承担责任。  
34.被执行人为金融机构的，对其交存在人民银行的存款准备金和备付金不得冻结和扣划，但对其在本机构、其他金融机构的存款，及其在人民银行的其他存款可以冻结、划拨，并可对被执行人的其他财产采取执行措施，但不得查封其营业场所。  
35.作为被执行人的公民，其收入转为储蓄存款的，应当责令其交出存单。拒不交出的，人民法院应当作出提取其存款的裁定，向金融机构发出协助执行通知书，并附生效法律文书，由金融机构提取被执行人的存款交人民法院或存入人民法院指定的账户。  
36.被执行人在有关单位的收入尚未支取的，人民法院应当作出裁定，向该单位发出协助执行通知书，由其协助扣留或提取。  
37.有关单位收到人民法院协助执行被执行人收入的通知后，擅自向被执行人或其他人支付的，人民法院有权责令其限期追回；逾期未追回的，应当裁定其在支付的数额内向申请执行人承担责任。  
38.被执行人无金钱给付能力的，人民法院有权裁定对被执行人的其他财产采取查封、扣押措施。裁定书应送达被执行人。  
采取前款措施需有关单位协助的，应当向有关单位发出协助执行通知书，连同裁定书副本一并送达有关单位。  
39.查封、扣押财产的价值应当与被执行人履行债务的价值相当。  
40.人民法院对被执行人所有的其他人享有抵押权、质押权或留置权的财产，可以采取查封、扣押措施。财产拍卖、变卖后所得价款，应当在抵押权人、质押权人或留置权人优先受偿后，其余额部分用于清偿申请执行人的债权。  
41.对动产的查封，应当采取加贴封条的方式。不便加贴封条的，应当张贴公告。  
对有产权证照的动产或不动产的查封，应当向有关管理机关发出协助执行通知书，要求其不得办理查封财产的转移过户手续，同时可以责令被执行人将有关财产权证照交人民法院保管。必要时也可以采取加贴封条或张贴公告的方法查封。  
既未向有关管理机关发出协助执行通知书，也未采取加贴封条或张贴公告的办法查封的，不得对抗其他人民法院的查封。  
42.被查封的财产，可以指令由被执行人负责保管。如继续使用被查封的财产对其价值无重大影响，可以允许被执行人继续使用。因被执行人保管或使用的过错造成的损失，由被执行人承担。  
43.被扣押的财产，人民法院可以自行保管，也可以委托其他单位或个人保管。对扣押的财产，保管人不得使用。  
44.被执行人或其他人擅自处分已被查封、扣押、冻结财产的，人民法院有权责令责任人限期追回财产或承担相应的赔偿责任。  
45.被执行人的财产经查封、扣押后，在人民法院指定的期间内履行义务的，人民法院应当及时解除查封、扣押措施。  
46.人民法院对查封、扣押的被执行人财产进行变价时，应当委托拍卖机构进行拍卖。  
财产无法委托拍卖、不适于拍卖或当事人双方同意不需要拍卖的，人民法院可以交由有关单位变卖或自行组织变卖。  
47.人民法院对拍卖、变卖被执行人的财产，应当委托依法成立的资产评估机构进行价格评估。  
48.被执行人申请对人民法院查封的财产自行变卖的，人民法院可以准许，但应当监督其按照合理价格在指定的期限内进行，并控制变卖的价款。  
49.拍卖、变卖被执行人的财产成交后，必须即时钱物两清。  
委托拍卖、组织变卖被执行人财产所发生的实际费用，从所得价款中优先扣除。所得价款超出执行标的数额和执行费用的部分，应当退还被执行人。  
50.被执行人不履行生效法律文书确定的义务，人民法院有权裁定禁止被执行人转让其专利权、注册商标专用权、著作权（财产权部分）等知识产权。上述权利有登记主管部门的，应当同时向有关部门发出协助执行通知书，要求其不得办理财产权转移手续，必要时可以责令被执行人将产权或使用权证照交人民法院保存。  
对前款财产权，可以采取拍卖、变卖等执行措施。  
51.对被执行人从有关企业中应得的已到期的股息或红利等收益，人民法院有权裁定禁止被执行人提取和有关企业向被执行人支付，并要求有关企业直接向申请执行人支付。  
对被执行人预期从有关企业中应得的股息或红利等收益，人民法院可以采取冻结措施，禁止到期后被执行人提取和有关企业向被执行人支付。到期后人民法院可从有关企业中提取，并出具提取收据。  
52.对被执行人在其他股份有限公司中持有的股份凭证（股票），人民法院可以扣押，并强制被执行人按照公司法的有关规定转让，也可以直接采取拍卖、变卖的方式进行处分，或直接将股票抵偿给债权人，用于清偿被执行人的债务。  
53.对被执行人在有限责任公司、其他法人企业中的投资权益或股权，人民法院可以采取冻结措施。  
冻结投资权益或股权的，应当通知有关企业不得办理被冻结投资权益或股权的转移手续，不得向被执行人支付股息或红利。被冻结的投资权益或股权，被执行人不得自行转让。  
54.被执行人在其独资开办的法人企业中拥有的投资权益被冻结后，人民法院可以直接裁定予以转让，以转让所得清偿其对申请执行人的债务。  
对被执行人在有限责任公司中被冻结的投资权益或股权，人民法院可以依据[《中华人民共和国公司法》第三十五条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-35)、第三十六条的规定，征得全体股东过半数同意后，予以拍卖、变卖或以其他方式转让。不同意转让的股东，应当购买该转让的投资权益或股权，不购买的，视为同意转让，不影响执行。  
人民法院也可允许并监督被执行人自行转让其投资权益或股权，将转让所得收益用于清偿对申请执行人的债务。  
55.对被执行人在中外合资、合作经营企业中的投资权益或股权，在征得合资或合作他方的同意和对外经济贸易主管机关的批准后，可以对冻结的投资权益或股权予以转让。  
如果被执行人除在中外合资、合作企业中的股权以外别无其他财产可供执行，其他股东又不同意转让的，可以直接强制转让被执行人的股权，但应当保护合资他方的优先购买权。  
56.有关企业收到人民法院发出的协助冻结通知后，擅自向被执行人支付股息或红利，或擅自为被执行人办理已冻结股权的转移手续，造成已转移的财产无法追回的，应当在所支付的股息或红利或转移的股权价值范围内向申请执行人承担责任。

**Article 5** V. Execution of Monetary Payment  
32. For inquiry, freezing, or transfer of deposits of the person subject to execution in banks (including their sub-branches, business offices, and savings offices), non-bank financial institutions, and other entities with savings business (hereinafter collectively referred to as financial institutions), the provisions of the Notice of the People's Bank of China, the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security on Inquiry, Freezing, and Transfer of Deposits of Enterprises, Institutions, Authorities, and Organizations shall apply.  
33. If a financial institution unlawfully releases frozen funds of the People's Court, causing the frozen funds to be transferred, the People's Court shall have the authority to order it to recover the transferred funds within a time limit. If it fails to recover within the time limit, the People's Court shall rule that the financial institution shall, to the extent of the transferred funds, bear liability to the applicant for execution with its own property.  
34. If the person subject to execution is a financial institution, its deposit reserves and provisions deposited with the People's Bank shall not be frozen or transferred, but its deposits in its own institution, other financial institutions, and other deposits with the People's Bank may be frozen or transferred, and execution measures may be taken against other property of the person subject to execution, except that its business premises shall not be sealed up.  
35. If a citizen who is the person subject to execution has turned his income into savings deposits, he shall be ordered to surrender the deposit certificate. If he refuses to surrender it, the People's Court shall make a ruling to withdraw the deposit, issue a notice of assistance in execution to the financial institution, and attach the effective legal document. The financial institution shall withdraw the deposit of the person subject to execution and deliver it to the People's Court or deposit it into an account designated by the People's Court.  
36. If the income of the person subject to execution from a relevant entity has not yet been withdrawn, the People's Court shall make a ruling and issue a notice of assistance in execution to that entity, which shall assist in deducting or withdrawing the income.  
37. If, after receiving a notice from the People's Court to assist in executing the income of the person subject to execution, the relevant entity makes payment to the person subject to execution or to others without authorization, the People's Court shall have the authority to order it to recover the payment within a time limit; if it fails to recover within the time limit, the People's Court shall rule that the entity shall bear liability to the applicant for execution to the extent of the amount paid.  
38. If the person subject to execution has no capacity to make monetary payment, the People's Court shall have the authority to rule to take measures of sealing up or distraining other property of the person subject to execution. The ruling shall be served on the person subject to execution.  
If the measures in the preceding paragraph require the assistance of a relevant entity, a notice of assistance in execution shall be issued to that entity, together with a copy of the ruling, which shall be served on the relevant entity.  
39. The value of the property sealed up or distrained shall be commensurate with the value of the debt to be performed by the person subject to execution.  
40. The People's Court may take measures to seal up or distrain property owned by the person subject to execution over which others have a mortgage, pledge, or lien. The proceeds from the auction or sale of the property shall, after priority satisfaction of the mortgagee, pledgee, or lienholder, be used for the remaining part to satisfy the creditor's rights of the applicant for execution.  
41. Sealing up of movable property shall be done by affixing seals. If affixing seals is inconvenient, a public notice shall be posted.  
For sealing up of movable property or immovable property with title certificates, a notice of assistance in execution shall be issued to the relevant administrative authority, requesting that it not handle the transfer or registration of the sealed-up property, and at the same time the person subject to execution may be ordered to deliver the relevant property title certificates to the People's Court for custody. If necessary, sealing may also be done by affixing seals or posting a public notice.  
If neither a notice of assistance in execution has been issued to the relevant administrative authority nor sealing has been done by affixing seals or posting a public notice, such sealing shall not be effective against sealing by other People's Courts.  
42. The sealed-up property may be ordered to be kept by the person subject to execution. If continued use of the sealed-up property does not significantly affect its value, the person subject to execution may be allowed to continue using it. Losses caused by the fault of the person subject to execution in keeping or using the property shall be borne by the person subject to execution.  
43. The distrained property may be kept by the People's Court itself, or may be entrusted to another entity or individual for custody. The custodian shall not use the distrained property.  
44. If the person subject to execution or another person disposes of property that has been sealed up, distrained, or frozen without authorization, the People's Court shall have the authority to order the responsible person to recover the property within a time limit or bear corresponding compensation liability.  
45. If, after the property of the person subject to execution has been sealed up or distrained, the obligation is performed within the period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court shall promptly lift the sealing or distraint measures.  
46. When converting the sealed-up or distrained property of the person subject to execution into value, the People's Court shall entrust an auction institution to conduct an auction.  
If the property cannot be entrusted for auction, is not suitable for auction, or both parties agree that auction is unnecessary, the People's Court may hand it over to a relevant entity for sale or organize the sale itself.  
47. The People's Court shall, for the auction or sale of the property of the person subject to execution, entrust a property appraisal institution established in accordance with law to conduct a price assessment.  
48. If the person subject to execution applies to sell the property sealed up by the People's Court on his own, the People's Court may permit it, but shall supervise the sale at a reasonable price within the designated period and control the proceeds of the sale.  
49. After the auction or sale of the property of the person subject to execution is concluded, the money and the goods must be settled immediately.  
The actual expenses incurred in entrusting auction or organizing sale of the property of the person subject to execution shall be deducted from the proceeds in priority. The portion of the proceeds exceeding the amount of the subject matter of execution and execution costs shall be returned to the person subject to execution.  
50. If the person subject to execution fails to perform the obligations specified in the effective legal document, the People's Court shall have the authority to rule to prohibit the person subject to execution from transferring his intellectual property rights such as patent rights, exclusive rights to registered trademarks, copyrights (the property rights part), etc. If such rights have a registration authority, a notice of assistance in execution shall be issued to the relevant authority at the same time, requesting that it not handle the transfer procedures for the property rights, and if necessary, the person subject to execution may be ordered to deliver the property or use rights certificates to the People's Court for safekeeping.  
Execution measures such as auction or sale may be taken against the property rights mentioned in the preceding paragraph.  
51. For income such as dividends or bonuses that have become due and payable to the person subject to execution from relevant enterprises, the People's Court shall have the authority to rule to prohibit the person subject to execution from withdrawing them and the relevant enterprise from paying them to the person subject to execution, and to require the relevant enterprise to pay them directly to the applicant for execution.  
For anticipated income such as dividends or bonuses that the person subject to execution expects to receive from relevant enterprises, the People's Court may take freezing measures, prohibiting the person subject to execution from withdrawing them after they become due and the relevant enterprise from paying them to the person subject to execution. After they become due, the People's Court may withdraw them from the relevant enterprise and issue a withdrawal receipt.  
52. The People's Court may distrain the share certificates (stocks) held by the person subject to execution in other joint-stock limited companies, and force the person subject to execution to transfer them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Company Law, or may directly dispose of them by auction or sale, or directly offset the stocks against the creditor's rights to satisfy the debt of the person subject to execution.  
53. The People's Court may take freezing measures against the investment interests or equity of the person subject to execution in a limited liability company or other legal person enterprise.  
When freezing investment interests or equity, the relevant enterprise shall be notified not to handle the transfer procedures for the frozen investment interests or equity, and not to pay dividends or bonuses to the person subject to execution. The person subject to execution shall not transfer the frozen investment interests or equity on his own.  
54. After the investment interests of the person subject to execution in a wholly-owned legal person enterprise have been frozen, the People's Court may directly rule to transfer them, and use the proceeds from the transfer to satisfy the debt owed to the applicant for execution.  
For the frozen investment interests or equity of the person subject to execution in a limited liability company, the People's Court may, in accordance with Articles 35 and 36 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, after obtaining the consent of more than half of all shareholders, auction, sell, or transfer them by other means. Shareholders who do not consent to the transfer shall purchase the transferred investment interests or equity; if they do not purchase, they shall be deemed to have consented to the transfer, without affecting the execution.  
The People's Court may also permit and supervise the person subject to execution to transfer his investment interests or equity on his own, using the proceeds from the transfer to satisfy the debt owed to the applicant for execution.  
55. For the investment interests or equity of the person subject to execution in a Chinese-foreign equity joint venture or contractual joint venture, after obtaining the consent of the other party to the joint venture or cooperative enterprise and the approval of the competent authority for foreign economic relations and trade, the frozen investment interests or equity may be transferred.  
If the person subject to execution has no other property available for execution other than the equity in the Chinese-foreign equity joint venture or contractual joint venture, and the other shareholders do not consent to the transfer, the equity of the person subject to execution may be directly and compulsorily transferred, but the preemptive right of the other party to the joint venture shall be protected.  
56. If, after receiving a notice of assistance in freezing from the People's Court, a relevant enterprise pays dividends or bonuses to the person subject to execution without authorization, or handles the transfer procedures for the frozen equity for the person subject to execution without authorization, causing the property already transferred to be irrecoverable, it shall bear liability to the applicant for execution to the extent of the dividends or bonuses paid or the value of the equity transferred.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、交付财产和完成行为的执行  
57.生效法律文书确定被执行人交付特定标的物的，应当执行原物。原物被隐匿或非法转移的，人民法院有权责令其交出。原物确已变质、损坏或灭失的，应当裁定折价赔偿或按标的物的价值强制执行被执行人的其他财产。  
58.有关单位或公民持有法律文书指定交付的财物或票证，在接到人民法院协助执行通知书或通知书后，协同被执行人转移财物或票证的，人民法院有权责令其限期追回；逾期未追回的，应当裁定其承担赔偿责任。  
59.被执行人的财产经拍卖、变卖或裁定以物抵债后，需从现占有人处交付给买受人或申请执行人的，适用[民事诉讼法第二百二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-228)、第二百二十九条和本规定57条、58条的规定。  
60.被执行人拒不履行生效法律文书中指定的行为的，人民法院可以强制其履行。  
对于可以替代履行的行为，可以委托有关单位或他人完成，因完成上述行为发生的费用由被执行人承担。  
对于只能由被执行人完成的行为，经教育，被执行人仍拒不履行的，人民法院应当按照妨害执行行为的有关规定处理。

**Article 6** VI. Execution of Delivery of Property and Completion of Acts  
57. If an effective legal document determines that the person subject to execution shall deliver a specific subject matter, the original item shall be executed. If the original item has been concealed or illegally transferred, the People's Court shall have the authority to order its surrender. If the original item has indeed deteriorated, been damaged, or lost, a ruling shall be made for compensation at a converted price or for compulsory execution against other property of the person subject to execution according to the value of the subject matter.  
58. If a relevant entity or citizen holds property or negotiable instruments designated for delivery by the legal document, and after receiving a notice of assistance in execution or a notice from the People's Court, cooperates with the person subject to execution in transferring the property or negotiable instruments, the People's Court shall have the authority to order the recovery within a time limit; if recovery is not made within the time limit, a ruling shall be made that it bear compensation liability.  
59. If, after the property of the person subject to execution has been auctioned, sold, or offset by a ruling to satisfy the debt with property, it needs to be delivered from the current possessor to the buyer or the applicant for execution, the provisions of Articles 228 and 229 of the Civil Procedure Law and Articles 57 and 58 of these Provisions shall apply.  
60. If the person subject to execution refuses to perform the act specified in the effective legal document, the People's Court may compel performance.  
For acts that can be performed by substitution, an entrustment may be made to a relevant entity or another person to complete them, and the expenses incurred in completing the above acts shall be borne by the person subject to execution.  
For acts that can only be performed by the person subject to execution, if after education, the person subject to execution still refuses to perform, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions on acts obstructing execution.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、被执行人到期债权的执行  
61.被执行人不能清偿债务，但对本案以外的第三人享有到期债权的，人民法院可以依申请执行人或被执行人的申请，向第三人发出履行到期债务的通知（以下简称履行通知）。履行通知必须直接送达第三人。  
履行通知应当包含下列内容：  
（1）第三人直接向申请执行人履行其对被执行人所负的债务，不得向被执行人清偿；  
（2）第三人应当在收到履行通知后的15日内向申请执行人履行债务；  
（3）第三人对履行到期债权有异议的，应当在收到履行通知后的15日内向执行法院提出；  
（4）第三人违背上述义务的法律后果。  
62.第三人对履行通知的异议一般应当以书面形式提出，口头提出的，执行人员应记入笔录，并由第三人签字或盖章。  
63.第三人在履行通知指定的期间内提出异议的，人民法院不得对第三人强制执行，对提出的异议不进行审查。  
64.第三人提出自己无履行能力或其与申请执行人无直接法律关系，不属于本规定所指的异议。  
第三人对债务部分承认、部分有异议的，可以对其承认的部分强制执行。  
65.第三人在履行通知指定的期限内没有提出异议，而又不履行的，执行法院有权裁定对其强制执行。此裁定同时送达第三人和被执行人。  
66.被执行人收到人民法院履行通知后，放弃其对第三人的债权或延缓第三人履行期限的行为无效，人民法院仍可在第三人无异议又不履行的情况下予以强制执行。  
67.第三人收到人民法院要求其履行到期债务的通知后，擅自向被执行人履行，造成已向被执行人履行的财产不能追回的，除在已履行的财产范围内与被执行人承担连带清偿责任外，可以追究其妨害执行的责任。  
68.在对第三人作出强制执行裁定后，第三人确无财产可供执行的，不得就第三人对他人享有的到期债权强制执行。  
69.第三人按照人民法院履行通知向申请执行人履行了债务或已被强制执行后，人民法院应当出具有关证明。

**Article 7** VII. Execution of Due Creditor's Rights of the Person Subject to Execution  
61. If the person subject to execution is unable to satisfy the debt but holds due creditor's rights against a third party outside the case, the People's Court may, upon the application of the applicant for execution or the person subject to execution, issue a notice to the third party to perform the due debt (hereinafter referred to as a performance notice). The performance notice must be directly served on the third party.  
The performance notice shall contain the following contents:  
(1) The third party shall directly perform the debt owed by him to the person subject to execution to the applicant for execution, and shall not satisfy the person subject to execution;  
(2) The third party shall perform the debt to the applicant for execution within 15 days after receiving the performance notice;  
(3) If the third party has objections to the performance of the due creditor's rights, he shall raise them with the executing court within 15 days after receiving the performance notice;  
(4) The legal consequences of the third party's breach of the above obligations.  
62. Objections of the third party to the performance notice shall generally be made in writing; if made orally, the execution officer shall record them in the record, which shall be signed or sealed by the third party.  
63. If the third party raises objections within the period designated by the performance notice, the People's Court shall not enforce against the third party compulsorily, and shall not review the objections raised.  
64. The third party's claim that he has no ability to perform or that he has no direct legal relationship with the applicant for execution does not constitute an objection as referred to in these Provisions.  
If the third party partially acknowledges the debt and partially objects, compulsory execution may be carried out against the acknowledged part.  
65. If the third party neither raises any objection within the period designated by the performance notice nor performs, the executing court shall have the authority to rule to enforce against him compulsorily. This ruling shall be served on both the third party and the person subject to execution at the same time.  
66. After the person subject to execution receives the performance notice from the People's Court, any act of waiving the creditor's right against the third party or delaying the performance period of the third party shall be invalid. The People's Court may still enforce compulsorily if the third party has no objection and fails to perform.  
67. If, after receiving the notice from the People's Court requiring him to perform the due debt, the third party performs to the person subject to execution without authorization, causing the property already performed to the person subject to execution to be irrecoverable, in addition to bearing joint and several liability for satisfaction with the person subject to execution to the extent of the property already performed, he may be held liable for obstructing execution.  
68. After a ruling for compulsory execution is made against the third party, if the third party indeed has no property available for execution, compulsory execution shall not be carried out against the due creditor's rights that the third party holds against others.  
69. After the third party performs the debt to the applicant for execution in accordance with the performance notice of the People's Court or has been compulsorily executed, the People's Court shall issue relevant certification.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、对案外人异议的处理  
70.案外人对执行标的主张权利的，可以向执行法院提出异议。  
案外人异议一般应当以书面形式提出，并提供相应的证据。以书面形式提出确有困难的，可以允许以口头形式提出。  
71.对案外人提出的异议，执行法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百零八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-208)的规定进行审查。  
审查期间可以对财产采取查封、扣押、冻结等保全措施，但不得进行处分。正在实施的处分措施应当停止。  
经审查认为案外人的异议理由不成立的，裁定驳回其异议，继续执行。  
72.案外人提出异议的执行标的物是法律文书指定交付的特定物，经审查认为案外人的异议成立的，报经院长批准，裁定对生效法律文书中该项内容中止执行。  
73.执行标的物不属生效法律文书指定交付的特定物，经审查认为案外人的异议成立的，报经院长批准，停止对该标的物的执行。已经采取的执行措施应当裁定立即解除或撤销，并将该标的物交还案外人。  
74.对案外人提出的异议一时难以确定是否成立，案外人已提供确实有效的担保的，可以解除查封、扣押措施。申请执行人提供确实有效的担保的，可以继续执行。因提供担保而解除查封扣押或继续执行有错误，给对方造成损失的，应裁定以担保的财产予以赔偿。  
75.执行上级人民法院的法律文书遇有本规定第72条规定的情形的，或执行的财产是上级人民法院裁定保全的财产时遇有本规定第73条、74条规定的情形的，需报经上级人民法院批准。

**Article 8** VIII. Handling of Objections by a Third Party Not Involved in the Case  
70. If a third party not involved in the case claims rights over the subject matter of execution, it may raise an objection with the executing court.  
Objections by a third party not involved in the case shall generally be made in writing and shall be accompanied by corresponding evidence. If there is genuine difficulty in making a written objection, an oral objection may be permitted.  
71. The executing court shall review objections raised by a third party not involved in the case in accordance with Article 208 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
During the review period, preservation measures such as sealing up, distraint, or freezing may be taken against the property, but no disposition shall be made. Disposition measures being implemented shall be suspended.  
If, after review, it is determined that the objections of the third party not involved in the case are unfounded, a ruling shall be made to reject the objections and execution shall continue.  
72. If the subject matter of execution objected to by the third party not involved in the case is a specific item designated for delivery by the legal document, and after review it is determined that the objections of the third party are well-founded, the matter shall be reported to the president for approval, and a ruling shall be made to suspend execution of that part of the effective legal document.  
73. If the subject matter of execution is not a specific item designated for delivery by the effective legal document, and after review it is determined that the objections of the third party not involved in the case are well-founded, the matter shall be reported to the president for approval, and execution of that subject matter shall be stopped. Execution measures already taken shall be immediately lifted or revoked by ruling, and the subject matter shall be returned to the third party not involved in the case.  
74. If it is temporarily difficult to determine whether the objections raised by a third party not involved in the case are well-founded, and the third party has provided reliable and effective security, the measures of sealing up and distraint may be lifted. If the applicant for execution provides reliable and effective security, execution may continue. If the lifting of sealing up and distraint or continued execution due to the provision of security was erroneous, causing losses to the other party, a ruling shall be made to compensate with the secured property.  
75. When executing a legal document of a higher People's Court, if the circumstances provided in Article 72 of these Provisions arise, or when executing property that was preserved by a ruling of a higher People's Court, if the circumstances provided in Articles 73 and 74 of these Provisions arise, approval from the higher People's Court must be obtained.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、被执行主体的变更和追加  
76.被执行人为无法人资格的私营独资企业，无能力履行法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院可以裁定执行该独资企业业主的其他财产。  
77.被执行人为个人合伙组织或合伙型联营企业，无能力履行生效法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院可以裁定追加该合伙组织的合伙人或参加该联营企业的法人为被执行人。  
78.被执行人为企业法人的分支机构不能清偿债务时，可以裁定企业法人为被执行人。企业法人直接经营管理的财产仍不能清偿债务的，人民法院可以裁定执行该企业法人其他分支机构的财产。  
若必须执行已被承包或租赁的企业法人分支机构的财产时，对承包人或承租人投入及应得的收益应依法保护。  
79.被执行人按法定程序分立为两个或多个具有法人资格的企业，分立后存续的企业按照分立协议确定的比例承担债务；不符合法定程序分立的，裁定由分立后存续的企业按照其从被执行企业分得的资产占原企业总资产的比例对申请执行人承担责任。  
80.被执行人无财产清偿债务，如果其开办单位对其开办时投入的注册资金不实或抽逃注册资金，可以裁定变更或追加其开办单位为被执行人，在注册资金不实或抽逃注册资金的范围内，对申请执行人承担责任。  
81.被执行人被撤销、注销或歇业后，上级主管部门或开办单位无偿接受被执行人的财产，致使被执行人无遗留财产清偿债务或遗留财产不足清偿的，可以裁定由上级主管部门或开办单位在所接受的财产范围内承担责任。  
82.被执行人的开办单位已经在注册资金范围内或接受财产的范围内向其他债权人承担了全部责任的，人民法院不得裁定开办单位重复承担责任。  
83.依照[民事诉讼法第二百一十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-213)、最高人民法院关于适用民事诉讼法若干问题的意见第271条至第274条及本规定裁定变更或追加被执行主体的，由执行法院的执行机构办理。

**Article 9** IX. Modification and Addition of Subjects of Execution  
76. If the person subject to execution is a private sole proprietorship enterprise without legal person capacity and is unable to perform the obligations specified in the legal document, the People's Court may rule to execute other property of the owner of the sole proprietorship enterprise.  
77. If the person subject to execution is a individual partnership organization or a partnership-type joint venture enterprise and is unable to perform the obligations specified in the effective legal document, the People's Court may rule to add the partners of the partnership organization or the legal persons participating in the joint venture enterprise as persons subject to execution.  
78. If the person subject to execution is a branch of an enterprise legal person and is unable to satisfy the debt, a ruling may be made to designate the enterprise legal person as the person subject to execution. If the property directly managed and operated by the enterprise legal person is still insufficient to satisfy the debt, the People's Court may rule to execute the property of other branches of the enterprise legal person.  
If it is necessary to execute the property of a branch of an enterprise legal person that has been contracted or leased, the investments and due income of the contractor or lessee shall be protected in accordance with law.  
79. If the person subject to execution is divided into two or more enterprises with legal person status in accordance with legal procedures, the enterprises surviving after the division shall bear the debt according to the proportion determined by the division agreement; if the division does not comply with legal procedures, a ruling shall be made that the enterprises surviving after the division shall bear liability to the applicant for execution according to the proportion of assets they received from the executed enterprise to the total assets of the original enterprise.  
80. If the person subject to execution has no property to satisfy the debt, and if the sponsoring unit made false registered capital investment or withdrew registered capital upon establishment, a ruling may be made to modify or add the sponsoring unit as the person subject to execution, which shall bear liability to the applicant for execution to the extent of the false registered capital or the withdrawn registered capital.  
81. If, after the person subject to execution is abolished, cancelled, or ceases business, the higher competent authority or sponsoring unit accepts the property of the person subject to execution without compensation, causing the person subject to execution to have no remaining property to satisfy the debt or insufficient remaining property to satisfy the debt, a ruling may be made that the higher competent authority or sponsoring unit shall bear liability within the scope of the property accepted.  
82. If the sponsoring unit of the person subject to execution has already borne full liability to other creditors within the scope of registered capital or within the scope of property accepted, the People's Court shall not rule that the sponsoring unit bears liability repeatedly.  
83. Where a ruling to modify or add the subject of execution is made in accordance with Article 213 of the Civil Procedure Law, Articles 271 to 274 of the Opinions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law, and these Provisions, the execution organ of the executing court shall handle it.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、执行担保和执行和解  
84.被执行人或其担保人以财产向人民法院提供执行担保的，应当依据《中华人民共和国担保法》的有关规定，按照担保物的种类、性质，将担保物移交执行法院，或依法到有关机关办理登记手续。  
85.人民法院在审理案件期间，保证人为被执行人提供保证，人民法院据此未对被执行人的财产采取保全措施或解除保全措施的，案件审结后如果被执行人无财产可供执行或其财产不足清偿债务时，即使生效法律文书中未确定保证人承担责任，人民法院有权裁定执行保证人在保证责任范围内的财产。  
86.在执行中，双方当事人可以自愿达成和解协议，变更生效法律文书确定的履行义务主体、标的物及其数额、履行期限和履行方式。  
和解协议一般应当采取书面形式。执行人员应将和解协议副本附卷。无书面协议的，执行人员应将和解协议的内容记入笔录，并由双方当事人签名或盖章。  
87.当事人之间达成的和解协议合法有效并已履行完毕的，人民法院作执行结案处理。

**Article 10** X. Execution Security and Execution Settlement  
84. If the person subject to execution or his guarantor provides property to the People's Court as security for execution, the secured property shall be transferred to the executing court in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Security Law of the People's Republic of China, based on the type and nature of the secured property, or registration procedures shall be completed with the relevant authority in accordance with law.  
85. If, during the trial of a case, a surety provides a guarantee for the person subject to execution, and the People's Court accordingly refrains from taking preservation measures against the property of the person subject to execution or lifts preservation measures, after the case is concluded, if the person subject to execution has no property available for execution or his property is insufficient to satisfy the debt, even if the effective legal document does not specify that the surety shall bear liability, the People's Court shall have the authority to rule to execute the property of the surety within the scope of the suretyship liability.  
86. During execution, the two parties may voluntarily reach a settlement agreement to modify the subject of the performance obligation, the subject matter and its amount, the performance period, and the method of performance as specified in the effective legal document.  
A settlement agreement shall generally be in writing. The execution officer shall attach a copy of the settlement agreement to the case file. If there is no written agreement, the execution officer shall record the contents of the settlement agreement in the record, which shall be signed or sealed by both parties.  
87. If a settlement agreement reached between the parties is lawful, valid, and fully performed, the People's Court shall handle the matter as concluded execution.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、多个债权人对一个债务人申请执行和参与分配  
88.多份生效法律文书确定金钱给付内容的多个债权人分别对同一被执行人申请执行，各债权人对执行标的物均无担保物权的，按照执行法院采取执行措施的先后顺序受偿。  
多个债权人的债权种类不同的，基于所有权和担保物权而享有的债权，优先于金钱债权受偿。有多个担保物权的，按照各担保物权成立的先后顺序清偿。  
一份生效法律文书确定金钱给付内容的多个债权人对同一被执行人申请执行，执行的财产不足清偿全部债务的，各债权人对执行标的物均无担保物权的，按照各债权比例受偿。  
89.被执行人为企业法人，其财产不足清偿全部债务的，可告知当事人依法申请被执行人破产。  
90.被执行人为公民或其他组织，其全部或主要财产已被一个人民法院因执行确定金钱给付的生效法律文书而查封、扣押或冻结，无其他财产可供执行或其他财产不足清偿全部债务的，在被执行人的财产被执行完毕前，对该被执行人已经取得金钱债权执行依据的其他债权人可以申请对该被执行人的财产参与分配。  
91.对参与被执行人财产的具体分配，应当由首先查封、扣押或冻结的法院主持进行。  
首先查封、扣押、冻结的法院所采取的执行措施如系为执行财产保全裁定，具体分配应当在该院案件审理终结后进行。  
92.债权人申请参与分配的，应当向其原申请执行法院提交参与分配申请书，写明参与分配的理由，并附有执行依据。该执行法院应将参与分配申请书转交给主持分配的法院，并说明执行情况。  
93.对人民法院查封、扣押或冻结的财产有优先权、担保物权的债权人，可以申请参加参与分配程序，主张优先受偿权。  
94.参与分配案件中可供执行的财产，在对享有优先权、担保权的债权人依照法律规定的顺序优先受偿后，按照各个案件债权额的比例进行分配。  
95.被执行人的财产被分配给各债权人后，被执行人对其剩余债务应当继续清偿。债权人发现被执行人有其他财产的，人民法院可以根据债权人的申请继续依法执行。  
96.被执行人为企业法人，未经清理或清算而撤销、注销或歇业，其财产不足清偿全部债务的，应当参照本规定第90条至95条的规定，对各债权人的债权按比例清偿。

**Article 11** XI. Multiple Creditors Applying for Execution Against One Debtor and Participation in Distribution  
88. If multiple creditors with multiple effective legal documents specifying monetary payment obligations respectively apply for execution against the same person subject to execution, and none of the creditors has a security interest over the subject matter of execution, they shall be satisfied in the order of the timing of the execution measures taken by the executing court.  
If the types of creditor's rights of multiple creditors are different, creditor's rights based on ownership or security interests shall be satisfied in priority over monetary creditor's rights. If there are multiple security interests, they shall be satisfied in the order of the establishment of each security interest.  
If multiple creditors under a single effective legal document specifying monetary payment obligations apply for execution against the same person subject to execution, and the property executed is insufficient to satisfy all debts, and none of the creditors has a security interest over the subject matter of execution, they shall be satisfied in proportion to each creditor's claim.  
89. If the person subject to execution is an enterprise legal person and its property is insufficient to satisfy all debts, the parties may be informed to apply for bankruptcy of the person subject to execution in accordance with law.  
90. If the person subject to execution is a citizen or other organization, and all or most of its property has been sealed up, distrained, or frozen by a People's Court due to execution of an effective legal document specifying monetary payment, and there is no other property available for execution or other property is insufficient to satisfy all debts, other creditors who have obtained a basis for execution of monetary creditor's rights against the person subject to execution may apply to participate in the distribution of the property of the person subject to execution before the execution of the property of the person subject to execution is completed.  
91. The specific distribution of property of the person subject to execution shall be conducted by the court that first carried out the sealing up, distraint, or freezing.  
If the execution measures taken by the court that first sealed up, distrained, or frozen were for the purpose of executing a property preservation ruling, the specific distribution shall be carried out after the trial of the case in that court is concluded.  
92. A creditor applying to participate in distribution shall submit a written application for participation in distribution to the court where it originally applied for execution, stating the reasons for participation in distribution and attaching the basis for execution. That executing court shall forward the application for participation in distribution to the court presiding over the distribution and explain the execution status.  
93. A creditor with a priority right or security interest over property sealed up, distrained, or frozen by the People's Court may apply to participate in the distribution proceedings and claim priority satisfaction.  
94. After creditors with priority rights or security interests are satisfied in priority in accordance with the order provided by law, the property available for execution in the participation in distribution case shall be distributed in proportion to the creditor's claim amount in each case.  
95. After the property of the person subject to execution is distributed to each creditor, the person subject to execution shall continue to satisfy the remaining debt. If a creditor discovers that the person subject to execution has other property, the People's Court may, upon the creditor's application, continue execution in accordance with law.  
96. If the person subject to execution is an enterprise legal person that has been abolished, cancelled, or ceased business without undergoing liquidation or settlement, and its property is insufficient to satisfy all debts, the creditor's claims of each creditor shall be satisfied proportionally by reference to the provisions of Articles 90 to 95 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、对妨害执行行为的强制措施的适用  
97.对必须到人民法院接受询问的被执行人或被执行人的法定代表人或负责人，经两次传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到场的，人民法院可以对其进行拘传。  
98.对被拘传人的调查询问不得超过24小时，调查询问后不得限制被拘传人的人身自由。  
99.在本辖区以外采取拘传措施时，应当将被拘传人拘传到当地法院，当地法院应予以协助。  
100.被执行人或其他人有下列拒不履行生效法律文书或者妨害执行行为之一的，人民法院可以依照[民事诉讼法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的规定处理：  
（1）隐藏、转移、变卖、毁损向人民法院提供执行担保的财产的；  
（2）案外人与被执行人恶意串通转移被执行人财产的；  
（3）故意撕毁人民法院执行公告、封条的；  
（4）伪造、隐藏、毁灭有关被执行人履行能力的重要证据，妨碍人民法院查明被执行人财产状况的；  
（5）指使、贿买、胁迫他人对被执行人的财产状况和履行义务的能力问题作伪证的；  
（6）妨碍人民法院依法搜查的；  
（7）以暴力、威胁或其他方法妨碍或抗拒执行的；  
（8）哄闹、冲击执行现场的；  
（9）对人民法院执行人员或协助执行人员进行侮辱、诽谤、诬陷、围攻、威胁、殴打或者打击报复的；  
（10）毁损、抢夺执行案件材料、执行公务车辆、其他执行器械、执行人员服装和执行公务证件的。  
101.在执行过程中遇有被执行人或其他人拒不履行生效法律文书或者妨害执行情节严重，需要追究刑事责任的，应将有关材料移交有关机关处理。

**Article 12** XII. Application of Compulsory Measures Against Acts Obstructing Execution  
97. If the person subject to execution or his legal representative or responsible person who must appear before the People's Court for questioning, after being summoned twice by summons, refuses to appear without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may take him into custody.  
98. The investigation and questioning of a person taken into custody shall not exceed 24 hours, and after the investigation and questioning, the personal freedom of the person taken into custody shall not be restricted.  
99. When taking custody measures outside the jurisdiction, the person taken into custody shall be taken to the local court, which shall provide assistance.  
100. If the person subject to execution or another person commits any of the following acts of refusing to perform an effective legal document or obstructing execution, the People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) Hiding, transferring, selling, or damaging property provided as security for execution to the People's Court;  
(2) A third party not involved in the case colludes maliciously with the person subject to execution to transfer the property of the person subject to execution;  
(3) Intentionally tearing up the execution announcement or seals of the People's Court;  
(4) Forging, hiding, or destroying important evidence concerning the performance ability of the person subject to execution, hindering the People's Court from ascertaining the property status of the person subject to execution;  
(5) Instigating, bribing, or coercing others to give false testimony on issues concerning the property status and ability to perform obligations of the person subject to execution;  
(6) Obstructing the People's Court from conducting a lawful search;  
(7) Obstructing or resisting execution by violence, threats, or other means;  
(8) Making a disturbance or storming the execution site;  
(9) Insulting, slandering, framing, surrounding, threatening, beating, or retaliating against execution personnel of the People's Court or personnel assisting in execution;  
(10) Damaging or seizing execution case materials, execution official vehicles, other execution instruments, execution personnel uniforms, or execution duty cards.  
101. If, during the execution process, the person subject to execution or another person refuses to perform an effective legal document or obstructs execution to a serious extent, and criminal liability needs to be pursued, the relevant materials shall be transferred to the relevant authority for handling.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、执行的中止、终结、结案和执行回转  
102.有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-234)第一款第五项的规定裁定中止执行：  
（1）人民法院已受理以被执行人为债务人的破产申请的；  
（2）被执行人确无财产可供执行的；  
（3）执行的标的物是其他法院或仲裁机构正在审理的案件争议标的物，需要等待该案件审理完毕确定权属的；  
（4）一方当事人申请执行仲裁裁决，另一方当事人申请撤销仲裁裁决的；  
（5）仲裁裁决的被申请执行人依据[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)第二款的规定向人民法院提出不予执行请求，并提供适当担保的。  
103.按照审判监督程序提审或再审的案件，执行机构根据上级法院或本院作出的中止执行裁定书中止执行。  
104.中止执行的情形消失后，执行法院可以根据当事人的申请或依职权恢复执行。恢复执行应当书面通知当事人。  
105.在执行中，被执行人被人民法院裁定宣告破产的，执行法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-235)第六项的规定，裁定终结执行。  
106.中止执行和终结执行的裁定书应当写明中止或终结执行的理由和法律依据。  
107.人民法院执行生效法律文书，一般应当在立案之日起6个月内执行结案，但中止执行的期间应当扣除。确有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。  
108.执行结案的方式为：  
（1）生效法律文书确定的内容全部执行完毕；  
（2）裁定终结执行；  
（3）裁定不予执行；  
（4）当事人之间达成执行和解协议并已履行完毕。  
109.在执行中或执行完毕后，据以执行的法律文书被人民法院或其他有关机关撤销或变更的，原执行机构应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-214)的规定，依当事人申请或依职权，按照新的生效法律文书，作出执行回转的裁定，责令原申请执行人返还已取得的财产及其孳息。拒不返还的，强制执行。  
执行回转应重新立案，适用执行程序的有关规定。  
110.执行回转时，已执行的标的物系特定物的，应当退还原物。不能退还原物的，可以折价抵偿。

**Article 13** XIII. Suspension, Termination, Conclusion of Execution, and Recovery of Execution  
102. A People's Court shall rule to suspend execution in accordance with Article 234, paragraph 1, item (5) of the Civil Procedure Law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The People's Court has accepted a bankruptcy application with the person subject to execution as the debtor;  
(2) The person subject to execution indeed has no property available for execution;  
(3) The subject matter of execution is the disputed subject matter in a case being tried by another court or arbitration institution, and it is necessary to wait for the conclusion of that case to determine ownership;  
(4) One party applies for execution of an arbitration award, while the other party applies for revocation of the arbitration award;  
(5) The person against whom execution of the arbitration award is sought submits a request for refusal of execution to the People's Court in accordance with Article 217, paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law and provides appropriate security.  
103. For cases brought up for trial or retried under the trial supervision procedure, the execution organ shall suspend execution based on the ruling on suspension of execution made by the higher court or this court.  
104. After the circumstances causing suspension of execution disappear, the executing court may resume execution upon the party's application or ex officio. The parties shall be notified in writing of the resumption of execution.  
105. If, during execution, the person subject to execution is declared bankrupt by a ruling of the People's Court, the executing court shall rule to terminate execution in accordance with Article 235, item (6) of the Civil Procedure Law.  
106. The ruling on suspension of execution and termination of execution shall state the reasons and legal basis for the suspension or termination of execution.  
107. The People's Court shall generally conclude execution of an effective legal document within six months from the date of docketing, but the period of suspension shall be deducted. If there are indeed special circumstances requiring an extension, approval shall be obtained from the president of this court.  
108. The methods of concluding execution are:  
(1) The contents specified in the effective legal document are fully executed;  
(2) A ruling to terminate execution;  
(3) A ruling to refuse execution;  
(4) A settlement agreement is reached between the parties and fully performed.  
109. If, during or after execution, the legal document on which the execution is based is revoked or modified by the People's Court or other relevant authority, the original execution organ shall, in accordance with Article 214 of the Civil Procedure Law, upon the party's application or ex officio, make a ruling for recovery of execution based on the new effective legal document, ordering the original applicant for execution to return the property and its fruits already obtained. If he refuses to return them, compulsory execution shall be carried out.  
Recovery of execution shall be re-docketed as a case, and the relevant provisions on execution procedures shall apply.  
110. When recovering execution, if the subject matter already executed is a specific item, the original item shall be returned. If the original item cannot be returned, compensation may be made by converting it into a monetary amount.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、委托执行、协助执行和执行争议的协调  
111.凡需要委托执行的案件，委托法院应在立案后1个月内办妥委托执行手续。超过此期限委托的，应当经对方法院同意。  
112.委托法院明知被执行人有下列情形的，应当及时依法裁定中止执行或终结执行，不得委托当地法院执行：  
（1）无确切住所，长期下落不明，又无财产可供执行的；  
（2）有关法院已经受理以被执行人为债务人的破产案件或者已经宣告其破产的。  
113.委托执行一般应在同级人民法院之间进行。经对方法院同意，也可委托上一级的法院执行。  
被执行人是军队企业的，可以委托其所在地的军事法院执行。  
执行标的物是船舶的，可以委托有关海事法院执行。  
114.委托法院应当向受委托法院出具书面委托函，并附送据以执行的生效法律文书副本原件、立案审批表复印件及有关情况说明，包括财产保全情况、被执行人的财产状况、生效法律文书履行的情况，并注明委托法院地址、联系电话、联系人等。  
115.委托执行案件的实际支出费用，由受托法院向被执行人收取，确有必要的，可以向申请执行人预收。委托法院已经向申请执行人预收费用的，应当将预收的费用转交受托法院。  
116.案件委托执行后，未经受托法院同意，委托法院不得自行执行。  
117.受托法院接到委托后，应当及时将指定的承办人、联系电话、地址等告知委托法院；如发现委托执行的手续、资料不全，应及时要求委托法院补办。但不得据此拒绝接受委托。  
118.受托法院对受托执行的案件应当严格按照民事诉讼法和最高人民法院有关规定执行，有权依法采取强制执行措施和对妨害执行行为的强制措施。  
119.被执行人在受托法院当地有工商登记或户籍登记，但人员下落不明，如有可供执行的财产，可以直接执行其财产。  
120.对执行担保和执行和解的情况以及案外人对非属法律文书指定交付的执行标的物提出的异议，受托法院可以按照有关法律规定处理，并及时通知委托法院。  
121.受托法院在执行中，认为需要变更被执行人的，应当将有关情况函告委托法院，由委托法院依法决定是否作出变更被执行人的裁定。  
122.受托法院认为受托执行的案件应当中止、终结执行的，应提供有关证据材料，函告委托法院作出裁定。受托法院提供的证据材料确实、充分的，委托法院应当及时作出中止或终结执行的裁定。  
123.受托法院认为委托执行的法律文书有错误，如执行可能造成执行回转困难或无法执行回转的，应当首先采取查封、扣押、冻结等保全措施，必要时要将保全款项划到法院账户，然后函请委托法院审查。受托法院按照委托法院的审查结果继续执行或停止执行。  
124.人民法院在异地执行时，当地人民法院应当积极配合，协同排除障碍，保证执行人员的人身安全和执行装备、执行标的物不受侵害。  
125.两个或两个以上人民法院在执行相关案件中发生争议的，应当协商解决。协商不成的，逐级报请上级法院，直至报请共同的上级法院协调处理。  
执行争议经高级人民法院协商不成的，由有关的高级人民法院书面报请最高人民法院协调处理。  
126.执行中发现两地法院或人民法院与仲裁机构就同一法律关系作出不同裁判内容的法律文书的，各有关法院应当立即停止执行，报请共同的上级法院处理。  
127.上级法院协调处理有关执行争议案件，认为必要时，可以决定将有关款项划到本院指定的账户。  
128.上级法院协调下级法院之间的执行争议所作出的处理决定，有关法院必须执行。

**Article 14** XIV. Commissioned Execution, Assistance in Execution, and Coordination of Execution Disputes  
111. For cases requiring commissioned execution, the commissioning court shall complete the formalities for commissioned execution within one month after docketing. If the commission exceeds this period, consent from the other court shall be obtained.  
112. If the commissioning court knows that the person subject to execution falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall promptly rule to suspend or terminate execution in accordance with law, and shall not commission the local court to execute:  
(1) Having no fixed address, being unaccounted for a long time, and having no property available for execution;  
(2) The relevant court has accepted a bankruptcy case with the person subject to execution as the debtor or has declared him bankrupt.  
113. Commissioned execution shall generally be carried out between People's Courts at the same level. With the consent of the other court, it may also be commissioned to a court at the next higher level for execution.  
If the person subject to execution is a military enterprise, execution may be commissioned to the military court at its location.  
If the subject matter of execution is a ship, execution may be commissioned to the relevant maritime court.  
114. The commissioning court shall issue a written commission letter to the commissioned court, attaching the original copy of the effective legal document to be executed, a copy of the case docketing approval form, and relevant explanations, including the status of property preservation, the property status of the person subject to execution, and the performance status of the effective legal document, and shall specify the address, contact number, and contact person of the commissioning court.  
115. The actual expenses incurred in the commissioned execution case shall be collected by the commissioned court from the person subject to execution; if truly necessary, they may be collected in advance from the applicant for execution. If the commissioning court has already collected fees in advance from the applicant for execution, it shall transfer the pre-collected fees to the commissioned court.  
116. After the case is commissioned for execution, without the consent of the commissioned court, the commissioning court shall not execute by itself.  
117. Upon receiving the commission, the commissioned court shall promptly inform the commissioning court of the designated undertaker, contact number, address, etc.; if it finds that the formalities or materials for the commissioned execution are incomplete, it shall promptly request the commissioning court to supplement them. However, it shall not refuse the commission on this ground.  
118. The commissioned court shall strictly execute the case commissioned for execution in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and the relevant provisions of the Supreme People's Court, and has the authority to take compulsory execution measures and compulsory measures against acts obstructing execution in accordance with law.  
119. If the person subject to execution has industrial and commercial registration or household registration in the locality of the commissioned court but his whereabouts are unknown, and there is property available for execution, the property may be executed directly.  
120. The commissioned court may handle the circumstances of execution security and execution settlement, as well as objections raised by a third party not involved in the case against the subject matter of execution that is not designated for delivery by the legal document, in accordance with the relevant provisions of law, and shall promptly notify the commissioning court.  
121. If, during execution, the commissioned court considers that the person subject to execution needs to be changed, it shall inform the commissioning court by letter of the relevant circumstances, and the commissioning court shall decide in accordance with law whether to make a ruling to change the person subject to execution.  
122. If the commissioned court considers that the execution of the commissioned case should be suspended or terminated, it shall provide relevant evidence materials and inform the commissioning court by letter to make a ruling. If the evidence materials provided by the commissioned court are true and sufficient, the commissioning court shall promptly make a ruling to suspend or terminate execution.  
123. If the commissioned court considers that the legal document commissioned for execution is erroneous, and execution may cause difficulty in or impossibility of recovery of execution, it shall first take preservation measures such as sealing up, distraint, or freezing, and if necessary, transfer the preserved funds to the court account, and then request the commissioning court by letter to review the matter. The commissioned court shall continue or stop execution based on the review result of the commissioning court.  
124. When a People's Court executes in another locality, the local People's Court shall actively cooperate, jointly remove obstacles, and ensure the personal safety of the execution personnel and that the execution equipment and subject matter of execution are not infringed upon.  
125. If two or more People's Courts have a dispute in executing related cases, they shall resolve it through negotiation. If negotiation fails, they shall report level by level to the higher court, up to the common higher court for coordination and handling.  
If a execution dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation by a Higher People's Court, the relevant Higher People's Court shall report in writing to the Supreme People's Court for coordination and handling.  
126. If, during execution, it is discovered that two courts or a People's Court and an arbitration institution have made legal documents with different adjudicative content on the same legal relationship, the relevant courts shall immediately stop execution and report to the common higher court for handling.  
127. When coordinating and handling relevant execution dispute cases, the higher court may, if it deems necessary, decide to transfer the relevant funds to an account designated by this court.  
128. The handling decisions made by the higher court in coordinating execution disputes between lower courts must be executed by the relevant courts.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、执行监督  
129.上级人民法院依法监督下级人民法院的执行工作。最高人民法院依法监督地方各级人民法院和专门法院的执行工作。  
130.上级法院发现下级法院在执行中作出的裁定、决定、通知或具体执行行为不当或有错误的，应当及时指令下级法院纠正，并可以通知有关法院暂缓执行。  
下级法院收到上级法院的指令后必须立即纠正。如果认为上级法院的指令有错误，可以在收到该指令后5日内请求上级法院复议。  
上级法院认为请求复议的理由不成立，而下级法院仍不纠正的，上级法院可直接作出裁定或决定予以纠正，送达有关法院及当事人，并可直接向有关单位发出协助执行通知书。  
131.上级法院发现下级法院执行的非诉讼生效法律文书有不予执行事由，应当依法作出不予执行裁定而不制作的，可以责令下级法院在指定时限内作出裁定，必要时可直接裁定不予执行。  
132.上级法院发现下级法院的执行案件（包括受委托执行的案件）在规定的期限内未能执行结案的，应当作出裁定、决定、通知而不制作的，或应当依法实施具体执行行为而不实施的，应当督促下级法院限期执行，及时作出有关裁定等法律文书，或采取相应措施。  
对下级法院长期未能执结的案件，确有必要的，上级法院可以决定由本院执行或与下级法院共同执行，也可以指定本辖区其他法院执行。  
133.上级法院在监督、指导、协调下级法院执行案件中，发现据以执行的生效法律文书确有错误的，应当书面通知下级法院暂缓执行，并按照审判监督程序处理。  
134.上级法院在申诉案件复查期间，决定对生效法律文书暂缓执行的，有关审判庭应当将暂缓执行的通知抄送执行机构。  
135.上级法院通知暂缓执行的，应同时指定暂缓执行的期限。暂缓执行的期限一般不得超过3个月。有特殊情况需要延长的，应报经院长批准，并及时通知下级法院。  
暂缓执行的原因消除后，应当及时通知执行法院恢复执行。期满后上级法院未通知继续暂缓执行的，执行法院可以恢复执行。  
136.下级法院不按照上级法院的裁定、决定或通知执行，造成严重后果的，按照有关规定追究有关主管人员和直接责任人员的责任。

**Article 15** XV. Supervision of Execution  
129. A higher People's Court shall supervise the execution work of lower People's Courts in accordance with law. The Supreme People's Court shall supervise the execution work of local People's Courts at all levels and special courts in accordance with law.  
130. If a higher court discovers that a ruling, decision, notice, or specific execution act made by a lower court during execution is improper or erroneous, it shall promptly instruct the lower court to correct it, and may notify the relevant court to suspend execution.  
Upon receiving the instruction from the higher court, the lower court must immediately correct the matter. If it considers the instruction of the higher court to be erroneous, it may request a review by the higher court within five days after receiving the instruction.  
If the higher court considers the reasons for the request for review to be unfounded, and the lower court still fails to correct, the higher court may directly make a ruling or decision to correct, serve it on the relevant court and parties, and may directly issue a notice of assistance in execution to the relevant entity.  
131. If a higher court discovers that a lower court's effective non-litigation legal document executed has grounds for refusal of execution, and the lower court should have made a ruling of refusal of execution in accordance with law but has not done so, the higher court may order the lower court to make a ruling within a designated time limit, and if necessary, may directly rule to refuse execution.  
132. If a higher court discovers that a lower court's execution case (including a case commissioned for execution) has not been concluded within the prescribed time limit, and the lower court should have made a ruling, decision, or notice but has not done so, or should have implemented specific execution acts in accordance with law but has not done so, the higher court shall urge the lower court to execute within a time limit, promptly make relevant rulings or other legal documents, or take corresponding measures.  
For cases that a lower court has long been unable to conclude, if truly necessary, the higher court may decide to execute by itself or jointly with the lower court, or may designate another court within its jurisdiction to execute.  
133. If, during supervision, guidance, or coordination of a lower court's execution case, a higher court discovers that the effective legal document on which execution is based is indeed erroneous, it shall notify the lower court in writing to suspend execution and handle the matter under the trial supervision procedure.  
134. If, during the review period of a petition case, a higher court decides to suspend execution of an effective legal document, the relevant trial division shall send a copy of the notice of suspension of execution to the execution organ.  
135. When a higher court notifies suspension of execution, it shall simultaneously designate the period of suspension. The period of suspension shall generally not exceed three months. If there are special circumstances requiring an extension, approval shall be obtained from the president, and the lower court shall be promptly notified.  
After the cause of suspension of execution is eliminated, the executing court shall be promptly notified to resume execution. If the higher court has not notified continued suspension of execution after the period expires, the executing court may resume execution.  
136. If a lower court fails to execute in accordance with a ruling, decision, or notice of a higher court, causing serious consequences, the relevant competent personnel and directly responsible personnel shall be held accountable in accordance with relevant provisions.

137.本规定自公布之日起试行。  
本院以前作出的司法解释与本规定有抵触的，以本规定为准。本规定未尽事宜，按照以前的规定办理。

**Article 16** XVI. Supplementary Provisions  
137. These Provisions shall be implemented on a trial basis from the date of promulgation.  
Where previous judicial interpretations of this Court conflict with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail. Matters not covered by these Provisions shall be handled in accordance with previous provisions.
