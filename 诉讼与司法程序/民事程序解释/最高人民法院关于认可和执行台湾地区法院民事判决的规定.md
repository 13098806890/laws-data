# 最高人民法院关于认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕13号  
**公布日期**：2015-06-29  
**生效日期**：2015-07-01

> 最高人民法院《关于认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决的规定》已于2015年6月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1653次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年7月1日起施行。 2015年6月29日 最高人民法院 关于认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决的规定 法释〔2015〕13号 为保障海峡两岸当事人的合法权益，更好地适应海峡两岸关系和平发展的新形势，根据民事诉讼法等有关法律，总结人民法院涉台审判工作经验，就认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决，制定本规定。 第一条　台湾地区法院民事判决的当事人可以根据本规定，作为申请人向人民法院申请认可和执行台湾地区有关法院民事判决。 第二条　本规定所称台湾地区法院民事判决，包括台湾地区法院作出的生效民事判决、裁定、和解笔录、调解笔录、支付命令等。 申请认可台湾地区法院在刑事案件中作出的有关民事损害赔偿的生效判决、裁定、和解笔录的，适用本规定。 申请认可由台湾地区乡镇市调解委员会等出具并经台湾地区法院核定，与台湾地区法院生效民事判决具有同等效力的调解文书的，参照适用本规定。 第三条　申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决申请的，人民法院先按照认可程序进行审查，裁定认可后，由人民法院执行机构执行。 申请人直接申请执行的，人民法院应当告知其一并提交认可申请；坚持不申请认可的，裁定驳回其申请。 第四条　申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决的案件，由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被申请人住所地、经常居住地、财产所在地中级人民法院或者专门人民法院受理。 申请人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。 申请人向被申请人财产所在地人民法院申请认可的，应当提供财产存在的相关证据。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　台湾地区法院民事判决的当事人可以根据本规定，作为申请人向人民法院申请认可和执行台湾地区有关法院民事判决。

**Article 1** A party to a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region may, as an applicant, apply to the People's Court for recognition and enforcement of the civil judgment of the relevant court in the Taiwan Region pursuant to these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称台湾地区法院民事判决，包括台湾地区法院作出的生效民事判决、裁定、和解笔录、调解笔录、支付命令等。  
申请认可台湾地区法院在刑事案件中作出的有关民事损害赔偿的生效判决、裁定、和解笔录的，适用本规定。  
申请认可由台湾地区乡镇市调解委员会等出具并经台湾地区法院核定，与台湾地区法院生效民事判决具有同等效力的调解文书的，参照适用本规定。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Provisions, "civil judgments of courts in the Taiwan Region" include effective civil judgments, rulings, settlement records, mediation records, payment orders, etc. made by courts in the Taiwan Region.  
Where an application is made for recognition of an effective judgment, ruling, or settlement record concerning civil damages for injury made by a court in the Taiwan Region in a criminal case, these Provisions shall apply.  
Where an application is made for recognition of a mediation document issued by a Township or City Mediation Committee, etc. in the Taiwan Region and verified by a court in the Taiwan Region, which has the same effect as an effective civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决申请的，人民法院先按照认可程序进行审查，裁定认可后，由人民法院执行机构执行。  
申请人直接申请执行的，人民法院应当告知其一并提交认可申请；坚持不申请认可的，裁定驳回其申请。

**Article 3** Where an applicant simultaneously applies for recognition and enforcement of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, the People's Court shall first conduct a review in accordance with the recognition procedure, and after a ruling recognizing the judgment is made, the enforcement organ of the People's Court shall enforce it.  
Where an applicant directly applies for enforcement, the People's Court shall inform the applicant to submit an application for recognition simultaneously; if the applicant insists on not applying for recognition, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the application.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决的案件，由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被申请人住所地、经常居住地、财产所在地中级人民法院或者专门人民法院受理。  
申请人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。  
申请人向被申请人财产所在地人民法院申请认可的，应当提供财产存在的相关证据。

**Article 4** A case concerning the application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court or a special People's Court of the place where the applicant has his domicile or habitual residence, or where the respondent has his domicile or habitual residence, or where the property is located.  
Where an applicant applies for recognition to two or more People's Courts that have jurisdiction, the People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.  
Where an applicant applies for recognition to the People's Court of the place where the respondent's property is located, the applicant shall provide relevant evidence of the existence of the property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决的案件，人民法院应当组成合议庭进行审查。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall form a collegiate panel to review a case concerning an application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请人委托他人代理申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决的，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。  
台湾地区、香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区或者外国当事人签名或者盖章的授权委托书应当履行相关的公证、认证或者其他证明手续，但授权委托书在人民法院法官的见证下签署或者经中国大陆公证机关公证证明是在中国大陆签署的除外。

**Article 6** Where an applicant entrusts another person to act as an agent in applying for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, the applicant shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal.  
A power of attorney signed or sealed by a party from the Taiwan Region, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or a foreign country shall undergo relevant notarization, authentication, or other certification procedures, except where the power of attorney is signed in the presence of a judge of the People's Court or is notarized by a notarial institution in Mainland China to prove that it was signed in Mainland China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请人申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决，应当提交申请书，并附有台湾地区有关法院民事判决文书和民事判决确定证明书的正本或者经证明无误的副本。台湾地区法院民事判决为缺席判决的，申请人应当同时提交台湾地区法院已经合法传唤当事人的证明文件，但判决已经对此予以明确说明的除外。  
申请书应当记明以下事项：  
（一）申请人和被申请人姓名、性别、年龄、职业、身份证件号码、住址（申请人或者被申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应当记明法人或者其他组织的名称、地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人姓名、职务）和通讯方式；  
（二）请求和理由；  
（三）申请认可的判决的执行情况；  
（四）其他需要说明的情况。

**Article 7** An applicant who applies for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region shall submit a written application, accompanied by the original or a certified true copy of the civil judgment document and the certificate of finality of the civil judgment of the relevant court in the Taiwan Region. Where the civil judgment of the court in the Taiwan Region is a default judgment, the applicant shall also submit the document certifying that the court in the Taiwan Region has lawfully summoned the party, unless the judgment has clearly stated this.  
The written application shall state the following particulars:  
(1) the name, gender, age, occupation, identity document number, address (if the applicant or the respondent is a legal person or any other organization, the name, address, and the name and title of the legal representative or principal responsible person of the legal person or other organization shall be stated) and method of communication of the applicant and the respondent;  
(2) the claims and grounds;  
(3) the status of enforcement of the judgment for which recognition is sought;  
(4) other matters that need to be stated.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对于符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第七条规定条件的申请，人民法院应当在收到申请后七日内立案，并通知申请人和被申请人，同时将申请书送达被申请人；不符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第七条规定条件的，应当在七日内裁定不予受理，同时说明不予受理的理由；申请人对裁定不服的，可以提起上诉。

**Article 8** Where an application meets the conditions set forth in Articles 4 and 7 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall docket the case within seven days after receiving the application, notify the applicant and the respondent, and serve a copy of the written application on the respondent. Where the application does not meet the conditions set forth in Articles 4 and 7 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall, within seven days, rule to dismiss the application and state the reasons for the dismissal. If the applicant refuses to accept the ruling, the applicant may file an appeal.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请人申请认可台湾地区法院民事判决，应当提供相关证明文件，以证明该判决真实并且已经生效。  
申请人可以申请人民法院通过海峡两岸调查取证司法互助途径查明台湾地区法院民事判决的真实性和是否生效以及当事人得到合法传唤的证明文件；人民法院认为必要时，也可以就有关事项依职权通过海峡两岸司法互助途径向台湾地区请求调查取证。

**Article 9** An applicant who applies for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region shall provide relevant certification documents to prove that the judgment is authentic and has taken effect.  
The applicant may apply to the People's Court to ascertain, through the channel of judicial mutual assistance in the investigation and collection of evidence between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait, the authenticity and the effective status of the civil judgment of the court in the Taiwan Region, as well as the document certifying that the party has been lawfully summoned. If the People's Court deems it necessary, it may also, ex officio, request the Taiwan Region through the channel of judicial mutual assistance between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait to investigate and collect evidence on relevant matters.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区法院民事判决的申请之前或者之后，可以按照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定，根据申请人的申请，裁定采取保全措施。

**Article 10** Before or after accepting an application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, the People's Court may, in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations, rule to take preservation measures upon the application of the applicant.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区法院民事判决的申请后，当事人就同一争议起诉的，不予受理。  
一方当事人向人民法院起诉后，另一方当事人向人民法院申请认可的，对于认可的申请不予受理。

**Article 11** After the People's Court accepts an application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, if a party files a lawsuit concerning the same dispute, the People's Court shall not accept such lawsuit.  
If after one party files a lawsuit with the People's Court, the other party applies for recognition to the People's Court, the application for recognition shall not be accepted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　案件虽经台湾地区有关法院判决，但当事人未申请认可，而是就同一争议向人民法院起诉的，应予受理。

**Article 12** Where a case has been adjudicated by a relevant court in the Taiwan Region, but a party does not apply for recognition and instead files a lawsuit with the People's Court concerning the same dispute, the People's Court shall accept such lawsuit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区法院民事判决的申请后，作出裁定前，申请人请求撤回申请的，可以裁定准许。

**Article 13** After the People's Court accepts an application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, but before a ruling is made, if the applicant requests to withdraw the application, the People's Court may rule to permit the withdrawal.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区法院民事判决的申请后，应当在立案之日起六个月内审结。有特殊情况需要延长的，报请上一级人民法院批准。  
通过海峡两岸司法互助途径送达文书和调查取证的期间，不计入审查期限。

**Article 14** After accepting an application for recognition of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, the People's Court shall conclude the case within six months from the date of docketing. If an extension is necessary due to special circumstances, approval shall be sought from the People's Court at the next higher level.  
The period for serving documents and investigating and collecting evidence through the channel of judicial mutual assistance between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait shall not be included in the period for review.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　台湾地区法院民事判决具有下列情形之一的，裁定不予认可：  
（一）申请认可的民事判决，是在被申请人缺席又未经合法传唤或者在被申请人无诉讼行为能力又未得到适当代理的情况下作出的；  
（二）案件系人民法院专属管辖的；  
（三）案件双方当事人订有有效仲裁协议，且无放弃仲裁管辖情形的；  
（四）案件系人民法院已作出判决或者中国大陆的仲裁庭已作出仲裁裁决的；  
（五）香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区或者外国的法院已就同一争议作出判决且已为人民法院所认可或者承认的；  
（六）台湾地区、香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区或者外国的仲裁庭已就同一争议作出仲裁裁决且已为人民法院所认可或者承认的。  
认可该民事判决将违反一个中国原则等国家法律的基本原则或者损害社会公共利益的，人民法院应当裁定不予认可。

**Article 15** Where a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule not to recognize it:  
(1) the civil judgment for which recognition is sought was made when the respondent was absent without being lawfully summoned, or when the respondent lacked capacity for litigation and was not properly represented;  
(2) the case is under the exclusive jurisdiction of the People's Court;  
(3) the parties to the case have concluded a valid arbitration agreement and there is no circumstance of waiving arbitration jurisdiction;  
(4) the case has been adjudicated by the People's Court or an arbitral tribunal in Mainland China has rendered an arbitral award;  
(5) a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or a foreign country has rendered a judgment on the same dispute and such judgment has been recognized or admitted by the People's Court; or  
(6) an arbitral tribunal of the Taiwan Region, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or a foreign country has rendered an arbitral award on the same dispute and such award has been recognized or admitted by the People's Court.  
If recognizing the civil judgment would violate the fundamental principles of national laws such as the One-China principle, or harm the social public interest, the People's Court shall rule not to recognize it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院经审查能够确认台湾地区法院民事判决真实并且已经生效，而且不具有[本规定第十五条](#art-15)所列情形的，裁定认可其效力；不能确认该民事判决的真实性或者已经生效的，裁定驳回申请人的申请。  
裁定驳回申请的案件，申请人再次申请并符合受理条件的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 16** If, after review, the People's Court can confirm that the civil judgment of the court in the Taiwan Region is authentic and has taken effect, and it does not fall under any of the circumstances listed in Article 15 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall rule to recognize its validity. If the authenticity or the effective status of the civil judgment cannot be confirmed, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the applicant's application.  
Where an application is dismissed by a ruling, and the applicant applies again and meets the conditions for acceptance, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经人民法院裁定认可的台湾地区法院民事判决，与人民法院作出的生效判决具有同等效力。

**Article 17** A civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region that has been recognized by a ruling of the People's Court shall have the same effect as an effective judgment rendered by the People's Court.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院依据[本规定第十五条](#art-15)和第十六条作出的裁定，一经送达即发生法律效力。  
当事人对上述裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。

**Article 18** The rulings made by the People's Court pursuant to Articles 15 and 16 of these Provisions shall take legal effect upon service.  
If a party refuses to accept the aforesaid ruling, the party may, within ten days from the date of service of the ruling, apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对人民法院裁定不予认可的台湾地区法院民事判决，申请人再次提出申请的，人民法院不予受理，但申请人可以就同一争议向人民法院起诉。

**Article 19** Where a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region has been ruled not to be recognized by the People's Court, and the applicant submits an application again, the People's Court shall not accept it; however, the applicant may file a lawsuit with the People's Court concerning the same dispute.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请人申请认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决的期间，适用[民事诉讼法第二百三十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-239)的规定，但申请认可台湾地区法院有关身份关系的判决除外。  
申请人仅申请认可而未同时申请执行的，申请执行的期间自人民法院对认可申请作出的裁定生效之日起重新计算。

**Article 20** The time limit for an applicant to apply for recognition and enforcement of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region shall be governed by Article 239 of the Civil Procedure Law, except where the application is for recognition of a judgment concerning status relations of a court in the Taiwan Region.  
Where an applicant applies only for recognition and does not simultaneously apply for enforcement, the time limit for applying for enforcement shall be recalculated from the date the ruling of the People's Court on the recognition application takes effect.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院在办理申请认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决案件中作出的法律文书，应当依法送达案件当事人。

**Article 21** The legal documents made by the People's Court in handling cases concerning applications for recognition and enforcement of civil judgments of courts in the Taiwan Region shall be served on the parties to the case in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请认可和执行台湾地区法院民事判决，应当参照《诉讼费用交纳办法》的规定，交纳相关费用。

**Article 22** For applying for recognition and enforcement of a civil judgment of a court in the Taiwan Region, relevant fees shall be paid by reference to the provisions of the Measures for the Payment of Litigation Costs.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定自2015年7月1日起施行。最高人民法院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的规定》（法释〔1998〕11号）、最高人民法院《关于当事人持台湾地区有关法院民事调解书或者有关机构出具或确认的调解协议书向人民法院申请认可人民法院应否受理的批复》（法释〔1999〕10号）、最高人民法院《关于当事人持台湾地区有关法院支付命令向人民法院申请认可人民法院应否受理的批复》（法释〔2001〕13号）和最高人民法院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的补充规定》（法释〔2009〕4号）同时废止。

**Article 23** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 2015. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Recognition of Civil Judgments of Relevant Courts in the Taiwan Region by the People's Court (Fa Shi [1998] No. 11), the Reply of the Supreme People's Court on Whether the People's Court Should Accept an Application for Recognition by a Party Holding a Civil Mediation Document of a Relevant Court in the Taiwan Region or a Mediation Agreement Issued or Confirmed by a Relevant Institution (Fa Shi [1999] No. 10), the Reply of the Supreme People's Court on Whether the People's Court Should Accept an Application for Recognition by a Party Holding a Payment Order of a Relevant Court in the Taiwan Region (Fa Shi [2001] No. 13), and the Supplementary Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Recognition of Civil Judgments of Relevant Courts in the Taiwan Region by the People's Court (Fa Shi [2009] No. 4) shall be abolished simultaneously.
