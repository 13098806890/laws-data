# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》执行程序若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕13号  
**公布日期**：2008-11-03  
**生效日期**：2009-01-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉执行程序若干问题的解释》已于2008年9月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1452次会议通过。现予公布，自2009年1月1日起施行。 2008年11月3日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》 执行程序若干问题的解释 法释〔2008〕13号 （2008年9月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第1452次会议通过　2008年11月3日最高人民法院公告公布　自2009年1月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　申请执行人向被执行的财产所在地人民法院申请执行的，应当提供该人民法院辖区有可供执行财产的证明材料。

**Article 1** Where an applicant for enforcement applies to the People's Court at the place where the property subject to enforcement is located, it shall provide evidentiary materials proving that there is property available for enforcement within the jurisdiction of that People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对两个以上人民法院都有管辖权的执行案件，人民法院在立案前发现其他有管辖权的人民法院已经立案的，不得重复立案。  
立案后发现其他有管辖权的人民法院已经立案的，应当撤销案件；已经采取执行措施的，应当将控制的财产交先立案的执行法院处理。

**Article 2** For an enforcement case over which two or more People's Courts have jurisdiction, where a People's Court, before docketing the case, discovers that another People's Court with jurisdiction has already docketed the case, it shall not docket the case again.  
Where, after docketing the case, it discovers that another People's Court with jurisdiction has already docketed the case, it shall quash the case; if enforcement measures have already been taken, it shall transfer the property under its control to the enforcement court that first docketed the case for handling.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院受理执行申请后，当事人对管辖权有异议的，应当自收到执行通知书之日起十日内提出。  
人民法院对当事人提出的异议，应当审查。异议成立的，应当撤销执行案件，并告知当事人向有管辖权的人民法院申请执行；异议不成立的，裁定驳回。当事人对裁定不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。  
管辖权异议审查和复议期间，不停止执行。

**Article 3** After a People's Court accepts an enforcement application, if a party raises an objection to jurisdiction, it shall raise such objection within ten days from the date of receiving the enforcement notice.  
The People's Court shall review the objection raised by the party. If the objection is sustained, it shall quash the enforcement case and inform the party to apply for enforcement to the People's Court with jurisdiction; if the objection is not sustained, it shall rule to reject it. If a party disagrees with the ruling, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level.  
Enforcement shall not be suspended during the period of review of the jurisdictional objection and reconsideration.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对人民法院采取财产保全措施的案件，申请执行人向采取保全措施的人民法院以外的其他有管辖权的人民法院申请执行的，采取保全措施的人民法院应当将保全的财产交执行法院处理。

**Article 4** For a case in which a People's Court has taken property preservation measures, if the applicant for enforcement applies for enforcement to another People's Court with jurisdiction other than the court that took the preservation measures, the court that took the preservation measures shall transfer the preserved property to the enforcement court for handling.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　执行过程中，当事人、利害关系人认为执行法院的执行行为违反法律规定的，可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-202)的规定提出异议。  
执行法院审查处理执行异议，应当自收到书面异议之日起十五日内作出裁定。

**Article 5** During the enforcement process, if a party or an interested person considers that an enforcement act of the enforcement court violates the provisions of the law, it may raise an objection in accordance with Article 202 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
When the enforcement court examines and handles an enforcement objection, it shall render a ruling within fifteen days from the date of receiving the written objection.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人、利害关系人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-202)规定申请复议的，应当采取书面形式。

**Article 6** Where a party or an interested person applies for reconsideration in accordance with Article 202 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall do so in writing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人、利害关系人申请复议的书面材料，可以通过执行法院转交，也可以直接向执行法院的上一级人民法院提交。  
执行法院收到复议申请后，应当在五日内将复议所需的案卷材料报送上一级人民法院；上一级人民法院收到复议申请后，应当通知执行法院在五日内报送复议所需的案卷材料。

**Article 7** The written materials for an application for reconsideration by a party or an interested person may be transmitted through the enforcement court or submitted directly to the People's Court at the next higher level of the enforcement court.  
After receiving the application for reconsideration, the enforcement court shall, within five days, submit the case file materials required for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level; after receiving the application for reconsideration, the People's Court at the next higher level shall notify the enforcement court to submit the case file materials required for reconsideration within five days.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　上一级人民法院对当事人、利害关系人的复议申请，应当组成合议庭进行审查。

**Article 8** The People's Court at the next higher level shall form a collegial panel to examine the application for reconsideration filed by a party or an interested person.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人、利害关系人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-202)规定申请复议的，上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起三十日内审查完毕，并作出裁定。有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长，延长的期限不得超过三十日。

**Article 9** Where a party or an interested person applies for reconsideration in accordance with Article 202 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court at the next higher level shall complete the examination within thirty days from the date of receiving the application for reconsideration and render a ruling. If extension is necessary due to special circumstances, with the approval of the president of the court, the period may be extended, but the extended period shall not exceed thirty days.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　执行异议审查和复议期间，不停止执行。  
被执行人、利害关系人提供充分、有效的担保请求停止相应处分措施的，人民法院可以准许；申请执行人提供充分、有效的担保请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。

**Article 10** During the period of review of an enforcement objection and reconsideration, enforcement shall not be suspended.  
If the person subject to enforcement or an interested person provides sufficient and effective security to request the suspension of the relevant disposition measures, the People's Court may grant it; if the applicant for enforcement provides sufficient and effective security to request continued enforcement, enforcement shall be continued.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依照[民事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-203)的规定，有下列情形之一的，上一级人民法院可以根据申请执行人的申请，责令执行法院限期执行或者变更执行法院：  
（一）债权人申请执行时被执行人有可供执行的财产，执行法院自收到申请执行书之日起超过六个月对该财产未执行完结的；  
（二）执行过程中发现被执行人可供执行的财产，执行法院自发现财产之日起超过六个月对该财产未执行完结的；  
（三）对法律文书确定的行为义务的执行，执行法院自收到申请执行书之日起超过六个月未依法采取相应执行措施的；  
（四）其他有条件执行超过六个月未执行的。

**Article 11** In accordance with Article 203 of the Civil Procedure Law, under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court at the next higher level may, upon the application of the applicant for enforcement, order the enforcement court to complete enforcement within a specified period or change the enforcement court:  
(1) Where, at the time the creditor applies for enforcement, the person subject to enforcement has property available for enforcement, and the enforcement court has not completed enforcement of that property within six months from the date of receiving the application for enforcement;  
(2) Where, during the enforcement process, property available for enforcement of the person subject to enforcement is discovered, and the enforcement court has not completed enforcement of that property within six months from the date of discovering the property;  
(3) Where, for the enforcement of a obligation to act specified in a legal document, the enforcement court has not taken corresponding enforcement measures according to law within six months from the date of receiving the application for enforcement;  
(4) Other circumstances where enforcement is possible but has not been enforced for more than six months.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　上一级人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-203)规定责令执行法院限期执行的，应当向其发出督促执行令，并将有关情况书面通知申请执行人。  
上一级人民法院决定由本院执行或者指令本辖区其他人民法院执行的，应当作出裁定，送达当事人并通知有关人民法院。

**Article 12** Where the People's Court at the next higher level orders the enforcement court to complete enforcement within a specified period in accordance with Article 203 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall issue a supervision and enforcement order to the enforcement court and notify the applicant for enforcement in writing of the relevant circumstances.  
If the People's Court at the next higher level decides to enforce the case by itself or to instruct another People's Court within its jurisdiction to enforce the case, it shall render a ruling, serve it on the parties, and notify the relevant People's Court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　上一级人民法院责令执行法院限期执行，执行法院在指定期间内无正当理由仍未执行完结的，上一级人民法院应当裁定由本院执行或者指令本辖区其他人民法院执行。

**Article 13** Where the People's Court at the next higher level orders the enforcement court to complete enforcement within a specified period, and the enforcement court, without justifiable reasons, still fails to complete enforcement within the specified period, the People's Court at the next higher level shall render a ruling to enforce the case by itself or instruct another People's Court within its jurisdiction to enforce the case.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　[民事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-203)规定的六个月期间，不应当计算执行中的公告期间、鉴定评估期间、管辖争议处理期间、执行争议协调期间、暂缓执行期间以及中止执行期间。

**Article 14** The six-month period specified in Article 203 of the Civil Procedure Law shall not include the period of public announcement, period of expert evaluation and appraisal, period for handling jurisdictional disputes, period for coordinating enforcement disputes, period for suspension of enforcement, and period for stay of enforcement during enforcement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　案外人对执行标的主张所有权或者有其他足以阻止执行标的转让、交付的实体权利的，可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)的规定，向执行法院提出异议。

**Article 15** Where a person not involved in the case claims ownership of the subject matter of enforcement or has other substantive rights sufficient to prevent the transfer or delivery of the subject matter of enforcement, such person may, in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, raise an objection with the enforcement court.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　案外人异议审查期间，人民法院不得对执行标的进行处分。  
案外人向人民法院提供充分、有效的担保请求解除对异议标的的查封、扣押、冻结的，人民法院可以准许；申请执行人提供充分、有效的担保请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。  
因案外人提供担保解除查封、扣押、冻结有错误，致使该标的无法执行的，人民法院可以直接执行担保财产；申请执行人提供担保请求继续执行有错误，给对方造成损失的，应当予以赔偿。

**Article 16** During the period of review of an objection raised by a person not involved in the case, the People's Court shall not dispose of the subject matter of enforcement.  
If the person not involved in the case provides sufficient and effective security to request the release of the seizure, impoundment, or freezing of the subject matter of the objection, the People's Court may grant it; if the applicant for enforcement provides sufficient and effective security to request continued enforcement, enforcement shall be continued.  
If, due to the error of the person not involved in the case in providing security to release the seizure, impoundment, or freezing, the subject matter cannot be enforced, the People's Court may directly enforce the secured property; if the applicant for enforcement provides security to request continued enforcement but is in error, causing losses to the other party, compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼，对执行标的主张实体权利，并请求对执行标的停止执行的，应当以申请执行人为被告；被执行人反对案外人对执行标的所主张的实体权利的，应当以申请执行人和被执行人为共同被告。

**Article 17** Where a person not involved in the case institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, claiming substantive rights over the subject matter of enforcement and requesting the suspension of enforcement of the subject matter, the applicant for enforcement shall be named as the defendant; if the person subject to enforcement opposes the substantive rights claimed by the person not involved in the case over the subject matter of enforcement, the applicant for enforcement and the person subject to enforcement shall be named as co-defendants.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼的，由执行法院管辖。

**Article 18** Where a person not involved in the case institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, such proceedings shall be under the jurisdiction of the enforcement court.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼的，执行法院应当依照诉讼程序审理。经审理，理由不成立的，判决驳回其诉讼请求；理由成立的，根据案外人的诉讼请求作出相应的裁判。

**Article 19** Where a person not involved in the case institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, the enforcement court shall try the case in accordance with the litigation procedure. Upon trial, if the grounds are not established, the court shall render a judgment dismissing the claim; if the grounds are established, it shall render a corresponding judgment or ruling in accordance with the claim of the person not involved in the case.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼的，诉讼期间，不停止执行。  
案外人的诉讼请求确有理由或者提供充分、有效的担保请求停止执行的，可以裁定停止对执行标的进行处分；申请执行人提供充分、有效的担保请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。  
案外人请求停止执行、请求解除查封、扣押、冻结或者申请执行人请求继续执行有错误，给对方造成损失的，应当予以赔偿。

**Article 20** Where a person not involved in the case institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, enforcement shall not be suspended during the litigation period.  
If the claim of the person not involved in the case has solid grounds or such person provides sufficient and effective security to request suspension of enforcement, the court may rule to suspend the disposition of the subject matter of enforcement; if the applicant for enforcement provides sufficient and effective security to request continued enforcement, enforcement shall be continued.  
If the person not involved in the case requests suspension of enforcement or release of seizure, impoundment, or freezing, or the applicant for enforcement requests continued enforcement, but is in error, causing losses to the other party, compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请执行人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼，请求对执行标的许可执行的，应当以案外人为被告；被执行人反对申请执行人请求的，应当以案外人和被执行人为共同被告。

**Article 21** Where the applicant for enforcement institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, requesting permission to enforce the subject matter of enforcement, the person not involved in the case shall be named as the defendant; if the person subject to enforcement opposes the request of the applicant for enforcement, the person not involved in the case and the person subject to enforcement shall be named as co-defendants.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请执行人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼的，由执行法院管辖。

**Article 22** Where the applicant for enforcement institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, such proceedings shall be under the jurisdiction of the enforcement court.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定裁定对异议标的中止执行后，申请执行人自裁定送达之日起十五日内未提起诉讼的，人民法院应当裁定解除已经采取的执行措施。

**Article 23** After the People's Court renders a ruling to suspend enforcement of the subject matter of the objection in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, if the applicant for enforcement fails to institute legal proceedings within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling, the People's Court shall render a ruling to release the enforcement measures already taken.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请执行人依照[民事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定提起诉讼的，执行法院应当依照诉讼程序审理。经审理，理由不成立的，判决驳回其诉讼请求；理由成立的，根据申请执行人的诉讼请求作出相应的裁判。

**Article 24** Where the applicant for enforcement institutes legal proceedings in accordance with Article 204 of the Civil Procedure Law, the enforcement court shall try the case in accordance with the litigation procedure. Upon trial, if the grounds are not established, the court shall render a judgment dismissing the claim; if the grounds are established, it shall render a corresponding judgment or ruling in accordance with the claim of the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　多个债权人对同一被执行人申请执行或者对执行财产申请参与分配的，执行法院应当制作财产分配方案，并送达各债权人和被执行人。债权人或者被执行人对分配方案有异议的，应当自收到分配方案之日起十五日内向执行法院提出书面异议。

**Article 25** Where multiple creditors apply for enforcement against the same person subject to enforcement or apply for participation in the distribution of the enforcement property, the enforcement court shall prepare a property distribution plan and serve it on each creditor and the person subject to enforcement. If a creditor or the person subject to enforcement raises an objection to the distribution plan, it shall submit a written objection to the enforcement court within fifteen days from the date of receiving the distribution plan.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　债权人或者被执行人对分配方案提出书面异议的，执行法院应当通知未提出异议的债权人或被执行人。  
未提出异议的债权人、被执行人收到通知之日起十五日内未提出反对意见的，执行法院依异议人的意见对分配方案审查修正后进行分配；提出反对意见的，应当通知异议人。异议人可以自收到通知之日起十五日内，以提出反对意见的债权人、被执行人为被告，向执行法院提起诉讼；异议人逾期未提起诉讼的，执行法院依原分配方案进行分配。  
诉讼期间进行分配的，执行法院应当将与争议债权数额相应的款项予以提存。

**Article 26** Where a creditor or the person subject to enforcement submits a written objection to the distribution plan, the enforcement court shall notify the creditors or the person subject to enforcement who did not raise the objection.  
If the creditors or the person subject to enforcement who did not raise the objection fail to raise a contrary opinion within fifteen days from the date of receiving the notification, the enforcement court shall review and revise the distribution plan in accordance with the opinion of the objector and then make distribution; if a contrary opinion is raised, the objector shall be notified. The objector may, within fifteen days from the date of receiving the notification, institute legal proceedings with the enforcement court, naming the creditor or the person subject to enforcement who raised the contrary opinion as the defendant; if the objector fails to institute legal proceedings within the time limit, the enforcement court shall make distribution in accordance with the original distribution plan.  
If distribution is made during the litigation period, the enforcement court shall deposit the amount corresponding to the disputed claim.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在申请执行时效期间的最后六个月内，因不可抗力或者其他障碍不能行使请求权的，申请执行时效中止。从中止时效的原因消除之日起，申请执行时效期间继续计算。

**Article 27** If, within the last six months of the limitation period for applying for enforcement, the right to claim cannot be exercised due to force majeure or other obstacles, the limitation period for applying for enforcement shall be suspended. From the date on which the cause for the suspension is eliminated, the limitation period for applying for enforcement shall continue to run.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　申请执行时效因申请执行、当事人双方达成和解协议、当事人一方提出履行要求或者同意履行义务而中断。从中断时起，申请执行时效期间重新计算。

**Article 28** The limitation period for applying for enforcement shall be interrupted by the application for enforcement, the conclusion of a settlement agreement between the parties, a request for performance made by one party, or the agreement to perform the obligation by one party. From the time of interruption, the limitation period for applying for enforcement shall run anew.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生效法律文书规定债务人负有不作为义务的，申请执行时效期间从债务人违反不作为义务之日起计算。

**Article 29** Where an effective legal document specifies that the debtor bears an obligation to refrain from an act, the limitation period for applying for enforcement shall be calculated from the date on which the debtor violates such obligation to refrain.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　执行员依照[民事诉讼法第二百一十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-216)规定立即采取强制执行措施的，可以同时或者自采取强制执行措施之日起三日内发送执行通知书。

**Article 30** Where the enforcement officer immediately takes compulsory enforcement measures in accordance with Article 216 of the Civil Procedure Law, the enforcement notice may be served simultaneously or within three days from the date of taking the compulsory enforcement measures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)规定责令被执行人报告财产情况的，应当向其发出报告财产令。报告财产令中应当写明报告财产的范围、报告财产的期间、拒绝报告或者虚假报告的法律后果等内容。

**Article 31** Where the People's Court orders the person subject to enforcement to report the status of property in accordance with Article 217 of the Civil Procedure Law, it shall issue an order to report property. The order to report property shall specify the scope of property to be reported, the period for reporting property, and the legal consequences of refusal to report or false reporting.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被执行人依照[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)的规定，应当书面报告下列财产情况：  
（一）收入、银行存款、现金、有价证券；  
（二）土地使用权、房屋等不动产；  
（三）交通运输工具、机器设备、产品、原材料等动产；  
（四）债权、股权、投资权益、基金、知识产权等财产性权利；  
（五）其他应当报告的财产。  
被执行人自收到执行通知之日前一年至当前财产发生变动的，应当对该变动情况进行报告。  
被执行人在报告财产期间履行全部债务的，人民法院应当裁定终结报告程序。

**Article 32** The person subject to enforcement shall, in accordance with Article 217 of the Civil Procedure Law, report in writing the following property:  
(1) Income, bank deposits, cash, and negotiable securities;  
(2) Immovable property such as land-use rights and houses;  
(3) Movable property such as means of transportation, machinery and equipment, products, and raw materials;  
(4) Property rights such as claims, equity interests, investment rights, fund interests, and intellectual property;  
(5) Other property that shall be reported.  
If the property of the person subject to enforcement changes during the period from one year before the date of receiving the enforcement notice to the present, such changes shall be reported.  
If the person subject to enforcement performs all debts during the period for reporting property, the People's Court shall render a ruling to terminate the reporting procedure.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　被执行人报告财产后，其财产情况发生变动，影响申请执行人债权实现的，应当自财产变动之日起十日内向人民法院补充报告。

**Article 33** After the person subject to enforcement reports property, if the status of its property changes, affecting the realization of the claim of the applicant for enforcement, it shall submit a supplementary report to the People's Court within ten days from the date of the property change.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对被执行人报告的财产情况，申请执行人请求查询的，人民法院应当准许。申请执行人对查询的被执行人财产情况，应当保密。

**Article 34** Where the applicant for enforcement requests access to the property status reported by the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall grant it. The applicant for enforcement shall keep confidential the property status of the person subject to enforcement that it accesses.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对被执行人报告的财产情况，执行法院可以依申请执行人的申请或者依职权调查核实。

**Article 35** The enforcement court may, upon the application of the applicant for enforcement or ex officio, investigate and verify the property status reported by the person subject to enforcement.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-231)规定对被执行人限制出境的，应当由申请执行人向执行法院提出书面申请；必要时，执行法院可以依职权决定。

**Article 36** Where the person subject to enforcement is restricted from leaving the country in accordance with Article 231 of the Civil Procedure Law, the applicant for enforcement shall submit a written application to the enforcement court; if necessary, the enforcement court may decide ex officio.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　被执行人为单位的，可以对其法定代表人、主要负责人或者影响债务履行的直接责任人员限制出境。  
被执行人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，可以对其法定代理人限制出境。

**Article 37** Where the person subject to enforcement is an entity, its legal representative, principal responsible person, or directly responsible personnel who affect the performance of the debt may be restricted from leaving the country.  
Where the person subject to enforcement is a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct, its statutory agent may be restricted from leaving the country.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在限制出境期间，被执行人履行法律文书确定的全部债务的，执行法院应当及时解除限制出境措施；被执行人提供充分、有效的担保或者申请执行人同意的，可以解除限制出境措施。

**Article 38** During the period of restriction from leaving the country, if the person subject to enforcement performs all debts specified in the legal document, the enforcement court shall promptly cancel the measure of restriction from leaving the country; if the person subject to enforcement provides sufficient and effective security or the applicant for enforcement agrees, the measure of restriction from leaving the country may be cancelled.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-231)的规定，执行法院可以依职权或者依申请执行人的申请，将被执行人不履行法律文书确定义务的信息，通过报纸、广播、电视、互联网等媒体公布。  
媒体公布的有关费用，由被执行人负担；申请执行人申请在媒体公布的，应当垫付有关费用。

**Article 39** In accordance with Article 231 of the Civil Procedure Law, the enforcement court may, ex officio or upon the application of the applicant for enforcement, publish information that the person subject to enforcement fails to perform the obligations specified in the legal document through media such as newspapers, radio, television, and the Internet.  
The relevant costs of publication through media shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement; if the applicant for enforcement applies for publication through media, it shall advance the relevant costs.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本解释施行前本院公布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 40** Where any judicial interpretation published by this Supreme People's Court before this Interpretation comes into force is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
