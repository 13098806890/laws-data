# 最高人民检察院关于以检察专递方式邮寄送达有关检察法律文书的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民检察院  
**发文字号**：高检发释字〔2015〕1号  
**公布日期**：2015-02-13  
**生效日期**：2015-02-13

> 《最高人民检察院关于以检察专递方式邮寄送达有关检察法律文书的若干规定》已于2014年12月30日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第三十三次会议通过，现予以公布，自即日起施行。 最高人民检察院 2015年2月13日 最高人民检察院关于以检察专递方式 邮寄送达有关检察法律文书的若干规定 （2014年12月30日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会 第三十三次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　法律规定可以邮寄送达的检察法律文书，人民检察院可以交由邮政企业以检察专递方式邮寄送达，但下列情形除外：  
（一）受送达人或者其诉讼代理人、受送达人指定的代收人同意在指定的期间内到人民检察院接受送达的；  
（二）受送达人下落不明的；  
（三）法律规定、我国缔结或者参加的国际条约中约定有特别送达方式的。

**Article 1** Where the law provides that procuratorial legal documents may be served by mail, the People's Procuratorate may entrust them to a postal enterprise for service by mail in the form of procuratorial express delivery, except under the following circumstances:  
(1) The person on whom service is to be made or his litigation agent or the authorized recipient designated by the person on whom service is to be made agrees to appear at the People's Procuratorate within the specified period to accept service;  
(2) The whereabouts of the person on whom service is to be made are unknown;  
(3) The law or an international treaty concluded or acceded to by China stipulates a special method of service.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　以检察专递方式邮寄送达有关检察法律文书的，该送达与人民检察院直接送达具有同等法律效力。

**Article 2** Where relevant procuratorial legal documents are served by mail in the form of procuratorial express delivery, such service shall have the same legal effect as service directly by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人向人民检察院申请监督、提出申诉或者提交答辩意见时，应当向人民检察院提供或者确认自己准确的送达地址及联系方式，并填写当事人联系方式确认书。

**Article 3** When a party applies to the People's Procuratorate for supervision, lodges a petition, or submits a defense opinion, the party shall provide or confirm his accurate service address and contact information to the People's Procuratorate and fill in the Party Contact Information Confirmation Form.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人联系方式确认书的内容应当包括送达地址的邮政编码、详细地址以及受送达人的联系电话等内容。  
对当事人联系方式确认书记载的内容，人民检察院和邮政企业应当为其保密。  
当事人变更送达地址的，应当及时以书面方式告知人民检察院。

**Article 4** The content of the Party Contact Information Confirmation Form shall include the postal code and detailed address of the service address as well as the contact telephone number of the person on whom service is to be made, among others.  
The People's Procuratorate and the postal enterprise shall keep confidential the content recorded in the Party Contact Information Confirmation Form.  
Where a party changes his service address, he shall promptly inform the People's Procuratorate in writing.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经人民检察院告知，当事人仍拒绝提供自己送达地址的，自然人以其户籍登记中的住所地或者经常居住地为其送达地址；法人或者其他组织以其工商登记或者其他依法登记、备案中的住所地为其送达地址。

**Article 5** Where, after being informed by the People's Procuratorate, a party still refuses to provide his service address, for a natural person, his domicile as recorded in his household registration or his habitual residence shall be deemed his service address; for a legal person or any other organization, its domicile as recorded in its business registration or other registration or filing according to law shall be deemed its service address.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　邮政企业按照当事人提供或者确认的送达地址送达的，应当在规定的日期内将回执退回人民检察院。  
邮政企业按照当事人提供或者确认的送达地址在五个工作日内投送三次未能送达，通过电话或者其他联系方式无法通知到受送达人的，应当将邮件退回人民检察院，并说明退回的理由。

**Article 6** Where a postal enterprise serves the documents in accordance with the service address provided or confirmed by the party, it shall return the receipt to the People's Procuratorate within the prescribed time limit.  
Where a postal enterprise, after three delivery attempts within five working days in accordance with the service address provided or confirmed by the party, fails to serve the documents and is unable to notify the person on whom service is to be made by telephone or other means of contact, it shall return the mail to the People's Procuratorate and state the reasons for the return.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　邮寄送达检察法律文书，应当直接送交受送达人。受送达人是公民的，由其本人签收，本人不在其提供或者确认的送达地址的，邮政企业可以将邮件交给与他同住的成年家属代收，但同住的成年家属是同一案件中另一方当事人的除外；受送达人是法人或者其他组织的，应当由法人的法定代表人、其他组织的主要负责人或者该法人、组织负责收件的工作人员签收；受送达人有诉讼代理人的，可以送交其代理人签收；受送达人已向人民检察院指定代收人的，送交代收人签收。

**Article 7** When serving procuratorial legal documents by mail, the documents shall be delivered directly to the person on whom service is to be made. Where the person on whom service is to be made is a citizen, the documents shall be signed for by him personally; if he is not at the service address he provided or confirmed, the postal enterprise may deliver the mail to an adult family member living with him for receipt on his behalf, except where such adult family member is the other party in the same case. Where the person on whom service is to be made is a legal person or any other organization, the documents shall be signed for by the legal representative of the legal person, the principal of the organization, or the staff member responsible for receiving mail of such legal person or organization. Where the person on whom service is to be made has a litigation agent, the documents may be delivered to the agent for signing. Where the person on whom service is to be made has designated a recipient to the People's Procuratorate, the documents shall be delivered to such designated recipient for signing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　受送达人或者其代收人应当在邮件回执上签名、盖章或者捺印。  
受送达人或者其代收人在签收时，应当出示其有效身份证件并在回执上填写该证件的号码，代收人还应填写其与受送达人的关系；受送达人或者其代收人拒绝签收的，由邮政企业的投递员记明情况，并将邮件退回人民检察院。

**Article 8** The person on whom service is to be made or his authorized recipient shall sign, affix his seal, or affix his fingerprint on the mail receipt.  
When signing for the mail, the person on whom service is to be made or his authorized recipient shall present his valid identity document and fill in the number of such document on the receipt, and the authorized recipient shall also fill in his relationship with the person on whom service is to be made; if the person on whom service is to be made or his authorized recipient refuses to sign for the mail, the courier of the postal enterprise shall record the circumstances and return the mail to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有下列情形之一的，即为送达：  
（一）受送达人在邮件回执上签名、盖章或者捺印的；  
（二）受送达人是无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的自然人，其法定代理人签收的；  
（三）受送达人是法人或者其他组织，其法人的法定代表人、该组织的主要负责人或者办公室、收发室、值班室的工作人员签收的；  
（四）受送达人的诉讼代理人签收的；  
　　（五）受送达人指定的代收人签收的；  
　　（六）受送达人的同住成年家属签收的。

**Article 9** Service shall be deemed completed in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The person on whom service is to be made signs, affixes his seal, or affixes his fingerprint on the mail receipt;  
(2) Where the person on whom service is to be made is a natural person without or with limited capacity for civil conduct, his legal representative signs for the mail;  
(3) Where the person on whom service is to be made is a legal person or any other organization, the legal representative of the legal person, the principal of the organization, or a staff member of its office, mailroom, or duty office signs for the mail;  
(4) The litigation agent of the person on whom service is to be made signs for the mail;  
(5) The authorized recipient designated by the person on whom service is to be made signs for the mail;  
(6) An adult family member living with the person on whom service is to be made signs for the mail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　签收人是受送达人本人或者是受送达人的法定代表人、主要负责人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人的，签收人应当当场核对邮件内容。签收人发现邮件内容与回执上的文书名称不一致的，应当当场向邮政企业的投递员提出，由投递员在回执上记明情况，并将邮件退回人民检察院。  
签收人是受送达人办公室、收发室、值班室的工作人员或者是与受送达人同住的成年家属，受送达人发现邮件内容与回执上的文书名称不一致的，应当在三日内将该邮件退回人民检察院，并以书面方式说明退回的理由。

**Article 10** Where the signatory is the person on whom service is to be made himself, or the legal representative, principal, legal agent, or litigation agent of such person, the signatory shall check the contents of the mail on the spot. If the signatory discovers that the contents of the mail are inconsistent with the name of the document indicated on the receipt, he shall raise the issue on the spot to the courier of the postal enterprise, and the courier shall record the circumstances on the receipt and return the mail to the People's Procuratorate.  
Where the signatory is a staff member of the office, mailroom, or duty office of the person on whom service is to be made, or an adult family member living with such person, and such person discovers that the contents of the mail are inconsistent with the name of the document indicated on the receipt, he shall return the mail to the People's Procuratorate within three days and state the reasons for the return in writing.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  邮寄送达检察法律文书的费用，从各级人民检察院办案经费中支出。

**Article 11** The expenses for serving procuratorial legal documents by mail shall be disbursed from the case-handling funds of the People's Procuratorates at all levels.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  本规定所称检察专递是指邮政企业针对送达检察法律文书所采取的特快专递邮寄形式。

**Article 12** For the purpose of these Provisions, procuratorial express delivery means the express mail service adopted by postal enterprises for serving procuratorial legal documents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定由最高人民检察院负责解释，自发布之日起施行。  
最高人民检察院以前的有关规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Supreme People's Procuratorate and shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.  
Where any previous relevant provisions of the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
