# 最高人民法院关于民事执行中变更、追加当事人若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕21号  
**公布日期**：2016-11-07  
**生效日期**：2016-12-01

> 最高人民法院《关于民事执行中变更、追加当事人若干问题的规定》已于2016年8月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1691次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年12月1日起施行。 2016年11月7日 最高人民法院 关于民事执行中变更、追加当事人若干问题的规定 法释〔2016〕21号 为正确处理民事执行中变更、追加当事人问题，维护当事人、利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合执行实践，制定本规定。 第一条　执行过程中，申请执行人或其继承人、权利承受人可以向人民法院申请变更、追加当事人。申请符合法定条件的，人民法院应予支持。 第二条　作为申请执行人的公民死亡或被宣告死亡，该公民的遗嘱执行人、受遗赠人、继承人或其他因该公民死亡或被宣告死亡依法承受生效法律文书确定权利的主体，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 作为申请执行人的公民被宣告失踪，该公民的财产代管人申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第三条　作为申请执行人的公民离婚时，生效法律文书确定的权利全部或部分分割给其配偶，该配偶申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第四条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织终止，因该法人或其他组织终止依法承受生效法律文书确定权利的主体，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第五条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织因合并而终止，合并后存续或新设的法人、其他组织申请变更其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第六条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织分立，依分立协议约定承受生效法律文书确定权利的新设法人或其他组织，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第七条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织清算或破产时，生效法律文书确定的权利依法分配给第三人，该第三人申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。 第八条　作为申请执行人的机关法人被撤销，继续履行其职能的主体申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持，但生效法律文书确定的权利依法应由其他主体承受的除外；没有继续履行其职能的主体，且生效法律文书确定权利的承受主体不明确，作出撤销决定的主体申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　执行过程中，申请执行人或其继承人、权利承受人可以向人民法院申请变更、追加当事人。申请符合法定条件的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 1** During enforcement, the applicant for enforcement or his/her successor, or the person succeeding to the rights may apply to the People's Court for a change or addition of a party. If the application meets the statutory conditions, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　作为申请执行人的公民死亡或被宣告死亡，该公民的遗嘱执行人、受遗赠人、继承人或其他因该公民死亡或被宣告死亡依法承受生效法律文书确定权利的主体，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。  
作为申请执行人的公民被宣告失踪，该公民的财产代管人申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** If a citizen who is the applicant for enforcement dies or is declared dead, the executor of the will, legatee, heir, or other subject that succeeds to the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument according to law due to the death or declaration of death of the citizen, and applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.  
If a citizen who is the applicant for enforcement is declared missing, the property custodian of the citizen who applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　作为申请执行人的公民离婚时，生效法律文书确定的权利全部或部分分割给其配偶，该配偶申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** If, when a citizen who is the applicant for enforcement divorces, the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument are wholly or partially divided to his/her spouse, and the spouse applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织终止，因该法人或其他组织终止依法承受生效法律文书确定权利的主体，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 4** If a legal person or other organization that is the applicant for enforcement terminates, the subject that succeeds to the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument according to law due to the termination of the legal person or other organization, and applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织因合并而终止，合并后存续或新设的法人、其他组织申请变更其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** If a legal person or other organization that is the applicant for enforcement terminates due to merger, the surviving or newly established legal person or other organization after the merger that applies for a change of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织分立，依分立协议约定承受生效法律文书确定权利的新设法人或其他组织，申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** If a legal person or other organization that is the applicant for enforcement splits, the newly established legal person or other organization that, according to the split agreement, succeeds to the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　作为申请执行人的法人或其他组织清算或破产时，生效法律文书确定的权利依法分配给第三人，该第三人申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** If, when a legal person or other organization that is the applicant for enforcement is liquidated or bankrupt, the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument are distributed to a third party according to law, and the third party applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　作为申请执行人的机关法人被撤销，继续履行其职能的主体申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持，但生效法律文书确定的权利依法应由其他主体承受的除外；没有继续履行其职能的主体，且生效法律文书确定权利的承受主体不明确，作出撤销决定的主体申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** If an organ legal person that is the applicant for enforcement is abolished, and the subject that continues to perform its functions applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it, unless the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument shall be succeeded by another subject according to law; if there is no subject that continues to perform its functions, and the successor of the rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument is not clear, the subject that made the abolition decision applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请执行人将生效法律文书确定的债权依法转让给第三人，且书面认可第三人取得该债权，该第三人申请变更、追加其为申请执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** If the applicant for enforcement transfers the creditor's rights confirmed by the effective legal instrument to a third party according to law, and acknowledges in writing that the third party has acquired such creditor's rights, and the third party applies for a change or addition of itself as the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　作为被执行人的公民死亡或被宣告死亡，申请执行人申请变更、追加该公民的遗嘱执行人、继承人、受遗赠人或其他因该公民死亡或被宣告死亡取得遗产的主体为被执行人，在遗产范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。继承人放弃继承或受遗赠人放弃受遗赠，又无遗嘱执行人的，人民法院可以直接执行遗产。  
作为被执行人的公民被宣告失踪，申请执行人申请变更该公民的财产代管人为被执行人，在代管的财产范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** If a citizen who is the person subject to enforcement dies or is declared dead, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the executor of the will, heir, legatee, or other subject that acquires the estate due to the death or declaration of death of the citizen as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the estate, the People's Court shall support it. If the heir disclaims the inheritance or the legatee disclaims the legacy, and there is no executor of the will, the People's Court may directly enforce against the estate.  
If a citizen who is the person subject to enforcement is declared missing, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change of the property custodian of the citizen as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the property under its custody, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　作为被执行人的法人或其他组织因合并而终止，申请执行人申请变更合并后存续或新设的法人、其他组织为被执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** If a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement terminates due to merger, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change of the surviving or newly established legal person or other organization after the merger as the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　作为被执行人的法人或其他组织分立，申请执行人申请变更、追加分立后新设的法人或其他组织为被执行人，对生效法律文书确定的债务承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。但被执行人在分立前与申请执行人就债务清偿达成的书面协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 12** If a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement splits, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the newly established legal person or other organization after the split as the person subject to enforcement, to bear joint and several liability for the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, the People's Court shall support it, unless otherwise agreed in the written agreement reached between the person subject to enforcement and the applicant for enforcement before the split regarding the discharge of debts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　作为被执行人的个人独资企业，不能清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加其投资人为被执行人的，人民法院应予支持。个人独资企业投资人作为被执行人的，人民法院可以直接执行该个人独资企业的财产。  
个体工商户的字号为被执行人的，人民法院可以直接执行该字号经营者的财产。

**Article 13** If a sole proprietorship enterprise that is the person subject to enforcement is unable to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of its investor as the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it. If the investor of a sole proprietorship enterprise is the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court may directly enforce against the property of the sole proprietorship enterprise.  
If the business name of an individual industrial or commercial household is the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court may directly enforce against the property of the operator of such business name.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　作为被执行人的合伙企业，不能清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加普通合伙人为被执行人的，人民法院应予支持。  
作为被执行人的有限合伙企业，财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加未按期足额缴纳出资的有限合伙人为被执行人，在未足额缴纳出资的范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** If a partnership enterprise that is the person subject to enforcement is unable to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the general partner as the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.  
If a limited partnership enterprise that is the person subject to enforcement has insufficient property to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the limited partner who fails to make capital contributions in full and on time as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the unpaid capital contributions, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　作为被执行人的法人分支机构，不能清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加该法人为被执行人的，人民法院应予支持。法人直接管理的责任财产仍不能清偿债务的，人民法院可以直接执行该法人其他分支机构的财产。  
作为被执行人的法人，直接管理的责任财产不能清偿生效法律文书确定债务的，人民法院可以直接执行该法人分支机构的财产。

**Article 15** If a branch of a legal person that is the person subject to enforcement is unable to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the legal person as the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it. If the liability property directly managed by the legal person is still unable to discharge the debts, the People's Court may directly enforce against the property of other branches of the legal person.  
If a legal person that is the person subject to enforcement has liability property directly managed that is unable to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, the People's Court may directly enforce against the property of the branches of such legal person.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　个人独资企业、合伙企业、法人分支机构以外的其他组织作为被执行人，不能清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加依法对该其他组织的债务承担责任的主体为被执行人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** If an other organization other than a sole proprietorship enterprise, partnership enterprise, or branch of a legal person is the person subject to enforcement and is unable to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the subject that bears liability for the debts of such other organization according to law as the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　作为被执行人的企业法人，财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加未缴纳或未足额缴纳出资的股东、出资人或依公司法规定对该出资承担连带责任的发起人为被执行人，在尚未缴纳出资的范围内依法承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** If an enterprise legal person that is the person subject to enforcement has insufficient property to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the shareholder, contributor, or promoter who bears joint and several liability for such capital contributions pursuant to the Company Law, and who has not paid or has not fully paid the capital contributions, as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability according to law within the scope of the unpaid capital contributions, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　作为被执行人的企业法人，财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加抽逃出资的股东、出资人为被执行人，在抽逃出资的范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 18** If an enterprise legal person that is the person subject to enforcement has insufficient property to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the shareholder or contributor who has withdrawn capital contributions as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the withdrawn capital contributions, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　作为被执行人的公司，财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，其股东未依法履行出资义务即转让股权，申请执行人申请变更、追加该原股东或依公司法规定对该出资承担连带责任的发起人为被执行人，在未依法出资的范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** If a company that is the person subject to enforcement has insufficient property to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and its shareholder transfers the equity without performing the capital contribution obligation according to law, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the original shareholder or the promoter who bears joint and several liability for such capital contributions pursuant to the Company Law as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the capital contributions not made according to law, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　作为被执行人的一人有限责任公司，财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，股东不能证明公司财产独立于自己的财产，申请执行人申请变更、追加该股东为被执行人，对公司债务承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** If a one-person limited liability company that is the person subject to enforcement has insufficient property to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the shareholder cannot prove that the company's property is independent of his/her own property, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of such shareholder as the person subject to enforcement, to bear joint and several liability for the company's debts, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　作为被执行人的公司，未经清算即办理注销登记，导致公司无法进行清算，申请执行人申请变更、追加有限责任公司的股东、股份有限公司的董事和控股股东为被执行人，对公司债务承担连带清偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** If a company that is the person subject to enforcement undergoes deregistration without liquidation, making it impossible to liquidate the company, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of the shareholders of a limited liability company, or the directors and controlling shareholders of a joint stock limited company, as the persons subject to enforcement, to bear joint and several liability for discharging the company's debts, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　作为被执行人的法人或其他组织，被注销或出现被吊销营业执照、被撤销、被责令关闭、歇业等解散事由后，其股东、出资人或主管部门无偿接受其财产，致使该被执行人无遗留财产或遗留财产不足以清偿债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加该股东、出资人或主管部门为被执行人，在接受的财产范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 22** If a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement is deregistered or, after the occurrence of a ground for dissolution such as having its business license revoked, being abolished, being ordered to close, or ceasing operations, its shareholders, contributors, or competent authority accept its property without consideration, resulting in the person subject to enforcement having no remaining property or insufficient remaining property to discharge the debts, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of such shareholders, contributors, or competent authority as the persons subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the property accepted, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　作为被执行人的法人或其他组织，未经依法清算即办理注销登记，在登记机关办理注销登记时，第三人书面承诺对被执行人的债务承担清偿责任，申请执行人申请变更、追加该第三人为被执行人，在承诺范围内承担清偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** If a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement undergoes deregistration without liquidation according to law, and when handling the deregistration with the registration authority, a third party makes a written promise to bear the liability for discharging the debts of the person subject to enforcement, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of such third party as the person subject to enforcement, to bear the liability for discharging within the scope of the promise, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　执行过程中，第三人向执行法院书面承诺自愿代被执行人履行生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加该第三人为被执行人，在承诺范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 24** During enforcement, if a third party makes a written promise to the enforcement court to voluntarily perform the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument on behalf of the person subject to enforcement, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of such third party as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the promise, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　作为被执行人的法人或其他组织，财产依行政命令被无偿调拨、划转给第三人，致使该被执行人财产不足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债务，申请执行人申请变更、追加该第三人为被执行人，在接受的财产范围内承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 25** If the property of a legal person or other organization that is the person subject to enforcement is transferred or allocated to a third party without consideration pursuant to an administrative order, causing the property of such person subject to enforcement to be insufficient to discharge the debts confirmed by the effective legal instrument, and the applicant for enforcement applies for a change or addition of such third party as the person subject to enforcement, to bear liability within the scope of the property accepted, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　被申请人在应承担责任范围内已承担相应责任的，人民法院不得责令其重复承担责任。

**Article 26** If the respondent has already borne corresponding liability within the scope of liability it shall bear, the People's Court shall not order it to bear liability repeatedly.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　执行当事人的姓名或名称发生变更的，人民法院可以直接将姓名或名称变更后的主体作为执行当事人，并在法律文书中注明变更前的姓名或名称。

**Article 27** If the name of a party to enforcement changes, the People's Court may directly take the subject with the changed name as the party to enforcement, and shall indicate the previous name in the legal instrument.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　申请人申请变更、追加执行当事人，应当向执行法院提交书面申请及相关证据材料。  
除事实清楚、权利义务关系明确、争议不大的案件外，执行法院应当组成合议庭审查并公开听证。经审查，理由成立的，裁定变更、追加；理由不成立的，裁定驳回。  
执行法院应当自收到书面申请之日起六十日内作出裁定。有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。

**Article 28** An applicant applying for a change or addition of a party to enforcement shall submit a written application and relevant evidentiary materials to the enforcement court.  
Except for cases where the facts are clear, the rights and obligations are clear, and the dispute is not significant, the enforcement court shall form a collegiate panel to examine and hold a public hearing. Upon examination, if the grounds are established, it shall make a ruling to change or add; if the grounds are not established, it shall make a ruling to reject.  
The enforcement court shall make a ruling within sixty days from the date of receiving the written application. If there are special circumstances requiring an extension, it shall be approved by the president of the court.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　执行法院审查变更、追加被执行人申请期间，申请人申请对被申请人的财产采取查封、扣押、冻结措施的，执行法院应当参照[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)的规定办理。  
申请执行人在申请变更、追加第三人前，向执行法院申请查封、扣押、冻结该第三人财产的，执行法院应当参照[民事诉讼法第一百零一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-101)的规定办理。

**Article 29** During the period when the enforcement court examines an application for a change or addition of a person subject to enforcement, if the applicant applies for measures of seizure, impoundment, or freezing against the property of the respondent, the enforcement court shall handle it by reference to Article 100 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
If the applicant for enforcement, before applying for a change or addition of a third party, applies to the enforcement court for seizure, impoundment, or freezing of the property of such third party, the enforcement court shall handle it by reference to Article 101 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被申请人、申请人或其他执行当事人对执行法院作出的变更、追加裁定或驳回申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定书送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，但依据[本规定第三十二条](#art-32)的规定应当提起诉讼的除外。

**Article 30** If the respondent, applicant, or other party to enforcement is dissatisfied with the ruling on change or addition or the ruling rejecting the application made by the enforcement court, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling, except where an action shall be filed pursuant to Article 32 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　上一级人民法院对复议申请应当组成合议庭审查，并自收到申请之日起六十日内作出复议裁定。有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。  
被裁定变更、追加的被申请人申请复议的，复议期间，人民法院不得对其争议范围内的财产进行处分。申请人请求人民法院继续执行并提供相应担保的，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 31** The People's Court at the next higher level shall form a collegiate panel to examine the application for reconsideration and shall make a reconsideration ruling within sixty days from the date of receiving the application. If there are special circumstances requiring an extension, it shall be approved by the president of the court.  
If the respondent that is ruled to be changed or added applies for reconsideration, during the reconsideration period, the People's Court shall not dispose of the property within the scope of dispute. If the applicant requests the People's Court to continue enforcement and provides corresponding security, the People's Court may permit it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被申请人或申请人对执行法院依据[本规定第十四条](#art-14)第二款、第十七条至第二十一条规定作出的变更、追加裁定或驳回申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定书送达之日起十五日内，向执行法院提起执行异议之诉。  
被申请人提起执行异议之诉的，以申请人为被告。申请人提起执行异议之诉的，以被申请人为被告。

**Article 32** If the respondent or applicant is dissatisfied with the ruling on change or addition or the ruling rejecting the application made by the enforcement court pursuant to Article 14(2) and Articles 17 to 21 of these Provisions, it may file an action for enforcement objection with the enforcement court within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling.  
If the respondent files the action for enforcement objection, the applicant shall be the defendant. If the applicant files the action for enforcement objection, the respondent shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　被申请人提起的执行异议之诉，人民法院经审理，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）理由成立的，判决不得变更、追加被申请人为被执行人或者判决变更责任范围；  
（二）理由不成立的，判决驳回诉讼请求。  
诉讼期间，人民法院不得对被申请人争议范围内的财产进行处分。申请人请求人民法院继续执行并提供相应担保的，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 33** If the respondent files an action for enforcement objection, the People's Court shall, after trial, handle it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the grounds are established, it shall render a judgment that the respondent shall not be changed or added as the person subject to enforcement, or that the scope of liability shall be changed;  
(2) If the grounds are not established, it shall render a judgment dismissing the claims.  
During the litigation period, the People's Court shall not dispose of the property of the respondent within the scope of dispute. If the applicant requests the People's Court to continue enforcement and provides corresponding security, the People's Court may permit it.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　申请人提起的执行异议之诉，人民法院经审理，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）理由成立的，判决变更、追加被申请人为被执行人并承担相应责任或者判决变更责任范围；  
（二）理由不成立的，判决驳回诉讼请求。

**Article 34** If the applicant files an action for enforcement objection, the People's Court shall, after trial, handle it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the grounds are established, it shall render a judgment changing or adding the respondent as the person subject to enforcement and requiring it to bear corresponding liability, or render a judgment changing the scope of liability;  
(2) If the grounds are not established, it shall render a judgment dismissing the claims.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本规定自2016年12月1日起施行。  
本规定施行后，本院以前公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 35** These Provisions shall come into force as of December 1, 2016.  
After these Provisions come into force, if any previous judicial interpretation published by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
