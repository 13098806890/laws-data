### 最高人民法院关于美国法院未通过外交途径迳直将离婚判决书寄给我人民法院如何处理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于美国法院未通过外交途径  
  
迳直将离婚判决书寄给我人民法院如何处理的批复  
  
（85）民他字第 37号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 85）民请第 27号请示报告及所附美国加利福尼亚高等法院给苏州市中级人民法院寄来的蔡 ××与周 ××离婚判决书副本收悉。经与有关部门研究，现答复如下：  
  
在中美两国目前尚无司法协定的情况下，美国加利福尼亚高等法院未通过外交途径，直接给苏州市中级人民法院寄来蔡 ××与周 ××离婚判决书副本，这种做法，不仅违反我国民事诉讼法的有关规定，也不符合一般国际关系中的互惠原则。因此，以上材料可由苏州市中级人民法院迳直退回美国加利福尼亚高等法院。  
  
一九八五年十二月二十六日

**Article 1** Reply on How to Handle a Divorce Judgment Sent Directly to a People's Court  
(85) Min Ta Zi No. 37  
To the Higher People's Court of Jiangsu Province:  
Your request for instructions (85) Min Qing Zi No. 27 and the attached copy of the divorce judgment between Cai ×× and Zhou ×× sent by the California Superior Court, United States, to the Suzhou Intermediate People's Court have been received. After study with the relevant departments, the reply is as follows:  
Under the current circumstances where there is no judicial assistance agreement between China and the United States, the California Superior Court, without going through diplomatic channels, directly sent the copy of the divorce judgment between Cai ×× and Zhou ×× to the Suzhou Intermediate People's Court. This practice not only violates the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of our country but also does not conform to the principle of reciprocity in general international relations. Therefore, the above materials may be directly returned by the Suzhou Intermediate People's Court to the California Superior Court, United States.  
December 26, 1985
