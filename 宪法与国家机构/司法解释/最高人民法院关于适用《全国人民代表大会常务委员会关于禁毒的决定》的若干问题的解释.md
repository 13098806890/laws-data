# 最高人民法院关于适用《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于禁毒的决定》的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕30号  
**公布日期**：1994-12-20

> 根据《决定》第四条第一款的规定，掩饰、隐瞒毒赃性质、来源罪，是指明知是出售毒品所得的财物而通过金融机构中转、投资等方式，掩盖其非法性质和来源，或者明知是出售毒品所得的财物而有意向司法机关隐瞒其非法性质和来源的行为。 本罪与窝藏毒赃罪的区别，在于行为人掩饰、隐瞒的是财物的非法性质和来源，而不是财物本身。 七、非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪 根据《决定》第五条第一款的规定，非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪，是指违反国家规定，运输、携带、邮寄醋酸酐、乙醚、三氯甲烷或者其他经常用于制造麻醉药品、精神药品的化学物品进出国（边）境的行为。 明知他人收买上述物品是为了非法运输、携带进出境，仍向其提供或者出售的，以非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪的共犯论处。 八、非法种植毒品原植物罪 根据《决定》第六条的规定，非法种植毒品原植物罪，是指明知是罂粟、大麻、古柯树等毒品原植物而非法种植且数量较大，或者经公安机关处理后又种植，或者抗拒铲除的行为。 向明知是非法种植毒品原植物的人出售较大数量毒品原植物种子的，以非法种植毒品原植物罪论处。 认定非法种植毒品原植物罪，要注意与制造毒品罪区别开来。前者是指种植毒品原植物的行为，后者是指将毒品原植物进行加工、提炼，制造毒品的行为。 非法种植毒品原植物数量较大，又以其为原料制造毒品的，应当以制造毒品罪从重处罚。 非法种植毒品原植物数量较大，又实施其他制造毒品行为的，应当分别定非法种植毒品原植物罪和制造毒品罪，实行并罚。 九、引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸毒罪 根据《决定》第七条第一款的规定，引诱、教唆他人吸毒，是指通过向他人宣扬吸食、注射毒品后的感受等方法，诱使、唆使他人吸食、注射毒品的行为。欺骗他人吸毒，是指用隐瞒事实真相或者制造假象等方法使他人吸食、注射毒品的行为。 本罪是选择性罪名。实施了引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品行为之一的，即以该行为确定罪名。实施了其中两种以上行为的，将所实施行为并列为一个罪名，不实行并罚。 被引诱、教唆、欺骗的人吸食、注射毒品后是否成瘾，不影响本罪的成立。

### 正文

七、非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪  
根据《决定》第五条第一款的规定，非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪，是指违反国家规定，运输、携带、邮寄醋酸酐、乙醚、三氯甲烷或者其他经常用于制造麻醉药品、精神药品的化学物品进出国（边）境的行为。  
明知他人收买上述物品是为了非法运输、携带进出境，仍向其提供或者出售的，以非法运输、携带制毒物品进出境罪的共犯论处。

**Article None** 7. Crime of Illegally Transporting or Carrying Precursor Chemicals into or out of the Territory  
According to the provisions of Article 5, Paragraph 1 of the Decision, the crime of illegally transporting or carrying precursor chemicals into or out of the territory refers to the act of transporting, carrying, or mailing acetic anhydride, ether, chloroform, or other chemical substances frequently used in the manufacture of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances into or out of the national (border) territory in violation of state regulations.  
Any person who, knowing that another person purchases the above-mentioned substances for the purpose of illegally transporting or carrying them into or out of the territory, still provides or sells them to that person shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of illegally transporting or carrying precursor chemicals into or out of the territory.

八、非法种植毒品原植物罪  
根据《决定》第六条的规定，非法种植毒品原植物罪，是指明知是罂粟、大麻、古柯树等毒品原植物而非法种植且数量较大，或者经公安机关处理后又种植，或者抗拒铲除的行为。  
向明知是非法种植毒品原植物的人出售较大数量毒品原植物种子的，以非法种植毒品原植物罪论处。  
认定非法种植毒品原植物罪，要注意与制造毒品罪区别开来。前者是指种植毒品原植物的行为，后者是指将毒品原植物进行加工、提炼，制造毒品的行为。  
非法种植毒品原植物数量较大，又以其为原料制造毒品的，应当以制造毒品罪从重处罚。  
非法种植毒品原植物数量较大，又实施其他制造毒品行为的，应当分别定非法种植毒品原植物罪和制造毒品罪，实行并罚。

**Article None** 8. Crime of Illegally Planting Narcotic Raw Plants  
According to the provisions of Article 6 of the Decision, the crime of illegally planting narcotic raw plants refers to the act of illegally planting opium poppy, cannabis, coca trees, or other narcotic raw plants in relatively large quantities while knowing the nature of such plants, or planting them after having been dealt with by a public security organ, or resisting the eradication of such plants.  
Any person who sells relatively large quantities of seeds of narcotic raw plants to a person whom he or she knows is illegally planting such plants shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally planting narcotic raw plants.  
In determining the crime of illegally planting narcotic raw plants, attention shall be paid to distinguishing it from the crime of manufacturing narcotic drugs. The former refers to the act of planting narcotic raw plants, while the latter refers to the act of processing and refining narcotic raw plants to manufacture narcotic drugs.  
If a person illegally plants narcotic raw plants in relatively large quantities and uses them as raw materials to manufacture narcotic drugs, he or she shall be given a severe punishment for the crime of manufacturing narcotic drugs.  
If a person illegally plants narcotic raw plants in relatively large quantities and also commits other acts of manufacturing narcotic drugs, he or she shall be separately convicted of the crime of illegally planting narcotic raw plants and the crime of manufacturing narcotic drugs, and the punishments shall be concurrently imposed.

九、引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸毒罪  
根据《决定》第七条第一款的规定，引诱、教唆他人吸毒，是指通过向他人宣扬吸食、注射毒品后的感受等方法，诱使、唆使他人吸食、注射毒品的行为。欺骗他人吸毒，是指用隐瞒事实真相或者制造假象等方法使他人吸食、注射毒品的行为。  
本罪是选择性罪名。实施了引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品行为之一的，即以该行为确定罪名。实施了其中两种以上行为的，将所实施行为并列为一个罪名，不实行并罚。  
被引诱、教唆、欺骗的人吸食、注射毒品后是否成瘾，不影响本罪的成立。

**Article None** 9. Crime of Inducing, Abetting, or Deceiving Others into Drug Use  
According to the provisions of Article 7, Paragraph 1 of the Decision, inducing or abetting others to use drugs refers to the act of enticing or instigating another person to ingest or inject drugs by, among other methods, propagating the sensations experienced after drug ingestion or injection. Deceiving others into drug use refers to the act of causing another person to ingest or inject drugs by means of concealing the true facts or creating false impressions.  
This crime is a selective charge. If a person commits one of the acts of inducing, abetting, or deceiving another person to ingest or inject drugs, the charge shall be determined according to that act. If the person commits two or more of such acts, the acts committed shall be listed together as one charge, and punishments shall not be concurrently imposed.  
Whether the person who is induced, abetted, or deceived becomes addicted after ingesting or injecting drugs shall not affect the establishment of this crime.

十、强迫他人吸毒罪  
根据《决定》第七条第二款的规定，强迫他人吸毒罪，是指违背他人意志，使用暴力、胁迫或者其他方法，迫使他人吸食、注射毒品的行为。  
被强迫的人吸食、注射毒品后是否成瘾，不影响本罪的成立。

**Article None** 10. Crime of Forcing Others to Use Drugs  
According to the provisions of Article 7, Paragraph 2 of the Decision, the crime of forcing others to use drugs refers to the act of compelling another person to ingest or inject drugs against his or her will by using violence, coercion, or other methods.  
Whether the person who is forced becomes addicted after ingesting or injecting drugs shall not affect the establishment of this crime.

十一、容留他人吸毒并出售毒品罪  
根据《决定》第九条的规定，容留他人吸毒并出售毒品罪，是指为他人吸食、注射毒品提供场所，并向其出售毒品的行为。  
容留他人吸食、注射毒品的人数和次数的多少，以及出售毒品数量的多少，不影响本罪的成立，但是应当作为量刑情节予以考虑。对犯本罪未经处理的，其出售毒品数量应累计计算。

**Article None** 11. Crime of Sheltering Others for Drug Use and Selling Drugs  
According to the provisions of Article 9 of the Decision, the crime of sheltering others for drug use and selling drugs refers to the act of providing a venue for another person to ingest or inject drugs and selling drugs to that person.  
The number of persons sheltered for drug ingestion or injection, the frequency of such acts, and the quantity of drugs sold shall not affect the establishment of this crime, but shall be considered as circumstances for sentencing. If the offender has not been dealt with for this crime, the quantity of drugs sold shall be calculated cumulatively.

十二、非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品罪  
根据《决定》第十条第二款的规定，非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品罪，是指依法从事生产、运输、管理、使用国家管制的麻醉药品、精神药品的单位和人员，明知他人是吸食、注射毒品的人，而向其提供国家管制的麻醉药品、精神药品的行为。  
提供毒品的对象，只能是吸食、注射毒品的人。如果明知对方是毒品犯罪分子，而向其提供国家管制的麻醉药品、精神药品的，则构成有关的毒品犯罪的共犯。

**Article None** 12. Crime of Illegally Providing Narcotic Drugs or Psychotropic Substances  
According to the provisions of Article 10, Paragraph 2 of the Decision, the crime of illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances refers to the act of a unit or person that is, according to law, engaged in the production, transportation, management, or use of state-controlled narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances, providing such substances to a person whom he or she knows is a drug user (one who ingests or injects drugs).  
The recipients of the provided drugs may only be drug users. If a person provides state-controlled narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances to another person whom he or she knows is a drug criminal, he or she shall constitute an accomplice in the relevant drug crime.

十三、《决定》第十一条第二款的适用  
《决定》第十一条第二款规定，因走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有毒品罪被判过刑，又犯《决定》规定之罪的，从重处罚。这是指凡因走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有毒品罪被判过刑又犯《决定》规定之罪的，无论是否构成累犯，一律依照《决定》第十一条第二款的规定从重处罚。

**Article None** 13. Application of Article 11, Paragraph 2 of the Decision  
Article 11, Paragraph 2 of the Decision provides that anyone who has been previously sentenced for the crime of smuggling, trafficking, transporting, manufacturing, or illegally possessing narcotic drugs and again commits a crime specified in the Decision shall be given a severe punishment. This means that, regardless of whether the person constitutes a recidivist, anyone who, having been previously sentenced for the crime of smuggling, trafficking, transporting, manufacturing, or illegally possessing narcotic drugs, again commits a crime specified in the Decision shall, in all cases, be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of Article 11, Paragraph 2 of the Decision.

十四、《决定》第十二条的适用  
《决定》第十二条规定的“由非法所得所获得的收益”，是指用毒品犯罪的非法所得，通过合法手段或者非法手段所获得的一切收益。  
《决定》第十二条规定的“供犯罪使用的财物，一律没收”，是对刑法第六十条的修改补充。《决定》施行后判处的毒品犯罪案件，对于供犯罪使用的财物，应当依照《决定》的规定一律予以没收，而不限于没收“供犯罪所用的本人财物”。

**Article None** 14. Application of Article 12 of the Decision  
The "income gained from illegal proceeds" as specified in Article 12 of the Decision refers to all income obtained through lawful or unlawful means using the illegal proceeds from drug crimes.  
The provision in Article 12 of the Decision that "all property used for the commission of a crime shall be confiscated" constitutes an amendment and supplement to Article 60 of the Criminal Law. In drug crime cases adjudicated after the Decision comes into force, property used for the commission of a crime shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Decision, be confiscated in all cases, without being limited to the confiscation of "the offender's own property used for the commission of the crime."

十五、《决定》第十四条的适用  
《决定》第十四条的规定，是指毒品犯罪分子在犯《决定》规定之罪后被司法机关发现并予以审查时（包括侦查、起诉、审判阶段），检举、揭发其他毒品犯罪活动或者其他毒品犯罪分子（含同案犯）罪行得到证实的，或者提供重要线索、证据，从而得以侦破其他毒品犯罪案件的，或者协助司法机关缉捕其他毒品犯罪分子（含同案犯）的，属于有立功表现，可以从轻、减轻处罚或者免除处罚。  
如果犯罪分子实施毒品犯罪后自首而没有上述立功表现的，则应当适用刑法第六十三条关于自首的规定。

**Article None** 15. Application of Article 14 of the Decision  
The provisions of Article 14 of the Decision mean that, when a drug criminal, after committing a crime specified in the Decision, is discovered and subjected to examination by the judicial organs (including the stages of investigation, prosecution, and adjudication), if he or she reports and exposes other drug criminal activities or other drug criminals (including co-defendants), which are verified, or provides important clues or evidence leading to the detection of other drug criminal cases, or assists the judicial organs in arresting other drug criminals (including co-defendants), such conduct shall be considered as meritorious service, and the punishment may be mitigated, reduced, or waived.  
If a criminal voluntarily surrenders after committing a drug crime but does not have the above-mentioned meritorious performance, the provisions of Article 63 of the Criminal Law on voluntary surrender shall apply.

十六、对已满十四岁不满十六岁的人走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的处理  
已满十四岁不满十六岁的人走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品，且具有《决定》第二条第一款和第二款规定的情形之一的，属于刑法第十四条第二款中规定的“其他严重破坏社会秩序罪”，应当负刑事责任。但是，在处理具体案件时，应当根据案件的不同情况区别对待，对于被利用、教唆、胁迫、诱骗参加上述毒品犯罪活动的已满十四岁不满十六岁的人，一般可以不追究其刑事责任，依照刑法第十四条第四款的规定处理。

**Article None** 16. Handling of Persons Over 14 but Under 16 Who Smuggle, Traffic, Transport, or Manufacture Drugs  
A person over 14 but under 16 years of age who smuggles, traffics, transports, or manufactures drugs, and who falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 2, Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Decision, shall be considered as committing "other crimes seriously undermining social order" as provided in Article 14, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law, and shall bear criminal liability. However, when handling specific cases, different situations shall be treated differently. For a person over 14 but under 16 years of age who is used, abetted, coerced, or induced to participate in the above-mentioned drug criminal activities, generally, criminal liability may not be investigated, and the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 14, Paragraph 4 of the Criminal Law.

十七、对以假毒品进行犯罪的定性  
明知是假毒品而冒充毒品贩卖的，以诈骗罪定罪处罚。不知道是假毒品而当作毒品走私、贩卖、运输、窝藏的，应当以走私、贩卖、运输、窝藏毒品犯罪（未遂）定罪处罚。  
如果行为人将精制毒品稀释后贩卖，或者是土法加工毒品因提炼不纯而含有较多杂质的，不论其中有多少其他成分，只要含有毒品，就应当以毒品犯罪认定。

**Article None** 17. Characterization of Crimes Involving Fake Drugs  
Any person who knowingly uses counterfeit drugs to pass them off as genuine narcotic drugs for sale shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud. Any person who, without knowing that the substance is a counterfeit drug, smuggles, traffics, transports, or conceals it as a genuine narcotic drug shall be convicted and punished for the crime of drug smuggling, trafficking, transporting, or concealing (attempted).  
If an actor dilutes refined drugs and sells them, or processes drugs by crude methods resulting in impure extracts containing many impurities, regardless of how many other components are present, as long as it contains a narcotic drug, it shall be determined as a drug crime.

十八、对毒品犯罪中共犯的处罚原则  
对走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品犯罪集团的首要分子，应当按照犯罪集团进行毒品犯罪的总数量和其他犯罪事实确定其罪责，予以处罚。  
对共同毒品犯罪中的主犯和其他犯罪分子，应当按照其参与毒品犯罪的毒品数量和在共同犯罪中的地位、作用，分别确定其应承担的罪责，予以处罚。

**Article None** 18. Principles for Punishing Accomplices in Drug Crimes  
The ringleader of a criminal group that smuggles, traffics, transports, or manufactures narcotic drugs shall be punished by determining his or her criminal responsibility based on the total quantity of drugs involved in the drug crimes committed by the criminal group and other criminal facts.  
For a principal offender and other criminals in joint drug crimes, the criminal responsibility they shall bear shall be determined separately based on the quantity of drugs involved in the drug crimes in which they participated and their status and role in the joint crime, and they shall be punished accordingly.

十九、对查获的毒品的鉴定  
对毒品犯罪案件中查获的毒品，应当鉴定，并作出鉴定结论。  
海洛因的含量在25%以上的，可视为《决定》和本解释所指的海洛因。含量不够25%的，应当折合成含量为25%的海洛因计算数量。  
对毒品的鉴定结论有疑义的，应当补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。

**Article None** 19. Expert Evaluation of Seized Drugs  
Drugs seized in drug crime cases shall be subjected to expert evaluation, and an expert conclusion shall be made.  
If the heroin content is 25% or more, it may be regarded as heroin as referred to in the Decision and this Interpretation. If the content is less than 25%, the quantity shall be calculated by converting it into heroin with a content of 25%.  
If the expert conclusion on the drugs is in doubt, a supplementary evaluation or a re-evaluation shall be conducted.

二十、办理毒品犯罪案件制作司法文书时对法律条文的援引  
鉴于《决定》已对刑法第一百七十一条和《关于严惩严重破坏经济的罪犯的决定》第一条第（一）项关于贩毒罪的规定以及《关于惩治走私罪的补充规定》第一条有关走私毒品的规定进行了修改、补充，因此，《决定》公布施行后适用《决定》判处的案件，在司法文书中不再引用上述法律条款，而应当直接援用《决定》的有关条文。  
1994年12月20日

**Article None** 20. Citation of Legal Provisions in Judicial Documents for Drug Crime Cases  
In view of the fact that the Decision has amended and supplemented the provisions of Article 171 of the Criminal Law, Article 1, Item (1) of the Decision on Severely Punishing Criminals Who Seriously Undermine the Economy regarding the crime of drug trafficking, and Article 1 of the Supplementary Provisions on the Punishment of the Crime of Smuggling regarding the crime of drug smuggling, after the Decision is promulgated and comes into force, for cases adjudicated under the Decision, the above-mentioned legal provisions shall no longer be cited in judicial documents; instead, the relevant articles of the Decision shall be directly invoked.  
December 20, 1994
