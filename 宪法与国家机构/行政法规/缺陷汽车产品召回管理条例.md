# 缺陷汽车产品召回管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2013-01-01

> (2012年10月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第626号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了规范缺陷汽车产品召回，加强监督管理，保障人身、财产安全，制定本条例。 第二条　在中国境内生产、销售的汽车和汽车挂车(以下统称汽车产品)的召回及其监督管理，适用本条例。 第三条　本条例所称缺陷，是指由于设计、制造、标识等原因导致的在同一批次、型号或者类别的汽车产品中普遍存在的不符合保障人身、财产安全的国家标准、行业标准的情形或者其他危及人身、财产安全的不合理的危险。 本条例所称召回，是指汽车产品生产者对其已售出的汽车产品采取措施消除缺陷的活动。 第四条　国务院产品质量监督部门负责全国缺陷汽车产品召回的监督管理工作。 国务院有关部门在各自职责范围内负责缺陷汽车产品召回的相关监督管理工作。 第五条　国务院产品质量监督部门根据工作需要，可以委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府产品质量监督部门负责缺陷汽车产品召回监督管理的部分工作。 国务院产品质量监督部门缺陷产品召回技术机构按照国务院产品质量监督部门的规定，承担缺陷汽车产品召回的具体技术工作。 第六条　任何单位和个人有权向产品质量监督部门投诉汽车产品可能存在的缺陷，国务院产品质量监督部门应当以便于公众知晓的方式向社会公布受理投诉的电话、电子邮箱和通信地址。 国务院产品质量监督部门应当建立缺陷汽车产品召回信息管理系统，收集汇总、分析处理有关缺陷汽车产品信息。 产品质量监督部门、汽车产品主管部门、商务主管部门、海关、公安机关交通管理部门、交通运输主管部门等有关部门应当建立汽车产品的生产、销售、进口、登记检验、维修、消费者投诉、召回等信息的共享机制。 第七条　产品质量监督部门和有关部门、机构及其工作人员对履行本条例规定职责所知悉的商业秘密和个人信息，不得泄露。 第八条　对缺陷汽车产品，生产者应当依照本条例全部召回；生产者未实施召回的，国务院产品质量监督部门应当依照本条例责令其召回。 本条例所称生产者，是指在中国境内依法设立的生产汽车产品并以其名义颁发产品合格证的企业。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范缺陷汽车产品召回，加强监督管理，保障人身、财产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating the recall of defective motor vehicle products, strengthening supervision and administration, and ensuring personal and property safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中国境内生产、销售的汽车和汽车挂车(以下统称汽车产品)的召回及其监督管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the recall of motor vehicles and motor vehicle trailers (hereinafter collectively referred to as motor vehicle products) produced and sold within the territory of China, and to the supervision and administration thereof.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称缺陷，是指由于设计、制造、标识等原因导致的在同一批次、型号或者类别的汽车产品中普遍存在的不符合保障人身、财产安全的国家标准、行业标准的情形或者其他危及人身、财产安全的不合理的危险。  
本条例所称召回，是指汽车产品生产者对其已售出的汽车产品采取措施消除缺陷的活动。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "defect" means a non-compliance with the national standards or industry standards for ensuring personal and property safety or other unreasonable risks endangering personal and property safety that commonly exist in motor vehicle products of the same batch, model or category due to reasons such as design, manufacturing, or marking.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "recall" means an activity in which a producer of motor vehicle products takes measures to eliminate defects in its motor vehicle products that have been sold.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院产品质量监督部门负责全国缺陷汽车产品召回的监督管理工作。  
国务院有关部门在各自职责范围内负责缺陷汽车产品召回的相关监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the recall of defective motor vehicle products nationwide.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant supervision and administration of the recall of defective motor vehicle products.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院产品质量监督部门根据工作需要，可以委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府产品质量监督部门负责缺陷汽车产品召回监督管理的部分工作。  
国务院产品质量监督部门缺陷产品召回技术机构按照国务院产品质量监督部门的规定，承担缺陷汽车产品召回的具体技术工作。

**Article 5** The product quality supervision department of the State Council may, as necessary for work, entrust the product quality supervision departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government with undertaking part of the work concerning the supervision and administration of the recall of defective motor vehicle products.  
The technical agency for product recall of the product quality supervision department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the product quality supervision department of the State Council, undertake the specific technical work for the recall of defective motor vehicle products.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人有权向产品质量监督部门投诉汽车产品可能存在的缺陷，国务院产品质量监督部门应当以便于公众知晓的方式向社会公布受理投诉的电话、电子邮箱和通信地址。  
国务院产品质量监督部门应当建立缺陷汽车产品召回信息管理系统，收集汇总、分析处理有关缺陷汽车产品信息。  
产品质量监督部门、汽车产品主管部门、商务主管部门、海关、公安机关交通管理部门、交通运输主管部门等有关部门应当建立汽车产品的生产、销售、进口、登记检验、维修、消费者投诉、召回等信息的共享机制。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual shall have the right to lodge a complaint with the product quality supervision department about a possible defect in a motor vehicle product. The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall make public, in a manner that is convenient for the public to know, the telephone number, email address, and correspondence address for accepting complaints.  
The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall establish an information management system for the recall of defective motor vehicle products to collect, summarize, analyze, and process information concerning defective motor vehicle products.  
The product quality supervision department, the competent department for motor vehicle products, the competent department for commerce, the customs, the traffic management department of the public security organ, and the competent department for transportation, among other relevant departments, shall establish a mechanism for sharing information on the production, sale, import, registration inspection, repair, consumer complaints, and recall of motor vehicle products.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　产品质量监督部门和有关部门、机构及其工作人员对履行本条例规定职责所知悉的商业秘密和个人信息，不得泄露。

**Article 7** The product quality supervision department, relevant departments and institutions, and their staff members shall not disclose any trade secret or personal information that they become aware of in the performance of their duties under these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对缺陷汽车产品，生产者应当依照本条例全部召回；生产者未实施召回的，国务院产品质量监督部门应当依照本条例责令其召回。  
本条例所称生产者，是指在中国境内依法设立的生产汽车产品并以其名义颁发产品合格证的企业。  
从中国境外进口汽车产品到境内销售的企业，视为前款所称的生产者。

**Article 8** With respect to defective motor vehicle products, the producer shall recall all of them in accordance with these Regulations; if the producer fails to implement a recall, the product quality supervision department of the State Council shall order it to do so in accordance with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "producer" means an enterprise established according to law within the territory of China that produces motor vehicle products and issues product conformity certificates in its own name.  
An enterprise that imports motor vehicle products into China from outside the territory for domestic sale shall be deemed to be a producer as mentioned in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　生产者应当建立并保存汽车产品设计、制造、标识、检验等方面的信息记录以及汽车产品初次销售的车主信息记录，保存期不得少于10年。

**Article 9** A producer shall establish and maintain records of information on the design, manufacturing, marking, and inspection of motor vehicle products, as well as records of information on the owners of motor vehicle products at the time of their initial sale. The retention period shall be no less than 10 years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　生产者应当将下列信息报国务院产品质量监督部门备案：  
(一)生产者基本信息；  
(二)汽车产品技术参数和汽车产品初次销售的车主信息；  
(三)因汽车产品存在危及人身、财产安全的故障而发生修理、更换、退货的信息；  
(四)汽车产品在中国境外实施召回的信息；  
(五)国务院产品质量监督部门要求备案的其他信息。

**Article 10** A producer shall report the following information to the product quality supervision department of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) basic information on the producer;  
(2) technical parameters of the motor vehicle product and information on the owner at the time of initial sale of the motor vehicle product;  
(3) information on repairs, replacements, or returns due to faults in the motor vehicle product that endanger personal or property safety;  
(4) information on recalls of motor vehicle products implemented outside China;  
(5) other information required to be recorded by the product quality supervision department of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　销售、租赁、维修汽车产品的经营者(以下统称经营者)应当按照国务院产品质量监督部门的规定建立并保存汽车产品相关信息记录，保存期不得少于5年。  
经营者获知汽车产品存在缺陷的，应当立即停止销售、租赁、使用缺陷汽车产品，并协助生产者实施召回。  
经营者应当向国务院产品质量监督部门报告和向生产者通报所获知的汽车产品可能存在缺陷的相关信息。

**Article 11** Any business operator that sells, leases, or repairs motor vehicle products (hereinafter collectively referred to as "business operator") shall, in accordance with the provisions of the product quality supervision department of the State Council, establish and maintain records of relevant information on motor vehicle products. The retention period shall be no less than 5 years.  
If a business operator becomes aware that a motor vehicle product has a defect, it shall immediately cease selling, leasing, or using the defective motor vehicle product and shall assist the producer in implementing the recall.  
A business operator shall report to the product quality supervision department of the State Council and notify the producer of any information it becomes aware of indicating that a motor vehicle product may have a defect.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　生产者获知汽车产品可能存在缺陷的，应当立即组织调查分析，并如实向国务院产品质量监督部门报告调查分析结果。  
生产者确认汽车产品存在缺陷的，应当立即停止生产、销售、进口缺陷汽车产品，并实施召回。

**Article 12** If a producer becomes aware that a motor vehicle product may have a defect, it shall immediately organize an investigation and analysis, and shall truthfully report the results of the investigation and analysis to the product quality supervision department of the State Council.  
If a producer confirms that a motor vehicle product has a defect, it shall immediately cease production, sale, and import of the defective motor vehicle product and shall implement a recall.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院产品质量监督部门获知汽车产品可能存在缺陷的，应当立即通知生产者开展调查分析；生产者未按照通知开展调查分析的，国务院产品质量监督部门应当开展缺陷调查。  
国务院产品质量监督部门认为汽车产品可能存在会造成严重后果的缺陷的，可以直接开展缺陷调查。

**Article 13** If the product quality supervision department of the State Council becomes aware that a motor vehicle product may have a defect, it shall immediately notify the producer to conduct an investigation and analysis; if the producer fails to conduct the investigation and analysis as notified, the product quality supervision department of the State Council shall conduct a defect investigation.  
If the product quality supervision department of the State Council considers that a motor vehicle product may have a defect that could cause serious consequences, it may directly conduct a defect investigation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院产品质量监督部门开展缺陷调查，可以进入生产者、经营者的生产经营场所进行现场调查，查阅、复制相关资料和记录，向相关单位和个人了解汽车产品可能存在缺陷的情况。  
生产者应当配合缺陷调查，提供调查需要的有关资料、产品和专用设备。经营者应当配合缺陷调查，提供调查需要的有关资料。  
国务院产品质量监督部门不得将生产者、经营者提供的资料、产品和专用设备用于缺陷调查所需的技术检测和鉴定以外的用途。

**Article 14** When conducting a defect investigation, the product quality supervision department of the State Council may enter the production and business premises of the producer or business operator to conduct on-site investigations, consult and copy relevant materials and records, and inquire with relevant entities or individuals about the circumstances under which the motor vehicle product may have a defect.  
The producer shall cooperate with the defect investigation and provide the relevant materials, products, and special equipment required for the investigation. The business operator shall cooperate with the defect investigation and provide the relevant materials required for the investigation.  
The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall not use the materials, products, or special equipment provided by the producer or business operator for any purpose other than technical testing and expert evaluation required for the defect investigation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院产品质量监督部门调查认为汽车产品存在缺陷的，应当通知生产者实施召回。  
生产者认为其汽车产品不存在缺陷的，可以自收到通知之日起15个工作日内向国务院产品质量监督部门提出异议，并提供证明材料。国务院产品质量监督部门应当组织与生产者无利害关系的专家对证明材料进行论证，必要时对汽车产品进行技术检测或者鉴定。  
生产者既不按照通知实施召回又不在本条第二款规定期限内提出异议的，或者经国务院产品质量监督部门依照本条第二款规定组织论证、技术检测、鉴定确认汽车产品存在缺陷的，国务院产品质量监督部门应当责令生产者实施召回；生产者应当立即停止生产、销售、进口缺陷汽车产品，并实施召回。

**Article 15** If, after investigation, the product quality supervision department of the State Council considers that a motor vehicle product has a defect, it shall notify the producer to implement a recall.  
If the producer considers that its motor vehicle product does not have a defect, it may, within 15 working days from the date of receiving the notification, raise an objection with the product quality supervision department of the State Council and provide supporting materials. The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall organize experts who have no conflict of interest with the producer to review the supporting materials and, if necessary, conduct technical testing or expert evaluation on the motor vehicle product.  
If the producer neither implements a recall in accordance with the notification nor raises an objection within the time limit specified in the second paragraph of this Article, or if the product quality supervision department of the State Council confirms that the motor vehicle product has a defect after organizing review, technical testing, and expert evaluation in accordance with the second paragraph of this Article, the product quality supervision department of the State Council shall order the producer to implement a recall; the producer shall immediately cease production, sale, and import of the defective motor vehicle product and implement the recall.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生产者实施召回，应当按照国务院产品质量监督部门的规定制定召回计划，并报国务院产品质量监督部门备案。修改已备案的召回计划应当重新备案。  
生产者应当按照召回计划实施召回。

**Article 16** When implementing a recall, a producer shall formulate a recall plan in accordance with the provisions of the product quality supervision department of the State Council and report it to the product quality supervision department of the State Council for recordation. Any modification to a recorded recall plan shall be re-recorded.  
The producer shall implement the recall in accordance with the recall plan.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　生产者应当将报国务院产品质量监督部门备案的召回计划同时通报销售者，销售者应当停止销售缺陷汽车产品。

**Article 17** The producer shall simultaneously notify the seller of the recall plan reported to the product quality supervision department of the State Council for recordation, and the seller shall cease selling the defective motor vehicle product.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　生产者实施召回，应当以便于公众知晓的方式发布信息，告知车主汽车产品存在的缺陷、避免损害发生的应急处置方法和生产者消除缺陷的措施等事项。  
国务院产品质量监督部门应当及时向社会公布已经确认的缺陷汽车产品信息以及生产者实施召回的相关信息。  
车主应当配合生产者实施召回。

**Article 18** When implementing a recall, the producer shall publish information in a manner that is convenient for the public to know, informing the owners of the defect in the motor vehicle product, the emergency measures to avoid harm, and the measures taken by the producer to eliminate the defect.  
The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall promptly make public to the society information on confirmed defective motor vehicle products and relevant information on the recall implemented by the producer.  
The owner shall cooperate with the producer in implementing the recall.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对实施召回的缺陷汽车产品，生产者应当及时采取修正或者补充标识、修理、更换、退货等措施消除缺陷。  
生产者应当承担消除缺陷的费用和必要的运送缺陷汽车产品的费用。

**Article 19** With respect to defective motor vehicle products subject to recall, the producer shall promptly take measures such as correcting or supplementing markings, repairing, replacing, or taking back the products to eliminate the defects.  
The producer shall bear the costs of eliminating the defects and the necessary costs of transporting the defective motor vehicle products.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生产者应当按照国务院产品质量监督部门的规定提交召回阶段性报告和召回总结报告。

**Article 20** The producer shall, in accordance with the provisions of the product quality supervision department of the State Council, submit periodic recall reports and a final recall summary report.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院产品质量监督部门应当对召回实施情况进行监督，并组织与生产者无利害关系的专家对生产者消除缺陷的效果进行评估。

**Article 21** The product quality supervision department of the State Council shall supervise the implementation of the recall and shall organize experts who have no conflict of interest with the producer to evaluate the effectiveness of the producer's defect elimination measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产者违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由产品质量监督部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定保存有关汽车产品、车主的信息记录；  
(二)未按照规定备案有关信息、召回计划；  
(三)未按照规定提交有关召回报告。

**Article 22** If a producer violates these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the product quality supervision department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to maintain records of information on relevant motor vehicle products and owners in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to record relevant information or recall plans in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to submit relevant recall reports in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由产品质量监督部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；情节严重的，由许可机关吊销有关许可：  
(一)生产者、经营者不配合产品质量监督部门缺陷调查；  
(二)生产者未按照已备案的召回计划实施召回；  
(三)生产者未将召回计划通报销售者。

**Article 23** If these Regulations are violated under any of the following circumstances, the product quality supervision department shall order corrections; if the party refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall also be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the licensing authority shall revoke the relevant license:  
(1) the producer or business operator fails to cooperate with the defect investigation conducted by the product quality supervision department;  
(2) the producer fails to implement the recall in accordance with the recorded recall plan;  
(3) the producer fails to notify the seller of the recall plan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　生产者违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由产品质量监督部门责令改正，处缺陷汽车产品货值金额1%以上10%以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；情节严重的，由许可机关吊销有关许可：  
(一)未停止生产、销售或者进口缺陷汽车产品；  
(二)隐瞒缺陷情况；  
(三)经责令召回拒不召回。

**Article 24** If a producer violates these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the product quality supervision department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 1% but not more than 10% of the value of the defective motor vehicle products; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall also be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the licensing authority shall revoke the relevant license:  
(1) failing to cease production, sale, or import of defective motor vehicle products;  
(2) concealing the defect situation;  
(3) refusing to recall after being ordered to do so.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，从事缺陷汽车产品召回监督管理工作的人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
(一)将生产者、经营者提供的资料、产品和专用设备用于缺陷调查所需的技术检测和鉴定以外的用途；  
(二)泄露当事人商业秘密或者个人信息；  
(三)其他玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权行为。

**Article 25** If any staff member engaged in the supervision and administration of the recall of defective motor vehicle products violates these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the staff member shall be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) using the materials, products, or special equipment provided by the producer or business operator for any purpose other than technical testing and expert evaluation required for the defect investigation;  
(2) disclosing the trade secrets or personal information of a party;  
(3) other acts of dereliction of duty, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, or abuse of power.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Any violation of these Regulations that constitutes a crime shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　汽车产品出厂时未随车装备的轮胎存在缺陷的，由轮胎的生产者负责召回。具体办法由国务院产品质量监督部门参照本条例制定。

**Article 27** If a tire that is not equipped with the motor vehicle product at the time of delivery from the factory has a defect, the producer of the tire shall be responsible for the recall. The specific measures shall be formulated by the product quality supervision department of the State Council by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生产者依照本条例召回缺陷汽车产品，不免除其依法应当承担的责任。  
汽车产品存在本条例规定的缺陷以外的质量问题的，车主有权依照产品质量法、消费者权益保护法等法律、行政法规和国家有关规定以及合同约定，要求生产者、销售者承担修理、更换、退货、赔偿损失等相应的法律责任。

**Article 28** A producer's recall of defective motor vehicle products in accordance with these Regulations shall not relieve it of the liability that it shall bear according to law.  
If a motor vehicle product has quality problems other than defects as specified in these Regulations, the owner shall have the right to demand, in accordance with the Product Quality Law, the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, and other laws, administrative regulations, relevant state provisions, and the contract, that the producer or seller bear the corresponding legal liability for repair, replacement, return, compensation for losses, etc.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自2013年1月1日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2013.
