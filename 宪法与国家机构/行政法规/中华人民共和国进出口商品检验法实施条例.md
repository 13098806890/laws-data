**法律部门**：宪法相关法

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国进出口商品检验法》（以下简称商检法）的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection (hereinafter referred to as the Commodity Inspection Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海关总署主管全国进出口商品检验工作。  
海关总署设在省、自治区、直辖市以及进出口商品的口岸、集散地的出入境检验检疫机构及其分支机构（以下简称出入境检验检疫机构），管理所负责地区的进出口商品检验工作。

**Article 2** The General Administration of Customs shall be in charge of the inspection of import and export commodities nationwide.  
The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions and their branches (hereinafter referred to as the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions) established by the General Administration of Customs in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as at ports and distribution centers for import and export commodities, shall administer the inspection of import and export commodities in the areas under their respective responsibilities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海关总署应当依照商检法第四条规定，制定、调整必须实施检验的进出口商品目录（以下简称目录）并公布实施。  
目录应当至少在实施之日30日前公布；在紧急情况下，应当不迟于实施之日公布。  
海关总署制定、调整目录时，应当征求国务院对外贸易主管部门等有关方面的意见。

**Article 3** The General Administration of Customs shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 4 of the Commodity Inspection Law, formulate and adjust the Catalogue of Import and Export Commodities Subject to Compulsory Inspection (hereinafter referred to as the Catalogue) and publish it for implementation.  
The Catalogue shall be published at least 30 days prior to the date of its implementation; in an emergency, it shall be published no later than the date of its implementation.  
When formulating or adjusting the Catalogue, the General Administration of Customs shall solicit opinions from the competent department of foreign trade of the State Council and other relevant parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　出入境检验检疫机构对列入目录的进出口商品以及法律、行政法规规定须经出入境检验检疫机构检验的其他进出口商品实施检验（以下称法定检验）。  
出入境检验检疫机构对法定检验以外的进出口商品，根据国家规定实施抽查检验。

**Article 4** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall conduct inspection on import and export commodities listed in the Catalogue and other import and export commodities that are required by laws and administrative regulations to be inspected by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions (hereinafter referred to as statutory inspection).  
The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall conduct random inspection on import and export commodities not subject to statutory inspection in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　进出口药品的质量检验、计量器具的量值检定、锅炉压力容器的安全监督检验、船舶（包括海上平台、主要船用设备及材料）和集装箱的规范检验、飞机（包括飞机发动机、机载设备）的适航检验以及核承压设备的安全检验等项目，由有关法律、行政法规规定的机构实施检验。

**Article 5** The quality inspection of imported and exported pharmaceuticals, the verification of measuring instruments, the safety supervision inspection of boilers and pressure vessels, the specification inspection of ships (including offshore platforms, main marine equipment and materials) and containers, the airworthiness inspection of aircraft (including aircraft engines and airborne equipment), and the safety inspection of nuclear pressure equipment shall be conducted by the institutions specified by relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进出境的样品、礼品、暂时进出境的货物以及其他非贸易性物品，免予检验。但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
列入目录的进出口商品符合国家规定的免予检验条件的，由收货人、发货人或者生产企业申请，经海关总署审查批准，出入境检验检疫机构免予检验。  
免予检验的具体办法，由海关总署商有关部门制定。

**Article 6** Samples, gifts, goods temporarily entering or leaving the country, and other non-trade items shall be exempted from inspection, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where import or export commodities listed in the Catalogue meet the conditions for exemption from inspection as prescribed by the state, the consignee, consignor, or production enterprise may apply, and upon examination and approval by the General Administration of Customs, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall exempt them from inspection.  
The specific measures for exemption from inspection shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs in consultation with relevant departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　法定检验的进出口商品，由出入境检验检疫机构依照商检法第七条规定实施检验。  
海关总署根据进出口商品检验工作的实际需要和国际标准，可以制定进出口商品检验方法的技术规范和行业标准。  
进出口商品检验依照或者参照的技术规范、标准以及检验方法的技术规范和标准，应当至少在实施之日6个月前公布；在紧急情况下，应当不迟于实施之日公布。

**Article 7** Statutory inspection of import and export commodities shall be conducted by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions in accordance with the provisions of Article 7 of the Commodity Inspection Law.  
The General Administration of Customs may, based on the actual needs of import and export commodity inspection work and international standards, formulate technical specifications and industry standards for inspection methods of import and export commodities.  
The technical specifications and standards followed or referenced in the inspection of import and export commodities, as well as the technical specifications and standards for inspection methods, shall be published at least six months prior to the date of their implementation; in an emergency, they shall be published no later than the date of their implementation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出入境检验检疫机构根据便利对外贸易的需要，对进出口企业实施分类管理，并按照根据国际通行的合格评定程序确定的检验监管方式，对进出口商品实施检验。

**Article 8** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, based on the need to facilitate foreign trade, implement classified management for import and export enterprises, and conduct inspection on import and export commodities in accordance with the inspection and supervision methods determined by internationally accepted conformity assessment procedures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出入境检验检疫机构对进出口商品实施检验的内容，包括是否符合安全、卫生、健康、环境保护、防止欺诈等要求以及相关的品质、数量、重量等项目。

**Article 9** The contents of inspection conducted by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions on import and export commodities shall include whether they meet the requirements for safety, sanitation, health, environmental protection, prevention of fraud, and other related items such as quality, quantity, and weight.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　出入境检验检疫机构依照商检法的规定，对实施许可制度和国家规定必须经过认证的进出口商品实行验证管理，查验单证，核对证货是否相符。  
实行验证管理的进出口商品目录，由海关总署商有关部门后制定、调整并公布。

**Article 10** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Commodity Inspection Law, implement verification management for import and export commodities subject to a licensing system or those that must undergo certification as required by state provisions, examine the documents, and verify whether the certificates and goods are consistent.  
The catalogue of import and export commodities subject to verification management shall be formulated, adjusted, and published by the General Administration of Customs after consultation with relevant departments.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进出口商品的收货人或者发货人可以自行办理报检手续，也可以委托代理报检企业办理报检手续；采用快件方式进出口商品的，收货人或者发货人应当委托出入境快件运营企业办理报检手续。

**Article 11** The consignee or consignor of import or export commodities may handle the declaration procedures on its own, or may entrust an agent declaration enterprise to handle the declaration procedures; where import or export commodities are shipped by express delivery, the consignee or consignor shall entrust an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise to handle the declaration procedures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进出口商品的收货人或者发货人办理报检手续，应当依法向出入境检验检疫机构备案。

**Article 12** The consignee or consignor of import or export commodities, when handling the declaration procedures, shall file a record with the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　代理报检企业接受进出口商品的收货人或者发货人的委托，以委托人的名义办理报检手续的，应当向出入境检验检疫机构提交授权委托书，遵守本条例对委托人的各项规定；以自己的名义办理报检手续的，应当承担与收货人或者发货人相同的法律责任。  
出入境快件运营企业接受进出口商品的收货人或者发货人的委托，应当以自己的名义办理报检手续，承担与收货人或者发货人相同的法律责任。  
委托人委托代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业办理报检手续的，应当向代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业提供所委托报检事项的真实情况；代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业接受委托人的委托办理报检手续的，应当对委托人所提供情况的真实性进行合理审查。

**Article 13** Where an agent declaration enterprise, upon entrustment by the consignee or consignor of import or export commodities, handles the declaration procedures in the name of the principal, it shall submit a power of attorney to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions and comply with all provisions of these Regulations applicable to the principal; where it handles the declaration procedures in its own name, it shall bear the same legal liability as the consignee or consignor.  
Where an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise, upon entrustment by the consignee or consignor of import or export commodities, handles the declaration procedures, it shall do so in its own name and bear the same legal liability as the consignee or consignor.  
Where the principal entrusts an agent declaration enterprise or an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise to handle the declaration procedures, the principal shall provide the true information concerning the declared matters to the agent declaration enterprise or the entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise; where an agent declaration enterprise or an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise accepts the entrustment and handles the declaration procedures, it shall conduct a reasonable review of the authenticity of the information provided by the principal.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海关总署建立进出口商品风险预警机制，通过收集进出口商品检验方面的信息，进行风险评估，确定风险的类型，采取相应的风险预警措施及快速反应措施。  
海关总署和出入境检验检疫机构应当及时向有关方面提供进出口商品检验方面的信息。

**Article 14** The General Administration of Customs shall establish a risk early warning mechanism for import and export commodities, by collecting information on the inspection of import and export commodities, conducting risk assessment, determining the types of risks, and taking corresponding risk early warning measures and rapid response measures.  
The General Administration of Customs and the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall promptly provide relevant parties with information on the inspection of import and export commodities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　出入境检验检疫机构工作人员依法执行职务，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，任何单位和个人不得非法干预和阻挠。

**Article 15** When staff members of the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions perform their duties according to law, the relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate, and no entity or individual may illegally intervene or obstruct.

### 第二章　进口商品的检验

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　法定检验的进口商品的收货人应当持合同、发票、装箱单、提单等必要的凭证和相关批准文件，向报关地的出入境检验检疫机构报检；通关放行后20日内，收货人应当依照[本条例第十八条](#art-18)的规定，向出入境检验检疫机构申请检验。法定检验的进口商品未经检验的，不准销售，不准使用。  
进口实行验证管理的商品，收货人应当向报关地的出入境检验检疫机构申请验证。出入境检验检疫机构按照海关总署的规定实施验证。

**Article 16** The consignee of import commodities subject to statutory inspection shall, holding necessary documents such as the contract, invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and relevant approval documents, make a declaration to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution at the place of customs declaration; within 20 days after customs clearance and release, the consignee shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for inspection in accordance with Article 18 of these Regulations. Import commodities subject to statutory inspection that have not been inspected shall not be sold or used.  
Where importing commodities subject to verification management, the consignee shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution at the place of customs declaration for verification. The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall conduct verification in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　法定检验的进口商品、实行验证管理的进口商品，海关按照规定办理海关通关手续。

**Article 17** For import commodities subject to statutory inspection and import commodities subject to verification management, the customs shall handle customs clearance procedures in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　法定检验的进口商品应当在收货人报检时申报的目的地检验。  
大宗散装商品、易腐烂变质商品、可用作原料的固体废物以及已发生残损、短缺的商品，应当在卸货口岸检验。  
对前两款规定的进口商品，海关总署可以根据便利对外贸易和进出口商品检验工作的需要，指定在其他地点检验。

**Article 18** Import commodities subject to statutory inspection shall be inspected at the destination declared by the consignee at the time of declaration.  
Bulk commodities, perishable commodities, solid waste that can be used as raw materials, and commodities that have suffered damage or shortage shall be inspected at the port of discharge.  
For the import commodities specified in the preceding two paragraphs, the General Administration of Customs may, based on the need to facilitate foreign trade and the work of import and export commodity inspection, designate other locations for inspection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　除法律、行政法规另有规定外，法定检验的进口商品经检验，涉及人身财产安全、健康、环境保护项目不合格的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令当事人销毁，或者出具退货处理通知单，办理退运手续；其他项目不合格的，可以在出入境检验检疫机构的监督下进行技术处理，经重新检验合格的，方可销售或者使用。当事人申请出入境检验检疫机构出证的，出入境检验检疫机构应当及时出证。  
出入境检验检疫机构对检验不合格的进口成套设备及其材料，签发不准安装使用通知书。经技术处理，并经出入境检验检疫机构重新检验合格的，方可安装使用。

**Article 19** Unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations, if, after inspection, import commodities subject to statutory inspection fail to meet the requirements concerning personal and property safety, health, or environmental protection, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order the party to destroy them, or issue a notice of return for processing and go through the procedures for return shipment; if other items are found to be non-conforming, technical treatment may be carried out under the supervision of the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, and the commodities may be sold or used only after they pass re-inspection. If the party applies to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for the issuance of a certificate, the institution shall issue the certificate promptly.  
For import complete sets of equipment and their materials that fail inspection, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall issue a notice prohibiting installation and use. They may be installed and used only after technical treatment and after they pass re-inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　法定检验以外的进口商品，经出入境检验检疫机构抽查检验不合格的，依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处理。  
实行验证管理的进口商品，经出入境检验检疫机构验证不合格的，参照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定处理或者移交有关部门处理。  
法定检验以外的进口商品的收货人，发现进口商品质量不合格或者残损、短缺，申请出证的，出入境检验检疫机构或者其他检验机构应当在检验后及时出证。

**Article 20** Where import commodities not subject to statutory inspection are found to be non-conforming after random inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, they shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 19 of these Regulations.  
Where import commodities subject to verification management are found to be non-conforming after verification by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, they shall be dealt with by reference to Article 19 of these Regulations or transferred to the relevant department for handling.  
If the consignee of import commodities not subject to statutory inspection discovers that the imported commodities are of substandard quality, damaged, or short in quantity, and applies for the issuance of a certificate, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or other inspection institution shall issue the certificate promptly after inspection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对属于法定检验范围内的关系国计民生、价值较高、技术复杂的以及其他重要的进口商品和大型成套设备，应当按照对外贸易合同约定监造、装运前检验或者监装。收货人保留到货后最终检验和索赔的权利。  
出入境检验检疫机构可以根据需要派出检验人员参加或者组织实施监造、装运前检验或者监装。

**Article 21** For import commodities within the scope of statutory inspection that are related to the national economy and people's livelihood, of high value, technically complex, or otherwise important, and for large complete sets of equipment, supervision over manufacture, pre-shipment inspection, or supervision over loading shall be carried out as agreed upon in the foreign trade contract. The consignee shall retain the right of final inspection and claim after arrival of the goods.  
The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution may, when necessary, dispatch inspection personnel to participate in or organize the implementation of supervision over manufacture, pre-shipment inspection, or supervision over loading.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对进口可用作原料的固体废物的国外供货商、国内收货人实行注册登记制度，国外供货商、国内收货人在签订对外贸易合同前，应当取得海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构的注册登记。国家对进口可用作原料的固体废物实行装运前检验制度，进口时，收货人应当提供出入境检验检疫机构或者检验机构出具的装运前检验证书。  
对价值较高，涉及人身财产安全、健康、环境保护项目的高风险进口旧机电产品，应当依照国家有关规定实施装运前检验，进口时，收货人应当提供出入境检验检疫机构或者检验机构出具的装运前检验证书。  
进口可用作原料的固体废物、国家允许进口的旧机电产品到货后，由出入境检验检疫机构依法实施检验。

**Article 22** The state shall implement a registration system for foreign suppliers and domestic consignees of solid waste imported as raw materials. Before signing a foreign trade contract, foreign suppliers and domestic consignees shall obtain registration from the General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution. The state shall implement a pre-shipment inspection system for solid waste imported as raw materials. At the time of import, the consignee shall provide a pre-shipment inspection certificate issued by an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or an inspection institution.  
For high-risk imported used mechanical and electrical products that are of high value and involve items concerning personal and property safety, health, or environmental protection, pre-shipment inspection shall be carried out in accordance with relevant state provisions. At the time of import, the consignee shall provide a pre-shipment inspection certificate issued by an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or an inspection institution.  
After the arrival of solid waste imported as raw materials and used mechanical and electrical products that are allowed to be imported by the state, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall conduct inspection according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　进口机动车辆到货后，收货人凭出入境检验检疫机构签发的进口机动车辆检验证单以及有关部门签发的其他单证向车辆管理机关申领行车牌证。在使用过程中发现有涉及人身财产安全的质量缺陷的，出入境检验检疫机构应当及时作出相应处理。

**Article 23** After the arrival of imported motor vehicles, the consignee shall apply to the vehicle administration authority for license plates and certificates based on the inspection certificate for imported motor vehicles issued by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution and other documents issued by relevant departments. If quality defects involving personal and property safety are discovered during use, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall take appropriate action promptly.

### 第三章　出口商品的检验

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　法定检验的出口商品的发货人应当在海关总署统一规定的地点和期限内，持合同等必要的凭证和相关批准文件向出入境检验检疫机构报检。法定检验的出口商品未经检验或者经检验不合格的，不准出口。  
出口商品应当在商品的生产地检验。海关总署可以根据便利对外贸易和进出口商品检验工作的需要，指定在其他地点检验。  
出口实行验证管理的商品，发货人应当向出入境检验检疫机构申请验证。出入境检验检疫机构按照海关总署的规定实施验证。

**Article 24** The consignor of export commodities subject to statutory inspection shall, within the time limit and at the place uniformly prescribed by the General Administration of Customs, hold necessary documents such as the contract and relevant approval documents to make a declaration to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution. Export commodities subject to statutory inspection that have not been inspected or have failed inspection shall not be exported.  
Export commodities shall be inspected at the place of production. The General Administration of Customs may, based on the need to facilitate foreign trade and the work of import and export commodity inspection, designate other locations for inspection.  
Where exporting commodities subject to verification management, the consignor shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for verification. The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall conduct verification in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在商品生产地检验的出口商品需要在口岸换证出口的，由商品生产地的出入境检验检疫机构按照规定签发检验换证凭单。发货人应当在规定的期限内持检验换证凭单和必要的凭证，向口岸出入境检验检疫机构申请查验。经查验合格的，由口岸出入境检验检疫机构签发货物通关单。

**Article 25** Where export commodities inspected at the place of production need to exchange certificates at the port for export, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution at the place of production shall issue a certificate exchange voucher for inspection in accordance with the provisions. The consignor shall, within the prescribed time limit, hold the certificate exchange voucher for inspection and necessary documents to apply to the port entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for inspection. If the goods pass the inspection, the port entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall issue a customs clearance certificate for goods.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　法定检验的出口商品、实行验证管理的出口商品，海关按照规定办理海关通关手续。

**Article 26** For export commodities subject to statutory inspection and export commodities subject to verification management, the customs shall handle customs clearance procedures in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　法定检验的出口商品经出入境检验检疫机构检验或者经口岸出入境检验检疫机构查验不合格的，可以在出入境检验检疫机构的监督下进行技术处理，经重新检验合格的，方准出口；不能进行技术处理或者技术处理后重新检验仍不合格的，不准出口。

**Article 27** Where export commodities subject to statutory inspection fail the inspection conducted by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or fail the inspection conducted by the port entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, technical treatment may be carried out under the supervision of the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, and the goods shall be allowed to be exported only after they pass re-inspection; if technical treatment cannot be carried out or the goods still fail re-inspection after technical treatment, they shall not be exported.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　法定检验以外的出口商品，经出入境检验检疫机构抽查检验不合格的，依照[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)的规定处理。  
实行验证管理的出口商品，经出入境检验检疫机构验证不合格的，参照[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)的规定处理或者移交有关部门处理。

**Article 28** Where export commodities not subject to statutory inspection are found to be non-conforming after random inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, they shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 27 of these Regulations.  
Where export commodities subject to verification management are found to be non-conforming after verification by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, they shall be dealt with by reference to Article 27 of these Regulations or transferred to the relevant department for handling.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　出口危险货物包装容器的生产企业，应当向出入境检验检疫机构申请包装容器的性能鉴定。包装容器经出入境检验检疫机构鉴定合格并取得性能鉴定证书的，方可用于包装危险货物。  
出口危险货物的生产企业，应当向出入境检验检疫机构申请危险货物包装容器的使用鉴定。使用未经鉴定或者经鉴定不合格的包装容器的危险货物，不准出口。

**Article 29** The production enterprise of packaging containers for the export of dangerous goods shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for performance appraisal of the packaging containers. Only packaging containers that have passed the appraisal by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution and obtained a performance appraisal certificate may be used for packaging dangerous goods.  
The production enterprise of exported dangerous goods shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for the use appraisal of packaging containers for dangerous goods. Dangerous goods packed in packaging containers that have not been appraised or have failed the appraisal shall not be exported.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对装运出口的易腐烂变质食品、冷冻品的集装箱、船舱、飞机、车辆等运载工具，承运人、装箱单位或者其代理人应当在装运前向出入境检验检疫机构申请清洁、卫生、冷藏、密固等适载检验。未经检验或者经检验不合格的，不准装运。

**Article 30** For containers, holds, aircraft, vehicles, and other means of transport used for carrying exported perishable food and frozen products, the carrier, loading unit, or its agent shall, before loading, apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for suitability inspection for cleanliness, hygiene, refrigeration, sealing, etc. Those that have not been inspected or have failed inspection shall not be loaded.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　出入境检验检疫机构根据便利对外贸易的需要，可以对列入目录的出口商品进行出厂前的质量监督管理和检验。  
出入境检验检疫机构进行出厂前的质量监督管理和检验的内容，包括对生产企业的质量保证工作进行监督检查，对出口商品进行出厂前的检验。

**Article 31** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions may, based on the need to facilitate foreign trade, conduct quality supervision, management and inspection of export commodities listed in the Catalogue before they leave the factory.  
The contents of pre-delivery quality supervision, management and inspection conducted by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall include supervising and inspecting the quality assurance work of the production enterprises, and conducting pre-delivery inspection of export commodities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家对进出口食品生产企业实施卫生注册登记管理。获得卫生注册登记的出口食品生产企业，方可生产、加工、储存出口食品。获得卫生注册登记的进出口食品生产企业生产的食品，方可进口或者出口。  
实施卫生注册登记管理的进口食品生产企业，应当按照规定向海关总署申请卫生注册登记。  
实施卫生注册登记管理的出口食品生产企业，应当按照规定向出入境检验检疫机构申请卫生注册登记。  
出口食品生产企业需要在国外卫生注册的，依照本条第三款规定进行卫生注册登记后，由海关总署统一对外办理。

**Article 32** The state shall implement sanitary registration management for food production enterprises that import or export food. An export food production enterprise that has obtained sanitary registration may produce, process, and store export food. Food produced by an import or export food production enterprise that has obtained sanitary registration may be imported or exported.  
An import food production enterprise subject to sanitary registration management shall apply to the General Administration of Customs for sanitary registration in accordance with the provisions.  
An export food production enterprise subject to sanitary registration management shall apply to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for sanitary registration in accordance with the provisions.  
Where an export food production enterprise needs to obtain sanitary registration abroad, after completing sanitary registration in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of this Article, the General Administration of Customs shall handle the matter uniformly with the foreign party.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　出入境检验检疫机构根据需要，对检验合格的进出口商品加施商检标志，对检验合格的以及其他需要加施封识的进出口商品加施封识。具体办法由海关总署制定。

**Article 33** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions may, when necessary, affix commodity inspection marks on import and export commodities that have passed inspection, and affix seals on import and export commodities that have passed inspection or on other commodities that require seals. The specific measures shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　出入境检验检疫机构按照有关规定对检验的进出口商品抽取样品。验余的样品，出入境检验检疫机构应当通知有关单位在规定的期限内领回；逾期不领回的，由出入境检验检疫机构处理。

**Article 34** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall take samples of import and export commodities under inspection in accordance with relevant provisions. For samples remaining after inspection, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall notify the relevant entity to reclaim them within the prescribed time limit; if not reclaimed within the time limit, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall dispose of them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　进出口商品的报检人对出入境检验检疫机构作出的检验结果有异议的，可以自收到检验结果之日起15日内，向作出检验结果的出入境检验检疫机构或者其上级出入境检验检疫机构以至海关总署申请复验，受理复验的出入境检验检疫机构或者海关总署应当自收到复验申请之日起60日内作出复验结论。技术复杂，不能在规定期限内作出复验结论的，经本机构负责人批准，可以适当延长，但是延长期限最多不超过30日。

**Article 35** If the declarant of import or export commodities disagrees with the inspection results made by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the declarant may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the inspection results, apply for a re-inspection to the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution that made the inspection results or to its superior entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or even to the General Administration of Customs. The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or the General Administration of Customs that accepts the application for re-inspection shall make a concluding opinion on the re-inspection within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application for re-inspection. If the matter is technically complex and the concluding opinion cannot be made within the prescribed time limit, the time limit may be appropriately extended with the approval of the head of the institution, but the extension shall not exceed 30 days at most.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构根据进出口商品检验工作的需要，可以指定符合规定资质条件的国内外检测机构承担出入境检验检疫机构委托的进出口商品检测。被指定的检测机构经检查不符合规定要求的，海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构可以取消指定。

**Article 36** The General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions may, based on the needs of import and export commodity inspection work, designate domestic or foreign testing institutions that meet the prescribed qualification requirements to undertake the testing of import and export commodities entrusted by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions. If a designated testing institution is found upon inspection not to meet the prescribed requirements, the General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution may cancel the designation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对检验机构的检验鉴定业务活动有异议的，可以向海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构投诉。

**Article 37** If a party has objections to the inspection and appraisal business activities of an inspection institution, it may lodge a complaint with the General Administration of Customs or an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　海关总署、出入境检验检疫机构实施监督管理或者对涉嫌违反进出口商品检验法律、行政法规的行为进行调查，有权查阅、复制当事人的有关合同、发票、账簿以及其他有关资料。出入境检验检疫机构对有根据认为涉及人身财产安全、健康、环境保护项目不合格的进出口商品，经本机构负责人批准，可以查封或者扣押。

**Article 38** When conducting supervision and administration or investigating acts suspected of violating laws and administrative regulations on import and export commodity inspection, the General Administration of Customs and the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall have the power to consult and copy the relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials of the parties. The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution may, with the approval of the head of the institution, seal up or detain import or export commodities that it has reason to believe fail to meet the requirements concerning personal and property safety, health, or environmental protection.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　海关总署、出入境检验检疫机构应当根据便利对外贸易的需要，采取有效措施，简化程序，方便进出口。  
办理进出口商品报检、检验、鉴定等手续，符合条件的，可以采用电子数据文件的形式。

**Article 39** The General Administration of Customs and the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, based on the need to facilitate foreign trade, take effective measures to simplify procedures and facilitate import and export.  
For handling procedures such as declaration, inspection, and appraisal of import and export commodities, where conditions are met, electronic data file forms may be used.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　出入境检验检疫机构依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，签发出口货物普惠制原产地证明、区域性优惠原产地证明、专用原产地证明。  
出口货物一般原产地证明的签发，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 40** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, issue certificates of origin under the Generalized System of Preferences (GSP), certificates of regional preferential origin, and certificates of special origin for exported goods.  
The issuance of general certificates of origin for exported goods shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　出入境检验检疫机构对进出保税区、出口加工区等海关特殊监管区域的货物以及边境小额贸易进出口商品的检验管理，由海关总署另行制定办法。

**Article 41** The measures for the inspection and management by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions of goods entering and leaving bonded zones, export processing zones, and other special customs supervision areas, as well as of import and export commodities under border small-volume trade, shall be separately formulated by the General Administration of Customs.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　擅自销售、使用未报检或者未经检验的属于法定检验的进口商品，或者擅自销售、使用应当申请进口验证而未申请的进口商品的，由出入境检验检疫机构没收违法所得，并处商品货值金额5%以上20%以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** If anyone, without authorization, sells or uses import commodities subject to statutory inspection that have not been declared or inspected, or sells or uses import commodities for which an import verification application should have been made but was not, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 20% of the value of the commodities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　擅自出口未报检或者未经检验的属于法定检验的出口商品，或者擅自出口应当申请出口验证而未申请的出口商品的，由出入境检验检疫机构没收违法所得，并处商品货值金额5%以上20%以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** If anyone, without authorization, exports export commodities subject to statutory inspection that have not been declared or inspected, or exports export commodities for which an export verification application should have been made but was not, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 20% of the value of the commodities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　销售、使用经法定检验、抽查检验或者验证不合格的进口商品，或者出口经法定检验、抽查检验或者验证不合格的商品的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令停止销售、使用或者出口，没收违法所得和违法销售、使用或者出口的商品，并处违法销售、使用或者出口的商品货值金额等值以上3倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** If anyone sells or uses import commodities that have failed statutory inspection, random inspection, or verification, or exports commodities that have failed statutory inspection, random inspection, or verification, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order the cessation of sale, use, or export, confiscate the illegal gains and the commodities illegally sold, used, or exported, and impose a fine of not less than the value of the commodities illegally sold, used, or exported but not more than three times that value; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　进出口商品的收货人、发货人、代理报检企业或者出入境快件运营企业、报检人员不如实提供进出口商品的真实情况，取得出入境检验检疫机构的有关证单，或者对法定检验的进出口商品不予报检，逃避进出口商品检验的，由出入境检验检疫机构没收违法所得，并处商品货值金额5%以上20%以下罚款。  
进出口商品的收货人或者发货人委托代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业办理报检手续，未按照规定向代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业提供所委托报检事项的真实情况，取得出入境检验检疫机构的有关证单的，对委托人依照前款规定予以处罚。  
代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业、报检人员对委托人所提供情况的真实性未进行合理审查或者因工作疏忽，导致骗取出入境检验检疫机构有关证单的结果的，由出入境检验检疫机构对代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业处2万元以上20万元以下罚款。

**Article 45** If the consignee, consignor, agent declaration enterprise, entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise, or declarant of import or export commodities fails to truthfully provide the true information about the import or export commodities to obtain relevant certificates or documents from the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, or fails to declare import or export commodities subject to statutory inspection, thereby evading the inspection of import and export commodities, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 20% of the value of the commodities.  
If the consignee or consignor of import or export commodities entrusts an agent declaration enterprise or an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise to handle the declaration procedures, fails to provide the true information about the consigned declaration matters to the agent declaration enterprise or the entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise in accordance with the provisions, and obtains relevant certificates or documents from the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the principal shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
If an agent declaration enterprise, entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise, or declarant fails to conduct a reasonable review of the authenticity of the information provided by the principal or, due to negligence, causes the result of fraudulently obtaining relevant certificates or documents from the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the agent declaration enterprise or the entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃检验证单、印章、标志、封识、货物通关单或者使用伪造、变造的检验证单、印章、标志、封识、货物通关单，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令改正，没收违法所得，并处商品货值金额等值以下罚款。

**Article 46** If anyone forges, alters, buys or sells, or steals inspection certificates, seals, marks, seals, or customs clearance certificates for goods, or uses forged or altered inspection certificates, seals, marks, seals, or customs clearance certificates for goods, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than the value of the commodities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　擅自调换出入境检验检疫机构抽取的样品或者出入境检验检疫机构检验合格的进出口商品的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，并处商品货值金额10%以上50%以下罚款。

**Article 47** If anyone, without authorization, substitutes samples taken by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or substitutes import or export commodities that have passed inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order correction and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the value of the commodities shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　进口或者出口国家实行卫生注册登记管理而未获得卫生注册登记的生产企业生产的食品的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令停止进口或者出口，没收违法所得，并处商品货值金额10%以上50%以下罚款。  
已获得卫生注册登记的进出口食品生产企业，经检查不符合规定要求的，由海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构责令限期整改；整改仍未达到规定要求或者有其他违法行为，情节严重的，吊销其卫生注册登记证书。

**Article 48** If anyone imports or exports food produced by a production enterprise that is subject to state sanitary registration management but has not obtained sanitary registration, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order the cessation of import or export, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the value of the commodities.  
If an import or export food production enterprise that has obtained sanitary registration is found upon inspection not to meet the prescribed requirements, the General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order it to make rectification within a time limit; if the rectification still fails to meet the requirements or if there are other illegal acts and the circumstances are serious, its sanitary registration certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　进口可用作原料的固体废物，国外供货商、国内收货人未取得注册登记，或者未进行装运前检验的，按照国家有关规定责令退货；情节严重的，由出入境检验检疫机构并处10万元以上100万元以下罚款。  
已获得注册登记的可用作原料的固体废物的国外供货商、国内收货人违反国家有关规定，情节严重的，由出入境检验检疫机构撤销其注册登记。  
进口国家允许进口的旧机电产品未按照规定进行装运前检验的，按照国家有关规定予以退货；情节严重的，由出入境检验检疫机构并处100万元以下罚款。

**Article 49** If solid waste imported as raw materials is imported without the foreign supplier or domestic consignee having obtained registration, or without pre-shipment inspection having been carried out, the goods shall be ordered to be returned in accordance with relevant state provisions; if the circumstances are serious, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.  
If a foreign supplier or domestic consignee of solid waste that can be used as raw materials, having obtained registration, violates relevant state provisions and the circumstances are serious, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall revoke its registration.  
If used mechanical and electrical products that are allowed to be imported by the state are imported without pre-shipment inspection having been carried out in accordance with the provisions, the goods shall be returned in accordance with relevant state provisions; if the circumstances are serious, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall also impose a fine of not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　提供或者使用未经出入境检验检疫机构鉴定的出口危险货物包装容器的，由出入境检验检疫机构处10万元以下罚款。  
提供或者使用经出入境检验检疫机构鉴定不合格的包装容器装运出口危险货物的，由出入境检验检疫机构处20万元以下罚款。

**Article 50** If anyone provides or uses packaging containers for the export of dangerous goods that have not been appraised by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.  
If anyone provides or uses packaging containers that have been appraised as unqualified by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for loading exported dangerous goods, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　提供或者使用未经出入境检验检疫机构适载检验的集装箱、船舱、飞机、车辆等运载工具装运易腐烂变质食品、冷冻品出口的，由出入境检验检疫机构处10万元以下罚款。  
提供或者使用经出入境检验检疫机构检验不合格的集装箱、船舱、飞机、车辆等运载工具装运易腐烂变质食品、冷冻品出口的，由出入境检验检疫机构处20万元以下罚款。

**Article 51** If anyone provides or uses containers, holds, aircraft, vehicles, or other means of transport that have not undergone suitability inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for loading perishable food or frozen products for export, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.  
If anyone provides or uses containers, holds, aircraft, vehicles, or other means of transport that have failed suitability inspection by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for loading perishable food or frozen products for export, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　擅自调换、损毁出入境检验检疫机构加施的商检标志、封识的，由出入境检验检疫机构处5万元以下罚款。

**Article 52** If anyone, without authorization, substitutes or damages the commodity inspection marks or seals affixed by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　从事进出口商品检验鉴定业务的检验机构违反国家有关规定，扰乱检验鉴定秩序的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处10万元以下罚款，海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构可以暂停其6个月以内检验鉴定业务。

**Article 53** If an inspection institution engaged in the business of inspection and appraisal of import and export commodities violates relevant state provisions and disrupts the order of inspection and appraisal, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan. The General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution may suspend its inspection and appraisal business for a period of not more than six months.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　代理报检企业、出入境快件运营企业违反国家有关规定，扰乱报检秩序的，由出入境检验检疫机构责令改正，没收违法所得，可以处10万元以下罚款，海关总署或者出入境检验检疫机构可以暂停其6个月以内代理报检业务。

**Article 54** If an agent declaration enterprise or an entry-exit express delivery operation enterprise violates relevant state provisions and disrupts the order of declaration, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan. The General Administration of Customs or the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution may suspend its agent declaration business for a period of not more than six months.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　出入境检验检疫机构的工作人员滥用职权，故意刁难当事人的，徇私舞弊，伪造检验结果的，或者玩忽职守，延误检验出证的，依法给予行政处分；违反有关法律、行政法规规定签发出口货物原产地证明的，依法给予行政处分，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** If a staff member of an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution abuses his or her power, deliberately makes difficulties for a party, engages in malpractice for personal gain, forges inspection results, or neglects his or her duty, causing delay in inspection and issuance of certificates, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if he or she issues certificates of origin for exported goods in violation of the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction according to law and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　出入境检验检疫机构对没收的商品依法予以处理所得价款、没收的违法所得、收缴的罚款，全部上缴国库。

**Article 56** All proceeds from the disposal of confiscated commodities according to law, confiscated illegal gains, and fines collected by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall be turned over to the state treasury.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　当事人对出入境检验检疫机构、海关总署作出的复验结论、处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
当事人逾期不履行处罚决定，又不申请行政复议或者向人民法院提起诉讼的，作出处罚决定的机构可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 57** If a party disagrees with a re-inspection conclusion or a penalty decision made by an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution or the General Administration of Customs, it may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law, or may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court according to law.  
If a party fails to comply with a penalty decision within the time limit and does not apply for administrative reconsideration or institute legal proceedings in a People's Court, the institution that made the penalty decision may apply to a People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　出入境检验检疫机构实施法定检验、依法设立的检验机构办理检验鉴定业务，按照国家有关规定收取费用。

**Article 58** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall charge fees for conducting statutory inspection, and inspection institutions established according to law shall charge fees for handling inspection and appraisal businesses, in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例自2005年12月1日起施行。1992年10月7日国务院批准、1992年10月23日原国家进出口商品检验局发布的《中华人民共和国进出口商品检验法实施条例》同时废止。

**Article 59** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2005. The Regulations for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Import and Export Commodity Inspection, approved by the State Council on October 7, 1992, and promulgated by the former State Administration of Import and Export Commodity Inspection on October 23, 1992, shall be abolished simultaneously.
