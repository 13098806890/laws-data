# 农作物病虫害防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-03-26  
**生效日期**：2020-05-01

> （一）一类农作物病虫害，是指常年发生面积特别大或者可能给农业生产造成特别重大损失的农作物病虫害，其名录由国务院农业农村主管部门制定、公布; （二）二类农作物病虫害，是指常年发生面积大或者可能给农业生产造成重大损失的农作物病虫害，其名录由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门制定、公布，并报国务院农业农村主管部门备案; （三）三类农作物病虫害，是指一类农作物病虫害和二类农作物病虫害以外的其他农作物病虫害。 新发现的农作物病虫害可能给农业生产造成重大或者特别重大损失的，在确定其分类前，按照一类农作物病虫害管理。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对农作物病虫害防治工作的组织领导，将防治工作经费纳入本级政府预算。 第六条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国农作物病虫害防治的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域农作物病虫害防治的监督管理工作。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门按照职责分工，做好农作物病虫害防治相关工作。 乡镇人民政府应当协助上级人民政府有关部门做好本行政区域农作物病虫害防治宣传、动员、组织等工作。 第七条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门组织植物保护工作机构开展农作物病虫害防治有关技术工作。 第八条　农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人应当做好生产经营范围内的农作物病虫害防治工作，并对各级人民政府及有关部门开展的防治工作予以配合。 农村集体经济组织、村民委员会应当配合各级人民政府及有关部门做好农作物病虫害防治工作。 第九条　国家鼓励和支持开展农作物病虫害防治科技创新、成果转化和依法推广应用，普及应用信息技术、生物技术，推进农作物病虫害防治的智能化、专业化、绿色化。 国家鼓励和支持农作物病虫害防治国际合作与交流。 第十条　国家鼓励和支持使用生态治理、健康栽培、生物防治、物理防治等绿色防控技术和先进施药机械以及安全、高效、经济的农药。 第十一条　对在农作物病虫害防治工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对农作物病虫害防治工作的组织领导，将防治工作经费纳入本级政府预算。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the organization and leadership of the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests, and shall include the funding for prevention and control work into their respective government budgets.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国农作物病虫害防治的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域农作物病虫害防治的监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门按照职责分工，做好农作物病虫害防治相关工作。  
乡镇人民政府应当协助上级人民政府有关部门做好本行政区域农作物病虫害防治宣传、动员、组织等工作。

**Article 6** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests nationwide. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly carry out the relevant work on the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests.  
The township people's governments shall assist the relevant departments of the people's governments at higher levels in carrying out the publicity, mobilization, organization and other work on the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门组织植物保护工作机构开展农作物病虫害防治有关技术工作。

**Article 7** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize plant protection work institutions to carry out technical work related to the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人应当做好生产经营范围内的农作物病虫害防治工作，并对各级人民政府及有关部门开展的防治工作予以配合。  
农村集体经济组织、村民委员会应当配合各级人民政府及有关部门做好农作物病虫害防治工作。

**Article 8** Agricultural operators and other relevant entities and individuals shall properly carry out the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests within their production and operation scope, and shall cooperate with the people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in the prevention and control work carried out.  
Rural collective economic organizations and villagers' committees shall cooperate with the people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in carrying out the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持开展农作物病虫害防治科技创新、成果转化和依法推广应用，普及应用信息技术、生物技术，推进农作物病虫害防治的智能化、专业化、绿色化。  
国家鼓励和支持农作物病虫害防治国际合作与交流。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological innovation, transformation of achievements, and promotion and application according to law in the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests, and promotes the application of information technology and biotechnology to advance the intelligent, specialized and green development of the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests.  
The State encourages and supports international cooperation and exchange in the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持使用生态治理、健康栽培、生物防治、物理防治等绿色防控技术和先进施药机械以及安全、高效、经济的农药。

**Article 10** The State encourages and supports the use of green prevention and control technologies such as ecological management, healthy cultivation, biological control, and physical control, as well as advanced application machinery and safe, efficient, and economical pesticides.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在农作物病虫害防治工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰。

**Article 11** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests shall be commended in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　监测与预报

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家建立农作物病虫害监测制度。国务院农业农村主管部门负责编制全国农作物病虫害监测网络建设规划并组织实施。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门负责编制本行政区域农作物病虫害监测网络建设规划并组织实施。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强对农作物病虫害监测网络的管理。

**Article 12** The State shall establish a monitoring system for crop diseases and insect pests. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the national plan for the construction of a monitoring network for crop diseases and insect pests and organizing its implementation. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for formulating the plans for the construction of monitoring networks for crop diseases and insect pests within their respective administrative regions and organizing their implementation.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of the monitoring networks for crop diseases and insect pests.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、拆除、擅自移动农作物病虫害监测设施设备，或者以其他方式妨害农作物病虫害监测设施设备正常运行。  
新建、改建、扩建建设工程应当避开农作物病虫害监测设施设备；确实无法避开、需要拆除农作物病虫害监测设施设备的，应当由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门按照有关技术要求组织迁建，迁建费用由建设单位承担。  
农作物病虫害监测设施设备毁损的，县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当及时组织修复或者重新建设。

**Article 13** No entity or individual may encroach upon, damage, dismantle, or remove without authorization the monitoring facilities and equipment for crop diseases and insect pests, or otherwise hinder their normal operation.  
New construction, reconstruction, or expansion projects shall avoid the monitoring facilities and equipment for crop diseases and insect pests; if it is truly impossible to avoid them and the monitoring facilities and equipment need to be dismantled, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize their relocation in accordance with the relevant technical requirements, and the relocation costs shall be borne by the construction entity.  
Where the monitoring facilities and equipment for crop diseases and insect pests are damaged, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall promptly organize their repair or reconstruction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当组织开展农作物病虫害监测。农作物病虫害监测包括下列内容：  
（一）农作物病虫害发生的种类、时间、范围、程度；  
（二）害虫主要天敌种类、分布与种群消长情况；  
（三）影响农作物病虫害发生的田间气候；  
（四）其他需要监测的内容。  
农作物病虫害监测技术规范由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门制定。  
农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人应当配合做好农作物病虫害监测。

**Article 14** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out monitoring of crop diseases and insect pests. The monitoring of crop diseases and insect pests shall include the following:  
(1) the types, timing, extent, and severity of occurrence of crop diseases and insect pests;  
(2) the species, distribution, and population dynamics of major natural enemies of pests;  
(3) the field climate affecting the occurrence of crop diseases and insect pests;  
(4) other contents that need to be monitored.  
The technical specifications for monitoring of crop diseases and insect pests shall be formulated by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs at or above the provincial level.  
Agricultural operators and other relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate in carrying out the monitoring of crop diseases and insect pests.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定及时向上级人民政府农业农村主管部门报告农作物病虫害监测信息。  
任何单位和个人不得瞒报、谎报农作物病虫害监测信息，不得授意他人编造虚假信息，不得阻挠他人如实报告。

**Article 15** The competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural and rural department of the State Council, promptly report crop pest monitoring information to the competent agricultural and rural departments of the people's government at the next higher level.  
No unit or individual shall conceal or falsify crop pest monitoring information, or instigate others to fabricate false information, or prevent others from reporting truthfully.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当在综合分析监测结果的基础上，按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定发布农作物病虫害预报，其他组织和个人不得向社会发布农作物病虫害预报。  
农作物病虫害预报包括农作物病虫害发生以及可能发生的种类、时间、范围、程度以及预防控制措施等内容。

**Article 16** The competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, on the basis of comprehensive analysis of monitoring results and in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural and rural department of the State Council, issue crop pest forecasts. No other organization or individual shall issue crop pest forecasts to the public.  
Crop pest forecasts shall include information on the types, time, scope, degree, and preventive and control measures of crop pests that have occurred or may occur.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　境外组织和个人不得在我国境内开展农作物病虫害监测活动。确需开展的，应当由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门组织境内有关单位与其联合进行，并遵守有关法律、法规的规定。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自向境外组织和个人提供未发布的农作物病虫害监测信息。

**Article 17** No foreign organization or individual shall carry out crop pest monitoring activities within the territory of China. If such activities are truly necessary, they shall be jointly conducted by the competent agricultural and rural department of the people's government at or above the provincial level organizing relevant domestic units with the foreign party, and shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
No unit or individual shall, without authorization, provide unpublished crop pest monitoring information to foreign organizations or individuals.

### 第三章　预防与控制

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院农业农村主管部门组织制定全国农作物病虫害预防控制方案，县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门组织制定本行政区域农作物病虫害预防控制方案。  
农作物病虫害预防控制方案根据农业生产情况、气候条件、农作物病虫害常年发生情况、监测预报情况以及发生趋势等因素制定，其内容包括预防控制目标、重点区域、防治阈值、预防控制措施和保障措施等方面。

**Article 18** The competent agricultural and rural department of the State Council shall organize the formulation of a national plan for the prevention and control of crop pests, and the competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the formulation of plans for the prevention and control of crop pests within their respective administrative regions.  
The plan for the prevention and control of crop pests shall be formulated based on factors such as agricultural production conditions, climatic conditions, the perennial occurrence of crop pests, monitoring and forecast information, and occurrence trends, and its content shall include aspects such as prevention and control objectives, key areas, control thresholds, prevention and control measures, and safeguard measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当健全农作物病虫害防治体系，并组织开展农作物病虫害抗药性监测评估，为农业生产经营者提供农作物病虫害预防控制技术培训、指导、服务。  
国家鼓励和支持科研单位、有关院校、农民专业合作社、企业、行业协会等单位和个人研究、依法推广绿色防控技术。  
对在农作物病虫害防治工作中接触有毒有害物质的人员，有关单位应当组织做好安全防护，并按照国家有关规定发放津贴补贴。

**Article 19** The competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall improve the crop pest prevention and control system, and organize and carry out monitoring and assessment of crop pest resistance, and provide training, guidance, and services on crop pest prevention and control techniques to agricultural producers and operators.  
The State encourages and supports scientific research units, relevant colleges and universities, farmers' specialized cooperatives, enterprises, industry associations, and other units and individuals to research and promote green prevention and control technologies in accordance with law.  
For personnel who are exposed to toxic and hazardous substances in the course of crop pest prevention and control work, the relevant units shall organize safety protection measures and issue subsidies and allowances in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当在农作物病虫害孳生地、源头区组织开展作物改种、植被改造、环境整治等生态治理工作，调整种植结构，防止农作物病虫害孳生和蔓延。

**Article 20** The competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in the breeding grounds and source areas of crop pests, organize and carry out ecological management work such as crop replacement, vegetation transformation, and environmental remediation, and adjust planting structures to prevent the breeding and spread of crop pests.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当指导农业生产经营者选用抗病、抗虫品种，采用包衣、拌种、消毒等种子处理措施，采取合理轮作、深耕除草、覆盖除草、土壤消毒、清除农作物病残体等健康栽培管理措施，预防农作物病虫害。

**Article 21** The competent agricultural and rural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide agricultural producers and operators in selecting disease-resistant and insect-resistant varieties, adopting seed treatment measures such as coating, seed dressing, and disinfection, and taking healthy cultivation management measures such as reasonable crop rotation, deep plowing for weeding, mulching for weeding, soil disinfection, and removal of diseased and residual crop parts to prevent crop pests.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事农作物病虫害研究、饲养、繁殖、运输、展览等活动的，应当采取措施防止其逃逸、扩散。

**Article 22** Those engaged in activities such as research, rearing, breeding, transportation, and exhibition of crop pests shall take measures to prevent their escape and spread.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农作物病虫害发生时，农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人应当及时采取防止农作物病虫害扩散的控制措施。发现农作物病虫害严重发生或者暴发的，应当及时报告所在地县级人民政府农业农村主管部门。

**Article 23** When crop pests occur, agricultural producers and operators and other relevant units and individuals shall promptly take control measures to prevent the spread of crop pests. If a serious occurrence or outbreak of crop pests is discovered, they shall promptly report to the competent agricultural and rural department of the local people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有关单位和个人开展农作物病虫害防治使用农药时，应当遵守农药安全、合理使用制度，严格按照农药标签或者说明书使用农药。  
农田除草时，应当防止除草剂危害当季和后茬作物；农田灭鼠时，应当防止杀鼠剂危害人畜安全。

**Article 24** When applying pesticides in the prevention and control of crop pests, the relevant units and individuals shall comply with the system of safe and rational use of pesticides, and strictly use pesticides in accordance with pesticide labels or instructions.  
When weeding farmland, they shall prevent herbicides from harming the current season and subsequent crops; when controlling rodents in farmland, they shall prevent rodenticides from endangering human and livestock safety.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农作物病虫害严重发生时，县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当按照农作物病虫害预防控制方案以及监测预报情况，及时组织、指导农业生产经营者、专业化病虫害防治服务组织等有关单位和个人采取统防统治等控制措施。  
一类农作物病虫害严重发生时，国务院农业农村主管部门应当对控制工作进行综合协调、指导。二类、三类农作物病虫害严重发生时，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门应当对控制工作进行综合协调、指导。  
国有荒地上发生的农作物病虫害由县级以上地方人民政府组织控制。

**Article 25** When crop diseases and insect pests occur severely, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the prevention and control plan for crop diseases and insect pests and the monitoring and forecasting conditions, promptly organize and guide agricultural producers and operators, specialized pest control service organizations, and other relevant entities and individuals to adopt control measures such as unified prevention and control.  
When Class I crop diseases and insect pests occur severely, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall conduct comprehensive coordination and guidance for the control work. When Class II or Class III crop diseases and insect pests occur severely, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall conduct comprehensive coordination and guidance for the control work.  
Crop diseases and insect pests occurring on state-owned wasteland shall be controlled under the organization of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农田鼠害严重发生时，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织采取统一灭鼠措施。

**Article 26** When farmland rodent infestation occurs severely, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize unified rodent eradication measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当组织做好农作物病虫害灾情调查汇总工作，将灾情信息及时报告本级人民政府和上一级人民政府农业农村主管部门，并抄送同级人民政府应急管理部门。  
农作物病虫害灾情信息由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门商同级人民政府应急管理部门发布，其他组织和个人不得向社会发布。

**Article 27** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the investigation and compilation of disaster information on crop diseases and insect pests, report such information in a timely manner to the people's government at the same level and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the next higher level, and send a copy to the emergency management department of the people's government at the same level.  
Disaster information on crop diseases and insect pests shall be released by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level after consultation with the emergency management department of the people's government at the same level. No other organization or individual may release such information to the public.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家鼓励和支持保险机构开展农作物病虫害防治相关保险业务，鼓励和支持农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人参加保险。

**Article 28** The State encourages and supports insurance institutions to carry out insurance services related to the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests, and encourages and supports agricultural producers and operators and other relevant entities and individuals to participate in such insurance.

### 第四章　应急处置

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当建立农作物病虫害防治应急响应和处置机制，制定应急预案。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当根据本行政区域农作物病虫害应急处置需要，组织制定应急预案，开展应急业务培训和演练，储备必要的应急物资。

**Article 29** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall establish an emergency response and handling mechanism for the prevention and control of crop diseases and insect pests, and formulate an emergency response plan.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, according to the needs of emergency response to crop diseases and insect pests within their respective administrative regions, organize the formulation of emergency response plans, carry out emergency operation training and drills, and stockpile necessary emergency materials.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农作物病虫害暴发时，县级以上地方人民政府应当立即启动应急响应，采取下列措施：  
（一）划定应急处置的范围和面积；  
（二）组织和调集应急处置队伍；  
（三）启用应急备用药剂、机械等物资；  
（四）组织应急处置行动。

**Article 30** When crop diseases and insect pests break out, the local people's government at or above the county level shall immediately activate an emergency response and take the following measures:  
(1) Delineating the scope and area for emergency response;  
(2) Organizing and mobilizing emergency response teams;  
(3) Activating emergency standby materials such as pesticides and machinery;  
(4) Organizing emergency response actions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当在各自职责范围内做好农作物病虫害应急处置工作。  
公安、交通运输等主管部门应当为应急处置所需物资的调度、运输提供便利条件，民用航空主管部门应当为应急处置航空作业提供优先保障，气象主管机构应当为应急处置提供气象信息服务。

**Article 31** The relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, carry out the emergency response work for crop diseases and insect pests.  
The competent departments of public security, transportation, and others shall provide convenient conditions for the dispatch and transport of materials required for emergency response; the competent department of civil aviation shall provide priority support for aerial operations in emergency response; and the competent meteorological authority shall provide meteorological information services for emergency response.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　农作物病虫害应急处置期间，县级以上地方人民政府可以根据需要依法调集必需的物资、运输工具以及相关设施设备。应急处置结束后，应当及时归还并对毁损、灭失的给予补偿。

**Article 32** During the period of emergency response to crop diseases and insect pests, the local people's government at or above the county level may, as necessary and according to law, mobilize essential materials, means of transport, and related facilities and equipment. After the emergency response ends, such items shall be returned in a timely manner, and compensation shall be given for those that have been damaged or lost.

### 第五章　专业化服务

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家通过政府购买服务等方式鼓励和扶持专业化病虫害防治服务组织，鼓励专业化病虫害防治服务组织使用绿色防控技术。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强对专业化病虫害防治服务组织的规范和管理，并为专业化病虫害防治服务组织提供技术培训、指导、服务。

**Article 33** The State, through means such as government procurement of services, encourages and supports specialized pest control service organizations, and encourages specialized pest control service organizations to use green prevention and control technologies.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the regulation and management of specialized pest control service organizations, and shall provide them with technical training, guidance, and services.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当具备相应的设施设备、技术人员、田间作业人员以及规范的管理制度。  
依照有关法律、行政法规需要办理登记的专业化病虫害防治服务组织，应当依法向县级以上人民政府有关部门申请登记。

**Article 34** Specialized pest control service organizations shall possess corresponding facilities and equipment, technical personnel, field operators, and standardized management systems.  
A specialized pest control service organization that is required to undergo registration in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply for registration with the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织的田间作业人员应当能够正确识别服务区域的农作物病虫害，正确掌握农药适用范围、施用方法、安全间隔期等专业知识以及田间作业安全防护知识，正确使用施药机械以及农作物病虫害防治相关用品。专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当定期组织田间作业人员参加技术培训。

**Article 35** Field operators of specialized pest control service organizations shall be able to correctly identify crop pests and diseases in the service area, correctly master professional knowledge such as the scope of application, application methods, and safety intervals of pesticides, as well as knowledge of field operation safety protection, and correctly use application machinery and items related to crop pest and disease control. Specialized pest control service organizations shall regularly organize field operators to participate in technical training.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当与服务对象共同商定服务方案或者签订服务合同。  
专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当遵守国家有关农药安全、合理使用制度，建立服务档案，如实记录服务的时间、地点、内容以及使用农药的名称、用量、生产企业、农药包装废弃物处置方式等信息。服务档案应当保存2年以上。

**Article 36** Specialized pest control service organizations shall jointly determine the service plan or sign a service contract with the service recipients.  
Specialized pest control service organizations shall comply with the national regulations on pesticide safety and rational use, establish service archives, and truthfully record information such as the time, location, and content of services, as well as the name, dosage, manufacturer of the pesticides used, and the disposal method of pesticide packaging waste. The service archives shall be kept for at least two years.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当按照国家有关规定为田间作业人员参加工伤保险缴纳工伤保险费。国家鼓励专业化病虫害防治服务组织为田间作业人员投保人身意外伤害保险。  
专业化病虫害防治服务组织应当为田间作业人员配备必要的防护用品。

**Article 37** Specialized pest control service organizations shall pay work-related injury insurance premiums for field operators to participate in work-related injury insurance in accordance with relevant state regulations. The State encourages specialized pest control service organizations to insure field operators with personal accident insurance.  
Specialized pest control service organizations shall equip field operators with necessary protective gear.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织开展农作物病虫害预防控制航空作业，应当按照国家有关规定向公众公告作业范围、时间、施药种类以及注意事项；需要办理飞行计划或者备案手续的，应当按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 38** When specialized pest control service organizations carry out aerial operations for the prevention and control of crop pests and diseases, they shall announce to the public the scope, time, types of pesticides to be applied, and precautions in accordance with relevant state regulations; if it is necessary to go through flight plan or filing procedures, they shall do so in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未依照本条例规定履行职责；  
（二）瞒报、谎报农作物病虫害监测信息，授意他人编造虚假信息或者阻挠他人如实报告；  
（三）擅自向境外组织和个人提供未发布的农作物病虫害监测信息；  
（四）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为。

**Article 39** Where local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level and their staff members commit any of the following acts, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to perform their duties in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) concealing or falsifying monitoring information on crop pests and diseases, instructing others to fabricate false information, or obstructing others from reporting truthfully;  
(3) providing unpublished monitoring information on crop pests and diseases to overseas organizations or individuals without authorization;  
(4) other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，侵占、损毁、拆除、擅自移动农作物病虫害监测设施设备或者以其他方式妨害农作物病虫害监测设施设备正常运行的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以处5万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, encroaches upon, damages, demolishes, or moves without authorization the monitoring facilities and equipment for crop pests and diseases, or otherwise hinders the normal operation of such facilities and equipment, shall be ordered by the competent agricultural and rural department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, restore the original condition or take other remedial measures within a time limit, and may be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门处5000元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）擅自向社会发布农作物病虫害预报或者灾情信息；  
（二）从事农作物病虫害研究、饲养、繁殖、运输、展览等活动未采取有效措施，造成农作物病虫害逃逸、扩散；  
（三）开展农作物病虫害预防控制航空作业未按照国家有关规定进行公告。

**Article 41** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts shall be fined by the competent agricultural and rural department of the people's government at or above the county level not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) releasing forecasts or disaster information on crop pests and diseases to the public without authorization;  
(2) engaging in activities such as research, breeding, reproduction, transportation, or exhibition of crop pests and diseases without taking effective measures, resulting in the escape or spread of crop pests and diseases;  
(3) carrying out aerial operations for the prevention and control of crop pests and diseases without making a public announcement in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　专业化病虫害防治服务组织有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不具备相应的设施设备、技术人员、田间作业人员以及规范的管理制度；  
（二）其田间作业人员不能正确识别服务区域的农作物病虫害，或者不能正确掌握农药适用范围、施用方法、安全间隔期等专业知识以及田间作业安全防护知识，或者不能正确使用施药机械以及农作物病虫害防治相关用品；  
（三）未按规定建立或者保存服务档案；  
（四）未为田间作业人员配备必要的防护用品。

**Article 42** Where a specialized pest control service organization commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural and rural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) lacking the corresponding facilities, equipment, technical personnel, field operators, and standardized management systems;  
(2) its field operators are unable to correctly identify crop pests and diseases in the service area, or correctly master professional knowledge such as the scope of application, application methods, and safety intervals of pesticides, as well as field operation safety protection knowledge, or correctly use application machinery and items related to crop pest and disease control;  
(3) failing to establish or keep service archives as required;  
(4) failing to provide field operators with necessary protective gear.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　境外组织和个人违反本条例规定，在我国境内开展农作物病虫害监测活动的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令其停止监测活动，没收监测数据和工具，并处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处50万元以上100万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where an overseas organization or individual, in violation of these Regulations, carries out monitoring activities for crop pests and diseases within the territory of China, the competent agricultural and rural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease the monitoring activities, confiscate the monitoring data and tools, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a concurrent fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　储存粮食的病虫害防治依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 44** The prevention and control of pests and diseases in stored grain shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2020年5月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force on May 1, 2020.
