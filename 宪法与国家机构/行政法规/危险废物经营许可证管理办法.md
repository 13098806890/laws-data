# 危险废物经营许可证管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2004-07-01

> (2004年5月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第408号公布　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对危险废物收集、贮存和处置经营活动的监督管理，防治危险废物污染环境，根据《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》，制定本办法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事危险废物收集、贮存、处置经营活动的单位，应当依照本办法的规定，领取危险废物经营许可证。 第三条　危险废物经营许可证按照经营方式，分为危险废物收集、贮存、处置综合经营许可证和危险废物收集经营许可证。 领取危险废物综合经营许可证的单位，可以从事各类别危险废物的收集、贮存、处置经营活动；领取危险废物收集经营许可证的单位，只能从事机动车维修活动中产生的废矿物油和居民日常生活中产生的废镉镍电池的危险废物收集经营活动。 第四条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门依照本办法的规定，负责危险废物经营许可证的审批颁发与监督管理工作。 第二章　申请领取危险废物经营许可证的条件 第五条　申请领取危险废物收集、贮存、处置综合经营许可证，应当具备下列条件： (一)有3名以上环境工程专业或者相关专业中级以上职称，并有3年以上固体废物污染治理经历的技术人员； (二)有符合国务院交通主管部门有关危险货物运输安全要求的运输工具； (三)有符合国家或者地方环境保护标准和安全要求的包装工具，中转和临时存放设施、设备以及经验收合格的贮存设施、设备； (四)有符合国家或者省、自治区、直辖市危险废物处置设施建设规划，符合国家或者地方环境保护标准和安全要求的处置设施、设备和配套的污染防治设施；其中，医疗废物集中处置设施，还应当符合国家有关医疗废物处置的卫生标准和要求； (五)有与所经营的危险废物类别相适应的处置技术和工艺； (六)有保证危险废物经营安全的规章制度、污染防治措施和事故应急救援措施；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对危险废物收集、贮存和处置经营活动的监督管理，防治危险废物污染环境，根据《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution by Solid Wastes for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and administration of the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes and preventing environmental pollution by hazardous wastes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事危险废物收集、贮存、处置经营活动的单位，应当依照本办法的规定，领取危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 2** Units engaged in the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, obtain a hazardous waste operating permit.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　危险废物经营许可证按照经营方式，分为危险废物收集、贮存、处置综合经营许可证和危险废物收集经营许可证。  
领取危险废物综合经营许可证的单位，可以从事各类别危险废物的收集、贮存、处置经营活动；领取危险废物收集经营许可证的单位，只能从事机动车维修活动中产生的废矿物油和居民日常生活中产生的废镉镍电池的危险废物收集经营活动。

**Article 3** Hazardous waste operating permits are classified, according to the mode of operation, into comprehensive operating permits for the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes and operating permits for the collection of hazardous wastes.  
Units that obtain a comprehensive operating permit for hazardous wastes may engage in the collection, storage and disposal of various categories of hazardous wastes; units that obtain an operating permit for the collection of hazardous wastes may only engage in the collection of waste mineral oil generated from motor vehicle maintenance activities and waste cadmium-nickel batteries generated from daily life of residents.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门依照本办法的规定，负责危险废物经营许可证的审批颁发与监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, be responsible for the examination, approval, issuance and supervision of hazardous waste operating permits.

### 第二章　申请领取危险废物经营许可证的条件

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　申请领取危险废物收集、贮存、处置综合经营许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有3名以上环境工程专业或者相关专业中级以上职称，并有3年以上固体废物污染治理经历的技术人员；  
(二)有符合国务院交通主管部门有关危险货物运输安全要求的运输工具；  
(三)有符合国家或者地方环境保护标准和安全要求的包装工具，中转和临时存放设施、设备以及经验收合格的贮存设施、设备；  
(四)有符合国家或者省、自治区、直辖市危险废物处置设施建设规划，符合国家或者地方环境保护标准和安全要求的处置设施、设备和配套的污染防治设施；其中，医疗废物集中处置设施，还应当符合国家有关医疗废物处置的卫生标准和要求；  
(五)有与所经营的危险废物类别相适应的处置技术和工艺；  
(六)有保证危险废物经营安全的规章制度、污染防治措施和事故应急救援措施；  
(七)以填埋方式处置危险废物的，应当依法取得填埋场所的土地使用权。

**Article 5** To apply for a comprehensive operating permit for the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes, the applicant shall possess the following conditions:  
(1) Having at least 3 technical personnel with intermediate or above technical titles in environmental engineering or related specialties and with more than 3 years of experience in solid waste pollution control;  
(2) Having transport vehicles that meet the safety requirements of the competent transport department of the State Council for the transport of dangerous goods;  
(3) Having packaging tools, transfer and temporary storage facilities and equipment that meet the national or local environmental protection standards and safety requirements, and storage facilities and equipment that have passed acceptance inspection;  
(4) Having disposal facilities, equipment and supporting pollution prevention facilities that conform to the national or provincial, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government's planning for the construction of hazardous waste disposal facilities and meet the national or local environmental protection standards and safety requirements; among which, centralized medical waste disposal facilities shall also meet the national sanitary standards and requirements for medical waste disposal;  
(5) Having disposal technologies and processes appropriate to the categories of hazardous wastes to be operated;  
(6) Having rules and regulations to ensure the safety of hazardous waste operations, pollution prevention measures and emergency rescue measures for accidents;  
(7) Where hazardous wastes are to be disposed of by landfill, the land use right of the landfill site shall be obtained according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请领取危险废物收集经营许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有防雨、防渗的运输工具；  
(二)有符合国家或者地方环境保护标准和安全要求的包装工具，中转和临时存放设施、设备；  
(三)有保证危险废物经营安全的规章制度、污染防治措施和事故应急救援措施。

**Article 6** To apply for an operating permit for the collection of hazardous wastes, the applicant shall possess the following conditions:  
(1) Having transport vehicles that are rain-proof and seepage-proof;  
(2) Having packaging tools, transfer and temporary storage facilities and equipment that meet the national or local environmental protection standards and safety requirements;  
(3) Having rules and regulations to ensure the safety of hazardous waste operations, pollution prevention measures and emergency rescue measures for accidents.

### 第三章　申请领取危险废物经营许可证的程序

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对危险废物经营许可证实行分级审批颁发。  
医疗废物集中处置单位的危险废物经营许可证，由医疗废物集中处置设施所在地设区的市级人民政府环境保护主管部门审批颁发。  
危险废物收集经营许可证，由县级人民政府环境保护主管部门审批颁发。  
本条第二款、第三款规定之外的危险废物经营许可证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护主管部门审批颁发。

**Article 7** The State implements a system of graded examination, approval and issuance of hazardous waste operating permits.  
The hazardous waste operating permit for a centralized medical waste disposal unit shall be examined, approved and issued by the competent environmental protection department of the people's government at the city divided into districts where the centralized medical waste disposal facility is located.  
The operating permit for the collection of hazardous wastes shall be examined, approved and issued by the competent environmental protection department of the people's government at the county level.  
Hazardous waste operating permits other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall be examined, approved and issued by the competent environmental protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请领取危险废物经营许可证的单位，应当在从事危险废物经营活动前向发证机关提出申请，并附具[本办法第五条](#art-5)或者第六条规定条件的证明材料。

**Article 8** A unit applying for a hazardous waste operating permit shall submit an application to the permit-issuing authority before engaging in hazardous waste operation activities, and attach the certification materials evidencing the conditions specified in Article 5 or Article 6 of these Measures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　发证机关应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，对申请单位提交的证明材料进行审查，并对申请单位的经营设施进行现场核查。符合条件的，颁发危险废物经营许可证，并予以公告；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
发证机关在颁发危险废物经营许可证前，可以根据实际需要征求卫生、城乡规划等有关主管部门和专家的意见。

**Article 9** The permit-issuing authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, examine the certification materials submitted by the applicant, and conduct an on-site inspection of the applicant's operating facilities. Where the conditions are met, it shall issue the hazardous waste operating permit and make a public announcement; where the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Before issuing a hazardous waste operating permit, the permit-issuing authority may, as actually needed, seek opinions from the competent departments of health, urban and rural planning and other relevant departments and experts.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　危险废物经营许可证包括下列主要内容：  
(一)法人名称、法定代表人、住所；  
(二)危险废物经营方式；  
(三)危险废物类别；  
(四)年经营规模；  
(五)有效期限；  
(六)发证日期和证书编号。  
危险废物综合经营许可证的内容，还应当包括贮存、处置设施的地址。

**Article 10** A hazardous waste operating permit includes the following main contents:  
(1) Name of the legal person, legal representative, and domicile;  
(2) Mode of hazardous waste operation;  
(3) Categories of hazardous wastes;  
(4) Annual operating scale;  
(5) Term of validity;  
(6) Date of issuance and certificate number.  
The contents of a comprehensive hazardous waste operating permit shall also include the addresses of the storage and disposal facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　危险废物经营单位变更法人名称、法定代表人和住所的，应当自工商变更登记之日起15个工作日内，向原发证机关申请办理危险废物经营许可证变更手续。

**Article 11** Where a hazardous waste operating unit changes its legal person name, legal representative or domicile, it shall, within 15 working days from the date of the industrial and commercial change registration, apply to the original permit-issuing authority for handling the change procedures of the hazardous waste operating permit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，危险废物经营单位应当按照原申请程序，重新申请领取危险废物经营许可证：  
(一)改变危险废物经营方式的；  
(二)增加危险废物类别的；  
(三)新建或者改建、扩建原有危险废物经营设施的；  
(四)经营危险废物超过原批准年经营规模20%以上的。

**Article 12** Under any of the following circumstances, a hazardous waste operating unit shall re-apply for a hazardous waste operating permit in accordance with the original application procedures:  
(1) Changing the mode of hazardous waste operation;  
(2) Adding categories of hazardous wastes;  
(3) Constructing a new or reconstructing or expanding an existing hazardous waste operating facility;  
(4) Operating hazardous wastes exceeding the originally approved annual operating scale by more than 20%.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　危险废物综合经营许可证有效期为5年；危险废物收集经营许可证有效期为3年。  
危险废物经营许可证有效期届满，危险废物经营单位继续从事危险废物经营活动的，应当于危险废物经营许可证有效期届满30个工作日前向原发证机关提出换证申请。原发证机关应当自受理换证申请之日起20个工作日内进行审查，符合条件的，予以换证；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 13** The term of validity of a comprehensive hazardous waste operating permit is 5 years; the term of validity of an operating permit for the collection of hazardous wastes is 3 years.  
Where a hazardous waste operating unit intends to continue engaging in hazardous waste operation activities upon the expiry of the term of validity of the hazardous waste operating permit, it shall submit an application for renewal to the original permit-issuing authority 30 working days prior to the expiry date. The original permit-issuing authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the renewal application, conduct an examination. Where the conditions are met, it shall renew the permit; where the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　危险废物经营单位终止从事收集、贮存、处置危险废物经营活动的，应当对经营设施、场所采取污染防治措施，并对未处置的危险废物作出妥善处理。  
危险废物经营单位应当在采取前款规定措施之日起20个工作日内向原发证机关提出注销申请，由原发证机关进行现场核查合格后注销危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 14** Where a hazardous waste operating unit terminates its operation activities of collection, storage or disposal of hazardous wastes, it shall take pollution prevention measures for the operating facilities and sites, and properly handle the undisposed hazardous wastes.  
The hazardous waste operating unit shall, within 20 working days from the date of taking the measures specified in the preceding paragraph, submit an application for cancellation to the original permit-issuing authority, and the original permit-issuing authority shall cancel the hazardous waste operating permit after conducting an on-site inspection and confirming that it is satisfactory.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止无经营许可证或者不按照经营许可证规定从事危险废物收集、贮存、处置经营活动。  
禁止从中华人民共和国境外进口或者经中华人民共和国过境转移电子类危险废物。  
禁止将危险废物提供或者委托给无经营许可证的单位从事收集、贮存、处置经营活动。  
禁止伪造、变造、转让危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 15** It is prohibited to engage in the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes without an operating permit or not in accordance with the provisions of the operating permit.  
It is prohibited to import electronic hazardous wastes from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China or to transfer such wastes through the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
It is prohibited to provide or entrust hazardous wastes to a unit without an operating permit for collection, storage and disposal operations.  
It is prohibited to forge, alter or transfer a hazardous waste operating permit.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门应当于每年3月31日前将上一年度危险废物经营许可证颁发情况报上一级人民政府环境保护主管部门备案。  
上级环境保护主管部门应当加强对下级环境保护主管部门审批颁发危险废物经营许可证情况的监督检查，及时纠正下级环境保护主管部门审批颁发危险废物经营许可证过程中的违法行为。

**Article 16** The competent environmental protection departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, before March 31 of each year, report the issuance of hazardous waste operating permits in the previous year to the competent environmental protection department at the next higher level for record.  
The competent environmental protection department at a higher level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the examination, approval and issuance of hazardous waste operating permits by the competent environmental protection departments at lower levels, and promptly correct any illegal acts in the process of examination, approval and issuance of hazardous waste operating permits by the competent environmental protection departments at lower levels.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门应当通过书面核查和实地检查等方式，加强对危险废物经营单位的监督检查，并将监督检查情况和处理结果予以记录，由监督检查人员签字后归档。  
公众有权查阅县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门的监督检查记录。  
县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门发现危险废物经营单位在经营活动中有不符合原发证条件的情形的，应当责令其限期整改。

**Article 17** The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of hazardous waste operating units through means such as written verification and on-site inspection, and shall record the supervision and inspection situation and the handling results, which shall be signed by the inspectors and then archived.  
The public has the right to consult the supervision and inspection records of the competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments at or above the county level.  
Where the competent environmental protection department of a people's government at or above the county level discovers that a hazardous waste operating unit no longer meets the conditions for issuance of the permit in its operation activities, it shall order it to rectify within a time limit.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门有权要求危险废物经营单位定期报告危险废物经营活动情况。危险废物经营单位应当建立危险废物经营情况记录簿，如实记载收集、贮存、处置危险废物的类别、来源、去向和有无事故等事项。  
危险废物经营单位应当将危险废物经营情况记录簿保存10年以上，以填埋方式处置危险废物的经营情况记录簿应当永久保存。终止经营活动的，应当将危险废物经营情况记录簿移交所在地县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门存档管理。

**Article 18** The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments at or above the county level have the right to require hazardous waste operating units to regularly report on the operation of hazardous wastes. A hazardous waste operating unit shall establish a record book of hazardous waste operation, truthfully recording matters such as the categories, sources, destinations and whether there have been accidents in the collection, storage and disposal of hazardous wastes.  
A hazardous waste operating unit shall keep the record book of hazardous waste operation for at least 10 years; where hazardous wastes are disposed of by landfill, the record book shall be kept permanently. Where the operation activities are terminated, the record book of hazardous waste operation shall be transferred to the competent environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level for archiving and management.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门应当建立、健全危险废物经营许可证的档案管理制度，并定期向社会公布审批颁发危险废物经营许可证的情况。

**Article 19** The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the archive management system for hazardous waste operating permits, and regularly publish to the public the situation of examination, approval and issuance of hazardous waste operating permits.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　领取危险废物收集经营许可证的单位，应当与处置单位签订接收合同，并将收集的废矿物油和废镉镍电池在90个工作日内提供或者委托给处置单位进行处置。

**Article 20** A unit that obtains an operating permit for the collection of hazardous wastes shall sign a receiving contract with a disposal unit, and shall provide or entrust the collected waste mineral oil and waste cadmium-nickel batteries to the disposal unit for disposal within 90 working days.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　危险废物的经营设施在废弃或者改作其他用途前，应当进行无害化处理。  
填埋危险废物的经营设施服役期届满后，危险废物经营单位应当按照有关规定对填埋过危险废物的土地采取封闭措施，并在划定的封闭区域设置永久性标记。

**Article 21** Before the abandonment or conversion to other uses of a hazardous waste operating facility, harmless treatment shall be carried out.  
After the service life of a landfill hazardous waste operating facility expires, the hazardous waste operating unit shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, take closure measures for the land where hazardous waste has been landfilled, and set permanent marks within the delineated closure area.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反[本办法第十一条](#art-11)规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关暂扣危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 22** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Measures shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and given a warning by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if the correction is not made within the time limit, the original licensing authority shall suspend the hazardous waste operating license.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反[本办法第十二条](#art-12)、第十三条第二款规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得超过10万元的，并处违法所得1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 23** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 12 and the second paragraph of Article 13 of these Measures shall be ordered to cease the illegal act by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains exceed 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the illegal gains shall be imposed additionally; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反[本办法第十四条](#art-14)第一款、第二十一条规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成污染事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Anyone who violates the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 14 and Article 21 of these Measures shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if the correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a pollution accident is caused and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反[本办法第十五条](#art-15)第一款、第二款、第三款规定的，依照《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》的规定予以处罚。  
违反[本办法第十五条](#art-15)第四款规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门收缴危险废物经营许可证或者由原发证机关吊销危险废物经营许可证，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Anyone who violates the provisions of the first, second, or third paragraph of Article 15 of these Measures shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution by Solid Wastes.  
Anyone who violates the provisions of the fourth paragraph of Article 15 of these Measures shall have its hazardous waste operating license confiscated by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or have its hazardous waste operating license revoked by the original licensing authority, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反[本办法第十八条](#art-18)规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 26** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 18 of these Measures shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and given a warning by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if the correction is not made within the time limit, the original licensing authority shall suspend or revoke the hazardous waste operating license.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反[本办法第二十条](#art-20)规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并可以由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 27** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 20 of these Measures shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and given a warning by the environmental protection administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if the correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the original licensing authority may suspend or revoke the hazardous waste operating license.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　危险废物经营单位被责令限期整改，逾期不整改或者经整改仍不符合原发证条件的，由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 28** Where a hazardous waste operating unit is ordered to make rectifications within a time limit, and fails to make rectifications within the time limit or still does not meet the original licensing conditions after rectification, the original licensing authority shall suspend or revoke the hazardous waste operating license.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　被依法吊销或者收缴危险废物经营许可证的单位，5年内不得再申请领取危险废物经营许可证。

**Article 29** A unit whose hazardous waste operating license has been revoked or confiscated according to law shall not apply for a new hazardous waste operating license within five years.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门的工作人员，有下列行为之一的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)向不符合本办法规定条件的单位颁发危险废物经营许可证的；  
(二)发现未依法取得危险废物经营许可证的单位和个人擅自从事危险废物经营活动不予查处或者接到举报后不依法处理的；  
(三)对依法取得危险废物经营许可证的单位不履行监督管理职责或者发现违反本办法规定的行为不予查处的；  
(四)在危险废物经营许可证管理工作中有其他渎职行为的。

**Article 30** Any staff member of the environmental protection administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level who commits any of the following acts shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing a hazardous waste operating license to a unit that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Measures;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with a unit or individual that engages in hazardous waste operating activities without obtaining a hazardous waste operating license according to law upon discovery, or failing to handle according to law after receiving a report;  
(3) failing to perform supervision and administration duties over a unit that has obtained a hazardous waste operating license according to law, or failing to investigate and deal with an act that violates the provisions of these Measures upon discovery;  
(4) having other acts of dereliction of duty in the management of hazardous waste operating licenses.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本办法下列用语的含义：  
(一)危险废物，是指列入国家危险废物名录或者根据国家规定的危险废物鉴别标准和鉴别方法认定的具有危险性的废物。  
(二)收集，是指危险废物经营单位将分散的危险废物进行集中的活动。  
(三)贮存，是指危险废物经营单位在危险废物处置前，将其放置在符合环境保护标准的场所或者设施中，以及为了将分散的危险废物进行集中，在自备的临时设施或者场所每批置放重量超过5000千克或者置放时间超过90个工作日的活动。  
(四)处置，是指危险废物经营单位将危险废物焚烧、煅烧、熔融、烧结、裂解、中和、消毒、蒸馏、萃取、沉淀、过滤、拆解以及用其他改变危险废物物理、化学、生物特性的方法，达到减少危险废物数量、缩小危险废物体积、减少或者消除其危险成分的活动，或者将危险废物最终置于符合环境保护规定要求的场所或者设施并不再回取的活动。

**Article 31** For the purposes of these Measures, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Hazardous waste" means waste that is listed in the national hazardous waste list or identified as hazardous according to the national hazardous waste identification standards and identification methods.  
(2) "Collection" means the activity of a hazardous waste operating unit to centralize scattered hazardous waste.  
(3) "Storage" means the activity of a hazardous waste operating unit, prior to the disposal of hazardous waste, to place it in a venue or facility that meets environmental protection standards, and, for the purpose of centralizing scattered hazardous waste, to place it in temporary facilities or venues owned by the unit where the weight of each batch exceeds 5,000 kilograms or the placement time exceeds 90 working days.  
(4) "Disposal" means the activities of a hazardous waste operating unit to reduce the quantity, reduce the volume, or reduce or eliminate the hazardous components of hazardous waste through incineration, calcination, melting, sintering, cracking, neutralization, disinfection, distillation, extraction, precipitation, filtration, dismantling, or other methods that change the physical, chemical, or biological characteristics of hazardous waste, or the activity of final placement of hazardous waste in a venue or facility that meets the requirements of environmental protection regulations without retrieval.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本办法施行前，依照地方性法规、规章或者其他文件的规定已经取得危险废物经营许可证的单位，应当在原危险废物经营许可证有效期届满30个工作日前，依照本办法的规定重新申请领取危险废物经营许可证。逾期不办理的，不得继续从事危险废物经营活动。

**Article 32** A unit that, before these Measures come into force, has obtained a hazardous waste operating license in accordance with the provisions of local regulations, rules, or other documents, shall, 30 working days before the expiration of the validity period of the original hazardous waste operating license, re-apply for a hazardous waste operating license in accordance with these Measures. If it fails to do so within the time limit, it shall not continue to engage in hazardous waste operating activities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本办法自2004年7月1日起施行。

**Article 33** These Measures shall come into force as of July 1, 2004.
