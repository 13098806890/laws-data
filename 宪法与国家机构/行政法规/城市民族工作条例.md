# 城市民族工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1993-09-15  
**生效日期**：1993-09-15

> (1993年8月29日国务院批准　1993年9月15日国家民族事务委员会令第2号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强城市民族工作，保障城市少数民族的合法权益，促进适应城市少数民族需要的经济、文化事业的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称的城市，是指国家按照行政建制设立的直辖市、市。 第三条　城市民族工作坚持民族平等、团结、互助和促进各民族共同繁荣的原则。 第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将城市民族工作作为一项重要职责，加强领导，统筹安排。 第五条　城市人民政府应当将适应当地少数民族需要的经济、文化事业列入国民经济和社会发展计划。 城市人民政府对于发展适应当地少数民族需要的经济、文化事业的资金，可以根据财力给予适当照顾。 第六条　城市人民政府根据实际情况，可以确定负责民族事务工作的部门或者配备专职干部，管理民族事务。 第七条　少数民族人口较多的城市的人民政府、少数民族聚居的街道的办事处，以及直接为少数民族生产、生活服务的部门或者单位，应当配备适当数量的少数民族干部。 第八条　城市人民政府应当重视少数民族干部的培养和选拔。 城市人民政府有关部门应当重视少数民族专业技术人员的培养和使用。 城市人民政府鼓励企业招收少数民族职工。 第九条　城市人民政府应当重视发展少数民族教育事业，加强对少数民族教育事业的领导和支持。 城市人民政府应当采取适当措施，提高少数民族教师队伍的素质，办好各级各类民族学校(班)，在经费、教师配备方面对民族学校(班)给予适当照顾，并根据当地少数民族的特点发展各种职业技术教育和成人教育。 地方招生部门可以按照国家有关规定，结合当地实际情况，对义务教育后阶段的少数民族考生，招生时给予适当照顾。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城市民族工作，保障城市少数民族的合法权益，促进适应城市少数民族需要的经济、文化事业的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening urban ethnic work, protecting the lawful rights and interests of urban ethnic minorities, and promoting the development of economic and cultural undertakings that meet the needs of urban ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称的城市，是指国家按照行政建制设立的直辖市、市。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "cities" means municipalities directly under the Central Government and other cities established by the State in accordance with the administrative divisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市民族工作坚持民族平等、团结、互助和促进各民族共同繁荣的原则。

**Article 3** Urban ethnic work shall adhere to the principles of ethnic equality, unity, mutual assistance, and the promotion of common prosperity of all ethnic groups.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将城市民族工作作为一项重要职责，加强领导，统筹安排。

**Article 4** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regard urban ethnic work as an important duty, strengthen leadership, and make overall arrangements.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　城市人民政府应当将适应当地少数民族需要的经济、文化事业列入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
城市人民政府对于发展适应当地少数民族需要的经济、文化事业的资金，可以根据财力给予适当照顾。

**Article 5** The people's government of a city shall include economic and cultural undertakings that meet the needs of local ethnic minorities in the national economic and social development plan.  
The people's government of a city may, based on its financial resources, grant appropriate preferences regarding funds for developing the economic and cultural undertakings that meet the needs of local ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　城市人民政府根据实际情况，可以确定负责民族事务工作的部门或者配备专职干部，管理民族事务。

**Article 6** The people's government of a city may, based on the actual circumstances, designate a department responsible for ethnic affairs or appoint full-time cadres to manage ethnic affairs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　少数民族人口较多的城市的人民政府、少数民族聚居的街道的办事处，以及直接为少数民族生产、生活服务的部门或者单位，应当配备适当数量的少数民族干部。

**Article 7** The people's government of a city with a relatively large ethnic minority population, the subdistrict offices in areas where ethnic minorities live in concentrated communities, and the departments or units directly providing services for the production and daily life of ethnic minorities shall appoint an appropriate number of ethnic minority cadres.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　城市人民政府应当重视少数民族干部的培养和选拔。  
城市人民政府有关部门应当重视少数民族专业技术人员的培养和使用。  
城市人民政府鼓励企业招收少数民族职工。

**Article 8** The people's government of a city shall attach importance to the cultivation and selection of ethnic minority cadres.  
The relevant departments of the people's government of a city shall attach importance to the cultivation and utilization of ethnic minority professional and technical personnel.  
The people's government of a city shall encourage enterprises to recruit ethnic minority employees.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市人民政府应当重视发展少数民族教育事业，加强对少数民族教育事业的领导和支持。  
城市人民政府应当采取适当措施，提高少数民族教师队伍的素质，办好各级各类民族学校(班)，在经费、教师配备方面对民族学校(班)给予适当照顾，并根据当地少数民族的特点发展各种职业技术教育和成人教育。  
地方招生部门可以按照国家有关规定，结合当地实际情况，对义务教育后阶段的少数民族考生，招生时给予适当照顾。

**Article 9** The people's government of a city shall attach importance to developing ethnic minority education and strengthen leadership and support for ethnic minority education.  
The people's government of a city shall adopt appropriate measures to improve the quality of the ethnic minority teacher team, run ethnic minority schools (classes) at all levels and of various types well, grant appropriate preferences to ethnic minority schools (classes) in terms of funds and teacher allocation, and develop various types of vocational and technical education and adult education in light of the characteristics of local ethnic minorities.  
Local admission authorities may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and in light of the local actual circumstances, grant appropriate preferences in the admission of ethnic minority examinees after compulsory education.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　信贷部门对以少数民族为主要服务对象的从事食品生产、加工、经营和饮食服务的国有企业和集体企业，在贷款额度、还款期限、自有资金比例方面给予优惠。

**Article 10** Credit departments shall grant preferences to state-owned enterprises and collective enterprises that primarily serve ethnic minorities and are engaged in food production, processing, distribution, and catering services, in terms of loan amount, repayment period, and ratio of self-owned funds.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城市人民政府对[本条例第十条](#art-10)所列企业以及生产经营少数民族用品企业的贷款，可以根据当地的实际需要和条件，予以贴息。

**Article 11** The people's government of a city may, based on the local actual needs and conditions, grant interest subsidies on loans to the enterprises listed in Article 10 of these Regulations and to enterprises producing and operating ethnic minority products.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　[本条例第十条](#art-10)所列企业纳税确有困难的，税务机关依照有关税收法律、法规的规定，给予减税或者免税。

**Article 12** Where an enterprise listed in Article 10 of these Regulations genuinely has difficulty in paying taxes, the tax authorities shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant tax laws and regulations, grant tax reductions or exemptions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　城市人民政府应当根据实际需要，合理设置清真饭店和清真食品生产加工、供应网点，并在投资、贷款、税收等方面给予扶持。

**Article 13** The people's government of a city shall, based on actual needs, rationally establish halal restaurants and halal food production, processing, and supply outlets, and shall provide support in terms of investment, loans, taxation, and other aspects.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对城市民族贸易企业和民族用品定点生产企业的优惠，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 14** Preferences for urban ethnic trade enterprises and designated producers of ethnic products shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市人民政府应当支持并组织有关经济、技术部门，加强同少数民族地区和农村散杂居少数民族开展横向经济技术协作。

**Article 15** The people's government of a city shall support and organize relevant economic and technical departments to strengthen horizontal economic and technical cooperation with ethnic minority areas and with ethnic minorities living in scattered communities in rural areas.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城市人民政府有关部门对进入本市兴办企业和从事其他合法经营活动的外地少数民族人员，应当根据情况提供便利条件，予以支持。  
城市人民政府应当加强对少数民族流动人员的教育和管理，保护其合法权益。  
少数民族流动人员应当自觉遵守国家的法律、法规，服从当地人民政府有关部门的管理。

**Article 16** The relevant departments of the people's government of a city shall, based on the circumstances, provide facilitation and support to ethnic minority persons from other areas who enter the city to establish enterprises or engage in other lawful business activities.  
The people's government of a city shall strengthen the education and management of ethnic minority migrant persons and protect their lawful rights and interests.  
Ethnic minority migrant persons shall consciously abide by the laws and regulations of the State and submit to the management of the relevant departments of the local people's government.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市人民政府应当教育各民族干部、群众相互尊重民族风俗习惯。宣传、报导、文艺创作、电影电视摄制，应当尊重少数民族风俗习惯、宗教信仰和民族感情。

**Article 17** The people's government of a city shall educate cadres and the masses of all ethnic groups to respect each other's ethnic customs. Publicity, reporting, literary and artistic creation, and film and television production shall respect the ethnic customs, religious beliefs, and ethnic sentiments of ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　清真饮食服务企业和食品生产、加工企业必须配备一定比例的食用清真食品的少数民族职工和管理干部。清真食品的运输车辆、计量器具、储藏容器和加工、出售场地应当保证专用。  
清真饮食服务企业和食品生产、加工企业实行承包、租赁时，一般应当由有关少数民族人员承包或者租赁。清真饮食服务企业和食品生产、加工企业兼并或者被兼并时，不得随意改变其服务方向，确实需要改变服务方向的，必须征得当地城市人民政府民族事务工作部门同意。

**Article 18** Halal catering service enterprises and food production and processing enterprises must employ a certain proportion of ethnic minority employees and management cadres who consume halal food. The transport vehicles, measuring instruments, storage containers, and processing and sales premises for halal food shall be dedicated.  
When halal catering service enterprises and food production and processing enterprises are contracted out or leased, the contracting or leasing shall generally be undertaken by relevant ethnic minority persons. When such enterprises merge or are merged, their service direction shall not be changed arbitrarily; if a change in service direction is genuinely necessary, the consent of the ethnic affairs department of the local people's government of the city must be obtained.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　少数民族人口较多的城市的人民政府，应当根据需要和条件，设立具有民族特点的文化馆(站)、图书馆。

**Article 19** The people's government of a city with a relatively large ethnic minority population shall, based on needs and conditions, establish cultural centers (stations) and libraries with ethnic characteristics.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　城市人民政府应当保障少数民族使用本民族语言文字的权利，并根据需要和条件，按照国家有关规定加强少数民族文字的翻译、出版和教学研究。

**Article 20** The people's government of a city shall protect the right of ethnic minorities to use their own languages and scripts, and shall, based on needs and conditions, strengthen the translation, publication, and teaching research of ethnic minority languages and scripts in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　少数民族人口较多的城市的人民政府，应当根据实际需要和条件，建立民族医院、民族医药学研究机构，发展少数民族传统医药科学。

**Article 21** The people's government of a city with a relatively large ethnic minority population shall, based on actual needs and conditions, establish ethnic hospitals and ethnic medical research institutions to develop the traditional medical sciences of ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　城市人民政府应当在少数民族中加强计划生育的宣传、教育和指导工作。

**Article 22** The people's government of a city shall strengthen the promotion, education, and guidance of family planning among ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　城市人民政府在少数民族聚居的街道，应当按照城市规划，保护和建设具有民族风格的建筑物。

**Article 23** The people's government of a city shall, in accordance with urban planning, protect and construct buildings with ethnic styles in streets where ethnic minorities live in concentrated communities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　城市人民政府应当保障少数民族保持或者改革民族风俗习惯的自由。

**Article 24** The people's government of a city shall protect the freedom of ethnic minorities to maintain or reform their ethnic customs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　城市人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，对具有特殊丧葬习俗的少数民族妥善安排墓地，并采取措施加强少数民族的殡葬服务。  
城市人民政府对少数民族人员自愿实行丧葬改革的，应当给予支持。

**Article 25** The people's government of a city shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, properly arrange cemeteries for ethnic minorities with special funeral customs, and shall take measures to strengthen funeral services for ethnic minorities.  
The people's government of a city shall support ethnic minority persons who voluntarily carry out funeral reforms.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　少数民族职工参加本民族重大节日活动，可以按照国家有关规定放假，并照发工资。

**Article 26** Ethnic minority employees who participate in major festivals of their own ethnic groups may be granted leave in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and their wages shall be paid as usual.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城市人民政府对于在城市民族工作中做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 27** The people's government of a city shall commend and reward units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements and contributions in urban ethnic work.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例，结合当地实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 28** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Regulations and in light of local actual circumstances, formulate implementing measures.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例由国家民族事务委员会负责解释。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Ethnic Affairs Commission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
