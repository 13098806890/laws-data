# 兽药管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-03-27  
**生效日期**：2020-03-27

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强兽药管理，保证兽药质量，防治动物疾病，促进养殖业的发展，维护人体健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of veterinary drugs, ensuring the quality of veterinary drugs, preventing and treating animal diseases, promoting the development of the breeding industry, and safeguarding human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事兽药的研制、生产、经营、进出口、使用和监督管理，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in the research and development, production, marketing, import and export, use, and supervision and administration of veterinary drugs within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院兽医行政管理部门负责全国的兽药监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府兽医行政管理部门负责本行政区域内的兽药监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of veterinary drugs nationwide.  
The veterinary administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of veterinary drugs within its administrative region.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行兽用处方药和非处方药分类管理制度。兽用处方药和非处方药分类管理的办法和具体实施步骤，由国务院兽医行政管理部门规定。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a classified management system for veterinary prescription drugs and non-prescription drugs. The measures and specific implementation steps for the classified management of veterinary prescription drugs and non-prescription drugs shall be prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家实行兽药储备制度。  
发生重大动物疫情、灾情或者其他突发事件时，国务院兽医行政管理部门可以紧急调用国家储备的兽药；必要时，也可以调用国家储备以外的兽药。

**Article 5** The State shall implement a veterinary drug reserve system.  
In the event of a major animal epidemic, disaster, or other emergency, the veterinary administrative department under the State Council may urgently deploy veterinary drugs from the national reserve; when necessary, it may also deploy veterinary drugs outside the national reserve.

### 第二章　新兽药研制

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励研制新兽药，依法保护研制者的合法权益。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage the research and development of new veterinary drugs and protect the legitimate rights and interests of the developers according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　研制新兽药，应当具有与研制相适应的场所、仪器设备、专业技术人员、安全管理规范和措施。  
研制新兽药，应当进行安全性评价。从事兽药安全性评价的单位应当遵守国务院兽医行政管理部门制定的兽药非临床研究质量管理规范和兽药临床试验质量管理规范。  
省级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门应当对兽药安全性评价单位是否符合兽药非临床研究质量管理规范和兽药临床试验质量管理规范的要求进行监督检查，并公布监督检查结果。

**Article 7** For the research and development of a new veterinary drug, there shall be facilities, instruments and equipment, professional technical personnel, safety management norms, and measures appropriate for such research and development.  
A safety evaluation shall be conducted for the research and development of a new veterinary drug. The entity conducting the veterinary drug safety evaluation shall comply with the Good Laboratory Practice for Veterinary Drugs and the Good Clinical Practice for Veterinary Drugs formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
The veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall supervise and inspect whether veterinary drug safety evaluation entities meet the requirements of the Good Laboratory Practice for Veterinary Drugs and the Good Clinical Practice for Veterinary Drugs, and shall publish the results of such supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　研制新兽药，应当在临床试验前向临床试验场所所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门备案，并附具该新兽药实验室阶段安全性评价报告及其他临床前研究资料。  
研制的新兽药属于生物制品的，应当在临床试验前向国务院兽医行政管理部门提出申请，国务院兽医行政管理部门应当自收到申请之日起60个工作日内将审查结果书面通知申请人。  
研制新兽药需要使用一类病原微生物的，还应当具备国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的条件，并在实验室阶段前报国务院兽医行政管理部门批准。

**Article 8** For the research and development of a new veterinary drug, the developer shall file a record with the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the clinical trial site is located before the clinical trial, and attach the safety evaluation report of the laboratory stage of such new veterinary drug and other pre-clinical research materials.  
If the new veterinary drug under research and development is a biological product, the developer shall submit an application to the veterinary administrative department under the State Council before the clinical trial, and the veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall notify the applicant in writing of the review results within 60 working days from the date of receipt of the application.  
If the research and development of a new veterinary drug requires the use of a Class I pathogenic microorganism, the developer shall also meet the conditions prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council and report to the veterinary administrative department under the State Council for approval before the laboratory stage.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　临床试验完成后，新兽药研制者向国务院兽医行政管理部门提出新兽药注册申请时，应当提交该新兽药的样品和下列资料：  
(一)名称、主要成分、理化性质；  
(二)研制方法、生产工艺、质量标准和检测方法；  
(三)药理和毒理试验结果、临床试验报告和稳定性试验报告；  
(四)环境影响报告和污染防治措施。  
研制的新兽药属于生物制品的，还应当提供菌(毒、虫)种、细胞等有关材料和资料。菌(毒、虫)种、细胞由国务院兽医行政管理部门指定的机构保藏。  
研制用于食用动物的新兽药，还应当按照国务院兽医行政管理部门的规定进行兽药残留试验并提供休药期、最高残留限量标准、残留检测方法及其制定依据等资料。  
国务院兽医行政管理部门应当自收到申请之日起10个工作日内，将决定受理的新兽药资料送其设立的兽药评审机构进行评审，将新兽药样品送其指定的检验机构复核检验，并自收到评审和复核检验结论之日起60个工作日内完成审查。审查合格的，发给新兽药注册证书，并发布该兽药的质量标准；不合格的，应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 9** Upon completion of the clinical trial, when the developer of a new veterinary drug submits an application for registration of the new veterinary drug to the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, it shall submit samples of such new veterinary drug and the following materials:  
(1) Name, main ingredients, and physical and chemical properties;  
(2) Development method, production process, quality standards, and testing methods;  
(3) Results of pharmacological and toxicological tests, clinical trial reports, and stability test reports;  
(4) Environmental impact report and pollution prevention measures.  
If the new veterinary drug under development is a biological product, the developer shall also provide relevant materials and information concerning the strains (viruses, toxins, insects), cells, etc. The strains (viruses, toxins, insects) and cells shall be deposited by an institution designated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
If the new veterinary drug under development is for food-producing animals, the developer shall also conduct veterinary drug residue tests in accordance with the provisions of the veterinary administrative department under the State Council and provide information such as withdrawal periods, maximum residue limits standards, residue testing methods, and the basis for their formulation.  
The veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall, within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the application, submit the materials of the new veterinary drug determined to be accepted to the veterinary drug evaluation institution established by it for evaluation, and submit the samples of the new veterinary drug to the inspection institution designated by it for re-inspection; and shall complete the review within 60 working days from the date of receipt of the evaluation and re-inspection conclusions. If the review is passed, it shall issue a new veterinary drug registration certificate and publish the quality standards for such veterinary drug; if not passed, it shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家对依法获得注册的、含有新化合物的兽药的申请人提交的其自己所取得且未披露的试验数据和其他数据实施保护。  
自注册之日起6年内，对其他申请人未经已获得注册兽药的申请人同意，使用前款规定的数据申请兽药注册的，兽药注册机关不予注册；但是，其他申请人提交其自己所取得的数据的除外。  
除下列情况外，兽药注册机关不得披露本条第一款规定的数据：  
(一)公共利益需要；  
(二)已采取措施确保该类信息不会被不正当地进行商业使用。

**Article 10** The State shall protect the test data and other data submitted by the applicant of a veterinary drug that has been lawfully registered and contains a new chemical entity, which data were obtained by the applicant and have not been disclosed.  
Within 6 years from the date of registration, if any other applicant uses the data specified in the preceding paragraph to apply for the registration of a veterinary drug without the consent of the applicant of the registered veterinary drug, the veterinary drug registration authority shall not grant registration; however, this shall not apply if the other applicant submits data obtained by itself.  
Except in the following circumstances, the veterinary drug registration authority shall not disclose the data specified in paragraph 1 of this Article:  
(1) Required by the public interest;  
(2) Measures have been taken to ensure that such information will not be improperly used for commercial purposes.

### 第三章　兽药生产

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　从事兽药生产的企业，应当符合国家兽药行业发展规划和产业政策，并具备下列条件：  
(一)与所生产的兽药相适应的兽医学、药学或者相关专业的技术人员；  
(二)与所生产的兽药相适应的厂房、设施；  
(三)与所生产的兽药相适应的兽药质量管理和质量检验的机构、人员、仪器设备；  
(四)符合安全、卫生要求的生产环境；  
(五)兽药生产质量管理规范规定的其他生产条件。  
符合前款规定条件的，申请人方可向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门提出申请，并附具符合前款规定条件的证明材料；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门应当自收到申请之日起40个工作日内完成审查。经审查合格的，发给兽药生产许可证；不合格的，应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 11** An enterprise engaged in the production of veterinary drugs shall comply with the national veterinary drug industry development plan and industrial policies, and shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having technical personnel in veterinary medicine, pharmacy, or related specialties appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be produced;  
(2) Having factory buildings and facilities appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be produced;  
(3) Having institutions, personnel, instruments, and equipment for veterinary drug quality management and quality inspection appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be produced;  
(4) Having a production environment that meets safety and hygiene requirements;  
(5) Other production conditions stipulated in the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Drugs.  
Only an applicant that meets the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph may submit an application to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph; the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the review within 40 working days from the date of receipt of the application. If the review is passed, it shall issue a Veterinary Drug Production License; if not passed, it shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　兽药生产许可证应当载明生产范围、生产地点、有效期和法定代表人姓名、住址等事项。  
兽药生产许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续生产兽药的，应当在许可证有效期届满前6个月到发证机关申请换发兽药生产许可证。

**Article 12** A Veterinary Drug Production License shall indicate the production scope, production location, validity period, and the name and address of the legal representative, among other matters.  
The validity period of a Veterinary Drug Production License shall be five years. If a veterinary drug production enterprise needs to continue producing veterinary drugs upon the expiration of the validity period, it shall apply to the license-issuing authority for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Production License within the six months prior to the expiration of the license validity period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　兽药生产企业变更生产范围、生产地点的，应当依照[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定申请换发兽药生产许可证；变更企业名称、法定代表人的，应当在办理工商变更登记手续后15个工作日内，到发证机关申请换发兽药生产许可证。

**Article 13** If a veterinary drug production enterprise changes its production scope or production location, it shall apply for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Production License in accordance with Article 11 of these Regulations; if it changes its enterprise name or legal representative, it shall apply to the license-issuing authority for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Production License within 15 working days after completing the industrial and commercial change registration procedures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　兽药生产企业应当按照国务院兽医行政管理部门制定的兽药生产质量管理规范组织生产。  
省级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门，应当对兽药生产企业是否符合兽药生产质量管理规范的要求进行监督检查，并公布检查结果。

**Article 14** A veterinary drug production enterprise shall organize production in accordance with the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Drugs formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
The veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall supervise and inspect whether veterinary drug production enterprises meet the requirements of the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Drugs, and shall publish the inspection results.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　兽药生产企业生产兽药，应当取得国务院兽医行政管理部门核发的产品批准文号，产品批准文号的有效期为5年。兽药产品批准文号的核发办法由国务院兽医行政管理部门制定。

**Article 15** A veterinary drug production enterprise that produces veterinary drugs shall obtain a product approval number issued by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council. The validity period of a product approval number shall be five years. The measures for the issuance of veterinary drug product approval numbers shall be formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　兽药生产企业应当按照兽药国家标准和国务院兽医行政管理部门批准的生产工艺进行生产。兽药生产企业改变影响兽药质量的生产工艺的，应当报原批准部门审核批准。  
兽药生产企业应当建立生产记录，生产记录应当完整、准确。

**Article 16** A veterinary drug production enterprise shall produce veterinary drugs in accordance with the national veterinary drug standards and the production process approved by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council. If a veterinary drug production enterprise changes a production process that affects the quality of veterinary drugs, it shall report to the original approving department for review and approval.  
A veterinary drug production enterprise shall establish production records, and the production records shall be complete and accurate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　生产兽药所需的原料、辅料，应当符合国家标准或者所生产兽药的质量要求。  
直接接触兽药的包装材料和容器应当符合药用要求。

**Article 17** The raw materials and auxiliary materials required for the production of veterinary drugs shall meet the national standards or the quality requirements of the veterinary drugs to be produced.  
Packaging materials and containers that come into direct contact with veterinary drugs shall meet pharmaceutical requirements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　兽药出厂前应当经过质量检验，不符合质量标准的不得出厂。  
兽药出厂应当附有产品质量合格证。  
禁止生产假、劣兽药。

**Article 18** Veterinary drugs shall undergo quality inspection before leaving the factory; those that do not meet the quality standards shall not leave the factory.  
Veterinary drugs shall be accompanied by a product quality certificate when leaving the factory.  
The production of fake or inferior veterinary drugs is prohibited.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　兽药生产企业生产的每批兽用生物制品，在出厂前应当由国务院兽医行政管理部门指定的检验机构审查核对，并在必要时进行抽查检验；未经审查核对或者抽查检验不合格的，不得销售。  
强制免疫所需兽用生物制品，由国务院兽医行政管理部门指定的企业生产。

**Article 19** Each batch of veterinary biological products produced by a veterinary drug production enterprise shall, before leaving the factory, be examined and verified by an inspection institution designated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and shall be subject to random inspection and testing when necessary; those that have not been examined and verified or that fail the random inspection and testing shall not be sold.  
Veterinary biological products required for compulsory immunization shall be produced by enterprises designated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　兽药包装应当按照规定印有或者贴有标签，附具说明书，并在显著位置注明“兽用”字样。  
兽药的标签和说明书经国务院兽医行政管理部门批准并公布后，方可使用。  
兽药的标签或者说明书，应当以中文注明兽药的通用名称、成分及其含量、规格、生产企业、产品批准文号(进口兽药注册证号)、产品批号、生产日期、有效期、适应症或者功能主治、用法、用量、休药期、禁忌、不良反应、注意事项、运输贮存保管条件及其他应当说明的内容。有商品名称的，还应当注明商品名称。  
除前款规定的内容外，兽用处方药的标签或者说明书还应当印有国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的警示内容，其中兽用麻醉药品、精神药品、毒性药品和放射性药品还应当印有国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的特殊标志；兽用非处方药的标签或者说明书还应当印有国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的非处方药标志。

**Article 20** The packaging of veterinary drugs shall bear or be affixed with a label and be accompanied by instructions, and the words "For Veterinary Use Only" shall be indicated in a conspicuous position.  
The labels and instructions of veterinary drugs may be used only after they have been approved and published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
The label or instructions of a veterinary drug shall, in Chinese, indicate the generic name of the veterinary drug, its ingredients and their content, specifications, the name of the production enterprise, the product approval number (or the import veterinary drug registration certificate number), the product batch number, the date of manufacture, the expiry date, the indications or functions, the usage and dosage, the withdrawal period, contraindications, adverse reactions, precautions, conditions for transportation, storage, and keeping, and other content that shall be stated. If there is a trade name, it shall also be indicated.  
In addition to the content specified in the preceding paragraph, the label or instructions of a veterinary prescription drug shall also bear warning content prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and for veterinary narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, toxic drugs, and radioactive drugs, they shall also bear special markings prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council; the label or instructions of a veterinary non-prescription drug shall also bear a non-prescription drug marking prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院兽医行政管理部门，根据保证动物产品质量安全和人体健康的需要，可以对新兽药设立不超过5年的监测期；在监测期内，不得批准其他企业生产或者进口该新兽药。生产企业应当在监测期内收集该新兽药的疗效、不良反应等资料，并及时报送国务院兽医行政管理部门。

**Article 21** The veterinary administrative department under the State Council may, based on the need to ensure the quality and safety of animal products and human health, establish a monitoring period of not more than five years for a new veterinary drug; during the monitoring period, no other enterprise shall be approved to produce or import such new veterinary drug. The production enterprise shall, during the monitoring period, collect information on the efficacy, adverse reactions, etc., of such new veterinary drug, and promptly submit it to the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

### 第四章　兽药经营

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经营兽药的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)与所经营的兽药相适应的兽药技术人员；  
(二)与所经营的兽药相适应的营业场所、设备、仓库设施；  
(三)与所经营的兽药相适应的质量管理机构或者人员；  
(四)兽药经营质量管理规范规定的其他经营条件。  
符合前款规定条件的，申请人方可向市、县人民政府兽医行政管理部门提出申请，并附具符合前款规定条件的证明材料；经营兽用生物制品的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门提出申请，并附具符合前款规定条件的证明材料。  
县级以上地方人民政府兽医行政管理部门，应当自收到申请之日起30个工作日内完成审查。审查合格的，发给兽药经营许可证；不合格的，应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 22** An enterprise engaged in the marketing of veterinary drugs shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having veterinary drug technical personnel appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be marketed;  
(2) Having business premises, equipment, and warehouse facilities appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be marketed;  
(3) Having a quality management institution or personnel appropriate to the veterinary drugs to be marketed;  
(4) Other marketing conditions stipulated in the Good Supply Practice for Veterinary Drugs.  
Only an applicant that meets the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph may submit an application to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the city or county, together with evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph; if the applicant markets veterinary biological products, it shall submit an application to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph.  
The veterinary administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall complete the review within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application. If the review is passed, it shall issue a Veterinary Drug Supply License; if not passed, it shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　兽药经营许可证应当载明经营范围、经营地点、有效期和法定代表人姓名、住址等事项。  
兽药经营许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续经营兽药的，应当在许可证有效期届满前6个月到发证机关申请换发兽药经营许可证。

**Article 23** A Veterinary Drug Supply License shall indicate the business scope, business location, validity period, and the name and address of the legal representative, among other matters.  
The validity period of a Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be five years. If a veterinary drug supply enterprise needs to continue marketing veterinary drugs upon the expiration of the validity period, it shall apply to the license-issuing authority for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Supply License within the six months prior to the expiration of the license validity period.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　兽药经营企业变更经营范围、经营地点的，应当依照[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)的规定申请换发兽药经营许可证；变更企业名称、法定代表人的，应当在办理工商变更登记手续后15个工作日内，到发证机关申请换发兽药经营许可证。

**Article 24** If a veterinary drug supply enterprise changes its business scope or business location, it shall apply for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Supply License in accordance with Article 22 of these Regulations; if it changes its enterprise name or legal representative, it shall apply to the license-issuing authority for renewal of the Veterinary Drug Supply License within 15 working days after completing the industrial and commercial change registration procedures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　兽药经营企业，应当遵守国务院兽医行政管理部门制定的兽药经营质量管理规范。  
县级以上地方人民政府兽医行政管理部门，应当对兽药经营企业是否符合兽药经营质量管理规范的要求进行监督检查，并公布检查结果。

**Article 25** A veterinary drug supply enterprise shall comply with the Good Supply Practice for Veterinary Drugs formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
The veterinary administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect whether veterinary drug supply enterprises meet the requirements of the Good Supply Practice for Veterinary Drugs, and shall publish the inspection results.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　兽药经营企业购进兽药，应当将兽药产品与产品标签或者说明书、产品质量合格证核对无误。

**Article 26** When purchasing veterinary drugs, a veterinary drug supply enterprise shall check the veterinary drug product against the product label or instructions and the product quality certificate to ensure accuracy.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　兽药经营企业，应当向购买者说明兽药的功能主治、用法、用量和注意事项。销售兽用处方药的，应当遵守兽用处方药管理办法。  
兽药经营企业销售兽用中药材的，应当注明产地。  
禁止兽药经营企业经营人用药品和假、劣兽药。

**Article 27** A veterinary drug supply enterprise shall explain to the purchaser the functions and indications, usage, dosage, and precautions of the veterinary drug. If it sells veterinary prescription drugs, it shall comply with the measures for the administration of veterinary prescription drugs.  
If a veterinary drug supply enterprise sells veterinary Chinese medicinal materials, it shall indicate the place of origin.  
It is prohibited for a veterinary drug supply enterprise to market human drugs or fake or inferior veterinary drugs.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　兽药经营企业购销兽药，应当建立购销记录。购销记录应当载明兽药的商品名称、通用名称、剂型、规格、批号、有效期、生产厂商、购销单位、购销数量、购销日期和国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 28** A veterinary drug supply enterprise shall establish purchase and sale records for the purchase and sale of veterinary drugs. The purchase and sale records shall indicate the trade name, generic name, dosage form, specifications, batch number, expiry date, manufacturer, purchasing and selling units, quantities purchased and sold, dates of purchase and sale, and other matters prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　兽药经营企业，应当建立兽药保管制度，采取必要的冷藏、防冻、防潮、防虫、防鼠等措施，保持所经营兽药的质量。  
兽药入库、出库，应当执行检查验收制度，并有准确记录。

**Article 29** A veterinary drug supply enterprise shall establish a veterinary drug storage system, and take necessary measures such as refrigeration, freeze protection, moisture protection, insect prevention, and rodent prevention to maintain the quality of the veterinary drugs it markets.  
The warehousing and outbound delivery of veterinary drugs shall be subject to an inspection and acceptance system, and accurate records shall be kept.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　强制免疫所需兽用生物制品的经营，应当符合国务院兽医行政管理部门的规定。

**Article 30** The marketing of veterinary biological products required for compulsory immunization shall comply with the provisions of the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　兽药广告的内容应当与兽药说明书内容相一致，在全国重点媒体发布兽药广告的，应当经国务院兽医行政管理部门审查批准，取得兽药广告审查批准文号。在地方媒体发布兽药广告的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门审查批准，取得兽药广告审查批准文号；未经批准的，不得发布。

**Article 31** The content of a veterinary drug advertisement shall be consistent with the content of the veterinary drug instructions. If a veterinary drug advertisement is to be published in national key media, it shall be subject to review and approval by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and an approval number for the veterinary drug advertisement review shall be obtained. If a veterinary drug advertisement is to be published in local media, it shall be subject to review and approval by the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and an approval number for the veterinary drug advertisement review shall be obtained; without approval, such advertisements shall not be published.

### 第五章　兽药进出口

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　首次向中国出口的兽药，由出口方驻中国境内的办事机构或者其委托的中国境内代理机构向国务院兽医行政管理部门申请注册，并提交下列资料和物品：  
(一)生产企业所在国家(地区)兽药管理部门批准生产、销售的证明文件。  
(二)生产企业所在国家(地区)兽药管理部门颁发的符合兽药生产质量管理规范的证明文件。  
(三)兽药的制造方法、生产工艺、质量标准、检测方法、药理和毒理试验结果、临床试验报告、稳定性试验报告及其他相关资料；用于食用动物的兽药的休药期、最高残留限量标准、残留检测方法及其制定依据等资料。  
(四)兽药的标签和说明书样本。  
(五)兽药的样品、对照品、标准品。  
(六)环境影响报告和污染防治措施。  
(七)涉及兽药安全性的其他资料。  
申请向中国出口兽用生物制品的，还应当提供菌(毒、虫)种、细胞等有关材料和资料。

**Article 32** For a veterinary drug to be exported to China for the first time, the office of the exporter stationed in China or its entrusted agent within the territory of China shall apply for registration to the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and submit the following materials and items:  
(1) Documents proving approval for production and marketing by the veterinary drug regulatory authority of the country (region) where the production enterprise is located;  
(2) Documents proving compliance with the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Drugs, issued by the veterinary drug regulatory authority of the country (region) where the production enterprise is located;  
(3) The manufacturing method, production process, quality standards, testing methods, results of pharmacological and toxicological tests, clinical trial reports, stability test reports, and other relevant materials; for veterinary drugs used for food-producing animals, information on withdrawal periods, maximum residue limits standards, residue testing methods, and the basis for their formulation;  
(4) Samples of the label and instructions of the veterinary drug;  
(5) Samples, reference substances, and standard substances of the veterinary drug;  
(6) Environmental impact report and pollution prevention measures;  
(7) Other materials concerning the safety of the veterinary drug.  
If applying for the export of veterinary biological products to China, the applicant shall also provide relevant materials and information concerning the strains (viruses, toxins, insects), cells, etc.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院兽医行政管理部门，应当自收到申请之日起10个工作日内组织初步审查。经初步审查合格的，应当将决定受理的兽药资料送其设立的兽药评审机构进行评审，将该兽药样品送其指定的检验机构复核检验，并自收到评审和复核检验结论之日起60个工作日内完成审查。经审查合格的，发给进口兽药注册证书，并发布该兽药的质量标准；不合格的，应当书面通知申请人。  
在审查过程中，国务院兽医行政管理部门可以对向中国出口兽药的企业是否符合兽药生产质量管理规范的要求进行考查，并有权要求该企业在国务院兽医行政管理部门指定的机构进行该兽药的安全性和有效性试验。  
国内急需兽药、少量科研用兽药或者注册兽药的样品、对照品、标准品的进口，按照国务院兽医行政管理部门的规定办理。

**Article 33** The veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall organize a preliminary review within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the application. If the preliminary review is passed, it shall submit the materials of the veterinary drug determined to be accepted to the veterinary drug evaluation institution established by it for evaluation, and submit the samples of such veterinary drug to the inspection institution designated by it for re-inspection; and shall complete the review within 60 working days from the date of receipt of the evaluation and re-inspection conclusions. If the review is passed, it shall issue an Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate and publish the quality standards for such veterinary drug; if not passed, it shall notify the applicant in writing.  
During the review process, the veterinary administrative department under the State Council may inspect whether the enterprise exporting veterinary drugs to China meets the requirements of the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Drugs, and shall have the right to require such enterprise to conduct safety and efficacy tests for such veterinary drug at an institution designated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
The import of veterinary drugs urgently needed domestically, veterinary drugs for a small amount of scientific research, or samples, reference substances, and standard substances of registered veterinary drugs shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　进口兽药注册证书的有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续向中国出口兽药的，应当在有效期届满前6个月到发证机关申请再注册。

**Article 34** The validity period of an Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate shall be five years. If it is necessary to continue exporting veterinary drugs to China upon the expiration of the validity period, an application for re-registration shall be made to the certificate-issuing authority within the six months prior to the expiration of the validity period.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　境外企业不得在中国直接销售兽药。境外企业在中国销售兽药，应当依法在中国境内设立销售机构或者委托符合条件的中国境内代理机构。  
进口在中国已取得进口兽药注册证书的兽药的，中国境内代理机构凭进口兽药注册证书到口岸所在地人民政府兽医行政管理部门办理进口兽药通关单。海关凭进口兽药通关单放行。兽药进口管理办法由国务院兽医行政管理部门会同海关总署制定。  
兽用生物制品进口后，应当依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定进行审查核对和抽查检验。其他兽药进口后，由当地兽医行政管理部门通知兽药检验机构进行抽查检验。

**Article 35** An overseas enterprise shall not directly sell veterinary drugs in China. An overseas enterprise that sells veterinary drugs in China shall, according to law, establish a sales institution within the territory of China or entrust a qualified agent within the territory of China.  
For the import of a veterinary drug that has already obtained an Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate in China, the agent within the territory of China shall, upon the strength of the Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate, apply to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at the port location for an Import Veterinary Drug Clearance Form. The Customs shall release the veterinary drug upon the strength of the Import Veterinary Drug Clearance Form. The measures for the administration of veterinary drug import shall be formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council jointly with the General Administration of Customs.  
After veterinary biological products are imported, they shall be subject to examination, verification, and random inspection and testing in accordance with Article 19 of these Regulations. After other veterinary drugs are imported, the local veterinary administrative department shall notify the veterinary drug inspection institution to conduct random inspection and testing.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　禁止进口下列兽药：  
(一)药效不确定、不良反应大以及可能对养殖业、人体健康造成危害或者存在潜在风险的；  
(二)来自疫区可能造成疫病在中国境内传播的兽用生物制品；  
(三)经考查生产条件不符合规定的；  
(四)国务院兽医行政管理部门禁止生产、经营和使用的。

**Article 36** The import of the following veterinary drugs is prohibited:  
(1) Those with uncertain efficacy, significant adverse reactions, or that may cause harm to the breeding industry or human health, or pose potential risks;  
(2) Veterinary biological products originating from epidemic areas that may cause the spread of diseases within the territory of China;  
(3) Those whose production conditions have been inspected and found not to meet the requirements;  
(4) Those whose production, marketing, and use are prohibited by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　向中国境外出口兽药，进口方要求提供兽药出口证明文件的，国务院兽医行政管理部门或者企业所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门可以出具出口兽药证明文件。  
国内防疫急需的疫苗，国务院兽医行政管理部门可以限制或者禁止出口。

**Article 37** For the export of veterinary drugs to destinations outside China, if the importing party requests the provision of veterinary drug export certification documents, the veterinary administrative department under the State Council or the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the enterprise is located may issue the veterinary drug export certification documents.  
For vaccines urgently needed for domestic epidemic prevention, the veterinary administrative department under the State Council may restrict or prohibit export.

### 第六章　兽药使用

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　兽药使用单位，应当遵守国务院兽医行政管理部门制定的兽药安全使用规定，并建立用药记录。

**Article 38** A veterinary drug user shall comply with the regulations on the safe use of veterinary drugs formulated by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and shall establish medication records.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　禁止使用假、劣兽药以及国务院兽医行政管理部门规定禁止使用的药品和其他化合物。禁止使用的药品和其他化合物目录由国务院兽医行政管理部门制定公布。

**Article 39** The use of fake or inferior veterinary drugs, as well as drugs and other compounds whose use is prohibited by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, is prohibited. The catalogue of prohibited drugs and other compounds shall be formulated and published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有休药期规定的兽药用于食用动物时，饲养者应当向购买者或者屠宰者提供准确、真实的用药记录；购买者或者屠宰者应当确保动物及其产品在用药期、休药期内不被用于食品消费。

**Article 40** When a veterinary drug for which a withdrawal period is prescribed is used for food-producing animals, the raiser shall provide accurate and true medication records to the purchaser or slaughterer; the purchaser or slaughterer shall ensure that the animals and their products are not used for food consumption during the medication period and withdrawal period.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院兽医行政管理部门，负责制定公布在饲料中允许添加的药物饲料添加剂品种目录。  
禁止在饲料和动物饮用水中添加激素类药品和国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的其他禁用药品。  
经批准可以在饲料中添加的兽药，应当由兽药生产企业制成药物饲料添加剂后方可添加。禁止将原料药直接添加到饲料及动物饮用水中或者直接饲喂动物。  
禁止将人用药品用于动物。

**Article 41** The veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating and publishing a catalogue of varieties of medicated feed additives that are permitted to be added to feed.  
It is prohibited to add hormonal drugs and other prohibited drugs prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council to feed and animal drinking water.  
Veterinary drugs approved for addition to feed shall be made into medicated feed additives by veterinary drug production enterprises before being added. It is prohibited to directly add active pharmaceutical ingredients to feed and animal drinking water or to directly feed them to animals.  
The use of human drugs for animals is prohibited.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院兽医行政管理部门，应当制定并组织实施国家动物及动物产品兽药残留监控计划。  
县级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门，负责组织对动物产品中兽药残留量的检测。兽药残留检测结果，由国务院兽医行政管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门按照权限予以公布。  
动物产品的生产者、销售者对检测结果有异议的，可以自收到检测结果之日起7个工作日内向组织实施兽药残留检测的兽医行政管理部门或者其上级兽医行政管理部门提出申请，由受理申请的兽医行政管理部门指定检验机构进行复检。  
兽药残留限量标准和残留检测方法，由国务院兽医行政管理部门制定发布。

**Article 42** The veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall formulate and organize the implementation of a national surveillance plan for veterinary drug residues in animals and animal products.  
The veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the testing of veterinary drug residues in animal products. The results of veterinary drug residue testing shall be published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council or the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with their respective authority.  
If a producer or seller of animal products disputes the test results, it may, within 7 working days from the date of receipt of the test results, file an application with the veterinary administrative department that organized the veterinary drug residue testing or its superior veterinary administrative department, and the veterinary administrative department that accepts the application shall designate an inspection institution to conduct a re-test.  
The maximum residue limits standards and residue testing methods shall be formulated and published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　禁止销售含有违禁药物或者兽药残留量超过标准的食用动物产品。

**Article 43** It is prohibited to sell edible animal products containing prohibited drugs or with veterinary drug residues exceeding the standards.

### 第七章　兽药监督管理

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门行使兽药监督管理权。  
兽药检验工作由国务院兽医行政管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门设立的兽药检验机构承担。国务院兽医行政管理部门，可以根据需要认定其他检验机构承担兽药检验工作。  
当事人对兽药检验结果有异议的，可以自收到检验结果之日起7个工作日内向实施检验的机构或者上级兽医行政管理部门设立的检验机构申请复检。

**Article 44** The veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall exercise the power of supervision and administration over veterinary drugs.  
The veterinary drug inspection work shall be undertaken by veterinary drug inspection institutions established by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council and the veterinary administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The veterinary administrative department under the State Council may, as needed, designate other inspection institutions to undertake veterinary drug inspection work.  
If a party disputes the veterinary drug inspection results, it may, within 7 working days from the date of receipt of the inspection results, apply for a re-inspection to the institution that conducted the inspection or to an inspection institution established by a superior veterinary administrative department.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　兽药应当符合兽药国家标准。  
国家兽药典委员会拟定的、国务院兽医行政管理部门发布的《中华人民共和国兽药典》和国务院兽医行政管理部门发布的其他兽药质量标准为兽药国家标准。  
兽药国家标准的标准品和对照品的标定工作由国务院兽医行政管理部门设立的兽药检验机构负责。

**Article 45** Veterinary drugs shall comply with the national veterinary drug standards.  
The Veterinary Pharmacopoeia of the People's Republic of China, which is drafted by the National Veterinary Pharmacopoeia Committee and published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council, and other veterinary drug quality standards published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council shall constitute the national veterinary drug standards.  
The calibration of standard substances and reference substances for the national veterinary drug standards shall be the responsibility of the veterinary drug inspection institution established by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　兽医行政管理部门依法进行监督检查时，对有证据证明可能是假、劣兽药的，应当采取查封、扣押的行政强制措施，并自采取行政强制措施之日起7个工作日内作出是否立案的决定；需要检验的，应当自检验报告书发出之日起15个工作日内作出是否立案的决定；不符合立案条件的，应当解除行政强制措施；需要暂停生产的，由国务院兽医行政管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医行政管理部门按照权限作出决定；需要暂停经营、使用的，由县级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门按照权限作出决定。  
未经行政强制措施决定机关或者其上级机关批准，不得擅自转移、使用、销毁、销售被查封或者扣押的兽药及有关材料。

**Article 46** When conducting supervision and inspection according to law, if there is evidence proving that a veterinary drug may be fake or inferior, the veterinary administrative department shall take the administrative compulsory measures of sealing up or distraining, and shall make a decision on whether to file a case within 7 working days from the date of taking such administrative compulsory measures; if testing is required, it shall make a decision on whether to file a case within 15 working days from the date of issuance of the test report; if the conditions for filing a case are not met, it shall lift the administrative compulsory measures; if production needs to be suspended, the veterinary administrative department under the State Council or the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a decision in accordance with its authority; if marketing or use needs to be suspended, the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall make a decision in accordance with its authority.  
Without the approval of the authority that decided on the administrative compulsory measures or its superior authority, no person shall transfer, use, destroy, or sell the sealed-up or distrained veterinary drugs and related materials without authorization.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列情形之一的，为假兽药：  
(一)以非兽药冒充兽药或者以他种兽药冒充此种兽药的；  
(二)兽药所含成分的种类、名称与兽药国家标准不符合的。  
有下列情形之一的，按照假兽药处理：  
(一)国务院兽医行政管理部门规定禁止使用的；  
(二)依照本条例规定应当经审查批准而未经审查批准即生产、进口的，或者依照本条例规定应当经抽查检验、审查核对而未经抽查检验、审查核对即销售、进口的；  
(三)变质的；  
(四)被污染的；  
(五)所标明的适应症或者功能主治超出规定范围的。

**Article 47** A veterinary drug shall be considered fake in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Passing off a non-veterinary drug as a veterinary drug, or passing off one type of veterinary drug as another;  
(2) The types or names of the ingredients contained in the veterinary drug do not conform to the national veterinary drug standards.  
A veterinary drug shall be treated as fake in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Its use is prohibited by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council;  
(2) It is produced or imported without the review and approval required under these Regulations, or it is sold or imported without the random inspection, testing, examination, or verification required under these Regulations;  
(3) It is deteriorated;  
(4) It is contaminated;  
(5) The indications or functions as indicated exceed the prescribed scope.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有下列情形之一的，为劣兽药：  
(一)成分含量不符合兽药国家标准或者不标明有效成分的；  
(二)不标明或者更改有效期或者超过有效期的；  
(三)不标明或者更改产品批号的；  
(四)其他不符合兽药国家标准，但不属于假兽药的。

**Article 48** A veterinary drug shall be considered inferior in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The content of ingredients does not conform to the national veterinary drug standards, or the active ingredients are not indicated;  
(2) The expiry date is not indicated or is altered, or the expiry date has passed;  
(3) The product batch number is not indicated or is altered;  
(4) Other instances where the veterinary drug does not conform to the national veterinary drug standards, but does not fall within the scope of fake veterinary drugs.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　禁止将兽用原料药拆零销售或者销售给兽药生产企业以外的单位和个人。  
禁止未经兽医开具处方销售、购买、使用国务院兽医行政管理部门规定实行处方药管理的兽药。

**Article 49** It is prohibited to sell veterinary active pharmaceutical ingredients in broken packages or to sell them to entities or individuals other than veterinary drug production enterprises.  
It is prohibited to sell, purchase, or use veterinary drugs that are subject to prescription drug management as prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council without a prescription issued by a veterinarian.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家实行兽药不良反应报告制度。  
兽药生产企业、经营企业、兽药使用单位和开具处方的兽医人员发现可能与兽药使用有关的严重不良反应，应当立即向所在地人民政府兽医行政管理部门报告。

**Article 50** The State shall implement a veterinary drug adverse reaction reporting system.  
If a veterinary drug production enterprise, supply enterprise, veterinary drug user, or a veterinarian who issues a prescription discovers a serious adverse reaction that may be related to the use of a veterinary drug, it or he shall immediately report to the veterinary administrative department of the local people's government.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　兽药生产企业、经营企业停止生产、经营超过6个月或者关闭的，由发证机关责令其交回兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证。

**Article 51** If a veterinary drug production enterprise or supply enterprise ceases production or marketing for more than six months or is closed, the license-issuing authority shall order it to return the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　禁止买卖、出租、出借兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证和兽药批准证明文件。

**Article 52** It is prohibited to buy, sell, lease, or lend the Veterinary Drug Production License, Veterinary Drug Supply License, or veterinary drug approval documents.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　兽药评审检验的收费项目和标准，由国务院财政部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定，并予以公告。

**Article 53** The charge items and standards for the evaluation and inspection of veterinary drugs shall be formulated by the financial department under the State Council jointly with the price regulatory authority under the State Council, and shall be announced.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各级兽医行政管理部门、兽药检验机构及其工作人员，不得参与兽药生产、经营活动，不得以其名义推荐或者监制、监销兽药。

**Article 54** Veterinary administrative departments at all levels, veterinary drug inspection institutions, and their staff members shall not participate in the production or marketing of veterinary drugs, and shall not recommend, supervise the production, or supervise the sale of veterinary drugs in their names.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　兽医行政管理部门及其工作人员利用职务上的便利收取他人财物或者谋取其他利益，对不符合法定条件的单位和个人核发许可证、签署审查同意意见，不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 55** If the veterinary administrative department or its staff members, by taking advantage of their positions, accept property from others or seek other benefits, issue licenses or sign review approval opinions to entities or individuals that do not meet the statutory conditions, fail to perform supervisory duties, or fail to investigate illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences, and if such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，无兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证生产、经营兽药的，或者虽有兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证，生产、经营假、劣兽药的，或者兽药经营企业经营人用药品的，责令其停止生产、经营，没收用于违法生产的原料、辅料、包装材料及生产、经营的兽药和违法所得，并处违法生产、经营的兽药(包括已出售的和未出售的兽药，下同)货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款，货值金额无法查证核实的，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款；无兽药生产许可证生产兽药，情节严重的，没收其生产设备；生产、经营假、劣兽药，情节严重的，吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。生产、经营企业的主要负责人和直接负责的主管人员终身不得从事兽药的生产、经营活动。  
擅自生产强制免疫所需兽用生物制品的，按照无兽药生产许可证生产兽药处罚。

**Article 56** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person produces or markets veterinary drugs without a Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License, or produces or markets fake or inferior veterinary drugs despite holding a Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License, or a veterinary drug supply enterprise markets human drugs, the violator shall be ordered to stop production and marketing; the raw materials, auxiliary materials, and packaging materials used for the illegal production, as well as the veterinary drugs produced or marketed and the illegal gains, shall be confiscated; and a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the veterinary drugs illegally produced or marketed (including both sold and unsold veterinary drugs, the same below) shall be imposed; if the value cannot be verified, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if veterinary drugs are produced without a Veterinary Drug Production License and the circumstances are serious, the production equipment shall be confiscated; if fake or inferior veterinary drugs are produced or marketed and the circumstances are serious, the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law. The principal responsible persons and directly responsible supervisors of the production or marketing enterprise shall be prohibited from engaging in the production or marketing of veterinary drugs for life.  
If any person produces veterinary biological products required for compulsory immunization without authorization, the penalty shall be imposed as for producing veterinary drugs without a Veterinary Drug Production License.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，提供虚假的资料、样品或者采取其他欺骗手段取得兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证或者兽药批准证明文件的，吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证或者撤销兽药批准证明文件，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。其主要负责人和直接负责的主管人员终身不得从事兽药的生产、经营和进出口活动。

**Article 57** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person obtains a Veterinary Drug Production License, Veterinary Drug Supply License, or veterinary drug approval documents by providing false materials or samples or by other fraudulent means, the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be revoked, or the veterinary drug approval documents shall be withdrawn, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law. The principal responsible persons and directly responsible supervisors shall be prohibited from engaging in the production, marketing, import, or export of veterinary drugs for life.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　买卖、出租、出借兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证和兽药批准证明文件的，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证或者撤销兽药批准证明文件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 58** If any person buys, sells, leases, or lends the Veterinary Drug Production License, Veterinary Drug Supply License, or veterinary drug approval documents, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be revoked, or the veterinary drug approval documents shall be withdrawn; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，兽药安全性评价单位、临床试验单位、生产和经营企业未按照规定实施兽药研究试验、生产、经营质量管理规范的，给予警告，责令其限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止兽药研究试验、生产、经营活动，并处5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，研制新兽药不具备规定的条件擅自使用一类病原微生物或者在实验室阶段前未经批准的，责令其停止实验，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，开展新兽药临床试验应当备案而未备案的，责令其立即改正，给予警告，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** If, in violation of these Regulations, a veterinary drug safety evaluation entity, clinical trial entity, production enterprise, or supply enterprise fails to implement the quality management standards for veterinary drug research and testing, production, or marketing as required, a warning shall be given and it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to stop the veterinary drug research and testing, production, or marketing activities, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be revoked; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
If, in violation of these Regulations, any person uses a Class I pathogenic microorganism without authorization while lacking the prescribed conditions for the research and development of a new veterinary drug, or without approval before the laboratory stage, it shall be ordered to stop the experiment, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
If, in violation of these Regulations, a new veterinary drug clinical trial is conducted without the required record-filing, the violator shall be ordered to make immediate corrections, given a warning, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，兽药的标签和说明书未经批准的，责令其限期改正；逾期不改正的，按照生产、经营假兽药处罚；有兽药产品批准文号的，撤销兽药产品批准文号；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
兽药包装上未附有标签和说明书，或者标签和说明书与批准的内容不一致的，责令其限期改正；情节严重的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 60** If, in violation of these Regulations, the label or instructions of a veterinary drug have not been approved, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the penalty shall be imposed as for producing or marketing fake veterinary drugs; if there is a veterinary drug product approval number, such product approval number shall be withdrawn; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
If a veterinary drug package is not accompanied by a label and instructions, or the label and instructions are inconsistent with the approved content, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，境外企业在中国直接销售兽药的，责令其限期改正，没收直接销售的兽药和违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销进口兽药注册证书；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 61** If, in violation of these Regulations, an overseas enterprise directly sells veterinary drugs in China, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; the directly sold veterinary drugs and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例规定，未按照国家有关兽药安全使用规定使用兽药的、未建立用药记录或者记录不完整真实的，或者使用禁止使用的药品和其他化合物的，或者将人用药品用于动物的，责令其立即改正，并对饲喂了违禁药物及其他化合物的动物及其产品进行无害化处理；对违法单位处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 62** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person uses veterinary drugs not in accordance with the relevant national regulations on the safe use of veterinary drugs, fails to establish medication records or the records are incomplete or untrue, uses prohibited drugs and other compounds, or uses human drugs for animals, the violator shall be ordered to make immediate corrections, and harmless treatment shall be carried out for the animals and their products that have been fed with prohibited drugs and other compounds; a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal entity; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，销售尚在用药期、休药期内的动物及其产品用于食品消费的，或者销售含有违禁药物和兽药残留超标的动物产品用于食品消费的，责令其对含有违禁药物和兽药残留超标的动物产品进行无害化处理，没收违法所得，并处3万元以上10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 63** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person sells animals or their products that are still within the medication period or withdrawal period for food consumption, or sells animal products containing prohibited drugs or with excessive veterinary drug residues for food consumption, the violator shall be ordered to carry out harmless treatment for the animal products containing prohibited drugs or with excessive veterinary drug residues, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例规定，擅自转移、使用、销毁、销售被查封或者扣押的兽药及有关材料的，责令其停止违法行为，给予警告，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 64** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person transfers, uses, destroys, or sells sealed-up or distrained veterinary drugs and related materials without authorization, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, given a warning, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例规定，兽药生产企业、经营企业、兽药使用单位和开具处方的兽医人员发现可能与兽药使用有关的严重不良反应，不向所在地人民政府兽医行政管理部门报告的，给予警告，并处5000元以上1万元以下罚款。  
生产企业在新兽药监测期内不收集或者不及时报送该新兽药的疗效、不良反应等资料的，责令其限期改正，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销该新兽药的产品批准文号。

**Article 65** If, in violation of these Regulations, a veterinary drug production enterprise, supply enterprise, veterinary drug user, or a veterinarian who issues a prescription fails to report to the veterinary administrative department of the local people's government upon discovering a serious adverse reaction that may be related to the use of a veterinary drug, a warning shall be given, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If a production enterprise fails to collect or fails to submit in a timely manner information on the efficacy, adverse reactions, etc., of a new veterinary drug during the monitoring period, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the product approval number for such new veterinary drug shall be withdrawn.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例规定，未经兽医开具处方销售、购买、使用兽用处方药的，责令其限期改正，没收违法所得，并处5万元以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 66** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person sells, purchases, or uses a veterinary prescription drug without a prescription issued by a veterinarian, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例规定，兽药生产、经营企业把原料药销售给兽药生产企业以外的单位和个人的，或者兽药经营企业拆零销售原料药的，责令其立即改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处2万元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 67** If, in violation of these Regulations, a veterinary drug production or supply enterprise sells active pharmaceutical ingredients to entities or individuals other than veterinary drug production enterprises, or a veterinary drug supply enterprise sells active pharmaceutical ingredients in broken packages, the violator shall be ordered to make immediate corrections, given a warning, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License shall be revoked; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例规定，在饲料和动物饮用水中添加激素类药品和国务院兽医行政管理部门规定的其他禁用药品，依照《饲料和饲料添加剂管理条例》的有关规定处罚；直接将原料药添加到饲料及动物饮用水中，或者饲喂动物的，责令其立即改正，并处1万元以上3万元以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 68** If, in violation of these Regulations, any person adds hormonal drugs or other prohibited drugs prescribed by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council to feed or animal drinking water, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Feed and Feed Additives; if any person directly adds active pharmaceutical ingredients to feed or animal drinking water, or feeds them to animals, the violator shall be ordered to make immediate corrections, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有下列情形之一的，撤销兽药的产品批准文号或者吊销进口兽药注册证书：  
(一)抽查检验连续2次不合格的；  
(二)药效不确定、不良反应大以及可能对养殖业、人体健康造成危害或者存在潜在风险的；  
(三)国务院兽医行政管理部门禁止生产、经营和使用的兽药。  
被撤销产品批准文号或者被吊销进口兽药注册证书的兽药，不得继续生产、进口、经营和使用。已经生产、进口的，由所在地兽医行政管理部门监督销毁，所需费用由违法行为人承担；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 69** In any of the following circumstances, the product approval number of a veterinary drug shall be withdrawn, or the Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) Having failed random inspection and testing twice consecutively;  
(2) Having uncertain efficacy, significant adverse reactions, or that may cause harm to the breeding industry or human health, or pose potential risks;  
(3) Veterinary drugs whose production, marketing, and use are prohibited by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council.  
A veterinary drug whose product approval number has been withdrawn or whose Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate has been revoked shall not continue to be produced, imported, marketed, or used. Those already produced or imported shall be destroyed under the supervision of the local veterinary administrative department, and the necessary expenses shall be borne by the violator; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本条例规定的行政处罚由县级以上人民政府兽医行政管理部门决定；其中吊销兽药生产许可证、兽药经营许可证，撤销兽药批准证明文件或者责令停止兽药研究试验的，由发证、批准、备案部门决定。  
上级兽医行政管理部门对下级兽医行政管理部门违反本条例的行政行为，应当责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，有权予以改变或者撤销。

**Article 70** The administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level; among them, the revocation of a Veterinary Drug Production License or Veterinary Drug Supply License, the withdrawal of veterinary drug approval documents, or the order to stop veterinary drug research and testing shall be decided by the licensing, approving, or record-filing department.  
If the veterinary administrative department at a higher level finds that a veterinary administrative department at a lower level has taken an administrative act in violation of these Regulations, it shall order the lower-level department to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the lower-level department fails to make corrections within the time limit, the higher-level department shall have the power to change or revoke such act.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本条例规定的货值金额以违法生产、经营兽药的标价计算；没有标价的，按照同类兽药的市场价格计算。

**Article 71** The value of goods as prescribed in these Regulations shall be calculated on the basis of the marked price of the illegally produced or marketed veterinary drugs; if there is no marked price, it shall be calculated on the basis of the market price of similar veterinary drugs.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
(一)兽药，是指用于预防、治疗、诊断动物疾病或者有目的地调节动物生理机能的物质(含药物饲料添加剂)，主要包括：血清制品、疫苗、诊断制品、微生态制品、中药材、中成药、化学药品、抗生素、生化药品、放射性药品及外用杀虫剂、消毒剂等。  
(二)兽用处方药，是指凭兽医处方方可购买和使用的兽药。  
(三)兽用非处方药，是指由国务院兽医行政管理部门公布的、不需要凭兽医处方就可以自行购买并按照说明书使用的兽药。  
(四)兽药生产企业，是指专门生产兽药的企业和兼产兽药的企业，包括从事兽药分装的企业。  
(五)兽药经营企业，是指经营兽药的专营企业或者兼营企业。  
(六)新兽药，是指未曾在中国境内上市销售的兽用药品。  
(七)兽药批准证明文件，是指兽药产品批准文号、进口兽药注册证书、出口兽药证明文件、新兽药注册证书等文件。

**Article 72** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Veterinary drugs" means substances (including medicated feed additives) used for the prevention, treatment, or diagnosis of animal diseases, or for the purposeful regulation of animal physiological functions, mainly including: serum products, vaccines, diagnostic products, microecological products, Chinese medicinal materials, Chinese patent medicines, chemical drugs, antibiotics, biochemical drugs, radioactive drugs, and external insecticides, disinfectants, etc.  
(2) "Veterinary prescription drugs" means veterinary drugs that may be purchased and used only upon a prescription issued by a veterinarian.  
(3) "Veterinary non-prescription drugs" means veterinary drugs that are published by the veterinary administrative department under the State Council and that may be purchased and used by oneself in accordance with the instructions without a prescription issued by a veterinarian.  
(4) "Veterinary drug production enterprise" means an enterprise that specializes in the production of veterinary drugs or concurrently produces veterinary drugs, including enterprises engaged in the repackaging of veterinary drugs.  
(5) "Veterinary drug supply enterprise" means an enterprise that specializes in or concurrently engages in the marketing of veterinary drugs.  
(6) "New veterinary drug" means a veterinary drug that has not yet been marketed within the territory of China.  
(7) "Veterinary drug approval documents" means documents such as the veterinary drug product approval number, Import Veterinary Drug Registration Certificate, veterinary drug export certification documents, and new veterinary drug registration certificate.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　兽用麻醉药品、精神药品、毒性药品和放射性药品等特殊药品，依照国家有关规定管理。

**Article 73** Special drugs such as veterinary narcotic drugs, psychotropic drugs, toxic drugs, and radioactive drugs shall be administered in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　水产养殖中的兽药使用、兽药残留检测和监督管理以及水产养殖过程中违法用药的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府渔业主管部门及其所属的渔政监督管理机构负责。

**Article 74** The use of veterinary drugs in aquaculture, the testing of veterinary drug residues, and the supervision and administration thereof, as well as the administrative penalties for illegal drug use in the course of aquaculture, shall be the responsibility of the competent fishery department of the people's government at or above the county level and its subordinate fishery administration and supervision institutions.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本条例自2004年11月1日起施行。

**Article 75** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2004.
