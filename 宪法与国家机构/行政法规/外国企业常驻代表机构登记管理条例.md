# 外国企业常驻代表机构登记管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （2010年11月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第584号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年9月18日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范外国企业常驻代表机构的设立及其业务活动，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称外国企业常驻代表机构（以下简称代表机构），是指外国企业依照本条例规定，在中国境内设立的从事与该外国企业业务有关的非营利性活动的办事机构。代表机构不具有法人资格。 第三条　代表机构应当遵守中国法律，不得损害中国国家安全和社会公共利益。 第四条　代表机构设立、变更、终止，应当依照本条例规定办理登记。 外国企业申请办理代表机构登记，应当对申请文件、材料的真实性负责。 第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府市场监督管理部门是代表机构的登记和管理机关（以下简称登记机关）。 登记机关应当与其他有关部门建立信息共享机制，相互提供有关代表机构的信息。 第六条　代表机构应当于每年3月1日至6月30日向登记机关提交年度报告。年度报告的内容包括外国企业的合法存续情况、代表机构的业务活动开展情况及其经会计师事务所审计的费用收支情况等相关情况。 第七条　代表机构应当依法设置会计账簿，真实记载外国企业经费拨付和代表机构费用收支情况，并置于代表机构驻在场所。 代表机构不得使用其他企业、组织或者个人的账户。 第八条　外国企业委派的首席代表、代表以及代表机构的工作人员应当遵守法律、行政法规关于出入境、居留、就业、纳税、外汇登记等规定；违反规定的，由有关部门依照法律、行政法规的相关规定予以处理。 第二章　登记事项 第九条　代表机构的登记事项包括：代表机构名称、首席代表姓名、业务范围、驻在场所、驻在期限、外国企业名称及其住所。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范外国企业常驻代表机构的设立及其业务活动，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate the establishment of resident representative offices of foreign enterprises and their business activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称外国企业常驻代表机构（以下简称代表机构），是指外国企业依照本条例规定，在中国境内设立的从事与该外国企业业务有关的非营利性活动的办事机构。代表机构不具有法人资格。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "resident representative office of a foreign enterprise" (hereinafter referred to as "representative office") means an office established by a foreign enterprise within the territory of China, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, to engage in non-profit activities related to the business of the foreign enterprise. A representative office shall not have the status of a legal person.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　代表机构应当遵守中国法律，不得损害中国国家安全和社会公共利益。

**Article 3** A representative office shall abide by Chinese laws and shall not harm China's national security or social public interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　代表机构设立、变更、终止，应当依照本条例规定办理登记。  
外国企业申请办理代表机构登记，应当对申请文件、材料的真实性负责。

**Article 4** The establishment, modification, or termination of a representative office shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
A foreign enterprise that applies for registration of a representative office shall be responsible for the authenticity of the application documents and materials.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府市场监督管理部门是代表机构的登记和管理机关（以下简称登记机关）。  
登记机关应当与其他有关部门建立信息共享机制，相互提供有关代表机构的信息。

**Article 5** The market supervision and administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be the registration and administration authority for representative offices (hereinafter referred to as the "registration authority").  
The registration authority shall establish an information sharing mechanism with other relevant departments and provide each other with information on representative offices.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　代表机构应当于每年3月1日至6月30日向登记机关提交年度报告。年度报告的内容包括外国企业的合法存续情况、代表机构的业务活动开展情况及其经会计师事务所审计的费用收支情况等相关情况。

**Article 6** A representative office shall submit an annual report to the registration authority between March 1 and June 30 each year. The annual report shall include the legal existence of the foreign enterprise, the business activities of the representative office, and its audited income and expenditure by an accounting firm, among other relevant information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　代表机构应当依法设置会计账簿，真实记载外国企业经费拨付和代表机构费用收支情况，并置于代表机构驻在场所。  
代表机构不得使用其他企业、组织或者个人的账户。

**Article 7** A representative office shall, according to law, set up accounting books, truthfully record the allocation of funds by the foreign enterprise and the income and expenditure of the representative office, and keep such books at the premises of the representative office.  
A representative office shall not use accounts of other enterprises, organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国企业委派的首席代表、代表以及代表机构的工作人员应当遵守法律、行政法规关于出入境、居留、就业、纳税、外汇登记等规定；违反规定的，由有关部门依照法律、行政法规的相关规定予以处理。

**Article 8** The chief representative, representatives, and staff of a representative office appointed by a foreign enterprise shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on entry and exit, residence, employment, tax payment, foreign exchange registration, etc.; those who violate such provisions shall be dealt with by the relevant departments in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

### 第二章　登记事项

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　代表机构的登记事项包括：代表机构名称、首席代表姓名、业务范围、驻在场所、驻在期限、外国企业名称及其住所。

**Article 9** The registration particulars of a representative office shall include: the name of the representative office, the name of the chief representative, the business scope, the premises, the term of presence, the name of the foreign enterprise and its address.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　代表机构名称应当由以下部分依次组成：外国企业国籍、外国企业中文名称、驻在城市名称以及“代表处”字样，并不得含有下列内容和文字：  
（一）有损于中国国家安全或者社会公共利益的；  
（二）国际组织名称；  
（三）法律、行政法规或者国务院规定禁止的。  
代表机构应当以登记机关登记的名称从事业务活动。

**Article 10** The name of a representative office shall consist of the following parts in order: the nationality of the foreign enterprise, the Chinese name of the foreign enterprise, the name of the city where it is located, and the words "representative office", and shall not contain any of the following contents or words:  
(1) Those detrimental to China's national security or social public interests;  
(2) Names of international organizations;  
(3) Those prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council.  
A representative office shall conduct business activities under the name registered with the registration authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国企业应当委派一名首席代表。首席代表在外国企业书面授权范围内，可以代表外国企业签署代表机构登记申请文件。  
外国企业可以根据业务需要，委派1至3名代表。

**Article 11** A foreign enterprise shall appoint a chief representative. The chief representative may, within the scope of written authorization from the foreign enterprise, sign the registration application documents of the representative office on behalf of the foreign enterprise.  
A foreign enterprise may, based on business needs, appoint one to three representatives.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任首席代表、代表：  
（一）因损害中国国家安全或者社会公共利益，被判处刑罚的；  
（二）因从事损害中国国家安全或者社会公共利益等违法活动，依法被撤销设立登记、吊销登记证或者被有关部门依法责令关闭的代表机构的首席代表、代表，自被撤销、吊销或者责令关闭之日起未逾5年的；  
（三）国务院市场监督管理部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 12** A person shall not serve as a chief representative or representative under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having been sentenced to criminal punishment for harming China's national security or social public interests;  
(2) Having been a chief representative or representative of a representative office that has had its establishment registration revoked, its registration certificate revoked, or has been ordered to close by relevant departments according to law for engaging in illegal activities such as harming China's national security or social public interests, if less than five years have elapsed since the date of such revocation or closure;  
(3) Other circumstances specified by the market supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　代表机构不得从事营利性活动。  
中国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定另有规定的，从其规定，但是中国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 13** A representative office shall not engage in for-profit activities.  
If otherwise provided in international treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by China, those provisions shall prevail, except for the clauses on which China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　代表机构可以从事与外国企业业务有关的下列活动：  
（一）与外国企业产品或者服务有关的市场调查、展示、宣传活动；  
（二）与外国企业产品销售、服务提供、境内采购、境内投资有关的联络活动。  
法律、行政法规或者国务院规定代表机构从事前款规定的业务活动须经批准的，应当取得批准。

**Article 14** A representative office may engage in the following activities related to the business of the foreign enterprise:  
(1) Market research, display, and promotion activities related to the products or services of the foreign enterprise;  
(2) Liaison activities related to the sales of products, provision of services, domestic procurement, and domestic investment of the foreign enterprise.  
If laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide that a representative office must obtain approval to engage in the activities specified in the preceding paragraph, such approval shall be obtained.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　代表机构的驻在场所由外国企业自行选择。  
根据国家安全和社会公共利益需要，有关部门可以要求代表机构调整驻在场所，并及时通知登记机关。

**Article 15** The premises of a representative office shall be selected by the foreign enterprise on its own.  
If necessary for national security or social public interests, the relevant departments may require the representative office to adjust its premises and shall promptly notify the registration authority.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　代表机构的驻在期限不得超过外国企业的存续期限。

**Article 16** The term of presence of a representative office shall not exceed the period of existence of the foreign enterprise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　登记机关应当将代表机构登记事项记载于代表机构登记簿，供社会公众查阅、复制。

**Article 17** The registration authority shall record the registration particulars of a representative office in the register of representative offices for public inspection and copying.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　代表机构应当将登记机关颁发的外国企业常驻代表机构登记证（以下简称登记证）置于代表机构驻在场所的显著位置。

**Article 18** A representative office shall place the Registration Certificate of Resident Representative Office of Foreign Enterprise (hereinafter referred to as "registration certificate") issued by the registration authority in a conspicuous position at its premises.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位和个人不得伪造、涂改、出租、出借、转让登记证和首席代表、代表的代表证（以下简称代表证）。  
登记证和代表证遗失或者毁坏的，代表机构应当在指定的媒体上声明作废，申请补领。  
登记机关依法作出准予变更登记、准予注销登记、撤销变更登记、吊销登记证决定的，代表机构原登记证和原首席代表、代表的代表证自动失效。

**Article 19** No unit or individual may forge, alter, lease, lend, or transfer the registration certificate and the representative certificates of the chief representative and representatives (hereinafter referred to as "representative certificates").  
If the registration certificate or representative certificates are lost or damaged, the representative office shall make a statement of loss on a designated medium and apply for reissuance.  
If the registration authority makes a decision to approve modification registration, approve cancellation registration, revoke modification registration, or revoke the registration certificate according to law, the original registration certificate and the original representative certificates of the chief representative and representatives shall automatically cease to have effect.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　代表机构设立、变更，外国企业应当在登记机关指定的媒体上向社会公告。  
代表机构注销或者被依法撤销设立登记、吊销登记证的，由登记机关进行公告。

**Article 20** The foreign enterprise shall make a public announcement on a medium designated by the registration authority upon the establishment or modification of a representative office.  
If a representative office is cancelled or its establishment registration is revoked or its registration certificate is revoked according to law, the registration authority shall make the announcement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　登记机关对代表机构涉嫌违反本条例的行为进行查处，可以依法行使下列职权：  
（一）向有关的单位和个人调查、了解情况；  
（二）查阅、复制、查封、扣押与违法行为有关的合同、票据、账簿以及其他资料；  
（三）查封、扣押专门用于从事违法行为的工具、设备、原材料、产品（商品）等财物；  
（四）查询从事违法行为的代表机构的账户以及与存款有关的会计凭证、账簿、对账单等。

**Article 21** When investigating and handling suspected violations of these Regulations by a representative office, the registration authority may, according to law, exercise the following powers:  
(1) Investigate and ascertain the situation from relevant units and individuals;  
(2) Inspect, copy, seal up, or seize contracts, vouchers, account books, and other materials related to the illegal act;  
(3) Seal up or seize tools, equipment, raw materials, products (goods), and other property specially used for the illegal act;  
(4) Inquire into the accounts of the representative office that committed the illegal act and the accounting vouchers, account books, bank statements, etc. related to deposits.

### 第三章　设立登记

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　设立代表机构应当向登记机关申请设立登记。

**Article 22** To establish a representative office, an application for establishment registration shall be filed with the registration authority.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　外国企业申请设立代表机构，应当向登记机关提交下列文件、材料：  
（一）代表机构设立登记申请书；  
（二）外国企业住所证明和存续2年以上的合法营业证明；  
（三）外国企业章程或者组织协议；  
（四）外国企业对首席代表、代表的任命文件；  
（五）首席代表、代表的身份证明和简历；  
（六）同外国企业有业务往来的金融机构出具的资金信用证明；  
（七）代表机构驻在场所的合法使用证明。  
法律、行政法规或者国务院规定设立代表机构须经批准的，外国企业应当自批准之日起90日内向登记机关申请设立登记，并提交有关批准文件。  
中国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定规定可以设立从事营利性活动的代表机构的，还应当依照法律、行政法规或者国务院规定提交相应文件。

**Article 23** A foreign enterprise that applies to establish a representative office shall submit the following documents and materials to the registration authority:  
(1) An application for establishment registration of the representative office;  
(2) Proof of the address of the foreign enterprise and proof of legal operation for more than two years;  
(3) The articles of association or organizational agreement of the foreign enterprise;  
(4) The appointment documents for the chief representative and representatives by the foreign enterprise;  
(5) Identification documents and resumes of the chief representative and representatives;  
(6) A credit certificate issued by a financial institution that has business dealings with the foreign enterprise;  
(7) Proof of legal use of the premises of the representative office.  
If laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide that establishment of a representative office must be approved, the foreign enterprise shall apply for establishment registration with the registration authority within 90 days from the date of approval and submit the relevant approval documents.  
If an international treaty or agreement concluded or acceded to by China provides that a representative office engaged in for-profit activities may be established, the corresponding documents shall also be submitted in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　登记机关应当自受理申请之日起15日内作出是否准予登记的决定，作出决定前可以根据需要征求有关部门的意见。作出准予登记决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内向申请人颁发登记证和代表证；作出不予登记决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内向申请人出具登记驳回通知书，说明不予登记的理由。  
登记证签发日期为代表机构成立日期。

**Article 24** The registration authority shall, within 15 days from the date of accepting an application, make a decision on whether to grant registration, and may, before making the decision, seek the opinions of relevant departments as necessary. If a decision to grant registration is made, the registration authority shall issue the registration certificate and representative certificates to the applicant within 5 days from the date of the decision; if a decision to refuse registration is made, the registration authority shall issue a notice of refusal of registration to the applicant within 5 days from the date of the decision, stating the reasons for refusal.  
The date of issuance of the registration certificate shall be the date of establishment of the representative office.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　代表机构、首席代表和代表凭登记证、代表证申请办理居留、就业、纳税、外汇登记等有关手续。

**Article 25** A representative office, chief representative, and representatives shall apply for relevant procedures such as residence, employment, tax payment, and foreign exchange registration by presenting the registration certificate and representative certificates.

### 第四章　变更登记

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　代表机构登记事项发生变更，外国企业应当向登记机关申请变更登记。

**Article 26** If the registration particulars of a representative office change, the foreign enterprise shall apply to the registration authority for modification registration.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　变更登记事项的，应当自登记事项发生变更之日起60日内申请变更登记。  
变更登记事项依照法律、行政法规或者国务院规定在登记前须经批准的，应当自批准之日起30日内申请变更登记。

**Article 27** For modification of registration particulars, an application for modification registration shall be filed within 60 days from the date the registration particulars change.  
If modification of registration particulars must be approved before registration according to laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council, an application for modification registration shall be filed within 30 days from the date of approval.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　代表机构驻在期限届满后继续从事业务活动的，外国企业应当在驻在期限届满前60日内向登记机关申请变更登记。

**Article 28** If a representative office continues to engage in business activities after the expiry of its term of presence, the foreign enterprise shall apply for modification registration with the registration authority within 60 days before the expiry of the term of presence.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　申请代表机构变更登记，应当提交代表机构变更登记申请书以及国务院市场监督管理部门规定提交的相关文件。  
变更登记事项依照法律、行政法规或者国务院规定在登记前须经批准的，还应当提交有关批准文件。

**Article 29** To apply for modification registration of a representative office, an application for modification registration of the representative office and the relevant documents as specified by the market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be submitted.  
If modification of registration particulars must be approved before registration according to laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council, the relevant approval documents shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　登记机关应当自受理申请之日起10日内作出是否准予变更登记的决定。作出准予变更登记决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内换发登记证和代表证；作出不予变更登记决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内向申请人出具变更登记驳回通知书，说明不予变更登记的理由。

**Article 30** The registration authority shall, within 10 days from the date of accepting an application, make a decision on whether to grant modification registration. If a decision to grant modification registration is made, the registration authority shall reissue the registration certificate and representative certificates within 5 days from the date of the decision; if a decision to refuse modification registration is made, the registration authority shall issue a notice of refusal of modification registration to the applicant within 5 days from the date of the decision, stating the reasons for refusal.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　外国企业的有权签字人、企业责任形式、资本（资产）、经营范围以及代表发生变更的，外国企业应当自上述事项发生变更之日起60日内向登记机关备案。

**Article 31** If the authorized signatory, form of enterprise liability, capital (assets), business scope, or representatives of a foreign enterprise change, the foreign enterprise shall, within 60 days from the date of such change, file for the record with the registration authority.

### 第五章　注销登记

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　有下列情形之一的，外国企业应当在下列事项发生之日起60日内向登记机关申请注销登记：  
（一）外国企业撤销代表机构；  
（二）代表机构驻在期限届满不再继续从事业务活动；  
（三）外国企业终止；  
（四）代表机构依法被撤销批准或者责令关闭。

**Article 32** Under any of the following circumstances, the foreign enterprise shall, within 60 days from the date the following events occur, apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration:  
(1) The foreign enterprise dissolves the representative office;  
(2) The representative office no longer continues business activities after the expiry of its term of presence;  
(3) The foreign enterprise terminates;  
(4) The representative office's approval is revoked or it is ordered to close according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国企业申请代表机构注销登记，应当向登记机关提交下列文件：  
（一）代表机构注销登记申请书；  
（二）代表机构税务登记注销证明；  
（三）海关出具的相关事宜已清理完结或者该代表机构未办理相关手续的证明；  
（四）国务院市场监督管理部门规定提交的其他文件。  
法律、行政法规或者国务院规定代表机构终止活动须经批准的，还应当提交有关批准文件。

**Article 33** A foreign enterprise applying for cancellation registration of a representative office shall submit the following documents to the registration authority:  
(1) An application for cancellation registration of the representative office;  
(2) Proof of cancellation of tax registration of the representative office;  
(3) A certificate issued by the customs stating that relevant matters have been settled or that the representative office has not handled relevant procedures;  
(4) Other documents as specified by the market supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
If laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide that termination of activities of a representative office must be approved, the relevant approval documents shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　登记机关应当自受理申请之日起10日内作出是否准予注销登记的决定。作出准予注销决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内出具准予注销通知书，收缴登记证和代表证；作出不予注销登记决定的，应当自作出决定之日起5日内向申请人出具注销登记驳回通知书，说明不予注销登记的理由。

**Article 34** The registration authority shall, within 10 days from the date of accepting an application, make a decision on whether to grant cancellation registration. If a decision to grant cancellation is made, the registration authority shall issue a notice of approval of cancellation within 5 days from the date of the decision and confiscate the registration certificate and representative certificates; if a decision to refuse cancellation registration is made, the registration authority shall issue a notice of refusal of cancellation registration to the applicant within 5 days from the date of the decision, stating the reasons for refusal.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　未经登记，擅自设立代表机构或者从事代表机构业务活动的，由登记机关责令停止活动，处以5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
代表机构违反本条例规定从事营利性活动的，由登记机关责令改正，没收违法所得，没收专门用于从事营利性活动的工具、设备、原材料、产品（商品）等财物，处以5万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销登记证。

**Article 35** If a representative office is established without registration or if representative office business activities are conducted without authorization, the registration authority shall order the cessation of activities and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan.  
If a representative office violates the provisions of these Regulations by engaging in for-profit activities, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections, confiscate illegal gains, confiscate tools, equipment, raw materials, products (goods), and other property specially used for for-profit activities, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the registration certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　提交虚假材料或者采取其他欺诈手段隐瞒真实情况，取得代表机构登记或者备案的，由登记机关责令改正，对代表机构处以2万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由登记机关撤销登记或者吊销登记证，缴销代表证。  
代表机构提交的年度报告隐瞒真实情况、弄虚作假的，由登记机关责令改正，对代表机构处以2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销登记证。  
伪造、涂改、出租、出借、转让登记证、代表证的，由登记机关对代表机构处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销登记证，缴销代表证。

**Article 36** If a registration or record-filing of a representative office is obtained by submitting false materials or by using other fraudulent means to conceal the true situation, the registration authority shall order corrections, impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan on the representative office, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority shall revoke the registration or revoke the registration certificate and cancel the representative certificates.  
If the annual report submitted by a representative office conceals the true situation or falsifies, the registration authority shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan on the representative office; if the circumstances are serious, the registration certificate shall be revoked.  
If the registration certificate or representative certificates are forged, altered, leased, lent, or transferred, the registration authority shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan on the representative office, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the registration certificate shall be revoked and the representative certificates shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　代表机构违反[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定从事业务活动以外活动的，由登记机关责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销登记证。

**Article 37** If a representative office engages in activities other than the business activities specified in Article 14 of these Regulations, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the registration certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　有下列情形之一的，由登记机关责令限期改正，处以1万元以上3万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，吊销登记证：  
（一）未依照本条例规定提交年度报告的；  
（二）未按照登记机关登记的名称从事业务活动的；  
（三）未按照中国政府有关部门要求调整驻在场所的；  
（四）未依照本条例规定公告其设立、变更情况的；  
（五）未依照本条例规定办理有关变更登记、注销登记或者备案的。

**Article 38** Under any of the following circumstances, the registration authority shall order corrections within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if corrections are not made within the time limit, the registration certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) Failure to submit an annual report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Failure to conduct business activities under the name registered with the registration authority;  
(3) Failure to adjust the premises as required by the relevant departments of the Chinese government;  
(4) Failure to publicly announce its establishment or modification in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(5) Failure to handle relevant modification registration, cancellation registration, or record-filing in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　代表机构从事危害中国国家安全或者社会公共利益等严重违法活动的，由登记机关吊销登记证。  
代表机构违反本条例规定被撤销设立登记、吊销登记证，或者被中国政府有关部门依法责令关闭的，自被撤销、吊销或者责令关闭之日起5年内，设立该代表机构的外国企业不得在中国境内设立代表机构。

**Article 39** If a representative office engages in serious illegal activities such as harming China's national security or social public interests, the registration authority shall revoke its registration certificate.  
If a representative office's establishment registration is revoked, its registration certificate is revoked, or it is ordered to close by the relevant departments of the Chinese government according to law for violating these Regulations, the foreign enterprise that established such representative office shall not establish a representative office within China within five years from the date of such revocation or closure.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　登记机关及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，未依照本条例规定办理登记、查处违法行为，或者支持、包庇、纵容违法行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 40** If the registration authority or its staff members abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain by failing to handle registration or investigate and handle illegal acts in accordance with these Regulations, or by supporting, harboring, or conniving at illegal acts, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** If a violation of these Regulations constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Penalties of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例所称外国企业，是指依照外国法律在中国境外设立的营利性组织。

**Article 42** For the purposes of these Regulations, "foreign enterprise" means a for-profit organization established outside China in accordance with foreign laws.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　代表机构登记的收费项目依照国务院财政部门、价格主管部门的有关规定执行，代表机构登记的收费标准依照国务院价格主管部门、财政部门的有关规定执行。

**Article 43** The charging items for representative office registration shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the finance department and the price authority of the State Council; the charging standards for representative office registration shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the price authority and the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区企业在中国境内设立代表机构的，参照本条例规定进行登记管理。

**Article 44** If enterprises from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region establish representative offices within China, their registration administration shall be carried out by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2011年3月1日起施行。1983年3月5日经国务院批准，1983年3月15日原国家工商行政管理局发布的《关于外国企业常驻代表机构登记管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2011. The "Measures for the Registration Administration of Resident Representative Offices of Foreign Enterprises" approved by the State Council on March 5, 1983 and promulgated by the former State Administration for Industry and Commerce on March 15, 1983 shall be abolished simultaneously.
