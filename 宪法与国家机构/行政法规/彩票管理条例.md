# 彩票管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-05-04  
**生效日期**：2009-07-01

> (2009年4月22日国务院第58次常务会议通过　2009年5月4日中华人民共和国国务院令第554号公布　自2009年7月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强彩票管理，规范彩票市场发展，维护彩票市场秩序，保护彩票参与者的合法权益，促进社会公益事业发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称彩票，是指国家为筹集社会公益资金，促进社会公益事业发展而特许发行、依法销售，自然人自愿购买，并按照特定规则获得中奖机会的凭证。 彩票不返还本金、不计付利息。 第三条　国务院特许发行福利彩票、体育彩票。未经国务院特许，禁止发行其他彩票。禁止在中华人民共和国境内发行、销售境外彩票。 第四条　彩票的发行、销售和开奖，应当遵循公开、公平、公正和诚实信用的原则。 第五条　国务院财政部门负责全国的彩票监督管理工作。国务院民政部门、体育行政部门按照各自的职责分别负责全国的福利彩票、体育彩票管理工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门负责本行政区域的彩票监督管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门、体育行政部门按照各自的职责分别负责本行政区域的福利彩票、体育彩票管理工作。 县级以上各级人民政府公安机关和县级以上工商行政管理机关，在各自的职责范围内，依法查处非法彩票，维护彩票市场秩序。 第二章　彩票发行和销售管理 第六条　国务院民政部门、体育行政部门依法设立的福利彩票发行机构、体育彩票发行机构(以下简称彩票发行机构)，分别负责全国的福利彩票、体育彩票发行和组织销售工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门、体育行政部门依法设立的福利彩票销售机构、体育彩票销售机构(以下简称彩票销售机构)，分别负责本行政区域的福利彩票、体育彩票销售工作。 第七条　彩票发行机构申请开设、停止福利彩票、体育彩票的具体品种(以下简称彩票品种)或者申请变更彩票品种审批事项的，应当依照本条例规定的程序报国务院财政部门批准。 国务院财政部门应当根据彩票市场健康发展的需要，按照合理规划彩票市场和彩票品种结构、严格控制彩票风险的原则，对彩票发行机构的申请进行审查。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强彩票管理，规范彩票市场发展，维护彩票市场秩序，保护彩票参与者的合法权益，促进社会公益事业发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening lottery management, standardizing the development of the lottery market, maintaining order in the lottery market, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of lottery participants, and promoting the development of social welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称彩票，是指国家为筹集社会公益资金，促进社会公益事业发展而特许发行、依法销售，自然人自愿购买，并按照特定规则获得中奖机会的凭证。  
彩票不返还本金、不计付利息。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "lottery" means a certificate issued under special permit by the State, sold according to law, purchased voluntarily by natural persons, and providing an opportunity to win a prize in accordance with specific rules, for the purpose of raising funds for social public welfare and promoting the development of social welfare undertakings.  
Lottery prizes shall not be refunded, and no interest shall be paid.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院特许发行福利彩票、体育彩票。未经国务院特许，禁止发行其他彩票。禁止在中华人民共和国境内发行、销售境外彩票。

**Article 3** The State Council authorizes the issuance of welfare lottery and sports lottery under special permit. No other lottery shall be issued without the special permit of the State Council. The issuance and sale of foreign lottery in the territory of the People's Republic of China are prohibited.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　彩票的发行、销售和开奖，应当遵循公开、公平、公正和诚实信用的原则。

**Article 4** The issuance, sale, and drawing of lottery shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, impartiality, and good faith.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院财政部门负责全国的彩票监督管理工作。国务院民政部门、体育行政部门按照各自的职责分别负责全国的福利彩票、体育彩票管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门负责本行政区域的彩票监督管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门、体育行政部门按照各自的职责分别负责本行政区域的福利彩票、体育彩票管理工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府公安机关和县级以上工商行政管理机关，在各自的职责范围内，依法查处非法彩票，维护彩票市场秩序。

**Article 5** The finance department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of lottery nationwide. The civil affairs department and the sports administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the administration of welfare lottery and sports lottery nationwide.  
The finance departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of lottery within their respective administrative regions. The civil affairs departments and the sports administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the administration of welfare lottery and sports lottery within their respective administrative regions.  
The public security organs of the people's governments at or above the county level and the administrative organs for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall, within the scope of their respective functions, investigate and deal with illegal lottery according to law, and maintain order in the lottery market.

### 第二章　彩票发行和销售管理

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院民政部门、体育行政部门依法设立的福利彩票发行机构、体育彩票发行机构(以下简称彩票发行机构)，分别负责全国的福利彩票、体育彩票发行和组织销售工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门、体育行政部门依法设立的福利彩票销售机构、体育彩票销售机构(以下简称彩票销售机构)，分别负责本行政区域的福利彩票、体育彩票销售工作。

**Article 6** The welfare lottery issuing institution and the sports lottery issuing institution (hereinafter referred to as lottery issuing institutions) established according to law by the civil affairs department and the sports administration department of the State Council shall be respectively responsible for the issuance and organized sale of welfare lottery and sports lottery nationwide.  
The welfare lottery sales institution and the sports lottery sales institution (hereinafter referred to as lottery sales institutions) established according to law by the civil affairs departments and the sports administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be respectively responsible for the sale of welfare lottery and sports lottery within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　彩票发行机构申请开设、停止福利彩票、体育彩票的具体品种(以下简称彩票品种)或者申请变更彩票品种审批事项的，应当依照本条例规定的程序报国务院财政部门批准。  
国务院财政部门应当根据彩票市场健康发展的需要，按照合理规划彩票市场和彩票品种结构、严格控制彩票风险的原则，对彩票发行机构的申请进行审查。

**Article 7** If a lottery issuing institution applies to open or terminate a specific type of welfare lottery or sports lottery (hereinafter referred to as a lottery type) or applies to change an approved matter of a lottery type, it shall submit the application to the finance department of the State Council for approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed in these Regulations.  
The finance department of the State Council shall, based on the need for the healthy development of the lottery market and in accordance with the principles of rationally planning the lottery market and lottery type structure and strictly controlling lottery risks, examine the application of the lottery issuing institution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　彩票发行机构申请开设彩票品种，应当经国务院民政部门或者国务院体育行政部门审核同意，向国务院财政部门提交下列申请材料：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)彩票品种的规则；  
(三)发行方式、发行范围；  
(四)市场分析报告及技术可行性分析报告；  
(五)开奖、兑奖操作规程；  
(六)风险控制方案。  
国务院财政部门应当自受理申请之日起90个工作日内，通过专家评审、听证会等方式对开设彩票品种听取社会意见，对申请进行审查并作出书面决定。

**Article 8** If a lottery issuing institution applies to open a lottery type, it shall obtain the examination and consent of the civil affairs department of the State Council or the sports administration department of the State Council, and submit the following application materials to the finance department of the State Council:  
(1) application letter;  
(2) rules of the lottery type;  
(3) issuance method and scope;  
(4) market analysis report and technical feasibility analysis report;  
(5) operational procedures for drawing and prize redemption;  
(6) risk control plan.  
The finance department of the State Council shall, within 90 working days from the date of accepting the application, solicit public opinions on opening the lottery type through expert review, hearings, and other methods, examine the application, and make a written decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　彩票发行机构申请变更彩票品种的规则、发行方式、发行范围等审批事项的，应当经国务院民政部门或者国务院体育行政部门审核同意，向国务院财政部门提出申请并提交与变更事项有关的材料。国务院财政部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内，对申请进行审查并作出书面决定。

**Article 9** If a lottery issuing institution applies to change approved matters such as the rules, issuance method, or issuance scope of a lottery type, it shall obtain the examination and consent of the civil affairs department of the State Council or the sports administration department of the State Council, submit an application to the finance department of the State Council, and provide materials related to the changes. The finance department of the State Council shall, within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application, examine the application and make a written decision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　彩票发行机构申请停止彩票品种的，应当经国务院民政部门或者国务院体育行政部门审核同意，向国务院财政部门提出书面申请并提交与停止彩票品种有关的材料。国务院财政部门应当自受理申请之日起10个工作日内，对申请进行审查并作出书面决定。

**Article 10** If a lottery issuing institution applies to terminate a lottery type, it shall obtain the examination and consent of the civil affairs department of the State Council or the sports administration department of the State Council, submit a written application to the finance department of the State Council, and provide materials related to the termination. The finance department of the State Council shall, within 10 working days from the date of accepting the application, examine the application and make a written decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经批准开设、停止彩票品种或者变更彩票品种审批事项的，彩票发行机构应当在开设、变更、停止的10个自然日前，将有关信息向社会公告。

**Article 11** If the opening or termination of a lottery type or the change of an approved matter of a lottery type is approved, the lottery issuing institution shall, 10 calendar days before the opening, change, or termination, announce the relevant information to the public.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因维护社会公共利益的需要，在紧急情况下，国务院财政部门可以采取必要措施，决定变更彩票品种审批事项或者停止彩票品种。

**Article 12** When necessary to protect the public interest, in an emergency, the finance department of the State Council may take necessary measures to decide to change an approved matter of a lottery type or to terminate a lottery type.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当依照政府采购法律、行政法规的规定，采购符合标准的彩票设备和技术服务。  
彩票设备和技术服务的标准，由国务院财政部门会同国务院民政部门、体育行政部门依照国家有关标准化法律、行政法规的规定制定。

**Article 13** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on government procurement, purchase lottery equipment and technical services that meet the standards.  
The standards for lottery equipment and technical services shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the civil affairs department and the sports administration department of the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the relevant state laws and administrative regulations on standardization.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当建立风险管理体系和可疑资金报告制度，保障彩票发行、销售的安全。  
彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构负责彩票销售系统的数据管理、开奖兑奖管理以及彩票资金的归集管理，不得委托他人管理。

**Article 14** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall establish a risk management system and a suspicious fund reporting system to ensure the safety of lottery issuance and sale.  
Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall be responsible for the data management of the lottery sales system, the management of drawing and prize redemption, and the management of lottery fund collection, and shall not entrust these tasks to any other person.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构可以委托单位、个人代理销售彩票。彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当与接受委托的彩票代销者签订彩票代销合同。福利彩票、体育彩票的代销合同示范文本分别由国务院民政部门、体育行政部门制定。  
彩票代销者不得委托他人代销彩票。

**Article 15** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions may entrust entities or individuals to act as agents for the sale of lottery. Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall enter into lottery agency contracts with the entrusted lottery agents. The model texts of agency contracts for welfare lottery and sports lottery shall be formulated by the civil affairs department and the sports administration department of the State Council respectively.  
A lottery agent shall not entrust any other person to sell lottery on its behalf.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　彩票销售机构应当为彩票代销者配置彩票投注专用设备。彩票投注专用设备属于彩票销售机构所有，彩票代销者不得转借、出租、出售。

**Article 16** Lottery sales institutions shall equip lottery agents with special lottery betting equipment. Such equipment shall be owned by the lottery sales institution, and a lottery agent shall not lend, lease, or sell it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　彩票销售机构应当在彩票发行机构的指导下，统筹规划彩票销售场所的布局。彩票销售场所应当按照彩票发行机构的统一要求，设置彩票销售标识，张贴警示标语。

**Article 17** Lottery sales institutions shall, under the guidance of lottery issuing institutions, make overall plans for the layout of lottery sales venues. Lottery sales venues shall, in accordance with the unified requirements of lottery issuing institutions, set up lottery sales signs and post warning slogans.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构、彩票代销者不得有下列行为：  
(一)进行虚假性、误导性宣传；  
(二)以诋毁同业者等手段进行不正当竞争；  
(三)向未成年人销售彩票；  
(四)以赊销或者信用方式销售彩票。

**Article 18** Lottery issuing institutions, lottery sales institutions, and lottery agents shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) making false or misleading publicity;  
(2) engaging in unfair competition by means such as disparaging competitors;  
(3) selling lottery to minors;  
(4) selling lottery on credit or with credit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　需要销毁彩票的，由彩票发行机构报国务院财政部门批准后，在国务院民政部门或者国务院体育行政部门的监督下销毁。

**Article 19** If lottery tickets need to be destroyed, the lottery issuing institution shall report to the finance department of the State Council for approval, and then destroy them under the supervision of the civil affairs department of the State Council or the sports administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当及时将彩票发行、销售情况向社会全面公布，接受社会公众的监督。

**Article 20** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall promptly and comprehensively publish the issuance and sale of lottery to the public, and accept supervision by the public.

### 第三章　彩票开奖和兑奖管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当按照批准的彩票品种的规则和开奖操作规程开奖。  
国务院民政部门、体育行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门、体育行政部门应当加强对彩票开奖活动的监督，确保彩票开奖的公开、公正。

**Article 21** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall conduct lottery draws in accordance with the approved rules of the lottery type and the operational procedures for drawing.  
The civil affairs departments and sports administration departments of the State Council and the civil affairs departments and sports administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen supervision over lottery drawing activities to ensure openness and fairness in lottery draws.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当确保彩票销售数据的完整、准确和安全。当期彩票销售数据封存后至开奖活动结束前，不得查阅、变更或者删除销售数据。

**Article 22** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall ensure the completeness, accuracy, and security of lottery sales data. After the current period's lottery sales data is sealed and before the drawing activity ends, the sales data shall not be accessed, altered, or deleted.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当加强对开奖设备的管理，确保开奖设备正常运行，并配置备用开奖设备。

**Article 23** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall strengthen the management of drawing equipment, ensure the normal operation of drawing equipment, and equip backup drawing equipment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当在每期彩票销售结束后，及时向社会公布当期彩票的销售情况和开奖结果。

**Article 24** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall, after the sale of each lottery period ends, promptly publish the sales status and drawing results of the current period to the public.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　彩票中奖者应当自开奖之日起60个自然日内，持中奖彩票到指定的地点兑奖，彩票品种的规则规定需要出示身份证件的，还应当出示本人身份证件。逾期不兑奖的视为弃奖。  
禁止使用伪造、变造的彩票兑奖。

**Article 25** A lottery winner shall, within 60 calendar days from the date of drawing, present the winning lottery ticket at the designated place for prize redemption; if the rules of the lottery type require the presentation of identification documents, the winner shall also present his/her identification documents. Failure to redeem the prize within the time limit shall be deemed as forfeiture of the prize.  
The use of forged or altered lottery tickets for prize redemption is prohibited.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构、彩票代销者应当按照彩票品种的规则和兑奖操作规程兑奖。  
彩票中奖奖金应当以人民币现金或者现金支票形式一次性兑付。  
不得向未成年人兑奖。

**Article 26** Lottery issuing institutions, lottery sales institutions, and lottery agents shall redeem prizes in accordance with the rules of the lottery type and the operational procedures for prize redemption.  
Lottery winnings shall be paid in a lump sum in the form of RMB cash or cashier's check.  
Prizes shall not be redeemed to minors.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构、彩票代销者以及其他因职务或者业务便利知悉彩票中奖者个人信息的人员，应当对彩票中奖者个人信息予以保密。

**Article 27** Lottery issuing institutions, lottery sales institutions, lottery agents, and other persons who have access to the personal information of lottery winners due to their positions or business convenience shall keep such personal information confidential.

### 第四章　彩票资金管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　彩票资金包括彩票奖金、彩票发行费和彩票公益金。彩票资金构成比例由国务院决定。  
彩票品种中彩票资金的具体构成比例，由国务院财政部门按照国务院的决定确定。  
随着彩票发行规模的扩大和彩票品种的增加，可以降低彩票发行费比例。

**Article 28** Lottery funds include lottery prizes, lottery issuance fees, and lottery public welfare funds. The proportion of lottery funds shall be determined by the State Council.  
The specific proportion of lottery funds in a lottery type shall be determined by the finance department of the State Council in accordance with the decision of the State Council.  
With the expansion of the scale of lottery issuance and the increase in lottery types, the proportion of lottery issuance fees may be reduced.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当按照国务院财政部门的规定开设彩票资金账户，用于核算彩票资金。

**Article 29** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the State Council, open lottery fund accounts for the accounting of lottery funds.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院财政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门应当建立彩票发行、销售和资金管理信息系统，及时掌握彩票销售和资金流动情况。

**Article 30** The finance department of the State Council and the finance departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish information systems for lottery issuance, sale, and fund management to keep timely track of lottery sales and fund flows.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　彩票奖金用于支付彩票中奖者。彩票单注奖金的最高限额，由国务院财政部门根据彩票市场发展情况决定。  
逾期未兑奖的奖金，纳入彩票公益金。

**Article 31** Lottery prizes shall be used to pay lottery winners. The maximum amount of a single lottery prize shall be determined by the finance department of the State Council based on the development of the lottery market.  
Prizes that are not claimed within the time limit shall be included in the lottery public welfare funds.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　彩票发行费专项用于彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构的业务费用支出以及彩票代销者的销售费用支出。  
彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构的业务费实行收支两条线管理，其支出应当符合彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构财务管理制度。

**Article 32** Lottery issuance fees shall be used exclusively for the business expenses of lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions, as well as the sales expenses of lottery agents.  
The business expenses of lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall be managed under the system of separate revenue and expenditure, and their expenditures shall comply with the financial management systems of lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　彩票公益金专项用于社会福利、体育等社会公益事业，不用于平衡财政一般预算。  
彩票公益金按照政府性基金管理办法纳入预算，实行收支两条线管理。

**Article 33** Lottery public welfare funds shall be used exclusively for social welfare, sports, and other social public welfare undertakings, and shall not be used to balance general fiscal budgets.  
Lottery public welfare funds shall be included in the budget in accordance with the management measures for government-managed funds, and shall be managed under the system of separate revenue and expenditure.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构应当按照国务院财政部门的规定，及时上缴彩票公益金和彩票发行费中的业务费，不得截留或者挪作他用。财政部门应当及时核拨彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构的业务费。

**Article 34** Lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the State Council, promptly remit the lottery public welfare funds and the business fees in the lottery issuance fees, and shall not withhold or divert them for other purposes. The finance department shall promptly allocate the business fees to lottery issuing institutions and lottery sales institutions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　彩票公益金的分配政策，由国务院财政部门会同国务院民政、体育行政等有关部门提出方案，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 35** The distribution policy for lottery public welfare funds shall be proposed by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the civil affairs department, sports administration department, and other relevant departments of the State Council, and be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　彩票发行费、彩票公益金的管理、使用单位，应当依法接受财政部门、审计机关和社会公众的监督。  
彩票公益金的管理、使用单位，应当每年向社会公告公益金的使用情况。

**Article 36** The entities that manage and use lottery issuance fees and lottery public welfare funds shall, according to law, accept supervision by the finance department, auditing authorities, and the public.  
The entities that manage and use lottery public welfare funds shall annually announce the use of the public welfare funds to the public.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院财政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门应当每年向本级人民政府报告上年度彩票公益金的筹集、分配和使用情况，并向社会公告。

**Article 37** The finance department of the State Council and the finance departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall report to the people's government at the same level each year on the raising, distribution, and use of lottery public welfare funds in the preceding year, and announce this information to the public.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，擅自发行、销售彩票，或者在中华人民共和国境内发行、销售境外彩票构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 38** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, issues or sells lottery without authorization, or issues or sells foreign lottery in the territory of the People's Republic of China, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构有下列行为之一的，由财政部门责令停业整顿；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得3倍的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未经批准开设、停止彩票品种或者未经批准变更彩票品种审批事项的；  
(二)未按批准的彩票品种的规则、发行方式、发行范围、开奖兑奖操作规程发行、销售彩票或者开奖兑奖的；  
(三)将彩票销售系统的数据管理、开奖兑奖管理或者彩票资金的归集管理委托他人管理的；  
(四)违反规定查阅、变更、删除彩票销售数据的；  
(五)以赊销或者信用方式销售彩票的；  
(六)未经批准销毁彩票的；  
(七)截留、挪用彩票资金的。

**Article 39** If a lottery issuing institution or lottery sales institution commits any of the following acts, the finance department shall order it to suspend business for rectification; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of three times the illegal gains shall be imposed; the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) opening or terminating a lottery type without approval or changing an approved matter of a lottery type without approval;  
(2) issuing or selling lottery, or conducting draws or prize redemptions, not in accordance with the approved rules, issuance methods, issuance scope, or operational procedures for drawing and prize redemption of the lottery type;  
(3) entrusting the data management of the lottery sales system, the management of drawing and prize redemption, or the management of lottery fund collection to any other person;  
(4) accessing, altering, or deleting lottery sales data in violation of regulations;  
(5) selling lottery on credit or with credit;  
(6) destroying lottery without approval;  
(7) withholding or misappropriating lottery funds.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构有下列行为之一的，由财政部门责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)采购不符合标准的彩票设备或者技术服务的；  
(二)进行虚假性、误导性宣传的；  
(三)以诋毁同业者等手段进行不正当竞争的；  
(四)向未成年人销售彩票的；  
(五)泄露彩票中奖者个人信息的；  
(六)未将逾期未兑奖的奖金纳入彩票公益金的；  
(七)未按规定上缴彩票公益金、彩票发行费中的业务费的。

**Article 40** If a lottery issuing institution or lottery sales institution commits any of the following acts, the finance department shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) purchasing lottery equipment or technical services that do not meet the standards;  
(2) making false or misleading publicity;  
(3) engaging in unfair competition by means such as disparaging competitors;  
(4) selling lottery to minors;  
(5) disclosing personal information of lottery winners;  
(6) failing to include prizes not claimed within the time limit into lottery public welfare funds;  
(7) failing to remit lottery public welfare funds and business fees in lottery issuance fees as required.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　彩票代销者有下列行为之一的，由民政部门、体育行政部门责令改正，处2000元以上1万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
(一)委托他人代销彩票或者转借、出租、出售彩票投注专用设备的；  
(二)进行虚假性、误导性宣传的；  
(三)以诋毁同业者等手段进行不正当竞争的；  
(四)向未成年人销售彩票的；  
(五)以赊销或者信用方式销售彩票的。  
彩票代销者有前款行为受到处罚的，彩票发行机构、彩票销售机构有权解除彩票代销合同。

**Article 41** If a lottery agent commits any of the following acts, the civil affairs department or the sports administration department shall order it to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 but not more than RMB 10,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) entrusting any other person to sell lottery on its behalf, or lending, leasing, or selling special lottery betting equipment;  
(2) making false or misleading publicity;  
(3) engaging in unfair competition by means such as disparaging competitors;  
(4) selling lottery to minors;  
(5) selling lottery on credit or with credit.  
If a lottery agent is penalized for any of the acts in the preceding paragraph, the lottery issuing institution or lottery sales institution shall have the right to terminate the lottery agency contract.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　伪造、变造彩票或使用伪造、变造的彩票兑奖的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Whoever forges or alters lottery tickets, or uses forged or altered lottery tickets for prize redemption, shall be given a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　彩票公益金管理、使用单位违反彩票公益金管理、使用规定的，由财政部门责令限期改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；在规定期限内不改正的，没收已使用彩票公益金形成的资产，取消其彩票公益金使用资格。

**Article 43** If an entity that manages or uses lottery public welfare funds violates the provisions on the management and use of lottery public welfare funds, the finance department shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it fails to make corrections within the specified time limit, the assets formed by the use of lottery public welfare funds shall be confiscated, and its qualification to use lottery public welfare funds shall be revoked.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　依照本条例的规定履行彩票管理职责的财政部门、民政部门、体育行政部门的工作人员，在彩票监督管理活动中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 44** If a staff member of the finance department, civil affairs department, or sports administration department who performs lottery management duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations abuses his/her power, neglects his/her duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the supervision and administration of lottery, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2009年7月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2009.
