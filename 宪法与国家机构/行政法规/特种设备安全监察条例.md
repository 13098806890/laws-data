# 特种设备安全监察条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-01-24  
**生效日期**：2003-06-01

> (2003年3月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第373号公布　根据2009年1月24日《国务院关于修改〈特种设备安全监察条例〉的决定》修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强特种设备的安全监察，防止和减少事故，保障人民群众生命和财产安全，促进经济发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the safety supervision of special equipment, preventing and reducing accidents, ensuring the safety of people's lives and property, and promoting economic development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称特种设备是指涉及生命安全、危险性较大的锅炉、压力容器(含气瓶，下同)、压力管道、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施和场(厂)内专用机动车辆。  
前款特种设备的目录由国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门(以下简称国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门)制订，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, special equipment refers to boilers, pressure vessels (including gas cylinders, hereinafter the same), pressure pipelines, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large amusement rides, and special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories) that are life-threatening and relatively dangerous.  
The catalogue of the special equipment mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the department of the State Council responsible for the safety supervision of special equipment (hereinafter referred to as the State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision) and shall be enforced after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　特种设备的生产(含设计、制造、安装、改造、维修，下同)、使用、检验检测及其监督检查，应当遵守本条例，但本条例另有规定的除外。  
军事装备、核设施、航空航天器、铁路机车、海上设施和船舶以及矿山井下使用的特种设备、民用机场专用设备的安全监察不适用本条例。  
房屋建筑工地和市政工程工地用起重机械、场(厂)内专用机动车辆的安装、使用的监督管理，由建设行政主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 3** The production (including design, manufacture, installation, renovation, and repair, hereinafter the same), use, inspection and testing, and supervision and inspection of special equipment shall comply with these Regulations, unless otherwise provided for herein.  
These Regulations shall not apply to the safety supervision of military equipment, nuclear facilities, aerospace vehicles, railway locomotives, offshore facilities and ships, as well as special equipment used in underground mines and special-purpose equipment for civil airports.  
The supervision and administration of the installation and use of hoisting machinery used in building construction sites and municipal engineering sites, as well as special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories), shall be carried out by the competent administrative department of construction in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门负责全国特种设备的安全监察工作，县以上地方负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门对本行政区域内特种设备实施安全监察(以下统称特种设备安全监督管理部门)。

**Article 4** The State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision shall be responsible for the safety supervision of special equipment nationwide. The departments responsible for the safety supervision and administration of special equipment at or above the county level shall implement safety supervision over special equipment within their respective administrative regions (hereinafter collectively referred to as the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　特种设备生产、使用单位应当建立健全特种设备安全、节能管理制度和岗位安全、节能责任制度。  
特种设备生产、使用单位的主要负责人应当对本单位特种设备的安全和节能全面负责。  
特种设备生产、使用单位和特种设备检验检测机构，应当接受特种设备安全监督管理部门依法进行的特种设备安全监察。

**Article 5** Special equipment production and use entities shall establish and improve a safety and energy conservation management system for special equipment, as well as a post safety and energy conservation responsibility system.  
The principal responsible persons of special equipment production and use entities shall be fully responsible for the safety and energy conservation of the special equipment of their respective entities.  
Special equipment production and use entities and special equipment inspection and testing institutions shall accept the special equipment safety supervision carried out by the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　特种设备检验检测机构，应当依照本条例规定，进行检验检测工作，对其检验检测结果、鉴定结论承担法律责任。

**Article 6** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions shall carry out inspection and testing work in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and shall bear legal liability for their inspection and testing results and expert evaluation conclusions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当督促、支持特种设备安全监督管理部门依法履行安全监察职责，对特种设备安全监察中存在的重大问题及时予以协调、解决。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and support the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments in performing their safety supervision duties according to law, and shall promptly coordinate and resolve major issues existing in the safety supervision of special equipment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励推行科学的管理方法，采用先进技术，提高特种设备安全性能和管理水平，增强特种设备生产、使用单位防范事故的能力，对取得显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。  
国家鼓励特种设备节能技术的研究、开发、示范和推广，促进特种设备节能技术创新和应用。  
特种设备生产、使用单位和特种设备检验检测机构，应当保证必要的安全和节能投入。  
国家鼓励实行特种设备责任保险制度，提高事故赔付能力。

**Article 8** The State encourages the promotion of scientific management methods, the adoption of advanced technologies, the improvement of the safety performance and management level of special equipment, and the enhancement of the ability of special equipment production and use entities to prevent accidents. Units and individuals that have achieved remarkable results shall be rewarded.  
The State encourages the research, development, demonstration, and promotion of energy-saving technologies for special equipment, and promotes innovation and application of energy-saving technologies for special equipment.  
Special equipment production and use entities and special equipment inspection and testing institutions shall ensure necessary investment in safety and energy conservation.  
The State encourages the implementation of a liability insurance system for special equipment to improve the ability to compensate for accidents.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向特种设备安全监督管理部门和行政监察等有关部门举报。  
特种设备安全监督管理部门应当建立特种设备安全监察举报制度，公布举报电话、信箱或者电子邮件地址，受理对特种设备生产、使用和检验检测违法行为的举报，并及时予以处理。  
特种设备安全监督管理部门和行政监察等有关部门应当为举报人保密，并按照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual has the right to report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments and relevant departments such as administrative supervision any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations.  
The special equipment safety supervision and administration departments shall establish a reporting system for special equipment safety supervision, publish reporting telephone numbers, mailboxes, or e-mail addresses, accept reports on illegal acts in the production, use, inspection, and testing of special equipment, and handle them in a timely manner.  
The special equipment safety supervision and administration departments and relevant departments such as administrative supervision shall keep the confidentiality of the whistleblower and give rewards in accordance with relevant State regulations.

### 第二章　特种设备的生产

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　特种设备生产单位，应当依照本条例规定以及国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门制订并公布的安全技术规范(以下简称安全技术规范)的要求，进行生产活动。  
特种设备生产单位对其生产的特种设备的安全性能和能效指标负责，不得生产不符合安全性能要求和能效指标的特种设备，不得生产国家产业政策明令淘汰的特种设备。

**Article 10** Special equipment production entities shall carry out production activities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and the requirements of the safety technical specifications formulated and published by the State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision (hereinafter referred to as safety technical specifications).  
Special equipment production entities shall be responsible for the safety performance and energy efficiency indicators of the special equipment they produce. They shall not produce special equipment that does not meet the safety performance requirements and energy efficiency indicators, and shall not produce special equipment that is explicitly eliminated by the national industrial policy.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　压力容器的设计单位应当经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门许可，方可从事压力容器的设计活动。  
压力容器的设计单位应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与压力容器设计相适应的设计人员、设计审核人员；  
(二)有与压力容器设计相适应的场所和设备；  
(三)有与压力容器设计相适应的健全的管理制度和责任制度。

**Article 11** A pressure vessel design entity shall obtain a license from the State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision before engaging in the design of pressure vessels.  
A pressure vessel design entity shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having designers and design reviewers commensurate with the design of pressure vessels;  
(2) having premises and equipment commensurate with the design of pressure vessels;  
(3) having sound management systems and responsibility systems commensurate with the design of pressure vessels.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　锅炉、压力容器中的气瓶(以下简称气瓶)、氧舱和客运索道、大型游乐设施以及高耗能特种设备的设计文件，应当经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准的检验检测机构鉴定，方可用于制造。

**Article 12** The design documents of gas cylinders (hereinafter referred to as gas cylinders) in boilers and pressure vessels, hyperbaric chambers, passenger ropeways, large amusement rides, and high-energy-consumption special equipment shall be verified by an inspection and testing institution approved by the State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision before they may be used for manufacturing.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　按照安全技术规范的要求，应当进行型式试验的特种设备产品、部件或者试制特种设备新产品、新部件、新材料，必须进行型式试验和能效测试。

**Article 13** Special equipment products, components, or trial-manufactured new special equipment products, new components, or new materials that, according to the requirements of safety technical specifications, shall undergo type testing must undergo type testing and energy efficiency testing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施及其安全附件、安全保护装置的制造、安装、改造单位，以及压力管道用管子、管件、阀门、法兰、补偿器、安全保护装置等(以下简称压力管道元件)的制造单位和场(厂)内专用机动车辆的制造、改造单位，应当经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门许可，方可从事相应的活动。  
前款特种设备的制造、安装、改造单位应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与特种设备制造、安装、改造相适应的专业技术人员和技术工人；  
(二)有与特种设备制造、安装、改造相适应的生产条件和检测手段；  
(三)有健全的质量管理制度和责任制度。

**Article 14** The entities that manufacture, install, or renovate boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large amusement rides, and their safety accessories and safety protection devices, as well as the entities that manufacture pressure pipeline components such as pipes, fittings, valves, flanges, compensators, and safety protection devices (hereinafter referred to as pressure pipeline components), and the entities that manufacture or renovate special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories), shall obtain a license from the State Administration of Special Equipment Safety Supervision before engaging in the corresponding activities.  
The entities that manufacture, install, or renovate the special equipment mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having professional and technical personnel and technical workers commensurate with the manufacture, installation, or renovation of the special equipment;  
(2) having production conditions and testing means commensurate with the manufacture, installation, or renovation of the special equipment;  
(3) having sound quality management systems and responsibility systems.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　特种设备出厂时，应当附有安全技术规范要求的设计文件、产品质量合格证明、安装及使用维修说明、监督检验证明等文件。

**Article 15** When a special equipment leaves the factory, it shall be accompanied by the design documents required by the safety technical specifications, product quality conformity certificates, installation and operation and maintenance instructions, supervision inspection certificates, and other documents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施、场(厂)内专用机动车辆的维修单位，应当有与特种设备维修相适应的专业技术人员和技术工人以及必要的检测手段，并经省、自治区、直辖市特种设备安全监督管理部门许可，方可从事相应的维修活动。

**Article 16** Entities that repair boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large amusement rides, and special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories) shall have professional and technical personnel and technical workers commensurate with the repair of the special equipment, as well as the necessary testing means, and shall obtain a license from the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government before engaging in the corresponding repair activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　锅炉、压力容器、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、维修以及场(厂)内专用机动车辆的改造、维修，必须由依照本条例取得许可的单位进行。  
电梯的安装、改造、维修，必须由电梯制造单位或者其通过合同委托、同意的依照本条例取得许可的单位进行。电梯制造单位对电梯质量以及安全运行涉及的质量问题负责。  
特种设备安装、改造、维修的施工单位应当在施工前将拟进行的特种设备安装、改造、维修情况书面告知直辖市或者设区的市的特种设备安全监督管理部门，告知后即可施工。

**Article 17** The installation, renovation, and repair of boilers, pressure vessels, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, and large amusement rides, as well as the renovation and repair of special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories), must be carried out by entities that have obtained a license according to these Regulations.  
The installation, renovation, and repair of elevators must be carried out by the elevator manufacturing entity or by an entity that has obtained a license according to these Regulations through entrustment or agreement under a contract with the elevator manufacturing entity. The elevator manufacturing entity shall be responsible for the quality of the elevator and the quality issues related to its safe operation.  
The construction entity for the installation, renovation, or repair of special equipment shall, before construction, inform the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts of the intended installation, renovation, or repair of special equipment in writing, and may commence construction after such notification.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　电梯井道的土建工程必须符合建筑工程质量要求。电梯安装施工过程中，电梯安装单位应当遵守施工现场的安全生产要求，落实现场安全防护措施。电梯安装施工过程中，施工现场的安全生产监督，由有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
电梯安装施工过程中，电梯安装单位应当服从建筑施工总承包单位对施工现场的安全生产管理，并订立合同，明确各自的安全责任。

**Article 18** The civil engineering of elevator shafts must meet the quality requirements of construction projects. During the installation construction of elevators, the elevator installation entity shall comply with the safety production requirements of the construction site and implement on-site safety protection measures. During the installation construction of elevators, the supervision of safety production at the construction site shall be carried out by the relevant departments in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
During the installation construction of elevators, the elevator installation entity shall submit to the safety production management of the construction site by the general construction contractor, and shall enter into a contract specifying the respective safety responsibilities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　电梯的制造、安装、改造和维修活动，必须严格遵守安全技术规范的要求。电梯制造单位委托或者同意其他单位进行电梯安装、改造、维修活动的，应当对其安装、改造、维修活动进行安全指导和监控。电梯的安装、改造、维修活动结束后，电梯制造单位应当按照安全技术规范的要求对电梯进行校验和调试，并对校验和调试的结果负责。

**Article 19** The manufacture, installation, renovation, and repair of elevators must strictly comply with the requirements of safety technical specifications. Where an elevator manufacturing entity entrusts or consents to another entity to carry out elevator installation, renovation, or repair activities, it shall provide safety guidance and supervision over such installation, renovation, or repair activities. After the completion of the installation, renovation, or repair activities of an elevator, the elevator manufacturing entity shall verify and debug the elevator in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications and shall be responsible for the results of such verification and debugging.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、维修以及场(厂)内专用机动车辆的改造、维修竣工后，安装、改造、维修的施工单位应当在验收后30日内将有关技术资料移交使用单位，高耗能特种设备还应当按照安全技术规范的要求提交能效测试报告。使用单位应当将其存入该特种设备的安全技术档案。

**Article 20** After the completion of installation, renovation, or repair of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, and large amusement rides, as well as the renovation or repair of special-purpose motor vehicles within premises (factories), the construction entity for installation, renovation, or repair shall, within 30 days after acceptance, transfer the relevant technical data to the using entity. For high-energy-consumption special equipment, an energy efficiency test report shall also be submitted in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications. The using entity shall keep such data in the safety technical archives of the special equipment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　锅炉、压力容器、压力管道元件、起重机械、大型游乐设施的制造过程和锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、重大维修过程，必须经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准的检验检测机构按照安全技术规范的要求进行监督检验；未经监督检验合格的不得出厂或者交付使用。

**Article 21** The manufacturing process of boilers, pressure vessels, pressure piping components, hoisting machinery, and large-scale amusement rides, as well as the installation, modification, and major repair process of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement rides, must be subject to supervision inspection by inspection and testing institutions approved by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications; those that have not passed supervision inspection may not be delivered from the factory or put into use.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　移动式压力容器、气瓶充装单位应当经省、自治区、直辖市的特种设备安全监督管理部门许可，方可从事充装活动。  
充装单位应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与充装和管理相适应的管理人员和技术人员；  
(二)有与充装和管理相适应的充装设备、检测手段、场地厂房、器具、安全设施；  
(三)有健全的充装管理制度、责任制度、紧急处理措施。  
气瓶充装单位应当向气体使用者提供符合安全技术规范要求的气瓶，对使用者进行气瓶安全使用指导，并按照安全技术规范的要求办理气瓶使用登记，提出气瓶的定期检验要求。

**Article 22** A mobile pressure vessel or gas cylinder filling unit shall be licensed by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government before it may engage in filling activities.  
A filling unit shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having management personnel and technical personnel that are appropriate for the filling and management;  
(2) having filling equipment, testing means, sites and workshops, appliances, and safety facilities that are appropriate for the filling and management;  
(3) having a sound filling management system, responsibility system, and emergency handling measures.  
A gas cylinder filling unit shall provide gas users with gas cylinders that meet the requirements of safety technical specifications, instruct users on safe use of gas cylinders, handle gas cylinder use registration in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, and propose requirements for periodic inspection of gas cylinders.

### 第三章　特种设备的使用

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　特种设备使用单位，应当严格执行本条例和有关安全生产的法律、行政法规的规定，保证特种设备的安全使用。

**Article 23** A special equipment user shall strictly implement these Regulations and the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on work safety to ensure the safe use of special equipment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　特种设备使用单位应当使用符合安全技术规范要求的特种设备。特种设备投入使用前，使用单位应当核对其是否附有[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的相关文件。

**Article 24** A special equipment user shall use special equipment that meets the requirements of safety technical specifications. Prior to putting special equipment into use, the user shall check whether it is accompanied by the relevant documents specified in Article 15 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　特种设备在投入使用前或者投入使用后30日内，特种设备使用单位应当向直辖市或者设区的市的特种设备安全监督管理部门登记。登记标志应当置于或者附着于该特种设备的显著位置。

**Article 25** Within 30 days before or after putting special equipment into use, the special equipment user shall register with the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts. The registration mark shall be placed or affixed in a conspicuous position on the special equipment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　特种设备使用单位应当建立特种设备安全技术档案。安全技术档案应当包括以下内容：  
(一)特种设备的设计文件、制造单位、产品质量合格证明、使用维护说明等文件以及安装技术文件和资料；  
(二)特种设备的定期检验和定期自行检查的记录；  
(三)特种设备的日常使用状况记录；  
(四)特种设备及其安全附件、安全保护装置、测量调控装置及有关附属仪器仪表的日常维护保养记录；  
(五)特种设备运行故障和事故记录；  
(六)高耗能特种设备的能效测试报告、能耗状况记录以及节能改造技术资料。

**Article 26** A special equipment user shall establish safety technical archives for special equipment. The safety technical archives shall include the following:  
(1) documents such as the design documents, manufacturer, product quality certificate, and maintenance instructions of the special equipment, as well as installation technical documents and materials;  
(2) records of periodic inspection and periodic self-inspection of the special equipment;  
(3) records of daily use status of the special equipment;  
(4) daily maintenance records of the special equipment and its safety accessories, safety protection devices, measurement and control devices, and related auxiliary instruments and meters;  
(5) records of operational failures and accidents of the special equipment;  
(6) energy efficiency test reports, energy consumption status records, and energy-saving renovation technical materials for high energy-consuming special equipment.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　特种设备使用单位应当对在用特种设备进行经常性日常维护保养，并定期自行检查。  
特种设备使用单位对在用特种设备应当至少每月进行一次自行检查，并作出记录。特种设备使用单位在对在用特种设备进行自行检查和日常维护保养时发现异常情况的，应当及时处理。  
特种设备使用单位应当对在用特种设备的安全附件、安全保护装置、测量调控装置及有关附属仪器仪表进行定期校验、检修，并作出记录。  
锅炉使用单位应当按照安全技术规范的要求进行锅炉水(介)质处理，并接受特种设备检验检测机构实施的水(介)质处理定期检验。  
从事锅炉清洗的单位，应当按照安全技术规范的要求进行锅炉清洗，并接受特种设备检验检测机构实施的锅炉清洗过程监督检验。

**Article 27** A special equipment user shall carry out regular daily maintenance of special equipment in use and conduct periodic self-inspections.  
A special equipment user shall conduct at least one self-inspection per month for special equipment in use and make records. If any abnormal condition is found during self-inspection and daily maintenance of special equipment in use, the user shall promptly handle it.  
A special equipment user shall periodically calibrate and repair the safety accessories, safety protection devices, measurement and control devices, and related auxiliary instruments and meters of special equipment in use, and make records.  
A boiler user shall conduct boiler water (medium) treatment in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, and accept the periodic inspection of water (medium) treatment carried out by a special equipment inspection and testing institution.  
A unit engaged in boiler cleaning shall conduct boiler cleaning in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, and accept the supervision inspection of the boiler cleaning process carried out by a special equipment inspection and testing institution.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　特种设备使用单位应当按照安全技术规范的定期检验要求，在安全检验合格有效期届满前1个月向特种设备检验检测机构提出定期检验要求。  
检验检测机构接到定期检验要求后，应当按照安全技术规范的要求及时进行安全性能检验和能效测试。  
未经定期检验或者检验不合格的特种设备，不得继续使用。

**Article 28** A special equipment user shall, in accordance with the periodic inspection requirements of safety technical specifications, submit a request for periodic inspection to the special equipment inspection and testing institution one month before the expiration of the validity period of the safety inspection pass.  
After receiving the request for periodic inspection, the inspection and testing institution shall promptly carry out safety performance inspection and energy efficiency testing in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications.  
Special equipment that has not undergone periodic inspection or has failed the inspection may not continue to be used.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　特种设备出现故障或者发生异常情况，使用单位应当对其进行全面检查，消除事故隐患后，方可重新投入使用。  
特种设备不符合能效指标的，特种设备使用单位应当采取相应措施进行整改。

**Article 29** If a special equipment malfunctions or an abnormal condition occurs, the user shall conduct a comprehensive inspection of it, and only after eliminating the potential accident hazard may it be put back into use.  
If a special equipment does not meet the energy efficiency indicators, the special equipment user shall take corresponding measures for rectification.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　特种设备存在严重事故隐患，无改造、维修价值，或者超过安全技术规范规定使用年限，特种设备使用单位应当及时予以报废，并应当向原登记的特种设备安全监督管理部门办理注销。

**Article 30** If a special equipment has a serious potential accident hazard, has no value for modification or repair, or exceeds the service life specified in safety technical specifications, the special equipment user shall promptly scrap it and shall go through the cancellation procedures with the original registration special equipment safety supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　电梯的日常维护保养必须由依照本条例取得许可的安装、改造、维修单位或者电梯制造单位进行。  
电梯应当至少每15日进行一次清洁、润滑、调整和检查。

**Article 31** Daily maintenance of an elevator must be carried out by an installation, modification, or repair unit that has obtained a license in accordance with these Regulations, or by the elevator manufacturer.  
An elevator shall be cleaned, lubricated, adjusted, and inspected at least once every 15 days.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　电梯的日常维护保养单位应当在维护保养中严格执行国家安全技术规范的要求，保证其维护保养的电梯的安全技术性能，并负责落实现场安全防护措施，保证施工安全。  
电梯的日常维护保养单位，应当对其维护保养的电梯的安全性能负责。接到故障通知后，应当立即赶赴现场，并采取必要的应急救援措施。

**Article 32** The daily maintenance unit of an elevator shall strictly implement the requirements of national safety technical specifications during maintenance, ensure the safety technical performance of the elevator it maintains, and be responsible for implementing on-site safety protection measures to ensure construction safety.  
The daily maintenance unit of an elevator shall be responsible for the safety performance of the elevator it maintains. Upon receiving a fault notice, it shall immediately rush to the scene and take necessary emergency rescue measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施等为公众提供服务的特种设备运营使用单位，应当设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职的安全管理人员；其他特种设备使用单位，应当根据情况设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职、兼职的安全管理人员。  
特种设备的安全管理人员应当对特种设备使用状况进行经常性检查，发现问题的应当立即处理；情况紧急时，可以决定停止使用特种设备并及时报告本单位有关负责人。

**Article 33** A special equipment operation and use unit that provides services to the public, such as elevators, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement rides, shall establish a special equipment safety management institution or assign full-time safety management personnel; other special equipment users shall, depending on the circumstances, establish a special equipment safety management institution or assign full-time or part-time safety management personnel.  
Safety management personnel of special equipment shall conduct regular inspections of the usage status of special equipment, and if any problem is found, they shall promptly handle it; in an emergency, they may decide to stop using the special equipment and promptly report to the relevant person in charge of the unit.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位在客运索道、大型游乐设施每日投入使用前，应当进行试运行和例行安全检查，并对安全装置进行检查确认。  
电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位应当将电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全注意事项和警示标志置于易于为乘客注意的显著位置。

**Article 34** The operation and use unit of a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride shall, before putting the passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride into use each day, carry out a trial run and routine safety inspection, and check and confirm the safety devices.  
The operation and use unit of an elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement ride shall place safety precautions and warning signs for the elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement ride in a conspicuous position that is easily noticed by passengers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位的主要负责人应当熟悉客运索道、大型游乐设施的相关安全知识，并全面负责客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全使用。  
客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位的主要负责人至少应当每月召开一次会议，督促、检查客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全使用工作。  
客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位，应当结合本单位的实际情况，配备相应数量的营救装备和急救物品。

**Article 35** The principal responsible person of the operation and use unit of a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride shall be familiar with the relevant safety knowledge of the passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride, and shall be fully responsible for the safe use of the passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride.  
The principal responsible person of the operation and use unit of a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride shall convene a meeting at least once a month to supervise and inspect the safe use of the passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride.  
The operation and use unit of a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride shall, based on its actual circumstances, be equipped with an appropriate number of rescue equipment and first-aid items.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的乘客应当遵守使用安全注意事项的要求，服从有关工作人员的指挥。

**Article 36** Passengers of elevators, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement rides shall comply with the requirements of safety precautions for use and obey the directions of relevant staff.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　电梯投入使用后，电梯制造单位应当对其制造的电梯的安全运行情况进行跟踪调查和了解，对电梯的日常维护保养单位或者电梯的使用单位在安全运行方面存在的问题，提出改进建议，并提供必要的技术帮助。发现电梯存在严重事故隐患的，应当及时向特种设备安全监督管理部门报告。电梯制造单位对调查和了解的情况，应当作出记录。

**Article 37** After an elevator is put into use, the elevator manufacturer shall track and understand the safe operation of the elevator it manufactured, put forward improvement suggestions regarding problems existing in the safe operation from the daily maintenance unit or the user of the elevator, and provide necessary technical assistance. If any serious potential accident hazard is found in the elevator, it shall promptly report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department. The elevator manufacturer shall make records of the investigation and understanding.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施、场(厂)内专用机动车辆的作业人员及其相关管理人员(以下统称特种设备作业人员)，应当按照国家有关规定经特种设备安全监督管理部门考核合格，取得国家统一格式的特种作业人员证书，方可从事相应的作业或者管理工作。

**Article 38** Operators and related management personnel of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large-scale amusement rides, and special motor vehicles within factories (plants) (hereinafter collectively referred to as special equipment operators) shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, pass the examination by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department and obtain a national unified format special operator certificate before they may engage in corresponding operations or management work.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　特种设备使用单位应当对特种设备作业人员进行特种设备安全、节能教育和培训，保证特种设备作业人员具备必要的特种设备安全、节能知识。  
特种设备作业人员在作业中应当严格执行特种设备的操作规程和有关的安全规章制度。

**Article 39** A special equipment user shall provide special equipment safety and energy-saving education and training to special equipment operators to ensure that the operators have the necessary special equipment safety and energy-saving knowledge.  
Special equipment operators shall strictly implement the operating procedures of special equipment and relevant safety rules and regulations during operation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　特种设备作业人员在作业过程中发现事故隐患或者其他不安全因素，应当立即向现场安全管理人员和单位有关负责人报告。

**Article 40** If a special equipment operator discovers a potential accident hazard or other unsafe factors during operation, the operator shall immediately report to the on-site safety management personnel and the relevant person in charge of the unit.

### 第四章　检验检测

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从事本条例规定的监督检验、定期检验、型式试验以及专门为特种设备生产、使用、检验检测提供无损检测服务的特种设备检验检测机构，应当经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准。  
特种设备使用单位设立的特种设备检验检测机构，经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准，负责本单位核准范围内的特种设备定期检验工作。

**Article 41** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions that engage in supervisory inspection, periodic inspection, type testing, and nondestructive testing services specifically for the production, use, inspection and testing of special equipment as prescribed in these Regulations shall be approved by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
A special equipment inspection and testing institution established by a special equipment user, upon approval by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, shall be responsible for the periodic inspection of special equipment within its approved scope.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　特种设备检验检测机构，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与所从事的检验检测工作相适应的检验检测人员；  
(二)有与所从事的检验检测工作相适应的检验检测仪器和设备；  
(三)有健全的检验检测管理制度、检验检测责任制度。

**Article 42** A special equipment inspection and testing institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having inspection and testing personnel appropriate for the inspection and testing work it engages in;  
(2) having inspection and testing instruments and equipment appropriate for the inspection and testing work it engages in;  
(3) having a sound inspection and testing management system and inspection and testing responsibility system.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　特种设备的监督检验、定期检验、型式试验和无损检测应当由依照本条例经核准的特种设备检验检测机构进行。  
特种设备检验检测工作应当符合安全技术规范的要求。

**Article 43** Supervisory inspection, periodic inspection, type testing and nondestructive testing of special equipment shall be carried out by special equipment inspection and testing institutions approved in accordance with these Regulations.  
The inspection and testing work of special equipment shall comply with the requirements of safety technical specifications.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　从事本条例规定的监督检验、定期检验、型式试验和无损检测的特种设备检验检测人员应当经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门组织考核合格，取得检验检测人员证书，方可从事检验检测工作。  
检验检测人员从事检验检测工作，必须在特种设备检验检测机构执业，但不得同时在两个以上检验检测机构中执业。

**Article 44** Personnel engaging in supervisory inspection, periodic inspection, type testing and nondestructive testing of special equipment as prescribed in these Regulations shall pass the examination organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and obtain an inspection and testing personnel certificate before they may engage in inspection and testing work.  
Inspection and testing personnel engaging in inspection and testing work must practice in a special equipment inspection and testing institution, but may not concurrently practice in two or more inspection and testing institutions.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员进行特种设备检验检测，应当遵循诚信原则和方便企业的原则，为特种设备生产、使用单位提供可靠、便捷的检验检测服务。  
特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员对涉及的被检验检测单位的商业秘密，负有保密义务。

**Article 45** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and inspection and testing personnel, when performing inspection and testing of special equipment, shall follow the principles of good faith and convenience for enterprises, and provide reliable and convenient inspection and testing services to special equipment manufacturers and users.  
Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and inspection and testing personnel shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets of the inspected and tested entities involved.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员应当客观、公正、及时地出具检验检测结果、鉴定结论。检验检测结果、鉴定结论经检验检测人员签字后，由检验检测机构负责人签署。  
特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员对检验检测结果、鉴定结论负责。  
国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门应当组织对特种设备检验检测机构的检验检测结果、鉴定结论进行监督抽查。县以上地方负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门在本行政区域内也可以组织监督抽查，但是要防止重复抽查。监督抽查结果应当向社会公布。

**Article 46** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and inspection and testing personnel shall issue inspection and testing results and appraisal conclusions objectively, impartially, and in a timely manner. After the inspection and testing results and appraisal conclusions are signed by the inspection and testing personnel, they shall be signed by the person in charge of the inspection and testing institution.  
Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and inspection and testing personnel shall be responsible for the inspection and testing results and appraisal conclusions.  
The special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall organize supervisory spot checks on the inspection and testing results and appraisal conclusions of special equipment inspection and testing institutions. The local departments responsible for special equipment safety supervision and administration at or above the county level may also organize supervisory spot checks within their respective administrative regions, but duplication of spot checks shall be avoided. The results of supervisory spot checks shall be made public.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员不得从事特种设备的生产、销售，不得以其名义推荐或者监制、监销特种设备。

**Article 47** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and inspection and testing personnel may not engage in the production or sale of special equipment, nor may they, in their own name, recommend or supervise the manufacture or sale of special equipment.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　特种设备检验检测机构进行特种设备检验检测，发现严重事故隐患或者能耗严重超标的，应当及时告知特种设备使用单位，并立即向特种设备安全监督管理部门报告。

**Article 48** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution, when performing inspection and testing of special equipment, discovers a serious potential accident hazard or seriously excessive energy consumption, it shall promptly notify the special equipment user and immediately report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员利用检验检测工作故意刁难特种设备生产、使用单位，特种设备生产、使用单位有权向特种设备安全监督管理部门投诉，接到投诉的特种设备安全监督管理部门应当及时进行调查处理。

**Article 49** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution or inspection and testing personnel deliberately creates difficulties for special equipment manufacturers or users in the course of inspection and testing work, the special equipment manufacturer or user shall have the right to complain to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department, and the special equipment safety supervision and administration department that receives the complaint shall promptly investigate and handle the matter.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　特种设备安全监督管理部门依照本条例规定，对特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构实施安全监察。  
对学校、幼儿园以及车站、客运码头、商场、体育场馆、展览馆、公园等公众聚集场所的特种设备，特种设备安全监督管理部门应当实施重点安全监察。

**Article 50** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall, in accordance with these Regulations, carry out safety supervision over special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions.  
For special equipment in public gathering places such as schools, kindergartens, railway stations, passenger terminals, shopping malls, stadiums, exhibition centers, and parks, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall carry out key safety supervision.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　特种设备安全监督管理部门根据举报或者取得的涉嫌违法证据，对涉嫌违反本条例规定的行为进行查处时，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)向特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构的法定代表人、主要负责人和其他有关人员调查、了解与涉嫌从事违反本条例的生产、使用、检验检测有关的情况；  
(二)查阅、复制特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构的有关合同、发票、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
(三)对有证据表明不符合安全技术规范要求的或者有其他严重事故隐患、能耗严重超标的特种设备，予以查封或者扣押。

**Article 51** When investigating and handling activities suspected of violating these Regulations based on reports or obtained evidence of suspected violations, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to investigate and learn from the legal representatives, principal persons in charge, and other relevant personnel of special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions about the circumstances related to the suspected production, use, inspection and testing that violate these Regulations;  
(2) to consult and copy relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials of special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions;  
(3) to seal up or seize special equipment that is proved not to meet the requirements of safety technical specifications, or that has other serious potential accident hazards or seriously excessive energy consumption.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　依照本条例规定实施许可、核准、登记的特种设备安全监督管理部门，应当严格依照本条例规定条件和安全技术规范要求对有关事项进行审查；不符合本条例规定条件和安全技术规范要求的，不得许可、核准、登记；在申请办理许可、核准期间，特种设备安全监督管理部门发现申请人未经许可从事特种设备相应活动或者伪造许可、核准证书的，不予受理或者不予许可、核准，并在1年内不再受理其新的许可、核准申请。  
未依法取得许可、核准、登记的单位擅自从事特种设备的生产、使用或者检验检测活动的，特种设备安全监督管理部门应当依法予以处理。  
违反本条例规定，被依法撤销许可的，自撤销许可之日起3年内，特种设备安全监督管理部门不予受理其新的许可申请。

**Article 52** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department that grants licenses, approvals, or registrations in accordance with these Regulations shall examine the relevant matters strictly in accordance with the conditions prescribed in these Regulations and the requirements of safety technical specifications; if the conditions and requirements are not met, licenses, approvals, or registrations shall not be granted. If, during the period when the applicant applies for a license or approval, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department discovers that the applicant has engaged in corresponding activities for special equipment without a license or has forged a license or approval certificate, the application shall not be accepted, or the license or approval shall not be granted, and new applications for licenses or approvals by the same applicant shall not be accepted within one year.  
If a unit that has not obtained a license, approval, or registration according to law engages in the production, use, or inspection and testing of special equipment without authorization, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall deal with it according to law.  
If a license is revoked according to law for violation of these Regulations, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall not accept any new license application from the same applicant within three years from the date of revocation of the license.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　特种设备安全监督管理部门在办理本条例规定的有关行政审批事项时，其受理、审查、许可、核准的程序必须公开，并应当自受理申请之日起30日内，作出许可、核准或者不予许可、核准的决定；不予许可、核准的，应当书面向申请人说明理由。

**Article 53** When handling relevant administrative approval matters prescribed in these Regulations, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department must make public the procedures for acceptance, examination, licensing, and approval, and shall, within 30 days from the date of acceptance of the application, make a decision to grant or deny the license or approval; if the license or approval is denied, reasons shall be provided in writing to the applicant.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　地方各级特种设备安全监督管理部门不得以任何形式进行地方保护和地区封锁，不得对已经依照本条例规定在其他地方取得许可的特种设备生产单位重复进行许可，也不得要求对依照本条例规定在其他地方检验检测合格的特种设备，重复进行检验检测。

**Article 54** Local special equipment safety supervision and administration departments at all levels may not engage in local protection or regional blockade in any form, may not repeatedly grant licenses to special equipment manufacturers that have already obtained licenses in other places in accordance with these Regulations, and may not require repeated inspection and testing of special equipment that have passed inspection and testing in other places in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　特种设备安全监督管理部门的安全监察人员(以下简称特种设备安全监察人员)应当熟悉相关法律、法规、规章和安全技术规范，具有相应的专业知识和工作经验，并经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门考核，取得特种设备安全监察人员证书。  
特种设备安全监察人员应当忠于职守、坚持原则、秉公执法。

**Article 55** Safety inspectors of the special equipment safety supervision and administration department (hereinafter referred to as special equipment safety inspectors) shall be familiar with relevant laws, regulations, rules and safety technical specifications, possess corresponding professional knowledge and work experience, and pass the examination organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and obtain a special equipment safety inspector certificate.  
Special equipment safety inspectors shall be dedicated to their duties, adhere to principles, and enforce the law impartially.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　特种设备安全监督管理部门对特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构实施安全监察时，应当有两名以上特种设备安全监察人员参加，并出示有效的特种设备安全监察人员证件。

**Article 56** When the special equipment safety supervision and administration department carries out safety supervision over special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions, there shall be at least two special equipment safety inspectors present, and they shall produce valid special equipment safety inspector certificates.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　特种设备安全监督管理部门对特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构实施安全监察，应当对每次安全监察的内容、发现的问题及处理情况，作出记录，并由参加安全监察的特种设备安全监察人员和被检查单位的有关负责人签字后归档。被检查单位的有关负责人拒绝签字的，特种设备安全监察人员应当将情况记录在案。

**Article 57** When the special equipment safety supervision and administration department carries out safety supervision over special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions, it shall make records of the content of each safety supervision, the problems discovered, and the handling thereof, and such records shall be signed by the special equipment safety inspectors participating in the supervision and the relevant persons in charge of the inspected unit, and then be archived. If the relevant person in charge of the inspected unit refuses to sign, the special equipment safety inspectors shall record such circumstances in the file.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　特种设备安全监督管理部门对特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构进行安全监察时，发现有违反本条例规定和安全技术规范要求的行为或者在用的特种设备存在事故隐患、不符合能效指标的，应当以书面形式发出特种设备安全监察指令，责令有关单位及时采取措施，予以改正或者消除事故隐患。紧急情况下需要采取紧急处置措施的，应当随后补发书面通知。

**Article 58** When the special equipment safety supervision and administration department carries out safety supervision over special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions and discovers acts violating the provisions of these Regulations or the requirements of safety technical specifications, or that in-use special equipment has potential accident hazards or fails to meet energy efficiency indicators, it shall issue a special equipment safety supervision directive in writing, ordering the relevant unit to take timely measures to correct such acts or eliminate the potential accident hazards. If emergency measures need to be taken under urgent circumstances, a written notice shall be subsequently issued.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　特种设备安全监督管理部门对特种设备生产、使用单位和检验检测机构进行安全监察，发现重大违法行为或者严重事故隐患时，应当在采取必要措施的同时，及时向上级特种设备安全监督管理部门报告。接到报告的特种设备安全监督管理部门应当采取必要措施，及时予以处理。  
对违法行为、严重事故隐患或者不符合能效指标的处理需要当地人民政府和有关部门的支持、配合时，特种设备安全监督管理部门应当报告当地人民政府，并通知其他有关部门。当地人民政府和其他有关部门应当采取必要措施，及时予以处理。

**Article 59** When the special equipment safety supervision and administration department carries out safety supervision over special equipment manufacturers, users, and inspection and testing institutions and discovers a major illegal act or a serious potential accident hazard, it shall, while taking necessary measures, promptly report to the superior special equipment safety supervision and administration department. The special equipment safety supervision and administration department that receives the report shall take necessary measures and handle the matter promptly.  
Where the handling of an illegal act, a serious potential accident hazard, or non-compliance with energy efficiency indicators requires support and cooperation from the local people's government and relevant departments, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall report to the local people's government and notify other relevant departments. The local people's government and other relevant departments shall take necessary measures and handle the matter promptly.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市特种设备安全监督管理部门应当定期向社会公布特种设备安全以及能效状况。  
公布特种设备安全以及能效状况，应当包括下列内容：  
(一)特种设备质量安全状况；  
(二)特种设备事故的情况、特点、原因分析、防范对策；  
(三)特种设备能效状况；  
(四)其他需要公布的情况。

**Article 60** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall periodically make public the safety and energy efficiency status of special equipment.  
The publication of the safety and energy efficiency status of special equipment shall include the following contents:  
(1) the quality and safety status of special equipment;  
(2) the circumstances, characteristics, cause analysis, and preventive measures of special equipment accidents;  
(3) the energy efficiency status of special equipment;  
(4) other circumstances that need to be published.

### 第六章　事故预防和调查处理

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有下列情形之一的，为特别重大事故：  
(一)特种设备事故造成30人以上死亡，或者100人以上重伤(包括急性工业中毒，下同)，或者1亿元以上直接经济损失的；  
(二)600兆瓦以上锅炉爆炸的；  
(三)压力容器、压力管道有毒介质泄漏，造成15万人以上转移的；  
(四)客运索道、大型游乐设施高空滞留100人以上并且时间在48小时以上的。

**Article 61** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be an extremely serious accident:  
(1) A special equipment accident causes the death of 30 or more persons, or causes serious injury to 100 or more persons (including acute industrial poisoning, similarly hereinafter), or causes direct economic losses of RMB 100 million Yuan or more;  
(2) A boiler of 600 MW or more explodes;  
(3) Leakage of toxic media from pressure vessels or pressure pipelines results in the evacuation of 150,000 or more persons;  
(4) Passenger ropeways or large-scale amusement rides strand 100 or more persons at a height for a period of 48 hours or more.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列情形之一的，为重大事故：  
(一)特种设备事故造成10人以上30人以下死亡，或者50人以上100人以下重伤，或者5000万元以上1亿元以下直接经济损失的；  
(二)600兆瓦以上锅炉因安全故障中断运行240小时以上的；  
(三)压力容器、压力管道有毒介质泄漏，造成5万人以上15万人以下转移的；  
(四)客运索道、大型游乐设施高空滞留100人以上并且时间在24小时以上48小时以下的。

**Article 62** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be a serious accident:  
(1) A special equipment accident causes the death of 10 or more but fewer than 30 persons, or causes serious injury to 50 or more but fewer than 100 persons, or causes direct economic losses of RMB 50 million Yuan or more but less than RMB 100 million Yuan;  
(2) A boiler of 600 MW or more is shut down due to a safety failure for 240 hours or more;  
(3) Leakage of toxic media from pressure vessels or pressure pipelines results in the evacuation of 50,000 or more but fewer than 150,000 persons;  
(4) Passenger ropeways or large-scale amusement rides strand 100 or more persons at a height for a period of 24 hours or more but fewer than 48 hours.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列情形之一的，为较大事故：  
(一)特种设备事故造成3人以上10人以下死亡，或者10人以上50人以下重伤，或者1000万元以上5000万元以下直接经济损失的；  
(二)锅炉、压力容器、压力管道爆炸的；  
(三)压力容器、压力管道有毒介质泄漏，造成1万人以上5万人以下转移的；  
(四)起重机械整体倾覆的；  
(五)客运索道、大型游乐设施高空滞留人员12小时以上的。

**Article 63** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be a relatively serious accident:  
(1) A special equipment accident causes the death of 3 or more but fewer than 10 persons, or causes serious injury to 10 or more but fewer than 50 persons, or causes direct economic losses of RMB 10 million Yuan or more but less than RMB 50 million Yuan;  
(2) A boiler, pressure vessel or pressure pipeline explodes;  
(3) Leakage of toxic media from pressure vessels or pressure pipelines results in the evacuation of 10,000 or more but fewer than 50,000 persons;  
(4) A hoisting machinery completely overturns;  
(5) Passenger ropeways or large-scale amusement rides strand persons at a height for 12 hours or more.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　有下列情形之一的，为一般事故：  
(一)特种设备事故造成3人以下死亡，或者10人以下重伤，或者1万元以上1000万元以下直接经济损失的；  
(二)压力容器、压力管道有毒介质泄漏，造成500人以上1万人以下转移的；  
(三)电梯轿厢滞留人员2小时以上的；  
(四)起重机械主要受力结构件折断或者起升机构坠落的；  
(五)客运索道高空滞留人员3.5小时以上12小时以下的；  
(六)大型游乐设施高空滞留人员1小时以上12小时以下的。  
除前款规定外，国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门可以对一般事故的其他情形做出补充规定。

**Article 64** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be a general accident:  
(1) A special equipment accident causes the death of fewer than 3 persons, or causes serious injury to fewer than 10 persons, or causes direct economic losses of RMB 10,000 Yuan or more but less than RMB 10 million Yuan;  
(2) Leakage of toxic media from pressure vessels or pressure pipelines results in the evacuation of 500 or more but fewer than 10,000 persons;  
(3) An elevator car strands persons for 2 hours or more;  
(4) The main load-bearing structural member of a hoisting machinery breaks or the hoisting mechanism falls;  
(5) Passenger ropeways strand persons at a height for 3.5 hours or more but fewer than 12 hours;  
(6) Large-scale amusement rides strand persons at a height for 1 hour or more but fewer than 12 hours.  
In addition to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council may make supplementary provisions for other circumstances of general accidents.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　特种设备安全监督管理部门应当制定特种设备应急预案。特种设备使用单位应当制定事故应急专项预案，并定期进行事故应急演练。  
压力容器、压力管道发生爆炸或者泄漏，在抢险救援时应当区分介质特性，严格按照相关预案规定程序处理，防止二次爆炸。

**Article 65** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall formulate emergency response plans for special equipment. The user of special equipment shall formulate special contingency plans for accidents, and shall conduct regular accident emergency drills.  
Where a pressure vessel or pressure pipeline explodes or leaks, the characteristics of the medium shall be distinguished during emergency rescue, and procedures shall be strictly followed in accordance with the relevant contingency plans to prevent secondary explosion.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　特种设备事故发生后，事故发生单位应当立即启动事故应急预案，组织抢救，防止事故扩大，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，并及时向事故发生地县以上特种设备安全监督管理部门和有关部门报告。  
县以上特种设备安全监督管理部门接到事故报告，应当尽快核实有关情况，立即向所在地人民政府报告，并逐级上报事故情况。必要时，特种设备安全监督管理部门可以越级上报事故情况。对特别重大事故、重大事故，国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门应当立即报告国务院并通报国务院安全生产监督管理部门等有关部门。

**Article 66** After the occurrence of a special equipment accident, the entity where the accident occurred shall immediately initiate the accident emergency plan, organize rescue, prevent the accident from escalating, reduce casualties and property losses, and shall promptly report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department at or above the county level in the place where the accident occurred and to relevant departments.  
Upon receiving an accident report, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall verify the relevant information as soon as possible, immediately report to the local people's government, and report the accident situation level by level. When necessary, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department may report the accident situation by skipping levels. For extremely serious accidents and serious accidents, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council shall immediately report to the State Council and notify the work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　特别重大事故由国务院或者国务院授权有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
重大事故由国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
较大事故由省、自治区、直辖市特种设备安全监督管理部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
一般事故由设区的市的特种设备安全监督管理部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。

**Article 67** An extremely serious accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the State Council or by a department authorized by the State Council.  
A serious accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council in conjunction with relevant departments.  
A relatively serious accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in conjunction with relevant departments.  
A general accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of a city divided into districts in conjunction with relevant departments.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　事故调查报告应当由负责组织事故调查的特种设备安全监督管理部门的所在地人民政府批复，并报上一级特种设备安全监督管理部门备案。  
有关机关应当按照批复，依照法律、行政法规规定的权限和程序，对事故责任单位和有关人员进行行政处罚，对负有事故责任的国家工作人员进行处分。

**Article 68** The accident investigation report shall be approved by the local people's government of the place where the special equipment safety supervision and administration department responsible for organizing the accident investigation is located, and shall be reported to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department at the next higher level for recordation.  
The relevant authorities shall, in accordance with the approval and within the limits of authority and procedures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, impose administrative penalties on the entity liable for the accident and the relevant persons, and shall impose sanctions on the state functionaries liable for the accident.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　特种设备安全监督管理部门应当在有关地方人民政府的领导下，组织开展特种设备事故调查处理工作。  
有关地方人民政府应当支持、配合上级人民政府或者特种设备安全监督管理部门的事故调查处理工作，并提供必要的便利条件。

**Article 69** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall, under the leadership of the relevant local people's government, organize and carry out the investigation and handling of special equipment accidents.  
The relevant local people's government shall support and cooperate with the investigation and handling work of the people's government at a higher level or the special equipment safety supervision and administration department, and shall provide the necessary convenience.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　特种设备安全监督管理部门应当对发生事故的原因进行分析，并根据特种设备的管理和技术特点、事故情况对相关安全技术规范进行评估；需要制定或者修订相关安全技术规范的，应当及时制定或者修订。

**Article 70** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall analyze the causes of accidents, and shall evaluate the relevant safety technical specifications based on the management and technical characteristics of special equipment and the accident circumstances; where it is necessary to formulate or revise relevant safety technical specifications, it shall formulate or revise them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本章所称的“以上”包括本数，所称的“以下”不包括本数。

**Article 71** The term "or more" as used in this Chapter includes the number itself, and the term "or less" as used in this Chapter does not include the number itself.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　未经许可，擅自从事压力容器设计活动的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门予以取缔，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Where any entity or individual, without permission, engages in pressure vessel design activities without authorization, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall ban such activities, impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 Yuan; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　锅炉、气瓶、氧舱和客运索道、大型游乐设施以及高耗能特种设备的设计文件，未经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准的检验检测机构鉴定，擅自用于制造的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，没收非法制造的产品，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于生产、销售伪劣产品罪、非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** Where the design documents of boilers, gas cylinders, oxygen chambers, passenger ropeways, large-scale amusement rides, and high energy-consuming special equipment are used for manufacturing without appraisal by an inspection and testing institution approved by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction, confiscate the illegally manufactured products, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 Yuan; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of producing or selling fake or substandard products, the crime of illegal business operation, or other crimes.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　按照安全技术规范的要求应当进行型式试验的特种设备产品、部件或者试制特种设备新产品、新部件，未进行整机或者部件型式试验的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 74** Where special equipment products, components, or trial-manufactured new special equipment products or new components that shall undergo type testing in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications have not undergone type testing for the complete machine or components, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction within a specified period; if correction is not made within the specified period, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 Yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　未经许可，擅自从事锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施、场(厂)内专用机动车辆及其安全附件、安全保护装置的制造、安装、改造以及压力管道元件的制造活动的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门予以取缔，没收非法制造的产品，已经实施安装、改造的，责令恢复原状或者责令限期由取得许可的单位重新安装、改造，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于生产、销售伪劣产品罪、非法经营罪、重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** Where any entity or individual, without permission, engages in the manufacture, installation, or retrofitting of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large-scale amusement rides, special-purpose motor vehicles in factories (yards) and their safety accessories and safety protection devices, or the manufacture of pressure piping components without authorization, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall ban such activities, confiscate the illegally manufactured products; if installation or retrofitting has been carried out, order restoration to the original condition or order re-installation or re-retrofitting within a specified period by a licensed entity, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 Yuan; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of producing or selling fake or substandard products, the crime of illegal business operation, the crime of major liability accident, or other crimes.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　特种设备出厂时，未按照安全技术规范的要求附有设计文件、产品质量合格证明、安装及使用维修说明、监督检验证明等文件的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正；情节严重的，责令停止生产、销售，处违法生产、销售货值金额30%以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 76** Where, upon delivery of special equipment, the design documents, product quality certificates, installation and operation maintenance instructions, supervision inspection certificates, and other documents are not attached in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, order cessation of production and sale, and impose a fine of not more than 30% of the value of the illegally produced or sold goods; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　未经许可，擅自从事锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施、场(厂)内专用机动车辆的维修或者日常维护保养的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门予以取缔，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于非法经营罪、重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 77** Where any entity or individual, without permission, engages in the repair or routine maintenance of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large-scale amusement rides, or special-purpose motor vehicles in factories (yards) without authorization, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall ban such activities, impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 Yuan; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation, the crime of major liability accident, or other crimes.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、维修的施工单位以及场(厂)内专用机动车辆的改造、维修单位，在施工前未将拟进行的特种设备安装、改造、维修情况书面告知直辖市或者设区的市的特种设备安全监督管理部门即行施工的，或者在验收后30日内未将有关技术资料移交锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的使用单位的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处2000元以上1万元以下罚款。

**Article 78** Where the construction entity for the installation, retrofitting, or repair of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, or large-scale amusement rides, or the entity for the retrofitting or repair of special-purpose motor vehicles in factories (yards), fails to notify in writing the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the municipality directly under the Central Government or the city divided into districts of the intended installation, retrofitting, or repair of special equipment before commencing construction, or fails to transfer the relevant technical data to the user of the boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, or large-scale amusement rides within 30 days after acceptance inspection, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction within a specified period; if correction is not made within the specified period, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 Yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　锅炉、压力容器、压力管道元件、起重机械、大型游乐设施的制造过程和锅炉、压力容器、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、重大维修过程，以及锅炉清洗过程，未经国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门核准的检验检测机构按照安全技术规范的要求进行监督检验的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，已经出厂的，没收违法生产、销售的产品，已经实施安装、改造、重大维修或者清洗的，责令限期进行监督检验，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，撤销制造、安装、改造或者维修单位已经取得的许可，并由工商行政管理部门吊销其营业执照；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于生产、销售伪劣产品罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 79** Where the manufacturing process of boilers, pressure vessels, pressure piping components, hoisting machinery, or large-scale amusement rides, or the installation, retrofitting, major repair process of boilers, pressure vessels, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, or large-scale amusement rides, or the cleaning process of boilers, has not undergone supervision inspection by an inspection and testing institution approved by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction; if the products have been delivered, confiscate the illegally produced or sold products; if installation, retrofitting, major repair, or cleaning has been carried out, order supervision inspection within a specified period, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 Yuan; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, revoke the license already obtained by the manufacturing, installation, retrofitting, or repair entity, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of producing or selling fake or substandard products or other crimes.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　未经许可，擅自从事移动式压力容器或者气瓶充装活动的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门予以取缔，没收违法充装的气瓶，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。  
移动式压力容器、气瓶充装单位未按照安全技术规范的要求进行充装活动的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销其充装资格。

**Article 80** Where any entity or individual, without permission, engages in the filling of mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders without authorization, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall ban such activities, confiscate the illegally filled gas cylinders, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 Yuan; if there is any illegal income, the illegal income shall be confiscated; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes.  
Where a filling unit of mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders fails to carry out filling activities in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 Yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 Yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its filling qualification shall be revoked.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　电梯制造单位有下列情形之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，予以通报批评：  
(一)未依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定对电梯进行校验、调试的；  
(二)对电梯的安全运行情况进行跟踪调查和了解时，发现存在严重事故隐患，未及时向特种设备安全监督管理部门报告的。

**Article 81** Where an elevator manufacturing unit falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be subject to circulating notices of criticism:  
(1) failing to calibrate and debug the elevator in accordance with the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations; or  
(2) failing to timely report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department when discovering serious potential accident hazards during tracking investigation and understanding of the safe operation of the elevator.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　已经取得许可、核准的特种设备生产单位、检验检测机构有下列行为之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销其相应资格：  
(一)未按照安全技术规范的要求办理许可证变更手续的；  
(二)不再符合本条例规定或者安全技术规范要求的条件，继续从事特种设备生产、检验检测的；  
(三)未依照本条例规定或者安全技术规范要求进行特种设备生产、检验检测的；  
(四)伪造、变造、出租、出借、转让许可证书或者监督检验报告的。

**Article 82** Where a special equipment production unit or inspection and testing institution that has obtained a license or approval commits any of the following acts, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant qualification shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to go through the formalities for alteration of the license certificate in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications;  
(2) continuing to engage in the production, inspection or testing of special equipment when it no longer meets the conditions specified in these Regulations or the requirements of the safety technical specifications;  
(3) failing to carry out the production, inspection or testing of special equipment in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations or the requirements of the safety technical specifications; or  
(4) forging, altering, leasing, lending or transferring the license certificate or supervision inspection report.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　特种设备使用单位有下列情形之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止使用或者停产停业整顿：  
(一)特种设备投入使用前或者投入使用后30日内，未向特种设备安全监督管理部门登记，擅自将其投入使用的；  
(二)未依照[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)的规定，建立特种设备安全技术档案的；  
(三)未依照[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)的规定，对在用特种设备进行经常性日常维护保养和定期自行检查的，或者对在用特种设备的安全附件、安全保护装置、测量调控装置及有关附属仪器仪表进行定期校验、检修，并作出记录的；  
(四)未按照安全技术规范的定期检验要求，在安全检验合格有效期届满前1个月向特种设备检验检测机构提出定期检验要求的；  
(五)使用未经定期检验或者检验不合格的特种设备的；  
(六)特种设备出现故障或者发生异常情况，未对其进行全面检查、消除事故隐患，继续投入使用的；  
(七)未制定特种设备事故应急专项预案的；  
(八)未依照[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)第二款的规定，对电梯进行清洁、润滑、调整和检查的；  
(九)未按照安全技术规范要求进行锅炉水(介)质处理的；  
(十)特种设备不符合能效指标，未及时采取相应措施进行整改的。  
特种设备使用单位使用未取得生产许可的单位生产的特种设备或者将非承压锅炉、非压力容器作为承压锅炉、压力容器使用的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令停止使用，予以没收，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 83** Where a special equipment user falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease use or suspend production and business for rectification:  
(1) putting special equipment into use without registration with the special equipment safety supervision and administration department before being put into use or within 30 days after being put into use;  
(2) failing to establish safety technical archives for special equipment in accordance with the provisions of Article 26 of these Regulations;  
(3) failing to carry out regular daily maintenance and regular self-inspection of special equipment in use in accordance with the provisions of Article 27 of these Regulations, or failing to carry out regular calibration and repair of safety accessories, safety protection devices, measurement and control devices and related auxiliary instruments and meters of special equipment in use and make records thereof;  
(4) failing to submit a request for periodic inspection to the special equipment inspection and testing institution one month before the expiry of the validity period of the safety inspection certificate in accordance with the periodic inspection requirements of the safety technical specifications;  
(5) using special equipment that has not undergone periodic inspection or has failed inspection;  
(6) continuing to use special equipment without conducting a comprehensive inspection and eliminating accident hazards when it malfunctions or becomes abnormal;  
(7) failing to formulate a special contingency plan for special equipment accidents;  
(8) failing to clean, lubricate, adjust and inspect the elevator in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 31 of these Regulations;  
(9) failing to treat boiler water (medium) in accordance with the requirements of the safety technical specifications; or  
(10) failing to take timely corresponding measures for rectification when special equipment does not meet the energy efficiency indicators.  
If a special equipment user uses special equipment produced by a unit that has not obtained a production license, or uses non-pressure-bearing boilers or non-pressure vessels as pressure-bearing boilers or pressure vessels, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to cease use, confiscate the equipment, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　特种设备存在严重事故隐患，无改造、维修价值，或者超过安全技术规范规定的使用年限，特种设备使用单位未予以报废，并向原登记的特种设备安全监督管理部门办理注销的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款。

**Article 84** Where special equipment has serious potential accident hazards, has no value for modification or repair, or exceeds the service life specified in the safety technical specifications, and the special equipment user fails to scrap it and go through cancellation formalities with the original registering special equipment safety supervision and administration department, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位有下列情形之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止使用或者停产停业整顿，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款：  
(一)客运索道、大型游乐设施每日投入使用前，未进行试运行和例行安全检查，并对安全装置进行检查确认的；  
(二)未将电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全注意事项和警示标志置于易于为乘客注意的显著位置的。

**Article 85** Where an operation and use unit of an elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement ride falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to cease use or suspend production and business for rectification, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to carry out trial operation and routine safety inspection before daily use of the passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride, and failing to check and confirm the safety devices; or  
(2) failing to place the safety precautions and warning signs of the elevator, passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement ride in a conspicuous position easily noticeable by passengers.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　特种设备使用单位有下列情形之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止使用或者停产停业整顿，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款：  
(一)未依照本条例规定设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职、兼职的安全管理人员的；  
(二)从事特种设备作业的人员，未取得相应特种作业人员证书，上岗作业的；  
(三)未对特种设备作业人员进行特种设备安全教育和培训的。

**Article 86** Where a special equipment user falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to cease use or suspend production and business for rectification, and a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to set up a special equipment safety management organization or to assign full-time or part-time safety management personnel in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) personnel engaged in special equipment operation performing work without obtaining the corresponding special operations personnel certificate; or  
(3) failing to provide special equipment safety education and training to special equipment operators.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　发生特种设备事故，有下列情形之一的，对单位，由特种设备安全监督管理部门处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；对主要负责人，由特种设备安全监督管理部门处4000元以上2万元以下罚款；属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)特种设备使用单位的主要负责人在本单位发生特种设备事故时，不立即组织抢救或者在事故调查处理期间擅离职守或者逃匿的；  
(二)特种设备使用单位的主要负责人对特种设备事故隐瞒不报、谎报或者拖延不报的。

**Article 87** Where a special equipment accident occurs and falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the unit, and a fine of not less than 4,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the principal responsible person; if the person is a state functionary, a sanction shall be given according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes:  
(1) the principal responsible person of the special equipment user fails to immediately organize rescue when a special equipment accident occurs in the unit, or leaves the post without permission or absconds during the investigation and handling of the accident; or  
(2) the principal responsible person of the special equipment user conceals, makes false reports, or delays reporting of the special equipment accident.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　对事故发生负有责任的单位，由特种设备安全监督管理部门依照下列规定处以罚款：  
(一)发生一般事故的，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款；  
(二)发生较大事故的，处20万元以上50万元以下罚款；  
(三)发生重大事故的，处50万元以上200万元以下罚款。

**Article 88** A unit that bears responsibility for the occurrence of an accident shall be fined by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) if a general accident occurs, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) if a relatively serious accident occurs, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; and  
(3) if a major accident occurs, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　对事故发生负有责任的单位的主要负责人未依法履行职责，导致事故发生的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门依照下列规定处以罚款；属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)发生一般事故的，处上一年年收入30%的罚款；  
(二)发生较大事故的，处上一年年收入40%的罚款；  
(三)发生重大事故的，处上一年年收入60%的罚款。

**Article 89** Where the principal responsible person of a unit that bears responsibility for the occurrence of an accident fails to perform his or her duties according to law, resulting in the accident, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall impose a fine in accordance with the following provisions; if the person is a state functionary, a sanction shall also be given according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes:  
(1) if a general accident occurs, a fine of 30% of the previous year's annual income shall be imposed;  
(2) if a relatively serious accident occurs, a fine of 40% of the previous year's annual income shall be imposed; and  
(3) if a major accident occurs, a fine of 60% of the previous year's annual income shall be imposed.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　特种设备作业人员违反特种设备的操作规程和有关的安全规章制度操作，或者在作业过程中发现事故隐患或者其他不安全因素，未立即向现场安全管理人员和单位有关负责人报告的，由特种设备使用单位给予批评教育、处分；情节严重的，撤销特种设备作业人员资格；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 90** Where a special equipment operator violates the operating procedures of the special equipment and relevant safety rules and regulations during operation, or fails to immediately report to the site safety management personnel and the relevant person in charge of the unit upon discovering potential accident hazards or other unsafe factors during the operation, the special equipment user shall give him or her criticism education and sanctions; if the circumstances are serious, his or her special equipment operator qualification shall be revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of major liability accident or other crimes.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　未经核准，擅自从事本条例所规定的监督检验、定期检验、型式试验以及无损检测等检验检测活动的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门予以取缔，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 91** Where any person, without approval, engages without authorization in inspection and testing activities such as supervision inspection, periodic inspection, type test and non-destructive testing as specified in these Regulations, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall ban such activities, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law against the competent persons in charge and other directly liable persons in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　特种设备检验检测机构，有下列情形之一的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销其检验检测资格：  
(一)聘用未经特种设备安全监督管理部门组织考核合格并取得检验检测人员证书的人员，从事相关检验检测工作的；  
(二)在进行特种设备检验检测中，发现严重事故隐患或者能耗严重超标，未及时告知特种设备使用单位，并立即向特种设备安全监督管理部门报告的。

**Article 92** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its inspection and testing qualification shall be revoked:  
(1) employing persons who have not passed the examination organized by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department and obtained the inspection and testing personnel certificate to engage in relevant inspection and testing work; or  
(2) failing to timely notify the special equipment user and immediately report to the special equipment safety supervision and administration department when finding serious potential accident hazards or serious energy consumption exceeding the standard during the inspection and testing of special equipment.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员，出具虚假的检验检测结果、鉴定结论或者检验检测结果、鉴定结论严重失实的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门对检验检测机构没收违法所得，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，情节严重的，撤销其检验检测资格；对检验检测人员处5000元以上5万元以下罚款，情节严重的，撤销其检验检测资格，触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于中介组织人员提供虚假证明文件罪、中介组织人员出具证明文件重大失实罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。  
特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员，出具虚假的检验检测结果、鉴定结论或者检验检测结果、鉴定结论严重失实，造成损害的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 93** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution or inspection and testing personnel issues false inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions, or the inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions are seriously untrue, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall confiscate the illegal gains of the inspection and testing institution, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and if the circumstances are serious, revoke its inspection and testing qualification; the inspection and testing personnel shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and if the circumstances are serious, his or her inspection and testing qualification shall be revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of providing false certification documents by personnel of intermediary organizations, the crime of issuing grossly untrue certification documents by personnel of intermediary organizations, or other crimes.  
Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution or inspection and testing personnel issues false inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions, or the inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions are seriously untrue, causing damage, it/he/she shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　特种设备检验检测机构或者检验检测人员从事特种设备的生产、销售，或者以其名义推荐或者监制、监销特种设备的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门撤销特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员的资格，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 94** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution or inspection and testing personnel engages in the production or sale of special equipment, or recommends, supervises the production or supervision of sales of special equipment in its/his/her name, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall revoke the qualification of the special equipment inspection and testing institution and the inspection and testing personnel, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　特种设备检验检测机构和检验检测人员利用检验检测工作故意刁难特种设备生产、使用单位，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，撤销其检验检测资格。

**Article 95** Where a special equipment inspection and testing institution or inspection and testing personnel deliberately makes difficulties for special equipment production or use units by taking advantage of inspection and testing work, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it/him/her to make corrections; if it/he/she refuses to make corrections, its/his/her inspection and testing qualification shall be revoked.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　检验检测人员，从事检验检测工作，不在特种设备检验检测机构执业或者同时在两个以上检验检测机构中执业的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，情节严重的，给予停止执业6个月以上2年以下的处罚；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 96** Where an inspection and testing personnel engages in inspection and testing work but does not practice in a special equipment inspection and testing institution, or practices in two or more inspection and testing institutions at the same time, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order him/her to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, a punishment of suspension from practice for not less than six months but not more than two years shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　特种设备安全监督管理部门及其特种设备安全监察人员，有下列违法行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级或者撤职的处分；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不按照本条例规定的条件和安全技术规范要求，实施许可、核准、登记的；  
(二)发现未经许可、核准、登记擅自从事特种设备的生产、使用或者检验检测活动不予取缔或者不依法予以处理的；  
(三)发现特种设备生产、使用单位不再具备本条例规定的条件而不撤销其原许可，或者发现特种设备生产、使用违法行为不予查处的；  
(四)发现特种设备检验检测机构不再具备本条例规定的条件而不撤销其原核准，或者对其出具虚假的检验检测结果、鉴定结论或者检验检测结果、鉴定结论严重失实的行为不予查处的；  
(五)对依照本条例规定在其他地方取得许可的特种设备生产单位重复进行许可，或者对依照本条例规定在其他地方检验检测合格的特种设备，重复进行检验检测的；  
(六)发现有违反本条例和安全技术规范的行为或者在用的特种设备存在严重事故隐患，不立即处理的；  
(七)发现重大的违法行为或者严重事故隐患，未及时向上级特种设备安全监督管理部门报告，或者接到报告的特种设备安全监督管理部门不立即处理的；  
(八)迟报、漏报、瞒报或者谎报事故的；  
(九)妨碍事故救援或者事故调查处理的。

**Article 97** Where the special equipment safety supervision and administration department or its special equipment safety supervisors commit any of the following illegal acts, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions of demotion or removal from office according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes:  
(1) failing to grant licenses, approvals, or registrations in accordance with the conditions specified in these Regulations and the requirements of the safety technical specifications;  
(2) failing to ban or handle according to law activities of production, use, inspection or testing of special equipment engaged in without authorization without a license, approval or registration;  
(3) failing to revoke the original license when discovering that a special equipment production or use unit no longer meets the conditions specified in these Regulations, or failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts in the production or use of special equipment;  
(4) failing to revoke the original approval when discovering that a special equipment inspection and testing institution no longer meets the conditions specified in these Regulations, or failing to investigate and deal with its acts of issuing false inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions, or acts where the inspection and testing results or appraisal conclusions are seriously untrue;  
(5) granting a duplicate license to a special equipment production unit that has obtained a license in another place in accordance with these Regulations, or conducting repeated inspection and testing on special equipment that has passed inspection and testing in another place in accordance with these Regulations;  
(6) failing to immediately handle any act that violates these Regulations or the safety technical specifications, or any serious potential accident hazard existing in special equipment in use;  
(7) failing to timely report to the superior special equipment safety supervision and administration department upon discovering major illegal acts or serious accident hazards, or the special equipment safety supervision and administration department that receives the report fails to handle it immediately;  
(8) delaying reporting, omitting reporting, concealing or falsely reporting accidents; or  
(9) obstructing accident rescue or accident investigation and handling.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　特种设备的生产、使用单位或者检验检测机构，拒不接受特种设备安全监督管理部门依法实施的安全监察的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于妨害公务罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。  
特种设备生产、使用单位擅自动用、调换、转移、损毁被查封、扣押的特种设备或者其主要部件的，由特种设备安全监督管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销其相应资格。

**Article 98** Where a special equipment production or use unit, or an inspection and testing institution, refuses to accept the safety supervision carried out by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department according to law, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business for rectification, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of obstructing official business or other crimes.  
If a special equipment production or use unit, without authorization, uses, exchanges, transfers, or damages the sealed or seized special equipment or its main components, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its relevant qualification shall be revoked.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
(一)锅炉，是指利用各种燃料、电或者其他能源，将所盛装的液体加热到一定的参数，并对外输出热能的设备，其范围规定为容积大于或者等于30L的承压蒸汽锅炉；出口水压大于或者等于0.1MPa(表压)，且额定功率大于或者等于0.1MW的承压热水锅炉；有机热载体锅炉。  
(二)压力容器，是指盛装气体或者液体，承载一定压力的密闭设备，其范围规定为最高工作压力大于或者等于0.1MPa(表压)，且压力与容积的乘积大于或者等于2.5MPa·L的气体、液化气体和最高工作温度高于或者等于标准沸点的液体的固定式容器和移动式容器；盛装公称工作压力大于或者等于0.2MPa(表压)，且压力与容积的乘积大于或者等于1.0MPa·L的气体、液化气体和标准沸点等于或者低于60℃液体的气瓶；氧舱等。  
(三)压力管道，是指利用一定的压力，用于输送气体或者液体的管状设备，其范围规定为最高工作压力大于或者等于0.1MPa(表压)的气体、液化气体、蒸汽介质或者可燃、易爆、有毒、有腐蚀性、最高工作温度高于或者等于标准沸点的液体介质，且公称直径大于25mm的管道。  
(四)电梯，是指动力驱动，利用沿刚性导轨运行的箱体或者沿固定线路运行的梯级(踏步)，进行升降或者平行运送人、货物的机电设备，包括载人(货)电梯、自动扶梯、自动人行道等。  
(五)起重机械，是指用于垂直升降或者垂直升降并水平移动重物的机电设备，其范围规定为额定起重量大于或者等于0.5t的升降机；额定起重量大于或者等于1t，且提升高度大于或者等于2m的起重机和承重形式固定的电动葫芦等。  
(六)客运索道，是指动力驱动，利用柔性绳索牵引箱体等运载工具运送人员的机电设备，包括客运架空索道、客运缆车、客运拖牵索道等。  
(七)大型游乐设施，是指用于经营目的，承载乘客游乐的设施，其范围规定为设计最大运行线速度大于或者等于2m/s，或者运行高度距地面高于或者等于2m的载人大型游乐设施。  
(八)场(厂)内专用机动车辆，是指除道路交通、农用车辆以外仅在工厂厂区、旅游景区、游乐场所等特定区域使用的专用机动车辆。  
特种设备包括其所用的材料、附属的安全附件、安全保护装置和与安全保护装置相关的设施。

**Article 99** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings specified below:  
(1) "Boiler" means equipment that uses various fuels, electricity or other energy sources to heat the contained liquid to certain parameters and output heat energy externally, and the scope is defined as: pressure steam boilers with a volume of not less than 30L; pressure hot water boilers with an outlet water pressure of not less than 0.1MPa (gauge pressure) and a rated power of not less than 0.1MW; and organic heat carrier boilers.  
(2) "Pressure vessel" means a closed device that contains gas or liquid and bears a certain pressure, and the scope is defined as: fixed and mobile containers for gases, liquefied gases and liquids with a maximum working temperature not lower than the standard boiling point, where the maximum working pressure is not less than 0.1MPa (gauge pressure) and the product of pressure and volume is not less than 2.5MPa·L; gas cylinders containing gases, liquefied gases and liquids with a standard boiling point not higher than 60°C, where the nominal working pressure is not less than 0.2MPa (gauge pressure) and the product of pressure and volume is not less than 1.0MPa·L; oxygen chambers, etc.  
(3) "Pressure pipeline" means a tubular device that uses a certain pressure to transport gas or liquid, and the scope is defined as pipelines with a nominal diameter greater than 25mm that transport gases, liquefied gases, steam media, or combustible, explosive, toxic, corrosive liquids or liquids with a maximum working temperature not lower than the standard boiling point, where the maximum working pressure is not less than 0.1MPa (gauge pressure).  
(4) "Elevator" means a power-driven electromechanical device that uses a car running along rigid guide rails or steps (treads) running along a fixed route to lift or transport people or goods horizontally, including passenger (freight) elevators, escalators, moving walkways, etc.  
(5) "Hoisting machinery" means electromechanical equipment used for vertical lifting or vertical lifting and horizontal movement of heavy objects, and the scope is defined as: lifts with a rated lifting capacity of not less than 0.5t; cranes with a rated lifting capacity of not less than 1t and a lifting height of not less than 2m; and electric hoists with a fixed load-bearing form, etc.  
(6) "Passenger ropeway" means a power-driven electromechanical device that uses flexible ropes to tow cars or other carriers to transport people, including passenger aerial ropeways, passenger cable cars, passenger towing ropeways, etc.  
(7) "Large-scale amusement ride" means a facility used for business purposes that carries passengers for amusement, and the scope is defined as large-scale amusement rides with a design maximum operating linear speed of not less than 2m/s, or an operating height from the ground of not less than 2m.  
(8) "Special-purpose motor vehicle within factory (premises)" means a special motor vehicle used only in specific areas such as factory premises, tourist attractions, amusement parks, etc., excluding road traffic vehicles and agricultural vehicles.  
Special equipment includes the materials used, ancillary safety accessories, safety protection devices and facilities related to safety protection devices.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　压力管道设计、安装、使用的安全监督管理办法由国务院另行制定。

**Article 100** The administrative measures for safety supervision of the design, installation and use of pressure pipelines shall be separately formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门可以授权省、自治区、直辖市特种设备安全监督管理部门负责本条例规定的特种设备行政许可工作，具体办法由国务院特种设备安全监督管理部门制定。

**Article 101** The special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council may authorize the special equipment safety supervision and administration departments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government to be responsible for the administrative license work for special equipment as prescribed in these Regulations, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the special equipment safety supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　特种设备行政许可、检验检测，应当按照国家有关规定收取费用。

**Article 102** Fees shall be charged for administrative license and inspection and testing of special equipment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　本条例自2003年6月1日起施行。1982年2月6日国务院发布的《锅炉压力容器安全监察暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 103** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2003. The Provisional Regulations on Safety Supervision of Boilers and Pressure Vessels promulgated by the State Council on February 6, 1982 shall be abolished simultaneously.
