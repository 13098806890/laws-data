# 外国民用航空器飞行管理规则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （国务院批准　1979年2月23日中国民用航空总局发布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行或者停留时，必须遵守本规则。 第二条　外国民用航空器只有根据中华人民共和国政府同该国政府签订的航空运输协定或者其他有关文件，或者通过外交途径向中华人民共和国政府申请，在得到答复接受后，才准飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行。 第三条　外国民用航空器及其空勤组成员和乘客，在中华人民共和国境内飞行或者停留时，必须遵守中华人民共和国的法律和有关入境、出境、过境的法令规章。 第四条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行，必须服从国务院民用航空主管部门各有关的空中交通管制部门的管制，并且遵守有关飞行的各项规章。 第五条　外国民用航空器根据中华人民共和国政府同该国政府签订的航空运输协定，可以在中华人民共和国境内按照协定中规定的航线进行定期航班飞行和加班飞行。 定期航班飞行，应当按照班期时刻表进行。班期时刻表必须由同中华人民共和国政府签订协定的对方政府指定的航空运输企业，预先提交国务院民用航空主管部门，并且征得同意。 加班飞行，由同中华人民共和国政府签订协定的对方政府指定的航空运输企业，最迟要在预计飞行开始前五天或者按照协定所规定的时间，向国务院民用航空主管部门提出，获得许可后，才能进行。 第六条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内进行定期航班飞行和加班飞行以外的一切不定期飞行，必须预先提出申请，在得到答复接受后，才能进行。 不定期飞行的申请，最迟要在预计飞行开始前十天通过外交途径提出。如果双边航空运输协定中另有规定的，依照规定。 第七条　不定期飞行的申请，应当包括下列内容： （一）航空器登记的国籍，航空器的所有人和经营人； （二）飞行的目的； （三）航空器的型别、最大起飞重量和最大着陆重量； （四）航空器的识别标志（包括国籍标志和登记标志）；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行或者停留时，必须遵守本规则。

**Article 1** Foreign civil aircraft, when flying into or out of the national borders of the People's Republic of China and when flying or staying within the territory of the People's Republic of China, must abide by these Rules.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外国民用航空器只有根据中华人民共和国政府同该国政府签订的航空运输协定或者其他有关文件，或者通过外交途径向中华人民共和国政府申请，在得到答复接受后，才准飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行。

**Article 2** Foreign civil aircraft shall be permitted to fly into or out of the national borders of the People's Republic of China and to fly within the territory of the People's Republic of China only on the basis of an air transport agreement or other relevant documents concluded between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the government of that country, or after applying to the Government of the People's Republic of China through diplomatic channels and obtaining an affirmative reply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外国民用航空器及其空勤组成员和乘客，在中华人民共和国境内飞行或者停留时，必须遵守中华人民共和国的法律和有关入境、出境、过境的法令规章。

**Article 3** Foreign civil aircraft, their crew members and passengers, when flying or staying within the territory of the People's Republic of China, must abide by the laws of the People's Republic of China and the relevant laws, decrees and regulations concerning entry, exit and transit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行，必须服从国务院民用航空主管部门各有关的空中交通管制部门的管制，并且遵守有关飞行的各项规章。

**Article 4** Foreign civil aircraft, when flying into or out of the national borders of the People's Republic of China and when flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China, must be subject to the control of the relevant air traffic control authorities under the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, and shall comply with all regulations concerning flight.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外国民用航空器根据中华人民共和国政府同该国政府签订的航空运输协定，可以在中华人民共和国境内按照协定中规定的航线进行定期航班飞行和加班飞行。  
定期航班飞行，应当按照班期时刻表进行。班期时刻表必须由同中华人民共和国政府签订协定的对方政府指定的航空运输企业，预先提交国务院民用航空主管部门，并且征得同意。  
加班飞行，由同中华人民共和国政府签订协定的对方政府指定的航空运输企业，最迟要在预计飞行开始前五天或者按照协定所规定的时间，向国务院民用航空主管部门提出，获得许可后，才能进行。

**Article 5** Foreign civil aircraft may, in accordance with the air transport agreement concluded between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the government of that country, conduct scheduled flights and additional flights within the territory of the People's Republic of China along the routes specified in the agreement.  
Scheduled flights shall be conducted in accordance with the timetable. The timetable must be submitted in advance by the air transport enterprise designated by the government of the other country that has concluded the agreement with the Government of the People's Republic of China to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council and obtain its consent.  
Additional flights shall be submitted by the air transport enterprise designated by the government of the other country that has concluded the agreement with the Government of the People's Republic of China to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council at the latest five days before the planned start of the flight or within the time limit specified in the agreement, and may only be conducted after obtaining permission.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内进行定期航班飞行和加班飞行以外的一切不定期飞行，必须预先提出申请，在得到答复接受后，才能进行。  
不定期飞行的申请，最迟要在预计飞行开始前十天通过外交途径提出。如果双边航空运输协定中另有规定的，依照规定。

**Article 6** All non-scheduled flights by foreign civil aircraft within the territory of the People's Republic of China other than scheduled flights and additional flights must be applied for in advance, and may only be conducted after obtaining an affirmative reply.  
Applications for non-scheduled flights shall be submitted through diplomatic channels at the latest ten days before the planned start of the flight. If otherwise provided in a bilateral air transport agreement, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　不定期飞行的申请，应当包括下列内容：  
（一）航空器登记的国籍，航空器的所有人和经营人；  
（二）飞行的目的；  
（三）航空器的型别、最大起飞重量和最大着陆重量；  
（四）航空器的识别标志（包括国籍标志和登记标志）；  
（五）航空器的无线电通话和通报的呼号；  
（六）航空器上无线电台使用的频率范围；  
（七）空勤组成员的姓名、职务和国籍，航空器上乘客的人数和货物的重量；  
（八）允许空勤组飞行的气象最低条件；  
（九）预计由起点机场至目的地机场的飞行航线、飞行日期和时刻，以及在中华人民共和国境内飞行的航路；  
（十）其他事项。

**Article 7** An application for a non-scheduled flight shall include the following:  
(1) Nationality of registration of the aircraft, owner and operator of the aircraft;  
(2) Purpose of the flight;  
(3) Type of aircraft, maximum take-off weight and maximum landing weight;  
(4) Identification marks of the aircraft (including nationality marks and registration marks);  
(5) Radiotelephony and radiotelegraphy call signs of the aircraft;  
(6) Frequency range used by the radio station on board the aircraft;  
(7) Names, positions and nationalities of crew members, number of passengers on board and weight of cargo;  
(8) Minimum meteorological conditions for the crew to fly;  
(9) Planned flight route from the departure airport to the destination airport, flight dates and times, and the airway within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(10) Other matters.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内进行不定期飞行时，由国务院民用航空主管部门指派飞行人员（包括领航员和无线电通信员）随机引导，如果许可中有特别规定的，依照规定。

**Article 8** When foreign civil aircraft conduct non-scheduled flights within the territory of the People's Republic of China, flight personnel (including navigators and radio operators) designated by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council shall accompany the aircraft for guidance; if the permit contains special provisions, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，必须具有国籍标志和登记标志。没有国籍标志和登记标志的外国民用航空器，禁止在中华人民共和国境内飞行。

**Article 9** Foreign civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China must bear nationality marks and registration marks. Foreign civil aircraft without nationality marks and registration marks are prohibited from flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，应当具有下列文件：  
（一）航空器登记证；  
（二）航空器适航证；  
（三）空勤组每一成员的专业执照或者证件；  
（四）航空器的航行记录簿；  
（五）航空器上无线电台使用许可证；  
（六）总申报单；  
（七）航空器如载运乘客，应当携带注明乘客姓名及其登机地与目的地的清单；  
（八）航空器如载运货物，应当携带货物仓单。

**Article 10** Foreign civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall carry the following documents:  
(1) Aircraft registration certificate;  
(2) Aircraft airworthiness certificate;  
(3) Professional license or certificate for each member of the crew;  
(4) Aircraft journey log book;  
(5) License for the use of the radio station on board the aircraft;  
(6) General declaration;  
(7) If the aircraft carries passengers, it shall carry a list specifying the names of passengers and their points of embarkation and destination;  
(8) If the aircraft carries cargo, it shall carry a cargo manifest.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界，必须从规定的空中走廊或者进出口通过。禁止偏离空中走廊或者进出口。

**Article 11** Foreign civil aircraft flying into or out of the national boundary of the People's Republic of China must pass through the prescribed air corridors or entry and exit points. Deviation from the air corridors or entry and exit points is prohibited.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界前二十至十五分钟，其空勤组必须向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门报告：航空器的呼号，预计飞入或者飞出国界的时间和飞行的高度，并且取得飞入或者飞出国界的许可。没有得到许可，不得飞入或者飞出国界。

**Article 12** For twenty to fifteen minutes before a foreign civil aircraft flies into or out of the national boundary of the People's Republic of China, its crew must report to the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council: the call sign of the aircraft, the estimated time of flying into or out of the national boundary, and the flight altitude, and obtain permission to fly into or out of the national boundary. Without such permission, the aircraft may not fly into or out of the national boundary.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国民用航空器飞越中华人民共和国国界和中华人民共和国境内规定的位置报告点，应当立即向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门作位置报告。位置报告的内容：  
（一）航空器呼号；  
（二）位置；  
（三）时间；  
（四）飞行高度或者飞行高度层；  
（五）预计飞越下一位置的时间或者预计到达降落机场的时间；  
（六）空中交通管制部门要求的或者空勤组认为需要报告的其他事项。

**Article 13** When a foreign civil aircraft flies over the national boundary of the People's Republic of China or the prescribed position reporting points within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall immediately make a position report to the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council. The contents of the position report shall be:  
(1) The call sign of the aircraft;  
(2) The position;  
(3) The time;  
(4) The flight altitude or flight level;  
(5) The estimated time of passing over the next position or the estimated time of arrival at the landing aerodrome;  
(6) Other matters required by the air traffic control department or deemed necessary to be reported by the crew.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国民用航空器飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界后，如果因为天气变坏、航空器发生故障或者其他特殊原因不能继续飞行，允许其从原航路及空中走廊或者进出口返航。此时，空勤组应当向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门报告：航空器呼号，被迫返航的原因，开始返航的时间，飞行的高度，以及返航后预定降落的机场。在中华人民共和国境内，如果没有接到国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的指示，通常应当在原高度层的下一反航向的高度层上返航；如果该高度层低于飞行的安全高度，则应当在原高度层的上一反航向的高度层上返航。

**Article 14** After a foreign civil aircraft flies into or out of the national boundary of the People's Republic of China, if it is unable to continue the flight due to weather deterioration, aircraft malfunction, or other special reasons, it is permitted to return along its original route and air corridor or entry and exit point. At that time, the crew shall report to the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council: the call sign of the aircraft, the reason for the forced return, the time of commencing the return, the flight altitude, and the aerodrome scheduled for landing after the return. Within the territory of the People's Republic of China, if no instructions are received from the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, the aircraft shall normally return at the opposite direction flight level of the next lower altitude layer below its original altitude layer; if that altitude layer is below the safe flight altitude, it shall return at the opposite direction flight level of the next higher altitude layer above its original altitude layer.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国民用航空器在没有同国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门沟通无线电联络以前，禁止飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行。

**Article 15** Before a foreign civil aircraft establishes radio communication with the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, it is prohibited to fly into or out of the national boundary of the People's Republic of China or to fly within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内飞行，如果与国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的航空电台通信联络中断时，其空勤组应当设法与其他航空电台或者空中其他航空器沟通联络，传递飞行情报。如果仍然无法恢复联络，则该航空器应当按照下列规定飞行：  
在目视气象条件下，应当继续保持在目视气象条件下飞行，飞往就近的机场（指起飞机场、预定的降落机场和国务院民用航空主管部门事先指定的备降机场）降落。降落时，应当按照本规则附件一《辅助指挥、联络的符号和信号》的规定进行。  
在仪表气象条件下或者在天气条件不允许在目视气象条件下飞往就近的机场降落时，应当严格按照现行飞行计划飞往预定的降落机场的导航台上空；根据现行飞行计划中预计到达时间开始下降，并且按照该导航设备的正常仪表进近程序，在预计到达时间之后三十分钟以内着陆。  
失去通信联络的航空器，如果无线电发信机工作正常，应当盲目发送机长对于继续飞行的意图和飞行情况，随后，在预定时刻或者位置报告点盲目发送报告；如果无线电收信机工作正常，应当不间断地守听地面航空电台有关飞行的指示。

**Article 16** When a foreign civil aircraft is flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China and the communication link with the aeronautical radio station of the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council is interrupted, its crew shall attempt to establish communication with other aeronautical radio stations or other aircraft in the air to transmit flight information. If the communication still cannot be restored, the aircraft shall fly in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Under visual meteorological conditions, it shall continue to maintain visual meteorological conditions and fly to the nearest aerodrome (referring to the departure aerodrome, the scheduled landing aerodrome, or the alternate aerodrome designated in advance by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council) for landing. When landing, the provisions of Appendix I "Symbols and Signals for Auxiliary Command and Communication" to these Rules shall be followed.  
(2) Under instrument meteorological conditions or when weather conditions do not permit flight under visual meteorological conditions to the nearest aerodrome for landing, the aircraft shall strictly follow the current flight plan to fly to the airspace above the navigation aid of the scheduled landing aerodrome; it shall commence descent based on the estimated time of arrival in the current flight plan and land within thirty minutes after the estimated time of arrival in accordance with the normal instrument approach procedures for that navigation aid.  
(3) If the radio transmitter of the aircraft that has lost communication is working properly, it shall blindly transmit the pilot's intention for continuing the flight and flight conditions, and subsequently transmit a blind report at the scheduled time or at the position reporting point; if the radio receiver is working properly, it shall continuously listen to the flight instructions from the ground aeronautical station.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中华人民共和国境内航空器飞行的目视气象条件：能见度不少于八公里，航空器距离云的垂直距离不少于三百米，航空器距离云的水平距离不少于一千五百米。

**Article 17** The visual meteorological conditions for aircraft flight within the territory of the People's Republic of China: visibility not less than eight kilometers, vertical distance from the aircraft to clouds not less than three hundred meters, and horizontal distance from the aircraft to clouds not less than one thousand five hundred meters.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　飞行的安全高度是保证航空器不致与地面障碍物相撞的最低的飞行高度。  
中华人民共和国境内航线飞行的安全高度，在高原、山岳地带应当高出航线两侧各二十五公里以内最高标高六百米；在高原、山岳以外的其他地带应当高出航线两侧各二十五公里以内最高标高四百米。

**Article 18** The safe altitude for flight is the minimum flight altitude that ensures the aircraft does not collide with ground obstacles.  
For flight on air routes within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the safe altitude in plateau and mountainous areas shall be six hundred meters above the highest elevation within twenty-five kilometers on each side of the air route; in areas other than plateaus and mountains, it shall be four hundred meters above the highest elevation within twenty-five kilometers on each side of the air route.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内飞行，必须在规定的飞行高度或者高度层上进行。  
中华人民共和国境内飞行的高度层，按照下列办法划分：真航线角在0度至179度范围内，高度由600米至6000米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在6000米以上，每隔2000米为一个高度层。真航线角在180度至359度范围内，高度由900米至5700米，每隔600米为一个高度层；高度在7000米以上，每隔2000米为一个高度层。  
飞行高度层应当根据特定气压七百六十毫米水银柱为基准的等压面计算。真航线角应当从航线起点和转弯点量取。

**Article 19** When foreign civil aircraft fly within the territory of the People's Republic of China, they must operate at the prescribed flight altitudes or flight levels.  
The flight levels within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be divided according to the following method: within the range of true track angle from 0 degrees to 179 degrees, from 600 meters to 6,000 meters, every 600 meters constitutes one flight level; for altitudes above 6,000 meters, every 2,000 meters constitutes one flight level. Within the range of true track angle from 180 degrees to 359 degrees, from 900 meters to 5,700 meters, every 600 meters constitutes one flight level; for altitudes above 7,000 meters, every 2,000 meters constitutes one flight level.  
Flight levels shall be calculated based on the isobaric surface using a standard pressure of 760 millimeters of mercury. The true track angle shall be measured from the starting point and turning point of the air route.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内每次飞行的高度或者高度层，由国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门指定。  
外国民用航空器在飞行中，无论气象条件如何，如果需要改变飞行高度或者高度层，必须经过国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的许可。

**Article 20** The flight altitude or flight level for each flight of a foreign civil aircraft within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be designated by the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
If a foreign civil aircraft needs to change its flight altitude or flight level during flight, regardless of weather conditions, it must obtain permission from the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内必须沿规定的航路飞行。禁止偏离航路。  
中华人民共和国境内航路的宽度最大为二十公里，最小为八公里。

**Article 21** A foreign civil aircraft shall fly along the prescribed air route within the territory of the People's Republic of China. Deviation from the air route is prohibited.  
The width of the air route within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be a maximum of twenty kilometers and a minimum of eight kilometers.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，其空勤组如果不能判定航空器的位置时，应当立即报告国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门。  
外国民用航空器在飞行中如果偏离规定的航路，国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门，在可能范围内帮助其回到原航路，但对该航空器由于偏离航路飞行所产生的一切后果，不负任何责任。

**Article 22** If the crew of a foreign civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China cannot determine the position of the aircraft, they shall immediately report to the relevant air traffic control authority of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
If a foreign civil aircraft deviates from the prescribed air route during flight, the relevant air traffic control authority of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within the possible scope, assist it in returning to the original air route, but shall not assume any responsibility for all consequences arising from the flight deviation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　目视飞行时航空器相遇，应当按照下列规定避让：  
（一）两航空器在同一个高度上对头相遇，应当各自向右避让，相互间保持五百米以上的间隔；  
（二）两航空器在同一个高度上交叉相遇，飞行员从坐舱左侧看到另一架航空器时应当下降高度，从坐舱右侧看到另一架航空器时应当上升高度；  
（三）在同一个高度上超越前面航空器，应当从前面航空器右侧保持五百米以上的间隔进行；  
（四）单独航空器应当主动避让编队或者拖曳物体的航空器，有动力装置的航空器应当主动避让无动力装置的航空器。

**Article 23** When aircraft meet in visual flight, they shall give way in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) When two aircraft meet head-on at the same altitude, they shall each turn to the right and maintain a separation of not less than 500 meters from each other;  
(2) When two aircraft cross at the same altitude, if a pilot sees another aircraft from the left side of the cockpit, he shall descend; if he sees another aircraft from the right side of the cockpit, he shall ascend;  
(3) When overtaking an aircraft ahead at the same altitude, it shall be carried out from the right side of the aircraft ahead with a separation of not less than 500 meters;  
(4) A single aircraft shall actively give way to an aircraft in formation or towing an object, and an aircraft with power plant shall actively give way to an aircraft without power plant.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内飞行时，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门规定的无线电通信的方式和无线电频率，同国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门保持不间断地守听，以便及时地进行通信联络。  
进行地空无线电联络，应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）通报时使用国际Q简语；通话时使用汉语，或者使用中华人民共和国政府同意的其他语言；  
（二）地理名称使用汉语现用名称或者用地名代码、地名代号、无线电导航设备识别讯号和经纬度表示；  
（三）计量单位：距离以米或者公里计；飞行高度、标高、离地高度以米计；水平速度、空中风速以公里/小时计；垂直速度、地面风速以米/秒计；风向以度计（真向）；能见度以公里或者米计；高度表拨正以毫米水银柱或者毫巴计；温度以度计（摄氏）；重量以吨或者公斤计；时间以小时和分钟计（格林威治平时二十四小时制，自子夜开始）。

**Article 24** When a foreign civil aircraft flies within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall maintain uninterrupted listening watch with the relevant air traffic control authority of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council in the modes and on the radio frequencies prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, so as to maintain timely communication.  
For ground-to-air radio communication, the following provisions shall be complied with:  
(1) Use the international Q code for reporting; use the Chinese language or other languages approved by the Government of the People's Republic of China for conversation;  
(2) Use the current Chinese names of geographical names, or express them by geographical name codes, geographical name designations, identification signals of radio navigation aids, and latitude and longitude;  
(3) Units of measurement: distance in meters or kilometers; flight altitude, elevation, and height above ground in meters; horizontal speed and airspeed in kilometers per hour; vertical speed and ground wind speed in meters per second; wind direction in degrees (true); visibility in kilometers or meters; altimeter setting in millimeters of mercury or millibars; temperature in degrees Celsius; weight in tons or kilograms; time in hours and minutes (Greenwich Mean Time, 24-hour system, starting from midnight).

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，应当在国务院民用航空主管部门指定的机场降落。降落前应当取得降落机场的空中交通管制部门的许可；降落后，没有经过许可，不得起飞。  
不定期飞行的外国民用航空器降落后，其机长还应当到机场空中交通管制部门报告在中华人民共和国境内的飞行情况，并且提交有关下一次飞行的申请。

**Article 25** A foreign civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall land at an airport designated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council. Before landing, it shall obtain permission from the air traffic control authority at the landing airport; after landing, without permission, it shall not take off.  
After an a foreign civil aircraft on non-scheduled flight lands, its pilot-in-command shall also report to the airport air traffic control authority on its flight within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and submit an application concerning the next flight.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　外国民用航空器的空勤组必须在起飞前做好飞行准备工作，机长或其代理人至少要在预计起飞前一小时向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门提交飞行计划。  
如果航空器延误超过规定起飞时间三十分钟以上时，应当修订该飞行计划，或者另行提交新的飞行计划，并且撤销原来的飞行计划。

**Article 26** The crew of a foreign civil aircraft must complete the flight preparation before takeoff, and the pilot-in-command or his agent shall submit a flight plan to the relevant air traffic control authority of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council at least one hour before the scheduled departure time.  
If the aircraft is delayed for more than thirty minutes beyond the prescribed departure time, it shall amend the flight plan, or submit a new flight plan separately, and cancel the original flight plan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中华人民共和国境内机场的起落航线飞行通常为左航线。起落航线的飞行高度，通常为三百米至五百米。进行起落航线飞行时，禁止超越同型或者速度相接近的航空器。航空器之间的纵向间隔，一般应当保持在二千米以上，并且还要考虑航空器尾流的影响。经过机场空中交通管制员许可，大速度航空器可以在第三转弯前从外侧超越小速度航空器，其横向间隔不得小于五百米。除被迫必须立即降落的航空器外，任何航空器不得从内侧超越前面的航空器。  
加入起落航线飞行必须经过机场空中交通管制员许可，并且应当顺沿航线加入，不得横向截入。

**Article 27** The traffic pattern flight at airports within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall normally be a left-hand pattern. The altitude for traffic pattern flight shall normally be 300 to 500 meters. During traffic pattern flight, overtaking aircraft of the same type or with similar speed is prohibited. The longitudinal separation between aircraft shall generally be maintained at not less than 2,000 meters, and the effect of wake turbulence shall also be considered. With permission from the airport air traffic controller, a high-speed aircraft may overtake a low-speed aircraft from the outside before the third turn, with a lateral separation of not less than 500 meters. Except for an aircraft that must land immediately due to force majeure, no aircraft shall overtake the aircraft ahead from the inside.  
Joining the traffic pattern flight must be with the permission of the airport air traffic controller, and shall be done by following the pattern, not by cutting in laterally.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　外国民用航空器在航空站区域内目视气象条件下飞行时，其空勤组应当进行严密的空中观察，防止与其他航空器碰撞；如果发生碰撞，航空器的机长应负直接责任。

**Article 28** When a foreign civil aircraft flies within the aerodrome area under visual meteorological conditions, its crew shall maintain vigilant observation to prevent collision with other aircraft; if a collision occurs, the pilot-in-command of the aircraft shall bear direct responsibility.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内的机场起飞或者降落，高度表拨正程序按照下列规定进行：  
（一）规定过渡高度和过渡高度层的机场  
航空器起飞前，应当将机场场面气压的数值对正航空器上气压高度表的固定指标；航空器起飞后，上升到过渡高度时，应当将航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度七百六十毫米对正固定指标。航空器降落前，下降到过渡高度层时，应当将机场场面气压的数值对正航空器上气压高度表的固定指标。  
（二）没有规定过渡高度和过渡高度层的机场  
航空器起飞前，应当将机场场面气压的数值对正航空器上气压高度表的固定指标；航空器起飞后，上升到六百米高度时，应当将航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度七百六十毫米对正固定指标。航空器降落前，进入航空站区域边界或者根据机场空中交通管制员的指示，将机场场面气压的数值对正航空器上气压高度表的固定指标。  
（三）高原机场  
航空器起飞前，当航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压的数值时，应当将气压高度表的气压刻度七百六十毫米对正固定指标（此时所指示的高度为假定零点高度）。航空器降落前，如果航空器上气压高度表的气压刻度不能调整到机场场面气压的数值时，应当按照降落机场空中交通管制员通知的假定零点高度（航空器着陆时所指示的高度）进行着陆。

**Article 29** When a foreign civil aircraft takes off or lands at an airport within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the altimeter setting procedure shall be carried out in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Airports with prescribed transition altitude and transition level  
Before takeoff, the crew shall set the airport QNH value to the altimeter's sub-scale reference index; after takeoff, when climbing to the transition altitude, they shall set the altimeter's pressure scale to 760 mm Hg. Before landing, when descending to the transition level, they shall set the airport QNH value to the altimeter's sub-scale reference index.  
(2) Airports without prescribed transition altitude and transition level  
Before takeoff, the crew shall set the airport QNH value to the altimeter's sub-scale reference index; after takeoff, when climbing to 600 meters altitude, they shall set the altimeter's pressure scale to 760 mm Hg. Before landing, when entering the aerodrome area boundary or as instructed by the airport air traffic controller, set the airport QNH value to the altimeter's sub-scale reference index.  
(3) High-altitude airports  
Before takeoff, when the altimeter's pressure scale cannot be adjusted to the airport QNH value, the crew shall set the altimeter's pressure scale to 760 mm Hg (the indicated altitude at this time is the assumed zero level). Before landing, if the altimeter's pressure scale cannot be adjusted to the airport QNH value, the crew shall land in accordance with the assumed zero level (the altitude indicated when the aircraft is to land) notified by the landing airport air traffic controller.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内起飞或者降落时，应当遵守国务院民用航空主管部门规定的机场气象最低条件。当机场的天气实况低于机场气象最低条件时，航空器不得起飞或者着陆。在紧急情况下，如果航空器的机长决定低于机场气象最低条件着陆，须对其决定和由此产生的后果负完全的责任。  
当机场天气实况十分恶劣，机场空中交通管制部门将关闭机场，禁止航空器起飞或者着陆。

**Article 30** When a foreign civil aircraft takes off or lands within the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall comply with the airport meteorological minimums prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council. When the actual weather conditions at the airport are below the airport meteorological minimums, the aircraft may not take off or land. In an emergency, if the pilot-in-command decides to land below the airport meteorological minimums, he must bear full responsibility for his decision and the consequences arising therefrom.  
When the actual weather conditions at the airport are extremely poor, the airport air traffic control authority shall close the airport and prohibit aircraft from taking off or landing.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在中华人民共和国境内的航路上或者起飞、降落机场附近有威胁航空器飞行的危险天气时，国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门可以向外国民用航空器的机长提出推迟起飞、返航或者飞往备降机场的建议；航空器的机长对此类建议有最后的决定权并对其决定负责。

**Article 31** When dangerous weather threatening aircraft flight exists on the air route within the People's Republic of China or near the takeoff or landing airport, the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council may propose to the captain of a foreign civil aircraft to postpone takeoff, return to the origin, or fly to an alternate airport; the captain of the aircraft shall have the final decision power on such proposals and shall be responsible for his decision.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，如果发现可能危及飞行安全的严重故障时，国务院民用航空主管部门的有关部门有权制止该航空器继续飞行，并且通知其登记国；该航空器可否继续飞行，由航空器登记国确定。

**Article 32** If a foreign civil aircraft flying within the People's Republic of China is found to have a serious malfunction that may endanger flight safety, the relevant department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall have the right to prohibit the aircraft from continuing to fly and shall notify its state of registry; whether the aircraft may continue to fly shall be determined by the state of registry.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内飞行时，无论在任何情况下，均不准飞入中华人民共和国划定的空中禁区。国务院民用航空主管部门对飞入空中禁区的外国民用航空器的机长，将给予严肃处理，并且对该航空器飞入空中禁区所产生的一切后果，不负任何责任。

**Article 33** When a foreign civil aircraft is flying within the People's Republic of China, under no circumstances shall it be allowed to fly into the prohibited airspace designated by the People's Republic of China. The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall deal seriously with the captain of a foreign civil aircraft that flies into such prohibited airspace and shall not be liable for any consequences arising from the aircraft flying into the prohibited airspace.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在特殊情况下，国务院民用航空主管部门公布临时关闭有关的航路或者机场时，与该航路或者机场飞行有关的外国民用航空器，必须根据国务院民用航空主管部门的航行通告或者有关的空中交通管制部门的通知，修订飞行计划。

**Article 34** Under special circumstances, when the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council announces the temporary closure of relevant air routes or airports, foreign civil aircraft related to flights on such air routes or at such airports must revise their flight plans in accordance with the navigation notice from the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council or the notice from the relevant air traffic control department.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，除遇险情况下的跳伞外，只有得到国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的许可，并且在指定的条件下，才可以向地面投掷物品、喷洒液体和使用降落伞。

**Article 35** Foreign civil aircraft flying within the People's Republic of China, except for parachuting in case of distress, may only jettison objects, spray liquids, or use parachutes toward the ground with the permission of the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council and under the specified conditions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，如果发生严重危及航空器和机上人员安全，并且需要立即援助的情况时，其空勤组应当立即向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门发出遇险信号，以便及时进行搜寻和援救。遇险信号以无线电话发出时用“MAYDAY”，以无线电报发出时用“SOS”。遇险航空器在发出遇险信号后，应当尽可能将航空器呼号，遇险性质，现在的位置、高度、航向和机长的意图在遇险通信中发出。遇险通信应当在当时使用的地空无线电通信频率上发出；必要时，按照国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的通知，将通信频率转到紧急频率上继续进行联络。这种紧急频率在航行资料汇编中提供。

**Article 36** If a foreign civil aircraft flying within the People's Republic of China encounters a situation that seriously endangers the safety of the aircraft and persons on board and requires immediate assistance, its flight crew shall immediately send a distress signal to the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council so that search and rescue may be carried out in a timely manner. The distress signal shall be "MAYDAY" when sent by radiotelephone and "SOS" when sent by radiotelegraph. After sending the distress signal, the aircraft in distress shall, as far as possible, include the aircraft call sign, nature of distress, current position, altitude, heading, and the intention of the captain in the distress communication. The distress communication shall be made on the air-ground radio communication frequency in use at the time; when necessary, in accordance with the notice from the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, the communication frequency shall be switched to the emergency frequency to continue communication. Such emergency frequency shall be provided in the aeronautical information publication.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，如果发生可能危及航空器或者机上人员安全，但不需要立即援助的情况时，其空勤组应当立即向国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门发出紧急信号。紧急信号以无线电话发出时用“PAN”，以无线电报发出时用“XXX”。遇有紧急情况的航空器，在发出紧急信号后，还应当将航空器呼号，紧急情况的性质，现在的位置、高度、航向和机长的意图在紧急通信中发出。紧急通信应当在当地使用的地空无线电通信频率上发出；必要时，按照国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的通知，将通信频率转到紧急频率上继续进行联络。这种紧急频率在航行资料汇编中提供。

**Article 37** If a foreign civil aircraft flying within the People's Republic of China encounters a situation that may endanger the safety of the aircraft or persons on board but does not require immediate assistance, its flight crew shall immediately send an urgency signal to the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council. The urgency signal shall be "PAN" when sent by radiotelephone and "XXX" when sent by radiotelegraph. After sending the urgency signal, the aircraft in an urgency situation shall also include the aircraft call sign, nature of the urgency, current position, altitude, heading, and the intention of the captain in the urgency communication. The urgency communication shall be made on the air-ground radio communication frequency in use at the local area; when necessary, in accordance with the notice from the relevant air traffic control department of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, the communication frequency shall be switched to the emergency frequency to continue communication. Such emergency frequency shall be provided in the aeronautical information publication.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界的外国民用航空器，必须在指定的设有海关和边防检查站的机场降落或者起飞。

**Article 38** Foreign civil aircraft entering or leaving the national boundary of the People's Republic of China shall land at or take off from a designated airport with customs and border inspection stations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在中华人民共和国境内的外国民用航空器（包括其必须具备的文件以及空勤组成员、乘客和所载物品），应受中华人民共和国有关机关的检查。

**Article 39** Foreign civil aircraft within the People's Republic of China (including their required documents and the crew members, passengers, and cargo on board) shall be subject to inspection by the relevant authorities of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，禁止载运爆炸物、易燃物、武器、弹药以及中华人民共和国政府规定的其他违禁品。

**Article 40** Foreign civil aircraft flying within the People's Republic of China are prohibited from carrying explosives, flammable materials, weapons, ammunition, and other contraband articles specified by the government of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　外国民用航空器在飞行中，如果空勤组成员或者乘客患急病，空勤组应当报告有关的空中交通管制部门，以便在降落后取得协助为病员进行必要的医疗。

**Article 41** If any crew member or passenger on board a foreign civil aircraft in flight falls ill, the crew shall report to the relevant air traffic control department so that assistance may be obtained after landing to provide necessary medical treatment for the patient.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的外国民用航空器，如果违反本规则，中国人民解放军防空值班飞机可以强迫其在指定机场降落。违反国务院民用航空主管部门有关的空中交通管制部门的指示，以违反本规则论。  
防空值班飞机拦截违反本规则的外国民用航空器和被拦截的外国民用航空器使用的信号，按照附件二的规定执行。  
被强迫降落的外国民用航空器，只有得到国务院民用航空主管部门的许可，才能继续飞行。

**Article 42** If a foreign civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China violates these Rules, the air defense alert aircraft of the Chinese People's Liberation Army may compel it to land at a designated airport. Any violation of the instructions of the relevant air traffic control department under the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council shall be deemed a violation of these Rules.  
The signals used by air defense alert aircraft intercepting a foreign civil aircraft that violates these Rules and by the intercepted foreign civil aircraft shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Appendix II.  
A foreign civil aircraft that has been compelled to land may continue its flight only with the permission of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国国界和在中华人民共和国境内飞行或者停留的外国民用航空器，其空勤组成员和乘客，如果违反本规则，由国务院民用航空主管部门或者其他主管机关根据具体情况给予罚款及其他处分；情节重大的由中华人民共和国人民法院处理。

**Article 43** If any crew member or passenger on a foreign civil aircraft that flies into or out of the national boundary of the People's Republic of China or flies or stays within the territory of the People's Republic of China violates these Rules, the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council or another competent authority shall, depending on the specific circumstances, impose a fine or other sanctions; if the circumstances are serious, the case shall be handled by the People's Court of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本规则经中华人民共和国国务院批准后，由国务院民用航空主管部门发布施行。
