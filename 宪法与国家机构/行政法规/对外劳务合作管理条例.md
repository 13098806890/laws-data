# 对外劳务合作管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2012-06-04  
**生效日期**：2012-08-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范对外劳务合作，保障劳务人员的合法权益，促进对外劳务合作健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating overseas labor cooperation, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of labor service personnel, and promoting the healthy development of overseas labor cooperation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称对外劳务合作，是指组织劳务人员赴其他国家或者地区为国外的企业或者机构(以下统称国外雇主)工作的经营性活动。  
国外的企业、机构或者个人不得在中国境内招收劳务人员赴国外工作。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "overseas labor cooperation" means the business activity of organizing labor service personnel to work in other countries or regions for foreign enterprises or institutions (hereinafter collectively referred to as foreign employers).  
No foreign enterprise, institution or individual may recruit labor service personnel within the territory of China for work abroad.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励和支持依法开展对外劳务合作，提高对外劳务合作水平，维护劳务人员的合法权益。  
国务院有关部门制定和完善促进对外劳务合作发展的政策措施，建立健全对外劳务合作服务体系以及风险防范和处置机制。

**Article 3** The State encourages and supports overseas labor cooperation conducted according to law, improves the level of overseas labor cooperation, and protects the legitimate rights and interests of labor service personnel.  
The relevant departments under the State Council shall formulate and improve policies and measures to promote the development of overseas labor cooperation, and establish and improve the service system for overseas labor cooperation as well as the risk prevention and handling mechanisms.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院商务主管部门负责全国的对外劳务合作监督管理工作。国务院外交、公安、人力资源社会保障、交通运输、住房城乡建设、渔业、工商行政管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内，负责对外劳务合作监督管理的相关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府统一领导、组织、协调本行政区域的对外劳务合作监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府商务主管部门负责本行政区域的对外劳务合作监督管理工作，其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责对外劳务合作监督管理的相关工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation nationwide. The relevant departments under the State Council, including those for foreign affairs, public security, human resources and social security, transportation, housing and urban-rural development, fishery, and industry and commerce administration, shall be responsible for the relevant work of supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation within their respective functions and duties.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall uniformly lead, organize, and coordinate the supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation within their respective administrative regions. The competent department of commerce of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation within its administrative region, and other relevant departments shall be responsible for the relevant work of supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation within their respective functions and duties.

### 第二章　从事对外劳务合作的企业与劳务人员

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事对外劳务合作，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，经省级或者设区的市级人民政府商务主管部门批准，取得对外劳务合作经营资格。

**Article 5** Any entity engaged in overseas labor cooperation shall, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, obtain the qualification for overseas labor cooperation business upon approval by the competent department of commerce of the people's government at the provincial level or at the level of a city divided into districts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请对外劳务合作经营资格，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合企业法人条件；  
(二)实缴注册资本不低于600万元人民币；  
(三)有3名以上熟悉对外劳务合作业务的管理人员；  
(四)有健全的内部管理制度和突发事件应急处置制度；  
(五)法定代表人没有故意犯罪记录。

**Article 6** To apply for the qualification for overseas labor cooperation business, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) meeting the conditions for a legal person enterprise;  
(2) having paid-in registered capital of not less than six million yuan;  
(3) having three or more managerial personnel familiar with overseas labor cooperation business;  
(4) having a sound internal management system and an emergency response system for contingencies; and  
(5) the legal representative has no record of intentional crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请对外劳务合作经营资格的企业，应当向所在地省级或者设区的市级人民政府商务主管部门(以下称负责审批的商务主管部门)提交其符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定条件的证明材料。负责审批的商务主管部门应当自收到证明材料之日起20个工作日内进行审查，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发对外劳务合作经营资格证书；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
申请人持对外劳务合作经营资格证书，依法向工商行政管理部门办理登记。  
负责审批的商务主管部门应当将依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格证书并办理登记的企业(以下称对外劳务合作企业)名单报至国务院商务主管部门，国务院商务主管部门应当及时通报中国驻外使馆、领馆。  
未依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格证书并办理登记，不得从事对外劳务合作。

**Article 7** An enterprise applying for the qualification for overseas labor cooperation business shall submit to the competent department of commerce of the local people's government at the provincial level or at the level of a city divided into districts (hereinafter referred to as the competent department of commerce responsible for approval) the evidentiary materials proving that it meets the conditions provided for in Article 6 of these Regulations. The competent department of commerce responsible for approval shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the evidentiary materials, conduct an examination and make a decision on approval or disapproval. If approval is granted, a qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business shall be issued; if approval is not granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
The applicant shall, with the qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business, undergo registration with the industry and commerce administration department according to law.  
The competent department of commerce responsible for approval shall submit the list of enterprises that have lawfully obtained the qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business and undergone registration (hereinafter referred to as overseas labor cooperation enterprises) to the competent department of commerce under the State Council, and the competent department of commerce under the State Council shall promptly notify the Chinese embassies and consulates abroad.  
No entity shall engage in overseas labor cooperation without lawfully obtaining the qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business and undergoing registration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对外劳务合作企业不得允许其他单位或者个人以本企业的名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作。  
任何单位和个人不得以商务、旅游、留学等名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作。

**Article 8** No overseas labor cooperation enterprise may permit any other unit or individual to organize labor service personnel to work abroad in its name.  
No unit or individual may organize labor service personnel to work abroad in the name of business, tourism, study, etc.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对外劳务合作企业应当自工商行政管理部门登记之日起5个工作日内，在负责审批的商务主管部门指定的银行开设专门账户，缴存不低于300万元人民币的对外劳务合作风险处置备用金(以下简称备用金)。备用金也可以通过向负责审批的商务主管部门提交等额银行保函的方式缴存。  
负责审批的商务主管部门应当将缴存备用金的对外劳务合作企业名单向社会公布。

**Article 9** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall, within five working days from the date of registration with the industry and commerce administration department, open a special account at a bank designated by the competent department of commerce responsible for approval, and deposit a risk reserve for overseas labor cooperation (hereinafter referred to as the reserve) of not less than three million yuan. The reserve may also be deposited by submitting a bank guarantee of an equivalent amount to the competent department of commerce responsible for approval.  
The competent department of commerce responsible for approval shall publish the list of overseas labor cooperation enterprises that have deposited the reserve to the public.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　备用金用于支付对外劳务合作企业拒绝承担或者无力承担的下列费用：  
(一)对外劳务合作企业违反国家规定收取，应当退还给劳务人员的服务费；  
(二)依法或者按照约定应当由对外劳务合作企业向劳务人员支付的劳动报酬；  
(三)依法赔偿劳务人员的损失所需费用；  
(四)因发生突发事件，劳务人员回国或者接受紧急救助所需费用。  
备用金使用后，对外劳务合作企业应当自使用之日起20个工作日内将备用金补足到原有数额。  
备用金缴存、使用和监督管理的具体办法由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 10** The reserve shall be used to pay the following expenses where the overseas labor cooperation enterprise refuses to bear or is unable to bear:  
(1) service fees that have been charged by the overseas labor cooperation enterprise in violation of State regulations and shall be refunded to the labor service personnel;  
(2) labor remuneration that shall be paid by the overseas labor cooperation enterprise to the labor service personnel according to law or as agreed;  
(3) expenses necessary for compensating the losses of the labor service personnel according to law;  
(4) expenses necessary for the repatriation or emergency assistance of the labor service personnel due to the occurrence of emergencies.  
After the reserve is used, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall replenish the reserve to its original amount within 20 working days from the date of use.  
The specific measures for the deposit, use, and supervision and administration of the reserve shall be formulated by the competent department of commerce under the State Council jointly with the finance department under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对外劳务合作企业不得组织劳务人员赴国外从事与赌博、色情活动相关的工作。

**Article 11** No overseas labor cooperation enterprise may organize labor service personnel to work abroad in jobs related to gambling or pornography.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对外劳务合作企业应当安排劳务人员接受赴国外工作所需的职业技能、安全防范知识、外语以及用工项目所在国家或者地区相关法律、宗教信仰、风俗习惯等知识的培训；未安排劳务人员接受培训的，不得组织劳务人员赴国外工作。  
劳务人员应当接受培训，掌握赴国外工作所需的相关技能和知识，提高适应国外工作岗位要求以及安全防范的能力。

**Article 12** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall arrange for labor service personnel to receive training in vocational skills, safety prevention knowledge, foreign languages, and knowledge of the laws, religious beliefs, customs and habits of the country or region where the employment project is located, as needed for working abroad; if the labor service personnel have not been arranged to receive training, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise may not organize them to work abroad.  
The labor service personnel shall receive training, master the relevant skills and knowledge needed for working abroad, and improve their ability to adapt to the requirements of the overseas work post and to prevent safety risks.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对外劳务合作企业应当为劳务人员购买在国外工作期间的人身意外伤害保险。但是，对外劳务合作企业与国外雇主约定由国外雇主为劳务人员购买的除外。

**Article 13** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall purchase personal accident insurance for labor service personnel during their work abroad, unless the overseas labor cooperation enterprise has agreed with the foreign employer that the foreign employer shall purchase such insurance for the labor service personnel.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对外劳务合作企业应当为劳务人员办理出境手续，并协助办理劳务人员在国外的居留、工作许可等手续。  
对外劳务合作企业组织劳务人员出境后，应当及时将有关情况向中国驻用工项目所在国使馆、领馆报告。

**Article 14** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall handle the exit procedures for labor service personnel and assist in handling the residence, work permit and other procedures for the labor service personnel in the foreign country.  
After an overseas labor cooperation enterprise organizes labor service personnel to go abroad, it shall promptly report the relevant information to the Chinese embassy or consulate in the country where the employment project is located.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对外劳务合作企业、劳务人员应当遵守用工项目所在国家或者地区的法律，尊重当地的宗教信仰、风俗习惯和文化传统。  
对外劳务合作企业、劳务人员不得从事损害国家安全和国家利益的活动。

**Article 15** Overseas labor cooperation enterprises and labor service personnel shall comply with the laws of the country or region where the employment project is located, and respect the local religious beliefs, customs and habits, and cultural traditions.  
Overseas labor cooperation enterprises and labor service personnel shall not engage in activities that harm national security or national interests.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对外劳务合作企业应当跟踪了解劳务人员在国外的工作、生活情况，协助解决劳务人员工作、生活中的困难和问题，及时向国外雇主反映劳务人员的合理要求。  
对外劳务合作企业向同一国家或者地区派出的劳务人员数量超过100人的，应当安排随行管理人员，并将随行管理人员名单报中国驻用工项目所在国使馆、领馆备案。

**Article 16** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall track and understand the working and living conditions of labor service personnel abroad, assist in solving difficulties and problems in the work and life of the labor service personnel, and promptly convey the reasonable requests of the labor service personnel to the foreign employer.  
Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise dispatches more than 100 labor service personnel to the same country or region, it shall arrange for accompanying managerial personnel and submit the list of accompanying managerial personnel to the Chinese embassy or consulate in the country where the employment project is located for record.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对外劳务合作企业应当制定突发事件应急预案。国外发生突发事件的，对外劳务合作企业应当及时、妥善处理，并立即向中国驻用工项目所在国使馆、领馆和国内有关部门报告。

**Article 17** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall formulate an emergency response plan for contingencies. Where an emergency occurs abroad, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall handle it promptly and properly, and immediately report to the Chinese embassy or consulate in the country where the employment project is located and the relevant domestic departments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　用工项目所在国家或者地区发生战争、暴乱、重大自然灾害等突发事件，中国政府作出相应避险安排的，对外劳务合作企业和劳务人员应当服从安排，予以配合。

**Article 18** Where an emergency such as war, riot, or major natural disaster occurs in the country or region where the employment project is located, and the Chinese government makes corresponding arrangements for risk avoidance, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise and the labor service personnel shall comply with the arrangements and cooperate.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对外劳务合作企业停止开展对外劳务合作的，应当对其派出的尚在国外工作的劳务人员作出妥善安排，并将安排方案报负责审批的商务主管部门备案。负责审批的商务主管部门应当将安排方案报至国务院商务主管部门，国务院商务主管部门应当及时通报中国驻用工项目所在国使馆、领馆。

**Article 19** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise ceases to carry out overseas labor cooperation, it shall make proper arrangements for the labor service personnel it has dispatched who are still working abroad, and submit the arrangement plan to the competent department of commerce responsible for approval for record. The competent department of commerce responsible for approval shall submit the arrangement plan to the competent department of commerce under the State Council, and the competent department of commerce under the State Council shall promptly notify the Chinese embassy or consulate in the country where the employment project is located.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　劳务人员有权向商务主管部门和其他有关部门投诉对外劳务合作企业违反合同约定或者其他侵害劳务人员合法权益的行为。接受投诉的部门应当按照职责依法及时处理，并将处理情况向投诉人反馈。

**Article 20** Labor service personnel have the right to lodge complaints with the competent department of commerce and other relevant departments against the overseas labor cooperation enterprise's breach of contract or other acts infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of the labor service personnel. The department receiving the complaint shall handle it promptly according to law within its functions and duties, and provide feedback on the handling situation to the complainant.

### 第三章　与对外劳务合作有关的合同

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对外劳务合作企业应当与国外雇主订立书面劳务合作合同；未与国外雇主订立书面劳务合作合同的，不得组织劳务人员赴国外工作。  
劳务合作合同应当载明与劳务人员权益保障相关的下列事项：  
(一)劳务人员的工作内容、工作地点、工作时间和休息休假；  
(二)合同期限；  
(三)劳务人员的劳动报酬及其支付方式；  
(四)劳务人员社会保险费的缴纳；  
(五)劳务人员的劳动条件、劳动保护、职业培训和职业危害防护；  
(六)劳务人员的福利待遇和生活条件；  
(七)劳务人员在国外居留、工作许可等手续的办理；  
(八)劳务人员人身意外伤害保险的购买；  
(九)因国外雇主原因解除与劳务人员的合同对劳务人员的经济补偿；  
(十)发生突发事件对劳务人员的协助、救助；  
(十一)违约责任。

**Article 21** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall conclude a written labor cooperation contract with the foreign employer; if it fails to conclude a written labor cooperation contract with the foreign employer, it may not organize labor service personnel to work abroad.  
The labor cooperation contract shall specify the following matters related to the protection of the rights and interests of labor service personnel:  
(1) the work content, work location, working hours, and rest and leave of the labor service personnel;  
(2) the term of the contract;  
(3) the labor remuneration of the labor service personnel and the method of payment;  
(4) the payment of social insurance premiums for the labor service personnel;  
(5) the working conditions, labor protection, vocational training, and protection against occupational hazards for the labor service personnel;  
(6) the welfare benefits and living conditions of the labor service personnel;  
(7) the handling of procedures such as residence and work permits for the labor service personnel abroad;  
(8) the purchase of personal accident insurance for the labor service personnel;  
(9) economic compensation to the labor service personnel upon termination of the contract with them by the foreign employer;  
(10) assistance and rescue to the labor service personnel in the event of emergencies; and  
(11) liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对外劳务合作企业与国外雇主订立劳务合作合同，应当事先了解国外雇主和用工项目的情况以及用工项目所在国家或者地区的相关法律。  
用工项目所在国家或者地区法律规定企业或者机构使用外籍劳务人员需经批准的，对外劳务合作企业只能与经批准的企业或者机构订立劳务合作合同。  
对外劳务合作企业不得与国外的个人订立劳务合作合同。

**Article 22** Before concluding a labor cooperation contract with a foreign employer, an overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall ascertain the circumstances of the foreign employer and the employment project, as well as the relevant laws of the country or region where the employment project is located.  
Where the laws of the country or region where the employment project is located require approval for enterprises or institutions to employ foreign labor service personnel, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise may only conclude a labor cooperation contract with an enterprise or institution that has obtained approval.  
No overseas labor cooperation enterprise may conclude a labor cooperation contract with a foreign individual.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　除本条第二款规定的情形外，对外劳务合作企业应当与劳务人员订立书面服务合同；未与劳务人员订立书面服务合同的，不得组织劳务人员赴国外工作。服务合同应当载明劳务合作合同中与劳务人员权益保障相关的事项，以及服务项目、服务费及其收取方式、违约责任。  
对外劳务合作企业组织与其建立劳动关系的劳务人员赴国外工作的，与劳务人员订立的劳动合同应当载明劳务合作合同中与劳务人员权益保障相关的事项；未与劳务人员订立劳动合同的，不得组织劳务人员赴国外工作。

**Article 23** Except for the circumstances provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article, an overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall conclude a written service contract with the labor service personnel; if it fails to conclude a written service contract with the labor service personnel, it may not organize them to work abroad. The service contract shall specify the matters related to the protection of the rights and interests of labor service personnel in the labor cooperation contract, as well as the service items, service fees and the method of collection, and liability for breach of contract.  
Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise organizes labor service personnel with whom it has established labor relations to work abroad, the labor contract concluded with the labor service personnel shall specify the matters related to the protection of the rights and interests of labor service personnel in the labor cooperation contract; if it fails to conclude a labor contract with the labor service personnel, it may not organize them to work abroad.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对外劳务合作企业与劳务人员订立服务合同或者劳动合同时，应当将劳务合作合同中与劳务人员权益保障相关的事项以及劳务人员要求了解的其他情况如实告知劳务人员，并向劳务人员明确提示包括人身安全风险在内的赴国外工作的风险，不得向劳务人员隐瞒有关信息或者提供虚假信息。  
对外劳务合作企业有权了解劳务人员与订立服务合同、劳动合同直接相关的个人基本情况，劳务人员应当如实说明。

**Article 24** When concluding a service contract or labor contract with labor service personnel, an overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall truthfully inform the labor service personnel of the matters related to the protection of their rights and interests in the labor cooperation contract and other information that the labor service personnel request to know, and shall clearly indicate to the labor service personnel the risks of working abroad, including personal safety risks, and shall not conceal relevant information from the labor service personnel or provide false information.  
An overseas labor cooperation enterprise has the right to know the basic personal information of the labor service personnel directly related to the conclusion of the service contract or labor contract, and the labor service personnel shall truthfully provide such information.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对外劳务合作企业向与其订立服务合同的劳务人员收取服务费，应当符合国务院价格主管部门会同国务院商务主管部门制定的有关规定。  
对外劳务合作企业不得向与其订立劳动合同的劳务人员收取服务费。  
对外劳务合作企业不得以任何名目向劳务人员收取押金或者要求劳务人员提供财产担保。

**Article 25** The service fees collected by an overseas labor cooperation enterprise from labor service personnel with whom it has concluded a service contract shall comply with the relevant provisions formulated by the competent department of price under the State Council jointly with the competent department of commerce under the State Council.  
No overseas labor cooperation enterprise may collect service fees from labor service personnel with whom it has concluded a labor contract.  
No overseas labor cooperation enterprise may, under any pretext, collect a deposit from labor service personnel or require labor service personnel to provide property guarantees.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对外劳务合作企业应当自与劳务人员订立服务合同或者劳动合同之日起10个工作日内，将服务合同或者劳动合同、劳务合作合同副本以及劳务人员名单报负责审批的商务主管部门备案。负责审批的商务主管部门应当将用工项目、国外雇主的有关信息以及劳务人员名单报至国务院商务主管部门。  
商务主管部门发现服务合同或者劳动合同、劳务合作合同未依照本条例规定载明必备事项的，应当要求对外劳务合作企业补正。

**Article 26** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall, within 10 working days from the date of concluding a service contract or labor contract with labor service personnel, submit the copies of the service contract or labor contract, the labor cooperation contract, and the list of labor service personnel to the competent department of commerce responsible for approval for record. The competent department of commerce responsible for approval shall submit the relevant information on the employment project and the foreign employer, as well as the list of labor service personnel, to the competent department of commerce under the State Council.  
Where the competent department of commerce discovers that the service contract or labor contract, or the labor cooperation contract fails to specify the mandatory matters in accordance with these Regulations, it shall require the overseas labor cooperation enterprise to make corrections.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对外劳务合作企业应当负责协助劳务人员与国外雇主订立确定劳动关系的合同，并保证合同中有关劳务人员权益保障的条款与劳务合作合同相应条款的内容一致。

**Article 27** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall be responsible for assisting the labor service personnel in concluding a contract with the foreign employer that establishes the labor relationship, and shall ensure that the terms concerning the protection of the rights and interests of the labor service personnel in such contract are consistent with the corresponding terms of the labor cooperation contract.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对外劳务合作企业、劳务人员应当信守合同，全面履行合同约定的各自的义务。

**Article 28** An overseas labor cooperation enterprise and the labor service personnel shall abide by the contract and fully perform their respective obligations as agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　劳务人员在国外实际享有的权益不符合合同约定的，对外劳务合作企业应当协助劳务人员维护合法权益，要求国外雇主履行约定义务、赔偿损失；劳务人员未得到应有赔偿的，有权要求对外劳务合作企业承担相应的赔偿责任。对外劳务合作企业不协助劳务人员向国外雇主要求赔偿的，劳务人员可以直接向对外劳务合作企业要求赔偿。  
劳务人员在国外实际享有的权益不符合用工项目所在国家或者地区法律规定的，对外劳务合作企业应当协助劳务人员维护合法权益，要求国外雇主履行法律规定的义务、赔偿损失。  
因对外劳务合作企业隐瞒有关信息或者提供虚假信息等原因，导致劳务人员在国外实际享有的权益不符合合同约定的，对外劳务合作企业应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 29** Where the rights and interests actually enjoyed by the labor service personnel abroad do not conform to the contract, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall assist the labor service personnel in safeguarding their lawful rights and interests, and require the foreign employer to perform the agreed obligations and compensate for losses; if the labor service personnel fail to obtain due compensation, they have the right to require the overseas labor cooperation enterprise to bear corresponding compensation liability. If the overseas labor cooperation enterprise fails to assist the labor service personnel in claiming compensation from the foreign employer, the labor service personnel may directly claim compensation from the overseas labor cooperation enterprise.  
Where the rights and interests actually enjoyed by the labor service personnel abroad do not conform to the laws of the country or region where the employment project is located, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall assist the labor service personnel in safeguarding their lawful rights and interests, and require the foreign employer to perform the legal obligations and compensate for losses.  
Where, due to reasons such as the overseas labor cooperation enterprise concealing relevant information or providing false information, the rights and interests actually enjoyed by the labor service personnel abroad do not conform to the contract, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise shall bear compensation liability.

### 第四章　政府的服务和管理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门建立对外劳务合作信息收集、通报制度，为对外劳务合作企业和劳务人员无偿提供信息服务。

**Article 30** The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, establish a system for the collection and notification of information on overseas labor cooperation, and provide information services free of charge to overseas labor cooperation enterprises and labor service personnel.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门建立对外劳务合作风险监测和评估机制，及时发布有关国家或者地区安全状况的评估结果，提供预警信息，指导对外劳务合作企业做好安全风险防范；有关国家或者地区安全状况难以保障劳务人员人身安全的，对外劳务合作企业不得组织劳务人员赴上述国家或者地区工作。

**Article 31** The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, establish a mechanism for risk monitoring and assessment of overseas labor cooperation, promptly publish the assessment results of the security situation of relevant countries or regions, provide early warning information, and guide overseas labor cooperation enterprises to take good safety risk prevention; where the security situation of a relevant country or region makes it difficult to guarantee the personal safety of labor service personnel, the overseas labor cooperation enterprise may not organize labor service personnel to work in such country or region.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院商务主管部门会同国务院统计部门建立对外劳务合作统计制度，及时掌握并汇总、分析对外劳务合作发展情况。

**Article 32** The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the statistics department under the State Council, establish a statistical system for overseas labor cooperation, and promptly grasp, summarize and analyze the development of overseas labor cooperation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家财政对劳务人员培训给予必要的支持。  
国务院商务主管部门会同国务院人力资源社会保障部门应当加强对劳务人员培训的指导和监督。

**Article 33** The State finance shall provide necessary support for the training of labor service personnel.  
The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the human resources and social security department under the State Council, strengthen guidance and supervision over the training of labor service personnel.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府根据本地区开展对外劳务合作的实际情况，按照国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门的规定，组织建立对外劳务合作服务平台(以下简称服务平台)，为对外劳务合作企业和劳务人员无偿提供相关服务，鼓励、引导对外劳务合作企业通过服务平台招收劳务人员。  
国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门应当加强对服务平台运行的指导和监督。

**Article 34** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual situation of overseas labor cooperation in their respective regions, and in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of commerce under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, organize the establishment of overseas labor cooperation service platforms (hereinafter referred to as service platforms) to provide relevant services free of charge to overseas labor cooperation enterprises and labor service personnel, and encourage and guide overseas labor cooperation enterprises to recruit labor service personnel through the service platforms.  
The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, strengthen guidance and supervision over the operation of the service platforms.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　中国驻外使馆、领馆为对外劳务合作企业了解国外雇主和用工项目的情况以及用工项目所在国家或者地区的法律提供必要的协助，依据职责维护对外劳务合作企业和劳务人员在国外的正当权益，发现违反本条例规定的行为及时通报国务院商务主管部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府。  
劳务人员可以合法、有序地向中国驻外使馆、领馆反映相关诉求，不得干扰使馆、领馆正常工作秩序。

**Article 35** The Chinese embassies and consulates abroad shall provide necessary assistance to overseas labor cooperation enterprises in ascertaining the circumstances of foreign employers and employment projects, as well as the laws of the country or region where the employment project is located; safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of overseas labor cooperation enterprises and labor service personnel abroad in accordance with their functions and duties; and promptly notify the competent department of commerce under the State Council and the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government upon discovering acts violating the provisions of these Regulations.  
Labor service personnel may lawfully and in an orderly manner submit relevant claims to the Chinese embassies and consulates abroad, and shall not disrupt the normal working order of the embassies and consulates.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院有关部门、有关县级以上地方人民政府应当建立健全对外劳务合作突发事件预警、防范和应急处置机制，制定对外劳务合作突发事件应急预案。  
对外劳务合作突发事件应急处置由组织劳务人员赴国外工作的单位或者个人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责，劳务人员户籍所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府予以配合。  
中国驻外使馆、领馆协助处置对外劳务合作突发事件。

**Article 36** The relevant departments under the State Council and the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the early warning, prevention, and emergency handling mechanisms for emergencies in overseas labor cooperation, and formulate emergency response plans for overseas labor cooperation emergencies.  
The emergency handling of overseas labor cooperation emergencies shall be the responsibility of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the unit or individual organizing labor service personnel to work abroad is located, and the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the labor service personnel are domiciled shall cooperate.  
The Chinese embassies and consulates abroad shall assist in handling overseas labor cooperation emergencies.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门建立对外劳务合作不良信用记录和公告制度，公布对外劳务合作企业和国外雇主不履行合同约定、侵害劳务人员合法权益的行为，以及对对外劳务合作企业违法行为的处罚决定。

**Article 37** The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, establish a system for recording and publicizing adverse credit information on overseas labor cooperation, and publish information on acts of overseas labor cooperation enterprises and foreign employers failing to perform contractual obligations or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of labor service personnel, as well as decisions on penalties imposed on overseas labor cooperation enterprises for illegal acts.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对违反本条例规定组织劳务人员赴国外工作，以及其他违反本条例规定的行为，任何单位和个人有权向商务、公安、工商行政管理等有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当在职责范围内及时处理。  
国务院商务主管部门会同国务院公安、工商行政管理等有关部门，建立健全相关管理制度，防范和制止非法组织劳务人员赴国外工作的行为。

**Article 38** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the relevant departments, such as commerce, public security, and industry and commerce administration, any act of organizing labor service personnel to work abroad in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, as well as other acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations. The department receiving the report shall handle it promptly within the scope of its functions and duties.  
The competent department of commerce under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, such as public security and industry and commerce administration, establish and improve relevant management systems to prevent and stop the illegal organization of labor service personnel to work abroad.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　未依法取得对外劳务合作经营资格，从事对外劳务合作的，由商务主管部门提请工商行政管理部门依照《无照经营查处取缔办法》的规定查处取缔；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where any entity engages in overseas labor cooperation without lawfully obtaining the qualification for overseas labor cooperation business, the competent department of commerce shall request the industry and commerce administration department to investigate and ban the entity in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Investigation and Banning of Unlicensed Business Operations; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对外劳务合作企业有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门吊销其对外劳务合作经营资格证书，有违法所得的予以没收：  
(一)以商务、旅游、留学等名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(二)允许其他单位或者个人以本企业的名义组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(三)组织劳务人员赴国外从事与赌博、色情活动相关的工作。

**Article 40** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of commerce shall revoke its qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business and confiscate any illegal gains:  
(1) organizing labor service personnel to work abroad in the name of business, tourism, study, etc.;  
(2) permitting any other unit or individual to organize labor service personnel to work abroad in its name; or  
(3) organizing labor service personnel to work abroad in jobs related to gambling or pornography.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对外劳务合作企业未依照本条例规定缴存或者补足备用金的，由商务主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，吊销其对外劳务合作经营资格证书。

**Article 41** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise fails to deposit or replenish the reserve in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of commerce shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, its qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business shall be revoked.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对外劳务合作企业有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并对其主要负责人处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未安排劳务人员接受培训，组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(二)未依照本条例规定为劳务人员购买在国外工作期间的人身意外伤害保险；  
(三)未依照本条例规定安排随行管理人员。

**Article 42** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of commerce shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed on its principal responsible person:  
(1) failing to arrange for labor service personnel to receive training and organizing them to work abroad;  
(2) failing to purchase personal accident insurance for labor service personnel during their work abroad in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; or  
(3) failing to arrange for accompanying managerial personnel in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对外劳务合作企业有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，并对其主要负责人处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；在国外引起重大劳务纠纷、突发事件或者造成其他严重后果的，吊销其对外劳务合作经营资格证书：  
(一)未与国外雇主订立劳务合作合同，组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(二)未依照本条例规定与劳务人员订立服务合同或者劳动合同，组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(三)违反本条例规定，与未经批准的国外雇主或者与国外的个人订立劳务合作合同，组织劳务人员赴国外工作；  
(四)与劳务人员订立服务合同或者劳动合同，隐瞒有关信息或者提供虚假信息；  
(五)在国外发生突发事件时不及时处理；  
(六)停止开展对外劳务合作，未对其派出的尚在国外工作的劳务人员作出安排。  
有前款第四项规定情形，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of commerce shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on its principal responsible person; if the act causes major labor disputes, emergencies or other serious consequences abroad, its qualification certificate for overseas labor cooperation business shall be revoked:  
(1) organizing labor service personnel to work abroad without concluding a labor cooperation contract with the foreign employer;  
(2) organizing labor service personnel to work abroad without concluding a service contract or labor contract with them in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) violating the provisions of these Regulations by concluding a labor cooperation contract with a foreign employer that has not obtained approval or with a foreign individual, and organizing labor service personnel to work abroad;  
(4) concealing relevant information or providing false information when concluding a service contract or labor contract with labor service personnel;  
(5) failing to handle emergencies promptly when they occur abroad; or  
(6) ceasing to carry out overseas labor cooperation without making arrangements for the labor service personnel dispatched who are still working abroad.  
Where the circumstances specified in item (4) of the preceding paragraph constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　对外劳务合作企业向与其订立服务合同的劳务人员收取服务费不符合国家有关规定，或者向劳务人员收取押金、要求劳务人员提供财产担保的，由价格主管部门依照有关价格的法律、行政法规的规定处罚。  
对外劳务合作企业向与其订立劳动合同的劳务人员收取费用的，依照《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》的规定处罚。

**Article 44** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise collects service fees from labor service personnel with whom it has concluded a service contract in violation of relevant State regulations, or collects a deposit from labor service personnel or requires labor service personnel to provide property guarantees, the competent department of price shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on price.  
Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise collects fees from labor service personnel with whom it has concluded a labor contract, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对外劳务合作企业有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款，并对其主要负责人处2000元以上5000元以下的罚款：  
(一)未将服务合同或者劳动合同、劳务合作合同副本以及劳务人员名单报商务主管部门备案；  
(二)组织劳务人员出境后，未将有关情况向中国驻用工项目所在国使馆、领馆报告，或者未依照本条例规定将随行管理人员名单报负责审批的商务主管部门备案；  
(三)未制定突发事件应急预案；  
(四)停止开展对外劳务合作，未将其对劳务人员的安排方案报商务主管部门备案。  
对外劳务合作企业拒不将服务合同或者劳动合同、劳务合作合同副本报商务主管部门备案，且合同未载明本条例规定的必备事项，或者在合同备案后拒不按照商务主管部门的要求补正合同必备事项的，依照[本条例第四十三条](#art-43)的规定处罚。

**Article 45** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of commerce shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on its principal responsible person:  
(1) failing to submit the copies of the service contract or labor contract, the labor cooperation contract, and the list of labor service personnel to the competent department of commerce for record;  
(2) after organizing labor service personnel to go abroad, failing to report the relevant information to the Chinese embassy or consulate in the country where the employment project is located, or failing to submit the list of accompanying managerial personnel to the competent department of commerce responsible for approval for record in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) failing to formulate an emergency response plan for contingencies; or  
(4) ceasing to carry out overseas labor cooperation without submitting its arrangement plan for labor service personnel to the competent department of commerce for record.  
Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise refuses to submit the copies of the service contract or labor contract and the labor cooperation contract to the competent department of commerce for record, and the contract does not specify the mandatory matters provided for in these Regulations, or after the contract is recorded, it refuses to make corrections to the mandatory matters of the contract as required by the competent department of commerce, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 43 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　商务主管部门、其他有关部门在查处违反本条例行为的过程中，发现违法行为涉嫌构成犯罪的，应当依法及时移送司法机关处理。

**Article 46** Where, in the process of investigating and dealing with acts violating the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of commerce or other relevant departments discover that the illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, they shall promptly transfer the case to the judicial organ for handling according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　商务主管部门和其他有关部门的工作人员，在对外劳务合作监督管理工作中有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对不符合本条例规定条件的对外劳务合作经营资格申请予以批准；  
(二)对外劳务合作企业不再具备本条例规定的条件而不撤销原批准；  
(三)对违反本条例规定组织劳务人员赴国外工作以及其他违反本条例规定的行为不依法查处；  
(四)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，不依法履行监督管理职责的行为。

**Article 47** Where a staff member of the competent department of commerce or other relevant departments commits any of the following acts in the supervision and administration of overseas labor cooperation, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) approving an application for the qualification for overseas labor cooperation business that does not meet the conditions provided for in these Regulations;  
(2) failing to revoke the original approval when an overseas labor cooperation enterprise no longer meets the conditions provided for in these Regulations;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with acts of organizing labor service personnel to work abroad in violation of the provisions of these Regulations or other acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations according to law; or  
(4) other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, or failing to perform supervision and administration duties according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有关对外劳务合作的商会按照依法制定的章程开展活动，为成员提供服务，发挥自律作用。

**Article 48** Chambers of commerce related to overseas labor cooperation shall carry out activities in accordance with their constitutions formulated according to law, provide services to their members, and play a self-regulatory role.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对外承包工程项下外派人员赴国外工作的管理，依照《对外承包工程管理条例》以及国务院商务主管部门、国务院住房城乡建设主管部门的规定执行。  
外派海员类(不含渔业船员)对外劳务合作的管理办法，由国务院交通运输主管部门根据《中华人民共和国船员条例》以及本条例的有关规定另行制定。

**Article 49** The administration of the dispatch of personnel to work abroad under international construction projects shall be governed by the Regulations on the Administration of International Construction Projects and the provisions of the competent department of commerce under the State Council and the competent department of housing and urban-rural development under the State Council.  
The administrative measures for overseas labor cooperation in the category of seafarers dispatched abroad (excluding fishing vessel crew) shall be separately formulated by the competent department of transportation under the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Seafarers and the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　组织劳务人员赴香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区工作的，参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 50** The provisions of these Regulations shall be applied mutatis mutandis to the organization of labor service personnel to work in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　对外劳务合作企业组织劳务人员赴国务院商务主管部门会同国务院外交等有关部门确定的特定国家或者地区工作的，应当经国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门批准。

**Article 51** Where an overseas labor cooperation enterprise organizes labor service personnel to work in a specific country or region designated by the competent department of commerce under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, such as the foreign affairs department, approval from the competent department of commerce under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council shall be required.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例施行前按照国家有关规定经批准从事对外劳务合作的企业，不具备本条例规定条件的，应当在国务院商务主管部门规定的期限内达到本条例规定的条件；逾期达不到本条例规定条件的，不得继续从事对外劳务合作。

**Article 52** Where an enterprise that, before the coming into force of these Regulations, was approved to engage in overseas labor cooperation in accordance with relevant State regulations does not meet the conditions provided for in these Regulations, it shall meet the conditions provided for in these Regulations within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of commerce under the State Council; if it fails to meet the conditions within the time limit, it may not continue to engage in overseas labor cooperation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例自2012年8月1日起施行。

**Article 53** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2012.
