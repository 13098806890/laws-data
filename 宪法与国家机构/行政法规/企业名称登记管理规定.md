**法律部门**：宪法相关法

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范企业名称登记管理，保护企业的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，优化营商环境，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of regulating the registration and administration of enterprise names, protecting the lawful rights and interests of enterprises, maintaining the social and economic order, and optimizing the business environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门（以下统称企业登记机关）负责中国境内设立企业的企业名称登记管理。  
国务院市场监督管理部门主管全国企业名称登记管理工作，负责制定企业名称登记管理的具体规范。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府市场监督管理部门负责建立本行政区域统一的企业名称申报系统和企业名称数据库，并向社会开放。

**Article 2** The market supervision and administration departments of the people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the enterprise registration authorities) shall be responsible for the registration and administration of names of enterprises established within the territory of China.  
The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall take charge of the registration and administration of enterprise names nationwide, and shall be responsible for formulating specific rules for the registration and administration of enterprise names.  
The market supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for establishing a unified enterprise name declaration system and enterprise name database within their respective administrative regions, and shall make them open to the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　企业登记机关应当不断提升企业名称登记管理规范化、便利化水平，为企业和群众提供高效、便捷的服务。

**Article 3** The enterprise registration authorities shall continuously improve the standardization and facilitation level of the registration and administration of enterprise names, and provide efficient and convenient services for enterprises and the public.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　企业只能登记一个企业名称，企业名称受法律保护。

**Article 4** An enterprise may register only one enterprise name, which shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　企业名称应当使用规范汉字。民族自治地方的企业名称可以同时使用本民族自治地方通用的民族文字。

**Article 5** Enterprise names shall use standard Chinese characters. The enterprise names in national autonomous areas may also use the ethnic characters commonly used in the relevant national autonomous areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业名称由行政区划名称、字号、行业或者经营特点、组织形式组成。跨省、自治区、直辖市经营的企业，其名称可以不含行政区划名称；跨行业综合经营的企业，其名称可以不含行业或者经营特点。

**Article 6** An enterprise name shall consist of the name of the administrative division, the name, the industry or business characteristics, and the organizational form. An enterprise operating across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may omit the name of the administrative division from its name; an enterprise engaging in comprehensive cross-industry operations may omit the industry or business characteristics from its name.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业名称中的行政区划名称应当是企业所在地的县级以上地方行政区划名称。市辖区名称在企业名称中使用时应当同时冠以其所属的设区的市的行政区划名称。开发区、垦区等区域名称在企业名称中使用时应当与行政区划名称连用，不得单独使用。

**Article 7** The name of the administrative division in an enterprise name shall be the name of the local administrative division at or above the county level where the enterprise is located. When the name of a municipal district is used in an enterprise name, it shall be preceded by the name of the administrative division of the city with districts to which the district belongs. When the names of development zones, reclamation areas, or other similar areas are used in an enterprise name, they shall be used together with the name of the administrative division, and shall not be used alone.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业名称中的字号应当由两个以上汉字组成。  
县级以上地方行政区划名称、行业或者经营特点不得作为字号，另有含义的除外。

**Article 8** The name in an enterprise name shall consist of two or more Chinese characters.  
The name of an administrative division at or above the county level, or the industry or business characteristics shall not be used as the name, except where it has another meaning.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业名称中的行业或者经营特点应当根据企业的主营业务和国民经济行业分类标准标明。国民经济行业分类标准中没有规定的，可以参照行业习惯或者专业文献等表述。

**Article 9** The industry or business characteristics in an enterprise name shall be indicated in accordance with the enterprise's principal business and the national economic industry classification standards. Where no provision exists in the national economic industry classification standards, reference may be made to industry practices or professional literature for the expression.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业应当根据其组织结构或者责任形式，依法在企业名称中标明组织形式。

**Article 10** An enterprise shall, according to its organizational structure or form of liability, indicate its organizational form in the enterprise name in accordance with law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业名称不得有下列情形：  
（一）损害国家尊严或者利益；  
（二）损害社会公共利益或者妨碍社会公共秩序；  
（三）使用或者变相使用政党、党政军机关、群团组织名称及其简称、特定称谓和部队番号；  
（四）使用外国国家（地区）、国际组织名称及其通用简称、特定称谓；  
（五）含有淫秽、色情、赌博、迷信、恐怖、暴力的内容；  
（六）含有民族、种族、宗教、性别歧视的内容；  
（七）违背公序良俗或者可能有其他不良影响；  
（八）可能使公众受骗或者产生误解；  
（九）法律、行政法规以及国家规定禁止的其他情形。

**Article 11** An enterprise name shall not fall under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) harming the dignity or interests of the State;  
(2) harming the public interest or disrupting public order;  
(3) using or using in disguised form the names of political parties, Party, government and military organs, and people's organizations, as well as their abbreviations, specific titles, and military designations;  
(4) using the names of foreign countries (regions) or international organizations, as well as their commonly used abbreviations and specific titles;  
(5) containing content that is obscene, pornographic, gambling, superstitious, terrifying, or violent;  
(6) containing content that discriminates against ethnicity, race, religion, or gender;  
(7) violating public order and good customs or likely having other adverse effects;  
(8) likely to deceive the public or cause misunderstanding;  
(9) other circumstances prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and State provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　企业名称冠以“中国”、“中华”、“中央”、“全国”、“国家”等字词，应当按照有关规定从严审核，并报国务院批准。国务院市场监督管理部门负责制定具体管理办法。  
企业名称中间含有“中国”、“中华”、“全国”、“国家”等字词的，该字词应当是行业限定语。  
使用外国投资者字号的外商独资或者控股的外商投资企业，企业名称中可以含有“（中国）”字样。

**Article 12** Where an enterprise name is prefixed with words such as “China”, “Zhonghua”, “Central”, “National”, or “State”, it shall be subject to strict examination in accordance with relevant provisions and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval. The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating specific management measures.  
Where an enterprise name contains words such as “China”, “Zhonghua”, “National”, or “State” in the middle, such words shall serve as industry qualifiers.  
A wholly foreign-owned enterprise or a foreign-invested enterprise with controlling stake that uses the trade name of a foreign investor may include the words “(China)” in its enterprise name.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业分支机构名称应当冠以其所从属企业的名称，并缀以“分公司”、“分厂”、“分店”等字词。境外企业分支机构还应当在名称中标明该企业的国籍及责任形式。

**Article 13** The name of a branch of an enterprise shall be prefixed with the name of the enterprise to which it is subordinate and suffixed with words such as “Branch”, “Subsidiary Factory”, or “Subsidiary Store”. The branch of an overseas enterprise shall also indicate the nationality and form of liability of the enterprise in its name.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业集团名称应当与控股企业名称的行政区划名称、字号、行业或者经营特点一致。控股企业可以在其名称的组织形式之前使用“集团”或者“（集团）”字样。

**Article 14** The name of an enterprise group shall be consistent with the administrative division name, trade name, industry or business characteristics of the controlling enterprise's name. The controlling enterprise may use the words “Group” or “(Group)” before the organizational form in its name.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有投资关系或者经过授权的企业，其名称中可以含有另一个企业的名称或者其他法人、非法人组织的名称。

**Article 15** An enterprise with an investment relationship or with authorization may include the name of another enterprise or the name of another legal person or unincorporated organization in its name.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业名称由申请人自主申报。  
申请人可以通过企业名称申报系统或者在企业登记机关服务窗口提交有关信息和材料，对拟定的企业名称进行查询、比对和筛选，选取符合本规定要求的企业名称。  
申请人提交的信息和材料应当真实、准确、完整，并承诺因其企业名称与他人企业名称近似侵犯他人合法权益的，依法承担法律责任。

**Article 16** An enterprise name shall be declared by the applicant on its own initiative.  
The applicant may submit relevant information and materials through the enterprise name declaration system or at the service window of the enterprise registration authority, to query, compare, and screen the proposed enterprise name, and select an enterprise name that complies with the requirements of these Provisions.  
The information and materials submitted by the applicant shall be truthful, accurate, and complete, and the applicant shall undertake that if its enterprise name infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of others due to similarity with another enterprise's name, it shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在同一企业登记机关，申请人拟定的企业名称中的字号不得与下列同行业或者不使用行业、经营特点表述的企业名称中的字号相同：  
（一）已经登记或者在保留期内的企业名称，有投资关系的除外；  
（二）已经注销或者变更登记未满1年的原企业名称，有投资关系或者受让企业名称的除外；  
（三）被撤销设立登记或者被撤销变更登记未满1年的原企业名称，有投资关系的除外。

**Article 17** Within the same enterprise registration authority, the trade name in the enterprise name proposed by the applicant shall not be identical to the trade name in any of the following enterprise names of the same industry or of enterprises that do not use industry or business characteristics statements:  
(1) an enterprise name that has been registered or is within the retention period, except where there is an investment relationship;  
(2) an original enterprise name that has been cancelled or has undergone change registration for less than one year, except where there is an investment relationship or the enterprise name has been transferred;  
(3) an original enterprise name that has had its establishment registration revoked or its change registration revoked for less than one year, except where there is an investment relationship.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业登记机关对通过企业名称申报系统提交完成的企业名称予以保留，保留期为2个月。设立企业依法应当报经批准或者企业经营范围中有在登记前须经批准的项目的，保留期为1年。  
申请人应当在保留期届满前办理企业登记。

**Article 18** The enterprise registration authority shall retain the enterprise name that has been submitted through the enterprise name declaration system, with a retention period of two months. Where the establishment of an enterprise requires approval according to law or there are items in the business scope that require approval before registration, the retention period shall be one year.  
The applicant shall complete the enterprise registration before the expiry of the retention period.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业名称转让或者授权他人使用的，相关企业应当依法通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示。

**Article 19** Where an enterprise name is transferred or licensed for use by others, the relevant enterprise shall, according to law, make a public announcement through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业登记机关在办理企业登记时，发现企业名称不符合本规定的，不予登记并书面说明理由。  
企业登记机关发现已经登记的企业名称不符合本规定的，应当及时纠正。其他单位或者个人认为已经登记的企业名称不符合本规定的，可以请求企业登记机关予以纠正。

**Article 20** Where the enterprise registration authority, when processing enterprise registration, finds that the enterprise name does not comply with these Provisions, it shall not register the name and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.  
Where the enterprise registration authority finds that a registered enterprise name does not comply with these Provisions, it shall promptly rectify the situation. Other entities or individuals that believe a registered enterprise name does not comply with these Provisions may request the enterprise registration authority to rectify it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　企业认为其他企业名称侵犯本企业名称合法权益的，可以向人民法院起诉或者请求为涉嫌侵权企业办理登记的企业登记机关处理。  
企业登记机关受理申请后，可以进行调解；调解不成的，企业登记机关应当自受理之日起3个月内作出行政裁决。

**Article 21** Where an enterprise believes that the name of another enterprise infringes upon its lawful rights and interests in its enterprise name, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court or request the enterprise registration authority that registered the allegedly infringing enterprise to handle the matter.  
After accepting the application, the enterprise registration authority may conduct mediation; if mediation fails, the enterprise registration authority shall make an administrative adjudication within three months from the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　利用企业名称实施不正当竞争等行为的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 22** Any act of using an enterprise name to commit unfair competition or other acts shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　使用企业名称应当遵守法律法规，诚实守信，不得损害他人合法权益。  
人民法院或者企业登记机关依法认定企业名称应当停止使用的，企业应当自收到人民法院生效的法律文书或者企业登记机关的处理决定之日起30日内办理企业名称变更登记。名称变更前，由企业登记机关以统一社会信用代码代替其名称。企业逾期未办理变更登记的，企业登记机关将其列入经营异常名录；完成变更登记后，企业登记机关将其移出经营异常名录。

**Article 23** The use of an enterprise name shall comply with laws and regulations, be honest and trustworthy, and shall not harm the lawful rights and interests of others.  
Where the People's Court or the enterprise registration authority determines according to law that the enterprise name shall cease to be used, the enterprise shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the effective legal document of the People's Court or the decision of the enterprise registration authority, undergo the change registration of the enterprise name. Before the change of name, the enterprise registration authority shall replace its name with a unified social credit code. If the enterprise fails to undergo the change registration within the time limit, the enterprise registration authority shall include it in the list of enterprises with abnormal operation; after the change registration is completed, the enterprise registration authority shall remove it from the list of enterprises with abnormal operation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请人登记或者使用企业名称违反本规定的，依照企业登记相关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。  
企业登记机关对不符合本规定的企业名称予以登记，或者对符合本规定的企业名称不予登记的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 24** Where an applicant registers or uses an enterprise name in violation of these Provisions, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on enterprise registration.  
Where an enterprise registration authority registers an enterprise name that does not comply with these Provisions, or fails to register an enterprise name that complies with these Provisions, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农民专业合作社和个体工商户的名称登记管理，参照本规定执行。

**Article 25** The registration management of names of specialized farmer cooperatives and individual industrial and commercial households shall be implemented with reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本规定自2021年3月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall come into force as of March 1, 2021.
