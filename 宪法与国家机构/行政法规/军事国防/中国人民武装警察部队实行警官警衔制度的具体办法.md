# 中国人民武装警察部队实行警官警衔制度的具体办法

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、中国人民武装警察部队警官警衔，是区分警官等级、表明警官身份的称号、标志和国家给予警官的荣誉。

**Article 1** I. The police ranks of the officers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force are the titles, insignia, and honors granted by the State to the officers, which distinguish their grades and indicate their status.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、中国人民武装警察部队现役警官警衔等级的设置，比照《中国人民解放军军官军衔条例》第二章第七条的规定执行。  
警官在警衔前冠以“武警”。专业技术警官在警衔前冠以“武警专业技术”。

**Article 2** II. The establishment of the police rank grades for active-duty officers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be carried out by reference to the provisions of Article 7 of Chapter II of the Regulations on the Military Ranks of Officers of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.  
The title "Armed Police" shall be prefixed to the police rank of an officer. The title "Armed Police Technical" shall be prefixed to the police rank of a technical officer.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、中国人民武装警察部队现役警官按照职务等级编制警衔：  
(一)武警部队司令员、政治委员：中将至少将；  
(二)武警部队副司令员、副政治委员：中将至大校；  
(三)正军职以下警官职务等级编制警衔，依照《中国人民解放军军官军衔条例》第三章第十一条的规定执行。

**Article 3** III. The police ranks shall be coded for active-duty officers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force according to their post grades:  
(1) Commander and Political Commissar of the Armed Police Force: Lieutenant General to Major General;  
(2) Deputy Commander and Deputy Political Commissar of the Armed Police Force: Lieutenant General to Senior Colonel;  
(3) The coding of police ranks for officer posts at and below the corps level shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of Chapter III of the Regulations on the Military Ranks of Officers of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、下列单位的现役警官，符合中央军事委员会规定的评定授予现役军官军衔范围的，评定授予警衔：  
(一)中国人民武装警察部队总部、总队、院校；  
(二)武警边防部队、消防部队、警卫部队；  
(三)武警水电指挥部、黄金指挥部、交通指挥部及所属部队，武装森林警察总队。

**Article 4** IV. Active-duty officers of the following units who meet the scope for evaluation and conferment of active-duty officer military ranks as prescribed by the Central Military Commission shall be evaluated and conferred with police ranks:  
(1) The headquarters, general detachments, and academies of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force;  
(2) The Armed Police border defense forces, firefighting forces, and guard forces;  
(3) The Armed Police water and power headquarters, gold headquarters, transportation headquarters and their subordinate forces, and the armed forest police general detachments.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、评定授予现役警官警衔的基本原则和标准按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 5** V. The basic principles and standards for evaluating and conferring police ranks on active-duty officers shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、首次授予现役警官警衔的批准权限：  
(一)中将、少将、大校、上校，由国务院总理、中央军事委员会主席批准授予；  
(二)中校、少校，由公安部部长、中国人民武装警察部队第一政治委员，司令员、政治委员批准授予；  
(三)上尉、中尉、少尉，由省、自治区、直辖市公安厅(局)长、中国人民武装警察部队总队第一政治委员，总队长、政治委员或者其他有警官职务任免权的军、师级单位正职首长批准授予。

**Article 6** VI. The approval authority for the initial conferment of police ranks on active-duty officers:  
(1) Lieutenant General, Major General, Senior Colonel, and Colonel shall be approved and conferred by the Premier of the State Council and the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) Lieutenant Colonel and Major shall be approved and conferred by the Minister of Public Security and the First Political Commissar, Commander, and Political Commissar of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force;  
(3) Captain, First Lieutenant, and Second Lieutenant shall be approved and conferred by the director of the public security department (bureau) of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and the First Political Commissar, Chief of the General Detachment, and Political Commissar of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force general detachment, or other commanding officers at the army or division level who have the authority to appoint and remove officer posts.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、现役警官警衔晋级的批准权限：  
(一)武警部队司令员、政治委员、副司令员、副政治委员和正军职警官警衔晋级，由国务院总理、中央军事委员会主席批准；  
(二)副军职以下警官警衔晋级，按照现役警官职务的任免权限批准。但是，下列警官警衔晋级，按照以下规定批准：  
副军职、正师职警官和担任高级专业技术职务的警官晋升为少将的，由国务院总理、中央军事委员会主席批准；  
副师职警官和担任高级专业技术职务的警官晋升为大校的，由公安部部长批准；  
正团职警官和担任中级专业技术职务的警官晋升为上校的，由中国人民武装警察部队第一政治委员、司令员、政治委员批准；  
正营职警官晋升为中校、副营职警官和担任初级专业技术职务的警官晋升为少校的，由省、自治区、直辖市公安厅(局)长、中国人民武装警察部队总队第一政治委员，总队长、政治委员或者其他有警官职务任免权的军、师级单位的正职首长批准。

**Article 7** VII. The approval authority for the promotion of police ranks of active-duty officers:  
(1) The promotion of police ranks for the Commander, Political Commissar, Deputy Commander, Deputy Political Commissar of the Armed Police Force, and officers at the corps level shall be approved by the Premier of the State Council and the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) The promotion of police ranks for officers below the corps level shall be approved in accordance with the authority for appointment and removal of active-duty officer posts. However, the promotion of police ranks for the following officers shall be approved in accordance with the following provisions:  
- The promotion to Major General for officers at the deputy corps level, officers at the division level, and officers holding senior technical posts shall be approved by the Premier of the State Council and the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
- The promotion to Senior Colonel for officers at the deputy division level and officers holding senior technical posts shall be approved by the Minister of Public Security;  
- The promotion to Colonel for officers at the regiment level and officers holding intermediate technical posts shall be approved by the First Political Commissar, Commander, and Political Commissar of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force;  
- The promotion to Lieutenant Colonel for officers at the battalion level, and the promotion to Major for officers at the deputy battalion level and officers holding junior technical posts shall be approved by the director of the public security department (bureau) of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and the First Political Commissar, Chief of the General Detachment, and Political Commissar of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force general detachment, or other commanding officers at the army or division level who have the authority to appoint and remove officer posts.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、现役警官警衔的降级、取消和剥夺，按照《中国人民解放军军官军衔条例》第六章的规定执行。

**Article 8** VIII. The demotion, cancellation, and deprivation of police ranks of active-duty officers shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VI of the Regulations on the Military Ranks of Officers of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、现役警官授衔仪式在公布授予警衔命令的同时举行。授予将官警衔的仪式，由武警总部组织实施，国务院、中央军事委员会领导同志或受国务院、中央 军事委员会委托的有关同志前往授衔。授予校官、尉官警衔的仪式，由武警总部机关、总队、支队、院校和其他军、师级单位根据具体情况组织实施。

**Article 9** IX. The ceremony for conferring police ranks on active-duty officers shall be held at the same time as the announcement of the order conferring the police ranks. The ceremony for conferring the ranks of general officers shall be organized and implemented by the Armed Police Headquarters, and leading comrades of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, or relevant comrades entrusted by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, shall attend to confer the ranks. The ceremony for conferring the ranks of field officers and company-grade officers shall be organized and implemented by the organs of the Armed Police Headquarters, general detachments, detachments, academies, and other units at the army or division level in light of specific circumstances.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、中国人民武装警察部队警官警衔的肩章、符号式样和佩带办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会颁布。

**Article 10** X. The patterns of epaulettes and insignia for police ranks of officers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and the method of wearing them shall be promulgated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、上述各条未作规定的事项，均按《中国人民解放军军官军衔条例》的有关规定执行。

**Article 11** For matters not specified in the preceding articles, the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Military Ranks of Officers of the Chinese People's Liberation Army shall be followed.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、中国人民武装警察部队实行警官警衔制度的同时，依照中央军事委员会颁布的《中国人民解放军文职干部暂行条例》，实行文职干部制度。部分现役警官改任文职干部，由武警总部结合实际情况组织实施。

**Article 12** While implementing the police officer rank system, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall also implement the civilian cadre system in accordance with the Interim Regulations on Civilian Cadres of the Chinese People's Liberation Army promulgated by the Central Military Commission. The conversion of some active-duty police officers to civilian cadres shall be organized and implemented by the General Headquarters of the Armed Police Force in light of actual circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、中国人民武装警察部队士兵，依照《中国人民解放军现役士兵服役条例》，实行士兵警衔制度，在称谓上应与人民解放军有所区别，授予士兵警衔的改称为“警士长”、“专业警士”、“警士”，士兵在警衔前冠以“武警”。其他士兵的警衔等级区分与中国人民解放军士兵军衔等级区分相同。  
中国人民武装警察部队士兵警衔制度，由武警总部组织实施。

**Article 13** Soldiers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall implement the soldier police rank system in accordance with the Regulations on Active Service Soldiers of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The titles shall be different from those of the People's Liberation Army. Those awarded soldier police ranks shall be renamed as "Police Sergeant Major", "Professional Police Sergeant", and "Police Sergeant", and soldiers shall be prefixed with "Armed Police" before their police ranks. The distinctions between grades of other soldiers' police ranks shall be the same as those of the soldiers' military ranks of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.  
The soldier police rank system of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be organized and implemented by the General Headquarters of the Armed Police Force.
