# 国防计量监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1990-04-05  
**生效日期**：1990-04-05

> (1990年4月5日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第54号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强国防计量工作的监督管理，保证军工产品(含航天产品，下同)的量值准确一致，根据《中华人民共和国计量法》第三十三条的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统的军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位(以下简称军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位)必须执行本条例。 第三条　国防计量是指军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用全过程中的计量工作。国防计量工作是国家计量工作的组成部分，在业务上接受国务院计量行政部门的指导。 第四条　国防计量实行国家法定计量单位。对军工产品特殊需要保留的非法定计量单位，由主管部门提出，经国防科学技术工业委员会(以下简称国防科工委)批准，报国务院计量行政部门备案。 第二章　计量机构 第五条　国防科工委计量管理机构，对中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统国防计量工作实施统一监督管理，其职责是： (一)贯彻执行国家计量法律、法规，制定国防计量工作方针、政策及规章制度； (二)编制与组织实施国防计量规划、计划； (三)负责国防计量考核认可工作，组织建立、调整国防计量管理与量值传递系统； (四)组织与检查国防计量工作； (五)组织研讨国内外国防计量新技术发展动态。 第六条　军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门计量管理机构，对本部门(行业)的国防计量工作实施监督管理，其职责是： (一)贯彻执行国家计量法律、法规和国防计量工作方针、政策及规章制度，制定本部门(行业)计量工作规章制度；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国防计量工作的监督管理，保证军工产品(含航天产品，下同)的量值准确一致，根据[《中华人民共和国计量法》第三十三条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国计量法.md#art-33)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with Article 33 of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and administration of national defense metrology work and ensuring the accuracy and consistency of measurement values of military products (including aerospace products, the same below).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统的军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位(以下简称军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位)必须执行本条例。

**Article 2** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the departments and units in the national defense science, technology and industry system that are engaged in the development, testing, production and use of military products (hereinafter referred to as the departments and units for the development, testing, production and use of military products) must implement these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国防计量是指军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用全过程中的计量工作。国防计量工作是国家计量工作的组成部分，在业务上接受国务院计量行政部门的指导。

**Article 3** National defense metrology shall refer to the metrology work in the entire process of development, testing, production and use of military products. The national defense metrology work shall be a component of the national metrology work and shall, in respect of its professional operations, accept the guidance of the metrology administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国防计量实行国家法定计量单位。对军工产品特殊需要保留的非法定计量单位，由主管部门提出，经国防科学技术工业委员会(以下简称国防科工委)批准，报国务院计量行政部门备案。

**Article 4** The national legal metrological units shall be applied in national defense metrology. The non-legal metrological units retained due to special needs of military products shall be proposed by the competent department, approved by the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense (hereinafter referred to as the COSTIND), and reported to the metrology administrative department under the State Council for archival filing.

### 第二章　计量机构

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国防科工委计量管理机构，对中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统国防计量工作实施统一监督管理，其职责是：  
(一)贯彻执行国家计量法律、法规，制定国防计量工作方针、政策及规章制度；  
(二)编制与组织实施国防计量规划、计划；  
(三)负责国防计量考核认可工作，组织建立、调整国防计量管理与量值传递系统；  
(四)组织与检查国防计量工作；  
(五)组织研讨国内外国防计量新技术发展动态。

**Article 5** The metrology administration body of the COSTIND shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the national defense metrology work of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the national defense science, technology and industry system, and its functions and duties shall be as follows:  
(1) to implement the national metrology laws and regulations, and formulate the guidelines, policies, rules and regulations for national defense metrology work;  
(2) to compile and organize the implementation of national defense metrology plans and programs;  
(3) to be responsible for the assessment and accreditation of national defense metrology, and organize the establishment and adjustment of the national defense metrology administration and value transfer system;  
(4) to organize and inspect the national defense metrology work;  
(5) to organize discussions on the development trends of new technologies in national defense metrology at home and abroad.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门计量管理机构，对本部门(行业)的国防计量工作实施监督管理，其职责是：  
(一)贯彻执行国家计量法律、法规和国防计量工作方针、政策及规章制度，制定本部门(行业)计量工作规章制度；  
(二)编制与组织实施本部门(行业)国防计量规划、计划；  
(三)根据国防科工委计量管理机构授权，负责本部门(行业)国防计量考核认可工作；  
(四)监督检查本部门(行业)的国防计量工作；  
(五)承办国防科工委计量管理机构交办的其他计量工作。

**Article 6** The metrology administration body of a department for the development, testing, production and use of military products shall exercise supervision and administration over the national defense metrology work of its own department (industry), and its functions and duties shall be as follows:  
(1) to implement the national metrology laws and regulations, as well as the guidelines, policies, rules and regulations for national defense metrology work, and formulate the rules and regulations for the metrology work of its own department (industry);  
(2) to compile and organize the implementation of the national defense metrology plans and programs of its own department (industry);  
(3) to be responsible for the assessment and accreditation of national defense metrology within its own department (industry) as authorized by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND;  
(4) to supervise and inspect the national defense metrology work of its own department (industry);  
(5) to undertake other metrology work assigned by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　省、自治区、直辖市主管军工任务的部门的计量管理机构，对本地区的国防计量工作实施监督管理，其职责是：  
(一)贯彻执行国家计量法律、法规和国防计量工作方针、政策及规章制度；  
(二)根据国防科工委计量管理机构授权，负责本地区国防计量考核认可工作；  
(三)监督检查和协调本地区的国防计量工作；  
(四)承办国防科工委计量管理机构交办的其他计量工作。

**Article 7** The metrology administration body of a department in charge of military tasks in a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall exercise supervision and administration over the national defense metrology work in its own region, and its functions and duties shall be as follows:  
(1) to implement the national metrology laws and regulations, as well as the guidelines, policies, rules and regulations for national defense metrology work;  
(2) to be responsible for the assessment and accreditation of national defense metrology in its own region as authorized by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND;  
(3) to supervise, inspect and coordinate the national defense metrology work in its own region;  
(4) to undertake other metrology work assigned by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国防计量技术机构分为三级：  
(一)经国防科工委批准设置的国防计量测试研究中心、计量一级站为一级，负责建立国防特殊需要的最高计量标准器具，负责国防计量量值传递和技术业务工作；  
(二)经国防科工委批准设置的国防计量区域计量站、专业计量站和军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门批准设置的计量站为二级，接受一级计量技术机构的业务指导，负责建立本地区、本部门的最高计量标准器具，负责本地区、本部门国防计量量值传递和技术业务工作；  
(三)经国防科工委计量管理机构考核认可的军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用单位计量技术机构为三级，接受一、二级计量技术机构的业务指导，负责建立本单位最高计量标准器具，负责本单位计量技术业务工作。

**Article 8** The national defense metrology technical organizations shall be divided into three levels:  
(1) the national defense metrology testing and research centers and the primary metrology stations approved by the COSTIND shall be at the first level, and shall be responsible for establishing the highest metrological standard instruments specially needed for national defense, and for the national defense metrology value transfer and technical operational work;  
(2) the regional metrology stations and specialized metrology stations of national defense metrology approved by the COSTIND, and the metrology stations approved by the departments for the development, testing, production and use of military products shall be at the second level, and shall accept the professional guidance of the first-level metrology technical organizations, and be responsible for establishing the highest metrological standard instruments of their own regions or departments, and for the national defense metrology value transfer and technical operational work within their own regions or departments;  
(3) the metrology technical organizations of units for the development, testing, production and use of military products that are assessed and accredited by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND shall be at the third level, and shall accept the professional guidance of the first-level and second-level metrology technical organizations, and be responsible for establishing the highest metrological standard instruments of their own units, and for the metrology technical operational work of their own units.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统所属的国防计量技术机构，执行本系统内的强制检定和其他检定测试任务。  
军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位生产民品的，其各项最高计量标准器具和列入国家强制检定目录的工作计量器具，根据有利生产、方便管理的原则，可由国防计量技术机构执行强制检定，也可按经济合理，就地就近的原则送当地人民政府计量行政部门执行强制检定。  
国防计量技术机构承担本系统以外的强制检定和其他检定测试任务，由国防科工委和国务院计量行政部门统筹规划，根据实际需要，按规定分级授权，并接受同级政府计量行政部门的监督。

**Article 9** The national defense metrology technical organizations belonging to the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the national defense science, technology and industry system shall perform compulsory verification and other verification and testing tasks within their own systems.  
If the departments and units for the development, testing, production and use of military products manufacture civilian products, their various highest metrological standard instruments and the working measuring instruments listed in the national compulsory verification catalog may, in accordance with the principle of facilitating production and convenient administration, be subject to compulsory verification by the national defense metrology technical organizations, or may, in accordance with the principle of economic rationality and local proximity, be submitted to the metrology administrative department of the local people's government for compulsory verification.  
Where national defense metrology technical organizations undertake compulsory verification and other verification and testing tasks outside their own systems, the COSTIND and the metrology administrative department under the State Council shall make overall plans, grant authorization at different levels according to actual needs and in accordance with regulations, and the organizations shall accept the supervision of the metrology administrative departments of the governments at the same level.

### 第三章　计量标准

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一级国防计量技术机构的各项最高计量标准器具，由国务院计量行政部门组织考核合格后使用。  
一级国防计量技术机构的最高计量标准器具，接受国家计量基准器具的量值传递。

**Article 10** The various highest metrological standard instruments of first-level national defense metrology technical organizations shall be used only after being assessed and certified as qualified by the metrology administrative department under the State Council.  
The highest metrological standard instruments of first-level national defense metrology technical organizations shall receive value transfer from the national metrological reference instruments.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　二级国防计量技术机构的各项最高计量标准器具，由国防科工委计量管理机构组织考核合格后使用，并向国务院计量行政部门备案。

**Article 11** The various highest metrological standard instruments of second-level national defense metrology technical organizations shall be used only after being assessed and certified as qualified by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND, and shall be reported to the metrology administrative department under the State Council for archival filing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　三级国防计量技术机构的各项最高计量标准器具，由省、自治区、直辖市主管军工任务的部门的计量管理机构组织考核合格后使用，并向所在省、自治区、直辖市计量行政部门备案。

**Article 12** The various highest metrological standard instruments of third-level national defense metrology technical organizations shall be used only after being assessed and certified as qualified by the metrology administration body of the department in charge of military tasks in the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the metrology administrative department of the said province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for archival filing.

### 第四章　计量检定

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位的计量标准器具以及用于军工产品质量管理、性能评定、定型鉴定和保证武器使用安全的工作计量器具，必须按规定实行计量检定，检定不合格的，不得使用。

**Article 13** The metrological standard instruments of the departments and units for the development, testing, production and use of military products, as well as the working measuring instruments used for quality management, performance evaluation, type approval and ensuring the safe use of weapons of military products, must undergo metrological verification in accordance with regulations. Those that fail the verification shall not be used.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国防计量技术机构的计量检定人员，必须经国防科工委计量管理机构或其指定的计量管理机构按技术干部和国家关于计量检定人员的要求组织考核合格。

**Article 14** The metrological verification personnel of national defense metrology technical organizations must be assessed and certified as qualified by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND or the metrology administration body designated by it, in accordance with the requirements for technical cadres and the state requirements for metrological verification personnel.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　计量检定必须按照国家计量检定系统表和计量检定规程进行。国家未制定计量检定规程的，由国防科工委制定国防计量检定规程，并向国务院计量行政部门备案。

**Article 15** Metrological verification must be carried out in accordance with the national metrological verification system tables and metrological verification regulations. If the state has not formulated metrological verification regulations, the COSTIND shall formulate national defense metrological verification regulations and report them to the metrology administrative department under the State Council for archival filing.

### 第五章　计量保证与监督

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用部门和单位的计量技术机构的计量标准器具、计量检定人员、环境条件和规章制度，经国防科工委计量管理机构或其指定的机构组织国防计量考核认可并发给证书后，方可承担军工产品研制、试验、生产、使用任务。

**Article 16** Only after the metrological standard instruments, metrological verification personnel, environmental conditions and rules and regulations of the metrology technical organizations of the departments and units for the development, testing, production and use of military products have been assessed and accredited by the metrology administration body of the COSTIND or the body designated by it and have been issued certificates, may they undertake the tasks of development, testing, production and use of military products.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军工产品研制阶段的计量保证与监督：  
(一)大型型号总体应由一名副总设计师兼任型号总计量师；  
(二)型号计量师根据型号总体或分系统的技术指标，对型号研制单位计量技术机构提出计量测试的技术指标要求；  
(三)型号计量师应提出型号总体或分系统研制过程中需要研制的计量标准器具和专用测试设备的预研课题，并组织落实承担单位及有关条件；  
(四)型号研制单位的计量技术机构，根据型号计量师的计量测试技术指标提出可行性论证方案，并组织实施；  
(五)军工产品设计定型，应当对定型委员会批准的专用测试设备和计量技术文件(包括计量检定规程)进行验收。

**Article 17** Metrological assurance and supervision during the development stage of military products:  
(1) For a large-scale model overall design, an assistant chief designer shall concurrently serve as the model chief metrologist;  
(2) The model metrologist shall, in accordance with the technical specifications of the model overall design or subsystems, put forward technical specification requirements for metrological testing to the metrology technical organization of the model development unit;  
(3) The model metrologist shall propose pre-research topics for the metrological standard instruments and special test equipment that need to be developed during the development process of the model overall design or subsystems, and organize the implementation of the undertaking units and relevant conditions;  
(4) The metrology technical organization of the model development unit shall, based on the technical specifications for metrological testing put forward by the model metrologist, propose a feasibility demonstration plan and organize its implementation;  
(5) For the design finalization of military products, the special test equipment and metrological technical documents (including metrological verification regulations) approved by the finalization committee shall be subject to acceptance check.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军工产品试验阶段的计量保证与监督：  
(一)在军工产品试验阶段中，型号计量师应提出型号总体和分系统对计量工作的要求，由相应的国防计量管理机构和技术机构组织实施。  
军工产品的计量工作应列入型号的试验大纲或试验计划；  
(二)计量器具和专用测试设备进入试验基地(靶场)，必须进行计量复查。

**Article 18** Metrological assurance and supervision during the testing stage of military products:  
(1) During the testing stage of military products, the model metrologist shall put forward the requirements of the model overall design and subsystems for metrological work, which shall be organized and implemented by the corresponding national defense metrology administration bodies and technical organizations.  
The metrological work of military products shall be included in the test outline or test plan of the model;  
(2) When measuring instruments and special test equipment enter the test base (range), they must undergo metrological re-inspection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　军工产品生产阶段的计量保证与监督：  
(一)生产单位必须按照产品的技术标准、工艺规范的要求，配备相应的计量器具和检测手段；  
(二)生产单位计量机构配备的和向使用单位验收代表提供的计量器具和检测手段，生产和使用单位应对其计量性能进行验收。

**Article 19** Metrological assurance and supervision during the production stage of military products:  
(1) The producing unit must, in accordance with the requirements of the product technical standards and process specifications, be equipped with corresponding measuring instruments and testing means;  
(2) For the measuring instruments and testing means equipped by the metrology organization of the producing unit and provided to the acceptance representative of the using unit, the producing unit and the using unit shall carry out acceptance check on their metrological performance.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　军工产品使用阶段的计量保证与监督：  
(一)军工产品研制单位应当向使用单位提出需要配备的计量测试手段和相应的计量技术文件。使用单位在接收军工产品时，必须对配套的专用测试设备及技术文件进行验收。  
(二)超过存储期需要延寿或进行技术改进的大型武器系统，必须有计量人员参与技术性能计量保证方案的论证工作。

**Article 20** Metrological assurance and supervision during the use stage of military products:  
(1) The developer of military products shall put forward to the using unit the metrological testing means and the corresponding metrological technical documents that need to be equipped. When receiving the military products, the using unit must carry out acceptance check on the supporting special test equipment and technical documents.  
(2) For large weapon systems that need to have their service life extended or undergo technical improvement beyond the storage period, metrological personnel must participate in the demonstration work of the technical performance metrological assurance plan.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军工产品的设计定型和生产定型，凡涉及产品技术指标量值的准确度，必须经国防计量技术机构签署意见后，方为有效。

**Article 21** For the design finalization and production finalization of military products, wherever the accuracy of the measurement value of product technical specifications is involved, the opinion of the national defense metrology technical organization must be affixed before the finalization shall be valid.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军工产品的质量评定、成果鉴定，必须经相应的国防计量技术机构进行计量审查，在确认测量方法正确、数据准确可靠并签署意见后，其结论方为有效。  
用于军工产品质量评定、成果鉴定的计量器具，必须经国防计量技术机构或其认可的其他计量技术机构检定合格，并在检定证书注明的有效期内使用。

**Article 22** For the quality evaluation and achievement appraisal of military products, a metrological review by the corresponding national defense metrology technical organization must be carried out. Only after confirming that the measurement method is correct and the data are accurate and reliable and affixing its opinion shall the conclusion be valid.  
The measuring instruments used for the quality evaluation and achievement appraisal of military products must be verified as qualified by the national defense metrology technical organization or other metrology technical organizations recognized by it, and shall be used within the validity period indicated in the verification certificate.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　引进军事技术和进口武器装备以及重大仪器设备，应同时引进必要的计量测试手段和技术资料。

**Article 23** When introducing military technology and importing weapons and equipment, as well as major instruments and equipment, the necessary metrological testing means and technical data shall be introduced simultaneously.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　军工产品因计量器具准确度引起的纠纷，由国防计量管理机构组织仲裁检定，并负责处理。

**Article 24** Disputes arising from the accuracy of measuring instruments in military products shall be subject to arbitration verification organized by the national defense metrology administration body, which shall be responsible for handling them.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例的，由国防计量管理机构依照《中华人民共和国计量法》的有关规定进行处理；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Anyone who violates these Regulations shall be dealt with by the national defense metrology administration body in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, the judicial authority shall, according to law, investigate the criminal liability.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国防科工委可以根据本条例制定具体实施办法。

**Article 26** The COSTIND may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate specific implementation measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例由国防科工委负责解释。

**Article 27** The COSTIND shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自发布之日起施行。一九八四年九月十日国务院、中央军委发布的《国防计量工作管理条例》即行废止。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Regulations on the Administration of National Defense Metrology Work promulgated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission on September 10, 1984 shall be abolished simultaneously.
