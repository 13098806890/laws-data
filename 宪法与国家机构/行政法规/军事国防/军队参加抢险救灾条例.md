# 军队参加抢险救灾条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2005-06-07  
**生效日期**：2005-07-01

> (2005年6月7日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第436号公布　自2005年7月1日起施行) 第一条　为了发挥中国人民解放军(以下称军队)在抢险救灾中的作用，保护人民生命和财产安全，根据国防法的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　军队是抢险救灾的突击力量，执行国家赋予的抢险救灾任务是军队的重要使命。 各级人民政府和军事机关应当按照本条例的规定，做好军队参加抢险救灾的组织、指挥、协调、保障等工作。 第三条　军队参加抢险救灾主要担负下列任务： (一)解救、转移或者疏散受困人员； (二)保护重要目标安全； (三)抢救、运送重要物资； (四)参加道路(桥梁、隧道)抢修、海上搜救、核生化救援、疫情控制、医疗救护等专业抢险； (五)排除或者控制其他危重险情、灾情。 必要时，军队可以协助地方人民政府开展灾后重建等工作。 第四条　国务院组织的抢险救灾需要军队参加的，由国务院有关主管部门向中国人民解放军总参谋部提出，中国人民解放军总参谋部按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。 县级以上地方人民政府组织的抢险救灾需要军队参加的，由县级以上地方人民政府通过当地同级军事机关提出，当地同级军事机关按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。 在险情、灾情紧急的情况下，地方人民政府可以直接向驻军部队提出救助请求，驻军部队应当按照规定立即实施救助，并向上级报告；驻军部队发现紧急险情、灾情也应当按照规定立即实施救助，并向上级报告。 抢险救灾需要动用军用飞机(直升机)、舰艇的，按照有关规定办理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发挥中国人民解放军(以下称军队)在抢险救灾中的作用，保护人民生命和财产安全，根据国防法的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the National Defense Law for the purpose of giving play to the role of the Chinese People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the military) in emergency rescue and disaster relief, and protecting the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　军队是抢险救灾的突击力量，执行国家赋予的抢险救灾任务是军队的重要使命。  
各级人民政府和军事机关应当按照本条例的规定，做好军队参加抢险救灾的组织、指挥、协调、保障等工作。

**Article 2** The military is the assault force for emergency rescue and disaster relief, and it is an important mission of the military to carry out the tasks of emergency rescue and disaster relief assigned by the State.  
People's governments at all levels and military organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, properly perform the work of organizing, commanding, coordinating, and ensuring the participation of the military in emergency rescue and disaster relief.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军队参加抢险救灾主要担负下列任务：  
(一)解救、转移或者疏散受困人员；  
(二)保护重要目标安全；  
(三)抢救、运送重要物资；  
(四)参加道路(桥梁、隧道)抢修、海上搜救、核生化救援、疫情控制、医疗救护等专业抢险；  
(五)排除或者控制其他危重险情、灾情。  
必要时，军队可以协助地方人民政府开展灾后重建等工作。

**Article 3** The military shall mainly undertake the following tasks when participating in emergency rescue and disaster relief:  
(1) rescuing, transferring, or evacuating trapped persons;  
(2) protecting the safety of important targets;  
(3) rescuing and transporting important materials;  
(4) participating in professional rescue operations such as road (bridge, tunnel) repairs, maritime search and rescue, nuclear, biological and chemical rescue, epidemic control, and medical aid;  
(5) eliminating or controlling other critical dangers and disasters.  
Where necessary, the military may assist local people's governments in post-disaster reconstruction and other work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院组织的抢险救灾需要军队参加的，由国务院有关主管部门向中国人民解放军总参谋部提出，中国人民解放军总参谋部按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。  
县级以上地方人民政府组织的抢险救灾需要军队参加的，由县级以上地方人民政府通过当地同级军事机关提出，当地同级军事机关按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。  
在险情、灾情紧急的情况下，地方人民政府可以直接向驻军部队提出救助请求，驻军部队应当按照规定立即实施救助，并向上级报告；驻军部队发现紧急险情、灾情也应当按照规定立即实施救助，并向上级报告。  
抢险救灾需要动用军用飞机(直升机)、舰艇的，按照有关规定办理。

**Article 4** Where emergency rescue and disaster relief organized by the State Council requires the participation of the military, the relevant competent department of the State Council shall submit the request to the General Staff Department of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, and the General Staff Department of the Chinese People's Liberation Army shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
Where emergency rescue and disaster relief organized by a local people's government at or above the county level requires the participation of the military, the local people's government at or above the county level shall submit the request through the local military organ at the same level, and the local military organ at the same level shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
Under urgent circumstances of danger or disaster, the local people's government may directly submit a request for assistance to the stationed troops, and the stationed troops shall immediately provide assistance in accordance with the provisions and report to their superiors; where the stationed troops discover an urgent danger or disaster, they shall also immediately provide assistance in accordance with the provisions and report to their superiors.  
Where the use of military aircraft (helicopters) or vessels is required for emergency rescue and disaster relief, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院有关主管部门、县级以上地方人民政府提出需要军队参加抢险救灾的，应当说明险情或者灾情发生的种类、时间、地域、危害程度、已经采取的措施，以及需要使用的兵力、装备等情况。

**Article 5** Where the relevant competent department of the State Council or a local people's government at or above the county level proposes that the military shall be required to participate in emergency rescue and disaster relief, it shall specify the type, time, location, degree of harm of the danger or disaster that has occurred, the measures already taken, and the troops and equipment that need to be used.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府组建的抢险救灾指挥机构，应当有当地同级军事机关的负责人参加；当地有驻军部队的，还应当有驻军部队的负责人参加。

**Article 6** The emergency rescue and disaster relief command organ established by a local people's government at or above the county level shall include the responsible person of the local military organ at the same level; where there are stationed troops in the locality, it shall also include the responsible person of the stationed troops.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军队参加抢险救灾应当在人民政府的统一领导下进行，具体任务由抢险救灾指挥机构赋予，部队的抢险救灾行动由军队负责指挥。

**Article 7** The participation of the military in emergency rescue and disaster relief shall be carried out under the unified leadership of the people's government, the specific tasks shall be assigned by the emergency rescue and disaster relief command organ, and the military's emergency rescue and disaster relief operations shall be commanded by the military.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当向当地军事机关及时通报有关险情、灾情的信息。  
在经常发生险情、灾情的地方，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织军地双方进行实地勘察和抢险救灾演习、训练。

**Article 8** A local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly notify the local military organ of information on relevant dangers and disasters.  
In places where dangers and disasters frequently occur, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize both military and local parties to conduct on-site surveys and emergency rescue and disaster relief drills and training.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省军区(卫戍区、警备区)、军分区(警备区)、县(市、市辖区)人民武装部应当及时掌握当地有关险情、灾情信息，办理当地人民政府提出的军队参加抢险救灾事宜，做好人民政府与执行抢险救灾任务的部队之间的协调工作。有关军事机关应当制定参加抢险救灾预案，组织部队开展必要的抢险救灾训练。

**Article 9** The provincial military command (garrison command, garrison district), the military sub-command (garrison district), and the county (city, municipal district) people's armed forces department shall promptly keep abreast of local information on relevant dangers and disasters, handle matters concerning the participation of the military in emergency rescue and disaster relief proposed by the local people's government, and coordinate the work between the people's government and the troops performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks. The relevant military organs shall formulate plans for participating in emergency rescue and disaster relief and organize the troops to carry out necessary training for emergency rescue and disaster relief.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军队参加抢险救灾时，当地人民政府应当提供必要的装备、物资、器材等保障，派出专业技术人员指导部队的抢险救灾行动；铁路、交通、民航、公安、电信、邮政、金融等部门和机构，应当为执行抢险救灾任务的部队提供优先、便捷的服务。  
军队执行抢险救灾任务所需要的燃油，由执行抢险救灾任务的部队和当地人民政府共同组织保障。

**Article 10** When the military participates in emergency rescue and disaster relief, the local people's government shall provide necessary support such as equipment, materials, and facilities, and dispatch professional technical personnel to guide the military's emergency rescue and disaster relief operations; the departments and institutions such as railway, transportation, civil aviation, public security, telecommunications, postal services, and finance shall provide priority and convenient services to the troops performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks.  
The fuel needed by the military for performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks shall be organized and ensured jointly by the troops performing the tasks and the local people's government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军队参加抢险救灾需要动用作战储备物资和装备器材的，必须按照规定报经批准。对消耗的部队携行装备器材和作战储备物资、装备器材，应当及时补充。

**Article 11** Where the use of combat reserve materials and equipment is required for the military's participation in emergency rescue and disaster relief, approval must be obtained in accordance with the provisions. The consumed equipment carried by the troops and combat reserve materials and equipment shall be promptly replenished.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　灾害发生地人民政府应当协助执行抢险救灾任务的部队做好饮食、住宿、供水、供电、供暖、医疗和卫生防病等必需的保障工作。  
地方人民政府与执行抢险救灾任务的部队应当互相通报疫情，共同做好卫生防疫工作。

**Article 12** The people's government of the disaster-affected area shall assist the troops performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks in properly carrying out the necessary support work such as food, accommodation, water supply, power supply, heating, medical care, and disease prevention.  
The local people's government and the troops performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks shall notify each other of epidemic situations and jointly perform the work of sanitation and epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军队参加国务院组织的抢险救灾所耗费用由中央财政负担。军队参加地方人民政府组织的抢险救灾所耗费用由地方财政负担。  
前款所指的费用包括：购置专用物资和器材费用，指挥通信、装备维修、燃油、交通运输等费用，补充消耗的携行装备器材和作战储备物资费用，以及人员生活、医疗的补助费用。  
抢险救灾任务完成后，军队有关部门应当及时统计军队执行抢险救灾任务所耗费用，报抢险救灾指挥机构审核。

**Article 13** The expenses incurred by the military for participating in emergency rescue and disaster relief organized by the State Council shall be borne by the central finance. The expenses incurred by the military for participating in emergency rescue and disaster relief organized by a local people's government shall be borne by the local finance.  
The expenses referred to in the preceding paragraph include: expenses for purchasing special materials and equipment; expenses for command communications, equipment maintenance, fuel, transportation, etc.; expenses for replenishing consumed carried equipment and combat reserve materials; and subsidies for personnel living expenses and medical care.  
After the emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks are completed, the relevant departments of the military shall promptly calculate the expenses incurred by the military in performing the emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks and submit them to the emergency rescue and disaster relief command organ for review.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院有关主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当在险情、灾情频繁发生或者列为灾害重点监视防御的地区储备抢险救灾专用装备、物资和器材，保障抢险救灾需要。

**Article 14** The relevant competent departments of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall stockpile special equipment, materials, and facilities for emergency rescue and disaster relief in areas where dangers and disasters frequently occur or which are designated as key areas for disaster monitoring and prevention, so as to ensure the needs of emergency rescue and disaster relief.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军队参加重大抢险救灾行动的宣传报道，由国家和军队有关主管部门统一组织实施。新闻单位采访、报道军队参加抢险救灾行动，应当遵守国家和军队的有关规定。

**Article 15** The publicity and reporting of the military's participation in major emergency rescue and disaster relief operations shall be uniformly organized and implemented by the relevant competent departments of the State and the military. When news organizations interview or report on the military's participation in emergency rescue and disaster relief operations, they shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对在执行抢险救灾任务中有突出贡献的军队单位和个人，按照国家和军队的有关规定给予奖励；对死亡或者致残的人员，按照国家有关规定给予抚恤优待。

**Article 16** Military units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in performing emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks shall be given rewards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military; those who have died or become disabled shall be given pensions and preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中国人民武装警察部队参加抢险救灾，参照本条例执行。

**Article 17** The participation of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force in emergency rescue and disaster relief shall be implemented by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例自2005年7月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2005.
