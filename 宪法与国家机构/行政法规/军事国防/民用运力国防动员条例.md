# 民用运力国防动员条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2004-01-01

> (2003年9月11日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第391号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了维护国家主权、统一、领土完整和安全，保证有效地组织和实施民用运力国防动员，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　民用运力国防动员，包括动员准备和动员实施。 在战时及平时特殊情况下，根据国防动员需要，国家有权依法对机关、社会团体、企业、事业单位和公民个人(以下简称单位和个人)所拥有或者管理的民用运载工具及相关设备、设施、人员，进行统一组织和调用。 国家在和平时期进行民用运力国防动员准备，增强动员潜力，保障战时及平时特殊情况下实施民用运力国防动员的需要。 第三条　一切拥有或者管理民用运力的单位和个人都应当依法履行民用运力国防动员义务。 因履行民用运力国防动员义务而遭受直接财产损失、人员伤亡的，依法享有获得补偿、抚恤的权利。 第四条　国家国防动员机构在国务院和中央军事委员会领导下，负责组织领导全国的民用运力国防动员工作。 军区国防动员机构负责组织领导本区域的民用运力国防动员工作。 县级以上地方各级国防动员机构负责组织领导本行政区域的民用运力国防动员工作。 第五条　国家国防交通主管机构负责具体实施全国的民用运力国防动员工作。 军区国防交通主管机构负责具体实施本区域的有关民用运力国防动员工作。 县级以上地方各级人民政府国防交通主管机构负责具体实施本行政区域的民用运力国防动员工作。 第六条　各级国民经济动员机构、人民武装动员机构和县级以上人民政府交通运输管理部门以及其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的民用运力国防动员工作。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国家主权、统一、领土完整和安全，保证有效地组织和实施民用运力国防动员，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws for the purposes of safeguarding national sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity and security, and ensuring the effective organization and implementation of national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　民用运力国防动员，包括动员准备和动员实施。  
在战时及平时特殊情况下，根据国防动员需要，国家有权依法对机关、社会团体、企业、事业单位和公民个人(以下简称单位和个人)所拥有或者管理的民用运载工具及相关设备、设施、人员，进行统一组织和调用。  
国家在和平时期进行民用运力国防动员准备，增强动员潜力，保障战时及平时特殊情况下实施民用运力国防动员的需要。

**Article 2** National defense mobilization of civil transport capacity includes mobilization preparation and mobilization implementation.  
In wartime and special circumstances in peacetime, according to the needs of national defense mobilization, the State has the right to, according to law, carry out unified organization and deployment of civil transport vehicles and related equipment, facilities and personnel owned or managed by state organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions and individual citizens (hereinafter referred to as units and individuals).  
The State shall carry out preparation for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity in peacetime, enhance mobilization potential, and ensure the needs for implementing national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity in wartime and special circumstances in peacetime.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　一切拥有或者管理民用运力的单位和个人都应当依法履行民用运力国防动员义务。  
因履行民用运力国防动员义务而遭受直接财产损失、人员伤亡的，依法享有获得补偿、抚恤的权利。

**Article 3** All units and individuals that own or manage civil transport capacity shall, according to law, fulfill the obligation of national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity.  
Those that suffer direct property losses, personal injury or death due to fulfilling the obligation of national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity shall, according to law, have the right to obtain compensation and pension.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家国防动员机构在国务院和中央军事委员会领导下，负责组织领导全国的民用运力国防动员工作。  
军区国防动员机构负责组织领导本区域的民用运力国防动员工作。  
县级以上地方各级国防动员机构负责组织领导本行政区域的民用运力国防动员工作。

**Article 4** The national defense mobilization agency of the State shall, under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, be responsible for organizing and leading the national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity nationwide.  
The national defense mobilization agency of a military region shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity in its region.  
The national defense mobilization agencies of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家国防交通主管机构负责具体实施全国的民用运力国防动员工作。  
军区国防交通主管机构负责具体实施本区域的有关民用运力国防动员工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府国防交通主管机构负责具体实施本行政区域的民用运力国防动员工作。

**Article 5** The national competent authority for national defense transportation shall be responsible for the specific implementation of the national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity nationwide.  
The competent authority for national defense transportation of a military region shall be responsible for the specific implementation of the relevant national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity in its region.  
The competent authorities for national defense transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the specific implementation of the national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级国民经济动员机构、人民武装动员机构和县级以上人民政府交通运输管理部门以及其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的民用运力国防动员工作。

**Article 6** The national economic mobilization agencies, the people's armed mobilization agencies at all levels, and the transportation administration departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, as well as other relevant departments, shall be responsible for the relevant national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，加强民用运力国防动员准备工作，将民用运力国防动员准备工作纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，增强动员潜力，支持和督促其有关部门依法履行职责，落实民用运力国防动员的各项工作。

**Article 7** People's governments at or above the county level shall take effective measures to strengthen the preparation for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, incorporate the preparation for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity into the national economic and social development plan, enhance mobilization potential, and support and supervise their relevant departments to perform their duties according to law and implement all work on national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家支持、鼓励单位和个人建造、购买、经营平战结合的民用运载工具及相关设备，按照有关规定给予扶持。

**Article 8** The State shall support and encourage units and individuals to construct, purchase and operate civil transport vehicles and related equipment that combine peacetime and wartime functions, and shall grant support in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　单位和个人在民用运力国防动员工作中做出突出贡献，有下列情形之一的，依照国家和地方有关规定给予表彰、奖励：  
(一)提供重要或者急需的民用运力，在保障军事行动中作用明显的；  
(二)组织和开展民用运力国防动员活动，取得突出成绩的；  
(三)坚决执行民用运力国防动员命令，克服困难，出色完成任务的；  
(四)勇于同干扰和破坏民用运力国防动员的行为作斗争，避免重大损失的；  
(五)在民用运载工具及相关设备贯彻国防要求或者加装改造方面，有重大发明创造，军事或者经济效益显著的。

**Article 9** Units or individuals that make outstanding contributions to the work of national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity and fall under any of the following circumstances shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and localities, be commended and rewarded:  
(1) providing important or urgently needed civil transport capacity, with a clear effect in ensuring military operations;  
(2) organizing and carrying out activities on national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity and achieving outstanding results;  
(3) resolutely executing orders on national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, overcoming difficulties, and accomplishing tasks with distinction;  
(4) courageously combating acts that interfere with or undermine national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, thereby avoiding significant losses;  
(5) making major inventions or creations in the implementation of national defense requirements or retrofitting of civil transport vehicles and related equipment, with remarkable military or economic benefits.

### 第二章　民用运力国防动员的准备

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家国防交通主管机构应当会同国家国民经济动员机构、国务院交通运输管理部门和其他有关部门、军队有关部门，根据民用运载工具及相关设备的设计、建造情况，按照突出重点、注重实效的原则，拟订新建民用运载工具及相关设备贯彻国防要求的总体规划，报国家国防动员机构批准。  
国家国民经济动员机构应当根据批准的总体规划，拟订新建民用运载工具及相关设备贯彻国防要求的具体实施计划并组织实施。

**Article 10** The national competent authority for national defense transportation shall, together with the national economic mobilization agency, the transportation administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments, and the relevant departments of the military, formulate a general plan for implementing national defense requirements in newly constructed civil transport vehicles and related equipment based on the design and construction of civil transport vehicles and related equipment, and in accordance with the principles of highlighting key points and emphasizing practical results, and shall submit it to the national defense mobilization agency for approval.  
The national economic mobilization agency shall, based on the approved general plan, formulate specific implementation plans for implementing national defense requirements in newly constructed civil transport vehicles and related equipment and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院交通运输管理部门和其他有关部门，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，应当加强对本行业、本行政区域内设计、建造民用运载工具及相关设备贯彻国防要求工作的管理和指导，为承担设计、建造任务的单位和个人提供政策和技术支持，保障有关国防要求的落实。

**Article 11** The transport management department of the State Council and other relevant departments, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall strengthen the management and guidance of the work of implementing national defense requirements in the design and construction of civil means of transport and related equipment within their respective industries and administrative regions, provide policy and technical support to the entities and individuals undertaking the design and construction tasks, and ensure the implementation of relevant national defense requirements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设计、建造列入贯彻国防要求具体实施计划的民用运载工具及相关设备的单位和个人，必须严格按照贯彻国防要求的设计标准和技术规范进行设计、建造。  
出资建造民用运载工具及相关设备的单位和个人，不得阻碍设计、建造单位和个人为贯彻国防要求所进行的设计、建造活动。  
设计、建造民用运载工具及相关设备，因贯彻国防要求所发生的费用，由中央财政和县级以上地方各级财政给予适当补助。具体办法由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 12** Entities and individuals that design and construct civil means of transport and related equipment included in the specific implementation plan for implementing national defense requirements must strictly design and construct in accordance with the design standards and technical specifications for implementing national defense requirements.  
  
Entities and individuals that fund the construction of civil means of transport and related equipment shall not obstruct the design and construction activities carried out by the designing and constructing entities and individuals for implementing national defense requirements.  
  
Expenses incurred due to implementing national defense requirements in the design and construction of civil means of transport and related equipment shall be appropriately subsidized by the central finance and the local finances at or above the county level. The specific measures shall be formulated by the national defense transportation authority of the state in conjunction with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具及相关设备竣工验收时，下达任务的机构和有关国防交通主管机构应当参加验收并签署意见，验收合格并经所在地国防交通主管机构登记后，方可交付使用。

**Article 13** When the civil means of transport and related equipment implementing national defense requirements undergo completion acceptance, the organization that assigned the task and the relevant defense transportation authority shall participate in the acceptance and sign opinions. Only after the acceptance is passed and the matter is registered with the local defense transportation authority may it be delivered for use.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府交通运输管理部门、公安交通管理部门和其他有关部门，应当结合本部门年度的交通工具统计、登记和审验(核)工作，按照民用运力国防动员准备登记的要求，于每年1月31日前，向同级国防交通主管机构报送上一年度民用运力登记的有关资料和情况。  
报送的民用运力资料和情况不符合规定要求的，国防交通主管机构可以要求前款所列有关部门按照规定要求重新提供，有关部门不得拒绝。

**Article 14** The transport management department, the public security traffic management department, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the annual statistics, registration, and inspection (verification) of means of transport of their respective departments, and in accordance with the requirements for registration in preparation for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, submit to the defense transportation authority at the same level the relevant materials and information on the registration of civil transport capacity for the preceding year before January 31 each year.  
  
If the submitted civil transport capacity materials and information do not meet the prescribed requirements, the defense transportation authority may require the relevant departments mentioned in the preceding paragraph to re-submit them in accordance with the prescribed requirements, and the relevant departments shall not refuse.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级国防交通主管机构应当对民用运力资料和情况分类整理，登记造册，妥善保管，及时更新。下级国防交通主管机构应当按照民用运力国防动员的要求将本级民用运力情况报送上一级国防交通主管机构；同时根据需要，及时向军队有关单位通报本地区的民用运力情况。  
国防交通主管机构以及获得情况通报的军队有关单位对民用运力资料和情况负有保密义务。

**Article 15** The defense transportation authorities at all levels shall classify, sort, register, keep properly, and update promptly the civil transport capacity materials and information. The subordinate defense transportation authority shall, in accordance with the requirements for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, submit the civil transport capacity information of its own level to the defense transportation authority at the next higher level; and at the same time, as needed, promptly notify the relevant military units of the civil transport capacity situation in its region.  
  
The defense transportation authority and the relevant military units that have received the notification have the obligation to keep the civil transport capacity materials and information confidential.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军队、人民武装警察部队、民兵组织，应当根据所担负的任务，评估和测算民用运力国防动员需求，并按照规定的程序将所需民用运载工具及相关设备的类型、数量及其技术要求等情况报送有关国防交通主管机构。

**Article 16** The armed forces, the People's Armed Police, and the militia organizations shall, based on the tasks they undertake, assess and calculate the needs for national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, and submit the types, quantities, and technical requirements of the required civil means of transport and related equipment, among other information, to the relevant defense transportation authority in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国防交通主管机构应当根据民用运力情况和使用单位提出的需求，组织拟订民用运力国防动员预案。  
全国民用运力国防动员预案，由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门拟订，报国家国防动员机构批准。  
军区民用运力国防动员预案，由军区国防交通主管机构根据全国民用运力国防动员预案，会同军区有关部门和区域内的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门拟订，报军区国防动员机构批准，并报国家国防交通主管机构备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市民用运力国防动员预案，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国防交通主管机构根据军区民用运力国防动员预案，会同本级人民政府有关部门和同级军事机关拟订，报本级国防动员机构批准，并报军区国防交通主管机构备案。

**Article 17** The defense transportation authority shall organize the formulation of a national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity based on the civil transport capacity situation and the needs put forward by the using units.  
  
The national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity nationwide shall be formulated by the national defense transportation authority of the state in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the armed forces, and shall be submitted to the national defense mobilization authority for approval.  
  
The national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity of a military region shall be formulated by the defense transportation authority of the military region based on the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity nationwide, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the military region and the relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government within the region, and shall be submitted to the defense mobilization authority of the military region for approval and reported to the national defense transportation authority of the state for record.  
  
The national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the defense transportation authority of the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government based on the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity of the military region, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and the military organs at the same level, and shall be submitted to the defense mobilization authority at the same level for approval and reported to the defense transportation authority of the military region for record.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海军、空军、第二炮兵(以下简称军兵种)根据所担负的特殊任务，需要单独制定民用运力国防动员预案的，经国家国防交通主管机构同意后，由军兵种主管国防交通工作的机构会同有关军区和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的国防交通主管机构以及其他有关部门拟订；经所属的军兵种审核同意后，报国家国防动员机构批准。

**Article 18** If the Navy, the Air Force, or the Second Artillery Force (hereinafter referred to as the military branches) need to separately formulate a national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity based on the special tasks they undertake, after obtaining the consent of the national defense transportation authority of the state, the plan shall be formulated by the organ in charge of defense transportation work of the military branch in conjunction with the defense transportation authority of the relevant military region and the defense transportation authorities and other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; after being reviewed and approved by the military branch to which they belong, it shall be submitted to the national defense mobilization authority for approval.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民用运力国防动员预案应当明确动员的任务、程序、要求和保障措施，便于操作执行，能够满足军事行动的需要。

**Article 19** The national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity shall specify the tasks, procedures, requirements, and guarantee measures of mobilization, be easy to operate and implement, and be able to meet the needs of military operations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民用运力国防动员预案的调整，按照原拟订程序和批准权限办理。

**Article 20** Adjustments to the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity shall be handled in accordance with the original formulation procedures and approval authority.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国防交通主管机构应当会同人民武装动员机构，根据民用运力国防动员预案，组织和指导有关部门确定预征民用运力，并将预征民用运载工具及相关设备的类型、数量、技术标准和对操作、保障人员的要求通知有关单位和个人。  
接到通知的单位和个人，应当按照要求做好预征民用运力的组织、技术保障等准备工作。

**Article 21** The competent department of national defense transportation shall, in conjunction with the people's armed mobilization department, in accordance with the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity, organize and guide the relevant departments to determine the pre-levied civil transport capacity, and shall notify the relevant units and individuals of the types, quantities, technical standards of the pre-levied civil transport vehicles and related equipment, and the requirements for operators and support personnel.  
The units and individuals receiving the notice shall, in accordance with the requirements, make preparations for the organization and technical support of the pre-levied civil transport capacity.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　预征民用运载工具及相关设备，需要进行加装改造论证和试验的，由国防交通主管机构会同同级国民经济动员机构，根据民用运力国防动员预案制定实施方案，并组织实施。其中重大论证课题和试验项目的实施方案应当报国家国防动员机构批准；涉及加装武器装备的，按照武器装备加装改造的有关规定办理。

**Article 22** Where pre-levied civil transport vehicles and related equipment need to undergo refit and modification demonstration and testing, the competent department of national defense transportation shall, in conjunction with the national economic mobilization department at the same level, formulate an implementation plan in accordance with the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity, and organize its implementation. Among these, the implementation plan for major demonstration topics and test projects shall be submitted to the national defense mobilization department for approval; where installation of weapons and equipment is involved, it shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on the installation and modification of weapons and equipment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承担民用运载工具及相关设备加装改造论证课题和试验项目的单位，应当按照规定的时间与要求完成论证和试验任务，并将论证结论和试验结果的资料报送国防交通主管机构和国民经济动员机构。  
拥有或者管理需要加装改造的预征民用运载工具及相关设备的单位和个人，应当向承担加装改造论证课题和试验项目的单位提供民用运载工具及相关设备的原始资料和情况，为加装改造论证课题和试验项目的顺利实施提供方便。

**Article 23** The units undertaking the demonstration topics and test projects for the refit and modification of civil transport vehicles and related equipment shall complete the demonstration and test tasks within the specified time and in accordance with the requirements, and shall submit the materials on the demonstration conclusions and test results to the competent department of national defense transportation and the national economic mobilization department.  
The units and individuals that own or manage the pre-levied civil transport vehicles and related equipment requiring refit and modification shall provide the units undertaking the demonstration topics and test projects for refit and modification with the original materials and information on the civil transport vehicles and related equipment, and shall facilitate the smooth implementation of the demonstration topics and test projects for refit and modification.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国民经济动员机构和国防交通主管机构，应当根据加装改造任务的要求，指导、帮助有关单位建立和完善加装改造的技术、材料及相关设备的储备制度。

**Article 24** The national economic mobilization department and the competent department of national defense transportation shall, in accordance with the requirements of the refit and modification tasks, guide and assist the relevant units in establishing and improving the reserve system for refit and modification technologies, materials, and related equipment.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民武装动员机构应当会同国防交通主管机构和军队负责军事交通运输工作的部门，根据民用运力国防动员准备要求，结合预征民用运力担负的运输生产任务，组织预征民用运力进行必要的军事训练和专业技术训练。  
参加预征民用运力训练的人员，训练期间的误工补贴或者在原单位的工资、奖金、福利待遇以及伙食补助、往返差旅费等，训练人员纳入民兵组织的，依照国家有关民兵参加军事训练的规定执行；训练人员未纳入民兵组织的，参照国家有关民兵参加军事训练的规定执行。

**Article 25** The people's armed mobilization department shall, in conjunction with the competent department of national defense transportation and the department of the armed forces responsible for military transportation, in accordance with the requirements for the preparation of national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity, and taking into account the transportation production tasks undertaken by the pre-levied civil transport capacity, organize the pre-levied civil transport capacity to undergo necessary military training and professional technical training.  
For personnel participating in the training of pre-levied civil transport capacity, the subsidies for lost working hours or the wages, bonuses, welfare benefits, meal subsidies, and round-trip travel expenses during the training period, if the training personnel are included in the militia organization, shall be executed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state on militia participation in military training; if the training personnel are not included in the militia organization, the relevant provisions of the state on militia participation in military training shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国防交通主管机构应当会同同级人民政府交通运输管理部门和其他有关部门，加强对预征民用运力的动态管理，建立相应的管理制度，采用先进的科技手段收集和掌握预征民用运力的动态信息。  
拥有或者管理预征民用运力的单位和个人，应当按照规定及时向国防交通主管机构报送预征民用运力的变动情况。

**Article 26** The competent department of national defense transportation shall, in conjunction with the transportation management department of the people's government at the same level and other relevant departments, strengthen the dynamic management of pre-levied civil transport capacity, establish corresponding management systems, and adopt advanced scientific and technological means to collect and master dynamic information on pre-levied civil transport capacity.  
The units and individuals that own or manage pre-levied civil transport capacity shall, in accordance with the provisions, timely report to the competent department of national defense transportation on the changes in the pre-levied civil transport capacity.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　军区级以上单位批准的军事训练、演习，可以征用民用运力。  
军事训练、演习需要征用民用运力的，按照中央军事委员会规定的程序报军区级以上单位批准后，由国防交通主管机构根据民用运力国防动员预案组织实施。  
军事训练、演习征用民用运力的补偿费用，按照租用方式计价结算。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门制定。

**Article 27** Military training and exercises approved by units at or above the military region level may requisition civil transport capacity.  
Where military training and exercises require the requisition of civil transport capacity, after reporting to and obtaining approval from units at or above the military region level in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Central Military Commission, the competent department of national defense transportation shall organize the implementation in accordance with the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity.  
The compensation fees for the requisition of civil transport capacity for military training and exercises shall be calculated and settled by means of leasing. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the armed forces.

### 第三章　民用运力国防动员的实施

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　战时的民用运力国防动员，依据国家主席发布的动员令实施。  
平时特殊情况下的民用运力国防动员，依据国务院、中央军事委员会发布的民用运力国防动员决定实施。

**Article 28** The national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity in wartime shall be implemented in accordance with the mobilization order issued by the President of the State.  
The national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity under special circumstances in peacetime shall be implemented in accordance with the decision on national defense mobilization of civil transport capacity issued by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国防交通主管机构应当根据上级下达的民用运力国防动员任务和使用单位提出的申请，按照快速动员的要求，迅速启动、实施民用运力国防动员预案。  
在实施民用运力国防动员预案的过程中，需要对预案进行调整的，按照规定程序、权限办理。

**Article 29** The competent department of national defense transportation shall, in accordance with the national defense mobilization tasks for civil transport capacity assigned by higher authorities and the applications submitted by the using units, and in compliance with the requirements for rapid mobilization, promptly activate and implement the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity.  
Where adjustments to the plan are necessary during the process of implementing the national defense mobilization plan for civil transport capacity, it shall be handled in accordance with the prescribed procedures and authority.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国防交通主管机构应当会同人民武装动员机构，按照上级下达的民用运力国防动员要求，通知被征民用运力的单位和个人，明确其被征民用运载工具及相关设备的类型、数量和操作、保障人员，以及民用运力集结的时间、地点和方式。  
被征民用运力的单位和个人必须按照通知要求，组织被征民用运力在规定时限内到达集结地点，并保证被征民用运载工具及相关设备的技术状态和操作、保障人员的技能符合军事行动的要求。

**Article 30** The competent department of national defense transportation shall, in conjunction with the people's armed mobilization department, in accordance with the national defense mobilization requirements for civil transport capacity assigned by higher authorities, notify the units and individuals whose civil transport capacity is requisitioned, specifying the types, quantities of the requisitioned civil transport vehicles and related equipment and the operators and support personnel, as well as the time, place, and manner of assembly of the civil transport capacity.  
The units and individuals whose civil transport capacity is requisitioned must, in accordance with the requirements of the notice, organize the requisitioned civil transport capacity to arrive at the assembly point within the prescribed time limit, and ensure that the technical condition of the requisitioned civil transport vehicles and related equipment and the skills of the operators and support personnel meet the requirements of military operations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被征民用运力集结地的人民武装动员机构应当会同国防交通主管机构及有关部门组成精干的指挥机构，对集结后的民用运力进行登记编组，查验整备情况，组织必要的应急训练，保证按时交付使用单位；被征民用运力来不及集结的，人民武装动员机构可以与使用单位商定报到时间和地点，并立即通知被征民用运力的单位和个人。被征民用运力交付使用单位时，应当办理交接手续。  
被征民用运力交接后，有关民用运载工具及相关设备的安全防护、后勤保障和装备维修等，由使用单位负责，其执行任务所在地的人民政府予以协助。

**Article 31** The people's armed mobilization organ at the assembly site of the requisitioned civil transport capacity shall, together with the competent department of national defense transportation and relevant departments, form a streamlined command organ, register and organize the assembled civil transport capacity, inspect the readiness status, organize necessary emergency training, and ensure delivery to the using unit on time; if the requisitioned civil transport capacity cannot be assembled in time, the people's armed mobilization organ may agree with the using unit on the reporting time and place, and shall immediately notify the unit or individual whose civil transport capacity is requisitioned. When the requisitioned civil transport capacity is delivered to the using unit, handover procedures shall be completed.  
After the handover of the requisitioned civil transport capacity, the using unit shall be responsible for the safety protection, logistics support, and equipment maintenance of the relevant civil means of transport and related equipment, and the people's government at the place where the task is performed shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被征民用运载工具及相关设备需要加装改造的，由国民经济动员机构会同国防交通主管机构和使用单位，按照民用运力国防动员预案确定的加装改造方案组织实施。  
承担民用运载工具及相关设备加装改造任务的单位和个人，应当严格按照国家安全技术标准和国防要求进行加装改造，保证按期交付使用。

**Article 32** Where the requisitioned civil means of transport and related equipment need to be retrofitted or modified, the national economic mobilization organ shall, together with the competent department of national defense transportation and the using unit, organize the implementation in accordance with the retrofitting and modification plan determined in the civil transport capacity national defense mobilization plan.  
The units or individuals undertaking the task of retrofitting or modifying the civil means of transport and related equipment shall strictly carry out the retrofitting or modification in accordance with national safety technical standards and national defense requirements, and shall ensure delivery for use on schedule.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民用运力国防动员实施过程中，因情况紧急来不及报告的，使用单位可以按照民用运力国防动员预案直接在当地征用所需的民用运力，但必须同时按照规定的程序补报。

**Article 33** During the implementation of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization, if, due to urgent circumstances, there is no time to report, the using unit may directly requisition the required civil transport capacity locally in accordance with the civil transport capacity national defense mobilization plan, but must simultaneously file a supplementary report in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　民用运力国防动员实施过程中，需要使用港口、码头、机场、车站和其他设施的，由国防交通主管机构事先向有关部门或者单位提出使用要求，有关部门和单位应当予以配合、支持。

**Article 34** During the implementation of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization, if it is necessary to use ports, wharves, airports, stations, and other facilities, the competent department of national defense transportation shall, in advance, put forward usage requirements to the relevant departments or units, and the relevant departments and units shall provide cooperation and support.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　使用民用运力的单位应当尽最大可能保证人员安全，并尽量避免民用运载工具及相关设备、设施受到损毁。

**Article 35** The unit using civil transport capacity shall do its utmost to ensure personnel safety and shall try its best to avoid damage to civil means of transport and related equipment and facilities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院、中央军事委员会根据民用运力国防动员的紧急需要，决定对某一行业或者地区的民用运力实施管制时，被实施民用运力管制的单位和个人，必须服从管制指挥机构的统一指挥，保证其拥有或者管理的民用运载工具及相关设备、设施和保障系统处于良好状态。

**Article 36** When the State Council and the Central Military Commission decide, based on the urgent needs of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization, to implement control over civil transport capacity in a certain industry or region, the units and individuals subject to the civil transport capacity control must obey the unified command of the control command organ and ensure that the civil means of transport and related equipment, facilities, and support systems they own or manage are in good condition.

### 第四章　补偿与抚恤

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　民用运力国防动员任务完成后，使用民用运力的单位应当收拢民用运力，清查动员民用运力数量，统计民用运载工具及相关设备、设施的损失、损坏情况以及操作、保障人员的伤亡情况，按照有关规定办理移交手续，并出具民用运力使用、损毁情况证明。

**Article 37** After the completion of the civil transport capacity national defense mobilization task, the unit using civil transport capacity shall gather the civil transport capacity, check and count the quantity of mobilized civil transport capacity, compile statistics on the loss and damage of civil means of transport and related equipment and facilities, as well as the casualties of operators and support personnel, handle the transfer procedures in accordance with relevant provisions, and issue a certificate of the use and damage of the civil transport capacity.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　加装改造的民用运载工具及相关设备需要并能够恢复原有功能的，国民经济动员机构应当在移交前会同国防交通主管机构和使用单位组织实施恢复工作。恢复工作完成并通过相应的检验后，应当及时移交。  
加装改造的民用运载工具及相关设备不影响原使用功能的，可以不实施恢复工作。国防交通主管机构应当登记造册，列为民用运力国防动员储备。

**Article 38** Where the retrofitted or modified civil means of transport and related equipment need to and can be restored to their original functions, the national economic mobilization organ shall, before the transfer, together with the competent department of national defense transportation and the using unit, organize the implementation of restoration work. After the restoration work is completed and passes the corresponding inspection, the transfer shall be made in a timely manner.  
If the retrofitted or modified civil means of transport and related equipment do not affect the original functions, restoration work may not be implemented. The competent department of national defense transportation shall register and list them as civil transport capacity national defense mobilization reserves.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　拥有或者管理民用运力的单位和个人，因履行民用运力国防动员义务造成的下列直接财产损失，由中央财政和县级以上地方各级财政给予适当补偿：  
(一)民用运载工具及相关设备和港口、码头、机场、车站等设施的灭失、损坏、折旧；  
(二)民用运载工具及相关设备和港口、码头、机场、车站等设施的操作、保障人员的工资或者津贴；  
(三)应当给予合理补偿的其他直接财产损失。  
补偿的具体办法，由国务院财政部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 39** The units or individuals that own or manage civil transport capacity shall be given appropriate compensation by the central finance and the local finances at or above the county level for the following direct property losses caused by the performance of the civil transport capacity national defense mobilization obligation:  
(1) loss, damage, or depreciation of civil means of transport and related equipment and facilities such as ports, wharves, airports, and stations;  
(2) salaries or allowances of operators and support personnel of civil means of transport and related equipment and facilities such as ports, wharves, airports, and stations;  
(3) other direct property losses for which reasonable compensation shall be given.  
The specific measures for compensation shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council together with relevant departments.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　拥有或者管理民用运力的单位和个人，凭使用单位出具的使用、损毁证明，向当地的国防交通主管机构申报，经国防交通主管机构审核情况属实，并报有关人民政府批准后，由当地的国防交通主管机构负责在规定的期限内实施补偿。

**Article 40** The units or individuals that own or manage civil transport capacity shall, on the basis of the use and damage certificate issued by the using unit, declare to the local competent department of national defense transportation; after the competent department of national defense transportation verifies the facts and reports to the relevant people's government for approval, the local competent department of national defense transportation shall be responsible for implementing the compensation within the prescribed period.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　拥有或者管理民用运力的单位和个人，因履行民用运力国防动员义务遭受人员伤亡的，其抚恤优待的办法和标准，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人事务部门依照《军人抚恤优待条例》的规定执行。

**Article 41** Where any entity or individual that owns or manages civil transport capacity sustains casualties due to fulfilling the obligations of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization, the measures and standards for their consolation and preferential treatment shall be implemented by the veterans affairs department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Consolation and Preferential Treatment for Servicemen.

### 第五章　经费保障

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民用运力国防动员所需费用，由中央财政和县级以上地方各级财政共同负担。  
中央财政负担的费用，列入中央预算；县级以上地方各级财政负担的费用，列入本级政府预算。

**Article 42** The expenses needed for civil transport capacity national defense mobilization shall be jointly borne by the central finance and local finances at or above the county level.  
The expenses borne by the central finance shall be included in the central budget; the expenses borne by local finances at or above the county level shall be included in the budgets of the respective governments.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民用运力国防动员准备所需费用，由国家和县级以上地方各级人民政府国防交通主管机构，根据本年度民用运力国防动员工作任务编制预算，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 43** The expenses needed for civil transport capacity national defense mobilization preparation shall be budgeted by the national and local competent departments for national defense transportation of the people's governments at or above the county level based on the tasks of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization for the current year, and the budgets shall be submitted to the people's governments at the corresponding levels for approval.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　民用运力国防动员实施所需费用，按照国家在战时及平时特殊情况下有关国防动员经费保障办法执行。

**Article 44** The expenses needed for the implementation of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization shall be executed in accordance with the national measures for ensuring national defense mobilization funds under wartime and peacetime special circumstances.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　民用运力国防动员经费应当专款专用，并接受财政、审计部门的监督。

**Article 45** The funds for civil transport capacity national defense mobilization shall be used for designated purposes only and shall be subject to the supervision of the finance and audit departments.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例的规定，预征民用运力的单位或者个人逃避或者拒不履行民用运力国防动员义务的，由设区的市级人民政府国防交通主管机构责令改正；拒不改正的，强制其履行义务，可以对单位处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对个人处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where any entity or individual that has been pre-requisitioned for civil transport capacity evades or refuses to fulfill the obligations of civil transport capacity national defense mobilization in violation of these Regulations, the competent department for national defense transportation of the people's government of a city divided into districts shall order rectification; if the entity or individual refuses to rectify, it shall be compelled to fulfill the obligations, and a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan may be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例的规定，被征民用运力的单位或者个人未按照规定的时间、地点和要求集结应征民用运力，由设区的市级人民政府国防交通主管机构责令改正；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where any entity or individual whose civil transport capacity has been requisitioned fails to assemble the requisitioned civil transport capacity at the specified time, place, and in accordance with the requirements in violation of these Regulations, the competent department for national defense transportation of the people's government of a city divided into districts shall order rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例的规定，承担设计、建造或者加装改造任务的单位、个人，未按照国防要求对民用运载工具及相关设备进行设计、建造、加装改造，或者出资建造民用运载工具及相关设备的单位、个人阻碍有关设计、建造或者加装改造的，由设区的市级人民政府国防交通主管机构责令改正；拒不改正的，强制其履行义务，可以对单位处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对个人处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where any entity or individual undertaking the task of designing, constructing, or retrofitting fails to design, construct, or retrofit civil carriers and related equipment in accordance with national defense requirements, or any entity or individual that finances the construction of civil carriers and related equipment obstructs the relevant design, construction, or retrofitting, in violation of these Regulations, the competent department for national defense transportation of the people's government of a city divided into districts shall order rectification; if the entity or individual refuses to rectify, it shall be compelled to fulfill the obligations, and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan may be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　破坏预征民用运载工具及相关设备或者以其他方式阻碍、干扰民用运力国防动员活动，造成损失或者不良影响的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where any person destroys pre-requisitioned civil carriers and related equipment or otherwise obstructs or interferes with civil transport capacity national defense mobilization activities, causing losses or adverse effects, the public security organ shall impose penalties in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例的规定，县级以上人民政府交通运输管理部门、公安交通管理部门和其他有关部门拒绝向国防交通主管机构报送或者迟延报送上一年度民用运力登记的有关资料和情况的，由本级人民政府责令限期报送；逾期未报送的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由有关主管机关依法给予记过、记大过、降级的行政处分。

**Article 50** Where the transportation administration department, the public security traffic administration department, or other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level refuse to report or delay reporting the relevant materials and information on the registration of civil transport capacity of the previous year to the competent department for national defense transportation in violation of these Regulations, the people's government at the same level shall order them to report within a time limit; if they fail to report within the time limit, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions of recording a demerit, recording a serious demerit, or demotion by the relevant competent authority according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例的规定，国防交通主管机构、国民经济动员机构、人民武装动员机构、民用运力使用单位有下列情形之一的，由有关主管机关对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予记大过、降级、撤职的行政处分或者依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定给予纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)泄露所收集、掌握的民用运力资料和情况的；  
(二)超越权限，擅自动员民用运力的；  
(三)对被征用民用运力管理不善，造成严重损失的；  
(四)不出具民用运力使用、损毁证明，经有关主管机关指出拒不改正的；  
(五)违反专款专用的规定，擅自使用民用运力国防动员经费的。

**Article 51** Where the competent authority of national defense transportation, the national economic mobilization authority, the people's armed forces mobilization authority, or the user of civil transport capacity under these Regulations falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent authority concerned shall impose, upon the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible, a major demerit record, demotion, or removal from office as an administrative sanction according to law, or shall impose disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) disclosing the collected or possessed information and data on civil transport capacity;  
(2) exceeding authority to mobilize civil transport capacity without authorization;  
(3) failing to properly manage the requisitioned civil transport capacity, causing serious losses;  
(4) failing to issue a certificate of use or damage to civil transport capacity and refusing to correct after being pointed out by the competent authority concerned;  
(5) violating the provisions on the use of funds for specific purposes to misappropriate the national defense mobilization funds for civil transport capacity without authorization.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　平时特殊情况，是指发生危及国家主权、统一、领土完整和安全的武装冲突以及其他突发性事件。

**Article 52** Special circumstances in peacetime refer to armed conflicts that endanger national sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity and security, as well as other emergent incidents.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例自2004年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2004.
