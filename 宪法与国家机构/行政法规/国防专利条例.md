# 国防专利条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2004-09-17  
**生效日期**：2004-11-01

> (2004年9月17日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第418号公布　自2004年11月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保护有关国防的发明专利权，确保国家秘密，便利发明创造的推广应用，促进国防科学技术的发展，适应国防现代化建设的需要，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》，制定本条例。 第二条　国防专利是指涉及国防利益以及对国防建设具有潜在作用需要保密的发明专利。 第三条　国家国防专利机构(以下简称国防专利机构)负责受理和审查国防专利申请。经国防专利机构审查认为符合本条例规定的，由国务院专利行政部门授予国防专利权。 国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门和中国人民解放军总装备部(以下简称总装备部)分别负责地方系统和军队系统的国防专利管理工作。 第四条　涉及国防利益或者对国防建设具有潜在作用被确定为绝密级国家秘密的发明不得申请国防专利。 国防专利申请以及国防专利的保密工作，在解密前依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》和国家有关规定进行管理。 第五条　国防专利权的保护期限为20年，自申请日起计算。 第六条　国防专利在保护期内，因情况变化需要变更密级、解密或者国防专利权终止后需要延长保密期限的，国防专利机构可以作出变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的决定；但是对在申请国防专利前已被确定为国家秘密的，应当征得原确定密级和保密期限的机关、单位或者其上级机关的同意。 被授予国防专利权的单位或者个人(以下统称国防专利权人)可以向国防专利机构提出变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的书面申请；属于国有企业事业单位或者军队单位的，应当附送原确定密级和保密期限的机关、单位或者其上级机关的意见。 国防专利机构应当将变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的决定，在该机构出版的《国防专利内部通报》上刊登，并通知国防专利权人，同时将解密的国防专利报送国务院专利行政部门转为普通专利。国务院专利行政部门应当及时将解密的国防专利向社会公告。 第七条　国防专利申请权和国防专利权经批准可以向国内的中国单位和个人转让。 转让国防专利申请权或者国防专利权，应当确保国家秘密不被泄露，保证国防和军队建设不受影响，并向国防专利机构提出书面申请，由国防专利机构进行初步审查后依照本条例第三条第二款规定的职责分工，及时报送国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部审批。 国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部应当自国防专利机构受理申请之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定；作出不批准决定的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护有关国防的发明专利权，确保国家秘密，便利发明创造的推广应用，促进国防科学技术的发展，适应国防现代化建设的需要，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of protecting patent rights for inventions relating to national defense, ensuring State secrets, facilitating the promotion and application of inventions-creations, promoting the development of national defense science and technology, and meeting the needs of national defense modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国防专利是指涉及国防利益以及对国防建设具有潜在作用需要保密的发明专利。

**Article 2** National defense patents refer to invention patents that involve national defense interests or have potential effects on national defense construction and require secrecy.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家国防专利机构(以下简称国防专利机构)负责受理和审查国防专利申请。经国防专利机构审查认为符合本条例规定的，由国务院专利行政部门授予国防专利权。  
国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门和中国人民解放军总装备部(以下简称总装备部)分别负责地方系统和军队系统的国防专利管理工作。

**Article 3** The State National Defense Patent Authority (hereinafter referred to as the National Defense Patent Authority) shall be responsible for receiving and examining national defense patent applications. Where the National Defense Patent Authority, after examination, deems that the applications comply with the provisions of these Regulations, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall grant the national defense patent right.  
The competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department of the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the General Armament Department) shall respectively be responsible for the administration of national defense patents in the local system and the military system.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　涉及国防利益或者对国防建设具有潜在作用被确定为绝密级国家秘密的发明不得申请国防专利。  
国防专利申请以及国防专利的保密工作，在解密前依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》和国家有关规定进行管理。

**Article 4** No invention that involves national defense interests or has potential effects on national defense construction and is determined to be a State secret at the top-secret level may apply for a national defense patent.  
The confidentiality work for national defense patent applications and national defense patents shall be managed in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and relevant State provisions before declassification.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国防专利权的保护期限为20年，自申请日起计算。

**Article 5** The term of protection for a national defense patent right shall be 20 years, calculated from the date of filing.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国防专利在保护期内，因情况变化需要变更密级、解密或者国防专利权终止后需要延长保密期限的，国防专利机构可以作出变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的决定；但是对在申请国防专利前已被确定为国家秘密的，应当征得原确定密级和保密期限的机关、单位或者其上级机关的同意。  
被授予国防专利权的单位或者个人(以下统称国防专利权人)可以向国防专利机构提出变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的书面申请；属于国有企业事业单位或者军队单位的，应当附送原确定密级和保密期限的机关、单位或者其上级机关的意见。  
国防专利机构应当将变更密级、解密或者延长保密期限的决定，在该机构出版的《国防专利内部通报》上刊登，并通知国防专利权人，同时将解密的国防专利报送国务院专利行政部门转为普通专利。国务院专利行政部门应当及时将解密的国防专利向社会公告。

**Article 6** During the period of protection of a national defense patent, if it is necessary to change the classification level, declassify the patent, or extend the period of secrecy after the termination of the national defense patent right due to changes in circumstances, the National Defense Patent Authority may make a decision to change the classification level, declassify, or extend the period of secrecy; provided that for a national defense patent that has been determined to be a State secret before the filing of the national defense patent application, the consent of the organ or unit that originally determined the classification level and period of secrecy, or its superior organ, shall be obtained.  
The unit or individual that is granted the national defense patent right (hereinafter collectively referred to as the national defense patentee) may submit a written request to the National Defense Patent Authority for changing the classification level, declassifying, or extending the period of secrecy; if it is a State-owned enterprise or institution or a military unit, the opinions of the organ or unit that originally determined the classification level and period of secrecy, or its superior organ, shall be attached.  
The National Defense Patent Authority shall publish the decision on changing the classification level, declassifying, or extending the period of secrecy in the "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents" published by it, notify the national defense patentee, and at the same time submit the declassified national defense patent to the patent administrative department of the State Council for conversion into an ordinary patent. The patent administrative department of the State Council shall promptly announce the declassified national defense patent to the public.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国防专利申请权和国防专利权经批准可以向国内的中国单位和个人转让。  
转让国防专利申请权或者国防专利权，应当确保国家秘密不被泄露，保证国防和军队建设不受影响，并向国防专利机构提出书面申请，由国防专利机构进行初步审查后依照[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的职责分工，及时报送国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部审批。  
国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部应当自国防专利机构受理申请之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定；作出不批准决定的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
经批准转让国防专利申请权或者国防专利权的，当事人应当订立书面合同，并向国防专利机构登记，由国防专利机构在《国防专利内部通报》上刊登。国防专利申请权或者国防专利权的转让自登记之日起生效。

**Article 7** The right to apply for a national defense patent and the national defense patent right may be transferred to Chinese units and individuals within China upon approval.  
The transfer of the right to apply for a national defense patent or the national defense patent right shall ensure that State secrets are not divulged, that national defense and military construction are not affected, and a written application shall be submitted to the National Defense Patent Authority. After preliminary examination by the National Defense Patent Authority, the application shall be promptly submitted to the competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department for approval in accordance with the division of responsibilities as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 3 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department shall, within 30 days from the date of acceptance of the application by the National Defense Patent Authority, make a decision of approval or disapproval; if a decision of disapproval is made, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
Where the transfer of the right to apply for a national defense patent or the national defense patent right is approved, the parties shall conclude a written contract and register it with the National Defense Patent Authority, and the National Defense Patent Authority shall publish it in the "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents". The transfer of the right to apply for a national defense patent or the national defense patent right shall take effect from the date of registration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　禁止向国外的单位和个人以及在国内的外国人和外国机构转让国防专利申请权和国防专利权。

**Article 8** The transfer of the right to apply for a national defense patent or the national defense patent right to foreign units and individuals, as well as to foreigners and foreign institutions within China, is prohibited.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　需要委托专利代理机构申请国防专利和办理其他国防专利事务的，应当委托国防专利机构指定的专利代理机构办理。专利代理机构及其工作人员对在办理国防专利申请和其他国防专利事务过程中知悉的国家秘密，负有保密义务。

**Article 9** Where it is necessary to entrust a patent agency to apply for a national defense patent or handle other national defense patent matters, the patent agency designated by the National Defense Patent Authority shall be entrusted. The patent agency and its staff members shall have the obligation to keep confidential the State secrets learned during the process of handling national defense patent applications and other national defense patent matters.

### 第二章　国防专利的申请、审查和授权

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请国防专利的，应当向国防专利机构提交请求书、说明书及其摘要和权利要求书等文件。  
国防专利申请人应当按照国防专利机构规定的要求和统一格式撰写申请文件，并亲自送交或者经过机要通信以及其他保密方式传交国防专利机构，不得按普通函件邮寄。  
国防专利机构收到国防专利申请文件之日为申请日；申请文件通过机要通信邮寄的，以寄出的邮戳日为申请日。

**Article 10** A person applying for a national defense patent shall submit a request, a description and its abstract, and claims, and other documents to the National Defense Patent Authority.  
The applicant for a national defense patent shall draft the application documents in accordance with the requirements and uniform format prescribed by the National Defense Patent Authority, and shall deliver them in person or transmit them to the National Defense Patent Authority through confidential communication or other confidential means, and shall not mail them as ordinary correspondence.  
The date on which the National Defense Patent Authority receives the national defense patent application documents shall be the filing date; if the application documents are mailed through confidential communication, the postmark date of mailing shall be the filing date.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国防专利机构定期派人到国务院专利行政部门查看普通专利申请，发现其中有涉及国防利益或者对国防建设具有潜在作用需要保密的，经国务院专利行政部门同意后转为国防专利申请，并通知申请人。  
普通专利申请转为国防专利申请后，国防专利机构依照本条例的有关规定对该国防专利申请进行审查。

**Article 11** The National Defense Patent Authority shall periodically send personnel to the patent administrative department of the State Council to inspect ordinary patent applications. If an ordinary patent application is found to involve national defense interests or have potential effects on national defense construction and requires secrecy, it shall be converted into a national defense patent application with the consent of the patent administrative department of the State Council, and the applicant shall be notified.  
After the ordinary patent application is converted into a national defense patent application, the National Defense Patent Authority shall examine the national defense patent application in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　授予国防专利权的发明，应当具备新颖性、创造性和实用性。  
新颖性，是指在申请日之前没有同样的发明在国外出版物上公开发表过、在国内出版物上发表过、在国内使用过或者以其他方式为公众所知，也没有同样的发明由他人提出过申请并在申请日以后获得国防专利权。  
创造性，是指同申请日之前已有的技术相比，该发明有突出的实质性特点和显著的进步。  
实用性，是指该发明能够制造或者使用，并且能够产生积极效果。

**Article 12** An invention for which a national defense patent right is granted shall possess novelty, inventiveness and practical applicability.  
Novelty means that, before the filing date, no identical invention has been publicly published in foreign publications, published in domestic publications, used in China or otherwise made known to the public, and no identical invention has been filed by another person and later granted a national defense patent right after the filing date.  
Inventiveness means that, compared with the technology existing before the filing date, the invention has prominent substantive features and represents a notable progress.  
Practical applicability means that the invention can be manufactured or used and can produce positive effects.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请国防专利的发明在申请日之前6个月内，有下列情形之一的，不丧失新颖性：  
(一)在国务院有关主管部门、中国人民解放军有关主管部门举办的内部展览会上首次展出的；  
(二)在国务院有关主管部门、中国人民解放军有关主管部门召开的内部学术会议或者技术会议上首次发表的；  
(三)他人未经国防专利申请人同意而泄露其内容的。  
有前款所列情形的，国防专利申请人应当在申请时声明，并自申请日起2个月内提供有关证明文件。

**Article 13** An invention for which a national defense patent is applied shall not lose its novelty if, within six months before the filing date, it falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It was first displayed at an internal exhibition organized by the relevant competent department of the State Council or the relevant competent department of the People's Liberation Army;  
(2) It was first published at an internal academic conference or technical conference convened by the relevant competent department of the State Council or the relevant competent department of the People's Liberation Army;  
(3) Its content was disclosed by another person without the consent of the national defense patent applicant.  
Where any of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph exists, the national defense patent applicant shall make a declaration at the time of filing, and shall provide relevant supporting documents within two months from the filing date.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国防专利机构对国防专利申请进行审查后，认为不符合本条例规定的，应当通知国防专利申请人在指定的期限内陈述意见或者对其国防专利申请进行修改、补正；无正当理由逾期不答复的，该国防专利申请即被视为撤回。  
国防专利申请人在自申请日起6个月内或者在对第一次审查意见通知书进行答复时，可以对其国防专利申请主动提出修改。  
申请人对其国防专利申请文件进行修改不得超出原说明书和权利要求书记载的范围。

**Article 14** Where, after examining a national defense patent application, the National Defense Patent Authority considers that it does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall notify the applicant to state opinions or to make amendments or corrections to the national defense patent application within a specified time limit; if no response is made within the time limit without justifiable reasons, the national defense patent application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.  
The national defense patent applicant may, within six months from the filing date or at the time of responding to the notification of the first examination opinion, voluntarily propose amendments to the national defense patent application.  
Amendments made by the applicant to the national defense patent application documents shall not go beyond the scope of the original description and claims.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国防专利申请人陈述意见或者对国防专利申请进行修改、补正后，国防专利机构认为仍然不符合本条例规定的，应当予以驳回。

**Article 15** Where, after the national defense patent applicant has stated opinions or made amendments or corrections to the national defense patent application, the National Defense Patent Authority still considers that it does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall reject the application.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国防专利机构设立国防专利复审委员会，负责国防专利的复审和无效宣告工作。  
国防专利复审委员会由技术专家和法律专家组成，其主任委员由国防专利机构负责人兼任。

**Article 16** The National Defense Patent Authority shall establish a National Defense Patent Reexamination Board, which shall be responsible for the reexamination of national defense patents and the invalidation of national defense patents.  
The National Defense Patent Reexamination Board shall be composed of technical experts and legal experts, and its chairperson shall be concurrently held by the head of the National Defense Patent Authority.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国防专利申请人对国防专利机构驳回申请的决定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起3个月内，向国防专利复审委员会请求复审。国防专利复审委员会复审并作出决定后，通知国防专利申请人。

**Article 17** Where a national defense patent applicant is dissatisfied with the decision of the National Defense Patent Authority to reject the application, it may, within three months from the date of receipt of the notification, request a reexamination from the National Defense Patent Reexamination Board. After the National Defense Patent Reexamination Board has reexamined and made a decision, it shall notify the national defense patent applicant.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国防专利申请经审查认为没有驳回理由或者驳回后经过复审认为不应当驳回的，由国务院专利行政部门作出授予国防专利权的决定，并委托国防专利机构颁发国防专利证书，同时在国务院专利行政部门出版的专利公报上公告该国防专利的申请日、授权日和专利号。国防专利机构应当将该国防专利的有关事项予以登记，并在《国防专利内部通报》上刊登。

**Article 18** Where, after examination, a national defense patent application is found to have no grounds for rejection, or after reexamination following rejection, it is found that the application should not have been rejected, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall make a decision to grant the national defense patent right, and shall entrust the National Defense Patent Authority to issue the national defense patent certificate, and at the same time announce the filing date, date of grant and patent number of the national defense patent in the patent gazette published by the patent administrative department of the State Council. The National Defense Patent Authority shall register the relevant matters of the national defense patent and publish them in the "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents".

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位或者个人认为国防专利权的授予不符合本条例规定的，可以向国防专利复审委员会提出宣告该国防专利权无效的请求。

**Article 19** Any unit or individual that believes that the grant of a national defense patent right does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations may file a request with the National Defense Patent Reexamination Board for a declaration of invalidity of the national defense patent right.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国防专利复审委员会对宣告国防专利权无效的请求进行审查并作出决定后，通知请求人和国防专利权人。宣告国防专利权无效的决定，国防专利机构应当予以登记并在《国防专利内部通报》上刊登，国务院专利行政部门应当在专利公报上公布。

**Article 20** After examining a request for a declaration of invalidity of a national defense patent right and making a decision, the National Defense Patent Reexamination Board shall notify the requester and the national defense patentee. The decision declaring the national defense patent right invalid shall be registered by the National Defense Patent Authority and published in the "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents", and the patent administrative department of the State Council shall publish it in the patent gazette.

### 第三章　国防专利的实施

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国防专利机构应当自授予国防专利权之日起3个月内，将该国防专利有关文件副本送交国务院有关主管部门或者中国人民解放军有关主管部门。收到文件副本的部门，应当在4个月内就该国防专利的实施提出书面意见，并通知国防专利机构。

**Article 21** The National Defense Patent Authority shall, within three months from the date of grant of the national defense patent right, send copies of the relevant documents of the national defense patent to the relevant competent department of the State Council or the relevant competent department of the People's Liberation Army. The department that receives the copies of the documents shall, within four months, give a written opinion on the implementation of the national defense patent and notify the National Defense Patent Authority.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院有关主管部门、中国人民解放军有关主管部门，可以允许其指定的单位实施本系统或者本部门内的国防专利；需要指定实施本系统或者本部门以外的国防专利的，应当向国防专利机构提出书面申请，由国防专利机构依照[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的职责分工报国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部批准后实施。  
国防专利机构对国防专利的指定实施予以登记，并在《国防专利内部通报》上刊登。

**Article 22** The relevant competent department of the State Council or the relevant competent department of the People's Liberation Army may permit the units designated by it to implement the national defense patents within its own system or department; where it is necessary to designate the implementation of a national defense patent outside its own system or department, a written application shall be submitted to the National Defense Patent Authority, which shall, in accordance with the division of responsibilities as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 3 of these Regulations, report to the competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department for approval before implementation.  
The National Defense Patent Authority shall register the designated implementation of the national defense patent and publish it in the "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents".

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　实施他人国防专利的单位应当与国防专利权人订立书面实施合同，依照[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)的规定向国防专利权人支付费用，并报国防专利机构备案。实施单位不得允许合同规定以外的单位实施该国防专利。

**Article 23** A unit that implements another person's national defense patent shall conclude a written implementation contract with the national defense patentee, pay fees to the national defense patentee in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, and report to the National Defense Patent Authority for recordation. The implementing unit shall not permit any unit other than those specified in the contract to implement the national defense patent.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国防专利权人许可国外的单位或者个人实施其国防专利的，应当确保国家秘密不被泄露，保证国防和军队建设不受影响，并向国防专利机构提出书面申请，由国防专利机构进行初步审查后依照[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的职责分工，及时报送国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部审批。  
国务院国防科学技术工业主管部门、总装备部应当自国防专利机构受理申请之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定；作出不批准决定的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 24** Where a national defense patentee licenses a foreign unit or individual to implement its national defense patent, it shall ensure that State secrets are not divulged, that national defense and military construction are not affected, and shall submit a written application to the National Defense Patent Authority. After preliminary examination by the National Defense Patent Authority, the application shall be promptly submitted to the competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department for approval in accordance with the division of responsibilities as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 3 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of the State Council for defence science, technology and industry and the General Armament Department shall, within 30 days from the date of acceptance of the application by the National Defense Patent Authority, make a decision of approval or disapproval; if a decision of disapproval is made, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　实施他人国防专利的，应当向国防专利权人支付国防专利使用费。实施使用国家直接投入的国防科研经费或者其他国防经费进行科研活动所产生的国防专利，符合产生该国防专利的经费使用目的的，可以只支付必要的国防专利实施费；但是，科研合同另有约定或者科研任务书另有规定的除外。  
前款所称国防专利实施费，是指国防专利实施中发生的为提供技术资料、培训人员以及进一步开发技术等所需的费用。

**Article 25** Any person who implements another person's national defense patent shall pay a national defense patent royalty to the national defense patentee. Where the implementation uses a national defense patent that results from research activities funded by State-appropriated national defense research funds or other national defense funds, and the implementation is consistent with the purpose for which the funds that generated the national defense patent were used, only the necessary national defense patent implementation fee may be paid; except where otherwise agreed in the research contract or otherwise provided in the research task document.  
The term "national defense patent implementation fee" referred to in the preceding paragraph means the expenses incurred in the implementation of a national defense patent that are necessary for providing technical data, training personnel, further developing the technology, etc.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国防专利指定实施的实施费或者使用费的数额，由国防专利权人与实施单位协商确定；不能达成协议的，由国防专利机构裁决。

**Article 26** The amount of the implementation fee or royalty for the designated implementation of a national defense patent shall be determined through consultation between the national defense patentee and the implementing unit; if no agreement can be reached, the National Defense Patent Authority shall make an arbitration.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家对国防专利权人给予补偿。国防专利机构在颁发国防专利证书后，向国防专利权人支付国防专利补偿费，具体数额由国防专利机构确定。属于职务发明的，国防专利权人应当将不少于50%的补偿费发给发明人。

**Article 27** The State shall grant compensation to national defense patentees. After issuing the national defense patent certificate, the National Defense Patent Authority shall pay a national defense patent compensation fee to the national defense patentee, the specific amount of which shall be determined by the National Defense Patent Authority. Where the invention is a service invention, the national defense patentee shall pay not less than 50% of the compensation fee to the inventor.

### 第四章　国防专利的管理和保护

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国防专利机构出版的《国防专利内部通报》属于国家秘密文件，其知悉范围由国防专利机构确定。  
《国防专利内部通报》刊登下列内容：  
(一)国防专利申请中记载的著录事项；  
(二)国防专利的权利要求书；  
(三)发明说明书的摘要；  
(四)国防专利权的授予；  
(五)国防专利权的终止；  
(六)国防专利权的无效宣告；  
(七)国防专利申请权、国防专利权的转移；  
(八)国防专利的指定实施；  
(九)国防专利实施许可合同的备案；  
(十)国防专利的变更密级、解密；  
(十一)国防专利保密期限的延长；  
(十二)国防专利权人的姓名或者名称、地址的变更；  
(十三)其他有关事项。

**Article 28** The "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents" published by the National Defense Patent Authority is a State secret document, and the scope of persons who may have access to it shall be determined by the National Defense Patent Authority.  
The "Internal Bulletin of National Defense Patents" shall publish the following:  
(1) Bibliographic data recorded in national defense patent applications;  
(2) Claims of national defense patents;  
(3) Abstracts of the descriptions of inventions;  
(4) Grant of national defense patent rights;  
(5) Termination of national defense patent rights;  
(6) Declarations of invalidity of national defense patent rights;  
(7) Transfer of the right to apply for national defense patents and national defense patent rights;  
(8) Designated implementation of national defense patents;  
(9) Recordation of licensing contracts for implementation of national defense patents;  
(10) Change of classification level or declassification of national defense patents;  
(11) Extension of the period of secrecy of national defense patents;  
(12) Change of name or title, or address of the national defense patentee;  
(13) Other relevant matters.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国防专利权被授予后，有下列情形之一的，经国防专利机构同意，可以查阅国防专利说明书：  
(一)提出宣告国防专利权无效请求的；  
(二)需要实施国防专利的；  
(三)发生国防专利纠纷的；  
(四)因国防科研需要的。  
查阅者对其在查阅过程中知悉的国家秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 29** After the grant of a national defense patent right, in any of the following circumstances, with the consent of the National Defense Patent Authority, the description of the national defense patent may be consulted:  
(1) A request for a declaration of invalidity of the national defense patent right has been filed;  
(2) There is a need to implement the national defense patent;  
(3) A dispute over the national defense patent has arisen;  
(4) It is necessary for national defense research.  
The person who consults the documents shall have the obligation to keep confidential the State secrets learned during the consultation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院有关主管部门、中国人民解放军有关主管部门和各省、自治区、直辖市的国防科学技术工业管理部门应当指定一个机构管理国防专利工作，并通知国防专利机构。该管理国防专利工作的机构在业务上受国防专利机构指导。  
承担国防科研、生产任务以及参与军事订货的军队单位、国务院履行出资人职责的企业和国务院直属事业单位，应当指定相应的机构管理本单位的国防专利工作。

**Article 30** The relevant competent department of the State Council, the relevant competent department of the People's Liberation Army, and the departments in charge of defence science, technology and industry of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall designate an organ to administer national defense patent work, and shall notify the National Defense Patent Authority. Such organ administering national defense patent work shall be subject to the guidance of the National Defense Patent Authority in professional matters.  
Military units undertaking national defense research and production tasks and participating in military procurement, enterprises for which the State Council performs the responsibilities of an investor, and institutions directly under the State Council shall designate corresponding organs to administer the national defense patent work of their own units.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国防专利机构应当事人请求，可以对下列国防专利纠纷进行调解：  
(一)国防专利申请权和国防专利权归属纠纷；  
(二)国防专利发明人资格纠纷；  
(三)职务发明的发明人的奖励和报酬纠纷；  
(四)国防专利使用费和实施费纠纷。

**Article 31** At the request of a party, the National Defense Patent Authority may mediate the following disputes over national defense patents:  
(1) Disputes over the ownership of the right to apply for a national defense patent or the national defense patent right;  
(2) Disputes over the qualification of the inventor of a national defense patent;  
(3) Disputes over the award and remuneration for inventors of service inventions;  
(4) Disputes over the royalty and implementation fee for national defense patents.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　除《中华人民共和国专利法》和本条例另有规定的以外，未经国防专利权人许可实施其国防专利，即侵犯其国防专利权，引起纠纷的，由当事人协商解决；不愿协商或者协商不成的，国防专利权人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院起诉，也可以请求国防专利机构处理。

**Article 32** Except as otherwise provided in the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, any implementation of another person's national defense patent without the permission of the national defense patentee shall constitute an infringement of the national defense patent right. If a dispute arises, the parties shall settle it through consultation; if the parties are unwilling to consult or if consultation fails, the national defense patentee or an interested party may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court, or may request the National Defense Patent Authority to handle the matter.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，泄露国家秘密的，依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》和国家有关规定处理。

**Article 33** Any person who violates the provisions of these Regulations and divulges State secrets shall be dealt with in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and the relevant State provisions.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　向国防专利机构申请国防专利和办理其他手续，应当按照规定缴纳费用。

**Article 34** Any person who applies for a national defense patent or goes through other formalities with the National Defense Patent Authority shall pay fees in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　《中华人民共和国专利法》和《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》的有关规定适用于国防专利，但本条例有专门规定的依照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 35** The relevant provisions of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China and the Implementing Regulations of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply to national defense patents, except that where these Regulations have special provisions, these Regulations shall prevail.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自2004年11月1日起施行。1990年7月30日国务院、中央军事委员会批准的《国防专利条例》同时废止。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2004. The Regulations on National Defense Patents approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission on July 30, 1990 shall be abolished simultaneously.
