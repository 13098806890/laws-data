# 中华人民共和国技术进出口管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (2001年12月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第331号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订  根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范技术进出口管理，维护技术进出口秩序，促进国民经济和社会发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》(以下简称对外贸易法)及其他有关法律的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称技术进出口，是指从中华人民共和国境外向中华人民共和国境内，或者从中华人民共和国境内向中华人民共和国境外，通过贸易、投资或者经济技术合作的方式转移技术的行为。 前款规定的行为包括专利权转让、专利申请权转让、专利实施许可、技术秘密转让、技术服务和其他方式的技术转移。 第三条　国家对技术进出口实行统一的管理制度，依法维护公平、自由的技术进出口秩序。 第四条　技术进出口应当符合国家的产业政策、科技政策和社会发展政策，有利于促进我国科技进步和对外经济技术合作的发展，有利于维护我国经济技术权益。 第五条　国家准许技术的自由进出口；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。 第六条　国务院对外经济贸易主管部门(以下简称国务院外经贸主管部门)依照对外贸易法和本条例的规定，负责全国的技术进出口管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府外经贸主管部门根据国务院外经贸主管部门的授权，负责本行政区域内的技术进出口管理工作。 国务院有关部门按照国务院的规定，履行技术进出口项目的有关管理职责。 第二章　技术进口管理 第七条　国家鼓励先进、适用的技术进口。 第八条　有对外贸易法第十六条规定情形之一的技术，禁止或者限制进口。 国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门，制定、调整并公布禁止或者限制进口的技术目录。 第九条　属于禁止进口的技术，不得进口。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范技术进出口管理，维护技术进出口秩序，促进国民经济和社会发展，根据《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》(以下简称对外贸易法)及其他有关法律的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Foreign Trade Law) and the relevant provisions of other laws, for the purpose of regulating the administration of technology import and export, maintaining the order of technology import and export, and promoting the development of the national economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称技术进出口，是指从中华人民共和国境外向中华人民共和国境内，或者从中华人民共和国境内向中华人民共和国境外，通过贸易、投资或者经济技术合作的方式转移技术的行为。  
前款规定的行为包括专利权转让、专利申请权转让、专利实施许可、技术秘密转让、技术服务和其他方式的技术转移。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "technology import and export" means the act of transferring technology from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China to inside the territory of the People's Republic of China, or from inside the territory of the People's Republic of China to outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, through trade, investment or economic and technological cooperation.  
The act as prescribed in the preceding paragraph includes the transfer of patent rights, transfer of patent application rights, patent exploitation licensing, transfer of technical know-how, technical services and other forms of technology transfer.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对技术进出口实行统一的管理制度，依法维护公平、自由的技术进出口秩序。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a unified management system for technology import and export, and shall maintain a fair and free order of technology import and export according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　技术进出口应当符合国家的产业政策、科技政策和社会发展政策，有利于促进我国科技进步和对外经济技术合作的发展，有利于维护我国经济技术权益。

**Article 4** Technology import and export shall conform to the State's industrial policies, scientific and technological policies and social development policies, shall be conducive to promoting the scientific and technological progress of our country and the development of foreign economic and technological cooperation, and shall be conducive to safeguarding the economic and technological rights and interests of our country.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家准许技术的自由进出口；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 5** The State permits the free import and export of technology, except where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院对外经济贸易主管部门(以下简称国务院外经贸主管部门)依照对外贸易法和本条例的规定，负责全国的技术进出口管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府外经贸主管部门根据国务院外经贸主管部门的授权，负责本行政区域内的技术进出口管理工作。  
国务院有关部门按照国务院的规定，履行技术进出口项目的有关管理职责。

**Article 6** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation) shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Foreign Trade Law and these Regulations, be responsible for the administration of technology import and export nationwide. The competent departments of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, subject to the authorization of the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation, be responsible for the administration of technology import and export within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, perform the relevant administrative duties for technology import and export projects.

### 第二章　技术进口管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励先进、适用的技术进口。

**Article 7** The State encourages the import of advanced and applicable technology.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有[对外贸易法第十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-16)规定情形之一的技术，禁止或者限制进口。  
国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门，制定、调整并公布禁止或者限制进口的技术目录。

**Article 8** Technologies that fall under any of the circumstances specified in Article 16 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be prohibited or restricted from import.  
The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate, adjust and publish a catalogue of technologies the import of which is prohibited or restricted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　属于禁止进口的技术，不得进口。

**Article 9** Technologies that are prohibited from import shall not be imported.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　属于限制进口的技术，实行许可证管理；未经许可，不得进口。

**Article 10** Technologies that are restricted from import shall be subject to license administration; without a license, they shall not be imported.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进口属于限制进口的技术，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门提出技术进口申请并附有关文件。  
技术进口项目需经有关部门批准的，还应当提交有关部门的批准文件。

**Article 11** For the import of technologies that are restricted from import, an application for technology import shall be submitted to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation together with relevant documents.  
If the technology import project requires approval from relevant departments, the approval documents from such departments shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院外经贸主管部门收到技术进口申请后，应当会同国务院有关部门对申请进行审查，并自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。

**Article 12** Upon receipt of an application for technology import, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, examine the application and shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　技术进口申请经批准的，由国务院外经贸主管部门发给技术进口许可意向书。  
进口经营者取得技术进口许可意向书后，可以对外签订技术进口合同。

**Article 13** If an application for technology import is approved, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall issue a letter of intent for technology import license.  
After obtaining the letter of intent for technology import license, the import operator may sign a technology import contract with the foreign party.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　进口经营者签订技术进口合同后，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门提交技术进口合同副本及有关文件，申请技术进口许可证。  
国务院外经贸主管部门对技术进口合同的真实性进行审查，并自收到前款规定的文件之日起10个工作日内，对技术进口作出许可或者不许可的决定。

**Article 14** After signing a technology import contract, the import operator shall submit a copy of the technology import contract and relevant documents to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation to apply for a technology import license.  
The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall examine the authenticity of the technology import contract and shall make a decision on whether to grant a license for the technology import within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the documents provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请人依照[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定向国务院外经贸主管部门提出技术进口申请时，可以一并提交已经签订的技术进口合同副本。  
国务院外经贸主管部门应当依照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)和第十四条的规定对申请及其技术进口合同的真实性一并进行审查，并自收到前款规定的文件之日起40个工作日内，对技术进口作出许可或者不许可的决定。

**Article 15** When submitting an application for technology import to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation in accordance with Article 11 of these Regulations, the applicant may submit a copy of the technology import contract that has already been signed together with the application.  
The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 12 and 14 of these Regulations, examine the application and the authenticity of the technology import contract simultaneously, and shall make a decision on whether to grant a license for the technology import within 40 working days from the date of receipt of the documents provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　技术进口经许可的，由国务院外经贸主管部门颁发技术进口许可证。技术进口合同自技术进口许可证颁发之日起生效。

**Article 16** If the technology import is licensed, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall issue a technology import license. The technology import contract shall take effect from the date of issuance of the technology import license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对属于自由进口的技术，实行合同登记管理。  
进口属于自由进口的技术，合同自依法成立时生效，不以登记为合同生效的条件。

**Article 17** For technologies that are freely imported, contract registration management shall be implemented.  
For the import of technologies that are freely imported, the contract shall take effect from the time it is lawfully concluded, and registration shall not be a condition for the contract to take effect.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　进口属于自由进口的技术，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门办理登记，并提交下列文件：  
(一)技术进口合同登记申请书；  
(二)技术进口合同副本；  
(三)签约双方法律地位的证明文件。

**Article 18** For the import of technologies that are freely imported, registration shall be completed with the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation, and the following documents shall be submitted:  
(1) an application for registration of a technology import contract;  
(2) a copy of the technology import contract;  
(3) documents evidencing the legal status of both contracting parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院外经贸主管部门应当自收到[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的文件之日起3个工作日内，对技术进口合同进行登记，颁发技术进口合同登记证。

**Article 19** The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, within 3 working days from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Article 18 of these Regulations, register the technology import contract and issue a certificate of registration of the technology import contract.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请人凭技术进口许可证或者技术进口合同登记证，办理外汇、银行、税务、海关等相关手续。

**Article 20** The applicant shall, on the strength of the technology import license or the certificate of registration of the technology import contract, handle the relevant procedures with the foreign exchange, banking, tax, customs and other authorities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　依照本条例的规定，经许可或者登记的技术进口合同，合同的主要内容发生变更的，应当重新办理许可或者登记手续。  
经许可或者登记的技术进口合同终止的，应当及时向国务院外经贸主管部门备案。

**Article 21** If the main contents of a technology import contract that has been licensed or registered in accordance with these Regulations change, the procedures for license or registration shall be handled anew.  
If a technology import contract that has been licensed or registered terminates, the matter shall be promptly reported to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation for the record.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院外经贸主管部门和有关部门及其工作人员在履行技术进口管理职责中，对所知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 22** The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation and the relevant departments and their staff members shall, in the performance of their duties in administering technology import, have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets they become aware of.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　技术进口合同的让与人应当保证自己是所提供技术的合法拥有者或者有权转让、许可者。  
技术进口合同的受让人按照合同约定使用让与人提供的技术，被第三方指控侵权的，受让人应当立即通知让与人；让与人接到通知后，应当协助受让人排除妨碍。

**Article 23** The assignor of a technology import contract shall guarantee that it is the lawful owner of the technology provided or has the right to assign or license such technology.  
If the assignee of a technology import contract uses the technology provided by the assignor in accordance with the contract and is accused of infringement by a third party, the assignee shall immediately notify the assignor; upon receipt of the notice, the assignor shall assist the assignee in removing the hindrance.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　技术进口合同的让与人应当保证所提供的技术完整、无误、有效，能够达到约定的技术目标。

**Article 24** The assignor of a technology import contract shall guarantee that the technology provided is complete, accurate, effective and capable of achieving the agreed technical objectives.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　技术进口合同的受让人、让与人应当在合同约定的保密范围和保密期限内，对让与人提供的技术中尚未公开的秘密部分承担保密义务。  
在保密期限内，承担保密义务的一方在保密技术非因自己的原因被公开后，其承担的保密义务即予终止。

**Article 25** The assignee and assignor of a technology import contract shall, within the scope and period of confidentiality agreed upon in the contract, assume the obligation to keep confidential the undisclosed secret part of the technology provided by the assignor.  
During the confidentiality period, if the confidential technology is disclosed for reasons not attributable to the party assuming the confidentiality obligation, the confidentiality obligation assumed by such party shall terminate.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　技术进口合同期满后，技术让与人和受让人可以依照公平合理的原则，就技术的继续使用进行协商。

**Article 26** After the expiry of a technology import contract, the assignor and assignee may negotiate on the continued use of the technology in accordance with the principle of fairness and reasonableness.

### 第三章　技术出口管理

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励成熟的产业化技术出口。

**Article 27** The State encourages the export of mature industrial technologies.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有[对外贸易法第十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国对外贸易法.md#art-16)规定情形之一的技术，禁止或者限制出口。  
国务院外经贸主管部门会同国务院有关部门，制定、调整并公布禁止或者限制出口的技术目录。

**Article 28** Technologies that fall under any of the circumstances specified in Article 16 of the Foreign Trade Law shall be prohibited or restricted from export.  
The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate, adjust and publish a catalogue of technologies the export of which is prohibited or restricted.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　属于禁止出口的技术，不得出口。

**Article 29** Technologies that are prohibited from export shall not be exported.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　属于限制出口的技术，实行许可证管理；未经许可，不得出口。

**Article 30** Technologies that are restricted from export shall be subject to license administration; without a license, they shall not be exported.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　出口属于限制出口的技术，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门提出申请。

**Article 31** For the export of technologies that are restricted from export, an application shall be submitted to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院外经贸主管部门收到技术出口申请后，应当会同国务院科技管理部门对申请出口的技术进行审查，并自收到申请之日起30个工作日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。  
限制出口的技术需经有关部门进行保密审查的，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 32** Upon receipt of an application for technology export, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, jointly with the State Council department in charge of science and technology, examine the technology to be exported and shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the application.  
If the technology restricted from export needs to undergo confidentiality examination by relevant departments, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　技术出口申请经批准的，由国务院外经贸主管部门发给技术出口许可意向书。  
申请人取得技术出口许可意向书后，方可对外进行实质性谈判，签订技术出口合同。

**Article 33** If an application for technology export is approved, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall issue a letter of intent for technology export license.  
Only after obtaining the letter of intent for technology export license may the applicant conduct substantive negotiations with the foreign party and sign a technology export contract.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　申请人签订技术出口合同后，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门提交下列文件，申请技术出口许可证：  
(一)技术出口许可意向书；  
(二)技术出口合同副本；  
(三)技术资料出口清单；  
(四)签约双方法律地位的证明文件。  
国务院外经贸主管部门对技术出口合同的真实性进行审查，并自收到前款规定的文件之日起15个工作日内，对技术出口作出许可或者不许可的决定。

**Article 34** After signing a technology export contract, the applicant shall submit the following documents to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation to apply for a technology export license:  
(1) the letter of intent for technology export license;  
(2) a copy of the technology export contract;  
(3) a list of technical documentation for export;  
(4) documents evidencing the legal status of both contracting parties.  
The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall examine the authenticity of the technology export contract and shall make a decision on whether to grant a license for the technology export within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the documents provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　技术出口经许可的，由国务院外经贸主管部门颁发技术出口许可证。技术出口合同自技术出口许可证颁发之日起生效。

**Article 35** If the technology export is licensed, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall issue a technology export license. The technology export contract shall take effect from the date of issuance of the technology export license.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对属于自由出口的技术，实行合同登记管理。  
出口属于自由出口的技术，合同自依法成立时生效，不以登记为合同生效的条件。

**Article 36** For technologies that are freely exported, contract registration management shall be implemented.  
For the export of technologies that are freely exported, the contract shall take effect from the time it is lawfully concluded, and registration shall not be a condition for the contract to take effect.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　出口属于自由出口的技术，应当向国务院外经贸主管部门办理登记，并提交下列文件：  
(一)技术出口合同登记申请书；  
(二)技术出口合同副本；  
(三)签约双方法律地位的证明文件。

**Article 37** For the export of technologies that are freely exported, registration shall be completed with the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation, and the following documents shall be submitted:  
(1) an application for registration of a technology export contract;  
(2) a copy of the technology export contract;  
(3) documents evidencing the legal status of both contracting parties.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院外经贸主管部门应当自收到[本条例第三十七条](#art-37)规定的文件之日起3个工作日内，对技术出口合同进行登记，颁发技术出口合同登记证。

**Article 38** The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall, within 3 working days from the date of receipt of the documents specified in Article 37 of these Regulations, register the technology export contract and issue a certificate of registration of the technology export contract.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　申请人凭技术出口许可证或者技术出口合同登记证办理外汇、银行、税务、海关等相关手续。

**Article 39** The applicant shall, on the strength of the technology export license or the certificate of registration of the technology export contract, handle the relevant procedures with the foreign exchange, banking, tax, customs and other authorities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依照本条例的规定，经许可或者登记的技术出口合同，合同的主要内容发生变更的，应当重新办理许可或者登记手续。  
经许可或者登记的技术出口合同终止的，应当及时向国务院外经贸主管部门备案。

**Article 40** If the main contents of a technology export contract that has been licensed or registered in accordance with these Regulations change, the procedures for license or registration shall be handled anew.  
If a technology export contract that has been licensed or registered terminates, the matter shall be promptly reported to the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation for the record.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院外经贸主管部门和有关部门及其工作人员在履行技术出口管理职责中，对国家秘密和所知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 41** The State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation and the relevant departments and their staff members shall, in the performance of their duties in administering technology export, have the obligation to keep confidential State secrets and trade secrets they become aware of.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　出口核技术、核两用品相关技术、监控化学品生产技术、军事技术等出口管制技术的，依照有关行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 42** The export of export-controlled technologies such as nuclear technology, technology related to nuclear dual-use items, production technology of monitoring chemicals, and military technology shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant administrative regulations.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　进口或者出口属于禁止进出口的技术的，或者未经许可擅自进口或者出口属于限制进出口的技术的，依照刑法关于走私罪、非法经营罪、泄露国家秘密罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，区别不同情况，依照海关法的有关规定处罚，或者由国务院外经贸主管部门给予警告，没收违法所得，处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；国务院外经贸主管部门并可以撤销其对外贸易经营许可。

**Article 43** If technology that is prohibited from import or export is imported or exported, or if technology that is restricted from import or export is imported or exported without a license, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of smuggling, the crime of illegal business operation, the crime of divulging State secrets or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law according to the circumstances, or a warning shall be given by the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; and the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation may also revoke its foreign trade operation license.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　擅自超出许可的范围进口或者出口属于限制进出口的技术的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，区别不同情况，依照海关法的有关规定处罚，或者由国务院外经贸主管部门给予警告，没收违法所得，处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；国务院外经贸主管部门并可以暂停直至撤销其对外贸易经营许可。

**Article 44** If technology that is restricted from import or export is imported or exported beyond the licensed scope without authorization, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law according to the circumstances, or a warning shall be given by the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains shall be imposed; and the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation may also suspend or even revoke its foreign trade operation license.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　伪造、变造或者买卖技术进出口许可证或者技术进出口合同登记证的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依照海关法的有关规定处罚；国务院外经贸主管部门并可以撤销其对外贸易经营许可。

**Article 45** If anyone forges, alters or trades in technology import and export licenses or technology import and export contract registration certificates, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or the crime of forging, altering, buying or selling official documents, certificates or seals of State organs; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law; and the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation may also revoke its foreign trade operation license.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获取技术进出口许可的，由国务院外经贸主管部门吊销其技术进出口许可证，暂停直至撤销其对外贸易经营许可。

**Article 46** If anyone obtains a technology import and export license by deception or other improper means, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall revoke its technology import and export license, and suspend or even revoke its foreign trade operation license.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获取技术进出口合同登记的，由国务院外经贸主管部门吊销其技术进出口合同登记证，暂停直至撤销其对外贸易经营许可。

**Article 47** If anyone obtains registration of a technology import and export contract by deception or other improper means, the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation shall revoke its technology import and export contract registration certificate, and suspend or even revoke its foreign trade operation license.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　技术进出口管理工作人员违反本条例的规定，泄露国家秘密或者所知悉的商业秘密的，依照刑法关于泄露国家秘密罪或者侵犯商业秘密罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 48** If any staff member in charge of technology import and export administration violates the provisions of these Regulations and divulges State secrets or trade secrets they become aware of, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of divulging State secrets or the crime of infringing trade secrets; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　技术进出口管理工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守或者利用职务上的便利收受、索取他人财物的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪、受贿罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 49** If any staff member in charge of technology import and export administration abuses his power, neglects his duty, or takes advantage of his position to accept or extort money or property from others, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of neglect of duty, the crime of bribery or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对国务院外经贸主管部门作出的有关技术进出口的批准、许可、登记或者行政处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 50** If a party is dissatisfied with a decision made by the State Council competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation on approval, licensing, registration or administrative penalty concerning technology import and export, it may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law, or may institute a prosecution in a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例公布前国务院制定的有关技术进出口管理的规定与本条例的规定不一致的，以本条例为准。

**Article 51** Where there is any inconsistency between the provisions of these Regulations and the provisions formulated by the State Council before the promulgation of these Regulations on the administration of technology import and export, these Regulations shall prevail.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。1985年5月24日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国技术引进合同管理条例》和1987年12月30日国务院批准、1988年1月20日对外经济贸易部发布的《中华人民共和国技术引进合同管理条例施行细则》同时废止。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Technology Import Contracts promulgated by the State Council on May 24, 1985 and the Rules for the Implementation of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Technology Import Contracts approved by the State Council on December 30, 1987 and promulgated by the Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade on January 20, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.
