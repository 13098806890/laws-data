# 教学成果奖励条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （1994年3月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第151号发布　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一条　为奖励取得教学成果的集体和个人，鼓励教育工作者从事教育教学研究，提高教学水平和教育质量，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称教学成果，是指反映教育教学规律，具有独创性、新颖性、实用性，对提高教学水平和教育质量、实现培养目标产生明显效果的教育教学方案。 第三条　各级各类学校、学术团体和其他社会组织、教师及其他个人，均可以依照本条例的规定申请教学成果奖。 第四条　教学成果奖，按其对提高教学水平和教育质量、实现培养目标产生的效果，分为国家级和省（部）级。 第五条　具备下列条件的，可以申请国家级教学成果奖： （一）国内首创的； （二）经过2年以上教育教学实践检验的； （三）在全国产生一定影响的。 第六条　国家级教学成果奖分为特等奖、一等奖、二等奖三个等级，授予相应的证书、奖章和奖金。 第七条　国家级教学成果奖的评审、批准和授予工作，由国务院教育行政部门负责；其中授予特等奖的，应当报经国务院批准。 第八条　申请国家级教学成果奖，由成果的持有单位或者个人，按照其行政隶属关系，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门或者国务院有关部门教育管理机构提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门或者教育管理机构向国务院教育行政部门推荐。 国务院有关部门所属单位或者个人也可以向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门向国务院教育行政部门推荐。 第九条　不属于同一省、自治区、直辖市或者国务院部门的两个以上单位或者个人共同完成的教学成果项目申请国家级教学成果奖的，由参加单位或者个人联合向主持单位或者主持人所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门或者国务院有关部门教育管理机构提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门或者教育管理机构向国务院教育行政部门推荐。 第十条　国务院教育行政部门对申请国家级教学成果奖的项目，应当自收到推荐之日起90日内予以公布；任何单位或者个人对该教学成果权属有异议的，可以自公布之日起90日内提出，报国务院教育行政部门裁定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为奖励取得教学成果的集体和个人，鼓励教育工作者从事教育教学研究，提高教学水平和教育质量，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of rewarding collectives and individuals that achieve teaching achievements, encouraging educators to engage in education and teaching research, and improving teaching standards and education quality.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称教学成果，是指反映教育教学规律，具有独创性、新颖性、实用性，对提高教学水平和教育质量、实现培养目标产生明显效果的教育教学方案。

**Article 2** The term 'teaching achievements' as used in these Regulations refers to education and teaching plans that reflect the laws of education and teaching, possess originality, novelty, and practicality, and produce significant effects on improving teaching standards and education quality and achieving training objectives.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级各类学校、学术团体和其他社会组织、教师及其他个人，均可以依照本条例的规定申请教学成果奖。

**Article 3** Schools of all levels and types, academic organizations and other social organizations, teachers and other individuals may apply for the Teaching Achievement Award in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　教学成果奖，按其对提高教学水平和教育质量、实现培养目标产生的效果，分为国家级和省（部）级。

**Article 4** The Teaching Achievement Award shall be divided into national level and provincial (ministerial) level according to the effects produced on improving teaching standards and education quality and achieving training objectives.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　具备下列条件的，可以申请国家级教学成果奖：  
（一）国内首创的；  
（二）经过2年以上教育教学实践检验的；  
（三）在全国产生一定影响的。

**Article 5** An applicant that meets the following conditions may apply for the National Teaching Achievement Award:  
(1) being the first of its kind in China;  
(2) having been tested through education and teaching practice for more than 2 years;  
(3) having produced a certain impact nationwide.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家级教学成果奖分为特等奖、一等奖、二等奖三个等级，授予相应的证书、奖章和奖金。

**Article 6** The National Teaching Achievement Award shall be divided into three grades: special prize, first prize, and second prize, and corresponding certificates, medals, and bonuses shall be awarded.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家级教学成果奖的评审、批准和授予工作，由国务院教育行政部门负责；其中授予特等奖的，应当报经国务院批准。

**Article 7** The evaluation, approval, and awarding of the National Teaching Achievement Award shall be the responsibility of the education administrative department of the State Council; among these, the awarding of the special prize shall be subject to approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请国家级教学成果奖，由成果的持有单位或者个人，按照其行政隶属关系，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门或者国务院有关部门教育管理机构提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门或者教育管理机构向国务院教育行政部门推荐。  
国务院有关部门所属单位或者个人也可以向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门向国务院教育行政部门推荐。

**Article 8** An application for the National Teaching Achievement Award shall be submitted by the unit or individual holding the achievement, according to its administrative subordination, to the education administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the education management institution of the relevant department of the State Council, and the education administrative department or education management institution that accepts the application shall recommend it to the education administrative department of the State Council.  
Units or individuals subordinate to the relevant departments of the State Council may also submit an application to the education administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located, and the education administrative department that accepts the application shall recommend it to the education administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　不属于同一省、自治区、直辖市或者国务院部门的两个以上单位或者个人共同完成的教学成果项目申请国家级教学成果奖的，由参加单位或者个人联合向主持单位或者主持人所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门或者国务院有关部门教育管理机构提出申请，由受理申请的教育行政部门或者教育管理机构向国务院教育行政部门推荐。

**Article 9** Where an application for the National Teaching Achievement Award is made for a teaching achievement project jointly completed by two or more units or individuals that are not subordinate to the same province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or department of the State Council, the participating units or individuals shall jointly submit the application to the education administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the presiding unit or presiding person is located or the education management institution of the relevant department of the State Council, and the education administrative department or education management institution that accepts the application shall recommend it to the education administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院教育行政部门对申请国家级教学成果奖的项目，应当自收到推荐之日起90日内予以公布；任何单位或者个人对该教学成果权属有异议的，可以自公布之日起90日内提出，报国务院教育行政部门裁定。

**Article 10** The education administrative department of the State Council shall, within 90 days from the date of receipt of the recommendation, publish the projects applying for the National Teaching Achievement Award; any unit or individual that has an objection to the ownership of the teaching achievement may raise it within 90 days from the date of publication, and report it to the education administrative department of the State Council for a ruling.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家级教学成果奖每4年评审一次。

**Article 11** The National Teaching Achievement Award shall be reviewed once every four years.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　省（部）级教学成果奖的评奖条件、奖励等级、奖金数额、评审组织和办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府、国务院有关部门参照本条例规定。其奖金来源，属于省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准授予的，从地方预算安排的事业费中支付；属于国务院有关部门批准授予的，从其事业费中支付。

**Article 12** The conditions for evaluation, award grades, prize amounts, evaluation organizations and methods for provincial (or ministerial) level teaching achievement awards shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and by the relevant departments of the State Council, with reference to these Regulations. Where the prize money originates from awards approved and granted by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall be paid from the operating expenses arranged under the local budget; where it originates from awards approved and granted by the relevant departments of the State Council, it shall be paid from their own operating expenses.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　教学成果奖的奖金，归项目获奖者所有，任何单位或者个人不得截留。

**Article 13** The prize money of a teaching achievement award shall belong to the project awardees, and no unit or individual may withhold it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　获得教学成果奖，应当记入本人考绩档案，作为评定职称、晋级增薪的一项重要依据。

**Article 14** Obtaining a teaching achievement award shall be recorded in the individual's performance evaluation file and shall serve as an important basis for evaluating professional titles and for promotion and salary increase.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　弄虚作假或者剽窃他人教学成果获奖的，由授奖单位予以撤销，收回证书、奖章和奖金，并责成有关单位给予行政处分。

**Article 15** Where anyone obtains an award by fraud or plagiarism of others' teaching achievements, the awarding unit shall revoke the award, recover the certificate, medal, and prize money, and shall instruct the relevant unit to impose administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 16** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of issuance.
