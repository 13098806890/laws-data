# 食盐专营办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-12-26  
**生效日期**：2017-12-26

> (1996年5月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第197号发布　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订　2017年12月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第696号修订　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对食盐的管理，保障食盐科学加碘工作的有效实施，确保食盐质量安全和供应安全，保护公民的身体健康，制定本办法。 第二条　国家实行食盐专营管理。 本办法所称食盐，是指直接食用和制作食品所用的盐。 第三条　本办法适用于中华人民共和国境内的食盐生产、销售和储备活动。 第四条　国务院盐业主管部门主管全国盐业工作，负责管理全国食盐专营工作。县级以上地方人民政府确定的盐业主管部门负责管理本行政区域的食盐专营工作。 国务院食品药品监督管理部门负责全国食盐质量安全监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府确定的食盐质量安全监督管理部门负责本行政区域的食盐质量安全监督管理。 第五条　盐业主管部门应当加强对工业用盐等非食用盐的管理，防止非食用盐流入食盐市场。 第六条　国务院盐业主管部门应当会同有关部门加强对食盐生产、批发企业及其董事、监事、高级管理人员的信用管理，建立健全信用信息记录、公示制度，提高食盐行业信用水平。 第七条　依法成立的盐业行业组织依照法律、行政法规和章程，保护企业合法权益，加强行业自律，促进企业守法、诚信经营，引导企业公平竞争。 第二章　食盐生产 第八条　国家实行食盐定点生产制度。非食盐定点生产企业不得生产食盐。 第九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管部门按照统一规划、合理布局的要求审批确定食盐定点生产企业，颁发食盐定点生产企业证书，及时向社会公布食盐定点生产企业名单，并报国务院盐业主管部门备案。 第十条　食盐定点生产企业和非食用盐生产企业应当建立生产销售记录制度，如实记录并保存相关凭证。记录和凭证保存期限不得少于2年。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对食盐的管理，保障食盐科学加碘工作的有效实施，确保食盐质量安全和供应安全，保护公民的身体健康，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of edible salt, ensuring the effective implementation of scientific iodization of edible salt, guaranteeing the quality safety and supply safety of edible salt, and protecting citizens' physical health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行食盐专营管理。  
本办法所称食盐，是指直接食用和制作食品所用的盐。

**Article 2** The State adopts a monopoly administration of edible salt.  
The term "edible salt" as used in these Measures means salt used for direct consumption and for food preparation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法适用于中华人民共和国境内的食盐生产、销售和储备活动。

**Article 3** These Measures apply to the production, sale, and storage of edible salt within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院盐业主管部门主管全国盐业工作，负责管理全国食盐专营工作。县级以上地方人民政府确定的盐业主管部门负责管理本行政区域的食盐专营工作。  
国务院食品药品监督管理部门负责全国食盐质量安全监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府确定的食盐质量安全监督管理部门负责本行政区域的食盐质量安全监督管理。

**Article 4** The salt industry competent department of the State Council shall be responsible for the national salt industry work and for the administration of the national monopoly of edible salt. The salt industry competent department determined by the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of the monopoly of edible salt within its administrative region.  
The food and drug regulatory authority of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality safety of edible salt nationwide. The department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety determined by the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality safety of edible salt within its administrative region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　盐业主管部门应当加强对工业用盐等非食用盐的管理，防止非食用盐流入食盐市场。

**Article 5** The salt industry competent department shall strengthen the administration of non-edible salt such as industrial salt and prevent non-edible salt from flowing into the edible salt market.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院盐业主管部门应当会同有关部门加强对食盐生产、批发企业及其董事、监事、高级管理人员的信用管理，建立健全信用信息记录、公示制度，提高食盐行业信用水平。

**Article 6** The salt industry competent department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, strengthen the credit administration of edible salt production and wholesale enterprises and their directors, supervisors, and senior officers, establish and improve the credit information recording and public disclosure systems, and enhance the credit level of the salt industry.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　依法成立的盐业行业组织依照法律、行政法规和章程，保护企业合法权益，加强行业自律，促进企业守法、诚信经营，引导企业公平竞争。

**Article 7** The salt industry organizations established according to law shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and their articles of association, protect the lawful rights and interests of enterprises, strengthen industry self-discipline, promote enterprises' law-abiding and honest operation, and guide enterprises to engage in fair competition.

### 第二章　食盐生产

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实行食盐定点生产制度。非食盐定点生产企业不得生产食盐。

**Article 8** The State adopts a system of designated production of edible salt. No enterprise other than a designated edible salt production enterprise may produce edible salt.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管部门按照统一规划、合理布局的要求审批确定食盐定点生产企业，颁发食盐定点生产企业证书，及时向社会公布食盐定点生产企业名单，并报国务院盐业主管部门备案。

**Article 9** The salt industry competent department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of unified planning and rational layout, examine and approve designated edible salt production enterprises, issue designated edible salt production enterprise certificates, promptly publicize the list of designated edible salt production enterprises to the public, and report to the salt industry competent department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　食盐定点生产企业和非食用盐生产企业应当建立生产销售记录制度，如实记录并保存相关凭证。记录和凭证保存期限不得少于2年。  
食盐应当按照规定在外包装上作出标识，非食用盐的包装、标识应当明显区别于食盐。

**Article 10** Designated edible salt production enterprises and non-edible salt production enterprises shall establish production and sales record systems, truthfully record and preserve relevant vouchers. The retention period for records and vouchers shall not be less than two years.  
Edible salt shall be marked on the outer packaging in accordance with the provisions. The packaging and marking of non-edible salt shall be clearly distinguishable from those of edible salt.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　禁止利用井矿盐卤水熬制食盐。

**Article 11** It is prohibited to boil edible salt from well and mine salt brine.

### 第三章　食盐销售

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家实行食盐定点批发制度。非食盐定点批发企业不得经营食盐批发业务。

**Article 12** The State adopts a system of designated wholesale of edible salt. No enterprise other than a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise may engage in the wholesale business of edible salt.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管部门按照统一规划、合理布局的要求审批确定食盐定点批发企业，颁发食盐定点批发企业证书，及时向社会公布食盐定点批发企业名单，并报国务院盐业主管部门备案。  
食盐定点生产企业申请经营食盐批发业务的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管部门应当确定其为食盐定点批发企业并颁发食盐定点批发企业证书。

**Article 13** The salt industry competent department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of unified planning and rational layout, examine and approve designated edible salt wholesale enterprises, issue designated edible salt wholesale enterprise certificates, promptly publicize the list of designated edible salt wholesale enterprises to the public, and report to the salt industry competent department of the State Council for recordation.  
Where a designated edible salt production enterprise applies to engage in the wholesale business of edible salt, the salt industry competent department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall designate it as a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise and issue a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise certificate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　食盐定点批发企业应当从食盐定点生产企业或者其他食盐定点批发企业购进食盐，在国家规定的范围内销售。  
食盐定点批发企业在国家规定的范围内销售食盐，任何单位或者个人不得阻止或者限制。

**Article 14** A designated edible salt wholesale enterprise shall purchase edible salt from a designated edible salt production enterprise or another designated edible salt wholesale enterprise, and shall sell it within the scope prescribed by the State.  
When a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise sells edible salt within the scope prescribed by the State, no entity or individual may prevent or restrict such sales.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　食盐定点批发企业应当建立采购销售记录制度，如实记录并保存相关凭证。记录和凭证保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 15** A designated edible salt wholesale enterprise shall establish purchase and sales record systems, truthfully record and preserve relevant vouchers. The retention period for records and vouchers shall not be less than two years.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　食盐零售单位应当从食盐定点批发企业购进食盐。

**Article 16** An edible salt retail unit shall purchase edible salt from a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　食盐价格由经营者自主确定。  
县级以上地方人民政府价格主管部门应当加强对食盐零售价格的市场日常监测。当食盐价格显著上涨或者有可能显著上涨时，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依法采取价格干预或者其他应急措施。

**Article 17** The price of edible salt shall be independently determined by the business operator.  
The price competent department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen daily market monitoring of the retail price of edible salt. When the price of edible salt rises significantly or is likely to rise significantly, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may take price intervention or other emergency measures according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据实际情况，采取必要措施，保障边远地区和民族地区的食盐供应。

**Article 18** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on actual circumstances, take necessary measures to ensure the supply of edible salt to remote and ethnic minority areas.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　禁止销售不符合食品安全标准的食盐。  
禁止将下列产品作为食盐销售：  
(一)液体盐(含天然卤水)；  
(二)工业用盐和其他非食用盐；  
(三)利用盐土、硝土或者工业废渣、废液制作的盐；  
(四)利用井矿盐卤水熬制的盐；  
(五)外包装上无标识或者标识不符合国家有关规定的盐。

**Article 19** It is prohibited to sell edible salt that does not conform to food safety standards.  
It is prohibited to sell the following products as edible salt:  
(1) liquid salt (including natural brine);  
(2) industrial salt and other non-edible salt;  
(3) salt made from salt earth, nitre earth, or industrial waste residue or waste liquid;  
(4) salt boiled from well and mine salt brine;  
(5) salt whose outer packaging has no marking or whose marking does not conform to the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第四章　食盐的储备和应急管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管部门应当根据本行政区域食盐供需情况，建立健全食盐储备制度，承担政府食盐储备责任。

**Article 20** The salt industry competent department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the supply and demand of edible salt in its administrative region, establish a sound edible salt storage system and assume the responsibility of government edible salt storage.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　食盐定点生产企业和食盐定点批发企业应当按照食盐储备制度要求，承担企业食盐储备责任，保持食盐的合理库存。

**Article 21** Designated edible salt production enterprises and designated edible salt wholesale enterprises shall, in accordance with the requirements of the edible salt storage system, assume the responsibility of enterprise edible salt storage and maintain reasonable inventory of edible salt.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　盐业主管部门应当会同有关部门制定食盐供应应急预案，在发生突发事件时协调、保障食盐供应。

**Article 22** The salt industry competent department shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate emergency plans for the supply of edible salt, and coordinate and ensure the supply of edible salt in the event of emergencies.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　盐业主管部门依法履行监督检查职责，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)向有关单位和个人了解情况；  
(二)查阅或者复制有关合同、票据、账簿、购销记录及其他有关资料；  
(三)查封、扣押与涉嫌盐业违法行为有关的食盐及原材料，以及用于违法生产或者销售食盐的工具、设备；  
(四)查封涉嫌违法生产或者销售食盐的场所。  
采取前款第三项、第四项规定的措施，应当向盐业主管部门主要负责人书面报告，并经批准。  
盐业主管部门调查涉嫌盐业违法行为，应当遵守《中华人民共和国行政强制法》和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 23** The salt industry competent department, when performing supervision and inspection duties according to law, may take the following measures:  
(1) to inquire about the circumstances from relevant entities and individuals;  
(2) to consult or copy relevant contracts, invoices, account books, purchase and sales records, and other relevant materials;  
(3) to seal up or seize edible salt and raw materials suspected of being involved in illegal salt activities, as well as tools and equipment used for the illegal production or sale of edible salt;  
(4) to seal up premises suspected of being used for the illegal production or sale of edible salt.  
Measures provided for in Items (3) and (4) of the preceding paragraph shall be subject to a written report to the principal of the salt industry competent department and approval.  
When investigating suspected illegal salt activities, the salt industry competent department shall comply with the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　盐业主管部门、食盐质量安全监督管理部门应当各司其职，加强协作，相互配合，通过政务信息系统等实现信息共享，建立健全行政执法协作配合制度。  
盐业主管部门、食盐质量安全监督管理部门在监督管理工作中发现依法不属于本部门处理权限的涉嫌违法行为，应当及时移交有权处理的部门；有权处理的部门应当依法及时处理，并及时反馈处理结果。

**Article 24** The salt industry competent department and the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety shall perform their respective duties, strengthen cooperation, coordinate with each other, achieve information sharing through government information systems and other means, and establish a sound system for coordination and cooperation in administrative law enforcement.  
Where, in the course of supervision and administration, the salt industry competent department or the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety discovers suspected illegal acts that are not within its competence according to law, it shall promptly transfer the case to the department with competent authority; the department with competent authority shall handle the matter promptly according to law and provide timely feedback on the outcome.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　盐业主管部门、食盐质量安全监督管理部门应当向社会公布本部门的联系方式，方便公众举报违法行为。  
盐业主管部门、食盐质量安全监督管理部门接到举报，应当及时依法调查处理。对实名举报，盐业主管部门、食盐质量安全监督管理部门应当为举报人保密，并将处理结果告知举报人。

**Article 25** The salt industry competent department and the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety shall publicize their contact information to the public to facilitate public reporting of illegal acts.  
Upon receiving a report, the salt industry competent department or the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety shall promptly investigate and handle it according to law. With respect to a report made under real name, the salt industry competent department or the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety shall keep the reporter confidential and inform the reporter of the handling result.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府盐业主管部门予以取缔，没收违法生产经营的食盐和违法所得。违法生产经营的食盐货值金额不足1万元的，可以处5万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款：  
(一)非食盐定点生产企业生产食盐；  
(二)非食盐定点批发企业经营食盐批发业务。

**Article 26** In any of the following circumstances, the salt industry competent department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall ban the conduct, confiscate the illegally produced or sold edible salt and illegal gains. If the value of the illegally produced or sold edible salt is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value shall also be imposed:  
(1) a non-designated edible salt production enterprise produces edible salt;  
(2) a non-designated edible salt wholesale enterprise engages in the wholesale business of edible salt.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府盐业主管部门责令改正，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业整顿，直至吊销食盐定点生产、食盐定点批发企业证书：  
(一)食盐定点生产企业、非食用盐生产企业未按照本办法规定保存生产销售记录；  
(二)食盐定点批发企业未按照本办法规定保存采购销售记录；  
(三)食盐定点批发企业超出国家规定的范围销售食盐；  
(四)将非食用盐产品作为食盐销售。

**Article 27** In any of the following circumstances, the salt industry competent department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification, up to revocation of the designated edible salt production enterprise certificate or designated edible salt wholesale enterprise certificate:  
(1) a designated edible salt production enterprise or a non-edible salt production enterprise fails to preserve production and sales records as required by these Measures;  
(2) a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise fails to preserve purchase and sales records as required by these Measures;  
(3) a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise sells edible salt beyond the scope prescribed by the State;  
(4) selling non-edible salt products as edible salt.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府盐业主管部门责令改正，没收违法购进的食盐，可以处违法购进的食盐货值金额3倍以下的罚款：  
(一)食盐定点批发企业从除食盐定点生产企业、其他食盐定点批发企业以外的单位或者个人购进食盐；  
(二)食盐零售单位从食盐定点批发企业以外的单位或者个人购进食盐。

**Article 28** In any of the following circumstances, the salt industry competent department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order corrective action, confiscate the illegally purchased edible salt, and may impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the illegally purchased edible salt:  
(1) a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise purchases edible salt from an entity or individual other than a designated edible salt production enterprise or another designated edible salt wholesale enterprise;  
(2) an edible salt retail unit purchases edible salt from an entity or individual other than a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　未按照[本办法第十条](#art-10)的规定作出标识的，由有关主管部门依据职责分工，责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where markings are not made in accordance with Article 10 of these Measures, the relevant competent department shall, based on the division of duties, order corrective action and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　经营者的行为违反本办法的规定同时违反《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的，由县级以上地方人民政府食盐质量安全监督管理部门依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》进行处罚。

**Article 30** Where the conduct of a business operator violates the provisions of these Measures and also violates the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the department for the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　食盐定点生产企业、食盐定点批发企业违反本办法的规定，被处以吊销食盐定点生产、食盐定点批发企业证书行政处罚的，其法定代表人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员自处罚决定作出之日起5年内不得从事食盐生产经营管理活动，不得担任食盐定点生产企业、食盐定点批发企业的董事、监事或者高级管理人员。  
食盐定点生产企业、食盐定点批发企业违反前款规定聘用人员的，由盐业主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，吊销其食盐定点生产、食盐定点批发企业证书。

**Article 31** Where a designated edible salt production enterprise or a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise violates the provisions of these Measures and is subject to the administrative penalty of revocation of the designated edible salt production enterprise certificate or designated edible salt wholesale enterprise certificate, its legal representative, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly responsible persons shall not engage in the management activities of edible salt production and operation, and shall not serve as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a designated edible salt production enterprise or designated edible salt wholesale enterprise, within five years from the date of the penalty decision.  
Where a designated edible salt production enterprise or a designated edible salt wholesale enterprise employs personnel in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the salt industry competent department shall order corrective action; if it refuses to correct, its designated edible salt production enterprise certificate or designated edible salt wholesale enterprise certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本办法的规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where a violation of the provisions of these Measures constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　盐业主管部门以及其他有关部门的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 33** Where any staff member of the salt industry competent department or other relevant departments abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, a disciplinary sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　除本办法的规定外，食盐质量安全监督管理、食盐加碘工作还应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 34** In addition to the provisions of these Measures, the supervision and administration of edible salt quality safety and the iodization of edible salt shall also be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　渔业、畜牧用盐管理办法，由国务院盐业主管部门会同国务院农业行政主管部门另行制定。

**Article 35** The measures for the administration of salt for fisheries and livestock shall be separately formulated by the salt industry competent department of the State Council together with the competent agricultural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本办法自公布之日起施行。1990年3月2日国务院发布的《盐业管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 36** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Regulations on the Administration of the Salt Industry promulgated by the State Council on March 2, 1990 shall be abolished simultaneously.
