# 食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：1994-10-01

> (1994年8月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第163号发布　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了消除碘缺乏危害，保护公民身体健康，制定本条例。 第二条　碘缺乏危害，是指由于环境缺碘、公民摄碘不足所引起的地方性甲状腺肿、地方性克汀病和对儿童智力发育的潜在性损伤。 第三条　国家对消除碘缺乏危害，采取长期供应加碘食盐(以下简称碘盐)为主的综合防治措施。 第四条　国务院卫生行政部门负责碘缺乏危害防治和碘盐的卫生监督管理工作；国务院授权的盐业主管机构(以下简称国务院盐业主管机构)负责全国碘盐加工、市场供应的监督管理工作。 第五条　各级人民政府应当将食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害的工作纳入本地区国民经济和社会发展计划，并组织实施。 县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，密切配合，共同做好食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害工作。 第六条　国家鼓励和支持在食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害方面的科学研究和先进技术推广工作。 对在食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。 第二章　碘盐的加工、运输和储存 第七条　从事碘盐加工的盐业企业，应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构指定，并取得同级人民政府卫生行政部门卫生许可后，报国务院盐业主管机构批准。 第八条　用于加工碘盐的食盐和碘酸钾必须符合国家卫生标准。 碘盐中碘酸钾的加入量由国务院卫生行政部门确定。 第九条　碘盐出厂前必须经质量检验，未达到规定含量标准的碘盐不得出厂。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了消除碘缺乏危害，保护公民身体健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of eliminating the hazards of iodine deficiency and protecting the physical health of citizens.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　碘缺乏危害，是指由于环境缺碘、公民摄碘不足所引起的地方性甲状腺肿、地方性克汀病和对儿童智力发育的潜在性损伤。

**Article 2** Hazards of iodine deficiency refer to endemic goiter, endemic cretinism, and potential damage to the intellectual development of children caused by environmental iodine deficiency and insufficient iodine intake by citizens.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对消除碘缺乏危害，采取长期供应加碘食盐(以下简称碘盐)为主的综合防治措施。

**Article 3** The State adopts comprehensive prevention and treatment measures focusing on the long-term supply of iodized edible salt (hereinafter referred to as iodized salt) to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院卫生行政部门负责碘缺乏危害防治和碘盐的卫生监督管理工作；国务院授权的盐业主管机构(以下简称国务院盐业主管机构)负责全国碘盐加工、市场供应的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The health administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the prevention and treatment of iodine deficiency hazards and the sanitary supervision and administration of iodized salt; the salt industry competent authority authorized by the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the salt industry competent authority of the State Council) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the processing and market supply of iodized salt nationwide.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府应当将食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害的工作纳入本地区国民经济和社会发展计划，并组织实施。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，密切配合，共同做好食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害工作。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate the work of iodizing edible salt to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency into the local national economic and social development plans and organize their implementation.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, closely cooperate and jointly do a good job in the work of iodizing edible salt to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持在食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害方面的科学研究和先进技术推广工作。  
对在食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports scientific research and the promotion of advanced technology in the field of iodizing edible salt to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency.  
Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in the work of iodizing edible salt to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency shall be rewarded.

### 第二章　碘盐的加工、运输和储存

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事碘盐加工的盐业企业，应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构指定，并取得同级人民政府卫生行政部门卫生许可后，报国务院盐业主管机构批准。

**Article 7** Salt industry enterprises engaged in the processing of iodized salt shall be designated by the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and after obtaining the sanitary permit from the health administrative department of the people's government at the same level, shall report to the salt industry competent authority of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　用于加工碘盐的食盐和碘酸钾必须符合国家卫生标准。  
碘盐中碘酸钾的加入量由国务院卫生行政部门确定。

**Article 8** The edible salt and potassium iodate used for processing iodized salt must comply with national hygiene standards.  
The amount of potassium iodate added to iodized salt shall be determined by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　碘盐出厂前必须经质量检验，未达到规定含量标准的碘盐不得出厂。

**Article 9** Iodized salt must undergo quality inspection before leaving the factory; iodized salt that does not meet the prescribed content standards shall not leave the factory.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　碘盐出厂前必须予以包装。碘盐的包装应当有明显标识，并附有加工企业名称、地址、加碘量、批号、生产日期和保管方法等说明。

**Article 10** Iodized salt must be packaged before leaving the factory. The packaging of iodized salt shall have clear markings and shall be accompanied by explanations such as the name and address of the processing enterprise, the amount of iodization, batch number, date of production, and storage methods.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　碘盐为国家重点运输物资。铁路、交通部门必须依照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构报送的年度、月度运输计划，及时运送。  
碘盐的运输工具和装卸工具，必须符合卫生要求，不得与有毒、有害物质同载、混放。

**Article 11** Iodized salt is a key transport material of the State. The railway and transportation departments must transport it in a timely manner in accordance with the annual and monthly transport plans submitted by the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The means of transport and loading/unloading tools for iodized salt must meet hygiene requirements and shall not be loaded or stored together with toxic or harmful substances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经营碘盐批发业务的企业和在交通不方便的地区经营碘盐零售业务的单位和个人，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构的规定，保持合理的碘盐库存量。  
碘盐和非碘盐在储存场地应当分库或者分垛存放，做到防晒、干燥、安全、卫生。

**Article 12** Enterprises engaged in the wholesale business of iodized salt and units and individuals engaged in the retail business of iodized salt in areas with inconvenient transportation shall maintain a reasonable inventory of iodized salt in accordance with the provisions of the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Iodized salt and non-iodized salt shall be stored in separate warehouses or separate stacks at the storage site, ensuring sun protection, dryness, safety, and hygiene.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　碘剂的购置费用以及盐业企业因加碘而发生的各种费用，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 13** The expenses for the purchase of iodine agents and all expenses incurred by salt industry enterprises due to iodization shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第三章　碘盐的供应

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门负责划定碘缺乏地区(以下简称缺碘地区)范围，经本级人民政府批准后，报国务院卫生行政部门、国务院盐业主管机构备案。

**Article 14** The health administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for delineating the scope of iodine-deficient areas (hereinafter referred to as iodine-deficient areas), and after approval by the people's government at the same level, shall report to the health administrative department of the State Council and the salt industry competent authority of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家优先保证缺碘地区居民的碘盐供应；除高碘地区外，逐步实施向全民供应碘盐。  
对于经济区域和行政区域不一致的缺碘地区，应当按照盐业运销渠道组织碘盐的供应。  
在缺碘地区产生、销售的食品和副食品，凡需添加食用盐的，必须使用碘盐。

**Article 15** The State shall give priority to ensuring the supply of iodized salt to residents in iodine-deficient areas; except for high-iodine areas, the supply of iodized salt to the entire population shall be gradually implemented.  
For iodine-deficient areas where the economic region and the administrative region are inconsistent, the supply of iodized salt shall be organized in accordance with the salt transportation and sales channels.  
For food and non-staple food produced and sold in iodine-deficient areas, if edible salt needs to be added, iodized salt must be used.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在缺碘地区销售的碘盐必须达到规定的含碘量，禁止非碘盐和不合格碘盐进入缺碘地区食用盐市场。  
对暂时不能供应碘盐的缺碘地区，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以暂时供应非碘盐；但是，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门应当采取其他补碘的防治措施。  
对缺碘地区季节性家庭工业、农业、副业、建筑业所需的非碘盐和非食用盐，由县级以上人民政府盐业主管机构组织供应。

**Article 16** Iodized salt sold in iodine-deficient areas must meet the prescribed iodine content; non-iodized salt and substandard iodized salt are prohibited from entering the edible salt market in iodine-deficient areas.  
For iodine-deficient areas where iodized salt cannot be supplied temporarily, upon approval by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, non-iodized salt may be supplied temporarily; however, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall adopt other iodine supplementation prevention and treatment measures.  
Non-iodized salt and non-edible salt required for seasonal household industry, agriculture, sideline production, and construction in iodine-deficient areas shall be supplied by the salt industry competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营碘盐批发业务的企业，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构审批。  
碘盐批发企业应当从国务院盐业主管机构批准的碘盐加工企业进货。经营碘盐零售业务的单位和个人，应当从碘盐批发企业进货，不得从未经批准的单位和个人购进碘盐。

**Article 17** Enterprises engaged in the wholesale business of iodized salt shall be subject to approval by the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Iodized salt wholesale enterprises shall purchase goods from iodized salt processing enterprises approved by the salt industry competent authority of the State Council. Units and individuals engaged in the retail business of iodized salt shall purchase goods from iodized salt wholesale enterprises and shall not purchase iodized salt from unapproved units or individuals.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　碘盐批发企业在从碘盐加工企业购进碘盐时，应当索取加碘证明，碘盐加工企业应当保证提供。

**Article 18** When an iodized salt wholesale enterprise purchases iodized salt from an iodized salt processing enterprise, it shall request the iodization certificate, and the iodized salt processing enterprise shall guarantee to provide it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　碘盐零售单位销售的碘盐应当为小包装，并应当符合本条例的有关规定。碘盐零售的管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际情况制定。

**Article 19** Iodized salt sold by iodized salt retail units shall be in small packages and shall comply with the relevant provisions of these Regulations. The administrative measures for the retail of iodized salt shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government based on actual circumstances.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　为防治疾病，在碘盐中同时添加其他营养强化剂的，应当符合《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的相关规定，并标明销售范围。  
因治疗疾病，不宜食用碘盐的，应当持当地县级人民政府卫生行政部门指定的医疗机构出具的证明，到当地人民政府盐业主管机构指定的单位购买非碘盐。

**Article 20** Where other nutritional fortifiers are added simultaneously to iodized salt for the prevention and treatment of diseases, the relevant provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China shall be complied with, and the scope of sale shall be indicated.  
Where a person is unsuitable to consume iodized salt due to the treatment of a disease, they shall, holding a certificate issued by a medical institution designated by the health administrative department of the local people's government at the county level, purchase non-iodized salt from a unit designated by the local people's government's salt industry competent authority.

### 第四章　监督和管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方各级人民政府卫生行政部门负责对本地区食盐加碘消除碘缺乏危害的卫生监督和碘盐的卫生监督以及防治效果评估；县级以上地方各级人民政府盐业主管机构负责对本地区碘盐加工、市场供应的监督管理。

**Article 21** The health administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the sanitary supervision of iodizing edible salt to eliminate the hazards of iodine deficiency, the sanitary supervision of iodized salt, and the evaluation of prevention and treatment effects in their respective regions; the salt industry competent authorities of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the processing and market supply of iodized salt in their respective regions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政部门有权按照国家规定，向碘酸钾生产企业和碘盐加工、经营单位抽检样品，索取与卫生监测有关的资料，任何单位和个人不得拒绝、隐瞒或者提供虚假资料。

**Article 22** The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the right, in accordance with State provisions, to take sample inspections from potassium iodate production enterprises and iodized salt processing and operating units, and to request information relevant to health monitoring; no unit or individual shall refuse, conceal, or provide false information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　卫生监督人员在实施卫生监督、监测时，应当主动出示卫生行政部门制发的监督证件；盐政人员在执行职务时，应当主动出示盐业主管机构制发的证件。

**Article 23** When carrying out sanitary supervision and monitoring, health supervision personnel shall proactively present the supervision credentials issued by the health administrative department; when performing their duties, salt administration personnel shall proactively present the credentials issued by the salt industry competent authority.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例的规定，擅自开办碘盐加工企业或者未经批准从事碘盐批发业务的，由县级以上人民政府盐业主管机构责令停止加工或者批发碘盐，没收全部碘盐和违法所得，可以并处该盐产品价值3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 24** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an iodized salt processing enterprise is established without authorization or the wholesale business of iodized salt is engaged in without approval, the salt industry competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of processing or wholesaling iodized salt, confiscate all iodized salt and illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the salt products.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　碘盐的加工企业、批发企业违反本条例的规定，加工、批发不合格碘盐的，由县级以上人民政府盐业主管机构责令停止出售并责令责任者按照国家规定标准对食盐补碘，没收违法所得，可以并处该盐产品价值3倍以下的罚款。情节严重的，对加工企业，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构报请国务院盐业主管机构批准后，取消其碘盐加工资格；对批发企业，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府盐业主管机构取消其碘盐批发资格。

**Article 25** Where an iodized salt processing enterprise or wholesale enterprise, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, processes or wholesales substandard iodized salt, the salt industry competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of sales and order the responsible person to supplement iodine to the edible salt in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, confiscate illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the salt products. If the circumstances are serious, for a processing enterprise, the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the salt industry competent authority of the State Council, revoke its qualification for processing iodized salt; for a wholesale enterprise, the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke its qualification for wholesaling iodized salt.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例的规定，在缺碘地区的食用盐市场销售不合格碘盐或者擅自销售非碘盐的，由县级以上人民政府盐业主管机构没收其经营的全部盐产品和违法所得，可以并处该盐产品价值3倍以下的罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, substandard iodized salt is sold or non-iodized salt is sold without authorization on the edible salt market in an iodine-deficient area, the salt industry competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate all salt products and illegal gains from its business operation, and may also impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the salt products; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例的规定，在碘盐的加工、运输、经营过程中不符合国家卫生标准的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令责任者改正，可以并处该盐产品价值3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the processing, transport, or business operation of iodized salt fails to meet national hygiene standards, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the responsible person to make corrections, and may also impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the salt products.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例的规定，出厂碘盐未予包装或者包装不符合国家卫生标准的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，可以并处该盐产品价值3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 28** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, iodized salt leaving the factory is not packaged or the packaging fails to meet national hygiene standards, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, and may also impose a fine of not more than three times the value of the salt products.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例的规定，在缺碘地区生产、销售的食品和副食品中添加非碘盐的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处该产品价值1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, non-iodized salt is added to food and non-staple food produced or sold in an iodine-deficient area, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than one time the value of the products.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　畜牧用盐适用本条例。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall apply to salt for animal husbandry.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 31** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementation measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门、盐业主管机构确定为应当供应碘盐的非缺碘地区适用[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第二款、第三款和第十六条第一款、第三款的规定。

**Article 32** The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 15 and paragraphs 1 and 3 of Article 16 of these Regulations shall apply to non-iodine-deficient areas determined by the health administrative department and the salt industry competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government as areas where iodized salt should be supplied.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例自1994年10月1日起施行。1979年12月21日国务院批转的《食盐加碘防治地方性甲状腺肿暂行办法》同时废止。

**Article 33** These Regulations shall come into force on October 1, 1994. The Interim Measures for the Prevention and Treatment of Endemic Goiter by Iodizing Edible Salt approved by the State Council on December 21, 1979 shall be abolished simultaneously.
