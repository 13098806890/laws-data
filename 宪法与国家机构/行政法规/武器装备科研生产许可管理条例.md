### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护武器装备科研生产秩序，加强武器装备科研生产安全保密管理，保证武器装备质量合格稳定，满足国防建设的需要，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of maintaining the order of research and production of weapons and equipment, strengthening the security and confidentiality management of such research and production, ensuring the quality and stability of weapons and equipment, and meeting the needs of national defense construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对列入武器装备科研生产许可目录(以下简称许可目录)的武器装备科研生产活动实行许可管理。但是，专门的武器装备科学研究活动除外。  
许可目录由国务院国防科技工业主管部门会同中国人民解放军总装备部(以下简称总装备部)和军工电子行业主管部门共同制定，并适时调整。许可目录的制定和调整，应当征求国务院有关部门和军队有关部门的意见。  
武器装备科研生产许可，应当在许可目录所确定的范围内实行分类管理。

**Article 2** The State shall implement license administration for research and production activities of weapons and equipment that are included in the Catalog of Licenses for Research and Production of Weapons and Equipment (hereinafter referred to as the License Catalog), except for specialized scientific research activities on weapons and equipment.  
The License Catalog shall be jointly formulated by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, the General Armament Department of the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the General Armament Department) and the competent department of the military electronics industry, and shall be adjusted as appropriate. The formulation and adjustment of the License Catalog shall solicit the opinions of the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military departments.  
Licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment shall be subject to classified administration within the scope determined by the License Catalog.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　未取得武器装备科研生产许可，不得从事许可目录所列的武器装备科研生产活动。但是，经国务院、中央军事委员会批准的除外。

**Article 3** Without obtaining a license for research and production of weapons and equipment, no person shall engage in the research and production activities of weapons and equipment listed in the License Catalog, except as approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　武器装备科研生产许可管理，应当遵循统筹兼顾、合理布局、鼓励竞争、安全保密的原则。

**Article 4** The administration of licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment shall follow the principles of overall planning, rational distribution, encouraging competition, and security and confidentiality.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门，依照本条例规定对全国的武器装备科研生产许可实施监督管理。  
总装备部协同国务院国防科技工业主管部门对全国的武器装备科研生产许可实施监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门，依照本条例规定对本行政区域的武器装备科研生产许可实施监督管理。

**Article 5** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council shall, in accordance with these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment nationwide.  
The General Armament Department shall, in coordination with the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, exercise supervision and administration over the licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment nationwide.  
The departments responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位，应当在许可范围内从事武器装备科研生产活动，按照国家要求或者合同约定提供合格的科研成果和武器装备。

**Article 6** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall engage in research and production activities of weapons and equipment within the licensed scope, and shall provide qualified scientific research achievements and weapons and equipment in accordance with state requirements or contractual agreements.

### 第二章　许可程序

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请武器装备科研生产许可的单位，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)具有法人资格；  
(二)有与申请从事的武器装备科研生产活动相适应的专业技术人员；  
(三)有与申请从事的武器装备科研生产活动相适应的科研生产条件和检验检测、试验手段；  
(四)有与申请从事的武器装备科研生产活动相适应的技术和工艺；  
(五)经评定合格的质量管理体系；  
(六)与申请从事的武器装备科研生产活动相适应的安全生产条件；  
(七)有与申请从事的武器装备科研生产活动相适应的保密资格。

**Article 7** A unit applying for a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having professional and technical personnel suitable for the research and production activities of weapons and equipment applied for;  
(3) having research and production conditions as well as inspection, testing and experimentation means suitable for the research and production activities of weapons and equipment applied for;  
(4) having technologies and processes suitable for the research and production activities of weapons and equipment applied for;  
(5) having a quality management system that has been evaluated and found qualified;  
(6) having work safety conditions suitable for the research and production activities of weapons and equipment applied for;  
(7) having the confidentiality qualification suitable for the research and production activities of weapons and equipment applied for.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请武器装备科研生产许可的单位，应当向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门提出申请。  
许可目录规定应当向国务院国防科技工业主管部门申请武器装备科研生产许可的，应当直接向国务院国防科技工业主管部门提出申请，并将申请材料同时报送总装备部。

**Article 8** A unit applying for a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall submit an application to the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
Where the License Catalog provides that an application for a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall be submitted to the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, the application shall be submitted directly to that department, and the application materials shall be submitted simultaneously to the General Armament Department.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门收到申请后，应当依照《中华人民共和国行政许可法》规定的程序办理。

**Article 9** After receiving an application, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall handle the application in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门组织对申请单位进行审查，应当征求中国人民解放军派驻的军事代表机构(以下简称军事代表机构)的意见，并自受理申请之日起30日内完成审查，将审查意见和全部申请材料报送国务院国防科技工业主管部门，同时报送总装备部。

**Article 10** The department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the examination of the applicant unit, shall solicit the opinions of the military representative office stationed by the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the military representative office), and shall complete the examination within 30 days from the date of accepting the application, and submit the examination opinions and all application materials to the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, and simultaneously submit them to the General Armament Department.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门受理申请后，应当进行审查，并自受理申请之日起60日内或者自收到省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门报送的审查意见和全部申请材料之日起30日内，做出决定。做出准予许可决定的，应当自做出决定之日起10日内向提出申请的单位颁发武器装备科研生产许可证；做出不准予许可决定的，应当书面通知提出申请的单位，并说明理由。  
国务院国防科技工业主管部门在做出决定前，应当书面征求总装备部的意见，总装备部应当在10日内回复意见。

**Article 11** After accepting an application, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council shall conduct an examination, and shall make a decision within 60 days from the date of accepting the application, or within 30 days from the date of receiving the examination opinions and all application materials submitted by the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. If a decision to grant the license is made, the competent department shall issue a license for research and production of weapons and equipment to the applicant unit within 10 days from the date of the decision; if a decision not to grant the license is made, the competent department shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.  
Before making a decision, the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council shall solicit the opinions of the General Armament Department in writing, and the General Armament Department shall reply within 10 days.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门根据国家武器装备科研生产能力布局的要求，按照武器装备科研生产的实际需要，经征求总装备部意见，可以对有特殊要求的武器装备科研生产许可做出数量限制。

**Article 12** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council may, in accordance with the requirements of the national layout of research and production capacity for weapons and equipment and the actual needs of research and production of weapons and equipment, and after soliciting the opinions of the General Armament Department, impose quantity restrictions on licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment with special requirements.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　武器装备科研生产许可证应当载明单位名称、法定代表人、许可专业或者产品名称、证书编号、发证日期、有效期等相关内容。  
武器装备科研生产许可证格式由国务院国防科技工业主管部门规定。

**Article 13** A license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall specify the name of the unit, the legal representative, the licensed specialty or product name, the certificate number, the date of issuance, the period of validity and other relevant contents.  
The format of the license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall be prescribed by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当妥善保管武器装备科研生产许可证，严格保密管理，不得泄露武器装备科研生产许可证载明的相关内容。

**Article 14** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall properly keep the license, strictly manage confidentiality, and shall not divulge the relevant contents specified in the license.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当在武器装备科研生产合同、产品出厂证书上标注武器装备科研生产许可证编号。

**Article 15** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall mark the license number on contracts for research and production of weapons and equipment and on product certificates of release.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　任何单位和个人不得伪造、变造武器装备科研生产许可证。取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位不得出租、出借或者以其他方式转让武器装备科研生产许可证。

**Article 16** No unit or individual shall forge or alter a license for research and production of weapons and equipment. A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall not lease, lend or otherwise transfer the license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门，应当将办理武器装备科研生产许可的有关材料及时归档，并妥善保存，严格保密。

**Article 17** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall promptly file the relevant materials for handling licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment, keep them properly, and maintain strict confidentiality.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　取得武器装备科研生产许可并承担武器装备科研生产任务的单位，应当接受军事代表机构的监督。

**Article 18** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment and undertakes research and production tasks of weapons and equipment shall accept the supervision of the military representative office.

### 第三章　保密管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当遵守国家保密法律、法规和有关规定，建立健全保密管理制度，按照积极防范、突出重点、严格标准、明确责任的原则，对落实保密管理制度的情况进行定期或者不定期的检查，及时研究解决保密工作中的问题。

**Article 19** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall comply with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, establish and improve a confidentiality management system, conduct regular or irregular inspections on the implementation of the confidentiality management system in accordance with the principles of active prevention, highlighting key points, strict standards and clear responsibilities, and promptly study and solve problems in confidentiality work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当建立保密管理领导责任制，其主要负责人应当加强对本单位保密工作的组织领导，切实履行保密职责和义务。

**Article 20** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall establish a leadership responsibility system for confidentiality management. Its principal responsible person shall strengthen the organizational leadership of the unit's confidentiality work and effectively fulfill confidentiality duties and obligations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当设立保密工作机构，配备保密管理人员。  
保密管理人员应当熟悉国家保密法律、法规和有关规定，具备保密管理工作能力，掌握保密技术基础知识，并经过必要的培训、考核。

**Article 21** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall set up a confidentiality work organization and be equipped with confidentiality management personnel.  
Confidentiality management personnel shall be familiar with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, possess the capability for confidentiality management work, master basic knowledge of confidentiality technology, and have undergone necessary training and assessment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当与承担武器装备科研生产任务的涉及国家秘密人员签订岗位保密责任书，明确岗位保密责任，并对其进行经常性的保密教育培训。  
涉及国家秘密人员应当熟悉国家保密法律、法规和有关规定，严格按照岗位保密责任书的要求，履行保密义务。

**Article 22** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall sign a post confidentiality responsibility letter with persons involved in state secrets who undertake research and production tasks of weapons and equipment, specify the post confidentiality responsibilities, and provide them with regular confidentiality education and training.  
Persons involved in state secrets shall be familiar with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, and shall strictly fulfill confidentiality obligations in accordance with the requirements of the post confidentiality responsibility letter.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当依照国家保密法律、法规和有关规定，制作、收发、传递、使用、复制、保存和销毁国家秘密载体，严格控制接触国家秘密载体的人员范围。

**Article 23** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall, in accordance with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, produce, receive, transmit, use, reproduce, preserve and destroy state secret carriers, and strictly control the scope of persons who have access to such carriers.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当采取措施，在涉及国家秘密的要害部门、部位设置安全可靠的保密防护设施。

**Article 24** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall take measures to set up safe and reliable confidentiality protection facilities in key departments and positions involving state secrets.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当依照国家保密法律、法规和有关规定对涉及国家秘密的计算机和信息系统采取安全保密防护措施，不得使用无安全保密保障的设备处理、传输、存储国家秘密信息。

**Article 25** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall, in accordance with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, take security and confidentiality protection measures for computers and information systems involving state secrets, and shall not use equipment without security and confidentiality safeguards to process, transmit or store state secret information.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位举办涉及国家秘密的重大会议或者活动，应当制订专项保密工作方案，并确定专人负责保密工作。涉及国家秘密的会议必须在有安全保密保障措施的场所进行，并严格控制与会人员的范围。

**Article 26** Where a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment holds a major meeting or activity involving state secrets, it shall formulate a special confidentiality work plan and designate a specific person to be responsible for confidentiality work. A meeting involving state secrets must be held in a place with security and confidentiality safeguards, and the scope of participants shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位在对外交流、合作和谈判等活动中，应当保守国家秘密，对外提供有关文件资料和实物样品，必须按照规定的程序事先经过批准。

**Article 27** In activities such as foreign exchanges, cooperation and negotiations, a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall maintain state secrets. Providing relevant documents, materials and physical samples to foreign parties must be approved in advance in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位应当依照国家保密法律、法规和有关规定建立保密档案制度，对涉及国家秘密人员的管理、泄密事件查处等情况进行记录，及时归档，并对涉及国家秘密的档案实施有效管理。

**Article 28** A unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall, in accordance with the state laws, regulations and relevant provisions on confidentiality, establish a confidentiality archives system, keep records of the management of persons involved in state secrets, the investigation and handling of leakage incidents and other matters, promptly file them, and effectively manage archives involving state secrets.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　未依照本条例规定申请取得武器装备科研生产许可，擅自从事许可目录范围内武器装备科研生产活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法生产的产品，并处违法生产产品货值金额1倍以上3倍以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 29** Where a person, without applying for and obtaining a license for research and production of weapons and equipment in accordance with these Regulations, engages without authorization in research and production activities of weapons and equipment within the scope of the License Catalog, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, the illegally produced products shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the illegally produced products shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位，出租、出借或者以其他方式转让武器装备科研生产许可证的，处10万元罚款；情节严重的，吊销武器装备科研生产许可证。违法接受并使用他人提供的武器装备科研生产许可证的，责令停止武器装备生产活动，没收违法生产的产品，并处违法生产产品货值金额1倍以上3倍以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 30** Where a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment leases, lends or otherwise transfers the license, a fine of 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the license for research and production of weapons and equipment shall be revoked. Where a person illegally accepts and uses a license for research and production of weapons and equipment provided by another person, the weapons and equipment production activities shall be ordered to cease, the illegally produced products shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the illegally produced products shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　伪造、变造武器装备科研生产许可证的，责令停止违法行为，处10万元罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 31** Where a person forges or alters a license for research and production of weapons and equipment, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, and a fine of 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得武器装备科研生产许可的，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，并依照《中华人民共和国行政许可法》的有关规定处理。

**Article 32** Where a person obtains a license for research and production of weapons and equipment by fraudulent, bribery or other improper means, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由同级监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)对符合本条例规定条件的申请不予受理的；  
(二)未依法说明不准予许可的理由的。

**Article 33** Where the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and their staff members violate the provisions of these Regulations and have any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ at the same level shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to accept an application that meets the conditions prescribed by these Regulations;  
(2) failing to state the reasons for not granting the license according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门有下列情形之一的，由同级监察机关责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)对不符合本条例规定条件的申请人准予许可或者超越法定职权做出准予许可决定的；  
(二)对符合本条例规定条件的申请人不准予许可或者不在法定期限内做出准予许可决定的；  
(三)发现未依照本条例规定申请取得武器装备科研生产许可而擅自从事列入许可目录的武器装备科研生产活动，不及时依法查处的。

**Article 34** Where the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council or the department responsible for the administration of science, technology and industry for national defense of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government has any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ at the same level shall order correction, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) granting a license to an applicant who does not meet the conditions prescribed by these Regulations, or making a decision to grant the license beyond the statutory authority;  
(2) not granting a license to an applicant who meets the conditions prescribed by these Regulations, or failing to make a decision on granting the license within the statutory time limit;  
(3) discovering that a person, without applying for and obtaining a license for research and production of weapons and equipment in accordance with these Regulations, engages without authorization in research and production activities of weapons and equipment included in the License Catalog, and failing to investigate and deal with it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位违反[本条例第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条、第二十一条、第二十二条、第二十八条规定的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment violates the provisions of Articles 19, 20, 21, 22 or 28 of these Regulations, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)、第二十四条、第二十五条、第二十六条、第二十七条规定的，责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，责令停业整顿直至吊销武器装备科研生产许可证。

**Article 36** Where a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment violates the provisions of Articles 23, 24, 25, 26 or 27 of these Regulations, it shall be ordered to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the unit shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the license for research and production of weapons and equipment may be revoked.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　取得武器装备科研生产许可的单位违反本条例规定，被吊销武器装备科研生产许可证的，在3年内不得再次申请武器装备科研生产许可。

**Article 37** Where a unit that has obtained a license for research and production of weapons and equipment violates the provisions of these Regulations and its license is revoked, it shall not apply for a license for research and production of weapons and equipment again within three years.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门实施。

**Article 38** The administrative penalties prescribed by these Regulations shall be imposed by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依照本条例规定实施武器装备科研生产许可，不得收取任何费用。

**Article 40** No fees shall be charged for implementing licenses for research and production of weapons and equipment in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例施行前已经从事武器装备科研生产活动的单位应当自本条例施行之日起，在国务院国防科技工业主管部门规定的期限内，依照本条例规定申请取得武器装备科研生产许可。

**Article 41** A unit that had already been engaged in research and production activities of weapons and equipment before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, from the date of coming into force of these Regulations and within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council, apply for and obtain a license for research and production of weapons and equipment in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　军工电子行业科研生产许可管理，由其主管部门参照本条例规定执行。

**Article 42** The administration of licenses for research and production of the military electronics industry shall be implemented by its competent department with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例自2008年4月1日起施行。

**Article 43** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 2008.
