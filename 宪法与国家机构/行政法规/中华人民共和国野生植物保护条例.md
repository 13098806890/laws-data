# 中华人民共和国野生植物保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：1997-01-01

> (1996年9月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第204号发布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保护、发展和合理利用野生植物资源，保护生物多样性，维护生态平衡，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事野生植物的保护、发展和利用活动，必须遵守本条例。 本条例所保护的野生植物，是指原生地天然生长的珍贵植物和原生地天然生长并具有重要经济、科学研究、文化价值的濒危、稀有植物。 药用野生植物和城市园林、自然保护区、风景名胜区内的野生植物的保护，同时适用有关法律、行政法规。 第三条　国家对野生植物资源实行加强保护、积极发展、合理利用的方针。 第四条　国家保护依法开发利用和经营管理野生植物资源的单位和个人的合法权益。 第五条　国家鼓励和支持野生植物科学研究、野生植物的就地保护和迁地保护。 在野生植物资源保护、科学研究、培育利用和宣传教育方面成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。 第六条　县级以上各级人民政府有关主管部门应当开展保护野生植物的宣传教育，普及野生植物知识，提高公民保护野生植物的意识。 第七条　任何单位和个人都有保护野生植物资源的义务，对侵占或者破坏野生植物及其生长环境的行为有权检举和控告。 第八条　国务院林业行政主管部门主管全国林区内野生植物和林区外珍贵野生树木的监督管理工作。国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国其他野生植物的监督管理工作。 国务院建设行政部门负责城市园林、风景名胜区内野生植物的监督管理工作。国务院环境保护部门负责对全国野生植物环境保护工作的协调和监督。国务院其他有关部门依照职责分工负责有关的野生植物保护工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责野生植物管理工作的部门及其职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地具体情况规定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护、发展和合理利用野生植物资源，保护生物多样性，维护生态平衡，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of protecting, developing and rationally utilizing wild plant resources, protecting biodiversity, and maintaining ecological balance.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事野生植物的保护、发展和利用活动，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所保护的野生植物，是指原生地天然生长的珍贵植物和原生地天然生长并具有重要经济、科学研究、文化价值的濒危、稀有植物。  
药用野生植物和城市园林、自然保护区、风景名胜区内的野生植物的保护，同时适用有关法律、行政法规。

**Article 2** Anyone who engages in the protection, development and utilization of wild plants within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.  
Wild plants protected under these Regulations refer to precious plants naturally growing in their native habitats and endangered or rare plants naturally growing in their native habitats and having important economic, scientific research or cultural value.  
The protection of medicinal wild plants and wild plants in urban gardens, nature reserves, and scenic and historic areas shall also be governed by the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对野生植物资源实行加强保护、积极发展、合理利用的方针。

**Article 3** The State shall implement the policy of strengthening protection, actively developing and rationally utilizing wild plant resources.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家保护依法开发利用和经营管理野生植物资源的单位和个人的合法权益。

**Article 4** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of units and individuals that develop, utilize, manage and operate wild plant resources according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持野生植物科学研究、野生植物的就地保护和迁地保护。  
在野生植物资源保护、科学研究、培育利用和宣传教育方面成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on wild plants, and in-situ conservation and ex-situ conservation of wild plants.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the protection, scientific research, cultivation and utilization of wild plant resources, and in publicity and education, shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上各级人民政府有关主管部门应当开展保护野生植物的宣传教育，普及野生植物知识，提高公民保护野生植物的意识。

**Article 6** The competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out publicity and education on the protection of wild plants, disseminate knowledge of wild plants, and raise citizens' awareness of wild plant protection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人都有保护野生植物资源的义务，对侵占或者破坏野生植物及其生长环境的行为有权检举和控告。

**Article 7** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to protect wild plant resources, and shall have the right to report and accuse any act of encroaching upon or destroying wild plants or their growing environment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院林业行政主管部门主管全国林区内野生植物和林区外珍贵野生树木的监督管理工作。国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国其他野生植物的监督管理工作。  
国务院建设行政部门负责城市园林、风景名胜区内野生植物的监督管理工作。国务院环境保护部门负责对全国野生植物环境保护工作的协调和监督。国务院其他有关部门依照职责分工负责有关的野生植物保护工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责野生植物管理工作的部门及其职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地具体情况规定。

**Article 8** The competent forestry administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of wild plants in forest regions and precious wild trees outside forest regions nationwide. The competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of other wild plants nationwide.  
The competent construction administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of wild plants in urban gardens and scenic and historic areas. The competent environmental protection department of the State Council shall be responsible for the coordination and supervision of the environmental protection work for wild plants nationwide. The other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant wild plant protection work in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
The departments responsible for wild plant management work of the local people's governments at or above the county level and their functions and duties shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in light of the specific local conditions.

### 第二章　野生植物保护

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家保护野生植物及其生长环境。禁止任何单位和个人非法采集野生植物或者破坏其生长环境。

**Article 9** The State shall protect wild plants and their growing environment. No unit or individual may illegally collect wild plants or destroy their growing environment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　野生植物分为国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物。  
国家重点保护野生植物分为国家一级保护野生植物和国家二级保护野生植物。国家重点保护野生植物名录，由国务院林业行政主管部门、农业行政主管部门(以下简称国务院野生植物行政主管部门)商国务院环境保护、建设等有关部门制定，报国务院批准公布。  
地方重点保护野生植物，是指国家重点保护野生植物以外，由省、自治区、直辖市保护的野生植物。地方重点保护野生植物名录，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定并公布，报国务院备案。

**Article 10** Wild plants are classified into state-key protected wild plants and local-key protected wild plants.  
State-key protected wild plants are classified into Grade I state-protected wild plants and Grade II state-protected wild plants. The list of state-key protected wild plants shall be formulated by the competent forestry administrative department and the competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the competent wild plant administrative departments of the State Council) after consultation with the competent environmental protection, construction and other relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before being published.  
Local-key protected wild plants refer to wild plants protected by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government other than state-key protected wild plants. The list of local-key protected wild plants shall be formulated and published by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在国家重点保护野生植物物种和地方重点保护野生植物物种的天然集中分布区域，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，建立自然保护区；在其他区域，县级以上地方人民政府野生植物行政主管部门和其他有关部门可以根据实际情况建立国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物的保护点或者设立保护标志。  
禁止破坏国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物的保护点的保护设施和保护标志。

**Article 11** In areas where species of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants are naturally concentrated, nature reserves shall be established in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; in other areas, the wild plant administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments may, based on actual conditions, establish protection sites for national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants or set up protection signs.  
It shall be prohibited to damage the protection facilities and protection signs of the protection sites for national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　野生植物行政主管部门及其他有关部门应当监视、监测环境对国家重点保护野生植物生长和地方重点保护野生植物生长的影响，并采取措施，维护和改善国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物的生长条件。由于环境影响对国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物的生长造成危害时，野生植物行政主管部门应当会同其他有关部门调查并依法处理。

**Article 12** The wild plant administrative departments and other relevant departments shall monitor and survey the impact of the environment on the growth of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants, and shall take measures to maintain and improve the growth conditions of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants. When environmental impact causes harm to the growth of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants, the wild plant administrative departments shall, together with other relevant departments, investigate and handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设项目对国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物的生长环境产生不利影响的，建设单位提交的环境影响报告书中必须对此作出评价；环境保护部门在审批环境影响报告书时，应当征求野生植物行政主管部门的意见。

**Article 13** Where a construction project has an adverse impact on the growth environment of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants, the environmental impact report submitted by the construction unit must make an evaluation thereof; when examining and approving the environmental impact report, the environmental protection department shall solicit the opinions of the wild plant administrative department.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　野生植物行政主管部门和有关单位对生长受到威胁的国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物应当采取拯救措施，保护或者恢复其生长环境，必要时应当建立繁育基地、种质资源库或者采取迁地保护措施。

**Article 14** The wild plant administrative departments and relevant units shall take rescue measures for national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants whose growth is threatened, protect or restore their growth environment, and where necessary, shall establish breeding bases, germplasm resource banks or take ex situ conservation measures.

### 第三章　野生植物管理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　野生植物行政主管部门应当定期组织国家重点保护野生植物和地方重点保护野生植物资源调查，建立资源档案。

**Article 15** The wild plant administrative departments shall periodically organize surveys of the resources of national key protected wild plants and local key protected wild plants, and establish resource archives.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　禁止采集国家一级保护野生植物。因科学研究、人工培育、文化交流等特殊需要，采集国家一级保护野生植物的，应当按照管理权限向国务院林业行政主管部门或者其授权的机构申请采集证；或者向采集地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门或者其授权的机构申请采集证。  
采集国家二级保护野生植物的，必须经采集地的县级人民政府野生植物行政主管部门签署意见后，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生植物行政主管部门或者其授权的机构申请采集证。  
采集城市园林或者风景名胜区内的国家一级或者二级保护野生植物的，须先征得城市园林或者风景名胜区管理机构同意，分别依照前两款的规定申请采集证。  
采集珍贵野生树木或者林区内、草原上的野生植物的，依照森林法、草原法的规定办理。  
野生植物行政主管部门发放采集证后，应当抄送环境保护部门备案。  
采集证的格式由国务院野生植物行政主管部门制定。

**Article 16** It shall be prohibited to collect wild plants under national first-class protection. Where collection of wild plants under national first-class protection is necessary for special needs such as scientific research, artificial cultivation, or cultural exchange, a collection permit shall be applied for from the forestry administrative department of the State Council or the institution authorized thereby according to the administrative authority; or a collection permit shall be applied for from the agricultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the collection is conducted, or from the institution authorized thereby.  
For collection of wild plants under national second-class protection, the collector must, after obtaining the comments signed by the wild plant administrative department of the county people's government where the collection is conducted, apply for a collection permit from the wild plant administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or from the institution authorized thereby.  
For collection of wild plants under national first-class or second-class protection within an urban garden or a scenic spot, consent of the administrative authority of the urban garden or scenic spot shall first be obtained, and a collection permit shall be applied for separately in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.  
Collection of precious wild trees or wild plants within forest areas or on grasslands shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Forest Law and the Grassland Law.  
After a wild plant administrative department issues a collection permit, it shall send a copy to the environmental protection department for the record.  
The format of the collection permit shall be formulated by the wild plant administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　采集国家重点保护野生植物的单位和个人，必须按照采集证规定的种类、数量、地点、期限和方法进行采集。  
县级人民政府野生植物行政主管部门对在本行政区域内采集国家重点保护野生植物的活动，应当进行监督检查，并及时报告批准采集的野生植物行政主管部门或者其授权的机构。

**Article 17** Units and individuals that collect national key protected wild plants must conduct collection in accordance with the species, quantity, location, time limit, and method specified in the collection permit.  
The wild plant administrative department of a county people's government shall supervise and inspect the activities of collecting national key protected wild plants within its administrative region, and shall promptly report to the wild plant administrative department that approved the collection or its authorized institution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止出售、收购国家一级保护野生植物。  
出售、收购国家二级保护野生植物的，必须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生植物行政主管部门或者其授权的机构批准。

**Article 18** It shall be prohibited to sell or purchase wild plants under national first-class protection.  
The sale or purchase of wild plants under national second-class protection must be approved by the wild plant administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or by the institution authorized thereby.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　野生植物行政主管部门应当对经营利用国家二级保护野生植物的活动进行监督检查。

**Article 19** The wild plant administrative departments shall supervise and inspect the activities of operating and utilizing wild plants under national second-class protection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出口国家重点保护野生植物或者进出口中国参加的国际公约所限制进出口的野生植物的，应当按照管理权限经国务院林业行政主管部门批准，或者经进出口者所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门审核后报国务院农业行政主管部门批准，并取得国家濒危物种进出口管理机构核发的允许进出口证明书或者标签。海关凭允许进出口证明书或者标签查验放行。国务院野生植物行政主管部门应当将有关野生植物进出口的资料抄送国务院环境保护部门。  
禁止出口未定名的或者新发现并有重要价值的野生植物。

**Article 20** For the export of national key protected wild plants or the import or export of wild plants restricted by international conventions to which China is a party, approval shall be obtained from the forestry administrative department of the State Council according to the administrative authority, or after examination by the agricultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the importer or exporter is located, the matter shall be submitted to the agricultural administrative department of the State Council for approval, and a permit certificate or label for import and export issued by the state administrative authority for the import and export of endangered species shall be obtained. Customs shall inspect and release the goods based on the permit certificate or label. The wild plant administrative department of the State Council shall send a copy of the relevant information on the import and export of wild plants to the environmental protection department of the State Council.  
It shall be prohibited to export unnamed or newly discovered wild plants of significant value.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国人不得在中国境内采集或者收购国家重点保护野生植物。  
外国人在中国境内对农业行政主管部门管理的国家重点保护野生植物进行野外考察的，应当经农业行政主管部门管理的国家重点保护野生植物所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 21** Foreigners shall not collect or purchase wild plants under special state protection within the territory of China.  
Where a foreigner conducts field investigation on wild plants under special state protection managed by the agricultural administrative department within the territory of China, approval shall be obtained from the agricultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the wild plants under special state protection managed by the agricultural administrative department are located.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地方重点保护野生植物的管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 22** The administrative measures for local key protected wild plants shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　未取得采集证或者未按照采集证的规定采集国家重点保护野生植物的，由野生植物行政主管部门没收所采集的野生植物和违法所得，可以并处违法所得10倍以下的罚款；有采集证的，并可以吊销采集证。

**Article 23** Where a person collects wild plants under special state protection without obtaining a collection permit or in violation of the provisions of the collection permit, the wild plant administrative department shall confiscate the collected wild plants and illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than ten times the illegal gains; where a collection permit has been obtained, the collection permit may also be revoked.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，出售、收购国家重点保护野生植物的，由工商行政管理部门或者野生植物行政主管部门按照职责分工没收野生植物和违法所得，可以并处违法所得10倍以下的罚款。

**Article 24** Where a person sells or purchases wild plants under special state protection in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce or the wild plant administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the wild plants and illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than ten times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　非法进出口野生植物的，由海关依照海关法的规定处罚。

**Article 25** Where a person illegally imports or exports wild plants, the customs shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　伪造、倒卖、转让采集证、允许进出口证明书或者有关批准文件、标签的，由野生植物行政主管部门或者工商行政管理部门按照职责分工收缴，没收违法所得，可以并处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Where a person forges, scalps, or transfers collection permits, import and export permits, or relevant approval documents or labels, the wild plant administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate them, confiscate illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　外国人在中国境内采集、收购国家重点保护野生植物，或者未经批准对农业行政主管部门管理的国家重点保护野生植物进行野外考察的，由野生植物行政主管部门没收所采集、收购的野生植物和考察资料，可以并处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where a foreigner collects or purchases wild plants under special state protection within the territory of China, or conducts field investigation on wild plants under special state protection managed by the agricultural administrative department without approval, the wild plant administrative department shall confiscate the collected or purchased wild plants and investigation materials, and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　野生植物行政主管部门的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 29** Where a staff member of the wild plant administrative department abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　依照本条例规定没收的实物，由作出没收决定的机关按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 30** The physical objects confiscated according to the provisions of these Regulations shall be disposed of by the organ that made the confiscation decision in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的与保护野生植物有关的国际条约与本条例有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 31** Where the provisions of an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China relating to the protection of wild plants differ from those of these Regulations, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply, except for the clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自1997年1月1日起施行。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1997.
