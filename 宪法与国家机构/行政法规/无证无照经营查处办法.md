# 无证无照经营查处办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-08-06  
**生效日期**：2017-10-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护社会主义市场经济秩序，促进公平竞争，保护经营者和消费者的合法权益，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to maintain the order of the socialist market economy, promote fair competition, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of business operators and consumers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　任何单位或者个人不得违反法律、法规、国务院决定的规定，从事无证无照经营。

**Article 2** No entity or individual shall engage in business operations without a license or without a business license in violation of laws, regulations, or decisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　下列经营活动，不属于无证无照经营：  
(一)在县级以上地方人民政府指定的场所和时间，销售农副产品、日常生活用品，或者个人利用自己的技能从事依法无须取得许可的便民劳务活动；  
(二)依照法律、行政法规、国务院决定的规定，从事无须取得许可或者办理注册登记的经营活动。

**Article 3** The following business activities shall not be deemed as unlicensed or unregistered business operations:  
(1) selling agricultural by-products or daily necessities at places and times designated by local people's governments at or above the county level, or individuals using their own skills to engage in便民 labor activities for which no license is required according to law; and  
(2) engaging in business activities for which no license or registration is required in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府负责组织、协调本行政区域的无证无照经营查处工作，建立有关部门分工负责、协调配合的无证无照经营查处工作机制。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and coordinating the investigation and punishment of unlicensed and unregistered business operations within their respective administrative regions, and shall establish a working mechanism for the investigation and punishment of such operations with relevant departments each assuming their respective responsibilities and coordinating with one another.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营者未依法取得许可从事经营活动的，由法律、法规、国务院决定规定的部门予以查处；法律、法规、国务院决定没有规定或者规定不明确的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的部门予以查处。

**Article 5** Where a business operator engages in business activities without obtaining a license according to law, the department specified by laws, regulations, or decisions of the State Council shall investigate and punish such activity; where no such department is specified or the specifications are unclear in laws, regulations, or decisions of the State Council, the department designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall investigate and punish such activity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营者未依法取得营业执照从事经营活动的，由履行工商行政管理职责的部门(以下称工商行政管理部门)予以查处。

**Article 6** Where a business operator engages in business activities without obtaining a business license according to law, the department performing the functions of industrial and commercial administration (hereinafter referred to as the administrative department for industry and commerce) shall investigate and punish such activity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经营者未依法取得许可且未依法取得营业执照从事经营活动的，依照[本办法第五条](#art-5)的规定予以查处。

**Article 7** Where a business operator engages in business activities without obtaining a license according to law and without obtaining a business license according to law, the investigation and punishment shall be conducted in accordance with Article 5 of these Measures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工商行政管理部门以及法律、法规、国务院决定规定的部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的部门(以下统称查处部门)应当依法履行职责，密切协同配合，利用信息网络平台加强信息共享；发现不属于本部门查处职责的无证无照经营，应当及时通报有关部门。

**Article 8** The administrative department for industry and commerce, the departments specified by laws, regulations, or decisions of the State Council, and the departments designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter collectively referred to as the investigating departments) shall perform their duties according to law, cooperate closely with one another, and use information network platforms to strengthen information sharing; where they discover any unlicensed or unregistered business operation that does not fall within their own investigating responsibilities, they shall promptly notify the relevant departments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位或者个人有权向查处部门举报无证无照经营。  
查处部门应当向社会公开受理举报的电话、信箱或者电子邮件地址，并安排人员受理举报，依法予以处理。对实名举报的，查处部门应当告知处理结果，并为举报人保密。

**Article 9** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report unlicensed or unregistered business operations to the investigating departments.  
The investigating departments shall make public to the society the telephone numbers, mailboxes, or e-mail addresses for receiving reports, and shall arrange personnel to receive reports and handle them according to law. For reports with real names, the investigating departments shall inform the reporters of the handling results and keep the reporters confidential.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　查处部门依法查处无证无照经营，应当坚持查处与引导相结合、处罚与教育相结合的原则，对具备办理证照的法定条件、经营者有继续经营意愿的，应当督促、引导其依法办理相应证照。

**Article 10** When investigating and punishing unlicensed or unregistered business operations according to law, the investigating departments shall adhere to the principles of combining investigation with guidance and combining punishment with education. Where the statutory requirements for obtaining licenses or business licenses are met and the business operator has the intention to continue business, the departments shall urge and guide the operator to obtain the corresponding licenses or business licenses according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门对涉嫌无照经营进行查处，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)责令停止相关经营活动；  
(二)向与涉嫌无照经营有关的单位和个人调查了解有关情况；  
(三)进入涉嫌从事无照经营的场所实施现场检查；  
(四)查阅、复制与涉嫌无照经营有关的合同、票据、账簿以及其他有关资料。  
对涉嫌从事无照经营的场所，可以予以查封；对涉嫌用于无照经营的工具、设备、原材料、产品(商品)等物品，可以予以查封、扣押。  
对涉嫌无证经营进行查处，依照相关法律、法规的规定采取措施。

**Article 11** When investigating and punishing suspected unregistered business operations, the administrative department for industry and commerce of a people's government at or above the county level may exercise the following powers:  
(1) ordering the cessation of the relevant business activities;  
(2) investigating and understanding the relevant circumstances from entities or individuals related to the suspected unregistered business operation;  
(3) entering the premises suspected of being used for unregistered business operations to conduct on-site inspections;  
(4) consulting and copying contracts, bills, account books, and other relevant materials related to the suspected unregistered business operation.  
The premises suspected of being used for unregistered business operations may be sealed up; the tools, equipment, raw materials, products (commodities), and other articles suspected of being used for unregistered business operations may be sealed up or seized.  
When investigating and punishing suspected unlicensed business operations, measures shall be taken in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事无证经营的，由查处部门依照相关法律、法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 12** Any person who engages in unlicensed business operations shall be punished by the investigating departments in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事无照经营的，由工商行政管理部门依照相关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。法律、行政法规对无照经营的处罚没有明确规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 13** Any person who engages in unregistered business operations shall be punished by the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations. Where the laws and administrative regulations have no clear provisions on the punishment for unregistered business operations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　明知属于无照经营而为经营者提供经营场所，或者提供运输、保管、仓储等条件的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 14** Any person who, knowing that a business operation is unregistered, provides business premises or provides conditions such as transportation, custody, or warehousing for the business operator, shall be ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and may be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　任何单位或者个人从事无证无照经营的，由查处部门记入信用记录，并依照相关法律、法规的规定予以公示。

**Article 15** Any entity or individual that engages in unlicensed or unregistered business operations shall have such information recorded in the credit record by the investigating departments and shall be publicized in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　妨害查处部门查处无证无照经营，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚。

**Article 16** Where any person obstructs the investigating departments in investigating and punishing unlicensed or unregistered business operations, constituting a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose punishment in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　查处部门及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 17** Where any investigating department or its staff member abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in irregularities for personal gain, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本办法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Any violation of the provisions of these Measures that constitutes a crime shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本办法自2017年10月1日起施行。2003年1月6日国务院公布的《无照经营查处取缔办法》同时废止。

**Article 19** These Measures shall come into force as of October 1, 2017. The Measures for the Investigation and Punishment of Unlicensed Business Operations promulgated by the State Council on January 6, 2003 shall be abolished simultaneously.
