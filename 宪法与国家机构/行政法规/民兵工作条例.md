**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1991-01-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好民兵工作，加强国防后备力量建设，根据《中华人民共和国兵役法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of ensuring proper militia work and strengthening the building of national defense reserve forces.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　民兵是中国共产党领导的不脱离生产的群众武装组织，是中华人民共和国武装力量的组成部分，是中国人民解放军的助手和后备力量。

**Article 2** The militia is a mass armed organization under the leadership of the Communist Party of China that does not divorce itself from production; it is a component of the armed forces of the People's Republic of China and an auxiliary and reserve force of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民兵工作的任务是：  
(一)建立和巩固民兵组织，提高民兵军政素质，配备和管理民兵武器装备，储备战时所需的后备兵员；  
(二)发动民兵参加社会主义现代化建设，组织民兵担负战备执勤，维护社会治安；  
(三)组织民兵参军参战，支援前线，抵抗侵略，保卫祖国。

**Article 3** The tasks of militia work are:  
(1) to establish and consolidate militia organizations, improve the military and political quality of the militia, equip and manage militia weapons and equipment, and reserve the necessary reserve personnel for wartime;  
(2) to mobilize the militia to participate in socialist modernization, organize the militia to undertake combat readiness duty and maintain public security;  
(3) to organize the militia to join the army and participate in war, support the front, resist aggression, and defend the motherland.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民兵工作应当贯彻人民战争思想，坚持劳武结合，坚持民兵制度与预备役制度、民兵工作与战时兵员动员准备工作的结合。

**Article 4** Militia work shall implement the thought of people's war, adhere to the combination of work and military training, and adhere to the integration of the militia system with the reserve service system, and the integration of militia work with wartime personnel mobilization preparations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国的民兵工作在国务院、中央军委领导下，由中国人民解放军总参谋部主管。  
军区按照上级赋予的任务，负责本区域的民兵工作。  
省军区(含卫戍区、警备区，下同)、军分区(含警备区，下同)、县(含市、市辖区，下同)人民武装部，是本地区的军事领导指挥机关，负责本区域的民兵工作。  
乡、民族乡、镇、街道和企业事业单位设立的人民武装部，负责办理本区域、本单位的民兵工作，按规定不设立人民武装部的街道、企业事业单位，确定一个部门办理。

**Article 5** Militia work nationwide shall be under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and shall be directed by the General Staff Department of the People's Liberation Army.  
Military regions shall, in accordance with the tasks assigned by higher authorities, be responsible for militia work within their respective regions.  
The provincial military commands (including the Beijing Garrison Command, the garrison commands, the same below), military sub-commands (including the garrison commands, the same below), and the people's armed forces departments of counties (including cities and municipal districts, the same below) are the military leadership and command organs of their respective areas and shall be responsible for militia work within their respective regions.  
The people's armed forces departments established in townships, ethnic townships, towns, subdistricts, enterprises, and institutions shall be responsible for handling militia work within their respective regions or units. For subdistricts, enterprises, and institutions that do not have a people's armed forces department as prescribed, a department shall be designated to handle such work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　乡、民族乡、镇、街道和企业事业单位的人民武装部体制的变动，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 6** Changes in the system of the people's armed forces departments in townships, ethnic townships, towns, subdistricts, enterprises, and institutions shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　地方各级人民政府必须加强对民兵工作的领导，统筹安排民兵工作，组织和监督完成民兵工作任务。地方各级人民政府的有关部门，应当协助军事机关开展民兵工作，解决有关问题。  
企业事业单位应当按照当地人民政府和本地区军事领导指挥机关的要求，把民兵工作纳入管理计划，完成民兵工作任务。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at all levels must strengthen leadership over militia work, make overall arrangements for militia work, and organize and supervise the fulfillment of militia work tasks. The relevant departments of local people's governments at all levels shall assist the military organs in carrying out militia work and solve relevant problems.  
Enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and the military leadership and command organs of the region, incorporate militia work into their management plans and fulfill militia work tasks.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　民兵应当做到：服从组织领导，听从上级指挥，掌握军事技术，爱护武器装备，学习政治文化，带头参加生产劳动，遵守法律、法规，保护群众利益。

**Article 8** The militia shall: obey organizational leadership, follow the commands of superiors, master military techniques, take care of weapons and equipment, study politics and culture, take the lead in participating in productive labor, abide by laws and regulations, and protect the interests of the masses.

### 第二章　民兵组织

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民兵的组建范围，按照[《中华人民共和国兵役法》第三十七条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国兵役法.md#art-37)的规定执行。

**Article 9** The scope of establishment of the militia shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 37 of the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公民应当依照[《中华人民共和国兵役法》第三十七条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国兵役法.md#art-37)、三十八条的规定参加民兵组织。

**Article 10** Citizens shall participate in militia organizations in accordance with the provisions of Articles 37 and 38 of the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　民兵按照便于领导，便于活动，便于执行任务的原则编组。农村一般以行政村为单位编民兵连或者营，城市一般以企业事业单位、街道为单位编民兵排、连、营、团。  
基干民兵单独编组，根据民兵人数分别编班、排、连、营或者团。  
根据战备需要和现有武器装备，在基干民兵中组建民兵专业技术分队；在重点人防城市、交通枢纽和其他重要防卫目标地区，组建民兵高炮营、团。民兵专业技术分队可以跨单位编组。

**Article 11** The militia shall be organized according to the principles of facilitating leadership, facilitating activities, and facilitating the execution of tasks. In rural areas, militia companies or battalions shall generally be organized on the basis of administrative villages; in urban areas, militia platoons, companies, battalions, or regiments shall generally be organized on the basis of enterprises, institutions, or subdistricts.  
The primary militia shall be organized separately, and shall be respectively organized into squads, platoons, companies, battalions, or regiments according to the number of militiamen.  
According to the needs of combat readiness and existing weapons and equipment, militia specialized technical detachments shall be formed among the primary militia; in key civil air defense cities, transportation hubs, and other important defense target areas, militia anti-aircraft artillery battalions and regiments shall be formed. Militia specialized technical detachments may be organized across units.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民兵干部由政治思想好、身体健康、年纪较轻、有一定文化知识和军事素质、热爱民兵工作的人员担任。  
民兵干部应当优先从转业、退伍军人中选拔。

**Article 12** Militia cadres shall be held by personnel who have good political ideology, good health, relatively young age, certain educational level and military quality, and love militia work.  
Militia cadres shall be selected preferentially from among demobilized and retired servicemen.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民兵干部由本单位提名，由基层人民武装部或者本地区军事领导指挥机关按照任免权限任命。  
企业事业单位的民兵连以上军政主官，由本单位负责人兼任。  
基干民兵连长或者营长，由专职人民武装干部或者本单位负责人兼任。

**Article 13** Militia cadres shall be nominated by their own units and appointed by the grassroots people's armed forces department or the military leadership command organ of the region according to the authority of appointment and removal.  
The military and political principal leaders at and above the company level of militia in enterprises and institutions shall be concurrently held by the responsible persons of the units.  
The company commander or battalion commander of the primary militia shall be concurrently held by a full-time people's armed forces cadre or the responsible person of the unit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民兵组织每年整顿一次。整顿的内容包括：对民兵进行宣传教育、民兵的出入转队、调配干部、工作总结、清点装备、健全制度、集结点验等项工作。  
退出现役的士兵，符合服预备役条件的，应当及时编入民兵组织。

**Article 14** Militia organizations shall be rectified once each year. The contents of rectification include: conducting propaganda and education for militiamen, the entry, exit and transfer of militiamen, the allocation of cadres, work summary, inventory of equipment, improvement of systems, muster and inspection, and other work.  
Soldiers who have retired from active service and meet the conditions for reserve service shall be promptly incorporated into militia organizations.

### 第三章　政治工作

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民兵政治工作应当学习人民解放军的政治工作经验，继承和发扬民兵工作的优良传统，保证民兵工作方针、政策的贯彻执行和各项任务的完成。

**Article 15** In militia political work, the experience of political work of the People's Liberation Army shall be learned, the fine traditions of militia work shall be inherited and carried forward, and the implementation of the principles and policies of militia work and the completion of various tasks shall be ensured.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民兵政治教育以中国共产党的基本路线和国防教育为重点，进行民兵性质任务、优良传统、爱国主义、革命英雄主义、形势战备和政策法制等教育。  
民兵政治教育主要结合组织整顿、军事训练、征兵和重大节日活动进行。

**Article 16** Militia political education shall focus on the basic line of the Chinese Communist Party and national defense education, and shall conduct education on the nature and tasks of the militia, fine traditions, patriotism, revolutionary heroism, situation and combat readiness, policies and legal system, etc.  
Militia political education shall mainly be conducted in conjunction with organizational rectification, military training, conscription, and major festival activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民兵政治教育，平时应当根据民兵军事训练、战备执勤的任务、要求和民兵思想实际做好民兵的思想政治工作，提高练兵习武的自觉性，发动民兵带头参加社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设。战时应当动员民兵参军参战，支援前线，组织民兵开展杀敌立功、瓦解敌军等活动，保证战斗、战勤任务的完成。

**Article 17** In militia political education, in peacetime, ideological and political work for the militia shall be done well according to the tasks and requirements of militia military training and combat readiness duty, as well as the actual ideological conditions of the militia, to enhance the consciousness of training and military practice, and to mobilize the militia to take the lead in participating in socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization construction. In wartime, the militia shall be mobilized to join the army and participate in combat, support the front, and organize the militia to carry out activities such as killing enemies and performing meritorious service, and demoralizing enemy troops, to ensure the completion of combat and combat service support tasks.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对专职人民武装干部的培养、选拔、调整和配备，应当按照有关规定进行。专职人民武装干部的任免，由本地区军事领导指挥机关按照任免权限办理。

**Article 18** The cultivation, selection, adjustment, and allocation of full-time people's armed forces cadres shall be carried out in accordance with relevant provisions. The appointment and removal of full-time people's armed forces cadres shall be handled by the military leadership command organ of the region according to the authority of appointment and removal.

### 第四章　军事训练

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民兵军事训练应当按照总参谋部颁发的《民兵军事训练大纲》进行，实施规范化训练。全国每年的训练任务，由总参谋部规定后，逐级下达。

**Article 19** Militia military training shall be conducted in accordance with the Outline of Militia Military Training issued by the General Staff Department, and standardized training shall be implemented. The annual training tasks nationwide shall be prescribed by the General Staff Department and then transmitted level by level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民兵的军事训练由县人民武装部组织实施。专职人民武装干部的军事训练，由军分区组织实施。  
军兵种机关、部队和军事院校，应当协助省军区、军分区、县人民武装部开展民兵军事训练。

**Article 20** Military training of the militia shall be organized and implemented by the county people's armed forces department. Military training of full-time people's armed forces cadres shall be organized and implemented by the military subdistrict.  
The organs, units and military academies of the services and arms shall assist the provincial military command, military subdistricts, and county people's armed forces departments in carrying out militia military training.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对参加军事训练的基干民兵，应当进行考核。考核合格的，由县人民武装部进行登记。民兵军事训练成绩评定标准，由总参谋部制定。

**Article 21** Core members of the militia who participate in military training shall be assessed. Those who pass the assessment shall be registered by the county people's armed forces department. The standards for evaluating militia military training performance shall be formulated by the General Staff Department.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县应当逐步建立民兵军事训练基地，对民兵实行集中训练。  
民兵军事训练基地应当健全管理制度，完善基本设施，保障军事训练的需要。

**Article 22** Counties shall gradually establish militia military training bases to conduct centralized training of the militia.  
Militia military training bases shall establish a sound management system, improve basic facilities, and ensure the needs of military training.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民兵军事训练的教材、器材，分级负责解决。总参谋部负责组织编印教材和配发部分制式训练器材，其余所需的训练器材，由省军区、军分区、县人民武装部分别购置或者调整解决。  
民兵训练教材、器材应当严格管理，不得挪作它用。

**Article 23** The teaching materials and equipment for militia military training shall be provided at different levels. The General Staff Department shall be responsible for organizing the compilation and printing of teaching materials and distributing some standard training equipment. The remaining required training equipment shall be purchased or adjusted by the provincial military command, military sub-command, and county people's armed forces department respectively.  
Militia training materials and equipment shall be strictly managed and shall not be misappropriated for other uses.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　农村的民兵和民兵干部在参加军事训练期间，由当地人民政府采取平衡负担的办法，按照当地同等劳力的收入水平给予误工补贴。  
企业事业单位的民兵和民兵干部在参加军事训练期间，由原单位照发工资和奖金，原有的福利待遇不变；其伙食补助和往返差旅费由原单位按照国家规定在有关项目中开支。  
企业事业单位自行组织的民兵活动，所需费用由本单位负责解决。

**Article 24** Rural militia members and militia cadres shall, during their participation in military training, be provided with compensation for lost working time by the local people's government through a method of balanced burden, based on the income level of equivalent labor in the locality.  
Militia members and militia cadres from enterprises and institutions shall, during their participation in military training, receive their wages and bonuses as usual from their original units, and their original welfare benefits shall remain unchanged; their meal subsidies and round-trip travel expenses shall be paid by the original units in accordance with state regulations under relevant items.  
For militia activities organized independently by enterprises and institutions, the necessary expenses shall be borne by the unit itself.

### 第五章　武器装备

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民兵武器装备的发展和配备，由总参谋部统一规划。军区、省军区、军分区和县人民武装部根据上级的规划，进行配备和补充。

**Article 25** The development and allocation of militia weapons and equipment shall be uniformly planned by the General Staff Department. The military region, provincial military command, military sub-command, and county people's armed forces department shall conduct allocation and replenishment in accordance with the plans of higher authorities.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民兵武器装备的配备应当根据基干民兵的组建计划和战备、执勤、军事训练的需要，做到保证重点，合理布局。

**Article 26** The allocation of militia weapons and equipment shall be based on the formation plan for core militia and the needs of combat readiness, duty, and military training, ensuring a focus on key areas and a reasonable distribution.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民兵配属部队执行作战、支前任务所需武器装备，由县人民武装部配发；到达部队后，由所在部队补充。

**Article 27** The weapons and equipment necessary for militia attached to troops to perform combat or support front-line tasks shall be issued by the county people's armed forces department; after arriving at the troops, they shall be supplemented by the unit to which they are attached.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民兵武器装备的调动，按照管辖范围，分别由县人民武装部、军分区、省军区、军区批准，跨军区调动或者调出民兵系统的，由总参谋部批准。  
民兵武器装备，不得擅自借出。因执勤、训练需要借用配发给民兵或者民兵组织的武器装备时，必须报经县人民武装部批准。

**Article 28** The transfer of militia weapons and equipment shall be approved, within the scope of jurisdiction, by the county people's armed forces department, military sub-command, provincial military command, or military region respectively. Transfers across military regions or out of the militia system shall be approved by the General Staff Department.  
Militia weapons and equipment shall not be lent out without authorization. Where it is necessary to borrow weapons and equipment allocated to militia members or militia organizations due to duty or training needs, approval must be obtained from the county people's armed forces department.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民兵武器装备的保管，按照总参谋部的规定办理。  
保管民兵武器装备的单位，必须有坚固的库(室)、健全的管理制度。武器库(室)必须有专人看管，有报警、消防等安全设施。

**Article 29** The storage of militia weapons and equipment shall be handled in accordance with the regulations of the General Staff Department.  
Units storing militia weapons and equipment must have sturdy warehouses (rooms) and a sound management system. The weapons warehouses (rooms) must have dedicated personnel for supervision and have safety facilities such as alarms and fire protection.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　掌握武器的民兵和民兵武器库(室)的看管人员，应当按照有关规定由人民武装部门审查批准。

**Article 30** The militia members who handle weapons and the custodial personnel of militia weapons warehouses (rooms) shall be reviewed and approved by the people's armed forces departments in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　民兵武器装备的修理，农村的，由县人民武装部负责；城市的，由配有武器装备的单位负责。上述单位不能修理的武器，由军分区、省军区、军区修械所(厂)修理。

**Article 31** The repair of militia weapons and equipment shall be the responsibility of the county people's armed forces department in rural areas, and of the units equipped with weapons and equipment in urban areas. Weapons that cannot be repaired by the above-mentioned units shall be repaired by the repair stations (factories) of the military sub-commands, provincial military commands, or military commands.

### 第六章　战备执勤

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　民兵战备执勤，由县人民武装部根据上级赋予的任务，制定计划，具体组织实施。

**Article 32** The militia's readiness duty shall be planned and specifically organized and implemented by the county people's armed forces department in accordance with the tasks assigned by the higher authorities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　陆海边防地区和其他战备重点地区的民兵组织，应当根据上级军事机关的要求，与驻地的人民解放军和人民武装警察部队实行联防。  
发现敌人袭扰、空降和潜入等紧急情况，民兵应当在当地军事机关组织指挥下迅速进行围歼或者搜捕。  
战时，民兵应当配合部队作战，担负各项战斗勤务，支援前线，保护群众，保卫生产。  
民兵应当配合公安机关维护社会治安。

**Article 33** Militia organizations in land and sea border defense areas and other key combat readiness areas shall, in accordance with the requirements of the higher military organs, implement joint defense with the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police Forces stationed in the area.  
Upon discovering emergency situations such as enemy harassment, airborne landings, or infiltration, the militia shall, under the organization and command of the local military organs, rapidly carry out encirclement and annihilation or search and capture.  
In wartime, the militia shall cooperate with the troops in combat, undertake various combat support duties, support the front, protect the masses, and safeguard production.  
The militia shall cooperate with public security organs in maintaining public order.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　组织民兵担负勤务，应当爱惜民力，严加控制。  
陆海边防民兵固定哨所的设立，由军分区根据战备需要提出方案，报省军区批准。  
使用民兵担负守护桥梁、隧道、仓库等重要目标勤务，由目标归属单位根据国家有关规定提出申请，报省军区批准。  
民兵担负治安勤务，由本地区军事领导指挥机关报同级人民政府批准，并报上级军事机关备案。在厂矿范围内，使用民兵担负维护治安、保护生产方面的勤务，由厂矿批准，报县人民武装部备案。

**Article 34** When organizing the militia to perform duties, the people's strength shall be treasured and strictly controlled.  
The establishment of fixed sentry posts for militia in land and sea border defense areas shall be proposed by the military sub-command based on combat readiness needs and submitted to the provincial military command for approval.  
For using the militia to perform duties of guarding important targets such as bridges, tunnels, and warehouses, the unit to which the target belongs shall file an application in accordance with relevant state regulations and submit it to the provincial military command for approval.  
For the militia to perform public security duties, the military leadership and command organ of the region shall submit the matter to the people's government at the same level for approval and report it to the higher military organ for record. Within the scope of factories and mines, using the militia to perform duties of maintaining public order and protecting production shall be approved by the factory or mine and reported to the county people's armed forces department for record.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　民兵担负勤务的报酬或者补助，由使用单位支付。  
民兵守护重要目标执勤点所需营房、营具、厨具和通信、照明、饮水、警戒等设施，以及执勤民兵的生活补贴、执勤用品、必要的文化用品、医疗、伤亡抚恤等经费，由目标归属单位解决。

**Article 35** The remuneration or subsidies for the militia performing duties shall be paid by the employing unit.  
The barracks, camp equipment, kitchen utensils, and facilities for communication, lighting, drinking water, and guard necessary for militia guard posts at important targets, as well as the living allowances, duty supplies, necessary cultural supplies, medical care, and compensation for injuries or deaths of the militia on duty, shall be provided by the unit to which the target belongs.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对参战、执行战勤任务、参加军事训练和维护社会治安中伤亡民兵的优待、安置和抚恤，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 36** The preferential treatment, placement, and compensation for militia members who are injured or die while participating in combat, performing combat support tasks, undergoing military training, or maintaining public order shall be handled in accordance with relevant state provisions.

### 第七章　民兵事业费

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　民兵事业费是保障民兵建设的专项经费，是国家预算的组成部分，应当严格管理，专款专用。

**Article 37** The militia undertaking expense is a special fund to guarantee militia building and a component of the state budget. It shall be strictly managed and used only for its designated purpose.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　民兵事业费由省军区、军分区、县人民武装部按级负责管理。  
民兵事业费的年度指标，由省军区根据全年民兵工作任务，向省(含自治区、直辖市，下同)人民政府编造预算，经批准后组织实施。  
省军区后勤部与省财政厅(局)建立财务领报关系。省军区、军分区司令部负责拟制经费的分配和使用计划，后勤部负责财务的管理和监督。  
县人民武装部是民兵事业费的基层开支单位，直接掌管民兵事业费的使用。

**Article 38** The militia undertaking expense shall be managed at different levels by the provincial military command, the military sub-command, and the county people's armed forces department.  
The annual quota of the militia undertaking expense shall be budgeted by the provincial military command to the people's government of the province (including autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the same below) based on the annual militia work tasks, and organized for implementation after approval.  
The logistics department of the provincial military command shall establish a financial reporting relationship with the provincial department (bureau) of finance. The headquarters of the provincial military command and the military sub-command shall be responsible for drafting plans for the allocation and use of funds, and the logistics department shall be responsible for financial management and supervision.  
The county people's armed forces department is the basic expenditure unit for the militia undertaking expense and shall directly manage the use of the militia undertaking expense.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　民兵事业费应当主要分配到县人民武装部使用。省军区、军分区两级留用的民兵事业费，除民兵装备管理维修费外，不得超过全省总指标的20%。

**Article 39** The militia undertaking expense shall be mainly allocated to the county people's armed forces department for use. The militia undertaking expense retained by the provincial military command and the military sub-command at the two levels, except for the militia equipment management and maintenance fee, shall not exceed 20% of the total provincial quota.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民兵事业费主要用于民兵的军事训练、武器装备管理维修、组织建设、政治工作等项开支。

**Article 40** The militia undertaking expense shall be mainly used for expenditures such as military training of the militia, management and maintenance of weapons and equipment, organizational construction, and political work.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　民兵事业费的使用和管理实施办法，由省人民政府、省军区制定。  
民兵事业费的预算、决算和使用管理，由财政部、总参谋部实施监督，并接受审计机关的审计。

**Article 41** Measures for the use and administration of militia operating expenses shall be formulated by the provincial people's government and the provincial military command.  
The budget, final accounts, use and administration of militia operating expenses shall be supervised by the Ministry of Finance and the General Staff Department, and shall be subject to audit by audit authorities.

### 第八章　奖励和惩处

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民兵、民兵组织和人民武装干部在参战、支前中做出显著成绩的，参照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》规定的奖励项目和批准权限，由军队给予奖励；在完成民兵工作或者执行维护社会治安等其他任务中做出显著成绩的，由地方人民政府、本地区的军事领导指挥机关给予奖励。

**Article 42** Militia members, militia organizations, and people's armed forces cadres who make outstanding achievements in combat or in supporting the front shall be given awards by the military in accordance with the award items and approval authority prescribed in the Regulations on Discipline of the Chinese People's Liberation Army; those who make outstanding achievements in completing militia work or carrying out other tasks such as maintaining public order shall be given awards by the local people's government and the military leadership command organs in the region.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　依照本条例规定，公民应当参加民兵组织而拒绝参加的，民兵拒绝、逃避军事训练和执行任务经教育不改的，由人民武装部门提请民兵所在单位给予行政处分，或者提请地方人民政府有关部门给予行政处罚，并强制其履行兵役义务。  
民兵拒绝、逃避参军、参战、支前、维护社会治安等重大任务，或者在执行任务中因玩忽职守造成严重后果的，参照《中华人民共和国兵役法》的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** According to the provisions of these Regulations, a citizen who is required to join a militia organization but refuses to do so, or a militia member who refuses or evades military training or the execution of tasks and does not change after education, the people's armed forces department shall request the unit to which the militia member belongs to impose administrative sanctions, or request the relevant department of the local people's government to impose an administrative penalty, and shall enforce the fulfillment of military service obligations.  
If a militia member refuses or evades major tasks such as joining the army, participating in combat, supporting the front, or maintaining public order, or commits dereliction of duty during the execution of tasks resulting in serious consequences, he shall be punished by reference to the relevant provisions of the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　对违反本条例规定，拒绝建立或者擅自取消民兵组织，拒绝完成民兵工作任务的单位，由本地区军事领导指挥机关报同级人民政府批准，对该单位给予批评或者行政处罚，对该单位负责人给予行政处分，并责令限期改正。

**Article 44** For a unit that violates the provisions of these Regulations, refuses to establish or arbitrarily cancels a militia organization, or refuses to complete militia work tasks, the military leadership command organ in the region shall, with the approval of the people's government at the same level, give the unit a criticism or administrative penalty, impose administrative sanctions on the person in charge of the unit, and order correction within a time limit.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例由中国人民解放军总参谋部负责解释。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the General Staff Department of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自1991年1月1日起施行。1978年8月国防部颁布的《民兵工作条例》即行废止。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1991. The Regulations on Militia Work promulgated by the Ministry of National Defense in August 1978 shall be abolished simultaneously.
