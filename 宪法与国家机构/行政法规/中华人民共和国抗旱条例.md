# 中华人民共和国抗旱条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-02-26  
**生效日期**：2009-02-26

> (2009年2月11日国务院第49次常务会议通过　2009年2月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第552号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了预防和减轻干旱灾害及其造成的损失，保障生活用水，协调生产、生态用水，促进经济社会全面、协调、可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事预防和减轻干旱灾害的活动，应当遵守本条例。 本条例所称干旱灾害，是指由于降水减少、水工程供水不足引起的用水短缺，并对生活、生产和生态造成危害的事件。 第三条　抗旱工作坚持以人为本、预防为主、防抗结合和因地制宜、统筹兼顾、局部利益服从全局利益的原则。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将抗旱工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算，保障抗旱工作的正常开展。 第五条　抗旱工作实行各级人民政府行政首长负责制，统一指挥、部门协作、分级负责。 第六条　国家防汛抗旱总指挥部负责组织、领导全国的抗旱工作。 国务院水行政主管部门负责全国抗旱的指导、监督、管理工作，承担国家防汛抗旱总指挥部的具体工作。国家防汛抗旱总指挥部的其他成员单位按照各自职责，负责有关抗旱工作。 第七条　国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的防汛抗旱指挥机构，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和该江河、湖泊的流域管理机构组成，负责协调所辖范围内的抗旱工作；流域管理机构承担流域防汛抗旱指挥机构的具体工作。 第八条　县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构，在上级防汛抗旱指挥机构和本级人民政府的领导下，负责组织、指挥本行政区域内的抗旱工作。 县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责本行政区域内抗旱的指导、监督、管理工作，承担本级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构的具体工作。县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构的其他成员单位按照各自职责，负责有关抗旱工作。 第九条　县级以上人民政府应当加强水利基础设施建设，完善抗旱工程体系，提高抗旱减灾能力。 第十条　各级人民政府、有关部门应当开展抗旱宣传教育活动，增强全社会抗旱减灾意识，鼓励和支持各种抗旱科学技术研究及其成果的推广应用。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和减轻干旱灾害及其造成的损失，保障生活用水，协调生产、生态用水，促进经济社会全面、协调、可持续发展，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of preventing and mitigating drought disasters and the losses caused thereby, ensuring water supply for daily life, coordinating water use for production and ecology, and promoting comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事预防和减轻干旱灾害的活动，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称干旱灾害，是指由于降水减少、水工程供水不足引起的用水短缺，并对生活、生产和生态造成危害的事件。

**Article 2** Any activities relating to the prevention and mitigation of drought disasters within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "drought disaster" means an event of water shortage caused by reduced precipitation or insufficient water supply from water projects, which causes harm to daily life, production and ecology.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　抗旱工作坚持以人为本、预防为主、防抗结合和因地制宜、统筹兼顾、局部利益服从全局利益的原则。

**Article 3** Drought relief work shall adhere to the principles of putting people first, giving priority to prevention, combining prevention with relief, suiting measures to local conditions, making overall plans and taking all factors into consideration, and subordinating local interests to overall interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将抗旱工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费纳入本级财政预算，保障抗旱工作的正常开展。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate drought relief work into their respective plans for national economic and social development, and include the necessary funds in their respective fiscal budgets, so as to ensure the normal development of drought relief work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　抗旱工作实行各级人民政府行政首长负责制，统一指挥、部门协作、分级负责。

**Article 5** Drought relief work shall implement a system of responsibility of chief executives of people's governments at all levels, with unified command, departmental coordination, and graded responsibility.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家防汛抗旱总指挥部负责组织、领导全国的抗旱工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门负责全国抗旱的指导、监督、管理工作，承担国家防汛抗旱总指挥部的具体工作。国家防汛抗旱总指挥部的其他成员单位按照各自职责，负责有关抗旱工作。

**Article 6** The State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national drought relief work.  
The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the guidance, supervision and administration of national drought relief, and shall undertake the specific work of the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters. Other member units of the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters shall be responsible for relevant drought relief work in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的防汛抗旱指挥机构，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和该江河、湖泊的流域管理机构组成，负责协调所辖范围内的抗旱工作；流域管理机构承担流域防汛抗旱指挥机构的具体工作。

**Article 7** The flood control and drought relief headquarters for major rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be composed of the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the river basin management organs for such rivers and lakes, and shall be responsible for coordinating drought relief work within their respective jurisdictions; the river basin management organs shall undertake the specific work of the river basin flood control and drought relief headquarters.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构，在上级防汛抗旱指挥机构和本级人民政府的领导下，负责组织、指挥本行政区域内的抗旱工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责本行政区域内抗旱的指导、监督、管理工作，承担本级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构的具体工作。县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构的其他成员单位按照各自职责，负责有关抗旱工作。

**Article 8** The flood control and drought relief headquarters of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the leadership of the flood control and drought relief headquarters at the next higher level and the people's government at the same level, be responsible for organizing and directing drought relief work within their respective administrative regions.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the guidance, supervision and administration of drought relief within their respective administrative regions, and shall undertake the specific work of the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the same level. Other member units of the flood control and drought relief headquarters of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant drought relief work in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当加强水利基础设施建设，完善抗旱工程体系，提高抗旱减灾能力。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of water conservancy infrastructure, improve the drought relief engineering system, and enhance the capacity for drought relief and disaster reduction.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府、有关部门应当开展抗旱宣传教育活动，增强全社会抗旱减灾意识，鼓励和支持各种抗旱科学技术研究及其成果的推广应用。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall carry out publicity and education activities on drought relief, enhance the awareness of the whole society on drought relief and disaster reduction, and encourage and support scientific and technological research on drought relief and the dissemination and application of its achievements.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人都有保护抗旱设施和依法参加抗旱的义务。

**Article 11** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to protect drought relief facilities and to participate in drought relief according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在抗旱工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 12** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to drought relief work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

### 第二章　旱灾预防

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级有关部门编制本行政区域的抗旱规划，报本级人民政府批准后实施，并抄送上一级人民政府水行政主管部门。

**Article 13** The water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, formulate a drought relief plan for its administrative region, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the people's government at the same level, and a copy shall be sent to the water administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　编制抗旱规划应当充分考虑本行政区域的国民经济和社会发展水平、水资源综合开发利用情况、干旱规律和特点、可供水资源量和抗旱能力以及城乡居民生活用水、工农业生产和生态用水的需求。  
抗旱规划应当与水资源开发利用等规划相衔接。  
下级抗旱规划应当与上一级的抗旱规划相协调。

**Article 14** In formulating a drought relief plan, full consideration shall be given to the level of national economic and social development of the administrative region, the comprehensive development and utilization of water resources, the patterns and characteristics of drought, the amount of available water resources and drought relief capacity, as well as the water demand for daily life of urban and rural residents, industrial and agricultural production, and ecological use.  
Drought relief plans shall be aligned with plans for water resources development and utilization, among others.  
Drought relief plans at lower levels shall be coordinated with the drought relief plan at the next higher level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　抗旱规划应当主要包括抗旱组织体系建设、抗旱应急水源建设、抗旱应急设施建设、抗旱物资储备、抗旱服务组织建设、旱情监测网络建设以及保障措施等。

**Article 15** A drought relief plan shall mainly include: the building of a drought relief organization system, the construction of emergency water sources for drought relief, the construction of emergency facilities for drought relief, the stockpiling of drought relief materials, the building of drought relief service organizations, the construction of a drought monitoring network, and safeguard measures, among others.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府应当加强农田水利基础设施建设和农村饮水工程建设，组织做好抗旱应急工程及其配套设施建设和节水改造，提高抗旱供水能力和水资源利用效率。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当组织做好农田水利基础设施和农村饮水工程的管理和维护，确保其正常运行。  
干旱缺水地区的地方人民政府及有关集体经济组织应当因地制宜修建中小微型蓄水、引水、提水工程和雨水集蓄利用工程。

**Article 16** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of farmland water conservancy infrastructure and rural drinking water projects, organize the construction of emergency drought relief projects and their supporting facilities and water-saving renovation, and improve the capacity for drought relief water supply and the efficiency of water resource utilization.  
The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the management and maintenance of farmland water conservancy infrastructure and rural drinking water projects to ensure their normal operation.  
Local people's governments and relevant collective economic organizations in drought and water-deficient areas shall, in light of local conditions, construct small, medium and micro water storage, diversion and lifting projects and rainwater harvesting and utilization projects.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励和扶持研发、使用抗旱节水机械和装备，推广农田节水技术，支持旱作地区修建抗旱设施，发展旱作节水农业。  
国家鼓励、引导、扶持社会组织和个人建设、经营抗旱设施，并保护其合法权益。

**Article 17** The State encourages and supports the research, development and use of drought relief and water-saving machinery and equipment, promotes farmland water-saving technologies, supports the construction of drought relief facilities in dry farming areas, and develops dryland water-saving agriculture.  
The State encourages, guides and supports social organizations and individuals to construct and operate drought relief facilities, and protects their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当做好干旱期城乡居民生活供水的应急水源贮备保障工作。

**Article 18** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure the reserve of emergency water sources for water supply to urban and rural residents during drought periods.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　干旱灾害频繁发生地区的县级以上地方人民政府，应当根据抗旱工作需要储备必要的抗旱物资，并加强日常管理。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where drought disasters occur frequently shall, based on the needs of drought relief work, stockpile necessary drought relief materials and strengthen routine management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府应当根据水资源和水环境的承载能力，调整、优化经济结构和产业布局，合理配置水资源。

**Article 20** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the carrying capacity of water resources and the water environment, adjust and optimize the economic structure and industrial layout, and rationally allocate water resources.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级人民政府应当开展节约用水宣传教育，推行节约用水措施，推广节约用水新技术、新工艺，建设节水型社会。

**Article 21** People's governments at all levels shall carry out publicity and education on water conservation, promote water-saving measures, disseminate new technologies and new processes for water conservation, and build a water-saving society.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当做好水资源的分配、调度和保护工作，组织建设抗旱应急水源工程和集雨设施。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当及时向人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构提供水情、雨情和墒情信息。

**Article 22** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall properly carry out the allocation, dispatch and protection of water resources, and organize the construction of emergency water source projects for drought relief and rainwater collection facilities.  
The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall promptly provide the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government with information on water conditions, rainfall conditions and soil moisture conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级气象主管机构应当加强气象科学技术研究，提高气象监测和预报水平，及时向人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构提供气象干旱及其他与抗旱有关的气象信息。

**Article 23** Meteorological authorities at all levels shall strengthen research on meteorological science and technology, improve the level of meteorological monitoring and forecasting, and promptly provide the flood control and drought relief headquarters of people's governments with information on meteorological drought and other meteorological information related to drought relief.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门应当做好农用抗旱物资的储备和管理工作，指导干旱地区农业种植结构的调整，培育和推广应用耐旱品种，及时向人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构提供农业旱情信息。

**Article 24** The agriculture administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall properly carry out the stockpiling and management of agricultural drought relief materials, guide the adjustment of agricultural planting structures in drought areas, cultivate and promote the application of drought-resistant crop varieties, and promptly provide the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government with information on agricultural drought conditions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　供水管理部门应当组织有关单位，加强供水管网的建设和维护，提高供水能力，保障居民生活用水，及时向人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构提供供水、用水信息。

**Article 25** The water supply administration departments shall organize relevant units to strengthen the construction and maintenance of water supply networks, improve water supply capacity, ensure water supply for residents' daily life, and promptly provide the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government with information on water supply and water use.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门，充分利用现有资源，建设完善旱情监测网络，加强对干旱灾害的监测。  
县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当组织完善抗旱信息系统，实现成员单位之间的信息共享，为抗旱指挥决策提供依据。

**Article 26** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to make full use of existing resources, build and improve drought monitoring networks, and strengthen the monitoring of drought disasters.  
The flood control and drought relief headquarters of people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the improvement of drought relief information systems, realize information sharing among member units, and provide a basis for drought relief command and decision-making.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家防汛抗旱总指挥部组织其成员单位编制国家防汛抗旱预案，经国务院批准后实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构组织其成员单位编制抗旱预案，经上一级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构审查同意，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
经批准的抗旱预案，有关部门和单位必须执行。修改抗旱预案，应当按照原批准程序报原批准机关批准。

**Article 27** The State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters shall organize its member units to formulate the national flood control and drought relief plan, which shall be implemented after approval by the State Council.  
The flood control and drought relief headquarters of local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize their member units to formulate drought relief plans, which shall be implemented after being reviewed and approved by the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the next higher level and submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.  
The approved drought relief plans must be implemented by the relevant departments and units. Any modification of a drought relief plan shall be submitted for approval to the original approving authority in accordance with the original approval procedures.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　抗旱预案应当包括预案的执行机构以及有关部门的职责、干旱灾害预警、干旱等级划分和按不同等级采取的应急措施、旱情紧急情况下水量调度预案和保障措施等内容。  
干旱灾害按照区域耕地和作物受旱的面积与程度以及因干旱导致饮水困难人口的数量，分为轻度干旱、中度干旱、严重干旱、特大干旱四级。

**Article 28** A drought relief plan shall include the executing agencies of the plan and the responsibilities of relevant departments, early warning of drought disasters, classification of drought grades and emergency measures to be taken according to different grades, a water dispatch plan under emergency drought conditions, and safeguard measures, among others.  
Drought disasters are classified into four grades: mild drought, moderate drought, severe drought, and extraordinary drought, based on the area and degree of drought-affected arable land and crops, as well as the number of people experiencing drinking water difficulties due to drought.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级人民政府和乡镇人民政府根据抗旱工作的需要，加强抗旱服务组织的建设。县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对抗旱服务组织的扶持。  
国家鼓励社会组织和个人兴办抗旱服务组织。

**Article 29** The people's governments at the county level and the people's governments of townships (towns) shall, based on the needs of drought relief work, strengthen the building of drought relief service organizations. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen support for drought relief service organizations.  
The State encourages social organizations and individuals to establish drought relief service organizations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府应当对抗旱责任制落实、抗旱预案编制、抗旱设施建设和维护、抗旱物资储备等情况加强监督检查，发现问题应当及时处理或者责成有关部门和单位限期处理。

**Article 30** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of the drought relief responsibility system, the formulation of drought relief plans, the construction and maintenance of drought relief facilities, and the stockpiling of drought relief materials; any problems discovered shall be promptly dealt with or the relevant departments and units shall be instructed to deal with them within a time limit.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　水工程管理单位应当定期对管护范围内的抗旱设施进行检查和维护。

**Article 31** Water project management units shall periodically inspect and maintain drought relief facilities within their management and protection scope.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　禁止非法引水、截水和侵占、破坏、污染水源。  
禁止破坏、侵占、毁损抗旱设施。

**Article 32** It is prohibited to illegally divert or intercept water, or to encroach upon, damage or pollute water sources.  
It is prohibited to damage, encroach upon or destroy drought relief facilities.

### 第三章　抗旱减灾

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当按照抗旱预案规定的权限，启动抗旱预案，组织开展抗旱减灾工作。

**Article 33** When a drought disaster occurs, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of a people's government at or above the county level shall, within the authority prescribed in the drought relief plan, activate the drought relief plan and organize the conduct of drought relief and disaster reduction work.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　发生轻度干旱和中度干旱，县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当按照抗旱预案的规定，采取下列措施：  
(一)启用应急备用水源或者应急打井、挖泉；  
(二)设置临时抽水泵站，开挖输水渠道或者临时在江河沟渠内截水；  
(三)使用再生水、微咸水、海水等非常规水源，组织实施人工增雨；  
(四)组织向人畜饮水困难地区送水。  
采取前款规定的措施，涉及其他行政区域的，应当报共同的上一级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构或者流域防汛抗旱指挥机构批准；涉及其他有关部门的，应当提前通知有关部门。旱情解除后，应当及时拆除临时取水和截水设施，并及时通报有关部门。

**Article 34** When a mild drought or a moderate drought occurs, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the drought relief plan, take the following measures:  
(1) activating emergency standby water sources or emergency well digging and spring excavation;  
(2) setting up temporary pumping stations, excavating water conveyance channels, or temporarily intercepting water in rivers, ditches and streams;  
(3) using non-conventional water sources such as reclaimed water, brackish water, and seawater, and organizing the implementation of artificial precipitation enhancement;  
(4) organizing the delivery of water to areas where humans and livestock have difficulty in accessing drinking water.  
If the measures prescribed in the preceding paragraph involve other administrative regions, approval shall be obtained from the common flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the next higher level or the river basin flood control and drought relief headquarters; if other relevant departments are involved, such departments shall be notified in advance. After the drought conditions have been alleviated, the temporary water intake and interception facilities shall be promptly dismantled, and the relevant departments shall be promptly notified.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　发生严重干旱和特大干旱，国家防汛抗旱总指挥部应当启动国家防汛抗旱预案，总指挥部各成员单位应当按照防汛抗旱预案的分工，做好相关工作。  
严重干旱和特大干旱发生地的县级以上地方人民政府在防汛抗旱指挥机构采取[本条例第三十四条](#art-34)规定的措施外，还可以采取下列措施：  
(一)压减供水指标；  
(二)限制或者暂停高耗水行业用水；  
(三)限制或者暂停排放工业污水；  
(四)缩小农业供水范围或者减少农业供水量；  
(五)限时或者限量供应城镇居民生活用水。

**Article 35** When a severe drought or an extraordinary drought occurs, the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters shall activate the national flood control and drought relief plan, and all member units of the Headquarters shall, in accordance with the division of work under the flood control and drought relief plan, properly carry out the relevant work.  
In addition to the measures taken by the flood control and drought relief headquarters under Article 34 of these Regulations, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the areas where the severe drought or extraordinary drought occurs may also take the following measures:  
(1) reducing water supply quotas;  
(2) restricting or suspending water use by industries with high water consumption;  
(3) restricting or suspending the discharge of industrial wastewater;  
(4) narrowing the scope of agricultural water supply or reducing the amount of agricultural water supply;  
(5) supplying water to urban residents for daily life on a time-limited or volume-limited basis.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上地方人民政府应当按照统一调度、保证重点、兼顾一般的原则对水源进行调配，优先保障城乡居民生活用水，合理安排生产和生态用水。

**Article 36** When a drought disaster occurs, local people's governments at or above the county level shall allocate water sources in accordance with the principles of unified dispatch, ensuring priority for key users while taking into account ordinary users, give priority to ensuring water supply for daily life of urban and rural residents, and rationally arrange water use for production and ecology.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构或者流域防汛抗旱指挥机构可以按照批准的抗旱预案，制订应急水量调度实施方案，统一调度辖区内的水库、水电站、闸坝、湖泊等所蓄的水量。有关地方人民政府、单位和个人必须服从统一调度和指挥，严格执行调度指令。

**Article 37** When a drought disaster occurs, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of a people's government at or above the county level or the river basin flood control and drought relief headquarters may, in accordance with the approved drought relief plan, formulate an implementation plan for emergency water dispatch and uniformly dispatch the water stored in reservoirs, hydropower stations, sluice gates, lakes, etc., within their jurisdiction. The relevant local people's governments, units and individuals must submit to the unified dispatch and command and strictly implement the dispatch instructions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当及时组织抗旱服务组织，解决农村人畜饮水困难，提供抗旱技术咨询等方面的服务。

**Article 38** When a drought disaster occurs, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of a local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly organize drought relief service organizations to solve the drinking water difficulties of rural people and livestock and provide services such as technical consultation on drought relief.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　发生干旱灾害，各级气象主管机构应当做好气象干旱监测和预报工作，并适时实施人工增雨作业。

**Article 39** When a drought disaster occurs, meteorological authorities at all levels shall properly carry out meteorological drought monitoring and forecasting, and implement artificial precipitation enhancement operations at appropriate times.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门应当做好干旱灾害发生地区疾病预防控制、医疗救护和卫生监督执法工作，监督、检测饮用水水源卫生状况，确保饮水卫生安全，防止干旱灾害导致重大传染病疫情的发生。

**Article 40** When a drought disaster occurs, the health administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall properly carry out disease prevention and control, medical rescue, and health supervision and law enforcement in the areas affected by the drought disaster, supervise and test the sanitary conditions of drinking water sources, ensure the sanitary safety of drinking water, and prevent the occurrence of major infectious disease epidemics caused by the drought disaster.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　发生干旱灾害，县级以上人民政府民政部门应当做好干旱灾害的救助工作，妥善安排受灾地区群众基本生活。

**Article 41** When a drought disaster occurs, the civil affairs departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall properly carry out relief work for the drought disaster and make proper arrangements for the basic living needs of the people in the affected areas.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　干旱灾害发生地区的乡镇人民政府、街道办事处、村民委员会、居民委员会应当组织力量，向村民、居民宣传节水抗旱知识，协助做好抗旱措施的落实工作。

**Article 42** The people's governments of townships (towns), sub-district offices, villagers' committees and residents' committees in areas affected by drought disasters shall organize forces to publicize knowledge on water conservation and drought relief among villagers and residents, and assist in the implementation of drought relief measures.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　发生干旱灾害，供水企事业单位应当加强对供水、水源和抗旱设施的管理与维护，按要求启用应急备用水源，确保城乡供水安全。

**Article 43** When a drought disaster occurs, water supply enterprises and institutions shall strengthen the management and maintenance of water supply, water sources and drought relief facilities, activate emergency standby water sources as required, and ensure the safety of urban and rural water supply.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　干旱灾害发生地区的单位和个人应当自觉节约用水，服从当地人民政府发布的决定，配合落实人民政府采取的抗旱措施，积极参加抗旱减灾活动。

**Article 44** Units and individuals in areas affected by drought disasters shall conscientiously conserve water, comply with the decisions issued by the local people's government, cooperate in implementing the drought relief measures taken by the people's government, and actively participate in drought relief and disaster reduction activities.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　发生特大干旱，严重危及城乡居民生活、生产用水安全，可能影响社会稳定的，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构经本级人民政府批准，可以宣布本辖区内的相关行政区域进入紧急抗旱期，并及时报告国家防汛抗旱总指挥部。  
特大干旱旱情缓解后，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当宣布结束紧急抗旱期，并及时报告国家防汛抗旱总指挥部。

**Article 45** When an extraordinary drought occurs that seriously endangers the safety of water for daily life and production of urban and rural residents and may affect social stability, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government may, with the approval of the people's government at the same level, declare the relevant administrative areas within its jurisdiction as entering into a period of emergency drought relief, and shall promptly report to the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters.  
After the extraordinary drought conditions have been alleviated, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall declare the end of the emergency drought relief period and promptly report to the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在紧急抗旱期，有关地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当组织动员本行政区域内各有关单位和个人投入抗旱工作。所有单位和个人必须服从指挥，承担人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构分配的抗旱工作任务。

**Article 46** During the period of emergency drought relief, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the relevant local people's government shall organize and mobilize all relevant units and individuals within its administrative region to engage in drought relief work. All units and individuals must obey the command and undertake the drought relief tasks assigned by the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在紧急抗旱期，有关地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构根据抗旱工作的需要，有权在其管辖范围内征用物资、设备、交通运输工具。

**Article 47** During the period of emergency drought relief, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the relevant local people's government shall, based on the needs of drought relief work, have the power to requisition materials, equipment, and means of transport within its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当组织有关部门，按照干旱灾害统计报表的要求，及时核实和统计所管辖范围内的旱情、干旱灾害和抗旱情况等信息，报上一级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构和本级人民政府。

**Article 48** The flood control and drought relief headquarters of local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the relevant departments to timely verify and compile statistics on information such as drought conditions, drought disasters and drought relief situations within their respective jurisdictions in accordance with the requirements for statistical reports on drought disasters, and report such information to the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the next higher level and the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家建立抗旱信息统一发布制度。旱情由县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构统一审核、发布；旱灾由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级民政部门审核、发布；农业灾情由县级以上人民政府农业主管部门发布；与抗旱有关的气象信息由气象主管机构发布。  
报刊、广播、电视和互联网等媒体，应当及时刊播抗旱信息并标明发布机构名称和发布时间。

**Article 49** The State establishes a system for the unified release of drought relief information. Drought conditions shall be uniformly examined and released by the flood control and drought relief headquarters of people's governments at or above the county level; drought disasters shall be examined and released by the water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level jointly with the civil affairs departments at the same level; agricultural disaster conditions shall be released by the agriculture administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level; meteorological information related to drought relief shall be released by the meteorological authorities.  
Newspapers, periodicals, radio stations, television stations and the Internet, among other media, shall promptly publish or broadcast drought relief information and indicate the name of the releasing authority and the time of release.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府应当建立和完善与经济社会发展水平以及抗旱减灾要求相适应的资金投入机制，在本级财政预算中安排必要的资金，保障抗旱减灾投入。

**Article 50** People's governments at all levels shall establish and improve a mechanism for capital investment that is commensurate with the level of economic and social development and the requirements of drought relief and disaster reduction, and shall allocate necessary funds in their respective fiscal budgets to ensure investment in drought relief and disaster reduction.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　因抗旱发生的水事纠纷，依照《中华人民共和国水法》的有关规定处理。

**Article 51** Water disputes arising from drought relief shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　灾后恢复

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　旱情缓解后，各级人民政府、有关主管部门应当帮助受灾群众恢复生产和灾后自救。

**Article 52** After the drought conditions are alleviated, people's governments at all levels and the relevant competent departments shall help the affected people restore production and carry out post-disaster self-recovery.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　旱情缓解后，县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当对水利工程进行检查评估，并及时组织修复遭受干旱灾害损坏的水利工程；县级以上人民政府有关主管部门应当将遭受干旱灾害损坏的水利工程，优先列入年度修复建设计划。

**Article 53** After the drought conditions are alleviated, the water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall inspect and assess water conservancy projects, and promptly organize the repair of water conservancy projects damaged by the drought disaster; the relevant competent departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall give priority to including water conservancy projects damaged by the drought disaster in the annual repair and construction plan.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　旱情缓解后，有关地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当及时归还紧急抗旱期征用的物资、设备、交通运输工具等，并按照有关法律规定给予补偿。

**Article 54** After the drought conditions are alleviated, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the relevant local people's government shall promptly return the materials, equipment, means of transport, etc. requisitioned during the period of emergency drought relief, and provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　旱情缓解后，县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构应当及时组织有关部门对干旱灾害影响、损失情况以及抗旱工作效果进行分析和评估；有关部门和单位应当予以配合，主动向本级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构报告相关情况，不得虚报、瞒报。  
县级以上人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构也可以委托具有灾害评估专业资质的单位进行分析和评估。

**Article 55** After the drought conditions are alleviated, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly organize the relevant departments to analyze and assess the impact and losses of the drought disaster and the effectiveness of the drought relief work; the relevant departments and units shall cooperate and proactively report the relevant information to the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the same level, and shall not make false reports or conceal the truth.  
The flood control and drought relief headquarters of people's governments at or above the county level may also entrust units with professional qualifications for disaster assessment to conduct analysis and assessment.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　抗旱经费和抗旱物资必须专项使用，任何单位和个人不得截留、挤占、挪用和私分。  
各级财政和审计部门应当加强对抗旱经费和物资管理的监督、检查和审计。

**Article 56** Drought relief funds and drought relief materials must be used for specified purposes only, and no unit or individual may withhold, divert, misappropriate or privately distribute them.  
The finance and audit departments at all levels shall strengthen supervision, inspection and audit over the management of drought relief funds and materials.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家鼓励在易旱地区逐步建立和推行旱灾保险制度。

**Article 57** The State encourages the gradual establishment and implementation of a drought disaster insurance system in drought-prone areas.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由所在单位或者上级主管机关、监察机关责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒不承担抗旱救灾任务的；  
(二)擅自向社会发布抗旱信息的；  
(三)虚报、瞒报旱情、灾情的；  
(四)拒不执行抗旱预案或者旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案以及应急水量调度实施方案的；  
(五)旱情解除后，拒不拆除临时取水和截水设施的；  
(六)滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守的其他行为。

**Article 58** Any unit or individual that, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the unit to which it belongs or by the competent authority at the next higher level or the supervisory authority to make corrections; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) refusing to undertake drought relief and disaster reduction tasks;  
(2) releasing drought relief information to the public without authorization;  
(3) making false reports or concealing the truth about drought conditions and disaster conditions;  
(4) refusing to implement the drought relief plan or the water dispatch plan under emergency drought conditions, or the implementation plan for emergency water dispatch;  
(5) after the drought conditions have been alleviated, refusing to dismantle temporary water intake and interception facilities;  
(6) other acts of abuse of power, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, or dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　截留、挤占、挪用、私分抗旱经费的，依照有关财政违法行为处罚处分等法律、行政法规的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Whoever withholds, diverts, misappropriates or privately distributes drought relief funds shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on punishment and sanction for financial violations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，水库、水电站、拦河闸坝等工程的管理单位以及其他经营工程设施的经营者拒不服从统一调度和指挥的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，强制执行，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the management units of projects such as reservoirs, hydropower stations, and river sluice gates, as well as other operators of engineering facilities, refuse to submit to unified dispatch and command, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management organ shall order them to make corrections and give a warning; if they refuse to make corrections, compulsory execution shall be carried out, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，侵占、破坏水源和抗旱设施的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令停止违法行为，采取补救措施，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成损坏的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, one encroaches upon or damages water sources or drought relief facilities, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management organ shall order the cessation of the illegal act, take remedial measures, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例规定，抢水、非法引水、截水或者哄抢抗旱物资的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令停止违法行为，予以警告；构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, one seizes water, illegally diverts or intercepts water, or plunders drought relief materials, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management organ shall order the cessation of the illegal act and give a warning; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，阻碍、威胁防汛抗旱指挥机构、水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构的工作人员依法执行职务的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构责令改正，予以警告；构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, one obstructs or threatens the staff members of the flood control and drought relief headquarters, the water administrative department or the river basin management organ in lawfully performing their duties, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management organ shall order corrections and give a warning; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队参加抗旱救灾，依照《军队参加抢险救灾条例》的有关规定执行。

**Article 64** The participation of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force in drought relief and disaster reduction shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Participation of the Armed Forces in Emergency Rescue and Disaster Relief.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 65** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
