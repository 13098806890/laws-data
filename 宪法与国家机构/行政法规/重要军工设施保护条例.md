# 重要军工设施保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2025-05-19  
**生效日期**：2025-09-15

> 第六条　重要军工设施通过划定重要军工设施保护区实施保护。 无法划定重要军工设施保护区的重要军工设施，依照本条例、有关法律法规和国家有关规定采取相应保护措施实施保护。 第七条　中华人民共和国的组织和公民都有保护重要军工设施的义务。 禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、危害重要军工设施。 任何组织或者个人对破坏、危害重要军工设施的行为，都有权检举、控告。 第八条　对在重要军工设施保护工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　重要军工设施保护区的划定 第九条　重要军工设施保护区的范围按照不超出重要军工设施管理单位使用的土地和海域海岛范围的原则，由重要军工设施管理单位拟制并报当地省级国防科技工业管理部门，省级国防科技工业管理部门会同省级人民政府其他有关部门、当地县级或者设区的市级人民政府、有关军事机关审核后，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府同意。自同意之日起15日内，省级国防科技工业管理部门应当向国务院国防科技工业主管部门报送重要军工设施保护区范围划定情况。 重要军工设施保护区范围的调整，依照前款规定办理。 第十条　重要军工设施保护区范围的划定或者调整，应当在确保重要军工设施安全保密和使用效能的前提下，与当地经济建设、生态环境保护和文物保护相协调，尽量降低对当地居民生产生活的影响。 因重要军工设施建设需要划定或者调整重要军工设施保护区范围的，应当在重要军工设施建设项目开工建设前完成。但是，经国务院国防科技工业主管部门批准的除外。 第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府按照国家统一规定的样式为重要军工设施保护区设置标志牌。 第三章　重要军工设施的保护措施 第十二条　重要军工设施管理单位应当根据具体条件，按照划定的重要军工设施保护区范围，为陆地重要军工设施保护区修筑围墙、设置栅栏等障碍物，为水域重要军工设施保护区设置障碍物或者界线标志。 陆地重要军工设施保护区因地形地貌等客观原因无法修筑围墙、设置栅栏等障碍物的，重要军工设施管理单位应当设置保护区周界警示标志并采取技术防护措施。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国的组织和公民都有保护重要军工设施的义务。  
禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、危害重要军工设施。  
任何组织或者个人对破坏、危害重要军工设施的行为，都有权检举、控告。

**Article 7** All organizations and citizens of the People's Republic of China shall have the obligation to protect important military industrial facilities.  
No organization or individual may destroy or endanger important military industrial facilities.  
Any organization or individual shall have the right to report and accuse any act of destroying or endangering important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在重要军工设施保护工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 8** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to the protection of important military industrial facilities shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　重要军工设施保护区的划定

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　重要军工设施保护区的范围按照不超出重要军工设施管理单位使用的土地和海域海岛范围的原则，由重要军工设施管理单位拟制并报当地省级国防科技工业管理部门，省级国防科技工业管理部门会同省级人民政府其他有关部门、当地县级或者设区的市级人民政府、有关军事机关审核后，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府同意。自同意之日起15日内，省级国防科技工业管理部门应当向国务院国防科技工业主管部门报送重要军工设施保护区范围划定情况。  
重要军工设施保护区范围的调整，依照前款规定办理。

**Article 9** The scope of a protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall be, in accordance with the principle of not exceeding the scope of land and sea areas and islands used by the management unit of the important military industrial facility, drafted by the management unit and submitted to the local provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense; after the provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense, together with other relevant departments of the provincial people's government, the local people's government at the county level or the city divided into districts, and relevant military organs, completes review, it shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval. Within 15 days from the date of approval, the provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense shall report to the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council the delimitation of the scope of the protection zone for important military industrial facilities.  
Adjustment of the scope of a protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　重要军工设施保护区范围的划定或者调整，应当在确保重要军工设施安全保密和使用效能的前提下，与当地经济建设、生态环境保护和文物保护相协调，尽量降低对当地居民生产生活的影响。  
因重要军工设施建设需要划定或者调整重要军工设施保护区范围的，应当在重要军工设施建设项目开工建设前完成。但是，经国务院国防科技工业主管部门批准的除外。

**Article 10** The delimitation or adjustment of the scope of a protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall, under the premise of ensuring the security, confidentiality, and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities, be coordinated with local economic construction, ecological environmental protection, and cultural relic protection, and shall minimize the impact on the production and life of local residents.  
Where the delimitation or adjustment of the scope of a protection zone for an important military industrial facility is necessary due to the construction of an important military industrial facility, it shall be completed before the construction of the important military industrial facility project starts. However, this shall not apply where approval is obtained from the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府按照国家统一规定的样式为重要军工设施保护区设置标志牌。

**Article 11** The local people's government at or above the county level shall set up signboards for the protection zone of important military industrial facilities in accordance with the styles uniformly prescribed by the State.

### 第三章　重要军工设施的保护措施

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　重要军工设施管理单位应当根据具体条件，按照划定的重要军工设施保护区范围，为陆地重要军工设施保护区修筑围墙、设置栅栏等障碍物，为水域重要军工设施保护区设置障碍物或者界线标志。  
陆地重要军工设施保护区因地形地貌等客观原因无法修筑围墙、设置栅栏等障碍物的，重要军工设施管理单位应当设置保护区周界警示标志并采取技术防护措施。  
水域重要军工设施保护区难以设置障碍物或者界线标志的，由有关海事管理机构按照重要军工设施管理单位向其报送的水域重要军工设施保护区范围划定情况，向社会公告水域重要军工设施保护区的位置和边界。海域的重要军工设施保护区应当在海图上标明。

**Article 12** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall, according to specific conditions and in accordance with the delimited scope of the protection zone for the important military industrial facility, construct walls, set up fences and other obstacles for the land-based protection zone for important military industrial facilities, and set up obstacles or boundary markers for the water-area protection zone for important military industrial facilities.  
Where it is impossible to construct walls, set up fences and other obstacles for a land-based protection zone due to objective reasons such as topography and landform, the management unit of the important military industrial facility shall set up perimeter warning signs of the protection zone and adopt technical protection measures.  
Where it is difficult to set up obstacles or boundary markers for a water-area protection zone for an important military industrial facility, the relevant maritime administrative agency shall, in accordance with the delimitation of the scope of the water-area protection zone for the important military industrial facility submitted to it by the management unit, announce to the public the location and boundary of the water-area protection zone for the important military industrial facility. The protection zone for important military industrial facilities in sea areas shall be marked on nautical charts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　陆地重要军工设施保护区应当实行封闭式管理，重要军工设施管理单位应当采取下列安全防护措施：  
（一）重要场所的出入口应当设置岗哨，采取电子监控、身份识别、出入控制、车辆拦阻等技术防护措施，对进出的人员、交通工具、物品物资进行安全检查，必要时应当进行危险物品检测；  
（二）具有重大危险因素或者对安全防护、电磁防护、保密等有特殊要求的区域或者部位，应当采取电子监控、危险物品检测、预警探测、险情感应处置、防侦察监视等技术防护措施。  
水域重要军工设施保护区应当设置岗哨，在保护区边界采取预警探测、入侵报警、拒止反制等安全防护措施。  
安全防护措施的具体标准由国务院国防科技工业主管部门规定。

**Article 13** A land-based protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall implement closed-off management, and the management unit of the important military industrial facility shall adopt the following safety protection measures:  
(1) Guard posts shall be set up at the entrances and exits of important places, and technical protection measures such as electronic monitoring, identity identification, access control, and vehicle interception shall be adopted; security checks shall be conducted on persons, vehicles, articles and materials entering and exiting; and dangerous goods detection shall be conducted when necessary;  
(2) For areas or parts with major risk factors or special requirements for safety protection, electromagnetic protection, confidentiality, etc., technical protection measures such as electronic monitoring, dangerous goods detection, early warning detection, hazard sensing and disposal, and anti-reconnaissance and anti-surveillance shall be adopted.  
A water-area protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall set up guard posts and adopt safety protection measures such as early warning detection, intrusion alarm, denial and countermeasures at the boundary of the protection zone.  
The specific standards for safety protection measures shall be prescribed by the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　未经重要军工设施管理单位同意，禁止重要军工设施管理单位以外的人员、车辆、船舶等进入重要军工设施保护区，禁止对重要军工设施保护区进行摄影、摄像、录音、描绘和记述。确需对重要军工设施保护区进行勘察、测量、定位的，应当经当地省级国防科技工业管理部门同意。  
重要军工设施管理单位以外的单位或者个人使用重要军工设施保护区的摄影、摄像、录音、描绘、记述、勘察、测量、定位资料，应当经当地省级国防科技工业管理部门同意。  
航空器在重要军工设施保护区上空飞行，应当依照国家有关规定获得批准，并按照批准的飞行计划实施。  
禁止毁坏或者移动重要军工设施保护区的围墙、障碍物、界线标志、警示标志、技术防护设备。

**Article 14** Without the consent of the management unit of an important military industrial facility, persons, vehicles, vessels, etc. other than those of the management unit shall be prohibited from entering the protection zone of the important military industrial facility, and photography, videography, recording, depiction, and description of the protection zone of the important military industrial facility shall be prohibited. Where it is truly necessary to conduct survey, measurement, or positioning of the protection zone of the important military industrial facility, consent shall be obtained from the local provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense.  
Where a unit or individual other than the management unit of an important military industrial facility uses photography, videography, recording, depiction, description, survey, measurement, or positioning data of the protection zone of the important military industrial facility, consent shall be obtained from the local provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense.  
Aircraft flying over the protection zone of an important military industrial facility shall obtain approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and implement the flight in accordance with the approved flight plan.  
It shall be prohibited to destroy or move the walls, obstacles, boundary markers, warning signs, or technical protection equipment of the protection zone of an important military industrial facility.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在重要军工设施保护区内，禁止建造、设置危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的设施。  
禁止开发利用陆地重要军工设施保护区地下空间，法律、行政法规另有规定或者经国务院国防科技工业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府同意的除外。

**Article 15** Within a protection zone for important military industrial facilities, it shall be prohibited to construct or set up facilities that endanger the safety and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities.  
It shall be prohibited to develop and utilize the underground space of a land-based protection zone for important military industrial facilities, except where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, or with the consent of the competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council and the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在重要军工设施保护区内采取的防护措施不足以保证重要军工设施安全保密和使用效能，或者重要军工设施保护区内的重要军工设施具有重大危险因素的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据重要军工设施性质、地形和当地经济建设、社会发展情况，可以在重要军工设施保护区外围划定安全控制范围，并在其外沿设置安全警戒标志。  
安全警戒标志由县级以上地方人民政府按照国家统一规定的样式设置，地点由省级国防科技工业管理部门、当地县级或者设区的市级人民政府共同确定。  
水域重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围难以在实际水域设置安全警戒标志的，依照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第三款的规定执行。

**Article 16** Where the protection measures taken within a protection zone for important military industrial facilities are insufficient to ensure the security, confidentiality, and use effectiveness of the important military industrial facilities, or the important military industrial facilities within the protection zone have major risk factors, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on the nature and terrain of the important military industrial facility and local economic construction and social development, delimit a safety control area outside the protection zone for important military industrial facilities and set up safety warning signs at its outer edge.  
The safety warning signs shall be set up by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the styles uniformly prescribed by the State, and the locations shall be jointly determined by the provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense and the local people's government at the county level or the city divided into districts.  
Where it is difficult to set up safety warning signs in the actual water area for the safety control area outside a water-area protection zone for important military industrial facilities, the provisions of Article 12, paragraph 3 of these Regulations shall be followed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　划定重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围，不改变原土地及土地附着物、水域的所有权。在重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围内，当地居民可以照常生产生活，但是不得进行爆破、射击以及其他危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的活动。  
因划定重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围影响不动产所有权人或者用益物权人行使权利的，依照有关法律法规的规定予以补偿。

**Article 17** The delimitation of a safety control area outside the protection zone for important military industrial facilities shall not change the ownership of the original land, fixtures on the land, or water areas. Within the safety control area outside the protection zone for important military industrial facilities, local residents may carry on production and life as usual, but shall not conduct blasting, shooting, or other activities that endanger the safety and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities.  
Where the delimitation of a safety control area outside the protection zone for an important military industrial facility affects the exercise of rights by the owner of immovable property or the usufructuary, compensation shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　作为重要军工设施的机场的净空保护区域参照军用机场净空保护标准划定。  
在机场净空保护区域内，禁止修建超出机场净空保护标准的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施，不得从事影响飞行安全和机场助航设施使用效能的活动。  
机场管理单位应当定期检查机场净空保护情况，发现修建的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施超过机场净空保护标准的，应当及时向国防科技工业管理部门及其他有关部门报告。有关部门应当依法及时处理。  
地方人民政府应当制定保护措施，督促有关单位对机场净空保护区域内的高大建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施设置飞行障碍标志。  
在作为重要军工设施的机场及周边一定范围的区域实施飞行活动，应当遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 18** The clearance protection area of an airport that is an important military industrial facility shall be delimited with reference to the clearance protection standards for military airports.  
Within the clearance protection area of an airport, it shall be prohibited to construct buildings, structures, or other facilities that exceed the airport clearance protection standards, and no activities affecting flight safety or the use effectiveness of airport navigational aids shall be conducted.  
The airport management unit shall regularly inspect the clearance protection condition of the airport; upon discovering that any buildings, structures, or other facilities constructed exceed the airport clearance protection standards, it shall promptly report to the department of science, technology and industry for national defense and other relevant departments. The relevant departments shall handle the matter promptly according to law.  
The local people's government shall formulate protection measures and urge relevant units to set up flight obstacle markers for tall buildings, structures, or other facilities within the clearance protection area of the airport.  
Flight activities conducted over an airport that is an important military industrial facility and a surrounding area of a certain scope shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　作为重要军工设施的无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施，在其电磁环境保护范围内，禁止建造、设置影响无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施使用效能的设备和电磁障碍物体，不得从事影响无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施电磁环境的活动。  
作为重要军工设施的无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施，其电磁环境的保护范围及保护措施，由军地无线电管理机构按照国家无线电管理有关规定和标准共同确定。使用无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施等应当遵守国家无线电管理有关规定。

**Article 19** For radio fixed facilities and electromagnetic test facilities that are important military industrial facilities, within their electromagnetic environment protection scope, it shall be prohibited to construct or set up equipment or electromagnetic obstacles that affect the use effectiveness of the radio fixed facilities or electromagnetic test facilities, and no activities affecting the electromagnetic environment of the radio fixed facilities or electromagnetic test facilities shall be conducted.  
For radio fixed facilities and electromagnetic test facilities that are important military industrial facilities, the protection scope and protection measures of their electromagnetic environment shall be jointly determined by military and local radio management agencies in accordance with the relevant provisions and standards of the State on radio management. The use of radio fixed facilities, electromagnetic test facilities, etc. shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on radio management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　利用重要军工设施开展重大科研、生产、试验活动，因安全、保密原因确有必要对周边特定区域进行临时管控，在特定时间内禁止人员、车辆、船舶、航空器进入或者禁止开展有关活动的，重要军工设施管理单位应当在活动开始15个工作日前向当地县级人民政府提出管控申请；涉及其他单位管理职权的，依法向其提出管控申请。管控申请经依法批准后，由有关单位、当地县级人民政府在活动开始前以适当方式向社会公布，并组织实施管控活动。

**Article 20** Where major scientific research, production, or test activities are conducted using important military industrial facilities, and it is truly necessary for security and confidentiality reasons to temporarily control a specific surrounding area and prohibit persons, vehicles, vessels, or aircraft from entering or prohibit the conduct of relevant activities within a specific time period, the management unit of the important military industrial facility shall submit a control application to the local people's government at the county level 15 working days before the activity begins; if it involves the management authority of other units, a control application shall be submitted to them according to law. After the control application is approved according to law, the relevant units and the local people's government at the county level shall announce it to the public before the activity begins in an appropriate manner and organize the implementation of the control activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　无法划定重要军工设施保护区的铁路专用线、专用铁路，在线路两侧按照《铁路安全管理条例》的规定划定铁路线路安全保护区予以保护并公告。  
铁路专用线、专用铁路管理单位应当设置与铁路安全需求相适应的安全防护设施，加强对铁路的管理和保护。

**Article 21** For dedicated railway lines and specialized railways for which a protection zone for important military industrial facilities cannot be delimited, railway line safety protection zones shall be delimited on both sides of the lines in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Railway Safety Management to provide protection and be announced.  
The management unit of the dedicated railway line or specialized railway shall set up safety protection facilities commensurate with the railway safety requirements and strengthen the management and protection of the railway.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　无法划定重要军工设施保护区的专用公路不得擅自占用、挖掘。因工程建设需要占用、挖掘、跨越、穿越专用公路或者使用专用公路用地的，建设单位应当事先征得当地省级国防科技工业管理部门的同意。  
禁止在专用公路及其用地范围内堆放物品、设置障碍、利用公路边沟排放污物或者进行其他影响安全畅通的活动，禁止在专用公路两侧从事危害公路安全和使用效能的爆破、挖砂、采石等活动。  
专用公路管理单位应当设置专用公路标志，加强公路养护，保证公路路面平整，路肩、边坡平顺，有关设施完好。

**Article 22** Dedicated highways for which a protection zone for important military industrial facilities cannot be delimited shall not be occupied or excavated without authorization. Where it is necessary to occupy, excavate, cross, or traverse a dedicated highway or use the land of a dedicated highway due to engineering construction, the construction unit shall obtain consent in advance from the local provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense.  
It shall be prohibited to pile up articles, set up obstacles, discharge sewage through highway side ditches, or conduct other activities affecting safety and smooth traffic within the dedicated highway and its land use scope; it shall be prohibited to conduct blasting, sand digging, quarrying, or other activities that endanger highway safety and use effectiveness on both sides of the dedicated highway.  
The management unit of the dedicated highway shall set up dedicated highway signs, strengthen highway maintenance, ensure that the highway pavement is smooth, the shoulders and slopes are even, and the relevant facilities are in good condition.

### 第四章　重要军工设施管理单位的责任义务

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　重要军工设施管理单位应当建立健全本单位重要军工设施保护制度和保护责任制，完善考核机制，确保重要军工设施安全保密和使用效能。

**Article 23** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall establish and improve the protection system and protection responsibility system for important military industrial facilities within that unit, improve the assessment mechanism, and ensure the security, confidentiality, and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　重要军工设施管理单位应当对重要军工设施建设、使用、维护等全过程实施安全管理。重要军工设施安全防护措施应当与重要军工设施同步规划、同步建设、同步使用。

**Article 24** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall implement safety management for the entire process of construction, use, maintenance, etc. of the important military industrial facility. The safety protection measures for important military industrial facilities shall be planned, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the important military industrial facility.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　重要军工设施管理单位应当定期对本单位人员开展培训，教育本单位人员爱护重要军工设施，落实重要军工设施安全保护要求，保守关于重要军工设施的国家秘密。

**Article 25** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall regularly conduct training for its personnel, educate its personnel to take care of important military industrial facilities, implement the safety protection requirements for important military industrial facilities, and keep State secrets concerning important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　重要军工设施管理单位应当建立重要军工设施档案，定期对重要军工设施进行检查、维护，根据需要升级完善安全防护措施。

**Article 26** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall establish archives for important military industrial facilities, regularly inspect and maintain important military industrial facilities, and upgrade and improve safety protection measures as needed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　重要军工设施管理单位应当制定本单位重要军工设施保护应急预案，定期开展应急演练。  
重要军工设施管理单位应当定期组织开展重要军工设施安全保护风险评估，及时发现和消除安全隐患。

**Article 27** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall formulate an emergency plan for the protection of important military industrial facilities within that unit and regularly conduct emergency drills.  
The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall regularly organize and carry out risk assessment on the safety protection of important military industrial facilities, and promptly discover and eliminate safety hazards.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　重要军工设施管理单位属于治安保卫重点单位的，应当设置与本单位治安保卫任务相适应的治安保卫机构，配备专职治安保卫人员，将重要军工设施确定为本单位的治安保卫重要部位并实施重点保护。  
重要军工设施管理单位属于反间谍安全防范重点单位的，应当履行反间谍安全防范工作要求，明确内设职能部门和人员承担反间谍安全防范职责。  
重要军工设施管理单位应当落实网络安全和数据安全保护责任，依照有关规定加强重要军工设施网络安全和数据安全管理、防护。重要军工设施涉及关键信息基础设施的，重要军工设施管理单位应当依照有关规定做好相关安全保护工作。  
重要军工设施属于人民防空工作中需要重点防护的目标、防范恐怖袭击的重点目标的，重要军工设施管理单位应当依照有关规定履行防护义务。

**Article 28** Where the management unit of an important military industrial facility is a key unit for public security protection, it shall set up a public security protection agency commensurate with its public security protection tasks, equip it with full-time public security protection personnel, designate the important military industrial facility as a key part of public security protection for that unit, and implement key protection.  
Where the management unit of an important military industrial facility is a key unit for anti-espionage security prevention, it shall fulfill the requirements for anti-espionage security prevention work, and designate internal functional departments and personnel to assume anti-espionage security prevention responsibilities.  
The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall implement cybersecurity and data security protection responsibilities, and strengthen the management and protection of cybersecurity and data security for important military industrial facilities in accordance with relevant provisions. Where an important military industrial facility involves critical information infrastructure, the management unit of the important military industrial facility shall properly carry out relevant security protection work in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Where an important military industrial facility is a target requiring key protection in civil air defense work or a key target for preventing terrorist attacks, the management unit of the important military industrial facility shall fulfill protection obligations in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　重要军工设施管理单位以外的单位使用重要军工设施的，重要军工设施管理单位应当明确使用单位的安全保护责任，指导监督其落实安全保护措施。

**Article 29** Where a unit other than the management unit of an important military industrial facility uses the important military industrial facility, the management unit shall specify the safety protection responsibilities of the user unit and guide and supervise it in implementing safety protection measures.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　重要军工设施管理单位对违反本条例规定、危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的行为应当予以制止，及时向有关部门报告，并配合依法处理。

**Article 30** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall stop any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations and endangers the safety and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities, promptly report to the relevant departments, and cooperate in handling it according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　重要军工设施管理单位应当了解掌握重要军工设施周边社会环境情况，发现可能危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的，应当及时向当地省级国防科技工业管理部门及其他有关部门报告，并配合依法处理。

**Article 31** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall understand and grasp the social environment around the important military industrial facility; upon discovering any potential endangerment to the safety and use effectiveness of the important military industrial facility, it shall promptly report to the local provincial department of science, technology and industry for national defense and other relevant departments, and cooperate in handling it according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　重要军工设施管理单位应当依照有关法律法规的规定，保护重要军工设施保护区内的生态环境、自然资源和文物。

**Article 32** The management unit of an important military industrial facility shall protect the ecological environment, natural resources, and cultural relics within the protection zone for important military industrial facilities in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第五章　保障和监督

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当制定重要军工设施保护的具体方案，可以公告施行。  
划定重要军工设施保护区的重要军工设施保护的具体方案，应当随重要军工设施保护区范围划定方案一并报批。

**Article 33** The local people's government at or above the county level shall formulate specific plans for the protection of important military industrial facilities, and may announce them for implementation.  
The specific plan for the protection of an important military industrial facility for which a protection zone has been delimited shall be submitted for approval together with the delimitation plan for the scope of the protection zone for important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府编制国民经济和社会发展规划，国务院有关部门、地方人民政府编制国土空间规划等规划，应当统筹重要军工设施保护的需要，充分听取国防科技工业管理部门的意见。  
县级以上地方人民政府安排可能影响重要军工设施保护的建设项目，应当充分听取国防科技工业管理部门的意见。必要时，可以由地方人民政府会同有关方面对建设项目进行评估。涉及国家安全事项的建设项目许可，依法由国家安全机关实施。  
国务院有关部门或者县级以上地方人民政府有关部门审批本条第二款规定的建设项目，应当审查征求国防科技工业管理部门意见的情况；对未按规定征求国防科技工业管理部门意见的，应当要求补充征求意见；建设项目内容在审批过程中发生的改变可能影响重要军工设施保护的，应当再次征求国防科技工业管理部门的意见。  
国防科技工业管理部门应当自收到征求意见函之日起30日内提出书面答复意见；需要请示上级机关或者需要勘察、测量、测试的，可以适当延长，但答复时间通常不得超过90日。

**Article 34** When formulating national economic and social development plans, the local people's government at or above the county level, and when formulating plans such as territorial space plans, the relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments, shall coordinate the needs for the protection of important military industrial facilities and fully solicit the opinions of the department of science, technology and industry for national defense.  
When arranging construction projects that may affect the protection of important military industrial facilities, the local people's government at or above the county level shall fully solicit the opinions of the department of science, technology and industry for national defense. Where necessary, the local people's government may, together with the relevant parties, evaluate the construction project. The permit for construction projects involving matters of national security shall be implemented by the State security organs according to law.  
When examining and approving a construction project as specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, the relevant department of the State Council or the relevant department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall review the status of soliciting opinions from the department of science, technology and industry for national defense; if opinions have not been solicited from the department of science, technology and industry for national defense as required, the relevant department shall require supplementary solicitation of opinions; if changes in the content of the construction project during the examination and approval process may affect the protection of important military industrial facilities, opinions shall be solicited again from the department of science, technology and industry for national defense.  
The department of science, technology and industry for national defense shall submit a written reply within 30 days from the date of receiving the request for opinions; if it is necessary to report to a superior authority or to conduct survey, measurement, or testing, the time limit may be appropriately extended, but the reply time shall generally not exceed 90 days.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　组织重要军工设施项目建设，应当综合考虑国防建设、地方经济建设、生态环境保护和社会发展的需要，符合国土空间规划等规划的要求，依法征求有关单位意见，进行安全保密环境评估和环境影响评价。  
重要军工设施具有爆炸、放射性、剧毒等重大危险因素的，在选址时应当按照国家有关标准要求与其他场所、设施、区域保持必要的外部安全距离。

**Article 35** When organizing the construction of an important military industrial facility project, the needs of national defense construction, local economic construction, ecological environmental protection, and social development shall be comprehensively considered, the requirements of plans such as territorial space plans shall be met, opinions from relevant units shall be solicited according to law, and security and confidentiality environment assessment and environmental impact assessment shall be conducted.  
Where an important military industrial facility has major risk factors such as explosion, radioactivity, or highly toxic substances, when selecting the site, the necessary external safety distance from other places, facilities, and areas shall be maintained in accordance with the requirements of relevant national standards.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府安排建设项目或者开发旅游景区，应当避开重要军工设施。  
确实不能避开，需要将重要军工设施拆除、迁建或者改建的，按规定报原批准或者备案机关履行相关程序后，由提出需求的地方人民政府依照有关规定给予补偿或者政策支持。将重要军工设施迁建、改建涉及用地用海用岛的，应当依法及时办理相关手续。

**Article 36** When arranging construction projects or developing tourist attractions, the local people's government at or above the county level shall avoid important military industrial facilities.  
Where it is truly impossible to avoid and it is necessary to demolish, relocate, or rebuild an important military industrial facility, after reporting to the original approval or filing authority and completing the relevant procedures as prescribed, the local people's government making the demand shall provide compensation or policy support in accordance with the relevant provisions. Where the relocation or reconstruction of an important military industrial facility involves land, sea areas, or islands, the relevant procedures shall be promptly handled according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级人民政府应当加强国防和重要军工设施保护教育，使全体公民增强国防观念，保护重要军工设施，保守关于重要军工设施的国家秘密，制止破坏、危害重要军工设施的行为。

**Article 37** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen education on national defense and the protection of important military industrial facilities, so that all citizens enhance their national defense awareness, protect important military industrial facilities, keep State secrets concerning important military industrial facilities, and stop acts of destroying or endangering important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门应当建立重要军工设施风险管控机制，制定监督检查计划，会同有关部门开展监督检查，及时处理发现的风险隐患。

**Article 38** The competent department of science, technology and industry for national defense under the State Council shall establish a risk management and control mechanism for important military industrial facilities, formulate supervision and inspection plans, carry out supervision and inspection jointly with relevant departments, and promptly address any risks and hidden dangers discovered.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当对重要军工设施周边的安全保密隐患开展综合治理，督促限期整改影响重要军工设施保护的隐患和问题，完善重要军工设施保护方案。

**Article 39** The local people's government at or above the county level shall carry out comprehensive management of security and confidentiality hazards around important military industrial facilities, urge the rectification of hazards and problems affecting the protection of important military industrial facilities within a time limit, and improve the protection plans for important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　重要军工设施管理单位的上级企业事业单位应当督促指导重要军工设施管理单位开展重要军工设施保护工作，并提供必要的支持。

**Article 40** The superior enterprise or institution of the management unit of an important military industrial facility shall supervise and guide the management unit in carrying out the protection of important military industrial facilities and provide necessary support.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列行为之一，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成间谍行为，尚不构成犯罪的，由国家安全机关依法处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）擅自进入重要军工设施保护区的；  
（二）在重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围内进行危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的活动的；  
（三）在作为重要军工设施的机场的净空保护区域内，进行影响飞行安全和机场助航设施使用效能的活动的；  
（四）对重要军工设施保护区非法进行摄影、摄像、录音、描绘、记述、勘察、测量、定位，或者非法使用重要军工设施保护区的摄影、摄像、录音、描绘、记述、勘察、测量、定位资料的；  
（五）毁坏或者移动重要军工设施保护区的围墙、障碍物、界线标志、警示标志、技术防护设备以及重要军工设施保护区标志牌、重要军工设施保护区外围安全控制范围安全警戒标志及其他重要军工设施标志的；  
（六）违反国家规定，故意干扰重要军工无线电设施正常工作的，或者对重要军工无线电设施产生有害干扰，拒不按照有关主管部门的要求改正的；  
（七）违反利用重要军工设施开展科研、生产、试验活动时的管控措施的；  
（八）其他扰乱重要军工设施保护区的科研生产秩序和危害重要军工设施安全的行为。

**Article 41** Whoever commits any of the following acts, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administrative penalty by the public security organ according to law; if the act constitutes espionage but is not serious enough to constitute a crime, shall be punished by the State security organ according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Entering a protection zone for important military industrial facilities without authorization;  
(2) Conducting activities within the safety control area outside a protection zone for important military industrial facilities that endanger the safety and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities;  
(3) Conducting activities within the clearance protection area of an airport that is an important military industrial facility that affect flight safety or the use effectiveness of airport navigational aids;  
(4) Illegally photographing, videotaping, recording, depicting, describing, surveying, measuring, or positioning a protection zone for important military industrial facilities, or illegally using photography, videotaping, recording, depiction, description, survey, measurement, or positioning data of a protection zone for important military industrial facilities;  
(5) Destroying or moving the walls, obstacles, boundary markers, warning signs, technical protection equipment, signboards of protection zones for important military industrial facilities, safety warning signs of the safety control area outside a protection zone for important military industrial facilities, or other signs of important military industrial facilities;  
(6) Violating State provisions by intentionally interfering with the normal operation of important military radio facilities, or causing harmful interference to important military radio facilities and refusing to correct it as required by the relevant competent authorities;  
(7) Violating the control measures when using important military industrial facilities to conduct scientific research, production, or test activities;  
(8) Other acts that disrupt the scientific research and production order of a protection zone for important military industrial facilities and endanger the safety of important military industrial facilities.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，在重要军工设施保护区内建造、设置危害重要军工设施安全和使用效能的设施，或者擅自开发利用陆地重要军工设施保护区地下空间的，由自然资源、住房城乡建设、渔业渔政等主管部门按照各自职责责令停止兴建活动，对已建成的责令限期拆除，依法处以罚款。

**Article 42** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by constructing or setting up facilities that endanger the safety and use effectiveness of important military industrial facilities within a protection zone for important military industrial facilities, or by developing and utilizing the underground space of a land-based protection zone for important military industrial facilities without authorization, the competent departments of natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, fisheries and fishery administration, etc. shall, within their respective functions and duties, order cessation of the construction activities, order demolition of the completed parts within a time limit, and impose a fine according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，在作为重要军工设施的机场的净空保护区域内修建超出机场净空保护标准的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的，由自然资源、住房城乡建设主管部门按照各自职责责令停止兴建活动、限期拆除超高部分，依法处以罚款。

**Article 43** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by constructing buildings, structures, or other facilities that exceed the airport clearance protection standards within the clearance protection area of an airport that is an important military industrial facility, the competent departments of natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction shall, within their respective functions and duties, order cessation of the construction activities, order demolition of the parts exceeding the height limit within a time limit, and impose a fine according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　未经批准或者未按照批准的飞行计划在重要军工设施保护区上空实施飞行活动的，依照有关法律、行政法规进行处罚。

**Article 44** Where any person conducts flight activities over a protection zone for important military industrial facilities without approval or not in accordance with the approved flight plan, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，在重要军工设施电磁环境保护范围内建造、设置影响无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施使用效能的设备和电磁障碍物体，或者从事影响无线电固定设施、电磁试验设施电磁环境活动的，由自然资源主管部门、无线电管理机构等单位按照各自职责给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，查封干扰设备或者强制拆除障碍物，依法处以罚款。

**Article 45** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by constructing or setting up equipment or electromagnetic obstacles that affect the use effectiveness of radio fixed facilities or electromagnetic test facilities within the electromagnetic environment protection scope of important military industrial facilities, or by engaging in activities that affect the electromagnetic environment of radio fixed facilities or electromagnetic test facilities, the competent department of natural resources, the radio regulatory agency, and other units shall, within their respective functions and duties, give a warning and order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the interfering equipment shall be sealed up or the obstacles shall be forcibly removed, and a fine shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，破坏、危害铁路专用线、专用铁路的，依照有关法律、行政法规进行处罚。

**Article 46** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by destroying or endangering a dedicated railway line or specialized railway, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，擅自占用、挖掘专用公路，或者在专用公路两侧从事危害公路安全和使用效能的爆破、挖砂、采石等活动的，由交通运输主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以处3万元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例规定，在专用公路及其用地范围内堆放物品、设置障碍、利用公路边沟排放污物或者进行其他影响安全畅通的活动，造成公路路面损坏、污染或者影响公路畅通的，由交通运输主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以处5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by occupying or excavating a dedicated highway without authorization, or conducting blasting, sand digging, quarrying, or other activities that endanger the safety and use effectiveness of a dedicated highway on both sides of the dedicated highway, the competent department of transportation shall order cessation of the illegal act, and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.  
Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by piling up articles, setting up obstacles, discharging sewage through highway side ditches, or conducting other activities affecting safety and smooth traffic within a dedicated highway and its land use scope, causing damage to or pollution of the highway pavement or affecting the smooth traffic of the highway, the competent department of transportation shall order cessation of the illegal act, and may impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　重要军工设施管理单位违反本条例规定，未实施保护措施、履行保护责任的，国防科技工业管理部门可以采取约谈、责令限期改正等措施，根据情节给予警告、通报批评；造成不良后果或者影响的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 48** Where a management unit of an important military industrial facility violates the provisions of these Regulations by failing to implement protection measures or fulfill protection responsibilities, the department of science, technology and industry for national defense may take measures such as conducting interviews, ordering correction within a time limit, and depending on the circumstances, give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism; if adverse consequences or impact are caused, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel with responsibility shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公职人员在重要军工设施保护工作中有玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊等行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 49** Where public officials commit dereliction of duty, abuse of power, engage in favoritism, or commit other acts in the protection of important military industrial facilities, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，造成重要军工设施损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes losses to important military industrial facilities, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例自2025年9月15日起施行。

**Article 51** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 15, 2025.
