# 外国人来华登山管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1991-08-28  
**生效日期**：1991-08-29

> (1991年7月31日国务院批准　1991年8月29日国家体育运动委员会令第16号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对外国人在中国境内登山的管理，有组织地进行国际登山交流，促进我国登山事业发展，制定本办法。 第二条　外国人在中国境内攀登下列对外开放的山峰以及附带在山峰区域内进行科学考察、测绘活动，适用本办法： (一)西藏自治区海拔五千米以上的山峰； (二)其他省、自治区海拔三千五百米以上的山峰。 第三条　外国人在中国境内进行登山活动，应当遵守中国的法律；外国人的正当权益，受中国法律保护。 第四条　对外国人在中国境内登山的管理，实行统一领导，分级负责的原则。 第五条　对外开放山峰由中华人民共和国国家体育运动委员会(以下简称国家体委)和公安部公布。 第二章　来华登山手续 第六条　外国人来华登山，可以自行组成团队，也可以由外国团队和中国团队组成联合团队。 第七条　外国人来华登山，应当向国家体委提出书面申请。 外国人组成外国团队来华登山的，由外国团队提出申请，也可以委托我国省、自治区登山协会代理申请事宜。 外国团队和中国团队组成中外联合团队登山的，由中国团队提出申请。 第八条　国家体委收到外国团队或者中外联合团队的登山申请后，应当在六十日内作出是否批准的决定，并以书面形式通知外国团队或者中外联合团队、代理申请事宜的省、自治区登山协会和登山活动所在省、自治区体委。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对外国人在中国境内登山的管理，有组织地进行国际登山交流，促进我国登山事业发展，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of mountaineering by foreigners within Chinese territory, organizing international mountaineering exchanges in a systematic manner, and promoting the development of mountaineering in China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外国人在中国境内攀登下列对外开放的山峰以及附带在山峰区域内进行科学考察、测绘活动，适用本办法：  
(一)西藏自治区海拔五千米以上的山峰；  
(二)其他省、自治区海拔三千五百米以上的山峰。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the climbing of the following mountains open to foreigners and the accompanying scientific survey and mapping activities within the mountain areas by foreigners within Chinese territory:  
(1) Mountains with an altitude of 5,000 meters or above in the Tibet Autonomous Region;  
(2) Mountains with an altitude of 3,500 meters or above in other provinces and autonomous regions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外国人在中国境内进行登山活动，应当遵守中国的法律；外国人的正当权益，受中国法律保护。

**Article 3** Foreigners conducting mountaineering activities within Chinese territory shall abide by Chinese laws; the legitimate rights and interests of foreigners shall be protected by Chinese laws.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对外国人在中国境内登山的管理，实行统一领导，分级负责的原则。

**Article 4** The administration of mountaineering by foreigners within Chinese territory shall follow the principle of unified leadership and graded responsibility.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对外开放山峰由中华人民共和国国家体育运动委员会(以下简称国家体委)和公安部公布。

**Article 5** The mountains open to foreigners shall be announced by the State Physical Culture and Sports Commission (hereinafter referred to as the State Sports Commission) and the Ministry of Public Security.

### 第二章　来华登山手续

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国人来华登山，可以自行组成团队，也可以由外国团队和中国团队组成联合团队。

**Article 6** Foreigners coming to China for mountaineering may form teams on their own, or foreign teams and Chinese teams may form joint teams.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国人来华登山，应当向国家体委提出书面申请。  
外国人组成外国团队来华登山的，由外国团队提出申请，也可以委托我国省、自治区登山协会代理申请事宜。  
外国团队和中国团队组成中外联合团队登山的，由中国团队提出申请。

**Article 7** Foreigners coming to China for mountaineering shall submit a written application to the State Sports Commission.  
Where foreigners form foreign teams to come to China for mountaineering, the foreign team shall submit the application, or may entrust the mountaineering association of a province or autonomous region in China to handle the application matters on its behalf.  
Where a foreign team and a Chinese team form a Sino-foreign joint team for mountaineering, the Chinese team shall submit the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家体委收到外国团队或者中外联合团队的登山申请后，应当在六十日内作出是否批准的决定，并以书面形式通知外国团队或者中外联合团队、代理申请事宜的省、自治区登山协会和登山活动所在省、自治区体委。

**Article 8** After receiving the mountaineering application from a foreign team or a Sino-foreign joint team, the State Sports Commission shall make a decision on whether to approve within 60 days, and shall notify the foreign team or the Sino-foreign joint team, the mountaineering association of the province or autonomous region handling the application, and the sports commission of the province or autonomous region where the mountaineering activity is to be conducted in writing.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国团队接到国家体委批准的登山通知后，应当按照通知要求缴纳注册费，并与通知中指定的单位签订登山议定书。  
与外国团队签订登山议定书的单位(以下简称中方签约单位)应当及时将议定书副本报送国家体委备案。

**Article 9** After receiving the notice of approval for mountaineering from the State Sports Commission, the foreign team shall pay the registration fee in accordance with the requirements of the notice and sign a mountaineering protocol with the unit designated in the notice.  
The unit that signs the mountaineering protocol with the foreign team (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese contracting unit) shall promptly submit a copy of the protocol to the State Sports Commission for recordation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　议定书报送国家体委备案后，不得任意变更。如需要变更，应当经签订议定书的中外双方协商确认；如果变更攀登的季节、路线或者山峰，应当重新报国家体委审批。

**Article 10** After the protocol is submitted to the State Sports Commission for recordation, it shall not be arbitrarily modified. If modification is necessary, it shall be confirmed through consultation between the Chinese and foreign parties that signed the protocol; if the season, route, or mountain for climbing is changed, it shall be re-submitted to the State Sports Commission for approval.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国团队应当在入境的一个月前，将在中国境内登山的经费按预算全额汇寄中方签约单位，并按照国家体委的通知在中国驻外使(领)馆办理签证。

**Article 11** The foreign team shall, one month before entering China, remit the full amount of funds for mountaineering within Chinese territory to the Chinese contracting unit according to the budget, and shall handle visa matters at Chinese embassies or consulates abroad in accordance with the notice of the State Sports Commission.

### 第三章　登山活动

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国团队在登山前应当为随队的中国公民办理有关保险事项，并根据国家体委的要求落实保护山区自然环境的各项措施。

**Article 12** The foreign team shall, before mountaineering, handle the relevant insurance matters for the Chinese citizens accompanying the team, and shall implement measures to protect the natural environment of the mountain area in accordance with the requirements of the State Sports Commission.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国人登山，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)按照国家体委批准的山峰和路线进行攀登，不得攀登其他山峰，不得越过批准的路线；外国登山团队之间不得互相转让攀登的山峰和路线；  
(二)外国登山团队不得吸收本团队以外的队员；  
(三)如要求展现外国团队所在国国旗，应当经中国国家体委同意，并同时展现规格相当的中国国旗；  
(四)使用山峰的名称和高度，应当以中国政府有关部门公布的为准；  
(五)保持登山路线和营区的环境卫生，不得自行在登山活动区域安放纪念品和其他物品；  
(六)将登山活动的结果和登山过程中的意外情况及时报告国家体委和中方签约单位。

**Article 13** Foreigners conducting mountaineering shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) They shall climb only the mountains and routes approved by the State Sports Commission, and shall not climb other mountains or exceed the approved routes; foreign mountaineering teams shall not transfer the mountains or routes for climbing to each other;  
(2) Foreign mountaineering teams shall not recruit members outside their own teams;  
(3) If they request to display the national flag of the country of the foreign team, approval from the State Sports Commission shall be obtained, and the Chinese national flag of equivalent specifications shall be displayed at the same time;  
(4) The names and heights of mountains used shall be based on those published by the relevant departments of the Chinese government;  
(5) They shall maintain the environmental hygiene of the climbing route and camps, and shall not place souvenirs or other items in the mountaineering activity area on their own;  
(6) They shall promptly report the results of the mountaineering activity and any unexpected situations during the mountaineering process to the State Sports Commission and the Chinese contracting unit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国人在登山活动结束后，应当以团队为单位写出总结报告书。  
登山活动总结报告书以及在登山期间摄录的音像资料，应当无偿向国家体委和登山活动所在省、自治区体委提供。

**Article 14** After the mountaineering activity, foreigners shall write a summary report on a team basis.  
The summary report of the mountaineering activity and any audio-visual materials recorded during the mountaineering shall be provided free of charge to the State Sports Commission and the sports commission of the province or autonomous region where the mountaineering activity took place.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国人在我国境内登顶成功，由国家体委确认后，发给其登顶证明书。

**Article 15** Where a foreigner successfully summits a mountain within Chinese territory, the State Sports Commission shall, after confirmation, issue a summit certificate to him/her.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国人在我国境内登山期间，必须有我国联络人员陪同。联络人员由中方签约单位指定，其职责是：  
(一)协助并监督外国人执行我国的有关规定；  
(二)协助解决外国人在登山活动中的有关问题；  
(三)向中方签约单位报告有关情况；  
(四)调解外国人与中方服务人员的纠纷。

**Article 16** During the period of mountaineering within Chinese territory, foreigners must be accompanied by Chinese liaison personnel. The liaison personnel shall be designated by the Chinese contracting unit, and their duties shall be: (1) to assist and supervise foreigners in implementing the relevant regulations of China; (2) to assist in resolving relevant issues concerning foreigners during the mountaineering activity; (3) to report relevant situations to the Chinese contracting unit; (4) to mediate disputes between foreigners and Chinese service personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国人登山，需要我国公民提供服务，由我国联络人员办理。  
中国公民向外国人提供服务，可以收取服务费用。服务费用的项目和标准，由国家体委公布。

**Article 17** Where foreigners need Chinese citizens to provide services during mountaineering, such matters shall be handled by the Chinese liaison personnel.  
Chinese citizens providing services to foreigners may charge service fees. The items and standards of service fees shall be published by the State Sports Commission.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外国团队应当为随队的中国公民提供医疗、急救以及必要的宿营、炊事用具。  
未经我国联络人员同意，外国团队不得自行解雇随队的中国公民或者停发津贴。  
中外联合团队向随队的中国公民提供医疗、急救和宿营、炊事用具的办法，由组成中外联合团队的各方协商。

**Article 18** The foreign team shall provide medical care, first aid, and necessary camping and cooking utensils for the Chinese citizens accompanying the team.  
Without the consent of the Chinese liaison personnel, the foreign team shall not dismiss the Chinese citizens accompanying the team or suspend their allowances on its own.  
The method of providing medical care, first aid, and camping and cooking utensils for the Chinese citizens accompanying the team by a Sino-foreign joint team shall be negotiated by the parties forming the joint team.

### 第四章　登山附带科学考察和测绘

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　登山附带科学考察和测绘的，应当在办理登山申请的同时，向国家体委申报科学考察和测绘计划，由国家体委分别转国家科学技术委员会或者国家测绘局审批。  
科学考察和测绘计划未经批准，外国登山人员不得对所经地区的生物、岩石、矿物、冰雪、水样和土样进行系统观测，不得采集标本、样品、化石，不得进行测绘活动。

**Article 19** Where mountaineering is accompanied by scientific survey and mapping, the scientific survey and mapping plan shall be reported to the State Sports Commission at the same time as the mountaineering application is handled, and the State Sports Commission shall forward it to the State Science and Technology Commission or the State Bureau of Surveying and Mapping for approval respectively.  
Where the scientific survey and mapping plan has not been approved, foreign mountaineering personnel shall not conduct systematic observation of the organisms, rocks, minerals, ice and snow, water samples, and soil samples in the areas they pass through, shall not collect specimens, samples, or fossils, and shall not carry out mapping activities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　外国团队、中外联合团队登山附带科学考察的，应当通过中方签约单位向国家科学技术委员会提供下列样品和资料：  
(一)采集的标本、样品和化石的清单；  
(二)发现的动植物新种或者特殊动植物的类群；  
(三)采集的动植物新种正模式标本、特缺动植物类群的标本；  
(四)标本、样品、化石的室内分析结果；  
(五)登山附带科学考察的音像资料复制本。  
外国团队、中外联合团体登山附带测绘的，应当通过中方签约单位向国家测绘局提供测绘成果的副本或复制件。

**Article 20** Where a foreign team or a Sino-foreign joint team conducts mountaineering accompanied by scientific survey, it shall provide the following samples and materials to the State Science and Technology Commission through the Chinese contracting unit:  
(1) A list of the specimens, samples, and fossils collected;  
(2) New species of plants or animals discovered or special groups of plants or animals;  
(3) Holotype specimens of new species of plants or animals collected, specimens of particularly scarce groups of plants or animals;  
(4) Laboratory analysis results of specimens, samples, and fossils;  
(5) Copies of audio-visual materials of the scientific survey accompanying the mountaineering.  
Where a foreign team or a Sino-foreign joint team conducts mountaineering accompanied by mapping, it shall provide copies or reproductions of the mapping results to the State Bureau of Surveying and Mapping through the Chinese contracting unit.

### 第五章　登山物资的入境和出境

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国人携带登山所需物资入境，按“特准进口物品”和“暂时进口物品”分别申报。经海关核准后，办理税收、担保手续。

**Article 21** Where foreigners bring materials necessary for mountaineering into China, they shall declare them as “specially approved imported goods” and “temporarily imported goods” respectively. After customs approval, they shall handle the tax and guarantee formalities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　登山物资中合理数量的专用食品、急救药品、防寒衣物、燃料等消耗性物品，可以特准免税入境；超过合理数量的，应当纳税。  
国家有关部门允许的通讯、摄影、录像、测绘器材和专用运输工具可以暂时免税入境。登山活动结束，上述物资应当复运出境。如因特殊原因无法复运出境的，应当通过国家体委依照国家有关规定办理手续。

**Article 22** Consumable items such as special food, first-aid medicines, cold-weather clothing, and fuel within a reasonable quantity in mountaineering materials may be specially approved for duty-free entry; those exceeding the reasonable quantity shall be subject to tax.  
Communication, photography, video recording, and mapping equipment and special transport tools permitted by the relevant state departments may be temporarily duty-free entry. After the mountaineering activity ends, the aforesaid materials shall be shipped out of China again. If they cannot be shipped out again due to special reasons, formalities shall be handled through the State Sports Commission in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　外国团队、中外联合团队登山时采集的标本、样品、化石以及制作的音像资料，经有关部门检验许可后，方可携带出境。

**Article 23** Specimens, samples, fossils collected, and audio-visual materials made by a foreign team or a Sino-foreign joint team during mountaineering may be taken out of China only after being inspected and permitted by the relevant departments.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　外国人来华登山，违反[本办法第十二条](#art-12)、第十三条、第十八条、第十九条、第二十条规定或者未经国家体委批准擅自登山的，国家体委或者省、自治区体委视情节轻重，可以分别给予警告、五千元至五万元的罚款以及停止登山活动等处罚。  
违反[本办法第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条规定的，国家体委或者省、自治区体委，还可以单处或者并处没收采集的标本、样品、化石和资料的处罚。

**Article 24** Where foreigners coming to China for mountaineering violate the provisions of Articles 12, 13, 18, 19, or 20 of these Measures or engage in mountaineering without the approval of the State Sports Commission, the State Sports Commission or the sports commission of the province or autonomous region may, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose penalties such as a warning, a fine of 5,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan, and suspension of mountaineering activities.  
Where the provisions of Articles 19 or 20 are violated, the State Sports Commission or the sports commission of the province or autonomous region may also impose, separately or concurrently, the penalty of confiscation of the collected specimens, samples, fossils, and materials.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以依照中国有关法律的规定先申请行政复议。当事人对复议决定不服的，可以依照中国有关法律的规定提起行政诉讼。当事人在规定的期限内不申请复议和不提起行政诉讼，逾期又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的行政机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 25** Where a party is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, it may first apply for administrative reconsideration in accordance with the provisions of relevant Chinese laws. Where the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, it may initiate an administrative action in accordance with the provisions of relevant Chinese laws. Where the party neither applies for reconsideration nor initiates an administrative action within the prescribed time limit, and fails to comply with the penalty decision upon expiration of the time limit, the administrative organ that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　台湾、香港、澳门同胞回大陆登山，参照执行本办法的规定。

**Article 26** Where compatriots from Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao return to the mainland for mountaineering, the provisions of these Measures shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本办法由国家体育运动委员会解释。

**Article 27** These Measures shall be interpreted by the State Physical Culture and Sports Commission.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
