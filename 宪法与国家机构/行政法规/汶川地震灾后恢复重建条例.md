# 汶川地震灾后恢复重建条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2008-06-08  
**生效日期**：2008-06-08

> (2008年6月4日国务院第11次常务会议通过　2008年6月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第526号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障汶川地震灾后恢复重建工作有力、有序、有效地开展，积极、稳妥恢复灾区群众正常的生活、生产、学习、工作条件，促进灾区经济社会的恢复和发展，根据《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》和《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》，制定本条例。 第二条　地震灾后恢复重建应当坚持以人为本、科学规划、统筹兼顾、分步实施、自力更生、国家支持、社会帮扶的方针。 第三条　地震灾后恢复重建应当遵循以下原则： (一)受灾地区自力更生、生产自救与国家支持、对口支援相结合； (二)政府主导与社会参与相结合； (三)就地恢复重建与异地新建相结合； (四)确保质量与注重效率相结合； (五)立足当前与兼顾长远相结合； (六)经济社会发展与生态环境资源保护相结合。 第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建工作的领导、组织和协调，必要时成立地震灾后恢复重建协调机构，组织协调地震灾后恢复重建工作。 县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在本级人民政府的统一领导下，按照职责分工，密切配合，采取有效措施，共同做好地震灾后恢复重建工作。 第五条　地震灾区的各级人民政府应当自力更生、艰苦奋斗、勤俭节约，多种渠道筹集资金、物资，开展地震灾后恢复重建。 国家对地震灾后恢复重建给予财政支持、税收优惠和金融扶持，并积极提供物资、技术和人力等方面的支持。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障汶川地震灾后恢复重建工作有力、有序、有效地开展，积极、稳妥恢复灾区群众正常的生活、生产、学习、工作条件，促进灾区经济社会的恢复和发展，根据《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》和《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protecting Against and Mitigating Earthquake Disasters, for the purposes of ensuring the forceful, orderly and effective implementation of post-Wenchuan earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction, actively and steadily restoring the normal living, production, study and working conditions of the people in the earthquake-stricken areas, and promoting the recovery and development of the economy and society in these areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地震灾后恢复重建应当坚持以人为本、科学规划、统筹兼顾、分步实施、自力更生、国家支持、社会帮扶的方针。

**Article 2** The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction shall adhere to the principles of putting people first, scientific planning, overall consideration, step-by-step implementation, self-reliance, state support and social assistance.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地震灾后恢复重建应当遵循以下原则：  
(一)受灾地区自力更生、生产自救与国家支持、对口支援相结合；  
(二)政府主导与社会参与相结合；  
(三)就地恢复重建与异地新建相结合；  
(四)确保质量与注重效率相结合；  
(五)立足当前与兼顾长远相结合；  
(六)经济社会发展与生态环境资源保护相结合。

**Article 3** The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction shall follow the following principles:  
(1) combining self-reliance and production自救 of the affected areas with state support and counterpart assistance;  
(2) combining government leadership with social participation;  
(3) combining on-site rehabilitation and reconstruction with construction at new sites;  
(4) combining quality assurance with efficiency focus;  
(5) combining meeting current needs with taking into account long-term development;  
(6) combining economic and social development with protection of ecological environment and resources.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建工作的领导、组织和协调，必要时成立地震灾后恢复重建协调机构，组织协调地震灾后恢复重建工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在本级人民政府的统一领导下，按照职责分工，密切配合，采取有效措施，共同做好地震灾后恢复重建工作。

**Article 4** The people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the leadership, organization and coordination of the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction work, and if necessary, set up coordinating bodies for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction to organize and coordinate such work.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the unified leadership of the people's governments at the same level, closely cooperate with each other in accordance with their respective functions and duties and take effective measures to jointly do a good job in the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地震灾区的各级人民政府应当自力更生、艰苦奋斗、勤俭节约，多种渠道筹集资金、物资，开展地震灾后恢复重建。  
国家对地震灾后恢复重建给予财政支持、税收优惠和金融扶持，并积极提供物资、技术和人力等方面的支持。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织积极参与地震灾后恢复重建工作，支持在地震灾后恢复重建中采用先进的技术、设备和材料。  
国家接受外国政府和国际组织提供的符合地震灾后恢复重建需要的援助。

**Article 5** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall rely on their own efforts, work hard, practice economy, and raise funds and materials through multiple channels to carry out post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction.  
The State shall provide financial support, tax preferences and financial assistance for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction, and actively provide support in terms of materials, technology and human resources.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to actively participate in the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction work, and supports the adoption of advanced technologies, equipment and materials in such work.  
The State accepts assistance provided by foreign governments and international organizations that meets the needs of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对在地震灾后恢复重建工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 6** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　过渡性安置

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对地震灾区的受灾群众进行过渡性安置，应当根据地震灾区的实际情况，采取就地安置与异地安置，集中安置与分散安置，政府安置与投亲靠友、自行安置相结合的方式。  
政府对投亲靠友和采取其他方式自行安置的受灾群众给予适当补助。具体办法由省级人民政府制定。

**Article 7** For the transitional resettlement of the affected people in the earthquake-stricken areas, methods combining local resettlement with resettlement in other places, centralized resettlement with decentralized resettlement, and government resettlement with resettlement by staying with relatives or friends or self-resettlement shall be adopted based on the actual conditions of the earthquake-stricken areas.  
The government shall provide appropriate subsidies to affected people who resettle themselves by staying with relatives or friends or through other means. Specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　过渡性安置地点应当选在交通条件便利、方便受灾群众恢复生产和生活的区域，并避开地震活动断层和可能发生洪灾、山体滑坡和崩塌、泥石流、地面塌陷、雷击等灾害的区域以及生产、储存易燃易爆危险品的工厂、仓库。  
实施过渡性安置应当占用废弃地、空旷地，尽量不占用或者少占用农田，并避免对自然保护区、饮用水水源保护区以及生态脆弱区域造成破坏。

**Article 8** The sites for transitional resettlement shall be selected in areas with convenient transportation conditions and conducive to the affected people's restoration of production and daily life, and shall avoid earthquake active faults and areas prone to floods, landslides, collapses, debris flows, ground subsidence, lightning strikes and other disasters, as well as factories and warehouses producing or storing inflammable and explosive dangerous goods.  
The implementation of transitional resettlement shall use废弃地 or vacant land, and shall, to the extent possible, not occupy or occupy less farmland, and shall avoid causing damage to nature reserves, drinking water source protection areas and ecologically fragile areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地震灾区的各级人民政府根据实际条件，因地制宜，为灾区群众安排临时住所。临时住所可以采用帐篷、篷布房，有条件的也可以采用简易住房、活动板房。安排临时住所确实存在困难的，可以将学校操场和经安全鉴定的体育场馆等作为临时避难场所。  
国家鼓励地震灾区农村居民自行筹建符合安全要求的临时住所，并予以补助。具体办法由省级人民政府制定。

**Article 9** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall, based on actual conditions and local circumstances, arrange temporary shelters for the affected people. Temporary shelters may be tents or canvas houses, and where conditions permit, simple houses or prefabricated houses may also be used. Where there are genuine difficulties in arranging temporary shelters, school playgrounds and sports venues that have passed safety expert evaluation may be used as temporary避难 places.  
The State encourages rural residents in earthquake-stricken areas to自行 build temporary shelters that meet safety requirements, and shall provide subsidies. Specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　用于过渡性安置的物资应当保证质量安全。生产单位应当确保帐篷、篷布房的产品质量。建设单位、生产单位应当采用质量合格的建筑材料，确保简易住房、活动板房的安全质量和抗震性能。

**Article 10** The quality and safety of materials used for transitional resettlement shall be guaranteed. The production units shall ensure the product quality of tents and canvas houses. The construction units and production units shall use building materials of qualified quality to ensure the safety quality and seismic performance of simple houses and prefabricated houses.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　过渡性安置地点应当配套建设水、电、道路等基础设施，并按比例配备学校、医疗点、集中供水点、公共卫生间、垃圾收集点、日常用品供应点、少数民族特需品供应点以及必要的文化宣传设施等配套公共服务设施，确保受灾群众的基本生活需要。  
过渡性安置地点的规模应当适度，并安装必要的防雷设施和预留必要的消防应急通道，配备相应的消防设施，防范火灾和雷击灾害发生。

**Article 11** Sites for transitional resettlement shall be equipped with supporting infrastructure such as water, electricity and roads, and shall be provided, in proportion, with supporting public service facilities such as schools, medical stations, centralized water supply points, public toilets, garbage collection points, daily necessities supply points, supply points for special needs of ethnic minorities, and necessary cultural and publicity facilities, so as to ensure the basic living needs of the affected people.  
The scale of transitional resettlement sites shall be moderate, and necessary lightning protection facilities shall be installed, necessary fire emergency passages shall be reserved, and corresponding fire-fighting facilities shall be provided to prevent the occurrence of fires and lightning strikes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　临时住所应当具备防火、防风、防雨等功能。

**Article 12** Temporary shelters shall have functions such as fire prevention, wind prevention and rain prevention.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　活动板房应当优先用于重灾区和需要异地安置的受灾群众，倒塌房屋在短期内难以恢复重建的重灾户特别是遇难者家庭、孕妇、婴幼儿、孤儿、孤老、残疾人员以及学校、医疗点等公共服务设施。

**Article 13** Prefabricated houses shall be given priority for use in severely affected areas and for affected people who need to be resettled in other places, for severely affected households whose collapsed houses are difficult to be rehabilitated and reconstructed in the short term, especially families of the deceased, pregnant women, infants, orphans, the elderly living alone, persons with disabilities, as well as public service facilities such as schools and medical stations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　临时住所、过渡性安置资金和物资的分配和使用，应当公开透明，定期公布，接受有关部门和社会监督。具体办法由省级人民政府制定。

**Article 14** The distribution and use of temporary shelters, transitional resettlement funds and materials shall be open and transparent, published regularly, and subject to the supervision of relevant departments and the public. Specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　过渡性安置用地按临时用地安排，可以先行使用，事后再依法办理有关用地手续；到期未转为永久性用地的，应当复垦后交还原土地使用者。

**Article 15** Land for transitional resettlement shall be arranged as temporary land, which may be used first, and the relevant land use procedures shall be handled afterwards according to law; if such land is not converted into permanent land upon expiry, it shall be reclaimed and returned to the original land user.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　过渡性安置地点所在地的县级人民政府，应当组织有关部门加强次生灾害、饮用水水质、食品卫生、疫情的监测和流行病学调查以及环境卫生整治。使用的消毒剂、清洗剂应当符合环境保护要求，避免对土壤、水资源、环境等造成污染。  
过渡性安置地点所在地的公安机关，应当加强治安管理，及时惩处违法行为，维护正常的社会秩序。  
受灾群众应当在过渡性安置地点所在地的县、乡(镇)人民政府组织下，建立治安、消防联队，开展治安、消防巡查等自防自救工作。

**Article 16** The people's government at the county level where the transitional resettlement site is located shall organize relevant departments to strengthen monitoring of secondary disasters, drinking water quality, food hygiene, epidemics and epidemiological investigation, as well as environmental sanitation improvement. The disinfectants and cleaning agents used shall meet environmental protection requirements to avoid polluting soil, water resources and the environment.  
The public security organ where the transitional resettlement site is located shall strengthen public security administration and promptly punish illegal acts to maintain normal social order.  
The affected people shall, under the organization of the people's government at the county or township (town) level where the transitional resettlement site is located, establish joint public security and fire-fighting teams to carry out self-prevention and self-rescue work such as public security and fire-fighting patrols.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地震灾区的各级人民政府，应当组织受灾群众和企业开展生产自救，积极恢复生产，并做好受灾群众的心理援助工作。

**Article 17** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall organize the affected people and enterprises to carry out production self-rescue, actively restore production, and do a good job in providing psychological assistance to the affected people.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地震灾区的各级人民政府及政府农业行政主管部门应当及时组织修复毁损的农业生产设施，开展抢种抢收，提供农业生产技术指导，保障农业投入品和农业机械设备的供应。

**Article 18** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas and their administrative departments of agriculture shall promptly organize the repair of damaged agricultural production facilities, carry out rush planting and rush harvesting, provide technical guidance for agricultural production, and ensure the supply of agricultural inputs and agricultural machinery equipment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地震灾区的各级人民政府及政府有关部门应当优先组织供电、供水、供气等企业恢复生产，并对大型骨干企业恢复生产提供支持，为全面恢复工业、服务业生产经营提供条件。

**Article 19** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas and their relevant departments shall give priority to organizing enterprises such as power supply, water supply and gas supply to resume production, and provide support for large backbone enterprises to resume production, so as to create conditions for the comprehensive resumption of industrial and service business operations.

### 第三章　调查评估

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院有关部门应当组织开展地震灾害调查评估工作，为编制地震灾后恢复重建规划提供依据。

**Article 20** The relevant departments of the State Council shall organize and carry out investigation and assessment of earthquake disasters to provide a basis for the formulation of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地震灾害调查评估应当包括下列事项：  
(一)城镇和乡村受损程度和数量；  
(二)人员伤亡情况，房屋破坏程度和数量，基础设施、公共服务设施、工农业生产设施与商贸流通设施受损程度和数量，农用地毁损程度和数量等；  
(三)需要安置人口的数量，需要救助的伤残人员数量，需要帮助的孤寡老人及未成年人的数量，需要提供的房屋数量，需要恢复重建的基础设施和公共服务设施，需要恢复重建的生产设施，需要整理和复垦的农用地等；  
(四)环境污染、生态损害以及自然和历史文化遗产毁损等情况；  
(五)资源环境承载能力以及地质灾害、地震次生灾害和隐患等情况；  
(六)水文地质、工程地质、环境地质、地形地貌以及河势和水文情势、重大水利水电工程的受影响情况；  
(七)突发公共卫生事件及其隐患；  
(八)编制地震灾后恢复重建规划需要调查评估的其他事项。

**Article 21** The investigation and assessment of earthquake disasters shall include the following:  
(1) the extent and quantity of damage to cities, towns and villages;  
(2) the situation of casualties, the extent and quantity of damage to houses, the extent and quantity of damage to infrastructure, public service facilities, industrial and agricultural production facilities and commercial and trading circulation facilities, and the extent and quantity of damage to agricultural land;  
(3) the number of people to be resettled, the number of injured or disabled people in need of assistance, the number of elderly people living alone and minors in need of help, the number of houses to be provided, the infrastructure and public service facilities to be rehabilitated and reconstructed, the production facilities to be rehabilitated and reconstructed, and the agricultural land to be consolidated and reclaimed;  
(4) environmental pollution, ecological damage and damage to natural and historical and cultural heritage;  
(5) the carrying capacity of resources and environment, as well as geological disasters, secondary earthquake disasters and potential hazards;  
(6) the affected conditions of hydrogeology, engineering geology, environmental geology, topography and geomorphology, river regime and hydrological regime, and major water conservancy and hydropower projects;  
(7) public health emergencies and their potential risks;  
(8) other matters requiring investigation and assessment for the formulation of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府应当依据各自职责分工组织有关部门和专家，对毁损严重的水利、道路、电力等基础设施，学校等公共服务设施以及其他建设工程进行工程质量和抗震性能鉴定，保存有关资料和样本，并开展地震活动对相关建设工程破坏机理的调查评估，为改进建设工程抗震设计规范和工程建设标准，采取抗震设防措施提供科学依据。

**Article 22** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize relevant departments and experts to carry out expert evaluation on the engineering quality and seismic performance of severely damaged water conservancy, road, power and other infrastructure, schools and other public service facilities, and other construction projects, preserve relevant materials and samples, and conduct investigation and assessment on the damage mechanism of relevant construction projects caused by seismic activities, so as to provide scientific basis for improving seismic design codes for construction projects and engineering construction standards and for adopting seismic fortification measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　地震灾害调查评估应当采用全面调查评估、实地调查评估、综合评估的方法，确保数据资料的真实性、准确性、及时性和评估结论的可靠性。  
地震部门、地震监测台网应当收集、保存地震前、地震中、地震后的所有资料和信息，并建立完整的档案。  
开展地震灾害调查评估工作，应当遵守国家法律、法规以及有关技术标准和要求。

**Article 23** The investigation and assessment of earthquake disasters shall adopt methods of comprehensive investigation and assessment, field investigation and assessment, and comprehensive evaluation, so as to ensure the authenticity, accuracy, timeliness of data and materials and the reliability of assessment conclusions.  
The earthquake departments and seismic monitoring networks shall collect and preserve all data and information before, during and after the earthquake, and establish complete archives.  
The conduct of earthquake disaster investigation and assessment work shall comply with national laws, regulations and relevant technical standards and requirements.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地震灾害调查评估报告应当及时上报国务院。

**Article 24** The investigation and assessment report on earthquake disasters shall be promptly submitted to the State Council.

### 第四章　恢复重建规划

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院发展改革部门会同国务院有关部门与地震灾区的省级人民政府共同组织编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
地震灾后恢复重建规划应当包括地震灾后恢复重建总体规划和城镇体系规划、农村建设规划、城乡住房建设规划、基础设施建设规划、公共服务设施建设规划、生产力布局和产业调整规划、市场服务体系规划、防灾减灾和生态修复规划、土地利用规划等专项规划。

**Article 25** The development and reform department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces in the earthquake-stricken areas, jointly organize the formulation of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans, which shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans shall include the overall plan for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction and special plans such as the urban system plan, rural construction plan, urban and rural housing construction plan, infrastructure construction plan, public service facility construction plan, productivity layout and industrial adjustment plan, market service system plan, disaster prevention, mitigation and ecological restoration plan, and land use plan.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　地震灾区的市、县人民政府应当在省级人民政府的指导下，组织编制本行政区域的地震灾后恢复重建实施规划。

**Article 26** The people's governments of cities and counties in the earthquake-stricken areas shall, under the guidance of the people's governments of provinces, organize the formulation of implementation plans for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，应当全面贯彻落实科学发展观，坚持以人为本，优先恢复重建受灾群众基本生活和公共服务设施；尊重科学、尊重自然，充分考虑资源环境承载能力；统筹兼顾，与推进工业化、城镇化、新农村建设、主体功能区建设、产业结构优化升级相结合，并坚持统一部署、分工负责，区分缓急、突出重点，相互衔接、上下协调，规范有序、依法推进的原则。  
编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，应当遵守法律、法规和国家有关标准。

**Article 27** In formulating post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans, the Scientific Outlook on Development shall be fully implemented, people-oriented approach shall be adhered to, priority shall be given to restoring the basic livelihood of the affected people and public service facilities; science and nature shall be respected, and the carrying capacity of resources and environment shall be fully considered; overall planning shall be made in combination with promoting industrialization, urbanization, new countryside construction, development priority zone construction, and industrial structure optimization and upgrading; and the principles of unified deployment with division of responsibilities, prioritization with highlighting key points, mutual coordination with上下 coordination, and standardized, orderly and lawful promotion shall be adhered to.  
The formulation of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans shall comply with laws, regulations and relevant national standards.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地震灾后调查评估获得的地质、勘察、测绘、水文、环境等基础资料，应当作为编制地震灾后恢复重建规划的依据。  
地震工作主管部门应当根据地震地质、地震活动特性的研究成果和地震烈度分布情况，对地震动参数区划图进行复核，为编制地震灾后恢复重建规划和进行建设工程抗震设防提供依据。

**Article 28** The basic data such as geology, survey, mapping, hydrology and environment obtained through post-earthquake investigation and assessment shall serve as the basis for formulating post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans.  
The competent department of earthquake work shall, based on research results of seismic geology and seismic activity characteristics and the distribution of seismic intensity, review the seismic ground motion parameter zoning map to provide a basis for formulating post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plans and for carrying out seismic fortification of construction projects.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地震灾后恢复重建规划应当包括地震灾害状况和区域分析，恢复重建原则和目标，恢复重建区域范围，恢复重建空间布局，恢复重建任务和政策措施，有科学价值的地震遗址、遗迹保护，受损文物和具有历史价值与少数民族特色的建筑物、构筑物的修复，实施步骤和阶段等主要内容。  
地震灾后恢复重建规划应当重点对城镇和乡村的布局、住房建设、基础设施建设、公共服务设施建设、农业生产设施建设、工业生产设施建设、防灾减灾和生态环境以及自然资源和历史文化遗产保护、土地整理和复垦等做出安排。

**Article 29** The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan shall include the main contents such as earthquake disaster situation and regional analysis, rehabilitation and reconstruction principles and objectives, scope of rehabilitation and reconstruction area, spatial layout of rehabilitation and reconstruction, rehabilitation and reconstruction tasks and policy measures, protection of scientifically valuable earthquake ruins and relics, restoration of damaged cultural relics and buildings and structures with historical value and ethnic minority characteristics, and implementation steps and phases.  
The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan shall focus on making arrangements for the layout of cities, towns and villages, housing construction, infrastructure construction, public service facility construction, agricultural production facility construction, industrial production facility construction, disaster prevention, mitigation and ecological environment, as well as protection of natural resources and historical and cultural heritage, land consolidation and reclamation, etc.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　地震灾区的中央所属企业生产、生活等设施的恢复重建，纳入地震灾后恢复重建规划统筹安排。

**Article 30** The rehabilitation and reconstruction of production and living facilities of enterprises under the central government in the earthquake-stricken areas shall be incorporated into the overall arrangements of the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，应当吸收有关部门、专家参加，并充分听取地震灾区受灾群众的意见；重大事项应当组织有关方面专家进行专题论证。

**Article 31** In formulating the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan, relevant departments and experts shall be invited to participate, and the opinions of the affected people in the earthquake-stricken areas shall be fully solicited; on major matters, experts from relevant fields shall be organized to conduct special论证.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地震灾区内的城镇和乡村完全毁损，存在重大安全隐患或者人口规模超出环境承载能力，需要异地新建的，重新选址时，应当避开地震活动断层或者生态脆弱和可能发生洪灾、山体滑坡、崩塌、泥石流、地面塌陷等灾害的区域以及传染病自然疫源地。  
地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门、专家对新址进行论证，听取公众意见，并报上一级人民政府批准。

**Article 32** Where cities, towns and villages within the earthquake-stricken areas are completely destroyed, have major safety hazards or have a population scale exceeding the environmental carrying capacity, and need to be newly built in other places, when reselecting the site, earthquake active faults or ecologically fragile areas and areas prone to floods, landslides, collapses, debris flows, ground subsidence and other disasters, as well as natural foci of infectious diseases, shall be avoided.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken areas shall organize relevant departments and experts to conduct论证 on the new site, solicit opinions from the public, and submit it to the people's government at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院批准的地震灾后恢复重建规划，是地震灾后恢复重建的基本依据，应当及时公布。任何单位和个人都应当遵守经依法批准公布的地震灾后恢复重建规划，服从规划管理。  
地震灾后恢复重建规划所依据的基础资料修改、其他客观条件发生变化需要修改的，或者因恢复重建工作需要修改的，由规划组织编制机关提出修改意见，报国务院批准。

**Article 33** The post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan approved by the State Council is the basic basis for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction and shall be published promptly. Any unit or individual shall abide by the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan approved and published according to law and comply with the planning management.  
Where the basic data on which the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan is based are modified, other objective conditions change and require modification, or modification is necessary due to the needs of rehabilitation and reconstruction work, the organ that organized the formulation of the plan shall propose modification opinions and submit them to the State Council for approval.

### 第五章　恢复重建的实施

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地震灾区的省级人民政府，应当根据地震灾后恢复重建规划和当地经济社会发展水平，有计划、分步骤地组织实施地震灾后恢复重建。  
国务院有关部门应当支持、协助、指导地震灾区的恢复重建工作。  
城镇恢复重建应当充分考虑原有城市、镇总体规划，注重体现原有少数民族建筑风格，合理确定城镇的建设规模和标准，并达到抗震设防要求。

**Article 34** The people's governments of provinces in the earthquake-stricken areas shall, in accordance with the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan and the local economic and social development level, organize the implementation of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction in a planned and step-by-step manner.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall support, assist and guide the rehabilitation and reconstruction work in the earthquake-stricken areas.  
The rehabilitation and reconstruction of cities and towns shall fully take into account the original master plans of the cities and towns, pay attention to reflecting the original architectural styles of ethnic minorities, reasonably determine the construction scale and standards of the cities and towns, and meet the requirements for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　发展改革部门具体负责灾后恢复重建的统筹规划、政策建议、投资计划、组织协调和重大建设项目的安排。  
财政部门会同有关部门负责提出资金安排和政策建议，并具体负责灾后恢复重建财政资金的拨付和管理。  
交通运输、水利、铁路、电力、通信、广播影视等部门按照职责分工，具体组织实施有关基础设施的灾后恢复重建。  
建设部门具体组织实施房屋和市政公用设施的灾后恢复重建。  
民政部门具体组织实施受灾群众的临时基本生活保障、生活困难救助、农村毁损房屋恢复重建补助、社会福利设施恢复重建以及对孤儿、孤老、残疾人员的安置、补助、心理援助和伤残康复。  
教育、科技、文化、卫生、广播影视、体育、人力资源社会保障、商务、工商等部门按照职责分工，具体组织实施公共服务设施的灾后恢复重建、卫生防疫和医疗救治、就业服务和社会保障、重要生活必需品供应以及维护市场秩序。高等学校、科学技术研究开发机构应当加强对有关问题的专题研究，为地震灾后恢复重建提供科学技术支撑。  
农业、林业、水利、国土资源、商务、工业等部门按照职责分工，具体组织实施动物疫情监测、农业生产设施恢复重建和农业生产条件恢复，地震灾后恢复重建用地安排、土地整理和复垦、地质灾害防治，商贸流通、工业生产设施等恢复重建。  
环保、林业、民政、水利、科技、安全生产、地震、气象、测绘等部门按照职责分工，具体负责生态环境保护和防灾减灾、安全生产的技术保障及公共服务设施恢复重建。  
中国人民银行和银行、证券、保险监督管理机构按照职责分工，具体负责地震灾后恢复重建金融支持和服务政策的制定与落实。  
公安部门具体负责维护和稳定地震灾区社会秩序。  
海关、出入境检验检疫部门按照职责分工，依法组织实施进口恢复重建物资、境外捐赠物资的验放、检验检疫。  
外交部会同有关部门按照职责分工，协调开展地震灾后恢复重建的涉外工作。

**Article 35** The development and reform department shall be specifically responsible for the overall planning, policy proposals, investment plans, organization and coordination of post-disaster rehabilitation and reconstruction, and the arrangement of major construction projects.  
The finance department shall, together with relevant departments, be responsible for proposing fund arrangements and policy proposals, and shall be specifically responsible for the allocation and management of post-disaster rehabilitation and reconstruction financial funds.  
The departments of transport, water resources, railways, electric power, communications, radio, film and television shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, specifically organize the implementation of post-disaster rehabilitation and reconstruction of relevant infrastructure.  
The construction department shall specifically organize the implementation of post-disaster rehabilitation and reconstruction of houses and municipal public utilities.  
The civil affairs department shall specifically organize the implementation of temporary basic livelihood guarantees for the affected people, assistance for living difficulties, subsidies for the rehabilitation and reconstruction of damaged rural houses, rehabilitation and reconstruction of social welfare facilities, as well as the resettlement, subsidies, psychological assistance and rehabilitation of orphans, the elderly living alone, and persons with disabilities.  
The departments of education, science and technology, culture, health, radio, film and television, sports, human resources and social security, commerce, industry and commerce shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, specifically organize the implementation of post-disaster rehabilitation and reconstruction of public service facilities, epidemic prevention and medical treatment, employment services and social security, supply of important daily necessities, and maintenance of market order. Institutions of higher learning and scientific research and development institutions shall strengthen special research on relevant issues to provide scientific and technological support for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction.  
The departments of agriculture, forestry, water resources, land and resources, commerce, and industry shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, specifically organize the implementation of animal epidemic monitoring, rehabilitation and reconstruction of agricultural production facilities and restoration of agricultural production conditions, land use arrangements for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction, land consolidation and reclamation, geological disaster prevention and control, and rehabilitation and reconstruction of commercial and trading circulation facilities and industrial production facilities.  
The departments of environmental protection, forestry, civil affairs, water resources, science and technology, work safety, earthquake, meteorology, and surveying and mapping shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be specifically responsible for ecological environment protection and disaster prevention and mitigation, technical support for work safety, and rehabilitation and reconstruction of public service facilities.  
The People's Bank of China and the regulatory authorities for banking, securities and insurance shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be specifically responsible for the formulation and implementation of financial support and service policies for post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction.  
The public security department shall be specifically responsible for maintaining and stabilizing social order in the earthquake-stricken areas.  
The customs and entry-exit inspection and quarantine departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize the verification, release, inspection and quarantine of imported rehabilitation and reconstruction materials and donated materials from overseas according to law.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, together with relevant departments in accordance with their respective functions and duties, coordinate the foreign-related work of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院地震工作主管部门应当会同文物等有关部门组织专家对地震废墟进行现场调查，对具有典型性、代表性、科学价值和纪念意义的地震遗址、遗迹划定范围，建立地震遗址博物馆。

**Article 36** The competent department of earthquake work of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments such as cultural relics, organize experts to conduct on-site investigation of the earthquake ruins, delineate the scope of earthquake ruins and relics that are typical, representative, of scientific value and commemorative significance, and establish earthquake ruins museums.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地震灾区的省级人民政府应当组织民族事务、建设、环保、地震、文物等部门和专家，根据地震灾害调查评估结果，制定清理保护方案，明确地震遗址、遗迹和文物保护单位以及具有历史价值与少数民族特色的建筑物、构筑物等保护对象及其区域范围，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 37** The people's governments of provinces in the earthquake-stricken areas shall organize departments of ethnic affairs, construction, environmental protection, earthquake, cultural relics and experts to, based on the results of earthquake disaster investigation and assessment, formulate a cleanup and protection plan, specify the protection objects such as earthquake ruins and relics, cultural heritage protection units, and buildings and structures with historical value and ethnic minority characteristics, and their regional scope, and submit it to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地震灾害现场的清理保护，应当在确定无人类生命迹象和无重大疫情的情况下，按照统一组织、科学规划、统筹兼顾、注重保护的原则实施。发现地震灾害现场有人类生命迹象的，应当立即实施救援。

**Article 38** The cleanup and protection of the earthquake disaster site shall be carried out under the principles of unified organization, scientific planning, overall consideration and emphasis on protection, after confirming that there are no signs of human life and no major epidemic. If signs of human life are found at the earthquake disaster site, rescue shall be carried out immediately.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对清理保护方案确定的地震遗址、遗迹应当在保护范围内采取有效措施进行保护，抢救、收集具有科学研究价值的技术资料和实物资料，并在不影响整体风貌的情况下，对有倒塌危险的建筑物、构筑物进行必要的加固，对废墟中有毒、有害的废弃物、残留物进行必要的清理。  
对文物保护单位应当实施原址保护。对尚可保留的不可移动文物和具有历史价值与少数民族特色的建筑物、构筑物以及历史建筑，应当采取加固等保护措施；对无法保留但将来可能恢复重建的，应当收集整理影像资料。  
对馆藏文物、民间收藏文物等可移动文物和非物质文化遗产的物质载体，应当及时抢救、整理、登记，并将清理出的可移动文物和非物质文化遗产的物质载体，运送到安全地点妥善保管。

**Article 39** For earthquake ruins and relics determined in the cleanup and protection plan, effective measures shall be taken within the protection scope to protect them, rescue and collect technical data and physical materials of scientific research value, and carry out necessary reinforcement of buildings and structures in danger of collapse and necessary cleanup of toxic and hazardous wastes and residues in the ruins without affecting the overall appearance.  
For cultural heritage protection units, protection on the original site shall be implemented. For immovable cultural relics that can still be preserved and buildings and structures with historical value and ethnic minority characteristics, as well as historic buildings, protection measures such as reinforcement shall be taken; for those that cannot be preserved but may be rehabilitated and reconstructed in the future, image data shall be collected and sorted.  
For movable cultural relics such as museum collections and folk collections, as well as material carriers of intangible cultural heritage, timely rescue, sorting and registration shall be carried out, and the cleaned movable cultural relics and material carriers of intangible cultural heritage shall be transported to safe places for proper storage.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对地震灾害现场的清理，应当按照清理保护方案分区、分类进行。清理出的遇难者遗体处理，应当尊重当地少数民族传统习惯；清理出的财物，应当对其种类、特征、数量、清理时间、地点等情况详细登记造册，妥善保存。有条件的，可以通知遇难者家属和所有权人到场。  
对清理出的废弃危险化学品和其他废弃物、残留物，应当实行分类处理，并遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 40** The cleanup of the earthquake disaster site shall be carried out by zones and categories in accordance with the cleanup and protection plan. The disposal of the remains of the deceased that are cleaned up shall respect the traditional customs of the local ethnic minorities; the property cleaned up shall be registered in detail with information such as its type, characteristics, quantity, cleanup time and location, and shall be properly kept. Where conditions permit, the family members of the deceased and the owners may be notified to be present.  
The废弃 dangerous chemicals and other wastes and residues cleaned up shall be subject to classified treatment and comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　地震灾区的各级人民政府应当做好地震灾区的动物疫情防控工作。对清理出的动物尸体，应当采取消毒、销毁等无害化处理措施，防止重大动物疫情的发生。

**Article 41** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall do a good job in animal epidemic prevention and control in the earthquake-stricken areas. For the animal carcasses cleaned up, harmless treatment measures such as disinfection and destruction shall be taken to prevent the occurrence of major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对现场清理过程中拆除或者拆解的废旧建筑材料以及过渡安置期结束后不再使用的活动板房等，能回收利用的，应当回收利用。

**Article 42** waste building materials demolished or dismantled during on-site cleanup and prefabricated houses that are no longer used after the transitional resettlement period shall be recycled and reused if possible.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　地震灾后恢复重建，应当统筹安排交通、铁路、通信、供水、供电、住房、学校、医院、社会福利、文化、广播电视、金融等基础设施和公共服务设施建设。  
城镇的地震灾后恢复重建，应当统筹安排市政公用设施、公共服务设施和其他设施，合理确定建设规模和时序。  
乡村的地震灾后恢复重建，应当尊重农民意愿，发挥村民自治组织的作用，以群众自建为主，政府补助、社会帮扶、对口支援，因地制宜，节约和集约利用土地，保护耕地。  
地震灾区的县级人民政府应当组织有关部门对村民住宅建设的选址予以指导，并提供能够符合当地实际的多种村民住宅设计图，供村民选择。村民住宅应当达到抗震设防要求，体现原有地方特色、民族特色和传统风貌。

**Article 43** In post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction, the construction of infrastructure and public service facilities such as transport, railways, communications, water supply, power supply, housing, schools, hospitals, social welfare, culture, radio and television, and finance shall be arranged in a coordinated manner.  
In the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction of cities and towns, the construction of municipal public utilities, public service facilities and other facilities shall be arranged in a coordinated manner, and the construction scale and sequence shall be reasonably determined.  
In the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction of villages, the wishes of farmers shall be respected, the role of villagers' self-governing organizations shall be brought into play, with self-construction by the masses as the mainstay, supplemented by government subsidies, social assistance and counterpart support, measures shall be adapted to local conditions, land shall be used economically and intensively, and farmland shall be protected.  
The people's government at the county level in the earthquake-stricken area shall organize relevant departments to provide guidance on the site selection of villagers' housing construction, and provide a variety of villagers' housing design drawings that conform to the local actual conditions for villagers to choose. Villagers' housing shall meet the requirements for seismic fortification and reflect the original local characteristics, ethnic characteristics and traditional style.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　经批准的地震灾后恢复重建项目可以根据土地利用总体规划，先行安排使用土地，实行边建设边报批，并按照有关规定办理用地手续。对因地震灾害毁损的耕地、农田道路、抢险救灾应急用地、过渡性安置用地、废弃的城镇、村庄和工矿旧址，应当依法进行土地整理和复垦，并治理地质灾害。

**Article 44** Approved post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction projects may, in accordance with the overall plan for land use, arrange for the use of land in advance, carry out construction while reporting for approval, and go through the land use procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions. For cultivated land, farmland roads, emergency land for rescue and disaster relief, transitional resettlement land, abandoned cities, towns, villages and former industrial and mining sites damaged by the earthquake disaster, land consolidation and reclamation shall be carried out according to law, and geological disasters shall be controlled.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院有关部门应当组织对地震灾区地震动参数、抗震设防要求、工程建设标准进行复审；确有必要修订的，应当及时组织修订。  
地震灾区的抗震设防要求和有关工程建设标准应当根据修订后的地震灾区地震动参数，进行相应修订。

**Article 45** The relevant departments of the State Council shall organize a review of the seismic ground motion parameters, seismic fortification requirements and engineering construction standards in the earthquake-stricken areas; if revision is truly necessary, it shall be organized promptly.  
The seismic fortification requirements and relevant engineering construction standards in the earthquake-stricken areas shall be revised correspondingly in accordance with the revised seismic ground motion parameters of the earthquake-stricken areas.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对地震灾区尚可使用的建筑物、构筑物和设施，应当按照地震灾区的抗震设防要求进行抗震性能鉴定，并根据鉴定结果采取加固、改造等措施。

**Article 46** For buildings, structures and facilities that are still usable in the earthquake-stricken areas, seismic performance evaluation shall be carried out in accordance with the seismic fortification requirements of the earthquake-stricken areas, and measures such as reinforcement and retrofitting shall be taken based on the evaluation results.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　地震灾后重建工程的选址，应当符合地震灾后恢复重建规划和抗震设防、防灾减灾要求，避开地震活动断层、生态脆弱地区、可能发生重大灾害的区域和传染病自然疫源地。

**Article 47** The site selection of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction projects shall comply with the post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction plan and the requirements for seismic fortification and disaster prevention and mitigation, and shall avoid earthquake active faults, ecologically fragile areas, areas prone to major disasters and natural foci of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　设计单位应当严格按照抗震设防要求和工程建设强制性标准进行抗震设计，并对抗震设计的质量以及出具的施工图的准确性负责。  
施工单位应当按照施工图设计文件和工程建设强制性标准进行施工，并对施工质量负责。  
建设单位、施工单位应当选用施工图设计文件和国家有关标准规定的材料、构配件和设备。  
工程监理单位应当依照施工图设计文件和工程建设强制性标准实施监理，并对施工质量承担监理责任。

**Article 48** Design units shall strictly carry out seismic design in accordance with the seismic fortification requirements and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall be responsible for the quality of seismic design and the accuracy of the construction drawings they issue.  
Construction units shall carry out construction in accordance with the construction drawing design documents and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall be responsible for the construction quality.  
Construction employers and construction units shall select materials, components and equipment specified in the construction drawing design documents and relevant national standards.  
Project supervision units shall carry out supervision in accordance with the construction drawing design documents and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall assume supervision responsibility for the construction quality.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　按照国家有关规定对地震灾后恢复重建工程进行竣工验收时，应当重点对工程是否符合抗震设防要求进行查验；对不符合抗震设防要求的，不得出具竣工验收报告。

**Article 49** When carrying out completion acceptance of post-earthquake rehabilitation and reconstruction projects in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, key inspection shall be conducted on whether the project meets the seismic fortification requirements; if it does not meet the seismic fortification requirements, the completion acceptance report shall not be issued.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对学校、医院、体育场馆、博物馆、文化馆、图书馆、影剧院、商场、交通枢纽等人员密集的公共服务设施，应当按照高于当地房屋建筑的抗震设防要求进行设计，增强抗震设防能力。

**Article 50** For public service facilities with high population density such as schools, hospitals, sports venues, museums, cultural centers, libraries, cinemas and theaters, shopping malls, and transport hubs, design shall be carried out in accordance with seismic fortification requirements higher than those for local housing buildings to enhance the seismic fortification capacity.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　地震灾后恢复重建中涉及文物保护、自然保护区、野生动植物保护和地震遗址、遗迹保护的，依照国家有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 51** Where the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction involves the protection of cultural relics, nature reserves, wildlife and earthquake ruins, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant national laws and regulations.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地震灾后恢复重建中，货物、工程和服务的政府采购活动，应当严格依照《中华人民共和国政府采购法》的有关规定执行。

**Article 52** In the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, government procurement of goods, projects and services shall be strictly carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Government Procurement Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第六章　资金筹集与政策扶持

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府应当通过政府投入、对口支援、社会募集、市场运作等方式筹集地震灾后恢复重建资金。

**Article 53** The people's governments at or above the county level shall raise funds for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction by means of government input, counterpart support, social fundraising and market operation.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家根据地震的强度和损失的实际情况等因素建立地震灾后恢复重建基金，专项用于地震灾后恢复重建。  
地震灾后恢复重建基金由预算资金以及其他财政资金构成。  
地震灾后恢复重建基金筹集使用管理办法，由国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 54** The State shall establish a post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction fund based on factors such as the intensity of the earthquake and the actual circumstances of losses, which shall be used exclusively for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction.  
The post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction fund shall consist of budgetary funds and other fiscal funds.  
The measures for the administration of the raising and use of the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction fund shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织为地震灾后恢复重建捐赠款物。捐赠款物的使用应当尊重捐赠人的意愿，并纳入地震灾后恢复重建规划。  
县级以上人民政府及其部门作为受赠人的，应当将捐赠款物用于地震灾后恢复重建。公益性社会团体、公益性非营利的事业单位作为受赠人的，应当公开接受捐赠的情况和受赠财产的使用、管理情况，接受政府有关部门、捐赠人和社会的监督。  
县级以上人民政府及其部门、公益性社会团体、公益性非营利的事业单位接受捐赠的，应当向捐赠人出具由省级以上财政部门统一印制的捐赠票据。  
外国政府和国际组织提供的地震灾后恢复重建资金、物资和人员服务以及安排实施的多双边地震灾后恢复重建项目等，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 55** The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to donate money and property for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction. The use of donated money and property shall respect the donors' wishes and be incorporated into the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan.  
Where the people's government at or above the county level or its department acts as a donee, it shall use the donated money and property for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction. Where a public welfare social organization or a public welfare non-profit institution acts as a donee, it shall disclose the circumstances of acceptance and the use and management of the donated property, and shall accept supervision from relevant government departments, donors and the public.  
Where the people's government at or above the county level or its department, a public welfare social organization, or a public welfare non-profit institution accepts donations, it shall issue to the donor a donation receipt uniformly printed by the finance department at or above the provincial level.  
Where foreign governments and international organizations provide funds, materials and personnel services for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, or arrange and implement bilateral or multilateral post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction projects, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法投资地震灾区基础设施和公共服务设施的恢复重建。

**Article 56** The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to invest, according to law, in the recovery and reconstruction of infrastructure and public service facilities in the earthquake-stricken areas.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家对地震灾后恢复重建依法实行税收优惠。具体办法由国务院财政部门、国务院税务部门制定。  
地震灾区灾后恢复重建期间，县级以上地方人民政府依法实施地方税收优惠措施。

**Article 57** The State shall, according to law, grant tax preferences for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council and the tax department of the State Council.  
During the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction period in the earthquake-stricken areas, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, implement local tax preference measures.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　地震灾区的各项行政事业性收费可以适当减免。具体办法由有关主管部门制定。

**Article 58** Administrative and institutional fees in the earthquake-stricken areas may be appropriately reduced or exempted. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent departments concerned.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家向地震灾区的房屋贷款和公共服务设施恢复重建贷款、工业和服务业恢复生产经营贷款、农业恢复生产贷款等提供财政贴息。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同其他有关部门制定。

**Article 59** The State shall provide financial interest subsidies for loans in the earthquake-stricken areas, including loans for housing, loans for the recovery and reconstruction of public service facilities, loans for the resumption of production and operations in industry and service sectors, and loans for the resumption of agricultural production. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家在安排建设资金时，应当优先考虑地震灾区的交通、铁路、能源、农业、水利、通信、金融、市政公用、教育、卫生、文化、广播电视、防灾减灾、环境保护等基础设施和公共服务设施以及关系国家安全的重点工程设施建设。  
测绘、气象、地震、水文等设施因地震遭受破坏的，地震灾区的人民政府应当采取紧急措施，组织力量修复，确保正常运行。

**Article 60** When allocating construction funds, the State shall give priority to the construction of infrastructure and public service facilities in the earthquake-stricken areas, such as transportation, railways, energy, agriculture, water conservancy, communications, finance, municipal utilities, education, health, culture, radio and television, disaster prevention and mitigation, and environmental protection, as well as key project facilities related to national security.  
Where surveying and mapping, meteorological, earthquake, hydrological and other facilities have been damaged by the earthquake, the people's government of the earthquake-stricken area shall take emergency measures and organize forces for repair to ensure normal operation.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　各级人民政府及政府有关部门应当加强对受灾群众的职业技能培训、就业服务和就业援助，鼓励企业、事业单位优先吸纳符合条件的受灾群众就业；可以采取以工代赈的方式组织受灾群众参加地震灾后恢复重建。

**Article 61** The people's governments at all levels and the relevant government departments shall strengthen vocational skill training, employment services and employment assistance for the affected people, and encourage enterprises and institutions to give priority to employing eligible affected people; they may organize the affected people to participate in post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction through work-relief programs.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　地震灾区接受义务教育的学生，其监护人因地震灾害死亡或者丧失劳动能力或者因地震灾害导致家庭经济困难的，由国家给予生活费补贴；地震灾区的其他学生，其父母因地震灾害死亡或者丧失劳动能力或者因地震灾害导致家庭经济困难的，在同等情况下其所在的学校可以优先将其纳入国家资助政策体系予以资助。

**Article 62** For students receiving compulsory education in the earthquake-stricken areas, if their guardians have died or lost the ability to work due to the earthquake disaster, or if their families have fallen into financial difficulties due to the earthquake disaster, the State shall grant living expense subsidies to them; for other students in the earthquake-stricken areas, if their parents have died or lost the ability to work due to the earthquake disaster, or if their families have fallen into financial difficulties due to the earthquake disaster, the school where they study may give them priority in being included in the national financial aid policy system for assistance under the same circumstances.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　非地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照国家和当地人民政府的安排，采取对口支援等多种形式支持地震灾区恢复重建。  
国家鼓励非地震灾区的企业、事业单位通过援建等多种形式支持地震灾区恢复重建。

**Article 63** The local people's governments at or above the county level in non-earthquake-stricken areas and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the arrangements of the State and the local people's governments, support the recovery and reconstruction of the earthquake-stricken areas through various forms such as counterpart support.  
The State encourages enterprises and institutions in non-earthquake-stricken areas to support the recovery and reconstruction of the earthquake-stricken areas through various forms such as aid construction.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对地震灾后恢复重建中需要办理行政审批手续的事项，有审批权的人民政府及有关部门应当按照方便群众、简化手续、提高效率的原则，依法及时予以办理。

**Article 64** For matters requiring administrative examination and approval procedures in post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, the people's governments and relevant departments with examination and approval authority shall handle them promptly according to law, following the principles of convenience for the people, simplification of procedures and improvement of efficiency.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对下级人民政府地震灾后恢复重建工作的监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建建设工程质量和安全以及产品质量的监督。

**Article 65** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction work of the people's governments at lower levels.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the quality and safety of construction projects for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction as well as over product quality.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　地震灾区的各级人民政府在确定地震灾后恢复重建资金和物资分配方案、房屋分配方案前，应当先行调查，经民主评议后予以公布。

**Article 66** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall, before determining the distribution plans for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds and materials and the housing distribution plan, first conduct investigations and, after democratic appraisal, publish them.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　地震灾区的各级人民政府应当定期公布地震灾后恢复重建资金和物资的来源、数量、发放和使用情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 67** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas shall regularly publish the sources, amounts, distribution and use of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds and materials, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　财政部门应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建资金的拨付和使用的监督管理。  
发展改革、建设、交通运输、水利、电力、铁路、工业和信息化等部门按照职责分工，组织开展对地震灾后恢复重建项目的监督检查。国务院发展改革部门组织开展对地震灾后恢复重建的重大建设项目的稽察。

**Article 68** The finance department shall strengthen supervision and management over the allocation and use of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds.  
The departments of development and reform, construction, transportation, water conservancy, electric power, railways, industry and information technology, among others, shall organize and carry out supervision and inspection of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction projects according to their respective functions and duties. The development and reform department of the State Council shall organize and conduct inspections of major construction projects for post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　审计机关应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建资金和物资的筹集、分配、拨付、使用和效果的全过程跟踪审计，定期公布地震灾后恢复重建资金和物资使用情况，并在审计结束后公布最终的审计结果。

**Article 69** The audit authorities shall strengthen the whole-process follow-up audit of the raising, allocation, distribution, use and effectiveness of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds and materials, regularly publish the use of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds and materials, and publish the final audit results after the audit is completed.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　地震灾区的各级人民政府及有关部门和单位，应当对建设项目以及地震灾后恢复重建资金和物资的筹集、分配、拨付、使用情况登记造册，建立、健全档案，并在建设工程竣工验收和地震灾后恢复重建结束后，及时向建设主管部门或者其他有关部门移交档案。

**Article 70** The people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken areas and the relevant departments and units shall register and record the construction projects and the raising, allocation, distribution and use of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds and materials, establish and improve archives, and after the completion acceptance of construction projects and the conclusion of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, promptly transfer the archives to the competent department of construction or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　监察机关应当加强对参与地震灾后恢复重建工作的国家机关和法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织及其工作人员的监察。

**Article 71** The supervisory organs shall strengthen supervision over the state organs participating in the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction work, as well as over the organizations authorized by laws and regulations to administer public affairs and their staff members.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　任何单位和个人对地震灾后恢复重建中的违法违纪行为，都有权进行举报。  
接到举报的人民政府或者有关部门应当立即调查，依法处理，并为举报人保密。实名举报的，应当将处理结果反馈举报人。社会影响较大的违法违纪行为，处理结果应当向社会公布。

**Article 72** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report illegal or disciplinary violations in post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction.  
The people's government or relevant department that receives the report shall immediately investigate, handle the matter according to law, and keep the confidentiality for the informant. Where the report is made with the real name, the handling result shall be fed back to the informant. For illegal or disciplinary violations with significant social impact, the handling result shall be published to the public.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有关地方人民政府及政府部门侵占、截留、挪用地震灾后恢复重建资金或者物资的，由财政部门、审计机关在各自职责范围内，责令改正，追回被侵占、截留、挪用的地震灾后恢复重建资金或者物资，没收违法所得，对单位给予警告或者通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由任免机关或者监察机关按照人事管理权限依法给予降级、撤职直至开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** Where a relevant local people's government or government department embezzles, withholds or misappropriates post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds or materials, the finance department and the audit authority shall, within their respective functions and duties, order correction, recover the embezzled, withheld or misappropriated post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction funds or materials, confiscate the illegal gains, and give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism to the unit; with respect to the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible, the appointing authority or the supervisory organ shall, according to the personnel management authority, give sanctions ranging from demotion, removal from office to expulsion according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　在地震灾后恢复重建中，有关地方人民政府及政府有关部门拖欠施工单位工程款，或者明示、暗示设计单位、施工单位违反抗震设防要求和工程建设强制性标准，降低建设工程质量，造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由任免机关或者监察机关按照人事管理权限依法给予降级、撤职直至开除的处分。

**Article 74** In the course of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, if a relevant local people's government or relevant government department defaults on project payments to the construction unit, or expressly or implicitly instructs the design unit or construction unit to violate the seismic fortification requirements and mandatory standards for engineering construction, thereby lowering the quality of the construction project and causing a major safety accident, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, with respect to the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible, the appointing authority or the supervisory organ shall, according to the personnel management authority, give sanctions ranging from demotion, removal from office to expulsion according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　在地震灾后恢复重建中，建设单位、勘察单位、设计单位、施工单位或者工程监理单位，降低建设工程质量，造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上地方人民政府建设主管部门或者其他有关部门依照《建设工程质量管理条例》的有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 75** In the course of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, if the construction unit, survey unit, design unit, construction unit or project supervision unit lowers the quality of the construction project, causing a major safety accident, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, the competent department of construction of the local people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Quality Management of Construction Projects.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　对毁损严重的基础设施、公共服务设施和其他建设工程，在调查评估中经鉴定确认工程质量存在重大问题，构成犯罪的，对负有责任的建设单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位的直接责任人员，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上地方人民政府建设主管部门或者其他有关部门依照《建设工程质量管理条例》的有关规定给予处罚。涉嫌行贿、受贿的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 76** For severely damaged infrastructure, public service facilities and other construction projects, if, during investigation and assessment, an expert evaluation confirms that there are major quality problems in the project, and if a crime is constituted, the persons directly responsible of the responsible construction unit, design unit, construction unit and project supervision unit shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if no crime is constituted, the competent department of construction of the local people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Quality Management of Construction Projects. If bribery or acceptance of bribes is suspected, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　在地震灾后恢复重建中，扰乱社会公共秩序，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 77** Where, in the course of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, public order is disturbed and the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国家工作人员在地震灾后恢复重建工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where a state functionary abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the work of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, he shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　地震灾后恢复重建中的其他有关法律的适用和有关政策，由国务院依法另行制定，或者由国务院有关部门、省级人民政府在各自职权范围内做出规定。

**Article 79** The application of other relevant laws and relevant policies in post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction shall be separately formulated by the State Council according to law, or stipulated by the relevant departments of the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government within their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 80** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
