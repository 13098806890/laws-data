# 中华人民共和国进出口货物原产地条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2005-01-01

> (2004年9月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第416号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了正确确定进出口货物的原产地，有效实施各项贸易措施，促进对外贸易发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于实施最惠国待遇、反倾销和反补贴、保障措施、原产地标记管理、国别数量限制、关税配额等非优惠性贸易措施以及进行政府采购、贸易统计等活动对进出口货物原产地的确定。 实施优惠性贸易措施对进出口货物原产地的确定，不适用本条例。具体办法依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定的有关规定另行制定。 第三条　完全在一个国家(地区)获得的货物，以该国(地区)为原产地；两个以上国家(地区)参与生产的货物，以最后完成实质性改变的国家(地区)为原产地。 第四条　本条例第三条所称完全在一个国家(地区)获得的货物，是指： (一)在该国(地区)出生并饲养的活的动物； (二)在该国(地区)野外捕捉、捕捞、搜集的动物； (三)从该国(地区)的活的动物获得的未经加工的物品； (四)在该国(地区)收获的植物和植物产品； (五)在该国(地区)采掘的矿物； (六)在该国(地区)获得的除本条第(一)项至第(五)项范围之外的其他天然生成的物品； (七)在该国(地区)生产过程中产生的只能弃置或者回收用作材料的废碎料； (八)在该国(地区)收集的不能修复或者修理的物品，或者从该物品中回收的零件或者材料； (九)由合法悬挂该国旗帜的船舶从其领海以外海域获得的海洋捕捞物和其他物品；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了正确确定进出口货物的原产地，有效实施各项贸易措施，促进对外贸易发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purpose of correctly determining the origin of import and export goods, effectively implementing various trade measures, and promoting the development of foreign trade, these Regulations are formulated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于实施最惠国待遇、反倾销和反补贴、保障措施、原产地标记管理、国别数量限制、关税配额等非优惠性贸易措施以及进行政府采购、贸易统计等活动对进出口货物原产地的确定。  
实施优惠性贸易措施对进出口货物原产地的确定，不适用本条例。具体办法依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定的有关规定另行制定。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the determination of the origin of import and export goods for implementing non-preferential trade measures such as most-favored-nation treatment, anti-dumping and countervailing duties, safeguard measures, origin marking administration, country-specific quantitative restrictions, tariff quotas, and for carrying out activities such as government procurement and trade statistics.  
The determination of the origin of import and export goods for implementing preferential trade measures shall not be governed by these Regulations. Specific measures shall be separately formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of international treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　完全在一个国家(地区)获得的货物，以该国(地区)为原产地；两个以上国家(地区)参与生产的货物，以最后完成实质性改变的国家(地区)为原产地。

**Article 3** Goods wholly obtained in one country (region) shall take that country (region) as their origin; goods in the production of which two or more countries (regions) are involved shall take the country (region) where the last substantial transformation was carried out as their origin.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[本条例第三条](#art-3)所称完全在一个国家(地区)获得的货物，是指：  
(一)在该国(地区)出生并饲养的活的动物；  
(二)在该国(地区)野外捕捉、捕捞、搜集的动物；  
(三)从该国(地区)的活的动物获得的未经加工的物品；  
(四)在该国(地区)收获的植物和植物产品；  
(五)在该国(地区)采掘的矿物；  
(六)在该国(地区)获得的除本条第(一)项至第(五)项范围之外的其他天然生成的物品；  
(七)在该国(地区)生产过程中产生的只能弃置或者回收用作材料的废碎料；  
(八)在该国(地区)收集的不能修复或者修理的物品，或者从该物品中回收的零件或者材料；  
(九)由合法悬挂该国旗帜的船舶从其领海以外海域获得的海洋捕捞物和其他物品；  
(十)在合法悬挂该国旗帜的加工船上加工本条第(九)项所列物品获得的产品；  
(十一)从该国领海以外享有专有开采权的海床或者海床底土获得的物品；  
(十二)在该国(地区)完全从本条第(一)项至第(十一)项所列物品中生产的产品。

**Article 4** The term "goods wholly obtained in one country (region)" as mentioned in Article 3 of these Regulations means:  
(1) live animals born and raised in that country (region);  
(2) animals captured, caught, or collected in the wild in that country (region);  
(3) unprocessed products obtained from live animals in that country (region);  
(4) plants and plant products harvested in that country (region);  
(5) minerals extracted in that country (region);  
(6) other naturally occurring products obtained in that country (region) other than those specified in items (1) to (5) of this Article;  
(7) waste and scrap derived from production processes in that country (region) which can only be discarded or recycled as materials;  
(8) articles collected in that country (region) which cannot be restored or repaired, or parts or materials recovered from such articles;  
(9) marine catches and other articles obtained from outside the territorial sea of that country (region) by vessels lawfully flying the flag of that country;  
(10) products obtained from processing the articles listed in item (9) of this Article aboard factory ships lawfully flying the flag of that country;  
(11) articles obtained from the seabed or subsoil outside the territorial sea of that country (region) over which that country (region) enjoys exclusive exploitation rights;  
(12) products wholly produced in that country (region) from the articles listed in items (1) to (11) of this Article.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在确定货物是否在一个国家(地区)完全获得时，不考虑下列微小加工或者处理：  
(一)为运输、贮存期间保存货物而作的加工或者处理；  
(二)为货物便于装卸而作的加工或者处理；  
(三)为货物销售而作的包装等加工或者处理。

**Article 5** When determining whether goods are wholly obtained in one country (region), the following minimal operations or processes shall not be taken into account:  
(1) operations or processes performed to preserve goods during transport or storage;  
(2) operations or processes performed to facilitate loading or unloading of goods;  
(3) operations or processes such as packaging performed for the sale of goods.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[本条例第三条](#art-3)规定的实质性改变的确定标准，以税则归类改变为基本标准；税则归类改变不能反映实质性改变的，以从价百分比、制造或者加工工序等为补充标准。具体标准由海关总署会同商务部制定。  
本条第一款所称税则归类改变，是指在某一国家(地区)对非该国(地区)原产材料进行制造、加工后，所得货物在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中某一级的税目归类发生了变化。  
本条第一款所称从价百分比，是指在某一国家(地区)对非该国(地区)原产材料进行制造、加工后的增值部分，超过所得货物价值一定的百分比。  
本条第一款所称制造或者加工工序，是指在某一国家(地区)进行的赋予制造、加工后所得货物基本特征的主要工序。  
世界贸易组织《协调非优惠原产地规则》实施前，确定进出口货物原产地实质性改变的具体标准，由海关总署会同商务部根据实际情况另行制定。

**Article 6** The criteria for determining substantial transformation as prescribed in Article 3 of these Regulations shall take the change in tariff classification as the basic criterion; where the change in tariff classification fails to reflect substantial transformation, supplementary criteria such as ad valorem percentage, manufacturing or processing operations shall be adopted. The specific criteria shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs in conjunction with the Ministry of Commerce.  
The term "change in tariff classification" as mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article means that after manufacturing or processing of non-originating materials in a certain country (region), the tariff heading of the resulting goods at a certain level in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China undergoes a change.  
The term "ad valorem percentage" as mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article means that the value added from manufacturing or processing of non-originating materials in a certain country (region) exceeds a certain percentage of the value of the resulting goods.  
The term "manufacturing or processing operations" as mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article means the principal operations carried out in a certain country (region) that confer the essential characteristics on the goods obtained after manufacturing or processing.  
Before the implementation of the World Trade Organization's "Harmonized Non-Preferential Rules of Origin", the specific criteria for determining substantial transformation of origin of import and export goods shall be separately formulated by the General Administration of Customs in conjunction with the Ministry of Commerce based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　货物生产过程中使用的能源、厂房、设备、机器和工具的原产地，以及未构成货物物质成分或者组成部件的材料的原产地，不影响该货物原产地的确定。

**Article 7** The origin of energy, plant, equipment, machinery and tools used in the production process of goods, and the origin of materials which do not constitute the material composition or component parts of the goods, shall not affect the determination of the origin of such goods.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　随所装货物进出口的包装、包装材料和容器，在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中与该货物一并归类的，该包装、包装材料和容器的原产地不影响所装货物原产地的确定；对该包装、包装材料和容器的原产地不再单独确定，所装货物的原产地即为该包装、包装材料和容器的原产地。  
随所装货物进出口的包装、包装材料和容器，在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中与该货物不一并归类的，依照本条例的规定确定该包装、包装材料和容器的原产地。

**Article 8** Where the packaging, packaging materials and containers imported or exported with the goods they contain are classified together with such goods in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China, the origin of such packaging, packaging materials and containers shall not affect the determination of the origin of the goods contained; the origin of such packaging, packaging materials and containers shall not be separately determined, and the origin of the goods contained shall be the origin of such packaging, packaging materials and containers.  
Where the packaging, packaging materials and containers imported or exported with the goods they contain are not classified together with such goods in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China, the origin of such packaging, packaging materials and containers shall be determined in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　按正常配备的种类和数量随货物进出口的附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料，在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中与该货物一并归类的，该附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料的原产地不影响该货物原产地的确定；对该附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料的原产地不再单独确定，该货物的原产地即为该附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料的原产地。  
随货物进出口的附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中虽与该货物一并归类，但超出正常配备的种类和数量的，以及在《中华人民共和国进出口税则》中与该货物不一并归类的，依照本条例的规定确定该附件、备件、工具和介绍说明性资料的原产地。

**Article 9** Where accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials imported or exported with the goods in the types and quantities normally equipped are classified together with such goods in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China, the origin of such accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials shall not affect the determination of the origin of the goods; the origin of such accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials shall not be separately determined, and the origin of the goods shall be the origin of such accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials.  
Where accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials imported or exported with the goods are classified together with such goods in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China but exceed the types and quantities normally equipped, or are not classified together with such goods in the Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China, the origin of such accessories, spare parts, tools and descriptive or instructional materials shall be determined in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对货物所进行的任何加工或者处理，是为了规避中华人民共和国关于反倾销、反补贴和保障措施等有关规定的，海关在确定该货物的原产地时可以不考虑这类加工和处理。

**Article 10** Where any processing or operation performed on goods is for the purpose of evading the relevant provisions of the People's Republic of China on anti-dumping, countervailing duties and safeguard measures, the Customs may disregard such processing or operation when determining the origin of the goods.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进口货物的收货人按照《中华人民共和国海关法》及有关规定办理进口货物的海关申报手续时，应当依照本条例规定的原产地确定标准如实申报进口货物的原产地；同一批货物的原产地不同的，应当分别申报原产地。

**Article 11** When going through Customs declaration formalities for import goods in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant provisions, the consignee of import goods shall truthfully declare the origin of the import goods in accordance with the origin determination criteria prescribed in these Regulations; where the origins of goods in the same batch are different, the origins shall be declared separately.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进口货物进口前，进口货物的收货人或者与进口货物直接相关的其他当事人，在有正当理由的情况下，可以书面申请海关对将要进口的货物的原产地作出预确定决定；申请人应当按照规定向海关提供作出原产地预确定决定所需的资料。  
海关应当在收到原产地预确定书面申请及全部必要资料之日起150天内，依照本条例的规定对该进口货物作出原产地预确定决定，并对外公布。

**Article 12** Before the import of import goods, the consignee of the import goods or other parties directly related to the import goods may, for justified reasons, apply in writing to the Customs for a pre-determination of the origin of the goods to be imported; the applicant shall provide the Customs with the information necessary for making the pre-determination of origin in accordance with the provisions.  
The Customs shall, within 150 days from the date of receipt of the written application for pre-determination of origin and all necessary information, make a pre-determination of origin for such import goods in accordance with these Regulations, and make it public.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海关接受申报后，应当按照本条例的规定审核确定进口货物的原产地。  
已作出原产地预确定决定的货物，自预确定决定作出之日起3年内实际进口时，经海关审核其实际进口的货物与预确定决定所述货物相符，且本条例规定的原产地确定标准未发生变化的，海关不再重新确定该进口货物的原产地；经海关审核其实际进口的货物与预确定决定所述货物不相符的，海关应当按照本条例的规定重新审核确定该进口货物的原产地。

**Article 13** After accepting the declaration, the Customs shall examine and determine the origin of the import goods in accordance with these Regulations.  
Where, within three years from the date of the pre-determination decision, goods for which a pre-determination of origin has been made are actually imported, if the Customs verifies that the actually imported goods are consistent with the goods described in the pre-determination decision and the origin determination criteria prescribed in these Regulations have not changed, the Customs shall not re-determine the origin of such import goods; if the Customs verifies that the actually imported goods are inconsistent with the goods described in the pre-determination decision, the Customs shall re-examine and determine the origin of such import goods in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海关在审核确定进口货物原产地时，可以要求进口货物的收货人提交该进口货物的原产地证书，并予以审验；必要时，可以请求该货物出口国(地区)的有关机构对该货物的原产地进行核查。

**Article 14** When examining and determining the origin of import goods, the Customs may require the consignee of the import goods to submit the certificate of origin of such import goods, and shall examine and verify it; when necessary, it may request the relevant authorities of the exporting country (region) of the goods to verify the origin of such goods.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　根据对外贸易经营者提出的书面申请，海关可以依照[《中华人民共和国海关法》第四十三条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国海关法.md#art-43)的规定，对将要进口的货物的原产地预先作出确定原产地的行政裁定，并对外公布。  
进口相同的货物，应当适用相同的行政裁定。

**Article 15** Based on a written application submitted by a foreign trade operator, the Customs may, in accordance with Article 43 of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, make an administrative ruling in advance on the origin of goods to be imported, and make it public.  
The same administrative ruling shall apply to the import of identical goods.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对原产地标记实施管理。货物或者其包装上标有原产地标记的，其原产地标记所标明的原产地应当与依照本条例所确定的原产地相一致。

**Article 16** The State shall implement administration of origin marking. Where the origin mark is affixed to the goods or their packaging, the origin indicated by such origin mark shall be consistent with the origin determined in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　出口货物发货人可以向海关、中国国际贸易促进委员会及其地方分会(以下简称签证机构)，申请领取出口货物原产地证书。

**Article 17** The consignor of export goods may apply to the Customs, the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade and its local sub-councils (hereinafter referred to as the visa-issuing authorities) for obtaining a certificate of origin for export goods.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　出口货物发货人申请领取出口货物原产地证书，应当在签证机构办理注册登记手续，按照规定如实申报出口货物的原产地，并向签证机构提供签发出口货物原产地证书所需的资料。

**Article 18** When applying for a certificate of origin for export goods, the consignor of export goods shall go through the registration formalities at the visa-issuing authority, truthfully declare the origin of the export goods in accordance with the provisions, and provide the visa-issuing authority with the information necessary for issuing the certificate of origin for export goods.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　签证机构接受出口货物发货人的申请后，应当按照规定审查确定出口货物的原产地，签发出口货物原产地证书；对不属于原产于中华人民共和国境内的出口货物，应当拒绝签发出口货物原产地证书。  
出口货物原产地证书签发管理的具体办法，由海关总署会同国务院其他有关部门、机构另行制定。

**Article 19** After accepting an application from the consignor of export goods, the visa-issuing authority shall, in accordance with the provisions, examine and determine the origin of the export goods and issue a certificate of origin for export goods; for export goods not originating in the territory of the People's Republic of China, it shall refuse to issue a certificate of origin for export goods.  
The specific measures for the issuance and administration of certificates of origin for export goods shall be separately formulated by the General Administration of Customs in conjunction with other relevant departments and agencies under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　应出口货物进口国(地区)有关机构的请求，海关、签证机构可以对出口货物的原产地情况进行核查，并及时将核查情况反馈进口国(地区)有关机构。

**Article 20** Upon request of the relevant authorities of the importing country (region) of export goods, the Customs or the visa-issuing authorities may verify the origin of the export goods, and shall promptly provide feedback on the verification results to the relevant authorities of the importing country (region).

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　用于确定货物原产地的资料和信息，除按有关规定可以提供或者经提供该资料和信息的单位、个人的允许，海关、签证机构应当对该资料和信息予以保密。

**Article 21** The data and information used for determining the origin of goods shall be kept confidential by the Customs and the visa-issuing authorities, unless they may be provided in accordance with relevant provisions or with the permission of the entity or individual that provided such data and information.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定申报进口货物原产地的，依照《中华人民共和国对外贸易法》、《中华人民共和国海关法》和《中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例》的有关规定进行处罚。

**Article 22** Any person who declares the origin of import goods in violation of the provisions of these Regulations shall be penalized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China, the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Regulation of the People's Republic of China on the Implementation of Customs Administrative Penalties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　提供虚假材料骗取出口货物原产地证书或者伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃出口货物原产地证书的，由海关处5000元以上10万元以下的罚款；骗取、伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃作为海关放行凭证的出口货物原产地证书的，处货值金额等值以下的罚款，但货值金额低于5000元的，处5000元罚款。有违法所得的，由海关没收违法所得。构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** Any person who obtains a certificate of origin for export goods by providing false materials, or forges, alters, buys, sells or steals a certificate of origin for export goods, shall be fined by the Customs not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; where the certificate of origin for export goods that is obtained by deception, forged, altered, bought, sold or stolen is used as a Customs release document, a fine of not more than the equivalent value of the goods shall be imposed, but where the value of the goods is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of 5,000 yuan shall be imposed. Any illegal gains shall be confiscated by the Customs. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　进出口货物的原产地标记与依照本条例所确定的原产地不一致的，由海关责令改正。

**Article 24** Where the origin mark on import or export goods is inconsistent with the origin determined in accordance with these Regulations, the Customs shall order corrections.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　确定进出口货物原产地的工作人员违反本条例规定的程序确定原产地的，或者泄露所知悉的商业秘密的，或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予行政处分；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where any staff member responsible for determining the origin of import and export goods determines the origin in violation of the procedures prescribed in these Regulations, or divulges any trade secret known to him, or abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, he shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
获得，是指捕捉、捕捞、搜集、收获、采掘、加工或者生产等。  
货物原产地，是指依照本条例确定的获得某一货物的国家(地区)。  
原产地证书，是指出口国(地区)根据原产地规则和有关要求签发的，明确指出该证中所列货物原产于某一特定国家(地区)的书面文件。  
原产地标记，是指在货物或者包装上用来表明该货物原产地的文字和图形。

**Article 26** For the purposes of these Regulations:  
"Obtained" means capturing, catching, collecting, harvesting, extracting, processing or producing, etc.  
"Origin of goods" means the country (region) in which goods are obtained as determined in accordance with these Regulations.  
"Certificate of origin" means a written document issued by the exporting country (region) in accordance with the rules of origin and relevant requirements, which clearly states that the goods listed therein originate in a specific country (region).  
"Origin mark" means the words and figures affixed to goods or their packaging to indicate the origin of such goods.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2005年1月1日起施行。1992年3月8日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国出口货物原产地规则》、1986年12月6日海关总署发布的《中华人民共和国海关关于进口货物原产地的暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2005. The Rules of the People's Republic of China on the Origin of Export Goods promulgated by the State Council on March 8, 1992, and the Interim Provisions of the Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Origin of Import Goods promulgated by the General Administration of Customs on December 6, 1986, shall be abolished simultaneously.
