# 国务院实施《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》若干规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2005-05-19  
**生效日期**：2005-05-31

> (2005年5月11日国务院第89次常务会议通过　2005年5月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第435号公布　自2005年5月31日起施行) 第一条　为了帮助民族自治地方加快经济和社会的发展，增进民族团结，促进各民族共同繁荣，根据《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》，制定本规定。 第二条　各级人民政府应当加强《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》以及相关法律、法规和民族政策的宣传教育，依法制订具体措施，保护少数民族的合法权益，妥善处理影响民族团结的问题，巩固和发展平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系，禁止破坏民族团结和制造民族分裂的行为。 第三条　维护祖国统一和民族团结是公民的职责和义务。 民族自治地方人民政府应当切实保障宪法和法律在本地方的遵守和执行，积极维护国家的整体利益。 第四条　各级人民政府应当积极开展促进民族团结进步的各项活动，对为民族团结进步事业作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 第五条　上级人民政府及其职能部门在制订经济和社会发展中长期规划时，应当听取民族自治地方和民族工作部门的意见，根据民族自治地方的特点和需要，支持和帮助民族自治地方加强基础设施建设、人力资源开发，扩大对外开放，调整、优化经济结构，合理利用自然资源，加强生态建设和环境保护，加速发展经济、教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育等各项事业，实现全面、协调、可持续发展。 第六条　国家实施西部大开发战略，促进民族自治地方加快发展。未列入西部大开发范围的自治县，由其所在的省级人民政府在职权范围内比照西部大开发的有关政策予以扶持。 第七条　上级人民政府应当根据民族自治地方的实际，优先在民族自治地方安排基础设施建设项目。 中央财政性建设资金、其他专项建设资金和政策性银行贷款，适当增加用于民族自治地方基础设施建设的比重。 国家安排的基础设施建设项目，需要民族自治地方承担配套资金的，适当降低配套资金的比例。民族自治地方的国家扶贫重点县和财政困难县确实无力负担的，免除配套资金。其中，基础设施建设项目属于地方事务的，由中央和省级人民政府确定建设资金负担比例后，按比例全额安排；属于中央事务的，由中央财政全额安排。 第八条　国家根据经济和社会发展规划以及西部大开发战略，优先在民族自治地方安排资源开发和深加工项目。在民族自治地方开采石油、天然气等资源的，要在带动当地经济发展、发展相应的服务产业以及促进就业等方面，对当地给予支持。 国家征收的矿产资源补偿费在安排使用时，加大对民族自治地方的投入，并优先考虑原产地的民族自治地方。 国家加快建立生态补偿机制，根据开发者付费、受益者补偿、破坏者赔偿的原则，从国家、区域、产业三个层面，通过财政转移支付、项目支持等措施，对在野生动植物保护和自然保护区建设等生态环境保护方面作出贡献的民族自治地方，给予合理补偿。 第九条　国家通过一般性财政转移支付、专项财政转移支付、民族优惠政策财政转移支付以及其他方式，充分考虑民族自治地方的公共服务支出成本差异，逐步加大对民族自治地方财政转移支付力度。上级人民政府有关部门各种专项资金的分配，应当向民族自治地方倾斜。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了帮助民族自治地方加快经济和社会的发展，增进民族团结，促进各民族共同繁荣，根据《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional Ethnic Autonomy for the purpose of helping the ethnic autonomous areas accelerate economic and social development, enhance ethnic unity, and promote common prosperity among all ethnic groups.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府应当加强《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》以及相关法律、法规和民族政策的宣传教育，依法制订具体措施，保护少数民族的合法权益，妥善处理影响民族团结的问题，巩固和发展平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系，禁止破坏民族团结和制造民族分裂的行为。

**Article 2** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the publicity and education on the Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional Ethnic Autonomy as well as relevant laws, regulations and ethnic policies, formulate specific measures according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of ethnic minorities, properly handle issues affecting ethnic unity, consolidate and develop a socialist ethnic relationship characterized by equality, solidarity and mutual assistance, and prohibit acts that undermine ethnic unity and create ethnic division.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　维护祖国统一和民族团结是公民的职责和义务。  
民族自治地方人民政府应当切实保障宪法和法律在本地方的遵守和执行，积极维护国家的整体利益。

**Article 3** It is the duty and obligation of citizens to safeguard the unity of the country and ethnic unity.  
People's governments of ethnic autonomous areas shall ensure the observance and enforcement of the Constitution and laws in their respective areas, and actively safeguard the overall interests of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当积极开展促进民族团结进步的各项活动，对为民族团结进步事业作出突出贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall actively carry out various activities to promote ethnic unity and progress, and commend and reward units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the cause of ethnic unity and progress.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　上级人民政府及其职能部门在制订经济和社会发展中长期规划时，应当听取民族自治地方和民族工作部门的意见，根据民族自治地方的特点和需要，支持和帮助民族自治地方加强基础设施建设、人力资源开发，扩大对外开放，调整、优化经济结构，合理利用自然资源，加强生态建设和环境保护，加速发展经济、教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育等各项事业，实现全面、协调、可持续发展。

**Article 5** When formulating medium and long-term plans for economic and social development, the people's governments at higher levels and their functional departments shall listen to the opinions of the ethnic autonomous areas and the ethnic affairs departments, and based on the characteristics and needs of the ethnic autonomous areas, support and assist the ethnic autonomous areas in strengthening infrastructure construction, developing human resources, expanding opening up to the outside world, adjusting and optimizing economic structure, rationally utilizing natural resources, strengthening ecological improvement and environmental protection, and accelerating the development of economy, education, science and technology, culture, health, sports and other undertakings, so as to achieve comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable development.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家实施西部大开发战略，促进民族自治地方加快发展。未列入西部大开发范围的自治县，由其所在的省级人民政府在职权范围内比照西部大开发的有关政策予以扶持。

**Article 6** The State implements the strategy of large-scale development of the western region to promote the accelerated development of ethnic autonomous areas. For autonomous counties not included in the scope of the large-scale development of the western region, the people's governments of the provinces where they are located shall provide support within the limits of their powers by reference to the relevant policies of the large-scale development of the western region.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　上级人民政府应当根据民族自治地方的实际，优先在民族自治地方安排基础设施建设项目。  
中央财政性建设资金、其他专项建设资金和政策性银行贷款，适当增加用于民族自治地方基础设施建设的比重。  
国家安排的基础设施建设项目，需要民族自治地方承担配套资金的，适当降低配套资金的比例。民族自治地方的国家扶贫重点县和财政困难县确实无力负担的，免除配套资金。其中，基础设施建设项目属于地方事务的，由中央和省级人民政府确定建设资金负担比例后，按比例全额安排；属于中央事务的，由中央财政全额安排。

**Article 7** The people's governments at higher levels shall, based on the actual conditions of the ethnic autonomous areas, give priority to arranging infrastructure construction projects in such areas.  
Central budgetary construction funds, other special construction funds and policy bank loans shall be appropriately increased in proportion for infrastructure construction in ethnic autonomous areas.  
Where infrastructure construction projects arranged by the State require counterpart funds from ethnic autonomous areas, the proportion of counterpart funds shall be appropriately reduced. Where the key poverty-stricken counties and financially difficult counties in ethnic autonomous areas are truly unable to bear the burden, the counterpart funds shall be exempted. Among them, where the infrastructure construction project falls under local affairs, the central and provincial people's governments shall determine the burden proportion of the construction funds and make full arrangement according to the proportion; where it falls under central affairs, the central finance shall make full arrangement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家根据经济和社会发展规划以及西部大开发战略，优先在民族自治地方安排资源开发和深加工项目。在民族自治地方开采石油、天然气等资源的，要在带动当地经济发展、发展相应的服务产业以及促进就业等方面，对当地给予支持。  
国家征收的矿产资源补偿费在安排使用时，加大对民族自治地方的投入，并优先考虑原产地的民族自治地方。  
国家加快建立生态补偿机制，根据开发者付费、受益者补偿、破坏者赔偿的原则，从国家、区域、产业三个层面，通过财政转移支付、项目支持等措施，对在野生动植物保护和自然保护区建设等生态环境保护方面作出贡献的民族自治地方，给予合理补偿。

**Article 8** The State shall, in accordance with the economic and social development plans and the strategy for large-scale development of the western region, give priority to arranging resource development and deep processing projects in ethnic autonomous areas. Where resources such as oil and natural gas are exploited in ethnic autonomous areas, support shall be given to local areas in terms of driving local economic development, developing corresponding service industries and promoting employment.  
The mineral resources compensation fees collected by the State shall, when arranged for use, increase input to ethnic autonomous areas and give priority to the ethnic autonomous areas where the resources are produced.  
The State shall accelerate the establishment of an ecological compensation mechanism. According to the principle of 'developer pays, beneficiary compensates, and destroyer indemnifies', at the three levels of the State, region and industry, through measures such as fiscal transfer payments and project support, reasonable compensation shall be given to ethnic autonomous areas that have made contributions to the protection of wild fauna and flora and the construction of nature reserves and other ecological and environmental protection.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家通过一般性财政转移支付、专项财政转移支付、民族优惠政策财政转移支付以及其他方式，充分考虑民族自治地方的公共服务支出成本差异，逐步加大对民族自治地方财政转移支付力度。上级人民政府有关部门各种专项资金的分配，应当向民族自治地方倾斜。  
上级财政支持民族自治地方财政保证民族自治地方的国家机关正常运转、财政供养人员工资按时足额发放、基础教育正常经费支出。  
上级人民政府出台的税收减免政策造成民族自治地方财政减收部分，在测算转移支付时作为因素给予照顾。  
国家规范省级以下财政转移支付制度，确保国家对民族自治地方的转移支付、税收返还等优惠政策落实到自治县。

**Article 9** The State shall, through general fiscal transfer payments, special fiscal transfer payments, fiscal transfer payments for ethnic preferential policies and other means, fully consider the cost differences of public service expenditures in ethnic autonomous areas, and gradually increase the intensity of fiscal transfer payments to ethnic autonomous areas. The distribution of various special funds by relevant departments of the people's governments at higher levels shall be tilted towards ethnic autonomous areas.  
The finance at higher levels shall support the finance of ethnic autonomous areas in ensuring the normal operation of State organs in ethnic autonomous areas, the timely and full payment of wages to persons supported by finance, and the normal expenditure on basic education.  
The part of reduced fiscal revenue of ethnic autonomous areas caused by tax reduction and exemption policies issued by the people's governments at higher levels shall be given consideration as a factor when calculating transfer payments.  
The State shall regulate the fiscal transfer payment system below the provincial level to ensure that the preferential policies of the State on transfer payments and tax rebates to ethnic autonomous areas are implemented in autonomous counties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家设立各项专用资金，扶助民族自治地方发展经济和社会各项事业。  
中央财政设立少数民族发展资金和民族工作经费。资金规模随着经济发展和中央财政收入的增长逐步增加。地方财政相应设立并安排少数民族发展资金和民族工作经费。

**Article 10** The State establishes various special funds to assist ethnic autonomous areas in developing economic and social undertakings.  
The central finance establishes development funds for ethnic minorities and operational funds for ethnic affairs. The scale of the funds gradually increases with economic development and the growth of central fiscal revenue. The local finance shall accordingly establish and arrange development funds for ethnic minorities and operational funds for ethnic affairs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家帮助民族自治地方拓宽间接和直接融资渠道，加大对民族自治地方的金融扶持力度。  
国家合理引导金融机构信贷投向，鼓励金融机构积极支持民族自治地方重点建设和农村发展。上级人民政府安排的国际组织和国外政府赠款以及优惠贷款，在条件许可的情况下，向民族自治地方倾斜。

**Article 11** The State helps ethnic autonomous areas to broaden indirect and direct financing channels and increase financial support to ethnic autonomous areas.  
The State reasonably guides the credit direction of financial institutions and encourages financial institutions to actively support key construction and rural development in ethnic autonomous areas. The grants from international organizations and foreign governments and preferential loans arranged by the people's governments at higher levels shall, where conditions permit, be tilted towards ethnic autonomous areas.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家完善扶持民族贸易、少数民族特需商品和传统手工业品生产发展的优惠政策，在税收、金融和财政政策上，对民族贸易、少数民族特需商品和传统手工业品生产予以照顾，对少数民族特需商品实行定点生产并建立必要的国家储备制度。

**Article 12** The State improves the preferential policies for supporting the development of the production of ethnic trade, commodities for the special needs of ethnic minorities, and traditional handicraft products, and provides preferential treatment with regard to tax, financial and fiscal policies for such production. For commodities for the special needs of ethnic minorities, the State shall implement designated production and establish a necessary national reserve system.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励与外国接壤的民族自治地方依法与周边国家开展区域经济技术合作和边境贸易。  
经国务院批准，可以在与外国接壤的民族自治地方边境地区设立边境贸易区。  
国家对边境地区与接壤国家边境地区之间的贸易以及边民互市贸易，采取灵活措施，给予优惠和便利。

**Article 13** The State encourages ethnic autonomous areas bordering foreign countries to carry out regional economic and technological cooperation and border trade with neighboring countries according to law.  
With the approval of the State Council, border trade zones may be established in the border areas of ethnic autonomous areas bordering foreign countries.  
The State shall adopt flexible measures, grant preferential treatment and provide conveniences for trade between border areas and border areas of neighboring countries, as well as for border residents' mutual trade.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家将边境地区建设纳入经济和社会发展规划，帮助民族自治地方加快边境地区建设，推进兴边富民行动，促进边境地区与内地的协调发展。  
国家对巩固边防、边境安全具有重大影响的边境地区居民，在居住、生活、文化、教育、医疗卫生、环境保护等方面采取特殊措施，加大扶持力度。

**Article 14** The State incorporates the construction of border areas into economic and social development plans, helps ethnic autonomous areas accelerate the construction of border areas, promote the action of invigorating the border areas and enriching the people there, and promote the coordinated development of border areas and inland areas.  
The State shall adopt special measures and increase support for residents in border areas who have a significant impact on the consolidation of border defense and border security in terms of housing, livelihood, culture, education, medical and health care, and environmental protection.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　上级人民政府将人口较少民族聚居的地区发展纳入经济和社会发展规划，加大扶持力度，在交通、能源、生态环境保护与建设、农业基础设施建设、广播影视、文化、教育、医疗卫生以及群众生产生活等方面，给予重点支持。

**Article 15** The people's governments at higher levels shall incorporate the development of areas inhabited by ethnic minorities with smaller populations into economic and social development plans, increase support, and give priority support in terms of transportation, energy, ecological environmental protection and improvement, agricultural infrastructure construction, radio, film and television, culture, education, medical and health care, and the production and livelihood of the masses.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家加强民族自治地方的扶贫开发，重点支持民族自治地方贫困乡村以通水、通电、通路、通广播电视和茅草房危房改造、生态移民等为重点的基础设施建设和农田基本建设，动员和组织社会力量参与民族自治地方的扶贫开发。

**Article 16** The State strengthens the poverty alleviation and development in ethnic autonomous areas, and gives priority support to the infrastructure construction and farmland capital construction in poor villages and townships of ethnic autonomous areas, focusing on projects such as access to water, electricity, roads, radio and television, renovation of thatched and dilapidated houses, and ecological resettlement. The State shall mobilize and organize social forces to participate in the poverty alleviation and development in ethnic autonomous areas.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励、支持和引导民族自治地方发展非公有制经济，鼓励社会资本参与民族自治地方的基础设施、公用事业以及其他领域的建设和国有、集体企业改制。

**Article 17** The State encourages, supports and guides the development of non-public economy in ethnic autonomous areas, and encourages social capital to participate in the construction of infrastructure, public utilities and other fields in ethnic autonomous areas, as well as the restructuring of State-owned and collective enterprises.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家组织和支持经济发达地区与民族自治地方的对口支援。通过劳动密集型和资源加工型产业的转移、技术转让、交流培训人才、加大资金投入、提供物资支持等多种方式，帮助民族自治地方加速经济、文化、教育、科技、卫生、体育事业的发展；鼓励和引导企业、高等院校和科研单位以及社会各方面力量加大对民族自治地方的支持力度。  
民族自治地方各级人民政府引导和组织当地群众有序地外出经商务工。有关地方人民政府应当切实保障外来经商务工的少数民族群众的合法权益。

**Article 18** The State organizes and supports the paired-up assistance of economically developed areas to ethnic autonomous areas. Through various means such as the transfer of labor-intensive and resource-processing industries, technology transfer, exchange and training of personnel, increasing capital input, and providing material support, the State helps ethnic autonomous areas accelerate the development of economy, culture, education, science and technology, health, and sports; and encourages and guides enterprises, institutions of higher education, scientific research units, and various social forces to increase support to ethnic autonomous areas.  
People's governments at all levels of ethnic autonomous areas shall guide and organize the local people to go out for business and work in an orderly manner. The relevant local people's governments shall effectively protect the lawful rights and interests of the ethnic minority people who come for business and work.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家帮助民族自治地方普及九年义务教育，扫除青壮年文盲，不断改善办学条件，大力支持民族自治地方有重点地办好寄宿制学校；在发达地区普通中学开设民族班或者开办民族中学，其办学条件、教学和管理水平要达到当地学校的办学标准和水平。  
国家采取措施，扶持民族自治地方因地制宜发展职业教育和成人教育，发展普通高中教育和现代远程教育，促进农村基础教育、成人教育、职业教育统筹发展。  
国家鼓励和支持社会力量以多种形式在民族自治地方办学，积极组织发达地区支援民族自治地方发展教育事业。

**Article 19** The State helps ethnic autonomous areas to popularize nine-year compulsory education, eliminate illiteracy among young and middle-aged people, continuously improve school conditions, and vigorously support ethnic autonomous areas in running boarding schools with focused efforts; in ordinary middle schools in developed areas, ethnic classes or ethnic middle schools shall be set up, and their school conditions, teaching and management shall meet the standards and levels of local schools.  
The State adopts measures to support ethnic autonomous areas in developing vocational education and adult education according to local conditions, developing regular senior secondary education and modern distance education, and promoting the coordinated development of rural basic education, adult education and vocational education.  
The State encourages and supports social forces to run schools in ethnic autonomous areas in various forms, and actively organizes developed areas to support ethnic autonomous areas in developing education.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级人民政府应当将民族自治地方义务教育纳入公共财政的保障范围。中央财政设立少数民族教育专项补助资金，地方财政相应安排少数民族教育专项补助资金。  
国家积极创造条件，对民族自治地方的边境地区、贫困地区和人口较少民族聚居地区的义务教育给予重点支持，并逐步在民族自治地方的农村实行免费义务教育。

**Article 20** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate the compulsory education in ethnic autonomous areas into the scope of public financial guarantee. The central finance establishes a special subsidy fund for minority education, and the local finance shall accordingly arrange a special subsidy fund for minority education.  
The State actively creates conditions to give priority support to compulsory education in border areas, poverty-stricken areas and areas inhabited by ethnic minorities with smaller populations in ethnic autonomous areas, and gradually implements free compulsory education in the rural areas of ethnic autonomous areas.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家帮助和支持民族自治地方发展高等教育，办好民族院校和全国普通高等学校民族预科班、民族班。对民族自治地方的高等学校以及民族院校的学科建设和研究生招生，给予特殊的政策扶持。  
各类高等学校面向民族自治地方招生时，招生比例按规模同比增长并适当倾斜。对报考专科、本科和研究生的少数民族考生，在录取时应当根据情况采取加分或者降分的办法，适当放宽录取标准和条件，并对人口特少的少数民族考生给予特殊照顾。

**Article 21** The State helps and supports ethnic autonomous areas in developing higher education, running ethnic colleges and universities as well as preparatory courses for ethnic minorities and ethnic classes in ordinary institutions of higher education nationwide. Special policy support shall be given to the discipline construction and graduate student enrollment of institutions of higher education in ethnic autonomous areas and ethnic colleges and universities.  
When various institutions of higher education recruit students in ethnic autonomous areas, the enrollment proportion shall grow at the same rate as the scale and be appropriately tilted. For ethnic minority candidates applying for junior college, undergraduate and graduate programs, the admission standards and conditions shall be appropriately relaxed by taking measures such as adding or reducing scores according to the circumstances, and special preferential treatment shall be given to ethnic minority candidates with very small populations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家保障各民族使用和发展本民族语言文字的自由，扶持少数民族语言文字的规范化、标准化和信息处理工作；推广使用全国通用的普通话和规范汉字；鼓励民族自治地方各民族公民互相学习语言文字。  
国家鼓励民族自治地方逐步推行少数民族语文和汉语文授课的“双语教学”，扶持少数民族语文和汉语文教材的研究、开发、编译和出版，支持建立和健全少数民族教材的编译和审查机构，帮助培养通晓少数民族语文和汉语文的教师。

**Article 22** The State guarantees the freedom of all ethnic groups to use and develop their own spoken and written languages, and supports the standardization, normalization and information processing of the spoken and written languages of ethnic minorities; promotes the use of Putonghua (common speech) and standard Chinese characters nationwide; and encourages citizens of all ethnic groups in ethnic autonomous areas to learn each other's spoken and written languages.  
The State encourages ethnic autonomous areas to gradually implement 'bilingual teaching' in the languages of ethnic minorities and Chinese language, supports the research, development, compilation, translation and publication of textbooks in the languages of ethnic minorities and Chinese language, supports the establishment and improvement of compilation, translation and review institutions for textbooks in ethnic minority languages, and helps train teachers who are proficient in both the languages of ethnic minorities and Chinese language.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家帮助民族自治地方建立健全科技服务体系和科学普及体系。中央财政通过国家科技计划、科学基金、专项资金等方式，加大对民族自治地方科技工作的支持力度，积极支持和促进民族自治地方科技事业的发展。

**Article 23** The State helps ethnic autonomous areas establish and improve the science and technology service system and the science popularization system. The central finance, through national science and technology plans, science funds, special funds, and other means, increases support for the science and technology work in ethnic autonomous areas, and actively supports and promotes the development of science and technology in ethnic autonomous areas.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　上级人民政府从政策和资金上支持民族自治地方少数民族文化事业发展，加强文化基础设施建设，重点扶持具有民族形式和民族特点的公益性文化事业，加强民族自治地方的公共文化服务体系建设，培育和发展民族文化产业。  
国家支持少数民族新闻出版事业发展，做好少数民族语言广播、电影、电视节目的译制、制作和播映，扶持少数民族语言文字出版物的翻译、出版。  
国家重视少数民族优秀传统文化的继承和发展，定期举办少数民族传统体育运动会、少数民族文艺会演，繁荣民族文艺创作，丰富各民族群众的文化生活。

**Article 24** The people's governments at higher levels support the development of the cultural undertakings of ethnic minorities in ethnic autonomous areas in terms of policies and funds, strengthen the construction of cultural infrastructure, give priority support to public cultural undertakings with ethnic forms and characteristics, strengthen the construction of the public cultural service system in ethnic autonomous areas, and cultivate and develop the ethnic cultural industry.  
The State supports the development of the news and publishing undertakings of ethnic minorities, does a good job in the translation, production and broadcasting of radio, film and television programs in ethnic minority languages, and supports the translation and publication of publications in ethnic minority languages.  
The State attaches importance to the inheritance and development of the excellent traditional culture of ethnic minorities, regularly holds traditional ethnic minority sports games and ethnic minority literary and art performances, enriches the ethnic literary and artistic creation, and enriches the cultural life of the masses of all ethnic groups.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　上级人民政府支持对少数民族非物质文化遗产和名胜古迹、文物等物质文化遗产的保护和抢救，支持对少数民族古籍的搜集、整理、出版。

**Article 25** The people's governments at higher levels support the protection and rescue of the intangible cultural heritage of ethnic minorities and the tangible cultural heritage such as places of historic interest and scenic beauty and cultural relics, and support the collection, collation and publication of ancient books of ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　上级人民政府加大对民族自治地方公共卫生体系建设的资金投入以及技术支持，采取有效措施预防控制传染病、地方病和寄生虫病，建立并完善农村卫生服务体系、新型农村合作医疗制度和医疗救助制度，减轻民族自治地方贫困群众医疗费的负担；各级人民政府加大对民族医药事业的投入，保护、扶持和发展民族医药学，提高各民族的健康水平。  
上级人民政府制定优惠政策，鼓励民族自治地方实行计划生育和优生优育，提高各民族人口素质。

**Article 26** The people's governments at higher levels increase capital input and technical support for the construction of the public health system in ethnic autonomous areas, adopt effective measures to prevent and control infectious diseases, endemic diseases and parasitic diseases, establish and improve the rural health service system, the new rural cooperative medical care system and the medical assistance system, and reduce the burden of medical expenses on the poor in ethnic autonomous areas; people's governments at all levels increase investment in the ethnic medicine cause, protect, support and develop ethnic medicine and pharmacy, and improve the health level of all ethnic groups.  
The people's governments at higher levels formulate preferential policies to encourage ethnic autonomous areas to practice family planning and eugenics and superior nurture, and improve the population quality of all ethnic groups.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　上级人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，帮助民族自治地方加快社会保障体系建设，建立和完善养老、失业、医疗、工伤、生育保险和城市居民最低生活保障等制度，形成与当地经济和社会发展水平相适应的社会保障体系。

**Article 27** The people's governments at higher levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, help ethnic autonomous areas accelerate the construction of the social security system, establish and improve the systems of endowment insurance, unemployment insurance, medical insurance, work-related injury insurance, maternity insurance and the minimum subsistence guarantee for urban residents, so as to form a social security system adapted to the level of local economic and social development.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　上级人民政府及其工作部门领导人员中应当合理配备少数民族干部；民族自治地方人民政府及其工作部门应当依法配备实行区域自治的民族和其他民族领导干部，在公开选拔、竞争上岗配备领导干部时，可以划出相应的名额和岗位，定向选拔少数民族干部。  
民族自治地方录用、聘用国家工作人员时，对实行区域自治的民族和其他少数民族予以照顾，具体办法由录用、聘用主管部门规定。

**Article 28** Among the leading personnel of the people's governments at higher levels and their work departments, ethnic minority cadres shall be reasonably allocated; the people's governments of ethnic autonomous areas and their work departments shall, according to law, equip with leading cadres from the ethnic group exercising regional autonomy and other ethnic groups. When selecting leading cadres through open selection and competition for posts, corresponding quotas and posts may be allocated for targeted selection of ethnic minority cadres.  
When recruiting and employing State functionaries in ethnic autonomous areas, preferential treatment shall be given to the ethnic group exercising regional autonomy and other ethnic minorities. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent departments for recruitment and employment.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　上级人民政府指导民族自治地方制订人才开发规划，采取各种有效措施，积极培养使用实行区域自治的民族和其他民族的各级各类人才。  
国家积极采取措施，加大对少数民族和民族自治地方干部的培训力度，扩大干部培训机构和高等院校为民族自治地方培训干部与人才的规模，建立和完善民族自治地方与中央国家机关和经济相对发达地区干部交流制度。  
国家鼓励和支持各级各类人才到民族自治地方发展、创业，当地人民政府应当为他们提供优惠便利的工作和生活条件；对到边远、高寒等条件比较艰苦的民族自治地方工作的汉族和其他民族人才的家属和子女，在就业、就学等方面给予适当照顾。

**Article 29** The people's governments at higher levels guide ethnic autonomous areas in formulating plans for talent development, and adopt various effective measures to actively train and use talents at all levels and of all types from the ethnic group exercising regional autonomy and other ethnic groups.  
The State actively takes measures to increase the training of cadres from ethnic minorities and ethnic autonomous areas, expand the scale of training cadres and talents for ethnic autonomous areas by cadre training institutions and institutions of higher education, and establish and improve the system of exchange of cadres between ethnic autonomous areas and the central State organs and economically relatively developed areas.  
The State encourages and supports talents at all levels and of all types to develop and start businesses in ethnic autonomous areas. The local people's governments shall provide them with favorable and convenient working and living conditions; appropriate preferential treatment shall be given in terms of employment and schooling to the family members and children of Han and other ethnic minority talents who work in ethnic autonomous areas with harsh conditions such as remote and high-altitude areas.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府民族工作部门对本规定的执行情况实施监督检查，每年将监督检查的情况向同级人民政府报告，并提出意见和建议。

**Article 30** The ethnic affairs departments of the people's governments at all levels shall exercise supervision and inspection over the implementation of these Provisions, and report to the people's governments at the same level on the results of supervision and inspection annually, and put forward opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对违反国家财政制度、财务制度，挪用、克扣、截留国家财政用于民族自治地方经费的，责令限期归还被挪用、克扣、截留的经费，并依法对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Where anyone, in violation of the State financial system and fiscal system, misappropriates, withholds or intercepts the State fiscal funds used for ethnic autonomous areas, he/she shall be ordered to return the misappropriated, withheld or intercepted funds within a specified time limit, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级人民政府行政部门违反本规定，不依法履行职责，由其上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正。  
各级行政机关工作人员在执行本规定过程中，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 32** Where the administrative departments of the people's governments at all levels violate these Provisions and fail to perform their duties according to law, the administrative organs at higher levels or the supervisory organs shall order them to make corrections.  
Where functionaries of administrative organs at all levels, in the course of implementing these Provisions, abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本规定所称上级人民政府，是指民族自治地方的上级人民政府。

**Article 33** The term 'people's governments at higher levels' as used in these Provisions refers to the people's governments at the levels above the ethnic autonomous areas.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院有关部门、自治区和辖有自治州、自治县的省、直辖市人民政府在职权范围内，根据本规定制订具体办法，并将执行情况向国务院报告。

**Article 34** The relevant departments of the State Council, the people's governments of autonomous regions and the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have autonomous prefectures or autonomous counties shall, within the limits of their powers, formulate specific measures in accordance with these Provisions and report the implementation to the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本规定自2005年5月31日起施行。

**Article 35** These Provisions shall come into force as of May 31, 2005.
