# 扫除文盲工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1993-08-01

> (1988年2月5日国务院发布　根据1993年8月1日《国务院关于修改〈扫除文盲工作条例〉的决定》修正) 第一条　为了提高中华民族的文化素质，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，根据《中华人民共和国宪法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　凡年满十五周岁以上的文盲、半文盲公民，除丧失学习能力的以外，不分性别、民族、种族，均有接受扫除文盲教育的权利和义务。 对丧失学习能力者的鉴定，由县级人民政府教育行政部门组织进行。 第三条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对扫除文盲工作的领导，制订本地区的规划和措施，组织有关方面分工协作，具体实施，并按规划的要求完成扫除文盲任务。地方各级教育行政部门应当加强对扫除文盲工作的具体管理。 城乡基层单位的扫除文盲工作，在当地人民政府的领导下，由单位行政领导负责。 村民委员会、居民委员会应当积极协助组织扫除文盲工作。 第四条　扫除文盲与普及初等义务教育应当统筹规划，同步实施。已经实现基本普及初等义务教育，尚未完成扫除文盲任务的地方，应在五年以内实现基本扫除文盲的目标。 第五条　扫除文盲教育应当讲求实效，把学习文化同学习科学技术知识结合起来，在农村把学习文化同学习农业科学技术知识结合起来。 扫除文盲教育的形式应当因地制宜，灵活多样。 扫除文盲教育的教材，由省、自治区、直辖市教育行政部门审定。 第六条　扫除文盲教学应当使用全国通用的普通话。在少数民族地区可以使用本民族语言文字教学，也可以使用当地各民族通用的语言文字教学。 第七条　个人脱盲的标准是：农民识一千五百个汉字，企业和事业单位职工、城镇居民识二千个汉字；能够看懂浅显通俗的报刊、文章，能够记简单的帐目，能够书写简单的应用文。 用当地民族语言文字扫盲的地方，脱盲标准由省、自治区人民政府根据前款规定制定。 基本扫除文盲单位的标准是：其下属的每个单位一九四九年十月一日以后出生的年满十五周岁以上人口中的非文盲人数，除丧失学习能力的以外，在农村达到95%以上，在城镇达到98%以上；复盲率低于5%。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了提高中华民族的文化素质，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，根据《中华人民共和国宪法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Constitution of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of improving the cultural quality of the Chinese nation and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and socialist spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡年满十五周岁以上的文盲、半文盲公民，除丧失学习能力的以外，不分性别、民族、种族，均有接受扫除文盲教育的权利和义务。  
对丧失学习能力者的鉴定，由县级人民政府教育行政部门组织进行。

**Article 2** All illiterate and semi-literate citizens who have reached the age of fifteen or above, except those who have lost the ability to learn, regardless of gender, nationality or race, shall have the right and obligation to receive education for the elimination of illiteracy.  
The expert evaluation of persons who have lost the ability to learn shall be organized by the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对扫除文盲工作的领导，制订本地区的规划和措施，组织有关方面分工协作，具体实施，并按规划的要求完成扫除文盲任务。地方各级教育行政部门应当加强对扫除文盲工作的具体管理。  
城乡基层单位的扫除文盲工作，在当地人民政府的领导下，由单位行政领导负责。  
村民委员会、居民委员会应当积极协助组织扫除文盲工作。

**Article 3** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over the work of eliminating illiteracy, formulate plans and measures for their respective regions, organize relevant departments to divide work and cooperate with each other, implement concrete actions, and accomplish the task of eliminating illiteracy in accordance with the requirements of the plans. Local education administrative departments at all levels shall strengthen the concrete management of the work of eliminating illiteracy.  
The work of eliminating illiteracy in urban and rural grassroots units shall be under the leadership of the local people's government and be the responsibility of the administrative head of the unit.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall actively assist in organizing the work of eliminating illiteracy.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　扫除文盲与普及初等义务教育应当统筹规划，同步实施。已经实现基本普及初等义务教育，尚未完成扫除文盲任务的地方，应在五年以内实现基本扫除文盲的目标。

**Article 4** The elimination of illiteracy and the popularization of compulsory primary education shall be planned as a whole and implemented simultaneously. In areas where compulsory primary education has been basically popularized but the task of eliminating illiteracy has not yet been completed, the goal of basically eliminating illiteracy shall be achieved within five years.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　扫除文盲教育应当讲求实效，把学习文化同学习科学技术知识结合起来，在农村把学习文化同学习农业科学技术知识结合起来。  
扫除文盲教育的形式应当因地制宜，灵活多样。  
扫除文盲教育的教材，由省、自治区、直辖市教育行政部门审定。

**Article 5** Education for the elimination of illiteracy shall stress practical results and combine the learning of culture with the learning of scientific and technological knowledge; in rural areas, the learning of culture shall be combined with the learning of agricultural science and technology.  
The forms of education for the elimination of illiteracy shall be adapted to local conditions and be flexible and diverse.  
The teaching materials for education for the elimination of illiteracy shall be examined and approved by the education administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　扫除文盲教学应当使用全国通用的普通话。在少数民族地区可以使用本民族语言文字教学，也可以使用当地各民族通用的语言文字教学。

**Article 6** Putonghua, the common speech of the whole country, shall be used in the teaching for the elimination of illiteracy. In areas inhabited by minority nationalities, teaching may be conducted in the spoken and written languages of the minority nationalities, or in the spoken and written languages commonly used by the local nationalities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　个人脱盲的标准是：农民识一千五百个汉字，企业和事业单位职工、城镇居民识二千个汉字；能够看懂浅显通俗的报刊、文章，能够记简单的帐目，能够书写简单的应用文。  
用当地民族语言文字扫盲的地方，脱盲标准由省、自治区人民政府根据前款规定制定。  
基本扫除文盲单位的标准是：其下属的每个单位一九四九年十月一日以后出生的年满十五周岁以上人口中的非文盲人数，除丧失学习能力的以外，在农村达到95%以上，在城镇达到98%以上；复盲率低于5%。  
基本扫除文盲的单位应当普及初等义务教育。

**Article 7** The standard for an individual to become literate is: farmers shall be able to recognize 1,500 Chinese characters; workers and staff members of enterprises and institutions and urban residents shall be able to recognize 2,000 Chinese characters; they shall be able to read simple and popular newspapers, periodicals and articles, keep simple accounts, and write simple practical writings.  
In places where literacy work is carried out in the local minority languages, the standard for becoming literate shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces and autonomous regions in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
The standard for a unit to have basically eliminated illiteracy is: among the population born after October 1, 1949 and having reached the age of fifteen or above in each subordinate unit, the number of literate persons, except those who have lost the ability to learn, shall reach over 95% in rural areas and over 98% in urban areas; the rate of return to illiteracy shall be lower than 5%.  
A unit that has basically eliminated illiteracy shall popularize compulsory primary education.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　扫除文盲实行验收制度。扫除文盲的学员由所在乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处或同级企业、事业单位组织考核，对达到脱盲标准的，发给“脱盲证书”。  
基本扫除文盲的市、县(区)，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府验收；乡(镇)、城市的街道，由上一级人民政府验收；企业、事业单位，由所在地人民政府验收。对符合标准的，发给“基本扫除文盲单位证书”。

**Article 8** An acceptance system shall be implemented for the elimination of illiteracy. Trainees in the elimination of illiteracy shall be examined by the people's government of the township (town), the sub-district office of the city, or the enterprise or institution at the same level; those who meet the standard for becoming literate shall be issued a Certificate of Becoming Literate.  
Cities and counties (districts) that have basically eliminated illiteracy shall be accepted by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; townships (towns) and urban sub-districts shall be accepted by the people's government at the next higher level; enterprises and institutions shall be accepted by the local people's government. Those that meet the standard shall be issued a Certificate of Unit Having Basically Eliminated Illiteracy.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级人民政府应当制定措施，督促基本扫除文盲的单位制订规划，继续扫除剩余文盲。在农村，应当积极办好乡(镇)、村文化技术学校，采取农科教相结合等多种形式巩固扫盲成果。

**Article 9** Local people's governments at all levels shall formulate measures to urge units that have basically eliminated illiteracy to formulate plans and continue to eliminate the remaining illiterates. In rural areas, cultural and technical schools at the township (town) and village levels shall be actively run well, and various forms such as combining agriculture, science and education shall be adopted to consolidate the results of literacy work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　扫除文盲教师由乡(镇)、街道、村和企业、事业单位聘用，并给予相应报酬。  
当地普通学校、文化馆(站)等有关方面均应积极承担扫除文盲的教学工作。  
鼓励社会上一切有扫除文盲教育能力的人员参与扫除文盲教学活动。

**Article 10** Teachers for the elimination of illiteracy shall be employed by townships (towns), sub-districts, villages, enterprises and institutions, and shall be given corresponding remuneration.  
Local ordinary schools, cultural centers (stations) and other relevant parties shall all actively undertake the teaching work for the elimination of illiteracy.  
All persons in society who have the ability to teach for the elimination of illiteracy are encouraged to participate in the teaching activities for the elimination of illiteracy.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地方各级人民政府应当在教育事业编制中，充实县、乡(镇)成人教育专职工作人员，加强对农村扫除文盲工作的管理。

**Article 11** Local people's governments at all levels shall, within the staffing for educational undertakings, strengthen the full-time staff for adult education at the county and township (town) levels, and strengthen the management of the work of eliminating illiteracy in rural areas.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　扫除文盲教育所需经费采取多渠道办法解决。除下列各项外，由地方各级人民政府给予必要的补助：  
(一)由乡(镇)人民政府、街道办事处组织村民委员会或有关单位自筹；  
(二)企业、事业单位的扫除文盲经费，在职工教育经费中列支；  
(三)农村征收的教育事业费附加，应当安排一部分用于农村扫除文盲教育。  
各级教育行政部门在扫除文盲工作中，培训专职工作人员和教师，编写教材和读物，开展教研活动，以及交流经验和奖励先进等所需费用，在教育事业费中列支。  
鼓励社会力量和个人自愿资助扫除文盲教育。

**Article 12** The funds needed for education for the elimination of illiteracy shall be raised through multiple channels. In addition to the items listed below, local people's governments at all levels shall provide necessary subsidies:  
(1) Funds raised by villagers' committees or relevant units organized by the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office;  
(2) Funds for the elimination of illiteracy in enterprises and institutions shall be listed under the expenses for staff and workers' education;  
(3) A portion of the surcharge for educational undertakings collected in rural areas shall be allocated for education for the elimination of illiteracy in rural areas.  
The expenses needed by education administrative departments at all levels in the work of eliminating illiteracy for training full-time staff and teachers, compiling teaching materials and reading materials, conducting teaching and research activities, and exchanging experience and rewarding advanced units and individuals shall be listed under the expenses for educational undertakings.  
Social forces and individuals are encouraged to voluntarily fund education for the elimination of illiteracy.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　扫除文盲工作实行行政领导责任制。扫盲任务应当列为县、乡(镇)、城市街道和企业、事业单位行政负责人的职责，作为考核工作成绩的一项重要内容。  
对未按规划完成扫除文盲任务的单位，由地方各级人民政府处理。  
地方各级人民政府应定期向上一级人民政府报告扫除文盲工作的情况，接受检查、监督。

**Article 13** The work of eliminating illiteracy shall implement a system of administrative leadership responsibility. The task of eliminating illiteracy shall be listed as a duty of the administrative heads of counties, townships (towns), urban sub-districts, enterprises and institutions, and shall be an important item in the assessment of their work performance.  
Units that fail to accomplish the task of eliminating illiteracy according to the plan shall be dealt with by the local people's governments at all levels.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall regularly report to the people's government at the next higher level on the situation of the work of eliminating illiteracy and shall accept inspection and supervision.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家教育委员会定期对在扫除文盲工作中做出突出贡献的单位或个人颁发“扫盲奖”。地方各级人民政府也应当对在扫除文盲工作中成绩显著的单位或个人予以表彰、奖励。  
对在规定期限内具备学习条件而不参加扫除文盲学习的适龄文盲、半文盲公民，当地人民政府应当进行批评教育，并采取切实有效的措施组织入学，使其达到脱盲标准。

**Article 14** The State Education Commission shall periodically issue the Literacy Award to units or individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the work of eliminating illiteracy. Local people's governments at all levels shall also commend and reward units or individuals that have achieved remarkable success in the work of eliminating illiteracy.  
For illiterate or semi-literate citizens of appropriate age who are capable of learning but do not participate in studies for the elimination of illiteracy within the prescribed time limit, the local people's government shall conduct criticism and education, and take practical and effective measures to organize them to enroll in school so that they may meet the standard for becoming literate.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例，结合本地实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 15** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Regulations and in light of the actual local conditions, formulate measures for implementation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例由国家教育委员会负责解释。

**Article 16** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 17** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
