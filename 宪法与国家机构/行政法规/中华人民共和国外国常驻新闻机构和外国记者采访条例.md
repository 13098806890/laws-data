# 中华人民共和国外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者采访条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2008-10-17  
**生效日期**：2008-10-17

> (2008年10月17日国务院第31次常务会议通过　2008年10月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第537号公布　自2008年10月17日起施行) 第一条　为了便于外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者在中华人民共和国境内依法采访报道，促进国际交往和信息传播，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称外国常驻新闻机构，是指外国新闻机构在中国境内设立、从事新闻采访报道业务的分支机构。 本条例所称外国记者包括外国常驻记者和外国短期采访记者。外国常驻记者是指由外国新闻机构派遣，在中国境内常驻6个月以上、从事新闻采访报道业务的职业记者；外国短期采访记者是指在中国境内停留期不超过6个月、从事新闻采访报道业务的职业记者。 第三条　中国实行对外开放的基本国策，依法保障外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者的合法权益，并为其依法从事新闻采访报道业务提供便利。 第四条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者应当遵守中国法律、法规和规章，遵守新闻职业道德，客观、公正地进行采访报道，不得进行与其机构性质或者记者身份不符的活动。 第五条　中华人民共和国外交部(以下简称外交部)主管外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者事务。国务院新闻办公室和其他部门在各自职责范围内负责外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者有关事务。 地方人民政府外事部门受外交部委托，办理本行政区域内外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者事务。地方人民政府新闻办公室和其他部门在各自职责范围内负责本行政区域内外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者有关事务。 第六条　外国新闻机构在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构、向中国派遣常驻记者，应当经外交部批准。 第七条　外国新闻机构申请在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构，应当直接或者通过中国驻外使领馆向外交部提交以下材料： (一)由该新闻机构总部主要负责人签署的书面申请； (二)该新闻机构情况介绍； (三)拟设立机构的负责人、拟派遣的常驻记者以及工作人员情况介绍； (四)该新闻机构在所在国设立的证明文件副本。 第八条　在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构的申请经批准后，该常驻新闻机构负责人应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部办理外国常驻新闻机构证；其中，驻北京市以外地区的常驻新闻机构，其负责人应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门办理外国常驻新闻机构证。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了便于外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者在中华人民共和国境内依法采访报道，促进国际交往和信息传播，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of facilitating foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists in conducting news interviews and reporting within the territory of the People's Republic of China according to law, and promoting international exchanges and information dissemination.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称外国常驻新闻机构，是指外国新闻机构在中国境内设立、从事新闻采访报道业务的分支机构。  
本条例所称外国记者包括外国常驻记者和外国短期采访记者。外国常驻记者是指由外国新闻机构派遣，在中国境内常驻6个月以上、从事新闻采访报道业务的职业记者；外国短期采访记者是指在中国境内停留期不超过6个月、从事新闻采访报道业务的职业记者。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "foreign permanent news agency" means a branch established within the territory of China by a foreign news organization to engage in news interview and reporting business.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "foreign journalist" includes foreign permanent correspondents and foreign short-term visiting journalists. "Foreign permanent correspondent" means a professional journalist dispatched by a foreign news organization to be stationed in China for more than six months to engage in news interview and reporting business; "foreign short-term visiting journalist" means a professional journalist who stays in China for no more than six months to engage in news interview and reporting business.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国实行对外开放的基本国策，依法保障外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者的合法权益，并为其依法从事新闻采访报道业务提供便利。

**Article 3** China implements the basic state policy of opening up to the outside world, protects the legitimate rights and interests of foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists according to law, and provides conveniences for them to engage in news interview and reporting business according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者应当遵守中国法律、法规和规章，遵守新闻职业道德，客观、公正地进行采访报道，不得进行与其机构性质或者记者身份不符的活动。

**Article 4** Foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists shall abide by Chinese laws, regulations and rules, comply with journalism ethics, conduct interviews and reporting objectively and impartially, and shall not engage in activities inconsistent with the nature of their agencies or their status as journalists.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国外交部(以下简称外交部)主管外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者事务。国务院新闻办公室和其他部门在各自职责范围内负责外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者有关事务。  
地方人民政府外事部门受外交部委托，办理本行政区域内外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者事务。地方人民政府新闻办公室和其他部门在各自职责范围内负责本行政区域内外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者有关事务。

**Article 5** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs) shall be in charge of matters concerning foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists. The State Council Information Office and other departments shall be responsible for relevant affairs concerning foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists within their respective functions and duties.  
Foreign affairs departments of local people's governments, as entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, shall handle matters concerning foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists within their respective administrative regions. Information offices and other departments of local people's governments shall be responsible for relevant affairs concerning foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists within their respective administrative regions and within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国新闻机构在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构、向中国派遣常驻记者，应当经外交部批准。

**Article 6** A foreign news organization that establishes a permanent news agency within the territory of China or dispatches permanent correspondents to China shall obtain approval from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国新闻机构申请在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构，应当直接或者通过中国驻外使领馆向外交部提交以下材料：  
(一)由该新闻机构总部主要负责人签署的书面申请；  
(二)该新闻机构情况介绍；  
(三)拟设立机构的负责人、拟派遣的常驻记者以及工作人员情况介绍；  
(四)该新闻机构在所在国设立的证明文件副本。

**Article 7** A foreign news organization applying to establish a permanent news agency within the territory of China shall submit the following materials directly or through Chinese embassies or consulates abroad to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs:  
(1) a written application signed by the principal responsible person of the headquarters of the news organization;  
(2) an introduction to the news organization;  
(3) an introduction to the person in charge of the proposed agency, the proposed permanent correspondents and the staff members;  
(4) a copy of the certification document of the establishment of the news organization in its home country.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在中国境内设立常驻新闻机构的申请经批准后，该常驻新闻机构负责人应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部办理外国常驻新闻机构证；其中，驻北京市以外地区的常驻新闻机构，其负责人应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门办理外国常驻新闻机构证。

**Article 8** After the application for establishing a permanent news agency within the territory of China is approved, the person in charge of the permanent news agency shall, within seven working days from the date of arrival in China, go through the formalities for obtaining the Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with his or her passport; for a permanent news agency stationed in a locality other than Beijing, its person in charge shall, within seven working days from the date of arrival in China, go through the formalities for obtaining the Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate at the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with his or her passport.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国新闻机构申请向中国派遣常驻记者，应当直接或者通过中国驻外使领馆向外交部提交以下材料：  
(一)由该新闻机构总部负责人签署的书面申请；  
(二)拟派遣记者情况介绍；  
(三)拟派遣记者在所在国从事职业活动的证明文件副本。  
两个以上外国新闻机构派遣同一名常驻记者的，应当依照前款规定分别办理申请手续，并在各自的书面申请中注明该记者所兼职的外国新闻机构。

**Article 9** A foreign news organization applying to dispatch permanent correspondents to China shall submit the following materials directly or through Chinese embassies or consulates abroad to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs:  
(1) a written application signed by the person in charge of the headquarters of the news organization;  
(2) an introduction to the proposed correspondent;  
(3) a copy of the certification document proving that the proposed correspondent engages in professional activities in his or her home country.  
Where two or more foreign news organizations dispatch the same permanent correspondent, they shall separately go through the application formalities in accordance with the preceding paragraph, and shall indicate in their respective written applications the foreign news organizations in which the correspondent holds concurrent posts.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　向中国派遣常驻记者的申请经批准后，被派遣的外国记者应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部办理外国常驻记者证；其中，驻北京市以外地区的常驻记者，应当自抵达中国之日起7个工作日内，持本人护照到外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门办理外国常驻记者证。  
外国记者办理外国常驻记者证后，应当到居住地公安机关办理居留证。

**Article 10** After the application for dispatching a permanent correspondent to China is approved, the dispatched foreign journalist shall, within seven working days from the date of arrival in China, go through the formalities for obtaining the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with his or her passport; for a permanent correspondent stationed in a locality other than Beijing, the correspondent shall, within seven working days from the date of arrival in China, go through the formalities for obtaining the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card at the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with his or her passport.  
After obtaining the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card, a foreign journalist shall apply for a residence permit at the public security organ of his or her place of residence.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国常驻新闻机构变更机构名称、常驻地区等事项，应当向外交部提交书面申请，经批准后办理变更手续。  
外国常驻新闻机构变更负责人、办公地址等事项，应当在变更后7个工作日内书面告知外交部；其中，驻北京市以外地区的常驻新闻机构变更负责人、办公地址等事项，应当在变更后7个工作日内书面告知外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门。

**Article 11** A foreign permanent news agency that changes its name, stationing locality or other matters shall submit a written application to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and go through the alteration formalities after approval.  
A foreign permanent news agency that changes its person in charge, office address or other matters shall inform the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing within seven working days after the change; for a permanent news agency stationed in a locality other than Beijing that changes its person in charge, office address or other matters, it shall inform the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing within seven working days after the change.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国常驻记者证有效期届满需要延期的，外国常驻记者应当提前向外交部或者外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门提出申请，办理延期手续；逾期不办理的，视为自动放弃外国常驻记者资格，其外国常驻记者证将被注销。

**Article 12** If a Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card needs to be renewed upon expiration of its validity, the foreign permanent correspondent shall apply in advance to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and go through the renewal formalities; if the correspondent fails to do so within the time limit, he or she shall be deemed to have voluntarily abandoned the status of foreign permanent correspondent, and his or her Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国常驻新闻机构拟终止业务的，应当在终止业务30日前告知外交部，并自终止业务之日起7个工作日内到外交部或者外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门办理外国常驻新闻机构证及其常驻记者的外国常驻记者证注销手续。  
外国常驻新闻机构连续10个月以上无常驻记者，视为该机构已经自动终止业务，其外国常驻新闻机构证将被注销。  
外国常驻记者在中国境内居留时间每年累计少于6个月的，其外国常驻记者证将被注销。  
外国常驻新闻机构应当在其常驻记者离任前到外交部或者外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门办理该记者外国常驻记者证注销手续。

**Article 13** If a foreign permanent news agency intends to terminate its business, it shall inform the Ministry of Foreign Affairs 30 days prior to the termination of business, and shall go through the cancellation formalities for the Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate and the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Cards of its permanent correspondents at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs within seven working days from the date of termination of business.  
If a foreign permanent news agency has no permanent correspondent for more than ten consecutive months, the agency shall be deemed to have automatically terminated its business, and its Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate shall be cancelled.  
If the cumulative period of stay in China of a foreign permanent correspondent is less than six months per year, his or her Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card shall be cancelled.  
A foreign permanent news agency shall go through the cancellation formalities for the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card of its permanent correspondent at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before the correspondent leaves his or her post.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国常驻新闻机构证、外国常驻记者证被注销后，应当向社会公布。  
外国常驻记者证被注销的记者，其记者签证自注销之日起10日后自动失效。  
外国常驻记者证被注销的记者，应当自外国常驻记者证被注销之日起10日内持相关证明，到居住地公安机关申请办理签证或者居留证变更登记。

**Article 14** After the Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate or the Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card is cancelled, it shall be announced to the public.  
For a journalist whose Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card has been cancelled, his or her journalist visa shall automatically cease to have effect 10 days after the cancellation.  
A journalist whose Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card has been cancelled shall, within 10 days from the date of cancellation, apply for alteration registration of his or her visa or residence permit at the public security organ of his or her place of residence with relevant certifications.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国记者常驻或者短期采访，应当向中国驻外使领馆或者外交部授权的签证机构申请办理记者签证。

**Article 15** A foreign journalist who will be stationed in China or conduct short-term interviews shall apply for a journalist visa at Chinese embassies or consulates abroad or at visa agencies authorized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国记者随国家元首、政府首脑、议长、王室成员或者高级政府官员来中国访问，应当由该国外交部或者相关部门向中国驻外使领馆或者外交部授权的签证机构统一申请办理记者签证。

**Article 16** Where foreign journalists accompany heads of state, heads of government, speakers of parliament, members of royal families or senior government officials on visits to China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of that country or relevant departments shall uniformly apply for journalist visas on their behalf at Chinese embassies or consulates abroad or at visa agencies authorized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国记者在中国境内采访，需征得被采访单位和个人的同意。  
外国记者采访时应当携带并出示外国常驻记者证或者短期采访记者签证。

**Article 17** Foreign journalists conducting interviews within the territory of China shall obtain the consent of the units and individuals being interviewed.  
Foreign journalists shall carry and present their Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card or short-term visiting journalist visa when conducting interviews.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者可以通过外事服务单位聘用中国公民从事辅助工作。外事服务单位由外交部或者外交部委托的地方人民政府外事部门指定。

**Article 18** Foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists may employ Chinese citizens to engage in auxiliary work through foreign affairs service units. Foreign affairs service units shall be designated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the foreign affairs department of the local people's government entrusted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者因采访报道需要，在依法履行报批手续后，可以临时进口、设置和使用无线电通信设备。

**Article 19** Foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists, if necessary for interviews and reporting, may temporarily import, install and use radio communication equipment after going through the approval formalities according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　外国人未取得或者未持有有效的外国常驻记者证或者短期采访记者签证，在中国境内从事新闻采访报道活动的，由公安机关责令其停止新闻采访报道活动，并依照有关法律予以处理。

**Article 20** If a foreigner who has not obtained or does not hold a valid Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card or short-term visiting journalist visa engages in news interview and reporting activities within the territory of China, the public security organ shall order him or her to stop such activities and shall handle the matter in accordance with relevant laws.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者违反本条例规定的，由外交部予以警告，责令暂停或者终止其业务活动；情节严重的，吊销其外国常驻新闻机构证、外国常驻记者证或者记者签证。

**Article 21** If a foreign permanent news agency or a foreign journalist violates the provisions of these Regulations, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall give a warning, order the suspension or termination of its or his or her activities; if the circumstances are serious, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall revoke its or his or her Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate, Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card or journalist visa.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者违反中国其他法律、法规和规章规定的，依法处理；情节严重的，由外交部吊销其外国常驻新闻机构证、外国常驻记者证或者记者签证。

**Article 22** If a foreign permanent news agency or a foreign journalist violates other Chinese laws, regulations and rules, the matter shall be handled according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall revoke its or his or her Foreign Permanent News Agency Certificate, Foreign Permanent Correspondent Card or journalist visa.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自2008年10月17日起施行。1990年1月19日国务院公布的《外国记者和外国常驻新闻机构管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 17, 2008. The Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Journalists and Foreign Permanent News Agencies promulgated by the State Council on January 19, 1990 shall be abolished simultaneously.
