# 城市道路管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-24  
**生效日期**：1996-10-01

> (1996年6月4日中华人民共和国国务院令第198号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2019年3月24日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强城市道路管理，保障城市道路完好，充分发挥城市道路功能，促进城市经济和社会发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称城市道路，是指城市供车辆、行人通行的，具备一定技术条件的道路、桥梁及其附属设施。 第三条　本条例适用于城市道路规划、建设、养护、维修和路政管理。 第四条　城市道路管理实行统一规划、配套建设、协调发展和建设、养护、管理并重的原则。 第五条　国家鼓励和支持城市道路科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市道路管理的科学技术水平。 第六条　国务院建设行政主管部门主管全国城市道路管理工作。 省、自治区人民政府城市建设行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的城市道路管理工作。 县级以上城市人民政府市政工程行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的城市道路管理工作。 第二章　规划和建设 第七条　县级以上城市人民政府应当组织市政工程、城市规划、公安交通等部门，根据城市总体规划编制城市道路发展规划。 市政工程行政主管部门应当根据城市道路发展规划，制定城市道路年度建设计划，经城市人民政府批准后实施。 第八条　城市道路建设资金可以按照国家有关规定，采取政府投资、集资、国内外贷款、国有土地有偿使用收入、发行债券等多种渠道筹集。 第九条　城市道路的建设应当符合城市道路技术规范。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城市道路管理，保障城市道路完好，充分发挥城市道路功能，促进城市经济和社会发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in order to strengthen the management of urban roads, ensure the integrity of urban roads, fully utilize the functions of urban roads, and promote urban economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称城市道路，是指城市供车辆、行人通行的，具备一定技术条件的道路、桥梁及其附属设施。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term "urban roads" means roads, bridges, and their ancillary facilities within urban areas that are provided for the passage of vehicles and pedestrians and that meet certain technical conditions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于城市道路规划、建设、养护、维修和路政管理。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to the planning, construction, maintenance, repair, and road administration of urban roads.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　城市道路管理实行统一规划、配套建设、协调发展和建设、养护、管理并重的原则。

**Article 4** The management of urban roads shall implement the principles of unified planning, coordinated construction, balanced development, and equal emphasis on construction, maintenance, and management.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持城市道路科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市道路管理的科学技术水平。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and support scientific and technological research on urban roads, promote advanced technologies, and raise the scientific and technical level of urban road management.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建设行政主管部门主管全国城市道路管理工作。  
省、自治区人民政府城市建设行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的城市道路管理工作。  
县级以上城市人民政府市政工程行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的城市道路管理工作。

**Article 6** The competent administrative department of construction under the State Council shall be responsible for urban road management nationwide.  
The competent administrative department of urban construction under the people's governments of provinces and autonomous regions shall be responsible for urban road management within their respective administrative areas.  
The competent administrative department of municipal engineering under the people's governments of cities at or above the county level shall be responsible for urban road management within their respective administrative areas.

### 第二章　规划和建设

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上城市人民政府应当组织市政工程、城市规划、公安交通等部门，根据城市总体规划编制城市道路发展规划。  
市政工程行政主管部门应当根据城市道路发展规划，制定城市道路年度建设计划，经城市人民政府批准后实施。

**Article 7** The people's governments of cities at or above the county level shall organize the departments of municipal engineering, urban planning, and public security traffic, among others, to formulate urban road development plans in accordance with the overall urban plan.  
The competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall, in accordance with the urban road development plans, formulate annual urban road construction plans, which shall be implemented after approval by the people's government of the city.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　城市道路建设资金可以按照国家有关规定，采取政府投资、集资、国内外贷款、国有土地有偿使用收入、发行债券等多种渠道筹集。

**Article 8** Funds for urban road construction may be raised through multiple channels, including government investment, pooled funds, domestic and foreign loans, income from the compensated use of State-owned land, and issuance of bonds, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市道路的建设应当符合城市道路技术规范。

**Article 9** The construction of urban roads shall comply with the technical specifications for urban roads.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　政府投资建设城市道路的，应当根据城市道路发展规划和年度建设计划，由市政工程行政主管部门组织建设。  
单位投资建设城市道路的，应当符合城市道路发展规划。  
城市住宅小区、开发区内的道路建设，应当分别纳入住宅小区、开发区的开发建设计划配套建设。

**Article 10** Where the government invests in the construction of urban roads, the construction shall be organized by the competent administrative department of municipal engineering in accordance with urban road development plans and annual construction plans.  
Where a unit invests in the construction of urban roads, such construction shall comply with urban road development plans.  
The construction of roads within urban residential communities and development zones shall be incorporated into the development and construction plans of such residential communities and development zones respectively and shall be constructed concomitantly.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励国内外企业和其他组织以及个人按照城市道路发展规划，投资建设城市道路。

**Article 11** The State shall encourage domestic and foreign enterprises, other organizations, and individuals to invest in the construction of urban roads in accordance with urban road development plans.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　城市供水、排水、燃气、热力、供电、通信、消防等依附于城市道路的各种管线、杆线等设施的建设计划，应当与城市道路发展规划和年度建设计划相协调，坚持先地下、后地上的施工原则，与城市道路同步建设。

**Article 12** Construction plans for various pipelines, poles, and other facilities that are attached to urban roads, such as those for urban water supply, drainage, gas, heating, power supply, telecommunications, and fire protection, shall be coordinated with urban road development plans and annual construction plans, adhere to the construction principle of "underground first, aboveground later," and be built concurrently with the urban roads.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　新建的城市道路与铁路干线相交的，应当根据需要在城市规划中预留立体交通设施的建设位置。  
城市道路与铁路相交的道口建设应当符合国家有关技术规范，并根据需要逐步建设立体交通设施。建设立体交通设施所需投资，按照国家规定由有关部门协商确定。

**Article 13** Where newly constructed urban roads intersect with main railway lines, sites for the construction of grade-separated traffic facilities shall be reserved in the urban plan as needed.  
The construction of crossings where urban roads intersect with railways shall comply with the relevant technical specifications of the State, and grade-separated traffic facilities shall be progressively constructed as needed. The investment required for the construction of grade-separated traffic facilities shall be determined through consultation by the relevant departments in accordance with State provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设跨越江河的桥梁和隧道，应当符合国家规定的防洪、通航标准和其他有关技术规范。

**Article 14** The construction of bridges and tunnels spanning rivers shall comply with the national standards for flood control, navigation, and other relevant technical specifications.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上城市人民政府应当有计划地按照城市道路技术规范改建、拓宽城市道路和公路的结合部，公路行政主管部门可以按照国家有关规定在资金上给予补助。

**Article 15** The people's governments of cities at or above the county level shall, in a planned manner, reconstruct and widen the junctions between urban roads and highways in accordance with the technical specifications for urban roads. The competent administrative department of highways may provide financial subsidies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　承担城市道路设计、施工的单位，应当具有相应的资质等级，并按照资质等级承担相应的城市道路的设计、施工任务。

**Article 16** Units undertaking the design and construction of urban roads shall possess the corresponding qualification grades and shall undertake the design and construction tasks of urban roads in accordance with such qualification grades.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市道路的设计、施工，应当严格执行国家和地方规定的城市道路设计、施工的技术规范。  
城市道路施工，实行工程质量监督制度。  
城市道路工程竣工，经验收合格后，方可交付使用；未经验收或者验收不合格的，不得交付使用。

**Article 17** The design and construction of urban roads shall strictly implement the technical specifications for urban road design and construction as prescribed by the State and local authorities.  
For the construction of urban roads, a project quality supervision system shall be implemented.  
Urban road projects shall be delivered for use only after they are completed and have passed acceptance inspection; those that have not undergone acceptance inspection or have failed such inspection shall not be delivered for use.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　城市道路实行工程质量保修制度。城市道路的保修期为1年，自交付使用之日起计算。保修期内出现工程质量问题，由有关责任单位负责保修。

**Article 18** A project quality warranty system shall be implemented for urban roads. The warranty period for urban roads shall be one year, calculated from the date of delivery for use. If any project quality problems arise within the warranty period, the responsible unit shall be liable for the warranty.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　市政工程行政主管部门对利用贷款或者集资建设的大型桥梁、隧道等，可以在一定期限内向过往车辆(军用车辆除外)收取通行费，用于偿还贷款或者集资款，不得挪作他用。  
收取通行费的范围和期限，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 19** The competent administrative department of municipal engineering may, within a certain period, collect tolls from passing vehicles (excluding military vehicles) for large bridges, tunnels, etc., constructed with loans or pooled funds, which shall be used to repay the loans or pooled funds and shall not be appropriated for any other purpose.  
The scope and duration of toll collection shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　养护和维修

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　市政工程行政主管部门对其组织建设和管理的城市道路，按照城市道路的等级、数量及养护和维修的定额，逐年核定养护、维修经费，统一安排养护、维修资金。

**Article 20** The competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall, for the urban roads it organizes to construct and manage, annually verify the maintenance and repair funds based on the grades, quantities, and maintenance and repair norms of urban roads, and shall arrange the maintenance and repair funds in a unified manner.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承担城市道路养护、维修的单位，应当严格执行城市道路养护、维修的技术规范，定期对城市道路进行养护、维修，确保养护、维修工程的质量。  
市政工程行政主管部门负责对养护、维修工程的质量进行监督检查，保障城市道路完好。

**Article 21** Units undertaking the maintenance and repair of urban roads shall strictly implement the technical specifications for urban road maintenance and repair, regularly maintain and repair urban roads, and ensure the quality of maintenance and repair projects.  
The competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall be responsible for supervising and inspecting the quality of maintenance and repair projects to ensure the integrity of urban roads.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　市政工程行政主管部门组织建设和管理的道路，由其委托的城市道路养护、维修单位负责养护、维修。单位投资建设和管理的道路，由投资建设的单位或者其委托的单位负责养护、维修。城市住宅小区、开发区内的道路，由建设单位或者其委托的单位负责养护、维修。

**Article 22** Roads organized for construction and managed by the competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall be maintained and repaired by the urban road maintenance and repair units entrusted by it. Roads invested in and managed by a unit shall be maintained and repaired by the investing unit or the unit entrusted by it. Roads within urban residential communities and development zones shall be maintained and repaired by the construction unit or the unit entrusted by it.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　设在城市道路上的各类管线的检查井、箱盖或者城市道路附属设施，应当符合城市道路养护规范。因缺损影响交通和安全时，有关产权单位应当及时补缺或者修复。

**Article 23** The inspection wells, covers, or ancillary facilities of various pipelines located on urban roads shall comply with the urban road maintenance specifications. Where defects affect traffic and safety, the relevant property rights units shall promptly replace or repair them.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　城市道路的养护、维修工程应当按照规定的期限修复竣工，并在养护、维修工程施工现场设置明显标志和安全防围设施，保障行人和交通车辆安全。

**Article 24** Maintenance and repair projects for urban roads shall be completed within the prescribed time limit, and conspicuous signs and safety guard facilities shall be set up at the construction site of maintenance and repair projects to ensure the safety of pedestrians and traffic vehicles.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　城市道路养护、维修的专用车辆应当使用统一标志；执行任务时，在保证交通安全畅通的情况下，不受行驶路线和行驶方向的限制。

**Article 25** Special vehicles for urban road maintenance and repair shall use uniform markings; when performing tasks, they shall not be subject to restrictions on travel routes or directions, provided that traffic safety and smoothness are ensured.

### 第四章　路政管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　市政工程行政主管部门执行路政管理的人员执行公务，应当按照有关规定佩戴标志，持证上岗。

**Article 26** Personnel of the competent administrative department of municipal engineering who perform road administration duties shall, when carrying out official duties, wear identification badges in accordance with the relevant provisions and hold certificates for their posts.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城市道路范围内禁止下列行为：  
(一)擅自占用或者挖掘城市道路；  
(二)履带车、铁轮车或者超重、超高、超长车辆擅自在城市道路上行驶；  
(三)机动车在桥梁或者非指定的城市道路上试刹车；  
(四)擅自在城市道路上建设建筑物、构筑物；  
(五)在桥梁上架设压力在4公斤/平方厘米(0.4兆帕)以上的煤气管道、10千伏以上的高压电力线和其他易燃易爆管线；  
(六)擅自在桥梁或者路灯设施上设置广告牌或者其他挂浮物；  
(七)其他损害、侵占城市道路的行为。

**Article 27** The following acts shall be prohibited within the scope of urban roads:  
(1) Unauthorized occupation or excavation of urban roads;  
(2) Driving tracked vehicles, iron-wheeled vehicles, or overweight, overlength, or overheight vehicles on urban roads without authorization;  
(3) Testing brakes of motor vehicles on bridges or on non-designated urban roads;  
(4) Unauthorized construction of buildings or structures on urban roads;  
(5) Installing gas pipelines with a pressure exceeding 4 kg per square centimeter (0.4 MPa), high-voltage power lines above 10 kV, or other flammable or explosive pipelines on bridges;  
(6) Unauthorized installation of billboards or other hanging objects on bridges or street lighting facilities;  
(7) Other acts that damage or encroach upon urban roads.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　履带车、铁轮车或者超重、超高、超长车辆需要在城市道路上行驶的，事先须征得市政工程行政主管部门同意，并按照公安交通管理部门指定的时间、路线行驶。  
军用车辆执行任务需要在城市道路上行驶的，可以不受前款限制，但是应当按照规定采取安全保护措施。

**Article 28** Where tracked vehicles, iron-wheeled vehicles, or overweight, overlength, or overheight vehicles need to travel on urban roads, consent must be obtained in advance from the competent administrative department of municipal engineering, and they shall travel at the times and on the routes designated by the public security traffic control department.  
Where military vehicles need to travel on urban roads to perform tasks, they may be exempted from the restrictions of the preceding paragraph, but shall take safety protection measures in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　依附于城市道路建设各种管线、杆线等设施的，应当经市政工程行政主管部门批准，方可建设。

**Article 29** The construction of various pipelines, poles, and other facilities attached to urban roads shall be subject to approval by the competent administrative department of municipal engineering before construction may proceed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未经市政工程行政主管部门和公安交通管理部门批准，任何单位或者个人不得占用或者挖掘城市道路。

**Article 30** No unit or individual may occupy or excavate urban roads without the approval of the competent administrative department of municipal engineering and the public security traffic control department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　因特殊情况需要临时占用城市道路的，须经市政工程行政主管部门和公安交通管理部门批准，方可按照规定占用。  
经批准临时占用城市道路的，不得损坏城市道路；占用期满后，应当及时清理占用现场，恢复城市道路原状；损坏城市道路的，应当修复或者给予赔偿。

**Article 31** Where temporary occupation of urban roads is necessary due to special circumstances, approval must be obtained from the competent administrative department of municipal engineering and the public security traffic control department before occupation may proceed in accordance with the provisions.  
Where temporary occupation of urban roads has been approved, the urban roads shall not be damaged; upon expiration of the occupation period, the occupied site shall be promptly cleaned and the urban roads restored to their original condition; if urban roads are damaged, they shall be repaired or compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　城市人民政府应当严格控制占用城市道路作为集贸市场。

**Article 32** The people's governments of cities shall strictly control the occupation of urban roads for use as marketplaces.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　因工程建设需要挖掘城市道路的，应当提交城市规划部门批准签发的文件和有关设计文件，经市政工程行政主管部门和公安交通管理部门批准，方可按照规定挖掘。  
新建、扩建、改建的城市道路交付使用后5年内、大修的城市道路竣工后3年内不得挖掘；因特殊情况需要挖掘的，须经县级以上城市人民政府批准。

**Article 33** Where excavation of urban roads is necessary for engineering construction, the documents approved and issued by the urban planning department and the relevant design documents shall be submitted, and approval from the competent administrative department of municipal engineering and the public security traffic control department shall be obtained before excavation may proceed in accordance with the provisions.  
Newly constructed, expanded, or reconstructed urban roads shall not be excavated within five years after their delivery for use, and heavily repaired urban roads shall not be excavated within three years after their completion; where excavation is necessary due to special circumstances, approval must be obtained from the people's government of the city at or above the county level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　埋设在城市道路下的管线发生故障需要紧急抢修的，可以先行破路抢修，并同时通知市政工程行政主管部门和公安交通管理部门，在24小时内按照规定补办批准手续。

**Article 34** Where pipelines buried under urban roads malfunction and require emergency repair, the road may be broken for emergency repair first, while simultaneously notifying the competent administrative department of municipal engineering and the public security traffic control department, and the approval formalities shall be retroactively completed within 24 hours in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经批准挖掘城市道路的，应当在施工现场设置明显标志和安全防围设施；竣工后，应当及时清理现场，通知市政工程行政主管部门检查验收。

**Article 35** Where excavation of urban roads has been approved, conspicuous signs and safety guard facilities shall be set up at the construction site; upon completion, the site shall be promptly cleaned and the competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall be notified for inspection and acceptance.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经批准占用或者挖掘城市道路的，应当按照批准的位置、面积、期限占用或者挖掘。需要移动位置、扩大面积、延长时间的，应当提前办理变更审批手续。

**Article 36** Where occupation or excavation of urban roads has been approved, the occupation or excavation shall be carried out in accordance with the approved location, area, and time limit. Where a change of location, expansion of area, or extension of time is necessary, the procedures for modification of approval shall be handled in advance.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　占用或者挖掘由市政工程行政主管部门管理的城市道路的，应当向市政工程行政主管部门交纳城市道路占用费或者城市道路挖掘修复费。  
城市道路占用费的收费标准，由省、自治区人民政府的建设行政主管部门、直辖市人民政府的市政工程行政主管部门拟订，报同级财政、物价主管部门核定；城市道路挖掘修复费的收费标准，由省、自治区人民政府的建设行政主管部门、直辖市人民政府的市政工程行政主管部门制定，报同级财政、物价主管部门备案。

**Article 37** Where urban roads managed by the competent administrative department of municipal engineering are occupied or excavated, an urban road occupation fee or an urban road excavation and restoration fee shall be paid to the competent administrative department of municipal engineering.  
The charging standard for urban road occupation fees shall be drafted by the competent administrative department of construction of the people's government of a province or autonomous region, or the competent administrative department of municipal engineering of the people's government of a municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the competent finance and pricing departments at the same level for verification; the charging standard for urban road excavation and restoration fees shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction of the people's government of a province or autonomous region, or the competent administrative department of municipal engineering of the people's government of a municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the competent finance and pricing departments at the same level for recordation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　根据城市建设或者其他特殊需要，市政工程行政主管部门可以对临时占用城市道路的单位或者个人决定缩小占用面积、缩短占用时间或者停止占用，并根据具体情况退还部分城市道路占用费。

**Article 38** In light of urban construction or other special needs, the competent administrative department of municipal engineering may decide to reduce the occupation area, shorten the occupation period, or terminate the occupation for units or individuals that temporarily occupy urban roads, and shall refund part of the urban road occupation fee based on the specific circumstances.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由市政工程行政主管部门责令停止设计、施工，限期改正，可以并处3万元以下的罚款；已经取得设计、施工资格证书，情节严重的，提请原发证机关吊销设计、施工资格证书：  
(一)未取得设计、施工资格或者未按照资质等级承担城市道路的设计、施工任务的；  
(二)未按照城市道路设计、施工技术规范设计、施工的；  
(三)未按照设计图纸施工或者擅自修改图纸的。

**Article 39** Where any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall order the cessation of design or construction, order rectification within a time limit, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan; if the party has already obtained a design or construction qualification certificate and the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall be requested to revoke the design or construction qualification certificate:  
(1) Undertaking design or construction tasks for urban roads without obtaining design or construction qualifications or without acting in accordance with the corresponding qualification grade;  
(2) Designing or constructing not in accordance with the technical specifications for urban road design or construction;  
(3) Constructing not in accordance with the design drawings or modifying the drawings without authorization.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反[本条例第十七条](#art-17)规定，擅自使用未经验收或者验收不合格的城市道路的，由市政工程行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处工程造价2%以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Where any person, in violation of Article 17 of these Regulations, uses urban roads that have not undergone acceptance inspection or have failed such inspection without authorization, the competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall order rectification within a time limit, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 2% of the project cost.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　承担城市道路养护、维修的单位违反本条例的规定，未定期对城市道路进行养护、维修或者未按照规定的期限修复竣工，并拒绝接受市政工程行政主管部门监督、检查的，由市政工程行政主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 41** Where a unit undertaking the maintenance and repair of urban roads, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to regularly maintain or repair urban roads or fails to complete the repair within the prescribed time limit, and refuses to accept the supervision and inspection of the competent administrative department of municipal engineering, the competent administrative department of municipal engineering shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定，或者有下列行为之一的，由市政工程行政主管部门或者其他有关部门责令限期改正，可以处以2万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未对设在城市道路上的各种管线的检查井、箱盖或者城市道路附属设施的缺损及时补缺或者修复的；  
(二)未在城市道路施工现场设置明显标志和安全防围设施的；  
(三)占用城市道路期满或者挖掘城市道路后，不及时清理现场的；  
(四)依附于城市道路建设各种管线、杆线等设施，不按照规定办理批准手续的；  
(五)紧急抢修埋设在城市道路下的管线，不按照规定补办批准手续的；  
(六)未按照批准的位置、面积、期限占用或者挖掘城市道路，或者需要移动位置、扩大面积、延长时间，未提前办理变更审批手续的。

**Article 42** Where any person violates Article 27 of these Regulations, or commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of municipal engineering or another relevant department shall order rectification within a time limit and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) Failing to promptly replace or repair defects in inspection wells, covers, or ancillary facilities of various pipelines located on urban roads;  
(2) Failing to set up conspicuous signs and safety guard facilities at the construction site of urban roads;  
(3) Failing to promptly clean the site after the expiration of the occupation period or after excavation of urban roads;  
(4) Constructing various pipelines, poles, and other facilities attached to urban roads without handling the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Failing to retroactively complete the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions after emergency repair of pipelines buried under urban roads;  
(6) Occupying or excavating urban roads not in accordance with the approved location, area, or time limit, or failing to apply in advance for modification of approval when a change of location, expansion of area, or extension of time is necessary.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定给予处罚。

**Article 43** Where any violation of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated by the judicial authorities according to law; where it does not constitute a crime but a public security penalty shall be imposed, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Penalties.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　市政工程行政主管部门人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 44** Where any functionary of the competent administrative department of municipal engineering neglects his or her duty, abuses his or her power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; where it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自1996年10月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1996.
