### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公平竞争，促进企业诚信自律，规范企业信息公示，强化企业信用约束，维护交易安全，提高政府监管效能，扩大社会监督，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure fair competition, promote enterprise integrity and self-discipline, regulate enterprise information disclosure, strengthen enterprise credit constraints, safeguard transaction security, improve government supervision efficiency, and expand social supervision.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称企业信息，是指在市场监督管理部门登记的企业从事生产经营活动过程中形成的信息，以及政府部门在履行职责过程中产生的能够反映企业状况的信息。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "enterprise information" means information formed in the course of production and business operations of enterprises registered with the market supervision and administration department, as well as information that can reflect the status of enterprises and is generated by government departments in the performance of their duties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　企业信息公示应当真实、及时。公示的企业信息涉及国家秘密、国家安全或者社会公共利益的，应当报请主管的保密行政管理部门或者国家安全机关批准。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门公示的企业信息涉及企业商业秘密或者个人隐私的，应当报请上级主管部门批准。

**Article 3** Enterprise information disclosure shall be truthful and timely. Where enterprise information to be disclosed involves State secrets, national security, or public interests, approval shall be obtained from the competent administrative department of secrecy or State security authority. Where enterprise information to be disclosed by relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level involves trade secrets of enterprises or personal privacy, approval shall be obtained from the competent authority at a higher level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府领导本行政区域的企业信息公示工作，按照国家社会信用信息平台建设的总体要求，推动本行政区域企业信用信息公示系统的建设。

**Article 4** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall lead the enterprise information disclosure work within their respective administrative regions, and promote the construction of an enterprise credit information disclosure system within their administrative regions in accordance with the overall requirements for the construction of the national social credit information platform.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院市场监督管理部门推进、监督企业信息公示工作，组织国家企业信用信息公示系统的建设。国务院其他有关部门依照本条例规定做好企业信息公示相关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门依照本条例规定做好企业信息公示工作。

**Article 5** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall promote and supervise the enterprise information disclosure work, and organize the construction of the National Enterprise Credit Information Disclosure System. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall properly perform the relevant work on enterprise information disclosure in accordance with these Regulations.  
Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall properly perform the enterprise information disclosure work in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　市场监督管理部门应当通过国家企业信用信息公示系统，公示其在履行职责过程中产生的下列企业信息：  
（一）注册登记、备案信息；  
（二）动产抵押登记信息；  
（三）股权出质登记信息；  
（四）行政处罚信息；  
（五）其他依法应当公示的信息。  
前款规定的企业信息应当自产生之日起20个工作日内予以公示。

**Article 6** The market supervision and administration department shall, through the National Enterprise Credit Information Disclosure System, disclose the following enterprise information generated in the course of performing its duties:  
(1) registration and record-filing information;  
(2) chattel mortgage registration information;  
(3) equity pledge registration information;  
(4) administrative penalty information;  
(5) other information that shall be disclosed according to law.  
The enterprise information specified in the preceding paragraph shall be disclosed within 20 working days from the date of its generation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　市场监督管理部门以外的其他政府部门（以下简称其他政府部门）应当公示其在履行职责过程中产生的下列企业信息：  
（一）行政许可准予、变更、延续信息；  
（二）行政处罚信息；  
（三）其他依法应当公示的信息。  
其他政府部门可以通过国家企业信用信息公示系统，也可以通过其他系统公示前款规定的企业信息。市场监督管理部门和其他政府部门应当按照国家社会信用信息平台建设的总体要求，实现企业信息的互联共享。

**Article 7** Other government departments other than the market supervision and administration department (hereinafter referred to as other government departments) shall disclose the following enterprise information generated in the course of performing their duties:  
(1) information on the grant, modification, and extension of administrative licenses;  
(2) administrative penalty information;  
(3) other information that shall be disclosed according to law.  
Other government departments may disclose the enterprise information specified in the preceding paragraph through the National Enterprise Credit Information Disclosure System or through other systems. The market supervision and administration department and other government departments shall, in accordance with the overall requirements for the construction of the national social credit information platform, realize the interconnection and sharing of enterprise information.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业应当于每年1月1日至6月30日，通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向市场监督管理部门报送上一年度年度报告，并向社会公示。  
当年设立登记的企业，自下一年起报送并公示年度报告。

**Article 8** An enterprise shall, from January 1 to June 30 each year, submit the annual report of the previous year to the market supervision and administration department through the National Enterprise Credit Information Disclosure System, and disclose it to the public.  
An enterprise that is registered and established in the current year shall submit and disclose the annual report from the following year.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业年度报告内容包括：  
（一）企业通信地址、邮政编码、联系电话、电子邮箱等信息；  
（二）企业开业、歇业、清算等存续状态信息；  
（三）企业投资设立企业、购买股权信息；  
（四）企业为有限责任公司或者股份有限公司的，其股东或者发起人认缴和实缴的出资额、出资时间、出资方式等信息；  
（五）有限责任公司股东股权转让等股权变更信息；  
（六）企业网站以及从事网络经营的网店的名称、网址等信息；  
（七）企业从业人数、资产总额、负债总额、对外提供保证担保、所有者权益合计、营业总收入、主营业务收入、利润总额、净利润、纳税总额信息。  
前款第一项至第六项规定的信息应当向社会公示，第七项规定的信息由企业选择是否向社会公示。  
经企业同意，公民、法人或者其他组织可以查询企业选择不公示的信息。

**Article 9** The annual report of an enterprise shall include the following:  
(1) information such as the enterprise's mailing address, postal code, contact telephone number, and email address;  
(2) information on the enterprise's status of operation, such as commencement of business, closure, or liquidation;  
(3) information on the enterprise's investment in establishing other enterprises and purchase of equity interests;  
(4) if the enterprise is a limited liability company or a joint stock limited company, information on the subscribed and actually paid capital contributions, time of contribution, and method of contribution of its shareholders or promoters;  
(5) information on equity changes such as equity transfer of shareholders of a limited liability company;  
(6) information on the name and website of the enterprise's website and online stores engaged in network operations;  
(7) information on the number of employees, total assets, total liabilities, external guarantees, total owners' equity, total operating revenue, main business revenue, total profit, net profit, and total tax paid.  
The information specified in items (1) to (6) of the preceding paragraph shall be disclosed to the public. For the information specified in item (7), the enterprise may choose whether to disclose it to the public.  
With the consent of the enterprise, citizens, legal persons, or other organizations may inquire about the information that the enterprise chooses not to disclose.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业应当自下列信息形成之日起20个工作日内通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示：  
（一）有限责任公司股东或者股份有限公司发起人认缴和实缴的出资额、出资时间、出资方式等信息；  
（二）有限责任公司股东股权转让等股权变更信息；  
（三）行政许可取得、变更、延续信息；  
（四）知识产权出质登记信息；  
（五）受到行政处罚的信息；  
（六）其他依法应当公示的信息。  
市场监督管理部门发现企业未依照前款规定履行公示义务的，应当责令其限期履行。

**Article 10** An enterprise shall, within 20 working days from the date on which the following information is generated, disclose it to the public through the National Enterprise Credit Information Disclosure System:  
(1) information on the subscribed and actually paid capital contributions, time of contribution, and method of contribution of shareholders of a limited liability company or promoters of a joint stock limited company;  
(2) information on equity changes such as equity transfer of shareholders of a limited liability company;  
(3) information on obtaining, modification, and extension of administrative licenses;  
(4) information on the registration of intellectual property pledges;  
(5) information on having received administrative penalties;  
(6) other information that shall be disclosed according to law.  
If the market supervision and administration department discovers that an enterprise fails to perform the disclosure obligations in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall order the enterprise to perform within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　政府部门和企业分别对其公示信息的真实性、及时性负责。

**Article 11** Government departments and enterprises shall respectively be responsible for the authenticity and timeliness of the information they publish.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　政府部门发现其公示的信息不准确的，应当及时更正。公民、法人或者其他组织有证据证明政府部门公示的信息不准确的，有权要求该政府部门予以更正。  
企业发现其公示的信息不准确的，应当及时更正；但是，企业年度报告公示信息的更正应当在每年6月30日之前完成。更正前后的信息应当同时公示。

**Article 12** Government departments that discover that the information they have published is inaccurate shall promptly correct it. Citizens, legal persons or other organizations having evidence proving that the information published by a government department is inaccurate shall have the right to request the government department to make corrections.  
An enterprise that discovers that the information it has published is inaccurate shall promptly correct it; however, corrections to the information published in the enterprise's annual report shall be completed before June 30 of each year. Both the pre-correction and post-correction information shall be published simultaneously.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公民、法人或者其他组织发现企业公示的信息虚假的，可以向市场监督管理部门举报，接到举报的市场监督管理部门应当自接到举报材料之日起20个工作日内进行核查，予以处理，并将处理情况书面告知举报人。  
公民、法人或者其他组织对依照本条例规定公示的企业信息有疑问的，可以向政府部门申请查询，收到查询申请的政府部门应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内书面答复申请人。

**Article 13** Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that discover that information published by an enterprise is false may report it to the market supervision and administration department. The market supervision and administration department that receives the report shall, within 20 working days from the date of receiving the report materials, conduct verification, handle the matter, and inform the whistleblower in writing of the handling status.  
Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that have doubts about the enterprise information published in accordance with these Regulations may apply to a government department for inquiry. The government department that receives the inquiry application shall, within 20 working days from the date of receiving the application, reply to the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院市场监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府市场监督管理部门应当按照公平规范的要求，根据企业注册号等随机摇号，确定抽查的企业，组织对企业公示信息的情况进行检查。  
市场监督管理部门抽查企业公示的信息，可以采取书面检查、实地核查、网络监测等方式。市场监督管理部门抽查企业公示的信息，可以委托会计师事务所、税务师事务所、律师事务所等专业机构开展相关工作，并依法利用其他政府部门作出的检查、核查结果或者专业机构作出的专业结论。  
抽查结果由市场监督管理部门通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公布。

**Article 14** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council and the market supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the requirements of fairness and standardization, determine the enterprises to be subject to random inspection by way of random drawing of numbers such as enterprise registration numbers, and organize inspections of the information published by enterprises.  
When conducting random inspections on information published by enterprises, market supervision and administration departments may adopt methods such as document review, on-site verification, and online monitoring. When conducting random inspections on information published by enterprises, market supervision and administration departments may entrust accounting firms, tax agent firms, law firms, and other professional institutions to carry out related work, and utilize, according to law, the inspection or verification results made by other government departments or the professional conclusions made by professional institutions.  
The results of random inspections shall be published to the public by market supervision and administration departments through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　市场监督管理部门对企业公示的信息依法开展抽查或者根据举报进行核查，企业应当配合，接受询问调查，如实反映情况，提供相关材料。  
对不予配合情节严重的企业，市场监督管理部门应当通过国家企业信用信息公示系统公示。

**Article 15** When a market supervision and administration department conducts a random inspection according to law or verification based on a report on information published by an enterprise, the enterprise shall cooperate, accept inquiries and investigations, truthfully report the situation, and provide relevant materials.  
For an enterprise that seriously fails to cooperate, the market supervision and administration department shall publicize this through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　市场监督管理部门对涉嫌违反本条例规定的行为进行查处，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）进入企业的经营场所实施现场检查；  
（二）查阅、复制、收集与企业经营活动相关的合同、票据、账簿以及其他资料；  
（三）向与企业经营活动有关的单位和个人调查了解情况；  
（四）依法查询涉嫌违法的企业银行账户；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他职权。  
市场监督管理部门行使前款第四项规定的职权的，应当经市场监督管理部门主要负责人批准。

**Article 16** When investigating and handling acts suspected of violating the provisions of these Regulations, a market supervision and administration department may exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) Entering the business premises of the enterprise to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Inspecting, copying, and collecting contracts, bills, account books, and other materials related to the business activities of the enterprise;  
(3) Investigating and ascertaining the circumstances from units and individuals related to the business activities of the enterprise;  
(4) Inquiring, according to law, into the bank accounts of enterprises suspected of illegal acts;  
(5) Other functions and powers provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a market supervision and administration department exercises the function and power provided for in Item (4) of the preceding paragraph, it shall obtain approval from the principal person in charge of the market supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　任何公民、法人或者其他组织不得非法修改公示的企业信息，不得非法获取企业信息。

**Article 17** No citizen, legal person, or other organization may illegally modify published enterprise information or illegally obtain enterprise information.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业未按照本条例规定的期限公示年度报告或者未按照市场监督管理部门责令的期限公示有关企业信息的，由县级以上市场监督管理部门列入经营异常名录，并依法给予行政处罚。企业因连续2年未按规定报送年度报告被列入经营异常名录未改正，且通过登记的住所或者经营场所无法取得联系的，由县级以上市场监督管理部门吊销营业执照。  
企业公示信息隐瞒真实情况、弄虚作假的，法律、行政法规有规定的，依照其规定；没有规定的，由市场监督管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，列入市场监督管理严重违法失信名单，并可以吊销营业执照。被列入市场监督管理严重违法失信名单的企业的法定代表人、负责人，3年内不得担任其他企业的法定代表人、负责人。  
企业被吊销营业执照后，应当依法办理注销登记；未办理注销登记的，由市场监督管理部门依法作出处理。

**Article 18** If an enterprise fails to publish its annual report within the time limit prescribed by these Regulations or fails to publish relevant enterprise information within the time limit ordered by the market supervision and administration department, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall include it in the list of enterprises with abnormal operations and impose an administrative penalty according to law. If an enterprise is included in the list of enterprises with abnormal operations for failing to submit the annual report as required for two consecutive years and fails to correct it, and cannot be contacted through its registered domicile or business premises, the market supervision and administration department at or above the county level shall revoke its business license.  
If an enterprise conceals the true situation or practices fraud in the information published, if there are provisions in laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail; if there are no provisions, the market supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it shall be included in the seriously illegal and untrustworthy list of market supervision and administration, and the business license may be revoked. The legal representative or principal of an enterprise included in the seriously illegal and untrustworthy list of market supervision and administration shall not serve as the legal representative or principal of any other enterprise within three years.  
After the business license of an enterprise is revoked, it shall apply for cancellation registration according to law; if it fails to apply for cancellation registration, the market supervision and administration department shall deal with it according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当建立健全信用约束机制，在政府采购、工程招投标、国有土地出让、授予荣誉称号等工作中，将企业信息作为重要考量因素，对被列入经营异常名录或者市场监督管理严重违法失信名单的企业依法予以限制或者禁入。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish and improve the credit constraint mechanism, take enterprise information as an important consideration factor in government procurement, project bidding, state-owned land transfer, granting honorary titles, and other work, and impose restrictions or exclusions according to law on enterprises that are included in the list of enterprises with abnormal operations or the seriously illegal and untrustworthy list of market supervision and administration.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　鼓励企业主动纠正违法失信行为、消除不良影响，依法申请修复失信记录。政府部门依法解除相关管理措施并修复失信记录的，应当及时将上述信息与有关部门共享。

**Article 20** Enterprises are encouraged to proactively rectify illegal and untrustworthy acts, eliminate adverse effects, and apply for restoration of untrustworthy records according to law. Where a government department lifts relevant management measures according to law and restores untrustworthy records, it shall promptly share the above information with relevant departments.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　政府部门未依照本条例规定履行职责的，由监察机关、上一级政府部门责令改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where a government department fails to perform its duties in accordance with these Regulations, the supervisory authority or the government department at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the responsible leaders and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　非法修改公示的企业信息，或者非法获取企业信息的，依照有关法律、行政法规规定追究法律责任。

**Article 22** Any person who illegally modifies the disclosed enterprise information or illegally obtains enterprise information shall be subject to legal liability in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公民、法人或者其他组织认为政府部门在企业信息公示工作中的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 23** If a citizen, legal person or other organization believes that a specific administrative act of a government department in the work of enterprise information disclosure infringes upon his or its lawful rights and interests, he or it may apply for administrative reconsideration or bring an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业依照本条例规定公示信息，不免除其依照其他有关法律、行政法规规定公示信息的义务。

**Article 24** The disclosure of information by an enterprise in accordance with these Regulations shall not exempt it from the obligation to disclose information in accordance with other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织公示企业信息适用本条例关于政府部门公示企业信息的规定。

**Article 25** The provisions of these Regulations on the disclosure of enterprise information by government departments shall apply to the disclosure of enterprise information by organizations authorized by laws and regulations to perform public affairs management functions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院市场监督管理部门负责制定国家企业信用信息公示系统的技术规范。  
个体工商户、农民专业合作社信息公示的具体办法由国务院市场监督管理部门另行制定。

**Article 26** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the technical specifications for the national enterprise credit information publicity system.  
The specific measures for information disclosure of individual industrial and commercial households and specialized farmer cooperatives shall be separately formulated by the market supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2014年10月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2014.
