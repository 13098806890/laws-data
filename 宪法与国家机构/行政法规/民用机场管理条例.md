# 民用机场管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2009-07-01

> (2009年4月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第553号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范民用机场的建设与管理，积极、稳步推进民用机场发展，保障民用机场安全和有序运营，维护有关当事人的合法权益，依据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内民用机场的规划、建设、使用、管理及其相关活动。 民用机场分为运输机场和通用机场。 第三条　民用机场是公共基础设施。各级人民政府应当采取必要的措施，鼓励、支持民用机场发展，提高民用机场的管理水平。 第四条　国务院民用航空主管部门依法对全国民用机场实施行业监督管理。地区民用航空管理机构依法对辖区内民用机场实施行业监督管理。 有关地方人民政府依法对民用机场实施监督管理。 第五条　全国民用机场布局规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展需求以及国防要求编制，并与综合交通发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划相衔接，严格控制建设用地规模，节约集约用地，保护生态环境。 第二章　民用机场的建设和使用 第六条　新建运输机场的场址应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门规定的条件。 运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当将运输机场场址纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划统筹安排，并对场址实施保护。 第七条　运输机场的新建、改建和扩建应当依照国家有关规定办理建设项目审批、核准手续。 第八条　运输机场总体规划由运输机场建设项目法人编制，并经国务院民用航空主管部门或者地区民用航空管理机构(以下统称民用航空管理部门)批准后方可实施。 飞行区指标为4E以上(含4E)的运输机场的总体规划，由国务院民用航空主管部门批准；飞行区指标为4D以下(含4D)的运输机场的总体规划，由所在地地区民用航空管理机构批准。民用航空管理部门审批运输机场总体规划，应当征求运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府意见。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范民用机场的建设与管理，积极、稳步推进民用机场发展，保障民用机场安全和有序运营，维护有关当事人的合法权益，依据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated pursuant to the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating the construction and administration of civil airports, promoting the development of civil airports actively and steadily, ensuring the safe and orderly operation of civil airports, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of relevant parties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内民用机场的规划、建设、使用、管理及其相关活动。  
民用机场分为运输机场和通用机场。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the planning, construction, use, and administration of civil airports within the territory of the People's Republic of China as well as related activities.  
Civil airports are divided into transport airports and general airports.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用机场是公共基础设施。各级人民政府应当采取必要的措施，鼓励、支持民用机场发展，提高民用机场的管理水平。

**Article 3** Civil airports are public infrastructure. People's governments at all levels shall take necessary measures to encourage and support the development of civil airports and improve the management level of civil airports.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院民用航空主管部门依法对全国民用机场实施行业监督管理。地区民用航空管理机构依法对辖区内民用机场实施行业监督管理。  
有关地方人民政府依法对民用机场实施监督管理。

**Article 4** The civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, according to law, exercise industry supervision and administration over civil airports nationwide. The regional civil aviation administration shall, according to law, exercise industry supervision and administration over civil airports within its jurisdiction.  
Relevant local people's governments shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over civil airports.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国民用机场布局规划应当根据国民经济和社会发展需求以及国防要求编制，并与综合交通发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划相衔接，严格控制建设用地规模，节约集约用地，保护生态环境。

**Article 5** The national layout plan for civil airports shall be formulated in accordance with the needs of national economic and social development as well as national defense requirements, and shall be coordinated with the comprehensive transport development plan, the general land use plan, and urban and rural plans. The scale of land for construction shall be strictly controlled, land shall be used economically and intensively, and the ecological environment shall be protected.

### 第二章　民用机场的建设和使用

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　新建运输机场的场址应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门规定的条件。  
运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当将运输机场场址纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划统筹安排，并对场址实施保护。

**Article 6** The site of a new transport airport shall meet the conditions prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
The relevant local people's government of the place where a transport airport is located shall incorporate the site of the transport airport into the general land use plan and the urban and rural plans for overall arrangements, and shall implement protection over the site.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　运输机场的新建、改建和扩建应当依照国家有关规定办理建设项目审批、核准手续。

**Article 7** The construction, reconstruction, and expansion of a transport airport shall undergo the formalities of examination and approval or verification of construction projects in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　运输机场总体规划由运输机场建设项目法人编制，并经国务院民用航空主管部门或者地区民用航空管理机构(以下统称民用航空管理部门)批准后方可实施。  
飞行区指标为4E以上(含4E)的运输机场的总体规划，由国务院民用航空主管部门批准；飞行区指标为4D以下(含4D)的运输机场的总体规划，由所在地地区民用航空管理机构批准。民用航空管理部门审批运输机场总体规划，应当征求运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府意见。  
运输机场建设项目法人编制运输机场总体规划，应当征求有关军事机关意见。

**Article 8** The master plan of a transport airport shall be prepared by the construction project legal person of the transport airport, and may be implemented only after being approved by the civil aviation authority under the State Council or a regional civil aviation administration (hereinafter collectively referred to as the civil aviation administrative department).  
The master plan of a transport airport with a flight area index of 4E or above (including 4E) shall be approved by the civil aviation authority under the State Council; the master plan of a transport airport with a flight area index of 4D or below (including 4D) shall be approved by the regional civil aviation administration of the place where it is located. When examining and approving the master plan of a transport airport, the civil aviation administrative department shall solicit opinions from the relevant local people's government of the place where the transport airport is located.  
When preparing the master plan of a transport airport, the construction project legal person of the transport airport shall solicit opinions from the relevant military authorities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当将运输机场总体规划纳入城乡规划，并根据运输机场的运营和发展需要，对运输机场周边地区的土地利用和建设实行规划控制。

**Article 9** The relevant local people's government of the place where a transport airport is located shall incorporate the master plan of the transport airport into the urban and rural plans, and shall, based on the operational and development needs of the transport airport, implement planning control over land use and construction in the surrounding areas of the transport airport.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　运输机场内的建设项目应当符合运输机场总体规划。任何单位和个人不得在运输机场内擅自新建、改建、扩建建筑物或者构筑物。

**Article 10** Construction projects within a transport airport shall conform to the master plan of the transport airport. No unit or individual may construct, reconstruct, or expand buildings or structures within a transport airport without authorization.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　运输机场新建、改建和扩建项目的安全设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时验收、同时投入使用。安全设施投资应当纳入建设项目概算。

**Article 11** Safety facilities for construction, reconstruction, and expansion projects of a transport airport shall be designed, constructed, accepted, and put into use simultaneously with the main project. The investment in safety facilities shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　运输机场内的供水、供电、供气、通信、道路等基础设施由机场建设项目法人负责建设；运输机场外的供水、供电、供气、通信、道路等基础设施由运输机场所在地地方人民政府统一规划，统筹建设。

**Article 12** Infrastructure such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, communications, and roads within a transport airport shall be constructed by the construction project legal person of the airport; infrastructure such as water supply, power supply, gas supply, communications, and roads outside a transport airport shall be planned uniformly and constructed comprehensively by the local people's government of the place where the transport airport is located.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　运输机场专业工程的设计应当符合国家有关标准，并经民用航空管理部门批准。  
飞行区指标为4E以上(含4E)的运输机场专业工程的设计，由国务院民用航空主管部门批准；飞行区指标为4D以下(含4D)的运输机场专业工程的设计，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构批准。  
运输机场专业工程经民用航空管理部门验收合格后，方可投入使用。  
运输机场专业工程目录由国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院建设主管部门制定并公布。

**Article 13** The design of specialized works of a transport airport shall comply with the relevant national standards and shall be approved by the civil aviation administrative department.  
The design of specialized works of a transport airport with a flight area index of 4E or above (including 4E) shall be approved by the civil aviation authority under the State Council; the design of specialized works of a transport airport with a flight area index of 4D or below (including 4D) shall be approved by the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located.  
Specialized works of a transport airport may be put into use only after being inspected and accepted as conforming to standards by the civil aviation administrative department.  
The catalogue of specialized works of transport airports shall be formulated and published by the civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the competent construction department under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　通用机场的规划、建设按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 14** The planning and construction of general airports shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　运输机场的安全和运营管理由依法组建的或者受委托的具有法人资格的机构(以下简称机场管理机构)负责。

**Article 15** The safety and operational management of a transport airport shall be the responsibility of an institution established according to law or entrusted with such responsibility and having legal person status (hereinafter referred to as the airport management authority).

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　运输机场投入使用应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有健全的安全运营管理体系、组织机构和管理制度；  
(二)有与其运营业务相适应的飞行区、航站区、工作区以及空中交通服务、航行情报、通信导航监视、气象等相关设施、设备和人员；  
(三)使用空域已经批准；  
(四)飞行程序和运行标准符合国务院民用航空主管部门的规定；  
(五)符合国家规定的民用航空安全保卫条件；  
(六)有处理突发事件的应急预案及相应的设施、设备。

**Article 16** A transport airport shall meet the following conditions before being put into use:  
(1) having a sound safety operation management system, organizational structure, and management rules;  
(2) having flight areas, terminal areas, work areas, and relevant facilities, equipment, and personnel for air traffic services, aeronautical information, communication, navigation, surveillance, meteorology, etc., commensurate with its operational business;  
(3) the airspace to be used has been approved;  
(4) flight procedures and operating standards comply with the provisions of the civil aviation authority under the State Council;  
(5) meeting the civil aviation security conditions prescribed by the State; and  
(6) having emergency response plans and corresponding facilities and equipment for handling emergencies.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　运输机场投入使用的，机场管理机构应当向国务院民用航空主管部门提出申请，并附送符合[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定条件的相关材料。  
国务院民用航空主管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内审查完毕，作出准予许可或者不予许可的决定。准予许可的，颁发运输机场使用许可证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 17** Where a transport airport is to be put into use, the airport management authority shall file an application with the civil aviation authority under the State Council, together with relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions set forth in Article 16 of these Regulations.  
The civil aviation authority under the State Council shall complete the examination within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application, and shall make a decision to grant or deny permission. If permission is granted, a transport airport use permit shall be issued; if permission is denied, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons therefor shall be stated.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　通用机场投入使用应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与运营业务相适应的飞行场地；  
(二)有保证飞行安全的空中交通服务、通信导航监视等设施和设备；  
(三)有健全的安全管理制度、符合国家规定的民用航空安全保卫条件以及处理突发事件的应急预案；  
(四)配备必要的管理人员和专业技术人员。

**Article 18** A general airport shall meet the following conditions before being put into use:  
(1) having flight areas commensurate with its operational business;  
(2) having facilities and equipment for air traffic services, communication, navigation, surveillance, etc., that ensure flight safety;  
(3) having sound safety management rules, meeting the civil aviation security conditions prescribed by the State, and having emergency response plans for handling emergencies; and  
(4) being staffed with necessary management personnel and professional and technical personnel.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　通用机场投入使用的，通用机场的管理者应当向通用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构提出申请，并附送符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定条件的相关材料。  
地区民用航空管理机构应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内审查完毕，作出准予许可或者不予许可的决定。准予许可的，颁发通用机场使用许可证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 19** Where a general airport is to be put into use, the manager of the general airport shall file an application with the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the general airport is located, together with relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions set forth in Article 18 of these Regulations.  
The regional civil aviation administration shall complete the examination within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, and shall make a decision to grant or deny permission. If permission is granted, a general airport use permit shall be issued; if permission is denied, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons therefor shall be stated.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　运输机场作为国际机场使用的，应当按照国家有关规定设立口岸查验机构，配备相应的人员、场地和设施，并经国务院有关部门验收合格。  
国际机场的开放使用，由国务院民用航空主管部门对外公告；国际机场资料由国务院民用航空主管部门统一对外提供。

**Article 20** Where a transport airport is used as an international airport, a port inspection authority shall be established in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, equipped with corresponding personnel, premises, and facilities, and shall be inspected and accepted by the relevant department under the State Council before being put into use.  
The opening for use of an international airport shall be publicly announced by the civil aviation authority under the State Council; information on international airports shall be uniformly provided to the public by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　机场管理机构应当按照运输机场使用许可证规定的范围开放使用运输机场，不得擅自关闭。  
运输机场因故不能保障民用航空器运行安全，需要临时关闭的，机场管理机构应当及时通知有关空中交通管理部门并及时向社会公告。空中交通管理部门应当按照相关规定发布航行通告。  
机场管理机构拟关闭运输机场的，应当提前45日报颁发运输机场使用许可证的机关，经批准后方可关闭，并向社会公告。

**Article 21** The airport management authority shall open and use a transport airport within the scope specified in the transport airport use permit, and shall not close it without authorization.  
Where a transport airport cannot ensure the safe operation of civil aircraft due to some reason and needs to be temporarily closed, the airport management authority shall promptly notify the relevant air traffic control authority and promptly make a public announcement. The air traffic control authority shall issue a notice to airmen in accordance with relevant provisions.  
If the airport management authority intends to close a transport airport, it shall report to the authority that issued the transport airport use permit 45 days in advance, and may close it only after approval, and shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　运输机场的命名或者更名应当符合国家有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 22** The naming or renaming of a transport airport shall comply with the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　运输机场废弃或者改作他用的，机场管理机构应当按照国家有关规定办理报批手续，并及时向社会公告。

**Article 23** Where a transport airport is abandoned or converted to other uses, the airport management authority shall go through the formalities for submission and approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall promptly make a public announcement.

### 第三章　民用机场安全和运营管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民用航空管理部门、有关地方人民政府应当加强对运输机场安全运营工作的领导，督促机场管理机构依法履行安全管理职责，协调、解决运输机场安全运营中的问题。

**Article 24** The civil aviation administrative department and the relevant local people's governments shall strengthen leadership over the safe operation of transport airports, supervise the airport management authority in performing its safety management duties according to law, and coordinate and resolve problems in the safe operation of transport airports.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民用航空管理部门、有关地方人民政府应当按照国家规定制定运输机场突发事件的应急预案。

**Article 25** The civil aviation administrative department and the relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, formulate emergency response plans for emergencies at transport airports.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　机场管理机构应当根据运输机场突发事件应急预案组织运输机场应急救援的演练和人员培训。  
机场管理机构、航空运输企业以及其他驻场单位应当配备必要的应急救援设备和器材，并加强日常管理。

**Article 26** The airport management authority shall, in accordance with the emergency response plan for a transport airport, organize emergency rescue drills and personnel training for the transport airport.  
The airport management authority, air transport enterprises, and other units stationed at the airport shall be equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and apparatus, and shall strengthen daily management.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　机场管理机构应当依照国家有关法律、法规和技术标准的规定，保证运输机场持续符合安全运营要求。运输机场不符合安全运营要求的，机场管理机构应当按照国家有关规定及时改正。

**Article 27** The airport management authority shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, regulations, and technical standards of the State, ensure that a transport airport continuously meets the requirements for safe operation. If a transport airport does not meet the requirements for safe operation, the airport management authority shall promptly take corrective actions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　机场管理机构对运输机场的安全运营实施统一协调管理，负责建立健全机场安全运营责任制，组织制定机场安全运营规章制度，保障机场安全投入的有效实施，督促检查安全运营工作，及时消除安全事故隐患，依法报告生产安全事故。  
航空运输企业及其他驻场单位应当按照各自的职责，共同保障运输机场的安全运营并承担相应的责任；发生影响运输机场安全运营情况的，应当立即报告机场管理机构。

**Article 28** The airport management authority shall exercise unified coordination and management over the safe operation of a transport airport, be responsible for establishing and improving the safety operation responsibility system of the airport, organize the formulation of rules and regulations for the safe operation of the airport, ensure the effective implementation of safety investment in the airport, supervise and inspect the safety operation work, promptly eliminate potential safety hazards, and report production safety accidents according to law.  
Air transport enterprises and other units stationed at the airport shall, in accordance with their respective duties, jointly ensure the safe operation of the transport airport and bear corresponding responsibilities; in the event of any situation affecting the safe operation of the transport airport, they shall immediately report to the airport management authority.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　机场管理机构、航空运输企业以及其他驻场单位应当定期对从业人员进行必要的安全运营培训，保证从业人员具备相关的知识和技能。

**Article 29** The airport management authority, air transport enterprises, and other units stationed at the airport shall regularly provide necessary safety operation training to their employees, and shall ensure that the employees possess relevant knowledge and skills.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民用机场专用设备应当符合国家规定的标准和相关技术规范，并经国务院民用航空主管部门认定的机构检验合格后，方可用于民用机场。  
民用航空管理部门应当加强对民用机场专用设备的监督检查。  
民用机场专用设备目录由国务院民用航空主管部门制定并公布。

**Article 30** Specialized equipment for civil airports shall comply with the standards prescribed by the State and relevant technical specifications, and may be used for civil airports only after passing inspection by an institution recognized by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
The civil aviation administrative department shall strengthen supervision and inspection over specialized equipment for civil airports.  
The catalogue of specialized equipment for civil airports shall be formulated and published by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在运输机场开放使用的情况下，不得在飞行区及与飞行区临近的航站区内进行施工。确需施工的，应当取得运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构的批准。

**Article 31** When a transport airport is open for use, no construction may be carried out in the flight area or the terminal area adjacent to the flight area. Where construction is truly necessary, approval from the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall be obtained.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　发生突发事件，运输机场所在地有关地方人民政府、民用航空管理部门、空中交通管理部门、机场管理机构等单位应当按照应急预案的要求及时、有效地开展应急救援。

**Article 32** In the event of an emergency, the relevant local people's government of the place where a transport airport is located, the civil aviation administrative department, the air traffic control authority, the airport management authority, and other entities shall promptly and effectively carry out emergency rescue in accordance with the requirements of the emergency response plan.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　机场管理机构统一协调、管理运输机场的生产运营，维护运输机场的正常秩序，为航空运输企业及其他驻场单位、旅客和货主提供公平、公正、便捷的服务。  
机场管理机构与航空运输企业及其他驻场单位应当签订书面协议，明确各方在生产运营、机场管理过程中以及发生航班延误等情况时的权利和义务。

**Article 33** The airport management authority shall uniformly coordinate and manage the production and operation of a transport airport, maintain normal order at the transport airport, and provide fair, just, and convenient services to air transport enterprises, other units stationed at the airport, passengers, and cargo owners.  
The airport management authority shall enter into written agreements with air transport enterprises and other units stationed at the airport, specifying the rights and obligations of each party in the course of production, operation, and airport management, as well as in cases of flight delays and other circumstances.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　机场管理机构应当组织航空运输企业及其他驻场单位制定服务规范并向社会公布。

**Article 34** The airport management authority shall organize air transport enterprises and other units stationed at the airport to formulate service standards and publish them to the public.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　机场管理机构应当按照国家规定的标准配备候机、餐饮、停车、医疗急救等设施、设备，并提供相应的服务。

**Article 35** The airport management authority shall, in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, be equipped with facilities and equipment for waiting lounges, catering, parking, medical first aid, etc., and shall provide corresponding services.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　机场管理机构应当与航空运输企业、空中交通管理部门等单位建立信息共享机制，相互提供必要的生产运营信息，及时为旅客和货主提供准确的信息。

**Article 36** The airport management authority shall establish an information sharing mechanism with air transport enterprises, air traffic control authorities, and other entities, provide each other with necessary production and operation information, and promptly provide accurate information to passengers and cargo owners.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　机场管理机构、航空运输企业以及其他驻场单位应当采取有效措施加强协调和配合，共同保证航班正常运行。  
航班发生延误，机场管理机构应当及时协调航空运输企业及其他有关驻场单位共同做好旅客和货主服务，及时通告相关信息。航空运输企业及其代理人应当按照有关规定和服务承诺为旅客和货主提供相应的服务。

**Article 37** The airport management authority, air transport enterprises, and other units stationed at the airport shall take effective measures to enhance coordination and cooperation, and jointly ensure the normal operation of flights.  
Where a flight is delayed, the airport management authority shall promptly coordinate with air transport enterprises and other relevant units stationed at the airport to jointly provide good services to passengers and cargo owners, and promptly notify relevant information. Air transport enterprises and their agents shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions and service commitments, provide corresponding services to passengers and cargo owners.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　机场范围内的零售、餐饮、航空地面服务等经营性业务采取有偿转让经营权的方式经营的，机场管理机构应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定与取得经营权的企业签订协议，明确服务标准、收费水平、安全规范和责任等事项。  
对于采取有偿转让经营权的方式经营的业务，机场管理机构及其关联企业不得参与经营。

**Article 38** Where operational businesses such as retail, catering, and air ground services within the airport are operated by way of compensatory transfer of operating rights, the airport management authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of the civil aviation authority under the State Council, enter into agreements with the enterprises that have obtained the operating rights, specifying matters such as service standards, fee levels, safety norms, and responsibilities.  
For businesses operated by way of compensatory transfer of operating rights, the airport management authority and its affiliated enterprises shall not participate in the operation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　机场管理机构应当向民用航空管理部门报送运输机场规划、建设和生产运营的有关资料，接受民用航空管理部门的监督检查。

**Article 39** The airport management authority shall submit relevant information on the planning, construction, and production and operation of transport airports to the civil aviation administrative department, and shall accept supervision and inspection by the civil aviation administrative department.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民用航空管理部门和机场管理机构应当建立投诉受理制度，公布投诉受理单位和投诉方式。对于旅客和货主的投诉，民用航空管理部门或者机场管理机构应当自受理之日起10个工作日内作出书面答复。

**Article 40** The civil aviation administrative department and the airport management authority shall establish a complaint acceptance system, and shall publish the complaint acceptance entity and the means of lodging complaints. For complaints from passengers and cargo owners, the civil aviation administrative department or the airport management authority shall make a written reply within 10 working days from the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在民用机场内从事航空燃油供应业务的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)取得成品油经营许可和危险化学品经营许可；  
(二)有符合国家有关标准、与经营业务规模相适应的航空燃油供应设施、设备；  
(三)有健全的航空燃油供应安全管理制度、油品检测和监控体系；  
(四)有满足业务经营需要的专业技术和管理人员。

**Article 41** An enterprise engaged in the business of supplying aviation fuel within a civil airport shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained a refined oil operation permit and a hazardous chemicals operation permit;  
(2) having aviation fuel supply facilities and equipment that conform to the relevant national standards and are commensurate with the scale of its business;  
(3) having a sound aviation fuel supply safety management system, oil product testing and monitoring system; and  
(4) having professional technical and management personnel sufficient to meet the needs of its business operations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　申请在民用机场内从事航空燃油供应业务的企业，应当向民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构提出申请，并附送符合[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)规定条件的相关材料。  
地区民用航空管理机构应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内，作出准予许可或者不予许可的决定。准予许可的，颁发民用机场航空燃油供应安全运营许可证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 42** An enterprise applying to engage in the business of supplying aviation fuel within a civil airport shall file an application with the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located, together with relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions set forth in Article 41 of these Regulations.  
The regional civil aviation administration shall, within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to grant or deny permission. If permission is granted, a civil airport aviation fuel supply safety operation permit shall be issued; if permission is denied, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons therefor shall be stated.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　航空燃油供应企业供应的航空燃油应当符合航空燃油适航标准。

**Article 43** Aviation fuel supplied by an aviation fuel supply enterprise shall comply with the airworthiness standards for aviation fuel.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　民用机场航空燃油供应设施应当公平地提供给航空燃油供应企业使用。

**Article 44** Aviation fuel supply facilities at a civil airport shall be made available fairly to aviation fuel supply enterprises.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　运输机场航空燃油供应企业停止运输机场航空燃油供应业务的，应当提前90日告知运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构、机场管理机构和相关航空运输企业。

**Article 45** Where an aviation fuel supply enterprise at a transport airport ceases its business of supplying aviation fuel at the transport airport, it shall notify the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located, the airport management authority, and the relevant air transport enterprises 90 days in advance.

### 第四章　民用机场安全环境保护

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构和有关地方人民政府，应当按照国家有关规定划定民用机场净空保护区域，并向社会公布。

**Article 46** The regional civil aviation administration of the place where a civil airport is located and the relevant local people's government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, delimit the clearance protection area of the civil airport and publish it to the public.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府审批民用机场净空保护区域内的建设项目，应当书面征求民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构的意见。

**Article 47** When a local people's government at or above the county level examines and approves a construction project within the clearance protection area of a civil airport, it shall seek the opinions in writing of the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在民用机场净空保护区域内设置22万伏以上(含22万伏)的高压输电塔的，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的有关规定设置障碍灯或者标志，保持其正常状态，并向民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构、空中交通管理部门和机场管理机构提供有关资料。

**Article 48** Where a high-voltage transmission tower of 220 kV or above (including 220 kV) is installed within the clearance protection area of a civil airport, obstruction lights or markers shall be installed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the civil aviation authority under the State Council, and shall be maintained in normal condition, and relevant information shall be provided to the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located, the air traffic control authority, and the airport management authority.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　禁止在民用机场净空保护区域内从事下列活动：  
(一)排放大量烟雾、粉尘、火焰、废气等影响飞行安全的物质；  
(二)修建靶场、强烈爆炸物仓库等影响飞行安全的建筑物或者其他设施；  
(三)设置影响民用机场目视助航设施使用或者飞行员视线的灯光、标志或者物体；  
(四)种植影响飞行安全或者影响民用机场助航设施使用的植物；  
(五)放飞影响飞行安全的鸟类，升放无人驾驶的自由气球、系留气球和其他升空物体；  
(六)焚烧产生大量烟雾的农作物秸秆、垃圾等物质，或者燃放烟花、焰火；  
(七)在民用机场围界外5米范围内，搭建建筑物、种植树木，或者从事挖掘、堆积物体等影响民用机场运营安全的活动；  
(八)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他影响民用机场净空保护的行为。

**Article 49** The following activities are prohibited within the clearance protection area of a civil airport:  
(1) discharging substances such as large amounts of smoke, dust, flames, or waste gas that affect flight safety;  
(2) constructing buildings or other facilities such as shooting ranges or warehouses for highly explosive materials that affect flight safety;  
(3) installing lights, markers, or objects that interfere with the use of visual aids to navigation at a civil airport or the pilot's line of sight;  
(4) planting plants that affect flight safety or the use of navigation aids at a civil airport;  
(5) releasing birds that affect flight safety, or launching unmanned free balloons, tethered balloons, or other airborne objects;  
(6) burning crop stalks, garbage, or other substances that produce large amounts of smoke, or setting off fireworks or pyrotechnics;  
(7) constructing buildings, planting trees, or engaging in activities such as excavation or stacking of objects within 5 meters outside the perimeter fence of a civil airport that affect the safe operation of the civil airport; and  
(8) other acts affecting the clearance protection of a civil airport as prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在民用机场净空保护区域外从事[本条例第四十九条](#art-49)所列活动的，不得影响民用机场净空保护。

**Article 50** No activities listed in Article 49 of these Regulations conducted outside the clearance protection area of a civil airport shall affect the clearance protection of the civil airport.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　禁止在距离航路两侧边界各30公里以内的地带修建对空射击的靶场和其他可能影响飞行安全的设施。

**Article 51** It is prohibited to construct shooting ranges for anti-aircraft firing or other facilities that may affect flight safety within a zone of 30 kilometers from each side of the boundary of an air route.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民用航空管理部门和机场管理机构应当加强对民用机场净空状况的核查。发现影响民用机场净空保护的情况，应当立即制止，并书面报告民用机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府。接到报告的县级以上地方人民政府应当及时采取有效措施，消除对飞行安全的影响。

**Article 52** The civil aviation administrative department and the airport management authority shall strengthen inspection of the clearance condition of civil airports. Upon discovering any situation affecting the clearance protection of a civil airport, they shall immediately stop it and report in writing to the local people's government at or above the county level of the place where the civil airport is located. The local people's government at or above the county level that receives the report shall promptly take effective measures to eliminate the impact on flight safety.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民用机场所在地地方无线电管理机构应当会同地区民用航空管理机构按照国家无线电管理的有关规定和标准确定民用机场电磁环境保护区域，并向社会公布。  
民用机场电磁环境保护区域包括设置在民用机场总体规划区域内的民用航空无线电台(站)电磁环境保护区域和民用机场飞行区电磁环境保护区域。

**Article 53** The local radio regulatory authority of the place where a civil airport is located shall, jointly with the regional civil aviation administration, determine the electromagnetic environment protection area of the civil airport in accordance with the relevant provisions and standards of the State on radio regulation, and shall publish it to the public.  
The electromagnetic environment protection area of a civil airport includes the electromagnetic environment protection area of civil aviation radio stations (stations) located within the master plan area of the civil airport and the electromagnetic environment protection area of the flight area of the civil airport.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　设置、使用地面民用航空无线电台(站)，应当经民用航空管理部门审核后，按照国家无线电管理有关规定办理审批手续，领取无线电台执照。

**Article 54** The installation and use of ground civil aviation radio stations (stations) shall be examined by the civil aviation administrative department, and then undergo the formalities of examination and approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on radio regulation, and a radio station license shall be obtained.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　在民用机场电磁环境保护区域内设置、使用非民用航空无线电台(站)的，无线电管理机构应当在征求民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构意见后，按照国家无线电管理的有关规定审批。

**Article 55** Where a non-civil aviation radio station (station) is to be installed or used within the electromagnetic environment protection area of a civil airport, the radio regulatory authority shall, after seeking opinions from the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located, examine and approve it in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on radio regulation.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　禁止在民用航空无线电台(站)电磁环境保护区域内，从事下列影响民用机场电磁环境的活动：  
(一)修建架空高压输电线、架空金属线、铁路、公路、电力排灌站；  
(二)存放金属堆积物；  
(三)种植高大植物；  
(四)从事掘土、采砂、采石等改变地形地貌的活动；  
(五)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他影响民用机场电磁环境的行为。

**Article 56** The following activities that affect the electromagnetic environment of a civil airport are prohibited within the electromagnetic environment protection area of a civil aviation radio station (station):  
(1) constructing overhead high-voltage transmission lines, overhead metal lines, railways, highways, or electric pumping and drainage stations;  
(2) storing metal piled materials;  
(3) planting tall plants;  
(4) engaging in activities that change the topography or landform such as digging, sand mining, or stone quarrying; and  
(5) other acts affecting the electromagnetic environment of a civil airport as prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　任何单位或者个人使用的无线电台(站)和其他仪器、装置，不得对民用航空无线电专用频率的正常使用产生干扰。

**Article 57** No radio station (station) or other instrument or device used by any unit or individual shall cause interference to the normal use of the dedicated civil aviation radio frequencies.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　民用航空无线电专用频率受到干扰时，机场管理机构和民用航空管理部门应当立即采取排查措施，及时消除；无法消除的，应当通报民用机场所在地地方无线电管理机构。接到通报的无线电管理机构应当采取措施，依法查处。

**Article 58** Where the dedicated civil aviation radio frequencies are interfered with, the airport management authority and the civil aviation administrative department shall immediately take investigation measures to eliminate the interference in a timely manner; if it cannot be eliminated, they shall notify the local radio regulatory authority of the place where the civil airport is located. The radio regulatory authority that receives the notification shall take measures to investigate and deal with the matter according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　在民用机场起降的民用航空器应当符合国家有关航空器噪声和涡轮发动机排出物的适航标准。

**Article 59** Civil aircraft taking off from or landing at a civil airport shall comply with the national airworthiness standards concerning aircraft noise and turbine engine emissions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　机场管理机构应当会同航空运输企业、空中交通管理部门等有关单位，采取技术手段和管理措施控制民用航空器噪声对运输机场周边地区的影响。

**Article 60** The airport management authority shall, jointly with air transport enterprises, air traffic control authorities, and other relevant entities, adopt technical means and management measures to control the impact of civil aircraft noise on the surrounding areas of a transport airport.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　民用机场所在地有关地方人民政府制定民用机场周边地区的土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，应当充分考虑民用航空器噪声对民用机场周边地区的影响，符合国家有关声环境质量标准。  
机场管理机构应当将民用航空器噪声对运输机场周边地区产生影响的情况，报告有关地方人民政府国土资源、规划建设、环境保护等主管部门。

**Article 61** When formulating the general land use plan and urban and rural plans for the surrounding areas of a civil airport, the relevant local people's government of the place where the civil airport is located shall give full consideration to the impact of civil aircraft noise on the surrounding areas of the civil airport, and shall comply with the national acoustic environmental quality standards.  
The airport management authority shall report the situation of the impact of civil aircraft noise on the surrounding areas of a transport airport to the competent departments of land and resources, planning and construction, environmental protection, etc., of the relevant local people's government.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　民用机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当在民用机场周边地区划定限制建设噪声敏感建筑物的区域并实施控制。确需在该区域内建设噪声敏感建筑物的，建设单位应当采取措施减轻或者避免民用航空器运行时对其产生的噪声影响。  
民用机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当会同地区民用航空管理机构协调解决在民用机场起降的民用航空器噪声影响引发的相关问题。

**Article 62** The relevant local people's government of the place where a civil airport is located shall delimit areas in the surrounding areas of the civil airport where the construction of noise-sensitive buildings is restricted and implement control. Where it is truly necessary to construct noise-sensitive buildings within such areas, the construction unit shall take measures to mitigate or avoid the noise impact caused by the operation of civil aircraft on them.  
The relevant local people's government of the place where a civil airport is located shall, jointly with the regional civil aviation administration, coordinate and resolve related issues arising from the noise impact of civil aircraft taking off from or landing at the civil airport.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在运输机场内进行不符合运输机场总体规划的建设活动；  
(二)擅自实施未经批准的运输机场专业工程的设计，或者将未经验收合格的运输机场专业工程投入使用；  
(三)在运输机场开放使用的情况下，未经批准在飞行区及与飞行区临近的航站区内进行施工。

**Article 63** In any of the following circumstances in violation of these Regulations, the civil aviation administrative department shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000:  
(1) carrying out construction activities within a transport airport that do not conform to the master plan of the transport airport;  
(2) implementing the design of specialized works of a transport airport that has not been approved without authorization, or putting specialized works of a transport airport into use that have not been inspected and accepted as conforming to standards; or  
(3) carrying out construction in the flight area or the terminal area adjacent to the flight area without approval when the transport airport is open for use.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构未按照运输机场使用许可证规定的范围使用运输机场的，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 64** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority uses a transport airport beyond the scope specified in the transport airport use permit, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构未经批准擅自关闭运输机场的，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority closes a transport airport without approval, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构因故不能保障民用航空器飞行安全，临时关闭运输机场，未及时通知有关空中交通管理部门并及时向社会公告，或者经批准关闭运输机场后未及时向社会公告的，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority temporarily closes a transport airport because it cannot ensure the flight safety of civil aircraft for some reason but fails to promptly notify the relevant air traffic control authority and promptly make a public announcement, or fails to promptly make a public announcement after closing the transport airport upon approval, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构未按照应急预案的要求进行应急救援演练或者未配备必要的应急救援设备和器材的，由地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 67** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority fails to conduct emergency rescue drills in accordance with the requirements of the emergency response plan or fails to be equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and apparatus, the regional civil aviation administration shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例的规定，运输机场投入使用后不符合安全运营要求，机场管理机构拒不改正，或者经改正仍不符合安全运营要求的，由民用航空管理部门作出限制使用的决定；情节严重的，吊销运输机场使用许可证。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a transport airport fails to meet the requirements for safe operation after being put into use, and the airport management authority refuses to take corrective action or still fails to meet the requirements for safe operation after taking corrective action, the civil aviation administrative department shall make a decision to restrict its use; if the circumstances are serious, the transport airport use permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　机场管理机构未依照本条例的规定履行管理职责，造成运输机场地面事故、民用航空器飞行事故或者严重事故征候的，民用航空管理部门应当责令改正，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 69** Where the airport management authority fails to perform its management duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, resulting in a ground accident at a transport airport, a civil aircraft flight accident, or a serious incident, the civil aviation administrative department shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构在运输机场内使用不符合国家规定标准和相关技术规范的民用机场专用设备的，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令停止使用，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 70** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority uses specialized equipment for civil airports that does not comply with the standards prescribed by the State and relevant technical specifications within a transport airport, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall order the cessation of use and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本条例的规定，发生突发事件，机场管理机构、空中交通管理部门等单位未按照应急预案的要求及时、有效开展应急救援的，由地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Where, in violation of these Regulations, when an emergency occurs, the airport management authority, the air traffic control authority, or other entities fail to promptly and effectively carry out emergency rescue in accordance with the requirements of the emergency response plan, the regional civil aviation administration shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例的规定，未取得民用机场航空燃油供应安全运营许可证，在民用机场内从事航空燃油供应业务的，由民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an enterprise engages in the business of supplying aviation fuel within a civil airport without obtaining a civil airport aviation fuel supply safety operation permit, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if there are illegal gains, such illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本条例的规定，航空燃油供应企业供应的航空燃油不符合航空燃油适航标准的，由民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销民用机场航空燃油供应安全运营许可证。

**Article 73** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an aviation fuel supply enterprise supplies aviation fuel that does not comply with the airworthiness standards for aviation fuel, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if the circumstances are serious, the civil airport aviation fuel supply safety operation permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本条例的规定，运输机场航空燃油供应企业停止运输机场航空燃油供应业务，未提前90日告知地区民用航空管理机构、机场管理机构和相关航空运输企业的，由运输机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构处5万元以上25万元以下的罚款。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an aviation fuel supply enterprise at a transport airport ceases its business of supplying aviation fuel at the transport airport without notifying the regional civil aviation administration, the airport management authority, and the relevant air transport enterprises 90 days in advance, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the transport airport is located shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 250,000.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)机场管理机构不按照国家规定的标准配备候机、餐饮、停车、医疗急救等设施、设备，并提供相应的服务；  
(二)航班发生延误时，机场管理机构、航空运输企业以及其他驻场单位不按照有关规定和服务承诺为旅客和货主提供相应的服务。

**Article 75** In any of the following circumstances in violation of these Regulations, the regional civil aviation administration shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000:  
(1) the airport management authority fails to be equipped with facilities and equipment such as waiting lounges, catering, parking, and medical first aid in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, and fails to provide corresponding services; or  
(2) when a flight is delayed, the airport management authority, air transport enterprises, or other units stationed at the airport fail to provide corresponding services to passengers and cargo owners in accordance with the relevant provisions and service commitments.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构及其关联企业参与经营采取有偿转让经营权的方式经营的业务的，由地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 76** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority or its affiliated enterprises participate in the operation of businesses operated by way of compensatory transfer of operating rights, the regional civil aviation administration shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if there are illegal gains, such illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本条例的规定，机场管理机构未向民用航空管理部门报送运输机场规划、建设和生产运营的有关资料的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the airport management authority fails to submit relevant information on the planning, construction, and production and operation of transport airports to the civil aviation administrative department, the civil aviation administrative department shall order corrective action; if it refuses to take corrective action, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本条例的规定，在民用机场净空保护区域内设置22万伏以上(含22万伏)的高压输电塔，未依照国务院民用航空主管部门的有关规定设置障碍灯或者标志的，由民用机场所在地地区民用航空管理机构责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a high-voltage transmission tower of 220 kV or above (including 220 kV) is installed within the clearance protection area of a civil airport without installing obstruction lights or markers in accordance with the relevant provisions of the civil aviation authority under the State Council, the regional civil aviation administration of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由民用机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府责令改正；情节严重的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)排放大量烟雾、粉尘、火焰、废气等影响飞行安全的物质；  
(二)修建靶场、强烈爆炸物仓库等影响飞行安全的建筑物或者其他设施；  
(三)设置影响民用机场目视助航设施使用或者飞行员视线的灯光、标志或者物体；  
(四)种植影响飞行安全或者影响民用机场助航设施使用的植物；  
(五)放飞影响飞行安全的鸟类、升放无人驾驶的自由气球、系留气球和其他升空物体；  
(六)焚烧产生大量烟雾的农作物秸秆、垃圾等物质，或者燃放烟花、焰火；  
(七)在民用机场围界外5米范围内，搭建建筑物、种植树木，或者从事挖掘、堆积物体等影响民用机场运营安全的活动；  
(八)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他影响民用机场净空保护的行为。

**Article 79** In any of the following circumstances in violation of these Regulations, the local people's government at or above the county level of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed:  
(1) discharging substances such as large amounts of smoke, dust, flames, or waste gas that affect flight safety;  
(2) constructing buildings or other facilities such as shooting ranges or warehouses for highly explosive materials that affect flight safety;  
(3) installing lights, markers, or objects that interfere with the use of visual aids to navigation at a civil airport or the pilot's line of sight;  
(4) planting plants that affect flight safety or the use of navigation aids at a civil airport;  
(5) releasing birds that affect flight safety, or launching unmanned free balloons, tethered balloons, or other airborne objects;  
(6) burning crop stalks, garbage, or other substances that produce large amounts of smoke, or setting off fireworks or pyrotechnics;  
(7) constructing buildings, planting trees, or engaging in activities such as excavation or stacking of objects within 5 meters outside the perimeter fence of a civil airport that affect the safe operation of the civil airport; and  
(8) other acts affecting the clearance protection of a civil airport as prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本条例的规定，使用的无线电台(站)或者其他仪器、装置，对民用航空无线电专用频率的正常使用产生干扰的，由民用机场所在地无线电管理机构责令改正；情节严重的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a radio station (station) or other instrument or device used causes interference to the normal use of the dedicated civil aviation radio frequencies, the radio regulatory authority of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本条例的规定，在民用航空无线电台(站)电磁环境保护区域内从事下列活动的，由民用机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府责令改正；情节严重的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)修建架空高压输电线、架空金属线、铁路、公路、电力排灌站；  
(二)存放金属堆积物；  
(三)从事掘土、采砂、采石等改变地形地貌的活动；  
(四)国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他影响民用机场电磁环境保护的行为。

**Article 81** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following activities is engaged in within the electromagnetic environment protection area of a civil aviation radio station (station), the local people's government at or above the county level of the place where the civil airport is located shall order corrective action; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed:  
(1) constructing overhead high-voltage transmission lines, overhead metal lines, railways, highways, or electric pumping and drainage stations;  
(2) storing metal piled materials;  
(3) engaging in activities that change the topography or landform such as digging, sand mining, or stone quarrying; and  
(4) other acts affecting the electromagnetic environment protection of a civil airport as prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本条例的规定，在民用机场起降的民用航空器不符合国家有关航空器噪声和涡轮发动机排出物的适航标准的，由民用航空管理部门责令相关航空运输企业改正，可以处10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 82** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a civil aircraft taking off from or landing at a civil airport does not comply with the national airworthiness standards concerning aircraft noise and turbine engine emissions, the civil aviation administrative department shall order the relevant air transport enterprise to take corrective action, and may impose a fine of not more than RMB 100,000; if it refuses to take corrective action, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　国家工作人员违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由有关部门依法给予处分：  
(一)不依照规定实施行政许可；  
(二)不依法履行监督检查职责；  
(三)不依法实施行政强制措施或者行政处罚；  
(四)滥用职权、玩忽职守的其他行为。

**Article 83** Where a state functionary, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the relevant department shall impose sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to implement administrative licensing in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to perform the duties of supervision and inspection according to law;  
(3) failing to implement administrative compulsory measures or administrative penalties according to law; or  
(4) other acts of abusing power or neglecting duties.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本条例所称运输机场是指为从事旅客、货物运输等公共航空运输活动的民用航空器提供起飞、降落等服务的机场。  
本条例所称通用机场是指为从事工业、农业、林业、渔业和建筑业的作业飞行，以及医疗卫生、抢险救灾、气象探测、海洋监测、科学实验、教育训练、文化体育等飞行活动的民用航空器提供起飞、降落等服务的机场。

**Article 84** For the purposes of these Regulations, a transport airport means an airport that provides takeoff and landing services for civil aircraft engaged in public air transport activities such as the transportation of passengers and cargo.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, a general airport means an airport that provides takeoff and landing services for civil aircraft engaged in operational flights in industries, agriculture, forestry, fisheries, and construction, as well as flight activities such as medical and public health, emergency rescue and disaster relief, meteorological detection, marine monitoring, scientific experiments, education and training, culture and sports, etc.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本条例所称飞行区指标为4D的运输机场是指可供基准飞行场地长度大于1800米、翼展在36米至52米之间、主起落架外轮外侧边间距在9米至14米之间的民用航空器起飞、降落的机场。  
本条例所称飞行区指标为4E的运输机场是指可供基准飞行场地长度大于1800米、翼展在52米至65米之间、主起落架外轮外侧边间距在9米至14米之间的民用航空器起飞、降落的机场。

**Article 85** For the purposes of these Regulations, a transport airport with a flight area index of 4D means an airport that can accommodate the takeoff and landing of civil aircraft with a reference field length greater than 1,800 meters, a wingspan between 36 and 52 meters, and an outer main landing gear wheel span between 9 and 14 meters.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, a transport airport with a flight area index of 4E means an airport that can accommodate the takeoff and landing of civil aircraft with a reference field length greater than 1,800 meters, a wingspan between 52 and 65 meters, and an outer main landing gear wheel span between 9 and 14 meters.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　军民合用机场民用部分的管理除遵守本条例的有关规定外，还应当遵守国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定。

**Article 86** The administration of the civil portion of a military-civilian joint-use airport shall, in addition to complying with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, also comply with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本条例自2009年7月1日起施行。

**Article 87** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2009.
