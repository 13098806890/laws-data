# 中华人民共和国电信条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2016-02-06

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范电信市场秩序，维护电信用户和电信业务经营者的合法权益，保障电信网络和信息的安全，促进电信业的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate the order of the telecommunications market, protect the legitimate rights and interests of telecommunications users and telecommunications business operators, ensure the security of telecommunications networks and information, and promote the healthy development of the telecommunications industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事电信活动或者与电信有关的活动，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所称电信，是指利用有线、无线的电磁系统或者光电系统，传送、发射或者接收语音、文字、数据、图像以及其他任何形式信息的活动。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in telecommunications activities or activities related to telecommunications within the territory of the People's Republic of China must abide by these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "telecommunications" means the activities of transmitting, emitting, or receiving voices, texts, data, images, or any other form of information by means of wireline or wireless electromagnetic systems or optoelectronic systems.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院信息产业主管部门依照本条例的规定对全国电信业实施监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构在国务院信息产业主管部门的领导下，依照本条例的规定对本行政区域内的电信业实施监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent department of information industry under the State Council shall, in accordance with these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the national telecommunications industry.  
The telecommunications administration authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, under the leadership of the competent department of information industry under the State Council and in accordance with these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the telecommunications industry within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　电信监督管理遵循政企分开、破除垄断、鼓励竞争、促进发展和公开、公平、公正的原则。  
电信业务经营者应当依法经营，遵守商业道德，接受依法实施的监督检查。

**Article 4** Telecommunications supervision and administration shall follow the principles of separating government functions from enterprise management, breaking monopolies, encouraging competition, promoting development, and openness, fairness, and impartiality.  
Telecommunications business operators shall operate according to law, abide by commercial ethics, and accept supervision and inspection conducted according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　电信业务经营者应当为电信用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便和价格合理的电信服务。

**Article 5** Telecommunications business operators shall provide telecommunications users with telecommunications services that are prompt, accurate, safe, convenient, and reasonably priced.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　电信网络和信息的安全受法律保护。任何组织或者个人不得利用电信网络从事危害国家安全、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的活动。

**Article 6** The security of telecommunications networks and information shall be protected by law. No organization or individual may use telecommunications networks to engage in activities that endanger national security, public interests, or the legitimate rights and interests of others.

### 第二章　电信市场

#### 第一节　电信业务许可

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对电信业务经营按照电信业务分类，实行许可制度。  
经营电信业务，必须依照本条例的规定取得国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构颁发的电信业务经营许可证。  
未取得电信业务经营许可证，任何组织或者个人不得从事电信业务经营活动。

**Article 7** The State shall implement a licensing system for telecommunications business operations according to the classification of telecommunications business.  
To operate a telecommunications business, an operator must obtain a telecommunications business operating license issued by the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications regulatory authority of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
No organization or individual may engage in telecommunications business operations without obtaining a telecommunications business operating license.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　电信业务分为基础电信业务和增值电信业务。  
基础电信业务，是指提供公共网络基础设施、公共数据传送和基本话音通信服务的业务。增值电信业务，是指利用公共网络基础设施提供的电信与信息服务的业务。  
电信业务分类的具体划分在本条例所附的《电信业务分类目录》中列出。国务院信息产业主管部门根据实际情况，可以对目录所列电信业务分类项目作局部调整，重新公布。

**Article 8** Telecommunications business is divided into basic telecommunications business and value-added telecommunications business.  
Basic telecommunications business refers to the business of providing public network infrastructure, public data transmission, and basic voice communication services. Value-added telecommunications business refers to the business of providing telecommunications and information services using the public network infrastructure.  
The specific classification of telecommunications business is listed in the Catalogue of Telecommunications Business Classifications attached to these Regulations. The competent information industry department of the State Council may, based on actual circumstances, make partial adjustments to the listed items of telecommunications business classification in the catalogue and re-promulgate them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营基础电信业务，须经国务院信息产业主管部门审查批准，取得《基础电信业务经营许可证》。  
经营增值电信业务，业务覆盖范围在两个以上省、自治区、直辖市的，须经国务院信息产业主管部门审查批准，取得《跨地区增值电信业务经营许可证》；业务覆盖范围在一个省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的，须经省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构审查批准，取得《增值电信业务经营许可证》。  
运用新技术试办《电信业务分类目录》未列出的新型电信业务的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构备案。

**Article 9** To operate a basic telecommunications business, approval from the competent information industry department of the State Council shall be obtained, and the Basic Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be obtained.  
To operate a value-added telecommunications business, if the business coverage spans two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, approval from the competent information industry department of the State Council shall be obtained, and the Cross-Regional Value-Added Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be obtained; if the business coverage is within the administrative region of one province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, approval from the telecommunications regulatory authority of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be obtained, and the Value-Added Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be obtained.  
When using new technologies to pilot a new type of telecommunications business not listed in the Catalogue of Telecommunications Business Classifications, the operator shall file a record with the telecommunications regulatory authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经营基础电信业务，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)经营者为依法设立的专门从事基础电信业务的公司，且公司中国有股权或者股份不少于51%；  
(二)有可行性研究报告和组网技术方案；  
(三)有与从事经营活动相适应的资金和专业人员；  
(四)有从事经营活动的场地及相应的资源；  
(五)有为用户提供长期服务的信誉或者能力；  
(六)国家规定的其他条件。

**Article 10** To operate a basic telecommunications business, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) The operator is a company established according to law that specializes in basic telecommunications business, and the company's state-owned equity or shares shall not be less than 51%;  
(2) A feasibility study report and a network deployment technical plan are available;  
(3) Funds and professional personnel suitable for the business operations are available;  
(4) Premises and corresponding resources for the business operations are available;  
(5) The reputation or capability to provide long-term services to users is available;  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请经营基础电信业务，应当向国务院信息产业主管部门提出申请，并提交[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的相关文件。国务院信息产业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起180日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发《基础电信业务经营许可证》；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 11** An application for a basic telecommunications business shall be submitted to the competent information industry department of the State Council, together with the relevant documents specified in Article 10 of these Regulations. The competent information industry department of the State Council shall complete the examination within 180 days from the date of accepting the application and make a decision to approve or disapprove. If the application is approved, the Basic Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be issued; if disapproved, the applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons stated.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院信息产业主管部门审查经营基础电信业务的申请时，应当考虑国家安全、电信网络安全、电信资源可持续利用、环境保护和电信市场的竞争状况等因素。  
颁发《基础电信业务经营许可证》，应当按照国家有关规定采用招标方式。

**Article 12** When examining an application for a basic telecommunications business, the competent information industry department of the State Council shall consider factors such as national security, telecommunications network security, sustainable utilization of telecommunications resources, environmental protection, and competition in the telecommunications market.  
The issuance of the Basic Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be conducted by way of tender in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经营增值电信业务，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)经营者为依法设立的公司；  
(二)有与开展经营活动相适应的资金和专业人员；  
(三)有为用户提供长期服务的信誉或者能力；  
(四)国家规定的其他条件。

**Article 13** To operate a value-added telecommunications business, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) The operator is a company established according to law;  
(2) Funds and professional personnel suitable for the business operations are available;  
(3) The reputation or capability to provide long-term services to users is available;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请经营增值电信业务，应当根据[本条例第九条](#art-9)第二款的规定，向国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构提出申请，并提交[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定的相关文件。申请经营的增值电信业务，按照国家有关规定须经有关主管部门审批的，还应当提交有关主管部门审核同意的文件。国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构应当自收到申请之日起60日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发《跨地区增值电信业务经营许可证》或者《增值电信业务经营许可证》；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 14** An application for a value-added telecommunications business shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 9 of these Regulations, be submitted to the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications regulatory authority of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with the relevant documents specified in Article 13 of these Regulations. If the value-added telecommunications business applied for requires approval from a competent authority pursuant to relevant State regulations, the documents approved by that authority shall also be submitted. The competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications regulatory authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the examination within 60 days from the date of receiving the application and make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approved, the Cross-Regional Value-Added Telecommunications Business Operating License or the Value-Added Telecommunications Business Operating License shall be issued; if disapproved, the applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons stated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　电信业务经营者在经营过程中，变更经营主体、业务范围或者停止经营的，应当提前90日向原颁发许可证的机关提出申请，并办理相应手续；停止经营的，还应当按照国家有关规定做好善后工作。

**Article 15** If a telecommunications business operator, during its operations, changes the operator, business scope, or ceases operations, it shall submit an application to the original license-issuing authority 90 days in advance and complete the corresponding procedures; if it ceases operations, it shall also properly handle the follow-up matters in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专用电信网运营单位在所在地区经营电信业务的，应当依照本条例规定的条件和程序提出申请，经批准，取得电信业务经营许可证。

**Article 16** If a dedicated telecommunications network operator intends to operate a telecommunications business in its local area, it shall submit an application in accordance with the conditions and procedures prescribed in these Regulations and, upon approval, obtain a telecommunications business operating license.

#### 第二节　电信网间互联

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　电信网之间应当按照技术可行、经济合理、公平公正、相互配合的原则，实现互联互通。  
主导的电信业务经营者不得拒绝其他电信业务经营者和专用网运营单位提出的互联互通要求。  
前款所称主导的电信业务经营者，是指控制必要的基础电信设施并且在电信业务市场中占有较大份额，能够对其他电信业务经营者进入电信业务市场构成实质性影响的经营者。  
主导的电信业务经营者由国务院信息产业主管部门确定。

**Article 17** Interconnection between telecommunications networks shall be achieved on the principles of technical feasibility, economic reasonableness, fairness, impartiality, and mutual cooperation.  
A dominant telecommunications business operator shall not refuse interconnection requests from other telecommunications business operators or dedicated network operators.  
The term "dominant telecommunications business operator" as used in the preceding paragraph refers to an operator that controls essential basic telecommunications infrastructure and holds a significant market share in the telecommunications business market, and is capable of materially affecting the ability of other telecommunications business operators to enter the telecommunications business market.  
The dominant telecommunications business operator shall be determined by the competent information industry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　主导的电信业务经营者应当按照非歧视和透明化的原则，制定包括网间互联的程序、时限、非捆绑网络元素目录等内容的互联规程。互联规程应当报国务院信息产业主管部门审查同意。该互联规程对主导的电信业务经营者的互联互通活动具有约束力。

**Article 18** A dominant telecommunications business operator shall, in accordance with the principles of non-discrimination and transparency, formulate interconnection rules that include procedures, time limits for network interconnection, and a catalogue of unbundled network elements. The interconnection rules shall be submitted to the competent information industry department of the State Council for review and approval. Such interconnection rules shall be binding on the interconnection activities of the dominant telecommunications business operator.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公用电信网之间、公用电信网与专用电信网之间的网间互联，由网间互联双方按照国务院信息产业主管部门的网间互联管理规定进行互联协商，并订立网间互联协议。

**Article 19** Interconnection between public telecommunications networks, or between public telecommunications networks and dedicated telecommunications networks, shall be negotiated by the parties to the interconnection in accordance with the interconnection management regulations of the competent information industry department of the State Council, and an interconnection agreement shall be concluded.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　网间互联双方经协商未能达成网间互联协议的，自一方提出互联要求之日起60日内，任何一方均可以按照网间互联覆盖范围向国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构申请协调；收到申请的机关应当依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)第一款规定的原则进行协调，促使网间互联双方达成协议；自网间互联一方或者双方申请协调之日起45日内经协调仍不能达成协议的，由协调机关随机邀请电信技术专家和其他有关方面专家进行公开论证并提出网间互联方案。协调机关应当根据专家论证结论和提出的网间互联方案作出决定，强制实现互联互通。

**Article 20** If the parties to interconnection fail to reach an interconnection agreement through negotiation, either party may, within 60 days from the date when one party proposed the interconnection, apply for mediation to the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications regulatory authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government based on the coverage of the interconnection. The authority that receives the application shall conduct mediation in accordance with the principles set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 17 of these Regulations to promote the parties to reach an agreement. If no agreement can be reached within 45 days from the date of the mediation application by one or both parties, the mediating authority shall randomly invite telecommunications technical experts and other relevant experts to conduct public deliberation and propose an interconnection plan. The mediating authority shall make a decision based on the conclusions of the expert deliberation and the proposed interconnection plan, and enforce interconnection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　网间互联双方必须在协议约定或者决定规定的时限内实现互联互通，遵守网间互联协议和国务院信息产业主管部门的相关规定，保障网间通信畅通，任何一方不得擅自中断互联互通。网间互联遇有通信技术障碍的，双方应当立即采取有效措施予以消除。网间互联双方在互联互通中发生争议的，依照[本条例第二十条](#art-20)规定的程序和办法处理。  
网间互联的通信质量应当符合国家有关标准。主导的电信业务经营者向其他电信业务经营者提供网间互联，服务质量不得低于本网内的同类业务及向其子公司或者分支机构提供的同类业务质量。

**Article 21** The parties to interconnection shall achieve interconnection within the time limit stipulated in the agreement or the decision, abide by the interconnection agreement and the relevant regulations of the competent information industry department of the State Council, ensure smooth communication over the interconnection, and neither party may arbitrarily interrupt the interconnection. If technical obstacles occur in the interconnection, both parties shall immediately take effective measures to eliminate them. Disputes arising during interconnection shall be handled in accordance with the procedures and methods provided in Article 20 of these Regulations.  
The communication quality of the interconnection shall comply with relevant State standards. A dominant telecommunications business operator providing interconnection to other telecommunications business operators shall ensure that the quality of service is not lower than that of similar services within its own network or similar services provided to its subsidiaries or branches.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　网间互联的费用结算与分摊应当执行国家有关规定，不得在规定标准之外加收费用。  
网间互联的技术标准、费用结算办法和具体管理规定，由国务院信息产业主管部门制定。

**Article 22** The settlement and allocation of costs for network interconnection shall comply with relevant State regulations, and no additional fees beyond the prescribed standards may be charged.  
The technical standards, cost settlement methods, and specific management regulations for network interconnection shall be formulated by the competent information industry department of the State Council.

#### 第三节　电信资费

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电信资费实行市场调节价。电信业务经营者应当统筹考虑生产经营成本、电信市场供求状况等因素，合理确定电信业务资费标准。

**Article 23** Telecommunications tariffs shall be subject to market-adjusted pricing. Telecommunications business operators shall comprehensively consider factors such as production and operation costs and market supply and demand conditions, and reasonably determine the tariff rates for telecommunications services.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家依法加强对电信业务经营者资费行为的监管，建立健全监管规则，维护消费者合法权益。

**Article 24** The State shall strengthen the supervision over the tariff practices of telecommunications business operators according to law, establish and improve regulatory rules, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of consumers.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　电信业务经营者应当根据国务院信息产业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构的要求，提供准确、完备的业务成本数据及其他有关资料。

**Article 25** Telecommunications business operators shall, at the request of the competent information industry department of the State Council and the telecommunications regulatory authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, provide accurate and complete data on business costs and other relevant information.

#### 第四节　电信资源

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家对电信资源统一规划、集中管理、合理分配，实行有偿使用制度。  
前款所称电信资源，是指无线电频率、卫星轨道位置、电信网码号等用于实现电信功能且有限的资源。

**Article 26** The State shall exercise unified planning, centralized management, and rational allocation of telecommunications resources, and implement a system of compensated use.  
The term "telecommunications resources" as used in the preceding paragraph refers to limited resources such as radio frequencies, satellite orbital positions, and telecommunications network code numbers that are used to realize telecommunications functions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电信业务经营者占有、使用电信资源，应当缴纳电信资源费。具体收费办法由国务院信息产业主管部门会同国务院财政部门、价格主管部门制定，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 27** Telecommunications business operators that occupy or use telecommunications resources shall pay telecommunications resource fees. The specific fee collection measures shall be formulated by the competent department of information industry under the State Council jointly with the finance department and the price department under the State Council, and shall be published and implemented after being approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　电信资源的分配，应当考虑电信资源规划、用途和预期服务能力。  
分配电信资源，可以采取指配的方式，也可以采用拍卖的方式。  
取得电信资源使用权的，应当在规定的时限内启用所分配的资源，并达到规定的最低使用规模。未经国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构批准，不得擅自使用、转让、出租电信资源或者改变电信资源的用途。

**Article 28** The allocation of telecommunications resources shall take into account the telecommunications resource plan, purpose and expected service capability.  
The allocation of telecommunications resources may be carried out by means of designation or by means of auction.  
A person who obtains the right to use telecommunications resources shall start to use the allocated resources within the specified time limit and reach the specified minimum scale of use. Without the approval of the competent department of information industry under the State Council or the telecommunications administration authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, no person may use, transfer, lease or change the purpose of telecommunications resources without authorization.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　电信资源使用者依法取得电信网码号资源后，主导的电信业务经营者和其他有关单位有义务采取必要的技术措施，配合电信资源使用者实现其电信网码号资源的功能。  
法律、行政法规对电信资源管理另有特别规定的，从其规定。

**Article 29** After a telecommunications resource user has obtained the telecommunications network code number resources according to law, the dominant telecommunications business operator and other relevant entities shall be obliged to take necessary technical measures to cooperate with the telecommunications resource user in realizing the functions of its telecommunications network code number resources.  
Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the management of telecommunications resources, such provisions shall prevail.

### 第三章　电信服务

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　电信业务经营者应当按照国家规定的电信服务标准向电信用户提供服务。电信业务经营者提供服务的种类、范围、资费标准和时限，应当向社会公布，并报省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构备案。  
电信用户有权自主选择使用依法开办的各类电信业务。

**Article 30** Telecommunications business operators shall provide services to telecommunications users in accordance with the telecommunications service standards prescribed by the State. The types, scope, tariff standards, and time limits of services provided by telecommunications business operators shall be published to the public and reported to the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for record.  
Telecommunications users shall have the right to independently choose and use various types of telecommunications services lawfully operated.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　电信用户申请安装、移装电信终端设备的，电信业务经营者应当在其公布的时限内保证装机开通；由于电信业务经营者的原因逾期未能装机开通的，应当每日按照收取的安装费、移装费或者其他费用数额1%的比例，向电信用户支付违约金。

**Article 31** Where a telecommunications user applies for installation or relocation of telecommunications terminal equipment, the telecommunications business operator shall guarantee the installation and activation within the time limit it has published. If the installation and activation are delayed due to reasons attributable to the telecommunications business operator, the operator shall pay liquidated damages to the telecommunications user at a rate of 1% of the installation fee, relocation fee, or other fees collected for each day of delay.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　电信用户申告电信服务障碍的，电信业务经营者应当自接到申告之日起，城镇48小时、农村72小时内修复或者调通；不能按期修复或者调通的，应当及时通知电信用户，并免收障碍期间的月租费用。但是，属于电信终端设备的原因造成电信服务障碍的除外。

**Article 32** Where a telecommunications user reports an obstruction of telecommunications services, the telecommunications business operator shall repair or restore the service within 48 hours in urban areas or 72 hours in rural areas from the date of receiving the report. If it cannot repair or restore the service within the prescribed time, it shall promptly notify the telecommunications user and waive the monthly rental fee for the period of obstruction, unless the obstruction is caused by the telecommunications terminal equipment.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　电信业务经营者应当为电信用户交费和查询提供方便。电信用户要求提供国内长途通信、国际通信、移动通信和信息服务等收费清单的，电信业务经营者应当免费提供。  
电信用户出现异常的巨额电信费用时，电信业务经营者一经发现，应当尽可能迅速告知电信用户，并采取相应的措施。  
前款所称巨额电信费用，是指突然出现超过电信用户此前3个月平均电信费用5倍以上的费用。

**Article 33** Telecommunications business operators shall provide convenience for telecommunications users to pay fees and make inquiries. Where a telecommunications user requests a billing statement for domestic long-distance communications, international communications, mobile communications, information services, or the like, the telecommunications business operator shall provide it free of charge.  
When an abnormally large telecommunications expense arises for a telecommunications user, the telecommunications business operator, upon discovery, shall inform the user as promptly as possible and take corresponding measures.  
The term "abnormally large telecommunications expense" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means an expense that suddenly exceeds five times the average telecommunications expense of the user for the preceding three months.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　电信用户应当按照约定的时间和方式及时、足额地向电信业务经营者交纳电信费用；电信用户逾期不交纳电信费用的，电信业务经营者有权要求补交电信费用，并可以按照所欠费用每日加收3‰的违约金。  
对超过收费约定期限30日仍不交纳电信费用的电信用户，电信业务经营者可以暂停向其提供电信服务。电信用户在电信业务经营者暂停服务60日内仍未补交电信费用和违约金的，电信业务经营者可以终止提供服务，并可以依法追缴欠费和违约金。  
经营移动电信业务的经营者可以与电信用户约定交纳电信费用的期限、方式，不受前款规定期限的限制。  
电信业务经营者应当在迟延交纳电信费用的电信用户补足电信费用、违约金后的48小时内，恢复暂停的电信服务。

**Article 34** Telecommunications users shall pay telecommunications fees to telecommunications business operators in a timely and full manner according to the agreed time and method. If a telecommunications user fails to pay telecommunications fees within the time limit, the telecommunications business operator shall have the right to demand payment of the overdue fees and may impose liquidated damages at a rate of 3‰ per day on the overdue amount.  
If a telecommunications user still fails to pay telecommunications fees within 30 days after the expiry of the agreed payment period, the telecommunications business operator may suspend the provision of telecommunications services to that user. If the telecommunications user fails to pay the overdue fees and liquidated damages within 60 days after the suspension of services by the telecommunications business operator, the operator may terminate the provision of services and may recover the overdue fees and liquidated damages according to law.  
Operators engaged in mobile telecommunications business may agree with telecommunications users on the time limit and method for payment of telecommunications fees, and such agreement shall not be subject to the time limit restrictions set forth in the preceding paragraph.  
Telecommunications business operators shall restore the suspended telecommunications services within 48 hours after the telecommunications user who delayed payment has paid the overdue telecommunications fees and liquidated damages.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　电信业务经营者因工程施工、网络建设等原因，影响或者可能影响正常电信服务的，必须按照规定的时限及时告知用户，并向省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构报告。  
因前款原因中断电信服务的，电信业务经营者应当相应减免用户在电信服务中断期间的相关费用。  
出现本条第一款规定的情形，电信业务经营者未及时告知用户的，应当赔偿由此给用户造成的损失。

**Article 35** Where a telecommunications business operator, due to project construction, network construction, or other reasons, affects or may affect normal telecommunications services, it must promptly inform users within the prescribed time limit and report to the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
If telecommunications services are interrupted due to the reasons set forth in the preceding paragraph, the telecommunications business operator shall correspondingly reduce or waive the related fees of the user during the period of service interruption.  
If, under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, the telecommunications business operator fails to inform the user in a timely manner, it shall compensate the user for the losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经营本地电话业务和移动电话业务的电信业务经营者，应当免费向用户提供火警、匪警、医疗急救、交通事故报警等公益性电信服务并保障通信线路畅通。

**Article 36** Telecommunications business operators engaged in local telephone services and mobile telephone services shall provide free of charge to users such public interest telecommunications services as fire alarm, police alarm, medical emergency, traffic accident alert, and shall ensure the smooth flow of communication lines.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　电信业务经营者应当及时为需要通过中继线接入其电信网的集团用户，提供平等、合理的接入服务。  
未经批准，电信业务经营者不得擅自中断接入服务。

**Article 37** Telecommunications business operators shall promptly provide equal and reasonable access services to group users that need to access their telecommunications networks through trunk lines.  
Without approval, a telecommunications business operator shall not arbitrarily interrupt access services.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　电信业务经营者应当建立健全内部服务质量管理制度，并可以制定并公布施行高于国家规定的电信服务标准的企业标准。  
电信业务经营者应当采取各种形式广泛听取电信用户意见，接受社会监督，不断提高电信服务质量。

**Article 38** Telecommunications business operators shall establish and improve internal service quality management systems, and may formulate, publish, and implement enterprise standards that are higher than the telecommunications service standards prescribed by the State.  
Telecommunications business operators shall adopt various forms to extensively solicit opinions from telecommunications users, accept social supervision, and continuously improve the quality of telecommunications services.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　电信业务经营者提供的电信服务达不到国家规定的电信服务标准或者其公布的企业标准的，或者电信用户对交纳电信费用持有异议的，电信用户有权要求电信业务经营者予以解决；电信业务经营者拒不解决或者电信用户对解决结果不满意的，电信用户有权向国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构或者其他有关部门申诉。收到申诉的机关必须对申诉及时处理，并自收到申诉之日起30日内向申诉者作出答复。  
电信用户对交纳本地电话费用有异议的，电信业务经营者还应当应电信用户的要求免费提供本地电话收费依据，并有义务采取必要措施协助电信用户查找原因。

**Article 39** If the telecommunications services provided by a telecommunications business operator fail to meet the telecommunications service standards prescribed by the State or its published enterprise standards, or if a telecommunications user disputes the payment of telecommunications fees, the user shall have the right to demand that the telecommunications business operator resolve the matter. If the telecommunications business operator refuses to resolve the matter or if the user is dissatisfied with the resolution, the user shall have the right to petition the competent department of information industry under the State Council, the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or any other relevant department. The organ receiving the petition must handle it promptly and shall provide a response to the petitioner within 30 days from the date of receiving the petition.  
If a telecommunications user disputes the payment of local telephone fees, the telecommunications business operator shall, at the user's request, provide evidence of the local telephone charges free of charge and shall be obliged to take necessary measures to assist the user in identifying the cause.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　电信业务经营者在电信服务中，不得有下列行为：  
(一)以任何方式限定电信用户使用其指定的业务；  
(二)限定电信用户购买其指定的电信终端设备或者拒绝电信用户使用自备的已经取得入网许可的电信终端设备；  
(三)无正当理由拒绝、拖延或者中止对电信用户的电信服务；  
(四)对电信用户不履行公开作出的承诺或者作容易引起误解的虚假宣传；  
(五)以不正当手段刁难电信用户或者对投诉的电信用户打击报复。

**Article 40** In the course of providing telecommunications services, telecommunications business operators shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) In any manner, restricting a telecommunications user to using services designated by the operator;  
(2) Restricting a telecommunications user to purchasing telecommunications terminal equipment designated by the operator, or refusing to allow a user to use self-provided telecommunications terminal equipment that has obtained network access permission;  
(3) Without justifiable reasons, refusing, delaying, or suspending telecommunications services to a user;  
(4) Failing to fulfill publicly made commitments to a telecommunications user or making false or misleading publicity;  
(5) Using improper means to make things difficult for a telecommunications user or retaliating against a user who has filed a complaint.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　电信业务经营者在电信业务经营活动中，不得有下列行为：  
(一)以任何方式限制电信用户选择其他电信业务经营者依法开办的电信服务；  
(二)对其经营的不同业务进行不合理的交叉补贴；  
(三)以排挤竞争对手为目的，低于成本提供电信业务或者服务，进行不正当竞争。

**Article 41** In the course of operating telecommunications business, telecommunications business operators shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) In any manner, restricting a telecommunications user from choosing telecommunications services lawfully operated by another telecommunications business operator;  
(2) Engaging in unreasonable cross-subsidization among the different services they operate;  
(3) For the purpose of excluding competitors, providing telecommunications business or services below cost to engage in unfair competition.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构应当依据职权对电信业务经营者的电信服务质量和经营活动进行监督检查，并向社会公布监督抽查结果。

**Article 42** The competent department of information industry under the State Council or the telecommunications administration authority of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, exercise supervision and inspection over the quality of telecommunications services and business activities of telecommunications business operators, and shall publish the results of supervision and random inspections to the public.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　电信业务经营者必须按照国家有关规定履行相应的电信普遍服务义务。  
国务院信息产业主管部门可以采取指定的或者招标的方式确定电信业务经营者具体承担电信普遍服务的义务。  
电信普遍服务成本补偿管理办法，由国务院信息产业主管部门会同国务院财政部门、价格主管部门制定，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 43** Telecommunications business operators must fulfill the corresponding universal telecommunications service obligations in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The competent department of information industry under the State Council may, by means of designation or tender, determine a telecommunications business operator to specifically undertake the universal telecommunications service obligations.  
The measures for the management of cost compensation for universal telecommunications services shall be formulated by the competent department of information industry under the State Council jointly with the finance department and the pricing department under the State Council, and shall be published for implementation after approval by the State Council.

### 第四章　电信建设

#### 第一节　电信设施建设

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　公用电信网、专用电信网、广播电视传输网的建设应当接受国务院信息产业主管部门的统筹规划和行业管理。  
属于全国性信息网络工程或者国家规定限额以上建设项目的公用电信网、专用电信网、广播电视传输网建设，在按照国家基本建设项目审批程序报批前，应当征得国务院信息产业主管部门同意。  
基础电信建设项目应当纳入地方各级人民政府城市建设总体规划和村镇、集镇建设总体规划。

**Article 44** The construction of public telecommunications networks, dedicated telecommunications networks, and broadcasting and television transmission networks shall be subject to the overall planning and industry management of the competent department of information industry under the State Council.  
The construction of public telecommunications networks, dedicated telecommunications networks, and broadcasting and television transmission networks that are national information network projects or construction projects exceeding the limits prescribed by the State shall, before being submitted for approval in accordance with the approval procedures for national capital construction projects, obtain the consent of the competent department of information industry under the State Council.  
Basic telecommunications construction projects shall be incorporated into the overall urban construction plan of local people's governments at all levels and the overall construction plan of villages, towns and market towns.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　城市建设和村镇、集镇建设应当配套设置电信设施。建筑物内的电信管线和配线设施以及建设项目用地范围内的电信管道，应当纳入建设项目的设计文件，并随建设项目同时施工与验收。所需经费应当纳入建设项目概算。  
有关单位或者部门规划、建设道路、桥梁、隧道或者地下铁道等，应当事先通知省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构和电信业务经营者，协商预留电信管线等事宜。

**Article 45** Urban construction and the construction of villages, towns and market towns shall be provided with supporting telecommunications facilities. The telecommunications conduits and distribution facilities within buildings and the telecommunications pipelines within the land area of a construction project shall be included in the design documents of the construction project and shall be constructed and accepted together with the construction project. The necessary funds shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.  
Where relevant entities or departments plan or construct roads, bridges, tunnels or subways, etc., they shall notify in advance the telecommunications administration authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government and the telecommunications business operators to negotiate matters such as reserving telecommunications conduits.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　基础电信业务经营者可以在民用建筑物上附挂电信线路或者设置小型天线、移动通信基站等公用电信设施，但是应当事先通知建筑物产权人或者使用人，并按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的标准向该建筑物的产权人或者其他权利人支付使用费。

**Article 46** A basic telecommunications business operator may attach telecommunications lines or install public telecommunications facilities such as small antennas and mobile communication base stations on civil buildings, but shall notify in advance the owner or user of the building and pay the usage fee to the owner or other right holder of the building at the rate prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　建设地下、水底等隐蔽电信设施和高空电信设施，应当按照国家有关规定设置标志。  
基础电信业务经营者建设海底电信缆线，应当征得国务院信息产业主管部门同意，并征求有关部门意见后，依法办理有关手续。海底电信缆线由国务院有关部门在海图上标出。

**Article 47** The construction of concealed telecommunications facilities such as underground or underwater facilities and elevated telecommunications facilities shall be marked in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
A basic telecommunications business operator that constructs submarine telecommunications cables shall obtain the consent of the competent department of information industry under the State Council and, after soliciting the opinions of relevant departments, go through the relevant procedures according to law. Submarine telecommunications cables shall be marked on charts by the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　任何单位或者个人不得擅自改动或者迁移他人的电信线路及其他电信设施；遇有特殊情况必须改动或者迁移的，应当征得该电信设施产权人同意，由提出改动或者迁移要求的单位或者个人承担改动或者迁移所需费用，并赔偿由此造成的经济损失。

**Article 48** No entity or individual may, without authorization, alter or relocate telecommunications lines or other telecommunications facilities of others; where alteration or relocation is necessary due to special circumstances, the consent of the owner of the telecommunications facilities shall be obtained, and the entity or individual that proposes the alteration or relocation shall bear the costs required for the alteration or relocation and compensate for the economic losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　从事施工、生产、种植树木等活动，不得危及电信线路或者其他电信设施的安全或者妨碍线路畅通；可能危及电信安全时，应当事先通知有关电信业务经营者，并由从事该活动的单位或者个人负责采取必要的安全防护措施。  
违反前款规定，损害电信线路或者其他电信设施或者妨碍线路畅通的，应当恢复原状或者予以修复，并赔偿由此造成的经济损失。

**Article 49** Activities such as construction, production, and tree planting shall not endanger the safety of telecommunications lines or other telecommunications facilities or impede the free flow of lines; where there is a possibility of endangering telecommunications safety, the relevant telecommunications business operator shall be notified in advance, and the entity or individual conducting the activity shall be responsible for taking necessary safety protection measures.  
Whoever, in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, damages telecommunications lines or other telecommunications facilities or impedes the free flow of lines shall restore the original state or make repairs and compensate for the economic losses caused thereby.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　从事电信线路建设，应当与已建的电信线路保持必要的安全距离；难以避开或者必须穿越，或者需要使用已建电信管道的，应当与已建电信线路的产权人协商，并签订协议；经协商不能达成协议的，根据不同情况，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构协调解决。

**Article 50** Entities engaged in the construction of telecommunications lines shall maintain a necessary safe distance from existing telecommunications lines; where it is difficult to avoid or it is necessary to cross, or where it is necessary to use existing telecommunications conduits, they shall negotiate with the owner of the existing telecommunications lines and conclude an agreement; where no agreement can be reached through negotiation, the matter shall be coordinated and resolved by the competent department of information industry under the State Council or the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, depending on the circumstances.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　任何组织或者个人不得阻止或者妨碍基础电信业务经营者依法从事电信设施建设和向电信用户提供公共电信服务；但是，国家规定禁止或者限制进入的区域除外。

**Article 51** No organization or individual may obstruct or impede a basic telecommunications business operator from engaging in telecommunications facility construction and providing public telecommunications services to telecommunications users according to law; except for areas where entry is prohibited or restricted by the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　执行特殊通信、应急通信和抢修、抢险任务的电信车辆，经公安交通管理机关批准，在保障交通安全畅通的前提下可以不受各种禁止机动车通行标志的限制。

**Article 52** Telecommunications vehicles performing tasks of special communications, emergency communications, emergency repairs and rescue operations may, with the approval of the public security traffic management authority and on the premise of ensuring traffic safety and smooth flow, be exempt from restrictions of various signs prohibiting motor vehicle traffic.

#### 第二节　电信设备进网

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家对电信终端设备、无线电通信设备和涉及网间互联的设备实行进网许可制度。  
接入公用电信网的电信终端设备、无线电通信设备和涉及网间互联的设备，必须符合国家规定的标准并取得进网许可证。  
实行进网许可制度的电信设备目录，由国务院信息产业主管部门会同国务院产品质量监督部门制定并公布施行。

**Article 53** The State implements a network access licensing system for telecommunications terminal equipment, radio communication equipment and equipment involving network interconnection.  
Telecommunications terminal equipment, radio communication equipment and equipment involving network interconnection that are connected to the public telecommunications network must comply with the standards prescribed by the State and obtain a network access license.  
The catalogue of telecommunications equipment subject to the network access licensing system shall be formulated and published for implementation by the competent department of information industry under the State Council jointly with the product quality supervision department under the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　办理电信设备进网许可证的，应当向国务院信息产业主管部门提出申请，并附送经国务院产品质量监督部门认可的电信设备检测机构出具的检测报告或者认证机构出具的产品质量认证证书。  
国务院信息产业主管部门应当自收到电信设备进网许可申请之日起60日内，对申请及电信设备检测报告或者产品质量认证证书审查完毕。经审查合格的，颁发进网许可证；经审查不合格的，应当书面答复并说明理由。

**Article 54** An applicant for a telecommunications equipment network access license shall submit an application to the competent department of information industry under the State Council and attach the inspection report issued by a telecommunications equipment inspection institution recognized by the product quality supervision department under the State Council or the product quality certification certificate issued by a certification institution.  
The competent department of information industry under the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application for a telecommunications equipment network access license, complete the examination of the application and the telecommunications equipment inspection report or product quality certification certificate. Where the examination is passed, the network access license shall be issued; where the examination fails, a written reply shall be given with the reasons stated.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　电信设备生产企业必须保证获得进网许可的电信设备的质量稳定、可靠，不得降低产品质量和性能。  
电信设备生产企业应当在其生产的获得进网许可的电信设备上粘贴进网许可标志。  
国务院产品质量监督部门应当会同国务院信息产业主管部门对获得进网许可证的电信设备进行质量跟踪和监督抽查，公布抽查结果。

**Article 55** A telecommunications equipment production enterprise must ensure that the telecommunications equipment that has obtained a network access license is stable and reliable in quality and shall not degrade the product quality and performance.  
A telecommunications equipment production enterprise shall affix a network access license mark on the telecommunications equipment that it produces and has obtained a network access license.  
The product quality supervision department under the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of information industry under the State Council, conduct quality tracking and supervisory spot checks on telecommunications equipment that has obtained a network access license, and publish the results of the spot checks.

### 第五章　电信安全

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　任何组织或者个人不得利用电信网络制作、复制、发布、传播含有下列内容的信息：  
(一)反对宪法所确定的基本原则的；  
(二)危害国家安全，泄露国家秘密，颠覆国家政权，破坏国家统一的；  
(三)损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
(四)煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结的；  
(五)破坏国家宗教政策，宣扬邪教和封建迷信的；  
(六)散布谣言，扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
(七)散布淫秽、色情、赌博、暴力、凶杀、恐怖或者教唆犯罪的；  
(八)侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
(九)含有法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容的。

**Article 56** No organization or individual shall use the telecommunications network to produce, reproduce, publish, or disseminate information containing the following content:  
(1) opposing the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) endangering national security, leaking state secrets, subverting state power, or undermining national unity;  
(3) damaging national honor or interests;  
(4) inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, or undermining ethnic solidarity;  
(5) undermining the state's religious policy, promoting cults or feudal superstitions;  
(6) spreading rumors, disrupting social order, or undermining social stability;  
(7) disseminating obscenity, pornography, gambling, violence, murder, terror, or incitement to crime;  
(8) insulting or slandering others, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) containing other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　任何组织或者个人不得有下列危害电信网络安全和信息安全的行为：  
(一)对电信网的功能或者存储、处理、传输的数据和应用程序进行删除或者修改；  
(二)利用电信网从事窃取或者破坏他人信息、损害他人合法权益的活动；  
(三)故意制作、复制、传播计算机病毒或者以其他方式攻击他人电信网络等电信设施；  
(四)危害电信网络安全和信息安全的其他行为。

**Article 57** No organization or individual shall engage in any of the following acts that endanger the security of telecommunications networks and information security:  
(1) deleting or modifying the functions of a telecommunications network or the data and application programs stored, processed, or transmitted therein;  
(2) using a telecommunications network to steal or destroy others' information or to impair others' lawful rights and interests;  
(3) intentionally producing, reproducing, or disseminating computer viruses or otherwise attacking the telecommunications network or other telecommunications facilities of others;  
(4) other acts that endanger the security of telecommunications networks and information security.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　任何组织或者个人不得有下列扰乱电信市场秩序的行为：  
(一)采取租用电信国际专线、私设转接设备或者其他方法，擅自经营国际或者香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区电信业务；  
(二)盗接他人电信线路，复制他人电信码号，使用明知是盗接、复制的电信设施或者码号；  
(三)伪造、变造电话卡及其他各种电信服务有价凭证；  
(四)以虚假、冒用的身份证件办理入网手续并使用移动电话。

**Article 58** No organization or individual shall engage in any of the following acts that disrupt the order of the telecommunications market:  
(1) engaging without authorization in international telecommunications services or telecommunications services for the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region by leasing international private lines of telecommunications, privately installing relay equipment, or by other methods;  
(2) stealing and connecting to others' telecommunications lines, reproducing others' telecommunications codes, or using telecommunications facilities or codes that are known to have been stolen, connected, or reproduced;  
(3) forging or altering telephone cards or other kinds of valuable vouchers for telecommunications services;  
(4) using false or fraudulently used identity documents to handle network access procedures and using mobile telephones.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　电信业务经营者应当按照国家有关电信安全的规定，建立健全内部安全保障制度，实行安全保障责任制。

**Article 59** Telecommunications service operators shall, in accordance with the state's provisions concerning telecommunications security, establish and improve internal security guarantee systems and implement a security guarantee responsibility system.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　电信业务经营者在电信网络的设计、建设和运行中，应当做到与国家安全和电信网络安全的需求同步规划，同步建设，同步运行。

**Article 60** When designing, constructing, and operating telecommunications networks, telecommunications service operators shall carry out synchronized planning, synchronized construction, and synchronized operation with the requirements for national security and telecommunications network security.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　在公共信息服务中，电信业务经营者发现电信网络中传输的信息明显属于[本条例第五十六条](#art-56)所列内容的，应当立即停止传输，保存有关记录，并向国家有关机关报告。

**Article 61** In public information services, if a telecommunications service operator discovers that information transmitted in a telecommunications network obviously falls within the content listed in Article 56 of these Regulations, it shall immediately stop the transmission, preserve the relevant records, and report to the relevant state authorities.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　使用电信网络传输信息的内容及其后果由电信用户负责。  
电信用户使用电信网络传输的信息属于国家秘密信息的，必须依照保守国家秘密法的规定采取保密措施。

**Article 62** The content of information transmitted using a telecommunications network and the consequences thereof shall be the responsibility of the telecommunications user.  
If information transmitted by a telecommunications user using a telecommunications network constitutes state secret information, the user must take confidentiality measures in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Guarding State Secrets.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　在发生重大自然灾害等紧急情况下，经国务院批准，国务院信息产业主管部门可以调用各种电信设施，确保重要通信畅通。

**Article 63** In emergency situations such as the occurrence of major natural disasters, the competent information industry department of the State Council may, upon approval of the State Council, mobilize various telecommunications facilities to ensure the smooth flow of important communications.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在中华人民共和国境内从事国际通信业务，必须通过国务院信息产业主管部门批准设立的国际通信出入口局进行。  
我国内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区之间的通信，参照前款规定办理。

**Article 64** Any person engaging in international communications business within the territory of the People's Republic of China must do so through international communications gateway offices established with the approval of the competent information industry department of the State Council.  
Communications between the mainland of China and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region shall be handled by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　电信用户依法使用电信的自由和通信秘密受法律保护。除因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪的需要，由公安机关、国家安全机关或者人民检察院依照法律规定的程序对电信内容进行检查外，任何组织或者个人不得以任何理由对电信内容进行检查。  
电信业务经营者及其工作人员不得擅自向他人提供电信用户使用电信网络所传输信息的内容。

**Article 65** The freedom of a telecommunications user to use telecommunications according to law and his secrecy of communications shall be protected by law. Except where it is necessary for national security or for the investigation of criminal offenses, and public security organs, state security organs, or a people's procuratorate conduct an inspection of the telecommunications content in accordance with the procedures provided by law, no organization or individual may inspect the content of telecommunications on any grounds.  
Telecommunications service operators and their staff shall not, without authorization, provide to others the content of information transmitted by telecommunications users through the use of telecommunications networks.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反[本条例第五十六条](#art-56)、第五十七条的规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关、国家安全机关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 66** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 56 or Article 57 of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ or the state security organ shall impose a punishment in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　有[本条例第五十八条](#art-58)第(二)、(三)、(四)项所列行为之一，扰乱电信市场秩序，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权责令改正，没收违法所得，处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 67** Whoever commits any of the acts listed in subparagraphs (2), (3), or (4) of Article 58 of these Regulations and thereby disrupts the order of the telecommunications market, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例的规定，伪造、冒用、转让电信业务经营许可证、电信设备进网许可证或者编造在电信设备上标注的进网许可证编号的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权没收违法所得，处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 68** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations by forging, fraudulently using, or transferring a telecommunications service operation license, a telecommunications equipment network access license, or fabricating the network access license number marked on telecommunications equipment, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权责令改正，没收违法所得，处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)第三款的规定或者有[本条例第五十八条](#art-58)第(一)项所列行为，擅自经营电信业务的，或者超范围经营电信业务的；  
(二)未通过国务院信息产业主管部门批准，设立国际通信出入口进行国际通信的；  
(三)擅自使用、转让、出租电信资源或者改变电信资源用途的；  
(四)擅自中断网间互联互通或者接入服务的；  
(五)拒不履行普遍服务义务的。

**Article 69** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) violating the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 7 of these Regulations or committing any of the acts listed in subparagraph (1) of Article 58 of these Regulations, and engaging without authorization in telecommunications services or engaging in telecommunications services beyond the approved scope;  
(2) establishing an international communications gateway to conduct international communications without the approval of the competent information industry department of the State Council;  
(3) using, transferring, or leasing telecommunications resources without authorization, or changing the purpose of telecommunications resources;  
(4) disconnecting network interconnection or access services without authorization;  
(5) refusing to perform the universal service obligation.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权责令改正，没收违法所得，处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)在电信网间互联中违反规定加收费用的；  
(二)遇有网间通信技术障碍，不采取有效措施予以消除的；  
(三)擅自向他人提供电信用户使用电信网络所传输信息的内容的；  
(四)拒不按照规定缴纳电信资源使用费的。

**Article 70** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) charging additional fees in violation of regulations in the interconnection of telecommunications networks;  
(2) failing to take effective measures to eliminate technical obstacles in inter-network communications when such obstacles occur;  
(3) providing to others without authorization the content of information transmitted by telecommunications users through the use of telecommunications networks;  
(4) refusing to pay the telecommunications resource usage fees in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)的规定，在电信业务经营活动中进行不正当竞争的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权责令改正，处10万元以上100万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 71** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 41 of these Regulations and engages in unfair competition in telecommunications service operation activities, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依据职权责令改正，处5万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)拒绝其他电信业务经营者提出的互联互通要求的；  
(二)拒不执行国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构依法作出的互联互通决定的；  
(三)向其他电信业务经营者提供网间互联的服务质量低于本网及其子公司或者分支机构的。

**Article 72** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within its authority, order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) refusing a request for interconnection made by another telecommunications service operator;  
(2) refusing to implement an interconnection decision lawfully made by the competent information industry department of the State Council or by the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(3) providing to another telecommunications service operator a quality of inter-network interconnection service that is lower than that of its own network or its subsidiaries or branches.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)第一款、第三十九条第二款的规定，电信业务经营者拒绝免费为电信用户提供国内长途通信、国际通信、移动通信和信息服务等收费清单，或者电信用户对交纳本地电话费用有异议并提出要求时，拒绝为电信用户免费提供本地电话收费依据的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正，并向电信用户赔礼道歉；拒不改正并赔礼道歉的，处以警告，并处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 73** Where a telecommunications service operator, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 33 or paragraph 2 of Article 39 of these Regulations, refuses to provide free of charge to a telecommunications user charge lists for domestic long-distance communications, international communications, mobile communications, and information services, or, when the telecommunications user has a disagreement with the local telephone charges paid and makes a request, refuses to provide free of charge to the telecommunications user the basis for the local telephone charges, the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction and require the operator to make an apology to the telecommunications user; if the operator refuses to make correction and apology, it shall be given a warning and concurrently a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反[本条例第四十条](#art-40)的规定，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正，并向电信用户赔礼道歉，赔偿电信用户损失；拒不改正并赔礼道歉、赔偿损失的，处以警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 74** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 40 of these Regulations, the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction, require the operator to make an apology to the telecommunications user, and compensate the telecommunications user for losses; if the operator refuses to make correction, apology, and compensation for losses, it shall be given a warning and concurrently a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)销售未取得进网许可的电信终端设备的；  
(二)非法阻止或者妨碍电信业务经营者向电信用户提供公共电信服务的；  
(三)擅自改动或者迁移他人的电信线路及其他电信设施的。

**Article 75** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the telecommunications administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) selling telecommunications terminal equipment that has not obtained network access permission;  
(2) illegally preventing or obstructing a telecommunications service operator from providing public telecommunications services to telecommunications users;  
(3) altering or relocating without authorization the telecommunications lines or other telecommunications facilities of others.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本条例的规定，获得电信设备进网许可证后降低产品质量和性能的，由产品质量监督部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 76** Any person who, after obtaining a network access license for telecommunications equipment, reduces the quality and performance of the product in violation of the provisions of these Regulations shall be punished by the product quality supervision department in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　有[本条例第五十六条](#art-56)、第五十七条和第五十八条所列禁止行为之一，情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销电信业务经营许可证。  
国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构吊销电信业务经营许可证后，应当通知企业登记机关。

**Article 77** Any person who commits any of the prohibited acts listed in Articles 56, 57 and 58 of these Regulations, if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the telecommunications business operation license.  
After the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government revokes the telecommunications business operation license, it shall notify the enterprise registration authority.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国务院信息产业主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 78** Where functionaries of the competent information industry department of the State Council or the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国境内投资与经营电信业务和香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区与台湾地区的组织或者个人在内地投资与经营电信业务的具体办法，由国务院另行制定。

**Article 79** The specific measures for foreign organizations or individuals to invest in and operate telecommunications businesses within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and for organizations or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region and the Taiwan region to invest in and operate telecommunications businesses in the mainland, shall be formulated separately by the State Council.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本条例自公布之日起施行。
