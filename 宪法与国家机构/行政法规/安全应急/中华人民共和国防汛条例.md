# 中华人民共和国防汛条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1991年7月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第86号公布　根据2005年7月15日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国防汛条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了做好防汛抗洪工作，保障人民生命财产安全和经济建设的顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内进行防汛抗洪活动，适用本条例。 第三条　防汛工作实行“安全第一，常备不懈，以防为主，全力抢险”的方针，遵循团结协作和局部利益服从全局利益的原则。 第四条　防汛工作实行各级人民政府行政首长负责制，实行统一指挥，分级分部门负责。各有关部门实行防汛岗位责任制。 第五条　任何单位和个人都有参加防汛抗洪的义务。 中国人民解放军和武装警察部队是防汛抗洪的重要力量。 第二章　防汛组织 第六条　国务院设立国家防汛总指挥部，负责组织领导全国的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在国务院水行政主管部门。 长江和黄河，可以设立由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和该江河的流域管理机构(以下简称流域机构)负责人等组成的防汛指挥机构，负责指挥所辖范围的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在流域机构。长江和黄河的重大防汛抗洪事项须经国家防汛总指挥部批准后执行。 国务院水行政主管部门所属的淮河、海河、珠江、松花江、辽河、太湖等流域机构，设立防汛办事机构，负责协调本流域的防汛日常工作。 第七条　有防汛任务的县级以上地方人民政府设立防汛指挥部，由有关部门、当地驻军、人民武装部负责人组成，由各级人民政府首长担任指挥。各级人民政府防汛指挥部在上级人民政府防汛指挥部和同级人民政府的领导下，执行上级防汛指令，制定各项防汛抗洪措施，统一指挥本地区的防汛抗洪工作。 各级人民政府防汛指挥部办事机构设在同级水行政主管部门；城市市区的防汛指挥部办事机构也可以设在城建主管部门，负责管理所辖范围的防汛日常工作。 第八条　石油、电力、邮电、铁路、公路、航运、工矿以及商业、物资等有防汛任务的部门和单位，汛期应当设立防汛机构，在有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部统一领导下，负责做好本行业和本单位的防汛工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好防汛抗洪工作，保障人民生命财产安全和经济建设的顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of doing a good job in flood prevention and flood fighting, ensuring the safety of people's lives and property, and the smooth progress of economic construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内进行防汛抗洪活动，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to activities of flood prevention and flood fighting conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　防汛工作实行“安全第一，常备不懈，以防为主，全力抢险”的方针，遵循团结协作和局部利益服从全局利益的原则。

**Article 3** Flood prevention work shall implement the policy of "safety first, constant preparedness, prevention as the mainstay, and all-out rescue", and shall follow the principles of unity and cooperation and subordination of partial interests to the overall interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　防汛工作实行各级人民政府行政首长负责制，实行统一指挥，分级分部门负责。各有关部门实行防汛岗位责任制。

**Article 4** Flood prevention work shall implement the responsibility system of the chief executive officers of people's governments at all levels, and shall implement unified command and division of responsibilities by levels and departments. All relevant departments shall implement the flood prevention post responsibility system.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人都有参加防汛抗洪的义务。  
中国人民解放军和武装警察部队是防汛抗洪的重要力量。

**Article 5** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to participate in flood prevention and flood fighting.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Armed Police Force are important forces for flood prevention and flood fighting.

### 第二章　防汛组织

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院设立国家防汛总指挥部，负责组织领导全国的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在国务院水行政主管部门。  
长江和黄河，可以设立由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和该江河的流域管理机构(以下简称流域机构)负责人等组成的防汛指挥机构，负责指挥所辖范围的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在流域机构。长江和黄河的重大防汛抗洪事项须经国家防汛总指挥部批准后执行。  
国务院水行政主管部门所属的淮河、海河、珠江、松花江、辽河、太湖等流域机构，设立防汛办事机构，负责协调本流域的防汛日常工作。

**Article 6** The State Council shall establish the State Flood Prevention and Relief Headquarters, which shall be responsible for organizing and leading the national flood prevention and flood fighting work, and its office shall be located in the water administrative department of the State Council.  
For the Yangtze River and the Yellow River, flood prevention command organizations may be established, composed of the responsible persons of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the river basin management organizations (hereinafter referred to as basin organizations) of the said rivers, which shall be responsible for directing flood prevention and flood fighting work within their respective jurisdictions, and their offices shall be located in the basin organizations. Major flood prevention and flood fighting matters concerning the Yangtze River and the Yellow River shall be implemented upon approval of the State Flood Prevention and Relief Headquarters.  
The basin organizations of the Huaihe, Haihe, Pearl, Songhua, Liaohe, Taihu and other rivers affiliated with the water administrative department of the State Council shall establish flood prevention offices, which shall be responsible for coordinating the daily flood prevention work of their respective basins.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有防汛任务的县级以上地方人民政府设立防汛指挥部，由有关部门、当地驻军、人民武装部负责人组成，由各级人民政府首长担任指挥。各级人民政府防汛指挥部在上级人民政府防汛指挥部和同级人民政府的领导下，执行上级防汛指令，制定各项防汛抗洪措施，统一指挥本地区的防汛抗洪工作。  
各级人民政府防汛指挥部办事机构设在同级水行政主管部门；城市市区的防汛指挥部办事机构也可以设在城建主管部门，负责管理所辖范围的防汛日常工作。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at or above the county level with flood prevention tasks shall establish flood prevention headquarters, composed of responsible persons from relevant departments, local garrisons, and the people's armed forces departments, and shall be commanded by the chief of the people's government at the corresponding level. The flood prevention headquarters of people's governments at all levels, under the leadership of the flood prevention headquarters of the higher-level people's government and the people's government at the same level, shall execute the flood prevention orders from higher levels, formulate various flood prevention and flood fighting measures, and uniformly direct the flood prevention and flood fighting work in their respective regions.  
The offices of the flood prevention headquarters of people's governments at all levels shall be located in the water administrative departments at the same level; the office of the flood prevention headquarters in urban areas may also be located in the competent department of urban construction, which shall be responsible for managing the daily flood prevention work in the areas under its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　石油、电力、邮电、铁路、公路、航运、工矿以及商业、物资等有防汛任务的部门和单位，汛期应当设立防汛机构，在有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部统一领导下，负责做好本行业和本单位的防汛工作。

**Article 8** Departments and units with flood prevention tasks, such as petroleum, electric power, posts and telecommunications, railways, highways, shipping, industrial and mining, commerce, and materials, shall, during the flood season, establish flood prevention organizations, and under the unified leadership of the flood prevention headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction, shall be responsible for doing a good job of flood prevention work in their respective trades and units.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　河道管理机构、水利水电工程管理单位和江河沿岸在建工程的建设单位，必须加强对所辖水工程设施的管理维护，保证其安全正常运行，组织和参加防汛抗洪工作。

**Article 9** River channel management organizations, water conservancy and hydropower project management units, and construction units of projects under construction along the banks of rivers shall strengthen the management and maintenance of the water engineering facilities under their jurisdiction, ensure their safe and normal operation, and organize and participate in flood prevention and flood fighting work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有防汛任务的地方人民政府应当组织以民兵为骨干的群众性防汛队伍，并责成有关部门将防汛队伍组成人员登记造册，明确各自的任务和责任。  
河道管理机构和其他防洪工程管理单位可以结合平时的管理任务，组织本单位的防汛抢险队伍，作为紧急抢险的骨干力量。

**Article 10** Local people's governments with flood prevention tasks shall organize mass flood prevention teams with the militia as the backbone, and shall instruct the relevant departments to register the members of the flood prevention teams and specify their respective tasks and responsibilities.  
River channel management organizations and other flood control engineering management units may, in combination with their routine management tasks, organize flood prevention and rescue teams of their own units, to serve as the backbone force for emergency rescue.

### 第三章　防汛准备

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有防汛任务的县级以上人民政府，应当根据流域综合规划、防洪工程实际状况和国家规定的防洪标准，制定防御洪水方案(包括对特大洪水的处置措施)。  
长江、黄河、淮河、海河的防御洪水方案，由国家防汛总指挥部制定，报国务院批准后施行；跨省、自治区、直辖市的其他江河的防御洪水方案，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定后，经有管辖权的流域机构审查同意，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报国务院或其授权的机构批准后施行。  
有防汛抗洪任务的城市人民政府，应当根据流域综合规划和江河的防御洪水方案，制定本城市的防御洪水方案，报上级人民政府或其授权的机构批准后施行。  
防御洪水方案经批准后，有关地方人民政府必须执行。

**Article 11** People's governments at or above the county level with flood prevention tasks shall, in accordance with the comprehensive river basin plan, the actual conditions of flood control projects, and the flood control standards prescribed by the State, formulate flood prevention plans (including measures for dealing with extraordinary floods).  
The flood prevention plans for the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, the Huaihe River, and the Haihe River shall be formulated by the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters and shall come into force after being reported to the State Council for approval; the flood prevention plans for other rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, after being formulated by the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government and after review and approval by the river basin authority with jurisdiction, be submitted by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government to the State Council or its authorized institution for approval and shall come into force after approval.  
The people's government of a city with flood prevention and flood fighting tasks shall, in accordance with the comprehensive river basin plan and the flood prevention plan for the river, formulate the flood prevention plan for that city and shall come into force after being reported to the people's government at the next higher level or its authorized institution for approval.  
After a flood prevention plan is approved, the relevant local people's governments must implement it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有防汛任务的地方，应当根据经批准的防御洪水方案制定洪水调度方案。长江、黄河、淮河、海河(海河流域的永定河、大清河、漳卫南运河和北三河)、松花江、辽河、珠江和太湖流域的洪水调度方案，由有关流域机构会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定，报国家防汛总指挥部批准。跨省、自治区、直辖市的其他江河的洪水调度方案，由有关流域机构会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定，报流域防汛指挥机构批准；没有设立流域防汛指挥机构的，报国家防汛总指挥部批准。其他江河的洪水调度方案，由有管辖权的水行政主管部门会同有关地方人民政府制定，报有管辖权的防汛指挥机构批准。  
洪水调度方案经批准后，有关地方人民政府必须执行。修改洪水调度方案，应当报经原批准机关批准。

**Article 12** In localities with flood prevention tasks, flood regulation plans shall be formulated in accordance with the approved flood prevention plans. The flood regulation plans for the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, the Huaihe River, the Haihe River (including the Yongding River, the Daqing River, the Zhangweinan Canal, and the North Three Rivers in the Haihe River Basin), the Songhua River, the Liaohe River, the Pearl River, and the Taihu Lake Basin shall be formulated by the relevant river basin authorities jointly with the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters for approval. The flood regulation plans for other rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the relevant river basin authorities jointly with the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the river basin flood control command for approval; if no river basin flood control command has been established, they shall be submitted to the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters for approval. The flood regulation plans for other rivers shall be formulated by the water administrative department with jurisdiction jointly with the relevant local people's governments, and shall be submitted to the flood control command with jurisdiction for approval.  
After a flood regulation plan is approved, the relevant local people's governments must implement it. Any modification to a flood regulation plan shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有防汛抗洪任务的企业应当根据所在流域或者地区经批准的防御洪水方案和洪水调度方案，规定本企业的防汛抗洪措施，在征得其所在地县级人民政府水行政主管部门同意后，由有管辖权的防汛指挥机构监督实施。

**Article 13** Enterprises with flood prevention and flood fighting tasks shall, in accordance with the approved flood prevention plans and flood regulation plans of the river basin or region in which they are located, prescribe the flood prevention and flood fighting measures of the enterprise, and after obtaining the consent of the water administrative department of the people's government at the county level of the place where it is located, such measures shall be supervised for implementation by the flood control command with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　水库、水电站、拦河闸坝等工程的管理部门，应当根据工程规划设计、经批准的防御洪水方案和洪水调度方案以及工程实际状况，在兴利服从防洪，保证安全的前提下，制定汛期调度运用计划，经上级主管部门审查批准后，报有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部备案，并接受其监督。  
经国家防汛总指挥部认定的对防汛抗洪关系重大的水电站，其防洪库容的汛期调度运用计划经上级主管部门审查同意后，须经有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部批准。  
汛期调度运用计划经批准后，由水库、水电站、拦河闸坝等工程的管理部门负责执行。  
有防凌任务的江河，其上游水库在凌汛期间的下泄水量，必须征得有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部的同意，并接受其监督。

**Article 14** The administrative departments of projects such as reservoirs, hydropower stations, and barrage gates shall, in accordance with the project planning and design, the approved flood prevention plans and flood regulation plans, and the actual conditions of the project, and under the premise that beneficial uses are subject to flood control and safety is ensured, formulate operation plans for flood season regulation. After review and approval by the competent authority at the next higher level, such plans shall be reported to the flood control headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction for recordation and shall be subject to its supervision.  
For hydropower stations that are deemed by the State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters to be of great importance to flood prevention and flood fighting, the flood season regulation operation plan for their flood control storage capacity, after review and approval by the competent authority at the next higher level, must be approved by the flood control headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction.  
After approval, the flood season regulation operation plan shall be implemented by the administrative departments of projects such as reservoirs, hydropower stations, and barrage gates.  
For rivers with ice prevention tasks, the discharge volume of water from upstream reservoirs during the ice flood period must obtain the consent of the flood control headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction and shall be subject to its supervision.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级防汛指挥部应当在汛前对各类防洪设施组织检查，发现影响防洪安全的问题，责成责任单位在规定的期限内处理，不得贻误防汛抗洪工作。  
各有关部门和单位按照防汛指挥部的统一部署，对所管辖的防洪工程设施进行汛前检查后，必须将影响防洪安全的问题和处理措施报有管辖权的防汛指挥部和上级主管部门，并按照该防汛指挥部的要求予以处理。

**Article 15** Flood control commands at all levels shall, before the flood season, organize inspections of various types of flood control facilities. Where problems affecting flood control safety are discovered, they shall instruct the responsible units to handle them within the prescribed time limit, and shall not delay flood prevention and flood fighting work.  
After conducting pre-flood season inspections of the flood control engineering facilities under their jurisdiction in accordance with the unified deployment of the flood control command, the relevant departments and units must report the problems affecting flood control safety and the measures for handling them to the flood control command with jurisdiction and the competent authority at the next higher level, and shall handle them in accordance with the requirements of that flood control command.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　关于河道清障和对壅水、阻水严重的桥梁、引道、码头和其他跨河工程设施的改建或者拆除，按照《中华人民共和国河道管理条例》的规定执行。

**Article 16** Regarding river channel clearing and the reconstruction or removal of bridges, approach roads, docks, and other river-crossing engineering facilities that cause severe water blockage or flow obstruction, the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on River Channel Administration shall be followed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　蓄滞洪区所在地的省级人民政府应当按照国务院的有关规定，组织有关部门和市、县，制定所管辖的蓄滞洪区的安全与建设规划，并予实施。  
各级地方人民政府必须对所管辖的蓄滞洪区的通信、预报警报、避洪、撤退道路等安全设施，以及紧急撤离和救生的准备工作进行汛前检查，发现影响安全的问题，及时处理。

**Article 17** The provincial people's government of the locality where a flood storage and detention area is located shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, organize the relevant departments, cities, and counties to formulate safety and construction plans for the flood storage and detention areas under its jurisdiction and implement them.  
Local people's governments at all levels must conduct pre-flood season inspections of the safety facilities such as communications, early warning and alarm systems, flood evacuation routes, and the preparatory work for emergency evacuation and rescue in the flood storage and detention areas under their jurisdiction. Where problems affecting safety are discovered, they shall promptly handle them.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　山洪、泥石流易发地区，当地有关部门应当指定预防监测员及时监测。雨季到来之前，当地人民政府防汛指挥部应当组织有关单位进行安全检查，对险情征兆明显的地区，应当及时把群众撤离险区。  
风暴潮易发地区，当地有关部门应当加强对水库、海堤、闸坝、高压电线等设施和房屋的安全检查，发现影响安全的问题，及时处理。

**Article 18** In areas prone to mountain torrents and debris flows, the relevant local departments shall designate prevention monitoring personnel to conduct timely monitoring. Before the rainy season arrives, the local people's government's flood control command shall organize relevant units to carry out safety inspections. For areas with obvious signs of danger, the masses shall be promptly evacuated from the dangerous areas.  
In areas prone to storm surges, the relevant local departments shall strengthen safety inspections of facilities such as reservoirs, sea dikes, gates and dams, high-voltage power lines, and houses. Where problems affecting safety are discovered, they shall promptly handle them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地区之间在防汛抗洪方面发生的水事纠纷，由发生纠纷地区共同的上一级人民政府或其授权的主管部门处理。  
前款所指人民政府或者部门在处理防汛抗洪方面的水事纠纷时，有权采取临时紧急处置措施，有关当事各方必须服从并贯彻执行。

**Article 19** Water disputes between regions arising from flood prevention and flood fighting shall be handled by the common people's government at the next higher level of the regions where the dispute occurs or its authorized competent department.  
When the people's government or department referred to in the preceding paragraph handles water disputes related to flood prevention and flood fighting, it shall have the right to take temporary emergency measures, and the relevant parties must comply with and implement them.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有防汛任务的地方人民政府应当建设和完善江河堤防、水库、蓄滞洪区等防洪设施，以及该地区的防汛通信、预报警报系统。

**Article 20** Local people's governments with flood prevention tasks shall construct and improve flood control facilities such as river levees, reservoirs, and flood storage and detention areas, as well as the flood prevention communication and early warning and alarm systems in the area.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级防汛指挥部应当储备一定数量的防汛抢险物资，由商业、供销、物资部门代储的，可以支付适当的保管费。受洪水威胁的单位和群众应当储备一定的防汛抢险物料。  
防汛抢险所需的主要物资，由计划主管部门在年度计划中予以安排。

**Article 21** Flood control headquarters at all levels shall stockpile a certain amount of flood control and emergency rescue materials. If such materials are stored on their behalf by commercial, supply and marketing, and material departments, appropriate storage fees may be paid. Units and the public threatened by flood shall stockpile a certain amount of flood control and emergency rescue supplies.  
The main materials required for flood control and emergency rescue shall be arranged by the competent planning department in the annual plan.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级人民政府防汛指挥部汛前应当向有关单位和当地驻军介绍防御洪水方案，组织交流防汛抢险经验。有关方面汛期应当及时通报水情。

**Article 22** Before the flood season, flood control headquarters of people's governments at all levels shall introduce the flood prevention plan to relevant units and local armed forces, and organize the exchange of experience in flood control and emergency rescue. Relevant parties shall promptly report hydrological conditions during the flood season.

### 第四章　防汛与抢险

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　省级人民政府防汛指挥部，可以根据当地的洪水规律，规定汛期起止日期。当江河、湖泊、水库的水情接近保证水位或者安全流量时，或者防洪工程设施发生重大险情，情况紧急时，县级以上地方人民政府可以宣布进入紧急防汛期，并报告上级人民政府防汛指挥部。

**Article 23** The flood control headquarters of provincial people's governments may, based on local flood patterns, prescribe the start and end dates of the flood season. When the hydrological conditions of rivers, lakes, or reservoirs approach the guaranteed water level or safe discharge, or when major dangers occur in flood control engineering facilities and the situation is urgent, local people's governments at or above the county level may declare the commencement of an emergency flood season and shall report to the flood control headquarters of the people's government at the higher level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　防汛期内，各级防汛指挥部必须有负责人主持工作。有关责任人员必须坚守岗位，及时掌握汛情，并按照防御洪水方案和汛期调度运用计划进行调度。

**Article 24** During the flood season, flood control headquarters at all levels must have a responsible person in charge. Relevant responsible personnel must remain at their posts, keep abreast of flood conditions in a timely manner, and carry out regulation in accordance with the flood prevention plan and the flood season regulation and operation plan.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在汛期，水利、电力、气象、海洋、农林等部门的水文站、雨量站，必须及时准确地向各级防汛指挥部提供实时水文信息；气象部门必须及时向各级防汛指挥部提供有关天气预报和实时气象信息；水文部门必须及时向各级防汛指挥部提供有关水文预报；海洋部门必须及时向沿海地区防汛指挥部提供风暴潮预报。

**Article 25** During the flood season, hydrological stations and rainfall stations under the departments of water resources, electric power, meteorology, oceanography, agriculture and forestry, etc., must provide real-time hydrological information to flood control headquarters at all levels in a timely and accurate manner; meteorological departments must provide relevant weather forecasts and real-time meteorological information to flood control headquarters at all levels in a timely manner; hydrological departments must provide relevant hydrological forecasts to flood control headquarters at all levels in a timely manner; oceanographic departments must provide storm surge forecasts to flood control headquarters in coastal areas in a timely manner.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在汛期，河道、水库、闸坝、水运设施等水工程管理单位及其主管部门在执行汛期调度运用计划时，必须服从有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部的统一调度指挥或者监督。  
在汛期，以发电为主的水库，其汛限水位以上的防洪库容以及洪水调度运用必须服从有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部的统一调度指挥。

**Article 26** During the flood season, water engineering management units of river courses, reservoirs, sluice gates, dams, water transport facilities, etc., and their competent authorities, when implementing the flood season regulation and operation plan, must comply with the unified regulation, command or supervision of the flood control headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction.  
During the flood season, for reservoirs primarily used for power generation, the flood control storage capacity above the flood limit water level and the flood regulation and operation must comply with the unified regulation and command of the flood control headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在汛期，河道、水库、水电站、闸坝等水工程管理单位必须按照规定对水工程进行巡查，发现险情，必须立即采取抢护措施，并及时向防汛指挥部和上级主管部门报告。其他任何单位和个人发现水工程设施出现险情，应当立即向防汛指挥部和水工程管理单位报告。

**Article 27** During the flood season, water engineering management units of river courses, reservoirs, hydropower stations, sluice gates, dams, etc., must conduct inspections on water engineering in accordance with regulations; upon discovering dangers, they must immediately take emergency rescue measures and promptly report to the flood control headquarters and the competent authority at the higher level. Any other unit or individual that discovers dangers in water engineering facilities shall immediately report to the flood control headquarters and the water engineering management unit.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在汛期，公路、铁路、航运、民航等部门应当及时运送防汛抢险人员和物资；电力部门应当保证防汛用电。

**Article 28** During the flood season, highway, railway, shipping, civil aviation and other departments shall timely transport flood control and emergency rescue personnel and materials; power departments shall ensure the supply of electricity for flood control.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在汛期，电力调度通信设施必须服从防汛工作需要；邮电部门必须保证汛情和防汛指令的及时、准确传递，电视、广播、公路、铁路、航运、民航、公安、林业、石油等部门应当运用本部门的通信工具优先为防汛抗洪服务。  
电视、广播、新闻单位应当根据人民政府防汛指挥部提供的汛情，及时向公众发布防汛信息。

**Article 29** During the flood season, power dispatching and communication facilities must comply with the needs of flood control work; postal and telecommunications departments must ensure timely and accurate transmission of flood conditions and flood control directives; television, radio, highway, railway, shipping, civil aviation, public security, forestry, petroleum and other departments shall use their own communication tools to give priority to serving flood control and flood fighting.  
Television, radio, and news units shall, based on the flood conditions provided by the flood control headquarters of the people's government, promptly release flood control information to the public.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在紧急防汛期，地方人民政府防汛指挥部必须由人民政府负责人主持工作，组织动员本地区各有关单位和个人投入抗洪抢险。所有单位和个人必须听从指挥，承担人民政府防汛指挥部分配的抗洪抢险任务。

**Article 30** During the emergency flood season, the flood control headquarters of the local people's government must be chaired by a responsible person of the people's government, organizing and mobilizing all relevant units and individuals in the region to engage in flood fighting and emergency rescue. All units and individuals must follow the command and undertake the flood fighting and emergency rescue tasks assigned by the flood control headquarters of the people's government.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在紧急防汛期，公安部门应当按照人民政府防汛指挥部的要求，加强治安管理和安全保卫工作。必要时须由有关部门依法实行陆地和水面交通管制。

**Article 31** During the emergency flood prevention period, the public security department shall, in accordance with the requirements of the flood prevention headquarters of the people's government, strengthen public security management and security protection. When necessary, the relevant departments must implement land and water traffic control according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在紧急防汛期，为了防汛抢险需要，防汛指挥部有权在其管辖范围内，调用物资、设备、交通运输工具和人力，事后应当及时归还或者给予适当补偿。因抢险需要取土占地、砍伐林木、清除阻水障碍物的，任何单位和个人不得阻拦。  
前款所指取土占地、砍伐林木的，事后应当依法向有关部门补办手续。

**Article 32** During the emergency flood prevention period, for the needs of flood prevention and emergency rescue, the flood prevention headquarters has the right to allocate materials, equipment, transportation vehicles, and manpower within its jurisdiction, and shall promptly return or provide appropriate compensation afterwards. Where it is necessary to take soil, occupy land, fell trees, or clear obstacles to water flow for emergency rescue, no unit or individual shall obstruct such actions.  
For the taking of soil, occupying land, and felling trees referred to in the preceding paragraph, the relevant formalities shall be completed afterwards with the relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　当河道水位或者流量达到规定的分洪、滞洪标准时，有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥部有权根据经批准的分洪、滞洪方案，采取分洪、滞洪措施。采取上述措施对毗邻地区有危害的，须经有管辖权的上级防汛指挥机构批准，并事先通知有关地区。  
在非常情况下，为保护国家确定的重点地区和大局安全，必须作出局部牺牲时，在报经有管辖权的上级人民政府防汛指挥部批准后，当地人民政府防汛指挥部可以采取非常紧急措施。  
实施上述措施时，任何单位和个人不得阻拦，如遇到阻拦和拖延时，有管辖权的人民政府有权组织强制实施。

**Article 33** When the water level or flow of a river reaches the prescribed standards for flood diversion or detention, the flood prevention headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction has the right to adopt flood diversion or detention measures in accordance with the approved flood diversion or detention plan. If the adoption of the above measures causes harm to adjacent areas, approval must be obtained from the superior flood prevention command organization with jurisdiction, and the relevant areas shall be notified in advance.  
Under extraordinary circumstances, if local sacrifices must be made to protect key areas determined by the state and overall safety, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the superior flood prevention headquarters of the people's government with jurisdiction, the local people's government's flood prevention headquarters may adopt extraordinarily emergency measures.  
When implementing the above measures, no unit or individual shall obstruct them. If obstruction or delay is encountered, the people's government with jurisdiction has the right to organize compulsory implementation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　当洪水威胁群众安全时，当地人民政府应当及时组织群众撤离至安全地带，并做好生活安排。

**Article 34** When flood threatens the safety of the masses, the local people's government shall promptly organize the masses to evacuate to safe areas and make proper living arrangements.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　按照水的天然流势或者防洪、排涝工程的设计标准，或者经批准的运行方案下泄的洪水，下游地区不得设障阻水或者缩小河道的过水能力；上游地区不得擅自增大下泄流量。  
未经有管辖权的人民政府或其授权的部门批准，任何单位和个人不得改变江河河势的自然控制点。

**Article 35** For floodwater discharged in accordance with the natural flow of water or the design standards of flood control and drainage projects, or the approved operation plan, the downstream areas shall not set up obstacles to block the water or reduce the flow capacity of the river channel; the upstream areas shall not unilaterally increase the discharge flow rate.  
Without approval from the people's government with jurisdiction or its authorized departments, no unit or individual shall alter the natural control points of river regime.

### 第五章　善后工作

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在发生洪水灾害的地区，物资、商业、供销、农业、公路、铁路、航运、民航等部门应当做好抢险救灾物资的供应和运输；民政、卫生、教育等部门应当做好灾区群众的生活供给、医疗防疫、学校复课以及恢复生产等救灾工作；水利、电力、邮电、公路等部门应当做好所管辖的水毁工程的修复工作。

**Article 36** In areas hit by flood disasters, the departments of materials, commerce, supply and marketing, agriculture, highway, railway, shipping, and civil aviation shall properly ensure the supply and transportation of emergency rescue and disaster relief materials; the departments of civil affairs, health, and education shall properly carry out disaster relief work such as the supply of daily necessities for the masses in the disaster area, medical treatment and epidemic prevention, resumption of school classes, and restoration of production; the departments of water resources, electric power, posts and telecommunications, and highway shall properly carry out the restoration of water-damaged projects under their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方各级人民政府防汛指挥部，应当按照国家统计部门批准的洪涝灾害统计报表的要求，核实和统计所管辖范围的洪涝灾情，报上级主管部门和同级统计部门，有关单位和个人不得虚报、瞒报、伪造、篡改。

**Article 37** The flood prevention headquarters of local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the requirements of the statistical report forms for flood and waterlogging disasters approved by the national statistical department, verify and compile statistics on the flood and waterlogging situations within their jurisdiction, and report to the superior competent department and the statistical department at the same level. Relevant units and individuals shall not make false reports, conceal reports, fabricate, or tamper with the data.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　洪水灾害发生后，各级人民政府防汛指挥部应当积极组织和帮助灾区群众恢复和发展生产。修复水毁工程所需费用，应当优先列入有关主管部门年度建设计划。

**Article 38** After a flood disaster occurs, the flood prevention headquarters of people's governments at all levels shall actively organize and help the masses in the disaster area to restore and develop production. The expenses needed for restoring water-damaged projects shall be given priority in the annual construction plans of the relevant competent departments.

### 第六章　防汛经费

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　由财政部门安排的防汛经费，按照分级管理的原则，分别列入中央财政和地方财政预算。  
在汛期，有防汛任务的地区的单位和个人应当承担一定的防汛抢险的劳务和费用，具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 39** The flood prevention funds arranged by the financial departments shall be included in the central and local fiscal budgets respectively according to the principle of management at different levels.  
During the flood season, units and individuals in areas with flood prevention tasks shall bear certain labor and expenses for flood prevention and emergency rescue. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　防御特大洪水的经费管理，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 40** The management of funds for defending against extraordinary floods shall be implemented in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对蓄滞洪区，逐步推行洪水保险制度，具体办法另行制定。

**Article 41** For flood detention basins, a flood insurance system shall be gradually implemented, and specific measures shall be formulated separately.

### 第七章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有下列事迹之一的单位和个人，可以由县级以上人民政府给予表彰或者奖励：  
(一)在执行抗洪抢险任务时，组织严密，指挥得当，防守得力，奋力抢险，出色完成任务者；  
(二)坚持巡堤查险，遇到险情及时报告，奋力抗洪抢险，成绩显著者；  
(三)在危险关头，组织群众保护国家和人民财产，抢救群众有功者；  
(四)为防汛调度、抗洪抢险献计献策，效益显著者；  
(五)气象、雨情、水情测报和预报准确及时，情报传递迅速，克服困难，抢测洪水，因而减轻重大洪水灾害者；  
(六)及时供应防汛物料和工具，爱护防汛器材，节约经费开支，完成防汛抢险任务成绩显著者；  
(七)有其他特殊贡献，成绩显著者。

**Article 42** Units and individuals that have performed any of the following deeds may be given commendation or rewards by the people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) Those who, during the execution of flood fighting and emergency rescue tasks, organize tightly, command appropriately, defend effectively, strive to rescue, and accomplish tasks outstandingly;  
(2) Those who persist in inspecting embankments and identifying dangers, report dangerous situations in a timely manner, strive to fight floods and rescue, and have achieved remarkable results;  
(3) Those who, at critical moments, organize the masses to protect state and people's property and have rendered meritorious service in rescuing the masses;  
(4) Those who offer suggestions for flood control scheduling and flood fighting and rescue with significant effects;  
(5) Those who provide accurate and timely meteorological, rainfall, and water regime observation and forecasts, transmit information quickly, overcome difficulties, rush to measure floods, thereby mitigating major flood disasters;  
(6) Those who timely supply flood fighting materials and tools, take good care of flood control equipment, save expenses, and accomplish flood fighting and rescue tasks with outstanding results;  
(7) Those who have other special contributions and have achieved remarkable results.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列行为之一者，视情节和危害后果，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒不执行经批准的防御洪水方案、洪水调度方案，或者拒不执行有管辖权的防汛指挥机构的防汛调度方案或者防汛抢险指令的；  
(二)玩忽职守，或者在防汛抢险的紧要关头临阵逃脱的；  
(三)非法扒口决堤或者开闸的；  
(四)挪用、盗窃、贪污防汛或者救灾的钱款或者物资的；  
(五)阻碍防汛指挥机构工作人员依法执行职务的；  
(六)盗窃、毁损或者破坏堤防、护岸、闸坝等水工程建筑物和防汛工程设施以及水文监测、测量设施、气象测报设施、河岸地质监测设施、通信照明设施的；  
(七)其他危害防汛抢险工作的。

**Article 43** Any person who commits any of the following acts shall, depending on the circumstances and harmful consequences, be given an administrative sanction by the unit to which he belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level; if a public security administration punishment should be given, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing to execute the approved flood prevention plan or flood control operation plan, or refusing to execute the flood control operation plan or flood fighting directive of the flood control headquarters with jurisdiction;  
(2) Neglecting duty, or deserting at the critical moment of flood fighting and rescue;  
(3) Illegally breaching dikes or opening sluice gates;  
(4) Misappropriating, stealing, or embezzling funds or materials for flood control or disaster relief;  
(5) Obstructing the staff of the flood control headquarters from performing their duties according to law;  
(6) Stealing, damaging, or destroying water engineering structures such as dikes, revetments, sluice dams, and flood control engineering facilities, as well as hydrological monitoring and surveying facilities, meteorological observation facilities, riverbank geological monitoring facilities, and communication and lighting facilities;  
(7) Other acts harmful to flood fighting and rescue work.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反河道和水库大坝的安全管理，依照《中华人民共和国河道管理条例》和《水库大坝安全管理条例》的有关规定处理。

**Article 44** Any violation of the safety management of river courses and reservoir dams shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on River Administration and the Regulations on the Safety Management of Reservoir Dams.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　虚报、瞒报洪涝灾情，或者伪造、篡改洪涝灾害统计资料的，依照《中华人民共和国统计法》及其实施细则的有关规定处理。

**Article 45** Any false reporting or concealment of flood disaster conditions, or forgery or alteration of flood disaster statistical data, shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and its implementing rules.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　当事人对行政处罚不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起15日内，向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，直接向人民法院起诉。  
当事人逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉，又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行；在汛期，也可以由作出处罚决定的机关强制执行；对治安管理处罚不服的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定办理。  
当事人在申请复议或者诉讼期间，不停止行政处罚决定的执行。

**Article 46** If a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level over the organ that made the penalty decision; if dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, institute prosecution in the People's Court. The party may also directly institute prosecution in the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice.  
If the party fails to apply for reconsideration or to institute prosecution within the time limit and does not perform the penalty decision, the organ that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution; during flood season, the organ that made the penalty decision may also enforce it; if dissatisfied with a public security administration punishment, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.  
During the period of applying for reconsideration or instituting prosecution, the party shall not suspend the execution of the administrative penalty decision.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，可以根据本条例的规定，结合本地区的实际情况，制定实施细则。

**Article 47** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and in light of the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate implementation rules.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例由国务院水行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
