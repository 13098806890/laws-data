# 中华人民共和国中外合作办学条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2003-09-01

> (2003年3月1日中华人民共和国国务院令第372号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范中外合作办学活动，加强教育对外交流与合作，促进教育事业的发展，根据《中华人民共和国教育法》、《中华人民共和国职业教育法》和《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法》，制定本条例。 第二条　外国教育机构同中国教育机构(以下简称中外合作办学者)在中国境内合作举办以中国公民为主要招生对象的教育机构(以下简称中外合作办学机构)的活动，适用本条例。 第三条　中外合作办学属于公益性事业，是中国教育事业的组成部分。 国家对中外合作办学实行扩大开放、规范办学、依法管理、促进发展的方针。 国家鼓励引进外国优质教育资源的中外合作办学。 国家鼓励在高等教育、职业教育领域开展中外合作办学，鼓励中国高等教育机构与外国知名的高等教育机构合作办学。 第四条　中外合作办学者、中外合作办学机构的合法权益，受中国法律保护。 中外合作办学机构依法享受国家规定的优惠政策，依法自主开展教育教学活动。 第五条　中外合作办学必须遵守中国法律，贯彻中国的教育方针，符合中国的公共道德，不得损害中国的国家主权、安全和社会公共利益。 中外合作办学应当符合中国教育事业发展的需要，保证教育教学质量，致力于培养中国社会主义建设事业的各类人才。 第六条　中外合作办学者可以合作举办各级各类教育机构。但是，不得举办实施义务教育和实施军事、警察、政治等特殊性质教育的机构。 第七条　外国宗教组织、宗教机构、宗教院校和宗教教职人员不得在中国境内从事合作办学活动。 中外合作办学机构不得进行宗教教育和开展宗教活动。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范中外合作办学活动，加强教育对外交流与合作，促进教育事业的发展，根据《中华人民共和国教育法》、《中华人民共和国职业教育法》和《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Education Law of the People's Republic of China, the Vocational Education Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Promotion of Privately-run Schools, for the purposes of regulating Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools, strengthening international exchanges and cooperation in education, and promoting the development of educational undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外国教育机构同中国教育机构(以下简称中外合作办学者)在中国境内合作举办以中国公民为主要招生对象的教育机构(以下简称中外合作办学机构)的活动，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to the activities of foreign educational institutions cooperating with Chinese educational institutions (hereinafter referred to as Chinese and foreign cooperators in running schools) in running educational institutions within the territory of China that mainly recruit Chinese citizens as students (hereinafter referred to as Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中外合作办学属于公益性事业，是中国教育事业的组成部分。  
国家对中外合作办学实行扩大开放、规范办学、依法管理、促进发展的方针。  
国家鼓励引进外国优质教育资源的中外合作办学。  
国家鼓励在高等教育、职业教育领域开展中外合作办学，鼓励中国高等教育机构与外国知名的高等教育机构合作办学。

**Article 3** Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools is a public welfare undertaking and a component of China's educational undertakings.  
The State shall pursue the policy of expanding opening-up, regulating school running, administering according to law, and promoting development in respect of Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools.  
The State encourages Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools that introduces high-quality foreign educational resources.  
The State encourages Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools in the fields of higher education and vocational education, and encourages Chinese higher education institutions to cooperate with renowned foreign higher education institutions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中外合作办学者、中外合作办学机构的合法权益，受中国法律保护。  
中外合作办学机构依法享受国家规定的优惠政策，依法自主开展教育教学活动。

**Article 4** The legitimate rights and interests of Chinese and foreign cooperators in running schools and Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions shall be protected by Chinese laws.  
Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions shall, according to law, enjoy the preferential policies prescribed by the State and independently conduct educational and teaching activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中外合作办学必须遵守中国法律，贯彻中国的教育方针，符合中国的公共道德，不得损害中国的国家主权、安全和社会公共利益。  
中外合作办学应当符合中国教育事业发展的需要，保证教育教学质量，致力于培养中国社会主义建设事业的各类人才。

**Article 5** Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools must comply with Chinese laws, implement China's educational policies, conform to Chinese public morals, and shall not harm China's national sovereignty, security, and public interests.  
Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools shall meet the needs of the development of China's educational undertakings, ensure the quality of education and teaching, and be committed to cultivating various types of talents for China's socialist construction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中外合作办学者可以合作举办各级各类教育机构。但是，不得举办实施义务教育和实施军事、警察、政治等特殊性质教育的机构。

**Article 6** Chinese and foreign cooperators in running schools may cooperate in running educational institutions of various levels and types. However, they may not run institutions that provide compulsory education or education of a special nature such as military, police, or political education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国宗教组织、宗教机构、宗教院校和宗教教职人员不得在中国境内从事合作办学活动。  
中外合作办学机构不得进行宗教教育和开展宗教活动。

**Article 7** Foreign religious organizations, religious institutions, religious schools, and religious personnel shall not engage in cooperative education activities within the territory of China.  
Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions shall not conduct religious education or carry out religious activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院教育行政部门负责全国中外合作办学工作的统筹规划、综合协调和宏观管理。国务院教育行政部门、劳动行政部门和其他有关行政部门在国务院规定的职责范围内负责有关的中外合作办学工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门负责本行政区域内中外合作办学工作的统筹规划、综合协调和宏观管理。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门、劳动行政部门和其他有关行政部门在其职责范围内负责本行政区域内有关的中外合作办学工作。

**Article 8** The education administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the overall planning, comprehensive coordination, and macro-administration of Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools nationwide. The education administration department, the labor administration department, and other relevant administrative departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools within the scope of their duties prescribed by the State Council.  
The education administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the overall planning, comprehensive coordination, and macro-administration of Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools within their respective administrative regions. The education administration departments, the labor administration departments, and other relevant administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the relevant Sino-foreign cooperation in running schools within their respective administrative regions within the scope of their duties.

### 第二章　设立

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立中外合作办学机构的教育机构应当具有法人资格。

**Article 9** An educational institution applying to establish a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall have the status of a legal person.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中外合作办学者可以用资金、实物、土地使用权、知识产权以及其他财产作为办学投入。  
中外合作办学者的知识产权投入不得超过各自投入的1/3。但是，接受国务院教育行政部门、劳动行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府邀请前来中国合作办学的外国教育机构的知识产权投入可以超过其投入的1/3。

**Article 10** Chinese and foreign cooperators in running schools may contribute funds, in-kind contributions, land-use rights, intellectual property, and other property as investment in running schools.  
The intellectual property investment of Chinese and foreign cooperators in running schools shall not exceed one-third of their respective investments. However, the intellectual property investment of a foreign educational institution that is invited by the education administration department, the labor administration department of the State Council, or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to cooperate in running schools in China may exceed one-third of its investment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中外合作办学机构应当具备《中华人民共和国教育法》、《中华人民共和国职业教育法》、《中华人民共和国高等教育法》等法律和有关行政法规规定的基本条件，并具有法人资格。但是，外国教育机构同中国实施学历教育的高等学校设立的实施高等教育的中外合作办学机构，可以不具有法人资格。  
设立中外合作办学机构，参照国家举办的同级同类教育机构的设置标准执行。

**Article 11** A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall satisfy the basic conditions prescribed by the Education Law of the People's Republic of China, the Vocational Education Law of the People's Republic of China, the Higher Education Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall have the status of a legal person. However, a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution that provides higher education and is established by a foreign education institution together with a Chinese institution of higher education that provides academic degree education may not have the status of a legal person.  
The establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be carried out with reference to the standards for the establishment of similar educational institutions run by the State at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请设立实施本科以上高等学历教育的中外合作办学机构，由国务院教育行政部门审批；申请设立实施高等专科教育和非学历高等教育的中外合作办学机构，由拟设立机构所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批。  
申请设立实施中等学历教育和自学考试助学、文化补习、学前教育等的中外合作办学机构，由拟设立机构所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门审批。  
申请设立实施职业技能培训的中外合作办学机构，由拟设立机构所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府劳动行政部门审批。

**Article 12** An application for the establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing undergraduate or higher academic degree education shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of education under the State Council; an application for the establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing higher vocational education or non-degree higher education shall be examined and approved by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed institution is to be located.  
An application for the establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing secondary academic degree education, self-study examination assistance, cultural tutoring, preschool education, etc. shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of education of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed institution is to be located.  
An application for the establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing vocational skill training shall be examined and approved by the administrative department of labor of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed institution is to be located.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立中外合作办学机构，分为筹备设立和正式设立两个步骤。但是，具备办学条件，达到设置标准的，可以直接申请正式设立。

**Article 13** The establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be divided into two steps: preparatory establishment and formal establishment. However, if the conditions for running the institution are met and the standards for establishment are reached, an application for formal establishment may be made directly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请筹备设立中外合作办学机构，应当提交下列文件：  
(一)申办报告，内容应当主要包括：中外合作办学者、拟设立中外合作办学机构的名称、培养目标、办学规模、办学层次、办学形式、办学条件、内部管理体制、经费筹措与管理使用等；  
(二)合作协议，内容应当包括：合作期限、争议解决办法等；  
(三)资产来源、资金数额及有效证明文件，并载明产权；  
(四)属捐赠性质的校产须提交捐赠协议，载明捐赠人的姓名、所捐资产的数额、用途和管理办法及相关有效证明文件；  
(五)不低于中外合作办学者资金投入15%的启动资金到位证明。

**Article 14** An application for preparatory establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall submit the following documents:  
(1) An application report, the content of which shall mainly include: the Chinese and foreign cooperators, the name of the proposed Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, training objectives, scale of the institution, level of education, form of education, conditions for running the institution, internal management system, fund raising and management and use, etc.;  
(2) A cooperation agreement, the content of which shall include: the term of cooperation, dispute resolution methods, etc.;  
(3) Documents certifying the source of assets, amount of funds, and validity, and specifying the property rights;  
(4) School property of a donative nature must be accompanied by a donation agreement specifying the donor's name, the amount of assets donated, the purpose and management measures, and relevant valid supporting documents;  
(5) A certificate showing that start-up funds of no less than 15% of the capital contribution of the Chinese and foreign cooperators have been received.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请筹备设立中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内作出是否批准的决定。批准的，发给筹备设立批准书；不批准的，应当书面说明理由。

**Article 15** Where an application is made for preparatory establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, the examination and approval authority shall, within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to grant approval. If approval is granted, a preparatory establishment approval certificate shall be issued; if approval is not granted, the reasons shall be stated in writing.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经批准筹备设立中外合作办学机构的，应当自批准之日起3年内提出正式设立申请；超过3年的，中外合作办学者应当重新申报。  
筹备设立期内，不得招生。

**Article 16** Where an institution has been approved for preparatory establishment, an application for formal establishment shall be submitted within three years from the date of approval; if more than three years have elapsed, the Chinese and foreign cooperators shall re-apply.  
No enrollment shall be conducted during the preparatory establishment period.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　完成筹备设立申请正式设立的，应当提交下列文件：  
(一)正式设立申请书；  
(二)筹备设立批准书；  
(三)筹备设立情况报告；  
(四)中外合作办学机构的章程，首届理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会组成人员名单；  
(五)中外合作办学机构资产的有效证明文件；  
(六)校长或者主要行政负责人、教师、财会人员的资格证明文件。  
直接申请正式设立中外合作办学机构的，应当提交前款第(一)项、第(四)项、第(五)项、第(六)项和第十四条第(二)项、第(三)项、第(四)项所列文件。

**Article 17** Where the preparatory establishment has been completed and an application for formal establishment is made, the following documents shall be submitted:  
(1) A formal establishment application letter;  
(2) The preparatory establishment approval certificate;  
(3) A report on the status of preparatory establishment;  
(4) The articles of association of the Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, and the list of members of the first council, board of directors, or joint management committee;  
(5) Valid documents certifying the assets of the Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution;  
(6) Qualification certificates of the principal or chief administrative officer, teachers, and financial and accounting personnel.  
Where an application is made directly for formal establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, the documents listed in subparagraphs (1), (4), (5), and (6) of the preceding paragraph and subparagraphs (2), (3), and (4) of Article 14 shall be submitted.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请正式设立实施非学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起3个月内作出是否批准的决定；申请正式设立实施学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起6个月内作出是否批准的决定。批准的，颁发统一格式、统一编号的中外合作办学许可证；不批准的，应当书面说明理由。  
中外合作办学许可证由国务院教育行政部门制定式样，由国务院教育行政部门和劳动行政部门按照职责分工分别组织印制；中外合作办学许可证由国务院教育行政部门统一编号，具体办法由国务院教育行政部门会同劳动行政部门确定。

**Article 18** Where an application is made for formal establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing non-degree education, the examination and approval authority shall, within three months from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to grant approval; where an application is made for formal establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing academic degree education, the examination and approval authority shall, within six months from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to grant approval. If approval is granted, a Chinese-foreign cooperative education license with uniform format and uniform serial number shall be issued; if approval is not granted, the reasons shall be stated in writing.  
The form of the Chinese-foreign cooperative education license shall be formulated by the administrative department of education under the State Council, and the license shall be printed separately by the administrative department of education and the administrative department of labor under the State Council according to their respective functions and duties; the serial number of the Chinese-foreign cooperative education license shall be uniformly assigned by the administrative department of education under the State Council, and the specific measures shall be determined by the administrative department of education under the State Council in conjunction with the administrative department of labor.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请正式设立实施学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关受理申请后，应当组织专家委员会评议，由专家委员会提出咨询意见。

**Article 19** Where an application is made for formal establishment of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution providing academic degree education, after accepting the application, the examination and approval authority shall organize an expert committee to conduct an evaluation, and the expert committee shall provide advisory opinions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中外合作办学机构取得中外合作办学许可证后，应当依照有关的法律、行政法规进行登记，登记机关应当依照有关规定即时予以办理。

**Article 20** After obtaining the Chinese-foreign cooperative education license, the Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall register in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the registration authority shall handle the matter promptly in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第三章　组织与管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　具有法人资格的中外合作办学机构应当设立理事会或者董事会，不具有法人资格的中外合作办学机构应当设立联合管理委员会。理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会的中方组成人员不得少于1/2。  
理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会由5人以上组成，设理事长、副理事长，董事长、副董事长或者主任、副主任各1人。中外合作办学者一方担任理事长、董事长或者主任的，由另一方担任副理事长、副董事长或者副主任。  
具有法人资格的中外合作办学机构的法定代表人，由中外合作办学者协商，在理事长、董事长或者校长中确定。

**Article 21** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution with legal person status shall establish a council or a board of directors. A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution without legal person status shall establish a joint management committee. The Chinese members of the council, board of directors or joint management committee shall not be less than one-half.  
A council, board of directors or joint management committee shall be composed of not less than five members, with one chairperson and one vice-chairperson, or one chairperson of the board of directors and one vice-chairperson, or one director and one deputy director, each respectively. Where one party of the Sino-foreign cooperative education sponsors serves as the chairperson, chairperson of the board of directors or director, the other party shall serve as the vice-chairperson, vice-chairperson of the board of directors or deputy director.  
The legal representative of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution with legal person status shall be determined by consultation between the Chinese and foreign cooperative education sponsors from among the chairperson, the chairperson of the board of directors or the president.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中外合作办学机构的理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会由中外合作办学者的代表、校长或者主要行政负责人、教职工代表等组成，其中1/3以上组成人员应当具有5年以上教育、教学经验。  
中外合作办学机构的理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会组成人员名单应当报审批机关备案。

**Article 22** The council, board of directors or joint management committee of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be composed of representatives of the Sino-foreign cooperative education sponsors, the president or chief administrative officer, representatives of the faculty and staff, among others, and more than one-third of the members shall have at least five years of experience in education or teaching.  
The list of members of the council, board of directors or joint management committee of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be filed with the examination and approval authority for record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　中外合作办学机构的理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会行使下列职权：  
(一)改选或者补选理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会组成人员；  
(二)聘任、解聘校长或者主要行政负责人；  
(三)修改章程，制定规章制度；  
(四)制定发展规划，批准年度工作计划；  
(五)筹集办学经费，审核预算、决算；  
(六)决定教职工的编制定额和工资标准；  
(七)决定中外合作办学机构的分立、合并、终止；  
(八)章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 23** The council, board of directors or joint management committee of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall exercise the following powers:  
(1) to re-elect or elect additional members of the council, board of directors or joint management committee;  
(2) to appoint or dismiss the president or chief administrative officer;  
(3) to amend the charter and formulate rules and regulations;  
(4) to formulate development plans and approve annual work plans;  
(5) to raise funds for school operation, and review budgets and final accounts;  
(6) to determine the staffing quota and salary standards for faculty and staff;  
(7) to decide on the division, merger or termination of the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution;  
(8) other powers as prescribed by the charter.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　中外合作办学机构的理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会每年至少召开一次会议。经1/3以上组成人员提议，可以召开理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会临时会议。  
中外合作办学机构的理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会讨论下列重大事项，应当经2/3以上组成人员同意方可通过：  
(一)聘任、解聘校长或者主要行政负责人；  
(二)修改章程；  
(三)制定发展规划；  
(四)决定中外合作办学机构的分立、合并、终止；  
(五)章程规定的其他重大事项。

**Article 24** The council, board of directors or joint management committee of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall hold a meeting at least once a year. An interim meeting of the council, board of directors or joint management committee may be convened upon a proposal by more than one-third of its members.  
When the council, board of directors or joint management committee of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution discusses the following major matters, the resolution shall be adopted only with the consent of more than two-thirds of its members:  
(1) appointing or dismissing the president or chief administrative officer;  
(2) amending the charter;  
(3) formulating development plans;  
(4) deciding on the division, merger or termination of the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution;  
(5) other major matters as prescribed by the charter.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　中外合作办学机构的校长或者主要行政负责人，应当具有中华人民共和国国籍，在中国境内定居，热爱祖国，品行良好，具有教育、教学经验，并具备相应的专业水平。

**Article 25** The president or chief administrative officer of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be a person with Chinese nationality, domiciled within the territory of China, who loves the motherland, has good conduct, possesses experience in education and teaching, and has the corresponding professional level.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中外合作办学机构的校长或者主要行政负责人行使下列职权：  
(一)执行理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会的决定；  
(二)实施发展规划，拟订年度工作计划、财务预算和规章制度；  
(三)聘任和解聘工作人员，实施奖惩；  
(四)组织教育教学、科学研究活动，保证教育教学质量；  
(五)负责日常管理工作；  
(六)章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 26** The president or chief administrative officer of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall exercise the following powers:  
(1) to execute the decisions of the council, board of directors or joint management committee;  
(2) to implement development plans, and draft annual work plans, financial budgets, and rules and regulations;  
(3) to appoint and dismiss staff members, and implement rewards and punishments;  
(4) to organize educational, teaching and scientific research activities, and ensure the quality of education and teaching;  
(5) to be responsible for daily management;  
(6) other powers as prescribed by the charter.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中外合作办学机构依法对教师、学生进行管理。  
中外合作办学机构聘任的外籍教师和外籍管理人员，应当具备学士以上学位和相应的职业证书，并具有2年以上教育、教学经验。  
外方合作办学者应当从本教育机构中选派一定数量的教师到中外合作办学机构任教。

**Article 27** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall manage its teachers and students according to law.  
Foreign teachers and foreign administrative personnel employed by a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall have at least a bachelor's degree and relevant professional certificates, and have at least two years of experience in education or teaching.  
The foreign cooperative education sponsor shall select and send a certain number of teachers from its own educational institution to teach at the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中外合作办学机构应当依法维护教师、学生的合法权益，保障教职工的工资、福利待遇，并为教职工缴纳社会保险费。  
中外合作办学机构的教职工依法建立工会等组织，并通过教职工代表大会等形式，参与中外合作办学机构的民主管理。

**Article 28** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall, according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of teachers and students, guarantee the salaries and welfare benefits of the faculty and staff, and pay social insurance premiums for the faculty and staff.  
The faculty and staff of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall, according to law, establish trade unions and other organizations, and participate in the democratic management of the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution through the faculty and staff congress and other forms.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　中外合作办学机构的外籍人员应当遵守外国人在中国就业的有关规定。

**Article 29** Foreign nationals in a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall comply with the relevant provisions on the employment of foreigners in China.

### 第四章　教育教学

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　中外合作办学机构应当按照中国对同级同类教育机构的要求开设关于宪法、法律、公民道德、国情等内容的课程。  
国家鼓励中外合作办学机构引进国内急需、在国际上具有先进性的课程和教材。  
中外合作办学机构应当将所开设的课程和引进的教材报审批机关备案。

**Article 30** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall, in accordance with China's requirements for educational institutions of the same level and type, offer courses on the Constitution, laws, civic morality, national conditions, and other topics.  
The State encourages Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions to introduce curricula and teaching materials that are urgently needed domestically and are internationally advanced.  
A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall report the courses offered and the teaching materials introduced to the examination and approval authority for record.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　中外合作办学机构根据需要，可以使用外国语言文字教学，但应当以普通话和规范汉字为基本教学语言文字。

**Article 31** A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution may, as needed, use foreign languages in teaching, but shall use Putonghua and standardized Chinese characters as the basic teaching language.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　实施高等学历教育的中外合作办学机构招收学生，纳入国家高等学校招生计划。实施其他学历教育的中外合作办学机构招收学生，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门的规定执行。  
中外合作办学机构招收境外学生，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 32** A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution that provides higher education for academic qualifications shall enroll students as part of the national enrollment plan for institutions of higher education. A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution that provides other education for academic qualifications shall enroll students in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of education of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution that enrolls overseas students shall do so in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中外合作办学机构的招生简章和广告应当报审批机关备案。  
中外合作办学机构应当将办学类型和层次、专业设置、课程内容和招生规模等有关情况，定期向社会公布。

**Article 33** The enrollment brochures and advertisements of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be filed with the approving authority.  
A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall regularly make public such information as the type and level of education, specialty setup, curriculum content, and enrollment scale.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　中外合作办学机构实施学历教育的，按照国家有关规定颁发学历证书或者其他学业证书；实施非学历教育的，按照国家有关规定颁发培训证书或者结业证书。对于接受职业技能培训的学生，经政府批准的职业技能鉴定机构鉴定合格的，可以按照国家有关规定颁发相应的国家职业资格证书。  
中外合作办学机构实施高等学历教育的，可以按照国家有关规定颁发中国相应的学位证书。  
中外合作办学机构颁发的外国教育机构的学历、学位证书，应当与该教育机构在其所属国颁发的学历、学位证书相同，并在该国获得承认。  
中国对中外合作办学机构颁发的外国教育机构的学历、学位证书的承认，依照中华人民共和国缔结或者加入的国际条约办理，或者按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 34** If a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution provides education for academic qualifications, it shall issue diplomas or other academic certificates in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if it provides non-academic education, it shall issue training certificates or completion certificates in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. For students receiving vocational skill training, if they pass the assessment conducted by a government-approved vocational skill assessment institution, the corresponding national vocational qualification certificates may be issued in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
If a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution provides higher education for academic qualifications, it may issue the corresponding Chinese degree certificates in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The diplomas and degree certificates of a foreign educational institution issued by a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be the same as those issued by such educational institution in its home country and shall be recognized in that country.  
The recognition by China of the diplomas and degree certificates of a foreign educational institution issued by a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be handled in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院教育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门及劳动行政部门等其他有关行政部门应当加强对中外合作办学机构的日常监督，组织或者委托社会中介组织对中外合作办学机构的办学水平和教育质量进行评估，并将评估结果向社会公布。

**Article 35** The administrative department of education of the State Council, or the administrative department of education of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, as well as the administrative department of labor and other relevant administrative departments, shall strengthen daily supervision over Chinese-foreign cooperative education institutions, organize or entrust social intermediary organizations to conduct evaluations of the educational level and quality of Chinese-foreign cooperative education institutions, and make the evaluation results public.

### 第五章　资产与财务

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　中外合作办学机构应当依法建立健全财务、会计制度和资产管理制度，并按照国家有关规定设置会计账簿。

**Article 36** A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall, according to law, establish and improve its financial, accounting, and asset management systems, and shall set up accounting books in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　中外合作办学机构存续期间，所有资产由中外合作办学机构依法享有法人财产权，任何组织和个人不得侵占。

**Article 37** During the existence of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, the institution shall enjoy legal person property rights over all its assets according to law, and no organization or individual may seize them.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　中外合作办学机构的收费项目和标准，依照国家有关政府定价的规定确定并公布；未经批准，不得增加项目或者提高标准。  
中外合作办学机构应当以人民币计收学费和其他费用，不得以外汇计收学费和其他费用。

**Article 38** The fee items and standards of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be determined and made public in accordance with the State's provisions on government-set prices; without approval, no fee item may be added or standard raised.  
A Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall collect tuition and other fees in RMB, and may not collect tuition and other fees in foreign exchange.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中外合作办学机构收取的费用应当主要用于教育教学活动和改善办学条件。

**Article 39** The fees collected by a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution shall be mainly used for educational and teaching activities and for improving the conditions of running the institution.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　中外合作办学机构的外汇收支活动以及开设和使用外汇账户，应当遵守国家外汇管理规定。

**Article 40** The foreign exchange receipts and payments activities of a Chinese-foreign cooperative education institution, as well as the opening and use of foreign exchange accounts, shall comply with the State's provisions on foreign exchange administration.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　中外合作办学机构应当在每个会计年度结束时制作财务会计报告，委托社会审计机构依法进行审计，向社会公布审计结果，并报审批机关备案。

**Article 41** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall, at the end of each fiscal year, prepare a financial accounting report, entrust a social audit institution to conduct an audit according to law, publish the audit results to the public, and submit them to the examination and approval authority for record.

### 第六章　变更与终止

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　中外合作办学机构的分立、合并，在进行财务清算后，由该机构理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会报审批机关批准。  
申请分立、合并实施非学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起3个月内以书面形式答复；申请分立、合并实施学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起6个月内以书面形式答复。

**Article 42** The division or merger of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall, after financial liquidation, be submitted by the council, board of directors, or joint management committee of the institution to the examination and approval authority for approval.  
Where an application is made for the division or merger of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution providing non-academic education, the examination and approval authority shall reply in writing within three months from the date of accepting the application; where an application is made for the division or merger of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution providing academic education, the examination and approval authority shall reply in writing within six months from the date of accepting the application.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　中外合作办学机构合作办学者的变更，应当由合作办学者提出，在进行财务清算后，经该机构理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会同意，报审批机关核准，并办理相应的变更手续。  
中外合作办学机构住所、法定代表人的变更，应当经审批机关核准，并办理相应的变更手续。中外合作办学机构校长或者主要行政负责人的变更，应当及时办理变更手续。

**Article 43** A change of the cooperative education partner of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be proposed by the cooperative education partner, and after financial liquidation, be subject to the consent of the council, board of directors, or joint management committee of the institution, submitted to the examination and approval authority for verification, and the corresponding modification procedures shall be handled.  
A change of the domicile or legal representative of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be subject to the verification of the examination and approval authority, and the corresponding modification procedures shall be handled. A change of the principal or chief administrative officer of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be handled by going through the modification procedures in a timely manner.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　中外合作办学机构名称、层次、类别的变更，由该机构理事会、董事会或者联合管理委员会报审批机关批准。  
申请变更为实施非学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起3个月内以书面形式答复；申请变更为实施学历教育的中外合作办学机构的，审批机关应当自受理申请之日起6个月内以书面形式答复。

**Article 44** A change of the name, level, or category of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be submitted by the council, board of directors, or joint management committee of the institution to the examination and approval authority for approval.  
Where an application is made to change a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution to provide non-academic education, the examination and approval authority shall reply in writing within three months from the date of accepting the application; where an application is made to change a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution to provide academic education, the examination and approval authority shall reply in writing within six months from the date of accepting the application.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中外合作办学机构有下列情形之一的，应当终止：  
(一)根据章程规定要求终止，并经审批机关批准的；  
(二)被吊销中外合作办学许可证的；  
(三)因资不抵债无法继续办学，并经审批机关批准的。  
中外合作办学机构终止，应当妥善安置在校学生；中外合作办学机构提出终止申请时，应当同时提交妥善安置在校学生的方案。

**Article 45** A Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It is required to terminate in accordance with the provisions of its charter and has obtained the approval of the examination and approval authority;  
(2) Its Sino-foreign cooperative education license is revoked;  
(3) It is unable to continue operation due to insolvency and has obtained the approval of the examination and approval authority.  
Upon termination of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution, the enrolled students shall be properly arranged; when the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution submits an application for termination, it shall simultaneously submit a plan for the proper arrangement of the enrolled students.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　中外合作办学机构终止时，应当依法进行财务清算。  
中外合作办学机构自己要求终止的，由中外合作办学机构组织清算；被审批机关依法撤销的，由审批机关组织清算；因资不抵债无法继续办学而被终止的，依法请求人民法院组织清算。

**Article 46** Upon termination, a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution shall conduct financial liquidation according to law.  
If the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution requests termination on its own, it shall organize the liquidation; if it is revoked by the examination and approval authority according to law, the examination and approval authority shall organize the liquidation; if it is terminated due to inability to continue operation because of insolvency, a request shall be made to the People's Court according to law to organize the liquidation.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　中外合作办学机构清算时，应当按照下列顺序清偿：  
(一)应当退还学生的学费和其他费用；  
(二)应当支付给教职工的工资和应当缴纳的社会保险费用；  
(三)应当偿还的其他债务。  
中外合作办学机构清偿上述债务后的剩余财产，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 47** When a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution is liquidated, it shall discharge its debts in the following order:  
(1) Tuition fees and other fees that shall be refunded to students;  
(2) Wages that shall be paid to faculty and staff, and social insurance premiums that shall be paid;  
(3) Other debts that shall be repaid.  
The remaining property of the Sino-foreign cooperative education institution after discharging the debts mentioned above shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　中外合作办学机构经批准终止或者被吊销中外合作办学许可证的，应当将中外合作办学许可证和印章交回审批机关，依法办理注销登记。

**Article 48** If a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution is terminated upon approval or has its Sino-foreign cooperative education license revoked, it shall return the Sino-foreign cooperative education license and seal to the examination and approval authority, and go through the cancellation of registration according to law.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　中外合作办学审批机关及其工作人员，利用职务上的便利收取他人财物或者获取其他利益，滥用职权、玩忽职守，对不符合本条例规定条件者颁发中外合作办学许可证，或者发现违法行为不予以查处，造成严重后果，触犯刑律的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 49** Where the examination and approval authority of Sino-foreign cooperative education or any of its staff members, taking advantage of the position to accept property from others or obtain other benefits, abuses power, neglects duties, issues a Sino-foreign cooperative education license to a person who does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, or fails to investigate and deal with illegal activities upon discovery, causing serious consequences, if such act violates criminal law, the person in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of abusing power, the crime of neglecting duties, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例的规定，超越职权审批中外合作办学机构的，其批准文件无效，由上级机关责令改正；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person approves a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution exceeding the authority, the approval document shall be null and void, and the superior authority shall order rectification; the person in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if such act causes significant losses to public property, the State, or the interests of the people, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abusing power or other crimes.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例的规定，未经批准擅自设立中外合作办学机构，或者以不正当手段骗取中外合作办学许可证的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工予以取缔或者会同公安机关予以取缔，责令退还向学生收取的费用，并处以10万元以下的罚款；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于诈骗罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, establishes a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution without approval, or obtains a Sino-foreign cooperative education license by improper means, shall be banned by the education administrative department or the labor administrative department according to their respective functions and duties, or shall be banned jointly with the public security organ, and shall be ordered to refund the fees collected from students and shall be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of fraud or any other crime.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例的规定，在中外合作办学机构筹备设立期间招收学生的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工责令停止招生，责令退还向学生收取的费用，并处以10万元以下的罚款；情节严重，拒不停止招生的，由审批机关撤销筹备设立批准书。

**Article 52** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, enrolls students during the preparatory establishment period of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution, shall be ordered by the education administrative department or the labor administrative department according to their respective functions and duties to stop enrollment, shall be ordered to refund the fees collected from students, and shall be imposed a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious and the violator refuses to stop enrollment, the examination and approval authority shall revoke the preparatory establishment approval document.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　中外合作办学者虚假出资或者在中外合作办学机构成立后抽逃出资的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工处以虚假出资金额或者抽逃出资金额2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 53** If a Chinese or foreign partner of a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution makes a false capital contribution or withdraws capital contribution after the establishment of the institution, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order rectification within a specified time limit; if the rectification is not made within the time limit, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, impose a fine of not more than two times the amount of the false capital contribution or the withdrawn capital contribution.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　伪造、变造和买卖中外合作办学许可证的，依照刑法关于伪造、变造、买卖国家机关证件罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Whoever forges, alters, or trades a Sino-foreign cooperative education license shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of forging, altering, or trading certificates of state organs or any other crime.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　中外合作办学机构未经批准增加收费项目或者提高收费标准的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工责令退还多收的费用，并由市场监督管理部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 55** If a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution, without approval, adds fee items or raises fee standards, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order the refund of the excess fees collected, and the market supervision and administration department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　中外合作办学机构管理混乱、教育教学质量低下，造成恶劣影响的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工责令限期整顿并予以公告；情节严重、逾期不整顿或者经整顿仍达不到要求的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工责令停止招生、吊销中外合作办学许可证。

**Article 56** If a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution is in disorderly management, has low education and teaching quality, and causes adverse effects, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order rectification within a specified time limit and make a public announcement; if the circumstances are serious, if the institution fails to rectify within the time limit, or if it still fails to meet the requirements after rectification, the education administrative department or the labor administrative department shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order the institution to stop enrollment and revoke the Sino-foreign cooperative education license.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例的规定，发布虚假招生简章，骗取钱财的，由教育行政部门、劳动行政部门按照职责分工，责令限期改正并予以警告；有违法所得的，退还所收费用后没收违法所得，并可处以10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停止招生、吊销中外合作办学许可证；构成犯罪的，依照刑法关于诈骗罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。  
中外合作办学机构发布虚假招生广告的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定追究其法律责任。

**Article 57** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, issues false enrollment brochures to defraud money, shall be ordered by the education administrative department or the labor administrative department according to their respective functions and duties to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be given a warning; if there are illegal gains, the gains shall be confiscated after refunding the fees collected, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the institution shall be ordered to stop enrollment and the Sino-foreign cooperative education license shall be revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of fraud or any other crime.  
If a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution issues false enrollment advertisements, its legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　中外合作办学机构被处以吊销中外合作办学许可证行政处罚的，其理事长或者董事长、校长或者主要行政负责人自中外合作办学许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得担任任何中外合作办学机构的理事长或者董事长、校长或者主要行政负责人。  
违反本条例的规定，触犯刑律被依法追究刑事责任的，自刑罚执行期满之日起10年内不得从事中外合作办学活动。

**Article 58** If a Sino-foreign cooperative education institution is subject to the administrative penalty of revocation of the Sino-foreign cooperative education license, its chairperson of the board of governors or board of directors, its principal or chief administrative officer shall not serve as the chairperson of the board of governors or board of directors, principal or chief administrative officer of any Sino-foreign cooperative education institution within 10 years from the date of revocation of the Sino-foreign cooperative education license.  
If a person violates the provisions of these Regulations, commits a crime and is investigated for criminal liability according to law, such person shall not engage in Sino-foreign cooperative education activities within 10 years from the date of completion of the execution of the criminal punishment.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的教育机构与内地教育机构合作办学的，参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 59** Where educational institutions of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region cooperate with educational institutions in the mainland in running schools, the provisions of these Regulations shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　在市场监督管理部门登记注册的经营性的中外合作举办的培训机构的管理办法，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 60** The administrative measures for for-profit Sino-foreign cooperative training institutions registered with the market supervision and administration department shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　外国教育机构同中国教育机构在中国境内合作举办以中国公民为主要招生对象的实施学历教育和自学考试助学、文化补习、学前教育等的合作办学项目的具体审批和管理办法，由国务院教育行政部门制定。  
外国教育机构同中国教育机构在中国境内合作举办以中国公民为主要招生对象的实施职业技能培训的合作办学项目的具体审批和管理办法，由国务院劳动行政部门制定。

**Article 61** The specific examination and approval procedures and administrative measures for Chinese-foreign cooperative education projects that provide academic education, assistance for self-study examinations, cultural remedial courses, preschool education, etc., and mainly recruit Chinese citizens, and are jointly established by foreign educational institutions and Chinese educational institutions within the territory of China, shall be formulated by the administrative department of education under the State Council.  
The specific examination and approval procedures and administrative measures for Chinese-foreign cooperative education projects that provide vocational skill training and mainly recruit Chinese citizens, and are jointly established by foreign educational institutions and Chinese educational institutions within the territory of China, shall be formulated by the administrative department of labor under the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　外国教育机构、其他组织或者个人不得在中国境内单独设立以中国公民为主要招生对象的学校及其他教育机构。

**Article 62** No foreign educational institution, other organization, or individual may independently establish within the territory of China schools or other educational institutions that mainly recruit Chinese citizens.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例施行前依法设立的中外合作办学机构，应当补办本条例规定的中外合作办学许可证。其中，不完全具备本条例所规定条件的，应当在本条例施行之日起2年内达到本条例规定的条件；逾期未达到本条例规定条件的，由审批机关予以撤销。

**Article 63** Chinese-foreign cooperative education institutions established according to law before these Regulations come into force shall retroactively apply for a Chinese-foreign cooperative education license as prescribed by these Regulations. If any such institution does not fully meet the requirements set forth in these Regulations, it shall meet such requirements within two years from the date these Regulations come into force; if it fails to meet the requirements within the time limit, the examination and approval authority shall revoke the institution.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2003年9月1日起施行。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2003.
