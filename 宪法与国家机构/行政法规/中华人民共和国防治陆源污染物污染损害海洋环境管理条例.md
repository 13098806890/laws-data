# 中华人民共和国防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1990-06-21  
**生效日期**：1990-08-01

> (1990年5月25日国务院第61次常务会议通过　1990年6月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第61号发布　自1990年8月1日起施行) 第一条　为加强对陆地污染源的监督管理，防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境，根据《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称陆地污染源(简称陆源)，是指从陆地向海域排放污染物，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染损害的场所、设施等。 本条例所称陆源污染物是指由前款陆源排放的污染物。 第三条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内向海域排放陆源污染物的一切单位和个人。 防止拆船污染损害海洋环境，依照《防止拆船污染环境管理条例》执行。 第四条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门，主管全国防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境工作。 沿海县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门，主管本行政区域内防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境工作。 第五条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，必须执行国家和地方发布的污染物排放标准和有关规定。 第六条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，必须向其所在地环境保护行政主管部门申报登记拥有的污染物排放设施、处理设施和在正常作业条件下排放污染物的种类、数量和浓度，提供防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境的资料，并将上述事项和资料抄送海洋行政主管部门。 排放污染物的种类、数量和浓度有重大改变或者拆除、闲置污染物处理设施的，应当征得所在地环境保护行政主管部门同意并经原审批部门批准。 第七条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，超过国家和地方污染物排放标准的，必须缴纳超标准排污费，并负责治理。 第八条　任何单位和个人，不得在海洋特别保护区、海上自然保护区、海滨风景游览区、盐场保护区、海水浴场、重要渔业水域和其他需要特殊保护的区域内兴建排污口。 对在前款区域内已建的排污口，排放污染物超过国家和地方排放标准的，限期治理。 第九条　对向海域排放陆源污染物造成海洋环境严重污染损害的企业事业单位，限期治理。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强对陆地污染源的监督管理，防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境，根据《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and management of land-based pollution sources and preventing and controlling the pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称陆地污染源(简称陆源)，是指从陆地向海域排放污染物，造成或者可能造成海洋环境污染损害的场所、设施等。  
本条例所称陆源污染物是指由前款陆源排放的污染物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "land-based pollution source" (hereinafter referred to as "land-based source") means any site, facility, etc., that discharges pollutants from land to sea, causing or likely to cause pollution damage to the marine environment.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "land-based pollutants" means pollutants discharged from the land-based sources referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内向海域排放陆源污染物的一切单位和个人。  
防止拆船污染损害海洋环境，依照《防止拆船污染环境管理条例》执行。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to all entities and individuals that discharge land-based pollutants into the sea within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
The prevention of pollution damage to the marine environment by ship dismantling shall be governed by the Regulations on the Administration of the Prevention of Environmental Pollution by Ship Dismantling.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门，主管全国防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境工作。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门，主管本行政区域内防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境工作。

**Article 4** The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall be in charge of the work of preventing and controlling pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants nationwide.  
The competent administrative departments of environmental protection of local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be in charge of the work of preventing and controlling pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，必须执行国家和地方发布的污染物排放标准和有关规定。

**Article 5** Any entity or individual that discharges land-based pollutants into the sea shall comply with the pollutant discharge standards and relevant provisions issued by the State and local authorities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，必须向其所在地环境保护行政主管部门申报登记拥有的污染物排放设施、处理设施和在正常作业条件下排放污染物的种类、数量和浓度，提供防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境的资料，并将上述事项和资料抄送海洋行政主管部门。  
排放污染物的种类、数量和浓度有重大改变或者拆除、闲置污染物处理设施的，应当征得所在地环境保护行政主管部门同意并经原审批部门批准。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual that discharges land-based pollutants into the sea shall register with the competent administrative department of environmental protection in its locality the pollutant discharge facilities and treatment facilities it possesses, as well as the types, quantities and concentrations of pollutants discharged under normal operating conditions, provide information on the prevention and control of pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants, and send a copy of the aforesaid matters and information to the competent administrative department of marine affairs.  
Where the types, quantities or concentrations of pollutants discharged undergo major changes, or where pollutant treatment facilities are dismantled or left idle, the matter shall be subject to the consent of the competent administrative department of environmental protection in the locality and the approval of the original approving department.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人向海域排放陆源污染物，超过国家和地方污染物排放标准的，必须缴纳超标准排污费，并负责治理。

**Article 7** Any entity or individual that discharges land-based pollutants into the sea in excess of the national or local pollutant discharge standards shall pay an excess pollutant discharge fee and shall be responsible for treatment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人，不得在海洋特别保护区、海上自然保护区、海滨风景游览区、盐场保护区、海水浴场、重要渔业水域和其他需要特殊保护的区域内兴建排污口。  
对在前款区域内已建的排污口，排放污染物超过国家和地方排放标准的，限期治理。

**Article 8** No entity or individual may construct a sewage outlet in marine special protection areas, marine nature reserves, coastal scenic areas, salt pan protection areas, bathing beaches, important fishing waters, or other areas requiring special protection.  
For existing sewage outlets in the areas referred to in the preceding paragraph that discharge pollutants in excess of the national or local discharge standards, a time limit for treatment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对向海域排放陆源污染物造成海洋环境严重污染损害的企业事业单位，限期治理。

**Article 9** For enterprises and institutions that discharge land-based pollutants into the sea and cause serious pollution damage to the marine environment, a time limit for treatment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院各部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府直接管辖的企业事业单位的限期治理，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的环境保护行政主管部门提出意见，报同级人民政府决定。市、县或者市、县以下人民政府管辖的企业事业单位的限期治理，由市、县人民政府环境保护行政主管部门提出意见，报同级人民政府决定。被限期治理的企业事业单位必须如期完成治理任务。

**Article 10** For enterprises and institutions directly under the jurisdiction of departments of the State Council or people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the time limit for treatment shall be proposed by the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and submitted to the people's government at the same level for decision. For enterprises and institutions under the jurisdiction of a city or county people's government or a people's government below the county level, the time limit for treatment shall be proposed by the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the city or county people's government and submitted to the people's government at the same level for decision. The enterprises and institutions on which a time limit for treatment is imposed shall complete the treatment task within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　禁止在岸滩擅自堆放、弃置和处理固体废弃物。确需临时堆放、处理固体废弃物的，必须按照沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门规定的审批程序，提出书面申请。其主要内容包括：  
(一)申请单位的名称、地址；  
(二)堆放、处理的地点和占地面积；  
(三)固体废弃物的种类、成分，年堆放量、处理量，积存堆放、处理的总量和堆放高度；  
(四)固体废弃物堆放、处理的期限，最终处置方式；  
(五)堆放、处理固体废弃物可能对海洋环境造成的污染损害；  
(六)防止堆放、处理固体废弃物污染损害海洋环境的技术和措施；  
(七)审批机关认为需要说明的其他事项。  
现有的固体废弃物临时堆放、处理场地，未经县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门批准的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令限期补办审批手续。

**Article 11** The piling, dumping, or disposal of solid waste on the shore or beach without authorization is prohibited. Where temporary piling or disposal of solid waste is truly necessary, a written application shall be submitted in accordance with the examination and approval procedures prescribed by the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the coastal province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The main contents of the application shall include:  
(1) the name and address of the applicant;  
(2) the location and area occupied for piling or disposal;  
(3) the types and composition of the solid waste, the annual piling and disposal quantities, the total accumulated piling and disposal quantities, and the piling height;  
(4) the period for piling or disposal of solid waste and the final disposal method;  
(5) the possible pollution damage to the marine environment caused by piling or disposal of solid waste;  
(6) the technology and measures for preventing pollution damage to the marine environment from piling or disposal of solid waste; and  
(7) other matters that the approving authority considers necessary to be explained.  
For existing sites for temporary piling or disposal of solid waste that have not been approved by the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level, the said department shall order the party concerned to go through the approval procedures within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被批准设置废弃物堆放场、处理场的单位和个人，必须建造防护堤和防渗漏、防扬尘等设施，经批准设置废弃物堆放场、处理场的环境保护行政主管部门验收合格后方可使用。  
在批准使用的废弃物堆放场、处理场内，不得擅自堆放、弃置未经批准的其他种类的废弃物。不得露天堆放含剧毒、放射性、易溶解和易挥发性物质的废弃物；非露天堆放上述废弃物，不得作为最终处置方式。

**Article 12** Entities and individuals that are approved to establish waste piling yards or treatment sites shall construct protective dikes and facilities for preventing leakage, dust, etc., and may use them only after they have been inspected and accepted by the competent administrative department of environmental protection that approved the establishment of such yards or sites.  
In the approved waste piling yards or treatment sites, no other types of waste not approved may be piled or dumped without authorization. No waste containing highly toxic, radioactive, readily soluble, or readily volatile substances may be piled in the open air; non-open-air piling of the aforesaid waste shall not be used as a final disposal method.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　禁止在岸滩采用不正当的稀释、渗透方式排放有毒、有害废水。

**Article 13** Discharging toxic or harmful wastewater by means of improper dilution or percolation on the shore or beach is prohibited.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　禁止向海域排放含高、中放射性物质的废水。  
向海域排放含低放射性物质的废水，必须执行国家有关放射防护的规定和标准。

**Article 14** Discharging wastewater containing high or medium radioactive substances into the sea is prohibited.  
Discharging wastewater containing low radioactive substances into the sea shall comply with the national regulations and standards concerning radiation protection.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止向海域排放油类、酸液、碱液和毒液。  
向海域排放含油废水、含有害重金属废水和其他工业废水，必须经过处理，符合国家和地方规定的排放标准和有关规定。处理后的残渣不得弃置入海。

**Article 15** Discharging oils, acid solutions, alkaline solutions, or toxic liquids into the sea is prohibited.  
Wastewater containing oil, wastewater containing harmful heavy metals, and other industrial wastewater discharged into the sea shall be treated to meet the national and local discharge standards and relevant provisions. The residues after treatment shall not be dumped into the sea.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　向海域排放含病原体的废水，必须经过处理，符合国家和地方规定的排放标准和有关规定。

**Article 16** Wastewater containing pathogens discharged into the sea shall be treated to meet the national and local discharge standards and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　向海域排放含热废水的水温应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 17** The water temperature of wastewater containing heat discharged into the sea shall comply with the relevant national provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　向自净能力较差的海域排放含有机物和营养物质的工业废水和生活废水，应当控制排放量；排污口应当设置在海水交换良好处，并采用合理的排放方式，防止海水富营养化。

**Article 18** The discharge of industrial wastewater and domestic wastewater containing organic matter and nutrients into sea areas with poor self-purification capacity shall be controlled; the sewage outlets shall be located in areas with good seawater exchange, and reasonable discharge methods shall be adopted to prevent eutrophication of the seawater.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　禁止将失效或者禁用的药物及药具弃置岸滩。

**Article 19** Dumping of expired or prohibited drugs and pharmaceutical devices on the shore or beach is prohibited.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　入海河口处发生陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境事故，确有证据证明是由河流携带污染物造成的，由入海河口处所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门调查处理；河流跨越省、自治区、直辖市的，由入海河口处所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门和水利部门会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门、水利部门和流域管理机构调查处理。

**Article 20** Where an accident of pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants occurs at the estuary of a river discharging into the sea and there is evidence proving that it is caused by pollutants carried by the river, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the estuary is located shall investigate and handle the matter; if the river flows across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the competent administrative department of environmental protection and the water conservancy department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the estuary is located shall, together with the competent administrative departments of environmental protection, water conservancy departments, and river basin management authorities of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, investigate and handle the matter.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　沿海相邻或者相向地区向同一海域排放陆源污染物的，由有关地方人民政府协商制定共同防治陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境的措施。

**Article 21** Where adjacent or opposite coastal areas discharge land-based pollutants into the same sea area, the relevant local people's governments shall negotiate and formulate joint measures for preventing and controlling pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　一切单位和个人造成陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境事故时，必须立即采取措施处理，并在事故发生后四十八小时内，向当地人民政府环境保护行政主管部门作出事故发生的时间、地点、类型和排放污染物的数量、经济损失、人员受害等情况的初步报告，并抄送有关部门。事故查清后，应当向当地人民政府环境保护行政主管部门作出书面报告，并附有关证明文件。  
各级人民政府环境保护行政主管部门接到陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境事故的初步报告后，应当立即会同有关部门采取措施，消除或者减轻污染，并由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门会同有关部门或者由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门授权的部门对事故进行调查处理。

**Article 22** When any entity or individual causes an accident of pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants, it shall immediately take measures to deal with the accident and, within 48 hours after the occurrence of the accident, submit a preliminary report to the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the local people's government on the time, place, type, and quantity of pollutants discharged, economic losses, and casualties, and send a copy of the report to the relevant departments. After the accident is clarified, a written report shall be submitted to the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the local people's government, together with relevant supporting documents.  
Upon receiving the preliminary report of an accident of pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants, the competent administrative departments of environmental protection of people's governments at all levels shall immediately take measures jointly with the relevant departments to eliminate or mitigate the pollution, and the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the relevant departments or a department authorized by it, investigate and handle the accident.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门，按照项目管理权限，可以会同项目主管部门对排放陆源污染物的单位和个人进行现场检查，被检查者必须如实反映情况、提供资料。检查者有责任为被检查者保守技术秘密和业务秘密。法律法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 23** The competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level may, within its authority of project management, carry out on-site inspections together with the project competent department of entities and individuals discharging land-based pollutants; the party being inspected shall truthfully report the situation and provide information. The inspectors shall have the duty to keep confidential the technical secrets and business secrets of the party being inspected, unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，具有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令改正，并可处以三百元以上三千元以下的罚款：  
(一)拒报或者谎报排污申报登记事项的；  
(二)拒绝、阻挠环境保护行政主管部门现场检查，或者在被检查中弄虚作假的。

**Article 24** In any of the following circumstances, in violation of these Regulations, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the violator to make corrections and may also impose a fine of not less than 300 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan:  
(1) refusing to report or making false reports on the matters registered for pollutant discharge declaration; or  
(2) refusing or obstructing on-site inspection by the competent administrative department of environmental protection, or practicing fraud during the inspection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　废弃物堆放场、处理场的防污染设施未经环境保护行政主管部门验收或者验收不合格而强行使用的，由环境保护行政主管部门责令改正，并可处以五千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 25** Where anti-pollution facilities of a waste piling yard or treatment site are used forcibly without being inspected and accepted by the competent administrative department of environmental protection, or after failing the inspection and acceptance, the competent administrative department of environmental protection shall order the violator to make corrections and may also impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，具有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令改正，并可处以五千元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未经所在地环境保护行政主管部门同意和原批准部门批准，擅自改变污染物排放的种类、增加污染物排放的数量、浓度或者拆除、闲置污染物处理设施的；  
(二)在[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一款规定的区域内兴建排污口的。

**Article 26** In any of the following circumstances, in violation of these Regulations, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the violator to make corrections and may also impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) without the consent of the competent administrative department of environmental protection in the locality and the approval of the original approving department, altering without authorization the types of pollutants discharged, increasing the quantities or concentrations of pollutants discharged, or dismantling or leaving idle the pollutant treatment facilities; or  
(2) constructing a sewage outlet in the area specified in paragraph 1 of Article 8 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，具有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令改正，并可处以一千元以上二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可处以二万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在岸滩采用不正当的稀释、渗透方式排放有毒、有害废水的；  
(二)向海域排放含高、中放射性物质的废水的；  
(三)向海域排放油类、酸液、碱液和毒液的；  
(四)向岸滩弃置失效或者禁用的药物和药具的；  
(五)向海域排放含油废水、含病原体废水、含热废水、含低放射性物质废水、含有害重金属废水和其他工业废水超过国家和地方规定的排放标准和有关规定或者将处理后的残渣弃置入海的；  
(六)未经县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门批准，擅自在岸滩堆放、弃置和处理废弃物或者在废弃物堆放场、处理场内，擅自堆放、处理未经批准的其他种类的废弃物或者露天堆放含剧毒、放射性、易溶解和易挥发性物质的废弃物的。

**Article 27** In any of the following circumstances, in violation of these Regulations, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the violator to make corrections and may also impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed:  
(1) discharging toxic or harmful wastewater by means of improper dilution or percolation on the shore or beach;  
(2) discharging wastewater containing high or medium radioactive substances into the sea;  
(3) discharging oils, acid solutions, alkaline solutions, or toxic liquids into the sea;  
(4) dumping expired or prohibited drugs and pharmaceutical devices on the shore or beach;  
(5) discharging wastewater containing oil, wastewater containing pathogens, wastewater containing heat, wastewater containing low radioactive substances, wastewater containing harmful heavy metals, or other industrial wastewater into the sea in excess of the national or local discharge standards and relevant provisions, or dumping the residues after treatment into the sea; or  
(6) without the approval of the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level, piling, dumping, or disposing of waste on the shore or beach without authorization, or piling or disposing of other types of waste not approved without authorization in a waste piling yard or treatment site, or piling waste containing highly toxic, radioactive, readily soluble, or readily volatile substances in the open air.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对逾期未完成限期治理任务的企业事业单位，征收两倍的超标准排污费，并可根据危害和损失后果，处以一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，或者责令停业、关闭。  
罚款由环境保护行政主管部门决定。责令停业、关闭，由作出限期治理决定的人民政府决定；责令国务院各部门直接管辖的企业事业单位停业、关闭，须报国务院批准。

**Article 28** For enterprises and institutions that fail to complete the task of treatment within the prescribed time limit, an excess pollutant discharge fee of two times the normal amount shall be levied, and depending on the harm and losses caused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed, or the violator may be ordered to suspend operations or close down.  
The fine shall be decided by the competent administrative department of environmental protection. The order to suspend operations or close down shall be decided by the people's government that made the decision on the time limit for treatment; an order to suspend operations or close down for enterprises and institutions directly under the jurisdiction of departments of the State Council shall be subject to the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　不按规定缴纳超标准排污费的，除追缴超标准排污费及滞纳金外，并可由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门处以一千元以上一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where the excess pollutant discharge fee is not paid according to the regulations, in addition to recovery of the excess pollutant discharge fee and overdue payment surcharge, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level may impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对造成陆源污染物污染损害海洋环境事故，导致重大经济损失的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门按照直接损失百分之三十计算罚款，但最高不得超过二十万元。

**Article 30** For causing an accident of pollution damage to the marine environment by land-based pollutants that results in significant economic losses, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall calculate the fine at 30% of the direct losses, provided that the maximum fine shall not exceed 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级人民政府环境保护行政主管部门可处以一万元以下的罚款，超过一万元的罚款，报上级环境保护行政主管部门批准。  
省辖市级人民政府环境保护行政主管部门可处以五万元以下的罚款，超过五万元的罚款，报上级环境保护行政主管部门批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门可处以二十万元以下的罚款。  
罚款全部上交国库，任何单位和个人不得截留、分成。

**Article 31** The competent administrative department of environmental protection of the county people's government may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; a fine exceeding 10,000 yuan shall be submitted to the superior competent administrative department of environmental protection for approval.  
The competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of a city under the jurisdiction of a province may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; a fine exceeding 50,000 yuan shall be submitted to the superior competent administrative department of environmental protection for approval.  
The competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan.  
All fines shall be turned over to the State Treasury in full, and no entity or individual may withhold or divide them.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　缴纳超标准排污费或者被处以罚款的单位、个人，并不免除消除污染、排除危害和赔偿损失的责任。

**Article 32** The payment of an excess pollutant discharge fee or the imposition of a fine shall not relieve an entity or individual of the liability for eliminating pollution, removing the hazard, and compensating for losses.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内，依法申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内，直接向人民法院起诉。当事人逾期不申请复议、也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 33** A party that is not satisfied with a decision on an administrative penalty may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, apply for reconsideration according to law; if it is not satisfied with the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, bring a prosecution before the People's Court. The party may also directly bring a prosecution before the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty. If the party neither applies for reconsideration nor brings a prosecution before the People's Court within the prescribed time limit, nor complies with the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　环境保护行政主管部门工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** Where a staff member of the competent administrative department of environmental protection abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, the entity to which he belongs or the competent authority at a higher level shall impose an administrative sanction upon him; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 35** The people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例由国务院环境保护行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自一九九○年八月一日起施行。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 1990.
