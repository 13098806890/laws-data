# 国防交通条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1995年2月24日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第173号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强国防交通建设，保障战时和特殊情况下国防交通顺畅，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事国防交通活动，必须遵守本条例。 本条例所称国防交通，是指为国防建设服务的铁路、道路、水路、航空、管道、邮电通信等交通体系。 第三条　国防交通工作实行统一领导、分级负责、全面规划、平战结合的原则。 第四条　各级人民政府、军事机关应当重视国防交通建设，为国防交通工作提供必要条件。 县级以上人民政府交通管理部门和有关交通企业事业单位，应当做好国防交通工作。 第五条　对在国防交通建设中做出重大贡献的单位和个人，各级人民政府、交通管理部门和军事机关应当给予奖励。 第二章　管理机构及其职责 第六条　国家国防交通主管机构在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，负责全国国防交通工作，履行下列职责： (一)拟订国防交通工作的方针、政策，草拟有关法律、行政法规； (二)规划全国国防交通网络布局，对国家交通建设提出有关国防要求的建议； (三)拟订全国国防交通保障计划，为重大军事行动和其他紧急任务组织交通保障； (四)组织全国国防交通科学技术研究；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国防交通建设，保障战时和特殊情况下国防交通顺畅，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the construction of national defense transportation and ensuring the smooth operation of national defense transportation during wartime and under special circumstances.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事国防交通活动，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所称国防交通，是指为国防建设服务的铁路、道路、水路、航空、管道、邮电通信等交通体系。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in national defense transportation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must abide by these Regulations.  
National defense transportation as mentioned in these Regulations refers to the transportation systems such as railways, highways, waterways, aviation, pipelines, and postal and telecommunications that serve national defense construction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国防交通工作实行统一领导、分级负责、全面规划、平战结合的原则。

**Article 3** The work of national defense transportation shall follow the principles of unified leadership, graded responsibility, comprehensive planning, and integration of peacetime and wartime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府、军事机关应当重视国防交通建设，为国防交通工作提供必要条件。  
县级以上人民政府交通管理部门和有关交通企业事业单位，应当做好国防交通工作。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels and military authorities shall attach importance to national defense transportation construction and provide necessary conditions for national defense transportation work.  
Transportation administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level and relevant transportation enterprises and institutions shall properly carry out national defense transportation work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对在国防交通建设中做出重大贡献的单位和个人，各级人民政府、交通管理部门和军事机关应当给予奖励。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels, transportation administration departments, and military authorities shall give rewards to units and individuals that have made significant contributions to national defense transportation construction.

### 第二章　管理机构及其职责

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家国防交通主管机构在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，负责全国国防交通工作，履行下列职责：  
(一)拟订国防交通工作的方针、政策，草拟有关法律、行政法规；  
(二)规划全国国防交通网络布局，对国家交通建设提出有关国防要求的建议；  
(三)拟订全国国防交通保障计划，为重大军事行动和其他紧急任务组织交通保障；  
(四)组织全国国防交通科学技术研究；  
(五)指导检查国防交通工作，协调有关方面的关系；  
(六)国务院、中央军事委员会赋予的其他职责。

**Article 6** The national competent authority for national defense transportation, under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, shall be responsible for the national defense transportation work nationwide and perform the following duties:  
(1) Formulating guidelines and policies for national defense transportation work, and drafting relevant laws and administrative regulations;  
(2) Planning the national defense transportation network layout, and making suggestions on national defense requirements for national transportation construction;  
(3) Formulating the national defense transportation support plan, and organizing transportation support for major military operations and other emergency tasks;  
(4) Organizing scientific and technological research on national defense transportation nationwide;  
(5) Guiding and inspecting national defense transportation work, and coordinating relations among relevant parties;  
(6) Other duties assigned by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军区国防交通主管机构和县级以上地方国防交通主管机构负责本地区国防交通工作，履行下列职责：  
(一)贯彻执行国家国防交通工作的方针、政策和法律、法规、规章，拟订本地区有关国防交通工作的规定；  
(二)规划本地区国防交通网络布局，对本地区交通建设提出有关国防要求的建议，参加有关交通工程设施的勘察、设计鉴(审)定和竣工验收；  
(三)拟订本地区国防交通保障计划，组织国防交通保障队伍，为本地区内的军事行动和其他紧急任务组织交通保障；  
(四)负责本地区的国防运力动员和运力征用；  
(五)按照国家有关规定，制定和实施本地区的国防交通物资储备计划，调用国防交通物资；  
(六)组织本地区国防交通科学技术研究及其成果的推广、应用；  
(七)指导、检查、监督本地区国防交通工作，协调处理有关问题；  
(八)上级国防交通主管机构和本级人民政府赋予的其他职责。

**Article 7** The national defense transportation competent authorities of military regions and the local national defense transportation competent authorities at or above the county level shall be responsible for national defense transportation work in their respective regions and perform the following duties:  
(1) Implementing the national guidelines, policies, laws, regulations, and rules for national defense transportation work, and formulating provisions on national defense transportation work for their respective regions;  
(2) Planning the national defense transportation network layout for their respective regions, making suggestions on national defense requirements for transportation construction in their regions, and participating in the survey, design review (examination) and completion acceptance of relevant transportation engineering facilities;  
(3) Formulating national defense transportation support plans for their respective regions, organizing national defense transportation support teams, and organizing transportation support for military operations and other emergency tasks within their regions;  
(4) Being responsible for the mobilization and requisition of national defense transportation capacity in their respective regions;  
(5) Formulating and implementing national defense transportation material reserve plans for their respective regions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and allocating national defense transportation materials;  
(6) Organizing scientific and technological research on national defense transportation in their respective regions and the promotion and application of its outcomes;  
(7) Guiding, inspecting, and supervising national defense transportation work in their respective regions, and coordinating and handling relevant issues;  
(8) Other duties assigned by the superior national defense transportation competent authority and the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院交通管理部门分别负责本系统的国防交通工作，履行下列职责：  
(一)贯彻执行国家国防交通工作的方针、政策和法律、法规、规章；  
(二)制定并组织落实本系统的国防交通建设规划和技术规范；  
(三)制定本系统的国防交通保障计划，指导国防交通专业保障队伍建设；  
(四)按照国家有关规定，管理和使用本系统的国防交通资产；  
(五)组织本系统国防交通科学技术研究及其成果的推广、应用；  
(六)指导、检查、监督本系统的国防交通工作，协调处理有关问题。

**Article 8** The transportation administration departments of the State Council shall respectively be responsible for national defense transportation work within their own systems and perform the following duties:  
(1) Implementing the national guidelines, policies, laws, regulations, and rules for national defense transportation work;  
(2) Formulating and organizing the implementation of national defense transportation construction plans and technical specifications for their respective systems;  
(3) Formulating national defense transportation support plans for their respective systems and guiding the construction of professional national defense transportation support teams;  
(4) Managing and using national defense transportation assets within their respective systems in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) Organizing scientific and technological research on national defense transportation within their respective systems and the promotion and application of its outcomes;  
(6) Guiding, inspecting, and supervising national defense transportation work within their respective systems, and coordinating and handling relevant issues.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承担国防交通任务的交通企业事业单位，在国防交通工作中履行下列职责：  
(一)贯彻执行国家国防交通工作的方针、政策和法律、法规、规章；  
(二)参加有关国防交通工程设施的勘察、设计鉴(审)定和竣工验收；  
(三)制定本单位国防交通保障计划，完成国防交通保障任务；  
(四)按照国家有关规定，管理和使用本单位的国防交通资产；  
(五)负责本单位的国防交通专业保障队伍的组织、训练和管理工作。

**Article 9** Transportation enterprises and institutions undertaking national defense transportation tasks shall perform the following duties in national defense transportation work:  
(1) Implementing the national guidelines, policies, laws, regulations, and rules for national defense transportation work;  
(2) Participating in the survey, design review (examination) and completion acceptance of relevant national defense transportation engineering facilities;  
(3) Formulating national defense transportation support plans for their own units and completing national defense transportation support tasks;  
(4) Managing and using national defense transportation assets of their own units in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) Being responsible for the organization, training, and management of professional national defense transportation support teams of their own units.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在特殊情况下，省级国防交通主管机构可以提请有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定，由公安机关、港务监督机构分别在自己的职责范围内对局部地区的道路、水路实行交通管制。

**Article 10** Under special circumstances, the provincial-level national defense transportation competent authority may request the relevant people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to decide that the public security organs and port supervision authorities shall, within their respective scope of duties, impose traffic control on roads and waterways in local areas.

### 第三章　保障计划

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本条例所称国防交通保障计划(以下简称保障计划)，是指保障战时和特殊情况下国防交通顺畅的预定方案，主要包括：国防交通保障的方针、任务，各项国防交通保障工作的技术措施和组织措施。  
保障计划分为：全国保障计划、军区保障计划、地区保障计划和专业保障计划。

**Article 11** For the purpose of these Regulations, the term "national defense traffic support plan" (hereinafter referred to as the support plan) means the predetermined scheme for ensuring smooth national defense traffic in time of war and under special circumstances, which mainly includes: the guidelines and tasks for national defense traffic support, and the technical and organizational measures for various national defense traffic support work.  
The support plans are divided into: national support plan, military command area support plan, regional support plan and specialized support plan.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全国保障计划由国家国防交通主管机构组织国务院有关部门和有关军事机关拟订，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 12** The national support plan shall be formulated by the national competent authority for national defense traffic in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and relevant military organs, and submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军区保障计划由军区国防交通主管机构组织本地区省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门和有关军事机关拟订，征求国家国防交通主管机构的意见后，报军区批准。

**Article 13** The military command area support plan shall be formulated by the competent authority for national defense traffic of the military command area in conjunction with the relevant departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government in its region and relevant military organs, and after seeking the opinions of the national competent authority for national defense traffic, submitted to the military command area for approval.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地区保障计划由县级以上地方国防交通主管机构组织本级人民政府有关部门和有关军事机关拟订，征求上一级国防交通主管机构意见后，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 14** The regional support plan shall be formulated by the local competent authority for national defense traffic at or above the county level in conjunction with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and relevant military organs, and after seeking the opinions of the competent authority for national defense traffic at the next higher level, submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专业保障计划由国务院交通管理部门在各自的职责范围内分别制定，征求国务院其他有关部门意见后，报国家国防交通主管机构同意。

**Article 15** The specialized support plans shall be formulated separately by the transport administration departments of the State Council within their respective duties, and after seeking the opinions of other relevant departments of the State Council, submitted to the national competent authority for national defense traffic for approval.

### 第四章　工程设施

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例所称国防交通工程设施，是指为保障战时和特殊情况下国防交通顺畅而建造的下列建筑和设备：  
(一)国家修建的主要为国防建设服务的交通基础设施；  
(二)专用的指挥、检修、仓储、防护等工程与设施；  
(三)专用的车辆、船舶、航空器；  
(四)国防需要的其他交通工程设施。

**Article 16** For the purpose of these Regulations, the term "national defense traffic engineering facilities" means the following constructions and equipment built for ensuring smooth national defense traffic in time of war and under special circumstances:  
(1) transportation infrastructure constructed by the State mainly for serving national defense development;  
(2) dedicated engineering works and facilities for command, maintenance, storage, protection, etc.;  
(3) dedicated vehicles, vessels and aircraft;  
(4) other traffic engineering facilities required for national defense.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设国防交通工程设施，应当兼顾经济建设的需要。  
建设其他交通工程设施或者研制重要交通工具，应当兼顾国防建设的需要。

**Article 17** In constructing national defense traffic engineering facilities, consideration shall also be given to the needs of economic development.  
In constructing other traffic engineering facilities or developing important means of transportation, consideration shall also be given to the needs of national defense development.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国防交通主管机构拟订的国防交通建设规划，应当送本级人民政府计划部门和交通管理部门综合平衡。  
县级以上人民政府计划部门和交通管理部门在制定交通建设规划时，应当征求本级国防交通主管机构的意见，并将已经确定的国防交通工程设施建设项目和需要贯彻国防要求的建设项目，列入交通建设规划。

**Article 18** The national defense traffic construction plan formulated by the competent authority for national defense traffic shall be submitted to the planning department and the transport administration department of the people's government at the same level for comprehensive balancing.  
When formulating traffic construction plans, the planning department and the transport administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall solicit the opinions of the competent authority for national defense traffic at the same level, and incorporate into the traffic construction plan the established national defense traffic engineering facility construction projects and the construction projects that need to implement defense requirements.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　交通建设规划中有关贯彻国防要求的建设项目，必须按照国防要求进行建设。

**Article 19** The construction projects in the traffic construction plan that concern the implementation of defense requirements must be constructed in accordance with defense requirements.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国防交通工程设施建设项目和有关贯彻国防要求的建设项目，其设计鉴(审)定、竣工验收应当经有关的国防交通主管机构同意。

**Article 20** The design appraisal (review) and completion acceptance of national defense traffic engineering facility construction projects and the construction projects that implement defense requirements shall be subject to the consent of the relevant competent authority for national defense traffic.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国防交通工程设施的管理单位，必须加强对国防交通工程设施的维护管理。  
改变国防交通工程设施的用途或者将其作报废处理的，必须经管理单位的上一级国防交通主管机构批准。

**Article 21** The management unit of national defense traffic engineering facilities shall strengthen the maintenance and management of national defense traffic engineering facilities.  
To change the use of national defense traffic engineering facilities or to scrap them, the approval of the superior national defense traffic competent authority of the management unit shall be obtained.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对国防交通工程设施的建设实行优惠政策。具体办法由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 22** The State shall implement preferential policies for the construction of national defense traffic engineering facilities. Specific measures shall be formulated by the national defense traffic competent authority of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　土地管理部门和城市规划主管部门，应当将经批准的预定抢建重要国防交通工程设施的土地作为国防交通控制用地，纳入土地利用总体规划和城市规划。  
未经土地管理部门、城市规划主管部门和国防交通主管机构批准，任何单位或者个人不得占用国防交通控制用地。

**Article 23** The land administration department and the urban planning competent authority shall include the land approved for pre-planned rush construction of important national defense traffic engineering facilities as national defense traffic control land into the overall land use plan and urban plan.  
No unit or individual shall occupy the national defense traffic control land without the approval of the land administration department, the urban planning competent authority, and the national defense traffic competent authority.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何单位或者个人进行生产和其他活动，不得影响国防交通工程设施的正常使用，不得危及国防交通工程设施的安全。

**Article 24** No unit or individual shall, when engaging in production or other activities, affect the normal use of national defense traffic engineering facilities or endanger the safety of such facilities.

### 第五章　保障队伍

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例所称国防交通保障队伍，是指战时和特殊情况下执行抢修、抢建、防护国防交通工程设施、抢运国防交通物资和通信保障任务的组织。  
国防交通保障队伍，分为专业保障队伍和交通沿线保障队伍。

**Article 25** For the purpose of these Regulations, national defense traffic support teams refer to organizations that perform tasks of rush repair, rush construction, protection of national defense traffic engineering facilities, rush transportation of national defense traffic materials, and communication support during wartime and under special circumstances.  
National defense traffic support teams shall be divided into professional support teams and traffic line support teams.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　专业保障队伍，由交通管理部门以本系统交通企业生产单位为基础进行组建；执行交通保障任务时，由国防交通主管机构统一调配。  
交通沿线保障队伍，由当地人民政府和有关军事机关负责组织。

**Article 26** Professional support teams shall be formed by the traffic administration department based on the production units of the traffic enterprises of its system; when performing traffic support tasks, they shall be uniformly deployed by the national defense traffic competent authority.  
Traffic line support teams shall be organized by the local people's government and the relevant military organs.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　交通管理部门负责专业保障队伍的训练，战时应当保持专业保障队伍人员稳定。  
有关军事机关负责组织交通沿线保障队伍的专业训练；国防交通主管机构负责提供教材、器材和业务指导。

**Article 27** The traffic administration department shall be responsible for the training of professional support teams, and shall maintain the stability of the personnel of professional support teams during wartime.  
The relevant military organs shall be responsible for organizing professional training of traffic line support teams; the national defense traffic competent authority shall be responsible for providing teaching materials, equipment, and professional guidance.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府及有关部门，对专业保障队伍应当给予必要的扶持。

**Article 28** The people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant departments shall provide necessary support to professional support teams.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　交通保障队伍的车辆、船舶和其他机动设备，应当按照国家国防交通主管机构的规定，设置统一标志；在战时和特殊情况下可以优先通行。

**Article 29** The vehicles, vessels, and other motorized equipment of traffic support teams shall be marked with unified signs in accordance with the provisions of the national defense traffic competent authority of the State; and may have priority passage during wartime and under special circumstances.

### 第六章　运力动员和运力征用

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例所称运力动员，是指战时国家发布动员令，对任何单位和个人所拥有的运载工具、设备以及操作人员，进行统一组织和调用的活动。  
本条例所称运力征用，是指在特殊情况下，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依法采取行政措施，调用单位和个人所拥有的运载工具、设备以及操作人员的活动。

**Article 30** For the purpose of these Regulations, transportation capacity mobilization refers to the activity wherein the State, after issuing a mobilization order during wartime, uniformly organizes and calls up the means of transport, equipment, and operators owned by any unit or individual.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, transportation capacity requisition refers to the activity wherein the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, under special circumstances, takes administrative measures according to law to call up the means of transport, equipment, and operators owned by units or individuals.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府交通管理部门和其他有关部门应当向国防交通主管机构提供运力注册登记的有关资料。

**Article 31** The transportation administration departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide the competent department of national defense transportation with relevant information on the registration of transportation capacity.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　战时军队需要使用动员的运力的，应当向所在地的军区国防交通主管机构提出申请。武装警察部队、民兵组织和其他单位需要使用动员的运力的，应当向当地国防交通主管机构提出申请。

**Article 32** Where the armed forces need to use mobilized transportation capacity during wartime, they shall submit an application to the competent department of national defense transportation of the military area command where they are located. Where the armed police forces, militia organizations, or other units need to use mobilized transportation capacity, they shall submit an application to the local competent department of national defense transportation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　动员国务院交通管理部门所属的运力，应当经国务院、中央军事委员会批准。动员地方交通管理部门所属的运力或者社会运力，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 33** Mobilization of transportation capacity subordinate to the transportation administration department of the State Council shall be approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission. Mobilization of transportation capacity subordinate to local transportation administration departments or social transportation capacity shall be approved by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在特殊情况下，军队或者其他单位需要使用征用的运力的，应当向当地国防交通主管机构提出申请，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 34** Under special circumstances, where the armed forces or other units need to use requisitioned transportation capacity, they shall submit an application to the local competent department of national defense transportation, and the application shall be approved by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　被动员或者被征用运力的单位和个人必须依法履行义务，保证被动员或者被征用的运载工具和设备的技术状况良好，并保证随同的操作人员具有相应的技能。

**Article 35** The units and individuals whose transportation capacity is mobilized or requisitioned must perform their obligations according to law, ensure that the mobilized or requisitioned means of transport and equipment are in good technical condition, and ensure that the accompanying operators possess the corresponding skills.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　需要对动员或者征用的运载工具、设备作重大改造的，必须经相应的国防交通主管机构批准。

**Article 36** Where major modifications need to be made to mobilized or requisitioned means of transport or equipment, approval from the corresponding competent department of national defense transportation must be obtained.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对被动员和被征用运力的操作人员的抚恤优待，按照国家有关规定执行；运载工具、设备的补偿办法另行规定。

**Article 37** The pension and preferential treatment for operators of mobilized or requisitioned transportation capacity shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; the compensation methods for means of transport and equipment shall be separately prescribed.

### 第七章　军事运输

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　交通管理部门和交通企业应当优先安排军事运输计划，重点保障紧急、重要的军事运输。运输军事人员、装备及其他军用物资，应当迅速准确、安全保密。

**Article 38** The transportation administration departments and transportation enterprises shall give priority to military transportation plans, with emphasis on ensuring urgent and important military transportation. The transportation of military personnel, equipment, and other military materials shall be prompt, accurate, safe, and confidential.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　地方各级人民政府和有条件的承运单位，应当为实施军事运输的人员提供饮食、住宿和医疗方便。

**Article 39** The local people's governments at all levels and carrier units that have the conditions shall provide food, accommodation, and medical convenience for personnel engaged in military transportation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军队可以在铁路、水路、航空等交通运输单位或其所在地区派驻军事代表，会同有关单位共同完成军事运输和交通保障任务。

**Article 40** The armed forces may station military representatives at railway, waterway, aviation, and other transportation units or in their localities to work together with the relevant units to accomplish military transportation and traffic support tasks.

### 第八章　物资储备

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家建立国防交通物资储备制度，保证战时和特殊情况下国防交通顺畅的需要。

**Article 41** The State shall establish a defense transportation material reserve system to ensure the needs of smooth defense transportation in wartime and under special circumstances.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国防交通物资储备分为国家储备、部门储备和地方储备，分别列入县级以上各级人民政府和有关部门的物资储备计划。

**Article 42** Defense transportation material reserves shall be divided into state reserves, departmental reserves, and local reserves, and shall be separately included in the material reserve plans of people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　负责储备国防交通物资的单位，必须对所储备的物资加强维护和管理，不得损坏、丢失。

**Article 43** The units responsible for reserving defense transportation materials must strengthen the maintenance and management of the reserved materials, and shall not damage or lose them.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　未经国防交通主管机构批准，任何单位或者个人不得动用储备的国防交通物资。  
经批准使用储备的国防交通物资，应当按照规定支付费用。

**Article 44** No unit or individual may use the reserved defense transportation materials without the approval of the competent defense transportation authority.  
If the use of reserved defense transportation materials is approved, fees shall be paid in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　由地方人民政府或者交通管理部门管理的用作战费、支前费、军费购置的交通保障物资，应当列入国防交通物资储备。

**Article 45** Transportation support materials purchased with war expenses, front support expenses, or military funds and managed by local people's governments or transportation administrative departments shall be included in the defense transportation material reserve.

### 第九章　教育与科研

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　交通管理部门和交通企业事业单位，应当对本系统、本单位的人员进行国防交通教育。  
交通运输院校和邮电通信院校，应当在相关课程中设置国防交通的内容。

**Article 46** Transportation administrative departments and transportation enterprises and institutions shall provide defense transportation education to personnel of their own systems and units.  
Transportation and communications colleges and posts and telecommunications colleges shall include defense transportation content in relevant courses.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　交通管理部门和有关的科研机构，应当加强国防交通科学技术研究。国防交通科学技术研究项目，应当纳入各级科学技术研究规划。

**Article 47** Transportation administrative departments and relevant scientific research institutions shall strengthen scientific and technological research on defense transportation. Defense transportation science and technology research projects shall be included in the scientific and technological research plans at all levels.

### 第十章　罚则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例有关规定，有下列行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)应当贯彻国防要求的交通工程设施，在施工过程中没有贯彻国防要求的；  
(二)对国防交通工程设施管理不善，造成损失的，或者擅自改变国防交通工程设施的用途或者擅自作报废处理的；  
(三)对储备的国防交通物资管理不善，造成损失的；  
(四)未经批准动用储备的国防交通物资的。

**Article 48** Whoever violates the relevant provisions of these Regulations and commits one of the following acts shall, if the act constitutes a crime, be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to implement the defense requirements for transportation engineering facilities that should implement defense requirements during the construction process;  
(2) Causing losses due to poor management of defense transportation engineering facilities, or unauthorized changes to the use of defense transportation engineering facilities or unauthorized disposal as scrapped;  
(3) Causing losses due to poor management of reserved defense transportation materials;  
(4) Using reserved defense transportation materials without approval.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　危及国防交通工程设施安全或者侵占国防交通控制用地的，由国防交通主管机构责令停止违法行为，给予警告，可以并处5万元以下的罚款；造成经济损失的，应当依法赔偿。

**Article 49** Whoever endangers the safety of defense transportation engineering facilities or encroaches on land for defense transportation control shall be ordered by the competent defense transportation authority to stop the illegal act, given a warning, and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if economic losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　逃避或者抗拒运力动员或者运力征用的，由国防交通主管机构给予警告，可以并处相当于被动员或者被征用的运载工具、设备价值2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Whoever evades or resists the mobilization or requisition of transport capacity shall be given a warning by the competent defense transportation authority, and may also be fined not more than twice the value of the mobilized or requisitioned vehicles or equipment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　有下列行为之一的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的有关规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)扰乱、妨碍军事运输和国防交通保障的；  
(二)扰乱、妨碍国防交通工程设施建设的；  
(三)破坏国防交通工程设施的；  
(四)盗窃、哄抢国防交通物资的。

**Article 51** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) disrupting or obstructing military transportation and national defense traffic support;  
(2) disrupting or obstructing the construction of national defense traffic engineering facilities;  
(3) destroying national defense traffic engineering facilities;  
(4) stealing or seizing by force national defense traffic materials.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国防交通主管机构的工作人员，滥用职权、玩忽职守的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Any staff member of a competent authority for national defense traffic who abuses his/her power or neglects his/her duty shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)特殊情况，是指局部战争、武装冲突和其他突发事件；  
(二)交通管理部门，是指主管铁路、道路、水路、航空和邮电通信的行业管理部门。

**Article 53** For the purpose of these Regulations, the meanings of the following terms are:  
(1) "special circumstances" means local wars, armed conflicts and other emergencies;  
(2) "traffic regulatory departments" means the competent industry departments in charge of railways, highways, waterways, aviation, and postal and telecommunications services.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国防交通经费由中央、地方、部门、企业共同承担。具体办法由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 54** The funds for national defense traffic shall be jointly borne by the central government, local governments, departments and enterprises. The specific measures shall be formulated by the national competent authority for national defense traffic jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
