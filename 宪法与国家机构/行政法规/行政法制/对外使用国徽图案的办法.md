# 对外使用国徽图案的办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2025-11-09  
**生效日期**：2025-11-09

> （1993年8月31日国务院批准　1993年9月24日外交部发布　2025年11月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第821号修订） 第一条　为了在对外活动中正确使用中华人民共和国国徽图案，根据《中华人民共和国国徽法》（以下简称国徽法），制定本办法。 第二条　外事活动和国家驻外使馆、领馆以及其他外交代表机构对外使用国徽图案，适用本办法。 第三条　为全国人民代表大会常务委员会、国务院、中央军事委员会、国家监察委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院印制的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，应当印有国徽图案。 为国徽法第八条第二项规定人员印制其使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，应当印有国徽图案。 第四条　外交部以及国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，应当印有国徽图案。 外交部部长、国家和政府的特使，以及国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构的馆长使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，应当印有国徽图案。 外交部副部长、部长助理使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。 第五条　国防部使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，国防部部长使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，以及以国防部名义对外举行的建军节招待会请柬等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。 第六条　中央人民政府驻香港特别行政区有关机构、中央人民政府驻澳门特别行政区有关机构使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，以及上述机构的正职负责人使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。 第七条　以省、自治区、直辖市人民政府名义对外举行的国庆招待会请柬等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。 第八条　下列机构对外使用的印章可以刻有国徽图案： （一）全国人民代表大会常务委员会、国家监察委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院负责外事工作的内设机构； （二）外交部有关内设机构、外交部驻香港特别行政区特派员公署、外交部驻澳门特别行政区特派员公署； （三）国务院各有关部委负责外事工作的内设机构；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了在对外活动中正确使用中华人民共和国国徽图案，根据《中华人民共和国国徽法》（以下简称国徽法），制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of correctly using the national emblem pattern of the People's Republic of China in external activities, in accordance with the National Emblem Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the National Emblem Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外事活动和国家驻外使馆、领馆以及其他外交代表机构对外使用国徽图案，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the use of the national emblem pattern in foreign affairs activities and by the embassies, consulates, and other diplomatic representative offices of the state abroad.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　为全国人民代表大会常务委员会、国务院、中央军事委员会、国家监察委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院印制的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，应当印有国徽图案。  
为[国徽法第八条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国国徽法.md#art-8)第二项规定人员印制其使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，应当印有国徽图案。

**Article 3** Diplomatic documents printed for the National People's Congress Standing Committee, the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the National Commission of Supervision, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and envelopes and letterheads used for external purposes, shall bear the national emblem pattern.  
Diplomatic documents printed for the personnel specified in Article 8, item 2 of the National Emblem Law for their use, and envelopes, letterheads, invitation cards, greeting cards, gift cards, etc., used in an official capacity for external purposes, shall bear the national emblem pattern.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外交部以及国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，应当印有国徽图案。  
外交部部长、国家和政府的特使，以及国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构的馆长使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，应当印有国徽图案。  
外交部副部长、部长助理使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。

**Article 4** Diplomatic documents used by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the embassies, consulates, and other diplomatic representative offices of the state abroad, and envelopes and letterheads used for external purposes, shall bear the national emblem pattern.  
Diplomatic documents used by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, special envoys of the state and government, and the heads of embassies, consulates, and other diplomatic representative offices of the state abroad, and envelopes, letterheads, invitation cards, greeting cards, gift cards, etc., used in an official capacity for external purposes, shall bear the national emblem pattern.  
Diplomatic documents used by the Vice Ministers of Foreign Affairs and Assistant Ministers of Foreign Affairs, and envelopes and letterheads used in an official capacity for external purposes, may bear the national emblem pattern as deemed necessary.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国防部使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，国防部部长使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，以及以国防部名义对外举行的建军节招待会请柬等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。

**Article 5** Diplomatic documents used by the Ministry of National Defense and envelopes and letterheads used for external purposes, diplomatic documents used by the Minister of National Defense and envelopes, letterheads, invitation cards, greeting cards, gift cards, etc., used in an official capacity for external purposes, as well as invitation cards for the Army Day reception held externally in the name of the Ministry of National Defense, may bear the national emblem pattern as deemed necessary.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中央人民政府驻香港特别行政区有关机构、中央人民政府驻澳门特别行政区有关机构使用的外交文书和对外使用的信封、信笺，以及上述机构的正职负责人使用的外交文书和以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬、贺卡、赠礼卡等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。

**Article 6** Diplomatic documents used by the relevant agencies of the Central People's Government in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the relevant agencies of the Central People's Government in the Macao Special Administrative Region, and envelopes and letterheads used for external purposes, as well as diplomatic documents used by the principal heads of the said agencies and envelopes, letterheads, invitation cards, greeting cards, gift cards, etc., used in an official capacity for external purposes, may bear the national emblem pattern as deemed necessary.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　以省、自治区、直辖市人民政府名义对外举行的国庆招待会请柬等，根据需要可以印有国徽图案。

**Article 7** Invitation cards for the National Day reception held externally in the name of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, may bear the national emblem pattern as deemed necessary.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下列机构对外使用的印章可以刻有国徽图案：  
（一）全国人民代表大会常务委员会、国家监察委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院负责外事工作的内设机构；  
（二）外交部有关内设机构、外交部驻香港特别行政区特派员公署、外交部驻澳门特别行政区特派员公署；  
（三）国务院各有关部委负责外事工作的内设机构；  
（四）各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的外事办公室；  
（五）计划单列市、经济特区和沿海开放城市人民政府的外事办公室；  
（六）国家办理护照、签证等出入境证件的机关。

**Article 8** The seals used for external purposes by the following institutions may be engraved with the national emblem pattern:  
(1) the internal institutions responsible for foreign affairs of the National People's Congress Standing Committee, the National Commission of Supervision, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(2) the relevant internal institutions of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Office of the Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, and the Office of the Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(3) the internal institutions responsible for foreign affairs of the relevant ministries and commissions of the State Council;  
(4) the foreign affairs offices of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) the foreign affairs offices of the people's governments of cities with independent planning status, special economic zones, and open coastal cities; and  
(6) the state authorities handling passports, visas, and other entry and exit documents.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列证件、文书等应当印有国徽图案或者印有带国徽图案的印章：  
（一）中华人民共和国护照和其他具有护照性质的证件；  
（二）中华人民共和国签证、停留证件、居留证件、永久居留身份证件；  
（三）加入、退出和恢复中华人民共和国国籍的证书；  
（四）中华人民共和国外交信使、领事信使的有关证件和外交邮袋、领事邮袋封印；  
（五）外交部及其委托的地方人民政府外事办公室，以及外交部驻香港特别行政区特派员公署、外交部驻澳门特别行政区特派员公署出具的领事认证书、附加证明书等；  
（六）国家驻外使馆、领馆出具的公证书、领事认证书、婚姻登记证、临时船舶国籍证书等；  
（七）外交部为外国驻华外交领事机构、政府间国际组织在华设立的总部或者代表机构的人员和外国驻华新闻机构颁发的有关证件；  
（八）各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府外事办公室为外国驻华领馆的人员和常驻当地的外国新闻机构颁发的有关证件。

**Article 9** The following documents, certificates, etc., shall bear the national emblem pattern or a seal with the national emblem pattern:  
(1) passports of the People's Republic of China and other documents with passport nature;  
(2) visas, stay documents, residence documents, and permanent residence identity documents of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) certificates of acquisition, renunciation, and restoration of the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) relevant documents of diplomatic couriers and consular couriers of the People's Republic of China, and seals of diplomatic bags and consular bags;  
(5) consular authentication certificates, apostilles, etc., issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and its authorized local foreign affairs offices of the people's governments, as well as the Office of the Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Office of the Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(6) notarial certificates, consular authentication certificates, marriage registration certificates, provisional ship nationality certificates, etc., issued by embassies and consulates of the state abroad;  
(7) relevant documents issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to personnel of foreign diplomatic consular missions in China, headquarters or representative offices of intergovernmental international organizations established in China, and foreign news agencies in China; and  
(8) relevant documents issued by the foreign affairs offices of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government to personnel of foreign consulates in China and foreign news agencies permanently stationed locally.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　以中华人民共和国、中华人民共和国政府或者中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结的条约、协定，可以加封带有国徽图案的火漆印；上述条约、协定的批准书、核准书、接受书、加入书以及条约夹的封面，应当印有国徽图案。

**Article 10** Treaties and agreements concluded in the name of the People's Republic of China, the government of the People's Republic of China, or a department of the government of the People's Republic of China may be sealed with a wax seal bearing the national emblem pattern; the instruments of ratification, approval, acceptance, accession, and the covers of treaty folders for the said treaties and agreements shall bear the national emblem pattern.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家体育、技能竞赛代表团、队参加国际体育比赛、技能竞赛时，可以按照有关规定在其人员的服装上使用国徽图案。

**Article 11** When national sports and skill competition delegations and teams participate in international sports competitions and skill competitions, they may use the national emblem pattern on the clothing of their personnel in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　标示国界线的界桩、界碑和标示领海基点方位的标志碑以及其他用于显示国家主权的标志物可以使用国徽图案。

**Article 12** The boundary markers and boundary tablets indicating the national boundary line, the marker tablets indicating the orientation of the base points of the territorial sea, and other markers used to demonstrate national sovereignty may use the national emblem pattern.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外交部具体负责指导对外使用国徽图案有关工作。

**Article 13** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall specifically be responsible for guiding the work related to the use of the national emblem pattern in foreign affairs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本办法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 14** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
