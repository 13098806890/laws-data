# 现役军人和人民武装警察居民身份证申领发放办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2007-10-21  
**生效日期**：2008-01-01

> (2007年10月21日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第510号公布　自2008年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证的申领和发放，依照《中华人民共和国居民身份证法》的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证的申领和发放，适用本办法。 第三条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证登记项目包括：姓名、性别、民族、出生日期、长期固定住址、公民身份号码、本人相片、证件的有效期和签发机关。 第四条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证登记项目内容，以本人档案记载内容和公安机关编制的公民身份号码为基础，由本人所在团级以上单位统一组织文字信息和人像信息的采集、录入、审核。 第五条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，应当填写《现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领登记表》，由本人所在团级以上单位向本人长期固定住址所在地的县级人民政府公安机关代为申请。 第六条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证由本人长期固定住址所在地的县级人民政府公安机关签发。 第七条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证的，公安机关应当依照《中华人民共和国居民身份证法》第十二条第一款的规定办理。 第八条　现役军人、人民武装警察在服役前已经领取居民身份证，服役期间居民身份证仍在有效期内的，不再换领新证；但是，应当向其所在团级以上单位登记备案。 第九条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取临时居民身份证的，由本人所在团级以上单位代为申请；公安机关应当依照《中华人民共和国临时居民身份证管理办法》的规定办理。 第十条　军队、武装警察部队团级以上单位代现役军人、人民武装警察领取居民身份证、临时居民身份证后，应当及时发给本人。 第十一条　现役军人、人民武装警察从事有关社会活动，需要证明公民身份的，凭居民身份证证明；执行任务、办理公务、享受抚恤优待等，需要证明现役军人或者人民武装警察身份的，凭军队、武装警察部队制发的身份证件证明。 第十二条　军队、武装警察部队团级以上单位应当建立健全所属人员居民身份证的申领登记、备案等制度；在所属人员的本人档案中，应当载明其公民身份号码。 第十三条　公安机关、军队、武装警察部队及其有关人员，在现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领和发放工作中，对所涉及的国家秘密和知悉的个人信息，应当予以保密。 第十四条　现役军人、人民武装警察有下列行为之一的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证的申领和发放，依照《中华人民共和国居民身份证法》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Resident Identity Card Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of regulating the application and issuance of resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证的申领和发放，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the application and issuance of resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证登记项目包括：姓名、性别、民族、出生日期、长期固定住址、公民身份号码、本人相片、证件的有效期和签发机关。

**Article 3** The registered items of resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police shall include: name, gender, ethnicity, date of birth, permanent fixed address, citizen identity number, personal photograph, validity period of the card, and issuing authority.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证登记项目内容，以本人档案记载内容和公安机关编制的公民身份号码为基础，由本人所在团级以上单位统一组织文字信息和人像信息的采集、录入、审核。

**Article 4** The content of the registered items of resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police shall be based on the recorded content in their personal files and the citizen identity numbers compiled by the public security organ, and the unit at or above the regimental level to which they belong shall uniformly organize the collection, entry, and verification of textual information and portrait information.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，应当填写《现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领登记表》，由本人所在团级以上单位向本人长期固定住址所在地的县级人民政府公安机关代为申请。

**Article 5** Active-duty servicemen and armed police who apply for, renew, or replace a resident identity card shall fill in the Application Registration Form for Resident Identity Cards for Active-Duty Servicemen and Armed Police, and the unit at or above the regimental level to which they belong shall apply on their behalf to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place of their permanent fixed address.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证由本人长期固定住址所在地的县级人民政府公安机关签发。

**Article 6** The resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police shall be issued by the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place of their permanent fixed address.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证的，公安机关应当依照[《中华人民共和国居民身份证法》第十二条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国居民身份证法.md#art-12)第一款的规定办理。

**Article 7** Where active-duty servicemen or armed police apply for, renew, or replace a resident identity card, the public security organ shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 12, paragraph 1 of the Resident Identity Card Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　现役军人、人民武装警察在服役前已经领取居民身份证，服役期间居民身份证仍在有效期内的，不再换领新证；但是，应当向其所在团级以上单位登记备案。

**Article 8** If an active-duty serviceman or armed police has already obtained a resident identity card before active service and the card is still within its validity period during the service period, they shall not renew the card; however, they shall register and file a record with the unit at or above the regimental level to which they belong.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　现役军人、人民武装警察申请领取临时居民身份证的，由本人所在团级以上单位代为申请；公安机关应当依照《中华人民共和国临时居民身份证管理办法》的规定办理。

**Article 9** Where active-duty servicemen or armed police apply for a temporary resident identity card, the unit at or above the regimental level to which they belong shall apply on their behalf; the public security organ shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Administration of Temporary Resident Identity Cards of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军队、武装警察部队团级以上单位代现役军人、人民武装警察领取居民身份证、临时居民身份证后，应当及时发给本人。

**Article 10** After the military or armed police unit at or above the regimental level receives the resident identity card or temporary resident identity card on behalf of the active-duty serviceman or armed police, it shall promptly issue it to the person concerned.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　现役军人、人民武装警察从事有关社会活动，需要证明公民身份的，凭居民身份证证明；执行任务、办理公务、享受抚恤优待等，需要证明现役军人或者人民武装警察身份的，凭军队、武装警察部队制发的身份证件证明。

**Article 11** Where active-duty servicemen or armed police engage in relevant social activities and need to certify their citizen identity, they shall certify it by means of their resident identity card; where they need to certify their identity as active-duty servicemen or armed police for performing tasks, handling official business, enjoying pensions and preferential treatment, etc., they shall certify it by means of the identity documents issued by the military or armed police.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军队、武装警察部队团级以上单位应当建立健全所属人员居民身份证的申领登记、备案等制度；在所属人员的本人档案中，应当载明其公民身份号码。

**Article 12** The military or armed police unit at or above the regimental level shall establish and improve the systems for application registration, record-filing, etc. of resident identity cards of its personnel; in the personal files of its personnel, their citizen identity numbers shall be recorded.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公安机关、军队、武装警察部队及其有关人员，在现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领和发放工作中，对所涉及的国家秘密和知悉的个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 13** The public security organs, the military, the armed police, and their relevant personnel shall keep confidential the state secrets involved and the personal information known to them in the work of applying for and issuing resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　现役军人、人民武装警察有下列行为之一的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分：  
(一)使用虚假证明材料骗领居民身份证的；  
(二)出租、出借、转让居民身份证的；  
(三)非法扣押他人居民身份证的；  
(四)冒用他人居民身份证或者使用骗领的居民身份证的；  
(五)购买、出售、使用伪造、变造的居民身份证的。  
对伪造、变造的居民身份证和骗领的居民身份证予以收缴。

**Article 14** An active-duty serviceman or armed police who commits any of the following acts shall be given a sanction in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army:  
(1) Using false supporting materials to fraudulently obtain a resident identity card;  
(2) Renting, lending, or transferring a resident identity card;  
(3) Unlawfully detaining another person's resident identity card;  
(4) Using another person's resident identity card or using a fraudulently obtained resident identity card;  
(5) Purchasing, selling, or using forged or altered resident identity cards.  
Forged or altered resident identity cards and fraudulently obtained resident identity cards shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　现役军人、人民武装警察伪造、变造居民身份证的，依法追究刑事责任。  
现役军人、人民武装警察有[本办法第十四条](#art-14)所列行为之一，从事犯罪活动的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 15** Where an active-duty serviceman or armed police forges or alters a resident identity card, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where an active-duty serviceman or armed police commits any of the acts listed in Article 14 of these Measures and engages in criminal activities, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军队、武装警察部队有关人员在现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领和发放工作中有下列行为之一的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对居民身份证登记项目审查不严，致使登记信息失实造成严重后果的；  
(二)利用办理居民身份证工作的便利，收受财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
(三)故意提供居民身份证登记项目虚假信息的；  
(四)泄露国家秘密或者个人信息的。  
公安机关人民警察在现役军人、人民武装警察居民身份证申领和发放工作中有前款第(四)项行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 16** Where relevant personnel of the military or armed police commit any of the following acts in the work of applying for and issuing resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police, they shall be given a sanction in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to strictly examine the registered items of resident identity cards, resulting in false registration information causing serious consequences;  
(2) Taking advantage of the convenience of handling resident identity card work to accept property or seek other benefits;  
(3) Intentionally providing false information on the registered items of resident identity cards;  
(4) Disclosing state secrets or personal information.  
Where a people's police officer of a public security organ commits the act under subparagraph (4) of the preceding paragraph in the work of applying for and issuing resident identity cards for active-duty servicemen and armed police, a sanction shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军队、武装警察部队管理的离休退休干部和待移交人民政府安置的离休退休干部、退休士官居民身份证的申领和发放，依照本办法执行。

**Article 17** The application and issuance of resident identity cards for retired cadres managed by the military or armed police, and retired cadres and retired non-commissioned officers awaiting transfer to the people's government for placement, shall be handled in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法自2008年1月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Measures shall come into force as of January 1, 2008.
