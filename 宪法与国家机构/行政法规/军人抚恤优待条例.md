# 军人抚恤优待条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-08-05  
**生效日期**：2024-10-01

> （2004年8月1日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第413号公布　根据2011年7月29日《国务院、中央军事委员会关于修改〈军人抚恤优待条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　2024年8月5日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第788号第三次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障国家对军人的抚恤优待，激励军人保卫祖国、建设祖国的献身精神，加强国防和军队现代化建设，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国兵役法》、《中华人民共和国军人地位和权益保障法》、《中华人民共和国退役军人保障法》等有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称抚恤优待对象包括： （一）军人； （二）服现役和退出现役的残疾军人； （三）烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属； （四）军人家属； （五）退役军人。 第三条　军人抚恤优待工作坚持中国共产党的领导。 军人抚恤优待工作应当践行社会主义核心价值观，贯彻待遇与贡献匹配、精神与物质并重、关爱与服务结合的原则，分类保障，突出重点，逐步推进抚恤优待制度城乡统筹，健全抚恤优待标准动态调整机制，确保抚恤优待保障水平与经济社会发展水平、国防和军队建设需要相适应。 第四条　国家保障抚恤优待对象享受社会保障和基本公共服务等公民普惠待遇，同时享受相应的抚恤优待待遇。 在审核抚恤优待对象是否符合享受相应社会保障和基本公共服务等条件时，抚恤金、补助金和优待金不计入抚恤优待对象个人和家庭收入。 第五条　国务院退役军人工作主管部门负责全国的军人抚恤优待工作；县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责本行政区域内的军人抚恤优待工作。 中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会有关部门、地方各级有关机关应当在各自职责范围内做好军人抚恤优待工作。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障国家对军人的抚恤优待，激励军人保卫祖国、建设祖国的献身精神，加强国防和军队现代化建设，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国兵役法》、《中华人民共和国军人地位和权益保障法》、《中华人民共和国退役军人保障法》等有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China, the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of the Status and Rights and Interests of Military Personnel, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Demobilized Military Personnel, and other relevant laws, for the purposes of ensuring the state's pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel, encouraging the spirit of dedication of military personnel in defending and building the motherland, strengthening the modernization of national defense and the armed forces, and making military personnel a profession respected by the whole society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称抚恤优待对象包括：  
（一）军人；  
（二）服现役和退出现役的残疾军人；  
（三）烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属；  
（四）军人家属；  
（五）退役军人。

**Article 2** The term "persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment" as used in these Regulations includes:  
(1) military personnel;  
(2) disabled military personnel on active service and those who have been discharged from active service;  
(3) survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness;  
(4) family members of military personnel;  
(5) demobilized military personnel.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军人抚恤优待工作坚持中国共产党的领导。  
军人抚恤优待工作应当践行社会主义核心价值观，贯彻待遇与贡献匹配、精神与物质并重、关爱与服务结合的原则，分类保障，突出重点，逐步推进抚恤优待制度城乡统筹，健全抚恤优待标准动态调整机制，确保抚恤优待保障水平与经济社会发展水平、国防和军队建设需要相适应。

**Article 3** The work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.  
The work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel shall practice the core socialist values, implement the principles of matching benefits with contributions, attaching equal importance to spiritual and material aspects, and combining care with service, provide categorized protection, highlight priorities, gradually promote the urban-rural coordination of the pension and preferential treatment system, improve the dynamic adjustment mechanism for pension and preferential treatment standards, and ensure that the level of pension and preferential treatment protection is commensurate with the level of economic and social development and the needs of national defense and armed forces modernization.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家保障抚恤优待对象享受社会保障和基本公共服务等公民普惠待遇，同时享受相应的抚恤优待待遇。  
在审核抚恤优待对象是否符合享受相应社会保障和基本公共服务等条件时，抚恤金、补助金和优待金不计入抚恤优待对象个人和家庭收入。

**Article 4** The state guarantees that persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment enjoy universal benefits for citizens such as social security and basic public services, and at the same time enjoy corresponding pension and preferential treatment benefits.  
When examining whether a person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment meets the conditions for enjoying corresponding social security and basic public services, pensions, subsidies, and preferential allowances shall not be counted as the personal or family income of the person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院退役军人工作主管部门负责全国的军人抚恤优待工作；县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责本行政区域内的军人抚恤优待工作。  
中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会有关部门、地方各级有关机关应当在各自职责范围内做好军人抚恤优待工作。

**Article 5** The competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel nationwide; the competent departments for demobilized military personnel affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant central and state organs, the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission, and the relevant organs at all levels in the localities shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　按照中央与地方财政事权和支出责任划分原则，军人抚恤优待所需经费主要由中央财政负担，适度加大省级财政投入力度，减轻基层财政压力。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当对军人抚恤优待工作经费予以保障。  
中央和地方财政安排的军人抚恤优待所需经费和工作经费，实施全过程预算绩效管理，并接受财政、审计部门的监督。

**Article 6** In accordance with the principle of division of fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities between the central and local governments, the funds required for pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel shall be mainly borne by the central finance, with appropriately increased investment from provincial-level finance to alleviate the financial pressure at the grassroots level.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure the funding for the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel.  
The funds required for pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel and the work funds arranged by the central and local finances shall be subject to full-process budget performance management and shall be subject to the supervision of the financial and audit departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和引导群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织、个人等社会力量依法通过捐赠、设立基金、志愿服务等方式为军人抚恤优待工作提供支持和帮助。  
全社会应当关怀、尊重抚恤优待对象，开展各种形式的拥军优属活动，营造爱国拥军、尊崇军人浓厚氛围。

**Article 7** The state encourages and guides social forces such as mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to provide support and assistance for the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel through donations, establishment of funds, voluntary services, and other means according to law.  
The whole society shall care for and respect persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, carry out various forms of activities to support the military and give preferential treatment to military families, and create a strong atmosphere of patriotism and respect for the military.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家推进军人抚恤优待工作信息化，加强抚恤优待对象综合信息平台建设，加强部门协同配合、信息共享，实现对抚恤优待对象的精准识别，提升军人抚恤优待工作服务能力和水平。  
国家建立享受定期抚恤补助对象年度确认制度和冒领待遇追责机制，确保抚恤优待资金准确发放。

**Article 8** The state shall promote the informatization of the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel, strengthen the construction of a comprehensive information platform for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, enhance inter-departmental coordination and information sharing, achieve accurate identification of persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, and improve the service capability and level of the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel.  
The state shall establish an annual confirmation system for recipients of regular pensions and subsidies and a mechanism for holding accountable those who fraudulently claim benefits, to ensure the accurate disbursement of pension and preferential treatment funds.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在军人抚恤优待工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work on pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel shall be given commendations and rewards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第二章　军人死亡抚恤

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　烈士遗属享受烈士褒扬金、一次性抚恤金，并可以按照规定享受定期抚恤金、丧葬补助、一次性特别抚恤金等。  
因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属享受一次性抚恤金，并可以按照规定享受定期抚恤金、丧葬补助、一次性特别抚恤金等。

**Article 10** Survivors of martyrs shall enjoy a martyr commendation award and a lump-sum pension, and may enjoy a regular pension, funeral subsidy, one-time special pension, etc. in accordance with the provisions.  
Survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty and survivors of military personnel who died of illness shall enjoy a lump-sum pension, and may enjoy a regular pension, funeral subsidy, one-time special pension, etc. in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军人牺牲，符合下列情形之一的，评定为烈士：  
（一）对敌作战牺牲，或者对敌作战负伤在医疗终结前因伤牺牲的；  
（二）因执行任务遭敌人或者犯罪分子杀害，或者被俘、被捕后不屈遭敌人杀害或者被折磨牺牲的；  
（三）为抢救和保护国家财产、集体财产、公民生命财产或者执行反恐怖任务和处置突发事件牺牲的；  
（四）因执行军事演习、战备航行飞行、空降和导弹发射训练、试航试飞任务以及参加武器装备科研试验牺牲的；  
（五）在执行外交任务或者国家派遣的对外援助、维持国际和平任务中牺牲的；  
（六）其他牺牲情节特别突出，堪为楷模的。  
军人在执行对敌作战、维持国际和平、边海防执勤或者抢险救灾等任务中失踪，被宣告死亡的，按照烈士对待。  
评定烈士，属于因战牺牲的，由军队团级以上单位政治工作部门批准；属于非因战牺牲的，由军队军级以上单位政治工作部门批准；属于本条第一款第六项规定情形的，由中央军事委员会政治工作部批准。

**Article 11** Military personnel who die under any of the following circumstances shall be assessed as martyrs:  
(1) dying in combat against the enemy, or dying of injuries sustained in combat against the enemy before the completion of medical treatment;  
(2) being killed by the enemy or criminals while performing duties, or being killed by the enemy or dying under torture after being captured or arrested without yielding;  
(3) dying while rescuing and protecting state property, collective property, citizens' lives and property, or performing counter-terrorism tasks and dealing with emergencies;  
(4) dying while performing military exercises, combat readiness navigation and flight, airborne and missile launch training, trial navigation and test flight missions, and participating in scientific research and testing of weapons and equipment;  
(5) dying while performing diplomatic missions or missions of foreign assistance or maintaining international peace dispatched by the state;  
(6) other circumstances where the sacrifice is particularly outstanding and worthy of being a model.  
Military personnel who go missing while performing tasks such as combat against the enemy, maintaining international peace, border and coastal defense duty, or emergency rescue and disaster relief, and are declared dead, shall be treated as martyrs.  
For the assessment of martyrs, if the death is due to combat, it shall be approved by the political work department at or above the regimental level of the armed forces; if the death is not due to combat, it shall be approved by the political work department at or above the corps level of the armed forces; if the circumstances fall under item (6) of paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall be approved by the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军人死亡，符合下列情形之一的，确认为因公牺牲：  
（一）在执行任务中、工作岗位上或者在上下班途中，由于意外事件死亡的；  
（二）被认定为因战、因公致残后因旧伤复发死亡的；  
（三）因患职业病死亡的；  
（四）在执行任务中或者在工作岗位上因病猝然死亡的；  
（五）其他因公死亡的。  
军人在执行对敌作战、维持国际和平、边海防执勤或者抢险救灾以外的其他任务中失踪，被宣告死亡的，按照因公牺牲对待。  
军人因公牺牲，由军队团级以上单位政治工作部门确认；属于本条第一款第五项规定情形的，由军队军级以上单位政治工作部门确认。

**Article 12** Military personnel who die under any of the following circumstances shall be confirmed as having died in the line of duty:  
(1) dying due to an accidental event while performing a task, at the workplace, or on the way to or from work;  
(2) dying from the recurrence of old injuries after being recognized as disabled due to combat or duty;  
(3) dying due to an occupational disease;  
(4) dying suddenly from illness while performing a task or at the workplace;  
(5) other deaths in the line of duty.  
Military personnel who go missing while performing tasks other than combat against the enemy, maintaining international peace, border and coastal defense duty, or emergency rescue and disaster relief, and are declared dead, shall be treated as having died in the line of duty.  
The death of military personnel in the line of duty shall be confirmed by the political work department at or above the regimental level of the armed forces; if the circumstances fall under item (5) of paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall be confirmed by the political work department at or above the corps level of the armed forces.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军人除[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第一款第三项、第四项规定情形以外，因其他疾病死亡的，确认为病故。  
军人非执行任务死亡，或者失踪被宣告死亡的，按照病故对待。  
军人病故，由军队团级以上单位政治工作部门确认。

**Article 13** Military personnel who die from diseases other than the circumstances specified in items (3) and (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Regulations shall be confirmed as having died of illness.  
Military personnel who die not while performing a task, or who go missing and are declared dead, shall be treated as having died of illness.  
The death of military personnel from illness shall be confirmed by the political work department at or above the regimental level of the armed forces.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军人牺牲被评定为烈士、确认为因公牺牲或者病故后，由军队有关部门或者单位向烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属户籍所在地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门发送《烈士评定通知书》、《军人因公牺牲通知书》、《军人病故通知书》和《军人因公牺牲证明书》、《军人病故证明书》。烈士证书的颁发按照《烈士褒扬条例》的规定执行，《军人因公牺牲证明书》、《军人病故证明书》由本条规定的县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门发给因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属。  
遗属均为军人且无户籍的，军人单位所在地作为遗属户籍地。

**Article 14** After a military person's death is assessed as a martyr, confirmed as having died in the line of duty, or confirmed as having died of illness, the relevant military department or unit shall send a "Notice of Assessment as Martyr", a "Notice of Death in the Line of Duty of Military Personnel", a "Notice of Death from Illness of Military Personnel", a "Certificate of Death in the Line of Duty of Military Personnel", and a "Certificate of Death from Illness of Military Personnel" to the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place of domicile of the survivors of the martyr, the survivors of the military personnel who died in the line of duty, or the survivors of the military personnel who died of illness. The issuance of martyr certificates shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Commending Martyrs; the "Certificate of Death in the Line of Duty of Military Personnel" and the "Certificate of Death from Illness of Military Personnel" shall be issued by the county-level people's government's competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs as provided in this Article to the survivors of the military personnel who died in the line of duty or died of illness.  
If all survivors are military personnel and have no domicile, the place of the military person's unit shall be regarded as the place of domicile of the survivors.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　烈士褒扬金由领取烈士证书的烈士遗属户籍所在地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门，按照烈士牺牲时上一年度全国城镇居民人均可支配收入30倍的标准发给其遗属。战时，参战牺牲的烈士褒扬金标准可以适当提高。  
军人死亡，根据其死亡性质和死亡时的月基本工资标准，由收到《烈士评定通知书》、《军人因公牺牲通知书》、《军人病故通知书》的县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门，按照以下标准发给其遗属一次性抚恤金：烈士和因公牺牲的，为上一年度全国城镇居民人均可支配收入的20倍加本人40个月的基本工资；病故的，为上一年度全国城镇居民人均可支配收入的2倍加本人40个月的基本工资。月基本工资或者津贴低于少尉军官基本工资标准的，按照少尉军官基本工资标准计算。被追授军衔的，按照所追授的军衔等级以及相应待遇级别确定月基本工资标准。

**Article 15** The martyr commendation award shall be paid by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place of domicile of the survivors who receive the martyr certificate, at a standard of 30 times the per capita disposable income of urban residents nationwide in the year preceding the martyr's death. In wartime, the standard of the martyr commendation award for those who died in combat may be appropriately increased.  
For the death of a military person, based on the nature of death and the monthly basic salary standard at the time of death, the county-level people's government's competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs that receives the "Notice of Assessment as Martyr", "Notice of Death in the Line of Duty of Military Personnel", or "Notice of Death from Illness of Military Personnel" shall pay a lump-sum pension to the survivors according to the following standards: for martyrs and those who died in the line of duty, 20 times the per capita disposable income of urban residents nationwide in the preceding year plus 40 months of the person's basic salary; for those who died of illness, 2 times the per capita disposable income of urban residents nationwide in the preceding year plus 40 months of the person's basic salary. If the monthly basic salary or allowance is lower than the basic salary standard of a junior lieutenant, the calculation shall be based on the basic salary standard of a junior lieutenant. If a posthumous promotion in rank is granted, the monthly basic salary standard shall be determined according to the posthumously awarded rank and the corresponding treatment level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　服现役期间获得功勋荣誉表彰的军人被评定为烈士、确认为因公牺牲或者病故的，其遗属在应当享受的一次性抚恤金的基础上，由县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照下列比例增发一次性抚恤金：  
（一）获得勋章或者国家荣誉称号的，增发40%；  
（二）获得党中央、国务院、中央军事委员会单独或者联合授予荣誉称号的，增发35%；  
（三）立一等战功、获得一级表彰或者获得中央军事委员会授权的单位授予荣誉称号的，增发30%；  
（四）立二等战功、一等功或者获得二级表彰并经批准的，增发25%；  
（五）立三等战功或者二等功的，增发15%；  
（六）立四等战功或者三等功的，增发5%。  
军人死亡后被追授功勋荣誉表彰的，比照前款规定增发一次性抚恤金。  
服现役期间多次获得功勋荣誉表彰的烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人，其遗属由县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照其中最高的增发比例，增发一次性抚恤金。

**Article 16** If a military person who has received meritorious service honors and commendations during active service is assessed as a martyr, confirmed as having died in the line of duty, or confirmed as having died of illness, the survivors shall, in addition to the lump-sum pension to which they are entitled, receive an additional lump-sum pension paid by the county-level people's government's competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs at the following percentages:  
(1) for those who have received a medal or a national honorary title, an additional 40%;  
(2) for those who have received an honorary title awarded individually or jointly by the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, the State Council, or the Central Military Commission, an additional 35%;  
(3) for those who have rendered first-class meritorious service in combat, received first-level commendation, or received an honorary title from a unit authorized by the Central Military Commission, an additional 30%;  
(4) for those who have rendered second-class meritorious service in combat, first-class meritorious service, or received second-level commendation with approval, an additional 25%;  
(5) for those who have rendered third-class meritorious service in combat or second-class meritorious service, an additional 15%;  
(6) for those who have rendered fourth-class meritorious service in combat or third-class meritorious service, an additional 5%.  
If meritorious service honors and commendations are posthumously awarded after the death of a military person, an additional lump-sum pension shall be made by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
For martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, or military personnel who died of illness who have received meritorious service honors and commendations multiple times during active service, the survivors shall receive an additional lump-sum pension from the county-level people's government's competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs at the highest of the additional percentages.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对生前作出特殊贡献的烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人，除按照本条例规定发给其遗属一次性抚恤金外，军队可以按照有关规定发给其遗属一次性特别抚恤金。

**Article 17** For martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, or military personnel who died of illness who made special contributions during their lifetime, in addition to the lump-sum pension paid to their survivors in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the armed forces may pay a one-time special pension to their survivors in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　烈士褒扬金发给烈士的父母（抚养人）、配偶、子女；没有父母（抚养人）、配偶、子女的，发给未满18周岁的兄弟姐妹和已满18周岁但无生活费来源且由该军人生前供养的兄弟姐妹。  
一次性抚恤金发给烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属，遗属的范围按照前款规定确定。

**Article 18** The martyr commendation award shall be paid to the parents (foster parents), spouse, and children of the martyr; if there are no parents (foster parents), spouse, or children, it shall be paid to siblings under the age of 18 and siblings who have reached the age of 18 but have no source of living expenses and were supported by the military person during his or her lifetime.  
The lump-sum pension shall be paid to the survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness; the scope of survivors shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对符合下列条件的烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属，由其户籍所在地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门依据其申请，在审核确认其符合条件当月起发给定期抚恤金：  
（一）父母（抚养人）、配偶无劳动能力、无生活费来源，或者收入水平低于当地居民平均生活水平的；  
（二）子女未满18周岁或者已满18周岁但因上学或者残疾无生活费来源的；  
（三）兄弟姐妹未满18周岁或者已满18周岁但因上学无生活费来源且由该军人生前供养的。  
定期抚恤金标准应当参照上一年度全国居民人均可支配收入水平确定，具体标准及其调整办法，由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同国务院财政部门规定。

**Article 19** For survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness who meet the following conditions, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place of their domicile shall, upon their application, pay a regular pension from the month in which they are reviewed and confirmed to meet the conditions:  
(1) the parents (foster parents) or spouse are unable to work, have no source of living expenses, or have an income level lower than the average living standard of local residents;  
(2) the children are under the age of 18, or have reached the age of 18 but have no source of living expenses due to schooling or disability;  
(3) the siblings are under the age of 18, or have reached the age of 18 but have no source of living expenses due to schooling and were supported by the military person during his or her lifetime.  
The standard of the regular pension shall be determined by reference to the per capita disposable income level of residents nationwide in the preceding year. The specific standards and adjustment measures shall be prescribed by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council jointly with the finance department under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人生前的配偶再婚后继续赡养烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人父母（抚养人），继续抚养烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人生前供养的未满18周岁或者已满18周岁但无劳动能力且无生活费来源的兄弟姐妹的，由其户籍所在地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门继续发放定期抚恤金。

**Article 20** If the spouse of a martyr, a military person who died in the line of duty, or a military person who died of illness, after remarriage, continues to support the parents (foster parents) of the martyr, the military person who died in the line of duty, or the military person who died of illness, and continues to raise the siblings under the age of 18, or siblings who have reached the age of 18 but are unable to work and have no source of living expenses, who were supported by the military person during his or her lifetime, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place of their domicile shall continue to pay the regular pension.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对领取定期抚恤金后生活仍有特殊困难的烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属，县级以上地方人民政府可以增发抚恤金或者采取其他方式予以困难补助。

**Article 21** For survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness who still have special difficulties in living after receiving the regular pension, local people's governments at or above the county level may make additional pension payments or adopt other measures to provide hardship subsidies.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　享受定期抚恤金的烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属死亡的，继续发放6个月其原享受的定期抚恤金，作为丧葬补助。

**Article 22** If a survivor of a martyr, a survivor of a military person who died in the line of duty, or a survivor of a military person who died of illness who is receiving a regular pension dies, the regular pension originally enjoyed shall continue to be paid for 6 months as a funeral subsidy.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　军人失踪被宣告死亡的，在其被评定为烈士、确认为因公牺牲或者病故后，又经法定程序撤销对其死亡宣告的，由原评定或者确认机关取消其烈士、因公牺牲军人或者病故军人资格，并由发证机关收回有关证件，终止其家属原享受的抚恤待遇。

**Article 23** If a military person who went missing and was declared dead, after being assessed as a martyr, confirmed as having died in the line of duty, or confirmed as having died of illness, has the declaration of death revoked through legal procedures, the original assessing or confirming authority shall cancel the status of martyr, military person who died in the line of duty, or military person who died of illness, and the issuing authority shall revoke the relevant certificates and terminate the pension treatment originally enjoyed by the family members.

### 第三章　军人残疾抚恤

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　残疾军人享受残疾抚恤金，并可以按照规定享受供养待遇、护理费等。

**Article 24** Disabled military personnel shall enjoy a disability pension, and may enjoy support benefits, nursing fees, etc. in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军人残疾，符合下列情形之一的，认定为因战致残：  
（一）对敌作战负伤致残的；  
（二）因执行任务遭敌人或者犯罪分子伤害致残，或者被俘、被捕后不屈遭敌人伤害或者被折磨致残的；  
（三）为抢救和保护国家财产、集体财产、公民生命财产或者执行反恐怖任务和处置突发事件致残的；  
（四）因执行军事演习、战备航行飞行、空降和导弹发射训练、试航试飞任务以及参加武器装备科研试验致残的；  
（五）在执行外交任务或者国家派遣的对外援助、维持国际和平任务中致残的；  
（六）其他因战致残的。  
军人残疾，符合下列情形之一的，认定为因公致残：  
（一）在执行任务中、工作岗位上或者在上下班途中，由于意外事件致残的；  
（二）因患职业病致残的；  
（三）在执行任务中或者在工作岗位上突发疾病受伤致残的；  
（四）其他因公致残的。  
义务兵和初级军士除前款第二项、第三项规定情形以外，因其他疾病导致残疾的，认定为因病致残。

**Article 25** A military person who becomes disabled under any of the following circumstances shall be recognized as having a disability due to combat:  
(1) becoming disabled from injuries sustained in combat against the enemy;  
(2) becoming disabled from injuries caused by the enemy or criminals while performing duties, or becoming disabled from injuries or torture inflicted by the enemy after being captured or arrested without yielding;  
(3) becoming disabled while rescuing and protecting state property, collective property, or citizens' lives and property, or performing counter-terrorism tasks and dealing with emergencies;  
(4) becoming disabled while performing military exercises, combat readiness navigation and flight, airborne and missile launch training, trial navigation and test flight missions, and participating in scientific research and testing of weapons and equipment;  
(5) becoming disabled while performing diplomatic missions or missions of foreign assistance or maintaining international peace dispatched by the state;  
(6) other disabilities due to combat.  
A military person who becomes disabled under any of the following circumstances shall be recognized as having a disability due to duty:  
(1) becoming disabled due to an accidental event while performing a task, at the workplace, or on the way to or from work;  
(2) becoming disabled due to an occupational disease;  
(3) becoming disabled from injuries caused by sudden illness while performing a task or at the workplace;  
(4) other disabilities due to duty.  
A conscript or junior non-commissioned officer who becomes disabled due to diseases other than the circumstances specified in items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be recognized as having a disability due to illness.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　残疾的等级，根据劳动功能障碍程度和生活自理障碍程度确定，由重到轻分为一级至十级。  
残疾等级的具体评定标准由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同国务院人力资源社会保障部门、卫生健康部门和军队有关部门规定。

**Article 26** The grades of disability shall be determined based on the degree of impairment in work ability and the degree of impairment in self-care ability, and shall be divided into grades one to ten, from the most severe to the least severe.  
The specific evaluation standards for disability grades shall be prescribed by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council jointly with the human resources and social security department under the State Council, the health department, and the relevant military departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　军人因战、因公致残经治疗伤情稳定后，符合评定残疾等级条件的，应当及时评定残疾等级。义务兵和初级军士因病致残经治疗病情稳定后，符合评定残疾等级条件的，本人（无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人由其监护人）或者所在单位应当及时提出申请，在服现役期间评定残疾等级。  
因战、因公致残，残疾等级被评定为一级至十级的，享受抚恤；因病致残，残疾等级被评定为一级至六级的，享受抚恤。评定残疾等级的，从批准当月起发给残疾抚恤金。

**Article 27** If a military person who becomes disabled due to combat or duty meets the conditions for disability grade assessment after the injury stabilizes following treatment, the disability grade shall be assessed in a timely manner. If a conscript or junior non-commissioned officer who becomes disabled due to illness meets the conditions for disability grade assessment after the condition stabilizes following treatment, the person (or the guardian if the person is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity) or the unit to which the person belongs shall promptly file an application for disability grade assessment during active service.  
Those who become disabled due to combat or duty and are assessed at disability grades one to ten shall enjoy pensions; those who become disabled due to illness and are assessed at disability grades one to six shall enjoy pensions. For those with assessed disability grades, the disability pension shall be paid from the month of approval.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　因战、因公、因病致残性质的认定和残疾等级的评定权限是：  
（一）义务兵和初级军士的残疾，由军队军级以上单位卫生部门会同相关部门认定和评定；  
（二）军官、中级以上军士的残疾，由军队战区级以上单位卫生部门会同相关部门认定和评定；  
（三）退出现役的军人和移交政府安置的军队离休退休干部、退休军士需要认定残疾性质和评定残疾等级的，由省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门认定和评定。  
评定残疾等级，应当依据医疗卫生专家小组出具的残疾等级医学鉴定意见。  
残疾军人由认定残疾性质和评定残疾等级的机关发给《中华人民共和国残疾军人证》。

**Article 28** The authority for determining the nature of disability (due to combat, duty, or illness) and assessing the disability grade shall be as follows:  
(1) For conscripts and junior non-commissioned officers, the determination and assessment shall be made by the health department at or above the corps level of the armed forces jointly with the relevant departments;  
(2) For officers and non-commissioned officers at or above the intermediate level, the determination and assessment shall be made by the health department at or above the theater level of the armed forces jointly with the relevant departments;  
(3) For military personnel who have been discharged from active service and retired officers and retired non-commissioned officers transferred to government placement who need to have the nature of disability determined and the disability grade assessed, the determination and assessment shall be made by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the provincial people's government.  
The assessment of disability grades shall be based on the medical evaluation opinions on disability grades issued by a medical and health expert panel.  
A disabled military person shall be issued a "Certificate of Disabled Military Personnel of the People's Republic of China" by the authority that determines the nature of disability and assesses the disability grade.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　军人因战、因公致残，未及时评定残疾等级，退出现役后，本人（无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人由其监护人）应当及时申请补办评定残疾等级；凭原始档案记载及原始病历能够证明服现役期间的残情和伤残性质符合评定残疾等级条件的，可以评定残疾等级。  
被诊断、鉴定为职业病或者因体内残留弹片致残，符合残疾等级评定条件的，可以补办评定残疾等级。  
军人被评定残疾等级后，在服现役期间或者退出现役后原致残部位残疾情况发生明显变化，原定残疾等级与残疾情况明显不符，本人（无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人由其监护人）申请或者军队卫生部门、地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门提出需要调整残疾等级的，可以重新评定残疾等级。申请调整残疾等级应当在上一次评定残疾等级1年后提出。

**Article 29** If a military person who becomes disabled due to combat or duty fails to have the disability grade assessed in a timely manner, after being discharged from active service, the person (or the guardian if the person is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity) shall promptly apply for a supplementary assessment of the disability grade; if the original archive records and original medical records can prove that the disability condition and nature of disability during active service meet the conditions for disability grade assessment, the disability grade may be assessed.  
Those who are diagnosed or identified as having an occupational disease or become disabled due to residual shrapnel in the body, and meet the conditions for disability grade assessment, may apply for a supplementary assessment of the disability grade.  
After a military person has been assessed a disability grade, if during active service or after discharge from active service, the disability condition at the original disabled site undergoes a significant change, and the original disability grade is clearly inconsistent with the disability condition, the person (or the guardian if the person is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity) may apply, or the military health department or the local people's government's competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs may propose that the disability grade needs to be adjusted, and a reassessment of the disability grade may be conducted. An application for adjustment of the disability grade shall be filed one year after the last disability grade assessment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　退出现役的残疾军人或者向政府移交的残疾军人，应当自军队办理退役手续或者移交手续后60日内，向户籍迁入地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门申请转入抚恤关系，按照残疾性质和等级享受残疾抚恤金。其退役或者向政府移交当年的残疾抚恤金由所在部队发给，迁入地县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门从下一年起按照当地的标准发给。  
因工作需要继续服现役的残疾军人，经军队军级以上单位批准，由所在部队按照规定发给残疾抚恤金。

**Article 30** A disabled military person who is discharged from active service or transferred to the government shall, within 60 days after the military completes the discharge or transfer procedures, apply to the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place to which the person's domicile is transferred for the transfer of the pension relationship, and shall enjoy the disability pension according to the nature and grade of disability. The disability pension for the year of discharge or transfer to the government shall be paid by the original military unit, and the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government in the place to which the person moves shall pay the pension from the following year at the local standard.  
For a disabled military person who continues to serve on active duty due to work needs, with the approval of the military unit at or above the corps level, the original military unit shall pay the disability pension in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　残疾军人的抚恤金标准应当参照上一年度全国城镇单位就业人员年平均工资水平确定。残疾抚恤金的标准以及一级至十级残疾军人享受残疾抚恤金的具体办法，由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同国务院财政部门规定。  
对领取残疾抚恤金后生活仍有特殊困难的残疾军人，县级以上地方人民政府可以增发抚恤金或者采取其他方式予以困难补助。

**Article 31** The pension standard for disabled military personnel shall be determined by reference to the average annual wage level of employed persons in urban units nationwide in the preceding year. The specific standards for disability pensions and the specific methods for disabled military personnel of grades one to ten to enjoy disability pensions shall be prescribed by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council jointly with the finance department under the State Council.  
For disabled military personnel who still have special difficulties in living after receiving the disability pension, local people's governments at or above the county level may make additional pension payments or adopt other measures to provide hardship subsidies.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　退出现役的因战、因公致残的残疾军人因旧伤复发死亡的，由县级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照因公牺牲军人的抚恤金标准发给其遗属一次性抚恤金，其遗属按照国家规定享受因公牺牲军人遗属定期抚恤金待遇。  
退出现役的残疾军人因病死亡的，对其遗属继续发放12个月其原享受的残疾抚恤金，作为丧葬补助；其中，因战、因公致残的一级至四级残疾军人因病死亡的，其遗属按照国家规定享受病故军人遗属定期抚恤金待遇。

**Article 32** If a disabled military person who has been discharged from active service and whose disability is due to combat or duty dies from the recurrence of old injuries, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the county-level people's government shall pay a lump-sum pension to the survivors at the pension standard for military personnel who died in the line of duty, and the survivors shall enjoy the regular pension treatment for survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty in accordance with state provisions.  
If a disabled military person who has been discharged from active service dies from illness, the survivors shall continue to receive the disability pension originally enjoyed for 12 months as a funeral subsidy; among them, if a disabled military person of grades one to four whose disability is due to combat or duty dies from illness, the survivors shall enjoy the regular pension treatment for survivors of military personnel who died of illness in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　退出现役时为一级至四级的残疾军人，由国家供养终身；其中，对需要长年医疗或者独身一人不便分散供养的，经省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门批准，可以集中供养。

**Article 33** Disabled military personnel who are at grades one to four at the time of discharge from active service shall be supported for life by the state; among them, those who require long-term medical care or who live alone and are not suitable for decentralized support may, with the approval of the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the provincial people's government, receive centralized support.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对退出现役时分散供养的一级至四级、退出现役后补办或者调整为一级至四级、服现役期间因患精神障碍评定为五级至六级的残疾军人发给护理费，护理费的标准为：  
（一）因战、因公一级和二级残疾的，为当地上一年度城镇单位就业人员月平均工资的50%；  
（二）因战、因公三级和四级残疾的，为当地上一年度城镇单位就业人员月平均工资的40%；  
（三）因病一级至四级残疾的，为当地上一年度城镇单位就业人员月平均工资的30%；  
（四）因精神障碍五级至六级残疾的，为当地上一年度城镇单位就业人员月平均工资的25%。  
退出现役并移交地方的残疾军人的护理费，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门发给。未退出现役或者未移交地方的残疾军人的护理费，由所在部队按照军队有关规定发给。移交政府安置的离休退休残疾军人的护理费，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。  
享受护理费的残疾军人在优抚医院集中收治期间，护理费由优抚医院统筹使用。享受护理费的残疾军人在部队期间，由单位从地方购买照护服务的，护理费按照规定由单位纳入购买社会服务费用统一管理使用。

**Article 34** Nursing fees shall be paid to disabled military personnel who are at grades one to four when receiving decentralized support at the time of discharge from active service, those whose disability grade is supplemented or adjusted to grades one to four after discharge from active service, and those who are assessed at grades five to six due to mental disorders during active service. The standards for nursing fees are:  
(1) For those with disabilities due to combat or duty at grades one and two, 50% of the local average monthly wage of employed persons in urban units in the preceding year;  
(2) For those with disabilities due to combat or duty at grades three and four, 40% of the local average monthly wage of employed persons in urban units in the preceding year;  
(3) For those with disabilities due to illness at grades one to four, 30% of the local average monthly wage of employed persons in urban units in the preceding year;  
(4) For those with disabilities due to mental disorders at grades five and six, 25% of the local average monthly wage of employed persons in urban units in the preceding year.  
Nursing fees for disabled military personnel who have been discharged from active service and transferred to local authorities shall be paid by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level. Nursing fees for disabled military personnel who have not been discharged from active service or have not been transferred to local authorities shall be paid by the military unit in accordance with the relevant military provisions. Nursing fees for retired disabled military personnel placed under government care shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state and military provisions.  
During the period when a disabled military person receiving nursing fees is hospitalized in a preferential treatment hospital, the nursing fees shall be used collectively by the preferential treatment hospital. If, during the period when a disabled military person receiving nursing fees is in the military unit, the unit purchases care services from local sources, the nursing fees shall, in accordance with the provisions, be included by the unit in the costs of purchasing social services for unified management and use.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　残疾军人因残情需要配制假肢、轮椅、助听器等康复辅助器具，正在服现役的，由军队军级以上单位负责解决；退出现役的，由省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责解决，所需经费由省级人民政府保障。

**Article 35** If a disabled military person needs to be fitted with artificial limbs, wheelchairs, hearing aids, or other rehabilitation assistive devices due to the disability, for those on active service, the military unit at or above the corps level shall be responsible for providing them; for those who have been discharged from active service, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the provincial people's government shall be responsible for providing them, and the necessary funds shall be guaranteed by the provincial people's government.

### 第四章　优待

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　抚恤优待对象依法享受家庭优待金、荣誉激励、关爱帮扶，以及教育、医疗、就业、住房、养老、交通、文化等方面的优待。

**Article 36** Persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment shall, according to law, enjoy family preferential allowances, honor incentives, care and assistance, as well as preferential treatment in areas such as education, medical care, employment, housing, elderly care, transportation, and culture.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家完善抚恤优待对象表彰、奖励办法，构建精神与物质并重的荣誉激励制度体系，建立抚恤优待对象荣誉激励机制，健全邀请参加重大庆典活动、开展典型宣传、悬挂光荣牌、制发优待证、送喜报、载入地方志、组织短期疗养等政策制度。

**Article 37** The state shall improve the measures for commendation and reward of persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, build a system of honor incentives that attaches equal importance to spiritual and material aspects, establish an honor incentive mechanism for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, and improve policies and systems such as inviting participation in major celebrations, conducting typical publicity, hanging glory plaques, issuing preferential treatment certificates, delivering good news certificates, recording in local chronicles, and organizing short-term recuperation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家建立抚恤优待对象关爱帮扶机制，逐步完善抚恤优待对象生活状况信息档案登记制度，有条件的地方可以设立退役军人关爱基金，充分利用退役军人关爱基金等开展帮扶援助，加大对生活发生重大变故、遇到特殊困难的抚恤优待对象的关爱帮扶力度。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处通过入户走访等方式，主动了解本行政区域抚恤优待对象的生活状况，及时发现生活困难的抚恤优待对象，提供协助申请、组织帮扶等服务。基层群众性自治组织应当协助做好抚恤优待对象的走访帮扶工作。鼓励发挥社会组织、社会工作者和志愿者作用，为抚恤优待对象提供心理疏导、精神抚慰、法律援助、人文关怀等服务。县级以上人民政府应当采取措施，为乡镇人民政府、街道办事处以及基层群众性自治组织开展相关工作提供条件和支持。

**Article 38** The state shall establish a care and assistance mechanism for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, gradually improve the registration system for information archives on the living conditions of persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment. Where conditions permit, local authorities may set up care funds for demobilized military personnel, make full use of such care funds to carry out assistance and aid, and increase the intensity of care and assistance for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment who have experienced major changes in their lives or encountered special difficulties.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall, through home visits and other means, proactively learn about the living conditions of persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment within their administrative areas, promptly identify persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment who are in need, and provide services such as assistance in applications and organizing aid. Grass-roots autonomous organizations shall assist in carrying out visits and aid for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment. The role of social organizations, social workers, and volunteers shall be encouraged in providing services such as psychological counseling, spiritual comfort, legal aid, and humanistic care to persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment. People's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to provide conditions and support for township people's governments, sub-district offices, and grass-roots autonomous organizations in carrying out relevant work.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家对烈士遗属逐步加大教育、医疗、就业、养老、住房、交通、文化等方面的优待力度。  
国务院有关部门、军队有关部门和地方人民政府应当关心烈士遗属的生活情况，开展走访慰问，及时给予烈士遗属荣誉激励和精神抚慰。  
烈士子女符合公务员、社区专职工作人员考录、聘用条件的，在同等条件下优先录用或者聘用。

**Article 39** The state shall gradually increase the intensity of preferential treatment for survivors of martyrs in areas such as education, medical care, employment, elderly care, housing, transportation, and culture.  
The relevant departments of the State Council, the relevant military departments, and local people's governments shall care about the living conditions of survivors of martyrs, conduct visits and condolences, and promptly provide honor incentives and spiritual comfort to survivors of martyrs.  
If children of martyrs meet the conditions for recruitment or employment as civil servants or full-time community workers, they shall be given priority in recruitment or employment under equal conditions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的子女、兄弟姐妹以及军人子女，本人自愿应征并且符合征兵条件的，优先批准服现役；报考军队文职人员的，按照规定享受优待。

**Article 40** Children, siblings, and children of military personnel of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness, if they voluntarily apply for enlistment and meet the recruitment conditions, shall be given priority in being approved for active service; if they apply for military civilian posts, they shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家兴办优抚医院、光荣院，按照规定为抚恤优待对象提供优待服务。县级以上人民政府应当充分利用现有医疗和养老服务资源，因地制宜加强优抚医院、光荣院建设，收治或者集中供养孤老、生活不能自理的退役军人。  
参战退役军人、烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属和军人家属，符合规定条件申请在国家兴办的优抚医院、光荣院集中供养、住院治疗、短期疗养的，享受优先、优惠待遇。  
各类社会福利机构应当优先接收抚恤优待对象。烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属和军人家属，符合规定条件申请入住公办养老机构的，同等条件下优先安排。

**Article 41** The state shall establish preferential treatment hospitals and homes of honor, and provide preferential treatment services to persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment in accordance with the provisions. People's governments at or above the county level shall make full use of existing medical and elderly care service resources, strengthen the construction of preferential treatment hospitals and homes of honor according to local conditions, and admit or provide centralized support for demobilized military personnel who are elderly, lonely, or unable to care for themselves.  
Demobilized military personnel who participated in combat, survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, survivors of military personnel who died of illness, and family members of military personnel, who meet the prescribed conditions and apply for centralized support, hospitalization, or short-term recuperation in state-established preferential treatment hospitals or homes of honor, shall enjoy priority and preferential treatment.  
All types of social welfare institutions shall give priority to admitting persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment. Survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, survivors of military personnel who died of illness, and family members of military personnel, who meet the prescribed conditions and apply for admission to public elderly care institutions, shall be given priority placement under equal conditions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家建立中央和地方财政分级负担的义务兵家庭优待金制度，义务兵服现役期间，其家庭由批准入伍地县级人民政府发给优待金，同时按照规定享受其他优待。  
义务兵和军士入伍前依法取得的农村土地承包经营权，服现役期间应当保留。  
义务兵从部队发出的平信，免费邮递。

**Article 42** The state shall establish a system of family preferential allowances for conscripts shared by the central and local finances according to their respective levels. During the period of active service of a conscript, the family shall be paid a preferential allowance by the county-level people's government in the place where enlistment was approved, and shall also enjoy other preferential treatment in accordance with the provisions.  
The right to the operation of contracted rural land lawfully obtained by a conscript or non-commissioned officer before enlistment shall be retained during the period of active service.  
Ordinary letters sent by conscripts from the military unit shall be posted free of charge.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　烈士子女报考普通高中、中等职业学校、高等学校，按照《烈士褒扬条例》等法律法规和国家有关规定享受优待。在公办幼儿园和公办学校就读的，按照国家有关规定享受各项学生资助等政策。  
因公牺牲军人子女、一级至四级残疾军人子女报考普通高中、中等职业学校、高等学校，在录取时按照国家有关规定给予优待；接受学历教育的，按照国家有关规定享受各项学生资助等政策。  
军人子女入读公办义务教育阶段学校和普惠性幼儿园，可以在本人、父母、祖父母、外祖父母或者其他法定监护人户籍所在地，或者父母居住地、部队驻地入学，享受当地军人子女教育优待政策；报考普通高中、中等职业学校、高等学校，按照国家有关规定优先录取；接受学历教育的，按照国家有关规定享受各项学生资助等政策。地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当按照法律法规和国家有关规定为军人子女创造接受良好教育的条件。  
残疾军人、义务兵和初级军士退出现役后，报考中等职业学校和高等学校，按照国家有关规定享受优待。优先安排残疾军人参加学习培训，按照规定享受国家资助政策。退役军人按照规定免费参加教育培训。符合条件的退役大学生士兵复学、转专业、攻读硕士研究生等，按照国家有关规定享受优待政策。  
抚恤优待对象享受教育优待的具体办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同国务院教育部门规定。

**Article 43** Children of martyrs who apply for admission to regular senior high schools, secondary vocational schools, or institutions of higher education shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the Regulations on Commending Martyrs and other laws, regulations, and relevant state provisions. Those studying in public kindergartens and public schools shall enjoy various student financial aid policies in accordance with relevant state provisions.  
Children of military personnel who died in the line of duty and children of disabled military personnel of grades one to four who apply for admission to regular senior high schools, secondary vocational schools, or institutions of higher education shall be given preferential treatment in admission in accordance with relevant state provisions; those receiving formal education shall enjoy various student financial aid policies in accordance with relevant state provisions.  
Children of military personnel who attend public compulsory education schools and inclusive kindergartens may be enrolled in the place of domicile of themselves, their parents, grandparents, maternal grandparents, or other legal guardians, or in the place of residence of their parents or the location of the military unit, and enjoy the local education preferential treatment policies for children of military personnel; those who apply for admission to regular senior high schools, secondary vocational schools, or institutions of higher education shall be given priority admission in accordance with relevant state provisions; those receiving formal education shall enjoy various student financial aid policies in accordance with relevant state provisions. Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and relevant state provisions, create conditions for children of military personnel to receive a good education.  
Disabled military personnel, conscripts, and junior non-commissioned officers who have been discharged from active service shall enjoy preferential treatment in applying for secondary vocational schools and institutions of higher education in accordance with relevant state provisions. Disabled military personnel shall be given priority in participating in study and training, and shall enjoy state financial aid policies in accordance with the provisions. Demobilized military personnel shall participate in education and training free of charge in accordance with the provisions. Eligible demobilized college soldiers who resume studies, change majors, or pursue master's degrees shall enjoy preferential treatment policies in accordance with relevant state provisions.  
The specific measures for educational preferential treatment for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment shall be prescribed by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council jointly with the education department under the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家对一级至六级残疾军人的医疗费用按照规定予以保障，其中参加工伤保险的一级至六级残疾军人旧伤复发的医疗费用，由工伤保险基金支付。  
七级至十级残疾军人旧伤复发的医疗费用，已经参加工伤保险的，由工伤保险基金支付；未参加工伤保险，有工作单位的由工作单位解决，没有工作单位的由当地县级以上地方人民政府负责解决。七级至十级残疾军人旧伤复发以外的医疗费用，未参加医疗保险且本人支付有困难的，由当地县级以上地方人民政府酌情给予补助。  
抚恤优待对象在军队医疗卫生机构和政府举办的医疗卫生机构按照规定享受优待服务，国家鼓励社会力量举办的医疗卫生机构为抚恤优待对象就医提供优待服务。参战退役军人、残疾军人按照规定享受医疗优惠。  
抚恤优待对象享受医疗优待和优惠的具体办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门和中央军事委员会后勤保障部会同国务院财政、卫生健康、医疗保障等部门规定。  
中央财政对地方给予适当补助，用于帮助解决抚恤优待对象的医疗费用困难问题。

**Article 44** The state shall guarantee the medical expenses of disabled military personnel of grades one to six in accordance with the provisions, among which, for those who have participated in work-related injury insurance, the medical expenses for the recurrence of old injuries shall be paid by the work-related injury insurance fund.  
For disabled military personnel of grades seven to ten with recurrence of old injuries, if they have participated in work-related injury insurance, the medical expenses shall be paid by the work-related injury insurance fund; if they have not participated in work-related injury insurance, if they have a work unit, the work unit shall bear the expenses; if they have no work unit, the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for solving it. For medical expenses of disabled military personnel of grades seven to ten other than those for recurrence of old injuries, if they have not participated in medical insurance and have difficulty paying themselves, the local people's government at or above the county level may give appropriate subsidies as the circumstances warrant.  
Persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment shall enjoy preferential treatment services in military medical and health institutions and medical and health institutions run by the government in accordance with the provisions. The state encourages medical and health institutions run by social forces to provide preferential treatment services for medical treatment to persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment. Demobilized military personnel who participated in combat and disabled military personnel shall enjoy medical preferential treatment in accordance with the provisions.  
The specific measures for medical preferential treatment and discounts for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment shall be prescribed by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council and the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission jointly with the finance, health, and medical security departments under the State Council.  
The central finance shall provide appropriate subsidies to local authorities to help solve the problem of medical expenses difficulties for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　义务兵和军士入伍前是机关、群团组织、事业单位或者国有企业工作人员，退出现役后以自主就业方式安置的，可以选择复职复工，其工资、福利待遇不得低于本单位同等条件工作人员的平均水平；服现役期间，其家属继续享受该单位工作人员家属的有关福利待遇。  
残疾军人、义务兵和初级军士退出现役后，报考公务员的，按照国家有关规定享受优待。

**Article 45** If a conscript or non-commissioned officer was a staff member of an organ, mass organization, public institution, or state-owned enterprise before enlistment, and is placed through self-employment after discharge from active service, the person may choose to resume the original post or work, and the salary and welfare benefits shall not be lower than the average level of staff members with equivalent conditions in the same unit; during the period of active service, the family members shall continue to enjoy the relevant welfare benefits of the family members of the unit's staff members.  
Disabled military personnel, conscripts, and junior non-commissioned officers who, after being discharged from active service, apply for civil service positions shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家依法保障军人配偶就业安置权益。机关、群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织，应当依法履行接收军人配偶就业安置的义务。经军队团级以上单位政治工作部门批准随军的军官家属、军士家属，由驻军所在地公安机关办理落户手续。  
军人配偶随军前在机关或者事业单位工作的，由安置地人民政府及其主管部门按照国家有关规定，安排到相应的工作单位。其中，随军前是公务员的，采取转任等方式，在规定的编制限额和职数内，结合当地和随军家属本人实际情况，原则上安置到机关相应岗位；随军前是事业单位工作人员的，采取交流方式，在规定的编制限额和设置的岗位数内，结合当地和随军家属本人实际情况，原则上安置到事业单位相应岗位。经个人和接收单位双向选择，也可以按照规定安置到其他单位适宜岗位。  
军人配偶随军前在其他单位工作或者无工作单位且有就业能力和就业意愿的，由安置地人民政府提供职业指导、职业介绍、职业培训等就业服务，按照规定落实相关扶持政策，帮助其实现就业。  
烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属和符合规定条件的军人配偶，当地人民政府应当优先安排就业。符合条件的军官和军士退出现役时，其配偶和子女可以按照国家有关规定随调随迁。

**Article 46** The state shall protect the employment placement rights and interests of spouses of military personnel according to law. Organs, mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations shall perform the obligation to accept the employment placement of spouses of military personnel according to law. For the family members of officers and non-commissioned officers who are approved to accompany the military by the political work department at or above the regimental level of the armed forces, the public security organ in the place where the military unit is stationed shall handle the household registration procedures.  
If a spouse of a military person worked in an organ or public institution before accompanying the military, the people's government of the place of placement and its competent departments shall arrange for the spouse to be placed in a corresponding work unit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state. Among them, if the spouse was a civil servant before accompanying the military, the spouse shall, through means such as transfer, be placed, in principle, in a corresponding post in an organ within the prescribed staffing quota and authorized number, taking into account the local conditions and the actual situation of the accompanying family member; if the spouse was a staff member of a public institution before accompanying the military, the spouse shall, through means such as exchange, be placed, in principle, in a corresponding post in a public institution within the prescribed staffing quota and established number of posts, taking into account the local conditions and the actual situation of the accompanying family member. Through two-way selection between the individual and the receiving unit, the spouse may also be placed in a suitable post in another unit in accordance with the provisions.  
If a spouse of a military person worked in another unit before accompanying the military or had no work unit but has the ability and willingness to work, the people's government of the place of placement shall provide employment services such as career guidance, job introduction, and vocational training, implement relevant support policies in accordance with the provisions, and help the spouse achieve employment.  
Local people's governments shall give priority to arranging employment for survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and spouses of military personnel who meet the prescribed conditions. When eligible officers and non-commissioned officers are discharged from active service, their spouses and children may be transferred and relocated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家鼓励有用工需求的用人单位优先安排随军家属就业。国有企业在新招录职工时，应当按照用工需求的适当比例聘用随军家属；有条件的民营企业在新招录职工时，可以按照用工需求的适当比例聘用随军家属。  
国家鼓励和扶持有条件、有意愿的军人配偶自主就业、自主创业，按照规定落实相关扶持政策。

**Article 47** The state shall encourage employers with labor demands to give priority to arranging employment for family members accompanying the military. When recruiting new employees, state-owned enterprises shall employ family members accompanying the military at an appropriate proportion of the labor demand; qualified private enterprises may, when recruiting new employees, employ family members accompanying the military at an appropriate proportion of the labor demand.  
The state shall encourage and support qualified and willing spouses of military personnel to pursue self-employment or start their own businesses, and implement relevant support policies in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　驻边疆国境的县（市）、沙漠区、国家确定的边远地区中的三类地区和军队确定的特、一、二类岛屿部队的军官、军士，其符合随军条件无法随军的家属，可以选择在军人、军人配偶原户籍所在地或者军人父母、军人配偶父母户籍所在地自愿落户，所在地人民政府应当妥善安置。

**Article 48** For officers and non-commissioned officers stationed in border counties (cities), desert areas, the third category of remote areas designated by the state, and troops on special, first, or second category islands designated by the armed forces, whose family members meet the conditions for accompanying the military but are unable to do so, the family members may choose to voluntarily settle in the place of the original domicile of the military person or the military person's spouse, or in the place of domicile of the military person's parents or the military person's spouse's parents. The local people's government shall properly arrange for their placement.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　随军的烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属，移交地方人民政府安置的，享受本条例和当地人民政府规定的优待。

**Article 49** Survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness who accompany the military and are transferred to the local people's government for placement shall enjoy the preferential treatment provided for in these Regulations and as prescribed by the local people's government.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　退出现役后，在机关、群团组织、企业事业单位和社会组织工作的残疾军人，享受与所在单位工伤人员同等的生活福利和医疗待遇。所在单位不得因其残疾将其辞退、解除聘用合同或者劳动合同。

**Article 50** Disabled military personnel who, after being discharged from active service, work in organs, mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, or social organizations shall enjoy the same living welfare and medical treatment as persons with work-related injuries in the same unit. The unit shall not dismiss the person, terminate the employment contract, or terminate the labor contract because of the disability.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家适应住房保障制度改革发展要求，逐步完善抚恤优待对象住房优待办法，适当加大对参战退役军人、烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属的优待力度。符合当地住房保障条件的抚恤优待对象承租、购买保障性住房的，县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当给予优先照顾。居住农村的符合条件的抚恤优待对象，同等条件下优先纳入国家或者地方实施的农村危房改造相关项目范围。

**Article 51** In response to the requirements of reform and development of the housing security system, the state shall gradually improve the housing preferential treatment measures for persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment, and appropriately increase the intensity of preferential treatment for demobilized military personnel who participated in combat, survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness. If a person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment who meets the local housing security conditions rents or purchases indemnificatory housing, the relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give priority consideration. Eligible persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment living in rural areas shall, under equal conditions, be given priority to be included in the scope of relevant projects for renovation of dilapidated rural houses implemented by the state or local authorities.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　军人凭军官证、军士证、义务兵证、学员证等有效证件，残疾军人凭《中华人民共和国残疾军人证》，烈士遗属、因公牺牲军人遗属、病故军人遗属凭优待证，乘坐境内运行的铁路旅客列车、轮船、长途客运班车和民航班机，享受购票、安检、候乘、通行等优先服务，随同出行的家属可以一同享受优先服务；残疾军人享受减收国内运输经营者对外公布票价50%的优待。  
军人、残疾军人凭证免费乘坐市内公共汽车、电车、轮渡和轨道交通工具。

**Article 52** Military personnel, with valid certificates such as officer's card, non-commissioned officer's card, conscript's card, or cadet's card; disabled military personnel with the "Certificate of Disabled Military Personnel of the People's Republic of China"; and survivors of martyrs, survivors of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and survivors of military personnel who died of illness with preferential treatment certificates, shall enjoy priority services such as ticket purchase, security check, waiting, boarding, and passage when taking railway passenger trains, ships, long-distance passenger buses, and civil aviation aircraft operating within the territory; family members accompanying them may also enjoy priority services together. Disabled military personnel shall enjoy the preferential treatment of a 50% reduction of the publicly announced fare of domestic transport operators.  
Military personnel and disabled military personnel shall, with their certificates, ride free of charge on urban public buses, trams, ferries, and rail transit vehicles.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　抚恤优待对象参观游览图书馆、博物馆、美术馆、科技馆、纪念馆、体育场馆等公共文化设施和公园、展览馆、名胜古迹等按照规定享受优待及优惠服务。

**Article 53** Persons entitled to pensions and preferential treatment shall, when visiting libraries, museums, art galleries, science and technology museums, memorial halls, sports venues, and other public cultural facilities, as well as parks, exhibition halls, places of historic interest and scenic beauty, etc., enjoy preferential treatment and discounted services in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　军人依法享受个人所得税优惠政策。退役军人从事个体经营或者企业招用退役军人，符合条件的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 54** Military personnel shall enjoy preferential individual income tax policies according to law. Demobilized military personnel who engage in self-employed businesses, or enterprises that employ demobilized military personnel, if meeting the conditions, shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　军人抚恤优待管理单位及其工作人员挪用、截留、私分军人抚恤优待所需经费和工作经费，构成犯罪的，依法追究相关责任人员的刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对相关责任人员依法给予处分。被挪用、截留、私分的军人抚恤优待所需经费和工作经费，由上一级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门、军队有关部门责令追回。

**Article 55** If the management units for military pensions and preferential treatment and their staff members misappropriate, intercept, or illegally distribute the funds required for military pensions and preferential treatment and work funds, and the act constitutes a crime, the criminal liability of the relevant responsible persons shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, the relevant responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law. The funds required for military pensions and preferential treatment and work funds that have been misappropriated, intercepted, or illegally distributed shall be ordered to be recovered by the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the people's government at the next higher level or the relevant military department.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　军人抚恤优待管理单位及其工作人员、参与军人抚恤优待工作的单位及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由其上级主管部门责令改正；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究相关责任人员的刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对相关责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）违反规定审批军人抚恤待遇的；  
（二）在审批军人抚恤待遇工作中出具虚假诊断、鉴定、证明的；  
（三）不按照规定的标准、数额、对象审批或者发放抚恤金、补助金、优待金的；  
（四）在军人抚恤优待工作中利用职权谋取私利的；  
（五）有其他违反法律法规行为的。

**Article 56** If the management units for military pensions and preferential treatment and their staff members, or the units participating in the work on military pensions and preferential treatment and their staff members, commit any of the following acts, the competent department at the next higher level shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, the criminal liability of the relevant responsible persons shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, the relevant responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) approving military pension treatment in violation of the provisions;  
(2) issuing false diagnoses, evaluations, or certificates in the work of approving military pension treatment;  
(3) approving or disbursing pensions, subsidies, or preferential allowances not in accordance with the prescribed standards, amounts, or recipients;  
(4) using power for personal gain in the work on military pensions and preferential treatment;  
(5) other acts in violation of laws or regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　负有军人优待义务的单位不履行优待义务的，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门责令限期履行义务；逾期仍未履行的，处以2万元以上5万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。因不履行优待义务使抚恤优待对象受到损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 57** If a unit that has the obligation to provide preferential treatment to military personnel fails to perform the obligation, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to perform the obligation within a specified period; if it still fails to perform after the expiration of the period, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law. If the person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment suffers losses due to the failure to perform the preferential treatment obligation, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　抚恤优待对象及其他人员有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门、军队有关部门取消相关待遇、追缴违法所得，并由其所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）冒领抚恤金、补助金、优待金的；  
（二）伪造残情、伤情、病情骗取医药费等费用或者相关抚恤优待待遇的；  
（三）出具虚假证明，伪造证件、印章骗取抚恤金、补助金、优待金的；  
（四）其他弄虚作假骗取抚恤优待待遇的。

**Article 58** If a person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment or any other person commits any of the following acts, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level or the relevant military department shall cancel the relevant treatment, recover the illegal gains, and the unit to which the person belongs or the relevant department shall impose sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) fraudulently claiming pensions, subsidies, or preferential allowances;  
(2) falsifying the degree of disability, injury, or illness to defraud medical expenses or other related pension and preferential treatment benefits;  
(3) issuing false certificates, forging documents or seals to defraud pensions, subsidies, or preferential allowances;  
(4) other acts of fraud to obtain pension and preferential treatment benefits.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　抚恤优待对象被判处有期徒刑、剥夺政治权利或者被通缉期间，中止发放抚恤金、补助金；被判处死刑、无期徒刑以及被军队开除军籍的，取消其抚恤优待资格。  
抚恤优待对象有前款规定情形的，由省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照国家有关规定中止或者取消其抚恤优待相关待遇，报国务院退役军人工作主管部门备案。

**Article 59** If a person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, deprived of political rights, or is wanted, the payment of pensions and subsidies shall be suspended; if the person is sentenced to the death penalty, life imprisonment, or is expelled from military service, the person's eligibility for pensions and preferential treatment shall be canceled.  
If a person entitled to pensions and preferential treatment falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs of the provincial people's government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, suspend or cancel the relevant pension and preferential treatment benefits, and shall report to the competent department for demobilized military personnel affairs under the State Council for the record.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例适用于中国人民武装警察部队。

**Article 60** These Regulations shall apply to the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　军队离休退休干部和退休军士的抚恤优待，按照本条例有关军人抚恤优待的规定执行。  
参试退役军人参照本条例有关参战退役军人的规定执行。  
因参战以及参加非战争军事行动、军事训练和执行军事勤务伤亡的预备役人员、民兵、民工、其他人员的抚恤，参照本条例的有关规定办理。

**Article 61** The pensions and preferential treatment for retired officers and retired non-commissioned officers of the armed forces shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations concerning pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel.  
Demobilized military personnel who participated in nuclear tests shall be handled by reference to the provisions of these Regulations concerning demobilized military personnel who participated in combat.  
The pensions for casualties of reserve personnel, militiamen, civilian workers, and other personnel who suffer casualties due to participation in combat, non-war military operations, military training, or performance of military duties shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家按照规定为符合条件的参战退役军人、带病回乡退役军人、年满60周岁农村籍退役士兵、1954年10月31日之前入伍后经批准退出现役的人员，以及居住在农村和城镇无工作单位且年满60周岁、在国家建立定期抚恤金制度时已满18周岁的烈士子女，发放定期生活补助。  
享受国家定期生活补助的参战退役军人去世后，继续发放6个月其原享受的定期生活补助，作为丧葬补助。

**Article 62** The state shall, in accordance with the provisions, pay a regular living allowance to eligible demobilized military personnel who participated in combat, demobilized military personnel who returned to their hometowns with illness, demobilized soldiers who are rural household registrants and have reached the age of 60, persons who enlisted before October 31, 1954 and were later approved to be discharged from active service, and children of martyrs who live in rural areas or in urban areas without a work unit and have reached the age of 60 and were at least 18 years old when the state established the regular pension system.  
After the death of a demobilized military person who participated in combat and was receiving a state regular living allowance, the original regular living allowance shall continue to be paid for 6 months as a funeral subsidy.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　深化国防和军队改革期间现役军人转改的文职人员，按照本条例有关军人抚恤优待的规定执行。  
其他文职人员因在作战和有作战背景的军事行动中承担支援保障任务、参加非战争军事行动以及军级以上单位批准且列入军事训练计划的军事训练伤亡的抚恤优待，参照本条例的有关规定办理。

**Article 63** Civilian personnel who are transferred from active service during the period of deepening national defense and military reform shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations concerning pensions and preferential treatment for military personnel.  
The pensions and preferential treatment for other civilian personnel who suffer casualties due to undertaking support and guarantee tasks in combat and military operations with a combat background, participating in non-war military operations, and military training approved by units at or above the corps level and included in military training plans shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2024年10月1日起施行。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force on October 1, 2024.
