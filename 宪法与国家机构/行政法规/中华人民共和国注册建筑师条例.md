---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对注册建筑师的管理，提高建筑设计质量与水平，保障公民生命和财产安全，维护社会公共利益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of registered architects, improving the quality and standard of architectural design, ensuring the safety of life and property of citizens, and safeguarding the public interests of the society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称注册建筑师，是指依法取得注册建筑师证书并从事房屋建筑设计及相关业务的人员。  
注册建筑师分为一级注册建筑师和二级注册建筑师。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "registered architect" means a person who has obtained a registered architect certificate according to law and engages in building architectural design and related business.  
Registered architects are classified into Class 1 registered architects and Class 2 registered architects.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　注册建筑师的考试、注册和执业，适用本条例。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to the examination, registration, and practice of registered architects.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院建设行政主管部门、人事行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门、人事行政主管部门依照本条例的规定对注册建筑师的考试、注册和执业实施指导和监督。

**Article 4** The competent administrative department of construction and the competent administrative department of personnel under the State Council, and the competent administrative departments of construction and personnel under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, provide guidance and supervision over the examination, registration, and practice of registered architects.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国注册建筑师管理委员会和省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会，依照本条例的规定负责注册建筑师的考试和注册的具体工作。  
全国注册建筑师管理委员会由国务院建设行政主管部门、人事行政主管部门、其他有关行政主管部门的代表和建筑设计专家组成。  
省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会由省、自治区、直辖市建设行政主管部门、人事行政主管部门、其他有关行政主管部门的代表和建筑设计专家组成。

**Article 5** The National Registered Architect Administration Committee and the registered architect administration committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the specific work of the examination and registration of registered architects.  
The National Registered Architect Administration Committee shall be composed of representatives from the competent administrative department of construction, the competent administrative department of personnel, and other relevant competent administrative departments under the State Council, as well as experts in architectural design.  
The registered architect administration committee of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be composed of representatives from the competent administrative department of construction, the competent administrative department of personnel, and other relevant competent administrative departments of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, as well as experts in architectural design.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　注册建筑师可以组建注册建筑师协会，维护会员的合法权益。

**Article 6** Registered architects may establish registered architect associations to protect the legitimate rights and interests of their members.

### 第二章　考试和注册

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家实行注册建筑师全国统一考试制度。注册建筑师全国统一考试办法，由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院人事行政主管部门商国务院其他有关行政主管部门共同制定，由全国注册建筑师管理委员会组织实施。

**Article 7** The State shall implement a national unified examination system for registered architects. The measures for the national unified examination for registered architects shall be jointly formulated by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council in conjunction with the competent administrative department of personnel under the State Council and after consultation with other relevant competent administrative departments under the State Council, and shall be organized and implemented by the National Registered Architect Administration Committee.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　符合下列条件之一的，可以申请参加一级注册建筑师考试：  
(一)取得建筑学硕士以上学位或者相近专业工学博士学位，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务2年以上的；  
(二)取得建筑学学士学位或者相近专业工学硕士学位，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务3年以上的；  
(三)具有建筑学专业大学本科毕业学历并从事建筑设计或者相关业务5年以上的，或者具有建筑学相近专业大学本科毕业学历并从事建筑设计或者相关业务7年以上的；  
(四)取得高级工程师技术职称并从事建筑设计或者相关业务3年以上的，或者取得工程师技术职称并从事建筑设计或者相关业务5年以上的；  
(五)不具有前四项规定的条件，但设计成绩突出，经全国注册建筑师管理委员会认定达到前四项规定的专业水平的。  
前款第三项至第五项规定的人员应当取得学士学位。

**Article 8** A person who meets any of the following conditions may apply to take the examination for Class 1 registered architects:  
(1) having obtained a master's degree or above in architecture or a doctoral degree in engineering in a related discipline, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 2 years;  
(2) having obtained a bachelor's degree in architecture or a master's degree in engineering in a related discipline, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 3 years;  
(3) having a university undergraduate diploma in architecture and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 5 years, or having a university undergraduate diploma in a discipline related to architecture and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 7 years;  
(4) having obtained the technical title of senior engineer and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 3 years, or having obtained the technical title of engineer and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 5 years;  
(5) not meeting the conditions specified in the preceding four items, but having achieved outstanding design results and having been recognized by the National Registered Architect Administration Committee as reaching the professional level specified in the preceding four items.  
The persons mentioned in items (3) to (5) of the preceding paragraph shall have obtained a bachelor's degree.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　符合下列条件之一的，可以申请参加二级注册建筑师考试：  
(一)具有建筑学或者相近专业大学本科毕业以上学历，从事建筑设计或者相关业务2年以上的；  
(二)具有建筑设计技术专业或者相近专业大专毕业以上学历，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务3年以上的；  
(三)具有建筑设计技术专业4年制中专毕业学历，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务5年以上的；  
(四)具有建筑设计技术相近专业中专毕业学历，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务7年以上的；  
(五)取得助理工程师以上技术职称，并从事建筑设计或者相关业务3年以上的。

**Article 9** A person who meets any of the following conditions may apply to take the examination for Class 2 registered architects:  
(1) having a university undergraduate diploma or above in architecture or a related discipline, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 2 years;  
(2) having a college diploma or above in the specialty of architectural design technology or a related discipline, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 3 years;  
(3) having a four-year secondary specialized school diploma in the specialty of architectural design technology, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 5 years;  
(4) having a secondary specialized school diploma in a discipline related to architectural design technology, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 7 years;  
(5) having obtained the technical title of assistant engineer or above, and having been engaged in architectural design or related business for at least 3 years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本条例施行前已取得高级、中级技术职称的建筑设计人员，经所在单位推荐，可以按照注册建筑师全国统一考试办法的规定，免予部分科目的考试。

**Article 10** Architectural design personnel who have obtained senior or intermediate technical titles before these Regulations come into force may, upon recommendation by their work units, be exempted from taking examinations in some subjects in accordance with the provisions of the measures for the national unified examination for registered architects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　注册建筑师考试合格，取得相应的注册建筑师资格的，可以申请注册。

**Article 11** A person who has passed the examination for registered architects and obtained the corresponding qualification as a registered architect may apply for registration.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　一级注册建筑师的注册，由全国注册建筑师管理委员会负责；二级注册建筑师的注册，由省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会负责。

**Article 12** The registration of Class 1 registered architects shall be the responsibility of the National Registered Architect Administration Committee; the registration of Class 2 registered architects shall be the responsibility of the registered architect administration committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，不予注册：  
(一)不具有完全民事行为能力的；  
(二)因受刑事处罚，自刑罚执行完毕之日起至申请注册之日止不满5年的；  
(三)因在建筑设计或者相关业务中犯有错误受行政处罚或者撤职以上行政处分，自处罚、处分决定之日起至申请注册之日止不满2年的；  
(四)受吊销注册建筑师证书的行政处罚，自处罚决定之日起至申请注册之日止不满5年的；  
(五)有国务院规定不予注册的其他情形的。

**Article 13** Registration shall be denied under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) lacking full capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) where less than 5 years have elapsed from the date of completion of execution of a criminal punishment to the date of application for registration due to having received a criminal punishment;  
(3) where less than 2 years have elapsed from the date of the decision on the administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction of removal from office or above to the date of application for registration due to having committed errors in architectural design or related business resulting in an administrative penalty or disciplinary sanction of removal from office or above;  
(4) where less than 5 years have elapsed from the date of the decision on the administrative penalty of having the registered architect certificate revoked to the date of application for registration;  
(5) other circumstances under which registration shall be denied as prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　全国注册建筑师管理委员会和省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)的规定，决定不予注册的，应当自决定之日起15日内书面通知申请人；申请人有异议的，可以自收到通知之日起15日内向国务院建设行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门申请复议。

**Article 14** If the National Registered Architect Administration Committee or the registered architect administration committee of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government decides to deny registration in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of these Regulations, it shall notify the applicant in writing within 15 days from the date of the decision; if the applicant disagrees, it may apply for reconsideration to the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council or the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　全国注册建筑师管理委员会应当将准予注册的一级注册建筑师名单报国务院建设行政主管部门备案；省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会应当将准予注册的二级注册建筑师名单报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门备案。  
国务院建设行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门发现有关注册建筑师管理委员会的注册不符合本条例规定的，应当通知有关注册建筑师管理委员会撤销注册，收回注册建筑师证书。

**Article 15** The National Registered Architect Administration Committee shall report the list of Class 1 registered architects who have been approved for registration to the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council for the record; the registered architect administration committee of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall report the list of Class 2 registered architects who have been approved for registration to the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for the record.  
If the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council or the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government discovers that the registration by a relevant registered architect administration committee does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall notify the relevant registered architect administration committee to revoke the registration and retrieve the registered architect certificate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　准予注册的申请人，分别由全国注册建筑师管理委员会和省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会核发由国务院建设行政主管部门统一制作的一级注册建筑师证书或者二级注册建筑师证书。

**Article 16** For applicants who have been approved for registration, the National Registered Architect Administration Committee and the registered architect administration committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, respectively, issue a Class 1 registered architect certificate or a Class 2 registered architect certificate uniformly produced by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　注册建筑师注册的有效期为2年。有效期届满需要继续注册的，应当在期满前30日内办理注册手续。

**Article 17** The validity period of a registered architect's registration shall be two years. If renewal of registration is required upon expiration of the validity period, the formalities for renewal shall be completed within 30 days before the expiration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　已取得注册建筑师证书的人员，除[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第二款规定的情形外，注册后有下列情形之一的，由准予注册的全国注册建筑师管理委员会或者省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会撤销注册，收回注册建筑师证书：  
(一)完全丧失民事行为能力的；  
(二)受刑事处罚的；  
(三)因在建筑设计或者相关业务中犯有错误，受到行政处罚或者撤职以上行政处分的；  
(四)自行停止注册建筑师业务满2年的。  
被撤销注册的当事人对撤销注册、收回注册建筑师证书有异议的，可以自接到撤销注册、收回注册建筑师证书的通知之日起15日内向国务院建设行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门申请复议。

**Article 18** For a person who has obtained a registered architect certificate, except in the circumstances specified in the second paragraph of Article 15 of these Regulations, if any of the following circumstances occurs after registration, the National Registered Architect Administration Committee or the registered architect administration committee of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that approved the registration shall revoke the registration and retrieve the registered architect certificate:  
(1) having completely lost capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) having received a criminal punishment;  
(3) having received an administrative penalty or a disciplinary sanction of removal from office or above due to errors committed in architectural design or related business;  
(4) having voluntarily ceased the practice of registered architect for a full two years.  
If the party whose registration has been revoked disagrees with the revocation of registration and retrieval of the registered architect certificate, it may apply for reconsideration to the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council or the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of revocation of registration and retrieval of the registered architect certificate.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　被撤销注册的人员可以依照本条例的规定重新注册。

**Article 19** A person whose registration has been revoked may re-register in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

### 第三章　执业

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　注册建筑师的执业范围：  
(一)建筑设计；  
(二)建筑设计技术咨询；  
(三)建筑物调查与鉴定；  
(四)对本人主持设计的项目进行施工指导和监督；  
(五)国务院建设行政主管部门规定的其他业务。

**Article 20** The scope of practice of a registered architect shall include:  
(1) architectural design;  
(2) technical consulting for architectural design;  
(3) investigation and appraisal of buildings;  
(4) providing construction guidance and supervision for projects designed by oneself;  
(5) other business prescribed by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　注册建筑师执行业务，应当加入建筑设计单位。  
建筑设计单位的资质等级及其业务范围，由国务院建设行政主管部门规定。

**Article 21** A registered architect shall join an architectural design unit to practice.  
The qualification grades and business scope of architectural design units shall be prescribed by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　一级注册建筑师的执业范围不受建筑规模和工程复杂程度的限制。二级注册建筑师的执业范围不得超越国家规定的建筑规模和工程复杂程度。

**Article 22** The scope of practice of a Class 1 registered architect shall not be limited by building scale or project complexity. The scope of practice of a Class 2 registered architect shall not exceed the building scale and project complexity prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　注册建筑师执行业务，由建筑设计单位统一接受委托并统一收费。

**Article 23** When a registered architect practices, the architectural design unit shall accept the commission and collect fees uniformly.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因设计质量造成的经济损失，由建筑设计单位承担赔偿责任；建筑设计单位有权向签字的注册建筑师追偿。

**Article 24** Economic losses caused by design quality shall be borne by the architectural design unit as compensation liability; the architectural design unit shall have the right to seek recourse from the registered architect who signed the design.

### 第四章　权利和义务

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　注册建筑师有权以注册建筑师的名义执行注册建筑师业务。  
非注册建筑师不得以注册建筑师的名义执行注册建筑师业务。二级注册建筑师不得以一级注册建筑师的名义执行业务，也不得超越国家规定的二级注册建筑师的执业范围执行业务。

**Article 25** A registered architect shall have the right to practice as a registered architect in the name of a registered architect.  
A person who is not a registered architect shall not practice as a registered architect in the name of a registered architect. A Class 2 registered architect shall not practice in the name of a Class 1 registered architect, nor shall it practice beyond the scope of practice of a Class 2 registered architect prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家规定的一定跨度、跨径和高度以上的房屋建筑，应当由注册建筑师进行设计。

**Article 26** Building construction with a certain span, clear span, or height above the thresholds prescribed by the State shall be designed by a registered architect.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　任何单位和个人修改注册建筑师的设计图纸，应当征得该注册建筑师同意；但是，因特殊情况不能征得该注册建筑师同意的除外。

**Article 27** Any unit or individual that intends to modify the design drawings of a registered architect shall obtain the consent of that registered architect; however, this shall not apply where, due to special circumstances, consent cannot be obtained from that registered architect.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　注册建筑师应当履行下列义务：  
(一)遵守法律、法规和职业道德，维护社会公共利益；  
(二)保证建筑设计的质量，并在其负责的设计图纸上签字；  
(三)保守在执业中知悉的单位和个人的秘密；  
(四)不得同时受聘于二个以上建筑设计单位执行业务；  
(五)不得准许他人以本人名义执行业务。

**Article 28** A registered architect shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to comply with laws, regulations, and professional ethics, and to safeguard the public interests of the society;  
(2) to ensure the quality of architectural design and to sign the design drawings for which it is responsible;  
(3) to keep confidential the secrets of units and individuals learned during practice;  
(4) not to be employed simultaneously by two or more architectural design units to practice;  
(5) not to permit another person to practice in its name.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　以不正当手段取得注册建筑师考试合格资格或者注册建筑师证书的，由全国注册建筑师管理委员会或者省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会取消考试合格资格或者吊销注册建筑师证书；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 29** If a person obtains the qualification of having passed the examination for registered architects or a registered architect certificate by improper means, the National Registered Architect Administration Committee or the registered architect administration committee of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall cancel the qualification of having passed the examination or revoke the registered architect certificate; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未经注册擅自以注册建筑师名义从事注册建筑师业务的，由县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收违法所得，并可以处以违法所得5倍以下的罚款；造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 30** If a person, without being registered, engages in the practice of a registered architect in the name of a registered architect, the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　注册建筑师违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收违法所得，并可以处以违法所得5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以责令停止执行业务或者由全国注册建筑师管理委员会或者省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会吊销注册建筑师证书：  
(一)以个人名义承接注册建筑师业务、收取费用的；  
(二)同时受聘于二个以上建筑设计单位执行业务的；  
(三)在建筑设计或者相关业务中侵犯他人合法权益的；  
(四)准许他人以本人名义执行业务的；  
(五)二级注册建筑师以一级注册建筑师的名义执行业务或者超越国家规定的执业范围执行业务的。

**Article 31** If a registered architect violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, it may order the cessation of practice, or the National Registered Architect Administration Committee or the registered architect administration committee of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may revoke the registered architect certificate:  
(1) undertaking registered architect business and collecting fees in its own name;  
(2) being employed simultaneously by two or more architectural design units to practice;  
(3) infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others in architectural design or related business;  
(4) permitting another person to practice in its name;  
(5) a Class 2 registered architect practicing in the name of a Class 1 registered architect or practicing beyond the scope of practice prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　因建筑设计质量不合格发生重大责任事故，造成重大损失的，对该建筑设计负有直接责任的注册建筑师，由县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门责令停止执行业务；情节严重的，由全国注册建筑师管理委员会或者省、自治区、直辖市注册建筑师管理委员会吊销注册建筑师证书。

**Article 32** If a major liability accident occurs due to substandard architectural design quality, causing significant losses, the registered architect who bears direct responsibility for the architectural design shall be ordered by the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government at or above the county level to cease practice; if the circumstances are serious, the National Registered Architect Administration Committee or the registered architect administration committee of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke the registered architect certificate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，未经注册建筑师同意擅自修改其设计图纸的，由县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门责令纠正；造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 33** If a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, modifies the design drawings of a registered architect without the consent of that registered architect, the competent administrative department of construction under the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** If a violation of the provisions of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例所称建筑设计单位，包括专门从事建筑设计的工程设计单位和其他从事建筑设计的工程设计单位。

**Article 35** For the purposes of these Regulations, "architectural design unit" includes engineering design units specializing in architectural design and other engineering design units engaged in architectural design.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　外国人申请参加中国注册建筑师全国统一考试和注册以及外国建筑师申请在中国境内执行注册建筑师业务，按照对等原则办理。

**Article 36** Applications by foreigners to take the Chinese national unified examination for registered architects and for registration, and applications by foreign architects to practice as registered architects within the territory of China, shall be handled in accordance with the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
