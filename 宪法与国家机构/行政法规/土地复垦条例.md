# 土地复垦条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2011-03-05  
**生效日期**：2011-03-05

> (2011年2月22日国务院第145次常务会议通过　2011年3月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第592号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了落实十分珍惜、合理利用土地和切实保护耕地的基本国策，规范土地复垦活动，加强土地复垦管理，提高土地利用的社会效益、经济效益和生态效益，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称土地复垦，是指对生产建设活动和自然灾害损毁的土地，采取整治措施，使其达到可供利用状态的活动。 第三条　生产建设活动损毁的土地，按照“谁损毁，谁复垦”的原则，由生产建设单位或者个人(以下称土地复垦义务人)负责复垦。但是，由于历史原因无法确定土地复垦义务人的生产建设活动损毁的土地(以下称历史遗留损毁土地)，由县级以上人民政府负责组织复垦。 自然灾害损毁的土地，由县级以上人民政府负责组织复垦。 第四条　生产建设活动应当节约集约利用土地，不占或者少占耕地；对依法占用的土地应当采取有效措施，减少土地损毁面积，降低土地损毁程度。 土地复垦应当坚持科学规划、因地制宜、综合治理、经济可行、合理利用的原则。复垦的土地应当优先用于农业。 第五条　国务院国土资源主管部门负责全国土地复垦的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门负责本行政区域土地复垦的监督管理工作。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责做好土地复垦有关工作。 第六条　编制土地复垦方案、实施土地复垦工程、进行土地复垦验收等活动，应当遵守土地复垦国家标准；没有国家标准的，应当遵守土地复垦行业标准。 制定土地复垦国家标准和行业标准，应当根据土地损毁的类型、程度、自然地理条件和复垦的可行性等因素，分类确定不同类型损毁土地的复垦方式、目标和要求等。 第七条　县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门应当建立土地复垦监测制度，及时掌握本行政区域土地资源损毁和土地复垦效果等情况。 国务院国土资源主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国土资源主管部门应当建立健全土地复垦信息管理系统，收集、汇总和发布土地复垦数据信息。 第八条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当依据职责加强对土地复垦情况的监督检查。被检查的单位或者个人应当如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了落实十分珍惜、合理利用土地和切实保护耕地的基本国策，规范土地复垦活动，加强土地复垦管理，提高土地利用的社会效益、经济效益和生态效益，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purpose of implementing the basic state policy of cherishing and rational use of land and effective protection of cultivated land, regulating land reclamation activities, strengthening land reclamation management, and improving the social, economic and ecological benefits of land use, these Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称土地复垦，是指对生产建设活动和自然灾害损毁的土地，采取整治措施，使其达到可供利用状态的活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'land reclamation' means the activities of taking remediation measures on land damaged by production and construction activities or natural disasters so as to bring it to a state available for use.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　生产建设活动损毁的土地，按照“谁损毁，谁复垦”的原则，由生产建设单位或者个人(以下称土地复垦义务人)负责复垦。但是，由于历史原因无法确定土地复垦义务人的生产建设活动损毁的土地(以下称历史遗留损毁土地)，由县级以上人民政府负责组织复垦。  
自然灾害损毁的土地，由县级以上人民政府负责组织复垦。

**Article 3** Land damaged by production and construction activities shall be reclaimed by the production and construction units or individuals (hereinafter referred to as the land reclamation obligors) in accordance with the principle of 'who damages, who reclaims'. However, land damaged by production and construction activities for which the land reclamation obligor cannot be determined due to historical reasons (hereinafter referred to as historical leftover damaged land) shall be organized for reclamation by the people's government at or above the county level.  
Land damaged by natural disasters shall be organized for reclamation by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产建设活动应当节约集约利用土地，不占或者少占耕地；对依法占用的土地应当采取有效措施，减少土地损毁面积，降低土地损毁程度。  
土地复垦应当坚持科学规划、因地制宜、综合治理、经济可行、合理利用的原则。复垦的土地应当优先用于农业。

**Article 4** Production and construction activities shall economize and intensively use land, and shall not occupy or shall occupy less cultivated land; effective measures shall be taken for land occupied according to law to reduce the area and degree of land damage.  
Land reclamation shall adhere to the principles of scientific planning, adaptation to local conditions, comprehensive treatment, economic feasibility, and rational use. Reclaimed land shall be given priority for agricultural use.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院国土资源主管部门负责全国土地复垦的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门负责本行政区域土地复垦的监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责做好土地复垦有关工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of land and resources under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of land reclamation nationwide. The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of land reclamation within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective duties, carry out the work related to land reclamation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　编制土地复垦方案、实施土地复垦工程、进行土地复垦验收等活动，应当遵守土地复垦国家标准；没有国家标准的，应当遵守土地复垦行业标准。  
制定土地复垦国家标准和行业标准，应当根据土地损毁的类型、程度、自然地理条件和复垦的可行性等因素，分类确定不同类型损毁土地的复垦方式、目标和要求等。

**Article 6** Activities such as preparing land reclamation plans, implementing land reclamation projects, and carrying out land reclamation acceptance inspections shall comply with the national standards for land reclamation; where there are no national standards, they shall comply with the industrial standards for land reclamation.  
In formulating national standards and industrial standards for land reclamation, the methods, objectives and requirements for reclaiming different types of damaged land shall be determined by category based on factors such as the type, degree, natural geographical conditions of land damage and the feasibility of reclamation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门应当建立土地复垦监测制度，及时掌握本行政区域土地资源损毁和土地复垦效果等情况。  
国务院国土资源主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国土资源主管部门应当建立健全土地复垦信息管理系统，收集、汇总和发布土地复垦数据信息。

**Article 7** The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a land reclamation monitoring system to keep timely track of the situation of land resource damage and land reclamation effects within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent department of land and resources under the State Council and the competent departments of land and resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish and improve a land reclamation information management system to collect, summarize and release land reclamation data and information.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当依据职责加强对土地复垦情况的监督检查。被检查的单位或者个人应当如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。  
任何单位和个人不得扰乱、阻挠土地复垦工作，破坏土地复垦工程、设施和设备。

**Article 8** The competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, strengthen the supervision and inspection of land reclamation. The units or individuals being inspected shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide necessary materials.  
No unit or individual may disturb or obstruct land reclamation work, or damage land reclamation projects, facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持土地复垦科学研究和技术创新，推广先进的土地复垦技术。  
对在土地复垦工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府给予表彰。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports scientific research and technological innovation in land reclamation, and promotes advanced land reclamation technologies.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to land reclamation work shall be commended by the people's government at or above the county level.

### 第二章　生产建设活动损毁土地的复垦

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　下列损毁土地由土地复垦义务人负责复垦：  
(一)露天采矿、烧制砖瓦、挖沙取土等地表挖掘所损毁的土地；  
(二)地下采矿等造成地表塌陷的土地；  
(三)堆放采矿剥离物、废石、矿渣、粉煤灰等固体废弃物压占的土地；  
(四)能源、交通、水利等基础设施建设和其他生产建设活动临时占用所损毁的土地。

**Article 10** The following damaged land shall be reclaimed by the land reclamation obligors:  
(1) Land damaged by surface excavation such as open-pit mining, brick and tile firing, sand and soil excavation;  
(2) Land damaged by surface subsidence caused by underground mining, etc.;  
(3) Land occupied by solid waste such as mining overburden, waste rock, slag, fly ash, etc.;  
(4) Land damaged by temporary occupation for infrastructure construction such as energy, transportation, water conservancy and other production and construction activities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地复垦义务人应当按照土地复垦标准和国务院国土资源主管部门的规定编制土地复垦方案。

**Article 11** Land reclamation obligors shall prepare land reclamation plans in accordance with the land reclamation standards and the provisions of the competent department of land and resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地复垦方案应当包括下列内容：  
(一)项目概况和项目区土地利用状况；  
(二)损毁土地的分析预测和土地复垦的可行性评价；  
(三)土地复垦的目标任务；  
(四)土地复垦应当达到的质量要求和采取的措施；  
(五)土地复垦工程和投资估(概)算；  
(六)土地复垦费用的安排；  
(七)土地复垦工作计划与进度安排；  
(八)国务院国土资源主管部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 12** A land reclamation plan shall include the following contents:  
(1) An overview of the project and the land use status in the project area;  
(2) Analysis and prediction of damaged land and feasibility assessment of land reclamation;  
(3) Objectives and tasks of land reclamation;  
(4) Quality requirements to be met by land reclamation and measures to be taken;  
(5) Estimates of land reclamation works and investment;  
(6) Arrangement of land reclamation costs;  
(7) Work plan and schedule for land reclamation;  
(8) Other contents specified by the competent department of land and resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　土地复垦义务人应当在办理建设用地申请或者采矿权申请手续时，随有关报批材料报送土地复垦方案。  
土地复垦义务人未编制土地复垦方案或者土地复垦方案不符合要求的，有批准权的人民政府不得批准建设用地，有批准权的国土资源主管部门不得颁发采矿许可证。  
本条例施行前已经办理建设用地手续或者领取采矿许可证，本条例施行后继续从事生产建设活动造成土地损毁的，土地复垦义务人应当按照国务院国土资源主管部门的规定补充编制土地复垦方案。

**Article 13** Land reclamation obligors shall submit the land reclamation plan together with relevant approval materials when applying for land for construction purposes or for mining rights.  
Where the land reclamation obligor fails to prepare a land reclamation plan or the land reclamation plan does not meet the requirements, the people's government with approval authority shall not approve the land for construction purposes, and the competent department of land and resources with approval authority shall not issue a mining license.  
Where the formalities for land for construction purposes have been completed or a mining license has been obtained before these Regulations come into force, and production and construction activities continue after these Regulations come into force causing land damage, the land reclamation obligor shall supplement the preparation of a land reclamation plan in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of land and resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　土地复垦义务人应当按照土地复垦方案开展土地复垦工作。矿山企业还应当对土地损毁情况进行动态监测和评价。  
生产建设周期长、需要分阶段实施复垦的，土地复垦义务人应当对土地复垦工作与生产建设活动统一规划、统筹实施，根据生产建设进度确定各阶段土地复垦的目标任务、工程规划设计、费用安排、工程实施进度和完成期限等。

**Article 14** Land reclamation obligors shall carry out land reclamation work in accordance with the land reclamation plan. Mining enterprises shall also conduct dynamic monitoring and evaluation of land damage.  
Where the production and construction cycle is long and reclamation needs to be implemented in stages, the land reclamation obligor shall uniformly plan and coordinate the implementation of land reclamation work and production and construction activities, and determine the objectives, tasks, engineering planning and design, cost arrangement, project implementation progress and completion deadlines for each stage according to the progress of production and construction.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　土地复垦义务人应当将土地复垦费用列入生产成本或者建设项目总投资。

**Article 15** Land reclamation obligors shall include land reclamation costs in production costs or total investment in construction projects.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　土地复垦义务人应当建立土地复垦质量控制制度，遵守土地复垦标准和环境保护标准，保护土壤质量与生态环境，避免污染土壤和地下水。  
土地复垦义务人应当首先对拟损毁的耕地、林地、牧草地进行表土剥离，剥离的表土用于被损毁土地的复垦。  
禁止将重金属污染物或者其他有毒有害物质用作回填或者充填材料。受重金属污染物或者其他有毒有害物质污染的土地复垦后，达不到国家有关标准的，不得用于种植食用农作物。

**Article 16** Land reclamation obligors shall establish a land reclamation quality control system, comply with land reclamation standards and environmental protection standards, protect soil quality and ecological environment, and avoid polluting soil and groundwater.  
Land reclamation obligors shall first strip the topsoil of cultivated land, forestland, and grassland to be damaged, and the stripped topsoil shall be used for the reclamation of the damaged land.  
It is prohibited to use heavy metal pollutants or other toxic and hazardous substances as backfill or filling materials. Where land contaminated by heavy metal pollutants or other toxic and hazardous substances, after reclamation, fails to meet the relevant national standards, it shall not be used for planting edible crops.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　土地复垦义务人应当于每年12月31日前向县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门报告当年的土地损毁情况、土地复垦费用使用情况以及土地复垦工程实施情况。  
县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门应当加强对土地复垦义务人使用土地复垦费用和实施土地复垦工程的监督。

**Article 17** Land reclamation obligors shall, before December 31 of each year, report to the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level on the land damage situation, the use of land reclamation costs, and the implementation of land reclamation projects for that year.  
The competent departments of land and resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision over the use of land reclamation costs and the implementation of land reclamation projects by land reclamation obligors.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　土地复垦义务人不复垦，或者复垦验收中经整改仍不合格的，应当缴纳土地复垦费，由有关国土资源主管部门代为组织复垦。  
确定土地复垦费的数额，应当综合考虑损毁前的土地类型、实际损毁面积、损毁程度、复垦标准、复垦用途和完成复垦任务所需的工程量等因素。土地复垦费的具体征收使用管理办法，由国务院财政、价格主管部门商国务院有关部门制定。  
土地复垦义务人缴纳的土地复垦费专项用于土地复垦。任何单位和个人不得截留、挤占、挪用。

**Article 18** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to reclaim, or the reclamation still fails to meet the requirements after rectification during the acceptance inspection, the land reclamation obligor shall pay a land reclamation fee, and the relevant competent department of land and resources shall organize the reclamation on its behalf.  
The amount of the land reclamation fee shall be determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the land type before damage, the actual damaged area, the degree of damage, the reclamation standards, the reclamation purpose, and the amount of work required to complete the reclamation task. The specific measures for the collection, use and management of land reclamation fees shall be formulated by the competent departments of finance and pricing under the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments under the State Council.  
The land reclamation fee paid by the land reclamation obligor shall be used exclusively for land reclamation. No unit or individual may intercept, misappropriate or divert such funds.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　土地复垦义务人对在生产建设活动中损毁的由其他单位或者个人使用的国有土地或者农民集体所有的土地，除负责复垦外，还应当向遭受损失的单位或者个人支付损失补偿费。  
损失补偿费由土地复垦义务人与遭受损失的单位或者个人按照造成的实际损失协商确定；协商不成的，可以向土地所在地人民政府国土资源主管部门申请调解或者依法向人民法院提起民事诉讼。

**Article 19** Where land reclamation obligors damage state-owned land used by other units or individuals or land collectively owned by farmers during production and construction activities, they shall, in addition to being responsible for reclamation, pay compensation for losses to the units or individuals that have suffered losses.  
The compensation for losses shall be determined through negotiation between the land reclamation obligor and the units or individuals that have suffered losses according to the actual losses caused; if negotiation fails, they may apply to the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at the location of the land for mediation, or initiate a civil action in a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　土地复垦义务人不依法履行土地复垦义务的，在申请新的建设用地时，有批准权的人民政府不得批准；在申请新的采矿许可证或者申请采矿许可证延续、变更、注销时，有批准权的国土资源主管部门不得批准。

**Article 20** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to perform the obligation of land reclamation according to law, the people's government with approval authority shall not approve an application for new land for construction purposes; the competent department of land and resources with approval authority shall not approve an application for a new mining license or for the renewal, alteration, or cancellation of a mining license.

### 第三章　历史遗留损毁土地和自然灾害损毁土地的复垦

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当对历史遗留损毁土地和自然灾害损毁土地进行调查评价。

**Article 21** The competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct investigation and assessment of historical leftover damaged land and land damaged by natural disasters.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当在调查评价的基础上，根据土地利用总体规划编制土地复垦专项规划，确定复垦的重点区域以及复垦的目标任务和要求，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 22** On the basis of investigation and assessment, the competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the general plan for land use, formulate special plans for land reclamation, determine the key areas for reclamation and the objectives, tasks and requirements for reclamation, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval before organizing implementation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对历史遗留损毁土地和自然灾害损毁土地，县级以上人民政府应当投入资金进行复垦，或者按照“谁投资，谁受益”的原则，吸引社会投资进行复垦。土地权利人明确的，可以采取扶持、优惠措施，鼓励土地权利人自行复垦。

**Article 23** For historical leftover damaged land and land damaged by natural disasters, the people's government at or above the county level shall invest funds for reclamation, or attract social investment in reclamation in accordance with the principle of 'who invests, who benefits'. Where the right holder of the land is identified, support and preferential measures may be adopted to encourage the land right holder to reclaim the land on their own.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家对历史遗留损毁土地和自然灾害损毁土地的复垦按项目实施管理。  
县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门应当根据土地复垦专项规划和年度土地复垦资金安排情况确定年度复垦项目。

**Article 24** The State manages the reclamation of historical leftover damaged land and land damaged by natural disasters on a project basis.  
The competent departments of land and resources of people's governments at or above the county level shall determine annual reclamation projects based on the special plan for land reclamation and the annual allocation of land reclamation funds.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　政府投资进行复垦的，负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门应当组织编制土地复垦项目设计书，明确复垦项目的位置、面积、目标任务、工程规划设计、实施进度及完成期限等。  
土地权利人自行复垦或者社会投资进行复垦的，土地权利人或者投资单位、个人应当组织编制土地复垦项目设计书，并报负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门审查同意后实施。

**Article 25** Where reclamation is carried out with government investment, the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project shall organize the preparation of a land reclamation project design document, specifying the location, area, objectives, tasks, engineering planning and design, implementation schedule and completion deadlines of the reclamation project.  
Where the land right holder reclaims on its own or reclamation is carried out with social investment, the land right holder or the investing unit or individual shall organize the preparation of a land reclamation project design document, and submit it to the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project for review and approval before implementation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　政府投资进行复垦的，有关国土资源主管部门应当依照招标投标法律法规的规定，通过公开招标的方式确定土地复垦项目的施工单位。  
土地权利人自行复垦或者社会投资进行复垦的，土地复垦项目的施工单位由土地权利人或者投资单位、个人依法自行确定。

**Article 26** Where reclamation is carried out with government investment, the relevant competent department of land and resources shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations on tendering and bidding, determine the construction unit for the land reclamation project through public tendering.  
Where the land right holder reclaims on its own or reclamation is carried out with social investment, the construction unit for the land reclamation project shall be determined by the land right holder or the investing unit or individual on its own according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　土地复垦项目的施工单位应当按照土地复垦项目设计书进行复垦。  
负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门应当健全项目管理制度，加强项目实施中的指导、管理和监督。

**Article 27** The construction unit of a land reclamation project shall carry out reclamation in accordance with the land reclamation project design document.  
The competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project shall improve the project management system and strengthen guidance, management and supervision during project implementation.

### 第四章　土地复垦验收

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　土地复垦义务人按照土地复垦方案的要求完成土地复垦任务后，应当按照国务院国土资源主管部门的规定向所在地县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门申请验收，接到申请的国土资源主管部门应当会同同级农业、林业、环境保护等有关部门进行验收。  
进行土地复垦验收，应当邀请有关专家进行现场踏勘，查验复垦后的土地是否符合土地复垦标准以及土地复垦方案的要求，核实复垦后的土地类型、面积和质量等情况，并将初步验收结果公告，听取相关权利人的意见。相关权利人对土地复垦完成情况提出异议的，国土资源主管部门应当会同有关部门进一步核查，并将核查情况向相关权利人反馈；情况属实的，应当向土地复垦义务人提出整改意见。

**Article 28** After completing the land reclamation tasks in accordance with the requirements of the land reclamation plan, the land reclamation obligor shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of land and resources under the State Council, apply to the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level for acceptance inspection. The competent department of land and resources that receives the application shall, together with the relevant departments such as agriculture, forestry and environmental protection at the same level, conduct the acceptance inspection.  
When conducting the acceptance inspection of land reclamation, experts shall be invited to conduct a site survey, inspect whether the reclaimed land meets the land reclamation standards and the requirements of the land reclamation plan, verify the type, area and quality of the reclaimed land, and publish the preliminary acceptance inspection results to solicit opinions from the relevant right holders. Where the relevant right holders raise objections to the completion of land reclamation, the competent department of land and resources shall, together with the relevant departments, further verify and feed back the verification results to the relevant right holders; if the situation is true, it shall propose rectification opinions to the land reclamation obligor.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　负责组织验收的国土资源主管部门应当会同有关部门在接到土地复垦验收申请之日起60个工作日内完成验收，经验收合格的，向土地复垦义务人出具验收合格确认书；经验收不合格的，向土地复垦义务人出具书面整改意见，列明需要整改的事项，由土地复垦义务人整改完成后重新申请验收。

**Article 29** The competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the acceptance inspection shall, together with the relevant departments, complete the acceptance inspection within 60 working days from the date of receiving the application for land reclamation acceptance inspection. If the inspection is passed, it shall issue a certificate of acceptance inspection to the land reclamation obligor; if the inspection fails, it shall issue a written rectification opinion to the land reclamation obligor, listing the matters requiring rectification, and the land reclamation obligor shall reapply for acceptance inspection after completing the rectification.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　政府投资的土地复垦项目竣工后，负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门应当依照[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)第二款的规定进行初步验收。初步验收完成后，负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门应当按照国务院国土资源主管部门的规定向上级人民政府国土资源主管部门申请最终验收。上级人民政府国土资源主管部门应当会同有关部门及时组织验收。  
土地权利人自行复垦或者社会投资进行复垦的土地复垦项目竣工后，由负责组织实施土地复垦项目的国土资源主管部门会同有关部门进行验收。

**Article 30** After the completion of a land reclamation project invested by the government, the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project shall conduct a preliminary acceptance inspection in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 28 of these Regulations. After the preliminary acceptance inspection is completed, the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of land and resources under the State Council, apply to the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at a higher level for final acceptance inspection. The competent department of land and resources of the people's government at a higher level shall, together with the relevant departments, promptly organize the final acceptance inspection.  
After the completion of a land reclamation project reclaimed by the land right holder on its own or with social investment, the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the implementation of the land reclamation project shall, together with the relevant departments, conduct the acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　复垦为农用地的，负责组织验收的国土资源主管部门应当会同有关部门在验收合格后的5年内对土地复垦效果进行跟踪评价，并提出改善土地质量的建议和措施。

**Article 31** Where land is reclaimed for agricultural use, the competent department of land and resources responsible for organizing the acceptance inspection shall, together with the relevant departments, conduct a follow-up evaluation of the land reclamation effects within five years after the acceptance inspection is passed, and propose recommendations and measures for improving land quality.

### 第五章　土地复垦激励措施

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　土地复垦义务人在规定的期限内将生产建设活动损毁的耕地、林地、牧草地等农用地复垦恢复原状的，依照国家有关税收法律法规的规定退还已经缴纳的耕地占用税。

**Article 32** Where a land reclamation obligor reclaims and restores to its original condition the cultivated land, forestland, grassland or other agricultural land damaged by production and construction activities within the prescribed period, the cultivated land occupation tax that has been paid shall be refunded in accordance with the provisions of the relevant tax laws and regulations of the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　社会投资复垦的历史遗留损毁土地或者自然灾害损毁土地，属于无使用权人的国有土地的，经县级以上人民政府依法批准，可以确定给投资单位或者个人长期从事种植业、林业、畜牧业或者渔业生产。  
社会投资复垦的历史遗留损毁土地或者自然灾害损毁土地，属于农民集体所有土地或者有使用权人的国有土地的，有关国土资源主管部门应当组织投资单位或者个人与土地权利人签订土地复垦协议，明确复垦的目标任务以及复垦后的土地使用和收益分配。

**Article 33** Where historical leftover damaged land or land damaged by natural disasters reclaimed with social investment is state-owned land without a right holder, it may, upon approval by the people's government at or above the county level according to law, be allocated to the investing unit or individual for long-term use in planting, forestry, animal husbandry or fishery production.  
Where historical leftover damaged land or land damaged by natural disasters reclaimed with social investment is land collectively owned by farmers or state-owned land with a right holder, the relevant competent department of land and resources shall organize the investing unit or individual and the land right holder to sign a land reclamation agreement, specifying the objectives and tasks of reclamation and the use of and distribution of proceeds from the reclaimed land.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　历史遗留损毁和自然灾害损毁的国有土地的使用权人，以及历史遗留损毁和自然灾害损毁的农民集体所有土地的所有权人、使用权人，自行将损毁土地复垦为耕地的，由县级以上地方人民政府给予补贴。

**Article 34** Where the right holder of state-owned land damaged by historical leftover or natural disasters, or the owner or right holder of land collectively owned by farmers damaged by historical leftover or natural disasters, reclaims the damaged land into cultivated land on its own, the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide a subsidy.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府将历史遗留损毁和自然灾害损毁的建设用地复垦为耕地的，按照国家有关规定可以作为本省、自治区、直辖市内进行非农建设占用耕地时的补充耕地指标。

**Article 35** Where a local people's government at or above the county level reclaims construction land damaged by historical leftover or natural disasters into cultivated land, such land may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, serve as a supplementary cultivated land index for the occupation of cultivated land for non-agricultural construction within the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　负有土地复垦监督管理职责的部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反本条例规定批准建设用地或者批准采矿许可证及采矿许可证的延续、变更、注销的；  
(二)截留、挤占、挪用土地复垦费的；  
(三)在土地复垦验收中弄虚作假的；  
(四)不依法履行监督管理职责或者对发现的违反本条例的行为不依法查处的；  
(五)在审查土地复垦方案、实施土地复垦项目、组织土地复垦验收以及实施监督检查过程中，索取、收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
(六)其他徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守行为。

**Article 36** Where a department and its staff members responsible for the supervision and administration of land reclamation commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Approving land for construction purposes or approving a mining license and the renewal, alteration, or cancellation of a mining license in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Intercepting, misappropriating or diverting land reclamation fees;  
(3) Engaging in fraud in the acceptance inspection of land reclamation;  
(4) Failing to perform supervision and administration duties according to law or failing to investigate and deal with violations of these Regulations that are discovered according to law;  
(5) Extorting, accepting property or seeking other benefits from others during the review of land reclamation plans, implementation of land reclamation projects, organization of land reclamation acceptance inspections, and carrying out of supervision and inspections;  
(6) Other acts of engaging in malpractice for personal gain, abusing power, or neglecting duties.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例施行前已经办理建设用地手续或者领取采矿许可证，本条例施行后继续从事生产建设活动造成土地损毁的土地复垦义务人未按照规定补充编制土地复垦方案的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Where a land reclamation obligor that has completed the formalities for land for construction purposes or obtained a mining license before these Regulations come into force and continues production and construction activities after these Regulations come into force causing land damage, fails to supplement the preparation of a land reclamation plan in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　土地复垦义务人未按照规定将土地复垦费用列入生产成本或者建设项目总投资的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 38** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to include land reclamation costs in production costs or total investment in construction projects in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　土地复垦义务人未按照规定对拟损毁的耕地、林地、牧草地进行表土剥离，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，按照应当进行表土剥离的土地面积处每公顷1万元的罚款。

**Article 39** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to strip the topsoil of cultivated land, forestland, or grassland to be damaged in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of 10,000 yuan per hectare shall be imposed based on the area of land on which topsoil stripping should have been carried out.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　土地复垦义务人将重金属污染物或者其他有毒有害物质用作回填或者充填材料的，由县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期采取治理措施，消除污染，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不采取治理措施的，环境保护主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 40** Where a land reclamation obligor uses heavy metal pollutants or other toxic and hazardous substances as backfill or filling materials, the competent department of environmental protection of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease the illegal act, take remediation measures within a prescribed time limit to eliminate pollution, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if it fails to take remediation measures within the time limit, the competent department of environmental protection may designate a unit with remediation capability to carry out remediation on its behalf, and the required costs shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　土地复垦义务人未按照规定报告土地损毁情况、土地复垦费用使用情况或者土地复垦工程实施情况的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to report the land damage situation, the use of land reclamation costs, or the implementation of land reclamation projects in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　土地复垦义务人依照本条例规定应当缴纳土地复垦费而不缴纳的，由县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期缴纳；逾期不缴纳的，处应缴纳土地复垦费1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，土地复垦义务人为矿山企业的，由颁发采矿许可证的机关吊销采矿许可证。

**Article 42** Where a land reclamation obligor fails to pay the land reclamation fee that shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department of land and resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to pay within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to pay within the time limit, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the amount of land reclamation fee payable shall be imposed, and if the land reclamation obligor is a mining enterprise, the mining license issued by the issuing authority shall be revoked.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　土地复垦义务人拒绝、阻碍国土资源主管部门监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由国土资源主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；有关责任人员构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以治安管理处罚；有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
破坏土地复垦工程、设施和设备，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where a land reclamation obligor refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection by the competent department of land and resources, or engages in fraud during such supervision and inspection, the competent department of land and resources shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the relevant liable person's act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the relevant liable person's act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the act of damaging land reclamation projects, facilities and equipment constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。1988年11月8日国务院发布的《土地复垦规定》同时废止。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisions on Land Reclamation promulgated by the State Council on November 8, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.
