# 劳动保障监察条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2004-11-01  
**生效日期**：2004-12-01

> (2004年10月26日国务院第68次常务会议通过　2004年11月1日中华人民共和国国务院令第423号公布　自2004年12月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了贯彻实施劳动和社会保障(以下称劳动保障)法律、法规和规章，规范劳动保障监察工作，维护劳动者的合法权益，根据劳动法和有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　对企业和个体工商户(以下称用人单位)进行劳动保障监察，适用本条例。 对职业介绍机构、职业技能培训机构和职业技能考核鉴定机构进行劳动保障监察，依照本条例执行。 第三条　国务院劳动保障行政部门主管全国的劳动保障监察工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府劳动保障行政部门主管本行政区域内的劳动保障监察工作。 县级以上各级人民政府有关部门根据各自职责，支持、协助劳动保障行政部门的劳动保障监察工作。 第四条　县级、设区的市级人民政府劳动保障行政部门可以委托符合监察执法条件的组织实施劳动保障监察。 劳动保障行政部门和受委托实施劳动保障监察的组织中的劳动保障监察员应当经过相应的考核或者考试录用。 劳动保障监察证件由国务院劳动保障行政部门监制。 第五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强劳动保障监察工作。劳动保障监察所需经费列入本级财政预算。 第六条　用人单位应当遵守劳动保障法律、法规和规章，接受并配合劳动保障监察。 第七条　各级工会依法维护劳动者的合法权益，对用人单位遵守劳动保障法律、法规和规章的情况进行监督。 劳动保障行政部门在劳动保障监察工作中应当注意听取工会组织的意见和建议。 第八条　劳动保障监察遵循公正、公开、高效、便民的原则。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了贯彻实施劳动和社会保障(以下称劳动保障)法律、法规和规章，规范劳动保障监察工作，维护劳动者的合法权益，根据劳动法和有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Labor Law and relevant laws in order to implement the laws, regulations and rules on labor and social security (hereinafter referred to as labor security), standardize the supervision and inspection of labor security, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of workers.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对企业和个体工商户(以下称用人单位)进行劳动保障监察，适用本条例。  
对职业介绍机构、职业技能培训机构和职业技能考核鉴定机构进行劳动保障监察，依照本条例执行。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to the supervision and inspection of labor security over enterprises and individual industrial and commercial households (hereinafter referred to as employing units).  
The supervision and inspection of labor security over job placement agencies, vocational skill training institutions and vocational skill appraisal institutions shall be carried out in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院劳动保障行政部门主管全国的劳动保障监察工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府劳动保障行政部门主管本行政区域内的劳动保障监察工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府有关部门根据各自职责，支持、协助劳动保障行政部门的劳动保障监察工作。

**Article 3** The labor security administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and inspection of labor security nationwide. The labor security administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the supervision and inspection of labor security within their respective administrative areas.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, support and assist the labor security administrative departments in carrying out the supervision and inspection of labor security.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级、设区的市级人民政府劳动保障行政部门可以委托符合监察执法条件的组织实施劳动保障监察。  
劳动保障行政部门和受委托实施劳动保障监察的组织中的劳动保障监察员应当经过相应的考核或者考试录用。  
劳动保障监察证件由国务院劳动保障行政部门监制。

**Article 4** The labor security administrative departments of the people's governments at the county level or at the level of a city divided into districts may entrust organizations that meet the conditions for law enforcement with the implementation of the supervision and inspection of labor security.  
The labor security inspectors in the labor security administrative departments and in the organizations entrusted with the implementation of the supervision and inspection of labor security shall be recruited after passing corresponding examinations or tests.  
The certificates for labor security inspectors shall be produced under the supervision of the labor security administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强劳动保障监察工作。劳动保障监察所需经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 5** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of labor security. The necessary funds for the supervision and inspection of labor security shall be included in the fiscal budget at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　用人单位应当遵守劳动保障法律、法规和规章，接受并配合劳动保障监察。

**Article 6** Employing units shall observe the laws, regulations and rules on labor security, and accept and cooperate with the supervision and inspection of labor security.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级工会依法维护劳动者的合法权益，对用人单位遵守劳动保障法律、法规和规章的情况进行监督。  
劳动保障行政部门在劳动保障监察工作中应当注意听取工会组织的意见和建议。

**Article 7** Trade unions at all levels shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of workers according to law, and supervise the observance of the laws, regulations and rules on labor security by employing units.  
The labor security administrative departments, in the course of carrying out the supervision and inspection of labor security, shall pay attention to listening to the opinions and suggestions of trade union organizations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　劳动保障监察遵循公正、公开、高效、便民的原则。  
实施劳动保障监察，坚持教育与处罚相结合，接受社会监督。

**Article 8** The supervision and inspection of labor security shall follow the principles of fairness, openness, efficiency and convenience for the people.  
In implementing the supervision and inspection of labor security, the combination of education with penalties shall be adhered to, and social supervision shall be accepted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何组织或者个人对违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为，有权向劳动保障行政部门举报。  
劳动者认为用人单位侵犯其劳动保障合法权益的，有权向劳动保障行政部门投诉。  
劳动保障行政部门应当为举报人保密；对举报属实，为查处重大违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为提供主要线索和证据的举报人，给予奖励。

**Article 9** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report to the labor security administrative department any act that violates the laws, regulations or rules on labor security.  
Any worker who considers that an employing unit has infringed upon his or her legitimate rights and interests in labor security shall have the right to complain to the labor security administrative department.  
The labor security administrative department shall keep the informant confidential; and shall give rewards to those informants whose reports are verified and who provide main clues and evidence for investigating and punishing major acts that violate the laws, regulations or rules on labor security.

### 第二章　劳动保障监察职责

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　劳动保障行政部门实施劳动保障监察，履行下列职责：  
(一)宣传劳动保障法律、法规和规章，督促用人单位贯彻执行；  
(二)检查用人单位遵守劳动保障法律、法规和规章的情况；  
(三)受理对违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为的举报、投诉；  
(四)依法纠正和查处违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为。

**Article 10** In implementing the supervision and inspection of labor security, the labor security administrative department shall perform the following duties:  
(1) publicizing the laws, regulations and rules on labor security and urging the employing units to implement them;  
(2) inspecting the employing units for their compliance with the laws, regulations and rules on labor security;  
(3) accepting reports and complaints against acts that violate the laws, regulations or rules on labor security;  
(4) correcting and investigating, according to law, acts that violate the laws, regulations or rules on labor security.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　劳动保障行政部门对下列事项实施劳动保障监察：  
(一)用人单位制定内部劳动保障规章制度的情况；  
(二)用人单位与劳动者订立劳动合同的情况；  
(三)用人单位遵守禁止使用童工规定的情况；  
(四)用人单位遵守女职工和未成年工特殊劳动保护规定的情况；  
(五)用人单位遵守工作时间和休息休假规定的情况；  
(六)用人单位支付劳动者工资和执行最低工资标准的情况；  
(七)用人单位参加各项社会保险和缴纳社会保险费的情况；  
(八)职业介绍机构、职业技能培训机构和职业技能考核鉴定机构遵守国家有关职业介绍、职业技能培训和职业技能考核鉴定的规定的情况；  
(九)法律、法规规定的其他劳动保障监察事项。

**Article 11** The labor security administrative department shall implement the supervision and inspection of labor security over the following matters:  
(1) the formulation of internal labor security rules and regulations by employing units;  
(2) the conclusion of labor contracts between employing units and workers;  
(3) the observation of the provisions on prohibiting the use of child labor by employing units;  
(4) the observation of the provisions on special labor protection for female workers and underage workers by employing units;  
(5) the observation of the provisions on working hours, rest and vacation by employing units;  
(6) the payment of wages by employing units and the implementation of the minimum wage standard;  
(7) the participation of employing units in various social insurance and the payment of social insurance premiums;  
(8) the observation by job placement agencies, vocational skill training institutions and vocational skill appraisal institutions of the state provisions on job placement, vocational skill training and vocational skill appraisal;  
(9) other matters concerning labor security supervision and inspection as prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　劳动保障监察员依法履行劳动保障监察职责，受法律保护。  
劳动保障监察员应当忠于职守，秉公执法，勤政廉洁，保守秘密。  
任何组织或者个人对劳动保障监察员的违法违纪行为，有权向劳动保障行政部门或者有关机关检举、控告。

**Article 12** Labor security inspectors shall perform their duties in accordance with law and shall be protected by law.  
Labor security inspectors shall be devoted to their duties, enforce the law impartially, be diligent and honest, and keep secrets.  
Any organization or individual shall have the right to report or accuse the labor security inspectors of illegal or disciplinary acts to the labor security administrative department or the relevant authority.

### 第三章　劳动保障监察的实施

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对用人单位的劳动保障监察，由用人单位用工所在地的县级或者设区的市级劳动保障行政部门管辖。  
上级劳动保障行政部门根据工作需要，可以调查处理下级劳动保障行政部门管辖的案件。劳动保障行政部门对劳动保障监察管辖发生争议的，报请共同的上一级劳动保障行政部门指定管辖。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以对劳动保障监察的管辖制定具体办法。

**Article 13** The supervision and inspection of labor security over an employing unit shall be under the jurisdiction of the labor security administrative department at the county level or at the level of a city divided into districts in the place where the employing unit operates.  
The labor security administrative department at a higher level may, when necessary, investigate and handle a case that falls under the jurisdiction of a lower level labor security administrative department. If a dispute arises over the jurisdiction of labor security supervision and inspection, the matter shall be submitted to the common higher-level labor security administrative department for designation of jurisdiction.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate specific measures for the jurisdiction of labor security supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　劳动保障监察以日常巡视检查、审查用人单位按照要求报送的书面材料以及接受举报投诉等形式进行。  
劳动保障行政部门认为用人单位有违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为，需要进行调查处理的，应当及时立案。  
劳动保障行政部门或者受委托实施劳动保障监察的组织应当设立举报、投诉信箱和电话。  
对因违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为引起的群体性事件，劳动保障行政部门应当根据应急预案，迅速会同有关部门处理。

**Article 14** The supervision and inspection of labor security shall be carried out in the forms of routine inspection, examination of written materials submitted by employing units as required, and acceptance of reports and complaints.  
If the labor security administrative department considers that an employing unit has committed an act violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security and that investigation and treatment are necessary, it shall promptly file a case.  
The labor security administrative department or the organization entrusted with the implementation of the supervision and inspection of labor security shall set up mailboxes and telephone lines for reports and complaints.  
In the case of a mass incident caused by an act violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security, the labor security administrative department shall, in accordance with the emergency plan, promptly join with the relevant departments in handling the matter.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　劳动保障行政部门实施劳动保障监察，有权采取下列调查、检查措施：  
(一)进入用人单位的劳动场所进行检查；  
(二)就调查、检查事项询问有关人员；  
(三)要求用人单位提供与调查、检查事项相关的文件资料，并作出解释和说明，必要时可以发出调查询问书；  
(四)采取记录、录音、录像、照像或者复制等方式收集有关情况和资料；  
(五)委托会计师事务所对用人单位工资支付、缴纳社会保险费的情况进行审计；  
(六)法律、法规规定可以由劳动保障行政部门采取的其他调查、检查措施。  
劳动保障行政部门对事实清楚、证据确凿、可以当场处理的违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为有权当场予以纠正。

**Article 15** In implementing the supervision and inspection of labor security, the labor security administrative department shall have the power to take the following investigation and inspection measures:  
(1) entering the workplace of the employing unit for inspection;  
(2) inquiring of relevant persons about the matters under investigation or inspection;  
(3) requiring the employing unit to provide documents and materials related to the matters under investigation or inspection, and to make explanations and clarifications; and when necessary, issuing a written inquiry;  
(4) collecting relevant information and materials by means of recording, audio recording, video recording, photographing or reproducing;  
(5) entrusting an accounting firm with auditing the payment of wages and the payment of social insurance premiums by the employing unit; and  
(6) other investigation and inspection measures that may be taken by the labor security administrative department as prescribed by laws and regulations.  
Where an act violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security is clear in fact, conclusive in evidence, and may be dealt with on the spot, the labor security administrative department shall have the power to correct it on the spot.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　劳动保障监察员进行调查、检查，不得少于2人，并应当佩戴劳动保障监察标志、出示劳动保障监察证件。  
劳动保障监察员办理的劳动保障监察事项与本人或者其近亲属有直接利害关系的，应当回避。

**Article 16** For investigation and inspection, there shall be no less than two labor security inspectors, who shall wear the labor security inspection badge and present their labor security inspection certificates.  
If a labor security inspector has a direct interest in the matter under supervision and inspection, either personally or with his or her close relatives, the inspector shall withdraw from the case.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　劳动保障行政部门对违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为的调查，应当自立案之日起60个工作日内完成；对情况复杂的，经劳动保障行政部门负责人批准，可以延长30个工作日。

**Article 17** The investigation by the labor security administrative department into an act violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security shall be completed within 60 working days from the date of filing the case; if the circumstances are complex, the period may be extended by 30 working days upon the approval of the person in charge of the labor security administrative department.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　劳动保障行政部门对违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为，根据调查、检查的结果，作出以下处理：  
(一)对依法应当受到行政处罚的，依法作出行政处罚决定；  
(二)对应当改正未改正的，依法责令改正或者作出相应的行政处理决定；  
(三)对情节轻微且已改正的，撤销立案。  
发现违法案件不属于劳动保障监察事项的，应当及时移送有关部门处理；涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法移送司法机关。

**Article 18** The labor security administrative department shall, based on the results of investigation and inspection, make the following dispositions for acts that violate the laws, regulations or rules on labor security:  
(1) if an administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law, make a decision on administrative penalty according to law;  
(2) if something that shall be corrected has not been corrected, order the correction or make a corresponding administrative disposition decision according to law;  
(3) if the circumstances are minor and have been corrected, close the case.  
If it is found that an illegal case does not fall within the matters of labor security supervision and inspection, the case shall be promptly transferred to the relevant department for handling; if the case is suspected of a crime, it shall be transferred to the judicial authority according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　劳动保障行政部门对违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为作出行政处罚或者行政处理决定前，应当听取用人单位的陈述、申辩；作出行政处罚或者行政处理决定，应当告知用人单位依法享有申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的权利。

**Article 19** Before making a decision on administrative penalty or administrative disposition for an act that violates the laws, regulations or rules on labor security, the labor security administrative department shall hear the statements and defenses of the employing unit; after making such a decision, it shall inform the employing unit of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or bring an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为在2年内未被劳动保障行政部门发现，也未被举报、投诉的，劳动保障行政部门不再查处。  
前款规定的期限，自违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为发生之日起计算；违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为有连续或者继续状态的，自行为终了之日起计算。

**Article 20** If an act that violates the laws, regulations or rules on labor security has not been discovered by the labor security administrative department or has not been reported or complained within two years, the labor security administrative department shall no longer investigate and punish it.  
The period prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be counted from the date on which the act violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security occurs; if such an act is continuous or ongoing, it shall be counted from the date on which the act ends.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　用人单位违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章，对劳动者造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。劳动者与用人单位就赔偿发生争议的，依照国家有关劳动争议处理的规定处理。  
对应当通过劳动争议处理程序解决的事项或者已经按照劳动争议处理程序申请调解、仲裁或者已经提起诉讼的事项，劳动保障行政部门应当告知投诉人依照劳动争议处理或者诉讼的程序办理。

**Article 21** If an employing unit causes damage to a worker by violating the laws, regulations or rules on labor security, it shall bear compensation liability according to law. If a dispute arises between a worker and the employing unit over compensation, it shall be handled in accordance with the state provisions on handling labor disputes.  
If a matter shall be resolved through the procedure for handling labor disputes, or if mediation, arbitration or prosecution has already been applied for in accordance with the procedure for handling labor disputes, the labor security administrative department shall inform the complainant to follow the procedure for handling labor disputes or litigation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　劳动保障行政部门应当建立用人单位劳动保障守法诚信档案。用人单位有重大违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章的行为的，由有关的劳动保障行政部门向社会公布。

**Article 22** The labor security administrative department shall establish a credit record for the employing units that observe labor security laws, regulations and rules in good faith. If an employing unit has a major act that violates the laws, regulations or rules on labor security, the relevant labor security administrative department shall make it known to the public.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　用人单位有下列行为之一的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正，按照受侵害的劳动者每人1000元以上5000元以下的标准计算，处以罚款：  
(一)安排女职工从事矿山井下劳动、国家规定的第四级体力劳动强度的劳动或者其他禁忌从事的劳动的；  
(二)安排女职工在经期从事高处、低温、冷水作业或者国家规定的第三级体力劳动强度的劳动的；  
(三)安排女职工在怀孕期间从事国家规定的第三级体力劳动强度的劳动或者孕期禁忌从事的劳动的；  
(四)安排怀孕7个月以上的女职工夜班劳动或者延长其工作时间的；  
(五)女职工生育享受产假少于90天的；  
(六)安排女职工在哺乳未满1周岁的婴儿期间从事国家规定的第三级体力劳动强度的劳动或者哺乳期禁忌从事的其他劳动，以及延长其工作时间或者安排其夜班劳动的；  
(七)安排未成年工从事矿山井下、有毒有害、国家规定的第四级体力劳动强度的劳动或者其他禁忌从事的劳动的；  
(八)未对未成年工定期进行健康检查的。

**Article 23** Where an employing unit commits any of the following acts, the labor security administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine calculated at the rate of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan per each victimized worker:  
(1) assigning female workers to work in mines, to engage in the fourth-grade physical labor intensity as stipulated by the state, or to engage in other work that is prohibited for them;  
(2) assigning female workers during their menstrual period to engage in high-altitude, low-temperature or cold water operations, or to engage in the third-grade physical labor intensity as stipulated by the state;  
(3) assigning female workers during pregnancy to engage in the third-grade physical labor intensity as stipulated by the state or to engage in work prohibited during pregnancy;  
(4) assigning female workers who have been pregnant for seven months or more to night shift work or extending their working hours;  
(5) granting female workers maternity leave of less than 90 days;  
(6) assigning female workers who are nursing infants under one year old to engage in the third-grade physical labor intensity as stipulated by the state or to engage in other work prohibited during the nursing period, or extending their working hours or assigning them to night shift work;  
(7) assigning underage workers to work in mines, to engage in toxic or hazardous work, to engage in the fourth-grade physical labor intensity as stipulated by the state, or to engage in other work prohibited for them;  
(8) failing to conduct regular health examinations for underage workers.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用人单位与劳动者建立劳动关系不依法订立劳动合同的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正。

**Article 24** Where an employing unit establishes a labor relationship with a worker but fails to conclude a labor contract according to law, the labor security administrative department shall order it to make corrections.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　用人单位违反劳动保障法律、法规或者规章延长劳动者工作时间的，由劳动保障行政部门给予警告，责令限期改正，并可以按照受侵害的劳动者每人100元以上500元以下的标准计算，处以罚款。

**Article 25** Where an employing unit violates the laws, regulations or rules on labor security by extending the working hours of workers, the labor security administrative department shall give it a warning, order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, and may impose a fine calculated at the rate of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan per each victimized worker.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用人单位有下列行为之一的，由劳动保障行政部门分别责令限期支付劳动者的工资报酬、劳动者工资低于当地最低工资标准的差额或者解除劳动合同的经济补偿；逾期不支付的，责令用人单位按照应付金额50%以上1倍以下的标准计算，向劳动者加付赔偿金：  
(一)克扣或者无故拖欠劳动者工资报酬的；  
(二)支付劳动者的工资低于当地最低工资标准的；  
(三)解除劳动合同未依法给予劳动者经济补偿的。

**Article 26** Where an employing unit commits any of the following acts, the labor security administrative department shall order it to pay the worker's remuneration, or the difference between the wage paid and the local minimum wage, or the economic compensation for rescission of the labor contract, each within a prescribed time limit; if payment is not made within the time limit, it shall order the employing unit to pay additional compensation to the worker calculated at a rate of not less than 50% but not more than 100% of the amount payable:  
(1) deducting or delaying the payment of the worker's remuneration without justification;  
(2) paying the worker's wage below the local minimum wage standard;  
(3) failing to give the worker economic compensation according to law after rescission of the labor contract.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　用人单位向社会保险经办机构申报应缴纳的社会保险费数额时，瞒报工资总额或者职工人数的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正，并处瞒报工资数额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款。  
骗取社会保险待遇或者骗取社会保险基金支出的，由劳动保障行政部门责令退还，并处骗取金额1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where an employing unit, when reporting the amount of social insurance premiums payable to the social insurance agency, conceals the total payroll or the number of employees, the labor security administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the concealed amount.  
Where a person fraudulently obtains social insurance benefits or fraudulently expends social insurance funds, the labor security administrative department shall order him or her to return the amount and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount fraudulently obtained; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　职业介绍机构、职业技能培训机构或者职业技能考核鉴定机构违反国家有关职业介绍、职业技能培训或者职业技能考核鉴定的规定的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销许可证。  
未经劳动保障行政部门许可，从事职业介绍、职业技能培训或者职业技能考核鉴定的组织或者个人，由劳动保障行政部门、工商行政管理部门依照国家有关无照经营查处取缔的规定查处取缔。

**Article 28** Where a job placement agency, a vocational skill training institution or a vocational skill appraisal institution violates the state provisions on job placement, vocational skill training or vocational skill appraisal, the labor security administrative department shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its license shall be revoked.  
Where an organization or individual engages in job placement, vocational skill training or vocational skill appraisal without permission from the labor security administrative department, the labor security administrative department and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with the state provisions on unlicensed operation, investigate and ban such activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　用人单位违反《中华人民共和国工会法》，有下列行为之一的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正：  
(一)阻挠劳动者依法参加和组织工会，或者阻挠上级工会帮助、指导劳动者筹建工会的；  
(二)无正当理由调动依法履行职责的工会工作人员的工作岗位，进行打击报复的；  
(三)劳动者因参加工会活动而被解除劳动合同的；  
(四)工会工作人员因依法履行职责被解除劳动合同的。

**Article 29** Where an employing unit commits any of the following acts in violation of the Trade Union Law of the People's Republic of China, the labor security administrative department shall order it to make corrections:  
(1) obstructing workers from joining and organizing trade unions according to law, or obstructing superior trade unions from helping and guiding workers in preparing for the establishment of a trade union;  
(2) without justifiable reasons, transferring the positions of trade union workers who are performing their duties according to law, or retaliating against them;  
(3) rescinding the labor contract of a worker because he or she participated in trade union activities;  
(4) rescinding the labor contract of a trade union worker because he or she performed duties according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有下列行为之一的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正；对有第(一)项、第(二)项或者第(三)项规定的行为的，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款：  
(一)无理抗拒、阻挠劳动保障行政部门依照本条例的规定实施劳动保障监察的；  
(二)不按照劳动保障行政部门的要求报送书面材料，隐瞒事实真相，出具伪证或者隐匿、毁灭证据的；  
(三)经劳动保障行政部门责令改正拒不改正，或者拒不履行劳动保障行政部门的行政处理决定的；  
(四)打击报复举报人、投诉人的。  
违反前款规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where a person commits any of the following acts, the labor security administrative department shall order him or her to make corrections; for an act under paragraph (1), (2) or (3), a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) unreasonably refusing or obstructing the labor security administrative department from implementing the supervision and inspection of labor security in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) failing to submit written materials in accordance with the requirements of the labor security administrative department, concealing the true facts, producing false evidence, or concealing or destroying evidence;  
(3) after being ordered to make corrections by the labor security administrative department, refusing to correct, or refusing to implement the administrative disposition decision of the labor security administrative department;  
(4) retaliating against the informant or complainant.  
If any act in the preceding paragraph constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administrative penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　劳动保障监察员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者泄露在履行职责过程中知悉的商业秘密的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
劳动保障行政部门和劳动保障监察员违法行使职权，侵犯用人单位或者劳动者的合法权益的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 31** Where a labor security inspector abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or discloses the trade secrets learned in the course of performing his or her duties, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the labor security administrative department or a labor security inspector illegally exercises its or his or her power, infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of an employing unit or a worker, it or he or she shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　属于本条例规定的劳动保障监察事项，法律、其他行政法规对处罚另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 32** Where, with respect to the matters of labor security supervision and inspection as provided for in these Regulations, other laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for penalties, those provisions shall prevail.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对无营业执照或者已被依法吊销营业执照，有劳动用工行为的，由劳动保障行政部门依照本条例实施劳动保障监察，并及时通报工商行政管理部门予以查处取缔。

**Article 33** Where an employing unit that does not have a business license or whose business license has been revoked according to law engages in labor employment activities, the labor security administrative department shall implement the supervision and inspection of labor security in accordance with these Regulations, and shall promptly notify the administrative department for industry and commerce to investigate and ban it.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家机关、事业单位、社会团体执行劳动保障法律、法规和规章的情况，由劳动保障行政部门根据其职责，依照本条例实施劳动保障监察。

**Article 34** The compliance with the laws, regulations and rules on labor security by state organs, institutions and social organizations shall be subject to the supervision and inspection of labor security implemented by the labor security administrative department in accordance with these Regulations and within its functions and duties.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　劳动安全卫生的监督检查，由卫生部门、安全生产监督管理部门、特种设备安全监督管理部门等有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 35** The supervision and inspection of work safety and occupational health shall be carried out by the health department, the work safety supervision and administration department, the special equipment safety supervision and administration department and other relevant departments in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自2004年12月1日起施行。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2004.
