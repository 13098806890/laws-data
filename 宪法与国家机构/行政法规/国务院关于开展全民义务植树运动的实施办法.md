# 国务院关于开展全民义务植树运动的实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1982-02-27

> 个别地方，由于气候、土地等条件限制确实难以开展植树运动的，经省、自治区、直辖市绿化委员会批准，可以不开展全民义务植树运动，不成立绿化委员会。 二、各级绿化委员会应当组织和推动本地区各部门、各单位，通过各种形式，广泛深入地宣传《关于开展全民义务植树运动的决议》和本实施办法，宣传全民植树、绿化祖国的重大意义，认真做好思想动员，提高认识，造成声势，做到家喻户晓，人人皆知。同时，要努力做好调查研究、规划安排、苗木培育、技术训练等准备工作，有计划有步骤地开展植树运动，要扎扎实实，讲求实效，不搞形式主义和“一刀切”。 三、凡是中华人民共和国公民，男十一岁至六十岁，女十一岁至五十五岁，除丧失劳动能力者外，均应承担义务植树任务，各单位要将人数据实统计上报当地绿化委员会，作为分配具体任务的依据。 县级绿化委员会在分配义务植树任务时，要按照每人每年植树三至五棵的要求，确定具体指标，因地制宜地进行灵活多样的安排。可以按单位划分责任地段，承担整地、育苗、栽植和管护任务；也可以按相应劳动量，分配承担造林绿化的某一单项和几个单项的任务。此项任务，可以一年一定，也可以一定几年。 对十一岁至十七岁的青少年，应当根据他们的实际情况，就近安排力所能及的劳动。 四、此项义务劳动，限于用在本县、本市所辖范围，营造国有林和集体林。义务植树的地段或参加绿化劳动的项目，各地要经过周密的调查研究，作出统一规划和安排。城市要优先搞好风景游览区、名胜古迹和主要街道等公共场所的绿化。农村要尽快搞好“四旁”绿化和农田防护林建设。机关、团体、企业、学校等单位和居民区，都要大力植树、种草、栽花，美化环境。 五、使用义务劳动，在国有土地上栽植的树木，林权归现在经营管理这些土地的单位所有；没有明确经营管理单位的，由当地政府指定的部门、单位所有。在集体土地上义务栽植的树木，林权归集体单位所有。如果情况特殊，另有协议或合同，按协议或合同的规定办理。对林权所有单位，县以上人民政府要发给证书，切实保障其合法权益。 六、为确保义务植树所需苗木，各地应当努力办好现有的国营苗圃和集体苗圃，并安排必需数量的土地和专业人员，扩建和新建苗木基地，培育良种壮苗。凡是有条件的单位，都要积极自办苗圃。提倡城镇家庭和农村社员开展营养钵育苗。 七、对义务栽植的树木和现有的林木，必须大力加强培育管护，确保成活成林，不受破坏。林木所有的单位或承担管护义务的单位，应当根据情况组织林场、专业队或确定专人负责管护。要严肃法制和纪律，建立爱林护林的乡规民约。采伐更新，必须按照森林法的规定，经过林业或园林部门批准。城市绿地要严加保护，不得侵占破坏。违者要给予经济处罚或法律制裁。 八、植树绿化，要讲究科学，注重实效。要培训技术骨干，加强技术指导，普及植树绿化的技术知识，严格按照技术规程办事，保证质量。 九、对义务植树，各单位每年都要进行检查，并将完成情况据实上报。绿化委员会应当定期组织评比，成绩优异的，要给予表扬和奖励；年满十八岁的成年公民无故不履行此项义务的，所在单位要进行批评教育，责令限期补栽，或者给予经济处罚。整个单位没有完成任务的，要追究领导责任，并由当地绿化委员会收缴一定数额的绿化费。 十、各级林业和园林部门，应当在绿化委员会领导下，会同有关部门，努力搞好规划设计和苗木培育等各项具体工作。基层机构不健全的，应当充实和加强。 十一、义务植树所需的苗木费、管护费，应当根据自力更生勤俭节约的原则，一般由林权所有单位负责解决。有的单位因绿化任务大，资金困难，确实无力承担全部费用的，按单位隶属关系，由各级财政酌情解决。参加义务植树的单位和个人所需交通等费用，由参加单位自理。 十二、开展全民义务植树运动是促进整个造林绿化的一个重大措施。各地在开展此项运动时，必须同加快整个造林绿化工作结合起来，在苗木、经费、技术力量的使用和林木管护等方面进行统筹安排，既要搞好义务植树，又要完成年度造林绿化计划。对于一个地方来说，完成了义务植树任务，但整个造林绿化工作没有做好，不能给予表扬和奖励。 十三、根据《关于开展全民义务植树运动的决议》和本实施办法的规定，各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以结合实际情况，制定实施细则。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>二、各级绿化委员会应当组织和推动本地区各部门、各单位，通过各种形式，广泛深入地宣传《关于开展全民义务植树运动的决议》和本实施办法，宣传全民植树、绿化祖国的重大意义，认真做好思想动员，提高认识，造成声势，做到家喻户晓，人人皆知。同时，要努力做好调查研究、规划安排、苗木培育、技术训练等准备工作，有计划有步骤地开展植树运动，要扎扎实实，讲求实效，不搞形式主义和“一刀切”。

**Article 1** 2. Green committees at all levels shall organize and promote all departments and units in their respective regions to widely and deeply publicize the Resolution on Launching a Nationwide Voluntary Tree-Planting Campaign and these Implementing Measures through various forms, propagate the great significance of nationwide tree planting and greening the motherland, earnestly conduct ideological mobilization, raise awareness, create momentum, and ensure that it is known to every household and everyone. At the same time, efforts shall be made to complete preparatory work such as investigation and research, planning and arrangement, seedling cultivation, and technical training, and to carry out the tree-planting campaign in a planned and step-by-step manner, in a down-to-earth and pragmatic way, without resorting to formalism or "one-size-fits-all" practices.

<a id="art-2"></a>三、凡是中华人民共和国公民，男十一岁至六十岁，女十一岁至五十五岁，除丧失劳动能力者外，均应承担义务植树任务，各单位要将人数据实统计上报当地绿化委员会，作为分配具体任务的依据。  
县级绿化委员会在分配义务植树任务时，要按照每人每年植树三至五棵的要求，确定具体指标，因地制宜地进行灵活多样的安排。可以按单位划分责任地段，承担整地、育苗、栽植和管护任务；也可以按相应劳动量，分配承担造林绿化的某一单项和几个单项的任务。此项任务，可以一年一定，也可以一定几年。  
对十一岁至十七岁的青少年，应当根据他们的实际情况，就近安排力所能及的劳动。

**Article 2** 3. All citizens of the People's Republic of China, men aged 11 to 60 and women aged 11 to 55, except for those who have lost the ability to work, shall assume the obligation to perform voluntary tree-planting tasks. Each unit shall truthfully count and report the number of such persons to the local green committee as the basis for allocating specific tasks.  
When allocating voluntary tree-planting tasks, the county-level green committee shall determine specific targets in accordance with the requirement of planting three to five trees per person per year, and make flexible and varied arrangements based on local conditions. It may divide responsibility zones by units, undertaking tasks of land preparation, seedling cultivation, planting, and tending; or it may, according to the corresponding labor volume, allocate tasks of a single or several items of afforestation and greening. Such tasks may be determined annually or for a period of several years.  
For adolescents aged 11 to 17, they shall be assigned, based on their actual conditions, labor within their capabilities at nearby locations.

<a id="art-3"></a>四、此项义务劳动，限于用在本县、本市所辖范围，营造国有林和集体林。义务植树的地段或参加绿化劳动的项目，各地要经过周密的调查研究，作出统一规划和安排。城市要优先搞好风景游览区、名胜古迹和主要街道等公共场所的绿化。农村要尽快搞好“四旁”绿化和农田防护林建设。机关、团体、企业、学校等单位和居民区，都要大力植树、种草、栽花，美化环境。

**Article 3** 4. This voluntary labor shall be limited to the scope under the jurisdiction of the county or city for establishing state-owned forests and collectively-owned forests. Each locality shall, through thorough investigation and research, make unified planning and arrangements for the plots for voluntary tree planting or the projects for participating in greening labor. Cities shall give priority to greening public places such as scenic spots, places of historic interest and scenic beauty, and main streets. Rural areas shall speed up the greening of the "four sides" (house sides, village sides, road sides, water sides) and the construction of farmland shelterbelts. All units such as government organs, organizations, enterprises, schools, and residential areas shall vigorously plant trees, grass, and flowers to beautify the environment.

<a id="art-4"></a>五、使用义务劳动，在国有土地上栽植的树木，林权归现在经营管理这些土地的单位所有；没有明确经营管理单位的，由当地政府指定的部门、单位所有。在集体土地上义务栽植的树木，林权归集体单位所有。如果情况特殊，另有协议或合同，按协议或合同的规定办理。对林权所有单位，县以上人民政府要发给证书，切实保障其合法权益。

**Article 4** 5. Trees planted on state-owned land using voluntary labor shall belong to the unit currently managing and operating the land; if there is no clearly designated management unit, they shall belong to the department or unit designated by the local government. Trees voluntarily planted on collectively-owned land shall belong to the collective unit. If special circumstances exist and other agreements or contracts have been made, such matters shall be handled according to the agreements or contracts. For units owning such trees, people's governments at or above the county level shall issue certificates to effectively protect their legitimate rights and interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>六、为确保义务植树所需苗木，各地应当努力办好现有的国营苗圃和集体苗圃，并安排必需数量的土地和专业人员，扩建和新建苗木基地，培育良种壮苗。凡是有条件的单位，都要积极自办苗圃。提倡城镇家庭和农村社员开展营养钵育苗。

**Article 5** 6. To ensure the seedlings needed for voluntary tree planting, all localities shall make efforts to run existing state-owned nurseries and collective nurseries well, allocate necessary amounts of land and professional personnel, expand and establish new seedling bases, and cultivate improved varieties and strong seedlings. All units that have conditions shall actively establish their own nurseries. Urban households and rural members are encouraged to carry out nutrient pot seedling cultivation.

<a id="art-6"></a>七、对义务栽植的树木和现有的林木，必须大力加强培育管护，确保成活成林，不受破坏。林木所有的单位或承担管护义务的单位，应当根据情况组织林场、专业队或确定专人负责管护。要严肃法制和纪律，建立爱林护林的乡规民约。采伐更新，必须按照森林法的规定，经过林业或园林部门批准。城市绿地要严加保护，不得侵占破坏。违者要给予经济处罚或法律制裁。

**Article 6** 7. The cultivation, protection, and management of voluntarily planted trees and existing forests must be vigorously strengthened to ensure survival and forest formation and to prevent damage. The units that own the trees or assume the obligation of protection and management shall, according to circumstances, organize forest farms, professional teams, or designate specific persons to take charge of protection and management. Legal systems and discipline shall be strictly enforced, and village regulations and folk conventions for loving and protecting forests shall be established. Harvesting and regeneration must be approved by the forestry or garden department in accordance with the provisions of the Forest Law. Urban green spaces shall be strictly protected and shall not be occupied or damaged. Violators shall be subject to economic penalties or legal sanctions.

<a id="art-7"></a>八、植树绿化，要讲究科学，注重实效。要培训技术骨干，加强技术指导，普及植树绿化的技术知识，严格按照技术规程办事，保证质量。

**Article 7** 8. Tree planting and greening shall be science-based and focused on practical results. Technical backbone personnel shall be trained, technical guidance shall be strengthened, technical knowledge of tree planting and greening shall be popularized, and operations shall be strictly carried out in accordance with technical regulations to ensure quality.

<a id="art-8"></a>九、对义务植树，各单位每年都要进行检查，并将完成情况据实上报。绿化委员会应当定期组织评比，成绩优异的，要给予表扬和奖励；年满十八岁的成年公民无故不履行此项义务的，所在单位要进行批评教育，责令限期补栽，或者给予经济处罚。整个单位没有完成任务的，要追究领导责任，并由当地绿化委员会收缴一定数额的绿化费。

**Article 8** 9. With respect to voluntary tree planting, each unit shall conduct inspections annually and truthfully report the completion status. The green committee shall regularly organize appraisals; those with outstanding achievements shall be commended and rewarded. For adult citizens aged 18 or above who fail to perform this obligation without valid reason, their units shall give criticism and education, order them to replant within a time limit, or impose economic penalties. If a whole unit fails to complete the task, the leadership responsibility shall be pursued, and the local green committee shall collect a certain amount of greening fee.

<a id="art-9"></a>十、各级林业和园林部门，应当在绿化委员会领导下，会同有关部门，努力搞好规划设计和苗木培育等各项具体工作。基层机构不健全的，应当充实和加强。

**Article 9** 10. Forestry and garden departments at all levels shall, under the leadership of green committees, work together with relevant departments to diligently carry out specific work such as planning, design, and seedling cultivation. If grassroots institutions are not well established, they shall be strengthened and reinforced.

<a id="art-10"></a>十一、义务植树所需的苗木费、管护费，应当根据自力更生勤俭节约的原则，一般由林权所有单位负责解决。有的单位因绿化任务大，资金困难，确实无力承担全部费用的，按单位隶属关系，由各级财政酌情解决。参加义务植树的单位和个人所需交通等费用，由参加单位自理。

**Article 10** 11. The costs for seedlings and for protection and management required for voluntary tree planting shall, in accordance with the principle of self-reliance and frugality, generally be borne by the unit that owns the trees. For units that have heavy greening tasks and financial difficulties and are truly unable to bear all the costs, the costs shall be resolved by the fiscal authorities at all levels according to the subordinate relationship of the units. Transportation and other expenses needed by units and individuals participating in voluntary tree planting shall be borne by the participating units themselves.

<a id="art-11"></a>十二、开展全民义务植树运动是促进整个造林绿化的一个重大措施。各地在开展此项运动时，必须同加快整个造林绿化工作结合起来，在苗木、经费、技术力量的使用和林木管护等方面进行统筹安排，既要搞好义务植树，又要完成年度造林绿化计划。对于一个地方来说，完成了义务植树任务，但整个造林绿化工作没有做好，不能给予表扬和奖励。

**Article 11** 12. Carrying out the nationwide voluntary tree-planting campaign is a major measure to promote overall afforestation and greening. When carrying out this campaign, all localities must combine it with accelerating the overall afforestation and greening work, and make overall arrangements in terms of the use of seedlings, funds, technical force, and forest management and protection, so as to both carry out voluntary tree planting well and complete the annual afforestation and greening plan. For a locality that has completed the voluntary tree-planting task but has not done a good job in overall afforestation and greening, it shall not be commended or rewarded.

<a id="art-12"></a>十三、根据《关于开展全民义务植树运动的决议》和本实施办法的规定，各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以结合实际情况，制定实施细则。  
人民解放军指战员参加营区外义务植树的办法，按国务院、中央军委《关于军队参加营区外义务植树的指示》(〔1982〕3号)的规定执行；营区内植树办法，由解放军总部另定。但各地人民政府必须密切合作，合理规划，帮助军队解决应解决的实际问题。

**Article 12** 13. In accordance with the provisions of the Resolution on Launching a Nationwide Voluntary Tree-Planting Campaign and these Implementing Measures, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate detailed implementation rules in light of actual conditions.  
Measures for officers and soldiers of the People's Liberation Army to participate in voluntary tree planting outside military camps shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the "Instruction on the Participation of the Military in Voluntary Tree Planting Outside Camps" (Document No. 3 of 1982) issued by the State Council and the Central Military Commission; measures for tree planting inside camps shall be separately formulated by the General Headquarters of the PLA. However, the local people's governments must cooperate closely, make reasonable plans, and help the military solve the practical problems that should be solved.
