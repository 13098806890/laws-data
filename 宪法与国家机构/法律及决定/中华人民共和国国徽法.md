# 中华人民共和国国徽法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-10-17  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （1991年3月2日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2020年10月17日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国国徽法〉的决定》第二次修正） 第一条　为了维护国徽的尊严，正确使用国徽，增强公民的国家观念，弘扬爱国主义精神，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国国徽，中间是五星照耀下的天安门，周围是谷穗和齿轮。 中华人民共和国国徽按照1950年中央人民政府委员会通过的《中华人民共和国国徽图案》和中央人民政府委员会办公厅公布的《中华人民共和国国徽图案制作说明》制作。 第三条　中华人民共和国国徽是中华人民共和国的象征和标志。 一切组织和公民，都应当尊重和爱护国徽。 第四条　下列机构应当悬挂国徽： （一）各级人民代表大会常务委员会； （二）各级人民政府； （三）中央军事委员会； （四）各级监察委员会； （五）各级人民法院和专门人民法院； （六）各级人民检察院和专门人民检察院； （七）外交部； （八）国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国徽的尊严，正确使用国徽，增强公民的国家观念，弘扬爱国主义精神，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of safeguarding the dignity of the National Emblem, ensuring its correct use, enhancing citizens' concept of the State, carrying forward the spirit of patriotism, and cultivating and practicing core socialist values.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国国徽，中间是五星照耀下的天安门，周围是谷穗和齿轮。  
中华人民共和国国徽按照1950年中央人民政府委员会通过的《中华人民共和国国徽图案》和中央人民政府委员会办公厅公布的《中华人民共和国国徽图案制作说明》制作。

**Article 2** The National Emblem of the People's Republic of China features Tiananmen under the illumination of five stars in the center, surrounded by ears of grain and a gear.  
The National Emblem of the People's Republic of China shall be made in accordance with the Design of the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China adopted by the Central People's Government Council in 1950 and the Instructions for the Manufacture of the Design of the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China published by the General Office of the Central People's Government Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国国徽是中华人民共和国的象征和标志。  
一切组织和公民，都应当尊重和爱护国徽。

**Article 3** The National Emblem of the People's Republic of China is the symbol and emblem of the People's Republic of China.  
All organizations and citizens shall respect and protect the National Emblem.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下列机构应当悬挂国徽：  
（一）各级人民代表大会常务委员会；  
（二）各级人民政府；  
（三）中央军事委员会；  
（四）各级监察委员会；  
（五）各级人民法院和专门人民法院；  
（六）各级人民检察院和专门人民检察院；  
（七）外交部；  
（八）国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构；  
（九）中央人民政府驻香港特别行政区有关机构、中央人民政府驻澳门特别行政区有关机构。  
国徽应当悬挂在机关正门上方正中处。

**Article 4** The following organs shall display the National Emblem:  
(1) Standing Committees of the People's Congresses at all levels;  
(2) People's Governments at all levels;  
(3) the Central Military Commission;  
(4) Supervision Commissions at all levels;  
(5) People's Courts and special People's Courts at all levels;  
(6) People's Procuratorates and special People's Procuratorates at all levels;  
(7) the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(8) diplomatic missions, consular posts and other diplomatic representations of the State abroad;  
(9) relevant organs of the Central People's Government stationed in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and relevant organs of the Central People's Government stationed in the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
The National Emblem shall be displayed above the center of the main entrance of the organ.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列场所应当悬挂国徽：  
（一）北京天安门城楼、人民大会堂；  
（二）县级以上各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会会议厅，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会会场；  
（三）各级人民法院和专门人民法院的审判庭；  
（四）宪法宣誓场所；  
（五）出境入境口岸的适当场所。

**Article 5** The National Emblem shall be displayed at the following places:  
(1) Tiananmen Rostrum and the Great Hall of the People in Beijing;  
(2) meeting halls of the People's Congresses and their Standing Committees at or above the county level, and meeting places of the People's Congresses of townships, ethnic townships and towns;  
(3) courtrooms of People's Courts and special People's Courts at all levels;  
(4) venues for swearing allegiance to the Constitution;  
(5) appropriate places at exit and entry ports.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列机构的印章应当刻有国徽图案：  
（一）全国人民代表大会常务委员会，国务院，中央军事委员会，国家监察委员会，最高人民法院，最高人民检察院；  
（二）全国人民代表大会各专门委员会和全国人民代表大会常务委员会办公厅、工作委员会，国务院各部、各委员会、各直属机构、国务院办公厅以及国务院规定应当使用刻有国徽图案印章的办事机构，中央军事委员会办公厅以及中央军事委员会规定应当使用刻有国徽图案印章的其他机构；  
（三）县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会、人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院，专门人民法院，专门人民检察院；  
（四）国家驻外使馆、领馆和其他外交代表机构。

**Article 6** The seals of the following organs shall bear the design of the National Emblem:  
(1) the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the National Commission of Supervision, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(2) the special committees of the National People's Congress, the General Office and working committees of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the ministries, commissions, directly affiliated organs and the General Office of the State Council, and other working organs as prescribed by the State Council that shall use seals bearing the design of the National Emblem; the General Office of the Central Military Commission and other organs as prescribed by the Central Military Commission that shall use seals bearing the design of the National Emblem;  
(3) Standing Committees of the People's Congresses, People's Governments, Supervision Commissions, People's Courts, and People's Procuratorates at or above the county level, as well as special People's Courts and special People's Procuratorates;  
(4) diplomatic missions, consular posts and other diplomatic representations of the State abroad.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[本法第六条](#art-6)规定的机构应当在其网站首页显著位置使用国徽图案。  
网站使用的国徽图案标准版本在中国人大网和中国政府网上发布。

**Article 7** The organs as prescribed in Article 6 of this Law shall use the design of the National Emblem at a prominent position on the home page of their websites.  
The standard version of the design of the National Emblem for use on websites shall be published on the website of the National People's Congress and the website of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下列文书、出版物等应当印有国徽图案：  
（一）全国人民代表大会常务委员会、中华人民共和国主席和国务院颁发的荣誉证书、任命书、外交文书；  
（二）中华人民共和国主席、副主席，全国人民代表大会常务委员会委员长、副委员长，国务院总理、副总理、国务委员，中央军事委员会主席、副主席，国家监察委员会主任，最高人民法院院长和最高人民检察院检察长以职务名义对外使用的信封、信笺、请柬等；  
（三）全国人民代表大会常务委员会公报、国务院公报、最高人民法院公报和最高人民检察院公报的封面；  
（四）国家出版的法律、法规正式版本的封面。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/对外使用国徽图案的办法.md#art-3" title="被《对外使用国徽图案的办法》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The following documents, publications, etc. shall bear the design of the National Emblem:  
(1) certificates of honor, letters of appointment, and diplomatic documents issued by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the President of the People's Republic of China, and the State Council;  
(2) envelopes, letter paper, invitation cards, etc. used externally in official capacity by the President and Vice-President of the People's Republic of China, the Chairman and Vice-Chairmen of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Premier and Vice-Premiers of the State Council and the State Councilors, the Chairman and Vice-Chairmen of the Central Military Commission, the Director of the National Commission of Supervision, the President of the Supreme People's Court, and the Procurator-General of the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(3) the covers of the Gazette of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Gazette of the State Council, the Gazette of the Supreme People's Court, and the Gazette of the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(4) the covers of the official versions of laws and regulations published by the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　标示国界线的界桩、界碑和标示领海基点方位的标志碑以及其他用于显示国家主权的标志物可以使用国徽图案。  
中国人民银行发行的法定货币可以使用国徽图案。

**Article 9** The design of the National Emblem may be used on boundary markers and boundary tablets indicating the national boundary, markers indicating the base points of the territorial sea, and other markers used to demonstrate State sovereignty.  
Legal tender issued by the People's Bank of China may bear the design of the National Emblem.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　下列证件、证照可以使用国徽图案：  
（一）国家机关工作人员的工作证件、执法证件等；  
（二）国家机关颁发的营业执照、许可证书、批准证书、资格证书、权利证书等；  
（三）居民身份证，中华人民共和国护照等法定出入境证件。  
国家机关和武装力量的徽章可以将国徽图案作为核心图案。  
公民在庄重的场合可以佩戴国徽徽章，表达爱国情感。

**Article 10** The design of the National Emblem may be used on the following certificates and licenses:  
(1) work IDs, law enforcement IDs, etc. of State functionaries;  
(2) business licenses, permits, approval certificates, qualification certificates, certificates of rights, etc. issued by State organs;  
(3) resident identity cards, passports of the People's Republic of China, and other statutory entry-exit documents.  
The design of the National Emblem may be used as the core element on the badges of State organs and armed forces.  
Citizens may wear National Emblem pins on solemn occasions to express patriotic sentiments.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外事活动和国家驻外使馆、领馆以及其他外交代表机构对外使用国徽图案的办法，由外交部规定，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 11** Measures for the use of the design of the National Emblem externally in foreign affairs activities and by diplomatic missions, consular posts and other diplomatic representations of the State abroad shall be formulated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在本法规定的范围以外需要悬挂国徽或者使用国徽图案的，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会办公厅或者国务院办公厅会同有关主管部门规定。

**Article 12** Where it is necessary to display the National Emblem or use its design beyond the scope provided for in this Law, the matter shall be regulated by the General Office of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the General Office of the State Council jointly with the competent departments concerned.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国徽及其图案不得用于：  
（一）商标、授予专利权的外观设计、商业广告；  
（二）日常用品、日常生活的陈设布置；  
（三）私人庆吊活动；  
（四）国务院办公厅规定不得使用国徽及其图案的其他场合。

**Article 13** The National Emblem and its design shall not be used on:  
(1) trademarks, designs for which patent rights are granted, or commercial advertisements;  
(2) articles for daily use or arrangements for everyday life;  
(3) private celebrations or memorial activities;  
(4) other occasions as prescribed by the General Office of the State Council where the National Emblem and its design shall not be used.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　不得悬挂破损、污损或者不合规格的国徽。

**Article 14** No damaged, defaced or non-conforming National Emblem shall be displayed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国徽应当作为爱国主义教育的重要内容。  
中小学应当教育学生了解国徽的历史和精神内涵。  
新闻媒体应当积极宣传国徽知识，引导公民和组织正确使用国徽及其图案。

**Article 15** The National Emblem shall be an important part of education in patriotism.  
Primary and secondary schools shall educate students on the history and spiritual connotation of the National Emblem.  
News media shall actively publicize knowledge about the National Emblem and guide citizens and organizations in the correct use of the National Emblem and its design.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　悬挂的国徽由国家指定的企业统一制作，其直径的通用尺度为下列三种：  
（一）一百厘米；  
（二）八十厘米；  
（三）六十厘米。  
需要悬挂非通用尺度国徽的，应当按照通用尺度成比例适当放大或者缩小，并与使用目的、所在建筑物、周边环境相适应。

**Article 16** National Emblems for display shall be manufactured uniformly by enterprises designated by the State, and the standard diameters shall be the following three types:  
(1) 100 cm;  
(2) 80 cm;  
(3) 60 cm.  
Where a National Emblem of a non-standard diameter needs to be displayed, it shall be appropriately enlarged or reduced in proportion to the standard diameter, and shall be compatible with the purpose of use, the building where it is located, and the surrounding environment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院办公厅统筹协调全国范围内国徽管理有关工作。地方各级人民政府统筹协调本行政区域内国徽管理有关工作。  
各级人民政府市场监督管理部门对国徽的制作和销售实施监督管理。  
县级人民政府确定的部门对本行政区域内国徽的悬挂、使用和收回实施监督管理。

**Article 17** The General Office of the State Council shall coordinate the management of the National Emblem nationwide. Local people's governments at all levels shall coordinate the management of the National Emblem within their respective administrative areas.  
The market supervision and management departments of people's governments at all levels shall supervise and manage the manufacture and sale of the National Emblem.  
The department designated by the people's government at the county level shall supervise and manage the display, use and retrieval of the National Emblem within its administrative area.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在公共场合故意以焚烧、毁损、涂划、玷污、践踏等方式侮辱中华人民共和国国徽的，依法追究刑事责任；情节较轻的，由公安机关处以十五日以下拘留。

**Article 18** Whoever intentionally insults the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China in a public place by burning, mutilating, defacing, besmirching, trampling upon, or in any other way, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the public security organ shall impose a detention of up to fifteen days.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本法自1991年10月1日起施行。

中华人民共和国国徽图案  
（1950年6月28日中央人民政府委员会第八次会议通过）  
说明：国徽的内容为国旗、天安门、齿轮和麦稻穗，象征中国人民自“五四”运动以来的新民主主义革命斗争和工人阶级领导的以工农联盟为基础的人民民主专政的新中国的诞生。  
中华人民共和国国徽图案制作说明  
（1950年9月20日中央人民政府委员会办公厅公布）  
一、两把麦稻组成正圆形的环。齿轮安在下方麦稻杆的交叉点上。齿轮的中心交结着红绶。红绶向左右绾住麦稻而下垂，把齿轮分成上下两部。  
二、从图案正中垂直画一直线，其左右两部分，完全对称。  
三、图案各部分之地位、尺寸，可根据方格墨线图之比例，放大或缩小。  
四、如制作浮雕，其各部位之高低，可根据断面图之比例放大或缩小。  
五、国徽之涂色为金红二色：麦稻、五星、天安门、齿轮为金色，圆环内之底子及垂绶为红色；红为正红（同于国旗），金为大赤金（淡色而有光泽之金）。

Annex:  
Design of the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China  
(Adopted at the Eighth Session of the Central People's Government Council on June 28, 1950)  
Explanation: The content of the National Emblem consists of the National Flag, Tiananmen, a gear, and ears of wheat and rice, symbolizing the new-democratic revolutionary struggle of the Chinese people since the May Fourth Movement and the birth of the new China under the people's democratic dictatorship led by the working class and based on the alliance of workers and peasants.  
Instructions for the Manufacture of the Design of the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China  
(Published by the General Office of the Central People's Government Council on September 20, 1950)  
1. Two ears of wheat and rice form a circular ring. A gear is placed at the crossing point of the stalks of wheat and rice below. A red ribbon is tied at the center of the gear. The red ribbon passes around the wheat and rice on the left and right and hangs down, dividing the gear into upper and lower parts.  
2. Draw a vertical line through the center of the design; the left and right parts are completely symmetrical.  
3. The positions and dimensions of each part of the design may be enlarged or reduced according to the proportions of the grid-and-ink drawing.  
4. If a relief is made, the heights of each part may be enlarged or reduced according to the proportions of the cross-section drawing.  
5. The colors of the National Emblem are gold and red: the wheat and rice, the five stars, Tiananmen, and the gear are gold; the background inside the circular ring and the hanging ribbon are red. The red is standard red (the same as the National Flag), and the gold is bright gold (pale-colored and lustrous gold).
