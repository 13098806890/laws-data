# 中华人民共和国教师法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1993年10月31日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第四次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　权利和义务 第三章　资格和任用 第四章　培养和培训 第五章　考　　核 第六章　待　　遇 第七章　奖　　励 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障教师的合法权益，建设具有良好思想品德修养和业务素质的教师队伍，促进社会主义教育事业的发展，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于在各级各类学校和其他教育机构中专门从事教育教学工作的教师。 第三条　教师是履行教育教学职责的专业人员，承担教书育人，培养社会主义事业建设者和接班人、提高民族素质的使命。教师应当忠诚于人民的教育事业。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障教师的合法权益，建设具有良好思想品德修养和业务素质的教师队伍，促进社会主义教育事业的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of teachers, building a contingent of teachers with good ideological and ethical cultivation and professional competence, and promoting the development of socialist education.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于在各级各类学校和其他教育机构中专门从事教育教学工作的教师。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to teachers who are specially engaged in education and teaching in schools of various levels and types and other educational institutions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　教师是履行教育教学职责的专业人员，承担教书育人，培养社会主义事业建设者和接班人、提高民族素质的使命。教师应当忠诚于人民的教育事业。

**Article 3** Teachers are professionals who perform the duties of education and teaching, and shall undertake the mission of imparting knowledge and educating people, cultivating builders and successors for the socialist cause, and improving the quality of the nation. Teachers shall be loyal to the people's educational cause.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，加强教师的思想政治教育和业务培训，改善教师的工作条件和生活条件，保障教师的合法权益，提高教师的社会地位。  
全社会都应当尊重教师。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to strengthen the ideological and political education and professional training of teachers, improve the working and living conditions of teachers, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of teachers, and raise the social status of teachers.  
The whole society shall respect teachers.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院教育行政部门主管全国的教师工作。  
国务院有关部门在各自职权范围内负责有关的教师工作。  
学校和其他教育机构根据国家规定，自主进行教师管理工作。

**Article 5** The administrative department for education under the State Council shall be in charge of teacher work nationwide.  
The relevant departments under the State Council shall, within the scope of their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant teacher work.  
Schools and other educational institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, carry out teacher administration autonomously.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　每年九月十日为教师节。

**Article 6** September 10 of each year shall be Teachers' Day.

### 第二章　权利和义务

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　教师享有下列权利：  
（一）进行教育教学活动，开展教育教学改革和实验；  
（二）从事科学研究、学术交流，参加专业的学术团体，在学术活动中充分发表意见；  
（三）指导学生的学习和发展，评定学生的品行和学业成绩；  
（四）按时获取工资报酬，享受国家规定的福利待遇以及寒暑假期的带薪休假；  
（五）对学校教育教学、管理工作和教育行政部门的工作提出意见和建议，通过教职工代表大会或者其他形式，参与学校的民主管理；  
（六）参加进修或者其他方式的培训。

**Article 7** Teachers shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to conduct educational and teaching activities, and to carry out educational and teaching reforms and experiments;  
(2) to engage in scientific research and academic exchanges, to participate in professional academic groups, and to fully express opinions in academic activities;  
(3) to guide students' learning and development, and to evaluate students' conduct and academic performance;  
(4) to obtain wages and remuneration on time, to enjoy the welfare benefits prescribed by the State, and to have paid vacations during winter and summer holidays;  
(5) to put forward opinions and suggestions on school education, teaching and administration, and on the work of the administrative department for education, and to participate in the democratic administration of the school through such forms as the conference of representatives of faculty and staff; and  
(6) to participate in in-service training or other forms of training.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　教师应当履行下列义务：  
（一）遵守宪法、法律和职业道德，为人师表；  
（二）贯彻国家的教育方针，遵守规章制度，执行学校的教学计划，履行教师聘约，完成教育教学工作任务；  
（三）对学生进行宪法所确定的基本原则的教育和爱国主义、民族团结的教育，法制教育以及思想品德、文化、科学技术教育，组织、带领学生开展有益的社会活动；  
（四）关心、爱护全体学生，尊重学生人格，促进学生在品德、智力、体质等方面全面发展；  
（五）制止有害于学生的行为或者其他侵犯学生合法权益的行为，批评和抵制有害于学生健康成长的现象；  
（六）不断提高思想政治觉悟和教育教学业务水平。

**Article 8** Teachers shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to abide by the Constitution, laws and professional ethics, and to set an example for others;  
(2) to implement the State's educational policy, to abide by rules and regulations, to carry out the school's teaching plans, to fulfill the teacher employment contract, and to complete educational and teaching work tasks;  
(3) to conduct education on the basic principles determined by the Constitution, education on patriotism and national unity, legal education, and education on ideological and ethical character, culture, science and technology for students, and to organize and lead students to carry out beneficial social activities;  
(4) to care for and cherish all students, to respect the personality of students, and to promote the all-round development of students in morality, intelligence, physique, etc.;  
(5) to stop acts harmful to students or other acts infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of students, and to criticize and resist phenomena harmful to the healthy growth of students; and  
(6) to continuously improve their ideological and political awareness and their professional level of education and teaching.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　为保障教师完成教育教学任务，各级人民政府、教育行政部门、有关部门、学校和其他教育机构应当履行下列职责：  
（一）提供符合国家安全标准的教育教学设施和设备；  
（二）提供必需的图书、资料及其他教育教学用品；  
（三）对教师在教育教学、科学研究中的创造性工作给以鼓励和帮助；  
（四）支持教师制止有害于学生的行为或者其他侵犯学生合法权益的行为。

**Article 9** To ensure that teachers complete their educational and teaching tasks, people's governments at all levels, administrative departments for education, relevant departments, schools and other educational institutions shall perform the following duties:  
(1) to provide educational and teaching facilities and equipment that meet the national safety standards;  
(2) to provide necessary books, materials and other educational and teaching supplies;  
(3) to give encouragement and assistance to teachers' creative work in education, teaching and scientific research; and  
(4) to support teachers in stopping acts harmful to students or other acts infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of students.

### 第三章　资格和任用

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家实行教师资格制度。  
中国公民凡遵守宪法和法律，热爱教育事业，具有良好的思想品德，具备本法规定的学历或者经国家教师资格考试合格，有教育教学能力，经认定合格的，可以取得教师资格。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/教师资格条例.md#art-6" title="被《教师资格条例》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 10** The State shall implement a system of teacher qualification.  
Any Chinese citizen who abides by the Constitution and laws, loves the educational cause, has good ideological and ethical character, possesses the academic qualifications prescribed by this Law or has passed the national teacher qualification examination, has the ability to educate and teach, and is recognized as qualified, may obtain teacher qualification.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　取得教师资格应当具备的相应学历是：  
（一）取得幼儿园教师资格，应当具备幼儿师范学校毕业及其以上学历；  
（二）取得小学教师资格，应当具备中等师范学校毕业及其以上学历；  
（三）取得初级中学教师、初级职业学校文化、专业课教师资格，应当具备高等师范专科学校或者其他大学专科毕业及其以上学历；  
（四）取得高级中学教师资格和中等专业学校、技工学校、职业高中文化课、专业课教师资格，应当具备高等师范院校本科或者其他大学本科毕业及其以上学历；取得中等专业学校、技工学校和职业高中学生实习指导教师资格应当具备的学历，由国务院教育行政部门规定；  
（五）取得高等学校教师资格，应当具备研究生或者大学本科毕业学历；  
（六）取得成人教育教师资格，应当按照成人教育的层次、类别，分别具备高等、中等学校毕业及其以上学历。  
不具备本法规定的教师资格学历的公民，申请获取教师资格，必须通过国家教师资格考试。国家教师资格考试制度由国务院规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/教师资格条例.md#art-7" title="被《教师资格条例》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** The corresponding academic qualifications required for obtaining teacher qualification shall be:  
(1) for obtaining the qualification of kindergarten teacher, one shall have graduated from a preschool normal school or above;  
(2) for obtaining the qualification of primary school teacher, one shall have graduated from a secondary normal school or above;  
(3) for obtaining the qualification of junior middle school teacher or teacher of general knowledge courses and specialized courses in junior vocational schools, one shall have graduated from a junior college of higher normal education or other junior college or above;  
(4) for obtaining the qualification of senior middle school teacher or teacher of general knowledge courses and specialized courses in specialized secondary schools, technical schools, and vocational high schools, one shall have graduated from a regular college or university of higher normal education or other regular college or university or above; the academic qualifications required for obtaining the qualification of teacher guiding students' practice in specialized secondary schools, technical schools and vocational high schools shall be prescribed by the administrative department for education under the State Council;  
(5) for obtaining the qualification of teacher in institutions of higher education, one shall have the educational background of postgraduate or undergraduate graduation from a regular college or university; and  
(6) for obtaining the qualification of teacher in adult education, one shall, according to the level and type of adult education, have graduated from an institution of higher education, a secondary school or above, respectively.  
Any citizen who does not possess the academic qualifications for teacher qualification prescribed by this Law, and who applies for obtaining teacher qualification, must pass the national teacher qualification examination. The system of national teacher qualification examination shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本法实施前已经在学校或者其他教育机构中任教的教师，未具备本法规定学历的，由国务院教育行政部门规定教师资格过渡办法。

**Article 12** For teachers who were already teaching in schools or other educational institutions before the implementation of this Law, but do not possess the academic qualifications prescribed by this Law, the administrative department for education under the State Council shall prescribe transitional measures for teacher qualification.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中小学教师资格由县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门认定。中等专业学校、技工学校的教师资格由县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门组织有关主管部门认定。普通高等学校的教师资格由国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市教育行政部门或者由其委托的学校认定。  
具备本法规定的学历或者经国家教师资格考试合格的公民，要求有关部门认定其教师资格的，有关部门应当依照本法规定的条件予以认定。  
取得教师资格的人员首次任教时，应当有试用期。

**Article 13** Teacher qualifications for primary and secondary schools shall be recognized by the administrative departments for education of local people's governments at or above the county level. Teacher qualifications for specialized secondary schools and technical schools shall be recognized by the administrative departments for education of local people's governments at or above the county level in conjunction with the relevant competent departments. Teacher qualifications for regular institutions of higher education shall be recognized by the administrative department for education under the State Council or by the administrative departments for education of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, or by schools authorized by them.  
Where a citizen who possesses the academic qualifications prescribed by this Law or has passed the national teacher qualification examination requests the relevant department to recognize his or her teacher qualification, the relevant department shall recognize it in accordance with the conditions prescribed by this Law.  
A person who has obtained teacher qualification shall, when first engaging in teaching, be subject to a probationary period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　受到剥夺政治权利或者故意犯罪受到有期徒刑以上刑事处罚的，不能取得教师资格；已经取得教师资格的，丧失教师资格。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/教师资格条例.md#art-18" title="被《教师资格条例》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 14** A person who has been subjected to deprivation of political rights or who has been subjected to criminal punishment of fixed-term imprisonment or above for an intentional crime shall not obtain teacher qualification; if teacher qualification has already been obtained, it shall be forfeited.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级师范学校毕业生，应当按照国家有关规定从事教育教学工作。  
国家鼓励非师范高等学校毕业生到中小学或者职业学校任教。

**Article 15** Graduates of normal schools at various levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, engage in educational and teaching work.  
The State shall encourage graduates of non-normal institutions of higher education to teach in primary schools, secondary schools or vocational schools.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实行教师职务制度，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 16** The State shall implement a system of teacher professional posts, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　学校和其他教育机构应当逐步实行教师聘任制。教师的聘任应当遵循双方地位平等的原则，由学校和教师签订聘任合同，明确规定双方的权利、义务和责任。  
实施教师聘任制的步骤、办法由国务院教育行政部门规定。

**Article 17** Schools and other educational institutions shall gradually implement a system of teacher employment. The employment of teachers shall follow the principle of equality of status between the two parties, and the school and the teacher shall conclude an employment contract, clearly specifying the rights, obligations and responsibilities of both parties.  
The procedures and measures for implementing the system of teacher employment shall be prescribed by the administrative department for education under the State Council.

### 第四章　培养和培训

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当办好师范教育，并采取措施，鼓励优秀青年进入各级师范学校学习。各级教师进修学校承担培训中小学教师的任务。  
非师范学校应当承担培养和培训中小学教师的任务。  
各级师范学校学生享受专业奖学金。

**Article 18** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall run normal education well, and adopt measures to encourage outstanding young people to enter normal schools at various levels. Teacher training schools at various levels shall undertake the task of training primary and secondary school teachers.  
Non-normal schools shall undertake the task of cultivating and training primary and secondary school teachers.  
Students in normal schools at various levels shall enjoy professional scholarships.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各级人民政府教育行政部门、学校主管部门和学校应当制定教师培训规划，对教师进行多种形式的思想政治、业务培训。

**Article 19** The administrative departments for education of people's governments at all levels, the competent departments of schools, and the schools themselves shall formulate plans for teacher training, and provide teachers with various forms of ideological, political and professional training.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家机关、企业事业单位和其他社会组织应当为教师的社会调查和社会实践提供方便，给予协助。

**Article 20** State organs, enterprises, institutions and other social organizations shall provide convenience and assistance for teachers' social investigations and social practices.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，为少数民族地区和边远贫困地区培养、培训教师。

**Article 21** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to cultivate and train teachers for minority nationality areas and remote and poverty-stricken areas.

### 第五章　考核

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　学校或者其他教育机构应当对教师的政治思想、业务水平、工作态度和工作成绩进行考核。  
教育行政部门对教师的考核工作进行指导、监督。

**Article 22** A school or other educational institution shall conduct assessment of teachers' political ideology, professional competence, work attitude and work performance.  
The administrative department for education shall guide and supervise the assessment of teachers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　考核应当客观、公正、准确，充分听取教师本人、其他教师以及学生的意见。

**Article 23** Assessment shall be objective, impartial and accurate, and full consideration shall be given to the opinions of the teacher himself or herself, other teachers, and students.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　教师考核结果是受聘任教、晋升工资、实施奖惩的依据。

**Article 24** The results of teacher assessment shall be the basis for employment, promotion of salary, and implementation of rewards and punishments.

### 第六章　待遇

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　教师的平均工资水平应当不低于或者高于国家公务员的平均工资水平，并逐步提高。建立正常晋级增薪制度，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 25** The average salary level of teachers shall be not lower than or shall be higher than the average salary level of State civil servants, and shall be gradually increased. A regular system for promotion and salary increase shall be established, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中小学教师和职业学校教师享受教龄津贴和其他津贴，具体办法由国务院教育行政部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 26** Teachers in primary and secondary schools and teachers in vocational schools shall enjoy seniority allowances and other allowances, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department for education under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　地方各级人民政府对教师以及具有中专以上学历的毕业生到少数民族地区和边远贫困地区从事教育教学工作的，应当予以补贴。

**Article 27** Local people's governments at all levels shall grant subsidies to teachers and graduates with educational backgrounds at or above the level of specialized secondary schools who engage in educational and teaching work in minority nationality areas and remote and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地方各级人民政府和国务院有关部门，对城市教师住房的建设、租赁、出售实行优先、优惠。  
县、乡两级人民政府应当为农村中小学教师解决住房提供方便。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments under the State Council shall give priority and preferential treatment to the construction, leasing and sale of housing for urban teachers.  
People's governments at the county and township levels shall provide convenience for solving the housing problems of rural primary and secondary school teachers.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　教师的医疗同当地国家公务员享受同等的待遇；定期对教师进行身体健康检查，并因地制宜安排教师进行休养。  
医疗机构应当对当地教师的医疗提供方便。

**Article 29** Teachers shall enjoy the same medical treatment as local State civil servants; regular physical examinations shall be conducted for teachers, and arrangements for teachers to have rest and recuperation shall be made in light of local conditions.  
Medical institutions shall provide convenience for the medical treatment of local teachers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　教师退休或者退职后，享受国家规定的退休或者退职待遇。  
县级以上地方人民政府可以适当提高长期从事教育教学工作的中小学退休教师的退休金比例。

**Article 30** After teachers retire or resign from their posts, they shall enjoy the retirement or resignation benefits prescribed by the State.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may appropriately increase the proportion of pension for retired primary and secondary school teachers who have been engaged in educational and teaching work for a long time.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，改善国家补助、集体支付工资的中小学教师的待遇，逐步做到在工资收入上与国家支付工资的教师同工同酬，具体办法由地方各级人民政府根据本地区的实际情况规定。

**Article 31** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures to improve the treatment of primary and secondary school teachers whose salaries are subsidized by the State and paid by collectives, and gradually achieve equal pay for equal work in terms of salary income with teachers whose salaries are paid by the State. The specific measures shall be prescribed by local people's governments at all levels according to the actual conditions of their respective regions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　社会力量所办学校的教师的待遇，由举办者自行确定并予以保障。

**Article 32** The treatment of teachers in schools run by social forces shall be determined and guaranteed by the sponsors themselves.

### 第七章　奖励

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　教师在教育教学、培养人才、科学研究、教学改革、学校建设、社会服务、勤工俭学等方面成绩优异的，由所在学校予以表彰、奖励。  
国务院和地方各级人民政府及其有关部门对有突出贡献的教师，应当予以表彰、奖励。  
对有重大贡献的教师，依照国家有关规定授予荣誉称号。

**Article 33** Teachers who achieve outstanding results in education and teaching, cultivation of talents, scientific research, teaching reform, school construction, social services, work-study programs, etc., shall be commended and rewarded by the school in which they work.  
The State Council, local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall commend and reward teachers who have made outstanding contributions.  
Teachers who have made significant contributions shall be awarded honorary titles in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家支持和鼓励社会组织或者个人向依法成立的奖励教师的基金组织捐助资金，对教师进行奖励。

**Article 34** The State shall support and encourage social organizations or individuals to donate funds to lawfully established fund organizations for rewarding teachers, so as to reward teachers.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　侮辱、殴打教师的，根据不同情况，分别给予行政处分或者行政处罚；造成损害的，责令赔偿损失；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Anyone who insults or assaults a teacher shall, depending on the circumstances, be given an administrative sanction or an administrative penalty; if damage is caused, the person shall be ordered to pay compensation; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对依法提出申诉、控告、检举的教师进行打击报复的，由其所在单位或者上级机关责令改正；情节严重的，可以根据具体情况给予行政处分。  
国家工作人员对教师打击报复构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Anyone who retaliates against a teacher who has, according to law, lodged a petition, accusation or report shall be ordered by the unit to which the person belongs or the competent authority at a higher level to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, an administrative sanction may be imposed according to the specific circumstances.  
If a State functionary retaliates against a teacher and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　教师有下列情形之一的，由所在学校、其他教育机构或者教育行政部门给予行政处分或者解聘：  
（一）故意不完成教育教学任务给教育教学工作造成损失的；  
（二）体罚学生，经教育不改的；  
（三）品行不良、侮辱学生，影响恶劣的。  
教师有前款第（二）项、第（三）项所列情形之一，情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where a teacher falls under any of the following circumstances, the school, other educational institution or the administrative department for education shall impose an administrative sanction or dismiss the teacher:  
(1) intentionally failing to complete educational and teaching tasks, thereby causing losses to educational and teaching work;  
(2) physically punishing a student and refusing to correct after being educated; or  
(3) having bad conduct and insulting a student, with a vile influence.  
Where a teacher falls under the circumstances listed in item (2) or (3) of the preceding paragraph, and the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方人民政府对违反本法规定，拖欠教师工资或者侵犯教师其他合法权益的，应当责令其限期改正。  
违反国家财政制度、财务制度，挪用国家财政用于教育的经费，严重妨碍教育教学工作，拖欠教师工资，损害教师合法权益的，由上级机关责令限期归还被挪用的经费，并对直接责任人员给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where a local people's government, in violation of the provisions of this Law, defaults on teachers' salaries or infringes upon other legitimate rights and interests of teachers, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit.  
Where anyone violates the State's financial system and fiscal system, misappropriates funds allocated by the State finance for education, seriously hinders educational and teaching work, defaults on teachers' salaries, or damages the legitimate rights and interests of teachers, the competent authority at a higher level shall order the return of the misappropriated funds within a specified time limit, and impose administrative sanctions on the persons directly responsible; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　教师对学校或者其他教育机构侵犯其合法权益的，或者对学校或者其他教育机构作出的处理不服的，可以向教育行政部门提出申诉，教育行政部门应当在接到申诉的三十日内，作出处理。  
教师认为当地人民政府有关行政部门侵犯其根据本法规定享有的权利的，可以向同级人民政府或者上一级人民政府有关部门提出申诉，同级人民政府或者上一级人民政府有关部门应当作出处理。

**Article 39** Where a teacher considers that a school or other educational institution has infringed upon his or her legitimate rights and interests, or disagrees with the treatment given by the school or other educational institution, the teacher may lodge a petition with the administrative department for education, which shall make a decision within 30 days from the date of receipt of the petition.  
Where a teacher considers that the relevant department of the local people's government has infringed upon the rights enjoyed by him or her under the provisions of this Law, the teacher may lodge a petition with the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level, and the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall make a decision.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
（一）各级各类学校，是指实施学前教育、普通初等教育、普通中等教育、职业教育、普通高等教育以及特殊教育、成人教育的学校。  
（二）其他教育机构，是指少年宫以及地方教研室、电化教育机构等。  
（三）中小学教师，是指幼儿园、特殊教育机构、普通中小学、成人初等中等教育机构、职业中学以及其他教育机构的教师。

**Article 40** For the purposes of this Law, the meanings of the following terms are:  
(1) "schools of various levels and types" means schools providing pre-school education, regular primary education, regular secondary education, vocational education, regular higher education, as well as special education and adult education.  
(2) "other educational institutions" means children's palaces, local teaching research offices, audio-visual education institutions, and the like.  
(3) "primary and secondary school teachers" means teachers in kindergartens, special education institutions, regular primary and secondary schools, adult primary and secondary educational institutions, vocational secondary schools, and other educational institutions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　学校和其他教育机构中的教育教学辅助人员，其他类型的学校的教师和教育教学辅助人员，可以根据实际情况参照本法的有关规定执行。  
军队所属院校的教师和教育教学辅助人员，由中央军事委员会依照本法制定有关规定。

**Article 41** Teaching and educational auxiliary staff in schools and other educational institutions, as well as teachers and teaching and educational auxiliary staff in other types of schools, may, in light of the actual circumstances, refer to the relevant provisions of this Law for implementation.  
Teachers and teaching and educational auxiliary staff in colleges and schools affiliated with the armed forces shall have relevant provisions formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　外籍教师的聘任办法由国务院教育行政部门规定。

**Article 42** The measures for engaging foreign teachers shall be prescribed by the administrative department for education under the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本法自1994年1月1日起施行。

**Article 43** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1994.
