# 中华人民共和国防沙治沙法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-26  
**生效日期**：2018-10-26

> （2001年8月31日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议通过　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》修正）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为预防土地沙化，治理沙化土地，维护生态安全，促进经济和社会的可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of preventing land desertification, rehabilitating desertified land, maintaining ecological security, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，从事土地沙化的预防、沙化土地的治理和开发利用活动，必须遵守本法。  
土地沙化是指因气候变化和人类活动所导致的天然沙漠扩张和沙质土壤上植被破坏、沙土裸露的过程。  
本法所称土地沙化，是指主要因人类不合理活动所导致的天然沙漠扩张和沙质土壤上植被及覆盖物被破坏，形成流沙及沙土裸露的过程。  
本法所称沙化土地，包括已经沙化的土地和具有明显沙化趋势的土地。具体范围，由国务院批准的全国防沙治沙规划确定。

**Article 2** Any activity concerning the prevention of land desertification, the rehabilitation of desertified land, and the development and utilization of such land within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with this Law.  
Land desertification means the process of natural desert expansion and the destruction of vegetation on sandy soil and the exposure of sandy soil resulting from climate change and human activities.  
Land desertification as mentioned in this Law means the process of natural desert expansion and the destruction of vegetation and cover on sandy soil, leading to the formation of shifting sand and exposure of sandy soil, mainly due to irrational human activities.  
Desertified land as mentioned in this Law includes land that has already been desertified and land that shows an obvious tendency toward desertification. The specific scope shall be determined by the national plan for prevention and control of desertification approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　防沙治沙工作应当遵循以下原则：  
（一）统一规划，因地制宜，分步实施，坚持区域防治与重点防治相结合；  
（二）预防为主，防治结合，综合治理；  
（三）保护和恢复植被与合理利用自然资源相结合；  
（四）遵循生态规律，依靠科技进步；  
（五）改善生态环境与帮助农牧民脱贫致富相结合；  
（六）国家支持与地方自力更生相结合，政府组织与社会各界参与相结合，鼓励单位、个人承包防治；  
（七）保障防沙治沙者的合法权益。

**Article 3** The work of prevention and control of desertification shall follow the following principles:  
(1) unified planning, adaptation to local conditions, step-by-step implementation, and adherence to the combination of regional prevention and control with key prevention and control;  
(2) giving priority to prevention, combining prevention with control, and comprehensive treatment;  
(3) combining the protection and restoration of vegetation with the rational use of natural resources;  
(4) following ecological laws and relying on scientific and technological progress;  
(5) combining the improvement of the ecological environment with helping farmers and herdsmen to get rid of poverty and become prosperous;  
(6) combining State support with local self-reliance, combining government organization with participation by all sectors of society, and encouraging entities and individuals to contract for prevention and control; and  
(7) protecting the legitimate rights and interests of persons engaged in prevention and control of desertification.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和沙化土地所在地区的县级以上地方人民政府，应当将防沙治沙纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，保障和支持防沙治沙工作的开展。  
沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府，应当采取有效措施，预防土地沙化，治理沙化土地，保护和改善本行政区域的生态质量。  
国家在沙化土地所在地区，建立政府行政领导防沙治沙任期目标责任考核奖惩制度。沙化土地所在地区的县级以上地方人民政府，应当向同级人民代表大会及其常务委员会报告防沙治沙工作情况。

**Article 4** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where there is desertified land shall incorporate the prevention and control of desertification into the national economic and social development plans, and shall guarantee and support the development of the work on prevention and control of desertification.  
The local people's governments at various levels in areas where there is desertified land shall take effective measures to prevent land desertification, rehabilitate desertified land, and protect and improve the ecological quality of their respective administrative areas.  
The State shall establish a system of assessment, rewards and penalties regarding the responsibility of government administrative leaders for prevention and control of desertification during their term of office in areas where there is desertified land. The local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where there is desertified land shall report to the people's congresses at the corresponding level and their standing committees on the work of prevention and control of desertification.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在国务院领导下，国务院林业草原行政主管部门负责组织、协调、指导全国防沙治沙工作。  
国务院林业草原、农业、水利、土地、生态环境等行政主管部门和气象主管机构，按照有关法律规定的职责和国务院确定的职责分工，各负其责，密切配合，共同做好防沙治沙工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府组织、领导所属有关部门，按照职责分工，各负其责，密切配合，共同做好本行政区域的防沙治沙工作。

**Article 5** Under the leadership of the State Council, the competent administrative department for forestry and grassland under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, and guiding the national work on prevention and control of desertification.  
The competent administrative departments for forestry and grassland, agriculture, water conservancy, land, ecology and environment under the State Council, and the meteorological authority shall, within the limits of their respective duties as prescribed by relevant laws and the division of responsibilities as determined by the State Council, assume their respective responsibilities, closely cooperate, and jointly do a good job of prevention and control of desertification.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize and lead their relevant departments to assume their respective responsibilities according to the division of duties, closely cooperate, and jointly do a good job of prevention and control of desertification within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　使用土地的单位和个人，有防止该土地沙化的义务。  
使用已经沙化的土地的单位和个人，有治理该沙化土地的义务。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual that uses land has the obligation to prevent such land from becoming desertified.  
Any entity or individual that uses land which has already become desertified has the obligation to rehabilitate such desertified land.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家支持防沙治沙的科学研究和技术推广工作，发挥科研部门、机构在防沙治沙工作中的作用，培养防沙治沙专门技术人员，提高防沙治沙的科学技术水平。  
国家支持开展防沙治沙的国际合作。

**Article 7** The State supports scientific research and technological extension in prevention and control of desertification, gives scope to the role of scientific research departments and institutions in the work of prevention and control of desertification, trains specialized technical personnel in prevention and control of desertification, and raises the scientific and technological level of prevention and control of desertification.  
The State supports the development of international cooperation in prevention and control of desertification.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在防沙治沙工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由人民政府给予表彰和奖励；对保护和改善生态质量作出突出贡献的应当给予重奖。

**Article 8** Any entity or individual that has made outstanding achievements in the work of prevention and control of desertification shall be commended and rewarded by the people's government; those who have made outstanding contributions to the protection and improvement of ecological quality shall be given heavy rewards.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　沙化土地所在地区的各级人民政府应当组织有关部门开展防沙治沙知识的宣传教育，增强公民的防沙治沙意识，提高公民防沙治沙的能力。

**Article 9** The people's governments at various levels in areas where there is desertified land shall organize relevant departments to carry out publicity and education on knowledge of prevention and control of desertification, enhance citizens' awareness of prevention and control of desertification, and improve citizens' ability to prevent and control desertification.

### 第二章　防沙治沙规划

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　防沙治沙实行统一规划。从事防沙治沙活动，以及在沙化土地范围内从事开发利用活动，必须遵循防沙治沙规划。  
防沙治沙规划应当对遏制土地沙化扩展趋势，逐步减少沙化土地的时限、步骤、措施等作出明确规定，并将具体实施方案纳入国民经济和社会发展五年计划和年度计划。

**Article 10** Unified planning shall be implemented for prevention and control of desertification. Any activity of prevention and control of desertification and any activity of development and utilization within the scope of desertified land must comply with the plan for prevention and control of desertification.  
The plan for prevention and control of desertification shall clearly specify the time limits, steps, and measures for curbing the expansion of land desertification and gradually reducing desertified land, and shall incorporate specific implementation schemes into the five-year plans and annual plans for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院林业草原行政主管部门会同国务院农业、水利、土地、生态环境等有关部门编制全国防沙治沙规划，报国务院批准后实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依据全国防沙治沙规划，编制本行政区域的防沙治沙规划，报国务院或者国务院指定的有关部门批准后实施。  
沙化土地所在地区的市、县人民政府，应当依据上一级人民政府的防沙治沙规划，组织编制本行政区域的防沙治沙规划，报上一级人民政府批准后实施。  
防沙治沙规划的修改，须经原批准机关批准；未经批准，任何单位和个人不得改变防沙治沙规划。

**Article 11** The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments such as agriculture, water conservancy, land, ecology and environment under the State Council, formulate the national plan for prevention and control of desertification, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, on the basis of the national plan for prevention and control of desertification, formulate plans for prevention and control of desertification for their respective administrative areas, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council or the relevant department designated by the State Council.  
The people's governments of cities and counties in areas where there is desertified land shall, on the basis of the plan for prevention and control of desertification of the people's government at the next higher level, organize the formulation of plans for prevention and control of desertification for their respective administrative areas, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the people's government at the next higher level.  
Any amendment to a plan for prevention and control of desertification must be approved by the original approving authority; without such approval, no entity or individual may alter the plan for prevention and control of desertification.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　编制防沙治沙规划，应当根据沙化土地所处的地理位置、土地类型、植被状况、气候和水资源状况、土地沙化程度等自然条件及其所发挥的生态、经济功能，对沙化土地实行分类保护、综合治理和合理利用。  
在规划期内不具备治理条件的以及因保护生态的需要不宜开发利用的连片沙化土地，应当规划为沙化土地封禁保护区，实行封禁保护。沙化土地封禁保护区的范围，由全国防沙治沙规划以及省、自治区、直辖市防沙治沙规划确定。

**Article 12** When formulating a plan for prevention and control of desertification, the natural conditions of desertified land such as its geographical location, land type, vegetation status, climate and water resources status, degree of desertification, and its ecological and economic functions shall be taken into account to implement classified protection, comprehensive treatment, and rational utilization of desertified land.  
The contiguous desertified land which is not suitable for rehabilitation during the planning period and which should not be developed and utilized due to the need for ecological protection shall be planned as a closed protected area for desertified land, where closed protection shall be implemented. The scope of closed protected areas for desertified land shall be determined by the national plan for prevention and control of desertification and the plans for prevention and control of desertification of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　防沙治沙规划应当与土地利用总体规划相衔接；防沙治沙规划中确定的沙化土地用途，应当符合本级人民政府的土地利用总体规划。

**Article 13** The plan for prevention and control of desertification shall be coordinated with the overall plan for land utilization; the use of desertified land as determined in the plan for prevention and control of desertification shall conform to the overall plan for land utilization of the people's government at the corresponding level.

### 第三章　土地沙化的预防

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院林业草原行政主管部门组织其他有关行政主管部门对全国土地沙化情况进行监测、统计和分析，并定期公布监测结果。  
县级以上地方人民政府林业草原或者其他有关行政主管部门，应当按照土地沙化监测技术规程，对沙化土地进行监测，并将监测结果向本级人民政府及上一级林业草原或者其他有关行政主管部门报告。

**Article 14** The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland under the State Council shall organize the other relevant administrative departments to monitor, compile statistics, and analyze the national situation of land desertification, and shall regularly publish the monitoring results.  
The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland or other relevant administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the technical regulations for monitoring land desertification, monitor desertified land, and report the monitoring results to the people's government at the corresponding level and to the competent administrative department for forestry and grassland or other relevant administrative department at the next higher level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方人民政府林业草原或者其他有关行政主管部门，在土地沙化监测过程中，发现土地发生沙化或者沙化程度加重的，应当及时报告本级人民政府。收到报告的人民政府应当责成有关行政主管部门制止导致土地沙化的行为，并采取有效措施进行治理。  
各级气象主管机构应当组织对气象干旱和沙尘暴天气进行监测、预报，发现气象干旱或者沙尘暴天气征兆时，应当及时报告当地人民政府。收到报告的人民政府应当采取预防措施，必要时公布灾情预报，并组织林业草原、农（牧）业等有关部门采取应急措施，避免或者减轻风沙危害。

**Article 15** Where, in the course of monitoring land desertification, the competent administrative department for forestry and grassland or other relevant administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level discovers that land is becoming desertified or the degree of desertification is increasing, it shall promptly report such discovery to the people's government at the corresponding level. The people's government that receives the report shall instruct the relevant administrative department to stop the acts causing land desertification and take effective measures for rehabilitation.  
The meteorological authorities at various levels shall organize the monitoring and forecasting of meteorological drought and sandstorm weather; when signs of meteorological drought or sandstorm weather are discovered, they shall promptly report to the local people's government. The people's government that receives the report shall take preventive measures, publish the disaster forecast when necessary, and organize the relevant departments such as forestry and grassland, agriculture (animal husbandry) to take emergency measures to avoid or mitigate wind and sand hazards.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　沙化土地所在地区的县级以上地方人民政府应当按照防沙治沙规划，划出一定比例的土地，因地制宜地营造防风固沙林网、林带，种植多年生灌木和草本植物。由林业草原行政主管部门负责确定植树造林的成活率、保存率的标准和具体任务，并逐片组织实施，明确责任，确保完成。  
除了抚育更新性质的采伐外，不得批准对防风固沙林网、林带进行采伐。在对防风固沙林网、林带进行抚育更新性质的采伐之前，必须在其附近预先形成接替林网和林带。  
对林木更新困难地区已有的防风固沙林网、林带，不得批准采伐。

**Article 16** The local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where there is desertified land shall, in accordance with the plan for prevention and control of desertification, designate a certain proportion of land and, based on local conditions, create windbreak and sand-fixing forest networks and belts and plant perennial shrubs and herbaceous plants. The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland shall be responsible for determining the standards of survival rate and preservation rate of afforestation and the specific tasks, and organize the implementation by individual plots, define responsibilities, and ensure completion.  
Except for felling for the purpose of tending and regeneration, no approval may be granted for felling of windbreak and sand-fixing forest networks and belts. Before felling for the purpose of tending and regeneration of windbreak and sand-fixing forest networks and belts, replacement forest networks and belts must be formed in advance nearby.  
With respect to existing windbreak and sand-fixing forest networks and belts in areas where forest regeneration is difficult, no approval for felling may be granted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止在沙化土地上砍挖灌木、药材及其他固沙植物。  
沙化土地所在地区的县级人民政府，应当制定植被管护制度，严格保护植被，并根据需要在乡（镇）、村建立植被管护组织，确定管护人员。  
在沙化土地范围内，各类土地承包合同应当包括植被保护责任的内容。

**Article 17** It is prohibited to cut or dig up shrubs, medicinal herbs, and other sand-fixing plants on desertified land.  
The people's governments at the county level in areas where there is desertified land shall formulate a system for the management and protection of vegetation, strictly protect vegetation, and, as needed, establish vegetation management and protection organizations at the township (town) and village levels and designate management and protection personnel.  
Within the scope of desertified land, all types of land contracts shall include provisions on the responsibility for vegetation protection.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　草原地区的地方各级人民政府，应当加强草原的管理和建设，由林业草原行政主管部门会同畜牧业行政主管部门负责指导、组织农牧民建设人工草场，控制载畜量，调整牲畜结构，改良牲畜品种，推行牲畜圈养和草场轮牧，消灭草原鼠害、虫害，保护草原植被，防止草原退化和沙化。  
草原实行以产草量确定载畜量的制度。由林业草原行政主管部门会同畜牧业行政主管部门负责制定载畜量的标准和有关规定，并逐级组织实施，明确责任，确保完成。

**Article 18** The local people's governments at various levels in grassland areas shall strengthen the management and construction of grasslands. The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland shall, jointly with the competent administrative department for animal husbandry, be responsible for guiding and organizing farmers and herdsmen to build artificial pastures, control the livestock carrying capacity, adjust the structure of livestock, improve livestock breeds, promote livestock pen feeding and rotational grazing of pastures, eliminate rodent and insect pests in grasslands, protect grassland vegetation, and prevent grassland degradation and desertification.  
A system of determining the livestock carrying capacity based on grass yield shall be implemented for grasslands. The competent administrative department for forestry and grassland shall, jointly with the competent administrative department for animal husbandry, be responsible for formulating the standards for livestock carrying capacity and relevant regulations, and organizing the implementation level by level, defining responsibilities, and ensuring completion.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　沙化土地所在地区的县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门，应当加强流域和区域水资源的统一调配和管理，在编制流域和区域水资源开发利用规划和供水计划时，必须考虑整个流域和区域植被保护的用水需求，防止因地下水和上游水资源的过度开发利用，导致植被破坏和土地沙化。该规划和计划经批准后，必须严格实施。  
沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府应当节约用水，发展节水型农牧业和其他产业。

**Article 19** The competent administrative department for water conservancy of the local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where there is desertified land shall strengthen the unified allocation and management of water resources in river basins and regions. When formulating plans for the development and utilization of water resources in river basins and regions and water supply plans, the water demand for vegetation protection in the entire basin and region must be taken into account to prevent damage to vegetation and land desertification caused by excessive exploitation and utilization of groundwater and upstream water resources. Such plans and schedules, once approved, must be strictly implemented.  
The local people's governments at various levels in areas where there is desertified land shall economize on water, develop water-saving agriculture, animal husbandry, and other industries.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　沙化土地所在地区的县级以上地方人民政府，不得批准在沙漠边缘地带和林地、草原开垦耕地；已经开垦并对生态产生不良影响的，应当有计划地组织退耕还林还草。

**Article 20** The local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where there is desertified land may not approve the reclamation of farmland on the fringes of deserts, in forest land, or in grasslands; where reclamation has already been carried out and has caused adverse effects on the ecology, the reclamation shall be organized to return farmland to forest or grassland in a planned manner.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在沙化土地范围内从事开发建设活动的，必须事先就该项目可能对当地及相关地区生态产生的影响进行环境影响评价，依法提交环境影响报告；环境影响报告应当包括有关防沙治沙的内容。

**Article 21** A party that engages in development and construction activities within the scope of desertified land must conduct an environmental impact assessment in advance on the possible impacts of the project on the ecology of the local and relevant areas, and submit an environmental impact report according to law; the environmental impact report shall include contents relating to sand prevention and control.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内，禁止一切破坏植被的活动。  
禁止在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内安置移民。对沙化土地封禁保护区范围内的农牧民，县级以上地方人民政府应当有计划地组织迁出，并妥善安置。沙化土地封禁保护区范围内尚未迁出的农牧民的生产生活，由沙化土地封禁保护区主管部门妥善安排。  
未经国务院或者国务院指定的部门同意，不得在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内进行修建铁路、公路等建设活动。

**Article 22** Within the scope of a closed desertified land protection area, all activities that destroy vegetation are prohibited.  
It is prohibited to resettle migrants within the scope of a closed desertified land protection area. With respect to the farmers and herdsmen within the scope of a closed desertified land protection area, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize their relocation in a planned manner and properly resettle them. The production and livelihood of the farmers and herdsmen who have not yet been relocated within the scope of the closed desertified land protection area shall be properly arranged by the competent department in charge of the closed desertified land protection area.  
Without the consent of the State Council or the department designated by the State Council, no construction activities such as building railways or highways may be carried out within the scope of a closed desertified land protection area.

### 第四章　沙化土地的治理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府，应当按照防沙治沙规划，组织有关部门、单位和个人，因地制宜地采取人工造林种草、飞机播种造林种草、封沙育林育草和合理调配生态用水等措施，恢复和增加植被，治理已经沙化的土地。

**Article 23** The local people's governments at all levels in areas where desertified land is located shall, in accordance with the sand prevention and control plan, organize relevant departments, entities and individuals to take measures such as artificial afforestation and grass planting, aerial seeding for afforestation and grass planting, fencing for sand afforestation and grass cultivation, and rational allocation of ecological water use, to restore and increase vegetation and to control land that has already become desertified.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励单位和个人在自愿的前提下，捐资或者以其他形式开展公益性的治沙活动。  
县级以上地方人民政府林业草原或者其他有关行政主管部门，应当为公益性治沙活动提供治理地点和无偿技术指导。  
从事公益性治沙的单位和个人，应当按照县级以上地方人民政府林业草原或者其他有关行政主管部门的技术要求进行治理，并可以将所种植的林、草委托他人管护或者交由当地人民政府有关行政主管部门管护。

**Article 24** The State encourages entities and individuals, on a voluntary basis, to donate funds or to carry out public welfare sand control activities in other forms.  
The forestry and grassland or other relevant competent administrative departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide control sites and free technical guidance for public welfare sand control activities.  
Entities and individuals engaged in public welfare sand control shall carry out control in accordance with the technical requirements of the forestry and grassland or other relevant competent administrative departments of the local people's government at or above the county level, and may entrust the planted trees and grasses to others for management and protection or to the relevant competent administrative departments of the local people's government for management and protection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　使用已经沙化的国有土地的使用权人和农民集体所有土地的承包经营权人，必须采取治理措施，改善土地质量；确实无能力完成治理任务的，可以委托他人治理或者与他人合作治理。委托或者合作治理的，应当签订协议，明确各方的权利和义务。  
沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府及其有关行政主管部门、技术推广单位，应当为土地使用权人和承包经营权人的治沙活动提供技术指导。  
采取退耕还林还草、植树种草或者封育措施治沙的土地使用权人和承包经营权人，按照国家有关规定，享受人民政府提供的政策优惠。

**Article 25** The holders of the right to use State-owned land that has already become desertified and the holders of the right to contracted management of collectively owned land must take control measures to improve the quality of the land; if they are genuinely unable to complete the control task, they may entrust others to carry out the control or cooperate with others in the control. Where control is entrusted or cooperated, an agreement shall be signed to clarify the rights and obligations of each party.  
The local people's governments at all levels in areas where desertified land is located and the relevant competent administrative departments and technology extension units shall provide technical guidance to the land use right holders and contracted management right holders in their sand control activities.  
Land use right holders and contracted management right holders who adopt measures such as returning farmland to forest or grassland, afforestation and grass planting, or fencing for cultivation to control sand shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, enjoy policy preferences provided by the people's government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　不具有土地所有权或者使用权的单位和个人从事营利性治沙活动的，应当先与土地所有权人或者使用权人签订协议，依法取得土地使用权。  
在治理活动开始之前，从事营利性治沙活动的单位和个人应当向治理项目所在地的县级以上地方人民政府林业草原行政主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府指定的其他行政主管部门提出治理申请，并附具下列文件：  
（一）被治理土地权属的合法证明文件和治理协议；  
（二）符合防沙治沙规划的治理方案；  
（三）治理所需的资金证明。

**Article 26** Where an entity or individual that does not have ownership or use rights over land engages in profit-making sand control activities, it shall first sign an agreement with the land owner or use right holder and obtain the land use right according to law.  
Before the commencement of the control activities, the entity or individual engaged in profit-making sand control activities shall submit a control application to the forestry and grassland competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the control project is located, or to another competent administrative department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall attach the following documents:  
(1) the legal certification documents for the ownership of the land to be controlled and the control agreement;  
(2) a control plan that complies with the sand prevention and control plan;  
(3) the certification of funds required for the control.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　[本法第二十六条](#art-26)第二款第二项所称治理方案，应当包括以下内容：  
（一）治理范围界限；  
（二）分阶段治理目标和治理期限；  
（三）主要治理措施；  
（四）经当地水行政主管部门同意的用水来源和用水量指标；  
（五）治理后的土地用途和植被管护措施；  
（六）其他需要载明的事项。

**Article 27** The control plan referred to in item (2) of paragraph 2 of Article 26 of this Law shall include the following contents:  
(1) the boundaries of the control scope;  
(2) the phased control objectives and the control period;  
(3) the main control measures;  
(4) the water use source and water use quantity indicators approved by the local water administrative department;  
(5) the intended use of the land after control and the vegetation management and protection measures;  
(6) other matters that need to be specified.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　从事营利性治沙活动的单位和个人，必须按照治理方案进行治理。  
国家保护沙化土地治理者的合法权益。在治理者取得合法土地权属的治理范围内，未经治理者同意，其他任何单位和个人不得从事治理或者开发利用活动。

**Article 28** An entity or individual engaged in profit-making sand control activities must carry out the control in accordance with the control plan.  
The State protects the lawful rights and interests of the controller of desertified land. Within the control scope for which the controller has obtained lawful land ownership, without the consent of the controller, no other entity or individual may engage in control or development and utilization activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　治理者完成治理任务后，应当向县级以上地方人民政府受理治理申请的行政主管部门提出验收申请。经验收合格的，受理治理申请的行政主管部门应当发给治理合格证明文件；经验收不合格的，治理者应当继续治理。

**Article 29** After completing the control task, the controller shall submit an application for acceptance inspection to the competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level that accepted the control application. If the acceptance inspection is passed, the competent administrative department that accepted the control application shall issue a certificate of qualified control; if the acceptance inspection is not passed, the controller shall continue the control.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　已经沙化的土地范围内的铁路、公路、河流和水渠两侧，城镇、村庄、厂矿和水库周围，实行单位治理责任制，由县级以上地方人民政府下达治理责任书，由责任单位负责组织造林种草或者采取其他治理措施。

**Article 30** On both sides of railways, highways, rivers and canals within the scope of desertified land, and around towns, villages, factories, mines and reservoirs, a responsibility system for entity control shall be implemented, and the local people's government at or above the county level shall issue a control responsibility document, and the responsible entity shall be responsible for organizing afforestation and grass planting or taking other control measures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府，可以组织当地农村集体经济组织及其成员在自愿的前提下，对已经沙化的土地进行集中治理。农村集体经济组织及其成员投入的资金和劳力，可以折算为治理项目的股份、资本金，也可以采取其他形式给予补偿。

**Article 31** The local people's governments at all levels in areas where desertified land is located may organize the local rural collective economic organizations and their members, on a voluntary basis, to carry out centralized control of desertified land. The funds and labor invested by the rural collective economic organizations and their members may be converted into shares or capital contributions of the control project, or compensation may be given in other forms.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院和沙化土地所在地区的地方各级人民政府应当在本级财政预算中按照防沙治沙规划通过项目预算安排资金，用于本级人民政府确定的防沙治沙工程。在安排扶贫、农业、水利、道路、矿产、能源、农业综合开发等项目时，应当根据具体情况，设立若干防沙治沙子项目。

**Article 32** The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in areas where desertified land is located shall, in their fiscal budgets, allocate funds through project budgets in accordance with the sand prevention and control plan for the sand prevention and control projects determined by the people's government at the corresponding level. When arranging projects for poverty alleviation, agriculture, water conservancy, roads, minerals, energy, comprehensive agricultural development, etc., they shall, based on the specific circumstances, establish a number of sub-projects for sand prevention and control.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当制定优惠政策，鼓励和支持单位和个人防沙治沙。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，根据防沙治沙的面积和难易程度，给予从事防沙治沙活动的单位和个人资金补助、财政贴息以及税费减免等政策优惠。  
单位和个人投资进行防沙治沙的，在投资阶段免征各种税收；取得一定收益后，可以免征或者减征有关税收。

**Article 33** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate preferential policies to encourage and support entities and individuals in sand prevention and control.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and based on the area and difficulty of sand prevention and control, grant policy preferences such as financial subsidies, fiscal interest subsidies, and tax and fee reductions or exemptions to entities and individuals engaged in sand prevention and control activities.  
Where entities and individuals invest in sand prevention and control, they shall be exempted from various taxes during the investment phase; after obtaining certain returns, they may be exempted from or subject to reduced taxes.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　使用已经沙化的国有土地从事治沙活动的，经县级以上人民政府依法批准，可以享有不超过七十年的土地使用权。具体年限和管理办法，由国务院规定。  
使用已经沙化的集体所有土地从事治沙活动的，治理者应当与土地所有人签订土地承包合同。具体承包期限和当事人的其他权利、义务由承包合同双方依法在土地承包合同中约定。县级人民政府依法根据土地承包合同向治理者颁发土地使用权证书，保护集体所有沙化土地治理者的土地使用权。

**Article 34** Where a party uses State-owned land that has already become desertified for sand control activities, with the approval according to law of the people's government at or above the county level, it may enjoy the land use right for a period not exceeding seventy years. The specific term and management measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Where a party uses collectively owned land that has already become desertified for sand control activities, the controller shall sign a land contract with the land owner. The specific contract period and other rights and obligations of the parties shall be agreed upon by both parties to the contract in the land contract according to law. The people's government at the county level shall, according to law, issue a certificate of land use right to the controller based on the land contract, protecting the land use right of the controller of collectively owned desertified land.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　因保护生态的特殊要求，将治理后的土地批准划为自然保护区或者沙化土地封禁保护区的，批准机关应当给予治理者合理的经济补偿。

**Article 35** Where, due to special requirements for ecological protection, the controlled land is approved to be designated as a nature reserve or a closed desertified land protection area, the approving authority shall grant reasonable economic compensation to the controller.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家根据防沙治沙的需要，组织设立防沙治沙重点科研项目和示范、推广项目，并对防沙治沙、沙区能源、沙生经济作物、节水灌溉、防止草原退化、沙地旱作农业等方面的科学研究与技术推广给予资金补助、税费减免等政策优惠。

**Article 36** The State, in accordance with the needs of sand prevention and control, organizes the establishment of key scientific research projects and demonstration and extension projects for sand prevention and control, and grants policy preferences such as financial subsidies and tax and fee reductions or exemptions for scientific research and technology extension in areas such as sand prevention and control, energy in sandy areas, economic crops growing in sandy areas, water-saving irrigation, prevention of grassland degradation, and dryland farming on sandy land.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得截留、挪用防沙治沙资金。  
县级以上人民政府审计机关，应当依法对防沙治沙资金使用情况实施审计监督。

**Article 37** No entity or individual may intercept or misappropriate funds for sand prevention and control.  
The auditing authorities of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, carry out audit supervision over the use of funds for sand prevention and control.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款规定，在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内从事破坏植被活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原行政主管部门责令停止违法行为；有违法所得的，没收其违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 22 of this Law, activities that destroy vegetation are engaged in within the scope of a closed desertified land protection area, the forestry and grassland competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第一款规定，国有土地使用权人和农民集体所有土地承包经营权人未采取防沙治沙措施，造成土地严重沙化的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原行政主管部门责令限期治理；造成国有土地严重沙化的，县级以上人民政府可以收回国有土地使用权。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 25 of this Law, the holder of the right to use State-owned land or the holder of the right to contracted management of collectively owned land fails to take sand prevention and control measures, causing serious desertification of the land, the forestry and grassland competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order control within a specified period; if serious desertification of State-owned land is caused, the people's government at or above the county level may recover the State-owned land use right.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本法规定，进行营利性治沙活动，造成土地沙化加重的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责受理营利性治沙申请的行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以并处每公顷五千元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, profit-making sand control activities are carried out, causing aggravation of land desertification, the competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for accepting profit-making sand control applications shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and may also impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan per hectare.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第一款规定，不按照治理方案进行治理的，或者违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)规定，经验收不合格又不按要求继续治理的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责受理营利性治沙申请的行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，可以并处相当于治理费用一倍以上三倍以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 28 of this Law, fails to carry out treatment in accordance with the treatment plan, or, in violation of the provisions of Article 29 of this Law, fails to meet the acceptance inspection and fails to continue treatment as required, the competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for accepting applications for for-profit desertification treatment shall order cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the treatment cost.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第二款规定，未经治理者同意，擅自在他人的治理范围内从事治理或者开发利用活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责受理营利性治沙申请的行政主管部门责令停止违法行为；给治理者造成损失的，应当赔偿损失。

**Article 42** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 28 of this Law, without the consent of the treatment party, engages in treatment or development and utilization activities within another person's treatment area, the competent administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for accepting applications for for-profit desertification treatment shall order cessation of the illegal act; if losses are caused to the treatment party, compensation shall be made for the losses.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由所在单位、监察机关或者上级行政主管部门依法给予行政处分：  
（一）违反[本法第十五条](#art-15)第一款规定，发现土地发生沙化或者沙化程度加重不及时报告的，或者收到报告后不责成有关行政主管部门采取措施的；  
（二）违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第二款、第三款规定，批准采伐防风固沙林网、林带的；  
（三）违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)规定，批准在沙漠边缘地带和林地、草原开垦耕地的；  
（四）违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第二款规定，在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内安置移民的；  
（五）违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第三款规定，未经批准在沙化土地封禁保护区范围内进行修建铁路、公路等建设活动的。

**Article 43** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of this Law, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law by the entity where they work, the supervisory organ, or the competent administrative department at a higher level:  
(1) In violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 15 of this Law, failing to timely report the discovery of land desertification or aggravation of desertification, or after receiving the report, failing to instruct the relevant administrative department to take measures;  
(2) In violation of the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 16 of this Law, approving the felling of windbreak and sand-fixing forest networks and belts;  
(3) In violation of the provisions of Article 20 of this Law, approving the reclamation of cultivated land in desert fringe areas and forest land and grassland;  
(4) In violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 22 of this Law, resettling migrants within the enclosed protected areas of desertified land;  
(5) In violation of the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 22 of this Law, engaging in construction activities such as building railways and highways within the enclosed protected areas of desertified land without approval.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反[本法第三十七条](#art-37)第一款规定，截留、挪用防沙治沙资金的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由监察机关或者上级行政主管部门依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 37 of this Law, intercepts or misappropriates funds for desertification prevention and control, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law by the supervisory organ or the competent administrative department at a higher level; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　防沙治沙监督管理人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** If any supervisor or manager of desertification prevention and control abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　[本法第五条](#art-5)第二款中所称的有关法律，是指《中华人民共和国森林法》《中华人民共和国草原法》《中华人民共和国水土保持法》《中华人民共和国土地管理法》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》和《中华人民共和国气象法》。

**Article 46** The relevant laws referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 5 of this Law mean the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China, the Grassland Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Water and Soil Conservation, the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Meteorology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本法自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 47** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
