### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了普及和加强国防教育，发扬爱国主义精神，促进国防建设和社会主义精神文明建设，根据宪法和《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国教育法》，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution, the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Education Law of the People's Republic of China, for the purposes of popularizing and strengthening national defense education, promoting the spirit of patriotism, and facilitating national defense construction and the building of socialist spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家在全体公民中开展以爱国主义为核心，以履行国防义务为目的，与国防和军队建设有关的理论、知识、技能以及科技、法律、心理等方面的国防教育。  
国防教育是建设和巩固国防的基础，是增强民族凝聚力、提高全民素质的重要途径。

**Article 2** The State shall carry out national defense education among all citizens, with patriotism as its core and for the purpose of fulfilling national defense obligations, covering theories, knowledge, skills, as well as science and technology, law, psychology, and other aspects related to national defense and military construction.  
National defense education is the foundation for building and consolidating national defense and an important means of enhancing national cohesion and improving the quality of the entire population.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国防教育坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚持总体国家安全观，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，使全体公民增强国防观念、强化忧患意识、掌握国防知识、提高国防技能，依法履行国防义务。

**Article 3** National defense education shall adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era; adhere to a holistic approach to national security; cultivate and practice the core socialist values; and strengthen the sense of community for the Chinese nation, so that all citizens may enhance their awareness of national defense, strengthen their sense of crisis, acquire national defense knowledge, improve national defense skills, and perform national defense obligations according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　坚持中国共产党对国防教育工作的领导，建立集中统一、分工负责、军地协同的国防教育领导体制。

**Article 4** The leadership of the Chinese Communist Party over national defense education shall be upheld, and a centralized, unified leadership system for national defense education that features division of responsibilities and military-civilian coordination shall be established.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央全民国防教育主管部门负责全国国防教育工作的指导、监督和统筹协调。中央国家机关各部门在各自的职责范围内负责国防教育工作。中央军事委员会机关有关部门按照职责分工，协同中央全民国防教育主管部门开展国防教育。  
县级以上地方全民国防教育主管部门负责本行政区域内国防教育工作的指导、监督和统筹协调；其他有关部门在规定的职责范围内开展国防教育工作。驻地军事机关协同地方全民国防教育主管部门开展国防教育。

**Article 5** The central competent department for national defense education for all citizens shall be responsible for guidance, supervision, and overall coordination of national defense education work nationwide. All departments of the central state organs shall be responsible for national defense education work within their respective scopes of duties. Relevant departments under the Central Military Commission shall, in accordance with their division of duties, coordinate with the central competent department for national defense education for all citizens in carrying out national defense education.  
The competent departments for national defense education for all citizens at or above the county level shall be responsible for guidance, supervision, and overall coordination of national defense education work within their respective administrative regions; other relevant departments shall carry out national defense education work within their prescribed scopes of duties. The local military organs shall coordinate with the local competent departments for national defense education for all citizens in carrying out national defense education.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国防教育贯彻全民参与、长期坚持、讲求实效的方针，实行经常教育与集中教育相结合、普及教育与重点教育相结合、理论教育与行为教育相结合的原则，针对不同对象确定相应的教育内容分类组织实施。

**Article 6** National defense education shall follow the policy of participation by all, long-term adherence, and emphasis on practical results, and shall implement the principles of combining regular education with intensive education, universal education with targeted education, and theoretical education with behavioral education, and shall determine corresponding educational content for different targets and organize implementation by category.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国公民都有接受国防教育的权利和义务。  
普及和加强国防教育是全社会的共同责任。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织和其他组织，都应当组织本地区、本部门、本单位开展国防教育。

**Article 7** Every citizen of the People's Republic of China has the right and obligation to receive national defense education.  
Popularizing and strengthening national defense education is the common responsibility of the whole society.  
All state organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations shall organize the conducting of national defense education in their respective regions, departments, or units.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国防动员、兵役、退役军人事务、国防科研生产、边防海防、人民防空、国防交通等工作的主管部门，依照本法和有关法律、法规的规定，开展国防教育。  
工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会和其他群团组织，应当在各自的工作范围内开展国防教育。

**Article 8** The competent departments for national defense mobilization, military service, veterans' affairs, national defense scientific research and production, border and coastal defense, civil air defense, national defense transportation, and other work shall carry out national defense education in accordance with this Law and the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and other mass organizations shall carry out national defense education within their respective scopes of work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队按照中央军事委员会的有关规定开展国防教育。

**Article 9** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall carry out national defense education in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持、鼓励社会组织和个人开展有益于国防教育的活动。

**Article 10** The State supports and encourages social organizations and individuals to carry out activities beneficial to national defense education.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在国防教育工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in national defense education work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　每年九月的第三个星期六为全民国防教育日。

**Article 12** The third Saturday of September each year shall be the National Defense Education Day for All Citizens.

### 第二章　学校国防教育

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　学校国防教育是全民国防教育的基础，是实施素质教育的重要内容。  
教育行政部门应当将国防教育列入工作计划，加强对学校国防教育的组织、指导和监督，并对学校国防教育工作定期进行考核。  
学校应当将国防教育列入学校的工作和教学计划，采取有效措施，保证国防教育的质量和效果。

**Article 13** School national defense education is the foundation of national defense education for all citizens and an important part of implementing quality education.  
Education administrative departments shall include national defense education in their work plans, strengthen the organization, guidance, and supervision of national defense education in schools, and conduct regular assessments of school national defense education work.  
Schools shall include national defense education in their work and teaching plans and take effective measures to ensure the quality and effectiveness of national defense education.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　小学和初级中学应当将国防教育的内容纳入有关课程，将课堂教学与课外活动相结合，使小学生具备一定的国防意识、初中学生掌握初步的国防知识和国防技能。  
小学和初级中学可以组织学生开展以国防教育为主题的少年军校活动。教育行政部门、共产主义青年团和其他有关部门应当加强对少年军校活动的指导与管理。  
小学和初级中学可以根据需要聘请校外辅导员，协助学校开展多种形式的国防教育活动。

**Article 14** Primary schools and junior secondary schools shall incorporate the content of national defense education into relevant courses, combine classroom teaching with extracurricular activities, so that primary school students acquire a certain awareness of national defense and junior secondary school students acquire basic national defense knowledge and skills.  
Primary schools and junior secondary schools may organize students to carry out juvenile military academy activities with national defense education as the theme. Education administrative departments, the Communist Youth League, and other relevant departments shall strengthen guidance and management over juvenile military academy activities.  
Primary schools and junior secondary schools may, as needed, engage off-campus counselors to assist schools in carrying out various forms of national defense education activities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　高中阶段学校应当在有关课程中安排专门的国防教育内容，将课堂教学与军事训练相结合，使学生掌握基本的国防理论、知识和技能，具备基本的国防观念。  
普通高等学校应当设置国防教育课程，加强国防教育相关学科建设，开展形式多样的国防教育活动，使学生掌握必要的国防理论、知识和技能，具备较强的国防观念。

**Article 15** Senior secondary schools shall arrange specific content of national defense education in relevant courses, combine classroom teaching with military training, so that students may master basic national defense theories, knowledge, and skills, and possess a basic concept of national defense.  
Regular institutions of higher education shall set up national defense education courses, strengthen the development of disciplines related to national defense education, and carry out various forms of national defense education activities, so that students may master necessary national defense theories, knowledge, and skills, and possess a relatively strong concept of national defense.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　学校国防教育应当与兵役宣传教育相结合，增强学生依法服兵役的意识，营造服兵役光荣的良好氛围。

**Article 16** School national defense education shall be combined with military service publicity and education to enhance students' awareness of performing military service according to law and create a favorable atmosphere that honors military service.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　普通高等学校、高中阶段学校应当按照规定组织学生军事训练。  
普通高等学校、高中阶段学校学生的军事训练，由学校负责军事训练的机构或者军事教员组织实施。  
学校组织军事训练活动，应当采取措施，加强安全保障。  
驻地军事机关应当协助学校组织学生军事训练。

**Article 17** Regular institutions of higher education and senior secondary schools shall organize military training for students in accordance with regulations.  
The military training of students in regular institutions of higher education and senior secondary schools shall be organized and implemented by the institution responsible for military training or military instructors in the schools.  
Schools organizing military training activities shall take measures to strengthen safety guarantees.  
The local military organs shall assist schools in organizing students' military training.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　中央全民国防教育主管部门、国务院教育行政部门、中央军事委员会机关有关部门负责全国学生军事训练工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门和驻地军事机关应当加强对学生军事训练工作的组织、指导和监督。

**Article 18** The central competent department for national defense education for all citizens, the education administrative department of the State Council, and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for the nationwide military training of students.  
Education administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level and local military organs shall strengthen the organization, guidance, and supervision of students' military training work.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　普通高等学校、高中阶段学校应当按照学生军事训练大纲，加强军事技能训练，磨练学生意志品质，增强组织纪律性，提高军事训练水平。  
学生军事训练大纲由国务院教育行政部门、中央军事委员会机关有关部门共同制定。

**Article 19** Regular institutions of higher education and senior secondary schools shall, in accordance with the students' military training syllabus, strengthen military skills training, temper students' will and character, enhance organizational discipline, and improve the level of military training.  
The students' military training syllabus shall be jointly formulated by the education administrative department of the State Council and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission.

### 第三章　社会国防教育

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家机关应当根据各自的工作性质和特点，采取多种形式对工作人员进行国防教育。  
国家机关工作人员应当具备较高的国防素养，发挥在全民国防教育中的模范带头作用。从事国防建设事业的国家机关工作人员，应当学习和掌握履行职责所必需的国防理论、知识和技能等。  
各地区、各部门的领导人员应当依法履行组织、领导本地区、本部门开展国防教育的职责。

**Article 20** State organs shall, based on the nature and characteristics of their respective work, adopt various forms to conduct national defense education for their staff members.  
Staff members of state organs shall possess a relatively high level of national defense literacy and play an exemplary and leading role in national defense education for all citizens. State organ staff members engaged in national defense construction shall study and master the national defense theories, knowledge, and skills necessary for performing their duties.  
Leading personnel of various regions and departments shall, according to law, perform their duties of organizing and leading the conduct of national defense education in their respective regions and departments.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　负责培训国家工作人员的各类教育机构，应当将国防教育纳入培训计划，设置适当的国防教育课程。  
国家根据需要选送地方和部门的负责人到有关军事院校接受培训，学习和掌握履行领导职责所必需的国防理论、知识和技能等。

**Article 21** All types of educational institutions responsible for training state functionaries shall include national defense education in their training plans and set up appropriate national defense education courses.  
The State, as needed, shall select and send heads of localities and departments to relevant military academies for training to study and master the national defense theories, knowledge, and skills necessary for performing leadership duties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业事业组织应当将国防教育列入职工教育计划，结合政治教育、业务培训、文化体育等活动，对职工进行国防教育。  
承担国防科研生产、国防设施建设、国防交通保障等任务的企业事业组织，应当根据所担负的任务，制定相应的国防教育计划，有针对性地对职工进行国防教育。  
社会组织应当根据各自的活动特点开展国防教育。

**Article 22** Enterprises and public institutions shall include national defense education in their employee education plans, and conduct national defense education for employees by integrating it with political education, professional training, cultural and sports activities, and other activities.  
Enterprises and public institutions undertaking tasks such as national defense scientific research and production, national defense facility construction, and national defense transportation support shall formulate corresponding national defense education plans based on the tasks they undertake, and conduct targeted national defense education for employees.  
Social organizations shall carry out national defense education in light of the characteristics of their respective activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　省军区（卫戍区、警备区）、军分区（警备区）和县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民武装部按照国家和军队的有关规定，结合政治教育和组织整顿、军事训练、执行勤务、征兵工作以及重大节日、纪念日活动，对民兵进行国防教育。  
民兵国防教育，应当以基干民兵和担任领导职务的民兵为重点，建立和完善制度，保证受教育的人员、教育时间和教育内容的落实。  
预备役人员所在单位应当按照有关规定开展预备役人员教育训练。

**Article 23** The provincial military commands (garrison commands, garrison commands), sub-military commands (garrison commands), and the people's armed forces departments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall conduct national defense education for the militia in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military, in combination with political education, organizational consolidation, military training, performance of duties, conscription work, and activities on major festivals and commemorative days.  
Militia national defense education shall focus on key militiamen and those serving in leadership positions, and systems shall be established and improved to ensure the implementation of the personnel receiving education, the duration of education, and the content of education.  
The units where reservists serve shall carry out education and training for reservists in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　居民委员会、村民委员会应当将国防教育纳入社会主义精神文明建设的内容，结合征兵工作、拥军优属以及重大节日、纪念日活动，对居民、村民进行国防教育。  
居民委员会、村民委员会可以聘请退役军人协助开展国防教育。

**Article 24** Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall incorporate national defense education into the content of building socialist spiritual civilization, and conduct national defense education for residents and villagers in combination with conscription work, supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to the families of servicemen, and activities on major festivals and commemorative days.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees may engage veterans to assist in carrying out national defense education.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　文化和旅游、新闻出版、广播电视、电影、网信等部门和单位应当根据形势和任务的要求，创新宣传报道方式，通过发挥红色资源教育功能、推出优秀文艺作品、宣传发布先进典型、运用新平台新技术新产品等形式和途径开展国防教育。  
中央和省、自治区、直辖市以及设区的市的广播电台、电视台、报刊、新闻网站等媒体应当开设国防教育节目或者栏目，普及国防知识。

**Article 25** Departments and units such as culture and tourism, press and publication, radio, television, film, and cyberspace administration shall, in accordance with the requirements of the situation and tasks, innovate methods of publicity and reporting, and carry out national defense education through forms and channels such as leveraging the educational function of red resources, launching excellent literary and artistic works, publicizing and releasing advanced models, and utilizing new platforms, new technologies, and new products.  
Media such as radio stations, television stations, newspapers, and news websites of the central level, provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities divided into districts shall offer national defense education programs or columns to popularize national defense knowledge.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各地区、各部门应当利用重大节日、纪念日和重大主题活动等，广泛开展群众性国防教育活动；在全民国防教育日集中开展主题鲜明、形式多样的国防教育活动。

**Article 26** All regions and departments shall make use of major festivals, commemorative days, and major themed activities to extensively carry out mass national defense education activities; on the National Defense Education Day for All Citizens, they shall intensively carry out national defense education activities with distinct themes and diverse forms.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　英雄烈士纪念设施、革命旧址和其他具有国防教育功能的博物馆、纪念馆、科技馆、文化馆、青少年宫等场所，应当为公民接受国防教育提供便利，对有组织的国防教育活动实行免费或者优惠。  
国防教育基地应当对军队人员、退役军人和学生免费开放，在全民国防教育日向社会免费开放。

**Article 27** Memorial facilities for heroes and martyrs, revolutionary sites, and other venues with national defense education functions such as museums, memorial halls, science and technology museums, cultural centers, and youth palaces shall provide convenience for citizens to receive national defense education, and shall offer free or preferential access for organized national defense education activities.  
National defense education bases shall be open free of charge to military personnel, veterans, and students, and shall be open free of charge to the public on the National Defense Education Day for All Citizens.

### 第四章　国防教育保障

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府应当将国防教育纳入国民经济和社会发展规划以及年度计划，将国防教育经费纳入预算。  
国家机关、事业组织、群团组织开展国防教育所需经费，在本单位预算经费内列支。  
企业开展国防教育所需经费，在本单位职工教育经费中列支。  
学校组织学生军事训练所需经费，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 28** People's governments at or above the county level shall include national defense education in the national economic and social development plan and annual plans, and shall include national defense education funds in the budget.  
The funds required for state organs, public institutions, and mass organizations to carry out national defense education shall be covered within their respective unit budget.  
The funds required for enterprises to carry out national defense education shall be covered within their employee education funds.  
The funds required for schools to organize students' military training shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会组织和个人捐赠财产，资助国防教育的开展。  
企业事业组织、社会组织和个人资助国防教育的财产，由国防教育领域相关组织依法管理。  
国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会组织和个人提供或者捐赠所收藏的具有国防教育意义的实物用于国防教育。使用单位对提供使用的实物应当妥善保管，使用完毕，及时归还。

**Article 29** The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to donate property to support the conduct of national defense education.  
Property donated by enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to support national defense education shall be managed by relevant organizations in the field of national defense education according to law.  
The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to provide or donate physical objects with national defense education significance from their collections for use in national defense education. The using unit shall properly keep the objects provided for use and return them promptly after use.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国防教育经费和企业事业组织、社会组织、个人资助国防教育的财产，必须用于国防教育事业，任何单位或者个人不得侵占、挪用、克扣。

**Article 30** The funds for national defense education and the property donated by enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to support national defense education must be used for national defense education, and no unit or individual may embezzle, misappropriate, or withhold such funds or property.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　具备下列条件的场所，可以由设区的市级以上全民国防教育主管部门会同同级军事机关命名为国防教育基地：  
（一）有明确的国防教育主题内容；  
（二）有健全的管理机构和规章制度；  
（三）有相应的国防教育设施；  
（四）有必要的经费保障；  
（五）有显著的社会教育效果。  
国防教育基地应当加强建设，不断完善，充分发挥国防教育功能。  
各级全民国防教育主管部门会同有关部门加强对国防教育基地的规划、建设和管理，并为其发挥作用提供必要的保障。  
被命名的国防教育基地不再具备本条第一款规定条件的，由命名机关撤销命名。

**Article 31** Venues that meet the following conditions may be designated as national defense education bases by the competent department for national defense education for all citizens at or above the level of a city divided into districts, in conjunction with the military organs at the same level:  
(1) Having a clear theme content of national defense education;  
(2) Having a sound management institution and rules and regulations;  
(3) Having corresponding national defense education facilities;  
(4) Having necessary financial guarantees;  
(5) Having significant social education effects.  
National defense education bases shall strengthen construction, continuously improve, and fully exert their national defense education functions.  
The competent departments for national defense education for all citizens at all levels, in conjunction with relevant departments, shall strengthen planning, construction, and management of national defense education bases and provide necessary guarantees for them to play their roles.  
If a designated national defense education base no longer meets the conditions specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the designating authority shall revoke the designation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级人民政府应当加强对具有国防教育意义的文物的调查、登记和保护工作。

**Article 32** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the survey, registration, and protection of cultural relics with national defense education significance.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　全民国防教育使用统一的国防教育大纲。国防教育大纲由中央全民国防教育主管部门组织制定。  
适用于不同类别、不同地区教育对象的国防教育教材，应当依据国防教育大纲由有关部门或者地方结合本部门、本地区的特点组织编写、审核。

**Article 33** A unified national defense education syllabus shall be used for national defense education for all citizens. The national defense education syllabus shall be formulated by the central competent department for national defense education for all citizens.  
National defense education textbooks applicable to different categories and regions of education targets shall be compiled and reviewed by relevant departments or localities in accordance with the national defense education syllabus, taking into account the characteristics of their respective departments or regions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级全民国防教育主管部门应当组织、协调有关部门做好国防教育教员的选拔、培训和管理工作，加强国防教育师资队伍建设。  
国防教育教员应当从热爱国防教育事业、具有扎实的国防理论、知识和必要的军事技能的人员中选拔，同等条件下优先招录、招聘退役军人。

**Article 34** The competent departments for national defense education for all citizens at all levels shall organize and coordinate relevant departments to do a good job in the selection, training, and management of national defense education instructors, and strengthen the construction of the national defense education teaching staff.  
National defense education instructors shall be selected from among persons who love the cause of national defense education, possess solid national defense theories, knowledge, and necessary military skills, and under equal conditions, veterans shall be given priority in recruitment and hiring.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队应当根据需要，按照有关规定为有组织的国防教育活动选派军事教员，提供必要的军事训练场地、设施、器材和其他便利条件。  
经批准的军营应当按照军队有关规定向社会开放。

**Article 35** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, as needed and in accordance with relevant provisions, assign military instructors for organized national defense education activities, and provide necessary military training venues, facilities, equipment, and other conveniences.  
Approved military camps shall be open to the public in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家机关、人民团体、企业事业组织以及社会组织和其他组织违反本法规定，拒不开展国防教育活动的，由有关部门或者上级机关给予批评教育，并责令限期改正；拒不改正，造成恶劣影响的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 36** Where state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations violate the provisions of this Law and refuse to carry out national defense education activities, the relevant departments or the higher authorities shall give them criticism and education, and order them to make corrections within a specified time limit; if they refuse to make corrections and cause adverse effects, the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本法规定，侵占、挪用、克扣国防教育经费或者企业事业组织、社会组织、个人资助的国防教育财产的，由有关主管部门责令限期归还；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。不适用处分的人员，由有关主管部门依法予以处理。

**Article 37** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, embezzles, misappropriates, or withholds funds for national defense education or property donated by enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, or individuals in support of national defense education, shall be ordered by the relevant competent departments to return the funds or property within a time limit; the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law. For persons to whom sanctions are not applicable, the relevant competent departments shall deal with them according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　侵占、破坏国防教育基地设施，损毁展品、器材的，由有关主管部门给予批评教育，并责令限期改正；有关责任人应当依法承担相应的民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 38** Whoever encroaches upon or damages the facilities of national defense education bases, or damages exhibits or equipment, shall be given criticism and education by the relevant competent departments and ordered to make corrections within a time limit; the responsible persons shall bear corresponding civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　寻衅滋事，扰乱国防教育工作和活动秩序的，或者盗用国防教育名义骗取钱财的，由有关主管部门给予批评教育，并予以制止；造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，应当依法承担相应的民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 39** Whoever engages in acts of picking quarrels and provoking trouble, disrupts the order of national defense education work and activities, or fraudulently uses the name of national defense education to obtain money or property, shall be given criticism and education by the relevant competent departments and shall be stopped; if personal injury or property damage or other losses are caused, the person shall bear corresponding civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　负责国防教育的公职人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 40** Where public officials responsible for national defense education abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本法自2024年9月21日起施行。

**Article 42** This Law shall come into force on September 21, 2024.
