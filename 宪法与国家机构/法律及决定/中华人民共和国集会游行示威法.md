# 中华人民共和国集会游行示威法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1989年10月31日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　集会游行示威的申请和许可 第三章　集会游行示威的举行 第四章　法律责任 第五章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障公民依法行使集会、游行、示威的权利，维护社会安定和公共秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内举行集会、游行、示威，均适用本法。 本法所称集会，是指聚集于露天公共场所，发表意见、表达意愿的活动。 本法所称游行，是指在公共道路、露天公共场所列队行进、表达共同意愿的活动。 本法所称示威，是指在露天公共场所或者公共道路上以集会、游行、静坐等方式，表达要求、抗议或者支持、声援等共同意愿的活动。 文娱、体育活动，正常的宗教活动，传统的民间习俗活动，不适用本法。 第三条　公民行使集会、游行、示威的权利，各级人民政府应当依照本法规定，予以保障。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公民依法行使集会、游行、示威的权利，维护社会安定和公共秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to safeguard citizens' exercise of the rights of assembly, procession and demonstration according to law, and to maintain social stability and public order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内举行集会、游行、示威，均适用本法。  
本法所称集会，是指聚集于露天公共场所，发表意见、表达意愿的活动。  
本法所称游行，是指在公共道路、露天公共场所列队行进、表达共同意愿的活动。  
本法所称示威，是指在露天公共场所或者公共道路上以集会、游行、静坐等方式，表达要求、抗议或者支持、声援等共同意愿的活动。  
文娱、体育活动，正常的宗教活动，传统的民间习俗活动，不适用本法。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-3" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to assemblies, processions and demonstrations held within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purpose of this Law, “assembly” means an activity in which people gather in a public place in the open air to express opinions or wishes.  
For the purpose of this Law, “procession” means an activity in which people march in procession on a public road or in a public place in the open air to express their common wishes.  
For the purpose of this Law, “demonstration” means an activity in which people express their common wishes, such as demands, protests or support and solidarity, in a public place in the open air or on a public road, by means of assemblies, processions, sit-ins, etc.  
This Law shall not apply to recreational or sports activities, normal religious activities, or traditional folk activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公民行使集会、游行、示威的权利，各级人民政府应当依照本法规定，予以保障。

**Article 3** When citizens exercise their rights of assembly, procession and demonstration, the people's governments at all levels shall guarantee such exercise in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公民在行使集会、游行、示威的权利的时候，必须遵守宪法和法律，不得反对宪法所确定的基本原则，不得损害国家的、社会的、集体的利益和其他公民的合法的自由和权利。

**Article 4** When exercising their rights of assembly, procession and demonstration, citizens must abide by the Constitution and laws, shall not oppose the basic principles prescribed in the Constitution, and shall not impair the interests of the state, society or the collective or the lawful freedoms and rights of other citizens.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　集会、游行、示威应当和平地进行，不得携带武器、管制刀具和爆炸物，不得使用暴力或者煽动使用暴力。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-5" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 5** Assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall be conducted peacefully, and no weapons, controlled cutting tools or explosives shall be carried, and no violence or incitement to violence shall be used.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　集会、游行、示威的主管机关，是集会、游行、示威举行地的市、县公安局、城市公安分局；游行、示威路线经过两个以上区、县的，主管机关为所经过区、县的公安机关的共同上一级公安机关。

**Article 6** The competent authorities for assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall be the public security organs of the cities, counties or municipal public security sub-bureaus where the assemblies, processions and demonstrations are held; if the route of a procession or demonstration passes through two or more districts or counties, the competent authority shall be the public security organ at the next higher level common to the public security organs of the districts or counties traversed.

### 第二章　集会游行示威的申请和许可

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　举行集会、游行、示威，必须依照本法规定向主管机关提出申请并获得许可。  
下列活动不需申请：  
（一）国家举行或者根据国家决定举行的庆祝、纪念等活动；  
（二）国家机关、政党、社会团体、企业事业组织依照法律、组织章程举行的集会。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-6" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 7** To hold an assembly, procession or demonstration, an application must be submitted to the competent authority and permission obtained in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The following activities shall not require an application:  
(1) Celebrations or commemorations held by the state or upon the decision of the state;  
(2) Assemblies held by state organs, political parties, social organizations, enterprises or institutions in accordance with laws or their respective articles of association.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　举行集会、游行、示威，必须有负责人。  
依照本法规定需要申请的集会、游行、示威，其负责人必须在举行日期的五日前向主管机关递交书面申请。申请书中应当载明集会、游行、示威的目的、方式、标语、口号、人数、车辆数、使用音响设备的种类与数量、起止时间、地点（包括集合地和解散地）、路线和负责人的姓名、职业、住址。

**Article 8** There must be a person in charge for holding an assembly, procession or demonstration.  
For assemblies, processions and demonstrations requiring an application under this Law, the person in charge must submit a written application to the competent authority five days before the date of the event. The application shall state the purpose, manner, slogans, chants, number of participants, number of vehicles, types and quantities of sound equipment to be used, start and end time, location (including assembly place and dispersal place), route, and the name, occupation and address of the person in charge.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　主管机关接到集会、游行、示威申请书后，应当在申请举行日期的二日前，将许可或者不许可的决定书面通知其负责人。不许可的，应当说明理由。逾期不通知的，视为许可。  
确因突然发生的事件临时要求举行集会、游行、示威的，必须立即报告主管机关；主管机关接到报告后，应当立即审查决定许可或者不许可。

**Article 9** After receiving an application for an assembly, procession or demonstration, the competent authority shall notify the person in charge in writing of its decision to permit or not to permit the event at least two days before the date of the proposed event. If permission is denied, the reasons shall be stated. Where no notification is given within the time limit, permission shall be deemed to have been granted.  
Where, due to an unexpected incident, an assembly, procession or demonstration is required to be held on an urgent basis, the matter must be immediately reported to the competent authority; upon receiving the report, the competent authority shall immediately examine the matter and decide whether to permit or not to permit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请举行集会、游行、示威要求解决具体问题的，主管机关接到申请书后，可以通知有关机关或者单位同集会、游行、示威的负责人协商解决问题，并可以将申请举行的时间推迟五日。

**Article 10** Where an application for an assembly, procession or demonstration is made for the purpose of resolving specific issues, the competent authority, after receiving the application, may notify the relevant organ or unit to negotiate with the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration for the resolution of the issues, and may postpone the proposed date of the event by five days.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　主管机关认为按照申请的时间、地点、路线举行集会、游行、示威，将对交通秩序和社会秩序造成严重影响的，在决定许可时或者决定许可后，可以变更举行集会、游行、示威的时间、地点、路线，并及时通知其负责人。

**Article 11** Where the competent authority believes that holding an assembly, procession or demonstration at the time, place and along the route as applied for will seriously affect traffic order and social order, it may, at the time of deciding to permit or after deciding to permit, change the time, place or route of the assembly, procession or demonstration, and shall promptly notify the person in charge thereof.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请举行的集会、游行、示威，有下列情形之一的，不予许可：  
（一）反对宪法所确定的基本原则的；  
（二）危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
（三）煽动民族分裂的；  
（四）有充分根据认定申请举行的集会、游行、示威将直接危害公共安全或者严重破坏社会秩序的。

**Article 12** An application for an assembly, procession or demonstration shall not be permitted under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It opposes the basic principles prescribed in the Constitution;  
(2) It endangers national unification, sovereignty and territorial integrity;  
(3) It incites national division;  
(4) There is sufficient evidence that the proposed assembly, procession or demonstration will directly endanger public security or seriously disrupt public order.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　集会、游行、示威的负责人对主管机关不许可的决定不服的，可以自接到决定通知之日起三日内，向同级人民政府申请复议，人民政府应当自接到申请复议书之日起三日内作出决定。

**Article 13** Where the person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration disagrees with the competent authority's decision not to permit the event, he or she may, within three days from receipt of the notice of the decision, apply for reconsideration to the people's government at the same level, and the people's government shall make a decision within three days from receipt of the application for reconsideration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　集会、游行、示威的负责人在提出申请后接到主管机关通知前，可以撤回申请；接到主管机关许可的通知后，决定不举行集会、游行、示威的，应当及时告知主管机关，参加人已经集合的，应当负责解散。

**Article 14** The person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration may withdraw the application after it is submitted but before receiving notification from the competent authority; after receiving the notice of permission, if a decision is made not to hold the assembly, procession or demonstration, the person in charge shall promptly inform the competent authority, and if participants have already assembled, the person in charge shall be responsible for their dispersal.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公民不得在其居住地以外的城市发动、组织、参加当地公民的集会、游行、示威。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 15** No citizen shall launch, organize or participate in an assembly, procession or demonstration of local citizens in a city other than the city of his or her residence.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家机关工作人员不得组织或者参加违背有关法律、法规规定的国家机关工作人员职责、义务的集会、游行、示威。

**Article 16** No functionary of a state organ shall organize or participate in any assembly, procession or demonstration that violates the duties and obligations of functionaries of state organs prescribed by relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　以国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织的名义组织或者参加集会、游行、示威，必须经本单位负责人批准。

**Article 17** The organization or participation of an assembly, procession or demonstration in the name of a state organ, social organization, enterprise or institution must be approved by the person in charge of that unit.

### 第三章　集会游行示威的举行

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对于依法举行的集会、游行、示威，主管机关应当派出人民警察维持交通秩序和社会秩序，保障集会、游行、示威的顺利进行。

**Article 18** For assemblies, processions and demonstrations conducted according to law, the competent authority shall dispatch people's police to maintain traffic order and public order and ensure the smooth progress of the assembly, procession or demonstration.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　依法举行的集会、游行、示威，任何人不得以暴力、胁迫或者其他非法手段进行扰乱、冲击和破坏。

**Article 19** No person may disrupt, break into or sabotage an assembly, procession or demonstration conducted according to law by means of violence, coercion or other illegal means.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　为了保障依法举行的游行的行进，负责维持交通秩序的人民警察可以临时变通执行交通规则的有关规定。

**Article 20** To ensure the progress of a procession conducted according to law, the people's police responsible for maintaining traffic order may temporarily make ad hoc adjustments in implementing the relevant provisions of traffic rules.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　游行在行进中遇有不可预料的情况，不能按照许可的路线行进时，人民警察现场负责人有权改变游行队伍的行进路线。

**Article 21** When a procession encounters unforeseen circumstances during its march and cannot proceed along the permitted route, the on-site person in charge of the people's police may change the marching route of the procession.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　集会、游行、示威在国家机关、军事机关、广播电台、电视台、外国驻华使馆领馆等单位所在地举行或者经过的，主管机关为了维持秩序，可以在附近设置临时警戒线，未经人民警察许可，不得逾越。

**Article 22** Where assemblies, processions, and demonstrations are held at or pass through the locations of state organs, military organs, radio stations, television stations, foreign embassies or consulates in China, or other units, the competent authority may, for maintaining order, set up temporary warning lines nearby; without the permission of the people's police, no one shall cross such lines.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在下列场所周边距离十米至三百米内，不得举行集会、游行、示威，经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府批准的除外：  
（一）全国人民代表大会常务委员会、国务院、中央军事委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院的所在地；  
（二）国宾下榻处；  
（三）重要军事设施；  
（四）航空港、火车站和港口。  
前款所列场所的具体周边距离，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Within a perimeter of ten to three hundred meters of the following places, no assemblies, processions, or demonstrations shall be held, except with the approval of the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) The locations of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(2) The state guesthouse;  
(3) Important military installations;  
(4) Airports, railway stations, and ports.  
The specific perimeter distances for the places listed in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　举行集会、游行、示威的时间限于早六时至晚十时，经当地人民政府决定或者批准的除外。

**Article 24** The time for holding assemblies, processions, or demonstrations shall be limited to between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m., except where otherwise decided or approved by the local people's government.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　集会、游行、示威应当按照许可的目的、方式、标语、口号、起止时间、地点、路线及其他事项进行。  
集会、游行、示威的负责人必须负责维持集会、游行、示威的秩序，并严格防止其他人加入。  
集会、游行、示威的负责人在必要时，应当指定专人协助人民警察维持秩序。负责维持秩序的人员应当佩戴标志。

**Article 25** Assemblies, processions, and demonstrations shall be conducted in accordance with the approved purpose, manner, slogans, placards, commencement and termination times, location, route, and other matters.  
The person in charge of an assembly, procession, or demonstration must bear responsibility for maintaining order and strictly prevent others from joining.  
When necessary, the person in charge of an assembly, procession, or demonstration shall designate specific persons to assist the people's police in maintaining order. Persons responsible for maintaining order shall wear identification badges.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　举行集会、游行、示威，不得违反治安管理法规，不得进行犯罪活动或者煽动犯罪。

**Article 26** When holding assemblies, processions, or demonstrations, no one shall violate public security administration regulations, engage in criminal activities, or incite crimes.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　举行集会、游行、示威，有下列情形之一的，人民警察应当予以制止：  
（一）未依照本法规定申请或者申请未获许可的；  
（二）未按照主管机关许可的目的、方式、标语、口号、起止时间、地点、路线进行的；  
（三）在进行中出现危害公共安全或者严重破坏社会秩序情况的。  
有前款所列情形之一，不听制止的，人民警察现场负责人有权命令解散；拒不解散的，人民警察现场负责人有权依照国家有关规定决定采取必要手段强行驱散，并对拒不服从的人员强行带离现场或者立即予以拘留。  
参加集会、游行、示威的人员越过依照[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定设置的临时警戒线、进入[本法第二十三条](#art-23)所列不得举行集会、游行、示威的特定场所周边一定范围或者有其他违法犯罪行为的，人民警察可以将其强行带离现场或者立即予以拘留。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第28条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Where an assembly, procession, or demonstration is held under any of the following circumstances, the people's police shall stop it:  
(1) An application has not been made in accordance with this Law or the application has not been approved;  
(2) It is not conducted in accordance with the purpose, manner, slogans, placards, commencement and termination times, location, and route approved by the competent authority;  
(3) Circumstances endangering public security or seriously disrupting social order occur during its conduct.  
Where any of the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph exists and persuasion fails, the on-site person in charge of the people's police may order dissolution; if the participants refuse to disperse, the on-site person in charge of the people's police may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, decide to take necessary measures to forcefully disperse them, and may forcibly remove from the scene those who refuse to obey, or detain them immediately.  
If participants in an assembly, procession, or demonstration cross the temporary warning lines set up in accordance with Article 22 of this Law, enter the periphery of the specific places listed in Article 23 where assemblies, processions, or demonstrations are prohibited, or commit other illegal or criminal acts, the people's police may forcibly remove them from the scene or detain them immediately.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　举行集会、游行、示威，有违反治安管理行为的，依照治安管理处罚法有关规定予以处罚。  
举行集会、游行、示威，有下列情形之一的，公安机关可以对其负责人和直接责任人员处以警告或者十五日以下拘留：  
（一）未依照本法规定申请或者申请未获许可的；  
（二）未按照主管机关许可的目的、方式、标语、口号、起止时间、地点、路线进行，不听制止的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** Where an assembly, procession, or demonstration involves conduct violating public security administration, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.  
Where an assembly, procession, or demonstration is held under any of the following circumstances, the public security organ may impose a warning or detention for not more than fifteen days on the person in charge and the persons directly responsible:  
(1) An application has not been made in accordance with this Law or the application has not been approved;  
(2) It is not conducted in accordance with the purpose, manner, slogans, placards, commencement and termination times, location, and route approved by the competent authority, and persuasion fails.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　举行集会、游行、示威，有犯罪行为的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
携带武器、管制刀具或者爆炸物的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
未依照本法规定申请或者申请未获许可，或者未按照主管机关许可的起止时间、地点、路线进行，又拒不服从解散命令，严重破坏社会秩序的，对集会、游行、示威的负责人和直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
包围、冲击国家机关，致使国家机关的公务活动或者国事活动不能正常进行的，对集会、游行、示威的负责人和直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
占领公共场所、拦截车辆行人或者聚众堵塞交通，严重破坏公共场所秩序、交通秩序的，对集会、游行、示威的负责人和直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-25" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第25条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** Where an assembly, procession, or demonstration involves criminal conduct, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where weapons, controlled knives, or explosives are carried, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where an application has not been made in accordance with this Law or the application has not been approved, or the assembly, procession, or demonstration is not conducted in accordance with the commencement and termination times, location, and route approved by the competent authority, and the participants refuse to obey an order to dissolve, thereby seriously disrupting social order, criminal liability shall be investigated against the person in charge and the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where state organs are surrounded or stormed, causing their official or state activities to be unable to proceed normally, criminal liability shall be investigated against the person in charge and the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where public places are occupied, vehicles or pedestrians are blocked, or crowds are gathered to block traffic, thereby seriously disrupting public order or traffic order, criminal liability shall be investigated against the person in charge and the persons directly responsible in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　扰乱、冲击或者以其他方法破坏依法举行的集会、游行、示威的，公安机关可以处以警告或者十五日以下拘留；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Where anyone disturbs, storms, or by other means disrupts an assembly, procession, or demonstration held according to law, the public security organ may impose a warning or detention for not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对公安机关依照[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第二款或者第三十条的规定给予的拘留处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起五日内，向上一级公安机关提出申诉，上一级公安机关应当自接到申诉之日起五日内作出裁决；对上一级公安机关裁决不服的，可以自接到裁决通知之日起五日内，向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 31** Where a party is dissatisfied with a decision on detention penalty imposed by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 28 or Article 30 of this Law, the party may, within five days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, file a petition with the public security organ at the next higher level, and the public security organ at the next higher level shall, within five days from the date of receipt of the petition, render a decision; if the party is dissatisfied with the decision of the public security organ at the next higher level, the party may, within five days from the date of receipt of the notice of the decision, initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在举行集会、游行、示威过程中，破坏公私财物或者侵害他人身体造成伤亡的，除依照刑法或者治安管理处罚法的有关规定可以予以处罚外，还应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 32** Where, during the holding of an assembly, procession, or demonstration, public or private property is damaged or personal injury or death is caused, in addition to being punishable in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law or the Public Security Administration Punishments Law, the responsible person shall also bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　公民在本人居住地以外的城市发动、组织当地公民的集会、游行、示威的，公安机关有权予以拘留或者强行遣回原地。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** Where a citizen initiates or organizes an assembly, procession, or demonstration of local citizens in a city other than the citizen's own place of residence, the public security organ may detain the citizen or forcibly send the citizen back to the original place.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外国人在中国境内举行集会、游行、示威，适用本法规定。  
外国人在中国境内未经主管机关批准不得参加中国公民举行的集会、游行、示威。

**Article 34** Where a foreigner holds an assembly, procession, or demonstration within the territory of China, the provisions of this Law shall apply.  
No foreigner shall, without the approval of the competent authority, participate in an assembly, procession, or demonstration held by Chinese citizens within the territory of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院公安部门可以根据本法制定实施条例，报国务院批准施行。  
省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法制定实施办法。

**Article 35** The public security department under the State Council may, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementation regulations, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before coming into force.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementation measures.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 36** This Law shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
