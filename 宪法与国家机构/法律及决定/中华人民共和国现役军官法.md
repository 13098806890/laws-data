# 中华人民共和国现役军官法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2000-12-28  
**生效日期**：2000-12-28

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建设一支革命化、年轻化、知识化、专业化的现役军官队伍，以利于人民解放军完成国家赋予的任务，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of building a contingent of officers in active service who are revolutionary, younger, better educated and more professional, so as to facilitate the People's Liberation Army in accomplishing the tasks assigned by the State.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民解放军现役军官（以下简称军官）是被任命为排级以上职务或者初级以上专业技术职务，并被授予相应军衔的现役军人。  
军官按照职务性质分为军事军官、政治军官、后勤军官、装备军官和专业技术军官。

**Article 2** Officers in active service of the People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as officers) refer to servicemen in active service who are appointed to posts at or above the platoon level or primary professional technical posts, and are conferred corresponding military ranks.  
Officers are classified, according to the nature of their posts, into military officers, political officers, logistics officers, equipment officers and professional technical officers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军官是国家工作人员的组成部分。  
军官履行宪法和法律赋予的神圣职责，在社会生活中享有与其职责相应的地位和荣誉。  
国家依法保障军官的合法权益。

**Article 3** Officers are a component part of State functionaries.  
Officers shall perform the sacred duties conferred by the Constitution and laws, and enjoy, in social life, the status and honor corresponding to their duties.  
The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of officers according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　军官的选拔和使用，坚持任人唯贤、德才兼备、注重实绩、适时交流的原则，实行民主监督，尊重群众公论。

**Article 4** The selection and utilization of officers shall adhere to the principles of appointing people on their merits, combining moral integrity with professional competence, emphasizing practical performance, and timely exchange, and shall practice democratic supervision and respect public opinion.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家按照优待现役军人的原则，确定军官的各种待遇。

**Article 5** The State shall determine the various treatments of officers in accordance with the principle of giving preferential treatment to servicemen in active service.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军官符合本法规定的退出现役条件的，应当退出现役。

**Article 6** An officer who meets the conditions for retirement from active service as prescribed by this Law shall retire from active service.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民解放军总政治部主管全军的军官管理工作，团级以上单位的政治机关主管本单位的军官管理工作。

**Article 7** The General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army shall be in charge of the management of officers throughout the armed forces, and the political organs of units at or above the regiment level shall be in charge of the management of officers in their respective units.

### 第二章　军官的基本条件、来源和培训

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军官必须具备下列基本条件：  
（一）忠于祖国，忠于中国共产党，有坚定的革命理想、信念，全心全意为人民服务，自觉献身国防事业；  
（二）遵守宪法和法律、法规，执行国家的方针、政策和军队的规章、制度，服从命令，听从指挥；  
（三）具有胜任本职工作所必需的理论、政策水平，现代军事、科学文化、专业知识，组织、指挥能力，经过院校培训并取得相应学历，身体健康；  
（四）爱护士兵，以身作则，公道正派，廉洁奉公，艰苦奋斗，不怕牺牲。

**Article 8** An officer shall possess the following basic qualifications:  
(1) Being loyal to the motherland, loyal to the Communist Party of China, having firm revolutionary ideals and convictions, serving the people wholeheartedly, and consciously dedicating to the cause of national defense;  
(2) Observing the Constitution, laws and regulations, implementing the State's policies and guidelines as well as the military's rules and systems, obeying orders, and following commands;  
(3) Possessing the theoretical and policy level, modern military, scientific, cultural and professional knowledge, and organizational and command capabilities necessary for the competent performance of duties, having received training in military academies and schools and obtained corresponding academic qualifications, and being in good health;  
(4) Caring for soldiers, setting an example by one's own conduct, being fair and upright, honest in performing official duties, working hard, and fearing no sacrifice.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军官的来源：  
（一）选拔优秀士兵和普通中学毕业生入军队院校学习毕业；  
（二）接收普通高等学校毕业生；  
（三）由文职干部改任；  
（四）招收军队以外的专业技术人员和其他人员。  
战时根据需要，可以从士兵、征召的预备役军官和非军事部门的人员中直接任命军官。

**Article 9** Sources of officers are:  
(1) Selecting outstanding soldiers and graduates from ordinary secondary schools to study in military academies and schools and graduate;  
(2) Recruiting graduates from institutions of higher learning;  
(3) Changing the posts of civilian cadres to officers;  
(4) Recruiting professional technical personnel and other personnel from outside the armed forces.  
In time of war, when necessary, officers may be directly appointed from among soldiers, mobilized reserve officers, and personnel from non-military departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民解放军实行经院校培训提拔军官的制度。  
军事、政治、后勤、装备军官每晋升一级指挥职务，应当经过相应的院校或者其他训练机构培训。担任营级以下指挥职务的军官，应当经过初级指挥院校培训；担任团级和师级指挥职务的军官，应当经过中级指挥院校培训；担任军级以上指挥职务的军官，应当经过高级指挥院校培训。  
在机关任职的军官应当经过相应的院校培训。  
专业技术军官每晋升一级专业技术职务，应当经过与其所从事专业相应的院校培训；院校培训不能满足需要时，应当通过其他方式，完成规定的继续教育任务。

**Article 10** The People's Liberation Army shall implement a system of promoting officers after training in military academies and schools.  
Military, political, logistics and equipment officers, for each promotion to the next higher command post, shall undergo training in corresponding military academies or other training institutions. Officers holding command posts at or below the battalion level shall undergo training in primary command academies; officers holding command posts at the regiment and division levels shall undergo training in intermediate command academies; officers holding command posts at the corps level and above shall undergo training in senior command academies.  
Officers serving in organs shall undergo training in corresponding military academies.  
Professional technical officers, for each promotion to the next higher professional technical post, shall undergo training in military academies corresponding to the specialty they are engaged in; where training in military academies cannot meet the needs, they shall complete the prescribed continuing education tasks through other means.

### 第三章　军官的考核和职务任免

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级首长和政治机关应当按照分工对所属军官进行考核。  
考核军官，应当实行领导和群众相结合，根据军官的基本条件和中央军事委员会规定的军官考核标准、程序、方法，以工作实绩为主，全面考核。考核结果分为优秀、称职、不称职三个等次，并作为任免军官职务的主要依据。考核结果应当告知本人。  
任免军官职务，应当先经考核；未经考核不得任免。

**Article 11** Leaders at all levels and political organs shall assess the officers under their respective charge according to the division of duties.  
The assessment of officers shall combine leadership with the masses, and shall be a comprehensive assessment based on the basic qualifications of officers and the criteria, procedures and methods for officer assessment prescribed by the Central Military Commission, with actual work performance as the main focus. The assessment results shall be classified into three grades: excellent, competent, and incompetent, and shall serve as the main basis for appointing and removing officers from their posts. The assessment results shall be notified to the officer concerned.  
Before appointing or removing an officer, assessment shall be conducted first; no appointment or removal shall be made without assessment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军官职务的任免权限：  
（一）总参谋长、总政治部主任至正师职军官职务，由中央军事委员会主席任免；  
（二）副师职（正旅职）、正团职（副旅职）军官职务和高级专业技术军官职务，由总参谋长、总政治部主任、总后勤部部长和政治委员、总装备部部长和政治委员、大军区及军兵种或者相当大军区级单位的正职首长任免，副大军区级单位的正团职（副旅职）军官职务由副大军区级单位的正职首长任免；  
（三）副团职、正营职军官职务和中级专业技术军官职务，由集团军或者其他有任免权的军级单位的正职首长任免，独立师的正营职军官职务由独立师的正职首长任免；  
（四）副营职以下军官职务和初级专业技术军官职务，由师（旅）或者其他有任免权的师（旅）级单位的正职首长任免。  
前款所列军官职务的任免，按照中央军事委员会规定的程序办理。

**Article 12** The authority for appointment and removal of officers' posts:  
(1) The posts of officers from the Chief of the General Staff and the Director of the General Political Department down to the post of regular division level shall be appointed and removed by the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) The posts of officers at the deputy division (regular brigade) level, regular regiment (deputy brigade) level and senior professional technical posts shall be appointed and removed by the Chief of the General Staff, the Director of the General Political Department, the Minister and Political Commissar of the General Logistics Department, the Minister and Political Commissar of the General Armament Department, the commanders of the major military regions, services and arms, or units equivalent to the major military region level; the regular regiment (deputy brigade) level officer posts of units at the deputy major military region level shall be appointed and removed by the commanders of such deputy major military region units;  
(3) The posts of officers at the deputy regiment level and regular battalion level and intermediate professional technical posts shall be appointed and removed by the commanders of group armies or other corps-level units with appointment and removal authority; the regular battalion level officer posts of independent divisions shall be appointed and removed by the commanders of such independent divisions;  
(4) The posts of officers at or below the deputy battalion level and primary professional technical posts shall be appointed and removed by the commanders of divisions (brigades) or other division (brigade)-level units with appointment and removal authority.  
The appointment and removal of the officer posts listed in the preceding paragraph shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在执行作战、抢险救灾等紧急任务时，上级首长有权暂时免去违抗命令、不履行职责或者不称职的所属军官的职务，并可以临时指派其他军人代理；因其他原因，军官职务出现空缺时，上级首长也可以临时指派军人代理。  
依照前款规定暂时免去或者临时指派军人代理军官职务，应当尽快报请有任免权的上级审核决定，履行任免手续。

**Article 13** When carrying out emergency missions such as combat operations or emergency rescue and disaster relief, a superior leader shall have the power to temporarily remove from his post any subordinate officer who disobeys orders, fails to perform his duties or is incompetent, and may temporarily assign another serviceman to act in his stead. Where a post falls vacant for other reasons, a superior leader may also temporarily assign a serviceman to act in the post.  
The temporary removal or temporary assignment of a serviceman to act in an officer's post in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be reported as soon as possible to the superior organ with appointment and removal authority for examination and decision, and the procedures for appointment and removal shall be fulfilled.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　作战部队的军事、政治、后勤、装备军官平时任职的最高年龄分别为：  
（一）担任排级职务的，三十岁；  
（二）担任连级职务的，三十五岁；  
（三）担任营级职务的，四十岁；  
（四）担任团级职务的，四十五岁；  
（五）担任师级职务的，五十岁；  
（六）担任军级职务的，五十五岁；  
（七）担任大军区级职务的，副职六十三岁，正职六十五岁。  
在舰艇上服役的营级和团级职务军官，任职的最高年龄分别为四十五岁和五十岁；从事飞行的团级职务军官，任职的最高年龄为五十岁。  
作战部队的师级和军级职务军官，少数工作需要的，按照任免权限经过批准，任职的最高年龄可以适当延长，但是师级和正军职军官延长的年龄最多不得超过五岁，副军职军官延长的年龄最多不得超过三岁。

**Article 14** The maximum ages for holding posts in peacetime of military, political, logistics and equipment officers in combat units are respectively:  
(1) For those holding platoon-level posts, thirty years old;  
(2) For those holding company-level posts, thirty-five years old;  
(3) For those holding battalion-level posts, forty years old;  
(4) For those holding regiment-level posts, forty-five years old;  
(5) For those holding division-level posts, fifty years old;  
(6) For those holding corps-level posts, fifty-five years old;  
(7) For those holding major military region-level posts, sixty-three for deputy posts and sixty-five for regular posts.  
For officers holding battalion-level and regiment-level posts serving on naval vessels, the maximum ages for holding posts are respectively forty-five and fifty years old; for officers holding regiment-level posts engaged in flying, the maximum age for holding posts is fifty years old.  
For officers holding division-level and corps-level posts in combat units, where a few are required by work, with approval according to the authority of appointment and removal, the maximum age for holding posts may be appropriately extended; however, for division-level and regular corps-level officers, the extension shall not exceed five years at most, and for deputy corps-level officers, the extension shall not exceed three years at most.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　作战部队以外单位的副团职以下军官和大军区级职务军官，任职的最高年龄依照[本法第十四条](#art-14)第一款的相应规定执行；正团职军官，任职的最高年龄为五十岁；师级职务军官，任职的最高年龄为五十五岁；副军职和正军职军官，任职的最高年龄分别为五十八岁和六十岁。

**Article 15** For officers at or below the deputy regiment level and officers holding major military region-level posts in units other than combat units, the maximum ages for holding posts shall be implemented in accordance with the corresponding provisions of Article 14, paragraph 1 of this Law; for officers holding regular regiment-level posts, the maximum age for holding posts is fifty years old; for officers holding division-level posts, the maximum age for holding posts is fifty-five years old; for officers holding deputy corps-level and regular corps-level posts, the maximum ages for holding posts are respectively fifty-eight and sixty years old.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专业技术军官平时任职的最高年龄分别为：  
（一）担任初级专业技术职务的，四十岁；  
（二）担任中级专业技术职务的，五十岁；  
（三）担任高级专业技术职务的，六十岁。  
担任高级专业技术职务的军官，少数工作需要的，按照任免权限经过批准，任职的最高年龄可以适当延长，但是延长的年龄最多不得超过五岁。

**Article 16** The maximum ages for holding posts in peacetime of professional technical officers are respectively:  
(1) For those holding primary professional technical posts, forty years old;  
(2) For those holding intermediate professional technical posts, fifty years old;  
(3) For those holding senior professional technical posts, sixty years old.  
For officers holding senior professional technical posts, where a few are required by work, with approval according to the authority of appointment and removal, the maximum age for holding posts may be appropriately extended; however, the extension shall not exceed five years at most.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　担任排、连、营、团、师（旅）、军级主官职务的军官，平时任职的最低年限分别为三年。

**Article 17** For officers holding the posts of principal at the platoon, company, battalion, regiment, division (brigade), and corps levels, the minimum number of years for holding posts in peacetime is respectively three years.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　机关和院校的股长、科长、处长、局长、部长及相当领导职务的军官，任职的最低年限参照[本法第十七条](#art-17)的规定执行。  
机关和院校的参谋、干事、秘书、助理员、教员等军官，每个职务等级任职的最低年限为三年。

**Article 18** For officers holding the posts of section chief, division chief, department chief, bureau chief, ministry chief and equivalent leading posts in organs and academies, the minimum number of years for holding posts shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of Article 17 of this Law.  
For officers such as staff officers, administrative officers, secretaries, assistants and instructors in organs and academies, the minimum number of years for holding each post level is three years.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　专业技术军官平时任职的最低年限，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 19** The minimum number of years for holding posts in peacetime of professional technical officers shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　军官任职满最低年限后，才能根据编制缺额和本人德才条件逐职晋升。  
军官德才优秀、实绩显著、工作需要的，可以提前晋升；特别优秀的，可以越职晋升。

**Article 20** Only after an officer has served the minimum number of years in his post may he be promoted step by step according to the authorized vacancies and his moral integrity and professional competence.  
An officer with outstanding moral integrity and professional competence, remarkable practical performance, and where required by work, may be promoted ahead of time; an officer who is exceptionally outstanding may be promoted skipping ranks.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军官晋升职务，应当具备拟任职务所要求的任职经历、文化程度、院校培训等资格。具体条件由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 21** An officer to be promoted to a post shall possess the qualifications required for the proposed post, such as experience in holding posts, educational level, and training in military academies. The specific conditions shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军官职务应当按照编制员额和编制职务等级任命。

**Article 22** Officers' posts shall be appointed in accordance with the authorized strength and authorized post levels.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　军官经考核不称职的，应当调任下级职务或者改做其他工作，并按照新任职务确定待遇。

**Article 23** Where an officer is assessed as incompetent, he shall be transferred to a lower post or assigned to other work, and his treatment shall be determined according to his new post.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　担任师、军、大军区级职务的军官，正职和副职平时任职的最高年限分别为十年。任职满最高年限的，应当免去现任职务。

**Article 24** For officers holding posts at the division, corps and major military region levels, the maximum number of years for holding posts in peacetime for both regular and deputy posts is respectively ten years. Those who have served the maximum number of years shall be removed from their current posts.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　根据国防建设的需要，军队可以向非军事部门派遣军官，执行军队交付的任务。

**Article 25** In accordance with the needs of national defense construction, the armed forces may dispatch officers to non-military departments to carry out tasks assigned by the armed forces.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　军官可以按照中央军事委员会的规定改任文职干部。

**Article 26** An officer may change his post to a civilian cadre in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　军官的交流和回避

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　军官应当在不同岗位或者不同单位之间进行交流，具体办法由中央军事委员会根据本法规定。

**Article 27** Officers shall be exchanged among different posts or different units. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　军官在一个岗位任职满下列年限的，应当交流：  
（一）作战部队担任师级以下主官职务的，四年；担任军级主官职务的，五年；  
（二）作战部队以外单位担任军级以下主官职务的，五年；  
（三）机关担任股长、科长、处长及相当领导职务的，四年；担任局长、部长及相当领导职务的，五年；但是少数专业性强和工作特别需要的除外。  
担任师级和军级领导职务的军官，在本单位连续工作分别满二十五年和三十年的，应当交流。  
担任其他职务的军官，也应当根据需要进行交流。

**Article 28** An officer who has served in one post for the following number of years shall be exchanged:  
(1) For those holding the posts of principal at or below the division level in combat units, four years; for those holding the posts of principal at the corps level, five years;  
(2) For those holding the posts of principal at or below the corps level in units other than combat units, five years;  
(3) For those holding the posts of section chief, division chief, department chief and equivalent leading posts in organs, four years; for those holding the posts of bureau chief, ministry chief and equivalent leading posts, five years; except for a few whose posts are highly specialized or are specially needed by work.  
Officers holding leading posts at the division and corps levels who have worked continuously in the same unit for respectively twenty-five and thirty years shall be exchanged.  
Officers holding other posts shall also be exchanged as required.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在艰苦地区工作的军官向其他地区交流，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 29** Exchange of officers working in arduous areas to other areas shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军官之间有夫妻关系、直系血亲关系、三代以内旁系血亲关系以及近姻亲关系的，不得担任有直接上下级或者间隔一级领导关系的职务，不得在同一单位担任双方直接隶属于同一首长的职务，也不得在担任领导职务一方的机关任职。

**Article 30** Officers who are related to each other by marriage, direct blood relationship, collateral blood relationship within three generations, or close affinity, shall not hold posts with a direct superior-subordinate relationship or a leadership relationship separated by one level, shall not hold posts in the same unit where both are directly subordinate to the same leader, and shall not serve in an organ where one of them holds a leading post.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　军官不得在其原籍所在地的军分区（师级警备区）和县、市、市辖区的人民武装部担任主官职务，但是工作特别需要的除外。

**Article 31** An officer shall not hold the post of principal in the military sub-area (division-level garrison area) where his place of origin is located, or in the people's armed forces department of a county, city or municipal district, except where specially needed by work.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　军官在执行职务时，涉及本人或者涉及与本人有[本法第三十条](#art-30)所列亲属关系人员的利益关系的，应当回避，但是执行作战任务和其他紧急任务的除外。

**Article 32** An officer, when performing his duties, shall withdraw if the matter involves an interest of himself or of a person who is related to him by the kinds of kinship listed in Article 30 of this Law, except where he is carrying out combat missions or other emergency tasks.

### 第五章　军官的奖励和处分

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　军官在作战和军队建设中做出突出贡献或者取得显著成绩，以及为国家和人民做出其他较大贡献的，按照中央军事委员会的规定给予奖励。  
奖励分为：嘉奖、三等功、二等功、一等功、荣誉称号以及中央军事委员会规定的其他奖励。

**Article 33** An officer who makes outstanding contributions or achieves remarkable results in combat or army building, or makes other significant contributions to the State and the people, shall be given rewards in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
Rewards are classified into: commendation, Merit Citation Class III, Merit Citation Class II, Merit Citation Class I, honorary title, and other rewards prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　军官违反军纪的，按照中央军事委员会的规定给予处分。  
处分分为：警告、严重警告、记过、记大过、降职（级）或者降衔、撤职、开除军籍以及中央军事委员会规定的其他处分。

**Article 34** An officer who violates military discipline shall be given sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
Sanctions are classified into: warning, serious warning, recording a demerit, recording a serious demerit, demotion in post (grade) or reduction in rank, removal from post, discharge from military service, and other sanctions prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对被撤职的军官，根据其所犯错误的具体情况，任命新的职务；未任命新的职务的，应当确定职务等级待遇。

**Article 35** For an officer who has been removed from his post, a new post shall be appointed according to the specific circumstances of his mistake; where no new post is appointed, the treatment at his post level shall be determined.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　军官违反法律，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** An officer who violates the law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　军官的待遇

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　军官实行职务军衔等级工资制和定期增资制度，按照国家和军队的有关规定享受津贴和补贴，并随着国民经济的发展适时调整。具体标准和办法由中央军事委员会规定。  
军官按照规定离职培训、休假、治病疗养以及免职待分配期间，工资照发。

**Article 37** Officers shall implement a wage system based on post and rank grade and a system of regular salary increases, and shall enjoy allowances and subsidies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces, with timely adjustments along with the development of the national economy. The specific standards and measures shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.  
During periods when officers are off-duty for training, leave, medical treatment or recuperation, or awaiting reassignment after removal from post, their wages shall be paid as usual.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　军官享受公费医疗待遇。有关部门应当做好军官的医疗保健工作，妥善安排军官的治病和疗养。  
军官按照国家和军队的有关规定享受军人保险待遇。

**Article 38** Officers shall enjoy free medical care. The relevant departments shall do a good job in the medical and health care of officers, and properly arrange for the treatment and recuperation of officers.  
Officers shall enjoy military insurance treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军官住房实行公寓住房与自有住房相结合的保障制度。军官按照规定住用公寓住房或者购买自有住房，享受相应的住房补贴和优惠待遇。

**Article 39** The housing of officers shall implement a security system combining apartment housing with self-owned housing. Officers shall reside in apartment housing or purchase self-owned housing according to regulations, and enjoy corresponding housing subsidies and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军官享受休假待遇。上级首长应当每年按照规定安排军官休假。  
执行作战任务部队的军官停止休假。  
国家发布动员令后，按照动员令应当返回部队的正在休假的军官，应当自动结束休假，立即返回本部。

**Article 40** Officers shall enjoy leave benefits. Superior leaders shall arrange leave for officers every year according to regulations.  
Officers in units carrying out combat missions shall suspend their leave.  
After the State issues a mobilization order, officers on leave who are required to return to their units according to the mobilization order shall automatically end their leave and immediately return to their units.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　军官的家属随军、就业、工作调动和子女教育，享受国家和社会优待。  
军官具备家属随军条件的，经师（旅）级以上单位的政治机关批准，其配偶和未成年子女、无独立生活能力的子女可以随军，是农村户口的，转为城镇户口。  
部队移防或者军官工作调动的，随军家属可以随调。  
军官年满五十岁、身边无子女的，可以调一名有工作的子女到军官所在地。所调子女已婚的，其配偶和未成年子女、无独立生活能力的子女可以随调。  
随军的军官家属、随调的军官子女及其配偶的就业和工作调动，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定办理。

**Article 41** The family members of officers accompanying the army, their employment, job transfers, and the education of their children shall enjoy preferential treatment from the State and society.  
Where an officer meets the conditions for his family members to accompany the army, upon approval by the political organ at or above the division (brigade) level, his spouse and minor children, and children without independent living ability may accompany the army; if they have rural household registration, they shall be changed to urban household registration.  
When a unit is relocated or an officer's post is transferred, the family members accompanying the army may be transferred accordingly.  
Where an officer has reached the age of fifty and has no children nearby, he may transfer one of his children who is employed to his place of residence. If the transferred child is married, his spouse and minor children, and children without independent living ability may be transferred accordingly.  
The employment and job transfers of officers' family members accompanying the army, and of transferred officers' children and their spouses, shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　军官牺牲、病故后，其随军家属移交政府安置管理。具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 42** After an officer dies in the line of duty or of illness, his family members accompanying the army shall be handed over to the government for placement and management. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第七章　军官退出现役

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军事、政治、后勤、装备军官平时服现役的最低年限分别为：  
（一）担任排级职务的，八年；  
（二）担任连级职务的，副职十年，正职十二年；  
（三）担任营级职务的，副职十四年，正职十六年；  
（四）担任团级职务的，副职十八年，正职二十年。

**Article 43** The minimum number of years for active service in peacetime of military, political, logistics and equipment officers are respectively:  
(1) For those holding platoon-level posts, eight years;  
(2) For those holding company-level posts, ten years for deputy and twelve years for regular;  
(3) For those holding battalion-level posts, fourteen years for deputy and sixteen years for regular;  
(4) For those holding regiment-level posts, eighteen years for deputy and twenty years for regular.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　专业技术军官平时服现役的最低年限分别为：  
（一）担任初级专业技术职务的，十二年；  
（二）担任中级专业技术职务的，十六年；  
（三）担任高级专业技术职务的，二十年。

**Article 44** The minimum number of years for active service in peacetime of professional technical officers are respectively:  
(1) For those holding primary professional technical posts, twelve years;  
(2) For those holding intermediate professional technical posts, sixteen years;  
(3) For those holding senior professional technical posts, twenty years.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军官未达到平时服现役的最低年限的，不得退出现役。但是有下列情形之一的，应当提前退出现役：  
（一）伤病残不能坚持正常工作的；  
（二）经考核不称职又不宜作其他安排的；  
（三）犯有严重错误不适合继续服现役的；  
（四）调离军队，到非军事部门工作的；  
（五）因军队体制编制调整精简需要退出现役的。  
军官未达到平时服现役的最低年限，要求提前退出现役未获批准，经教育仍坚持退出现役的，给予降职（级）处分或者取消其军官身份后，可以作出退出现役处理。

**Article 45** An officer who has not completed the minimum number of years for active service in peacetime shall not retire from active service. However, if any of the following circumstances exists, he shall retire from active service early:  
(1) Being injured, ill or disabled and unable to persist in normal work;  
(2) Being assessed as incompetent and unsuitable for other arrangements;  
(3) Having committed serious mistakes and being unsuitable to continue active service;  
(4) Being transferred out of the armed forces to work in non-military departments;  
(5) Being required to retire from active service due to the adjustment and reduction of the military's organization and establishment.  
If an officer who has not completed the minimum number of years for active service in peacetime requests early retirement from active service and is not approved, and after education still insists on retiring from active service, he may be given a sanction of demotion in post (grade) or his officer status may be revoked, and then he may be dealt with as retired from active service.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　军官达到平时服现役的最高年龄的，应当退出现役。  
军官平时服现役的最高年龄分别为：  
（一）担任正团职职务的，五十岁；  
（二）担任师级职务的，五十五岁；  
（三）担任军级职务的，副职五十八岁，正职六十岁；  
（四）担任其他职务的，服现役的最高年龄与任职的最高年龄相同。

**Article 46** An officer who has reached the maximum age for active service in peacetime shall retire from active service.  
The maximum ages for active service in peacetime of officers are respectively:  
(1) For those holding regular regiment-level posts, fifty years old;  
(2) For those holding division-level posts, fifty-five years old;  
(3) For those holding corps-level posts, fifty-eight for deputy posts and sixty for regular posts;  
(4) For those holding other posts, the maximum age for active service is the same as the maximum age for holding the post.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　军官未达到平时服现役的最高年龄，有下列情形之一的，应当退出现役：  
（一）任职满最高年限后需要退出现役的；  
（二）伤病残不能坚持正常工作的；  
（三）受军队编制员额限制，不能调整使用的；  
（四）调离军队，到非军事部门工作的；  
（五）有其他原因需要退出现役的。

**Article 47** An officer who has not reached the maximum age for active service in peacetime shall retire from active service if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) Having served the maximum number of years in his post and needing to retire from active service;  
(2) Being injured, ill or disabled and unable to persist in normal work;  
(3) Being limited by the authorized strength of the armed forces and unable to be reassigned;  
(4) Being transferred out of the armed forces to work in non-military departments;  
(5) Having other reasons requiring retirement from active service.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　军官退出现役的批准权限与军官职务的任免权限相同。

**Article 48** The authority for approving officers' retirement from active service shall be the same as the authority for appointing and removing officers from their posts.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　军官退出现役后，采取转业由政府安排工作和职务，或者由政府协助就业、发给退役金的方式安置；有的也可以采取复员或者退休的方式安置。  
担任师级以上职务和高级专业技术职务的军官，退出现役后作退休安置，有的也可以作转业安置或者其他安置。  
担任团级以下职务和初级、中级专业技术职务的军官，退出现役后作转业安置或者其他安置。  
对退出现役由政府安排工作和职务以及由政府协助就业、发给退役金的军官，政府应当根据需要进行职业培训。  
未达到服现役的最高年龄，基本丧失工作能力的军官，退出现役后作退休安置。  
服现役满三十年以上或者服现役和参加工作满三十年以上，或者年满五十岁以上的军官，担任师级以上职务，本人提出申请，经组织批准的，退出现役后可以作退休安置；担任团级职务，不宜作转业或者其他安置的，可以由组织批准退出现役后作退休安置。

**Article 49** After officers retire from active service, they shall be placed by means of transfer to civilian work with arrangements made by the government for jobs and posts, or by the government assisting employment and paying retirement pension; some may also be placed by means of demobilization or retirement.  
Officers holding posts at or above the division level and senior professional technical posts shall be placed by retirement after retirement from active service; some may also be placed by transfer to civilian work or other means.  
Officers holding posts at or below the regiment level and primary and intermediate professional technical posts shall be placed by transfer to civilian work or other means after retirement from active service.  
For officers who retire from active service and are arranged by the government for jobs and posts, or who are assisted by the government in employment and paid retirement pension, the government shall provide vocational training as needed.  
Officers who have not reached the maximum age for active service and have basically lost the ability to work shall be placed by retirement after retirement from active service.  
Officers who have served on active duty for not less than thirty years, or who have served on active duty and worked for not less than thirty years in total, or who have reached the age of fifty or above, holding posts at or above the division level, who apply and are approved by the organization, may be placed by retirement after retirement from active service; officers holding posts at the regiment level, if unsuitable for transfer to civilian work or other placement, may, upon approval by the organization, be placed by retirement after retirement from active service.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　军官达到服现役的最高年龄，符合国家规定的离休条件的，可以离职休养。因工作需要或者其他原因，经过批准，可以提前或者推迟离休。

**Article 50** An officer who has reached the maximum age for active service and meets the conditions prescribed by the State for leaving post for rest may leave his post for rest. For reasons of work needs or other reasons, with approval, the leaving post for rest may be advanced or postponed.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　军官退出现役后的安置管理具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。  
军官离职休养和军级以上职务军官退休后，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定安置管理。

**Article 51** Specific measures for the placement and management of officers after retirement from active service shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
After officers leave posts for rest and after officers with posts at or above the corps level retire, they shall be placed and managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　人民解放军总政治部根据本法制定实施办法，报国务院和中央军事委员会批准后施行。

**Article 52** The General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army shall formulate implementing measures in accordance with this Law, and submit them to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval before coming into force.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　中国人民武装警察部队现役警官适用本法，具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 53** This Law shall apply to active-duty police officers of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本法（原称《中国人民解放军现役军官服役条例》）自1989年1月1日起施行。1978年8月18日第五届全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准、1978年8月19日国务院和中央军事委员会颁布的《中国人民解放军干部服役条例》同时废止。

**Article 54** This Law (originally called the Regulations on the Service of Officers in Active Service of the Chinese People's Liberation Army) shall come into force on January 1, 1989. The Regulations on the Service of Cadres of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, approved by the Standing Committee of the Fifth National People's Congress on August 18, 1978, and promulgated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission on August 19, 1978, shall be abolished simultaneously.
