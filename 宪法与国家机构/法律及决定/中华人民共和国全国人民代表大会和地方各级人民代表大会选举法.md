### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据中华人民共和国宪法，制定全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会选举法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution of the People's Republic of China for the election of the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会代表的选举工作，坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持充分发扬民主，坚持严格依法办事。

**Article 2** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, the full development of democracy, and strict compliance with the law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　全国人民代表大会的代表，省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会的代表，由下一级人民代表大会选举。  
不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会的代表，由选民直接选举。

**Article 3** Deputies to the National People's Congress and deputies to the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall be elected by the people's congresses at the next lower level.  
Deputies to the people's congresses of cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, autonomous counties, townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall be directly elected by the voters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国年满十八周岁的公民，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、家庭出身、宗教信仰、教育程度、财产状况和居住期限，都有选举权和被选举权。  
依照法律被剥夺政治权利的人没有选举权和被选举权。

**Article 4** All citizens of the People's Republic of China who have reached the age of 18 shall have the right to vote and stand for election, regardless of ethnic status, race, sex, occupation, family background, religious belief, education, property status, or length of residence.  
Persons who have been deprived of political rights according to law shall not have the right to vote or stand for election.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　每一选民在一次选举中只有一个投票权。

**Article 5** Each voter shall have only one vote in an election.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民解放军单独进行选举，选举办法另订。

**Article 6** The People's Liberation Army shall hold elections separately, and the electoral procedures therefor shall be separately prescribed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会的代表应当具有广泛的代表性，应当有适当数量的基层代表，特别是工人、农民和知识分子代表；应当有适当数量的妇女代表，并逐步提高妇女代表的比例。  
全国人民代表大会和归侨人数较多地区的地方人民代表大会，应当有适当名额的归侨代表。  
旅居国外的中华人民共和国公民在县级以下人民代表大会代表选举期间在国内的，可以参加原籍地或者出国前居住地的选举。

**Article 7** Deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be broadly representative and shall include an appropriate number of grassroots deputies, especially workers, farmers, and intellectuals; there shall be an appropriate number of women deputies, and the proportion of women deputies shall be gradually increased.  
The National People's Congress and the local people's congresses of areas where there is a relatively large number of returned overseas Chinese shall have an appropriate number of deputies who are returned overseas Chinese.  
Citizens of the People's Republic of China residing abroad who are in the country during the period of election of deputies to people's congresses below the county level may participate in the election in their place of origin or the place where they resided before leaving the country.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会的选举经费，列入财政预算，由国库开支。

**Article 8** The election expenses for the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be included in the fiscal budget and disbursed from the state treasury.

### 第二章　选举机构

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会主持全国人民代表大会代表的选举。省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会常务委员会主持本级人民代表大会代表的选举。  
不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县、乡、民族乡、镇设立选举委员会，主持本级人民代表大会代表的选举。不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县的选举委员会受本级人民代表大会常务委员会的领导。乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会受不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县的人民代表大会常务委员会的领导。  
省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会常务委员会指导本行政区域内县级以下人民代表大会代表的选举工作。

**Article 9** The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall preside over the election of deputies to the National People's Congress. The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall preside over the election of deputies to the people's congresses at the corresponding levels.  
Electoral commissions shall be established in cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, autonomous counties, townships, ethnic townships, and towns to preside over the election of deputies to the people's congresses at the corresponding levels. The electoral commissions of cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, and autonomous counties shall be subject to the leadership of the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels. The electoral commissions of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall be subject to the leadership of the standing committees of the people's congresses of the cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, and autonomous counties.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall guide the election work for deputies to people's congresses below the county level within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县的选举委员会的组成人员由本级人民代表大会常务委员会任命。乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会的组成人员由不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县的人民代表大会常务委员会任命。  
选举委员会的组成人员为代表候选人的，应当辞去选举委员会的职务。

**Article 10** The members of the electoral commissions of cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, and autonomous counties shall be appointed by the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels. The members of the electoral commissions of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall be appointed by the standing committees of the people's congresses of the cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, and autonomous counties.  
If a member of an electoral commission becomes a candidate for deputy, he or she shall resign from the electoral commission.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　选举委员会履行下列职责：  
（一）划分选举本级人民代表大会代表的选区，分配各选区应选代表的名额；  
（二）进行选民登记，审查选民资格，公布选民名单；受理对于选民名单不同意见的申诉，并作出决定；  
（三）确定选举日期；  
（四）了解核实并组织介绍代表候选人的情况；根据较多数选民的意见，确定和公布正式代表候选人名单；  
（五）主持投票选举；  
（六）确定选举结果是否有效，公布当选代表名单；  
（七）法律规定的其他职责。  
选举委员会应当及时公布选举信息。

**Article 11** An electoral commission shall perform the following duties:  
(1) To divide electoral districts for the election of deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level and to allocate the number of deputies to be elected in each electoral district;  
(2) To register voters, examine their qualifications, and publish the list of voters; to accept petitions concerning objections to the list of voters and make decisions thereon;  
(3) To determine the date of election;  
(4) To ascertain and verify the information on candidates for deputies and organize the introduction of such information; to determine and publish the list of official candidates for deputies in accordance with the opinions of the majority of voters;  
(5) To preside over the voting and election;  
(6) To determine whether the election results are valid and publish the list of elected deputies;  
(7) To perform other duties as prescribed by law.  
An electoral commission shall promptly publish election information.

### 第三章　地方各级人民代表大会代表名额

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地方各级人民代表大会的代表名额，按照下列规定确定：  
（一）省、自治区、直辖市的代表名额基数为三百五十名，省、自治区每十五万人可以增加一名代表，直辖市每二万五千人可以增加一名代表；但是，代表总名额不得超过一千名；  
（二）设区的市、自治州的代表名额基数为二百四十名，每二万五千人可以增加一名代表；人口超过一千万的，代表总名额不得超过六百五十名；  
（三）不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县的代表名额基数为一百四十名，每五千人可以增加一名代表；人口超过一百五十五万的，代表总名额不得超过四百五十名；人口不足五万的，代表总名额可以少于一百四十名；  
（四）乡、民族乡、镇的代表名额基数为四十五名，每一千五百人可以增加一名代表；但是，代表总名额不得超过一百六十名；人口不足二千的，代表总名额可以少于四十五名。  
按照前款规定的地方各级人民代表大会的代表名额基数与按人口数增加的代表数相加，即为地方各级人民代表大会的代表总名额。  
自治区、聚居的少数民族多的省，经全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定，代表名额可以另加百分之五。聚居的少数民族多或者人口居住分散的县、自治县、乡、民族乡，经省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会决定，代表名额可以另加百分之五。

**Article 12** The number of deputies to local people's congresses at all levels shall be determined in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the base number of deputies shall be 350. For provinces and autonomous regions, one deputy may be added for every 150,000 people; for municipalities directly under the Central Government, one deputy may be added for every 25,000 people. However, the total number of deputies shall not exceed 1,000;  
(2) For cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures, the base number of deputies shall be 240. One deputy may be added for every 25,000 people; if the population exceeds 10 million, the total number of deputies shall not exceed 650;  
(3) For cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, counties, and autonomous counties, the base number of deputies shall be 140. One deputy may be added for every 5,000 people; if the population exceeds 1.55 million, the total number of deputies shall not exceed 450; if the population is less than 50,000, the total number of deputies may be less than 140;  
(4) For townships, ethnic townships, and towns, the base number of deputies shall be 45. One deputy may be added for every 1,500 people. However, the total number of deputies shall not exceed 160; if the population is less than 2,000, the total number of deputies may be less than 45.  
The total number of deputies to a local people's congress at any level shall be the sum of the base number provided for in the preceding paragraph and the number of deputies added on the basis of population.  
For autonomous regions and provinces with a large number of compactly inhabited ethnic minorities, the number of deputies may be increased by an additional 5% subject to the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. For counties, autonomous counties, townships, and ethnic townships with a large number of compactly inhabited ethnic minorities or a dispersed population, the number of deputies may be increased by an additional 5% subject to the decision of the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会代表的具体名额，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会依照本法确定。设区的市、自治州和县级的人民代表大会代表的具体名额，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会依照本法确定，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。乡级的人民代表大会代表的具体名额，由县级的人民代表大会常务委员会依照本法确定，报上一级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 13** The specific number of deputies to the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be determined by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress in accordance with this Law. The specific number of deputies to the people's congresses of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, and counties shall be determined by the standing committees of the people's congresses of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with this Law and shall be reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for the record. The specific number of deputies to a township people's congress shall be determined by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant county in accordance with this Law and shall be reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the next higher level for the record.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地方各级人民代表大会的代表总名额经确定后，不再变动。如果由于行政区划变动或者由于重大工程建设等原因造成人口较大变动的，该级人民代表大会的代表总名额依照本法的规定重新确定。  
依照前款规定重新确定代表名额的，省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会应当在三十日内将重新确定代表名额的情况报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 14** Once the total number of deputies to a local people's congress at any level is determined, it shall not be changed. However, if a substantial change in population occurs due to an adjustment of administrative divisions or major construction projects, the total number of deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level shall be redetermined in accordance with this Law.  
In the event of redetermination of the number of deputies under the preceding paragraph, the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days, report the circumstances of the redetermination to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for the record.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地方各级人民代表大会代表名额，由本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者本级选举委员会根据本行政区域所辖的下一级各行政区域或者各选区的人口数，按照每一代表所代表的城乡人口数相同的原则，以及保证各地区、各民族、各方面都有适当数量代表的要求进行分配。在县、自治县的人民代表大会中，人口特少的乡、民族乡、镇，至少应有代表一人。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表名额的分配办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会参照全国人民代表大会代表名额分配的办法，结合本地区的具体情况规定。

**Article 15** The number of deputies to a local people's congress at any level shall be allocated by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the electoral commission thereof on the basis of the population of the next lower administrative areas or electoral districts under its jurisdiction, in accordance with the principle of equal representation of urban and rural populations per deputy, and the requirement that all regions, ethnic groups, and sectors shall be assured of an appropriate number of deputies. In the people's congress of a county or autonomous county, townships, ethnic townships, and towns with a very small population shall have at least one deputy each.  
The method for allocating the number of deputies to local people's congresses at all levels shall be formulated by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government by reference to the allocation method for the number of deputies to the National People's Congress, taking into account the specific local conditions.

### 第四章　全国人民代表大会代表名额

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　全国人民代表大会的代表，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会和人民解放军选举产生。  
全国人民代表大会代表的名额不超过三千人。  
香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区应选全国人民代表大会代表的名额和代表产生办法，由全国人民代表大会另行规定。

**Article 16** Deputies to the National People's Congress shall be elected by the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and by the People's Liberation Army.  
The total number of deputies to the National People's Congress shall not exceed 3,000.  
The number of deputies to be elected from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region to the National People's Congress and the method of their election shall be separately prescribed by the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　全国人民代表大会代表名额，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会根据各省、自治区、直辖市的人口数，按照每一代表所代表的城乡人口数相同的原则，以及保证各地区、各民族、各方面都有适当数量代表的要求进行分配。  
省、自治区、直辖市应选全国人民代表大会代表名额，由根据人口数计算确定的名额数、相同的地区基本名额数和其他应选名额数构成。  
全国人民代表大会代表名额的具体分配，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。

**Article 17** The number of deputies to the National People's Congress shall be allocated by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the basis of the population of each province, autonomous region, and municipality directly under the Central Government, in accordance with the principle of equal representation of urban and rural populations per deputy, and the requirement that all regions, ethnic groups, and sectors shall be assured of an appropriate number of deputies.  
The number of deputies to be elected from a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to the National People's Congress shall consist of the number determined by calculation on the basis of population, the same basic number for each region, and other numbers to be elected.  
The specific allocation of the number of deputies to the National People's Congress shall be determined by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　全国少数民族应选全国人民代表大会代表，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会参照各少数民族的人口数和分布等情况，分配给各省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会选出。人口特少的民族，至少应有代表一人。

**Article 18** Deputies to the National People's Congress to be elected by the minority nationalities of the whole country shall be allocated by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress to the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for election, taking into account the population and distribution of each minority nationality. A minority nationality with a very small population shall have at least one deputy.

### 第五章　各少数民族的选举

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有少数民族聚居的地方，每一聚居的少数民族都应有代表参加当地的人民代表大会。  
聚居境内同一少数民族的总人口数占境内总人口数百分之三十以上的，每一代表所代表的人口数应相当于当地人民代表大会每一代表所代表的人口数。  
聚居境内同一少数民族的总人口数不足境内总人口数百分之十五的，每一代表所代表的人口数可以适当少于当地人民代表大会每一代表所代表的人口数，但不得少于二分之一；实行区域自治的民族人口特少的自治县，经省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会决定，可以少于二分之一。人口特少的其他聚居民族，至少应有代表一人。  
聚居境内同一少数民族的总人口数占境内总人口数百分之十五以上、不足百分之三十的，每一代表所代表的人口数，可以适当少于当地人民代表大会每一代表所代表的人口数，但分配给该少数民族的应选代表名额不得超过代表总名额的百分之三十。

**Article 19** In areas where minority nationalities live in compact communities, each such minority nationality shall have deputies to participate in the local people's congress.  
Where the total population of a minority nationality living in a compact community accounts for 30% or more of the total population in the area, the population represented by each deputy shall be equal to that represented by each deputy of the local people's congress.  
Where the total population of a minority nationality living in a compact community accounts for less than 15% of the total population in the area, the population represented by each deputy may be appropriately less than that represented by each deputy of the local people's congress, but shall not be less than one-half thereof. For an autonomous county with a very small population of the nationality exercising regional autonomy, it may be less than one-half, subject to the decision of the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province or autonomous region. Other compactly inhabited minority nationalities with a very small population shall each have at least one deputy.  
Where the total population of a minority nationality living in a compact community accounts for 15% or more but less than 30% of the total population in the area, the population represented by each deputy may be appropriately less than that represented by each deputy of the local people's congress, but the number of deputies to be elected allocated to that minority nationality shall not exceed 30% of the total number of deputies.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　自治区、自治州、自治县和有少数民族聚居的乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会，对于聚居在境内的其他少数民族和汉族代表的选举，适用[本法第十九条](#art-19)的规定。

**Article 20** The provisions of Article 19 of this Law shall apply to the election of deputies from other minority nationalities and the Han nationality living in compact communities within the territory of an autonomous region, autonomous prefecture, autonomous county, or a township, ethnic township, or town where minority nationalities live in compact communities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　散居的少数民族应选当地人民代表大会的代表，每一代表所代表的人口数可以少于当地人民代表大会每一代表所代表的人口数。  
自治区、自治州、自治县和有少数民族聚居的乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会，对于散居的其他少数民族和汉族代表的选举，适用前款的规定。

**Article 21** For deputies to be elected from minority nationalities living in scattered groups to the local people's congress, the population represented by each deputy may be less than that represented by each deputy of the local people's congress.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to the election of deputies from other minority nationalities and the Han nationality living in scattered groups within the territory of an autonomous region, autonomous prefecture, autonomous county, or a township, ethnic township, or town where minority nationalities live in compact communities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有少数民族聚居的不设区的市、市辖区、县、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表的产生，按照当地的民族关系和居住状况，各少数民族选民可以单独选举或者联合选举。  
自治县和有少数民族聚居的乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会，对于居住在境内的其他少数民族和汉族代表的选举办法，适用前款的规定。

**Article 22** For the election of deputies to the people's congress in a city not divided into districts, municipal district, county, township, ethnic township, or town where minority nationalities live in compact communities, voters of each minority nationality may vote separately or jointly, in light of the local ethnic relations and residential conditions.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to the electoral methods of deputies from other minority nationalities and the Han nationality living within the territory of an autonomous county or a township, ethnic township, or town where minority nationalities live in compact communities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　自治区、自治州、自治县制定或者公布的选举文件、选民名单、选民证、代表候选人名单、代表当选证书和选举委员会的印章等，都应当同时使用当地通用的民族文字。

**Article 23** In autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures, and autonomous counties, the electoral documents, voters' lists, voters' cards, lists of candidates for deputies, certificates of election for deputies, and seals of electoral commissions, etc., formulated or published shall be simultaneously in the locally commonly used minority nationality languages.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　少数民族选举的其他事项，参照本法有关各条的规定办理。

**Article 24** Other matters concerning the election of minority nationalities shall be handled with reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

### 第六章　选区划分

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　不设区的市、市辖区、县、自治县、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会的代表名额分配到选区，按选区进行选举。选区可以按居住状况划分，也可以按生产单位、事业单位、工作单位划分。  
选区的大小，按照每一选区选一名至三名代表划分。

**Article 25** The number of deputies to the people's congress of a city not divided into districts, municipal district, county, autonomous county, township, ethnic township, or town shall be allocated to electoral districts, and elections shall be conducted by electoral districts. Electoral districts may be delineated on the basis of residential conditions or on the basis of production units, institutions, or work units.  
The size of an electoral district shall be such that one to three deputies are to be elected from each electoral district.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本行政区域内各选区每一代表所代表的人口数应当大体相等。

**Article 26** The population represented by each deputy in the various electoral districts within an administrative area shall be approximately equal.

### 第七章　选民登记

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　选民登记按选区进行，经登记确认的选民资格长期有效。每次选举前对上次选民登记以后新满十八周岁的、被剥夺政治权利期满后恢复政治权利的选民，予以登记。对选民经登记后迁出原选区的，列入新迁入的选区的选民名单；对死亡的和依照法律被剥夺政治权利的人，从选民名单上除名。  
精神病患者不能行使选举权利的，经选举委员会确认，不列入选民名单。

**Article 27** Voter registration shall be conducted by electoral districts. The eligibility of a voter, once registered, shall be valid for a long term. Before each election, voters who have reached the age of 18 after the last voter registration and those who have been restored to political rights after the expiration of the period for deprivation of political rights shall be registered. Voters who have moved out of the original electoral district after registration shall be included in the voters' list of the electoral district to which they have moved; those who have died or have been deprived of political rights according to law shall be removed from the voters' list.  
A person suffering from a mental disorder who is unable to exercise the right to vote shall not be included in the voters' list upon confirmation by the electoral commission.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　选民名单应在选举日的二十日以前公布，实行凭选民证参加投票选举的，并应当发给选民证。

**Article 28** The voters' list shall be published 20 days before the election date. Where voting is conducted on the basis of a voters' card, a voters' card shall be issued to each voter.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对于公布的选民名单有不同意见的，可以在选民名单公布之日起五日内向选举委员会提出申诉。选举委员会对申诉意见，应在三日内作出处理决定。申诉人如果对处理决定不服，可以在选举日的五日以前向人民法院起诉，人民法院应在选举日以前作出判决。人民法院的判决为最后决定。

**Article 29** Any person who has an objection to the published voters' list may file a petition with the electoral commission within five days from the date of publication. The electoral commission shall make a decision on the petition within three days. If the petitioner is dissatisfied with the decision, he or she may file a prosecution with the People's Court within five days before the election date, and the People's Court shall render a judgment before the election date. The judgment of the People's Court shall be final.

### 第八章　代表候选人的提出

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　全国和地方各级人民代表大会的代表候选人，按选区或者选举单位提名产生。  
各政党、各人民团体，可以联合或者单独推荐代表候选人。选民或者代表，十人以上联名，也可以推荐代表候选人。推荐者应向选举委员会或者大会主席团介绍代表候选人的情况。接受推荐的代表候选人应当向选举委员会或者大会主席团如实提供个人身份、简历等基本情况。提供的基本情况不实的，选举委员会或者大会主席团应当向选民或者代表通报。  
各政党、各人民团体联合或者单独推荐的代表候选人的人数，每一选民或者代表参加联名推荐的代表候选人的人数，均不得超过本选区或者选举单位应选代表的名额。

**Article 30** Candidates for deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be nominated by electoral districts or electoral units.  
Political parties and people's organizations may, jointly or separately, recommend candidates for deputies. A group of ten or more voters or deputies may also recommend candidates for deputies jointly. The recommender shall introduce to the electoral commission or the presidium of the congress the information on the recommended candidates for deputies. A recommended candidate for deputy shall truthfully provide to the electoral commission or the presidium of the congress basic information such as his or her personal identity and resume. If the basic information provided is untrue, the electoral commission or the presidium of the congress shall inform the voters or deputies thereof.  
The number of candidates recommended jointly or separately by political parties and people's organizations, and the number of candidates recommended jointly by each voter or deputy participating in the joint recommendation shall not exceed the number of deputies to be elected in the electoral district or electoral unit.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　全国和地方各级人民代表大会代表实行差额选举，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表的名额。  
由选民直接选举人民代表大会代表的，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表名额三分之一至一倍；由县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会选举上一级人民代表大会代表的，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表名额五分之一至二分之一。

**Article 31** Competitive elections shall be conducted for deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels. The number of candidates for deputies shall be greater than the number of deputies to be elected.  
Where deputies to a people's congress are directly elected by voters, the number of candidates for deputies shall be one-third to one time greater than the number of deputies to be elected. Where deputies to a people's congress at a higher level are elected by a local people's congress at or above the county level, the number of candidates for deputies shall be one-fifth to one-half greater than the number of deputies to be elected.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　由选民直接选举人民代表大会代表的，代表候选人由各选区选民和各政党、各人民团体提名推荐。选举委员会汇总后，将代表候选人名单及代表候选人的基本情况在选举日的十五日以前公布，并交各该选区的选民小组讨论、协商，确定正式代表候选人名单。如果所提代表候选人的人数超过[本法第三十一条](#art-31)规定的最高差额比例，由选举委员会交各该选区的选民小组讨论、协商，根据较多数选民的意见，确定正式代表候选人名单；对正式代表候选人不能形成较为一致意见的，进行预选，根据预选时得票多少的顺序，确定正式代表候选人名单。正式代表候选人名单及代表候选人的基本情况应当在选举日的七日以前公布。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会在选举上一级人民代表大会代表时，提名、酝酿代表候选人的时间不得少于两天。各该级人民代表大会主席团将依法提出的代表候选人名单及代表候选人的基本情况印发全体代表，由全体代表酝酿、讨论。如果所提代表候选人的人数符合[本法第三十一条](#art-31)规定的差额比例，直接进行投票选举。如果所提代表候选人的人数超过[本法第三十一条](#art-31)规定的最高差额比例，进行预选，根据预选时得票多少的顺序，按照本级人民代表大会的选举办法根据本法确定的具体差额比例，确定正式代表候选人名单，进行投票选举。

**Article 32** Where deputies to a people's congress are directly elected by voters, candidates for deputies shall be nominated and recommended by voters of each electoral district and by political parties and people's organizations. After the electoral commission has gathered the nominations, it shall publish the list of candidates for deputies and their basic information 15 days before the election date and submit them to the voters' groups in the respective electoral districts for discussion and consultation to determine the list of official candidates for deputies. If the number of candidates proposed exceeds the maximum margin ratio prescribed in Article 31 of this Law, the electoral commission shall refer the matter to the voters' groups in the respective electoral districts for discussion and consultation, and shall determine the list of official candidates for deputies in accordance with the opinions of the majority of voters; if no relatively consistent opinion can be formed on the official candidates, a primary election shall be held, and the list of official candidates for deputies shall be determined according to the order of votes received in the primary election. The list of official candidates for deputies and their basic information shall be published seven days before the election date.  
Where deputies to a people's congress at a higher level are elected by a local people's congress at or above the county level, the time for nomination and deliberation of candidates for deputies shall not be less than two days. The presidium of the respective level of the people's congress shall print and distribute to all deputies the list of candidates for deputies nominated in accordance with law and their basic information for deliberation and discussion by all deputies. If the number of candidates proposed is within the margin ratio prescribed in Article 31 of this Law, voting shall be conducted directly. If the number of candidates proposed exceeds the maximum margin ratio prescribed in Article 31 of this Law, a primary election shall be held, and the list of official candidates for deputies shall be determined according to the order of votes received in the primary election, subject to the specific margin ratio determined by the electoral method of the respective level of the people's congress in accordance with this Law, followed by voting.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会在选举上一级人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人不限于各该级人民代表大会的代表。

**Article 33** When a local people's congress at or above the county level elects deputies to a people's congress at a higher level, the candidates for deputies shall not be limited to the deputies of the respective level of the people's congress.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　选举委员会或者人民代表大会主席团应当向选民或者代表介绍代表候选人的情况。推荐代表候选人的政党、人民团体和选民、代表可以在选民小组或者代表小组会议上介绍所推荐的代表候选人的情况。选举委员会根据选民的要求，应当组织代表候选人与选民见面，由代表候选人介绍本人的情况，回答选民的问题。但是，在选举日必须停止代表候选人的介绍。

**Article 34** The electoral commission or the presidium of the people's congress shall introduce to the voters or deputies the information on candidates for deputies. The political parties, people's organizations, voters, and deputies that recommended the candidates for deputies may introduce the information on the recommended candidates at meetings of voters' groups or deputies' groups. Upon request of the voters, the electoral commission shall organize meetings between candidates for deputies and voters, at which the candidates shall introduce their own information and answer voters' questions. However, the introduction of candidates for deputies shall be stopped on the election day.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公民参加各级人民代表大会代表的选举，不得直接或者间接接受境外机构、组织、个人提供的与选举有关的任何形式的资助。  
违反前款规定的，不列入代表候选人名单；已经列入代表候选人名单的，从名单中除名；已经当选的，其当选无效。

**Article 35** When citizens participate in the election of deputies to people's congresses at all levels, they shall not, directly or indirectly, accept any form of funding related to the election from overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals.  
Anyone who violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not be included in the list of candidates for deputies; if already included, he or she shall be removed from the list; if already elected, his or her election shall be invalid.

### 第九章　选举程序

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会代表的选举，应当严格依照法定程序进行，并接受监督。任何组织或者个人都不得以任何方式干预选民或者代表自由行使选举权。

**Article 36** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be conducted strictly in accordance with the statutory procedures and shall be subject to supervision. No organization or individual may, in any manner, interfere with the free exercise of the right to vote by voters or deputies.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在选民直接选举人民代表大会代表时，选民根据选举委员会的规定，凭身份证或者选民证领取选票。

**Article 37** When deputies to a people's congress are directly elected by voters, voters shall obtain their ballot papers by presenting their identity cards or voters' cards in accordance with the provisions of the electoral commission.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　选举委员会应当根据各选区选民分布状况，按照方便选民投票的原则设立投票站，进行选举。选民居住比较集中的，可以召开选举大会，进行选举；因患有疾病等原因行动不便或者居住分散并且交通不便的选民，可以在流动票箱投票。

**Article 38** The electoral commission shall, based on the distribution of voters in each electoral district and in accordance with the principle of facilitating voting by voters, set up polling stations for conducting the election. Where voters live in relatively concentrated areas, an election meeting may be convened for the election. Voters who have difficulty moving due to illness or other reasons or who live in scattered areas with inconvenient transportation may vote in mobile ballot boxes.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会在选举上一级人民代表大会代表时，由各该级人民代表大会主席团主持。

**Article 39** Where a local people's congress at or above the county level elects deputies to a people's congress at a higher level, the election shall be presided over by the presidium of the respective level of the people's congress.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　全国和地方各级人民代表大会代表的选举，一律采用无记名投票的方法。选举时应当设有秘密写票处。  
选民如果是文盲或者因残疾不能写选票的，可以委托他信任的人代写。

**Article 40** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be conducted by secret ballot. Secret voting booths shall be provided during the election.  
If a voter is illiterate or unable to write a ballot paper due to a disability, he or she may entrust a person he or she trusts to write on his or her behalf.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　选举人对于代表候选人可以投赞成票，可以投反对票，可以另选其他任何选民，也可以弃权。

**Article 41** An elector may vote for a candidate for deputy, vote against, vote for any other voter, or abstain from voting.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　选民如果在选举期间外出，经选举委员会同意，可以书面委托其他选民代为投票。每一选民接受的委托不得超过三人，并应当按照委托人的意愿代为投票。

**Article 42** If a voter is away during the election period, he or she may, with the consent of the electoral commission, entrust another voter in writing to vote on his or her behalf. No voter may accept more than three such entrustments, and the vote shall be cast in accordance with the will of the principal.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　投票结束后，由选民或者代表推选的监票、计票人员和选举委员会或者人民代表大会主席团的人员将投票人数和票数加以核对，作出记录，并由监票人签字。  
代表候选人的近亲属不得担任监票人、计票人。

**Article 43** After the voting is concluded, the scrutineers and vote counters elected by the voters or deputies and the personnel of the electoral commission or the presidium of the people's congress shall check the number of voters and votes, make a record, and the scrutineers shall sign it.  
Close relatives of a candidate for deputy shall not serve as scrutineers or vote counters.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　每次选举所投的票数，多于投票人数的无效，等于或者少于投票人数的有效。  
每一选票所选的人数，多于规定应选代表人数的作废，等于或者少于规定应选代表人数的有效。

**Article 44** If the number of votes cast in an election exceeds the number of voters, the election shall be invalid; if it is equal to or less than the number of voters, the election shall be valid.  
If the number of persons voted for on a ballot paper exceeds the prescribed number of persons to be elected, the ballot paper shall be void; if it is equal to or less than the prescribed number of persons to be elected, the ballot paper shall be valid.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在选民直接选举人民代表大会代表时，选区全体选民的过半数参加投票，选举有效。代表候选人获得参加投票的选民过半数的选票时，始得当选。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会在选举上一级人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人获得全体代表过半数的选票时，始得当选。  
获得过半数选票的代表候选人的人数超过应选代表名额时，以得票多的当选。如遇票数相等不能确定当选人时，应当就票数相等的候选人再次投票，以得票多的当选。  
获得过半数选票的当选代表的人数少于应选代表的名额时，不足的名额另行选举。另行选举时，根据在第一次投票时得票多少的顺序，按照[本法第三十一条](#art-31)规定的差额比例，确定候选人名单。如果只选一人，候选人应为二人。  
依照前款规定另行选举县级和乡级的人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人以得票多的当选，但是得票数不得少于选票的三分之一；县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会在另行选举上一级人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人获得全体代表过半数的选票，始得当选。

**Article 45** When deputies to a people's congress are directly elected by voters, the election shall be valid if more than half of the voters in the electoral district participate in the voting. A candidate for deputy shall be elected only if he or she receives more than half of the votes cast by the voters participating in the voting.  
When a local people's congress at or above the county level elects deputies to a people's congress at a higher level, a candidate for deputy shall be elected only if he or she receives more than half of the votes of all the deputies.  
Where the number of candidates receiving a majority of votes exceeds the number of deputies to be elected, those with the highest number of votes shall be elected. In the event of a tie in the number of votes such that the elected candidates cannot be determined, a second ballot shall be held for the tied candidates, and those with the highest number of votes in the second ballot shall be elected.  
Where the number of elected deputies receiving a majority of votes is less than the number of deputies to be elected, the remaining seats shall be filled by a supplementary election. In the supplementary election, the list of candidates shall be determined in accordance with the order of the number of votes received in the first ballot, subject to the margin ratio prescribed in Article 31 of this Law. If only one deputy is to be elected, there shall be two candidates.  
When a supplementary election is held in accordance with the preceding paragraph for deputies to a people's congress at the county or township level, the candidate with the highest number of votes shall be elected, provided that the number of votes received is not less than one-third of the votes cast. When a local people's congress at or above the county level holds a supplementary election for deputies to a people's congress at a higher level, a candidate for deputy shall be elected only if he or she receives more than half of the votes of all the deputies.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　选举结果由选举委员会或者人民代表大会主席团根据本法确定是否有效，并予以宣布。  
当选代表名单由选举委员会或者人民代表大会主席团予以公布。

**Article 46** The electoral commission or the presidium of the people's congress shall determine whether the election results are valid in accordance with this Law and shall announce the results.  
The list of elected deputies shall be published by the electoral commission or the presidium of the people's congress.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　代表资格审查委员会依法对当选代表是否符合宪法、法律规定的代表的基本条件，选举是否符合法律规定的程序，以及是否存在破坏选举和其他当选无效的违法行为进行审查，提出代表当选是否有效的意见，向本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团报告。  
县级以上的各级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团根据代表资格审查委员会提出的报告，确认代表的资格或者确定代表的当选无效，在每届人民代表大会第一次会议前公布代表名单。

**Article 47** The credentials committee shall, in accordance with law, examine whether the elected deputies meet the basic conditions for deputies as prescribed by the Constitution and laws, whether the election complies with the procedures prescribed by law, and whether there exist any illegal acts such as undermining the election or other acts rendering the election invalid, and shall provide an opinion on whether the election of the deputy is valid, and report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or to the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town.  
The standing committee of the people's congress at or above the county level or the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town shall, based on the report submitted by the credentials committee, confirm the qualification of the deputy or determine the invalidity of the deputy's election, and shall publish the list of deputies before the first session of each term of the people's congress.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　公民不得同时担任两个以上无隶属关系的行政区域的人民代表大会代表。

**Article 48** A citizen shall not serve simultaneously as a deputy to people's congresses of two or more administrative areas that are not subordinate to each other.

### 第十章　对代表的监督和罢免、辞职、补选

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　全国和地方各级人民代表大会的代表，受选民和原选举单位的监督。选民或者选举单位都有权罢免自己选出的代表。

**Article 49** Deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at all levels shall be subject to the supervision of the voters and the original electoral units. Both voters and electoral units shall have the right to recall the deputies they have elected.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对于县级的人民代表大会代表，原选区选民五十人以上联名，对于乡级的人民代表大会代表，原选区选民三十人以上联名，可以向县级的人民代表大会常务委员会书面提出罢免要求。  
罢免要求应当写明罢免理由。被提出罢免的代表有权在选民会议上提出申辩意见，也可以书面提出申辩意见。  
县级的人民代表大会常务委员会应当将罢免要求和被提出罢免的代表的书面申辩意见印发原选区选民。  
表决罢免要求，由县级的人民代表大会常务委员会派有关负责人员主持。

**Article 50** For a deputy to a people's congress at the county level, a petition for recall may be submitted in writing to the standing committee of the people's congress at the county level by 50 or more voters of the original electoral district jointly; for a deputy to a people's congress at the township level, a petition for recall may be submitted in writing to the standing committee of the people's congress at the county level by 30 or more voters of the original electoral district jointly.  
The petition for recall shall specify the reasons for the recall. The deputy proposed to be recalled shall have the right to present a defense at a meeting of voters or to submit a written defense.  
The standing committee of the county people's congress shall distribute the petition for recall and the written defense of the deputy proposed to be recalled to the voters of the original electoral district.  
The vote on the petition for recall shall be presided over by the relevant responsible personnel sent by the standing committee of the county people's congress.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，主席团或者十分之一以上代表联名，可以提出对由该级人民代表大会选出的上一级人民代表大会代表的罢免案。在人民代表大会闭会期间，县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会主任会议或者常务委员会五分之一以上组成人员联名，可以向常务委员会提出对由该级人民代表大会选出的上一级人民代表大会代表的罢免案。罢免案应当写明罢免理由。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，被提出罢免的代表有权在主席团会议和大会全体会议上提出申辩意见，或者书面提出申辩意见，由主席团印发会议。罢免案经会议审议后，由主席团提请全体会议表决。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会举行会议的时候，被提出罢免的代表有权在主任会议和常务委员会全体会议上提出申辩意见，或者书面提出申辩意见，由主任会议印发会议。罢免案经会议审议后，由主任会议提请全体会议表决。

**Article 51** When a local people's congress at or above the county level is in session, the presidium or one-tenth or more of the deputies jointly may submit a proposal for the recall of a deputy to the people's congress at the next higher level elected by that people's congress. During the recess of the people's congress, the council of chairpersons of the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level, or one-fifth or more of the members of the standing committee jointly, may submit to the standing committee a proposal for the recall of a deputy to the people's congress at the next higher level elected by that people's congress. The recall proposal shall state the reasons for the recall.  
When a local people's congress at or above the county level is in session, the deputy proposed to be recalled shall have the right to present a defense at a meeting of the presidium and at a plenary meeting of the congress, or to submit a written defense which shall be printed and distributed to the congress by the presidium. After deliberation on the recall proposal, the presidium shall submit it to the plenary meeting for a vote.  
When the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level is in session, the deputy proposed to be recalled shall have the right to present a defense at a meeting of the council of chairpersons and at a plenary meeting of the standing committee, or to submit a written defense which shall be printed and distributed to the meeting by the council of chairpersons. After deliberation on the recall proposal, the council of chairpersons shall submit it to the plenary meeting for a vote.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　罢免代表采用无记名的表决方式。

**Article 52** The recall of a deputy shall be voted by secret ballot.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　罢免县级和乡级的人民代表大会代表，须经原选区过半数的选民通过。  
罢免由县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会选出的代表，须经各该级人民代表大会过半数的代表通过；在代表大会闭会期间，须经常务委员会组成人员的过半数通过。罢免的决议，须报送上一级人民代表大会常务委员会备案、公告。

**Article 53** The recall of a deputy to the people's congress at the county or township level shall be approved by a majority of the voters in the original electoral district for that deputy.  
The recall of a deputy elected by a local people's congress at or above the county level shall be approved by a majority of the deputies to that people's congress; during the recess of the congress, it shall be approved by a majority of the members of the standing committee. The resolution on recall shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the next higher level for the record and announcement.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上的各级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员，县级以上的各级人民代表大会专门委员会成员的代表职务被罢免的，其常务委员会组成人员或者专门委员会成员的职务相应撤销，由主席团或者常务委员会予以公告。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席的代表职务被罢免的，其主席、副主席的职务相应撤销，由主席团予以公告。

**Article 54** Where a deputy's post in the standing committee of a people's congress at or above the county level or in a special committee of such a people's congress is recalled, the posts of that deputy as a member of the standing committee or as a member of the special committee shall be revoked accordingly, and the presidium or the standing committee shall make an announcement thereof.  
Where a deputy's post as chairman or vice-chairman of the people's congress of a township, nationality township, or town is recalled, the post of that deputy as chairman or vice-chairman shall be revoked accordingly, and the presidium shall make an announcement thereof.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　全国人民代表大会代表，省、自治区、直辖市、设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会代表，可以向选举他的人民代表大会的常务委员会书面提出辞职。常务委员会接受辞职，须经常务委员会组成人员的过半数通过。接受辞职的决议，须报送上一级人民代表大会常务委员会备案、公告。  
县级的人民代表大会代表可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会书面提出辞职，乡级的人民代表大会代表可以向本级人民代表大会书面提出辞职。县级的人民代表大会常务委员会接受辞职，须经常务委员会组成人员的过半数通过。乡级的人民代表大会接受辞职，须经人民代表大会过半数的代表通过。接受辞职的，应当予以公告。

**Article 55** A deputy to the National People's Congress, or to the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, city divided into districts, or autonomous prefecture, may submit a written resignation to the standing committee of the people's congress that elected him or her. The standing committee's acceptance of the resignation shall be approved by a majority of its members. The resolution on acceptance of the resignation shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the next higher level for the record and announcement.  
A deputy to a people's congress at the county level may submit a written resignation to the standing committee of that people's congress, and a deputy to a people's congress at the township level may submit a written resignation to that people's congress. Acceptance of the resignation by the standing committee of a people's congress at the county level shall be approved by a majority of its members. Acceptance of the resignation by a people's congress at the township level shall be approved by a majority of its deputies. Acceptance of the resignation shall be announced.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上的各级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员，县级以上的各级人民代表大会的专门委员会成员，辞去代表职务的请求被接受的，其常务委员会组成人员、专门委员会成员的职务相应终止，由常务委员会予以公告。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席，辞去代表职务的请求被接受的，其主席、副主席的职务相应终止，由主席团予以公告。

**Article 56** Where a member of the standing committee of a people's congress at or above the county level, or a member of a special committee of such a people's congress, submits a request to resign from the post of deputy and the request is accepted, the posts of that member as a member of the standing committee or of the special committee shall terminate accordingly, and the standing committee shall make an announcement thereof.  
Where the chairman or vice-chairman of the people's congress of a township, nationality township, or town submits a request to resign from the post of deputy and the request is accepted, the post of that chairman or vice-chairman shall terminate accordingly, and the presidium shall make an announcement thereof.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　代表在任期内，因故出缺，由原选区或者原选举单位补选。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表在任期内调离或者迁出本行政区域的，其代表资格自行终止，缺额另行补选。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会闭会期间，可以由本级人民代表大会常务委员会补选上一级人民代表大会代表。  
补选出缺的代表时，代表候选人的名额可以多于应选代表的名额，也可以同应选代表的名额相等。补选的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会规定。  
对补选产生的代表，依照[本法第四十七条](#art-47)的规定进行代表资格审查。

**Article 57** If a deputy's post becomes vacant during his or her term of office for any reason, a by-election shall be held in the original electoral district or by the original electoral unit.  
If a deputy to a local people's congress at any level is transferred or moves out of the administrative area during his or her term of office, the post of the deputy shall be terminated automatically, and the vacancy shall be filled through a separate by-election.  
During the recess of a local people's congress at or above the county level, the standing committee of that people's congress may hold a by-election for a deputy to the people's congress at the next higher level.  
In a by-election to fill a vacancy, the number of candidates for deputy may be greater than the number of deputies to be elected, or may equal that number. The specific measures for by-elections shall be prescribed by the standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The qualifications of deputies elected in by-elections shall be examined in accordance with Article 47 of this Law.

### 第十一章　对破坏选举的制裁

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　为保障选民和代表自由行使选举权和被选举权，对有下列行为之一，破坏选举，违反治安管理规定的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）以金钱或者其他财物贿赂选民或者代表，妨害选民和代表自由行使选举权和被选举权的；  
（二）以暴力、威胁、欺骗或者其他非法手段妨害选民和代表自由行使选举权和被选举权的；  
（三）伪造选举文件、虚报选举票数或者有其他违法行为的；  
（四）对于控告、检举选举中违法行为的人，或者对于提出要求罢免代表的人进行压制、报复的。  
国家工作人员有前款所列行为的，还应当由监察机关给予政务处分或者由所在机关、单位给予处分。  
以本条第一款所列违法行为当选的，其当选无效。

**Article 58** To ensure that voters and deputies freely exercise their right to vote and stand for election, where any of the following acts disrupt an election and violate public security administration, the offender shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; where the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Bribing voters or deputies with money or other property to hinder them from freely exercising their right to vote and stand for election;  
(2) Using violence, threats, deception, or other illegal means to hinder voters or deputies from freely exercising their right to vote and stand for election;  
(3) Forging electoral documents, fabricating vote counts, or committing other illegal acts;  
(4) Suppressing or retaliating against persons who accuse or report illegal acts in an election or persons who propose to recall a deputy.  
Where a public official commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the supervisory organ shall also impose a government disciplinary sanction, or the organ or unit to which the official belongs shall impose a sanction.  
Where a person is elected by committing an illegal act listed in paragraph 1 of this Article, the election shall be invalid.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　主持选举的机构发现有破坏选举的行为或者收到对破坏选举行为的举报，应当及时依法调查处理；需要追究法律责任的，及时移送有关机关予以处理。

**Article 59** The agency in charge of election organization that discovers any act disrupting an election or receives any report of such an act shall promptly investigate and handle the matter according to law; where legal liability shall be pursued, the matter shall be promptly transferred to the relevant authority for handling.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会及其常务委员会根据本法可以制定选举实施细则，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 60** The people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and its standing committee may, in accordance with this Law, formulate rules for the implementation of elections, and report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for the record.
