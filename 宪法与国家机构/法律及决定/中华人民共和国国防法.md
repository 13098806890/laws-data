### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建设和巩固国防，保障改革开放和社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，实现中华民族伟大复兴，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of building and consolidating national defense, ensuring the smooth progress of reform and opening up as well as socialist modernization, and realizing the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家为防备和抵抗侵略，制止武装颠覆和分裂，保卫国家主权、统一、领土完整、安全和发展利益所进行的军事活动，以及与军事有关的政治、经济、外交、科技、教育等方面的活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the military activities conducted by the State for the purpose of preventing and resisting aggression, curbing armed subversion and secession, and safeguarding the national sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity, security and development interests, as well as the political, economic, diplomatic, scientific, technological, educational and other activities related to military affairs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国防是国家生存与发展的安全保障。  
国家加强武装力量建设，加强边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域防卫建设，发展国防科研生产，普及全民国防教育，完善国防动员体系，实现国防现代化。

**Article 3** National defense is the security guarantee for the survival and development of the State.  
The State shall strengthen the building of the armed forces, strengthen the construction of border defense, coastal defense, air defense and defense in other major security areas, develop defense scientific research and production, popularize national defense education among all citizens, improve the national defense mobilization system, and realize the modernization of national defense.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国防活动坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，贯彻习近平强军思想，坚持总体国家安全观，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，建设与我国国际地位相称、与国家安全和发展利益相适应的巩固国防和强大武装力量。

**Article 4** National defense activities shall adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, adhere to a holistic approach to national security, implement the military strategic guidelines for the new era, and build a consolidated national defense and strong armed forces commensurate with China's international status and compatible with its national security and development interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对国防活动实行统一的领导。

**Article 5** The State shall exercise unified leadership over national defense activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中华人民共和国奉行防御性国防政策，独立自主、自力更生地建设和巩固国防，实行积极防御，坚持全民国防。  
国家坚持经济建设和国防建设协调、平衡、兼容发展，依法开展国防活动，加快国防和军队现代化，实现富国和强军相统一。

**Article 6** The People's Republic of China pursues a defensive national defense policy, builds and consolidates its national defense independently and self-reliantly, practices active defense, and adheres to the principle of national defense by all the people.  
The State shall ensure coordinated, balanced and compatible development of economic construction and national defense construction, conduct national defense activities according to law, accelerate the modernization of national defense and the armed forces, and achieve the unity of a prosperous country and a strong military.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保卫祖国、抵抗侵略是中华人民共和国每一个公民的神圣职责。  
中华人民共和国公民应当依法履行国防义务。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织和其他组织，都应当支持和依法参与国防建设，履行国防职责，完成国防任务。

**Article 7** It is the sacred duty of every citizen of the People's Republic of China to defend the motherland and resist aggression.  
Citizens of the People's Republic of China shall perform their national defense obligations according to law.  
All State organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations and other organizations shall support and participate in national defense construction according to law, perform national defense duties, and fulfill national defense tasks.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家和社会尊重、优待军人，保障军人的地位和合法权益，开展各种形式的拥军优属活动，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队开展拥政爱民活动，巩固军政军民团结。

**Article 8** The State and society shall respect and give preferential treatment to servicemen, protect their status and legitimate rights and interests, and carry out various forms of activities to support the military and give preferential treatment to families of servicemen and martyrs, so that military service becomes a profession respected by the whole society.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall carry out activities to support the government and cherish the people, and consolidate the unity between the government, the military and the people.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中华人民共和国积极推进国际军事交流与合作，维护世界和平，反对侵略扩张行为。

**Article 9** The People's Republic of China shall actively promote international military exchanges and cooperation, safeguard world peace, and oppose acts of aggression and expansion.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在国防活动中作出贡献的组织和个人，依照有关法律、法规的规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Organizations and individuals that have made contributions to national defense activities shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何组织和个人违反本法和有关法律，拒绝履行国防义务或者危害国防利益的，依法追究法律责任。  
公职人员在国防活动中，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 11** Any organization or individual that violates this Law or relevant laws, refuses to perform national defense obligations or harms national defense interests shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.  
Where public officials, in national defense activities, abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

### 第二章　国家机构的国防职权

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全国人民代表大会依照宪法规定，决定战争和和平的问题，并行使宪法规定的国防方面的其他职权。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会依照宪法规定，决定战争状态的宣布，决定全国总动员或者局部动员，并行使宪法规定的国防方面的其他职权。

**Article 12** The National People's Congress shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution, decide on questions of war and peace, and exercise other powers relating to national defense as stipulated by the Constitution.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution, decide on the proclamation of a state of war, decide on general mobilization or partial mobilization, and exercise other powers relating to national defense as stipulated by the Constitution.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会的决定和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，宣布战争状态，发布动员令，并行使宪法规定的国防方面的其他职权。

**Article 13** The President of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the decisions of the National People's Congress and the decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, proclaim a state of war, issue mobilization orders, and exercise other powers relating to national defense as stipulated by the Constitution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院领导和管理国防建设事业，行使下列职权：  
（一）编制国防建设的有关发展规划和计划；  
（二）制定国防建设方面的有关政策和行政法规；  
（三）领导和管理国防科研生产；  
（四）管理国防经费和国防资产；  
（五）领导和管理国民经济动员工作和人民防空、国防交通等方面的建设和组织实施工作；  
（六）领导和管理拥军优属工作和退役军人保障工作；  
（七）与中央军事委员会共同领导民兵的建设，征兵工作，边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域防卫的管理工作；  
（八）法律规定的与国防建设事业有关的其他职权。

**Article 14** The State Council shall lead and administer the undertakings of national defense construction and exercise the following powers:  
(1) Formulating relevant development plans and programs for national defense construction;  
(2) Formulating relevant policies and administrative regulations for national defense construction;  
(3) Leading and administering national defense scientific research and production;  
(4) Managing national defense funds and national defense assets;  
(5) Leading and administering the work of national economic mobilization, civil air defense, national defense transportation and other related construction and implementation;  
(6) Leading and administering the work of supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to families of servicemen and martyrs, and the work of safeguarding the rights and interests of retired military personnel;  
(7) Jointly leading with the Central Military Commission the building of the militia, conscription work, and the administration of border defense, coastal defense, air defense and defense in other major security areas;  
(8) Other powers relating to national defense construction as provided by law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中央军事委员会领导全国武装力量，行使下列职权：  
（一）统一指挥全国武装力量；  
（二）决定军事战略和武装力量的作战方针；  
（三）领导和管理中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的建设，制定规划、计划并组织实施；  
（四）向全国人民代表大会或者全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出议案；  
（五）根据宪法和法律，制定军事法规，发布决定和命令；  
（六）决定中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的体制和编制，规定中央军事委员会机关部门、战区、军兵种和中国人民武装警察部队等单位的任务和职责；  
（七）依照法律、军事法规的规定，任免、培训、考核和奖惩武装力量成员；  
（八）决定武装力量的武器装备体制，制定武器装备发展规划、计划，协同国务院领导和管理国防科研生产；  
（九）会同国务院管理国防经费和国防资产；  
（十）领导和管理人民武装动员、预备役工作；  
（十一）组织开展国际军事交流与合作；  
（十二）法律规定的其他职权。

**Article 15** The Central Military Commission shall lead the armed forces of the country and exercise the following powers:  
(1) Exercising unified command over the armed forces of the country;  
(2) Deciding on military strategy and operational guidelines for the armed forces;  
(3) Leading and administering the building of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, formulating plans, programs and organizing their implementation;  
(4) Submitting proposals to the National People's Congress or the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress;  
(5) Formulating military regulations, issuing decisions and orders in accordance with the Constitution and laws;  
(6) Deciding on the structure and organization of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, specifying the tasks and responsibilities of the departments of the Central Military Commission, theater commands, services and arms, and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force;  
(7) In accordance with the provisions of laws and military regulations, appointing, removing, training, assessing, rewarding and punishing members of the armed forces;  
(8) Deciding on the weapons and equipment system of the armed forces, formulating development plans and programs for weapons and equipment, and coordinating with the State Council in leading and administering national defense scientific research and production;  
(9) Managing national defense funds and national defense assets jointly with the State Council;  
(10) Leading and administering the work of people's armed forces mobilization and reserve service;  
(11) Organizing and carrying out international military exchanges and cooperation;  
(12) Other powers as provided by law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中央军事委员会实行主席负责制。

**Article 16** The Central Military Commission shall practice a system of chairman responsibility.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院和中央军事委员会建立协调机制，解决国防事务的重大问题。  
中央国家机关与中央军事委员会机关有关部门可以根据情况召开会议，协调解决有关国防事务的问题。

**Article 17** The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall establish a coordination mechanism to resolve major issues in national defense affairs.  
The relevant departments of central State organs and the Central Military Commission may convene meetings as appropriate to coordinate and resolve issues relating to national defense affairs.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地方各级人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会在本行政区域内，保证有关国防事务的法律、法规的遵守和执行。  
地方各级人民政府依照法律规定的权限，管理本行政区域内的征兵、民兵、国民经济动员、人民防空、国防交通、国防设施保护，以及退役军人保障和拥军优属等工作。

**Article 18** The local people's congresses at all levels and the standing committees of the local people's congresses at or above the county level shall, within their respective administrative areas, ensure the observance and implementation of laws and regulations concerning national defense affairs.  
The local people's governments at all levels shall, within the limits of their powers as prescribed by law, administer the work of conscription, militia, national economic mobilization, civil air defense, national defense transportation, protection of national defense installations, as well as safeguarding the rights and interests of retired military personnel, supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to families of servicemen and martyrs within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级人民政府和驻地军事机关根据需要召开军地联席会议，协调解决本行政区域内有关国防事务的问题。  
军地联席会议由地方人民政府的负责人和驻地军事机关的负责人共同召集。军地联席会议的参加人员由会议召集人确定。  
军地联席会议议定的事项，由地方人民政府和驻地军事机关根据各自职责和任务分工办理，重大事项应当分别向上级报告。

**Article 19** The local people's governments at all levels and the military organs stationed in the locality shall, as necessary, convene joint military-civilian meetings to coordinate and resolve issues concerning national defense affairs within their respective administrative areas.  
The joint military-civilian meetings shall be jointly convened by the responsible persons of the local people's governments and the responsible persons of the stationed military organs. The participants of the joint military-civilian meetings shall be determined by the conveners.  
Matters agreed upon at the joint military-civilian meetings shall be handled by the local people's governments and the stationed military organs in accordance with their respective functions, duties and division of tasks. Major matters shall be reported separately to higher authorities.

### 第三章　武装力量

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中华人民共和国的武装力量属于人民。它的任务是巩固国防，抵抗侵略，保卫祖国，保卫人民的和平劳动，参加国家建设事业，全心全意为人民服务。

**Article 20** The armed forces of the People's Republic of China belong to the people. Their tasks are to consolidate national defense, resist aggression, defend the motherland, safeguard the peaceful labor of the people, participate in the national construction undertakings, and serve the people wholeheartedly.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　中华人民共和国的武装力量受中国共产党领导。武装力量中的中国共产党组织依照中国共产党章程进行活动。

**Article 21** The armed forces of the People's Republic of China are under the leadership of the Communist Party of China. The Communist Party of China organizations in the armed forces shall conduct their activities in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中华人民共和国的武装力量，由中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队、民兵组成。  
中国人民解放军由现役部队和预备役部队组成，在新时代的使命任务是为巩固中国共产党领导和社会主义制度，为捍卫国家主权、统一、领土完整，为维护国家海外利益，为促进世界和平与发展，提供战略支撑。现役部队是国家的常备军，主要担负防卫作战任务，按照规定执行非战争军事行动任务。预备役部队按照规定进行军事训练、执行防卫作战任务和非战争军事行动任务；根据国家发布的动员令，由中央军事委员会下达命令转为现役部队。  
中国人民武装警察部队担负执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、海上维权执法、抢险救援和防卫作战以及中央军事委员会赋予的其他任务。  
民兵在军事机关的指挥下，担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务和防卫作战任务。

**Article 22** The armed forces of the People's Republic of China shall be composed of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army shall be composed of active forces and reserve forces. Its mission in the new era is to provide strategic support for consolidating the leadership of the Communist Party of China and the socialist system, safeguarding national sovereignty, unity and territorial integrity, protecting national overseas interests, and promoting world peace and development. The active forces are the standing army of the State, mainly responsible for defense and combat missions, and shall perform non-war military operations as prescribed. The reserve forces shall conduct military training, perform defense and combat missions and non-war military operations as prescribed; upon the mobilization order issued by the State, they shall be converted into active forces by order of the Central Military Commission.  
The Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be responsible for guard duties, handling of unexpected social security incidents, preventing and handling terrorist activities, maritime rights protection and law enforcement, disaster relief and rescue, and defense and combat missions, as well as other tasks assigned by the Central Military Commission.  
The militia, under the command of military organs, shall be responsible for combat readiness services, non-war military operations and defense and combat missions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　中华人民共和国的武装力量必须遵守宪法和法律。

**Article 23** The armed forces of the People's Republic of China must abide by the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　中华人民共和国武装力量建设坚持走中国特色强军之路，坚持政治建军、改革强军、科技强军、人才强军、依法治军，加强军事训练，开展政治工作，提高保障水平，全面推进军事理论、军队组织形态、军事人员和武器装备现代化，构建中国特色现代作战体系，全面提高战斗力，努力实现党在新时代的强军目标。

**Article 24** The building of the armed forces of the People's Republic of China shall adhere to the path of building a strong military with Chinese characteristics, adhere to political building of the military, strengthening the military through reform, through science and technology, through talent cultivation, and through law-based governance, strengthen military training, conduct political work, improve support levels, comprehensively promote the modernization of military theory, organizational form of the armed forces, military personnel and weapons and equipment, build a modern combat system with Chinese characteristics, comprehensively enhance combat effectiveness, and strive to achieve the Party's goal of building a strong military in the new era.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　中华人民共和国武装力量的规模应当与保卫国家主权、安全、发展利益的需要相适应。

**Article 25** The size of the armed forces of the People's Republic of China shall be compatible with the needs of safeguarding national sovereignty, security and development interests.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中华人民共和国的兵役分为现役和预备役。军人和预备役人员的服役制度由法律规定。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队依照法律规定实行衔级制度。

**Article 26** Military service in the People's Republic of China shall be divided into active service and reserve service. The service system for servicemen and reservists shall be prescribed by law.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall implement a rank system in accordance with the provisions of law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队在规定岗位实行文职人员制度。

**Article 27** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall implement a civilian personnel system for designated positions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中国人民解放军军旗、军徽是中国人民解放军的象征和标志。中国人民武装警察部队旗、徽是中国人民武装警察部队的象征和标志。  
公民和组织应当尊重中国人民解放军军旗、军徽和中国人民武装警察部队旗、徽。  
中国人民解放军军旗、军徽和中国人民武装警察部队旗、徽的图案、样式以及使用管理办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 28** The flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Liberation Army are the symbols and marks of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force are the symbols and marks of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.  
Citizens and organizations shall respect the flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.  
The patterns, styles, and measures for the use and management of the flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the flag and emblem of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家禁止任何组织或者个人非法建立武装组织，禁止非法武装活动，禁止冒充军人或者武装力量组织。

**Article 29** The State shall prohibit any organization or individual from illegally establishing armed organizations, prohibit illegal armed activities, and prohibit impersonating a serviceman or an armed force organization.

### 第四章　边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域防卫

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　中华人民共和国的领陆、领水、领空神圣不可侵犯。国家建设强大稳固的现代边防、海防和空防，采取有效的防卫和管理措施，保卫领陆、领水、领空的安全，维护国家海洋权益。  
国家采取必要的措施，维护在太空、电磁、网络空间等其他重大安全领域的活动、资产和其他利益的安全。

**Article 30** The land territory, territorial waters and territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China are inviolable. The State shall build strong and stable modern border defense, coastal defense and air defense, take effective defense and management measures to safeguard the security of the land territory, territorial waters and territorial airspace, and protect the national maritime rights and interests.  
The State shall take necessary measures to safeguard the security of activities, assets and other interests in outer space, electromagnetic, cyberspace and other major security areas.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　中央军事委员会统一领导边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域的防卫工作。  
中央国家机关、地方各级人民政府和有关军事机关，按照规定的职权范围，分工负责边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域的管理和防卫工作，共同维护国家的安全和利益。

**Article 31** The Central Military Commission shall exercise unified leadership over the defense work of border defense, coastal defense, air defense and other major security areas.  
The central State organs, local people's governments at all levels and relevant military organs shall, within their prescribed scope of functions and powers, be responsible for the management and defense of border defense, coastal defense, air defense and other major security areas in accordance with the division of duties, and jointly safeguard the security and interests of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家根据边防、海防、空防和其他重大安全领域防卫的需要，加强防卫力量建设，建设作战、指挥、通信、测控、导航、防护、交通、保障等国防设施。各级人民政府和军事机关应当依照法律、法规的规定，保障国防设施的建设，保护国防设施的安全。

**Article 32** The State shall, in light of the needs of defense in border defense, coastal defense, air defense and other major security areas, strengthen the building of defense forces, and construct national defense facilities for combat, command, communications, measurement and control, navigation, protection, transportation, support, etc. The people's governments at all levels and the military organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, ensure the construction of national defense facilities and protect the safety of national defense facilities.

### 第五章　国防科研生产和军事采购

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立和完善国防科技工业体系，发展国防科研生产，为武装力量提供性能先进、质量可靠、配套完善、便于操作和维修的武器装备以及其他适用的军用物资，满足国防需要。

**Article 33** The State shall establish and improve the national defense science, technology and industry system, develop national defense scientific research and production, and provide the armed forces with weapons and equipment that have advanced performance, reliable quality, complete sets, ease of operation and maintenance, as well as other applicable military supplies, to meet the needs of national defense.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国防科技工业实行军民结合、平战结合、军品优先、创新驱动、自主可控的方针。  
国家统筹规划国防科技工业建设，坚持国家主导、分工协作、专业配套、开放融合，保持规模适度、布局合理的国防科研生产能力。

**Article 34** The national defense science, technology and industry shall implement the policy of combining military with civilian purposes, combining peacetime with wartime, prioritizing military products, being driven by innovation, and being independently controllable.  
The State shall make overall plans for the construction of the national defense science, technology and industry, adhere to the principles of State leadership, division of labor and cooperation, professional matching, open integration, and maintain a national defense scientific research and production capacity with appropriate scale and reasonable distribution.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家充分利用全社会优势资源，促进国防科学技术进步，加快技术自主研发，发挥高新技术在武器装备发展中的先导作用，增加技术储备，完善国防知识产权制度，促进国防科技成果转化，推进科技资源共享和协同创新，提高国防科研能力和武器装备技术水平。

**Article 35** The State shall make full use of the superior resources of the whole society, promote the progress of national defense science and technology, accelerate independent research and development of technologies, give play to the leading role of high and new technologies in the development of weapons and equipment, increase technology reserves, improve the national defense intellectual property system, promote the transformation of national defense scientific and technological achievements, advance the sharing of scientific and technological resources and collaborative innovation, and enhance national defense scientific research capabilities and the level of weapons and equipment technology.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家创造有利的环境和条件，加强国防科学技术人才培养，鼓励和吸引优秀人才进入国防科研生产领域，激发人才创新活力。  
国防科学技术工作者应当受到全社会的尊重。国家逐步提高国防科学技术工作者的待遇，保护其合法权益。

**Article 36** The State shall create a favorable environment and conditions, strengthen the cultivation of national defense scientific and technological talents, encourage and attract outstanding talents to enter the field of national defense scientific research and production, and stimulate the innovation vitality of talents.  
National defense scientific and technological workers shall be respected by the whole society. The State shall gradually raise the treatment of national defense scientific and technological workers and protect their legitimate rights and interests.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家依法实行军事采购制度，保障武装力量所需武器装备和物资、工程、服务的采购供应。

**Article 37** The State shall implement a military procurement system according to law to ensure the procurement and supply of weapons, equipment, materials, engineering and services required by the armed forces.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家对国防科研生产实行统一领导和计划调控；注重发挥市场机制作用，推进国防科研生产和军事采购活动公平竞争。  
国家为承担国防科研生产任务和接受军事采购的组织和个人依法提供必要的保障条件和优惠政策。地方各级人民政府应当依法对承担国防科研生产任务和接受军事采购的组织和个人给予协助和支持。  
承担国防科研生产任务和接受军事采购的组织和个人应当保守秘密，及时高效完成任务，保证质量，提供相应的服务保障。  
国家对供应武装力量的武器装备和物资、工程、服务，依法实行质量责任追究制度。

**Article 38** The State shall exercise unified leadership and planned regulation over national defense scientific research and production; pay attention to the role of market mechanisms, and promote fair competition in national defense scientific research, production and military procurement activities.  
The State shall provide necessary guarantee conditions and preferential policies according to law for organizations and individuals undertaking national defense scientific research and production tasks and accepting military procurement. The local people's governments at all levels shall, according to law, provide assistance and support to organizations and individuals undertaking national defense scientific research and production tasks and accepting military procurement.  
Organizations and individuals undertaking national defense scientific research and production tasks and accepting military procurement shall keep secrets, complete tasks in a timely and efficient manner, ensure quality, and provide corresponding service guarantees.  
The State shall implement a quality responsibility investigation system according to law for the weapons, equipment, materials, engineering and services supplied to the armed forces.

### 第六章　国防经费和国防资产

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家保障国防事业的必要经费。国防经费的增长应当与国防需求和国民经济发展水平相适应。  
国防经费依法实行预算管理。

**Article 39** The State shall guarantee the necessary funds for national defense undertakings. The increase of national defense expenditure shall be compatible with national defense needs and the level of national economic development.  
National defense expenditure shall be managed through budgets according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家为武装力量建设、国防科研生产和其他国防建设直接投入的资金、划拨使用的土地等资源，以及由此形成的用于国防目的的武器装备和设备设施、物资器材、技术成果等属于国防资产。  
国防资产属于国家所有。

**Article 40** The funds directly invested by the State in the building of the armed forces, national defense scientific research and production, and other national defense construction, the land and other resources allocated for use, and the weapons and equipment, equipment and facilities, materials and equipment, technological achievements, etc., formed therefrom and used for national defense purposes, shall constitute national defense assets.  
National defense assets shall be owned by the State.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家根据国防建设和经济建设的需要，确定国防资产的规模、结构和布局，调整和处分国防资产。  
国防资产的管理机构和占有、使用单位，应当依法管理国防资产，充分发挥国防资产的效能。

**Article 41** The State shall, in accordance with the needs of national defense construction and economic construction, determine the scale, structure and distribution of national defense assets, and adjust and dispose of national defense assets.  
The administrative organs of national defense assets and the units that possess and use them shall manage national defense assets according to law and give full play to the effectiveness of national defense assets.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家保护国防资产不受侵害，保障国防资产的安全、完整和有效。  
禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、损害和侵占国防资产。未经国务院、中央军事委员会或者国务院、中央军事委员会授权的机构批准，国防资产的占有、使用单位不得改变国防资产用于国防的目的。国防资产中的技术成果，在坚持国防优先、确保安全的前提下，可以根据国家有关规定用于其他用途。  
国防资产的管理机构或者占有、使用单位对不再用于国防目的的国防资产，应当按照规定报批，依法改作其他用途或者进行处置。

**Article 42** The State shall protect national defense assets from infringement and ensure the safety, integrity and effectiveness of national defense assets.  
No organization or individual may destroy, damage or misappropriate national defense assets. Without the approval of the State Council, the Central Military Commission, or an institution authorized by the State Council or the Central Military Commission, units that possess or use national defense assets shall not change the use of national defense assets for national defense purposes. The technological achievements in national defense assets may, under the premise of adhering to the priority of national defense and ensuring security, be used for other purposes in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The administrative organ of national defense assets or the unit that possesses or uses them shall, for national defense assets that are no longer used for national defense purposes, submit a report for approval as prescribed, and convert them to other uses or dispose of them according to law.

### 第七章　国防教育

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家通过开展国防教育，使全体公民增强国防观念、强化忧患意识、掌握国防知识、提高国防技能、发扬爱国主义精神，依法履行国防义务。  
普及和加强国防教育是全社会的共同责任。

**Article 43** The State, through carrying out national defense education, shall enable all citizens to enhance their awareness of national defense, strengthen their sense of urgency, master national defense knowledge, improve national defense skills, carry forward the spirit of patriotism, and perform national defense obligations according to law.  
Disseminating and strengthening national defense education is the common responsibility of the whole society.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国防教育贯彻全民参与、长期坚持、讲求实效的方针，实行经常教育与集中教育相结合、普及教育与重点教育相结合、理论教育与行为教育相结合的原则。

**Article 44** National defense education shall implement the policy of participation by all the people, long-term persistence, and emphasis on practical results, and shall follow the principles of combining regular education with concentrated education, combining popular education with key education, and combining theoretical education with behavioral education.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国防教育主管部门应当加强国防教育的组织管理，其他有关部门应当按照规定的职责做好国防教育工作。  
军事机关应当支持有关机关和组织开展国防教育工作，依法提供有关便利条件。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织和其他组织，都应当组织本地区、本部门、本单位开展国防教育。  
学校的国防教育是全民国防教育的基础。各级各类学校应当设置适当的国防教育课程，或者在有关课程中增加国防教育的内容。普通高等学校和高中阶段学校应当按照规定组织学生军事训练。  
公职人员应当积极参加国防教育，提升国防素养，发挥在全民国防教育中的模范带头作用。

**Article 45** The competent department of national defense education shall strengthen the organization and management of national defense education, and other relevant departments shall do a good job in national defense education in accordance with their prescribed duties.  
The military organs shall support relevant organs and organizations in carrying out national defense education work and provide relevant conveniences according to law.  
All State organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations and other organizations shall organize the carrying out of national defense education in their respective regions, departments and units.  
School national defense education shall be the foundation of national defense education for all citizens. Schools at all levels and of all types shall set up appropriate national defense education courses, or add content of national defense education in relevant courses. Regular institutions of higher learning and senior high school stage schools shall organize military training for students as prescribed.  
Public officials shall actively participate in national defense education, enhance their national defense literacy, and play an exemplary and leading role in national defense education for all citizens.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民政府应当将国防教育纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，保障国防教育所需的经费。

**Article 46** The people's governments at all levels shall incorporate national defense education into the national economic and social development plans and guarantee the funds required for national defense education.

### 第八章　国防动员和战争状态

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　中华人民共和国的主权、统一、领土完整、安全和发展利益遭受威胁时，国家依照宪法和法律规定，进行全国总动员或者局部动员。

**Article 47** When the sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity, security and development interests of the People's Republic of China are threatened, the State shall, in accordance with the Constitution and laws, carry out general mobilization or partial mobilization.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家将国防动员准备纳入国家总体发展规划和计划，完善国防动员体制，增强国防动员潜力，提高国防动员能力。

**Article 48** The State shall incorporate the preparation for national defense mobilization into the overall national development plans and programs, improve the national defense mobilization system, enhance the potential for national defense mobilization, and increase the capacity for national defense mobilization.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家建立战略物资储备制度。战略物资储备应当规模适度、储存安全、调用方便、定期更换，保障战时的需要。

**Article 49** The State shall establish a system of strategic material reserves. Strategic material reserves shall have an appropriate scale, safe storage, convenient transfer, and regular replacement, to meet the needs in wartime.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家国防动员领导机构、中央国家机关、中央军事委员会机关有关部门按照职责分工，组织国防动员准备和实施工作。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织、其他组织和公民，都必须依照法律规定完成国防动员准备工作；在国家发布动员令后，必须完成规定的国防动员任务。

**Article 50** The national defense mobilization leading body of the State, the central State organs, and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize the preparation and implementation of national defense mobilization.  
All State organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, other organizations and citizens must complete the preparation for national defense mobilization in accordance with the provisions of law; after the State issues a mobilization order, they must complete the prescribed tasks of national defense mobilization.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家根据国防动员需要，可以依法征收、征用组织和个人的设备设施、交通工具、场所和其他财产。  
县级以上人民政府对被征收、征用者因征收、征用所造成的直接经济损失，按照国家有关规定给予公平、合理的补偿。

**Article 51** The State may, according to the needs of national defense mobilization, expropriate or requisition the equipment, facilities, means of transportation, premises and other property of organizations and individuals in accordance with law.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall provide fair and reasonable compensation to the expropriated or requisitioned persons for the direct economic losses caused by expropriation or requisition in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家依照宪法规定宣布战争状态，采取各种措施集中人力、物力和财力，领导全体公民保卫祖国、抵抗侵略。

**Article 52** The State, in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution, shall declare a state of war, take various measures to concentrate human, material and financial resources, and lead all citizens to defend the motherland and resist aggression.

### 第九章　公民、组织的国防义务和权利

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　依照法律服兵役和参加民兵组织是中华人民共和国公民的光荣义务。  
各级兵役机关和基层人民武装机构应当依法办理兵役工作，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的命令完成征兵任务，保证兵员质量。有关国家机关、人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织和其他组织，应当依法完成民兵和预备役工作，协助完成征兵任务。

**Article 53** It is the glorious duty of citizens of the People's Republic of China to perform military service and join the militia organization in accordance with law.  
The military service organs at all levels and grassroots people's armed forces organs shall handle military service work in accordance with law, complete the recruitment tasks in accordance with the orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and ensure the quality of recruits. The relevant State organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations and other organizations shall complete the militia and reserve service work in accordance with law, and assist in completing recruitment tasks.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　企业事业组织和个人承担国防科研生产任务或者接受军事采购，应当按照要求提供符合质量标准的武器装备或者物资、工程、服务。  
企业事业组织和个人应当按照国家规定在与国防密切相关的建设项目中贯彻国防要求，依法保障国防建设和军事行动的需要。车站、港口、机场、道路等交通设施的管理、运营单位应当为军人和军用车辆、船舶的通行提供优先服务，按照规定给予优待。

**Article 54** Where enterprises, public institutions and individuals undertake national defense scientific research and production tasks or accept military procurement, they shall provide weapons, equipment or materials, projects, and services that meet the quality standards as required.  
Enterprises, public institutions and individuals shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, implement national defense requirements in construction projects closely related to national defense, and safeguard the needs of national defense construction and military operations in accordance with law. The management and operation units of transportation facilities such as stations, ports, airports, and roads shall provide priority services for the passage of military personnel and military vehicles and vessels, and grant preferential treatment in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　公民应当接受国防教育。  
公民和组织应当保护国防设施，不得破坏、危害国防设施。  
公民和组织应当遵守保密规定，不得泄露国防方面的国家秘密，不得非法持有国防方面的秘密文件、资料和其他秘密物品。

**Article 55** Citizens shall receive national defense education.  
Citizens and organizations shall protect national defense facilities, and shall not damage or endanger national defense facilities.  
Citizens and organizations shall comply with confidentiality provisions, and shall not divulge State secrets concerning national defense, and shall not illegally hold secret documents, materials and other secret items concerning national defense.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　公民和组织应当支持国防建设，为武装力量的军事训练、战备勤务、防卫作战、非战争军事行动等活动提供便利条件或者其他协助。  
国家鼓励和支持符合条件的公民和企业投资国防事业，保障投资者的合法权益并依法给予政策优惠。

**Article 56** Citizens and organizations shall support national defense construction, and provide facilitation or other assistance for activities such as military training, combat readiness services, defensive operations, and non-war military operations of the armed forces.  
The State encourages and supports eligible citizens and enterprises to invest in national defense undertakings, protects the lawful rights and interests of investors, and grants policy preferences in accordance with law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　公民和组织有对国防建设提出建议的权利，有对危害国防利益的行为进行制止或者检举的权利。

**Article 57** Citizens and organizations have the right to make suggestions on national defense construction, and have the right to stop or report acts that harm national defense interests.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　民兵、预备役人员和其他公民依法参加军事训练，担负战备勤务、防卫作战、非战争军事行动等任务时，应当履行自己的职责和义务；国家和社会保障其享有相应的待遇，按照有关规定对其实行抚恤优待。  
公民和组织因国防建设和军事活动在经济上受到直接损失的，可以依照国家有关规定获得补偿。

**Article 58** When militiamen, reservists and other citizens participate in military training, assume tasks such as combat readiness services, defensive operations, and non-war military operations in accordance with law, they shall perform their duties and obligations; the State and society shall ensure that they enjoy corresponding treatment, and shall grant them pensions and preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Citizens and organizations that suffer direct economic losses due to national defense construction and military activities may receive compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第十章　军人的义务和权益

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　军人必须忠于祖国，忠于中国共产党，履行职责，英勇战斗，不怕牺牲，捍卫祖国的安全、荣誉和利益。

**Article 59** Servicemen must be loyal to the motherland, loyal to the Communist Party of China, perform their duties, fight bravely, fear no sacrifice, and defend the security, honor and interests of the motherland.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　军人必须模范地遵守宪法和法律，遵守军事法规，执行命令，严守纪律。

**Article 60** Servicemen must scrupulously abide by the Constitution and laws, observe military regulations, execute orders, and strictly observe discipline.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　军人应当发扬人民军队的优良传统，热爱人民，保护人民，积极参加社会主义现代化建设，完成抢险救灾等任务。

**Article 61** Servicemen shall carry forward the fine traditions of the people's army, love the people, protect the people, actively participate in socialist modernization, and accomplish tasks such as emergency rescue and disaster relief.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　军人应当受到全社会的尊崇。  
国家建立军人功勋荣誉表彰制度。  
国家采取有效措施保护军人的荣誉、人格尊严，依照法律规定对军人的婚姻实行特别保护。  
军人依法履行职责的行为受法律保护。

**Article 62** Servicemen shall be held in high esteem by the whole society.  
The State shall establish a system of commendation for military merits and honors.  
The State shall take effective measures to protect the honor and personal dignity of servicemen, and shall provide special protection for the marriage of servicemen in accordance with the provisions of law.  
The acts of servicemen in performing their duties in accordance with law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家和社会优待军人。  
国家建立与军事职业相适应、与国民经济发展相协调的军人待遇保障制度。

**Article 63** The State and society shall give preferential treatment to servicemen.  
The State shall establish a system for guaranteeing the treatment of servicemen that is compatible with the military profession and coordinated with the development of the national economy.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家建立退役军人保障制度，妥善安置退役军人，维护退役军人的合法权益。

**Article 64** The State shall establish a guarantee system for retired servicemen, properly place them, and protect their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家和社会抚恤优待残疾军人，对残疾军人的生活和医疗依法给予特别保障。  
因战、因公致残或者致病的残疾军人退出现役后，县级以上人民政府应当及时接收安置，并保障其生活不低于当地的平均生活水平。

**Article 65** The State and society shall grant pensions and preferential treatment to disabled servicemen, and shall provide special guarantees for the living and medical care of disabled servicemen in accordance with law.  
After disabled servicemen who become disabled or ill due to war or duty retire from active service, the people's government at or above the county level shall promptly accept and place them, and shall ensure that their living standard is not lower than the average living standard of the locality.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家和社会优待军人家属，抚恤优待烈士家属和因公牺牲、病故军人的家属。

**Article 66** The State and society shall give preferential treatment to the family members of servicemen, and grant pensions and preferential treatment to the family members of martyrs and the family members of servicemen who died in the line of duty or of illness.

### 第十一章　对外军事关系

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　中华人民共和国坚持互相尊重主权和领土完整、互不侵犯、互不干涉内政、平等互利、和平共处五项原则，维护以联合国为核心的国际体系和以国际法为基础的国际秩序，坚持共同、综合、合作、可持续的安全观，推动构建人类命运共同体，独立自主地处理对外军事关系，开展军事交流与合作。

**Article 67** The People's Republic of China adheres to the five principles of mutual respect for sovereignty and territorial integrity, mutual non-aggression, non-interference in each other's internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence; upholds the international system with the United Nations at its core and the international order based on international law; adheres to a concept of common, comprehensive, cooperative and sustainable security; promotes the building of a community with a shared future for mankind; and independently handles foreign military relations and conducts military exchanges and cooperation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　中华人民共和国遵循以联合国宪章宗旨和原则为基础的国际关系基本准则，依照国家有关法律运用武装力量，保护海外中国公民、组织、机构和设施的安全，参加联合国维和、国际救援、海上护航、联演联训、打击恐怖主义等活动，履行国际安全义务，维护国家海外利益。

**Article 68** The People's Republic of China follows the basic norms of international relations based on the purposes and principles of the Charter of the United Nations, uses armed forces in accordance with the relevant laws of the State to protect the safety of overseas Chinese citizens, organizations, institutions and facilities, participates in activities such as United Nations peacekeeping, international rescue, maritime escort, joint exercises and training, and combating terrorism, fulfills international security obligations, and safeguards the overseas interests of the State.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　中华人民共和国支持国际社会实施的有利于维护世界和地区和平、安全、稳定的与军事有关的活动，支持国际社会为公正合理地解决国际争端以及国际军备控制、裁军和防扩散所做的努力，参与安全领域多边对话谈判，推动制定普遍接受、公正合理的国际规则。

**Article 69** The People's Republic of China supports military-related activities carried out by the international community that are conducive to maintaining world and regional peace, security and stability; supports the efforts of the international community to justly and reasonably resolve international disputes and to pursue international arms control, disarmament and non-proliferation; participates in multilateral dialogues and negotiations in the security field; and promotes the formulation of universally accepted, just and reasonable international rules.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　中华人民共和国在对外军事关系中遵守同外国、国际组织缔结或者参加的有关条约和协定。

**Article 70** The People's Republic of China, in its foreign military relations, shall comply with the relevant treaties and agreements concluded or acceded to with foreign countries and international organizations.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本法所称军人，是指在中国人民解放军服现役的军官、军士、义务兵等人员。  
本法关于军人的规定，适用于人民武装警察。

**Article 71** For the purposes of this Law, "servicemen" means officers, non-commissioned officers, conscripts and other personnel serving in the Chinese People's Liberation Army.  
The provisions of this Law concerning servicemen shall apply to the People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　中华人民共和国特别行政区的防务，由特别行政区基本法和有关法律规定。

**Article 72** The defense of the special administrative regions of the People's Republic of China shall be provided for by the basic laws of the special administrative regions and relevant laws.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本法自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 73** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
