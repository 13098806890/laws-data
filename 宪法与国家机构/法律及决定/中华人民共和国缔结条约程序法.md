# 中华人民共和国缔结条约程序法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1990-12-28  
**生效日期**：1990-12-28

> （1990年12月28日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十七次会议通过） 第一条　根据中华人民共和国宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国同外国缔结的双边和多边条约、协定和其他具有条约、协定性质的文件。 第三条　中华人民共和国国务院，即中央人民政府，同外国缔结条约和协定。 中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定同外国缔结的条约和重要协定的批准和废除。 中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，批准和废除同外国缔结的条约和重要协定。 中华人民共和国外交部在国务院领导下管理同外国缔结条约和协定的具体事务。 第四条　中华人民共和国以下列名义同外国缔结条约和协定： （一）中华人民共和国； （二）中华人民共和国政府； （三）中华人民共和国政府部门。 第五条　谈判和签署条约、协定的决定程序如下： （一）以中华人民共和国名义谈判和签署条约、协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，报请国务院审核决定； （二）以中华人民共和国政府名义谈判和签署条约、协定，由外交部提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，或者由国务院有关部门提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，同外交部会商后，报请国务院审核决定。属于具体业务事项的协定，经国务院同意，协定的中方草案由国务院有关部门审核决定，必要时同外交部会商； （三）以中华人民共和国政府部门名义谈判和签署属于本部门职权范围内事项的协定，由本部门决定或者本部门同外交部会商后决定；涉及重大问题或者涉及国务院其他有关部门职权范围的，由本部门或者本部门同国务院其他有关部门会商后，报请国务院决定。协定的中方草案由本部门审核决定，必要时同外交部会商。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据中华人民共和国宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国同外国缔结的双边和多边条约、协定和其他具有条约、协定性质的文件。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to bilateral and multilateral treaties, agreements and other documents of a treaty or agreement nature concluded by the People's Republic of China with foreign states.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国国务院，即中央人民政府，同外国缔结条约和协定。  
中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定同外国缔结的条约和重要协定的批准和废除。  
中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，批准和废除同外国缔结的条约和重要协定。  
中华人民共和国外交部在国务院领导下管理同外国缔结条约和协定的具体事务。

**Article 3** The State Council of the People's Republic of China, that is, the Central People's Government, shall conclude treaties and agreements with foreign states.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress of the People's Republic of China shall decide on the ratification and denunciation of treaties and important agreements concluded with foreign states.  
The President of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, ratify and denounce treaties and important agreements concluded with foreign states.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China shall, under the leadership of the State Council, manage the specific affairs concerning the conclusion of treaties and agreements with foreign states.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国以下列名义同外国缔结条约和协定：  
（一）中华人民共和国；  
（二）中华人民共和国政府；  
（三）中华人民共和国政府部门。

**Article 4** The People's Republic of China shall conclude treaties and agreements with foreign states in the following names:  
(1) the People's Republic of China;  
(2) the Government of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) the government departments of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　谈判和签署条约、协定的决定程序如下：  
（一）以中华人民共和国名义谈判和签署条约、协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，报请国务院审核决定；  
（二）以中华人民共和国政府名义谈判和签署条约、协定，由外交部提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，或者由国务院有关部门提出建议并拟订条约、协定的中方草案，同外交部会商后，报请国务院审核决定。属于具体业务事项的协定，经国务院同意，协定的中方草案由国务院有关部门审核决定，必要时同外交部会商；  
（三）以中华人民共和国政府部门名义谈判和签署属于本部门职权范围内事项的协定，由本部门决定或者本部门同外交部会商后决定；涉及重大问题或者涉及国务院其他有关部门职权范围的，由本部门或者本部门同国务院其他有关部门会商后，报请国务院决定。协定的中方草案由本部门审核决定，必要时同外交部会商。  
经国务院审核决定的条约、协定的中方草案，经谈判需要作重要改动的，重新报请国务院审核决定。

**Article 5** The decision-making procedures for negotiating and signing treaties and agreements shall be as follows:  
(1) For negotiating and signing treaties and agreements in the name of the People's Republic of China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall make proposals and draft the Chinese texts of the treaties and agreements, and shall submit them to the State Council for examination and decision;  
(2) For negotiating and signing treaties and agreements in the name of the Government of the People's Republic of China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall make proposals and draft the Chinese texts, or the relevant department of the State Council shall make proposals and draft the Chinese texts, and after consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, shall submit them to the State Council for examination and decision. For agreements concerning specific operational matters, with the consent of the State Council, the Chinese texts of the agreements shall be examined and decided by the relevant department of the State Council, and, where necessary, after consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(3) For negotiating and signing agreements falling within the scope of functions and powers of a government department in the name of that government department, the decision shall be made by the said department or after consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; where major issues or the functions and powers of other relevant departments of the State Council are involved, the matter shall be reported to the State Council for decision after consultation between the said department or the said department and other relevant departments of the State Council. The Chinese texts of the agreements shall be examined and decided by the said department, and, where necessary, after consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.  
Where the Chinese texts of treaties and agreements examined and decided by the State Council need to be substantially altered during negotiations, they shall be re-submitted to the State Council for examination and decision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　谈判和签署条约、协定的代表按照下列程序委派：  
（一）以中华人民共和国名义或者中华人民共和国政府名义缔结条约、协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门报请国务院委派代表。代表的全权证书由国务院总理签署，也可以由外交部长签署；  
（二）以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结协定，由部门首长委派代表。代表的授权证书由部门首长签署。部门首长签署以本部门名义缔结的协定，各方约定出具全权证书的，全权证书由国务院总理签署，也可以由外交部长签署。  
下列人员谈判、签署条约、协定，无须出具全权证书：  
（一）国务院总理、外交部长；  
（二）谈判、签署与驻在国缔结条约、协定的中华人民共和国驻该国使馆馆长，但是各方另有约定的除外；  
（三）谈判、签署以本部门名义缔结协定的中华人民共和国政府部门首长，但是各方另有约定的除外；  
（四）中华人民共和国派往国际会议或者派驻国际组织，并在该会议或者该组织内参加条约、协定谈判的代表，但是该会议另有约定或者该组织章程另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Representatives for negotiating and signing treaties and agreements shall be appointed in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) For concluding treaties and agreements in the name of the People's Republic of China or the Government of the People's Republic of China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council shall report to the State Council for the appointment of representatives. The full powers of the representatives shall be signed by the Premier of the State Council or, alternatively, by the Minister of Foreign Affairs;  
(2) For concluding agreements in the name of a government department of the People's Republic of China, the head of that department shall appoint the representatives. The authorization certificates of the representatives shall be signed by the head of that department. Where the head of the department signs an agreement concluded in the name of the department, and if the parties agree that full powers are required, the full powers shall be signed by the Premier of the State Council or, alternatively, by the Minister of Foreign Affairs.  
The following persons shall not be required to produce full powers for negotiating and signing treaties and agreements:  
(1) the Premier of the State Council and the Minister of Foreign Affairs;  
(2) the head of the diplomatic mission of the People's Republic of China accredited to a state, for negotiating and signing treaties and agreements with that state, unless otherwise agreed by the parties;  
(3) the head of a government department of the People's Republic of China, for negotiating and signing agreements concluded in the name of the said department, unless otherwise agreed by the parties;  
(4) the representative of the People's Republic of China sent to an international conference or accredited to an international organization, for participating in the negotiation of treaties and agreements within that conference or organization, unless otherwise provided by the conference or the constitution of the organization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　条约和重要协定的批准由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。  
前款规定的条约和重要协定是指：  
（一）友好合作条约、和平条约等政治性条约；  
（二）有关领土和划定边界的条约、协定；  
（三）有关司法协助、引渡的条约、协定；  
（四）同中华人民共和国法律有不同规定的条约、协定；  
（五）缔约各方议定须经批准的条约、协定；  
（六）其他须经批准的条约、协定。  
条约和重要协定签署后，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部，报请国务院审核；由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准；中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定予以批准。  
双边条约和重要协定经批准后，由外交部办理与缔约另一方互换批准书的手续；多边条约和重要协定经批准后，由外交部办理向条约、协定的保存国或者国际组织交存批准书的手续。批准书由中华人民共和国主席签署，外交部长副署。

**Article 7** The ratification of treaties and important agreements shall be decided by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
Treaties and important agreements as referred to in the preceding paragraph mean:  
(1) political treaties such as treaties of friendship and cooperation and peace treaties;  
(2) treaties and agreements concerning territory and delimitation of boundaries;  
(3) treaties and agreements concerning judicial assistance and extradition;  
(4) treaties and agreements which contain provisions inconsistent with the laws of the People's Republic of China;  
(5) treaties and agreements which the parties have agreed require ratification;  
(6) other treaties and agreements which require ratification.  
After a treaty or important agreement is signed, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall report it to the State Council for examination; the State Council shall submit it to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for a decision on ratification; the President of the People's Republic of China shall ratify it in accordance with the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
After a bilateral treaty or important agreement is ratified, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall handle the procedures for exchanging the instruments of ratification with the other contracting party; after a multilateral treaty or important agreement is ratified, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall handle the procedures for depositing the instrument of ratification with the depositary state or international organization. The instrument of ratification shall be signed by the President of the People's Republic of China and countersigned by the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[本法第七条](#art-7)第二款所列范围以外的国务院规定须经核准或者缔约各方议定须经核准的协定和其他具有条约性质的文件签署后，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部，报请国务院核准。  
协定和其他具有条约性质的文件经核准后，属于双边的，由外交部办理与缔约另一方互换核准书或者以外交照会方式相互通知业已核准的手续；属于多边的，由外交部办理向有关保存国或者国际组织交存核准书的手续。核准书由国务院总理签署，也可以由外交部长签署。

**Article 8** After the signing of agreements and other documents of a treaty nature which fall outside the scope specified in Article 7, paragraph 2 of this Law and which require approval under regulations of the State Council or by agreement among the parties, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall submit them to the State Council for approval.  
After an agreement or other document of a treaty nature is approved, if it is bilateral, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall handle the procedures for exchanging the instruments of approval or mutually notifying the approval by diplomatic notes; if it is multilateral, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall handle the procedures for depositing the instrument of approval with the relevant depositary state or international organization. The instrument of approval shall be signed by the Premier of the State Council or, alternatively, by the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　无须全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者国务院核准的协定签署后，除以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结的协定由本部门送外交部登记外，其他协定由国务院有关部门报国务院备案。

**Article 9** After the signing of agreements which do not require a decision on ratification by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or approval by the State Council, except for agreements concluded in the name of a government department of the People's Republic of China which shall be sent by the said department to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for registration, other agreements shall be reported by the relevant department of the State Council to the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　缔约双方为使同一条约、协定生效需要履行的国内法律程序不同的，该条约、协定于缔约双方完成各自法律程序并以外交照会方式相互通知后生效。  
前款所列条约、协定签署后，应当区别情况依照[本法第七条](#art-7)、第八条、第九条的规定办理批准、核准、备案或者登记手续。通知照会的手续由外交部办理。

**Article 10** Where the domestic legal procedures required by the contracting parties for bringing the same treaty or agreement into force are different, the treaty or agreement shall take effect after the parties have completed their respective legal procedures and have mutually notified each other by diplomatic notes.  
After the signing of the treaties or agreements referred to in the preceding paragraph, the procedures for ratification, approval, record-keeping or registration shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Articles 7, 8 and 9 of this Law, as the case may be. The procedures for notification by diplomatic notes shall be handled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　加入多边条约和协定，分别由全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者国务院决定。  
加入多边条约和协定的程序如下：  
（一）加入属于[本法第七条](#art-7)第二款所列范围的多边条约和重要协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部审查后，提出建议，报请国务院审核；由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会作出加入的决定。加入书由外交部长签署，具体手续由外交部办理；  
（二）加入不属于[本法第七条](#art-7)第二款所列范围的多边条约、协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部审查后，提出建议，报请国务院作出加入的决定。加入书由外交部长签署，具体手续由外交部办理。

**Article 11** The accession to multilateral treaties and agreements shall be decided by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the State Council, respectively.  
The procedures for accession to multilateral treaties and agreements shall be as follows:  
(1) For accession to multilateral treaties and important agreements falling within the scope specified in Article 7, paragraph 2 of this Law, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, after examination, make proposals and submit them to the State Council for examination; the State Council shall submit them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for a decision on accession. The instrument of accession shall be signed by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, and the specific formalities shall be handled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(2) For accession to multilateral treaties and agreements not falling within the scope specified in Article 7, paragraph 2 of this Law, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, after examination, make proposals and submit them to the State Council for a decision on accession. The instrument of accession shall be signed by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, and the specific formalities shall be handled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　接受多边条约和协定，由国务院决定。  
经中国代表签署的或者无须签署的载有接受条款的多边条约、协定，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部审查后，提出建议，报请国务院作出接受的决定。接受书由外交部长签署，具体手续由外交部办理。

**Article 12** The acceptance of multilateral treaties and agreements shall be decided by the State Council.  
For multilateral treaties and agreements containing acceptance clauses which have been signed by Chinese representatives or do not require signature, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council together with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, after examination, make proposals and submit them to the State Council for a decision on acceptance. The instrument of acceptance shall be signed by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, and the specific formalities shall be handled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国同外国缔结的双边条约、协定，以中文和缔约另一方的官方文字写成，两种文本同等作准；必要时，可以附加使用缔约双方同意的一种第三国文字，作为同等作准的第三种正式文本或者作为起参考作用的非正式文本；经缔约双方同意，也可以规定对条约、协定的解释发生分歧时，以该第三种文本为准。  
某些属于具体业务事项的协定，以及同国际组织缔结的条约、协定，经缔约双方同意或者依照有关国际组织章程的规定，也可以只使用国际上较通用的一种文字。

**Article 13** Bilateral treaties and agreements concluded by the People's Republic of China with foreign states shall be written in the Chinese language and the official language of the other contracting party, both texts being equally authentic. When necessary, a third language agreed upon by the parties may be used as an additional equally authentic third official text or as a non-official text for reference. With the consent of the parties, it may also be provided that, in case of divergence in interpretation of the treaty or agreement, the third text shall prevail.  
For certain agreements concerning specific operational matters and treaties and agreements concluded with international organizations, with the consent of the parties or in accordance with the provisions of the constitution of the relevant international organization, only one commonly used international language may be used.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　以中华人民共和国或者中华人民共和国政府名义缔结的双边条约、协定的签字正本，以及经条约、协定的保存国或者国际组织核证无误的多边条约、协定的副本，由外交部保存；以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结的双边协定的签字正本，由本部门保存。

**Article 14** The signed originals of bilateral treaties and agreements concluded in the name of the People's Republic of China or the Government of the People's Republic of China, and the certified true copies of multilateral treaties and agreements certified by the depositary state or international organization, shall be kept by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; the signed originals of bilateral agreements concluded in the name of a government department of the People's Republic of China shall be kept by the said department.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　经全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入的条约和重要协定，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会公报公布。其他条约、协定的公布办法由国务院规定。

**Article 15** Treaties and important agreements which have been decided on ratification or accession by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall be published in the Gazette of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. The measures for the publication of other treaties and agreements shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中华人民共和国缔结的条约和协定由外交部编入《中华人民共和国条约集》。

**Article 16** Treaties and agreements concluded by the People's Republic of China shall be compiled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs into the "Collection of Treaties of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中华人民共和国缔结的条约和协定由外交部按照联合国宪章的有关规定向联合国秘书处登记。  
中华人民共和国缔结的条约和协定需要向其他国际组织登记的，由外交部或者国务院有关部门按照各该国际组织章程的规定办理。

**Article 17** Treaties and agreements concluded by the People's Republic of China shall be registered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with the Secretariat of the United Nations in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Charter of the United Nations.  
Treaties and agreements concluded by the People's Republic of China which require registration with other international organizations shall be handled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the constitutions of such international organizations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　中华人民共和国同国际组织缔结条约和协定的程序，依照本法及有关国际组织章程的规定办理。

**Article 18** The procedures for concluding treaties and agreements between the People's Republic of China and international organizations shall be governed by the provisions of this Law and the relevant constitutions of the international organizations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中华人民共和国缔结的条约和协定的修改、废除或者退出的程序，比照各该条约、协定的缔结的程序办理。

**Article 19** The procedures for the amendment, denunciation or withdrawal from treaties and agreements concluded by the People's Republic of China shall follow the procedures for concluding the said treaties and agreements.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院可以根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 20** The State Council may, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementing regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 21** This Law shall come into force from the date of its promulgation.
