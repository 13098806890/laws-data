# 全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于在沿海港口城市设立海事法院的决定

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1984-11-14  
**生效日期**：1984-11-14

> （1984年11月14日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第八次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、根据需要在沿海一定的港口城市设立海事法院。  
海事法院的设置或者变更、撤销，由最高人民法院决定。  
海事法院的审判机构和办事机构的设置，由最高人民法院规定。

**Article 1** I. A maritime court shall be established in certain coastal port cities as needed. The establishment, alteration or abolition of a maritime court shall be decided by the Supreme People's Court. The setup of the adjudication organs and administrative offices of a maritime court shall be prescribed by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、海事法院对所在地的市人民代表大会常务委员会负责。  
海事法院的审判工作受所在地的高级人民法院监督。

**Article 2** II. A maritime court shall be responsible to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of the city where it is located. The adjudication work of a maritime court shall be subject to the supervision of the Higher People's Court of the place where it is located.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、海事法院管辖第一审海事案件和海商案件，不受理刑事案件和其他民事案件。  
各海事法院管辖区域的划分，由最高人民法院规定。  
对海事法院的判决和裁定的上诉案件由海事法院所在地的高级人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** III. A maritime court shall have jurisdiction over maritime cases and maritime trade cases of first instance, and shall not accept criminal cases or other civil cases. The division of the jurisdictional areas of each maritime court shall be prescribed by the Supreme People's Court. Cases on appeal against judgments and rulings of a maritime court shall be under the jurisdiction of the Higher People's Court of the place where the maritime court is located.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、海事法院院长由所在地的市人民代表大会常务委员会主任提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
海事法院副院长、庭长、副庭长、审判员和审判委员会委员，由海事法院院长提请所在地的市人民代表大会常务委员会任免。

**Article 4** IV. The president of a maritime court shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of the city where it is located upon the proposal of the chairman of such Standing Committee. The vice presidents, division chiefs, deputy division chiefs, judges, and members of the adjudication committee of a maritime court shall be appointed or removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of the city where it is located upon the proposal of the president of the maritime court.
