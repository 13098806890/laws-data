# 中华人民共和国中医药法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2016-12-25  
**生效日期**：2017-07-01

> （2016年12月25日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　中医药服务 第三章　中药保护与发展 第四章　中医药人才培养 第五章　中医药科学研究 第六章　中医药传承与文化传播 第七章　保障措施 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了继承和弘扬中医药，保障和促进中医药事业发展，保护人民健康，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称中医药，是包括汉族和少数民族医药在内的我国各民族医药的统称，是反映中华民族对生命、健康和疾病的认识，具有悠久历史传统和独特理论及技术方法的医药学体系。 第三条　中医药事业是我国医药卫生事业的重要组成部分。国家大力发展中医药事业，实行中西医并重的方针，建立符合中医药特点的管理制度，充分发挥中医药在我国医药卫生事业中的作用。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了继承和弘扬中医药，保障和促进中医药事业发展，保护人民健康，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of inheriting and promoting traditional Chinese medicine, ensuring and advancing the development of traditional Chinese medicine, and protecting people's health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称中医药，是包括汉族和少数民族医药在内的我国各民族医药的统称，是反映中华民族对生命、健康和疾病的认识，具有悠久历史传统和独特理论及技术方法的医药学体系。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) refers to the collective term for the medicine of all ethnic groups in China, including the medicine of the Han and minority ethnic groups, and is a medical system that reflects the Chinese nation's understanding of life, health, and disease, with a long historical tradition and unique theories and technical methods.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中医药事业是我国医药卫生事业的重要组成部分。国家大力发展中医药事业，实行中西医并重的方针，建立符合中医药特点的管理制度，充分发挥中医药在我国医药卫生事业中的作用。  
发展中医药事业应当遵循中医药发展规律，坚持继承和创新相结合，保持和发挥中医药特色和优势，运用现代科学技术，促进中医药理论和实践的发展。  
国家鼓励中医西医相互学习，相互补充，协调发展，发挥各自优势，促进中西医结合。

**Article 3** The traditional Chinese medicine cause is an important component of the medical and health cause in China. The State shall vigorously develop the traditional Chinese medicine cause, implement a policy of equal emphasis on traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, establish a management system that conforms to the characteristics of traditional Chinese medicine, and give full play to the role of traditional Chinese medicine in the medical and health cause in China.  
  
The development of the traditional Chinese medicine cause shall follow the laws of traditional Chinese medicine development, adhere to the combination of inheritance and innovation, maintain and give play to the characteristics and advantages of traditional Chinese medicine, utilize modern science and technology, and promote the development of traditional Chinese medicine theory and practice.  
  
The State shall encourage mutual learning, mutual complementarity, and coordinated development between traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, give play to their respective advantages, and promote the integration of traditional Chinese and Western medicine.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将中医药事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建立健全中医药管理体系，统筹推进中医药事业发展。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the traditional Chinese medicine cause into their national economic and social development plans, establish and improve the traditional Chinese medicine management system, and coordinate the promotion of the development of the traditional Chinese medicine cause.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院中医药主管部门负责全国的中医药管理工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责与中医药管理有关的工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府中医药主管部门负责本行政区域的中医药管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责与中医药管理有关的工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide management of traditional Chinese medicine. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for work related to the management of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
The competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management of traditional Chinese medicine within its administrative region. Other relevant departments under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for work related to the management of traditional Chinese medicine.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家加强中医药服务体系建设，合理规划和配置中医药服务资源，为公民获得中医药服务提供保障。  
国家支持社会力量投资中医药事业，支持组织和个人捐赠、资助中医药事业。

**Article 6** The State shall strengthen the construction of a traditional Chinese medicine service system, rationally plan and allocate traditional Chinese medicine service resources, and provide guarantees for citizens to obtain traditional Chinese medicine services.  
  
The State shall support social forces in investing in the traditional Chinese medicine cause, and support organizations and individuals in donating to and funding the traditional Chinese medicine cause.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家发展中医药教育，建立适应中医药事业发展需要、规模适宜、结构合理、形式多样的中医药教育体系，培养中医药人才。

**Article 7** The State shall develop traditional Chinese medicine education, establish a traditional Chinese medicine education system that meets the needs of the development of the traditional Chinese medicine cause, with an appropriate scale, reasonable structure, and diverse forms, and cultivate traditional Chinese medicine talents.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家支持中医药科学研究和技术开发，鼓励中医药科学技术创新，推广应用中医药科学技术成果，保护中医药知识产权，提高中医药科学技术水平。

**Article 8** The State shall support scientific research and technological development in traditional Chinese medicine, encourage scientific and technological innovation in traditional Chinese medicine, promote the application of scientific and technological achievements in traditional Chinese medicine, protect the intellectual property of traditional Chinese medicine, and enhance the level of science and technology in traditional Chinese medicine.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持中医药对外交流与合作，促进中医药的国际传播和应用。

**Article 9** The State shall support international exchanges and cooperation in traditional Chinese medicine, and promote the international dissemination and application of traditional Chinese medicine.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在中医药事业中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 10** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the traditional Chinese medicine cause shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　中医药服务

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府应当将中医医疗机构建设纳入医疗机构设置规划，举办规模适宜的中医医疗机构，扶持有中医药特色和优势的医疗机构发展。  
合并、撤销政府举办的中医医疗机构或者改变其中医医疗性质，应当征求上一级人民政府中医药主管部门的意见。

**Article 11** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions into the plan for the establishment of medical institutions, establish traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions of appropriate scale, and support the development of medical institutions with traditional Chinese medicine characteristics and advantages.  
  
Where a traditional Chinese medicine medical institution established by the government is merged, dissolved, or its nature as a traditional Chinese medicine medical institution is changed, the opinions of the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the people's government at the next higher level shall be solicited.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　政府举办的综合医院、妇幼保健机构和有条件的专科医院、社区卫生服务中心、乡镇卫生院，应当设置中医药科室。  
县级以上人民政府应当采取措施，增强社区卫生服务站和村卫生室提供中医药服务的能力。

**Article 12** General hospitals, maternal and child health care institutions, and qualified specialized hospitals, community health service centers, and township health centers established by the government shall set up departments of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
People's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to enhance the ability of community health service stations and village health rooms to provide traditional Chinese medicine services.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家支持社会力量举办中医医疗机构。  
社会力量举办的中医医疗机构在准入、执业、基本医疗保险、科研教学、医务人员职称评定等方面享有与政府举办的中医医疗机构同等的权利。

**Article 13** The State shall support social forces in establishing traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions.  
  
Traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions established by social forces shall enjoy equal rights with traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions established by the government in terms of access, practice, basic medical insurance, scientific research and teaching, and professional title evaluation of medical personnel.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　举办中医医疗机构应当按照国家有关医疗机构管理的规定办理审批手续，并遵守医疗机构管理的有关规定。  
举办中医诊所的，将诊所的名称、地址、诊疗范围、人员配备情况等报所在地县级人民政府中医药主管部门备案后即可开展执业活动。中医诊所应当将本诊所的诊疗范围、中医医师的姓名及其执业范围在诊所的明显位置公示，不得超出备案范围开展医疗活动。具体办法由国务院中医药主管部门拟订，报国务院卫生行政部门审核、发布。

**Article 14** The establishment of a traditional Chinese medicine medical institution shall go through the approval procedures in accordance with the provisions of the State on the management of medical institutions, and shall comply with the relevant provisions on the management of medical institutions.  
  
For the establishment of a traditional Chinese medicine clinic, upon reporting the name, address, scope of diagnosis and treatment, personnel allocation, etc. of the clinic to the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the local people's government at the county level for recordation, the clinic may commence practice activities. The traditional Chinese medicine clinic shall publish the scope of diagnosis and treatment of the clinic, the names of the traditional Chinese medicine physicians, and their scope of practice in a conspicuous location of the clinic, and shall not carry out medical activities beyond the recorded scope. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council, and submitted to the administrative department of health under the State Council for examination and issuance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　从事中医医疗活动的人员应当依照《中华人民共和国执业医师法》的规定，通过中医医师资格考试取得中医医师资格，并进行执业注册。中医医师资格考试的内容应当体现中医药特点。  
以师承方式学习中医或者经多年实践，医术确有专长的人员，由至少两名中医医师推荐，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府中医药主管部门组织实践技能和效果考核合格后，即可取得中医医师资格；按照考核内容进行执业注册后，即可在注册的执业范围内，以个人开业的方式或者在医疗机构内从事中医医疗活动。国务院中医药主管部门应当根据中医药技术方法的安全风险拟订本款规定人员的分类考核办法，报国务院卫生行政部门审核、发布。

**Article 15** Persons engaged in traditional Chinese medicine medical activities shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Licensed Doctors, pass the traditional Chinese medicine physician qualification examination to obtain traditional Chinese medicine physician qualifications, and shall undergo practice registration. The content of the traditional Chinese medicine physician qualification examination shall reflect the characteristics of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
For persons who have studied traditional Chinese medicine through apprenticeship or have many years of practice and have proven skills, upon recommendation by at least two traditional Chinese medicine physicians, and after passing the practical skills and effectiveness assessment organized by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, they may obtain traditional Chinese medicine physician qualifications; after undergoing practice registration in accordance with the assessment content, they may engage in traditional Chinese medicine medical activities by opening a practice individually or within a medical institution within the scope of practice registered. The competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council shall, based on the safety risks of traditional Chinese medicine technical methods, formulate a classification assessment method for the persons specified in this paragraph, and submit it to the administrative department of health under the State Council for examination and issuance.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中医医疗机构配备医务人员应当以中医药专业技术人员为主，主要提供中医药服务；经考试取得医师资格的中医医师按照国家有关规定，经培训、考核合格后，可以在执业活动中采用与其专业相关的现代科学技术方法。在医疗活动中采用现代科学技术方法的，应当有利于保持和发挥中医药特色和优势。  
社区卫生服务中心、乡镇卫生院、社区卫生服务站以及有条件的村卫生室应当合理配备中医药专业技术人员，并运用和推广适宜的中医药技术方法。

**Article 16** A traditional Chinese medicine medical institution shall allocate medical personnel mainly with traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians, and mainly provide traditional Chinese medicine services; a traditional Chinese medicine physician who has obtained physician qualifications through examination may, after training and passing assessment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, adopt modern scientific and technological methods related to his or her specialty in practice activities. Where modern scientific and technological methods are adopted in medical activities, it shall be conducive to maintaining and giving play to the characteristics and advantages of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
Community health service centers, township health centers, community health service stations, and qualified village health rooms shall reasonably allocate traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians, and apply and promote appropriate traditional Chinese medicine technical methods.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　开展中医药服务，应当以中医药理论为指导，运用中医药技术方法，并符合国务院中医药主管部门制定的中医药服务基本要求。

**Article 17** The provision of traditional Chinese medicine services shall be guided by the theory of traditional Chinese medicine, apply traditional Chinese medicine technical methods, and comply with the basic requirements for traditional Chinese medicine services formulated by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当发展中医药预防、保健服务，并按照国家有关规定将其纳入基本公共卫生服务项目统筹实施。  
县级以上人民政府应当发挥中医药在突发公共卫生事件应急工作中的作用，加强中医药应急物资、设备、设施、技术与人才资源储备。  
医疗卫生机构应当在疾病预防与控制中积极运用中医药理论和技术方法。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level shall develop traditional Chinese medicine preventive and health care services, and incorporate them into basic public health service projects for coordinated implementation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
  
People's governments at or above the county level shall give play to the role of traditional Chinese medicine in emergency response to public health emergencies, and strengthen the reserve of traditional Chinese medicine emergency materials, equipment, facilities, technology, and human resources.  
  
Medical and health institutions shall actively apply traditional Chinese medicine theories and technical methods in disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　医疗机构发布中医医疗广告，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府中医药主管部门审查批准；未经审查批准，不得发布。发布的中医医疗广告内容应当与经审查批准的内容相符合，并符合《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定。

**Article 19** A medical institution that publishes a traditional Chinese medicine medical advertisement shall obtain examination and approval from the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located; without examination and approval, it shall not publish such advertisement. The content of the published traditional Chinese medicine medical advertisement shall be consistent with the content that has been examined and approved, and shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府中医药主管部门应当加强对中医药服务的监督检查，并将下列事项作为监督检查的重点：  
（一）中医医疗机构、中医医师是否超出规定的范围开展医疗活动；  
（二）开展中医药服务是否符合国务院中医药主管部门制定的中医药服务基本要求；  
（三）中医医疗广告发布行为是否符合本法的规定。  
中医药主管部门依法开展监督检查，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝或者阻挠。

**Article 20** The competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of traditional Chinese medicine services, and shall take the following matters as the focus of supervision and inspection:  
(1) Whether a traditional Chinese medicine medical institution or a traditional Chinese medicine physician carries out medical activities beyond the prescribed scope;  
(2) Whether the provision of traditional Chinese medicine services complies with the basic requirements for traditional Chinese medicine services formulated by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council;  
(3) Whether the act of publishing traditional Chinese medicine medical advertisements complies with the provisions of this Law.  
When the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine conducts supervision and inspection according to law, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

### 第三章　中药保护与发展

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家制定中药材种植养殖、采集、贮存和初加工的技术规范、标准，加强对中药材生产流通全过程的质量监督管理，保障中药材质量安全。

**Article 21** The State shall formulate technical specifications and standards for the planting, breeding, collection, storage, and primary processing of Chinese medicinal materials, strengthen the quality supervision and management of the entire process of production and circulation of Chinese medicinal materials, and ensure the quality and safety of Chinese medicinal materials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励发展中药材规范化种植养殖，严格管理农药、肥料等农业投入品的使用，禁止在中药材种植过程中使用剧毒、高毒农药，支持中药材良种繁育，提高中药材质量。

**Article 22** The State shall encourage the development of standardized planting and breeding of Chinese medicinal materials, strictly manage the use of pesticides, fertilizers, and other agricultural inputs, prohibit the use of highly toxic and high-toxicity pesticides in the planting of Chinese medicinal materials, support the breeding of improved varieties of Chinese medicinal materials, and improve the quality of Chinese medicinal materials.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家建立道地中药材评价体系，支持道地中药材品种选育，扶持道地中药材生产基地建设，加强道地中药材生产基地生态环境保护，鼓励采取地理标志产品保护等措施保护道地中药材。  
前款所称道地中药材，是指经过中医临床长期应用优选出来的，产在特定地域，与其他地区所产同种中药材相比，品质和疗效更好，且质量稳定，具有较高知名度的中药材。

**Article 23** The State shall establish an evaluation system for authentic Chinese medicinal materials, support the selection and breeding of authentic Chinese medicinal material varieties, support the construction of production bases for authentic Chinese medicinal materials, strengthen the ecological environmental protection of production bases for authentic Chinese medicinal materials, and encourage the adoption of measures such as geographical indication product protection to protect authentic Chinese medicinal materials.  
  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, 'authentic Chinese medicinal materials' refers to Chinese medicinal materials that have been selected through long-term clinical application of traditional Chinese medicine, produced in specific regions, and compared with the same species of Chinese medicinal materials produced in other regions, have better quality and efficacy, stable quality, and relatively high visibility.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院药品监督管理部门应当组织并加强对中药材质量的监测，定期向社会公布监测结果。国务院有关部门应当协助做好中药材质量监测有关工作。  
采集、贮存中药材以及对中药材进行初加工，应当符合国家有关技术规范、标准和管理规定。  
国家鼓励发展中药材现代流通体系，提高中药材包装、仓储等技术水平，建立中药材流通追溯体系。药品生产企业购进中药材应当建立进货查验记录制度。中药材经营者应当建立进货查验和购销记录制度，并标明中药材产地。

**Article 24** The drug regulatory department under the State Council shall organize and strengthen the monitoring of the quality of Chinese medicinal materials, and regularly publish the monitoring results to the public. The relevant departments under the State Council shall assist in the work related to the quality monitoring of Chinese medicinal materials.  
  
The collection, storage, and primary processing of Chinese medicinal materials shall comply with the relevant national technical specifications, standards, and management provisions.  
  
The State shall encourage the development of a modern circulation system for Chinese medicinal materials, improve the technical level of packaging, warehousing, etc. of Chinese medicinal materials, and establish a traceability system for the circulation of Chinese medicinal materials. A drug manufacturer that purchases Chinese medicinal materials shall establish a system for inspection and recording upon purchase. An operator of Chinese medicinal materials shall establish a system for inspection and recording of purchases and sales, and shall indicate the place of origin of the Chinese medicinal materials.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家保护药用野生动植物资源，对药用野生动植物资源实行动态监测和定期普查，建立药用野生动植物资源种质基因库，鼓励发展人工种植养殖，支持依法开展珍贵、濒危药用野生动植物的保护、繁育及其相关研究。

**Article 25** The State shall protect wildlife resources used for medicine, carry out dynamic monitoring and regular surveys of wildlife resources used for medicine, establish a germplasm gene bank for wildlife resources used for medicine, encourage the development of artificial planting and breeding, and support the protection, breeding, and related research of precious and endangered wildlife used for medicine in accordance with law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在村医疗机构执业的中医医师、具备中药材知识和识别能力的乡村医生，按照国家有关规定可以自种、自采地产中药材并在其执业活动中使用。

**Article 26** A traditional Chinese medicine physician practicing in a village medical institution, or a village doctor who possesses knowledge of Chinese medicinal materials and the ability to identify them, may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, self-plant and self-harvest locally produced Chinese medicinal materials and use them in his or her practice activities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家保护中药饮片传统炮制技术和工艺，支持应用传统工艺炮制中药饮片，鼓励运用现代科学技术开展中药饮片炮制技术研究。

**Article 27** The State shall protect the traditional processing techniques and processes for Chinese medicinal decoction pieces, support the use of traditional processes to process Chinese medicinal decoction pieces, and encourage the use of modern science and technology to carry out research on the processing technology of Chinese medicinal decoction pieces.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对市场上没有供应的中药饮片，医疗机构可以根据本医疗机构医师处方的需要，在本医疗机构内炮制、使用。医疗机构应当遵守中药饮片炮制的有关规定，对其炮制的中药饮片的质量负责，保证药品安全。医疗机构炮制中药饮片，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。  
根据临床用药需要，医疗机构可以凭本医疗机构医师的处方对中药饮片进行再加工。

**Article 28** For Chinese medicinal decoction pieces that are not available on the market, a medical institution may process and use them within the medical institution according to the needs of the prescriptions of physicians of the medical institution. The medical institution shall comply with the relevant provisions on the processing of Chinese medicinal decoction pieces, be responsible for the quality of the Chinese medicinal decoction pieces it processes, and ensure drug safety. When a medical institution processes Chinese medicinal decoction pieces, it shall file a record with the drug regulatory department under the people's government at the city with districts level where it is located.  
  
According to the needs of clinical medication, a medical institution may reprocess Chinese medicinal decoction pieces with the prescriptions of physicians of the medical institution.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家鼓励和支持中药新药的研制和生产。  
国家保护传统中药加工技术和工艺，支持传统剂型中成药的生产，鼓励运用现代科学技术研究开发传统中成药。

**Article 29** The State shall encourage and support the research and development and production of new traditional Chinese medicine drugs.  
  
The State shall protect traditional traditional Chinese medicine processing technologies and processes, support the production of traditional dosage forms of Chinese patent medicines, and encourage the use of modern science and technology to research and develop traditional Chinese patent medicines.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　生产符合国家规定条件的来源于古代经典名方的中药复方制剂，在申请药品批准文号时，可以仅提供非临床安全性研究资料。具体管理办法由国务院药品监督管理部门会同中医药主管部门制定。  
前款所称古代经典名方，是指至今仍广泛应用、疗效确切、具有明显特色与优势的古代中医典籍所记载的方剂。具体目录由国务院中医药主管部门会同药品监督管理部门制定。

**Article 30** For the production of a compound preparation of traditional Chinese medicine derived from ancient classical famous prescriptions that meets the conditions prescribed by the State, when applying for the drug approval number, only non-clinical safety research materials may be provided. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council jointly with the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, 'ancient classical famous prescriptions' refer to prescriptions recorded in ancient traditional Chinese medicine classics that are still widely used, have definite curative effects, and have obvious characteristics and advantages. The specific list shall be formulated by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council jointly with the drug regulatory department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励医疗机构根据本医疗机构临床用药需要配制和使用中药制剂，支持应用传统工艺配制中药制剂，支持以中药制剂为基础研制中药新药。  
医疗机构配制中药制剂，应当依照《中华人民共和国药品管理法》的规定取得医疗机构制剂许可证，或者委托取得药品生产许可证的药品生产企业、取得医疗机构制剂许可证的其他医疗机构配制中药制剂。委托配制中药制剂，应当向委托方所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。  
医疗机构对其配制的中药制剂的质量负责；委托配制中药制剂的，委托方和受托方对所配制的中药制剂的质量分别承担相应责任。

**Article 31** The State shall encourage medical institutions to prepare and use traditional Chinese medicine preparations according to the clinical medication needs of the medical institutions, support the use of traditional processes to prepare traditional Chinese medicine preparations, and support the research and development of new traditional Chinese medicine drugs based on traditional Chinese medicine preparations.  
  
A medical institution that prepares traditional Chinese medicine preparations shall obtain a medical institution preparation license in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, or entrust a drug manufacturer that has obtained a drug manufacturing license or another medical institution that has obtained a medical institution preparation license to prepare traditional Chinese medicine preparations. Where the preparation of traditional Chinese medicine preparations is entrusted, a record shall be filed with the drug regulatory department under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the entrusting party is located.  
  
A medical institution shall be responsible for the quality of the traditional Chinese medicine preparations it prepares; where the preparation of traditional Chinese medicine preparations is entrusted, the entrusting party and the entrusted party shall respectively bear corresponding responsibilities for the quality of the prepared traditional Chinese medicine preparations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医疗机构配制的中药制剂品种，应当依法取得制剂批准文号。但是，仅应用传统工艺配制的中药制剂品种，向医疗机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案后即可配制，不需要取得制剂批准文号。  
医疗机构应当加强对备案的中药制剂品种的不良反应监测，并按照国家有关规定进行报告。药品监督管理部门应当加强对备案的中药制剂品种配制、使用的监督检查。

**Article 32** The varieties of traditional Chinese medicine preparations prepared by a medical institution shall obtain preparation approval numbers according to law. However, for varieties of traditional Chinese medicine preparations prepared only by using traditional processes, they may be prepared after filing with the drug regulatory department under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the medical institution is located, without the need to obtain preparation approval numbers.  
  
A medical institution shall strengthen the adverse reaction monitoring of the filed varieties of traditional Chinese medicine preparations, and report in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The drug regulatory department shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the preparation and use of the filed varieties of traditional Chinese medicine preparations.

### 第四章　中医药人才培养

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中医药教育应当遵循中医药人才成长规律，以中医药内容为主，体现中医药文化特色，注重中医药经典理论和中医药临床实践、现代教育方式和传统教育方式相结合。

**Article 33** Traditional Chinese medicine education shall follow the laws of talent development in traditional Chinese medicine, primarily focus on traditional Chinese medicine content, reflect the cultural characteristics of traditional Chinese medicine, and emphasize the combination of traditional Chinese medicine classical theories and clinical practice, as well as modern educational methods and traditional educational methods.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家完善中医药学校教育体系，支持专门实施中医药教育的高等学校、中等职业学校和其他教育机构的发展。  
中医药学校教育的培养目标、修业年限、教学形式、教学内容、教学评价及学术水平评价标准等，应当体现中医药学科特色，符合中医药学科发展规律。

**Article 34** The State shall improve the school education system for traditional Chinese medicine, and support the development of higher education institutions, secondary vocational schools, and other educational institutions that specifically implement traditional Chinese medicine education.  
  
The training objectives, duration of study, teaching forms, teaching content, teaching evaluation, and academic level evaluation standards of traditional Chinese medicine school education shall reflect the characteristics of the traditional Chinese medicine discipline and conform to the laws of development of the traditional Chinese medicine discipline.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家发展中医药师承教育，支持有丰富临床经验和技术专长的中医医师、中药专业技术人员在执业、业务活动中带徒授业，传授中医药理论和技术方法，培养中医药专业技术人员。

**Article 35** The State shall develop apprenticeship education in traditional Chinese medicine, support traditional Chinese medicine physicians and traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians with rich clinical experience and technical expertise to take on apprentices and teach in their practice and professional activities, imparting traditional Chinese medicine theories and technical methods, and cultivate traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家加强对中医医师和城乡基层中医药专业技术人员的培养和培训。  
国家发展中西医结合教育，培养高层次的中西医结合人才。

**Article 36** The State shall strengthen the cultivation and training of traditional Chinese medicine physicians and traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians at the urban and rural grassroots level.  
  
The State shall develop integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine education, and cultivate high-level talents in integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上地方人民政府中医药主管部门应当组织开展中医药继续教育，加强对医务人员，特别是城乡基层医务人员中医药基本知识和技能的培训。  
中医药专业技术人员应当按照规定参加继续教育，所在机构应当为其接受继续教育创造条件。

**Article 37** The competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize and carry out continuing education in traditional Chinese medicine, strengthen the training of medical personnel, especially medical personnel at the urban and rural grassroots level, on basic knowledge and skills of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
Traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians shall participate in continuing education as prescribed, and the institutions where they work shall create conditions for them to receive continuing education.

### 第五章　中医药科学研究

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家鼓励科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构和药品生产企业等，运用现代科学技术和传统中医药研究方法，开展中医药科学研究，加强中西医结合研究，促进中医药理论和技术方法的继承和创新。

**Article 38** The State shall encourage scientific research institutions, higher education institutions, medical institutions, and drug manufacturers, etc., to use modern science and technology and traditional Chinese medicine research methods to carry out scientific research in traditional Chinese medicine, strengthen research on the integration of traditional Chinese and Western medicine, and promote the inheritance and innovation of traditional Chinese medicine theories and technical methods.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家采取措施支持对中医药古籍文献、著名中医药专家的学术思想和诊疗经验以及民间中医药技术方法的整理、研究和利用。  
国家鼓励组织和个人捐献有科学研究和临床应用价值的中医药文献、秘方、验方、诊疗方法和技术。

**Article 39** The State shall take measures to support the collation, research, and utilization of ancient traditional Chinese medicine books and documents, the academic thoughts and diagnosis and treatment experience of famous traditional Chinese medicine experts, and folk traditional Chinese medicine technical methods.  
  
The State shall encourage organizations and individuals to donate traditional Chinese medicine documents, secret prescriptions, proven prescriptions, diagnosis and treatment methods, and technologies that have scientific research and clinical application value.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家建立和完善符合中医药特点的科学技术创新体系、评价体系和管理体制，推动中医药科学技术进步与创新。

**Article 40** The State shall establish and improve a scientific and technological innovation system, evaluation system, and management system that conform to the characteristics of traditional Chinese medicine, and promote the progress and innovation of traditional Chinese medicine science and technology.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家采取措施，加强对中医药基础理论和辨证论治方法，常见病、多发病、慢性病和重大疑难疾病、重大传染病的中医药防治，以及其他对中医药理论和实践发展有重大促进作用的项目的科学研究。

**Article 41** The State shall take measures to strengthen scientific research on the basic theories and methods of syndrome differentiation and treatment of traditional Chinese medicine, the prevention and treatment by traditional Chinese medicine of common diseases, frequently occurring diseases, chronic diseases, major and difficult diseases, and major infectious diseases, as well as other projects that have a significant promoting effect on the development of traditional Chinese medicine theory and practice.

### 第六章　中医药传承与文化传播

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对具有重要学术价值的中医药理论和技术方法，省级以上人民政府中医药主管部门应当组织遴选本行政区域内的中医药学术传承项目和传承人，并为传承活动提供必要的条件。传承人应当开展传承活动，培养后继人才，收集整理并妥善保存相关的学术资料。属于非物质文化遗产代表性项目的，依照《中华人民共和国非物质文化遗产法》的有关规定开展传承活动。

**Article 42** For traditional Chinese medicine theories and technical methods with important academic value, the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the people's government at or above the provincial level shall organize the selection of traditional Chinese medicine academic inheritance projects and inheritors within their administrative regions, and provide necessary conditions for inheritance activities. The inheritors shall carry out inheritance activities, cultivate successors, collect, collate, and properly preserve relevant academic materials. Where a project is a representative item of intangible cultural heritage, the inheritance activities shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Intangible Cultural Heritage Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立中医药传统知识保护数据库、保护名录和保护制度。  
中医药传统知识持有人对其持有的中医药传统知识享有传承使用的权利，对他人获取、利用其持有的中医药传统知识享有知情同意和利益分享等权利。  
国家对经依法认定属于国家秘密的传统中药处方组成和生产工艺实行特殊保护。

**Article 43** The State shall establish a database, a protection list, and a protection system for the protection of traditional knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine.  
  
Holders of traditional knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine shall enjoy the right to inherit and use the traditional knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine they hold, and shall enjoy rights such as informed consent and benefit-sharing regarding others' acquisition and utilization of the traditional knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine they hold.  
  
The State shall implement special protection for the composition and production processes of traditional Chinese medicine prescriptions that have been determined to be State secrets according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家发展中医养生保健服务，支持社会力量举办规范的中医养生保健机构。中医养生保健服务规范、标准由国务院中医药主管部门制定。

**Article 44** The State shall develop traditional Chinese medicine health preservation and health care services, and support social forces in establishing standardized traditional Chinese medicine health preservation and health care institutions. The specifications and standards for traditional Chinese medicine health preservation and health care services shall be formulated by the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine under the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强中医药文化宣传，普及中医药知识，鼓励组织和个人创作中医药文化和科普作品。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the promotion of traditional Chinese medicine culture, popularize knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine, and encourage organizations and individuals to create cultural and popular science works on traditional Chinese medicine.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　开展中医药文化宣传和知识普及活动，应当遵守国家有关规定。任何组织或者个人不得对中医药作虚假、夸大宣传，不得冒用中医药名义牟取不正当利益。  
广播、电视、报刊、互联网等媒体开展中医药知识宣传，应当聘请中医药专业技术人员进行。

**Article 46** The conduct of activities for the promotion of traditional Chinese medicine culture and the popularization of knowledge shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State. No organization or individual shall make false or exaggerated publicity about traditional Chinese medicine, or use the name of traditional Chinese medicine to seek illegitimate benefits.  
  
When radio, television, newspapers, the Internet, and other media carry out publicity of traditional Chinese medicine knowledge, they shall engage traditional Chinese medicine professionals and technicians to do so.

### 第七章　保障措施

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府应当为中医药事业发展提供政策支持和条件保障，将中医药事业发展经费纳入本级财政预算。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门制定基本医疗保险支付政策、药物政策等医药卫生政策，应当有中医药主管部门参加，注重发挥中医药的优势，支持提供和利用中医药服务。

**Article 47** People's governments at or above the county level shall provide policy support and conditional guarantees for the development of the traditional Chinese medicine cause, and incorporate the funds for the development of the traditional Chinese medicine cause into the financial budgets at the corresponding level.  
  
When people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments formulate medical and health policies such as basic medical insurance payment policies and drug policies, the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine shall participate, attention shall be paid to giving play to the advantages of traditional Chinese medicine, and support shall be provided for the provision and utilization of traditional Chinese medicine services.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当按照法定价格管理权限，合理确定中医医疗服务的收费项目和标准，体现中医医疗服务成本和专业技术价值。

**Article 48** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the statutory price management authority, reasonably determine the charging items and standards for traditional Chinese medicine medical services, reflecting the cost and professional technical value of traditional Chinese medicine medical services.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当按照国家规定，将符合条件的中医医疗机构纳入基本医疗保险定点医疗机构范围，将符合条件的中医诊疗项目、中药饮片、中成药和医疗机构中药制剂纳入基本医疗保险基金支付范围。

**Article 49** The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, include qualified traditional Chinese medicine medical institutions within the scope of designated medical institutions for basic medical insurance, and include qualified traditional Chinese medicine diagnosis and treatment items, Chinese medicinal decoction pieces, Chinese patent medicines, and traditional Chinese medicine preparations of medical institutions within the scope of payment by the basic medical insurance fund.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家加强中医药标准体系建设，根据中医药特点对需要统一的技术要求制定标准并及时修订。  
中医药国家标准、行业标准由国务院有关部门依据职责制定或者修订，并在其网站上公布，供公众免费查阅。  
国家推动建立中医药国际标准体系。

**Article 50** The State shall strengthen the construction of a standard system for traditional Chinese medicine, formulate standards for technical requirements that need to be unified according to the characteristics of traditional Chinese medicine, and revise them in a timely manner.  
  
National standards and industry standards for traditional Chinese medicine shall be formulated or revised by the relevant departments under the State Council according to their functions and duties, and published on their websites for free public access.  
  
The State shall promote the establishment of an international standard system for traditional Chinese medicine.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　开展法律、行政法规规定的与中医药有关的评审、评估、鉴定活动，应当成立中医药评审、评估、鉴定的专门组织，或者有中医药专家参加。

**Article 51** For carrying out review, assessment, and expert evaluation activities related to traditional Chinese medicine as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, a specialized organization for traditional Chinese medicine review, assessment, and expert evaluation shall be established, or experts in traditional Chinese medicine shall participate.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家采取措施，加大对少数民族医药传承创新、应用发展和人才培养的扶持力度，加强少数民族医疗机构和医师队伍建设，促进和规范少数民族医药事业发展。

**Article 52** The State shall take measures to increase support for the inheritance, innovation, application development, and personnel training of ethnic minority medicine, strengthen the construction of ethnic minority medical institutions and physician teams, and promote and regulate the development of ethnic minority medicine.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府中医药主管部门及其他有关部门未履行本法规定的职责的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 53** Where the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments fail to perform the duties prescribed in this Law, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，中医诊所超出备案范围开展医疗活动的，由所在地县级人民政府中医药主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上三万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停止执业活动。  
中医诊所被责令停止执业活动的，其直接负责的主管人员自处罚决定作出之日起五年内不得在医疗机构内从事管理工作。医疗机构聘用上述不得从事管理工作的人员从事管理工作的，由原发证部门吊销执业许可证或者由原备案部门责令停止执业活动。

**Article 54** Where a traditional Chinese medicine clinic, in violation of this Law, conducts medical activities beyond the scope of its registration, the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine of the local county people's government shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the suspension of practice activities.  
Where a traditional Chinese medicine clinic is ordered to suspend its practice activities, the directly responsible supervisors of such clinic shall not engage in management work in any medical institution within five years from the date on which the penalty decision is made. Where a medical institution employs the persons specified above who are not allowed to engage in management work to perform management work, the original licensing authority shall revoke the practice license or the original registration authority shall order the suspension of practice activities.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，经考核取得医师资格的中医医师超出注册的执业范围从事医疗活动的，由县级以上人民政府中医药主管部门责令暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动，并处一万元以上三万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销执业证书。

**Article 55** Where a traditional Chinese medicine physician who has obtained physician qualification through examination, in violation of this Law, engages in medical activities beyond the scope of the registered practice, the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the suspension of practice activities for not less than six months but not more than one year, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the practice certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，举办中医诊所、炮制中药饮片、委托配制中药制剂应当备案而未备案，或者备案时提供虚假材料的，由中医药主管部门和药品监督管理部门按照各自职责分工责令改正，没收违法所得，并处三万元以下罚款，向社会公告相关信息；拒不改正的，责令停止执业活动或者责令停止炮制中药饮片、委托配制中药制剂活动，其直接责任人员五年内不得从事中医药相关活动。  
医疗机构应用传统工艺配制中药制剂未依照本法规定备案，或者未按照备案材料载明的要求配制中药制剂的，按生产假药给予处罚。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of this Law, a person establishes a traditional Chinese medicine clinic, processes prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs, or entrusts the preparation of traditional Chinese medicine preparations that shall be filed for record but fails to do so, or provides false materials at the time of filing, the competent department of traditional Chinese medicine and the drug regulatory authority, according to their respective functions, shall order corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than RMB 30,000 yuan, and publicly announce the relevant information; if the person refuses to make corrections, the practice activities shall be ordered to be suspended or the activities of processing prepared slices of Chinese crude drugs or entrusting the preparation of traditional Chinese medicine preparations shall be ordered to be suspended, and the directly responsible persons shall not engage in any activities related to traditional Chinese medicine for five years.  
Where a medical institution uses traditional techniques to prepare traditional Chinese medicine preparations without filing for record according to the provisions of this Law, or prepares traditional Chinese medicine preparations not in accordance with the requirements specified in the recorded materials, it shall be punished as for producing counterfeit drugs.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，发布的中医医疗广告内容与经审查批准的内容不相符的，由原审查部门撤销该广告的审查批准文件，一年内不受理该医疗机构的广告审查申请。  
违反本法规定，发布中医医疗广告有前款规定以外违法行为的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的规定给予处罚。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of this Law, the content of a published traditional Chinese medicine medical advertisement is inconsistent with the content approved upon examination, the original examining department shall revoke the examination and approval document for such advertisement, and shall not accept the application for advertisement examination of such medical institution within one year.  
Where, in violation of this Law, a published traditional Chinese medicine medical advertisement has other illegal acts beyond those specified in the preceding paragraph, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，在中药材种植过程中使用剧毒、高毒农药的，依照有关法律、法规规定给予处罚；情节严重的，可以由公安机关对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of this Law, a person uses highly toxic or highly toxic pesticides in the cultivation of Chinese crude drugs, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; if the circumstances are serious, the public security organ may impose a detention of not less than five days but not more than fifteen days on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of this Law, personal injury or property damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　中医药的管理，本法未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国执业医师法》、《中华人民共和国药品管理法》等相关法律、行政法规的规定。  
军队的中医药管理，由军队卫生主管部门依照本法和军队有关规定组织实施。

**Article 60** For the management of traditional Chinese medicine not provided for in this Law, the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Licensed Physicians, the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.  
The management of traditional Chinese medicine in the military shall be organized and implemented by the competent health department of the military in accordance with this Law and the relevant military provisions.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　民族自治地方可以根据《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》和本法的有关规定，结合实际，制定促进和规范本地方少数民族医药事业发展的办法。

**Article 61** Ethnic autonomous areas may, in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional Ethnic Autonomy and this Law, and in light of local conditions, formulate measures to promote and regulate the development of ethnic minority medicine in their localities.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　盲人按照国家有关规定取得盲人医疗按摩人员资格的，可以以个人开业的方式或者在医疗机构内提供医疗按摩服务。

**Article 62** Blind persons who have obtained the qualification of blind medical massage personnel in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State may provide medical massage services in the form of individual practice or within medical institutions.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2017年7月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2017.
