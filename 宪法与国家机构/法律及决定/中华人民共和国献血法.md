# 中华人民共和国献血法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1997-12-29  
**生效日期**：1998-10-01

> （1997年12月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保证医疗临床用血需要和安全，保障献血者和用血者身体健康，发扬人道主义精神，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本法。


**Article 1** This Law is enacted to ensure the supply and safety of blood for medical clinical use, protect the health of blood donors and blood recipients, promote humanitarianism, and advance socialist material and spiritual civilization.
<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行无偿献血制度。  

**Article 2** The state shall implement a system of voluntary blood donation without compensation.  
The state encourages healthy citizens between the ages of eighteen and fifty-five to donate blood voluntarily.
国家提倡十八周岁至五十五周岁的健康公民自愿献血。

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地方各级人民政府领导本行政区域内的献血工作，统一规划并负责组织、协调有关部门共同做好献血工作。


**Article 3** Local people's governments at all levels shall lead the blood donation work within their respective administrative areas, make unified plans, and be responsible for organizing and coordinating relevant departments to jointly carry out the blood donation work well.
<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政部门监督管理献血工作。  

**Article 4** The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and administer blood donation work.  
Red Cross Societies at all levels shall participate in and promote blood donation work in accordance with the law.
各级红十字会依法参与、推动献血工作。

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府采取措施广泛宣传献血的意义，普及献血的科学知识，开展预防和控制经血液途径传播的疾病的教育。  

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to widely publicize the significance of blood donation, popularize scientific knowledge about blood donation, and carry out education on the prevention and control of diseases transmitted through blood routes.  
The news media shall conduct public welfare publicity on blood donation.
新闻媒介应当开展献血的社会公益性宣传。

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家机关、军队、社会团体、企业事业组织、居民委员会、村民委员会，应当动员和组织本单位或者本居住区的适龄公民参加献血。  

**Article 6** State organs, the armed forces, social organizations, enterprises and institutions, residents committees, and villagers committees shall mobilize and organize eligible citizens within their respective units or residential areas to participate in blood donation.  
The measures for mobilizing and organizing active-duty military personnel to donate blood shall be formulated by the health authority of the People's Liberation Army.  
A certificate of voluntary blood donation without compensation, issued by the health administrative department of the State Council, shall be given to blood donors, and relevant organizations may provide appropriate subsidies.
现役军人献血的动员和组织办法，由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门制定。  
对献血者，发给国务院卫生行政部门制作的无偿献血证书，有关单位可以给予适当补贴。

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励国家工作人员、现役军人和高等学校在校学生率先献血，为树立社会新风尚作表率。


**Article 7** The state encourages state functionaries, active-duty military personnel, and students enrolled in institutions of higher learning to take the lead in donating blood, setting an example for fostering new social trends.
<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　血站是采集、提供临床用血的机构，是不以营利为目的的公益性组织。设立血站向公民采集血液，必须经国务院卫生行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门批准。血站应当为献血者提供各种安全、卫生、便利的条件。血站的设立条件和管理办法由国务院卫生行政部门制定。


**Article 8** A blood station is an institution that collects and supplies blood for clinical use, and is a non-profit public welfare organization. The establishment of a blood station to collect blood from citizens must be approved by the health administrative department of the State Council or the health administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. A blood station shall provide blood donors with various safe, hygienic, and convenient conditions. The conditions for establishing blood stations and the administrative measures thereof shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.
<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　血站对献血者必须免费进行必要的健康检查；身体状况不符合献血条件的，血站应当向其说明情况，不得采集血液。献血者的身体健康条件由国务院卫生行政部门规定。  

**Article 9** A blood station must conduct necessary health checks for blood donors free of charge; if a donor's physical condition does not meet the donation criteria, the blood station shall explain the situation to them and shall not collect their blood. The physical health conditions for blood donors shall be prescribed by the health administrative department of the State Council. The amount of blood collected by a blood station from a donor each time shall generally be 200 milliliters, and shall not exceed 400 milliliters at most, and the interval between two collections shall not be less than six months. It is strictly prohibited for a blood station to collect blood from donors in excessive amounts or at frequent intervals in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph.
血站对献血者每次采集血液量一般为二百毫升，最多不得超过四百毫升，两次采集间隔期不少于六个月。  
严格禁止血站违反前款规定对献血者超量、频繁采集血液。

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　血站采集血液必须严格遵守有关操作规程和制度，采血必须由具有采血资格的医务人员进行，一次性采血器材用后必须销毁，确保献血者的身体健康。  

**Article 10** A blood station must strictly comply with the relevant operating procedures and rules when collecting blood. Blood collection must be conducted by medical personnel qualified for blood collection. Disposable blood collection equipment must be destroyed after use to ensure the health of blood donors. A blood station shall ensure the quality of blood in accordance with the standards formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council. A blood station must test the collected blood; blood that has not been tested or has failed the test shall not be supplied to medical institutions.
血站应当根据国务院卫生行政部门制定的标准，保证血液质量。  
血站对采集的血液必须进行检测；未经检测或者检测不合格的血液，不得向医疗机构提供。

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　无偿献血的血液必须用于临床，不得买卖。血站、医疗机构不得将无偿献血的血液出售给单采血浆站或者血液制品生产单位。


**Article 11** Blood donated without compensation must be used for clinical purposes and shall not be bought or sold. Blood stations and medical institutions shall not sell blood donated without compensation to plasmapheresis stations or blood product manufacturing units.
<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　临床用血的包装、储存、运输，必须符合国家规定的卫生标准和要求。


**Article 12** The packaging, storage, and transportation of blood for clinical use must comply with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the state.
<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　医疗机构对临床用血必须进行核查，不得将不符合国家规定标准的血液用于临床。


**Article 13** Medical institutions must verify blood for clinical use and shall not use blood that does not meet the standards prescribed by the state for clinical purposes.
<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公民临床用血时只交付用于血液的采集、储存、分离、检验等费用；具体收费标准由国务院卫生行政部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定。  

**Article 14** When a citizen uses blood for clinical purposes, they shall only pay the costs for the collection, storage, separation, and testing of the blood; the specific fee standards shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council jointly with the price authority of the State Council.  
When a voluntary blood donor without compensation needs blood for clinical use, they shall be exempted from the fees prescribed in the preceding paragraph; when the spouse or direct relatives of a voluntary blood donor without compensation need blood for clinical use, they may be exempted from or have reduced the fees prescribed in the preceding paragraph in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.
无偿献血者临床需要用血时，免交前款规定的费用；无偿献血者的配偶和直系亲属临床需要用血时，可以按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定免交或者减交前款规定的费用。

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　为保障公民临床急救用血的需要，国家提倡并指导择期手术的患者自身储血，动员家庭、亲友、所在单位以及社会互助献血。  

**Article 15** To meet the needs of citizens for emergency clinical blood use, the state encourages and guides patients undergoing elective surgery to store their own blood, and mobilizes families, relatives and friends, employers, and the community for mutual-assistance blood donation.  
To ensure blood supply for emergencies, medical institutions may temporarily collect blood, but they shall do so in accordance with the provisions of this Law to ensure the safety of blood collection and use.
为保证应急用血，医疗机构可以临时采集血液，但应当依照本法规定，确保采血用血安全。

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　医疗机构临床用血应当制定用血计划，遵循合理、科学的原则，不得浪费和滥用血液。  

**Article 16** Medical institutions shall formulate blood use plans for clinical purposes, follow the principles of rationality and science, and shall not waste or misuse blood.  
Medical institutions shall actively promote blood transfusion based on blood components according to actual medical needs; the specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.  
The state encourages the research and promotion of new technologies for clinical blood use.
医疗机构应当积极推行按血液成份针对医疗实际需要输血，具体管理办法由国务院卫生行政部门制定。  
国家鼓励临床用血新技术的研究和推广。

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级人民政府和红十字会对积极参加献血和在献血工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。


**Article 17** People's governments at all levels and Red Cross Societies shall award units and individuals that actively participate in blood donation or make outstanding achievements in blood donation work.
<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门予以取缔，没收违法所得，可以并处十万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  

**Article 18** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be banned by the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, have their illegal gains confiscated, and may be concurrently fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law: (1) illegally collecting blood; (2) blood stations or medical institutions selling blood donated without compensation; (3) illegally organizing others to sell their blood.
（一）非法采集血液的；  
（二）血站、医疗机构出售无偿献血的血液的；  
（三）非法组织他人出卖血液的。

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　血站违反有关操作规程和制度采集血液，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正；给献血者健康造成损害的，应当依法赔偿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。


**Article 19** If a blood station collects blood in violation of relevant operating procedures and rules, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections; if it causes harm to the health of blood donors, it shall pay compensation according to law, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.
<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　临床用血的包装、储存、运输，不符合国家规定的卫生标准和要求的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，给予警告，可以并处一万元以下的罚款。


**Article 20** If the packaging, storage, or transportation of blood for clinical use fails to comply with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the state, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.
<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　血站违反本法的规定，向医疗机构提供不符合国家规定标准的血液的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正；情节严重，造成经血液途径传播的疾病传播或者有传播严重危险的，限期整顿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。


**Article 21** Where a blood station, in violation of the provisions of this Law, provides blood that fails to meet the standards prescribed by the State to a medical institution, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, causing the spread of a disease transmitted through blood or posing a serious risk of such spread, the blood station shall be ordered to rectify within a time limit, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.
<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　医疗机构的医务人员违反本法规定，将不符合国家规定标准的血液用于患者的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正；给患者健康造成损害的，应当依法赔偿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。


**Article 22** Where a medical worker of a medical institution, in violation of the provisions of this Law, uses blood that fails to meet the standards prescribed by the State on a patient, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order him or her to make corrections; if harm is caused to the patient's health, compensation shall be made according to law, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.
<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　卫生行政部门及其工作人员在献血、用血的监督管理工作中，玩忽职守，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。


**Article 23** Where the health administrative department or any of its staff members, in the work of supervision and administration over blood donation and blood use, neglects his or her duty, causing serious consequences, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.
<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本法自1998年10月1日起施行。


**Article 24** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 1998.
