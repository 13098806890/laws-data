# 中华人民共和国戒严法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1996-03-01  
**生效日期**：1996-03-01

> （1996年3月1日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议通过）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据中华人民共和国宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在发生严重危及国家的统一、安全或者社会公共安全的动乱、暴乱或者严重骚乱，不采取非常措施不足以维护社会秩序、保护人民的生命和财产安全的紧急状态时，国家可以决定实行戒严。

**Article 2** When a state of emergency occurs, characterized by turmoil, insurrection, or serious riot that severely endangers the unity, security, or public safety of the State, and where ordinary measures are insufficient to maintain social order and protect the lives and property of the people, the State may decide to impose martial law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　全国或者个别省、自治区、直辖市的戒严，由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定；中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，发布戒严令。  
省、自治区、直辖市的范围内部分地区的戒严，由国务院决定，国务院总理发布戒严令。

**Article 3** The State Council shall submit a proposal to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for a decision on the imposition of martial law throughout the country or in individual provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; the President of the People's Republic of China shall issue a martial law order in accordance with the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
The imposition of martial law in parts of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be decided by the State Council, and the Premier of the State Council shall issue the martial law order.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　戒严期间，为保证戒严的实施和维护社会治安秩序，国家可以依照本法在戒严地区内，对宪法、法律规定的公民权利和自由的行使作出特别规定。

**Article 4** During the period of martial law, the State may, in accordance with this Law, make special provisions within the martial law area regarding the exercise of citizens' rights and freedoms prescribed by the Constitution and laws, in order to ensure the implementation of martial law and maintain public security and order.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　戒严地区内的人民政府应当依照本法采取必要的措施，尽快恢复正常社会秩序，保障人民的生命和财产安全以及基本生活必需品的供应。

**Article 5** The People's Government within the martial law area shall take necessary measures in accordance with this Law to restore normal social order as soon as possible, and to ensure the safety of the people's lives and property and the supply of basic necessities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　戒严地区内的一切组织和个人，必须严格遵守戒严令和实施戒严令的规定，积极协助人民政府恢复正常社会秩序。

**Article 6** All organizations and individuals within the martial law area must strictly abide by the martial law order and the provisions for implementing the martial law order, and actively assist the People's Government in restoring normal social order.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对遵守戒严令和实施戒严令的规定的组织和个人，采取有效措施保护其合法权益不受侵犯。

**Article 7** The State shall take effective measures to protect the lawful rights and interests of organizations and individuals that comply with the martial law order and the provisions for implementing the martial law order from being infringed upon.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　戒严任务由人民警察、人民武装警察执行；必要时，国务院可以向中央军事委员会提出，由中央军事委员会决定派出人民解放军协助执行戒严任务。

**Article 8** Martial law tasks shall be executed by the People's Police and the People's Armed Police; where necessary, the State Council may propose to the Central Military Commission, which shall decide to dispatch the People's Liberation Army to assist in executing martial law tasks.

### 第二章　戒严的实施

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国或者个别省、自治区、直辖市的戒严，由国务院组织实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市的范围内部分地区的戒严，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织实施；必要时，国务院可以直接组织实施。  
组织实施戒严的机关称为戒严实施机关。

**Article 9** The State Council shall organize the implementation of martial law throughout the country or in individual provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the implementation of martial law in parts of such areas; where necessary, the State Council may directly organize the implementation.  
The organ that organizes and implements martial law is referred to as the martial law enforcement organ.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　戒严实施机关建立戒严指挥机构，由戒严指挥机构协调执行戒严任务的有关方面的行动，统一部署和实施戒严措施。  
执行戒严任务的人民解放军，在戒严指挥机构的统一部署下，由中央军事委员会指定的军事机关实施指挥。

**Article 10** The martial law enforcement organ shall establish a martial law command structure, which shall coordinate the actions of relevant parties executing martial law tasks and uniformly deploy and implement martial law measures.  
The People's Liberation Army executing martial law tasks shall, under the unified deployment of the martial law command structure, be commanded by the military organ designated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　戒严令应当规定戒严的地域范围、起始时间、实施机关等事项。

**Article 11** The martial law order shall specify matters such as the territorial scope, starting time, and enforcement organ of the martial law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　根据本法第二条规定实行戒严的紧急状态消除后，应当及时解除戒严。  
解除戒严的程序与决定戒严的程序相同。

**Article 12** After the state of emergency for which martial law was imposed in accordance with Article 2 of this Law has been eliminated, the martial law shall be lifted in a timely manner.  
The procedure for lifting martial law shall be the same as the procedure for deciding to impose martial law.

### 第三章　实施戒严的措施

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　戒严期间，戒严实施机关可以决定在戒严地区采取下列措施，并可以制定具体实施办法：  
（一）禁止或者限制集会、游行、示威、街头讲演以及其他聚众活动；  
（二）禁止罢工、罢市、罢课；  
（三）实行新闻管制；  
（四）实行通讯、邮政、电信管制；  
（五）实行出境入境管制；  
（六）禁止任何反对戒严的活动。

**Article 13** During the period of martial law, the martial law enforcement organ may decide to take the following measures within the martial law area, and may formulate specific implementation rules:  
(1) prohibit or restrict assemblies, processions, demonstrations, street speeches, and other crowd-gathering activities;  
(2) prohibit strikes, shop closures, and class boycotts;  
(3) impose press controls;  
(4) impose controls on communications, postal services, and telecommunications;  
(5) impose controls on exit and entry;  
(6) prohibit any activity opposing the martial law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　戒严期间，戒严实施机关可以决定在戒严地区采取交通管制措施，限制人员进出交通管制区域，并对进出交通管制区域人员的证件、车辆、物品进行检查。

**Article 14** During the period of martial law, the martial law enforcement organ may decide to adopt traffic control measures within the martial law area, restrict the entry and exit of persons into and from the traffic control zone, and inspect the documents, vehicles, and articles of persons entering and leaving the traffic control zone.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　戒严期间，戒严实施机关可以决定在戒严地区采取宵禁措施。宵禁期间，在实行宵禁地区的街道或者其他公共场所通行，必须持有本人身份证件和戒严实施机关制发的特别通行证。

**Article 15** During the period of martial law, the martial law enforcement organ may decide to adopt curfew measures within the martial law area. During the curfew, any person who walks on the streets or in other public places within the curfew area must hold his or her own identity document and a special pass issued by the martial law enforcement organ.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　戒严期间，戒严实施机关或者戒严指挥机构可以在戒严地区对下列物品采取特别管理措施：  
（一）武器、弹药；  
（二）管制刀具；  
（三）易燃易爆物品；  
（四）化学危险物品、放射性物品、剧毒物品等。

**Article 16** During the period of martial law, the martial law enforcement organ or the martial law command structure may adopt special control measures within the martial law area over the following articles:  
(1) weapons and ammunition;  
(2) restricted knives;  
(3) flammable and explosive articles;  
(4) dangerous chemicals, radioactive articles, highly toxic articles, and the like.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　根据执行戒严任务的需要，戒严地区的县级以上人民政府可以临时征用国家机关、企业事业组织、社会团体以及公民个人的房屋、场所、设施、运输工具、工程机械等。在非常紧急的情况下，执行戒严任务的人民警察、人民武装警察、人民解放军的现场指挥员可以直接决定临时征用，地方人民政府应当给予协助。实施征用应当开具征用单据。  
前款规定的临时征用物，在使用完毕或者戒严解除后应当及时归还；因征用造成损坏的，由县级以上人民政府按照国家有关规定给予相应补偿。

**Article 17** In accordance with the needs of executing martial law tasks, the people's government at or above the county level within the martial law area may temporarily requisition houses, premises, facilities, means of transport, engineering machinery, etc., belonging to State organs, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individual citizens. In cases of extreme urgency, the on-site commander of the People's Police, the People's Armed Police, or the People's Liberation Army executing martial law tasks may directly decide to make temporary requisitions, and the local people's government shall provide assistance. A requisition receipt shall be issued when carrying out a requisition. The temporarily requisitioned items specified in the preceding paragraph shall be returned in a timely manner after use or upon the lifting of the martial law; where damage is caused by the requisition, the people's government at or above the county level shall provide appropriate compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　戒严期间，对戒严地区的下列单位、场所，采取措施，加强警卫：  
（一）首脑机关；  
（二）军事机关和重要军事设施；  
（三）外国驻华使领馆、国际组织驻华代表机构和国宾下榻处；  
（四）广播电台、电视台、国家通讯社等重要新闻单位及其重要设施；  
（五）与国计民生有重大关系的公用企业和公共设施；  
（六）机场、火车站和港口；  
（七）监狱、劳教场所、看守所；  
（八）其他需要加强警卫的单位和场所。

**Article 18** During the period of martial law, measures shall be taken to strengthen security at the following units and premises within the martial law area:  
(1) leading organs of the State;  
(2) military organs and important military installations;  
(3) embassies and consulates of foreign countries in China, representative offices of international organizations in China, and the residences of state guests;  
(4) important news units such as radio stations, television stations, and the national news agency, and their important facilities;  
(5) public utilities and public facilities vital to the national economy and people's livelihood;  
(6) airports, railway stations, and ports;  
(7) prisons, re-education-through-labor sites, and detention houses;  
(8) other units and premises requiring strengthened security.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　为保障戒严地区内的人民基本生活必需品的供应，戒严实施机关可以对基本生活必需品的生产、运输、供应、价格，采取特别管理措施。

**Article 19** To ensure the supply of basic necessities for the people within the martial law area, the martial law enforcement organ may adopt special control measures over the production, transportation, supply, and prices of basic necessities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　戒严实施机关依照本法采取的实施戒严令的措施和办法，需要公众遵守的，应当公布；在实施过程中，根据情况，对于不需要继续实施的措施和办法，应当及时公布停止实施。

**Article 20** Measures and methods adopted by the martial law enforcement organ in accordance with this Law for implementing the martial law order that require public compliance shall be made public; during the implementation, where, based on circumstances, certain measures and methods no longer need to be implemented, their discontinuation shall be made public in a timely manner.

### 第四章　戒严执勤人员的职责

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　执行戒严任务的人民警察、人民武装警察和人民解放军是戒严执勤人员。  
戒严执勤人员执行戒严任务时，应当佩带由戒严实施机关统一规定的标志。

**Article 21** The people's police, the people's armed police, and the People's Liberation Army that perform martial law tasks shall be the personnel on martial law duty.  
When performing martial law tasks, the personnel on martial law duty shall wear the insignia uniformly prescribed by the organ that imposes the martial law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　戒严执勤人员依照戒严实施机关的规定，有权对戒严地区公共道路上或者其他公共场所内的人员的证件、车辆、物品进行检查。

**Article 22** The personnel on martial law duty, in accordance with the provisions of the organ that imposes the martial law, shall have the power to inspect the documents, vehicles, and articles of persons on public roads or in other public places in the area under martial law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　戒严执勤人员依照戒严实施机关的规定，有权对违反宵禁规定的人予以扣留，直至清晨宵禁结束；并有权对被扣留者的人身进行搜查，对其携带的物品进行检查。

**Article 23** The personnel on martial law duty, in accordance with the provisions of the organ that imposes the martial law, shall have the power to detain any person who violates the curfew regulations until the end of the curfew in the early morning; and shall have the power to search the person of the detainee and inspect the articles carried by him.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　戒严执勤人员依照戒严实施机关的规定，有权对下列人员立即予以拘留：  
（一）正在实施危害国家安全、破坏社会秩序的犯罪或者有重大嫌疑的；  
（二）阻挠或者抗拒戒严执勤人员执行戒严任务的；  
（三）抗拒交通管制或者宵禁规定的；  
（四）从事其他抗拒戒严令的活动的。

**Article 24** The personnel on martial law duty, in accordance with the provisions of the organ that imposes the martial law, shall have the power to immediately detain the following persons:  
(1) Those who are committing crimes that endanger national security or disrupt public order, or who are under strong suspicion of doing so;  
(2) Those who obstruct or resist the personnel on martial law duty in performing their martial law tasks;  
(3) Those who resist traffic control or curfew regulations  
(4) Those who engage in other activities of resisting the martial law order.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　戒严执勤人员依照戒严实施机关的规定，有权对被拘留的人员的人身进行搜查，有权对犯罪嫌疑分子的住所和涉嫌藏匿犯罪分子、犯罪嫌疑分子或者武器、弹药等危险物品的场所进行搜查。

**Article 25** The personnel on martial law duty, in accordance with the provisions of the organ that imposes the martial law, shall have the power to search the person of any detained person, and shall have the power to search the residence of a criminal suspect and the places suspected of hiding criminals, criminal suspects, or dangerous articles such as weapons and ammunition.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在戒严地区有下列聚众情形之一、阻止无效的，戒严执勤人员根据有关规定，可以使用警械强行制止或者驱散，并将其组织者和拒不服从的人员强行带离现场或者立即予以拘留：  
（一）非法进行集会、游行、示威以及其他聚众活动的；  
（二）非法占据公共场所或者在公共场所煽动进行破坏活动的；  
（三）冲击国家机关或者其他重要单位、场所的；  
（四）扰乱交通秩序或者故意堵塞交通的；  
（五）哄抢或者破坏机关、团体、企业事业组织和公民个人的财产的。

**Article 26** Where, in the area under martial law, any of the following crowd situations occur and warnings are ineffective, the personnel on martial law duty may, in accordance with the relevant provisions, use police implements to forcibly stop or disperse the crowd, and forcibly remove the organizers and those who refuse to obey from the scene or immediately detain them:  
(1) Illegally holding assemblies, processions, demonstrations, or other crowd activities;  
(2) Illegally occupying a public place or inciting destructive activities in a public place;  
(3) Storming state organs or other important units or places;  
(4) Disrupting traffic order or intentionally blocking traffic  
(5) Looting or destroying the property of state organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, or citizens.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　戒严执勤人员对于依照本法规定予以拘留的人员，应当及时登记和讯问，发现不需要继续拘留的，应当立即释放。  
戒严期间拘留、逮捕的程序和期限可以不受中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法有关规定的限制，但逮捕须经人民检察院批准或者决定。

**Article 27** The personnel on martial law duty shall promptly register and interrogate the persons detained in accordance with the provisions of this Law, and shall immediately release any person who is found to need not be detained further.  
The procedures and time limits for detention and arrest during the period of martial law may not be bound by the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, but an arrest must be approved or decided by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在戒严地区遇有下列特别紧急情形之一，使用警械无法制止时，戒严执勤人员可以使用枪支等武器：  
（一）公民或者戒严执勤人员的生命安全受到暴力危害时；  
（二）拘留、逮捕、押解人犯，遇有暴力抗拒、行凶或者脱逃时；  
（三）遇暴力抢夺武器、弹药时；  
（四）警卫的重要对象、目标受到暴力袭击，或者有受到暴力袭击的紧迫危险时；  
（五）在执行消防、抢险、救护作业以及其他重大紧急任务中，受到严重暴力阻挠时；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定可以使用枪支等武器的其他情形。  
戒严执勤人员必须严格遵守使用枪支等武器的规定。

**Article 28** Where, in the area under martial law, any of the following extremely urgent circumstances occurs and cannot be stopped by using police implements, the personnel on martial law duty may use firearms and other weapons:  
(1) When the life safety of a citizen or the personnel on martial law duty is endangered by violence;  
(2) When violent resistance, assault, or escape occurs during the detention, arrest, or escort of a criminal;  
(3) When violent seizure of weapons or ammunition occurs;  
(4) When an important object or target under guard is attacked by violence, or there is an imminent danger of being attacked by violence;  
(5) When severely obstructed by violence while performing firefighting, rescue, emergency medical, or other major urgent missions  
(6) Other circumstances as provided by laws or administrative regulations under which firearms and other weapons may be used. The personnel on martial law duty must strictly observe the regulations on the use of firearms and other weapons.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　戒严执勤人员应当遵守法律、法规和执勤规则，服从命令，履行职责，尊重当地民族风俗习惯，不得侵犯和损害公民的合法权益。

**Article 29** The personnel on martial law duty shall abide by laws, regulations, and duty rules, obey orders, fulfill their duties, respect local ethnic customs and habits, and shall not infringe upon or damage the lawful rights and interests of citizens.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　戒严执勤人员依法执行任务的行为受法律保护。  
戒严执勤人员违反本法规定，滥用职权，侵犯和损害公民合法权益的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 30** The acts of the personnel on martial law duty in performing their tasks according to law shall be protected by law.  
Personnel on martial law duty who violate the provisions of this Law, abuse their powers, and infringe upon or damage the lawful rights and interests of citizens shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在个别县、市的局部范围内突然发生严重骚乱，严重危及国家安全、社会公共安全和人民的生命财产安全，国家没有作出戒严决定时，当地省级人民政府报经国务院批准，可以决定并组织人民警察、人民武装警察实施交通管制和现场管制，限制人员进出管制区域，对进出管制区域人员的证件、车辆、物品进行检查，对参与骚乱的人可以强行予以驱散、强行带离现场、搜查，对组织者和拒不服从的人员可以立即予以拘留；在人民警察、人民武装警察力量还不足以维持社会秩序时，可以报请国务院向中央军事委员会提出，由中央军事委员会决定派出人民解放军协助当地人民政府恢复和维持正常社会秩序。

**Article 31** Where a serious riot suddenly occurs within a part of a county or city, seriously endangering national security, public safety, and the safety of people's lives and property, and the state has not made the decision to impose martial law, the local provincial people's government may, upon approval by the State Council, decide to organize the people's police and the people's armed police to implement traffic control and on-site control, restrict persons from entering or leaving the controlled area, inspect the documents, vehicles, and articles of persons entering or leaving the controlled area, forcibly disperse, forcibly remove from the scene, and search the persons participating in the riot, and immediately detain the organizers and those who refuse to obey; where the forces of the people's police and the people's armed police are insufficient to maintain public order, the matter may be referred to the Central Military Commission through the State Council, and the Central Military Commission may decide to dispatch the People's Liberation Army to assist the local people's government in restoring and maintaining normal public order.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 32** This Law shall come into force on the date of its promulgation.
