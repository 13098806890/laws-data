# 中华人民共和国反恐怖主义法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-04-27  
**生效日期**：2018-04-27

> （2015年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议通过　根据2018年4月27日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法〉等六部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　恐怖活动组织和人员的认定 第三章　安全防范 第四章　情报信息 第五章　调　　查 第六章　应对处置 第七章　国际合作 第八章　保障措施 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了防范和惩治恐怖活动，加强反恐怖主义工作，维护国家安全、公共安全和人民生命财产安全，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　国家反对一切形式的恐怖主义，依法取缔恐怖活动组织，对任何组织、策划、准备实施、实施恐怖活动，宣扬恐怖主义，煽动实施恐怖活动，组织、领导、参加恐怖活动组织，为恐怖活动提供帮助的，依法追究法律责任。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防范和惩治恐怖活动，加强反恐怖主义工作，维护国家安全、公共安全和人民生命财产安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of preventing and punishing terrorist activities, strengthening counterterrorism work, and safeguarding national security, public security, and the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家反对一切形式的恐怖主义，依法取缔恐怖活动组织，对任何组织、策划、准备实施、实施恐怖活动，宣扬恐怖主义，煽动实施恐怖活动，组织、领导、参加恐怖活动组织，为恐怖活动提供帮助的，依法追究法律责任。  
国家不向任何恐怖活动组织和人员作出妥协，不向任何恐怖活动人员提供庇护或者给予难民地位。

**Article 2** The State opposes all forms of terrorism, shall outlaw terrorist activity organizations according to law, and shall investigate the legal liability according to law of any person who organizes, plans, prepares for or carries out terrorist activities, advocates terrorism, incites the commission of terrorist activities, organizes, leads or participates in terrorist activity organizations, or provides assistance for terrorist activities.  
The State shall not make any compromise with any terrorist activity organization or individual, and shall not provide any shelter or grant refugee status to any terrorist activity individual.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称恐怖主义，是指通过暴力、破坏、恐吓等手段，制造社会恐慌、危害公共安全、侵犯人身财产，或者胁迫国家机关、国际组织，以实现其政治、意识形态等目的的主张和行为。  
本法所称恐怖活动，是指恐怖主义性质的下列行为：  
（一）组织、策划、准备实施、实施造成或者意图造成人员伤亡、重大财产损失、公共设施损坏、社会秩序混乱等严重社会危害的活动的；  
（二）宣扬恐怖主义，煽动实施恐怖活动，或者非法持有宣扬恐怖主义的物品，强制他人在公共场所穿戴宣扬恐怖主义的服饰、标志的；  
（三）组织、领导、参加恐怖活动组织的；  
（四）为恐怖活动组织、恐怖活动人员、实施恐怖活动或者恐怖活动培训提供信息、资金、物资、劳务、技术、场所等支持、协助、便利的；  
（五）其他恐怖活动。  
本法所称恐怖活动组织，是指三人以上为实施恐怖活动而组成的犯罪组织。  
本法所称恐怖活动人员，是指实施恐怖活动的人和恐怖活动组织的成员。  
本法所称恐怖事件，是指正在发生或者已经发生的造成或者可能造成重大社会危害的恐怖活动。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, terrorism means advocacy and actions that, by means of violence, sabotage, intimidation, etc., create social panic, endanger public security, infringe on personal or property rights, or coerce state organs or international organizations, in order to achieve political, ideological or other purposes.  
For the purposes of this Law, terrorist activities mean the following acts of a terrorist nature:  
(1) organizing, planning, preparing for or carrying out activities that cause or are intended to cause serious social harm, such as casualties, heavy property losses, damage to public facilities, or disruption of social order;  
(2) advocating terrorism, inciting the commission of terrorist activities, or illegally possessing articles that advocate terrorism, or forcing others to wear clothing or symbols that advocate terrorism in public places;  
(3) organizing, leading or participating in a terrorist activity organization;  
(4) providing information, funds, materials, labor, technology, premises, or other support, assistance or convenience to a terrorist activity organization, a terrorist activity individual, or for the commission of terrorist activities or terrorist activity training;  
(5) other terrorist activities.  
For the purposes of this Law, a terrorist activity organization means a criminal organization formed by three or more persons for the purpose of committing terrorist activities.  
For the purposes of this Law, a terrorist activity individual means a person who commits terrorist activities and a member of a terrorist activity organization.  
For the purposes of this Law, a terrorist incident means a terrorist activity that is occurring or has occurred and that causes or may cause serious social harm.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家将反恐怖主义纳入国家安全战略，综合施策，标本兼治，加强反恐怖主义的能力建设，运用政治、经济、法律、文化、教育、外交、军事等手段，开展反恐怖主义工作。  
国家反对一切形式的以歪曲宗教教义或者其他方法煽动仇恨、煽动歧视、鼓吹暴力等极端主义，消除恐怖主义的思想基础。

**Article 4** The State shall incorporate counterterrorism into the national security strategy, adopt comprehensive measures to address both the symptoms and root causes, strengthen capacity building for counterterrorism, and conduct counterterrorism work by using political, economic, legal, cultural, educational, diplomatic and military means.  
The State opposes all forms of extremism that incite hatred, incite discrimination, or advocate violence by distorting religious teachings or other means, and shall eliminate the ideological foundation of terrorism.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　反恐怖主义工作坚持专门工作与群众路线相结合，防范为主、惩防结合和先发制敌、保持主动的原则。

**Article 5** Counterterrorism work shall adhere to the principles of combining specialized work with the mass line, focusing on prevention while integrating punishment with prevention, and preemptively striking the enemy while maintaining the initiative.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　反恐怖主义工作应当依法进行，尊重和保障人权，维护公民和组织的合法权益。  
在反恐怖主义工作中，应当尊重公民的宗教信仰自由和民族风俗习惯，禁止任何基于地域、民族、宗教等理由的歧视性做法。

**Article 6** Counterterrorism work shall be conducted in accordance with law, respecting and protecting human rights, and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of citizens and organizations.  
In counterterrorism work, citizens' freedom of religious belief and ethnic customs shall be respected, and any discriminatory practices based on region, ethnicity, religion or other reasons shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家设立反恐怖主义工作领导机构，统一领导和指挥全国反恐怖主义工作。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府设立反恐怖主义工作领导机构，县级人民政府根据需要设立反恐怖主义工作领导机构，在上级反恐怖主义工作领导机构的领导和指挥下，负责本地区反恐怖主义工作。

**Article 7** The State shall establish a counterterrorism work leadership organ to exercise unified leadership and command over the national counterterrorism work.  
Local people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall establish counterterrorism work leadership organs, and county people's governments shall establish such organs as needed, which shall, under the leadership and command of the counterterrorism work leadership organ at a higher level, be responsible for the counterterrorism work in their respective regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　公安机关、国家安全机关和人民检察院、人民法院、司法行政机关以及其他有关国家机关，应当根据分工，实行工作责任制，依法做好反恐怖主义工作。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规、军事法规以及国务院、中央军事委员会的命令，并根据反恐怖主义工作领导机构的部署，防范和处置恐怖活动。  
有关部门应当建立联动配合机制，依靠、动员村民委员会、居民委员会、企业事业单位、社会组织，共同开展反恐怖主义工作。

**Article 8** The public security organs, state security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, judicial administrative organs and other relevant state organs shall, in accordance with their division of duties, implement a work responsibility system and properly conduct counterterrorism work in accordance with law.  
The People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and the militia organizations shall, in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws, administrative regulations, military regulations, and the orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and in accordance with the deployment of the counterterrorism work leadership organ, prevent and handle terrorist activities.  
Relevant departments shall establish a mechanism for joint action and coordination, and rely on and mobilize villagers' committees, residents' committees, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations to jointly carry out counterterrorism work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都有协助、配合有关部门开展反恐怖主义工作的义务，发现恐怖活动嫌疑或者恐怖活动嫌疑人员的，应当及时向公安机关或者有关部门报告。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall have the duty to assist and cooperate with relevant departments in carrying out counterterrorism work, and upon discovering a suspected terrorist activity or a suspected terrorist activity individual, shall promptly report to the public security organ or a relevant department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对举报恐怖活动或者协助防范、制止恐怖活动有突出贡献的单位和个人，以及在反恐怖主义工作中作出其他突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 10** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in reporting terrorist activities or assisting in the prevention and suppression of terrorist activities, as well as units and individuals that have made other outstanding contributions in counterterrorism work, shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在中华人民共和国领域外对中华人民共和国国家、公民或者机构实施的恐怖活动犯罪，或者实施的中华人民共和国缔结、参加的国际条约所规定的恐怖活动犯罪，中华人民共和国行使刑事管辖权，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** The People's Republic of China shall exercise criminal jurisdiction over terrorist activity crimes committed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China against the State, citizens or institutions of the People's Republic of China, or over terrorist activity crimes provided for in international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party or has acceded, and shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第二章　恐怖活动组织和人员的认定

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构根据[本法第三条](#art-3)的规定，认定恐怖活动组织和人员，由国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构予以公告。

**Article 12** The State counterterrorism work leadership organ shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 3 of this Law, identify terrorist activity organizations and individuals, and the office of the State counterterrorism work leadership organ shall make a public announcement thereof.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院公安部门、国家安全部门、外交部门和省级反恐怖主义工作领导机构对于需要认定恐怖活动组织和人员的，应当向国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构提出申请。

**Article 13** The public security department, state security department, and foreign affairs department under the State Council, and the counterterrorism work leadership organ at the provincial level, for any need to identify a terrorist activity organization or individual, shall submit an application to the State counterterrorism work leadership organ.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　金融机构和特定非金融机构对国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构公告的恐怖活动组织和人员的资金或者其他资产，应当立即予以冻结，并按照规定及时向国务院公安部门、国家安全部门和反洗钱行政主管部门报告。

**Article 14** Financial institutions and designated non-financial institutions shall immediately freeze the funds or other assets of terrorist activity organizations and individuals publicly announced by the office of the State counterterrorism work leadership organ, and shall promptly report to the public security department, state security department, and anti-money laundering administrative department under the State Council in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被认定的恐怖活动组织和人员对认定不服的，可以通过国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构申请复核。国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当及时进行复核，作出维持或者撤销认定的决定。复核决定为最终决定。  
国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构作出撤销认定的决定的，由国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构予以公告；资金、资产已被冻结的，应当解除冻结。

**Article 15** If a terrorist activity organization or individual that has been identified disagrees with the identification, it/he may apply for review through the office of the State counterterrorism work leadership organ. The State counterterrorism work leadership organ shall promptly conduct a review and make a decision to uphold or revoke the identification. The review decision shall be final.  
If the State counterterrorism work leadership organ makes a decision to revoke the identification, the office of the State counterterrorism work leadership organ shall make a public announcement thereof; if funds or assets have been frozen, they shall be unfrozen.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　根据刑事诉讼法的规定，有管辖权的中级以上人民法院在审判刑事案件的过程中，可以依法认定恐怖活动组织和人员。对于在判决生效后需要由国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构予以公告的，适用本章的有关规定。

**Article 16** In accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law, an Intermediate People's Court with jurisdiction may, in the process of adjudicating a criminal case, identify a terrorist activity organization or individual in accordance with law. For cases that need to be publicly announced by the office of the State counterterrorism work leadership organ after the judgment takes effect, the relevant provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

### 第三章　安全防范

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当组织开展反恐怖主义宣传教育，提高公民的反恐怖主义意识。  
教育、人力资源行政主管部门和学校、有关职业培训机构应当将恐怖活动预防、应急知识纳入教育、教学、培训的内容。  
新闻、广播、电视、文化、宗教、互联网等有关单位，应当有针对性地面向社会进行反恐怖主义宣传教育。  
村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助人民政府以及有关部门，加强反恐怖主义宣传教育。

**Article 17** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall organize and carry out counterterrorism publicity and education to enhance citizens' awareness of counterterrorism.  
The administrative departments of education and human resources, as well as schools and relevant vocational training institutions, shall incorporate knowledge on the prevention of and emergency response to terrorist activities into the content of education, teaching, and training.  
Relevant units such as news, broadcasting, television, culture, religion, and the Internet shall conduct targeted counterterrorism publicity and education towards the public.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist the people's governments and relevant departments in strengthening counterterrorism publicity and education.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当为公安机关、国家安全机关依法进行防范、调查恐怖活动提供技术接口和解密等技术支持和协助。

**Article 18** Telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers shall provide technical support and assistance, such as technical interfaces and decryption, to the public security organs and state security organs for their lawful prevention and investigation of terrorist activities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当依照法律、行政法规规定，落实网络安全、信息内容监督制度和安全技术防范措施，防止含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息传播；发现含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息的，应当立即停止传输，保存相关记录，删除相关信息，并向公安机关或者有关部门报告。  
网信、电信、公安、国家安全等主管部门对含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息，应当按照职责分工，及时责令有关单位停止传输、删除相关信息，或者关闭相关网站、关停相关服务。有关单位应当立即执行，并保存相关记录，协助进行调查。对互联网上跨境传输的含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息，电信主管部门应当采取技术措施，阻断传播。

**Article 19** Telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers shall, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, implement network security and information content supervision systems and security technical prevention measures to prevent the dissemination of information containing terrorist or extremist content; upon discovering information containing terrorist or extremist content, they shall immediately stop transmission, preserve relevant records, delete the relevant information, and report to the public security organ or a relevant department.  
The competent departments for cyberspace affairs, telecommunications, public security, state security, etc., shall, in accordance with their respective duties, promptly order the relevant units to stop transmission, delete the relevant information, or shut down the relevant websites or services for information containing terrorist or extremist content. The relevant units shall immediately execute such orders, preserve relevant records, and assist in the investigation. For information containing terrorist or extremist content that is transmitted across borders on the Internet, the competent telecommunications department shall take technical measures to block its dissemination.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　铁路、公路、水上、航空的货运和邮政、快递等物流运营单位应当实行安全查验制度，对客户身份进行查验，依照规定对运输、寄递物品进行安全检查或者开封验视。对禁止运输、寄递，存在重大安全隐患，或者客户拒绝安全查验的物品，不得运输、寄递。  
前款规定的物流运营单位，应当实行运输、寄递客户身份、物品信息登记制度。

**Article 20** Logistics operation units such as railway, highway, waterway, and air freight, as well as postal and express delivery services, shall implement a security inspection system, verify customer identities, and conduct safety checks or open-package inspections of items to be transported or delivered in accordance with regulations. Items that are prohibited from transportation or delivery, have major safety hazards, or for which the customer refuses security inspection shall not be transported or delivered.  
The logistics operation units specified in the preceding paragraph shall implement a registration system for customer identity and item information in transportation and delivery.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电信、互联网、金融、住宿、长途客运、机动车租赁等业务经营者、服务提供者，应当对客户身份进行查验。对身份不明或者拒绝身份查验的，不得提供服务。

**Article 21** Business operators and service providers in telecommunications, Internet, finance, accommodation, long-distance passenger transport, motor vehicle rental, etc., shall verify customer identities. They shall not provide services to persons whose identities are unclear or who refuse identity verification.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产和进口单位应当依照规定对枪支等武器、弹药、管制器具、危险化学品、民用爆炸物品、核与放射物品作出电子追踪标识，对民用爆炸物品添加安检示踪标识物。  
运输单位应当依照规定对运营中的危险化学品、民用爆炸物品、核与放射物品的运输工具通过定位系统实行监控。  
有关单位应当依照规定对传染病病原体等物质实行严格的监督管理，严密防范传染病病原体等物质扩散或者流入非法渠道。  
对管制器具、危险化学品、民用爆炸物品，国务院有关主管部门或者省级人民政府根据需要，在特定区域、特定时间，可以决定对生产、进出口、运输、销售、使用、报废实施管制，可以禁止使用现金、实物进行交易或者对交易活动作出其他限制。

**Article 22** Production and import units shall, in accordance with regulations, affix electronic tracking identifiers to firearms, other weapons, ammunition, controlled instruments, dangerous chemicals, civil explosives, and nuclear and radioactive items, and add security trace identifiers to civil explosives.  
Transport units shall, in accordance with regulations, monitor vehicles transporting dangerous chemicals, civil explosives, and nuclear and radioactive items in operation through positioning systems.  
Relevant units shall, in accordance with regulations, exercise strict supervision and control over substances such as infectious disease pathogens, and strictly prevent such substances from spreading or flowing into illegal channels.  
For controlled instruments, dangerous chemicals, and civil explosives, the relevant competent department of the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, as needed, decide to impose controls on production, import, export, transportation, sale, use, and disposal in specific areas or during specific periods, and may prohibit the use of cash or in-kind transactions or impose other restrictions on transaction activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　发生枪支等武器、弹药、危险化学品、民用爆炸物品、核与放射物品、传染病病原体等物质被盗、被抢、丢失或者其他流失的情形，案发单位应当立即采取必要的控制措施，并立即向公安机关报告，同时依照规定向有关主管部门报告。公安机关接到报告后，应当及时开展调查。有关主管部门应当配合公安机关开展工作。  
任何单位和个人不得非法制作、生产、储存、运输、进出口、销售、提供、购买、使用、持有、报废、销毁前款规定的物品。公安机关发现的，应当予以扣押；其他主管部门发现的，应当予以扣押，并立即通报公安机关；其他单位、个人发现的，应当立即向公安机关报告。

**Article 23** If firearms, other weapons, ammunition, dangerous chemicals, civil explosives, nuclear and radioactive items, infectious disease pathogens, or other substances are stolen, robbed, lost, or otherwise go missing, the unit where the incident occurred shall immediately take necessary control measures, and shall immediately report to the public security organ, and concurrently report to the relevant competent department in accordance with regulations. Upon receiving the report, the public security organ shall promptly conduct an investigation. The relevant competent departments shall cooperate with the public security organ in carrying out the work.  
No unit or individual shall illegally manufacture, produce, store, transport, import, export, sell, provide, purchase, use, possess, dispose of, or destroy the items specified in the preceding paragraph. If discovered by the public security organ, they shall be seized; if discovered by other competent departments, they shall be seized and the public security organ shall be immediately notified; if discovered by other units or individuals, they shall immediately report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门、国务院有关部门、机构依法对金融机构和特定非金融机构履行反恐怖主义融资义务的情况进行监督管理。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门发现涉嫌恐怖主义融资的，可以依法进行调查，采取临时冻结措施。

**Article 24** The anti-money laundering administrative department under the State Council and relevant departments and institutions under the State Council shall, in accordance with law, supervise and manage the performance of counterterrorism financing obligations by financial institutions and designated non-financial institutions.  
If the anti-money laundering administrative department under the State Council discovers suspected terrorist financing, it may, in accordance with law, conduct an investigation and take temporary freezing measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　审计、财政、税务等部门在依照法律、行政法规的规定对有关单位实施监督检查的过程中，发现资金流入流出涉嫌恐怖主义融资的，应当及时通报公安机关。

**Article 25** If, in the process of conducting supervision and inspection of relevant units in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, the audit, finance, taxation and other departments discover that inflows or outflows of funds are suspected of terrorist financing, they shall promptly notify the public security organ.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海关在对进出境人员携带现金和无记名有价证券实施监管的过程中，发现涉嫌恐怖主义融资的，应当立即通报国务院反洗钱行政主管部门和有管辖权的公安机关。

**Article 26** If, in the process of supervising cash and bearer negotiable securities carried by persons entering or leaving the country, the customs discovers suspected terrorist financing, it shall immediately notify the anti-money laundering administrative department under the State Council and the public security organ with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　地方各级人民政府制定、组织实施城乡规划，应当符合反恐怖主义工作的需要。  
地方各级人民政府应当根据需要，组织、督促有关建设单位在主要道路、交通枢纽、城市公共区域的重点部位，配备、安装公共安全视频图像信息系统等防范恐怖袭击的技防、物防设备、设施。

**Article 27** Local people's governments at all levels shall, when formulating and organizing the implementation of urban and rural plans, meet the needs of counterterrorism work.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall, as needed, organize and urge relevant construction units to equip and install public security video image information systems and other technical and physical prevention equipment and facilities for preventing terrorist attacks at key points of main roads, transportation hubs, and urban public areas.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公安机关和有关部门对宣扬极端主义，利用极端主义危害公共安全、扰乱公共秩序、侵犯人身财产、妨害社会管理的，应当及时予以制止，依法追究法律责任。  
公安机关发现极端主义活动的，应当责令立即停止，将有关人员强行带离现场并登记身份信息，对有关物品、资料予以收缴，对非法活动场所予以查封。  
任何单位和个人发现宣扬极端主义的物品、资料、信息的，应当立即向公安机关报告。

**Article 28** Public security organs and relevant departments shall promptly stop and investigate legal liability according to law for any person who advocates extremism or uses extremism to endanger public security, disrupt public order, infringe on persons or property, or hinder social management.  
If the public security organ discovers extremist activities, it shall order an immediate stop, forcibly remove the relevant persons from the scene and register their identity information, confiscate the relevant articles and materials, and seal up the illegal activity premises.  
Any unit or individual that discovers articles, materials, or information advocating extremism shall immediately report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对被教唆、胁迫、引诱参与恐怖活动、极端主义活动，或者参与恐怖活动、极端主义活动情节轻微，尚不构成犯罪的人员，公安机关应当组织有关部门、村民委员会、居民委员会、所在单位、就读学校、家庭和监护人对其进行帮教。  
监狱、看守所、社区矫正机构应当加强对服刑的恐怖活动罪犯和极端主义罪犯的管理、教育、矫正等工作。监狱、看守所对恐怖活动罪犯和极端主义罪犯，根据教育改造和维护监管秩序的需要，可以与普通刑事罪犯混合关押，也可以个别关押。

**Article 29** For persons who have been instigated, coerced, or enticed into participating in terrorist or extremist activities, or whose participation in such activities is of a minor nature and does not yet constitute a crime, the public security organ shall organize relevant departments, villagers' committees, residents' committees, the unit where they work, the school they attend, their families, and guardians to provide them with assistance and education.  
Prisons, detention centers, and community correction institutions shall strengthen the management, education, and correction of incarcerated terrorist crime offenders and extremist crime offenders. Prisons and detention centers may, based on the needs of education and reform and the maintenance of supervision order, hold terrorist crime offenders and extremist crime offenders either mixed with ordinary criminal offenders or separately.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对恐怖活动罪犯和极端主义罪犯被判处徒刑以上刑罚的，监狱、看守所应当在刑满释放前根据其犯罪性质、情节和社会危害程度，服刑期间的表现，释放后对所居住社区的影响等进行社会危险性评估。进行社会危险性评估，应当听取有关基层组织和原办案机关的意见。经评估具有社会危险性的，监狱、看守所应当向罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院提出安置教育建议，并将建议书副本抄送同级人民检察院。  
罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院对于确有社会危险性的，应当在罪犯刑满释放前作出责令其在刑满释放后接受安置教育的决定。决定书副本应当抄送同级人民检察院。被决定安置教育的人员对决定不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。  
安置教育由省级人民政府组织实施。安置教育机构应当每年对被安置教育人员进行评估，对于确有悔改表现，不致再危害社会的，应当及时提出解除安置教育的意见，报决定安置教育的中级人民法院作出决定。被安置教育人员有权申请解除安置教育。  
人民检察院对安置教育的决定和执行实行监督。

**Article 30** For a terrorist crime offender or extremist crime offender who has been sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a more severe penalty, the prison or detention center shall, before release upon completion of the sentence, conduct a social danger assessment based on the nature, circumstances, and degree of social harm of the crime, the offender's behavior during service of the sentence, and the impact on the community where the offender will reside after release. In conducting the social danger assessment, the opinions of the relevant grassroots organizations and the original case-handling organ shall be solicited. If the assessment indicates social danger, the prison or detention center shall submit a proposal for placement in education to the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the offender is serving the sentence, and shall send a copy of the proposal to the People's Procuratorate at the same level.  
If the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the offender is serving the sentence finds that the offender indeed has social danger, it shall, before the offender is released upon completion of the sentence, make a decision ordering the offender to receive placement in education after release. The copy of the decision shall be sent to the People's Procuratorate at the same level. If the person placed in education disagrees with the decision, he/she may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level.  
Placement in education shall be organized and implemented by the people's government at the provincial level. The placement in education institution shall conduct an annual assessment of the person placed in education; if the person shows genuine repentance and is no longer likely to endanger society, the institution shall promptly propose the release from placement in education and submit it to the Intermediate People's Court that made the placement decision for a decision. The person placed in education has the right to apply for release from placement in education.  
The People's Procuratorate shall supervise the decision and implementation of placement in education.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安机关应当会同有关部门，将遭受恐怖袭击的可能性较大以及遭受恐怖袭击可能造成重大的人身伤亡、财产损失或者社会影响的单位、场所、活动、设施等确定为防范恐怖袭击的重点目标，报本级反恐怖主义工作领导机构备案。

**Article 31** Public security organs shall, together with relevant departments, identify units, places, activities, facilities, etc., that are more likely to be subject to terrorist attacks or that, if attacked, could cause major casualties, property losses, or social impact, as key targets for preventing terrorist attacks, and report them to the counterterrorism work leadership organ at the same level for filing.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　重点目标的管理单位应当履行下列职责：  
（一）制定防范和应对处置恐怖活动的预案、措施，定期进行培训和演练；  
（二）建立反恐怖主义工作专项经费保障制度，配备、更新防范和处置设备、设施；  
（三）指定相关机构或者落实责任人员，明确岗位职责；  
（四）实行风险评估，实时监测安全威胁，完善内部安全管理；  
（五）定期向公安机关和有关部门报告防范措施落实情况。  
重点目标的管理单位应当根据城乡规划、相关标准和实际需要，对重点目标同步设计、同步建设、同步运行符合[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定的技防、物防设备、设施。  
重点目标的管理单位应当建立公共安全视频图像信息系统值班监看、信息保存使用、运行维护等管理制度，保障相关系统正常运行。采集的视频图像信息保存期限不得少于九十日。  
对重点目标以外的涉及公共安全的其他单位、场所、活动、设施，其主管部门和管理单位应当依照法律、行政法规规定，建立健全安全管理制度，落实安全责任。

**Article 32** The management units of key targets shall perform the following duties:  
(1) formulate plans and measures for preventing and responding to terrorist activities, and conduct regular training and drills;  
(2) establish a special fund guarantee system for counterterrorism work, and equip and update prevention and response equipment and facilities;  
(3) designate relevant organs or assign responsible personnel, and clarify job duties;  
(4) carry out risk assessment, monitor security threats in real time, and improve internal security management;  
(5) regularly report to the public security organ and relevant departments on the implementation of prevention measures.  
The management units of key targets shall, in accordance with urban and rural planning, relevant standards, and actual needs, simultaneously design, construct, and operate the technical and physical prevention equipment and facilities that comply with the provisions of Article 27 of this Law for the key targets.  
The management units of key targets shall establish management systems for the duty monitoring of public security video image information systems, information preservation and use, and operation and maintenance, to ensure the normal operation of the relevant systems. The preservation period for collected video image information shall not be less than ninety days.  
For other units, places, activities, and facilities involving public security other than key targets, the competent departments and management units shall, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, establish and improve security management systems and implement security responsibilities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　重点目标的管理单位应当对重要岗位人员进行安全背景审查。对有不适合情形的人员，应当调整工作岗位，并将有关情况通报公安机关。

**Article 33** The management units of key targets shall conduct security background checks on personnel in important positions. For persons who are unsuitable, their positions shall be adjusted, and the relevant information shall be reported to the public security organ.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　大型活动承办单位以及重点目标的管理单位应当依照规定，对进入大型活动场所、机场、火车站、码头、城市轨道交通站、公路长途客运站、口岸等重点目标的人员、物品和交通工具进行安全检查。发现违禁品和管制物品，应当予以扣留并立即向公安机关报告；发现涉嫌违法犯罪人员，应当立即向公安机关报告。

**Article 34** The organizers of large-scale events and the management units of key targets shall, in accordance with regulations, conduct security checks on persons, articles, and vehicles entering key targets such as large-scale event venues, airports, railway stations, docks, urban rail transit stations, long-distance road passenger stations, and ports. If they discover prohibited and controlled articles, they shall detain them and immediately report to the public security organ; if they discover persons suspected of committing illegal or criminal acts, they shall immediately report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对航空器、列车、船舶、城市轨道车辆、公共电汽车等公共交通运输工具，营运单位应当依照规定配备安保人员和相应设备、设施，加强安全检查和保卫工作。

**Article 35** For public transport vehicles such as aircraft, trains, ships, urban rail vehicles, and public buses and trolleybuses, the operating units shall, in accordance with regulations, equip security personnel and corresponding equipment and facilities, and strengthen security inspection and protection work.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公安机关和有关部门应当掌握重点目标的基础信息和重要动态，指导、监督重点目标的管理单位履行防范恐怖袭击的各项职责。  
公安机关、中国人民武装警察部队应当依照有关规定对重点目标进行警戒、巡逻、检查。

**Article 36** Public security organs and relevant departments shall obtain basic information and important dynamics of key targets, and guide and supervise the management units of key targets in performing their duties to prevent terrorist attacks.  
Public security organs and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, conduct guard, patrol, and inspection of key targets.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　飞行管制、民用航空、公安等主管部门应当按照职责分工，加强空域、航空器和飞行活动管理，严密防范针对航空器或者利用飞行活动实施的恐怖活动。

**Article 37** The competent departments for flight control, civil aviation, public security, etc., shall, in accordance with their respective duties, strengthen the management of airspace, aircraft, and flight activities, and rigorously prevent terrorist activities targeting aircraft or committed through flight activities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　各级人民政府和军事机关应当在重点国（边）境地段和口岸设置拦阻隔离网、视频图像采集和防越境报警设施。  
公安机关和中国人民解放军应当严密组织国（边）境巡逻，依照规定对抵离国（边）境前沿、进出国（边）境管理区和国（边）境通道、口岸的人员、交通运输工具、物品，以及沿海沿边地区的船舶进行查验。

**Article 38** People's governments at all levels and military organs shall set up barrier fences, video image capture and anti-crossing alarm facilities at key border (frontier) sections and ports.  
The public security organs and the People's Liberation Army shall strictly organize border (frontier) patrols, and in accordance with regulations, conduct inspection of persons, means of transport, and articles arriving at or leaving the border (frontier) forward areas, entering or leaving border (frontier) management zones and border (frontier) passages and ports, as well as vessels in coastal and border areas.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　出入境证件签发机关、出入境边防检查机关对恐怖活动人员和恐怖活动嫌疑人员，有权决定不准其出境入境、不予签发出境入境证件或者宣布其出境入境证件作废。

**Article 39** The authorities issuing entry and exit documents and the border inspection authorities for entry and exit have the power to decide not to allow terrorist activity individuals and suspected terrorist activity individuals to exit or enter the country, not to issue them entry or exit documents, or to declare their entry or exit documents void.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　海关、出入境边防检查机关发现恐怖活动嫌疑人员或者涉嫌恐怖活动物品的，应当依法扣留，并立即移送公安机关或者国家安全机关。

**Article 40** If the customs or the border inspection authorities for entry and exit discover a suspected terrorist activity individual or an article suspected of being used for terrorist activities, they shall detain them according to law and immediately transfer them to the public security organ or the state security organ.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院外交、公安、国家安全、发展改革、工业和信息化、商务、旅游等主管部门应当建立境外投资合作、旅游等安全风险评估制度，对中国在境外的公民以及驻外机构、设施、财产加强安全保护，防范和应对恐怖袭击。

**Article 41** The competent departments for foreign affairs, public security, state security, development and reform, industry and information technology, commerce, tourism, etc., under the State Council shall establish a security risk assessment system for overseas investment cooperation, tourism, etc., and strengthen the security protection of Chinese citizens abroad as well as Chinese overseas institutions, facilities, and property, to prevent and respond to terrorist attacks.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　驻外机构应当建立健全安全防范制度和应对处置预案，加强对有关人员、设施、财产的安全保护。

**Article 42** Overseas institutions shall establish and improve security prevention systems and response and handling plans, and strengthen the security protection of relevant personnel, facilities, and property.

### 第四章　情报信息

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构建立国家反恐怖主义情报中心，实行跨部门、跨地区情报信息工作机制，统筹反恐怖主义情报信息工作。  
有关部门应当加强反恐怖主义情报信息搜集工作，对搜集的有关线索、人员、行动类情报信息，应当依照规定及时统一归口报送国家反恐怖主义情报中心。  
地方反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当建立跨部门情报信息工作机制，组织开展反恐怖主义情报信息工作，对重要的情报信息，应当及时向上级反恐怖主义工作领导机构报告，对涉及其他地方的紧急情报信息，应当及时通报相关地方。

**Article 43** The State counterterrorism work leadership organ shall establish a National Counterterrorism Intelligence Center, implement a cross-departmental and cross-regional intelligence information working mechanism, and coordinate counterterrorism intelligence information work.  
Relevant departments shall strengthen the collection of counterterrorism intelligence information, and shall, in accordance with regulations, promptly and uniformly report the collected intelligence information on clues, persons, and operations to the National Counterterrorism Intelligence Center.  
Local counterterrorism work leadership organs shall establish cross-departmental intelligence information working mechanisms, organize and carry out counterterrorism intelligence information work, and shall promptly report important intelligence information to the counterterrorism work leadership organ at the next higher level, and shall promptly notify relevant localities of emergency intelligence information involving other localities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　公安机关、国家安全机关和有关部门应当依靠群众，加强基层基础工作，建立基层情报信息工作力量，提高反恐怖主义情报信息工作能力。

**Article 44** Public security organs, state security organs, and relevant departments shall rely on the masses, strengthen grassroots basic work, establish grassroots intelligence information work forces, and enhance the capacity for counterterrorism intelligence information work.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　公安机关、国家安全机关、军事机关在其职责范围内，因反恐怖主义情报信息工作的需要，根据国家有关规定，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦察措施。  
依照前款规定获取的材料，只能用于反恐怖主义应对处置和对恐怖活动犯罪、极端主义犯罪的侦查、起诉和审判，不得用于其他用途。

**Article 45** Public security organs, state security organs, and military organs may, within their respective duties, for the needs of counterterrorism intelligence information work, and in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and after strict approval procedures, adopt technical reconnaissance measures.  
Materials obtained in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph may only be used for counterterrorism response and handling, and for the investigation, prosecution, and adjudication of terrorist activity crimes and extremist crimes, and shall not be used for other purposes.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　有关部门对于在本法第三章规定的安全防范工作中获取的信息，应当根据国家反恐怖主义情报中心的要求，及时提供。

**Article 46** Relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of the National Counterterrorism Intelligence Center, promptly provide information obtained in the security prevention work stipulated in Chapter III of this Law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家反恐怖主义情报中心、地方反恐怖主义工作领导机构以及公安机关等有关部门应当对有关情报信息进行筛查、研判、核查、监控，认为有发生恐怖事件危险，需要采取相应的安全防范、应对处置措施的，应当及时通报有关部门和单位，并可以根据情况发出预警。有关部门和单位应当根据通报做好安全防范、应对处置工作。

**Article 47** The National Counterterrorism Intelligence Center, local counterterrorism work leadership organs, and relevant departments such as public security organs shall screen, assess, verify, and monitor relevant intelligence information, and if they believe there is a danger of a terrorist incident occurring and that corresponding security prevention and response handling measures are needed, they shall promptly notify the relevant departments and units, and may issue early warnings as appropriate. The relevant departments and units shall, based on the notification, properly carry out security prevention and response handling work.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　反恐怖主义工作领导机构、有关部门和单位、个人应当对履行反恐怖主义工作职责、义务过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私予以保密。  
违反规定泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 48** Counterterrorism work leadership organs, relevant departments and units, and individuals shall keep confidential the state secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy that they become aware of in the course of performing their counterterrorism work duties and obligations.  
Any person who, in violation of regulations, divulges state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

### 第五章　调查

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公安机关接到恐怖活动嫌疑的报告或者发现恐怖活动嫌疑，需要调查核实的，应当迅速进行调查。

**Article 49** Upon receiving a report of a suspected terrorist activity or discovering a suspected terrorist activity, if investigation and verification are needed, the public security organ shall promptly conduct an investigation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公安机关调查恐怖活动嫌疑，可以依照有关法律规定对嫌疑人员进行盘问、检查、传唤，可以提取或者采集肖像、指纹、虹膜图像等人体生物识别信息和血液、尿液、脱落细胞等生物样本，并留存其签名。  
公安机关调查恐怖活动嫌疑，可以通知了解有关情况的人员到公安机关或者其他地点接受询问。

**Article 50** When investigating a suspected terrorist activity, the public security organ may, in accordance with relevant laws, interrogate, inspect, or summon the suspect, and may extract or collect biometric identification information such as portraits, fingerprints, and iris images, and biological samples such as blood, urine, and exfoliated cells, and retain the suspect's signature.  
When investigating a suspected terrorist activity, the public security organ may notify persons who are aware of the relevant circumstances to come to the public security organ or another location to receive questioning.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公安机关调查恐怖活动嫌疑，有权向有关单位和个人收集、调取相关信息和材料。有关单位和个人应当如实提供。

**Article 51** : When investigating terrorist suspect, the public security organ shall have the authority to collect and obtain relevant information and materials from the relevant entities and individuals. The relevant entities and individuals shall provide such information and materials truthfully.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公安机关调查恐怖活动嫌疑，经县级以上公安机关负责人批准，可以查询嫌疑人员的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产，可以采取查封、扣押、冻结措施。查封、扣押、冻结的期限不得超过二个月，情况复杂的，可以经上一级公安机关负责人批准延长一个月。

**Article 52** : When investigating terrorist suspect, the public security organ may, upon approval by the person in charge of the public security organ at or above the county level, inquire into the property of the suspect, including deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, etc., and may take measures of sealing up, seizure, or freezing. The period of sealing up, seizure, or freezing shall not exceed two months; if the circumstances are complex, the period may be extended by one month upon approval by the person in charge of the public security organ at the next higher level.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　公安机关调查恐怖活动嫌疑，经县级以上公安机关负责人批准，可以根据其危险程度，责令恐怖活动嫌疑人员遵守下列一项或者多项约束措施：  
（一）未经公安机关批准不得离开所居住的市、县或者指定的处所；  
（二）不得参加大型群众性活动或者从事特定的活动；  
（三）未经公安机关批准不得乘坐公共交通工具或者进入特定的场所；  
（四）不得与特定的人员会见或者通信；  
（五）定期向公安机关报告活动情况；  
（六）将护照等出入境证件、身份证件、驾驶证件交公安机关保存。  
公安机关可以采取电子监控、不定期检查等方式对其遵守约束措施的情况进行监督。  
采取前两款规定的约束措施的期限不得超过三个月。对不需要继续采取约束措施的，应当及时解除。

**Article 53** : When investigating terrorist suspect, the public security organ may, upon approval by the person in charge of the public security organ at or above the county level, and according to the degree of danger, order the terrorist suspect to comply with one or more of the following restrictive measures:  
(a) shall not leave the city or county of residence or a designated place without the approval of the public security organ;  
(b) shall not participate in large-scale mass activities or engage in specific activities;  
(c) shall not take public transportation or enter specific places without the approval of the public security organ;  
(d) shall not meet or communicate with specified persons;  
(e) regularly report to the public security organ on their activities;  
(f) hand over passports and other exit-entry documents, identity cards, and driving licenses to the public security organ for safekeeping.  
The public security organ may supervise compliance with the restrictive measures by means of electronic monitoring, irregular inspections, or other methods.  
The period of the restrictive measures prescribed in the preceding two paragraphs shall not exceed three months. If the restrictive measures are no longer necessary, they shall be lifted in a timely manner.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　公安机关经调查，发现犯罪事实或者犯罪嫌疑人的，应当依照刑事诉讼法的规定立案侦查。本章规定的有关期限届满，公安机关未立案侦查的，应当解除有关措施。

**Article 54** : If, after investigation, the public security organ finds a criminal fact or a criminal suspect, it shall file a case for criminal investigation in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law. If the relevant period prescribed in this Chapter expires and the public security organ has not filed a case for investigation, it shall lift the relevant measures.

### 第六章　应对处置

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家建立健全恐怖事件应对处置预案体系。  
国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当针对恐怖事件的规律、特点和可能造成的社会危害，分级、分类制定国家应对处置预案，具体规定恐怖事件应对处置的组织指挥体系和恐怖事件安全防范、应对处置程序以及事后社会秩序恢复等内容。  
有关部门、地方反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当制定相应的应对处置预案。

**Article 55** : The State shall establish and improve a system of response and handling plans for terrorist incidents.  
The national counterterrorism leading body shall, in light of the patterns, characteristics, and possible social harm of terrorist incidents, develop national response and handling plans by level and category, specifying the organizational command system for responding to and handling terrorist incidents, as well as the procedures for security prevention, response and handling, and post-incident restoration of social order.  
The relevant departments and local counterterrorism leading bodies shall develop corresponding response and handling plans.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　应对处置恐怖事件，各级反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当成立由有关部门参加的指挥机构，实行指挥长负责制。反恐怖主义工作领导机构负责人可以担任指挥长，也可以确定公安机关负责人或者反恐怖主义工作领导机构的其他成员单位负责人担任指挥长。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市发生的恐怖事件或者特别重大恐怖事件的应对处置，由国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构负责指挥；在省、自治区、直辖市范围内发生的涉及多个行政区域的恐怖事件或者重大恐怖事件的应对处置，由省级反恐怖主义工作领导机构负责指挥。

**Article 56** : In responding to and handling terrorist incidents, counterterrorism leading bodies at all levels shall establish command bodies composed of relevant departments, and shall implement a commander-responsibility system. The person in charge of the counterterrorism leading body may serve as the commander, or may designate the person in charge of the public security organ or the person in charge of another member unit of the counterterrorism leading body to serve as the commander.  
The response and handling of a terrorist incident spanning provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, or a particularly serious terrorist incident, shall be directed by the national counterterrorism leading body. The response and handling of a terrorist incident occurring within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government involving multiple administrative regions, or a major terrorist incident, shall be directed by the provincial-level counterterrorism leading body.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　恐怖事件发生后，发生地反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当立即启动恐怖事件应对处置预案，确定指挥长。有关部门和中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队、民兵组织，按照反恐怖主义工作领导机构和指挥长的统一领导、指挥，协同开展打击、控制、救援、救护等现场应对处置工作。  
上级反恐怖主义工作领导机构可以对应对处置工作进行指导，必要时调动有关反恐怖主义力量进行支援。  
需要进入紧急状态的，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者国务院依照宪法和其他有关法律规定的权限和程序决定。

**Article 57** : After a terrorist incident occurs, the counterterrorism leading body in the locality where the incident occurs shall immediately activate the terrorist incident response and handling plan and designate the commander. The relevant departments, the People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organization shall, under the unified leadership and command of the counterterrorism leading body and the commander, coordinate to carry out on-site response and handling operations such as striking, controlling, rescuing, and providing medical assistance.  
The counterterrorism leading body at a higher level may provide guidance on the response and handling operations and, when necessary, mobilize relevant counterterrorism forces for support.  
If it is necessary to enter a state of emergency, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the State Council shall decide in accordance with the powers and procedures prescribed by the Constitution and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　发现恐怖事件或者疑似恐怖事件后，公安机关应当立即进行处置，并向反恐怖主义工作领导机构报告；中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队发现正在实施恐怖活动的，应当立即予以控制并将案件及时移交公安机关。  
反恐怖主义工作领导机构尚未确定指挥长的，由在场处置的公安机关职级最高的人员担任现场指挥员。公安机关未能到达现场的，由在场处置的中国人民解放军或者中国人民武装警察部队职级最高的人员担任现场指挥员。现场应对处置人员无论是否属于同一单位、系统，均应当服从现场指挥员的指挥。  
指挥长确定后，现场指挥员应当向其请示、报告工作或者有关情况。

**Article 58** : Upon discovering a terrorist incident or a suspected terrorist incident, the public security organ shall immediately handle it and report to the counterterrorism leading body. If the People's Liberation Army or the Chinese People's Armed Police Force discovers an ongoing terrorist activity, it shall immediately bring it under control and promptly transfer the case to the public security organ.  
If the counterterrorism leading body has not yet designated a commander, the public security officer with the highest rank on-site shall serve as the on-site commander. If no public security personnel have arrived on-site, the member of the People's Liberation Army or the Chinese People's Armed Police Force with the highest rank on-site shall serve as the on-site commander. All on-site response and handling personnel, regardless of whether they belong to the same unit or system, shall obey the command of the on-site commander.  
After the commander is designated, the on-site commander shall request instructions from and report work or relevant information to the commander.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　中华人民共和国在境外的机构、人员、重要设施遭受或者可能遭受恐怖袭击的，国务院外交、公安、国家安全、商务、金融、国有资产监督管理、旅游、交通运输等主管部门应当及时启动应对处置预案。国务院外交部门应当协调有关国家采取相应措施。  
中华人民共和国在境外的机构、人员、重要设施遭受严重恐怖袭击后，经与有关国家协商同意，国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构可以组织外交、公安、国家安全等部门派出工作人员赴境外开展应对处置工作。

**Article 59** : If the organs, personnel, or important facilities of the People's Republic of China abroad suffer or may suffer a terrorist attack, the competent departments of the State Council for foreign affairs, public security, state security, commerce, finance, state-owned assets supervision and administration, tourism, transportation, etc. shall promptly activate the response and handling plan. The department of foreign affairs of the State Council shall coordinate with the relevant countries to take corresponding measures.  
After the organs, personnel, or important facilities of the People's Republic of China abroad suffer a serious terrorist attack, with the consent of the relevant countries through consultation, the national counterterrorism leading body may organize the departments of foreign affairs, public security, state security, etc. to send staff members abroad to carry out response and handling operations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　应对处置恐怖事件，应当优先保护直接受到恐怖活动危害、威胁人员的人身安全。

**Article 60** : In responding to and handling terrorist incidents, priority shall be given to protecting the personal safety of persons directly harmed or threatened by terrorist activities.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　恐怖事件发生后，负责应对处置的反恐怖主义工作领导机构可以决定由有关部门和单位采取下列一项或者多项应对处置措施：  
（一）组织营救和救治受害人员，疏散、撤离并妥善安置受到威胁的人员以及采取其他救助措施；  
（二）封锁现场和周边道路，查验现场人员的身份证件，在有关场所附近设置临时警戒线；  
（三）在特定区域内实施空域、海（水）域管制，对特定区域内的交通运输工具进行检查；  
（四）在特定区域内实施互联网、无线电、通讯管制；  
（五）在特定区域内或者针对特定人员实施出境入境管制；  
（六）禁止或者限制使用有关设备、设施，关闭或者限制使用有关场所，中止人员密集的活动或者可能导致危害扩大的生产经营活动；  
（七）抢修被损坏的交通、电信、互联网、广播电视、供水、排水、供电、供气、供热等公共设施；  
（八）组织志愿人员参加反恐怖主义救援工作，要求具有特定专长的人员提供服务；  
（九）其他必要的应对处置措施。  
采取前款第三项至第五项规定的应对处置措施，由省级以上反恐怖主义工作领导机构决定或者批准；采取前款第六项规定的应对处置措施，由设区的市级以上反恐怖主义工作领导机构决定。应对处置措施应当明确适用的时间和空间范围，并向社会公布。

**Article 61** : After a terrorist incident occurs, the counterterrorism leading body responsible for response and handling may decide to adopt one or more of the following response and handling measures by the relevant departments and entities:  
(a) Organize rescue and treatment of victims, evacuate, withdraw, and properly relocate threatened persons, and take other relief measures;  
(b) Seal off the scene and surrounding roads, check the identity documents of persons at the scene, and set up temporary cordons near relevant places;  
(c) Implement airspace and sea (water) area control within a specific area, and inspect means of transport within the specific area;  
(d) Implement internet, radio, and communication control within a specific area;  
(e) Implement exit-entry control within a specific area or for specific persons;  
(f) Prohibit or restrict the use of relevant equipment and facilities, close or restrict the use of relevant places, suspend activities with dense crowds or production and business operations that may lead to the expansion of harm;  
(g) Rush-repair damaged public facilities such as transportation, telecommunications, internet, radio and television, water supply, drainage, power supply, gas supply, and heating;  
(h) Organize volunteers to participate in counterterrorism rescue work, and require persons with specific expertise to provide services;  
(i) Other necessary response and handling measures.  
The adoption of the response and handling measures prescribed in subparagraphs (3) to (5) of the preceding paragraph shall be decided or approved by the counterterrorism leading body at or above the provincial level. The adoption of the response and handling measures prescribed in subparagraph (6) of the preceding paragraph shall be decided by the counterterrorism leading body at or above the level of a city divided into districts. Response and handling measures shall clearly specify the time and space scope of application and shall be announced to the public.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　人民警察、人民武装警察以及其他依法配备、携带武器的应对处置人员，对在现场持枪支、刀具等凶器或者使用其他危险方法，正在或者准备实施暴力行为的人员，经警告无效的，可以使用武器；紧急情况下或者警告后可能导致更为严重危害后果的，可以直接使用武器。

**Article 62** : People's police, people's armed police, and other response and handling personnel who are lawfully equipped with or carry weapons may use weapons against persons on the scene who are using firearms, knives, or other lethal weapons, or using other dangerous methods, and who are committing or preparing to commit violent acts, if a warning is ineffective. In an emergency or if giving a warning may result in more serious harmful consequences, they may use weapons directly.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　恐怖事件发生、发展和应对处置信息，由恐怖事件发生地的省级反恐怖主义工作领导机构统一发布；跨省、自治区、直辖市发生的恐怖事件，由指定的省级反恐怖主义工作领导机构统一发布。  
任何单位和个人不得编造、传播虚假恐怖事件信息；不得报道、传播可能引起模仿的恐怖活动的实施细节；不得发布恐怖事件中残忍、不人道的场景；在恐怖事件的应对处置过程中，除新闻媒体经负责发布信息的反恐怖主义工作领导机构批准外，不得报道、传播现场应对处置的工作人员、人质身份信息和应对处置行动情况。

**Article 63** : Information on the occurrence, development, and response and handling of a terrorist incident shall be uniformly released by the provincial-level counterterrorism leading body in the locality where the incident occurs. For terrorist incidents spanning provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, such information shall be uniformly released by a designated provincial-level counterterrorism leading body.  
No entity or individual shall fabricate or disseminate false terrorist incident information; shall not report or disseminate details of the implementation of terrorist activities that may provoke imitation; shall not release cruel or inhumane scenes from a terrorist incident. During the response and handling of a terrorist incident, except where news media are approved by the counterterrorism leading body responsible for releasing information, no entity or individual shall report or disseminate information on the identity of on-site response and handling personnel or hostages, or on the actions of the response and handling operations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　恐怖事件应对处置结束后，各级人民政府应当组织有关部门帮助受影响的单位和个人尽快恢复生活、生产，稳定受影响地区的社会秩序和公众情绪。

**Article 64** : After the response and handling of a terrorist incident is concluded, people's governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments to assist affected entities and individuals in restoring their lives and production as soon as possible, and to stabilize social order and public sentiment in the affected areas.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　当地人民政府应当及时给予恐怖事件受害人员及其近亲属适当的救助，并向失去基本生活条件的受害人员及其近亲属及时提供基本生活保障。卫生、医疗保障等主管部门应当为恐怖事件受害人员及其近亲属提供心理、医疗等方面的援助。

**Article 65** : The local people's government shall promptly provide appropriate assistance to victims of terrorist incidents and their close relatives, and shall promptly provide basic living security to victims and their close relatives who have lost basic living conditions. The competent departments for health, medical security, etc. shall provide psychological, medical, and other forms of assistance to victims of terrorist incidents and their close relatives.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　公安机关应当及时对恐怖事件立案侦查，查明事件发生的原因、经过和结果，依法追究恐怖活动组织、人员的刑事责任。

**Article 66** : The public security organ shall promptly file a case for investigation into the terrorist incident, ascertain the causes, process, and consequences of the incident, and investigate the criminal liability of the terrorist organization or persons according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　反恐怖主义工作领导机构应当对恐怖事件的发生和应对处置工作进行全面分析、总结评估，提出防范和应对处置改进措施，向上一级反恐怖主义工作领导机构报告。

**Article 67** : The counterterrorism leading body shall conduct a comprehensive analysis, summary, and evaluation of the occurrence of the terrorist incident and the response and handling operations, propose improvement measures for prevention and response and handling, and report to the counterterrorism leading body at the next higher level.

### 第七章　国际合作

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　中华人民共和国根据缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照平等互惠原则，与其他国家、地区、国际组织开展反恐怖主义合作。

**Article 68** : The People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the international treaties it has concluded or acceded to, or on the principle of equality and mutual benefit, conduct counterterrorism cooperation with other countries, regions, and international organizations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国务院有关部门根据国务院授权，代表中国政府与外国政府和有关国际组织开展反恐怖主义政策对话、情报信息交流、执法合作和国际资金监管合作。  
在不违背我国法律的前提下，边境地区的县级以上地方人民政府及其主管部门，经国务院或者中央有关部门批准，可以与相邻国家或者地区开展反恐怖主义情报信息交流、执法合作和国际资金监管合作。

**Article 69** : The relevant departments of the State Council shall, as authorized by the State Council, represent the Chinese government in conducting counterterrorism policy dialogue, intelligence information exchange, law enforcement cooperation, and international fund monitoring cooperation with foreign governments and relevant international organizations.  
Without prejudice to Chinese laws, the local people's governments at or above the county level in border areas and their competent departments may, with the approval of the State Council or relevant central departments, conduct counterterrorism intelligence information exchange, law enforcement cooperation, and international fund monitoring cooperation with neighboring countries or regions.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　涉及恐怖活动犯罪的刑事司法协助、引渡和被判刑人移管，依照有关法律规定执行。

**Article 70** : Criminal judicial assistance, extradition, and transfer of sentenced persons concerning terrorist activity crimes shall be carried out in accordance with relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　经与有关国家达成协议，并报国务院批准，国务院公安部门、国家安全部门可以派员出境执行反恐怖主义任务。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队派员出境执行反恐怖主义任务，由中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 71** : After reaching an agreement with the relevant country and reporting to the State Council for approval, the public security department and the state security department of the State Council may send personnel abroad to carry out counterterrorism missions.  
The sending of personnel abroad by the People's Liberation Army or the Chinese People's Armed Police Force to carry out counterterrorism missions shall be approved by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　通过反恐怖主义国际合作取得的材料可以在行政处罚、刑事诉讼中作为证据使用，但我方承诺不作为证据使用的除外。

**Article 72** : Materials obtained through counterterrorism international cooperation may be used as evidence in administrative penalties and criminal proceedings, except where China has undertaken not to use them as evidence.

### 第八章　保障措施

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当按照事权划分，将反恐怖主义工作经费分别列入同级财政预算。  
国家对反恐怖主义重点地区给予必要的经费支持，对应对处置大规模恐怖事件给予经费保障。

**Article 73** : The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of powers, include counterterrorism work funds in their respective fiscal budgets at the same level.  
The State shall provide necessary financial support to key areas for counterterrorism and shall ensure funding for responding to and handling large-scale terrorist incidents.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　公安机关、国家安全机关和有关部门，以及中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队，应当依照法律规定的职责，建立反恐怖主义专业力量，加强专业训练，配备必要的反恐怖主义专业设备、设施。  
县级、乡级人民政府根据需要，指导有关单位、村民委员会、居民委员会建立反恐怖主义工作力量、志愿者队伍，协助、配合有关部门开展反恐怖主义工作。

**Article 74** : The public security organs, state security organs, and relevant departments, as well as the People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, shall, in accordance with their legally prescribed duties, establish professional counterterrorism forces, strengthen professional training, and equip themselves with necessary professional counterterrorism equipment and facilities.  
People's governments at the county and township levels shall, as needed, guide relevant entities, villagers' committees, and residents' committees in establishing counterterrorism work forces and volunteer teams to assist and cooperate with relevant departments in carrying out counterterrorism work.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　对因履行反恐怖主义工作职责或者协助、配合有关部门开展反恐怖主义工作导致伤残或者死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予相应的待遇。

**Article 75** : Persons who are disabled or killed due to performing counterterrorism duties or assisting and cooperating with relevant departments in carrying out counterterrorism work shall be given corresponding benefits in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　因报告和制止恐怖活动，在恐怖活动犯罪案件中作证，或者从事反恐怖主义工作，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全面临危险的，经本人或者其近亲属提出申请，公安机关、有关部门应当采取下列一项或者多项保护措施：  
（一）不公开真实姓名、住址和工作单位等个人信息；  
（二）禁止特定的人接触被保护人员；  
（三）对人身和住宅采取专门性保护措施；  
（四）变更被保护人员的姓名，重新安排住所和工作单位；  
（五）其他必要的保护措施。  
公安机关、有关部门应当依照前款规定，采取不公开被保护单位的真实名称、地址，禁止特定的人接近被保护单位，对被保护单位办公、经营场所采取专门性保护措施，以及其他必要的保护措施。

**Article 76** : If the personal safety of a person who reports or stops terrorist activities, testifies in a terrorist activity criminal case, or engages in counterterrorism work, or the personal safety of their close relatives, is in danger, upon application by the person or their close relatives, the public security organ and relevant departments shall take one or more of the following protection measures:  
(a) Not disclose personal information such as the real name, address, and work unit;  
(b) Prohibit specific persons from contacting the protected person;  
(c) Take special protective measures for the person and their residence;  
(d) Change the name of the protected person and rearrange their residence and work unit;  
(e) Other necessary protection measures.  
The public security organ and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, take measures such as not disclosing the real name and address of the protected entity, prohibiting specific persons from approaching the protected entity, taking special protective measures for the entity's office and business premises, and other necessary protection measures.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国家鼓励、支持反恐怖主义科学研究和技术创新，开发和推广使用先进的反恐怖主义技术、设备。

**Article 77** : The State shall encourage and support counterterrorism scientific research and technological innovation, and develop and promote the use of advanced counterterrorism technologies and equipment.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　公安机关、国家安全机关、中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队因履行反恐怖主义职责的紧急需要，根据国家有关规定，可以征用单位和个人的财产。任务完成后应当及时归还或者恢复原状，并依照规定支付相应费用；造成损失的，应当补偿。  
因开展反恐怖主义工作对有关单位和个人的合法权益造成损害的，应当依法给予赔偿、补偿。有关单位和个人有权依法请求赔偿、补偿。

**Article 78** : The public security organs, state security organs, the People's Liberation Army, and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force may, due to urgent needs in performing counterterrorism duties and in accordance with relevant state provisions, requisition property of entities and individuals. After the mission is completed, they shall promptly return the property or restore it to its original condition, and pay the corresponding fees in accordance with regulations; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made.  
If damage is caused to the lawful rights and interests of relevant entities and individuals due to counterterrorism work, compensation shall be given in accordance with law. The relevant entities and individuals shall have the right to claim compensation in accordance with law.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　组织、策划、准备实施、实施恐怖活动，宣扬恐怖主义，煽动实施恐怖活动，非法持有宣扬恐怖主义的物品，强制他人在公共场所穿戴宣扬恐怖主义的服饰、标志，组织、领导、参加恐怖活动组织，为恐怖活动组织、恐怖活动人员、实施恐怖活动或者恐怖活动培训提供帮助的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 79** : Whoever organizes, plans, prepares to carry out, or carries out terrorist activities; advocates terrorism; incites the carrying out of terrorist activities; illegally holds articles advocating terrorism; compels others to wear clothing or symbols that advocate terrorism in public places; organizes, leads, or participates in a terrorist organization; or provides assistance to a terrorist organization, terrorist personnel, the carrying out of terrorist activities, or terrorist activity training shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　参与下列活动之一，情节轻微，尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款：  
（一）宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义或者煽动实施恐怖活动、极端主义活动的；  
（二）制作、传播、非法持有宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义的物品的；  
（三）强制他人在公共场所穿戴宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义的服饰、标志的；  
（四）为宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义或者实施恐怖主义、极端主义活动提供信息、资金、物资、劳务、技术、场所等支持、协助、便利的。

**Article 80** : Whoever engages in any of the following activities, if the circumstances are minor and the act does not constitute a crime, shall be detained by the public security organ for not less than ten days and not more than fifteen days, and may also be fined not more than 10,000 yuan:  
(a) Advocating terrorism or extremism, or inciting the carrying out of terrorist or extremist activities;  
(b) Producing, disseminating, or illegally holding articles that advocate terrorism or extremism;  
(c) Compelling others to wear clothing or symbols that advocate terrorism or extremism in public places;  
(d) Providing support, assistance, or convenience such as information, funds, materials, labor, technology, or venues for advocating terrorism or extremism or for carrying out terrorist or extremist activities.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　利用极端主义，实施下列行为之一，情节轻微，尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处五日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款：  
（一）强迫他人参加宗教活动，或者强迫他人向宗教活动场所、宗教教职人员提供财物或者劳务的；  
（二）以恐吓、骚扰等方式驱赶其他民族或者有其他信仰的人员离开居住地的；  
（三）以恐吓、骚扰等方式干涉他人与其他民族或者有其他信仰的人员交往、共同生活的；  
（四）以恐吓、骚扰等方式干涉他人生活习俗、方式和生产经营的；  
（五）阻碍国家机关工作人员依法执行职务的；  
（六）歪曲、诋毁国家政策、法律、行政法规，煽动、教唆抵制人民政府依法管理的；  
（七）煽动、胁迫群众损毁或者故意损毁居民身份证、户口簿等国家法定证件以及人民币的；  
（八）煽动、胁迫他人以宗教仪式取代结婚、离婚登记的；  
（九）煽动、胁迫未成年人不接受义务教育的；  
（十）其他利用极端主义破坏国家法律制度实施的。

**Article 81** : Whoever uses extremism and commits any of the following acts, if the circumstances are minor and the act does not constitute a crime, shall be detained by the public security organ for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days, and may also be fined not more than 10,000 yuan:  
(a) Forcing others to participate in religious activities, or forcing others to provide property or labor to religious activity sites or religious personnel;  
(b) Driving persons of other ethnic groups or with other beliefs away from their places of residence by means of intimidation, harassment, or other methods;  
(c) Interfering with the interactions or cohabitation of others with persons of other ethnic groups or with other beliefs by means of intimidation, harassment, or other methods;  
(d) Interfering with the life customs, ways, or production and business operations of others by means of intimidation, harassment, or other methods;  
(e) Obstructing state functionaries from performing their duties in accordance with law;  
(f) Distorting or slandering state policies, laws, or administrative regulations, or instigating or inciting resistance to lawful administration by the people's government;  
(g) Inciting or coercing the public to destroy or intentionally destroy state-issued documents such as resident identity cards or household registers, or the Renminbi;  
(h) Inciting or coercing others to replace marriage or divorce registration with religious ceremonies;  
(i) Inciting or coercing minors to not accept compulsory education;  
(j) Other acts that use extremism to undermine the implementation of the national legal system.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　明知他人有恐怖活动犯罪、极端主义犯罪行为，窝藏、包庇，情节轻微，尚不构成犯罪的，或者在司法机关向其调查有关情况、收集有关证据时，拒绝提供的，由公安机关处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 82** : Whoever knowingly harbors or covers up another person's terrorist activity crime or extremist activity crime, if the circumstances are minor and the act does not constitute a crime, or refuses to provide relevant information or evidence when a judicial organ investigates the relevant information or collects the relevant evidence from them, shall be detained by the public security organ for not less than ten days and not more than fifteen days, and may also be fined not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　金融机构和特定非金融机构对国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构的办事机构公告的恐怖活动组织及恐怖活动人员的资金或者其他资产，未立即予以冻结的，由公安机关处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并对直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上罚款，并对直接负责的董事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，可以并处五日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 83** : If a financial institution or a specific non-financial institution fails to immediately freeze the funds or other assets of a terrorist organization or terrorist personnel publicly announced by the working office of the national counterterrorism leading body, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan on the institution, and shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible directors, senior officers, and other directly liable persons. If the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible directors, senior officers, and other directly liable persons, and may also impose detention for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者有下列情形之一的，由主管部门处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，可以由公安机关对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五日以上十五日以下拘留：  
（一）未依照规定为公安机关、国家安全机关依法进行防范、调查恐怖活动提供技术接口和解密等技术支持和协助的；  
（二）未按照主管部门的要求，停止传输、删除含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息，保存相关记录，关闭相关网站或者关停相关服务的；  
（三）未落实网络安全、信息内容监督制度和安全技术防范措施，造成含有恐怖主义、极端主义内容的信息传播，情节严重的。

**Article 84** : If a telecommunications service operator or internet service provider commits any of the following circumstances, the competent department shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons, and the public security organ may detain the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days:  
(a) Failing to provide technical interfaces, decryption, or other technical support and assistance to the public security organ or state security organ for their lawful prevention and investigation of terrorist activities in accordance with regulations;  
(b) Failing to stop transmission or delete information containing terrorist or extremist content, preserve relevant records, close relevant websites, or suspend relevant services as required by the competent department;  
(c) Failing to implement cybersecurity and information content supervision systems and security technical prevention measures, resulting in the dissemination of information containing terrorist or extremist content, if the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　铁路、公路、水上、航空的货运和邮政、快递等物流运营单位有下列情形之一的，由主管部门处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未实行安全查验制度，对客户身份进行查验，或者未依照规定对运输、寄递物品进行安全检查或者开封验视的；  
（二）对禁止运输、寄递，存在重大安全隐患，或者客户拒绝安全查验的物品予以运输、寄递的；  
（三）未实行运输、寄递客户身份、物品信息登记制度的。

**Article 85** : If a logistics operation entity in railway, highway, waterway, or air freight, or in postal or express delivery services, commits any of the following circumstances, the competent department shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(a) Failing to implement a safety inspection system, failing to check the identity of customers, or failing to conduct a safety check or open-package inspection on items for transport or delivery in accordance with regulations;  
(b) Transporting or delivering items that are prohibited from transport or delivery, that have major safety hazards, or that customers refuse to submit to a safety check;  
(c) Failing to implement a system for recording customer identities and item information for transport or delivery.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　电信、互联网、金融业务经营者、服务提供者未按规定对客户身份进行查验，或者对身份不明、拒绝身份查验的客户提供服务的，主管部门应当责令改正；拒不改正的，处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款。  
住宿、长途客运、机动车租赁等业务经营者、服务提供者有前款规定情形的，由主管部门处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十万元以下罚款。

**Article 86** : If a telecommunications, internet, or financial business operator or service provider fails to check the identity of customers in accordance with regulations, or provides services to customers whose identity is unknown or who refuse identity checks, the competent department shall order rectification. If the entity refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons. If the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.  
If a business operator or service provider in accommodation, long-distance passenger transport, motor vehicle rental, or similar services commits the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the competent department shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由主管部门给予警告，并责令改正；拒不改正的，处十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下罚款：  
（一）未依照规定对枪支等武器、弹药、管制器具、危险化学品、民用爆炸物品、核与放射物品作出电子追踪标识，对民用爆炸物品添加安检示踪标识物的；  
（二）未依照规定对运营中的危险化学品、民用爆炸物品、核与放射物品的运输工具通过定位系统实行监控的；  
（三）未依照规定对传染病病原体等物质实行严格的监督管理，情节严重的；  
（四）违反国务院有关主管部门或者省级人民政府对管制器具、危险化学品、民用爆炸物品决定的管制或者限制交易措施的。

**Article 87** : Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following circumstances shall be given a warning by the competent department and ordered to rectify. If the entity refuses to rectify, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(a) Failing to affix electronic tracking identifiers to firearms and other weapons, ammunition, controlled instruments, hazardous chemicals, civil explosives, or nuclear and radioactive materials, or failing to add security inspection tracer markers to civil explosives in accordance with regulations;  
(b) Failing to monitor the vehicles carrying hazardous chemicals, civil explosives, or nuclear and radioactive materials in operation through a positioning system in accordance with regulations;  
(c) Failing to implement strict supervision and management of substances such as pathogens of infectious diseases in accordance with regulations, if the circumstances are serious;  
(d) Violating control or restricted transaction measures for controlled instruments, hazardous chemicals, or civil explosives as determined by the relevant competent department of the State Council or a provincial people's government.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　防范恐怖袭击重点目标的管理、营运单位违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由公安机关给予警告，并责令改正；拒不改正的，处十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下罚款：  
（一）未制定防范和应对处置恐怖活动的预案、措施的；  
（二）未建立反恐怖主义工作专项经费保障制度，或者未配备防范和处置设备、设施的；  
（三）未落实工作机构或者责任人员的；  
（四）未对重要岗位人员进行安全背景审查，或者未将有不适合情形的人员调整工作岗位的；  
（五）对公共交通运输工具未依照规定配备安保人员和相应设备、设施的；  
（六）未建立公共安全视频图像信息系统值班监看、信息保存使用、运行维护等管理制度的。  
大型活动承办单位以及重点目标的管理单位未依照规定对进入大型活动场所、机场、火车站、码头、城市轨道交通站、公路长途客运站、口岸等重点目标的人员、物品和交通工具进行安全检查的，公安机关应当责令改正；拒不改正的，处十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 88** : If the management or operation entity of a key target for counterterrorism protection violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall give a warning and order rectification. If the entity refuses to rectify, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(a) Failing to formulate plans and measures for preventing and responding to terrorist activities;  
(b) Failing to establish a special fund guarantee system for counterterrorism work, or failing to equip itself with prevention and handling equipment and facilities;  
(c) Failing to designate a working body or responsible person;  
(d) Failing to conduct security background checks on persons in important positions, or failing to transfer persons who are unsuitable out of their positions;  
(e) Failing to equip public transport vehicles with security personnel and corresponding equipment and facilities in accordance with regulations;  
(f) Failing to establish management systems for the shift monitoring, information preservation and use, operation and maintenance of public security video image information systems.  
If the organizer of a large-scale event or the management entity of a key target fails to conduct a security check on persons, items, and means of transport entering the venue of the large-scale event, airport, railway station, port, urban rail transit station, long-distance bus station, border port, or other key target in accordance with regulations, the public security organ shall order rectification. If the entity refuses to rectify, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　恐怖活动嫌疑人员违反公安机关责令其遵守的约束措施的，由公安机关给予警告，并责令改正；拒不改正的，处五日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 89** : If a terrorist suspect violates the restrictive measures ordered by the public security organ, the public security organ shall give a warning and order rectification. If the person refuses to rectify, detention for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days shall be imposed.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　新闻媒体等单位编造、传播虚假恐怖事件信息，报道、传播可能引起模仿的恐怖活动的实施细节，发布恐怖事件中残忍、不人道的场景，或者未经批准，报道、传播现场应对处置的工作人员、人质身份信息和应对处置行动情况的，由公安机关处二十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五万元以下罚款。  
个人有前款规定行为的，由公安机关处五日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 90** : If a news media or other entity fabricates or disseminates false terrorist incident information, reports or disseminates details of the implementation of terrorist activities that may provoke imitation, releases cruel or inhumane scenes from a terrorist incident, or, without approval, reports or disseminates information on the identity of on-site response and handling personnel or hostages, or on the actions of the response and handling operations, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan, and shall detain the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days, and may also impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.  
If an individual commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the public security organ shall detain the individual for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days, and may also impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　拒不配合有关部门开展反恐怖主义安全防范、情报信息、调查、应对处置工作的，由主管部门处二千元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处五日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一万元以下罚款。  
单位有前款规定行为的，由主管部门处五万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处十万元以下罚款；并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 91** : Whoever refuses to cooperate with relevant departments in carrying out counterterrorism security prevention, intelligence information, investigation, or response and handling work shall be fined not more than 2,000 yuan by the competent department. If serious consequences are caused, detention for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days shall be imposed, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan may also be imposed.  
If an entity commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the competent department shall impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan. If serious consequences are caused, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be penalized in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　阻碍有关部门开展反恐怖主义工作的，由公安机关处五日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五万元以下罚款。  
单位有前款规定行为的，由公安机关处二十万元以下罚款，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照前款规定处罚。  
阻碍人民警察、人民解放军、人民武装警察依法执行职务的，从重处罚。

**Article 92** : Whoever obstructs the relevant departments in carrying out counterterrorism work shall be detained by the public security organ for not less than five days and not more than fifteen days, and may also be fined not more than 50,000 yuan.  
If an entity commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan, and shall penalize the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
Whoever obstructs the people's police, the People's Liberation Army, or the People's Armed Police from lawfully performing their duties shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　单位违反本法规定，情节严重的，由主管部门责令停止从事相关业务、提供相关服务或者责令停产停业；造成严重后果的，吊销有关证照或者撤销登记。

**Article 93** : If an entity violates the provisions of this Law and the circumstances are serious, the competent department shall order it to cease the relevant business or provision of services, or order it to suspend production or operations. If serious consequences are caused, the relevant licenses shall be revoked or the registration shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　反恐怖主义工作领导机构、有关部门的工作人员在反恐怖主义工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者有违反规定泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私等行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。  
反恐怖主义工作领导机构、有关部门及其工作人员在反恐怖主义工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者有其他违法违纪行为的，任何单位和个人有权向有关部门检举、控告。有关部门接到检举、控告后，应当及时处理并回复检举、控告人。

**Article 94** : If personnel of a counterterrorism leading body or a relevant department, in the course of counterterrorism work, abuse their power, neglect their duty, engage in malpractice for personal gain, or violate regulations by disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law. If the act does not constitute a crime, disciplinary action shall be taken in accordance with law.  
If a counterterrorism leading body, a relevant department, or any of their personnel abuses power, neglects duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or commits other illegal or disciplinary violations in counterterrorism work, any entity or individual shall have the right to report or accuse the matter to the relevant department. The relevant department, upon receiving the report or accusation, shall promptly handle it and give a reply to the reporter or accuser.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　对依照本法规定查封、扣押、冻结、扣留、收缴的物品、资金等，经审查发现与恐怖主义无关的，应当及时解除有关措施，予以退还。

**Article 95** : If, upon review, items, funds, or other things seized, impounded, frozen, detained, or collected in accordance with the provisions of this Law are found to be unrelated to terrorism, the relevant measures shall be promptly lifted and the items shall be returned.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　有关单位和个人对依照本法作出的行政处罚和行政强制措施决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 96** : If a relevant entity or individual disagrees with a decision on an administrative penalty or administrative compulsory measure made in accordance with this Law, they may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action in accordance with law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　本法自2016年1月1日起施行。2011年10月29日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议通过的《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于加强反恐怖工作有关问题的决定》同时废止。

**Article 97** : This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2016. The Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Strengthening Counterterrorism Work adopted at the 23rd Session of the Standing Committee of the 11th National People's Congress on October 29, 2011 shall be abolished at the same time.
