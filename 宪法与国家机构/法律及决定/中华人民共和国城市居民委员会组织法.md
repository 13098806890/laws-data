### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全基层群众自治制度，由城市居民依法办理自己的事情，发展基层民主，维护居民的合法权益，推进基层治理体系和治理能力现代化，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of improving the grassroots self-governance system, enabling urban residents to handle their own affairs according to law, developing grassroots democracy, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of residents, and modernizing the grassroots governance system and governance capacity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　居民委员会是居民自我管理、自我服务、自我教育、自我监督的基层群众性自治组织，实行民主选举、民主协商、民主决策、民主管理、民主监督。  
居民委员会向居民会议、居民代表会议负责并报告工作。

**Article 2** The residents' committee is a mass organization of grassroots self-governance for residents to conduct self-management, self-service, self-education, and self-supervision, and it shall practice democratic election, democratic consultation, democratic decision-making, democratic management, and democratic supervision.  
The residents' committee shall be responsible to and report on its work to the residents' assembly and the residents' representatives' assembly.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　居民委员会根据居民居住状况，按照便于居民自治，有利于基层治理的原则，一般在一千户至三千户的范围内设立，也可以根据实际需要，在适当范围内设立。设立居民委员会的区域称为社区。  
居民委员会的设立、撤销、规模调整，由街道办事处提出，报不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府批准。

**Article 3** The residents' committee shall be established based on the residential conditions of residents, in accordance with the principles of facilitating residents' self-governance and benefiting grassroots governance, generally within the scope of 1,000 to 3,000 households, or may be established within an appropriate scope based on actual needs. The area where a residents' committee is established shall be called a community.  
The establishment, dissolution, or scale adjustment of a residents' committee shall be proposed by the sub-district office and shall be submitted to the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city for approval.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　居民委员会工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持和发展全过程人民民主，坚持自治、法治、德治相结合。  
中国共产党在社区的基层组织，按照中国共产党章程进行工作，领导和支持居民委员会行使职权；依照宪法和法律，支持和保障居民开展自治活动、直接行使民主权利。

**Article 4** The work of the residents' committee shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, uphold and develop whole-process people's democracy, and combine self-governance, rule of law, and rule of virtue.  
The grassroots organizations of the Communist Party of China in the community shall carry out their work in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China, lead and support the residents' committee in exercising its functions and powers; and in accordance with the Constitution and laws, support and guarantee that residents conduct self-governance activities and directly exercise democratic rights.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央和地方基层群众自治指导监督部门负责指导和监督城市基层群众自治工作。

**Article 5** The central and local departments for guidance and supervision of grassroots self-governance shall be responsible for guiding and supervising urban grassroots self-governance work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府和街道办事处对居民委员会的工作给予指导、支持和帮助。居民委员会协助不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处开展工作。

**Article 6** The people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city and the sub-district office shall provide guidance, support, and assistance to the work of the residents' committee. The residents' committee shall assist the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office in carrying out its work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在居民自治工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to residents' self-governance work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　居民委员会的组成和职责

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　居民委员会由主任、副主任和委员共五至九人组成。  
居民委员会成员中，应当有妇女成员。居民委员会成员实行近亲属回避。  
居民委员会主任可以由社区党组织负责人通过法定程序担任。居民委员会成员和社区党组织领导班子成员可以交叉任职。

**Article 8** A residents' committee shall be composed of a director, deputy directors, and members, totaling five to nine persons.  
Among the members of the residents' committee, there shall be female members. The members of the residents' committee shall implement the avoidance system for close relatives.  
The director of the residents' committee may be held by the person in charge of the community Party organization through legal procedures. The members of the residents' committee and the members of the leadership of the community Party organization may hold cross posts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　居民委员会根据需要设人民调解、治安保卫、公共卫生、环境和物业管理、老年人和妇女儿童工作等委员会。  
居民委员会成员可以兼任下属委员会的成员。居民较少的居民委员会可以不设下属委员会，由居民委员会的成员分工负责有关工作。

**Article 9** The residents' committee shall, as needed, set up committees for people's mediation, public security, public health, environmental and property management, work concerning the elderly and women and children, etc.  
Members of the residents' committee may concurrently serve as members of its subordinate committees. A residents' committee with a small number of residents may not set up subordinate committees, and its members shall be responsible for the relevant work through division of labor.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　居民委员会履行下列职责：  
（一）宣传宪法、法律、法规，宣传党和国家的政策，支持和推动居民履行法律规定的义务，维护居民的合法权益；  
（二）支持和引导居民践行社会主义核心价值观，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，倡导良好社会风俗和文明健康、绿色环保的生活方式；  
（三）办理本社区居民的公共事务和公益事业，开展便民利民的社区服务活动，关心关爱老年人、儿童、残疾人和困难居民；  
（四）调解民间纠纷，促进家庭和睦、邻里和谐、社区安宁；  
（五）协助维护社会治安，组织居民参与群防群治，协助处理信访事项和协调化解矛盾纠纷，协助做好社区矫正工作和刑满释放人员帮教工作，协助做好突发事件应对工作；  
（六）支持和引导社会组织、社会工作者、志愿者等社会力量参与社区治理，提供社区服务；  
（七）指导和协助设立业主大会和选举业主委员会，协助指导和监督业主大会和业主委员会依法履行职责，协助调解物业纠纷；  
（八）协助人民政府或者街道办事处做好与居民利益有关的其他工作；  
（九）向人民政府或者街道办事处反映居民的意见、要求和提出建议。  
多民族居住地区的居民委员会，应当支持和引导居民增进团结、互相尊重、互相帮助。

**Article 10** The residents' committee shall perform the following duties:  
(1) to publicize the Constitution, laws, and regulations, publicize the policies of the Party and the State, support and promote residents to perform their obligations prescribed by law, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of residents;  
(2) to support and guide residents to practice core socialist values, forge a strong sense of community of the Chinese nation, advocate good social customs and a civilized, healthy, green, and environmentally friendly lifestyle;  
(3) to handle public affairs and public welfare undertakings of the residents in the community, carry out community service activities convenient and beneficial to the people, and care for and show concern for the elderly, children, persons with disabilities, and needy residents;  
(4) to mediate civil disputes, promote family harmony, neighborly harmony, and community tranquility;  
(5) to assist in maintaining public security, organize residents to participate in mass prevention and mass governance, assist in handling petition matters and coordinating the resolution of conflicts and disputes, assist in community correction work and the education and assistance of persons released after serving their sentences, and assist in responding to emergencies;  
(6) to support and guide social forces including social organizations, social workers, and volunteers to participate in community governance and provide community services;  
(7) to guide and assist in the establishment of the owners' assembly and the election of the owners' committee, assist in guiding and supervising the owners' assembly and the owners' committee in performing their duties according to law, and assist in mediating property disputes;  
(8) to assist the people's government or the sub-district office in carrying out other work related to the interests of residents;  
(9) to reflect the opinions, demands of residents and make suggestions to the people's government or the sub-district office.  
In areas inhabited by multiple ethnic groups, the residents' committee shall support and guide residents in enhancing unity, mutual respect, and mutual assistance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　居民委员会及其成员应当遵守宪法、法律、法规，遵守党和国家的政策，遵守并组织实施居民自治章程、居民公约，执行居民会议、居民代表会议的决定、决议，办事公道，廉洁奉公，热心为居民服务，接受居民监督。

**Article 11** The residents' committee and its members shall abide by the Constitution, laws, and regulations, abide by the policies of the Party and the State, abide by and organize the implementation of the residents' self-governance charter and the residents' pact, implement the decisions and resolutions of the residents' assembly and the residents' representatives' assembly, act impartially, be honest and dedicated, serve the residents with enthusiasm, and accept supervision by residents.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　居民委员会具有基层群众性自治组织法人资格，居民委员会主任为法定代表人。居民委员会可以从事为履行职能所需要的民事活动，但是不得提供担保。

**Article 12** The residents' committee shall have the status of a legal person as a mass organization of grassroots self-governance, and the director of the residents' committee shall be the legal representative. The residents' committee may engage in civil activities necessary for performing its functions, but shall not provide guarantees.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　居民委员会设立若干居民小组。居民小组在居民委员会的组织下开展活动。  
居民代表由居民小组一般按每二十户至五十户推选一人产生，也可以根据实际需要在适当范围内推选产生。居民小组组长由居民小组从居民代表中推选。居民小组组长和居民代表的任期与居民委员会的任期相同，可以连选连任。  
居民代表应当向所在居民小组负责，接受居民监督。

**Article 13** The residents' committee shall establish a number of residents' groups. The residents' groups shall carry out activities under the organization of the residents' committee.  
Residents' representatives shall be elected by residents' groups generally at the ratio of one representative for every 20 to 50 households, or may be elected within an appropriate scope based on actual needs. The group leader of a residents' group shall be elected by the residents' group from among the residents' representatives. The term of office of the group leader of a residents' group and the residents' representatives shall be the same as that of the residents' committee, and they may be re-elected.  
Residents' representatives shall be responsible to the residents' group to which they belong and accept supervision by residents.

### 第三章　居民委员会的选举

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　居民委员会主任、副主任和委员，由本社区全体有选举权的居民或者由每户派代表选举产生；根据居民意见，也可以由居民代表选举产生。  
居民委员会每届任期五年，届满应当及时举行换届选举，居民委员会成员可以连选连任。

**Article 14** The director, deputy directors, and members of the residents' committee shall be elected by all residents in the community who have the right to vote or by representatives elected by each household; or, based on the opinions of residents, may be elected by residents' representatives.  
The term of office of each residents' committee shall be five years, and upon expiration, a reelection shall be held in a timely manner. Members of the residents' committee may be re-elected.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　居民委员会的选举，由居民选举委员会主持。  
居民选举委员会由主任、副主任和委员组成，由居民会议或者居民代表会议推选产生。  
居民选举委员会成员或者其近亲属被提名为居民委员会成员候选人的，应当退出居民选举委员会。  
居民选举委员会成员退出居民选举委员会或者因其他原因出缺的，按照原推选结果依次递补，也可以另行推选。

**Article 15** The election of the residents' committee shall be presided over by the residents' election committee.  
The residents' election committee shall be composed of a director, deputy directors, and members, and shall be elected by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly.  
If a member of the residents' election committee or his or her close relative is nominated as a candidate for a member of the residents' committee, such member shall withdraw from the residents' election committee.  
If a member of the residents' election committee withdraws from the residents' election committee or becomes vacant due to other reasons, the successor shall be determined in order according to the original election results, or a new election may be held.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　年满十八周岁的居民，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、家庭出身、宗教信仰、教育程度、财产状况、居住期限，都有选举权和被选举权；但是，依照法律被剥夺政治权利的人除外。  
居民委员会选举前，应当对下列表示参加选举的人员进行登记：  
（一）户籍在本社区的居民；  
（二）户籍不在本社区但在本社区常住的居民。  
户籍不在本社区，在本社区工作六个月以上的社区工作者，本人申请参加选举的，由居民选举委员会确认并进行登记。

**Article 16** Residents who have reached the age of 18 shall have the right to vote and stand for election, irrespective of ethnic status, race, gender, occupation, family background, religious belief, education level, property status, or length of residence, except persons who are deprived of political rights according to law.  
Before the election of the residents' committee, registration shall be conducted for the following persons who express their intention to participate in the election:  
(1) residents whose household registration is in the community;  
(2) residents whose household registration is not in the community but who ordinarily reside in the community.  
For a community worker whose household registration is not in the community but who has worked in the community for more than six months, if he or she applies to participate in the election, the residents' election committee shall confirm and register him or her.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经登记的选民名单应当在选举日的二十日前公布；采取每户派代表或者居民代表选举方式的，应当同时公布户的代表或者新一届居民代表名单。  
对名单有异议的，应当自名单公布之日起五日内向居民选举委员会提出申诉，居民选举委员会应当自收到申诉之日起三日内作出处理决定，并公布处理结果。

**Article 17** The list of registered voters shall be published 20 days before the election date; if the election method of representatives dispatched by each household or residents' representatives is adopted, the list of representatives of households or the list of new residents' representatives shall be published at the same time.  
If any person has an objection to the list, he or she shall lodge a petition with the residents' election committee within five days from the date of publication of the list, and the residents' election committee shall make a handling decision within three days from the date of receipt of the petition and publish the handling result.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　居民委员会成员候选人由社区党组织或者十名以上选民联合提名推荐。提名推荐候选人，应当从全体居民利益出发，推荐拥护中国共产党的领导、奉公守法、品行良好、公道正派、热心公益、具有一定文化水平和工作能力的人为候选人。被开除中国共产党党籍，因犯罪受过刑事处罚，利用黑恶势力从事非法活动，组织或者参加非法宗教活动或者邪教活动的，不得作为候选人。候选人的名额应当多于应选名额。居民选举委员会应当组织候选人与居民见面，由候选人介绍履行职责的设想，回答居民提出的问题。

**Article 18** Candidates for members of the residents' committee shall be nominated and recommended by the community Party organization or jointly by more than ten voters. When nominating and recommending candidates, the interests of all residents shall be taken as the starting point, and persons who uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, abide by the law, have good conduct, are fair and upright, are enthusiastic about public welfare, and have a certain level of education and work ability shall be recommended as candidates. Persons expelled from the Communist Party of China, subject to criminal punishment for crimes, who have used mafia-style gangs to engage in illegal activities, or who have organized or participated in illegal religious activities or cult activities shall not be candidates. The number of candidates shall be greater than the number of positions to be filled. The residents' election committee shall organize meetings between candidates and residents, where candidates shall introduce their vision for performing their duties and answer questions raised by residents.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　选举居民委员会，采取全体有选举权的居民选举方式的，有选民的过半数参加投票，选举有效；采取每户派代表选举方式的，有户的代表的过半数参加投票，选举有效；采取居民代表选举方式的，有超过三分之二的居民代表参加投票，选举有效。  
候选人获得参加投票的人员过半数的选票，始得当选。当选人数不足应选名额的，不足的名额另行选举。另行选举的，第一次投票未当选的人员得票多的为候选人，候选人以得票多的当选，但是所得票数不得少于已投选票总数的三分之一。  
选举实行无记名投票、公开计票的方法，选举结果应当当场公布。选举时，应当设立秘密写票处。  
选举期间外出的选民，可以书面委托本社区其他选民代为投票。每一选民接受的委托不得超过三人。候选人不得接受委托投票。居民选举委员会应当公布委托人和受托人的名单。采取居民代表选举方式的，不实行委托投票。  
具体选举办法由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会规定。

**Article 19** In the election of the residents' committee, if the method of election by all residents with the right to vote is adopted, the election shall be valid if more than half of the voters participate in the voting; if the method of election by representatives dispatched by each household is adopted, the election shall be valid if more than half of the representatives of households participate in the voting; if the method of election by residents' representatives is adopted, the election shall be valid if more than two-thirds of the residents' representatives participate in the voting.  
A candidate shall be elected only if he or she obtains more than half of the votes cast by the persons participating in the voting. If the number of persons elected is less than the number of positions to be filled, a supplementary election shall be held for the remaining positions. In the supplementary election, the persons who failed to be elected in the first round of voting with the highest number of votes shall be candidates, and the candidate with the highest number of votes shall be elected, provided that the number of votes obtained shall not be less than one-third of the total number of votes cast.  
The election shall be conducted by secret ballot and public counting of votes, and the election result shall be announced on the spot. During the election, a secret ballot booth shall be set up.  
Voters who are away during the election period may entrust other voters in the community in writing to vote on their behalf. The number of entrustments accepted by each voter shall not exceed three. Candidates shall not accept entrusted voting. The residents' election committee shall publish the list of principals and trustees. If the election method of residents' representatives is adopted, entrusted voting shall not be implemented.  
Specific election measures shall be formulated by the standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本社区十分之一以上有选举权的居民或者户的代表，或者三分之一以上的居民代表联名，可以提出罢免居民委员会成员的要求，并说明要求罢免的理由。被提出罢免的居民委员会成员有权提出申辩意见。  
罢免居民委员会成员，应当按照产生时的选举方式组织进行投票，须有选民或者户的代表过半数或者超过三分之二的居民代表投票，并须经投票人员的过半数通过。

**Article 20** More than one-tenth of the residents with the right to vote in the community, or representatives of households, or more than one-third of the residents' representatives may jointly submit a request to recall a member of the residents' committee, and shall state the reasons for the recall request. The member of the residents' committee subject to recall shall have the right to present a defense.  
The recall of a member of the residents' committee shall be organized and voted in accordance with the election method used at the time of his or her election. It shall require the participation of more than half of the voters or representatives of households, or more than two-thirds of the residents' representatives, and shall be adopted by more than half of the persons who cast votes.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　居民委员会成员可以向居民委员会提出辞职申请，其职务自居民会议或者居民代表会议审议通过之日起终止。

**Article 21** A member of the residents' committee may submit a resignation application to the residents' committee, and his or her position shall terminate from the date of approval by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly upon deliberation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　以暴力、威胁、欺骗、贿赂、伪造选票、虚报选举票数等不正当手段当选居民委员会成员的，当选无效。  
对以暴力、威胁、欺骗、贿赂、伪造选票、虚报选举票数等不正当手段，妨害居民行使选举权、被选举权，破坏居民委员会选举的行为，居民有权向不设区的市、市辖区人民代表大会常务委员会举报，也可以向街道办事处或者不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府及其有关主管部门举报，由街道办事处或者不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府负责调查并依法处理。

**Article 22** Where a person is elected as a member of the residents' committee by improper means such as violence, threats, deception, bribery, forging ballots, or falsifying election vote counts, the election shall be null and void.  
In the case of acts that hinder residents from exercising the right to vote and the right to stand for election, or sabotage the election of the residents' committee by improper means such as violence, threats, deception, bribery, forging ballots, or falsifying election vote counts, residents shall have the right to report to the standing committee of the people's congress of a city without districts or of a district of a city, or may report to the sub-district office or the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city and its relevant competent departments, and the sub-district office or the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city shall be responsible for investigating and handling the matter according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　居民委员会成员丧失行为能力或者被判处刑罚的，其职务自行终止。

**Article 23** If a member of the residents' committee loses capacity or is sentenced to criminal punishment, his or her position shall automatically terminate.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　居民委员会成员出缺，可以通过居民会议或者居民代表会议补选。  
补选程序参照[本法第十八条](#art-18)、第十九条的规定办理。补选的居民委员会成员的任期到本届居民委员会任期届满时止。

**Article 24** If a vacancy occurs among the members of the residents' committee, a by-election may be held by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly.  
The by-election procedures shall be handled by reference to the provisions of Articles 18 and 19 of this Law. The term of office of a by-elected member of the residents' committee shall end upon the expiration of the term of the current residents' committee.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　居民委员会应当自新一届居民委员会产生之日起十日内完成工作移交。工作移交由居民选举委员会主持，由街道办事处监督。

**Article 25** The residents' committee shall complete the handover of work within ten days from the date when the new residents' committee is formed. The work handover shall be presided over by the residents' election committee and supervised by the sub-district office.

### 第四章　居民会议和居民代表会议

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　居民会议由本社区十八周岁以上的居民组成。  
居民会议由居民委员会召集。有十分之一以上的年满十八周岁居民、户的代表或者三分之一以上的居民代表提议，应当召集居民会议。召集居民会议，应当提前十日通知居民；遇有特殊情况的，可以临时通知居民。

**Article 26** The residents' assembly shall be composed of residents in the community who have reached the age of 18.  
The residents' assembly shall be convened by the residents' committee. Where the proposal is made by more than one-tenth of the residents who have reached the age of 18, or representatives of households, or more than one-third of the residents' representatives, the residents' assembly shall be convened. When convening a residents' assembly, the residents shall be notified ten days in advance; under special circumstances, the residents may be notified temporarily.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　居民会议应当有全体十八周岁以上的本社区居民或者户的代表的过半数参加。居民会议所作决定，应当经到会人员的过半数通过。

**Article 27** A residents' assembly shall be attended by more than half of all residents of the community who have reached the age of 18 or representatives of households. The decisions made by a residents' assembly shall be adopted by more than half of the persons present at the meeting.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　居民代表会议由居民委员会成员和居民代表组成。居民代表应当占居民代表会议组成人员的五分之四以上。  
居民代表会议由居民委员会召集，每半年至少召开一次。有五分之一以上的居民代表提议，应当召集居民代表会议。召开居民代表会议，其他居民可以列席会议并发表意见。

**Article 28** The residents' representatives' assembly shall be composed of members of the residents' committee and residents' representatives. Residents' representatives shall account for at least four-fifths of the members of the residents' representatives' assembly.  
The residents' representatives' assembly shall be convened by the residents' committee, and shall be held at least once every half year. Where the proposal is made by more than one-fifth of the residents' representatives, the residents' representatives' assembly shall be convened. When a residents' representatives' assembly is held, other residents may attend the meeting as observers and express their opinions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　居民代表会议应当有三分之二以上的组成人员参加方可召开。居民代表会议所作决定，应当经到会组成人员的过半数通过。

**Article 29** A residents' representatives' assembly shall be held only if more than two-thirds of its members are present. Decisions made by a residents' representatives' assembly shall be adopted by more than half of the members present at the meeting.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　居民会议制定或者修改居民自治章程、居民公约；审议居民委员会的年度工作报告，评议居民委员会成员的工作；讨论决定其他涉及全体居民利益的重要事项。  
居民会议可以授权居民代表会议讨论决定上述事项。  
居民会议有权撤销或者变更居民代表会议和居民委员会不适当的决定。居民代表会议有权撤销或者变更居民委员会不适当的决定。  
需由居民会议或者居民代表会议决定的重要事项，应当先经社区党组织研究讨论。

**Article 30** The residents' assembly shall formulate or amend the residents' self-governance charter and the residents' pact; review the annual work report of the residents' committee, assess the work of members of the residents' committee; and discuss and decide on other important matters involving the interests of all residents.  
The residents' assembly may authorize the residents' representatives' assembly to discuss and decide on the above matters.  
The residents' assembly shall have the power to revoke or alter inappropriate decisions made by the residents' representatives' assembly and the residents' committee. The residents' representatives' assembly shall have the power to revoke or alter inappropriate decisions made by the residents' committee.  
Important matters that need to be decided by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly shall first be studied and discussed by the community Party organization.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　居民自治章程、居民公约应当报不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处备案。  
居民自治章程、居民公约以及居民会议或者居民代表会议的决定不得与宪法、法律、法规相抵触，不得违背公序良俗，不得有侵犯居民的人身权利、民主权利和财产权利的内容。  
居民自治章程、居民公约以及居民会议或者居民代表会议的决定违反前款规定的，由不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处责令改正。

**Article 31** The residents' self-governance charter and the residents' pact shall be reported to the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office for filing.  
The residents' self-governance charter, the residents' pact, and the decisions of the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly shall not contravene the Constitution, laws, or regulations, shall not violate public order and good customs, and shall not contain provisions infringing upon the personal rights, democratic rights, or property rights of residents.  
If the residents' self-governance charter, the residents' pact, or the decisions of the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly violate the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office shall order corrections.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对涉及居民切身利益的公共事务、公益事业以及居民反映的实际困难和矛盾纠纷，居民委员会应当组织居民及其他利益相关方开展协商。  
协商可以采取议事会、听证会、恳谈会等多种形式，根据需要邀请人民政府有关部门、街道办事处、群团组织、社会组织和人民代表大会代表、政治协商会议委员会委员、相关专业人员等参加。  
对协商确定的事项，居民委员会应当及时组织实施或者监督落实；需要提交居民会议或者居民代表会议的，应当召集会议讨论决定。

**Article 32** With respect to public affairs and public welfare undertakings involving the vital interests of residents, as well as practical difficulties and conflicts and disputes reported by residents, the residents' committee shall organize residents and other interested parties to conduct consultations.  
Consultations may take various forms such as deliberation meetings, hearings, and forums, and as needed, relevant departments of the people's government, sub-district offices, mass organizations, social organizations, deputies to the people's congress, members of the political consultative conference, and relevant professionals may be invited to participate.  
Matters determined through consultation shall be implemented or supervised for implementation by the residents' committee in a timely manner; if the matter needs to be submitted to the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly, a meeting shall be convened to discuss and decide.

### 第五章　民主管理和民主监督

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　居民委员会应当实行少数服从多数的民主决策机制和公开透明的工作原则，建立健全各种工作制度。

**Article 33** The residents' committee shall practice the democratic decision-making mechanism of the minority subordinate to the majority and the work principle of openness and transparency, and shall establish and improve various work systems.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　居民委员会实行居务公开制度。  
居民委员会应当及时公布下列事项，接受居民的监督：  
（一）居民会议或者居民代表会议讨论决定的事项及其实施情况；  
（二）政府拨付和接受社会捐赠的资金、物资的管理使用情况；  
（三）居民委员会所有和使用的设施的管理使用情况；  
（四）社区服务项目实施情况；  
（五）居民委员会组织协商确定的事项及其落实情况；  
（六）居民委员会协助政府开展工作的情况；  
（七）涉及本社区居民利益、居民普遍关心的其他事项。  
居民委员会应当设立居务公开栏，可以采用现代信息技术进行居务公开。公布的期限不得少于十五日。居民委员会应当保证公布事项的真实性，并接受居民的查询。

**Article 34** The residents' committee shall implement a system of openness of community affairs.  
The residents' committee shall promptly publish the following matters and accept supervision by residents:  
(1) matters discussed and decided by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly and the implementation thereof;  
(2) the management and use of funds and materials allocated by the government and donated by society;  
(3) the management and use of facilities owned and used by the residents' committee;  
(4) the implementation of community service projects;  
(5) matters determined through consultation organized by the residents' committee and the implementation thereof;  
(6) the work of the residents' committee in assisting the government;  
(7) other matters involving the interests of residents in the community and of general concern to residents.  
The residents' committee shall set up a bulletin board for community affairs, and may use modern information technology to publish community affairs. The publication period shall not be less than 15 days. The residents' committee shall ensure the authenticity of the published matters and accept inquiries from residents.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　居民委员会不及时公布应当公布的事项或者公布的事项不真实的，居民有权向不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府及其有关主管部门或者街道办事处反映，不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府及其有关主管部门或者街道办事处应当负责调查核实，责令依法公布；经查证确有违法行为的，有关人员应当依法承担责任。

**Article 35** If the residents' committee fails to promptly publish matters that should be published or publishes untrue matters, residents shall have the right to report to the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city and its relevant competent departments, or the sub-district office, and the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city and its relevant competent departments, or the sub-district office shall be responsible for investigating and verifying, and order publication according to law; if the verification confirms that there are illegal acts, the relevant persons shall bear responsibility according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　社区应当建立居务监督委员会，对居民委员会工作进行监督，其成员由居民会议或者居民代表会议在居民或者社区工作者中推选产生。居民委员会成员及其近亲属不得担任居务监督委员会成员。居务监督委员会的任期与居民委员会的任期相同。  
居务监督委员会向居民会议和居民代表会议负责，其成员可以列席居民委员会会议和居民代表会议，参与各项协商活动。  
居务监督委员会在履行职责过程中发现有侵害群众利益等违纪违法行为的，应当向街道办事处或者不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府和监察机关反映。

**Article 36** A community shall establish a community affairs supervision committee to supervise the work of the residents' committee, the members of which shall be elected by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly from among the residents or community workers. Members of the residents' committee and their close relatives shall not serve as members of the community affairs supervision committee. The term of office of the community affairs supervision committee shall be the same as that of the residents' committee.  
The community affairs supervision committee shall be responsible to the residents' assembly and the residents' representatives' assembly, and its members may attend meetings of the residents' committee and the residents' representatives' assembly as non-voting participants and participate in various consultation activities.  
If, in the course of performing its duties, the community affairs supervision committee discovers violations of discipline or law that harm the interests of the masses, it shall report to the sub-district office or the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city and the supervisory organ.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　居民委员会成员接受居民会议或者居民代表会议对其履行职责情况的民主评议。民主评议每年至少进行一次，由居务监督委员会主持，民主评议结果应当及时向居民公布。  
居民委员会成员连续两次被评议不称职的，其职务终止。

**Article 37** Members of the residents' committee shall accept democratic appraisal by the residents' assembly or the residents' representatives' assembly regarding their performance of duties. The democratic appraisal shall be conducted at least once a year, shall be presided over by the community affairs supervision committee, and the results of the democratic appraisal shall be promptly published to residents.  
If a member of the residents' committee is assessed as incompetent in two consecutive appraisals, his or her position shall terminate.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　居民委员会主任实行任期和离任经济责任审计，审计包括下列事项：  
（一）居民委员会财务收支情况；  
（二）政府拨付和接受社会捐赠的资金、物资管理使用情况；  
（三）筹集资金使用情况；  
（四）本社区三分之一以上居民代表或者十分之一以上的居民要求审计的其他事项。  
居民委员会主任的任期和离任经济责任审计，由不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府财政部门、审计部门指导，街道办事处负责组织，审计结果应当公布，其中离任经济责任审计结果应当在下一届居民委员会选举之前公布。

**Article 38** The director of the residents' committee shall be subject to audit of economic responsibility during his or her term of office and upon leaving office, and the audit shall include the following matters:  
(1) the financial revenue and expenditure of the residents' committee;  
(2) the management and use of funds and materials allocated by the government and donated by society;  
(3) the use of raised funds;  
(4) other matters for which an audit is requested by more than one-third of the residents' representatives in the community or more than one-tenth of the residents.  
The audit of the economic responsibility of the director of the residents' committee during his or her term of office and upon leaving office shall be guided by the finance department and the audit department of the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city, and shall be organized by the sub-district office. The audit results shall be published, and the audit results upon leaving office shall be published before the election of the next residents' committee.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　居民委员会和居务监督委员会应当建立居务档案。居务档案应当真实、准确、完整和规范。

**Article 39** The residents' committee and the community affairs supervision committee shall establish community affairs archives. The community affairs archives shall be truthful, accurate, complete, and standardized.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　居民委员会或者居民委员会成员作出的决定侵害居民合法权益的，受侵害的居民可以申请人民法院予以撤销，责任人依法承担法律责任。  
居民委员会不依照法律、法规的规定履行法定义务的，由不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处责令改正。

**Article 40** If a decision made by the residents' committee or a member thereof infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a resident, the aggrieved resident may apply to the People's Court for revocation, and the responsible person shall bear legal liability according to law.  
If the residents' committee fails to perform its statutory duties in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office shall order it to make corrections.

### 第六章　居民委员会工作的保障

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　居民委员会日常运转经费、成员报酬及其标准，由不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者上级人民政府规定并拨付。

**Article 41** The funds for daily operation of the residents' committee, the remuneration of its members, and the standards thereof shall be prescribed and allocated by the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the people's government at a higher level.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　居民委员会开展本社区公益活动或者社区服务项目，可以向街道办事处以及不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府申请经费支持，也可以根据自愿原则向居民或者受益的驻社区单位筹集资金，还可以依法与有公开募捐资格的慈善组织合作募集资金。收支账目应当及时公布，接受居民监督。

**Article 42** Where the residents' committee carries out public welfare activities or community service projects in the community, it may apply for financial support from the sub-district office and the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city, or may raise funds from residents or benefiting units stationed in the community on a voluntary basis, or may cooperate with charitable organizations that have the qualification for public fundraising to raise funds according to law. The income and expenditure accounts shall be published promptly and subject to supervision by residents.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处需要居民委员会协助开展工作，应当提供必要的经费和条件。  
地方各级人民政府应当建立健全整治形式主义为基层减负长效机制，有关部门委托居民委员会协助开展工作的事项，应当符合法律、法规规定，所需经费由委托部门承担。

**Article 43** If the people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office needs the assistance of the residents' committee in carrying out its work, it shall provide necessary funds and conditions.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall establish and improve a long-term mechanism for rectifying formalism and reducing burdens at the grassroots level. Matters for which relevant departments entrust the residents' committee to assist in carrying out work shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations, and the necessary expenses shall be borne by the entrusting department.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　社区综合服务设施建设应当纳入相关规划，由县级以上地方人民政府统筹解决。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当对社区信息化建设进行统筹规划和实施，鼓励和支持居民委员会运用现代信息技术服务居民。

**Article 44** The construction of comprehensive community service facilities shall be included in relevant plans and shall be solved through overall planning by local people's governments at or above the county level.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall make overall plans and implement the construction of community informatization, and encourage and support the residents' committee in using modern information technology to serve residents.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府或者街道办事处应当为居民委员会成员以及其他社区工作者提供培训，帮助其提升政治素质、法治意识、政策水平和服务能力。

**Article 45** The people's government of a city without districts or of a district of a city or the sub-district office shall provide training for members of the residents' committee and other community workers, and help them improve their political quality, awareness of the rule of law, policy level, and service capacity.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　机关、团体、部队、企业事业组织等驻社区单位，不参加本社区的居民委员会，但是应当支持居民委员会的工作。居民委员会组织讨论同驻社区单位有关的问题，需要驻社区单位参加会议时，驻社区单位应当派代表参加。驻社区单位在参与社区治理、提供社区服务中接受居民委员会指导，遵守居民公约，促进社区共建共治共享。

**Article 46** Units stationed in the community, such as State organs, organizations, military units, enterprises, and public institutions, shall not participate in the residents' committee of the community, but shall support the work of the residents' committee. When the residents' committee organizes discussions on issues related to the units stationed in the community and needs such units to attend the meeting, the units stationed in the community shall send representatives to attend. In participating in community governance and providing community services, units stationed in the community shall accept guidance from the residents' committee, abide by the residents' pact, and promote joint construction, joint governance, and shared benefits of the community.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县、自治县、乡、民族乡、镇以及开发区、独立工矿区、林区、垦区等设立居民委员会的，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 47** Where a residents' committee is established in counties, autonomous counties, townships, ethnic townships, towns, as well as development zones, independent mining areas, forest areas, reclamation areas, etc., the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地方各级人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会在本行政区域内保证本法的实施，保障居民依法行使自治权利。

**Article 48** Local people's congresses at all levels and standing committees of local people's congresses at or above the county level shall ensure the implementation of this Law within their respective administrative regions and guarantee that residents exercise their self-governance rights according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法，结合本行政区域的实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 49** The standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and in light of the actual conditions of its administrative region, formulate implementation measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自2026年1月1日起施行。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2026.
