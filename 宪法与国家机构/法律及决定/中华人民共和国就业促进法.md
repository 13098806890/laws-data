# 中华人民共和国就业促进法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2015-04-24  
**生效日期**：2015-04-24

> （2007年8月30日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国电力法〉等六部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　政策支持 第三章　公平就业 第四章　就业服务和管理 第五章　职业教育和培训 第六章　就业援助 第七章　监督检查 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了促进就业，促进经济发展与扩大就业相协调，促进社会和谐稳定，制定本法。 第二条　国家把扩大就业放在经济社会发展的突出位置，实施积极的就业政策，坚持劳动者自主择业、市场调节就业、政府促进就业的方针，多渠道扩大就业。 第三条　劳动者依法享有平等就业和自主择业的权利。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进就业，促进经济发展与扩大就业相协调，促进社会和谐稳定，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of promoting employment, promoting coordination between economic development and employment expansion, and promoting social harmony and stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家把扩大就业放在经济社会发展的突出位置，实施积极的就业政策，坚持劳动者自主择业、市场调节就业、政府促进就业的方针，多渠道扩大就业。

**Article 2** The State shall place the expansion of employment in a prominent position in economic and social development, implement a proactive employment policy, adhere to the principle of workers choosing their own jobs, market regulation of employment, and government promotion of employment, and expand employment through multiple channels.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　劳动者依法享有平等就业和自主择业的权利。  
劳动者就业，不因民族、种族、性别、宗教信仰等不同而受歧视。

**Article 3** Workers shall enjoy the right to equal employment and independent job selection according to law.  
Workers shall not be discriminated against in employment on account of their ethnic group, race, sex, religious belief, etc.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府把扩大就业作为经济和社会发展的重要目标，纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并制定促进就业的中长期规划和年度工作计划。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall take the expansion of employment as an important goal of economic and social development, include it in the national economic and social development plan, and formulate medium- and long-term plans and annual work plans for promoting employment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府通过发展经济和调整产业结构、规范人力资源市场、完善就业服务、加强职业教育和培训、提供就业援助等措施，创造就业条件，扩大就业。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall, through measures such as developing the economy and adjusting the industrial structure, regulating the human resources market, improving employment services, strengthening vocational education and training, and providing employment assistance, create employment conditions and expand employment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建立全国促进就业工作协调机制，研究就业工作中的重大问题，协调推动全国的促进就业工作。国务院劳动行政部门具体负责全国的促进就业工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据促进就业工作的需要，建立促进就业工作协调机制，协调解决本行政区域就业工作中的重大问题。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门按照各自的职责分工，共同做好促进就业工作。

**Article 6** The State Council shall establish a national coordination mechanism for promoting employment, study major issues in employment work, and coordinate and promote the nationwide work of promoting employment. The labor administrative department of the State Council shall be specifically responsible for the nationwide work of promoting employment.  
People's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the needs of promoting employment, establish coordination mechanisms for promoting employment to coordinate and resolve major issues in employment work within their administrative areas.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, jointly carry out the work of promoting employment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家倡导劳动者树立正确的择业观念，提高就业能力和创业能力；鼓励劳动者自主创业、自谋职业。  
各级人民政府和有关部门应当简化程序，提高效率，为劳动者自主创业、自谋职业提供便利。

**Article 7** The State shall advocate that workers establish a correct concept of job selection, improve their employability and entrepreneurship; encourage workers to start their own businesses and seek their own employment.  
People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall simplify procedures, improve efficiency, and provide convenience for workers to start their own businesses and seek their own employment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　用人单位依法享有自主用人的权利。  
用人单位应当依照本法以及其他法律、法规的规定，保障劳动者的合法权益。

**Article 8** Employing units shall enjoy the right to independently employ personnel according to law.  
Employing units shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of workers in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会以及其他社会组织，协助人民政府开展促进就业工作，依法维护劳动者的劳动权利。

**Article 9** Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, and other social organizations shall assist the people's governments in carrying out work to promote employment and safeguard workers' labor rights according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府和有关部门对在促进就业工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall commend and reward units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in promoting employment.

### 第二章　政策支持

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府应当把扩大就业作为重要职责，统筹协调产业政策与就业政策。

**Article 11** People's governments at or above the county level shall take the expansion of employment as an important duty and coordinate industrial policies with employment policies.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励各类企业在法律、法规规定的范围内，通过兴办产业或者拓展经营，增加就业岗位。  
国家鼓励发展劳动密集型产业、服务业，扶持中小企业，多渠道、多方式增加就业岗位。  
国家鼓励、支持、引导非公有制经济发展，扩大就业，增加就业岗位。

**Article 12** The State shall encourage all types of enterprises to increase employment positions by establishing industries or expanding operations within the scope stipulated by laws and regulations.  
The State shall encourage the development of labor-intensive industries and service industries, support small and medium-sized enterprises, and increase employment positions through multiple channels and methods.  
The State shall encourage, support, and guide the development of the non-public sector of the economy to expand employment and increase employment positions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家发展国内外贸易和国际经济合作，拓宽就业渠道。

**Article 13** The State shall develop domestic and foreign trade and international economic cooperation to broaden employment channels.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府在安排政府投资和确定重大建设项目时，应当发挥投资和重大建设项目带动就业的作用，增加就业岗位。

**Article 14** When arranging government investment and determining major construction projects, people's governments at or above the county level shall leverage the role of investment and major construction projects in driving employment and increase employment positions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行有利于促进就业的财政政策，加大资金投入，改善就业环境，扩大就业。  
县级以上人民政府应当根据就业状况和就业工作目标，在财政预算中安排就业专项资金用于促进就业工作。  
就业专项资金用于职业介绍、职业培训、公益性岗位、职业技能鉴定、特定就业政策和社会保险等的补贴，小额贷款担保基金和微利项目的小额担保贷款贴息，以及扶持公共就业服务等。就业专项资金的使用管理办法由国务院财政部门和劳动行政部门规定。

**Article 15** The State shall implement fiscal policies conducive to promoting employment, increase capital investment, improve the employment environment, and expand employment.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the employment situation and employment work objectives, allocate special employment funds in their fiscal budgets for promoting employment.  
Special employment funds shall be used for subsidies for job placement, vocational training, public welfare positions, vocational skill appraisal, specific employment policies, and social insurance, etc., as well as for guarantee funds for small loans and interest subsidies for small guaranteed loans for micro-profit projects, and for supporting public employment services. The measures for the management and use of special employment funds shall be stipulated by the finance department and the labor administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家建立健全失业保险制度，依法确保失业人员的基本生活，并促进其实现就业。

**Article 16** The State shall establish and improve the unemployment insurance system, ensure the basic livelihood of unemployed persons according to law, and promote their reemployment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励企业增加就业岗位，扶持失业人员和残疾人就业，对下列企业、人员依法给予税收优惠：  
（一）吸纳符合国家规定条件的失业人员达到规定要求的企业；  
（二）失业人员创办的中小企业；  
（三）安置残疾人员达到规定比例或者集中使用残疾人的企业；  
（四）从事个体经营的符合国家规定条件的失业人员；  
（五）从事个体经营的残疾人；  
（六）国务院规定给予税收优惠的其他企业、人员。

**Article 17** The State shall encourage enterprises to increase employment positions and support the employment of unemployed persons and disabled persons, and shall grant tax preferences according to law to the following enterprises and persons:  
(1) Enterprises that have absorbed unemployed persons meeting the conditions prescribed by the State up to the required requirements;  
(2) Small and medium-sized enterprises established by unemployed persons;  
(3) Enterprises that have placed disabled persons up to the required proportion or that intensively employ disabled persons;  
(4) Unemployed persons meeting the conditions prescribed by the State who engage in self-employment;  
(5) Disabled persons who engage in self-employment;  
(6) Other enterprises and persons to whom the State Council stipulates tax preferences shall be granted.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对[本法第十七条](#art-17)第四项、第五项规定的人员，有关部门应当在经营场地等方面给予照顾，免除行政事业性收费。

**Article 18** For the persons specified in items (4) and (5) of Article 17 of this Law, the relevant departments shall give consideration in terms of business premises, etc., and exempt them from administrative and institutional fees.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家实行有利于促进就业的金融政策，增加中小企业的融资渠道；鼓励金融机构改进金融服务，加大对中小企业的信贷支持，并对自主创业人员在一定期限内给予小额信贷等扶持。

**Article 19** The State shall implement financial policies conducive to promoting employment, increase financing channels for small and medium-sized enterprises; encourage financial institutions to improve financial services, increase credit support for small and medium-sized enterprises, and provide support such as small loans to self-employed persons within a certain period.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家实行城乡统筹的就业政策，建立健全城乡劳动者平等就业的制度，引导农业富余劳动力有序转移就业。  
县级以上地方人民政府推进小城镇建设和加快县域经济发展，引导农业富余劳动力就地就近转移就业；在制定小城镇规划时，将本地区农业富余劳动力转移就业作为重要内容。  
县级以上地方人民政府引导农业富余劳动力有序向城市异地转移就业；劳动力输出地和输入地人民政府应当互相配合，改善农村劳动者进城就业的环境和条件。

**Article 20** The State shall implement a coordinated urban and rural employment policy, establish and improve a system of equal employment for urban and rural workers, and guide the orderly transfer of surplus agricultural labor for employment.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall promote the construction of small towns and accelerate the development of the county economy, guide the local and nearby transfer of surplus agricultural labor for employment; when formulating plans for small towns, they shall take the transfer of surplus agricultural labor from the local area as an important component.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide the orderly transfer of surplus agricultural labor to cities for employment; the people's governments of labor-exporting areas and labor-importing areas shall cooperate with each other to improve the environment and conditions for rural workers to work in cities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家支持区域经济发展，鼓励区域协作，统筹协调不同地区就业的均衡增长。  
国家支持民族地区发展经济，扩大就业。

**Article 21** The State shall support regional economic development, encourage regional cooperation, and coordinate the balanced growth of employment in different regions.  
The State shall support the development of the economy in ethnic minority areas and expand employment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级人民政府统筹做好城镇新增劳动力就业、农业富余劳动力转移就业和失业人员就业工作。

**Article 22** People's governments at all levels shall make overall plans for the employment of new urban labor, the transfer of surplus agricultural labor, and the employment of unemployed persons.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府采取措施，逐步完善和实施与非全日制用工等灵活就业相适应的劳动和社会保险政策，为灵活就业人员提供帮助和服务。

**Article 23** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to gradually improve and implement labor and social insurance policies adapted to flexible employment such as part-time employment, and provide assistance and services to flexibly employed persons.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门应当加强对失业人员从事个体经营的指导，提供政策咨询、就业培训和开业指导等服务。

**Article 24** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall strengthen guidance for unemployed persons who engage in self-employment, and provide services such as policy consultation, employment training, and business start-up guidance.

### 第三章　公平就业

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级人民政府创造公平就业的环境，消除就业歧视，制定政策并采取措施对就业困难人员给予扶持和援助。

**Article 25** People's governments at all levels shall create a fair employment environment, eliminate employment discrimination, formulate policies and take measures to provide support and assistance to persons with employment difficulties.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　用人单位招用人员、职业中介机构从事职业中介活动，应当向劳动者提供平等的就业机会和公平的就业条件，不得实施就业歧视。

**Article 26** When employing personnel and when employment agencies engage in job placement activities, employing units and employment agencies shall provide workers with equal employment opportunities and fair employment conditions, and shall not commit employment discrimination.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家保障妇女享有与男子平等的劳动权利。  
用人单位招用人员，除国家规定的不适合妇女的工种或者岗位外，不得以性别为由拒绝录用妇女或者提高对妇女的录用标准。  
用人单位录用女职工，不得在劳动合同中规定限制女职工结婚、生育的内容。

**Article 27** The State shall guarantee that women enjoy equal labor rights with men.  
When employing personnel, employing units shall not refuse to employ women or raise the employment standards for women on the grounds of gender, except for types of work or positions that are deemed unsuitable for women as prescribed by the State.  
When employing female workers, employing units shall not stipulate in the labor contract any content restricting the marriage or childbirth of female workers.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各民族劳动者享有平等的劳动权利。  
用人单位招用人员，应当依法对少数民族劳动者给予适当照顾。

**Article 28** Workers of all ethnic groups shall enjoy equal labor rights.  
When employing personnel, employing units shall give appropriate consideration to ethnic minority workers according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家保障残疾人的劳动权利。  
各级人民政府应当对残疾人就业统筹规划，为残疾人创造就业条件。  
用人单位招用人员，不得歧视残疾人。

**Article 29** The State shall guarantee the labor rights of disabled persons.  
People's governments at all levels shall make overall plans for the employment of disabled persons and create employment conditions for them.  
When employing personnel, employing units shall not discriminate against disabled persons.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　用人单位招用人员，不得以是传染病病原携带者为由拒绝录用。但是，经医学鉴定传染病病原携带者在治愈前或者排除传染嫌疑前，不得从事法律、行政法规和国务院卫生行政部门规定禁止从事的易使传染病扩散的工作。

**Article 30** When employing personnel, employing units shall not refuse to employ a person on the grounds that he/she is a carrier of an infectious disease pathogen. However, if a carrier of an infectious disease pathogen is determined by medical expert evaluation, before being cured or before the suspicion of infection is ruled out, he/she shall not engage in work that is prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and the health administrative department of the State Council as being prone to the spread of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农村劳动者进城就业享有与城镇劳动者平等的劳动权利，不得对农村劳动者进城就业设置歧视性限制。

**Article 31** Rural workers who enter cities for employment shall enjoy equal labor rights with urban workers, and no discriminatory restrictions shall be imposed on rural workers entering cities for employment.

### 第四章　就业服务和管理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府培育和完善统一开放、竞争有序的人力资源市场，为劳动者就业提供服务。

**Article 32** People's governments at or above the county level shall cultivate and improve a unified, open, competitive, and orderly human resources market to provide services for the employment of workers.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上人民政府鼓励社会各方面依法开展就业服务活动，加强对公共就业服务和职业中介服务的指导和监督，逐步完善覆盖城乡的就业服务体系。

**Article 33** People's governments at or above the county level shall encourage all sectors of society to carry out employment service activities according to law, strengthen guidance and supervision over public employment services and job placement services, and gradually improve the employment service system covering both urban and rural areas.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府加强人力资源市场信息网络及相关设施建设，建立健全人力资源市场信息服务体系，完善市场信息发布制度。

**Article 34** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of human resources market information networks and related facilities, establish and improve the human resources market information service system, and improve the market information release system.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府建立健全公共就业服务体系，设立公共就业服务机构，为劳动者免费提供下列服务：  
（一）就业政策法规咨询；  
（二）职业供求信息、市场工资指导价位信息和职业培训信息发布；  
（三）职业指导和职业介绍；  
（四）对就业困难人员实施就业援助；  
（五）办理就业登记、失业登记等事务；  
（六）其他公共就业服务。  
公共就业服务机构应当不断提高服务的质量和效率，不得从事经营性活动。  
公共就业服务经费纳入同级财政预算。

**Article 35** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the public employment service system, set up public employment service institutions, and provide the following services to workers free of charge:  
(1) Consultation on employment policies, laws, and regulations;  
(2) Release of information on job supply and demand, market wage guidance rates, and vocational training information;  
(3) Vocational guidance and job placement;  
(4) Provision of employment assistance to persons with employment difficulties;  
(5) Handling of procedures such as employment registration and unemployment registration;  
(6) Other public employment services.  
Public employment service institutions shall continuously improve the quality and efficiency of their services and shall not engage in for-profit activities.  
The funds for public employment services shall be included in the fiscal budgets at the same level.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府对职业中介机构提供公益性就业服务的，按照规定给予补贴。  
国家鼓励社会各界为公益性就业服务提供捐赠、资助。

**Article 36** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with regulations, provide subsidies to employment agencies that provide public welfare employment services.  
The State shall encourage all sectors of society to make donations and provide financial support for public welfare employment services.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门不得举办或者与他人联合举办经营性的职业中介机构。  
地方各级人民政府和有关部门、公共就业服务机构举办的招聘会，不得向劳动者收取费用。

**Article 37** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall not establish or jointly establish for-profit employment agencies with others.  
Job fairs organized by local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, and public employment service institutions shall not charge fees to workers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府和有关部门加强对职业中介机构的管理，鼓励其提高服务质量，发挥其在促进就业中的作用。

**Article 38** People's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall strengthen the management of employment agencies, encourage them to improve service quality, and give play to their role in promoting employment.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　从事职业中介活动，应当遵循合法、诚实信用、公平、公开的原则。  
用人单位通过职业中介机构招用人员，应当如实向职业中介机构提供岗位需求信息。  
禁止任何组织或者个人利用职业中介活动侵害劳动者的合法权益。

**Article 39** Those engaged in job placement activities shall follow the principles of legality, honesty and credibility, fairness, and openness.  
When employing personnel through employment agencies, employing units shall truthfully provide employment agencies with information on job requirements.  
No organization or individual may use job placement activities to infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of workers.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　设立职业中介机构应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有明确的章程和管理制度；  
（二）有开展业务必备的固定场所、办公设施和一定数额的开办资金；  
（三）有一定数量具备相应职业资格的专职工作人员；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他条件。  
设立职业中介机构应当在工商行政管理部门办理登记后，向劳动行政部门申请行政许可。  
未经依法许可和登记的机构，不得从事职业中介活动。  
国家对外商投资职业中介机构和向劳动者提供境外就业服务的职业中介机构另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 40** To establish an employment agency, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Having clear articles of association and management systems;  
(2) Having the necessary fixed premises, office facilities, and a certain amount of start-up funds for conducting business;  
(3) Having a certain number of full-time staff with corresponding professional qualifications;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.  
An employment agency shall, after completing registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, apply to the labor administrative department for an administrative license.  
Institutions that have not been licensed and registered according to law shall not engage in job placement activities.  
Where there are separate provisions of the State regarding employment agencies with foreign investment and employment agencies that provide overseas employment services to workers, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　职业中介机构不得有下列行为：  
（一）提供虚假就业信息；  
（二）为无合法证照的用人单位提供职业中介服务；  
（三）伪造、涂改、转让职业中介许可证；  
（四）扣押劳动者的居民身份证和其他证件，或者向劳动者收取押金；  
（五）其他违反法律、法规规定的行为。

**Article 41** Employment agencies shall not engage in any of the following activities:  
(1) Providing false employment information;  
(2) Providing job placement services to employing units without legitimate licenses;  
(3) Forging, altering, or transferring employment agency licenses;  
(4) Withholding workers' resident identity cards or other documents, or collecting deposits from workers;  
(5) Other activities in violation of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府建立失业预警制度，对可能出现的较大规模的失业，实施预防、调节和控制。

**Article 42** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish an unemployment early warning system to implement prevention, regulation, and control of possible large-scale unemployment.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立劳动力调查统计制度和就业登记、失业登记制度，开展劳动力资源和就业、失业状况调查统计，并公布调查统计结果。  
统计部门和劳动行政部门进行劳动力调查统计和就业、失业登记时，用人单位和个人应当如实提供调查统计和登记所需要的情况。

**Article 43** The State shall establish a labor force survey and statistical system and an employment registration and unemployment registration system, conduct surveys and statistics on labor resources and employment and unemployment conditions, and publish the results of such surveys and statistics.  
When the statistical department and the labor administrative department conduct labor force surveys and statistics and employment and unemployment registration, employing units and individuals shall truthfully provide the information needed for the surveys, statistics, and registration.

### 第五章　职业教育和培训

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家依法发展职业教育，鼓励开展职业培训，促进劳动者提高职业技能，增强就业能力和创业能力。

**Article 44** The State shall develop vocational education according to law, encourage the provision of vocational training, and promote the improvement of workers' vocational skills to enhance their employability and entrepreneurship ability.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府根据经济社会发展和市场需求，制定并实施职业能力开发计划。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on economic and social development and market demand, formulate and implement vocational ability development plans.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府加强统筹协调，鼓励和支持各类职业院校、职业技能培训机构和用人单位依法开展就业前培训、在职培训、再就业培训和创业培训；鼓励劳动者参加各种形式的培训。

**Article 46** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen overall coordination, encourage and support various types of vocational schools, vocational skill training institutions, and employing units to carry out pre-employment training, on-the-job training, reemployment training, and entrepreneurship training according to law; encourage workers to participate in various forms of training.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府和有关部门根据市场需求和产业发展方向，鼓励、指导企业加强职业教育和培训。  
职业院校、职业技能培训机构与企业应当密切联系，实行产教结合，为经济建设服务，培养实用人才和熟练劳动者。  
企业应当按照国家有关规定提取职工教育经费，对劳动者进行职业技能培训和继续教育培训。

**Article 47** Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall, based on market demand and industrial development direction, encourage and guide enterprises to strengthen vocational education and training.  
Vocational schools and vocational skill training institutions shall maintain close ties with enterprises, implement the combination of production and education, serve economic construction, and cultivate practical talents and skilled workers.  
Enterprises shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, allocate funds for employee education and provide vocational skill training and continuing education training to workers.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家采取措施建立健全劳动预备制度，县级以上地方人民政府对有就业要求的初高中毕业生实行一定期限的职业教育和培训，使其取得相应的职业资格或者掌握一定的职业技能。

**Article 48** The State shall take measures to establish and improve a labor preparation system. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide vocational education and training for a certain period to junior and senior high school graduates who have employment requirements, so that they may obtain corresponding vocational qualifications or master certain vocational skills.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　地方各级人民政府鼓励和支持开展就业培训，帮助失业人员提高职业技能，增强其就业能力和创业能力。失业人员参加就业培训的，按照有关规定享受政府培训补贴。

**Article 49** Local people's governments at all levels shall encourage and support the provision of employment training to help unemployed persons improve their vocational skills and enhance their employability and entrepreneurship ability. If unemployed persons participate in employment training, they shall enjoy government training subsidies in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　地方各级人民政府采取有效措施，组织和引导进城就业的农村劳动者参加技能培训，鼓励各类培训机构为进城就业的农村劳动者提供技能培训，增强其就业能力和创业能力。

**Article 50** Local people's governments at all levels shall take effective measures to organize and guide rural workers who have entered cities for employment to participate in skill training, encourage various types of training institutions to provide skill training for such rural workers, and enhance their employability and entrepreneurship ability.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家对从事涉及公共安全、人身健康、生命财产安全等特殊工种的劳动者，实行职业资格证书制度，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 51** The State shall implement a vocational qualification certificate system for workers engaged in special types of work involving public safety, personal health, or the safety of life and property. The specific measures shall be stipulated by the State Council.

### 第六章　就业援助

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　各级人民政府建立健全就业援助制度，采取税费减免、贷款贴息、社会保险补贴、岗位补贴等办法，通过公益性岗位安置等途径，对就业困难人员实行优先扶持和重点帮助。  
就业困难人员是指因身体状况、技能水平、家庭因素、失去土地等原因难以实现就业，以及连续失业一定时间仍未能实现就业的人员。就业困难人员的具体范围，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本行政区域的实际情况规定。

**Article 52** People's governments at all levels shall establish and improve an employment assistance system, adopt measures such as tax and fee reductions, loan interest subsidies, social insurance subsidies, and job subsidies, and provide priority support and key assistance to persons with employment difficulties through avenues such as placement in public welfare positions.  
Persons with employment difficulties refer to those who have difficulty in obtaining employment due to physical conditions, skill levels, family factors, loss of land, etc., or who remain unemployed for a certain period. The specific scope of persons with employment difficulties shall be defined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on the actual conditions of their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　政府投资开发的公益性岗位，应当优先安排符合岗位要求的就业困难人员。被安排在公益性岗位工作的，按照国家规定给予岗位补贴。

**Article 53** Public welfare positions developed with government investment shall give priority to the placement of persons with employment difficulties who meet the job requirements. Those placed in public welfare positions shall be given job subsidies in accordance with State regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　地方各级人民政府加强基层就业援助服务工作，对就业困难人员实施重点帮助，提供有针对性的就业服务和公益性岗位援助。  
地方各级人民政府鼓励和支持社会各方面为就业困难人员提供技能培训、岗位信息等服务。

**Article 54** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen grassroots employment assistance services, provide key help to persons with employment difficulties, and offer targeted employment services and public welfare position assistance.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall encourage and support all sectors of society to provide services such as skill training and job information to persons with employment difficulties.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各级人民政府采取特别扶助措施，促进残疾人就业。  
用人单位应当按照国家规定安排残疾人就业，具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 55** People's governments at all levels shall take special support measures to promote the employment of disabled persons.  
Employing units shall arrange for the employment of disabled persons in accordance with State regulations. The specific measures shall be stipulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上地方人民政府采取多种就业形式，拓宽公益性岗位范围，开发就业岗位，确保城市有就业需求的家庭至少有一人实现就业。  
法定劳动年龄内的家庭人员均处于失业状况的城市居民家庭，可以向住所地街道、社区公共就业服务机构申请就业援助。街道、社区公共就业服务机构经确认属实的，应当为该家庭中至少一人提供适当的就业岗位。

**Article 56** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall adopt various forms of employment, expand the scope of public welfare positions, and develop employment positions to ensure that at least one person in each urban household with employment needs is employed.  
Urban resident households in which all family members within the statutory working age are unemployed may apply for employment assistance to the street or community public employment service institution in their place of residence. If the street or community public employment service institution confirms the situation, it shall provide at least one member of such household with an appropriate employment position.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家鼓励资源开采型城市和独立工矿区发展与市场需求相适应的产业，引导劳动者转移就业。  
对因资源枯竭或者经济结构调整等原因造成就业困难人员集中的地区，上级人民政府应当给予必要的扶持和帮助。

**Article 57** The State shall encourage resource-based cities and independent mining areas to develop industries that are compatible with market demand and guide workers to transfer employment.  
For areas where persons with employment difficulties are concentrated due to resource depletion or economic restructuring, the people's government at a higher level shall provide necessary support and assistance.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当建立促进就业的目标责任制度。县级以上人民政府按照促进就业目标责任制的要求，对所属的有关部门和下一级人民政府进行考核和监督。

**Article 58** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall establish a target responsibility system for promoting employment.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the target responsibility system for promoting employment, conduct assessment and supervision of their relevant departments and the people's governments at the next lower level.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　审计机关、财政部门应当依法对就业专项资金的管理和使用情况进行监督检查。

**Article 59** Audit institutions and finance departments shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the management and use of special employment funds.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　劳动行政部门应当对本法实施情况进行监督检查，建立举报制度，受理对违反本法行为的举报，并及时予以核实、处理。

**Article 60** The labor administrative department shall supervise and inspect the implementation of this Law, establish a reporting system, accept reports of violations of this Law, and promptly verify and handle them.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本法规定，劳动行政等有关部门及其工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the labor administrative department or other relevant departments and their staff members abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，实施就业歧视的，劳动者可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 62** Where employment discrimination is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, workers may institute legal proceedings in the People's Court.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府和有关部门、公共就业服务机构举办经营性的职业中介机构，从事经营性职业中介活动，向劳动者收取费用的，由上级主管机关责令限期改正，将违法收取的费用退还劳动者，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments, or public employment service institutions establish for-profit employment agencies, engage in for-profit job placement activities, or charge fees to workers, the competent authority at a higher level shall order them to make corrections within a time limit, return the illegally charged fees to the workers, and impose sanctions on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，未经许可和登记，擅自从事职业中介活动的，由劳动行政部门或者其他主管部门依法予以关闭；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 64** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an institution engages in job placement activities without a license and registration, the labor administrative department or other competent department shall close it down according to law; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，职业中介机构提供虚假就业信息，为无合法证照的用人单位提供职业中介服务，伪造、涂改、转让职业中介许可证的，由劳动行政部门或者其他主管部门责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销职业中介许可证。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an employment agency provides false employment information, provides job placement services to an employing unit without a legitimate license, or forges, alters, or transfers an employment agency license, the labor administrative department or other competent department shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the employment agency license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，职业中介机构扣押劳动者居民身份证等证件的，由劳动行政部门责令限期退还劳动者，并依照有关法律规定给予处罚。  
违反本法规定，职业中介机构向劳动者收取押金的，由劳动行政部门责令限期退还劳动者，并以每人五百元以上二千元以下的标准处以罚款。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an employment agency withholds workers' resident identity cards or other documents, the labor administrative department shall order it to return them to the workers within a time limit and impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an employment agency collects deposits from workers, the labor administrative department shall order it to return the deposits to the workers within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan per person.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，企业未按照国家规定提取职工教育经费，或者挪用职工教育经费的，由劳动行政部门责令改正，并依法给予处罚。

**Article 67** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an enterprise fails to allocate funds for employee education in accordance with State regulations, or misappropriates such funds, the labor administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本法规定，侵害劳动者合法权益，造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the legitimate rights and interests of workers are infringed upon, causing property losses or other damages, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　本法自2008年1月1日起施行。

**Article 69** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2008.
