# 第五届全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于批准《广东省经济特区条例》的决议

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1980-08-26  
**生效日期**：1980-08-26

> 第五届全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于批准广东省经济特区条例的决议

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为发展对外经济合作和技术交流，促进社会主义现代化建设，在广东省深圳、珠海、汕头三市分别划出一定区域，设置经济特区（以下简称特区）。特区鼓励外国公民、华侨、港澳同胞及其公司、企业（以下简称客商），投资设厂或者与我方合资设厂，兴办企业和其他事业，并依法保护其资产、应得利润和其他合法权益。


**Article 1** : To develop foreign economic cooperation and technical exchange and to promote socialist modernization, certain areas shall be demarcated in the cities of Shenzhen, Zhuhai, and Shantou in Guangdong Province to establish special economic zones (hereinafter referred to as the SEZs). The SEZs shall encourage foreign citizens, overseas Chinese, compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao, and their companies and enterprises (hereinafter referred to as investors) to invest in and establish factories or to establish joint ventures with the Chinese side, to set up enterprises and other undertakings, and shall protect their assets, due profits, and other lawful rights and interests according to law.
<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　特区内的企业和个人，必须遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法令和有关规定。本条例有特别规定的，按照本条例的规定执行。


**Article 2** : Enterprises and individuals within the SEZs must comply with the laws, decrees, and relevant regulations of the People's Republic of China. Where these Regulations provide otherwise, such provisions of these Regulations shall prevail.
<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　设立广东省经济特区管理委员会，代表广东省人民政府对各特区实行统一管理。


**Article 3** : A Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee shall be established to exercise unified administration over each SEZ on behalf of the People's Government of Guangdong Province.
<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　特区为客商提供广阔的经营范围，创造良好的经营条件，保证稳定的经营场所。一切在国际经济合作和技术交流中具有积极意义的工业、农业、畜牧业、养殖业、旅游业、住宅和建筑业、高级技术研究制造业，以及客商与我方共同感兴趣的其他行业，都可以投资兴办或者与我方合资兴办。


**Article 4** : The SEZs shall provide investors with a broad scope of business operations, create favorable business conditions, and ensure stable business premises. All industries that have positive significance in international economic cooperation and technical exchange, such as industry, agriculture, animal husbandry, aquaculture, tourism, housing and construction, high-technology research and manufacturing, and other industries of mutual interest to investors and the Chinese side, may be established through investment by investors or through joint ventures with the Chinese side.
<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　特区的土地平整工程和供水、排水、供电、道路、码头、通讯、仓储等各项公共设施，由广东省经济特区管理委员会负责兴建，必要时也可以吸收外资参与兴建。


**Article 5** : The land leveling projects and various public facilities such as water supply, drainage, electricity supply, roads, wharves, communications, and warehousing in the SEZs shall be constructed by the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee, which may, when necessary, also involve foreign investment in the construction.
<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各特区分别聘请国内外专家和热心我国现代化建设的有关人士组成顾问委员会，作为该特区的咨询机构。


**Article 6** : Each SEZ shall respectively engage domestic and foreign experts and relevant persons enthusiastic about China's modernization to form an advisory committee, which shall serve as the consulting body for such SEZ.
### 第二章　注册和经营

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　客商在特区投资设厂，兴办各项经济事业，应向广东省经济特区管理委员会提出申请，经审核、批准后，发给注册证书和土地使用证书。


**Article 7** : Investors who invest in and establish factories or set up various economic undertakings in the SEZs shall submit applications to the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee, and after examination and approval, shall be issued registration certificates and land use certificates.
<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　客商可在特区内设立的中国银行或者其他经我方批准设立的银行开户，并办理有关外汇事宜。  

**Article 8** : Investors may open accounts with the Bank of China established within the SEZs or with other banks approved for establishment by the Chinese side, and handle relevant foreign exchange matters.  
All insurance of investors may be placed with the People's Insurance Company of China established within the SEZs or with other insurance companies approved for establishment by the Chinese side.
客商的各项保险，可向特区内设立的中国人民保险公司或者其他经我方批准设立的保险公司投保。

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　特区企业的产品供国际市场销售；其产品如向我国内地销售，须经广东省经济特区管理委员会核准，并办理海关补税手续。


**Article 9** : The products of SEZ enterprises shall be supplied for sale on the international market; if such products are to be sold to the interior of China, they must be approved by the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee, and the procedures for supplementary customs duties shall be completed.
<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　客商在特区内可以独立经营自己的企业，雇用外籍人员担任技术和管理工作。


**Article 10** : Investors may independently operate their own enterprises within the SEZs and may employ foreign nationals to undertake technical and managerial work.
<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　客商在特区所办的企业中途停业，应向广东省经济特区管理委员会申报理由，办理停业手续，清理债权债务；停业后，其资产可转让，资金可汇出。


**Article 11** : Where an enterprise established by an investor in an SEZ ceases business midway, the investor shall report the reasons to the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee, go through the procedures for cessation of business, and liquidate claims and debts; after the cessation of business, its assets may be transferred, and funds may be remitted out.
### 第三章　优惠办法

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　特区的土地为中华人民共和国所有。客商用地，按实际需要提供，其使用年限、使用费数额和缴纳办法，根据不同行业和用途，给予优惠，具体办法另行规定。


**Article 12** : Land in the SEZs is owned by the People's Republic of China. Land for use by investors shall be provided according to actual needs, and the term of use, the amount of land use fees, and the method of payment shall be preferential according to different industries and purposes; the specific measures shall be separately prescribed.
<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　特区企业进口生产所必需的机器设备、零配件、原材料、运输工具和其他生产资料，免征进口税；对必需的生活用品、可以根据具体情况、分别征税或者减免进口税。上述物品进口和特区产品出口时，均应向海关办理申报手续。


**Article 13** : SEZ enterprises shall be exempted from import duties on machinery, equipment, spare parts, raw materials, means of transport, and other means of production necessary for production; for essential articles of daily use, duties may be levied or exempted separately according to specific circumstances. When the aforementioned articles are imported or when SEZ products are exported, declaration procedures shall be completed with the Customs.
<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　特区企业所得税税率为百分之十五。对在本条例公布后两年内投资兴办的企业，或者投资额达五百万美元以上的企业，或者技术性较高、资金周转期较长的企业，给予特别优惠待遇。


**Article 14** : The enterprise income tax rate in the SEZs shall be fifteen percent. Special preferential treatment shall be granted to enterprises established by investment within two years after the promulgation of these Regulations, or to enterprises with an investment amount of five million US dollars or more, or to enterprises with higher technological content or a longer capital turnover period.
<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　客商在缴纳企业所得税后所得的合法利润，特区企业的外籍职工、华侨职工、港澳职工在缴纳个人所得税后的工资和其他正当收入，可以按照特区外汇管理办法的规定，通过特区内的中国银行或者其他银行汇出。


**Article 15** : The lawful profits of investors after payment of enterprise income tax, and the wages and other legitimate income after payment of individual income tax of foreign employees, overseas Chinese employees, and employees from Hong Kong and Macao of SEZ enterprises, may be remitted out through the Bank of China or other banks within the SEZs in accordance with the provisions of the SEZ foreign exchange administration measures.
<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　客商将所得利润用于在特区内进行再投资为期五年以上者，可申请减免用于再投资部分的所得税。


**Article 16** : Investors who use their obtained profits for reinvestment within the SEZs for a period of five years or more may apply for a reduction or exemption of the income tax on the portion used for reinvestment.
<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　鼓励特区企业采用我国生产的机器设备、原材料和其他物资，其价格可按我国当时同类商品的出口价格给予优惠，以外汇结算。这些产品和物资，可凭售货单位的销售凭证直接运往特区。


**Article 17** : SEZ enterprises shall be encouraged to adopt machinery, equipment, raw materials, and other goods produced in China, the prices of which may be preferential according to the export prices of similar Chinese goods at the time, and settled in foreign exchange. Such products and goods may be transported directly to the SEZs on the basis of the sales vouchers of the selling units.
<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　凡来往特区的外籍人员、华侨和港澳同胞，出入境均简化手续，给予方便。


**Article 18** : For all foreign nationals, overseas Chinese, and compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao traveling to and from the SEZs, entry and exit procedures shall be simplified and facilitated.
### 第四章　劳动管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各特区设立劳动服务公司。特区企业雇用中国职员和工人，或者由当地劳动服务公司介绍，或者经广东省经济特区管理委员会同意由客商自行招聘，都由企业考核录用，同职工签订劳动合同。


**Article 19** : Labor service companies shall be established in each SEZ. When SEZ enterprises employ Chinese staff and workers, whether introduced by the local labor service company or recruited by the investors themselves with the agreement of the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zones Administration Committee, the enterprises shall assess and recruit them, and shall sign labor contracts with the employees.
<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　特区企业雇用的职工，由该企业按其经营的要求进行管理，必要时可以解雇，其手续按照劳动合同的规定办理。  

**Article 20** : Employees of SEZ enterprises shall be managed by such enterprises according to their operational requirements, and may be dismissed when necessary; the procedures therefor shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the labor contracts.  
Employees of SEZ enterprises may submit their resignation to the enterprise in accordance with the provisions of the labor contract.
特区企业职工可按照劳动合同规定，向企业提请辞职。

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　特区企业中的中国职工工资水平、工资形式、奖励办法，以及劳动保险、国家对职工的各项补贴，按照广东省经济特区管理委员会的规定，由企业同职工签订合同。


**Article 21** The wage level, wage forms, and reward methods for Chinese workers in special zone enterprises, as well as labor insurance and various state subsidies for workers, shall be stipulated in contracts signed between the enterprise and its workers in accordance with the provisions of the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zone Management Committee.
<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　特区企业应有必要的劳动保护措施，保证职工在安全、卫生的条件下进行工作。


**Article 22** Special zone enterprises shall have the necessary labor protection measures to ensure that workers work under safe and sanitary conditions.
### 第五章　组织管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　广东省经济特区管理委员会行使以下职权：  

**Article 23** The Guangdong Province Special Economic Zone Management Committee shall exercise the following powers: 1.  
Formulating development plans for the special zones and organizing their implementation; 2.  
Reviewing and approving investment projects of foreign investors in the special zones; 3.  
Handling industrial and commercial registration and land allocation in the special zones; 4.  
Coordinating the working relations among the institutions such as banks, insurance companies, tax authorities, customs, border inspection, and post and telecommunications established in the special zones; 5.  
Providing sources for the workers needed by special zone enterprises and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of workers; 6.  
Organizing education, culture, health and various public welfare undertakings in the special zones; 7.  
Maintaining public order in the special zones and protecting persons and property within the special zones from infringement according to law.
1.制订特区发展计划并组织实施；  
2.审核、批准客商在特区的投资项目；  
3.办理特区工商登记和土地核配；  
4.协调设在特区内的银行、保险、税务、海关、边检、邮电等机构的工作关系；  
5.为特区企业所需的职工提供来源，并保护职工的正当权益；  
6.举办特区教育、文化、卫生和各项公益事业；  
7.维护特区治安，依法保护特区内人身和财产不受侵犯。

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　深圳特区由广东省经济特区管理委员会直接经营管理；珠海、汕头特区设立必要的办事机构。


**Article 24** The Shenzhen Special Zone shall be directly operated and managed by the Guangdong Province Special Economic Zone Management Committee; the Zhuhai and Shantou Special Zones shall establish necessary offices.
<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　为适应特区经济活动的开展，设立广东省经济特区发展公司。公司业务范围：承办资金筹集和信托投资业务；经营或者与客商合资经营特区的有关企业；代理特区客商与内地贸易往来的购销事宜，并提供洽商服务。


**Article 25** To meet the development of economic activities in the special zones, a Guangdong Province Special Economic Zone Development Company shall be established. The business scope of the Company shall be: undertaking fund raising and trust investment business; operating or jointly operating relevant enterprises in the special zones with foreign investors; acting as an agent for the purchase and sale matters in trade exchanges between foreign investors in the special zones and the inland, and providing negotiation services.
### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例由广东省人民代表大会通过，并报中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准后施行。


**Article 26** These Regulations shall be adopted by the Guangdong Province People's Congress and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress of the People's Republic of China.
