# 中华人民共和国畜牧法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2022-10-30  
**生效日期**：2023-03-01

> （2005年12月29日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国计量法〉等五部法律的决定》修正　2022年10月30日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十七次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　畜禽遗传资源保护 第三章　种畜禽品种选育与生产经营 第四章　畜禽养殖 第五章　草原畜牧业 第六章　畜禽交易与运输 第七章　畜禽屠宰 第八章　保障与监督 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范畜牧业生产经营行为，保障畜禽产品供给和质量安全，保护和合理利用畜禽遗传资源，培育和推广畜禽优良品种，振兴畜禽种业，维护畜牧业生产经营者的合法权益，防范公共卫生风险，促进畜牧业高质量发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事畜禽的遗传资源保护利用、繁育、饲养、经营、运输、屠宰等活动，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范畜牧业生产经营行为，保障畜禽产品供给和质量安全，保护和合理利用畜禽遗传资源，培育和推广畜禽优良品种，振兴畜禽种业，维护畜牧业生产经营者的合法权益，防范公共卫生风险，促进畜牧业高质量发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating livestock and poultry production and operation activities, ensuring the supply and quality safety of livestock and poultry products, protecting and rationally utilizing livestock and poultry genetic resources, cultivating and promoting excellent varieties of livestock and poultry, revitalizing the livestock and poultry seed industry, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of livestock and poultry producers and operators, preventing public health risks, and promoting high-quality development of the livestock and poultry industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事畜禽的遗传资源保护利用、繁育、饲养、经营、运输、屠宰等活动，适用本法。  
本法所称畜禽，是指列入依照[本法第十二条](#art-12)规定公布的畜禽遗传资源目录的畜禽。  
蜂、蚕的资源保护利用和生产经营，适用本法有关规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as the protection, utilization, breeding, raising, operation, transportation, and slaughter of livestock and poultry within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Livestock and poultry as mentioned in this Law shall refer to those included in the catalog of livestock and poultry genetic resources published in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of this Law.  
The protection, utilization, production, and operation of bee and silkworm resources shall be governed by the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家支持畜牧业发展，发挥畜牧业在发展农业、农村经济和增加农民收入中的作用。  
县级以上人民政府应当将畜牧业发展纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，加强畜牧业基础设施建设，鼓励和扶持发展规模化、标准化和智能化养殖，促进种养结合和农牧循环、绿色发展，推进畜牧产业化经营，提高畜牧业综合生产能力，发展安全、优质、高效、生态的畜牧业。  
国家帮助和扶持民族地区、欠发达地区畜牧业的发展，保护和合理利用草原，改善畜牧业生产条件。

**Article 3** The State supports the development of the livestock and poultry industry and shall give play to the role of the livestock and poultry industry in developing agriculture and the rural economy and increasing farmers' income.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the development of the livestock and poultry industry into the national economic and social development plan, strengthen the construction of livestock and poultry industry infrastructure, encourage and support the development of scaled, standardized, and smart farming, promote the integration of planting and breeding and the green development of agriculture and animal husbandry cycles, advance the industrialized operation of the livestock and poultry industry, improve the comprehensive production capacity of the livestock and poultry industry, and develop a safe, high-quality, efficient, and ecological livestock and poultry industry.  
The State shall assist and support the development of the livestock and poultry industry in ethnic areas and underdeveloped areas, protect and rationally utilize grasslands, and improve livestock and poultry production conditions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家采取措施，培养畜牧兽医专业人才，加强畜禽疫病监测、畜禽疫苗研制，健全基层畜牧兽医技术推广体系，发展畜牧兽医科学技术研究和推广事业，完善畜牧业标准，开展畜牧兽医科学技术知识的教育宣传工作和畜牧兽医信息服务，推进畜牧业科技进步和创新。

**Article 4** The State shall take measures to cultivate professional talents in animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, strengthen the monitoring of livestock and poultry diseases and the development of livestock and poultry vaccines, improve the grassroots animal husbandry and veterinary technology extension system, develop scientific and technological research and extension in animal husbandry and veterinary medicine, improve livestock and poultry industry standards, carry out educational and promotional work on animal husbandry and veterinary science and technology knowledge and animal husbandry and veterinary information services, and promote scientific and technological progress and innovation in the livestock and poultry industry.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国畜牧业的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域内的畜牧业监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关主管部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关促进畜牧业发展的工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the livestock and poultry industry nationwide. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the livestock and poultry industry within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant competent departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for work related to promoting the development of the livestock and poultry industry.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当指导畜牧业生产经营者改善畜禽繁育、饲养、运输、屠宰的条件和环境。

**Article 6** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall guide livestock and poultry producers and operators in improving the conditions and environment for livestock and poultry breeding, raising, transportation, and slaughter.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当加强畜牧业相关法律法规的宣传。  
对在畜牧业发展中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity of laws and regulations related to the livestock and poultry industry.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the development of the livestock and poultry industry shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　畜牧业生产经营者可以依法自愿成立行业协会，为成员提供信息、技术、营销、培训等服务，加强行业自律，维护成员和行业利益。

**Article 8** Livestock and poultry producers and operators may, according to law, voluntarily establish industry associations to provide members with services such as information, technology, marketing, and training, strengthen industry self-discipline, and safeguard the interests of members and the industry.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　畜牧业生产经营者应当依法履行动物防疫和生态环境保护义务，接受有关主管部门依法实施的监督检查。

**Article 9** Livestock and poultry producers and operators shall, according to law, fulfill obligations for animal epidemic prevention and ecological environment protection, and shall accept the supervisory inspections conducted by relevant competent departments according to law.

### 第二章　畜禽遗传资源保护

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家建立畜禽遗传资源保护制度，开展资源调查、保护、鉴定、登记、监测和利用等工作。各级人民政府应当采取措施，加强畜禽遗传资源保护，将畜禽遗传资源保护经费列入预算。  
畜禽遗传资源保护以国家为主、多元参与，坚持保护优先、高效利用的原则，实行分类分级保护。  
国家鼓励和支持有关单位、个人依法发展畜禽遗传资源保护事业，鼓励和支持高等学校、科研机构、企业加强畜禽遗传资源保护、利用的基础研究，提高科技创新能力。

**Article 10** The State shall establish a system for the protection of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and shall carry out work such as resource investigation, protection, identification, registration, monitoring, and utilization. People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the protection of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and shall include the funds for the protection of livestock and poultry genetic resources in their budgets.  
The protection of livestock and poultry genetic resources shall be based on the State as the mainstay, with multi-stakeholder participation, and shall adhere to the principles of prioritizing protection and efficient utilization, implementing classified and graded protection.  
The State encourages and supports relevant units and individuals to develop the cause of livestock and poultry genetic resources protection according to law, and encourages and supports institutions of higher education, scientific research institutions, and enterprises to strengthen basic research on the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and to improve the capacity for scientific and technological innovation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院农业农村主管部门设立由专业人员组成的国家畜禽遗传资源委员会，负责畜禽遗传资源的鉴定、评估和畜禽新品种、配套系的审定，承担畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划论证及有关畜禽遗传资源保护的咨询工作。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国畜禽遗传资源进出境和对外合作研究利用审批办法.md#art-3" title="被《中华人民共和国畜禽遗传资源进出境和对外合作研究利用审批办法》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall establish a National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources composed of professionals, which shall be responsible for the identification and evaluation of livestock and poultry genetic resources and the approval of new varieties and supporting lines of livestock and poultry, and shall undertake the demonstration of plans for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources and the advisory work concerning the protection of livestock and poultry genetic resources.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责定期组织畜禽遗传资源的调查工作，发布国家畜禽遗传资源状况报告，公布经国务院批准的畜禽遗传资源目录。  
经过驯化和选育而成，遗传性状稳定，有成熟的品种和一定的种群规模，能够不依赖于野生种群而独立繁衍的驯养动物，可以列入畜禽遗传资源目录。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国野生动物保护法》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the investigation of livestock and poultry genetic resources on a regular basis, issuing a national report on the status of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and publishing the catalog of livestock and poultry genetic resources approved by the State Council.  
Domesticated animals that have been formed through domestication and breeding, have stable genetic traits, have mature varieties and a certain population size, and are capable of reproducing independently without relying on wild populations, may be included in the catalog of livestock and poultry genetic resources.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院农业农村主管部门根据畜禽遗传资源分布状况，制定全国畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划，制定、调整并公布国家级畜禽遗传资源保护名录，对原产我国的珍贵、稀有、濒危的畜禽遗传资源实行重点保护。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据全国畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划及本行政区域内的畜禽遗传资源状况，制定、调整并公布省级畜禽遗传资源保护名录，并报国务院农业农村主管部门备案，加强对地方畜禽遗传资源的保护。

**Article 13** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, in light of the distribution of livestock and poultry genetic resources, formulate a national plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and formulate, adjust, and publish a national list of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources, and shall implement key protection for precious, rare, and endangered livestock and poultry genetic resources originating in China.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the national plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources and the status of livestock and poultry genetic resources within their respective administrative regions, formulate, adjust, and publish provincial lists of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources, and shall report them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for record, and shall strengthen the protection of local livestock and poultry genetic resources.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院农业农村主管部门根据全国畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划及国家级畜禽遗传资源保护名录，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据省级畜禽遗传资源保护名录，分别建立或者确定畜禽遗传资源保种场、保护区和基因库，承担畜禽遗传资源保护任务。  
享受中央和省级财政资金支持的畜禽遗传资源保种场、保护区和基因库，未经国务院农业农村主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门批准，不得擅自处理受保护的畜禽遗传资源。  
畜禽遗传资源基因库应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门的规定，定期采集和更新畜禽遗传材料。有关单位、个人应当配合畜禽遗传资源基因库采集畜禽遗传材料，并有权获得适当的经济补偿。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当保障畜禽遗传资源保种场和基因库用地的需求。确需关闭或者搬迁的，应当经原建立或者确定机关批准，搬迁的按照先建后拆的原则妥善安置。  
畜禽遗传资源保种场、保护区和基因库的管理办法，由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 14** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, in accordance with the national plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources and the national list of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources, and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provincial lists of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources, respectively establish or designate livestock and poultry genetic resources conservation farms, conservation areas, and gene banks to undertake the tasks of protecting livestock and poultry genetic resources.  
Livestock and poultry genetic resources conservation farms, conservation areas, and gene banks that receive financial support from the central and provincial governments shall not, without the approval of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, dispose of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources.  
Livestock and poultry genetic resources gene banks shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, regularly collect and update livestock and poultry genetic materials. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with livestock and poultry genetic resources gene banks in collecting livestock and poultry genetic materials, and shall have the right to receive appropriate economic compensation.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall guarantee the land use needs of livestock and poultry genetic resources conservation farms and gene banks. If closure or relocation is truly necessary, it shall be approved by the original establishing or designating authority, and in the case of relocation, proper arrangements shall be made in accordance with the principle of building before demolition.  
The administrative measures for livestock and poultry genetic resources conservation farms, conservation areas, and gene banks shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　新发现的畜禽遗传资源在国家畜禽遗传资源委员会鉴定前，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门应当制定保护方案，采取临时保护措施，并报国务院农业农村主管部门备案。

**Article 15** Before the identification of newly discovered livestock and poultry genetic resources by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall formulate a protection plan, take temporary protection measures, and report to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门提出申请；受理申请的农业农村主管部门经审核，报国务院农业农村主管部门经评估论证后批准；但是国务院对批准机关另有规定的除外。经批准的，依照《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》的规定办理相关手续并实施检疫。  
从境外引进的畜禽遗传资源被发现对境内畜禽遗传资源、生态环境有危害或者可能产生危害的，国务院农业农村主管部门应当商有关主管部门，及时采取相应的安全控制措施。

**Article 16** Where livestock and poultry genetic resources are introduced from outside the territory, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that accepts the application shall, after examination, submit it to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for approval after evaluation and demonstration; unless the State Council otherwise provides for the approving authority. Where approved, the relevant procedures shall be handled and quarantine shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Animals and Plants.  
Where livestock and poultry genetic resources introduced from outside the territory are found to be harmful or likely to cause harm to domestic livestock and poultry genetic resources or the ecological environment, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall consult with the relevant competent departments and take timely corresponding safety control measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对畜禽遗传资源享有主权。向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门提出申请，同时提出国家共享惠益的方案；受理申请的农业农村主管部门经审核，报国务院农业农村主管部门批准。  
向境外输出畜禽遗传资源的，还应当依照《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》的规定办理相关手续并实施检疫。  
新发现的畜禽遗传资源在国家畜禽遗传资源委员会鉴定前，不得向境外输出，不得与境外机构、个人合作研究利用。

**Article 17** The State shall have sovereignty over livestock and poultry genetic resources. Where livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the protection list are to be exported or to be used in cooperative research with overseas institutions or individuals within the territory, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with a plan for national benefit-sharing; the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that accepts the application shall, after examination, submit it to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for approval.  
Where livestock and poultry genetic resources are exported, the relevant procedures shall also be handled and quarantine shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection and Quarantine of Entry and Exit Animals and Plants.  
Before the identification of newly discovered livestock and poultry genetic resources by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, such resources shall not be exported or used in cooperative research with overseas institutions or individuals.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　畜禽遗传资源的进出境和对外合作研究利用的审批办法由国务院规定。

**Article 18** The measures for the examination and approval of the entry and exit of livestock and poultry genetic resources and their use in foreign cooperative research shall be formulated by the State Council.

### 第三章　种畜禽品种选育与生产经营

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家扶持畜禽品种的选育和优良品种的推广使用，实施全国畜禽遗传改良计划；支持企业、高等学校、科研机构和技术推广单位开展联合育种，建立健全畜禽良种繁育体系。  
县级以上人民政府支持开发利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的品种，增加特色畜禽产品供给，满足多元化消费需求。

**Article 19** The State shall support the selection and breeding of livestock and poultry varieties and the promotion and use of excellent varieties, and implement a national plan for genetic improvement of livestock and poultry; it shall support enterprises, institutions of higher education, scientific research institutions, and technology extension units in carrying out joint breeding, and establish and improve a system for breeding superior livestock and poultry varieties.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall support the development and utilization of varieties included in the list of protected livestock and poultry genetic resources, increase the supply of characteristic livestock and poultry products, and meet diversified consumer demands.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家鼓励和支持畜禽种业自主创新，加强育种技术攻关，扶持选育生产经营相结合的创新型企业发展。

**Article 20** The State encourages and supports independent innovation in the livestock and poultry seed industry, strengthens research on breeding technologies, and supports the development of innovative enterprises that integrate breeding, production, and operation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　培育的畜禽新品种、配套系和新发现的畜禽遗传资源在销售、推广前，应当通过国家畜禽遗传资源委员会审定或者鉴定，并由国务院农业农村主管部门公告。畜禽新品种、配套系的审定办法和畜禽遗传资源的鉴定办法，由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。审定或者鉴定所需的试验、检测等费用由申请者承担。  
畜禽新品种、配套系培育者的合法权益受法律保护。

**Article 21** Before sale or promotion, cultivated new livestock or poultry breeds, supporting lines, and newly discovered livestock or poultry genetic resources shall be subject to evaluation or expert evaluation by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, and shall be announced by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council. The measures for evaluation of new livestock or poultry breeds and supporting lines and the measures for expert evaluation of livestock or poultry genetic resources shall be formulated by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council. The expenses for tests, inspections, etc., required for evaluation or expert evaluation shall be borne by the applicant.  
The legitimate rights and interests of cultivators of new livestock or poultry breeds and supporting lines shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　转基因畜禽品种的引进、培育、试验、审定和推广，应当符合国家有关农业转基因生物安全管理的规定。

**Article 22** The introduction, cultivation, testing, evaluation, and promotion of genetically modified livestock or poultry breeds shall comply with the provisions of the State on the safety management of agricultural genetically modified organisms.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　省级以上畜牧兽医技术推广机构应当组织开展种畜质量监测、优良个体登记，向社会推荐优良种畜。优良种畜登记规则由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 23** Animal husbandry and veterinary technology extension institutions at or above the provincial level shall organize and carry out monitoring of breeding stock quality, registration of excellent individuals, and recommend excellent breeding stock to the public. The rules for registration of excellent breeding stock shall be formulated by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　从事种畜禽生产经营或者生产经营商品代仔畜、雏禽的单位、个人，应当取得种畜禽生产经营许可证。  
申请取得种畜禽生产经营许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）生产经营的种畜禽是通过国家畜禽遗传资源委员会审定或者鉴定的品种、配套系，或者是经批准引进的境外品种、配套系；  
（二）有与生产经营规模相适应的畜牧兽医技术人员；  
（三）有与生产经营规模相适应的繁育设施设备；  
（四）具备法律、行政法规和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的种畜禽防疫条件；  
（五）有完善的质量管理和育种记录制度；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 24** Any entity or individual that engages in the production and operation of breeding livestock or poultry, or the production and operation of commercial generation offspring or chicks, shall obtain a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit.  
An applicant for a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The breeding livestock or poultry to be produced and operated are varieties or supporting lines that have been evaluated or expert-evaluated by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, or are foreign varieties or supporting lines approved for introduction;  
(2) There are animal husbandry and veterinary technicians commensurate with the scale of production and operation;  
(3) There are breeding facilities and equipment commensurate with the scale of production and operation;  
(4) The epidemic prevention conditions for breeding livestock or poultry as provided by laws, administrative regulations, and the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council are met;  
(5) There are sound quality management and breeding record systems; and  
(6) Other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　申请取得生产家畜卵子、精液、胚胎等遗传材料的生产经营许可证，除应当符合[本法第二十四条](#art-24)第二款规定的条件外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的实验室、保存和运输条件；  
（二）符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的种畜数量和质量要求；  
（三）体外受精取得的胚胎、使用的卵子来源明确，供体畜符合国家规定的种畜健康标准和质量要求；  
（四）符合有关国家强制性标准和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的技术要求。

**Article 25** An applicant for a production and operation permit for producing genetic materials such as ova, semen, and embryos of domestic animals shall, in addition to meeting the conditions provided in paragraph 2 of Article 24 of this Law, also meet the following conditions:  
(1) Meeting the laboratory, storage, and transport conditions prescribed by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council;  
(2) Meeting the quantity and quality requirements for breeding stock prescribed by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council;  
(3) The embryos obtained through in vitro fertilization and the ova used have clear sources, and the donor animals meet the national health standards and quality requirements for breeding stock;  
(4) Meeting the relevant national mandatory standards and the technical requirements prescribed by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请取得生产家畜卵子、精液、胚胎等遗传材料的生产经营许可证，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门提出申请。受理申请的农业农村主管部门应当自收到申请之日起六十个工作日内依法决定是否发放生产经营许可证。  
其他种畜禽的生产经营许可证由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门审核发放。  
国家对种畜禽生产经营许可证实行统一管理、分级负责，在统一的信息平台办理。种畜禽生产经营许可证的审批和发放信息应当依法向社会公开。具体办法和许可证样式由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 26** An applicant for a production and operation permit for producing genetic materials such as ova, semen, and embryos of domestic animals shall file an application with the agricultural and rural affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The agricultural and rural affairs department that accepts the application shall decide according to law whether to issue the production and operation permit within sixty working days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The production and operation permits for other breeding livestock or poultry shall be examined and issued by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The State shall implement unified management and level-by-level responsibility for breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permits, and handle them on a unified information platform. The approval and issuance information of breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permits shall be disclosed to the public according to law. The specific measures and permit format shall be formulated by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　种畜禽生产经营许可证应当注明生产经营者名称、场（厂）址、生产经营范围及许可证有效期的起止日期等。  
禁止无种畜禽生产经营许可证或者违反种畜禽生产经营许可证的规定生产经营种畜禽或者商品代仔畜、雏禽。禁止伪造、变造、转让、租借种畜禽生产经营许可证。

**Article 27** A breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit shall specify the name of the producer/operator, the site (yard/factory) address, the scope of production and operation, and the start and end dates of the validity period of the permit, among other things.  
It is prohibited to produce or operate breeding livestock or poultry or commercial generation offspring or chicks without a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit or in violation of the provisions of such a permit. It is prohibited to forge, alter, transfer, or lend a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　农户饲养的种畜禽用于自繁自养和有少量剩余仔畜、雏禽出售的，农户饲养种公畜进行互助配种的，不需要办理种畜禽生产经营许可证。

**Article 28** Where a farmer raises breeding livestock or poultry for self-breeding and self-raising and sells a small surplus of offspring or chicks, or where a farmer raises male breeding stock for mutual breeding, the farmer is not required to apply for a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　发布种畜禽广告的，广告主应当持有或者提供种畜禽生产经营许可证和营业执照。广告内容应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定，并注明种畜禽品种、配套系的审定或者鉴定名称，对主要性状的描述应当符合该品种、配套系的标准。

**Article 29** An advertiser that publishes advertisements for breeding livestock or poultry shall hold or provide a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit and a business license. The content of the advertisement shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall indicate the evaluation or expert evaluation name of the breeding livestock or poultry variety or supporting line. The description of main traits shall conform to the standards for such variety or supporting line.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　销售的种畜禽、家畜配种站（点）使用的种公畜，应当符合种用标准。销售种畜禽时，应当附具种畜禽场出具的种畜禽合格证明、动物卫生监督机构出具的检疫证明，销售的种畜还应当附具种畜禽场出具的家畜系谱。  
生产家畜卵子、精液、胚胎等遗传材料，应当有完整的采集、销售、移植等记录，记录应当保存二年。

**Article 30** The breeding livestock or poultry sold and the male breeding stock used by livestock breeding stations (points) shall meet the breeding standards. When selling breeding livestock or poultry, a certificate of conformity for breeding livestock or poultry issued by the breeding livestock or poultry farm and a quarantine certificate issued by the animal health supervision institution shall be attached. For breeding stock sold, a livestock pedigree issued by the breeding livestock or poultry farm shall also be attached.  
For the production of genetic materials such as ova, semen, and embryos of domestic animals, complete records of collection, sale, transplantation, etc., shall be kept, and such records shall be preserved for two years.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　销售种畜禽，不得有下列行为：  
（一）以其他畜禽品种、配套系冒充所销售的种畜禽品种、配套系；  
（二）以低代别种畜禽冒充高代别种畜禽；  
（三）以不符合种用标准的畜禽冒充种畜禽；  
（四）销售未经批准进口的种畜禽；  
（五）销售未附具[本法第三十条](#art-30)规定的种畜禽合格证明、检疫证明的种畜禽或者未附具家畜系谱的种畜；  
（六）销售未经审定或者鉴定的种畜禽品种、配套系。

**Article 31** When selling breeding livestock or poultry, the following acts are prohibited:  
(1) Passing off other livestock or poultry varieties or supporting lines as the breeding livestock or poultry varieties or supporting lines being sold;  
(2) Passing off lower-generation breeding livestock or poultry as higher-generation breeding livestock or poultry;  
(3) Passing off livestock or poultry not conforming to breeding standards as breeding livestock or poultry;  
(4) Selling breeding livestock or poultry imported without approval;  
(5) Selling breeding livestock or poultry without the certificate of conformity for breeding livestock or poultry or the quarantine certificate as provided in Article 30 of this Law, or selling breeding stock without the livestock pedigree; or  
(6) Selling breeding livestock or poultry varieties or supporting lines that have not been evaluated or expert-evaluated.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　申请进口种畜禽的，应当持有种畜禽生产经营许可证。因没有种畜禽而未取得种畜禽生产经营许可证的，应当提供省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门的说明文件。进口种畜禽的批准文件有效期为六个月。  
进口的种畜禽应当符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的技术要求。首次进口的种畜禽还应当由国家畜禽遗传资源委员会进行种用性能的评估。  
种畜禽的进出口管理除适用本条前两款的规定外，还适用[本法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条和第二十二条的相关规定。  
国家鼓励畜禽养殖者利用进口的种畜禽进行新品种、配套系的培育；培育的新品种、配套系在推广前，应当经国家畜禽遗传资源委员会审定。

**Article 32** An applicant for importing breeding livestock or poultry shall hold a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation permit. If the applicant fails to obtain such permit due to not having breeding livestock or poultry, the applicant shall provide a statement document from the agricultural and rural affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The validity period of the approval document for importing breeding livestock or poultry is six months.  
The imported breeding livestock or poultry shall meet the technical requirements prescribed by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council. For first-time import of breeding livestock or poultry, an assessment of breeding performance shall also be conducted by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources.  
In addition to the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs of this Article, the import and export management of breeding livestock or poultry shall also apply the relevant provisions of Articles 16, 17, and 22 of this Law.  
The State encourages livestock and poultry breeders to use imported breeding livestock or poultry to cultivate new varieties and supporting lines; cultivated new varieties and supporting lines shall be evaluated by the National Committee on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources before promotion.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　销售商品代仔畜、雏禽的，应当向购买者提供其销售的商品代仔畜、雏禽的主要生产性能指标、免疫情况、饲养技术要求和有关咨询服务，并附具动物卫生监督机构出具的检疫证明。  
销售种畜禽和商品代仔畜、雏禽，因质量问题给畜禽养殖者造成损失的，应当依法赔偿损失。

**Article 33** A seller of commercial generation offspring or chicks shall provide the purchaser with the main production performance indicators, immunization status, feeding technical requirements, and related consulting services for the commercial generation offspring or chicks sold, and shall attach a quarantine certificate issued by the animal health supervision institution.  
If the sale of breeding livestock or poultry and commercial generation offspring or chicks causes losses to livestock or poultry breeders due to quality problems, compensation for losses shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门负责种畜禽质量安全的监督管理工作。种畜禽质量安全的监督检验应当委托具有法定资质的种畜禽质量检验机构进行；所需检验费用由同级预算列支，不得向被检验人收取。

**Article 34** The agricultural and rural affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and management of the quality and safety of breeding livestock or poultry. The supervision and inspection of the quality and safety of breeding livestock or poultry shall be entrusted to a breeding livestock or poultry quality inspection institution with statutory qualifications; the required inspection costs shall be covered by the budget at the same level and shall not be charged to the person being inspected.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　蜂种、蚕种的资源保护、新品种选育、生产经营和推广，适用本法有关规定，具体管理办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 35** The protection of resources, selection and breeding of new varieties, production and operation, and promotion of bee breeds and silkworm breeds shall apply the relevant provisions of this Law. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council.

### 第四章　畜禽养殖

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家建立健全现代畜禽养殖体系。县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当根据畜牧业发展规划和市场需求，引导和支持畜牧业结构调整，发展优势畜禽生产，提高畜禽产品市场竞争力。

**Article 36** The State shall establish and improve a modern livestock and poultry breeding system. The agricultural and rural affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the animal husbandry development plan and market demand, guide and support the structural adjustment of the animal husbandry industry, develop advantageous livestock and poultry production, and enhance the market competitiveness of livestock and poultry products.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级人民政府应当保障畜禽养殖用地合理需求。县级国土空间规划根据本地实际情况，安排畜禽养殖用地。畜禽养殖用地按照农业用地管理。畜禽养殖用地使用期限届满或者不再从事养殖活动，需要恢复为原用途的，由畜禽养殖用地使用人负责恢复。在畜禽养殖用地范围内需要兴建永久性建（构）筑物，涉及农用地转用的，依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》的规定办理。

**Article 37** People's governments at all levels shall ensure reasonable demand for land for livestock and poultry breeding. The county-level territorial spatial plan shall, based on local actual conditions, allocate land for livestock and poultry breeding. Land for livestock and poultry breeding shall be managed as agricultural land. If the term of use of land for livestock and poultry breeding expires or breeding activities are no longer carried out, and the land needs to be restored to its original use, the user of the land for livestock and poultry breeding shall be responsible for restoration. If permanent buildings (structures) need to be constructed within the land for livestock and poultry breeding, and involves conversion of agricultural land, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家设立的畜牧兽医技术推广机构，应当提供畜禽养殖、畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用技术培训，以及良种推广、疫病防治等服务。县级以上人民政府应当保障国家设立的畜牧兽医技术推广机构从事公益性技术服务的工作经费。  
国家鼓励畜禽产品加工企业和其他相关生产经营者为畜禽养殖者提供所需的服务。

**Article 38** Animal husbandry and veterinary technology extension institutions established by the State shall provide technical training on livestock and poultry breeding, harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, as well as services such as promotion of improved varieties and disease prevention and control. People's governments at or above the county level shall ensure the working funds for the animal husbandry and veterinary technology extension institutions established by the State to engage in public welfare technical services.  
The State encourages livestock and poultry product processing enterprises and other relevant producers and operators to provide needed services to livestock and poultry breeders.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　畜禽养殖场应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与其饲养规模相适应的生产场所和配套的生产设施；  
（二）有为其服务的畜牧兽医技术人员；  
（三）具备法律、行政法规和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的防疫条件；  
（四）有与畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用相适应的设施设备；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
畜禽养殖场兴办者应当将畜禽养殖场的名称、养殖地址、畜禽品种和养殖规模，向养殖场所在地县级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案，取得畜禽标识代码。  
畜禽养殖场的规模标准和备案管理办法，由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
畜禽养殖户的防疫条件、畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用要求，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门会同有关部门规定。

**Article 39** A livestock and poultry breeding farm shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having production premises and supporting production facilities commensurate with its breeding scale;  
(2) Having animal husbandry and veterinary technicians to serve it;  
(3) Meeting the epidemic prevention conditions as provided by laws, administrative regulations, and the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council;  
(4) Having facilities and equipment suitable for the harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure; and  
(5) Other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The person who establishes a livestock and poultry breeding farm shall register the name, breeding address, livestock and poultry variety, and breeding scale of the farm with the agricultural and rural affairs department of the county-level people's government where the farm is located, and obtain a livestock and poultry identification code.  
The scale standards and registration management measures for livestock and poultry breeding farms shall be formulated by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the State Council.  
The epidemic prevention conditions and requirements for harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure for livestock and poultry breeding households shall be prescribed by the agricultural and rural affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with relevant departments.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　畜禽养殖场的选址、建设应当符合国土空间规划，并遵守有关法律法规的规定；不得违反法律法规的规定，在禁养区域建设畜禽养殖场。

**Article 40** The site selection and construction of a livestock and poultry breeding farm shall conform to the territorial spatial plan and comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; no livestock and poultry breeding farm shall be constructed in a prohibited breeding area in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　畜禽养殖场应当建立养殖档案，载明下列内容：  
（一）畜禽的品种、数量、繁殖记录、标识情况、来源和进出场日期；  
（二）饲料、饲料添加剂、兽药等投入品的来源、名称、使用对象、时间和用量；  
（三）检疫、免疫、消毒情况；  
（四）畜禽发病、死亡和无害化处理情况；  
（五）畜禽粪污收集、储存、无害化处理和资源化利用情况；  
（六）国务院农业农村主管部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 41** A livestock and poultry farm shall establish a breeding archive, recording the following:  
(1) the breed, quantity, breeding records, identification status, source, and date of entry and exit of livestock and poultry;  
(2) the source, name, target, time, and dosage of inputs such as feed, feed additives, and veterinary drugs;  
(3) quarantine, immunization, and disinfection status;  
(4) the incidence, death, and harmless treatment of livestock and poultry;  
(5) the collection, storage, harmless treatment, and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure;  
(6) other contents prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　畜禽养殖者应当为其饲养的畜禽提供适当的繁殖条件和生存、生长环境。

**Article 42** A livestock and poultry breeder shall provide appropriate breeding conditions and living and growing environments for the livestock and poultry he raises.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　从事畜禽养殖，不得有下列行为：  
（一）违反法律、行政法规和国家有关强制性标准、国务院农业农村主管部门的规定使用饲料、饲料添加剂、兽药；  
（二）使用未经高温处理的餐馆、食堂的泔水饲喂家畜；  
（三）在垃圾场或者使用垃圾场中的物质饲养畜禽；  
（四）随意弃置和处理病死畜禽；  
（五）法律、行政法规和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的危害人和畜禽健康的其他行为。

**Article 43** Anyone engaged in livestock and poultry breeding shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) using feed, feed additives, or veterinary drugs in violation of laws, administrative regulations, relevant mandatory national standards, or provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council;  
(2) using untreated swill from restaurants or canteens to feed livestock;  
(3) raising livestock and poultry at garbage dumps or using substances from garbage dumps;  
(4) arbitrarily discarding or disposing of diseased or dead livestock and poultry;  
(5) other acts that endanger human or livestock and poultry health as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　从事畜禽养殖，应当依照《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》、《中华人民共和国农产品质量安全法》的规定，做好畜禽疫病防治和质量安全工作。

**Article 44** Anyone engaged in livestock and poultry breeding shall, in accordance with the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Quality and Safety of Agricultural Products, properly carry out the prevention and control of livestock and poultry diseases and quality safety work.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　畜禽养殖者应当按照国家关于畜禽标识管理的规定，在应当加施标识的畜禽的指定部位加施标识。农业农村主管部门提供标识不得收费，所需费用列入省、自治区、直辖市人民政府预算。  
禁止伪造、变造或者重复使用畜禽标识。禁止持有、使用伪造、变造的畜禽标识。

**Article 45** A livestock and poultry breeder shall, in accordance with the national provisions on the management of livestock and poultry identification, affix identification to the designated parts of livestock and poultry to which identification shall be affixed. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs shall not charge fees for providing identification, and the necessary expenses shall be included in the budget of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
It is prohibited to forge, alter, or reuse livestock and poultry identification. It is prohibited to possess or use forged or altered livestock and poultry identification.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　畜禽养殖场应当保证畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用设施的正常运转，保证畜禽粪污综合利用或者达标排放，防止污染环境。违法排放或者因管理不当污染环境的，应当排除危害，依法赔偿损失。  
国家支持建设畜禽粪污收集、储存、粪污无害化处理和资源化利用设施，推行畜禽粪污养分平衡管理，促进农用有机肥利用和种养结合发展。

**Article 46** A livestock and poultry farm shall ensure the normal operation of facilities for harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, ensure comprehensive utilization or discharge up to standards of livestock and poultry manure, and prevent environmental pollution. Where pollution is caused by illegal discharge or improper management, the harm shall be eliminated and compensation for losses shall be paid according to law.  
The State supports the construction of facilities for collection, storage, harmless treatment, and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, promotes nutrient balance management of livestock and poultry manure, and facilitates the use of agricultural organic fertilizer and the development of crop-livestock integration.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家引导畜禽养殖户按照畜牧业发展规划有序发展，加强对畜禽养殖户的指导帮扶，保护其合法权益，不得随意以行政手段强行清退。  
国家鼓励涉农企业带动畜禽养殖户融入现代畜牧业产业链，加强面向畜禽养殖户的社会化服务，支持畜禽养殖户和畜牧业专业合作社发展畜禽规模化、标准化养殖，支持发展新产业、新业态，促进与旅游、文化、生态等产业融合。

**Article 47** The State shall guide livestock and poultry farming households in orderly development in accordance with the livestock industry development plan, strengthen guidance and assistance to livestock and poultry farming households, protect their legitimate rights and interests, and shall not arbitrarily force them to withdraw by administrative means.  
The State shall encourage agriculture-related enterprises to lead livestock and poultry farming households into the modern livestock industry chain, strengthen socialized services for livestock and poultry farming households, support livestock and poultry farming households and specialized livestock cooperatives in developing large-scale and standardized breeding of livestock and poultry, support the development of new industries and new business forms, and promote integration with tourism, culture, ecology, and other industries.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家支持发展特种畜禽养殖。县级以上人民政府应当采取措施支持建立与特种畜禽养殖业发展相适应的养殖体系。

**Article 48** The State supports the development of special livestock and poultry breeding. The people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to support the establishment of a breeding system compatible with the development of the special livestock and poultry breeding industry.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家支持发展养蜂业，保护养蜂生产者的合法权益。  
有关部门应当积极宣传和推广蜂授粉农艺措施。

**Article 49** The State supports the development of beekeeping and protects the legitimate rights and interests of beekeeping producers.  
The relevant departments shall actively publicize and promote agricultural measures for bee pollination.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　养蜂生产者在生产过程中，不得使用危害蜂产品质量安全的药品和容器，确保蜂产品质量。养蜂器具应当符合国家标准和国务院有关部门规定的技术要求。

**Article 50** A beekeeping producer shall not, during the production process, use drugs or containers that endanger the quality and safety of bee products, and shall ensure the quality of bee products. Beekeeping equipment shall comply with national standards and the technical requirements prescribed by the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　养蜂生产者在转地放蜂时，当地公安、交通运输、农业农村等有关部门应当为其提供必要的便利。  
养蜂生产者在国内转地放蜂，凭国务院农业农村主管部门统一格式印制的检疫证明运输蜂群，在检疫证明有效期内不得重复检疫。

**Article 51** When beekeeping producers move their bees to new locations, the local public security, transport, agriculture and rural affairs, and other relevant departments shall provide them with necessary conveniences.  
When beekeeping producers move their bees to new locations within the country, they shall transport the bee colonies on the strength of a quarantine certificate printed in a unified format by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and shall not be subject to repeated quarantine within the validity period of the quarantine certificate.

### 第五章　草原畜牧业

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家支持科学利用草原，协调推进草原保护与草原畜牧业发展，坚持生态优先、生产生态有机结合，发展特色优势产业，促进农牧民增加收入，提高草原可持续发展能力，筑牢生态安全屏障，推进牧区生产生活生态协同发展。

**Article 52** The State supports scientific utilization of grasslands, coordinates the advancement of grassland protection and grassland livestock development, adheres to the principle of ecological priority and organic integration of production and ecology, develops characteristic and advantageous industries, promotes income increase for farmers and herdsmen, enhances the sustainable development capacity of grasslands, builds a solid ecological security barrier, and promotes the coordinated development of production, living, and ecology in pastoral areas.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家支持牧区转变草原畜牧业发展方式，加强草原水利、草原围栏、饲草料生产加工储备、牲畜圈舍、牧道等基础设施建设。  
国家鼓励推行舍饲半舍饲圈养、季节性放牧、划区轮牧等饲养方式，合理配置畜群，保持草畜平衡。

**Article 53** The State supports pastoral areas in transforming the development mode of grassland livestock, strengthening infrastructure construction such as grassland water conservancy, grassland fencing, production, processing and storage of forage, livestock sheds, and pastoral roads.  
The State encourages the adoption of feeding methods such as barn feeding, semi-barn feeding, seasonal grazing, and rotational grazing, rational allocation of livestock groups, and maintenance of grass-livestock balance.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家支持优良饲草品种的选育、引进和推广使用，因地制宜开展人工草地建设、天然草原改良和饲草料基地建设，优化种植结构，提高饲草料供应保障能力。

**Article 54** The State supports the selection, breeding, introduction, and promotion of high-quality forage varieties, carries out the construction of artificial grasslands, improvement of natural grasslands, and construction of forage bases in light of local conditions, optimizes the planting structure, and improves the capacity to ensure the supply of forage.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家支持农牧民发展畜牧业专业合作社和现代家庭牧场，推行适度规模养殖，提升标准化生产水平，建设牛羊等重要畜产品生产基地。

**Article 55** The State supports farmers and herdsmen in developing specialized livestock cooperatives and modern family ranches, promotes moderately scaled breeding, improves the level of standardized production, and builds production bases for important livestock products such as cattle and sheep.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　牧区各级人民政府农业农村主管部门应当鼓励和指导农牧民改良家畜品种，优化畜群结构，实行科学饲养，合理加快出栏周转，促进草原畜牧业节本、提质、增效。

**Article 56** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at all levels in pastoral areas shall encourage and guide farmers and herdsmen to improve livestock breeds, optimize herd structure, practice scientific feeding, reasonably accelerate the turnover of slaughter, and promote cost reduction, quality improvement, and efficiency increase in grassland livestock production.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家加强草原畜牧业灾害防御保障，将草原畜牧业防灾减灾列入预算，优化设施装备条件，完善牧区牛羊等家畜保险制度，提高抵御自然灾害的能力。

**Article 57** The State strengthens the disaster prevention and protection guarantees for grassland livestock, includes the disaster prevention and mitigation of grassland livestock in the budget, optimizes the conditions of facilities and equipment, improves the insurance system for cattle, sheep, and other livestock in pastoral areas, and enhances the capacity to resist natural disasters.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家完善草原生态保护补助奖励政策，对采取禁牧和草畜平衡措施的农牧民按照国家有关规定给予补助奖励。

**Article 58** The State improves the subsidy and reward policy for grassland ecological protection, and grants subsidies and rewards to farmers and herdsmen who adopt measures for grazing prohibition and grass-livestock balance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有关地方人民政府应当支持草原畜牧业与乡村旅游、文化等产业协同发展，推动一二三产业融合，提升产业化、品牌化、特色化水平，持续增加农牧民收入，促进牧区振兴。

**Article 59** The relevant local people's governments shall support the coordinated development of grassland livestock with rural tourism, culture, and other industries, promote the integration of primary, secondary, and tertiary industries, enhance the level of industrialization, branding, and specialization, continuously increase the income of farmers and herdsmen, and promote the revitalization of pastoral areas.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　草原畜牧业发展涉及草原保护、建设、利用和管理活动的，应当遵守有关草原保护法律法规的规定。

**Article 60** Where the development of grassland livestock involves activities of grassland protection, construction, utilization, and management, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on grassland protection shall be complied with.

### 第六章　畜禽交易与运输

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家加快建立统一开放、竞争有序、安全便捷的畜禽交易市场体系。

**Article 61** The State shall accelerate the establishment of a unified, open, competitive, orderly, safe, and convenient livestock and poultry trading market system.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据农产品批发市场发展规划，对在畜禽集散地建立畜禽批发市场给予扶持。  
畜禽批发市场选址，应当符合法律、行政法规和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的动物防疫条件，并距离种畜禽场和大型畜禽养殖场三公里以外。

**Article 62** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the development plan for agricultural product wholesale markets, provide support for the establishment of livestock and poultry wholesale markets at livestock and poultry distribution centers.  
The site selection of livestock and poultry wholesale markets shall comply with the animal epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and shall be at a distance of more than three kilometers from breeding livestock and poultry farms and large-scale livestock and poultry farms.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　进行交易的畜禽应当符合农产品质量安全标准和国务院有关部门规定的技术要求。  
国务院农业农村主管部门规定应当加施标识而没有标识的畜禽，不得销售、收购。  
国家鼓励畜禽屠宰经营者直接从畜禽养殖者收购畜禽，建立稳定收购渠道，降低动物疫病和质量安全风险。

**Article 63** Livestock and poultry traded shall meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products and the technical requirements prescribed by the relevant departments of the State Council.  
Livestock and poultry that should have been marked as prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council but are not marked shall not be sold or purchased.  
The State encourages livestock and poultry slaughtering operators to purchase livestock and poultry directly from livestock and poultry farmers, establish stable purchasing channels, and reduce animal disease and quality safety risks.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　运输畜禽，应当符合法律、行政法规和国务院农业农村主管部门规定的动物防疫条件，采取措施保护畜禽安全，并为运输的畜禽提供必要的空间和饲喂饮水条件。  
有关部门对运输中的畜禽进行检查，应当有法律、行政法规的依据。

**Article 64** The transportation of livestock and poultry shall comply with the animal epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and measures shall be taken to protect the safety of livestock and poultry, and provide necessary space and feeding and drinking conditions for the livestock and poultry being transported.  
Relevant departments that inspect livestock and poultry during transportation shall have the basis of laws and administrative regulations.

### 第七章　畜禽屠宰

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家实行生猪定点屠宰制度。对生猪以外的其他畜禽可以实行定点屠宰，具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。农村地区个人自宰自食的除外。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照科学布局、集中屠宰、有利流通、方便群众的原则，结合畜禽养殖、动物疫病防控和畜禽产品消费等实际情况，制定畜禽屠宰行业发展规划并组织实施。

**Article 65** The State shall implement a designated slaughter system for pigs. For livestock and poultry other than pigs, designated slaughter may be implemented, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, except for individual self-slaughter and self-consumption in rural areas.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the principles of scientific layout, centralized slaughter, convenient circulation, and convenience for the masses, and taking into account the actual conditions of livestock and poultry farming, animal disease prevention and control, and consumption of livestock and poultry products, formulate development plans for the livestock and poultry slaughter industry and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家鼓励畜禽就地屠宰，引导畜禽屠宰企业向养殖主产区转移，支持畜禽产品加工、储存、运输冷链体系建设。

**Article 66** The State encourages local slaughter of livestock and poultry, guides livestock and poultry slaughtering enterprises to relocate to major farming areas, and supports the construction of cold chain systems for processing, storage, and transportation of livestock and poultry products.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　畜禽屠宰企业应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与屠宰规模相适应、水质符合国家规定标准的用水供应条件；  
（二）有符合国家规定的设施设备和运载工具；  
（三）有依法取得健康证明的屠宰技术人员；  
（四）有经考核合格的兽医卫生检验人员；  
（五）依法取得动物防疫条件合格证和其他法律法规规定的证明文件。

**Article 67** A livestock and poultry slaughtering enterprise shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) There shall be a water supply that meets the scale of slaughter and whose water quality conforms to the standards prescribed by the State;  
(2) There shall be facilities, equipment, and means of transport that conform to the provisions of the State;  
(3) There shall be slaughtering technical personnel who have obtained health certificates according to law;  
(4) There shall be veterinary hygiene inspection personnel who have passed the assessment;  
(5) There shall be an animal epidemic prevention conditions certificate obtained according to law and other certification documents prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　畜禽屠宰经营者应当加强畜禽屠宰质量安全管理。畜禽屠宰企业应当建立畜禽屠宰质量安全管理制度。  
未经检验、检疫或者经检验、检疫不合格的畜禽产品不得出厂销售。经检验、检疫不合格的畜禽产品，按照国家有关规定处理。  
地方各级人民政府应当按照规定对无害化处理的费用和损失给予补助。

**Article 68** Livestock and poultry slaughtering operators shall strengthen the quality and safety management of livestock and poultry slaughtering. Livestock and poultry slaughtering enterprises shall establish a quality and safety management system for livestock and poultry slaughtering.  
Livestock and poultry products that have not been inspected or quarantined, or have been inspected or quarantined and found unqualified, shall not leave the factory for sale. Livestock and poultry products that have been inspected or quarantined and found unqualified shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with regulations, provide subsidies for the costs and losses of harmless treatment.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责组织制定畜禽屠宰质量安全风险监测计划。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据国家畜禽屠宰质量安全风险监测计划，结合实际情况，制定本行政区域畜禽屠宰质量安全风险监测方案并组织实施。

**Article 69** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the formulation of risk monitoring plans for the quality and safety of livestock and poultry slaughtering.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the national risk monitoring plan for the quality and safety of livestock and poultry slaughtering and in light of actual conditions, formulate risk monitoring plans for the quality and safety of livestock and poultry slaughtering in their respective administrative regions and organize their implementation.

### 第八章　保障与监督

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　省级以上人民政府应当在其预算内安排支持畜禽种业创新和畜牧业发展的良种补贴、贴息补助、保费补贴等资金，并鼓励有关金融机构提供金融服务，支持畜禽养殖者购买优良畜禽、繁育良种、防控疫病，支持改善生产设施、畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用设施设备、扩大养殖规模，提高养殖效益。

**Article 70** People's governments at or above the provincial level shall arrange within their budgets funds such as subsidies for improved varieties, interest subsidy subsidies, and premium subsidies to support innovation in livestock and poultry breeding and the development of animal husbandry, and shall encourage relevant financial institutions to provide financial services, support livestock and poultry farmers in purchasing superior livestock and poultry, breeding improved varieties, preventing and controlling epidemics, support the improvement of production facilities, facilities and equipment for harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, expand the scale of farming, and improve farming efficiency.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　县级以上人民政府应当组织农业农村主管部门和其他有关部门，依照本法和有关法律、行政法规的规定，加强对畜禽饲养环境、种畜禽质量、畜禽交易与运输、畜禽屠宰以及饲料、饲料添加剂、兽药等投入品的生产、经营、使用的监督管理。

**Article 71** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and other relevant departments to strengthen the supervision and management of livestock and poultry breeding environments, the quality of breeding livestock and poultry, livestock and poultry trading and transportation, livestock and poultry slaughtering, and the production, operation, and use of inputs such as feed, feed additives, and veterinary drugs, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当制定畜禽标识和养殖档案管理办法，采取措施落实畜禽产品质量安全追溯和责任追究制度。

**Article 72** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall formulate measures for the management of livestock and poultry identification and farming records, and take measures to implement the traceability and accountability system for the quality and safety of livestock and poultry products.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当制定畜禽质量安全监督抽查计划，并按照计划开展监督抽查工作。

**Article 73** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate supervision and inspection plans for the quality and safety of livestock and poultry, and carry out supervision and inspection work in accordance with the plans.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当组织制定畜禽生产规范，指导畜禽的安全生产。

**Article 74** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall organize the formulation of production standards for livestock and poultry, and guide the safe production of livestock and poultry.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国家建立统一的畜禽生产和畜禽产品市场监测预警制度，逐步完善有关畜禽产品储备调节机制，加强市场调控，促进市场供需平衡和畜牧业健康发展。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当及时发布畜禽产销信息，为畜禽生产经营者提供信息服务。

**Article 75** The State shall establish a unified monitoring and early warning system for livestock and poultry production and the market of livestock and poultry products, gradually improve the relevant reserve and regulation mechanism for livestock and poultry products, strengthen market regulation, and promote a balance between market supply and demand and the healthy development of animal husbandry.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly issue information on livestock and poultry production and marketing, and provide information services to livestock and poultry producers and operators.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国家加强畜禽生产、加工、销售、运输体系建设，提升畜禽产品供应安全保障能力。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责保障本行政区域内的畜禽产品供给，建立稳产保供的政策保障和责任考核体系。  
国家鼓励畜禽主销区通过跨区域合作、建立养殖基地等方式，与主产区建立稳定的合作关系。

**Article 76** The State shall strengthen the construction of the livestock and poultry production, processing, sales, and transportation system, and enhance the ability to ensure the safety of the supply of livestock and poultry products.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for ensuring the supply of livestock and poultry products within their respective administrative regions, and establish a policy guarantee and responsibility assessment system for stable production and supply.  
The State encourages major livestock and poultry sales areas to establish stable cooperative relations with major production areas through cross-regional cooperation, establishment of farming bases, and other means.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）利用职务上的便利，收受他人财物或者牟取其他利益；  
（二）对不符合条件的申请人准予许可，或者超越法定职权准予许可；  
（三）发现违法行为不予查处；  
（四）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊等不依法履行监督管理工作职责的行为。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level and their staff members commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Taking advantage of their positions to accept property from others or seek other benefits;  
(2) Granting permits to applicants who do not meet the conditions, or granting permits beyond statutory authority;  
(3) Failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered;  
(4) Other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain that fail to perform supervision and management duties according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)第二款规定，擅自处理受保护的畜禽遗传资源，造成畜禽遗传资源损失的，由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 14 of this Law, protected livestock and poultry genetic resources are disposed of without authorization, causing losses to livestock and poultry genetic resources, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收畜禽遗传资源和违法所得，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未经审核批准，从境外引进畜禽遗传资源；  
（二）未经审核批准，在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入保护名录的畜禽遗传资源；  
（三）在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用未经国家畜禽遗传资源委员会鉴定的新发现的畜禽遗传资源。

**Article 79** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the livestock and poultry genetic resources and illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan:  
(1) Introducing livestock and poultry genetic resources from abroad without examination and approval;  
(2) Cooperating with foreign institutions or individuals within the territory to research and utilize livestock and poultry genetic resources listed in the protection list without examination and approval;  
(3) Cooperating with foreign institutions or individuals within the territory to research and utilize newly discovered livestock and poultry genetic resources that have not been evaluated by the National Committee for Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，未经国务院农业农村主管部门批准，向境外输出畜禽遗传资源的，依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的有关规定追究法律责任。海关应当将扣留的畜禽遗传资源移送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门处理。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, livestock and poultry genetic resources are exported abroad without the approval of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China. The customs shall transfer the detained livestock and poultry genetic resources to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for handling.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，销售、推广未经审定或者鉴定的畜禽品种、配套系的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收畜禽和违法所得；违法所得在五万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 81** Whoever, in violation of this Law, sells or promotes livestock or poultry breeds or combinations that have not been subject to approval or expert evaluation shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, and their livestock or poultry and illegal income shall be confiscated; if the illegal income is 50,000 RMB or more, they shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal income; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 50,000 RMB, they shall be fined not less than 5,000 RMB but not more than 50,000 RMB.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，无种畜禽生产经营许可证或者违反种畜禽生产经营许可证规定生产经营，或者伪造、变造、转让、租借种畜禽生产经营许可证的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，收缴伪造、变造的种畜禽生产经营许可证，没收种畜禽、商品代仔畜、雏禽和违法所得；违法所得在三万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足三万元的，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款。违反种畜禽生产经营许可证的规定生产经营或者转让、租借种畜禽生产经营许可证，情节严重的，并处吊销种畜禽生产经营许可证。

**Article 82** Whoever, in violation of this Law, produces or operates without a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation license or in violation of the provisions of such license, or forges, alters, transfers, or lends a breeding livestock or poultry production and operation license shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act; the forged or altered license shall be confiscated; the breeding livestock or poultry, commercial offspring, young poultry, and illegal income shall be confiscated; if the illegal income is 30,000 RMB or more, they shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal income; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 30,000 RMB, they shall be fined not less than 3,000 RMB but not more than 30,000 RMB. Where the violation of the provisions of the breeding livestock or poultry production and operation license in production or operation, or the transfer or lending of such license, is of a serious nature, the breeding livestock or poultry production and operation license shall also be revoked.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)规定的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 83** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 29 of this Law shall be subject to legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，使用的种畜禽不符合种用标准的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收种畜禽和违法所得；违法所得在五千元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上二倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五千元的，并处一千元以上五千元以下罚款。

**Article 84** Whoever, in violation of this Law, uses breeding livestock or poultry that do not meet the breeding standards shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, and the breeding livestock or poultry and illegal income shall be confiscated; if the illegal income is 5,000 RMB or more, they shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than two times the illegal income; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 5,000 RMB, they shall be fined not less than 1,000 RMB but not more than 5,000 RMB.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　销售种畜禽有[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第一项至第四项违法行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工责令停止销售，没收违法销售的（种）畜禽和违法所得；违法所得在五万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处吊销种畜禽生产经营许可证或者营业执照。

**Article 85** Where selling breeding livestock or poultry involves any of the illegal acts specified in Items 1 to 4 of Article 31 of this Law, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs and the market supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order the cessation of the sale, confiscate the illegally sold (breeding) livestock or poultry and illegal income; if the illegal income is 50,000 RMB or more, they shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal income; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 50,000 RMB, they shall be fined not less than 5,000 RMB but not more than 50,000 RMB; if the circumstances are serious, the breeding livestock or poultry production and operation license or business license shall also be revoked.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，兴办畜禽养殖场未备案，畜禽养殖场未建立养殖档案或者未按照规定保存养殖档案的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正，可以处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 86** Whoever, in violation of this Law, establishes a livestock or poultry farm without filing for record, or fails to establish breeding files or fails to preserve breeding files as required, shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a specified period, and may be fined not more than 10,000 RMB.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反[本法第四十三条](#art-43)规定养殖畜禽的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 87** Whoever raises livestock or poultry in violation of the provisions of Article 43 of this Law shall be dealt with and punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，销售的种畜禽未附具种畜禽合格证明、家畜系谱，销售、收购国务院农业农村主管部门规定应当加施标识而没有标识的畜禽，或者重复使用畜禽标识的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工责令改正，可以处二千元以下罚款。  
销售的种畜禽未附具检疫证明，伪造、变造畜禽标识，或者持有、使用伪造、变造的畜禽标识的，依照《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 88** Whoever, in violation of this Law, sells breeding livestock or poultry without attaching a certificate of conformity for breeding livestock or poultry or a livestock pedigree, sells or purchases livestock or poultry that, as required by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, should have an identifier but does not have one, or reuses livestock or poultry identifiers shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs and the market supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level, according to their respective functions and duties, to make corrections, and may be fined not more than 2,000 RMB.  
  
Where breeding livestock or poultry are sold without attaching a quarantine certificate, livestock or poultry identifiers are forged or altered, or forged or altered livestock or poultry identifiers are held or used, legal liability shall be pursued in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　违反本法规定，未经定点从事畜禽屠宰活动的，依照有关法律法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 89** Whoever, in violation of this Law, engages in livestock or poultry slaughter without being designated shall be dealt with and punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门发现畜禽屠宰企业不再具备本法规定条件的，应当责令停业整顿，并限期整改；逾期仍未达到本法规定条件的，责令关闭，对实行定点屠宰管理的，由发证机关依法吊销定点屠宰证书。

**Article 90** Where the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level finds that a livestock or poultry slaughter enterprise no longer meets the conditions prescribed in this Law, it shall order the enterprise to suspend operations for rectification and make corrections within a specified period; if the enterprise still fails to meet the conditions prescribed in this Law upon the expiry of the period, it shall order the closure of the enterprise, and for enterprises subject to designated slaughter management, the issuing authority shall revoke the designated slaughter certificate according to law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　违反[本法第六十八条](#art-68)规定，畜禽屠宰企业未建立畜禽屠宰质量安全管理制度，或者畜禽屠宰经营者对经检验不合格的畜禽产品未按照国家有关规定处理的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二千元以上二万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令关闭，对实行定点屠宰管理的，由发证机关依法吊销定点屠宰证书。  
违反[本法第六十八条](#art-68)规定的其他行为的，依照有关法律法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 91** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 68 of this Law, a livestock or poultry slaughter enterprise fails to establish a quality safety management system for livestock or poultry slaughter, or a livestock or poultry slaughter operator fails to handle livestock or poultry products that have been tested as unqualified in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections and give a warning; if corrections are refused, it shall order suspension of operations for rectification and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 RMB but not more than 50,000 RMB, and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 RMB but not more than 20,000 RMB on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order closure, and for enterprises subject to designated slaughter management, the issuing authority shall revoke the designated slaughter certificate according to law.  
  
Other acts in violation of the provisions of Article 68 of this Law shall be dealt with and punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 92** Whoever, in violation of this Law, commits a criminal offense shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　本法所称畜禽遗传资源，是指畜禽及其卵子（蛋）、精液、胚胎、基因物质等遗传材料。  
本法所称种畜禽，是指经过选育、具有种用价值、适于繁殖后代的畜禽及其卵子（蛋）、精液、胚胎等。

**Article 93** For the purposes of this Law, "livestock and poultry genetic resources" means livestock and poultry and their genetic materials such as ova (eggs), semen, embryos, and genetic substances.  
  
For the purposes of this Law, "breeding livestock and poultry" means livestock and poultry that have been selected and bred, have breeding value, and are suitable for reproduction, as well as their ova (eggs), semen, embryos, and the like.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　本法自2023年3月1日起施行。

**Article 94** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2023.
