### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强计量监督管理，保障国家计量单位制的统一和量值的准确可靠，有利于生产、贸易和科学技术的发展，适应社会主义现代化建设的需要，维护国家、人民的利益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to strengthen the supervision and administration of measurements, ensure the uniformity of the national system of measurement units and the accuracy and reliability of measurement values, facilitate the development of production, trade and science and technology, meet the needs of socialist modernization, and safeguard the interests of the State and the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，建立计量基准器具、计量标准器具，进行计量检定，制造、修理、销售、使用计量器具，必须遵守本法。

**Article 2** All units and individuals that establish primary measurement standards or measurement standards, conduct measurement verification, or manufacture, repair, sell or use measuring instruments within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行法定计量单位制度。  
国际单位制计量单位和国家选定的其他计量单位，为国家法定计量单位。国家法定计量单位的名称、符号由国务院公布。  
因特殊需要采用非法定计量单位的管理办法，由国务院计量行政部门另行制定。

**Article 3** The State shall adopt a system of legal measurement units.  
The International System of Units (SI) measurement units and other measurement units selected by the State shall be the national legal measurement units. The names and symbols of the national legal measurement units shall be promulgated by the State Council.  
The administrative measures for the use of non-legal measurement units due to special needs shall be formulated separately by the metrological administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院计量行政部门对全国计量工作实施统一监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门对本行政区域内的计量工作实施监督管理。

**Article 4** The metrological administration department under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the nationwide measurement work.  
The metrological administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise supervision and administration over the measurement work within their respective administrative areas.

### 第二章　计量基准器具、计量标准器具和计量检定

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院计量行政部门负责建立各种计量基准器具，作为统一全国量值的最高依据。

**Article 5** The metrological administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for establishing various primary measurement standards to serve as the supreme basis for unifying the measurement values nationwide.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门根据本地区的需要，建立社会公用计量标准器具，经上级人民政府计量行政部门主持考核合格后使用。

**Article 6** The metrological administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level may, based on the needs of their respective areas, establish public measurement standards and use them after passing the assessment presided over by the metrological administration department of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关主管部门，根据本部门的特殊需要，可以建立本部门使用的计量标准器具，其各项最高计量标准器具经同级人民政府计量行政部门主持考核合格后使用。

**Article 7** The relevant competent departments under the State Council and the relevant competent departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the special needs of their respective departments, establish measurement standards for use by their own departments, and the highest measurement standards of each type shall be used after passing the assessment presided over by the metrological administration department of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业、事业单位根据需要，可以建立本单位使用的计量标准器具，其各项最高计量标准器具经有关人民政府计量行政部门主持考核合格后使用。

**Article 8** Enterprises and institutions may, based on their needs, establish measurement standards for use by their own units, and the highest measurement standards of each type shall be used after passing the assessment presided over by the relevant metrological administration department of the people's government.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门对社会公用计量标准器具，部门和企业、事业单位使用的最高计量标准器具，以及用于贸易结算、安全防护、医疗卫生、环境监测方面的列入强制检定目录的工作计量器具，实行强制检定。未按照规定申请检定或者检定不合格的，不得使用。实行强制检定的工作计量器具的目录和管理办法，由国务院制定。  
对前款规定以外的其他计量标准器具和工作计量器具，使用单位应当自行定期检定或者送其他计量检定机构检定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具检定管理办法.md#art-1" title="被《中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具检定管理办法》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** The metrological administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall subject the following to compulsory verification: public measurement standards; the highest measurement standards used by departments, enterprises and institutions; and working measuring instruments listed in the catalogue of compulsory verification and used in trade settlement, safety protection, medical and health care, and environmental monitoring. Those that have not applied for verification as prescribed or have been found unqualified after verification shall not be used. The catalogue and administrative measures for working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification shall be formulated by the State Council.  
For measurement standards and working measuring instruments other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, the user units shall conduct periodical verification themselves or send them to other measurement verification institutions for verification.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　计量检定必须按照国家计量检定系统表进行。国家计量检定系统表由国务院计量行政部门制定。  
计量检定必须执行计量检定规程。国家计量检定规程由国务院计量行政部门制定。没有国家计量检定规程的，由国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府计量行政部门分别制定部门计量检定规程和地方计量检定规程。

**Article 10** Measurement verification must be carried out in accordance with the national measurement verification system tables. The national measurement verification system tables shall be formulated by the metrological administration department under the State Council.  
Measurement verification must be executed in accordance with the measurement verification regulations. The national measurement verification regulations shall be formulated by the metrological administration department under the State Council. In the absence of national measurement verification regulations, the relevant competent departments under the State Council and the metrological administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate departmental and local measurement verification regulations respectively.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　计量检定工作应当按照经济合理的原则，就地就近进行。

**Article 11** Measurement verification work shall be carried out locally or in nearby locations in accordance with the principle of economic rationality.

### 第三章　计量器具管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　制造、修理计量器具的企业、事业单位，必须具有与所制造、修理的计量器具相适应的设施、人员和检定仪器设备。

**Article 12** Enterprises and institutions that manufacture or repair measuring instruments must possess facilities, personnel and verification instruments and equipment appropriate for the measuring instruments they manufacture or repair.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　制造计量器具的企业、事业单位生产本单位未生产过的计量器具新产品，必须经省级以上人民政府计量行政部门对其样品的计量性能考核合格，方可投入生产。

**Article 13** Enterprises and institutions that manufacture measuring instruments must, before putting into production new types of measuring instruments that they have not previously manufactured, obtain assessment approval from the metrological administration department of the people's government at or above the provincial level regarding the metrological performance of the sample products.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位和个人不得违反规定制造、销售和进口非法定计量单位的计量器具。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国计量法实施细则.md#art-41" title="被《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》第41条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 14** No unit or individual shall manufacture, sell or import measuring instruments in non-legal measurement units in violation of the provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　制造、修理计量器具的企业、事业单位必须对制造、修理的计量器具进行检定，保证产品计量性能合格，并对合格产品出具产品合格证。

**Article 15** Enterprises and institutions that manufacture or repair measuring instruments must verify the measuring instruments they manufacture or repair, ensure that the metrological performance of the products is up to standard, and issue product certificates for qualified products.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　使用计量器具不得破坏其准确度，损害国家和消费者的利益。

**Article 16** No one shall, when using measuring instruments, damage their accuracy so as to harm the interests of the State or consumers.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　个体工商户可以制造、修理简易的计量器具。  
个体工商户制造、修理计量器具的范围和管理办法，由国务院计量行政部门制定。

**Article 17** Individual industrial and commercial households may manufacture or repair simple measuring instruments.  
The scope and administrative measures for the manufacture and repair of measuring instruments by individual industrial and commercial households shall be formulated by the metrological administration department under the State Council.

### 第四章　计量监督

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门应当依法对制造、修理、销售、进口和使用计量器具，以及计量检定等相关计量活动进行监督检查。有关单位和个人不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 18** The metrological administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection over the manufacture, repair, sale, import and use of measuring instruments, as well as over measurement verification and other related measurement activities. The relevant units and individuals shall not refuse or obstruct such supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门，根据需要设置计量监督员。计量监督员管理办法，由国务院计量行政部门制定。

**Article 19** The metrological administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level may, as needed, appoint metrological supervisors. The administrative measures for metrological supervisors shall be formulated by the metrological administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门可以根据需要设置计量检定机构，或者授权其他单位的计量检定机构，执行强制检定和其他检定、测试任务。  
执行前款规定的检定、测试任务的人员，必须经考核合格。

**Article 20** The metrological administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level may, as needed, establish measurement verification institutions or authorize measurement verification institutions of other units to carry out compulsory verification and other verification and testing tasks.  
Personnel performing the verification and testing tasks specified in the preceding paragraph must pass the assessment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　处理因计量器具准确度所引起的纠纷，以国家计量基准器具或者社会公用计量标准器具检定的数据为准。

**Article 21** Disputes arising from the accuracy of measuring instruments shall be settled based on the data verified by national primary measurement standards or public measurement standards.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　为社会提供公证数据的产品质量检验机构，必须经省级以上人民政府计量行政部门对其计量检定、测试的能力和可靠性考核合格。

**Article 22** Product quality inspection institutions that provide notarized data to the society must obtain assessment approval from the metrological administration department of the people's government at or above the provincial level regarding their capability and reliability in measurement verification and testing.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　制造、销售未经考核合格的计量器具新产品的，责令停止制造、销售该种新产品，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 23** Where anyone manufactures or sells new types of measuring instruments that have not passed the assessment, the said person shall be ordered to stop manufacturing or selling such new types, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　制造、修理、销售的计量器具不合格的，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 24** Where measuring instruments manufactured, repaired or sold are unqualified, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　属于强制检定范围的计量器具，未按照规定申请检定或者检定不合格继续使用的，责令停止使用，可以并处罚款。

**Article 25** Where measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification continue to be used without having been applied for verification as prescribed or after being found unqualified upon verification, the user shall be ordered to stop using them, and a fine may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　使用不合格的计量器具或者破坏计量器具准确度，给国家和消费者造成损失的，责令赔偿损失，没收计量器具和违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 26** Where anyone uses unqualified measuring instruments or damages the accuracy of measuring instruments, causing losses to the State or consumers, the person shall be ordered to compensate for the losses, the measuring instruments and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　制造、销售、使用以欺骗消费者为目的的计量器具的，没收计量器具和违法所得，处以罚款；情节严重的，并对个人或者单位直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where anyone manufactures, sells or uses measuring instruments for the purpose of deceiving consumers, the measuring instruments and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the individual or the person directly in charge of the unit shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本法规定，制造、修理、销售的计量器具不合格，造成人身伤亡或者重大财产损失的，依照刑法有关规定，对个人或者单位直接责任人员追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, manufactures, repairs or sells unqualified measuring instruments, causing personal injury or death or major property losses, the individual or the person directly in charge of the unit shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　计量监督人员违法失职，情节严重的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；情节轻微的，给予行政处分。

**Article 29** Where metrological supervisors violate the law or are derelict in their duties, if the circumstances are serious, they shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the circumstances are minor, they shall be given administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法规定的行政处罚，由县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门决定。

**Article 30** The administrative penalties provided for in this Law shall be decided by the metrological administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉；对罚款、没收违法所得的行政处罚决定期满不起诉又不履行的，由作出行政处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 31** If a party is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, file a prosecution with the People's Court. If, upon expiry of the time limit, the party has neither initiated prosecution nor complied with the decision on the administrative penalty of a fine or confiscation of illegal gains, the organ that made the decision on the administrative penalty may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统计量工作的监督管理办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会依据本法另行制定。

**Article 32** The supervision and administration measures for measurement work in the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the national defense science, technology and industry system shall be formulated separately by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院计量行政部门根据本法制定实施细则，报国务院批准施行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/国防计量监督管理条例.md#art-1" title="被《国防计量监督管理条例》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** The metrological administration department under the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementing rules and submit them to the State Council for approval before they come into force.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本法自1986年7月1日起施行。

**Article 34** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 1986.
