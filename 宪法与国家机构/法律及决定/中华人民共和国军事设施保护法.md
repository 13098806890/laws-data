### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护军事设施的安全，保障军事设施的使用效能和军事活动的正常进行，加强国防现代化建设，巩固国防，抵御侵略，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of protecting the security of military installations, ensuring the operational effectiveness of military installations and the normal conduct of military activities, strengthening the modernization of national defense, consolidating national defense, and resisting aggression.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称军事设施，是指国家直接用于军事目的的下列建筑、场地和设备：  
（一）指挥机关，地上和地下的指挥工程、作战工程；  
（二）军用机场、港口、码头；  
（三）营区、训练场、试验场；  
（四）军用洞库、仓库；  
（五）军用信息基础设施，军用侦察、导航、观测台站，军用测量、导航、助航标志；  
（六）军用公路、铁路专用线，军用输电线路，军用输油、输水、输气管道；  
（七）边防、海防管控设施；  
（八）国务院和中央军事委员会规定的其他军事设施。  
前款规定的军事设施，包括军队为执行任务必需设置的临时设施。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "military installations" mean the following buildings, sites and equipment directly used by the State for military purposes:  
(1) command organs, above-ground and underground command works, and combat works;  
(2) military airfields, ports, and docks;  
(3) barracks, training grounds, and test ranges;  
(4) military caves and warehouses;  
(5) military information infrastructure, military reconnaissance, navigation, and observation stations, and military surveying, navigation, and navigation aids;  
(6) military highways, railway special lines, military power transmission lines, and military oil, water, and gas pipelines;  
(7) border defense and coastal defense control facilities;  
(8) other military installations as prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The military installations specified in the preceding paragraph include temporary installations the military must set up for the purpose of performing tasks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军事设施保护工作坚持中国共产党的领导。各级人民政府和军事机关应当共同保护军事设施，维护国防利益。  
国务院、中央军事委员会按照职责分工，管理全国的军事设施保护工作。地方各级人民政府会同有关军事机关，管理本行政区域内的军事设施保护工作。  
有关军事机关应当按照规定的权限和程序，提出需要地方人民政府落实的军事设施保护需求，地方人民政府应当会同有关军事机关制定具体保护措施并予以落实。  
设有军事设施的地方，有关军事机关和县级以上地方人民政府应当建立军地军事设施保护协调机制，相互配合，监督、检查军事设施的保护工作，协调解决军事设施保护工作中的问题。

**Article 3** The work of protecting military installations shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China. People's governments at all levels and military organs shall jointly protect military installations and safeguard national defense interests.  
The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, administer the work of protecting military installations nationwide. Local people's governments at all levels shall, in conjunction with the relevant military organs, administer the work of protecting military installations within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant military organs shall, in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures, propose the requirements for the protection of military installations that need to be implemented by local people's governments, and the local people's governments shall, in conjunction with the relevant military organs, formulate specific protection measures and implement them.  
In places where military installations are located, the relevant military organs and local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a military-local coordination mechanism for the protection of military installations, cooperate with each other, supervise and inspect the protection of military installations, and coordinate to resolve problems arising in the work of protecting military installations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国的组织和公民都有保护军事设施的义务。  
禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、危害军事设施。  
任何组织或者个人对破坏、危害军事设施的行为，都有权检举、控告。

**Article 4** All organizations and citizens of the People's Republic of China shall have the obligation to protect military installations.  
It is prohibited for any organization or individual to damage or endanger military installations.  
Any organization or individual shall have the right to report and accuse any act of damaging or endangering military installations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家统筹兼顾经济建设、社会发展和军事设施保护，促进经济社会发展和军事设施保护相协调。

**Article 5** The State shall make overall plans and take into consideration economic construction, social development, and the protection of military installations, so as to promote the coordination between economic and social development and the protection of military installations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家对军事设施实行分类保护、确保重点的方针。军事设施的分类和保护标准，由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 6** The State shall adopt the policy of classified protection and ensuring key points for military installations. The classification and protection standards of military installations shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对因设有军事设施、经济建设受到较大影响的地方，采取相应扶持政策和措施。具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 7** The State shall adopt corresponding support policies and measures for places where economic construction is significantly affected due to the establishment of military installations. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在军事设施保护工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，依照有关法律、法规的规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 8** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions in the work of protecting military installations shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第二章　军事禁区、军事管理区的划定

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军事禁区、军事管理区根据军事设施的性质、作用、安全保密的需要和使用效能的要求划定，具体划定标准和确定程序，由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。  
本法所称军事禁区，是指设有重要军事设施或者军事设施安全保密要求高、具有重大危险因素，需要国家采取特殊措施加以重点保护，依照法定程序和标准划定的军事区域。  
本法所称军事管理区，是指设有较重要军事设施或者军事设施安全保密要求较高、具有较大危险因素，需要国家采取特殊措施加以保护，依照法定程序和标准划定的军事区域。

**Article 9** Military restricted zones and military management zones shall be delimited based on the nature, function, security and confidentiality requirements, and operational effectiveness needs of military installations. The specific delimitation standards and determination procedures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
For the purposes of this Law, "military restricted zone" means a military area delimited in accordance with statutory procedures and standards, where important military installations are located, or where military installations have high security and confidentiality requirements and significant risk factors, and which requires the State to take special measures for key protection.  
For the purposes of this Law, "military management zone" means a military area delimited in accordance with statutory procedures and standards, where relatively important military installations are located, or where military installations have relatively high security and confidentiality requirements and relatively large risk factors, and which requires the State to take special measures for protection.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军事禁区、军事管理区由国务院和中央军事委员会确定，或者由有关军事机关根据国务院和中央军事委员会的规定确定。  
军事禁区、军事管理区的撤销或者变更，依照前款规定办理。

**Article 10** Military restricted zones and military management zones shall be determined by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, or by the relevant military organs in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The revocation or alteration of military restricted zones and military management zones shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　陆地和水域的军事禁区、军事管理区的范围，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和有关军级以上军事机关共同划定，或者由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府、国务院有关部门和有关军级以上军事机关共同划定。空中军事禁区和特别重要的陆地、水域军事禁区的范围，由国务院和中央军事委员会划定。  
军事禁区、军事管理区的范围调整，依照前款规定办理。

**Article 11** The scope of military restricted zones and military management zones on land and water shall be jointly delimited by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the relevant military organs at or above the corps level, or jointly delimited by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the relevant departments of the State Council, and the relevant military organs at or above the corps level. The scope of air military restricted zones and particularly important land and water military restricted zones shall be delimited by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The adjustment of the scope of military restricted zones and military management zones shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军事禁区、军事管理区应当由县级以上地方人民政府按照国家统一规定的样式设置标志牌。

**Article 12** Military restricted zones and military management zones shall be provided with signs by local people's governments at or above the county level in the style uniformly prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军事禁区、军事管理区范围的划定或者调整，应当在确保军事设施安全保密和使用效能的前提下，兼顾经济建设、生态环境保护和当地居民的生产生活。  
因军事设施建设需要划定或者调整军事禁区、军事管理区范围的，应当在军事设施建设项目开工建设前完成。但是，经战区级以上军事机关批准的除外。

**Article 13** The delimitation or adjustment of the scope of military restricted zones and military management zones shall, on the premise of ensuring the security, confidentiality, and operational effectiveness of military installations, take into account economic construction, ecological environment protection, and the production and daily life of local residents.  
Where the delimitation or adjustment of the scope of military restricted zones and military management zones is required due to the construction of military installations, it shall be completed before the commencement of the military installation construction project, unless otherwise approved by a military organ at or above the theater level.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军事禁区、军事管理区范围的划定或者调整，需要征收、征用土地、房屋等不动产，压覆矿产资源，或者使用海域、空域等的，依照有关法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 14** Where the delimitation or adjustment of the scope of military restricted zones and military management zones requires the expropriation or requisition of immovable property such as land and houses, the overlapping of mineral resources, or the use of sea areas and airspace, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军队为执行任务设置的临时军事设施需要划定陆地、水域临时军事禁区、临时军事管理区范围的，由县级以上地方人民政府和有关团级以上军事机关共同划定，并各自向上一级机关备案。其中，涉及有关海事管理机构职权的，应当在划定前征求其意见。划定之后，由县级以上地方人民政府或者有关海事管理机构予以公告。  
军队执行任务结束后，应当依照前款规定的程序及时撤销划定的陆地、水域临时军事禁区、临时军事管理区。

**Article 15** Where temporary military installations set up by the military for the purpose of performing tasks require the delimitation of temporary military restricted zones and temporary military management zones on land or water, they shall be jointly delimited by local people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant military organs at or above the regiment level, and each shall file a record with the authority at the next higher level. Where the functions and powers of the relevant maritime administrative agency are involved, its opinions shall be sought before the delimitation. After the delimitation, the local people's government at or above the county level or the relevant maritime administrative agency shall make a public announcement.  
After the military completes its tasks, it shall promptly revoke the delimited temporary military restricted zones and temporary military management zones on land or water in accordance with the procedures prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

### 第三章　军事禁区的保护

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军事禁区管理单位应当根据具体条件，按照划定的范围，为陆地军事禁区修筑围墙、设置铁丝网等障碍物，为水域军事禁区设置障碍物或者界线标志。  
水域军事禁区的范围难以在实际水域设置障碍物或者界线标志的，有关海事管理机构应当向社会公告水域军事禁区的位置和边界。海域的军事禁区应当在海图上标明。

**Article 16** The management unit of a military restricted zone shall, based on specific conditions and in accordance with the delimited scope, construct walls, set up barbed wire and other obstacles for land military restricted zones, and set up obstacles or boundary markers for water military restricted zones.  
Where it is difficult to set up obstacles or boundary markers in the actual waters due to the scope of a water military restricted zone, the relevant maritime administrative agency shall publicly announce the location and boundaries of the water military restricted zone. Military restricted zones in sea areas shall be marked on nautical charts.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止陆地、水域军事禁区管理单位以外的人员、车辆、船舶等进入军事禁区，禁止航空器在陆地、水域军事禁区上空进行低空飞行，禁止对军事禁区进行摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述。但是，经有关军事机关批准的除外。  
禁止航空器进入空中军事禁区，但依照国家有关规定获得批准的除外。  
使用军事禁区的摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述资料，应当经有关军事机关批准。

**Article 17** It is prohibited for persons, vehicles, ships, etc., other than those of the management unit of a military restricted zone, to enter the military restricted zone; it is prohibited for aircraft to conduct low-altitude flights over land or water military restricted zones; and it is prohibited to photograph, videotape, record, survey, measure, position, depict, or describe military restricted zones, unless otherwise approved by the relevant military organ.  
It is prohibited for aircraft to enter air military restricted zones, unless otherwise approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The use of photographs, videotapes, recordings, surveys, measurements, positioning data, depictions, or descriptions of military restricted zones shall be subject to the approval of the relevant military organ.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在陆地军事禁区内，禁止建造、设置非军事设施，禁止开发利用地下空间。但是，经战区级以上军事机关批准的除外。  
在水域军事禁区内，禁止建造、设置非军事设施，禁止从事水产养殖、捕捞以及其他妨碍军用舰船行动、危害军事设施安全和使用效能的活动。

**Article 18** Within land military restricted zones, it is prohibited to construct or set up non-military installations and to develop and utilize underground space, unless otherwise approved by a military organ at or above the theater level.  
Within water military restricted zones, it is prohibited to construct or set up non-military installations and to engage in aquaculture, fishing, or other activities that obstruct the operation of military vessels or endanger the security and operational effectiveness of military installations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在陆地、水域军事禁区内采取的防护措施不足以保证军事设施安全保密和使用效能，或者陆地、水域军事禁区内的军事设施具有重大危险因素的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和有关军事机关，或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府、国务院有关部门和有关军事机关根据军事设施性质、地形和当地经济建设、社会发展情况，可以在共同划定陆地、水域军事禁区范围的同时，在禁区外围共同划定安全控制范围，并在其外沿设置安全警戒标志。  
安全警戒标志由县级以上地方人民政府按照国家统一规定的样式设置，地点由军事禁区管理单位和当地县级以上地方人民政府共同确定。  
水域军事禁区外围安全控制范围难以在实际水域设置安全警戒标志的，依照[本法第十六条](#art-16)第二款的规定执行。

**Article 19** Where the protective measures taken within land or water military restricted zones are insufficient to ensure the security, confidentiality, and operational effectiveness of military installations, or where military installations within land or water military restricted zones have significant risk factors, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the relevant military organs, or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the relevant departments of the State Council, and the relevant military organs, may, while jointly delimiting the scope of the land or water military restricted zones, jointly delimit a safety control perimeter around the restricted zones and set up safety warning signs on the outer edge thereof.  
The safety warning signs shall be set up by local people's governments at or above the county level in the style uniformly prescribed by the State, and the locations shall be jointly determined by the management unit of the military restricted zone and the local people's government at or above the county level.  
Where it is difficult to set up safety warning signs in the actual waters within the safety control perimeter outside a water military restricted zone, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 16 of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　划定陆地、水域军事禁区外围安全控制范围，不改变原土地及土地附着物、水域的所有权。在陆地、水域军事禁区外围安全控制范围内，当地居民可以照常生产生活，但是不得进行爆破、射击以及其他危害军事设施安全和使用效能的活动。  
因划定军事禁区外围安全控制范围影响不动产所有权人或者用益物权人行使权利的，依照有关法律、法规的规定予以补偿。

**Article 20** The delimitation of a safety control perimeter outside land or water military restricted zones shall not change the ownership of the original land and its attachments or the water area. Within the safety control perimeter outside land or water military restricted zones, local residents may carry out production and daily life as usual, but shall not engage in blasting, shooting, or other activities that endanger the security or operational effectiveness of military installations.  
Where the delimitation of a safety control perimeter outside a military restricted zone affects the exercise of rights by the owner of the immovable property or the usufructuary, compensation shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第四章　军事管理区的保护

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军事管理区管理单位应当根据具体条件，按照划定的范围，为军事管理区修筑围墙、设置铁丝网或者界线标志。

**Article 21** The administrative unit of a military administrative zone shall, based on specific conditions and in accordance with the delineated scope, construct walls, install barbed wire, or set up boundary markers for the military administrative zone.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军事管理区管理单位以外的人员、车辆、船舶等进入军事管理区，或者对军事管理区进行摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述，必须经军事管理区管理单位批准。

**Article 22** Any persons, vehicles, ships, etc., other than the administrative unit of a military administrative zone, who enter the military administrative zone, or take photographs, video recordings, sound recordings, surveys, measurements, positioning, drawings, or descriptions of the military administrative zone, must obtain approval from the administrative unit of the military administrative zone.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在陆地军事管理区内，禁止建造、设置非军事设施，禁止开发利用地下空间。但是，经军级以上军事机关批准的除外。  
在水域军事管理区内，禁止从事水产养殖；未经军级以上军事机关批准，不得建造、设置非军事设施；从事捕捞或者其他活动，不得影响军用舰船的战备、训练、执勤等行动。

**Article 23** In a terrestrial military administrative zone, it is prohibited to construct or install non-military facilities, and to develop or utilize underground space. However, this shall not apply to cases approved by a military organ at or above the corps level.  
In a water area military administrative zone, aquaculture is prohibited; without approval from a military organ at or above the corps level, no non-military facilities shall be constructed or installed; fishing or other activities shall not affect the combat readiness, training, duty, or other operations of military vessels.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　划为军事管理区的军民合用港口的水域，实行军地分区管理；在地方管理的水域内需要新建非军事设施的，必须事先征得军事设施管理单位的同意。  
划为军事管理区的军民合用机场、港口、码头的管理办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 24** For waters of harbors jointly used by the military and civilians that are designated as military administrative zones, separate management by the military and local authorities shall be implemented; if a new non-military facility needs to be constructed in the waters under local management, prior consent from the military facility administrative unit must be obtained.  
Measures for the administration of airports, harbors, and docks jointly used by the military and civilians that are designated as military administrative zones shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第五章　没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施的保护

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施，军事设施管理单位应当采取措施予以保护；军队团级以上管理单位也可以委托当地人民政府予以保护。

**Article 25** For military facilities that are not included in a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, the military facility administrative unit shall take measures to protect them; a military administrative unit at the regimental level or above may also entrust the local people's government with their protection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施一定距离内进行采石、取土、爆破等活动，不得危害军事设施的安全和使用效能。

**Article 26** Quarrying, soil extraction, blasting, or other activities conducted within a certain distance from military facilities that are not included in a military restricted zone or military administrative zone shall not endanger the safety or operational effectiveness of the military facilities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的作战工程外围应当划定安全保护范围。作战工程的安全保护范围，应当根据作战工程性质、地形和当地经济建设、社会发展情况，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和有关军事机关共同划定，或者由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府、国务院有关部门和有关军事机关共同划定。在作战工程布局相对集中的地区，作战工程安全保护范围可以连片划定。县级以上地方人民政府应当按照有关规定为作战工程安全保护范围设置界线标志。  
作战工程安全保护范围的撤销或者调整，依照前款规定办理。

**Article 27** A safety protection zone shall be delineated around the periphery of operational engineering works that are not included in a military restricted zone or military administrative zone. The safety protection zone of operational engineering works shall be jointly delineated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the relevant military organs, or jointly by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the relevant department of the State Council, and the relevant military organs, based on the nature of the operational engineering works, the terrain, and local economic construction and social development. In areas where operational engineering works are relatively concentrated, the safety protection zones may be delineated contiguously. The local people's government at or above the county level shall set up boundary markers for the safety protection zones of operational engineering works in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The cancellation or adjustment of a safety protection zone of operational engineering works shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　划定作战工程安全保护范围，不改变原土地及土地附着物的所有权。在作战工程安全保护范围内，当地居民可以照常生产生活，但是不得进行开山采石、采矿、爆破；从事修筑建筑物、构筑物、道路和进行农田水利基本建设、采伐林木等活动，不得危害作战工程安全和使用效能。  
因划定作战工程安全保护范围影响不动产所有权人或者用益物权人行使权利的，依照有关法律、法规的规定予以补偿。  
禁止私自开启封闭的作战工程，禁止破坏作战工程的伪装，禁止阻断进出作战工程的通道。未经作战工程管理单位师级以上的上级主管军事机关批准，不得对作战工程进行摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述，不得在作战工程内存放非军用物资器材或者从事种植、养殖等生产活动。  
新建工程和建设项目，确实难以避开作战工程的，应当按照国家有关规定提出拆除或者迁建、改建作战工程的申请；申请未获批准的，不得拆除或者迁建、改建作战工程。

**Article 28** Delineating a safety protection zone for operational engineering works shall not alter the ownership of the original land and attachments thereon. Within the safety protection zone of operational engineering works, local residents may engage in production and daily life as usual, but shall not engage in mountain quarrying, mining, or blasting; activities such as constructing buildings, structures, roads, carrying out farmland water conservancy capital construction, or felling trees shall not endanger the safety or operational effectiveness of the operational engineering works.  
If the delineation of a safety protection zone for operational engineering works affects the exercise of rights by the owner of the real property or the usufructuary, compensation shall be granted in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
It is prohibited to open sealed operational engineering works without authorization, to damage the camouflage of operational engineering works, or to block the passages to and from operational engineering works. Without approval from the superior competent military organ at or above the division level of the administrative unit of the operational engineering works, no photography, video recording, sound recording, survey, measurement, positioning, drawing, or description of the operational engineering works shall be conducted, and no non-military materials or equipment shall be stored in operational engineering works, nor shall production activities such as planting or breeding be carried out.  
For new projects and construction items that are truly unable to avoid operational engineering works, an application for demolition, relocation, or reconstruction of the operational engineering works shall be submitted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if the application is not approved, the operational engineering works shall not be demolished, relocated, or reconstructed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在军用机场净空保护区域内，禁止修建超出机场净空标准的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施，不得从事影响飞行安全和机场助航设施使用效能的活动。  
军用机场管理单位应当定期检查机场净空保护情况，发现修建的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施超过军用机场净空保护标准的，应当及时向有关军事机关和当地人民政府主管部门报告。有关军事机关和当地人民政府主管部门应当依照本法规定及时处理。

**Article 29** Within the clear zone protection area of a military airport, it is prohibited to construct buildings, structures, or other facilities that exceed the airport clear zone standards, and no activities that affect flight safety or the operational effectiveness of airport navigation aids shall be conducted.  
The administrative unit of a military airport shall regularly inspect the airport clear zone protection status. If it discovers that the constructed buildings, structures, or other facilities exceed the clear zone protection standards of the military airport, it shall promptly report to the relevant military organ and the competent department of the local people's government. The relevant military organ and the competent department of the local people's government shall promptly handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有关军事机关应当向地方人民政府通报当地军用机场净空保护有关情况和需求。  
地方人民政府应当向有关军事机关通报可能影响军用机场净空保护的当地有关国土空间规划和高大建筑项目建设计划。  
地方人民政府应当制定保护措施，督促有关单位对军用机场净空保护区域内的高大建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施设置飞行障碍标志。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国军事设施保护法实施办法.md#art-42" title="被《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法实施办法》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** The relevant military organ shall notify the local people's government of the relevant situations and needs concerning the clear zone protection of the local military airport.  
The local people's government shall notify the relevant military organ of the relevant local territorial spatial plans and construction plans for tall buildings that may affect the clear zone protection of the military airport.  
The local people's government shall formulate protection measures and urge the relevant units to set up flight obstacle markers for tall buildings, structures, or other facilities within the clear zone protection area of the military airport.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　军民合用机场以及由军队管理的保留旧机场、直升机起落坪的净空保护工作，适用军用机场净空保护的有关规定。  
公路飞机跑道的净空保护工作，参照军用机场净空保护的有关规定执行。

**Article 31** The clear zone protection work for airports jointly used by the military and civilians, and for retained old airports and helicopter landing pads managed by the military, shall apply the relevant provisions on clear zone protection for military airports.  
The clear zone protection work for highway airstrips shall be carried out with reference to the relevant provisions on clear zone protection for military airports.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府和有关军事机关采取委托看管、分段负责等方式，实行军民联防，保护军用管线安全。  
地下军用管线应当设立路由标石或者永久性标志，易遭损坏的路段、部位应当设置标志牌。已经公布具体位置、边界和路由的海域水下军用管线应当在海图上标明。

**Article 32** The local people's governments at all levels and the relevant military organs shall adopt methods such as entrusted guard and segmented responsibility to implement joint defense by the military and civilians, thereby protecting the safety of military pipelines.  
Underground military pipelines shall have route markers or permanent signs installed; sections or parts prone to damage shall be provided with signboards. Submarine military pipelines whose specific locations, boundaries, and routes have been published shall be marked on nautical charts.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在军用无线电固定设施电磁环境保护范围内，禁止建造、设置影响军用无线电固定设施使用效能的设备和电磁障碍物体，不得从事影响军用无线电固定设施电磁环境的活动。  
军用无线电固定设施电磁环境的保护措施，由军地无线电管理机构按照国家无线电管理相关规定和标准共同确定。  
军事禁区、军事管理区内无线电固定设施电磁环境的保护，适用前两款规定。  
军用无线电固定设施电磁环境保护涉及军事系统与非军事系统间的无线电管理事宜的，按照国家无线电管理的有关规定执行。

**Article 33** Within the electromagnetic environment protection zone of fixed military radio facilities, it is prohibited to construct or install equipment or electromagnetic obstacles that affect the operational effectiveness of fixed military radio facilities, and no activities that affect the electromagnetic environment of fixed military radio facilities shall be conducted.  
Protection measures for the electromagnetic environment of fixed military radio facilities shall be jointly determined by the military and local radio regulatory authorities in accordance with the relevant provisions and standards of the national radio regulatory system.  
For the protection of the electromagnetic environment of fixed radio facilities within military restricted zones and military administrative zones, the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply.  
Where the electromagnetic environment protection of fixed military radio facilities involves radio regulatory matters between military systems and non-military systems, the relevant provisions of the national radio regulatory system shall be followed.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　未经国务院和中央军事委员会批准或者国务院和中央军事委员会授权的机关批准，不得拆除、移动边防、海防管控设施，不得在边防、海防管控设施上搭建、设置民用设施。在边防、海防管控设施周边安排建设项目，不得危害边防、海防管控设施安全和使用效能。

**Article 34** Without approval from the State Council and the Central Military Commission or from an organ authorized by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, no border or coastal defense control facilities shall be dismantled or moved, and no civilian facilities shall be erected or installed on such facilities. In arranging construction projects around border or coastal defense control facilities, the safety and operational effectiveness of such facilities shall not be endangered.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　任何组织和个人不得损毁或者擅自移动军用测量标志。在军用测量标志周边安排建设项目，不得危害军用测量标志安全和使用效能。  
军用测量标志的保护，依照有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 35** No organization or individual may damage or remove military survey markers without authorization. In arranging construction projects around military survey markers, the safety and operational effectiveness of such markers shall not be endangered.  
The protection of military survey markers shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第六章　管理职责

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府编制国民经济和社会发展规划、安排可能影响军事设施保护的建设项目，国务院有关部门、地方人民政府编制国土空间规划等规划，应当兼顾军事设施保护的需要，并按照规定书面征求有关军事机关的意见。必要时，可以由地方人民政府会同有关部门、有关军事机关对建设项目进行评估。  
国务院有关部门或者县级以上地方人民政府有关部门审批前款规定的建设项目，应当审查征求军事机关意见的情况；对未按规定征求军事机关意见的，应当要求补充征求意见；建设项目内容在审批过程中发生的改变可能影响军事设施保护的，应当再次征求有关军事机关的意见。  
有关军事机关应当自收到征求意见函之日起三十日内提出书面答复意见；需要请示上级军事机关或者需要勘察、测量、测试的，答复时间可以适当延长，但通常不得超过九十日。

**Article 36** When a local people's government at or above the county level formulates a national economic and social development plan or arranges construction projects that may affect the protection of military facilities, and when a relevant department of the State Council or a local people's government formulates territorial spatial plans or other plans, the needs of military facility protection shall be taken into account, and the opinion of the relevant military organ shall be sought in writing in accordance with the provisions. When necessary, the local people's government may, together with the relevant departments and the relevant military organ, evaluate the construction project.  
When the relevant department of the State Council or the relevant department of a local people's government at or above the county level examines and approves a construction project as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the circumstances of seeking opinions from the military organ shall be reviewed; if the opinion of the military organ has not been sought in accordance with the provisions, a supplementary request for opinions shall be required; if changes occur to the content of the construction project during the examination and approval process that may affect military facility protection, the opinion of the relevant military organ shall be sought again.  
The relevant military organ shall submit a written reply within thirty days from the date of receipt of the opinion-seeking letter; if it is necessary to request instructions from a higher military organ or to conduct surveys, measurements, or tests, the reply time may be appropriately extended, but generally shall not exceed ninety days.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　军队编制军事设施建设规划、组织军事设施项目建设，应当考虑地方经济建设、生态环境保护和社会发展的需要，符合国土空间规划等规划的总体要求，并进行安全保密环境评估和环境影响评价。涉及国土空间规划等规划的，应当征求国务院有关部门、地方人民政府的意见，尽量避开生态保护红线、自然保护地、地方经济建设热点区域和民用设施密集区域。确实不能避开，需要将生产生活设施拆除或者迁建的，应当依法进行。

**Article 37** When the military formulates military facility construction plans or organizes the construction of military facility projects, it shall take into account the needs of local economic construction, ecological environmental protection, and social development, comply with the overall requirements of territorial spatial plans and other plans, and conduct security and confidentiality environmental assessments and environmental impact assessments. Where territorial spatial plans or other plans are involved, the opinion of the relevant department of the State Council or the local people's government shall be sought, and efforts shall be made to avoid ecological protection red lines, nature reserves, local economic construction hotspots, and areas with dense civilian facilities as far as possible. If avoidance is truly impossible and the demolition or relocation of production and living facilities is necessary, such actions shall be carried out in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上地方人民政府安排建设项目或者开辟旅游景点，应当避开军事设施。确实不能避开，需要将军事设施拆除、迁建或者改作民用的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关部门和战区级军事机关商定，并报国务院和中央军事委员会批准或者国务院和中央军事委员会授权的机关批准；需要将军事设施改建的，由有关军事机关批准。  
因前款原因将军事设施拆除、迁建、改建或者改作民用的，由提出需求的地方人民政府依照有关规定给予有关军事机关政策支持或者经费补助。将军事设施迁建、改建涉及用地用海用岛的，地方人民政府应当依法及时办理相关手续。

**Article 38** When a local people's government at or above the county level arranges a construction project or opens a tourist attraction, it shall avoid military facilities. If avoidance is truly impossible and it is necessary to demolish, relocate, or convert military facilities to civilian use, the matter shall be determined through consultation by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or by the relevant department of the State Council and the theater-level military organ, and shall be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission, or to an organ authorized by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, for approval; if a military facility needs to be reconstructed, the relevant military organ shall grant approval.  
Where a military facility is demolished, relocated, reconstructed, or converted to civilian use for the reason mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the local people's government that put forward the requirement shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, provide policy support or financial subsidies to the relevant military organ. Where the relocation or reconstruction of a military facility involves the use of land, sea, or islands, the local people's government shall promptly handle the relevant procedures in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军事设施因军事任务调整、周边环境变化和自然损毁等原因，失去使用效能并无需恢复重建的，军事设施管理单位应当按照规定程序及时报国务院和中央军事委员会批准或者国务院和中央军事委员会授权的机关批准，予以拆除或者改作民用。  
军队执行任务结束后，应当及时将设置的临时军事设施拆除。

**Article 39** Where a military facility loses its operational effectiveness due to adjustments in military missions, changes in the surrounding environment, natural damage, or other reasons, and does not need to be restored or reconstructed, the military facility administrative unit shall promptly report to the State Council and the Central Military Commission, or to an organ authorized by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures, and shall demolish it or convert it to civilian use.  
After the military completes its mission, the temporary military facilities that have been set up shall be promptly dismantled.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军用机场、港口实行军民合用的，需经国务院和中央军事委员会批准。军用码头实行军民合用的，需经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关部门会同战区级军事机关批准。

**Article 40** Where a military airport or harbor is to be jointly used by the military and civilians, approval from the State Council and the Central Military Commission is required. Where a military dock is to be jointly used by the military and civilians, approval from the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or from the relevant department of the State Council, in conjunction with the theater-level military organ, is required.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　军事禁区、军事管理区和没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施，县级以上地方人民政府应当会同军事设施管理单位制定具体保护措施，可以公告施行。  
划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施的具体保护措施，应当随军事禁区、军事管理区范围划定方案一并报批。

**Article 41** With respect to military restricted zones, military administrative zones, and military installations not designated as military restricted zones or military administrative zones, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the military installation administration unit, formulate specific protection measures, which may be promulgated for implementation.  
The specific protection measures for military installations designated as military restricted zones or military administrative zones shall be submitted for approval together with the plan for delimiting the boundaries of the military restricted zones or military administrative zones.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　各级军事机关应当严格履行保护军事设施的职责，教育军队人员爱护军事设施，保守军事设施秘密，建立健全保护军事设施的规章制度，监督、检查、解决军事设施保护工作中的问题。  
有关军事机关应当支持配合军事设施保护执法、司法活动。

**Article 42** Military organs at all levels shall strictly perform their duties of protecting military installations, educate military personnel to cherish military installations, keep confidential the secrets of military installations, establish and improve rules and regulations for protecting military installations, and supervise, inspect, and resolve issues in the protection of military installations.  
The relevant military organs shall support and cooperate with law enforcement and judicial activities concerning the protection of military installations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军事设施管理单位应当认真执行有关保护军事设施的规章制度，建立军事设施档案，对军事设施进行检查、维护。  
军事设施管理单位对军事设施的重要部位应当采取安全监控和技术防范措施，并及时根据军事设施保护需要和科技进步升级完善。  
军事设施管理单位不得将军事设施用于非军事目的，但因执行应急救援等紧急任务的除外。

**Article 43** The military installation administration unit shall diligently implement the relevant rules and regulations on protecting military installations, establish archives of military installations, and inspect and maintain military installations.  
The military installation administration unit shall adopt security monitoring and technical prevention measures for the key parts of military installations, and shall promptly upgrade and improve them in accordance with the needs of military installation protection and technological progress.  
The military installation administration unit shall not use military installations for non-military purposes, except for the performance of emergency tasks such as emergency rescue and disaster relief.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　军事设施管理单位应当了解掌握军事设施周边建设项目等情况，发现可能危害军事设施安全和使用效能的，应当及时向有关军事机关和当地人民政府主管部门报告，并配合有关部门依法处理。

**Article 44** The military installation administration unit shall keep abreast of the construction projects and other situations around the military installations, and upon discovering any potential harm to the safety and operational effectiveness of military installations, shall promptly report to the relevant military organs and the competent departments of the local people's government, and shall cooperate with the relevant departments in handling the matter according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军事禁区、军事管理区的管理单位应当依照有关法律、法规的规定，保护军事禁区、军事管理区内的生态环境、自然资源和文物。

**Article 45** The administration units of military restricted zones and military administrative zones shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, protect the ecological environment, natural resources, and cultural relics within the military restricted zones and military administrative zones.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　军事设施管理单位必要时应当向县级以上地方人民政府提供地下、水下军用管线的位置资料。地方进行建设时，当地人民政府应当对地下、水下军用管线予以保护。

**Article 46** The military installation administration unit shall, when necessary, provide the local people's government at or above the county level with the location data of underground and underwater military pipelines. When construction is carried out by local authorities, the local people's government shall protect the underground and underwater military pipelines.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　各级人民政府应当加强国防和军事设施保护教育，使全体公民增强国防观念，保护军事设施，保守军事设施秘密，制止破坏、危害军事设施的行为。

**Article 47** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen education on national defense and the protection of military installations, so that all citizens may enhance their awareness of national defense, protect military installations, keep confidential the secrets of military installations, and stop acts that damage or endanger military installations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当会同有关军事机关，定期组织检查和评估本行政区域内军事设施保护情况，督促限期整改影响军事设施保护的隐患和问题，完善军事设施保护措施。

**Article 48** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the relevant military organs, regularly organize inspections and assessments of the protection of military installations within its administrative region, urge the rectification of hidden dangers and problems affecting the protection of military installations within a specified time limit, and improve the protection measures for military installations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家实行军事设施保护目标责任制和考核评价制度，将军事设施保护目标完成情况作为对地方人民政府、有关军事机关和军事设施管理单位及其负责人考核评价的内容。

**Article 49** The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an appraisal and evaluation system for the protection of military installations, and shall take the completion of targets for the protection of military installations as part of the appraisal and evaluation of local people's governments, relevant military organs, and military installation administration units and their responsible persons.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　军事禁区、军事管理区需要公安机关协助维护治安管理秩序的，经国务院和中央军事委员会决定或者由有关军事机关提请省、自治区、直辖市公安机关批准，可以设立公安机构。

**Article 50** Where military restricted zones or military administrative zones require the assistance of public security organs in maintaining public security management order, a public security organization may be established with the decision of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, or upon the request of the relevant military organ and approval of the public security organ of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，军事设施管理单位的执勤人员应当予以制止：  
（一）非法进入军事禁区、军事管理区或者在陆地、水域军事禁区上空低空飞行的；  
（二）对军事禁区、军事管理区非法进行摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述的；  
（三）进行破坏、危害军事设施的活动的。

**Article 51** Where any of the following circumstances exists in violation of the provisions of this Law, the personnel on duty of the military installation administration unit shall stop it:  
(1) Illegally entering a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, or flying at low altitude over the airspace of a military restricted zone on land or water;  
(2) Illegally taking photographs, making video recordings, audio recordings, surveys, measurements, positioning, drawings, or descriptions of a military restricted zone or military administrative zone;  
(3) Engaging in activities that damage or endanger military installations.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　有[本法第五十一条](#art-51)所列情形之一，不听制止的，军事设施管理单位依照国家有关规定，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）强制带离、控制非法进入军事禁区、军事管理区或者驾驶、操控航空器在陆地、水域军事禁区上空低空飞行的人员，对违法情节严重的人员予以扣留并立即移送公安、国家安全等有管辖权的机关；  
（二）立即制止信息传输等行为，扣押用于实施违法行为的器材、工具或者其他物品，并移送公安、国家安全等有管辖权的机关；  
（三）在紧急情况下，清除严重危害军事设施安全和使用效能的障碍物；  
（四）在危及军事设施安全或者执勤人员生命安全等紧急情况下依法使用武器。  
军人、军队文职人员和军队其他人员有[本法第五十一条](#art-51)所列情形之一的，依照军队有关规定处理。

**Article 52** Where any of the circumstances listed in Article 51 of this Law occurs and the person refuses to heed the order to stop, the military installation administration unit may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, take the following measures:  
(1) Forcibly taking away and controlling persons who illegally enter a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, or who drive or operate an aircraft to fly at low altitude over the airspace of a military restricted zone on land or water, and detaining persons whose illegal acts are serious and immediately transferring them to the public security organ, state security organ, or other competent authority;  
(2) Immediately stopping acts such as information transmission, seizing the equipment, tools, or other articles used for the illegal acts, and transferring them to the public security organ, state security organ, or other competent authority;  
(3) Under emergency circumstances, removing obstacles that seriously endanger the safety and operational effectiveness of military installations;  
(4) Under emergency circumstances such as when the safety of military installations or the life safety of personnel on duty is endangered, using weapons according to law.  
Where military personnel, civilian personnel of the armed forces, or other military personnel commit any of the circumstances listed in Article 51 of this Law, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)、第十八条、第二十三条规定，擅自进入水域军事禁区，在水域军事禁区内从事水产养殖、捕捞，在水域军事管理区内从事水产养殖，或者在水域军事管理区内从事捕捞等活动影响军用舰船行动的，由交通运输、渔业等主管部门给予警告，责令离开，没收渔具、渔获物。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 17, 18, and 23 of this Law, a person enters a military restricted zone on water without authorization, engages in aquaculture or fishing within a military restricted zone on water, engages in aquaculture within a military administrative zone on water, or engages in fishing or other activities within a military administrative zone on water that affect the movement of military vessels, the competent departments of transportation, fisheries, and the like shall give a warning, order the person to leave, and confiscate the fishing gear and catch.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反[本法第十八条](#art-18)、第二十三条、第二十四条规定，在陆地、水域军事禁区、军事管理区内建造、设置非军事设施，擅自开发利用陆地军事禁区、军事管理区地下空间，或者在划为军事管理区的军民合用港口地方管理的水域未征得军事设施管理单位同意建造、设置非军事设施的，由住房和城乡建设、自然资源、交通运输、渔业等主管部门责令停止兴建活动，对已建成的责令限期拆除。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of the provisions of Articles 18, 23, and 24 of this Law, a person constructs or installs non-military facilities within a military restricted zone or military administrative zone on land or water, develops or utilizes the underground space of a military restricted zone or military administrative zone on land without authorization, or constructs or installs non-military facilities in the waters under local management of a military-civilian dual-use port designated as a military administrative zone without the consent of the military installation administration unit, the competent departments of housing and urban-rural construction, natural resources, transportation, fisheries, and the like shall order the cessation of the construction activities, and for those already constructed, order their demolition within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第一款规定，在作战工程安全保护范围内开山采石、采矿、爆破的，由自然资源、生态环境等主管部门以及公安机关责令停止违法行为，没收采出的产品和违法所得；修筑建筑物、构筑物、道路或者进行农田水利基本建设影响作战工程安全和使用效能的，由自然资源、生态环境、交通运输、农业农村、住房和城乡建设等主管部门给予警告，责令限期改正。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 28 of this Law, a person quarries stone, mines, or blasts within the safety protection zone of combat engineering, the competent departments of natural resources, ecological environment, and the like, as well as the public security organs, shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and confiscate the extracted products and illegal gains; where the construction of buildings, structures, or roads, or the carrying out of capital construction for farmland and water conservancy affects the safety and operational effectiveness of combat engineering, the competent departments of natural resources, ecological environment, transportation, agriculture and rural affairs, housing and urban-rural construction, and the like shall give a warning and order rectification within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第三款规定，私自开启封闭的作战工程，破坏作战工程伪装，阻断作战工程通道，将作战工程用于存放非军用物资器材或者种植、养殖等生产活动的，由公安机关以及自然资源等主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 28 of this Law, a person privately opens a sealed combat engineering work, damages the camouflage of a combat engineering work, blocks a passage of a combat engineering work, or uses a combat engineering work for storing non-military materials or equipment or for production activities such as planting or breeding, the public security organs and the competent departments of natural resources, and the like, shall order the cessation of the illegal act and the restoration to the original condition within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第四款、第三十四条规定，擅自拆除、迁建、改建作战工程，或者擅自拆除、移动边防、海防管控设施的，由住房和城乡建设主管部门、公安机关等责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 28 and Article 34 of this Law, a person demolishes, relocates, or modifies a combat engineering work without authorization, or demolishes or moves border defense or coastal defense control facilities without authorization, the competent department of housing and urban-rural construction, the public security organs, and the like shall order the cessation of the illegal act and the restoration to the original condition within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第一款规定，在军用机场净空保护区域内修建超出军用机场净空保护标准的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的，由住房和城乡建设、自然资源主管部门责令限期拆除超高部分。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 29 of this Law, a person constructs buildings, structures, or other facilities that exceed the clearance protection standards of a military airport within the clearance protection zone of the military airport, the competent departments of housing and urban-rural construction and natural resources shall order the demolition of the excessively high parts within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[本法第三十三条](#art-33)规定，在军用无线电固定设施电磁环境保护范围内建造、设置影响军用无线电固定设施使用效能的设备和电磁障碍物体，或者从事影响军用无线电固定设施电磁环境的活动的，由自然资源、生态环境等主管部门以及无线电管理机构给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，查封干扰设备或者强制拆除障碍物。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 33 of this Law, a person constructs or installs, within the electromagnetic environment protection zone of a fixed military radio facility, equipment or electromagnetic obstacles that affect the operational effectiveness of the fixed military radio facility, or engages in activities that affect the electromagnetic environment of the fixed military radio facility, the competent departments of natural resources, ecological environment, and the like, as well as the radio regulatory authority, shall give a warning and order rectification within a specified time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, they shall seal up the interfering equipment or forcibly remove the obstacles.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列行为之一的，适用[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第二十三条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-23)的处罚规定：  
（一）非法进入军事禁区、军事管理区或者驾驶、操控航空器在陆地、水域军事禁区上空低空飞行，不听制止的；  
（二）在军事禁区外围安全控制范围内，或者在没有划入军事禁区、军事管理区的军事设施一定距离内，进行危害军事设施安全和使用效能的活动，不听制止的；  
（三）在军用机场净空保护区域内，进行影响飞行安全和机场助航设施使用效能的活动，不听制止的；  
（四）对军事禁区、军事管理区非法进行摄影、摄像、录音、勘察、测量、定位、描绘和记述，不听制止的；  
（五）其他扰乱军事禁区、军事管理区管理秩序和危害军事设施安全的行为，情节轻微，尚不够刑事处罚的。

**Article 60** Where any of the following acts is committed, the penalty provisions of Article 23 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply:  
(1) Illegally entering a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, or driving or operating an aircraft to fly at low altitude over the airspace of a military restricted zone on land or water, and refusing to heed the order to stop;  
(2) Engaging in activities that endanger the safety and operational effectiveness of military installations within the safety control zone around a military restricted zone, or within a certain distance from military installations not designated as a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, and refusing to heed the order to stop;  
(3) Engaging in activities that affect flight safety and the operational effectiveness of airport navigation aids within the clearance protection zone of a military airport, and refusing to heed the order to stop;  
(4) Illegally taking photographs, making video recordings, audio recordings, surveys, measurements, positioning, drawings, or descriptions of a military restricted zone or military administrative zone, and refusing to heed the order to stop;  
(5) Other acts that disrupt the management order of a military restricted zone or military administrative zone and endanger the safety of military installations, where the circumstances are minor and do not yet constitute a criminal offense.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反国家规定，故意干扰军用无线电设施正常工作的，或者对军用无线电设施产生有害干扰，拒不按照有关主管部门的要求改正的，依照[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第二十八条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-28)的规定处罚。

**Article 61** Whoever violates state regulations by intentionally interfering with the normal operation of military radio facilities, or causing harmful interference to military radio facilities and refusing to make corrections as required by the competent authorities, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 28 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　毁坏边防、海防管控设施以及军事禁区、军事管理区的围墙、铁丝网、界线标志或者其他军事设施的，依照[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第三十三条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-33)的规定处罚。

**Article 62** Whoever destroys border control or coastal defense control facilities, or the walls, barbed wire, boundary markers or other military facilities of military restricted zones or military administrative zones, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）破坏军事设施的；  
（二）过失损坏军事设施，造成严重后果的；  
（三）盗窃、抢夺、抢劫军事设施的装备、物资、器材的；  
（四）泄露军事设施秘密，或者为境外的机构、组织、人员窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供军事设施秘密的；  
（五）破坏军用无线电固定设施电磁环境，干扰军用无线电通讯，情节严重的；  
（六）其他扰乱军事禁区、军事管理区管理秩序和危害军事设施安全的行为，情节严重的。

**Article 63** Whoever commits any of the following acts, if it constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) destroying military facilities;  
(2) negligently damaging military facilities, causing serious consequences;  
(3) stealing, robbing, or forcibly taking the equipment, materials, or apparatus of military facilities;  
(4) divulging the secrets of military facilities, or stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing secrets of military facilities for overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals;  
(5) damaging the electromagnetic environment of fixed military radio facilities, interfering with military radio communications, where the circumstances are serious;  
(6) other acts disturbing the management order of military restricted zones or military administrative zones and endangering the safety of military facilities, where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　军人、军队文职人员和军队其他人员有下列行为之一，按照军队有关规定给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）有[本法第五十三条](#art-53)至第六十三条规定行为的；  
（二）擅自将军事设施用于非军事目的，或者有其他滥用职权行为的；  
（三）擅离职守或者玩忽职守的。

**Article 64** Any soldier, civilian staff member of the military, or other military personnel who commits any of the following acts shall be given disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) having the acts specified in Articles 53 through 63 of this Law;  
(2) without authorization, using military facilities for non-military purposes, or having other acts of abuse of authority;  
(3) leaving one's post without authorization or neglecting one's duty.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　公职人员在军事设施保护工作中有玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊等行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Any public official who has acts such as neglect of duty, abuse of authority, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in the protection of military facilities shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，破坏、危害军事设施的，属海警机构职权范围的，由海警机构依法处理。  
违反本法规定，有其他破坏、危害军事设施行为的，由有关主管部门依法处理。

**Article 66** Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law and destroys or endangers military facilities, if such act falls within the scope of duties of the maritime police agency, the maritime police agency shall handle the matter according to law.  
Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law and has other acts of destroying or endangering military facilities shall be handled by the relevant competent authorities according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，造成军事设施损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 67** Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law and causes losses to military facilities shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　战时违反本法的，依法从重追究法律责任。

**Article 68** Anyone who violates this Law during wartime shall be investigated for legal liability with heavier punishment according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　中国人民武装警察部队所属军事设施的保护，适用本法。

**Article 69** The protection of military facilities under the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国防科技工业重要武器装备的科研、生产、试验、存储等设施的保护，参照本法有关规定执行。具体办法和设施目录由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 70** The protection of facilities for the scientific research, production, testing, storage, etc., of important weaponry and equipment of the defense science, technology and industry shall be implemented with reference to the relevant provisions of this Law. The specific measures and the catalogue of facilities shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国务院和中央军事委员会根据本法制定实施办法。

**Article 71** The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall formulate implementation measures in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本法自2021年8月1日起施行。

**Article 72** This Law shall come into force as of August 1, 2021.
