# 中华人民共和国外交特权与豁免条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1986-09-05  
**生效日期**：1986-09-05

> （1986年9月5日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十七次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为确定外国驻中国使馆和使馆人员的外交特权与豁免，便于外国驻中国使馆代表其国家有效地执行职务，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated with a view to defining the diplomatic privileges and immunities of the diplomatic missions in China and their members and facilitating the efficient performance of the functions of such missions as representing their States.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　使馆外交人员原则上应当是具有派遣国国籍的人。如果委派中国或者第三国国籍的人为使馆外交人员，必须征得中国主管机关的同意。中国主管机关可以随时撤销此项同意。

**Article 2** Diplomatic agents of a mission shall in principle be persons having the nationality of the sending State. If a person having Chinese or a third State nationality is appointed as a diplomatic agent, the consent of the competent authorities of China must be obtained. The competent authorities of China may withdraw such consent at any time.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　使馆及其馆长有权在使馆馆舍和使馆馆长交通工具上，使用派遣国的国旗或者国徽。

**Article 3** The mission and its head shall have the right to use the flag and emblem of the sending State on the premises of the mission and on the means of transport of the head of the mission.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　使馆馆舍不受侵犯。中国国家工作人员进入使馆馆舍，须经使馆馆长或者其授权人员的同意。中国有关机关应当采取适当措施，保护使馆馆舍免受侵犯或者损害。  
使馆的馆舍、设备及馆舍内其他财产和使馆交通工具免受搜查、征用、扣押或者强制执行。

**Article 4** The premises of the mission shall be inviolable. The entry of agents of the Chinese authorities into the premises of the mission shall require the consent of the head of mission or an authorized person of the mission. The relevant authorities of China shall take appropriate measures to protect the premises of the mission against any intrusion or damage. The premises of the mission, their furnishings and other property thereon and the means of transport of the mission shall be immune from search, requisition, attachment or compulsory execution.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　使馆馆舍免纳捐税，但为其提供特定服务所收的费用不在此限。  
使馆办理公务所收规费和手续费免纳捐税。

**Article 5** The premises of the mission shall be exempt from all dues and taxes, except such as represent payment for specific services rendered.  
The fees and charges levied by the mission in the course of its official duties shall be exempt from all dues and taxes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　使馆的档案和文件不受侵犯。

**Article 6** The archives and documents of the mission shall be inviolable.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　使馆人员在中国境内有行动和旅行的自由，中国政府规定禁止或者限制进入的区域除外。

**Article 7** All members of the mission shall enjoy freedom of movement and travel within the territory of China, except such areas as are prohibited or restricted by the regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　使馆为公务目的可以与派遣国政府以及派遣国其他使馆和领事馆自由通讯。通讯可以采用一切适当方法，包括外交信使、外交邮袋和明码、密码电信在内。

**Article 8** The mission may for official purposes communicate freely with the Government of the sending State and with its other missions and consular posts. In communicating with them, the mission may employ all appropriate means, including diplomatic couriers, the diplomatic bag, and messages in code or cipher.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　使馆设置和使用供通讯用的无线电收发信机，必须经中国政府同意。使馆运进上述设备，按中国政府的有关规定办理。

**Article 9** The installation and use of a wireless transmitter for communication by the mission shall be subject to the consent of the Chinese Government. The importation of such equipment by the mission shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　使馆来往的公文不受侵犯。  
外交邮袋不得开拆或者扣留。  
外交邮袋以装载外交文件或者公务用品为限，应予加封并附有可资识别的外部标记。

**Article 10** The official correspondence of the mission shall be inviolable.  
The diplomatic bag shall not be opened or detained.  
The diplomatic bag may contain only diplomatic documents or articles for official use, and shall be sealed and bear visible external marks of its character.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外交信使必须持有派遣国主管机关出具的信使证明书。外交信使人身不受侵犯，不受逮捕或者拘留。  
临时外交信使必须持有派遣国主管机关出具的临时信使证明书，在其负责携带外交邮袋期间，享有与外交信使同等的豁免。  
商业飞机机长受委托可以转递外交邮袋，但机长必须持有委托国官方证明文件，注明所携带的外交邮袋件数。机长不得视为外交信使。使馆应当派使馆人员向机长接交外交邮袋。

**Article 11** A diplomatic courier must be provided with a courier certificate issued by the competent authorities of the sending State. The person of a diplomatic courier shall be inviolable; he shall not be liable to arrest or detention. A temporary diplomatic courier must be provided with a temporary courier certificate issued by the competent authorities of the sending State. He shall enjoy the same immunities as a diplomatic courier while charged with the custody of the diplomatic bag. A diplomatic bag may be entrusted to the captain of a commercial aircraft, but the captain must be provided with an official document issued by the entrusting State indicating the number of packages constituting the bag. The captain shall not be considered to be a diplomatic courier. The mission shall send its members to take delivery of the diplomatic bag from the captain.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外交代表人身不受侵犯，不受逮捕或者拘留。中国有关机关应当采取适当措施，防止外交代表的人身自由和尊严受到侵犯。

**Article 12** The person of a diplomatic agent shall be inviolable; he shall not be liable to arrest or detention. The relevant authorities of China shall take appropriate measures to prevent any attack on his personal freedom and dignity.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外交代表的寓所不受侵犯，并受保护。  
外交代表的文书和信件不受侵犯。外交代表的财产不受侵犯，但第十四条另有规定的除外。

**Article 13** The private residence of a diplomatic agent shall be inviolable and shall enjoy protection. His papers and correspondence shall be inviolable. His property shall be inviolable, subject to the provisions of Article 14.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外交代表享有刑事管辖豁免。  
外交代表享有民事管辖豁免和行政管辖豁免，但下列各项除外：  
（一）外交代表以私人身份进行的遗产继承的诉讼；  
（二）外交代表违反第二十五条第三项规定在中国境内从事公务范围以外的职业或者商业活动的诉讼。  
外交代表免受强制执行，但对前款所列情况，强制执行对其人身和寓所不构成侵犯的，不在此限。  
外交代表没有以证人身份作证的义务。

**Article 14** A diplomatic agent shall enjoy immunity from criminal jurisdiction. He shall enjoy immunity from civil and administrative jurisdiction, except in the case of: (1) an action relating to succession in which the diplomatic agent is involved as a private person; and (2) an action relating to any professional or commercial activity exercised by the diplomatic agent outside his official functions in violation of paragraph 3 of Article 25. A diplomatic agent shall be immune from compulsory execution, except in the cases coming under the preceding paragraph and provided that the measures of compulsory execution do not constitute any violation of his person or his residence. A diplomatic agent shall not be obliged to give evidence as a witness.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外交代表和第二十条规定享有豁免的人员的管辖豁免可以由派遣国政府明确表示放弃。  
外交代表和第二十条规定享有豁免的人员如果主动提起诉讼，对与本诉直接有关的反诉，不得援用管辖豁免。  
放弃民事管辖豁免或者行政管辖豁免，不包括对判决的执行也放弃豁免。放弃对判决执行的豁免须另作明确表示。

**Article 15** The immunity from jurisdiction of diplomatic agents and of persons enjoying immunity under Article 20 may be waived by the Government of the sending State, expressed explicitly.  
If a diplomatic agent or a person enjoying immunity under Article 20 initiates proceedings, he shall not be entitled to invoke immunity from jurisdiction in respect of any counterclaim directly connected with the principal claim.  
Waiver of immunity from civil or administrative jurisdiction shall not be held to imply waiver of immunity in respect of the execution of the judgment, for which a separate and explicit waiver shall be required.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外交代表免纳捐税，但下列各项除外：  
（一）通常计入商品价格或者服务价格内的捐税；  
（二）有关遗产的各种捐税，但外交代表亡故，其在中国境内的动产不在此限；  
（三）对来源于中国境内的私人收入所征的捐税；  
（四）为其提供特定服务所收的费用。

**Article 16** A diplomatic agent shall be exempt from all dues and taxes, except: (1) dues and taxes of a kind which are normally incorporated in the price of goods or services; (2) estate, succession or inheritance duties, except for movable property in China if the diplomatic agent dies; and (3) dues and taxes on private income having its source in China; and (4) charges levied for specific services rendered.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外交代表免除一切个人和公共劳务以及军事义务。

**Article 17** A diplomatic agent shall be exempt from all personal and public services and from military obligations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　使馆运进的公务用品、外交代表运进的自用物品，按照中国政府的有关规定免纳关税和其他捐税。  
外交代表的私人行李免受查验，但中国有关机关有重大理由推定其中装有不属于前款规定免税的物品或者中国法律和政府规定禁止运进、运出或者检疫法规规定管制的物品的，可以查验。查验时，须有外交代表或者其授权人员在场。

**Article 18** Articles for official use imported by the mission and articles for personal use imported by a diplomatic agent shall be exempt from customs duties and all other dues and taxes in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government. The personal baggage of a diplomatic agent shall be exempt from inspection, unless there are serious grounds for presuming by the relevant authorities of China that it contains articles not covered by the exemptions provided in the preceding paragraph, or articles the import or export of which is prohibited by the laws and regulations of China or controlled by the quarantine laws and regulations. Such inspection shall be conducted only in the presence of the diplomatic agent or his authorized person.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　使馆和使馆人员携运自用的枪支、子弹入境，必须经中国政府批准，并且按中国政府的有关规定办理。

**Article 19** The importation of firearms and ammunition for personal use by the mission and its members shall be subject to the approval of the Chinese Government and shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　与外交代表共同生活的配偶及未成年子女，如果不是中国公民，享有第十二条至第十八条所规定的特权与豁免。  
使馆行政技术人员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女，如果不是中国公民并且不是在中国永久居留的，享有第十二条至第十七条所规定的特权与豁免，但民事管辖豁免和行政管辖豁免，仅限于执行公务的行为。使馆行政技术人员到任后半年内运进的安家物品享有第十八条第一款所规定的免税的特权。  
使馆服务人员如果不是中国公民并且不是在中国永久居留的，其执行公务的行为享有豁免，其受雇所得报酬免纳所得税。其到任后半年内运进的安家物品享有第十八条第一款所规定的免税的特权。  
使馆人员的私人服务员如果不是中国公民并且不是在中国永久居留的，其受雇所得的报酬免纳所得税。

**Article 20** The spouse and minor children of a diplomatic agent, if not nationals of China, shall enjoy the privileges and immunities specified in Articles 12 to 18. Members of the administrative and technical staff of the mission, together with their spouses and minor children, if not nationals of and not permanently resident in China, shall enjoy the privileges and immunities specified in Articles 12 to 17, except that the immunity from civil and administrative jurisdiction shall be limited to acts performed in the course of their duties. Articles for the establishment of the household imported by members of the administrative and technical staff of the mission within six months of their first arrival shall enjoy the privilege of exemption from duties as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 18. Members of the service staff of the mission who are not nationals of and not permanently resident in China shall enjoy immunity in respect of acts performed in the course of their duties and exemption from income tax on the emoluments they receive by reason of their employment. Articles for the establishment of the household imported by them within six months of their first arrival shall enjoy the privilege of exemption from duties as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 18. Private servants of members of the mission, if not nationals of and not permanently resident in China, shall be exempt from income tax on the emoluments they receive by reason of their employment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外交代表如果是中国公民或者获得在中国永久居留资格的外国人，仅就其执行公务的行为，享有管辖豁免和不受侵犯。

**Article 21** A diplomatic representative who is a Chinese citizen or a foreigner who has obtained the permanent residence status in China shall enjoy immunity from jurisdiction and inviolability only in respect of acts performed in the exercise of their official functions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　下列人员享有在中国过境或者逗留期间所必需的豁免和不受侵犯：  
（一）途经中国的外国驻第三国的外交代表和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女；  
（二）持有中国外交签证或者持有外交护照（仅限互免签证的国家）来中国的外国官员；  
（三）经中国政府同意给予本条所规定的特权与豁免的其他来中国访问的外国人士。  
对途经中国的第三国外交信使及其所携带的外交邮袋，参照第十条、第十一条的规定办理。

**Article 22** The following persons shall enjoy the immunity and inviolability necessary during their transit or stay in China: (1) diplomatic representatives of foreign states accredited to a third state who pass through China, and their spouses and minor children living with them; (2) foreign officials coming to China who hold a Chinese diplomatic visa or hold a diplomatic passport (only for countries with mutual visa exemption); and (3) other foreign persons visiting China to whom the privileges and immunities prescribed in this Article are granted with the consent of the Chinese Government. The provisions of Articles 10 and 11 shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to a diplomatic courier of a third state passing through China and the diplomatic bag carried by such courier.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　来中国访问的外国国家元首、政府首脑、外交部长及其他具有同等身份的官员，享有本条例所规定的特权与豁免。

**Article 23** Foreign heads of state, heads of government, ministers of foreign affairs, and other officials of equivalent status who are visiting China shall enjoy the privileges and immunities prescribed in these Regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　来中国参加联合国及其专门机构召开的国际会议的外国代表、临时来中国的联合国及其专门机构的官员和专家、联合国及其专门机构驻中国的代表机构和人员的待遇，按中国已加入的有关国际公约和中国与有关国际组织签订的协议办理。

**Article 24** The treatment of foreign representatives coming to China to attend international conferences convened by the United Nations and its specialized agencies, officials and experts of the United Nations and its specialized agencies temporarily coming to China, and the representative offices and personnel of the United Nations and its specialized agencies stationed in China shall be handled in accordance with the relevant international conventions to which China has acceded and agreements concluded between China and the relevant international organizations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　享有外交特权与豁免的人员：  
（一）应当尊重中国的法律、法规；  
（二）不得干涉中国的内政；  
（三）不得在中国境内为私人利益从事任何职业或者商业活动；  
（四）不得将使馆馆舍和使馆工作人员寓所充作与使馆职务不相符合的用途。

**Article 25** Persons enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunities shall: (1) respect the laws and regulations of China; (2) not interfere in the internal affairs of China; (3) not engage in any professional or commercial activity for personal profit within the Chinese territory; and (4) not use the premises of the diplomatic mission and the residence of the staff members of the mission for purposes inconsistent with the functions of the mission.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　如果外国给予中国驻该国使馆、使馆人员以及临时去该国的有关人员的外交特权与豁免，低于中国按本条例给予该国驻中国使馆、使馆人员以及临时来中国的有关人员的外交特权与豁免，中国政府根据对等原则，可以给予该国驻中国使馆、使馆人员以及临时来中国的有关人员以相应的外交特权与豁免。

**Article 26** If the diplomatic privileges and immunities granted by a foreign state to the Chinese embassy accredited to that state, its staff members, and other relevant persons temporarily going to that state are less than those granted by China to the embassy of that state in China, its staff members, and other relevant persons temporarily coming to China in accordance with these Regulations, the Chinese Government may, on the principle of reciprocity, grant corresponding diplomatic privileges and immunities to the embassy of that state in China, its staff members, and other relevant persons temporarily coming to China.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的，按照国际条约的规定办理，但中国声明保留的条款除外。  
中国与外国签订的外交特权与豁免协议另有规定的，按照协议的规定执行。

**Article 27** Where there are contrary provisions in an international treaty concluded or acceded to by China, the provisions of such international treaty shall prevail, except for the clauses on which China has made reservations.  
Where there are contrary provisions in an agreement on diplomatic privileges and immunities concluded between China and a foreign state, the provisions of such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例中下列用语的含义是：  
（一）“使馆馆长”是指派遣国委派担任此项职位的大使、公使、代办以及其他同等级别的人；  
（二）“使馆人员”是指使馆馆长和使馆工作人员；  
（三）“使馆工作人员”是指使馆外交人员、行政技术人员和服务人员；  
（四）“使馆外交人员”是指具有外交官衔的使馆工作人员；  
（五）“外交代表”是指使馆馆长或者使馆外交人员；  
（六）“使馆行政技术人员”是指从事行政和技术工作的使馆工作人员；  
（七）“使馆服务人员”是指从事服务工作的使馆工作人员；  
（八）“私人服务员”是指使馆人员私人雇用的人员；  
（九）“使馆馆舍”是指使馆使用和使馆馆长官邸的建筑物及其附属的土地。

**Article 28** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms mean: (1) "head of the mission" means the ambassador, minister, charge d'affaires, or other person of equivalent rank appointed by the sending state to hold that position; (2) "members of the mission" means the head of the mission and the staff members of the mission; (3) "staff members of the mission" means the diplomatic staff, administrative and technical staff, and service staff of the mission; (4) "diplomatic staff of the mission" means the staff members of the mission who hold diplomatic rank; (5) "diplomatic representative" means the head of the mission or a member of the diplomatic staff of the mission; (6) "administrative and technical staff of the mission" means the staff members of the mission engaged in administrative and technical work; (7) "service staff of the mission" means the staff members of the mission engaged in domestic service; (8) "private servant" means a person employed by a member of the mission for private service; (9) "premises of the mission" means the buildings occupied by the mission and the residence of the head of the mission, together with the land ancillary thereto.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
