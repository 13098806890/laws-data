# 中华人民共和国反洗钱法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-11-08  
**生效日期**：2025-01-01

> （2006年10月31日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过　2024年11月8日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议修订）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防洗钱活动，遏制洗钱以及相关犯罪，加强和规范反洗钱工作，维护金融秩序、社会公共利益和国家安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of preventing money laundering activities, curbing money laundering and related crimes, strengthening and regulating anti-money laundering work, and maintaining financial order, social public interests, and national security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称反洗钱，是指为了预防通过各种方式掩饰、隐瞒毒品犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、走私犯罪、贪污贿赂犯罪、破坏金融管理秩序犯罪、金融诈骗犯罪和其他犯罪所得及其收益的来源、性质的洗钱活动，依照本法规定采取相关措施的行为。  
预防恐怖主义融资活动适用本法；其他法律另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "anti-money laundering" refers to the actions taken in accordance with the provisions of this Law to prevent money laundering activities that, through various means, conceal or disguise the source and nature of proceeds from drug-related crimes, organized crimes of a gangster nature, terrorist activities crimes, smuggling crimes, crimes of embezzlement and bribery, crimes undermining the order of financial management, financial fraud crimes, and other crimes, as well as the income derived therefrom.  
This Law shall apply to the prevention of terrorist financing; where other laws provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　反洗钱工作应当贯彻落实党和国家路线方针政策、决策部署，坚持总体国家安全观，完善监督管理体制机制，健全风险防控体系。

**Article 3** Anti-money laundering work shall implement the Party's and the State's guidelines, policies, and decisions, adhere to a holistic approach to national security, improve the supervision and management system and mechanism, and strengthen the risk prevention and control system.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　反洗钱工作应当依法进行，确保反洗钱措施与洗钱风险相适应，保障正常金融服务和资金流转顺利进行，维护单位和个人的合法权益。

**Article 4** Anti-money laundering work shall be carried out according to law, ensuring that anti-money laundering measures are commensurate with money laundering risks, guaranteeing the smooth progress of normal financial services and capital flows, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of entities and individuals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门负责全国的反洗钱监督管理工作。国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内履行反洗钱监督管理职责。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门、国务院有关部门、监察机关和司法机关在反洗钱工作中应当相互配合。

**Article 5** The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide supervision and administration of anti-money laundering work. The relevant departments under the State Council shall perform their anti-money laundering supervision and administration duties within their respective functions.  
The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council, the relevant departments under the State Council, supervisory organs, and judicial organs shall cooperate with each other in anti-money laundering work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在中华人民共和国境内（以下简称境内）设立的金融机构和依照本法规定应当履行反洗钱义务的特定非金融机构，应当依法采取预防、监控措施，建立健全反洗钱内部控制制度，履行客户尽职调查、客户身份资料和交易记录保存、大额交易和可疑交易报告、反洗钱特别预防措施等反洗钱义务。

**Article 6** Financial institutions established within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as "within the territory") and specific non-financial institutions that shall perform anti-money laundering obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall take preventive and monitoring measures according to law, establish and improve internal anti-money laundering control systems, and perform anti-money laundering obligations such as customer due diligence, retention of customer identity information and transaction records, reporting of large-value transactions and suspicious transactions, and special anti-money laundering preventive measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对依法履行反洗钱职责或者义务获得的客户身份资料和交易信息、反洗钱调查信息等反洗钱信息，应当予以保密；非依法律规定，不得向任何单位和个人提供。  
反洗钱行政主管部门和其他依法负有反洗钱监督管理职责的部门履行反洗钱职责获得的客户身份资料和交易信息，只能用于反洗钱监督管理和行政调查工作。  
司法机关依照本法获得的客户身份资料和交易信息，只能用于反洗钱相关刑事诉讼。  
国家有关机关使用反洗钱信息应当依法保护国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私、个人信息。

**Article 7** Anti-money laundering information, including customer identity information and transaction information, as well as anti-money laundering investigation information, obtained in the course of performing anti-money laundering functions or obligations according to law shall be kept confidential; no such information shall be provided to any entity or individual except as provided by law.  
Where the administrative department of anti-money laundering and other departments legally responsible for anti-money laundering supervision and administration obtain customer identity information and transaction information in performing their anti-money laundering duties, such information may be used only for anti-money laundering supervision, administration and administrative investigation.  
Where judicial organs obtain customer identity information and transaction information in accordance with this Law, such information may be used only for criminal proceedings related to anti-money laundering.  
When using anti-money laundering information, relevant state organs shall protect State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　履行反洗钱义务的机构及其工作人员依法开展提交大额交易和可疑交易报告等工作，受法律保护。

**Article 8** The institutions that perform anti-money laundering obligations and their staff members shall be protected by law when they carry out work such as submitting reports on large-value transactions and suspicious transactions according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　反洗钱行政主管部门会同国家有关机关通过多种形式开展反洗钱宣传教育活动，向社会公众宣传洗钱活动的违法性、危害性及其表现形式等，增强社会公众对洗钱活动的防范意识和识别能力。

**Article 9** The administrative department of anti-money laundering shall, together with relevant state organs, carry out anti-money laundering publicity and education activities through various forms, publicizing to the public the illegality, harmfulness, and manifestations of money laundering activities, thereby enhancing the public's awareness of prevention and ability to identify money laundering activities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　任何单位和个人不得从事洗钱活动或者为洗钱活动提供便利，并应当配合金融机构和特定非金融机构依法开展的客户尽职调查。

**Article 10** No entity or individual shall engage in money laundering activities or facilitate money laundering activities, and shall cooperate with financial institutions and specific non-financial institutions in conducting customer due diligence according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人发现洗钱活动，有权向反洗钱行政主管部门、公安机关或者其他有关国家机关举报。接受举报的机关应当对举报人和举报内容保密。  
对在反洗钱工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 11** Any entity or individual that discovers money laundering activities shall have the right to report them to the administrative department of anti-money laundering, a public security organ, or other relevant state organs. The organ that receives the report shall keep the identity of the reporter and the content of the report confidential.  
Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to anti-money laundering work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在中华人民共和国境外（以下简称境外）的洗钱和恐怖主义融资活动，危害中华人民共和国主权和安全，侵犯中华人民共和国公民、法人和其他组织合法权益，或者扰乱境内金融秩序的，依照本法以及相关法律规定处理并追究法律责任。

**Article 12** Where money laundering and terrorist financing activities outside the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as "outside the territory") endanger the sovereignty or security of the People's Republic of China, infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations of the People's Republic of China, or disrupt the financial order within the territory, they shall be dealt with and legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with this Law and relevant legal provisions.

### 第二章　反洗钱监督管理

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门组织、协调全国的反洗钱工作，负责反洗钱的资金监测，制定或者会同国务院有关金融管理部门制定金融机构反洗钱管理规定，监督检查金融机构履行反洗钱义务的情况，在职责范围内调查可疑交易活动，履行法律和国务院规定的有关反洗钱的其他职责。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门的派出机构在国务院反洗钱行政主管部门的授权范围内，对金融机构履行反洗钱义务的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 13** The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall organize and coordinate nationwide anti-money laundering work, be responsible for anti-money laundering fund monitoring, formulate or jointly formulate with the relevant financial regulatory authorities under the State Council the anti-money laundering management regulations for financial institutions, supervise and inspect the performance of anti-money laundering obligations by financial institutions, investigate suspicious transaction activities within its functions, and perform other anti-money laundering duties as provided by laws and the State Council.  
The dispatched agencies of the administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall, within the scope of authorization from the said department, supervise and inspect the performance of anti-money laundering obligations by financial institutions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院有关金融管理部门参与制定所监督管理的金融机构反洗钱管理规定，履行法律和国务院规定的有关反洗钱的其他职责。  
有关金融管理部门应当在金融机构市场准入中落实反洗钱审查要求，在监督管理工作中发现金融机构违反反洗钱规定的，应当将线索移送反洗钱行政主管部门，并配合其进行处理。

**Article 14** The relevant financial regulatory authorities under the State Council shall participate in the formulation of anti-money laundering management regulations for financial institutions under their supervision and administration, and perform other anti-money laundering duties as provided by laws and the State Council.  
The relevant financial regulatory authorities shall implement anti-money laundering review requirements in the market access of financial institutions; where they discover, during supervision and administration, that a financial institution violates anti-money laundering provisions, they shall transfer the leads to the administrative department of anti-money laundering and cooperate with it in handling the matter.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院有关特定非金融机构主管部门制定或者国务院反洗钱行政主管部门会同其制定特定非金融机构反洗钱管理规定。  
有关特定非金融机构主管部门监督检查特定非金融机构履行反洗钱义务的情况，处理反洗钱行政主管部门提出的反洗钱监督管理建议，履行法律和国务院规定的有关反洗钱的其他职责。有关特定非金融机构主管部门根据需要，可以请求反洗钱行政主管部门协助其监督检查。

**Article 15** The competent authorities of specific non-financial institutions under the State Council shall formulate, or the administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall jointly formulate with them, the anti-money laundering management regulations for specific non-financial institutions.  
The competent authorities of specific non-financial institutions shall supervise and inspect the performance of anti-money laundering obligations by specific non-financial institutions, handle the anti-money laundering supervision and administration suggestions raised by the administrative department of anti-money laundering, and perform other anti-money laundering duties as provided by laws and the State Council. Where necessary, the competent authorities of specific non-financial institutions may request the administrative department of anti-money laundering to assist them in supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门设立反洗钱监测分析机构。反洗钱监测分析机构开展反洗钱资金监测，负责接收、分析大额交易和可疑交易报告，移送分析结果，并按照规定向国务院反洗钱行政主管部门报告工作情况，履行国务院反洗钱行政主管部门规定的其他职责。  
反洗钱监测分析机构根据依法履行职责的需要，可以要求履行反洗钱义务的机构提供与大额交易和可疑交易相关的补充信息。  
反洗钱监测分析机构应当健全监测分析体系，根据洗钱风险状况有针对性地开展监测分析工作，按照规定向履行反洗钱义务的机构反馈可疑交易报告使用情况，不断提高监测分析水平。

**Article 16** The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall establish an anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis institution. The anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis institution shall conduct anti-money laundering fund monitoring, be responsible for receiving and analyzing reports on large-value transactions and suspicious transactions, transferring analysis results, reporting its work to the administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council in accordance with regulations, and performing other duties stipulated by the said administrative department.  
The anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis institution may, as necessary for performing its duties according to law, require the institutions that perform anti-money laundering obligations to provide supplementary information related to large-value transactions and suspicious transactions.  
The anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis institution shall improve its monitoring and analysis system, conduct targeted monitoring and analysis based on the money laundering risk situation, provide feedback to the institutions that perform anti-money laundering obligations on the use of suspicious transaction reports in accordance with regulations, and continuously improve its monitoring and analysis capabilities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门为履行反洗钱职责，可以从国家有关机关获取所必需的信息，国家有关机关应当依法提供。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门应当向国家有关机关定期通报反洗钱工作情况，依法向履行与反洗钱相关的监督管理、行政调查、监察调查、刑事诉讼等职责的国家有关机关提供所必需的反洗钱信息。

**Article 17** The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council may, for the purpose of performing its anti-money laundering duties, obtain necessary information from relevant state organs, and the relevant state organs shall provide such information according to law.  
The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall regularly inform the relevant state organs of the status of anti-money laundering work, and provide, according to law, necessary anti-money laundering information to the relevant state organs that perform duties related to anti-money laundering supervision and administration, administrative investigation, supervision investigation, criminal proceedings, and other duties.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　出入境人员携带的现金、无记名支付凭证等超过规定金额的，应当按照规定向海关申报。海关发现个人出入境携带的现金、无记名支付凭证等超过规定金额的，应当及时向反洗钱行政主管部门通报。  
前款规定的申报范围、金额标准以及通报机制等，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门、国务院外汇管理部门按照职责分工会同海关总署规定。

**Article 18** Where the cash, bearer negotiable instruments, etc., carried by persons entering or leaving the territory exceed the prescribed amounts, they shall declare to the customs in accordance with regulations. Where the customs discovers that the cash, bearer negotiable instruments, etc., carried by individuals entering or leaving the territory exceed the prescribed amounts, it shall promptly notify the administrative department of anti-money laundering.  
The scope of declaration, the amount standards, and the notification mechanism as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council and the foreign exchange administration department under the State Council, within their respective functions, jointly with the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门建立法人、非法人组织受益所有人信息管理制度。  
法人、非法人组织应当保存并及时更新受益所有人信息，按照规定向登记机关如实提交并及时更新受益所有人信息。反洗钱行政主管部门、登记机关按照规定管理受益所有人信息。  
反洗钱行政主管部门、国家有关机关为履行职责需要，可以依法使用受益所有人信息。金融机构和特定非金融机构在履行反洗钱义务时依法查询核对受益所有人信息；发现受益所有人信息错误、不一致或者不完整的，应当按照规定进行反馈。使用受益所有人信息应当依法保护信息安全。  
本法所称法人、非法人组织的受益所有人，是指最终拥有或者实际控制法人、非法人组织，或者享有法人、非法人组织最终收益的自然人。具体认定标准由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 19** The administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, establish a management system for information on beneficial owners of legal persons and unincorporated organizations.  
Legal persons and unincorporated organizations shall retain and promptly update the information on beneficial owners, and truthfully submit and promptly update the information on beneficial owners to the registration authority in accordance with regulations. The administrative department of anti-money laundering and the registration authority shall manage the information on beneficial owners in accordance with regulations.  
The administrative department of anti-money laundering and relevant state organs may use the information on beneficial owners according to law as necessary for performing their duties. Financial institutions and specific non-financial institutions shall, when performing anti-money laundering obligations, inquire about and verify the information on beneficial owners according to law; where they discover that the information on beneficial owners is incorrect, inconsistent, or incomplete, they shall provide feedback in accordance with regulations. The use of information on beneficial owners shall protect information security according to law.  
For the purposes of this Law, the beneficial owner of a legal person or unincorporated organization refers to a natural person who ultimately owns or actually controls the legal person or unincorporated organization, or enjoys the ultimate benefits of the legal person or unincorporated organization. The specific criteria for determination shall be formulated by the administrative department of anti-money laundering under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　反洗钱行政主管部门和其他依法负有反洗钱监督管理职责的部门发现涉嫌洗钱以及相关违法犯罪的交易活动，应当将线索和相关证据材料移送有管辖权的机关处理。接受移送的机关应当按照有关规定反馈处理结果。

**Article 20** Where the administrative department of anti-money laundering and other departments legally responsible for anti-money laundering supervision and administration discover transaction activities suspected of money laundering or related illegal activities, they shall transfer the leads and relevant evidentiary materials to the organ with jurisdiction for handling. The organ that accepts the transfer shall provide feedback on the handling results in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　反洗钱行政主管部门为依法履行监督管理职责，可以要求金融机构报送履行反洗钱义务情况，对金融机构实施风险监测、评估，并就金融机构执行本法以及相关管理规定的情况进行评价。必要时可以按照规定约谈金融机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员以及反洗钱工作直接负责人，要求其就有关事项说明情况；对金融机构履行反洗钱义务存在的问题进行提示。

**Article 21** The anti-money laundering administrative department, for the purpose of performing its supervision and administration duties according to law, may require a financial institution to report its fulfillment of anti-money laundering obligations, conduct risk monitoring and assessment of the financial institution, and evaluate the financial institution's implementation of this Law and relevant regulatory provisions. When necessary, it may, in accordance with the provisions, interview the directors, supervisors, senior officers, and persons directly responsible for anti-money laundering work of the financial institution, requiring them to explain relevant matters; and provide guidance on problems existing in the financial institution's fulfillment of anti-money laundering obligations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　反洗钱行政主管部门进行监督检查时，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入金融机构进行检查；  
（二）询问金融机构的工作人员，要求其对有关被检查事项作出说明；  
（三）查阅、复制金融机构与被检查事项有关的文件、资料，对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件、资料予以封存；  
（四）检查金融机构的计算机网络与信息系统，调取、保存金融机构的计算机网络与信息系统中的有关数据、信息。  
进行前款规定的监督检查，应当经国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构负责人批准。检查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件和检查通知书；检查人员少于二人或者未出示执法证件和检查通知书的，金融机构有权拒绝接受检查。

**Article 22** When conducting supervision and inspection, the anti-money laundering administrative department may take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the financial institution to conduct inspection;  
(2) Question the staff of the financial institution and require them to provide explanations on the matters under inspection;  
(3) Inspect and copy documents and materials related to the matters under inspection from the financial institution, and seal up documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed, or damaged;  
(4) Inspect the computer network and information system of the financial institution, and retrieve and preserve relevant data and information in the computer network and information system of the financial institution.  
The supervision and inspection prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be approved by the person in charge of the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agency at or above the level of a city divided into districts. There shall be no fewer than two inspectors, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials and the inspection notice; if there are fewer than two inspectors or they fail to present their law enforcement credentials and the inspection notice, the financial institution has the right to refuse the inspection.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门会同国家有关机关评估国家、行业面临的洗钱风险，发布洗钱风险指引，加强对履行反洗钱义务的机构指导，支持和鼓励反洗钱领域技术创新，及时监测与新领域、新业态相关的新型洗钱风险，根据洗钱风险状况优化资源配置，完善监督管理措施。

**Article 23** The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant State authorities, assess the money laundering risks faced by the country and industries, issue money laundering risk guidelines, strengthen guidance to institutions performing anti-money laundering obligations, support and encourage technological innovation in the field of anti-money laundering, promptly monitor new types of money laundering risks related to new fields and new business forms, optimize resource allocation based on the money laundering risk situation, and improve supervision and administration measures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对存在严重洗钱风险的国家或者地区，国务院反洗钱行政主管部门可以在征求国家有关机关意见的基础上，经国务院批准，将其列为洗钱高风险国家或者地区，并采取相应措施。

**Article 24** For a country or region with serious money laundering risks, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council may, after soliciting opinions from relevant State authorities and with the approval of the State Council, list it as a high-risk country or region for money laundering and take corresponding measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　履行反洗钱义务的机构可以依法成立反洗钱自律组织。反洗钱自律组织与相关行业自律组织协同开展反洗钱领域的自律管理。  
反洗钱自律组织接受国务院反洗钱行政主管部门的指导。

**Article 25** Institutions performing anti-money laundering obligations may establish a self-regulatory organization for anti-money laundering according to law. The self-regulatory organization for anti-money laundering shall cooperate with relevant industry self-regulatory organizations to carry out self-regulation in the field of anti-money laundering.  
The self-regulatory organization for anti-money laundering shall accept the guidance of the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　提供反洗钱咨询、技术、专业能力评价等服务的机构及其工作人员，应当勤勉尽责、恪尽职守地提供服务；对于因提供服务获得的数据、信息，应当依法妥善处理，确保数据、信息安全。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门应当加强对上述机构开展反洗钱有关服务工作的指导。

**Article 26** Institutions providing anti-money laundering consulting, technology, professional capability assessment, and other services, as well as their staff, shall provide services diligently and faithfully; the data and information obtained from providing services shall be properly handled according to law to ensure data and information security.  
The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall strengthen guidance to the above-mentioned institutions in carrying out anti-money laundering related services.

### 第三章　反洗钱义务

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　金融机构应当依照本法规定建立健全反洗钱内部控制制度，设立专门机构或者指定内设机构牵头负责反洗钱工作，根据经营规模和洗钱风险状况配备相应的人员，按照要求开展反洗钱培训和宣传。  
金融机构应当定期评估洗钱风险状况并制定相应的风险管理制度和流程，根据需要建立相关信息系统。  
金融机构应当通过内部审计或者社会审计等方式，监督反洗钱内部控制制度的有效实施。  
金融机构的负责人对反洗钱内部控制制度的有效实施负责。

**Article 27** A financial institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, establish and improve an internal control system for anti-money laundering, set up a specialized agency or designate an internal department to take the lead in anti-money laundering work, equip corresponding personnel according to its business scale and money laundering risk situation, and carry out anti-money laundering training and publicity as required.  
A financial institution shall regularly assess the money laundering risk situation and formulate corresponding risk management systems and procedures, and establish relevant information systems as needed.  
A financial institution shall supervise the effective implementation of the internal control system for anti-money laundering through internal audit or external audit.  
The person in charge of a financial institution shall be responsible for the effective implementation of the internal control system for anti-money laundering.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　金融机构应当按照规定建立客户尽职调查制度。  
金融机构不得为身份不明的客户提供服务或者与其进行交易，不得为客户开立匿名账户或者假名账户，不得为冒用他人身份的客户开立账户。

**Article 28** A financial institution shall establish a customer due diligence system in accordance with the provisions.  
A financial institution shall not provide services to or conduct transactions with a customer whose identity is unclear, shall not open anonymous accounts or pseudonymous accounts for customers, and shall not open accounts for customers who assume the identity of another person.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有下列情形之一的，金融机构应当开展客户尽职调查：  
（一）与客户建立业务关系或者为客户提供规定金额以上的一次性金融服务；  
（二）有合理理由怀疑客户及其交易涉嫌洗钱活动；  
（三）对先前获得的客户身份资料的真实性、有效性、完整性存在疑问。  
客户尽职调查包括识别并采取合理措施核实客户及其受益所有人身份，了解客户建立业务关系和交易的目的，涉及较高洗钱风险的，还应当了解相关资金来源和用途。  
金融机构开展客户尽职调查，应当根据客户特征和交易活动的性质、风险状况进行，对于涉及较低洗钱风险的，金融机构应当根据情况简化客户尽职调查。

**Article 29** A financial institution shall conduct customer due diligence under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Establishing a business relationship with a customer or providing a one-time financial service exceeding a prescribed amount;  
(2) Having reasonable grounds to suspect that the customer or its transactions involve money laundering activities;  
(3) Having doubts about the authenticity, validity, or completeness of previously obtained customer identity information.  
Customer due diligence includes identifying and taking reasonable measures to verify the identity of the customer and its beneficial owner, understanding the purpose of the customer's establishment of business relationships and transactions, and where higher money laundering risks are involved, also understanding the source and use of relevant funds.  
When conducting customer due diligence, a financial institution shall do so based on the customer's characteristics and the nature and risk situation of the transaction activities. Where lower money laundering risks are involved, the financial institution shall simplify customer due diligence according to the circumstances.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在业务关系存续期间，金融机构应当持续关注并评估客户整体状况及交易情况，了解客户的洗钱风险。发现客户进行的交易与金融机构所掌握的客户身份、风险状况等不符的，应当进一步核实客户及其交易有关情况；对存在洗钱高风险情形的，必要时可以采取限制交易方式、金额或者频次，限制业务类型，拒绝办理业务，终止业务关系等洗钱风险管理措施。  
金融机构采取洗钱风险管理措施，应当在其业务权限范围内按照有关管理规定的要求和程序进行，平衡好管理洗钱风险与优化金融服务的关系，不得采取与洗钱风险状况明显不相匹配的措施，保障与客户依法享有的医疗、社会保障、公用事业服务等相关的基本的、必需的金融服务。

**Article 30** During the term of a business relationship, a financial institution shall continuously monitor and assess the overall situation of the customer and its transactions, and understand the money laundering risk of the customer. If it is found that a customer's transactions are inconsistent with the customer's identity, risk situation, etc. known to the financial institution, the financial institution shall further verify the customer and the relevant circumstances of its transactions; where there are high money laundering risk circumstances, the financial institution may, where necessary, take money laundering risk management measures such as restricting transaction methods, amounts, or frequencies, restricting business types, refusing to handle business, or terminating business relationships.  
When taking money laundering risk management measures, a financial institution shall do so within its business authority and in accordance with the requirements and procedures of relevant regulatory provisions, balancing the relationship between managing money laundering risks and optimizing financial services, and shall not take measures that are clearly disproportionate to the money laundering risk situation, ensuring the basic and essential financial services related to medical care, social security, public utility services, etc. to which the customer is entitled according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　客户由他人代理办理业务的，金融机构应当按照规定核实代理关系，识别并核实代理人的身份。  
金融机构与客户订立人身保险、信托等合同，合同的受益人不是客户本人的，金融机构应当识别并核实受益人的身份。

**Article 31** Where a customer conducts business through an agent, the financial institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, verify the agency relationship and identify and verify the identity of the agent.  
Where a financial institution enters into a contract such as a personal insurance contract or a trust contract with a customer, and the beneficiary of the contract is not the customer itself, the financial institution shall identify and verify the identity of the beneficiary.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　金融机构依托第三方开展客户尽职调查的，应当评估第三方的风险状况及其履行反洗钱义务的能力。第三方具有较高风险情形或者不具备履行反洗钱义务能力的，金融机构不得依托其开展客户尽职调查。  
金融机构应当确保第三方已经采取符合本法要求的客户尽职调查措施。第三方未采取符合本法要求的客户尽职调查措施的，由该金融机构承担未履行客户尽职调查义务的法律责任。  
第三方应当向金融机构提供必要的客户尽职调查信息，并配合金融机构持续开展客户尽职调查。

**Article 32** Where a financial institution relies on a third party to conduct customer due diligence, it shall assess the risk situation of the third party and its ability to perform anti-money laundering obligations. If the third party presents high-risk circumstances or does not have the ability to perform anti-money laundering obligations, the financial institution shall not rely on it to conduct customer due diligence.  
A financial institution shall ensure that the third party has taken customer due diligence measures that comply with the requirements of this Law. If the third party fails to take customer due diligence measures that comply with the requirements of this Law, the financial institution shall bear the legal liability for failing to fulfill customer due diligence obligations.  
The third party shall provide necessary customer due diligence information to the financial institution and cooperate with the financial institution in continuously conducting customer due diligence.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　金融机构进行客户尽职调查，可以通过反洗钱行政主管部门以及公安、市场监督管理、民政、税务、移民管理、电信管理等部门依法核实客户身份等有关信息，相关部门应当依法予以支持。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门应当协调推动相关部门为金融机构开展客户尽职调查提供必要的便利。

**Article 33** When conducting customer due diligence, a financial institution may verify customer identity and other relevant information through the anti-money laundering administrative department and the public security, market supervision and administration, civil affairs, taxation, immigration administration, telecommunications administration, and other departments according to law, and the relevant departments shall provide support according to law.  
The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall coordinate and promote relevant departments to provide necessary convenience for financial institutions to conduct customer due diligence.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　金融机构应当按照规定建立客户身份资料和交易记录保存制度。  
在业务关系存续期间，客户身份信息发生变更的，应当及时更新。  
客户身份资料在业务关系结束后、客户交易信息在交易结束后，应当至少保存十年。  
金融机构解散、被撤销或者被宣告破产时，应当将客户身份资料和客户交易信息移交国务院有关部门指定的机构。

**Article 34** A financial institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, establish a system for retaining customer identity information and transaction records.  
During the term of a business relationship, if customer identity information changes, it shall be updated in a timely manner.  
Customer identity information shall be retained for at least ten years after the end of the business relationship, and customer transaction information shall be retained for at least ten years after the end of the transaction.  
When a financial institution is dissolved, revoked, or declared bankrupt, it shall transfer the customer identity information and customer transaction information to an institution designated by the relevant department of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　金融机构应当按照规定执行大额交易报告制度，客户单笔交易或者在一定期限内的累计交易超过规定金额的，应当及时向反洗钱监测分析机构报告。  
金融机构应当按照规定执行可疑交易报告制度，制定并不断优化监测标准，有效识别、分析可疑交易活动，及时向反洗钱监测分析机构提交可疑交易报告；提交可疑交易报告的情况应当保密。

**Article 35** A financial institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, implement a large-value transaction reporting system. Where a customer's single transaction or cumulative transaction within a certain period exceeds the prescribed amount, the financial institution shall promptly report to the anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis agency.  
A financial institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, implement a suspicious transaction reporting system, formulate and continuously optimize monitoring standards, effectively identify and analyze suspicious transaction activities, and promptly submit suspicious transaction reports to the anti-money laundering monitoring and analysis agency; the circumstances of submitting suspicious transaction reports shall be kept confidential.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　金融机构应当在反洗钱行政主管部门的指导下，关注、评估运用新技术、新产品、新业务等带来的洗钱风险，根据情形采取相应措施，降低洗钱风险。

**Article 36** A financial institution shall, under the guidance of the anti-money laundering administrative department, pay attention to and assess the money laundering risks arising from the use of new technologies, new products, new businesses, etc., and take corresponding measures according to the circumstances to reduce money laundering risks.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在境内外设有分支机构或者控股其他金融机构的金融机构，以及金融控股公司，应当在总部或者集团层面统筹安排反洗钱工作。为履行反洗钱义务在公司内部、集团成员之间共享必要的反洗钱信息的，应当明确信息共享机制和程序。共享反洗钱信息，应当符合有关信息保护的法律规定，并确保相关信息不被用于反洗钱和反恐怖主义融资以外的用途。

**Article 37** A financial institution that has set up branches or controls other financial institutions within or outside China, as well as a financial holding company, shall coordinate and arrange anti-money laundering work at the headquarters or group level. Where necessary anti-money laundering information is shared within the company or among group members for the purpose of fulfilling anti-money laundering obligations, the information sharing mechanism and procedures shall be clearly defined. The sharing of anti-money laundering information shall comply with the legal provisions on information protection and ensure that the relevant information is not used for purposes other than anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　与金融机构存在业务关系的单位和个人应当配合金融机构的客户尽职调查，提供真实有效的身份证件或者其他身份证明文件，准确、完整填报身份信息，如实提供与交易和资金相关的资料。  
单位和个人拒不配合金融机构依照本法采取的合理的客户尽职调查措施的，金融机构按照规定的程序，可以采取限制或者拒绝办理业务、终止业务关系等洗钱风险管理措施，并根据情况提交可疑交易报告。

**Article 38** An entity or individual that has a business relationship with a financial institution shall cooperate with the financial institution's customer due diligence, provide genuine and valid identity documents or other identity certification documents, accurately and completely fill in identity information, and truthfully provide materials related to transactions and funds.  
Where an entity or individual refuses to cooperate with reasonable customer due diligence measures taken by a financial institution in accordance with this Law, the financial institution may, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, take money laundering risk management measures such as restricting or refusing to handle business, terminating business relationships, and submit a suspicious transaction report based on the circumstances.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　单位和个人对金融机构采取洗钱风险管理措施有异议的，可以向金融机构提出。金融机构应当在十五日内进行处理，并将结果答复当事人；涉及客户基本的、必需的金融服务的，应当及时处理并答复当事人。相关单位和个人逾期未收到答复，或者对处理结果不满意的，可以向反洗钱行政主管部门投诉。  
前款规定的单位和个人对金融机构采取洗钱风险管理措施有异议的，也可以依法直接向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 39** Where an entity or individual has objections to the money laundering risk management measures taken by a financial institution, it may raise them with the financial institution. The financial institution shall process the objection within fifteen days and reply with the result to the party; where it involves the customer's basic and essential financial services, it shall process and reply promptly. If the relevant entity or individual does not receive a reply within the time limit, or is dissatisfied with the handling result, it may file a complaint with the anti-money laundering administrative department.  
The entity or individual referred to in the preceding paragraph that has objections to the money laundering risk management measures taken by a financial institution may also directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　任何单位和个人应当按照国家有关机关要求对下列名单所列对象采取反洗钱特别预防措施：  
（一）国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构认定并由其办事机构公告的恐怖活动组织和人员名单；  
（二）外交部发布的执行联合国安理会决议通知中涉及定向金融制裁的组织和人员名单；  
（三）国务院反洗钱行政主管部门认定或者会同国家有关机关认定的，具有重大洗钱风险、不采取措施可能造成严重后果的组织和人员名单。  
对前款第一项规定的名单有异议的，当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国反恐怖主义法》的规定申请复核。对前款第二项规定的名单有异议的，当事人可以按照有关程序提出从名单中除去的申请。对前款第三项规定的名单有异议的，当事人可以向作出认定的部门申请行政复议；对行政复议决定不服的，可以依法提起行政诉讼。  
反洗钱特别预防措施包括立即停止向名单所列对象及其代理人、受其指使的组织和人员、其直接或者间接控制的组织提供金融等服务或者资金、资产，立即限制相关资金、资产转移等。  
第一款规定的名单所列对象可以按照规定向国家有关机关申请使用被限制的资金、资产用于单位和个人的基本开支及其他必需支付的费用。采取反洗钱特别预防措施应当保护善意第三人合法权益，善意第三人可以依法进行权利救济。

**Article 40** Any entity or individual shall, as required by the relevant State authorities, take special anti-money laundering preventive measures against the objects listed in the following lists:  
(1) The list of terrorist organizations and individuals identified by the national counter-terrorism work leading body and published by its office;  
(2) The list of organizations and individuals subject to targeted financial sanctions in the notice issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for implementing resolutions of the United Nations Security Council;  
(3) The list of organizations and individuals identified by the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or jointly with relevant State authorities as having significant money laundering risks and where failure to take measures may cause serious consequences.  
Where a party has objections to the list specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph, it may apply for review in accordance with the provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Law of the People's Republic of China. Where a party has objections to the list specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, it may apply for removal from the list in accordance with relevant procedures. Where a party has objections to the list specified in item (3) of the preceding paragraph, it may apply for administrative reconsideration to the department that made the identification; if dissatisfied with the administrative reconsideration decision, it may initiate administrative litigation according to law.  
Special anti-money laundering preventive measures include immediately ceasing to provide financial services, funds, or assets to the objects listed in the lists, their agents, organizations and persons under their instruction, and organizations directly or indirectly controlled by them, and immediately restricting the transfer of relevant funds and assets.  
The objects listed in the lists specified in the first paragraph may apply to the relevant State authorities, in accordance with the provisions, to use the restricted funds or assets for basic expenses of the entity or individual and other necessary payments. The adoption of special anti-money laundering preventive measures shall protect the lawful rights and interests of bona fide third parties, who may seek legal remedies according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　金融机构应当识别、评估相关风险并制定相应的制度，及时获取[本法第四十条](#art-40)第一款规定的名单，对客户及其交易对象进行核查，采取相应措施，并向反洗钱行政主管部门报告。

**Article 41** A financial institution shall identify and assess relevant risks, formulate corresponding systems, obtain in a timely manner the list prescribed in Paragraph 1 of Article 40 of this Law, verify its customers and their transaction counterparts, take corresponding measures, and report to the anti-money laundering administrative department.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　特定非金融机构在从事规定的特定业务时，参照本章关于金融机构履行反洗钱义务的相关规定，根据行业特点、经营规模、洗钱风险状况履行反洗钱义务。

**Article 42** When engaging in the specified specific businesses, a specific non-financial institution shall, by reference to the relevant provisions of this Chapter on the performance of anti-money laundering obligations by financial institutions, perform its anti-money laundering obligations based on the characteristics of its industry, its business scale, and the money laundering risk situation.

### 第四章　反洗钱调查

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构发现涉嫌洗钱的可疑交易活动或者违反本法规定的其他行为，需要调查核实的，经国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构负责人批准，可以向金融机构、特定非金融机构发出调查通知书，开展反洗钱调查。  
反洗钱行政主管部门开展反洗钱调查，涉及特定非金融机构的，必要时可以请求有关特定非金融机构主管部门予以协助。  
金融机构、特定非金融机构应当配合反洗钱调查，在规定时限内如实提供有关文件、资料。  
开展反洗钱调查，调查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件和调查通知书；调查人员少于二人或者未出示执法证件和调查通知书的，金融机构、特定非金融机构有权拒绝接受调查。

**Article 43** Where the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts discovers suspicious transaction activities suspected of money laundering or other acts violating the provisions of this Law that need to be investigated and verified, with the approval of the person in charge of the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts, it may issue an investigation notice to financial institutions or specific non-financial institutions and conduct an anti-money laundering investigation.  
Where the anti-money laundering administrative department conducts an anti-money laundering investigation involving a specific non-financial institution, it may, when necessary, request assistance from the competent department of the relevant specific non-financial institution.  
A financial institution or a specific non-financial institution shall cooperate with the anti-money laundering investigation and truthfully provide relevant documents and materials within the prescribed time limit.  
When conducting an anti-money laundering investigation, there shall be no fewer than two investigators, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials and the investigation notice; if there are fewer than two investigators or they fail to present their law enforcement credentials and the investigation notice, the financial institution or specific non-financial institution shall have the right to refuse to accept the investigation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构开展反洗钱调查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）询问金融机构、特定非金融机构有关人员，要求其说明情况；  
（二）查阅、复制被调查对象的账户信息、交易记录和其他有关资料；  
（三）对可能被转移、隐匿、篡改或者毁损的文件、资料予以封存。  
询问应当制作询问笔录。询问笔录应当交被询问人核对。记载有遗漏或者差错的，被询问人可以要求补充或者更正。被询问人确认笔录无误后，应当签名或者盖章；调查人员也应当在笔录上签名。  
调查人员封存文件、资料，应当会同金融机构、特定非金融机构的工作人员查点清楚，当场开列清单一式二份，由调查人员和金融机构、特定非金融机构的工作人员签名或者盖章，一份交金融机构或者特定非金融机构，一份附卷备查。

**Article 44** When conducting an anti-money laundering investigation, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts may take the following measures:  
(1) Inquire with relevant persons of financial institutions or specific non-financial institutions and require them to explain the situation;  
(2) Inspect and copy the account information, transaction records, and other relevant materials of the investigated party;  
(3) Seal up documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed, tampered with, or destroyed.  
Inquiry records shall be made for inquiries. The inquiry records shall be given to the person being inquired for verification. If there are omissions or errors in the records, the person being inquired may request supplements or corrections. After the person being inquired confirms that the records are correct, he or she shall sign or affix his or her seal; the investigators shall also sign the records.  
When investigators seal up documents and materials, they shall, together with the staff of the financial institution or specific non-financial institution, check them clearly, make a list in duplicate on the spot, which shall be signed or sealed by the investigators and the staff of the financial institution or specific non-financial institution, with one copy being delivered to the financial institution or specific non-financial institution and the other copy being attached to the file for reference.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　经调查仍不能排除洗钱嫌疑或者发现其他违法犯罪线索的，应当及时向有管辖权的机关移送。接受移送的机关应当按照有关规定反馈处理结果。  
客户转移调查所涉及的账户资金的，国务院反洗钱行政主管部门认为必要时，经其负责人批准，可以采取临时冻结措施。  
接受移送的机关接到线索后，对已依照前款规定临时冻结的资金，应当及时决定是否继续冻结。接受移送的机关认为需要继续冻结的，依照相关法律规定采取冻结措施；认为不需要继续冻结的，应当立即通知国务院反洗钱行政主管部门，国务院反洗钱行政主管部门应当立即通知金融机构解除冻结。  
临时冻结不得超过四十八小时。金融机构在按照国务院反洗钱行政主管部门的要求采取临时冻结措施后四十八小时内，未接到国家有关机关继续冻结通知的，应当立即解除冻结。

**Article 45** Where, after investigation, the suspicion of money laundering cannot be eliminated or clues to other illegal or criminal activities are discovered, the matter shall be transferred to the competent authority with jurisdiction in a timely manner. The authority receiving the transfer shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, provide feedback on the outcome of the handling.  
If a customer transfers funds involved in the investigation, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council may, when it deems it necessary and with the approval of its person in charge, take temporary freezing measures.  
After receiving the clues, the authority that accepted the transfer shall promptly decide whether to continue freezing the funds that have been temporarily frozen in accordance with the preceding paragraph. If the authority that accepted the transfer deems it necessary to continue the freezing, it shall take freezing measures in accordance with relevant legal provisions; if it deems it unnecessary to continue the freezing, it shall immediately notify the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council, which shall immediately notify the financial institution to lift the freezing.  
The temporary freezing shall not exceed forty-eight hours. If, within forty-eight hours after taking the temporary freezing measures as required by the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council, the financial institution does not receive a notice from the relevant state authority to continue the freezing, it shall immediately lift the freezing.

### 第五章　反洗钱国际合作

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　中华人民共和国根据缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照平等互惠原则，开展反洗钱国际合作。

**Article 46** The People's Republic of China shall carry out international cooperation on anti-money laundering in accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to, or on the principle of equality and mutual benefit.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门根据国务院授权，负责组织、协调反洗钱国际合作，代表中国政府参与有关国际组织活动，依法与境外相关机构开展反洗钱合作，交换反洗钱信息。  
国家有关机关依法在职责范围内开展反洗钱国际合作。

**Article 47** The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall, as authorized by the State Council, be responsible for organizing and coordinating international cooperation on anti-money laundering, representing the Chinese government in participating in activities of relevant international organizations, conducting anti-money laundering cooperation with relevant overseas institutions according to law, and exchanging anti-money laundering information.  
Relevant state authorities shall, within their respective functions and duties, carry out international cooperation on anti-money laundering according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　涉及追究洗钱犯罪的司法协助，依照《中华人民共和国国际刑事司法协助法》以及有关法律的规定办理。

**Article 48** Judicial assistance involving the investigation of criminal liability for money laundering shall be handled in accordance with the International Criminal Judicial Assistance Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家有关机关在依法调查洗钱和恐怖主义融资活动过程中，按照对等原则或者经与有关国家协商一致，可以要求在境内开立代理行账户或者与我国存在其他密切金融联系的境外金融机构予以配合。

**Article 49** In the process of investigating money laundering and terrorist financing activities according to law, relevant state authorities may, on the principle of reciprocity or after consultation and agreement with the relevant country, require an overseas financial institution that maintains a correspondent account within China or has other close financial ties with China to cooperate.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　外国国家、组织违反对等、协商一致原则直接要求境内金融机构提交客户身份资料、交易信息，扣押、冻结、划转境内资金、资产，或者作出其他行动的，金融机构不得擅自执行，并应当及时向国务院有关金融管理部门报告。  
除前款规定外，外国国家、组织基于合规监管的需要，要求境内金融机构提供概要性合规信息、经营信息等信息的，境内金融机构向国务院有关金融管理部门和国家有关机关报告后可以提供或者予以配合。  
前两款规定的资料、信息涉及重要数据和个人信息的，还应当符合国家数据安全管理、个人信息保护有关规定。

**Article 50** If a foreign country or organization, in violation of the principle of reciprocity or agreement through consultation, directly requests a domestic financial institution to submit customer identity data or transaction information, or to seize, freeze, or transfer domestic funds or assets, or to take other actions, the financial institution shall not execute on its own and shall promptly report to the relevant financial regulatory department of the State Council.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, if a foreign country or organization, based on compliance supervision needs, requests a domestic financial institution to provide summary compliance information, business information, or other such information, the domestic financial institution may provide such information or cooperate after reporting to the relevant financial regulatory department of the State Council and relevant state authorities.  
If the materials or information referred to in the preceding two paragraphs involve important data or personal information, they shall also comply with the relevant provisions of national data security management and personal information protection.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　反洗钱行政主管部门和其他依法负有反洗钱监督管理职责的部门从事反洗钱工作的人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
（一）违反规定进行检查、调查或者采取临时冻结措施；  
（二）泄露因反洗钱知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私、个人信息；  
（三）违反规定对有关机构和人员实施行政处罚；  
（四）其他不依法履行职责的行为。  
其他国家机关工作人员有前款第二项行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 51** Any staff member of the anti-money laundering administrative department or other departments responsible for anti-money laundering supervision and management according to law who commits one of the following acts shall be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) Conducting inspections, investigations, or taking temporary freezing measures in violation of regulations;  
(2) Disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or personal information learned due to anti-money laundering work;  
(3) Imposing administrative penalties on relevant institutions and personnel in violation of regulations;  
(4) Other acts of failing to perform duties according to law.  
If any other state functionary commits the act specified in Item (2) of the preceding paragraph, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　金融机构有下列情形之一的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正；情节较重的，给予警告或者处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期未改正的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款，可以根据情形在职责范围内或者建议有关金融管理部门限制或者禁止其开展相关业务：  
（一）未按照规定制定、完善反洗钱内部控制制度规范；  
（二）未按照规定设立专门机构或者指定内设机构牵头负责反洗钱工作；  
（三）未按照规定根据经营规模和洗钱风险状况配备相应人员；  
（四）未按照规定开展洗钱风险评估或者健全相应的风险管理制度；  
（五）未按照规定制定、完善可疑交易监测标准；  
（六）未按照规定开展反洗钱内部审计或者社会审计；  
（七）未按照规定开展反洗钱培训；  
（八）应当建立反洗钱相关信息系统而未建立，或者未按照规定完善反洗钱相关信息系统；  
（九）金融机构的负责人未能有效履行反洗钱职责。

**Article 52** Where a financial institution is in any of the following circumstances, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, it shall be given a warning or a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan, and may, depending on the circumstances, within the scope of its functions and duties or by recommending the relevant financial regulatory department, restrict or prohibit it from conducting relevant business:  
(1) Failing to formulate or improve the norms for the internal control system of anti-money laundering in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Failing to establish a specialized agency or designate an internal institution to take the lead in anti-money laundering work in accordance with regulations;  
(3) Failing to assign corresponding personnel according to business scale and money laundering risk situation in accordance with regulations;  
(4) Failing to conduct money laundering risk assessment or improve the corresponding risk management system in accordance with regulations;  
(5) Failing to formulate or improve the monitoring criteria for suspicious transactions in accordance with regulations;  
(6) Failing to conduct internal audits or social audits on anti-money laundering in accordance with regulations;  
(7) Failing to carry out anti-money laundering training in accordance with regulations;  
(8) Failing to establish an information system related to anti-money laundering when required, or failing to improve the information system related to anti-money laundering in accordance with regulations;  
(9) The person in charge of a financial institution fails to effectively perform his or her anti-money laundering duties.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　金融机构有下列行为之一的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正，可以给予警告或者处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期未改正的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款：  
（一）未按照规定开展客户尽职调查；  
（二）未按照规定保存客户身份资料和交易记录；  
（三）未按照规定报告大额交易；  
（四）未按照规定报告可疑交易。

**Article 53** Where a financial institution commits any of the following acts, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, and may give a warning or impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan:  
(1) Failing to carry out customer due diligence in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Failing to retain customer identity materials and transaction records in accordance with regulations;  
(3) Failing to report large-value transactions in accordance with regulations;  
(4) Failing to report suspicious transactions in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　金融机构有下列行为之一的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正，处五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，可以根据情形在职责范围内或者建议有关金融管理部门限制或者禁止其开展相关业务：  
（一）为身份不明的客户提供服务、与其进行交易，为客户开立匿名账户、假名账户，或者为冒用他人身份的客户开立账户；  
（二）未按照规定对洗钱高风险情形采取相应洗钱风险管理措施；  
（三）未按照规定采取反洗钱特别预防措施；  
（四）违反保密规定，查询、泄露有关信息；  
（五）拒绝、阻碍反洗钱监督管理、调查，或者故意提供虚假材料；  
（六）篡改、伪造或者无正当理由删除客户身份资料、交易记录；  
（七）自行或者协助客户以拆分交易等方式故意逃避履行反洗钱义务。

**Article 54** Where a financial institution commits any of the following acts, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and impose a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and may, depending on the circumstances, within the scope of its functions and duties or by recommending the relevant financial regulatory department, restrict or prohibit it from conducting relevant business:  
(1) Providing services or conducting transactions for customers whose identities are unclear, opening anonymous accounts or pseudonymous accounts for customers, or opening accounts for customers who are using another person's identity;  
(2) Failing to take corresponding money laundering risk management measures for high money laundering risk situations in accordance with regulations;  
(3) Failing to take special preventive measures against money laundering in accordance with regulations;  
(4) Inquiring about or disclosing relevant information in violation of confidentiality provisions;  
(5) Refusing or obstructing anti-money laundering supervision, management, or investigation, or intentionally providing false materials;  
(6) Tampering with, forging, or deleting customer identity materials or transaction records without justifiable reasons;  
(7) Deliberately evading the performance of anti-money laundering obligations by splitting transactions or assisting customers to do so.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　金融机构有[本法第五十三条](#art-53)、第五十四条规定的行为，致使犯罪所得及其收益通过本机构得以掩饰、隐瞒的，或者致使恐怖主义融资后果发生的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正，涉及金额不足一千万元的，处五十万元以上一千万元以下罚款；涉及金额一千万元以上的，处涉及金额百分之二十以上二倍以下罚款；情节严重的，可以根据情形在职责范围内实施或者建议有关金融管理部门实施限制、禁止其开展相关业务，或者责令停业整顿、吊销经营许可证等处罚。

**Article 55** Where a financial institution commits any of the acts specified in Article 53 or Article 54 of this Law, resulting in the concealment of proceeds of crime and their gains through the institution, or causing the occurrence of terrorist financing consequences, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if the amount involved is less than 10,000,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 10,000,000 yuan; if the amount involved is 10,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be fined not less than 20% but not more than two times the amount involved; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, within the scope of its functions and duties or by recommending the relevant financial regulatory department, it may impose restrictions or prohibitions on conducting relevant business, or order suspension of business for rectification, revocation of the business license, or other penalties.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构依照[本法第五十二条](#art-52)至第五十四条规定对金融机构进行处罚的，还可以根据情形对负有责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员或者其他直接责任人员，给予警告或者处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以根据情形在职责范围内实施或者建议有关金融管理部门实施取消其任职资格、禁止其从事有关金融行业工作等处罚。  
国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构依照[本法第五十五条](#art-55)规定对金融机构进行处罚的，还可以根据情形对负有责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员或者其他直接责任人员，处二十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以根据情形在职责范围内实施或者建议有关金融管理部门实施取消其任职资格、禁止其从事有关金融行业工作等处罚。  
前两款规定的金融机构董事、监事、高级管理人员或者其他直接责任人员能够证明自己已经勤勉尽责采取反洗钱措施的，可以不予处罚。

**Article 56** Where the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts imposes a penalty on a financial institution in accordance with Articles 52 to 54 of this Law, it may also, depending on the circumstances, give a warning to or impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan on the directors, supervisors, senior officers, or other persons directly responsible; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, within the scope of its functions and duties or by recommending the relevant financial regulatory department, it may impose penalties such as disqualifying them from holding positions or prohibiting them from engaging in relevant financial industry work.  
Where the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts imposes a penalty on a financial institution in accordance with Article 55 of this Law, it may also, depending on the circumstances, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan on the directors, supervisors, senior officers, or other persons directly responsible; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, within the scope of its functions and duties or by recommending the relevant financial regulatory department, it may impose penalties such as disqualifying them from holding positions or prohibiting them from engaging in relevant financial industry work.  
If the directors, supervisors, senior officers, or other persons directly responsible referred to in the preceding two paragraphs can prove that they have diligently and diligently taken anti-money laundering measures, they may not be penalized.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　金融机构违反[本法第五十条](#art-50)规定擅自采取行动的，由国务院有关金融管理部门处五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款；造成损失的，并处所造成直接经济损失一倍以上五倍以下罚款。对负有责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员或者其他直接责任人员，可以由国务院有关金融管理部门给予警告或者处五十万元以下罚款。  
境外金融机构违反[本法第四十九条](#art-49)规定，对国家有关机关的调查不予配合的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门依照[本法第五十四条](#art-54)、第五十六条规定进行处罚，并可以根据情形将其列入[本法第四十条](#art-40)第一款第三项规定的名单。

**Article 57** Where a financial institution acts on its own in violation of Article 50 of this Law, the relevant financial regulatory department of the State Council shall impose a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the direct economic losses caused. The relevant financial regulatory department of the State Council may give a warning or impose a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan on the directors, supervisors, senior officers, or other persons directly responsible.  
If an overseas financial institution fails to cooperate with the investigation of a relevant state authority in violation of Article 49 of this Law, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall impose penalties in accordance with Articles 54 and 56 of this Law, and may, depending on the circumstances, include it in the list specified in Item (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 40 of this Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　特定非金融机构违反本法规定的，由有关特定非金融机构主管部门责令限期改正；情节较重的，给予警告或者处五万元以下罚款；情节严重或者逾期未改正的，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；对有关负责人，可以给予警告或者处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 58** Where a specific non-financial institution violates the provisions of this Law, the relevant competent department of the specific non-financial institution shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, it shall be given a warning or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the person in charge may be given a warning or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　金融机构、特定非金融机构以外的单位和个人未依照[本法第四十条](#art-40)规定履行反洗钱特别预防措施义务的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正；情节严重的，对单位给予警告或者处二十万元以下罚款，对个人给予警告或者处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 59** Where any unit or individual other than a financial institution or a specific non-financial institution fails to perform the obligation of special preventive measures against money laundering in accordance with Article 40 of this Law, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, a warning or a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a warning or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　法人、非法人组织未按照规定向登记机关提交受益所有人信息的，由登记机关责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处五万元以下罚款。向登记机关提交虚假或者不实的受益所有人信息，或者未按照规定及时更新受益所有人信息的，由国务院反洗钱行政主管部门或者其设区的市级以上派出机构责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处五万元以下罚款。

**Article 60** Where a legal person or an unincorporated organization fails to submit beneficial owner information to the registration authority in accordance with regulations, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan. If false or inaccurate beneficial owner information is submitted to the registration authority, or the beneficial owner information is not updated in a timely manner in accordance with regulations, the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council or its dispatched agencies at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be fined not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国务院反洗钱行政主管部门应当综合考虑金融机构的经营规模、内部控制制度执行情况、勤勉尽责程度、违法行为持续时间、危害程度以及整改情况等因素，制定本法相关行政处罚裁量基准。

**Article 61** The anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council shall take into comprehensive consideration factors such as the business scale, implementation of internal control systems, degree of due diligence, duration of illegal acts, degree of harm, and rectification circumstances of financial institutions, and formulate the administrative penalty discretion benchmarks relevant to this Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
利用金融机构、特定非金融机构实施或者通过非法渠道实施洗钱犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Any person who violates the provisions of this Law, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.  
Any person who commits money laundering crimes through financial institutions, specific non-financial institutions, or through illegal channels shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　在境内设立的下列机构，履行本法规定的金融机构反洗钱义务：  
（一）银行业、证券基金期货业、保险业、信托业金融机构；  
（二）非银行支付机构；  
（三）国务院反洗钱行政主管部门确定并公布的其他从事金融业务的机构。

**Article 63** The following institutions established within the territory shall perform the anti-money laundering obligations of financial institutions as provided for in this Law:  
(1) Financial institutions in the banking, securities fund futures, insurance, and trust industries;  
(2) Non-bank payment institutions;  
(3) Other institutions engaged in financial business as determined and published by the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在境内设立的下列机构，履行本法规定的特定非金融机构反洗钱义务：  
（一）提供房屋销售、房屋买卖经纪服务的房地产开发企业或者房地产中介机构；  
（二）接受委托为客户办理买卖不动产，代管资金、证券或者其他资产，代管银行账户、证券账户，为成立、运营企业筹措资金以及代理买卖经营性实体业务的会计师事务所、律师事务所、公证机构；  
（三）从事规定金额以上贵金属、宝石现货交易的交易商；  
（四）国务院反洗钱行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门根据洗钱风险状况确定的其他需要履行反洗钱义务的机构。

**Article 64** The following institutions established within the territory shall perform the anti-money laundering obligations of specific non-financial institutions as provided for in this Law:  
(1) Real estate development enterprises or real estate intermediary institutions that provide housing sales and housing trading brokerage services;  
(2) Accounting firms, law firms, and notarization institutions that accept entrustments to handle for clients the purchase and sale of real estate, custodianship of funds, securities or other assets, custodianship of bank accounts and securities accounts, fundraising for the establishment and operation of enterprises, and agency trading of operational entity businesses;  
(3) Dealers engaged in spot transactions of precious metals and gemstones above the specified amount;  
(4) Other institutions that need to perform anti-money laundering obligations as determined by the anti-money laundering administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council based on money laundering risk conditions.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2025年1月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2025.
