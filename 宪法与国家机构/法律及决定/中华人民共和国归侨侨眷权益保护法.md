# 中华人民共和国归侨侨眷权益保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1990年9月7日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十五次会议通过　根据2000年10月31日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国归侨侨眷权益保护法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第二次修正） 第一条　为了保护归侨、侨眷的合法的权利和利益，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　归侨是指回国定居的华侨。华侨是指定居在国外的中国公民。 侨眷是指华侨、归侨在国内的眷属。 本法所称侨眷包括：华侨、归侨的配偶，父母，子女及其配偶，兄弟姐妹，祖父母、外祖父母，孙子女、外孙子女，以及同华侨、归侨有长期扶养关系的其他亲属。 第三条　归侨、侨眷享有宪法和法律规定的公民的权利，并履行宪法和法律规定的公民的义务，任何组织或者个人不得歧视。 国家根据实际情况和归侨、侨眷的特点，给予适当照顾，具体办法由国务院或者国务院有关主管部门规定。 第四条　县级以上各级人民政府及其负责侨务工作的机构，组织协调有关部门做好保护归侨、侨眷的合法权益的工作。 第五条　国家对回国定居的华侨给予安置。 第六条　全国人民代表大会和归侨人数较多地区的地方人民代表大会应当有适当名额的归侨代表。 第七条　归侨、侨眷有权依法申请成立社会团体，进行适合归侨、侨眷需要的合法的社会活动。 归侨、侨眷依法成立的社会团体的财产受法律保护，任何组织或者个人不得侵犯。 第八条　中华全国归国华侨联合会和地方归国华侨联合会代表归侨、侨眷的利益，依法维护归侨、侨眷的合法权益。 第九条　国家对安置归侨的农场、林场等企业给予扶持，任何组织或者个人不得侵占其合法使用的土地，不得侵犯其合法权益。 在安置归侨的农场、林场等企业所在的地方，可以根据需要合理设置学校和医疗保健机构，国家在人员、设备、经费等方面给予扶助。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护归侨、侨眷的合法的权利和利益，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of protecting the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese and the family members of overseas Chinese.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　归侨是指回国定居的华侨。华侨是指定居在国外的中国公民。  
侨眷是指华侨、归侨在国内的眷属。  
本法所称侨眷包括：华侨、归侨的配偶，父母，子女及其配偶，兄弟姐妹，祖父母、外祖父母，孙子女、外孙子女，以及同华侨、归侨有长期扶养关系的其他亲属。

**Article 2** "Returned overseas Chinese" means overseas Chinese who have returned to China for permanent residence. "Overseas Chinese" means Chinese citizens residing abroad.  
"Family members of overseas Chinese" means the dependents of overseas Chinese and returned overseas Chinese who are within the territory of China.  
The term "family members of overseas Chinese" as used in this Law includes the spouses, parents, children and their spouses, brothers and sisters, grandparents, maternal grandparents, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and other relatives who have a long-term supporting relationship with overseas Chinese or returned overseas Chinese.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　归侨、侨眷享有宪法和法律规定的公民的权利，并履行宪法和法律规定的公民的义务，任何组织或者个人不得歧视。  
国家根据实际情况和归侨、侨眷的特点，给予适当照顾，具体办法由国务院或者国务院有关主管部门规定。

**Article 3** Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese shall enjoy the rights of citizens as prescribed by the Constitution and laws, and shall fulfill the duties of citizens as prescribed by the Constitution and laws. No organization or individual may discriminate against them.  
The State shall, in light of the actual circumstances and the special characteristics of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese, grant them appropriate preferential treatment. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council or the relevant competent departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上各级人民政府及其负责侨务工作的机构，组织协调有关部门做好保护归侨、侨眷的合法权益的工作。

**Article 4** The people's governments at or above the county level and their organs responsible for affairs concerning overseas Chinese shall organize and coordinate relevant departments to perform well the work of protecting the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对回国定居的华侨给予安置。

**Article 5** The State shall arrange for the resettlement of overseas Chinese who have returned to China for permanent residence.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国人民代表大会和归侨人数较多地区的地方人民代表大会应当有适当名额的归侨代表。

**Article 6** The National People's Congress and the local people's congresses of areas where there is a relatively large number of returned overseas Chinese shall have an appropriate number of deputies who are returned overseas Chinese.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　归侨、侨眷有权依法申请成立社会团体，进行适合归侨、侨眷需要的合法的社会活动。  
归侨、侨眷依法成立的社会团体的财产受法律保护，任何组织或者个人不得侵犯。

**Article 7** Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese shall have the right to apply for the establishment of social organizations according to law and to conduct lawful social activities suited to their needs.  
The property of social organizations lawfully established by returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese shall be protected by law. No organization or individual may infringe upon it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中华全国归国华侨联合会和地方归国华侨联合会代表归侨、侨眷的利益，依法维护归侨、侨眷的合法权益。

**Article 8** The All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese and the local federations of returned overseas Chinese shall represent the interests of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese and safeguard their legitimate rights and interests according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对安置归侨的农场、林场等企业给予扶持，任何组织或者个人不得侵占其合法使用的土地，不得侵犯其合法权益。  
在安置归侨的农场、林场等企业所在的地方，可以根据需要合理设置学校和医疗保健机构，国家在人员、设备、经费等方面给予扶助。

**Article 9** The State shall give support to enterprises such as farms and forestry centers that resettle returned overseas Chinese. No organization or individual may encroach upon the land lawfully used by such enterprises or infringe upon their legitimate rights and interests.  
In localities where enterprises such as farms and forestry centers that resettle returned overseas Chinese are located, schools and medical and health care institutions may be rationally established as needed, and the State shall provide assistance in terms of personnel, equipment, funds, etc.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家依法维护归侨、侨眷职工的社会保障权益。用人单位及归侨、侨眷职工应当依法参加当地的社会保险，缴纳社会保险费用。  
对丧失劳动能力又无经济来源或者生活确有困难的归侨、侨眷，当地人民政府应当给予救济。

**Article 10** The State shall safeguard the social security rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese who are workers and staff members according to law. The employing units and the said workers and staff members shall participate in local social insurance and pay social insurance premiums according to law.  
With respect to returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese who have lost the ability to work and have no financial resources or who are indeed in financial difficulties, the local people's government shall provide relief.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励和引导归侨、侨眷依法投资兴办产业，特别是兴办高新技术企业，各级人民政府应当给予支持，其合法权益受法律保护。

**Article 11** The State shall encourage and guide returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese to invest in and establish industries according to law, especially high-tech enterprises. People's governments at all levels shall give support to them, and their legitimate rights and interests shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　归侨、侨眷在国内兴办公益事业，各级人民政府应当给予支持，其合法权益受法律保护。  
归侨、侨眷境外亲友捐赠的物资用于国内公益事业的，依照法律、行政法规的规定减征或者免征关税和进口环节的增值税。

**Article 12** Where returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese establish public welfare undertakings within China, the people's governments at all levels shall give support to them, and their legitimate rights and interests shall be protected by law.  
Where goods donated by relatives and friends abroad of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese are used for public welfare undertakings within China, the customs duties and value-added tax on imports shall be reduced or exempted in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家依法保护归侨、侨眷在国内私有房屋的所有权。  
依法征收、征用、拆迁归侨、侨眷私有房屋的，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定给予合理补偿和妥善安置。

**Article 13** The State shall protect the ownership of private houses within China owned by returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese according to law.  
Where private houses of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese are lawfully expropriated, requisitioned, or demolished, the construction entity shall provide reasonable compensation and proper resettlement in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级人民政府应当对归侨、侨眷就业给予照顾，提供必要的指导和服务。  
归侨学生、归侨子女和华侨在国内的子女升学，按照国家有关规定给予照顾。

**Article 14** People's governments at all levels shall give preferential treatment to returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese in employment and provide necessary guidance and services.  
For returned overseas Chinese students, children of returned overseas Chinese, and children of overseas Chinese within China, preferential treatment shall be given in entering schools of a higher level in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家保护归侨、侨眷的侨汇收入。

**Article 15** The State shall protect the overseas remittance income of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　归侨、侨眷有权接受境外亲友的遗赠或者赠与。  
归侨、侨眷继承境外遗产的权益受法律保护。  
归侨、侨眷有权处分其在境外的财产。

**Article 16** Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese shall have the right to accept bequests or gifts from relatives and friends abroad.  
The rights of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese to inherit estates abroad shall be protected by law.  
Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese shall have the right to dispose of their property abroad.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　归侨、侨眷与境外亲友的往来和通讯受法律保护。

**Article 17** The contacts and communications between returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese and their relatives and friends abroad shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　归侨、侨眷申请出境，有关主管部门应当在规定期限内办理手续。  
归侨、侨眷确因境外直系亲属病危、死亡或者限期处理境外财产等特殊情况急需出境的，有关主管部门应当根据申请人提供的有效证明优先办理手续。

**Article 18** Where returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese apply for exit from China, the relevant competent department shall go through the procedures within the prescribed time limit.  
Where returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese urgently need to exit from China due to special circumstances such as critical illness or death of a direct relative abroad or the need to deal with property abroad within a time limit, the relevant competent department shall give priority to going through the formalities based on the valid certificates provided by the applicant.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家保障归侨、侨眷出境探亲的权利。  
归侨、侨眷职工按照国家有关规定享受出境探亲的待遇。

**Article 19** The State shall guarantee the right of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese to exit from China for visiting relatives.  
Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese who are workers and staff members shall enjoy the treatment of exiting from China for visiting relatives in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　归侨、侨眷可以按照国家有关规定申请出境定居，经批准出境定居的，任何组织或者个人不得损害其合法权益。  
离休、退休、退职的归侨、侨眷职工出境定居的，其离休金、退休金、退职金、养老金照发。

**Article 20** Returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese may apply for exit from China for permanent residence in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State. Where their exit for permanent residence is approved, no organization or individual may harm their legitimate rights and interests.  
Where returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese who have retired or resigned from their posts exit from China for permanent residence, their retirement pensions, resignation subsidies, and old-age pensions shall continue to be paid.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　归侨、侨眷申请自费出境学习、讲学的，或者因经商出境的，其所在单位和有关部门应当提供便利。

**Article 21** Where returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese apply for exit from China for self-funded study or lecturing, or for business purposes, their employing units and the relevant departments shall provide convenience.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对归侨、侨眷在境外的正当权益，根据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约或者国际惯例，给予保护。

**Article 22** The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese and family members of overseas Chinese abroad in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China or international practice.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　归侨、侨眷合法权益受到侵害时，被侵害人有权要求有关主管部门依法处理，或者向人民法院提起诉讼。归国华侨联合会应当给予支持和帮助。

**Article 23** Where the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese are infringed upon, the infringed party shall have the right to request the relevant competent department to deal with the matter according to law, or to institute proceedings in a People's Court. The Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese shall give support and assistance.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家机关工作人员玩忽职守或者滥用职权，致使归侨、侨眷合法权益受到损害的，其所在单位或者上级主管机关应当责令改正或者给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where a functionary of a State organ neglects his duty or abuses his power, causing damage to the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese, his unit or the competent authority at a higher level shall order him to make corrections or shall impose administrative sanctions on him; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织或者个人侵害归侨、侨眷的合法权益，造成归侨、侨眷财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Any organization or individual that infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese, causing property loss or other harm to them, shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反[本法第九条](#art-9)第一款规定，非法占用安置归侨的农场、林场合法使用的土地，有关主管部门应当责令退还；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 26** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 9 of this Law, illegally occupies land lawfully used by farms or forestry centers that resettle returned overseas Chinese, the relevant competent department shall order the return of the land; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反[本法第十三条](#art-13)规定，非法侵占归侨、侨眷在国内私有房屋的，有关主管部门应当责令退还；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 27** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of Article 13 of this Law, illegally occupies private houses of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese within China, the relevant competent department shall order the return of the houses; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)第二款规定，停发、扣发、侵占或者挪用出境定居的归侨、侨眷的离休金、退休金、退职金、养老金的，有关单位或者有关主管部门应当责令补发，并依法给予赔偿；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 20 of this Law, stops paying, withholds, embezzles, or misappropriates the retirement pensions, resignation subsidies, or old-age pensions of returned overseas Chinese or family members of overseas Chinese who have exited from China for permanent residence, the relevant unit or the relevant competent department shall order the payment of the arrearage, and compensation shall be made according to law; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院根据本法制定实施办法。  
省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法和国务院的实施办法，制定实施办法。

**Article 29** The State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate measures for implementation.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and the measures for implementation formulated by the State Council, formulate measures for implementation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法自1991年1月1日起施行。

**Article 30** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1991.
