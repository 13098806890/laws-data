### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强进出口商品检验工作，规范进出口商品检验行为，维护社会公共利益和进出口贸易有关各方的合法权益，促进对外经济贸易关系的顺利发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of strengthening the inspection of import and export commodities, regulating the conduct of such inspection, protecting the public interest and the lawful rights and interests of the parties concerned in import and export trade, and promoting the smooth development of foreign economic and trade relations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院设立进出口商品检验部门（以下简称国家商检部门），主管全国进出口商品检验工作。国家商检部门设在各地的进出口商品检验机构（以下简称商检机构）管理所辖地区的进出口商品检验工作。

**Article 2** The State Council shall establish an import and export commodity inspection department (hereinafter referred to as the State Administration for Commodity Inspection), which shall be in charge of the inspection of import and export commodities nationwide. The import and export commodity inspection authorities (hereinafter referred to as the commodity inspection authorities) established by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection in various localities shall administer the inspection of import and export commodities within their respective areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　商检机构和依法设立的检验机构（以下称其他检验机构），依法对进出口商品实施检验。

**Article 3** The commodity inspection authorities and the inspection authorities established according to law (hereinafter referred to as other inspection authorities) shall, according to law, conduct inspection of import and export commodities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　进出口商品检验应当根据保护人类健康和安全、保护动物或者植物的生命和健康、保护环境、防止欺诈行为、维护国家安全的原则，由国家商检部门制定、调整必须实施检验的进出口商品目录（以下简称目录）并公布实施。

**Article 4** The inspection of import and export commodities shall, in accordance with the principles of protecting human health and safety, protecting the life and health of animals or plants, protecting the environment, preventing fraudulent practices, and safeguarding national security, be carried out by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection, which shall formulate and adjust the catalogue of import and export commodities subject to mandatory inspection (hereinafter referred to as the catalogue) and publish it for implementation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　列入目录的进出口商品，由商检机构实施检验。  
前款规定的进口商品未经检验的，不准销售、使用；前款规定的出口商品未经检验合格的，不准出口。  
本条第一款规定的进出口商品，其中符合国家规定的免予检验条件的，由收货人或者发货人申请，经国家商检部门审查批准，可以免予检验。

**Article 5** Import and export commodities included in the catalogue shall be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities.  
Import commodities as specified in the preceding paragraph that have not been inspected shall not be sold or used; export commodities as specified in the preceding paragraph that have not been inspected and found to be in conformity with the requirements shall not be exported.  
For import and export commodities as specified in the first paragraph of this Article, those meeting the conditions for exemption from inspection as prescribed by the State may be exempted from inspection upon application by the consignee or consignor and upon examination and approval by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　必须实施的进出口商品检验，是指确定列入目录的进出口商品是否符合国家技术规范的强制性要求的合格评定活动。  
合格评定程序包括：抽样、检验和检查；评估、验证和合格保证；注册、认可和批准以及各项的组合。  
对本条第一款规定的进出口商品检验，商检机构可以采信检验机构的检验结果；国家商检部门对前述检验机构实行目录管理。

**Article 6** Mandatory inspection of import and export commodities means the conformity assessment activities to determine whether the import and export commodities included in the catalogue comply with the compulsory requirements of the national technical specifications.  
Conformity assessment procedures include: sampling, inspection and examination; evaluation, verification and assurance of conformity; registration, accreditation and approval as well as combinations thereof.  
With respect to the inspection of import and export commodities as specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the commodity inspection authorities may accept the inspection results of inspection authorities; the State Administration for Commodity Inspection shall exercise catalogue management over the aforementioned inspection authorities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　列入目录的进出口商品，按照国家技术规范的强制性要求进行检验；尚未制定国家技术规范的强制性要求的，应当依法及时制定，未制定之前，可以参照国家商检部门指定的国外有关标准进行检验。

**Article 7** Import and export commodities included in the catalogue shall be inspected in accordance with the compulsory requirements of the national technical specifications; where no such compulsory requirements have been formulated, they shall be formulated promptly according to law, and before such formulation, inspection may be carried out with reference to the relevant foreign standards designated by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　其他检验机构可以接受对外贸易关系人或者外国检验机构的委托，办理进出口商品检验鉴定业务。

**Article 8** Other inspection authorities may accept entrustment by parties to foreign trade or foreign inspection authorities to handle inspection and survey services for import and export commodities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　法律、行政法规规定由其他检验机构实施检验的进出口商品或者检验项目，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 9** Import and export commodities or inspection items which, as provided for by laws and administrative regulations, shall be inspected by other inspection authorities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家商检部门和商检机构应当及时收集和向有关方面提供进出口商品检验方面的信息。  
国家商检部门和商检机构的工作人员在履行进出口商品检验的职责中，对所知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 10** The State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities shall promptly collect and provide to the relevant parties information concerning the inspection of import and export commodities.  
The staff members of the State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities shall, in the performance of their duties of inspecting import and export commodities, keep confidential any trade secrets they become aware of.

### 第二章　进口商品的检验

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本法规定必须经商检机构检验的进口商品的收货人或者其代理人，应当向报关地的商检机构报检。

**Article 11** The consignee or its agent of import commodities which, as provided for by this Law, must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities shall apply for inspection to the commodity inspection authorities at the place of customs declaration.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本法规定必须经商检机构检验的进口商品的收货人或者其代理人，应当在商检机构规定的地点和期限内，接受商检机构对进口商品的检验。商检机构应当在国家商检部门统一规定的期限内检验完毕，并出具检验证单。

**Article 12** The consignee or its agent of import commodities which, as provided for by this Law, must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities shall, at the place and within the time limit prescribed by the commodity inspection authorities, submit to the inspection of the import commodities by the commodity inspection authorities. The commodity inspection authorities shall complete the inspection within the time limit uniformly prescribed by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection and issue an inspection certificate.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本法规定必须经商检机构检验的进口商品以外的进口商品的收货人，发现进口商品质量不合格或者残损短缺，需要由商检机构出证索赔的，应当向商检机构申请检验出证。

**Article 13** Where the consignee of import commodities other than those which, as provided for by this Law, must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities discovers that the import commodities are of substandard quality, damaged or short in quantity, and needs to claim compensation with a certificate issued by the commodity inspection authorities, the consignee shall apply to the commodity inspection authorities for inspection and issuance of a certificate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对重要的进口商品和大型的成套设备，收货人应当依据对外贸易合同约定在出口国装运前进行预检验、监造或者监装，主管部门应当加强监督；商检机构根据需要可以派出检验人员参加。

**Article 14** For important import commodities and large complete sets of equipment, the consignee shall, in accordance with the provisions of the foreign trade contract, arrange for pre-inspection, supervision of manufacture or supervision of loading before shipment in the exporting country, and the competent department shall strengthen supervision; the commodity inspection authorities may, when necessary, assign inspectors to participate.

### 第三章　出口商品的检验

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本法规定必须经商检机构检验的出口商品的发货人或者其代理人，应当在商检机构规定的地点和期限内，向商检机构报检。商检机构应当在国家商检部门统一规定的期限内检验完毕，并出具检验证单。

**Article 15** The consignor or its agent of export commodities which, as provided for by this Law, must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities shall apply for inspection to the commodity inspection authorities at the place and within the time limit prescribed by the commodity inspection authorities. The commodity inspection authorities shall complete the inspection within the time limit uniformly prescribed by the State Administration for Commodity Inspection and issue an inspection certificate.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经商检机构检验合格发给检验证单的出口商品，应当在商检机构规定的期限内报关出口；超过期限的，应当重新报检。

**Article 16** Export commodities which have been inspected and found to be in conformity with the requirements and for which an inspection certificate has been issued by the commodity inspection authorities shall be declared for export within the time limit prescribed by the commodity inspection authorities; if the time limit is exceeded, a new application for inspection shall be made.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　为出口危险货物生产包装容器的企业，必须申请商检机构进行包装容器的性能鉴定。生产出口危险货物的企业，必须申请商检机构进行包装容器的使用鉴定。使用未经鉴定合格的包装容器的危险货物，不准出口。

**Article 17** Enterprises producing packaging containers for the export of dangerous goods must apply to the commodity inspection authorities for the performance appraisal of the packaging containers. Enterprises producing export dangerous goods must apply to the commodity inspection authorities for the use appraisal of the packaging containers. Dangerous goods packed in containers which have not been appraised and found to be in conformity with the requirements shall not be exported.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对装运出口易腐烂变质食品的船舱和集装箱，承运人或者装箱单位必须在装货前申请检验。未经检验合格的，不准装运。

**Article 18** For holds and containers used for carrying export foods that are prone to deterioration or spoilage, the carrier or the container packing unit must apply for inspection before loading. Those which have not been inspected and found to be in conformity with the requirements shall not be loaded.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　商检机构对本法规定必须经商检机构检验的进出口商品以外的进出口商品，根据国家规定实施抽查检验。  
国家商检部门可以公布抽查检验结果或者向有关部门通报抽查检验情况。

**Article 19** The commodity inspection authorities shall, in accordance with State regulations, carry out random inspection of import and export commodities other than those which, as provided for by this Law, must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities.  
The State Administration for Commodity Inspection may publish the results of random inspection or report the situation of random inspection to the relevant departments.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　商检机构根据便利对外贸易的需要，可以按照国家规定对列入目录的出口商品进行出厂前的质量监督管理和检验。

**Article 20** The commodity inspection authorities may, in light of the need to facilitate foreign trade and in accordance with State regulations, carry out quality supervision and pre-shipment inspection of export commodities included in the catalogue.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　为进出口货物的收发货人办理报检手续的代理人办理报检手续时应当向商检机构提交授权委托书。

**Article 21** An agent handling the inspection application procedures for the consignee or consignor of import or export goods shall, when handling the inspection application procedures, submit a power of attorney to the commodity inspection authorities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家商检部门和商检机构依法对其他检验机构的进出口商品检验鉴定业务活动进行监督，可以对其检验的商品抽查检验。

**Article 22** The State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities shall, according to law, supervise the inspection and survey business activities of other inspection authorities concerning import and export commodities, and may conduct random inspection of the commodities inspected by them.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院认证认可监督管理部门根据国家统一的认证制度，对有关的进出口商品实施认证管理。

**Article 23** The certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the unified national certification system, implement certification management for relevant import and export commodities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　认证机构可以根据国务院认证认可监督管理部门同外国有关机构签订的协议或者接受外国有关机构的委托进行进出口商品质量认证工作，准许在认证合格的进出口商品上使用质量认证标志。

**Article 24** Certification bodies may, in accordance with agreements concluded between the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council and the relevant foreign institutions, or upon entrustment by the relevant foreign institutions, carry out quality certification of import and export commodities, and permit the use of quality certification marks on import and export commodities that have passed certification.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　商检机构依照本法对实施许可制度的进出口商品实行验证管理，查验单证，核对证货是否相符。

**Article 25** The commodity inspection authorities shall, in accordance with this Law, conduct verification management of import and export commodities subject to a licensing system, examine the documents, and verify whether the documents correspond to the goods.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　商检机构根据需要，对检验合格的进出口商品，可以加施商检标志或者封识。

**Article 26** The commodity inspection authorities may, when necessary, affix commodity inspection marks or seals on import and export commodities that have passed inspection.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　进出口商品的报检人对商检机构作出的检验结果有异议的，可以向原商检机构或者其上级商检机构以至国家商检部门申请复验，由受理复验的商检机构或者国家商检部门及时作出复验结论。

**Article 27** Where the applicant for inspection of import or export commodities disagrees with the inspection results made by the commodity inspection authorities, the applicant may apply to the original commodity inspection authorities, their superior commodity inspection authorities, or even to the State Administration for Commodity Inspection for a reinspection, and the commodity inspection authorities or the State Administration for Commodity Inspection that accepts the reinspection application shall promptly make a reinspection conclusion.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人对商检机构、国家商检部门作出的复验结论不服或者对商检机构作出的处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 28** Where a party is not satisfied with the reinspection conclusion made by the commodity inspection authorities or the State Administration for Commodity Inspection, or is not satisfied with the penalty decision made by the commodity inspection authorities, the party may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration, or may bring a lawsuit before a People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家商检部门和商检机构履行职责，必须遵守法律，维护国家利益，依照法定职权和法定程序严格执法，接受监督。  
国家商检部门和商检机构应当根据依法履行职责的需要，加强队伍建设，使商检工作人员具有良好的政治、业务素质。商检工作人员应当定期接受业务培训和考核，经考核合格，方可上岗执行职务。  
商检工作人员必须忠于职守，文明服务，遵守职业道德，不得滥用职权，谋取私利。

**Article 29** The State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities, in performing their duties, must abide by the law, safeguard the interests of the State, strictly enforce the law in accordance with their statutory functions and statutory procedures, and accept supervision.  
The State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities shall, in light of the need to perform their duties according to law, strengthen the development of their teams so that the inspection staff have good political and professional competence. The inspection staff shall receive regular professional training and assessment, and may only assume their posts and perform their duties upon passing the assessment.  
The inspection staff must be devoted to their duties, provide services in a civilized manner, abide by professional ethics, and shall not abuse their power to seek personal gain.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家商检部门和商检机构应当建立健全内部监督制度，对其工作人员的执法活动进行监督检查。  
商检机构内部负责受理报检、检验、出证放行等主要岗位的职责权限应当明确，并相互分离、相互制约。

**Article 30** The State Administration for Commodity Inspection and the commodity inspection authorities shall establish and improve internal supervision systems to supervise and inspect the law enforcement activities of their staff members.  
The functions and powers of the main posts within the commodity inspection authorities responsible for accepting inspection applications, conducting inspections, issuing certificates and releasing goods shall be clearly defined, and shall be mutually separate and mutually restraining.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人均有权对国家商检部门、商检机构及其工作人员的违法、违纪行为进行控告、检举。收到控告、检举的机关应当依法按照职责分工及时查处，并为控告人、检举人保密。

**Article 31** Any unit or individual shall have the right to accuse or report any illegal or disciplinary conduct of the State Administration for Commodity Inspection, the commodity inspection authorities, or their staff members. The authority receiving the accusation or report shall promptly investigate and handle the matter according to law in accordance with the division of responsibilities, and shall keep the accuser or reporter confidential.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本法规定，将必须经商检机构检验的进口商品未报经检验而擅自销售或者使用的，或者将必须经商检机构检验的出口商品未报经检验合格而擅自出口的，由商检机构没收违法所得，并处货值金额百分之五以上百分之二十以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, sells or uses import commodities which must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities without applying for inspection, or exports export commodities which must be inspected by the commodity inspection authorities without applying for inspection and obtaining a certificate of conformity, shall be subject to confiscation of illegal gains by the commodity inspection authorities and shall also be imposed a fine of not less than 5 percent but not more than 20 percent of the value of the goods; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　进口或者出口属于掺杂掺假、以假充真、以次充好的商品或者以不合格进出口商品冒充合格进出口商品的，由商检机构责令停止进口或者出口，没收违法所得，并处货值金额百分之五十以上三倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Anyone who imports or exports goods that are adulterated, fake, shoddy, or passes off substandard import or export commodities as conforming ones, shall be ordered by the commodity inspection authorities to stop importing or exporting, shall have the illegal gains confiscated, and shall also be imposed a fine of not less than 50 percent but not more than three times the value of the goods; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃商检单证、印章、标志、封识、质量认证标志的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由商检机构、认证认可监督管理部门依据各自职责责令改正，没收违法所得，并处货值金额等值以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Anyone who forges, alters, buys or sells, or steals commodity inspection certificates, seals, marks, seals, or quality certification marks shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the commodity inspection authorities or the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department shall, in accordance with their respective functions, order the person to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and also impose a fine not exceeding the value of the goods.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家商检部门、商检机构的工作人员违反本法规定，泄露所知悉的商业秘密的，依法给予行政处分，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where a staff member of the State Administration for Commodity Inspection or the commodity inspection authorities, in violation of the provisions of this Law, discloses any trade secrets he becomes aware of, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家商检部门、商检机构的工作人员滥用职权，故意刁难的，徇私舞弊，伪造检验结果的，或者玩忽职守，延误检验出证的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where a staff member of the State Administration for Commodity Inspection or the commodity inspection authorities abuses his power, deliberately makes difficulties, engages in malpractices for personal gain, forges inspection results, or neglects his duty, causing delay in inspection and issuance of certificates, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　商检机构和其他检验机构依照本法的规定实施检验和办理检验鉴定业务，依照国家有关规定收取费用。

**Article 37** The commodity inspection authorities and other inspection authorities shall, when conducting inspection and handling inspection and survey services in accordance with the provisions of this Law, collect fees in accordance with the relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 38** The State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, formulate implementing regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本法自1989年8月1日起施行。

**Article 39** This Law shall come into force as of August 1, 1989.
