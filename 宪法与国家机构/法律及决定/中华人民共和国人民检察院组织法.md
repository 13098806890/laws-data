### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范人民检察院的设置、组织和职权，保障人民检察院依法履行职责，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to regulate the establishment, organization, and functions and powers of the People's Procuratorates, and to ensure that the People's Procuratorates perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民检察院是国家的法律监督机关。  
人民检察院通过行使检察权，追诉犯罪，维护国家安全和社会秩序，维护个人和组织的合法权益，维护国家利益和社会公共利益，保障法律正确实施，维护社会公平正义，维护国家法制统一、尊严和权威，保障中国特色社会主义建设的顺利进行。

**Article 2** The People's Procuratorates are the legal supervision organs of the State.  
The People's Procuratorates shall, by exercising the procuratorial power, prosecute crimes, safeguard national security and social order, protect the lawful rights and interests of individuals and organizations, protect national interests and social public interests, ensure the correct implementation of laws, uphold social fairness and justice, safeguard the unity, dignity and authority of the national legal system, and ensure the smooth progress of the construction of socialism with Chinese characteristics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民检察院依照宪法、法律和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定设置。

**Article 3** The People's Procuratorates shall be established in accordance with the Constitution, laws, and decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民检察院依照法律规定独立行使检察权，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 4** The People's Procuratorates shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws, independently exercise the procuratorial power and shall not be subject to interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民检察院行使检察权在适用法律上一律平等，不允许任何组织和个人有超越法律的特权，禁止任何形式的歧视。

**Article 5** In exercising the procuratorial power, the People's Procuratorates shall apply the law equally to all and shall not permit any organization or individual to have privileges beyond the law, and any form of discrimination shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民检察院坚持司法公正，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，遵守法定程序，尊重和保障人权。

**Article 6** The People's Procuratorates shall uphold judicial fairness, base their actions on facts, take law as the criterion, observe statutory procedures, and respect and safeguard human rights.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民检察院实行司法公开，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 7** The People's Procuratorates shall practice judicial openness, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院实行司法责任制，建立健全权责统一的司法权力运行机制。

**Article 8** The People's Procuratorates shall implement the judicial accountability system and establish and improve a judicial power operation mechanism with unified powers and responsibilities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　最高人民检察院对全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责并报告工作。地方各级人民检察院对本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责并报告工作。  
各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会对本级人民检察院的工作实施监督。

**Article 9** The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be responsible and report its work to the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee. Local People's Procuratorates at various levels shall be responsible and report their work to the People's Congresses and their Standing Committees at the corresponding levels.  
The People's Congresses and their Standing Committees at various levels shall supervise the work of the People's Procuratorates at the corresponding levels.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　最高人民检察院是最高检察机关。  
最高人民检察院领导地方各级人民检察院和专门人民检察院的工作，上级人民检察院领导下级人民检察院的工作。

**Article 10** The Supreme People's Procuratorate is the highest procuratorial organ.  
The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall lead the work of local People's Procuratorates at various levels and special People's Procuratorates, and a People's Procuratorate at a higher level shall lead the work of a People's Procuratorate at a lower level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民检察院应当接受人民群众监督，保障人民群众对人民检察院工作依法享有知情权、参与权和监督权。

**Article 11** The People's Procuratorates shall accept supervision by the people and ensure that the people enjoy, according to law, the right to be informed, the right to participate, and the right to supervise in respect of the work of the People's Procuratorates.

### 第二章　人民检察院的设置和职权

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民检察院分为：  
（一）最高人民检察院；  
（二）地方各级人民检察院；  
（三）军事检察院等专门人民检察院。

**Article 12** The People's Procuratorates are classified as:  
(1) the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
(2) local People's Procuratorates at various levels; and  
(3) special People's Procuratorates, such as the military procuratorates.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民检察院分为：  
（一）省级人民检察院，包括省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院；  
（二）设区的市级人民检察院，包括省、自治区辖市人民检察院，自治州人民检察院，省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院分院；  
（三）基层人民检察院，包括县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民检察院。

**Article 13** Local People's Procuratorates at various levels are classified as:  
(1) the People's Procuratorates at the provincial level, including the People's Procuratorates of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) the People's Procuratorates at the level of a city divided into districts, including the People's Procuratorates of a city under the jurisdiction of a province or autonomous region, the People's Procuratorates of an autonomous prefecture, and the branch procuratorates of the People's Procuratorates of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; and  
(3) the basic-level People's Procuratorates, including the People's Procuratorates of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在新疆生产建设兵团设立的人民检察院的组织、案件管辖范围和检察官任免，依照全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关规定。

**Article 14** The organization, scope of case jurisdiction, and appointment and removal of procurators of the People's Procuratorate established in the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专门人民检察院的设置、组织、职权和检察官任免，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会规定。

**Article 15** The establishment, organization, functions and powers, and appointment and removal of procurators of special People's Procuratorates shall be prescribed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省级人民检察院和设区的市级人民检察院根据检察工作需要，经最高人民检察院和省级有关部门同意，并提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准，可以在辖区内特定区域设立人民检察院，作为派出机构。

**Article 16** A People's Procuratorate at the provincial level or at the level of a city divided into districts may, based on the needs of procuratorial work, with the consent of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the relevant provincial authorities, and after submitting a request to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for approval, establish a People's Procuratorate in a specific area within its jurisdiction as an dispatched agency.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民检察院根据检察工作需要，可以在监狱、看守所等场所设立检察室，行使派出它的人民检察院的部分职权，也可以对上述场所进行巡回检察。  
省级人民检察院设立检察室，应当经最高人民检察院和省级有关部门同意。设区的市级人民检察院、基层人民检察院设立检察室，应当经省级人民检察院和省级有关部门同意。

**Article 17** A People's Procuratorate may, based on the needs of procuratorial work, set up procuratorial offices in prisons, detention centers, and other places to exercise part of the functions and powers of the People's Procuratorate that dispatches them, and may also conduct circuit inspections of the aforesaid places.  
The establishment of a procuratorial office by a People's Procuratorate at the provincial level shall obtain the consent of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the relevant provincial authorities. The establishment of a procuratorial office by a People's Procuratorate at the level of a city divided into districts or a basic-level People's Procuratorate shall obtain the consent of the People's Procuratorate at the provincial level and the relevant provincial authorities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民检察院根据检察工作需要，设必要的业务机构。检察官员额较少的设区的市级人民检察院和基层人民检察院，可以设综合业务机构。

**Article 18** A People's Procuratorate shall, based on the needs of procuratorial work, establish necessary operational divisions. A People's Procuratorate at the level of a city divided into districts or a basic-level People's Procuratorate with a relatively small procurator headcount may establish a comprehensive operational division.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民检察院根据工作需要，可以设必要的检察辅助机构和行政管理机构。

**Article 19** A People's Procuratorate may, based on work needs, establish necessary procuratorial auxiliary organs and administrative management organs.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民检察院行使下列职权：  
（一）依照法律规定对有关刑事案件行使侦查权；  
（二）对刑事案件进行审查，批准或者决定是否逮捕犯罪嫌疑人；  
（三）对刑事案件进行审查，决定是否提起公诉，对决定提起公诉的案件支持公诉；  
（四）依照法律规定提起公益诉讼；  
（五）对诉讼活动实行法律监督；  
（六）对判决、裁定等生效法律文书的执行工作实行法律监督；  
（七）对监狱、看守所的执法活动实行法律监督；  
（八）法律规定的其他职权。

**Article 20** The People's Procuratorates shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) exercising the power of investigation in relation to relevant criminal cases in accordance with the provisions of laws;  
(2) examining criminal cases, approving or deciding whether to arrest a criminal suspect;  
(3) examining criminal cases, deciding whether to initiate a public prosecution, and supporting the public prosecution in cases for which it has been decided to initiate a public prosecution;  
(4) initiating public interest litigation in accordance with the provisions of laws;  
(5) exercising legal supervision over litigation activities;  
(6) exercising legal supervision over the execution of effective legal documents such as judgments and rulings;  
(7) exercising legal supervision over law enforcement activities in prisons and detention centers; and  
(8) other functions and powers as provided by law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民检察院行使[本法第二十条](#art-20)规定的法律监督职权，可以进行调查核实，并依法提出抗诉、纠正意见、检察建议。有关单位应当予以配合，并及时将采纳纠正意见、检察建议的情况书面回复人民检察院。  
抗诉、纠正意见、检察建议的适用范围及其程序，依照法律有关规定。

**Article 21** In exercising the legal supervision functions and powers as provided for in Article 20 of this Law, the People's Procuratorates may conduct investigation and verification, and shall, according to law, lodge a protest, issue corrective opinions, or make procuratorial suggestions. The relevant entity shall cooperate and promptly provide a written reply to the People's Procuratorate on whether the corrective opinions or procuratorial suggestions have been adopted.  
The scope of application and procedures for protests, corrective opinions, and procuratorial suggestions shall be governed by the relevant provisions of laws.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　最高人民检察院对最高人民法院的死刑复核活动实行监督；对报请核准追诉的案件进行审查，决定是否追诉。

**Article 22** The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall exercise supervision over the review of death sentences by the Supreme People's Court; and shall examine cases submitted for approval of prosecution and decide whether to prosecute.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　最高人民检察院可以对属于检察工作中具体应用法律的问题进行解释。  
最高人民检察院可以发布指导性案例。

**Article 23** The Supreme People's Procuratorate may issue interpretations on issues concerning the specific application of laws in procuratorial work.  
The Supreme People's Procuratorate may issue guiding cases.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　上级人民检察院对下级人民检察院行使下列职权：  
（一）认为下级人民检察院的决定错误的，指令下级人民检察院纠正，或者依法撤销、变更；  
（二）可以对下级人民检察院管辖的案件指定管辖；  
（三）可以办理下级人民检察院管辖的案件；  
（四）可以统一调用辖区的检察人员办理案件。  
上级人民检察院的决定，应当以书面形式作出。

**Article 24** A People's Procuratorate at a higher level shall exercise the following functions and powers over a People's Procuratorate at a lower level:  
(1) if it considers a decision made by the lower-level People's Procuratorate to be erroneous, it shall instruct the lower-level People's Procuratorate to correct it, or revoke or change it according to law;  
(2) it may designate jurisdiction over a case within the jurisdiction of the lower-level People's Procuratorate;  
(3) it may handle a case within the jurisdiction of the lower-level People's Procuratorate; and  
(4) it may uniformly assign procuratorial personnel within its jurisdiction to handle cases.  
A decision of a higher-level People's Procuratorate shall be made in writing.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　下级人民检察院应当执行上级人民检察院的决定；有不同意见的，可以在执行的同时向上级人民检察院报告。

**Article 25** A lower-level People's Procuratorate shall execute the decision of a higher-level People's Procuratorate; if it has objections, it may simultaneously report to the higher-level People's Procuratorate while executing the decision.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民检察院检察长或者检察长委托的副检察长，可以列席同级人民法院审判委员会会议。

**Article 26** The chief procurator of a People's Procuratorate, or a deputy chief procurator authorized by the chief procurator, may attend the meetings of the Adjudication Committee of the People's Court at the corresponding level as a nonvoting participant.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民监督员依照规定对人民检察院的办案活动实行监督。

**Article 27** People's supervisors shall, in accordance with the provisions, supervise the case-handling activities of the People's Procuratorates.

### 第三章　人民检察院的办案组织

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民检察院办理案件，根据案件情况可以由一名检察官独任办理，也可以由两名以上检察官组成办案组办理。  
由检察官办案组办理的，检察长应当指定一名检察官担任主办检察官，组织、指挥办案组办理案件。

**Article 28** When handling cases, the People's Procuratorates may, depending on the circumstances of the case, have a single procurator handle the case independently, or have a case-handling group composed of two or more procurators handle the case.  
Where a case is handled by a procurator case-handling group, the chief procurator shall designate a procurator to serve as the lead procurator to organize and direct the case-handling group in handling the case.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　检察官在检察长领导下开展工作，重大办案事项由检察长决定。检察长可以将部分职权委托检察官行使，可以授权检察官签发法律文书。

**Article 29** Procurators shall carry out their work under the leadership of the chief procurator, and major case-handling matters shall be decided by the chief procurator. The chief procurator may delegate part of his or her functions and powers to procurators for exercise and may authorize procurators to issue legal documents.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民检察院设检察委员会。检察委员会由检察长、副检察长和若干资深检察官组成，成员应当为单数。

**Article 30** People's Procuratorates at various levels shall establish a Procuratorial Committee. The Procuratorial Committee shall be composed of the chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, and a number of senior procurators, and the number of its members shall be odd.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　检察委员会履行下列职能：  
（一）总结检察工作经验；  
（二）讨论决定重大、疑难、复杂案件；  
（三）讨论决定其他有关检察工作的重大问题。  
最高人民检察院对属于检察工作中具体应用法律的问题进行解释、发布指导性案例，应当由检察委员会讨论通过。

**Article 31** The Procuratorial Committee shall perform the following functions:  
(1) summarizing procuratorial work experience;  
(2) discussing and deciding on major, difficult, and complex cases;  
(3) discussing and deciding on other major issues related to procuratorial work.  
Where the Supreme People's Procuratorate issues interpretations on issues concerning the specific application of laws in procuratorial work or issues guiding cases, such matters shall be discussed and adopted by the Procuratorial Committee.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　检察委员会召开会议，应当有其组成人员的过半数出席。  
检察委员会会议由检察长或者检察长委托的副检察长主持。检察委员会实行民主集中制。  
地方各级人民检察院的检察长不同意本院检察委员会多数人的意见，属于办理案件的，可以报请上一级人民检察院决定；属于重大事项的，可以报请上一级人民检察院或者本级人民代表大会常务委员会决定。

**Article 32** A meeting of the Procuratorial Committee shall be held only if more than half of its members are present.  
A meeting of the Procuratorial Committee shall be presided over by the chief procurator or a deputy chief procurator authorized by the chief procurator. The Procuratorial Committee shall practice democratic centralism.  
If the chief procurator of a local People's Procuratorate at any level disagrees with the opinion of the majority of the members of the Procuratorial Committee of that procuratorate, and the matter concerns case handling, the chief procurator may submit the matter to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for a decision; if the matter concerns important issues, the chief procurator may submit the matter to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level or the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for a decision.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　检察官可以就重大案件和其他重大问题，提请检察长决定。检察长可以根据案件情况，提交检察委员会讨论决定。  
检察委员会讨论案件，检察官对其汇报的事实负责，检察委员会委员对本人发表的意见和表决负责。检察委员会的决定，检察官应当执行。

**Article 33** A procurator may, with respect to major cases and other major issues, request the chief procurator to make a decision. The chief procurator may, depending on the circumstances of the case, submit the matter to the Procuratorial Committee for discussion and decision.  
When the Procuratorial Committee discusses a case, the procurator shall be responsible for the facts reported, and the members of the Procuratorial Committee shall be responsible for their own opinions expressed and votes cast. The decisions of the Procuratorial Committee shall be executed by the procurators.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民检察院实行检察官办案责任制。检察官对其职权范围内就案件作出的决定负责。检察长、检察委员会对案件作出决定的，承担相应责任。

**Article 34** The People's Procuratorates shall implement the procurator case-handling accountability system. A procurator shall be responsible for the decisions made within the scope of his or her functions and powers in relation to cases. If the chief procurator or the Procuratorial Committee makes a decision on a case, the corresponding responsibility shall be borne.

### 第四章　人民检察院的人员组成

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人民检察院的检察人员由检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员等人员组成。

**Article 35** The procuratorial personnel of a People's Procuratorate shall be composed of the chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators, among others.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民检察院检察长领导本院检察工作，管理本院行政事务。人民检察院副检察长协助检察长工作。

**Article 36** The chief procurator of a People's Procuratorate shall lead the procuratorial work of that procuratorate and manage the administrative affairs of that procuratorate. The deputy chief procurators of a People's Procuratorate shall assist the chief procurator in his or her work.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　最高人民检察院检察长由全国人民代表大会选举和罢免，副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员由检察长提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。

**Article 37** The chief procurator of the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be elected and removed by the National People's Congress. The deputy chief procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the chief procurator.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方各级人民检察院检察长由本级人民代表大会选举和罢免，副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员由检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
地方各级人民检察院检察长的任免，须报上一级人民检察院检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院分院检察长、副检察长、检察委员会委员和检察员，由省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院检察长提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。

**Article 38** The chief procurator of a local People's Procuratorate at any level shall be elected and removed by the People's Congress at the corresponding level. The deputy chief procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the chief procurator.  
The appointment and removal of the chief procurator of a local People's Procuratorate at any level shall be reported to the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level, who shall submit the matter to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the corresponding level for approval.  
The chief procurator, deputy chief procurators, members of the Procuratorial Committee, and procurators of a branch procuratorate of the People's Procuratorate of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government upon the recommendation of the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民检察院检察长任期与产生它的人民代表大会每届任期相同。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会和省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会根据本级人民检察院检察长的建议，可以撤换下级人民检察院检察长、副检察长和检察委员会委员。

**Article 39** The term of office of the chief procurator of a People's Procuratorate shall be the same as the term of office of the People's Congress that elects him or her.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the Standing Committees of the People's Congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, upon the proposal of the chief procurator of the People's Procuratorate at the corresponding level, remove the chief procurators, deputy chief procurators, and members of the Procuratorial Committees of the People's Procuratorates at lower levels.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民检察院的检察官、检察辅助人员和司法行政人员实行分类管理。

**Article 40** The procurators, procuratorial auxiliary personnel, and judicial administrative personnel of the People's Procuratorates shall be subject to classified management.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　检察官实行员额制。检察官员额根据案件数量、经济社会发展情况、人口数量和人民检察院层级等因素确定。  
最高人民检察院检察官员额由最高人民检察院商有关部门确定。地方各级人民检察院检察官员额，在省、自治区、直辖市内实行总量控制、动态管理。

**Article 41** The procurator post system shall be implemented. The number of procurator posts shall be determined based on factors such as the number of cases, economic and social development, population size, and the level of the People's Procuratorate.  
The number of procurator posts in the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be determined by the Supreme People's Procuratorate in consultation with relevant departments. The number of procurator posts in local People's Procuratorates at various levels shall be subject to total amount control and dynamic management within the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　检察官从取得法律职业资格并且具备法律规定的其他条件的人员中选任。初任检察官应当由检察官遴选委员会进行专业能力审核。上级人民检察院的检察官一般从下级人民检察院的检察官中择优遴选。  
检察长应当具有法学专业知识和法律职业经历。副检察长、检察委员会委员应当从检察官、法官或者其他具备检察官、法官条件的人员中产生。  
检察官的职责、管理和保障，依照《中华人民共和国检察官法》的规定。

**Article 42** Procurators shall be selected and appointed from among persons who have obtained the legal professional qualification and meet other conditions prescribed by law. The professional competence of first-appointed procurators shall be reviewed by a procurator selection committee. Procurators of a People's Procuratorate at a higher level shall generally be selected and appointed on a competitive basis from among procurators of People's Procuratorates at lower levels.  
The chief procurator shall have professional legal knowledge and legal professional experience. Deputy chief procurators and members of the Procuratorial Committee shall be selected from among procurators, judges, or other persons who meet the conditions for being a procurator or judge.  
The duties, management, and guarantees of procurators shall be governed by the provisions of the Procurators Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　人民检察院的检察官助理在检察官指导下负责审查案件材料、草拟法律文书等检察辅助事务。  
符合检察官任职条件的检察官助理，经遴选后可以按照检察官任免程序任命为检察官。

**Article 43** Procurator assistants of a People's Procuratorate shall, under the guidance of procurators, be responsible for procuratorial auxiliary matters such as reviewing case materials and drafting legal documents.  
Procurator assistants who meet the conditions for serving as a procurator may, after selection, be appointed as procurators in accordance with the procurator appointment and removal procedures.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　人民检察院的书记员负责案件记录等检察辅助事务。

**Article 44** Clerks of a People's Procuratorate shall be responsible for procuratorial auxiliary matters such as case recording.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　人民检察院的司法警察负责办案场所警戒、人员押解和看管等警务事项。  
司法警察依照《中华人民共和国人民警察法》管理。

**Article 45** The judicial police of a People's Procuratorate shall be responsible for police affairs such as guarding case-handling sites, escorting and guarding persons.  
The judicial police shall be managed in accordance with the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　人民检察院根据检察工作需要，可以设检察技术人员，负责与检察工作有关的事项。

**Article 46** A People's Procuratorate may, based on the needs of procuratorial work, appoint procuratorial technical personnel to be responsible for matters related to procuratorial work.

### 第五章　人民检察院行使职权的保障

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　任何单位或者个人不得要求检察官从事超出法定职责范围的事务。  
对于领导干部等干预司法活动、插手具体案件处理，或者人民检察院内部人员过问案件情况的，办案人员应当全面如实记录并报告；有违法违纪情形的，由有关机关根据情节轻重追究行为人的责任。

**Article 47** No entity or individual may require a procurator to engage in matters beyond the scope of statutory duties.  
Where leading cadres or other persons interfere in judicial activities, meddle in the handling of specific cases, or where internal personnel of a People's Procuratorate inquire about the circumstances of a case, the case-handling personnel shall comprehensively and truthfully record and report such conduct; if such conduct is illegal or in violation of discipline, the relevant authorities shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, pursue the liability of the person who committed the act.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　人民检察院采取必要措施，维护办案安全。对妨碍人民检察院依法行使职权的违法犯罪行为，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 48** The People's Procuratorates shall take necessary measures to maintain the security of case handling. For illegal or criminal acts that obstruct the People's Procuratorates from exercising their functions and powers according to law, the legal liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　人民检察院实行培训制度，检察官、检察辅助人员和司法行政人员应当接受理论和业务培训。

**Article 49** The People's Procuratorates shall implement a training system. Procurators, procuratorial auxiliary personnel, and judicial administrative personnel shall receive theoretical and professional training.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民检察院人员编制实行专项管理。

**Article 50** The staffing of the People's Procuratorates shall be subject to special management.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民检察院的经费按照事权划分的原则列入财政预算，保障检察工作需要。

**Article 51** The funds of the People's Procuratorates shall be included in the fiscal budget in accordance with the principle of division of powers and responsibilities to meet the needs of procuratorial work.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　人民检察院应当加强信息化建设，运用现代信息技术，促进司法公开，提高工作效率。

**Article 52** The People's Procuratorates shall strengthen information technology development, use modern information technology to promote judicial openness and improve work efficiency.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本法自2019年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2019.
