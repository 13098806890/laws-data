# 中华人民共和国公益事业捐赠法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1999-06-28  
**生效日期**：1999-09-01

> （1999年6月28日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　捐赠和受赠 第三章　捐赠财产的使用和管理 第四章　优惠措施 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了鼓励捐赠，规范捐赠和受赠行为，保护捐赠人、受赠人和受益人的合法权益，促进公益事业的发展，制定本法。 第二条　自然人、法人或者其他组织自愿无偿向依法成立的公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位捐赠财产，用于公益事业的，适用本法。 第三条　本法所称公益事业是指非营利的下列事项： （一）救助灾害、救济贫困、扶助残疾人等困难的社会群体和个人的活动； （二）教育、科学、文化、卫生、体育事业； （三）环境保护、社会公共设施建设；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了鼓励捐赠，规范捐赠和受赠行为，保护捐赠人、受赠人和受益人的合法权益，促进公益事业的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of encouraging donations, regulating donation and acceptance of donations, protecting the lawful rights and interests of donors, donees, and beneficiaries, and promoting the development of public welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　自然人、法人或者其他组织自愿无偿向依法成立的公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位捐赠财产，用于公益事业的，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply where natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations voluntarily and gratuitously donate property to lawfully established public welfare social organizations and public welfare non-profit institutional units for use in public welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称公益事业是指非营利的下列事项：  
（一）救助灾害、救济贫困、扶助残疾人等困难的社会群体和个人的活动；  
（二）教育、科学、文化、卫生、体育事业；  
（三）环境保护、社会公共设施建设；  
（四）促进社会发展和进步的其他社会公共和福利事业。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "public welfare undertakings" means the following non-profit matters:  
(1) activities for disaster relief, poverty relief, and assistance to disadvantaged social groups and individuals such as the disabled;  
(2) undertakings of education, science, culture, health, and sports;  
(3) environmental protection and construction of social public facilities;  
(4) other social public and welfare undertakings for promoting social development and progress.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　捐赠应当是自愿和无偿的，禁止强行摊派或者变相摊派，不得以捐赠为名从事营利活动。

**Article 4** Donations shall be voluntary and gratuitous. Forced apportionment or apportionment in disguised form is prohibited, and no person shall engage in for-profit activities in the name of donations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　捐赠财产的使用应当尊重捐赠人的意愿，符合公益目的，不得将捐赠财产挪作他用。

**Article 5** The use of donated property shall respect the wishes of the donor, conform to the public welfare purpose, and the donated property shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　捐赠应当遵守法律、法规，不得违背社会公德，不得损害公共利益和其他公民的合法权益。

**Article 6** Donations shall comply with laws and regulations, shall not contravene social ethics, and shall not harm the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of other citizens.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公益性社会团体受赠的财产及其增值为社会公共财产，受国家法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵占、挪用和损毁。

**Article 7** Property accepted by a public welfare social organization and any appreciation thereof shall be social public property, protected by the laws of the State, and no entity or individual may embezzle, misappropriate, or damage it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励公益事业的发展，对公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位给予扶持和优待。  
国家鼓励自然人、法人或者其他组织对公益事业进行捐赠。  
对公益事业捐赠有突出贡献的自然人、法人或者其他组织，由人民政府或者有关部门予以表彰。对捐赠人进行公开表彰，应当事先征求捐赠人的意见。

**Article 8** The State encourages the development of public welfare undertakings and provides support and preferential treatment to public welfare social organizations and public welfare non-profit institutional units. The State encourages natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations to make donations to public welfare undertakings. Natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations that have made outstanding contributions to public welfare donations shall be commended by the people's government or the relevant departments. Public commendation of a donor shall be subject to the donor's prior consent.

### 第二章　捐赠和受赠

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　自然人、法人或者其他组织可以选择符合其捐赠意愿的公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位进行捐赠。捐赠的财产应当是其有权处分的合法财产。

**Article 9** A natural person, legal person, or other organization may choose a public welfare social organization or public welfare non-profit institutional unit that matches its donation intention to make a donation. The donated property shall be lawful property over which it has the right of disposal.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位可以依照本法接受捐赠。  
本法所称公益性社会团体是指依法成立的，以发展公益事业为宗旨的基金会、慈善组织等社会团体。  
本法所称公益性非营利的事业单位是指依法成立的，从事公益事业的不以营利为目的的教育机构、科学研究机构、医疗卫生机构、社会公共文化机构、社会公共体育机构和社会福利机构等。

**Article 10** Public welfare social organizations and public welfare non-profit institutional units may accept donations in accordance with this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "public welfare social organization" means a social organization such as a foundation or charitable organization that is lawfully established and aims to develop public welfare undertakings.  
For the purposes of this Law, "public welfare non-profit institutional unit" means a lawfully established institution such as an educational institution, scientific research institution, medical and health institution, social public cultural institution, social public sports institution, or social welfare institution that engages in public welfare undertakings and does not aim at making profits.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在发生自然灾害时或者境外捐赠人要求县级以上人民政府及其部门作为受赠人时，县级以上人民政府及其部门可以接受捐赠，并依照本法的有关规定对捐赠财产进行管理。  
县级以上人民政府及其部门可以将受赠财产转交公益性社会团体或者公益性非营利的事业单位；也可以按照捐赠人的意愿分发或者兴办公益事业，但是不得以本机关为受益对象。

**Article 11** At the time of a natural disaster or when an overseas donor requires a people's government at or above the county level or its department to act as the donee, such people's government or department may accept the donation and manage the donated property in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.  
A people's government at or above the county level or its department may transfer the donated property to a public welfare social organization or public welfare non-profit institutional unit; it may also distribute the property or undertake public welfare projects in accordance with the donor's wishes, but the government organ itself shall not be the beneficiary.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　捐赠人可以与受赠人就捐赠财产的种类、质量、数量和用途等内容订立捐赠协议。捐赠人有权决定捐赠的数量、用途和方式。  
捐赠人应当依法履行捐赠协议，按照捐赠协议约定的期限和方式将捐赠财产转移给受赠人。

**Article 12** A donor may enter into a donation agreement with a donee regarding the type, quality, quantity, and use of the donated property. The donor shall have the right to decide the quantity, use, and method of the donation. The donor shall perform the donation agreement according to law and transfer the donated property to the donee within the time limit and in the manner agreed upon in the donation agreement.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　捐赠人捐赠财产兴建公益事业工程项目，应当与受赠人订立捐赠协议，对工程项目的资金、建设、管理和使用作出约定。  
捐赠的公益事业工程项目由受赠单位按照国家有关规定办理项目审批手续，并组织施工或者由受赠人和捐赠人共同组织施工。工程质量应当符合国家质量标准。  
捐赠的公益事业工程项目竣工后，受赠单位应当将工程建设、建设资金的使用和工程质量验收情况向捐赠人通报。

**Article 13** Where a donor donates property for the construction of a public welfare project, it shall enter into a donation agreement with the donee, stipulating the funding, construction, management, and use of the project. The public welfare project constructed with the donation shall have its project approval procedures handled by the donee unit in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, and the construction shall be organized by the donee unit or jointly by the donee and the donor. The project quality shall conform to the national quality standards. Upon completion of the public welfare project constructed with the donation, the donee unit shall report to the donor on the project construction, use of construction funds, and the acceptance of project quality.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　捐赠人对于捐赠的公益事业工程项目可以留名纪念；捐赠人单独捐赠的工程项目或者主要由捐赠人出资兴建的工程项目，可以由捐赠人提出工程项目的名称，报县级以上人民政府批准。

**Article 14** A donor may leave its name on the public welfare project constructed with its donation as a commemoration; for a project donated solely by a donor or funded mainly by a donor, the donor may propose the name of the project, which shall be submitted to the people's government at or above the county level for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　境外捐赠人捐赠的财产，由受赠人按照国家有关规定办理入境手续；捐赠实行许可证管理的物品，由受赠人按照国家有关规定办理许可证申领手续，海关凭许可证验放、监管。  
华侨向境内捐赠的，县级以上人民政府侨务部门可以协助办理有关入境手续，为捐赠人实施捐赠项目提供帮助。

**Article 15** For property donated by an overseas donor, the donee shall handle the import procedures in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State; for donated articles subject to licensing control, the donee shall handle the procedures for obtaining the license in accordance with the regulations of the State, and the Customs shall inspect, release, and supervise such articles against the license.  
Where an overseas Chinese makes a donation into the territory, the overseas Chinese affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level may assist in handling the relevant import procedures and provide assistance to the donor in implementing the donation project.

### 第三章　捐赠财产的使用和管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　受赠人接受捐赠后，应当向捐赠人出具合法、有效的收据，将受赠财产登记造册，妥善保管。

**Article 16** After accepting a donation, the donee shall issue a lawful and valid receipt to the donor, register the donated property, maintain an inventory, and keep it in proper custody.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公益性社会团体应当将受赠财产用于资助符合其宗旨的活动和事业。对于接受的救助灾害的捐赠财产，应当及时用于救助活动。基金会每年用于资助公益事业的资金数额，不得低于国家规定的比例。  
公益性社会团体应当严格遵守国家的有关规定，按照合法、安全、有效的原则，积极实现捐赠财产的保值增值。  
公益性非营利的事业单位应当将受赠财产用于发展本单位的公益事业，不得挪作他用。  
对于不易储存、运输和超过实际需要的受赠财产，受赠人可以变卖，所取得的全部收入，应当用于捐赠目的。

**Article 17** A public welfare social organization shall use the donated property to support activities and undertakings consistent with its purposes. Donated property received for disaster relief shall be used in a timely manner for relief activities. The annual amount of funds used by a foundation to support public welfare undertakings shall not be lower than the proportion prescribed by the State. A public welfare social organization shall strictly abide by the relevant regulations of the State and actively preserve and increase the value of the donated property based on the principles of lawfulness, safety, and effectiveness. A public welfare non-profit institutional unit shall use the donated property for developing its own public welfare undertakings and shall not misappropriate it for other purposes. Where the donated property is difficult to store or transport or exceeds actual needs, the donee may sell it, and all proceeds derived therefrom shall be used for the purposes of the donation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　受赠人与捐赠人订立了捐赠协议的，应当按照协议约定的用途使用捐赠财产，不得擅自改变捐赠财产的用途。如果确需改变用途的，应当征得捐赠人的同意。

**Article 18** Where a donee has entered into a donation agreement with a donor, it shall use the donated property for the purposes agreed upon in the agreement and shall not change the use of the donated property without authorization. If a change of use is genuinely necessary, the consent of the donor shall be obtained.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　受赠人应当依照国家有关规定，建立健全财务会计制度和受赠财产的使用制度，加强对受赠财产的管理。

**Article 19** The donee shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, establish and improve a financial accounting system and a system for the use of donated property, and shall strengthen the management of the donated property.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　受赠人每年度应当向政府有关部门报告受赠财产的使用、管理情况，接受监督。必要时，政府有关部门可以对其财务进行审计。  
海关对减免关税的捐赠物品依法实施监督和管理。  
县级以上人民政府侨务部门可以参与对华侨向境内捐赠财产使用与管理的监督。

**Article 20** Each year, a donee shall report to the relevant government department on the use and management of the donated property and accept supervision. When necessary, the relevant government department may audit its financial affairs. The Customs shall supervise and administer donated articles granted duty reduction or exemption according to law. The overseas Chinese affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level may participate in the supervision of the use and management of property donated by overseas Chinese into the territory.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　捐赠人有权向受赠人查询捐赠财产的使用、管理情况，并提出意见和建议。对于捐赠人的查询，受赠人应当如实答复。

**Article 21** A donor shall have the right to inquire of the donee about the use and management of the donated property and to put forward opinions and suggestions. The donee shall reply truthfully to the donor's inquiries.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　受赠人应当公开接受捐赠的情况和受赠财产的使用、管理情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 22** A donee shall make public the acceptance of donations and the use and management of the donated property, and shall submit to public supervision.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公益性社会团体应当厉行节约，降低管理成本，工作人员的工资和办公费用从利息等收入中按照国家规定的标准开支。

**Article 23** A public welfare social organization shall practice strict economy and reduce management costs. The wages of the staff and office expenses shall be paid from interest and other income at the standards prescribed by the State.

### 第四章　优惠措施

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公司和其他企业依照本法的规定捐赠财产用于公益事业，依照法律、行政法规的规定享受企业所得税方面的优惠。

**Article 24** Companies and other enterprises that donate property for public welfare undertakings in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall enjoy preferential treatment in respect of enterprise income tax in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然人和个体工商户依照本法的规定捐赠财产用于公益事业，依照法律、行政法规的规定享受个人所得税方面的优惠。

**Article 25** Natural persons and individual industrial and commercial households that donate property for public welfare undertakings in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall enjoy preferential treatment in respect of individual income tax in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　境外向公益性社会团体和公益性非营利的事业单位捐赠的用于公益事业的物资，依照法律、行政法规的规定减征或者免征进口关税和进口环节的增值税。

**Article 26** For materials donated from abroad to public welfare social organizations and public welfare non-profit institutions for public welfare undertakings, import duties and value-added tax at the import stage shall be reduced or exempted in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对于捐赠的工程项目，当地人民政府应当给予支持和优惠。

**Article 27** For donated projects, the local people's government shall provide support and preferential treatment.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　受赠人未征得捐赠人的许可，擅自改变捐赠财产的性质、用途的，由县级以上人民政府有关部门责令改正，给予警告。拒不改正的，经征求捐赠人的意见，由县级以上人民政府将捐赠财产交由与其宗旨相同或者相似的公益性社会团体或者公益性非营利的事业单位管理。

**Article 28** If a donee, without obtaining the donor's consent, changes the nature or use of the donated property without authorization, the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and give a warning. If the donee refuses to make correction, after seeking the donor's opinion, the people's government at or above the county level shall transfer the donated property to a public welfare social organization or a public welfare non-profit institution whose purpose is the same as or similar to that of the donee for management.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　挪用、侵占或者贪污捐赠款物的，由县级以上人民政府有关部门责令退还所用、所得款物，并处以罚款；对直接责任人员，由所在单位依照有关规定予以处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
依照前款追回、追缴的捐赠款物，应当用于原捐赠目的和用途。

**Article 29** If anyone misappropriates, embezzles or corrupts donated money or property, the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the money or property so used or obtained and impose a fine; the unit to which the person directly responsible for such act belongs shall deal with the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.  
The donated money or property recovered or recovered in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be used for the original purposes and uses of the donation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在捐赠活动中，有下列行为之一的，依照法律、法规的有关规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）逃汇、骗购外汇的；  
（二）偷税、逃税的；  
（三）进行走私活动的；  
（四）未经海关许可并且未补缴应缴税额，擅自将减税、免税进口的捐赠物资在境内销售、转让或者移作他用的。

**Article 30** In donation activities, any of the following acts shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and regulations; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law:  
(1) evading foreign exchange or fraudulently purchasing foreign exchange;  
(2) evading taxes;  
(3) engaging in smuggling activities;  
(4) without the permission of the Customs and without paying the amount of tax payable, selling, transferring, or using for other purposes the donated materials imported with reduced or exempted tax duties within the territory without authorization.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　受赠单位的工作人员，滥用职权，玩忽职守，徇私舞弊，致使捐赠财产造成重大损失的，由所在单位依照有关规定予以处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Where staff members of a donee unit, by abusing their powers, neglecting their duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, cause significant losses to the donated property, the unit to which they belong shall deal with the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法自1999年9月1日起施行。

**Article 32** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 1999.
