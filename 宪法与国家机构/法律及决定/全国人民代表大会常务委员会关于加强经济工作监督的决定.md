# 全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于加强经济工作监督的决定

**分类**：有关法律问题和重大问题的决定（部分）  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-12-24  
**生效日期**：2021-12-24

> （2000年3月1日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议通过　2021年12月24日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十二次会议修订） 为更好地履行宪法和法律赋予全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的职责，贯彻落实党中央决策部署，进一步加强经济工作监督，切实增强监督实效，推动高质量发展，推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化，结合实践经验，作如下决定： 一、全国人民代表大会常务委员会依法对国务院经济工作行使监督职权。全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会在全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会领导下，承担有关具体工作。国务院及其有关部门应当做好协助和配合。 二、全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展经济工作监督，应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚持以人民为中心，坚持和完善社会主义基本经济制度，保障和促进市场在资源配置中起决定性作用和更好发挥政府作用，立足新发展阶段，贯彻新发展理念，构建新发展格局，推动高质量发展。 三、根据全国人民代表大会议事规则的有关规定，全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会应当在全国人民代表大会会议举行的四十五日前，会同有关专门委员会，对国民经济和社会发展年度计划进行初步审查，形成初步审查意见，送国务院有关主管部门。国务院有关主管部门应当将处理情况及时反馈财政经济委员会。 全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会开展初步审查阶段，有关专门委员会可以开展专项审查，提出专项审查意见，送财政经济委员会研究处理。 四、对国民经济和社会发展年度计划初步审查时，国务院有关主管部门应当提交以下材料： （一）关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况与本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的报告，其中应当报告上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划主要目标和任务完成情况、全国人民代表大会决议贯彻落实情况，对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划主要目标、工作任务及相应的主要政策、措施的编制依据和考虑作出说明和解释； （二）本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的初步方案； （三）关于上一年度中央预算内投资计划执行情况的说明和本年度中央预算内投资计划的安排； （四）初步审查所需要的其他材料。 五、对国民经济和社会发展年度计划初步审查的重点是：上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划完成情况，特别是主要目标和任务的完成情况；本年度国民经济和社会发展计划编制的指导思想应当符合党中央决策部署和中央经济工作会议精神，符合国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要；主要目标、重点任务和重大工程项目应当符合经济社会发展条件特别是资源、财力、环境实际支撑能力，符合五年规划纲要实施的基本要求，有利于经济社会长期健康发展；主要政策取向和措施安排应当符合完善体制机制和依法行政的要求，坚持目标导向和问题导向，针对性强且切实可行，财政政策、货币政策应当与主要目标相匹配。 六、全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会向全国人民代表大会主席团提出关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况和本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的审查结果报告。审查结果报告应当包括下列内容： （一）关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况的总体评价，需要关注的主要问题； （二）对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划报告和计划草案的可行性作出评价，对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行工作提出意见和建议；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、全国人民代表大会常务委员会依法对国务院经济工作行使监督职权。全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会在全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会领导下，承担有关具体工作。国务院及其有关部门应当做好协助和配合。

**Article 1** I. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall exercise its supervisory power over the economic work of the State Council according to law. The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress and relevant special committees shall, under the leadership of the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee, undertake relevant specific work. The State Council and its relevant departments shall provide assistance and cooperation.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展经济工作监督，应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚持以人民为中心，坚持和完善社会主义基本经济制度，保障和促进市场在资源配置中起决定性作用和更好发挥政府作用，立足新发展阶段，贯彻新发展理念，构建新发展格局，推动高质量发展。

**Article 2** II. In carrying out supervision over economic work, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, adhere to the people-centered development philosophy, uphold and improve the basic socialist economic system, ensure and promote the market to play a decisive role in resource allocation and better play the role of the government, base on the new stage of development, implement the new development philosophy, build a new development pattern, and promote high-quality development.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、根据全国人民代表大会议事规则的有关规定，全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会应当在全国人民代表大会会议举行的四十五日前，会同有关专门委员会，对国民经济和社会发展年度计划进行初步审查，形成初步审查意见，送国务院有关主管部门。国务院有关主管部门应当将处理情况及时反馈财政经济委员会。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会开展初步审查阶段，有关专门委员会可以开展专项审查，提出专项审查意见，送财政经济委员会研究处理。

**Article 3** III. Pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Rules of Procedure of the National People's Congress, the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress shall, forty-five days before the session of the National People's Congress, together with the relevant special committees, conduct a preliminary examination of the annual plan for national economic and social development, form preliminary examination opinions, and send them to the relevant competent department of the State Council. The relevant competent department of the State Council shall promptly provide feedback on the handling status to the Financial and Economic Committee.  
During the preliminary examination conducted by the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress, the relevant special committees may carry out special examinations, put forward special examination opinions, and send them to the Financial and Economic Committee for study and handling.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、对国民经济和社会发展年度计划初步审查时，国务院有关主管部门应当提交以下材料：  
（一）关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况与本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的报告，其中应当报告上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划主要目标和任务完成情况、全国人民代表大会决议贯彻落实情况，对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划主要目标、工作任务及相应的主要政策、措施的编制依据和考虑作出说明和解释；  
（二）本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的初步方案；  
（三）关于上一年度中央预算内投资计划执行情况的说明和本年度中央预算内投资计划的安排；  
（四）初步审查所需要的其他材料。

**Article 4** IV. When conducting the preliminary examination of the annual plan for national economic and social development, the relevant competent department of the State Council shall submit the following materials:  
(1) A report on the implementation of the previous year's plan for national economic and social development and the draft annual plan for national economic and social development for the current year, which shall include a report on the completion of the main targets and tasks of the previous year's plan, the implementation of the resolutions of the National People's Congress, and provide explanations and interpretations on the basis and considerations for formulating the main targets, work tasks, and corresponding major policies and measures of the current year's plan;  
(2) The preliminary plan of the draft annual plan for national economic and social development for the current year;  
(3) An explanation of the implementation of the central budget investment plan for the previous year and the arrangement of the central budget investment plan for the current year;  
(4) Other materials required for the preliminary examination.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、对国民经济和社会发展年度计划初步审查的重点是：上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划完成情况，特别是主要目标和任务的完成情况；本年度国民经济和社会发展计划编制的指导思想应当符合党中央决策部署和中央经济工作会议精神，符合国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要；主要目标、重点任务和重大工程项目应当符合经济社会发展条件特别是资源、财力、环境实际支撑能力，符合五年规划纲要实施的基本要求，有利于经济社会长期健康发展；主要政策取向和措施安排应当符合完善体制机制和依法行政的要求，坚持目标导向和问题导向，针对性强且切实可行，财政政策、货币政策应当与主要目标相匹配。

**Article 5** V. The key points of the preliminary examination of the annual plan for national economic and social development are: the completion status of the previous year's plan for national economic and social development, especially the completion of the main targets and tasks; the guiding ideology for the formulation of the current year's plan for national economic and social development shall conform to the decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee and the spirit of the Central Economic Work Conference, and comply with the outline of the five-year plan for national economic and social development and the medium- and long-term plan outline; the main targets, key tasks, and major engineering projects shall conform to the conditions of economic and social development, especially the actual supporting capacity of resources, financial resources, and the environment, meet the basic requirements for implementing the five-year plan outline, and be conducive to long-term healthy economic and social development; the main policy direction and measures arrangements shall conform to the requirements of improving institutional mechanisms and law-based administration, adhere to goal orientation and problem orientation, be targeted and feasible, and fiscal and monetary policies shall match the main targets.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会向全国人民代表大会主席团提出关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况和本年度国民经济和社会发展计划草案的审查结果报告。审查结果报告应当包括下列内容：  
（一）关于上一年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况的总体评价，需要关注的主要问题；  
（二）对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划报告和计划草案的可行性作出评价，对本年度国民经济和社会发展计划执行工作提出意见和建议；  
（三）对全国人民代表大会会议批准国民经济和社会发展年度计划报告和计划草案提出建议。

**Article 6** VI. The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress shall submit to the Presidium of the National People's Congress a report on the results of the examination of the implementation of the previous year's plan for national economic and social development and the draft annual plan for national economic and social development for the current year. The report on the results of the examination shall include the following contents:  
(1) An overall evaluation of the implementation of the previous year's plan for national economic and social development and the main issues requiring attention;  
(2) An evaluation of the feasibility of the report and the draft plan for national economic and social development for the current year, and putting forward opinions and suggestions on the implementation of the current year's plan for national economic and social development;  
(3) Proposing suggestions for the National People's Congress to approve the annual plan report and the draft plan for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、全国人民代表大会常务委员会应当加强对全国人民代表大会批准的国民经济和社会发展年度计划执行的监督。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会应当在每年八月听取和审议国务院关于本年度上一阶段国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况的报告。常务委员会组成人员的审议意见交由国务院研究处理，国务院应当将研究处理情况向常务委员会提出书面报告。国民经济和社会发展年度计划执行情况的报告、常务委员会组成人员的审议意见和国务院对审议意见的研究处理情况，向全国人民代表大会代表通报并向社会公布。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会结合上半年经济形势分析做好相关准备工作，向常务委员会提出分析报告。

**Article 7** VII. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen the supervision over the implementation of the annual plan for national economic and social development approved by the National People's Congress.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, in August each year, hear and deliberate the report of the State Council on the implementation of the plan for national economic and social development for the preceding period of the current year. The deliberation opinions of the members of the Standing Committee shall be submitted to the State Council for study and handling, and the State Council shall submit a written report to the Standing Committee on the status of study and handling. The report on the implementation of the annual plan for national economic and social development, the deliberation opinions of the members of the Standing Committee, and the State Council's study and handling of the deliberation opinions shall be notified to the deputies to the National People's Congress and published to the public.  
The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress shall, in conjunction with the analysis of the economic situation in the first half of the year, make relevant preparations and submit an analysis report to the Standing Committee.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、对国民经济和社会发展年度计划执行监督的重点是：国民经济和社会发展年度计划执行应当贯彻党中央决策部署和中央经济工作会议精神，落实全国人民代表大会决议要求，符合政府工作报告中提出的各项目标和任务要求；主要目标特别是约束性指标完成情况、重点任务和重大工程项目进展情况应当符合国民经济和社会发展年度计划进度安排；国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况的报告应当深入分析存在的主要困难和问题及其原因，对未达到预期进度的指标和任务应当作出说明和解释，提出具有针对性且切实可行的政策措施，推动国民经济和社会发展年度计划顺利完成。

**Article 8** VIII. The key points of supervision over the implementation of the annual plan for national economic and social development are: the implementation of the annual plan for national economic and social development shall implement the decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee and the spirit of the Central Economic Work Conference, implement the requirements of the resolutions of the National People's Congress, and conform to the requirements of various goals and tasks set forth in the government work report; the completion of main targets, especially binding targets, and the progress of key tasks and major engineering projects shall conform to the schedule of the annual plan for national economic and social development; the report on the implementation of the plan for national economic and social development shall conduct in-depth analysis of the main difficulties and problems and their causes, provide explanations and interpretations for indicators and tasks that have not reached the expected progress, and propose targeted and feasible policy measures to promote the smooth completion of the annual plan for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会在每年四月、七月和十月中旬分别召开季度经济形势分析会议，听取国务院有关部门关于一季度、上半年、前三季度国民经济运行情况的汇报，进行分析研究，将会议对国民经济运行情况的分析和提出的意见建议向委员长会议报告，并以会议纪要和简报形式发送国务院办公厅及有关部门，各省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会办公厅。

**Article 9** IX. The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress shall hold quarterly economic situation analysis meetings in mid-April, July, and October each year, to hear reports from the relevant departments of the State Council on the operation of the national economy in the first quarter, the first half year, and the first three quarters, conduct analysis and research, report the analysis of the operation of the national economy and the opinions and suggestions raised at the meetings to the Council of Chairmen, and send them to the General Office of the State Council and relevant departments, as well as the general offices of the standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the form of meeting minutes and briefs.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案的初步审查和审查，参照本决定第三条、第六条的规定执行。  
五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案提请全国人民代表大会审查批准的前一年，全国人民代表大会常务委员会围绕五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要编制工作开展专题调研，听取调研工作情况的报告，并将调研报告送有关方面研究参考，为全国人民代表大会审查批准做好准备工作。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会办公厅和财政经济委员会承担具体组织工作，拟定调研工作方案，协调有关专门委员会和常务委员会工作机构开展专题调研，汇总集成调研成果。

**Article 10** X. The preliminary examination and examination of the draft outline of the five-year plan for national economic and social development and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan shall be carried out with reference to the provisions of Articles 3 and 6 of this Decision.  
In the year before the draft outline of the five-year plan and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan are submitted to the National People's Congress for examination and approval, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall conduct special investigations on the preparation of the five-year plan outline and the medium- and long-term plan outline, hear reports on the investigation work, and send the investigation report to relevant parties for study and reference, so as to make preparations for the examination and approval by the National People's Congress.  
The General Office of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the Financial and Economic Committee shall undertake specific organizational work, formulate investigation work plans, coordinate relevant special committees and working bodies of the Standing Committee to conduct special investigations, and integrate the investigation results.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、对五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案初步审查时，国务院有关主管部门应当提交以下材料：  
（一）五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案；  
（二）关于五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案及其编制情况的说明，其中应当对上一个五年规划纲要主要目标和任务完成情况、全国人民代表大会决议贯彻落实情况、本五年规划纲要主要目标和重点任务的编制依据和考虑等作出说明和解释；  
（三）关于重大工程项目的安排；  
（四）初步审查所需要的其他材料。

**Article 11** XI. When conducting the preliminary examination of the draft outline of the five-year plan and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan, the relevant competent department of the State Council shall submit the following materials:  
(1) The draft outline of the five-year plan and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan;  
(2) An explanation of the draft outline of the five-year plan and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan and their preparation, which shall include explanations and interpretations on the completion of the main targets and tasks of the previous five-year plan outline, the implementation of the resolutions of the National People's Congress, and the basis and considerations for the formulation of the main targets and key tasks of this five-year plan outline;  
(3) The arrangement of major engineering projects;  
(4) Other materials required for the preliminary examination.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、对五年规划纲要和中长期规划纲要草案初步审查的重点是：上一个五年规划纲要实施情况；本五年规划纲要编制的指导思想应当符合党中央关于五年规划的建议精神，能够发挥未来五年发展蓝图和行动纲领的作用；主要目标、重点任务和重大工程项目应当符合我国国情和发展阶段，符合经济社会发展的客观规律，符合国家中长期发展战略目标，兼顾必要性与可行性；主要政策取向应当符合党的基本理论、基本路线、基本方略，针对性强且切实可行。

**Article 12** XII. The key points of the preliminary examination of the draft outline of the five-year plan and the draft outline of the medium- and long-term plan are: the implementation of the previous five-year plan outline; the guiding ideology for the formulation of this five-year plan outline shall conform to the spirit of the Party Central Committee's proposals on the five-year plan, and be able to play the role of a blueprint and action program for the next five years; the main targets, key tasks, and major engineering projects shall conform to China's national conditions and development stage, conform to the objective laws of economic and social development, conform to the national medium- and long-term strategic development goals, and take into account both necessity and feasibility; the main policy direction shall conform to the Party's basic theory, basic line, and basic strategy, be targeted and feasible.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、国务院应当加强对五年规划纲要实施情况的动态监测、中期评估和总结评估。全国人民代表大会常务委员会应当加强对五年规划纲要实施的监督。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会在全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会领导下，有针对性地做好五年规划纲要实施的监督工作，推动五年规划纲要顺利实施。  
国务院有关主管部门应当将五年规划纲要实施情况的动态监测材料送全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会。

**Article 13** XIII. The State Council shall strengthen the dynamic monitoring, mid-term evaluation, and final evaluation of the implementation of the five-year plan outline. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen the supervision over the implementation of the five-year plan outline.  
The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress and the relevant special committees shall, under the leadership of the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee, carry out targeted supervision over the implementation of the five-year plan outline and promote the smooth implementation of the five-year plan outline.  
The relevant competent department of the State Council shall submit the dynamic monitoring materials on the implementation of the five-year plan outline to the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、五年规划纲要实施的中期阶段，国务院应当将五年规划纲要实施情况的中期评估报告提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会审议。常务委员会组成人员的审议意见交由国务院研究处理，国务院应当将研究处理情况向常务委员会提出书面报告。五年规划纲要实施情况的中期评估报告、常务委员会组成人员的审议意见和国务院对审议意见的研究处理情况，向全国人民代表大会代表通报并向社会公布。财政经济委员会会同有关专门委员会开展专题调研，向常务委员会提出调研报告。  
对五年规划纲要实施情况中期评估的监督重点是：五年规划纲要实施应当符合党中央的建议精神，贯彻落实全国人民代表大会决议要求；主要目标特别是约束性指标完成情况、重点任务和重大工程项目进展情况应当符合五年规划纲要进度安排；五年规划纲要实施情况的中期评估报告应当深入分析存在的主要困难和问题及其原因，对未达到预期进度的指标和任务应当作出解释和说明，提出有针对性且切实可行的政策措施，推动五年规划纲要顺利完成。

**Article 14** XIV. During the mid-term stage of the implementation of the five-year plan outline, the State Council shall submit the mid-term evaluation report on the implementation of the five-year plan outline to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for deliberation. The deliberation opinions of the members of the Standing Committee shall be submitted to the State Council for study and handling, and the State Council shall submit a written report to the Standing Committee on the status of study and handling. The mid-term evaluation report on the implementation of the five-year plan outline, the deliberation opinions of the members of the Standing Committee, and the State Council's study and handling of the deliberation opinions shall be notified to the deputies to the National People's Congress and published to the public. The Financial and Economic Committee shall, together with the relevant special committees, conduct special investigations and submit an investigation report to the Standing Committee.  
The key points of supervision over the mid-term evaluation of the implementation of the five-year plan outline are: the implementation of the five-year plan outline shall conform to the spirit of the Party Central Committee's proposals, implement the requirements of the resolutions of the National People's Congress; the completion of main targets, especially binding targets, and the progress of key tasks and major engineering projects shall conform to the schedule of the five-year plan outline; the mid-term evaluation report on the implementation of the five-year plan outline shall conduct in-depth analysis of the main difficulties and problems and their causes, provide explanations and interpretations for indicators and tasks that have not reached the expected progress, and propose targeted and feasible policy measures to promote the smooth completion of the five-year plan outline.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、国务院应当对上一个五年规划纲要实施情况进行总结评估，形成总结评估报告，与提请全国人民代表大会审查批准的五年规划纲要草案一并印发全国人民代表大会会议。五年规划纲要的总结评估报告应当包括下列内容：  
（一）主要指标完成情况；  
（二）重点任务落实情况；  
（三）重大工程项目实施情况；  
（四）存在的主要困难和问题；  
（五）相关意见建议。

**Article 15** XV. The State Council shall conduct a final evaluation of the implementation of the previous five-year plan outline, form a final evaluation report, and print and distribute it together with the draft five-year plan outline submitted to the National People's Congress for examination and approval at the session of the National People's Congress. The final evaluation report on the five-year plan outline shall include the following contents:  
(1) Completion of main indicators;  
(2) Implementation of key tasks;  
(3) Implementation of major engineering projects;  
(4) Main difficulties and problems existing;  
(5) Relevant opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-16"></a>十六、经全国人民代表大会批准的国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要在执行过程中，出现下列情况之一的，可以进行调整：  
（一）因国内外经济形势发生重大变化导致宏观调控政策取向和主要目标、重点任务等必须作出重大调整的；  
（二）国家发生特别重大自然灾害、全局性的重大公共安全事件或者进入紧急状态等导致国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要无法正常执行或者完成的；  
（三）其他特殊情况导致国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要无法正常执行或者完成的。

**Article 16** XVI. During the implementation of the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline approved by the National People's Congress, if any of the following circumstances occurs, adjustments may be made:  
(1) Where major changes in the domestic and international economic situation require a major adjustment in the orientation of macro-control policies and main targets and key tasks;  
(2) Where the occurrence of particularly major natural disasters, major public security incidents of a global nature, or a state of emergency leads to the inability to normally implement or complete the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline;  
(3) Other special circumstances that lead to the inability to normally implement or complete the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline.

<a id="art-17"></a>十七、国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要经全国人民代表大会批准后，在执行过程中需要作部分调整的，国务院应当将调整方案提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。国民经济和社会发展年度计划调整方案的提出一般不迟于当年第三季度末；五年规划纲要调整方案的提出一般不迟于其实施的第四年第二季度末。除特殊情况外，国务院应当在全国人民代表大会常务委员会会议举行的三十日前，将调整方案报送常务委员会。  
除特殊情况外，国务院有关主管部门应当在全国人民代表大会常务委员会会议举行的四十五日前，将国务院的调整方案送交全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会，由财政经济委员会进行初步审查，并向常务委员会提出审查结果报告。  
经全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准的国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要调整方案，应当向全国人民代表大会下次会议报告。

**Article 17** XVII. After the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline are approved by the National People's Congress, if partial adjustments are needed during implementation, the State Council shall submit the adjustment plan to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for examination and approval. The submission of the adjustment plan for the annual plan for national economic and social development shall generally not be later than the end of the third quarter of the current year; the submission of the adjustment plan for the five-year plan outline shall generally not be later than the end of the second quarter of the fourth year of its implementation. Except under special circumstances, the State Council shall submit the adjustment plan to the Standing Committee thirty days before the meeting of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
Except under special circumstances, the relevant competent department of the State Council shall, forty-five days before the meeting of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, submit the State Council's adjustment plan to the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress, which shall conduct a preliminary examination and submit a report on the results of the examination to the Standing Committee.  
The adjustment plan for the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline approved by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall be reported to the next session of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-18"></a>十八、全国人民代表大会常务委员会围绕党和国家经济工作中心和全局依法加强监督，重点关注深化经济体制改革、优化营商环境、加强科技创新、推动区域协调发展、坚持绿色低碳发展、保障和改善民生、促进共同富裕、推进高水平对外开放、维护国家经济安全等方面工作落实情况，必要时可以听取和审议国务院专项工作报告、开展专题询问或者作出决议。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会、有关专门委员会和常务委员会有关工作机构在常务委员会领导下做好相关工作，督促国务院有关部门更好地推进落实工作。

**Article 18** XVIII. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen supervision according to law around the central economic work and overall situation of the Party and the state, focusing on the implementation of work in such areas as deepening economic system reform, optimizing the business environment, strengthening scientific and technological innovation, promoting coordinated regional development, adhering to green and low-carbon development, ensuring and improving people's livelihood, promoting common prosperity, advancing high-level opening up, and safeguarding national economic security. Where necessary, it may hear and deliberate special work reports of the State Council, conduct special inquiries, or adopt resolutions.  
The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress, relevant special committees, and relevant working bodies of the Standing Committee shall, under the leadership of the Standing Committee, do a good job in relevant work and urge the relevant departments of the State Council to better advance the implementation of work.

<a id="art-19"></a>十九、国务院对事关国民经济和社会发展全局、涉及人民群众切身利益的重大决策，依法在出台前向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告。  
出现下列情况之一的，国务院或者国务院有关部门应当向全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会报告，作出说明：  
（一）因国际经济形势或者国内经济运行发生重大变化需要对宏观调控政策取向作出重大调整；  
（二）涉及国计民生、国家经济安全、人民群众切身利益的重大经济体制改革或者对外开放方案出台前；  
（三）重大自然灾害或者给国家财产、集体财产、人民群众生命财产造成严重损失的重大事件发生后；  
（四）其他有必要向全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会报告的重大经济事项。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会认为必要时，可以依法作出决定决议，也可以将讨论中的意见建议转送国务院及其有关部门研究处理。

**Article 19** XIX. The State Council shall, according to law, report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress before the introduction of major decisions that concern the overall situation of national economic and social development and involve the vital interests of the people.  
If any of the following circumstances occurs, the State Council or its relevant departments shall report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or to the Financial and Economic Committee and relevant special committees and provide explanations:  
(1) Where major changes in the international economic situation or domestic economic operation require a major adjustment in the orientation of macro-control policies;  
(2) Before the introduction of major economic system reforms or opening-up plans that concern the national economy and people's livelihood, national economic security, and the vital interests of the people;  
(3) After the occurrence of a major natural disaster or a major event that causes serious losses to state property, collective property, or the lives and property of the people;  
(4) Other major economic matters that need to be reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or to the Financial and Economic Committee and relevant special committees.  
Where the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress deems it necessary, it may make decisions or resolutions according to law, or may transfer the opinions and suggestions under discussion to the State Council and its relevant departments for study and handling.

<a id="art-20"></a>二十、对涉及面广、影响深远、投资巨大的国家特别重大建设项目，国务院可以向全国人民代表大会或者常务委员会提出议案，由全国人民代表大会或者常务委员会审议并作出决定。  
根据全国人民代表大会或者常务委员会安排，财政经济委员会会同有关专门委员会对前款所述议案进行初步审查，并向全国人民代表大会或者常务委员会提出审查报告。

**Article 20** XX. For particularly major national construction projects that are wide-ranging, far-reaching, and involve huge investment, the State Council may submit a bill to the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee, which shall be deliberated and decided by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee.  
Pursuant to the arrangements of the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee, the Financial and Economic Committee shall, together with the relevant special committees, conduct a preliminary examination of the bill mentioned in the preceding paragraph and submit an examination report to the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee.

<a id="art-21"></a>二十一、全国人民代表大会常务委员会对国民经济和社会发展年度计划、五年规划纲要确定的重大工程项目和本决定第二十条所述的国家特别重大建设项目等，根据需要听取国务院的工作汇报，进行审议，认为必要时可以作出决议。  
根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会安排，财政经济委员会会同有关专门委员会和常务委员会有关工作机构，可以对前款所述项目的实施情况开展专题调研，向常务委员会提出专题调研报告。  
国务院有关主管部门应当每半年向全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会提供中央预算内投资计划实施情况的有关材料。

**Article 21** XXI. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, as necessary, hear work reports from the State Council and deliberate on major engineering projects determined in the annual plan for national economic and social development and the five-year plan outline, and the particularly major national construction projects mentioned in Article 20 of this Decision. Where it deems necessary, it may adopt resolutions.  
Pursuant to the arrangements of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Financial and Economic Committee may, together with the relevant special committees and relevant working bodies of the Standing Committee, conduct special investigations on the implementation of the projects mentioned in the preceding paragraph and submit special investigation reports to the Standing Committee.  
The relevant competent department of the State Council shall, every half year, provide the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress with relevant materials on the implementation of the central budget investment plan.

<a id="art-22"></a>二十二、全国人民代表大会常务委员会应当加强对金融工作的监督。国务院应当在每年十月向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告下列情况：  
（一）货币政策执行情况；  
（二）金融业运行情况和监督管理工作情况；  
（三）金融支持实体经济情况；  
（四）金融体系改革和对外开放情况；  
（五）防范化解金融风险隐患情况。  
国务院有关主管部门应当及时向全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会提供月度、季度和年度金融运行数据和相关材料，配合支持跟踪监督工作。

**Article 22** XXII. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen supervision over financial work. The State Council shall, in October each year, report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the following:  
(1) Implementation of monetary policy;  
(2) Operation of the financial industry and the situation of supervision and management;  
(3) Financial support for the real economy;  
(4) Reform and opening up of the financial system;  
(5) Prevention and resolution of financial risks and hidden dangers.  
The relevant competent department of the State Council shall promptly provide the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress with monthly, quarterly, and annual financial operation data and relevant materials, and cooperate and support tracking supervision.

<a id="art-23"></a>二十三、同外国或者国际组织缔结有关经济方面的条约和协定，凡根据缔结条约程序法的规定，应当由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入的，由国务院向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出议案，提请审议决定。按照常务委员会议事规则有关规定，可以将议案交由财政经济委员会提出审议意见。  
上述条约和协定的修改、废除或者退出，比照其缔结的程序办理。

**Article 23** XXIII. For treaties and agreements on economic matters concluded with foreign countries or international organizations, where, pursuant to the provisions of the Law on the Procedure for the Conclusion of Treaties, they shall be subject to the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for ratification or accession, the State Council shall submit a bill to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for deliberation and decision. In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Rules of Procedure of the Standing Committee, the bill may be referred to the Financial and Economic Committee for deliberation opinions.  
The amendment, denunciation, or withdrawal from the treaties and agreements mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be handled by reference to the procedures for their conclusion.

<a id="art-24"></a>二十四、全国人民代表大会常务委员会通过听取和审议专项工作报告、执法检查、询问和质询、特定问题调查、专题调研等方式，加强对国务院及其有关部门经济工作的监督。  
根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会安排，财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会可以召开会议，听取国务院有关部门的专题汇报。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会，财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会可以运用审计监督、财会监督和统计监督成果，聘请研究机构和专家学者，委托第三方评估，利用大数据技术等，提高经济工作监督效能。

**Article 24** XXIV. The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall strengthen the supervision over the economic work of the State Council and its relevant departments by means of hearing and deliberating special work reports, law enforcement inspections, inquiries and interpellations, investigations of specific issues, special investigations, etc.  
Pursuant to the arrangements of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Financial and Economic Committee and the relevant special committees may hold meetings to hear special reports from the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Financial and Economic Committee, and the relevant special committees may use the results of audit supervision, financial and accounting supervision, and statistical supervision, engage research institutions and experts and scholars, entrust third-party evaluations, and use big data technologies to improve the effectiveness of economic work supervision.

<a id="art-25"></a>二十五、对全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会在经济工作监督中作出的决议、决定和审议意见等，常务委员会应当加强跟踪监督，督促国务院及其有关部门贯彻执行决议和决定，认真研究处理意见和建议并及时反馈。常务委员会认为必要时，可以就有关情况听取和审议国务院的专项工作报告。国务院应当在规定期限内，将决定决议的执行情况或者审议意见的研究处理情况向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告。财政经济委员会承担跟踪监督的具体工作。  
对不执行决定决议或者执行决定决议不力造成严重后果的，全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以通过专题询问、质询、特定问题调查等方式加强监督。

**Article 25** XXV. With respect to the resolutions, decisions, and deliberation opinions made by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee in the supervision over economic work, the Standing Committee shall strengthen follow-up supervision, urge the State Council and its relevant departments to implement the resolutions and decisions, carefully study and handle opinions and suggestions, and provide timely feedback. Where the Standing Committee deems it necessary, it may hear and deliberate the special work report of the State Council on relevant matters. The State Council shall, within the prescribed period, report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the implementation of the decisions and resolutions or the study and handling of deliberation opinions. The Financial and Economic Committee shall undertake the specific work of follow-up supervision.  
Where a decision or resolution is not implemented or is implemented ineffectively, resulting in serious consequences, the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee may strengthen supervision through means such as special inquiries, interpellations, and investigations of specific issues.

<a id="art-26"></a>二十六、全国人民代表大会常务委员会行使经济工作监督职权的情况，应当向全国人民代表大会报告，接受监督。财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会提出的意见和建议，应当报告委员长会议，由委员长会议决定是否批转国务院及其有关部门研究处理，并将结果报告全国人民代表大会常务委员会。

**Article 26** XXVI. The situation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress in exercising its powers of supervision over economic work shall be reported to the National People's Congress and subject to supervision. The opinions and suggestions put forward by the Financial and Economic Committee and the relevant special committees shall be reported to the Council of Chairmen, which shall decide whether to forward them to the State Council and its relevant departments for study and handling, and shall report the results to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-27"></a>二十七、全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展经济工作监督，应当充分发挥全国人民代表大会代表的作用，认真听取代表意见建议，主动回应代表关切，支持代表依法履职。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会应当建立健全经济工作监督联系代表工作机制。确定监督项目、开展监督工作，应当认真听取全国人民代表大会代表的意见建议。财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会围绕代表议案建议提出的、代表普遍关注的经济社会发展工作中的突出问题，组织开展专题调研。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会对国民经济和社会发展年度计划和五年规划纲要草案进行初步审查时，应当邀请全国人民代表大会代表参加。本决定所列其他事项的监督工作，可以根据需要邀请有关方面的全国人民代表大会代表参加。  
开展经济工作监督的有关情况应当通过代表工作机构及时向全国人民代表大会代表通报，有关材料应当及时发送全国人民代表大会代表。

**Article 27** XXVII. In carrying out supervision over economic work, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall give full play to the role of the deputies to the National People's Congress, earnestly listen to their opinions and suggestions, actively respond to their concerns, and support them in performing their duties according to law.  
The Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress and the relevant special committees shall establish and improve the working mechanism for contacting deputies regarding supervision over economic work. When determining supervision projects and carrying out supervision work, they shall earnestly listen to the opinions and suggestions of the deputies to the National People's Congress. The Financial and Economic Committee and the relevant special committees shall, focusing on the prominent issues in economic and social development raised in deputies' proposals and suggestions and of general concern to deputies, organize and carry out special investigations.  
When the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress conducts a preliminary examination of the annual plan for national economic and social development and the draft outline of the five-year plan, it shall invite deputies to the National People's Congress to participate. For supervision work on other matters listed in this Decision, deputies to the National People's Congress from relevant fields may be invited to participate as needed.  
The relevant situation of carrying out supervision over economic work shall be promptly notified to the deputies to the National People's Congress through the deputies' working body, and relevant materials shall be promptly sent to the deputies to the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-28"></a>二十八、全国人民代表大会常务委员会听取和审议、讨论本决定所列事项时，国务院及其有关部门应当根据要求，及时提供相关的信息资料和情况说明，并派国务院负责人或者有关部门负责人到会汇报情况，听取意见，回答询问。  
全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会听取和审议、讨论本决定所列事项时，国务院有关部门应当根据要求，及时提供相关的信息资料和情况说明，并派本部门有关负责人到会汇报情况，听取意见，回答询问。  
国务院有关部门根据全国人民代表大会财政经济委员会和有关专门委员会的要求，利用国家电子政务网等方式，定期提供国民经济和社会发展数据和相关材料。

**Article 28** XXVIII. When the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress hears, deliberates, and discusses the matters listed in this Decision, the State Council and its relevant departments shall, as required, promptly provide relevant information, data, and explanations of the situation, and send a responsible person from the State Council or the relevant department to report the situation at the meeting, listen to opinions, and answer inquiries.  
When the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress and the relevant special committees hear, deliberate, and discuss the matters listed in this Decision, the relevant departments of the State Council shall, as required, promptly provide relevant information, data, and explanations of the situation, and send the relevant responsible person from the department to report the situation at the meeting, listen to opinions, and answer inquiries.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the requirements of the Financial and Economic Committee of the National People's Congress and the relevant special committees, use the national e-government network and other means to regularly provide data and relevant materials on national economic and social development.

<a id="art-29"></a>二十九、全国人民代表大会常务委员会开展经济工作监督的情况，除法律另有规定外，向社会公开。  
本决定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 29** XXIX. The situation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress in carrying out supervision over economic work shall be made public to the society, except as otherwise provided by law.  
This Decision shall come into force on the date of its promulgation.
