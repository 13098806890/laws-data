# 中国人民解放军选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表的办法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-29  
**生效日期**：2021-04-30

> （1981年6月10日第五届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过　1996年10月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议修订　根据2012年6月30日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十七次会议《关于修改〈中国人民解放军选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表的办法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2021年4月29日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议《关于修改〈中国人民解放军选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表的办法〉的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　选举委员会 第三章　代表名额的决定和分配 第四章　选区和选举单位 第五章　代表候选人的提出 第六章　选举程序 第七章　对代表的监督和罢免、辞职、补选 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会选举法》的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　人民解放军军人和参加军队选举的其他人员依照本办法选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表。 第三条　人民解放军及人民解放军团级以上单位设立选举委员会。 人民解放军选举委员会领导全军的选举工作，其他各级选举委员会主持本单位的选举工作。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会选举法》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Constitution of the People's Republic of China and the Electoral Law of the National People's Congress and Local People's Congresses of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民解放军军人和参加军队选举的其他人员依照本办法选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表。

**Article 2** Servicemen and women of the People's Liberation Army and other personnel participating in military elections shall, in accordance with these Measures, elect deputies to the National People's Congress and to the local people's congresses at or above the county level.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民解放军及人民解放军团级以上单位设立选举委员会。  
人民解放军选举委员会领导全军的选举工作，其他各级选举委员会主持本单位的选举工作。

**Article 3** The People's Liberation Army and units at or above the regiment level of the People's Liberation Army shall establish election committees.  
The Election Committee of the People's Liberation Army shall lead the election work of the entire army, and election committees at other levels shall preside over the election work of their respective units.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　连和其他基层单位的军人委员会，主持本单位的选举工作。

**Article 4** The servicemen committees of companies and other grassroots units shall preside over the election work of their respective units.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民解放军军人、文职人员，军队管理的离休、退休人员和其他人员，参加军队选举。  
驻军的驻地距离当地居民的居住地较远，随军家属参加地方选举有困难的，经选举委员会或者军人委员会批准，可以参加军队选举。

**Article 5** Servicemen and women of the People's Liberation Army, civilian staff, retired and demobilized personnel managed by the military, and other personnel shall participate in military elections.  
If the garrison is located far from the residential areas of local residents and it is difficult for family members accompanying the military to participate in local elections, they may, with the approval of the election committee or the servicemen committee, participate in military elections.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　驻地方工厂、铁路、水运、科研等单位的军代表，在地方院校学习的军队人员，可以参加地方选举。

**Article 6** Military representatives stationed in local factories, railways, water transport, scientific research units, and military personnel studying in local colleges and universities may participate in local elections.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[本办法第五条](#art-5)所列人员，凡年满十八周岁，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、家庭出身、宗教信仰、教育程度、财产状况、居住期限，都具有选民资格，享有选举权和被选举权。  
依照法律被剥夺政治权利的人没有选举权和被选举权。  
精神病患者不能行使选举权利的，经选举委员会确认，不参加选举。

**Article 7** Any person listed in Article 5 of these Measures who has reached the age of 18 shall have the qualifications of a voter and enjoy the right to vote and stand for election, regardless of ethnicity, race, gender, occupation, family background, religious belief, education level, property status, or length of residence.  
Persons who have been deprived of political rights according to law shall not have the right to vote or stand for election.  
A person with mental illness who is unable to exercise the right to vote shall, upon confirmation by the election committee, not participate in the election.

### 第二章　选举委员会

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民解放军选举委员会的组成人员，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准。其他各级选举委员会的组成人员，由上一级选举委员会批准。  
下级选举委员会受上级选举委员会的领导。  
选举委员会任期五年，行使职权至新的选举委员会产生为止。选举委员会的组成人员调离本单位或者免职、退役的，其在选举委员会中担任的职务自行终止；因职务调整或者其他原因不宜继续在选举委员会中担任职务的，应当免除其在选举委员会中担任的职务。选举委员会的组成人员出缺时，应当及时增补。

**Article 8** The members of the Election Committee of the People's Liberation Army shall be approved by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. The members of election committees at other levels shall be approved by the election committee at the next higher level.  
A lower-level election committee shall be under the leadership of the higher-level election committee.  
The term of office of an election committee shall be five years, and it shall exercise its powers until a new election committee is formed. If a member of an election committee is transferred away from the unit or removed from office or retired, the position he or she holds in the election committee shall terminate automatically; if a member is no longer suitable to continue serving on the election committee due to adjustment of duties or other reasons, he or she shall be removed from the position on the election committee. When a vacancy occurs among the members of an election committee, a replacement shall be promptly made.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民解放军选举委员会由十一至十九人组成，设主任一人，副主任一至三人，委员若干人。其他各级选举委员会由七至十七人组成，设主任一人，副主任一至二人，委员若干人。

**Article 9** The Election Committee of the People's Liberation Army shall be composed of 11 to 19 members, with one chairperson, one to three vice-chairpersons, and a number of committee members. Election committees at other levels shall be composed of 7 to 17 members, with one chairperson, one to two vice-chairpersons, and a number of committee members.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　团级以上单位的选举委员会组织、指导所属单位的选举，办理下列事项：  
（一）审查军人代表大会代表资格；  
（二）确定选举日期；  
（三）公布人民代表大会代表候选人名单；  
（四）主持本级军人代表大会或者军人大会的投票选举；  
（五）确定选举结果是否有效，公布当选的人民代表大会代表名单；  
（六）主持本级军人代表大会或者军人大会罢免和补选人民代表大会代表、接受人民代表大会代表辞职。

**Article 10** The election committees at or above the regiment level shall organize and guide the elections of units under their jurisdiction and handle the following matters:  
(1) reviewing the qualifications of deputies to the servicemen congress;  
(2) determining the date of election;  
(3) publishing the list of candidates for deputies to the people's congress;  
(4) presiding over the voting election of the servicemen congress or servicemen assembly at the corresponding level;  
(5) determining the validity of the election results and publishing the list of elected deputies to the people's congress;  
(6) presiding over the recall and by-election of deputies to the people's congress by the servicemen congress or servicemen assembly at the corresponding level, and accepting the resignation of deputies to the people's congress.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　选举委员会下设办公室，具体承办本级有关选举的日常工作。  
办公室设在政治工作部门，工作人员由本级选举委员会确定。

**Article 11** An office shall be established under the election committee to specifically handle the daily work related to the election at the corresponding level.  
The office shall be located in the political work department, and its staff shall be determined by the election committee at the same level.

### 第三章　代表名额的决定和分配

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民解放军应选全国人民代表大会代表的名额，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。

**Article 12** The number of deputies to the National People's Congress to be elected by the People's Liberation Army shall be decided by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中央军事委员会机关部门和战区、军兵种、军事科学院、国防大学、国防科技大学等单位应选全国人民代表大会代表的名额，由人民解放军选举委员会分配。中央军事委员会直属机构参加其代管部门的选举。

**Article 13** The number of deputies to the National People's Congress to be elected by the departments of the Central Military Commission, theater commands, services and arms, the Academy of Military Sciences, the National Defense University, the National University of Defense Technology, and other units shall be allocated by the Election Committee of the People's Liberation Army. The organs directly under the Central Military Commission shall participate in the election of the department that manages them on its behalf.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各地驻军应选县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表的名额，由驻地各该级人民代表大会常务委员会决定。  
有关选举事宜，由省军区（卫戍区、警备区）、军分区（警备区）、人民武装部分别与驻地的人民代表大会常务委员会协商决定。

**Article 14** The number of deputies to local people's congresses at or above the county level to be elected by the garrisons in various localities shall be decided by the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels in the localities.  
Matters concerning the election shall be decided through consultation between the provincial military commands (garrison commands, garrison areas), military sub-commands (garrison areas), and the people's armed forces departments, respectively, and the standing committees of the people's congresses in the localities.

### 第四章　选区和选举单位

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　驻军选举县级人民代表大会代表，由驻该行政区域的军人和参加军队选举的其他人员按选区直接选举产生。选区按该行政区域内驻军各单位的分布情况划分。  
选区的大小，按照每一选区选一名至三名代表划分。

**Article 15** For the election of deputies to the county people's congress by the garrisons, the servicemen and women stationed in that administrative area and other personnel participating in military elections shall directly elect them on the basis of electoral districts. The electoral districts shall be delineated according to the distribution of the garrison units within that administrative area.  
The size of an electoral district shall be delineated on the basis of electing one to three deputies per electoral district.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　驻军应选的设区的市、自治州、省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会代表，由团级以上单位召开军人代表大会选举产生。  
中央军事委员会机关部门和战区、军兵种、军事科学院、国防大学、国防科技大学等单位的军人代表大会，选举全国人民代表大会代表。

**Article 16** Deputies to the people's congresses of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government to be elected by the garrisons shall be elected by servicemen congresses convened by units at or above the regiment level.  
The servicemen congresses of the departments of the Central Military Commission, theater commands, services and arms, the Academy of Military Sciences, the National Defense University, and the National University of Defense Technology shall elect deputies to the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民解放军师级以上单位的军人代表大会代表，由下级军人代表大会选举产生。下级单位不召开军人代表大会的，由军人大会选举产生。  
旅、团级单位的军人代表大会代表，由连和其他基层单位召开军人大会选举产生。  
军人代表大会由选举委员会召集，军人大会由选举委员会或者军人委员会召集。  
军人代表大会每届任期五年。军人代表大会代表任期从本届军人代表大会举行第一次会议开始，到下届军人代表大会举行第一次会议为止。

**Article 17** Deputies to the servicemen congress of units at or above the division level of the People's Liberation Army shall be elected by the servicemen congress at the next lower level. If a lower-level unit does not convene a servicemen congress, the deputies shall be elected by the servicemen assembly.  
Deputies to the servicemen congress of brigade and regiment level units shall be elected by servicemen assemblies convened by companies and other grassroots units.  
The servicemen congress shall be convened by the election committee, and the servicemen assembly shall be convened by the election committee or the servicemen committee.  
The term of office of each servicemen congress shall be five years. The term of office of deputies to the servicemen congress shall begin from the convening of the first meeting of the current servicemen congress and end at the convening of the first meeting of the next servicemen congress.

### 第五章　代表候选人的提出

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民解放军选举全国和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表，候选人按选区或者选举单位提名产生。  
中国共产党在军队中的各级组织，可以推荐代表候选人。选民或者军人代表大会代表，十人以上联名，也可以推荐代表候选人。推荐者应向选举委员会或者军人委员会介绍候选人的情况。接受推荐的代表候选人应当向选举委员会或者军人委员会如实提供个人基本情况。提供的基本情况不实的，选举委员会或者军人委员会应当向选民或者军人代表大会代表通报。

**Article 18** When the People's Liberation Army elects deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level, candidates shall be nominated on the basis of electoral districts or electoral units.  
Organizations of the Communist Party of China at various levels in the military may nominate candidates for deputies. Voters or deputies to the servicemen congress, jointly recommended by ten or more persons, may also nominate candidates for deputies. Recommenders shall introduce the candidates' information to the election committee or the servicemen committee. Recommended candidates for deputies shall truthfully provide their basic personal information to the election committee or the servicemen committee. If the basic information provided is untrue, the election committee or the servicemen committee shall notify the voters or deputies to the servicemen congress.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民解放军选举全国和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表实行差额选举，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表的名额。  
由选民直接选举的，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表名额三分之一至一倍；由军人代表大会选举的，代表候选人的人数应多于应选代表名额五分之一至二分之一。

**Article 19** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level by the People's Liberation Army shall adopt contested elections, and the number of candidates for deputies shall be greater than the number of deputies to be elected.  
For direct election by voters, the number of candidates for deputies shall be one third to one time greater than the number of deputies to be elected; for election by the servicemen congress, the number of candidates for deputies shall be one fifth to one half greater than the number of deputies to be elected.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　由选民直接选举的，代表候选人由选举委员会或者军人委员会汇总后，将代表候选人名单以及代表候选人的基本情况在选举日的十五日以前公布，并交各该选区的选民反复讨论、协商，确定正式代表候选人名单。如果所提代表候选人的人数超过[本办法第十九条](#art-19)规定的最高差额比例，由选举委员会或者军人委员会交各该选区的选民讨论、协商，根据较多数选民的意见，确定正式代表候选人名单；对正式代表候选人不能形成较为一致意见的，进行预选，根据预选时得票多少的顺序，确定正式代表候选人名单。正式代表候选人名单以及代表候选人的基本情况应当在选举日的七日以前公布。  
团级以上单位的军人代表大会在选举人民代表大会代表时，提名、酝酿代表候选人的时间不得少于两天。各该级选举委员会将依法提出的代表候选人名单以及代表候选人的基本情况印发军人代表大会全体代表酝酿、讨论。如果所提代表候选人的人数符合[本办法第十九条](#art-19)规定的差额比例，直接进行投票选举。如果所提代表候选人的人数超过[本办法第十九条](#art-19)规定的最高差额比例，进行预选，根据预选时得票多少的顺序，按照本级军人代表大会确定的具体差额比例，确定正式代表候选人名单，进行投票选举。

**Article 20** For direct election by voters, after the election committee or the servicemen committee has collected the candidates for deputies, it shall publish the list of candidates for deputies and their basic information 15 days before the election day, and submit them to the voters of the respective electoral districts for repeated discussion and consultation to determine the list of formal candidates for deputies. If the number of proposed candidates for deputies exceeds the maximum differential ratio specified in Article 19 of these Measures, the election committee or the servicemen committee shall submit them to the voters of the respective electoral districts for discussion and consultation, and determine the list of formal candidates for deputies based on the opinions of the majority of voters; if no relatively consistent opinion can be formed on the formal candidates, a preliminary election shall be conducted, and the list of formal candidates for deputies shall be determined according to the order of the number of votes obtained in the preliminary election. The list of formal candidates for deputies and their basic information shall be published seven days before the election day.  
When a servicemen congress at or above the regiment level elects deputies to the people's congress, the time for nomination and deliberation of candidates for deputies shall not be less than two days. The election committee at that level shall print and distribute the list of candidates for deputies proposed according to law and their basic information to all deputies of the servicemen congress for deliberation and discussion. If the number of proposed candidates for deputies meets the differential ratio specified in Article 19 of these Measures, voting and election shall be conducted directly. If the number of proposed candidates for deputies exceeds the maximum differential ratio specified in Article 19 of these Measures, a preliminary election shall be conducted, and the list of formal candidates for deputies shall be determined according to the order of votes obtained in the preliminary election and the specific differential ratio determined by the servicemen congress at that level, followed by voting and election.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军人代表大会在选举全国和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人不限于本级军人代表大会代表。

**Article 21** When a servicemen congress elects deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level, candidates for deputies shall not be limited to deputies of the servicemen congress at that level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　选举委员会或者军人委员会应当介绍代表候选人的情况。  
推荐代表候选人的组织或者个人可以在选民小组或者军人代表大会小组会议上介绍所推荐的代表候选人的情况。直接选举时，选举委员会或者军人委员会根据选民的要求，应当组织代表候选人与选民见面，由代表候选人介绍本人的情况，回答选民的问题。但是，在选举日必须停止对代表候选人的介绍。

**Article 22** The election committee or the servicemen committee shall introduce the information of candidates for deputies.  
Organizations or individuals that recommend candidates for deputies may introduce the information of the recommended candidates at meetings of voter groups or groups of the servicemen congress.  
In direct elections, the election committee or the servicemen committee shall, at the request of the voters, organize meetings between candidates and voters, where candidates shall introduce themselves and answer questions from voters. However, on the election day, any introduction of candidates for deputies must cease.

### 第六章　选举程序

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　直接选举时，各选区应当召开军人大会进行选举，或者按照方便选民投票的原则设立投票站进行选举。驻地分散或者行动不便的选民，可以在流动票箱投票。投票选举由军人委员会或者选举委员会主持。  
军人代表大会的投票选举，由选举委员会主持。

**Article 23** In direct elections, each electoral district shall convene a servicemen assembly to conduct the election, or set up polling stations in accordance with the principle of facilitating voters to vote. Voters who are scattered in location or have difficulty in mobility may vote in mobile ballot boxes. The voting and election shall be presided over by the servicemen committee or the election committee.  
The voting and election of the servicemen congress shall be presided over by the election committee.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民解放军选举全国和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表，一律采用无记名投票的方法。选举时应当设有秘密写票处。  
选民因残疾等原因不能写选票，可以委托他信任的人代写。

**Article 24** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level by the People's Liberation Army shall uniformly adopt the method of secret ballot. During the election, a secret ballot writing area shall be set up.  
If a voter is unable to fill in a ballot paper due to disability or other reasons, he or she may entrust a person he or she trusts to fill it in on his or her behalf.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　选民如果在选举期间外出，经军人委员会或者选举委员会同意，可以书面委托其他选民代为投票。每一选民接受的委托不得超过三人，并应当按照委托人的意愿代为投票。

**Article 25** If a voter is away during the election period, with the consent of the servicemen committee or the election committee, he or she may entrust another voter in writing to vote on his or her behalf. Each voter may accept no more than three such entrustments and shall vote in accordance with the entrusting voter's wishes.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　选举人对代表候选人可以投赞成票，可以投反对票，可以另选其他任何选民，也可以弃权。

**Article 26** A voter may cast a favorable vote, cast an opposing vote, vote for any other voter, or abstain from voting for candidates for deputies.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　投票结束后，由选民推选的或者军人代表大会代表推选的监票、计票人员和选举委员会或者军人委员会的人员将投票人数和票数加以核对，作出记录，并由监票人签字。  
代表候选人的近亲属不得担任监票人、计票人。

**Article 27** After the voting ends, the scrutineers and vote counters elected by the voters or by the deputies to the servicemen congress, together with personnel of the election committee or the servicemen committee, shall check the number of voters who voted and the number of votes, make a record, and have it signed by the scrutineers.  
Close relatives of candidates for deputies shall not serve as scrutineers or vote counters.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　每次选举所投的票数，多于投票人数的无效，等于或者少于投票人数的有效。  
每一选票所选的人数，多于规定应选代表人数的作废，等于或者少于规定应选代表人数的有效。

**Article 28** If the number of votes cast in each election exceeds the number of voters who voted, the election shall be invalid; if it is equal to or less than the number of voters who voted, it shall be valid.  
If the number of persons voted for on each ballot paper exceeds the prescribed number of deputies to be elected, the ballot paper shall be void; if it is equal to or less than the prescribed number, it shall be valid.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　直接选举时，参加投票的选民超过选区全体选民的半数，选举有效。代表候选人获得参加投票的选民过半数的选票时，始得当选。  
军人代表大会选举时，代表候选人获得全体代表过半数的选票，始得当选。

**Article 29** In direct elections, if the number of voters who participate in voting exceeds half of all voters in the electoral district, the election shall be valid. A candidate for deputy shall be elected only when he or she obtains more than half of the votes of the voters who participate in voting.  
In elections by the servicemen congress, a candidate for deputy shall be elected only when he or she obtains more than half of the votes of all deputies.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　获得过半数选票的代表候选人的人数超过应选代表名额时，以得票多的当选。如遇票数相等不能确定当选人时，应就票数相等的候选人再次投票，以得票多的当选。  
获得过半数选票的当选代表的人数少于应选代表名额时，不足的名额另行选举。另行选举时，根据在第一次投票时得票多少的顺序，按照[本办法第十九条](#art-19)规定的差额比例，确定候选人名单。如果只选一人，候选人应为二人。  
依照前款规定另行选举县级人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人以得票多的当选，但是得票数不得少于选票的三分之一；团级以上单位的军人代表大会在另行选举设区的市、自治州、省、自治区、直辖市和全国人民代表大会代表时，代表候选人获得军人代表大会全体代表过半数的选票，始得当选。

**Article 30** If the number of candidates for deputies who have obtained more than half of the votes exceeds the number of deputies to be elected, those with the highest number of votes shall be elected. If two or more candidates have tied votes and it is impossible to determine the elected person, another vote shall be taken on the tied candidates, and the ones with the highest number of votes shall be elected.  
If the number of elected deputies who have obtained more than half of the votes is less than the number of deputies to be elected, the remaining seats shall be filled in a separate election. In the separate election, a list of candidates shall be determined according to the order of the number of votes obtained in the first voting and in accordance with the differential ratio specified in Article 19 of these Measures. If only one person is to be elected, the candidates shall be two persons.  
When separately electing deputies to a county people's congress in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the candidate for deputy with the highest number of votes shall be elected, provided that the number of votes obtained shall not be less than one third of the ballot papers; when separately electing deputies to people's congresses of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the National People's Congress by a servicemen congress at or above the regiment level, the candidate for deputy shall be elected only when he or she obtains more than half of the votes of all deputies of the servicemen congress.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　选举结果由选举委员会或者军人委员会根据本办法确定是否有效，并予以宣布。

**Article 31** The election committee or the servicemen committee shall determine whether the election result is valid in accordance with these Measures and shall announce it.

### 第七章　对代表的监督和罢免、辞职、补选

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民解放军选出的全国和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表，受选民和原选举单位的监督。选民或者选举单位都有权罢免自己选出的代表。

**Article 32** The deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level elected by the People's Liberation Army shall be subject to the supervision of the voters and the original electoral units. Voters or electoral units shall have the right to recall the deputies they have elected.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对于县级人民代表大会代表，原选区选民十人以上联名，可以向旅、团级选举委员会书面提出罢免要求。  
罢免要求应当写明罢免理由。被提出罢免的代表有权在军人大会上提出申辩意见，也可以书面提出申辩意见。  
旅、团级选举委员会应当将罢免要求和被提出罢免的代表的书面申辩意见印发原选区选民。  
表决罢免要求，由旅、团级选举委员会主持。

**Article 33** For deputies to a county people's congress, if jointly requested by ten or more voters of the original electoral district, a written demand for recall may be submitted to the election committee at the brigade or regiment level.  
The demand for recall shall state the reasons for the recall. The deputy proposed to be recalled shall have the right to present a defense at the servicemen assembly or submit a written defense.  
The election committee at the brigade or regiment level shall print and distribute the demand for recall and the written defense of the deputy proposed to be recalled to the voters of the original electoral district.  
The voting on the demand for recall shall be presided over by the election committee at the brigade or regiment level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　军人代表大会举行会议时，团级以上单位的选举委员会可以提出对由该级军人代表大会选出的人民代表大会代表的罢免案。罢免案应当写明罢免理由。  
军人代表大会举行会议时，被提出罢免的代表有权在会议上提出申辩意见，或者书面提出申辩意见。罢免案经会议审议后予以表决。

**Article 34** When a servicemen congress is in session, the election committee at or above the regiment level may submit a recall case against a deputy to the people's congress elected by the servicemen congress at that level. The recall case shall state the reasons for the recall.  
When a servicemen congress is in session, the deputy proposed to be recalled shall have the right to present a defense at the meeting or submit a written defense. The recall case shall be deliberated at the meeting and then put to a vote.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　罢免代表采用无记名投票的表决方式。

**Article 35** The recall of a deputy shall be conducted by secret ballot.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　罢免县级人民代表大会代表，须经原选区过半数的选民通过。  
罢免由军人代表大会选出的人民代表大会代表，由各该级军人代表大会过半数的代表通过。  
罢免的决议，须报送同级人民代表大会常务委员会和军队上一级选举委员会备案。

**Article 36** The recall of a deputy to a county people's congress shall require approval by more than half of the voters of the original electoral district.  
The recall of a deputy to a people's congress elected by a servicemen congress shall require approval by more than half of the deputies of the servicemen congress at that level.  
The resolution on the recall shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level and the election committee at the next higher level in the military for recordation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民解放军选出的设区的市、自治州、省、自治区、直辖市和全国人民代表大会代表，可以向原选举单位的选举委员会书面提出辞职。人民解放军选出的县级人民代表大会代表，可以向原选区的选举委员会或者军人委员会书面提出辞职。接受辞职，须经军人代表大会或者军人大会全体人员的过半数通过，并报送各该级人民代表大会常务委员会和军队上一级选举委员会备案。  
因执行任务等原因无法召开军人代表大会的，团级以上单位的选举委员会可以接受各该级选出的设区的市、自治州、省、自治区、直辖市和全国人民代表大会代表辞职。选举委员会接受人民代表大会代表辞职后，应当及时通报选举产生该代表的军人代表大会的代表，并报送各该级人民代表大会常务委员会和军队上一级选举委员会备案。

**Article 37** Deputies to the people's congresses of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the National People's Congress elected by the People's Liberation Army may submit a written resignation to the election committee of the original electoral unit. Deputies to county people's congresses elected by the People's Liberation Army may submit a written resignation to the election committee or the servicemen committee of the original electoral district.  
Acceptance of a resignation shall require approval by more than half of all members of the servicemen congress or the servicemen assembly, and shall be reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the election committee at the next higher level in the military for recordation.  
If it is impossible to convene a servicemen congress due to tasks or other reasons, the election committee at or above the regiment level may accept the resignation of deputies to the people's congresses of cities divided into districts, autonomous prefectures, provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the National People's Congress elected at that level. After the election committee accepts the resignation of a deputy to the people's congress, it shall promptly notify the deputies of the servicemen congress that elected the deputy, and shall report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the election committee at the next higher level in the military for recordation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　代表在任期内因故出缺，由原选区或者原选举单位补选。  
人民解放军选出的县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表，在任期内调离本行政区域的，其代表资格自行终止，缺额另行补选。  
补选代表时，代表候选人的名额可以多于应选代表的名额，也可以同应选代表的名额相等。  
因执行任务等原因无法召开军人代表大会的，可以由本级选举委员会进行补选。

**Article 38** If a deputy's position becomes vacant during his or her term of office for any reason, a by-election shall be held in the original electoral district or original electoral unit.  
If a deputy to a local people's congress at or above the county level elected by the People's Liberation Army is transferred out of that administrative area during his or her term of office, the qualifications of the deputy shall terminate automatically, and the vacancy shall be filled by a separate by-election.  
In a by-election, the number of candidates for deputies may be greater than or equal to the number of deputies to be elected.  
If it is impossible to convene a servicemen congress due to tasks or other reasons, a by-election may be conducted by the election committee at the same level.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民解放军的选举经费，由军费开支。

**Article 39** The election expenses of the People's Liberation Army shall be borne by military funds.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民武装警察部队选举全国人民代表大会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会代表，适用本办法。

**Article 40** The election of deputies to the National People's Congress and local people's congresses at or above the county level by the People's Armed Police Force shall be governed by these Measures.
