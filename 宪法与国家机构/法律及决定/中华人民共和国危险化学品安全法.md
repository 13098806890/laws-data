# 中华人民共和国危险化学品安全法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2025-12-27  
**生效日期**：2026-05-01

> （2025年12月27日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强危险化学品的安全管理，预防和减少危险化学品事故，保障人民群众身体健康、生命安全和财产安全，保护生态环境，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated to strengthen the safety management of hazardous chemicals, prevent and reduce hazardous chemical accidents, protect the people's health, life safety and property safety, and protect the ecological environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　危险化学品生产、储存、使用、经营和运输的安全管理，适用本法。  
废弃危险化学品的处置，依照有关生态环境保护的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 2** The safety management of the production, storage, use, operation and transport of hazardous chemicals shall be governed by this Law.  
The disposal of discarded hazardous chemicals shall be carried out in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on ecological environment protection and the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称危险化学品，是指具有毒害、腐蚀、爆炸、燃烧、助燃等性质，对人体、设施、生态环境具有危害的剧毒化学品和其他化学品。  
国家建立健全危险化学品目录管理制度。危险化学品目录，由国务院应急管理部门会同工业和信息化、公安、生态环境、交通运输、农业农村、卫生健康、海关、市场监督管理、铁路、民用航空等部门，根据化学品危险特性的鉴定和分类标准确定、公布，并适时调整。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "hazardous chemicals" means highly toxic chemicals and other chemicals that have toxic, corrosive, explosive, combustible, combustion-supporting, and other properties and are harmful to human beings, facilities and the ecological environment.  
The State shall establish and improve a catalog management system for hazardous chemicals. The catalog of hazardous chemicals shall be determined, published and adjusted in a timely manner by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the departments of industry and information technology, public security, ecological environment, transport, agriculture and rural affairs, health, customs, market regulation, railway, civil aviation, and other departments, in accordance with the standards for the identification and classification of the hazardous characteristics of chemicals.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　危险化学品安全管理工作坚持中国共产党的领导。  
危险化学品安全管理工作应当坚持人民至上、生命至上，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，坚持安全第一、预防为主、综合治理的方针，从源头上防范化解重大安全风险。  
危险化学品安全管理工作实行管行业必须管安全、管业务必须管安全、管生产经营必须管安全，强化和落实单位主体责任与政府监管责任，建立单位负责、职工参与、政府监管、行业自律、社会监督的机制。

**Article 4** The safety management of hazardous chemicals shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.  
The safety management of hazardous chemicals shall adhere to the principle of putting the people first and life first, implement the holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security, adhere to the policy of safety first, prevention-oriented, and comprehensive management, and prevent and mitigate major safety risks from the source.  
The safety management of hazardous chemicals shall implement the principle that those who manage an industry must also manage safety, those who manage business must also manage safety, and those who manage production and operation must also manage safety, strengthen and implement the primary responsibilities of units and the regulatory responsibilities of the government, and establish a mechanism featuring unit responsibility, employee participation, government supervision, industry self-discipline, and public oversight.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　生产、储存、使用、经营、运输危险化学品的企业、学校、科研机构、医疗机构、检测机构、检验机构等单位（以下统称危险化学品单位）应当实行全员安全生产责任制，构建安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防机制，加强安全生产标准化、信息化建设，其主要负责人对本单位的危险化学品安全生产工作全面负责。  
危险化学品单位应当具备法律、行政法规规定和国家标准、行业标准要求的安全条件，建立健全安全管理规章制度和岗位安全责任制度，对从业人员进行安全生产教育和培训，为从业人员提供符合国家标准或者行业标准的劳动防护用品，依法参加工伤保险。从业人员应当接受教育和培训，考核合格后上岗作业；对有资格要求的岗位，应当配备依法取得相应资格的人员。

**Article 5** Enterprises, schools, scientific research institutions, medical institutions, testing institutions, inspection institutions and other units that produce, store, use, operate, or transport hazardous chemicals (hereinafter collectively referred to as "hazardous chemical units") shall implement a full-time safety production responsibility system, establish a dual prevention mechanism combining graded control of safety risks and investigation and management of hidden dangers, strengthen the standardization and informatization of safety production, and the principal responsible person of each unit shall bear overall responsibility for the safety production of hazardous chemicals within the unit.  
A hazardous chemical unit shall possess the safety conditions required by laws, administrative regulations, national standards and industry standards, establish and improve safety management rules and regulations and post safety responsibility systems, provide safety production education and training to its employees, provide employees with labor protection articles that conform to national standards or industry standards, and participate in work-related injury insurance according to law. Employees shall receive education and training, and may only take up posts after passing the examination; for posts with qualification requirements, persons who have obtained the corresponding qualifications according to law shall be assigned.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人不得生产、使用、经营国家禁止生产、使用、经营的危险化学品。  
国家对危险化学品的使用有限制性规定的，任何单位和个人不得违反限制性规定使用危险化学品。

**Article 6** No unit or individual may produce, use, or operate hazardous chemicals the production, use, or operation of which is prohibited by the State.  
Where the State has restrictive provisions on the use of hazardous chemicals, no unit or individual may use hazardous chemicals in violation of such restrictive provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对危险化学品的生产、储存、使用、经营、运输实施安全监督管理的有关部门（以下统称负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门），依照下列规定履行职责：  
（一）应急管理部门负责危险化学品安全生产监督管理工作和危险化学品安全监督管理综合工作，组织确定、公布、调整危险化学品目录，依法对新建、改建、扩建生产、储存危险化学品（包括使用长输管道输送危险化学品，下同）的建设项目（以下统称危险化学品建设项目）进行安全条件审查和安全设施设计审查，核发危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品安全使用许可证和危险化学品经营许可证，并负责危险化学品登记工作，组织危险化学品事故应急处置。  
（二）公安机关负责危险化学品的公共安全管理，核发剧毒化学品购买许可证、剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，负责运输危险化学品的车辆进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行区域的许可，并负责危险化学品运输车辆的道路交通安全管理。  
（三）市场监督管理部门负责对纳入工业产品生产许可证管理目录的危险化学品及其包装物、容器（包括罐体，不包括储存危险化学品的固定式大型储罐，下同）实施工业产品生产许可证管理和产品质量监督，签发危险化学品生产、储存、经营、运输企业营业执照。  
（四）生态环境主管部门负责废弃危险化学品处置的监督管理，组织危险化学品的生态环境危害性鉴定和生态环境风险程度评估，负责新化学物质环境管理登记，依照职责分工调查相关危险化学品环境污染事故和生态破坏事件，负责危险化学品事故现场的生态环境应急监测。  
（五）交通运输主管部门负责危险化学品道路、水路运输活动安全监督管理，负责危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的许可或者备案，以及运输工具的安全监督管理，负责危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员的资格认定。铁路监管部门负责危险化学品铁路运输及其运输工具的安全监督管理。民用航空主管部门负责危险化学品航空运输以及航空运输企业及其运输工具的安全监督管理。邮政管理部门负责依法查处寄递危险化学品的行为。  
（六）卫生健康主管部门负责危险化学品毒性鉴定的管理，负责危险化学品单位职业健康的监督管理，负责组织、协调危险化学品事故受伤人员的医疗卫生救援工作。  
（七）自然资源主管部门负责在编制国土空间规划时，将危险化学品建设项目及化工园区、储存危险化学品的专门区域和周边安全控制距离等有关内容按照程序纳入当地国土空间规划，并做好规划监督实施。  
（八）工业和信息化主管部门依照职责负责危险化学品生产、储存的相关行业规划和布局，组织制定化工园区的建设标准和认定管理办法，并推动落后工艺、产能退出。  
（九）海关负责依法对进出口危险化学品及其包装实施检验。  
（十）其他部门依照各自职责履行危险化学品安全监督管理职责。  
对因涉及新兴行业、领域，危险化学品安全监督管理职责不明确的，由县级以上人民政府按照业务相近的原则及时确定监督管理部门。

**Article 7** The relevant departments that exercise safety supervision and administration over the production, storage, use, operation and transport of hazardous chemicals (hereinafter collectively referred to as "departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals") shall perform their duties in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) The emergency management department shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the safety production of hazardous chemicals and the comprehensive work on the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals; organize the determination, publication and adjustment of the catalog of hazardous chemicals; conduct, according to law, the safety condition review and the safety facility design review of construction projects for the production and storage of new, renovated or expanded hazardous chemicals (including the transport of hazardous chemicals through long-distance pipelines, the same below) (hereinafter collectively referred to as "hazardous chemical construction projects"); issue hazardous chemical safety production permits, hazardous chemical safety use permits and hazardous chemical operation permits; and be responsible for the registration of hazardous chemicals and organize the emergency response to hazardous chemical accidents.  
(2) The public security organ shall be responsible for the public safety management of hazardous chemicals; issue purchase permits for highly toxic chemicals and road transport passes for highly toxic chemicals; be responsible for the permission of vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals to enter areas where the transport of hazardous chemical vehicles is restricted; and be responsible for the road traffic safety management of vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals.  
(3) The market regulation department shall be responsible for the implementation of industrial product production permit management and product quality supervision over hazardous chemicals included in the catalog of industrial product production permit management and their packaging and containers (including tanks, excluding fixed large storage tanks for storing hazardous chemicals, the same below), and issue business licenses for enterprises producing, storing, operating or transporting hazardous chemicals.  
(4) The competent department of ecological environment shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the disposal of discarded hazardous chemicals; organize the identification of ecological and environmental hazards of hazardous chemicals and the assessment of the degree of ecological and environmental risks; be responsible for the environmental management registration of new chemical substances; investigate, in accordance with the division of responsibilities, relevant environmental pollution accidents and ecological damage incidents involving hazardous chemicals; and be responsible for the emergency ecological and environmental monitoring at the site of hazardous chemical accidents.  
(5) The competent department of transport shall be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of road and waterway transport activities of hazardous chemicals; be responsible for the permission or record-filing of road and waterway transport of hazardous chemicals and the safety supervision and administration of means of transport; and be responsible for the qualification recognition of drivers, crew members, loading and unloading managers, escorts, declarants and on-site container packing inspectors of enterprises engaged in road and waterway transport of hazardous chemicals. The railway regulatory department shall be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of the railway transport of hazardous chemicals and their means of transport. The competent department of civil aviation shall be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of the air transport of hazardous chemicals and air transport enterprises and their means of transport. The postal administration department shall be responsible for investigating and punishing, according to law, the sending of hazardous chemicals by post.  
(6) The competent department of health shall be responsible for the management of the toxicity identification of hazardous chemicals; be responsible for the supervision and administration of the occupational health of hazardous chemical units; and be responsible for organizing and coordinating the medical and health rescue of persons injured in hazardous chemical accidents.  
(7) The competent department of natural resources shall, when preparing the territorial space plan, incorporate, in accordance with the procedures, relevant contents such as hazardous chemical construction projects and industrial parks for the chemical industry, special areas for storing hazardous chemicals, and surrounding safety control distances into the local territorial space plan, and properly supervise the implementation of the plan.  
(8) The competent department of industry and information technology shall, in accordance with its functions, be responsible for the planning and layout of relevant industries for the production and storage of hazardous chemicals; organize the formulation of construction standards and the identification management measures for industrial parks for the chemical industry; and promote the exit of backward technologies and production capacities.  
(9) The customs shall be responsible for implementing, according to law, inspections of imported and exported hazardous chemicals and their packaging.  
(10) Other departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions, perform the duties of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals.  
Where, due to emerging industries or fields, the responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals are unclear, the people's government at or above the county level shall promptly determine the supervision and administration department in accordance with the principle of business proximity.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法进行监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入危险化学品作业场所实施现场检查，向有关单位和人员了解情况，查阅、复制有关文件、资料，必要时可以对危险化学品实施抽样检测；  
（二）运用信息化手段对危险化学品单位的重大危险源开展在线巡查抽查；  
（三）发现危险化学品事故隐患，责令立即消除或者限期消除；  
（四）对不符合法律、行政法规、规章规定或者国家标准、行业标准要求的设施、设备、装置、器材、运输工具等，责令立即停止使用；  
（五）经本部门主要负责人批准，查封违法生产、储存、使用、经营危险化学品的场所，扣押违法生产、储存、使用、经营、运输的危险化学品以及用于违法生产、使用、运输危险化学品的原材料、设备、运输工具；  
（六）发现影响危险化学品安全的违法行为，当场予以纠正或者责令限期改正。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法进行监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件；有关单位和个人对依法进行的监督检查应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 8** Departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals may, when conducting supervision and inspection according to law, take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the workplaces of hazardous chemicals to conduct on-site inspections, inquire about the situation from relevant units and persons, consult and copy relevant documents and materials, and, where necessary, conduct sampling tests on hazardous chemicals;  
(2) Use information technology means to carry out online patrols and spot checks of major hazard installations of hazardous chemical units;  
(3) Upon discovering hidden dangers of hazardous chemical accidents, order immediate elimination or elimination within a specified time limit;  
(4) Order the immediate cessation of use of facilities, equipment, devices, apparatus, means of transport, etc. that do not meet the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, rules, national standards or industry standards;  
(5) With the approval of the principal responsible person of the department, seal up the places where hazardous chemicals are illegally produced, stored, used or operated, and detain the hazardous chemicals illegally produced, stored, used, operated or transported, as well as the raw materials, equipment and means of transport used for the illegal production, use or transport of hazardous chemicals;  
(6) Upon discovering illegal acts affecting the safety of hazardous chemicals, correct them on the spot or order rectification within a specified time limit.  
Where departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals conduct supervision and inspection according to law, the number of inspectors shall be no less than two, and they shall produce law enforcement credentials; the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted according to law, and may not refuse or obstruct it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当建立危险化学品安全监督管理工作协调机制，支持、督促负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法履行职责，协调、解决危险化学品安全监督管理工作中的重大问题。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门应当加强监管协作和联合执法，密切协调配合，实现信息及时、充分、有效共享，依法加强对危险化学品的安全监督管理。发现依法应当由其他部门处理的违法行为，及时移交其他部门处理。

**Article 9** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals, support and supervise the departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals in performing their duties according to law, and coordinate and resolve major issues in the work of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals.  
Departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals shall strengthen regulatory cooperation and joint law enforcement, closely coordinate and cooperate, achieve timely, full and effective sharing of information, and strengthen the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals according to law. Where illegal acts that should be handled by other departments according to law are discovered, they shall be promptly transferred to the other departments for handling.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　任何单位和个人对违反本法规定的行为或者事故隐患，有权向负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门举报或者报告。县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对举报违法行为或者报告重大事故隐患的有功人员依法给予奖励，并对举报人、报告人的信息进行严格保密。

**Article 10** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or inform against any act violating the provisions of this Law or any hidden danger of accident to the departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals. The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, according to law, reward the persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting illegal acts or reporting major accident hazards, and shall strictly keep confidential the information of the informants and reporters.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门应当加强危险化学品信息化监管，对危险化学品实行电子标识和全生命周期信息化管理和监控。  
化工园区应当明确负责安全生产监督管理的有关工作机构及其职责，加强危险化学品信息化建设，实现信息化安全监测、监控和预警，并与政府有关部门实现互联互通。

**Article 11** Departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals under the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the informatization supervision of hazardous chemicals, and implement electronic identification and full life cycle information management and monitoring of hazardous chemicals.  
An industrial park for the chemical industry shall specify the relevant work organs responsible for the supervision and administration of safety production and their functions, strengthen the informatization construction of hazardous chemicals, achieve informatization-based safety monitoring, surveillance and early warning, and realize connectivity with the relevant government departments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励危险化学品单位采用有利于提高安全保障水平的先进技术、工艺、设备以及自动控制系统，鼓励对危险化学品实行专门储存、统一配送、集中销售。

**Article 12** The State encourages hazardous chemical units to adopt advanced technologies, processes, equipment and automatic control systems conducive to improving the level of safety assurance, and encourages specialized storage, unified distribution and centralized sales of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产、储存、使用、经营危险化学品的单位应当按照国家有关规定对危险化学品重大危险源登记建档，进行定期检测、评估、监控，制定应急预案，建立重大危险源安全责任制，并将重大危险源的储存数量、储存地点、管理人员的情况及有关安全措施、应急措施报所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门、消防救援机构和有关部门备案；在港区内储存的，报港口行政管理部门、消防救援机构和有关部门备案。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门应当分级分类定期对危险化学品重大危险源开展专项监督检查，督促危险化学品单位及时消除事故隐患，防范重特大事故发生。  
本法所称重大危险源，是指长期或者临时生产、储存、使用和经营危险化学品，且危险化学品的数量等于或者超过临界量的单元（包括场所和设施）。

**Article 13** Units that produce, store, use or operate hazardous chemicals shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, register and establish archives for major hazard installations of hazardous chemicals, conduct regular testing, evaluation and monitoring, formulate emergency response plans, establish a safety responsibility system for major hazard installations, and report the storage quantity, storage location, information on the management personnel, and relevant safety measures and emergency measures of the major hazard installations to the emergency management department, the fire rescue organization and the relevant departments of the people's government at the county level where the unit is located for record; if stored in a port area, they shall report to the port administrative department, the fire rescue organization and the relevant departments for record.  
Departments with responsibilities for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals shall, in a graded and classified manner and on a regular basis, carry out special supervision and inspection of major hazard installations of hazardous chemicals, and urge hazardous chemical units to promptly eliminate hidden dangers of accidents and prevent the occurrence of major and extraordinarily serious accidents.  
For the purposes of this Law, "major hazard installation" means a unit (including a place and a facility) where hazardous chemicals are produced, stored, used or operated on a long-term or temporary basis, and the quantity of hazardous chemicals equals or exceeds the threshold quantity.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式，加强危险化学品安全法律法规和危险化学品安全知识宣传普及工作，促进全社会危险化学品安全意识的提升。  
化工园区、危险化学品单位应当对周边群众和其他生产经营单位进行危险化学品安全法律法规和危险化学品安全知识宣传。  
新闻媒体应当开展危险化学品安全法律法规和危险化学品安全知识公益宣传，对危险化学品安全违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 14** The people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall, in various forms, strengthen the publicity and popularization of laws and regulations on hazardous chemical safety and knowledge on hazardous chemical safety, so as to promote the enhancement of the whole society's safety awareness of hazardous chemicals.  
Industrial parks for the chemical industry and hazardous chemical units shall conduct publicity on the laws and regulations on hazardous chemical safety and knowledge on hazardous chemical safety among nearby people and other production and operating units.  
The news media shall conduct public-interest publicity on the laws and regulations on hazardous chemical safety and knowledge on hazardous chemical safety, and exercise public opinion supervision over illegal acts on hazardous chemical safety.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对在危险化学品安全工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 15** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the work on hazardous chemical safety shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　规划布局

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对危险化学品的生产、储存实行统筹规划、合理布局。  
国务院工业和信息化主管部门以及国务院其他有关部门依照各自职责，负责危险化学品生产、储存的行业规划和布局。  
地方人民政府组织编制国土空间规划时，应当根据本地区的实际情况，按照确保安全的原则，规划适当区域（包括化工园区）专门用于危险化学品的生产、储存。

**Article 16** The State shall implement overall planning and rational layout for the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.  
The competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the industry planning and layout of the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.  
When organizing the preparation of the territorial space plan, the local people's government shall, based on the actual conditions of the region and in accordance with the principle of ensuring safety, plan appropriate areas (including industrial parks for the chemical industry) for the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　化工园区应当由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其授权的部门认定公布并定期复核。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当合理建设化工园区，组织开展化工园区的安全风险等级评估、论证，建立并落实管控措施。  
化工园区应当对进出园区的所有危险化学品实行动态监管，对园区内企业、重点场所、重大危险源、基础设施实施风险监测预警。

**Article 17** An industrial park for the chemical industry shall be identified, published and subject to periodic review by the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, or by the department authorized thereby.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall construct industrial parks for the chemical industry in a reasonable manner, organize the assessment and verification of the safety risk levels of such industrial parks, and establish and implement control measures.  
An industrial park for the chemical industry shall implement dynamic supervision over all hazardous chemicals entering and leaving the park, and implement risk monitoring and early warning for enterprises within the park, key places, major hazard installations and infrastructure.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　新建、扩建危险化学品生产建设项目应当进入化工园区，与其他行业生产装置配套建设的项目和符合国家规定的其他项目除外。  
除为化工企业提供配套服务的企业外，非化工企业禁止进入化工园区。

**Article 18** New and expanded construction projects for the production of hazardous chemicals shall be located in an industrial park for the chemical industry, except for projects constructed as supporting facilities for production installations in other industries and other projects that comply with State provisions.  
Non-chemical enterprises are prohibited from entering an industrial park for the chemical industry, except for enterprises providing supporting services to chemical enterprises.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　化工园区应当至少每三年开展一次整体性安全风险评估，提出消除、降低、管控安全风险的对策措施并有效实施。  
当化工园区及危险化学品品种、数量、布局等发生变化，按照国家有关规定需要调整化工园区风险控制条件时，应当及时组织重新开展化工园区整体性安全风险评估，并修订相关应急预案。

**Article 19** An industrial park for the chemical industry shall, at least once every three years, conduct an overall safety risk assessment, propose countermeasures to eliminate, reduce and control safety risks, and effectively implement them.  
When the industrial park for the chemical industry undergoes changes in the types, quantities, layout, etc. of hazardous chemicals, and the risk control conditions of the industrial park need to be adjusted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the overall safety risk assessment of the industrial park for the chemical industry shall be promptly re-conducted, and the relevant emergency response plans shall be revised.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　化工园区与城市建成区、人员密集场所、重要设施、敏感目标等应当保持符合相关法律、法规规定和国家标准要求的安全距离。  
化工园区应当依照相关法律、法规规定和国家标准要求，拟定化工园区周边规划安全控制线，并报送化工园区所在地设区的市级人民政府或者其授权的部门确定后，按照程序纳入当地国土空间规划。  
化工园区所在地设区的市级和县级人民政府自然资源主管部门应当严格控制化工园区周边规划安全控制线内的土地开发利用，规划安全控制线范围内的建设项目应当满足安全风险控制要求。

**Article 20** An industrial park for the chemical industry shall maintain a safety distance from urban built-up areas, densely populated places, important facilities, sensitive targets, etc., that complies with the requirements of relevant laws, regulations and national standards.  
An industrial park for the chemical industry shall, in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws, regulations and national standards, formulate a safety control line for the planning of the surrounding area of the industrial park, submit it to the people's government of the city divided into districts at the municipal level where the industrial park is located, or the department authorized thereby, for determination, and then incorporate it into the local territorial space plan in accordance with the procedures.  
The competent departments of natural resources of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the municipal level and the people's government at the county level where the industrial park for the chemical industry is located shall strictly control the land development and utilization within the planning safety control line around the industrial park, and the construction projects within the scope of the planning safety control line shall meet the requirements for safety risk control.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强危险化学品运输安全保障，对涉及危险化学品储存、装卸、运输的物流园区、集中停车区域，以及高速公路服务区的加油站、加气站等进行规划。

**Article 21** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the safety of the transportation of hazardous chemicals, and shall plan logistics parks, centralized parking areas involved in the storage, loading and unloading, and transportation of hazardous chemicals, as well as gas stations and gas filling stations in highway service areas.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　危险化学品生产装置或者储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施，与下列场所、设施、区域的安全距离应当符合国家有关规定：  
（一）居住区以及商业中心、公园等人员密集场所；  
（二）学校、医院、影剧院、体育场馆等公共设施；  
（三）饮用水水源、水厂以及饮用水水源保护区；  
（四）车站、码头（依法经许可从事危险化学品装卸作业的除外）、机场以及通信干线、通信枢纽、铁路线路、道路交通干线、水路交通干线、地铁风亭以及地铁站出入口；  
（五）生态保护红线、自然保护地、永久基本农田、基本草原、种质资源库（场、区、圃）、畜禽规模化养殖场、渔业水域以及种子、种畜禽、水产苗种生产基地；  
（六）河流、湖泊、水库、海洋、重要调水输水线路、蓄滞洪区；  
（七）军事禁区、军事管理区以及有关军事设施；  
（八）核设施；  
（九）法律、行政法规规定的其他场所、设施、区域。  
已建的危险化学品生产装置或者储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施不符合前款规定的，由所在地设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门会同有关部门监督其所属单位在规定期限内进行整改；需要转产、停产、搬迁、关闭的，由本级人民政府决定并组织实施。  
储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施的选址，应当避开地震活动断层和容易发生洪灾、地质灾害、森林草原火灾的区域。

**Article 22** The safety distance between hazardous chemical production facilities or storage facilities where the storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source and the following places, facilities, and areas shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) residential areas, as well as densely populated places such as commercial centers and parks;  
(2) public facilities such as schools, hospitals, theaters, and sports venues;  
(3) drinking water sources, water plants, and drinking water source protection zones;  
(4) stations, docks (except those lawfully permitted to engage in hazardous chemical loading and unloading operations), airports, as well as communication trunk lines, communication hubs, railway lines, road traffic trunk lines, waterway traffic trunk lines, subway vents, and subway station entrances and exits;  
(5) ecological protection redlines, nature reserves, permanent basic farmland, basic grasslands, germplasm resource banks (fields, areas, nurseries), large-scale livestock and poultry farms, fishery waters, and production bases for seeds, breeding livestock and poultry, and aquatic fry and fingerlings;  
(6) rivers, lakes, reservoirs, oceans, important water diversion and transmission lines, and flood storage and detention areas;  
(7) military restricted zones, military administrative zones, and relevant military installations;  
(8) nuclear facilities;  
(9) other places, facilities, and areas prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where existing hazardous chemical production facilities or storage facilities where the storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source do not comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the emergency management department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts where the facilities are located shall, together with the relevant departments, supervise the entity to which the facilities belong to make rectifications within the prescribed time limit; where conversion, suspension, relocation, or closure is required, the people's government at the same level shall decide and organize the implementation.  
The site selection for hazardous chemical storage facilities where the storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source shall avoid seismic active fault zones and areas prone to flood, geological disasters, and forest and grassland fires.

### 第三章　生产和储存安全

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　危险化学品建设项目应当由应急管理部门进行安全条件审查。  
建设单位应当委托具备国家规定的资质条件的机构对危险化学品建设项目进行安全评价，并将安全评价情况报建设项目所在地设区的市级以上人民政府应急管理部门；应急管理部门应当自收到报告之日起二十个工作日内作出安全条件审查决定，并书面通知建设单位。具体办法由国务院应急管理部门制定。  
新建、改建、扩建储存、装卸危险化学品的港口建设项目（以下统称危险化学品港口建设项目），由港口行政管理部门按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定进行安全条件审查。  
承担安全评价等职责的机构的资质条件由国务院应急管理部门会同有关部门规定。

**Article 23** Safety condition review shall be conducted by the emergency management department for hazardous chemical construction projects.  
The construction unit shall entrust an institution with qualifications meeting the requirements prescribed by the State to conduct a safety assessment for the hazardous chemical construction project, and shall report the safety assessment situation to the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the level of the city divided into districts where the construction project is located; the emergency management department shall make a decision on the safety condition review within twenty working days from the date of receiving the report, and shall notify the construction unit in writing. The specific measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council.  
For port construction projects involving the storage or loading and unloading of hazardous chemicals that are newly built, renovated, or expanded (hereinafter collectively referred to as hazardous chemical port construction projects), the port administrative department shall conduct the safety condition review in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transportation of the State Council.  
The qualification conditions of institutions undertaking duties such as safety assessment shall be prescribed by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　危险化学品建设项目和危险化学品港口建设项目的安全设施，必须与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入生产和使用。安全设施投资应当纳入建设项目概算。

**Article 24** The safety facilities of hazardous chemical construction projects and hazardous chemical port construction projects must be designed, constructed, and put into production and use simultaneously with the main project. The investment in safety facilities shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　危险化学品建设项目和危险化学品港口建设项目安全设施的设计人、设计单位应当对安全设施设计负责。在安全设施设计中应当提出保障安全的措施建议。

**Article 25** The designer and design unit of safety facilities for hazardous chemical construction projects and hazardous chemical port construction projects shall be responsible for the design of the safety facilities. In the design of safety facilities, measures and suggestions for ensuring safety shall be proposed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　危险化学品建设项目的安全设施设计应当报所在地设区的市级以上人民政府应急管理部门审查。具体办法由国务院应急管理部门制定。  
危险化学品港口建设项目的安全设施设计，由港口行政管理部门按照国家有关规定进行审查。

**Article 26** The design of safety facilities for hazardous chemical construction projects shall be submitted to the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the level of the city divided into districts where the project is located for review. The specific measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council.  
The design of safety facilities for hazardous chemical port construction projects shall be reviewed by the port administrative department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　施工单位必须按照批准的安全设施设计施工，并对安全设施的工程质量和施工安全负责。实行工程监理的危险化学品建设项目，建设单位应当对安全设施施工一并委托监理。  
建设单位应当组织对安全设施进行验收，验收合格方可投入生产和使用。  
对危险化学品建设项目和危险化学品港口建设项目负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当加强对建设单位验收活动和验收结果的监督核查。

**Article 27** The construction unit must construct in accordance with the approved design of safety facilities, and shall be responsible for the project quality and construction safety of the safety facilities. For hazardous chemical construction projects where project supervision is implemented, the construction unit shall entrust supervision for the construction of safety facilities together.  
The construction unit shall organize the acceptance inspection of the safety facilities. Only after acceptance inspection and passing may they be put into production and use.  
The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of production safety for hazardous chemical construction projects and hazardous chemical port construction projects shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the construction unit's acceptance inspection activities and acceptance inspection results.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位在公共区域埋地、地面和架空的危险化学品输送管道及其附属设施的安全管理，应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和国家标准、行业标准的要求。  
生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当对其敷设的危险化学品管道设置明显标志，并对危险化学品管道定期检查、检测、巡护。  
进行可能危及危险化学品管道安全的施工作业，施工单位应当在开工的七日前将施工方案书面通知管道所属单位，并与管道所属单位共同制定应急预案，采取相应的安全防护措施。管道所属单位应当指派专门人员到现场进行管道安全保护指导。

**Article 28** The safety management of buried, ground, and overhead hazardous chemical transmission pipelines and their auxiliary facilities in public areas by units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the requirements of national standards and industry standards.  
Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall set up clear markings for the hazardous chemical pipelines they lay, and shall conduct regular inspection, testing, and patrol of the hazardous chemical pipelines.  
Where construction operations that may endanger the safety of hazardous chemical pipelines are carried out, the construction unit shall notify the pipeline owner unit in writing of the construction plan seven days before the start of construction, and shall jointly formulate an emergency response plan with the pipeline owner unit and take corresponding safety protection measures. The pipeline owner unit shall assign specialized personnel to the site to provide guidance on pipeline safety protection.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　危险化学品生产企业主要负责人（包括法定代表人、实际控制人、实际负责人）和安全生产管理人员应当具备相应的安全生产知识和管理能力，并经应急管理部门考核合格。  
危险化学品生产企业从业人员应当满足国家规定的学历要求，接受安全生产教育和培训，考核合格后上岗作业。  
危险化学品生产企业应当建立健全安全培训管理制度，定期组织培训，提高从业人员安全意识和安全生产技能水平。

**Article 29** The main responsible persons (including the legal representative, actual controller, and actual person in charge) and production safety management personnel of hazardous chemical production enterprises shall possess corresponding production safety knowledge and management capabilities, and shall pass the assessment of the emergency management department.  
The employees of hazardous chemical production enterprises shall meet the educational requirements prescribed by the State, receive production safety education and training, and take up their posts after passing the assessment.  
Hazardous chemical production enterprises shall establish and improve a safety training management system, organize regular training, and improve the safety awareness and production safety skill level of employees.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　危险化学品生产企业进行生产前，应当依照有关安全生产许可法律、行政法规的规定，取得危险化学品安全生产许可证。  
生产列入国家实行生产许可证制度的工业产品目录的危险化学品的企业，还应当依照有关工业产品生产许可法律、行政法规的规定，取得工业产品生产许可证。试生产的危险化学品，在取得工业产品生产许可证后，经具有相应资质的检验机构检验合格可以销售。  
负责颁发危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证的部门，应当将其颁发许可证的情况依照有关法律、行政法规的规定及时向社会公开。

**Article 30** Before commencing production, a hazardous chemical production enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on production safety permits, obtain a hazardous chemical production safety permit.  
An enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals listed in the catalogue of industrial products subject to the production licensing system of the State shall also, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on industrial product production permits, obtain an industrial product production permit. For trial-produced hazardous chemicals, after obtaining the industrial product production permit and passing the inspection by an inspection institution with corresponding qualifications, they may be sold.  
The department responsible for issuing hazardous chemical production safety permits and industrial product production permits shall promptly disclose to the public the circumstances of its issuance of permits in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　危险化学品生产企业、进口企业应当提供与其生产、进口的危险化学品相符的中文化学品安全技术说明书，并在危险化学品包装（包括外包装件，下同）上粘贴、印刷或者拴挂与包装内危险化学品相符的中文化学品安全标签。化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签应当符合国家标准的要求。  
危险化学品生产企业、进口企业发现其生产或者进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性的，应当立即公告，及时修订其化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签。

**Article 31** Hazardous chemical production enterprises and import enterprises shall provide a Chinese safety data sheet that matches the hazardous chemicals they produce or import, and shall affix, print, or attach a Chinese safety label that matches the hazardous chemicals in the packaging (including outer packaging, the same below) on the packaging. The safety data sheet and safety label shall comply with the requirements of national standards.  
If a hazardous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise discovers that the hazardous chemicals it produces or import have new hazardous characteristics, it shall immediately announce and promptly revise its safety data sheet and safety label.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　危险化学品的包装应当符合法律、行政法规、规章的规定和国家标准、行业标准的要求。  
危险化学品包装物、容器的材质以及危险化学品包装的型式、规格、方法和单件质量，应当与所包装的危险化学品的性质和用途相适应。

**Article 32** The packaging of hazardous chemicals shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and rules, and the requirements of national standards and industry standards.  
The materials of packaging materials and containers for hazardous chemicals, as well as the types, specifications, methods, and unit mass of packaging, shall be suitable for the nature and use of the hazardous chemicals being packaged.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　生产列入国家实行生产许可证制度的工业产品目录的危险化学品包装物、容器的企业，应当依照有关工业产品生产许可法律、行政法规的规定，取得工业产品生产许可证；其生产的危险化学品包装物、容器经具有相应资质的检验机构检验合格，方可出厂销售。  
运输危险化学品的船舶及其配载的容器，应当符合有关法律、行政法规、规章以及强制性标准和技术规范的要求，并经国家海事管理机构认定的船舶检验机构检验合格，方可投入使用。  
对重复使用的危险化学品包装物、容器，使用单位在重复使用前应当进行检查；发现存在安全隐患的，应当及时维修或者更换。使用单位应当对检查情况作出记录，记录的保存期限不得少于三年。

**Article 33** An enterprise that produces packaging materials and containers for hazardous chemicals listed in the catalogue of industrial products subject to the production licensing system of the State shall, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on industrial product production permits, obtain an industrial product production permit; the packaging materials and containers for hazardous chemicals it produces may be delivered from the factory and sold only after passing the inspection by an inspection institution with corresponding qualifications.  
Ships transporting hazardous chemicals and their containers used for loading shall comply with the requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, rules, and mandatory standards and technical specifications, and may be put into use only after passing the inspection by a ship inspection institution recognized by the national maritime administration.  
For reusable packaging materials and containers for hazardous chemicals, the using unit shall inspect them before reuse; if potential safety hazards are found, they shall be promptly repaired or replaced. The using unit shall make records of the inspection, and the retention period of the records shall not be less than three years.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　生产、储存危险化学品的企业应当建立安全风险分级管控制度，开展安全风险辨识评估，按照安全风险分级采取相应的管控措施。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业的工艺、设施、设备、原料等发生变更时应当重新进行安全风险辨识评估。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业不得使用国家明令淘汰或者禁止使用的危及生产安全的工艺、技术、设施、设备，具体目录由国务院应急管理部门会同有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 34** Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall establish a graded management and control system for safety risks, carry out safety risk identification and assessment, and adopt corresponding control measures according to the classification of safety risks.  
When processes, facilities, equipment, raw materials, etc. of enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals are changed, the safety risk identification and assessment shall be re-conducted.  
Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall not use processes, technologies, facilities, or equipment that are explicitly eliminated or prohibited by the State and that endanger production safety. The specific catalogues shall be formulated and published by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　生产、储存危险化学品的企业应当建立包括工艺操作、特殊作业、设备管理、储存条件、开停车和检维修、变更等全部生产作业环节在内的过程安全管理制度，明确责任人、岗位职责和操作规程，并组织有效实施。

**Article 35** Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall establish a process safety management system that includes all production operation links such as process operations, special operations, equipment management, storage conditions, start-up and shutdown and inspection and maintenance, changes, etc., shall specify the responsible persons, job responsibilities, and operating procedures, and shall organize effective implementation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　生产、储存危险化学品的企业应当按照国家标准或者行业标准装备自动控制系统和安全仪表系统，建立安全风险监测预警系统，并与政府有关部门实现互联互通。

**Article 36** Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall, in accordance with national standards or industry standards, equip automatic control systems and safety instrumented systems, establish a safety risk monitoring and early warning system, and achieve interconnection with relevant government departments.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当根据其生产、储存的危险化学品的种类和危险特性，在作业场所设置相应的监测、监控、通风、防晒、调温、防火、灭火、防爆、泄压、防毒、中和、防潮、防雷、防静电、防腐、防泄漏以及防护围堤或者隔离操作等安全设施、设备，并按照国家标准、行业标准或者国家有关规定对安全设施、设备进行经常性维护、保养，保证安全设施、设备的正常使用。  
生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当在其作业场所和安全设施、设备上设置明显的安全警示标志。

**Article 37** Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall, based on the types and hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals they produce or store, set up corresponding safety facilities and equipment such as monitoring, surveillance, ventilation, sun protection, temperature regulation, fire prevention, fire extinguishing, explosion prevention, pressure relief, poison prevention, neutralization, moisture prevention, lightning protection, static electricity prevention, corrosion prevention, leakage prevention, and protective embankments or isolation operations at the workplace, and shall conduct regular maintenance and upkeep of the safety facilities and equipment in accordance with national standards, industry standards, or relevant provisions of the State to ensure the normal use of the safety facilities and equipment.  
Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall set up clear safety warning signs at their workplaces and on safety facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当在其作业场所设置通信、报警装置，并保证处于适用状态。  
生产、储存危险化学品的单位，不得关闭、破坏直接关系生产安全的监控、报警、防护、救生设施、设备，或者以其他任何方式影响其正常使用，不得篡改、隐瞒、销毁其相关数据、信息。

**Article 38** Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall set up communication and alarm devices at their workplaces and ensure they are in usable condition.  
Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall not turn off or damage monitoring, alarm, protection, and lifesaving facilities and equipment directly related to production safety, or affect their normal use in any other way, and shall not tamper with, conceal, or destroy related data and information.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　生产、储存危险化学品的企业，应当委托具备国家规定的资质条件的机构，对本企业的安全生产条件每三年进行一次安全评价，提出安全评价报告。安全评价报告的内容应当包括对安全生产条件存在的问题进行整改的方案和整改完成后的结论性意见。安全评价报告应当按照规定向社会公开。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业，应当将安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况报所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门备案。  
在港区内储存、装卸危险化学品的企业，应当依照本条第一款要求进行安全评价，安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况按照规定报港口行政管理部门或者所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门备案。

**Article 39** Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall entrust an institution with qualifications prescribed by the State to conduct a safety assessment on their production safety conditions every three years and produce a safety assessment report. The content of the safety assessment report shall include a plan for rectifying problems existing in the production safety conditions and conclusive opinions after the completion of rectification. The safety assessment report shall be disclosed to the public in accordance with the provisions.  
Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall file the safety assessment report and the implementation status of the rectification plan with the emergency management department of the people's government at the county level where they are located.  
Enterprises that store or load and unload hazardous chemicals within port areas shall conduct safety assessments in accordance with the requirements of paragraph 1 of this Article, and shall file the safety assessment report and the implementation status of the rectification plan with the port administrative department or the emergency management department of the people's government at the county level where they are located in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　生产、储存剧毒化学品或者国务院公安部门规定的可用于制造爆炸物品的危险化学品（以下简称易制爆危险化学品）的单位，应当如实记录其生产、储存的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、流向，并采取必要的安全防范措施，防止剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗；发现剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗的，应当立即向当地公安机关报告；公安机关应当根据实际情况及时通报应急管理等有关部门。  
生产、储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位，应当设置治安保卫机构，配备专职治安保卫人员，并依法报公安机关备案。

**Article 40** Units that produce or store highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that may be used to manufacture explosives as prescribed by the public security department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as explosive-prone hazardous chemicals) shall truthfully record the varieties, quantities, and flows of the highly toxic chemicals and explosive-prone hazardous chemicals they produce or store, and shall take necessary safety preventive measures to prevent loss or theft of highly toxic chemicals and explosive-prone hazardous chemicals; if loss or theft of highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals is discovered, they shall immediately report to the local public security organ; the public security organ shall promptly notify the emergency management and other relevant departments according to the actual situation.  
Units that produce or store highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals shall set up a security protection institution, equip it with full-time security protection personnel, and file with the public security organ according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　危险化学品应当储存在专用仓库、专用场地或者专用储存室、储存专柜（以下统称专用储存场所）内，并由专人负责管理；剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品，应当在专用储存场所内单独存放，实行双人收发、双人保管制度，收发记录的保存期限不得少于三年。  
危险化学品的储存方式、方法以及储存数量应当符合国家标准或者国家有关规定。

**Article 41** Hazardous chemicals shall be stored in dedicated warehouses, dedicated sites, or dedicated storage rooms or cabinets (hereinafter collectively referred to as dedicated storage premises) and shall be managed by designated persons. Highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source shall be stored separately in dedicated storage premises, and a system of dual-person receipt and dual-person custody shall be implemented. The retention period for receipt and delivery records shall not be less than three years.  
The storage methods, means, and quantities of hazardous chemicals shall conform to national standards or relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　储存危险化学品的单位应当建立危险化学品出入库核查、登记制度。  
剧毒化学品储存单位应当将剧毒化学品的储存数量、储存地点以及管理人员的情况，报所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门、消防救援机构和公安机关备案；在港区内储存的，报港口行政管理部门、消防救援机构和公安机关备案。

**Article 42** Units that store hazardous chemicals shall establish a system for checking and registering the entry and exit of hazardous chemicals.  
Units storing highly toxic chemicals shall report the storage quantity, storage location, and information on management personnel of highly toxic chemicals to the emergency management department, fire rescue organization, and public security organ of the local county-level people's government for the record; if storage is within a port area, they shall report to the port administrative department, fire rescue organization, and public security organ for the record.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　危险化学品专用储存场所应当符合国家标准、行业标准的要求，并设置明显的标志。储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的专用储存场所，应当按照国家有关规定设置相应的实体防范、技术防范设施。  
储存危险化学品的单位应当对其危险化学品专用储存场所的安全设施、设备定期进行检测、检验；检测、检验不合格的，应当停止使用，并按照规定予以维修或者更换。

**Article 43** Dedicated storage premises for hazardous chemicals shall meet the requirements of national standards and industry standards and shall be provided with conspicuous signs. Dedicated storage premises for storing highly toxic chemicals and easy-explosive hazardous chemicals shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, be equipped with corresponding physical and technical prevention facilities.  
Units that store hazardous chemicals shall regularly conduct testing and inspection of the safety facilities and equipment in their dedicated hazardous chemical storage premises; if the testing or inspection results are unsatisfactory, such premises shall cease to be used and shall be repaired or replaced in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　研制开发单位进行危险化学品新工艺、新技术、新产品开发，应当加强研制开发过程的安全管理，确保研制开发过程安全，不得将未经小试、中试、工业化试验的新工艺、新技术直接用于工业化生产。  
研制开发单位转让危险化学品新工艺、新技术时，应当提供新工艺、新技术的安全论证报告及相关资料，并进行技术指导。

**Article 44** Research and development units conducting the development of new processes, technologies, or products for hazardous chemicals shall strengthen the safety management of the research and development process, ensure the safety of the research and development process, and shall not directly apply new processes or technologies that have not undergone laboratory-scale experiments, pilot-scale experiments, or industrial tests to industrial production.  
When transferring new processes or technologies for hazardous chemicals, research and development units shall provide a safety demonstration report and relevant materials for the new processes or technologies and provide technical guidance.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位整体或者部分转产、停产、停业、解散的，应当采取有效措施，及时、妥善处置其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存的危险化学品，不得丢弃危险化学品；处置方案应当报所在地县级人民政府应急管理、工业和信息化、生态环境部门和公安机关、消防救援机构备案。应急管理部门应当会同生态环境主管部门和公安机关、消防救援机构对处置情况进行监督检查，发现未按照规定处置的，应当责令其立即处置。

**Article 45** If a unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals completely or partially converts production, suspends production, ceases operation, or dissolves, it shall take effective measures to promptly and properly dispose of its hazardous chemical production equipment, storage facilities, and stored hazardous chemicals, and shall not discard hazardous chemicals. The disposal plan shall be reported to the emergency management, industry and information technology, ecological environment departments, public security organ, and fire rescue organization of the local county-level people's government for the record. The emergency management department shall, together with the competent ecological environment department, public security organ, and fire rescue organization, supervise and inspect the disposal situation; if it finds that disposal is not carried out in accordance with regulations, it shall order immediate disposal.

### 第四章　使用安全

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当依照职责分工，加强对企业、学校、科研机构、医疗机构、检测机构、检验机构等单位使用危险化学品的监督管理。

**Article 46** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen the supervision and management of the use of hazardous chemicals by enterprises, schools, scientific research institutions, medical institutions, testing institutions, inspection institutions, and other entities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　使用危险化学品的单位的使用条件（包括工艺）应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和国家标准、行业标准的要求，并根据所使用的危险化学品的种类、危险特性以及使用量和使用方式，建立健全使用危险化学品的安全管理规章制度和安全操作规程，保证危险化学品的安全使用。

**Article 47** The use conditions (including processes) of units that use hazardous chemicals shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the requirements of national standards and industry standards, and based on the type, hazardous characteristics, quantity used, and manner of use of the hazardous chemicals used, they shall establish and improve safety management regulations and safe operating procedures for the use of hazardous chemicals to ensure the safe use of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　使用国家规定种类的危险化学品从事生产并且使用量达到规定数量的化工企业（属于危险化学品生产企业的除外，下同），应当依照本法的规定取得危险化学品安全使用许可证。  
前款规定的危险化学品种类及其使用量的数量标准，由国务院应急管理部门会同公安、农业农村部门确定并公布。

**Article 48** Chemical enterprises that use the types of hazardous chemicals prescribed by the state for production and whose usage reaches the prescribed amount (excluding hazardous chemical production enterprises; the same below) shall obtain a hazardous chemicals safety use permit in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The types of hazardous chemicals prescribed in the preceding paragraph and the quantitative standards for their usage shall be determined and published by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the public security and agricultural and rural departments.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　申请危险化学品安全使用许可证的化工企业，除应当符合[本法第四十七条](#art-47)的规定外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与所使用的危险化学品相适应的专业技术人员，从业人员经考核合格；  
（二）有安全生产管理机构和专职安全生产管理人员；  
（三）有符合国家规定的危险化学品事故应急预案和必要的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资；  
（四）依法进行了安全评价；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 49** In addition to meeting the provisions of Article 47 of this Law, chemical enterprises applying for a hazardous chemicals safety use permit shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) having professional and technical personnel suitable for the hazardous chemicals used, with employees having passed the assessment;  
(2) having a work safety management organization and full-time work safety management personnel;  
(3) having a hazardous chemical accident emergency response plan that meets state regulations, as well as necessary emergency rescue equipment, apparatus, facilities, and materials;  
(4) having conducted a safety evaluation according to law; and  
(5) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　申请危险化学品安全使用许可证的化工企业，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门提出申请，并提交其符合[本法第四十九条](#art-49)规定条件的证明材料。设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门应当依法进行审查，自收到证明材料之日起二十个工作日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发危险化学品安全使用许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门应当将其颁发危险化学品安全使用许可证的情况依照有关法律、行政法规的规定及时向社会公开。

**Article 50** Chemical enterprises applying for a hazardous chemicals safety use permit shall submit an application to the emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level where they are located, and submit the supporting materials proving that they meet the conditions specified in Article 49 of this Law. The emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level shall conduct a review according to law and make a decision to approve or disapprove within twenty working days from the date of receiving the supporting materials. If approved, it shall issue a hazardous chemicals safety use permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level shall promptly disclose to the public the issuance of hazardous chemicals safety use permits in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　使用危险化学品的单位应当将其作业场所使用的化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签提供给从业人员，并加强培训教育，告知从业人员正确使用的方法和在紧急情况下应当采取的措施。  
使用危险化学品的个人，应当了解危险化学品的危险特性、正确使用方法和防护措施，不得违法使用、储存、处置危险化学品。

**Article 51** Units that use hazardous chemicals shall provide the chemical safety data sheets and chemical safety labels used at their workplaces to employees, and shall strengthen training and education, informing employees of the correct methods of use and the measures to be taken in emergencies.  
Individuals who use hazardous chemicals shall understand the hazardous characteristics, correct methods of use, and protective measures of hazardous chemicals, and shall not illegally use, store, or dispose of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　[本法第三十七条](#art-37)、第三十八条、第四十条第一款、第四十五条关于生产、储存危险化学品的单位的规定，适用于使用危险化学品的单位；第三十四条、第三十五条、第三十六条、第三十九条关于生产、储存危险化学品的企业的规定，适用于使用危险化学品从事生产的企业。

**Article 52** The provisions of Articles 37, 38, paragraph 1 of Article 40, and Article 45 of this Law concerning units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall apply to units that use hazardous chemicals; the provisions of Articles 34, 35, 36, and 39 concerning enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall apply to enterprises that use hazardous chemicals for production.

### 第五章　经营安全

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家对危险化学品经营（包括仓储经营，下同）实行许可制度。未经许可，任何单位和个人不得经营危险化学品。危险化学品经营企业不得向未经许可从事危险化学品生产、经营活动的企业采购危险化学品。  
依法设立的危险化学品生产企业在其厂区范围内销售本企业生产的危险化学品，不需要取得危险化学品经营许可。  
依照《中华人民共和国港口法》的规定取得港口经营许可证的港口经营人，在港区内从事危险化学品仓储经营，不需要取得危险化学品经营许可。

**Article 53** The State implements a licensing system for the operation of hazardous chemicals (including warehousing operations; the same below). Without a license, no entity or individual may engage in the operation of hazardous chemicals. Hazardous chemical operating enterprises shall not purchase hazardous chemicals from enterprises that engage in hazardous chemical production or operation activities without a license.  
A hazardous chemical production enterprise established according to law that sells the hazardous chemicals it produces within its plant area does not need to obtain a hazardous chemical operation license.  
A port operator that has obtained a port operation license in accordance with the provisions of the Port Law of the People's Republic of China and engages in the warehousing operation of hazardous chemicals within a port area does not need to obtain a hazardous chemical operation license.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　从事危险化学品经营的企业应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合国家标准、行业标准的经营场所，储存危险化学品的，还应当有符合国家标准、行业标准的储存设施；  
（二）从业人员经过专业技术培训并经考核合格；  
（三）有健全的安全管理规章制度；  
（四）有专职安全生产管理人员，主要负责人和安全生产管理人员具备与本企业危险化学品经营活动相适应的安全生产知识和管理能力，经应急管理部门考核合格；  
（五）有符合国家规定的危险化学品事故应急预案和必要的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资；  
（六）法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 54** Enterprises engaged in the operation of hazardous chemicals shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having business premises that conform to national standards and industry standards, and if storing hazardous chemicals, also having storage facilities that conform to national standards and industry standards;  
(2) employees have undergone professional technical training and passed the assessment;  
(3) having sound safety management regulations;  
(4) having full-time work safety management personnel, with the principal responsible person and work safety management personnel possessing the work safety knowledge and management capabilities commensurate with the enterprise's hazardous chemical operation activities, and having passed the assessment by the emergency management department;  
(5) having a hazardous chemical accident emergency plan that meets state regulations and necessary emergency rescue equipment, apparatus, facilities, and materials;  
(6) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　从事剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品经营的企业，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门提出申请。从事其他危险化学品经营的企业，应当向所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门提出申请；有储存设施且储存数量构成重大危险源的，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门提出申请。申请人应当提交其符合[本法第五十四条](#art-54)规定条件的证明材料。设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门或者县级人民政府应急管理部门应当依法进行审查，并按照规定对申请人的经营场所、储存设施进行现场核查。自收到证明材料之日起二十个工作日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发危险化学品经营许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
设区的市级人民政府应急管理部门和县级人民政府应急管理部门应当将其颁发危险化学品经营许可证的情况依照有关法律、行政法规的规定及时向社会公开。  
申请人持危险化学品经营许可证向市场监督管理部门办理登记手续后，方可从事危险化学品经营活动。法律、行政法规或者国务院规定经营危险化学品还需要经其他有关部门许可的，申请人向市场监督管理部门办理登记手续时应当持相应的许可证件。

**Article 55** Enterprises engaged in the operation of highly toxic chemicals or easy-explosive hazardous chemicals shall submit an application to the emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level where they are located. Enterprises engaged in the operation of other hazardous chemicals shall submit an application to the emergency management department of the county-level people's government where they are located; if they have storage facilities and the storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source, they shall submit an application to the emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level where they are located. The applicant shall submit supporting materials proving that it meets the conditions specified in Article 54 of this Law. The emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level or the county-level people's government shall conduct a review according to law and conduct an on-site inspection of the applicant's business premises and storage facilities in accordance with regulations. It shall make a decision to approve or disapprove within twenty working days from the date of receiving the supporting materials. If approved, it shall issue a hazardous chemical operation license; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The emergency management department of the people's government at the city divided into districts level and the county-level people's government shall promptly disclose to the public the issuance of hazardous chemical operation licenses in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The applicant may engage in hazardous chemical operation activities only after completing registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department by presenting the hazardous chemical operation license. If laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide that the operation of hazardous chemicals also requires permission from other relevant departments, the applicant shall present the corresponding license documents when completing registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　危险化学品经营企业储存危险化学品的，应当遵守本法第三章关于储存危险化学品的规定。危险化学品商店内只能存放民用小包装的危险化学品。

**Article 56** If a hazardous chemical operating enterprise stores hazardous chemicals, it shall comply with the provisions of Chapter III of this Law on the storage of hazardous chemicals. Only civil small-package hazardous chemicals may be stored in hazardous chemical stores.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业在销售危险化学品时，应当向购买单位或者个人提供符合法律、行政法规规定和国家标准、行业标准要求的化学品安全技术说明书，不得经营没有化学品安全技术说明书或者化学品安全标签的危险化学品，不得擅自更改化学品安全技术说明书或者化学品安全标签。  
任何单位和个人购买危险化学品时，有权向危险化学品生产企业、经营企业索取有关危险化学品的安全技术说明书，了解其危险特性、防护措施和使用方法。

**Article 57** When selling hazardous chemicals, hazardous chemical production enterprises and operating enterprises shall provide the purchasing entity or individual with a chemical safety data sheet that meets the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, national standards, and industry standards. They shall not operate hazardous chemicals without a chemical safety data sheet or chemical safety label, and shall not arbitrarily alter the chemical safety data sheet or chemical safety label.  
Any entity or individual purchasing hazardous chemicals has the right to request from hazardous chemical production enterprises and operating enterprises the safety data sheet of the relevant hazardous chemicals, in order to understand their hazardous characteristics, protective measures, and methods of use.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　依法取得危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品安全使用许可证或者危险化学品经营许可证的企业，凭相应的许可证件购买剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品。民用爆炸物品、烟花爆竹、武器装备科研生产企业凭相应许可证购买易制爆危险化学品。  
前款规定以外的单位购买剧毒化学品的，应当向所在地县级人民政府公安机关申请取得剧毒化学品购买许可证；购买易制爆危险化学品的，应当持本单位出具的合法用途说明。  
个人不得购买剧毒化学品（属于剧毒化学品的农药除外）和易制爆危险化学品（含有易制爆危险化学品的食品添加剂、药品、兽药、消毒剂等生活用品除外）。

**Article 58** Enterprises that have lawfully obtained a hazardous chemical safety production license, hazardous chemical safety use permit, or hazardous chemical operation license may purchase highly toxic chemicals and easy-explosive hazardous chemicals by presenting the corresponding license documents. Enterprises producing civil explosives, fireworks and firecrackers, or weapons and equipment for scientific research may purchase easy-explosive hazardous chemicals by presenting the corresponding licenses.  
Entities other than those specified in the preceding paragraph that purchase highly toxic chemicals shall apply to the public security organ of the county-level people's government where they are located for a highly toxic chemical purchase permit; those purchasing easy-explosive hazardous chemicals shall present a statement of lawful use issued by their own unit.  
Individuals shall not purchase highly toxic chemicals (except for pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or easy-explosive hazardous chemicals (except for food additives, drugs, veterinary drugs, disinfectants, and other daily necessities containing easy-explosive hazardous chemicals).

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　申请取得剧毒化学品购买许可证，申请人应当向所在地县级人民政府公安机关提交下列材料：  
（一）营业执照或者法人证书（登记证书）的复印件；  
（二）拟购买的剧毒化学品品种、数量的说明；  
（三）购买剧毒化学品用途的说明；  
（四）经办人的身份证明。  
县级人民政府公安机关应当自收到前款规定的材料之日起三个工作日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发剧毒化学品购买许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
剧毒化学品购买许可管理按照国务院公安部门的规定执行。

**Article 59** To apply for a highly toxic chemical purchase permit, the applicant shall submit the following materials to the public security organ of the county-level people's government where it is located:  
(1) a copy of the business license or legal person certificate (registration certificate);  
(2) a statement of the types and quantities of highly toxic chemicals to be purchased;  
(3) a statement of the purpose of purchasing highly toxic chemicals; and  
(4) the identification document of the handling person.  
The public security organ of the county-level people's government shall, within three working days from the date of receiving the materials specified in the preceding paragraph, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approved, it shall issue a highly toxic chemical purchase permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The management of highly toxic chemical purchase permits shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，应当查验[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件，不得向不具有相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品。对持剧毒化学品购买许可证购买剧毒化学品的，应当按照许可证载明的品种、数量销售。  
禁止向个人销售剧毒化学品（属于剧毒化学品的农药除外）和易制爆危险化学品（含有易制爆危险化学品的食品添加剂、药品、兽药、消毒剂等生活用品除外）。

**Article 60** When selling highly toxic chemicals or easy-explosive hazardous chemicals, hazardous chemical production enterprises and operating enterprises shall verify the relevant license documents or certification documents as specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 58 of this Law, and shall not sell highly toxic chemicals or easy-explosive hazardous chemicals to entities that do not possess the relevant license documents or certification documents. For those purchasing highly toxic chemicals with a highly toxic chemical purchase permit, sales shall be made according to the types and quantities specified in the permit.  
It shall be prohibited to sell highly toxic chemicals (except for pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or easy-explosive hazardous chemicals (except for food additives, drugs, veterinary drugs, disinfectants, and other daily necessities containing easy-explosive hazardous chemicals) to individuals.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，应当如实记录购买单位的名称、地址，经办人的姓名、身份证号码以及所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、用途。销售记录以及经办人的身份证明复印件、相关许可证件复印件或者证明文件的保存期限不得少于三年。  
剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的销售企业、购买单位应当在销售、购买后三日内，将所销售、购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量以及流向信息报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案，并录入计算机系统。

**Article 61** An enterprise that produces or deals in hazardous chemicals and sells highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives shall truthfully record the name and address of the purchasing entity, the name and identity card number of the person handling the transaction, and the type, quantity, and purpose of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives purchased. The retention period for sale records and copies of the identity certificate of the person handling the transaction, copies of relevant permits, or certifying documents shall not be less than three years.  
The enterprise selling highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives and the purchasing entity shall, within three days after the sale or purchase, report the type, quantity, and flow direction information of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives sold or purchased to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level for the record, and enter such information into the computer system.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位不得出借、转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品；因转产、停产、搬迁、关闭等确需转让的，应当向具有[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位转让，并在转让后三日内将所转让的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品品种、数量以及流向信息报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 62** An entity that uses highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives shall not lend or transfer the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives it has purchased. If a transfer is truly necessary due to product conversion, cessation of production, relocation, closure, or other reasons, it shall transfer them to an entity that possesses the relevant permits or certifying documents as specified in Article 58, paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Law, and shall, within three days after the transfer, report the type, quantity, and flow direction information of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives transferred to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level for the record.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　禁止在互联网上销售、购买剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品。

**Article 63** The sale or purchase of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives on the Internet is prohibited.

### 第六章　运输安全

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　通过道路、水路运输按照危险货物管理的危险化学品，应当遵守本法和有关法律、行政法规、国务院交通运输主管部门关于危险货物道路、水路运输安全的规定。

**Article 64** The transport of hazardous chemicals that are managed as dangerous goods by road or waterway shall comply with this Law and the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the competent department of transport under the State Council regarding the safety of dangerous goods transport by road or waterway.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　从事危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的，应当向市场监督管理部门办理登记手续，并分别依照有关道路运输、水路运输的法律、行政法规的规定，取得危险货物道路运输许可、相应危险货物水路运输许可或者办理备案手续。  
通过道路、水路运输危险化学品，应当由依法取得相应许可或者办理备案手续的运输企业承运，其他单位和个人不得承运。托运人应当委托依法取得相应许可或者办理备案手续的运输企业承运，不得委托其他单位和个人承运。其他单位和个人的危险化学品运输车辆不得挂靠经营。  
危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业应当配备专职安全生产管理人员。  
经妥善处理后可以按照普通货物管理的危险化学品按照普通货物运输，具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门制定。

**Article 65** An entity engaged in the road transport or waterway transport of hazardous chemicals shall undergo registration procedures with the market regulatory department, and shall obtain a permit for the road transport of dangerous goods or a corresponding permit for the waterway transport of dangerous goods, or undergo filing procedures, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on road transport or waterway transport, respectively.  
The transport of hazardous chemicals by road or waterway shall be carried by a transport enterprise that has obtained the corresponding permit or undergone filing procedures according to law; no other entity or individual may carry out such transport. The consignor shall entrust a transport enterprise that has obtained the corresponding permit or undergone filing procedures according to law, and shall not entrust any other entity or individual. The hazardous chemicals transport vehicles of other entities or individuals shall not be operated under the name of another enterprise (挂靠经营).  
A hazardous chemicals road transport enterprise or waterway transport enterprise shall assign full-time safety production management personnel.  
Hazardous chemicals that can be managed as ordinary goods after proper treatment may be transported as ordinary goods. The specific measures therefor shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业的驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员应当经交通运输主管部门考核合格，取得从业资格。具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门制定。  
危险化学品的装卸作业应当遵守安全作业标准、规程和制度，并在装卸管理人员的现场指挥或者监控下进行。水路运输危险化学品的集装箱装箱作业应当在集装箱装箱现场检查员的指挥或者监控下进行，并符合积载、隔离的规范和要求；装箱作业完毕后，集装箱装箱现场检查员应当签署装箱证明书。

**Article 66** Drivers, crew members, loading and unloading management personnel, escorts, declarants, and on-site container packing inspectors of hazardous chemicals road transport enterprises or waterway transport enterprises shall pass the examination conducted by the competent department of transport and obtain professional qualifications. The specific measures therefor shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council.  
The loading and unloading operations of hazardous chemicals shall comply with safe operation standards, procedures, and rules, and shall be conducted under the on-site direction or monitoring of loading and unloading management personnel. Container packing operations for hazardous chemicals transported by waterway shall be conducted under the direction or monitoring of the on-site container packing inspector, and shall conform to the specifications and requirements for stowage and segregation. After the packing operations are completed, the on-site container packing inspector shall sign the container packing certificate.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　运输危险化学品，应当根据危险化学品的危险特性采取相应的安全防护措施，并配备必要的防护用品和应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资。  
用于运输危险化学品的槽罐以及其他容器应当封口严密，能够防止危险化学品在运输过程中因温度、湿度或者压力的变化发生渗漏、洒漏；槽罐以及其他容器的溢流和泄压装置应当设置准确、启闭灵活。  
运输危险化学品，驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员应当熟悉所运输的危险化学品的危险特性及其包装物、容器的使用要求，掌握出现危险情况时的应急处置方法。

**Article 67** When transporting hazardous chemicals, corresponding safety protection measures shall be taken based on the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, and necessary protective equipment and emergency rescue equipment, apparatus, devices, and materials shall be provided.  
Tank containers and other containers used for transporting hazardous chemicals shall be tightly sealed to prevent leakage or spillage of hazardous chemicals due to changes in temperature, humidity, or pressure during transport. The overflow and pressure relief devices of tank containers and other containers shall be set accurately and operate flexibly.  
When transporting hazardous chemicals, drivers, crew members, loading and unloading management personnel, escorts, declarants, and on-site container packing inspectors shall be familiar with the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals being transported and the usage requirements for their packaging and containers, and shall master the emergency response methods in the event of a dangerous situation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　通过道路运输危险化学品，应当按照运输车辆的核定载质量装载危险化学品，不得超载。  
危险化学品运输车辆应当符合国家标准要求的安全技术条件，并按照国家有关规定定期进行安全技术检验。  
危险化学品运输车辆应当悬挂或者喷涂符合国家标准要求的警示标志，不得遮挡、拆除。  
危险化学品运输车辆应当安装符合国家标准、行业标准要求的卫星定位监控装置并确保处于良好运行状态，不得拆除、关闭或者采取屏蔽信号等方式影响其正常运行，不得删除、篡改监控数据。

**Article 68** When transporting hazardous chemicals by road, the loading of hazardous chemicals shall be in accordance with the rated loading capacity of the transport vehicle, and overloading shall not be permitted.  
Hazardous chemicals transport vehicles shall meet the safety technical conditions required by national standards, and shall undergo regular safety technical inspections in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
Hazardous chemicals transport vehicles shall display or have painted on them warning signs that meet the requirements of national standards, and such signs shall not be obscured or removed.  
Hazardous chemicals transport vehicles shall be equipped with satellite positioning and monitoring devices that meet the requirements of national standards and industry standards, and shall ensure that such devices are in good working order. Such devices shall not be removed, disabled, or affected in their normal operation by means such as signal shielding, and the monitoring data shall not be deleted or tampered with.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　通过道路运输危险化学品，应当按照要求配备押运人员，并保证所运输的危险化学品处于押运人员的监控之下。具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
危险化学品道路运输企业应当对运输车辆、驾驶人员的作业状态进行实时监控、管理，及时纠正超速行驶、疲劳驾驶、不按规定线路行驶等违法违规驾驶行为，实现安全监测、监控和预警。运输危险化学品车辆的驾驶人员应当严格遵守道路交通安全法律法规，日间连续驾驶时间不得超过四小时，夜间连续驾驶时间不得超过二小时，每次停车休息时间不少于二十分钟。  
运输危险化学品途中需要停车的，驾驶人员、押运人员应当采取相应的安全防范措施；运输剧毒化学品或者易制爆危险化学品因住宿或者发生影响正常运输的情况，需要较长时间停车的，还应当向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 69** When transporting hazardous chemicals by road, escorts shall be assigned as required, and the hazardous chemicals being transported shall be kept under the monitoring of the escorts. The specific measures therefor shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.  
A hazardous chemicals road transport enterprise shall conduct real-time monitoring and management of the operational status of transport vehicles and drivers, and shall promptly rectify illegal or irregular driving behaviors such as speeding, fatigue driving, and driving not in accordance with the prescribed route, so as to achieve safety monitoring, surveillance, and early warning. Drivers of vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals shall strictly observe the road traffic safety laws and regulations; the continuous driving time during the daytime shall not exceed four hours, and the continuous driving time during the nighttime shall not exceed two hours, with each stop for rest being not less than twenty minutes.  
When a stop is necessary during the transport of hazardous chemicals, the driver and escort shall take corresponding safety precautions. If a longer stop is required for accommodation or due to circumstances affecting normal transport when transporting highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives, the local public security organ shall also be notified.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　未经公安机关批准，运输危险化学品的车辆不得进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域。危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域由县级人民政府公安机关划定或者会同有关部门划定，并设置明显的标志。  
运输危险化学品的车辆进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行区域许可管理按照国务院公安部门的规定执行。

**Article 70** Without the approval of the public security organ, vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals shall not enter areas where the passage of hazardous chemicals transport vehicles is restricted. The areas where the passage of hazardous chemicals transport vehicles is restricted shall be delineated by the public security organ of the people's government at the county level, or jointly with relevant departments, and shall be marked with conspicuous signs.  
The permit management for vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals to enter areas where the passage of hazardous chemicals transport vehicles is restricted shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　通过道路运输剧毒化学品，托运人应当向运输始发地或者目的地县级人民政府公安机关申请剧毒化学品道路运输通行证。  
申请剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，托运人应当向县级人民政府公安机关提交下列材料：  
（一）拟运输的剧毒化学品品种、数量的说明；  
（二）运输始发地、目的地、运输时间和运输路线的说明；  
（三）承运人取得相应危险货物道路运输许可、运输车辆取得相应营运证以及驾驶人员、押运人员取得相应上岗资格的证明文件；  
（四）[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的购买剧毒化学品的相关许可证件，或者海关出具的进出口证明文件。  
县级人民政府公安机关应当自收到前款规定的材料之日起七个工作日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发剧毒化学品道路运输通行证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
剧毒化学品道路运输通行证管理按照国务院公安部门的规定执行。

**Article 71** When transporting highly toxic chemicals by road, the consignor shall apply to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level of the place of departure or destination for a road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals.  
To apply for a road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals, the consignor shall submit the following materials to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level:  
(1) a description of the type and quantity of the highly toxic chemicals to be transported;  
(2) a description of the place of departure, place of destination, transport time, and transport route;  
(3) documentary evidence that the carrier has obtained the corresponding permit for the road transport of dangerous goods, that the transport vehicle has obtained the corresponding operating certificate, and that the driver and escort have obtained the corresponding qualification certificates;  
(4) the relevant permits for purchasing highly toxic chemicals as specified in Article 58, paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Law, or the import/export certifying documents issued by the customs.  
The public security organ of the people's government at the county level shall, within seven working days from the date of receipt of the materials specified in the preceding paragraph, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approval is granted, it shall issue a road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals; if not, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons therefor.  
The management of the road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品在道路运输途中丢失、被盗、被抢或者出现流散、泄漏等情况的，驾驶人员、押运人员应当立即采取相应的警示措施和安全措施，并向当地公安机关报告。公安机关接到报告后，应当根据实际情况立即向交通运输、应急管理、生态环境、卫生健康部门通报。有关部门应当采取必要的应急处置措施。

**Article 72** If highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are susceptible to becoming explosives are lost, stolen, or robbed, or if they spill or leak during road transport, the driver and escort shall immediately take corresponding warning and safety measures, and shall report to the local public security organ. Upon receiving the report, the public security organ shall, based on the actual circumstances, immediately notify the departments of transport, emergency management, ecology and environment, and health. The relevant departments shall take necessary emergency response measures.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　海事管理机构应当根据危险化学品的种类和危险特性，确定船舶运输危险化学品的安全运输条件。  
拟交付船舶运输的化学品的安全运输条件不明确的，货物所有人或者代理人应当委托相关技术机构进行评估，明确相关安全运输条件并经海事管理机构确认后，方可交付船舶运输。

**Article 73** The maritime administrative agency shall, based on the type and hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, determine the safe transport conditions for the transport of hazardous chemicals by ship.  
If the safe transport conditions for a chemical to be delivered for transport by ship are not clear, the cargo owner or its agent shall entrust a relevant technical institution to conduct an assessment, and only after the relevant safe transport conditions are clarified and confirmed by the maritime administrative agency may the chemical be delivered for transport by ship.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　禁止通过内河封闭水域运输剧毒化学品。  
禁止通过内河水域运输国家规定禁止通过内河运输的危险化学品。禁止通过内河运输的危险化学品的范围，由国务院交通运输主管部门会同生态环境、应急管理、工业和信息化部门，根据危险化学品的危险特性、危险化学品对人体和水环境的危害程度以及消除危害后果的难易程度等因素规定并公布。

**Article 74** The transport of highly toxic chemicals through enclosed inland waters is prohibited.  
The transport through inland waters of hazardous chemicals that are prohibited by state regulations from being transported through inland waters is prohibited. The scope of hazardous chemicals prohibited from being transported through inland waters shall be prescribed and published by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the departments of ecology and environment, emergency management, and industry and information technology, based on factors such as the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, the degree of harm to the human body and the water environment, and the ease of eliminating the harmful consequences.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国务院交通运输主管部门应当根据危险化学品的危险特性，对通过内河运输[本法第七十四条](#art-74)规定以外的危险化学品（以下简称通过内河运输危险化学品）实行分类管理，对各类危险化学品的运输方式、包装规范和安全防护措施等分别作出规定并监督实施。

**Article 75** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall, based on the hazardous characteristics of hazardous chemicals, implement classified management for the transport through inland waters of hazardous chemicals other than those specified in Article 74 of this Law (hereinafter referred to as the transport of hazardous chemicals through inland waters), and shall separately prescribe the transport mode, packaging specifications, and safety protection measures for each category of hazardous chemicals and supervise their implementation.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　通过内河运输危险化学品，应当使用依法取得危险货物适装证书的运输船舶。水路运输企业应当针对所运输的危险化学品的危险特性，制定运输船舶危险化学品事故应急救援预案，并为运输船舶配备充足、有效的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资。  
通过内河运输危险化学品的船舶，其所有人或者经营人应当取得船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明。船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明的副本应当随船携带。

**Article 76** When transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waters, transport ships that have lawfully obtained a certificate of fitness for loading dangerous goods shall be used. A waterway transport enterprise shall, based on the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals being transported, formulate an emergency rescue plan for hazardous chemicals accidents on transport ships, and shall equip the transport ships with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment, apparatus, devices, and materials.  
For a ship transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waters, the owner or operator shall obtain a ship pollution damage liability insurance certificate or a financial guarantee certificate. A copy of the ship pollution damage liability insurance certificate or financial guarantee certificate shall be carried on board the ship.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　通过内河运输危险化学品，危险化学品包装物的材质、型式、强度以及包装方法应当符合水路运输相关包装规范的要求。国务院交通运输主管部门对单船运输的危险化学品数量有限制性规定的，承运人应当按照规定安排运输数量。

**Article 77** When transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waters, the material, type, strength, and packaging method of the hazardous chemicals packaging shall comply with the requirements of the relevant packaging specifications for waterway transport. If the competent department of transport under the State Council has restrictive provisions on the quantity of hazardous chemicals transported by a single ship, the carrier shall arrange the transport quantity in accordance with such provisions.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位应当符合国家有关安全规范，与饮用水取水口保持国家规定的安全距离。有关管理单位应当制定码头、泊位危险化学品事故应急预案，并为码头、泊位配备充足、有效的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资。  
用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位，按照国家有关规定验收合格后方可投入使用。

**Article 78** Inland river docks and berths used for hazardous chemicals transport operations shall comply with relevant national safety specifications and shall maintain a safe distance from drinking water intakes as prescribed by the state. The relevant management entity shall formulate an emergency rescue plan for hazardous chemicals accidents at the docks and berths, and shall equip the docks and berths with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment, apparatus, devices, and materials.  
Inland river docks and berths used for hazardous chemicals transport operations may only be put into use after passing acceptance inspection in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　船舶载运危险化学品进出内河港口，应当依照法律、行政法规的规定向海事管理机构报告。海事管理机构接到报告后，应当在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，同时通报港口行政管理部门。  
在内河港口内进行危险化学品的装卸、过驳作业，应当将危险化学品的名称、危险特性、包装和作业的时间、地点等事项报告港口行政管理部门。港口行政管理部门接到报告后，应当在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，同时通报海事管理机构。  
载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，通过通航建筑物的，应当符合通航建筑物安全管理有关要求，并提前向通航建筑物运行单位报告。运行单位应当及时转报交通运输主管部门。载运危险化学品的船舶通过通航建筑物时，应当接受交通运输主管部门的管理。

**Article 79** When a ship carrying hazardous chemicals enters or leaves an inland river port, it shall report to the maritime administrative agency in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations. Upon receiving the report, the maritime administrative agency shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of transport under the State Council, decide whether to grant approval, notify the reporting party, and simultaneously notify the port administrative department.  
When conducting loading, unloading, or transshipment operations of hazardous chemicals within an inland river port, matters such as the name, hazardous characteristics, and packaging of the hazardous chemicals, as well as the time and place of the operation, shall be reported to the port administrative department. Upon receiving the report, the port administrative department shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent department of transport under the State Council, decide whether to grant approval, notify the reporting party, and simultaneously notify the maritime administrative agency.  
When a ship carrying hazardous chemicals navigates through inland waters and passes through a navigation structure, it shall comply with the relevant safety management requirements for navigation structures and shall report in advance to the entity operating the navigation structure. The operating entity shall promptly forward the report to the competent department of transport. When a ship carrying hazardous chemicals passes through a navigation structure, it shall accept the management of the competent department of transport.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行、装卸或者停泊，应当悬挂专用的警示标志，按照规定显示专用信号。  
载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，依照法律、行政法规以及国务院交通运输主管部门的规定需要引航的，应当申请引航。

**Article 80** A ship carrying hazardous chemicals, when navigating, loading, unloading, or mooring on inland waters, shall display special warning signs and exhibit special signals as required.  
A ship carrying hazardous chemicals navigating on inland waters shall, if pilotage is required in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council, apply for pilotage.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家其他有关饮用水水源和自然保护地保护的规定。内河航道发展规划应当与依法经批准的饮用水水源保护区划定方案和自然保护地总体规划相协调。

**Article 81** Vessels carrying dangerous chemicals navigating on inland rivers shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations and other state provisions concerning the protection of drinking water sources and natural reserves. The development plan for inland waterways shall be coordinated with the approved delimitation plan for drinking water source protection zones and the overall plan for natural reserves in accordance with law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　托运危险化学品，托运人应当向承运人提交电子或者纸质的危险货物托运清单，说明所托运的危险化学品的种类、数量、危险特性以及发生危险情况的应急处置措施，提供化学品安全技术说明书，并按照国家有关规定对所托运的危险化学品妥善包装，在危险化学品包装上粘贴、印刷或者拴挂与包装内危险化学品相符的化学品安全标签，并保证随时可查。  
运输危险化学品需要添加抑制剂或者稳定剂的，托运人应当按照规定添加，并将有关情况告知承运人。  
装货人应当在充装或者装载危险化学品前履行查验责任，并按照标准要求进行充装、装载作业。

**Article 82** When consigning dangerous chemicals for carriage, the consignor shall submit to the carrier an electronic or paper dangerous goods consignment manifest, stating the type, quantity, dangerous characteristics of the dangerous chemicals consigned and the emergency response measures in case of danger, provide a safety data sheet, and package the consigned dangerous chemicals properly in accordance with the relevant state provisions, affix, print or attach on the dangerous chemicals packaging a chemical safety label consistent with the dangerous chemicals in the packaging, and ensure that it is available for inspection at any time.  
Where an inhibitor or stabilizer needs to be added for the transport of dangerous chemicals, the consignor shall add it in accordance with the provisions and inform the carrier of the relevant circumstances.  
The loader shall perform the inspection responsibility before filling or loading dangerous chemicals and carry out the filling and loading operations in accordance with the standard requirements.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　托运人不得在托运的普通货物中夹带危险化学品，不得将危险化学品匿报或者谎报为普通货物托运。  
任何单位和个人不得交寄危险化学品或者在邮件、快件内夹带危险化学品，不得将危险化学品匿报或者谎报为普通物品交寄。邮政企业、快递企业不得收寄危险化学品。  
对涉嫌违反本条第一款、第二款规定的，交通运输主管部门、邮政管理部门可以依法开拆，并通过取样检验等多种方式开展查验。

**Article 83** The consignor shall not conceal dangerous chemicals in ordinary goods consigned for carriage, nor misrepresent or falsely declare dangerous chemicals as ordinary goods for carriage.  
No unit or individual may send dangerous chemicals by post or conceal dangerous chemicals in mails or express items, nor misrepresent or falsely declare dangerous chemicals as ordinary articles for posting. Postal enterprises and courier enterprises shall not accept dangerous chemicals for delivery.  
Where any suspected violation of the provisions of the first or second paragraph of this Article occurs, the competent department of transport or the postal administration department may in accordance with law open the items and carry out inspection through various means including sampling and testing.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　通过铁路、航空运输危险化学品的安全管理，依照有关铁路、航空运输的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行。

**Article 84** The safety management of dangerous chemicals transported by railway or air shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules on railway or air transport.

### 第七章　危险化学品登记

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　国家实行危险化学品登记制度，为危险化学品安全管理以及危险化学品事故预防和应急救援提供技术、信息支持。

**Article 85** The state applies a dangerous chemicals registration system to provide technical and information support for the safety management of dangerous chemicals and the prevention of and emergency rescue for dangerous chemicals accidents.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　危险化学品生产企业、进口企业应当向国务院应急管理部门负责危险化学品登记的机构（以下简称危险化学品登记机构）办理危险化学品登记。  
危险化学品登记包括下列内容：  
（一）分类和标签信息；  
（二）物理、化学性质；  
（三）主要用途；  
（四）危险特性；  
（五）储存、使用、运输的安全要求；  
（六）出现危险情况的应急处置措施。  
对同一企业生产、进口的同一品种的危险化学品，不进行重复登记。危险化学品生产企业、进口企业发现其生产、进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性或者其他登记内容发生变更的，应当及时向危险化学品登记机构办理登记内容变更手续。  
危险化学品登记的具体办法由国务院应急管理部门制定。

**Article 86** Manufacturers and importers of dangerous chemicals shall register with the institution responsible for dangerous chemicals registration under the emergency management department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the dangerous chemicals registration institution).  
The registration of dangerous chemicals includes the following:  
(1) classification and label information;  
(2) physical and chemical properties;  
(3) main uses;  
(4) dangerous characteristics;  
(5) safety requirements for storage, use and transport;  
(6) emergency response measures in case of danger.  
No duplicate registration shall be made for the same type of dangerous chemicals manufactured or imported by the same enterprise. Where a manufacturer or importer of dangerous chemicals discovers new dangerous characteristics of the dangerous chemicals it manufactures or imports, or any change in other registration contents, it shall promptly apply to the dangerous chemicals registration institution for modification of the registration contents.  
The specific measures for dangerous chemicals registration shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　国家对研究开发、试产试销过程中的低量低释放、低暴露的危险化学品等免予登记。免予登记的具体办法由国务院应急管理部门会同工业和信息化、公安、生态环境、农业农村、卫生健康、海关等部门制定。

**Article 87** The state grants exemption from registration for dangerous chemicals with low quantities, low release and low exposure in the process of research and development, trial production and trial marketing. The specific measures for exemption from registration shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with the departments of industry and information technology, public security, ecological environment, agriculture and rural affairs, health, customs, and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　危险化学品登记机构应当将危险化学品登记信息数据与工业和信息化、公安、自然资源、生态环境、交通运输、农业农村、卫生健康、海关、市场监督管理、能源、军事机关等单位共享。

**Article 88** The dangerous chemicals registration institution shall share the dangerous chemicals registration information data with units including the departments of industry and information technology, public security, natural resources, ecological environment, transport, agriculture and rural affairs, health, customs, market regulation, energy, and military authorities.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　新化学物质环境管理登记，依照有关生态环境保护的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行。

**Article 89** The environmental management registration of new chemical substances shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules on ecological and environmental protection.

### 第八章　事故应急救援

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门应当会同工业和信息化、公安、生态环境、交通运输、卫生健康、市场监督管理等部门，根据本地区实际情况，制定危险化学品事故应急预案，报本级人民政府批准，并依法向社会公布。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门在制定本部门应急预案时应当包含危险化学品事故应急处置的内容。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队按照中央军事委员会的命令，依法参加危险化学品事故应急救援工作。

**Article 90** The emergency management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of industry and information technology, public security, ecological environment, transport, health, and market regulation, formulate an emergency response plan for dangerous chemicals accidents based on the actual local conditions, submit it to the people's government at the same level for approval, and publish it to the public in accordance with law.  
The departments responsible for the safety supervision and administration of dangerous chemicals shall, when formulating their own emergency response plans, include the content of emergency response to dangerous chemicals accidents.  
The People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with the orders of the Central Military Commission, participate in the emergency rescue work for dangerous chemicals accidents in accordance with law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　危险化学品单位应当做好应急准备工作，健全应急管理制度，制定本单位危险化学品事故应急预案，依法建立专职或者兼职应急救援队伍，配备必要的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资，并定期组织应急救援演练，提高从业人员的应急处置能力。生产经营规模较小的，可以不建立应急救援队伍，但应当指定兼职的应急救援人员，并可以与邻近的应急救援队伍签订应急救援协议。  
危险化学品单位应当将其危险化学品事故应急预案按照国家有关规定报送县级以上人民政府应急管理部门备案，并依法向社会公布。

**Article 91** Entities dealing with dangerous chemicals shall make good emergency preparations, improve the emergency management system, formulate their own emergency response plans for dangerous chemicals accidents, establish full-time or part-time emergency rescue teams in accordance with law, be equipped with necessary emergency rescue appliances, equipment, devices and materials, and regularly organize emergency rescue drills to improve the emergency response capability of employees. Those with a relatively small scale of production and operation may not establish an emergency rescue team, but shall designate part-time emergency rescue personnel and may sign an emergency rescue agreement with a nearby emergency rescue team.  
The entity dealing with dangerous chemicals shall submit its emergency response plan for dangerous chemicals accidents to the emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level for record in accordance with the relevant state provisions, and publish it to the public in accordance with law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　县级以上地方人民政府根据生产安全事故应急工作的实际需要，加强化工园区危险化学品专业应急救援力量建设，可以依托有条件的生产经营单位、社会组织共同建立危险化学品专业应急救援队伍，配备必要的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资，并定期组织训练。  
化工园区内的危险化学品单位，可以联合建立应急救援队伍。

**Article 92** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual needs of emergency work for production safety accidents, strengthen the construction of professional emergency rescue forces for dangerous chemicals in chemical industry parks, and may rely on qualified production and operation entities and social organizations to jointly establish professional emergency rescue teams for dangerous chemicals, be equipped with necessary emergency rescue appliances, equipment, devices and materials, and regularly organize training.  
Entities dealing with dangerous chemicals within chemical industry parks may jointly establish emergency rescue teams.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　发生危险化学品事故，事故单位主要负责人应当立即按照本单位危险化学品事故应急预案组织救援，并向当地应急管理、生态环境、公安、卫生健康、市场监督管理部门报告，及时通知可能受影响的单位和人员；道路运输、水路运输过程中发生危险化学品事故的，驾驶人员、船员或者押运人员还应当向事故发生地交通运输主管部门报告。

**Article 93** In the event of a dangerous chemicals accident, the principal responsible person of the entity involved in the accident shall immediately organize rescue in accordance with the entity's emergency response plan for dangerous chemicals accidents, and report to the local departments of emergency management, ecological environment, public security, health, and market regulation, and promptly notify units and persons that may be affected; in the case of a dangerous chemicals accident during road transport or waterway transport, the driver, crew member or escort shall also report to the competent transport department at the place where the accident occurred.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　危险化学品单位发生直接危及人身安全的紧急情况时，在确保安全的前提下，作业现场带班人员、班组长、调度人员有权立即下达停产撤人命令，指挥有关遇险人员撤离。

**Article 94** In the event of an emergency that directly endangers personal safety in an entity dealing with dangerous chemicals, on the premise of ensuring safety, the shift supervisor, team leader, or dispatcher at the work site shall have the right to immediately issue an order to stop production and evacuate personnel, directing the relevant persons in danger to evacuate.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　发生危险化学品事故，有关地方人民政府应当立即组织应急管理、生态环境、公安、卫生健康、交通运输等有关部门，按照本地区危险化学品事故应急预案组织实施救援，不得拖延、推诿。  
有关地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照下列规定，采取必要的应急处置措施，减少事故损失，防止事故蔓延、扩大：  
（一）立即组织营救和救治受害人员，转移、疏散、撤离并妥善安置受到威胁的其他人员或者采取其他保护措施；  
（二）迅速控制危害源，测定危险化学品的性质，评估事故的危害区域及危害程度；  
（三）针对事故对人体、动植物、土壤、水源、水体、大气造成的现实危害和可能产生的危害，迅速采取封闭、隔离、洗消等措施；  
（四）对危险化学品事故造成的环境污染和生态破坏状况进行监测、评估，并采取相应的环境污染治理和生态修复措施。

**Article 95** In the event of a dangerous chemicals accident, the relevant local people's government shall immediately organize the relevant departments of emergency management, ecological environment, public security, health, transport, etc., to implement rescue in accordance with the local emergency response plan for dangerous chemicals accidents, and shall not delay or shirk responsibilities.  
The relevant local people's government and its relevant departments shall take necessary emergency response measures in accordance with the following provisions to reduce accident losses and prevent the accident from spreading or escalating:  
(1) immediately organize the rescue and treatment of injured persons, transfer, evacuate, relocate and properly shelter other persons threatened, or take other protective measures;  
(2) quickly control the source of danger, determine the nature of the dangerous chemicals, and assess the hazardous area and extent of harm of the accident;  
(3) promptly take measures such as closure, isolation, decontamination, etc., in response to the actual harm and potential harm caused by the accident to human beings, animals and plants, soil, water sources, water bodies, and the atmosphere;  
(4) monitor and assess the environmental pollution and ecological damage caused by the dangerous chemicals accident, and take corresponding measures for environmental pollution control and ecological restoration.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　有关危险化学品单位应当为危险化学品事故应急救援提供技术指导和必要的协助。

**Article 96** The relevant entities dealing with dangerous chemicals shall provide technical guidance and necessary assistance for emergency rescue of dangerous chemicals accidents.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　危险化学品事故造成环境污染和生态破坏的，由履行统一领导职责的人民政府统一发布有关信息。

**Article 97** Where a dangerous chemicals accident causes environmental pollution and ecological damage, the people's government exercising unified leadership shall uniformly release the relevant information.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　生产、使用、经营国家禁止生产、使用、经营的危险化学品的，由应急管理部门责令停止生产、使用、经营活动，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并没收违法生产、使用、经营的危险化学品以及用于违法生产、使用、经营的工具、设备、原料等；违法生产、使用、经营的危险化学品货值金额不足十万元的，并处三十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；货值金额十万元以上的，并处货值金额五倍以上十倍以下的罚款。  
有前款规定行为的，应急管理部门还应当责令其对所生产、使用、经营的危险化学品进行无害化处理。  
违反国家关于危险化学品使用的限制性规定使用危险化学品的，依照本条第一款的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 98** Where any person produces, uses, or deals in dangerous chemicals that the state prohibits the production, use, or dealing in, the emergency management department shall order the cessation of the production, use, or dealing activities; if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains, and confiscate the illegally produced, used, or dealt dangerous chemicals as well as the tools, equipment, raw materials, etc. used for the illegal production, use, or dealing; if the value of the illegally produced, used, or dealt dangerous chemicals is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is not less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall also be imposed.  
Where a person commits an act described in the preceding paragraph, the emergency management department shall also order that person to carry out harmless treatment of the dangerous chemicals it has produced, used, or dealt.  
Where a person uses dangerous chemicals in violation of the restrictive provisions of the state on the use of dangerous chemicals, the matter shall be handled and penalized in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　有下列情形之一的，由负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门按照职责分工责令停止建设或者停产停业整顿，限期改正，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）危险化学品建设项目未经安全条件审查；  
（二）危险化学品建设项目没有安全设施设计或者安全设施设计未报有关应急管理部门审查同意；  
（三）施工单位未按照批准的安全设施设计施工；  
（四）实行工程监理的危险化学品建设项目，建设单位未对安全设施施工一并委托监理；  
（五）危险化学品建设项目竣工投入生产或者使用前，安全设施未经验收合格。  
危险化学品港口建设项目有前款规定情形的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。

**Article 99** Under any of the following circumstances, the department responsible for safety supervision and administration of dangerous chemicals shall, according to its functions and duties, order the cessation of construction or suspension of production or business for rectification, order rectification within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) a construction project for dangerous chemicals has not undergone safety condition review;  
(2) a construction project for dangerous chemicals has no safety facility design or the safety facility design has not been submitted to the relevant emergency management department for review and approval;  
(3) the construction unit fails to construct according to the approved safety facility design;  
(4) for a construction project for dangerous chemicals subject to project supervision, the construction entity fails to commission supervision for the construction of safety facilities simultaneously;  
(5) before a construction project for dangerous chemicals is completed and put into production or use, the safety facilities have not passed the acceptance inspection.  
Where a port construction project for dangerous chemicals falls under the circumstances described in the preceding paragraph, the port administrative department shall handle and penalize it in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　未依法取得危险化学品安全生产许可证从事危险化学品生产，或者未依法取得工业产品生产许可证从事危险化学品及其包装物、容器生产的，分别依照有关安全生产许可、工业产品生产许可法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。  
违反本法规定，化工企业未取得危险化学品安全使用许可证，使用危险化学品从事生产的，由应急管理部门责令限期改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产整顿。  
违反本法规定，未取得危险化学品经营许可证从事危险化学品经营的，由应急管理部门责令停止经营活动，没收违法经营的危险化学品以及违法所得，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 100** Where any person engages in the production of dangerous chemicals without having lawfully obtained a dangerous chemicals safety production license, or engages in the production of dangerous chemicals and their packaging materials and containers without having lawfully obtained an industrial product production license, such person shall be handled and penalized respectively in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on work safety permits and industrial product production permits.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a chemical enterprise engages in the production using dangerous chemicals without having obtained a dangerous chemicals safety use license, the emergency management department shall order rectification within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production for rectification.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any person engages in the dealing of dangerous chemicals without having obtained a dangerous chemicals operation license, the emergency management department shall order cessation of the dealing activities, confiscate the illegally dealt dangerous chemicals and the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　有下列情形之一的，由负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，可以处十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业整顿：  
（一）危险化学品生产企业、进口企业未提供中文化学品安全技术说明书，或者未在危险化学品包装上粘贴、印刷、拴挂中文化学品安全标签；  
（二）危险化学品生产企业、进口企业提供的中文化学品安全技术说明书与其生产、进口的危险化学品不相符，在危险化学品包装上粘贴、印刷、拴挂的中文化学品安全标签与包装内危险化学品不相符，或者化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签不符合国家标准要求；  
（三）危险化学品生产企业、进口企业发现其生产或者进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性不立即公告，或者不及时修订其化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签；  
（四）生产、储存危险化学品的单位未对其敷设的危险化学品管道设置明显标志，或者未对危险化学品管道定期检查、检测、巡护；  
（五）进行可能危及危险化学品管道安全的施工作业，施工单位未按照规定将施工方案书面通知管道所属单位，未与管道所属单位共同制定应急预案、采取相应的安全防护措施，或者管道所属单位未指派专门人员到现场进行管道安全保护指导；  
（六）危险化学品生产企业从业人员未满足国家规定的学历要求，未接受安全生产教育和培训，或者未经考核合格上岗作业；  
（七）危险化学品包装物、容器的材质以及包装的型式、规格、方法和单件质量与所包装的危险化学品的性质和用途不相适应；  
（八）生产、储存危险化学品的企业以及使用危险化学品从事生产的企业未明确责任人、岗位职责和操作规程；  
（九）生产、储存危险化学品的企业以及使用危险化学品从事生产的企业未按照规定装备自动控制系统和安全仪表系统，未建立安全风险监测预警系统，或者安全风险监测预警系统未与政府有关部门实现互联互通；  
（十）生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位未在作业场所和安全设施、设备上设置明显的安全警示标志，或者未在作业场所设置通信、报警装置并处于适用状态；  
（十一）危险化学品专用储存场所未设专人负责管理，对储存的剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品未实行双人收发、双人保管制度，或者收发记录的保存期限少于三年；  
（十二）储存危险化学品的单位未建立危险化学品出入库核查、登记制度；  
（十三）危险化学品专用储存场所未设置明显标志；  
（十四）研制开发单位将未经小试、中试、工业化试验的新工艺、新技术直接用于工业化生产，或者转让危险化学品新工艺、新技术时，未提供新工艺、新技术的安全论证报告及相关资料；  
（十五）使用危险化学品的单位未将其作业场所使用的化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签提供给从业人员，或者未告知从业人员正确使用的方法、在紧急情况下应当采取的措施；  
（十六）危险化学品生产企业、经营企业经营没有化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签的危险化学品，或者擅自更改化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签；  
（十七）危险化学品生产企业、进口企业不办理危险化学品登记，或者发现其生产、进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性、其他登记内容发生变更，不办理危险化学品登记内容变更手续。  
从事危险化学品仓储经营的港口经营人有前款规定情形的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的专用储存场所未按照国家有关规定设置相应的实体防范、技术防范设施的，由公安机关依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。  
生产、储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位未设置治安保卫机构、配备专职治安保卫人员的，依照有关企业事业单位内部治安保卫法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 101** In any of the following circumstances, the department with the authority to supervise and manage the safety of hazardous chemicals shall, according to the division of duties, order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification:  
(1) A hazardous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise fails to provide a Chinese version of the chemical safety data sheet, or fails to affix, print, or attach a Chinese version of the chemical safety label on the hazardous chemical packaging;  
(2) The Chinese version of the chemical safety data sheet provided by a hazardous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise does not conform to the hazardous chemicals it produces or imports, the Chinese version of the chemical safety label affixed, printed, or attached on the hazardous chemical packaging does not conform to the hazardous chemicals in the packaging, or the chemical safety data sheet or chemical safety label does not meet the requirements of national standards;  
(3) A hazardous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise, upon discovering that the hazardous chemicals it produces or imports have new hazardous characteristics, fails to promptly announce them, or fails to timely revise its chemical safety data sheets or chemical safety labels;  
(4) A unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals fails to set up conspicuous markings for the hazardous chemical pipelines it lays, or fails to regularly inspect, test, or patrol the hazardous chemical pipelines;  
(5) In a construction operation that may endanger the safety of hazardous chemical pipelines, the construction unit fails to notify the pipeline owner in writing of the construction plan in accordance with the provisions, fails to formulate an emergency response plan and take corresponding safety protection measures jointly with the pipeline owner, or the pipeline owner fails to assign specialized personnel to the site to provide guidance on pipeline safety protection;  
(6) The employees of a hazardous chemical production enterprise fail to meet the education requirements prescribed by the state, fail to receive safety production education and training, or take up posts without passing the assessment;  
(7) The materials of hazardous chemical packaging and containers, as well as the form, specifications, method, and unit weight of the packaging, are not compatible with the nature and use of the hazardous chemicals being packaged;  
(8) An enterprise that produces or stores hazardous chemicals or an enterprise that uses hazardous chemicals in production fails to clearly define the responsible persons, post responsibilities, and operating procedures;  
(9) An enterprise that produces or stores hazardous chemicals or an enterprise that uses hazardous chemicals in production fails to equip itself with automatic control systems and safety instrumented systems as required, fails to establish a safety risk monitoring and early warning system, or the safety risk monitoring and early warning system fails to achieve interconnection and intercommunication with relevant government departments;  
(10) A unit that produces, stores, or uses hazardous chemicals fails to set up conspicuous safety warning signs at the workplace and on safety facilities and equipment, or fails to install communication and alarm devices at the workplace and keep them in serviceable condition;  
(11) A dedicated storage area for hazardous chemicals fails to have a designated person responsible for management, fails to implement a dual-person receipt and dual-person custody system for stored highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source, or the retention period of receipt records is less than three years;  
(12) A unit that stores hazardous chemicals fails to establish a system for verification and registration of the storage and retrieval of hazardous chemicals;  
(13) A dedicated storage area for hazardous chemicals fails to be marked with conspicuous signs;  
(14) A research and development unit directly applies a new process or new technology that has not undergone small-scale testing, pilot testing, or industrial testing to industrial production, or fails to provide a safety argumentation report and relevant materials for the new process or new technology when transferring it;  
(15) A unit that uses hazardous chemicals fails to provide its employees with the chemical safety data sheets and chemical safety labels for the chemicals used at the workplace, or fails to inform employees of the correct methods of use and the measures to be taken in emergency situations;  
(16) A hazardous chemical production enterprise or trading enterprise deals in hazardous chemicals without chemical safety data sheets or chemical safety labels, or arbitrarily alters the chemical safety data sheets or chemical safety labels;  
(17) A hazardous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise fails to register the hazardous chemicals, or, upon discovering that the hazardous chemicals it produces or imports have new hazardous characteristics or other registered contents have changed, fails to undergo the procedures for changing the registered contents of hazardous chemicals.  
If a port operator engaged in the storage and trading of hazardous chemicals falls under the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, the port administrative department shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the preceding paragraph. If a dedicated storage area for highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives fails to be equipped with corresponding physical and technical prevention facilities as prescribed by the state, the public security organ shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
If a unit that produces or stores highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives fails to establish a security protection institution or assign full-time security protection personnel, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations concerning internal security protection of enterprises and institutions.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　危险化学品包装物、容器生产企业销售未经检验或者经检验不合格的危险化学品包装物、容器的，由市场监督管理部门责令限期改正，处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。  
将未经检验合格的运输危险化学品的船舶及其配载的容器投入使用的，由海事管理机构依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。

**Article 102** If a hazardous chemical packaging or container production enterprise sells hazardous chemical packaging or containers that have not been inspected or have been inspected and found to be non-conforming, the market regulatory authority shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.  
If a vessel used for transporting hazardous chemicals and its stowage containers that have not passed the inspection are put into use, the maritime administrative agency shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　有下列情形之一的，由负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿直至由原发证机关吊销其相关许可证件，并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款；相关许可证件被吊销的，由市场监督管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照：  
（一）未按照国家有关规定对危险化学品重大危险源登记建档，未进行定期检测、评估、监控，未制定应急预案，或者未建立重大危险源安全责任制；  
（二）对重复使用的危险化学品包装物、容器，在重复使用前不进行检查，或者使用存在安全隐患的危险化学品包装物、容器；  
（三）未根据其生产、储存的危险化学品的种类和危险特性，在作业场所设置相关安全设施、设备，或者未按照国家标准、行业标准或者国家有关规定对安全设施、设备进行经常性维护、保养；  
（四）未依照本法规定对其安全生产条件定期进行安全评价；  
（五）未将危险化学品储存在专用储存场所内，或者未将剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品在专用储存场所内单独存放；  
（六）危险化学品的储存方式、方法或者储存数量不符合国家标准或者国家有关规定；  
（七）危险化学品专用储存场所不符合国家标准、行业标准的要求；  
（八）未对危险化学品专用储存场所的安全设施、设备定期进行检测、检验，或者经检测、检验不合格，没有停止使用并按照规定予以维修或者更换；  
（九）生产、储存危险化学品的企业以及使用危险化学品从事生产的企业未建立安全风险分级管控制度，未开展安全风险辨识评估，未按照安全风险分级采取相应安全管控措施，或者工艺、设施、设备、原料等发生变更时未重新进行安全风险辨识评估。  
从事危险化学品仓储经营的港口经营人有前款规定情形的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。

**Article 103** In any of the following circumstances, the department with the authority to supervise and manage the safety of hazardous chemicals shall, according to the division of duties, order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification until the original issuing authority revokes the relevant license, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the relevant license is revoked, the market regulatory authority shall order the business scope change registration or revoke its business license:  
(1) Failing to register and file major hazard sources of hazardous chemicals, failing to conduct regular testing, assessment, and monitoring, failing to formulate emergency response plans, or failing to establish a safety responsibility system for major hazard sources in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state;  
(2) Failing to inspect reusable hazardous chemical packaging or containers before reuse, or using hazardous chemical packaging or containers with potential safety hazards;  
(3) Failing to set up relevant safety facilities and equipment at the workplace based on the types and hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals produced or stored, or failing to regularly maintain and service the safety facilities and equipment in accordance with national standards, industry standards, or relevant provisions of the state;  
(4) Failing to regularly conduct safety assessments of its safety production conditions in accordance with this Law;  
(5) Failing to store hazardous chemicals in dedicated storage areas, or failing to store highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose storage quantity constitutes a major hazard source separately in dedicated storage areas;  
(6) The storage mode, method, or quantity of hazardous chemicals does not comply with national standards or relevant provisions of the state;  
(7) The dedicated storage area for hazardous chemicals does not meet the requirements of national standards or industry standards;  
(8) Failing to regularly test and inspect the safety facilities and equipment of the dedicated storage area for hazardous chemicals, or failing to cease use and repair or replace them as required after testing or inspection reveals non-conformity;  
(9) An enterprise that produces or stores hazardous chemicals or an enterprise that uses hazardous chemicals in production fails to establish a graded control system for safety risks, fails to conduct safety risk identification and assessment, fails to take corresponding safety control measures according to the risk grade, or, when changes occur in processes, facilities, equipment, or raw materials, fails to conduct a new safety risk identification and assessment.  
If a port operator engaged in the storage and trading of hazardous chemicals falls under the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, the port administrative department shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，可以处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）生产、储存、使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位不如实记录其生产、储存、使用的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、流向；  
（二）生产、储存、使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位发现剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗，不立即向公安机关报告；  
（三）储存剧毒化学品的单位未将剧毒化学品的储存数量、储存地点以及管理人员的情况报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案；  
（四）危险化学品生产企业、经营企业不如实记录剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品购买单位的名称、地址，经办人的姓名、身份证号码以及所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、用途，或者保存销售记录和相关材料的时间少于三年；  
（五）剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的销售企业、购买单位未在规定的时限内将所销售、购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量以及流向信息报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案；  
（六）使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位依照本法规定转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，未将有关情况在转让后三日内向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业，使用危险化学品从事生产的企业，或者在港区内储存、装卸危险化学品的企业未依照本法规定将安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况报所在地县级人民政府应急管理部门或者港口行政管理部门备案的，分别由应急管理部门或者港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处理、处罚。  
未依照本法规定将剧毒化学品以及危险化学品重大危险源的情况报应急管理部门、港口行政管理部门或者消防救援机构备案的，分别由应急管理部门、港口行政管理部门或者消防救援机构依照本条第一款的规定予以处理、处罚。

**Article 104** In any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and may impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) A unit that produces, stores, or uses highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives fails to truthfully record the types, quantities, and flows of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives it produces, stores, or uses;  
(2) A unit that produces, stores, or uses highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, upon discovering that the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives are lost or stolen, fails to immediately report to the public security organ;  
(3) A unit that stores highly toxic chemicals fails to report the storage quantity, storage location, and information on the management personnel of the highly toxic chemicals to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level for the record;  
(4) A hazardous chemical production enterprise or trading enterprise fails to truthfully record the name and address of the purchasing unit of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, the name and identity card number of the handling person, and the types, quantities, and uses of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives purchased, or the retention period of sales records and relevant materials is less than three years;  
(5) A sales enterprise or purchasing unit of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives fails to report the types, quantities, and flow information of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives sold or purchased to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level for the record within the prescribed time limit;  
(6) A unit that uses highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, when transferring the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives it purchased in accordance with this Law, fails to report the relevant information to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level for the record within three days after the transfer.  
If an enterprise that produces or stores hazardous chemicals, an enterprise that uses hazardous chemicals in production, or an enterprise that stores or loads and unloads hazardous chemicals in a port area fails to report the safety assessment report and the implementation of rectification measures to the local emergency management department of the people's government at the county level or the port administrative department for the record in accordance with this Law, the emergency management department or the port administrative department shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
If a party fails to report the information on highly toxic chemicals and major hazard sources of hazardous chemicals to the emergency management department, port administrative department, or fire rescue authority for the record in accordance with this Law, the emergency management department, port administrative department, or fire rescue authority shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位转产、停产、停业或者解散，未采取有效措施及时、妥善处置其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存的危险化学品，或者丢弃危险化学品的，由应急管理部门责令限期改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位转产、停产、停业或者解散，未依照本法规定将其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存危险化学品的处置方案报有关部门备案的，分别由有关部门责令限期改正，可以处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 105** If a unit that produces, stores, or uses hazardous chemicals, when changing its product line, suspending production, ceasing business, or dissolving, fails to take effective measures to promptly and properly dispose of its hazardous chemical production facilities, storage facilities, and stored hazardous chemicals, or abandons hazardous chemicals, the emergency management department shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
If a unit that produces, stores, or uses hazardous chemicals, when changing its product line, suspending production, ceasing business, or dissolving, fails to report the plan for disposal of its hazardous chemical production facilities, storage facilities, and stored hazardous chemicals to the relevant department for the record in accordance with this Law, the relevant department shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and may impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　危险化学品经营企业向未经许可违法从事危险化学品生产、经营活动的企业采购危险化学品的，由应急管理部门责令限期改正，处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿直至由原发证机关吊销其危险化学品经营许可证，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；相关许可证件被吊销的，由市场监督管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照。

**Article 106** If a hazardous chemical trading enterprise purchases hazardous chemicals from an enterprise that illegally engages in the production or trading of hazardous chemicals without a license, the emergency management department shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of business for rectification until the original issuing authority revokes its hazardous chemical trading license, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the relevant license is revoked, the market regulatory authority shall order the business scope change registration or revoke its business license.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业有下列情形之一的，由应急管理部门责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿直至由原发证机关吊销其危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品经营许可证，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；相关许可证件被吊销的，由市场监督管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照：  
（一）向不具有[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品；  
（二）不按照剧毒化学品购买许可证载明的品种、数量销售剧毒化学品；  
（三）向个人销售剧毒化学品（属于剧毒化学品的农药除外）、易制爆危险化学品（含有易制爆危险化学品的食品添加剂、药品、兽药、消毒剂等生活用品除外）。  
不具有[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位购买剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，或者个人购买剧毒化学品（属于剧毒化学品的农药除外）、易制爆危险化学品（含有易制爆危险化学品的食品添加剂、药品、兽药、消毒剂等生活用品除外）的，由公安机关没收所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，可以并处二万元以上五万元以下的罚款。  
使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位出借或者向不具有[本法第五十八条](#art-58)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，或者向个人转让其购买的剧毒化学品（属于剧毒化学品的农药除外）、易制爆危险化学品（含有易制爆危险化学品的食品添加剂、药品、兽药、消毒剂等生活用品除外）的，由公安机关责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿。  
违反本法规定，在互联网上销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的，由负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门按照职责分工依照本条第一款的规定予以处理、处罚。

**Article 107** If a hazardous chemical production enterprise or trading enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the emergency management department shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification until the original issuing authority revokes its hazardous chemical safety production license or hazardous chemical trading license, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the relevant license is revoked, the market regulatory authority shall order the business scope change registration or revoke its business license:  
(1) Selling highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives to a unit that does not have the relevant license or certifying document as specified in Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 58 of this Law;  
(2) Selling highly toxic chemicals not in accordance with the type and quantity specified in the purchase permit for highly toxic chemicals;  
(3) Selling highly toxic chemicals (excluding pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives (excluding household items such as food additives, drugs, veterinary drugs, and disinfectants containing hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives) to individuals.  
If a unit that does not have the relevant license or certifying document as specified in Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 58 of this Law purchases highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, or an individual purchases highly toxic chemicals (excluding pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives (excluding household items such as food additives, drugs, veterinary drugs, and disinfectants containing hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives), the public security organ shall confiscate the purchased highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
If a unit that uses highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives lends or transfers the purchased highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives to a unit that does not have the relevant license or certifying document as specified in Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 58 of this Law, or transfers the purchased highly toxic chemicals (excluding pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives (excluding household items such as food additives, drugs, veterinary drugs, and disinfectants containing hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives) to an individual, the public security organ shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification.  
If, in violation of this Law, highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives are sold on the Internet, the department with the authority to supervise and manage the safety of hazardous chemicals shall, according to the division of duties, handle and impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　未依法取得危险货物道路运输许可、相应危险货物水路运输许可或者未办理备案手续，从事危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的，分别依照有关道路运输、水路运输的法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 108** If a party engages in the road or waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals without having obtained the road transport permit for dangerous goods, the corresponding waterway transport permit for dangerous goods, or without having completed the filing formalities according to law, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on road transportation and waterway transportation, respectively.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令限期改正，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业的驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员未取得从业资格上岗作业；  
（二）危险化学品的装卸作业未遵守安全作业标准、规程和制度，或者未在装卸管理人员的现场指挥或者监控下进行；  
（三）水路运输危险化学品的集装箱装箱作业未在集装箱装箱现场检查员的指挥或者监控下进行，或者不符合积载、隔离的规范和要求；  
（四）运输危险化学品，未根据危险化学品的危险特性采取相应的安全防护措施，或者未配备必要的防护用品和应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资；  
（五）危险化学品道路运输企业未对运输车辆、驾驶人员的作业状态进行实时监控、管理；  
（六）使用未依法取得危险货物适装证书的船舶，通过内河运输危险化学品；  
（七）通过内河运输危险化学品的承运人违反国务院交通运输主管部门对单船运输的危险化学品数量的限制性规定运输危险化学品；  
（八）用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位不符合国家有关安全规范，未与饮用水取水口保持国家规定的安全距离，或者未按照国家有关规定验收合格投入使用；  
（九）托运人未向承运人提交电子或者纸质的危险货物托运清单说明所托运的危险化学品相关情况，未提供化学品安全技术说明书，或者未按照国家有关规定对所托运的危险化学品妥善包装并在危险化学品包装上粘贴、印刷、拴挂与包装内危险化学品相符的化学品安全标签；  
（十）运输危险化学品需要添加抑制剂或者稳定剂，托运人未按照规定添加或者未将有关情况告知承运人。  
水路运输企业的申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员违反法律、行政法规或者规章关于危险化学品安全管理规定的，由交通运输主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令暂停从业活动直至吊销其从业资格。

**Article 109** In any of the following circumstances, the transportation authority shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) A driver, crew member, loading and unloading manager, escort, declarant, or on-site container packing inspector of a hazardous chemical road transport enterprise or waterway transport enterprise takes up a post without having obtained the relevant professional qualification;  
(2) The loading and unloading operations of hazardous chemicals are not carried out in compliance with safety operation standards, procedures, and systems, or are not conducted under the on-site direction or monitoring of a loading and unloading manager;  
(3) The container packing operation for waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals is not carried out under the direction or monitoring of an on-site container packing inspector, or does not comply with the specifications and requirements for stowage and segregation;  
(4) When transporting hazardous chemicals, corresponding safety protection measures are not taken based on the hazardous characteristics of the chemicals, or necessary protective articles and emergency rescue equipment, tools, devices, and materials are not provided;  
(5) A hazardous chemical road transport enterprise fails to conduct real-time monitoring and management of the working status of its transport vehicles and drivers;  
(6) Using a vessel that has not obtained the certificate of fitness for carrying dangerous goods according to law to transport hazardous chemicals via inland waterways;  
(7) A carrier transporting hazardous chemicals via inland waterways violates the restrictive provisions of the competent department of transportation under the State Council on the quantity of hazardous chemicals allowed to be carried by a single vessel;  
(8) An inland river wharf or berth used for hazardous chemical transport operations does not comply with relevant national safety specifications, fails to maintain the safety distance prescribed by the state from drinking water intakes, or is put into use without passing the acceptance inspection in accordance with relevant state provisions;  
(9) A consignor fails to submit an electronic or paper dangerous goods consignment list to the carrier explaining the relevant information of the hazardous chemicals being consigned, fails to provide the chemical safety data sheet, or fails to properly package the consigned hazardous chemicals in accordance with state provisions and affix, print, or attach a chemical safety label consistent with the hazardous chemicals in the package;  
(10) Where an inhibitor or stabilizer needs to be added during the transportation of hazardous chemicals, the consignor fails to add it according to the provisions or fails to inform the carrier of the relevant information.  
If the declarant or on-site container packing inspector of a waterway transport enterprise violates the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, or rules on the safety management of hazardous chemicals, the transportation authority shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of the professional activity until revocation of the professional qualification.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令限期改正，处二十万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）委托未依法取得相应许可或者办理备案手续的单位或者个人承运危险化学品；  
（二）通过内河封闭水域运输剧毒化学品；  
（三）通过内河水域运输国家规定禁止通过内河运输的危险化学品；  
（四）载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行时通过通航建筑物，未提前向通航建筑物运行单位报告，或者不服从交通运输主管部门管理；  
（五）在托运的普通货物中夹带危险化学品，或者将危险化学品匿报、谎报为普通货物托运。  
邮政企业、快递企业收寄危险化学品的，依照《中华人民共和国邮政法》的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 110** In any of the following circumstances, the transportation authority shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) Entrusting a unit or individual that has not obtained the corresponding license or completed the filing formalities according to law to carry hazardous chemicals;  
(2) Transporting highly toxic chemicals through enclosed inland water bodies;  
(3) Transporting hazardous chemicals that the state prohibits from being transported via inland waterways through such waterways;  
(4) A vessel carrying hazardous chemicals, when navigating inland waterways and passing through a navigation structure, fails to report to the operator of the navigation structure in advance, or fails to comply with the management of the transportation authority;  
(5) Concealing hazardous chemicals in ordinary goods consigned for transportation, or consigning hazardous chemicals as ordinary goods by concealing or misrepresenting them.  
If a postal enterprise or express delivery enterprise accepts hazardous chemicals for delivery, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）超过运输车辆的核定载质量装载危险化学品；  
（二）使用安全技术条件不符合国家标准要求的车辆运输危险化学品；  
（三）运输危险化学品的车辆未经公安机关批准进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域；  
（四）未取得剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，通过道路运输剧毒化学品。

**Article 111** In any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) Loading hazardous chemicals in excess of the rated load capacity of the transport vehicle;  
(2) Using a vehicle whose safety technical conditions do not meet the requirements of national standards to transport hazardous chemicals;  
(3) A vehicle transporting hazardous chemicals enters an area where the passage of hazardous chemical transport vehicles is restricted without the approval of the public security organ;  
(4) Transporting highly toxic chemicals by road without obtaining a road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）危险化学品运输车辆未悬挂、喷涂或者遮挡、拆除警示标志，或者警示标志不符合国家标准要求；  
（二）通过道路运输危险化学品，不按照要求配备押运人员；  
（三）运输剧毒化学品或者易制爆危险化学品途中需要较长时间停车，驾驶人员、押运人员不向当地公安机关报告；  
（四）剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品在道路运输途中丢失、被盗、被抢或者出现流散、泄漏等情况，驾驶人员、押运人员不采取相应的警示措施和安全措施，或者不向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 112** In any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) A hazardous chemical transport vehicle fails to display, spray, or has its warning signs obscured or removed, or the warning signs do not meet the requirements of national standards;  
(2) When transporting hazardous chemicals by road, the required escort personnel are not provided;  
(3) During the transportation of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives, if a long stop is required, the driver or escort fails to report to the local public security organ;  
(4) If highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that are easy to produce explosives are lost, stolen, robbed, or become scattered or leaked during road transportation, the driver or escort fails to take corresponding warning and safety measures, or fails to report to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　对发生交通事故负有全部责任或者主要责任的危险化学品道路运输企业，由公安机关责令消除安全隐患，未消除安全隐患的危险化学品运输车辆，禁止上道路行驶。

**Article 113** A hazardous chemical road transport enterprise that bears full or primary liability for a traffic accident shall be ordered by the public security organ to eliminate the safety hazards; hazardous chemical transport vehicles from which safety hazards have not been eliminated shall be prohibited from operating on roads.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令限期改正，可以处五万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业未配备专职安全生产管理人员；  
（二）用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位的管理单位未制定码头、泊位危险化学品事故应急预案，或者未为码头、泊位配备充足、有效的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资。

**Article 114** In any of the following circumstances, the transportation authority shall order rectification within a prescribed time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) A hazardous chemical road transport enterprise or waterway transport enterprise fails to assign full-time safety production management personnel;  
(2) The management unit of an inland river wharf or berth used for hazardous chemical transport operations fails to formulate an emergency response plan for hazardous chemical accidents at the wharf or berth, or fails to equip the wharf or berth with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment, tools, devices, and materials.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　有下列情形之一的，依照有关内河交通安全管理法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚：  
（一）通过内河运输危险化学品的水路运输企业未制定运输船舶危险化学品事故应急救援预案，或者未为运输船舶配备充足、有效的应急救援器材、装备、设备和物资；  
（二）通过内河运输危险化学品的船舶的所有人或者经营人未取得船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明；  
（三）船舶载运危险化学品进出内河港口，未依照法律、行政法规的规定报告海事管理机构并经其同意；  
（四）载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行、装卸或者停泊，未悬挂专用的警示标志，未按照规定显示专用信号，或者未按照规定申请引航。  
未向港口行政管理部门报告并经其同意，在内河港口内进行危险化学品的装卸、过驳作业的，依照《中华人民共和国港口法》的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 115** In any of the following circumstances, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on inland water traffic safety management:  
(1) A waterway transport enterprise transporting hazardous chemicals via inland waterways fails to formulate an emergency response plan for hazardous chemical accidents on transport vessels, or fails to equip transport vessels with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment, tools, devices, and materials;  
(2) The owner or operator of a vessel transporting hazardous chemicals via inland waterways fails to obtain a certificate of insurance or financial guarantee for ship pollution damage liability;  
(3) A vessel carrying hazardous chemicals entering or leaving an inland river port fails to report to the maritime administrative agency and obtain its consent in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations;  
(4) A vessel carrying hazardous chemicals, when navigating, loading or unloading, or berthing on inland waterways, fails to display the dedicated warning signs, fails to exhibit the dedicated signals as required, or fails to apply for pilotage as required.  
If the loading, unloading, or lightering of hazardous chemicals is conducted in an inland river port without reporting to and obtaining the consent of the port administrative department, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Port Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　伪造、变造或者出租、出借、转让危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证，或者使用伪造、变造的危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证的，分别依照有关安全生产许可、工业产品生产许可法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。  
伪造、变造或者出租、出借、转让本法规定的其他许可证，或者使用伪造、变造的本法规定的其他许可证的，分别由相关许可证的颁发管理机关处二十万元以上三十万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 116** If a party forges, alters, leases, lends, or transfers a hazardous chemical safety production license or an industrial product production license, or uses a forged or altered hazardous chemical safety production license or industrial product production license, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on safety production licensing and industrial product production licensing, respectively.  
If a party forges, alters, leases, lends, or transfers any other license provided for in this Law, or uses a forged or altered other license provided for in this Law, the authority that issued the relevant license shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and confiscate any illegal gains.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　危险化学品单位发生危险化学品事故，其主要负责人不立即组织救援或者不立即向有关部门报告的，依照有关生产安全事故报告和调查处理法律、行政法规的规定处理、处罚。  
危险化学品单位的主要负责人未履行安全生产管理职责，导致发生生产安全事故的，依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》的规定限制从业。  
危险化学品单位发生危险化学品事故，造成他人人身伤害或者财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 117** If a hazardous chemical unit has a hazardous chemical accident, and its principal responsible person fails to immediately organize rescue or fails to immediately report to the relevant department, the matter shall be handled and penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on reporting and investigation and handling of production safety accidents.  
If the principal responsible person of a hazardous chemical unit fails to perform the duties of safety production management, resulting in a production safety accident, his/her engagement in relevant activities shall be restricted in accordance with the provisions of the Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If a hazardous chemical unit has a hazardous chemical accident, causing personal injury or property damage to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　发生危险化学品事故，有关地方人民政府及其有关部门不立即组织实施救援，或者不采取必要的应急处置措施减少事故损失，防止事故蔓延、扩大的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 118** If a hazardous chemical accident occurs, and the relevant local people's government and its relevant departments fail to immediately organize and implement rescue, or fail to take necessary emergency response measures to reduce accident losses and prevent the accident from spreading or expanding, the responsible leaders and directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门的工作人员，在危险化学品安全监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 119** If a staff member of a department with the authority to supervise and manage the safety of hazardous chemicals abuses his/her power, neglects his/her duty, or engages in favoritism and irregularities in the supervision and management of hazardous chemical safety, he/she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 120** Any violation of this Law that constitutes an act violating public security administration shall be subject to public security administration penalties according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　监控化学品、属于危险化学品的药品和农药的安全管理，依照本法的规定执行；法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。  
民用爆炸物品、烟花爆竹、放射性物品、核能物质以及用于国防科研生产的危险化学品的安全管理，不适用本法。  
法律、行政法规对燃气的安全管理另有规定的，依照其规定。  
危险化学品容器属于特种设备的，其安全管理依照有关特种设备安全的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 121** The safety management of monitoring chemicals, pharmaceuticals and pesticides classified as dangerous chemicals shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law; if any other provisions exist in laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail.  
The safety management of civil explosives, fireworks and firecrackers, radioactive articles, nuclear energy substances, and dangerous chemicals used for national defense scientific research and production shall not be governed by this Law.  
If any other provisions on the safety management of gas exist in laws or administrative regulations, those provisions shall prevail.  
If a container for dangerous chemicals is a special equipment, its safety management shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on special equipment safety.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　危险化学品的进出口管理，依照有关对外贸易的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行；出口危险化学品涉及《中华人民共和国出口管制法》规定的管制物项的，依照有关出口管制的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行；进口的危险化学品的储存、使用、经营、运输的安全管理，依照本法的规定执行。

**Article 122** The import and export management of dangerous chemicals shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules on foreign trade; where the export of dangerous chemicals involves controlled items as stipulated in the Export Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of the laws, administrative regulations and rules on export control shall apply; the safety management of the storage, use, handling and transport of imported dangerous chemicals shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　公众发现、捡拾的无主危险化学品，由公安机关或者应急管理部门接收。有关部门接收或者依法没收的危险化学品，需要进行处置的，交由生态环境主管部门组织其认定的专业单位进行处置，或者交由有关危险化学品生产企业进行处置。处置所需费用由政府负担。

**Article 123** Where the public discovers or picks up unclaimed dangerous chemicals, such chemicals shall be taken over by the public security organ or the emergency management department. Where the relevant department takes over or confiscates dangerous chemicals according to law and such chemicals need to be disposed of, they shall be handed over to the ecological environment competent department to organize the professional units recognized by it for disposal, or to the relevant dangerous chemicals production enterprise for disposal. The costs incurred for disposal shall be borne by the government.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　化学品的危险特性尚未确定的，由国务院应急管理、生态环境、卫生健康部门分别负责组织对该化学品的物理危险性、生态环境危害性、毒理特性进行鉴定。根据鉴定结果需要调整危险化学品目录的，依照[本法第三条](#art-3)第二款的规定办理。  
化学品的危险特性尚未确定的，任何单位和个人不得擅自从事该化学品的生产、储存、使用、经营、运输等活动。

**Article 124** Where the hazardous characteristics of a chemical have not yet been determined, the emergency management, ecological environment, and health departments of the State Council shall respectively be responsible for organizing expert evaluation on the physical hazards, ecological and environmental hazards, and toxicological characteristics of such chemical. If adjustment of the catalog of dangerous chemicals is necessary based on the evaluation results, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 3 of this Law.  
Where the hazardous characteristics of a chemical have not yet been determined, no entity or individual may engage in the production, storage, use, handling, transport or other activities concerning such chemical without authorization.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　港区内危险化学品生产和使用危险化学品的生产装置及相连储罐部分，由应急管理部门负责安全监督管理；仅与危险化学品码头相连的储罐部分，由港口行政管理部门负责安全监督管理。

**Article 125** The safety supervision and administration of the production facilities and connected storage tanks for dangerous chemicals production and use within the port area shall be the responsibility of the emergency management department; the safety supervision and administration of storage tanks solely connected to dangerous chemical wharves shall be the responsibility of the port administrative department.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的危险化学品安全管理，依照本法有关规定和中央军事委员会的规定执行。

**Article 126** The safety management of dangerous chemicals by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law and the provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　本法自2026年5月1日起施行。

**Article 127** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2026.
