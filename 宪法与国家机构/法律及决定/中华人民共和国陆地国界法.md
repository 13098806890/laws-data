# 中华人民共和国陆地国界法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-10-23  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> （2021年10月23日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十一次会议通过）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和加强陆地国界工作，保障陆地国界及边境的安全稳定，促进我国与陆地邻国睦邻友好和交流合作，维护国家主权、安全和领土完整，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of regulating and strengthening the work on land borders, ensuring the security and stability of land borders and border areas, promoting good-neighborliness, friendship, exchanges and cooperation with neighboring land countries, and safeguarding national sovereignty, security and territorial integrity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国陆地国界的划定和勘定，陆地国界及边境的防卫、管理和建设，陆地国界事务的国际合作等，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the delimitation and demarcation of the land borders of the People's Republic of China, the defense, management and development of land borders and border areas, and international cooperation in matters relating to land borders.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　陆地国界是指划分中华人民共和国与陆地邻国接壤的领陆和内水的界限。陆地国界垂直划分中华人民共和国与陆地邻国的领空和底土。  
中华人民共和国陆地国界内侧一定范围内的区域为边境。

**Article 3** Land borders refer to the boundaries that divide the land territory and internal waters of the People's Republic of China from those of its neighboring land countries. The land borders shall vertically divide the airspace and subsoil of the People's Republic of China from those of its neighboring land countries.  
The areas within a certain range on the inner side of the land borders of the People's Republic of China shall be the border areas.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国的主权和领土完整神圣不可侵犯。  
国家采取有效措施，坚决维护领土主权和陆地国界安全，防范和打击任何损害领土主权和破坏陆地国界的行为。

**Article 4** The sovereignty and territorial integrity of the People's Republic of China are sacred and inviolable.  
The State shall take effective measures to resolutely safeguard territorial sovereignty and the security of land borders, and prevent and combat any act that impairs territorial sovereignty or undermines land borders.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对陆地国界工作实行统一的领导。

**Article 5** The State shall exercise unified leadership over the work on land borders.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外交部负责陆地国界涉外事务，参与陆地国界管理相关工作，牵头开展对外谈判、缔约、履约及国际合作，处理需要通过外交途径解决的问题，组织开展国界线和界标维护管理。  
国务院公安部门负责边境地区公安工作，指导、监督边境公安机关加强社会治安管理，防范和打击边境违法犯罪活动。  
海关总署负责边境口岸等的进出境相关监督管理工作，依法组织实施进出境交通运输工具、货物、物品和人员的海关监管、检疫。  
国家移民管理部门负责边境地区移民管理工作，依法组织实施出入境边防检查、边民往来管理和边境地区边防管理。  
国务院其他有关部门按照各自职责分工，依法行使职权，开展相关工作。

**Article 6** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall be responsible for foreign affairs concerning land borders, participate in the relevant management of land borders, take the lead in conducting negotiations, concluding treaties, fulfilling treaties and international cooperation, deal with issues that need to be resolved through diplomatic channels, and organize the maintenance and management of border lines and boundary markers.  
The public security department under the State Council shall be responsible for public security work in border areas, guide and supervise border public security organs to strengthen social security management, and prevent and combat illegal and criminal activities in border areas.  
The General Administration of Customs shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of entry and exit at border ports, among others, and organize the implementation of customs supervision and quarantine of means of transport, goods, articles and persons entering or exiting the country according to law.  
The national immigration administration department shall be responsible for immigration management in border areas, organize the implementation of exit-entry border inspection, the management of border residents' visits and the border area border defense management according to law.  
Other relevant departments under the State Council shall exercise their powers and carry out relevant work according to law in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在中央军事委员会领导下，有关军事机关组织、指导、协调陆地国界及边境的防卫管控、维护社会稳定、处置突发事件、边防合作及相关工作。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队按照各自任务分工，警戒守卫陆地国界，抵御武装侵略，处置陆地国界及边境重大突发事件和恐怖活动，会同或者协助地方有关部门防范、制止和打击非法越界，保卫陆地国界及边境的安全稳定。

**Article 7** Under the leadership of the Central Military Commission, relevant military organs shall organize, guide and coordinate the defense and control of land borders and border areas, maintenance of social stability, handling of emergencies, border defense cooperation and related work.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with their respective tasks and responsibilities, guard and defend the land borders, resist armed aggression, handle major emergencies and terrorist activities at land borders and border areas, and jointly or assist local relevant departments in preventing, stopping and combating illegal border crossings, and protect the security and stability of land borders and border areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　边境省、自治区的各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会在本行政区域内，保证有关陆地国界及边境的法律法规的遵守和执行。  
边境省、自治区的各级人民政府依照法律法规规定管理本行政区域内的陆地国界及边境相关工作。

**Article 8** The people's congresses and their standing committees at all levels in the border provinces and autonomous regions shall ensure the observance and implementation of laws and regulations concerning the land borders and border areas within their respective administrative regions.  
The people's governments at all levels in the border provinces and autonomous regions shall manage the work related to land borders and border areas within their respective administrative regions in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军地有关部门、单位依托有关统筹协调机构，合力推进强边固防，组织开展边防防卫管控、边防基础设施建设与维护管理等工作，共同维护陆地国界及边境的安全稳定与正常秩序。

**Article 9** Relevant military and local departments and units shall, relying on relevant overall coordination mechanisms, jointly promote the strengthening of borders and consolidation of defense, organize and carry out border defense and control, the construction, maintenance and management of border defense infrastructure, and other work, and jointly safeguard the security, stability and normal order of land borders and border areas.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家采取有效措施，加强边防建设，支持边境经济社会发展和对外开放，推进固边兴边富民行动，提高边境公共服务和基础设施建设水平，改善边境生产生活条件，鼓励和支持边民在边境生产生活，促进边防建设与边境经济社会协调发展。

**Article 10** The State shall take effective measures to strengthen border defense development, support the economic and social development and opening up of border areas, advance the action of consolidating borders, developing border areas and enriching the people, improve the level of public services and infrastructure construction in border areas, improve the working and living conditions in border areas, encourage and support border residents to work and live in border areas, and promote the coordinated development of border defense development and the economy and society of border areas.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强陆地国界宣传教育，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，弘扬中华民族捍卫祖国统一和领土完整的精神，增强公民的国家观念和国土安全意识，构筑中华民族共有精神家园。  
各级人民政府和有关教育科研机构应当加强对陆地国界及边境相关史料的收集、保护和研究。  
公民和组织发现陆地国界及边境相关史料、史迹和实物，应当依法及时上报或者上交国家有关部门。

**Article 11** The State shall strengthen the publicity and education on land borders, forge a strong sense of community of the Chinese nation, promote the spirit of the Chinese nation in defending the unity of the motherland and territorial integrity, enhance citizens' national consciousness and awareness of national land security, and build a shared spiritual home of the Chinese nation.  
People's governments at all levels and relevant educational and scientific research institutions shall strengthen the collection, protection and research of historical materials relating to land borders and border areas.  
Citizens and organizations that discover historical materials, relics and physical objects relating to land borders and border areas shall promptly report or submit them to the relevant state departments according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家保障陆地国界工作经费。  
国务院和边境省、自治区的县级以上各级人民政府，应当将陆地国界及边境相关工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 12** The State shall guarantee the funding for the work on land borders.  
The State Council and the people's governments at or above the county level in border provinces and autonomous regions shall incorporate the work related to land borders and border areas into the national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公民和组织应当维护陆地国界及边境安全稳定，保护界标和边防基础设施，配合、协助开展陆地国界相关工作。  
国家对配合、协助开展陆地国界相关工作的公民和组织给予鼓励和支持，对作出突出贡献的公民和组织按照有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 13** Citizens and organizations shall safeguard the security and stability of land borders and border areas, protect boundary markers and border defense infrastructure, and cooperate and assist in carrying out the work related to land borders.  
The State shall give encouragement and support to citizens and organizations that cooperate and assist in carrying out the work related to land borders, and shall commend and reward citizens and organizations that have made outstanding contributions in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中华人民共和国遵守同外国缔结或者共同参加的有关陆地国界事务的条约。

**Article 14** The People's Republic of China shall observe the treaties concluded with foreign countries or jointly acceded to by it concerning matters of land borders.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家坚持平等互信、友好协商的原则，通过谈判与陆地邻国处理陆地国界及相关事务，妥善解决争端和历史遗留的边界问题。

**Article 15** The State shall adhere to the principles of equality, mutual trust, and friendly consultation, and handle land borders and related affairs with neighboring land countries through negotiations, and properly resolve disputes and boundary issues left over from history.

### 第二章　陆地国界的划定和勘定

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家与陆地邻国通过谈判缔结划定陆地国界的条约，规定陆地国界的走向和位置。  
划定陆地国界的条约应当依照法律规定由国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准，由中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定予以批准。

**Article 16** The State shall conclude treaties on the delimitation of land borders with neighboring land countries through negotiations, specifying the alignment and location of the land borders.  
Treaties on the delimitation of land borders shall, in accordance with legal provisions, be submitted by the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for decision on ratification, and be ratified by the President of the People's Republic of China pursuant to the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家与陆地邻国根据划界条约，实地勘定陆地国界并缔结勘界条约。  
勘界条约应当依照法律规定由国务院核准。

**Article 17** The State shall, pursuant to the treaty on delimitation of land borders, demarcate the land borders on the ground with neighboring land countries and conclude a treaty on demarcation.  
Treaties on demarcation shall, in accordance with legal provisions, be approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　为保持国界线清晰稳定，国家与有关陆地邻国开展陆地国界联合检查，缔结联合检查条约。

**Article 18** To maintain the clarity and stability of the border line, the State shall conduct joint inspections of land borders with relevant neighboring land countries and conclude treaties on joint inspections.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　勘定陆地国界依据的自然地理环境发生无法恢复原状的重大变化时，国家可与陆地邻国协商，重新勘定陆地国界。

**Article 19** When major changes occur to the natural geographical environment on the basis of which the land borders are demarcated and such changes cannot be restored to their original state, the State may negotiate with the neighboring land countries to re-demarcate the land borders.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家设置界标在实地标示陆地国界。  
界标的位置、种类、规格、材质及设置方式等，由外交部与陆地邻国相关部门协商确定。

**Article 20** The State shall set up boundary markers to mark the land borders on the ground.  
The positions, types, specifications, materials and methods of installation of boundary markers shall be determined through consultation between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the relevant departments of neighboring land countries.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　陆地国界的划定、勘定、联合检查和设置界标等具体工作，由外交部会同国务院有关部门、有关军事机关和有关边境省、自治区依法组织实施。

**Article 21** The specific work of delimitation, survey, joint inspection, and erection of boundary markers regarding the land boundary shall be organized and implemented by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, relevant military authorities, and relevant border provinces and autonomous regions according to law.

### 第三章　陆地国界及边境的防卫

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队应当在边境开展边防执勤、管控，组织演训和勘察等活动，坚决防范、制止和打击入侵、蚕食、渗透、挑衅等行为，守卫陆地国界，维护边境安全稳定。

**Article 22** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall conduct border guarding duty, control, and organize activities such as exercises, training, and reconnaissance in border areas, and shall resolutely prevent, stop, and combat acts of invasion, encroachment, infiltration, provocation, etc., to guard the land boundary and maintain border security and stability.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　边境省、自治区的各级人民政府统筹资源配置，加强维护国界安全的群防队伍建设，支持和配合边防执勤、管控工作。  
边境省、自治区的各级人民政府建设基础设施，应当统筹兼顾陆地国界及边境防卫需求。  
公民和组织应当支持边防执勤、管控活动，为其提供便利条件或者其他协助。

**Article 23** The people's governments at various levels in border provinces and autonomous regions shall coordinate the allocation of resources, strengthen the building of mass defense teams for maintaining national boundary security, and support and cooperate with border guarding duty and control work.  
The people's governments at various levels in border provinces and autonomous regions, when constructing infrastructure, shall balance the needs of land boundary and border defense.  
Citizens and organizations shall support border guarding duty and control activities and provide convenient conditions or other assistance therefor.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家根据边防管控需要，可以在靠近陆地国界的特定区域划定边境禁区并设置警示标志，禁止无关人员进入。  
划定边境禁区应当兼顾经济社会发展、自然资源和生态环境保护、居民生产生活，由有关军事机关会同边境省、自治区人民政府提出方案，经征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院和中央军事委员会批准。  
边境禁区的变更或者撤销，依照前款规定程序办理。

**Article 24** The State may, based on the needs of border control, designate a border restricted zone in a specific area adjacent to the land boundary and set up warning signs, and prohibit irrelevant persons from entering.  
Designation of a border restricted zone shall take into account economic and social development, natural resources and ecological environment protection, and production and living of residents. A proposal shall be submitted by the relevant military authority jointly with the people's government of the border province or autonomous region, and after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council, shall be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.  
Alteration or revocation of a border restricted zone shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家根据陆地国界及边境防卫需要，可以在陆地国界内侧建设拦阻、交通、通信、监控、警戒、防卫及辅助设施等边防基础设施，也可以与陆地邻国协商后在陆地国界线上建设拦阻设施。  
边防基础设施的建设和维护在保证国家主权、安全和领土完整的前提下，兼顾经济社会发展、自然资源和生态环境保护、野生动物迁徙、居民生产生活的需要，并且不得损害陆地国界与边防基础设施之间领土的有效管控。  
边防基础设施的建设规划由国务院和中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 25** The State may, based on the needs of land boundary and border defense, construct border defense infrastructure including barricade, transportation, communication, surveillance, warning, defense, and auxiliary facilities within the inner side of the land boundary, and may also, after consultation with the neighboring land country, construct barricade facilities on the land boundary line.  
The construction and maintenance of border defense infrastructure, under the premise of ensuring national sovereignty, security, and territorial integrity, shall take into account the needs of economic and social development, natural resources and ecological environment protection, wildlife migration, and production and living of residents, and shall not impair the effective control of the territory between the land boundary and the border defense infrastructure.  
The construction plan for border defense infrastructure shall be approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　陆地国界及边境的管理

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家对陆地国界及边境的管理和相关建设实行统筹协调、分工负责、依法管理。


<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　陆地国界及边境管理应当保障陆地国界清晰和安全稳定。


<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　依照本法规定在边境地区设立经济、贸易、旅游等跨境合作区域或者开展跨境合作活动，应当符合边防管控要求，不得危害边防安全。


<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在陆地国界及边境管理中遇有重要情况和重大问题，有关地方人民政府、军事机关应当立即按照规定向上级机关报告。


<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　边境省、自治区可以根据本行政区域的具体情况和实际需要制定地方性法规、地方政府规章，对陆地国界及边境管理执行中的问题作出规定。


<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家可以与有关陆地邻国缔结条约，就陆地国界及边境管理制度作出规定。条约对陆地国界及边境管理制度另有规定的，按照条约的规定执行。


#### 第二节　陆地国界管理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　界标和边防基础设施受法律保护。  
任何组织或者个人不得擅自移动、损毁界标和边防基础设施。  
界标被移动、损毁、遗失的，由外交部与陆地邻国相关部门协商后组织恢复、修缮或者重建。


<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　为保持陆地国界清晰可视，国家可以在陆地国界内侧开辟和清理通视道。


<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院有关部门和边境省、自治区的各级人民政府应当采取措施维护界河（江、湖）走向稳定，并依照有关条约保护和合理利用边界水。  
船舶和人员需要进入界河（江、湖）活动的，应当由有关主管部门批准或者备案，向公安机关报告，并接受查验。


<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家经与陆地邻国协商，可以在靠近陆地国界的适当地点设立边境口岸。  
边境口岸的设立、关闭、管理等，应当依照法律法规和有关条约确定，并通过外交途径通知陆地邻国。


<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院有关部门经与陆地邻国相关部门协商或者根据照顾边民往来、维护边境安全稳定的需要，可以设立边民通道并予以规范管理。


<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品等应当依照相关法律法规通过陆地国界出境入境，并接受有关主管部门的检查、检疫和监管。  
特殊情况下，通过缔结条约或者经外交途径、主管部门协商后，有关人员、交通运输工具、货物、物品等应当从国务院、中央军事委员会或者其授权的部门批准的地点出境入境。


<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　禁止任何个人非法越界。  
非法越界人员被控制后，由公安机关等主管部门处理；非法越界人员为武装部队人员的，由有关军事机关处理。  
非法越界人员行凶、拒捕或者实施其他暴力行为，危及他人人身和财产安全的，执法执勤人员可以依法使用警械和武器。


<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　航空器飞越陆地国界，应当经有关主管机关批准并且遵守我国法律法规规定。未经批准飞越陆地国界的，有关主管机关应当采取必要措施进行处置。  
任何组织或者个人未经有关主管机关批准不得在陆地国界附近操控无人驾驶航空器飞行。模型航空器、三角翼、无人驾驶自由气球等的飞行活动，参照无人驾驶航空器管理。


<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　任何组织或者个人未经有关主管部门批准不得在陆地国界附近修建永久性建筑物。


<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何组织或者个人不得在陆地国界附近通过声音、光照、展示标示物、投掷或者传递物品、放置漂流物或者空飘物等方式从事危害国家安全或者影响我国与邻国友好关系的活动。  
个人在陆地国界及其附近打捞或者捡拾的漂流物、空飘物等可疑物品，应当及时交当地人民政府或者有关军事机关，不得擅自处理。


#### 第三节　边境管理

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家根据边防管理需要可以划定边境管理区。边境管理区人员通行、居住依照国家有关规定实行专门管理措施。  
边境管理区的划定、变更、撤销由边境省、自治区人民政府提出方案，经征求国务院有关部门和有关军事机关意见后，报国务院批准并公告。


<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家支持沿边城镇建设，健全沿边城镇体系，完善边境城镇功能，强化支撑能力建设。


<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院有关部门和边境省、自治区人民政府依照有关规定，可以建设或者批准设立边民互市贸易区（点）、边境经济合作区等区域。


<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院有关部门和边境省、自治区的各级人民政府应当采取措施保护边境生态环境，防止生态破坏，防治大气、水、土壤和其他污染。


<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院有关部门和边境省、自治区的各级人民政府应当采取措施预防传染病、动植物疫情、外来物种入侵以及洪涝、火灾等从陆地国界传入或者在边境传播、蔓延。


<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列情形之一的，国家可以封控边境、关闭口岸，并依照有关法律法规采取其他紧急措施：  
（一）周边发生战争或者武装冲突可能影响国家边防安全稳定；  
（二）发生使国家安全或者边境居民的人身财产安全受到严重威胁的重大事件；  
（三）边境受到自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件或者核生化污染的严重威胁；  
（四）其他严重影响陆地国界及边境安全稳定的情形。  
前款规定的措施由国务院有关部门、有关军事机关和地方人民政府依照相关法律和规定组织实施。


### 第五章　陆地国界事务的国际合作

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家按照平等互利原则与陆地邻国开展国际合作，处理陆地国界事务，推进安全合作，深化互利共赢。

**Article 48** The State shall, in accordance with the principle of equality and mutual benefit, conduct international cooperation with neighboring land countries, handle land boundary affairs, promote security cooperation, and deepen mutual benefit and win-win outcomes.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家可以与有关陆地邻国协商建立边界联合委员会机制，指导和协调有关国际合作，执行有关条约，协商并处理与陆地国界管理有关的重要事项。

**Article 49** The State may, through consultation with the relevant neighboring land country, establish a joint boundary commission mechanism to guide and coordinate relevant international cooperation, implement relevant treaties, and consult on and handle important matters relating to land boundary management.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　有关军事机关可以与陆地邻国相关部门建立边防合作机制，沟通协商边防交往合作中的重大事项与问题，通过与陆地邻国相关边防机构建立边防会谈会晤机制，交涉处理边防有关事务，巩固和发展睦邻友好关系，共同维护陆地国界的安全稳定。

**Article 50** The relevant military authorities may establish a border defense cooperation mechanism with the relevant departments of the neighboring land country to communicate and consult on major matters and issues in border defense exchanges and cooperation, and by establishing a border defense talks and meetings mechanism with the relevant border defense organs of the neighboring land country, negotiate and handle border defense-related affairs, consolidate and develop friendly and good-neighborly relations, and jointly maintain the security and stability of the land boundary.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家可以与有关陆地邻国在相应国界地段协商建立边界（边防）代表机制，由代表、副代表和相关工作人员组成，通过会谈、会晤和联合调查等方式处理边界事件、日常纠纷等问题。  
边界（边防）代表的具体工作在国务院有关部门、中央军事委员会机关有关部门指导下，由中国人民解放军会同外事、公安、移民等相关部门组织实施。

**Article 51** The State may, through consultation with the relevant neighboring land country in the corresponding boundary sector, establish a boundary (border defense) representative mechanism composed of representatives, deputy representatives, and relevant staff members, to handle boundary incidents, daily disputes, and other issues through talks, meetings, and joint investigations.  
The specific work of the boundary (border defense) representatives shall, under the guidance of the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission, be organized and implemented by the Chinese People's Liberation Army jointly with the relevant departments of foreign affairs, public security, immigration, and others.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公安、海关、移民等部门可以与陆地邻国相关部门建立合作机制，交流信息，开展执法合作，共同防范和打击跨界违法犯罪活动。

**Article 52** The departments of public security, customs, immigration, and others may establish cooperation mechanisms with the relevant departments of the neighboring land country to exchange information, conduct law enforcement cooperation, and jointly prevent and combat cross-border illegal and criminal activities.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家有关主管机关和有关军事机关可以与陆地邻国相关部门开展合作，共同打击恐怖主义、分裂主义和极端主义活动。

**Article 53** The relevant competent authorities of the State and the relevant military authorities may conduct cooperation with the relevant departments of the neighboring land country to jointly combat terrorism, separatism, and extremism activities.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家提升沿边对外开放便利化水平，优化边境地区营商环境；经与陆地邻国协商，可以在双方接壤区域设立跨境经济合作区、跨境旅游合作区、生态保护区等区域。  
国家与陆地邻国共同建设并维护跨界设施，可以在陆地国界内侧设立临时的封闭建设区。

**Article 54** The State shall enhance the facilitation level of opening up along the border and optimize the business environment in border areas; after consultation with the neighboring land country, areas such as cross-border economic cooperation zones, cross-border tourism cooperation zones, and ecological protection zones may be established in the contiguous areas of the two sides.  
The State and the neighboring land country shall jointly construct and maintain cross-border facilities, and may establish temporary enclosed construction zones on the inner side of the land boundary.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　边境省、自治区的县级以上各级人民政府依照国家有关规定，可以与陆地邻国相应行政区域地方政府开展经济、旅游、文化、体育、抢险救灾和生态环境等领域合作。

**Article 55** The people's governments at or above the county level in border provinces and autonomous regions may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, conduct cooperation with the local governments of the corresponding administrative regions of the neighboring land country in fields such as economy, tourism, culture, sports, emergency rescue and disaster relief, and ecological environment.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院有关部门可以与陆地邻国相关部门在口岸建设和管理、自然资源利用、生态环境保护、疫情防控、应急管理等领域开展合作，建立相互通报、信息共享、技术与人才交流等合作机制。  
边境省、自治区的县级以上各级人民政府可以在国家有关主管部门指导下，参与相关合作机制，承担具体合作工作。

**Article 56** The relevant departments of the State Council may conduct cooperation with the relevant departments of the neighboring land country in fields such as port construction and management, utilization of natural resources, ecological environment protection, epidemic prevention and control, and emergency management, and establish cooperation mechanisms such as mutual notification, information sharing, and technical and personnel exchanges.  
The people's governments at or above the county level in border provinces and autonomous regions may, under the guidance of the relevant competent departments of the State, participate in the relevant cooperation mechanisms and undertake specific cooperation work.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　有违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)第二款行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》、《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》有关规定处罚。损毁界标、边防基础设施的，应当责令行为人赔偿损失。  
有违反[本法第三十四条](#art-34)第二款或者第四十一条规定行为之一的，由公安机关处警告或者两千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以下拘留或者两千元以上一万元以下罚款。

**Article 57** Any person who violates paragraph 2 of Article 32 of this Law shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Military Facilities. If boundary markers or border defense infrastructure are damaged, the person who caused the damage shall be ordered to compensate for the loss.  
Any person who violates paragraph 2 of Article 34 or Article 41 of this Law shall be given a warning or a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan by the public security organ; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　有违反[本法第三十七条](#art-37)、第三十八条第一款或者第三十九条规定行为的，由有关部门依照相关法律法规，按照职责分工处罚。  
有违反[本法第四十条](#art-40)规定行为的，由有关主管部门责令停止违法行为、恢复原状，处五千元以上两万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处两万元以上五万元以下罚款。单位有违反该条规定行为的，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款。  
有违反[本法第三十四条](#art-34)第二款、第三十九条第二款或者第四十一条规定行为之一的，还可以收缴用于实施违反陆地国界及边境管理行为的工具。

**Article 58** Any person who violates Article 37, paragraph 1 of Article 38, or Article 39 of this Law shall be punished by the relevant departments in accordance with relevant laws and regulations according to their respective functions and duties.  
Any person who violates Article 40 of this Law shall be ordered by the relevant competent department to cease the illegal act and restore the original state, and shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan. If a unit violates the provisions of that Article, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.  
In any case where the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 34, paragraph 2 of Article 39, or Article 41 of this Law are violated, the tools used for carrying out the acts violating the land boundary and border management may also be confiscated.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家机关及其工作人员在陆地国界工作中不履行法定职责，泄露国家秘密或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 59** If a State organ or its staff member fails to perform statutory duties in the land boundary work, divulges State secrets, or abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　界标是指竖立在陆地国界上或者陆地国界两侧，在实地标示陆地国界走向，且其地理坐标已测定并记载于勘界条约或者联合检查条约中的标志，包括基本界标、辅助界标、导标和浮标等。  
通视道是指为使陆地国界保持通视，在陆地国界两侧一定宽度范围内开辟的通道。

**Article 61** Boundary markers refer to signs erected on the land boundary or on both sides of the land boundary, which indicate the alignment of the land boundary on the ground and whose geographical coordinates have been surveyed and recorded in the boundary delimitation treaty or joint inspection treaty, including basic boundary markers, auxiliary boundary markers, guide markers, and buoys, etc.  
Visibility line refers to a corridor opened within a certain width on both sides of the land boundary to maintain visibility of the land boundary.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本法自2022年1月1日起施行。

**Article 62** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2022.
