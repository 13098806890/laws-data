# 中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-06-20  
**生效日期**：2020-07-01

> （2020年6月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　政务处分的种类和适用 第三章　违法行为及其适用的政务处分 第四章　政务处分的程序 第五章　复审、复核 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范政务处分，加强对所有行使公权力的公职人员的监督，促进公职人员依法履职、秉公用权、廉洁从政从业、坚持道德操守，根据《中华人民共和国监察法》，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于监察机关对违法的公职人员给予政务处分的活动。 本法第二章、第三章适用于公职人员任免机关、单位对违法的公职人员给予处分。处分的程序、申诉等适用其他法律、行政法规、国务院部门规章和国家有关规定。 本法所称公职人员，是指《中华人民共和国监察法》第十五条规定的人员。 第三条　监察机关应当按照管理权限，加强对公职人员的监督，依法给予违法的公职人员政务处分。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范政务处分，加强对所有行使公权力的公职人员的监督，促进公职人员依法履职、秉公用权、廉洁从政从业、坚持道德操守，根据《中华人民共和国监察法》，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating disciplinary sanctions for public officials, strengthening supervision over all public officials exercising public power, and promoting public officials to perform their duties according to law, exercise their powers impartially, be honest and clean in politics and business, and adhere to moral integrity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于监察机关对违法的公职人员给予政务处分的活动。  
本法第二章、第三章适用于公职人员任免机关、单位对违法的公职人员给予处分。处分的程序、申诉等适用其他法律、行政法规、国务院部门规章和国家有关规定。  
本法所称公职人员，是指[《中华人民共和国监察法》第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法.md#art-15)规定的人员。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the activities of supervisory organs in imposing disciplinary sanctions on illegal public officials.  
Chapter II and Chapter III of this Law shall apply to the imposition of sanctions on illegal public officials by the organs and entities that appoint and remove public officials. The procedures for sanctions, petitions, and other matters shall be governed by other laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules of the State Council, and relevant state provisions.  
The term "public officials" as used in this Law refers to the personnel as prescribed in Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　监察机关应当按照管理权限，加强对公职人员的监督，依法给予违法的公职人员政务处分。  
公职人员任免机关、单位应当按照管理权限，加强对公职人员的教育、管理、监督，依法给予违法的公职人员处分。  
监察机关发现公职人员任免机关、单位应当给予处分而未给予，或者给予的处分违法、不当的，应当及时提出监察建议。

**Article 3** Supervisory organs shall, in accordance with their management authority, strengthen supervision over public officials and impose disciplinary sanctions on illegal public officials according to law.  
The organs and entities that appoint and remove public officials shall, in accordance with their management authority, strengthen education, management, and supervision over public officials, and impose sanctions on illegal public officials according to law.  
Where a supervisory organ discovers that an organ or entity that appoints and removes public officials should have imposed a sanction but fails to do so, or imposes an illegal or improper sanction, it shall promptly make a supervisory recommendation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　给予公职人员政务处分，坚持党管干部原则，集体讨论决定；坚持法律面前一律平等，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，给予的政务处分与违法行为的性质、情节、危害程度相当；坚持惩戒与教育相结合，宽严相济。

**Article 4** The imposition of disciplinary sanctions on public officials shall adhere to the principle of the Party managing cadres, with collective discussion and decision; adhere to equality before the law, taking facts as the basis and law as the criterion, ensuring that the disciplinary sanction imposed is commensurate with the nature, circumstances, and degree of harm of the illegal act; adhere to the combination of punishment and education, with leniency and severity being appropriately applied.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　给予公职人员政务处分，应当事实清楚、证据确凿、定性准确、处理恰当、程序合法、手续完备。

**Article 5** The imposition of disciplinary sanctions on public officials shall be based on clear facts, conclusive evidence, accurate characterization, appropriate handling, lawful procedures, and complete formalities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公职人员依法履行职责受法律保护，非因法定事由、非经法定程序，不受政务处分。

**Article 6** Public officials who perform their duties according to law shall be protected by law, and shall not be subject to disciplinary sanctions except for statutory reasons and through statutory procedures.

### 第二章　政务处分的种类和适用

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　政务处分的种类为：  
（一）警告；  
（二）记过；  
（三）记大过；  
（四）降级；  
（五）撤职；  
（六）开除。

**Article 7** The types of disciplinary sanctions are:  
(1) warning;  
(2) demerit;  
(3) serious demerit;  
(4) demotion;  
(5) removal from office;  
(6) dismissal.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　政务处分的期间为：  
（一）警告，六个月；  
（二）记过，十二个月；  
（三）记大过，十八个月；  
（四）降级、撤职，二十四个月。  
政务处分决定自作出之日起生效，政务处分期自政务处分决定生效之日起计算。

**Article 8** The duration of disciplinary sanctions shall be:  
(1) warning: six months;  
(2) demerit: twelve months;  
(3) serious demerit: eighteen months;  
(4) demotion and removal from office: twenty-four months.  
A decision on a disciplinary sanction shall take effect from the date it is made, and the period of the disciplinary sanction shall be calculated from the date the decision takes effect.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公职人员二人以上共同违法，根据各自在违法行为中所起的作用和应当承担的法律责任，分别给予政务处分。

**Article 9** Where two or more public officials jointly commit an illegal act, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed on each according to their respective roles in the illegal act and the legal liability they shall bear.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有关机关、单位、组织集体作出的决定违法或者实施违法行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员中的公职人员依法给予政务处分。

**Article 10** Where a decision made collectively by a relevant organ, entity, or organization is illegal or an illegal act is committed, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the public officials among the responsible leading personnel and directly liable personnel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公职人员有下列情形之一的，可以从轻或者减轻给予政务处分：  
（一）主动交代本人应当受到政务处分的违法行为的；  
（二）配合调查，如实说明本人违法事实的；  
（三）检举他人违纪违法行为，经查证属实的；  
（四）主动采取措施，有效避免、挽回损失或者消除不良影响的；  
（五）在共同违法行为中起次要或者辅助作用的；  
（六）主动上交或者退赔违法所得的；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他从轻或者减轻情节。

**Article 11** A disciplinary sanction may be mitigated or reduced under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) voluntarily confessing to the illegal act for which a disciplinary sanction should be imposed;  
(2) cooperating with the investigation and truthfully explaining the facts of the illegal act;  
(3) reporting the disciplinary or illegal acts of others, which is verified to be true;  
(4) voluntarily taking measures to effectively avoid, recover losses, or eliminate adverse effects;  
(5) playing a secondary or auxiliary role in a joint illegal act;  
(6) voluntarily surrendering or returning illegal gains;  
(7) other circumstances under which a lighter or mitigated sanction may be imposed as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公职人员违法行为情节轻微，且具有[本法第十一条](#art-11)规定的情形之一的，可以对其进行谈话提醒、批评教育、责令检查或者予以诫勉，免予或者不予政务处分。  
公职人员因不明真相被裹挟或者被胁迫参与违法活动，经批评教育后确有悔改表现的，可以减轻、免予或者不予政务处分。

**Article 12** Where the circumstances of a public official's illegal act are minor and any of the circumstances provided for in Article 11 of this Law exist, the public official may be given a talk as a reminder, criticism and education, ordered to make a self-examination, or admonished, and may be exempted from or not given a disciplinary sanction.  
Where a public official is coerced or compelled to participate in an illegal activity due to being unaware of the truth, and after criticism and education shows genuine repentance, a disciplinary sanction may be mitigated, exempted, or not imposed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公职人员有下列情形之一的，应当从重给予政务处分：  
（一）在政务处分期内再次故意违法，应当受到政务处分的；  
（二）阻止他人检举、提供证据的；  
（三）串供或者伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据的；  
（四）包庇同案人员的；  
（五）胁迫、唆使他人实施违法行为的；  
（六）拒不上交或者退赔违法所得的；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他从重情节。

**Article 13** A heavier disciplinary sanction shall be imposed under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) intentionally committing another illegal act during the period of a disciplinary sanction for which a disciplinary sanction should be imposed;  
(2) preventing others from reporting or providing evidence;  
(3) colluding to make false statements, or forging, concealing, or destroying evidence;  
(4) harboring other persons involved in the case;  
(5) coercing or instigating others to commit an illegal act;  
(6) refusing to surrender or return illegal gains;  
(7) other circumstances under which a heavier sanction shall be imposed as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公职人员犯罪，有下列情形之一的，予以开除：  
（一）因故意犯罪被判处管制、拘役或者有期徒刑以上刑罚（含宣告缓刑）的；  
（二）因过失犯罪被判处有期徒刑，刑期超过三年的；  
（三）因犯罪被单处或者并处剥夺政治权利的。  
因过失犯罪被判处管制、拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑的，一般应当予以开除；案件情况特殊，予以撤职更为适当的，可以不予开除，但是应当报请上一级机关批准。  
公职人员因犯罪被单处罚金，或者犯罪情节轻微，人民检察院依法作出不起诉决定或者人民法院依法免予刑事处罚的，予以撤职；造成不良影响的，予以开除。

**Article 14** Where a public official commits a crime and falls under any of the following circumstances, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) being sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment or above (including probation) for an intentional crime;  
(2) being sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment for a negligent crime with a term exceeding three years;  
(3) being sentenced to deprivation of political rights as a principal or supplementary punishment for a crime.  
Where a public official is sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years for a negligent crime, he or she shall generally be dismissed; if the circumstances of the case are special and removal from office is more appropriate, he or she may not be dismissed, but approval shall be sought from the authority at the next higher level.  
Where a public official is solely fined for a crime, or where the circumstances of the crime are minor and the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute according to law or the People's Court exempts from criminal punishment according to law, he or she shall be removed from office; if adverse effects are caused, he or she shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公职人员有两个以上违法行为的，应当分别确定政务处分。应当给予两种以上政务处分的，执行其中最重的政务处分；应当给予撤职以下多个相同政务处分的，可以在一个政务处分期以上、多个政务处分期之和以下确定政务处分期，但是最长不得超过四十八个月。

**Article 15** Where a public official has two or more illegal acts, disciplinary sanctions shall be determined separately. Where two or more types of disciplinary sanctions should be imposed, the heaviest of the sanctions shall be enforced; where multiple identical disciplinary sanctions below removal from office should be imposed, the period of the disciplinary sanction may be determined as not less than the period for one sanction and not more than the sum of the periods for all sanctions, but shall not exceed forty-eight months.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对公职人员的同一违法行为，监察机关和公职人员任免机关、单位不得重复给予政务处分和处分。

**Article 16** For the same illegal act of a public official, the supervisory organ and the organ or entity that appoints and removes the public official shall not impose both a disciplinary sanction and a sanction repeatedly.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公职人员有违法行为，有关机关依照规定给予组织处理的，监察机关可以同时给予政务处分。

**Article 17** Where a public official has an illegal act and the relevant organ imposes organizational handling in accordance with regulations, the supervisory organ may simultaneously impose a disciplinary sanction.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　担任领导职务的公职人员有违法行为，被罢免、撤销、免去或者辞去领导职务的，监察机关可以同时给予政务处分。

**Article 18** Where a public official holding a leadership position commits an illegal act and is removed from, dismissed from, relieved of, or resigns from the leadership position, the supervisory organ may simultaneously impose a disciplinary sanction.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公务员以及参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的人员在政务处分期内，不得晋升职务、职级、衔级和级别；其中，被记过、记大过、降级、撤职的，不得晋升工资档次。被撤职的，按照规定降低职务、职级、衔级和级别，同时降低工资和待遇。

**Article 19** Civil servants and personnel managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China shall not be promoted in position, rank, grade, or level during the period of a disciplinary sanction; among them, those who have received a demerit, serious demerit, demotion, or removal from office shall not be promoted in wage scales. Those who have been removed from office shall be demoted in position, rank, grade, and level in accordance with regulations, and their wages and benefits shall also be reduced accordingly.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　法律、法规授权或者受国家机关依法委托管理公共事务的组织中从事公务的人员，以及公办的教育、科研、文化、医疗卫生、体育等单位中从事管理的人员，在政务处分期内，不得晋升职务、岗位和职员等级、职称；其中，被记过、记大过、降级、撤职的，不得晋升薪酬待遇等级。被撤职的，降低职务、岗位或者职员等级，同时降低薪酬待遇。

**Article 20** Personnel who perform official duties in organizations authorized by laws and regulations or entrusted by state organs according to law to manage public affairs, as well as personnel who engage in management in public educational, scientific research, cultural, medical and health, sports and other units, shall not be promoted in position, post, staff rank, or professional title during the period of a disciplinary sanction; among them, those who have received a demerit, serious demerit, demotion, or removal from office shall not be promoted in salary and benefit grades. Those who have been removed from office shall be demoted in position, post, or staff rank, and their salary and benefits shall also be reduced accordingly.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国有企业管理人员在政务处分期内，不得晋升职务、岗位等级和职称；其中，被记过、记大过、降级、撤职的，不得晋升薪酬待遇等级。被撤职的，降低职务或者岗位等级，同时降低薪酬待遇。

**Article 21** A manager of a state-owned enterprise shall, during the period of a disciplinary sanction, not be promoted in position, rank, or professional title; if he or she has been given a demerit, a serious demerit, demotion, or removal from office, he or she shall not be promoted in salary grade. If removed from office, his or her position or rank shall be reduced, and salary shall be reduced accordingly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　基层群众性自治组织中从事管理的人员有违法行为的，监察机关可以予以警告、记过、记大过。  
基层群众性自治组织中从事管理的人员受到政务处分的，应当由县级或者乡镇人民政府根据具体情况减发或者扣发补贴、奖金。

**Article 22** If a person engaged in management in a grassroots self-governing mass organization commits an illegal act, the supervisory organ may issue a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit.  
If a person engaged in management in a grassroots self-governing mass organization is subject to a disciplinary sanction, the people's government at the county or township level shall, according to the specific circumstances, reduce or withhold subsidies and bonuses.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　[《中华人民共和国监察法》第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法.md#art-15)第六项规定的人员有违法行为的，监察机关可以予以警告、记过、记大过。情节严重的，由所在单位直接给予或者监察机关建议有关机关、单位给予降低薪酬待遇、调离岗位、解除人事关系或者劳动关系等处理。  
[《中华人民共和国监察法》第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法.md#art-15)第二项规定的人员，未担任公务员、参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的人员、事业单位工作人员或者国有企业人员职务的，对其违法行为依照前款规定处理。

**Article 23** If a person specified in Item 6 of Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China commits an illegal act, the supervisory organ may issue a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit. If the circumstances are serious, the unit to which he or she belongs shall directly impose, or the supervisory organ shall recommend that the relevant organ or unit impose, a reduction in salary, transfer from the post, or termination of the personnel relationship or labor relationship.  
If a person specified in Item 2 of Article 15 of the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China does not hold a position as a public servant, personnel managed by reference to the Public Servants Law of the People's Republic of China, staff of a public institution, or a manager of a state-owned enterprise, his or her illegal act shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公职人员被开除，或者依照[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定，受到解除人事关系或者劳动关系处理的，不得录用为公务员以及参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的人员。

**Article 24** If a public official is dismissed, or, in accordance with Article 23 of this Law, is subject to termination of the personnel relationship or labor relationship, he or she shall not be recruited as a public servant or as personnel managed by reference to the Public Servants Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公职人员违法取得的财物和用于违法行为的本人财物，除依法应当由其他机关没收、追缴或者责令退赔的，由监察机关没收、追缴或者责令退赔；应当退还原所有人或者原持有人的，依法予以退还；属于国家财产或者不应当退还以及无法退还的，上缴国库。  
公职人员因违法行为获得的职务、职级、衔级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、待遇、资格、学历、学位、荣誉、奖励等其他利益，监察机关应当建议有关机关、单位、组织按规定予以纠正。

**Article 25** The property illegally obtained by a public official and his or her own property used for the illegal act shall, unless it should be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be returned or compensated by another organ according to law, be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be returned or compensated by the supervisory organ; if it shall be returned to the original owner or holder, it shall be returned according to law; if it is state property or should not be returned or cannot be returned, it shall be turned over to the state treasury.  
If a public official has obtained other benefits such as position, rank, grade, level, post and staff grade, professional title, treatment, qualification, educational background, degree, honor, or reward as a result of an illegal act, the supervisory organ shall recommend that the relevant organ, unit, or organization correct such benefits in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公职人员被开除的，自政务处分决定生效之日起，应当解除其与所在机关、单位的人事关系或者劳动关系。  
公职人员受到开除以外的政务处分，在政务处分期内有悔改表现，并且没有再发生应当给予政务处分的违法行为的，政务处分期满后自动解除，晋升职务、职级、衔级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、薪酬待遇不再受原政务处分影响。但是，解除降级、撤职的，不恢复原职务、职级、衔级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、薪酬待遇。

**Article 26** If a public official is dismissed, the personnel relationship or labor relationship between him or her and the organ or unit to which he or she belongs shall be terminated from the date on which the decision on the disciplinary sanction takes effect.  
If a public official is subject to a disciplinary sanction other than dismissal, and during the period of the disciplinary sanction he or she shows repentance and no longer commits any illegal act for which a disciplinary sanction should be imposed, the disciplinary sanction shall be automatically lifted upon expiry of the period, and promotion in position, rank, grade, level, post and staff grade, professional title, and salary shall no longer be affected by the original disciplinary sanction. However, if demotion or removal from office is lifted, the original position, rank, grade, level, post and staff grade, professional title, and salary shall not be restored.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　已经退休的公职人员退休前或者退休后有违法行为的，不再给予政务处分，但是可以对其立案调查；依法应当予以降级、撤职、开除的，应当按照规定相应调整其享受的待遇，对其违法取得的财物和用于违法行为的本人财物依照[本法第二十五条](#art-25)的规定处理。  
已经离职或者死亡的公职人员在履职期间有违法行为的，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 27** If a public official who has retired commits an illegal act before or after retirement, no disciplinary sanction shall be imposed, but a case may be filed for investigation; if demotion, removal from office, or dismissal should be imposed according to law, the benefits he or she enjoys shall be adjusted accordingly in accordance with the regulations, and the property illegally obtained and his or her own property used for the illegal act shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 25 of this Law.  
If a public official who has left office or died commits an illegal act during his or her tenure, such act shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

### 第三章　违法行为及其适用的政务处分

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列行为之一的，予以记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）散布有损宪法权威、中国共产党领导和国家声誉的言论的；  
（二）参加旨在反对宪法、中国共产党领导和国家的集会、游行、示威等活动的；  
（三）拒不执行或者变相不执行中国共产党和国家的路线方针政策、重大决策部署的；  
（四）参加非法组织、非法活动的；  
（五）挑拨、破坏民族关系，或者参加民族分裂活动的；  
（六）利用宗教活动破坏民族团结和社会稳定的；  
（七）在对外交往中损害国家荣誉和利益的。  
有前款第二项、第四项、第五项和第六项行为之一的，对策划者、组织者和骨干分子，予以开除。  
公开发表反对宪法确立的国家指导思想，反对中国共产党领导，反对社会主义制度，反对改革开放的文章、演说、宣言、声明等的，予以开除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-17" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a demerit or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) Spreading remarks that harm the authority of the Constitution, the leadership of the Communist Party of China, or the reputation of the State;  
(2) Participating in assemblies, processions, demonstrations, or other activities aimed at opposing the Constitution, the leadership of the Communist Party of China, or the State;  
(3) Refusing to implement or covertly failing to implement the lines, principles, policies, and major decisions and deployments of the Communist Party of China and the State;  
(4) Participating in illegal organizations or illegal activities;  
(5) Instigating or undermining ethnic relations, or participating in ethnic separatist activities;  
(6) Using religious activities to undermine ethnic unity and social stability;  
(7) Damaging the honor or interests of the State in foreign exchanges.  
If a person commits any of the acts specified in Items 2, 4, 5, or 6 of the preceding paragraph, the planners, organizers, and key members shall be dismissed.  
If a person publicly publishes articles, speeches, declarations, or statements opposing the guiding ideology of the State established by the Constitution, opposing the leadership of the Communist Party of China, opposing the socialist system, or opposing reform and opening up, he or she shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　不按照规定请示、报告重大事项，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职。  
违反个人有关事项报告规定，隐瞒不报，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过。  
篡改、伪造本人档案资料的，予以记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职。

**Article 29** If a person fails to request instructions or report major matters in accordance with the regulations, and if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office.  
If a person violates the provisions on reporting personal related matters and conceals facts or fails to report, and if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit.  
If a person falsifies or forges his or her own archival materials, he or she shall be given a demerit or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职：  
（一）违反民主集中制原则，个人或者少数人决定重大事项，或者拒不执行、擅自改变集体作出的重大决定的；  
（二）拒不执行或者变相不执行、拖延执行上级依法作出的决定、命令的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-18" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office:  
(1) Violating the principle of democratic centralism by having an individual or a minority decide major matters, or refusing to implement or arbitrarily changing major decisions made by the collective;  
(2) Refusing to implement, covertly failing to implement, or delaying the implementation of decisions or orders made by superiors according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反规定出境或者办理因私出境证件的，予以记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职。  
违反规定取得外国国籍或者获取境外永久居留资格、长期居留许可的，予以撤职或者开除。

**Article 31** If a person leaves the country or applies for a passport for private purposes in violation of regulations, he or she shall be given a demerit or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office.  
If a person obtains foreign nationality or acquires permanent residence status or long-term residence permit abroad in violation of regulations, he or she shall be removed from office or dismissed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）在选拔任用、录用、聘用、考核、晋升、评选等干部人事工作中违反有关规定的；  
（二）弄虚作假，骗取职务、职级、衔级、级别、岗位和职员等级、职称、待遇、资格、学历、学位、荣誉、奖励或者其他利益的；  
（三）对依法行使批评、申诉、控告、检举等权利的行为进行压制或者打击报复的；  
（四）诬告陷害，意图使他人受到名誉损害或者责任追究等不良影响的；  
（五）以暴力、威胁、贿赂、欺骗等手段破坏选举的。

**Article 32** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) Violating relevant provisions in cadre and personnel work such as selection, appointment, recruitment, employment, assessment, promotion, or evaluation;  
(2) Engaging in fraud to obtain positions, ranks, grades, levels, posts and staff grades, professional titles, treatments, qualifications, educational backgrounds, degrees, honors, rewards, or other benefits;  
(3) Suppressing or retaliating against acts of exercising rights such as criticism, petition, accusation, or reporting according to law;  
(4) Making false accusations with the intent to cause adverse effects such as damage to reputation or accountability;  
(5) Disrupting elections by violence, threat, bribery, deception, or other means.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）贪污贿赂的；  
（二）利用职权或者职务上的影响为本人或者他人谋取私利的；  
（三）纵容、默许特定关系人利用本人职权或者职务上的影响谋取私利的。  
拒不按照规定纠正特定关系人违规任职、兼职或者从事经营活动，且不服从职务调整的，予以撤职。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-19" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) Engaging in embezzlement or bribery;  
(2) Using his or her authority or the influence of his or her position to seek private interests for himself or herself or others;  
(3) Indulging or acquiescing to a specific relation using his or her authority or the influence of his or her position to seek private interests.  
If a person refuses to correct the specific relation's illegal tenure, part-time employment, or business activities in accordance with the regulations and does not comply with an adjustment of post, he or she shall be removed from office.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　收受可能影响公正行使公权力的礼品、礼金、有价证券等财物的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除。  
向公职人员及其特定关系人赠送可能影响公正行使公权力的礼品、礼金、有价证券等财物，或者接受、提供可能影响公正行使公权力的宴请、旅游、健身、娱乐等活动安排，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职。

**Article 34** Any person who accepts gifts, cash, securities, or other property that may affect the impartial exercise of public power shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed.  
If a person gives gifts, cash, securities, or other property to a public official or his or her specific relation that may affect the impartial exercise of public power, or accepts or provides arrangements for banquets, travel, fitness, entertainment, or other activities that may affect the impartial exercise of public power, and if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有下列行为之一，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职：  
（一）违反规定设定、发放薪酬或者津贴、补贴、奖金的；  
（二）违反规定，在公务接待、公务交通、会议活动、办公用房以及其他工作生活保障等方面超标准、超范围的；  
（三）违反规定公款消费的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-20" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office:  
(1) Setting or distributing salaries, allowances, subsidies, or bonuses in violation of regulations;  
(2) Exceeding standards or scope in official reception, official transportation, meetings and activities, office space, or other work and life support in violation of regulations;  
(3) Using public funds for consumption in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反规定从事或者参与营利性活动，或者违反规定兼任职务、领取报酬的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-21" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** Any person who engages in or participates in for-profit activities in violation of regulations, or concurrently holds a position or receives remuneration in violation of regulations, shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　利用宗族或者黑恶势力等欺压群众，或者纵容、包庇黑恶势力活动的，予以撤职；情节严重的，予以开除。

**Article 37** Any person who uses clan or mafia-like forces to oppress the masses, or indulges or harbors activities of mafia-like forces, shall be removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　有下列行为之一，情节较重的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节严重的，予以降级或者撤职：  
（一）违反规定向管理服务对象收取、摊派财物的；  
（二）在管理服务活动中故意刁难、吃拿卡要的；  
（三）在管理服务活动中态度恶劣粗暴，造成不良后果或者影响的；  
（四）不按照规定公开工作信息，侵犯管理服务对象知情权，造成不良后果或者影响的；  
（五）其他侵犯管理服务对象利益的行为，造成不良后果或者影响的。  
有前款第一项、第二项和第五项行为，情节特别严重的，予以开除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-22" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall, if the circumstances are relatively serious, be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office:  
(1) Collecting or apportioning property from the objects of management or service in violation of regulations;  
(2) Deliberately causing difficulties or extorting bribes in management or service activities;  
(3) Having a bad or rough attitude in management or service activities, causing adverse consequences or effects;  
(4) Failing to disclose work information in accordance with regulations, infringing upon the right to know of the objects of management or service, causing adverse consequences or effects;  
(5) Other acts that infringe upon the interests of the objects of management or service, causing adverse consequences or effects.  
If a person commits any of the acts specified in Items 1, 2, and 5 of the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are especially serious, he or she shall be dismissed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有下列行为之一，造成不良后果或者影响的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）滥用职权，危害国家利益、社会公共利益或者侵害公民、法人、其他组织合法权益的；  
（二）不履行或者不正确履行职责，玩忽职守，贻误工作的；  
（三）工作中有形式主义、官僚主义行为的；  
（四）工作中有弄虚作假，误导、欺骗行为的；  
（五）泄露国家秘密、工作秘密，或者泄露因履行职责掌握的商业秘密、个人隐私的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-23" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-24" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第24条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-25" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第25条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 39** Any person who commits one of the following acts causing adverse consequences or effects shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) Abusing authority, endangering the interests of the State or social public interests, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations;  
(2) Failing to perform or improperly performing duties, neglecting duties, or delaying work;  
(3) Engaging in formalism or bureaucracy in work;  
(4) Engaging in falsification, misleading, or deception in work;  
(5) Disclosing State secrets, work secrets, or trade secrets or personal privacy obtained in the performance of duties.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有下列行为之一的，予以警告、记过或者记大过；情节较重的，予以降级或者撤职；情节严重的，予以开除：  
（一）违背社会公序良俗，在公共场所有不当行为，造成不良影响的；  
（二）参与或者支持迷信活动，造成不良影响的；  
（三）参与赌博的；  
（四）拒不承担赡养、抚养、扶养义务的；  
（五）实施家庭暴力，虐待、遗弃家庭成员的；  
（六）其他严重违反家庭美德、社会公德的行为。  
吸食、注射毒品，组织赌博，组织、支持、参与卖淫、嫖娼、色情淫乱活动的，予以撤职或者开除。

**Article 40** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning, a demerit, or a serious demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he or she shall be demoted or removed from office; if the circumstances are serious, he or she shall be dismissed:  
(1) Violating public order and good customs, engaging in inappropriate behavior in public places, causing adverse effects;  
(2) Participating in or supporting superstitious activities, causing adverse effects;  
(3) Participating in gambling;  
(4) Refusing to fulfill obligations of support, maintenance, or alimony;  
(5) Committing domestic violence, abusing or abandoning family members;  
(6) Other acts that seriously violate family virtues or social morality.  
If a person uses or injects drugs, organizes gambling, or organizes, supports, or participates in prostitution, solicitation, or obscene and promiscuous activities, he or she shall be removed from office or dismissed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　公职人员有其他违法行为，影响公职人员形象，损害国家和人民利益的，可以根据情节轻重给予相应政务处分。

**Article 41** If a public official commits other illegal acts that affect the image of public officials and harm the interests of the State and the people, the corresponding administrative sanction may be imposed according to the gravity of the circumstances.

### 第四章　政务处分的程序

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　监察机关对涉嫌违法的公职人员进行调查，应当由二名以上工作人员进行。监察机关进行调查时，有权依法向有关单位和个人了解情况，收集、调取证据。有关单位和个人应当如实提供情况。  
严禁以威胁、引诱、欺骗及其他非法方式收集证据。以非法方式收集的证据不得作为给予政务处分的依据。

**Article 42** A supervisory organ shall conduct investigation of a public official suspected of violating the law by no fewer than two staff members. When conducting an investigation, the supervisory organ shall have the right to learn about the situation from relevant entities and individuals and collect and obtain evidence according to law. The relevant entities and individuals shall provide truthful information.  
It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence by means of threats, inducement, deception, or other illegal methods. Evidence collected by illegal methods shall not be used as the basis for imposing an administrative sanction.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　作出政务处分决定前，监察机关应当将调查认定的违法事实及拟给予政务处分的依据告知被调查人，听取被调查人的陈述和申辩，并对其陈述的事实、理由和证据进行核实，记录在案。被调查人提出的事实、理由和证据成立的，应予采纳。不得因被调查人的申辩而加重政务处分。

**Article 43** Before making a decision on an administrative sanction, the supervisory organ shall inform the person under investigation of the illegal facts identified through investigation and the basis for the proposed administrative sanction, hear his or her statements and defense, verify the facts, reasons, and evidence presented, and record them in the case file. If the facts, reasons, and evidence presented by the person under investigation are established, they shall be adopted. The administrative sanction shall not be aggravated because of the person under investigation's defense.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　调查终结后，监察机关应当根据下列不同情况，分别作出处理：  
（一）确有应受政务处分的违法行为的，根据情节轻重，按照政务处分决定权限，履行规定的审批手续后，作出政务处分决定；  
（二）违法事实不能成立的，撤销案件；  
（三）符合免予、不予政务处分条件的，作出免予、不予政务处分决定；  
（四）被调查人涉嫌其他违法或者犯罪行为的，依法移送主管机关处理。

**Article 44** After the investigation is concluded, the supervisory organ shall, according to the different circumstances, make the following dispositions:  
(1) if there is indeed an illegal act that shall be subject to an administrative sanction, it shall, based on the gravity of the circumstances and within the power to decide on administrative sanctions, go through the prescribed approval procedures and make a decision on an administrative sanction;  
(2) if the illegal facts cannot be established, it shall dismiss the case;  
(3) if the conditions for exemption from or non-imposition of an administrative sanction are met, it shall make a decision on exemption from or non-imposition of an administrative sanction; and  
(4) if the person under investigation is suspected of other illegal or criminal acts, it shall transfer the case to the competent organ for handling according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　决定给予政务处分的，应当制作政务处分决定书。  
政务处分决定书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）被处分人的姓名、工作单位和职务；  
（二）违法事实和证据；  
（三）政务处分的种类和依据；  
（四）不服政务处分决定，申请复审、复核的途径和期限；  
（五）作出政务处分决定的机关名称和日期。  
政务处分决定书应当盖有作出决定的监察机关的印章。

**Article 45** If a decision is made to impose an administrative sanction, a written decision on the administrative sanction shall be prepared.  
The written decision on the administrative sanction shall specify the following:  
(1) the name, work unit, and position of the person sanctioned;  
(2) the illegal facts and evidence;  
(3) the type of administrative sanction and the legal basis;  
(4) the channels and time limit for applying for review or reconsideration if the decision is not accepted; and  
(5) the name of the organ making the decision on the administrative sanction and the date.  
The written decision on the administrative sanction shall bear the seal of the supervisory organ that made the decision.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　政务处分决定书应当及时送达被处分人和被处分人所在机关、单位，并在一定范围内宣布。  
作出政务处分决定后，监察机关应当根据被处分人的具体身份书面告知相关的机关、单位。

**Article 46** The written decision on the administrative sanction shall be timely served on the person sanctioned and the organ or entity where the person sanctioned works, and shall be announced within a certain scope.  
After making the decision on the administrative sanction, the supervisory organ shall, based on the specific identity of the person sanctioned, inform the relevant organs or entities in writing.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　参与公职人员违法案件调查、处理的人员有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，被调查人、检举人及其他有关人员也有权要求其回避：  
（一）是被调查人或者检举人的近亲属的；  
（二）担任过本案的证人的；  
（三）本人或者其近亲属与调查的案件有利害关系的；  
（四）可能影响案件公正调查、处理的其他情形。

**Article 47** Any person participating in the investigation or handling of a case involving illegal acts of a public official shall withdraw from the case under any of the following circumstances, and the person under investigation, the informant, and other relevant persons shall also have the right to request his or her withdrawal:  
(1) being a close relative of the person under investigation or the informant;  
(2) having served as a witness in the case;  
(3) having a personal interest in the case under investigation, or such interest existing for his or her close relatives; or  
(4) any other circumstances that may affect the impartial investigation or handling of the case.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　监察机关负责人的回避，由上级监察机关决定；其他参与违法案件调查、处理人员的回避，由监察机关负责人决定。  
监察机关或者上级监察机关发现参与违法案件调查、处理人员有应当回避情形的，可以直接决定该人员回避。

**Article 48** The withdrawal of the person in charge of a supervisory organ shall be decided by the supervisory organ at the next higher level; the withdrawal of other persons participating in the investigation or handling of an illegal case shall be decided by the person in charge of the supervisory organ.  
If a supervisory organ or the supervisory organ at the next higher level discovers that a person participating in the investigation or handling of an illegal case is under a circumstance that requires withdrawal, it may directly decide on the withdrawal of that person.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公职人员依法受到刑事责任追究的，监察机关应当根据司法机关的生效判决、裁定、决定及其认定的事实和情节，依照本法规定给予政务处分。  
公职人员依法受到行政处罚，应当给予政务处分的，监察机关可以根据行政处罚决定认定的事实和情节，经立案调查核实后，依照本法给予政务处分。  
监察机关根据本条第一款、第二款的规定作出政务处分后，司法机关、行政机关依法改变原生效判决、裁定、决定等，对原政务处分决定产生影响的，监察机关应当根据改变后的判决、裁定、决定等重新作出相应处理。

**Article 49** Where a public official is subject to criminal liability according to law, the supervisory organ shall impose an administrative sanction in accordance with the provisions of this Law based on the effective judgments, rulings, or decisions of the judicial organs and the facts and circumstances determined thereby.  
Where a public official is subject to an administrative penalty according to law and shall be given an administrative sanction, the supervisory organ may, after filing a case for investigation and verification, impose an administrative sanction in accordance with this Law based on the facts and circumstances determined in the administrative penalty decision.  
After the supervisory organ imposes an administrative sanction in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, if the judicial organ or administrative organ subsequently changes the original effective judgment, ruling, decision, etc. according to law, and such change affects the original administrative sanction decision, the supervisory organ shall make corresponding disposition anew based on the changed judgment, ruling, decision, etc.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　监察机关对经各级人民代表大会、县级以上各级人民代表大会常务委员会选举或者决定任命的公职人员予以撤职、开除的，应当先依法罢免、撤销或者免去其职务，再依法作出政务处分决定。  
监察机关对经中国人民政治协商会议各级委员会全体会议或者其常务委员会选举或者决定任命的公职人员予以撤职、开除的，应当先依章程免去其职务，再依法作出政务处分决定。  
监察机关对各级人民代表大会代表、中国人民政治协商会议各级委员会委员给予政务处分的，应当向有关的人民代表大会常务委员会，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团或者中国人民政治协商会议委员会常务委员会通报。

**Article 50** Where a supervisory organ imposes the sanction of removal from office or expulsion on a public official elected or appointed by decision through the people's congresses at all levels or the standing committees of the people's congresses at or above the county level, it shall first, according to law, have the official recalled, removed, or dismissed from the post, and then make a decision on the administrative sanction according to law.  
Where a supervisory organ imposes the sanction of removal from office or expulsion on a public official elected or appointed by decision through the plenary sessions of the committees of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) at all levels or their standing committees, it shall first remove the official from the post in accordance with the relevant constitution, and then make a decision on the administrative sanction according to law.  
Where a supervisory organ imposes an administrative sanction on a deputy to a people's congress at any level or a member of a CPPCC committee at any level, it shall inform the relevant standing committee of the people's congress, the presidium of the people's congress of a township, nationality township, or town, or the standing committee of the CPPCC committee.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　下级监察机关根据上级监察机关的指定管辖决定进行调查的案件，调查终结后，对不属于本监察机关管辖范围内的监察对象，应当交有管理权限的监察机关依法作出政务处分决定。

**Article 51** For a case investigated by a lower-level supervisory organ in accordance with the decision on designated jurisdiction of a higher-level supervisory organ, after the investigation is concluded, if the subject of supervision is not within the jurisdiction of the said supervisory organ, the case shall be transferred to a supervisory organ with the authority to handle it, which shall make a decision on the administrative sanction according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公职人员涉嫌违法，已经被立案调查，不宜继续履行职责的，公职人员任免机关、单位可以决定暂停其履行职务。  
公职人员在被立案调查期间，未经监察机关同意，不得出境、辞去公职；被调查公职人员所在机关、单位及上级机关、单位不得对其交流、晋升、奖励、处分或者办理退休手续。

**Article 52** If a public official is suspected of violating the law and has been placed on file for investigation, and it is inappropriate for him or her to continue to perform duties, the organ or entity that appoints or removes the public official may decide to suspend his or her duties.  
During the period when a public official is under investigation, without the consent of the supervisory organ, he or she shall not leave the country or resign from public office; the organ or entity where the public official under investigation works and the higher-level organ or entity thereof shall not arrange for his or her transfer, promotion, reward, sanction, or handle retirement procedures.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　监察机关在调查中发现公职人员受到不实检举、控告或者诬告陷害，造成不良影响的，应当按照规定及时澄清事实，恢复名誉，消除不良影响。

**Article 53** If, during an investigation, a supervisory organ discovers that a public official has been subjected to false reporting, accusation, or framing, which has caused adverse effects, it shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly clarify the facts, restore the official's reputation, and eliminate the adverse effects.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　公职人员受到政务处分的，应当将政务处分决定书存入其本人档案。对于受到降级以上政务处分的，应当由人事部门按照管理权限在作出政务处分决定后一个月内办理职务、工资及其他有关待遇等的变更手续；特殊情况下，经批准可以适当延长办理期限，但是最长不得超过六个月。

**Article 54** Where a public official is given an administrative sanction, the written decision on the administrative sanction shall be placed in his or her personal file. For a person who receives an administrative sanction of demotion or a heavier sanction, the personnel department shall, in accordance with the management authority, handle the procedures for changing the post, salary, and other relevant benefits within one month after the decision on the administrative sanction is made; under special circumstances, the time limit may be appropriately extended upon approval, but the extension shall not exceed six months at most.

### 第五章　复审、复核

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　公职人员对监察机关作出的涉及本人的政务处分决定不服的，可以依法向作出决定的监察机关申请复审；公职人员对复审决定仍不服的，可以向上一级监察机关申请复核。  
监察机关发现本机关或者下级监察机关作出的政务处分决定确有错误的，应当及时予以纠正或者责令下级监察机关及时予以纠正。

**Article 55** If a public official refuses to accept the decision on an administrative sanction made by a supervisory organ concerning himself or herself, he or she may apply for review to the supervisory organ that made the decision according to law; if the public official still refuses to accept the review decision, he or she may apply for reconsideration to the supervisory organ at the next higher level.  
If a supervisory organ finds that a decision on an administrative sanction made by itself or by a lower-level supervisory organ is indeed erroneous, it shall promptly correct it or order the lower-level supervisory organ to promptly correct it.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　复审、复核期间，不停止原政务处分决定的执行。  
公职人员不因提出复审、复核而被加重政务处分。

**Article 56** During the period of review or reconsideration, the enforcement of the original decision on the administrative sanction shall not be suspended.  
A public official shall not be given a heavier administrative sanction because he or she files for review or reconsideration.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　有下列情形之一的，复审、复核机关应当撤销原政务处分决定，重新作出决定或者责令原作出决定的监察机关重新作出决定：  
（一）政务处分所依据的违法事实不清或者证据不足的；  
（二）违反法定程序，影响案件公正处理的；  
（三）超越职权或者滥用职权作出政务处分决定的。

**Article 57** Under any of the following circumstances, the organ conducting review or reconsideration shall revoke the original decision on the administrative sanction and make a new decision or order the supervisory organ that made the original decision to make a new decision:  
(1) the illegal facts on which the administrative sanction is based are unclear or the evidence is insufficient;  
(2) the statutory procedures have been violated, affecting the impartial handling of the case; or  
(3) the decision on the administrative sanction was made by exceeding the power of authority or by abusing power.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　有下列情形之一的，复审、复核机关应当变更原政务处分决定，或者责令原作出决定的监察机关予以变更：  
（一）适用法律、法规确有错误的；  
（二）对违法行为的情节认定确有错误的；  
（三）政务处分不当的。

**Article 58** Under any of the following circumstances, the organ conducting review or reconsideration shall modify the original decision on the administrative sanction, or order the supervisory organ that made the original decision to modify it:  
(1) the application of laws or regulations is indeed erroneous;  
(2) the determination of the circumstances of the illegal act is indeed erroneous; or  
(3) the administrative sanction is inappropriate.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　复审、复核机关认为政务处分决定认定事实清楚，适用法律正确的，应当予以维持。

**Article 59** If the organ conducting review or reconsideration considers that the facts determined in the decision on the administrative sanction are clear and the applicable laws are correct, it shall affirm the decision.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　公职人员的政务处分决定被变更，需要调整该公职人员的职务、职级、衔级、级别、岗位和职员等级或者薪酬待遇等的，应当按照规定予以调整。政务处分决定被撤销的，应当恢复该公职人员的级别、薪酬待遇，按照原职务、职级、衔级、岗位和职员等级安排相应的职务、职级、衔级、岗位和职员等级，并在原政务处分决定公布范围内为其恢复名誉。没收、追缴财物错误的，应当依法予以返还、赔偿。  
公职人员因有[本法第五十七条](#art-57)、第五十八条规定的情形被撤销政务处分或者减轻政务处分的，应当对其薪酬待遇受到的损失予以补偿。

**Article 60** If a decision on an administrative sanction imposed on a public official is modified, and it is necessary to adjust the official's post, rank, grade, level, position, staff grade, or compensation, such adjustments shall be made in accordance with the provisions. If the decision on the administrative sanction is revoked, the official's grade and compensation shall be restored, and the official shall be assigned a corresponding post, rank, grade, level, position, and staff grade according to the original post, rank, grade, level, position, and staff grade, and the official's reputation shall be restored within the scope where the original decision was announced. If the confiscation or recovery of property was erroneous, the property shall be returned or compensation shall be provided according to law.  
If the administrative sanction imposed on a public official is revoked or mitigated due to the circumstances provided for in Article 57 or Article 58 of this Law, the official shall be compensated for the losses incurred in his or her compensation.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有关机关、单位无正当理由拒不采纳监察建议的，由其上级机关、主管部门责令改正，对该机关、单位给予通报批评，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-46" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第46条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 61** Where a relevant authority or entity refuses to adopt a supervisory suggestion without justifiable reasons, the authority at the next higher level or the competent department shall order it to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism on the authority or entity, and impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有关机关、单位、组织或者人员有下列情形之一的，由其上级机关，主管部门，任免机关、单位或者监察机关责令改正，依法给予处理：  
（一）拒不执行政务处分决定的；  
（二）拒不配合或者阻碍调查的；  
（三）对检举人、证人或者调查人员进行打击报复的；  
（四）诬告陷害公职人员的；  
（五）其他违反本法规定的情形。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/工商市场/国有企业管理人员处分条例.md#art-47" title="被《国有企业管理人员处分条例》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 62** Where a relevant authority, entity, organization, or person falls under any of the following circumstances, the authority at the next higher level, the competent department, the appointing authority or entity, or the supervisory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose sanctions according to law:  
(1) refusing to enforce a government disciplinary decision;  
(2) refusing to cooperate with or obstructing an investigation;  
(3) retaliating against a whistleblower, witness, or investigator;  
(4) making false accusations against public officials;  
(5) other circumstances violating the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　监察机关及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处理：  
（一）违反规定处置问题线索的；  
（二）窃取、泄露调查工作信息，或者泄露检举事项、检举受理情况以及检举人信息的；  
（三）对被调查人或者涉案人员逼供、诱供，或者侮辱、打骂、虐待、体罚或者变相体罚的；  
（四）收受被调查人或者涉案人员的财物以及其他利益的；  
（五）违反规定处置涉案财物的；  
（六）违反规定采取调查措施的；  
（七）利用职权或者职务上的影响干预调查工作、以案谋私的；  
（八）违反规定发生办案安全事故，或者发生安全事故后隐瞒不报、报告失实、处置不当的；  
（九）违反回避等程序规定，造成不良影响的；  
（十）不依法受理和处理公职人员复审、复核的；  
（十一）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 63** Where a supervisory authority or its staff member falls under any of the following circumstances, sanctions shall be imposed on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law:  
(1) disposing of clues of problems in violation of regulations;  
(2) stealing or disclosing investigation information, or disclosing matters of whistleblowing, the acceptance of whistleblowing, or the identity of whistleblowers;  
(3) coercing a person under investigation or a person involved in a case to confess through the use of force, inducement, or other means, or insulting, beating, abusing, subjecting to corporal punishment, or subjecting to corporal punishment in a disguised form;  
(4) accepting property or other benefits from a person under investigation or a person involved in a case;  
(5) disposing of property involved in a case in violation of regulations;  
(6) taking investigation measures in violation of regulations;  
(7) using one's authority or the influence of one's position to interfere with investigation work or seeking personal gain through case handling;  
(8) causing a safety accident in case handling in violation of regulations, or failing to report, reporting falsely, or handling improperly after a safety accident occurs;  
(9) violating procedures such as withdrawal rules, causing adverse effects;  
(10) failing to accept and handle applications for review or reconsideration from public officials according to law;  
(11) other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国务院及其相关主管部门根据本法的原则和精神，结合事业单位、国有企业等的实际情况，对事业单位、国有企业等的违法的公职人员处分事宜作出具体规定。

**Article 65** The State Council and the relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with the principles and spirit of this Law and in light of the actual conditions of public institutions, state-owned enterprises, etc., formulate specific provisions on the sanction of illegal public officials in public institutions, state-owned enterprises, etc.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　中央军事委员会可以根据本法制定相关具体规定。

**Article 66** The Central Military Commission may, in accordance with this Law, formulate relevant specific provisions.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本法施行前，已结案的案件如果需要复审、复核，适用当时的规定。尚未结案的案件，如果行为发生时的规定不认为是违法的，适用当时的规定；如果行为发生时的规定认为是违法的，依照当时的规定处理，但是如果本法不认为是违法或者根据本法处理较轻的，适用本法。

**Article 67** For cases that have been concluded before this Law comes into force, if review or reconsideration is required, the provisions in force at that time shall apply. For cases that have not been concluded, if the provisions in force at the time of the act do not deem it illegal, the provisions in force at that time shall apply; if the provisions in force at the time of the act deem it illegal, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions in force at that time, except that if this Law does not deem it illegal or if the sanction under this Law is lighter, this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法自2020年7月1日起施行。

**Article 68** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2020.
