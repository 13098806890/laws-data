# 中华人民共和国兵役法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-08-20  
**生效日期**：2021-10-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和加强国家兵役工作，保证公民依法服兵役，保障军队兵员补充和储备，建设巩固国防和强大军队，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of regulating and strengthening the national military service work, ensuring citizens perform military service according to law, guaranteeing the supplement and reserve of military personnel, and building a solid national defense and a strong military.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　保卫祖国、抵抗侵略是中华人民共和国每一个公民的神圣职责。

**Article 2** It is the sacred duty of every citizen of the People's Republic of China to defend the motherland and resist aggression.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国实行以志愿兵役为主体的志愿兵役与义务兵役相结合的兵役制度。

**Article 3** The People's Republic of China practices a military service system combining voluntary military service as the main body with compulsory military service.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　兵役工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，坚持与国家经济社会发展相协调，坚持与国防和军队建设相适应，遵循服从国防需要、聚焦备战打仗、彰显服役光荣、体现权利和义务一致的原则。

**Article 4** Military service work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, implement the military strategic guidelines for the new era, persist in coordinating with national economic and social development, persist in adapting to national defense and military construction, and follow the principles of subordinating to the needs of national defense, focusing on combat readiness and warfare, highlighting the glory of service, and reflecting the unity of rights and obligations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国公民，不分民族、种族、职业、家庭出身、宗教信仰和教育程度，都有义务依照本法的规定服兵役。  
有严重生理缺陷或者严重残疾不适合服兵役的公民，免服兵役。  
依照法律被剥夺政治权利的公民，不得服兵役。

**Article 5** All citizens of the People's Republic of China, regardless of nationality, race, occupation, family background, religious belief, or education level, have the obligation to perform military service in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
Citizens who have serious physiological defects or severe disabilities and are unfit for military service shall be exempted from military service.  
Citizens who are deprived of political rights according to law shall not perform military service.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　兵役分为现役和预备役。在中国人民解放军服现役的称军人；预编到现役部队或者编入预备役部队服预备役的，称预备役人员。

**Article 6** Military service is divided into active service and reserve service. Those who serve on active duty in the Chinese People's Liberation Army are called servicemen; those who are pre-assigned to active units or incorporated into reserve units for reserve service are called reservists.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军人和预备役人员，必须遵守宪法和法律，履行公民的义务，同时享有公民的权利；由于服兵役而产生的权利和义务，由本法和其他相关法律法规规定。

**Article 7** Servicemen and reservists must abide by the Constitution and laws, fulfill the obligations of citizens, and at the same time enjoy the rights of citizens. The rights and obligations arising from military service shall be prescribed by this Law and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军人必须遵守军队的条令和条例，忠于职守，随时为保卫祖国而战斗。  
预备役人员必须按照规定参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务，随时准备应召参战，保卫祖国。  
军人和预备役人员入役时应当依法进行服役宣誓。

**Article 8** Servicemen must abide by the regulations and rules of the military, be loyal to their duties, and be ready to fight at any time for the defense of the motherland.  
Reservists must participate in military training, undertake combat readiness service, and perform non-war military operations tasks as prescribed, and be ready to be called to join the fight to defend the motherland.  
Servicemen and reservists shall take an oath of service according to law upon entering service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国的兵役工作，在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，由国防部负责。  
省军区（卫戍区、警备区）、军分区（警备区）和县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民武装部，兼各该级人民政府的兵役机关，在上级军事机关和同级人民政府领导下，负责办理本行政区域的兵役工作。  
机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府，依照本法的规定完成兵役工作任务。兵役工作业务，在设有人民武装部的单位，由人民武装部办理；不设人民武装部的单位，确定一个部门办理。普通高等学校应当有负责兵役工作的机构。

**Article 9** The national military service work shall be the responsibility of the Ministry of National Defense under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The provincial military commands (garrison commands, garrison districts), military sub-districts (garrison districts), and the people's armed forces departments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts, which also serve as the military service organs of the people's governments at the corresponding levels, shall be responsible for handling the military service work in their respective administrative regions under the leadership of higher military organs and the people's governments at the same level.  
State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall fulfill the tasks of military service work in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The business of military service work, in units where there is a people's armed forces department, shall be handled by that department; in units where there is no people's armed forces department, a department shall be designated for handling. Regular institutions of higher learning shall have an institution responsible for military service work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关应当会同相关部门，加强对本行政区域内兵役工作的组织协调和监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关应当将兵役工作情况作为拥军优属、拥政爱民评比和有关单位及其负责人考核评价的内容。

**Article 10** The military service organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, strengthen the organization, coordination, supervision, and inspection of military service work within their respective administrative regions.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and military organs at the same level shall include the situation of military service work as part of the evaluation of 'supporting the army and giving preferential treatment to families of servicemen and martyrs' and 'supporting the government and cherishing the people', and as content for the assessment and evaluation of relevant units and their responsible persons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强兵役工作信息化建设，采取有效措施实现有关部门之间信息共享，推进兵役信息收集、处理、传输、存储等技术的现代化，为提高兵役工作质量效益提供支持。  
兵役工作有关部门及其工作人员应当对收集的个人信息严格保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。

**Article 11** The State shall strengthen the informatization construction of military service work, take effective measures to realize information sharing among relevant departments, promote the modernization of technologies such as the collection, processing, transmission, and storage of military service information, and provide support for improving the quality and efficiency of military service work.  
Relevant departments of military service work and their staff members shall strictly keep confidential the collected personal information and shall not disclose or illegally provide it to others.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家采取措施，加强兵役宣传教育，增强公民依法服兵役意识，营造服役光荣的良好社会氛围。

**Article 12** The State shall take measures to strengthen the publicity and education of military service, enhance citizens' awareness of performing military service according to law, and create a good social atmosphere where service is glorious.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军人和预备役人员建立功勋的，按照国家和军队关于功勋荣誉表彰的规定予以褒奖。  
组织和个人在兵役工作中作出突出贡献的，按照国家和军队有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 13** Servicemen and reservists who render meritorious service shall be commended in accordance with the provisions of the State and the military on meritorious honors and commendations.  
Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions in military service work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant provisions of the State and the military.

### 第二章　兵役登记

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家实行兵役登记制度。兵役登记包括初次兵役登记和预备役登记。

**Article 14** The State practices a military service registration system. Military service registration includes initial military service registration and reserve service registration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　每年十二月三十一日以前年满十八周岁的男性公民，都应当按照兵役机关的安排在当年进行初次兵役登记。  
机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府，应当根据县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关的安排，负责组织本单位和本行政区域的适龄男性公民进行初次兵役登记。  
初次兵役登记可以采取网络登记的方式进行，也可以到兵役登记站（点）现场登记。进行兵役登记，应当如实填写个人信息。

**Article 15** Male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen by December 31 of each year shall undergo initial military service registration in that year in accordance with the arrangements of the military service organs.  
State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall, in accordance with the arrangements of the military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts, be responsible for organizing the initial military service registration of eligible male citizens in their units and administrative regions.  
Initial military service registration may be conducted through online registration, or by on-site registration at military service registration stations (points). When conducting military service registration, personal information shall be truthfully filled in.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经过初次兵役登记的未服现役的公民，符合预备役条件的，县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关可以根据需要，对其进行预备役登记。

**Article 16** For citizens who have undergone initial military service registration but have not served on active duty and meet the conditions for reserve service, the military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts may register them for reserve service as needed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　退出现役的士兵自退出现役之日起四十日内，退出现役的军官自确定安置地之日起三十日内，到安置地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关进行兵役登记信息变更；其中，符合预备役条件，经部队确定需要办理预备役登记的，还应当办理预备役登记。

**Article 17** Soldiers who retire from active service shall, within forty days from the date of retirement, and officers who retire from active service shall, within thirty days from the date of determination of their resettlement location, go to the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district of their resettlement location to change their military service registration information. Among them, those who meet the conditions for reserve service and are determined by the military unit to need to undergo reserve service registration shall also undergo reserve service registration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关负责本行政区域兵役登记工作。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关每年组织兵役登记信息核验，会同有关部门对公民兵役登记情况进行查验，确保兵役登记及时，信息准确完整。

**Article 18** The military service organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the military service registration work in their respective administrative regions.  
The military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, and municipal districts shall organize annual verification of military service registration information and, together with relevant departments, examine the status of citizens' military service registration to ensure that military service registration is timely and the information is accurate and complete.

### 第三章　平时征集

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　全国每年征集服现役的士兵的人数、次数、时间和要求，由国务院和中央军事委员会的命令规定。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府组织兵役机关和有关部门组成征集工作机构，负责组织实施征集工作。

**Article 19** The number, times, time, and requirements for the annual recruitment of soldiers for active service across the country shall be prescribed by orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the military service organs and relevant departments to form recruitment work institutions, which shall be responsible for organizing and implementing the recruitment work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　年满十八周岁的男性公民，应当被征集服现役；当年未被征集的，在二十二周岁以前仍可以被征集服现役。普通高等学校毕业生的征集年龄可以放宽至二十四周岁，研究生的征集年龄可以放宽至二十六周岁。  
根据军队需要，可以按照前款规定征集女性公民服现役。  
根据军队需要和本人自愿，可以征集年满十七周岁未满十八周岁的公民服现役。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/军事国防/中国人民解放军现役士兵服役条例.md#art-43" title="被《中国人民解放军现役士兵服役条例》第43条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 20** Male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen shall be conscripted for active service; those who are not conscripted in that year may still be conscripted for active service before reaching the age of twenty-two. The conscription age for graduates of regular institutions of higher learning may be relaxed to twenty-four years old, and for graduate students, to twenty-six years old.  
In accordance with the needs of the military, female citizens may be conscripted for active service in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
In accordance with the needs of the military and with the individual's consent, citizens who have reached the age of seventeen but not yet eighteen may be conscripted for active service.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经初次兵役登记并初步审查符合征集条件的公民，称应征公民。  
在征集期间，应征公民应当按照县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区征集工作机构的通知，按时参加体格检查等征集活动。  
应征公民符合服现役条件，并经县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区征集工作机构批准的，被征集服现役。

**Article 21** Citizens who have registered for initial military service and are found, after preliminary examination, to meet the recruitment conditions shall be called citizens to be recruited.  
During the recruitment period, citizens to be recruited shall, in accordance with the notice of the recruitment work organs of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts, timely participate in physical examinations and other recruitment activities.  
Citizens to be recruited who meet the conditions for active service and have been approved by the recruitment work organs of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts shall be recruited for active service.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在征集期间，应征公民被征集服现役，同时被机关、团体、企业事业组织招录或者聘用的，应当优先履行服兵役义务；有关机关、团体、企业事业组织应当服从国防和军队建设的需要，支持兵员征集工作。

**Article 22** During the recruitment period, if a citizen to be recruited is recruited for active service and is concurrently recruited or employed by a State organ, organization, enterprise, or public institution, he or she shall give priority to performing the obligation of military service; the relevant State organs, organizations, enterprises, or public institutions shall comply with the needs of national defense and military building and support the recruitment of military personnel.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　应征公民是维持家庭生活唯一劳动力的，可以缓征。

**Article 23** A citizen to be recruited who is the sole laborer supporting the family may have his or her recruitment deferred.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　应征公民因涉嫌犯罪正在被依法监察调查、侦查、起诉、审判或者被判处徒刑、拘役、管制正在服刑的，不征集。

**Article 24** A citizen to be recruited who is under supervision investigation, investigation, prosecution, or adjudication according to law on suspicion of committing a crime, or who is serving a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or public surveillance shall not be recruited.

### 第四章　士兵的现役和预备役

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　现役士兵包括义务兵役制士兵和志愿兵役制士兵，义务兵役制士兵称义务兵，志愿兵役制士兵称军士。

**Article 25** Active service soldiers include soldiers under the compulsory service system and soldiers under the voluntary service system. Soldiers under the compulsory service system shall be called compulsory servicemen, and soldiers under the voluntary service system shall be called non-commissioned officers.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　义务兵服现役的期限为二年。

**Article 26** The term of active service for compulsory servicemen shall be two years.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　义务兵服现役期满，根据军队需要和本人自愿，经批准可以选改为军士；服现役期间表现特别优秀的，经批准可以提前选改为军士。根据军队需要，可以直接从非军事部门具有专业技能的公民中招收军士。  
军士实行分级服现役制度。军士服现役的期限一般不超过三十年，年龄不超过五十五周岁。  
军士分级服现役的办法和直接从非军事部门招收军士的办法，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 27** Upon expiration of the term of active service, a compulsory serviceman may, according to the needs of the military and his or her own will, be selected and changed to a non-commissioned officer upon approval; during the period of active service, those who have performed exceptionally well may be selected and changed to a non-commissioned officer ahead of schedule upon approval. According to the needs of the military, non-commissioned officers may be directly recruited from citizens with professional skills in non-military departments.  
Non-commissioned officers shall implement a system of graded active service. The term of active service for non-commissioned officers shall generally not exceed thirty years, and the age shall not exceed fifty-five years old.  
The measures for graded active service of non-commissioned officers and the measures for directly recruiting non-commissioned officers from non-military departments shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　士兵服现役期满，应当退出现役。  
士兵因国家建设或者军队编制调整需要退出现役的，经军队医院诊断证明本人健康状况不适合继续服现役的，或者因其他特殊原因需要退出现役的，经批准可以提前退出现役。

**Article 28** A soldier whose term of active service has expired shall be discharged from active service.  
A soldier who needs to be discharged from active service due to national construction or adjustment of military organization, who is certified by a military hospital as being unfit for continued active service due to his or her health condition, or who needs to be discharged from active service for other special reasons, may be discharged from active service ahead of schedule upon approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　士兵服现役的时间自征集工作机构批准入伍之日起算。  
士兵退出现役的时间为部队下达退出现役命令之日。

**Article 29** The period of active service for a soldier shall be calculated from the date of approval of enlistment by the recruitment work organ.  
The date of discharge from active service for a soldier shall be the date on which the military unit issues the order for discharge from active service.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　依照[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定经过预备役登记的退出现役的士兵，由部队会同兵役机关根据军队需要，遴选确定服士兵预备役；经过考核，适合担任预备役军官职务的，服军官预备役。

**Article 30** Discharged soldiers who have registered as reservists in accordance with Article 17 of this Law shall be selected and designated by the military unit together with the military service organs according to the needs of the military to perform service as privates in the reserve service; those who, after assessment, are suitable for serving as reserve officers shall perform service as reserve officers.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　依照[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定经过预备役登记的公民，符合士兵预备役条件的，由部队会同兵役机关根据军队需要，遴选确定服士兵预备役。

**Article 31** Citizens who have registered as reservists in accordance with Article 16 of this Law and meet the conditions for privates in the reserve service shall be selected and designated by the military unit together with the military service organs according to the needs of the military to perform service as privates in the reserve service.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　预备役士兵服预备役的最高年龄，依照其他有关法律规定执行。  
预备役士兵达到服预备役最高年龄的，退出预备役。

**Article 32** The maximum age for privates in the reserve service to perform reserve service shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of other relevant laws.  
If a private in the reserve service reaches the maximum age for reserve service, he or she shall be discharged from the reserve service.

### 第五章　军官的现役和预备役

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　现役军官从下列人员中选拔、招收：  
（一）军队院校毕业学员；  
（二）普通高等学校应届毕业生；  
（三）表现优秀的现役士兵；  
（四）军队需要的专业技术人员和其他人员。  
战时根据需要，可以从现役士兵、军队院校学员、征召的预备役军官和其他人员中直接任命军官。

**Article 33** Active service officers shall be selected and recruited from the following persons:  
(1) Graduates of military academies;  
(2) Fresh graduates of regular institutions of higher learning;  
(3) Active service soldiers with outstanding performance;  
(4) Professional technical personnel and other persons needed by the military.  
In wartime, according to needs, officers may be directly appointed from among active service soldiers, students of military academies, called-up reserve officers, and other persons.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　预备役军官包括下列人员：  
（一）确定服军官预备役的退出现役的军官；  
（二）确定服军官预备役的退出现役的士兵；  
（三）确定服军官预备役的专业技术人员和其他人员。

**Article 34** Reserve officers shall include the following persons:  
(1) Discharged officers who are designated to perform service as reserve officers;  
(2) Discharged soldiers who are designated to perform service as reserve officers;  
(3) Professional technical personnel and other persons who are designated to perform service as reserve officers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　军官服现役和服预备役的最高年龄，依照其他有关法律规定执行。

**Article 35** The maximum age for officers to perform active service and reserve service shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of other relevant laws.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　现役军官按照规定服现役已满最高年龄或者衔级最高年限的，退出现役；需要延长服现役或者暂缓退出现役的，依照有关法律规定执行。  
现役军官按照规定服现役未满最高年龄或者衔级最高年限，因特殊情况需要退出现役的，经批准可以退出现役。

**Article 36** If an active service officer has, in accordance with regulations, served active service for the full maximum age or the maximum rank tenure, he or she shall be discharged from active service; if extension of active service or postponement of discharge from active service is needed, it shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.  
If an active service officer has not yet reached the maximum age or maximum rank tenure in accordance with regulations and needs to be discharged from active service due to special circumstances, he or she may be discharged from active service upon approval.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　依照[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定经过预备役登记的退出现役的军官、依照[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定经过预备役登记的公民，符合军官预备役条件的，由部队会同兵役机关根据军队需要，遴选确定服军官预备役。  
预备役军官按照规定服预备役已满最高年龄的，退出预备役。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/军事国防/民兵工作条例.md#art-9" title="被《民兵工作条例》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/军事国防/民兵工作条例.md#art-10" title="被《民兵工作条例》第10条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 37** Discharged officers who have registered as reservists in accordance with Article 17 of this Law, and citizens who have registered as reservists in accordance with Article 16 of this Law, who meet the conditions for reserve officers, shall be selected and designated by the military unit together with the military service organs according to the needs of the military to perform service as reserve officers.  
If a reserve officer has, in accordance with regulations, served reserve service for the full maximum age, he or she shall be discharged from the reserve service.

### 第六章　军队院校从青年学生中招收的学员

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　根据军队建设的需要，军队院校可以从青年学生中招收学员。招收学员的年龄，不受征集服现役年龄的限制。

**Article 38** According to the needs of military building, military academies may recruit students from among young students. The age of recruited students shall not be subject to the age limit for recruitment into active service.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　学员完成学业达到军队培养目标的，由院校发给毕业证书；按照规定任命为现役军官或者军士。

**Article 39** If a student completes the studies and meets the military training objectives, the academy shall issue a graduation certificate to him or her; and he or she shall be appointed as an active service officer or non-commissioned officer in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　学员未达到军队培养目标或者不符合军队培养要求的，由院校按照国家和军队有关规定发给相应证书，并采取多种方式分流；其中，回入学前户口所在地的学员，就读期间其父母已办理户口迁移手续的，可以回父母现户口所在地，由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府按照国家有关规定接收安置。

**Article 40** If a student fails to meet the military training objectives or does not meet the military training requirements, the academy shall issue a corresponding certificate to him or her in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military, and adopt multiple methods for diversion; among them, for students who return to their place of household registration before enrollment, if their parents have gone through the procedures for household registration transfer during the period of study, they may return to the current place of household registration of their parents, and shall be received and resettled by the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　学员被开除学籍的，回入学前户口所在地；就读期间其父母已办理户口迁移手续的，可以回父母现户口所在地，由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 41** Where a student is expelled from school, he shall return to the place of his registered permanent residence before enrollment; if his parents have completed the procedures for the transfer of registered permanent residence during the period of his study, he may return to the current place of registered permanent residence of his parents, and the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　军队院校从现役士兵中招收的学员，适用[本法第三十九条](#art-39)、第四十条、第四十一条的规定。

**Article 42** The provisions of Articles 39, 40 and 41 of this Law shall apply to students recruited by military academies from active-duty soldiers.

### 第七章　战时兵员动员

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　为了应对国家主权、统一、领土完整、安全和发展利益遭受的威胁，抵抗侵略，各级人民政府、各级军事机关，在平时必须做好战时兵员动员的准备工作。

**Article 43** In order to respond to threats to the national sovereignty, unification, territorial integrity, security and development interests, and to resist aggression, the people's governments and military organs at all levels must, in peacetime, make good preparations for wartime manpower mobilization.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在国家发布动员令或者国务院、中央军事委员会依照《中华人民共和国国防动员法》采取必要的国防动员措施后，各级人民政府、各级军事机关必须依法迅速实施动员，军人停止退出现役，休假、探亲的军人立即归队，预备役人员随时准备应召服现役，经过预备役登记的公民做好服预备役被征召的准备。

**Article 44** After the State issues a mobilization order or the State Council and the Central Military Commission take necessary defense mobilization measures in accordance with the National Defense Mobilization Law of the People's Republic of China, the people's governments and military organs at all levels must rapidly implement mobilization according to law; soldiers shall cease to be discharged from active service; soldiers on leave or visiting family shall immediately return to their units; reservists shall be ready at all times to respond to call-ups for active service; and citizens who have undergone reserve service registration shall prepare to be called up for reserve service.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　战时根据需要，国务院和中央军事委员会可以决定适当放宽征召男性公民服现役的年龄上限，可以决定延长公民服现役的期限。

**Article 45** In wartime, as needed, the State Council and the Central Military Commission may decide to appropriately relax the upper age limit for the call-up of male citizens for active service, and may decide to extend the period of active service for citizens.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　战争结束后，需要复员的军人，根据国务院和中央军事委员会的复员命令，分期分批地退出现役，由各级人民政府妥善安置。

**Article 46** After the end of war, soldiers who need to be demobilized shall be discharged from active service in batches according to the demobilization order of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and shall be properly resettled by the people's governments at all levels.

### 第八章　服役待遇和抚恤优待

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家保障军人享有符合军事职业特点、与其履行职责相适应的工资、津贴、住房、医疗、保险、休假、疗养等待遇。军人的待遇应当与国民经济发展相协调，与社会进步相适应。  
女军人的合法权益受法律保护。军队应当根据女军人的特点，合理安排女军人的工作任务和休息休假，在生育、健康等方面为女军人提供特别保护。

**Article 47** The State guarantees that soldiers enjoy such benefits as wages, allowances, housing, medical care, insurance, leave and recuperation that conform to the characteristics of the military profession and are commensurate with the performance of their duties. The treatment of soldiers shall be coordinated with national economic development and adapted to social progress.  
The lawful rights and interests of female soldiers are protected by law. The armed forces shall, in light of the characteristics of female soldiers, reasonably arrange their work tasks and rest and leave, and provide special protection for female soldiers in terms of childbirth, health and other aspects.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　预备役人员参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务，享受国家规定的伙食、交通等补助。预备役人员是机关、团体、企业事业组织工作人员的，参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，所在单位应当保持其原有的工资、奖金和福利待遇。预备役人员的其他待遇保障依照有关法律法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 48** Reservists who participate in combat, military training, combat readiness duties, or non-war military operations shall enjoy subsidies for meals, transportation, etc. as prescribed by the State. If a reservist is a staff member of a state organ, organization, enterprise or public institution, the unit he belongs to shall maintain his original wages, bonuses and welfare benefits during his participation in combat, military training, combat readiness duties or non-war military operations. Other benefits and guarantees for reservists shall be implemented in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　军人按照国家有关规定，在医疗、金融、交通、参观游览、法律服务、文化体育设施服务、邮政服务等方面享受优待政策。公民入伍时保留户籍。  
军人因战、因公、因病致残的，按照国家规定评定残疾等级，发给残疾军人证，享受国家规定的待遇、优待和残疾抚恤金。因工作需要继续服现役的残疾军人，由所在部队按照规定发给残疾抚恤金。  
军人牺牲、病故，国家按照规定发给其遗属抚恤金。

**Article 49** Soldiers shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, enjoy preferential policies in such aspects as medical care, finance, transportation, visits and tours, legal services, cultural and sports facilities services, and postal services. Citizens shall retain their registered permanent residence when enlisted.  
If a soldier becomes disabled due to war, duty or illness, he shall have his disability grade assessed in accordance with State provisions, receive a certificate for disabled soldiers, and enjoy the treatment, preferential treatment and disability pension prescribed by the State. For disabled soldiers who continue to serve in active service due to work needs, the unit they belong to shall pay the disability pension in accordance with the provisions.  
If a soldier is killed in action or dies of illness, the State shall pay a pension to his survivors in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家建立义务兵家庭优待金制度。义务兵家庭优待金标准由地方人民政府制定，中央财政给予定额补助。具体补助办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门、财政部门会同中央军事委员会机关有关部门制定。  
义务兵和军士入伍前是机关、团体、事业单位或者国有企业工作人员的，退出现役后可以选择复职复工。  
义务兵和军士入伍前依法取得的农村土地承包经营权，服现役期间应当保留。

**Article 50** The State shall establish a system of preferential allowances for the families of compulsory servicemen. The standard of preferential allowances for families of compulsory servicemen shall be determined by the local people's governments, and the central finance shall provide a fixed subsidy. The specific subsidy measures shall be formulated by the competent department of veterans' affairs of the State Council, the finance department, together with the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission organs.  
If a compulsory serviceman or non-commissioned officer was a staff member of a state organ, organization, public institution or State-owned enterprise before enlistment, he may choose to return to his original post or work after being discharged from active service.  
The contractual management right of rural land lawfully obtained by a compulsory serviceman or non-commissioned officer before enlistment shall be retained during the period of active service.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　现役军官和军士的子女教育，家属的随军、就业创业以及工作调动，享受国家和社会的优待。  
符合条件的军人家属，其住房、医疗、养老按照有关规定享受优待。  
军人配偶随军未就业期间，按照国家有关规定享受相应的保障待遇。

**Article 51** The education of children of active-duty officers and non-commissioned officers, and the accompanying of military family members, employment and entrepreneurship, and job transfers shall enjoy preferential treatment from the State and society.  
Military family members who meet the conditions shall enjoy preferential treatment in housing, medical care and old-age care in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
During the period when a military spouse accompanies the military but is not employed, he or she shall enjoy corresponding security benefits in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　预备役人员因参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务致残、牺牲的，由当地人民政府依照有关规定给予抚恤优待。

**Article 52** If a reservist becomes disabled or is killed in action due to participation in combat, military training, combat readiness duties, or non-war military operations, the local people's government shall provide pension and preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第九章　退役军人的安置

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对退出现役的义务兵，国家采取自主就业、安排工作、供养等方式妥善安置。  
义务兵退出现役自主就业的，按照国家规定发给一次性退役金，由安置地的县级以上地方人民政府接收，根据当地的实际情况，可以发给经济补助。国家根据经济社会发展，适时调整退役金的标准。  
服现役期间平时获得二等功以上荣誉或者战时获得三等功以上荣誉以及属于烈士子女的义务兵退出现役，由安置地的县级以上地方人民政府安排工作；待安排工作期间由当地人民政府按照国家有关规定发给生活补助费；根据本人自愿，也可以选择自主就业。  
因战、因公、因病致残的义务兵退出现役，按照国家规定的评定残疾等级采取安排工作、供养等方式予以妥善安置；符合安排工作条件的，根据本人自愿，也可以选择自主就业。

**Article 53** For compulsory servicemen discharged from active service, the State shall adopt methods such as self-employment, job placement, and support to ensure proper resettlement.  
If a compulsory serviceman discharged from active service chooses self-employment, he shall be given a one-time retirement payment in accordance with State provisions, and shall be received by the local people's government at or above the county level of the place of resettlement, which may, based on local actual conditions, provide an economic subsidy. The State shall adjust the standard of retirement payments in a timely manner in light of economic and social development.  
For a compulsory serviceman who, during his active service, has won an honor of second-class merit or above in peacetime, or third-class merit or above in wartime, or who is a child of a martyr, upon discharge from active service, he shall be assigned a job by the local people's government at or above the county level of the place of resettlement; during the period awaiting job assignment, the local people's government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, pay a living allowance; and, at his own discretion, he may also choose self-employment.  
For a compulsory serviceman who becomes disabled due to war, duty or illness and is discharged from active service, he shall be properly resettled by means of job placement, support, etc., in accordance with the disability grade assessed under State provisions; if he meets the conditions for job placement, he may also choose self-employment at his own discretion.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对退出现役的军士，国家采取逐月领取退役金、自主就业、安排工作、退休、供养等方式妥善安置。  
军士退出现役，服现役满规定年限的，采取逐月领取退役金方式予以妥善安置。  
军士退出现役，服现役满十二年或者符合国家规定的其他条件的，由安置地的县级以上地方人民政府安排工作；待安排工作期间由当地人民政府按照国家有关规定发给生活补助费；根据本人自愿，也可以选择自主就业。  
军士服现役满三十年或者年满五十五周岁或者符合国家规定的其他条件的，作退休安置。  
因战、因公、因病致残的军士退出现役，按照国家规定的评定残疾等级采取安排工作、退休、供养等方式予以妥善安置；符合安排工作条件的，根据本人自愿，也可以选择自主就业。  
军士退出现役，不符合本条第二款至第五款规定条件的，依照[本法第五十三条](#art-53)规定的自主就业方式予以妥善安置。

**Article 54** For non-commissioned officers discharged from active service, the State shall adopt methods such as monthly pension payments, self-employment, job placement, retirement, and support to ensure proper resettlement.  
A non-commissioned officer discharged from active service who has served for the prescribed number of years shall be properly resettled by means of monthly pension payments.  
A non-commissioned officer discharged from active service who has served for not less than twelve years or meets other conditions prescribed by the State shall be assigned a job by the local people's government at or above the county level of the place of resettlement; during the period awaiting job assignment, the local people's government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, pay a living allowance; and, at his own discretion, he may also choose self-employment.  
A non-commissioned officer who has served for thirty years or has reached the age of fifty-five or meets other conditions prescribed by the State shall be resettled by retirement.  
For a non-commissioned officer who becomes disabled due to war, duty or illness and is discharged from active service, he shall be properly resettled by means of job placement, retirement, support, etc., in accordance with the disability grade assessed under State provisions; if he meets the conditions for job placement, he may also choose self-employment at his own discretion.  
If a non-commissioned officer discharged from active service does not meet the conditions provided for in paragraphs 2 through 5 of this Article, he shall be properly resettled by the method of self-employment provided for in Article 53 of this Law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对退出现役的军官，国家采取退休、转业、逐月领取退役金、复员等方式妥善安置；其安置方式的适用条件，依照有关法律法规的规定执行。

**Article 55** For officers discharged from active service, the State shall adopt methods such as retirement, transfer to civilian work, monthly pension payments, and demobilization to ensure proper resettlement; the applicable conditions for the resettlement methods shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　残疾军人、患慢性病的军人退出现役后，由安置地的县级以上地方人民政府按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定负责接收安置；其中，患过慢性病旧病复发需要治疗的，由当地医疗机构负责给予治疗，所需医疗和生活费用，本人经济困难的，按照国家规定给予补助。

**Article 56** After disabled soldiers or soldiers suffering from chronic diseases are discharged from active service, the local people's government at or above the county level of the place of resettlement shall be responsible for receiving and resettling them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission; among them, if a person who has suffered from a chronic disease has a relapse and needs treatment, the local medical institution shall be responsible for providing treatment, and if the person is in financial difficulty, the necessary medical and living expenses shall be subsidized in accordance with State provisions.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　有服兵役义务的公民有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由县级人民政府强制其履行兵役义务，并处以罚款：  
（一）拒绝、逃避兵役登记的；  
（二）应征公民拒绝、逃避征集服现役的；  
（三）预备役人员拒绝、逃避参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务和征召的。  
有前款第二项行为，拒不改正的，不得录用为公务员或者参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的工作人员，不得招录、聘用为国有企业和事业单位工作人员，两年内不准出境或者升学复学，纳入履行国防义务严重失信主体名单实施联合惩戒。

**Article 57** If a citizen who has the obligation to perform military service commits any of the following acts, the people's government at the county level shall order him to make corrections within a specified time limit; if he fails to make corrections within the time limit, the people's government at the county level shall compel him to perform his military service obligation and impose a fine:  
(1) refusing or evading registration for military service;  
(2) a citizen liable for enlistment refusing or evading enlistment for active service;  
(3) a reservist refusing or evading participation in military training, combat readiness duties, non-war military operations, or call-up.  
If a person commits the act specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph and refuses to make corrections, he shall not be employed as a civil servant or staff member managed with reference to the Civil Service Law of the People's Republic of China, shall not be recruited or employed as staff of State-owned enterprises or public institutions, shall not be allowed to leave the country or to enter a higher school or resume schooling within two years, and shall be included in the list of seriously dishonest subjects for failing to perform national defense obligations for joint disciplinary action.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　军人以逃避服兵役为目的，拒绝履行职责或者逃离部队的，按照中央军事委员会的规定给予处分。  
军人有前款行为被军队除名、开除军籍或者被依法追究刑事责任的，依照[本法第五十七条](#art-57)第二款的规定处罚；其中，被军队除名的，并处以罚款。  
明知是逃离部队的军人而招录、聘用的，由县级人民政府责令改正，并处以罚款。

**Article 58** If a soldier, for the purpose of evading military service, refuses to perform his duties or deserts the armed forces, he shall be given a sanction in accordance with the provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
If a soldier who commits the act in the preceding paragraph is removed from the army, expelled from military status, or subject to criminal liability according to law, he shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 57 of this Law; if he is removed from the army, a fine shall also be imposed.  
If an entity knowingly recruits or employs a soldier who has deserted the armed forces, the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　机关、团体、企业事业组织拒绝完成本法规定的兵役工作任务的，阻挠公民履行兵役义务的，或者有其他妨害兵役工作行为的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令改正，并可以处以罚款；对单位负有责任的领导人员、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法予以处罚。

**Article 59** If a state organ, organization, enterprise or public institution refuses to complete the military service work tasks prescribed in this Law, obstructs a citizen from performing his military service obligation, or commits any other act that hinders military service work, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine; the leading persons responsible, the directly responsible supervisors, and other directly responsible persons of the unit shall be punished according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　扰乱兵役工作秩序，或者阻碍兵役工作人员依法执行职务的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。

**Article 60** Any person who disrupts the order of military service work or obstructs a military service worker from performing his duties according to law shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家工作人员和军人在兵役工作中，有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
（一）贪污贿赂的；  
（二）滥用职权或者玩忽职守的；  
（三）徇私舞弊，接送不合格兵员的；  
（四）泄露或者向他人非法提供兵役个人信息的。

**Article 61** State functionaries and military personnel who, in the work of military service, commit any of the following acts shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law:  
(1) engaging in embezzlement or bribery;  
(2) abusing power or neglecting duties;  
(3) engaging in malpractices for personal gain by accepting or delivering unqualified recruits;  
(4) disclosing or illegally providing personal information of military service to others.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　[本法第五十七条](#art-57)、第五十八条、第五十九条规定的处罚，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关会同有关部门查明事实，经同级地方人民政府作出处罚决定后，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关、发展改革、公安、退役军人工作、卫生健康、教育、人力资源和社会保障等部门按照职责分工具体执行。

**Article 63** The penalties provided for in Articles 57, 58, and 59 of this Law shall, after the military service organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level ascertain the facts jointly with relevant departments, and after the local people's government at the same level makes a decision on punishment, be specifically executed by the military service organs, development and reform, public security, veterans affairs, health, education, human resources and social security departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本法适用于中国人民武装警察部队。

**Article 64** This Law shall apply to the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2021年10月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force on October 1, 2021.
