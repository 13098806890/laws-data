# 中华人民共和国人民武装警察法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2020-06-20  
**生效日期**：2020-06-21

> （2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过　2020年6月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　组织和指挥 第三章　任务和权限 第四章　义务和纪律 第五章　保障措施 第六章　监督检查 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范和保障人民武装警察部队履行职责，建设强大的现代化人民武装警察部队，维护国家安全和社会稳定，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，制定本法。 第二条　人民武装警察部队是中华人民共和国武装力量的重要组成部分，由党中央、中央军事委员会集中统一领导。 第三条　人民武装警察部队坚持中国共产党的绝对领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，按照多能一体、维稳维权的战略要求，加强练兵备战、坚持依法从严、加快建设发展，有效履行职责。 第四条　人民武装警察部队担负执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、海上维权执法、抢险救援和防卫作战以及中央军事委员会赋予的其他任务。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和保障人民武装警察部队履行职责，建设强大的现代化人民武装警察部队，维护国家安全和社会稳定，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to regulate and guarantee the performance of duties by the People's Armed Police Force, to build a strong modern People's Armed Police Force, to safeguard national security and social stability, and to protect the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民武装警察部队是中华人民共和国武装力量的重要组成部分，由党中央、中央军事委员会集中统一领导。

**Article 2** The People's Armed Police Force is an important component of the armed forces of the People's Republic of China and shall be under the centralized and unified leadership of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民武装警察部队坚持中国共产党的绝对领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，按照多能一体、维稳维权的战略要求，加强练兵备战、坚持依法从严、加快建设发展，有效履行职责。

**Article 3** The People's Armed Police Force shall adhere to the absolute leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, implement the military strategic guidelines for the new era, and in accordance with the strategic requirements of multi-functional integration and maintaining stability and safeguarding rights, strengthen military training and combat readiness, adhere to strictness according to law, accelerate construction and development, and effectively perform its duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民武装警察部队担负执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、海上维权执法、抢险救援和防卫作战以及中央军事委员会赋予的其他任务。

**Article 4** The People's Armed Police Force shall undertake tasks such as guard duty, handling of sudden public security incidents, preventing and handling terrorist activities, maritime rights protection and law enforcement, emergency rescue and disaster relief, defense operations, and other tasks assigned by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民武装警察部队应当遵守宪法和法律，忠于职守，依照本法和其他法律的有关规定履行职责。  
人民武装警察部队依法履行职责的行为受法律保护。

**Article 5** The People's Armed Police Force shall abide by the Constitution and laws, be faithful to their duties, and perform their duties in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law and other laws.  
The acts of the People's Armed Police Force in performing their duties according to law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对在执行任务中做出突出贡献的人民武装警察，依照有关法律和中央军事委员会的规定给予表彰和奖励。  
对协助人民武装警察执行任务有突出贡献的个人和组织，依照有关法律、法规的规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 6** People's Armed Police personnel who make outstanding contributions in the performance of tasks shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant laws and the provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
Individuals and organizations that make outstanding contributions in assisting the People's Armed Police Force in the performance of tasks shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民武装警察部队实行衔级制度，衔级制度的具体内容由法律另行规定。

**Article 7** The People's Armed Police Force shall implement a rank system. The specific contents of the rank system shall be prescribed separately by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民武装警察享有法律、法规规定的现役军人的权益。

**Article 8** People's Armed Police personnel shall enjoy the rights and interests of active service soldiers as provided by laws and regulations.

### 第二章　组织和指挥

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民武装警察部队由内卫部队、机动部队、海警部队和院校、研究机构等组成。  
内卫部队按照行政区划编设，机动部队按照任务编设，海警部队在沿海地区按照行政区划和任务区域编设。具体编设由中央军事委员会确定。

**Article 9** The People's Armed Police Force shall be composed of internal security forces, mobile forces, coast guard forces, and academies, research institutions, etc.  
Internal security forces shall be organized according to administrative divisions, mobile forces shall be organized according to tasks, and coast guard forces shall be organized in coastal areas according to administrative divisions and task areas. The specific organization shall be determined by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民武装警察部队平时执行任务，由中央军事委员会或者中央军事委员会授权人民武装警察部队组织指挥。  
人民武装警察部队平时与人民解放军共同参加抢险救援、维稳处突、联合训练演习等非战争军事行动，由中央军事委员会授权战区指挥。  
人民武装警察部队战时执行任务，由中央军事委员会或者中央军事委员会授权战区组织指挥。  
组织指挥具体办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 10** When the People's Armed Police Force performs tasks in peacetime, it shall be organized and commanded by the Central Military Commission or by the People's Armed Police Force authorized by the Central Military Commission.  
When the People's Armed Police Force jointly participates with the People's Liberation Army in non-war military operations such as emergency rescue and disaster relief, stability maintenance and emergency response, and joint training exercises in peacetime, it shall be commanded by the theater of operations authorized by the Central Military Commission.  
When the People's Armed Police Force performs tasks in wartime, it shall be organized and commanded by the Central Military Commission or by the theater of operations authorized by the Central Military Commission.  
The specific measures for organization and command shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府应当与人民武装警察部队建立任务需求和工作协调机制。  
中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府因重大活动安全保卫、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、抢险救援等需要人民武装警察部队协助的，应当按照国家有关规定提出需求。  
执勤目标单位可以向负责执勤任务的人民武装警察部队提出需求。

**Article 11** Central state organs and local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish mechanisms for task requirements and work coordination with the People's Armed Police Force.  
Where central state organs and local people's governments at or above the county level need the assistance of the People's Armed Police Force for security protection of major events, handling of sudden public security incidents, preventing and handling terrorist activities, emergency rescue and disaster relief, etc., they shall submit requirements in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
A guard duty target unit may submit requirements to the People's Armed Police Force responsible for the guard duty task.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　调动人民武装警察部队执行任务，坚持依法用兵、严格审批的原则，按照指挥关系、职责权限和运行机制组织实施。批准权限和程序由中央军事委员会规定。  
遇有重大灾情、险情或者暴力恐怖事件等严重威胁公共安全或者公民人身财产安全的紧急情况，人民武装警察部队应当依照中央军事委员会有关规定采取行动并同时报告。

**Article 12** The deployment of the People's Armed Police Force to perform tasks shall adhere to the principles of using troops according to law and strict approval, and shall be organized and implemented in accordance with command relationships, duties and authorities, and operational mechanisms. The authority and procedures for approval shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.  
In the event of emergencies such as major disasters, dangerous situations, or violent terrorist incidents that seriously threaten public security or the personal and property safety of citizens, the People's Armed Police Force shall take action in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission and report simultaneously.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民武装警察部队根据执行任务需要，参加中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府设立的指挥机构，在指挥机构领导下，依照中央军事委员会有关规定实施组织指挥。

**Article 13** The People's Armed Police Force, as required by the performance of tasks, shall participate in command organizations established by central state organs or local people's governments at or above the county level, and under the leadership of such command organizations, shall exercise organization and command in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府对人民武装警察部队执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、抢险救援工作进行业务指导。  
人民武装警察部队执行武装警卫、武装守卫、武装守护、武装警戒、押解、押运等任务，执勤目标单位可以对在本单位担负执勤任务的人民武装警察部队进行执勤业务指导。

**Article 14** Central state organs and local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide professional guidance to the People's Armed Police Force in guard duty, handling of sudden public security incidents, preventing and handling terrorist activities, and emergency rescue and disaster relief.  
When the People's Armed Police Force performs tasks such as armed guard, armed defense, armed protection, armed patrol, escort, and convoy, the guard duty target unit may provide professional guidance on guard duty to the People's Armed Police Force performing guard duty tasks at the unit.

### 第三章　任务和权限

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民武装警察部队主要担负下列执勤任务：  
（一）警卫对象、重要警卫目标的武装警卫；  
（二）重大活动的安全保卫；  
（三）重要的公共设施、核设施、企业、仓库、水源地、水利工程、电力设施、通信枢纽等目标的核心要害部位的武装守卫；  
（四）重要的桥梁和隧道的武装守护；  
（五）监狱、看守所等场所的外围武装警戒；  
（六）直辖市，省、自治区人民政府所在地的市和其他重要城市（镇）的重点区域、特殊时期以及特定内陆边界的武装巡逻；  
（七）协助公安机关、国家安全机关依法执行逮捕、追捕任务，协助监狱、看守所等执勤目标单位执行押解、追捕任务，协助中国人民银行、国防军工单位等执勤目标单位执行押运任务。  
前款规定的执勤任务的具体范围，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 15** The People's Armed Police Force shall mainly undertake the following guard duty tasks:  
(1) Armed guard of guard objects and important guard targets;  
(2) Security protection of major events;  
(3) Armed defense of core and critical parts of targets such as important public facilities, nuclear facilities, enterprises, warehouses, water sources, water conservancy projects, power facilities, and communication hubs;  
(4) Armed protection of important bridges and tunnels;  
(5) Outer armed patrol of places such as prisons and detention houses;  
(6) Armed patrol of key areas, special periods, and specific inland borders of municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities where provincial and autonomous regional people's governments are located, and other important cities (towns);  
(7) Assisting public security organs and state security organs in executing arrest and pursuit tasks according to law, assisting guard duty target units such as prisons and detention houses in executing escort and pursuit tasks, and assisting guard duty target units such as the People's Bank of China and national defense military industrial units in executing convoy tasks.  
The specific scope of the guard duty tasks specified in the preceding paragraph shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民武装警察部队参与处置动乱、暴乱、骚乱、非法聚集事件、群体性事件等突发事件，主要担负下列任务：  
（一）保卫重要目标安全；  
（二）封锁、控制有关场所和道路；  
（三）实施隔离、疏导、带离、驱散行动，制止违法犯罪行为；  
（四）营救和救护受困人员；  
（五）武装巡逻，协助开展群众工作，恢复社会秩序。

**Article 16** When participating in handling emergencies such as riots, rebellions, disturbances, illegal assembly incidents, and mass incidents, the People's Armed Police Force shall mainly undertake the following tasks:  
(1) Protecting the security of important targets;  
(2) Blocking and controlling relevant places and roads;  
(3) Carrying out actions of isolation, diversion, removal, and dispersal, and stopping illegal and criminal acts;  
(4) Rescuing and providing aid to trapped persons;  
(5) Conducting armed patrols, assisting in mass work, and restoring social order.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民武装警察部队参与防范和处置恐怖活动，主要担负下列任务：  
（一）实施恐怖事件现场控制、救援、救护，以及武装巡逻、重点目标警戒；  
（二）协助公安机关逮捕、追捕恐怖活动人员；  
（三）营救人质、排除爆炸物；  
（四）参与处置劫持航空器等交通工具事件。

**Article 17** When participating in preventing and handling terrorist activities, the People's Armed Police Force shall mainly undertake the following tasks:  
(1) Implementing on-site control, rescue, and medical aid for terrorist incidents, as well as armed patrols and key target guard;  
(2) Assisting public security organs in arresting and pursuing terrorist activity personnel;  
(3) Rescuing hostages and disposing of explosives;  
(4) Participating in handling incidents of hijacking aircraft and other means of transport.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民武装警察部队参与自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件的抢险救援，主要担负下列任务：  
（一）参与搜寻、营救、转移或者疏散受困人员；  
（二）参与危险区域、危险场所和警戒区的外围警戒；  
（三）参与排除、控制灾情和险情，防范次生和衍生灾害；  
（四）参与核生化救援、医疗救护、疫情防控、交通设施抢修抢建等专业抢险；  
（五）参与抢救、运送、转移重要物资。

**Article 18** When participating in emergency rescue and disaster relief for natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, and other emergencies, the People's Armed Police Force shall mainly undertake the following tasks:  
(1) Participating in searching, rescuing, transferring, or evacuating trapped persons;  
(2) Participating in outer guard of dangerous areas, dangerous places, and alert zones;  
(3) Participating in eliminating and controlling disasters and dangers, and preventing secondary and derivative disasters;  
(4) Participating in specialized rescues such as nuclear, biological, chemical rescue, medical treatment, epidemic prevention and control, and emergency repair and construction of transportation facilities;  
(5) Participating in rescuing, transporting, and transferring important materials.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民武装警察执行任务时，可以依法采取下列措施：  
（一）对进出警戒区域、通过警戒哨卡的人员、物品、交通工具等按照规定进行检查；对不允许进出、通过的，予以阻止；对强行进出、通过的，采取必要措施予以制止；  
（二）在武装巡逻中，经现场指挥员同意并出示人民武装警察证件，对有违法犯罪嫌疑的人员当场进行盘问并查验其证件，对可疑物品和交通工具进行检查；  
（三）协助执行交通管制或者现场管制；  
（四）对聚众扰乱社会治安秩序、危及公民人身财产安全、危害公共安全或者执勤目标安全的，采取必要措施予以制止、带离、驱散；  
（五）根据执行任务的需要，向相关单位和人员了解有关情况或者在现场以及与执行任务相关的场所实施必要的侦察。

**Article 19** When performing tasks, the People's Armed Police may take the following measures according to law:  
(1) Inspecting as prescribed persons, articles, and means of transport entering or exiting a guard zone or passing a guard checkpoint; preventing those that are not allowed to enter, exit, or pass; taking necessary measures to stop those that forcibly enter, exit, or pass;  
(2) During armed patrols, with the consent of the on-site commander and after presenting their People's Armed Police certificate, questioning persons suspected of illegal or criminal activities on the spot and examining their certificates, and inspecting suspicious articles and means of transport;  
(3) Assisting in implementing traffic control or on-site control;  
(4) In cases of mass disturbances of public order, endangering citizens' personal and property safety, harming public security or the security of guard duty targets, taking necessary measures to stop, remove, and disperse;  
(5) As required by the performance of tasks, inquiring about relevant circumstances from related units and personnel, or carrying out necessary reconnaissance at the scene or at places related to task performance.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民武装警察执行任务时，发现有下列情形的人员，经现场指挥员同意，应当及时予以控制并移交公安机关、国家安全机关或者其他有管辖权的机关处理：  
（一）正在实施犯罪的；  
（二）通缉在案的；  
（三）违法携带危及公共安全物品的；  
（四）正在实施危害执勤目标安全行为的；  
（五）以暴力、威胁等方式阻碍人民武装警察执行任务的。

**Article 20** When performing tasks, if the People's Armed Police find persons in any of the following circumstances, with the consent of the on-site commander, they shall promptly control them and transfer them to the public security organ, state security organ, or other competent authority for handling:  
(1) Those who are committing a crime;  
(2) Those who are wanted;  
(3) Those who illegally carry articles endangering public security;  
(4) Those who are engaging in acts harmful to the security of guard duty targets;  
(5) Those who obstruct the People's Armed Police in performing tasks by violence, threat, or other means.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民武装警察部队协助公安机关、国家安全机关和监狱等执行逮捕、追捕任务，根据所协助机关的决定，协助搜查犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、罪犯的人身和住所以及涉嫌藏匿犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、罪犯或者违法物品的场所、交通工具等。

**Article 21** The People's Armed Police Force, when assisting public security organs, state security organs, and prisons in executing arrest and pursuit tasks, shall, in accordance with the decisions of the assisted organs, assist in searching the person and residence of criminal suspects, defendants, and convicts, as well as places, means of transport, etc., suspected of harboring criminal suspects, defendants, convicts, or illegal items.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民武装警察执行执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动任务使用警械和武器，依照人民警察使用警械和武器的规定以及其他有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 22** When People's Armed Police use police equipment and weapons in performing guard duty, handling sudden public security incidents, and preventing and handling terrorist activities, they shall do so in accordance with the provisions on the use of police equipment and weapons by the People's Police and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民武装警察执行任务，遇有妨碍、干扰的，可以采取必要措施排除阻碍、强制实施。  
人民武装警察执行任务需要采取措施的，应当严格控制在必要限度内，有多种措施可供选择的，应当选择有利于最大程度地保护个人和组织权益的措施。

**Article 23** When performing tasks, if People's Armed Police encounter obstruction or interference, they may take necessary measures to remove obstacles and enforce implementation.  
When People's Armed Police need to take measures in the performance of tasks, they shall strictly control them within the necessary limits; where multiple measures are available, they shall choose the measure that is most conducive to protecting the rights and interests of individuals and organizations to the greatest extent.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民武装警察因执行任务的紧急需要，经出示人民武装警察证件，可以优先乘坐公共交通工具；遇交通阻碍时，优先通行。

**Article 24** People's Armed Police, due to urgent needs of task performance, may, after presenting their People's Armed Police certificate, have priority in taking public transport; when encountering traffic obstruction, they shall have priority of passage.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民武装警察因执行任务的需要，在紧急情况下，经现场指挥员出示人民武装警察证件，可以优先使用或者依法征用个人和组织的设备、设施、场地、建筑物、交通工具以及其他物资、器材，任务完成后应当及时归还或者恢复原状，并按照国家有关规定支付费用；造成损失的，按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 25** People's Armed Police, as required by task performance, may, in emergency situations, after the on-site commander presents their People's Armed Police certificate, have priority use or lawfully requisition equipment, facilities, sites, buildings, means of transport, and other materials and appliances of individuals and organizations. After the task is completed, they shall promptly return or restore to original condition, and pay fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state; if losses are caused, compensation shall be given in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民武装警察部队出境执行防范和处置恐怖活动等任务，依照有关法律、法规和中央军事委员会的规定执行。

**Article 26** When the People's Armed Police Force goes abroad to perform tasks such as preventing and handling terrorist activities, it shall do so in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations and the provisions of the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　义务和纪律

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民武装警察应当服从命令、听从指挥，依法履职尽责，坚决完成任务。

**Article 27** People's Armed Police shall obey orders, follow command, perform duties and fulfill responsibilities according to law, and resolutely complete tasks.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民武装警察遇有公民的人身财产安全受到侵犯或者处于其他危难情形，应当及时救助。

**Article 28** Where People's Armed Police encounter a situation where a citizen's personal or property safety is infringed or the citizen is in other distress, they shall promptly provide rescue.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人民武装警察不得有下列行为：  
（一）违抗上级决定和命令、行动消极或者临阵脱逃；  
（二）违反规定使用警械、武器；  
（三）非法剥夺、限制他人人身自由，非法检查、搜查人身、物品、交通工具、住所、场所；  
（四）体罚、虐待、殴打监管羁押、控制的对象；  
（五）滥用职权、徇私舞弊，擅离职守或者玩忽职守；  
（六）包庇、纵容违法犯罪活动；  
（七）泄露国家秘密、军事秘密；  
（八）其他违法违纪行为。

**Article 29** People's Armed Police shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) Disobeying superior decisions and orders, acting passively, or fleeing before battle;  
(2) Using police equipment or weapons in violation of regulations;  
(3) Unlawfully depriving or restricting others' personal freedom, unlawfully inspecting or searching persons, articles, means of transport, residences, or premises;  
(4) Subjecting persons in custody or control to corporal punishment, abuse, or beating;  
(5) Abusing power, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, leaving posts without authorization, or neglecting duties;  
(6) Harboring or tolerating illegal or criminal activities;  
(7) Divulging state secrets or military secrets;  
(8) Other illegal or disciplinary acts.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民武装警察执行任务，应当按照规定着装，持有人民武装警察证件，按照规定使用摄录器材录像取证、出示证件。

**Article 30** When performing tasks, People's Armed Police shall wear uniforms as prescribed, carry their People's Armed Police certificate, and use recording equipment to videotape evidence and present certificates as prescribed.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民武装警察应当举止文明，礼貌待人，遵守社会公德，尊重公民的宗教信仰和民族风俗习惯。

**Article 31** People's Armed Police shall behave in a civilized manner, be polite to others, abide by social morality, and respect citizens' religious beliefs and ethnic customs.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　为了保障人民武装警察部队执行任务，中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当依据职责及时向人民武装警察部队通报下列情报信息：  
（一）社会安全信息；  
（二）恐怖事件、突发事件的情报信息；  
（三）气象、水文、海洋环境、地理空间、灾害预警等信息；  
（四）其他与执行任务相关的情报信息。  
中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府应当与人民武装警察部队建立情报信息共享机制，可以采取联通安全信息网络和情报信息系统以及数据库等方式，提供与执行任务相关的情报信息及数据资源。  
人民武装警察部队对获取的相关信息，应当严格保密、依法运用。

**Article 32** In order to ensure the performance of tasks by the People's Armed Police Force, central state organs, local people's governments at or above the county level, and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with their duties, promptly notify the People's Armed Police Force of the following intelligence information:  
(1) Social security information;  
(2) Intelligence information on terrorist incidents and emergencies;  
(3) Information on meteorology, hydrology, marine environment, geospatial, disaster warnings, etc.;  
(4) Other intelligence information related to task performance.  
Central state organs and local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish intelligence information sharing mechanisms with the People's Armed Police Force, and may adopt methods such as connecting security information networks and intelligence information systems and databases to provide intelligence information and data resources related to task performance.  
The People's Armed Police Force shall maintain strict confidentiality and use the relevant information obtained according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立与经济社会发展相适应、与人民武装警察部队担负任务和建设发展相协调的经费保障机制。所需经费按照国家有关规定列入预算。

**Article 33** The state shall establish a funding guarantee mechanism that is commensurate with economic and social development and coordinated with the tasks and construction development undertaken by the People's Armed Police Force. The required funds shall be included in the budget in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　执勤目标单位及其上级主管部门应当按照国家有关规定，为担负执勤任务的人民武装警察部队提供执勤设施、生活设施等必要的保障。

**Article 34** Guard duty target units and their superior competent departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, provide necessary guarantees such as guard duty facilities and living facilities for the People's Armed Police Force undertaking guard duty tasks.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在有毒、粉尘、辐射、噪声等严重污染或者高温、低温、缺氧以及其他恶劣环境下的执勤目标单位执行任务的人民武装警察，享有与执勤目标单位工作人员同等的保护条件和福利补助，由执勤目标单位或者其上级主管部门给予保障。

**Article 35** People's Armed Police performing tasks in guard duty target units under severe pollution such as toxic, dust, radiation, noise, or under harsh environments such as high temperature, low temperature, oxygen deficiency, and others, shall enjoy the same protection conditions and welfare subsidies as the staff of the guard duty target unit, which shall be guaranteed by the guard duty target unit or its superior competent department.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民武装警察部队的专用标志、制式服装、警械装备、证件、印章，按照中央军事委员会有关规定监制和配备。

**Article 36** The special signs, uniforms, police equipment, certificates, and seals of the People's Armed Police Force shall be manufactured under supervision and provided in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民武装警察部队应当根据执行任务的需要，加强对所属人民武装警察的教育和训练，提高依法执行任务的能力。

**Article 37** The People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with the needs of task performance, strengthen the education and training of its People's Armed Police personnel, and improve their ability to perform tasks according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　人民武装警察因执行任务牺牲、伤残的，按照国家有关军人抚恤优待的规定给予抚恤优待。

**Article 38** Where People's Armed Police personnel die or become disabled due to task performance, they shall be given pensions and preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant state provisions on military pensions and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民武装警察部队依法执行任务，公民、法人和其他组织应当给予必要的支持和协助。  
公民、法人和其他组织对人民武装警察部队执行任务给予协助的行为受法律保护。  
公民、法人和其他组织因协助人民武装警察部队执行任务牺牲、伤残或者遭受财产损失的，按照国家有关规定给予抚恤优待或者相应补偿。

**Article 39** When the People's Armed Police Force performs tasks according to law, citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall provide necessary support and assistance.  
The acts of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations in providing assistance to the People's Armed Police Force in task performance shall be protected by law.  
Where citizens, legal persons, and other organizations die, become disabled, or suffer property losses due to assisting the People's Armed Police Force in task performance, they shall be given pensions and preferential treatment or corresponding compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民武装警察部队应当对所属单位和人员执行法律、法规和遵守纪律的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 40** The People's Armed Police Force shall supervise and inspect the implementation of laws and regulations and compliance with discipline by its units and personnel.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　人民武装警察受中央军事委员会监察委员会、人民武装警察部队各级监察委员会的监督。  
人民武装警察执行执勤、处置突发社会安全事件、防范和处置恐怖活动、海上维权执法、抢险救援任务，接受人民政府及其有关部门、公民、法人和其他组织的监督。

**Article 41** People's Armed Police personnel shall be subject to supervision by the Supervision Commission of the Central Military Commission and by the people's armed police supervision commissions at all levels.  
When People's Armed Police perform tasks such as guard duty, handling of sudden public security incidents, preventing and handling terrorist activities, maritime rights protection and law enforcement, and emergency rescue and disaster relief, they shall accept supervision by the people's governments and their relevant departments, as well as by citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　中央军事委员会监察委员会、人民武装警察部队各级监察委员会接到公民、法人和其他组织的检举、控告，或者接到县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对人民武装警察违法违纪行为的情况通报后，应当依法及时查处，按照有关规定将处理结果反馈检举人、控告人或者通报县级以上人民政府及其有关部门。

**Article 42** After receiving reports or accusations from citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, or after receiving notifications from people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments regarding illegal or disciplinary acts by People's Armed Police personnel, the Supervision Commission of the Central Military Commission and the people's armed police supervision commissions at all levels shall promptly investigate and handle the matter according to law, and shall feedback the handling results to the informants or accusers, or notify the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　人民武装警察在执行任务中不履行职责，或者有[本法第二十九条](#art-29)所列行为之一的，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定给予处分。

**Article 43** Where People's Armed Police personnel fail to perform their duties in task performance, or commit any of the acts listed in Article 29 of this Law, they shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　妨碍人民武装警察依法执行任务，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚：  
（一）侮辱、威胁、围堵、拦截、袭击正在执行任务的人民武装警察的；  
（二）强行冲闯人民武装警察部队设置的警戒带、警戒区的；  
（三）拒绝或者阻碍人民武装警察执行追捕、检查、搜查、救险、警戒等任务的；  
（四）阻碍执行任务的人民武装警察部队的交通工具和人员通行的；  
（五）其他严重妨碍人民武装警察执行任务的行为。

**Article 44** Where any of the following acts obstruct the People's Armed Police in performing tasks according to law, the public security organ shall impose public security administrative penalties according to law:  
(1) Insulting, threatening, surrounding, intercepting, or attacking People's Armed Police who are performing tasks;  
(2) Forcibly breaking through warning ropes or warning zones set up by the People's Armed Police Force;  
(3) Refusing or obstructing People's Armed Police in performing tasks such as pursuit, inspection, search, rescue, and guard;  
(4) Obstructing the passage of means of transport and personnel of the People's Armed Police Force performing tasks;  
(5) Other acts that seriously obstruct the People's Armed Police in performing tasks.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　非法制造、买卖、持有、使用人民武装警察部队专用标志、警械装备、证件、印章的，由公安机关处十五日以下拘留或者警告，可以并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Whoever illegally manufactures, buys, sells, possesses, or uses the special signs, police equipment, certificates, or seals of the People's Armed Police Force shall be detained for not more than 15 days or given a warning by the public security organ, and may concurrently be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal income.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　人民武装警察部队执行海上维权执法任务，由法律另行规定。

**Article 47** The performance of maritime rights protection and law enforcement tasks by the People's Armed Police Force shall be separately prescribed by law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　人民武装警察部队执行防卫作战任务，依照中央军事委员会的命令执行。

**Article 48** When the People's Armed Police Force performs defense operation tasks, it shall do so in accordance with the orders of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　人民武装警察部队执行戒严任务，依照《中华人民共和国戒严法》的有关规定执行。

**Article 49** When the People's Armed Police Force performs martial law tasks, it shall do so in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Martial Law Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　人民武装警察部队文职人员在执行本法规定的任务时，依法履行人民武装警察的有关职责和义务，享有相应权益。

**Article 50** When civilian personnel of the People's Armed Police Force perform the tasks prescribed in this Law, they shall perform the relevant duties and obligations of the People's Armed Police according to law and enjoy corresponding rights and interests.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本法自2020年6月21日起施行。

**Article 51** This Law shall come into force as of June 21, 2020.
