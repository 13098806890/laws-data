# 中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2013-10-25  
**生效日期**：2014-03-15

> （1993年10月31日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第四次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2013年10月25日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第五次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法〉的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　消费者的权利 第三章　经营者的义务 第四章　国家对消费者合法权益的保护 第五章　消费者组织 第六章　争议的解决 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为保护消费者的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济健康发展，制定本法。 第二条　消费者为生活消费需要购买、使用商品或者接受服务，其权益受本法保护；本法未作规定的，受其他有关法律、法规保护。 第三条　经营者为消费者提供其生产、销售的商品或者提供服务，应当遵守本法；本法未作规定的，应当遵守其他有关法律、法规。 第四条　经营者与消费者进行交易，应当遵循自愿、平等、公平、诚实信用的原则。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保护消费者的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, maintaining the social and economic order, and promoting the healthy development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　消费者为生活消费需要购买、使用商品或者接受服务，其权益受本法保护；本法未作规定的，受其他有关法律、法规保护。

**Article 2** The rights and interests of a consumer who purchases or uses a commodity or accepts a service for the need of daily consumption shall be protected by this Law; where this Law has no provisions, such consumer shall be protected by other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经营者为消费者提供其生产、销售的商品或者提供服务，应当遵守本法；本法未作规定的，应当遵守其他有关法律、法规。

**Article 3** A business operator that provides commodities produced or sold by it or provides services to consumers shall comply with this Law; where this Law has no provisions, it shall comply with other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经营者与消费者进行交易，应当遵循自愿、平等、公平、诚实信用的原则。

**Article 4** A business operator and a consumer in a transaction shall follow the principles of voluntariness, equality, fairness, and good faith.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家保护消费者的合法权益不受侵害。  
国家采取措施，保障消费者依法行使权利，维护消费者的合法权益。  
国家倡导文明、健康、节约资源和保护环境的消费方式，反对浪费。

**Article 5** The State protects the legitimate rights and interests of consumers from encroachment.  
The State adopts measures to ensure consumers exercise their rights according to law and safeguards the legitimate rights and interests of consumers.  
The State advocates civilized, healthy, resource-conserving and environment-friendly patterns of consumption, and opposes waste.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保护消费者的合法权益是全社会的共同责任。  
国家鼓励、支持一切组织和个人对损害消费者合法权益的行为进行社会监督。  
大众传播媒介应当做好维护消费者合法权益的宣传，对损害消费者合法权益的行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 6** Protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers is the common responsibility of the whole society.  
The State encourages and supports all organizations and individuals to conduct social supervision over acts that infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of consumers.  
The mass media shall conduct good publicity on safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, and shall exercise supervision by public opinion over acts that infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of consumers.

### 第二章　消费者的权利

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　消费者在购买、使用商品和接受服务时享有人身、财产安全不受损害的权利。  
消费者有权要求经营者提供的商品和服务，符合保障人身、财产安全的要求。

**Article 7** A consumer shall have the right that his or her person or property is not harmed when purchasing or using a commodity or accepting a service.  
A consumer shall have the right to demand that the commodities and services provided by a business operator meet the requirements for the protection of personal and property safety.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　消费者享有知悉其购买、使用的商品或者接受的服务的真实情况的权利。  
消费者有权根据商品或者服务的不同情况，要求经营者提供商品的价格、产地、生产者、用途、性能、规格、等级、主要成份、生产日期、有效期限、检验合格证明、使用方法说明书、售后服务，或者服务的内容、规格、费用等有关情况。

**Article 8** A consumer shall have the right to know the true facts about the commodity he or she purchases or uses or the service he or she accepts.  
A consumer shall have the right, in light of the different circumstances of the commodity or service, to require the business operator to provide such information as the price, place of origin, producer, purpose, performance, specifications, grade, main ingredients, date of production, validity period, inspection certificate, instruction manual, after-sales service of the commodity, or the content, specifications, fees, and other relevant information of the service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　消费者享有自主选择商品或者服务的权利。  
消费者有权自主选择提供商品或者服务的经营者，自主选择商品品种或者服务方式，自主决定购买或者不购买任何一种商品、接受或者不接受任何一项服务。  
消费者在自主选择商品或者服务时，有权进行比较、鉴别和挑选。

**Article 9** A consumer shall have the right to make a free choice of commodities or services.  
A consumer shall have the right to freely choose the business operator providing commodities or services, freely choose the variety of commodities or the mode of service, and freely decide whether to purchase any commodity or accept any service.  
When making a free choice of commodities or services, a consumer shall have the right to make comparisons, appraisals, and selections.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　消费者享有公平交易的权利。  
消费者在购买商品或者接受服务时，有权获得质量保障、价格合理、计量正确等公平交易条件，有权拒绝经营者的强制交易行为。

**Article 10** A consumer shall have the right to a fair transaction.  
When purchasing a commodity or accepting a service, a consumer shall have the right to obtain fair transaction conditions, such as quality assurance, reasonable prices, and correct weights and measures, and shall have the right to refuse any forced transaction by a business operator.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　消费者因购买、使用商品或者接受服务受到人身、财产损害的，享有依法获得赔偿的权利。

**Article 11** A consumer who suffers personal injury or property damage as a result of purchasing or using a commodity or accepting a service shall have the right to obtain compensation according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　消费者享有依法成立维护自身合法权益的社会组织的权利。

**Article 12** A consumer shall have the right to establish, according to law, social organizations to safeguard his or her legitimate rights and interests.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　消费者享有获得有关消费和消费者权益保护方面的知识的权利。  
消费者应当努力掌握所需商品或者服务的知识和使用技能，正确使用商品，提高自我保护意识。

**Article 13** A consumer shall have the right to acquire knowledge concerning consumption and protection of consumers' rights and interests.  
A consumer shall endeavor to master the knowledge and use skills of the required commodity or service, correctly use the commodity, and enhance the awareness of self-protection.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　消费者在购买、使用商品和接受服务时，享有人格尊严、民族风俗习惯得到尊重的权利，享有个人信息依法得到保护的权利。

**Article 14** A consumer shall, when purchasing or using a commodity or accepting a service, have the right that his or her personal dignity and ethnic customs are respected, and have the right that his or her personal information is protected according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　消费者享有对商品和服务以及保护消费者权益工作进行监督的权利。  
消费者有权检举、控告侵害消费者权益的行为和国家机关及其工作人员在保护消费者权益工作中的违法失职行为，有权对保护消费者权益工作提出批评、建议。

**Article 15** A consumer shall have the right to supervise commodities and services and the work of protecting consumers' rights and interests.  
A consumer shall have the right to report and accuse acts that infringe upon consumers' rights and interests, and the illegal or derelict conduct of state organs and their staff members in the work of protecting consumers' rights and interests, and shall have the right to criticize and make suggestions on the work of protecting consumers' rights and interests.

### 第三章　经营者的义务

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经营者向消费者提供商品或者服务，应当依照本法和其他有关法律、法规的规定履行义务。  
经营者和消费者有约定的，应当按照约定履行义务，但双方的约定不得违背法律、法规的规定。  
经营者向消费者提供商品或者服务，应当恪守社会公德，诚信经营，保障消费者的合法权益；不得设定不公平、不合理的交易条件，不得强制交易。

**Article 16** A business operator that provides commodities or services to consumers shall perform its obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations.  
Where the business operator and the consumer have an agreement, the business operator shall perform its obligations in accordance with the agreement, but the agreement between the parties shall not contravene the provisions of laws and regulations.  
A business operator that provides commodities or services to consumers shall abide by social morality, conduct business in good faith, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of consumers; it shall not set unfair or unreasonable transaction conditions, and shall not force transactions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营者应当听取消费者对其提供的商品或者服务的意见，接受消费者的监督。

**Article 17** A business operator shall listen to the opinions of consumers on the commodities or services it provides, and accept the supervision of consumers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经营者应当保证其提供的商品或者服务符合保障人身、财产安全的要求。对可能危及人身、财产安全的商品和服务，应当向消费者作出真实的说明和明确的警示，并说明和标明正确使用商品或者接受服务的方法以及防止危害发生的方法。  
宾馆、商场、餐馆、银行、机场、车站、港口、影剧院等经营场所的经营者，应当对消费者尽到安全保障义务。

**Article 18** A business operator shall ensure that the commodities or services it provides meet the requirements for the protection of personal and property safety. For commodities and services that may be dangerous to personal or property safety, it shall give consumers truthful explanations and clear warnings, and shall explain and indicate the correct methods of using the commodity or accepting the service and the methods of preventing the occurrence of hazards.  
Business operators of such business premises as hotels, shopping malls, restaurants, banks, airports, railway stations, ports, and theaters shall fulfill the obligation of safety protection to consumers.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　经营者发现其提供的商品或者服务存在缺陷，有危及人身、财产安全危险的，应当立即向有关行政部门报告和告知消费者，并采取停止销售、警示、召回、无害化处理、销毁、停止生产或者服务等措施。采取召回措施的，经营者应当承担消费者因商品被召回支出的必要费用。

**Article 19** Where a business operator discovers that the commodities or services it provides have a defect that may endanger personal or property safety, it shall immediately report to the relevant administrative department and inform consumers, and shall take measures such as suspending sales, issuing warnings, recalling, harmless treatment, destruction, or suspending production or services. Where the measure of recall is taken, the business operator shall bear the necessary expenses incurred by consumers as a result of the recall of the commodity.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经营者向消费者提供有关商品或者服务的质量、性能、用途、有效期限等信息，应当真实、全面，不得作虚假或者引人误解的宣传。  
经营者对消费者就其提供的商品或者服务的质量和使用方法等问题提出的询问，应当作出真实、明确的答复。  
经营者提供商品或者服务应当明码标价。

**Article 20** A business operator that provides consumers with information on the quality, performance, purpose, and validity period of commodities or services shall provide truthful and comprehensive information, and shall not make false or misleading publicity.  
A business operator shall give truthful and definite answers to the inquiries of consumers regarding the quality, methods of use, and other issues of the commodities or services it provides.  
A business operator shall clearly mark the prices of the commodities or services it provides.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经营者应当标明其真实名称和标记。  
租赁他人柜台或者场地的经营者，应当标明其真实名称和标记。

**Article 21** A business operator shall indicate its true name and mark. A business operator leasing counters or venues from others shall indicate its true name and mark.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经营者提供商品或者服务，应当按照国家有关规定或者商业惯例向消费者出具发票等购货凭证或者服务单据；消费者索要发票等购货凭证或者服务单据的，经营者必须出具。

**Article 22** When providing commodities or services, a business operator shall, in accordance with relevant provisions of the State or commercial practices, issue invoices or other purchase vouchers or service documents to consumers; where a consumer requests invoices or other purchase vouchers or service documents, the business operator must issue them.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经营者应当保证在正常使用商品或者接受服务的情况下其提供的商品或者服务应当具有的质量、性能、用途和有效期限；但消费者在购买该商品或者接受该服务前已经知道其存在瑕疵，且存在该瑕疵不违反法律强制性规定的除外。  
经营者以广告、产品说明、实物样品或者其他方式表明商品或者服务的质量状况的，应当保证其提供的商品或者服务的实际质量与表明的质量状况相符。  
经营者提供的机动车、计算机、电视机、电冰箱、空调器、洗衣机等耐用商品或者装饰装修等服务，消费者自接受商品或者服务之日起六个月内发现瑕疵，发生争议的，由经营者承担有关瑕疵的举证责任。

**Article 23** A business operator shall guarantee that the commodities or services provided by it have the quality, performance, use and duration that they shall have when used normally or received normally, except where the consumer knew of the existence of the defect before purchasing the commodity or receiving the service and such defect does not violate the mandatory provisions of the law.  
Where a business operator indicates the quality status of commodities or services by means of advertisements, product descriptions, physical samples or any other means, it shall guarantee that the actual quality of the commodities or services provided by it conforms to the indicated quality status.  
Where a business operator provides durable commodities such as motor vehicles, computers, televisions, refrigerators, air conditioners, washing machines, or services such as decoration and renovation, if a consumer finds a defect within six months from the date of receiving the commodity or service and a dispute arises, the business operator shall bear the burden of adducing evidence concerning the defect.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经营者提供的商品或者服务不符合质量要求的，消费者可以依照国家规定、当事人约定退货，或者要求经营者履行更换、修理等义务。没有国家规定和当事人约定的，消费者可以自收到商品之日起七日内退货；七日后符合法定解除合同条件的，消费者可以及时退货，不符合法定解除合同条件的，可以要求经营者履行更换、修理等义务。  
依照前款规定进行退货、更换、修理的，经营者应当承担运输等必要费用。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理旅游纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理旅游纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** Where the commodities or services provided by a business operator do not meet the quality requirements, the consumer may, in accordance with the provisions of the State or the agreement of the parties, return the commodities, or require the business operator to fulfill obligations such as replacement or repair. Where there are no provisions of the State or agreement of the parties, the consumer may return the commodities within seven days from the date of receipt; after seven days, if the conditions for dissolution of a contract prescribed by law are met, the consumer may return the commodities in a timely manner; if the conditions for dissolution of a contract are not met, the consumer may require the business operator to fulfill obligations such as replacement or repair.  
Where the return, replacement, or repair is carried out in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the business operator shall bear the necessary expenses such as transportation costs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营者采用网络、电视、电话、邮购等方式销售商品，消费者有权自收到商品之日起七日内退货，且无需说明理由，但下列商品除外：  
（一）消费者定作的；  
（二）鲜活易腐的；  
（三）在线下载或者消费者拆封的音像制品、计算机软件等数字化商品；  
（四）交付的报纸、期刊。  
除前款所列商品外，其他根据商品性质并经消费者在购买时确认不宜退货的商品，不适用无理由退货。  
消费者退货的商品应当完好。经营者应当自收到退回商品之日起七日内返还消费者支付的商品价款。退回商品的运费由消费者承担；经营者和消费者另有约定的，按照约定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[3]</sup>

**Article 25** Where a business operator sells commodities by means of the internet, television, telephone, mail order, etc., the consumer has the right to return the commodities within seven days from the date of receipt without stating any reason, except for the following commodities:  
(1) commodities made to the consumer's specifications;  
(2) fresh, live, or perishable commodities;  
(3) audio-visual products, computer software and other digital commodities downloaded online or unpacked by the consumer;  
(4) newspapers and periodicals delivered.  
In addition to the commodities listed in the preceding paragraph, other commodities that, depending on their nature and upon confirmation by the consumer at the time of purchase, are not suitable for return shall not be subject to no-reason return.  
The commodities returned by the consumer shall be in good condition. The business operator shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the returned commodities, refund to the consumer the price paid for the commodities. The transportation costs for returning the commodities shall be borne by the consumer; where otherwise agreed between the business operator and the consumer, the agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经营者在经营活动中使用格式条款的，应当以显著方式提请消费者注意商品或者服务的数量和质量、价款或者费用、履行期限和方式、安全注意事项和风险警示、售后服务、民事责任等与消费者有重大利害关系的内容，并按照消费者的要求予以说明。  
经营者不得以格式条款、通知、声明、店堂告示等方式，作出排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理的规定，不得利用格式条款并借助技术手段强制交易。  
格式条款、通知、声明、店堂告示等含有前款所列内容的，其内容无效。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>

**Article 26** Where a business operator uses standard clauses in its business activities, it shall, in a conspicuous manner, draw the consumer's attention to the contents that have a significant bearing on the consumer's interests, such as the quantity and quality of the commodities or services, the price or fees, the time limit and method of performance, safety precautions and risk warnings, after-sales service, civil liability, etc., and shall provide explanations at the consumer's request.  
A business operator shall not, by means of standard clauses, notices, declarations, store announcements, etc., make provisions that exclude or limit the consumer's rights, reduce or exempt the business operator's responsibilities, or increase the consumer's responsibilities, which are unfair or unreasonable to the consumer, and shall not use standard clauses with the aid of technical means to force transactions.  
Where standard clauses, notices, declarations, store announcements, etc. contain the contents listed in the preceding paragraph, such contents shall be null and void.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　经营者不得对消费者进行侮辱、诽谤，不得搜查消费者的身体及其携带的物品，不得侵犯消费者的人身自由。

**Article 27** A business operator shall not insult or slander consumers, nor search the body of consumers or the articles they carry, nor infringe upon the personal freedom of consumers.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　采用网络、电视、电话、邮购等方式提供商品或者服务的经营者，以及提供证券、保险、银行等金融服务的经营者，应当向消费者提供经营地址、联系方式、商品或者服务的数量和质量、价款或者费用、履行期限和方式、安全注意事项和风险警示、售后服务、民事责任等信息。

**Article 28** Business operators that provide commodities or services by means of the internet, television, telephone, mail order, etc., as well as business operators that provide financial services such as securities, insurance, and banking, shall provide consumers with information such as business address, contact information, the quantity and quality of commodities or services, price or fees, time limit and method of performance, safety precautions and risk warnings, after-sales service, civil liability, etc.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　经营者收集、使用消费者个人信息，应当遵循合法、正当、必要的原则，明示收集、使用信息的目的、方式和范围，并经消费者同意。经营者收集、使用消费者个人信息，应当公开其收集、使用规则，不得违反法律、法规的规定和双方的约定收集、使用信息。  
经营者及其工作人员对收集的消费者个人信息必须严格保密，不得泄露、出售或者非法向他人提供。经营者应当采取技术措施和其他必要措施，确保信息安全，防止消费者个人信息泄露、丢失。在发生或者可能发生信息泄露、丢失的情况时，应当立即采取补救措施。  
经营者未经消费者同意或者请求，或者消费者明确表示拒绝的，不得向其发送商业性信息。

**Article 29** When collecting and using consumers' personal information, a business operator shall comply with the principles of lawfulness, propriety and necessity, expressly indicate the purposes, methods and scope of collecting and using the information, and obtain the consent of the consumer. When collecting and using consumers' personal information, a business operator shall disclose its rules for collection and use, and shall not collect or use the information in violation of the provisions of laws or regulations or the agreement between the parties.  
A business operator and its staff members must keep strictly confidential the personal information of consumers collected, and shall not disclose, sell or illegally provide it to others. A business operator shall adopt technical measures and other necessary measures to ensure the security of information and prevent the disclosure or loss of consumers' personal information. Where disclosure or loss occurs or may occur, the business operator shall immediately take remedial measures.  
A business operator shall not send commercial information to a consumer without the consumer's consent or request, or where the consumer has expressly refused.

### 第四章　国家对消费者合法权益的保护

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家制定有关消费者权益的法律、法规、规章和强制性标准，应当听取消费者和消费者协会等组织的意见。

**Article 30** Where the State formulates laws, regulations, rules and compulsory standards concerning the rights and interests of consumers, it shall solicit opinions from consumers and organizations such as consumer associations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民政府应当加强领导，组织、协调、督促有关行政部门做好保护消费者合法权益的工作，落实保护消费者合法权益的职责。  
各级人民政府应当加强监督，预防危害消费者人身、财产安全行为的发生，及时制止危害消费者人身、财产安全的行为。

**Article 31** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership, organize, coordinate and urge relevant administrative departments to do a good job in protecting the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and implement the responsibilities for protecting the lawful rights and interests of consumers.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen supervision, prevent the occurrence of acts endangering the personal or property safety of consumers, and timely stop acts endangering the personal or property safety of consumers.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　各级人民政府工商行政管理部门和其他有关行政部门应当依照法律、法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内，采取措施，保护消费者的合法权益。  
有关行政部门应当听取消费者和消费者协会等组织对经营者交易行为、商品和服务质量问题的意见，及时调查处理。

**Article 32** The administrative departments for industry and commerce under the people's governments at all levels and other relevant administrative departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, take measures within their respective functions and duties to protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers.  
Relevant administrative departments shall listen to opinions from consumers and organizations such as consumer associations on the transaction behavior of business operators and the quality of commodities and services, and shall investigate and handle them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有关行政部门在各自的职责范围内，应当定期或者不定期对经营者提供的商品和服务进行抽查检验，并及时向社会公布抽查检验结果。  
有关行政部门发现并认定经营者提供的商品或者服务存在缺陷，有危及人身、财产安全危险的，应当立即责令经营者采取停止销售、警示、召回、无害化处理、销毁、停止生产或者服务等措施。

**Article 33** Relevant administrative departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, regularly or irregularly conduct random inspection and testing of the commodities and services provided by business operators, and promptly make public the results of such random inspection and testing.  
Where relevant administrative departments discover and determine that the commodities or services provided by a business operator have defects that pose a danger to personal or property safety, they shall immediately order the business operator to take measures such as suspending sales, issuing warnings, recalling, harmless disposal, destruction, or suspending production or services.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　有关国家机关应当依照法律、法规的规定，惩处经营者在提供商品和服务中侵害消费者合法权益的违法犯罪行为。

**Article 34** Relevant State authorities shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, punish illegal or criminal acts committed by business operators in providing commodities or services that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of consumers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人民法院应当采取措施，方便消费者提起诉讼。对符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》起诉条件的消费者权益争议，必须受理，及时审理。

**Article 35** People's Courts shall take measures to facilitate consumers in initiating prosecutions. Disputes over the rights and interests of consumers that meet the conditions for prosecution under the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China must be accepted and tried in a timely manner.

### 第五章　消费者组织

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　消费者协会和其他消费者组织是依法成立的对商品和服务进行社会监督的保护消费者合法权益的社会组织。

**Article 36** Consumer associations and other consumer organizations are social organizations established according to law that exercise social supervision over commodities and services to protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　消费者协会履行下列公益性职责：  
（一）向消费者提供消费信息和咨询服务，提高消费者维护自身合法权益的能力，引导文明、健康、节约资源和保护环境的消费方式；  
（二）参与制定有关消费者权益的法律、法规、规章和强制性标准；  
（三）参与有关行政部门对商品和服务的监督、检查；  
（四）就有关消费者合法权益的问题，向有关部门反映、查询，提出建议；  
（五）受理消费者的投诉，并对投诉事项进行调查、调解；  
（六）投诉事项涉及商品和服务质量问题的，可以委托具备资格的鉴定人鉴定，鉴定人应当告知鉴定意见；  
（七）就损害消费者合法权益的行为，支持受损害的消费者提起诉讼或者依照本法提起诉讼；  
（八）对损害消费者合法权益的行为，通过大众传播媒介予以揭露、批评。  
各级人民政府对消费者协会履行职责应当予以必要的经费等支持。  
消费者协会应当认真履行保护消费者合法权益的职责，听取消费者的意见和建议，接受社会监督。  
依法成立的其他消费者组织依照法律、法规及其章程的规定，开展保护消费者合法权益的活动。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 37** Consumer associations shall perform the following public interest functions:  
(1) provide consumers with consumption information and consulting services, improve consumers' ability to protect their own lawful rights and interests, and guide consumption patterns that are civilized, healthy, resource-conserving and environment-friendly;  
(2) participate in the formulation of laws, regulations, rules and compulsory standards concerning the rights and interests of consumers;  
(3) participate in the supervision and inspection of commodities and services by relevant administrative departments;  
(4) reflect and inquire with relevant departments on issues concerning the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and make suggestions;  
(5) accept consumer complaints, and investigate and mediate the complaints;  
(6) where a complaint involves the quality of commodities or services, may entrust an expert evaluation institution with the necessary qualifications to conduct an expert evaluation, and the expert evaluator shall inform the evaluation opinion;  
(7) support consumers whose rights have been harmed in instituting legal proceedings, or institute proceedings in accordance with this Law, against acts harming the lawful rights and interests of consumers;  
(8) expose and criticize acts harming the lawful rights and interests of consumers through the mass media.  
People's governments at all levels shall provide necessary financial and other support for consumer associations to perform their duties.  
Consumer associations shall conscientiously perform their duties of protecting the lawful rights and interests of consumers, listen to the opinions and suggestions of consumers, and accept social supervision.  
Other consumer organizations established according to law shall carry out activities to protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations and their own articles of association.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　消费者组织不得从事商品经营和营利性服务，不得以收取费用或者其他牟取利益的方式向消费者推荐商品和服务。

**Article 38** Consumer organizations shall not engage in commodity business or for-profit services, and shall not recommend commodities or services to consumers by means of collecting fees or seeking other profits.

### 第六章　争议的解决

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　消费者和经营者发生消费者权益争议的，可以通过下列途径解决：  
（一）与经营者协商和解；  
（二）请求消费者协会或者依法成立的其他调解组织调解；  
（三）向有关行政部门投诉；  
（四）根据与经营者达成的仲裁协议提请仲裁机构仲裁；  
（五）向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 39** Where disputes over the rights and interests of consumers arise between consumers and business operators, they may be resolved through the following channels:  
(1) negotiation and settlement with the business operator;  
(2) requesting mediation by a consumer association or another mediation organization established according to law;  
(3) filing a complaint with the relevant administrative department;  
(4) submitting the dispute to an arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with an arbitration agreement reached with the business operator;  
(5) instituting legal proceedings with the People's Court.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　消费者在购买、使用商品时，其合法权益受到损害的，可以向销售者要求赔偿。销售者赔偿后，属于生产者的责任或者属于向销售者提供商品的其他销售者的责任的，销售者有权向生产者或者其他销售者追偿。  
消费者或者其他受害人因商品缺陷造成人身、财产损害的，可以向销售者要求赔偿，也可以向生产者要求赔偿。属于生产者责任的，销售者赔偿后，有权向生产者追偿。属于销售者责任的，生产者赔偿后，有权向销售者追偿。  
消费者在接受服务时，其合法权益受到损害的，可以向服务者要求赔偿。

**Article 40** Where a consumer's lawful rights and interests are harmed when purchasing or using a commodity, the consumer may claim compensation from the seller. After the seller compensates, if the responsibility lies with the producer or with another seller who supplied the commodity to the seller, the seller has the right to recover from the producer or the other seller.  
Where a consumer or another victim suffers personal injury or property damage due to a defect in a commodity, the consumer may claim compensation from the seller or from the producer. If the responsibility lies with the producer, the seller, after compensating, has the right to recover from the producer. If the responsibility lies with the seller, the producer, after compensating, has the right to recover from the seller.  
Where a consumer's lawful rights and interests are harmed when receiving a service, the consumer may claim compensation from the service provider.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　消费者在购买、使用商品或者接受服务时，其合法权益受到损害，因原企业分立、合并的，可以向变更后承受其权利义务的企业要求赔偿。

**Article 41** Where a consumer's legitimate rights and interests are harmed when purchasing or using commodities or receiving services, and such harm is caused by the division or merger of the original enterprise, the consumer may claim compensation from the enterprise that succeeds to the rights and obligations after the change.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　使用他人营业执照的违法经营者提供商品或者服务，损害消费者合法权益的，消费者可以向其要求赔偿，也可以向营业执照的持有人要求赔偿。

**Article 42** Where an illegal business operator using another person's business license provides commodities or services and harms the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, the consumer may claim compensation from such operator, or may claim compensation from the holder of the business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　消费者在展销会、租赁柜台购买商品或者接受服务，其合法权益受到损害的，可以向销售者或者服务者要求赔偿。展销会结束或者柜台租赁期满后，也可以向展销会的举办者、柜台的出租者要求赔偿。展销会的举办者、柜台的出租者赔偿后，有权向销售者或者服务者追偿。

**Article 43** Where a consumer's legitimate rights and interests are harmed when purchasing commodities or receiving services at a trade fair or from a leased counter, the consumer may claim compensation from the seller or service provider. After the conclusion of the trade fair or the expiration of the counter lease, the consumer may also claim compensation from the organizer of the trade fair or the lessor of the counter. The organizer of the trade fair or the lessor of the counter, after paying compensation, shall have the right to recourse against the seller or service provider.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　消费者通过网络交易平台购买商品或者接受服务，其合法权益受到损害的，可以向销售者或者服务者要求赔偿。网络交易平台提供者不能提供销售者或者服务者的真实名称、地址和有效联系方式的，消费者也可以向网络交易平台提供者要求赔偿；网络交易平台提供者作出更有利于消费者的承诺的，应当履行承诺。网络交易平台提供者赔偿后，有权向销售者或者服务者追偿。  
网络交易平台提供者明知或者应知销售者或者服务者利用其平台侵害消费者合法权益，未采取必要措施的，依法与该销售者或者服务者承担连带责任。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 44** Where a consumer’s legitimate rights and interests are harmed when purchasing commodities or receiving services through an online trading platform, the consumer may claim compensation from the seller or service provider. If the online trading platform provider fails to provide the true name, address and valid contact information of the seller or service provider, the consumer may also claim compensation from the online trading platform provider; if the online trading platform provider makes a commitment more favorable to consumers, it shall fulfill such commitment. The online trading platform provider, after paying compensation, shall have the right to recourse against the seller or service provider.  
Where an online trading platform provider knows or should have known that a seller or service provider is using its platform to infringe upon consumers’ legitimate rights and interests, but fails to take necessary measures, it shall bear joint and several liability with the seller or service provider according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　消费者因经营者利用虚假广告或者其他虚假宣传方式提供商品或者服务，其合法权益受到损害的，可以向经营者要求赔偿。广告经营者、发布者发布虚假广告的，消费者可以请求行政主管部门予以惩处。广告经营者、发布者不能提供经营者的真实名称、地址和有效联系方式的，应当承担赔偿责任。  
广告经营者、发布者设计、制作、发布关系消费者生命健康商品或者服务的虚假广告，造成消费者损害的，应当与提供该商品或者服务的经营者承担连带责任。  
社会团体或者其他组织、个人在关系消费者生命健康商品或者服务的虚假广告或者其他虚假宣传中向消费者推荐商品或者服务，造成消费者损害的，应当与提供该商品或者服务的经营者承担连带责任。

**Article 45** Where a consumer’s legitimate rights and interests are harmed because a business operator provides commodities or services by means of false advertising or other false publicity, the consumer may claim compensation from the business operator. Where an advertising operator or publisher publishes false advertisements, the consumer may request the competent administrative department to impose sanctions. If the advertising operator or publisher fails to provide the true name, address and valid contact information of the business operator, it shall bear compensation liability.  
Where an advertising operator or publisher designs, produces, and publishes false advertisements concerning commodities or services relating to the life and health of consumers, causing harm to consumers, it shall bear joint and several liability with the business operator providing such commodities or services.  
Where a social organization or other organization or individual recommends commodities or services to consumers in false advertisements or other false publicity concerning commodities or services relating to the life and health of consumers, causing harm to consumers, it or he shall bear joint and several liability with the business operator providing such commodities or services.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　消费者向有关行政部门投诉的，该部门应当自收到投诉之日起七个工作日内，予以处理并告知消费者。

**Article 46** Where a consumer files a complaint with the relevant administrative department, the department shall, within seven working days from the date of receipt of the complaint, handle the matter and notify the consumer.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对侵害众多消费者合法权益的行为，中国消费者协会以及在省、自治区、直辖市设立的消费者协会，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事程序解释/最高人民法院关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>

**Article 47** For conduct that infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of many consumers, the China Consumers' Association and the consumers' associations established in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　经营者提供商品或者服务有下列情形之一的，除本法另有规定外，应当依照其他有关法律、法规的规定，承担民事责任：  
（一）商品或者服务存在缺陷的；  
（二）不具备商品应当具备的使用性能而出售时未作说明的；  
（三）不符合在商品或者其包装上注明采用的商品标准的；  
（四）不符合商品说明、实物样品等方式表明的质量状况的；  
（五）生产国家明令淘汰的商品或者销售失效、变质的商品的；  
（六）销售的商品数量不足的；  
（七）服务的内容和费用违反约定的；  
（八）对消费者提出的修理、重作、更换、退货、补足商品数量、退还货款和服务费用或者赔偿损失的要求，故意拖延或者无理拒绝的；  
（九）法律、法规规定的其他损害消费者权益的情形。  
经营者对消费者未尽到安全保障义务，造成消费者损害的，应当承担侵权责任。

**Article 48** Where a business operator provides commodities or services under any of the following circumstances, except as otherwise provided in this Law, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with the provisions of other relevant laws and regulations:  
(1) The commodities or services have defects;  
(2) The commodities do not possess the performance they should possess and no explanation is given at the time of sale;  
(3) The commodities do not conform to the standards indicated on the commodities or their packaging;  
(4) The commodities do not conform to the quality status indicated by the description, physical sample or other means;  
(5) Producing commodities that are explicitly eliminated by the state or selling expired or deteriorated commodities;  
(6) Selling commodities in short quantity;  
(7) The content and fees of services violate the agreement;  
(8) Deliberately delaying or unreasonably refusing the consumer's requests for repair, rework, replacement, return of goods, supplement of quantity, refund of price and service fees, or compensation for losses;  
(9) Other circumstances that harm the rights and interests of consumers as provided for by laws and regulations.  
Where a business operator fails to fulfill its safety guarantee obligation towards consumers, causing harm to consumers, it shall bear tort liability.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　经营者提供商品或者服务，造成消费者或者其他受害人人身伤害的，应当赔偿医疗费、护理费、交通费等为治疗和康复支出的合理费用，以及因误工减少的收入。造成残疾的，还应当赔偿残疾生活辅助具费和残疾赔偿金。造成死亡的，还应当赔偿丧葬费和死亡赔偿金。

**Article 49** Where a business operator provides commodities or services, causing personal injury to consumers or other victims, it shall compensate for medical expenses, nursing fees, transportation expenses and other reasonable expenses incurred for treatment and rehabilitation, as well as the income reduced due to loss of working time. If disability is caused, it shall also compensate for the cost of assistive devices for disabled persons and disability compensation. If death is caused, it shall also compensate for funeral expenses and death compensation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　经营者侵害消费者的人格尊严、侵犯消费者人身自由或者侵害消费者个人信息依法得到保护的权利的，应当停止侵害、恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉，并赔偿损失。

**Article 50** Where a business operator infringes upon a consumer's personal dignity, violates a consumer's personal freedom, or infringes upon a consumer's right to personal information protected according to law, it shall cease the infringement, restore the consumer's reputation, eliminate the effects, make an apology, and compensate for losses.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　经营者有侮辱诽谤、搜查身体、侵犯人身自由等侵害消费者或者其他受害人人身权益的行为，造成严重精神损害的，受害人可以要求精神损害赔偿。

**Article 51** Where a business operator engages in conduct such as insulting and defaming, searching the body, or violating personal freedom, which infringes upon the personal rights and interests of consumers or other victims, causing serious mental harm, the victim may claim compensation for mental damages.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　经营者提供商品或者服务，造成消费者财产损害的，应当依照法律规定或者当事人约定承担修理、重作、更换、退货、补足商品数量、退还货款和服务费用或者赔偿损失等民事责任。

**Article 52** Where a business operator provides commodities or services, causing property damage to consumers, it shall bear civil liability such as repair, rework, replacement, return of goods, supplement of quantity, refund of price and service fees, or compensation for losses, in accordance with the provisions of laws or the agreement of the parties.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　经营者以预收款方式提供商品或者服务的，应当按照约定提供。未按照约定提供的，应当按照消费者的要求履行约定或者退回预付款；并应当承担预付款的利息、消费者必须支付的合理费用。

**Article 53** Where a business operator provides commodities or services on a prepayment basis, it shall provide them in accordance with the agreement. If it fails to provide in accordance with the agreement, it shall perform the agreement or refund the prepayment as demanded by the consumer; and it shall bear the interest on the prepayment and the reasonable expenses necessarily paid by the consumer.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依法经有关行政部门认定为不合格的商品，消费者要求退货的，经营者应当负责退货。

**Article 54** If a commodity is determined to be nonconforming according to law by the relevant administrative department, and the consumer requests a return of the goods, the business operator shall be responsible for the return.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　经营者提供商品或者服务有欺诈行为的，应当按照消费者的要求增加赔偿其受到的损失，增加赔偿的金额为消费者购买商品的价款或者接受服务的费用的三倍；增加赔偿的金额不足五百元的，为五百元。法律另有规定的，依照其规定。  
经营者明知商品或者服务存在缺陷，仍然向消费者提供，造成消费者或者其他受害人死亡或者健康严重损害的，受害人有权要求经营者依照[本法第四十九条](#art-49)、第五十一条等法律规定赔偿损失，并有权要求所受损失二倍以下的惩罚性赔偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例.md#art-49" title="被《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例》第49条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[3]</sup>

**Article 55** Where a business operator engages in fraudulent conduct in providing commodities or services, it shall, at the consumer's demand, increase the compensation for the losses suffered, and the amount of the increased compensation shall be three times the price of the commodities purchased or the fees for the services received; if the amount of the increased compensation is less than 500 yuan, it shall be 500 yuan. If otherwise provided by law, those provisions shall prevail.  
Where a business operator, knowing that commodities or services have defects, still provides them to consumers, causing death or severe harm to health of consumers or other victims, the victim shall have the right to claim compensation for losses in accordance with the provisions of Article 49, Article 51 and other provisions of this Law, and shall have the right to claim punitive damages of not more than two times the losses suffered.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　经营者有下列情形之一，除承担相应的民事责任外，其他有关法律、法规对处罚机关和处罚方式有规定的，依照法律、法规的规定执行；法律、法规未作规定的，由工商行政管理部门或者其他有关行政部门责令改正，可以根据情节单处或者并处警告、没收违法所得、处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处以五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿、吊销营业执照：  
（一）提供的商品或者服务不符合保障人身、财产安全要求的；  
（二）在商品中掺杂、掺假，以假充真，以次充好，或者以不合格商品冒充合格商品的；  
（三）生产国家明令淘汰的商品或者销售失效、变质的商品的；  
（四）伪造商品的产地，伪造或者冒用他人的厂名、厂址，篡改生产日期，伪造或者冒用认证标志等质量标志的；  
（五）销售的商品应当检验、检疫而未检验、检疫或者伪造检验、检疫结果的；  
（六）对商品或者服务作虚假或者引人误解的宣传的；  
（七）拒绝或者拖延有关行政部门责令对缺陷商品或者服务采取停止销售、警示、召回、无害化处理、销毁、停止生产或者服务等措施的；  
（八）对消费者提出的修理、重作、更换、退货、补足商品数量、退还货款和服务费用或者赔偿损失的要求，故意拖延或者无理拒绝的；  
（九）侵害消费者人格尊严、侵犯消费者人身自由或者侵害消费者个人信息依法得到保护的权利的；  
（十）法律、法规规定的对损害消费者权益应当予以处罚的其他情形。  
经营者有前款规定情形的，除依照法律、法规规定予以处罚外，处罚机关应当记入信用档案，向社会公布。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/工商市场/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 56** Where a business operator is under any of the following circumstances, in addition to bearing corresponding civil liability, if other relevant laws and regulations have provisions on the punishing authority and methods of punishment, such provisions shall be followed; if laws and regulations have no such provisions, the administrative department for industry and commerce or other relevant administrative department shall order correction, and may, depending on the circumstances, impose a single or combined punishment of a warning, confiscation of illegal gains, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the business operator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification and its business license shall be revoked:  
(1) The commodities or services provided do not meet the requirements for the protection of personal and property safety;  
(2) Adulterating or mixing impurities into commodities, passing off fake commodities as genuine, shoddy goods as high-quality goods, or passing off non-conforming commodities as conforming commodities;  
(3) Producing commodities explicitly eliminated by the state, or selling expired or deteriorated commodities;  
(4) Falsifying the place of origin of commodities, forging or fraudulently using the name or address of another person, altering the date of production, forging or fraudulently using quality marks such as certification marks;  
(5) Selling commodities that should have been inspected or quarantined but have not been inspected or quarantined, or forging inspection or quarantine results;  
(6) Making false or misleading publicity about commodities or services;  
(7) Refusing or delaying the adoption of measures such as suspension of sales, warnings, recalls, harmless treatment, destruction, suspension of production or services ordered by the relevant administrative department for defective commodities or services;  
(8) Deliberately delaying or unreasonably refusing the consumer's requests for repair, rework, replacement, return of goods, supplement of quantity, refund of price and service fees, or compensation for losses;  
(9) Infringing upon consumers' personal dignity, violating consumers' personal freedom, or infringing upon consumers' right to personal information protected according to law;  
(10) Other circumstances that should be punished for harming consumers' rights and interests as provided by laws and regulations.  
Where a business operator falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, in addition to being punished in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, the punishing authority shall record the matter in the credit file and make it public.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　经营者违反本法规定提供商品或者服务，侵害消费者合法权益，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of this Law, provides commodities or services and infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　经营者违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金，其财产不足以同时支付的，先承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 58** Where a business operator, in violation of the provisions of this Law, shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines and pecuniary penalties, if its property is insufficient to pay both, it shall first bear the civil compensation liability.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　经营者对行政处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 59** Where a business operator is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, it may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　以暴力、威胁等方法阻碍有关行政部门工作人员依法执行职务的，依法追究刑事责任；拒绝、阻碍有关行政部门工作人员依法执行职务，未使用暴力、威胁方法的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚。

**Article 60** Whoever obstructs the staff members of relevant administrative departments from performing their duties according to law by means of violence, threats, or otherwise shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; whoever refuses to or obstructs the staff members of relevant administrative departments from performing their duties according to law without resorting to violence or threats shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家机关工作人员玩忽职守或者包庇经营者侵害消费者合法权益的行为的，由其所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where a state agency functionary neglects his duty or shields business operators in infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of consumers, administrative sanctions shall be imposed upon him by the unit he belongs to or the superior authority; if the circumstances are so serious as to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　农民购买、使用直接用于农业生产的生产资料，参照本法执行。

**Article 62** Matters pertaining to farmers' purchase or use of means of production directly used for agricultural production shall be governed by reference to this Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自1994年1月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1994.
