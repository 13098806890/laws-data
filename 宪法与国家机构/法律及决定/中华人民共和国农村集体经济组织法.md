### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护农村集体经济组织及其成员的合法权益，规范农村集体经济组织及其运行管理，促进新型农村集体经济高质量发展，巩固和完善农村基本经营制度和社会主义基本经济制度，推进乡村全面振兴，加快建设农业强国，促进共同富裕，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of rural collective economic organizations and their members, regulating rural collective economic organizations and their operation and management, promoting the high-quality development of the new-type rural collective economy, consolidating and improving the basic rural operation system and the basic socialist economic system, advancing comprehensive rural revitalization, accelerating the building of a strong agricultural country, and promoting common prosperity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农村集体经济组织，是指以土地集体所有为基础，依法代表成员集体行使所有权，实行家庭承包经营为基础、统分结合双层经营体制的区域性经济组织，包括乡镇级农村集体经济组织、村级农村集体经济组织、组级农村集体经济组织。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "rural collective economic organization" means a regional economic organization that is based on collective ownership of land, exercises ownership on behalf of its members collectively in accordance with the law, and implements the two-tier management system combining centralized and decentralized operations based on household contractual management, including township-level, village-level, and group-level rural collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　农村集体经济组织是发展壮大新型农村集体经济、巩固社会主义公有制、促进共同富裕的重要主体，是健全乡村治理体系、实现乡村善治的重要力量，是提升中国共产党农村基层组织凝聚力、巩固党在农村执政根基的重要保障。

**Article 3** Rural collective economic organizations are important entities for developing and strengthening the new-type rural collective economy, consolidating socialist public ownership, and promoting common prosperity; they are important forces for improving the rural governance system and achieving good governance in rural areas; and they are important safeguards for enhancing the cohesion of the Chinese Communist Party's rural grassroots organizations and consolidating the Party's governance foundation in rural areas.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农村集体经济组织应当坚持以下原则：  
（一）坚持中国共产党的领导，在乡镇党委、街道党工委和村党组织的领导下依法履职；  
（二）坚持社会主义集体所有制，维护集体及其成员的合法权益；  
（三）坚持民主管理，农村集体经济组织成员依照法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程平等享有权利、承担义务；  
（四）坚持按劳分配为主体、多种分配方式并存，促进农村共同富裕。

**Article 4** A rural collective economic organization shall adhere to the following principles:  
(1) upholding the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party, and performing its duties according to law under the leadership of the township Party committee, the sub-district Party working committee, and the village Party organization;  
(2) upholding the socialist collective ownership, and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of the collective and its members;  
(3) upholding democratic management, and members of the rural collective economic organization shall equally enjoy rights and assume obligations in accordance with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization;  
(4) upholding distribution according to work as the mainstay and allowing coexistence of multiple modes of distribution, to promote common prosperity in rural areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　农村集体经济组织依法代表成员集体行使所有权，履行下列职能：  
（一）发包农村土地；  
（二）办理农村宅基地申请、使用事项；  
（三）合理开发利用和保护耕地、林地、草地等土地资源并进行监督；  
（四）使用集体经营性建设用地或者通过出让、出租等方式交由单位、个人使用；  
（五）组织开展集体财产经营、管理；  
（六）决定集体出资的企业所有权变动；  
（七）分配、使用集体收益；  
（八）分配、使用集体土地被征收征用的土地补偿费等；  
（九）为成员的生产经营提供技术、信息等服务；  
（十）支持和配合村民委员会在村党组织领导下开展村民自治；  
（十一）支持农村其他经济组织、社会组织依法发挥作用；  
（十二）法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他职能。

**Article 5** A rural collective economic organization shall, in accordance with the law, exercise ownership on behalf of its members collectively and perform the following functions:  
(1) contracting out rural land;  
(2) handling matters concerning the application for and use of rural residential land;  
(3) rationally developing, utilizing, and protecting land resources such as arable land, forestland, and grassland, and supervising the same;  
(4) using collectively-operated construction land or making it available to entities or individuals through transfer, lease, or other means;  
(5) organizing the operation and management of collective property;  
(6) deciding on changes in the ownership of enterprises invested in with collective funds;  
(7) distributing and using collective proceeds;  
(8) distributing and using land compensation fees, etc. for expropriated or requisitioned collective land;  
(9) providing technical, information, and other services to members for their production and operation;  
(10) supporting and cooperating with the villagers' committee in carrying out villagers' self-governance under the leadership of the village Party organization;  
(11) supporting other rural economic organizations and social organizations to play their roles in accordance with the law;  
(12) other functions provided for by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　农村集体经济组织依照本法登记，取得特别法人资格，依法从事与其履行职能相适应的民事活动。  
农村集体经济组织不适用有关破产法律的规定。  
农村集体经济组织可以依法出资设立或者参与设立公司、农民专业合作社等市场主体，以其出资为限对其设立或者参与设立的市场主体的债务承担责任。

**Article 6** A rural collective economic organization shall, upon registration in accordance with this Law, acquire the status of a special legal person, and engage in civil activities corresponding to its functions in accordance with the law.  
The provisions on bankruptcy laws shall not apply to rural collective economic organizations.  
A rural collective economic organization may, in accordance with the law, invest to establish or participate in the establishment of market entities such as companies and specialized farmers' cooperatives, and shall bear liability for the debts of such market entities it establishes or participates in establishing to the extent of its capital contribution.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农村集体经济组织从事经营管理和服务活动，应当遵守法律法规，遵守社会公德、商业道德，诚实守信，承担社会责任。

**Article 7** When engaging in business management and service activities, a rural collective economic organization shall comply with laws and regulations, observe social ethics and business ethics, act in good faith, and assume social responsibilities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家保护农村集体经济组织及其成员的合法权益，任何组织和个人不得侵犯。  
农村集体经济组织成员集体所有的财产受法律保护，任何组织和个人不得侵占、挪用、截留、哄抢、私分、破坏。  
妇女享有与男子平等的权利，不得以妇女未婚、结婚、离婚、丧偶、户无男性等为由，侵害妇女在农村集体经济组织中的各项权益。

**Article 8** The State protects the lawful rights and interests of rural collective economic organizations and their members, and no organization or individual may infringe upon them.  
The property collectively owned by members of a rural collective economic organization shall be protected by law, and no organization or individual may embezzle, misappropriate, intercept, plunder, privately distribute, or damage such property.  
Women shall enjoy equal rights with men, and no woman's rights and interests in a rural collective economic organization shall be infringed upon on the grounds of her being unmarried, married, divorced, widowed, or having no male in the household, among others.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家通过财政、税收、金融、土地、人才以及产业政策等扶持措施，促进农村集体经济组织发展，壮大新型农村集体经济。  
国家鼓励和支持机关、企事业单位、社会团体等组织和个人为农村集体经济组织提供帮助和服务。  
对发展农村集体经济组织事业做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** The State adopts supportive measures such as fiscal, tax, financial, land, talent, and industrial policies to promote the development of rural collective economic organizations and strengthen the new-type rural collective economy.  
The State encourages and supports organizations, including state organs, enterprises, institutions, and social groups, as well as individuals, to provide assistance and services to rural collective economic organizations.  
Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of rural collective economic organizations shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with State regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责指导全国农村集体经济组织的建设和发展。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域内农村集体经济组织的登记管理、运行监督指导以及承包地、宅基地等集体财产管理和产权流转交易等的监督指导。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的工作。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处负责本行政区域内农村集体经济组织的监督管理等。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当会同有关部门加强对农村集体经济组织工作的综合协调，指导、协调、扶持、推动农村集体经济组织的建设和发展。  
地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当采取措施，建立健全集体财产监督管理服务体系，加强基层队伍建设，配备与集体财产监督管理工作相适应的专业人员。

**Article 10** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for guiding the construction and development of rural collective economic organizations nationwide. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for related work within their respective functions.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the registration management, operation supervision and guidance, and supervision and guidance of collective property management and property rights circulation transactions such as contracted land and residential land within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for related work within their respective functions.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall be responsible for the supervision and management of rural collective economic organizations within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, strengthen the comprehensive coordination of work concerning rural collective economic organizations, and guide, coordinate, support, and promote the construction and development of rural collective economic organizations.  
Local people's governments at all levels and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to establish and improve the collective property supervision and management service system, strengthen the building of grassroots teams, and equip them with professional personnel suitable for collective property supervision and management work.

### 第二章　成员

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　户籍在或者曾经在农村集体经济组织并与农村集体经济组织形成稳定的权利义务关系，以农村集体经济组织成员集体所有的土地等财产为基本生活保障的居民，为农村集体经济组织成员。

**Article 11** Residents whose household registration is in or was in a rural collective economic organization and who have formed a stable relationship of rights and obligations with the rural collective economic organization, and who take the land and other property collectively owned by members of the rural collective economic organization as their basic living guarantee, shall be members of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农村集体经济组织通过成员大会，依据前条规定确认农村集体经济组织成员。  
对因成员生育而增加的人员，农村集体经济组织应当确认为农村集体经济组织成员。对因成员结婚、收养或者因政策性移民而增加的人员，农村集体经济组织一般应当确认为农村集体经济组织成员。  
确认农村集体经济组织成员，不得违反本法和其他法律法规的规定。  
农村集体经济组织应当制作或者变更成员名册。成员名册应当报乡镇人民政府、街道办事处和县级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以根据本法，结合本行政区域实际情况，对农村集体经济组织的成员确认作出具体规定。

**Article 12** A rural collective economic organization shall confirm its members through the members' assembly in accordance with the provisions of the preceding article.  
A rural collective economic organization shall confirm persons added due to the childbirth of a member as members of the rural collective economic organization. Persons added due to the marriage or adoption of a member or due to policy-induced resettlement shall generally be confirmed as members of the rural collective economic organization.  
The confirmation of members of a rural collective economic organization shall not violate the provisions of this Law or other laws and regulations.  
A rural collective economic organization shall prepare or update a roster of members. The roster of members shall be filed with the township people's government, sub-district office, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level for recordation.  
The people's congresses and their standing committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and in light of the actual circumstances of their respective administrative regions, make specific provisions on the confirmation of members of rural collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农村集体经济组织成员享有下列权利：  
（一）依照法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程选举和被选举为成员代表、理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事；  
（二）依照法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程参加成员大会、成员代表大会，参与表决决定农村集体经济组织重大事项和重要事务；  
（三）查阅、复制农村集体经济组织财务会计报告、会议记录等资料，了解有关情况；  
（四）监督农村集体经济组织的生产经营管理活动和集体收益的分配、使用，并提出意见和建议；  
（五）依法承包农村集体经济组织发包的农村土地；  
（六）依法申请取得宅基地使用权；  
（七）参与分配集体收益；  
（八）集体土地被征收征用时参与分配土地补偿费等；  
（九）享受农村集体经济组织提供的服务和福利；  
（十）法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他权利。

**Article 13** Members of a rural collective economic organization shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to elect and be elected as a representative of members, a member of the council, a member of the supervisory board, or a supervisor in accordance with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization;  
(2) to participate in the members' assembly or the members' representative assembly in accordance with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, and to participate in voting on major matters and important affairs of the rural collective economic organization;  
(3) to consult and copy financial accounting reports, minutes of meetings, and other materials of the rural collective economic organization, and to learn about relevant information;  
(4) to supervise the production, operation, management activities of the rural collective economic organization and the distribution and use of collective proceeds, and to put forward opinions and suggestions;  
(5) to contract rural land contracted out by the rural collective economic organization in accordance with the law;  
(6) to apply for and obtain the right to use residential land in accordance with the law;  
(7) to participate in the distribution of collective proceeds;  
(8) to participate in the distribution of land compensation fees, etc. when collective land is expropriated or requisitioned;  
(9) to enjoy services and welfare provided by the rural collective economic organization;  
(10) other rights provided for by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农村集体经济组织成员履行下列义务：  
（一）遵守法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程；  
（二）执行农村集体经济组织依照法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程作出的决定；  
（三）维护农村集体经济组织合法权益；  
（四）合理利用和保护集体土地等资源；  
（五）参与、支持农村集体经济组织的生产经营管理活动和公益活动；  
（六）法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他义务。

**Article 14** Members of a rural collective economic organization shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to comply with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization;  
(2) to implement decisions made by the rural collective economic organization in accordance with laws, regulations, and its articles of association;  
(3) to safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the rural collective economic organization;  
(4) to rationally utilize and protect collective land and other resources;  
(5) to participate in and support the production, operation, management activities and public welfare activities of the rural collective economic organization;  
(6) other obligations provided for by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　非农村集体经济组织成员长期在农村集体经济组织工作，对集体做出贡献的，经农村集体经济组织成员大会全体成员四分之三以上同意，可以享有[本法第十三条](#art-13)第七项、第九项、第十项规定的权利。

**Article 15** A person who is not a member of a rural collective economic organization but has worked in the organization for a long time and has made contributions to the collective may, with the consent of three-fourths or more of all members of the members' assembly of the rural collective economic organization, enjoy the rights provided for in items (7), (9), and (10) of Article 13 of this Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农村集体经济组织成员提出书面申请并经农村集体经济组织同意的，可以自愿退出农村集体经济组织。  
农村集体经济组织成员自愿退出的，可以与农村集体经济组织协商获得适当补偿或者在一定期限内保留其已经享有的财产权益，但是不得要求分割集体财产。

**Article 16** A member of a rural collective economic organization may voluntarily withdraw from the organization by submitting a written application and obtaining the consent of the rural collective economic organization.  
A member who voluntarily withdraws from a rural collective economic organization may negotiate with the organization to obtain appropriate compensation or retain the property rights and interests already enjoyed within a certain period, but may not demand the division of collective property.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有下列情形之一的，丧失农村集体经济组织成员身份：  
（一）死亡；  
（二）丧失中华人民共和国国籍；  
（三）已经取得其他农村集体经济组织成员身份；  
（四）已经成为公务员，但是聘任制公务员除外；  
（五）法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他情形。  
因前款第三项、第四项情形而丧失农村集体经济组织成员身份的，依照法律法规、国家有关规定和农村集体经济组织章程，经与农村集体经济组织协商，可以在一定期限内保留其已经享有的相关权益。

**Article 17** A person shall lose the status of a member of a rural collective economic organization under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) death;  
(2) loss of nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) having already acquired the status of a member of another rural collective economic organization;  
(4) having become a public servant, except for public servants employed under a contract system;  
(5) other circumstances provided for by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.  
A person who loses the status of a member of a rural collective economic organization due to the circumstances specified in items (3) and (4) of the preceding paragraph may, in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant State provisions, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, and after negotiation with the rural collective economic organization, retain the relevant rights and interests already enjoyed within a certain period.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农村集体经济组织成员不因就学、服役、务工、经商、离婚、丧偶、服刑等原因而丧失农村集体经济组织成员身份。  
农村集体经济组织成员结婚，未取得其他农村集体经济组织成员身份的，原农村集体经济组织不得取消其成员身份。

**Article 18** A member of a rural collective economic organization shall not lose his or her membership status due to reasons such as schooling, military service, employment, business operation, divorce, bereavement, or imprisonment.  
Where a member of a rural collective economic organization gets married and has not acquired membership in another rural collective economic organization, the original rural collective economic organization shall not cancel his or her membership status.

### 第三章　组织登记

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农村集体经济组织应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合本法规定的成员；  
（二）有符合本法规定的集体财产；  
（三）有符合本法规定的农村集体经济组织章程；  
（四）有符合本法规定的名称和住所；  
（五）有符合本法规定的组织机构。  
符合前款规定条件的村一般应当设立农村集体经济组织，村民小组可以根据情况设立农村集体经济组织；乡镇确有需要的，可以设立农村集体经济组织。  
设立农村集体经济组织不得改变集体土地所有权。

**Article 19** A rural collective economic organization shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having members that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(2) having collective property that complies with the provisions of this Law;  
(3) having articles of association that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) having a name and domicile that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(5) having an organizational structure that complies with the provisions of this Law.  
A village that meets the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph shall generally establish a rural collective economic organization; a villagers' group may establish a rural collective economic organization based on the circumstances; where a township or town has actual needs, it may establish a rural collective economic organization.  
The establishment of a rural collective economic organization shall not change the collective ownership of land.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农村集体经济组织章程应当载明下列事项：  
（一）农村集体经济组织的名称、法定代表人、住所和财产范围；  
（二）农村集体经济组织成员确认规则和程序；  
（三）农村集体经济组织的机构；  
（四）集体财产经营和财务管理；  
（五）集体经营性财产收益权的量化与分配；  
（六）农村集体经济组织的变更和注销；  
（七）需要载明的其他事项。  
农村集体经济组织章程应当报乡镇人民政府、街道办事处和县级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案。  
国务院农业农村主管部门根据本法和其他有关法律法规制定农村集体经济组织示范章程。

**Article 20** The articles of association of a rural collective economic organization shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the name, legal representative, domicile, and scope of property of the rural collective economic organization;  
(2) the rules and procedures for confirming members of the rural collective economic organization;  
(3) the institutions of the rural collective economic organization;  
(4) the operation and financial management of collective property;  
(5) the quantification and distribution of income rights of collective operating property;  
(6) the alteration and dissolution of the rural collective economic organization;  
(7) other matters that need to be specified.  
The articles of association of a rural collective economic organization shall be filed with the township people's government, sub-district office, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level for recordation.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall formulate model articles of association for rural collective economic organizations in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农村集体经济组织的名称中应当标明“集体经济组织”字样，以及所在县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇、村或者组的名称。  
农村集体经济组织以其主要办事机构所在地为住所。

**Article 21** The name of a rural collective economic organization shall include the words "collective economic organization" and the name of the county, city not divided into districts, municipal district, township, ethnic township, town, village, or group where it is located.  
The domicile of a rural collective economic organization shall be the place where its main office is located.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　农村集体经济组织成员大会表决通过本农村集体经济组织章程、确认本农村集体经济组织成员、选举本农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事后，应当及时向县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门申请登记，取得农村集体经济组织登记证书。  
农村集体经济组织登记办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 22** After the members' assembly of a rural collective economic organization has voted to adopt the articles of association, confirm the members, and elect the members of the council and the members of the supervisory board or supervisors of the organization, the organization shall promptly apply to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level for registration and obtain the registration certificate of the rural collective economic organization.  
The measures for the registration of rural collective economic organizations shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农村集体经济组织合并的，应当在清产核资的基础上编制资产负债表和财产清单。  
农村集体经济组织合并的，应当由各自的成员大会形成决定，经乡镇人民政府、街道办事处审核后，报县级以上地方人民政府批准。  
农村集体经济组织应当在获得批准合并之日起十日内通知债权人，债权人可以要求农村集体经济组织清偿债务或者提供相应担保。  
合并各方的债权债务由合并后的农村集体经济组织承继。

**Article 23** Where rural collective economic organizations merge, they shall, on the basis of asset verification, prepare a balance sheet and an inventory of property.  
Where rural collective economic organizations merge, the decision shall be made by their respective members' assemblies, submitted to the township people's government or sub-district office for review, and then reported to the local people's government at or above the county level for approval.  
The rural collective economic organization shall, within ten days from the date of obtaining approval for the merger, notify its creditors, who may demand that the rural collective economic organization discharge the debts or provide corresponding guarantees.  
The claims and debts of all parties to the merger shall be assumed by the rural collective economic organization after the merger.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　农村集体经济组织分立的，应当在清产核资的基础上分配财产、分解债权债务。  
农村集体经济组织分立的，应当由成员大会形成决定，经乡镇人民政府、街道办事处审核后，报县级以上地方人民政府批准。  
农村集体经济组织应当在获得批准分立之日起十日内通知债权人。  
农村集体经济组织分立前的债权债务，由分立后的农村集体经济组织享有连带债权，承担连带债务，但是农村集体经济组织分立时已经与债权人或者债务人达成清偿债务的书面协议的，从其约定。

**Article 24** Where a rural collective economic organization splits, it shall, on the basis of asset verification, distribute property and split claims and debts.  
Where a rural collective economic organization splits, the decision shall be made by the members' assembly, submitted to the township people's government or sub-district office for review, and then reported to the local people's government at or above the county level for approval.  
The rural collective economic organization shall, within ten days from the date of obtaining approval for the split, notify its creditors.  
The claims and debts of the rural collective economic organization before the split shall be enjoyed jointly and assumed jointly by the rural collective economic organizations after the split, except that if the rural collective economic organization has reached a written agreement with the creditor or debtor on the discharge of debts at the time of the split, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农村集体经济组织合并、分立或者登记事项变动的，应当办理变更登记。  
农村集体经济组织因合并、分立等原因需要解散的，依法办理注销登记后终止。

**Article 25** Where a rural collective economic organization merges, splits, or undergoes changes in registered items, it shall undergo registration of changes.  
Where a rural collective economic organization needs to be dissolved due to merger, split, or other reasons, it shall terminate after undergoing cancellation registration in accordance with the law.

### 第四章　组织机构

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农村集体经济组织成员大会由具有完全民事行为能力的全体成员组成，是本农村集体经济组织的权力机构，依法行使下列职权：  
（一）制定、修改农村集体经济组织章程；  
（二）制定、修改农村集体经济组织内部管理制度；  
（三）确认农村集体经济组织成员；  
（四）选举、罢免农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事；  
（五）审议农村集体经济组织理事会、监事会或者监事的工作报告；  
（六）决定农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事的报酬及主要经营管理人员的聘任、解聘和报酬；  
（七）批准农村集体经济组织的集体经济发展规划、业务经营计划、年度财务预决算、收益分配方案；  
（八）对农村土地承包、宅基地使用和集体经营性财产收益权份额量化方案等事项作出决定；  
（九）对集体经营性建设用地使用、出让、出租方案等事项作出决定；  
（十）决定土地补偿费等的分配、使用办法；  
（十一）决定投资等重大事项；  
（十二）决定农村集体经济组织合并、分立等重大事项；  
（十三）法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他职权。  
需由成员大会审议决定的重要事项，应当先经乡镇党委、街道党工委或者村党组织研究讨论。

**Article 26** The members' assembly of a rural collective economic organization shall be composed of all members with full capacity for civil conduct, and shall be the authority of the rural collective economic organization, exercising the following functions and powers in accordance with the law:  
(1) formulating and amending the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization;  
(2) formulating and amending the internal management system of the rural collective economic organization;  
(3) confirming members of the rural collective economic organization;  
(4) electing and removing members of the council and members of the supervisory board or supervisors of the rural collective economic organization;  
(5) reviewing the work reports of the council, the supervisory board, or supervisors of the rural collective economic organization;  
(6) deciding on the remuneration of members of the council and members of the supervisory board or supervisors of the rural collective economic organization, and the appointment, dismissal, and remuneration of key management personnel;  
(7) approving the collective economic development plan, business operation plan, annual financial budget and final accounts, and income distribution plan of the rural collective economic organization;  
(8) making decisions on matters such as rural land contracting, the use of residential land, and the plan for quantifying income rights shares of collective operating property;  
(9) making decisions on matters such as the use, transfer, and lease of collective operating construction land;  
(10) deciding on the distribution and use methods of land compensation fees, etc.;  
(11) deciding on major matters such as investment;  
(12) deciding on major matters such as the merger and split of the rural collective economic organization;  
(13) other functions and powers provided for by laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.  
Important matters that need to be deliberated and decided by the members' assembly shall first be studied and discussed by the township Party committee, the sub-district Party working committee, or the village Party organization.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农村集体经济组织召开成员大会，应当将会议召开的时间、地点和审议的事项于会议召开十日前通知全体成员，有三分之二以上具有完全民事行为能力的成员参加。成员无法在现场参加会议的，可以通过即时通讯工具在线参加会议，或者书面委托本农村集体经济组织同一户内具有完全民事行为能力的其他家庭成员代为参加会议。  
成员大会每年至少召开一次，并由理事会召集，由理事长、副理事长或者理事长指定的成员主持。  
成员大会实行一人一票的表决方式。成员大会作出决定，应当经本农村集体经济组织成员大会全体成员三分之二以上同意，本法或者其他法律法规、农村集体经济组织章程有更严格规定的，从其规定。

**Article 27** When convening the members' assembly, a rural collective economic organization shall notify all members of the time, place, and matters to be deliberated no less than ten days before the meeting, and the meeting shall be attended by two-thirds or more of the members with full capacity for civil conduct. Members who are unable to attend the meeting in person may attend the meeting online through instant communication tools, or entrust in writing another family member with full capacity for civil conduct within the same household of the rural collective economic organization to attend the meeting on their behalf.  
The members' assembly shall be convened at least once a year, and shall be convened by the council and presided over by the chairperson, vice chairperson, or a member designated by the chairperson.  
The members' assembly shall adopt a one-person-one-vote voting method. Decisions made by the members' assembly shall be approved by two-thirds or more of all members of the members' assembly of the rural collective economic organization; where this Law, other laws or regulations, or the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization have stricter provisions, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　农村集体经济组织成员较多的，可以按照农村集体经济组织章程规定设立成员代表大会。  
设立成员代表大会的，一般每五户至十五户选举代表一人，代表人数应当多于二十人，并且有适当数量的妇女代表。  
成员代表的任期为五年，可以连选连任。  
成员代表大会按照农村集体经济组织章程规定行使[本法第二十六条](#art-26)第一款规定的成员大会部分职权，但是第一项、第三项、第八项、第十项、第十二项规定的职权除外。  
成员代表大会实行一人一票的表决方式。成员代表大会作出决定，应当经全体成员代表三分之二以上同意。

**Article 28** Where a rural collective economic organization has a relatively large number of members, it may establish a members' representative assembly in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association.  
Where a members' representative assembly is established, generally one representative shall be elected for every five to fifteen households, the number of representatives shall be more than twenty, and there shall be an appropriate number of women representatives.  
The term of office of representatives of members shall be five years, and they may be re-elected.  
The members' representative assembly shall, in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, exercise part of the functions and powers of the members' assembly as specified in the first paragraph of Article 26 of this Law, except for those specified in items (1), (3), (8), (10), and (12).  
The members' representative assembly shall adopt a one-person-one-vote voting method. Decisions made by the members' representative assembly shall be approved by two-thirds or more of all representatives of members.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农村集体经济组织设理事会，一般由三至七名单数成员组成。理事会设理事长一名，可以设副理事长。理事长、副理事长、理事的产生办法由农村集体经济组织章程规定。理事会成员之间应当实行近亲属回避。理事会成员的任期为五年，可以连选连任。  
理事长是农村集体经济组织的法定代表人。  
乡镇党委、街道党工委或者村党组织可以提名推荐农村集体经济组织理事会成员候选人，党组织负责人可以通过法定程序担任农村集体经济组织理事长。

**Article 29** A rural collective economic organization shall have a council, generally composed of three to seven members in an odd number. The council shall have one chairperson, and may have vice chairpersons. The methods for the election of the chairperson, vice chairpersons, and members of the council shall be provided for in the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization. Close relatives shall be avoided among members of the council. The term of office of members of the council shall be five years, and they may be re-elected.  
The chairperson of the council shall be the legal representative of the rural collective economic organization.  
The township Party committee, the sub-district Party working committee, or the village Party organization may nominate and recommend candidates for members of the council of the rural collective economic organization, and the person in charge of the Party organization may, through statutory procedures, serve as the chairperson of the council of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　理事会对成员大会、成员代表大会负责，行使下列职权：  
（一）召集、主持成员大会、成员代表大会，并向其报告工作；  
（二）执行成员大会、成员代表大会的决定；  
（三）起草农村集体经济组织章程修改草案；  
（四）起草集体经济发展规划、业务经营计划、内部管理制度等；  
（五）起草农村土地承包、宅基地使用、集体经营性财产收益权份额量化，以及集体经营性建设用地使用、出让或者出租等方案；  
（六）起草投资方案；  
（七）起草年度财务预决算、收益分配方案等；  
（八）提出聘任、解聘主要经营管理人员及决定其报酬的建议；  
（九）依照法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程管理集体财产和财务，保障集体财产安全；  
（十）代表农村集体经济组织签订承包、出租、入股等合同，监督、督促承包方、承租方、被投资方等履行合同；  
（十一）接受、处理有关质询、建议并作出答复；  
（十二）农村集体经济组织章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 30** The council shall be responsible to the members' assembly and the members' representative assembly, and shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) convening and presiding over meetings of the members' assembly and the members' representative assembly, and reporting work to them;  
(2) implementing the decisions of the members' assembly and the members' representative assembly;  
(3) drafting amendments to the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization;  
(4) drafting the collective economic development plan, business operation plan, internal management system, etc.;  
(5) drafting plans for rural land contracting, residential land use, quantification of income rights shares of collective operating property, and the use, transfer, or lease of collective operating construction land;  
(6) drafting investment plans;  
(7) drafting annual financial budgets and final accounts, income distribution plans, etc.;  
(8) proposing the appointment or dismissal of key management personnel and deciding on their remuneration;  
(9) managing collective property and finances in accordance with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, and ensuring the safety of collective property;  
(10) signing contracts such as contracting, leasing, and equity investment contracts on behalf of the rural collective economic organization, and supervising and urging the contractor, lessee, investee, etc. to perform the contracts;  
(11) accepting and handling inquiries and suggestions, and making responses;  
(12) other functions and powers provided for in the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　理事会会议应当有三分之二以上的理事会成员出席。  
理事会实行一人一票的表决方式。理事会作出决定，应当经全体理事的过半数同意。  
理事会的议事方式和表决程序由农村集体经济组织章程具体规定。

**Article 31** Meetings of the council shall be attended by two-thirds or more of the members of the council.  
The council shall adopt a one-person-one-vote voting method. Decisions made by the council shall be approved by more than half of all members of the council.  
The deliberation methods and voting procedures of the council shall be specifically provided for in the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　农村集体经济组织设监事会，成员较少的可以设一至二名监事，行使监督理事会执行成员大会和成员代表大会决定、监督检查集体财产经营管理情况、审核监督本农村集体经济组织财务状况等内部监督职权。必要时，监事会或者监事可以组织对本农村集体经济组织的财务进行内部审计，审计结果应当向成员大会、成员代表大会报告。  
监事会或者监事的产生办法、具体职权、议事方式和表决程序等，由农村集体经济组织章程规定。

**Article 32** A rural collective economic organization shall have a supervisory board, or if the number of members is relatively small, may have one or two supervisors, exercising internal supervision functions such as supervising the council's implementation of decisions of the members' assembly and the members' representative assembly, supervising and inspecting the operation and management of collective property, and reviewing and supervising the financial status of the rural collective economic organization. When necessary, the supervisory board or the supervisors may organize an internal audit of the finances of the rural collective economic organization, and the audit results shall be reported to the members' assembly and the members' representative assembly.  
The methods for the election, specific functions and powers, deliberation methods, and voting procedures of the supervisory board or the supervisors shall be provided for in the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　农村集体经济组织成员大会、成员代表大会、理事会、监事会或者监事召开会议，应当按照规定制作、保存会议记录。

**Article 33** When the members' assembly, the members' representative assembly, the council, the supervisory board, or the supervisors of a rural collective economic organization hold meetings, they shall prepare and keep minutes of the meetings in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事与村党组织领导班子成员、村民委员会成员可以根据情况交叉任职。  
农村集体经济组织理事会成员、财务人员、会计人员及其近亲属不得担任监事会成员或者监事。

**Article 34** Members of the council, members of the supervisory board, or supervisors of a rural collective economic organization may, based on circumstances, hold concurrent positions with members of the leading bodies of the village Party organization and members of the villagers' committee.  
Members of the council, financial personnel, accounting personnel, and their close relatives of a rural collective economic organization shall not serve as members of the supervisory board or supervisors.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事应当遵守法律法规和农村集体经济组织章程，履行诚实信用、勤勉谨慎的义务，为农村集体经济组织及其成员的利益管理集体财产，处理农村集体经济组织事务。  
农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事、主要经营管理人员不得有下列行为：  
（一）侵占、挪用、截留、哄抢、私分、破坏集体财产；  
（二）直接或者间接向农村集体经济组织借款；  
（三）以集体财产为本人或者他人债务提供担保；  
（四）违反法律法规或者国家有关规定为地方政府举借债务；  
（五）以农村集体经济组织名义开展非法集资等非法金融活动；  
（六）将集体财产低价折股、转让、租赁；  
（七）以集体财产加入合伙企业成为普通合伙人；  
（八）接受他人与农村集体经济组织交易的佣金归为己有；  
（九）泄露农村集体经济组织的商业秘密；  
（十）其他损害农村集体经济组织合法权益的行为。

**Article 35** Members of the council, members of the supervisory board, or supervisors of a rural collective economic organization shall comply with laws, regulations, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, perform the obligations of good faith, diligence, and prudence, manage collective property, and handle affairs of the rural collective economic organization for the benefit of the rural collective economic organization and its members.  
Members of the council, members of the supervisory board, or supervisors, and key management personnel of a rural collective economic organization shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) embezzling, misappropriating, intercepting, plundering, privately distributing, or damaging collective property;  
(2) borrowing money directly or indirectly from the rural collective economic organization;  
(3) providing a guarantee with collective property for debts of themselves or others;  
(4) contracting debts for local governments in violation of laws, regulations, or relevant State provisions;  
(5) conducting illegal financial activities such as illegal fundraising in the name of the rural collective economic organization;  
(6) converting collective property into shares, transferring, or leasing it at a low price;  
(7) using collective property to join a partnership enterprise as a general partner;  
(8) accepting commissions from others for transactions with the rural collective economic organization and keeping them for themselves;  
(9) disclosing trade secrets of the rural collective economic organization;  
(10) other acts that harm the lawful rights and interests of the rural collective economic organization.

### 第五章　财产经营管理和收益分配

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　集体财产主要包括：  
（一）集体所有的土地和森林、山岭、草原、荒地、滩涂；  
（二）集体所有的建筑物、生产设施、农田水利设施；  
（三）集体所有的教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育、交通等设施和农村人居环境基础设施；  
（四）集体所有的资金；  
（五）集体投资兴办的企业和集体持有的其他经济组织的股权及其他投资性权利；  
（六）集体所有的无形资产；  
（七）集体所有的接受国家扶持、社会捐赠、减免税费等形成的财产；  
（八）集体所有的其他财产。  
集体财产依法由农村集体经济组织成员集体所有，由农村集体经济组织依法代表成员集体行使所有权，不得分割到成员个人。

**Article 36** Collective property mainly includes:  
(1) land, forests, mountains, grasslands, wasteland, and tidal flats that are collectively owned;  
(2) buildings, production facilities, and farmland water conservancy facilities that are collectively owned;  
(3) facilities for education, science and technology, culture, health, sports, transportation, etc., and rural living environment infrastructure that are collectively owned;  
(4) funds that are collectively owned;  
(5) enterprises invested and established with collective funds, and equity interests and other investment rights held by the collective in other economic organizations;  
(6) intangible assets that are collectively owned;  
(7) property formed from State support, social donations, tax and fee reductions or exemptions, etc., that is collectively owned;  
(8) other property that is collectively owned.  
Collective property shall be collectively owned by the members of the rural collective economic organization in accordance with the law, and the rural collective economic organization shall, in accordance with the law, exercise ownership on behalf of its members collectively; it shall not be divided among individual members.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　集体所有和国家所有依法由农民集体使用的耕地、林地、草地以及其他依法用于农业的土地，依照农村土地承包的法律实行承包经营。  
集体所有的宅基地等建设用地，依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定取得、使用、管理。  
集体所有的建筑物、生产设施、农田水利设施，由农村集体经济组织按照国家有关规定和农村集体经济组织章程使用、管理。  
集体所有的教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育、交通等设施和农村人居环境基础设施，依照法律法规、国家有关规定和农村集体经济组织章程使用、管理。

**Article 37** Arable land, forestland, grassland, and other land used for agriculture that are collectively owned or owned by the State but used by peasant collectives according to law shall be operated under contract in accordance with the laws on rural land contracting.  
Collectively-owned construction land such as residential land shall be obtained, used, and managed in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant State provisions.  
Collectively-owned buildings, production facilities, and farmland water conservancy facilities shall be used and managed by the rural collective economic organization in accordance with relevant State provisions and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.  
Collectively-owned facilities for education, science and technology, culture, health, sports, transportation, etc., and rural living environment infrastructure shall be used and managed in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant State provisions, and the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　依法应当实行家庭承包的耕地、林地、草地以外的其他农村土地，农村集体经济组织可以直接组织经营或者依法实行承包经营，也可以依法采取土地经营权出租、入股等方式经营。

**Article 38** For rural land other than arable land, forestland, and grassland that should be subject to household contractual management according to law, a rural collective economic organization may directly organize the operation, or implement contractual operation in accordance with the law, or may adopt methods such as leasing or contributing the land operation rights as shares for operation in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对符合国家规定的集体经营性建设用地，农村集体经济组织应当优先用于保障乡村产业发展和乡村建设，也可以依法通过出让、出租等方式交由单位或者个人有偿使用。

**Article 39** For collective operating construction land that complies with State regulations, a rural collective economic organization shall give priority to using it for ensuring the development of rural industries and rural construction, and may also, in accordance with the law, make it available to entities or individuals for compensated use through transfer, lease, or other means.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　农村集体经济组织可以将集体所有的经营性财产的收益权以份额形式量化到本农村集体经济组织成员，作为其参与集体收益分配的基本依据。  
集体所有的经营性财产包括[本法第三十六条](#art-36)第一款第一项中可以依法入市、流转的财产用益物权和第二项、第四项至第七项的财产。  
国务院农业农村主管部门可以根据本法制定集体经营性财产收益权量化的具体办法。

**Article 40** A rural collective economic organization may quantify the income rights of collectively-owned operating property to its members in the form of shares, which shall serve as the basic basis for their participation in the distribution of collective proceeds.  
Collectively-owned operating property includes the usufruct of property that may be listed on the market and circulated in accordance with the law under item (1) of the first paragraph of Article 36 of this Law, and the property under items (2) and (4) to (7).  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council may, in accordance with this Law, formulate specific measures for quantifying the income rights of collective operating property.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　农村集体经济组织可以探索通过资源发包、物业出租、居间服务、经营性财产参股等多样化途径发展新型农村集体经济。

**Article 41** A rural collective economic organization may explore the development of the new-type rural collective economy through diverse channels such as resource contracting, property leasing, intermediary services, and equity participation with operating property.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　农村集体经济组织当年收益应当按照农村集体经济组织章程规定提取公积公益金，用于弥补亏损、扩大生产经营等，剩余的可分配收益按照量化给农村集体经济组织成员的集体经营性财产收益权份额进行分配。

**Article 42** The current year's proceeds of a rural collective economic organization shall, in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association, withdraw public reserve funds and public welfare funds to cover losses, expand production and operation, etc., and the remaining distributable proceeds shall be distributed according to the shares of income rights of collective operating property quantified to the members of the rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　农村集体经济组织应当加强集体财产管理，建立集体财产清查、保管、使用、处置、公开等制度，促进集体财产保值增值。  
省、自治区、直辖市可以根据实际情况，制定本行政区域农村集体财产管理具体办法，实现集体财产管理制度化、规范化和信息化。

**Article 43** A rural collective economic organization shall strengthen the management of collective property, and establish systems for checking, keeping, using, disposing of, and making public collective property, so as to promote the preservation and increase of the value of collective property.  
Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual circumstances, formulate specific measures for the management of rural collective property in their respective administrative regions, so as to achieve institutionalized, standardized, and information-based management of collective property.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　农村集体经济组织应当按照国务院有关部门制定的农村集体经济组织财务会计制度进行财务管理和会计核算。  
农村集体经济组织应当根据会计业务的需要，设置会计机构，或者设置会计人员并指定会计主管人员，也可以按照规定委托代理记账。  
集体所有的资金不得存入以个人名义开立的账户。

**Article 44** A rural collective economic organization shall conduct financial management and accounting in accordance with the financial accounting system for rural collective economic organizations formulated by the relevant department under the State Council.  
A rural collective economic organization shall, based on the needs of its accounting business, set up an accounting institution, or appoint accounting personnel and designate a person in charge of accounting, or may entrust agency bookkeeping in accordance with regulations.  
Collectively-owned funds shall not be deposited into accounts opened in the name of an individual.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　农村集体经济组织应当定期将财务情况向农村集体经济组织成员公布。集体财产使用管理情况、涉及农村集体经济组织及其成员利益的重大事项应当及时公布。农村集体经济组织理事会应当保证所公布事项的真实性。

**Article 45** A rural collective economic organization shall regularly disclose its financial situation to the members of the rural collective economic organization. The use and management of collective property, and major matters involving the interests of the rural collective economic organization and its members, shall be disclosed in a timely manner. The council of the rural collective economic organization shall ensure the authenticity of the disclosed matters.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　农村集体经济组织应当编制年度经营报告、年度财务会计报告和收益分配方案，并于成员大会、成员代表大会召开十日前，提供给农村集体经济组织成员查阅。

**Article 46** A rural collective economic organization shall prepare an annual operation report, an annual financial accounting report, and an income distribution plan, and shall make them available to the members of the rural collective economic organization for inspection no less than ten days before the convening of the members' assembly or the members' representative assembly.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　农村集体经济组织应当依法接受审计监督。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处根据情况对农村集体经济组织开展定期审计、专项审计。审计办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
审计机关依法对农村集体经济组织接受、运用财政资金的真实、合法和效益情况进行审计监督。

**Article 47** A rural collective economic organization shall accept audit supervision in accordance with the law.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level, and township people's governments and sub-district offices, shall, based on the circumstances, conduct regular audits and special audits of rural collective economic organizations. The audit measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council.  
Audit institutions shall, in accordance with the law, conduct audit supervision over the truthfulness, legality, and efficiency of rural collective economic organizations' receipt and use of fiscal funds.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　农村集体经济组织应当自觉接受有关机关和组织对集体财产使用管理情况的监督。

**Article 48** A rural collective economic organization shall voluntarily accept supervision by relevant organs and organizations over the use and management of collective property.

### 第六章　扶持措施

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府应当合理安排资金，支持农村集体经济组织发展新型农村集体经济、服务集体成员。  
各级财政支持的农业发展和农村建设项目，依法将适宜的项目优先交由符合条件的农村集体经济组织承担。国家对欠发达地区和革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区的农村集体经济组织给予优先扶助。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当依法加强对财政补助资金使用情况的监督。

**Article 49** People's governments at or above the county level shall reasonably arrange funds to support rural collective economic organizations in developing the new-type rural collective economy and serving their collective members.  
For agricultural development and rural construction projects supported by fiscal funds at all levels, suitable projects shall, in accordance with the law, be preferentially assigned to qualified rural collective economic organizations. The State gives priority support to rural collective economic organizations in underdeveloped regions, old revolutionary base areas, ethnic areas, and border areas.  
The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the law, strengthen supervision over the use of fiscal subsidy funds.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　农村集体经济组织依法履行纳税义务，依法享受税收优惠。  
农村集体经济组织开展生产经营管理活动或者因开展农村集体产权制度改革办理土地、房屋权属变更，按照国家规定享受税收优惠。

**Article 50** A rural collective economic organization shall perform its tax obligations in accordance with the law and enjoy tax preferences in accordance with the law.  
Where a rural collective economic organization conducts production, operation, and management activities, or undergoes changes in land or house property rights due to the reform of the rural collective property rights system, it shall enjoy tax preferences in accordance with State regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　农村集体经济组织用于集体公益和综合服务、保障村级组织和村务运转等支出，按照国家规定计入相应成本。

**Article 51** Expenditures of rural collective economic organizations for collective public welfare and comprehensive services, and for ensuring the operation of village-level organizations and village affairs, shall be recorded as corresponding costs in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家鼓励政策性金融机构立足职能定位，在业务范围内采取多种形式对农村集体经济组织发展新型农村集体经济提供多渠道资金支持。  
国家鼓励商业性金融机构为农村集体经济组织及其成员提供多样化金融服务，优先支持符合条件的农村集体经济发展项目，支持农村集体经济组织开展集体经营性财产股权质押贷款；鼓励融资担保机构为农村集体经济组织提供融资担保服务；鼓励保险机构为农村集体经济组织提供保险服务。

**Article 52** The State shall encourage policy-based financial institutions to perform their functions, and within their business scope, provide multi-channel financial support through various forms to rural collective economic organizations for the development of a new type of rural collective economy.  
The State shall encourage commercial financial institutions to provide diversified financial services to rural collective economic organizations and their members, give priority to supporting qualified rural collective economic development projects, support rural collective economic organizations in conducting equity pledge loans on collective operational property; encourage financing guarantee institutions to provide financing guarantee services for rural collective economic organizations; encourage insurance institutions to provide insurance services for rural collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　乡镇人民政府编制村庄规划应当根据实际需要合理安排集体经济发展各项建设用地。  
土地整理新增耕地形成土地指标交易的收益，应当保障农村集体经济组织和相关权利人的合法权益。

**Article 53** The people's governments of townships and towns shall, when formulating village plans, rationally arrange various construction land for collective economic development according to actual needs.  
The proceeds from the trading of land quotas formed by newly increased arable land through land consolidation shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of rural collective economic organizations and relevant right holders.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级人民政府和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当加强农村集体经济组织经营管理队伍建设，制定农村集体经济组织人才培养计划，完善激励机制，支持和引导各类人才服务新型农村集体经济发展。

**Article 54** The people's governments at the county level, the people's governments of townships and towns, and sub-district offices shall strengthen the building of the management team of rural collective economic organizations, formulate talent training plans for rural collective economic organizations, improve incentive mechanisms, and support and guide various talents to serve the development of the new type of rural collective economy.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各级人民政府应当在用水、用电、用气以及网络、交通等公共设施和农村人居环境基础设施配置方面为农村集体经济组织建设发展提供支持。

**Article 55** People's governments at all levels shall provide support for the construction and development of rural collective economic organizations in terms of the allocation of public facilities such as water, electricity, gas, and network and transportation, as well as infrastructure for the rural living environment.

### 第七章　争议的解决和法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　对确认农村集体经济组织成员身份有异议，或者农村集体经济组织因内部管理、运行、收益分配等发生纠纷的，当事人可以请求乡镇人民政府、街道办事处或者县级人民政府农业农村主管部门调解解决；不愿调解或者调解不成的，可以向农村土地承包仲裁机构申请仲裁，也可以直接向人民法院提起诉讼。  
确认农村集体经济组织成员身份时侵害妇女合法权益，导致社会公共利益受损的，检察机关可以发出检察建议或者依法提起公益诉讼。

**Article 56** Where there is a dispute over the confirmation of the membership status of a rural collective economic organization, or a dispute arises from internal management, operation, income distribution, etc. of the rural collective economic organization, the parties may request the people's government of the township or town, the sub-district office, or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level to mediate for settlement; if mediation is not desired or mediation fails, they may apply for arbitration to the rural land contract arbitration institution, or may directly initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.  
Where the lawful rights and interests of women are infringed upon in the confirmation of the membership status of a rural collective economic organization, causing damage to the public interest, the procuratorial organ may issue a procuratorial suggestion or initiate a public interest litigation according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　农村集体经济组织成员大会、成员代表大会、理事会或者农村集体经济组织负责人作出的决定侵害农村集体经济组织成员合法权益的，受侵害的农村集体经济组织成员可以请求人民法院予以撤销。但是，农村集体经济组织按照该决定与善意相对人形成的民事法律关系不受影响。  
受侵害的农村集体经济组织成员自知道或者应当知道撤销事由之日起一年内或者自该决定作出之日起五年内未行使撤销权的，撤销权消灭。

**Article 57** Where a decision made by the general meeting of members, the meeting of member representatives, the council, or the person in charge of a rural collective economic organization infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the members of the rural collective economic organization, the infringed members of the rural collective economic organization may request the People's Court to revoke the decision. However, the civil legal relationship formed by the rural collective economic organization with a bona fide counterparty in accordance with that decision shall not be affected.  
If the infringed member of the rural collective economic organization fails to exercise the right of revocation within one year from the date when he knows or should have known the cause of revocation, or within five years from the date when the decision was made, the right of revocation shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事、主要经营管理人员有[本法第三十五条](#art-35)第二款规定行为的，由乡镇人民政府、街道办事处或者县级人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，依法给予处分或者行政处罚；造成集体财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
前款规定的人员违反本法规定，以集体财产为本人或者他人债务提供担保的，该担保无效。

**Article 58** Where a member of the council, a member of the supervisory board or a supervisor, or a chief executive officer of a rural collective economic organization commits the act specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 35 of this Law, the people's government of the township or town, the sub-district office, or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level shall order rectification within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction or administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law; if collective property losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a person specified in the preceding paragraph, in violation of this Law, provides a guarantee with collective property for his own debts or the debts of another, such guarantee shall be void.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对于侵害农村集体经济组织合法权益的行为，农村集体经济组织可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 59** For acts that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of a rural collective economic organization, the rural collective economic organization may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　农村集体经济组织理事会成员、监事会成员或者监事、主要经营管理人员执行职务时违反法律法规或者农村集体经济组织章程的规定，给农村集体经济组织造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
前款规定的人员有前款行为的，农村集体经济组织理事会、监事会或者监事应当向人民法院提起诉讼；未及时提起诉讼的，十名以上具有完全民事行为能力的农村集体经济组织成员可以书面请求监事会或者监事向人民法院提起诉讼。  
监事会或者监事收到书面请求后拒绝提起诉讼或者自收到请求之日起十五日内未提起诉讼的，前款规定的提出书面请求的农村集体经济组织成员可以为农村集体经济组织的利益，以自己的名义向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 60** Where a member of the council, a member of the supervisory board or a supervisor, or a chief executive officer of a rural collective economic organization, in performing his duties, violates the provisions of laws or regulations or the articles of association of the rural collective economic organization, thereby causing losses to the rural collective economic organization, he shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where a person specified in the preceding paragraph commits an act as described in the preceding paragraph, the council, supervisory board or supervisor of the rural collective economic organization shall initiate a prosecution in the People's Court; if no prosecution is initiated in a timely manner, ten or more members of the rural collective economic organization who have full civil capacity may request in writing the supervisory board or the supervisor to initiate a prosecution in the People's Court.  
If the supervisory board or the supervisor refuses to initiate a prosecution after receiving the written request, or fails to initiate a prosecution within fifteen days from the date of receiving the request, the member of the rural collective economic organization who made the written request as specified in the preceding paragraph may, in the interests of the rural collective economic organization, initiate a prosecution in the People's Court in his own name.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　农村集体经济组织章程或者农村集体经济组织成员大会、成员代表大会所作的决定违反本法或者其他法律法规规定的，由乡镇人民政府、街道办事处或者县级人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正。

**Article 61** Where the articles of association of a rural collective economic organization, or a decision made by the general meeting of members or the meeting of member representatives of the rural collective economic organization, violates the provisions of this Law or other laws or regulations, the people's government of the township or town, the sub-district office, or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level shall order rectification within a time limit.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　地方人民政府及其有关部门非法干预农村集体经济组织经营管理和财产管理活动或者未依法履行相应监管职责的，由上级人民政府责令限期改正；情节严重的，依法追究相关责任人员的法律责任。

**Article 62** Where a local people's government or a relevant department thereof illegally interferes with the operational management and property management activities of a rural collective economic organization, or fails to perform corresponding regulatory duties according to law, the people's government at the next higher level shall order rectification within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the legal liability of the relevant responsible persons shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　农村集体经济组织对行政机关的行政行为不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 63** Where a rural collective economic organization is dissatisfied with an administrative act of an administrative organ, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　未设立农村集体经济组织的，村民委员会、村民小组可以依法代行农村集体经济组织的职能。  
村民委员会、村民小组依法代行农村集体经济组织职能的，讨论决定有关集体财产和成员权益的事项参照适用本法的相关规定。

**Article 64** Where no rural collective economic organization is established, the villagers' committee or villagers' group may exercise the functions of a rural collective economic organization on its behalf according to law.  
Where a villagers' committee or villagers' group exercises the functions of a rural collective economic organization on its behalf according to law, the discussion and decision on matters concerning collective property and the rights and interests of members shall refer to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法施行前已经按照国家规定登记的农村集体经济组织及其名称，本法施行后在法人登记证书有效期限内继续有效。

**Article 65** A rural collective economic organization that has been registered in accordance with state provisions before the coming into force of this Law, and its name, shall remain valid within the validity period of the legal person registration certificate after the coming into force of this Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本法施行前农村集体经济组织开展农村集体产权制度改革时已经被确认的成员，本法施行后不需要重新确认。

**Article 66** Members that have been confirmed when a rural collective economic organization carried out reform of the rural collective property rights system before the coming into force of this Law shall not require reconfirmation after the coming into force of this Law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本法自2025年5月1日起施行。

**Article 67** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2025.
