### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国家的领空主权和民用航空权利，保障民用航空活动安全、有序进行，保护民用航空活动当事人各方的合法权益，促进民用航空事业高质量发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of safeguarding the national airspace sovereignty and civil aviation rights, ensuring safe and orderly civil aviation activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of all parties involved in civil aviation activities, and promoting the high-quality development of the civil aviation industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国的领陆和领水之上的空域为中华人民共和国领空。中华人民共和国对领空享有完全的、排他的主权。

**Article 2** The airspace over the land territory and territorial waters of the People's Republic of China shall be the airspace of the People's Republic of China. The People's Republic of China shall enjoy complete and exclusive sovereignty over the airspace.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用航空事业坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以人民为中心，坚持安全第一，统筹发展和安全，为全面建设社会主义现代化国家提供有力支撑。

**Article 3** The civil aviation industry shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to a people-centered approach, prioritize safety, coordinate development and security, and provide strong support for the comprehensive construction of a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院民用航空主管部门对全国民用航空活动实施统一监督管理；根据法律、行政法规和国务院的决定、命令，在本部门的权限内，发布有关民用航空活动的规定、决定。  
国务院民用航空主管部门设立的地区民用航空管理机构依照国务院民用航空主管部门的授权，监督管理各该地区的民用航空活动。

**Article 4** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over civil aviation activities nationwide; and shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and decisions and orders of the State Council, within its authority, issue regulations and decisions concerning civil aviation activities.  
The regional civil aviation administrative organs established by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, pursuant to the authorization of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, supervise and administer civil aviation activities in their respective regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院有关部门依据国民经济和社会发展规划、综合交通运输规划，编制民用航空发展规划。

**Article 5** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the civil aviation development plan based on the national economic and social development plan and the comprehensive transportation plan.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　投资公共航空运输、通用航空、民用机场、空中交通管理等领域，应当符合法律、行政法规和国务院民用航空主管部门有关规定。  
外商投资民用航空业的，还应当符合关于外商投资的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。

**Article 6** Investment in public air transport, general aviation, civil airports, air traffic management, and other fields shall comply with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
Where foreign investors invest in the civil aviation industry, they shall also comply with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State concerning foreign investment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家加强民用航空基础设施建设，鼓励和支持民用航空科学技术研究，发展民用航空教育事业，加大民用航空专业人才培养力度，提升民用航空安全保障水平和服务质量，推动民用航空事业高质量发展。

**Article 7** The State shall strengthen the construction of civil aviation infrastructure, encourage and support scientific and technological research in civil aviation, develop civil aviation education, increase the training of civil aviation professionals, improve the level of civil aviation safety and service quality, and promote the high-quality development of the civil aviation industry.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　从事民用航空活动应当遵守生态环境保护的法律、法规和国家有关规定，控制并减少污染物和温室气体排放。

**Article 8** Persons engaged in civil aviation activities shall comply with the laws, regulations, and relevant provisions of the State on ecological and environmental protection, and shall control and reduce pollutant and greenhouse gas emissions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位应当落实安全生产主体责任，建立健全安全生产规章制度，保证安全投入，改善安全生产条件，构建实施民用航空安全风险分级管控和隐患排查治理双重预防机制，提高民用航空活动安全生产水平。

**Article 9** Entities engaged in civil aviation production and operation activities shall fulfill the primary responsibility for production safety, establish and improve production safety rules and regulations, ensure safety investment, improve production safety conditions, establish and implement a dual prevention mechanism for graded control of civil aviation safety risks and investigation and management of hidden dangers, and improve the level of production safety in civil aviation activities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在民用航空事业发展、民用航空安全等工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 10** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of the civil aviation industry, civil aviation safety, or other related work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　民用航空器

#### 第一节　民用航空器国籍

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本法所称民用航空器，是指除用于执行军事、海关、警务、消防救援飞行任务外的航空器。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国民用航空器权利登记条例.md#art-6" title="被《中华人民共和国民用航空器权利登记条例》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** For the purposes of this Law, "civil aircraft" means aircraft other than those used for the execution of military, customs, police, and fire rescue flight missions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经国务院民用航空主管部门依法进行国籍登记的民用航空器，具有中华人民共和国国籍，由国务院民用航空主管部门发给国籍登记证书。  
国务院民用航空主管部门设立中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记簿，统一记载民用航空器的国籍登记事项。

**Article 12** A civil aircraft that has registered its nationality with the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council according to law shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China and shall be issued a nationality registration certificate by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
The competent civil aviation authority of the State Council shall establish a Register of Nationality of Civil Aircraft of the People's Republic of China to uniformly record matters of nationality registration of civil aircraft.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民用航空器不得具有双重国籍。未注销外国国籍的民用航空器不得在中华人民共和国申请国籍登记。

**Article 13** A civil aircraft shall not have dual nationality. A civil aircraft that has not canceled its foreign nationality shall not apply for nationality registration in the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中华人民共和国国家机构的民用航空器应当进行中华人民共和国国籍登记。  
下列民用航空器可以进行中华人民共和国国籍登记：  
（一）依照中华人民共和国法律设立的法人和非法人组织的民用航空器；  
（二）在中华人民共和国境内有住所的中国公民的民用航空器；  
（三）国务院民用航空主管部门准予登记的其他民用航空器。  
自境外租赁的民用航空器，承租人符合前款规定且该民用航空器的机组人员由承租人配备的，可以申请进行中华人民共和国国籍登记，但是必须先注销该民用航空器原国籍登记。

**Article 14** Civil aircraft of state organs of the People's Republic of China shall undergo nationality registration of the People's Republic of China.  
The following civil aircraft may undergo nationality registration of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Civil aircraft of legal persons and unincorporated organizations established according to the laws of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Civil aircraft of Chinese citizens domiciled within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) Other civil aircraft approved for registration by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
For a civil aircraft leased from abroad, if the lessee complies with the provisions of the preceding paragraph and the crew of the civil aircraft is provided by the lessee, the lessee may apply for nationality registration of the People's Republic of China, provided that the civil aircraft's original nationality registration is first canceled.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依法取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，应当标明规定的国籍标志和登记标志。  
民用航空器取得中华人民共和国国籍后，方可向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得适航证书。

**Article 15** A civil aircraft that has obtained the nationality of the People's Republic of China according to law shall display the prescribed nationality marks and registration marks.  
A civil aircraft may apply to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for an airworthiness certificate only after obtaining the nationality of the People's Republic of China.

#### 第二节　民用航空器权利

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本法规定的对民用航空器的权利，包括对民用航空器构架、发动机、螺旋桨、无线电设备和其他一切为了在民用航空器上使用的，无论安装于其上或者暂时拆离的物品的权利。

**Article 16** The rights in civil aircraft as provided for in this Law include rights in the airframe, engines, propellers, radio equipment, and all other articles intended for use on a civil aircraft, whether installed thereon or temporarily removed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民用航空器权利人可以就下列权利分别向国务院民用航空主管部门申请办理权利登记；未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人：  
（一）民用航空器所有权；  
（二）通过购买行为取得并占有民用航空器的权利；  
（三）根据融资租赁合同或者租赁期限为六个月以上的租赁合同占有民用航空器的权利；  
（四）民用航空器抵押权。  
转让民用航空器所有权，应当签订书面合同。  
民用航空器抵押权登记由抵押权人和抵押人共同向国务院民用航空主管部门申请办理。

**Article 17** The right holder of a civil aircraft may apply to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for registration of the rights in respect of the following rights separately; without registration, it shall not be set up against a bona fide third party:  
(1) Ownership of a civil aircraft;  
(2) The right to acquire and possess a civil aircraft through purchase;  
(3) The right to possess a civil aircraft under a financial lease contract or a lease contract with a lease term of six months or more;  
(4) Mortgage on a civil aircraft.  
Transfer of ownership of a civil aircraft shall be made in writing.  
Registration of a mortgage on a civil aircraft shall be applied for jointly by the mortgagee and the mortgagor to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院民用航空主管部门设立民用航空器权利登记簿。同一民用航空器的权利登记事项应当记载于同一权利登记簿中。  
民用航空器权利登记事项，可以供公众查询、复制或者摘录。

**Article 18** The competent civil aviation authority of the State Council shall establish a Register of Rights in Civil Aircraft. The matters of rights registration of the same civil aircraft shall be recorded in the same rights register.  
The matters of rights registration of civil aircraft may be inspected, copied, or extracted by the public.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　除民用航空器经依法强制拍卖外，在已经登记的民用航空器权利得到补偿或者民用航空器权利人同意之前，民用航空器的国籍登记或者权利登记不得转移至国外。

**Article 19** Except where a civil aircraft is sold by compulsory auction according to law, the nationality registration or rights registration of a civil aircraft shall not be transferred abroad before the registered rights in the civil aircraft are compensated or the right holder of the civil aircraft gives consent.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民用航空器优先权，是指债权人依照[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定，向民用航空器所有人、承租人提出请求，对产生该请求的民用航空器具有优先受偿的权利。  
民用航空器优先权先于民用航空器抵押权受偿。  
[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定的债权转移的，其民用航空器优先权随之转移。

**Article 20** "Civil aircraft priority" means a right of a creditor, who makes a claim against the owner or lessee of a civil aircraft in accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of this Law, to have preferential payment from the civil aircraft that gave rise to such claim.  
A civil aircraft priority shall be satisfied prior to a civil aircraft mortgage.  
Where a claim provided for in Article 21 of this Law is transferred, the civil aircraft priority shall be transferred therewith.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　下列各项债权具有民用航空器优先权：  
（一）援救该民用航空器的报酬；  
（二）保管维护该民用航空器的必需费用。  
前款规定的债权，后发生的先受偿；债权人应当自援救或者保管维护工作终了之日起三个月内，就其债权向国务院民用航空主管部门申请登记。

**Article 21** The following claims shall have a civil aircraft priority:  
(1) Remuneration for the rescue of the civil aircraft;  
(2) Necessary expenses for the custody and maintenance of the civil aircraft.  
Claims under the preceding paragraph shall be satisfied in reverse order of their occurrence; the creditor shall, within three months from the date of completion of the rescue or the custody and maintenance work, apply to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for registration of its claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民用航空器优先权应当通过人民法院扣押产生优先权的民用航空器行使。

**Article 22** A civil aircraft priority shall be exercised through the People's Court's seizure of the civil aircraft that gave rise to the priority.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民用航空器优先权自援救或者保管维护工作终了之日起满三个月时终止；但是，债权人就其债权已经依照[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定登记，并具有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）债权人、债务人已经就此项债权的金额达成协议；  
（二）有关此项债权的诉讼已经开始。  
民用航空器优先权不因民用航空器所有权的转让而消灭；但是，民用航空器经依法强制拍卖的除外。

**Article 23** A civil aircraft priority shall terminate upon the expiry of three months from the date of completion of the rescue or the custody and maintenance work; except where the creditor has registered its claim in accordance with Article 21 of this Law and any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) The creditor and the debtor have reached an agreement on the amount of the claim;  
(2) Litigation concerning the claim has commenced.  
A civil aircraft priority shall not be extinguished by the transfer of ownership of the civil aircraft; except where the civil aircraft is sold by compulsory auction according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　为了债权人的共同利益，在执行人民法院判决以及拍卖过程中产生的费用，应当从民用航空器拍卖所得价款中先行拨付。

**Article 24** Expenses incurred in the execution of a judgment of the People's Court and in the course of auction for the common benefit of creditors shall be first deducted from the proceeds of the auction of the civil aircraft.

#### 第三节　民用航空器适航管理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事民用航空器及其发动机、螺旋桨（以下统称民用航空产品）和民用航空产品零部件设计、设计更改的机构，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，建立相应的设计保证系统，并向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得设计机构许可证书。  
取得设计机构许可证书的机构可以接受国务院民用航空主管部门的委托提供技术检查等相关服务。

**Article 25** An organization engaged in the design or design change of civil aircraft, their engines and propellers (hereinafter collectively referred to as "civil aviation products") and parts of civil aviation products shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, establish a corresponding design assurance system and apply to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for a design organization approval certificate.  
An organization that has obtained a design organization approval certificate may, upon entrustment by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, provide technical inspection and other related services.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　设计民用航空产品，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得型号合格证书。  
设计民用航空产品零部件，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得设计许可证书。  
已取得外国颁发的型号合格证书的民用航空产品首次进口中国或者在中国境内生产的，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得型号认可证书。  
已取得外国颁发的设计许可证书的民用航空产品零部件首次进口中国或者在中国境内生产的，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得设计认可证书。

**Article 26** For designing a civil aviation product, an application shall be made for a type certificate in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
For designing a part of a civil aviation product, an application shall be made for a design approval certificate in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
Where a civil aviation product that has obtained a type certificate issued by a foreign country is imported into China for the first time or produced within the territory of China, an application shall be made for a type acceptance certificate in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
Where a part of a civil aviation product that has obtained a design approval certificate issued by a foreign country is imported into China for the first time or produced within the territory of China, an application shall be made for a design acceptance certificate in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对依照[本法第二十六条](#art-26)第一款、第三款规定已取得相应证书的民用航空产品设计进行更改，应当由取得设计机构许可证书的机构进行，并按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得补充型号合格证书或者改装设计批准证书；但是，相关更改属于设计小改的除外。  
已取得外国颁发的补充型号合格证书的民用航空产品设计更改，拟在具有中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器上实施的，应当向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得补充型号认可证书。  
取得设计机构许可证书的机构可以为本单位进行的设计小改签发合格证明。  
本法所称设计小改，是指对民用航空产品的重量、平衡、结构强度、可靠性、使用特性以及其他与民用航空产品适航性相关的特性没有显著影响的设计更改。  
转让经许可的设计或者设计更改的，受让人应当具备与拟转让的设计或者设计更改相适应的设计机构许可证书。

**Article 27** A change to the design of a civil aviation product that has obtained the corresponding certificate in accordance with Articles 26(1) and 26(3) of this Law shall be carried out by an organization that has obtained a design organization approval certificate, and an application shall be made for a supplemental type certificate or a modification design approval certificate in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council; except where the change is a minor design change.  
Where a design change of a civil aviation product that has obtained a supplemental type certificate issued by a foreign country is intended to be implemented on a civil aircraft having the nationality of the People's Republic of China, an application shall be made to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for a supplemental type acceptance certificate.  
An organization that has obtained a design organization approval certificate may issue a certificate of compliance for minor design changes carried out by itself.  
For the purposes of this Law, "minor design change" means a design change that has no significant effect on the weight, balance, structural strength, reliability, operational characteristics, or other characteristics related to the airworthiness of the civil aviation product.  
Where a licensed design or design change is transferred, the transferee shall possess a design organization approval certificate appropriate to the design or design change to be transferred.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　从事民用航空产品和零部件生产的机构，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定建立生产质量系统，并申请取得生产机构许可证书。  
取得生产机构许可证书的机构可以在许可范围内为本单位生产的民用航空产品和零部件签发合格证明，并可以接受国务院民用航空主管部门的委托提供技术检查等相关服务。

**Article 28** An organization engaged in the production of civil aviation products and parts shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, establish a production quality system and apply for a production organization approval certificate.  
An organization that has obtained a production organization approval certificate may, within the scope of its approval, issue certificates of compliance for the civil aviation products and parts it produces, and may, upon entrustment by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, provide technical inspection and other related services.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　仅设计和生产装于已获得型号合格证书或者型号认可证书的民用航空产品后只构成设计小改的民用航空产品零部件，按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定无需取得设计机构许可证书和生产机构许可证书的，应当向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得零部件制造人许可证书。

**Article 29** Where only the design and production of parts of civil aviation products that, when installed on a civil aviation product that has obtained a type certificate or type acceptance certificate, constitute only a minor design change, and where, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, no design organization approval certificate or production organization approval certificate is required, an application shall be made to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for a parts manufacturer approval certificate.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从事民用航空产品和零部件维修的机构，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得维修机构许可证书。

**Article 30** An organization engaged in the maintenance of civil aviation products and parts shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council, apply for a maintenance organization approval certificate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　具有中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得适航证书，方可飞行。尚未取得适航证书，为生产试飞等目的需要进行飞行的，按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得特许飞行证书后，可以在许可范围内从事飞行活动。  
租用具有外国国籍的民用航空器从事国务院民用航空主管部门规定的飞行活动，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请对原国籍登记国颁发的适航证书审查认可或者申请取得适航证书，方可飞行。  
出口民用航空器，可以向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得出口适航证书。

**Article 31** The civil aircraft with the nationality of the People's Republic of China shall, according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, apply for and obtain an airworthiness certificate before it may fly. Where it has not yet obtained an airworthiness certificate but needs to fly for purposes such as production test flight, it may engage in flight activities within the permitted scope after applying for and obtaining a special flight permit certificate according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
Where a civil aircraft with foreign nationality is leased to engage in flight activities specified by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, the lessee shall, according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, apply for review and recognition of the airworthiness certificate issued by the state of original nationality registration, or apply for an airworthiness certificate, before it may fly.  
Where a civil aircraft is exported, an application may be made to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for an export airworthiness certificate.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　从事民用航空化学产品设计、生产和民用航空油料供应、测试的单位，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得相关许可证书。

**Article 32** Any entity engaged in the design and production of civil aviation chemical products and the supply and testing of civil aviation fuel shall, according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, apply for and obtain the relevant license certificate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民用航空器的所有人或者承租人应当按照适航证书规定的使用范围使用民用航空器，做好民用航空器的维修保养工作，保证民用航空器处于适航状态；民用航空产品和零部件需要维修的，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定由取得相关许可的机构或者人员实施。

**Article 33** The owner or lessee of a civil aircraft shall use the civil aircraft within the scope of use prescribed in the airworthiness certificate, perform proper maintenance and repair work on the civil aircraft, and ensure that the civil aircraft is in an airworthy condition. Where civil aviation products and parts need repair, such repair shall be carried out by an institution or person who has obtained the relevant license according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事民用无人驾驶航空器的设计、生产、进口、维修和飞行活动，应当按照国家有关规定向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得适航许可，按照规定无需取得适航许可的除外。  
从事民用无人驾驶航空器生产的机构应当按照国家有关规定为其生产的无人驾驶航空器设置唯一产品识别码。

**Article 34** Any person engaged in the design, production, import, repair, and flight activities of civil unmanned aircraft shall apply to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for an airworthiness permit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, except where an airworthiness permit is not required according to the provisions.  
Any institution engaged in the production of civil unmanned aircraft shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, affix a unique product identification code to the unmanned aircraft it produces.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院根据本法制定民用航空器适航管理规定。

**Article 35** The State Council shall formulate regulations on the administration of airworthiness of civil aircraft in accordance with this Law.

### 第三章　航空人员

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本法所称航空人员，是指下列从事民用航空活动的空勤人员和地面人员：  
（一）空勤人员，包括驾驶员、飞行机械员、乘务员、航空安全员；  
（二）地面人员，包括民用航空器维修人员、空中交通管制员、飞行签派员、航空电信人员、航空情报员、航空气象人员。

**Article 36** The term "aviation personnel" as used in this Law refers to the following flight crew and ground personnel engaged in civil aviation activities:  
(1) Flight crew, including pilots, flight engineers, cabin attendants, and aviation security officers;  
(2) Ground personnel, including civil aircraft maintenance personnel, air traffic controllers, flight dispatchers, aviation telecommunication personnel, aviation intelligence personnel, and aviation meteorological personnel.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　乘务员应当接受专门训练，取得公共航空运输企业颁发的训练合格证书，其他航空人员应当接受专门训练，经考核合格，取得国务院民用航空主管部门颁发的执照，方可履行相应的工作职责。  
空勤人员和空中交通管制员在取得执照或者训练合格证书前，还应当接受符合国务院民用航空主管部门要求的体格检查单位的检查，并取得国务院民用航空主管部门颁发的体格检查合格证书。

**Article 37** Cabin attendants shall receive specialized training and obtain a training certificate issued by a public air transport enterprise, and other aviation personnel shall receive specialized training and, upon passing assessment, obtain a license issued by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, before they may perform their corresponding duties.  
Before obtaining a license or training certificate, flight crew and air traffic controllers shall also undergo an examination by a physical examination unit that meets the requirements of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council and obtain a physical examination certificate issued by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　从事飞行活动时，驾驶员、飞行机械员和航空安全员应当携带执照和体格检查合格证书，乘务员应当携带训练合格证书和体格检查合格证书，并接受国务院民用航空主管部门的查验。

**Article 38** When engaging in flight activities, pilots, flight engineers, and aviation security officers shall carry their licenses and physical examination certificates, and cabin attendants shall carry their training certificates and physical examination certificates, and shall accept inspection by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　航空人员应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定接受训练以及定期或者不定期的检查、考核，检查、考核合格后方可继续履行相应的工作职责。  
航空人员所属单位应当按照规定建立完善的航空人员劳动保护、健康管理制度，为航空人员保持良好的身体和心理状态提供保障。

**Article 39** Aviation personnel shall receive training and regular or irregular inspections and assessments according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and may continue to perform their corresponding duties only after passing such inspections and assessments.  
The unit to which the aviation personnel belong shall, in accordance with the provisions, establish a sound labor protection and health management system for aviation personnel, and provide guarantees for aviation personnel to maintain good physical and psychological conditions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　为民用航空器驾驶员、维修人员、飞行签派员提供执照及资格训练的机构，应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定申请取得相应许可证书，并在许可的范围内按照训练大纲实施训练。

**Article 40** Any institution providing license and qualification training for civil aircraft pilots, maintenance personnel, and flight dispatchers shall, according to the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, apply for and obtain the corresponding license certificate, and shall conduct training within the scope of the license and in accordance with the training syllabus.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　用于满足民用航空器驾驶员训练、考试或者检查要求的飞行模拟训练设备，应当通过国务院民用航空主管部门的鉴定，取得飞行模拟训练设备合格证书，方可投入使用。

**Article 41** Any flight simulation training device used to meet the training, examination, or inspection requirements for civil aircraft pilots shall pass the evaluation of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council and obtain a flight simulation training device certificate before it may be put into use.

#### 第二节　机组

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民用航空器机组由机长和其他空勤人员组成。机长应当由具有独立驾驶该型号民用航空器的技术和经验的驾驶员担任。  
机组的组成和人员数额，应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门的规定。

**Article 42** The crew of a civil aircraft shall consist of the pilot-in-command and other flight crew members. The pilot-in-command shall be a pilot who has the technical skills and experience to independently pilot that type of civil aircraft.  
The composition and number of crew members shall comply with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民用航空器的操作由机长负责，机长应当严格履行职责，保护民用航空器及其所载人员和财产的安全。  
机长在其职权范围内发布的命令，民用航空器所载人员都应当执行。

**Article 43** The operation of a civil aircraft shall be the responsibility of the pilot-in-command, who shall strictly perform his duties to protect the safety of the civil aircraft and the persons and property on board.  
All persons on board the civil aircraft shall obey the orders issued by the pilot-in-command within the scope of his authority.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　飞行前，机长应当对民用航空器实施必要的检查；未经检查，不得起飞。  
机长发现民用航空器、机场、气象条件等不符合规定，不能保证飞行安全的，有权拒绝起飞。

**Article 44** Before flight, the pilot-in-command shall carry out the necessary inspection of the civil aircraft; without such inspection, the aircraft shall not take off.  
Where the pilot-in-command finds that the civil aircraft, airport, meteorological conditions, or other factors do not meet the requirements and that flight safety cannot be ensured, he shall have the right to refuse takeoff.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　飞行中，对于任何破坏民用航空器、扰乱民用航空器内秩序、危害民用航空器所载人员或者财产安全以及其他危及飞行安全的行为，在保证安全的前提下，机长有权采取包括必要的管束措施在内的合理措施，必要时可以请求或者授权旅客提供协助。  
飞行中，遇到特殊情况时，为保证民用航空器及其所载人员的安全，机长有权对民用航空器作出处置。

**Article 45** During flight, with respect to any act that damages the civil aircraft, disturbs order within the civil aircraft, endangers the safety of persons or property on board the civil aircraft, or otherwise jeopardizes flight safety, the pilot-in-command shall have the right, on the premise of ensuring safety, to take reasonable measures, including necessary restraint measures, and may, when necessary, request or authorize passengers to provide assistance.  
During flight, in the event of special circumstances, the pilot-in-command shall have the right to take measures concerning the civil aircraft in order to ensure the safety of the civil aircraft and the persons on board.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　机长发现机组人员不适宜执行飞行任务的，为保证飞行安全，有权提出调整。

**Article 46** Where the pilot-in-command finds that a crew member is unfit to perform flight duties, he shall have the right to propose an adjustment in order to ensure flight safety.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　民用航空器遇险时，机长有权采取一切必要措施，并指挥机组人员和民用航空器上其他人员采取抢救措施。在必须撤离遇险民用航空器的紧急情况下，机长应当采取措施，首先组织旅客安全离开民用航空器；未经机长允许，机组人员不得擅自离开民用航空器；机长应当最后离开民用航空器。

**Article 47** When a civil aircraft is in distress, the pilot-in-command shall have the right to take all necessary measures and to direct the crew members and other persons on board the civil aircraft to take rescue measures. In an emergency requiring evacuation of the civil aircraft in distress, the pilot-in-command shall take measures to first organize the passengers to leave the civil aircraft safely; without the permission of the pilot-in-command, no crew member may leave the civil aircraft without authorization; the pilot-in-command shall be the last to leave the civil aircraft.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　民用航空器发生事故，机长应当直接或者通过空中交通管制单位，将事故情况及时、如实报告国务院民用航空主管部门。

**Article 48** Where an accident occurs to a civil aircraft, the pilot-in-command shall, directly or through the air traffic control unit, report the accident promptly and truthfully to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　机长收到船舶或者其他航空器的遇险信号，或者发现遇险的船舶、航空器及其人员，应当将遇险情况及时报告就近的空中交通管制单位并给予可能的合理的援助。

**Article 49** Where the pilot-in-command receives a distress signal from a vessel or other aircraft, or discovers a vessel, aircraft, or persons in distress, he shall promptly report the distress situation to the nearest air traffic control unit and provide such reasonable assistance as may be possible.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　飞行中，机长因故不能履行职务的，由仅次于机长职务的驾驶员代理机长；在下一个经停地起飞前，民用航空器所有人或者承租人应当指派新机长接任。

**Article 50** If during flight the pilot-in-command is unable to perform his duties for any reason, the pilot next in rank to the pilot-in-command shall act as the pilot-in-command; before taking off at the next stopover, the owner or lessee of the civil aircraft shall appoint a new pilot-in-command to take over.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　只有一名驾驶员，不需配备其他空勤人员的民用航空器，本节对机长的规定，适用于该驾驶员。

**Article 51** Where a civil aircraft is operated by only one pilot and does not require other crew members, the provisions of this Section regarding the captain shall apply to such pilot.

### 第四章　民用机场

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本法所称民用机场，是指供民用航空器起飞、降落、滑行、停放以及进行其他活动使用的划定区域，包括附属的建筑物、装置和设施。  
民用机场分为公共航空运输机场（以下简称运输机场）和通用航空机场（以下简称通用机场）。  
军民合用机场由国务院、中央军事委员会另行制定管理办法。  
临时起降场地的使用和管理，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 52** For the purpose of this Law, a civil airport means a designated area, including ancillary buildings, installations and facilities, used for the take-off, landing, taxiing, parking and other activities of civil aircraft.  
Civil airports are divided into public air transport airports (hereinafter referred to as transport airports) and general aviation airports (hereinafter referred to as general airports).  
The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall separately formulate administrative measures for airports used by both civil and military entities.  
The use and management of temporary landing sites shall be governed by relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民用机场的规划、建设和运行应当统筹安排、合理布局，依法落实国防要求，提高安全水平和运行效率，促进民用航空发展与生态环境保护相协调。

**Article 53** The planning, construction and operation of civil airports shall be coordinated, rationally arranged, embody national defense requirements according to law, enhance safety level and operational efficiency, and promote the coordination between the development of civil aviation and the protection of the ecological environment.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将本行政区域内民用机场场址和总体规划纳入本级国土空间规划统筹安排，并根据机场运行安全、国防要求以及生态环境保护的需要，依法对周边区域实行规划控制，依法划定噪声敏感建筑物禁止建设区域和限制建设区域。

**Article 54** The local people's government at or above the county level shall incorporate the site selection and overall planning of civil airports within its administrative region into the territorial space planning at the same level for coordinated arrangement, and shall, in accordance with the needs of airport operation safety, national defense requirements and ecological environment protection, exercise planning control over surrounding areas according to law, and designate by law areas where construction of noise-sensitive buildings is prohibited or restricted.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　新建、扩建民用机场，应当由民用机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府发布项目建设公告。  
前款规定的公告应当在当地主要媒体上发布，并在拟新建、扩建机场周围地区张贴。

**Article 55** For the construction of a new civil airport or the expansion of an existing one, the local people's government at or above the county level where the airport is located shall issue a project construction announcement.  
The announcement as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be published in the local major media and posted in the surrounding areas of the airport to be newly built or expanded.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　民用机场所在地有关地方人民政府应当会同地区民用航空管理机构，按照国家有关规定划定民用机场净空保护区域，并向社会公布。  
禁止在依法划定的民用机场范围内和民用机场净空保护区域内从事下列妨碍民用机场净空保护的行为：  
（一）修建靶场、强烈爆炸物仓库等影响飞行安全的建筑物或者其他设施；  
（二）修建不符合机场净空要求的建筑物或者设施；  
（三）设置影响机场目视助航设施使用的灯光、激光、标志或者物体；  
（四）种植影响飞行安全或者影响机场助航设施使用的植物；  
（五）放飞影响飞行安全的鸟类，升放无人驾驶的自由气球、系留气球或者其他升空物体；  
（六）排放大量烟雾、粉尘、火焰、废气等影响飞行安全的物质；  
（七）法律、行政法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 56** The relevant local people's government at the place where the civil airport is located shall, in conjunction with the regional civil aviation administration, delimit the airspace protection area for the civil airport in accordance with relevant provisions of the State, and make it known to the public.  
It is prohibited to engage in the following acts that obstruct the airspace protection of the civil airport within the legally delimited civil airport area and the civil airport airspace protection area:  
(1) constructing shooting ranges, warehouses for strong explosives or other buildings or facilities that affect flight safety;  
(2) constructing buildings or facilities that do not meet the airspace requirements of the airport;  
(3) installing lights, lasers, signs or objects that affect the use of visual aids for navigation of the airport;  
(4) planting plants that affect flight safety or the use of airport navigation aids;  
(5) releasing birds that affect flight safety, or flying free balloons, tethered balloons or other airborne objects without a pilot;  
(6) emitting large amounts of smoke, dust, flames, exhaust gases or other substances that affect flight safety;  
(7) other acts prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　民用机场所在地地方无线电管理机构应当会同地区民用航空管理机构，按照国家无线电管理的有关规定和标准划定民用机场电磁环境保护区域，并向社会公布。  
禁止在依法划定的民用机场电磁环境保护区域内从事下列影响民用机场电磁环境的行为：  
（一）未经批准设置、使用无线电台（站）；  
（二）设置、使用影响民用航空无线电台（站）使用的设备、仪器、装置；  
（三）修建、设置影响民用航空无线电台（站）使用的铁路、公路、堤坝、电力设施、架空金属线、金属堆积物等建筑物或者其他设施；  
（四）开展影响民用航空无线电信号传播的掘土、采砂、采石等改变地形地貌的活动；  
（五）法律、行政法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 57** The local radio regulatory authority at the place where the civil airport is located shall, in conjunction with the regional civil aviation administration, delimit the electromagnetic environment protection area for the civil airport in accordance with relevant provisions and standards on radio regulation of the State, and make it known to the public.  
It is prohibited to engage in the following acts that affect the electromagnetic environment of the civil airport within the legally delimited civil airport electromagnetic environment protection area:  
(1) installing or using radio stations (or radio terminals) without approval;  
(2) installing or using equipment, instruments or devices that affect the use of civil aviation radio stations (or terminals);  
(3) constructing or installing railways, highways, dykes, power facilities, overhead metal wires, metal accumulations or other buildings or facilities that affect the use of civil aviation radio stations (or terminals);  
(4) carrying out activities that affect the propagation of civil aviation radio signals, such as earth excavation, sand mining, stone quarrying, or other activities that change the landform or topography;  
(5) other acts prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　民用机场项目建设公告发布前，在依法划定的民用机场范围内和民用机场净空保护区域、电磁环境保护区域内存在的可能影响飞行安全的建筑物、构筑物、树木、照明设施、无线电台（站）和其他障碍物体，应当在规定的期限内清除；当事人逾期未清除，经催告仍不清除的，由机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府依法予以清除。对由此造成的损失，应当依法给予补偿或者采取其他补救措施。

**Article 58** Buildings, structures, trees, lighting facilities, radio stations (or radio terminals) and other obstacles that exist within the legally delimited civil airport area, airspace protection area and electromagnetic environment protection area before the issuance of the project construction announcement for the civil airport and that may affect flight safety shall be removed within the prescribed time limit; if the party fails to remove them within the time limit and still fails to do so after a reminder, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the airport is located shall remove them according to law. Losses caused thereby shall be compensated according to law or other remedial measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　民用机场项目建设公告发布后，任何单位或者个人违反本法和有关法规的规定，在依法划定的民用机场范围内和民用机场净空保护区域、电磁环境保护区域内修建、种植或者设置可能影响飞行安全的障碍物体的，由机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府作出要求当事人在规定期限内履行排除妨碍、恢复原状等义务的行政决定，当事人逾期不履行，经催告仍不履行的，由机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府依法代履行。障碍物体造成的损失以及代履行的费用，由修建、种植或者设置该障碍物体的单位或者个人承担。

**Article 59** After the issuance of the project construction announcement for a civil airport, if any entity or individual, in violation of this Law and relevant regulations, constructs, plants or installs any obstacle that may affect flight safety within the legally delimited civil airport area, airspace protection area or electromagnetic environment protection area, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the airport is located shall make an administrative decision requiring the party to fulfill the obligation of removing the obstacle, restoring the original condition, etc. within a specified time limit. If the party fails to do so within the time limit and, after a reminder, still fails to do so, the local people's government at or above the county level at the place where the airport is located shall perform the obligation by substitution according to law. The losses caused by the obstacle and the expenses for the substitute performance shall be borne by the entity or individual that constructed, planted or installed the obstacle.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　民用机场运营人应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，设置必要设施、采取适当措施，维护民用机场运营秩序、保障民用机场运营安全。

**Article 60** The operator of a civil airport shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, set up necessary facilities and take appropriate measures to maintain the order of civil airport operations and ensure the safety of civil airport operations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家在民用机场以及周边一定范围的区域依法划定无人驾驶航空器管制空域，并向社会公布。  
民用机场应当具备相应的对无人驾驶航空器的防范和处置能力，依法配备必要的探测、反制设备，在有关机关的监督和指导下严格按照规定的权限和程序使用。

**Article 61** The State shall delimit, according to law, controlled airspace for unmanned aircraft within and around a certain area of civil airports, and make it known to the public.  
A civil airport shall have the corresponding capability to prevent and handle unmanned aircraft, be equipped with necessary detection and countermeasure equipment according to law, and use such equipment strictly in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures under the supervision and guidance of relevant authorities.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　民用航空器在民用机场运行的时刻应当按照公开、公平、公正的原则，在机场容量范围内协调配置。具体管理办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 62** The operation times of civil aircraft at civil airports shall be coordinated and allocated within the airport's capacity in accordance with the principles of openness, fairness and impartiality. The specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　民用航空器使用民用机场及其助航设施，应当缴纳使用费、服务费；使用费、服务费的收费标准，由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 63** For the use of a civil airport and its navigation aids by a civil aircraft, usage fees and service fees shall be paid; the fee rates shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　民用机场废弃或者改作他用，应当按照国家有关规定办理报批手续。

**Article 64** The abandonment of a civil airport or its conversion to other uses shall go through the procedures of submission for approval in accordance with relevant provisions of the State.

#### 第二节　运输机场

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　全国运输机场的布局和建设规划，由国务院发展改革部门会同国务院民用航空主管部门制定，并按照国家规定的程序，经批准后组织实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据全国运输机场布局和建设规划，制定本行政区域内的运输机场建设规划，并按照国家规定的程序报经批准后，将运输机场建设工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 65** The layout and construction plan for national transport airports shall be formulated by the development and reform department of the State Council in conjunction with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and shall be implemented after approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall formulate the transport airport construction plan within its administrative region based on the national transport airport layout and construction plan, and after approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State, incorporate the construction of transport airports into its national economic and social development plan at the same level.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　新建、改建和扩建运输机场，应当符合依法制定的运输机场布局和建设规划以及相关技术标准，按照国家有关规定报经批准后实施。  
不符合依法制定的运输机场布局和建设规划的运输机场建设项目，不得批准。

**Article 66** The construction, reconstruction or expansion of a transport airport shall conform to the legally formulated transport airport layout and construction plan and relevant technical standards, and shall be implemented after approval in accordance with relevant provisions of the State.  
No transport airport construction project that does not conform to the legally formulated transport airport layout and construction plan shall be approved.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　运输机场应当依法取得机场运营许可证，方可开放运营。

**Article 67** A transport airport shall obtain an airport operation permit according to law before it may open for operation.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　设立国际机场，由机场所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报请国务院批准。  
国际机场应当具备国际通航条件，口岸查验工作由移民管理、海关等口岸查验机构依法承担。口岸查验基础设施建设应当符合国家口岸查验基础设施建设标准和口岸公共卫生核心能力建设标准。  
国际机场的开放使用，由国务院民用航空主管部门对外公告；国际机场资料由国务院民用航空主管部门统一对外提供。

**Article 68** The establishment of an international airport shall be submitted by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government at the place where the airport is located to the State Council for approval.  
An international airport shall meet the conditions for international air service, and the port inspection work shall be undertaken by immigration management, customs and other port inspection authorities according to law. The construction of port inspection infrastructure shall meet the national standards for port inspection infrastructure construction and the standards for core capabilities of port public health.  
The opening and use of an international airport shall be announced to the public by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council; information on international airports shall be uniformly provided to the public by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

#### 第三节　通用机场

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　通用机场根据业务类型、建设规模和标准，实施分类分级管理，具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 69** General airports shall be managed by categories and grades according to business type, construction scale and standards. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当综合考虑本行政区域内人口、土地、空域资源、交通运输、产业基础等条件，因地制宜推进通用机场建设，合理确定通用机场建设规模和标准。

**Article 70** The people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, taking into comprehensive consideration the conditions of population, land, airspace resources, transportation, industrial foundation, etc., within its administrative region, promote the construction of general airports in light of local conditions, and reasonably determine the construction scale and standards of general airports.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府发展改革部门组织编制本行政区域内通用机场布局规划，征得地区民用航空管理机构和其他相关单位同意后，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。  
新建、改建和扩建通用机场，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据通用机场布局规划，按照国家有关规定审批、核准或者备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当加强对本行政区域内通用机场建设工程的质量和安全生产监督管理。

**Article 71** The development and reform department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the formulation of a layout plan for general aviation airports within its administrative region, and after obtaining the consent of the regional civil aviation administration and other relevant units, shall submit it to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.  
The construction, renovation, and expansion of general aviation airports shall be subject to approval, verification, or recordation by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the layout plan for general aviation airports and the relevant provisions of the State.  
The relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and management of the quality and production safety of the construction projects of general aviation airports within their administrative regions.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国务院民用航空主管部门规定的对公众开放的通用机场应当依法取得机场运营许可证，方可开放运营。  
其他通用机场应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定进行备案。

**Article 72** A general aviation airport that is open to the public as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall obtain an airport operation permit according to law before it may be open for operation.  
Other general aviation airports shall be registered for recordation in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

### 第五章　空中航行

#### 第一节　空域管理

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国家对空域实行统一管理。国家空中交通管理机构依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定管理全国空域资源。

**Article 73** The State shall exercise unified management over airspace. The State air traffic management authority shall manage national airspace resources in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　划分空域，应当兼顾民用航空和国防安全、低空经济发展需要以及公众利益，使空域得到合理、安全、充分、有效利用。

**Article 74** The division of airspace shall take into account the needs of civil aviation and national defense security, the development of the low-altitude economy, and the public interest, so that the airspace may be used reasonably, safely, fully, and effectively.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　空域管理的具体办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 75** Specific measures for airspace management shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

#### 第二节　飞行管理

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　在一个划定的管制空域内，由一个空中交通管制单位负责该空域内的航空器的空中交通管制。

**Article 76** Within a designated controlled airspace, one air traffic control unit shall be responsible for the air traffic control of aircraft in that airspace.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　民用航空器在管制空域内进行飞行活动，应当按照有关规定取得空中交通管制单位的许可。

**Article 77** A civil aircraft conducting flight activities in controlled airspace shall obtain permission from the air traffic control unit in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　从事飞行的民用航空器，应当携带下列文件：  
（一）民用航空器国籍登记证书；  
（二）民用航空器适航证书；  
（三）机组人员的相应执照、训练合格证书和体格检查合格证书；  
（四）民用航空器航行记录簿；  
（五）装有无线电设备的民用航空器，其无线电台执照；  
（六）载有旅客的民用航空器，其所载旅客姓名及其出发地点和目的地点的清单；  
（七）载有货物的民用航空器，其所载货物的舱单和明细的申报单；  
（八）根据飞行任务应当携带的其他文件。  
民用航空器未按规定携带前款所列文件的，国务院民用航空主管部门或者其授权的地区民用航空管理机构可以禁止该民用航空器起飞。

**Article 78** A civil aircraft engaged in flight shall carry the following documents:  
(1) The certificate of nationality registration of the civil aircraft;  
(2) The airworthiness certificate of the civil aircraft;  
(3) The corresponding licenses, training qualification certificates, and physical examination qualification certificates of the crew members;  
(4) The navigation logbook of the civil aircraft;  
(5) For a civil aircraft equipped with radio equipment, its radio station license;  
(6) For a civil aircraft carrying passengers, a list of the names of the passengers carried and their places of departure and destination;  
(7) For a civil aircraft carrying cargo, the cargo manifest and detailed declaration form;  
(8) Other documents that shall be carried according to the flight mission.  
Where a civil aircraft fails to carry the documents listed in the preceding paragraph as required, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council or its authorized regional civil aviation administration may prohibit the civil aircraft from taking off.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　民用航空器应当按照空中交通管制单位指定的航路和飞行高度飞行，因故确需偏离指定的航路或者改变飞行高度飞行的，应当取得空中交通管制单位的许可。  
民用航空器应当遵守国务院民用航空主管部门规定的机场飞行程序和运行标准。

**Article 79** A civil aircraft shall fly along the airway and at the altitude designated by the air traffic control unit. If it is necessary to deviate from the designated airway or change the flight altitude for a valid reason, permission from the air traffic control unit shall be obtained.  
A civil aircraft shall comply with the airport flight procedures and operating standards prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的民用航空器，必须遵守统一的飞行规则。  
进行目视飞行的民用航空器，应当遵守目视飞行规则，并与其他航空器、地面障碍物体保持安全距离。  
进行仪表飞行的民用航空器，应当遵守仪表飞行规则。  
飞行规则由国务院、中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 80** A civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with uniform flight rules.  
A civil aircraft conducting visual flight shall comply with visual flight rules and maintain a safe distance from other aircraft and ground obstacles.  
A civil aircraft conducting instrument flight shall comply with instrument flight rules.  
Flight rules shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　民用航空器机组人员的飞行时间、值勤时间、休息时间应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门的规定。  
民用航空器机组人员受到酒精类饮料、麻醉剂或者其他药物的影响，损及工作能力的，不得执行飞行任务。

**Article 81** The flight time, duty time, and rest time of the crew members of a civil aircraft shall comply with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
A crew member of a civil aircraft whose ability to work is impaired by the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, or other drugs shall not perform flight duties.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　民用航空器除按照国家有关规定经特别批准外，不得飞入空中禁区；除遵守规定的限制条件外，不得飞入空中限制区。  
前款规定的空中禁区和空中限制区，按照国家有关规定划定。

**Article 82** A civil aircraft shall not fly into a prohibited airspace unless specially approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; and shall not fly into a restricted airspace unless it complies with the prescribed restrictions.  
The prohibited airspace and restricted airspace mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be designated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　民用航空器不得飞越城市上空；但是，有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）起飞、降落或者指定的航路所必需；  
（二）飞行高度足以使该航空器在发生紧急情况时离开城市上空，而不致危及地面上的人员、财产安全；  
（三）按照国家规定的程序获得批准。

**Article 83** A civil aircraft shall not fly over the urban area, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It is necessary for takeoff, landing, or the designated airway;  
(2) The flight altitude is sufficient to enable the aircraft to leave the urban area in an emergency without endangering the safety of persons or property on the ground;  
(3) Approval has been obtained in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　飞行中，民用航空器不得投掷物品；但是，有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）飞行安全所必需；  
（二）执行救助任务或者符合社会公共利益的其他飞行任务所必需。

**Article 84** During flight, a civil aircraft shall not drop objects, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It is necessary for flight safety;  
(2) It is necessary for carrying out rescue missions or other flight missions that conform to the public interest.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　民用航空器未经批准不得飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国领空。  
对未经批准正在飞入或者飞出中华人民共和国领空的民用航空器，有关部门有权根据具体情况采取必要措施，予以制止。

**Article 85** A civil aircraft shall not fly into or out of the territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China without approval.  
For a civil aircraft that is flying into or out of the territorial airspace of the People's Republic of China without approval, the relevant department shall have the right to take necessary measures to stop it according to the specific circumstances.

#### 第三节　飞行保障

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　空中交通管制单位应当按照分工为飞行中的航空器提供空中交通服务，包括空中交通管制服务、飞行情报服务和告警服务。  
提供空中交通管制服务，旨在防止民用航空器同其他航空器或者障碍物体相撞，维持空中交通秩序，提高通行效率。  
提供飞行情报服务，旨在提供有助于安全和有效地实施飞行的情报和建议。  
提供告警服务，旨在当民用航空器需要搜寻援救时，通知有关部门，并根据要求协助进行搜寻援救。

**Article 86** An air traffic control unit shall, according to its division of responsibilities, provide air traffic services to aircraft in flight, including air traffic control service, flight information service, and alerting service.  
The provision of air traffic control service aims to prevent collisions between civil aircraft and other aircraft or obstacles, maintain air traffic order, and improve traffic efficiency.  
The provision of flight information service aims to provide information and advice conducive to the safe and efficient conduct of flights.  
The provision of alerting service aims to notify relevant departments when a civil aircraft needs search and rescue assistance and to assist in the search and rescue as required.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　空中交通管制单位发现民用航空器偏离指定航路、迷失航向时，应当迅速采取一切必要措施，使其回归航路。

**Article 87** Where an air traffic control unit finds that a civil aircraft has deviated from the designated airway or lost its heading, it shall promptly take all necessary measures to bring it back to the airway.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　空中交通管制员值勤时间不得超过国务院民用航空主管部门规定的时限。  
空中交通管制员受到酒精类饮料、麻醉剂或者其他药物的影响，损及工作能力的，不得进行空中交通管制工作。

**Article 88** The duty time of an air traffic controller shall not exceed the time limit prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
An air traffic controller whose ability to work is impaired by the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, or other drugs shall not perform air traffic control work.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　空中交通管制单位、公共航空运输企业、从事定期运输的通用航空企业等单位应当采取有效措施，实时掌握民用航空器飞行动态，保障民用航空活动安全。

**Article 89** Air traffic control units, public air transport enterprises, general aviation enterprises engaged in scheduled transport, and other relevant entities shall take effective measures to monitor the flight dynamics of civil aircraft in real time and ensure the safety of civil aviation activities.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　空域内应当设置必要的符合国家规定标准和技术规范的通信、导航、监视、气象设备。  
通信、导航、监视设备应当取得国务院民用航空主管部门颁发的设备型号许可证，具体目录由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。  
通信、导航、监视设备用于民用航空空中交通服务，应当满足开放运行条件；导航设备用于民用航空空中交通服务，还应当向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得开放运行许可。  
按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定需要进行飞行校验验证的通信、导航、监视设备，应当经飞行校验验证合格后，方可开放运行。

**Article 90** Necessary communication, navigation, surveillance, and meteorological equipment that conform to the national standards and technical specifications shall be installed in the airspace.  
Communication, navigation, and surveillance equipment shall obtain the equipment type approval certificate issued by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and the specific catalogue shall be formulated by the said authority.  
Where communication, navigation, and surveillance equipment is used for civil aviation air traffic services, it shall meet the conditions for operation; where navigation equipment is used for civil aviation air traffic services, an application shall also be submitted to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for an operation permit.  
Where flight calibration and verification are required for communication, navigation, and surveillance equipment in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, such equipment may only be put into operation after passing the flight calibration and verification.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　空域内影响飞行安全的自然障碍物体，应当在航图上标明；空域内影响飞行安全的人工障碍物体，应当设置航空障碍灯和标志，并使其保持正常状态。

**Article 91** Natural obstacles affecting flight safety within the airspace shall be marked on aeronautical charts; artificial obstacles affecting flight safety within the airspace shall be equipped with aviation obstacle lights and markings, and shall be maintained in a normal condition.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　在距离航路边界三十公里以内的地带，禁止修建靶场和其他可能影响飞行安全的设施；但是，平射轻武器靶场除外。  
在前款规定地带以外修建固定的或者临时性对空发射场，应当按照国家有关规定获得批准；对空发射场的发射方向，不得与航路交叉。

**Article 92** Within the zone of thirty kilometers from the boundary of an air route, the construction of shooting ranges and other facilities that may affect flight safety is prohibited; however, flat-trajectory light weapon shooting ranges are excluded.  
The construction of fixed or temporary anti-aircraft launching sites outside the zone specified in the preceding paragraph shall be subject to approval in accordance with relevant state regulations; the launch direction of such anti-aircraft launching sites shall not cross the air route.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　在依法划定的民用机场及其净空保护区域外，对可能影响飞行安全的高大建筑物或者设施，应当按照国家有关规定设置航空障碍灯和标志，并使其保持正常状态。

**Article 93** Outside the lawfully designated civil airport and its clearance protection area, for tall buildings or facilities that may affect flight safety, aviation obstacle lights and markings shall be installed in accordance with relevant state regulations, and shall be maintained in a normal condition.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　任何可能影响飞行安全的活动，应当依法获得批准，并采取确保飞行安全的必要措施，方可进行。

**Article 94** Any activity that may affect flight safety shall be approved according to law, and necessary measures to ensure flight safety shall be taken, before such activity may be carried out.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当依法对民用航空无线电台（站）和分配给民用航空系统使用的专用频率实施管理，并制定相关管理规定。  
任何单位或者个人使用的无线电台（站）和其他仪器、装置，不得妨碍民用航空无线电专用频率的正常使用。对民用航空无线电专用频率造成有害干扰的，有关单位或者个人应当迅速排除干扰；未排除干扰前，应当停止使用该无线电台（站）或者其他仪器、装置。  
无线电管理机构应当加强监测，发现民用航空无线电专用频率受到有害干扰的，应当要求有关单位或者个人迅速采取排除干扰等处置措施。

**Article 95** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, according to law, administer the civil aviation radio stations (or stations) and the dedicated frequencies allocated for civil aviation system, and shall formulate relevant administrative regulations.  
No radio station (or stations) or other instruments or devices used by any unit or individual shall interfere with the normal use of civil aviation radio dedicated frequencies. Where harmful interference is caused to civil aviation radio dedicated frequencies, the relevant unit or individual shall promptly eliminate the interference; before the elimination of the interference, the use of such radio station (or stations) or other instruments or devices shall be discontinued.  
The radio regulatory body shall strengthen monitoring; if it discovers that civil aviation radio dedicated frequencies are subjected to harmful interference, it shall require the relevant unit or individual to promptly take measures such as eliminating the interference.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　信息通信企业应当对民用航空电信传输优先提供服务。  
国务院气象主管机构应当向民用航空气象机构提供必要的气象资料和预报产品。

**Article 96** Information and communication enterprises shall provide priority service for civil aviation telecommunication transmission.  
The competent meteorological authority under the State Council shall provide necessary meteorological data and forecast products to civil aviation meteorological agencies.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　民用航空器使用空中交通服务设施应当缴纳使用费、服务费；使用费、服务费的收费标准，由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 97** The user of air traffic service facilities by civil aircraft shall pay usage fees and service fees; the charging standards for usage fees and service fees shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

### 第六章　公共航空运输

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　公共航空运输，是指以营利为目的，使用国务院民用航空主管部门规定的民用航空器运送旅客、行李、邮件或者货物的民用航空活动。

**Article 98** Public air transport means a civil aviation activity carried out for profit, using civil aircraft as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council to transport passengers, baggage, mail, or cargo.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　本章适用于公共航空运输企业使用民用航空器经营的旅客、行李或者货物的运输，包括免费运输。  
本章不适用于使用民用航空器办理的邮件运输。  
对多式联运方式的运输，本章规定适用于其中的航空运输部分。

**Article 99** This Chapter applies to the transport of passengers, baggage, or cargo operated by public air transport enterprises using civil aircraft, including free transport.  
This Chapter does not apply to the transport of mail handled by civil aircraft.  
For transport by multimodal means, the provisions of this Chapter apply to the air transport part thereof.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　本法所称国内航空运输，是指根据当事人订立的航空运输合同，运输的出发地点、约定的经停地点和目的地点均在中华人民共和国境内的运输。  
本法所称国际航空运输，是指根据当事人订立的航空运输合同，无论运输有无间断或者有无转运，运输的出发地点、目的地点或者约定的经停地点之一不在中华人民共和国境内的运输。

**Article 100** For the purposes of this Law, domestic air transport means transport where, according to the air transport contract concluded by the parties, the place of departure, the agreed stopping place, and the place of destination are all within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, international air transport means transport where, according to the air transport contract concluded by the parties, whether the transport is continuous or not or involves a transfer, any one of the place of departure, the place of destination, or an agreed stopping place is not within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　航空运输合同各方认为几个连续的航空运输承运人办理的运输是一项单一业务活动的，无论其形式是以一个合同订立或者数个合同订立，应当视为一项不可分割的运输。

**Article 101** If the parties to an air transport contract consider that the transport performed by several successive air transport carriers constitutes a single operation, regardless of whether it is concluded in the form of a single contract or several contracts, it shall be deemed an indivisible transport.

#### 第二节　公共航空运输企业

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　企业从事公共航空运输，应当向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得公共航空运输企业经营许可证和运行合格证。

**Article 102** An enterprise engaged in public air transport shall apply to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for obtaining a public air transport enterprise operating license and an operation certificate.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　取得公共航空运输企业经营许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）在中国境内设立的企业法人，且法定代表人为中国公民；  
（二）有与其申请经营活动相适应的民用航空器和航空人员；  
（三）控股股东及实际控制人具有良好的财务状况和诚信记录，最近三年无重大违法违规记录；  
（四）从事旅客、行李运输业务的，实缴注册资本不低于人民币六亿元，从事货物、邮件运输业务的，实缴注册资本不低于人民币四亿元；  
（五）高级管理人员具备相应的专业知识、管理经验和能力；  
（六）完成国务院民用航空主管部门规定的筹建程序。

**Article 103** To obtain a public air transport enterprise operating license, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Being an enterprise legal person established within the territory of China, and the legal representative is a Chinese citizen;  
(2) Having civil aircraft and aviation personnel commensurate with the applied business activities;  
(3) The controlling shareholder and actual controller have good financial conditions and integrity records, and have no records of major illegal or irregular conducts in the past three years;  
(4) For engaging in passenger and baggage transport business, the paid-in registered capital shall not be less than RMB 600 million yuan; for engaging in cargo and mail transport business, the paid-in registered capital shall not be less than RMB 400 million yuan;  
(5) Senior officers possess the relevant professional knowledge, management experience, and capabilities;  
(6) Completion of the preparatory procedures prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　取得公共航空运输运行合格证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有实施安全运行所必需的具有相应资格的管理人员、航空人员等专业人员；  
（二）有符合安全运行要求的民用航空器及其他设备、设施和航空活动场所；  
（三）配备实施安全运行所需的手册和其他资料；  
（四）建立包括组织机构、工作流程和规范在内的必要的安全运行管理体系。  
公共航空运输企业应当按照运行许可的规定实施运行。

**Article 104** To obtain a public air transport operation certificate, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Having management personnel, aviation personnel, and other professionals with appropriate qualifications necessary for safe operation;  
(2) Having civil aircraft and other equipment, facilities, and aviation activity sites that meet the requirements for safe operation;  
(3) Having equipped manuals and other materials necessary for implementing safe operation;  
(4) Establishing a necessary safety operation management system including organizational structure, work processes, and standards.  
Public air transport enterprises shall conduct operations in accordance with the provisions of the operation permit.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当自受理公共航空运输企业经营许可证申请之日起六个月内、受理公共航空运输运行合格证申请之日起十二个月内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发相应许可证件；不予批准的，书面告知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 105** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within six months from the date of accepting an application for a public air transport enterprise operating license, and within twelve months from the date of accepting an application for a public air transport operation certificate, make a decision of approval or disapproval. If the decision is to approve, the relevant license shall be issued; if the decision is not to approve, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　公共航空运输企业申请经营定期航班运输（以下简称航班运输）的航线，暂停、终止经营的航线，应当报经国务院民用航空主管部门批准。  
公共航空运输企业经营航班运输，应当公布班期时刻。

**Article 106** The application by a public air transport enterprise to operate a route for scheduled flight transport (hereinafter referred to as flight transport), or to suspend or terminate the operation of a route, shall be submitted to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for approval.  
Public air transport enterprises operating flight transport shall publish flight schedules.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　公共航空运输企业的营业收费项目，由国务院民用航空主管部门确定。  
国内航空运输的运价管理办法，由国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定，报国务院批准后执行。  
国际航空运输运价的制定按照中华人民共和国政府与外国政府签订的协定、协议的规定执行；没有协定、协议的，参照国际航空运输市场价格确定。

**Article 107** The items of business charges of public air transport enterprises shall be determined by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
The measures for the administration of domestic air transport fares shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the competent pricing authority under the State Council, and shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.  
The formulation of international air transport fares shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of agreements and treaties concluded between the government of the People's Republic of China and foreign governments; in the absence of such agreements or treaties, fares shall be determined with reference to the international air transport market prices.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　公共航空运输企业从事不定期运输，应当经国务院民用航空主管部门批准，并不得影响航班运输的正常经营。

**Article 108** A public air transport enterprise engaging in non-scheduled transport shall obtain approval from the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and shall not affect the normal operation of flight transport.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　公共航空运输企业不得运输法律、行政法规禁止运输的物品。  
公共航空运输企业未经国务院民用航空主管部门批准，不得运输作战军火、作战物资。

**Article 109** A public air transport enterprise shall not transport articles prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.  
A public air transport enterprise shall not transport combat munitions or combat materials without the approval of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　公共航空运输企业从事危险品运输，应当遵守国家有关规定，并取得危险品航空运输许可。  
航空运输危险品目录由国务院民用航空主管部门规定并公布。

**Article 110** A public air transport enterprise engaging in the transport of dangerous goods shall comply with relevant state regulations and obtain a dangerous goods air transport permit.  
The catalogue of dangerous goods for air transport shall be prescribed and published by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　公共航空运输企业不得运输拒绝接受安全检查的旅客，不得违反国家有关规定运输未经安全检查的行李。  
公共航空运输企业应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，对承运的货物进行安全检查或者采取其他保证安全的措施。

**Article 111** A public air transport enterprise shall not transport passengers who refuse to undergo security inspection, and shall not, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, transport luggage that has not undergone security inspection.  
A public air transport enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, conduct security inspection on the cargo carried or take other measures to ensure safety.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　公共航空运输企业从事国际航空运输的民用航空器及其所载人员、行李、货物应当接受移民管理、海关等主管部门依法实施的检查、检疫；检查、检疫应当避免不必要的延误。  
有关主管部门依法禁止人员、行李和货物入境、出境或者过境的，应当通知公共航空运输企业。

**Article 112** For civil aircraft engaged in international air transport by a public air transport enterprise and the persons, luggage, and cargo carried thereon, they shall undergo inspections and quarantine conducted by the competent departments such as immigration administration and customs according to law; inspections and quarantine shall avoid unnecessary delay.  
Where the relevant competent department prohibits the entry, exit, or transit of persons, luggage, and cargo according to law, it shall notify the public air transport enterprise.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　公共航空运输企业应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定优先运输邮件。

**Article 113** A public air transport enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, give priority to the transport of mail.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　公共航空运输企业应当就所从事的公共航空运输的旅客、行李、货物以及可能对第三人承担的赔偿责任依法投保责任保险或者取得相应的财务担保。

**Article 114** A public air transport enterprise shall, in respect of passengers, luggage, and cargo carried in the public air transport it engages in, and the compensation liability it may bear to third parties, take out liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial guarantees according to law.

#### 第三节　运输凭证

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　承运人运送旅客，应当出具客票。  
客票应当包括出发地点、目的地点和经停地点以及国务院民用航空主管部门规定的其他内容。

**Article 115** When carrying passengers, the carrier shall issue a passenger ticket.  
The passenger ticket shall include the place of departure, place of destination, and places of stopover, as well as other contents prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　客票是航空旅客运输合同订立和运输合同条件的初步证据。  
旅客未能出示客票、客票不符合规定或者客票遗失，不影响运输合同的存在或者有效。

**Article 116** A passenger ticket is prima facie evidence of the conclusion of the air passenger transport contract and the conditions of the transport contract.  
The passenger's failure to present the passenger ticket, the passenger ticket's non-compliance with the provisions, or the loss of the passenger ticket shall not affect the existence or validity of the transport contract.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　承运人应当就每一件托运行李向旅客出具行李票。  
承运人载运托运行李时，行李票可以包含在客票之内或者与客票相结合。行李票应当包括下列内容：  
（一）出发地点、目的地点和经停地点；  
（二）托运行李的件数和重量；  
（三）需要声明托运行李在目的地点交付时的利益的，注明声明金额。  
行李票是行李托运和运输合同条件的初步证据。  
旅客未能出示行李票、行李票不符合规定或者行李票遗失，不影响运输合同的存在或者有效。

**Article 117** The carrier shall issue a luggage ticket to the passenger for each piece of checked luggage.  
When the carrier carries checked luggage, the luggage ticket may be included in the passenger ticket or combined with the passenger ticket. The luggage ticket shall include the following contents:  
(i) the place of departure, place of destination, and places of stopover;  
(ii) the number and weight of the pieces of checked luggage;  
(iii) where a declaration of interest in delivery at the destination is required, the declared amount.  
The luggage ticket is prima facie evidence of the conditions of the luggage consignment and the transport contract.  
The passenger's failure to present the luggage ticket, the luggage ticket's non-compliance with the provisions, or the loss of the luggage ticket shall not affect the existence or validity of the transport contract.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　货物运输应当出具航空货运单，并经双方签名、盖章后生效。  
托运人未能出示航空货运单、航空货运单不符合规定或者遗失，不影响运输合同的存在或者有效。

**Article 118** For cargo transport, an air waybill shall be issued, and it shall take effect after being signed and sealed by both parties.  
The consignor's failure to present the air waybill, the air waybill's non-compliance with the provisions, or its loss shall not affect the existence or validity of the transport contract.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　托运人应当填写航空货运单，连同货物交给承运人。  
承运人根据托运人的请求填写航空货运单的，在没有相反证据的情况下，应当视为代托运人填写。

**Article 119** The consignor shall fill in the air waybill and deliver it together with the cargo to the carrier.  
Where the carrier fills in the air waybill at the request of the consignor, it shall, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, be deemed to have filled it in on behalf of the consignor.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　航空货运单应当包括的内容由国务院民用航空主管部门规定，至少应当包括以下内容：  
（一）出发地点、目的地点和经停地点；  
（二）货物品名、重量。

**Article 120** The contents to be included in an air waybill shall be prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and shall at least include the following:  
(i) the place of departure, place of destination, and places of stopover;  
(ii) the description and weight of the cargo.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　托运人应当对航空货运单上关于货物的说明和声明的正确性负责。  
因航空货运单上的说明和声明不符合规定、不正确或者不完全，给承运人或者承运人对之负责的其他人造成损失的，托运人应当承担赔偿责任。  
因承运人在航空货运单上填写的说明和声明不符合规定、不正确或者不完全，给托运人或者托运人对之负责的其他人造成损失的，承运人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 121** The consignor shall be responsible for the correctness of the particulars and statements relating to the cargo entered on the air waybill.  
Where, due to the non-compliance, incorrectness, or incompleteness of the particulars and statements entered on the air waybill, loss is caused to the carrier or any other person for whom the carrier is responsible, the consignor shall bear compensation liability.  
Where, due to the non-compliance, incorrectness, or incompleteness of the particulars and statements entered on the air waybill by the carrier, loss is caused to the consignor or any other person for whom the consignor is responsible, the carrier shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　航空货运单是航空货物运输合同订立和运输条件以及承运人接受货物的初步证据。  
航空货运单上关于货物的重量、尺寸、包装和包装件数的说明具有初步证据的效力。除经过承运人和托运人当面查对并在航空货运单上注明经过查对或者书写关于货物的外表情况的说明外，航空货运单上关于货物的数量、体积和情况的说明不能构成不利于承运人的证据。

**Article 122** The air waybill is prima facie evidence of the conclusion of the air cargo transport contract, the conditions of transport, and the receipt of the cargo by the carrier.  
The statements on the air waybill relating to the weight, dimensions, packaging, and number of packages of the cargo have the effect of prima facie evidence. Except for those which have been checked by the carrier and the consignor in each other's presence and noted on the air waybill, or those which relate to the apparent condition of the cargo, the statements on the air waybill relating to the quantity, volume, and condition of the cargo shall not constitute evidence against the carrier.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　托运人在履行航空货物运输合同规定的义务的条件下，有权在出发地机场或者目的地机场将货物提回，或者在途中经停时中止运输，或者在目的地点或者途中要求将货物交给非航空货运单上指定的收货人，或者要求将货物运回出发地机场；但是，托运人不得因行使此种权利而使承运人或者其他托运人遭受损失，并应当偿付由此产生的费用。  
托运人的指示不能执行的，承运人应当立即通知托运人。  
承运人按照托运人的指示处理货物，没有要求托运人出示其所收执的航空货运单，给该航空货运单的合法持有人造成损失的，承运人应当承担责任，但是不妨碍承运人向托运人追偿。  
收货人的权利依照[本法第一百二十四条](#art-124)规定开始时，托运人的权利即告终止；但是，收货人拒绝接受货物，或者承运人无法同收货人联系的，托运人恢复其对货物的处置权。

**Article 123** Subject to the fulfillment of the obligations under the air cargo transport contract, the consignor has the right to retrieve the cargo at the airport of departure or destination, or to stop the cargo in the course of the journey at any stopover, or to require the cargo to be delivered at the place of destination or in the course of the journey to a consignee other than the one named in the air waybill, or to require the cargo to be returned to the airport of departure; provided that the consignor shall not, by exercising such right, cause loss to the carrier or other consignors, and shall reimburse the expenses arising therefrom.  
Where the instructions of the consignor cannot be carried out, the carrier shall immediately notify the consignor.  
Where the carrier handles the cargo in accordance with the consignor's instructions without requiring the consignor to produce the copy of the air waybill held by him, and thereby causes loss to the lawful holder of the air waybill, the carrier shall bear liability, without prejudice to the carrier's right of recourse against the consignor.  
When the right of the consignee begins in accordance with the provisions of Article 124 of this Law, the right of the consignor shall terminate; however, if the consignee refuses to take delivery of the cargo or the carrier cannot contact the consignee, the consignor shall resume his right of disposal over the cargo.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　除[本法第一百二十三条](#art-123)所列情形外，收货人于货物到达目的地点，并在缴付应付款项和履行运输条件后，有权要求承运人交付货物。  
除另有约定外，承运人应当在货物到达后立即通知收货人，收货人应当及时提货。收货人逾期提货的，应当向承运人支付保管费等费用。  
承运人承认货物已经遗失，或者货物在应当到达之日起七日后仍未到达的，收货人有权依据航空货物运输合同向承运人主张权利。  
[本法第一百二十三条](#art-123)和本条的规定，不影响托运人同收货人之间的相互关系，也不影响从托运人或者收货人获得权利的第三人之间的关系。

**Article 124** Except for the circumstances listed in Article 123 of this Law, the consignee shall, upon arrival of the cargo at the place of destination and after paying the amounts due and fulfilling the conditions of transport, be entitled to require the carrier to deliver the cargo.  
Unless otherwise agreed, the carrier shall notify the consignee immediately after the arrival of the cargo, and the consignee shall take delivery promptly. If the consignee fails to take delivery within the time limit, he shall pay the carrier such expenses as storage fees.  
Where the carrier acknowledges the loss of the cargo, or the cargo has not arrived within seven days after the date it should have arrived, the consignee shall be entitled to assert rights against the carrier under the air cargo transport contract.  
The provisions of Article 123 and this Article of this Law shall not affect the relationship between the consignor and the consignee, nor the relationship between third parties whose rights are derived from the consignor or the consignee.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　托运人和收货人在履行航空货物运输合同规定的义务的条件下，无论为本人或者他人的利益，可以以本人的名义分别行使[本法第一百二十三条](#art-123)和第一百二十四条所赋予的权利。  
任何与[本法第一百二十三条](#art-123)和第一百二十四条规定不同的合同条款，应当在航空货运单上载明。

**Article 125** Subject to the fulfillment of the obligations under the air cargo transport contract, the consignor and the consignee may, respectively in their own names, exercise the rights conferred by Article 123 and Article 124 of this Law, whether for their own benefit or for the benefit of others.  
Any contractual clause different from the provisions of Article 123 and Article 124 of this Law shall be stated on the air waybill.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　托运人应当提供必需的资料和文件，以便在货物交付收货人前完成法律、行政法规规定的有关手续；因没有此种资料、文件，或者此种资料、文件不充足或者不符合规定造成的损失，除由于承运人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成的外，托运人应当对承运人承担责任。  
除法律、行政法规另有规定外，承运人没有对前款规定的资料或者文件进行检查的义务。

**Article 126** The consignor shall provide such information and documents as are necessary to complete the relevant formalities prescribed by laws and administrative regulations before the cargo is delivered to the consignee; for loss caused by the absence, insufficiency, or non-compliance of such information or documents, the consignor shall be liable to the carrier, unless such loss is caused by the fault of the carrier or its employees or agents.  
Unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations, the carrier shall have no obligation to check the information or documents referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　客票、行李票、航空货运单等运输凭证应当为书面形式。  
前款所称书面形式，包括合同书、信件、电报、电传、传真以及数据电文等可以有形地表现所载内容的形式。  
承运人以数据电文形式出具运输凭证，旅客、托运人要求提供纸质凭证的，承运人应当提供。

**Article 127** Transport documents such as passenger tickets, luggage tickets, and air waybills shall be in written form.  
The written form referred to in the preceding paragraph includes forms such as contracts, letters, telegrams, telexes, facsimiles, and data messages which can tangibly express the contents contained therein.  
Where the carrier issues transport documents in the form of data messages, and a passenger or consignor requests the provision of paper documents, the carrier shall provide them.

#### 第四节　承运人责任

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　因发生在民用航空器上或者在旅客上、下民用航空器过程中的事故，造成旅客人身伤亡的，承运人应当承担责任；但是，旅客的人身伤亡完全是由于旅客本人的健康状况造成的，承运人不承担责任。

**Article 128** Where personal injury or death of a passenger is caused by an accident occurring on board a civil aircraft or in the course of embarking or disembarking, the carrier shall bear liability; however, if the personal injury or death of the passenger is solely due to the passenger's own health condition, the carrier shall not bear liability.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　因发生在民用航空器上或者在旅客上、下民用航空器过程中的事件，造成旅客非托运行李（包括个人物件）毁灭、遗失或者损坏的，承运人应当对因其过错或者其受雇人、代理人过错造成的损失承担责任。  
因发生在航空运输期间的事件，造成旅客的托运行李毁灭、遗失或者损坏的，承运人应当承担责任。  
旅客行李的毁灭、遗失或者损坏是由于行李本身的自然属性、质量或者缺陷造成的，承运人在此范围内不承担责任。  
本法所称行李，包括托运行李和非托运行李。

**Article 129** Where the destruction, loss, or damage of a passenger's unchecked luggage (including personal effects) is caused by an event occurring on board a civil aircraft or in the course of embarking or disembarking, the carrier shall be liable for loss caused by its fault or the fault of its employees or agents.  
Where the destruction, loss, or damage of a passenger's checked luggage is caused by an event occurring during the air transport period, the carrier shall bear liability.  
If the destruction, loss, or damage of the passenger's luggage is caused by the inherent defect, quality, or defect of the luggage itself, the carrier shall not be liable to the extent thereof.  
The term "luggage" as used in this Law includes checked luggage and unchecked luggage.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　本法所称航空运输期间，是指在机场内、民用航空器上或者机场外降落的任何地点，托运行李、货物处于承运人掌管之下的全部期间。  
航空运输期间不包括机场外的任何陆路运输、海上运输、内河运输过程；但是，此种陆路运输、海上运输、内河运输是为了履行航空运输合同而装载、交付或者转运，在没有相反证据的情况下，所发生的损失视为在航空运输期间发生的损失。承运人未经托运人同意，以其他运输方式代替合同约定采用航空方式的全部或者部分运输的，前述以其他方式履行的运输视为在航空运输期间。

**Article 130** The "air transport period" as used in this Law means the entire period during which checked luggage or cargo is in the custody of the carrier, whether at an airport, on board a civil aircraft, or at any place of landing outside an airport.  
The air transport period does not include any land transport, sea transport, or inland waterway transport outside the airport; however, if such land transport, sea transport, or inland waterway transport is carried out for the purpose of loading, delivery, or transshipment in performance of an air transport contract, any loss occurring during such transport shall, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, be deemed to be loss occurring during the air transport period. Where the carrier replaces all or part of the air transport agreed in the contract with other modes of transport without the consent of the consignor, the transport performed by such other modes shall be deemed to be within the air transport period.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　因发生在航空运输期间的事件，造成货物毁灭、遗失或者损坏的，承运人应当承担责任；但是，承运人证明货物的毁灭、遗失或者损坏完全是由于下列原因之一造成的，不承担责任：  
（一）货物本身的自然属性、质量或者缺陷；  
（二）承运人或者其受雇人、代理人以外的人包装货物并且货物包装不良；  
（三）战争或者武装冲突；  
（四）政府有关部门实施的与货物入境、出境或者过境有关的行为。

**Article 131** The carrier shall be liable for damage caused by events occurring during the carriage by air resulting in destruction, loss or damage to cargo; however, the carrier shall not be liable if it proves that the destruction, loss or damage was solely caused by one of the following reasons:  
(1) the inherent nature, quality or defect of the cargo;  
(2) defective packing of cargo by a person other than the carrier or its servants or agents;  
(3) an act of war or an armed conflict;  
(4) an act of the competent public authority in connection with the entry, exit or transit of the cargo.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　旅客、行李或者货物在航空运输中因延误造成的损失，承运人应当承担责任；但是，承运人证明本人或者其受雇人、代理人为了避免损失的发生，已经采取一切合理措施或者不可能采取此种措施的，不承担责任。

**Article 132** The carrier shall be liable for damage occasioned by delay in the carriage by air of passengers, baggage or cargo; however, the carrier shall not be liable if it proves that it and its servants or agents have taken all reasonable measures to avoid the damage or that it was impossible for them to take such measures.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　在旅客、行李运输中，经承运人证明，损失是由索赔人或者代行权利人的过错造成或者促成的，应当根据造成或者促成此种损失的过错的程度，相应免除或者减轻承运人的责任。旅客以外的其他人就旅客人身伤亡提出赔偿请求时，经承运人证明，人身伤亡是旅客本人的过错造成或者促成的，同样应当根据造成或者促成此种损失的过错的程度，相应免除或者减轻承运人的责任。  
在货物运输中，经承运人证明，损失是由索赔人或者代行权利人的过错造成或者促成的，应当根据造成或者促成此种损失的过错的程度，相应免除或者减轻承运人的责任。

**Article 133** In the carriage of passengers and baggage, if the carrier proves that the damage was caused or contributed to by the fault of the claimant or the person from whom he derives his rights, the carrier shall be wholly or partly exonerated from its liability in accordance with the degree of fault causing or contributing to the damage. When a claim for compensation for personal injury or death of a passenger is brought by a person other than the passenger, if the carrier proves that the personal injury or death was caused or contributed to by the fault of the passenger himself, the carrier shall likewise be wholly or partly exonerated from its liability in accordance with the degree of fault causing or contributing to the damage.  
In the carriage of cargo, if the carrier proves that the damage was caused or contributed to by the fault of the claimant or the person from whom he derives his rights, the carrier shall be wholly or partly exonerated from its liability in accordance with the degree of fault causing or contributing to the damage.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　对于依据[本法第一百二十八条](#art-128)规定所产生的赔偿责任，按照造成损害的事故发生时我国参加的相关国际民用航空公约规定的赔偿责任限额确定。  
对于超过前款规定的赔偿责任限额的损害，承运人证明有下列情形之一的，不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）损失不是由于承运人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成；  
（二）损失完全是由第三人的过错造成。

**Article 134** The liability for damages arising from the provisions of Article 128 of this Law shall be determined in accordance with the limits of liability prescribed by the relevant international civil aviation convention to which China is a party at the time when the accident causing the damage occurred.  
For damage exceeding the limits of liability prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the carrier shall not be liable if it proves any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the damage was not caused by the fault of the carrier or its servants or agents; or  
(2) the damage was caused solely by the fault of a third party.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　在航空运输中，行李、货物毁灭、遗失、损坏，或者旅客、行李和货物延误所造成的损失，承运人的赔偿责任，以造成损害的事件或者延误发生时我国参加的相关国际民用航空公约规定的赔偿责任限额为限。

**Article 135** In the carriage by air, the liability of the carrier for damage resulting from the destruction, loss or damage of baggage or cargo, or from delay in the carriage of passengers, baggage or cargo, shall be limited to the limits of liability prescribed by the relevant international civil aviation convention to which China is a party at the time when the event causing the damage or the delay occurred.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　[本法第一百三十四条](#art-134)第一款和第一百三十五条规定的赔偿责任限额，由国务院民用航空主管部门公布。

**Article 136** The limits of liability prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 134 and Article 135 of this Law shall be published by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　旅客或者托运人在交运托运行李或者货物时，特别声明在目的地点交付时的利益，并在必要时支付附加费的，除承运人证明旅客或者托运人声明的金额高于托运行李或者货物在目的地点交付时的实际利益外，承运人应当在声明金额范围内承担责任。  
托运行李或者货物的一部分或者托运行李、货物中的任何物件毁灭、遗失、损坏或者延误的，用以确定承运人赔偿责任限额的重量，仅为该一包件或者数包件的总重量；但是，因托运行李或者货物的一部分或者托运行李、货物中的任何物件的毁灭、遗失、损坏或者延误，影响同一份行李票或者同一份航空货运单所列其他包件的价值的，确定承运人的赔偿责任限额时，此种包件的总重量也应当考虑在内。

**Article 137** If, at the time when the checked baggage or cargo is handed over to the carrier, the passenger or consignor has made a special declaration of interest in delivery at destination and has paid a supplementary sum if so required, the carrier shall be liable to pay a sum not exceeding the declared sum, unless it proves that the sum declared by the passenger or consignor is greater than the actual interest of the passenger or consignor in delivery at destination.  
In the case of destruction, loss, damage or delay of part of the checked baggage or cargo, or of any object contained therein, the weight taken into consideration in determining the amount to which the carrier's limit of liability is limited shall be only the total weight of the package or packages concerned. However, if the destruction, loss, damage or delay of a part of the checked baggage or cargo, or of an object contained therein, affects the value of other packages covered by the same baggage check or the same air waybill, the total weight of such packages shall also be taken into consideration in determining the limit of liability.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　任何旨在免除本法规定的承运人责任或者降低本法规定的赔偿责任限额的条款，均属无效；但是，此种条款的无效，不影响整个航空运输合同的效力。

**Article 138** Any provision purporting to exonerate the carrier from liability or to fix a lower limit of liability than that provided for in this Law shall be null and void; however, the nullity of such provision shall not affect the validity of the whole contract of carriage by air.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　就航空运输中的损失向承运人的受雇人、代理人提起诉讼时，该受雇人、代理人证明其是在受雇、代理范围内行事的，有权援用承运人依据本法可以援用的有关赔偿责任限制的规定。  
在前款规定情形下，承运人及其受雇人、代理人的赔偿总额不得超过法定的赔偿责任限额。  
经证明，航空运输中的损失是由于承运人的受雇人、代理人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，不适用本条第一款和第二款的规定，但货物运输除外。

**Article 139** When an action is brought against a servant or agent of the carrier in respect of damage suffered in the course of carriage by air, such servant or agent, if he proves that he acted within the scope of his employment or agency, shall be entitled to invoke the provisions concerning limitation of liability which the carrier may invoke in accordance with this Law.  
In the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, the aggregate of the amounts recoverable from the carrier, its servants and its agents shall not exceed the statutory limits of liability.  
If it is proved that the damage suffered in the course of carriage by air resulted from an act or omission of the servant or agent of the carrier done with intent to cause damage or recklessly and with knowledge that damage would probably result, the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply, except in the case of carriage of cargo.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　经证明，航空运输中的损失是由于承运人或者其受雇人、代理人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，承运人无权援用本法有关旅客延误以及行李运输赔偿责任限制的规定。  
证明承运人的受雇人、代理人有此种作为或者不作为的，还应当证明该受雇人、代理人是在受雇、代理范围内行事。

**Article 140** If it is proved that the damage suffered in the course of carriage by air resulted from an act or omission of the carrier or its servants or agents done with intent to cause damage or recklessly and with knowledge that damage would probably result, the carrier shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of this Law concerning limitation of liability for delay of passengers and for carriage of baggage.  
To prove that a servant or agent of the carrier has done such act or omission, it shall also be proved that the servant or agent acted within the scope of his employment or agency.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　有关航空运输中发生的损失的诉讼，不论其根据如何，只能依照本法规定的条件和赔偿责任限额提出，但是不妨碍谁有权提起诉讼以及他们各自的权利。

**Article 141** Any action for damages suffered in the course of carriage by air, whatever its basis, can only be brought subject to the conditions and limits of liability set out in this Law, without prejudice to the question as to who are the persons who have the right to bring suit and what are their respective rights.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　旅客或者收货人收受托运行李或者货物而未提出异议，为托运行李或者货物已经完好交付并与运输凭证相符的初步证据。  
托运行李或者货物发生损失的，旅客或者收货人应当在发现损失后向承运人提出异议。托运行李发生损失的，至迟应当自收到托运行李之日起七日内提出；货物发生损失的，至迟应当自收到货物之日起十四日内提出。托运行李或者货物发生延误的，至迟应当自托运行李或者货物交付旅客或者收货人处置之日起二十一日内提出。  
任何异议均应当在前款规定的期间内以书面形式提出。  
除承运人有欺诈行为外，旅客或者收货人未在本条第二款规定的期间内提出异议的，不能向承运人提出索赔诉讼。

**Article 142** The receipt by the passenger or consignee of checked baggage or cargo without complaint constitutes prima facie evidence that the same has been delivered in good condition and in accordance with the transport document.  
In case of damage to checked baggage or cargo, the passenger or consignee shall complain to the carrier. In the case of checked baggage, the complaint must be made within seven days from the date of receipt thereof; in the case of cargo, the complaint must be made within fourteen days from the date of receipt thereof. In case of delay, the complaint must be made within twenty-one days from the date on which the checked baggage or cargo was placed at the disposal of the passenger or consignee.  
Every complaint must be made in writing within the period prescribed in the preceding paragraph.  
Unless there is fraud on the part of the carrier, no action for damages may be brought against the carrier if the passenger or consignee fails to make a complaint within the period prescribed in paragraph 2 of this Article.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　航空运输的诉讼时效期间为二年，自民用航空器到达目的地点、应当到达目的地点或者运输终止之日起计算。

**Article 143** The period of limitation of action for carriage by air shall be two years, computed from the date of arrival at the destination, or from the date on which the aircraft ought to have arrived, or from the date on which the carriage stopped.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　由几个航空承运人办理的连续运输，接受旅客、行李或者货物的每一个承运人应当受本法规定的约束，并就其根据合同办理的运输区段作为运输合同的订约一方。  
对前款规定的连续运输，除合同明文约定第一承运人应当对全程运输承担责任外，旅客或者任何行使其索赔权利的人只能对发生事故或者延误的运输区段的承运人提起诉讼。  
行李或者货物的毁灭、遗失、损坏或者延误，旅客或者托运人有权对第一承运人提起诉讼，旅客或者收货人有权对最后承运人提起诉讼，旅客、托运人和收货人均可以对发生毁灭、遗失、损坏或者延误的运输区段的承运人提起诉讼。上述承运人应当对旅客、托运人或者收货人承担连带责任。

**Article 144** In the case of successive carriage to be performed by several successive air carriers, each carrier that accepts passengers, baggage or cargo shall be subject to the provisions of this Law and shall be deemed to be one of the contracting parties to the contract of carriage insofar as the contract deals with that part of the carriage which is performed under its supervision.  
In the case of successive carriage provided in the preceding paragraph, unless the contract expressly provides that the first carrier shall be liable for the whole carriage, the passenger or any person entitled to claim in his name may bring an action only against the carrier who performed the carriage during which the accident or delay occurred.  
In the case of destruction, loss, damage or delay of baggage or cargo, the passenger or consignor may bring an action against the first carrier, and the passenger or consignee may bring an action against the last carrier, and the passenger, consignor and consignee may each bring an action against the carrier who performed the carriage during which the destruction, loss, damage or delay occurred. The aforesaid carriers shall bear joint and several liability to the passenger, consignor or consignee.

#### 第五节　实际承运人履行航空运输的特别规定

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　本法所称缔约承运人，是指以本人名义与旅客或者托运人，或者与旅客或者托运人的代理人，订立本章调整的航空运输合同的人。  
本法所称实际承运人，是指根据缔约承运人的授权，履行前款全部或者部分运输，并且不是从事本法规定的连续运输的人；在没有相反证据的情况下，此种授权被认为是存在的。

**Article 145** For the purposes of this Law, "contracting carrier" means a person who enters into a contract of carriage by air governed by this Chapter with a passenger or consignor, or with an agent of the passenger or consignor, in his own name.  
For the purposes of this Law, "actual carrier" means a person who, by virtue of authorization from the contracting carrier, performs the whole or part of the carriage referred to in the preceding paragraph, and who is not a person performing successive carriage within the meaning of this Law; in the absence of evidence to the contrary, such authorization shall be presumed to exist.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　除本节另有规定外，缔约承运人和实际承运人都应当受本章规定的约束。缔约承运人应当对合同约定的全部运输负责。实际承运人应当对其履行的运输负责。

**Article 146** Unless otherwise provided in this Section, both the contracting carrier and the actual carrier shall be subject to the provisions of this Chapter. The contracting carrier shall be responsible for the whole of the carriage contemplated in the contract. The actual carrier shall be responsible for the carriage performed by him.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　实际承运人的作为和不作为，实际承运人的受雇人、代理人在受雇、代理范围内实施的与实际承运人履行运输义务有关的行为，视为缔约承运人的行为，相应法律后果由缔约承运人承担。  
缔约承运人的作为和不作为，缔约承运人的受雇人、代理人在受雇、代理范围内实施的与实际承运人履行运输义务有关的行为，视为实际承运人的行为，相应法律后果由实际承运人承担；但是，实际承运人不因缔约承运人及其受雇人、代理人的行为而承担超过法定赔偿责任限额的责任。  
任何有关缔约承运人承担本章未规定的义务或者放弃本章赋予的权利的特别协议，或者任何有关依照[本法第一百三十七条](#art-137)规定所作的在目的地点交付时利益的特别声明，除经实际承运人同意外，均不得影响实际承运人。

**Article 147** The acts and omissions of the actual carrier, and those of its servants or agents acting within the scope of their employment or agency, which relate to the performance of the actual carrier's obligation to carry, shall be deemed to be also those of the contracting carrier, and the corresponding legal consequences shall be borne by the contracting carrier.  
The acts and omissions of the contracting carrier, and those of its servants or agents acting within the scope of their employment or agency, which relate to the performance of the actual carrier's obligation to carry, shall be deemed to be also those of the actual carrier, and the corresponding legal consequences shall be borne by the actual carrier; however, the actual carrier shall not be liable for the acts of the contracting carrier or its servants or agents in excess of the statutory limits of liability.  
Any special agreement under which the contracting carrier assumes obligations not imposed by this Chapter or waives rights conferred by this Chapter, or any special declaration of interest in delivery at destination made in accordance with Article 137 of this Law, shall not affect the actual carrier unless agreed to by him.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　依照本章规定提出的异议或者发出的指示，无论是向缔约承运人还是向实际承运人提出或者发出的，具有同等效力；但是，[本法第一百二十三条](#art-123)规定的指示，仅在向缔约承运人发出时有效。

**Article 148** Any complaint or instruction made or given in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, whether addressed to the contracting carrier or to the actual carrier, shall have the same effect; however, instructions provided for in Article 123 of this Law shall be effective only when addressed to the contracting carrier.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　实际承运人的受雇人、代理人或者缔约承运人的受雇人、代理人，证明其是在受雇、代理范围内行事的，就实际承运人履行的运输而言，有权援用[本法第一百三十四条](#art-134)、第一百三十五条有关赔偿责任限制的规定，但是依照本法规定不得援用赔偿责任限制规定的除外。

**Article 149** In relation to the carriage performed by the actual carrier, any servant or agent of the actual carrier or any servant or agent of the contracting carrier, if he proves that he acted within the scope of his employment or agency, shall be entitled to invoke the provisions of Articles 134 and 135 of this Law concerning limitation of liability, unless, according to the provisions of this Law, he is not entitled to invoke the limitation of liability.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　对于实际承运人履行的运输，实际承运人、缔约承运人以及他们的在受雇、代理范围内行事的受雇人、代理人的赔偿总额，不得超过依照本法得以从缔约承运人或者实际承运人获得赔偿的最高数额；同时，其中任何人都不承担超过对其适用的赔偿责任限额。

**Article 150** In respect of the carriage performed by the actual carrier, the total amount of compensation due from the actual carrier, the contracting carrier and their servants or agents acting within the scope of their employment or agency shall not exceed the highest amount which may be claimed against the contracting carrier or the actual carrier under this Law; at the same time, none of them shall be liable for a sum exceeding the limit of liability applicable to him.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　对实际承运人履行的运输提起的诉讼，可以分别对实际承运人或者缔约承运人提起，也可以同时对实际承运人和缔约承运人提起；被提起诉讼的承运人有权要求另一承运人参加诉讼。  
除前款规定外，本节规定不影响实际承运人和缔约承运人之间的权利、义务。

**Article 151** An action brought in respect of the carriage performed by the actual carrier may be brought against the actual carrier or the contracting carrier, or against both the actual carrier and the contracting carrier; the carrier against whom the action is brought shall have the right to require the other carrier to participate in the proceedings.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, the provisions of this Section shall not affect the rights and obligations between the actual carrier and the contracting carrier.

#### 第六节　旅客权益保护

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人应当以保证飞行安全和航班正常，提供良好服务为准则，采取有效措施，提高运输服务质量。  
公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人应当加强教育培训，要求本企业职工严格履行职责，以文明礼貌、热情周到的服务态度，认真做好旅客运输的各项服务工作。

**Article 152** Public air transport enterprises and transport airport operators shall take flight safety and flight punctuality, and the provision of good services as their criteria, adopt effective measures, and improve the quality of transport services.  
Public air transport enterprises and transport airport operators shall strengthen education and training, require their employees to strictly perform their duties, and conscientiously perform all services for passenger transport in a manner of civilized politeness and warm and thoughtful service attitude.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　公共航空运输企业制定运输总条件等格式条款，应当遵循公平原则确定当事人之间的权利和义务，予以公布，并在售票等环节中明确告知旅客。  
运输总条件等格式条款是民用航空运输合同的组成部分，应当包括客票销售、退票与变更、乘机、行李运输、不正常航班处置、旅客服务和投诉等旅客权益保护相关内容。  
运输总条件等格式条款应当报国务院民用航空主管部门备案。

**Article 153** When formulating standard terms such as general conditions of carriage, public air transport enterprises shall determine the rights and obligations between the parties in accordance with the principle of fairness, publish them, and clearly inform passengers at the ticket sales stage and other stages.  
Standard terms such as general conditions of carriage shall be an integral part of civil air transport contracts and shall include relevant content for the protection of passengers' rights and interests, such as ticket sales, refund and change, boarding, baggage transport, handling of irregular flights, passenger services and complaints.  
Standard terms such as general conditions of carriage shall be filed with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人应当合理安排运力、调配资源，加强对设施设备的检查和维护，减少因自身原因导致的航班延误或者取消。  
航班延误或者取消的，公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人应当按照国家有关规定及时、准确发布信息通告，告知旅客延误或者取消原因以及航班动态，并按照规定和合同约定做好客票变更、食宿安排等旅客服务工作。  
发生大面积航班延误时，运输机场运营人应当加强组织协调，及时疏散旅客。

**Article 154** Public air transport enterprises and transport airport operators shall reasonably arrange transport capacity and allocate resources, strengthen the inspection and maintenance of facilities and equipment, and reduce flight delays or cancellations caused by their own reasons.  
If a flight is delayed or cancelled, the public air transport enterprise and transport airport operator shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, timely and accurately issue information notices, inform passengers of the reasons for the delay or cancellation and the flight status, and properly perform passenger services such as ticket change and accommodation arrangements in accordance with the provisions and the contract.  
In the event of large-scale flight delays, the transport airport operator shall strengthen organization and coordination, and timely evacuate passengers.

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　公共航空运输企业、航空销售代理人、航空销售网络平台经营者、航空信息企业等参与民用航空运输服务的单位应当按照个人信息保护有关法律、行政法规的规定，履行个人信息保护义务。

**Article 155** Public air transport enterprises, air sales agents, air sales network platform operators, air information enterprises and other units participating in civil air transport services shall perform their obligations of personal information protection in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on personal information protection.

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条　公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人、航空销售网络平台经营者应当以适当方式向社会公布投诉受理方式等信息，按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定及时受理、处理投诉，并及时向投诉人反馈相关情况。

**Article 156** Public air transport enterprises, transport airport operators, and air sales network platform operators shall announce to the public by appropriate means the complaint acceptance methods and other information, timely accept and handle complaints in accordance with the provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and timely give feedback to the complainants on the relevant situation.

### 第七章　通用航空

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条　通用航空，是指使用民用航空器从事公共航空运输以外的民用航空活动。

**Article 157** General aviation refers to civil aviation activities conducted with civil aircraft other than public air transport.

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条　从事经营性通用航空活动的，应当具备下列条件，并向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得通用航空运营许可证：  
（一）在中国境内设立的企业法人，且法定代表人为中国公民；  
（二）有与所从事通用航空活动相适应的、符合安全运营要求的民用航空器、人员、设施设备、运营场地、手册和其他资料；从事通用航空定期载客运输的，实缴注册资本不低于人民币三千万元；  
（三）有健全的保障安全运营的管理制度；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
取得通用航空运营许可证的企业，应当在运营许可规定的范围内开展运营。  
从事非经营性通用航空活动的，应当向国务院民用航空主管部门备案。

**Article 158** Any person engaged in commercial general aviation activities shall meet the following conditions and apply to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for a general aviation operation license:  
(1) It is a legal person enterprise established within the territory of China, and its legal representative is a Chinese citizen;  
(2) It has civil aircraft, personnel, facilities and equipment, operational sites, manuals, and other materials that are adapted to the general aviation activities to be conducted and meet the requirements for safe operation; where scheduled passenger transport by general aviation is conducted, the paid-in registered capital shall not be less than RMB 30 million;  
(3) It has a sound management system for ensuring safe operation;  
(4) Other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
An enterprise that has obtained a general aviation operation license shall conduct operations within the scope specified in the operation license.  
Any person engaged in non-commercial general aviation activities shall file a record with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条　通用航空企业从事经营性通用航空活动，应当与用户订立书面合同，但是紧急情况下的应急救援飞行除外。

**Article 159** A general aviation enterprise engaging in commercial general aviation activities shall enter into a written contract with the user, except for emergency rescue flights in urgent situations.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条　组织实施通用航空飞行时，应当采取有效措施，保证飞行安全，保护环境和生态平衡，防止对环境、居民、作物或者牲畜等造成损害。

**Article 160** When organizing and implementing general aviation flights, effective measures shall be taken to ensure flight safety, protect the environment and ecological balance, and prevent damage to the environment, residents, crops, or livestock, among others.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条　从事通用航空活动的，应当投保第三人责任险。  
从事通用航空定期运输的，还应当就所从事的定期运输的旅客、行李、货物承担的赔偿责任依法投保责任保险或者取得相应的财务担保。

**Article 161** Any person engaged in general aviation activities shall take out third-party liability insurance.  
Any person engaged in scheduled transport by general aviation shall also, in accordance with law, take out liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial security for the compensation liability incurred to passengers, baggage, and cargo in the scheduled transport.

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条　从事通用航空定期运输的，还应当遵守本法第六章关于公共航空运输的有关规定。  
通用航空活动根据业务类型实施分类管理，具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 162** Any person engaged in scheduled transport by general aviation shall also comply with the relevant provisions of this Law in Chapter VI concerning public air transport.  
General aviation activities shall be subject to classified management based on business type, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

### 第八章　民用航空安全保卫

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条　国务院民用航空主管部门依照本法和有关法律、行政法规的规定负责民用航空安全保卫管理工作，国务院公安部门依照本法和有关法律、行政法规的规定负责民用航空安全保卫管理有关工作，其他有关部门和单位应当密切配合、协调联动，按照职责共同维护民用航空安全和秩序。  
国家对民用航空安全保卫管理职责作出调整的，依照其规定。

**Article 163** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of civil aviation security protection in accordance with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations; the public security department of the State Council shall be responsible for the relevant work of civil aviation security protection administration in accordance with this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations; other relevant departments and entities shall closely cooperate and coordinate, and jointly maintain civil aviation safety and order in accordance with their respective duties.  
Where the State makes adjustments to the responsibilities for civil aviation security protection administration, the adjustments shall be followed.

<a id="art-164"></a>第一百六十四条　有关公安机关应当依法维护民用机场内的安全和秩序，预防、制止和惩治危害民用航空运输活动的违法犯罪行为。

**Article 164** The relevant public security organs shall, in accordance with law, maintain safety and order within civil airports, and prevent, stop, and punish illegal and criminal acts that endanger civil aviation transport activities.

<a id="art-165"></a>第一百六十五条　旅客、行李和货物应当接受安全检查；但是，国务院规定免检的除外。  
公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人设立的民用航空安全检查机构对于存在安全风险嫌疑的行李和货物，可以在旅客、托运人或者其代理人不在场的情况下实施开包检查。不在场开包检查应当全程录像并在检查后书面告知，检查中发现的违禁物品和危险物品按照规定程序处理。  
未经安全检查、不按照规定接受安全检查、经检查无法排除安全风险嫌疑的旅客、行李和货物不得进入民用机场控制区。  
民用航空安全检查程序、标准由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 165** Passengers, baggage, and cargo shall be subject to security inspection, except where the State Council prescribes exemption.  
The civil aviation security inspection agencies established by public air transport enterprises or transport airport operators may, when there is suspicion of security risks in baggage and cargo, conduct unpacking inspections in the absence of the passengers, consignors, or their agents. Unpacking inspections conducted in the absence of the parties shall be fully videotaped, and the parties shall be notified in writing after the inspection. Any prohibited articles and dangerous articles discovered during the inspection shall be handled in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
Passengers, baggage, and cargo that have not undergone security inspection, have not accepted security inspection in accordance with the regulations, or have not had their security risk suspicion eliminated after inspection, shall not enter the controlled area of a civil airport.  
The procedures and standards for civil aviation security inspection shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-166"></a>第一百六十六条　民用航空安全检查应当使用经国务院民用航空主管部门许可的安全检查仪器设备。  
民用航空安全检查人员应当取得国家职业资格证书。

**Article 166** Civil aviation security inspections shall use security inspection instruments and equipment that have been permitted by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
Civil aviation security inspection personnel shall obtain national professional qualification certificates.

<a id="art-167"></a>第一百六十七条　公共航空运输企业、运输机场运营人、民用航空空中交通管制单位等应当按照国家民用航空安全保卫方案，制定本单位的安全保卫方案并报国务院民用航空主管部门备案。

**Article 167** Public air transport enterprises, transport airport operators, civil air traffic control units, and others shall formulate their own security programs in accordance with the national civil aviation security program and report them to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-168"></a>第一百六十八条　民用机场运营人应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，采取措施保障机场内人员和财产的安全。

**Article 168** Civil airport operators shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, take measures to ensure the safety of persons and property within the airport.

<a id="art-169"></a>第一百六十九条　航空人员、民用航空安全保卫人员以及其他需要进入民用机场控制区的相关人员应当依法接受安全背景审查。

**Article 169** Aircrew, civil aviation security personnel, and other relevant persons who need to enter the civil airport controlled area shall undergo security background checks according to law.

<a id="art-170"></a>第一百七十条　机长和机组其他人员，应当严格履行安全保卫职责，维护民用航空器内秩序，保护民用航空器及其所载人员、财产的安全。

**Article 170** The pilot-in-command and other crew members shall strictly perform their security duties, maintain order on board the civil aircraft, and protect the safety of the civil aircraft and the persons and property carried therein.

<a id="art-171"></a>第一百七十一条　禁止实施下列妨害民用航空运输安全和秩序的行为：  
（一）盗窃、故意损坏、擅自移动民用航空器设备或者机场内其他民用航空设施设备，擅自开启民用航空器应急舱门，或者违规进入民用航空器驾驶舱；  
（二）破坏用于民用航空用途的信息和通信技术系统或者用于空中交通管制的设施设备；  
（三）强占民用航空器内座位、行李架，堵塞、强占值机柜台、安检通道或者登机口；  
（四）妨碍或者煽动他人妨碍机组人员、安检员履行职责；  
（五）辱骂、殴打机组人员、地面工作人员；  
（六）非法进入、滞留、拦截民用航空器，或者在民用航空器内打架斗殴、寻衅滋事；  
（七）在民用航空器内使用火种、吸烟（包括电子烟），或者违反规定使用可能影响民用航空安全的电子设备；  
（八）非法进入民用机场防护围栏、损毁安全防护设施；  
（九）无机场控制区通行证或者冒用、伪造、变造机场控制区通行证进入民用机场控制区；  
（十）以散布关于民用航空安全的谣言，扬言实施放火、爆炸等危险行为，或者投放虚假的爆炸性、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性物质或者传染病病原体等危险物质等方式，扰乱民用航空运行秩序；  
（十一）违反规定随身携带、托运禁止、限制运输的物品乘坐民用航空器，或者在普通货物、邮件中夹带危险物品；  
（十二）法律、行政法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 171** The following acts that endanger civil aviation transport safety and order are prohibited:  
(1) Stealing, intentionally damaging, or unauthorized moving of civil aircraft equipment or other civil aviation facilities and equipment within the airport, unauthorized opening of an aircraft emergency exit door, or illegally entering the cockpit of a civil aircraft;  
(2) Destroying information and communication technology systems used for civil aviation purposes or facilities and equipment used for air traffic control;  
(3) Forcibly occupying seats or luggage racks on a civil aircraft, blocking or forcibly occupying check-in counters, security check passages, or boarding gates;  
(4) Obstructing or inciting others to obstruct crew members or security inspectors from performing their duties;  
(5) Abusing or assaulting crew members or ground staff;  
(6) Illegally entering, remaining in, or intercepting a civil aircraft, or fighting, quarreling, or creating disturbances on a civil aircraft;  
(7) Using fire, smoking (including e-cigarettes) on a civil aircraft, or using electronic devices that may affect civil aviation safety in violation of regulations;  
(8) Illegally entering the protective fence of a civil airport or damaging safety protection facilities;  
(9) Entering a civil airport controlled area without an airport controlled area pass, or by using a falsely obtained, forged, or altered pass;  
(10) Disrupting the order of civil aviation operations by spreading rumors about civil aviation safety, threatening to commit dangerous acts such as arson or explosion, or releasing false dangerous substances such as explosive, toxic, radioactive, corrosive substances or pathogens of infectious diseases;  
(11) Carrying or checking in prohibited or restricted items on a civil aircraft in violation of regulations, or concealing dangerous goods in ordinary cargo or mail;  
(12) Other acts prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-172"></a>第一百七十二条　通用航空活动安全保卫工作应当根据通用航空活动类型，在安全保卫机构、方案、措施等方面实施分类分级管理。具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 172** Security work for general aviation activities shall implement classified and graded management in terms of security institutions, programs, and measures according to the type of general aviation activities. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

### 第九章　搜寻援救和事故调查

<a id="art-173"></a>第一百七十三条　国务院有关部门、空中交通管制单位、航空运输企业、民用机场运营人及其他有关单位应当依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，制定民用航空器事故应急预案，并定期开展应急演练。

**Article 173** The relevant departments of the State Council, air traffic control units, air transport enterprises, civil airport operators, and other relevant entities shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, formulate emergency response plans for civil aircraft accidents and conduct regular emergency drills.

<a id="art-174"></a>第一百七十四条　民用航空器遇到紧急情况时，应当发送信号，并向空中交通管制单位报告，提出援救请求；空中交通管制单位应当立即通知搜寻援救协调中心。民用航空器在海上遇到紧急情况时，还应当向船舶和国家海上搜寻援救组织发送信号。

**Article 174** When a civil aircraft encounters an emergency, it shall send signals, report to the air traffic control unit, and request rescue; the air traffic control unit shall immediately notify the search and rescue coordination center. When a civil aircraft encounters an emergency at sea, it shall also send signals to ships and the national maritime search and rescue organization.

<a id="art-175"></a>第一百七十五条　发现民用航空器遇到紧急情况或者收听到民用航空器遇到紧急情况的信号的单位或者个人，应当立即通知有关的搜寻援救协调中心、海上搜寻援救组织或者当地人民政府。

**Article 175** Any unit or individual that discovers a civil aircraft in an emergency or hears signals indicating that a civil aircraft is in an emergency shall immediately notify the relevant search and rescue coordination center, maritime search and rescue organization, or the local people's government.

<a id="art-176"></a>第一百七十六条　收到通知的搜寻援救协调中心、海上搜寻援救组织和地方人民政府，应当立即组织搜寻援救。  
收到通知的搜寻援救协调中心，应当设法将已经采取的搜寻援救措施通知遇到紧急情况的民用航空器。  
搜寻援救民用航空器的具体办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 176** The search and rescue coordination center, maritime search and rescue organization, and local people's government that have received the notice shall immediately organize search and rescue.  
The search and rescue coordination center that has received the notice shall endeavor to notify the civil aircraft in emergency of the search and rescue measures already taken.  
The specific measures for searching for and rescuing civil aircraft shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-177"></a>第一百七十七条　公共航空运输企业、运输机场，以及从事定期运输的通用航空企业、通用机场应当根据国务院民用航空主管部门的规定，制定民用航空器事故家属援助计划。  
民用航空器事故发生后，国务院有关部门、有关地方人民政府、公共航空运输企业、运输机场、从事定期运输的通用航空企业、通用机场及其他有关单位应当立即启动应急预案，并按照家属援助计划做好事故罹难者、幸存者、失踪者及其家属的援助工作。

**Article 177** Public air transport enterprises, transport airports, and general aviation enterprises and general airports engaged in scheduled transport shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, formulate family assistance plans for civil aircraft accidents.  
After a civil aircraft accident occurs, the relevant departments of the State Council, relevant local people's governments, public air transport enterprises, transport airports, general aviation enterprises engaged in scheduled transport, general airports, and other relevant entities shall immediately activate the emergency response plan and, in accordance with the family assistance plan, provide assistance to the victims, survivors, missing persons, and their families.

<a id="art-178"></a>第一百七十八条　执行搜寻援救任务的单位或者个人，应当尽力抢救民用航空器所载人员，按照规定对民用航空器采取抢救措施并保护现场，保存证据。

**Article 178** Units or individuals performing search and rescue missions shall do their utmost to rescue persons on board the civil aircraft, take rescue measures for the civil aircraft in accordance with regulations, protect the scene, and preserve evidence.

<a id="art-179"></a>第一百七十九条　民用航空器事故根据造成的损害后果分为特别重大事故、重大事故、较大事故和一般事故。事故等级划分的人身伤亡标准依照有关安全生产的法律、行政法规的规定确定；事故等级划分的直接经济损失标准，由国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院有关部门根据民用航空器事故中的特殊情况确定，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 179** Civil aircraft accidents are classified into particularly serious accidents, serious accidents, relatively serious accidents, and ordinary accidents according to the damage consequences. The criteria for personal casualty classification of accident grades shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on safety in production; the criteria for direct economic loss classification of accident grades shall be determined by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council based on the special circumstances of civil aircraft accidents, and shall be published and implemented after approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-180"></a>第一百八十条　特别重大民用航空器事故由国务院或者国务院授权的部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
其他等级的民用航空器事故由国务院民用航空主管部门或者事故发生地地区民用航空管理机构组织事故调查组进行调查，具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 180** Particularly serious civil aircraft accidents shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council.  
Civil aircraft accidents of other grades shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council or the regional civil aviation administration at the place of the accident; the specific measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-181"></a>第一百八十一条　事故调查组可以根据调查需要，依法要求有关单位提供相关记录、设备、资料，询问当事人等有关人员，查阅、检验和留存证据。有关单位和人员应当如实提供现场情况和事故相关信息。  
相关地方人民政府对事故现场实施管理时，应当保障调查组进入现场、获取和使用相关取证资料等权利。

**Article 181** The accident investigation team may, as necessary for the investigation, require relevant units to provide relevant records, equipment, and materials according to law, interrogate parties and other relevant persons, and examine, inspect, and retain evidence. The relevant units and persons shall truthfully provide on-site information and accident-related information.  
When the relevant local people's government manages the accident scene, it shall guarantee the investigation team's rights to enter the scene, obtain and use relevant evidence-gathering materials.

<a id="art-182"></a>第一百八十二条　为了查明民用航空器事故原因开展的技术调查工作应当与其他事故调查工作相互协调。开展民用航空器事故技术调查的具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 182** Technical investigations conducted to identify the causes of civil aircraft accidents shall be coordinated with other accident investigations. The specific measures for conducting technical investigations of civil aircraft accidents shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-183"></a>第一百八十三条　组织事故调查的单位应当自民用航空器事故发生之日起十二个月内依法公布事故调查报告。特殊情况下不能按期公布事故调查报告的，应当及时公布调查进展情况。

**Article 183** The unit organizing the accident investigation shall publish the accident investigation report according to law within twelve months from the date of the civil aircraft accident. If the accident investigation report cannot be published within the time limit under special circumstances, the progress of the investigation shall be published promptly.

<a id="art-184"></a>第一百八十四条　我国参加或者组织的对境外发生的民用航空器事故的调查工作，按照我国缔结或者参加的国际条约的规定执行。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[3]</sup>

**Article 184** Investigations of civil aircraft accidents occurring outside China in which China participates or organizes shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of international treaties concluded or acceded to by China.

### 第十章　对第三人损害的赔偿责任

<a id="art-185"></a>第一百八十五条　因飞行中的民用航空器或者从飞行中的民用航空器上落下的人或者物，造成第三人人身伤亡或者财产损害的，受害人有权获得赔偿；但是，所受损害并非造成损害的事故的直接后果，或者所受损害仅是民用航空器依照国家有关空中交通规则在空中通过造成的，受害人无权要求赔偿。  
前款所称飞行中，是指自民用航空器完成登机或者装货后所有外部舱门均已关闭时起，至其任一此种舱门为下机或者卸货目的开启时止的任何时间；就轻于空气的民用航空器而言，飞行中是指自其离开地面时起至其重新着地时止。

**Article 185** If a civil aircraft in flight or any person or object falling therefrom causes personal injury or property damage to a third party, the injured party shall have the right to compensation; however, if the damage sustained is not a direct consequence of the accident causing the damage, or if the damage sustained is merely caused by the passage of the civil aircraft through the air in conformity with state air traffic rules, the injured party shall not be entitled to claim compensation.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, "in flight" means any time from the moment when all external doors of the civil aircraft are closed after boarding or loading is completed until any such door is opened for disembarkation or unloading; in the case of a lighter-than-air civil aircraft, "in flight" means the period from the time it leaves the ground until it lands again.

<a id="art-186"></a>第一百八十六条　[本法第一百八十五条](#art-185)规定的赔偿责任，由民用航空器运营人承担。  
本法所称民用航空器运营人，是指使用民用航空器的人。民用航空器的使用权已经直接或者间接地授予他人，本人保留对该民用航空器的航行控制权的，本人仍被视为民用航空器运营人。  
民用航空器运营人的受雇人、代理人在受雇、代理过程中使用民用航空器，无论是否在其受雇、代理范围内行事，均视为民用航空器运营人使用民用航空器。  
民用航空器登记的所有人应当被视为民用航空器运营人，并承担民用航空器运营人的责任；除非在判定其责任的诉讼中，所有人证明民用航空器运营人是他人，并在法律程序许可的范围内采取适当措施使该人成为诉讼当事人之一。

**Article 186** The liability for compensation as provided in Article 185 of this Law shall be borne by the operator of the civil aircraft.  
For the purposes of this Law, "operator of a civil aircraft" means the person who uses the civil aircraft. If the right to use the civil aircraft has been granted directly or indirectly to another person while the grantor retains the navigational control of the civil aircraft, the grantor shall still be deemed the operator of the civil aircraft.  
The use of a civil aircraft by an employee or agent of the operator in the course of employment or agency, whether or not within the scope of employment or agency, shall be deemed use by the operator.  
The registered owner of a civil aircraft shall be deemed the operator of the civil aircraft and shall bear the liability of the operator, unless in proceedings to determine his liability he proves that another person is the operator and, within the limits permitted by legal procedures, takes appropriate measures to make that person a party to the proceedings.

<a id="art-187"></a>第一百八十七条　民用航空器所有人、出租人或者保留民用航空器所有权或者持有其担保权益的融资人，或者其受雇人、代理人，不是[本法第一百八十六条](#art-186)规定的民用航空器运营人的，不对第三人所受的损害承担赔偿责任，但是故意造成此种损害的人除外。

**Article 187** Where the owner, lessor, or financier retaining ownership or holding security interests in a civil aircraft, or their employees or agents, are not the operator of the civil aircraft as defined in Article 186 of this Law, they shall not bear compensation liability for damages suffered by a third party, except for those who intentionally cause such damage.

<a id="art-188"></a>第一百八十八条　未经对民用航空器有航行控制权的人同意而使用民用航空器，对第三人造成损害的，有航行控制权的人除证明本人已经适当注意防止此种使用外，应当与该非法使用人承担连带责任。

**Article 188** Where a person uses a civil aircraft without the consent of the person having control over the navigation of the civil aircraft, causing damage to a third party, the person having control over the navigation shall bear joint and several liability with the illegal user, unless the person proves that they have taken due care to prevent such use.

<a id="art-189"></a>第一百八十九条　对于武装冲突或者骚乱直接造成的损害，以及由核事件造成的核损害，依照本章规定应当承担责任的人不承担责任。  
依照本章规定应当承担责任的人在损害发生时对民用航空器的使用权业经国家机关依法剥夺的，不承担责任。

**Article 189** The person who shall bear liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter shall not bear liability for damages directly caused by armed conflict or civil disturbance, nor for nuclear damage caused by a nuclear incident.  
Where the person who shall bear liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, at the time of the occurrence of the damage, has been deprived of the right to use the civil aircraft by a state organ according to law, such person shall not bear liability.

<a id="art-190"></a>第一百九十条　依照本章规定应当承担责任的人证明损害是完全由于受害人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成的，免除其赔偿责任；应当承担责任的人证明损害是部分由于受害人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成的，相应减轻其赔偿责任。但是，损害是由于受害人的受雇人、代理人的过错造成的，受害人证明其受雇人、代理人的行为超出其所授权的范围的，不免除或者不减轻应当承担责任的人的赔偿责任。  
一人对另一人的死亡或者伤害提起诉讼，请求赔偿时，损害是该另一人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成的，适用前款规定。

**Article 190** Where the person who shall bear liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter proves that the damage is entirely caused by the fault of the victim or the victim's employees or agents, such person shall be exempted from compensation liability; where the person who shall bear liability proves that the damage is partially caused by the fault of the victim or the victim's employees or agents, the compensation liability shall be reduced accordingly. However, if the damage is caused by the fault of the victim's employees or agents, and the victim proves that the employees or agents acted beyond the scope of their authority, the person who shall bear liability shall not be exempted or have the liability reduced.  
Where a person brings an action for compensation for the death or injury of another person, and the damage is caused by the fault of such other person or his employees or agents, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-191"></a>第一百九十一条　造成损害的事件涉及两架或者两架以上民用航空器，有关民用航空器运营人应当对第三人所受的损害承担连带责任。

**Article 191** Where an incident causing damage involves two or more civil aircraft, the operators of the relevant civil aircraft shall bear joint and several liability for the damage suffered by a third party.

<a id="art-192"></a>第一百九十二条　[本法第一百八十六条](#art-186)第四款和第一百八十八条规定的人，享有依照本章规定民用航空器运营人所能援用的抗辩权。

**Article 192** The persons specified in Article 186, paragraph 4, and Article 188 of this Law shall enjoy the rights of defense that the operator of a civil aircraft may invoke in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-193"></a>第一百九十三条　本章不妨碍依照本章规定应当对损害承担责任的人向他人追偿的权利。

**Article 193** This Chapter shall not prejudice the right of the person who shall bear liability for damage in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter to recover compensation from others.

<a id="art-194"></a>第一百九十四条　在中华人民共和国境内起飞或者降落的民用航空器，其运营人应当投保第三人责任险或者取得相应的财务担保。

**Article 194** The operator of a civil aircraft that takes off or lands within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall take out third-party liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial guarantees.

<a id="art-195"></a>第一百九十五条　保险人和担保人除享有与民用航空器运营人相同的抗辩权，以及对于伪造证件进行抗辩的权利外，对于依照本章规定提出的赔偿请求只能进行下列抗辩：  
（一）损害发生在保险或者担保终止有效后；然而保险或者担保在飞行中期满的，该项保险或者担保在飞行计划中所载下一次降落前继续有效，但是不得超过二十四小时；  
（二）损害发生在保险或者担保所指定的地区范围外，除非飞行超出该范围是由于不可抗力、援助他人所必需，或者驾驶、航行或者领航上的差错造成的。  
前款关于保险或者担保继续有效的规定，只在对受害人有利时适用。

**Article 195** Except for the same rights of defense as the operator of a civil aircraft, and the right to defend against forged documents, the insurer and guarantor may only raise the following defenses against claims for compensation submitted in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter:  
(1) the damage occurred after the insurance or guarantee has terminated and become ineffective; however, if the insurance or guarantee expires during flight, such insurance or guarantee shall remain effective until the next landing as specified in the flight plan, but not exceeding twenty-four hours;  
(2) the damage occurred outside the geographical scope specified in the insurance or guarantee, unless the flight exceeded such scope due to force majeure, necessity to assist others, or errors in piloting, navigation, or airmanship.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph regarding the continued validity of insurance or guarantee shall apply only when it is favorable to the victim.

<a id="art-196"></a>第一百九十六条　仅在下列情形下，受害人可以直接对保险人或者担保人提起诉讼：  
（一）依据[本法第一百九十五条](#art-195)第一款第一项、第二项规定，保险或者担保继续有效的；  
（二）民用航空器运营人破产的；  
（三）依据有关保险合同或者担保合同的法律规定提起直接诉讼的。  
除[本法第一百九十五条](#art-195)第一款规定的抗辩权，保险人或者担保人对受害人依照本章规定提起的直接诉讼不得以保险或者担保的无效或者追溯力终止为由进行抗辩。

**Article 196** The victim may directly institute an action against the insurer or guarantor only under the following circumstances:  
(1) the insurance or guarantee remains valid in accordance with Article 195, paragraph 1, items (1) and (2) of this Law;  
(2) the operator of the civil aircraft is bankrupt;  
(3) a direct action is instituted in accordance with the legal provisions on the insurance contract or guarantee contract.  
Except for the rights of defense provided in Article 195, paragraph 1 of this Law, the insurer or guarantor shall not defend against a direct action instituted by the victim in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter on the grounds that the insurance or guarantee is void or its retroactive effect has ceased.

<a id="art-197"></a>第一百九十七条　依照[本法第一百九十四条](#art-194)规定提供的保险或者担保，应当被专门指定优先支付本章规定的赔偿。

**Article 197** The insurance or guarantee provided in accordance with Article 194 of this Law shall be specifically designated for the priority payment of compensation under this Chapter.

<a id="art-198"></a>第一百九十八条　保险人应当支付给民用航空器运营人的款项，在本章规定的第三人的赔偿请求未满足前，不受民用航空器运营人的债权人的扣留和处理。

**Article 198** The sums payable by the insurer to the operator of a civil aircraft shall not be subject to attachment or disposal by the creditors of the civil aircraft operator before the claims for compensation of a third party under this Chapter are satisfied.

<a id="art-199"></a>第一百九十九条　第三人损害赔偿的诉讼时效期间为二年，自损害发生之日起计算；但是，在任何情况下，时效期间不得超过自损害发生之日起三年。

**Article 199** The limitation of action for damages to a third party shall be two years, commencing from the date on which the damage occurs; however, in any case, the limitation period shall not exceed three years from the date on which the damage occurs.

<a id="art-200"></a>第二百条　本章所称第三人，是指除民用航空器运营人、旅客、货物的托运人及收货人以外的其他人。  
本章规定不适用于下列损害：  
（一）对飞行中的民用航空器或者对该航空器上的人或者物造成的损害；  
（二）为受害人同民用航空器运营人或者同发生损害时对民用航空器有使用权的人订立的合同所约束，或者为适用两方之间的劳动合同的法律有关职工赔偿的规定所约束的损害。

**Article 200** For the purposes of this Chapter, "third party" means any person other than the operator of the civil aircraft, passengers, consignors of cargo, and consignees.  
The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to the following damage:  
(1) damage caused to a civil aircraft in flight, or to persons or property on board such aircraft;  
(2) damage that is governed by a contract concluded between the victim and the operator of the civil aircraft or the person having the right to use the civil aircraft at the time the damage occurs, or that is governed by the provisions of the law applicable to labor contracts between the two parties concerning workers' compensation.

### 第十一章　对外国民用航空器的特别规定

<a id="art-201"></a>第二百零一条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内从事民用航空活动，适用本章规定；本章没有规定的，适用本法其他有关规定。

**Article 201** Where a foreign civil aircraft engages in civil aviation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply; where this Chapter has no provisions, other relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-202"></a>第二百零二条　外国民用航空器根据其国籍登记国政府与中华人民共和国政府签订的协定、协议的规定，或者经国务院民用航空主管部门批准或者接受，方可飞入、飞出中华人民共和国领空和在中华人民共和国境内飞行、降落。  
对于不符合前款规定，擅自飞入、飞出中华人民共和国领空的外国民用航空器，中华人民共和国有关机关有权采取必要措施，令其在指定的机场降落；对于虽然符合前款规定，但是有合理的根据认为需要对其进行检查的，有关机关有权令其在指定的机场降落。

**Article 202** A foreign civil aircraft may fly into or out of the airspace of the People's Republic of China, and fly and land within the territory of the People's Republic of China, only in accordance with the provisions of agreements or accords concluded between the government of the country of its nationality registration and the government of the People's Republic of China, or with the approval or acceptance of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
For a foreign civil aircraft that flies into or out of the airspace of the People's Republic of China in violation of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the relevant authorities of the People's Republic of China shall have the power to take necessary measures to order it to land at a designated airport; for a foreign civil aircraft that, although complying with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, is reasonably suspected of requiring inspection, the relevant authorities shall have the power to order it to land at a designated airport.

<a id="art-203"></a>第二百零三条　外国民用航空器飞入中华人民共和国领空，其运营人应当提供有关证明书，证明其已经投保第三人责任险或者已经取得相应的财务担保；其运营人未提供有关证明书的，国务院民用航空主管部门有权拒绝其飞入中华人民共和国领空。

**Article 203** When a foreign civil aircraft flies into the airspace of the People's Republic of China, its operator shall provide relevant certificates proving that it has taken out third-party liability insurance or obtained corresponding financial guarantees; if the operator fails to provide such certificates, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall have the power to refuse its entry into the airspace of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-204"></a>第二百零四条　中华人民共和国政府根据与外国政府签订的协定、协议开放航权，允许该外国的民用航空器运营人经营相关国际航班运输。  
外国民用航空器运营人经其本国政府指定，并取得国务院民用航空主管部门颁发的航线经营许可和运行许可，方可经营中华人民共和国政府与该外国政府签订的协定、协议规定的国际航班运输；外国民用航空器运营人经其本国政府批准，并获得国务院民用航空主管部门批准，方可经营中华人民共和国境内一地和境外一地之间的不定期航空运输。  
外国民用航空器运营人应当依照中华人民共和国政府与该外国政府签订的协定、协议以及中华人民共和国法律、行政法规的规定，制定相应的安全保卫方案，报国务院民用航空主管部门备案。

**Article 204** The government of the People's Republic of China opens traffic rights in accordance with agreements or accords concluded with foreign governments, allowing the operators of civil aircraft of such foreign countries to operate relevant international scheduled air services.  
A foreign civil aircraft operator, after being designated by its own government and obtaining the route operation permit and operation permit issued by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, may operate the international scheduled air services provided for in the agreements or accords concluded between the government of the People's Republic of China and the foreign government; a foreign civil aircraft operator, after being approved by its own government and obtaining the approval of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, may operate non-scheduled air transport between a place within the territory of the People's Republic of China and a place outside the territory.  
A foreign civil aircraft operator shall, in accordance with the provisions of the agreements or accords concluded between the government of the People's Republic of China and the foreign government, as well as the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China, formulate corresponding security plans and submit them to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-205"></a>第二百零五条　外国民用航空器运营人制定运输总条件等格式条款的，应当公布中文版本，并设立具备中文能力的专门机构或者指定专人负责受理投诉。

**Article 205** Where a foreign civil aircraft operator formulates standard terms such as general terms and conditions of carriage, it shall publish a Chinese version and establish a specialized institution with Chinese language capability or designate a specific person responsible for handling complaints.

<a id="art-206"></a>第二百零六条　外国民用航空器运营人，不得经营中华人民共和国境内两点之间的航空运输。

**Article 206** A foreign civil aircraft operator shall not operate air transport between two points within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-207"></a>第二百零七条　外国民用航空器应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门批准的班期时刻或者飞行计划飞行；变更班期时刻或者飞行计划的，其运营人应当获得国务院民用航空主管部门的批准；因故变更或者取消飞行的，其运营人应当及时报告国务院民用航空主管部门。  
外国民用航空器未经批准擅自变更班期时刻或者飞行计划的，国务院民用航空主管部门和其他主管机关有权责令改正，并采取必要措施。

**Article 207** Foreign civil aircraft shall fly in accordance with the scheduled timetable or flight plan approved by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council; where the scheduled timetable or flight plan is changed, the operator thereof shall obtain the approval of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council; where a flight is changed or cancelled for any reason, the operator thereof shall promptly report to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.  
Where a foreign civil aircraft changes its scheduled timetable or flight plan without approval, the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council and other competent authorities shall have the power to order it to make corrections and take necessary measures.

<a id="art-208"></a>第二百零八条　外国民用航空器应当在国务院民用航空主管部门指定的国际机场起飞或者降落。

**Article 208** Foreign civil aircraft shall take off or land at the international airport designated by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-209"></a>第二百零九条　国务院民用航空主管部门和其他主管机关，有权在外国民用航空器降落或者起飞时查验[本法第七十八条](#art-78)规定的文件。

**Article 209** The competent civil aviation authority of the State Council and other competent authorities shall have the power to inspect the documents specified in Article 78 of this Law when a foreign civil aircraft lands or takes off.

<a id="art-210"></a>第二百一十条　外国民用航空器国籍登记国颁发或者核准的民用航空器适航证书、机组人员合格证书和执照，中华人民共和国政府承认其有效；但是，颁发或者核准此项证书或者执照的要求，应当等于或者高于国际民用航空组织制定的最低标准。

**Article 210** The Government of the People's Republic of China recognizes the validity of the airworthiness certificate, crew member qualification certificate, and license issued or approved by the State of registry of a foreign civil aircraft; however, the requirements for issuing or approving such certificates or licenses shall be equal to or higher than the minimum standards established by the International Civil Aviation Organization.

<a id="art-211"></a>第二百一十一条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内使用无线电设备，应当遵守中华人民共和国无线电管理有关规定。

**Article 211** Foreign civil aircraft using radio equipment within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with the relevant provisions of the People's Republic of China on radio administration.

<a id="art-212"></a>第二百一十二条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国搜寻援救区内遇险，其所有人或者国籍登记国参加搜寻援救工作，应当经国务院民用航空主管部门批准或者按照两国政府协议进行。

**Article 212** Where a foreign civil aircraft is in distress within the search and rescue zone of the People's Republic of China, the owner or the State of registry thereof participating in the search and rescue work shall obtain the approval of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council or proceed in accordance with an agreement between the two governments.

<a id="art-213"></a>第二百一十三条　外国民用航空器在中华人民共和国境内发生事故，其国籍登记国和其他有关国家可以按照我国缔结或者参加的国际条约的规定参加事故调查。事故调查报告和调查结果，由国务院民用航空主管部门告知该外国民用航空器的国籍登记国和其他有关国家。

**Article 213** Where a foreign civil aircraft has an accident within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the State of registry thereof and other relevant States may participate in the accident investigation in accordance with the provisions of an international treaty concluded or acceded to by China. The accident investigation report and findings shall be notified by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council to the State of registry of such foreign civil aircraft and other relevant States.

### 第十二章　涉外关系的法律适用

<a id="art-214"></a>第二百一十四条　民用航空器所有权的取得、转让和消灭，适用民用航空器国籍登记国法律。

**Article 214** The acquisition, transfer, and extinction of ownership of a civil aircraft shall be governed by the law of the State of registry of the civil aircraft.

<a id="art-215"></a>第二百一十五条　民用航空器抵押权适用民用航空器国籍登记国法律。

**Article 215** The mortgage over a civil aircraft shall be governed by the law of the State of registry of the civil aircraft.

<a id="art-216"></a>第二百一十六条　民用航空器优先权适用受理案件的法院所在地法律。

**Article 216** The priority right over a civil aircraft shall be governed by the law of the place where the court accepting the case is located.

<a id="art-217"></a>第二百一十七条　民用航空运输合同当事人可以选择合同适用的法律，但是法律另有规定的除外；合同当事人没有选择的，适用与合同有最密切联系的国家的法律。

**Article 217** The parties to a civil air transport contract may choose the law applicable to the contract, unless otherwise provided by law; where the parties to the contract have not made a choice, the law of the country most closely connected with the contract shall apply.

<a id="art-218"></a>第二百一十八条　民用航空器对第三人的损害赔偿，适用侵权行为地法律。  
民用航空器在公海上空对第三人的损害赔偿，适用受理案件的法院所在地法律。  
同一国籍的民用航空器发生碰撞的，无论碰撞发生在何地，碰撞航空器之间的损害赔偿适用其国籍登记国法律。

**Article 218** Compensation for damage caused by a civil aircraft to a third party shall be governed by the law of the place where the tortious act occurs.  
Compensation for damage caused by a civil aircraft to a third party over the high seas shall be governed by the law of the place where the court accepting the case is located.  
Where civil aircraft of the same nationality collide, regardless of the place where the collision occurs, compensation for damage between the colliding aircraft shall be governed by the law of their State of registry.

<a id="art-219"></a>第二百一十九条　依照本章规定适用外国法律或者国际惯例，不得危害中华人民共和国主权、安全、发展利益，不得违背中华人民共和国的社会公共利益。

**Article 219** The application of foreign law or international practice in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter shall not jeopardize the sovereignty, security, and development interests of the People's Republic of China, nor shall it contravene the public interest of the People's Republic of China.

### 第十三章　发展促进

<a id="art-220"></a>第二百二十条　国家完善以企业为主体、市场为导向、产学研相结合的民用航空制造业创新体系，支持关键核心技术研发，提高大型飞机和先进发动机等研发设计能力，推进科技创新成果产业化，促进民用航空制造业发展，为民用航空活动提供安全、先进、绿色、经济、适用的民用航空产品。

**Article 220** The State shall improve the civil aircraft manufacturing innovation system with enterprises as the main body, the market as the orientation, and the combination of industry, academia, and research, support the research and development of key core technologies, enhance the research and design capabilities of large aircraft and advanced engines, promote the industrialization of scientific and technological innovation achievements, promote the development of the civil aircraft manufacturing industry, and provide safe, advanced, green, economical, and applicable civil aviation products for civil aviation activities.

<a id="art-221"></a>第二百二十一条　国家完善民用航空器适航审定组织体系和标准规范，加强适航审定人才培养，建立健全基于风险的分类分级适航管理方式，提升适航审定能力和水平。

**Article 221** The State shall improve the organizational system and standards and specifications for civil aircraft airworthiness certification, strengthen the training of airworthiness certification personnel, establish and improve a risk-based, classified, and graded airworthiness management approach, and enhance the capability and level of airworthiness certification.

<a id="art-222"></a>第二百二十二条　民用机场是公共基础设施。各级人民政府应当采取必要措施，支持民用机场的建设、运营和维护。  
国务院有关部门按照建设现代化综合交通运输体系的要求，完善民用机场布局，加快民用航空枢纽建设，统筹运输机场和通用机场协调发展，完善通用机场网络。

**Article 222** Civil airports are public infrastructure. People's governments at all levels shall take necessary measures to support the construction, operation, and maintenance of civil airports.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the requirements of building a modern comprehensive transportation system, improve the layout of civil airports, accelerate the construction of civil aviation hubs, coordinate the development of transport airports and general aviation airports, and improve the general aviation airport network.

<a id="art-223"></a>第二百二十三条　国家采取措施优化航线网络，提高资源配置效率，构建覆盖广泛、优质高效的民用航空服务体系。

**Article 223** The State shall take measures to optimize the route network, improve the efficiency of resource allocation, and build a widely covered, high-quality, and efficient civil aviation service system.

<a id="art-224"></a>第二百二十四条　国家鼓励发展通用航空，加快构建通用航空基础设施网络，培育通用航空市场。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本地区发展实际，采取措施支持通用航空产业发展。

**Article 224** The State shall encourage the development of general aviation, accelerate the construction of a general aviation infrastructure network, and cultivate the general aviation market.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual development of their respective regions, take measures to support the development of the general aviation industry.

<a id="art-225"></a>第二百二十五条　国家采取措施优化低空空域资源配置，推动建设民用低空飞行和应用相关监管服务平台，建立健全适应低空经济发展要求的适航审定、飞行管理等制度和标准。  
国务院有关部门和省级人民政府根据需要制定低空经济相关发展规划，完善支持政策措施，鼓励低空经济领域技术创新和应用拓展，促进低空经济发展。

**Article 225** The State shall take measures to optimize the allocation of low-altitude airspace resources, promote the construction of regulatory service platforms related to civil low-altitude flight and applications, and establish and improve airworthiness certification, flight management, and other systems and standards that meet the requirements of the development of the low-altitude economy.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, as necessary, formulate development plans related to the low-altitude economy, improve support policies and measures, encourage technological innovation and application expansion in the field of the low-altitude economy, and promote the development of the low-altitude economy.

<a id="art-226"></a>第二百二十六条　国家坚持平等互利、合作共赢的方针，促进民用航空领域国际合作，积极参与国际民用航空标准制定，推进民用航空运输市场高水平开放，提升民用航空国际竞争力。

**Article 226** The State shall adhere to the principle of equality, mutual benefit, and win-win cooperation, promote international cooperation in the field of civil aviation, actively participate in the formulation of international civil aviation standards, advance the high-level opening of the civil air transport market, and enhance the international competitiveness of civil aviation.

### 第十四章　监督管理

<a id="art-227"></a>第二百二十七条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当加强对民用航空活动的监督检查，按照分类分级监督管理的要求，根据民用航空活动类别、单位信用记录和民用航空管理需要等因素，合理确定检查频次和检查方式。

**Article 227** The civil aviation authority under the State Council shall strengthen supervision and inspection of civil aviation activities, and shall, in accordance with the requirements of classified and tiered supervision and management, and based on factors such as the category of civil aviation activities, the credit records of entities, and the needs of civil aviation management, reasonably determine the frequency and methods of inspection.

<a id="art-228"></a>第二百二十八条　国务院民用航空主管部门履行监督检查职责，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）询问有关当事人，调查与民用航空活动有关的情况；  
（二）查阅、复制、调取当事人与民用航空活动有关的记录、文件和资料；  
（三）实施现场检查，对涉嫌违法的民用航空活动进行调查；  
（四）检查与民用航空活动有关的证件、设备、场所；  
（五）有证据证明存在危及民用航空安全的活动的，暂扣相关执照、许可证件，经国务院民用航空主管部门负责人或者国务院民用航空主管部门授权的地区民用航空管理机构主要负责人批准，扣押民用航空器等相关设备、查封相关场所；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。  
国务院民用航空主管部门依法履行职责，当事人应当予以协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 228** The civil aviation authority under the State Council may take the following measures when performing its supervision and inspection duties:  
(1) questioning the relevant parties and investigating circumstances related to civil aviation activities;  
(2) consulting, copying, and obtaining records, documents, and materials of the parties related to civil aviation activities;  
(3) conducting on-site inspections and investigating suspected illegal civil aviation activities;  
(4) inspecting certificates, equipment, and premises related to civil aviation activities;  
(5) where there is evidence proving the existence of activities endangering civil aviation safety, temporarily seizing the relevant licenses and permits, and upon approval by the head of the civil aviation authority under the State Council or the principal head of the regional civil aviation management institution authorized by the civil aviation authority under the State Council, seizing civil aircraft and other relevant equipment and sealing up the relevant premises;  
(6) other measures provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where the civil aviation authority under the State Council performs its duties according to law, the parties shall provide assistance and cooperation and shall not refuse or obstruct such performance.

<a id="art-229"></a>第二百二十九条　遇有突发事件、特殊保障条件等情形，必须采取不同于国家有关民用航空器和民用机场运营相关规定的特别措施的，应当经国务院民用航空主管部门批准。

**Article 229** Where emergency events, special support conditions, or other circumstances arise, and special measures that differ from the relevant national provisions on the operation of civil aircraft and civil airports must be taken, approval from the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall be obtained.

<a id="art-230"></a>第二百三十条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当运用大数据等现代信息技术手段，提高监督管理工作能力和水平，并依照有关法律、行政法规的规定做好数据安全保护工作。  
从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位应当按照民用航空统计调查制度等规定，向国务院民用航空主管部门提供相关资料。

**Article 230** The civil aviation authority under the State Council shall use modern information technology means such as big data to improve the capability and standard of supervision and management, and shall carry out data security protection work in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Entities engaged in civil aviation production and operation activities shall, in accordance with the provisions of the civil aviation statistical investigation system and other regulations, provide relevant materials to the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-231"></a>第二百三十一条　从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位应当建立民用航空安全信息收集、分析和发布制度，并按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定报告和发布安全信息。

**Article 231** Entities engaged in civil aviation production and operation activities shall establish a system for the collection, analysis, and publication of civil aviation safety information, and shall report and publish safety information in accordance with the regulations of the civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-232"></a>第二百三十二条　国务院民用航空主管部门、地方各级人民政府及其有关部门，以及从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位应当加强对民用航空法律、法规和安全知识的宣传教育，提高全社会的民用航空安全意识。

**Article 232** The civil aviation authority under the State Council, the local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments, and entities engaged in civil aviation production and operation activities shall strengthen publicity and education on civil aviation laws, regulations, and safety knowledge, and raise the awareness of civil aviation safety throughout society.

### 第十五章　法律责任

<a id="art-233"></a>第二百三十三条　投资民用航空业违反国务院民用航空主管部门有关规定的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，可以吊销公共航空运输企业、通用航空企业、民用机场的相关许可证件。  
外商投资民用航空业违反有关外商投资的法律、行政法规或者国家有关规定的，依照相关法律、行政法规的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 233** Where investment in the civil aviation industry violates the relevant regulations of the civil aviation authority under the State Council, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order rectification within a specified period, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; if rectification is not completed within the specified period, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are particularly serious, the relevant licenses and permits of public air transport enterprises, general aviation enterprises, and civil airports may be revoked.  
Where foreign investment in the civil aviation industry violates the relevant laws, administrative regulations, or relevant state provisions on foreign investment, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-234"></a>第二百三十四条　违反本法规定，未经许可从事民用航空产品和零部件设计、生产、维修活动，民用航空化学产品设计、生产或者民用航空油料供应、测试活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 234** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, engages without permission in the design, production, or maintenance of civil aviation products and parts, or in the design or production of civil aviation chemical products, or in the supply or testing of civil aviation fuel, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-235"></a>第二百三十五条　取得本法规定的设计、生产、维修许可证书，民用航空化学产品设计、生产或者民用航空油料供应、测试相关许可证书的企业，因质量问题造成严重事故的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停业整顿；情节严重的，可以吊销相关许可证件。

**Article 235** Where an enterprise that has obtained the design, production, or maintenance permit certificate as provided for in this Law, or the relevant permit certificate for the design or production of civil aviation chemical products or the supply or testing of civil aviation fuel, causes a serious accident due to quality problems, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order the enterprise to suspend business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant permits and certificates may be revoked.

<a id="art-236"></a>第二百三十六条　违反本法规定，未取得适航证书、特许飞行证书，或者超出证书有效期、规定范围进行飞行活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止飞行，没收违法所得，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以吊销相关许可证件。

**Article 236** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, conducts flight activities without obtaining an airworthiness certificate or a special flight permit, or beyond the validity period or specified scope of such certificates, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the flight, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant licenses and permits may be revoked.

<a id="art-237"></a>第二百三十七条　违反本法规定，未取得相关航空人员执照、证书，或者超出执照、证书载明事项范围从事民用航空活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止民用航空活动，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对超出执照、证书载明事项范围从事民用航空活动的航空人员，可以吊销相关执照或者责令所属单位取消训练合格证书。  
航空人员有前款情形的，对其所属单位处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，可以吊销公共航空运输企业或者通用航空企业的相关许可证件。

**Article 237** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, engages in civil aviation activities without obtaining the relevant aviation personnel license or certificate, or beyond the scope stated in the license or certificate, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the civil aviation activities, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; for aviation personnel who engage in civil aviation activities beyond the scope stated in the license or certificate, the relevant license may be revoked or the entity to which they belong shall be ordered to cancel the training qualification certificate.  
Where an aviation personnel is in the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity to which they belong; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are particularly serious, the relevant licenses and permits of the public air transport enterprise or the general aviation enterprise may be revoked.

<a id="art-238"></a>第二百三十八条　违反本法规定，未经许可为民用航空器驾驶员、维修人员、飞行签派员提供执照和资格训练的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，使用未经鉴定合格的飞行模拟训练设备的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 238** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, provides license and qualification training for civil aircraft pilots, maintenance personnel, or flight dispatchers without permission, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan.  
Where anyone, in violation of this Law, uses flight simulation training equipment that has not been appraised as qualified, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-239"></a>第二百三十九条　违反本法规定，在依法划定的民用机场范围内和民用机场净空保护区域、电磁环境保护区域内从事禁止性活动的，由机场所在地县级以上地方人民政府责令停止违法行为，给予通报批评；情节严重的，对生产经营单位处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对个人处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 239** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, engages in prohibited activities within the legally demarcated scope of a civil airport, the protected area for the obstacle limitation surfaces of a civil airport, or the electromagnetic environment protection zone, the local people's government at or above the county level where the airport is located shall order cessation of the illegal act and issue a circulative notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the production and operation entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual.

<a id="art-240"></a>第二百四十条　违反本法规定，运输机场或者对公众开放的通用机场未取得机场运营许可证开放运营的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止开放运营，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，不对公众开放的通用机场未按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定进行备案的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正，处一万元以上三万元以下的罚款；逾期未改正的，处三万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 240** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, opens a transport airport or a general aviation airport open to the public for operation without obtaining an airport operation permit, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the open operation, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan.  
Where anyone, in violation of this Law, fails to file a general aviation airport not open to the public for record in accordance with the regulations of the civil aviation authority under the State Council, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order rectification within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if rectification is not completed within the specified period, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-241"></a>第二百四十一条　违反本法规定，民用航空器未按照有关规定经空中交通管制单位许可进行飞行活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止飞行，对该民用航空器所有人或者承租人处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，暂扣相关许可证件一个月至三个月，情节严重的，吊销相关许可证件；对机长给予警告，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款，暂扣执照一个月至六个月，情节严重的，吊销执照。

**Article 241** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, conducts flight activities with a civil aircraft without obtaining permission from the air traffic control unit in accordance with relevant regulations, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the flight, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan on the owner or lessee of the civil aircraft, and temporarily seize the relevant permits and certificates for a period of not less than one month but not more than three months; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant permits and certificates shall be revoked; and the pilot-in-command shall be given a warning, with a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan, and the license shall be temporarily seized for a period of not less than one month but not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, the license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-242"></a>第二百四十二条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院民用航空主管部门对民用航空器的机长给予警告，暂扣执照一个月至六个月；情节严重的，吊销执照：  
（一）未对民用航空器实施检查而起飞；  
（二）民用航空器发生事故，未将事故情况及时、如实报告国务院民用航空主管部门；  
（三）民用航空器未按照空中交通管制单位指定的航路和飞行高度飞行；  
（四）民用航空器未遵守国务院民用航空主管部门规定的机场飞行程序和运行标准；  
（五）民用航空器违规飞越城市上空；  
（六）民用航空器在飞行中违规投掷物品。

**Article 242** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, falls under any of the following circumstances, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall give a warning to the pilot-in-command of the civil aircraft, temporarily seize the license for a period of not less than one month but not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, the license shall be revoked:  
(1) taking off without having conducted an inspection of the civil aircraft;  
(2) failing to report the accident situation to the civil aviation authority under the State Council in a timely and truthful manner when an accident occurs involving the civil aircraft;  
(3) the civil aircraft flying not in accordance with the route and flight altitude designated by the air traffic control unit;  
(4) the civil aircraft failing to comply with the airport flight procedures and operation standards prescribed by the civil aviation authority under the State Council;  
(5) the civil aircraft flying over a city in violation of regulations;  
(6) the civil aircraft throwing objects from the aircraft during flight in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-243"></a>第二百四十三条　违反本法规定，民用航空器的机长或者机组其他人员有下列情形之一的，由国务院民用航空主管部门给予警告，暂扣相关执照或者训练合格证书一个月至六个月；情节严重的，吊销相关执照或者责令所属单位取消训练合格证书：  
（一）从事飞行活动时，未携带执照、训练合格证书或者体格检查合格证书；  
（二）民用航空器遇险时未采取必要处置措施，或者擅自离开民用航空器；  
（三）受到酒精类饮料、麻醉剂或者其他药物的影响，损及工作能力时从事飞行活动。  
机组人员违反国务院民用航空主管部门关于飞行时间、值勤时间、休息时间规定的，对公共航空运输企业或者通用航空企业处每次飞行一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；对公共航空运输企业或者通用航空企业直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，处每次飞行一千元以上五千元以下的罚款。

**Article 243** Where the pilot-in-command or other crew members of a civil aircraft, in violation of this Law, fall under any of the following circumstances, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall give a warning and temporarily seize the relevant license or training qualification certificate for a period of not less than one month but not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant license shall be revoked or the entity to which they belong shall be ordered to cancel the training qualification certificate:  
(1) failing to carry the license, training qualification certificate, or physical examination certificate when engaging in flight activities;  
(2) failing to take necessary disposal measures when the civil aircraft is in distress, or leaving the civil aircraft without authorization;  
(3) engaging in flight activities while under the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, or other drugs, which impairs work capacity.  
Where crew members violate the regulations of the civil aviation authority under the State Council on flight time, duty time, and rest time, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan per flight shall be imposed on the public air transport enterprise or the general aviation enterprise; a warning shall be given to the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible of the public air transport enterprise or the general aviation enterprise, and a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5,000 yuan per flight shall be imposed.

<a id="art-244"></a>第二百四十四条　违反本法规定，空中交通管制员值勤时间超过规定时限的，由国务院民用航空主管部门对民用航空空中交通管制单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，空中交通管制员从事空中交通管制工作时，受到酒精类饮料、麻醉剂或者其他药物的影响，损及工作能力的，由国务院民用航空主管部门给予警告，暂扣执照一个月至六个月；情节严重的，吊销执照，对民用航空空中交通管制单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 244** Where an air traffic controller, in violation of this Law, exceeds the prescribed time limit for duty, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan on the civil aviation air traffic control unit.  
Where an air traffic controller, in violation of this Law, engages in air traffic control work while under the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, or other drugs, which impairs work capacity, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall give a warning and temporarily seize the license for a period of not less than one month but not more than six months; if the circumstances are serious, the license shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the civil aviation air traffic control unit.

<a id="art-245"></a>第二百四十五条　违反本法规定，使用未经许可或者未经飞行校验验证合格的通信、导航、监视设备的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 245** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, uses communication, navigation, or surveillance equipment that is not permitted or has not passed flight calibration verification, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order rectification within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-246"></a>第二百四十六条　违反本法规定，运输法律、行政法规禁止运输的物品，或者未经许可从事危险品航空运输活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以吊销违法公共航空运输企业、通用航空企业的相关许可证件。

**Article 246** Where anyone, in violation of this Law, transports items prohibited for transport by laws or administrative regulations, or engages in the air transport of dangerous goods without permission, the civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant licenses and permits of the illegal public air transport enterprise or general aviation enterprise may be revoked.

<a id="art-247"></a>第二百四十七条　违反本法规定，运输机场、公共航空运输企业不依法开展安全检查工作的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，可以吊销违法运输机场、公共航空运输企业的相关许可证件。

**Article 247** : Where a transport airport or a public air transport enterprise violates the provisions of this Law by failing to carry out security inspection work according to law, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan on it, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are especially serious, the relevant licenses of the illegal transport airport or public air transport enterprise may be revoked.

<a id="art-248"></a>第二百四十八条　违反本法规定，未经许可从事公共航空运输的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，未经许可从事经营性通用航空活动的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，从事非经营性通用航空活动未向国务院民用航空主管部门备案的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处三万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 248** : Where a person violates the provisions of this Law by engaging in public air transport without permission, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan.  
  
Where a person violates the provisions of this Law by engaging in operational general aviation activities without permission, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.  
  
Where a person violates the provisions of this Law by engaging in non-operational general aviation activities without filing with the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, the said authority shall order rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-249"></a>第二百四十九条　公共航空运输企业、通用航空企业、民用机场航班运行效率较低、不符合航班正常管理要求或者安全运营保障能力不足的，国务院民用航空主管部门可以暂停受理其新增业务申请或者限缩其业务范围。  
已经取得许可的从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位或者个人出现不符合许可条件的情形的，由国务院民用航空主管部门限期整改，可以暂停受理其新增业务申请或者限缩其业务范围；逾期仍不符合许可条件的，吊销相关许可证件。

**Article 249** : Where a public air transport enterprise, a general aviation enterprise, or a civil airport has low flight operation efficiency, fails to meet the requirements for normal flight management, or has insufficient capability to ensure safe operation, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council may suspend acceptance of its application for new business or restrict its business scope.  
  
Where a unit or individual that has obtained a permit to engage in civil aviation production and operation activities falls into a situation that does not meet the permit conditions, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order rectification within a time limit, and may suspend acceptance of its application for new business or restrict its business scope; if it still does not meet the permit conditions upon expiry of the time limit, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall revoke the relevant license.

<a id="art-250"></a>第二百五十条　违反本法规定，未制定民用航空安全保卫方案并备案的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 250** : Where a person violates the provisions of this Law by failing to formulate and file a civil aviation security plan, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-251"></a>第二百五十一条　违反本法规定，公共航空运输企业和从事定期运输的通用航空企业有下列情形之一的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，可以吊销相关许可证件：  
（一）未就所从事的运输的旅客、行李、货物以及可能对第三人承担的赔偿责任依法投保责任保险或者取得相应的财务担保；  
（二）未按规定制定、发布、备案或者告知运输总条件等格式条款，或者未在售票等环节中明确告知旅客；  
（三）航班延误或者取消，未按规定发布信息通告，或者未按要求开展处置和旅客服务工作；  
（四）未向社会公布投诉受理方式等信息，未按要求受理、处理旅客投诉或者未及时向投诉人反馈相关情况；  
（五）未依法制定民用航空器事故家属援助计划，或者未按计划做好家属援助工作；  
（六）发生民用航空器事故，未按规定开展应急援救。  
民用机场运营人有前款第三项至第六项情形的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 251** : Where a public air transport enterprise or a general aviation enterprise engaged in scheduled transport is under any of the following circumstances, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are especially serious, the relevant license may be revoked:  
  
(1) Failing to take out liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial guarantee according to law for the passengers, baggage, cargo transported, and the possible compensation liability to third parties;  
  
(2) Failing to formulate, publish, file, or inform about standard terms such as general conditions of carriage as required, or failing to clearly inform passengers during ticket sales or other links;  
  
(3) In case of flight delays or cancellations, failing to issue information notices as required, or failing to carry out disposal and passenger service work as required;  
  
(4) Failing to publicly disclose information such as complaint acceptance methods to the public, failing to accept and handle passenger complaints as required, or failing to timely report relevant situations to the complainants;  
  
(5) Failing to formulate a family assistance plan for civil aircraft accidents according to law, or failing to properly carry out family assistance work according to the plan;  
  
(6) In case of a civil aircraft accident, failing to carry out emergency rescue as required.  
  
Where a civil airport operator falls under the circumstances in items 3 to 6 of the preceding paragraph, it shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-252"></a>第二百五十二条　违反本法规定，实施妨害民用航空运输安全和秩序的行为情节严重的，国务院民用航空主管部门可以在一定期限内限制其乘坐民用航空器。

**Article 252** : Where a person commits an act that endangers the safety and order of civil air transport with serious circumstances, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council may restrict his/her boarding of civil aircraft for a certain period.

<a id="art-253"></a>第二百五十三条　违反本法规定，从事民用航空生产经营活动的单位有下列情形之一的，由国务院民用航空主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照民用航空统计调查制度等规定，向国务院民用航空主管部门提供相关资料；  
（二）未建立民用航空安全信息收集、分析和发布制度，或者未按照规定报告或者发布安全信息。

**Article 253** : Where a unit engaged in civil aviation production and operation activities violates the provisions of this Law by being under any of the following circumstances, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
  
(1) Failing to provide relevant materials to the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council in accordance with the civil aviation statistical survey system and other provisions;  
  
(2) Failing to establish a system for the collection, analysis, and release of civil aviation safety information, or failing to report or release safety information as required.

<a id="art-254"></a>第二百五十四条　国务院民用航空主管部门和地区民用航空管理机构的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 254** : Where a staff member of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council or a regional civil aviation administrative agency neglects his/her duties, abuses his/her powers, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, he/she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-255"></a>第二百五十五条　违反本法规定，实施危害民用航空安全或者扰乱民用航空秩序的行为，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 255** : Where a person violates the provisions of this Law by committing an act that endangers civil aviation safety or disrupts civil aviation order, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-256"></a>第二百五十六条　航空人员因故意犯罪被判处刑罚的，由国务院民用航空主管部门吊销相关执照。

**Article 256** : Where an aviation person is sentenced to a criminal penalty for an intentional crime, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall revoke the relevant license.

### 第十六章　附则

<a id="art-257"></a>第二百五十七条　国家根据民用航空事业发展需要设立政府性基金，具体执行期限由国务院规定。

**Article 257** : The State shall establish a government fund based on the needs for the development of the civil aviation industry, and the specific implementation period shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-258"></a>第二百五十八条　国务院、中央军事委员会对无人驾驶航空器的管理另有规定的，从其规定。  
国家空中交通管理机构依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，制定空域和飞行管理有关规章。

**Article 258** : Where the State Council and the Central Military Commission have other provisions on the management of unmanned aircraft, such provisions shall prevail.  
  
The national air traffic management authority shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, formulate regulations on airspace and flight management.

<a id="art-259"></a>第二百五十九条　取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，由在其他国家有主营业地或者永久居所的运营人根据租用、包用或者互换民用航空器的协议或者其他类似协议运营的，中华人民共和国政府可以按照与该外国政府签订的协定、协议，将本法规定的飞行管理、无线电台执照、适航证书以及机组人员执照的管理职责和义务转移至该外国，中华人民共和国对已转移的职责和义务的责任相应解除。

**Article 259** : Where a civil aircraft that has obtained the nationality of the People's Republic of China is operated by an operator whose principal place of business or permanent residence is in another country, under an agreement for lease, charter, or interchange of civil aircraft or other similar agreement, the Government of the People's Republic of China may, in accordance with the agreements or protocols signed with the foreign government, transfer to that foreign country the management responsibilities and obligations under this Law for flight management, radio station licenses, airworthiness certificates, and crew member licenses; the responsibility of the People's Republic of China for the transferred responsibilities and obligations shall be discharged accordingly.

<a id="art-260"></a>第二百六十条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。  
中华人民共和国法律和中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约没有规定的，可以适用国际惯例。

**Article 260** : Where the provisions of an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China differ from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall prevail, except for the clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.  
  
Where there are no provisions in the laws of the People's Republic of China or in an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, international custom may be applied.

<a id="art-261"></a>第二百六十一条　任何国家或者地区在民用航空运输和民用航空器制造相关领域对中华人民共和国采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对有关国家或者地区采取相应的措施。

**Article 261** : Where any country or region adopts discriminatory prohibitions, restrictions, or other similar measures against the People's Republic of China in the fields related to civil air transport and civil aircraft manufacturing, the People's Republic of China may, based on the actual circumstances, adopt corresponding measures against the country or region concerned.

<a id="art-262"></a>第二百六十二条　本法自2026年7月1日起施行。

**Article 262** : This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2026.
