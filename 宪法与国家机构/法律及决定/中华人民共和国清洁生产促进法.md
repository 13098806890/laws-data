### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进清洁生产，提高资源利用效率，减少和避免污染物的产生，保护和改善环境，保障人体健康，促进经济与社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of promoting cleaner production, increasing the efficiency of resource utilization, reducing and preventing the generation of pollutants, protecting and improving the environment, safeguarding human health, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称清洁生产，是指不断采取改进设计、使用清洁的能源和原料、采用先进的工艺技术与设备、改善管理、综合利用等措施，从源头削减污染，提高资源利用效率，减少或者避免生产、服务和产品使用过程中污染物的产生和排放，以减轻或者消除对人类健康和环境的危害。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "cleaner production" means the continuous adoption of measures such as improving design, using clean energy and raw materials, adopting advanced process technologies and equipment, improving management, and comprehensive utilization, to cut pollution at the source, increase the efficiency of resource utilization, reduce or prevent the generation and discharge of pollutants in the course of production, services, and product use, so as to mitigate or eliminate harm to human health and the environment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国领域内，从事生产和服务活动的单位以及从事相关管理活动的部门依照本法规定，组织、实施清洁生产。

**Article 3** Entities that engage in production and service activities and departments that engage in relevant management activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall organize and implement cleaner production in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励和促进清洁生产。国务院和县级以上地方人民政府，应当将清洁生产促进工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、年度计划以及环境保护、资源利用、产业发展、区域开发等规划。

**Article 4** The State encourages and promotes cleaner production. The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the promotion of cleaner production into the national economic and social development plans, annual plans, and plans for environmental protection, resource utilization, industrial development, and regional development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院清洁生产综合协调部门负责组织、协调全国的清洁生产促进工作。国务院环境保护、工业、科学技术、财政部门和其他有关部门，按照各自的职责，负责有关的清洁生产促进工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责领导本行政区域内的清洁生产促进工作。县级以上地方人民政府确定的清洁生产综合协调部门负责组织、协调本行政区域内的清洁生产促进工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门，按照各自的职责，负责有关的清洁生产促进工作。

**Article 5** The comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing and coordinating the promotion of cleaner production nationwide. The environmental protection, industry, science and technology, finance departments and other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the promotion of cleaner production in their respective fields.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for leading the promotion of cleaner production within their respective administrative regions. The comprehensive coordination departments for cleaner production designated by the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and coordinating the promotion of cleaner production within their respective administrative regions. The other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the promotion of cleaner production in their respective fields.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励开展有关清洁生产的科学研究、技术开发和国际合作，组织宣传、普及清洁生产知识，推广清洁生产技术。  
国家鼓励社会团体和公众参与清洁生产的宣传、教育、推广、实施及监督。

**Article 6** The State encourages the conduct of scientific research, technological development, and international cooperation on cleaner production, and organizes the dissemination and popularization of cleaner production knowledge and the promotion of cleaner production technologies.  
The State encourages social organizations and the public to participate in the publicity, education, promotion, implementation, and supervision of cleaner production.

### 第二章　清洁生产的推行

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院应当制定有利于实施清洁生产的财政税收政策。  
国务院及其有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，应当制定有利于实施清洁生产的产业政策、技术开发和推广政策。

**Article 7** The State Council shall formulate fiscal and tax policies conducive to the implementation of cleaner production.  
The State Council and its relevant departments and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate industrial policies, technological development and promotion policies conducive to the implementation of cleaner production.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院清洁生产综合协调部门会同国务院环境保护、工业、科学技术部门和其他有关部门，根据国民经济和社会发展规划及国家节约资源、降低能源消耗、减少重点污染物排放的要求，编制国家清洁生产推行规划，报经国务院批准后及时公布。  
国家清洁生产推行规划应当包括：推行清洁生产的目标、主要任务和保障措施，按照资源能源消耗、污染物排放水平确定开展清洁生产的重点领域、重点行业和重点工程。  
国务院有关行业主管部门根据国家清洁生产推行规划确定本行业清洁生产的重点项目，制定行业专项清洁生产推行规划并组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据国家清洁生产推行规划、有关行业专项清洁生产推行规划，按照本地区节约资源、降低能源消耗、减少重点污染物排放的要求，确定本地区清洁生产的重点项目，制定推行清洁生产的实施规划并组织落实。

**Article 8** The comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production under the State Council shall, in conjunction with the environmental protection, industry, science and technology departments and other relevant departments under the State Council, formulate a national cleaner production promotion plan based on the national economic and social development plan and the requirements for resource conservation, energy consumption reduction, and reduction of major pollutant emissions, and shall publish it in a timely manner after submitting it to the State Council for approval.  
The national cleaner production promotion plan shall include: the objectives, main tasks, and safeguard measures for promoting cleaner production; and the key areas, key industries, and key projects for carrying out cleaner production as determined by the levels of resource and energy consumption and pollutant emissions.  
The competent industry departments under the State Council shall, based on the national cleaner production promotion plan, determine the key cleaner production projects in their respective industries, formulate special cleaner production promotion plans for their industries, and organize their implementation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the national cleaner production promotion plan and the relevant special industry cleaner production promotion plans, and in accordance with the requirements for resource conservation, energy consumption reduction, and reduction of major pollutant emissions in their respective regions, determine the key cleaner production projects in their regions, formulate implementation plans for promoting cleaner production, and organize their implementation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中央预算应当加强对清洁生产促进工作的资金投入，包括中央财政清洁生产专项资金和中央预算安排的其他清洁生产资金，用于支持国家清洁生产推行规划确定的重点领域、重点行业、重点工程实施清洁生产及其技术推广工作，以及生态脆弱地区实施清洁生产的项目。中央预算用于支持清洁生产促进工作的资金使用的具体办法，由国务院财政部门、清洁生产综合协调部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹地方财政安排的清洁生产促进工作的资金，引导社会资金，支持清洁生产重点项目。

**Article 9** The central budget shall strengthen financial input for the promotion of cleaner production, including special funds for cleaner production from the central finance and other cleaner production funds arranged by the central budget, to support the implementation of cleaner production and the promotion of its technologies in the key areas, key industries, and key projects determined in the national cleaner production promotion plan, as well as projects for implementing cleaner production in ecologically fragile areas. The specific measures for the use of funds from the central budget to support the promotion of cleaner production shall be formulated by the finance department and the comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production under the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments under the State Council.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate the funds for the promotion of cleaner production arranged by local finances, and guide social funds to support key cleaner production projects.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的有关部门，应当组织和支持建立促进清洁生产信息系统和技术咨询服务体系，向社会提供有关清洁生产方法和技术、可再生利用的废物供求以及清洁生产政策等方面的信息和服务。

**Article 10** The relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize and support the establishment of information systems and technical advisory service systems for promoting cleaner production, and provide the public with information and services on cleaner production methods and technologies, the supply and demand of recyclable waste, and cleaner production policies.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院清洁生产综合协调部门会同国务院环境保护、工业、科学技术、建设、农业等有关部门定期发布清洁生产技术、工艺、设备和产品导向目录。  
国务院清洁生产综合协调部门、环境保护部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责清洁生产综合协调的部门、环境保护部门会同同级有关部门，组织编制重点行业或者地区的清洁生产指南，指导实施清洁生产。

**Article 11** The comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production under the State Council shall, in conjunction with the environmental protection, industry, science and technology, construction, agriculture and other relevant departments under the State Council, periodically issue a guidance catalogue of cleaner production technologies, processes, equipment, and products.  
The comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production under the State Council, the environmental protection department, and the departments responsible for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, together with the environmental protection departments and other relevant departments at the same level, shall organize the compilation of cleaner production guidelines for key industries or regions to guide the implementation of cleaner production.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对浪费资源和严重污染环境的落后生产技术、工艺、设备和产品实行限期淘汰制度。国务院有关部门按照职责分工，制定并发布限期淘汰的生产技术、工艺、设备以及产品的名录。

**Article 12** The State implements a system of phasing out outdated production technologies, processes, equipment, and products that waste resources and seriously pollute the environment within a prescribed time limit. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their division of functions and duties, formulate and publish a list of production technologies, processes, equipment, and products to be phased out within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院有关部门可以根据需要批准设立节能、节水、废物再生利用等环境与资源保护方面的产品标志，并按照国家规定制定相应标准。

**Article 13** The relevant departments of the State Council may, as necessary, approve the establishment of product labels for energy conservation, water conservation, waste recycling, and other aspects of environmental and resource protection, and shall formulate corresponding standards in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府科学技术部门和其他有关部门，应当指导和支持清洁生产技术和有利于环境与资源保护的产品的研究、开发以及清洁生产技术的示范和推广工作。

**Article 14** The science and technology departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall guide and support the research and development of cleaner production technologies and products conducive to environmental and resource protection, as well as the demonstration and promotion of cleaner production technologies.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院教育部门，应当将清洁生产技术和管理课程纳入有关高等教育、职业教育和技术培训体系。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门组织开展清洁生产的宣传和培训，提高国家工作人员、企业经营管理者和公众的清洁生产意识，培养清洁生产管理和技术人员。  
新闻出版、广播影视、文化等单位和有关社会团体，应当发挥各自优势做好清洁生产宣传工作。

**Article 15** The education department under the State Council shall incorporate cleaner production technology and management courses into the relevant higher education, vocational education, and technical training systems.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the promotion and training of cleaner production to raise the cleaner production awareness of state functionaries, enterprise managers, and the public, and to train cleaner production management and technical personnel.  
Units such as the press and publishing, radio, film and television, and cultural units, as well as relevant social organizations, shall give full play to their respective advantages and do a good job of promoting cleaner production.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级人民政府应当优先采购节能、节水、废物再生利用等有利于环境与资源保护的产品。  
各级人民政府应当通过宣传、教育等措施，鼓励公众购买和使用节能、节水、废物再生利用等有利于环境与资源保护的产品。

**Article 16** The people's governments at all levels shall give priority to the procurement of products conducive to environmental and resource protection, such as energy-saving, water-saving, and waste recycling products.  
The people's governments at all levels shall, through measures such as publicity and education, encourage the public to purchase and use products conducive to environmental and resource protection, such as energy-saving, water-saving, and waste recycling products.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责清洁生产综合协调的部门、环境保护部门，根据促进清洁生产工作的需要，在本地区主要媒体上公布未达到能源消耗控制指标、重点污染物排放控制指标的企业的名单，为公众监督企业实施清洁生产提供依据。  
列入前款规定名单的企业，应当按照国务院清洁生产综合协调部门、环境保护部门的规定公布能源消耗或者重点污染物产生、排放情况，接受公众监督。

**Article 17** The departments responsible for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production and the environmental protection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the needs of promoting cleaner production, publish in the major local media the list of enterprises that have not met the energy consumption control targets and the major pollutant emission control targets, so as to provide a basis for public supervision over the implementation of cleaner production by such enterprises.  
Enterprises included in the list specified in the preceding paragraph shall, in accordance with the provisions of the comprehensive coordination department for cleaner production and the environmental protection department under the State Council, publish their energy consumption or the generation and discharge of major pollutants, and shall accept public supervision.

### 第三章　清洁生产的实施

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　新建、改建和扩建项目应当进行环境影响评价，对原料使用、资源消耗、资源综合利用以及污染物产生与处置等进行分析论证，优先采用资源利用率高以及污染物产生量少的清洁生产技术、工艺和设备。

**Article 18** New construction, renovation, and expansion projects shall undergo environmental impact assessment, conduct analysis and demonstration on raw material use, resource consumption, comprehensive utilization of resources, and the generation and disposal of pollutants, and shall give priority to the adoption of cleaner production technologies, processes, and equipment that have high resource utilization rates and low pollutant generation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业在进行技术改造过程中，应当采取以下清洁生产措施：  
（一）采用无毒、无害或者低毒、低害的原料，替代毒性大、危害严重的原料；  
（二）采用资源利用率高、污染物产生量少的工艺和设备，替代资源利用率低、污染物产生量多的工艺和设备；  
（三）对生产过程中产生的废物、废水和余热等进行综合利用或者循环使用；  
（四）采用能够达到国家或者地方规定的污染物排放标准和污染物排放总量控制指标的污染防治技术。

**Article 19** In the course of technical renovation, an enterprise shall adopt the following cleaner production measures:  
(1) Use non-toxic, harmless, or low-toxic, low-hazard raw materials to replace highly toxic and seriously hazardous raw materials;  
(2) Use processes and equipment with high resource utilization rates and low pollutant generation to replace processes and equipment with low resource utilization rates and high pollutant generation;  
(3) Carry out comprehensive utilization or recycling of wastes, wastewater, and waste heat generated in the production process; and  
(4) Adopt pollution prevention technologies that can meet the pollutant discharge standards and the total pollutant discharge control indicators prescribed by the State or local authorities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　产品和包装物的设计，应当考虑其在生命周期中对人类健康和环境的影响，优先选择无毒、无害、易于降解或者便于回收利用的方案。  
企业对产品的包装应当合理，包装的材质、结构和成本应当与内装产品的质量、规格和成本相适应，减少包装性废物的产生，不得进行过度包装。

**Article 20** The design of products and packaging shall take into consideration their impact on human health and the environment throughout their life cycle, and shall give priority to options that are non-toxic, harmless, easily degradable, or convenient for recycling.  
An enterprise shall package its products in a reasonable manner. The material, structure, and cost of the packaging shall be commensurate with the quality, specification, and cost of the packaged products, so as to reduce the generation of packaging waste, and excessive packaging shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生产大型机电设备、机动运输工具以及国务院工业部门指定的其他产品的企业，应当按照国务院标准化部门或者其授权机构制定的技术规范，在产品的主体构件上注明材料成分的标准牌号。

**Article 21** Enterprises that produce large-scale mechanical and electrical equipment, motor vehicles, and other products designated by the industrial departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the technical specifications formulated by the standardization department under the State Council or its authorized body, indicate the standard grades of material composition on the main components of the products.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　农业生产者应当科学地使用化肥、农药、农用薄膜和饲料添加剂，改进种植和养殖技术，实现农产品的优质、无害和农业生产废物的资源化，防止农业环境污染。  
禁止将有毒、有害废物用作肥料或者用于造田。

**Article 22** Agricultural producers shall use chemical fertilizers, pesticides, agricultural films, and feed additives in a scientific manner, improve planting and breeding techniques, achieve high quality and harmlessness of agricultural products and resource utilization of agricultural production waste, and prevent agricultural environmental pollution.  
It is prohibited to use toxic or hazardous waste as fertilizer or for land reclamation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　餐饮、娱乐、宾馆等服务性企业，应当采用节能、节水和其他有利于环境保护的技术和设备，减少使用或者不使用浪费资源、污染环境的消费品。

**Article 23** Service enterprises such as catering, entertainment, and hotels shall adopt technologies and equipment that save energy, water, and are otherwise conducive to environmental protection, and reduce or refrain from using consumer goods that waste resources and pollute the environment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建筑工程应当采用节能、节水等有利于环境与资源保护的建筑设计方案、建筑和装修材料、建筑构配件及设备。  
建筑和装修材料必须符合国家标准。禁止生产、销售和使用有毒、有害物质超过国家标准的建筑和装修材料。

**Article 24** Construction projects shall adopt building design schemes, building and decoration materials, building components and fittings, and equipment that are conducive to environmental and resource protection, such as energy saving and water saving.  
Building and decoration materials must conform to national standards. It is prohibited to produce, sell, or use building and decoration materials in which toxic or hazardous substances exceed the national standards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　矿产资源的勘查、开采，应当采用有利于合理利用资源、保护环境和防止污染的勘查、开采方法和工艺技术，提高资源利用水平。

**Article 25** The exploration and mining of mineral resources shall adopt exploration and mining methods, techniques, and technologies conducive to rational utilization of resources, environmental protection, and pollution prevention, so as to improve the level of resource utilization.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业应当在经济技术可行的条件下对生产和服务过程中产生的废物、余热等自行回收利用或者转让给有条件的其他企业和个人利用。

**Article 26** Enterprises shall, under conditions that are economically and technically feasible, recycle and reuse the waste, residual heat, etc. generated in the process of production and service, or transfer them to other enterprises or individuals that have the conditions for utilization.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　企业应当对生产和服务过程中的资源消耗以及废物的产生情况进行监测，并根据需要对生产和服务实施清洁生产审核。  
有下列情形之一的企业，应当实施强制性清洁生产审核：  
（一）污染物排放超过国家或者地方规定的排放标准，或者虽未超过国家或者地方规定的排放标准，但超过重点污染物排放总量控制指标的；  
（二）超过单位产品能源消耗限额标准构成高耗能的；  
（三）使用有毒、有害原料进行生产或者在生产中排放有毒、有害物质的。  
污染物排放超过国家或者地方规定的排放标准的企业，应当按照环境保护相关法律的规定治理。  
实施强制性清洁生产审核的企业，应当将审核结果向所在地县级以上地方人民政府负责清洁生产综合协调的部门、环境保护部门报告，并在本地区主要媒体上公布，接受公众监督，但涉及商业秘密的除外。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当对企业实施强制性清洁生产审核的情况进行监督，必要时可以组织对企业实施清洁生产的效果进行评估验收，所需费用纳入同级政府预算。承担评估验收工作的部门或者单位不得向被评估验收企业收取费用。  
实施清洁生产审核的具体办法，由国务院清洁生产综合协调部门、环境保护部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 27** Enterprises shall monitor the consumption of resources and the generation of waste in the process of production and service, and conduct cleaner production audits for their production and service as needed.  
An enterprise under any of the following circumstances shall carry out a compulsory cleaner production audit:  
(1) pollutant discharge exceeds the discharge standards prescribed by the State or local authorities, or, though not exceeding such standards, exceeds the total emission control indicator for key pollutants;  
(2) its energy consumption per unit product exceeds the quota standard, constituting high energy consumption; or  
(3) it uses toxic or hazardous raw materials in production or discharges toxic or hazardous substances in production.  
An enterprise whose pollutant discharge exceeds the discharge standards prescribed by the State or local authorities shall undertake treatment in accordance with the provisions of relevant environmental protection laws.  
An enterprise that carries out a compulsory cleaner production audit shall report the audit results to the department responsible for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production and the environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level where it is located, and shall publish the results in the major media of the region for public supervision, except for those involving trade secrets.  
The relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall supervise the implementation of compulsory cleaner production audits by enterprises, and may, when necessary, organize evaluation and acceptance of the effects of cleaner production implemented by enterprises; the expenses required shall be included in the government budget at the same level. The department or unit undertaking the evaluation and acceptance work shall not charge fees to the enterprises under evaluation and acceptance.  
The specific measures for implementing cleaner production audits shall be formulated by the department for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production under the State Council and the environmental protection department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　[本法第二十七条](#art-27)第二款规定以外的企业，可以自愿与清洁生产综合协调部门和环境保护部门签订进一步节约资源、削减污染物排放量的协议。该清洁生产综合协调部门和环境保护部门应当在本地区主要媒体上公布该企业的名称以及节约资源、防治污染的成果。

**Article 28** Enterprises other than those specified in the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 27 of this Law may voluntarily enter into agreements with the department for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production and the environmental protection department for further resource conservation and reduction of pollutant discharge. The said department for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production and environmental protection department shall publish the names of such enterprises and the results of resource conservation and pollution prevention in the major media of the region.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　企业可以根据自愿原则，按照国家有关环境管理体系等认证的规定，委托经国务院认证认可监督管理部门认可的认证机构进行认证，提高清洁生产水平。

**Article 29** Enterprises may, on a voluntary basis and in accordance with the provisions of the State on certification of environmental management systems, entrust a certification body accredited by the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department under the State Council to carry out certification, so as to improve the level of cleaner production.

### 第四章　鼓励措施

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家建立清洁生产表彰奖励制度。对在清洁生产工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由人民政府给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 30** The State shall establish a system of commendation and reward for cleaner production. Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in cleaner production shall be commended and rewarded by the people's governments.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对从事清洁生产研究、示范和培训，实施国家清洁生产重点技术改造项目和[本法第二十八条](#art-28)规定的自愿节约资源、削减污染物排放量协议中载明的技术改造项目，由县级以上人民政府给予资金支持。

**Article 31** For those engaged in cleaner production research, demonstration, and training, and for the implementation of national key technological transformation projects for cleaner production and the technological transformation projects specified in the voluntary agreements for resource conservation and pollutant discharge reduction as provided in Article 28 of this Law, the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide financial support.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在依照国家规定设立的中小企业发展基金中，应当根据需要安排适当数额用于支持中小企业实施清洁生产。

**Article 32** In the small and medium-sized enterprise development fund established according to the State regulations, an appropriate amount shall be allocated as needed to support small and medium-sized enterprises in implementing cleaner production.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　依法利用废物和从废物中回收原料生产产品的，按照国家规定享受税收优惠。

**Article 33** Those who utilize waste and recycle raw materials from waste to produce products according to law shall enjoy tax preferences in accordance with the State regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　企业用于清洁生产审核和培训的费用，可以列入企业经营成本。

**Article 34** The expenses of an enterprise for cleaner production audit and training may be included in the operating costs of the enterprise.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　清洁生产综合协调部门或者其他有关部门未依照本法规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where the department for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production or other relevant departments fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)第二款规定，未按照规定公布能源消耗或者重点污染物产生、排放情况的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责清洁生产综合协调的部门、环境保护部门按照职责分工责令公布，可以处十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 17 of this Law by failing to publish energy consumption or the generation or discharge of key pollutants as required shall be ordered to publish by the department responsible for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production or the environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level according to their respective functions and duties, and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定，未标注产品材料的成分或者不如实标注的，由县级以上地方人民政府质量技术监督部门责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处以五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 21 of this Law by failing to indicate the composition of product materials or failing to indicate it truthfully shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit by the quality and technical supervision department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if corrections are refused, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反[本法第二十四条](#art-24)第二款规定，生产、销售有毒、有害物质超过国家标准的建筑和装修材料的，依照产品质量法和有关民事、刑事法律的规定，追究行政、民事、刑事法律责任。

**Article 38** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 24 of this Law by producing or selling building or decoration materials in which toxic or hazardous substances exceed the national standards shall be subject to administrative, civil, and criminal legal liability in accordance with the Product Quality Law and relevant civil and criminal laws.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)第二款、第四款规定，不实施强制性清洁生产审核或者在清洁生产审核中弄虚作假的，或者实施强制性清洁生产审核的企业不报告或者不如实报告审核结果的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责清洁生产综合协调的部门、环境保护部门按照职责分工责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)第五款规定，承担评估验收工作的部门或者单位及其工作人员向被评估验收企业收取费用的，不如实评估验收或者在评估验收中弄虚作假的，或者利用职务上的便利谋取利益的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 4 of Article 27 of this Law by failing to carry out a compulsory cleaner production audit or resorting to fraud in the cleaner production audit, or an enterprise that carries out a compulsory cleaner production audit that fails to report or truthfully report the audit results, shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit by the department responsible for comprehensive coordination of cleaner production or the environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level according to their respective functions and duties; if corrections are refused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 5 of Article 27 of this Law, where the department or unit undertaking the evaluation and acceptance work and its staff members charge fees to the enterprises under evaluation and acceptance, fail to conduct evaluation and acceptance truthfully or resort to fraud in the evaluation and acceptance, or take advantage of their positions to seek gains, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本法自2003年1月1日起施行。

**Article 40** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2003.
