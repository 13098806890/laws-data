# 中国人民解放军军官军衔条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1994-05-12  
**生效日期**：1994-05-12

> （1988年7月1日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过　根据1994年5月12日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议《关于修改（中国人民解放军军官军衔条例）的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　现役军官军衔等级的设置 第三章　现役军官职务等级编制军衔 第四章　现役军官军衔的首次授予 第五章　现役军官军衔的晋级 第六章　现役军官军衔的降级、取消和剥夺 第七章　现役军官军衔的标志和佩带 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国兵役法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　为加强中国人民解放军的革命化、现代化、正规化建设，有利于军队的指挥和管理，增强军官的责任心和荣誉感，实行军官军衔制度。 第三条　军官军衔是区分军官等级、表明军官身份的称号、标志和国家给予军官的荣誉。 第四条　军官军衔按照军官的服役性质分为现役军官军衔和预备役军官军衔。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国兵役法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Constitution of the People's Republic of China and the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　为加强中国人民解放军的革命化、现代化、正规化建设，有利于军队的指挥和管理，增强军官的责任心和荣誉感，实行军官军衔制度。

**Article 2** A system of military ranks for officers shall be implemented to strengthen the revolutionary, modern and regularized development of the People's Liberation Army, to facilitate the command and management of the armed forces, and to enhance the sense of responsibility and honor of officers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军官军衔是区分军官等级、表明军官身份的称号、标志和国家给予军官的荣誉。

**Article 3** Military ranks for officers are titles, insignia and honors conferred by the State to distinguish the grades and indicate the status of officers.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　军官军衔按照军官的服役性质分为现役军官军衔和预备役军官军衔。

**Article 4** Military ranks for officers shall be divided into ranks for officers on active service and ranks for officers in reserve according to the nature of military service of officers.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军衔高的军官对军衔低的军官，军衔高的为上级。当军衔高的军官在职务上隶属于军衔低的军官时，职务高的为上级。

**Article 5** An officer with a higher military rank shall be the superior of an officer with a lower military rank. Where an officer with a higher military rank is subordinate in position to an officer with a lower military rank, the officer with the higher position shall be the superior.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　现役军官转入预备役的，在其军衔前冠以“预备役”。现役军官退役的，其军衔予以保留，在其军衔前冠以“退役”。

**Article 6** Where an officer on active service is transferred to the reserve, the word “Reserve” shall be prefixed to his or her military rank. Where an officer on active service retires from active service, his or her military rank shall be retained, and the word “Retired” shall be prefixed to his or her military rank.

### 第二章　现役军官军衔等级的设置

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军官军衔设下列三等十级：  
（一）将官：上将、中将、少将；  
（二）校官：大校、上校、中校、少校；  
（三）尉官：上尉、中尉、少尉。

**Article 7** Military ranks for officers shall be divided into the following three categories and ten grades:  
(1) General officers: General, Lieutenant General, Major General;  
(2) Field officers: Senior Colonel, Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, Major;  
(3) Company officers: Captain, First Lieutenant, Second Lieutenant.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军官军衔依照下列规定区分：  
（一）军事、政治、后勤军官：上将、中将、少将，大校、上校、中校、少校，上尉、中尉、少尉。  
海军、空军军官在军衔前分别冠以“海军”、“空军”。  
（二）专业技术军官：中将、少将，大校、上校、中校、少校，上尉、中尉、少尉。在军衔前冠以“专业技术”。

**Article 8** Military ranks for officers shall be differentiated according to the following provisions:  
(1) Officers in the military, political and logistics fields: General, Lieutenant General, Major General, Senior Colonel, Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, Major, Captain, First Lieutenant, Second Lieutenant. For officers of the Navy and the Air Force, the words “Navy” and “Air Force” shall be respectively prefixed to their ranks.  
(2) Officers in the technical and professional fields: Lieutenant General, Major General, Senior Colonel, Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, Major, Captain, First Lieutenant, Second Lieutenant. The words “Technical and Professional” shall be prefixed to their ranks.

### 第三章　现役军官职务等级编制军衔

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民解放军实行军官职务等级编制军衔。

**Article 9** The People's Liberation Army shall implement a system of authorized military ranks for positions and grades of officers.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中华人民共和国中央军事委员会领导全国武装力量。中央军事委员会实行主席负责制。中央军事委员会主席不授予军衔。  
中央军事委员会副主席的职务等级编制军衔为上将。  
中央军事委员会委员的职务等级编制军衔为上将。

**Article 10** The Central Military Commission of the People's Republic of China shall lead the national armed forces. The Central Military Commission shall practice a chairman responsibility system. The Chairman of the Central Military Commission shall not be awarded a military rank. The military rank authorized for the position and grade of the Vice-Chairman of the Central Military Commission shall be General. The military rank authorized for the position and grade of the members of the Central Military Commission shall be General.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军事、政治、后勤军官实行下列职务等级编制军衔：  
人民解放军总参谋长、总政治部主任：上将；  
正大军区职：上将、中将；  
副大军区职：中将、少将；  
正军职：少将、中将；  
副军职：少将、大校；  
正师职：大校、少将；  
副师职（正旅职）：上校、大校；  
正团职（副旅职）：上校、中校；  
副团职：中校、少校；  
正营职：少校、中校；  
副营职：上尉、少校；  
正连职：上尉、中尉；  
副连职：中尉、上尉；  
排职：少尉、中尉。

**Article 11** Officers in the military, political and logistics fields shall implement the following military ranks authorized for positions and grades: Chief of the General Staff of the People's Liberation Army, Director of the General Political Department: General; Principal at the level of a major military region: General, Lieutenant General; Deputy at the level of a major military region: Lieutenant General, Major General; Principal at the level of an army: Major General, Lieutenant General; Deputy at the level of an army: Major General, Senior Colonel; Principal at the level of a division: Senior Colonel, Major General; Deputy at the level of a division (Principal at the level of a brigade): Colonel, Senior Colonel; Principal at the level of a regiment (Deputy at the level of a brigade): Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel; Deputy at the level of a regiment: Lieutenant Colonel, Major; Principal at the level of a battalion: Major, Lieutenant Colonel; Deputy at the level of a battalion: Captain, Major; Principal at the level of a company: Captain, First Lieutenant; Deputy at the level of a company: First Lieutenant, Captain; Principal at the level of a platoon: Second Lieutenant, First Lieutenant.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　专业技术军官实行下列职务等级编制军衔：  
高级专业技术职务：中将至少校；  
中级专业技术职务：大校至上尉；  
初级专业技术职务：中校至少尉。

**Article 12** Officers in the technical and professional fields shall implement the following military ranks authorized for positions and grades: Senior technical and professional posts: Lieutenant General to Major; Intermediate technical and professional posts: Senior Colonel to Captain; Junior technical and professional posts: Lieutenant Colonel to Second Lieutenant.

### 第四章　现役军官军衔的首次授予

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军官军衔按照军官职务等级编制军衔授予。

**Article 13** Military ranks for officers shall be awarded in accordance with the military ranks authorized for the positions and grades of officers.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　授予军官军衔以军官所任职务、德才表现、工作实绩、对革命事业的贡献和在军队中服役的经历为依据。

**Article 14** The awarding of a military rank to an officer shall be based on the officer's post, moral character and ability, work performance, contributions to the revolutionary cause and length of service in the armed forces.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　初任军官职务的人员依照下列规定首次授予军衔：  
（一）军队中等专业学校毕业的，授予少尉军衔；  
大学专科毕业的，授予少尉军衔，可以按照人民解放军总政治部的有关规定授予中尉军衔；  
大学本科毕业的，授予中尉军衔，可以按照人民解放军总政治部的有关规定授予少尉军衔；  
获得硕士学位的，授予上尉军衔，可以按照人民解放军总政治部的有关规定授予中尉军衔；研究生班毕业，未获得硕士学位的，授予中尉军衔；  
获得博士学位的，授予少校军衔，可以按照人民解放军总政治部的有关规定授予上尉军衔。  
（二）战时士兵被任命为军官职务的，按照军官职务等级编制军衔，授予相应的军衔。  
（三）军队文职干部和非军事部门的人员被任命为军官职务的，按照军官职务等级编制军衔，授予相应的军衔。

**Article 15** Persons initially appointed to officer positions shall be awarded their first military ranks according to the following provisions:  
(1) Those graduating from a secondary specialized military school shall be awarded the rank of Second Lieutenant; Those graduating from a junior college (three-year higher education program) shall be awarded the rank of Second Lieutenant, and may be awarded the rank of First Lieutenant in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army; Those graduating from a university undergraduate program shall be awarded the rank of First Lieutenant, and may be awarded the rank of Second Lieutenant in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army; Those having obtained a master's degree shall be awarded the rank of Captain, and may be awarded the rank of First Lieutenant in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army; those graduating from a postgraduate program but not having obtained a master's degree shall be awarded the rank of First Lieutenant; Those having obtained a doctoral degree shall be awarded the rank of Major, and may be awarded the rank of Captain in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army.  
(2) Where a soldier is appointed to an officer position in wartime, he or she shall be awarded the appropriate rank according to the military rank authorized for the position and grade of the officer.  
(3) Where a civilian cadre of the armed forces or a person from a non-military department is appointed to an officer position, he or she shall be awarded the appropriate rank according to the military rank authorized for the position and grade of the officer.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　首次授予军官军衔，依照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）上将、中将、少将、大校、上校，由中央军事委员会主席批准授予；  
（二）中校、少校，由人民解放军各总部、大军区、军兵种或者其他相当于大军区级单位的正职首长批准授予；  
（三）上尉、中尉、少尉，由集团军或者其他有军官职务任免权的军级单位的正职首长批准授予。

**Article 16** The first award of military ranks for officers shall be approved within the following authority limits:  
(1) The ranks of General, Lieutenant General, Major General, Senior Colonel and Colonel shall be approved and awarded by the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) The ranks of Lieutenant Colonel and Major shall be approved and awarded by the principal chiefs of the General Departments of the People's Liberation Army, major military regions, services and arms, or other units equivalent in grade to a major military region;  
(3) The ranks of Captain, First Lieutenant and Second Lieutenant shall be approved and awarded by the principal chiefs of group armies or other units at the army level which have the authority to appoint and remove officers.

### 第五章　现役军官军衔的晋级

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军官军衔按照下列期限晋级：  
（一）平时军官军衔晋级的期限：少尉晋升中尉，大学专科以上毕业的为二年，其他为三年；中尉晋升上尉、上尉晋升少校、少校晋升中校、中校晋升上校、上校晋升大校各为四年；大校以上军衔晋级为选升，以军官所任职务、德才表现和对国防建设的贡献为依据；  
（二）战时军官军衔晋级的期限可以缩短，具体办法由中央军事委员会根据战时情况规定。  
军官在院校学习的时间，计算在军衔晋级的期限内。

**Article 17** Military ranks for officers shall be promoted according to the following periods:  
(1) The period for promotion of military ranks for officers in peacetime: For promotion from Second Lieutenant to First Lieutenant, the period shall be two years for those having graduated from a junior college or above, and three years for others; the periods for promotion from First Lieutenant to Captain, from Captain to Major, from Major to Lieutenant Colonel, from Lieutenant Colonel to Colonel, and from Colonel to Senior Colonel shall each be four years; promotions to the ranks of Senior Colonel and above shall be selective promotions, based on the officer's post, moral character and ability, and contributions to national defense construction;  
(2) The period for promotion of military ranks for officers in wartime may be shortened, and specific measures shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission according to wartime circumstances. The time an officer spends studying in an educational institution shall be counted within the period for military rank promotion.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军官军衔一般应当按照规定的期限逐级晋升。

**Article 18** Military ranks for officers shall generally be promoted step by step within the prescribed periods.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　军官军衔晋级的期限届满，因违犯军纪，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定不够晋级条件的，延期晋级或者退出现役。

**Article 19** Where an officer, upon expiration of the period for military rank promotion, due to a violation of military discipline, does not meet the conditions for promotion in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission, his or her promotion shall be deferred or the officer shall retire from active service.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　军官由于职务提升，其军衔低于新任职务等级编制军衔的最低军衔的，提前晋升至新任职务等级编制军衔的最低军衔。

**Article 20** Where an officer's military rank is lower than the minimum rank authorized for the position and grade to which the officer is newly promoted due to a promotion in post, the officer shall be promoted in advance to such minimum rank authorized for the new position and grade.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军官在作战或者工作中建立突出功绩的，其军衔可以提前晋级。

**Article 21** Where an officer has made outstanding achievements in combat or work, his or her rank may be promoted ahead of schedule.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被决定任命为中央军事委员会副主席、委员职务的军官晋升为上将的，由中央军事委员会主席授予上将军衔。

**Article 22** Where an officer who has been decided to be appointed as Vice Chairman or Member of the Central Military Commission is promoted to the rank of General, such rank shall be conferred by the Chairman of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定以外的军官军衔的晋级，按照军官职务的任免权限批准。但是，下列军官军衔晋级，按照以下规定批准：  
（一）副师职（正旅职）军官晋升为大校的，专业技术军官晋升为大校、少将、中将的，由中央军事委员会主席批准；  
（二）专业技术军官晋升为上校的，由人民解放军各总部、大军区、军兵种或者其他相当于大军区级单位的正职首长批准；  
（三）副营职军官晋升为少校的，专业技术军官晋升为少校、中校的，由集团军或者其他有军官职务任免权的军级单位的正职首长批准。

**Article 23** Promotions of the ranks of officers other than those provided for in Article 22 of this Law shall be approved in accordance with the authority for appointment and removal of officers' positions. However, the following promotions of officers' ranks shall be approved in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) The promotion of a Deputy Division-level (Principal Brigade-level) officer to the rank of Senior Colonel, and the promotion of a professional and technical officer to the rank of Senior Colonel, Major General, or Lieutenant General, shall be approved by the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) The promotion of a professional and technical officer to the rank of Colonel shall be approved by the principal heads of the General Departments, Military Regions, Services and Arms, or other units equivalent to Military Regions of the People's Liberation Army;  
(3) The promotion of a Deputy Battalion-level officer to the rank of Major, and the promotion of a professional and technical officer to the rank of Major or Lieutenant Colonel, shall be approved by the principal heads of a Combined Corps or other Corps-level units with the authority to appoint and remove officers.

### 第六章　现役军官军衔的降级、取消和剥夺

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　军官因不胜任现任职务被调任下级职务，其军衔高于新任职务等级编制军衔的最高军衔的，应当调整至新任职务等级编制军衔的最高军衔。调整军衔的批准权限与其原军衔的批准权限相同。

**Article 24** Where an officer is transferred to a lower position due to incompetence in the current position, if his or her rank is higher than the highest rank prescribed for the new position level, the rank shall be adjusted to the highest rank prescribed for the new position level. The authority for approving the adjustment of the rank shall be the same as that for approving the original rank.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军官违犯军纪的，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定，可以给予军衔降级处分。军衔降级的批准权限与首次批准授予该级军衔的权限相同。  
军官军衔降级不适用于少尉军官。

**Article 25** Where an officer violates military discipline, a disciplinary sanction of demotion in rank may be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission. The authority for approving the demotion in rank shall be the same as that for approving the initial conferral of that rank. Demotion in rank shall not apply to officers holding the rank of Second Lieutenant.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　军官军衔降级的，其军衔晋级的期限按照降级后的军衔等级重新计算。  
军官受军衔降级处分后，对所犯错误已经改正并在作战或者工作中有显著成绩的，其军衔晋级的期限可以缩短。

**Article 26** Where an officer's rank is demoted, the time limit for the promotion of his or her rank shall be recalculated based on the demoted rank level.  
Where, after receiving the disciplinary sanction of demotion in rank, an officer has corrected the mistake committed and has achieved notable results in combat or work, the time limit for the promotion of his or her rank may be shortened.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对撤销军官职务并取消军官身份的人员，取消其军官军衔。取消军官军衔的批准权限与首次批准授予该级军衔的权限相同。  
军官被开除军籍的，取消其军衔。取消军衔的批准权限与批准开除军籍的权限相同。

**Article 27** For a person whose position as an officer is removed and whose status as an officer is cancelled, his or her military rank shall be revoked. The authority for approving the revocation of the military rank shall be the same as that for approving the initial conferral of that rank. Where an officer is expelled from military service, his or her rank shall be revoked. The authority for approving the revocation of the rank shall be the same as that for approving the expulsion from military service.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　军官犯罪，被依法判处剥夺政治权利或者三年以上有期徒刑的，由法院判决剥夺其军衔。  
退役军官犯罪的，依照前款规定剥夺其军衔。  
军官犯罪被剥夺军衔，在服刑期满后，需要在军队中服役并授予军官军衔的，依照[本条例第十六条](#art-16)的规定办理。

**Article 28** Where an officer commits a crime and is lawfully sentenced to deprivation of political rights or fixed-term imprisonment of three years or more, the court shall sentence that the officer be deprived of his or her military rank.  
Where a retired officer commits a crime, his or her military rank shall be deprived in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
Where an officer whose military rank has been deprived due to a crime, upon completion of the sentence, needs to serve in the military and be conferred a military rank, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 16 of these Regulations.

### 第七章　现役军官军衔的标志和佩带

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　军官军衔的肩章、符号式样和佩带办法，由中央军事委员会颁布。

**Article 29** The patterns of shoulder boards and insignia for military ranks of officers and the methods of wearing them shall be promulgated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军官佩带的肩章、符号必须与其军衔相符。

**Article 30** The shoulder boards and insignia worn by an officer must be consistent with his or her military rank.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　预备役军官军衔制度，另行规定。

**Article 31** The system of military ranks for reserve officers shall be separately provided for.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　士兵军衔制度，由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 32** The system of military ranks for soldiers shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中国人民武装警察部队实行警衔制度，具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 33** The Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall implement a system of police ranks, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民解放军总参谋部、总政治部根据本条例制定实施办法，报中央军事委员会批准后施行。

**Article 34** The General Staff Department and the General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army shall formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations, which shall be implemented upon approval by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
