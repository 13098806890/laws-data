---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了推动国家通用语言文字的规范化、标准化及其健康发展，更好发挥国家通用语言文字在社会生活和促进各民族、各地区经济文化交流中的作用，铸牢中华民族共同体意识，坚定文化自信，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of promoting the standardization, normalization and healthy development of the national common language and characters, giving better play to the role of the national common language and characters in social life and in promoting economic and cultural exchanges among all ethnic groups and regions, forging a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, and strengthening cultural confidence.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称的国家通用语言文字是普通话和规范汉字，是国家法定全国通用的语言文字。

**Article 2** The national common language and characters referred to in this Law are Putonghua and standardized Chinese characters, which are the legally prescribed national common language and characters.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家推广普及国家通用语言文字。

**Article 3** The State shall promote and popularize the national common language and characters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公民有学习和使用国家通用语言文字的权利。任何组织或者个人都不得妨碍公民学习和使用国家通用语言文字。  
国家为公民学习和使用国家通用语言文字提供条件。

**Article 4** Citizens shall have the right to learn and use the national common language and characters. No organization or individual may obstruct a citizen from learning and using the national common language and characters.  
The State shall provide conditions for citizens to learn and use the national common language and characters.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家语言文字事业坚持中国共产党的领导。  
国家通用语言文字的使用应当有利于维护国家主权和民族尊严，有利于国家统一和民族团结，有利于培育和弘扬社会主义核心价值观，有利于传承发展中华优秀文化和构筑中华民族共有精神家园，有利于社会主义物质文明建设和精神文明建设。

**Article 5** The State's language and characters cause shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.  
The use of the national common language and characters shall be conducive to safeguarding national sovereignty and national dignity, conducive to national unity and ethnic solidarity, conducive to cultivating and promoting the core socialist values, conducive to inheriting and developing the fine traditional Chinese culture and building the common spiritual home of the Chinese nation, and conducive to the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家颁布国家通用语言文字的规范和标准，管理国家通用语言文字的社会应用，支持国家通用语言文字的教学和科学研究，推进国家通用语言文字的信息化、数字化、智能化建设，促进国家通用语言文字的规范、丰富和发展。

**Article 6** The State shall promulgate the norms and standards of the national common language and characters, regulate the social application of the national common language and characters, support the teaching and scientific research of the national common language and characters, advance the informatization, digitalization and intelligent construction of the national common language and characters, and promote the standardization, enrichment and development of the national common language and characters.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府应当将语言文字事业纳入国民经济和社会发展相关规划，将语言文字工作所需经费列入本级预算。  
各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，推广普及国家通用语言文字。  
国家加强民族地区、农村地区和边远地区推广普及国家通用语言文字的条件保障。

**Article 7** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the language and characters cause into the relevant plans for national economic and social development, and include the funds required for language and characters work in their respective budgets.  
The people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall take measures to promote and popularize the national common language and characters.  
The State shall strengthen the guarantee of conditions for promoting and popularizing the national common language and characters in ethnic minority areas, rural areas and remote areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对为国家通用语言文字事业做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。  
每年9月的第三周为国家通用语言文字推广普及宣传周。

**Article 8** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to the cause of the national common language and characters shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
The third week of September each year shall be the Promotion Week for the National Common Language and Characters.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各民族都有使用和发展自己的语言文字的自由。  
少数民族语言文字的使用依据宪法、民族区域自治法及其他法律的有关规定。

**Article 9** All ethnic groups shall have the freedom to use and develop their own languages and characters.  
The use of the spoken and written languages of ethnic minorities shall be subject to the relevant provisions of the Constitution, the Law on Regional Ethnic Autonomy and other laws.

### 第二章　国家通用语言文字的使用

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家机关以国家通用语言文字为公务用语用字。法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 10** State organs shall use the national common language and characters as the official language and characters. Unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　学校及其他教育机构以国家通用语言文字为基本的教育教学用语用字。  
学校及其他教育机构使用的教材，应当符合国家通用语言文字的规范和标准。学校及其他教育机构应当按照国家有关规定使用国家统编教材。  
受教育者在完成义务教育时应当能够基本掌握国家通用语言文字。

**Article 11** Schools and other educational institutions shall use the national common language and characters as the basic language and characters for education and teaching.  
The teaching materials used by schools and other educational institutions shall conform to the norms and standards of the national common language and characters. Schools and other educational institutions shall use the unified national textbooks in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
Educated persons shall, upon completing compulsory education, be able to basically master the national common language and characters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中文出版物应当符合国家通用语言文字的规范和标准。  
中文出版物中需要使用外国语言文字的，应当用国家通用语言文字作必要的注释。

**Article 12** Chinese publications shall conform to the norms and standards of the national common language and characters.  
Where foreign languages and characters are needed in Chinese publications, necessary annotations shall be made in the national common language and characters.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　广播电台、电视台以普通话为基本的播音用语。  
需要使用外国语言为播音用语的，须经国务院广播电视主管部门批准。

**Article 13** Radio and television stations shall use Putonghua as the basic broadcasting language.  
Where foreign languages are needed as the broadcasting language, approval from the competent radio and television department of the State Council shall be obtained.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公共服务行业以规范汉字为基本的服务用字。因公共服务需要，招牌、广告、告示、标志牌等使用外国文字并同时使用中文的，应当使用规范汉字，并以规范汉字为主。  
提倡公共服务行业以普通话为服务用语。

**Article 14** Public service industries shall use standardized Chinese characters as the basic service characters. Where, due to the need of public services, foreign characters are used on signboards, advertisements, notices, signposts, etc., together with Chinese characters, standardized Chinese characters shall be used and shall be the primary ones.  
The public service industry is encouraged to use Putonghua as the service language.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　下列情形，应当以国家通用语言文字为基本的用语用字：  
（一）广播、电影、电视用语用字；  
（二）网络文艺节目、网络剧、网络电影等网络视听节目以及网络游戏等网络出版物用语用字；  
（三）公共场所的设施用字；  
（四）招牌、广告用字；  
（五）企业事业组织名称；  
（六）在境内销售的商品的名称、包装、说明；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 15** In the following circumstances, the national common language and characters shall be used as the basic language and characters:  
(1) Language and characters used in radio, film, and television;  
(2) Language and characters used in online audio-visual programs such as online literary and art programs, online dramas, and online movies, as well as online publications such as online games;  
(3) Characters used in facilities in public places;  
(4) Characters used in signboards and advertisements;  
(5) Names of enterprises and public institutions;  
(6) Names, packaging, and descriptions of goods sold within the territory of China;  
(7) Other circumstances provided for by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在境内举办的国际展览、国际会议等，其标识、标牌、宣传品等需要使用外国语言文字的，应当同时使用国家通用语言文字。

**Article 16** Where foreign languages and characters are needed on signs, signboards, promotional materials, etc. of international exhibitions, international conferences, and other events held within the territory of China, the national common language and characters shall be used simultaneously.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　信息处理和信息技术产品以及政府主办的或者提供社会公共服务的门户网站、移动应用程序等互联网信息平台中使用的国家通用语言文字，应当符合国家的规范和标准。

**Article 17** The national common language and characters used in information processing and information technology products, as well as in Internet information platforms such as portals and mobile applications that are hosted by the government or provide public services, shall conform to the State's norms and standards.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本章有关规定中，有下列情形的，可以使用方言：  
（一）国家机关工作人员执行公务时确需使用的；  
（二）经国务院广播电视主管部门或者省级人民政府广播电视主管部门批准的播音用语；  
（三）戏曲、影视等艺术形式中需要使用的；  
（四）出版、教学、研究中确需使用的。

**Article 18** In the relevant provisions of this Chapter, dialects may be used in the following circumstances:  
(1) Where it is truly necessary for State organ staff to use when performing official duties;  
(2) Broadcasting language approved by the competent radio and television department of the State Council or the competent radio and television department of the people's government at the provincial level;  
(3) Where it is necessary for use in artistic forms such as traditional opera and film and television;  
(4) Where it is truly necessary for use in publishing, teaching, and research.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本章有关规定中，有下列情形的，可以保留或者使用繁体字、异体字：  
（一）文物古迹；  
（二）姓氏中的异体字；  
（三）书法、篆刻等艺术作品；  
（四）题词和招牌的手书字；  
（五）出版、教学、研究中需要使用的；  
（六）经国务院有关部门批准的特殊情形。

**Article 19** In the relevant provisions of this Chapter, traditional Chinese characters or variant Chinese characters may be retained or used in the following circumstances:  
(1) Cultural relics and historic sites;  
(2) Variant characters in surnames;  
(3) Artistic works such as calligraphy and seal cutting;  
(4) Handwritten characters in inscriptions and signboards;  
(5) Where it is necessary for use in publishing, teaching, and research;  
(6) Special circumstances approved by the relevant department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家通用语言文字以《汉语拼音方案》作为拼写和注音工具。  
《汉语拼音方案》是中国人名、地名和中文文献罗马字母拼写法的统一规范，并用于汉字不便或者不能使用的领域。  
初等教育应当进行汉语拼音教学。

**Article 20** The national common language and characters shall use the Scheme for the Chinese Phonetic Alphabet as the tool for spelling and phonetic notation.  
The Scheme for the Chinese Phonetic Alphabet is the unified norm for the Roman alphabet spelling of Chinese personal names, place names, and Chinese literature, and shall be used in areas where Chinese characters are inconvenient or cannot be used.  
Primary education shall provide teaching of Chinese phonetic alphabet.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　以普通话作为工作语言的岗位，其工作人员应当具备说普通话的能力。  
播音员、节目主持人、影视话剧演员、教师、国家机关工作人员的普通话水平应当达到国家规定的普通话等级标准；对尚未达到国家规定的普通话等级标准的，区分情况进行培训。  
文化和旅游、交通运输等行业中面向公众的从业人员，其需要达到的普通话等级标准，由国务院有关行业主管部门会同国务院语言文字工作部门规定。

**Article 21** Staff members in positions where Putonghua is used as the working language shall have the ability to speak Putonghua.  
The Putonghua proficiency of announcers, program hosts, film and television drama actors, teachers, and staff members of State organs shall meet the Putonghua proficiency grade standards prescribed by the State; those who have not yet met the prescribed Putonghua proficiency grade standards shall be trained on a case-by-case basis.  
The Putonghua proficiency grade standards required for practitioners who face the public in industries such as culture and tourism, and transportation, shall be prescribed by the relevant competent industry department of the State Council jointly with the language and characters working department of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国际中文教育应当教授国家通用语言文字，通过国际交流合作促进人类文明交流互鉴。

**Article 22** International Chinese language education shall teach the national common language and characters, and promote mutual learning among civilizations through international exchanges and cooperation.

### 第三章　管理和监督

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家通用语言文字工作由国务院语言文字工作部门负责规划指导、管理监督。  
国务院有关部门管理本系统、本行业的国家通用语言文字的使用。

**Article 23** The work on the national common language and characters shall be under the responsibility of the language and characters working department of the State Council for planning, guidance, administration, and supervision.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall administer the use of the national common language and characters in their respective systems and industries.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方语言文字工作部门和其他有关部门，管理和监督本行政区域内的国家通用语言文字的使用。

**Article 24** The local language and characters working departments and other relevant departments shall administer and supervise the use of the national common language and characters within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门依法对企业名称和商品的名称、包装、说明以及广告的用语用字进行管理和监督。

**Article 25** The market regulatory departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, administer and supervise the language and characters used in enterprise names, commodity names, packaging, descriptions, and advertisements.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院语言文字工作部门颁布普通话水平测试等级标准。

**Article 26** The language and characters working department of the State Council shall promulgate the proficiency test grade standards for Putonghua.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　外国人名、地名等专有名词和科学技术术语译成国家通用语言文字，由国务院语言文字工作部门或者其他有关部门组织审定。

**Article 27** The translation of proper nouns such as foreign personal names and place names, and scientific and technical terms into the national common language and characters shall be organized and examined by the language and characters working department of the State Council or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对违反本法第二章有关规定，不按照国家通用语言文字的规范和标准使用语言文字的，公民、组织可以提出批评和建议，并可以向有关机关投诉、举报。接到投诉、举报的机关应当按照规定及时处理。

**Article 28** Where anyone violates the relevant provisions of Chapter II of this Law and fails to use the language and characters in accordance with the norms and standards of the national common language and characters, citizens and organizations may criticize and make suggestions, and may lodge complaints or report to the relevant authorities. The authorities that receive such complaints or reports shall handle them in a timely manner in accordance with regulations.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[本法第二十一条](#art-21)第二款规定的人员用语违反本法第二章有关规定的，有关单位应当对直接责任人员进行批评教育，责令改正；拒不改正的，由有关单位作出处理。

**Article 29** Where the language used by the personnel specified in paragraph 2 of Article 21 of this Law violates the relevant provisions of Chapter II of this Law, the relevant unit shall criticize and educate the directly responsible personnel and order them to make corrections; if they refuse to make corrections, the relevant unit shall handle the matter.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反本法第二章有关规定的，由教育、新闻出版、广播电视、市场监督管理、住房城乡建设、文化和旅游、电影、交通运输、民政、公安、文物、网信、工业和信息化等有关部门依据各自职责责令改正；拒不改正的，给予警告或者通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。法律、行政法规另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 30** Where anyone violates the relevant provisions of Chapter II of this Law, the relevant departments such as education, press and publication, radio and television, market regulation, housing and urban-rural construction, culture and tourism, film, transportation, civil affairs, public security, cultural relics, cyberspace administration, and industry and information technology shall, pursuant to their respective functions and duties, order corrections; if the violator refuses to make corrections, a warning or a notice of criticism shall be given, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law. If otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本法规定，干涉他人学习和使用国家通用语言文字的，由有关部门进行批评教育，责令改正，可以给予警告；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 31** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law by interfering with another person's learning and use of the national common language and characters, the relevant department shall criticize and educate the person, order corrections, and may give a warning; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the person shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法自2026年1月1日起施行。

**Article 32** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2026.
