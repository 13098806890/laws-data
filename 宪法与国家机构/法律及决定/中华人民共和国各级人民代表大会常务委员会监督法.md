# 中华人民共和国各级人民代表大会常务委员会监督法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-11-08  
**生效日期**：2024-11-08

> （2006年8月27日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议通过　根据2024年11月8日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国各级人民代表大会常务委员会监督法〉的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　听取和审议专项工作报告 第三章　财政经济工作监督 第四章　法律法规实施情况的检查 第五章　规范性文件的备案审查 第六章　询问、专题询问和质询 第七章　特定问题调查 第八章　撤职案的审议和决定 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障全国人民代表大会常务委员会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会依法行使监督职权，坚持好、完善好、运行好人民代表大会制度，发展社会主义民主政治，推进全面依法治国，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会依据宪法和有关法律的规定，行使监督职权。 各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权的程序，适用本法；本法没有规定的，适用有关法律的规定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障全国人民代表大会常务委员会和县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会依法行使监督职权，坚持好、完善好、运行好人民代表大会制度，发展社会主义民主政治，推进全面依法治国，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to ensure that the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the standing committees of the local people's congresses at and above the county level exercise their supervisory powers according to law, uphold, improve and operate the system of people's congresses, develop socialist democratic politics, and advance the comprehensive rule of law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会依据宪法和有关法律的规定，行使监督职权。  
各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权的程序，适用本法；本法没有规定的，适用有关法律的规定。

**Article 2** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall exercise their supervisory powers in accordance with the Constitution and relevant laws.  
The procedures for the standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels to exercise their supervisory powers shall be governed by this Law; where this Law does not provide, the provisions of relevant laws shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权，应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚持中国特色社会主义道路，确保宪法和法律、法规得到全面有效实施，确保行政权、监察权、审判权、检察权依法正确行使。

**Article 3** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels, when exercising their supervisory powers, shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, and adhere to the path of socialism with Chinese characteristics, ensuring the comprehensive and effective implementation of the Constitution, laws, and regulations, and ensuring the correct exercise of administrative power, supervisory power, adjudicative power, and procuratorial power according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权，应当围绕党和国家工作大局，以经济建设为中心，坚持改革开放，贯彻新发展理念，推动高质量发展，保障全面建设社会主义现代化国家、以中国式现代化全面推进中华民族伟大复兴。

**Article 4** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels, when exercising their supervisory powers, shall focus on the overall work of the Party and the state, take economic construction as the central task, adhere to reform and opening up, implement the new development philosophy, promote high-quality development, and ensure the comprehensive building of a modern socialist country and the comprehensive advancement of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation through Chinese-style modernization.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会监督宪法和法律的实施，地方各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会在本行政区域内保证宪法、法律、行政法规和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会决议的遵守和执行，维护国家法治统一、尊严、权威。

**Article 5** The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall supervise the implementation of the Constitution and laws; the local people's congresses at all levels and their standing committees shall ensure the observance and enforcement of the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the resolutions of the people's congresses at higher levels and their standing committees within their respective administrative regions, and safeguard the unity, dignity, and authority of the national legal system.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会对本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院的工作实施监督，实行正确监督、有效监督、依法监督，促进依法行政、依法监察、公正司法。  
各级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院应当严格依法行使职权、履行职责、开展工作，自觉接受本级人民代表大会常务委员会的监督。

**Article 6** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall exercise supervision over the work of the people's governments, supervisory commissions, people's courts, and people's procuratorates at the corresponding level, carry out correct supervision, effective supervision, and lawful supervision, and promote law-based administration, law-based supervision, and impartial administration of justice.  
The people's governments, supervisory commissions, people's courts, and people's procuratorates at all levels shall strictly exercise their powers, perform their duties, and carry out their work according to law, and consciously accept the supervision of the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权，应当坚持和发展全过程人民民主，尊重和保障人权，维护和促进社会公平正义。  
各级人民代表大会常务委员会应当扩大人民代表大会代表对监督工作的参与，充分发挥代表作用。

**Article 7** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels, when exercising their supervisory powers, shall uphold and develop whole-process people's democracy, respect and safeguard human rights, and maintain and promote social fairness and justice.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall expand the participation of deputies to the people's congresses in supervisory work and give full play to the role of deputies.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会按照民主集中制的原则，集体行使监督职权。

**Article 8** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall exercise their supervisory powers collectively in accordance with the principle of democratic centralism.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权的情况，应当向本级人民代表大会报告，接受监督。

**Article 9** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall report to the people's congresses at the corresponding level on their exercise of supervisory powers and accept supervision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使监督职权的情况，向社会公开。

**Article 10** The situation of the exercise of supervisory powers by the standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall be made public to society.

### 第二章　听取和审议专项工作报告

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会每年选择若干关系改革发展稳定大局和群众切身利益、社会普遍关注的重大问题，有计划地安排听取和审议本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院的专项工作报告。

**Article 11** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall each year select several major issues relating to the overall situation of reform, development and stability, the immediate interests of the people, and issues of general public concern, and in a planned manner arrange to hear and deliberate special work reports of the people's governments, supervisory commissions, people's courts, and people's procuratorates at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　常务委员会听取和审议本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院的专项工作报告的议题，根据有关法律的规定和下列途径反映的问题确定：  
（一）本级人民代表大会常务委员会在执法检查中发现的突出问题；  
（二）本级人民代表大会代表对人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院工作提出的建议、批评和意见集中反映的问题；  
（三）本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员提出的比较集中的问题；  
（四）本级人民代表大会专门委员会、常务委员会工作机构在调查研究中发现的突出问题；  
（五）人民来信来访集中反映的问题；  
（六）社会普遍关注的其他问题。  
人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会要求报告专项工作。  
常务委员会根据法律规定，听取和审议本级人民政府关于环境状况和环境保护目标完成情况的报告。

**Article 12** The topics for the standing committee to hear and deliberate special work reports of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court and people's procuratorate at the corresponding level shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and the problems reflected through the following channels:  
(1) prominent problems discovered by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level in law enforcement inspections;  
(2) problems collectively reflected in the suggestions, criticisms and opinions raised by deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level on the work of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court and people's procuratorate;  
(3) relatively concentrated issues raised by members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(4) prominent problems discovered by the special committees of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the working bodies of the standing committee in investigations and studies;  
(5) problems collectively reflected in letters and visits from the people;  
(6) other issues of general public concern.  
The people's government, supervisory commission, people's court and people's procuratorate may request the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level to hear a special work report.  
The standing committee shall, in accordance with legal provisions, hear and deliberate the report of the people's government at the corresponding level on the environmental situation and the completion of environmental protection targets.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　常务委员会听取和审议专项工作报告前，委员长会议或者主任会议可以组织本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员和本级人民代表大会代表，对有关工作进行视察或者专题调查研究。  
常务委员会可以安排参加视察或者专题调查研究的代表列席常务委员会会议，听取专项工作报告，提出意见。  
常务委员会听取和审议专项工作报告前，本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构可以进行专题调查研究，提出报告并印发常务委员会会议。

**Article 13** Before the standing committee hears and deliberates a special work report, the Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may organize members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level and deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level to conduct inspections or special investigations and studies on the relevant work.  
The standing committee may arrange for deputies who participated in inspections or special investigations and studies to attend the standing committee meeting as non-voting participants, hear the special work report, and present opinions.  
Before the standing committee hears and deliberates a special work report, the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee may conduct special investigations and studies, submit a report, and distribute it to the standing committee meeting.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　常务委员会听取和审议专项工作报告前，常务委员会办事机构应当将各方面对该项工作的意见汇总，交由本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院研究并在专项工作报告中作出回应。

**Article 14** Before the standing committee hears and deliberates a special work report, the working office of the standing committee shall compile opinions on the work from all sides, deliver them to the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level for study, and require them to respond in the special work report.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当在常务委员会举行会议的二十日前，由其办事机构将专项工作报告送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见；人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院对报告修改后，在常务委员会举行会议的十日前送交常务委员会。  
常务委员会办事机构应当在常务委员会举行会议的七日前，将专项工作报告发给常务委员会组成人员。

**Article 15** The people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate shall, not less than twenty days before the date of the standing committee meeting, submit the special work report through its working office to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee for comments; after the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate revises the report, it shall submit it to the standing committee not less than ten days before the meeting.  
The working office of the standing committee shall, not less than seven days before the standing committee meeting, distribute the special work report to the members of the standing committee.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专项工作报告由人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院的负责人向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告，人民政府也可以委托有关部门负责人向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告。  
委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将报告交有关专门委员会审议。

**Article 16** The special work report shall be made by the responsible person of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level; the people's government may also entrust the responsible person of a relevant department to make the report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.  
The Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the report to the relevant special committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　常务委员会组成人员对专项工作报告的审议意见交由本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院研究处理。人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当将研究处理情况由其办事机构送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见后，向常务委员会提出书面报告。常务委员会认为必要时，可以对专项工作报告作出决议；本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当在决议规定的期限内，将执行决议的情况向常务委员会报告。  
委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将审议意见研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告提请常务委员会审议。必要时，常务委员会可以组织开展跟踪监督。  
常务委员会听取的专项工作报告及审议意见，人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院对审议意见研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告，向本级人民代表大会代表通报并向社会公布。

**Article 17** The deliberation opinions of the members of the standing committee on the special work report shall be delivered to the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level for study and handling. The people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate shall, after submitting the information on the study and handling through its working office to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee for comments, submit a written report to the standing committee. If the standing committee deems it necessary, it may adopt a resolution on the special work report; the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level shall, within the period prescribed in the resolution, report to the standing committee on the implementation of the resolution.  
The Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the report on the study and handling of the deliberation opinions or the implementation of the resolution to the standing committee for deliberation. If necessary, the standing committee may organize follow-up supervision.  
The special work report heard by the standing committee and the deliberation opinions, and the report of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate on the study and handling of the deliberation opinions or the implementation of the resolution shall be notified to the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level and published to the public.

### 第三章　财政经济工作监督

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本法所称财政经济工作监督，是指常务委员会依法对下列事项进行监督：  
（一）审查和批准本级决算；  
（二）国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要实施情况，国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况；  
（三）预算执行情况；  
（四）审查和批准国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要、计划的调整方案；  
（五）审查和批准预算调整方案；  
（六）国有资产管理情况；  
（七）政府债务管理情况；  
（八）金融工作情况；  
（九）预算执行和其他财政收支的审计工作情况、审计查出问题整改情况；  
（十）财政经济领域其他重要事项。

**Article 18** For the purposes of this Law, supervision of fiscal and economic work means the supervision exercised by the standing committee according to law over the following matters:  
(1) examination and approval of the final accounts at the corresponding level;  
(2) implementation of the outline of the five-year plan for national economic and social development, and implementation of the plan for national economic and social development;  
(3) implementation of the budget;  
(4) examination and approval of adjustment plans to the outline of the five-year plan for national economic and social development and to the plan;  
(5) examination and approval of adjustment plans to the budget;  
(6) management of state-owned assets;  
(7) management of government debt;  
(8) financial work;  
(9) audit work on budget implementation and other fiscal revenues and expenditures, and rectification of problems discovered by audit;  
(10) other important matters in the fiscal and economic field.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院应当在每年六月，将上一年度的中央决算草案提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当在每年六月至九月期间，将上一年度的本级决算草案提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。  
决算草案应当按照本级人民代表大会批准的预算所列科目编制，按预算数、调整预算数以及决算数分别列出，并作出说明。

**Article 19** The State Council shall, in June of each year, submit the draft central final accounts of the previous year to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for examination and approval.  
The local people's governments at all levels at and above the county level shall, from June to September of each year, submit the draft final accounts of the previous year at the corresponding level to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level for examination and approval.  
The draft final accounts shall be prepared in accordance with the budget items approved by the people's congress at the corresponding level, list respectively the budget figures, adjusted budget figures, and final accounts figures, and include explanations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当在每年六月至九月期间，向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告本年度上一阶段国民经济和社会发展计划、预算的执行情况。

**Article 20** The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels at and above the county level shall, from June to September of each year, report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level on the implementation of the plan for national economic and social development and the budget for the preceding period of the current year.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　常务委员会根据《中华人民共和国预算法》和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关决定，确定对决算草案和预算执行情况报告的重点审查内容。

**Article 21** The standing committee shall determine the key contents for examination of the draft final accounts and the report on budget implementation in accordance with the Budget Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国民经济和社会发展计划、预算经人民代表大会批准后，在执行过程中需要作部分调整的，国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当将调整方案提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。

**Article 22** After the plan for national economic and social development and the budget are approved by the people's congress, if partial adjustments are needed in the course of implementation, the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels at and above the county level shall submit the adjustment plans to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level for examination and approval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国民经济和社会发展五年规划纲要经人民代表大会批准后，在实施的中期阶段，人民政府应当将规划纲要实施情况的中期评估报告提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审议。规划纲要经中期评估需要调整的，人民政府应当将调整方案提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会审查和批准。

**Article 23** After the outline of the five-year plan for national economic and social development is approved by the people's congress, at the mid-term stage of its implementation, the people's government shall submit a mid-term evaluation report on the implementation of the outline to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for deliberation. If the outline needs to be adjusted after the mid-term evaluation, the people's government shall submit an adjustment plan to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for examination and approval.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　常务委员会应当对国有资产管理情况进行监督，建立健全国有资产管理情况报告制度。  
国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当每年向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告国有资产管理情况。

**Article 24** The standing committee shall supervise the management of state-owned assets and establish and improve a reporting system on the management of state-owned assets.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels at and above the county level shall report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level each year on the management of state-owned assets.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　常务委员会应当对政府债务进行监督，建立健全政府债务管理情况报告制度。  
国务院和县级以上地方各级人民政府应当每年向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告政府债务管理情况。

**Article 25** The standing committee shall supervise government debt and establish and improve a reporting system on the management of government debt.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels at and above the county level shall report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level each year on the management of government debt.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　常务委员会应当对金融工作进行监督，建立健全金融工作情况报告制度。  
国务院应当每年向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告金融工作有关情况。

**Article 26** The standing committee shall supervise financial work and establish and improve a reporting system on financial work.  
The State Council shall report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress each year on relevant matters concerning financial work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　常务委员会每年审查和批准决算的同时，听取和审议本级人民政府提出的审计机关关于上一年度预算执行和其他财政收支的审计工作报告。  
常务委员会在听取和审议审计工作报告后的六个月内，听取和审议本级人民政府关于审计查出问题整改情况的报告。常务委员会认为必要时，可以对审计工作报告、审计查出问题整改情况报告作出决议。

**Article 27** While examining and approving the final accounts each year, the standing committee shall hear and deliberate the audit work report submitted by the people's government at the corresponding level on the audit of the implementation of the budget and other fiscal revenues and expenditures of the previous year.  
Within six months after hearing and deliberating the audit work report, the standing committee shall hear and deliberate the report of the people's government at the corresponding level on the rectification of problems discovered by audit. If the standing committee deems it necessary, it may adopt a resolution on the audit work report or the report on rectification of problems discovered by audit.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　常务委员会开展财政经济工作监督，可以组织开展专题调查研究，提出报告。  
专题调查研究报告印发常务委员会会议；必要时，委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将有关专题调查研究报告提请常务委员会审议。

**Article 28** When carrying out supervision of fiscal and economic work, the standing committee may organize special investigations and studies and submit reports.  
The special investigation and study report shall be distributed to the standing committee meeting; if necessary, the Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the relevant special investigation and study report to the standing committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　常务委员会组成人员对本章规定的有关报告的审议意见交由本级人民政府研究处理。人民政府应当将研究处理情况由其办事机构送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见后，向常务委员会提出书面报告。常务委员会认为必要时，可以对有关报告作出决议；本级人民政府应当在决议规定的期限内，将执行决议的情况向常务委员会报告。  
委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将审议意见研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告提请常务委员会审议。必要时，常务委员会可以组织开展跟踪监督。  
常务委员会听取的本章规定的有关报告及审议意见，人民政府对审议意见研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告，向本级人民代表大会代表通报并向社会公布。

**Article 29** The deliberation opinions of the members of the standing committee on the relevant reports provided for in this Chapter shall be delivered to the people's government at the corresponding level for study and handling. The people's government shall, after submitting the information on the study and handling through its working office to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee for comments, submit a written report to the standing committee. If the standing committee deems it necessary, it may adopt a resolution on the relevant report; the people's government at the corresponding level shall, within the period prescribed in the resolution, report to the standing committee on the implementation of the resolution.  
The Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the report on the study and handling of the deliberation opinions or the implementation of the resolution to the standing committee for deliberation. If necessary, the standing committee may organize follow-up supervision.  
The relevant reports provided for in this Chapter heard by the standing committee and the deliberation opinions, and the report of the people's government on the study and handling of the deliberation opinions or the implementation of the resolution shall be notified to the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level and published to the public.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　常务委员会运用现代信息技术开展联网监督，建立健全信息共享和工作贯通协调机制，提高财政经济工作监督效能。

**Article 30** The standing committee shall use modern information technology to carry out online supervision, establish and improve mechanisms for information sharing and work coordination, and enhance the effectiveness of supervision of fiscal and economic work.

### 第四章　法律法规实施情况的检查

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会参照[本法第十二条](#art-12)规定的途径，每年选择若干关系改革发展稳定大局和群众切身利益、社会普遍关注的重大问题，有计划地对有关法律、法规或者相关法律制度实施情况组织执法检查。

**Article 31** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall, by reference to the channels provided for in Article 12 of this Law, select each year several major issues relating to the overall situation of reform, development and stability, the immediate interests of the people, and issues of general public concern, and in a planned manner organize law enforcement inspections on the implementation of relevant laws, regulations or relevant legal systems.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　常务委员会执法检查工作由本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构具体组织实施。  
执法检查前，本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构可以对重点问题开展专题调查研究。

**Article 32** The law enforcement inspection work of the standing committee shall be specifically organized and implemented by the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee.  
Before the law enforcement inspection, the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee may conduct special investigations and studies on key issues.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　常务委员会按照精干、效能的原则，组织执法检查组。  
执法检查组的组成人员，从本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员以及本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会组成人员或者常务委员会有关工作机构的人员中确定，并可以邀请本级人民代表大会代表参加。

**Article 33** The standing committee shall organize a law enforcement inspection team in accordance with the principles of conciseness and efficiency.  
The members of the law enforcement inspection team shall be determined from among the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, the members of the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, or the personnel of the relevant working body of the standing committee, and deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level may be invited to participate.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会和省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会根据需要，可以委托下一级人民代表大会常务委员会对有关法律、法规或者相关法律制度在本行政区域内的实施情况进行检查。受委托的人民代表大会常务委员会应当将检查情况书面报送上一级人民代表大会常务委员会。  
上级人民代表大会常务委员会根据需要，可以与下级人民代表大会常务委员会联动开展执法检查。  
有关地方人民代表大会常务委员会根据区域协调发展的需要，可以协同开展执法检查。

**Article 34** The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, as necessary, entrust the standing committees of the people's congresses at the next lower level to inspect the implementation of relevant laws, regulations or relevant legal systems within their respective administrative regions. The entrusted standing committee of the people's congress shall submit a written report on the inspection to the standing committee of the people's congress at the next higher level.  
The standing committee of the people's congress at a higher level may, as necessary, conduct law enforcement inspections jointly with the standing committee of the people's congress at a lower level.  
The relevant standing committees of the local people's congresses may, in light of the need for coordinated regional development, conduct law enforcement inspections in coordination.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　执法检查可以采取座谈会、实地检查、第三方评估、问卷调查或者抽查等形式，深入了解情况，广泛听取意见。

**Article 35** Law enforcement inspections may be conducted in the forms of symposiums, on-site inspections, third-party evaluations, questionnaire surveys, or spot checks, to gain in-depth understanding of the situation and extensively solicit opinions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　执法检查结束后，执法检查组应当及时提出执法检查报告，由委员长会议或者主任会议决定提请常务委员会审议。  
执法检查报告包括下列内容：  
（一）对所检查的法律、法规或者相关法律制度实施情况进行评价，提出执法中存在的问题和改进执法工作的建议；  
（二）对有关法律、法规提出修改完善的建议。

**Article 36** After the conclusion of a law enforcement inspection, the law enforcement inspection team shall promptly submit a law enforcement inspection report, and the Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors shall decide to submit it to the standing committee for deliberation.  
The law enforcement inspection report shall include the following:  
(1) evaluation of the implementation of the inspected laws, regulations or relevant legal systems, identifying problems in law enforcement and making recommendations for improvement of law enforcement work;  
(2) recommendations for amendment and improvement of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　常务委员会组成人员对执法检查报告的审议意见及执法检查报告交由本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院研究处理。人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当将研究处理情况由其办事机构送交本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构征求意见后，向常务委员会提出书面报告。常务委员会认为必要时，可以对执法检查报告作出决议；本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当在决议规定的期限内，将执行决议的情况向常务委员会报告。  
委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院对执法检查报告及审议意见研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告提请常务委员会审议。必要时，常务委员会可以组织跟踪检查，也可以委托本级人民代表大会有关专门委员会或者常务委员会有关工作机构组织跟踪检查。  
常务委员会的执法检查报告及审议意见，人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院对其研究处理情况或者执行决议情况的报告，向本级人民代表大会代表通报并向社会公布。

**Article 37** The deliberation opinions of the members of the standing committee on the law enforcement inspection report and the law enforcement inspection report itself shall be delivered to the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level for study and handling. The people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate shall, after submitting the information on the study and handling through its working office to the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee for comments, submit a written report to the standing committee. If the standing committee deems it necessary, it may adopt a resolution on the law enforcement inspection report; the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level shall, within the period prescribed in the resolution, report to the standing committee on the implementation of the resolution.  
The Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the report of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate on the study and handling of the law enforcement inspection report and the deliberation opinions or the implementation of the resolution to the standing committee for deliberation. If necessary, the standing committee may organize a follow-up inspection, or may entrust the relevant special committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the relevant working body of the standing committee to organize a follow-up inspection.  
The law enforcement inspection report and the deliberation opinions of the standing committee, and the report of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate on the study and handling thereof or the implementation of the resolution shall be notified to the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level and published to the public.

### 第五章　规范性文件的备案审查

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　行政法规、监察法规、地方性法规、自治条例和单行条例、规章等的备案、审查和撤销，依照《中华人民共和国立法法》和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关决定办理。

**Article 38** The filing, review, and annulment of administrative regulations, supervisory regulations, local regulations, autonomous regulations, separate regulations, rules, etc., shall be handled in accordance with the Legislation Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会对下一级人民代表大会及其常务委员会作出的决议、决定和本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院制定的规范性文件，经审查，认为有下列不适当的情形之一的，有权予以撤销：  
（一）超越法定权限，限制或者剥夺公民、法人和其他组织的合法权利，或者增加公民、法人和其他组织的义务的；  
（二）同法律、法规规定相抵触的；  
（三）有其他不适当的情形，应当予以撤销的。

**Article 39** The standing committees of the local people's congresses at all levels at and above the county level may, upon review, annul any resolution or decision made by the people's congress at the next lower level or its standing committee, or any normative document formulated by the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level, if they find it to be inappropriate in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) exceeding the statutory authority, restricting or depriving citizens, legal persons or other organizations of their lawful rights, or increasing the obligations of citizens, legal persons or other organizations;  
(2) contravening the provisions of laws or regulations;  
(3) any other inappropriate circumstance for which annulment is warranted.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会审查、撤销下一级人民代表大会及其常务委员会作出的不适当的决议、决定和本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院制定的不适当的规范性文件的程序，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会参照《中华人民共和国立法法》和全国人民代表大会常务委员会的有关决定，作出具体规定。

**Article 40** The procedures for the standing committees of the local people's congresses at all levels at and above the county level to review and annul any inappropriate resolution or decision made by the people's congress at the next lower level or its standing committee, or any inappropriate normative document formulated by the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level, shall be specified by the standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government by reference to the Legislation Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　最高人民法院、最高人民检察院作出的属于审判、检察工作中具体应用法律的解释，应当自公布之日起三十日内报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 41** Any interpretation by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate on the specific application of laws in adjudicative or procuratorial work shall, within thirty days from the date of promulgation, be submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for filing.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院、中央军事委员会、国家监察委员会和省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会认为最高人民法院、最高人民检察院作出的具体应用法律的解释同宪法或者法律相抵触，或者存在合宪性、合法性问题的，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院之间认为对方作出的具体应用法律的解释同宪法或者法律相抵触，或者存在合宪性、合法性问题的，可以向全国人民代表大会常务委员会书面提出进行审查的要求，由全国人民代表大会有关专门委员会和常务委员会工作机构进行审查、提出意见。  
前款规定以外的其他国家机关和社会团体、企业事业组织以及公民认为最高人民法院、最高人民检察院作出的具体应用法律的解释同宪法或者法律相抵触的，可以向全国人民代表大会常务委员会书面提出进行审查的建议，由常务委员会工作机构进行审查；必要时，送有关专门委员会进行审查、提出意见。  
全国人民代表大会有关专门委员会、常务委员会工作机构可以对报送备案的具体应用法律的解释进行主动审查，并可以根据需要进行专项审查。

**Article 42** If the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the National Commission of Supervision, or the standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government considers that an interpretation on the specific application of laws made by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate contradicts the Constitution or laws, or has issues of constitutionality or legality, or if the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate consider that an interpretation on the specific application of laws made by the other contradicts the Constitution or laws, or has issues of constitutionality or legality, they may submit a written request to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for review, and the relevant special committees of the National People's Congress and the working bodies of the Standing Committee shall conduct the review and provide opinions.  
If any other state organ, social organization, enterprise, institution, or citizen not covered by the preceding paragraph considers that an interpretation on the specific application of laws made by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate contradicts the Constitution or laws, they may submit a written recommendation to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for review; the working bodies of the Standing Committee shall conduct the review; if necessary, they shall refer it to the relevant special committee for review and opinions.  
The relevant special committees of the National People's Congress and the working bodies of the Standing Committee may proactively review interpretations on the specific application of laws submitted for filing, and may conduct special reviews as needed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　全国人民代表大会宪法和法律委员会、有关专门委员会、常务委员会工作机构经审查认为最高人民法院或者最高人民检察院作出的具体应用法律的解释同宪法或者法律相抵触，或者存在合宪性、合法性问题需要修改或者废止，而最高人民法院或者最高人民检察院不予修改或者废止的，应当提出撤销或者要求最高人民法院或者最高人民检察院予以修改、废止的议案、建议，或者提出由全国人民代表大会常务委员会作出法律解释的议案、建议，由委员长会议决定提请常务委员会审议。

**Article 43** If, after review, the Constitution and Law Committee of the National People's Congress, the relevant special committee, or the working body of the Standing Committee considers that an interpretation on the specific application of laws made by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate contradicts the Constitution or laws, or has issues of constitutionality or legality requiring amendment or annulment, and the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate fails to amend or annul it, they shall propose a bill or recommendation for annulment or requiring the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate to amend or annul it, or propose a bill or recommendation for the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress to make a legal interpretation, and the Council of Chairpersons shall decide to submit it to the Standing Committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　备案审查机关应当建立健全备案审查衔接联动机制，对应当由其他机关处理的审查要求或者审查建议，及时移送有关机关处理。

**Article 44** The filing and review authorities shall establish and improve mechanisms for linkage and coordination in filing and review, and shall promptly transfer any review request or review recommendation that should be handled by another authority to the relevant authority for handling.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　常务委员会应当每年听取和审议备案审查工作情况报告。

**Article 45** The standing committee shall hear and deliberate a report on the work of filing and review each year.

### 第六章　询问、专题询问和质询

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会会议审议议案和有关报告时，本级人民政府或者有关部门、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院应当派有关负责人员到会，听取意见，回答询问。

**Article 46** When the standing committee of the people's congress at any level deliberates bills and relevant reports, the people's government or relevant departments, supervisory commission, people's court, or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level shall send relevant responsible persons to attend the meeting, listen to opinions, and answer questions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会围绕关系改革发展稳定大局和群众切身利益、社会普遍关注的重大问题，可以召开全体会议、联组会议或者分组会议，进行专题询问。本级人民政府及其有关部门、监察委员会、人民法院或者人民检察院的负责人应当到会，听取意见，回答询问。

**Article 47** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels may, with respect to major issues relating to the overall situation of reform, development and stability, the immediate interests of the people, and issues of general public concern, convene plenary meetings, joint group meetings, or group meetings to conduct specific inquiries. The responsible persons of the people's government at the corresponding level and its relevant departments, supervisory commission, people's court, or people's procuratorate shall attend the meetings, listen to opinions, and answer inquiries.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　专题询问应当坚持问题导向，增强针对性、实效性，积极回应社会关切。  
专题询问可以结合审议专项工作报告、执法检查报告或者其他报告进行。

**Article 48** Specific inquiries shall adhere to a problem-oriented approach, enhance pertinence and effectiveness, and actively respond to public concerns.  
Specific inquiries may be conducted in combination with the deliberation of special work reports, law enforcement inspection reports, or other reports.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　常务委员会开展专题询问前，可以组织开展专题调查研究，深入了解情况，广泛听取意见。  
常务委员会办事机构应当及时将有关专题调查研究报告和汇总的有关方面意见发给常务委员会组成人员。

**Article 49** Before conducting specific inquiries, the standing committee may organize special investigations and studies to gain in-depth understanding of the situation and extensively solicit opinions.  
The working office of the standing committee shall promptly distribute the relevant special investigation and study report and the compiled opinions from relevant parties to the members of the standing committee.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　专题询问中提出的意见交由有关国家机关研究处理，有关国家机关应当及时向常务委员会提交研究处理情况报告。必要时，委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定将研究处理情况报告提请常务委员会审议。

**Article 50** The opinions raised during specific inquiries shall be delivered to the relevant state organs for study and handling, and the relevant state organs shall promptly submit a report on the study and handling to the standing committee. If necessary, the Council of Chairpersons or the Council of Directors may decide to submit the report on the study and handling to the standing committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员十人以上联名，省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员五人以上联名，县级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员三人以上联名，可以向常务委员会书面提出对本级人民政府及其部门和监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院的质询案。  
质询案应当写明质询对象、质询的问题和内容。

**Article 51** No fewer than ten members of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, no fewer than five members of the standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts, or no fewer than three members of the standing committee of the people's congress of a county may, by joint signature, submit a proposal for a question in writing to the standing committee regarding the people's government at the same level and its departments, the supervisory commission, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate.  
A proposal for a question shall specify the organ to be questioned, the question raised, and the content thereof.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　质询案由委员长会议或者主任会议决定交由受质询的机关答复。  
委员长会议或者主任会议可以决定由受质询机关在常务委员会会议上或者有关专门委员会会议上口头答复，或者由受质询机关书面答复。在专门委员会会议上答复的，提质询案的常务委员会组成人员有权列席会议，发表意见。委员长会议或者主任会议认为必要时，可以将答复质询案的情况报告印发常务委员会会议。

**Article 52** The Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen shall decide to refer a proposal for a question to the organ to be questioned for a reply.  
The Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen may decide that the organ to be questioned shall give an oral reply at a meeting of the standing committee or at a meeting of the relevant special committee, or that it shall give a written reply. If the reply is given at a meeting of a special committee, the members of the standing committee who proposed the question shall have the right to attend the meeting as observers and express opinions. When the Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen deems it necessary, it may distribute the report on the reply to the question to the meeting of the standing committee.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　提质询案的常务委员会组成人员的过半数对受质询机关的答复不满意的，可以提出要求，经委员长会议或者主任会议决定，由受质询机关再作答复。

**Article 53** If a majority of the members of the standing committee who proposed the question are dissatisfied with the reply given by the organ to be questioned, they may make a request, and the question shall be answered again by the organ to be questioned after the Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen so decides.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　质询案以口头答复的，由受质询机关的负责人到会答复。质询案以书面答复的，由受质询机关的负责人签署。

**Article 54** If a proposal for a question is to be answered orally, the responsible person of the organ to be questioned shall attend the meeting to give the reply. If a proposal for a question is to be answered in writing, the reply shall be signed by the responsible person of the organ to be questioned.

### 第七章　特定问题调查

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会对属于其职权范围内的事项，需要作出决议、决定，但有关重大事实不清的，可以组织关于特定问题的调查委员会。

**Article 55** If a standing committee of a people's congress at any level needs to make a resolution or decision on a matter within its functions and powers, but the relevant material facts are unclear, it may establish an investigation committee on a specific issue.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　委员长会议或者主任会议可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提议组织关于特定问题的调查委员会，提请常务委员会审议。  
五分之一以上常务委员会组成人员书面联名，可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提议组织关于特定问题的调查委员会，由委员长会议或者主任会议决定提请常务委员会审议，或者先交有关的专门委员会审议、提出报告，再决定提请常务委员会审议。

**Article 56** The Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen may propose to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level to establish an investigation committee on a specific issue and shall submit the proposal to the standing committee for deliberation.  
No fewer than one-fifth of the members of the standing committee may, by joint signature in writing, propose to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level to establish an investigation committee on a specific issue. The Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen shall decide whether to submit the proposal to the standing committee for deliberation, or to refer it first to the relevant special committee for deliberation and report, and then decide whether to submit it to the standing committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　调查委员会由主任委员、副主任委员和委员组成，由委员长会议或者主任会议在本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员和本级人民代表大会代表中提名，提请常务委员会审议通过。调查委员会可以聘请有关专家参加调查工作。  
与调查的问题有利害关系的常务委员会组成人员和其他人员不得参加调查委员会。

**Article 57** An investigation committee shall be composed of a chairperson, vice chairpersons, and members, nominated by the Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen from among the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level, and shall be submitted to the standing committee for deliberation and adoption. The investigation committee may engage relevant experts to participate in the investigation work.  
Any member of the standing committee or other person who has an interest in the matter under investigation shall not participate in the investigation committee.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　调查委员会进行调查时，有关的国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织和公民都有义务向其提供必要的材料。  
提供材料的公民要求对材料来源保密的，调查委员会应当予以保密。  
调查委员会在调查过程中，可以不公布调查的情况和材料。

**Article 58** When an investigation committee conducts an investigation, the relevant state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and citizens shall have the obligation to provide it with necessary materials.  
If a citizen providing materials requests that the source of the materials be kept confidential, the investigation committee shall keep it confidential.  
The investigation committee may, during the investigation, refrain from disclosing the circumstances and materials of the investigation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　调查委员会应当向产生它的常务委员会提出调查报告。常务委员会根据报告，可以作出相应的决议、决定。

**Article 59** The investigation committee shall submit an investigation report to the standing committee that established it. The standing committee may, based on the report, make a corresponding resolution or decision.

### 第八章　撤职案的审议和决定

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，可以决定撤销本级人民政府个别副省长、自治区副主席、副市长、副州长、副县长、副区长的职务；可以撤销由它任命的本级人民政府其他组成人员和监察委员会副主任、委员，人民法院副院长、庭长、副庭长、审判委员会委员、审判员，人民检察院副检察长、检察委员会委员、检察员，中级人民法院院长，人民检察院分院检察长的职务。

**Article 60** During the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, the standing committee of the local people's congress at or above the county level may decide to remove from office individual vice governors of a province, vice chairpersons of an autonomous region, vice mayors of a city, vice prefects of a prefecture, vice heads of a county, or vice heads of a district of the people's government at the corresponding level; and may remove from office other members of the people's government at the corresponding level appointed by it, vice chairpersons and members of the supervisory commission, vice presidents of the people's court, chief judges of divisions, deputy chief judges of divisions, members of the judicial committee, judges, deputy chief procurators of the people's procuratorate, members of the procuratorial committee, procurators, presidents of an intermediate people's court, and chief procurators of a branch of the people's procuratorate.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上地方各级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院，可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出对[本法第六十条](#art-60)所列国家机关工作人员的撤职案。  
县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会主任会议，可以向常务委员会提出对[本法第六十条](#art-60)所列国家机关工作人员的撤职案。  
县级以上地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会五分之一以上的组成人员书面联名，可以向常务委员会提出对[本法第六十条](#art-60)所列国家机关工作人员的撤职案，由主任会议决定是否提请常务委员会会议审议；或者由主任会议提议，经全体会议决定，组织调查委员会，由以后的常务委员会会议根据调查委员会的报告审议决定。

**Article 61** The people's government, the supervisory commission, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate at or above the county level may submit to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level a proposal for removal of a functionary of a state organ listed in Article 60 of this Law.  
The council of chairmen of the standing committee of the people's congress at or above the county level may submit to the standing committee a proposal for removal of a functionary of a state organ listed in Article 60 of this Law.  
No fewer than one-fifth of the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at or above the county level may, by joint signature in writing, submit to the standing committee a proposal for removal of a functionary of a state organ listed in Article 60 of this Law. The council of chairmen shall decide whether to submit the proposal to the meeting of the standing committee for deliberation; or the council of chairmen may propose, and the plenary meeting decides, to establish an investigation committee, and the subsequent meeting of the standing committee shall deliberate and decide based on the report of the investigation committee.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　撤职案应当写明撤职的对象和理由，并提供有关的材料。  
撤职案在提请表决前，被提出撤职的人员有权在常务委员会会议上提出申辩意见，或者书面提出申辩意见，由主任会议决定印发常务委员会会议。  
撤职案的表决采用无记名投票的方式，由常务委员会全体组成人员的过半数通过。

**Article 62** A proposal for removal shall specify the person to be removed and the reasons therefor, and shall provide relevant materials.  
Before a proposal for removal is submitted for a vote, the person proposed for removal shall have the right to present a defense at a meeting of the standing committee, or to submit a defense in writing, which shall be distributed to the meeting of the standing committee upon decision of the council of chairmen.  
The vote on a proposal for removal shall be conducted by secret ballot and shall be adopted by a majority of all the members of the standing committee.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会制定年度监督工作计划，加强工作统筹，综合运用听取和审议专项工作报告、执法检查、专题询问、专题调研等方式进行监督，增强监督工作的针对性、协调性、实效性。  
年度监督工作计划由委员长会议或者主任会议通过并向社会公布。  
年度监督工作计划可以根据实际需要作出适当调整。

**Article 63** The standing committee of a people's congress at any level shall formulate an annual supervision work plan, strengthen overall planning of work, and comprehensively use methods such as hearing and deliberating special work reports, inspecting law enforcement, conducting special inquiries, and carrying out special investigations to exercise supervision, thereby enhancing the pertinence, coordination, and effectiveness of supervision.  
The annual supervision work plan shall be adopted by the Council of Chairpersons or the council of chairmen and be made public.  
The annual supervision work plan may be appropriately adjusted according to actual needs.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法和有关法律，结合本地实际情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 64** The standing committee of the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and relevant laws, and in light of the actual local conditions, formulate measures for implementation.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2007年1月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2007.
