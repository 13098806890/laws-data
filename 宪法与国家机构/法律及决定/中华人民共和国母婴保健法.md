# 中华人民共和国母婴保健法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-11-04  
**生效日期**：2017-11-05

> （1994年10月27日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2017年11月4日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国会计法〉等十一部法律的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　婚前保健 第三章　孕产期保健 第四章　技术鉴定 第五章　行政管理 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障母亲和婴儿健康，提高出生人口素质，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　国家发展母婴保健事业，提供必要条　件和物质帮助，使母亲和婴儿获得医疗保健服务。 国家对边远贫困地区的母婴保健事业给予扶持。 第三条　各级人民政府领导母婴保健工作。 母婴保健事业应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障母亲和婴儿健康，提高出生人口素质，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution with a view to safeguarding the health of mothers and infants and improving the quality of the newborn population.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家发展母婴保健事业，提供必要条　件和物质帮助，使母亲和婴儿获得医疗保健服务。  
国家对边远贫困地区的母婴保健事业给予扶持。

**Article 2** The State shall develop maternal and infant health care, provide necessary conditions and material assistance to enable mothers and infants to obtain medical and health care services.  
The State shall give support to maternal and infant health care in remote and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民政府领导母婴保健工作。  
母婴保健事业应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 3** People's governments at all levels shall lead the work of maternal and infant health care.  
Maternal and infant health care shall be incorporated into the plans for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院卫生行政部门主管全国母婴保健工作，根据不同地区情况提出分级分类指导原则，并对全国母婴保健工作实施监督管理。  
国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内，配合卫生行政部门做好母婴保健工作。

**Article 4** The health administration department under the State Council shall be in charge of maternal and infant health care throughout the country, put forward principles for guidance at different levels and in different categories in light of the conditions of various areas, and exercise supervision and administration over maternal and infant health care nationwide.  
Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, within the scope of their respective functions and duties, cooperate with the health administration department in carrying out maternal and infant health care.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励、支持母婴保健领域的教育和科学研究，推广先进、实用的母婴保健技术，普及母婴保健科学知识。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage and support education and scientific research in the field of maternal and infant health care, disseminate advanced and practical maternal and infant health care technologies, and popularize scientific knowledge about maternal and infant health care.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对在母婴保健工作中做出显著成绩和在母婴保健科学研究中取得显著成果的组织和个人，应当给予奖励。

**Article 6** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in maternal and infant health care work or have obtained remarkable results in scientific research on maternal and infant health care shall be rewarded.

### 第二章　婚前保健

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　医疗保健机构应当为公民提供婚前保健服务。  
婚前保健服务包括下列内容：  
（一）婚前卫生指导：关于性卫生知识、生育知识和遗传病知识的教育；  
（二）婚前卫生咨询：对有关婚配、生育保健等问题提供医学意见；  
（三）婚前医学检查：对准备结婚的男女双方可能患影响结婚和生育的疾病进行医学检查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 7** Medical and health care institutions shall provide premarital health care services for citizens.  
Premarital health care services shall include the following:  
(1) premarital health instruction: education on sexual hygiene knowledge, reproductive knowledge, and knowledge of genetic diseases;  
(2) premarital health consultation: provision of medical advice on matters such as marriage and reproductive health;  
(3) premarital medical examination: medical examination for both men and women preparing for marriage concerning diseases that may affect marriage and childbearing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　婚前医学检查包括对下列疾病的检查：  
（一）严重遗传性疾病；  
（二）指定传染病；  
（三）有关精神病。  
经婚前医学检查，医疗保健机构应当出具婚前医学检查证明。

**Article 8** Premarital medical examination shall include examination for the following diseases:  
(1) serious genetic diseases;  
(2) designated infectious diseases;  
(3) relevant mental diseases.  
After the premarital medical examination, the medical and health care institution shall issue a certificate of premarital medical examination.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经婚前医学检查，对患指定传染病在传染期内或者有关精神病在发病期内的，医师应当提出医学意见；准备结婚的男女双方应当暂缓结婚。

**Article 9** Where, upon premarital medical examination, a person is found to have a designated infectious disease in the infectious period or a relevant mental disease in the active period, the physician shall give medical advice; the man and woman preparing for marriage shall postpone their marriage.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经婚前医学检查，对诊断患医学上认为不宜生育的严重遗传性疾病的，医师应当向男女双方说明情况，提出医学意见；经男女双方同意，采取长效避孕措施或者施行结扎手术后不生育的，可以结婚。但《中华人民共和国婚姻法》规定禁止结婚的除外。

**Article 10** Where, upon premarital medical examination, a person is diagnosed with a serious genetic disease which is medically considered unsuitable for childbearing, the physician shall explain the situation to both the man and the woman and give medical advice; where the man and woman agree to adopt long-term contraceptive measures or undergo ligation operations to avoid childbearing, they may get married. However, this shall not apply where marriage is prohibited under the Marriage Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　接受婚前医学检查的人员对检查结果持有异议的，可以申请医学技术鉴定，取得医学鉴定证明。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** Any person who has undergone a premarital medical examination and disagrees with the results may apply for medical technical evaluation and obtain a certificate of medical evaluation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　男女双方在结婚登记时，应当持有婚前医学检查证明或者医学鉴定证明。

**Article 12** At the time of marriage registration, both the man and the woman shall hold a certificate of premarital medical examination or a certificate of medical evaluation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地区的实际情况，制定婚前医学检查制度实施办法。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对婚前医学检查应当规定合理的收费标准，对边远贫困地区或者交费确有困难的人员应当给予减免。

**Article 13** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate measures for implementing the premarital medical examination system based on the actual conditions of their respective localities.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall prescribe reasonable fees for premarital medical examination and shall provide reductions or exemptions for persons in remote and poverty-stricken areas or those who genuinely have difficulty in paying the fees.

### 第三章　孕产期保健

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　医疗保健机构应当为育龄妇女和孕产妇提供孕产期保健服务。  
孕产期保健服务包括下列内容：  
（一）母婴保健指导：对孕育健康后代以及严重遗传性疾病和碘缺乏病等地方病的发病原因、治疗和预防方法提供医学意见；  
（二）孕妇、产妇保健：为孕妇、产妇提供卫生、营养、心理等方面的咨询和指导以及产前定期检查等医疗保健服务；  
（三）胎儿保健：为胎儿生长发育进行监护，提供咨询和医学指导；  
（四）新生儿保健：为新生儿生长发育、哺乳和护理提供医疗保健服务。

**Article 14** Medical and health care institutions shall provide pregnancy and perinatal health care services for women of childbearing age and pregnant and lying-in women.  
Pregnancy and perinatal health care services shall include the following:  
(1) maternal and infant health care guidance: provision of medical advice on the causes, treatment, and prevention of serious genetic diseases, iodine deficiency disorders, and other endemic diseases, as well as on bearing healthy offspring;  
(2) health care for pregnant and lying-in women: provision of consultation and guidance on hygiene, nutrition, psychology, and other aspects, as well as regular prenatal checkups and other medical and health care services for pregnant and lying-in women;  
(3) fetal health care: monitoring the growth and development of the fetus, and providing consultation and medical guidance;  
(4) neonatal health care: provision of medical and health care services for the growth, development, breastfeeding, and nursing of newborns.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对患严重疾病或者接触致畸物质，妊娠可能危及孕妇生命安全或者可能严重影响孕妇健康和胎儿正常发育的，医疗保健机构应当予以医学指导。

**Article 15** Where a pregnant woman suffers from a serious disease or has been exposed to teratogenic substances, such that pregnancy may endanger her life or may seriously impair her health or the normal development of the fetus, the medical and health care institution shall provide medical guidance.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　医师发现或者怀疑患严重遗传性疾病的育龄夫妻，应当提出医学意见。育龄夫妻应当根据医师的医学意见采取相应的措施。

**Article 16** Where a physician discovers or suspects that a couple of childbearing age have or may have a serious genetic disease, the physician shall give medical advice. The couple of childbearing age shall take appropriate measures based on the physician's medical advice.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经产前检查，医师发现或者怀疑胎儿异常的，应当对孕妇进行产前诊断。

**Article 17** Where, upon prenatal examination, a physician discovers or suspects that the fetus is abnormal, the physician shall conduct prenatal diagnosis for the pregnant woman.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经产前诊断，有下列情形之一的，医师应当向夫妻双方说明情况，并提出终止妊娠的医学意见：  
（一）胎儿患严重遗传性疾病的；  
（二）胎儿有严重缺陷的；  
（三）因患严重疾病，继续妊娠可能危及孕妇生命安全或者严重危害孕妇健康的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/行政法规/中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国母婴保健法实施办法》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** Where, upon prenatal diagnosis, any of the following circumstances is found, the physician shall explain the situation to both husband and wife and give medical advice on terminating the pregnancy:  
(1) the fetus suffers from a serious genetic disease;  
(2) the fetus has a serious defect;  
(3) due to a serious disease, continuation of the pregnancy may endanger the pregnant woman's life or seriously harm her health.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　依照本法规定施行终止妊娠或者结扎手术，应当经本人同意，并签署意见。本人无行为能力的，应当经其监护人同意，并签署意见。  
依照本法规定施行终止妊娠或者结扎手术的，接受免费服务。

**Article 19** The performance of an abortion or ligation operation in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be subject to the consent of the person concerned, who shall sign an opinion. Where the person concerned has no capacity for civil conduct, the consent of the guardian shall be obtained, and the guardian shall sign an opinion.  
A person who undergoes an abortion or ligation operation in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall receive the service free of charge.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生育过严重缺陷患儿的妇女再次妊娠前，夫妻双方应当到县级以上医疗保健机构接受医学检查。

**Article 20** Before a woman who has given birth to a child with a serious defect becomes pregnant again, both the husband and wife shall go to a medical and health care institution at or above the county level for medical examination.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　医师和助产人员应当严格遵守有关操作规程，提高助产技术和服务质量，预防和减少产伤。

**Article 21** Physicians and midwives shall strictly observe the relevant operating procedures, improve midwifery skills and service quality, and prevent and reduce birth injuries.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　不能住院分娩的孕妇应当由经过培训、具备相应接生能力的接生人员实行消毒接生。

**Article 22** For a pregnant woman who cannot give birth in a hospital, delivery shall be conducted under sterile conditions by a birth attendant who has been trained and has the necessary delivery skills.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　医疗保健机构和从事家庭接生的人员按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定，出具统一制发的新生儿出生医学证明；有产妇和婴儿死亡以及新生儿出生缺陷情况的，应当向卫生行政部门报告。

**Article 23** Medical and health care institutions and persons engaged in home delivery shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administration department under the State Council, issue uniformly produced medical certificates of live birth for newborns; where there are cases of maternal or infant death or birth defects in newborns, they shall report to the health administration department.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗保健机构为产妇提供科学育儿、合理营养和母乳喂养的指导。  
医疗保健机构对婴儿进行体格检查和预防接种，逐步开展新生儿疾病筛查、婴儿多发病和常见病防治等医疗保健服务。

**Article 24** Medical and health care institutions shall provide guidance for lying-in women on scientific child care, proper nutrition, and breastfeeding.  
Medical and health care institutions shall conduct physical examinations and vaccinations for infants, and progressively carry out medical and health care services such as neonatal disease screening and prevention and treatment of frequently occurring and common diseases in infants.

### 第四章　技术鉴定

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府可以设立医学技术鉴定组织，负责对婚前医学检查、遗传病诊断和产前诊断结果有异议的进行医学技术鉴定。

**Article 25** Local people's governments at or above the county level may establish medical technical evaluation organizations, which shall be responsible for carrying out medical technical evaluation for persons who disagree with the results of premarital medical examination, genetic disease diagnosis, or prenatal diagnosis.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　从事医学技术鉴定的人员，必须具有临床经验和医学遗传学知识，并具有主治医师以上的专业技术职务。  
医学技术鉴定组织的组成人员，由卫生行政部门提名，同级人民政府聘任。

**Article 26** Persons engaged in medical technical evaluation must have clinical experience and knowledge of medical genetics, and hold a professional technical position of attending physician or above.  
The members of a medical technical evaluation organization shall be nominated by the health administration department and appointed by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　医学技术鉴定实行回避制度。凡与当事人有利害关系，可能影响公正鉴定的人员，应当回避。

**Article 27** A challenge system shall be practiced in medical technical evaluation. Any person who has an interest in a party and may affect the impartiality of the evaluation shall withdraw.

### 第五章　行政管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，加强母婴保健工作，提高医疗保健服务水平，积极防治由环境因素所致严重危害母亲和婴儿健康的地方性高发性疾病，促进母婴保健事业的发展。

**Article 28** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen maternal and infant health care, improve the level of medical and health care services, actively prevent and control endemic and high-incidence diseases caused by environmental factors that seriously endanger the health of mothers and infants, and promote the development of maternal and infant health care.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门管理本行政区域内的母婴保健工作。

**Article 29** The health administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall administer maternal and infant health care within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门指定的医疗保健机构负责本行政区域内的母婴保健监测和技术指导。

**Article 30** Medical and health care institutions designated by the health administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for maternal and infant health care monitoring and technical guidance within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　医疗保健机构按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定，负责其职责范围内的母婴保健工作，建立医疗保健工作规范，提高医学技术水平，采取各种措施方便人民群众，做好母婴保健服务工作。

**Article 31** Medical and health care institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administration department under the State Council, be responsible for maternal and infant health care within the scope of their duties, establish norms for medical and health care work, raise the level of medical technology, take various measures to facilitate the people, and provide good maternal and infant health care services.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医疗保健机构依照本法规定开展婚前医学检查、遗传病诊断、产前诊断以及施行结扎手术和终止妊娠手术的，必须符合国务院卫生行政部门规定的条件和技术标准，并经县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门许可。  
严禁采用技术手段对胎儿进行性别鉴定，但医学上确有需要的除外。

**Article 32** Medical and health care institutions that carry out premarital medical examination, genetic disease diagnosis, prenatal diagnosis, ligation operations, and abortion operations in accordance with the provisions of this Law must meet the conditions and technical standards prescribed by the health administration department under the State Council and obtain permission from the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
It is strictly prohibited to use technical means for gender identification of a fetus, except where medically necessary.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事本法规定的遗传病诊断、产前诊断的人员，必须经过省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门的考核，并取得相应的合格证书。  
从事本法规定的婚前医学检查、施行结扎手术和终止妊娠手术的人员，必须经过县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门的考核，并取得相应的合格证书。

**Article 33** Persons engaged in genetic disease diagnosis and prenatal diagnosis as provided for in this Law must pass the examination conducted by the health administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and obtain the corresponding qualification certificate.  
Persons engaged in premarital medical examination, ligation operations, and abortion operations as provided for in this Law must pass the examination conducted by the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level and obtain the corresponding qualification certificate.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事母婴保健工作的人员应当严格遵守职业道德，为当事人保守秘密。

**Article 34** Persons engaged in maternal and infant health care shall strictly observe professional ethics and keep confidential the secrets of the parties.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　未取得国家颁发的有关合格证书的，有下列行为之一，县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当予以制止，并可以根据情节给予警告或者处以罚款：  
（一）从事婚前医学检查、遗传病诊断、产前诊断或者医学技术鉴定的；  
（二）施行终止妊娠手术的；  
（三）出具本法规定的有关医学证明的。  
上款第（三）项出具的有关医学证明无效。

**Article 35** A person who, without obtaining the relevant qualification certificate issued by the State, commits any of the following acts shall be stopped by the health administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and may be given a warning or fined depending on the circumstances:  
(1) engaging in premarital medical examination, genetic disease diagnosis, prenatal diagnosis, or medical technical evaluation;  
(2) performing abortion operations;  
(3) issuing relevant medical certificates provided for in this Law.  
The relevant medical certificates issued under item (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be invalid.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　未取得国家颁发的有关合格证书，施行终止妊娠手术或者采取其他方法终止妊娠，致人死亡、残疾、丧失或者基本丧失劳动能力的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Any person who, without obtaining the relevant qualification certificate issued by the State, performs an abortion operation or uses other methods to terminate pregnancy, thereby causing death, disability, or loss or basic loss of the ability to work, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事母婴保健工作的人员违反本法规定，出具有关虚假医学证明或者进行胎儿性别鉴定的，由医疗保健机构或者卫生行政部门根据情节给予行政处分；情节严重的，依法取消执业资格。

**Article 37** Where a person engaged in maternal and infant health care violates the provisions of this Law by issuing a false relevant medical certificate or conducting fetal gender identification, the medical and health care institution or the health administration department shall impose administrative sanctions depending on the circumstances; if the circumstances are serious, the person's practice qualification shall be revoked according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本法下列用语的含义：  
指定传染病，是指《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》中规定的艾滋病、淋病、梅毒、麻疯病以及医学上认为影响结婚和生育的其他传染病。  
严重遗传性疾病，是指由于遗传因素先天形成，患者全部或者部分丧失自主生活能力，后代再现风险高，医学上认为不宜生育的遗传性疾病。  
有关精神病，是指精神分裂症、躁狂抑郁型精神病以及其他重型精神病。  
产前诊断，是指对胎儿进行先天性缺陷和遗传性疾病的诊断。

**Article 38** For the purposes of this Law, the meanings of the following terms are:  
Designated infectious diseases refer to AIDS, gonorrhea, syphilis, leprosy, and other infectious diseases that are medically considered to affect marriage and childbearing as stipulated in the Law on Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases of the People's Republic of China.  
Serious genetic diseases refer to genetic diseases that are formed congenitally due to genetic factors, causing the patient to lose all or part of the ability to live independently, with a high risk of recurrence in offspring, and are medically considered unsuitable for childbearing.  
Relevant mental diseases refer to schizophrenia, manic-depressive psychosis, and other severe mental diseases.  
Prenatal diagnosis refers to the diagnosis of congenital defects and genetic diseases in a fetus.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本法自1995年6月1日起施行。

**Article 39** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 1995.
