# 中华人民共和国人民陪审员法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-04-27  
**生效日期**：2018-04-27

> （2018年4月27日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过） 第一条　为了保障公民依法参加审判活动，促进司法公正，提升司法公信，制定本法。 第二条　公民有依法担任人民陪审员的权利和义务。 人民陪审员依照本法产生，依法参加人民法院的审判活动，除法律另有规定外，同法官有同等权利。 第三条　人民陪审员依法享有参加审判活动、独立发表意见、获得履职保障等权利。 人民陪审员应当忠实履行审判职责，保守审判秘密，注重司法礼仪，维护司法形象。 第四条　人民陪审员依法参加审判活动，受法律保护。 人民法院应当依法保障人民陪审员履行审判职责。 人民陪审员所在单位、户籍所在地或者经常居住地的基层群众性自治组织应当依法保障人民陪审员参加审判活动。 第五条　公民担任人民陪审员，应当具备下列条件： （一）拥护中华人民共和国宪法； （二）年满二十八周岁； （三）遵纪守法、品行良好、公道正派； （四）具有正常履行职责的身体条件。 担任人民陪审员，一般应当具有高中以上文化程度。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公民依法参加审判活动，促进司法公正，提升司法公信，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to safeguard citizens' participation in adjudication activities according to law, promote judicial justice, and enhance judicial credibility.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公民有依法担任人民陪审员的权利和义务。  
人民陪审员依照本法产生，依法参加人民法院的审判活动，除法律另有规定外，同法官有同等权利。

**Article 2** Citizens shall have the right and obligation to serve as people's assessors according to law.  
People's assessors shall be selected in accordance with this Law, and participate in the adjudication activities of the People's Court according to law. Unless otherwise provided by law, they shall have the same rights as judges.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民陪审员依法享有参加审判活动、独立发表意见、获得履职保障等权利。  
人民陪审员应当忠实履行审判职责，保守审判秘密，注重司法礼仪，维护司法形象。

**Article 3** People's assessors shall enjoy, according to law, the rights to participate in adjudication activities, independently express opinions, and obtain guarantees for performing their duties.  
People's assessors shall faithfully perform adjudication duties, keep trial secrets, observe judicial etiquette, and uphold the judicial image.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民陪审员依法参加审判活动，受法律保护。  
人民法院应当依法保障人民陪审员履行审判职责。  
人民陪审员所在单位、户籍所在地或者经常居住地的基层群众性自治组织应当依法保障人民陪审员参加审判活动。

**Article 4** People's assessors participating in adjudication activities according to law shall be protected by law.  
The People's Court shall, according to law, guarantee that people's assessors perform their adjudication duties.  
The unit where a people's assessor works, the grassroots self-governing organization of the place of domicile or habitual residence shall, according to law, guarantee the people's assessor's participation in adjudication activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民担任人民陪审员，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）拥护中华人民共和国宪法；  
（二）年满二十八周岁；  
（三）遵纪守法、品行良好、公道正派；  
（四）具有正常履行职责的身体条件。  
担任人民陪审员，一般应当具有高中以上文化程度。

**Article 5** A citizen serving as a people's assessor shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Supporting the Constitution of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Being at least 28 years old;  
(3) Observing discipline and law, having good conduct, and being fair and upright;  
(4) Having physical conditions enabling normal performance of duties.  
A person serving as a people's assessor shall generally have an education level of senior high school or above.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列人员不能担任人民陪审员：  
（一）人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员，监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、司法行政机关的工作人员；  
（二）律师、公证员、仲裁员、基层法律服务工作者；  
（三）其他因职务原因不适宜担任人民陪审员的人员。

**Article 6** The following persons shall not serve as people's assessors:  
(1) Members of the Standing Committee of the People's Congress, staff of the Supervisory Commission, the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ, the State security organ, and the judicial administration organ;  
(2) Lawyers, notaries, arbitrators, and grassroots legal service workers;  
(3) Other persons unsuitable to serve as people's assessors due to their positions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任人民陪审员：  
（一）受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）被开除公职的；  
（三）被吊销律师、公证员执业证书的；  
（四）被纳入失信被执行人名单的；  
（五）因受惩戒被免除人民陪审员职务的；  
（六）其他有严重违法违纪行为，可能影响司法公信的。

**Article 7** A person falling under any of the following circumstances shall not be appointed as a people's assessor:  
(1) Having been subjected to criminal punishment;  
(2) Having been expelled from public office;  
(3) Having had his/her lawyer's or notary's practice certificate revoked;  
(4) Having been included in the list of dishonest judgment debtors;  
(5) Having been removed from the position of people's assessor due to disciplinary punishment;  
(6) Having other serious illegal or disciplinary acts that may affect judicial credibility.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民陪审员的名额，由基层人民法院根据审判案件的需要，提请同级人民代表大会常务委员会确定。  
人民陪审员的名额数不低于本院法官数的三倍。

**Article 8** The number of people's assessors shall be determined by the Primary People's Court upon submission to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the same level, based on the needs of adjudicating cases.  
The number of people's assessors shall be no less than three times the number of judges in that court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　司法行政机关会同基层人民法院、公安机关，从辖区内的常住居民名单中随机抽选拟任命人民陪审员数五倍以上的人员作为人民陪审员候选人，对人民陪审员候选人进行资格审查，征求候选人意见。

**Article 9** The judicial administration organ shall, jointly with the Primary People's Court and the public security organ, randomly select from the list of permanent residents within its jurisdiction persons who are at least five times the number of people's assessors to be appointed, as candidates for people's assessors, conduct qualification examination of the candidates, and seek their opinions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　司法行政机关会同基层人民法院，从通过资格审查的人民陪审员候选人名单中随机抽选确定人民陪审员人选，由基层人民法院院长提请同级人民代表大会常务委员会任命。

**Article 10** The judicial administration organ shall, jointly with the Primary People's Court, randomly select and determine the persons to be people's assessors from the list of candidates who have passed the qualification examination. The president of the Primary People's Court shall submit to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the same level for appointment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因审判活动需要，可以通过个人申请和所在单位、户籍所在地或者经常居住地的基层群众性自治组织、人民团体推荐的方式产生人民陪审员候选人，经司法行政机关会同基层人民法院、公安机关进行资格审查，确定人民陪审员人选，由基层人民法院院长提请同级人民代表大会常务委员会任命。  
依照前款规定产生的人民陪审员，不得超过人民陪审员名额数的五分之一。

**Article 11** Where necessary for adjudication activities, candidates for people's assessors may be produced through individual application or recommendation by the unit, the grassroots self-governing organization of the place of domicile or habitual residence, or a people's organization. After the qualification examination jointly conducted by the judicial administration organ, the Primary People's Court, and the public security organ, the persons to be people's assessors shall be determined, and the president of the Primary People's Court shall submit to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the same level for appointment.  
The number of people's assessors produced in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall not exceed one-fifth of the total number of people's assessors.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民陪审员经人民代表大会常务委员会任命后，应当公开进行就职宣誓。宣誓仪式由基层人民法院会同司法行政机关组织。

**Article 12** After being appointed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress, a people's assessor shall take a public oath of office. The oath ceremony shall be organized by the Primary People's Court jointly with the judicial administration organ.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民陪审员的任期为五年，一般不得连任。

**Article 13** The term of office of a people's assessor shall be five years, and generally no reappointment shall be allowed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判案件，由法官担任审判长，可以组成三人合议庭，也可以由法官三人与人民陪审员四人组成七人合议庭。

**Article 14** A collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges shall adjudicate cases, with a judge serving as the presiding judge. A three-person collegial panel may be formed, or a seven-person collegial panel may be formed consisting of three judges and four people's assessors.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院审判第一审刑事、民事、行政案件，有下列情形之一的，由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭进行：  
（一）涉及群体利益、公共利益的；  
（二）人民群众广泛关注或者其他社会影响较大的；  
（三）案情复杂或者有其他情形，需要由人民陪审员参加审判的。  
人民法院审判前款规定的案件，法律规定由法官独任审理或者由法官组成合议庭审理的，从其规定。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[2]</sup>&thinsp;<sup>[3]</sup>

**Article 15** Where a People's Court adjudicates a criminal, civil, or administrative case of first instance falling under any of the following circumstances, a collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges shall hear the case:  
(1) Involving group interests or public interests;  
(2) Widely concerned by the public or otherwise having relatively major social impact;  
(3) Complex case facts or other circumstances requiring participation of people's assessors in the adjudication.  
Where the law provides that a case specified in the preceding paragraph shall be tried by a sole judge or by a collegial panel composed of judges, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审判下列第一审案件，由人民陪审员和法官组成七人合议庭进行：  
（一）可能判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑、死刑，社会影响重大的刑事案件；  
（二）根据民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法提起的公益诉讼案件；  
（三）涉及征地拆迁、生态环境保护、食品药品安全，社会影响重大的案件；  
（四）其他社会影响重大的案件。

**Article 16** A People's Court shall adjudicate the following first-instance cases by a seven-person collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges:  
(1) Criminal cases where the possible sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of ten years or more, life imprisonment, or the death penalty, and which have major social impact;  
(2) Public interest litigation cases filed in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law;  
(3) Cases involving land expropriation and relocation, ecological environmental protection, or food and drug safety, and which have major social impact;  
(4) Other cases having major social impact.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　第一审刑事案件被告人、民事案件原告或者被告、行政案件原告申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，人民法院可以决定由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判。

**Article 17** Where the defendant in a criminal case of first instance, the plaintiff or defendant in a civil case, or the plaintiff in an administrative case applies for a collegial panel including people's assessors, the People's Court may decide to form a collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges for adjudication.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民陪审员的回避，适用审判人员回避的法律规定。

**Article 18** The withdrawal of a people's assessor shall be governed by the legal provisions on the withdrawal of adjudicators.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　基层人民法院审判案件需要由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，应当在人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。  
中级人民法院、高级人民法院审判案件需要由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，在其辖区内的基层人民法院的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

**Article 19** Where a Primary People's Court needs people's assessors to participate in a collegial panel for adjudicating a case, it shall randomly select from the list of people's assessors.  
Where an Intermediate People's Court or a Higher People's Court needs people's assessors to participate in a collegial panel for adjudicating a case, it shall randomly select from the list of people's assessors of the Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　审判长应当履行与案件审判相关的指引、提示义务，但不得妨碍人民陪审员对案件的独立判断。  
合议庭评议案件，审判长应当对本案中涉及的事实认定、证据规则、法律规定等事项及应当注意的问题，向人民陪审员进行必要的解释和说明。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 20** The presiding judge shall perform the obligations of guidance and reminders related to the adjudication of the case, but shall not hinder the independent judgment of the people's assessors on the case.  
When the collegial panel deliberates a case, the presiding judge shall provide necessary explanations and clarifications to the people's assessors regarding matters such as factual findings, rules of evidence, legal provisions, and issues that should be noted in the case.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民陪审员参加三人合议庭审判案件，对事实认定、法律适用，独立发表意见，行使表决权。

**Article 21** When a people's assessor participates in a three-person collegial panel to adjudicate a case, he/she shall independently express opinions on factual findings and application of law, and exercise the right to vote.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民陪审员参加七人合议庭审判案件，对事实认定，独立发表意见，并与法官共同表决；对法律适用，可以发表意见，但不参加表决。

**Article 22** When a people's assessor participates in a seven-person collegial panel to adjudicate a case, he/she shall independently express opinions on factual findings and vote together with the judges; regarding the application of law, he/she may express opinions but shall not participate in the vote.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　合议庭评议案件，实行少数服从多数的原则。人民陪审员同合议庭其他组成人员意见分歧的，应当将其意见写入笔录。  
合议庭组成人员意见有重大分歧的，人民陪审员或者法官可以要求合议庭将案件提请院长决定是否提交审判委员会讨论决定。

**Article 23** When the collegial panel deliberates a case, the principle of minority being subordinate to majority shall apply. Where a people's assessor disagrees with other members of the collegial panel, his/her opinion shall be recorded in the minutes.  
Where there is a significant disagreement among the members of the collegial panel, a people's assessor or a judge may request that the collegial panel submit the case to the president for a decision on whether to refer it to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民法院应当结合本辖区实际情况，合理确定每名人民陪审员年度参加审判案件的数量上限，并向社会公告。

**Article 24** The People's Court shall, taking into account the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction, reasonably determine the maximum number of cases each people's assessor may participate in adjudicating within a year, and shall announce this to the public.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民陪审员的培训、考核和奖惩等日常管理工作，由基层人民法院会同司法行政机关负责。  
对人民陪审员应当有计划地进行培训。人民陪审员应当按照要求参加培训。

**Article 25** The routine management of training, assessment, rewards, and punishments of people's assessors shall be the responsibility of the Primary People's Court jointly with the judicial administration organ.  
People's assessors shall receive training in a planned manner. People's assessors shall participate in training as required.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对于在审判工作中有显著成绩或者有其他突出事迹的人民陪审员，依照有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 26** People's assessors who have made remarkable achievements or outstanding contributions in adjudication work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民陪审员有下列情形之一，经所在基层人民法院会同司法行政机关查证属实的，由院长提请同级人民代表大会常务委员会免除其人民陪审员职务：  
（一）本人因正当理由申请辞去人民陪审员职务的；  
（二）具有[本法第六条](#art-6)、第七条所列情形之一的；  
（三）无正当理由，拒绝参加审判活动，影响审判工作正常进行的；  
（四）违反与审判工作有关的法律及相关规定，徇私舞弊，造成错误裁判或者其他严重后果的。  
人民陪审员有前款第三项、第四项所列行为的，可以采取通知其所在单位、户籍所在地或者经常居住地的基层群众性自治组织、人民团体，在辖区范围内公开通报等措施进行惩戒；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where a people's assessor falls under any of the following circumstances, and after verification by the Primary People's Court jointly with the judicial administration organ, the president shall submit to the Standing Committee of the People's Congress at the same level for removal from the position of people's assessor:  
(1) The person applies to resign from the position of people's assessor for a legitimate reason;  
(2) Falling under any of the circumstances specified in Article 6 or Article 7 of this Law;  
(3) Without legitimate reason, refusing to participate in adjudication activities, affecting the normal conduct of adjudication work;  
(4) Violating laws and relevant provisions related to adjudication work, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, causing erroneous judgments or other serious consequences.  
Where a people's assessor has acted as specified in Items (3) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, disciplinary measures such as notifying the unit, the grassroots self-governing organization of the place of domicile or habitual residence, or a people's organization, and public disclosure within the jurisdiction, may be taken; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民陪审员的人身和住所安全受法律保护。任何单位和个人不得对人民陪审员及其近亲属打击报复。  
对报复陷害、侮辱诽谤、暴力侵害人民陪审员及其近亲属的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 28** The personal safety and safety of residence of a people's assessor shall be protected by law. No entity or individual shall retaliate against a people's assessor or his/her close relatives.  
Anyone who retaliates, frames, insults, slanders, or violently infringes upon a people's assessor or his/her close relatives shall be subject to legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人民陪审员参加审判活动期间，所在单位不得克扣或者变相克扣其工资、奖金及其他福利待遇。  
人民陪审员所在单位违反前款规定的，基层人民法院应当及时向人民陪审员所在单位或者所在单位的主管部门、上级部门提出纠正意见。

**Article 29** During the period when a people's assessor participates in adjudication activities, the unit where he/she works shall not deduct or covertly deduct his/her wages, bonuses, or other welfare benefits.  
Where the unit where a people's assessor works violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the Primary People's Court shall promptly submit corrective opinions to the unit or the competent authority or superior department of the unit.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民陪审员参加审判活动期间，由人民法院依照有关规定按实际工作日给予补助。  
人民陪审员因参加审判活动而支出的交通、就餐等费用，由人民法院依照有关规定给予补助。

**Article 30** During the period when a people's assessor participates in adjudication activities, the People's Court shall provide subsidies according to the actual working days in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Expenses such as transportation and meals incurred by a people's assessor due to participation in adjudication activities shall be subsidized by the People's Court in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民陪审员因参加审判活动应当享受的补助，人民法院和司法行政机关为实施人民陪审员制度所必需的开支，列入人民法院和司法行政机关业务经费，由相应政府财政予以保障。具体办法由最高人民法院、国务院司法行政部门会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 31** The subsidies that people's assessors shall enjoy for participating in adjudication activities, and the necessary expenses incurred by the People's Court and the judicial administration organ for implementing the people's assessor system, shall be included in the operating expenses of the People's Court and the judicial administration organ, and shall be guaranteed by the corresponding government finance. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Supreme People's Court and the judicial administration department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法自公布之日起施行。2004年8月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议通过的《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于完善人民陪审员制度的决定》同时废止。

**Article 32** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Improving the People's Assessor System adopted at the 11th Meeting of the Standing Committee of the 10th National People's Congress on August 28, 2004 shall be abolished simultaneously.
