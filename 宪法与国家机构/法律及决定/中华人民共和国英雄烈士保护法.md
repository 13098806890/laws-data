# 中华人民共和国英雄烈士保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-04-27  
**生效日期**：2018-05-01

> （2018年4月27日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过） 第一条　为了加强对英雄烈士的保护，维护社会公共利益，传承和弘扬英雄烈士精神、爱国主义精神，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，激发实现中华民族伟大复兴中国梦的强大精神力量，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　国家和人民永远尊崇、铭记英雄烈士为国家、人民和民族作出的牺牲和贡献。 近代以来，为了争取民族独立和人民解放，实现国家富强和人民幸福，促进世界和平和人类进步而毕生奋斗、英勇献身的英雄烈士，功勋彪炳史册，精神永垂不朽。 第三条　英雄烈士事迹和精神是中华民族的共同历史记忆和社会主义核心价值观的重要体现。 国家保护英雄烈士，对英雄烈士予以褒扬、纪念，加强对英雄烈士事迹和精神的宣传、教育，维护英雄烈士尊严和合法权益。 全社会都应当崇尚、学习、捍卫英雄烈士。 第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对英雄烈士的保护，将宣传、弘扬英雄烈士事迹和精神作为社会主义精神文明建设的重要内容。 县级以上人民政府负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门和其他有关部门应当依法履行职责，做好英雄烈士保护工作。 军队有关部门按照国务院、中央军事委员会的规定，做好英雄烈士保护工作。 县级以上人民政府应当将英雄烈士保护工作经费列入本级预算。 第五条　每年9月30日为烈士纪念日，国家在首都北京天安门广场人民英雄纪念碑前举行纪念仪式，缅怀英雄烈士。 县级以上地方人民政府、军队有关部门应当在烈士纪念日举行纪念活动。 举行英雄烈士纪念活动，邀请英雄烈士遗属代表参加。 第六条　在清明节和重要纪念日，机关、团体、乡村、社区、学校、企业事业单位和军队有关单位根据实际情况，组织开展英雄烈士纪念活动。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对英雄烈士的保护，维护社会公共利益，传承和弘扬英雄烈士精神、爱国主义精神，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，激发实现中华民族伟大复兴中国梦的强大精神力量，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of strengthening the protection of heroes and martyrs, safeguarding public interests, inheriting and promoting the spirit of heroes and martyrs and the spirit of patriotism, cultivating and practicing the core socialist values, and inspiring a powerful spiritual force for realizing the Chinese Dream of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家和人民永远尊崇、铭记英雄烈士为国家、人民和民族作出的牺牲和贡献。  
近代以来，为了争取民族独立和人民解放，实现国家富强和人民幸福，促进世界和平和人类进步而毕生奋斗、英勇献身的英雄烈士，功勋彪炳史册，精神永垂不朽。

**Article 2** The State and the people shall forever respect and remember the sacrifices and contributions made by heroes and martyrs to the State, the people, and the nation.  
Since modern times, heroes and martyrs who have fought their entire lives and heroically sacrificed themselves for national independence and people's liberation, for national prosperity and people's happiness, and for world peace and human progress, have their merits recorded in history and their spirit immortal.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　英雄烈士事迹和精神是中华民族的共同历史记忆和社会主义核心价值观的重要体现。  
国家保护英雄烈士，对英雄烈士予以褒扬、纪念，加强对英雄烈士事迹和精神的宣传、教育，维护英雄烈士尊严和合法权益。  
全社会都应当崇尚、学习、捍卫英雄烈士。

**Article 3** The deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs are the common historical memory of the Chinese nation and an important manifestation of the core socialist values.  
The State shall protect heroes and martyrs, commend and commemorate them, strengthen the publicity and education of their deeds and spirit, and safeguard their dignity and lawful rights and interests.  
The whole society shall advocate, learn from, and defend heroes and martyrs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对英雄烈士的保护，将宣传、弘扬英雄烈士事迹和精神作为社会主义精神文明建设的重要内容。  
县级以上人民政府负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门和其他有关部门应当依法履行职责，做好英雄烈士保护工作。  
军队有关部门按照国务院、中央军事委员会的规定，做好英雄烈士保护工作。  
县级以上人民政府应当将英雄烈士保护工作经费列入本级预算。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the protection of heroes and martyrs, and regard the publicity and promotion of the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs as an important part of the development of socialist spiritual civilization.  
The departments responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs under the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall perform their duties according to law and do a good job in the protection of heroes and martyrs.  
Relevant departments of the armed forces shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, do a good job in the protection of heroes and martyrs.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall include the funds for the protection of heroes and martyrs in their budgets.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　每年9月30日为烈士纪念日，国家在首都北京天安门广场人民英雄纪念碑前举行纪念仪式，缅怀英雄烈士。  
县级以上地方人民政府、军队有关部门应当在烈士纪念日举行纪念活动。  
举行英雄烈士纪念活动，邀请英雄烈士遗属代表参加。

**Article 5** September 30 of each year shall be Martyrs' Memorial Day, and the State shall hold a commemorative ceremony at the Monument to the People's Heroes in Tiananmen Square, Beijing, the capital, to remember heroes and martyrs.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments of the armed forces shall hold commemorative activities on Martyrs' Memorial Day.  
When holding commemorative activities for heroes and martyrs, representatives of the surviving family members of heroes and martyrs shall be invited to participate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在清明节和重要纪念日，机关、团体、乡村、社区、学校、企业事业单位和军队有关单位根据实际情况，组织开展英雄烈士纪念活动。

**Article 6** On Qingming Festival and important commemorative days, state organs, organizations, villages, communities, schools, enterprises, public institutions, and relevant units of the armed forces shall, based on actual conditions, organize commemorative activities for heroes and martyrs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立并保护英雄烈士纪念设施，纪念、缅怀英雄烈士。  
矗立在首都北京天安门广场的人民英雄纪念碑，是近代以来中国人民和中华民族争取民族独立解放、人民自由幸福和国家繁荣富强精神的象征，是国家和人民纪念、缅怀英雄烈士的永久性纪念设施。  
人民英雄纪念碑及其名称、碑题、碑文、浮雕、图形、标志等受法律保护。

**Article 7** The State shall establish and protect commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs to commemorate and remember them.  
The Monument to the People's Heroes standing in Tiananmen Square, Beijing, the capital, is a symbol of the spirit of the Chinese people and the Chinese nation in striving for national independence and liberation, people's freedom and happiness, and national prosperity and strength since modern times, and is a permanent commemorative facility for the State and the people to commemorate and remember heroes and martyrs.  
The Monument to the People's Heroes and its name, inscription, text, reliefs, graphics, emblems, etc. shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府应当将英雄烈士纪念设施建设和保护纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、城乡规划，加强对英雄烈士纪念设施的保护和管理；对具有重要纪念意义、教育意义的英雄烈士纪念设施依照《中华人民共和国文物保护法》的规定，核定公布为文物保护单位。  
中央财政对革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、贫困地区英雄烈士纪念设施的修缮保护，应当按照国家规定予以补助。

**Article 8** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction and protection of commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs into the national economic and social development plans and urban and rural plans, and strengthen the protection and management of such facilities; for commemorative facilities with important commemorative and educational significance, they shall verify and publish them as protected cultural heritage sites in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Cultural Relics.  
The central finance shall, in accordance with state provisions, provide subsidies for the repair and protection of commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　英雄烈士纪念设施应当免费向社会开放，供公众瞻仰、悼念英雄烈士，开展纪念教育活动，告慰先烈英灵。  
前款规定的纪念设施由军队有关单位管理的，按照军队有关规定实行开放。

**Article 9** Commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs shall be open to the public free of charge for the public to pay their respects, mourn heroes and martyrs, carry out commemorative education activities, and comfort the souls of the martyrs.  
Where the commemorative facilities specified in the preceding paragraph are managed by relevant units of the armed forces, they shall be opened in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　英雄烈士纪念设施保护单位应当健全服务和管理工作规范，方便瞻仰、悼念英雄烈士，保持英雄烈士纪念设施庄严、肃穆、清净的环境和氛围。  
任何组织和个人不得在英雄烈士纪念设施保护范围内从事有损纪念英雄烈士环境和氛围的活动，不得侵占英雄烈士纪念设施保护范围内的土地和设施，不得破坏、污损英雄烈士纪念设施。

**Article 10** The units responsible for the protection of commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs shall improve their service and management standards to facilitate paying respects and mourning heroes and martyrs, and maintain a solemn, respectful, and peaceful environment and atmosphere of such facilities.  
No organization or individual may engage in activities detrimental to the environment and atmosphere of commemorating heroes and martyrs within the protected areas of commemorative facilities, may not encroach upon the land and facilities within such protected areas, and may not damage or defile such commemorative facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　安葬英雄烈士时，县级以上人民政府、军队有关部门应当举行庄严、肃穆、文明、节俭的送迎、安葬仪式。

**Article 11** When burying heroes and martyrs, the people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments of the armed forces shall hold solemn, respectful, civilized, and frugal sending-off and burial ceremonies.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家建立健全英雄烈士祭扫制度和礼仪规范，引导公民庄严有序地开展祭扫活动。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当为英雄烈士遗属祭扫提供便利。

**Article 12** The State shall establish and improve the system and etiquette norms for paying homage to heroes and martyrs, and guide citizens to carry out homage activities in a solemn and orderly manner.  
Relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide convenience for the surviving family members of heroes and martyrs to pay homage.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当引导公民通过瞻仰英雄烈士纪念设施、集体宣誓、网上祭奠等形式，铭记英雄烈士的事迹，传承和弘扬英雄烈士的精神。

**Article 13** Relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall guide citizens to remember the deeds of heroes and martyrs and inherit and promote their spirit through such forms as visiting commemorative facilities, collective oaths, online tributes, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　英雄烈士在国外安葬的，中华人民共和国驻该国外交、领事代表机构应当结合驻在国实际情况组织开展祭扫活动。  
国家通过与有关国家的合作，查找、收集英雄烈士遗骸、遗物和史料，加强对位于国外的英雄烈士纪念设施的修缮保护工作。

**Article 14** Where heroes and martyrs are buried abroad, the diplomatic and consular missions of the People's Republic of China in those countries shall organize and carry out homage activities in light of the actual conditions of the host country.  
The State shall, through cooperation with relevant countries, search for and collect the remains, relics, and historical materials of heroes and martyrs, and strengthen the repair and protection of commemorative facilities for heroes and martyrs located abroad.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家鼓励和支持开展对英雄烈士事迹和精神的研究，以辩证唯物主义和历史唯物主义为指导认识和记述历史。

**Article 15** The State shall encourage and support research on the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs, and recognize and record history under the guidance of dialectical materialism and historical materialism.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级人民政府、军队有关部门应当加强对英雄烈士遗物、史料的收集、保护和陈列展示工作，组织开展英雄烈士史料的研究、编纂和宣传工作。  
国家鼓励和支持革命老区发挥当地资源优势，开展英雄烈士事迹和精神的研究、宣传和教育工作。

**Article 16** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments of the armed forces shall strengthen the collection, protection, and display of the relics and historical materials of heroes and martyrs, and organize and carry out research, compilation, and publicity of such materials.  
The State shall encourage and support old revolutionary base areas to leverage local resource advantages to carry out research, publicity, and education on the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　教育行政部门应当以青少年学生为重点，将英雄烈士事迹和精神的宣传教育纳入国民教育体系。  
教育行政部门、各级各类学校应当将英雄烈士事迹和精神纳入教育内容，组织开展纪念教育活动，加强对学生的爱国主义、集体主义、社会主义教育。

**Article 17** The administrative department of education shall, with a focus on young students, incorporate the publicity and education of the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs into the national education system.  
The administrative department of education and schools of all types and levels shall incorporate the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs into educational content, organize commemorative education activities, and strengthen education on patriotism, collectivism, and socialism for students.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　文化、新闻出版、广播电视、电影、网信等部门应当鼓励和支持以英雄烈士事迹为题材、弘扬英雄烈士精神的优秀文学艺术作品、广播电视节目以及出版物的创作生产和宣传推广。

**Article 18** The departments of culture, press and publication, radio and television, film, and cyberspace administration shall encourage and support the creation, production, and promotion of excellent literary and artistic works, radio and television programs, and publications that are themed on the deeds of heroes and martyrs and promote their spirit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　广播电台、电视台、报刊出版单位、互联网信息服务提供者，应当通过播放或者刊登英雄烈士题材作品、发布公益广告、开设专栏等方式，广泛宣传英雄烈士事迹和精神。

**Article 19** Radio stations, television stations, newspaper and periodical publishers, and internet information service providers shall, through broadcast or publication of works themed on heroes and martyrs, release of public service advertisements, setting up special columns, and other means, extensively publicize the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家鼓励和支持自然人、法人和非法人组织以捐赠财产、义务宣讲英雄烈士事迹和精神、帮扶英雄烈士遗属等公益活动的方式，参与英雄烈士保护工作。  
自然人、法人和非法人组织捐赠财产用于英雄烈士保护的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 20** The State shall encourage and support natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to participate in the protection of heroes and martyrs through such public welfare activities as donating property, voluntarily giving lectures on the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs, and assisting the surviving family members of heroes and martyrs.  
Where natural persons, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations donate property for the protection of heroes and martyrs, they shall enjoy tax benefits according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家实行英雄烈士抚恤优待制度。英雄烈士遗属按照国家规定享受教育、就业、养老、住房、医疗等方面的优待。抚恤优待水平应当与国民经济和社会发展相适应并逐步提高。  
国务院有关部门、军队有关部门和地方人民政府应当关心英雄烈士遗属的生活情况，每年定期走访慰问英雄烈士遗属。

**Article 21** The State shall implement a system of pensions and preferential treatment for heroes and martyrs. The surviving family members of heroes and martyrs shall enjoy preferential treatment in education, employment, elderly care, housing, medical care, etc. according to state provisions. The level of pensions and preferential treatment shall be compatible with the national economic and social development and shall be gradually improved.  
Relevant departments of the State Council, relevant departments of the armed forces, and local people's governments shall care about the living conditions of the surviving family members of heroes and martyrs, and visit and express condolences to them regularly each year.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止歪曲、丑化、亵渎、否定英雄烈士事迹和精神。  
英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉受法律保护。任何组织和个人不得在公共场所、互联网或者利用广播电视、电影、出版物等，以侮辱、诽谤或者其他方式侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉。任何组织和个人不得将英雄烈士的姓名、肖像用于或者变相用于商标、商业广告，损害英雄烈士的名誉、荣誉。  
公安、文化、新闻出版、广播电视、电影、网信、市场监督管理、负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门发现前款规定行为的，应当依法及时处理。

**Article 22** It is prohibited to distort, vilify, desecrate, or deny the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs.  
The names, portraits, reputation, and honor of heroes and martyrs shall be protected by law. No organization or individual may infringe upon the names, portraits, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs by means of insult, defamation, or other means in public places, on the internet, or through radio, television, film, publications, etc. No organization or individual may use or indirectly use the names or portraits of heroes and martyrs for trademarks or commercial advertisements, thereby damaging their reputation and honor.  
The public security departments, cultural departments, press and publication departments, radio and television departments, film departments, cyberspace administration departments, market supervision and administration departments, and departments responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs shall, upon discovering any conduct as specified in the preceding paragraph, handle it in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　网信和电信、公安等有关部门在对网络信息进行依法监督管理工作中，发现发布或者传输以侮辱、诽谤或者其他方式侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉的信息的，应当要求网络运营者停止传输，采取消除等处置措施和其他必要措施；对来源于中华人民共和国境外的上述信息，应当通知有关机构采取技术措施和其他必要措施阻断传播。  
网络运营者发现其用户发布前款规定的信息的，应当立即停止传输该信息，采取消除等处置措施，防止信息扩散，保存有关记录，并向有关主管部门报告。网络运营者未采取停止传输、消除等处置措施的，依照《中华人民共和国网络安全法》的规定处罚。

**Article 23** The departments of cyberspace administration, telecommunications, and public security, among others, shall, in the course of supervising and managing online information according to law, upon discovering the release or transmission of information that infringes upon the names, portraits, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs by means of insult, defamation, or other means, require the network operator to stop transmission, take measures such as removal and other necessary measures; for the aforesaid information originating from outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, they shall notify relevant agencies to take technical measures and other necessary measures to block its dissemination.  
Where a network operator discovers that its user has released information as specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall immediately stop transmission of such information, take measures such as removal to prevent dissemination, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant competent department. Where a network operator fails to take measures such as stopping transmission or removal, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何组织和个人有权对侵害英雄烈士合法权益和其他违反本法规定的行为，向负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门、网信、公安等有关部门举报，接到举报的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 24** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report to the departments responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs, cyberspace administration, public security, and other relevant departments any conduct that infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of heroes and martyrs or otherwise violates the provisions of this Law, and the department that receives the report shall handle it in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉的行为，英雄烈士的近亲属可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
英雄烈士没有近亲属或者近亲属不提起诉讼的，检察机关依法对侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉，损害社会公共利益的行为向人民法院提起诉讼。  
负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门和其他有关部门在履行职责过程中发现第一款规定的行为，需要检察机关提起诉讼的，应当向检察机关报告。  
英雄烈士近亲属依照第一款规定提起诉讼的，法律援助机构应当依法提供法律援助服务。

**Article 25** For conduct infringing upon the names, portraits, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs, the close relatives of heroes and martyrs may initiate litigation in the People's Court according to law.  
Where there is no close relative of the hero or martyr, or where the close relative does not initiate litigation, the procuratorial organ shall initiate litigation in the People's Court according to law against conduct that infringes upon the names, portraits, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs and damages public interests.  
Where the department responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs or other relevant departments, in the course of performing their duties, discover conduct as specified in the first paragraph and need the procuratorial organ to initiate litigation, they shall report to the procuratorial organ.  
Where a close relative of a hero or martyr initiates litigation in accordance with the first paragraph, a legal aid institution shall provide legal aid services according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　以侮辱、诽谤或者其他方式侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉，损害社会公共利益的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where a person infringes upon the names, portraits, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs by means of insult, defamation, or other means, and damages public interests, he or she shall bear civil liability according to law; if the conduct constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在英雄烈士纪念设施保护范围内从事有损纪念英雄烈士环境和氛围的活动的，纪念设施保护单位应当及时劝阻；不听劝阻的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门、文物主管部门按照职责规定给予批评教育，责令改正；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。  
亵渎、否定英雄烈士事迹和精神，宣扬、美化侵略战争和侵略行为，寻衅滋事，扰乱公共秩序，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where a person engages in activities detrimental to the environment and atmosphere of commemorating heroes and martyrs within the protected areas of commemorative facilities, the unit responsible for the protection of the facility shall promptly dissuade him or her; if the person refuses to heed the dissuasion, the department responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs under the local people's government at or above the county level and the department of cultural relics shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, give criticism and education and order rectification; if the conduct constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.  
Where a person desecrates or denies the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs, promotes or glorifies wars of aggression and acts of aggression, causes trouble, or disrupts public order, if the conduct constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　侵占、破坏、污损英雄烈士纪念设施的，由县级以上人民政府负责英雄烈士保护工作的部门责令改正；造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；被侵占、破坏、污损的纪念设施属于文物保护单位的，依照《中华人民共和国文物保护法》的规定处罚；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where a person encroaches upon, damages, or defiles a commemorative facility for heroes and martyrs, the department responsible for the protection of heroes and martyrs under the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification; if losses are caused, the person shall bear civil liability according to law; if the encroached, damaged, or defiled commemorative facility is a protected cultural heritage site, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Cultural Relics; if the conduct constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府有关部门及其工作人员在英雄烈士保护工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Where the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and their staff members abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in the protection of heroes and martyrs, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法自2018年5月1日起施行。

**Article 30** This Law shall come into force on May 1, 2018.
