# 中华人民共和国旅游法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-26  
**生效日期**：2018-10-26

> （2013年4月25日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过　根据2016年11月7日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外贸易法〉等十二部法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》第二次修正）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障旅游者和旅游经营者的合法权益，规范旅游市场秩序，保护和合理利用旅游资源，促进旅游业持续健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of tourists and tour operators, regulating the order of the tourism market, protecting and rationally utilizing tourism resources, and promoting the sustained and healthy development of the tourism industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的和在中华人民共和国境内组织到境外的游览、度假、休闲等形式的旅游活动以及为旅游活动提供相关服务的经营活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to tourism activities such as sightseeing, vacation, and leisure organized within the territory of the People's Republic of China as well as those organized to go abroad from the territory of the People's Republic of China, and to the business operations providing related services for tourism activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家发展旅游事业，完善旅游公共服务，依法保护旅游者在旅游活动中的权利。

**Article 3** The State shall develop tourism undertakings, improve public tourism services, and protect the rights of tourists in tourism activities according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　旅游业发展应当遵循社会效益、经济效益和生态效益相统一的原则。国家鼓励各类市场主体在有效保护旅游资源的前提下，依法合理利用旅游资源。利用公共资源建设的游览场所应当体现公益性质。

**Article 4** The development of the tourism industry shall follow the principle of integrating social, economic, and ecological benefits. The State encourages various market entities to rationally utilize tourism resources according to law on the premise of effectively protecting tourism resources. Tour sites built with public resources shall reflect their public welfare nature.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家倡导健康、文明、环保的旅游方式，支持和鼓励各类社会机构开展旅游公益宣传，对促进旅游业发展做出突出贡献的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 5** The State advocates healthy, civilized, and environmentally friendly tourism modes, supports and encourages various social organizations to carry out public welfare publicity on tourism, and shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to promoting the development of the tourism industry.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立健全旅游服务标准和市场规则，禁止行业垄断和地区垄断。旅游经营者应当诚信经营，公平竞争，承担社会责任，为旅游者提供安全、健康、卫生、方便的旅游服务。

**Article 6** The State shall establish and improve tourism service standards and market rules, and prohibit industry monopoly and regional monopoly. Tour operators shall operate in good faith, compete fairly, bear social responsibilities, and provide safe, healthy, sanitary, and convenient tourism services for tourists.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院建立健全旅游综合协调机制，对旅游业发展进行综合协调。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对旅游工作的组织和领导，明确相关部门或者机构，对本行政区域的旅游业发展和监督管理进行统筹协调。

**Article 7** The State Council shall establish and improve a comprehensive coordination mechanism for tourism to conduct comprehensive coordination for the development of the tourism industry.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the organization and leadership of tourism work, designate relevant departments or agencies, and exercise overall coordination over the development and supervision and management of the tourism industry within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依法成立的旅游行业组织，实行自律管理。

**Article 8** Tourism industry organizations established according to law shall exercise self-discipline management.

### 第二章　旅游者

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　旅游者有权自主选择旅游产品和服务，有权拒绝旅游经营者的强制交易行为。  
旅游者有权知悉其购买的旅游产品和服务的真实情况。  
旅游者有权要求旅游经营者按照约定提供产品和服务。

**Article 9** Tourists shall have the right to independently choose tourism products and services, and the right to refuse forced transaction conduct of tour operators.  
Tourists shall have the right to know the true information about the tourism products and services they purchase.  
Tourists shall have the right to require tour operators to provide products and services in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　旅游者的人格尊严、民族风俗习惯和宗教信仰应当得到尊重。

**Article 10** The personal dignity, ethnic customs and religious beliefs of tourists shall be respected.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　残疾人、老年人、未成年人等旅游者在旅游活动中依照法律、法规和有关规定享受便利和优惠。

**Article 11** Disabled persons, elderly persons, minors and other tourists shall, in tourism activities, enjoy conveniences and preferences in accordance with laws, regulations and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　旅游者在人身、财产安全遇有危险时，有请求救助和保护的权利。  
旅游者人身、财产受到侵害的，有依法获得赔偿的权利。

**Article 12** Tourists shall have the right to request rescue and protection when their personal or property safety is in danger.  
Tourists whose personal or property rights are infringed upon shall have the right to obtain compensation according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　旅游者在旅游活动中应当遵守社会公共秩序和社会公德，尊重当地的风俗习惯、文化传统和宗教信仰，爱护旅游资源，保护生态环境，遵守旅游文明行为规范。

**Article 13** Tourists shall, in tourism activities, observe social public order and social morality, respect local customs, cultural traditions and religious beliefs, care for tourism resources, protect the ecological environment, and abide by the norms of civilized tourism conduct.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　旅游者在旅游活动中或者在解决纠纷时，不得损害当地居民的合法权益，不得干扰他人的旅游活动，不得损害旅游经营者和旅游从业人员的合法权益。

**Article 14** Tourists shall not, when engaging in tourism activities or resolving disputes, harm the legitimate rights and interests of local residents, disturb others' tourism activities, or harm the legitimate rights and interests of tour operators and tourism practitioners.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　旅游者购买、接受旅游服务时，应当向旅游经营者如实告知与旅游活动相关的个人健康信息，遵守旅游活动中的安全警示规定。  
旅游者对国家应对重大突发事件暂时限制旅游活动的措施以及有关部门、机构或者旅游经营者采取的安全防范和应急处置措施，应当予以配合。  
旅游者违反安全警示规定，或者对国家应对重大突发事件暂时限制旅游活动的措施、安全防范和应急处置措施不予配合的，依法承担相应责任。

**Article 15** When purchasing or accepting tourism services, tourists shall truthfully inform tour operators of personal health information related to the tourism activities, and comply with the safety warning provisions in tourism activities.  
Tourists shall cooperate with the measures taken by the State to temporarily restrict tourism activities in response to major emergencies, as well as the safety precautions and emergency response measures taken by relevant departments, agencies, or tour operators.  
If a tourist violates safety warning provisions, or fails to cooperate with measures of the State to temporarily restrict tourism activities in response to major emergencies, safety precautions and emergency response measures, the tourist shall bear corresponding liability according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出境旅游者不得在境外非法滞留，随团出境的旅游者不得擅自分团、脱团。  
入境旅游者不得在境内非法滞留，随团入境的旅游者不得擅自分团、脱团。

**Article 16** Outbound tourists shall not illegally overstay abroad, and tourists traveling in a group outbound shall not split from the group or leave the group without authorization.  
Inbound tourists shall not illegally overstay within the territory, and tourists traveling in a group inbound shall not split from the group or leave the group without authorization.

### 第三章　旅游规划和促进

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当将旅游业发展纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府以及旅游资源丰富的设区的市和县级人民政府，应当按照国民经济和社会发展规划的要求，组织编制旅游发展规划。对跨行政区域且适宜进行整体利用的旅游资源进行利用时，应当由上级人民政府组织编制或者由相关地方人民政府协商编制统一的旅游发展规划。

**Article 17** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the development of the tourism industry into the national economic and social development plan.  
The State Council, people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as people's governments of cities divided into districts and counties with rich tourism resources, shall organize the formulation of tourism development plans in accordance with the requirements of the national economic and social development plan. When utilizing tourism resources that cross administrative regions and are suitable for overall utilization, the superior people's government shall organize the formulation of a unified tourism development plan, or the relevant local people's governments shall negotiate to formulate such a plan.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　旅游发展规划应当包括旅游业发展的总体要求和发展目标，旅游资源保护和利用的要求和措施，以及旅游产品开发、旅游服务质量提升、旅游文化建设、旅游形象推广、旅游基础设施和公共服务设施建设的要求和促进措施等内容。  
根据旅游发展规划，县级以上地方人民政府可以编制重点旅游资源开发利用的专项规划，对特定区域内的旅游项目、设施和服务功能配套提出专门要求。

**Article 18** A tourism development plan shall include the overall requirements and development objectives of the tourism industry, the requirements and measures for the protection and utilization of tourism resources, as well as the requirements and promotion measures for the development of tourism products, improvement of tourism service quality, construction of tourism culture, promotion of tourism image, and construction of tourism infrastructure and public service facilities.  
Based on the tourism development plan, local people's governments at or above the county level may formulate special plans for the development and utilization of key tourism resources, and propose specific requirements for the supporting of tourism projects, facilities and service functions in specific areas.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　旅游发展规划应当与土地利用总体规划、城乡规划、环境保护规划以及其他自然资源和文物等人文资源的保护和利用规划相衔接。

**Article 19** Tourism development plans shall be aligned with the overall land use plans, urban and rural plans, environmental protection plans, and other plans for the protection and utilization of natural resources and cultural resources such as cultural relics.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级人民政府编制土地利用总体规划、城乡规划，应当充分考虑相关旅游项目、设施的空间布局和建设用地要求。规划和建设交通、通信、供水、供电、环保等基础设施和公共服务设施，应当兼顾旅游业发展的需要。

**Article 20** When compiling overall land use plans and urban and rural plans, people's governments at all levels shall fully consider the spatial layout and land use requirements of relevant tourism projects and facilities. When planning and constructing infrastructure and public service facilities such as transportation, communication, water supply, power supply, and environmental protection, the needs of the development of the tourism industry shall be taken into account.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对自然资源和文物等人文资源进行旅游利用，必须严格遵守有关法律、法规的规定，符合资源、生态保护和文物安全的要求，尊重和维护当地传统文化和习俗，维护资源的区域整体性、文化代表性和地域特殊性，并考虑军事设施保护的需要。有关主管部门应当加强对资源保护和旅游利用状况的监督检查。

**Article 21** Where natural resources and cultural resources such as cultural relics are utilized for tourism, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations must be strictly complied with, the requirements for resource and ecological protection and the safety of cultural relics shall be met, the local traditional culture and customs shall be respected and preserved, the regional integrity, cultural representativeness, and geographical particularity of the resources shall be maintained, and the need for the protection of military facilities shall be taken into consideration. The relevant competent departments shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the protection of resources and the utilization of tourism.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各级人民政府应当组织对本级政府编制的旅游发展规划的执行情况进行评估，并向社会公布。

**Article 22** People's governments at all levels shall organize an evaluation of the implementation of the tourism development plans formulated by the governments at their own levels and publish the results to the public.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当制定并组织实施有利于旅游业持续健康发展的产业政策，推进旅游休闲体系建设，采取措施推动区域旅游合作，鼓励跨区域旅游线路和产品开发，促进旅游与工业、农业、商业、文化、卫生、体育、科教等领域的融合，扶持少数民族地区、革命老区、边远地区和贫困地区旅游业发展。

**Article 23** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate and organize the implementation of industrial policies conducive to the sustainable and healthy development of the tourism industry, promote the construction of a tourism and leisure system, take measures to promote regional tourism cooperation, encourage the development of cross-regional tourism routes and products, promote the integration of tourism with industries such as industry, agriculture, commerce, culture, health, sports, science and education, and support the development of the tourism industry in ethnic minority areas, old revolutionary base areas, remote areas, and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院和县级以上地方人民政府应当根据实际情况安排资金，加强旅游基础设施建设、旅游公共服务和旅游形象推广。

**Article 24** The State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall arrange funds according to the actual circumstances to strengthen the construction of tourism infrastructure, tourism public services, and the promotion of tourism image.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家制定并实施旅游形象推广战略。国务院旅游主管部门统筹组织国家旅游形象的境外推广工作，建立旅游形象推广机构和网络，开展旅游国际合作与交流。  
县级以上地方人民政府统筹组织本地的旅游形象推广工作。

**Article 25** The State shall formulate and implement a strategy for the promotion of the national tourism image. The tourism administrative department of the State Council shall coordinate the organization of overseas promotion of the national tourism image, establish institutions and networks for tourism image promotion, and carry out international cooperation and exchanges in tourism.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate the organization of local tourism image promotion.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院旅游主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当根据需要建立旅游公共信息和咨询平台，无偿向旅游者提供旅游景区、线路、交通、气象、住宿、安全、医疗急救等必要信息和咨询服务。设区的市和县级人民政府有关部门应当根据需要在交通枢纽、商业中心和旅游者集中场所设置旅游咨询中心，在景区和通往主要景区的道路设置旅游指示标识。  
旅游资源丰富的设区的市和县级人民政府可以根据本地的实际情况，建立旅游客运专线或者游客中转站，为旅游者在城市及周边旅游提供服务。

**Article 26** The tourism administrative department of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, establish tourism public information and consultation platforms to provide tourists free of charge with necessary information and consulting services on scenic spots, routes, transportation, weather, accommodation, safety, medical first aid, etc. The relevant departments of the people's governments of cities divided into districts and of counties shall, as needed, set up tourism consultation centers at transportation hubs, commercial centers, and places where tourists gather, and set up tourism guide signs in scenic spots and on roads leading to main scenic spots.  
People's governments of cities divided into districts and of counties with rich tourism resources may, according to their actual local conditions, establish special tourism passenger transport lines or tourist transfer stations to provide services for tourists traveling in and around the city.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励和支持发展旅游职业教育和培训，提高旅游从业人员素质。

**Article 27** The State encourages and supports the development of vocational education and training in tourism to improve the quality of tourism practitioners.

### 第四章　旅游经营

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　设立旅行社，招徕、组织、接待旅游者，为其提供旅游服务，应当具备下列条件，取得旅游主管部门的许可，依法办理工商登记：  
（一）有固定的经营场所；  
（二）有必要的营业设施；  
（三）有符合规定的注册资本；  
（四）有必要的经营管理人员和导游；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 28** To establish a travel agency to solicit, organize, and receive tourists and provide tourism services to them, the following conditions shall be met, the permission of the tourism administrative department shall be obtained, and industrial and commercial registration shall be handled according to law:  
(1) having a fixed business premises;  
(2) having necessary business facilities;  
(3) having the registered capital meeting the requirements;  
(4) having necessary management personnel and tour guides;  
(5) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　旅行社可以经营下列业务：  
（一）境内旅游；  
（二）出境旅游；  
（三）边境旅游；  
（四）入境旅游；  
（五）其他旅游业务。  
旅行社经营前款第二项和第三项业务，应当取得相应的业务经营许可，具体条件由国务院规定。

**Article 29** A travel agency may engage in the following business:  
(1) domestic tourism;  
(2) outbound tourism;  
(3) border tourism;  
(4) inbound tourism;  
(5) other tourism businesses.  
A travel agency engaging in the businesses specified in items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall obtain the corresponding business operation permission, and the specific conditions shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　旅行社不得出租、出借旅行社业务经营许可证，或者以其他形式非法转让旅行社业务经营许可。

**Article 30** A travel agency shall not rent out or lend its travel agency business operation license, or illegally transfer the travel agency business operation permission in any other form.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　旅行社应当按照规定交纳旅游服务质量保证金，用于旅游者权益损害赔偿和垫付旅游者人身安全遇有危险时紧急救助的费用。

**Article 31** A travel agency shall pay a tourism service quality guarantee deposit in accordance with the provisions, to be used for compensation for damages to tourists' rights and interests and for advancing expenses for emergency rescue when tourists' personal safety is in danger.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　旅行社为招徕、组织旅游者发布信息，必须真实、准确，不得进行虚假宣传，误导旅游者。

**Article 32** The information published by a travel agency for soliciting and organizing tourists must be true and accurate, and the travel agency shall not engage in false publicity to mislead tourists.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　旅行社及其从业人员组织、接待旅游者，不得安排参观或者参与违反我国法律、法规和社会公德的项目或者活动。

**Article 33** When organizing and receiving tourists, a travel agency and its employees shall not arrange for them to visit or participate in projects or activities that violate the laws, regulations, and social morality of China.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　旅行社组织旅游活动应当向合格的供应商订购产品和服务。

**Article 34** When organizing tourism activities, a travel agency shall order products and services from qualified suppliers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　旅行社不得以不合理的低价组织旅游活动，诱骗旅游者，并通过安排购物或者另行付费旅游项目获取回扣等不正当利益。  
旅行社组织、接待旅游者，不得指定具体购物场所，不得安排另行付费旅游项目。但是，经双方协商一致或者旅游者要求，且不影响其他旅游者行程安排的除外。  
发生违反前两款规定情形的，旅游者有权在旅游行程结束后三十日内，要求旅行社为其办理退货并先行垫付退货货款，或者退还另行付费旅游项目的费用。

**Article 35** A travel agency shall not organize tourism activities at unreasonably low prices to deceive tourists and obtain improper benefits such as kickbacks by arranging shopping or additional paid tourism items.  
When organizing and receiving tourists, a travel agency shall not designate specific shopping places or arrange additional paid tourism items, except where both parties have reached consensus or the tourist requests it and it does not affect the itinerary of other tourists.  
Where a violation of the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs occurs, the tourist shall have the right, within thirty days after the end of the tourism itinerary, to request the travel agency to handle returns and advance the refund of the purchase price, or to refund the fees for the additional paid tourism items.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　旅行社组织团队出境旅游或者组织、接待团队入境旅游，应当按照规定安排领队或者导游全程陪同。

**Article 36** When organizing group outbound tourism or organizing or receiving group inbound tourism, a travel agency shall arrange for a tour leader or a tour guide to accompany the group throughout the entire trip in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　参加导游资格考试成绩合格，与旅行社订立劳动合同或者在相关旅游行业组织注册的人员，可以申请取得导游证。

**Article 37** Persons who have passed the tour guide qualification examination and have signed a labor contract with a travel agency or have registered with a relevant tourism industry organization may apply for a tour guide certificate.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　旅行社应当与其聘用的导游依法订立劳动合同，支付劳动报酬，缴纳社会保险费用。  
旅行社临时聘用导游为旅游者提供服务的，应当全额向导游支付[本法第六十条](#art-60)第三款规定的导游服务费用。  
旅行社安排导游为团队旅游提供服务的，不得要求导游垫付或者向导游收取任何费用。

**Article 38** A travel agency shall sign a labor contract with the tour guide it employs according to law, pay labor remuneration, and pay social insurance premiums.  
Where a travel agency temporarily employs a tour guide to provide services to tourists, it shall pay the tour guide in full the tour guide service fee as prescribed in paragraph 3 of Article 60 of this Law.  
Where a travel agency arranges for a tour guide to provide services for group tours, it shall not require the tour guide to advance payments or charge any fees from the tour guide.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　从事领队业务，应当取得导游证，具有相应的学历、语言能力和旅游从业经历，并与委派其从事领队业务的取得出境旅游业务经营许可的旅行社订立劳动合同。

**Article 39** To engage in tour leader business, a person shall obtain a tour guide certificate, have the corresponding educational background, language ability, and tourism work experience, and sign a labor contract with a travel agency that has obtained the permission to operate outbound tourism business and that assigns him or her to engage in tour leader business.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　导游和领队为旅游者提供服务必须接受旅行社委派，不得私自承揽导游和领队业务。

**Article 40** Tour guides and tour leaders must accept the assignment of the travel agency when providing services to tourists and shall not privately undertake tour guide or tour leader business.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　导游和领队从事业务活动，应当佩戴导游证，遵守职业道德，尊重旅游者的风俗习惯和宗教信仰，应当向旅游者告知和解释旅游文明行为规范，引导旅游者健康、文明旅游，劝阻旅游者违反社会公德的行为。  
导游和领队应当严格执行旅游行程安排，不得擅自变更旅游行程或者中止服务活动，不得向旅游者索取小费，不得诱导、欺骗、强迫或者变相强迫旅游者购物或者参加另行付费旅游项目。

**Article 41** Tour guides and tour managers, when conducting business activities, shall wear their guide certificates, abide by professional ethics, respect the customs and religious beliefs of tourists, and shall inform and explain the norms of civilized tourism to tourists, guide tourists to engage in healthy and civilized tourism, and dissuade tourists from acts that violate social morals.  
Tour guides and tour managers shall strictly implement the tour itinerary arrangements, and shall not alter the tour itinerary or suspend service activities without authorization, shall not solicit tips from tourists, and shall not induce, deceive, coerce, or covertly coerce tourists into shopping or participating in separately charged tourism items.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　景区开放应当具备下列条件，并听取旅游主管部门的意见：  
（一）有必要的旅游配套服务和辅助设施；  
（二）有必要的安全设施及制度，经过安全风险评估，满足安全条件；  
（三）有必要的环境保护设施和生态保护措施；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 42** The opening of a scenic spot shall meet the following conditions and shall seek the opinions of the tourism administrative department:  
(1) Having necessary supporting tourism services and auxiliary facilities;  
(2) Having necessary safety facilities and systems, having passed safety risk assessment, and meeting safety conditions;  
(3) Having necessary environmental protection facilities and ecological protection measures;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　利用公共资源建设的景区的门票以及景区内的游览场所、交通工具等另行收费项目，实行政府定价或者政府指导价，严格控制价格上涨。拟收费或者提高价格的，应当举行听证会，征求旅游者、经营者和有关方面的意见，论证其必要性、可行性。  
利用公共资源建设的景区，不得通过增加另行收费项目等方式变相涨价；另行收费项目已收回投资成本的，应当相应降低价格或者取消收费。  
公益性的城市公园、博物馆、纪念馆等，除重点文物保护单位和珍贵文物收藏单位外，应当逐步免费开放。

**Article 43** The ticket prices of scenic spots built with public resources, as well as separately charged items such as sightseeing venues and transportation facilities within the scenic spots, shall be subject to government pricing or government-guided pricing, and price increases shall be strictly controlled. Where charges are to be imposed or prices are to be increased, a hearing shall be held to solicit opinions from tourists, operators, and relevant parties, and to demonstrate the necessity and feasibility thereof.  
Scenic spots built with public resources shall not raise prices in disguised form by adding separately charged items; where the investment cost of a separately charged item has been recovered, the price shall be correspondingly reduced or the charge shall be eliminated.  
Public urban parks, museums, memorial halls, and the like, with the exception of key cultural relics protection units and units that collect precious cultural relics, shall gradually open free of charge.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　景区应当在醒目位置公示门票价格、另行收费项目的价格及团体收费价格。景区提高门票价格应当提前六个月公布。  
将不同景区的门票或者同一景区内不同游览场所的门票合并出售的，合并后的价格不得高于各单项门票的价格之和，且旅游者有权选择购买其中的单项票。  
景区内的核心游览项目因故暂停向旅游者开放或者停止提供服务的，应当公示并相应减少收费。

**Article 44** Scenic spots shall publicly display the ticket prices, the prices of separately charged items, and the group ticket prices in conspicuous places. A scenic spot that raises ticket prices shall announce the increase six months in advance.  
Where tickets of different scenic spots or tickets of different sightseeing venues within the same scenic spot are sold in combination, the combined price shall not exceed the sum of the individual ticket prices, and tourists shall have the right to choose to purchase individual tickets.  
Where a core sightseeing project within a scenic spot is temporarily suspended from opening to tourists or ceases to provide services, such suspension shall be publicly announced and the charges shall be correspondingly reduced.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　景区接待旅游者不得超过景区主管部门核定的最大承载量。景区应当公布景区主管部门核定的最大承载量，制定和实施旅游者流量控制方案，并可以采取门票预约等方式，对景区接待旅游者的数量进行控制。  
旅游者数量可能达到最大承载量时，景区应当提前公告并同时向当地人民政府报告，景区和当地人民政府应当及时采取疏导、分流等措施。

**Article 45** Scenic spots shall not receive more tourists than the maximum carrying capacity verified by the competent department of the scenic spot. A scenic spot shall publish the maximum carrying capacity verified by the competent department, formulate and implement a plan for controlling tourist flow, and may adopt methods such as ticket reservation to control the number of tourists received.  
Where the number of tourists may reach the maximum carrying capacity, the scenic spot shall announce this in advance and simultaneously report to the local people's government, and the scenic spot and the local people's government shall promptly take measures such as guidance and diversion.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　城镇和乡村居民利用自有住宅或者其他条件依法从事旅游经营，其管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 46** Where urban and rural residents engage in tourism operations according to law using their own houses or other conditions, the management measures therefor shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　经营高空、高速、水上、潜水、探险等高风险旅游项目，应当按照国家有关规定取得经营许可。

**Article 47** Those operating high-risk tourism items such as high-altitude, high-speed, water, diving, and adventure projects shall obtain business licenses in accordance with the relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　通过网络经营旅行社业务的，应当依法取得旅行社业务经营许可，并在其网站主页的显著位置标明其业务经营许可证信息。  
发布旅游经营信息的网站，应当保证其信息真实、准确。

**Article 48** Those operating travel agency business through the network shall obtain a travel agency business license according to law, and shall indicate the information of their business license in a conspicuous position on the homepage of their website.  
Websites publishing tourism business information shall ensure that the information is true and accurate.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　为旅游者提供交通、住宿、餐饮、娱乐等服务的经营者，应当符合法律、法规规定的要求，按照合同约定履行义务。

**Article 49** Operators providing services such as transportation, accommodation, catering, and entertainment to tourists shall meet the requirements prescribed by laws and regulations and shall perform their obligations in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　旅游经营者应当保证其提供的商品和服务符合保障人身、财产安全的要求。  
旅游经营者取得相关质量标准等级的，其设施和服务不得低于相应标准；未取得质量标准等级的，不得使用相关质量等级的称谓和标识。

**Article 50** Tourism operators shall ensure that the goods and services they provide meet the requirements for protecting personal and property safety.  
Where a tourism operator has obtained a relevant quality standard grade, its facilities and services shall not be lower than the corresponding standards; where it has not obtained a quality standard grade, it shall not use the titles and logos of such quality grade.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　旅游经营者销售、购买商品或者服务，不得给予或者收受贿赂。

**Article 51** Tourism operators, when selling or purchasing goods or services, shall not give or accept bribes.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　旅游经营者对其在经营活动中知悉的旅游者个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 52** Tourism operators shall keep confidential the personal information of tourists that they have learned during their business activities.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　从事道路旅游客运的经营者应当遵守道路客运安全管理的各项制度，并在车辆显著位置明示道路旅游客运专用标识，在车厢内显著位置公示经营者和驾驶人信息、道路运输管理机构监督电话等事项。

**Article 53** Operators engaged in road tourism passenger transport shall comply with the various systems for road passenger transport safety management, and shall clearly display the special identification for road tourism passenger transport on a conspicuous position of the vehicle, and shall publicly display, in a conspicuous position inside the carriage, information such as the operator and driver information and the supervision telephone number of the road transport management institution.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　景区、住宿经营者将其部分经营项目或者场地交由他人从事住宿、餐饮、购物、游览、娱乐、旅游交通等经营的，应当对实际经营者的经营行为给旅游者造成的损害承担连带责任。

**Article 54** Where scenic spot or accommodation operators entrust part of their business projects or premises to others for operation of accommodation, catering, shopping, sightseeing, entertainment, tourism transportation, etc., they shall bear joint and several liability for damages caused to tourists by the business activities of the actual operator.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　旅游经营者组织、接待出入境旅游，发现旅游者从事违法活动或者有违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定情形的，应当及时向公安机关、旅游主管部门或者我国驻外机构报告。

**Article 55** Where tourism operators organize or receive inbound and outbound tourism and discover that tourists are engaged in illegal activities or have circumstances that violate the provisions of Article 16 of this Law, they shall promptly report to the public security organ, the tourism administrative department, or the Chinese diplomatic missions abroad.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家根据旅游活动的风险程度，对旅行社、住宿、旅游交通以及[本法第四十七条](#art-47)规定的高风险旅游项目等经营者实施责任保险制度。

**Article 56** The State shall implement a liability insurance system for operators such as travel agencies, accommodation, tourism transportation, and the high-risk tourism projects specified in Article 47 of this Law, based on the level of risk of tourism activities.

### 第五章　旅游服务合同

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　旅行社组织和安排旅游活动，应当与旅游者订立合同。

**Article 57** Travel agencies organizing and arranging tourism activities shall conclude a contract with the tourists.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　包价旅游合同应当采用书面形式，包括下列内容：  
（一）旅行社、旅游者的基本信息；  
（二）旅游行程安排；  
（三）旅游团成团的最低人数；  
（四）交通、住宿、餐饮等旅游服务安排和标准；  
（五）游览、娱乐等项目的具体内容和时间；  
（六）自由活动时间安排；  
（七）旅游费用及其交纳的期限和方式；  
（八）违约责任和解决纠纷的方式；  
（九）法律、法规规定和双方约定的其他事项。  
订立包价旅游合同时，旅行社应当向旅游者详细说明前款第二项至第八项所载内容。

**Article 58** A package tour contract shall be in written form and shall include the following contents:  
(1) The basic information of the travel agency and the tourists;  
(2) The tour itinerary arrangement;  
(3) The minimum number of persons required to form a tour group;  
(4) The arrangement and standards of tourism services such as transportation, accommodation, and catering;  
(5) The specific contents and time of items such as sightseeing and entertainment;  
(6) The arrangement of free time;  
(7) The tourism expenses and the time limit and method of payment;  
(8) The liability for breach of contract and the method for dispute resolution;  
(9) Other matters prescribed by laws and regulations and agreed upon by both parties.  
When concluding a package tour contract, the travel agency shall explain in detail to the tourists the contents set forth in items (2) to (8) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　旅行社应当在旅游行程开始前向旅游者提供旅游行程单。旅游行程单是包价旅游合同的组成部分。

**Article 59** A travel agency shall provide the tourists with a tour itinerary before the commencement of the tour. The tour itinerary is an integral part of the package tour contract.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　旅行社委托其他旅行社代理销售包价旅游产品并与旅游者订立包价旅游合同的，应当在包价旅游合同中载明委托社和代理社的基本信息。  
旅行社依照本法规定将包价旅游合同中的接待业务委托给地接社履行的，应当在包价旅游合同中载明地接社的基本信息。  
安排导游为旅游者提供服务的，应当在包价旅游合同中载明导游服务费用。

**Article 60** Where a travel agency entrusts another travel agency to act as an agent to sell package tour products and to conclude a package tour contract with tourists, it shall state clearly in the package tour contract the basic information of the entrusting travel agency and the agency travel agency.  
Where a travel agency entrusts the reception business under a package tour contract to a local receiving travel agency for performance in accordance with the provisions of this Law, it shall state clearly in the package tour contract the basic information of the local receiving travel agency.  
Where a tour guide is arranged to provide services for tourists, the tour guide service fee shall be stated clearly in the package tour contract.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　旅行社应当提示参加团队旅游的旅游者按照规定投保人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 61** A travel agency shall remind tourists participating in group tours to purchase personal accident insurance in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　订立包价旅游合同时，旅行社应当向旅游者告知下列事项：  
（一）旅游者不适合参加旅游活动的情形；  
（二）旅游活动中的安全注意事项；  
（三）旅行社依法可以减免责任的信息；  
（四）旅游者应当注意的旅游目的地相关法律、法规和风俗习惯、宗教禁忌，依照中国法律不宜参加的活动等；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他应当告知的事项。  
在包价旅游合同履行中，遇有前款规定事项的，旅行社也应当告知旅游者。

**Article 62** When concluding a package tour contract, a travel agency shall inform the tourists of the following matters:  
(1) the circumstances under which tourists are unsuitable to participate in tourism activities;  
(2) safety precautions during tourism activities;  
(3) information on the reduction or exemption of liability that the travel agency may enjoy according to law;  
(4) the relevant laws, regulations, customs, religious taboos of the tourist destination that tourists shall pay attention to, activities that are not appropriate to participate in according to Chinese law, etc.;  
(5) other matters that shall be informed as prescribed by laws and regulations.  
During the performance of the package tour contract, if any of the matters specified in the preceding paragraph occurs, the travel agency shall also inform the tourists.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　旅行社招徕旅游者组团旅游，因未达到约定人数不能出团的，组团社可以解除合同。但是，境内旅游应当至少提前七日通知旅游者，出境旅游应当至少提前三十日通知旅游者。  
因未达到约定人数不能出团的，组团社经征得旅游者书面同意，可以委托其他旅行社履行合同。组团社对旅游者承担责任，受委托的旅行社对组团社承担责任。旅游者不同意的，可以解除合同。  
因未达到约定的成团人数解除合同的，组团社应当向旅游者退还已收取的全部费用。

**Article 63** If a travel agency solicits tourists to form a group tour but cannot depart due to failure to reach the agreed number of participants, the organizing travel agency may dissolve the contract. However, for domestic tours, it shall notify the tourists at least seven days in advance; for outbound tours, it shall notify the tourists at least thirty days in advance.  
If the tour cannot depart due to failure to reach the agreed number of participants, the organizing travel agency, after obtaining the written consent of the tourists, may entrust another travel agency to perform the contract. The organizing travel agency shall bear liability to the tourists, and the entrusted travel agency shall bear liability to the organizing travel agency. If the tourists do not consent, they may dissolve the contract.  
If the contract is dissolved due to failure to reach the agreed number of participants, the organizing travel agency shall refund all fees collected to the tourists.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　旅游行程开始前，旅游者可以将包价旅游合同中自身的权利义务转让给第三人，旅行社没有正当理由的不得拒绝，因此增加的费用由旅游者和第三人承担。

**Article 64** Before the start of the tour itinerary, a tourist may transfer his/her own rights and obligations under the package tour contract to a third party. The travel agency shall not refuse without justifiable reasons, and any additional costs incurred thereby shall be borne by the tourist and the third party.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　旅游行程结束前，旅游者解除合同的，组团社应当在扣除必要的费用后，将余款退还旅游者。

**Article 65** If a tourist dissolves the contract before the end of the tour itinerary, the organizing travel agency shall refund the balance to the tourist after deducting necessary expenses.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　旅游者有下列情形之一的，旅行社可以解除合同：  
（一）患有传染病等疾病，可能危害其他旅游者健康和安全的；  
（二）携带危害公共安全的物品且不同意交有关部门处理的；  
（三）从事违法或者违反社会公德的活动的；  
（四）从事严重影响其他旅游者权益的活动，且不听劝阻、不能制止的；  
（五）法律规定的其他情形。  
因前款规定情形解除合同的，组团社应当在扣除必要的费用后，将余款退还旅游者；给旅行社造成损失的，旅游者应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 66** A travel agency may dissolve the contract if a tourist falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) suffering from a contagious disease or other illness that may endanger the health and safety of other tourists;  
(2) carrying items that endanger public safety and refusing to hand them over to the relevant authorities for disposal;  
(3) engaging in illegal activities or activities that violate social morality;  
(4) engaging in activities that seriously affect the rights and interests of other tourists and refusing to heed warnings or cannot be stopped;  
(5) other circumstances prescribed by law.  
If the contract is dissolved due to the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the organizing travel agency shall refund the balance to the tourist after deducting necessary expenses; if losses are caused to the travel agency, the tourist shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　因不可抗力或者旅行社、履行辅助人已尽合理注意义务仍不能避免的事件，影响旅游行程的，按照下列情形处理：  
（一）合同不能继续履行的，旅行社和旅游者均可以解除合同。合同不能完全履行的，旅行社经向旅游者作出说明，可以在合理范围内变更合同；旅游者不同意变更的，可以解除合同。  
（二）合同解除的，组团社应当在扣除已向地接社或者履行辅助人支付且不可退还的费用后，将余款退还旅游者；合同变更的，因此增加的费用由旅游者承担，减少的费用退还旅游者。  
（三）危及旅游者人身、财产安全的，旅行社应当采取相应的安全措施，因此支出的费用，由旅行社与旅游者分担。  
（四）造成旅游者滞留的，旅行社应当采取相应的安置措施。因此增加的食宿费用，由旅游者承担；增加的返程费用，由旅行社与旅游者分担。

**Article 67** If the tour itinerary is affected by force majeure or events that cannot be avoided despite the travel agency or performance assistant having exercised reasonable care, the matter shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the contract cannot continue to be performed, both the travel agency and the tourist may dissolve the contract. If the contract cannot be fully performed, the travel agency, after explaining to the tourists, may modify the contract within a reasonable scope; if the tourists do not agree to the modification, they may dissolve the contract.  
(2) If the contract is dissolved, the organizing travel agency shall refund the balance to the tourists after deducting the fees that have been paid to the local reception travel agency or performance assistant and are non-refundable; if the contract is modified, any additional costs incurred thereby shall be borne by the tourists, and any reduced costs shall be refunded to the tourists.  
(3) If the personal or property safety of tourists is endangered, the travel agency shall take corresponding safety measures, and the expenses incurred thereby shall be shared by the travel agency and the tourists.  
(4) If tourists are stranded, the travel agency shall take corresponding accommodation measures. The additional accommodation and meal expenses incurred thereby shall be borne by the tourists; the additional return expenses shall be shared by the travel agency and the tourists.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　旅游行程中解除合同的，旅行社应当协助旅游者返回出发地或者旅游者指定的合理地点。由于旅行社或者履行辅助人的原因导致合同解除的，返程费用由旅行社承担。

**Article 68** If the contract is dissolved during the tour itinerary, the travel agency shall assist the tourists in returning to the place of departure or a reasonable location designated by the tourists. If the contract is dissolved due to the fault of the travel agency or the performance assistant, the return expenses shall be borne by the travel agency.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　旅行社应当按照包价旅游合同的约定履行义务，不得擅自变更旅游行程安排。  
经旅游者同意，旅行社将包价旅游合同中的接待业务委托给其他具有相应资质的地接社履行的，应当与地接社订立书面委托合同，约定双方的权利和义务，向地接社提供与旅游者订立的包价旅游合同的副本，并向地接社支付不低于接待和服务成本的费用。地接社应当按照包价旅游合同和委托合同提供服务。

**Article 69** A travel agency shall perform its obligations in accordance with the package tour contract and shall not arbitrarily modify the tour itinerary arrangement.  
With the consent of the tourists, if the travel agency entrusts the reception business under the package tour contract to another local reception travel agency with corresponding qualifications, it shall conclude a written entrustment contract with the local reception travel agency, stipulating the rights and obligations of both parties, provide the local reception travel agency with a copy of the package tour contract concluded with the tourists, and pay the local reception travel agency fees not lower than the reception and service cost. The local reception travel agency shall provide services in accordance with the package tour contract and the entrustment contract.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　旅行社不履行包价旅游合同义务或者履行合同义务不符合约定的，应当依法承担继续履行、采取补救措施或者赔偿损失等违约责任；造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。旅行社具备履行条件，经旅游者要求仍拒绝履行合同，造成旅游者人身损害、滞留等严重后果的，旅游者还可以要求旅行社支付旅游费用一倍以上三倍以下的赔偿金。  
由于旅游者自身原因导致包价旅游合同不能履行或者不能按照约定履行，或者造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失的，旅行社不承担责任。  
在旅游者自行安排活动期间，旅行社未尽到安全提示、救助义务的，应当对旅游者的人身损害、财产损失承担相应责任。

**Article 70** If a travel agency fails to perform its obligations under the package tour contract or performs the contract obligations not in conformity with the agreement, it shall bear liability for breach of contract, such as continuing performance, taking remedial measures, or compensating for losses, according to law; if personal injury or property damage is caused to the tourists, it shall bear compensation liability according to law. If the travel agency has the conditions for performance but still refuses to perform the contract after being requested by the tourists, causing serious consequences such as personal injury or stranding of the tourists, the tourists may also require the travel agency to pay compensation of not less than one time but not more than three times the tourism expenses.  
If the package tour contract cannot be performed or cannot be performed in accordance with the agreement, or if personal injury or property damage is caused to the tourists due to the tourists' own reasons, the travel agency shall not bear liability.  
During the period of self-arranged activities by the tourists, if the travel agency fails to fulfill its obligations of safety tips and rescue, it shall bear corresponding liability for the personal injury or property damage of the tourists.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　由于地接社、履行辅助人的原因导致违约的，由组团社承担责任；组团社承担责任后可以向地接社、履行辅助人追偿。  
由于地接社、履行辅助人的原因造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失的，旅游者可以要求地接社、履行辅助人承担赔偿责任，也可以要求组团社承担赔偿责任；组团社承担责任后可以向地接社、履行辅助人追偿。但是，由于公共交通经营者的原因造成旅游者人身损害、财产损失的，由公共交通经营者依法承担赔偿责任，旅行社应当协助旅游者向公共交通经营者索赔。

**Article 71** If a breach of contract is caused by the local reception travel agency or the performance assistant, the organizing travel agency shall bear liability; after bearing liability, the organizing travel agency may seek recourse against the local reception travel agency or the performance assistant.  
If personal injury or property damage is caused to the tourists due to the local reception travel agency or the performance assistant, the tourists may require the local reception travel agency or the performance assistant to bear compensation liability, or may require the organizing travel agency to bear compensation liability; after bearing liability, the organizing travel agency may seek recourse against the local reception travel agency or the performance assistant. However, if personal injury or property damage is caused to the tourists due to a public transport operator, the public transport operator shall bear compensation liability according to law, and the travel agency shall assist the tourists in claiming compensation from the public transport operator.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　旅游者在旅游活动中或者在解决纠纷时，损害旅行社、履行辅助人、旅游从业人员或者其他旅游者的合法权益的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 72** If a tourist damages the legitimate rights and interests of a travel agency, performance assistant, tourism practitioner, or other tourists during tourism activities or in the course of dispute resolution, the tourist shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　旅行社根据旅游者的具体要求安排旅游行程，与旅游者订立包价旅游合同的，旅游者请求变更旅游行程安排，因此增加的费用由旅游者承担，减少的费用退还旅游者。

**Article 73** If a travel agency arranges the tour itinerary according to the specific requirements of the tourists and concludes a package tour contract with the tourists, and the tourists request a modification of the tour itinerary arrangement, any additional costs incurred thereby shall be borne by the tourists, and any reduced costs shall be refunded to the tourists.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　旅行社接受旅游者的委托，为其代订交通、住宿、餐饮、游览、娱乐等旅游服务，收取代办费用的，应当亲自处理委托事务。因旅行社的过错给旅游者造成损失的，旅行社应当承担赔偿责任。  
旅行社接受旅游者的委托，为其提供旅游行程设计、旅游信息咨询等服务的，应当保证设计合理、可行，信息及时、准确。

**Article 74** If a travel agency accepts a mandate from a tourist to book tourism services such as transportation, accommodation, meals, sightseeing, and entertainment on behalf of the tourist and collects agency fees, it shall handle the mandate personally. If losses are caused to the tourist due to the fault of the travel agency, the travel agency shall bear compensation liability.  
If a travel agency accepts a mandate from a tourist to provide services such as tour itinerary design and tourism information consultation, it shall ensure that the design is reasonable and feasible and that the information is timely and accurate.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　住宿经营者应当按照旅游服务合同的约定为团队旅游者提供住宿服务。住宿经营者未能按照旅游服务合同提供服务的，应当为旅游者提供不低于原定标准的住宿服务，因此增加的费用由住宿经营者承担；但由于不可抗力、政府因公共利益需要采取措施造成不能提供服务的，住宿经营者应当协助安排旅游者住宿。

**Article 75** An accommodation operator shall provide accommodation services to group tourists in accordance with the tourism service contract. If the accommodation operator fails to provide services in accordance with the tourism service contract, it shall provide accommodation services not lower than the originally agreed standard for the tourists, and any additional costs incurred thereby shall be borne by the accommodation operator; however, if the inability to provide services is caused by force majeure or measures taken by the government for public interest, the accommodation operator shall assist in arranging accommodation for the tourists.

### 第六章　旅游安全

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上人民政府统一负责旅游安全工作。县级以上人民政府有关部门依照法律、法规履行旅游安全监管职责。

**Article 76** The people's governments at or above the county level shall be uniformly responsible for tourism safety work. The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their tourism safety supervision duties in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国家建立旅游目的地安全风险提示制度。旅游目的地安全风险提示的级别划分和实施程序，由国务院旅游主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当将旅游安全作为突发事件监测和评估的重要内容。

**Article 77** The state shall establish a system for safety risk warnings for tourist destinations. The classification of safety risk warning levels for tourist destinations and the implementation procedures shall be formulated by the tourism department of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall take tourism safety as an important part of the monitoring and assessment of emergencies.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　县级以上人民政府应当依法将旅游应急管理纳入政府应急管理体系，制定应急预案，建立旅游突发事件应对机制。  
突发事件发生后，当地人民政府及其有关部门和机构应当采取措施开展救援，并协助旅游者返回出发地或者旅游者指定的合理地点。

**Article 78** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, incorporate tourism emergency management into the government emergency management system, formulate emergency plans, and establish a response mechanism for tourism emergencies.  
After the occurrence of an emergency, the local people's government and its relevant departments and institutions shall take measures to carry out rescue and assist tourists in returning to the place of departure or a reasonable location designated by the tourists.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　旅游经营者应当严格执行安全生产管理和消防安全管理的法律、法规和国家标准、行业标准，具备相应的安全生产条件，制定旅游者安全保护制度和应急预案。  
旅游经营者应当对直接为旅游者提供服务的从业人员开展经常性应急救助技能培训，对提供的产品和服务进行安全检验、监测和评估，采取必要措施防止危害发生。  
旅游经营者组织、接待老年人、未成年人、残疾人等旅游者，应当采取相应的安全保障措施。

**Article 79** Tourism operators shall strictly implement the laws, regulations, national standards, and industry standards on safety production management and fire safety management, possess corresponding safety production conditions, and formulate tourist safety protection systems and emergency plans.  
Tourism operators shall conduct regular emergency rescue skills training for employees who directly provide services to tourists, carry out safety inspection, monitoring, and assessment of the products and services provided, and take necessary measures to prevent hazards.  
When organizing or receiving tourists such as the elderly, minors, and disabled persons, tourism operators shall take corresponding safety protection measures.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　旅游经营者应当就旅游活动中的下列事项，以明示的方式事先向旅游者作出说明或者警示：  
（一）正确使用相关设施、设备的方法；  
（二）必要的安全防范和应急措施；  
（三）未向旅游者开放的经营、服务场所和设施、设备；  
（四）不适宜参加相关活动的群体；  
（五）可能危及旅游者人身、财产安全的其他情形。

**Article 80** Tourism operators shall, in an explicit manner, make explanations or warnings to tourists in advance regarding the following matters in tourism activities:  
(1) methods for proper use of relevant facilities and equipment;  
(2) necessary safety precautions and emergency measures;  
(3) business and service premises, facilities, and equipment not open to tourists;  
(4) groups unsuitable for participating in relevant activities;  
(5) other circumstances that may endanger the personal or property safety of tourists.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　突发事件或者旅游安全事故发生后，旅游经营者应当立即采取必要的救助和处置措施，依法履行报告义务，并对旅游者作出妥善安排。

**Article 81** After the occurrence of an emergency or a tourism safety accident, a tourism business operator shall immediately take necessary rescue and handling measures, perform the reporting obligation according to law, and make appropriate arrangements for tourists.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　旅游者在人身、财产安全遇有危险时，有权请求旅游经营者、当地政府和相关机构进行及时救助。  
中国出境旅游者在境外陷于困境时，有权请求我国驻当地机构在其职责范围内给予协助和保护。  
旅游者接受相关组织或者机构的救助后，应当支付应由个人承担的费用。

**Article 82** Tourists, when in danger of personal or property safety, have the right to request timely rescue from tourism business operators, local governments, and relevant agencies.  
Chinese outbound tourists, when stranded in a foreign country, have the right to request the relevant Chinese institutions stationed there to provide assistance and protection within the scope of their duties.  
After receiving rescue from relevant organizations or institutions, tourists shall pay the expenses that shall be borne by individuals.

### 第七章　旅游监督管理

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府旅游主管部门和有关部门依照本法和有关法律、法规的规定，在各自职责范围内对旅游市场实施监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织旅游主管部门、有关主管部门和市场监督管理、交通等执法部门对相关旅游经营行为实施监督检查。

**Article 83** The competent tourism department of the people's government at or above the county level and the relevant departments shall, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, exercise supervisory administration over the tourism market within their respective duties.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall organize the competent tourism departments, relevant competent departments, and market supervision and administration, transportation and other law enforcement departments to conduct inspection and supervision over the relevant tourism business activities.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　旅游主管部门履行监督管理职责，不得违反法律、行政法规的规定向监督管理对象收取费用。  
旅游主管部门及其工作人员不得参与任何形式的旅游经营活动。

**Article 84** When performing supervisory administration duties, the competent tourism department shall not collect fees from the objects of supervision in violation of the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.  
No competent tourism department or its staff member may participate in any form of tourism business activities.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　县级以上人民政府旅游主管部门有权对下列事项实施监督检查：  
（一）经营旅行社业务以及从事导游、领队服务是否取得经营、执业许可；  
（二）旅行社的经营行为；  
（三）导游和领队等旅游从业人员的服务行为；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他事项。  
旅游主管部门依照前款规定实施监督检查，可以对涉嫌违法的合同、票据、账簿以及其他资料进行查阅、复制。

**Article 85** The competent tourism department of the people's government at or above the county level has the right to exercise inspection and supervision over the following matters:  
(1) whether the license for business operations and practice has been obtained for the operation of travel agency business and the provision of tour guide and tour leader services;  
(2) the business conduct of travel agencies;  
(3) the service conduct of tourism practitioners such as tour guides and tour leaders;  
(4) other matters as stipulated by laws and regulations.  
When exercising inspection and supervision in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the competent tourism department may inspect and copy contracts, bills, account books, and other materials suspected of violating the law.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　旅游主管部门和有关部门依法实施监督检查，其监督检查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示合法证件。监督检查人员少于二人或者未出示合法证件的，被检查单位和个人有权拒绝。  
监督检查人员对在监督检查中知悉的被检查单位的商业秘密和个人信息应当依法保密。

**Article 86** When implementing inspection and supervision according to law, the competent tourism department and relevant departments shall have not less than two inspectors, and they shall present their legal credentials. If there are less than two inspectors or they fail to present their legal credentials, the unit or individual under inspection has the right to refuse.  
Inspectors shall keep confidential the trade secrets and personal information of the inspected unit that they become aware of during inspection and supervision according to law.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　对依法实施的监督检查，有关单位和个人应当配合，如实说明情况并提供文件、资料，不得拒绝、阻碍和隐瞒。

**Article 87** Regarding inspection and supervision implemented according to law, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate, truthfully explain the situation and provide documents and materials, and shall not refuse, obstruct, or conceal.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　县级以上人民政府旅游主管部门和有关部门，在履行监督检查职责中或者在处理举报、投诉时，发现违反本法规定行为的，应当依法及时作出处理；对不属于本部门职责范围的事项，应当及时书面通知并移交有关部门查处。

**Article 88** The competent tourism department of the people's government at or above the county level and relevant departments, when performing inspection and supervision duties or handling reports and complaints, if they discover any violation of this Law, shall handle it promptly according to law; for matters not within the scope of their duties, they shall promptly notify in writing and transfer to the relevant department for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　县级以上地方人民政府建立旅游违法行为查处信息的共享机制，对需要跨部门、跨地区联合查处的违法行为，应当进行督办。  
旅游主管部门和有关部门应当按照各自职责，及时向社会公布监督检查的情况。

**Article 89** The local people's government at or above the county level shall establish a sharing mechanism for information on the investigation and handling of illegal tourism acts, and shall supervise the handling of illegal acts that require joint investigation across departments and regions.  
The competent tourism department and relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, promptly disclose to the public the circumstances of inspection and supervision.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　依法成立的旅游行业组织依照法律、行政法规和章程的规定，制定行业经营规范和服务标准，对其会员的经营行为和服务质量进行自律管理，组织开展职业道德教育和业务培训，提高从业人员素质。

**Article 90** A tourism industry organization established according to law shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and its articles of association, formulate industry operation norms and service standards, conduct self-discipline management over the business conduct and service quality of its members, organize and carry out professional ethics education and vocational training, and improve the quality of practitioners.

### 第八章　旅游纠纷处理

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　县级以上人民政府应当指定或者设立统一的旅游投诉受理机构。受理机构接到投诉，应当及时进行处理或者移交有关部门处理，并告知投诉者。

**Article 91** The people's government at or above the county level shall designate or establish a unified tourism complaint acceptance agency. Upon receiving a complaint, the acceptance agency shall promptly handle it or transfer it to the relevant department for handling, and inform the complainant.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　旅游者与旅游经营者发生纠纷，可以通过下列途径解决：  
（一）双方协商；  
（二）向消费者协会、旅游投诉受理机构或者有关调解组织申请调解；  
（三）根据与旅游经营者达成的仲裁协议提请仲裁机构仲裁；  
（四）向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 92** Disputes between tourists and tourism business operators may be resolved through the following channels:  
(1) negotiation between the parties;  
(2) applying to consumer associations, tourism complaint acceptance agencies, or relevant mediation organizations for mediation;  
(3) submitting to an arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration agreement reached with the tourism business operator;  
(4) initiating a lawsuit in a People's Court.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　消费者协会、旅游投诉受理机构和有关调解组织在双方自愿的基础上，依法对旅游者与旅游经营者之间的纠纷进行调解。

**Article 93** Consumer associations, tourism complaint acceptance agencies, and relevant mediation organizations shall, on a voluntary basis of both parties, mediate disputes between tourists and tourism business operators according to law.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　旅游者与旅游经营者发生纠纷，旅游者一方人数众多并有共同请求的，可以推选代表人参加协商、调解、仲裁、诉讼活动。

**Article 94** When a dispute arises between tourists and tourism business operators, if the tourists are large in number and have common claims, they may elect representatives to participate in negotiation, mediation, arbitration, or litigation activities.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，未经许可经营旅行社业务的，由旅游主管部门或者市场监督管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；对有关责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款。  
旅行社违反本法规定，未经许可经营[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第一款第二项、第三项业务，或者出租、出借旅行社业务经营许可证，或者以其他方式非法转让旅行社业务经营许可的，除依照前款规定处罚外，并责令停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款。

**Article 95** Whoever, in violation of this Law, operates travel agency business without a license shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent tourism department or the market supervision and administration department, and his illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be additionally imposed; the directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan.  
If a travel agency, in violation of this Law, operates the business specified in Items (2) and (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 29 of this Law without a license, or leases or lends its travel agency business operation license, or illegally transfers the travel agency business operation license by other means, in addition to the penalty imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　旅行社违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款：  
（一）未按照规定为出境或者入境团队旅游安排领队或者导游全程陪同的；  
（二）安排未取得导游证的人员提供导游服务或者安排不具备领队条件的人员提供领队服务的；  
（三）未向临时聘用的导游支付导游服务费用的；  
（四）要求导游垫付或者向导游收取费用的。

**Article 96** If a travel agency, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent tourism department, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to arrange a tour leader or a tour guide to accompany the entire outbound or inbound group tour as required;  
(2) arranging personnel without a tour guide certificate to provide tour guide services, or arranging personnel who do not meet the conditions for tour leaders to provide tour leader services;  
(3) failing to pay tour guide service fees to temporarily employed tour guides;  
(4) requiring tour guides to advance funds or collecting fees from tour guides.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　旅行社违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由旅游主管部门或者有关部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款：  
（一）进行虚假宣传，误导旅游者的；  
（二）向不合格的供应商订购产品和服务的；  
（三）未按照规定投保旅行社责任保险的。

**Article 97** If a travel agency, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent tourism department or relevant department, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be additionally imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan:  
(1) conducting false publicity to mislead tourists;  
(2) ordering products and services from unqualified suppliers;  
(3) failing to purchase travel agency liability insurance as required.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　旅行社违反[本法第三十五条](#art-35)规定的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，责令停业整顿，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款；违法所得三十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，没收违法所得，处二千元以上二万以下罚款，并暂扣或者吊销导游证。

**Article 98** If a travel agency violates the provisions of Article 35 of this Law, it shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent tourism department, its illegal gains shall be confiscated, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be additionally imposed; if the circumstances are serious, its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall have their illegal gains confiscated, be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan, and their tour guide certificates shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　旅行社未履行[本法第五十五条](#art-55)规定的报告义务的，由旅游主管部门处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款，并暂扣或者吊销导游证。

**Article 99** If a travel agency fails to perform the reporting obligation as provided in Article 55 of this Law, the competent tourism department shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan, and their tour guide certificates shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　旅行社违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款，并责令停业整顿；造成旅游者滞留等严重后果的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款，并暂扣或者吊销导游证：  
（一）在旅游行程中擅自变更旅游行程安排，严重损害旅游者权益的；  
（二）拒绝履行合同的；  
（三）未征得旅游者书面同意，委托其他旅行社履行包价旅游合同的。

**Article 100** If a travel agency, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent tourism department, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification; if serious consequences such as tourists being stranded occur, its travel agency business operation license shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan, and their tour guide certificates shall be suspended or revoked:  
(1) arbitrarily changing the travel itinerary arrangement during the tour, seriously damaging the rights and interests of tourists;  
(2) refusing to perform the contract;  
(3) entrusting another travel agency to perform the package tour contract without obtaining the written consent of the tourists.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　旅行社违反本法规定，安排旅游者参观或者参与违反我国法律、法规和社会公德的项目或者活动的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，责令停业整顿，并处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款，并暂扣或者吊销导游证。

**Article 101** Where a travel agency, in violation of this Law, arranges for tourists to visit or participate in projects or activities that violate the laws, regulations, or public morals of China, the tourism administrative department shall order it to rectify, confiscate its illegal gains, order it to suspend operations for rectification, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its travel agency business license shall be revoked; and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan, and their tour guide certificates shall be temporarily sealed or revoked.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，未取得导游证或者不具备领队条件而从事导游、领队活动的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处一千元以上一万元以下罚款，予以公告。  
导游、领队违反本法规定，私自承揽业务的，由旅游主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，处一千元以上一万元以下罚款，并暂扣或者吊销导游证。  
导游、领队违反本法规定，向旅游者索取小费的，由旅游主管部门责令退还，处一千元以上一万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并暂扣或者吊销导游证。

**Article 102** Where a person, in violation of this Law, engages in tour guide or tour leader activities without having obtained a tour guide certificate or without meeting the conditions for being a tour leader, the tourism administrative department shall order him to rectify, confiscate his illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan, and shall make a public announcement.  
  
Where a tour guide or tour leader, in violation of this Law, privately undertakes business, the tourism administrative department shall order him to rectify, confiscate his illegal gains, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan, and temporarily seal or revoke his tour guide certificate.  
  
Where a tour guide or tour leader, in violation of this Law, solicits tips from tourists, the tourism administrative department shall order him to return the tips, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, his tour guide certificate shall also be temporarily sealed or revoked.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　违反本法规定被吊销导游证的导游、领队和受到吊销旅行社业务经营许可证处罚的旅行社的有关管理人员，自处罚之日起未逾三年的，不得重新申请导游证或者从事旅行社业务。

**Article 103** Where a tour guide or tour leader has had his tour guide certificate revoked for violating this Law, or a relevant manager of a travel agency has been punished by revocation of the travel agency business license, such persons shall not re-apply for a tour guide certificate or engage in travel agency business within three years from the date of the punishment.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　旅游经营者违反本法规定，给予或者收受贿赂的，由市场监督管理部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚；情节严重的，并由旅游主管部门吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 104** Where a tourism business operator, in violation of this Law, gives or accepts bribes, the market regulatory authority shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department shall also revoke its travel agency business license.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　景区不符合本法规定的开放条件而接待旅游者的，由景区主管部门责令停业整顿直至符合开放条件，并处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款。  
景区在旅游者数量可能达到最大承载量时，未依照本法规定公告或者未向当地人民政府报告，未及时采取疏导、分流等措施，或者超过最大承载量接待旅游者的，由景区主管部门责令改正，情节严重的，责令停业整顿一个月至六个月。

**Article 105** Where a scenic spot receives tourists without meeting the conditions for opening as provided for in this Law, the competent department of scenic spots shall order it to suspend operations for rectification until it meets the conditions for opening, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.  
  
Where a scenic spot, when the number of tourists may reach the maximum carrying capacity, fails to make an announcement in accordance with this Law, or fails to report to the local people's government, or fails to take timely measures such as guidance and diversion, or receives tourists exceeding the maximum carrying capacity, the competent department of scenic spots shall order it to rectify; if the circumstances are serious, order it to suspend operations for rectification for one to six months.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　景区违反本法规定，擅自提高门票或者另行收费项目的价格，或者有其他价格违法行为的，由有关主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚。

**Article 106** Where a scenic spot, in violation of this Law, raises the price of tickets or separately charged items without authorization, or commits other illegal pricing acts, the relevant competent department shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　旅游经营者违反有关安全生产管理和消防安全管理的法律、法规或者国家标准、行业标准的，由有关主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚。

**Article 107** Where a tourism business operator violates the laws, regulations, or national standards or industry standards concerning work safety management and fire safety management, the relevant competent department shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　对违反本法规定的旅游经营者及其从业人员，旅游主管部门和有关部门应当记入信用档案，向社会公布。

**Article 108** The tourism administrative department and relevant departments shall record tourism business operators and their employees who violate this Law into the credit files and make them public to the society.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　旅游主管部门和有关部门的工作人员在履行监督管理职责中，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 109** Where a staff member of the tourism administrative department or a relevant department, in performing the duty of supervision and administration, abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, which does not constitute a crime, he shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 110** Whoever violates this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）旅游经营者，是指旅行社、景区以及为旅游者提供交通、住宿、餐饮、购物、娱乐等服务的经营者。  
（二）景区，是指为旅游者提供游览服务、有明确的管理界限的场所或者区域。  
（三）包价旅游合同，是指旅行社预先安排行程，提供或者通过履行辅助人提供交通、住宿、餐饮、游览、导游或者领队等两项以上旅游服务，旅游者以总价支付旅游费用的合同。  
（四）组团社，是指与旅游者订立包价旅游合同的旅行社。  
（五）地接社，是指接受组团社委托，在目的地接待旅游者的旅行社。  
（六）履行辅助人，是指与旅行社存在合同关系，协助其履行包价旅游合同义务，实际提供相关服务的法人或者自然人。

**Article 111** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Tourism business operator" means an operator such as a travel agency, a scenic spot, and an operator that provides services such as transportation, accommodation, catering, shopping, and entertainment for tourists.  
(2) "Scenic spot" means a place or area that provides sightseeing services for tourists and has a clear management boundary.  
(3) "Package tour contract" means a contract under which a travel agency arranges the itinerary in advance, provides or provides through the performance assistant two or more tourism services such as transportation, accommodation, catering, sightseeing, tour guide or tour leader, and the tourist pays the total price for the tour.  
(4) "Organizing travel agency" means a travel agency that concludes a package tour contract with a tourist.  
(5) "Receiving travel agency" means a travel agency that accepts the entrustment of the organizing travel agency and receives tourists at the destination.  
(6) "Performance assistant" means a legal person or a natural person that has a contractual relationship with a travel agency, assists it in performing its obligations under the package tour contract, and actually provides relevant services.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　本法自2013年10月1日起施行。

**Article 112** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2013.
