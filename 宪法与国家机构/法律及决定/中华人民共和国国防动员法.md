# 中华人民共和国国防动员法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2010-02-26  
**生效日期**：2010-07-01

> （2010年2月26日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　组织领导机构及其职权 第三章　国防动员计划、实施预案与潜力统计调查 第四章　与国防密切相关的建设项目和重要产品 第五章　预备役人员的储备与征召 第六章　战略物资储备与调用 第七章　军品科研、生产与维修保障 第八章　战争灾害的预防与救助 第九章　国防勤务 第十章　民用资源征用与补偿 第十一章　宣传教育 第十二章　特别措施 第十三章　法律责任

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国防建设，完善国防动员制度，保障国防动员工作的顺利进行，维护国家的主权、统一、领土完整和安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of strengthening national defense construction, improving the national defense mobilization system, ensuring the smooth progress of national defense mobilization, and safeguarding the sovereignty, unification, territorial integrity and security of the State.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国防动员的准备、实施以及相关活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the preparation and implementation of national defense mobilization and relevant activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家加强国防动员建设，建立健全与国防安全需要相适应、与经济社会发展相协调、与突发事件应急机制相衔接的国防动员体系，增强国防动员能力。

**Article 3** The State shall strengthen the development of national defense mobilization, establish and improve a national defense mobilization system that is compatible with the needs of national defense security, coordinated with economic and social development, and linked to the emergency response mechanism for emergencies, and enhance the national defense mobilization capability.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国防动员坚持平战结合、军民结合、寓军于民的方针，遵循统一领导、全民参与、长期准备、重点建设、统筹兼顾、有序高效的原则。

**Article 4** National defense mobilization shall adhere to the policy of combining peacetime with wartime, combining the military with the civilian, and incorporating military potential into the civilian sector, and follow the principles of unified leadership, participation of the entire people, long-term preparation, priority construction, overall planning, and orderly and efficient operation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民和组织在和平时期应当依法完成国防动员准备工作；国家决定实施国防动员后，应当完成规定的国防动员任务。

**Article 5** Citizens and organizations shall, during peacetime, complete the preparatory work for national defense mobilization according to law; after the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, they shall fulfill the prescribed national defense mobilization tasks.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家保障国防动员所需经费。国防动员经费按照事权划分的原则，分别列入中央和地方财政预算。

**Article 6** The State shall guarantee the funds necessary for national defense mobilization. The funds for national defense mobilization shall, in accordance with the principle of division of powers, be included in the central and local fiscal budgets respectively.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对在国防动员工作中作出突出贡献的公民和组织，给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** The State shall commend and reward citizens and organizations that have made outstanding contributions in national defense mobilization work.

### 第二章　组织领导机构及其职权

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家的主权、统一、领土完整和安全遭受威胁时，全国人民代表大会常务委员会依照宪法和有关法律的规定，决定全国总动员或者局部动员。国家主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，发布动员令。

**Article 8** When the sovereignty, unification, territorial integrity and security of the State are threatened, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution and relevant laws, decide on general mobilization or partial mobilization. The President of the State shall, in accordance with the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, issue a mobilization order.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院、中央军事委员会共同领导全国的国防动员工作，制定国防动员工作的方针、政策和法规，向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出实施全国总动员或者局部动员的议案，根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定和国家主席发布的动员令，组织国防动员的实施。  
国家的主权、统一、领土完整和安全遭受直接威胁必须立即采取应对措施时，国务院、中央军事委员会可以根据应急处置的需要，采取本法规定的必要的国防动员措施，同时向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 9** The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall jointly lead the national defense mobilization work nationwide, formulate the guidelines, policies and regulations for national defense mobilization work, submit bills to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the implementation of general mobilization or partial mobilization, and organize the implementation of national defense mobilization in accordance with the decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the mobilization order issued by the President of the State.  
When the sovereignty, unification, territorial integrity and security of the State are directly threatened and immediate countermeasures must be taken, the State Council and the Central Military Commission may, in light of the needs of emergency response, take the necessary national defense mobilization measures as provided for in this Law, and shall simultaneously report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方人民政府应当贯彻和执行国防动员工作的方针、政策和法律、法规；国家决定实施国防动员后，应当根据上级下达的国防动员任务，组织本行政区域国防动员的实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府依照法律规定的权限管理本行政区域的国防动员工作。

**Article 10** Local people's governments shall implement and enforce the guidelines, policies, laws and regulations on national defense mobilization work; after the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, they shall organize the implementation of national defense mobilization within their respective administrative regions in accordance with the tasks of national defense mobilization assigned by the higher authorities.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within the limits of their powers prescribed by law, administer the national defense mobilization work in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门和军队有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的国防动员工作。

**Article 11** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant departments of the armed forces shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant national defense mobilization work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家国防动员委员会在国务院、中央军事委员会的领导下负责组织、指导、协调全国的国防动员工作；按照规定的权限和程序议定的事项，由国务院和中央军事委员会的有关部门按照各自职责分工组织实施。军区国防动员委员会、县级以上地方各级国防动员委员会负责组织、指导、协调本区域的国防动员工作。

**Article 12** The National Defense Mobilization Commission of the State shall, under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, be responsible for organizing, guiding and coordinating the national defense mobilization work nationwide; matters decided in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures shall be organized and implemented by the relevant departments of the State Council and the Central Military Commission according to their respective functions and duties. The national defense mobilization commissions of the military commands and the local national defense mobilization commissions at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing, guiding and coordinating the national defense mobilization work in their respective regions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国防动员委员会的办事机构承担本级国防动员委员会的日常工作，依法履行有关的国防动员职责。

**Article 13** The office of a national defense mobilization commission shall undertake the daily work of the national defense mobilization commission at the corresponding level and perform the relevant national defense mobilization functions according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家的主权、统一、领土完整和安全遭受的威胁消除后，应当按照决定实施国防动员的权限和程序解除国防动员的实施措施。

**Article 14** After the threat to the sovereignty, unification, territorial integrity and security of the State is eliminated, the implementation measures for national defense mobilization shall be lifted in accordance with the authority and procedures for deciding to implement national defense mobilization.

### 第三章　国防动员计划、实施预案与潜力统计调查

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行国防动员计划、国防动员实施预案和国防动员潜力统计调查制度。

**Article 15** The State shall practice the systems of national defense mobilization plans, national defense mobilization implementation plans and statistical surveys of national defense mobilization potential.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国防动员计划和国防动员实施预案，根据国防动员的方针和原则、国防动员潜力状况和军事需求编制。军事需求由军队有关部门按照规定的权限和程序提出。  
国防动员实施预案与突发事件应急处置预案应当在指挥、力量使用、信息和保障等方面相互衔接。

**Article 16** National defense mobilization plans and national defense mobilization implementation plans shall be compiled according to the guidelines and principles of national defense mobilization, the status of national defense mobilization potential and military requirements. Military requirements shall be put forward by the relevant departments of the armed forces according to the prescribed authority and procedures.  
National defense mobilization implementation plans and emergency response plans for emergencies shall be linked with each other in terms of command, use of forces, information and support.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级国防动员计划和国防动员实施预案的编制和审批，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 17** The compilation and approval of national defense mobilization plans and national defense mobilization implementation plans at all levels shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当将国防动员的相关内容纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。军队有关部门应当将国防动员实施预案纳入战备计划。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门和军队有关部门应当按照职责落实国防动员计划和国防动员实施预案。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the relevant contents of national defense mobilization into the plans for national economic and social development. The relevant departments of the armed forces shall incorporate national defense mobilization implementation plans into combat readiness plans.  
People's governments at or above the county level, their relevant departments and the relevant departments of the armed forces shall, according to their functions and duties, implement the national defense mobilization plans and national defense mobilization implementation plans.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府统计机构和有关部门应当根据国防动员的需要，准确及时地向本级国防动员委员会的办事机构提供有关统计资料。提供的统计资料不能满足需要时，国防动员委员会办事机构可以依据《中华人民共和国统计法》和国家有关规定组织开展国防动员潜力专项统计调查。

**Article 19** The statistical institutions and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the needs of national defense mobilization, provide accurate and timely statistical data to the office of the national defense mobilization commission at the corresponding level. When the statistical data provided cannot meet the needs, the office of the national defense mobilization commission may, in accordance with the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of the State, organize and carry out special statistical surveys on national defense mobilization potential.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家建立国防动员计划和国防动员实施预案执行情况的评估检查制度。

**Article 20** The State shall establish an evaluation and inspection system for the implementation of national defense mobilization plans and national defense mobilization implementation plans.

### 第四章　与国防密切相关的建设项目和重要产品

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　根据国防动员的需要，与国防密切相关的建设项目和重要产品应当贯彻国防要求，具备国防功能。

**Article 21** According to the needs of national defense mobilization, construction projects and important products that are closely related to national defense shall incorporate national defense requirements and possess national defense functions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　与国防密切相关的建设项目和重要产品目录，由国务院经济发展综合管理部门会同国务院其他有关部门以及军队有关部门拟定，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准。  
列入目录的建设项目和重要产品，其军事需求由军队有关部门提出；建设项目审批、核准和重要产品设计定型时，县级以上人民政府有关主管部门应当按照规定征求军队有关部门的意见。

**Article 22** The catalogue of construction projects and important products closely related to national defense shall be drafted by the comprehensive economic development department of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the armed forces, and shall be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.  
For the construction projects and important products listed in the catalogue, their military requirements shall be put forward by the relevant departments of the armed forces; when approving or ratifying construction projects and when finalizing the design of important products, the competent departments concerned of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions, solicit the opinions of the relevant departments of the armed forces.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　列入目录的建设项目和重要产品，应当依照有关法律、行政法规和贯彻国防要求的技术规范和标准进行设计、生产、施工、监理和验收，保证建设项目和重要产品的质量。

**Article 23** The construction projects and important products listed in the catalogue shall be designed, produced, constructed, supervised and accepted in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations and the technical specifications and standards that implement national defense requirements, so as to ensure the quality of the construction projects and important products.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业事业单位投资或者参与投资列入目录的建设项目建设或者重要产品研究、开发、制造的，依照有关法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，享受补贴或者其他政策优惠。

**Article 24** Where enterprises and institutions invest or participate in the investment in the construction of construction projects listed in the catalogue or in the research, development and manufacture of important products, they shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and the relevant provisions of the State, enjoy subsidies or other preferential policies.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府应当对列入目录的建设项目和重要产品贯彻国防要求工作给予指导和政策扶持，有关部门应当按照职责做好有关的管理工作。

**Article 25** People's governments at or above the county level shall provide guidance and policy support for the work of incorporating national defense requirements into the construction projects and important products listed in the catalogue, and the relevant departments shall, according to their functions and duties, properly carry out the relevant management work.

### 第五章　预备役人员的储备与征召

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家实行预备役人员储备制度。  
国家根据国防动员的需要，按照规模适度、结构科学、布局合理的原则，储备所需的预备役人员。  
国务院、中央军事委员会根据国防动员的需要，决定预备役人员储备的规模、种类和方式。

**Article 26** The State shall practice a system for the reserve of reservists.  
The State shall, in light of the needs of national defense mobilization and in accordance with the principles of appropriate scale, scientific structure and reasonable distribution, reserve the required reservists.  
The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall, in light of the needs of national defense mobilization, decide on the scale, types and methods of reserve of reservists.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　预备役人员按照专业对口、便于动员的原则，采取预编到现役部队、编入预备役部队、编入民兵组织或者其他形式进行储备。  
国家根据国防动员的需要，建立预备役专业技术兵员储备区。  
国家为预备役人员训练、储备提供条件和保障。预备役人员应当依法参加训练。

**Article 27** Reservists shall, in accordance with the principles of matching specialties and facilitating mobilization, be reserved by means of pre-assignment to active service units, incorporation into reserve service units, incorporation into militia organizations or other forms.  
The State shall, in light of the needs of national defense mobilization, establish reserve areas for reservists with specialized technical skills.  
The State shall provide conditions and guarantees for the training and reserve of reservists. Reservists shall participate in training according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关负责组织实施本行政区域预备役人员的储备工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门、预备役人员所在乡（镇）人民政府、街道办事处或者企业事业单位，应当协助兵役机关做好预备役人员储备的有关工作。

**Article 28** The military service organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of the reserve of reservists within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level, the people's governments of the townships (towns) or sub-district offices where the reservists are located, or the enterprises and institutions concerned shall assist the military service organs in properly carrying out the relevant work concerning the reserve of reservists.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　预编到现役部队和编入预备役部队的预备役人员、预定征召的其他预备役人员，离开预备役登记地一个月以上的，应当向其预备役登记的兵役机关报告。

**Article 29** Reservists who are pre-assigned to active service units or incorporated into reserve service units, and other reservists scheduled for call-up, who leave the place of their registration for reserve service for more than one month, shall report to the military service organ where they are registered for reserve service.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家决定实施国防动员后，县级人民政府兵役机关应当根据上级的命令，迅速向被征召的预备役人员下达征召通知。  
接到征召通知的预备役人员应当按照通知要求，到指定地点报到。

**Article 30** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, the military service organs of the people's governments at the county level shall, in accordance with the orders of the higher authorities, promptly issue notices of call-up to the reservists who are to be called up.  
Reservists who receive a notice of call-up shall report to the designated place as required by the notice.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被征召的预备役人员所在单位应当协助兵役机关做好预备役人员的征召工作。  
从事交通运输的单位和个人，应当优先运送被征召的预备役人员。

**Article 31** The units where the called-up reservists work shall assist the military service organs in properly carrying out the call-up of reservists.  
Units and individuals engaged in transport shall give priority to the transportation of called-up reservists.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家决定实施国防动员后，预定征召的预备役人员，未经其预备役登记地的县级人民政府兵役机关批准，不得离开预备役登记地；已经离开预备役登记地的，接到兵役机关通知后，应当立即返回或者到指定地点报到。

**Article 32** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, reservists scheduled for call-up shall not leave the place of their registration for reserve service without the approval of the military service organ of the people's government at the county level of that place; those who have already left the place of registration shall, upon receiving the notice from the military service organ, immediately return or report to the designated place.

### 第六章　战略物资储备与调用

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家实行适应国防动员需要的战略物资储备和调用制度。  
战略物资储备由国务院有关主管部门组织实施。

**Article 33** The State shall practice a system for the reserve and allocation of strategic materials that meets the needs of national defense mobilization.  
The reserve of strategic materials shall be organized and implemented by the competent departments concerned of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　承担战略物资储备任务的单位，应当按照国家有关规定和标准对储备物资进行保管和维护，定期调整更换，保证储备物资的使用效能和安全。  
国家按照有关规定对承担战略物资储备任务的单位给予补贴。

**Article 34** Units undertaking tasks of strategic material reserve shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions and standards of the State, keep and maintain the reserved materials, and regularly adjust and replace them, so as to ensure the use effectiveness and safety of the reserved materials.  
The State shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, grant subsidies to units undertaking tasks of strategic material reserve.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　战略物资按照国家有关规定调用。国家决定实施国防动员后，战略物资的调用由国务院和中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 35** Strategic materials shall be allocated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, the allocation of strategic materials shall be approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国防动员所需的其他物资的储备和调用，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 36** The reserve and allocation of other materials needed for national defense mobilization shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第七章　军品科研、生产与维修保障

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家建立军品科研、生产和维修保障动员体系，根据战时军队订货和装备保障的需要，储备军品科研、生产和维修保障能力。  
本法所称军品，是指用于军事目的的装备、物资以及专用生产设备、器材等。

**Article 37** The State shall establish a mobilization system for scientific research, production and maintenance support of military products, and reserve the capability for scientific research, production and maintenance support of military products in light of the needs of wartime military orders and equipment support.  
For the purposes of this Law, "military products" mean equipment, materials and specialized production equipment, apparatus, etc. used for military purposes.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　军品科研、生产和维修保障能力储备的种类、布局和规模，由国务院有关主管部门会同军队有关部门提出方案，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准后组织实施。

**Article 38** The types, layout and scale of the reserve of capability for scientific research, production and maintenance support of military products shall be proposed by the competent departments concerned of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the armed forces, and shall be organized and implemented after being submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　承担转产、扩大生产军品和维修保障任务的单位，应当根据所担负的国防动员任务，储备所需的设备、材料、配套产品、技术，建立所需的专业技术队伍，制定和完善预案与措施。

**Article 39** Units undertaking the tasks of converting to production or expanding the production of military products and of maintenance support shall, in light of the national defense mobilization tasks they assume, reserve the necessary equipment, materials, supporting products and technologies, set up the necessary specialized technical teams, and formulate and improve plans and measures.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　各级人民政府应当支持和帮助承担转产、扩大生产军品任务的单位开发和应用先进的军民两用技术，推广军民通用的技术标准，提高转产、扩大生产军品的综合保障能力。  
国务院有关主管部门应当对重大的跨地区、跨行业的转产、扩大生产军品任务的实施进行协调，并给予支持。

**Article 40** People's governments at all levels shall support and assist units undertaking the tasks of converting to production or expanding the production of military products in developing and applying advanced dual-use technologies, promoting dual-use technical standards, and improving the comprehensive support capability for converting to production or expanding the production of military products.  
The competent departments concerned of the State Council shall coordinate and support the implementation of major cross-regional and cross-industry tasks of converting to production or expanding the production of military products.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家决定实施国防动员后，承担转产、扩大生产军品任务的单位，应当按照国家军事订货合同和转产、扩大生产的要求，组织军品科研、生产，保证军品质量，按时交付订货，协助军队完成维修保障任务。为转产、扩大生产军品提供能源、材料、设备和配套产品的单位，应当优先满足转产、扩大生产军品的需要。  
国家对因承担转产、扩大生产军品任务造成直接经济损失的单位给予补偿。

**Article 41** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, units undertaking the tasks of converting to production or expanding the production of military products shall, in accordance with the State's military order contracts and the requirements for conversion or expansion, organize scientific research and production of military products, ensure the quality of military products, deliver the orders on time, and assist the armed forces in completing maintenance support tasks. Units that provide energy, materials, equipment and supporting products for the conversion to or expansion of production of military products shall give priority to meeting the needs of such conversion or expansion.  
The State shall provide compensation to units that suffer direct economic losses due to undertaking the tasks of converting to production or expanding the production of military products.

### 第八章　战争灾害的预防与救助

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家实行战争灾害的预防与救助制度，保护人民生命和财产安全，保障国防动员潜力和持续动员能力。

**Article 42** The State shall practice a system for the prevention of and relief from war disasters, protect the safety of people's lives and property, and safeguard the national defense mobilization potential and sustained mobilization capability.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家建立军事、经济、社会目标和首脑机关分级防护制度。分级防护标准由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。  
军事、经济、社会目标和首脑机关的防护工作，由县级以上人民政府会同有关军事机关共同组织实施。

**Article 43** The State shall establish a graded protection system for military, economic and social targets and leading organs. The standards for graded protection shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The protection of military, economic and social targets and leading organs shall be jointly organized and implemented by the people's governments at or above the county level together with the relevant military organs.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　承担军事、经济、社会目标和首脑机关防护任务的单位，应当制定防护计划和抢险抢修预案，组织防护演练，落实防护措施，提高综合防护效能。

**Article 44** Units undertaking the tasks of protecting military, economic and social targets and leading organs shall formulate protection plans and emergency repair plans, organize protection drills, implement protection measures, and enhance the comprehensive protection effectiveness.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家建立平战结合的医疗卫生救护体系。国家决定实施国防动员后，动员医疗卫生人员、调用药品器材和设备设施，保障战时医疗救护和卫生防疫。

**Article 45** The State shall establish a medical and health rescue system that combines peacetime with wartime. After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, it shall mobilize medical and health personnel, deploy medicines, equipment and facilities, and ensure wartime medical rescue and health epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家决定实施国防动员后，人员、物资的疏散和隐蔽，在本行政区域进行的，由本级人民政府决定并组织实施；跨行政区域进行的，由相关行政区域共同的上一级人民政府决定并组织实施。  
承担人员、物资疏散和隐蔽任务的单位，应当按照有关人民政府的决定，在规定时间内完成疏散和隐蔽任务。

**Article 46** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, the evacuation and concealment of personnel and materials shall, if conducted within the administrative region, be decided and organized by the people's government at the corresponding level; if conducted across administrative regions, be decided and organized by the common people's government at the next higher level of the relevant administrative regions.  
Units undertaking the tasks of evacuating and concealing personnel and materials shall, in accordance with the decisions of the relevant people's government, complete the evacuation and concealment tasks within the prescribed time.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　战争灾害发生时，当地人民政府应当迅速启动应急救助机制，组织力量抢救伤员、安置灾民、保护财产，尽快消除战争灾害后果，恢复正常生产生活秩序。  
遭受战争灾害的人员和组织应当及时采取自救、互救措施，减少战争灾害造成的损失。

**Article 47** When a war disaster occurs, the local people's government shall promptly activate the emergency rescue mechanism, organize forces to rescue the wounded, resettle the victims, protect property, eliminate the consequences of the war disaster as soon as possible, and restore normal production and living order.  
Persons and organizations affected by war disasters shall promptly take self-rescue and mutual-rescue measures to reduce the losses caused by the war disaster.

### 第九章　国防勤务

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家决定实施国防动员后，县级以上人民政府根据国防动员实施的需要，可以动员符合本法规定条件的公民和组织担负国防勤务。  
本法所称国防勤务，是指支援保障军队作战、承担预防与救助战争灾害以及协助维护社会秩序的任务。

**Article 48** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, the people's governments at or above the county level may, in light of the needs of implementing national defense mobilization, mobilize citizens and organizations that meet the conditions prescribed in this Law to undertake national defense service.  
For the purposes of this Law, "national defense service" means the tasks of supporting and ensuring the combat operations of the armed forces, undertaking the prevention of and relief from war disasters, and assisting in maintaining social order.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　十八周岁至六十周岁的男性公民和十八周岁至五十五周岁的女性公民，应当担负国防勤务；但有下列情形之一的，免予担负国防勤务：  
（一）在托儿所、幼儿园和孤儿院、养老院、残疾人康复机构、救助站等社会福利机构从事管理和服务工作的公民；  
（二）从事义务教育阶段学校教学、管理和服务工作的公民；  
（三）怀孕和在哺乳期内的女性公民；  
（四）患病无法担负国防勤务的公民；  
（五）丧失劳动能力的公民；  
（六）在联合国等政府间国际组织任职的公民；  
（七）其他经县级以上人民政府决定免予担负国防勤务的公民。  
有特殊专长的专业技术人员担负特定的国防勤务，不受前款规定的年龄限制。

**Article 49** Male citizens between the ages of eighteen and sixty and female citizens between the ages of eighteen and fifty-five shall undertake national defense service; however, those under any of the following circumstances shall be exempted from undertaking national defense service:  
(1) citizens engaged in management and service work in social welfare institutions such as nurseries, kindergartens, orphanages, homes for the elderly, rehabilitation institutions for persons with disabilities, and rescue stations;  
(2) citizens engaged in teaching, management and service work in schools at the compulsory education stage;  
(3) female citizens who are pregnant or within the lactation period;  
(4) citizens who are ill and cannot undertake national defense service;  
(5) citizens who have lost the ability to work;  
(6) citizens serving in intergovernmental international organizations such as the United Nations;  
(7) other citizens exempted from undertaking national defense service by decision of the people's government at or above the county level.  
Professional technicians with special expertise who undertake specific national defense service shall not be subject to the age restrictions specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　被确定担负国防勤务的人员，应当服从指挥、履行职责、遵守纪律、保守秘密。担负国防勤务的人员所在单位应当给予支持和协助。

**Article 50** Persons designated to undertake national defense service shall obey commands, perform their duties, observe discipline and keep secrets. The units where persons undertaking national defense service work shall provide support and assistance.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　交通运输、邮政、电信、医药卫生、食品和粮食供应、工程建筑、能源化工、大型水利设施、民用核设施、新闻媒体、国防科研生产和市政设施保障等单位，应当依法担负国防勤务。  
前款规定的单位平时应当按照专业对口、人员精干、应急有效的原则组建专业保障队伍，组织训练、演练，提高完成国防勤务的能力。

**Article 51** Units of transportation, postal service, telecommunications, medical and health care, food and grain supply, engineering construction, energy and chemical industry, large water conservancy facilities, civil nuclear facilities, news media, national defense scientific research and production, and municipal facilities support, among others, shall undertake national defense service according to law.  
The units prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall, in peacetime, form specialized support teams in accordance with the principles of matching specialties, lean personnel, and effective emergency response, organize training and drills, and enhance their capability to complete national defense service.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公民和组织担负国防勤务，由县级以上人民政府负责组织。  
担负预防与救助战争灾害、协助维护社会秩序勤务的公民和专业保障队伍，由当地人民政府指挥，并提供勤务和生活保障；跨行政区域执行勤务的，由相关行政区域的县级以上地方人民政府组织落实相关保障。  
担负支援保障军队作战勤务的公民和专业保障队伍，由军事机关指挥，伴随部队行动的由所在部队提供勤务和生活保障；其他的由当地人民政府提供勤务和生活保障。

**Article 52** The people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing citizens and organizations to undertake national defense service.  
Citizens and specialized support teams undertaking service in preventing and rescuing war disasters and assisting in maintaining social order shall be commanded by the local people's government, which shall also provide service and livelihood support; those performing service across administrative regions shall have the relevant support organized and implemented by the local people's governments at or above the county level of the relevant administrative regions.  
Citizens and specialized support teams undertaking service in supporting and ensuring military operations in combat shall be commanded by military organs; those accompanying troops shall have service and livelihood support provided by the unit they accompany; others shall have service and livelihood support provided by the local people's government.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　担负国防勤务的人员在执行勤务期间，继续享有原工作单位的工资、津贴和其他福利待遇；没有工作单位的，由当地县级人民政府参照民兵执行战备勤务的补贴标准给予补贴；因执行国防勤务伤亡的，由当地县级人民政府依照《军人抚恤优待条例》等有关规定给予抚恤优待。

**Article 53** Personnel undertaking national defense service shall, during the period of performing service, continue to enjoy the wages, allowances and other welfare benefits from their original work unit; those without a work unit shall be provided subsidies by the local people's government at the county level with reference to the subsidy standards for militiamen performing combat readiness service; those injured or killed due to performance of national defense service shall be accorded pension and preferential treatment by the local people's government at the county level in accordance with the Regulations on Pensions and Preferential Treatment for Servicemen and other relevant provisions.

### 第十章　民用资源征用与补偿

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家决定实施国防动员后，储备物资无法及时满足动员需要的，县级以上人民政府可以依法对民用资源进行征用。  
本法所称民用资源，是指组织和个人所有或者使用的用于社会生产、服务和生活的设施、设备、场所和其他物资。

**Article 54** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, if the reserve materials cannot meet the mobilization needs in a timely manner, the people's governments at or above the county level may, according to law, requisition civilian resources.  
For the purposes of this Law, "civilian resources" means the facilities, equipment, premises and other materials owned or used by organizations and individuals for social production, service and daily life.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　任何组织和个人都有接受依法征用民用资源的义务。  
需要使用民用资源的中国人民解放军现役部队和预备役部队、中国人民武装警察部队、民兵组织，应当提出征用需求，由县级以上地方人民政府统一组织征用。县级以上地方人民政府应当对被征用的民用资源予以登记，向被征用人出具凭证。

**Article 55** Every organization and individual shall have the obligation to accept the requisition of civilian resources according to law.  
The active service units and reserve service units of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organizations that need to use civilian resources shall submit requisition demands, and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the requisition in a unified manner. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall register the requisitioned civilian resources and issue a certificate to the person from whom the resources are requisitioned.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　下列民用资源免予征用：  
（一）个人和家庭生活必需的物品和居住场所；  
（二）托儿所、幼儿园和孤儿院、养老院、残疾人康复机构、救助站等社会福利机构保障儿童、老人、残疾人和救助对象生活必需的物品和居住场所；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定免予征用的其他民用资源。

**Article 56** The following civilian resources shall be exempted from requisition:  
(1) Articles and living premises necessary for the daily life of individuals and families;  
(2) Articles and living premises necessary for ensuring the lives of children, the elderly, persons with disabilities and relief recipients in social welfare institutions such as nurseries, kindergartens, orphanages, nursing homes, rehabilitation institutions for persons with disabilities, and relief stations;  
(3) Other civilian resources that are exempted from requisition as provided for by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　被征用的民用资源根据军事要求需要进行改造的，由县级以上地方人民政府会同有关军事机关组织实施。  
承担改造任务的单位应当按照使用单位提出的军事要求和改造方案进行改造，并保证按期交付使用。改造所需经费由国家负担。

**Article 57** Where the requisitioned civilian resources need to be modified according to military requirements, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the relevant military organs, organize and implement the modification.  
The unit undertaking the modification task shall carry out the modification in accordance with the military requirements and modification plan proposed by the using unit, and ensure that the resources are delivered for use on schedule. The funds required for modification shall be borne by the State.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　被征用的民用资源使用完毕，县级以上地方人民政府应当及时组织返还；经过改造的，应当恢复原使用功能后返还；不能修复或者灭失的，以及因征用造成直接经济损失的，按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 58** After the requisitioned civilian resources have been used up, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly organize their return; those that have been modified shall be returned after restoration of their original functions; those that cannot be repaired or are lost, and direct economic losses caused by the requisition, shall be compensated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　中国人民解放军现役部队和预备役部队、中国人民武装警察部队、民兵组织进行军事演习、训练，需要征用民用资源或者采取临时性管制措施的，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 59** Where the active service units and reserve service units of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organizations need to requisition civilian resources or adopt temporary control measures for military exercises or training, it shall be done in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第十一章　宣传教育

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　各级人民政府应当组织开展国防动员的宣传教育，增强公民的国防观念和依法履行国防义务的意识。有关军事机关应当协助做好国防动员的宣传教育工作。

**Article 60** People's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out publicity and education on national defense mobilization, to enhance citizens' awareness of national defense and their awareness of performing national defense obligations according to law. The relevant military organs shall assist in the publicity and education on national defense mobilization.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位和基层群众性自治组织，应当组织所属人员学习和掌握必要的国防知识与技能。

**Article 61** State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions, and grass-roots self-governing organizations shall organize their personnel to learn and master necessary national defense knowledge and skills.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　各级人民政府应当运用各种宣传媒体和宣传手段，对公民进行爱国主义、革命英雄主义宣传教育，激发公民的爱国热情，鼓励公民踊跃参战支前，采取多种形式开展拥军优属和慰问活动，按照国家有关规定做好抚恤优待工作。  
新闻出版、广播影视和网络传媒等单位，应当按照国防动员的要求做好宣传教育和相关工作。

**Article 62** People's governments at all levels shall use various publicity media and means to conduct publicity and education on patriotism and revolutionary heroism among citizens, inspire citizens' patriotic fervor, encourage citizens to actively participate in combat and support the front, carry out activities to support the army and give preferential treatment to the families of servicemen and martyrs in various forms, and do a good job in pension and preferential treatment in accordance with relevant State provisions.  
Units of press and publication, radio, film and television, and network media, among others, shall do a good job in publicity, education and relevant work in accordance with the requirements of national defense mobilization.

### 第十二章　特别措施

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家决定实施国防动员后，根据需要，可以依法在实施国防动员的区域采取下列特别措施：  
（一）对金融、交通运输、邮政、电信、新闻出版、广播影视、信息网络、能源水源供应、医药卫生、食品和粮食供应、商业贸易等行业实行管制；  
（二）对人员活动的区域、时间、方式以及物资、运载工具进出的区域进行必要的限制；  
（三）在国家机关、社会团体和企业事业单位实行特殊工作制度；  
（四）为武装力量优先提供各种交通保障；  
（五）需要采取的其他特别措施。

**Article 63** After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, it may, according to need and in accordance with law, adopt the following special measures in the areas where national defense mobilization is implemented:  
(1) Impose controls on industries such as finance, transportation, postal service, telecommunications, press and publication, radio, film and television, information network, energy and water supply, medical and health care, food and grain supply, and commercial trade;  
(2) Impose necessary restrictions on the areas, times and methods of personnel activities and on the entry and exit of materials and means of transport;  
(3) Adopt special working systems in State organs, social organizations, enterprises and institutions;  
(4) Give priority to providing various transport support for the armed forces;  
(5) Other special measures that need to be adopted.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在全国或者部分省、自治区、直辖市实行特别措施，由国务院、中央军事委员会决定并组织实施；在省、自治区、直辖市范围内的部分地区实行特别措施，由国务院、中央军事委员会决定，由特别措施实施区域所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和同级军事机关组织实施。

**Article 64** The adoption of special measures throughout the country or in some provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be decided and organized for implementation by the State Council and the Central Military Commission; the adoption of special measures in a part of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be decided by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and organized for implementation by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the special measures area is located and the military organ at the same level.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　组织实施特别措施的机关应当在规定的权限、区域和时限内实施特别措施。特别措施实施区域内的公民和组织，应当服从组织实施特别措施的机关的管理。

**Article 65** The organs organizing and implementing special measures shall implement the special measures within the prescribed authority, area and time limit. Citizens and organizations within the area where special measures are implemented shall submit to the management of the organs organizing and implementing the special measures.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　采取特别措施不再必要时，应当及时终止。

**Article 66** When special measures are no longer necessary, they shall be terminated promptly.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　因国家发布动员令，诉讼、行政复议、仲裁活动不能正常进行的，适用有关时效中止和程序中止的规定，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 67** Where, due to the issuance of a mobilization order by the State, litigation, administrative reconsideration, or arbitration proceedings cannot be conducted normally, the provisions on suspension of limitation periods and suspension of procedures shall apply, unless otherwise provided by law.

### 第十三章　法律责任

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　公民有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改的，强制其履行义务：  
（一）预编到现役部队和编入预备役部队的预备役人员、预定征召的其他预备役人员离开预备役登记地一个月以上未向预备役登记的兵役机关报告的；  
（二）国家决定实施国防动员后，预定征召的预备役人员未经预备役登记的兵役机关批准离开预备役登记地，或者未按照兵役机关要求及时返回，或者未到指定地点报到的；  
（三）拒绝、逃避征召或者拒绝、逃避担负国防勤务的；  
（四）拒绝、拖延民用资源征用或者阻碍对被征用的民用资源进行改造的；  
（五）干扰、破坏国防动员工作秩序或者阻碍从事国防动员工作的人员依法履行职责的。

**Article 68** Where a citizen commits any of the following acts, the people's government at the county level shall order him to make corrections within a specified time limit; if he fails to make corrections within the time limit, he shall be compelled to perform his obligations:  
(1) A reservist pre-assigned to an active service unit or incorporated into a reserve service unit, or other reservist scheduled for call-up, who leaves the place of reserve registration for more than one month without reporting to the military service organ where the reserve registration is made;  
(2) After the State decides to implement national defense mobilization, a reservist scheduled for call-up who leaves the place of reserve registration without approval from the military service organ where the reserve registration is made, or fails to return promptly as required by the military service organ, or fails to report to the designated location;  
(3) Refusing or evading call-up, or refusing or evading undertaking national defense service;  
(4) Refusing or delaying the requisition of civilian resources, or obstructing the modification of requisitioned civilian resources;  
(5) Interfering with or disrupting the order of national defense mobilization work, or obstructing personnel engaged in national defense mobilization work from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　企业事业单位有下列行为之一的，由有关人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改的，强制其履行义务，并可以处以罚款：  
（一）在承建的贯彻国防要求的建设项目中未按照国防要求和技术规范、标准进行设计或者施工、生产的；  
（二）因管理不善导致战略储备物资丢失、损坏或者不服从战略物资调用的；  
（三）未按照转产、扩大生产军品和维修保障任务的要求进行军品科研、生产和维修保障能力储备，或者未按照规定组建专业技术队伍的；  
（四）拒绝、拖延执行专业保障任务的；  
（五）拒绝或者故意延误军事订货的；  
（六）拒绝、拖延民用资源征用或者阻碍对被征用的民用资源进行改造的；  
（七）阻挠公民履行征召、担负国防勤务义务的。

**Article 69** Where an enterprise or institution commits any of the following acts, the relevant people's government shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be compelled to perform its obligations and may also be imposed a fine:  
(1) Failing to design, construct or produce in accordance with national defense requirements and technical specifications or standards in a construction project that undertakes the implementation of national defense requirements;  
(2) Causing loss of or damage to strategic reserve materials due to poor management, or failing to comply with the deployment of strategic materials;  
(3) Failing to conduct military product research and development, production and maintenance capability reserve in accordance with the requirements of conversion to or expansion of production of military products and maintenance support tasks, or failing to form specialized technical teams according to the provisions;  
(4) Refusing or delaying the performance of specialized support tasks;  
(5) Refusing or deliberately delaying military orders;  
(6) Refusing or delaying the requisition of civilian resources, or obstructing the modification of requisitioned civilian resources;  
(7) Hindering citizens from performing their obligations of call-up or undertaking national defense service.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）拒不执行上级下达的国防动员命令的；  
（二）滥用职权或者玩忽职守，给国防动员工作造成严重损失的；  
（三）对征用的民用资源，拒不登记、出具凭证，或者违反规定使用造成严重损坏，以及不按照规定予以返还或者补偿的；  
（四）泄露国防动员秘密的；  
（五）贪污、挪用国防动员经费、物资的；  
（六）滥用职权，侵犯和损害公民或者组织合法权益的。

**Article 70** Where any of the following acts is committed, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Refusing to carry out orders on national defense mobilization issued by a higher authority;  
(2) Abusing power or neglecting duties, causing serious losses to national defense mobilization work;  
(3) Refusing to register or issue a certificate for requisitioned civilian resources, or using them in violation of regulations causing serious damage, or failing to return or compensate as required;  
(4) Disclosing national defense mobilization secrets;  
(5) Embezzling or misappropriating national defense mobilization funds or materials;  
(6) Abusing power to infringe upon or damage the lawful rights and interests of citizens or organizations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law, constituting a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十四章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本法自2010年7月1日起施行。

**Article 72** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2010.
