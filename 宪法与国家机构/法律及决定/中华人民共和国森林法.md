# 中华人民共和国森林法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2019-12-28  
**生效日期**：2020-07-01

> （1984年9月20日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第七次会议通过　根据1998年4月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国森林法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第二次修正　2019年12月28日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十五次会议修订）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了践行绿水青山就是金山银山理念，保护、培育和合理利用森林资源，加快国土绿化，保障森林生态安全，建设生态文明，实现人与自然和谐共生，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of practicing the concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets, protecting, nurturing and rationally utilizing forest resources, accelerating the greening of the country, ensuring forest ecological security, building an ecological civilization, and achieving harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事森林、林木的保护、培育、利用和森林、林木、林地的经营管理活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the activities of protecting, nurturing and utilizing forests and trees, and the management of forests, trees and forest land within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　保护、培育、利用森林资源应当尊重自然、顺应自然，坚持生态优先、保护优先、保育结合、可持续发展的原则。

**Article 3** The protection, nurturing and utilization of forest resources shall respect and conform to nature, and adhere to the principles of giving priority to ecology and protection, combining conservation with nurturing, and sustainable development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行森林资源保护发展目标责任制和考核评价制度。上级人民政府对下级人民政府完成森林资源保护发展目标和森林防火、重大林业有害生物防治工作的情况进行考核，并公开考核结果。  
地方人民政府可以根据本行政区域森林资源保护发展的需要，建立林长制。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for the protection and development of forest resources. The people's government at a higher level shall assess the completion of forest resource protection and development targets, forest fire prevention, and major forestry pest control work by the people's government at a lower level, and shall make public the assessment results.  
Local people's governments may, based on the needs of forest resource protection and development within their respective administrative regions, establish a forest chief system.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家采取财政、税收、金融等方面的措施，支持森林资源保护发展。各级人民政府应当保障森林生态保护修复的投入，促进林业发展。

**Article 5** The State shall adopt fiscal, taxation, and financial measures to support the protection and development of forest resources. People's governments at all levels shall guarantee investment in the protection and restoration of forest ecology and promote the development of forestry.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家以培育稳定、健康、优质、高效的森林生态系统为目标，对公益林和商品林实行分类经营管理，突出主导功能，发挥多种功能，实现森林资源永续利用。

**Article 6** With the goal of cultivating a stable, healthy, high-quality and efficient forest ecosystem, the State shall implement classified management for public welfare forests and commercial forests, highlight their dominant functions, leverage multiple functions, and realize the sustainable utilization of forest resources.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立森林生态效益补偿制度，加大公益林保护支持力度，完善重点生态功能区转移支付政策，指导受益地区和森林生态保护地区人民政府通过协商等方式进行生态效益补偿。

**Article 7** The State shall establish a forest ecological benefit compensation system, increase support for the protection of public welfare forests, improve the transfer payment policy for key ecological function areas, and guide the people's governments of beneficiary areas and forest ecological protection areas to provide ecological benefit compensation through consultation and other means.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依照国家对民族自治地方自治权的规定，对民族自治地方的森林保护和林业发展实行更加优惠的政策。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国森林法实施条例.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国森林法实施条例》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the provisions of the State on the autonomous rights of national autonomous areas, implement more preferential policies for forest protection and forestry development in national autonomous areas.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院林业主管部门主管全国林业工作。县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门，主管本行政区域的林业工作。  
乡镇人民政府可以确定相关机构或者设置专职、兼职人员承担林业相关工作。

**Article 9** The competent forestry department of the State Council shall be in charge of forestry work nationwide. The competent forestry departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of forestry work within their respective administrative regions.  
The people's governments of townships and towns may designate relevant institutions or assign full-time or part-time personnel to undertake forestry-related work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　植树造林、保护森林，是公民应尽的义务。各级人民政府应当组织开展全民义务植树活动。  
每年三月十二日为植树节。

**Article 10** Afforestation and forest protection are the duties of citizens. People's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out compulsory tree-planting activities for all citizens.  
March 12 of each year shall be the Arbor Day.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家采取措施，鼓励和支持林业科学研究，推广先进适用的林业技术，提高林业科学技术水平。

**Article 11** The State shall take measures to encourage and support forestry scientific research, disseminate advanced and applicable forestry technologies, and improve the level of forestry science and technology.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级人民政府应当加强森林资源保护的宣传教育和知识普及工作，鼓励和支持基层群众性自治组织、新闻媒体、林业企业事业单位、志愿者等开展森林资源保护宣传活动。  
教育行政部门、学校应当对学生进行森林资源保护教育。

**Article 12** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the publicity, education and knowledge popularization work on forest resource protection, and encourage and support grassroots self-governing mass organizations, news media, forestry enterprises and institutions, volunteers, etc. to carry out publicity activities on forest resource protection.  
Education administrative departments and schools shall provide education on forest resource protection to students.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对在造林绿化、森林保护、森林经营管理以及林业科学研究等方面成绩显著的组织或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 13** Organizations or individuals that have made remarkable achievements in afforestation, forest protection, forest management, and forestry scientific research shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　森林权属

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　森林资源属于国家所有，由法律规定属于集体所有的除外。  
国家所有的森林资源的所有权由国务院代表国家行使。国务院可以授权国务院自然资源主管部门统一履行国有森林资源所有者职责。

**Article 14** Forest resources shall be owned by the State, except those which are owned by collectives as provided for by law.  
The ownership of State-owned forest resources shall be exercised by the State Council on behalf of the State. The State Council may authorize the competent department of natural resources of the State Council to uniformly perform the duties of the owner of State-owned forest resources.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　林地和林地上的森林、林木的所有权、使用权，由不动产登记机构统一登记造册，核发证书。国务院确定的国家重点林区（以下简称重点林区）的森林、林木和林地，由国务院自然资源主管部门负责登记。  
森林、林木、林地的所有者和使用者的合法权益受法律保护，任何组织和个人不得侵犯。  
森林、林木、林地的所有者和使用者应当依法保护和合理利用森林、林木、林地，不得非法改变林地用途和毁坏森林、林木、林地。

**Article 15** The ownership and right to use of forest land and the forests and trees thereon shall be uniformly registered and recorded by the real estate registration authority, which shall issue certificates. The forests, trees and forest land in the key State forest areas designated by the State Council (hereinafter referred to as key forest areas) shall be registered by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council.  
The lawful rights and interests of the owners and users of forests, trees and forest land shall be protected by law, and no organization or individual may infringe upon them.  
The owners and users of forests, trees and forest land shall protect and rationally utilize forests, trees and forest land according to law, and shall not illegally change the use of forest land or damage forests, trees or forest land.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家所有的林地和林地上的森林、林木可以依法确定给林业经营者使用。林业经营者依法取得的国有林地和林地上的森林、林木的使用权，经批准可以转让、出租、作价出资等。具体办法由国务院制定。  
林业经营者应当履行保护、培育森林资源的义务，保证国有森林资源稳定增长，提高森林生态功能。

**Article 16** State-owned forest land and the forests and trees thereon may be designated for use by forestry operators according to law. The right to use State-owned forest land and the forests and trees thereon legally obtained by forestry operators may, upon approval, be transferred, leased, or contributed as investment in value, etc. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.  
Forestry operators shall fulfill the obligation to protect and nurture forest resources, ensure the steady growth of State-owned forest resources, and improve the ecological functions of forests.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　集体所有和国家所有依法由农民集体使用的林地（以下简称集体林地）实行承包经营的，承包方享有林地承包经营权和承包林地上的林木所有权，合同另有约定的从其约定。承包方可以依法采取出租（转包）、入股、转让等方式流转林地经营权、林木所有权和使用权。

**Article 17** Where contract operations are implemented for forest land collectively owned or owned by the State but used by farmer collectives according to law (hereinafter referred to as collective forest land), the contractor shall enjoy the right to contract and operate the forest land and the ownership of the trees on the contracted forest land, unless otherwise agreed in the contract. The contractor may, according to law, transfer the right to operate forest land, the ownership and right to use trees by means of leasing (subcontracting), shareholding, transfer, etc.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　未实行承包经营的集体林地以及林地上的林木，由农村集体经济组织统一经营。经本集体经济组织成员的村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表同意并公示，可以通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式依法流转林地经营权、林木所有权和使用权。

**Article 18** Collective forest land for which contract operations are not implemented and the trees thereon shall be managed uniformly by the rural collective economic organization. With the consent of more than two-thirds of the members of the villagers' assembly of the members of the collective economic organization or more than two-thirds of the villagers' representatives and after public announcement, the right to operate forest land, the ownership and right to use trees may be transferred by means of bidding, auction, public negotiation, etc. according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　集体林地经营权流转应当签订书面合同。林地经营权流转合同一般包括流转双方的权利义务、流转期限、流转价款及支付方式、流转期限届满林地上的林木和固定生产设施的处置、违约责任等内容。  
受让方违反法律规定或者合同约定造成森林、林木、林地严重毁坏的，发包方或者承包方有权收回林地经营权。

**Article 19** The transfer of the right to operate collective forest land shall be concluded in a written contract. A contract for the transfer of the right to operate forest land shall generally include the rights and obligations of both parties, the term of transfer, the transfer price and payment method, the disposal of trees and fixed production facilities on the forest land upon expiration of the transfer term, and liability for breach of contract.  
Where the transferee, in violation of legal provisions or the contract, causes severe damage to forests, trees or forest land, the lessor or the contractor shall have the right to recover the right to operate the forest land.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国有企业事业单位、机关、团体、部队营造的林木，由营造单位管护并按照国家规定支配林木收益。  
农村居民在房前屋后、自留地、自留山种植的林木，归个人所有。城镇居民在自有房屋的庭院内种植的林木，归个人所有。  
集体或者个人承包国家所有和集体所有的宜林荒山荒地荒滩营造的林木，归承包的集体或者个人所有；合同另有约定的从其约定。  
其他组织或者个人营造的林木，依法由营造者所有并享有林木收益；合同另有约定的从其约定。

**Article 20** Trees planted by State-owned enterprises and institutions, government organs, organizations and armed forces shall be managed and protected by the planting units, which shall dispose of the proceeds from the trees in accordance with State regulations.  
Trees planted by rural residents in front of or behind their houses, on their private plots or private hills shall be owned by the individuals. Trees planted by urban residents in the courtyards of their own houses shall be owned by the individuals.  
Trees planted on barren mountains, wasteland and barren beaches suitable for afforestation owned by the State or owned by collectives and contracted by collectives or individuals shall be owned by the contracting collectives or individuals, unless otherwise agreed in the contract.  
Trees planted by other organizations or individuals shall be owned by the planters according to law, and the planters shall enjoy the proceeds from the trees, unless otherwise agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　为了生态保护、基础设施建设等公共利益的需要，确需征收、征用林地、林木的，应当依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》等法律、行政法规的规定办理审批手续，并给予公平、合理的补偿。

**Article 21** For the needs of public interests such as ecological protection and infrastructure construction, where it is truly necessary to expropriate or requisition forestland and forest trees, approval formalities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations such as the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, and fair and reasonable compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　单位之间发生的林木、林地所有权和使用权争议，由县级以上人民政府依法处理。  
个人之间、个人与单位之间发生的林木所有权和林地使用权争议，由乡镇人民政府或者县级以上人民政府依法处理。  
当事人对有关人民政府的处理决定不服的，可以自接到处理决定通知之日起三十日内，向人民法院起诉。  
在林木、林地权属争议解决前，除因森林防火、林业有害生物防治、国家重大基础设施建设等需要外，当事人任何一方不得砍伐有争议的林木或者改变林地现状。

**Article 22** Disputes over the ownership and right to use of forest trees and forestland between entities shall be handled by the people's government at or above the county level according to law.  
Disputes over the ownership of forest trees and the right to use of forestland between individuals, or between individuals and entities, shall be handled by the people's government at the township level or the people's government at or above the county level according to law.  
Where a party is dissatisfied with the handling decision of the relevant people's government, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within thirty days from the date of receipt of the notice of the handling decision.  
Before the dispute over forest tree and forestland ownership is resolved, no party shall fell the disputed forest trees or change the current status of the forestland, except for needs such as forest fire prevention, forest pest control, and major national infrastructure construction.

### 第三章　发展规划

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府应当将森林资源保护和林业发展纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 23** The people's government at or above the county level shall incorporate forest resource protection and forestry development into the national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府应当落实国土空间开发保护要求，合理规划森林资源保护利用结构和布局，制定森林资源保护发展目标，提高森林覆盖率、森林蓄积量，提升森林生态系统质量和稳定性。

**Article 24** The people's government at or above the county level shall implement the requirements for territorial space development and protection, rationally plan the structure and layout of forest resource protection and utilization, formulate forest resource protection and development targets, increase the forest coverage rate and forest growing stock, and improve the quality and stability of the forest ecosystem.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当根据森林资源保护发展目标，编制林业发展规划。下级林业发展规划依据上级林业发展规划编制。

**Article 25** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the forest resource protection and development targets, formulate a forestry development plan. The lower-level forestry development plan shall be formulated in accordance with the upper-level forestry development plan.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门可以结合本地实际，编制林地保护利用、造林绿化、森林经营、天然林保护等相关专项规划。

**Article 26** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level may, in light of local conditions, formulate relevant special plans such as forestland protection and utilization, afforestation, forest management, and natural forest protection.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家建立森林资源调查监测制度，对全国森林资源现状及变化情况进行调查、监测和评价，并定期公布。

**Article 27** The State shall establish a forest resource survey and monitoring system to survey, monitor and evaluate the current status and changes of national forest resources, and regularly publish the results.

### 第四章　森林保护

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家加强森林资源保护，发挥森林蓄水保土、调节气候、改善环境、维护生物多样性和提供林产品等多种功能。

**Article 28** The State shall strengthen forest resource protection to give full play to multiple functions of forests, such as water conservation, soil conservation, climate regulation, environmental improvement, biodiversity maintenance, and provision of forest products.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　中央和地方财政分别安排资金，用于公益林的营造、抚育、保护、管理和非国有公益林权利人的经济补偿等，实行专款专用。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同林业主管部门制定。

**Article 29** The central and local finance departments shall respectively allocate funds for the establishment, tending, protection, and management of public welfare forests, as well as economic compensation to non-state public welfare forest right holders, and shall ensure that such funds are used for specified purposes only. Specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the competent forestry department.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家支持重点林区的转型发展和森林资源保护修复，改善生产生活条件，促进所在地区经济社会发展。重点林区按照规定享受国家重点生态功能区转移支付等政策。

**Article 30** The State shall support the transformation and development of key forest areas and the protection and restoration of forest resources, improve production and living conditions, and promote the economic and social development of the regions where they are located. Key forest areas shall enjoy policies such as transfer payments from the national key ecological function zones in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家在不同自然地带的典型森林生态地区、珍贵动物和植物生长繁殖的林区、天然热带雨林区和具有特殊保护价值的其他天然林区，建立以国家公园为主体的自然保护地体系，加强保护管理。  
国家支持生态脆弱地区森林资源的保护修复。  
县级以上人民政府应当采取措施对具有特殊价值的野生植物资源予以保护。

**Article 31** The State shall establish a system of natural protected areas with national parks as the main body in typical forest ecological areas of different natural zones, forest areas for the growth and reproduction of precious animals and plants, natural tropical rainforest areas, and other natural forest areas with special protection value, to strengthen protection and management.  
The State shall support the protection and restoration of forest resources in ecologically fragile areas.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall take measures to protect wild plant resources with special value.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家实行天然林全面保护制度，严格限制天然林采伐，加强天然林管护能力建设，保护和修复天然林资源，逐步提高天然林生态功能。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 32** The State shall implement a comprehensive natural forest protection system, strictly restrict the felling of natural forests, strengthen the capacity building of natural forest management and protection, protect and restore natural forest resources, and gradually improve the ecological functions of natural forests. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　地方各级人民政府应当组织有关部门建立护林组织，负责护林工作；根据实际需要建设护林设施，加强森林资源保护；督促相关组织订立护林公约、组织群众护林、划定护林责任区、配备专职或者兼职护林员。  
县级或者乡镇人民政府可以聘用护林员，其主要职责是巡护森林，发现火情、林业有害生物以及破坏森林资源的行为，应当及时处理并向当地林业等有关部门报告。

**Article 33** Local people's governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments to establish forest protection organizations responsible for forest protection work; construct forest protection facilities according to actual needs to strengthen forest resource protection; urge relevant organizations to conclude forest protection covenants, organize the masses to protect forests, designate forest protection responsibility areas, and assign full-time or part-time forest rangers.  
The people's government at the county level or township level may employ forest rangers, whose main duties are to patrol forests, detect fires, forest pests, and acts that damage forest resources, and shall promptly handle and report to the local forestry and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域的森林防火工作，发挥群防作用；县级以上人民政府组织领导应急管理、林业、公安等部门按照职责分工密切配合做好森林火灾的科学预防、扑救和处置工作：  
（一）组织开展森林防火宣传活动，普及森林防火知识；  
（二）划定森林防火区，规定森林防火期；  
（三）设置防火设施，配备防灭火装备和物资；  
（四）建立森林火灾监测预警体系，及时消除隐患；  
（五）制定森林火灾应急预案，发生森林火灾，立即组织扑救；  
（六）保障预防和扑救森林火灾所需费用。  
国家综合性消防救援队伍承担国家规定的森林火灾扑救任务和预防相关工作。

**Article 34** Local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible for forest fire prevention work within their administrative areas and give play to the role of mass prevention; the people's government at or above the county level shall organize and lead the emergency management, forestry, public security and other departments to closely cooperate according to their respective responsibilities to scientifically prevent, fight and dispose of forest fires:  
(1) organize forest fire prevention publicity activities to popularize forest fire prevention knowledge;  
(2) designate forest fire prevention zones and stipulate forest fire prevention periods;  
(3) set up fire prevention facilities and equip with fire prevention and fighting equipment and materials;  
(4) establish a forest fire monitoring and early warning system to promptly eliminate hidden dangers;  
(5) formulate forest fire emergency plans, and upon the occurrence of a forest fire, immediately organize firefighting;  
(6) guarantee the expenses needed for the prevention and fighting of forest fires.  
The national comprehensive fire rescue team shall undertake the tasks of forest fire fighting and related prevention work as stipulated by the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门负责本行政区域的林业有害生物的监测、检疫和防治。  
省级以上人民政府林业主管部门负责确定林业植物及其产品的检疫性有害生物，划定疫区和保护区。  
重大林业有害生物灾害防治实行地方人民政府负责制。发生暴发性、危险性等重大林业有害生物灾害时，当地人民政府应当及时组织除治。  
林业经营者在政府支持引导下，对其经营管理范围内的林业有害生物进行防治。

**Article 35** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the monitoring, quarantine and control of forest pests in its administrative area.  
The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall be responsible for determining the quarantine pests of forest plants and their products, and designating epidemic areas and protected areas.  
For the prevention and control of major forest pest disasters, the local people's government shall assume responsibility. When outbreaks or dangerous major forest pest disasters occur, the local people's government shall promptly organize extermination.  
Forestry operators, under the guidance and support of the government, shall carry out the prevention and control of forest pests within their management and operation scope.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家保护林地，严格控制林地转为非林地，实行占用林地总量控制，确保林地保有量不减少。各类建设项目占用林地不得超过本行政区域的占用林地总量控制指标。

**Article 36** The State shall protect forestland, strictly control the conversion of forestland to non-forestland, implement total quantity control of forestland occupation, and ensure that the area of forestland does not decrease. All types of construction projects occupying forestland shall not exceed the total quantity control target for forestland occupation in the administrative area.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　矿藏勘查、开采以及其他各类工程建设，应当不占或者少占林地；确需占用林地的，应当经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门审核同意，依法办理建设用地审批手续。  
占用林地的单位应当缴纳森林植被恢复费。森林植被恢复费征收使用管理办法由国务院财政部门会同林业主管部门制定。  
县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当按照规定安排植树造林，恢复森林植被，植树造林面积不得少于因占用林地而减少的森林植被面积。上级林业主管部门应当定期督促下级林业主管部门组织植树造林、恢复森林植被，并进行检查。

**Article 37** For mineral resources exploration, mining, and other types of engineering construction, forestland shall not be occupied or shall be occupied as little as possible; where it is truly necessary to occupy forestland, the approval of the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained, and the formalities for approving construction land shall be handled according to law.  
The unit occupying forestland shall pay a forest vegetation restoration fee. The measures for the collection and use management of the forest vegetation restoration fee shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the competent forestry department.  
The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall arrange for afforestation according to regulations to restore forest vegetation, and the area of afforestation shall not be less than the area of forest vegetation reduced due to forestland occupation. The higher-level competent forestry department shall regularly urge the lower-level competent forestry department to organize afforestation and restore forest vegetation, and conduct inspections.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　需要临时使用林地的，应当经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门批准；临时使用林地的期限一般不超过二年，并不得在临时使用的林地上修建永久性建筑物。  
临时使用林地期满后一年内，用地单位或者个人应当恢复植被和林业生产条件。

**Article 38** Where it is necessary to use forestland temporarily, the approval of the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained; the period of temporary use of forestland shall generally not exceed two years, and no permanent structures shall be constructed on the temporarily used forestland.  
Within one year after the expiration of the temporary use of forestland, the unit or individual using the land shall restore the vegetation and forestry production conditions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　禁止毁林开垦、采石、采砂、采土以及其他毁坏林木和林地的行为。  
禁止向林地排放重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污水、污泥，以及可能造成林地污染的清淤底泥、尾矿、矿渣等。  
禁止在幼林地砍柴、毁苗、放牧。  
禁止擅自移动或者损坏森林保护标志。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/自然资源/森林采伐更新管理办法.md#art-19" title="被《森林采伐更新管理办法》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/自然资源/森林采伐更新管理办法.md#art-20" title="被《森林采伐更新管理办法》第20条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 39** It shall be prohibited to destroy forests by cultivation, quarrying, sand mining, earth digging, or other acts that damage forest trees and forestland.  
It shall be prohibited to discharge into forestland sewage or sludge containing heavy metals or other toxic and harmful substances exceeding the standards, as well as dredged sediment, tailings, slag, etc. that may cause pollution of forestland.  
It shall be prohibited to chop firewood, destroy seedlings, or graze livestock in young forests.  
It shall be prohibited to move or damage forest protection signs without authorization.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家保护古树名木和珍贵树木。禁止破坏古树名木和珍贵树木及其生存的自然环境。

**Article 40** The State shall protect ancient and famous trees as well as precious trees. It shall be prohibited to destroy ancient and famous trees, precious trees, and their natural living environment.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级人民政府应当加强林业基础设施建设，应用先进适用的科技手段，提高森林防火、林业有害生物防治等森林管护能力。  
各有关单位应当加强森林管护。国有林业企业事业单位应当加大投入，加强森林防火、林业有害生物防治，预防和制止破坏森林资源的行为。

**Article 41** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen forestry infrastructure construction, apply advanced and applicable scientific and technological means, and improve the capabilities of forest management and protection such as forest fire prevention and forestry pest control.  
All relevant units shall strengthen forest management and protection. State-owned forestry enterprises and institutions shall increase investment, strengthen forest fire prevention and forestry pest control, and prevent and stop acts of destroying forest resources.

### 第五章　造林绿化

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家统筹城乡造林绿化，开展大规模国土绿化行动，绿化美化城乡，推动森林城市建设，促进乡村振兴，建设美丽家园。

**Article 42** The State shall coordinate urban and rural afforestation and greening, carry out large-scale national land greening actions, green and beautify urban and rural areas, promote the construction of forest cities, facilitate rural revitalization, and build a beautiful homeland.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　各级人民政府应当组织各行各业和城乡居民造林绿化。  
宜林荒山荒地荒滩，属于国家所有的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门和其他有关主管部门组织开展造林绿化；属于集体所有的，由集体经济组织组织开展造林绿化。  
城市规划区内、铁路公路两侧、江河两侧、湖泊水库周围，由各有关主管部门按照有关规定因地制宜组织开展造林绿化；工矿区、工业园区、机关、学校用地，部队营区以及农场、牧场、渔场经营地区，由各该单位负责造林绿化。组织开展城市造林绿化的具体办法由国务院制定。  
国家所有和集体所有的宜林荒山荒地荒滩可以由单位或者个人承包造林绿化。

**Article 43** People's governments at all levels shall organize all trades and industries as well as urban and rural residents to engage in afforestation and greening.  
Barren mountains, wasteland, and barren beaches suitable for afforestation that are owned by the State shall be subject to organized afforestation and greening by the competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant competent departments; those owned by collectives shall be subject to organized afforestation and greening by collective economic organizations.  
Within urban planning areas, along both sides of railways and highways, along both sides of rivers, and around lakes and reservoirs, the relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, organize afforestation and greening according to local conditions; on land used by mining areas, industrial parks, government organs, schools, military camps, and areas operated by farms, pastures, and fisheries, the respective units shall be responsible for afforestation and greening. The specific measures for organizing urban afforestation and greening shall be formulated by the State Council.  
Barren mountains, wasteland, and barren beaches suitable for afforestation that are owned by the State or collectives may be contracted by units or individuals for afforestation and greening.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家鼓励公民通过植树造林、抚育管护、认建认养等方式参与造林绿化。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国森林法实施条例.md#art-41" title="被《中华人民共和国森林法实施条例》第41条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 44** The State shall encourage citizens to participate in afforestation and greening through methods such as tree planting, tending and management, and voluntary construction and adoption.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　各级人民政府组织造林绿化，应当科学规划、因地制宜，优化林种、树种结构，鼓励使用乡土树种和林木良种、营造混交林，提高造林绿化质量。  
国家投资或者以国家投资为主的造林绿化项目，应当按照国家规定使用林木良种。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/自然资源/森林采伐更新管理办法.md#art-23" title="被《森林采伐更新管理办法》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 45** People's governments at all levels, when organizing afforestation and greening, shall carry out scientific planning and adapt measures to local conditions, optimize the structure of forest categories and tree species, encourage the use of native tree species and improved tree varieties, and create mixed forests, so as to improve the quality of afforestation and greening.  
Afforestation and greening projects invested by the State or mainly invested by the State shall use improved tree varieties in accordance with national regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民政府应当采取以自然恢复为主、自然恢复和人工修复相结合的措施，科学保护修复森林生态系统。新造幼林地和其他应当封山育林的地方，由当地人民政府组织封山育林。  
各级人民政府应当对国务院确定的坡耕地、严重沙化耕地、严重石漠化耕地、严重污染耕地等需要生态修复的耕地，有计划地组织实施退耕还林还草。  
各级人民政府应当对自然因素等导致的荒废和受损山体、退化林地以及宜林荒山荒地荒滩，因地制宜实施森林生态修复工程，恢复植被。

**Article 46** People's governments at all levels shall adopt measures that combine natural restoration as the primary method with artificial restoration, to scientifically protect and restore forest ecosystems. For newly planted young forests and other areas where hillsides should be closed for afforestation, the local people's governments shall organize the closure of hillsides for afforestation.  
People's governments at all levels shall, in a planned manner, organize and implement the conversion of cropland to forest and grassland for croplands that require ecological restoration, such as sloping croplands, severely desertified croplands, severely rock-desertified croplands, and severely polluted croplands as determined by the State Council.  
People's governments at all levels shall, according to local conditions, implement forest ecological restoration projects for abandoned and damaged mountains, degraded forests, and barren mountains, wasteland, and barren beaches suitable for afforestation caused by natural factors, to restore vegetation.

### 第六章　经营管理

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家根据生态保护的需要，将森林生态区位重要或者生态状况脆弱，以发挥生态效益为主要目的的林地和林地上的森林划定为公益林。未划定为公益林的林地和林地上的森林属于商品林。

**Article 47** The State may, based on the needs of ecological protection, designate forestlands and forests on such forestlands that are of important ecological location or ecologically fragile and primarily for ecological benefits as public welfare forests. Forestlands and forests on such forestlands not designated as public welfare forests shall be commercial forests.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　公益林由国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府划定并公布。  
下列区域的林地和林地上的森林，应当划定为公益林：  
（一）重要江河源头汇水区域；  
（二）重要江河干流及支流两岸、饮用水水源地保护区；  
（三）重要湿地和重要水库周围；  
（四）森林和陆生野生动物类型的自然保护区；  
（五）荒漠化和水土流失严重地区的防风固沙林基干林带；  
（六）沿海防护林基干林带；  
（七）未开发利用的原始林地区；  
（八）需要划定的其他区域。  
公益林划定涉及非国有林地的，应当与权利人签订书面协议，并给予合理补偿。  
公益林进行调整的，应当经原划定机关同意，并予以公布。  
国家级公益林划定和管理的办法由国务院制定；地方级公益林划定和管理的办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 48** Public welfare forests shall be designated and announced by the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Forestlands and forests on such forestlands in the following areas shall be designated as public welfare forests:  
(1) Water catchment areas of important river sources;  
(2) Both banks of the main streams and tributaries of important rivers, and protected areas of drinking water sources;  
(3) Surrounding areas of important wetlands and important reservoirs;  
(4) Nature reserves of the forest and terrestrial wildlife types;  
(5) Key shelterbelt forest belts for wind and sand fixation in areas with severe desertification and soil erosion;  
(6) Key shelterbelt forest belts along the coast;  
(7) Undeveloped primeval forest areas;  
(8) Other areas that need to be designated.  
Where public welfare forest designation involves non-State-owned forestlands, a written agreement shall be signed with the right holder, and reasonable compensation shall be given.  
Where public welfare forests are adjusted, the consent of the original designation authority shall be obtained, and the adjustment shall be announced.  
Measures for the designation and management of national-level public welfare forests shall be formulated by the State Council; measures for the designation and management of local-level public welfare forests shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家对公益林实施严格保护。  
县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当有计划地组织公益林经营者对公益林中生态功能低下的疏林、残次林等低质低效林，采取林分改造、森林抚育等措施，提高公益林的质量和生态保护功能。  
在符合公益林生态区位保护要求和不影响公益林生态功能的前提下，经科学论证，可以合理利用公益林林地资源和森林景观资源，适度开展林下经济、森林旅游等。利用公益林开展上述活动应当严格遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 49** The State shall implement strict protection over public welfare forests.  
The competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in a planned manner, organize operators of public welfare forests to take measures such as stand improvement and forest tending for low-quality and low-efficiency forests, such as open forests and degraded forests with low ecological functions in public welfare forests, so as to improve the quality and ecological protection function of public welfare forests.  
On the premise of meeting the protection requirements of the ecological location of public welfare forests and without affecting the ecological functions of public welfare forests, and after scientific demonstration, the forestland resources and forest landscape resources of public welfare forests may be reasonably utilized to moderately develop under-forest economy, forest tourism, etc. The utilization of public welfare forests for the aforementioned activities shall strictly comply with relevant national regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家鼓励发展下列商品林：  
（一）以生产木材为主要目的的森林；  
（二）以生产果品、油料、饮料、调料、工业原料和药材等林产品为主要目的的森林；  
（三）以生产燃料和其他生物质能源为主要目的的森林；  
（四）其他以发挥经济效益为主要目的的森林。  
在保障生态安全的前提下，国家鼓励建设速生丰产、珍贵树种和大径级用材林，增加林木储备，保障木材供给安全。

**Article 50** The State shall encourage the development of the following commercial forests:  
(1) Forests mainly for timber production;  
(2) Forests mainly for the production of forest products such as fruits, oilseeds, beverages, spices, industrial raw materials, and medicinal materials;  
(3) Forests mainly for the production of fuel and other biomass energy;  
(4) Other forests mainly for economic benefits.  
Under the premise of ensuring ecological security, the State shall encourage the construction of fast-growing and high-yielding forests, precious tree species forests, and large-diameter timber forests to increase forest stock and ensure the security of timber supply.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　商品林由林业经营者依法自主经营。在不破坏生态的前提下，可以采取集约化经营措施，合理利用森林、林木、林地，提高商品林经济效益。

**Article 51** Commercial forests shall be independently operated by forestry operators in accordance with law. On the premise of not damaging the ecology, intensive management measures may be adopted to reasonably utilize forests, trees, and forestlands, and to improve the economic benefits of commercial forests.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在林地上修筑下列直接为林业生产经营服务的工程设施，符合国家有关部门规定的标准的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门批准，不需要办理建设用地审批手续；超出标准需要占用林地的，应当依法办理建设用地审批手续：  
（一）培育、生产种子、苗木的设施；  
（二）贮存种子、苗木、木材的设施；  
（三）集材道、运材道、防火巡护道、森林步道；  
（四）林业科研、科普教育设施；  
（五）野生动植物保护、护林、林业有害生物防治、森林防火、木材检疫的设施；  
（六）供水、供电、供热、供气、通讯基础设施；  
（七）其他直接为林业生产服务的工程设施。

**Article 52** The construction of the following engineering facilities directly serving forestry production and operation on forestlands that meet the standards prescribed by relevant State departments shall be approved by the competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and no formalities for approval of land use for construction shall be required; where it exceeds the standards and requires occupation of forestlands, formalities for approval of land use for construction shall be handled in accordance with law:  
(1) Facilities for cultivating and producing seeds and seedlings;  
(2) Facilities for storing seeds, seedlings, and timber;  
(3) Skid trails, logging roads, fire patrol roads, and forest trails;  
(4) Facilities for forestry scientific research and science popularization education;  
(5) Facilities for wildlife protection, forest protection, forestry pest control, forest fire prevention, and timber quarantine;  
(6) Infrastructure for water supply, power supply, heating, gas supply, and communications;  
(7) Other engineering facilities directly serving forestry production.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国有林业企业事业单位应当编制森林经营方案，明确森林培育和管护的经营措施，报县级以上人民政府林业主管部门批准后实施。重点林区的森林经营方案由国务院林业主管部门批准后实施。  
国家支持、引导其他林业经营者编制森林经营方案。  
编制森林经营方案的具体办法由国务院林业主管部门制定。

**Article 53** State-owned forestry enterprises and institutions shall formulate forest management plans, specify management measures for forest cultivation and management and protection, and submit them to the competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level for approval before implementation. Forest management plans for key forest regions shall be approved by the competent forestry department of the State Council before implementation.  
The State shall support and guide other forestry operators in formulating forest management plans.  
Specific measures for formulating forest management plans shall be formulated by the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家严格控制森林年采伐量。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门根据消耗量低于生长量和森林分类经营管理的原则，编制本行政区域的年采伐限额，经征求国务院林业主管部门意见，报本级人民政府批准后公布实施，并报国务院备案。重点林区的年采伐限额，由国务院林业主管部门编制，报国务院批准后公布实施。

**Article 54** The State shall strictly control the annual volume of forest felling. The competent forestry departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the principle that consumption is less than growth and forest classification management, prepare the annual felling quotas for their respective administrative regions, and after soliciting the opinions of the competent forestry department of the State Council, submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval before announcement and implementation, and report them to the State Council for recordation. The annual felling quotas for key forest regions shall be prepared by the competent forestry department of the State Council and submitted to the State Council for approval before announcement and implementation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　采伐森林、林木应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）公益林只能进行抚育、更新和低质低效林改造性质的采伐。但是，因科研或者实验、防治林业有害生物、建设护林防火设施、营造生物防火隔离带、遭受自然灾害等需要采伐的除外。  
（二）商品林应当根据不同情况，采取不同采伐方式，严格控制皆伐面积，伐育同步规划实施。  
（三）自然保护区的林木，禁止采伐。但是，因防治林业有害生物、森林防火、维护主要保护对象生存环境、遭受自然灾害等特殊情况必须采伐的和实验区的竹林除外。  
省级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当根据前款规定，按照森林分类经营管理、保护优先、注重效率和效益等原则，制定相应的林木采伐技术规程。

**Article 55** Felling of forests and trees shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) For public welfare forests, only fellings of the nature of tending, regeneration, and improvement of low-quality and low-efficiency forests shall be allowed. However, fellings required for scientific research or experiment, prevention and control of forestry pests, construction of forest protection and fire prevention facilities, creation of biological firebreaks, or suffering from natural disasters shall be excepted.  
(2) For commercial forests, different felling methods shall be adopted according to different circumstances, the area of clear-cutting shall be strictly controlled, and felling and regeneration shall be planned and implemented simultaneously.  
(3) Felling of trees in nature reserves shall be prohibited. However, exceptions shall be made for bamboo forests in experimental areas and in special circumstances where felling is necessary for prevention and control of forestry pests, forest fire prevention, maintenance of the living environment of the main protected objects, or suffering from natural disasters.  
The competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with the preceding paragraph and based on the principles of forest classification management, protection priority, emphasis on efficiency and benefits, formulate corresponding technical regulations for tree felling.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　采伐林地上的林木应当申请采伐许可证，并按照采伐许可证的规定进行采伐；采伐自然保护区以外的竹林，不需要申请采伐许可证，但应当符合林木采伐技术规程。  
农村居民采伐自留地和房前屋后个人所有的零星林木，不需要申请采伐许可证。  
非林地上的农田防护林、防风固沙林、护路林、护岸护堤林和城镇林木等的更新采伐，由有关主管部门按照有关规定管理。  
采挖移植林木按照采伐林木管理。具体办法由国务院林业主管部门制定。  
禁止伪造、变造、买卖、租借采伐许可证。

**Article 56** Felling of trees on forestlands shall require an application for a felling permit, and felling shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the felling permit; for felling of bamboo forests outside nature reserves, no application for a felling permit is required, but the technical regulations for tree felling shall be complied with.  
Rural residents felling scattered trees individually owned on their private plots and around their houses and courtyards need not apply for a felling permit.  
Regeneration felling of farmland shelterbelts, wind and sand fixation shelterbelts, roadside shelterbelts, bank and dike protection shelterbelts, and urban trees on non-forestlands shall be managed by the relevant competent departments in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Digging and transplanting of trees shall be managed as felling of trees. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent forestry department of the State Council.  
Forgery, alteration, sale, or leasing of felling permits shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　采伐许可证由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门核发。  
县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当采取措施，方便申请人办理采伐许可证。  
农村居民采伐自留山和个人承包集体林地上的林木，由县级人民政府林业主管部门或者其委托的乡镇人民政府核发采伐许可证。

**Article 57** Felling permits shall be issued by the competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level.  
The competent forestry departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to facilitate applicants in handling felling permits.  
For felling of trees on private hills and collectively-owned forestlands contracted by individuals by rural residents, felling permits shall be issued by the competent forestry departments of the people's governments at the county level or the township people's governments authorized by them.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　申请采伐许可证，应当提交有关采伐的地点、林种、树种、面积、蓄积、方式、更新措施和林木权属等内容的材料。超过省级以上人民政府林业主管部门规定面积或者蓄积量的，还应当提交伐区调查设计材料。

**Article 58** To apply for a felling permit, materials concerning the location, forest category, tree species, area, volume, method, regeneration measures, and ownership of the trees to be felled shall be submitted. Where the area or volume exceeds the amount prescribed by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, materials for the survey and design of the felling area shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　符合林木采伐技术规程的，审核发放采伐许可证的部门应当及时核发采伐许可证。但是，审核发放采伐许可证的部门不得超过年采伐限额发放采伐许可证。

**Article 59** If the technical regulations for tree felling are complied with, the department responsible for examining and issuing felling permits shall promptly issue the felling permit. However, the department responsible for examining and issuing felling permits shall not issue felling permits in excess of the annual felling quota.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列情形之一的，不得核发采伐许可证：  
（一）采伐封山育林期、封山育林区内的林木；  
（二）上年度采伐后未按照规定完成更新造林任务；  
（三）上年度发生重大滥伐案件、森林火灾或者林业有害生物灾害，未采取预防和改进措施；  
（四）法律法规和国务院林业主管部门规定的禁止采伐的其他情形。

**Article 60** A felling permit shall not be issued under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Felling of trees in the period or area of closed hillsides for afforestation;  
(2) Failure to complete the task of regeneration afforestation in accordance with regulations after felling in the previous year;  
(3) The occurrence of major illegal felling cases, forest fires, or forestry pest disasters in the previous year without taking preventive and improvement measures;  
(4) Other circumstances where felling is prohibited as prescribed by laws, regulations, and the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　采伐林木的组织和个人应当按照有关规定完成更新造林。更新造林的面积不得少于采伐的面积，更新造林应当达到相关技术规程规定的标准。

**Article 61** Organizations and individuals that fell trees shall complete reforestation in accordance with relevant regulations. The area of reforestation shall not be less than the area of felling, and reforestation shall meet the standards prescribed by relevant technical regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家通过贴息、林权收储担保补助等措施，鼓励和引导金融机构开展涉林抵押贷款、林农信用贷款等符合林业特点的信贷业务，扶持林权收储机构进行市场化收储担保。

**Article 62** The State shall, through measures such as interest subsidies, forest right acquisition and storage guarantee subsidies, encourage and guide financial institutions to carry out credit business that meets the characteristics of forestry, such as forest-related mortgage loans and forest farmer credit loans, and support forest right acquisition and storage institutions in carrying out market-based acquisition and storage guarantees.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家支持发展森林保险。县级以上人民政府依法对森林保险提供保险费补贴。

**Article 63** The State shall support the development of forest insurance. The people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with law, provide premium subsidies for forest insurance.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　林业经营者可以自愿申请森林认证，促进森林经营水平提高和可持续经营。

**Article 64** Forest operators may voluntarily apply for forest certification to promote the improvement of forest management levels and sustainable management.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　木材经营加工企业应当建立原料和产品出入库台账。任何单位和个人不得收购、加工、运输明知是盗伐、滥伐等非法来源的林木。

**Article 65** Timber operation and processing enterprises shall establish ledgers for the inbound and outbound of raw materials and products. No unit or individual may purchase, process, or transport trees that they know are from illegal sources such as illegal felling or excessive felling.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门依照本法规定，对森林资源的保护、修复、利用、更新等进行监督检查，依法查处破坏森林资源等违法行为。

**Article 66** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law, conduct supervision and inspection on the protection, restoration, utilization, and regeneration of forest resources, and investigate and punish illegal acts such as damaging forest resources in accordance with law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门履行森林资源保护监督检查职责，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生产经营场所进行现场检查；  
（二）查阅、复制有关文件、资料，对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存；  
（三）查封、扣押有证据证明来源非法的林木以及从事破坏森林资源活动的工具、设备或者财物；  
（四）查封与破坏森林资源活动有关的场所。  
省级以上人民政府林业主管部门对森林资源保护发展工作不力、问题突出、群众反映强烈的地区，可以约谈所在地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门主要负责人，要求其采取措施及时整改。约谈整改情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 67** When performing the duty of supervising and inspecting the protection of forest resources, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Entering the premises for production and operation to conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) Consulting and copying relevant documents and materials, and sealing up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, concealed, or altered;  
(3) Sealing or seizing trees that are proven to be from illegal sources, as well as tools, equipment, or property used in activities that damage forest resources;  
(4) Sealing the places related to activities that damage forest resources.  
Where an area has poor performance in forest resource protection and development, prominent problems, or strong public complaints, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the provincial level may summon the principal persons in charge of the local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments in that area, requiring them to take measures for timely rectification. The rectification status of such summons shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　破坏森林资源造成生态环境损害的，县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门、林业主管部门可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼，对侵权人提出损害赔偿要求。

**Article 68** Where damage to forest resources causes ecological environmental damage, the competent department of natural resources and the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level may, in accordance with law, initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court and claim damages against the tortfeasor.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　审计机关按照国家有关规定对国有森林资源资产进行审计监督。

**Article 69** Audit authorities shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, conduct audit supervision over state-owned forest resource assets.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门或者其他有关国家机关未依照本法规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
依照本法规定应当作出行政处罚决定而未作出的，上级主管部门有权责令下级主管部门作出行政处罚决定或者直接给予行政处罚。

**Article 70** Where the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant state organs fail to perform their duties in accordance with this Law, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.  
Where an administrative penalty decision should be made in accordance with this Law but is not made, the competent department at the higher level shall have the authority to order the competent department at the lower level to make an administrative penalty decision or directly impose an administrative penalty.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，侵害森林、林木、林地的所有者或者使用者的合法权益的，依法承担侵权责任。

**Article 71** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the owners or users of forests, trees, or forestland shall bear tort liability in accordance with law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本法规定，国有林业企业事业单位未履行保护培育森林资源义务、未编制森林经营方案或者未按照批准的森林经营方案开展森林经营活动的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of this Law, a state-owned forestry enterprise or institution fails to fulfill its obligation to protect and cultivate forest resources, fails to prepare a forest management plan, or fails to carry out forest management activities in accordance with the approved forest management plan, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose sanctions on the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons in accordance with law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，未经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门审核同意，擅自改变林地用途的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期恢复植被和林业生产条件，可以处恢复植被和林业生产条件所需费用三倍以下的罚款。  
虽经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门审核同意，但未办理建设用地审批手续擅自占用林地的，依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》的有关规定处罚。  
在临时使用的林地上修建永久性建筑物，或者临时使用林地期满后一年内未恢复植被或者林业生产条件的，依照本条第一款规定处罚。

**Article 73** Where, in violation of this Law, without the examination and approval of the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level, the purpose of forestland is changed without authorization, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the restoration of vegetation and forest production conditions within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than three times the cost required for restoring vegetation and forest production conditions.  
Where, although the examination and approval of the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level have been obtained, forestland is occupied without authorization without going through the procedures for approval of construction land use, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where permanent buildings are constructed on temporarily used forestland, or within one year after the expiration of the term of temporary use of forestland, vegetation or forest production conditions have not been restored, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本法规定，进行开垦、采石、采砂、采土或者其他活动，造成林木毁坏的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期在原地或者异地补种毁坏株数一倍以上三倍以下的树木，可以处毁坏林木价值五倍以下的罚款；造成林地毁坏的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复植被和林业生产条件，可以处恢复植被和林业生产条件所需费用三倍以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，在幼林地砍柴、毁苗、放牧造成林木毁坏的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期在原地或者异地补种毁坏株数一倍以上三倍以下的树木。  
向林地排放重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污水、污泥，以及可能造成林地污染的清淤底泥、尾矿、矿渣等的，依照《中华人民共和国土壤污染防治法》的有关规定处罚。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of this Law, by carrying out activities such as reclamation, quarrying, sand extraction, soil extraction, or other activities, trees are damaged, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, replant trees of not less than one time but not more than three times the number of trees damaged on the original site or at another site within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than five times the value of the damaged trees; where forestland is damaged, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restore vegetation and forest production conditions within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than three times the cost required for restoring vegetation and forest production conditions.  
Where, in violation of this Law, by gathering firewood, destroying seedlings, or grazing in young forestland, trees are damaged, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and replant trees of not less than one time but not more than three times the number of trees damaged on the original site or at another site within a specified time limit.  
Where sewage, sludge, or other substances such as dredged sediment, tailings, or slag that contain heavy metals or other toxic and hazardous substances exceeding the standard, or that may cause pollution to forestland, are discharged onto forestland, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Soil Pollution.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，擅自移动或者毁坏森林保护标志的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门恢复森林保护标志，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 75** Where, in violation of this Law, a forest protection sign is moved or damaged without authorization, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall restore the forest protection sign, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　盗伐林木的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期在原地或者异地补种盗伐株数一倍以上五倍以下的树木，并处盗伐林木价值五倍以上十倍以下的罚款。  
滥伐林木的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期在原地或者异地补种滥伐株数一倍以上三倍以下的树木，可以处滥伐林木价值三倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 76** Whoever illegally fells trees shall be ordered by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level to replant trees of not less than one time but not more than five times the number of trees illegally felled on the original site or at another site within a specified time limit, and shall be concurrently fined not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the illegally felled trees.  
Whoever excessively fells trees shall be ordered by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level to replant trees of not less than one time but not more than three times the number of trees excessively felled on the original site or at another site within a specified time limit, and may be fined not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the excessively felled trees.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，伪造、变造、买卖、租借采伐许可证的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收证件和违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以处二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of this Law, anyone forges, alters, buys, sells, or lends a felling permit, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the permit and the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，收购、加工、运输明知是盗伐、滥伐等非法来源的林木的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法收购、加工、运输的林木或者变卖所得，可以处违法收购、加工、运输林木价款三倍以下的罚款。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of this Law, anyone purchases, processes, or transports trees that they know are from illegal sources such as illegal felling or excessive felling, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally purchased, processed, or transported trees or the proceeds from their sale, and may impose a fine of not more than three times the price of the illegally purchased, processed, or transported trees.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，未完成更新造林任务的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期完成；逾期未完成的，可以处未完成造林任务所需费用二倍以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 79** Where, in violation of this Law, the reforestation task is not completed, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order its completion within a specified time limit; if it remains uncompleted upon expiry of the time limit, a fine of not more than two times the cost required for the uncompleted reforestation task may be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，拒绝、阻碍县级以上人民政府林业主管部门依法实施监督检查的，可以处五万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，可以责令停产停业整顿。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of this Law, anyone refuses or obstructs the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level from lawfully carrying out supervision and inspection, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the violator may be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门依法组织代为履行，代为履行所需费用由违法者承担：  
（一）拒不恢复植被和林业生产条件，或者恢复植被和林业生产条件不符合国家有关规定；  
（二）拒不补种树木，或者补种不符合国家有关规定。  
恢复植被和林业生产条件、树木补种的标准，由省级以上人民政府林业主管部门制定。

**Article 81** Where one of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, organize substitute performance, and the expenses required for substitute performance shall be borne by the violator:  
(1) Refuses to restore vegetation and forestry production conditions, or the restoration of vegetation and forestry production conditions does not meet the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) Refuses to replant trees, or the replanting does not meet the relevant provisions of the State.  
The standards for restoring vegetation and forestry production conditions and replanting trees shall be formulated by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　公安机关按照国家有关规定，可以依法行使[本法第七十四条](#art-74)第一款、第七十六条、第七十七条、第七十八条规定的行政处罚权。  
违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** The public security organs may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, exercise according to law the administrative penalty powers provided for in Article 74, paragraph 1, Articles 76, 77, and 78 of this Law.  
If a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
（一）森林，包括乔木林、竹林和国家特别规定的灌木林。按照用途可以分为防护林、特种用途林、用材林、经济林和能源林。  
（二）林木，包括树木和竹子。  
（三）林地，是指县级以上人民政府规划确定的用于发展林业的土地。包括郁闭度0.2以上的乔木林地以及竹林地、灌木林地、疏林地、采伐迹地、火烧迹地、未成林造林地、苗圃地等。

**Article 83** For the purposes of this Law, the meanings of the following terms are:  
(1) Forests include arbor forests, bamboo forests, and shrub forests specially provided for by the State. According to their uses, they may be classified into shelter forests, forests for special purposes, timber forests, economic forests, and energy forests.  
(2) Forest trees include trees and bamboos.  
(3) Forestland means land that is planned and determined by the people's government at or above the county level for the development of forestry. It includes arbor forestland with a canopy density of 0.2 or above, as well as bamboo forestland, shrub forestland, sparse forestland, cutover land, burned land, land for immature afforestation, and nursery land, etc.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本法自2020年7月1日起施行。

**Article 84** This Law shall come into force on July 1, 2020.
