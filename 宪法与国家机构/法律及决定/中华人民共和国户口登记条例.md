# 中华人民共和国户口登记条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1958-01-09  
**生效日期**：1958-01-09

> （1958年1月9日全国人民代表大会常务委员会第九十一次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维持社会秩序，保护公民的权利和利益，服务于社会主义建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of maintaining social order, protecting the rights and interests of citizens, and serving socialist construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国公民，都应当依照本条例的规定履行户口登记。  
现役军人的户口登记，由军事机关按照管理现役军人的有关规定办理。  
居留在中华人民共和国境内的外国人和无国籍的人的户口登记，除法令另有规定外，适用本条例。

**Article 2** All citizens of the People's Republic of China shall register their household registration in accordance with these Regulations.  
The household registration of active duty military personnel shall be handled by the military authorities in accordance with the relevant provisions on the administration of active duty military personnel.  
The household registration of aliens and stateless persons residing in the People's Republic of China shall be governed by these Regulations, unless otherwise provided by laws or decrees.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　户口登记工作，由各级公安机关主管。  
城市和设有公安派出所的镇，以公安派出所管辖区为户口管辖区；乡和不设公安派出所的镇，以乡、镇管辖区为户口管辖区。乡、镇人民委员会和公安派出所为户口登记机关。  
居住在机关、团体、学校、企业、事业等单位内部和公共宿舍的户口，由各单位指定专人，协助户口登记机关办理户口登记；分散居住的户口，由户口登记机关直接办理户口登记。  
居住在军事机关和军人宿舍的非现役军人的户口，由各单位指定专人，协助户口登记机关办理户口登记。  
农业、渔业、盐业、林业、牧畜业、手工业等生产合作社的户口，由合作社指定专人，协助户口登记机关办理户口登记。合作社以外的户口，由户口登记机关直接办理户口登记。

**Article 3** The work of household registration shall be under the charge of the public security organs at all levels. In cities and towns where a public security police station is established, the jurisdictional area of the public security police station shall be the household registration area; in townships and towns where no public security police station is established, the jurisdictional area of the township or town shall be the household registration area. The township or town people's committee and the public security police station shall be the household registration authorities. For the household registration of persons residing within the premises of state organs, organizations, schools, enterprises, institutions, and other entities, as well as public dormitories, each entity shall designate a specific person to assist the household registration authority in handling the household registration. For scattered households, the household registration authority shall handle the household registration directly. For the household registration of non-active duty military personnel residing in military organs or military dormitories, each entity shall designate a specific person to assist the household registration authority in handling the household registration. For the household registration of members of production cooperatives such as agricultural, fishery, salt industry, forestry, animal husbandry, and handicraft cooperatives, the cooperative shall designate a specific person to assist the household registration authority in handling the household registration. For households not belonging to cooperatives, the household registration authority shall handle the household registration directly.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　户口登记机关应当设立户口登记簿。  
城市、水上和设有公安派出所的镇，应当每户发给一本户口簿。  
农村以合作社为单位发给户口簿；合作社以外的户口不发给户口簿。  
户口登记簿和户口簿登记的事项，具有证明公民身份的效力。

**Article 4** The household registration authority shall establish a household registration ledger.  
In cities, on water, and in towns where a public security police station is established, one household register shall be issued to each household.  
In rural areas, household registers shall be issued on a cooperative basis; households not belonging to cooperatives shall not be issued a household register.  
The entries in the household registration ledger and the household register shall have the effect of certifying the citizenship status of a citizen.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　户口登记以户为单位。同主管人共同居住一处的立为一户，以主管人为户主。单身居住的自立一户，以本人为户主。居住在机关、团体、学校、企业、事业等单位内部和公共宿舍的户口共立一户或者分别立户。户主负责按照本条例的规定申报户口登记。

**Article 5** Household registration shall be based on the household unit. Persons living together with the head of a household in one place shall be registered as one household, with the head of the household as the householder. A person living alone shall establish an independent household, with that person as the householder. Households of persons residing within the premises of state organs, organizations, schools, enterprises, institutions, and public dormitories may be registered as one household or as separate households. The householder shall be responsible for reporting household registration in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公民应当在经常居住的地方登记为常住人口，一个公民只能在一个地方登记为常住人口。

**Article 6** A citizen shall register as a permanent resident in the place where he or she habitually resides, and a citizen may only register as a permanent resident in one place.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　婴儿出生后一个月以内，由户主、亲属、抚养人或者邻居向婴儿常住地户口登记机关申报出生登记。  
弃婴，由收养人或者育婴机关向户口登记机关申报出生登记。

**Article 7** Within one month after the birth of an infant, the householder, a relative, a guardian, or a neighbor shall report the birth registration to the household registration authority of the infant's place of permanent residence.  
For an abandoned infant, the adopter or the infant care institution shall report the birth registration to the household registration authority.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　公民死亡，城市在葬前，农村在一个月以内，由户主、亲属、抚养人或者邻居向户口登记机关申报死亡登记，注销户口。公民如果在暂住地死亡，由暂住地户口登记机关通知常住地户口登记机关注销户口。  
公民因意外事故致死或者死因不明，户主、发现人应当立即报告当地公安派出所或者乡、镇人民委员会。

**Article 8** Upon the death of a citizen, the householder, a relative, a guardian, or a neighbor shall report the death registration to the household registration authority and cancel the household registration. In cities, this shall be done before burial; in rural areas, within one month. If a citizen dies in a place of temporary residence, the household registration authority of the place of temporary residence shall notify the household registration authority of the place of permanent residence to cancel the household registration. If a citizen dies due to an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the householder or the person who discovered the deceased shall immediately report to the local public security police station or the township or town people's committee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　婴儿出生后，在申报出生登记前死亡的，应当同时申报出生、死亡两项登记。

**Article 9** If an infant dies after birth but before the birth registration is reported, both the birth registration and the death registration shall be reported simultaneously.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公民迁出本户口管辖区，由本人或者户主在迁出前向户口登记机关申报迁出登记，领取迁移证件，注销户口。  
公民由农村迁往城市，必须持有城市劳动部门的录用证明，学校的录取证明，或者城市户口登记机关的准予迁入的证明，向常住地户口登记机关申请办理迁出手续。  
公民迁往边防地区，必须经过常住地县、市、市辖区公安机关批准。

**Article 10** When a citizen moves out of the household registration area, the citizen or the householder shall, before the move, report the out-migration registration to the household registration authority, obtain the migration certificate, and cancel the household registration.  
When a citizen moves from a rural area to a city, he or she must hold the employment certification from the city's labor department, the admission certification from a school, or the certification of permission for in-migration from the city's household registration authority, and apply to the household registration authority of the place of permanent residence for going through the out-migration formalities.  
When a citizen moves to a border area, the move must be approved by the public security organ of the county, city, or municipal district of the citizen's place of permanent residence.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被征集服现役的公民，在入伍前，由本人或者户主持应征公民入伍通知书向常住地户口登记机关申报迁出登记，注销户口，不发迁移证件。

**Article 11** For a citizen conscripted for active service, before enlistment, the citizen or the householder shall, by presenting the enlistment notice for the conscripted citizen, report the out-migration registration to the household registration authority of the place of permanent residence, cancel the household registration, and no migration certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被逮捕的人犯，由逮捕机关在通知人犯家属的同时，通知人犯常住地户口登记机关注销户口。

**Article 12** For an offender who has been arrested, the arresting organ shall, at the same time as notifying the offender's family members, notify the household registration authority of the offender's place of permanent residence to cancel the household registration.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公民迁移，从到达迁入地的时候起，城市在三日以内，农村在十日以内，由本人或者户主持迁移证件向户口登记机关申报迁入登记，缴销迁移证件。  
没有迁移证件的公民，凭下列证件到迁入地的户口登记机关申报迁入登记：  
一、复员、转业和退伍的军人，凭县、市兵役机关或者团以上军事机关发给的证件；  
二、从国外回来的华侨和留学生，凭中华人民共和国护照或者入境证件；  
三、被人民法院、人民检察院或者公安机关释放的人，凭释放机关发给的证件。

**Article 13** When a citizen migrates, within three days after arrival at the place of in-migration in cities, or within ten days in rural areas, the citizen or the householder shall, by presenting the migration certificate, report the in-migration registration to the household registration authority and surrender the migration certificate.  
A citizen without a migration certificate may report the in-migration registration to the household registration authority of the place of in-migration by presenting one of the following certificates: 1.  
For demobilized, transferred to civilian work, or discharged military personnel, presenting the certificate issued by the county or city military service organ or a military organ at or above the regiment level; 2.  
For overseas Chinese and returned students returning from abroad, presenting the passport of the People's Republic of China or an entry certificate; 3.  
For persons released by a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, or a public security organ, presenting the certificate issued by the releasing organ.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被假释、缓刑的犯人，被管制分子和其他依法被剥夺政治权利的人，在迁移的时候，必须经过户口登记机关转报县、市、市辖区人民法院或者公安机关批准，才可以办理迁出登记；到达迁入地后，应当立即向户口登记机关申报迁入登记。

**Article 14** Convicted persons on parole or probation, persons under public surveillance, and other persons deprived of political rights according to law must, when migrating, have the matter forwarded by the household registration authority to the People's Court of the county, city, or municipal district, or the public security organ for approval before the out-migration registration may be processed. After arriving at the place of in-migration, they shall immediately report the in-migration registration to the household registration authority.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公民在常住地市、县范围以外的城市暂住三日以上的，由暂住地的户主或者本人在三日以内向户口登记机关申报暂住登记，离开前申报注销；暂住在旅店的，由旅店设置旅客登记簿随时登记。  
公民在常住地市、县范围以内暂住，或者在常住地市、县范围以外的农村暂住，除暂住在旅店的由旅店设置旅客登记簿随时登记以外，不办理暂住登记。

**Article 15** When a citizen stays temporarily for more than three days in a city outside the scope of the city or county of permanent residence, the householder of the place of temporary stay or the citizen himself or herself shall, within three days, report the temporary stay registration to the household registration authority, and report the cancellation before leaving. If staying temporarily in a hotel, the hotel shall maintain a guest register and make entries promptly. When a citizen stays temporarily within the scope of the city or county of permanent residence, or stays temporarily in a rural area outside the scope of the city or county of permanent residence, no temporary stay registration shall be processed, except that if staying temporarily in a hotel, the hotel shall maintain a guest register and make entries promptly.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公民因私事离开常住地外出、暂住的时间超过三个月的，应当向户口登记机关申请延长时间或者办理迁移手续；既无理由延长时间又无迁移条件的，应当返回常住地。

**Article 16** If a citizen leaves his or her place of permanent residence for personal reasons and the period of stay outside temporarily exceeds three months, the citizen shall apply to the household registration authority for an extension of time or go through the migration formalities. If there are no grounds for an extension of time and no conditions for migration, the citizen shall return to the place of permanent residence.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　户口登记的内容需要变更或者更正的时候，由户主或者本人向户口登记机关申报；户口登记机关审查属实后予以变更或者更正。  
户口登记机关认为必要的时候，可以向申请人索取有关变更或者更正的证明。

**Article 17** When the content of a household registration needs to be modified or corrected, the householder or the citizen himself or herself shall report to the household registration authority. After verification and confirmation of the facts, the household registration authority shall make the modification or correction. When the household registration authority deems it necessary, it may request the applicant to provide relevant proof for the modification or correction.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公民变更姓名，依照下列规定办理：  
一、未满十八周岁的人需要变更姓名的时候，由本人或者父母、收养人向户口登记机关申请变更登记；  
二、十八周岁以上的人需要变更姓名的时候，由本人向户口登记机关申请变更登记。

**Article 18** A change of name by a citizen shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions: 1.  
When a person under the age of 18 needs to change his or her name, the person himself or herself, or his or her parents or adoptive parents, shall apply to the household registration authority for a change of registration; 2.  
When a person aged 18 or above needs to change his or her name, the person himself or herself shall apply to the household registration authority for a change of registration.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公民因结婚、离婚、收养、认领、分户、并户、失踪、寻回或者其他事由引起户口变动的时候，由户主或者本人向户口登记机关申报变更登记。

**Article 19** When a citizen's household registration changes due to marriage, divorce, adoption, recognition, separation of households, consolidation of households, disappearance, recovery of a missing person, or other reasons, the householder or the citizen himself or herself shall report the change of registration to the household registration authority.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，根据情节轻重，依法给予治安管理处罚或者追究刑事责任：  
一、不按照本条例的规定申报户口的；  
二、假报户口的；  
三、伪造、涂改、转让、出借、出卖户口证件的；  
四、冒名顶替他人户口的；  
五、旅店管理人不按照规定办理旅客登记的。

**Article 20** Anyone who commits any of the following acts shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, be subjected to a public security administrative penalty or have criminal liability investigated according to law: 1.  
Failing to report household registration in accordance with these Regulations; 2.  
Falsely reporting household registration; 3.  
Forging, altering, transferring, lending, or selling household registration certificates; 4.  
Assuming another person's household registration under a false name; 5.  
The manager of a hotel failing to handle guest registration in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　户口登记机关在户口登记工作中，如果发现有反革命分子和其他犯罪分子，应当提请司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** If a household registration authority discovers counterrevolutionaries or other criminal offenders during the household registration process, it shall refer the matter to the judicial authority for investigating criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　户口簿、册、表格、证件，由中华人民共和国公安部统一制定式样，由省、自治区、直辖市公安机关统筹印制。  
公民领取户口簿和迁移证应当缴纳工本费。

**Article 22** The formats of household registers, books, forms, and certificates shall be uniformly prescribed by the Ministry of Public Security of the People's Republic of China, and shall be uniformly printed by the public security organs of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
A citizen shall pay the production cost for obtaining a household register or a migration certificate.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民族自治地方的自治机关可以根据本条例的精神，结合当地具体情况，制定单行办法。

**Article 23** The autonomous organs of national autonomous areas may formulate separate measures in accordance with the spirit of these Regulations and in light of the local specific circumstances.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
