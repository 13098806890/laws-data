# 中华人民共和国电影产业促进法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2016-11-07  
**生效日期**：2017-03-01

> （2016年11月7日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　电影创作、摄制 第三章　电影发行、放映 第四章　电影产业支持、保障 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了促进电影产业健康繁荣发展，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，规范电影市场秩序，丰富人民群众精神文化生活，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事电影创作、摄制、发行、放映等活动（以下统称电影活动），适用本法。 本法所称电影，是指运用视听技术和艺术手段摄制、以胶片或者数字载体记录、由表达一定内容的有声或者无声的连续画面组成、符合国家规定的技术标准、用于电影院等固定放映场所或者流动放映设备公开放映的作品。 通过互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络传播电影的，还应当遵守互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络管理的法律、行政法规的规定。 第三条　从事电影活动，应当坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务，坚持社会效益优先，实现社会效益与经济效益相统一。 第四条　国家坚持以人民为中心的创作导向，坚持百花齐放、百家争鸣的方针，尊重和保障电影创作自由，倡导电影创作贴近实际、贴近生活、贴近群众，鼓励创作思想性、艺术性、观赏性相统一的优秀电影。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进电影产业健康繁荣发展，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，规范电影市场秩序，丰富人民群众精神文化生活，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of promoting the healthy and prosperous development of the film industry, promoting the core socialist values, regulating the order of the film market, and enriching the spiritual and cultural life of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事电影创作、摄制、发行、放映等活动（以下统称电影活动），适用本法。  
本法所称电影，是指运用视听技术和艺术手段摄制、以胶片或者数字载体记录、由表达一定内容的有声或者无声的连续画面组成、符合国家规定的技术标准、用于电影院等固定放映场所或者流动放映设备公开放映的作品。  
通过互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络传播电影的，还应当遵守互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络管理的法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as film creation, production, distribution, and projection (hereinafter collectively referred to as film activities) conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, "film" means a work that is produced by using audio-visual technology and artistic means, recorded on film or digital carrier, composed of continuous sound or silent images expressing certain content, conforming to the technical standards prescribed by the State, and intended for public projection in fixed projection venues such as cinemas or through mobile projection equipment.  
Where a film is disseminated through information networks such as the Internet, telecommunications networks, or radio and television networks, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the administration of such information networks shall also be observed.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事电影活动，应当坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务，坚持社会效益优先，实现社会效益与经济效益相统一。

**Article 3** In conducting film activities, one shall adhere to serving the people and serving socialism, give priority to social benefits, and achieve the unity of social benefits and economic benefits.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家坚持以人民为中心的创作导向，坚持百花齐放、百家争鸣的方针，尊重和保障电影创作自由，倡导电影创作贴近实际、贴近生活、贴近群众，鼓励创作思想性、艺术性、观赏性相统一的优秀电影。

**Article 4** The State adheres to a people-centered creative orientation, upholds the principle of letting a hundred flowers bloom and a hundred schools of thought contend, respects and guarantees the freedom of film creation, advocates that film creation be close to reality, life, and the masses, and encourages the creation of excellent films that integrate ideological content, artistic quality, and观赏性 (viewability).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院应当将电影产业发展纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。县级以上地方人民政府根据当地实际情况将电影产业发展纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国家制定电影及其相关产业政策，引导形成统一开放、公平竞争的电影市场，促进电影市场繁荣发展。

**Article 5** The State Council shall incorporate the development of the film industry into the national economic and social development plan. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on local realities, incorporate the development of the film industry into their respective plans for national economic and social development.  
The State shall formulate industrial policies for film and related industries, guide the formation of a unified, open, and fair competitive film market, and promote the prosperity and development of the film market.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励电影科技的研发、应用，制定并完善电影技术标准，构建以企业为主体、市场为导向、产学研相结合的电影技术创新体系。

**Article 6** The State encourages the research, development, and application of film science and technology, formulates and improves film technical standards, and establishes a film technology innovation system that takes enterprises as the main body, market as the orientation, and integrates production, education, and research.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　与电影有关的知识产权受法律保护，任何组织和个人不得侵犯。  
县级以上人民政府负责知识产权执法的部门应当采取措施，保护与电影有关的知识产权，依法查处侵犯与电影有关的知识产权的行为。  
从事电影活动的公民、法人和其他组织应当增强知识产权意识，提高运用、保护和管理知识产权的能力。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法开发电影形象产品等衍生产品。

**Article 7** Intellectual property rights related to film shall be protected by law, and no organization or individual may infringe upon them.  
The departments responsible for intellectual property law enforcement of the people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to protect intellectual property rights related to film and investigate and punish acts infringing upon intellectual property rights related to film according to law.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations engaged in film activities shall enhance their awareness of intellectual property rights and improve their ability to use, protect, and manage intellectual property rights.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to develop derivative products such as film image products according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院电影主管部门负责全国的电影工作；县级以上地方人民政府电影主管部门负责本行政区域内的电影工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内，负责有关的电影工作。

**Article 8** The film主管部门 (competent department) under the State Council shall be responsible for the film work nationwide; the film主管部门 of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the film work within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for relevant film work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　电影行业组织依法制定行业自律规范，开展业务交流，加强职业道德教育，维护其成员的合法权益。  
演员、导演等电影从业人员应当坚持德艺双馨，遵守法律法规，尊重社会公德，恪守职业道德，加强自律，树立良好社会形象。

**Article 9** Film industry organizations shall formulate industry self-discipline norms according to law, conduct business exchanges, strengthen professional ethics education, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of their members.  
Film practitioners, such as actors and directors, shall uphold both moral integrity and professional excellence, abide by laws and regulations, respect social morality, strictly observe professional ethics, strengthen self-discipline, and establish a good social image.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持建立电影评价体系，鼓励开展电影评论。  
对优秀电影以及为促进电影产业发展作出突出贡献的组织、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** The State supports the establishment of a film evaluation system and encourages the conduct of film criticism.  
Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to excellent films and to the promotion of the film industry shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励开展平等、互利的电影国际合作与交流，支持参加境外电影节（展）。

**Article 11** The State encourages the conduct of equal and mutually beneficial international cooperation and exchange in film, and supports participation in overseas film festivals (exhibitions).

### 第二章　电影创作、摄制

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励电影剧本创作和题材、体裁、形式、手段等创新，鼓励电影学术研讨和业务交流。  
县级以上人民政府电影主管部门根据电影创作的需要，为电影创作人员深入基层、深入群众、体验生活等提供必要的便利和帮助。

**Article 12** The State encourages the creation of film scripts and innovation in themes, genres, forms, and means, and encourages academic discussions and business exchanges in film.  
The film主管部门 of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of film creation, provide necessary convenience and assistance for film creators to go down to the grassroots, mingle with the masses, and experience life.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拟摄制电影的法人、其他组织应当将电影剧本梗概向国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门备案；其中，涉及重大题材或者国家安全、外交、民族、宗教、军事等方面题材的，应当按照国家有关规定将电影剧本报送审查。  
电影剧本梗概或者电影剧本符合[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定的，由国务院电影主管部门将拟摄制电影的基本情况予以公告，并由国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门出具备案证明文件或者颁发批准文件。具体办法由国务院电影主管部门制定。

**Article 13** A legal person or other organization intending to produce a film shall file the film script synopsis with the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; where the film involves major themes or themes concerning national security, foreign affairs, ethnic groups, religion, or military affairs, the film script shall be submitted for examination in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
Where the film script synopsis or film script conforms to the provisions of Article 16 of this Law, the film主管部门 under the State Council shall announce the basic information of the film to be produced, and the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue a filing certificate or an approval document. Specific measures shall be formulated by the film主管部门 under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　法人、其他组织经国务院电影主管部门批准，可以与境外组织合作摄制电影；但是，不得与从事损害我国国家尊严、荣誉和利益，危害社会稳定，伤害民族感情等活动的境外组织合作，也不得聘用有上述行为的个人参加电影摄制。  
合作摄制电影符合创作、出资、收益分配等方面比例要求的，该电影视同境内法人、其他组织摄制的电影。  
境外组织不得在境内独立从事电影摄制活动；境外个人不得在境内从事电影摄制活动。

**Article 14** A legal person or other organization may, with the approval of the film主管部门 under the State Council, co-produce a film with an overseas organization; however, it may not cooperate with an overseas organization that engages in activities harming the dignity, honor, or interests of the State, endangering social stability, or hurting national sentiments, nor may it employ any individual who has engaged in such activities to participate in the film production.  
Where a co-produced film meets the proportional requirements in terms of creation, capital contribution, and distribution of proceeds, the film shall be deemed as a film produced by a domestic legal person or other organization.  
No overseas organization may independently engage in film production activities within the territory of China; no overseas individual may engage in film production activities within the territory of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府电影主管部门应当协调公安、文物保护、风景名胜区管理等部门，为法人、其他组织依照本法从事电影摄制活动提供必要的便利和帮助。  
从事电影摄制活动的，应当遵守有关环境保护、文物保护、风景名胜区管理和安全生产等方面的法律、法规，并在摄制过程中采取必要的保护、防护措施。

**Article 15** The film主管部门 of the people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate with the departments of public security, cultural relics protection, scenic area management, etc., to provide necessary convenience and assistance for legal persons and other organizations to engage in film production activities in accordance with this Law.  
Those engaged in film production activities shall observe laws and regulations on environmental protection, cultural relics protection, scenic area management, and production safety, and take necessary protective and preventative measures during the production process.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电影不得含有下列内容：  
（一）违反宪法确定的基本原则，煽动抗拒或者破坏宪法、法律、行政法规实施；  
（二）危害国家统一、主权和领土完整，泄露国家秘密，危害国家安全，损害国家尊严、荣誉和利益，宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义；  
（三）诋毁民族优秀文化传统，煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，侵害民族风俗习惯，歪曲民族历史或者民族历史人物，伤害民族感情，破坏民族团结；  
（四）煽动破坏国家宗教政策，宣扬邪教、迷信；  
（五）危害社会公德，扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定，宣扬淫秽、赌博、吸毒，渲染暴力、恐怖，教唆犯罪或者传授犯罪方法；  
（六）侵害未成年人合法权益或者损害未成年人身心健康；  
（七）侮辱、诽谤他人或者散布他人隐私，侵害他人合法权益；  
（八）法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容。

**Article 16** A film shall not contain any of the following content:  
(1) Content that violates the basic principles established by the Constitution, incites resistance to or undermines the implementation of the Constitution, laws, or administrative regulations;  
(2) Content that endangers the unity, sovereignty, or territorial integrity of the State, divulges State secrets, endangers national security, harms the dignity, honor, or interests of the State, or promotes terrorism or extremism;  
(3) Content that defames the excellent cultural traditions of ethnic groups, incites ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, infringes upon ethnic customs and habits, distorts ethnic history or ethnic historical figures, hurts national sentiments, or undermines ethnic unity;  
(4) Content that incites the disruption of the State's religious policies or promotes cults or superstitions;  
(5) Content that endangers social morality, disrupts social order, undermines social stability, promotes obscenity, gambling, or drug use, glorifies violence or terror, incites crime, or teaches criminal methods;  
(6) Content that infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of minors or harms the physical or mental health of minors;  
(7) Content that insults or defames others or disseminates the privacy of others, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(8) Other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　法人、其他组织应当将其摄制完成的电影送国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门审查。  
国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门应当自受理申请之日起三十日内作出审查决定。对符合本法规定的，准予公映，颁发电影公映许可证，并予以公布；对不符合本法规定的，不准予公映，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
国务院电影主管部门应当根据本法制定完善电影审查的具体标准和程序，并向社会公布。制定完善电影审查的具体标准应当向社会公开征求意见，并组织专家进行论证。

**Article 17** A legal person or other organization shall submit the completed film to the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination.  
The film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a review decision within thirty days from the date of accepting the application. Where the film complies with the provisions of this Law, it shall be approved for public projection, a Film Public Projection Permit shall be issued, and such approval shall be announced; where the film does not comply with the provisions of this Law, it shall not be approved for public projection, and the applicant shall be notified in writing with the reasons stated.  
The film主管部门 under the State Council shall, based on this Law, formulate and improve specific standards and procedures for film review, and make them public to the society. The formulation and improvement of specific standards for film review shall seek opinions from the public and organize expert论证.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　进行电影审查应当组织不少于五名专家进行评审，由专家提出评审意见。法人、其他组织对专家评审意见有异议的，国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门可以另行组织专家再次评审。专家的评审意见应当作为作出审查决定的重要依据。  
前款规定的评审专家包括专家库中的专家和根据电影题材特别聘请的专家。专家遴选和评审的具体办法由国务院电影主管部门制定。

**Article 18** In conducting film review, no fewer than five experts shall be organized to carry out appraisal, and the experts shall provide appraisal opinions. Where a legal person or other organization has objections to the expert appraisal opinions, the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may organize another group of experts to conduct a second appraisal. The expert appraisal opinions shall serve as an important basis for making the review decision.  
The appraisal experts referred to in the preceding paragraph include experts from the expert pool and experts specially engaged according to the film theme. Specific measures for the selection and appraisal of experts shall be formulated by the film主管部门 under the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　取得电影公映许可证的电影需要变更内容的，应当依照本法规定重新报送审查。

**Article 19** Where the content of a film that has obtained the Film Public Projection Permit needs to be changed, it shall be re-submitted for review in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　摄制电影的法人、其他组织应当将取得的电影公映许可证标识置于电影的片头处；电影放映可能引起未成年人等观众身体或者心理不适的，应当予以提示。  
未取得电影公映许可证的电影，不得发行、放映，不得通过互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络进行传播，不得制作为音像制品；但是，国家另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 20** A legal person or other organization that produces a film shall place the mark of the obtained Film Public Projection Permit at the beginning of the film; where the projection of the film may cause physical or psychological discomfort to audiences such as minors, a warning shall be given.  
A film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit shall not be distributed or projected, shall not be disseminated through information networks such as the Internet, telecommunications networks, or radio and television networks, and shall not be made into audio-visual products; however, where the State provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　摄制完成的电影取得电影公映许可证，方可参加电影节（展）。拟参加境外电影节（展）的，送展法人、其他组织应当在该境外电影节（展）举办前，将相关材料报国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门备案。

**Article 21** A completed film may participate in a film festival (exhibition) only after it has obtained the Film Public Projection Permit. Where a film is to participate in an overseas film festival (exhibition), the legal person or other organization submitting the film shall, before the holding of the overseas film festival (exhibition), report the relevant materials to the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公民、法人和其他组织可以承接境外电影的洗印、加工、后期制作等业务，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门备案，但是不得承接含有损害我国国家尊严、荣誉和利益，危害社会稳定，伤害民族感情等内容的境外电影的相关业务。

**Article 22** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations may undertake businesses such as developing, processing, and post-production of overseas films, and shall report to the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation; however, they may not undertake relevant businesses for overseas films that contain content harming the dignity, honor, or interests of the State, endangering social stability, or hurting national sentiments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家设立的电影档案机构依法接收、收集、整理、保管并向社会开放电影档案。  
国家设立的电影档案机构应当配置必要的设备，采用先进技术，提高电影档案管理现代化水平。  
摄制电影的法人、其他组织依照《中华人民共和国档案法》的规定，做好电影档案保管工作，并向国家设立的电影档案机构移交、捐赠、寄存电影档案。

**Article 23** The film archive institution established by the State shall, according to law, receive, collect, sort out, preserve, and make available to the public film archives.  
The film archive institution established by the State shall equip necessary facilities, adopt advanced technologies, and enhance the modernization level of film archive management.  
Legal persons or other organizations that produce films shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Archives Law of the People's Republic of China, properly preserve film archives, and shall transfer, donate, or deposit film archives with the film archive institution established by the State.

### 第三章　电影发行、放映

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业具有与所从事的电影发行活动相适应的人员、资金条件的，经国务院电影主管部门或者所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门批准，可以从事电影发行活动。  
企业、个体工商户具有与所从事的电影放映活动相适应的人员、场所、技术和设备等条件的，经所在地县级人民政府电影主管部门批准，可以从事电影院等固定放映场所电影放映活动。

**Article 24** An enterprise that has personnel and capital conditions commensurate with the film distribution activities it engages in may, with the approval of the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, engage in film distribution activities.  
An enterprise or individual industrial and commercial household that has personnel, premises, technology, equipment, and other conditions commensurate with the film projection activities it engages in may, with the approval of the film主管部门 of the people's government at the county level where it is located, engage in film projection activities in fixed projection venues such as cinemas.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依照本法规定负责电影发行、放映活动审批的电影主管部门，应当自受理申请之日起三十日内，作出批准或者不批准的决定。对符合条件的，予以批准，颁发电影发行经营许可证或者电影放映经营许可证，并予以公布；对不符合条件的，不予批准，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 25** The film主管部门 responsible for the approval of film distribution and projection activities in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall, within thirty days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove. For those meeting the conditions, approval shall be granted, a Film Distribution Operation Permit or a Film Projection Operation Permit shall be issued, and such approval shall be announced; for those not meeting the conditions, approval shall not be granted, and the applicant shall be notified in writing with the reasons stated.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业、个人从事电影流动放映活动，应当将企业名称或者经营者姓名、地址、联系方式、放映设备等向经营区域所在地县级人民政府电影主管部门备案。

**Article 26** An enterprise or individual engaging in mobile film projection activities shall report the enterprise name or operator's name, address, contact information, projection equipment, etc., to the film主管部门 of the people's government at the county level where the business area is located for recordation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家加大对农村电影放映的扶持力度，由政府出资建立完善农村电影公益放映服务网络，积极引导社会资金投资农村电影放映，不断改善农村地区观看电影条件，统筹保障农村地区群众观看电影需求。  
县级以上人民政府应当将农村电影公益放映纳入农村公共文化服务体系建设，按照国家有关规定对农村电影公益放映活动给予补贴。  
从事农村电影公益放映活动的，不得以虚报、冒领等手段骗取农村电影公益放映补贴资金。

**Article 27** The State shall increase support for film projection in rural areas, the government shall fund the establishment and improvement of a service network for public welfare film projection in rural areas, actively guide social capital to invest in rural film projection, continuously improve the conditions for watching films in rural areas, and ensure the overall needs of the masses in rural areas for watching films.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate public welfare film projection in rural areas into the construction of the rural public cultural service system, and provide subsidies for public welfare film projection activities in rural areas in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
Those engaged in public welfare film projection activities in rural areas shall not fraudulently obtain subsidy funds for such projection through false reports or false claims.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院教育、电影主管部门可以共同推荐有利于未成年人健康成长的电影，并采取措施支持接受义务教育的学生免费观看，由所在学校组织安排。  
国家鼓励电影院以及从事电影流动放映活动的企业、个人采取票价优惠、建设不同条件的放映厅、设立社区放映点等多种措施，为未成年人、老年人、残疾人、城镇低收入居民以及进城务工人员等观看电影提供便利；电影院以及从事电影流动放映活动的企业、个人所在地人民政府可以对其发放奖励性补贴。

**Article 28** The education department and the film主管部门 under the State Council may jointly recommend films that are conducive to the healthy growth of minors, and take measures to support students receiving compulsory education in watching such films free of charge, with arrangements organized by their schools.  
The State encourages cinemas and enterprises or individuals engaged in mobile film projection activities to adopt various measures such as ticket price concessions, building projection halls with different conditions, and setting up community projection points to facilitate film viewing for minors, the elderly, persons with disabilities, low-income urban residents, and migrant workers; the people's government at the location of the cinema or the enterprise or individual engaged in mobile film projection may grant incentive subsidies to them.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　电影院应当合理安排由境内法人、其他组织所摄制电影的放映场次和时段，并且放映的时长不得低于年放映电影时长总和的三分之二。  
电影院以及从事电影流动放映活动的企业、个人应当保障电影放映质量。

**Article 29** Cinemas shall reasonably arrange the number of screening sessions and time slots for films produced by domestic legal persons or other organizations, and the total screening duration of such films shall not be less than two-thirds of the annual total film screening duration.  
Cinemas and enterprises or individuals engaged in mobile film projection activities shall ensure the quality of film projection.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　电影院的设施、设备以及用于流动放映的设备应当符合电影放映技术的国家标准。  
电影院应当按照国家有关规定安装计算机售票系统。

**Article 30** The facilities and equipment of cinemas, as well as equipment used for mobile projection, shall comply with the national standards for film projection technology.  
Cinemas shall install computer ticketing systems in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　未经权利人许可，任何人不得对正在放映的电影进行录音录像。发现进行录音录像的，电影院工作人员有权予以制止，并要求其删除；对拒不听从的，有权要求其离场。

**Article 31** Without the permission of the right holder, no person may make an audio or video recording of a film being projected. Where such recording is found, the staff of the cinema shall have the right to stop it and demand its deletion; if the person refuses to comply, the staff shall have the right to demand that the person leave the venue.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励电影院在向观众明示的电影开始放映时间之前放映公益广告。  
电影院在向观众明示的电影开始放映时间之后至电影放映结束前，不得放映广告。

**Article 32** The State encourages cinemas to screen public service advertisements before the film start time indicated to the audience.  
Cinemas shall not screen any advertisements after the film start time indicated to the audience and before the end of the film screening.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　电影院应当遵守治安、消防、公共场所卫生等法律、行政法规，维护放映场所的公共秩序和环境卫生，保障观众的安全与健康。  
任何人不得携带爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、腐蚀性物品进入电影院等放映场所，不得非法携带枪支、弹药、管制器具进入电影院等放映场所；发现非法携带上述物品的，有关工作人员应当拒绝其进入，并向有关部门报告。

**Article 33** Cinemas shall observe laws and administrative regulations on public security, fire protection, and public place hygiene, maintain public order and environmental hygiene in projection venues, and ensure the safety and health of the audience.  
No person may bring explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic, or corrosive substances into cinemas or other projection venues, nor may they illegally bring guns, ammunition, or controlled instruments into cinemas or other projection venues; where such illegal items are discovered, the relevant staff shall refuse the person entry and report to the relevant department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　电影发行企业、电影院等应当如实统计电影销售收入，提供真实准确的统计数据，不得采取制造虚假交易、虚报瞒报销售收入等不正当手段，欺骗、误导观众，扰乱电影市场秩序。

**Article 34** Film distribution enterprises, cinemas, etc., shall truthfully count film sales revenue, provide true and accurate statistical data, and shall not resort to improper means such as fabricating false transactions, falsely reporting or concealing sales revenue, to deceive or mislead the audience or disrupt the order of the film market.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在境内举办涉外电影节（展），须经国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门批准。

**Article 35** To hold a foreign-related film festival (exhibition) within the territory, approval from the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government must be obtained.

### 第四章　电影产业支持、保障

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家支持下列电影的创作、摄制：  
（一）传播中华优秀文化、弘扬社会主义核心价值观的重大题材电影；  
（二）促进未成年人健康成长的电影；  
（三）展现艺术创新成果、促进艺术进步的电影；  
（四）推动科学教育事业发展和科学技术普及的电影；  
（五）其他符合国家支持政策的电影。

**Article 36** The State supports the creation and production of the following films:  
(1) Films with major themes that disseminate excellent Chinese culture and promote the core socialist values;  
(2) Films that promote the healthy growth of minors;  
(3) Films that demonstrate artistic innovation achievements and promote artistic progress;  
(4) Films that promote the development of science education and the popularization of science and technology;  
(5) Other films that meet the State support policies.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家引导相关文化产业专项资金、基金加大对电影产业的投入力度，根据不同阶段和时期电影产业的发展情况，结合财力状况和经济社会发展需要，综合考虑、统筹安排财政资金对电影产业的支持，并加强对相关资金、基金使用情况的审计。

**Article 37** The State shall guide special funds and funds for related cultural industries to increase their investment in the film industry. Based on the development of the film industry at different stages and periods, in light of financial capacity and the needs of economic and social development, the State shall comprehensively consider and make overall arrangements for fiscal support to the film industry, and strengthen the audit of the use of relevant funds and funds.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家实施必要的税收优惠政策，促进电影产业发展，具体办法由国务院财税主管部门依照税收法律、行政法规的规定制定。

**Article 38** The State shall implement necessary preferential tax policies to promote the development of the film industry. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent departments for finance and taxation under the State Council in accordance with tax laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当依据人民群众需求和电影市场发展需要，将电影院建设和改造纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、土地利用总体规划和城乡规划等。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，有效保障电影院用地需求，积极盘活现有电影院用地资源，支持电影院建设和改造。

**Article 39** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of the people and the development of the film market, incorporate the construction and renovation of cinemas into the national economic and social development plan, the overall land use plan, and the urban and rural plans.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, effectively ensure the land demand for cinemas, actively revitalize existing land resources for cinemas, and support the construction and renovation of cinemas.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家鼓励金融机构为从事电影活动以及改善电影基础设施提供融资服务，依法开展与电影有关的知识产权质押融资业务，并通过信贷等方式支持电影产业发展。  
国家鼓励保险机构依法开发适应电影产业发展需要的保险产品。  
国家鼓励融资担保机构依法向电影产业提供融资担保，通过再担保、联合担保以及担保与保险相结合等方式分散风险。  
对国务院电影主管部门依照本法规定公告的电影的摄制，按照国家有关规定合理确定贷款期限和利率。

**Article 40** The State encourages financial institutions to provide financing services for film activities and for the improvement of film infrastructure, to carry out intellectual property pledge financing business related to film according to law, and to support the development of the film industry through credit and other means.  
The State encourages insurance institutions to develop insurance products that meet the needs of the film industry according to law.  
The State encourages financing guarantee institutions to provide financing guarantees to the film industry according to law, and to spread risks through means such as re-guarantee, joint guarantee, and the combination of guarantee and insurance.  
For the production of films announced by the film主管部门 under the State Council in accordance with this Law, the loan term and interest rate shall be reasonably determined in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家鼓励法人、其他组织通过到境外合作摄制电影等方式进行跨境投资，依法保障其对外贸易、跨境融资和投资等合理用汇需求。

**Article 41** The State encourages legal persons and other organizations to make cross-border investments through means such as co-producing films overseas, and shall, according to law, guarantee their reasonable foreign exchange needs for foreign trade, cross-border financing, and investment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家实施电影人才扶持计划。  
国家支持有条件的高等学校、中等职业学校和其他教育机构、培训机构等开设与电影相关的专业和课程，采取多种方式培养适应电影产业发展需要的人才。  
国家鼓励从事电影活动的法人和其他组织参与学校相关人才培养。

**Article 42** The State shall implement a film talent support plan.  
The State supports qualified institutions of higher education, secondary vocational schools, and other educational institutions and training institutions in offering film-related majors and courses, and in cultivating talents that meet the needs of the film industry through various means.  
The State encourages legal persons and other organizations engaged in film activities to participate in relevant talent cultivation in schools.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家采取措施，扶持农村地区、边疆地区、贫困地区和民族地区开展电影活动。  
国家鼓励、支持少数民族题材电影创作，加强电影的少数民族语言文字译制工作，统筹保障民族地区群众观看电影需求。

**Article 43** The State shall take measures to support the conduct of film activities in rural areas, border areas, poverty-stricken areas, and ethnic minority areas.  
The State encourages and supports the creation of films on ethnic minority themes, strengthens the translation and dubbing of films into ethnic minority languages, and makes overall arrangements to ensure the needs of the masses in ethnic minority areas for watching films.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家对优秀电影的外语翻译制作予以支持，并综合利用外交、文化、教育等对外交流资源开展电影的境外推广活动。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织从事电影的境外推广。

**Article 44** The State shall support the foreign language translation and production of excellent films, and comprehensively utilize foreign exchange resources such as diplomacy, culture, and education to conduct overseas promotion activities for films.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to engage in the overseas promotion of films.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家鼓励社会力量以捐赠、资助等方式支持电影产业发展，并依法给予优惠。

**Article 45** The State encourages social forces to support the development of the film industry through means such as donations and funding, and shall grant preferences according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府电影主管部门应当加强对电影活动的日常监督管理，受理对违反本法规定的行为的投诉、举报，并及时核实、处理、答复；将从事电影活动的单位和个人因违反本法规定受到行政处罚的情形记入信用档案，并向社会公布。

**Article 46** The film主管部门 of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen daily supervision and management of film activities, accept complaints and reports of acts violating the provisions of this Law, and shall verify, handle, and respond in a timely manner; they shall record in the credit file the circumstances where units or individuals engaged in film activities have been subjected to administrative penalties for violating the provisions of this Law, and make such information public.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本法规定擅自从事电影摄制、发行、放映活动的，由县级以上人民政府电影主管部门予以取缔，没收电影片和违法所得以及从事违法活动的专用工具、设备；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得五倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处二十五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in film production, distribution, or projection activities without authorization, the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level shall ban such activities, confiscate the film, illegal gains, and the special tools and equipment used for the illegal activities; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may also be imposed.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有下列情形之一的，由原发证机关吊销有关许可证、撤销有关批准或者证明文件；县级以上人民政府电影主管部门没收违法所得；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得五倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处二十五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）伪造、变造、出租、出借、买卖本法规定的许可证、批准或者证明文件，或者以其他形式非法转让本法规定的许可证、批准或者证明文件的；  
（二）以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得本法规定的许可证、批准或者证明文件的。

**Article 48** In any of the following circumstances, the original issuing authority shall revoke the relevant permit, or withdraw the relevant approval or certification document; the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may also be imposed:  
(1) Forging, altering, leasing, lending, or selling the permits, approvals, or certification documents provided for in this Law, or illegally transferring such permits, approvals, or certification documents in other forms;  
(2) Obtaining the permits, approvals, or certification documents provided for in this Law by deceptive, bribery, or other improper means.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　有下列情形之一的，由原发证机关吊销许可证；县级以上人民政府电影主管部门没收电影片和违法所得；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得十倍以上二十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处五十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）发行、放映未取得电影公映许可证的电影的；  
（二）取得电影公映许可证后变更电影内容，未依照规定重新取得电影公映许可证擅自发行、放映、送展的；  
（三）提供未取得电影公映许可证的电影参加电影节（展）的。

**Article 49** In any of the following circumstances, the original issuing authority shall revoke the permit; the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the film and the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan may also be imposed:  
(1) Distributing or projecting a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit;  
(2) After obtaining the Film Public Projection Permit, changing the content of the film and distributing, projecting, or submitting it to a festival without re-obtaining the Film Public Projection Permit in accordance with the regulations;  
(3) Providing a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit for participation in a film festival (exhibition).

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　承接含有损害我国国家尊严、荣誉和利益，危害社会稳定，伤害民族感情等内容的境外电影的洗印、加工、后期制作等业务的，由县级以上人民政府电影主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收电影片和违法所得；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得三倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处十五万元以下的罚款。情节严重的，由电影主管部门通报工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 50** Where any person undertakes businesses such as developing, processing, or post-production of overseas films that contain content harming the dignity, honor, or interests of the State, endangering social stability, or hurting national sentiments, the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the film and the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 150,000 yuan may also be imposed. If the circumstances are serious, the film主管部门 shall notify the administrative department for industry and commerce, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　电影发行企业、电影院等有制造虚假交易、虚报瞒报销售收入等行为，扰乱电影市场秩序的，由县级以上人民政府电影主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；违法所得五十万元以上的，处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。情节严重的，责令停业整顿；情节特别严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证。  
电影院在向观众明示的电影开始放映时间之后至电影放映结束前放映广告的，由县级人民政府电影主管部门给予警告，责令改正；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 51** Where a film distribution enterprise, cinema, or the like engages in acts such as fabricating false transactions, falsely reporting or concealing sales revenue, thereby disrupting the order of the film market, the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the illegal gains are 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of the illegal gains shall be imposed. If the circumstances are serious, the entity shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification; if the circumstances are especially serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the permit.  
Where a cinema screens advertisements after the film start time indicated to the audience and before the end of the film screening, the film主管部门 of the people's government at the county level shall give a warning and order correction; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　法人或者其他组织未经许可擅自在境内举办涉外电影节（展）的，由国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收参展的电影片和违法所得；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得五倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处二十五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，自受到处罚之日起五年内不得举办涉外电影节（展）。  
个人擅自在境内举办涉外电影节（展），或者擅自提供未取得电影公映许可证的电影参加电影节（展）的，由国务院电影主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影主管部门责令停止违法活动，没收参展的电影片和违法所得；违法所得五万元以上的，并处违法所得五倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，可以并处二十五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，自受到处罚之日起五年内不得从事相关电影活动。

**Article 52** Where a legal person or other organization holds a foreign-related film festival (exhibition) within the territory without permission, the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the films submitted to the exhibition and the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the entity shall not hold foreign-related film festivals (exhibitions) within five years from the date of the penalty.  
Where an individual holds a foreign-related film festival (exhibition) within the territory without permission, or provides a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit for participation in a film festival (exhibition) without authorization, the film主管部门 under the State Council or the film主管部门 of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the films submitted to the exhibition and the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the amount of the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the individual shall not engage in relevant film activities within five years from the date of the penalty.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　法人、其他组织或者个体工商户因违反本法规定被吊销许可证的，自吊销许可证之日起五年内不得从事该项业务活动；其法定代表人或者主要负责人自吊销许可证之日起五年内不得担任从事电影活动的法人、其他组织的法定代表人或者主要负责人。

**Article 53** Where a legal person, other organization, or individual industrial and commercial household has its permit revoked for violating the provisions of this Law, it shall not engage in the relevant business activities within five years from the date of revocation; its legal representative or principal responsible person shall not serve as the legal representative or principal responsible person of any legal person or other organization engaged in film activities within five years from the date of revocation.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　有下列情形之一的，依照有关法律、行政法规及国家有关规定予以处罚：  
（一）违反国家有关规定，擅自将未取得电影公映许可证的电影制作为音像制品的；  
（二）违反国家有关规定，擅自通过互联网、电信网、广播电视网等信息网络传播未取得电影公映许可证的电影的；  
（三）以虚报、冒领等手段骗取农村电影公益放映补贴资金的；  
（四）侵犯与电影有关的知识产权的；  
（五）未依法接收、收集、整理、保管、移交电影档案的。  
电影院有前款第四项规定行为，情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 54** In any of the following circumstances, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and relevant State regulations:  
(1) In violation of relevant State regulations, making a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit into an audio-visual product without authorization;  
(2) In violation of relevant State regulations, disseminating a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit through information networks such as the Internet, telecommunications networks, or radio and television networks without authorization;  
(3) Fraudulently obtaining subsidy funds for public welfare film projection in rural areas through false reports or false claims;  
(4) Infringing upon intellectual property rights related to film;  
(5) Failing to receive, collect, sort out, preserve, or transfer film archives according to law.  
Where a cinema commits the act specified in item (4) of the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the permit.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上人民政府电影主管部门或者其他有关部门的工作人员有下列情形之一，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分：  
（一）利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处的；  
（二）违反本法规定进行审批活动的；  
（三）不履行监督职责的；  
（四）发现违法行为不予查处的；  
（五）贪污、挪用、截留、克扣农村电影公益放映补贴资金或者相关专项资金、基金的；  
（六）其他违反本法规定滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的情形。

**Article 55** Where a staff member of the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law:  
(1) Taking advantage of the position to accept money, property, or other benefits from others;  
(2) Conducting approval activities in violation of the provisions of this Law;  
(3) Failing to perform supervision duties;  
(4) Failing to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered;  
(5) Embezzling, misappropriating, intercepting, or withholding subsidy funds for public welfare film projection in rural areas or relevant special funds or funds;  
(6) Other circumstances of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain in violation of the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
因违反本法规定二年内受到二次以上行政处罚，又有依照本法规定应当处罚的违法行为的，从重处罚。

**Article 56** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes personal injury or property damage shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person has been subjected to two or more administrative penalties within two years for violating the provisions of this Law, and commits another illegal act that shall be punished under this Law, a severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府电影主管部门及其工作人员应当严格依照本法规定的处罚种类和幅度，根据违法行为的性质和具体情节行使行政处罚权，具体办法由国务院电影主管部门制定。  
县级以上人民政府电影主管部门对有证据证明违反本法规定的行为进行查处时，可以依法查封与违法行为有关的场所、设施或者查封、扣押用于违法行为的财物。

**Article 57** The film主管部门 of the people's governments at or above the county level and their staff shall exercise the power of administrative penalty strictly in accordance with the types and ranges of penalties provided for in this Law, based on the nature and specific circumstances of the illegal act. Specific measures shall be formulated by the film主管部门 under the State Council.  
When investigating and punishing an act with evidence proving a violation of the provisions of this Law, the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level may, according to law, seal up the premises or facilities related to the illegal act, or seal up or seize the property used for the illegal act.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　当事人对县级以上人民政府电影主管部门以及其他有关部门依照本法作出的行政行为不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。其中，对国务院电影主管部门作出的不准予电影公映的决定不服的，应当先依法申请行政复议，对行政复议决定不服的可以提起行政诉讼。

**Article 58** Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative act made by the film主管部门 of the people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department in accordance with this Law, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law. In particular, where a party is dissatisfied with the decision made by the film主管部门 under the State Council not to approve the public projection of a film, it shall first apply for administrative reconsideration according to law, and if still dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, may initiate an administrative action.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　境外资本在中华人民共和国境内设立从事电影活动的企业的，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 59** Where overseas capital establishes an enterprise engaged in film activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the relevant State regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自2017年3月1日起施行。

**Article 60** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2017.
