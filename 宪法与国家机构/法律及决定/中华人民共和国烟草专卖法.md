# 中华人民共和国烟草专卖法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2015-04-24  
**生效日期**：2015-04-24

> （1991年6月29日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2013年12月28日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法〉等七部法律的决定》第二次修正　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国计量法〉等五部法律的决定》第三次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　烟叶的种植、收购和调拨 第三章　烟草制品的生产 第四章　烟草制品的销售和运输 第五章　卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的生产和销售 第六章　进出口贸易和对外经济技术合作 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为实行烟草专卖管理，有计划地组织烟草专卖品的生产和经营，提高烟草制品质量，维护消费者利益，保证国家财政收入，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称烟草专卖品是指卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶、烟叶、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械。 卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶统称烟草制品。 第三条　国家对烟草专卖品的生产、销售、进出口依法实行专卖管理，并实行烟草专卖许可证制度。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为实行烟草专卖管理，有计划地组织烟草专卖品的生产和经营，提高烟草制品质量，维护消费者利益，保证国家财政收入，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of implementing the monopoly administration of tobacco, organizing the production and operation of tobacco monopoly commodities in a planned manner, improving the quality of tobacco products, protecting the interests of consumers, and ensuring the state's fiscal revenue.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称烟草专卖品是指卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶、烟叶、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械。  
卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶统称烟草制品。

**Article 2** For the purpose of this Law, "tobacco monopoly commodities" refer to cigarettes, cigars, cut tobacco, re-dried tobacco leaves, tobacco leaves, cigarette paper, filter rods, tow for cigarette use, and specialized machinery for tobacco.  
Cigarettes, cigars, cut tobacco and re-dried tobacco leaves are collectively referred to as tobacco products.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对烟草专卖品的生产、销售、进出口依法实行专卖管理，并实行烟草专卖许可证制度。

**Article 3** The state shall, according to law, exercise monopoly administration over the production, sale, import and export of tobacco monopoly commodities, and shall implement a tobacco monopoly license system.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门主管全国烟草专卖工作。省、自治区、直辖市烟草专卖行政主管部门主管本辖区的烟草专卖工作，受国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的双重领导，以国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门的领导为主。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例.md#art-4" title="被《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 4** The tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide tobacco monopoly work. The tobacco monopoly administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the tobacco monopoly work within their respective jurisdictions and shall be under the dual leadership of the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, with the leadership of the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council as the main.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家加强对烟草专卖品的科学研究和技术开发，提高烟草制品的质量，降低焦油和其他有害成份的含量。  
国家和社会加强吸烟危害健康的宣传教育，禁止或者限制在公共交通工具和公共场所吸烟，劝阻青少年吸烟，禁止中小学生吸烟。

**Article 5** The state shall strengthen scientific research and technological development of tobacco monopoly commodities, improve the quality of tobacco products, and reduce the content of tar and other harmful components.  
The state and society shall strengthen publicity and education on the harm of smoking to health, prohibit or restrict smoking on public transport vehicles and in public places, dissuade adolescents from smoking, and prohibit primary and middle school students from smoking.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家在民族自治地方实行烟草专卖管理，应当依照本法和民族区域自治法的有关规定，照顾民族自治地方的利益，对民族自治地方的烟叶种植和烟草制品生产给予照顾。

**Article 6** Where the state implements tobacco monopoly administration in national autonomous areas, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law and the Law on Regional National Autonomy, take into consideration the interests of national autonomous areas and give preferential treatment to the cultivation of tobacco leaves and the production of tobacco products in such areas.

### 第二章　烟叶的种植、收购和调拨

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本法所称烟叶是指生产烟草制品所需的烤烟和名晾晒烟，名晾晒烟的名录由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定。  
未列入名晾晒烟名录的其他晾晒烟可以在集市贸易市场出售。

**Article 7** For the purpose of this Law, "tobacco leaves" refer to flue-cured tobacco and famous sun-cured or air-cured tobacco required for the production of tobacco products. The list of famous sun-cured or air-cured tobacco shall be prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.  
Sun-cured or air-cured tobacco not included in the list of famous sun-cured or air-cured tobacco may be sold in rural trade markets.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　烟草种植应当因地制宜地培育和推广优良品种。优良品种由当地烟草公司组织供应。

**Article 8** Tobacco cultivation shall foster and spread fine varieties in the light of local conditions. Fine varieties shall be supplied by the local tobacco company.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　烟叶收购计划由县级以上地方人民政府计划部门根据国务院计划部门下达的计划下达，其他单位和个人不得变更。  
烟草公司或者其委托单位应当与烟叶种植者签订烟叶收购合同。烟叶收购合同应当约定烟叶种植面积、烟叶收购价格。

**Article 9** The plan for the purchase of tobacco leaves shall be issued by the planning department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the plan issued by the planning department of the State Council, and no other unit or individual may change it.  
The tobacco company or the unit entrusted by it shall conclude a tobacco leaf purchase contract with the tobacco leaf grower. The tobacco leaf purchase contract shall stipulate the area for tobacco leaf cultivation and the purchase price for tobacco leaves.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　烟叶由烟草公司或者其委托单位按照国家规定的收购标准统一收购，其他单位和个人不得收购。  
烟草公司及其委托单位对烟叶种植者按照烟叶收购合同约定的种植面积生产的烟叶，应当按照合同约定的收购价格，全部收购，不得压级压价，并妥善处理收购烟叶发生的纠纷。

**Article 10** Tobacco leaves shall be purchased uniformly by the tobacco company or the unit entrusted by it in accordance with the purchase standards prescribed by the state, and no other unit or individual may purchase them.  
For tobacco leaves produced by tobacco leaf growers in accordance with the planting area stipulated in the tobacco leaf purchase contract, the tobacco company and the unit entrusted by it shall purchase all such leaves at the purchase price stipulated in the contract, shall not depress the grade or price, and shall properly handle any disputes arising from the purchase of tobacco leaves.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市之间的烟叶、复烤烟叶的调拨计划由国务院计划部门下达，省、自治区、直辖市辖区内的烟叶、复烤烟叶的调拨计划由省、自治区、直辖市计划部门下达，其他单位和个人不得变更。  
烟叶、复烤烟叶的调拨必须签订合同。

**Article 11** The plan for the allocation of tobacco leaves and re-dried tobacco leaves among provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be issued by the planning department of the State Council, and the plan for such allocation within the territory of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be issued by the planning department of such province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; no other unit or individual may change them.  
A contract must be concluded for the allocation of tobacco leaves and re-dried tobacco leaves.

### 第三章　烟草制品的生产

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　开办烟草制品生产企业，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准，取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证，并经工商行政管理部门核准登记；其分立、合并、撤销，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准，并向工商行政管理部门办理变更、注销登记手续。未取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证的，工商行政管理部门不得核准登记。

**Article 12** To establish a tobacco product production enterprise, approval must be obtained from the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council, a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license must be obtained, and registration must be approved by the administrative department for industry and commerce; any split, merger or dissolution of such an enterprise must be approved by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council, and the procedures for alteration or cancellation of registration shall be completed with the administrative department for industry and commerce. The administrative department for industry and commerce shall not approve the registration of any enterprise that has not obtained a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　烟草制品生产企业为扩大生产能力进行基本建设或者技术改造，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准。

**Article 13** A tobacco product production enterprise that carries out capital construction or technical renovation for the purpose of expanding production capacity must obtain approval from the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　省、自治区、直辖市的卷烟、雪茄烟年度总产量计划由国务院计划部门下达。烟草制品生产企业的卷烟、雪茄烟年度总产量计划，由省级烟草专卖行政主管部门根据国务院计划部门下达的计划，结合市场销售情况下达，地方人民政府不得向烟草制品生产企业下达超产任务。烟草制品生产企业根据市场销售情况，需要超过年度总产量计划生产卷烟、雪茄烟，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准。  
全国烟草总公司根据国务院计划部门下达的年度总产量计划向省级烟草公司下达分等级、分种类的卷烟产量指标。省级烟草公司根据全国烟草总公司下达的分等级、分种类的卷烟产量指标，结合市场销售情况，向烟草制品生产企业下达分等级、分种类的卷烟产量指标。烟草制品生产企业可以根据市场销售情况，在该企业的年度总产量计划的范围内，对分等级、分种类的卷烟产量指标适当调整。

**Article 14** The annual total output plan for cigarettes and cigars of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be issued by the planning department of the State Council. The annual total output plan for cigarettes and cigars of a tobacco product production enterprise shall be issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department in accordance with the plan issued by the planning department of the State Council and in light of market sales conditions; no local people's government may assign over-quota production tasks to a tobacco product production enterprise. Where a tobacco product production enterprise, in view of market sales conditions, needs to produce cigarettes or cigars in excess of the annual total output plan, it must obtain approval from the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.  
The China National Tobacco Corporation shall, in accordance with the annual total output plan issued by the planning department of the State Council, issue to provincial tobacco companies the output quotas for cigarettes by grade and type. The provincial tobacco company shall, in accordance with the output quotas for cigarettes by grade and type issued by the China National Tobacco Corporation and in light of market sales conditions, issue to tobacco product production enterprises the output quotas for cigarettes by grade and type. The tobacco product production enterprise may, in light of market sales conditions, make appropriate adjustments to the output quotas for cigarettes by grade and type within the scope of the enterprise's annual total output plan.

### 第四章　烟草制品的销售和运输

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　经营烟草制品批发业务的企业，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门或者省级烟草专卖行政主管部门批准，取得烟草专卖批发企业许可证，并经工商行政管理部门核准登记。

**Article 15** An enterprise engaging in the wholesale of tobacco products must obtain approval from the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council or the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department, obtain a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license, and have its registration approved by the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　经营烟草制品零售业务的企业或者个人，由县级人民政府工商行政管理部门根据上一级烟草专卖行政主管部门的委托，审查批准发给烟草专卖零售许可证。已经设立县级烟草专卖行政主管部门的地方，也可以由县级烟草专卖行政主管部门审查批准发给烟草专卖零售许可证。

**Article 16** For an enterprise or individual engaging in the retail of tobacco products, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the county people's government shall, upon the entrustment of the tobacco monopoly administrative department at the next higher level, examine and approve the issuance of a tobacco monopoly retail license. In places where a county-level tobacco monopoly administrative department has been established, such department may also examine and approve the issuance of a tobacco monopoly retail license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家制定卷烟、雪茄烟的焦油含量级标准。卷烟、雪茄烟应当在包装上标明焦油含量级和“吸烟有害健康”。

**Article 17** The state shall formulate the standards for tar content grades of cigarettes and cigars. Cigarettes and cigars shall have their tar content grades and the words "Smoking is harmful to health" marked on their packages.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止在广播电台、电视台、报刊播放、刊登烟草制品广告。

**Article 18** It is prohibited to broadcast or publish advertisements for tobacco products on radio stations, television stations, or in newspapers or periodicals.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　卷烟、雪茄烟和有包装的烟丝必须申请商标注册，未经核准注册的，不得生产、销售。  
禁止生产、销售假冒他人注册商标的烟草制品。

**Article 19** Cigarettes, cigars and packaged cut tobacco must apply for trademark registration. Those not approved for registration may not be produced or sold.  
It is prohibited to produce or sell tobacco products that counterfeit the registered trademark of another person.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　烟草制品商标标识必须由省级工商行政管理部门指定的企业印制；非指定的企业不得印制烟草制品商标标识。

**Article 20** The trademark labels of tobacco products must be printed by enterprises designated by the provincial administrative department for industry and commerce; non-designated enterprises may not print such trademark labels.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　托运或者自运烟草专卖品必须持有烟草专卖行政主管部门或者烟草专卖行政主管部门授权的机构签发的准运证；无准运证的，承运人不得承运。

**Article 21** For consigning or self-transporting tobacco monopoly products, a transport permit issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department or an institution authorized by such department must be held; without a transport permit, the carrier shall not carry the goods.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　邮寄、异地携带烟叶、烟草制品的，不得超过国务院有关主管部门规定的限量。

**Article 22** The quantity of tobacco leaves or tobacco products mailed or carried to another place shall not exceed the limit prescribed by the relevant competent department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　个人进入中国境内携带烟草制品的，不得超过国务院有关主管部门规定的限量。

**Article 23** An individual bringing tobacco products into China shall not exceed the limit prescribed by the relevant competent department of the State Council.

### 第五章　卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的生产和销售

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　生产卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的企业，必须报国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准，取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证。  
本法所称烟草专用机械是指烟草专用机械的整机。

**Article 24** An enterprise that produces cigarette paper, filter rods, tow for cigarette use, or tobacco-specific machinery must report to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for approval and obtain a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license.  
For the purpose of this Law, tobacco-specific machinery refers to complete sets of tobacco-specific machinery.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　生产卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的企业，应当按照国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门的计划以及与烟草制品生产企业签订的订货合同组织生产。

**Article 25** An enterprise that produces cigarette paper, filter rods, tow for cigarette use, or tobacco-specific machinery shall organize production in accordance with the plans of the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council and the purchase contracts signed with tobacco product production enterprises.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　生产卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的企业，只可将产品销售给烟草公司和持有烟草专卖生产企业许可证的烟草制品生产企业。

**Article 26** An enterprise that produces cigarette paper, filter rods, tow for cigarette use, or tobacco-specific machinery may only sell such products to tobacco companies and tobacco product production enterprises holding tobacco monopoly production enterprise licenses.

### 第六章　进出口贸易和对外经济技术合作

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门根据国务院规定，管理烟草行业的进出口贸易和对外经济技术合作。

**Article 27** The tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, administer the import and export trade and foreign economic and technological cooperation of the tobacco industry.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本法规定擅自收购烟叶的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门处以罚款，并按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烟叶平均收购价格的百分之七十收购违法收购的烟叶；数量巨大的，没收违法收购的烟叶和违法所得。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例.md#art-51" title="被《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, purchases tobacco leaves without authorization shall be imposed a fine by the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and the illegally purchased tobacco leaves shall be purchased at seventy percent of the average purchase price of tobacco leaves of the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they are seized; if the quantity is large, the illegally purchased tobacco leaves and illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　无准运证或者超过准运证规定的数量托运或者自运烟草专卖品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门处以罚款，可以按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烟叶平均收购价格的百分之七十收购违法运输的烟叶，按照市场批发价格的百分之七十收购违法运输的除烟叶外的其他烟草专卖品；情节严重的，没收违法运输的烟草专卖品和违法所得。  
承运人明知是烟草专卖品而为无准运证的单位、个人运输的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门没收违法所得，并处罚款。  
超过国家规定的限量异地携带烟叶、烟草制品，数量较大的，依照第一款的规定处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例.md#art-52" title="被《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** Whoever, without a transport permit or in excess of the quantity specified in the transport permit, consigns or self-transports tobacco monopoly products shall be imposed a fine by the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and may be subject to the purchase of illegally transported tobacco leaves at seventy percent of the average purchase price of tobacco leaves of the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they are seized, and the purchase of other illegally transported tobacco monopoly products other than tobacco leaves at seventy percent of the market wholesale price; if the circumstances are serious, the illegally transported tobacco monopoly products and illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
If a carrier, knowing that the goods are tobacco monopoly products, transports them for any entity or individual without a transport permit, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine.  
If any person carries tobacco leaves or tobacco products to another place in excess of the limit prescribed by the State and in relatively large quantities, the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　无烟草专卖生产企业许可证生产烟草制品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令关闭，没收违法所得，并处罚款。  
无烟草专卖生产企业许可证生产卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束或者烟草专用机械的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令停止生产上述产品，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Whoever produces tobacco products without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license shall be ordered to close down by the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed.  
Whoever produces cigarette paper, filter rods, tow for cigarette use, or tobacco-specific machinery without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license shall be ordered to stop producing the said products by the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine may also be imposed.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　无烟草专卖批发企业许可证经营烟草制品批发业务的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令关闭或者停止经营烟草制品批发业务，没收违法所得，并处罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例.md#art-54" title="被《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法实施条例》第54条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** Whoever engages in the wholesale business of tobacco products without a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license shall be ordered to close down or to cease the wholesale business of tobacco products by the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　无烟草专卖零售许可证经营烟草制品零售业务的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止经营烟草制品零售业务，没收违法所得，并处罚款。

**Article 32** Whoever engages in the retail business of tobacco products without a tobacco monopoly retail license shall be ordered to cease the retail business of tobacco products by the administrative department for industry and commerce, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　生产、销售没有注册商标的卷烟、雪茄烟、有包装的烟丝的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止生产、销售，并处罚款。  
生产、销售假冒他人注册商标的烟草制品的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止侵权行为，赔偿被侵权人的损失，可以并处罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Whoever produces or sells cigarettes, cigars, or packaged cut tobacco without a registered trademark shall be ordered to stop production and sale by the administrative department for industry and commerce, and a fine shall be imposed.  
Whoever produces or sells tobacco products that counterfeit the registered trademark of another person shall be ordered to cease the infringement by the administrative department for industry and commerce, compensate the losses of the infringed party, and a fine may also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)的规定，非法印制烟草制品商标标识的，由工商行政管理部门销毁印制的商标标识，没收违法所得，并处罚款。

**Article 34** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of Article 20 of this Law, illegally prints trademark labels of tobacco products shall have the printed trademark labels destroyed by the administrative department for industry and commerce, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine shall be imposed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　倒卖烟草专卖品，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；情节轻微，不构成犯罪的，由工商行政管理部门没收倒卖的烟草专卖品和违法所得，可以并处罚款。  
烟草专卖行政主管部门和烟草公司工作人员利用职务上的便利犯前款罪的，依法从重处罚。

**Article 35** Whoever profiteers in tobacco monopoly products, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the circumstances are minor and do not constitute a crime, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall confiscate the profiteered tobacco monopoly products and illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.  
If any staff member of the tobacco monopoly administrative department or a tobacco company commits the crime mentioned in the preceding paragraph by taking advantage of his position, he shall be given a severe punishment according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　伪造、变造、买卖本法规定的烟草专卖生产企业许可证、烟草专卖经营许可证等许可证件和准运证的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
烟草专卖行政主管部门和烟草公司工作人员利用职务上的便利犯前款罪的，依法从重处罚。

**Article 36** Whoever forges, alters, or buys or sells the tobacco monopoly production enterprise license, tobacco monopoly operation license, or other licenses or transport permits provided for in this Law shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
If any staff member of the tobacco monopoly administrative department or a tobacco company commits the crime mentioned in the preceding paragraph by taking advantage of his position, he shall be given a severe punishment according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　走私烟草专卖品，构成走私罪的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；走私烟草专卖品，数额不大，不构成走私罪的，由海关没收走私货物、物品和违法所得，可以并处罚款。  
烟草专卖行政主管部门和烟草公司工作人员利用职务上的便利犯前款罪的，依法从重处罚。

**Article 37** Whoever smuggles tobacco monopoly products, if the act constitutes the crime of smuggling, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the amount of smuggled tobacco monopoly products is not large and does not constitute the crime of smuggling, the Customs shall confiscate the smuggled goods, articles and illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.  
If any staff member of the tobacco monopoly administrative department or a tobacco company commits the crime mentioned in the preceding paragraph by taking advantage of his position, he shall be given a severe punishment according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　烟草专卖行政主管部门有权对本法实施情况进行检查。以暴力、威胁方法阻碍烟草专卖检查人员依法执行职务的，依法追究刑事责任；拒绝、阻碍烟草专卖检查人员依法执行职务未使用暴力、威胁方法的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚。

**Article 38** The tobacco monopoly administrative department shall have the power to inspect the implementation of this Law. Whoever obstructs a tobacco monopoly inspector from performing his duties according to law by means of violence or threat shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; whoever refuses or obstructs a tobacco monopoly inspector from performing his duties according to law without using violence or threat shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Punishments.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民法院和处理违法案件的有关部门的工作人员私分没收的烟草制品，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
人民法院和处理违法案件的有关部门的工作人员购买没收的烟草制品的，责令退还，可以给予行政处分。

**Article 39** If any staff member of a People's Court or the relevant department handling illegal cases privately divides up confiscated tobacco products, he shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
If any staff member of a People's Court or the relevant department handling illegal cases purchases confiscated tobacco products, he shall be ordered to return them, and may be given an administrative sanction.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　烟草专卖行政主管部门和烟草公司的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊或者玩忽职守的，给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** If any staff member of the tobacco monopoly administrative department or a tobacco company abuses his power, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or neglects his duty, he shall be given an administrative sanction; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, he shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　当事人对烟草专卖行政主管部门和工商行政管理部门作出的行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内直接向人民法院起诉。  
复议机关应当在接到复议申请之日起六十日内作出复议决定。当事人对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉；复议机关逾期不作出复议决定的，当事人可以在复议期满之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。  
当事人逾期不申请复议也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 41** A party that disagrees with an administrative penalty decision made by the competent department of tobacco monopoly administration or the administrative department for industry and commerce may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply for a review to the authority at the next higher level of the authority that made the penalty decision; the party may also, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, directly institute a prosecution with the People's Court.  
The review authority shall make a review decision within sixty days from the date of receipt of the review application. A party that disagrees with the review decision may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the review decision, institute a prosecution with the People's Court; if the review authority fails to make a review decision within the time limit, the party may, within fifteen days from the expiration of the review period, institute a prosecution with the People's Court.  
If a party neither applies for review nor institutes a prosecution with the People's Court within the time limit, and fails to comply with the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 42** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本法自1992年1月1日起施行。1983年9月23日国务院发布的《烟草专卖条例》同时废止。

**Article 43** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1992. The Regulations on Tobacco Monopoly promulgated by the State Council on September 23, 1983 shall be abolished simultaneously.
