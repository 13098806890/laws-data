# 全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于县级以下人民代表大会代表直接选举的若干规定

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1983-03-05  
**生效日期**：1983-03-05

> （1983年3月5日第五届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议通过） 为了便于实施《中华人民共和国全国人民代表大会和地方各级人民代表大会选举法》，对县级以下人民代表大会代表直接选举中的若干问题作如下规定： 一、县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇设立选举委员会。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的选举委员会的组成人员由本级人民代表大会常务委员会任命。乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会的组成人员由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民代表大会常务委员会任命。 选举委员会设立办事机构，办理选举的具体事务。 二、选举委员会的职权是： （一）主持本级人民代表大会代表的选举； （二）进行选民登记，审查选民资格，公布选民名单；受理对于选民名单不同意见的申诉，并做出决定； （三）划分选举本级人民代表大会代表的选区，分配各选区应选代表的名额； （四）根据较多数选民的意见，确定和公布正式代表候选人的名单； （五）规定选举日期； （六）确定选举结果是否有效，公布当选代表名单。 县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的选举委员会指导乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会的工作。 三、精神病患者不能行使选举权利的，经选举委员会确认，不行使选举权利。 四、因反革命案或者其他严重刑事犯罪案被羁押，正在受侦查、起诉、审判的人，经人民检察院或者人民法院决定，在被羁押期间停止行使选举权利。 五、下列人员准予行使选举权利：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇设立选举委员会。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的选举委员会的组成人员由本级人民代表大会常务委员会任命。乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会的组成人员由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民代表大会常务委员会任命。  
选举委员会设立办事机构，办理选举的具体事务。

**Article 1** 1. Election committees shall be established in counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, municipal districts, townships, ethnic townships, and towns. The members of the election committee of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall be appointed by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level. The members of the election committee of a township, ethnic township, or town shall be appointed by the standing committee of the people's congress of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district. An election committee shall establish an operational office to handle specific election affairs.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、选举委员会的职权是：  
（一）主持本级人民代表大会代表的选举；  
（二）进行选民登记，审查选民资格，公布选民名单；受理对于选民名单不同意见的申诉，并做出决定；  
（三）划分选举本级人民代表大会代表的选区，分配各选区应选代表的名额；  
（四）根据较多数选民的意见，确定和公布正式代表候选人的名单；  
（五）规定选举日期；  
（六）确定选举结果是否有效，公布当选代表名单。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的选举委员会指导乡、民族乡、镇的选举委员会的工作。

**Article 2** 2. The functions and powers of an election committee are as follows:  
(1) to preside over the election of deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(2) to conduct voter registration, examine voters' qualifications, announce the list of voters, accept appeals concerning objections to the list of voters, and make decisions thereon;  
(3) to divide electoral districts for the election of deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level and allocate the number of deputies to be elected in each electoral district;  
(4) to determine and announce the list of official candidates for deputies based on the opinions of the majority of voters;  
(5) to prescribe the date of election; and (6) to determine whether the election results are valid and announce the list of elected deputies. The election committee of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall supervise the work of the election committees of townships, ethnic townships, and towns.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、精神病患者不能行使选举权利的，经选举委员会确认，不行使选举权利。

**Article 3** 3. A person with a mental disorder who is incapable of exercising the right to vote shall, upon confirmation by the election committee, not exercise the right to vote.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、因反革命案或者其他严重刑事犯罪案被羁押，正在受侦查、起诉、审判的人，经人民检察院或者人民法院决定，在被羁押期间停止行使选举权利。

**Article 4** 4. A person in custody who is undergoing investigation, prosecution, or adjudication for a counter-revolutionary case or other serious criminal case shall, by decision of the people's procuratorate or the people's court, cease to exercise the right to vote during the period of custody.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、下列人员准予行使选举权利：  
（一）被判处有期徒刑、拘役、管制而没有附加剥夺政治权利的；  
（二）被羁押，正在受侦查、起诉、审判，人民检察院或者人民法院没有决定停止行使选举权利的；  
（三）正在取保候审或者被监视居住的；  
（四）正在被劳动教养的；  
（五）正在受拘留处罚的。  
以上所列人员参加选举，由选举委员会和执行监禁、羁押、拘留或者劳动教养的机关共同决定，可以在流动票箱投票，或者委托有选举权的亲属或者其他选民代为投票。被判处拘役、受拘留处罚或者被劳动教养的人也可以在选举日回原选区参加选举。

**Article 5** 5. The following persons shall be permitted to exercise the right to vote:  
(1) those sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or public surveillance without the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights;  
(2) those in custody undergoing investigation, prosecution, or adjudication, and for whom the people's procuratorate or the people's court has not decided to cease the exercise of the right to vote;  
(3) those released on bail pending investigation or trial or under residential surveillance;  
(4) those undergoing re-education through labor; and (5) those subject to a penalty of detention. The participation of the above-listed persons in elections shall be jointly decided by the election committee and the organ executing imprisonment, custody, detention, or re-education through labor, and they may vote in a mobile ballot box or entrust a relative who has the right to vote or another voter to vote on their behalf. Persons sentenced to criminal detention, subject to a penalty of detention, or undergoing re-education through labor may also return to their original electoral district to vote on election day.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、县、自治县的人民政府驻地在市区内的，其所属机关、团体和企业事业组织的职工，参加县、自治县的人民代表大会代表的选举，不参加市、市辖区的人民代表大会代表的选举。

**Article 6** 6. Where the seat of the people's government of a county or autonomous county is located within a city district, the employees of its subordinate organs, organizations, enterprises, and institutions shall participate in the election of deputies to the people's congress of the county or autonomous county and shall not participate in the election of deputies to the people's congress of the city or municipal district.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、驻在乡、民族乡、镇的不属于县级以下人民政府领导的企业事业组织的职工，可以只参加县级人民代表大会代表的选举，不参加乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表的选举。

**Article 7** 7. Employees of enterprises and institutions stationed in townships, ethnic townships, or towns that are not under the leadership of the people's government at or below the county level may participate only in the election of deputies to the people's congress at the county level and not in the election of deputies to the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、选区的大小，按照每一选区选一至三名代表划分。

**Article 8** 8. The size of an electoral district shall be divided based on the election of one to three deputies per electoral district.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、选民在选举期间临时在外地劳动、工作或者居住，不能回原选区参加选举的，经原居住地的选举委员会认可，可以书面委托有选举权的亲属或者其他选民在原选区代为投票。  
选民实际上已经迁居外地但是没有转出户口的，在取得原选区选民资格的证明后，可以在现居住地的选区参加选举。

**Article 9** 9. Where a voter is temporarily working, employed, or residing in another place during the election period and cannot return to the original electoral district to participate in the election, with the approval of the election committee of the original place of residence, he or she may, in writing, entrust a relative who has the right to vote or another voter to vote on his or her behalf in the original electoral district. Where a voter has actually moved to another place but has not transferred his or her household registration, he or she may, after obtaining a certificate of voter qualification from the original electoral district, participate in the election in the electoral district of the current place of residence.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、每一选民（三人以上附议）推荐的代表候选人的名额，不得超过本选区应选代表的名额。  
选民和各政党、各人民团体推荐的代表候选人都应当列入代表候选人名单，选举委员会不得调换或者增减。  
正式代表候选人名单，经过预选确定的，按得票多少的顺序排列。

**Article 10** 10. The number of candidates for deputies nominated by each voter (with the endorsement of three or more persons) shall not exceed the number of deputies to be elected in that electoral district. All candidates for deputies nominated by voters, political parties, and people's organizations shall be included in the list of candidates for deputies, and the election committee shall not substitute, add, or delete any of them. The list of official candidates for deputies, if determined through a preliminary election, shall be arranged in descending order of the number of votes received.
