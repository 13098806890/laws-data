### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护国旗的尊严，规范国旗的使用，增强公民的国家观念，弘扬爱国主义精神，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of safeguarding the dignity of the national flag, regulating the use of the national flag, enhancing citizens' sense of state, promoting the spirit of patriotism, and fostering and practicing core socialist values.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国国旗是五星红旗。  
中华人民共和国国旗按照中国人民政治协商会议第一届全体会议主席团公布的国旗制法说明制作。

**Article 2** The national flag of the People's Republic of China is a red flag with five stars.  
The national flag of the People's Republic of China shall be manufactured in accordance with the Instructions for the Manufacture of the National Flag promulgated by the Presidium of the First Plenary Session of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国旗的通用尺度为国旗制法说明中所列明的五种尺度。特殊情况使用其他尺度的国旗，应当按照通用尺度成比例适当放大或者缩小。  
国旗、旗杆的尺度比例应当适当，并与使用目的、周围建筑、周边环境相适应。

**Article 3** The general dimensions of the national flag are the five dimensions listed in the Instructions for the Manufacture of the National Flag. Where it is necessary under special circumstances to use a national flag of other dimensions, it shall be appropriately enlarged or reduced in proportion to the general dimensions.  
The dimensional ratio of the national flag and the flagpole shall be appropriate and shall be compatible with the purpose of use, the surrounding buildings, and the surrounding environment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国国旗是中华人民共和国的象征和标志。  
每个公民和组织，都应当尊重和爱护国旗。

**Article 4** The national flag of the People's Republic of China is the symbol and emblem of the People's Republic of China.  
Every citizen and organization shall respect and cherish the national flag.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列场所或者机构所在地，应当每日升挂国旗：  
（一）北京天安门广场、新华门；  
（二）中国共产党中央委员会，全国人民代表大会常务委员会，国务院，中央军事委员会，中国共产党中央纪律检查委员会、国家监察委员会，最高人民法院，最高人民检察院；  
中国人民政治协商会议全国委员会；  
（三）外交部；  
（四）出境入境的机场、港口、火车站和其他边境口岸，边防海防哨所。

**Article 5** The national flag shall be hoisted daily at the following places or at the premises of the following organs:  
(1) Beijing Tiananmen Square and Xinhua Gate;  
(2) the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the State Council, the Central Military Commission, the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection of the Communist Party of China and the National Commission of Supervision, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuratorate;  
the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference;  
(3) the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(4) airports, ports, railway stations and other border ports for exit and entry, and border and coastal defense posts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列机构所在地应当在工作日升挂国旗：  
（一）中国共产党中央各部门和地方各级委员会；  
（二）国务院各部门；  
（三）地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会；  
（四）地方各级人民政府；  
（五）中国共产党地方各级纪律检查委员会、地方各级监察委员会；  
（六）地方各级人民法院和专门人民法院；  
（七）地方各级人民检察院和专门人民检察院；  
（八）中国人民政治协商会议地方各级委员会；  
（九）各民主党派、各人民团体；  
（十）中央人民政府驻香港特别行政区有关机构、中央人民政府驻澳门特别行政区有关机构。  
学校除寒假、暑假和休息日外，应当每日升挂国旗。有条件的幼儿园参照学校的规定升挂国旗。  
图书馆、博物馆、文化馆、美术馆、科技馆、纪念馆、展览馆、体育馆、青少年宫等公共文化体育设施应当在开放日升挂、悬挂国旗。

**Article 6** The national flag shall be hoisted on working days at the premises of the following organs:  
(1) the various departments of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and local committees at all levels;  
(2) the various departments of the State Council;  
(3) the standing committees of the local people's congresses at all levels;  
(4) the local people's governments at all levels;  
(5) the local commissions for discipline inspection of the Communist Party of China at all levels and local supervisory commissions at all levels;  
(6) local people's courts at all levels and special people's courts;  
(7) local people's procuratorates at all levels and special people's procuratorates;  
(8) local committees of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference at all levels;  
(9) the democratic parties and people's organizations;  
(10) the relevant institutions of the Central People's Government in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the relevant institutions of the Central People's Government in the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
Schools shall hoist the national flag daily except during winter and summer vacations and on rest days. Kindergartens that have the conditions may hoist the national flag by reference to the provisions concerning schools.  
Public cultural and sports facilities such as libraries, museums, cultural centers, art galleries, science and technology museums, memorials, exhibition halls, gymnasiums, and youth palaces shall hoist or hang the national flag on their opening days.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国庆节、国际劳动节、元旦、春节和国家宪法日等重要节日、纪念日，各级国家机关、各人民团体以及大型广场、公园等公共活动场所应当升挂国旗；企业事业组织，村民委员会、居民委员会，居民院（楼、小区）有条件的应当升挂国旗。  
民族自治地方在民族自治地方成立纪念日和主要传统民族节日应当升挂国旗。  
举行宪法宣誓仪式时，应当在宣誓场所悬挂国旗。

**Article 7** On important festivals and commemorative days such as the National Day, International Labor Day, New Year's Day, the Spring Festival, and the National Constitution Day, state organs at all levels and people's organizations, as well as public activity venues such as large squares and parks, shall hoist the national flag; enterprises, public institutions, villagers' committees, residents' committees, and residential courtyards (buildings, neighborhoods) that have the conditions shall hoist the national flag.  
In national autonomous areas, the national flag shall be hoisted on the anniversary of the establishment of the national autonomous area and on major traditional ethnic festivals.  
When a constitutional oath ceremony is held, the national flag shall be hung at the oath-taking venue.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　举行重大庆祝、纪念活动，大型文化、体育活动，大型展览会，可以升挂国旗。

**Article 8** The national flag may be hoisted on the occasion of major celebrations, commemorative activities, large-scale cultural and sports activities, and large exhibitions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家倡导公民和组织在适宜的场合使用国旗及其图案，表达爱国情感。  
公民和组织在网络中使用国旗图案，应当遵守相关网络管理规定，不得损害国旗尊严。  
网络使用的国旗图案标准版本在中国人大网和中国政府网上发布。

**Article 9** The State encourages citizens and organizations to use the national flag and its design on appropriate occasions to express patriotic sentiments.  
Citizens and organizations that use the design of the national flag on the internet shall comply with relevant internet management regulations and shall not impair the dignity of the national flag.  
The standard version of the design of the national flag for use on the internet is published on the website of the National People's Congress (www.npc.gov.cn) and the website of the Chinese government (www.gov.cn).

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外交活动以及国家驻外使馆领馆和其他外交代表机构升挂、使用国旗的办法，由外交部规定。

**Article 10** The measures for hoisting and using the national flag in diplomatic activities and at embassies, consulates and other diplomatic missions of the State abroad shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队升挂、使用国旗的办法，由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 11** The measures for hoisting and using the national flag by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民用船舶和进入中国领水的外国船舶升挂国旗的办法，由国务院交通主管部门规定。  
执行出入境边防检查、边境管理、治安任务的船舶升挂国旗的办法，由国务院公安部门规定。  
国家综合性消防救援队伍的船舶升挂国旗的办法，由国务院应急管理部门规定。

**Article 12** The measures for hoisting the national flag on civil vessels and on foreign vessels entering the territorial waters of China shall be prescribed by the competent department of transportation under the State Council.  
The measures for hoisting the national flag on vessels performing duties of entry-exit border inspection, border management, and public security shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.  
The measures for hoisting the national flag on vessels of the national comprehensive fire rescue team shall be prescribed by the emergency management department under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　依照[本法第五条](#art-5)、第六条、第七条的规定升挂国旗的，应当早晨升起，傍晚降下。  
依照本法规定应当升挂国旗的，遇有恶劣天气，可以不升挂。

**Article 13** Where the national flag is to be hoisted in accordance with the provisions of Articles 5, 6, and 7 of this Law, it shall be hoisted in the morning and lowered in the evening.  
Where the national flag is required to be hoisted in accordance with the provisions of this Law, it may not be hoisted in case of inclement weather.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　升挂国旗时，可以举行升旗仪式。  
举行升旗仪式时，应当奏唱国歌。在国旗升起的过程中，在场人员应当面向国旗肃立，行注目礼或者按照规定要求敬礼，不得有损害国旗尊严的行为。  
北京天安门广场每日举行升旗仪式。  
学校除假期外，每周举行一次升旗仪式。

**Article 14** A flag-raising ceremony may be held when the national flag is hoisted.  
When a flag-raising ceremony is held, the national anthem shall be played and sung. During the process of raising the national flag, persons present shall stand solemnly facing the national flag, pay attention with their eyes or salute as required by regulations, and shall not engage in any act that impairs the dignity of the national flag.  
A flag-raising ceremony is held daily at Beijing Tiananmen Square.  
Schools, except during holidays, shall hold a flag-raising ceremony once a week.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　下列人士逝世，下半旗志哀：  
（一）中华人民共和国主席、全国人民代表大会常务委员会委员长、国务院总理、中央军事委员会主席；  
（二）中国人民政治协商会议全国委员会主席；  
（三）对中华人民共和国作出杰出贡献的人；  
（四）对世界和平或者人类进步事业作出杰出贡献的人。  
举行国家公祭仪式或者发生严重自然灾害、突发公共卫生事件以及其他不幸事件造成特别重大伤亡的，可以在全国范围内下半旗志哀，也可以在部分地区或者特定场所下半旗志哀。  
依照本条第一款第三项、第四项和第二款的规定下半旗，由国务院有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报国务院决定。  
依照本条规定下半旗的日期和场所，由国家成立的治丧机构或者国务院决定。

**Article 15** The national flag shall be flown at half-mast as a sign of mourning upon the death of the following persons:  
(1) the President of the People's Republic of China, the Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, the Premier of the State Council, and the Chairman of the Central Military Commission;  
(2) the Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference;  
(3) persons who have made outstanding contributions to the People's Republic of China; and  
(4) persons who have made outstanding contributions to world peace or the cause of human progress.  
Where a national memorial ceremony is held or where serious natural disasters, public health emergencies, or other unfortunate events result in particularly heavy casualties, the national flag may be flown at half-mast nationwide or in certain areas or at specific places.  
Where the national flag is flown at half-mast in accordance with subparagraphs (3) and (4) of the first paragraph and the second paragraph, the relevant department of the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall report to the State Council for a decision.  
The date and place for flying the national flag at half-mast in accordance with this Article shall be determined by the funeral institution established by the State or by the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下列人士逝世，举行哀悼仪式时，其遗体、灵柩或者骨灰盒可以覆盖国旗：  
（一）[本法第十五条](#art-15)第一款第一项至第三项规定的人士；  
（二）烈士；  
（三）国家规定的其他人士。  
覆盖国旗时，国旗不得触及地面，仪式结束后应当将国旗收回保存。

**Article 16** Upon the death of the following persons, when a mourning ceremony is held, their remains, coffin, or urn may be covered with the national flag:  
(1) the persons specified in subparagraphs (1) to (3) of the first paragraph of Article 15 of this Law;  
(2) martyrs; and  
(3) other persons prescribed by the State.  
When the national flag is used for covering, the flag shall not touch the ground. After the ceremony, the national flag shall be taken back and kept.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　升挂国旗，应当将国旗置于显著的位置。  
列队举持国旗和其他旗帜行进时，国旗应当在其他旗帜之前。  
国旗与其他旗帜同时升挂时，应当将国旗置于中心、较高或者突出的位置。  
在外事活动中同时升挂两个以上国家的国旗时，应当按照外交部的规定或者国际惯例升挂。

**Article 17** When hoisting the national flag, it shall be placed in a prominent position.  
When marching in procession carrying the national flag and other flags, the national flag shall be in front of the other flags.  
When the national flag is hoisted together with other flags, the national flag shall be placed in the center, at a higher position, or in a prominent position.  
When the national flags of two or more countries are hoisted together during foreign affairs activities, they shall be hoisted in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or international practice.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在直立的旗杆上升降国旗，应当徐徐升降。升起时，必须将国旗升至杆顶；降下时，不得使国旗落地。  
下半旗时，应当先将国旗升至杆顶，然后降至旗顶与杆顶之间的距离为旗杆全长的三分之一处；降下时，应当先将国旗升至杆顶，然后再降下。

**Article 18** When hoisting or lowering the national flag on a vertical flagpole, it shall be hoisted or lowered slowly. When hoisting, the national flag must be raised to the top of the pole; when lowering, the national flag shall not touch the ground.  
When flying the national flag at half-mast, the national flag shall first be raised to the top of the pole and then lowered to the point where the distance between the top of the flag and the top of the pole is one-third of the total length of the flagpole; when lowering the flag, it shall first be raised to the top of the pole and then lowered.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　不得升挂或者使用破损、污损、褪色或者不合规格的国旗，不得倒挂、倒插或者以其他有损国旗尊严的方式升挂、使用国旗。  
不得随意丢弃国旗。破损、污损、褪色或者不合规格的国旗应当按照国家有关规定收回、处置。大型群众性活动结束后，活动主办方应当收回或者妥善处置活动现场使用的国旗。

**Article 19** No national flag that is damaged, defaced, faded, or of substandard specifications shall be hoisted or used. No national flag shall be hoisted or used upside down, inserted inverted, or in any other manner that impairs the dignity of the national flag.  
No national flag shall be discarded at will. Damaged, defaced, faded, or substandard national flags shall be taken back and disposed of in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State. After the conclusion of a large-scale mass activity, the organizer of the activity shall take back or properly dispose of the national flags used at the activity site.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国旗及其图案不得用作商标、授予专利权的外观设计和商业广告，不得用于私人丧事活动等不适宜的情形。

**Article 20** The national flag and its design shall not be used as trademarks, as designs for which patent rights are granted, or in commercial advertisements, and shall not be used for inappropriate occasions such as private funeral activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国旗应当作为爱国主义教育的重要内容。  
中小学应当教育学生了解国旗的历史和精神内涵、遵守国旗升挂使用规范和升旗仪式礼仪。  
新闻媒体应当积极宣传国旗知识，引导公民和组织正确使用国旗及其图案。

**Article 21** The national flag shall be an important part of patriotic education.  
Primary and secondary schools shall educate students to understand the history and spiritual connotation of the national flag, to comply with the norms for hoisting and using the national flag, and to follow the etiquette of flag-raising ceremonies.  
News media shall actively publicize knowledge of the national flag and guide citizens and organizations in the correct use of the national flag and its design.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院办公厅统筹协调全国范围内国旗管理有关工作。地方各级人民政府统筹协调本行政区域内国旗管理有关工作。  
各级人民政府市场监督管理部门对国旗的制作和销售实施监督管理。  
县级人民政府确定的部门对本行政区域内国旗的升挂、使用和收回实施监督管理。  
外交部、国务院交通主管部门、中央军事委员会有关部门对各自管辖范围内国旗的升挂、使用和收回实施监督管理。

**Article 22** The General Office of the State Council shall coordinate and manage the work related to the administration of the national flag nationwide. Local people's governments at all levels shall coordinate and manage the work related to the administration of the national flag within their respective administrative regions.  
The market supervision and administration departments of the people's governments at all levels shall exercise supervision and administration over the manufacture and sale of the national flag.  
The department designated by the people's government at the county level shall exercise supervision and administration over the hoisting, use, and taking back of the national flag within its administrative region.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the competent department of transportation under the State Council, and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission shall exercise supervision and administration over the hoisting, use, and taking back of the national flag within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在公共场合故意以焚烧、毁损、涂划、玷污、践踏等方式侮辱中华人民共和国国旗的，依法追究刑事责任；情节较轻的，由公安机关处以十五日以下拘留。

**Article 23** Whoever intentionally desecrates the national flag of the People's Republic of China in a public place by burning, mutilating, defacing, defiling, trampling upon, or by other means shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; where the circumstances are minor, the public security organ shall impose a detention of not more than 15 days.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本法自1990年10月1日起施行。

国旗制法说明  
（1949年9月28日中国人民政治协商会议第一届全体会议主席团公布）  
国旗的形状、颜色两面相同，旗上五星两面相对。为便利计，本件仅以旗杆在左之一面为说明之标准。对于旗杆在右之一面，凡本件所称左均应改右，所称右均应改左。  
（一）旗面为红色，长方形，其长与高为三与二之比，旗面左上方缀黄色五角星五颗。一星较大，其外接圆直径为旗高十分之三，居左；四星较小，其外接圆直径为旗高十分之一，环拱于大星之右。旗杆套为白色。  
（二）五星之位置与画法如下：  
甲、为便于确定五星之位置，先将旗面对分为四个相等的长方形，将左上方之长方形上下划为十等分，左右划为十五等分。  
乙、大五角星的中心点，在该长方形上五下五、左五右十之处。其画法为：以此点为圆心，以三等分为半径作一圆。在此圆周上，定出五个等距离的点，其一点须位于圆之正上方。然后将此五点中各相隔的两点相联，使各成一直线。此五直线所构成之外轮廓线，即为所需之大五角星。五角星之一个角尖正向上方。  
丙、四颗小五角星的中心点，第一点在该长方形上二下八、左十右五之处，第二点在上四下六、左十二右三之处，第三点在上七下三、左十二右三之处，第四点在上九下一、左十右五之处。其画法为：以以上四点为圆心，各以一等分为半径，分别作四个圆。在每个圆上各定出五个等距离的点，其中均须各有一点位于大五角星中心点与以上四个圆心的各联结线上。然后用构成大五角星的同样方法，构成小五角星。此四颗小五角星均各有一个角尖正对大五角星的中心点。  
（三）国旗之通用尺度定为如下五种，各界酌情选用：  
甲、长288公分，高192公分。  
乙、长240公分，高160公分。  
丙、长192公分，高128公分。  
丁、长144公分，高96公分。  
戊、长96公分，高64公分。

Appendix:  
  
Instructions for the Manufacture of the National Flag  
(Promulgated by the Presidium of the First Plenary Session of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference on September 28, 1949)  
  
The shape and colors of the national flag are the same on both sides, and the five stars on the flag are opposite on both sides. For convenience, this document only takes the side with the flagpole on the left as the standard for description. For the side with the flagpole on the right, wherever "left" is used in this document, it shall be changed to "right", and wherever "right" is used, it shall be changed to "left".  
  
(1) The flag is red and rectangular, with a ratio of length to height of 3:2. Five yellow five-pointed stars are placed in the upper left corner of the flag. One star is larger, with a circumscribed circle diameter of three-tenths of the flag's height, located on the left; the four stars are smaller, each with a circumscribed circle diameter of one-tenth of the flag's height, arranged in an arc to the right of the larger star. The flagpole sleeve is white.  
  
(2) The positions and drawing method of the five stars are as follows:  
A. To facilitate the determination of the positions of the five stars, first divide the flag into four equal rectangles. Divide the upper left rectangle into ten equal parts vertically and fifteen equal parts horizontally.  
B. The center point of the large five-pointed star is at the position of five units from the top and bottom, and five units from the left and ten units from the right within that rectangle. The drawing method is: take this point as the center and three units as the radius to draw a circle. On this circumference, determine five points at equal distances, with one point directly above the center of the circle. Then connect each pair of points that are separated by one point, forming straight lines. The outer contour formed by these five lines is the required large five-pointed star. One point of the five-pointed star points directly upward.  
C. The center points of the four small five-pointed stars: the first point is at the position of two units from the top and eight units from the bottom, ten units from the left and five units from the right; the second point at four units from the top and six units from the bottom, twelve units from the left and three units from the right; the third point at seven units from the top and three units from the bottom, twelve units from the left and three units from the right; the fourth point at nine units from the top and one unit from the bottom, ten units from the left and five units from the right. The drawing method is: take the above four points as centers and one unit as the radius to draw four circles respectively. On each circle, determine five points at equal distances, each of which must have one point located on the line connecting the center of the large five-pointed star and the respective center of the four circles. Then, using the same method as for constructing the large five-pointed star, construct the small five-pointed stars. Each of these four small five-pointed stars has one point pointing directly toward the center of the large five-pointed star.  
  
(3) The general dimensions of the national flag are set as the following five types, which may be selected as appropriate by various sectors:  
A. Length 288 cm, height 192 cm.  
B. Length 240 cm, height 160 cm.  
C. Length 192 cm, height 128 cm.  
D. Length 144 cm, height 96 cm.  
E. Length 96 cm, height 64 cm.
