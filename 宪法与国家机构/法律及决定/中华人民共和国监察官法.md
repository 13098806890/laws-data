### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对监察官的管理和监督，保障监察官依法履行职责，维护监察官合法权益，推进高素质专业化监察官队伍建设，推进监察工作规范化、法治化，根据宪法和《中华人民共和国监察法》，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution and the Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China to strengthen the management and supervision of supervision officers, ensure that supervision officers perform their duties according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of supervision officers, advance the building of a high-quality and professional team of supervision officers, and promote the standardization and rule of law in supervision work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　监察官的管理和监督坚持中国共产党领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚持党管干部原则，增强监察官的使命感、责任感、荣誉感，建设忠诚干净担当的监察官队伍。

**Article 2** The management and supervision of supervision officers shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Theory of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, adhere to the principle of the Party managing officials, enhance the sense of mission, responsibility, and honor of supervision officers, and build a team of supervision officers who are loyal, clean, and responsible.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　监察官包括下列人员：  
（一）各级监察委员会的主任、副主任、委员；  
（二）各级监察委员会机关中的监察人员；  
（三）各级监察委员会派驻或者派出到中国共产党机关、国家机关、法律法规授权或者委托管理公共事务的组织和单位以及所管辖的行政区域等的监察机构中的监察人员、监察专员；  
（四）其他依法行使监察权的监察机构中的监察人员。  
对各级监察委员会派驻到国有企业的监察机构工作人员、监察专员，以及国有企业中其他依法行使监察权的监察机构工作人员的监督管理，参照执行本法有关规定。

**Article 3** Supervision officers include the following personnel:  
(1) Directors, deputy directors, and members of supervisory commissions at all levels;  
(2) Supervision personnel in the organs of supervisory commissions at all levels;  
(3) Supervision personnel and supervision commissioners in supervisory institutions stationed or dispatched by supervisory commissions at all levels to organs of the Communist Party of China, state organs, organizations and units authorized or entrusted by laws and regulations to manage public affairs, and the administrative regions under their jurisdiction;  
(4) Supervision personnel in other supervisory institutions that exercise supervisory power according to law.  
The supervision and management of the staff and supervision commissioners of supervisory institutions stationed in state-owned enterprises by supervisory commissions at all levels, as well as the staff of other supervisory institutions in state-owned enterprises that exercise supervisory power according to law, shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　监察官应当忠诚坚定、担当尽责、清正廉洁，做严格自律、作风优良、拒腐防变的表率。

**Article 4** Supervision officers shall be loyal and steadfast, responsible and diligent, upright and incorruptible, and shall set an example of strict self-discipline, excellent conduct, and resistance to corruption and prevention of degeneracy.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　监察官应当维护宪法和法律的尊严和权威，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，客观公正地履行职责，保障当事人的合法权益。

**Article 5** Supervision officers shall uphold the dignity and authority of the Constitution and laws, take facts as the basis and law as the criterion, perform their duties objectively and impartially, and protect the lawful rights and interests of the parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　监察官应当严格按照规定的权限和程序履行职责，坚持民主集中制，重大事项集体研究。

**Article 6** Supervision officers shall strictly perform their duties in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures, adhere to democratic centralism, and collectively study major matters.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　监察机关应当建立健全对监察官的监督制度和机制，确保权力受到严格约束。  
监察官应当自觉接受组织监督和民主监督、社会监督、舆论监督。

**Article 7** Supervisory organs shall establish and improve the supervision system and mechanism for supervision officers to ensure that power is strictly constrained.  
Supervision officers shall consciously accept organizational supervision and democratic supervision, social supervision, and public opinion supervision.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　监察官依法履行职责受法律保护，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。

**Article 8** Supervision officers' performance of their duties according to law shall be protected by law and shall not be interfered with by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

### 第二章　监察官的职责、义务和权利

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　监察官依法履行下列职责：  
（一）对公职人员开展廉政教育；  
（二）对公职人员依法履职、秉公用权、廉洁从政从业以及道德操守情况进行监督检查；  
（三）对法律规定由监察机关管辖的职务违法和职务犯罪进行调查；  
（四）根据监督、调查的结果，对办理的监察事项提出处置意见；  
（五）开展反腐败国际合作方面的工作；  
（六）法律规定的其他职责。  
监察官在职权范围内对所办理的监察事项负责。

**Article 9** Supervision officers shall perform the following duties according to law:  
(1) Conduct integrity education for public officials;  
(2) Supervise and inspect the performance of duties according to law, exercise of power impartially, integrity in politics and profession, and moral conduct of public officials;  
(3) Investigate duty violations and duty crimes that are under the jurisdiction of supervisory organs as prescribed by law;  
(4) Propose opinions on handling the supervisory matters under their charge based on the results of supervision and investigation;  
(5) Carry out work on international cooperation against corruption;  
(6) Other duties as prescribed by law.  
Supervision officers shall be responsible for the supervisory matters they handle within the scope of their functions and powers.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　监察官应当履行下列义务：  
（一）自觉坚持中国共产党领导，严格执行中国共产党和国家的路线方针政策、重大决策部署；  
（二）模范遵守宪法和法律；  
（三）维护国家和人民利益，秉公执法，勇于担当、敢于监督，坚决同腐败现象作斗争；  
（四）依法保障监察对象及有关人员的合法权益；  
（五）忠于职守，勤勉尽责，努力提高工作质量和效率；  
（六）保守国家秘密和监察工作秘密，对履行职责中知悉的商业秘密和个人隐私、个人信息予以保密；  
（七）严守纪律，恪守职业道德，模范遵守社会公德、家庭美德；  
（八）自觉接受监督；  
（九）法律规定的其他义务。

**Article 10** Supervision officers shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) Consciously adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, and strictly implement the line, principles, policies, and major decisions and deployments of the Communist Party of China and the state;  
(2) Exemplarily observe the Constitution and laws;  
(3) Safeguard the interests of the state and the people, enforce the law impartially, be courageous and dare to supervise, and resolutely fight against corruption;  
(4) Protect the lawful rights and interests of supervision targets and relevant persons according to law;  
(5) Be dedicated to their duties, diligent and responsible, and strive to improve work quality and efficiency;  
(6) Keep state secrets and supervisory work secrets, and keep confidential the trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information learned during the performance of duties;  
(7) Strictly observe discipline, abide by professional ethics, and exemplarily observe social morality and family virtues;  
(8) Consciously accept supervision;  
(9) Other obligations as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　监察官享有下列权利：  
（一）履行监察官职责应当具有的职权和工作条件；  
（二）履行监察官职责应当享有的职业保障和福利待遇；  
（三）人身、财产和住所安全受法律保护；  
（四）提出申诉或者控告；  
（五）《中华人民共和国公务员法》等法律规定的其他权利。

**Article 11** Supervision officers shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) The functions and powers and working conditions necessary for performing the duties of supervision officers;  
(2) The occupational security and welfare benefits that they shall enjoy for performing the duties of supervision officers;  
(3) The security of their person, property, and domicile shall be protected by law;  
(4) To file a petition or make an accusation;  
(5) Other rights as stipulated by the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws.

### 第三章　监察官的条件和选用

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　担任监察官应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）忠于宪法，坚持中国共产党领导和社会主义制度；  
（三）具有良好的政治素质、道德品行和廉洁作风；  
（四）熟悉法律、法规、政策，具有履行监督、调查、处置等职责的专业知识和能力；  
（五）具有正常履行职责的身体条件和心理素质；  
（六）具备高等学校本科及以上学历；  
（七）法律规定的其他条件。  
本法施行前的监察人员不具备前款第六项规定的学历条件的，应当接受培训和考核，具体办法由国家监察委员会制定。

**Article 12** To serve as a supervision officer, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Having the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Being loyal to the Constitution, and adhering to the leadership of the Communist Party of China and the socialist system;  
(3) Having good political quality, moral character, and incorruptible style;  
(4) Being familiar with laws, regulations, and policies, and having professional knowledge and ability to perform duties such as supervision, investigation, and disposal;  
(5) Having the physical condition and psychological quality to perform duties normally;  
(6) Having a bachelor's degree or above from an institution of higher education;  
(7) Other conditions as prescribed by law.  
If supervision personnel before the coming into force of this Law do not meet the educational requirements specified in paragraph (6), they shall receive training and assessment, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the National Commission of Supervision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任监察官：  
（一）因犯罪受过刑事处罚，以及因犯罪情节轻微被人民检察院依法作出不起诉决定或者被人民法院依法免予刑事处罚的；  
（二）被撤销中国共产党党内职务、留党察看、开除党籍的；  
（三）被撤职或者开除公职的；  
（四）被依法列为失信联合惩戒对象的；  
（五）配偶已移居国（境）外，或者没有配偶但是子女均已移居国（境）外的；  
（六）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 13** A person shall not serve as a supervision officer under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having received criminal punishment for a crime, or having been exempted from criminal punishment by a People's Court or having been subject to a decision of non-prosecution by a People's Procuratorate according to law due to a minor crime;  
(2) Having been removed from a Party position within the Communist Party of China, placed on probation within the Party, or expelled from the Party;  
(3) Having been removed from office or dismissed from public employment;  
(4) Having been listed as a person subject to joint disciplinary action for dishonesty according to law;  
(5) The spouse has emigrated abroad, or if there is no spouse, all children have emigrated abroad;  
(6) Other circumstances as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　监察官的选用，坚持德才兼备、以德为先，坚持五湖四海、任人唯贤，坚持事业为上、公道正派，突出政治标准，注重工作实绩。

**Article 14** The selection and appointment of supervision officers shall adhere to the principles of having both integrity and ability, with integrity as the priority; selecting from all over the country with merit; putting the cause first and being fair and upright; highlighting political standards; and focusing on work performance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　监察官采用考试、考核的办法，从符合监察官条件的人员中择优选用。

**Article 15** Supervision officers shall be selected and appointed through examination and assessment from among personnel who meet the conditions for supervision officers.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　录用监察官，应当依照法律和国家有关规定采取公开考试、严格考察、平等竞争、择优录取的办法。

**Article 16** The recruitment of supervision officers shall adopt methods of open examination, strict assessment, equal competition, and merit-based admission in accordance with laws and relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　监察委员会可以根据监察工作需要，依照法律和国家有关规定从中国共产党机关、国家机关、事业单位、国有企业等机关、单位从事公务的人员中选择符合任职条件的人员担任监察官。

**Article 17** A supervisory commission may, based on the needs of supervisory work and in accordance with laws and relevant state provisions, select from among personnel engaged in public affairs in organs such as organs of the Communist Party of China, state organs, public institutions, and state-owned enterprises, persons who meet the qualifications to serve as supervision officers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　监察委员会可以根据监察工作需要，依照法律和国家有关规定在从事与监察机关职能职责相关的职业或者教学、研究的人员中选拔或者聘任符合任职条件的人员担任监察官。

**Article 18** A supervisory commission may, based on the needs of supervisory work and in accordance with laws and relevant state provisions, select or appoint from among personnel engaged in occupations or teaching and research related to the functions and duties of supervisory organs, persons who meet the qualifications to serve as supervision officers.

### 第四章　监察官的任免

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家监察委员会主任由全国人民代表大会选举和罢免，副主任、委员由国家监察委员会主任提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
地方各级监察委员会主任由本级人民代表大会选举和罢免，副主任、委员由监察委员会主任提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
新疆生产建设兵团各级监察委员会主任、副主任、委员，由新疆维吾尔自治区监察委员会主任提请自治区人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
其他监察官的任免，按照管理权限和规定的程序办理。

**Article 19** The Director of the National Commission of Supervision shall be elected and removed by the National People's Congress, and the Deputy Directors and members shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the proposal of the Director of the National Commission of Supervision.  
The Directors of local supervisory commissions at all levels shall be elected and removed by the people's congresses at the corresponding levels, and the Deputy Directors and members shall be appointed and removed by the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels upon the proposal of the Directors of the supervisory commissions.  
The Directors, Deputy Directors, and members of supervisory commissions at all levels of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps shall be appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the People's Congress of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region upon the proposal of the Director of the Supervisory Commission of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.  
The appointment and removal of other supervision officers shall be handled in accordance with the management authority and prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　监察官就职时应当依照法律规定进行宪法宣誓。

**Article 20** Supervision officers shall take a constitutional oath in accordance with legal provisions when taking office.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　监察官有下列情形之一的，应当免去其监察官职务：  
（一）丧失中华人民共和国国籍的；  
（二）职务变动不需要保留监察官职务的；  
（三）退休的；  
（四）辞职或者依法应当予以辞退的；  
（五）因违纪违法被调离或者开除的；  
（六）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 21** A supervision officer shall be removed from the post of supervision officer under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Losing the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Change of position does not require retaining the post of supervision officer;  
(3) Retirement;  
(4) Resignation or being subject to dismissal according to law;  
(5) Being transferred away or expelled due to violation of discipline or law;  
(6) Other circumstances as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　监察官不得兼任人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员，不得兼任行政机关、审判机关、检察机关的职务，不得兼任企业或者其他营利性组织、事业单位的职务，不得兼任人民陪审员、人民监督员、执业律师、仲裁员和公证员。  
监察官因工作需要兼职的，应当按照管理权限批准，但是不得领取兼职报酬。

**Article 22** A supervision officer shall not concurrently hold the position of a member of a standing committee of a people's congress, nor shall he or she concurrently hold a position in an administrative organ, an adjudicatory organ, or a procuratorial organ, nor shall he or she concurrently hold a position in an enterprise or other for-profit organization or a public institution, nor shall he or she concurrently serve as a people's assessor, a people's supervisor, a practicing lawyer, an arbitrator, or a notary.  
If a supervision officer needs to hold a concurrent position due to work requirements, it shall be approved in accordance with the management authority, but he or she shall not receive remuneration for the concurrent position.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　监察官担任县级、设区的市级监察委员会主任的，应当按照有关规定实行地域回避。

**Article 23** If a supervision officer serves as the Director of a supervisory commission at the county level or at the level of a city divided into districts, he or she shall implement territorial avoidance in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　监察官之间有夫妻关系、直系血亲关系、三代以内旁系血亲以及近姻亲关系的，不得同时担任下列职务：  
（一）同一监察委员会的主任、副主任、委员，上述人员和其他监察官；  
（二）监察委员会机关同一部门的监察官；  
（三）同一派驻机构、派出机构或者其他监察机构的监察官；  
（四）上下相邻两级监察委员会的主任、副主任、委员。

**Article 24** If supervision officers are related by marriage, by direct blood line, by collateral blood line within three generations, or by close affinity, they shall not concurrently hold the following positions:  
(1) Directors, Deputy Directors, and members of the same supervisory commission, as well as these persons and other supervision officers;  
(2) Supervision officers in the same department of a supervisory commission organ;  
(3) Supervision officers in the same stationed agency, dispatched agency, or other supervisory agency;  
(4) Directors, Deputy Directors, and members of two adjacent levels of supervisory commissions.

### 第五章　监察官的管理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　监察官等级分为十三级，依次为总监察官、一级副总监察官、二级副总监察官，一级高级监察官、二级高级监察官、三级高级监察官、四级高级监察官，一级监察官、二级监察官、三级监察官、四级监察官、五级监察官、六级监察官。

**Article 25** The ranks of supervision officers are divided into thirteen grades, which are, in order: General Supervision Officer, First-level Deputy General Supervision Officer, Second-level Deputy General Supervision Officer, First-level Senior Supervision Officer, Second-level Senior Supervision Officer, Third-level Senior Supervision Officer, Fourth-level Senior Supervision Officer, First-level Supervision Officer, Second-level Supervision Officer, Third-level Supervision Officer, Fourth-level Supervision Officer, Fifth-level Supervision Officer, and Sixth-level Supervision Officer.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家监察委员会主任为总监察官。

**Article 26** The Director of the National Commission of Supervision shall be the General Supervision Officer.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　监察官等级的确定，以监察官担任的职务职级、德才表现、业务水平、工作实绩和工作年限等为依据。  
监察官等级晋升采取按期晋升和择优选升相结合的方式，特别优秀或者作出特别贡献的，可以提前选升。

**Article 27** The determination of the rank of a supervision officer shall be based on the position and rank held, moral and ability performance, professional level, work performance, and length of service.  
The promotion of supervision officer ranks shall adopt a combination of periodic promotion and selective merit-based promotion. Those who are exceptionally outstanding or have made special contributions may be promoted ahead of schedule.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　监察官的等级设置、确定和晋升的具体办法，由国家另行规定。

**Article 28** The specific measures for the establishment, determination, and promotion of supervision officer ranks shall be separately prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　初任监察官实行职前培训制度。

**Article 29** A pre-service training system shall be implemented for newly appointed supervision officers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对监察官应当有计划地进行政治、理论和业务培训。  
培训应当突出政治机关特色，坚持理论联系实际、按需施教、讲求实效，提高专业能力。  
监察官培训情况，作为监察官考核的内容和任职、等级晋升的依据之一。

**Article 30** Political, theoretical, and professional training shall be systematically provided to supervision officers.  
The training shall highlight the characteristics of political organs, adhere to the integration of theory with practice, teach according to needs, and emphasize practical results, to improve professional competence.  
The training status of supervision officers shall serve as one of the bases for assessment and for appointment and rank promotion.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　监察官培训机构按照有关规定承担培训监察官的任务。

**Article 31** Training institutions for supervision officers shall undertake the task of training supervision officers in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家加强监察学科建设，鼓励具备条件的普通高等学校设置监察专业或者开设监察课程，培养德才兼备的高素质监察官后备人才，提高监察官的专业能力。

**Article 32** The state shall strengthen the development of the discipline of supervision, encourage ordinary institutions of higher education that meet the conditions to set up a supervision major or offer supervision courses, cultivate high-quality reserve talents of supervision officers with both integrity and ability, and improve the professional competence of supervision officers.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　监察官依照法律和国家有关规定实行任职交流。

**Article 33** Supervision officers shall be subject to job rotation in accordance with laws and relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　监察官申请辞职，应当由本人书面提出，按照管理权限批准后，依照规定的程序免去其职务。

**Article 34** If a supervision officer applies for resignation, he or she shall submit a written application, and after approval according to the management authority, his or her position shall be removed in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　监察官有依法应当予以辞退情形的，依照规定的程序免去其职务。  
辞退监察官应当按照管理权限决定。辞退决定应当以书面形式通知被辞退的监察官，并列明作出决定的理由和依据。

**Article 35** If a supervision officer falls under any circumstance requiring dismissal according to law, his or her position shall be removed in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
The dismissal of a supervision officer shall be decided according to the management authority. The decision of dismissal shall be notified in writing to the supervision officer being dismissed, and shall state the reasons and basis for the decision.

### 第六章　监察官的考核和奖励

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对监察官的考核，应当全面、客观、公正，实行平时考核、专项考核和年度考核相结合。

**Article 36** The assessment of supervision officers shall be comprehensive, objective, and fair, and shall combine routine assessment, special assessment, and annual assessment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　监察官的考核应当按照管理权限，全面考核监察官的德、能、勤、绩、廉，重点考核政治素质、工作实绩和廉洁自律情况。

**Article 37** The assessment of supervision officers shall, in accordance with the management authority, comprehensively assess the virtue, ability, diligence, performance, and integrity of supervision officers, with a focus on political quality, work performance, and self-discipline.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　年度考核结果分为优秀、称职、基本称职和不称职四个等次。  
考核结果作为调整监察官等级、工资以及监察官奖惩、免职、降职、辞退的依据。

**Article 38** The annual assessment results shall be divided into four grades: excellent, competent, basically competent, and incompetent.  
The assessment results shall serve as the basis for adjusting the rank and salary of supervision officers, as well as for rewards and punishments, removal from post, demotion, and dismissal.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　年度考核结果以书面形式通知监察官本人。监察官对考核结果如果有异议，可以申请复核。

**Article 39** The annual assessment results shall be notified in writing to the supervision officer concerned. If the supervision officer has any objection to the assessment result, he or she may apply for a review.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对在监察工作中有显著成绩和贡献，或者有其他突出事迹的监察官、监察官集体，给予奖励。

**Article 40** Rewards shall be given to supervision officers or groups of supervision officers who have made remarkable achievements and contributions in supervisory work, or who have other outstanding deeds.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　监察官有下列表现之一的，给予奖励：  
（一）履行监督职责，成效显著的；  
（二）在调查、处置职务违法和职务犯罪工作中，做出显著成绩和贡献的；  
（三）提出有价值的监察建议，对防止和消除重大风险隐患效果显著的；  
（四）研究监察理论、总结监察实践经验成果突出，对监察工作有指导作用的；  
（五）有其他功绩的。  
监察官的奖励按照有关规定办理。

**Article 41** Rewards shall be given to supervision officers who have one of the following performances:  
(1) Performing supervision duties with remarkable results;  
(2) Making remarkable achievements and contributions in the investigation and handling of duty violations and duty crimes;  
(3) Proposing valuable supervisory suggestions that are remarkably effective in preventing and eliminating major risks and hazards;  
(4) Having outstanding achievements in researching supervision theory and summarizing supervision practice experience, which are instructive for supervisory work;  
(5) Having other meritorious deeds.  
The rewards for supervision officers shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第七章　监察官的监督和惩戒

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　监察机关应当规范工作流程，加强内部监督制约机制建设，强化对监察官执行职务和遵守法律情况的监督。

**Article 42** Supervisory organs shall standardize work procedures, strengthen the construction of internal supervision and constraint mechanisms, and enhance the supervision of supervision officers' performance of duties and compliance with laws.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　任何单位和个人对监察官的违纪违法行为，有权检举、控告。受理检举、控告的机关应当及时调查处理，并将结果告知检举人、控告人。  
对依法检举、控告的单位和个人，任何人不得压制和打击报复。

**Article 43** Any unit or individual has the right to report or accuse supervision officers of violations of discipline or law. The organ that receives the report or accusation shall timely investigate and handle the matter and inform the informant or accuser of the result.  
No one shall suppress or retaliate against any unit or individual that reports or accuses according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　对于审判机关、检察机关、执法部门等移送的监察官违纪违法履行职责的问题线索，监察机关应当及时调查处理。

**Article 44** For clues of problems concerning supervision officers' violations of discipline or law in performing duties transferred by adjudicatory organs, procuratorial organs, law enforcement departments, etc., the supervisory organs shall timely investigate and handle them.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　监察委员会根据工作需要，按照规定从各方面代表中聘请特约监察员等监督人员，对监察官履行职责情况进行监督，提出加强和改进监察工作的意见、建议。

**Article 45** A supervisory commission may, based on work needs and in accordance with regulations, engage special supervision officers and other supervisory personnel from among representatives of various sectors to supervise the performance of duties by supervision officers and to put forward opinions and suggestions for strengthening and improving supervisory work.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　监察官不得打听案情、过问案件、说情干预。对于上述行为，办理监察事项的监察官应当及时向上级报告。有关情况应当登记备案。  
办理监察事项的监察官未经批准不得接触被调查人、涉案人员及其特定关系人，或者与其进行交往。对于上述行为，知悉情况的监察官应当及时向上级报告。有关情况应当登记备案。

**Article 46** Supervision officers shall not inquire about case details, intervene in cases, or intercede with favor. For the above acts, supervision officers handling supervisory matters shall promptly report to their superiors. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and recorded.  
Supervision officers handling supervisory matters shall not, without approval, contact the person under investigation, persons involved in the case, or their specific related persons, or engage in interactions with them. For the above acts, supervision officers who become aware of the situation shall promptly report to their superiors. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and recorded.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　办理监察事项的监察官有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，监察对象、检举人、控告人及其他有关人员也有权要求其回避；没有主动申请回避的，监察机关应当依法决定其回避：  
（一）是监察对象或者检举人、控告人的近亲属的；  
（二）担任过本案的证人的；  
（三）本人或者其近亲属与办理的监察事项有利害关系的；  
（四）有可能影响监察事项公正处理的其他情形的。

**Article 47** A supervision officer handling a supervisory matter shall voluntarily withdraw under any of the following circumstances, and the supervision target, informant, accuser, and other relevant persons also have the right to request his or her withdrawal; if he or she does not voluntarily apply for withdrawal, the supervisory organ shall decide on his or her withdrawal according to law:  
(1) Being a close relative of the supervision target, informant, or accuser;  
(2) Having served as a witness in the case;  
(3) The supervision officer or his or her close relative has an interest in the supervisory matter being handled;  
(4) Other circumstances that may affect the fair handling of the supervisory matter.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　监察官应当严格执行保密制度，控制监察事项知悉范围和时间，不得私自留存、隐匿、查阅、摘抄、复制、携带问题线索和涉案资料，严禁泄露监察工作秘密。  
监察官离岗离职后，应当遵守脱密期管理规定，严格履行保密义务，不得泄露相关秘密。

**Article 48** Supervision officers shall strictly implement the confidentiality system, control the scope and duration of knowledge of supervisory matters, and shall not privately retain, conceal, consult, excerpt, copy, or carry clues and related materials, and are strictly prohibited from disclosing supervisory work secrets.  
After leaving the post or resigning, supervision officers shall comply with the management regulations on the declassification period, strictly perform confidentiality obligations, and shall not disclose relevant secrets.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　监察官离任三年内，不得从事与监察和司法工作相关联且可能发生利益冲突的职业。  
监察官离任后，不得担任原任职监察机关办理案件的诉讼代理人或者辩护人，但是作为当事人的监护人或者近亲属代理诉讼、进行辩护的除外。  
监察官被开除后，不得担任诉讼代理人或者辩护人，但是作为当事人的监护人或者近亲属代理诉讼、进行辩护的除外。

**Article 49** Within three years after leaving office, a supervision officer shall not engage in occupations related to supervisory and judicial work that may create a conflict of interest.  
After leaving office, a supervision officer shall not serve as a litigation agent or defender in cases handled by the supervisory organ where he or she previously served, except when acting as a guardian or close relative of a party to represent the party in litigation or to defend.  
After being expelled, a supervision officer shall not serve as a litigation agent or defender, except when acting as a guardian or close relative of a party to represent the party in litigation or to defend.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　监察官应当遵守有关规范领导干部配偶、子女及其配偶经商办企业行为的规定。违反规定的，予以处理。

**Article 50** Supervision officers shall comply with the provisions on regulating the business activities of leading officials' spouses, children, and children's spouses. Those who violate the provisions shall be dealt with.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　监察官的配偶、父母、子女及其配偶不得以律师身份担任该监察官所任职监察机关办理案件的诉讼代理人、辩护人，或者提供其他有偿法律服务。

**Article 51** The spouse, parents, children and their spouses of a supervisory official shall not act as litigation agents or defenders in the capacity of lawyers, or provide other paid legal services, in cases handled by the supervisory organ where the supervisory official works.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　监察官有下列行为之一的，依法给予处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）贪污贿赂的；  
（二）不履行或者不正确履行监督职责，应当发现的问题没有发现，或者发现问题不报告、不处置，造成恶劣影响的；  
（三）未经批准、授权处置问题线索，发现重大案情隐瞒不报，或者私自留存、处理涉案材料的；  
（四）利用职权或者职务上的影响干预调查工作、以案谋私的；  
（五）窃取、泄露调查工作信息，或者泄露举报事项、举报受理情况以及举报人信息的；  
（六）隐瞒、伪造、变造、故意损毁证据、案件材料的；  
（七）对被调查人或者涉案人员逼供、诱供，或者侮辱、打骂、虐待、体罚、变相体罚的；  
（八）违反规定采取调查措施或者处置涉案财物的；  
（九）违反规定发生办案安全事故，或者发生安全事故后隐瞒不报、报告失实、处置不当的；  
（十）其他职务违法犯罪行为。  
监察官有其他违纪违法行为，影响监察官队伍形象，损害国家和人民利益的，依法追究相应责任。

**Article 52** A supervisory official who commits any of the following acts shall be subject to action according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) accepting bribes or engaging in embezzlement;  
(2) failing to perform or correctly perform supervisory duties, failing to discover problems that should have been discovered, or failing to report or handle problems discovered, resulting in adverse effects;  
(3) disposing of problem clues without approval or authorization, concealing and failing to report major case situations, or privately retaining or handling case-related materials;  
(4) using one's authority or influence of one's position to interfere with investigation work, or seeking personal gain from cases;  
(5) stealing or disclosing investigation work information, or disclosing reporting matters, reporting acceptance information, or information on whistleblowers;  
(6) concealing, falsifying, altering, or intentionally destroying evidence or case materials;  
(7) extorting confessions or inducing confessions from the person under investigation or related persons, or insulting, beating, abusing, physically punishing, or imposing disguised physical punishment;  
(8) taking investigation measures or disposing of case-related property in violation of provisions;  
(9) causing work safety accidents in case handling in violation of provisions, or concealing and failing to report, making false reports, or improperly handling after an accident occurs;  
(10) other duty-related illegal or criminal acts.  
If a supervisory official has other acts that violate discipline or law, affecting the image of the supervisory official team and harming the interests of the State and the people, corresponding liability shall be pursued according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　监察官涉嫌违纪违法，已经被立案审查、调查、侦查，不宜继续履行职责的，按照管理权限和规定的程序暂时停止其履行职务。

**Article 53** Where a supervisory official is suspected of violating discipline or law and has been placed on file for review, investigation, or criminal investigation, and is unsuitable to continue performing duties, the performance of the duties of the supervisory official shall be temporarily suspended in accordance with the authority and procedures provided.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　实行监察官责任追究制度，对滥用职权、失职失责造成严重后果的，终身追究责任或者进行问责。  
监察官涉嫌严重职务违法、职务犯罪或者对案件处置出现重大失误的，应当追究负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员的责任。

**Article 54** A system of accountability for supervisory officials shall be implemented, and supervisory officials who abuse their powers or are negligent in their duties, causing serious consequences, shall be held accountable for life or shall be subject to accountability.  
Where a supervisory official is suspected of serious duty-related illegal acts or duty-related crimes, or has committed a major error in case handling, the liability of the responsible leading officials and directly responsible persons shall be investigated.

### 第八章　监察官的职业保障

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　除下列情形外，不得将监察官调离：  
（一）按规定需要任职回避的；  
（二）按规定实行任职交流的；  
（三）因机构、编制调整需要调整工作的；  
（四）因违纪违法不适合继续从事监察工作的；  
（五）法律规定的其他情形。

**Article 55** A supervisory official shall not be transferred away except under the following circumstances:  
(1) where post avoidance is required according to provisions;  
(2) where rotational assignments are implemented according to provisions;  
(3) where work adjustment is required due to changes in the institution or staffing;  
(4) where the supervisory official is unsuitable to continue engaging in supervisory work due to violation of discipline or law; or  
(5) other circumstances provided by law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　任何单位或者个人不得要求监察官从事超出法定职责范围的事务。  
对任何干涉监察官依法履职的行为，监察官有权拒绝并予以全面如实记录和报告；有违纪违法情形的，由有关机关根据情节轻重追究有关人员的责任。

**Article 56** No entity or individual shall require a supervisory official to engage in matters that exceed the scope of statutory duties.  
For any act that interferes with a supervisory official's performance of duties according to law, the supervisory official shall have the right to refuse and shall fully and truthfully record and report it; if there are circumstances that violate discipline or law, the relevant organ shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, pursue the liability of the relevant persons.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　监察官的职业尊严和人身安全受法律保护。  
任何单位和个人不得对监察官及其近亲属打击报复。  
对监察官及其近亲属实施报复陷害、侮辱诽谤、暴力侵害、威胁恐吓、滋事骚扰等违法犯罪行为的，应当依法从严惩治。

**Article 57** The professional dignity and personal safety of supervisory officials shall be protected by law.  
No entity or individual shall retaliate against a supervisory official or his or her close relatives.  
Any illegal or criminal act such as retaliation, false accusation, insult, defamation, violent assault, threat, intimidation, or harassment against a supervisory official or his or her close relatives shall be severely punished according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　监察官因依法履行职责遭受不实举报、诬告陷害、侮辱诽谤，致使名誉受到损害的，监察机关应当会同有关部门及时澄清事实，消除不良影响，并依法追究相关单位或者个人的责任。

**Article 58** Where a supervisory official suffers false reporting, false accusation, insult, or defamation due to performing duties according to law, resulting in damage to his or her reputation, the supervisory organ shall, together with relevant departments, promptly clarify the facts and eliminate adverse effects, and shall pursue the liability of the relevant entity or individual according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　监察官因依法履行职责，本人及其近亲属人身安全面临危险的，监察机关、公安机关应当对监察官及其近亲属采取人身保护、禁止特定人员接触等必要保护措施。

**Article 59** Where a supervisory official, due to performing duties according to law, faces danger to the personal safety of himself or herself and his or her close relatives, the supervisory organ and the public security organ shall take necessary protective measures such as personal protection and prohibiting specific persons from approaching.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　监察官实行国家规定的工资制度，享受监察官等级津贴和其他津贴、补贴、奖金、保险、福利待遇。监察官的工资及等级津贴制度，由国家另行规定。

**Article 60** A supervisory official shall implement the salary system as provided by the State, and shall enjoy grade allowances for supervisory officials and other allowances, subsidies, bonuses, insurance, and welfare benefits. The salary and grade allowance system for supervisory officials shall be separately provided by the State.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　监察官因公致残的，享受国家规定的伤残待遇。监察官因公牺牲或者病故的，其亲属享受国家规定的抚恤和优待。

**Article 61** Where a supervisory official is disabled in the line of duty, he or she shall enjoy the disability benefits provided by the State. Where a supervisory official dies in the line of duty or dies of illness, his or her relatives shall enjoy the pensions and preferential treatment provided by the State.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　监察官退休后，享受国家规定的养老金和其他待遇。

**Article 62** After retirement, a supervisory official shall enjoy the pension and other benefits provided by the State.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　对于国家机关及其工作人员侵犯监察官权利的行为，监察官有权提出控告。  
受理控告的机关应当依法调查处理，并将调查处理结果及时告知本人。

**Article 63** For any act by a State organ or its staff that infringes upon the rights of a supervisory official, the supervisory official shall have the right to file a complaint.  
The organ that accepts the complaint shall investigate and handle the matter according to law, and shall promptly inform the supervisory official of the investigation and handling results.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　监察官对涉及本人的政务处分、处分和人事处理不服的，可以依照规定的程序申请复审、复核，提出申诉。

**Article 64** Where a supervisory official does not accept the disciplinary sanction, penalty, or personnel action concerning himself or herself, he or she may apply for review or reexamination, or lodge a petition in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对监察官的政务处分、处分或者人事处理错误的，应当及时予以纠正；造成名誉损害的，应当恢复名誉、消除影响、赔礼道歉；造成经济损失的，应当赔偿。对打击报复的直接责任人员，应当依法追究其责任。

**Article 65** Where a disciplinary sanction, penalty, or personnel action against a supervisory official is erroneous, it shall be corrected promptly; if reputation damage is caused, the reputation shall be restored, the adverse effects shall be eliminated, and an apology shall be made; if economic losses are caused, compensation shall be made. The direct responsible persons who have retaliated shall have their liability pursued according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有关监察官的权利、义务和管理制度，本法已有规定的，适用本法的规定；本法未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国公务员法》等法律法规的规定。

**Article 66** Where provisions on the rights, obligations, and management system of supervisory officials are already provided in this Law, the provisions of this Law shall apply; where this Law provides no provisions, the provisions of the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队的监察官制度，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 67** The system of supervisory officials of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be executed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法自2022年1月1日起施行。

**Article 68** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2022.
