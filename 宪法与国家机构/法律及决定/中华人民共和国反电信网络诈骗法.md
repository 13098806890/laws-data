# 中华人民共和国反电信网络诈骗法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2022-09-02  
**生效日期**：2022-12-01

> （2022年9月2日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十六次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　电信治理 第三章　金融治理 第四章　互联网治理 第五章　综合措施 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防、遏制和惩治电信网络诈骗活动，加强反电信网络诈骗工作，保护公民和组织的合法权益，维护社会稳定和国家安全，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称电信网络诈骗，是指以非法占有为目的，利用电信网络技术手段，通过远程、非接触等方式，诈骗公私财物的行为。 第三条　打击治理在中华人民共和国境内实施的电信网络诈骗活动或者中华人民共和国公民在境外实施的电信网络诈骗活动，适用本法。 境外的组织、个人针对中华人民共和国境内实施电信网络诈骗活动的，或者为他人针对境内实施电信网络诈骗活动提供产品、服务等帮助的，依照本法有关规定处理和追究责任。 第四条　反电信网络诈骗工作坚持以人民为中心，统筹发展和安全；坚持系统观念、法治思维，注重源头治理、综合治理；坚持齐抓共管、群防群治，全面落实打防管控各项措施，加强社会宣传教育防范；坚持精准防治，保障正常生产经营活动和群众生活便利。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防、遏制和惩治电信网络诈骗活动，加强反电信网络诈骗工作，保护公民和组织的合法权益，维护社会稳定和国家安全，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of preventing, curbing and punishing telecommunications and network fraud activities, strengthening the work against telecommunications and network fraud, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens and organizations, and maintaining social stability and national security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称电信网络诈骗，是指以非法占有为目的，利用电信网络技术手段，通过远程、非接触等方式，诈骗公私财物的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "telecommunications and network fraud" means an act of defrauding public or private property by means of telecommunications and network technologies, through remote, non-contact and other means, with the intent of illegal possession.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　打击治理在中华人民共和国境内实施的电信网络诈骗活动或者中华人民共和国公民在境外实施的电信网络诈骗活动，适用本法。  
境外的组织、个人针对中华人民共和国境内实施电信网络诈骗活动的，或者为他人针对境内实施电信网络诈骗活动提供产品、服务等帮助的，依照本法有关规定处理和追究责任。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to the combating and governance of telecommunications and network fraud activities carried out within the territory of the People's Republic of China or by citizens of the People's Republic of China outside the territory.  
Where an organization or individual outside the territory carries out telecommunications and network fraud activities against the territory of the People's Republic of China, or provides assistance such as products or services to others for carrying out telecommunications and network fraud activities against the territory, such organization or individual shall be dealt with and investigated for liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　反电信网络诈骗工作坚持以人民为中心，统筹发展和安全；坚持系统观念、法治思维，注重源头治理、综合治理；坚持齐抓共管、群防群治，全面落实打防管控各项措施，加强社会宣传教育防范；坚持精准防治，保障正常生产经营活动和群众生活便利。

**Article 4** The work against telecommunications and network fraud shall adhere to a people-centered approach, balancing development and security; adhere to a systematic concept and rule-of-law thinking, emphasizing source governance and comprehensive governance; adhere to joint management and mass prevention and mass governance, fully implement various measures of cracking down, prevention, control and management, and strengthen social publicity, education and prevention; adhere to targeted prevention and control, ensuring normal production and business activities and convenience for the people's lives.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　反电信网络诈骗工作应当依法进行，维护公民和组织的合法权益。  
有关部门和单位、个人应当对在反电信网络诈骗工作过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私、个人信息予以保密。

**Article 5** The work against telecommunications and network fraud shall be carried out in accordance with law, and the lawful rights and interests of citizens and organizations shall be safeguarded.  
Relevant departments, entities and individuals shall keep confidential any State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy and personal information that come to their knowledge in the course of the work against telecommunications and network fraud.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建立反电信网络诈骗工作机制，统筹协调打击治理工作。  
地方各级人民政府组织领导本行政区域内反电信网络诈骗工作，确定反电信网络诈骗目标任务和工作机制，开展综合治理。  
公安机关牵头负责反电信网络诈骗工作，金融、电信、网信、市场监管等有关部门依照职责履行监管主体责任，负责本行业领域反电信网络诈骗工作。  
人民法院、人民检察院发挥审判、检察职能作用，依法防范、惩治电信网络诈骗活动。  
电信业务经营者、银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、互联网服务提供者承担风险防控责任，建立反电信网络诈骗内部控制机制和安全责任制度，加强新业务涉诈风险安全评估。

**Article 6** The State Council shall establish a work mechanism against telecommunications and network fraud to coordinate the combating and governance work.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall organize and lead the work against telecommunications and network fraud within their respective administrative regions, determine the objectives, tasks and work mechanisms for anti-telecommunications and network fraud, and carry out comprehensive governance.  
Public security organs shall take the lead in the work against telecommunications and network fraud; the relevant departments such as finance, telecommunications, cyberspace administration, and market regulation shall, in accordance with their duties, assume the primary responsibility for supervision and be responsible for the work against telecommunications and network fraud in their respective industries and fields.  
People's courts and people's procuratorates shall perform their adjudicative and procuratorial functions, and prevent and punish telecommunications and network fraud activities in accordance with law.  
Telecommunications business operators, banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, and Internet service providers shall assume risk prevention and control responsibilities, establish internal control mechanisms and safety responsibility systems against telecommunications and network fraud, and strengthen safety assessment of fraud-related risks of new businesses.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有关部门、单位在反电信网络诈骗工作中应当密切协作，实现跨行业、跨地域协同配合、快速联动，加强专业队伍建设，有效打击治理电信网络诈骗活动。

**Article 7** Relevant departments and entities shall closely cooperate in the work against telecommunications and network fraud, achieve cross-industry and cross-regional coordination and rapid linkage, strengthen the building of professional teams, and effectively combat and govern telecommunications and network fraud activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当加强反电信网络诈骗宣传，普及相关法律和知识，提高公众对各类电信网络诈骗方式的防骗意识和识骗能力。  
教育行政、市场监管、民政等有关部门和村民委员会、居民委员会，应当结合电信网络诈骗受害群体的分布等特征，加强对老年人、青少年等群体的宣传教育，增强反电信网络诈骗宣传教育的针对性、精准性，开展反电信网络诈骗宣传教育进学校、进企业、进社区、进农村、进家庭等活动。  
各单位应当加强内部防范电信网络诈骗工作，对工作人员开展防范电信网络诈骗教育；个人应当加强电信网络诈骗防范意识。单位、个人应当协助、配合有关部门依照本法规定开展反电信网络诈骗工作。

**Article 8** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall strengthen publicity against telecommunications and network fraud, popularize relevant laws and knowledge, and enhance the public's awareness of fraud prevention and ability to identify various types of telecommunications and network fraud.  
Relevant departments such as education administration, market regulation, civil affairs, and villagers' committees and neighborhood committees shall, taking into account the distribution characteristics of victims of telecommunications and network fraud, strengthen publicity and education for groups such as the elderly and adolescents, enhance the targetedness and precision of anti-telecommunications and network fraud publicity and education, and conduct activities such as bringing anti-telecommunications and network fraud publicity and education into schools, enterprises, communities, rural areas, and households.  
Each entity shall strengthen its internal work on preventing telecommunications and network fraud, and shall provide education on fraud prevention to its staff; individuals shall enhance their awareness of telecommunications and network fraud prevention. Entities and individuals shall assist and cooperate with relevant departments in carrying out the work against telecommunications and network fraud in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### 第二章　电信治理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　电信业务经营者应当依法全面落实电话用户真实身份信息登记制度。  
基础电信企业和移动通信转售企业应当承担对代理商落实电话用户实名制管理责任，在协议中明确代理商实名制登记的责任和有关违约处置措施。

**Article 9** Telecommunications business operators shall fully implement the real identity information registration system for telephone subscribers in accordance with law.  
Basic telecommunications enterprises and mobile communication resale enterprises shall assume the responsibility for managing agents to implement the real-name system for telephone subscribers, and shall specify in the agreements the agents' responsibilities for real-name registration and the relevant measures for dealing with breach of contract.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　办理电话卡不得超出国家有关规定限制的数量。  
对经识别存在异常办卡情形的，电信业务经营者有权加强核查或者拒绝办卡。具体识别办法由国务院电信主管部门制定。  
国务院电信主管部门组织建立电话用户开卡数量核验机制和风险信息共享机制，并为用户查询名下电话卡信息提供便捷渠道。

**Article 10** The number of telephone cards applied for shall not exceed the limit prescribed by the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where an abnormal application for a telephone card is identified, the telecommunications business operator shall have the right to strengthen verification or refuse to issue the card. The specific identification method shall be formulated by the competent telecommunications department under the State Council.  
The competent telecommunications department under the State Council shall organize the establishment of a verification mechanism for the number of telephone cards opened by telephone subscribers and a risk information sharing mechanism, and shall provide convenient channels for subscribers to inquire about the information of telephone cards under their names.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　电信业务经营者对监测识别的涉诈异常电话卡用户应当重新进行实名核验，根据风险等级采取有区别的、相应的核验措施。对未按规定核验或者核验未通过的，电信业务经营者可以限制、暂停有关电话卡功能。

**Article 11** Telecommunications business operators shall re-conduct real-name verification on users of abnormal telephone cards identified through monitoring as being fraud-related, and shall take differentiated and corresponding verification measures according to the risk level. Where verification has not been carried out in accordance with the provisions or fails, the telecommunications business operator may restrict or suspend the functions of the relevant telephone card.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　电信业务经营者建立物联网卡用户风险评估制度，评估未通过的，不得向其销售物联网卡；严格登记物联网卡用户身份信息；采取有效技术措施限定物联网卡开通功能、使用场景和适用设备。  
单位用户从电信业务经营者购买物联网卡再将载有物联网卡的设备销售给其他用户的，应当核验和登记用户身份信息，并将销量、存量及用户实名信息传送给号码归属的电信业务经营者。  
电信业务经营者对物联网卡的使用建立监测预警机制。对存在异常使用情形的，应当采取暂停服务、重新核验身份和使用场景或者其他合同约定的处置措施。

**Article 12** Telecommunications business operators shall establish a risk assessment system for users of Internet of Things (IoT) cards. Where the assessment fails, they shall not sell IoT cards to such users; they shall strictly register the identity information of IoT card users; they shall take effective technical measures to limit the functions, use scenarios, and applicable devices of IoT cards.  
Where an entity user purchases IoT cards from a telecommunications business operator and then sells the devices equipped with IoT cards to other users, such entity user shall verify and register the identity information of the users and transmit the sales volume, existing stock and real-name information of users to the telecommunications business operator to which the numbers belong.  
Telecommunications business operators shall establish a monitoring and early warning mechanism for the use of IoT cards. Where abnormal use is found, they shall take disposal measures such as suspending service, re-verifying identity and use scenarios, or other measures agreed upon in the contract.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　电信业务经营者应当规范真实主叫号码传送和电信线路出租，对改号电话进行封堵拦截和溯源核查。  
电信业务经营者应当严格规范国际通信业务出入口局主叫号码传送，真实、准确向用户提示来电号码所属国家或者地区，对网内和网间虚假主叫、不规范主叫进行识别、拦截。

**Article 13** Telecommunications business operators shall regulate the transmission of true calling numbers and the leasing of telecommunications lines, and shall block, intercept and trace the source of number-altered calls.  
Telecommunications business operators shall strictly regulate the transmission of calling numbers at international communications gateway exchanges, truthfully and accurately indicate to users the country or region to which the calling number belongs, and identify and intercept false calling and non-compliant calling within and between networks.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位和个人不得非法制造、买卖、提供或者使用下列设备、软件：  
（一）电话卡批量插入设备；  
（二）具有改变主叫号码、虚拟拨号、互联网电话违规接入公用电信网络等功能的设备、软件；  
（三）批量账号、网络地址自动切换系统，批量接收提供短信验证、语音验证的平台；  
（四）其他用于实施电信网络诈骗等违法犯罪的设备、软件。  
电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当采取技术措施，及时识别、阻断前款规定的非法设备、软件接入网络，并向公安机关和相关行业主管部门报告。

**Article 14** No entity or individual shall illegally manufacture, trade, provide or use the following equipment or software:  
(1) equipment for batch insertion of telephone cards;  
(2) equipment or software with functions such as changing calling numbers, virtual dialing, or unauthorized access to public telecommunications networks via Internet telephony;  
(3) systems for automatically switching batch accounts and network addresses, and platforms for batch receipt and provision of SMS verification and voice verification;  
(4) other equipment or software used for carrying out illegal or criminal activities such as telecommunications and network fraud.  
Telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers shall take technical measures to promptly identify and block the access of illegal equipment or software specified in the preceding paragraph to the network, and shall report to the public security organs and relevant industry competent departments.

### 第三章　金融治理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构为客户开立银行账户、支付账户及提供支付结算服务，和与客户业务关系存续期间，应当建立客户尽职调查制度，依法识别受益所有人，采取相应风险管理措施，防范银行账户、支付账户等被用于电信网络诈骗活动。

**Article 15** Banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall, when opening bank accounts or payment accounts for customers, providing payment and settlement services, and during the period of business relationship with customers, establish a customer due diligence system, identify beneficial owners in accordance with law, take corresponding risk management measures, and prevent bank accounts, payment accounts, etc. from being used for telecommunications and network fraud activities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　开立银行账户、支付账户不得超出国家有关规定限制的数量。  
对经识别存在异常开户情形的，银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构有权加强核查或者拒绝开户。  
中国人民银行、国务院银行业监督管理机构组织有关清算机构建立跨机构开户数量核验机制和风险信息共享机制，并为客户提供查询名下银行账户、支付账户的便捷渠道。银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当按照国家有关规定提供开户情况和有关风险信息。相关信息不得用于反电信网络诈骗以外的其他用途。

**Article 16** The number of bank accounts or payment accounts opened shall not exceed the limit prescribed by the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where an abnormal account opening is identified, the banking financial institution or non-bank payment institution shall have the right to strengthen verification or refuse to open the account.  
The People's Bank of China and the banking regulatory authority under the State Council shall organize relevant clearing institutions to establish a cross-institution account opening number verification mechanism and a risk information sharing mechanism, and shall provide convenient channels for customers to inquire about bank accounts and payment accounts under their names. Banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide account opening status and relevant risk information. Such information shall not be used for purposes other than anti-telecommunications and network fraud.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当建立开立企业账户异常情形的风险防控机制。金融、电信、市场监管、税务等有关部门建立开立企业账户相关信息共享查询系统，提供联网核查服务。  
市场主体登记机关应当依法对企业实名登记履行身份信息核验职责；依照规定对登记事项进行监督检查，对可能存在虚假登记、涉诈异常的企业重点监督检查，依法撤销登记的，依照前款的规定及时共享信息；为银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构进行客户尽职调查和依法识别受益所有人提供便利。

**Article 17** Banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall establish risk prevention and control mechanisms for abnormal situations in opening enterprise accounts. The relevant departments such as finance, telecommunications, market regulation, and taxation shall establish a shared query system for information related to opening enterprise accounts and provide online verification services.  
Market entity registration authorities shall, in accordance with law, perform the duty of verifying identity information for real-name registration of enterprises; conduct supervision and inspection over registration matters in accordance with regulations, and conduct focused supervision and inspection over enterprises that may have false registration or fraud-related abnormalities; and where a registration is revoked in accordance with law, shall promptly share the information in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph; and provide convenience for banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions to conduct customer due diligence and identify beneficial owners in accordance with law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当对银行账户、支付账户及支付结算服务加强监测，建立完善符合电信网络诈骗活动特征的异常账户和可疑交易监测机制。  
中国人民银行统筹建立跨银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构的反洗钱统一监测系统，会同国务院公安部门完善与电信网络诈骗犯罪资金流转特点相适应的反洗钱可疑交易报告制度。  
对监测识别的异常账户和可疑交易，银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当根据风险情况，采取核实交易情况、重新核验身份、延迟支付结算、限制或者中止有关业务等必要的防范措施。  
银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构依照第一款规定开展异常账户和可疑交易监测时，可以收集异常客户互联网协议地址、网卡地址、支付受理终端信息等必要的交易信息、设备位置信息。上述信息未经客户授权，不得用于反电信网络诈骗以外的其他用途。

**Article 18** Banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall strengthen monitoring of bank accounts, payment accounts and payment and settlement services, and establish and improve monitoring mechanisms for abnormal accounts and suspicious transactions that are consistent with the characteristics of telecommunications and network fraud activities.  
The People's Bank of China shall coordinate the establishment of a unified anti-money laundering monitoring system across banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions, and shall, together with the public security department under the State Council, improve the anti-money laundering suspicious transaction reporting system that is suitable for the characteristics of the flow of funds in telecommunications and network fraud crimes.  
For abnormal accounts and suspicious transactions identified through monitoring, banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall, based on the risk situation, take necessary preventive measures such as verifying transaction details, re-verifying identity, delaying payment and settlement, and restricting or suspending relevant businesses.  
When carrying out monitoring of abnormal accounts and suspicious transactions in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1, banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions may collect necessary transaction information and equipment location information such as the abnormal customer's Internet Protocol address, network card address, and payment acceptance terminal information. The foregoing information shall not be used for purposes other than anti-telecommunications and network fraud without the customer's authorization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当按照国家有关规定，完整、准确传输直接提供商品或者服务的商户名称、收付款客户名称及账号等交易信息，保证交易信息的真实、完整和支付全流程中的一致性。

**Article 19** Banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, completely and accurately transmit transaction information such as the name of the merchant directly providing goods or services, the names and account numbers of paying and receiving customers, and shall ensure the authenticity and completeness of transaction information and consistency throughout the entire payment process.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院公安部门会同有关部门建立完善电信网络诈骗涉案资金即时查询、紧急止付、快速冻结、及时解冻和资金返还制度，明确有关条件、程序和救济措施。  
公安机关依法决定采取上述措施的，银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构应当予以配合。

**Article 20** The public security department under the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, establish and improve a system for immediate inquiry, emergency payment suspension, rapid freezing, timely unfreezing and return of funds involved in telecommunications and network fraud, and shall specify relevant conditions, procedures and remedies.  
Where a public security organ decides to take the above measures in accordance with law, banking financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions shall cooperate.

### 第四章　互联网治理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者为用户提供下列服务，在与用户签订协议或者确认提供服务时，应当依法要求用户提供真实身份信息，用户不提供真实身份信息的，不得提供服务：  
（一）提供互联网接入服务；  
（二）提供网络代理等网络地址转换服务；  
（三）提供互联网域名注册、服务器托管、空间租用、云服务、内容分发服务；  
（四）提供信息、软件发布服务，或者提供即时通讯、网络交易、网络游戏、网络直播发布、广告推广服务。

**Article 21** When providing the following services to users and when entering into an agreement with the user or confirming the provision of services, telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers shall, in accordance with law, require the user to provide true identity information; if the user fails to provide true identity information, they shall not provide the services:  
(1) providing Internet access services;  
(2) providing network address translation services such as network proxy;  
(3) providing Internet domain name registration, server hosting, space rental, cloud services, content delivery services;  
(4) providing information and software publishing services, or providing instant messaging, online trading, online gaming, online live streaming publishing, or advertising promotion services.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　互联网服务提供者对监测识别的涉诈异常账号应当重新核验，根据国家有关规定采取限制功能、暂停服务等处置措施。  
互联网服务提供者应当根据公安机关、电信主管部门要求，对涉案电话卡、涉诈异常电话卡所关联注册的有关互联网账号进行核验，根据风险情况，采取限期改正、限制功能、暂停使用、关闭账号、禁止重新注册等处置措施。

**Article 22** Internet service providers shall re-verify accounts identified through monitoring as being fraud-related and abnormal, and shall take disposal measures such as restricting functions or suspending services in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Internet service providers shall, at the request of public security organs or telecommunications competent departments, verify the relevant Internet accounts registered in connection with telephone cards involved in cases or fraud-related abnormal telephone cards, and shall, based on the risk situation, take disposal measures such as ordering correction within a time limit, restricting functions, suspending use, closing accounts, or prohibiting re-registration.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　设立移动互联网应用程序应当按照国家有关规定向电信主管部门办理许可或者备案手续。  
为应用程序提供封装、分发服务的，应当登记并核验应用程序开发运营者的真实身份信息，核验应用程序的功能、用途。  
公安、电信、网信等部门和电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当加强对分发平台以外途径下载传播的涉诈应用程序重点监测、及时处置。

**Article 23** The establishment of a mobile Internet application shall go through the procedures of licensing or filing with the telecommunications competent department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Whoever provides packaging or distribution services for applications shall register and verify the true identity information of the application developer and operator, and verify the functions and purposes of the application.  
Public security, telecommunications, cyberspace administration and other departments, as well as telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers, shall strengthen key monitoring and timely disposal of fraud-related applications downloaded and disseminated through channels other than distribution platforms.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　提供域名解析、域名跳转、网址链接转换服务的，应当按照国家有关规定，核验域名注册、解析信息和互联网协议地址的真实性、准确性，规范域名跳转，记录并留存所提供相应服务的日志信息，支持实现对解析、跳转、转换记录的溯源。

**Article 24** Whoever provides domain name resolution, domain name redirection, or URL link conversion services shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, verify the authenticity and accuracy of domain name registration, resolution information and Internet Protocol addresses, regulate domain name redirection, record and retain log information of the corresponding services provided, and support the traceability of resolution, redirection and conversion records.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何单位和个人不得为他人实施电信网络诈骗活动提供下列支持或者帮助：  
（一）出售、提供个人信息；  
（二）帮助他人通过虚拟货币交易等方式洗钱；  
（三）其他为电信网络诈骗活动提供支持或者帮助的行为。  
电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当依照国家有关规定，履行合理注意义务，对利用下列业务从事涉诈支持、帮助活动进行监测识别和处置：  
（一）提供互联网接入、服务器托管、网络存储、通讯传输、线路出租、域名解析等网络资源服务；  
（二）提供信息发布或者搜索、广告推广、引流推广等网络推广服务；  
（三）提供应用程序、网站等网络技术、产品的制作、维护服务；  
（四）提供支付结算服务。

**Article 25** No entity or individual shall provide the following support or assistance to others in carrying out telecommunications and network fraud activities:  
(1) selling or providing personal information;  
(2) helping others to launder money through virtual currency transactions or other means;  
(3) other acts of providing support or assistance for telecommunications and network fraud activities.  
Telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, perform reasonable care obligations, and monitor, identify and dispose of the use of the following businesses to engage in fraud-related support or assistance activities:  
(1) providing network resource services such as Internet access, server hosting, network storage, communication transmission, line leasing, and domain name resolution;  
(2) providing online promotion services such as information publishing or search, advertising promotion, and traffic promotion;  
(3) providing development and maintenance services for network technologies or products such as applications and websites;  
(4) providing payment and settlement services.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公安机关办理电信网络诈骗案件依法调取证据的，互联网服务提供者应当及时提供技术支持和协助。  
互联网服务提供者依照本法规定对有关涉诈信息、活动进行监测时，发现涉诈违法犯罪线索、风险信息的，应当依照国家有关规定，根据涉诈风险类型、程度情况移送公安、金融、电信、网信等部门。有关部门应当建立完善反馈机制，将相关情况及时告知移送单位。

**Article 26** Where a public security organ, in handling a telecommunications and network fraud case, lawfully obtains evidence, the Internet service provider shall promptly provide technical support and assistance.  
Where an Internet service provider, when monitoring relevant fraud-related information or activities in accordance with the provisions of this Law, discovers clues or risk information concerning fraud-related illegal or criminal activities, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, transfer such information to the public security, financial, telecommunications, cyberspace administration and other departments based on the type and severity of the fraud-related risk. Relevant departments shall establish and improve a feedback mechanism, and promptly inform the transferring unit of the relevant situation.

### 第五章　综合措施

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公安机关应当建立完善打击治理电信网络诈骗工作机制，加强专门队伍和专业技术建设，各警种、各地公安机关应当密切配合，依法有效惩处电信网络诈骗活动。  
公安机关接到电信网络诈骗活动的报案或者发现电信网络诈骗活动，应当依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定立案侦查。

**Article 27** Public security organs shall establish and improve work mechanisms for combating and governing telecommunications and network fraud, strengthen the building of specialized teams and professional techniques, and all police branches and public security organs across different regions shall closely cooperate and effectively punish telecommunications and network fraud activities in accordance with law.  
Upon receiving a report of a telecommunications and network fraud activity or discovering such an activity, a public security organ shall file a case for investigation in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　金融、电信、网信部门依照职责对银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者落实本法规定情况进行监督检查。有关监督检查活动应当依法规范开展。

**Article 28** The financial, telecommunications, and cyberspace administration departments shall, in accordance with their duties, supervise and inspect the implementation of the provisions of this Law by banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, telecommunications business operators, and Internet service providers. Relevant supervision and inspection activities shall be carried out in a standardized manner in accordance with law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　个人信息处理者应当依照《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》等法律规定，规范个人信息处理，加强个人信息保护，建立个人信息被用于电信网络诈骗的防范机制。  
履行个人信息保护职责的部门、单位对可能被电信网络诈骗利用的物流信息、交易信息、贷款信息、医疗信息、婚介信息等实施重点保护。公安机关办理电信网络诈骗案件，应当同时查证犯罪所利用的个人信息来源，依法追究相关人员和单位责任。

**Article 29** Personal information processors shall, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other legal provisions, regulate the processing of personal information, strengthen the protection of personal information, and establish a prevention mechanism against personal information being used for telecommunications and network fraud.  
Departments and entities responsible for personal information protection shall provide key protection for logistics information, transaction information, loan information, medical information, matchmaking information, and other information that may be exploited for telecommunications and network fraud. When handling telecommunications and network fraud cases, public security organs shall simultaneously investigate the source of personal information used for the crime and investigate the liability of relevant individuals and entities in accordance with law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　电信业务经营者、银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、互联网服务提供者应当对从业人员和用户开展反电信网络诈骗宣传，在有关业务活动中对防范电信网络诈骗作出提示，对本领域新出现的电信网络诈骗手段及时向用户作出提醒，对非法买卖、出租、出借本人有关卡、账户、账号等被用于电信网络诈骗的法律责任作出警示。  
新闻、广播、电视、文化、互联网信息服务等单位，应当面向社会有针对性地开展反电信网络诈骗宣传教育。  
任何单位和个人有权举报电信网络诈骗活动，有关部门应当依法及时处理，对提供有效信息的举报人依照规定给予奖励和保护。

**Article 30** Telecommunications business operators, banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, and Internet service providers shall carry out anti-telecommunications and network fraud publicity among their employees and users, provide tips on preventing telecommunications and network fraud in relevant business activities, promptly remind users of new telecommunications and network fraud methods emerging in their respective fields, and warn of the legal liability for illegal sale, lease or lending of one's own relevant cards, accounts, accounts, etc. being used for telecommunications and network fraud.  
News, radio, television, culture, Internet information services and other entities shall conduct targeted anti-telecommunications and network fraud publicity and education for the public.  
Any entity or individual shall have the right to report telecommunications and network fraud activities, and relevant departments shall promptly handle such reports in accordance with law, and shall reward and protect whistleblowers who provide valid information in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人不得非法买卖、出租、出借电话卡、物联网卡、电信线路、短信端口、银行账户、支付账户、互联网账号等，不得提供实名核验帮助；不得假冒他人身份或者虚构代理关系开立上述卡、账户、账号等。  
对经设区的市级以上公安机关认定的实施前款行为的单位、个人和相关组织者，以及因从事电信网络诈骗活动或者关联犯罪受过刑事处罚的人员，可以按照国家有关规定记入信用记录，采取限制其有关卡、账户、账号等功能和停止非柜面业务、暂停新业务、限制入网等措施。对上述认定和措施有异议的，可以提出申诉，有关部门应当建立健全申诉渠道、信用修复和救济制度。具体办法由国务院公安部门会同有关主管部门规定。

**Article 31** No entity or individual shall illegally buy, sell, lease or lend telephone cards, IoT cards, telecommunications lines, SMS ports, bank accounts, payment accounts, Internet accounts, etc., or provide real-name verification assistance; or impersonate another person or fabricate an agency relationship to open the aforementioned cards, accounts, accounts, etc.  
For entities, individuals and relevant organizers that have engaged in the acts specified in the preceding paragraph as determined by a public security organ at or above the city divided into districts level, and for persons who have been subjected to criminal punishment for engaging in telecommunications and network fraud activities or related crimes, such persons may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, be recorded in the credit record, and measures such as restricting the functions of relevant cards, accounts, accounts, etc., and suspending non-counter business, suspending new businesses, and restricting network access may be taken. Where there is any objection to the aforesaid determination and measures, a petition may be lodged, and the relevant departments shall establish and improve petition channels, credit repair and relief systems. Specific measures shall be stipulated by the public security department under the State Council together with the relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家支持电信业务经营者、银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、互联网服务提供者研究开发有关电信网络诈骗反制技术，用于监测识别、动态封堵和处置涉诈异常信息、活动。  
国务院公安部门、金融管理部门、电信主管部门和国家网信部门等应当统筹负责本行业领域反制技术措施建设，推进涉电信网络诈骗样本信息数据共享，加强涉诈用户信息交叉核验，建立有关涉诈异常信息、活动的监测识别、动态封堵和处置机制。  
依据[本法第十一条](#art-11)、第十二条、第十八条、第二十二条和前款规定，对涉诈异常情形采取限制、暂停服务等处置措施的，应当告知处置原因、救济渠道及需要提交的资料等事项，被处置对象可以向作出决定或者采取措施的部门、单位提出申诉。作出决定的部门、单位应当建立完善申诉渠道，及时受理申诉并核查，核查通过的，应当即时解除有关措施。

**Article 32** The State supports telecommunications business operators, banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, and Internet service providers in researching and developing relevant anti-telecommunications and network fraud technologies for monitoring, identifying, dynamically blocking and disposing of fraud-related abnormal information and activities.  
The public security department, the financial regulatory department, the telecommunications competent department, the national cyberspace administration and other departments under the State Council shall coordinate the construction of anti-fraud technical measures in their respective industries and fields, promote the sharing of sample information and data related to telecommunications and network fraud, strengthen cross-verification of fraud-related user information, and establish mechanisms for monitoring, identifying, dynamically blocking and disposing of relevant fraud-related abnormal information and activities.  
Where, in accordance with Articles 11, 12, 18, 22 and the preceding paragraph of this Law, disposal measures such as restriction or suspension of services are taken for fraud-related abnormal situations, the reasons for the disposal, channels for relief, and materials to be submitted shall be notified. The party subject to the disposal may lodge a petition with the department or entity that made the decision or took the measures. The department or entity that made the decision shall establish and improve petition channels, promptly accept and verify petitions; if the verification is passed, the relevant measures shall be immediately lifted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家推进网络身份认证公共服务建设，支持个人、企业自愿使用，电信业务经营者、银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、互联网服务提供者对存在涉诈异常的电话卡、银行账户、支付账户、互联网账号，可以通过国家网络身份认证公共服务对用户身份重新进行核验。

**Article 33** The State shall promote the construction of public services for network identity authentication, and support individuals and enterprises in voluntarily using them. Telecommunications business operators, banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, and Internet service providers may re-verify the identity of users of telephone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, and Internet accounts that are subject to fraud-related abnormalities through the national network identity authentication public service.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　公安机关应当会同金融、电信、网信部门组织银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者等建立预警劝阻系统，对预警发现的潜在被害人，根据情况及时采取相应劝阻措施。对电信网络诈骗案件应当加强追赃挽损，完善涉案资金处置制度，及时返还被害人的合法财产。对遭受重大生活困难的被害人，符合国家有关救助条件的，有关方面依照规定给予救助。

**Article 34** Public security organs shall, together with the financial, telecommunications and cyberspace administration departments, organize banking financial institutions, non-bank payment institutions, telecommunications business operators, Internet service providers, etc. to establish an early warning and dissuasion system, and shall, based on the situation, promptly take corresponding dissuasion measures for potential victims identified through early warning. For telecommunications and network fraud cases, efforts shall be strengthened to recover stolen goods and losses, the system for disposing of funds involved in cases shall be improved, and the lawful property of victims shall be promptly returned. For victims suffering from significant living difficulties who meet the relevant conditions of State assistance, relevant parties shall provide assistance in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经国务院反电信网络诈骗工作机制决定或者批准，公安、金融、电信等部门对电信网络诈骗活动严重的特定地区，可以依照国家有关规定采取必要的临时风险防范措施。

**Article 35** Upon decision or approval by the work mechanism against telecommunications and network fraud of the State Council, the public security, financial, telecommunications and other departments may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, take necessary temporary risk prevention measures in specific regions where telecommunications and network fraud activities are severe.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对前往电信网络诈骗活动严重地区的人员，出境活动存在重大涉电信网络诈骗活动嫌疑的，移民管理机构可以决定不准其出境。  
因从事电信网络诈骗活动受过刑事处罚的人员，设区的市级以上公安机关可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，决定自处罚完毕之日起六个月至三年以内不准其出境，并通知移民管理机构执行。

**Article 36** For persons going to regions where telecommunications and network fraud activities are severe, if there is a significant suspicion that their exit activities involve telecommunications and network fraud, the immigration administration authority may decide not to allow them to exit the country.  
For persons who have been subjected to criminal punishment for engaging in telecommunications and network fraud activities, a public security organ at or above the city divided into districts level may, based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent recidivism, decide not to allow them to exit the country for a period of six months to three years from the date the punishment is completed, and shall notify the immigration administration authority for enforcement.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院公安部门等会同外交部门加强国际执法司法合作，与有关国家、地区、国际组织建立有效合作机制，通过开展国际警务合作等方式，提升在信息交流、调查取证、侦查抓捕、追赃挽损等方面的合作水平，有效打击遏制跨境电信网络诈骗活动。

**Article 37** The public security department under the State Council, together with the foreign affairs department, shall strengthen international law enforcement and judicial cooperation, establish effective cooperation mechanisms with relevant countries, regions and international organizations, and enhance the level of cooperation in information exchange, investigation and evidence collection, investigation and arrest, recovery of stolen goods and losses, etc., through international police cooperation and other means, so as to effectively combat and curb cross-border telecommunications and network fraud activities.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　组织、策划、实施、参与电信网络诈骗活动或者为电信网络诈骗活动提供帮助，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
前款行为尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处十日以上十五日以下拘留；没收违法所得，处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一万元的，处十万元以下罚款。

**Article 38** Whoever organizes, plans, carries out or participates in telecommunications and network fraud activities, or provides assistance for such activities, if constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with law.  
Where the act specified in the preceding paragraph does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose detention of not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; confiscate illegal gains; and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains, or if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　电信业务经营者违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由有关主管部门责令改正，情节较轻的，给予警告、通报批评，或者处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，并可以由有关主管部门责令暂停相关业务、停业整顿、吊销相关业务许可证或者吊销营业执照，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处一万元以上二十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未落实国家有关规定确定的反电信网络诈骗内部控制机制的；  
（二）未履行电话卡、物联网卡实名制登记职责的；  
（三）未履行对电话卡、物联网卡的监测识别、监测预警和相关处置职责的；  
（四）未对物联网卡用户进行风险评估，或者未限定物联网卡的开通功能、使用场景和适用设备的；  
（五）未采取措施对改号电话、虚假主叫或者具有相应功能的非法设备进行监测处置的。

**Article 39** Where a telecommunications business operator violates the provisions of this Law and falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant competent department shall order correction; if the circumstances are relatively minor, it shall give a warning, circulate a notice of criticism, or impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and may also order the suspension of relevant businesses, business consolidation, revocation of relevant business permits or revocation of the business license, and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) failing to implement the internal control mechanism against telecommunications and network fraud as determined by the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) failing to perform the duty of real-name registration for telephone cards and IoT cards;  
(3) failing to perform the duties of monitoring, identification, early warning and relevant disposal of telephone cards and IoT cards;  
(4) failing to conduct risk assessment on IoT card users, or failing to limit the functions, use scenarios and applicable devices of IoT cards;  
(5) failing to take measures to monitor and dispose of number-altered calls, false calling numbers, or illegal equipment with corresponding functions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由有关主管部门责令改正，情节较轻的，给予警告、通报批评，或者处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，并可以由有关主管部门责令停止新增业务、缩减业务类型或者业务范围、暂停相关业务、停业整顿、吊销相关业务许可证或者吊销营业执照，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处一万元以上二十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未落实国家有关规定确定的反电信网络诈骗内部控制机制的；  
（二）未履行尽职调查义务和有关风险管理措施的；  
（三）未履行对异常账户、可疑交易的风险监测和相关处置义务的；  
（四）未按照规定完整、准确传输有关交易信息的。

**Article 40** Where a banking financial institution or non-bank payment institution violates the provisions of this Law and falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant competent department shall order correction; if the circumstances are relatively minor, it shall give a warning, circulate a notice of criticism, or impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and may also order the cessation of new businesses, reduction of business types or business scope, suspension of relevant businesses, business consolidation, revocation of relevant business permits or revocation of the business license, and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) failing to implement the internal control mechanism against telecommunications and network fraud as determined by the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) failing to perform the duty of due diligence and relevant risk management measures;  
(3) failing to perform the obligation of risk monitoring and relevant disposal of abnormal accounts and suspicious transactions;  
(4) failing to completely and accurately transmit relevant transaction information in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由有关主管部门责令改正，情节较轻的，给予警告、通报批评，或者处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，并可以由有关主管部门责令暂停相关业务、停业整顿、关闭网站或者应用程序、吊销相关业务许可证或者吊销营业执照，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处一万元以上二十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未落实国家有关规定确定的反电信网络诈骗内部控制机制的；  
（二）未履行网络服务实名制职责，或者未对涉案、涉诈电话卡关联注册互联网账号进行核验的；  
（三）未按照国家有关规定，核验域名注册、解析信息和互联网协议地址的真实性、准确性，规范域名跳转，或者记录并留存所提供相应服务的日志信息的；  
（四）未登记核验移动互联网应用程序开发运营者的真实身份信息或者未核验应用程序的功能、用途，为其提供应用程序封装、分发服务的；  
（五）未履行对涉诈互联网账号和应用程序，以及其他电信网络诈骗信息、活动的监测识别和处置义务的；  
（六）拒不依法为查处电信网络诈骗犯罪提供技术支持和协助，或者未按规定移送有关违法犯罪线索、风险信息的。

**Article 41** Where a telecommunications business operator or Internet service provider violates the provisions of this Law and falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant competent department shall order correction; if the circumstances are relatively minor, it shall give a warning, circulate a notice of criticism, or impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and may also order the suspension of relevant businesses, business consolidation, closure of the website or application, revocation of relevant business permits or revocation of the business license, and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) failing to implement the internal control mechanism against telecommunications and network fraud as determined by the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) failing to perform the duty of real-name system for network services, or failing to verify Internet accounts registered in connection with telephone cards involved in cases or fraud-related abnormal telephone cards;  
(3) failing to verify the authenticity and accuracy of domain name registration, resolution information and Internet Protocol addresses, regulate domain name redirection, or record and retain log information of the corresponding services provided in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) failing to register and verify the true identity information of mobile Internet application developers and operators, or failing to verify the functions and purposes of applications, and providing them with application packaging or distribution services;  
(5) failing to perform the obligation of monitoring, identifying and disposing of fraud-related Internet accounts and applications, and other telecommunications and network fraud information or activities;  
(6) refusing to provide technical support and assistance for investigating and punishing telecommunications and network fraud crimes in accordance with law, or failing to transfer relevant clues or risk information of illegal or criminal activities as required.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反[本法第十四条](#art-14)、第二十五条第一款规定的，没收违法所得，由公安机关或者有关主管部门处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，处五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，由公安机关并处十五日以下拘留。

**Article 42** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 14 or paragraph 1 of Article 25 of this Law shall have the illegal gains confiscated, and the public security organ or the relevant competent department shall impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains, or if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the public security organ shall also impose detention of not more than fifteen days.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第二款规定，由有关主管部门责令改正，情节较轻的，给予警告、通报批评，或者处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，并可以由有关主管部门责令暂停相关业务、停业整顿、关闭网站或者应用程序，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处一万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 43** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 25 of this Law shall be ordered by the relevant competent department to make correction; if the circumstances are relatively minor, a warning, a notice of criticism, or a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the relevant competent department may also order the suspension of relevant businesses, business consolidation, closure of the website or application, and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第一款规定的，没收违法所得，由公安机关处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二万元的，处二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处十五日以下拘留。

**Article 44** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 31 of this Law shall have the illegal gains confiscated, and the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains, or if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a detention of not more than fifteen days shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　反电信网络诈骗工作有关部门、单位的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者有其他违反本法规定行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where a staff member of a relevant department or entity in the work against telecommunications and network fraud abuses power, neglects duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or commits any other act in violation of the provisions of this Law, if constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　组织、策划、实施、参与电信网络诈骗活动或者为电信网络诈骗活动提供相关帮助的违法犯罪人员，除依法承担刑事责任、行政责任以外，造成他人损害的，依照《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律的规定承担民事责任。  
电信业务经营者、银行业金融机构、非银行支付机构、互联网服务提供者等违反本法规定，造成他人损害的，依照《中华人民共和国民法典》等法律的规定承担民事责任。

**Article 46** Where a person who organizes, plans, carries out or participates in telecommunications and network fraud activities or provides relevant assistance for such activities, in addition to bearing criminal liability or administrative liability in accordance with law, causes damage to others, such person shall bear civil liability in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other laws.  
Where a telecommunications business operator, banking financial institution, non-bank payment institution, Internet service provider, etc. violates the provisions of this Law and causes damage to others, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other laws.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　人民检察院在履行反电信网络诈骗职责中，对于侵害国家利益和社会公共利益的行为，可以依法向人民法院提起公益诉讼。

**Article 47** Where, in performing its duties against telecommunications and network fraud, a people's procuratorate discovers any act that infringes upon the national interest or the public interest, it may initiate a public interest lawsuit with the people's court in accordance with law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有关单位和个人对依照本法作出的行政处罚和行政强制措施决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 48** Where a relevant entity or individual disagrees with a decision on administrative penalty or an administrative compulsory measure made in accordance with this Law, it or he may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action in accordance with law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　反电信网络诈骗工作涉及的有关管理和责任制度，本法没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国网络安全法》、《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》、《中华人民共和国反洗钱法》等相关法律规定。

**Article 49** Where this Law does not provide for the relevant management and liability systems involved in the work against telecommunications and network fraud, the provisions of the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Anti-Money Laundering Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws shall apply.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自2022年12月1日起施行。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force on December 1, 2022.
