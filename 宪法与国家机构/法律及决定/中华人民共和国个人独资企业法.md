# 中华人民共和国个人独资企业法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1999-08-30  
**生效日期**：2000-01-01

> （1999年8月30日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　个人独资企业的设立 第三章　个人独资企业的投资人及事务管理 第四章　个人独资企业的解散和清算 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范个人独资企业的行为，保护个人独资企业投资人和债权人的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济的发展，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称个人独资企业，是指依照本法在中国境内设立，由一个自然人投资，财产为投资人个人所有，投资人以其个人财产对企业债务承担无限责任的经营实体。 第三条　个人独资企业以其主要办事机构所在地为住所。 第四条　个人独资企业从事经营活动必须遵守法律、行政法规，遵守诚实信用原则，不得损害社会公共利益。 个人独资企业应当依法履行纳税义务。 第五条　国家依法保护个人独资企业的财产和其他合法权益。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范个人独资企业的行为，保护个人独资企业投资人和债权人的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济的发展，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of regulating the conduct of sole proprietorship enterprises, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of investors and creditors of such enterprises, maintaining the social and economic order, and promoting the development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称个人独资企业，是指依照本法在中国境内设立，由一个自然人投资，财产为投资人个人所有，投资人以其个人财产对企业债务承担无限责任的经营实体。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, a "sole proprietorship enterprise" refers to an operational entity established within the territory of China in accordance with this Law, invested in by a single natural person, whose property is owned by the investor, and who bears unlimited liability for the enterprise's debts with his personal property.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　个人独资企业以其主要办事机构所在地为住所。

**Article 3** The domicile of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall be the location of its principal office.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　个人独资企业从事经营活动必须遵守法律、行政法规，遵守诚实信用原则，不得损害社会公共利益。  
个人独资企业应当依法履行纳税义务。

**Article 4** A sole proprietorship enterprise shall, in its business activities, comply with laws and administrative regulations, adhere to the principle of good faith, and shall not harm the public interest.  
A sole proprietorship enterprise shall fulfill its tax obligations according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家依法保护个人独资企业的财产和其他合法权益。

**Article 5** The State shall protect the property and other legitimate rights and interests of sole proprietorship enterprises according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　个人独资企业应当依法招用职工。职工的合法权益受法律保护。  
个人独资企业职工依法建立工会，工会依法开展活动。

**Article 6** A sole proprietorship enterprise shall recruit employees according to law. The legitimate rights and interests of employees shall be protected by law.  
Employees of a sole proprietorship enterprise may establish a trade union according to law, and the trade union shall conduct its activities according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在个人独资企业中的中国共产党党员依照中国共产党章程进行活动。

**Article 7** Members of the Communist Party of China in a sole proprietorship enterprise shall conduct their activities in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China.

### 第二章　个人独资企业的设立

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立个人独资企业应当具备下列条件：  
（一）投资人为一个自然人；  
（二）有合法的企业名称；  
（三）有投资人申报的出资；  
（四）有固定的生产经营场所和必要的生产经营条件；  
（五）有必要的从业人员。

**Article 8** The establishment of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the investor is a single natural person;  
(2) there is a lawful enterprise name;  
(3) there is capital contribution declared by the investor;  
(4) there are fixed business premises and necessary business conditions; and  
(5) there are necessary employees.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立个人独资企业，应当由投资人或者其委托的代理人向个人独资企业所在地的登记机关提交设立申请书、投资人身份证明、生产经营场所使用证明等文件。委托代理人申请设立登记时，应当出具投资人的委托书和代理人的合法证明。  
个人独资企业不得从事法律、行政法规禁止经营的业务；从事法律、行政法规规定须报经有关部门审批的业务，应当在申请设立登记时提交有关部门的批准文件。

**Article 9** To apply for the establishment of a sole proprietorship enterprise, the investor or his authorized agent shall submit to the registration authority at the location of the enterprise the application for establishment, the identity certificate of the investor, the certificate of use of the business premises, and other documents. Where an authorized agent applies for establishment registration, he shall produce the power of attorney from the investor and the legal certificate of the agent.  
A sole proprietorship enterprise shall not engage in any business prohibited by laws or administrative regulations; where it engages in any business which, according to laws or administrative regulations, requires approval from the relevant department, it shall submit the approval document from the relevant department when applying for establishment registration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　个人独资企业设立申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）企业的名称和住所；  
（二）投资人的姓名和居所；  
（三）投资人的出资额和出资方式；  
（四）经营范围。

**Article 10** The application for establishment of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall state the following matters:  
(1) the name and domicile of the enterprise;  
(2) the name and residence of the investor;  
(3) the amount and method of capital contribution of the investor; and  
(4) the scope of business.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　个人独资企业的名称应当与其责任形式及从事的营业相符合。

**Article 11** The name of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall be consistent with its form of liability and the business it engages in.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　登记机关应当在收到设立申请文件之日起十五日内，对符合本法规定条件的，予以登记，发给营业执照；对不符合本法规定条件的，不予登记，并应当给予书面答复，说明理由。

**Article 12** The registration authority shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the application documents for establishment, register those that meet the conditions prescribed in this Law, and issue a business license; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall not register and shall give a written reply stating the reasons.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　个人独资企业的营业执照的签发日期，为个人独资企业成立日期。  
在领取个人独资企业营业执照前，投资人不得以个人独资企业名义从事经营活动。

**Article 13** The date of issuance of the business license of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall be the date of its establishment.  
Before obtaining the business license, the investor shall not engage in business activities in the name of the sole proprietorship enterprise.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　个人独资企业设立分支机构，应当由投资人或者其委托的代理人向分支机构所在地的登记机关申请登记，领取营业执照。  
分支机构经核准登记后，应将登记情况报该分支机构隶属的个人独资企业的登记机关备案。  
分支机构的民事责任由设立该分支机构的个人独资企业承担。

**Article 14** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise establishes a branch, the investor or his authorized agent shall apply for registration to the registration authority at the location of the branch and obtain a business license.  
After the branch is approved and registered, the registration status shall be reported to the registration authority of the sole proprietorship enterprise to which the branch belongs for archival purposes.  
The civil liability of the branch shall be borne by the sole proprietorship enterprise that established the branch.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　个人独资企业存续期间登记事项发生变更的，应当在作出变更决定之日起的十五日内依法向登记机关申请办理变更登记。

**Article 15** Where the registered items of a sole proprietorship enterprise change during its existence, it shall apply to the registration authority for modification registration according to law within 15 days from the date of the decision to make the change.

### 第三章　个人独资企业的投资人及事务管理

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　法律、行政法规禁止从事营利性活动的人，不得作为投资人申请设立个人独资企业。

**Article 16** Any person who is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations from engaging in profit-making activities shall not apply to establish a sole proprietorship enterprise as an investor.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　个人独资企业投资人对本企业的财产依法享有所有权，其有关权利可以依法进行转让或继承。

**Article 17** The investor of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall enjoy ownership of the property of the enterprise according to law, and the related rights may be transferred or inherited according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　个人独资企业投资人在申请企业设立登记时明确以其家庭共有财产作为个人出资的，应当依法以家庭共有财产对企业债务承担无限责任。

**Article 18** Where the investor specifies at the time of applying for enterprise establishment registration that his family's common property is used as personal capital contribution, he shall bear unlimited liability for the enterprise's debts with the family's common property according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　个人独资企业投资人可以自行管理企业事务，也可以委托或者聘用其他具有民事行为能力的人负责企业的事务管理。  
投资人委托或者聘用他人管理个人独资企业事务，应当与受托人或者被聘用的人签订书面合同，明确委托的具体内容和授予的权利范围。  
受托人或者被聘用的人员应当履行诚信、勤勉义务，按照与投资人签订的合同负责个人独资企业的事务管理。  
投资人对受托人或者被聘用的人员职权的限制，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 19** The investor of a sole proprietorship enterprise may manage the enterprise's affairs himself, or may entrust or employ another person with civil capacity to be responsible for the management of the enterprise's affairs.  
Where the investor entrusts or employs another person to manage the affairs of the sole proprietorship enterprise, he shall enter into a written contract with the trustee or the employed person, specifying the specific contents of the entrustment and the scope of authority granted.  
The trustee or the employed person shall fulfill the duties of good faith and diligence, and shall be responsible for managing the affairs of the sole proprietorship enterprise in accordance with the contract signed with the investor.  
The investor's restrictions on the authority of the trustee or the employed person shall not be set up against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　投资人委托或者聘用的管理个人独资企业事务的人员不得有下列行为：  
（一）利用职务上的便利，索取或者收受贿赂；  
（二）利用职务或者工作上的便利侵占企业财产；  
（三）挪用企业的资金归个人使用或者借贷给他人；  
（四）擅自将企业资金以个人名义或者以他人名义开立帐户储存；  
（五）擅自以企业财产提供担保；  
（六）未经投资人同意，从事与本企业相竞争的业务；  
（七）未经投资人同意，同本企业订立合同或者进行交易；  
（八）未经投资人同意，擅自将企业商标或者其他知识产权转让给他人使用；  
（九）泄露本企业的商业秘密；  
（十）法律、行政法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 20** A person entrusted or employed by the investor to manage the affairs of the sole proprietorship enterprise shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) soliciting or accepting bribes by taking advantage of his position;  
(2) misappropriating the property of the enterprise by taking advantage of his position or work;  
(3) diverting the funds of the enterprise for personal use or lending them to others;  
(4) opening an account in his own name or in another person's name to deposit the funds of the enterprise without authorization;  
(5) providing a guarantee with the property of the enterprise without authorization;  
(6) engaging in a business competing with the enterprise without the consent of the investor;  
(7) entering into a contract or conducting a transaction with the enterprise without the consent of the investor;  
(8) transferring the trademark or other intellectual property of the enterprise to others for use without the consent of the investor;  
(9) disclosing the trade secrets of the enterprise; or  
(10) other acts prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　个人独资企业应当依法设置会计帐簿，进行会计核算。

**Article 21** A sole proprietorship enterprise shall set up accounting books and conduct financial accounting according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　个人独资企业招用职工的，应当依法与职工签订劳动合同，保障职工的劳动安全，按时、足额发放职工工资。

**Article 22** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise recruits employees, it shall enter into labor contracts with the employees according to law, ensure the labor safety of the employees, and pay the employees' wages in full and on time.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　个人独资企业应当按照国家规定参加社会保险，为职工缴纳社会保险费。

**Article 23** A sole proprietorship enterprise shall participate in social insurance in accordance with state regulations and pay social insurance premiums for its employees.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　个人独资企业可以依法申请贷款、取得土地使用权，并享有法律、行政法规规定的其他权利。

**Article 24** A sole proprietorship enterprise may apply for loans, obtain the right to use land according to law, and enjoy other rights prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何单位和个人不得违反法律、行政法规的规定，以任何方式强制个人独资企业提供财力、物力、人力；对于违法强制提供财力、物力、人力的行为，个人独资企业有权拒绝。

**Article 25** No unit or individual shall, in violation of the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, compel a sole proprietorship enterprise to provide financial, material, or human resources in any manner; a sole proprietorship enterprise shall have the right to refuse any such illegal compulsion.

### 第四章　个人独资企业的解散和清算

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　个人独资企业有下列情形之一时，应当解散：  
（一）投资人决定解散；  
（二）投资人死亡或者被宣告死亡，无继承人或者继承人决定放弃继承；  
（三）被依法吊销营业执照；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 26** A sole proprietorship enterprise shall be dissolved under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the investor decides to dissolve it;  
(2) the investor dies or is declared dead, and there is no successor or the successor decides to renounce the succession;  
(3) the business license is revoked according to law; or  
(4) other circumstances prescribed by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　个人独资企业解散，由投资人自行清算或者由债权人申请人民法院指定清算人进行清算。  
投资人自行清算的，应当在清算前十五日内书面通知债权人，无法通知的，应当予以公告。债权人应当在接到通知之日起三十日内，未接到通知的应当在公告之日起六十日内，向投资人申报其债权。

**Article 27** The dissolution of a sole proprietorship enterprise shall be liquidated by the investor himself or by a liquidator designated by the People's Court upon application by the creditors.  
Where the investor liquidates the enterprise himself, he shall notify the creditors in writing within 15 days before the liquidation, or if notification is impossible, he shall make a public announcement. Creditors shall declare their claims to the investor within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice, or within 60 days from the date of the announcement if they have not received notice.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　个人独资企业解散后，原投资人对个人独资企业存续期间的债务仍应承担偿还责任，但债权人在五年内未向债务人提出偿债请求的，该责任消灭。

**Article 28** After the dissolution of a sole proprietorship enterprise, the original investor shall still bear the liability for repayment of debts incurred during the existence of the enterprise, but such liability shall be extinguished if the creditors do not make a claim for repayment within five years.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　个人独资企业解散的，财产应当按照下列顺序清偿：  
（一）所欠职工工资和社会保险费用；  
（二）所欠税款；  
（三）其他债务。

**Article 29** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise is dissolved, its property shall be liquidated in the following order:  
(1) wages and social insurance premiums owed to employees;  
(2) taxes owed; and  
(3) other debts.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　清算期间，个人独资企业不得开展与清算目的无关的经营活动。在按前条规定清偿债务前，投资人不得转移、隐匿财产。

**Article 30** During the liquidation period, the sole proprietorship enterprise shall not engage in business activities unrelated to the purpose of liquidation. Before settling debts in accordance with the provisions of the preceding article, the investor shall not transfer or conceal property.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　个人独资企业财产不足以清偿债务的，投资人应当以其个人的其他财产予以清偿。&thinsp;<sup>[1]</sup>

**Article 31** Where the property of a sole proprietorship enterprise is insufficient to settle its debts, the investor shall settle them with his other personal property.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　个人独资企业清算结束后，投资人或者人民法院指定的清算人应当编制清算报告，并于十五日内到登记机关办理注销登记。

**Article 32** After the completion of the liquidation, the investor or the liquidator designated by the People's Court shall prepare a liquidation report and go through the cancellation registration with the registration authority within 15 days.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本法规定，提交虚假文件或采取其他欺骗手段，取得企业登记的，责令改正，处以五千元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处吊销营业执照。

**Article 33** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, submits false documents or adopts other fraudulent means to obtain enterprise registration shall be ordered to make corrections and be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked concurrently.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本法规定，个人独资企业使用的名称与其在登记机关登记的名称不相符合的，责令限期改正，处以二千元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Where the name used by a sole proprietorship enterprise does not match the name registered with the registration authority in violation of the provisions of this Law, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit and be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　涂改、出租、转让营业执照的，责令改正，没收违法所得，处以三千元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。  
伪造营业执照的，责令停业，没收违法所得，处以五千元以下的罚款。构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Whoever alters, leases, or transfers the business license shall be ordered to make corrections, confiscated of illegal gains, and imposed a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.  
Whoever forges the business license shall be ordered to suspend business, confiscated of illegal gains, and imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　个人独资企业成立后无正当理由超过六个月未开业的，或者开业后自行停业连续六个月以上的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 36** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise, without justifiable reasons, fails to commence business for more than six months after its establishment, or suspends business on its own for six consecutive months or more after commencing business, its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本法规定，未领取营业执照，以个人独资企业名义从事经营活动的，责令停止经营活动，处以三千元以下的罚款。  
个人独资企业登记事项发生变更时，未按本法规定办理有关变更登记的，责令限期办理变更登记；逾期不办理的，处以二千元以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Whoever engages in business activities in the name of a sole proprietorship enterprise without obtaining a business license in violation of the provisions of this Law shall be ordered to cease business activities and be imposed a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan.  
Where the registered items of a sole proprietorship enterprise change but it fails to go through the relevant modification registration in accordance with this Law, it shall be ordered to go through the modification registration within a time limit; if it fails to do so within the time limit, it shall be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　投资人委托或者聘用的人员管理个人独资企业事务时违反双方订立的合同，给投资人造成损害的，承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 38** Where a person entrusted or employed by the investor to manage the affairs of the sole proprietorship enterprise violates the contract concluded between the parties and causes damage to the investor, he shall bear civil compensation liability.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　个人独资企业违反本法规定，侵犯职工合法权益，未保障职工劳动安全，不缴纳社会保险费用的，按照有关法律、行政法规予以处罚，并追究有关责任人员的责任。

**Article 39** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise, in violation of the provisions of this Law, infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of employees, fails to ensure labor safety of employees, or fails to pay social insurance premiums, it shall be punished in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the responsible persons shall be held accountable.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　投资人委托或者聘用的人员违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)规定，侵犯个人独资企业财产权益的，责令退还侵占的财产；给企业造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where a person entrusted or employed by the investor, in violation of the provisions of Article 20 of this Law, infringes upon the property rights and interests of the sole proprietorship enterprise, he shall be ordered to return the property misappropriated; if losses are caused to the enterprise, he shall bear compensation liability according to law; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反法律、行政法规的规定强制个人独资企业提供财力、物力、人力的，按照有关法律、行政法规予以处罚，并追究有关责任人员的责任。

**Article 41** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, compels a sole proprietorship enterprise to provide financial, material, or human resources shall be punished in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the responsible persons shall be held accountable.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　个人独资企业及其投资人在清算前或清算期间隐匿或转移财产，逃避债务的，依法追回其财产，并按照有关规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a sole proprietorship enterprise or its investor conceals or transfers property before or during liquidation to evade debts, the property shall be recovered according to law, and the enterprise or investor shall be punished in accordance with relevant regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　投资人违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金，其财产不足以支付的，或者被判处没收财产的，应当先承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Where an investor, in violation of the provisions of this Law, shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines or penalties, and his property is insufficient to pay all such amounts, or if he is sentenced to confiscation of property, he shall first bear civil compensation liability.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　登记机关对不符合本法规定条件的个人独资企业予以登记，或者对符合本法规定条件的企业不予登记的，对直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where a registration authority registers a sole proprietorship enterprise that does not meet the conditions prescribed in this Law, or fails to register an enterprise that meets such conditions, the directly responsible person shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　登记机关的上级部门的有关主管人员强令登记机关对不符合本法规定条件的企业予以登记，或者对符合本法规定条件的企业不予登记的，或者对登记机关的违法登记行为进行包庇的，对直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where a competent person in the superior department of a registration authority orders the registration authority to register an enterprise that does not meet the conditions prescribed in this Law, or to fail to register an enterprise that meets such conditions, or covers up the illegal registration act of the registration authority, the directly responsible person shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　登记机关对符合法定条件的申请不予登记或者超过法定时限不予答复的，当事人可依法申请行政复议或提起行政诉讼。

**Article 46** Where a registration authority fails to register an application that meets the statutory conditions or fails to reply within the statutory time limit, the party may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate administrative action according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　外商独资企业不适用本法。

**Article 47** This Law shall not apply to enterprises with foreign investment as sole proprietorship enterprises.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本法自2000年1月1日起施行。

**Article 48** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2000.
