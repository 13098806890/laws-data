### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护社会治安秩序，保障公共安全，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，规范和保障公安机关及其人民警察依法履行治安管理职责，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of maintaining public security order, ensuring public safety, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons and other organizations, and regulating and safeguarding the performance of public security administration duties by public security organs and their people's police according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　治安管理工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持综合治理。  
各级人民政府应当加强社会治安综合治理，采取有效措施，预防和化解社会矛盾纠纷，增进社会和谐，维护社会稳定。

**Article 2** Public security administration work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China and adhere to comprehensive governance.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen comprehensive governance of public security, take effective measures to prevent and resolve social conflicts and disputes, promote social harmony, and maintain social stability.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　扰乱公共秩序，妨害公共安全，侵犯人身权利、财产权利，妨害社会管理，具有社会危害性，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》的规定构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由公安机关依照本法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 3** Anyone who disrupts public order, endangers public safety, infringes upon personal rights or property rights, or obstructs social management, if such act is socially harmful and constitutes a crime under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not yet constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　治安管理处罚的程序，适用本法的规定；本法没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》、《中华人民共和国行政强制法》的有关规定。

**Article 4** The procedures for public security administration penalties shall be governed by the provisions of this Law; where this Law does not provide, the relevant provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在中华人民共和国领域内发生的违反治安管理行为，除法律有特别规定的外，适用本法。  
在中华人民共和国船舶和航空器内发生的违反治安管理行为，除法律有特别规定的外，适用本法。  
在外国船舶和航空器内发生的违反治安管理行为，依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约，中华人民共和国行使管辖权的，适用本法。

**Article 5** This Law shall apply to acts violating public security administration that occur within the territory of the People's Republic of China, except as otherwise provided by law.  
This Law shall apply to acts violating public security administration that occur on board a vessel or aircraft of the People's Republic of China, except as otherwise provided by law.  
Where acts violating public security administration occur on board a foreign vessel or aircraft, and the People's Republic of China exercises jurisdiction in accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　治安管理处罚必须以事实为依据，与违反治安管理的事实、性质、情节以及社会危害程度相当。  
实施治安管理处罚，应当公开、公正，尊重和保障人权，保护公民的人格尊严。  
办理治安案件应当坚持教育与处罚相结合的原则，充分释法说理，教育公民、法人或者其他组织自觉守法。

**Article 6** Public security administration penalties shall be based on facts and be commensurate with the nature, circumstances, and degree of social harm of the act violating public security administration.  
The imposition of public security administration penalties shall be open and fair, respect and safeguard human rights, and protect the personal dignity of citizens.  
In handling public security cases, the principle of combining education with punishment shall be adhered to, with full explanation of the law and reasoning, to educate citizens, legal persons, or other organizations to voluntarily abide by the law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院公安部门负责全国的治安管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关负责本行政区域内的治安管理工作。  
治安案件的管辖由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 7** The public security department of the State Council shall be responsible for public security administration work nationwide. The public security organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for public security administration work within their respective administrative regions.  
The jurisdiction of public security cases shall be determined by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　违反治安管理行为对他人造成损害的，除依照本法给予治安管理处罚外，行为人或者其监护人还应当依法承担民事责任。  
违反治安管理行为构成犯罪，应当依法追究刑事责任的，不得以治安管理处罚代替刑事处罚。

**Article 8** Where an act violating public security administration causes harm to another person, in addition to being given a public security administration penalty in accordance with this Law, the actor or his guardian shall also bear civil liability according to law.  
Where an act violating public security administration constitutes a crime and criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, no public security administration penalty shall be imposed in lieu of criminal punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对于因民间纠纷引起的打架斗殴或者损毁他人财物等违反治安管理行为，情节较轻的，公安机关可以调解处理。  
调解处理治安案件，应当查明事实，并遵循合法、公正、自愿、及时的原则，注重教育和疏导，促进化解矛盾纠纷。  
经公安机关调解，当事人达成协议的，不予处罚。经调解未达成协议或者达成协议后不履行的，公安机关应当依照本法的规定对违反治安管理行为作出处理，并告知当事人可以就民事争议依法向人民法院提起民事诉讼。  
对属于第一款规定的调解范围的治安案件，公安机关作出处理决定前，当事人自行和解或者经人民调解委员会调解达成协议并履行，书面申请经公安机关认可的，不予处罚。

**Article 9** For acts violating public security administration such as fighting or damaging another person's property arising from civil disputes, if the circumstances are minor, the public security organ may mediate.  
When mediating a public security case, the facts shall be ascertained, and the principles of legality, fairness, voluntariness, and timeliness shall be followed, with emphasis on education and guidance to promote the resolution of conflicts and disputes.  
Where the parties reach an agreement through mediation by the public security organ, no penalty shall be imposed. Where no agreement is reached through mediation or the agreement is not performed after being reached, the public security organ shall handle the act violating public security administration in accordance with the provisions of this Law and inform the parties that they may initiate a civil action in the People's Court regarding the civil dispute according to law.  
For public security cases that fall within the scope of mediation as provided in paragraph 1, if, before the public security organ makes a handling decision, the parties reach a settlement on their own or reach an agreement through mediation by the people's mediation committee and perform it, and submit a written application recognized by the public security organ, no penalty shall be imposed.

### 第二章　处罚的种类和适用

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　治安管理处罚的种类分为：  
（一）警告；  
（二）罚款；  
（三）行政拘留；  
（四）吊销公安机关发放的许可证件。  
对违反治安管理的外国人，可以附加适用限期出境或者驱逐出境。

**Article 10** The types of public security administration penalties are:  
(1) Warning;  
(2) Fine;  
(3) Administrative detention;  
(4) Revocation of permits and licenses issued by public security organs.  
For foreigners who violate public security administration, a supplementary penalty of restricted entry or deportation may be imposed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　办理治安案件所查获的毒品、淫秽物品等违禁品，赌具、赌资，吸食、注射毒品的用具以及直接用于实施违反治安管理行为的本人所有的工具，应当收缴，按照规定处理。  
违反治安管理所得的财物，追缴退还被侵害人；没有被侵害人的，登记造册，公开拍卖或者按照国家有关规定处理，所得款项上缴国库。

**Article 11** Narcotics, obscene articles, and other prohibited items, gambling equipment and gambling funds, implements for inhaling or injecting narcotics, and tools owned by the perpetrator and directly used for committing acts violating public security administration that are seized in handling public security cases shall be confiscated and disposed of according to regulations.  
Property obtained from acts violating public security administration shall be recovered and returned to the victim; if there is no victim, it shall be registered, publicly auctioned or disposed of in accordance with relevant state regulations, and the proceeds shall be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　已满十四周岁不满十八周岁的人违反治安管理的，从轻或者减轻处罚；不满十四周岁的人违反治安管理的，不予处罚，但是应当责令其监护人严加管教。

**Article 12** Where a person who has reached the age of 14 but not the age of 18 violates public security administration, a lighter or mitigated punishment shall be imposed; where a person under the age of 14 violates public security administration, no penalty shall be imposed, but the guardian shall be ordered to strictly discipline him.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　精神病人、智力残疾人在不能辨认或者不能控制自己行为的时候违反治安管理的，不予处罚，但是应当责令其监护人加强看护管理和治疗。间歇性的精神病人在精神正常的时候违反治安管理的，应当给予处罚。尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人、智力残疾人违反治安管理的，应当给予处罚，但是可以从轻或者减轻处罚。

**Article 13** Where a mental patient or an intellectually disabled person violates public security administration when he is unable to recognize or control his own conduct, no penalty shall be imposed, but his guardian shall be ordered to strengthen care, management, and treatment. Where an intermittent mental patient violates public security administration when he is in a normal mental state, a penalty shall be imposed. Where a mental patient or an intellectually disabled person who has not completely lost the ability to recognize or control his own conduct violates public security administration, a penalty shall be imposed, but a lighter or mitigated punishment may be given.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　盲人或者又聋又哑的人违反治安管理的，可以从轻、减轻或者不予处罚。

**Article 14** Where a blind person or a person who is both deaf and mute violates public security administration, a lighter, mitigated, or no penalty may be imposed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　醉酒的人违反治安管理的，应当给予处罚。  
醉酒的人在醉酒状态中，对本人有危险或者对他人的人身、财产或者公共安全有威胁的，应当对其采取保护性措施约束至酒醒。

**Article 15** Where a drunk person violates public security administration, a penalty shall be imposed.  
Where a drunk person, while intoxicated, poses a danger to himself or threatens the person, property, or public safety of others, protective measures shall be taken to restrain him until he sobers up.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有两种以上违反治安管理行为的，分别决定，合并执行处罚。行政拘留处罚合并执行的，最长不超过二十日。

**Article 16** Where there are two or more acts violating public security administration, separate decisions shall be made and the penalties shall be executed concurrently. Where administrative detention penalties are executed concurrently, the maximum term shall not exceed 20 days.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　共同违反治安管理的，根据行为人在违反治安管理行为中所起的作用，分别处罚。  
教唆、胁迫、诱骗他人违反治安管理的，按照其教唆、胁迫、诱骗的行为处罚。

**Article 17** Where two or more persons jointly violate public security administration, they shall be penalized separately according to the role each played in the act.  
Where a person instigates, coerces, or induces another to violate public security administration, he shall be penalized according to the act he instigated, coerced, or induced.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　单位违反治安管理的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照本法的规定处罚。其他法律、行政法规对同一行为规定给予单位处罚的，依照其规定处罚。

**Article 18** Where a unit violates public security administration, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of this Law. Where other laws or administrative regulations provide for penalties against the unit for the same act, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with those provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　为了免受正在进行的不法侵害而采取的制止行为，造成损害的，不属于违反治安管理行为，不受处罚；制止行为明显超过必要限度，造成较大损害的，依法给予处罚，但是应当减轻处罚；情节较轻的，不予处罚。

**Article 19** A stop act taken to avoid an ongoing unlawful infringement, if it causes damage, shall not be considered an act violating public security administration and shall not be penalized; if the stop act clearly exceeds the necessary limit and causes relatively significant damage, a penalty shall be imposed according to law, but the punishment shall be mitigated; if the circumstances are minor, no penalty shall be imposed.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反治安管理有下列情形之一的，从轻、减轻或者不予处罚：  
（一）情节轻微的；  
（二）主动消除或者减轻违法后果的；  
（三）取得被侵害人谅解的；  
（四）出于他人胁迫或者诱骗的；  
（五）主动投案，向公安机关如实陈述自己的违法行为的；  
（六）有立功表现的。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances of a violation of public security administration, a lighter, mitigated, or no penalty shall be imposed:  
(1) The circumstances are minor;  
(2) The actor proactively eliminates or mitigates the illegal consequences;  
(3) The actor obtains understanding from the victim;  
(4) The actor is coerced or induced by another person;  
(5) The actor voluntarily surrenders and truthfully states his illegal conduct to the public security organ;  
(6) The actor has performed meritorious services.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　违反治安管理行为人自愿向公安机关如实陈述自己的违法行为，承认违法事实，愿意接受处罚的，可以依法从宽处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/拘留所条例.md#art-12" title="被《拘留所条例》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 21** A person who violates public security administration and voluntarily makes a truthful statement to the public security organ about his illegal act, admits the illegal fact, and is willing to accept punishment may be dealt with leniently according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反治安管理有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）有较严重后果的；  
（二）教唆、胁迫、诱骗他人违反治安管理的；  
（三）对报案人、控告人、举报人、证人打击报复的；  
（四）一年以内曾受过治安管理处罚的。

**Article 22** Where a person violates public security administration under any of the following circumstances, he shall be given a severe punishment:  
(1) where relatively serious consequences have occurred;  
(2) where he instigates, coerces, or deceives others into violating public security administration;  
(3) where he retaliates against a reporter, accuser, informant, or witness;  
(4) where he has previously been subjected to a public security administration punishment within one year.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反治安管理行为人有下列情形之一，依照本法应当给予行政拘留处罚的，不执行行政拘留处罚：  
（一）已满十四周岁不满十六周岁的；  
（二）已满十六周岁不满十八周岁，初次违反治安管理的；  
（三）七十周岁以上的；  
（四）怀孕或者哺乳自己不满一周岁婴儿的。  
前款第一项、第二项、第三项规定的行为人违反治安管理情节严重、影响恶劣的，或者第一项、第三项规定的行为人在一年以内二次以上违反治安管理的，不受前款规定的限制。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国军事设施保护法.md#art-60" title="被《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》第60条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Where a person who violates public security administration has any of the following circumstances and shall be given an administrative detention punishment according to this Law, the administrative detention punishment shall not be enforced:  
(1) where he has reached the age of 14 but not the age of 16;  
(2) where he has reached the age of 16 but not the age of 18 and violates public security administration for the first time;  
(3) where he is 70 years of age or older;  
(4) where she is pregnant or breastfeeding her own infant under one year old.  
Where the actor specified in subparagraph (1), (2), or (3) of the preceding paragraph violates public security administration with serious circumstances and adverse impact, or the actor specified in subparagraph (1) or (3) of the preceding paragraph violates public security administration twice or more within one year, the restriction of the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对依照[本法第十二条](#art-12)规定不予处罚或者依照[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定不执行行政拘留处罚的未成年人，公安机关依照《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》的规定采取相应矫治教育等措施。

**Article 24** With respect to a minor who is not punished according to Article 12 of this Law or whose administrative detention punishment is not enforced according to Article 23 of this Law, the public security organ shall take corresponding corrective education and other measures in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Prevention of Juvenile Delinquency.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反治安管理行为在六个月以内没有被公安机关发现的，不再处罚。  
前款规定的期限，从违反治安管理行为发生之日起计算；违反治安管理行为有连续或者继续状态的，从行为终了之日起计算。

**Article 25** Where an act violating public security administration is not discovered by the public security organ within six months, no punishment shall be imposed.  
The period prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be counted from the date on which the act violating public security administration occurs; where the act violating public security administration is of a continuous or continuing status, it shall be counted from the date on which the act ends.

### 第三章　违反治安管理的行为和处罚

#### 第一节　扰乱公共秩序的行为和处罚

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有下列行为之一的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）扰乱机关、团体、企业、事业单位秩序，致使工作、生产、营业、医疗、教学、科研不能正常进行，尚未造成严重损失的；  
（二）扰乱车站、港口、码头、机场、商场、公园、展览馆或者其他公共场所秩序的；  
（三）扰乱公共汽车、电车、城市轨道交通车辆、火车、船舶、航空器或者其他公共交通工具上的秩序的；  
（四）非法拦截或者强登、扒乘机动车、船舶、航空器以及其他交通工具，影响交通工具正常行驶的；  
（五）破坏依法进行的选举秩序的。  
聚众实施前款行为的，对首要分子处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款。

**Article 26** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) disrupting the order of state organs, social organizations, enterprises, or institutions, thereby hindering the normal conduct of work, production, business, medical care, teaching, or scientific research, without causing serious losses;  
(2) disrupting the order of railway stations, ports, docks, airports, shopping malls, parks, exhibition halls, or other public places;  
(3) disrupting the order on buses, trams, urban rail transit vehicles, trains, ships, aircraft, or other public transport vehicles;  
(4) illegally intercepting or forcibly boarding or clinging on motor vehicles, ships, aircraft, or other means of transport, affecting their normal operation;  
(5) disrupting the order of elections conducted according to law.  
Where a crowd gathers to commit any of the acts prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the ringleader shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在法律、行政法规规定的国家考试中，有下列行为之一，扰乱考试秩序的，处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一千元的，处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十五日以下拘留：  
（一）组织作弊的；  
（二）为他人组织作弊提供作弊器材或者其他帮助的；  
（三）为实施考试作弊行为，向他人非法出售、提供考试试题、答案的；  
（四）代替他人或者让他人代替自己参加考试的。

**Article 27** : In a national examination prescribed by laws or administrative regulations, any person who commits any of the following acts and disrupts the examination order shall be imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 1,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than fifteen days:  
(1) organizing cheating;  
(2) providing cheating equipment or other assistance to others for organizing cheating;  
(3) illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers to others for the purpose of cheating in the examination;  
(4) taking the examination on behalf of another person or having another person take the examination on one's behalf.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列行为之一，扰乱体育、文化等大型群众性活动秩序的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）强行进入场内的；  
（二）违反规定，在场内燃放烟花爆竹或者其他物品的；  
（三）展示侮辱性标语、条幅等物品的；  
（四）围攻裁判员、运动员或者其他工作人员的；  
（五）向场内投掷杂物，不听制止的；  
（六）扰乱大型群众性活动秩序的其他行为。  
因扰乱体育比赛、文艺演出活动秩序被处以拘留处罚的，可以同时责令其六个月至一年以内不得进入体育场馆、演出场馆观看同类比赛、演出；违反规定进入体育场馆、演出场馆的，强行带离现场，可以处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国军事设施保护法.md#art-61" title="被《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》第61条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 28** : Any person who commits any of the following acts, disrupting the order of large-scale mass activities such as sports or cultural events, shall be given a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) forcibly entering the venue;  
(2) setting off fireworks, firecrackers, or other items in the venue in violation of regulations;  
(3) displaying insulting slogans, banners, or other items;  
(4) surrounding referees, athletes, or other staff members;  
(5) throwing debris into the venue despite being ordered to stop;  
(6) other acts disrupting the order of large-scale mass activities.  
If a person is detained for disrupting the order of sports events or performances, he may at the same time be ordered not to enter stadiums or performance venues to watch similar events or performances for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year; if he enters such venues in violation of the order, he shall be forcibly taken away from the site, and may be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款：  
（一）故意散布谣言，谎报险情、疫情、灾情、警情或者以其他方法故意扰乱公共秩序的；  
（二）投放虚假的爆炸性、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性物质或者传染病病原体等危险物质扰乱公共秩序的；  
（三）扬言实施放火、爆炸、投放危险物质等危害公共安全犯罪行为扰乱公共秩序的。

**Article 29** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) intentionally spreading rumors, falsely reporting dangerous situations, epidemic situations, disasters, or alarms, or intentionally disrupting public order by other means;  
(2) distributing dangerous substances such as fake explosive, toxic, radioactive, corrosive substances or infectious disease pathogens to disrupt public order;  
(3) threatening to commit arson, explosion, or distribution of dangerous substances or other acts endangering public security to disrupt public order.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款：  
（一）结伙斗殴或者随意殴打他人的；  
（二）追逐、拦截他人的；  
（三）强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物的；  
（四）其他无故侵扰他人、扰乱社会秩序的寻衅滋事行为。

**Article 30** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan:  
(1) gang fighting or randomly beating others;  
(2) chasing or intercepting others;  
(3) forcibly taking or arbitrarily damaging or occupying public or private property;  
(4) other acts of picking quarrels and provoking trouble that unreasonably disturb others and disrupt social order.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）组织、教唆、胁迫、诱骗、煽动他人从事邪教活动、会道门活动、非法的宗教活动或者利用邪教组织、会道门、迷信活动，扰乱社会秩序、损害他人身体健康的；  
（二）冒用宗教、气功名义进行扰乱社会秩序、损害他人身体健康活动的；  
（三）制作、传播宣扬邪教、会道门内容的物品、信息、资料的。

**Article 31** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) organizing, instigating, coercing, inducing, or inciting others to engage in cult activities, sect activities, illegal religious activities, or using cult organizations, sects, or superstitious activities to disrupt social order or harm the physical health of others;  
(2) carrying out activities by falsely using the name of religion or Qigong to disrupt social order or harm the physical health of others;  
(3) producing or disseminating articles, information, or materials that propagate the contents of cults or sects.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反国家规定，有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留；情节严重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留：  
（一）故意干扰无线电业务正常进行的；  
（二）对正常运行的无线电台（站）产生有害干扰，经有关主管部门指出后，拒不采取有效措施消除的；  
（三）未经批准设置无线电广播电台、通信基站等无线电台（站）的，或者非法使用、占用无线电频率，从事违法活动的。

**Article 32** : Any person who, in violation of state regulations, commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days:  
(1) intentionally interfering with the normal operation of radio services;  
(2) causing harmful interference to the normal operation of radio stations (including base stations), and after being pointed out by the relevant competent authority, refusing to take effective measures to eliminate the interference;  
(3) setting up radio broadcasting stations, communication base stations, or other radio stations without approval, or illegally using or occupying radio frequencies to engage in illegal activities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有下列行为之一，造成危害的，处五日以下拘留；情节较重的，处五日以上十五日以下拘留：  
（一）违反国家规定，侵入计算机信息系统或者采用其他技术手段，获取计算机信息系统中存储、处理或者传输的数据，或者对计算机信息系统实施非法控制的；  
（二）违反国家规定，对计算机信息系统功能进行删除、修改、增加、干扰的；  
（三）违反国家规定，对计算机信息系统中存储、处理、传输的数据和应用程序进行删除、修改、增加的；  
（四）故意制作、传播计算机病毒等破坏性程序的；  
（五）提供专门用于侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具，或者明知他人实施侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的违法犯罪行为而为其提供程序、工具的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国军事设施保护法.md#art-62" title="被《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》第62条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** : Any person who commits any of the following acts, causing harm, shall be detained for not more than five days; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than fifteen days:  
(1) in violation of state regulations, intruding into a computer information system or using other technical means to obtain data stored, processed, or transmitted in the computer information system, or illegally controlling the computer information system;  
(2) in violation of state regulations, deleting, modifying, adding, or interfering with the functions of a computer information system;  
(3) in violation of state regulations, deleting, modifying, or adding data or application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in a computer information system;  
(4) intentionally creating or disseminating destructive programs such as computer viruses;  
(5) providing programs or tools specially used for intruding into or illegally controlling computer information systems, or knowingly providing programs or tools to others who commit illegal or criminal acts of intruding into or illegally controlling computer information systems.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　组织、领导传销活动的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。  
胁迫、诱骗他人参加传销活动的，处五日以上十日以下拘留；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 34** : Any person who organizes or leads pyramid selling activities shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.  
Any person who coerces or induces others to participate in pyramid selling activities shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留或者一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款：  
（一）在国家举行庆祝、纪念、缅怀、公祭等重要活动的场所及周边管控区域，故意从事与活动主题和氛围相违背的行为，不听劝阻，造成不良社会影响的；  
（二）在英雄烈士纪念设施保护范围内从事有损纪念英雄烈士环境和氛围的活动，不听劝阻的，或者侵占、破坏、污损英雄烈士纪念设施的；  
（三）以侮辱、诽谤或者其他方式侵害英雄烈士的姓名、肖像、名誉、荣誉，损害社会公共利益的；  
（四）亵渎、否定英雄烈士事迹和精神，或者制作、传播、散布宣扬、美化侵略战争、侵略行为的言论或者图片、音视频等物品，扰乱公共秩序的；  
（五）在公共场所或者强制他人在公共场所穿着、佩戴宣扬、美化侵略战争、侵略行为的服饰、标志，不听劝阻，造成不良社会影响的。

**Article 35** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days or imposed a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan:  
(1) in the venues or surrounding controlled areas where important state activities such as celebrations, commemorations, memorials, or public sacrifices are held, intentionally engaging in acts contrary to the theme and atmosphere of the activity, refusing to heed persuasion, and causing adverse social impact;  
(2) within the protected areas of heroes and martyrs' memorial facilities, engaging in activities that harm the environment and atmosphere of commemorating heroes and martyrs, refusing to heed persuasion, or encroaching upon, damaging, or defiling the memorial facilities;  
(3) insulting, slandering, or otherwise infringing upon the names, portraits, reputations, or honors of heroes and martyrs, damaging the public interest;  
(4) desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of heroes and martyrs, or producing, disseminating, or spreading words, pictures, audio-visual materials, or other items that promote or glorify wars of aggression or acts of aggression, disrupting public order;  
(5) in public places or compelling others to wear or display clothing or symbols that promote or glorify wars of aggression or acts of aggression in public places, refusing to heed persuasion, and causing adverse social impact.

#### 第二节　妨害公共安全的行为和处罚

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反国家规定，制造、买卖、储存、运输、邮寄、携带、使用、提供、处置爆炸性、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性物质或者传染病病原体等危险物质的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 36** : Any person who, in violation of state regulations, manufactures, buys or sells, stores, transports, mails, carries, uses, provides, or disposes of dangerous substances such as explosive, toxic, radioactive, corrosive substances or infectious disease pathogens shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　爆炸性、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性物质或者传染病病原体等危险物质被盗、被抢或者丢失，未按规定报告的，处五日以下拘留；故意隐瞒不报的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 37** : If dangerous substances such as explosive, toxic, radioactive, corrosive substances or infectious disease pathogens are stolen, robbed, or lost, and the person fails to report as required, he shall be detained for not more than five days; if he intentionally conceals the matter and fails to report, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　非法携带枪支、弹药或者弩、匕首等国家规定的管制器具的，处五日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款。  
非法携带枪支、弹药或者弩、匕首等国家规定的管制器具进入公共场所或者公共交通工具的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款。

**Article 38** : Any person who illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or state-controlled instruments such as crossbows or daggers shall be detained for not more than five days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed.  
Any person who illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or state-controlled instruments such as crossbows or daggers into a public place or on a public transport vehicle shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留：  
（一）盗窃、损毁油气管道设施、电力电信设施、广播电视设施、水利工程设施、公共供水设施、公路及附属设施或者水文监测、测量、气象测报、生态环境监测、地质监测、地震监测等公共设施，危及公共安全的；  
（二）移动、损毁国家边境的界碑、界桩以及其他边境标志、边境设施或者领土、领海基点标志设施的；  
（三）非法进行影响国（边）界线走向的活动或者修建有碍国（边）境管理的设施的。

**Article 39** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not more than five days:  
(1) stealing, damaging, oil and gas pipeline facilities, power and telecommunications facilities, radio and television facilities, water conservancy engineering facilities, public water supply facilities, highways and ancillary facilities, or public facilities such as hydrological monitoring, surveying, meteorological forecasting, ecological environment monitoring, geological monitoring, or earthquake monitoring, thereby endangering public safety;  
(2) moving or damaging boundary markers, boundary pillars, or other boundary signs, boundary facilities, or facilities marking the baselines of the territory or territorial sea;  
(3) illegally conducting activities that affect the direction of national (border) boundaries or constructing facilities that hinder the management of national (border) boundaries.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　盗窃、损坏、擅自移动使用中的航空设施，或者强行进入航空器驾驶舱的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留。  
在使用中的航空器上使用可能影响导航系统正常功能的器具、工具，不听劝阻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。  
盗窃、损坏、擅自移动使用中的其他公共交通工具设施、设备，或者以抢控驾驶操纵装置、拉扯、殴打驾驶人员等方式，干扰公共交通工具正常行驶的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 40** : Any person who steals, damages, or unauthorizedly moves aviation facilities in use, or forcibly enters the cockpit of an aircraft, shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days.  
Any person who, on an aircraft in use, uses instruments or tools that may affect the normal function of the navigation system, and refuses to heed persuasion, shall be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan.  
Any person who steals, damages, or unauthorizedly moves facilities or equipment of other public transport means in use, or interferes with the normal operation of a public transport vehicle by seizing the driving control device, pulling, or beating the driver, shall be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, he shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款：  
（一）盗窃、损毁、擅自移动铁路、城市轨道交通设施、设备、机车车辆配件或者安全标志的；  
（二）在铁路、城市轨道交通线路上放置障碍物，或者故意向列车投掷物品的；  
（三）在铁路、城市轨道交通线路、桥梁、隧道、涵洞处挖掘坑穴、采石取沙的；  
（四）在铁路、城市轨道交通线路上私设道口或者平交过道的。

**Article 41** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, he shall be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) stealing, damaging, or unauthorizedly moving railway or urban rail transit facilities, equipment, rolling stock parts, or safety signs;  
(2) placing obstacles on railway or urban rail transit lines, or intentionally throwing objects at trains;  
(3) digging pits or quarries, or extracting sand, on railway or urban rail transit lines, bridges, tunnels, or culverts;  
(4) privately setting up crossings or level crossings on railway or urban rail transit lines.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　擅自进入铁路、城市轨道交通防护网或者火车、城市轨道交通列车来临时在铁路、城市轨道交通线路上行走坐卧，抢越铁路、城市轨道，影响行车安全的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款。

**Article 42** : Any person who unauthorizedly enters the protective net of a railway or urban rail transit, or when a train or urban rail transit vehicle is approaching, walks, sits, or lies on the railway or urban rail transit line, or rushes across the railway or urban rail transit, thereby affecting traffic safety, shall be given a warning or imposed a fine of not more than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）未经批准，安装、使用电网的，或者安装、使用电网不符合安全规定的；  
（二）在车辆、行人通行的地方施工，对沟井坎穴不设覆盖物、防围和警示标志的，或者故意损毁、移动覆盖物、防围和警示标志的；  
（三）盗窃、损毁路面井盖、照明等公共设施的；  
（四）违反有关法律法规规定，升放携带明火的升空物体，有发生火灾事故危险，不听劝阻的；  
（五）从建筑物或者其他高空抛掷物品，有危害他人人身安全、公私财产安全或者公共安全危险的。

**Article 43** : Any person who commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not more than five days or imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and may concurrently be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) installing or using an electric fence without approval, or installing or using an electric fence that does not comply with safety requirements;  
(2) when constructing in a place where vehicles or pedestrians pass, failing to set up covers, guardrails, or warning signs over trenches, pits, or holes, or intentionally damaging or moving such covers, guardrails, or warning signs;  
(3) stealing or damaging public facilities such as road manhole covers or lighting;  
(4) violating relevant laws and regulations by releasing airborne objects that carry open flames, creating a risk of fire accident, and refusing to heed persuasion;  
(5) throwing objects from a building or other high places, creating a risk of harm to personal safety of others, public or private property safety, or public safety.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　举办体育、文化等大型群众性活动，违反有关规定，有发生安全事故危险，经公安机关责令改正而拒不改正或者无法改正的，责令停止活动，立即疏散；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款，可以同时责令六个月至一年以内不得举办大型群众性活动。

**Article 44** : Where a person holding sports, cultural, or other large-scale mass activities violates relevant provisions, creating a risk of a safety accident, and after being ordered by the public security organ to make corrections, refuses to do so or is unable to do so, the activities shall be ordered to stop and personnel shall be immediately evacuated; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and concurrently imposed a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and concurrently imposed a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan, and may at the same time be ordered not to hold large-scale mass activities for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　旅馆、饭店、影剧院、娱乐场、体育场馆、展览馆或者其他供社会公众活动的场所违反安全规定，致使该场所有发生安全事故危险，经公安机关责令改正而拒不改正的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以下拘留；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 45** : Where a hotel, restaurant, cinema, theater, entertainment venue, sports venue, exhibition hall, or other place for public activities violates safety regulations, causing the place to have a risk of a safety accident, and after being ordered by the public security organ to make corrections, refuses to do so, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be detained for not more than five days; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反有关法律法规关于飞行空域管理规定，飞行民用无人驾驶航空器、航空运动器材，或者升放无人驾驶自由气球、系留气球等升空物体，情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。  
飞行、升放前款规定的物体非法穿越国（边）境的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 46** Whoever, in violation of the relevant laws and regulations on the administration of flight airspace, flies civil unmanned aerial vehicles or aviation sports equipment, or releases ascending objects such as unmanned free balloons or tethered balloons, if the circumstances are relatively serious, shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.  
If the objects specified in the preceding paragraph are flown or released to illegally cross the national (border) boundary, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days.

#### 第三节　侵犯人身权利、财产权利的行为和处罚

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）组织、胁迫、诱骗不满十六周岁的人或者残疾人进行恐怖、残忍表演的；  
（二）以暴力、威胁或者其他手段强迫他人劳动的；  
（三）非法限制他人人身自由、非法侵入他人住宅或者非法搜查他人身体的。

**Article 47** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and shall also be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and shall also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) organizing, coercing, or enticing a person under the age of sixteen or a disabled person to perform a terroristic or cruel performance;  
(2) forcing another person to work by violence, threat, or other means;  
(3) illegally restricting another person's personal freedom, illegally intruding into another person's residence, or illegally searching another person's body.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　组织、胁迫未成年人在不适宜未成年人活动的经营场所从事陪酒、陪唱等有偿陪侍活动的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者五千元以下罚款。

**Article 48** Whoever organizes or coerces a minor to engage in paid companionship activities such as accompanying drinking or singing at a business premises unsuitable for minors shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and shall also be fined not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　胁迫、诱骗或者利用他人乞讨的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款。  
反复纠缠、强行讨要或者以其他滋扰他人的方式乞讨的，处五日以下拘留或者警告。

**Article 49** Whoever coerces, entices, or uses another person to beg shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 2,000 yuan.  
Whoever repeatedly harasses, forcefully demands, or begs in other ways that disturb others shall be detained for not more than five days or be given a warning.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）写恐吓信或者以其他方法威胁他人人身安全的；  
（二）公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人的；  
（三）捏造事实诬告陷害他人，企图使他人受到刑事追究或者受到治安管理处罚的；  
（四）对证人及其近亲属进行威胁、侮辱、殴打或者打击报复的；  
（五）多次发送淫秽、侮辱、恐吓等信息或者采取滋扰、纠缠、跟踪等方法，干扰他人正常生活的；  
（六）偷窥、偷拍、窃听、散布他人隐私的。  
有前款第五项规定的滋扰、纠缠、跟踪行为的，除依照前款规定给予处罚外，经公安机关负责人批准，可以责令其一定期限内禁止接触被侵害人。对违反禁止接触规定的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款。

**Article 50** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) writing a threatening letter or threatening another person's personal safety by any other means;  
(2) openly insulting another person or fabricating facts to defame another person;  
(3) fabricating facts to falsely accuse another person, with the intent to cause that person to be subject to criminal investigation or public security administration penalties;  
(4) threatening, insulting, assaulting, or retaliating against a witness or his or her close relatives;  
(5) repeatedly sending obscene, insulting, threatening, or other information, or disturbing another person's normal life by means of harassment, pestering, following, or the like;  
(6) peeping, secretly photographing, eavesdropping on, or disseminating another person's privacy.  
If a person commits the acts of harassment, pestering, or following as specified in item (5) of the preceding paragraph, in addition to being punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the person may, upon approval by the person in charge of the public security organ, be ordered to refrain from contacting the victim within a specified period. Whoever violates the order prohibiting contact shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　殴打他人的，或者故意伤害他人身体的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处五百元以上一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。  
有下列情形之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款：  
（一）结伙殴打、伤害他人的；  
（二）殴打、伤害残疾人、孕妇、不满十四周岁的人或者七十周岁以上的人的；  
（三）多次殴打、伤害他人或者一次殴打、伤害多人的。

**Article 51** Whoever assaults another person or intentionally injures another person's body shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and shall also be fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan.  
In any of the following circumstances, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and shall also be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan:  
(1) assaulting or injuring another person in a group;  
(2) assaulting or injuring a disabled person, a pregnant woman, a person under the age of fourteen, or a person aged seventy or older;  
(3) repeatedly assaulting or injuring another person, or assaulting or injuring multiple persons on one occasion.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　猥亵他人的，处五日以上十日以下拘留；猥亵精神病人、智力残疾人、不满十四周岁的人或者有其他严重情节的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留。  
在公共场所故意裸露身体隐私部位的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节恶劣的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 52** Whoever indecently assaults another person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days; if the person indecently assaults a person with a mental disorder, an intellectually disabled person, a person under the age of fourteen, or if there are other serious circumstances, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days.  
Whoever intentionally exposes intimate body parts in a public place shall be given a warning or fined not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are flagrant, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以下拘留或者警告；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）虐待家庭成员，被虐待人或者其监护人要求处理的；  
（二）对未成年人、老年人、患病的人、残疾人等负有监护、看护职责的人虐待被监护、看护的人的；  
（三）遗弃没有独立生活能力的被扶养人的。

**Article 53** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not more than five days or be given a warning; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) maltreating a family member, where the maltreated person or his or her guardian requests handling;  
(2) a person who has the duty of guardianship or care over a minor, an elderly person, a sick person, a disabled person, or the like maltreats the person under guardianship or care;  
(3) abandoning a dependent person who has no independent living ability.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　强买强卖商品，强迫他人提供服务或者强迫他人接受服务的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。

**Article 54** Whoever forces a sale or purchase of commodities, forces another person to provide a service, or forces another person to accept a service shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and shall also be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，或者在出版物、信息网络中刊载民族歧视、侮辱内容的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者三千元以下罚款。

**Article 55** Whoever incites ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, or publishes content inciting ethnic discrimination or insult in publications or information networks, shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反国家有关规定，向他人出售或者提供个人信息的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留。  
窃取或者以其他方法非法获取个人信息的，依照前款的规定处罚。

**Article 56** Whoever, in violation of the relevant provisions of the State, sells or provides another person with personal information shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days.  
Whoever steals or illegally obtains personal information by any other means shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　冒领、隐匿、毁弃、倒卖、私自开拆或者非法检查他人邮件、快件的，处警告或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 57** Whoever falsely claims, conceals, destroys, resells, privately opens, or illegally inspects another person's mail or express items shall be given a warning or fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　盗窃、诈骗、哄抢、抢夺或者敲诈勒索的，处五日以上十日以下拘留或者二千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款。

**Article 58** Whoever steals, defrauds, forcibly seizes, snatches, or extorts property shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days or fined not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　故意损毁公私财物的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款。

**Article 59** Whoever intentionally damages public or private property shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　以殴打、侮辱、恐吓等方式实施学生欺凌，违反治安管理的，公安机关应当依照本法、《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》的规定，给予治安管理处罚、采取相应矫治教育等措施。  
学校违反有关法律法规规定，明知发生严重的学生欺凌或者明知发生其他侵害未成年学生的犯罪，不按规定报告或者处置的，责令改正，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，建议有关部门依法予以处分。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、司法部关于对判处管制、宣告缓刑的犯罪分子适用禁止令有关问题的规定（试行）.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、司法部关于对判处管制、宣告缓刑的犯罪分子适用禁止令有关问题的规定（试行）》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 60** If a person commits student bullying by means such as assaulting, insulting, intimidating, or the like, which violates public security administration, the public security organ shall, in accordance with this Law and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention of Juvenile Delinquency, impose public security administration penalties and take corresponding measures such as corrective education.  
If a school, in violation of the relevant laws and regulations, knowingly fails to report or handle serious student bullying or other criminal acts infringing upon the rights of minor students, the school shall be ordered to make corrections, and a suggestion shall be made to the relevant department to impose sanctions according to law against the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.

#### 第四节　妨害社会管理的行为和处罚

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有下列行为之一的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）拒不执行人民政府在紧急状态情况下依法发布的决定、命令的；  
（二）阻碍国家机关工作人员依法执行职务的；  
（三）阻碍执行紧急任务的消防车、救护车、工程抢险车、警车或者执行上述紧急任务的专用船舶通行的；  
（四）强行冲闯公安机关设置的警戒带、警戒区或者检查点的。  
阻碍人民警察依法执行职务的，从重处罚。

**Article 61** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning or fined not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan:  
(1) refusing to execute a decision or order lawfully issued by a people's government under a state of emergency;  
(2) obstructing a state organ functionary from performing duties according to law;  
(3) obstructing the passage of fire engines, ambulances, emergency repair vehicles, police cars, or specialized vessels performing the said emergency tasks;  
(4) forcibly breaking through a cordon, security zone, or checkpoint set up by a public security organ.  
Whoever obstructs a people's police officer from performing duties according to law shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　冒充国家机关工作人员招摇撞骗的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。  
冒充军警人员招摇撞骗的，从重处罚。  
盗用、冒用个人、组织的身份、名义或者以其他虚假身份招摇撞骗的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款。

**Article 62** Whoever impersonates a state organ functionary to cheat and bluff shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.  
Whoever impersonates a military or police officer to cheat and bluff shall be given a severe punishment.  
Whoever, by stealing or falsely using the identity or name of an individual or organization, or by any other false identity, cheats and bluffs shall be detained for not more than five days or fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款：  
（一）伪造、变造或者买卖国家机关、人民团体、企业、事业单位或者其他组织的公文、证件、证明文件、印章的；  
（二）出租、出借国家机关、人民团体、企业、事业单位或者其他组织的公文、证件、证明文件、印章供他人非法使用的；  
（三）买卖或者使用伪造、变造的国家机关、人民团体、企业、事业单位或者其他组织的公文、证件、证明文件、印章的；  
（四）伪造、变造或者倒卖车票、船票、航空客票、文艺演出票、体育比赛入场券或者其他有价票证、凭证的；  
（五）伪造、变造船舶户牌，买卖或者使用伪造、变造的船舶户牌，或者涂改船舶发动机号码的。

**Article 63** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days and may also be fined not more than 3,000 yuan:  
(1) forging, altering, or buying or selling official documents, certificates, evidentiary documents, or seals of state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, or other organizations;  
(2) renting or lending official documents, certificates, evidentiary documents, or seals of state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, or other organizations for illegal use by others;  
(3) buying or selling or using forged or altered official documents, certificates, evidentiary documents, or seals of state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, institutions, or other organizations;  
(4) forging, altering, or reselling tickets for trains, ships, air passenger transport, theatrical performances, sports events, or other valuable tickets or vouchers;  
(5) forging or altering vessel licenses, buying or selling or using forged or altered vessel licenses, or altering vessel engine numbers.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　船舶擅自进入、停靠国家禁止、限制进入的水域或者岛屿的，对船舶负责人及有关责任人员处一千元以上二千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款。

**Article 64** If a vessel illegally enters or berths in waters or islands that the State prohibits or restricts entry into, the person in charge of the vessel and the persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not more than five days and may also be fined not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留或者一千元以上三千元以下罚款：  
（一）违反国家规定，未经注册登记，以社会团体、基金会、社会服务机构等社会组织名义进行活动，被取缔后，仍进行活动的；  
（二）被依法撤销登记或者吊销登记证书的社会团体、基金会、社会服务机构等社会组织，仍以原社会组织名义进行活动的；  
（三）未经许可，擅自经营按照国家规定需要由公安机关许可的行业的。  
有前款第三项行为的，予以取缔。被取缔一年以内又实施的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款。  
取得公安机关许可的经营者，违反国家有关管理规定，情节严重的，公安机关可以吊销许可证件。

**Article 65** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and may also be fined not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days or fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan:  
(1) conducting activities in the name of a social organization such as a social group, foundation, or social service agency without being registered in accordance with the provisions of the State, and continuing such activities after being banned;  
(2) conducting activities in the name of the original social organization after the registration or license of such social organization as a social group, foundation, or social service agency has been revoked according to law;  
(3) operating without a license an industry that, according to the provisions of the State, requires a license from a public security organ.  
If a person commits the act specified in item (3) of the preceding paragraph, the operation shall be banned. If the person commits such act again within one year after being banned, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days and shall also be fined not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.  
If an operator who has obtained a license from a public security organ violates the relevant administrative provisions of the State, and the circumstances are serious, the public security organ may revoke the license.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　煽动、策划非法集会、游行、示威，不听劝阻的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留。

**Article 66** Whoever incites or plans an illegal assembly, procession, or demonstration and refuses to heed persuasion shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　从事旅馆业经营活动不按规定登记住宿人员姓名、有效身份证件种类和号码等信息的，或者为身份不明、拒绝登记身份信息的人提供住宿服务的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五百元以上一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款。  
实施前款行为，妨害反恐怖主义工作进行，违反《中华人民共和国反恐怖主义法》规定的，依照其规定处罚。  
从事旅馆业经营活动有下列行为之一的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款：  
（一）明知住宿人员违反规定将危险物质带入住宿区域，不予制止的；  
（二）明知住宿人员是犯罪嫌疑人员或者被公安机关通缉的人员，不向公安机关报告的；  
（三）明知住宿人员利用旅馆实施犯罪活动，不向公安机关报告的。

**Article 67** Where a person engaged in hotel business activities fails to register the names, types of valid identity documents and numbers, or other information of the lodgers in accordance with the provisions, or provides accommodation to persons whose identity is unknown or who refuse to register their identity information, the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 500 yuan nor more than RMB 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, they shall be given a warning or fined not more than RMB 500 yuan.  
Where the preceding paragraph act is committed and hinders the anti-terrorism work, violating the provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Law of the People's Republic of China, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with its provisions.  
Where a person engaged in hotel business activities commits one of the following acts, the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, they shall be detained for not more than 5 days, and may also be fined not less than RMB 3,000 yuan nor more than RMB 5,000 yuan:  
(1) Knowing that a lodger brings dangerous substances into the accommodation area in violation of the provisions, and failing to stop it;  
(2) Knowing that a lodger is a criminal suspect or a person wanted by a public security organ, and failing to report to the public security organ;  
(3) Knowing that a lodger uses the hotel to commit criminal activities, and failing to report to the public security organ.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　房屋出租人将房屋出租给身份不明、拒绝登记身份信息的人的，或者不按规定登记承租人姓名、有效身份证件种类和号码等信息的，处五百元以上一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款。  
房屋出租人明知承租人利用出租房屋实施犯罪活动，不向公安机关报告的，处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款。

**Article 68** Where a house lessor leases the house to a person whose identity is unknown or who refuses to register identity information, or fails to register the lessee's name, type and number of valid identity documents, and other information in accordance with the provisions, the lessor shall be fined not less than RMB 500 yuan nor more than RMB 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the lessor shall be given a warning or fined not more than RMB 500 yuan.  
Where a house lessor, knowing that the lessee uses the leased house to commit criminal activities, fails to report to the public security organ, the lessor shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, the lessor shall be detained for not more than 5 days, and may also be fined not less than RMB 3,000 yuan nor more than RMB 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　娱乐场所和公章刻制、机动车修理、报废机动车回收行业经营者违反法律法规关于要求登记信息的规定，不登记信息的，处警告；拒不改正或者造成后果的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以下拘留或者三千元以下罚款。

**Article 69** Where operators of entertainment venues, seal engraving, motor vehicle repair, and end-of-life motor vehicle recycling industries violate the provisions of laws and regulations on information registration by failing to register information, they shall be given a warning; where they refuse to make corrections or cause consequences, the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　非法安装、使用、提供窃听、窃照专用器材的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处三千元以上五千元以下罚款。

**Article 70** Whoever illegally installs, uses, or provides specialized eavesdropping or secret photographing equipment shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 3,000 yuan nor more than RMB 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　有下列行为之一的，处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处一千元以上三千元以下罚款：  
（一）典当业工作人员承接典当的物品，不查验有关证明、不履行登记手续的，或者违反国家规定对明知是违法犯罪嫌疑人、赃物而不向公安机关报告的；  
（二）违反国家规定，收购铁路、油田、供电、电信、矿山、水利、测量和城市公用设施等废旧专用器材的；  
（三）收购公安机关通报寻查的赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品的；  
（四）收购国家禁止收购的其他物品的。

**Article 71** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan:  
(1) A pawnbroking employee, when accepting pawned items, fails to check the relevant certificates or fails to perform the registration procedures, or, in violation of state provisions, fails to report to a public security organ knowing that the person is a suspected offender or the items are stolen goods;  
(2) Purchasing, in violation of state provisions, waste special equipment such as railway, oilfield, power supply, telecommunications, mining, water conservancy, surveying, and municipal public facilities;  
(3) Purchasing stolen goods or goods suspected of being stolen goods notified by a public security organ for tracing; or  
(4) Purchasing other goods prohibited by the state from being purchased.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处警告或者一千元以下罚款：  
（一）隐藏、转移、变卖、擅自使用或者损毁行政执法机关依法扣押、查封、冻结、扣留、先行登记保存的财物的；  
（二）伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据或者提供虚假证言、谎报案情，影响行政执法机关依法办案的；  
（三）明知是赃物而窝藏、转移或者代为销售的；  
（四）被依法执行管制、剥夺政治权利或者在缓刑、暂予监外执行中的罪犯或者被依法采取刑事强制措施的人，有违反法律、行政法规或者国务院有关部门的监督管理规定的行为的。

**Article 72** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be given a warning or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan:  
(1) Concealing, transferring, selling, using without authorization, or damaging property lawfully impounded, seized, frozen, detained, or registered for preservation in advance by an administrative law enforcement organ;  
(2) Forging, concealing, or destroying evidence, or providing false testimony or making false statements about a case, thereby affecting the handling of a case by an administrative law enforcement organ according to law;  
(3) Knowing that property is stolen goods, and harboring, transferring, or selling it on behalf of another person; or  
(4) A convict under public surveillance, deprivation of political rights, probation, or temporary execution outside prison, or a person against whom criminal compulsory measures are taken according to law, who commits an act violating the laws, administrative regulations, or the supervision and management provisions of the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有下列行为之一的，处警告或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款：  
（一）违反人民法院刑事判决中的禁止令或者职业禁止决定的；  
（二）拒不执行公安机关依照《中华人民共和国反家庭暴力法》、《中华人民共和国妇女权益保障法》出具的禁止家庭暴力告诫书、禁止性骚扰告诫书的；  
（三）违反监察机关在监察工作中、司法机关在刑事诉讼中依法采取的禁止接触证人、鉴定人、被害人及其近亲属保护措施的。

**Article 73** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan:  
(1) Violating a restraining order or a professional prohibition decision in a criminal judgment of a People's Court;  
(2) Refusing to implement a domestic violence admonition letter or a sexual harassment admonition letter issued by a public security organ in accordance with the Anti-Domestic Violence Law of the People's Republic of China or the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Women's Rights and Interests; or  
(3) Violating protective measures taken according to law by a supervisory organ in its supervisory work or by a judicial organ in a criminal action prohibiting contact with a witness, expert evaluator, victim, or their close relatives.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　依法被关押的违法行为人脱逃的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 74** Where a person who has been lawfully detained escapes, the person shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　有下列行为之一的，处警告或者五百元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，并处五百元以上一千元以下罚款：  
（一）刻划、涂污或者以其他方式故意损坏国家保护的文物、名胜古迹的；  
（二）违反国家规定，在文物保护单位附近进行爆破、钻探、挖掘等活动，危及文物安全的。

**Article 75** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning or fined not more than RMB 500 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 500 yuan nor more than RMB 1,000 yuan:  
(1) Intentionally damaging cultural relics or scenic spots protected by the state by carving, smearing, or other means; or  
(2) In violation of state provisions, conducting blasting, drilling, excavation, or other activities near a cultural relic protection unit, thereby endangering the safety of the cultural relics.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　有下列行为之一的，处一千元以上二千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款：  
（一）偷开他人机动车的；  
（二）未取得驾驶证驾驶或者偷开他人航空器、机动船舶的。

**Article 76** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 2,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 2,000 yuan:  
(1) Driving another person's motor vehicle without authorization; or  
(2) Without a driving license, driving or operating without authorization another person's aircraft or motor vessel.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　有下列行为之一的，处五日以上十日以下拘留；情节严重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处二千元以下罚款：  
（一）故意破坏、污损他人坟墓或者毁坏、丢弃他人尸骨、骨灰的；  
（二）在公共场所停放尸体或者因停放尸体影响他人正常生活、工作秩序，不听劝阻的。

**Article 77** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be detained for not less than 5 days nor more than 10 days; where the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 2,000 yuan:  
(1) Intentionally damaging or defiling another person's grave, or destroying or discarding another person's bones or ashes; or  
(2) Placing a corpse in a public place or, by placing a corpse, affecting the normal living or working order of others, and refusing to heed persuasion.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　卖淫、嫖娼的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。  
在公共场所拉客招嫖的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。

**Article 78** A person who engages in prostitution or patronizes prostitution shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 5,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan.  
A person who solicits prostitution in a public place shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以上二千元以下罚款。

**Article 79** A person who induces, shelters, or introduces another person to engage in prostitution shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 5,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　制作、运输、复制、出售、出租淫秽的书刊、图片、影片、音像制品等淫秽物品或者利用信息网络、电话以及其他通讯工具传播淫秽信息的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以上三千元以下罚款。  
前款规定的淫秽物品或者淫秽信息中涉及未成年人的，从重处罚。

**Article 80** A person who produces, transports, reproduces, sells, or rents obscene books, pictures, films, audio-video products, or other obscene articles, or disseminates obscene information via information networks, telephones, or other communication tools, shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 5,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 3,000 yuan.  
Where the obscene articles or obscene information mentioned in the preceding paragraph involve minors, a severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一千元以上二千元以下罚款：  
（一）组织播放淫秽音像的；  
（二）组织或者进行淫秽表演的；  
（三）参与聚众淫乱活动的。  
明知他人从事前款活动，为其提供条件的，依照前款的规定处罚。  
组织未成年人从事第一款活动的，从重处罚。

**Article 81** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 2,000 yuan:  
(1) Organizing the broadcast of obscene audio or video;  
(2) Organizing or performing an obscene show; or  
(3) Participating in group licentious activities.  
A person who, knowing that another person is engaged in the activities mentioned in the preceding paragraph, provides conditions therefor shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
A person who organizes a minor to engage in the activities mentioned in the first paragraph shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　以营利为目的，为赌博提供条件的，或者参与赌博赌资较大的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一千元以上五千元以下罚款。

**Article 82** A person who, for the purpose of profit, provides conditions for gambling, or participates in gambling with relatively large stakes shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款：  
（一）非法种植罂粟不满五百株或者其他少量毒品原植物的；  
（二）非法买卖、运输、携带、持有少量未经灭活的罂粟等毒品原植物种子或者幼苗的；  
（三）非法运输、买卖、储存、使用少量罂粟壳的。  
有前款第一项行为，在成熟前自行铲除的，不予处罚。

**Article 83** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 5,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan:  
(1) Illegally planting less than 500 poppy plants or a small quantity of other drug-origin plants;  
(2) Illegally buying, selling, transporting, carrying, or possessing a small quantity of non-viable poppy seeds or seedlings, or seeds or seedlings of other drug-origin plants; or  
(3) Illegally transporting, buying, selling, storing, or using a small quantity of poppy husks.  
Where a person who commits the act under item (1) of the preceding paragraph voluntarily removes the plants before they mature, no punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　有下列行为之一的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款：  
（一）非法持有鸦片不满二百克、海洛因或者甲基苯丙胺不满十克或者其他少量毒品的；  
（二）向他人提供毒品的；  
（三）吸食、注射毒品的；  
（四）胁迫、欺骗医务人员开具麻醉药品、精神药品的。  
聚众、组织吸食、注射毒品的，对首要分子、组织者依照前款的规定从重处罚。  
吸食、注射毒品的，可以同时责令其六个月至一年以内不得进入娱乐场所、不得擅自接触涉及毒品违法犯罪人员。违反规定的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。

**Article 84** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan:  
(1) Illegally possessing less than 200 grams of opium, less than 10 grams of heroin or methamphetamine, or a small quantity of other drugs;  
(2) Providing drugs to another person;  
(3) Smoking or injecting drugs; or  
(4) Coercing or deceiving a medical worker into prescribing narcotic or psychotropic substances.  
Where a person gathers others or organizes others to smoke or inject drugs, the ringleader or organizer shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
A person who smokes or injects drugs may at the same time be ordered not to enter entertainment venues or contact persons involved in drug-related illegal or criminal acts without authorization for a period of six months to one year. Where the person violates such order, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　引诱、教唆、欺骗或者强迫他人吸食、注射毒品的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处一千元以上五千元以下罚款。  
容留他人吸食、注射毒品或者介绍买卖毒品的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，可以并处三千元以下罚款；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款。

**Article 85** A person who induces, instigates, deceives, or compels another person to smoke or inject drugs shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and shall also be fined not less than RMB 1,000 yuan nor more than RMB 5,000 yuan.  
A person who shelters another person to smoke or inject drugs, or introduces the sale or purchase of drugs, shall be detained for not less than 10 days nor more than 15 days, and may also be fined not more than RMB 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than 5 days or fined not more than RMB 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反国家规定，非法生产、经营、购买、运输用于制造毒品的原料、配剂的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。

**Article 86** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of the State, illegally produces, operates, purchases, or transports raw materials or precursors for the manufacture of drugs shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　旅馆业、饮食服务业、文化娱乐业、出租汽车业等单位的人员，在公安机关查处吸毒、赌博、卖淫、嫖娼活动时，为违法犯罪行为人通风报信的，或者以其他方式为上述活动提供条件的，处十日以上十五日以下拘留；情节较轻的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以上二千元以下罚款。

**Article 87** Where any person from an entity such as the hotel industry, catering service industry, cultural and entertainment industry, or taxi industry, provides information to a person who violates the law or commits a crime to tip off the inspection activities of the public security organ in investigating drug use, gambling, prostitution, or whoring activities, or otherwise provides conditions for the aforesaid activities, the person shall be detained for not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反关于社会生活噪声污染防治的法律法规规定，产生社会生活噪声，经基层群众性自治组织、业主委员会、物业服务人、有关部门依法劝阻、调解和处理未能制止，继续干扰他人正常生活、工作和学习的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留，可以并处一千元以下罚款。

**Article 88** Any person who violates the provisions of laws and regulations on the prevention and control of social life noise pollution, causing social life noise, and after being dissuaded, mediated, and dealt with according to law by a grassroots self-governing organization, the owners' committee, a property service provider, or the relevant authorities, continues to disturb the normal life, work, or study of others, shall be detained for not more than five days or be fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days, and may also be fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　饲养动物，干扰他人正常生活的，处警告；警告后不改正的，或者放任动物恐吓他人的，处一千元以下罚款。  
违反有关法律、法规、规章规定，出售、饲养烈性犬等危险动物的，处警告；警告后不改正的，或者致使动物伤害他人的，处五日以下拘留或者一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。  
未对动物采取安全措施，致使动物伤害他人的，处一千元以下罚款；情节较重的，处五日以上十日以下拘留。  
驱使动物伤害他人的，依照[本法第五十一条](#art-51)的规定处罚。

**Article 89** Any person who keeps animals so as to disturb the normal life of others shall be given a warning; if the person fails to correct the conduct after the warning, or allows animals to intimidate others, the person shall be fined not more than 1,000 yuan.  
Any person who sells or keeps dangerous animals such as ferocious dogs in violation of the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, or rules shall be given a warning; if the person fails to correct the conduct after the warning, or causes animals to injure others, the person shall be detained for not more than five days or be fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.  
Any person who fails to take safety measures for animals, thereby causing the animals to injure others, shall be fined not more than 1,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the person shall be detained for not less than five days but not more than ten days.  
Any person who drives animals to injure others shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 51 of this Law.

### 第四章　处罚程序

#### 第一节　调查

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　公安机关对报案、控告、举报或者违反治安管理行为人主动投案，以及其他国家机关移送的违反治安管理案件，应当立即立案并进行调查；认为不属于违反治安管理行为的，应当告知报案人、控告人、举报人、投案人，并说明理由。

**Article 90** Upon receiving a report, complaint, or information, or upon the voluntary surrender of a person who has committed an act against public security administration, or upon the transfer of a case concerning an act against public security administration by another State organ, the public security organ shall immediately place the case on file and investigate it; if it believes that the act does not constitute an act against public security administration, it shall inform the person who reported, complained, informed, or surrendered of the fact and shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　公安机关及其人民警察对治安案件的调查，应当依法进行。严禁刑讯逼供或者采用威胁、引诱、欺骗等非法手段收集证据。  
以非法手段收集的证据不得作为处罚的根据。

**Article 91** The investigation of a public security case by a public security organ and its people's police shall be conducted according to law. Obtaining evidence by illegal means such as extorting confessions by torture, or through threats, enticement, or deception is strictly prohibited.  
Evidence obtained by illegal means shall not be used as a basis for imposing a penalty.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　公安机关办理治安案件，有权向有关单位和个人收集、调取证据。有关单位和个人应当如实提供证据。  
公安机关向有关单位和个人收集、调取证据时，应当告知其必须如实提供证据，以及伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据或者提供虚假证言应当承担的法律责任。

**Article 92** When handling a public security case, a public security organ has the right to collect and obtain evidence from the relevant entities and individuals. The relevant entities and individuals shall provide evidence truthfully.  
When collecting and obtaining evidence from the relevant entities and individuals, the public security organ shall inform them that they must provide evidence truthfully, and of the legal liability they shall bear if they forge, conceal, or destroy evidence, or provide false testimony.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　在办理刑事案件过程中以及其他执法办案机关在移送案件前依法收集的物证、书证、视听资料、电子数据等证据材料，可以作为治安案件的证据使用。

**Article 93** Evidence materials such as physical evidence, documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and electronic data that are lawfully collected during the process of handling a criminal case, or lawfully collected by other law enforcement and case-handling authorities before transferring the case, may be used as evidence in a public security case.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　公安机关及其人民警察在办理治安案件时，对涉及的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私或者个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 94** When handling a public security case, a public security organ and its people's police shall maintain confidentiality regarding the State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or personal information involved.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　人民警察在办理治安案件过程中，遇有下列情形之一的，应当回避；违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人或者其法定代理人也有权要求他们回避：  
（一）是本案当事人或者当事人的近亲属的；  
（二）本人或者其近亲属与本案有利害关系的；  
（三）与本案当事人有其他关系，可能影响案件公正处理的。  
人民警察的回避，由其所属的公安机关决定；公安机关负责人的回避，由上一级公安机关决定。

**Article 95** When a people's police officer handles a public security case, if any of the following circumstances arises, the officer shall withdraw; the person who committed the act against public security administration, the victim, or their legal representative shall also have the right to request their withdrawal:  
(1) The officer is a party to the case or a close relative of a party;  
(2) The officer or a close relative has an interest in the case;  
(3) The officer has another relationship with a party to the case that may affect the fair handling of the case.  
The withdrawal of a people's police officer shall be decided by the public security organ to which the officer belongs; the withdrawal of a principal of a public security organ shall be decided by the public security organ at the next higher level.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　需要传唤违反治安管理行为人接受调查的，经公安机关办案部门负责人批准，使用传唤证传唤。对现场发现的违反治安管理行为人，人民警察经出示人民警察证，可以口头传唤，但应当在询问笔录中注明。  
公安机关应当将传唤的原因和依据告知被传唤人。对无正当理由不接受传唤或者逃避传唤的人，经公安机关办案部门负责人批准，可以强制传唤。

**Article 96** When it is necessary to summon a person who has committed an act against public security administration for investigation, a summons certificate shall be used with the approval of the person in charge of the case-handling department of the public security organ. For a person who has committed an act against public security administration discovered at the scene, a people's police officer may, upon producing the officer's certificate, make an oral summons, which shall, however, be noted in the interrogation record.  
The public security organ shall inform the summoned person of the reason and basis for the summons. Where a person without justifiable reasons refuses to be summoned or evades the summons, the person may be forcibly summoned with the approval of the person in charge of the case-handling department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　对违反治安管理行为人，公安机关传唤后应当及时询问查证，询问查证的时间不得超过八小时；涉案人数众多、违反治安管理行为人身份不明的，询问查证的时间不得超过十二小时；情况复杂，依照本法规定可能适用行政拘留处罚的，询问查证的时间不得超过二十四小时。在执法办案场所询问违反治安管理行为人，应当全程同步录音录像。  
公安机关应当及时将传唤的原因和处所通知被传唤人家属。  
询问查证期间，公安机关应当保证违反治安管理行为人的饮食、必要的休息时间等正当需求。

**Article 97** After summoning a person who has committed an act against public security administration, the public security organ shall promptly interrogate and verify the facts, and the period of interrogation and verification shall not exceed eight hours; if the number of persons involved in the case is large or the identity of the person who committed the act against public security administration is unknown, the period of interrogation and verification shall not exceed twelve hours; if the circumstances are complex and, according to the provisions of this Law, a penalty of administrative detention may be imposed, the period of interrogation and verification shall not exceed twenty-four hours. When interrogating a person who has committed an act against public security administration in a law enforcement and case-handling site, the interrogation shall be recorded synchronously with audio and video recording throughout the entire process.  
The public security organ shall promptly notify the family of the summoned person of the reason and place of the summons.  
During the period of interrogation and verification, the public security organ shall ensure the legitimate needs of the person who committed the act against public security administration, such as food and necessary rest time.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　询问笔录应当交被询问人核对；对没有阅读能力的，应当向其宣读。记载有遗漏或者差错的，被询问人可以提出补充或者更正。被询问人确认笔录无误后，应当签名、盖章或者按指印，询问的人民警察也应当在笔录上签名。  
被询问人要求就被询问事项自行提供书面材料的，应当准许；必要时，人民警察也可以要求被询问人自行书写。  
询问不满十八周岁的违反治安管理行为人，应当通知其父母或者其他监护人到场；其父母或者其他监护人不能到场的，也可以通知其他成年亲属，所在学校、单位、居住地基层组织或者未成年人保护组织的代表等合适成年人到场，并将有关情况记录在案。确实无法通知或者通知后未到场的，应当在笔录中注明。

**Article 98** The interrogation record shall be shown to the person being interrogated for verification; if the person is unable to read, it shall be read out to the person. If there are omissions or errors in the record, the person being interrogated may propose supplements or corrections. After the person being interrogated confirms that the record is correct, the person shall sign, affix a seal, or leave a thumbprint, and the people's police officer who conducted the interrogation shall also sign the record.  
Where the person being interrogated requests to provide written materials voluntarily regarding the matters under interrogation, the request shall be permitted; when necessary, the people's police officer may also require the person being interrogated to write the materials themselves.  
When interrogating a person who has committed an act against public security administration under the age of eighteen, the person's parents or other guardians shall be notified to be present; if the parents or other guardians cannot be present, other adult relatives, representatives from the school, work unit, place of residence grassroots organization, or organization for the protection of minors, or other suitable adults may be notified to be present, and the relevant circumstances shall be recorded in the file. If it is truly impossible to notify or the persons notified fail to appear after notification, this shall be noted in the record.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　人民警察询问被侵害人或者其他证人，可以在现场进行，也可以到其所在单位、住处或者其提出的地点进行；必要时，也可以通知其到公安机关提供证言。  
人民警察在公安机关以外询问被侵害人或者其他证人，应当出示人民警察证。  
询问被侵害人或者其他证人，同时适用[本法第九十八条](#art-98)的规定。

**Article 99** When a people's police officer interrogates a victim or another witness, the interrogation may be conducted at the scene, at the person's work unit, place of residence, or a place designated by the person; when necessary, the person may also be notified to provide testimony at the public security organ.  
When a people's police officer interrogates a victim or another witness outside the public security organ, the officer shall produce the officer's certificate.  
When interrogating a victim or another witness, the provisions of Article 98 of this Law shall also apply.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人或者其他证人在异地的，公安机关可以委托异地公安机关代为询问，也可以通过公安机关的视频系统远程询问。  
通过远程视频方式询问的，应当向被询问人宣读询问笔录，被询问人确认笔录无误后，询问的人民警察应当在笔录上注明。询问和宣读过程应当全程同步录音录像。

**Article 100** Where a person who has committed an act against public security administration, a victim, or another witness is in a different place, the public security organ may entrust the public security organ in the other place to conduct the interrogation on its behalf, or may conduct remote interrogation through the video system of the public security organ.  
When conducting interrogation through remote video, the interrogation record shall be read out to the person being interrogated; after the person being interrogated confirms that the record is correct, the people's police officer conducting the interrogation shall note this in the record. The entire process of interrogation and reading shall be recorded synchronously with audio and video recording.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　询问聋哑的违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人或者其他证人，应当有通晓手语等交流方式的人提供帮助，并在笔录上注明。  
询问不通晓当地通用的语言文字的违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人或者其他证人，应当配备翻译人员，并在笔录上注明。

**Article 101** When interrogating a deaf-mute person who has committed an act against public security administration, a victim, or another witness, a person who is proficient in sign language or other means of communication shall be provided to give assistance, and this shall be noted in the record.  
When interrogating a person who has committed an act against public security administration, a victim, or another witness who is not proficient in the local spoken and written language commonly used, an interpreter shall be provided, and this shall be noted in the record.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　为了查明案件事实，确定违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人的某些特征、伤害情况或者生理状态，需要对其人身进行检查，提取或者采集肖像、指纹信息和血液、尿液等生物样本的，经公安机关办案部门负责人批准后进行。对已经提取、采集的信息或者样本，不得重复提取、采集。提取或者采集被侵害人的信息或者样本，应当征得被侵害人或者其监护人同意。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/旅馆业治安管理办法.md#art-18" title="被《旅馆业治安管理办法》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 102** For the purpose of ascertaining the facts of a case and determining certain characteristics, injury conditions, or physiological conditions of the person who has committed an act against public security administration or the victim, where it is necessary to conduct a physical examination of such person, or to extract or collect portrait information, fingerprint information, and biological samples such as blood and urine, such action shall be taken after approval by the person in charge of the case-handling department of the public security organ. Information or samples that have already been extracted or collected shall not be extracted or collected repeatedly. To extract or collect information or samples from the victim, the consent of the victim or the victim's guardian shall be obtained.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　公安机关对与违反治安管理行为有关的场所或者违反治安管理行为人的人身、物品可以进行检查。检查时，人民警察不得少于二人，并应当出示人民警察证。  
对场所进行检查的，经县级以上人民政府公安机关负责人批准，使用检查证检查；对确有必要立即进行检查的，人民警察经出示人民警察证，可以当场检查，并应当全程同步录音录像。检查公民住所应当出示县级以上人民政府公安机关开具的检查证。  
检查妇女的身体，应当由女性工作人员或者医师进行。

**Article 103** A public security organ may inspect places related to an act against public security administration, or the person or articles of a person who has committed an act against public security administration. When conducting an inspection, there shall be not less than two people's police officers, and they shall produce their people's police officer certificates.  
To inspect a place, an inspection certificate shall be used with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ at or above the county level; if it is truly necessary to conduct an immediate inspection, a people's police officer may, upon producing the officer's certificate, conduct an on-site inspection, which shall be recorded synchronously with audio and video recording throughout the entire process. To inspect a citizen's residence, an inspection certificate issued by the public security organ at or above the county level shall be produced.  
When inspecting the body of a woman, it shall be conducted by female staff members or a physician.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　检查的情况应当制作检查笔录，由检查人、被检查人和见证人签名、盖章或者按指印；被检查人不在场或者被检查人、见证人拒绝签名的，人民警察应当在笔录上注明。

**Article 104** The circumstances of an inspection shall be recorded in an inspection record, which shall be signed, affixed with a seal, or left with a thumbprint by the inspector, the person inspected, and a witness; if the person inspected is not present, or the person inspected or the witness refuses to sign, the people's police officer shall note this in the record.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　公安机关办理治安案件，对与案件有关的需要作为证据的物品，可以扣押；对被侵害人或者善意第三人合法占有的财产，不得扣押，应当予以登记，但是对其中与案件有关的必须鉴定的物品，可以扣押，鉴定后应当立即解除。对与案件无关的物品，不得扣押。  
对扣押的物品，应当会同在场见证人和被扣押物品持有人查点清楚，当场开列清单一式二份，由调查人员、见证人和持有人签名或者盖章，一份交给持有人，另一份附卷备查。  
实施扣押前应当报经公安机关负责人批准；因情况紧急或者物品价值不大，当场实施扣押的，人民警察应当及时向其所属公安机关负责人报告，并补办批准手续。公安机关负责人认为不应当扣押的，应当立即解除。当场实施扣押的，应当全程同步录音录像。  
对扣押的物品，应当妥善保管，不得挪作他用；对不宜长期保存的物品，按照有关规定处理。经查明与案件无关或者经核实属于被侵害人或者他人合法财产的，应当登记后立即退还；满六个月无人对该财产主张权利或者无法查清权利人的，应当公开拍卖或者按照国家有关规定处理，所得款项上缴国库。

**Article 105** When handling a public security case, the public security organ may seize any articles related to the case that need to be used as evidence; property lawfully possessed by the victim or a bona fide third party shall not be seized but shall be registered; however, articles that must be appraised and are related to the case may be seized, and the seizure shall be immediately released after appraisal. Articles unrelated to the case shall not be seized.  
Regarding seized articles, the articles shall be checked and counted in the presence of a witness present and the holder of the seized articles, and a list in duplicate shall be made on the spot, signed or sealed by the investigators, the witness, and the holder; one copy shall be given to the holder, and the other copy shall be attached to the file for reference.  
Before implementing seizure, approval from the person in charge of the public security organ shall be obtained; if seizure is implemented on the spot due to emergency circumstances or the articles are of low value, the people's police officer shall promptly report to the person in charge of the public security organ to which the officer belongs and complete the approval procedures retroactively. If the person in charge of the public security organ considers that seizure should not be made, it shall be immediately released. Seizure implemented on the spot shall be recorded synchronously with audio and video recording throughout the entire process.  
Seized articles shall be properly kept and shall not be misappropriated; articles that are not suitable for long-term storage shall be dealt with according to relevant provisions. If it is ascertained through investigation that an article is unrelated to the case or is verified to be the lawful property of the victim or another person, the article shall be returned immediately after registration; if, after six months, no person claims a right to the property or the right holder cannot be identified, the property shall be sold by public auction or dealt with according to the relevant State provisions, and the proceeds therefrom shall be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　为了查明案情，需要解决案件中有争议的专门性问题的，应当指派或者聘请具有专门知识的人员进行鉴定；鉴定人鉴定后，应当写出鉴定意见，并且签名。

**Article 106** : To ascertain the facts of a case, where it is necessary to resolve specialized issues in dispute in the case, experts shall be assigned or engaged to conduct an expert evaluation; after the expert evaluation, the expert shall produce an expert opinion and sign it.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　为了查明案情，人民警察可以让违反治安管理行为人、被侵害人和其他证人对与违反治安管理行为有关的场所、物品进行辨认，也可以让被侵害人、其他证人对违反治安管理行为人进行辨认，或者让违反治安管理行为人对其他违反治安管理行为人进行辨认。  
辨认应当制作辨认笔录，由人民警察和辨认人签名、盖章或者按指印。

**Article 107** : To ascertain the facts of a case, the people's police may have the person who violates public security administration, the victim and other witnesses identify the places and articles related to the act violating public security administration, may also have the victim or other witnesses identify the person who violates public security administration, or have the person who violates public security administration identify other persons who violate public security administration.  
Records of identification shall be made and shall be signed, sealed or fingerprinted by the people's police and the identifier.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　公安机关进行询问、辨认、勘验，实施行政强制措施等调查取证工作时，人民警察不得少于二人。  
公安机关在规范设置、严格管理的执法办案场所进行询问、扣押、辨认的，或者进行调解的，可以由一名人民警察进行。  
依照前款规定由一名人民警察进行询问、扣押、辨认、调解的，应当全程同步录音录像。未按规定全程同步录音录像或者录音录像资料损毁、丢失的，相关证据不能作为处罚的根据。

**Article 108** : When a public security organ conducts interrogation, identification, inspection, or implements administrative compulsion measures and other investigation and evidence collection work, there shall be no fewer than two people's police.  
Where a public security organ conducts interrogation, seizure, identification in a standardized and strictly managed law enforcement case handling venue, or conducts mediation, it may be conducted by one people's police.  
Where interrogation, seizure, identification, or mediation is conducted by one people's police in accordance with the preceding paragraph, synchronous audio and video recording shall be made throughout the process. If synchronous audio and video recording is not made in accordance with the provisions, or if the audio and video recording materials are damaged or lost, the relevant evidence shall not be used as the basis for punishment.

#### 第二节　决定

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　治安管理处罚由县级以上地方人民政府公安机关决定；其中警告、一千元以下的罚款，可以由公安派出所决定。

**Article 109** : Public security administration punishments shall be decided by the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level; among them, a warning or a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan may be decided by a public security police station.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　对决定给予行政拘留处罚的人，在处罚前已经采取强制措施限制人身自由的时间，应当折抵。限制人身自由一日，折抵行政拘留一日。

**Article 110** : For a person who is decided to be given administrative detention punishment, the period during which compulsory measures restricting personal freedom have been taken before the punishment shall be offset. One day of restriction of personal freedom shall offset one day of administrative detention.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　公安机关查处治安案件，对没有本人陈述，但其他证据能够证明案件事实的，可以作出治安管理处罚决定。但是，只有本人陈述，没有其他证据证明的，不能作出治安管理处罚决定。

**Article 111** : When investigating a public security case, a public security organ may make a decision on public security administration punishment if there is no statement from the person himself but other evidence can prove the facts of the case. However, if there is only the person's own statement and no other evidence to prove it, no decision on public security administration punishment shall be made.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　公安机关作出治安管理处罚决定前，应当告知违反治安管理行为人拟作出治安管理处罚的内容及事实、理由、依据，并告知违反治安管理行为人依法享有的权利。  
违反治安管理行为人有权陈述和申辩。公安机关必须充分听取违反治安管理行为人的意见，对违反治安管理行为人提出的事实、理由和证据，应当进行复核；违反治安管理行为人提出的事实、理由或者证据成立的，公安机关应当采纳。  
违反治安管理行为人不满十八周岁的，还应当依照前两款的规定告知未成年人的父母或者其他监护人，充分听取其意见。  
公安机关不得因违反治安管理行为人的陈述、申辩而加重其处罚。

**Article 112** : Before making a decision on public security administration punishment, a public security organ shall inform the person who violates public security administration of the content, facts, reasons, and basis of the proposed public security administration punishment, and shall inform him of the rights he enjoys according to law.  
The person who violates public security administration has the right to make statements and defenses. The public security organ must fully hear the opinions of the person who violates public security administration, and shall review the facts, reasons, and evidence put forward by him; if the facts, reasons, or evidence put forward by him are established, the public security organ shall adopt them.  
If the person who violates public security administration is under the age of 18, his parents or other guardians shall also be informed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs, and their opinions shall be fully heard.  
A public security organ shall not impose a heavier punishment on a person who violates public security administration because of his statements or defenses.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　治安案件调查结束后，公安机关应当根据不同情况，分别作出以下处理：  
（一）确有依法应当给予治安管理处罚的违法行为的，根据情节轻重及具体情况，作出处罚决定；  
（二）依法不予处罚的，或者违法事实不能成立的，作出不予处罚决定；  
（三）违法行为已涉嫌犯罪的，移送有关主管机关依法追究刑事责任；  
（四）发现违反治安管理行为人有其他违法行为的，在对违反治安管理行为作出处罚决定的同时，通知或者移送有关主管机关处理。  
对情节复杂或者重大违法行为给予治安管理处罚，公安机关负责人应当集体讨论决定。

**Article 113** : After the investigation of a public security case is concluded, the public security organ shall, based on different circumstances, make the following dispositions respectively:  
(1) If there is indeed an illegal act that should be given a public security administration punishment according to law, a punishment decision shall be made based on the severity of the circumstances and specific conditions;  
(2) If a punishment is not to be imposed according to law, or if the facts of the illegal act cannot be established, a decision of no punishment shall be made;  
(3) If the illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority to investigate criminal liability according to law;  
(4) If it is discovered that the person who violates public security administration has other illegal acts, while making a punishment decision for the act violating public security administration, the relevant competent authority shall be notified or the case shall be transferred to it for handling.  
For imposing public security administration punishment on cases with complex circumstances or major illegal acts, the person in charge of the public security organ shall make a decision after collective discussion.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　有下列情形之一的，在公安机关作出治安管理处罚决定之前，应当由从事治安管理处罚决定法制审核的人员进行法制审核；未经法制审核或者审核未通过的，不得作出决定：  
（一）涉及重大公共利益的；  
（二）直接关系当事人或者第三人重大权益，经过听证程序的；  
（三）案件情况疑难复杂、涉及多个法律关系的。  
公安机关中初次从事治安管理处罚决定法制审核的人员，应当通过国家统一法律职业资格考试取得法律职业资格。

**Article 114** : Under any of the following circumstances, before a public security organ makes a decision on public security administration punishment, a legal review shall be conducted by the personnel engaged in legal review of public security administration punishment decisions; if no legal review is conducted or the review is not passed, no decision shall be made:  
(1) Involving major public interests;  
(2) Directly involving major rights and interests of a party or a third party, and having undergone hearing procedures;  
(3) The case is difficult, complex, and involves multiple legal relationships.  
Personnel who initially engage in legal review of public security administration punishment decisions in a public security organ shall have obtained legal professional qualification by passing the national unified legal professional qualification examination.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　公安机关作出治安管理处罚决定的，应当制作治安管理处罚决定书。决定书应当载明下列内容：  
（一）被处罚人的姓名、性别、年龄、身份证件的名称和号码、住址；  
（二）违法事实和证据；  
（三）处罚的种类和依据；  
（四）处罚的执行方式和期限；  
（五）对处罚决定不服，申请行政复议、提起行政诉讼的途径和期限；  
（六）作出处罚决定的公安机关的名称和作出决定的日期。  
决定书应当由作出处罚决定的公安机关加盖印章。

**Article 115** : Where a public security organ makes a decision on public security administration punishment, it shall produce a written decision on public security administration punishment. The written decision shall contain the following:  
(1) The name, gender, age, name and number of identity document, and address of the person subjected to punishment;  
(2) The facts of the illegal act and evidence;  
(3) The type of punishment and its basis;  
(4) The method and term of enforcement of the punishment;  
(5) The channels and time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration or initiating administrative action against the punishment decision;  
(6) The name of the public security organ making the punishment decision and the date of the decision.  
The written decision shall be affixed with the seal of the public security organ making the punishment decision.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　公安机关应当向被处罚人宣告治安管理处罚决定书，并当场交付被处罚人；无法当场向被处罚人宣告的，应当在二日以内送达被处罚人。决定给予行政拘留处罚的，应当及时通知被处罚人的家属。  
有被侵害人的，公安机关应当将决定书送达被侵害人。

**Article 116** : A public security organ shall pronounce the written decision on public security administration punishment to the person subjected to punishment and deliver it to him on the spot; if it cannot be pronounced on the spot to the person subjected to punishment, it shall be served on him within two days. Where a decision on administrative detention punishment is made, the family members of the person subjected to punishment shall be notified promptly.  
If there is a victim, the public security organ shall serve the written decision on the victim.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　公安机关作出吊销许可证件、处四千元以上罚款的治安管理处罚决定或者采取责令停业整顿措施前，应当告知违反治安管理行为人有权要求举行听证；违反治安管理行为人要求听证的，公安机关应当及时依法举行听证。  
对依照[本法第二十三条](#art-23)第二款规定可能执行行政拘留的未成年人，公安机关应当告知未成年人和其监护人有权要求举行听证；未成年人和其监护人要求听证的，公安机关应当及时依法举行听证。对未成年人案件的听证不公开举行。  
前两款规定以外的案情复杂或者具有重大社会影响的案件，违反治安管理行为人要求听证，公安机关认为必要的，应当及时依法举行听证。  
公安机关不得因违反治安管理行为人要求听证而加重其处罚。

**Article 117** : Before a public security organ makes a decision on public security administration punishment of revoking a permit or imposing a fine of more than 4,000 yuan, or takes the measure of ordering suspension of business for rectification, it shall inform the person who violates public security administration of his right to request a hearing; if the person who violates public security administration requests a hearing, the public security organ shall promptly hold a hearing according to law.  
For a minor who may be subject to administrative detention in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 23 of this Law, the public security organ shall inform the minor and his guardian of their right to request a hearing; if the minor and his guardian request a hearing, the public security organ shall promptly hold a hearing according to law. The hearing for a case involving a minor shall not be held publicly.  
For cases other than those provided for in the preceding two paragraphs where the circumstances are complex or have major social impact, if the person who violates public security administration requests a hearing and the public security organ deems it necessary, a hearing shall be promptly held according to law.  
A public security organ shall not impose a heavier punishment on a person who violates public security administration because he requests a hearing.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　公安机关办理治安案件的期限，自立案之日起不得超过三十日；案情重大、复杂的，经上一级公安机关批准，可以延长三十日。期限延长以二次为限。公安派出所办理的案件需要延长期限的，由所属公安机关批准。  
为了查明案情进行鉴定的期间、听证的期间，不计入办理治安案件的期限。

**Article 118** : The time limit for a public security organ to handle a public security case shall not exceed 30 days from the date of filing the case; if the case is major or complex, it may be extended by 30 days with the approval of the public security organ at the next higher level. The extension of the time limit is limited to two times. If a case handled by a public security police station needs an extension, it shall be approved by the public security organ to which it belongs.  
The period for conducting expert evaluation to ascertain the facts of the case and the period for hearing shall not be counted in the time limit for handling the public security case.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　违反治安管理行为事实清楚，证据确凿，处警告或者五百元以下罚款的，可以当场作出治安管理处罚决定。

**Article 119** : Where the facts of an act violating public security administration are clear and the evidence is conclusive, and a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan is to be imposed, a decision on public security administration punishment may be made on the spot.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　当场作出治安管理处罚决定的，人民警察应当向违反治安管理行为人出示人民警察证，并填写处罚决定书。处罚决定书应当当场交付被处罚人；有被侵害人的，并应当将决定书送达被侵害人。  
前款规定的处罚决定书，应当载明被处罚人的姓名、违法行为、处罚依据、罚款数额、时间、地点以及公安机关名称，并由经办的人民警察签名或者盖章。  
适用当场处罚，被处罚人对拟作出治安管理处罚的内容及事实、理由、依据没有异议的，可以由一名人民警察作出治安管理处罚决定，并应当全程同步录音录像。  
当场作出治安管理处罚决定的，经办的人民警察应当在二十四小时以内报所属公安机关备案。

**Article 120** : Where a decision on public security administration punishment is made on the spot, the people's police shall show their people's police identification to the person who violates public security administration, and fill out a written punishment decision. The written punishment decision shall be delivered to the person subjected to punishment on the spot; if there is a victim, the written decision shall also be served on the victim.  
The written punishment decision specified in the preceding paragraph shall contain the name of the person subjected to punishment, the illegal act, the basis of punishment, the amount of the fine, the time, the place, and the name of the public security organ, and shall be signed or sealed by the people's police handling the case.  
Where on-the-spot punishment is applied, and the person subjected to punishment has no objection to the content, facts, reasons, and basis of the proposed public security administration punishment, the decision on public security administration punishment may be made by one people's police, and synchronous audio and video recording shall be made throughout the process.  
Where a decision on public security administration punishment is made on the spot, the people's police handling the case shall report it to the public security organ to which they belong for record within 24 hours.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　被处罚人、被侵害人对公安机关依照本法规定作出的治安管理处罚决定，作出的收缴、追缴决定，或者采取的有关限制性、禁止性措施等不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 121** : If the person subjected to punishment or the victim is dissatisfied with the decision on public security administration punishment made by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the decision on confiscation or recovery, or the relevant restrictive or prohibitive measures taken, he may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate administrative action according to law.

#### 第三节　执行

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　对被决定给予行政拘留处罚的人，由作出决定的公安机关送拘留所执行；执行期满，拘留所应当按时解除拘留，发给解除拘留证明书。  
被决定给予行政拘留处罚的人在异地被抓获或者有其他有必要在异地拘留所执行情形的，经异地拘留所主管公安机关批准，可以在异地执行。

**Article 122** : A person who is decided to be given administrative detention punishment shall be sent by the public security organ that made the decision to a detention center for enforcement; upon expiration of the enforcement term, the detention center shall timely release the person from detention and issue a certificate of release.  
If a person who is decided to be given administrative detention punishment is arrested in a different place or there are other circumstances where it is necessary to enforce the detention in a different place, enforcement may be carried out in a different place with the approval of the public security organ in charge of the detention center in the different place.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　受到罚款处罚的人应当自收到处罚决定书之日起十五日以内，到指定的银行或者通过电子支付系统缴纳罚款。但是，有下列情形之一的，人民警察可以当场收缴罚款：  
（一）被处二百元以下罚款，被处罚人对罚款无异议的；  
（二）在边远、水上、交通不便地区，旅客列车上或者口岸，公安机关及其人民警察依照本法的规定作出罚款决定后，被处罚人到指定的银行或者通过电子支付系统缴纳罚款确有困难，经被处罚人提出的；  
（三）被处罚人在当地没有固定住所，不当场收缴事后难以执行的。

**Article 123** : A person subjected to a fine shall pay the fine within 15 days from the date of receiving the punishment decision to a designated bank or through an electronic payment system. However, under any of the following circumstances, the people's police may collect the fine on the spot:  
(1) The fine imposed is not more than 200 yuan and the person subjected to punishment has no objection to the fine;  
(2) In remote areas, on water, areas with inconvenient transportation, on passenger trains, or at ports, after the public security organ and its people's police make a fine decision in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the person subjected to punishment has difficulty in paying the fine to a designated bank or through an electronic payment system, and the person subjected to punishment makes a request;  
(3) The person subjected to punishment has no fixed residence locally, and it would be difficult to enforce the fine later if not collected on the spot.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　人民警察当场收缴的罚款，应当自收缴罚款之日起二日以内，交至所属的公安机关；在水上、旅客列车上当场收缴的罚款，应当自抵岸或者到站之日起二日以内，交至所属的公安机关；公安机关应当自收到罚款之日起二日以内将罚款缴付指定的银行。

**Article 124** : Fines collected on the spot by the people's police shall be handed over to the public security organ to which they belong within two days from the date of collection; fines collected on the spot on water or on passenger trains shall be handed over to the public security organ to which they belong within two days from the date of reaching the shore or arrival at the station; a public security organ shall pay the fines to a designated bank within two days from the date of receipt.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　人民警察当场收缴罚款的，应当向被处罚人出具省级以上人民政府财政部门统一制发的专用票据；不出具统一制发的专用票据的，被处罚人有权拒绝缴纳罚款。

**Article 125** : Where the people's police collect fines on the spot, they shall issue a special receipt uniformly printed and distributed by the financial department of the people's government at or above the provincial level to the person subjected to punishment; if no uniformly printed and distributed special receipt is issued, the person subjected to punishment has the right to refuse to pay the fine.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　被处罚人不服行政拘留处罚决定，申请行政复议、提起行政诉讼的，遇有参加升学考试、子女出生或者近亲属病危、死亡等情形的，可以向公安机关提出暂缓执行行政拘留的申请。公安机关认为暂缓执行行政拘留不致发生社会危险的，由被处罚人或者其近亲属提出符合[本法第一百二十七条](#art-127)规定条件的担保人，或者按每日行政拘留二百元的标准交纳保证金，行政拘留的处罚决定暂缓执行。  
正在被执行行政拘留处罚的人遇有参加升学考试、子女出生或者近亲属病危、死亡等情形，被拘留人或者其近亲属申请出所的，由公安机关依照前款规定执行。被拘留人出所的时间不计入拘留期限。

**Article 126** Where a person subject to penalty is dissatisfied with the decision on administrative detention penalty and applies for administrative reconsideration or initiates an administrative action, and encounters circumstances such as participating in entrance examinations, childbirth, or critical illness or death of a close relative, the person may file an application with the public security organ for suspending the enforcement of the administrative detention. If the public security organ considers that the suspension of enforcement of the administrative detention will not cause social danger, the person subject to penalty or a close relative thereof shall provide a guarantor who meets the conditions specified in Article 127 of this Law, or pay a deposit at the rate of RMB 200 per day of administrative detention, and the decision on administrative detention penalty shall be suspended.  
Where a person who is undergoing enforcement of administrative detention penalty encounters circumstances such as participating in entrance examinations, childbirth, or critical illness or death of a close relative, and the detained person or a close relative thereof applies for release from the detention facility, the public security organ shall enforce it in accordance with the preceding paragraph. The time of release of the detained person from the detention facility shall not be counted in the detention period.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　担保人应当符合下列条件：  
（一）与本案无牵连；  
（二）享有政治权利，人身自由未受到限制；  
（三）在当地有常住户口和固定住所；  
（四）有能力履行担保义务。

**Article 127** A guarantor shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having no involvement in the case;  
(2) enjoying political rights and not being subject to restrictions on personal freedom;  
(3) having a permanent household registration and a fixed residence in the locality; and  
(4) being capable of performing the guarantee obligations.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　担保人应当保证被担保人不逃避行政拘留处罚的执行。  
担保人不履行担保义务，致使被担保人逃避行政拘留处罚的执行的，处三千元以下罚款。

**Article 128** A guarantor shall ensure that the guaranteed person does not evade the enforcement of the administrative detention penalty.  
If a guarantor fails to perform the guarantee obligations, thereby causing the guaranteed person to evade the enforcement of the administrative detention penalty, the guarantor shall be fined not more than RMB 3,000.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　被决定给予行政拘留处罚的人交纳保证金，暂缓行政拘留或者出所后，逃避行政拘留处罚的执行的，保证金予以没收并上缴国库，已经作出的行政拘留决定仍应执行。

**Article 129** Where a person who has been decided to be given administrative detention penalty pays a deposit, and after the administrative detention is suspended or the person is released from the detention facility, evades the enforcement of the administrative detention penalty, the deposit shall be confiscated and turned over to the State Treasury, and the administrative detention decision that has been made shall still be enforced.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　行政拘留的处罚决定被撤销，行政拘留处罚开始执行，或者出所后继续执行的，公安机关收取的保证金应当及时退还交纳人。

**Article 130** If the decision on administrative detention penalty is revoked, the enforcement of the administrative detention penalty commences, or the person continues enforcement after release from the detention facility, the deposit collected by the public security organ shall be returned to the depositor in a timely manner.

### 第五章　执法监督

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　公安机关及其人民警察应当依法、公正、严格、高效办理治安案件，文明执法，不得徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、滥用职权。

**Article 131** The public security organs and their people's police shall handle public security cases according to law, impartially, strictly, and efficiently, enforce the law in a civilized manner, and shall not engage in favoritism, neglect of duty, or abuse of power.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　公安机关及其人民警察办理治安案件，禁止对违反治安管理行为人打骂、虐待或者侮辱。

**Article 132** When handling public security cases, the public security organs and their people's police are prohibited from beating, abusing, or insulting a person who violates public security administration.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　公安机关及其人民警察办理治安案件，应当自觉接受社会和公民的监督。  
公安机关及其人民警察办理治安案件，不严格执法或者有违法违纪行为的，任何单位和个人都有权向公安机关或者人民检察院、监察机关检举、控告；收到检举、控告的机关，应当依据职责及时处理。

**Article 133** The public security organs and their people's police shall consciously accept the supervision of society and citizens when handling public security cases.  
Where the public security organs and their people's police fail to strictly enforce the law or have illegal or disciplinary violations when handling public security cases, any unit or individual has the right to report or accuse them to the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate, or the supervisory organ; the organ that receives the report or accusation shall handle it promptly in accordance with its duties.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　公安机关作出治安管理处罚决定，发现被处罚人是公职人员，依照《中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法》的规定需要给予政务处分的，应当依照有关规定及时通报监察机关等有关单位。

**Article 134** Where a public security organ makes a decision on public security administration punishment and discovers that the person punished is a public official and needs to be given a disciplinary sanction in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Governmental Disciplinary Sanctions for Public Officials, it shall promptly notify the supervisory organ and other relevant units in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　公安机关依法实施罚款处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款应当全部上缴国库，不得返还、变相返还，不得与经费保障挂钩。

**Article 135** Where a public security organ imposes a fine penalty according to law, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, implement the separation of fine decision and fine collection; the fines collected shall be fully turned over to the state treasury, and shall not be returned or returned in disguised form, and shall not be linked to funding guarantees.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　违反治安管理的记录应当予以封存，不得向任何单位和个人提供或者公开，但有关国家机关为办案需要或者有关单位根据国家规定进行查询的除外。依法进行查询的单位，应当对被封存的违法记录的情况予以保密。

**Article 136** Records of violations of public security administration shall be sealed and shall not be provided or disclosed to any unit or individual, except where relevant state organs need them for case handling or relevant units make inquiries in accordance with state regulations. Units that make inquiries according to law shall keep the sealed illegal record confidential.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　公安机关应当履行同步录音录像运行安全管理职责，完善技术措施，定期维护设施设备，保障录音录像设备运行连续、稳定、安全。

**Article 137** The public security organ shall fulfill the duties of safety management of synchronous audio and video recording operation, improve technical measures, regularly maintain facilities and equipment, and ensure that the audio and video recording equipment operates continuously, stably, and safely.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　公安机关及其人民警察不得将在办理治安案件过程中获得的个人信息，依法提取、采集的相关信息、样本用于与治安管理、查处犯罪无关的用途，不得出售、提供给其他单位或者个人。

**Article 138** The public security organs and their people's police shall not use personal information obtained in the process of handling public security cases, and relevant information and samples legally extracted and collected, for purposes unrelated to public security administration and investigation of crimes, and shall not sell or provide them to other units or individuals.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　人民警察办理治安案件，有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）刑讯逼供、体罚、打骂、虐待、侮辱他人的；  
（二）超过询问查证的时间限制人身自由的；  
（三）不执行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离制度或者不按规定将罚没的财物上缴国库或者依法处理的；  
（四）私分、侵占、挪用、故意损毁所收缴、追缴、扣押的财物的；  
（五）违反规定使用或者不及时返还被侵害人财物的；  
（六）违反规定不及时退还保证金的；  
（七）利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
（八）当场收缴罚款不出具专用票据或者不如实填写罚款数额的；  
（九）接到要求制止违反治安管理行为的报警后，不及时出警的；  
（十）在查处违反治安管理活动时，为违法犯罪行为人通风报信的；  
（十一）泄露办理治安案件过程中的工作秘密或者其他依法应当保密的信息的；  
（十二）将在办理治安案件过程中获得的个人信息，依法提取、采集的相关信息、样本用于与治安管理、查处犯罪无关的用途，或者出售、提供给其他单位或者个人的；  
（十三）剪接、删改、损毁、丢失办理治安案件的同步录音录像资料的；  
（十四）有徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、滥用职权，不依法履行法定职责的其他情形的。  
办理治安案件的公安机关有前款所列行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 139** Where a people's police officer handling a public security case commits any of the following acts, he shall be given a disciplinary sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) extorting confessions by torture, imposing corporal punishment, beating, abusing, or insulting others;  
(2) restricting personal freedom beyond the time limit for inquiry and verification;  
(3) failing to implement the system of separation of fine decision and fine collection or failing to turn over confiscated property to the state treasury or handle it according to law;  
(4) privately dividing, embezzling, misappropriating, or intentionally damaging the property confiscated, recovered, or seized;  
(5) using property of the victim in violation of regulations or failing to return it promptly;  
(6) failing to return the bond promptly in violation of regulations;  
(7) taking advantage of his position to accept property from others or seek other benefits;  
(8) failing to issue a special receipt when collecting fines on the spot or failing to truthfully fill in the amount of fine;  
(9) failing to respond promptly after receiving a report requesting to stop an act violating public security administration;  
(10) tipping off an offender or illegal actor when investigating activities violating public security administration;  
(11) disclosing work secrets or other information that should be kept confidential according to law in the process of handling a public security case;  
(12) using personal information obtained in the process of handling a public security case, and relevant information and samples legally extracted and collected, for purposes unrelated to public security administration and investigation of crimes, or selling or providing them to other units or individuals;  
(13) editing, deleting, damaging, or losing synchronous audio and video recording materials of a public security case;  
(14) engaging in favoritism, neglect of duty, abuse of power, or other circumstances of failing to perform statutory duties according to law.  
Where a public security organ handling a public security case commits any act listed in the preceding paragraph, the leading person responsible and the directly responsible person shall be given a disciplinary sanction according to law.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　公安机关及其人民警察违法行使职权，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益的，应当赔礼道歉；造成损害的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 140** Where a public security organ or its people's police exercise their powers illegally and infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, they shall make an apology; if damage is caused, they shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　其他法律中规定由公安机关给予行政拘留处罚的，其处罚程序适用本法规定。  
公安机关依照《中华人民共和国枪支管理法》、《民用爆炸物品安全管理条例》等直接关系公共安全和社会治安秩序的法律、行政法规实施处罚的，其处罚程序适用[本法规定。  
本法第三十二条](#art-32)、第三十四条、第四十六条、第五十六条规定给予行政拘留处罚，其他法律、行政法规同时规定给予罚款、没收违法所得、没收非法财物等其他行政处罚的行为，由相关主管部门依照相应规定处罚；需要给予行政拘留处罚的，由公安机关依照本法规定处理。

**Article 141** Where other laws provide that the public security organ shall impose an administrative detention punishment, the punishment procedures shall be governed by the provisions of this Law.  
Where the public security organ imposes a punishment in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Control of Firearms, the Regulations on the Safety Management of Civil Explosives, and other laws and administrative regulations that directly concern public safety and public security order, the punishment procedures shall be governed by the provisions of this Law.  
For acts that are subject to administrative detention punishment under Articles 32, 34, 46, and 56 of this Law, and for which other laws and administrative regulations also provide for other administrative punishments such as fines, confiscation of illegal gains, and confiscation of illegal property, the relevant competent departments shall impose punishment in accordance with the corresponding provisions; where an administrative detention punishment is necessary, the public security organ shall handle it in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　海警机构履行海上治安管理职责，行使本法规定的公安机关的职权，但是法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 142** The Coast Guard agency shall perform the duties of maritime public security administration and exercise the powers of the public security organ as provided for in this Law, except where laws provide otherwise.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　本法所称以上、以下、以内，包括本数。

**Article 143** The terms 'or above', 'or below', and 'within' as used in this Law include the number itself.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　本法自2026年1月1日起施行。

**Article 144** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2026.
