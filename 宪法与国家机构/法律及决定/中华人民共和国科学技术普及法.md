# 中华人民共和国科学技术普及法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-25  
**生效日期**：2024-12-25

> （2002年6月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过　2024年12月25日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十三次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　组织管理 第三章　社会责任 第四章　科普活动 第五章　科普人员 第六章　保障措施 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了实施科教兴国战略、人才强国战略和创新驱动发展战略，全面促进科学技术普及，加强国家科学技术普及能力建设，提高公民的科学文化素质，推进实现高水平科技自立自强，推动经济发展和社会进步，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于国家和社会普及科学技术知识、倡导科学方法、传播科学思想、弘扬科学精神的活动。 开展科学技术普及（以下简称科普），应当采取公众易于接触、理解、接受、参与的方式。 第三条　坚持中国共产党对科普事业的全面领导。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了实施科教兴国战略、人才强国战略和创新驱动发展战略，全面促进科学技术普及，加强国家科学技术普及能力建设，提高公民的科学文化素质，推进实现高水平科技自立自强，推动经济发展和社会进步，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of implementing the strategy of rejuvenating the country through science and education, the strategy of strengthening the country through talents, and the innovation-driven development strategy, comprehensively promoting the popularization of science and technology, strengthening the national capacity building for science and technology popularization, improving citizens' scientific and cultural literacy, advancing the realization of high-level scientific and technological self-reliance and self-improvement, and promoting economic development and social progress.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于国家和社会普及科学技术知识、倡导科学方法、传播科学思想、弘扬科学精神的活动。  
开展科学技术普及（以下简称科普），应当采取公众易于接触、理解、接受、参与的方式。

**Article 2** This Law applies to activities of the State and society in popularizing scientific and technological knowledge, advocating scientific methods, disseminating scientific thought, and promoting the scientific spirit.  
The popularization of science and technology (hereinafter referred to as science popularization) shall be carried out in a manner that is easily accessible, understandable, acceptable, and participable by the public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　坚持中国共产党对科普事业的全面领导。  
开展科普，应当以人民为中心，坚持面向世界科技前沿、面向经济主战场、面向国家重大需求、面向人民生命健康，培育和弘扬创新文化，推动形成崇尚科学、追求创新的风尚，服务高质量发展，为建设科技强国奠定坚实基础。

**Article 3** The overall leadership of the Communist Party of China over the science popularization cause shall be upheld.  
In carrying out science popularization, the people shall be placed at the center, and efforts shall be made to keep abreast of global scientific and technological frontiers, serve the main economic battlefield, meet the major needs of the country, and safeguard the lives and health of the people, cultivate and promote an innovation culture, foster a prevailing trend of respecting science and pursuing innovation, serve high-quality development, and lay a solid foundation for building a country strong in science and technology.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　科普是国家创新体系的重要组成部分，是实现创新发展的基础性工作。国家把科普放在与科技创新同等重要的位置，加强科普工作总体布局、统筹部署，推动科普与科技创新紧密协同，充分发挥科普在一体推进教育科技人才事业发展中的作用。

**Article 4** Science popularization is an important component of the national innovation system and a fundamental work for achieving innovation-driven development. The State shall place science popularization on an equal footing with scientific and technological innovation, strengthen the overall layout and coordinated deployment of science popularization work, promote close synergy between science popularization and scientific and technological innovation, and fully leverage the role of science popularization in advancing the integrated development of education, science and technology, and talent.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　科普是公益事业，是社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设的重要内容。发展科普事业是国家的长期任务，国家推动科普全面融入经济、政治、文化、社会、生态文明建设，构建政府、社会、市场等协同推进的科普发展格局。  
国家加强农村的科普工作，扶持革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、经济欠发达地区的科普工作，建立完善跨区域科普合作和共享机制，促进铸牢中华民族共同体意识，推进乡村振兴。

**Article 5** Science popularization is a public welfare undertaking and an important part of the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization. Developing the science popularization cause is a long-term task of the State. The State shall promote the comprehensive integration of science popularization into economic, political, cultural, social, and ecological civilization construction, and build a science popularization development pattern with coordinated advancement by the government, society, and the market.  
The State shall strengthen science popularization work in rural areas, support science popularization work in old revolutionary base areas, areas with large ethnic minority populations, border areas, and economically underdeveloped areas, establish and improve cross-regional science popularization cooperation and sharing mechanisms, promote the consolidation of a sense of community for the Chinese nation, and advance rural revitalization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　科普工作应当践行社会主义核心价值观，弘扬科学精神和科学家精神，遵守科技伦理，反对和抵制伪科学。  
任何组织和个人不得以科普为名从事损害国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的活动。

**Article 6** Science popularization work shall practice the core socialist values, promote the scientific spirit and the spirit of scientists, abide by science and technology ethics, and oppose and resist pseudoscience.  
No organization or individual may engage in activities that harm the interests of the State, the public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others under the pretext of science popularization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家机关、武装力量、社会团体、企业事业单位、基层群众性自治组织及其他组织应当开展科普工作，可以通过多种形式广泛开展科普活动。  
每年9月为全国科普月。  
公民有参与科普活动的权利。

**Article 7** State organs, armed forces, social organizations, enterprises and public institutions, grassroots self-governing organizations, and other organizations shall carry out science popularization work and may extensively conduct science popularization activities in various forms.  
September of each year shall be the National Science Popularization Month.  
Citizens have the right to participate in science popularization activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家保护科普组织和科普人员的合法权益，鼓励科普组织和科普人员自主开展科普活动，依法兴办科普事业。

**Article 8** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of science popularization organizations and science popularization personnel, encourage science popularization organizations and science popularization personnel to independently carry out science popularization activities, and establish science popularization undertakings in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持社会力量兴办科普事业。社会力量兴办科普事业可以按照市场机制运行。

**Article 9** The State shall support social forces in establishing science popularization undertakings. Social forces establishing science popularization undertakings may operate in accordance with market mechanisms.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　科普工作应当坚持群众性、社会性和经常性，结合实际，因地制宜，采取多种方式。

**Article 10** Science popularization work shall adhere to the principles of mass participation, social involvement, and regular activity, be adapted to local conditions and actual circumstances, and adopt multiple approaches.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家实施全民科学素质行动，制定全民科学素质行动规划，引导公民培育科学和理性思维，树立科学的世界观和方法论，养成文明、健康、绿色、环保的科学生活方式，提高劳动、生产、创新创造的技能。

**Article 11** The State shall implement the national scientific literacy action, formulate plans for national scientific literacy action, guide citizens to cultivate scientific and rational thinking, establish a scientific worldview and methodology, develop civilized, healthy, green, and environmentally friendly scientific lifestyles, and improve skills in labor, production, innovation, and creation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持和促进科普对外合作与交流。

**Article 12** The State shall support and promote international cooperation and exchange in science popularization.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对在科普工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。  
国家鼓励社会力量依法设立科普奖项。

**Article 13** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to science popularization work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The State shall encourage social forces to establish science popularization awards in accordance with law.

### 第二章　组织管理

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级人民政府领导科普工作，应当将科普工作纳入国民经济和社会发展相关规划，为开展科普工作创造良好的环境和条件。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立科普工作协调制度。

**Article 14** People's governments at all levels shall lead science popularization work, incorporate science popularization work into the relevant plans for national economic and social development, and create a favorable environment and conditions for carrying out science popularization work.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination system for science popularization work.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院科学技术行政部门负责制定全国科普工作规划，实行政策引导，进行督促检查，加强统筹协调，推动科普工作发展。  
国务院其他部门按照各自的职责分工，负责有关的科普工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府科学技术行政部门及其他部门在同级人民政府领导下按照各自的职责分工，负责本地区有关的科普工作。

**Article 15** The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the national science popularization work plan, providing policy guidance, conducting supervision and inspection, strengthening overall coordination, and promoting the development of science popularization work.  
Other departments of the State Council shall be responsible for related science popularization work in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
The science and technology administrative departments and other departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the leadership of the people's government at the same level, be responsible for related science popularization work in their respective regions in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　行业主管部门应当结合本行业特点和实际情况，组织开展相关科普活动。

**Article 16** The competent departments of various industries shall, in light of the characteristics and actual circumstances of their respective industries, organize and carry out relevant science popularization activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　科学技术协会是科普工作的主要社会力量，牵头实施全民科学素质行动，组织开展群众性、社会性和经常性的科普活动，加强国际科技人文交流，支持有关组织和企业事业单位开展科普活动，协助政府制定科普工作规划，为政府科普工作决策提供建议和咨询服务。

**Article 17** The science and technology associations shall be the main social force in science popularization work, take the lead in implementing the national scientific literacy action, organize and carry out mass, social, and regular science popularization activities, strengthen international scientific, technological, and cultural exchanges, support relevant organizations, enterprises, and public institutions in carrying out science popularization activities, assist the government in formulating science popularization work plans, and provide suggestions and consulting services for the government's decision-making on science popularization work.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会等群团组织应当结合各自工作对象的特点组织开展科普活动。

**Article 18** Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and other people's organizations shall organize and carry out science popularization activities in light of the characteristics of their respective target groups.

### 第三章　社会责任

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　科普是全社会的共同责任。社会各界都应当组织、参加各类科普活动。

**Article 19** Science popularization is a common responsibility of the whole society. All sectors of society shall organize and participate in various types of science popularization activities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级各类学校及其他教育机构，应当把科普作为素质教育的重要内容，加强科学教育，提升师生科学文化素质，支持和组织师生开展多种形式的科普活动。  
高等学校应当发挥科教资源优势，开设科技相关通识课程，开展科研诚信和科技伦理教育，把科普纳入社会服务职能，提供必要保障。  
中小学校、特殊教育学校应当利用校内、校外资源，提高科学教育质量，完善科学教育课程和实践活动，激发学生对科学的兴趣，培养科学思维、创新意识和创新能力。  
学前教育机构应当根据学前儿童年龄特点和身心发展规律，加强科学启蒙教育，培育、保护好奇心和探索意识。

**Article 20** Schools of all levels and types and other educational institutions shall regard science popularization as an important part of quality education, strengthen science education, improve the scientific and cultural literacy of teachers and students, and support and organize teachers and students to conduct various forms of science popularization activities.  
Institutions of higher education shall leverage their advantages in scientific and educational resources, offer general courses related to science and technology, carry out education on research integrity and science and technology ethics, incorporate science popularization into their social service functions, and provide necessary guarantees.  
Primary and secondary schools and special education schools shall utilize resources both inside and outside the school to improve the quality of science education, improve science education curricula and practical activities, stimulate students' interest in science, and cultivate scientific thinking, innovation awareness, and innovation ability.  
Preschool education institutions shall, based on the age characteristics and physical and mental development patterns of preschool children, strengthen scientific enlightenment education, and cultivate and protect curiosity and the awareness of exploration.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　开放大学、老年大学、老年科技大学、社区学院等应当普及卫生健康、网络通信、智能技术、应急安全等知识技能，提升老年人、残疾人等群体信息获取、识别和应用等能力。

**Article 21** Open universities, universities for the elderly, technology universities for the elderly, community colleges, and the like shall popularize knowledge and skills such as health care, network communication, intelligent technology, and emergency safety, and enhance the ability of the elderly, persons with disabilities, and other groups to obtain, identify, and apply information.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　科学研究和技术开发机构、高等学校应当支持和组织科学技术人员、教师开展科普活动，有条件的可以设置专职科普岗位和专门科普场所，使科普成为机构运行的重要内容，为开展科普活动提供必要的支持和保障，促进科技研发、科技成果转化与科普紧密结合。

**Article 22** Scientific research and technology development institutions and institutions of higher education shall support and organize scientific and technical personnel and teachers to carry out science popularization activities. If conditions permit, they may set up dedicated science popularization positions and specialized science popularization venues, make science popularization an important part of the institution's operation, provide necessary support and guarantees for carrying out science popularization activities, and promote the close integration of scientific and technological research and development, transformation of scientific and technological achievements, and science popularization.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　科技企业应当把科普作为履行社会责任的重要内容，结合科技创新和职工技能培训面向公众开展科普活动。  
鼓励企业将自身科技资源转化为科普资源，向公众开放实验室、生产线等科研、生产设施，有条件的可以设立向公众开放的科普场馆和设施。

**Article 23** Science and technology enterprises shall regard science popularization as an important part of fulfilling their social responsibilities, and carry out science popularization activities for the public in conjunction with scientific and technological innovation and staff skills training.  
Enterprises are encouraged to transform their own scientific and technological resources into science popularization resources, open their laboratories, production lines and other scientific research and production facilities to the public, and if conditions permit, they may establish science popularization venues and facilities open to the public.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　自然科学和社会科学类社会团体等应当组织开展专业领域科普活动，促进科学技术的普及推广。

**Article 24** Social groups in natural sciences and social sciences and the like shall organize and carry out science popularization activities in their professional fields to promote the popularization and dissemination of science and technology.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　新闻出版、电影、广播电视、文化、互联网信息服务等机构和团体应当发挥各自优势做好科普宣传工作。  
综合类报纸、期刊、广播电台、电视台应当开展公益科普宣传；电影、广播电视生产、发行和播映机构应当加强科普作品的制作、发行和播映；书刊出版、发行机构应当扶持科普书刊的出版、发行；综合性互联网平台应当开设科普网页或者科普专区。  
鼓励组织和个人利用新兴媒体开展多种形式的科普，拓展科普渠道和手段。

**Article 25** Institutions and groups in the fields of news and publishing, film, radio and television, culture, internet information services, and the like shall leverage their respective advantages to effectively conduct science popularization publicity.  
Comprehensive newspapers, periodicals, radio stations, and television stations shall carry out public-interest science popularization publicity; film and radio/television production, distribution, and broadcasting institutions shall strengthen the production, distribution, and broadcasting of science popularization works; book and periodical publishing and distribution institutions shall support the publication and distribution of science popularization books and periodicals; comprehensive internet platforms shall set up science popularization web pages or science popularization sections.  
Organizations and individuals are encouraged to use emerging media to carry out various forms of science popularization and expand science popularization channels and means.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农村基层群众性自治组织协助当地人民政府根据当地经济与社会发展的需要，围绕科学生产、文明健康生活，发挥农村科普组织、农村学校、基层医疗卫生机构等作用，开展科普工作，提升农民科学文化素质。  
各类农村经济组织、农业科研和技术推广机构、农民教育培训机构、农村专业技术协（学）会以及科技特派员等，应当开展农民科技培训和农业科技服务，结合推广先进适用技术和科技成果转化应用向农民普及科学技术。

**Article 26** Rural grassroots self-governing organizations shall assist the local people's government in carrying out science popularization work in accordance with the needs of local economic and social development, focusing on scientific production and civilized and healthy living, leveraging the role of rural science popularization organizations, rural schools, and primary-level medical and health institutions, and improving the scientific and cultural literacy of farmers.  
Various types of rural economic organizations, agricultural research and technology extension institutions, farmer education and training institutions, rural professional technical associations (societies), and science and technology commissioners shall carry out scientific and technological training for farmers and agricultural science and technology services, and popularize science and technology to farmers in conjunction with the promotion of advanced and applicable technologies and the transformation and application of scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城市基层群众性自治组织协助当地人民政府利用当地科技、教育、文化、旅游、医疗卫生等资源，结合居民的生活、学习等需要开展科普活动，完善社区综合服务设施科普功能，提高科普服务质量和水平。

**Article 27** Urban grassroots self-governing organizations shall assist the local people's government in utilizing local science and technology, education, culture, tourism, medical and health, and other resources, to carry out science popularization activities in conjunction with the needs of residents' life and study, improve the science popularization functions of community comprehensive service facilities, and enhance the quality and level of science popularization services.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　科技馆（站）、科技活动中心和其他科普教育基地，应当组织开展科普教育活动。图书馆、博物馆、文化馆、规划展览馆等文化场所应当发挥科普教育的作用。  
公园、自然保护地、风景名胜区、商场、机场、车站、码头等各类公共场所以及重大基础设施的经营管理单位，应当在所辖范围内加强科普宣传。

**Article 28** Science and technology museums (stations), science and technology activity centers, and other science popularization education bases shall organize and carry out science popularization education activities. Libraries, museums, cultural centers, planning exhibition halls, and other cultural venues shall play a role in science popularization education.  
The management and operation units of parks, nature reserves, scenic spots, shopping malls, airports, railway stations, docks, and other public places, as well as major infrastructure, shall strengthen science popularization publicity within their respective jurisdictions.

### 第四章　科普活动

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家支持科普产品和服务研究开发，鼓励新颖、独创、科学性强的高质量科普作品创作，提升科普原创能力，依法保护科普成果知识产权。  
鼓励科学研究和技术开发机构、高等学校、企业等依托现有资源并根据发展需要建设科普创作中心。

**Article 29** The State shall support the research and development of science popularization products and services, encourage the creation of novel, original, and highly scientific high-quality science popularization works, enhance the original capacity of science popularization, and protect the intellectual property of science popularization achievements in accordance with law.  
Scientific research and technology development institutions, institutions of higher education, enterprises, and the like are encouraged to build science popularization creation centers based on existing resources and in accordance with development needs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家发展科普产业，鼓励兴办科普企业，促进科普与文化、旅游、体育、卫生健康、农业、生态环保等产业融合发展。

**Article 30** The State shall develop the science popularization industry, encourage the establishment of science popularization enterprises, and promote the integrated development of science popularization with industries such as culture, tourism, sports, health care, agriculture, and ecological and environmental protection.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家推动新技术、新知识在全社会各类人群中的传播与推广，鼓励各类创新主体围绕新技术、新知识开展科普，鼓励在科普中应用新技术，引导社会正确认识和使用科技成果，为科技成果应用创造良好环境。

**Article 31** The State shall promote the dissemination and popularization of new technologies and new knowledge among all groups in society, encourage various innovation entities to carry out science popularization around new technologies and new knowledge, encourage the application of new technologies in science popularization, guide the public to correctly understand and use scientific and technological achievements, and create a favorable environment for the application of scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家部署实施新技术领域重大科技任务，在符合保密法律法规的前提下，可以组织开展必要的科普，增进公众理解、认同和支持。

**Article 32** When the State deploys and implements major scientific and technological tasks in the field of new technologies, it may, on the premise of complying with laws and regulations on confidentiality, organize and carry out necessary science popularization to enhance public understanding, identification, and support.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家加强自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件预防、救援、应急处置等方面的科普工作，加强应急科普资源和平台建设，完善应急科普响应机制，提升公众应急处理能力和自我保护意识。

**Article 33** The State shall strengthen science popularization work in the prevention, rescue, emergency response and other aspects of natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, and other emergencies, strengthen the construction of emergency science popularization resources and platforms, improve the emergency science popularization response mechanism, and enhance the public's emergency handling capabilities and self-protection awareness.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家鼓励在职业培训、农民技能培训和干部教育培训中增加科普内容，促进培育高素质产业工人和农民，提高公职人员科学履职能力。

**Article 34** The State shall encourage the inclusion of science popularization content in vocational training, skills training for farmers, and education and training of cadres, to promote the cultivation of high-quality industrial workers and farmers, and improve the scientific performance capacity of public officials.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　组织和个人提供的科普产品和服务、发布的科普信息应当具有合法性、科学性，不得有虚假错误的内容。

**Article 35** The science popularization products and services provided and the science popularization information published by organizations and individuals shall be lawful and scientific, and shall not contain false or erroneous content.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家加强对科普信息发布和传播的监测与评估。对传播范围广、社会危害大的虚假错误信息，科学技术或者有关主管部门应当按照职责分工及时予以澄清和纠正。  
网络服务提供者发现用户传播虚假错误信息的，应当立即采取处置措施，防止信息扩散。

**Article 36** The State shall strengthen the monitoring and evaluation of the release and dissemination of science popularization information. With respect to false or erroneous information that is widely disseminated and causes great social harm, the science and technology department or the relevant competent departments shall promptly clarify and correct it in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
If an internet service provider discovers that a user is disseminating false or erroneous information, it shall immediately take measures to prevent the spread of such information.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有条件的科普组织和科学技术人员应当结合自身专业特色组织、参与国际科普活动，开展国际科技人文交流，拓展国际科普合作渠道，促进优秀科普成果共享。国家支持开展青少年国际科普交流。

**Article 37** Science popularization organizations and scientific and technical personnel with the necessary conditions shall, in light of their professional characteristics, organize and participate in international science popularization activities, carry out international scientific, technological, and cultural exchanges, expand international science popularization cooperation channels, and promote the sharing of excellent science popularization achievements. The State shall support the conduct of international science popularization exchanges for youth.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家完善科普工作评估体系和公民科学素质监测评估体系，开展科普调查统计和公民科学素质测评，监测和评估科普事业发展成效。

**Article 38** The State shall improve the evaluation system for science popularization work and the monitoring and evaluation system for citizens' scientific literacy, conduct survey statistics on science popularization and assessment of citizens' scientific literacy, and monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of the development of the science popularization cause.

### 第五章　科普人员

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家加强科普工作人员培训和交流，提升科普工作人员思想道德品质、科学文化素质和业务水平，建立专业化科普工作人员队伍。

**Article 39** The State shall strengthen the training and exchange of science popularization personnel, enhance their ideological and moral quality, scientific and cultural literacy, and professional competence, and establish a professional team of science popularization personnel.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　科学技术人员和教师应当发挥自身优势和专长，积极参与和支持科普活动。  
科技领军人才和团队应当发挥表率作用，带头开展科普。  
鼓励和支持老年科学技术人员积极参与科普工作。

**Article 40** Scientific and technical personnel and teachers shall give full play to their own advantages and expertise, and actively participate in and support science popularization activities.  
Leading scientific and technological talents and teams shall play an exemplary role and take the lead in carrying out science popularization.  
Elderly scientific and technical personnel shall be encouraged and supported to actively participate in science popularization work.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家支持有条件的高等学校、职业学校设置和完善科普相关学科和专业，培养科普专业人才。

**Article 41** The State shall support institutions of higher education and vocational schools with the necessary conditions to set up and improve science popularization-related disciplines and majors, and cultivate professional science popularization talents.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家完善科普志愿服务制度和工作体系，支持志愿者开展科普志愿服务，加强培训与监督。

**Article 42** The State shall improve the science popularization volunteer service system and work system, support volunteers in conducting science popularization volunteer services, and strengthen training and supervision.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家健全科普人员评价、激励机制，鼓励相关单位建立符合科普特点的职称评定、绩效考核等评价制度，为科普人员提供有效激励。

**Article 43** The State shall improve the evaluation and incentive mechanisms for science popularization personnel, encourage relevant units to establish evaluation systems for professional title assessment and performance assessment that are consistent with the characteristics of science popularization, and provide effective incentives for science popularization personnel.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　各级人民政府应当将科普经费列入本级预算，完善科普投入经费保障机制，逐步提高科普投入水平，保障科普工作顺利开展。  
各级人民政府有关部门应当根据需要安排经费支持科普工作。

**Article 44** People's governments at all levels shall include science popularization funds in their respective budgets, improve the funding guarantee mechanism for science popularization input, gradually increase the level of science popularization input, and ensure the smooth conduct of science popularization work.  
The relevant departments of people's governments at all levels shall, as needed, arrange funds to support science popularization work.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家完善科普场馆和科普基地建设布局，扩大科普设施覆盖面，促进城乡科普设施均衡发展。  
国家鼓励有条件的地方和组织建设综合型科普场馆和专业型科普场馆，发展数字科普场馆，推进科普信息化发展，加强与社区建设、文化设施融合发展。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和其他有条件的地方人民政府，应当将科普场馆、设施建设纳入国土空间规划；对现有科普场馆、设施应当加强利用、维修和改造升级。

**Article 45** The State shall improve the layout of science popularization venues and science popularization bases, expand the coverage of science popularization facilities, and promote the balanced development of science popularization facilities in urban and rural areas.  
The State shall encourage localities and organizations with the necessary conditions to build comprehensive science popularization venues and specialized science popularization venues, develop digital science popularization venues, promote the development of science popularization informatization, and strengthen integrated development with community construction and cultural facilities.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and other local people's governments with the necessary conditions, shall incorporate the construction of science popularization venues and facilities into territorial spatial planning; they shall strengthen the utilization, maintenance, renovation, and upgrading of existing science popularization venues and facilities.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　各级人民政府应当对符合规划的科普场馆、设施建设给予支持，开展财政性资金资助的科普场馆运营绩效评估，保障科普场馆有效运行。  
政府投资建设的科普场馆，应当配备必要的专职人员，常年向公众开放，对青少年实行免费或者优惠，并不得擅自改为他用；经费困难的，政府可以根据需要予以补贴，使其正常运行。  
尚无条件建立科普场馆的地方，应当利用现有的科技、教育、文化、旅游、医疗卫生、体育、交通运输、应急等设施开展科普，并设立科普画廊、橱窗等。

**Article 46** People's governments at all levels shall support the construction of science popularization venues and facilities that conform to the plan, conduct performance evaluation of the operation of science popularization venues funded by fiscal funds, and ensure the effective operation of science popularization venues.  
Science popularization venues built with government investment shall be equipped with necessary full-time personnel, be open to the public throughout the year, provide free or discounted admission to young people, and may not be used for other purposes without authorization; if there are funding difficulties, the government may provide subsidies as needed to maintain their normal operation.  
In localities that do not yet have the conditions to build science popularization venues, existing facilities in science and technology, education, culture, tourism, medical and health care, sports, transportation, emergency response, and other fields shall be used to carry out science popularization, and science popularization galleries, display windows, etc. shall be set up.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家建设完善开放、共享的国家科普资源库和科普资源公共服务平台，推动全社会科普资源共建共享。  
利用财政性资金设立的科学研究和技术开发机构、高等学校、职业学校，有条件的应当向公众开放科技基础设施和科技资源，为公众了解、认识、参与科学研究活动提供便利。

**Article 47** The State shall build and improve an open and shared national science popularization resource database and a public service platform for science popularization resources, and promote the joint construction and sharing of science popularization resources across the whole society.  
Scientific research and technology development institutions, institutions of higher education, and vocational schools established with fiscal funds shall, if conditions permit, open their science and technology infrastructure and resources to the public to facilitate the public's understanding, knowledge, and participation in scientific research activities.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家鼓励和引导社会资金投入科普事业。国家鼓励境内外的组织和个人设立科普基金，用于资助科普事业。

**Article 48** The State shall encourage and guide the investment of social funds in the science popularization cause. The State shall encourage organizations and individuals within and outside the territory to establish science popularization funds to be used for funding the science popularization cause.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家鼓励境内外的组织和个人依法捐赠财产资助科普事业；对捐赠财产用于科普事业或者投资建设科普场馆、设施的，依法给予优惠。  
科普组织开展科普活动、兴办科普事业，可以依法获得资助和捐赠。

**Article 49** The State shall encourage organizations and individuals within and outside the territory to donate property in accordance with law to support the science popularization cause; those who donate property for the science popularization cause or invest in the construction of science popularization venues and facilities shall be granted preferences in accordance with law.  
Science popularization organizations carrying out science popularization activities and establishing science popularization undertakings may obtain funding and donations in accordance with law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家依法对科普事业实行税收优惠。

**Article 50** The State shall implement tax preferences for the science popularization cause in accordance with law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　利用财政性资金设立科学技术计划项目，除涉密项目外，应当结合任务需求，合理设置科普工作任务，充分发挥社会效益。

**Article 51** For science and technology plan projects established using fiscal funds, except for projects involving state secrets, science popularization tasks shall be reasonably set based on task requirements to fully leverage social benefits.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　科学研究和技术开发机构、学校、企业的主管部门以及科学技术等相关行政部门应当支持开展科普活动，建立有利于促进科普的评价标准和制度机制。

**Article 52** The competent departments of scientific research and technological development institutions, schools, enterprises, and relevant administrative departments such as science and technology shall support the development of science popularization activities and establish evaluation standards and institutional mechanisms conducive to promoting science popularization.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　科普经费和组织、个人资助科普事业的财产，应当用于科普事业，任何组织和个人不得克扣、截留、挪用。

**Article 53** Science popularization funds and property donated by organizations or individuals to the science popularization cause shall be used for the science popularization cause, and no organization or individual shall withhold, intercept, or misappropriate them.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，制作、发布、传播虚假错误信息，或者以科普为名损害国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的，由有关主管部门责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，没收违法所得，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 54** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, produces, publishes, or disseminates false or erroneous information, or harms the national interest, public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others in the name of science popularization, the relevant competent department shall order correction, give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism, confiscate illegal gains, and the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，克扣、截留、挪用科普款物或者骗取科普优惠政策支持的，由有关主管部门责令限期退还相关款物；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，禁止一定期限内申请科普优惠政策支持。

**Article 55** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, withholds, intercepts, or misappropriates science popularization funds or materials, or defrauds for support under science popularization preferential policies, the relevant competent department shall order the return of the relevant funds and materials within a time limit; the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be prohibited from applying for support under science popularization preferential policies for a certain period.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　擅自将政府投资建设的科普场馆改为他用的，由有关主管部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，给予警告或者通报批评，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 56** Where anyone, without authorization, converts science popularization venues constructed with government investment to other uses, the relevant competent department shall order correction within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, a warning or a circulate of criticism shall be given, and the responsible leaders and directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　骗取科普表彰、奖励的，由授予表彰、奖励的部门或者单位撤销其所获荣誉，收回奖章、证书，追回其所获奖金等物质奖励，并由其所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 57** Where anyone defrauds for science popularization commendations or awards, the department or unit that granted the commendation or award shall revoke the honors obtained, reclaim the medals and certificates, recover the monetary awards and other material rewards obtained, and the unit to which the person belongs or the relevant department shall give sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　公职人员在科普工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 58** Where public officials abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in favoritism and irregularities in science popularization work, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，造成人身损害或者财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes personal injury or property damage, the person shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the person shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 60** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
