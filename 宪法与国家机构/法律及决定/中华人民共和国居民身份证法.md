# 中华人民共和国居民身份证法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2011-10-29  
**生效日期**：2012-01-01

> （2003年6月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议通过　根据2011年10月29日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国居民身份证法〉的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　申领和发放 第三章　使用和查验 第四章　法律责任 第五章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了证明居住在中华人民共和国境内的公民的身份，保障公民的合法权益，便利公民进行社会活动，维护社会秩序，制定本法。 第二条　居住在中华人民共和国境内的年满十六周岁的中国公民，应当依照本法的规定申请领取居民身份证；未满十六周岁的中国公民，可以依照本法的规定申请领取居民身份证。 第三条　居民身份证登记的项目包括：姓名、性别、民族、出生日期、常住户口所在地住址、公民身份号码、本人相片、指纹信息、证件的有效期和签发机关。 公民身份号码是每个公民唯一的、终身不变的身份代码，由公安机关按照公民身份号码国家标准编制。 公民申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，应当登记指纹信息。 第四条　居民身份证使用规范汉字和符合国家标准的数字符号填写。 民族自治地方的自治机关根据本地区的实际情况，对居民身份证用汉字登记的内容，可以决定同时使用实行区域自治的民族的文字或者选用一种当地通用的文字。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了证明居住在中华人民共和国境内的公民的身份，保障公民的合法权益，便利公民进行社会活动，维护社会秩序，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of proving the identity of citizens residing within the territory of the People's Republic of China, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, facilitating citizens' social activities, and maintaining social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　居住在中华人民共和国境内的年满十六周岁的中国公民，应当依照本法的规定申请领取居民身份证；未满十六周岁的中国公民，可以依照本法的规定申请领取居民身份证。

**Article 2** Chinese citizens who have reached the age of sixteen and reside within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall apply for resident identity cards in accordance with the provisions of this Law; Chinese citizens under the age of sixteen may apply for resident identity cards in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　居民身份证登记的项目包括：姓名、性别、民族、出生日期、常住户口所在地住址、公民身份号码、本人相片、指纹信息、证件的有效期和签发机关。  
公民身份号码是每个公民唯一的、终身不变的身份代码，由公安机关按照公民身份号码国家标准编制。  
公民申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，应当登记指纹信息。

**Article 3** The items registered in a resident identity card include: name, sex, ethnicity, date of birth, address of permanent residence, citizen identity number, personal photograph, fingerprint information, validity period of the card, and the issuing authority.  
The citizen identity number is a unique and lifelong identity code for each citizen, compiled by the public security organ in accordance with the national standards for citizen identity numbers.  
When a citizen applies for, renews, or replaces a resident identity card, fingerprint information shall be registered.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　居民身份证使用规范汉字和符合国家标准的数字符号填写。  
民族自治地方的自治机关根据本地区的实际情况，对居民身份证用汉字登记的内容，可以决定同时使用实行区域自治的民族的文字或者选用一种当地通用的文字。

**Article 4** Resident identity cards shall be filled in using standard Chinese characters and numerical symbols that conform to national standards.  
The self-governing organs of ethnic autonomous areas may, based on the actual conditions of their regions, decide to also use the script of the ethnic group exercising regional autonomy or choose a commonly used local script for the content registered in Chinese characters on the resident identity card.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　十六周岁以上公民的居民身份证的有效期为十年、二十年、长期。十六周岁至二十五周岁的，发给有效期十年的居民身份证；二十六周岁至四十五周岁的，发给有效期二十年的居民身份证；四十六周岁以上的，发给长期有效的居民身份证。  
未满十六周岁的公民，自愿申请领取居民身份证的，发给有效期五年的居民身份证。

**Article 5** The validity period of a resident identity card for citizens aged sixteen or above is ten years, twenty years, or long-term. For citizens aged sixteen to twenty-five, a resident identity card with a validity period of ten years shall be issued; for citizens aged twenty-six to forty-five, a resident identity card with a validity period of twenty years shall be issued; for citizens aged forty-six or above, a resident identity card with a long-term validity shall be issued.  
For citizens under the age of sixteen who voluntarily apply for a resident identity card, a card with a validity period of five years shall be issued.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　居民身份证式样由国务院公安部门制定。居民身份证由公安机关统一制作、发放。  
居民身份证具备视读与机读两种功能，视读、机读的内容限于[本法第三条](#art-3)第一款规定的项目。  
公安机关及其人民警察对因制作、发放、查验、扣押居民身份证而知悉的公民的个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 6** The design of the resident identity card shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council. Resident identity cards shall be uniformly produced and issued by the public security organs.  
A resident identity card shall have both visual and machine-readable functions, and the content for visual and machine reading shall be limited to the items specified in paragraph 1 of Article 3 of this Law.  
Public security organs and their people's police shall keep confidential the personal information of citizens learned in the course of producing, issuing, checking, or seizing resident identity cards.

### 第二章　申领和发放

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公民应当自年满十六周岁之日起三个月内，向常住户口所在地的公安机关申请领取居民身份证。  
未满十六周岁的公民，由监护人代为申请领取居民身份证。

**Article 7** A citizen shall, within three months from the date of reaching the age of sixteen, apply to the public security organ at the place of permanent residence for a resident identity card.  
For a citizen under the age of sixteen, the guardian shall apply on his or her behalf for a resident identity card.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　居民身份证由居民常住户口所在地的县级人民政府公安机关签发。

**Article 8** The resident identity card shall be issued by the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where the citizen's permanent residence is located.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　香港同胞、澳门同胞、台湾同胞迁入内地定居的，华侨回国定居的，以及外国人、无国籍人在中华人民共和国境内定居并被批准加入或者恢复中华人民共和国国籍的，在办理常住户口登记时，应当依照本法规定申请领取居民身份证。

**Article 9** Compatriots from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan who move to the mainland for settlement, overseas Chinese who return to China for settlement, and foreigners or stateless persons who settle within the territory of the People's Republic of China and are approved to acquire or restore Chinese nationality shall, when undergoing permanent residence registration, apply for resident identity cards in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请领取居民身份证，应当填写《居民身份证申领登记表》，交验居民户口簿。

**Article 10** To apply for a resident identity card, the applicant shall fill in the "Resident Identity Card Application Registration Form" and submit for inspection the resident household registration booklet.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家决定换发新一代居民身份证、居民身份证有效期满、公民姓名变更或者证件严重损坏不能辨认的，公民应当换领新证；居民身份证登记项目出现错误的，公安机关应当及时更正，换发新证；领取新证时，必须交回原证。居民身份证丢失的，应当申请补领。  
未满十六周岁公民的居民身份证有前款情形的，可以申请换领、换发或者补领新证。  
公民办理常住户口迁移手续时，公安机关应当在居民身份证的机读项目中记载公民常住户口所在地住址变动的情况，并告知本人。

**Article 11** Where the State decides to issue a new generation of resident identity cards, the validity period of a resident identity card expires, the citizen's name is changed, or the card is severely damaged and illegible, the citizen shall replace it with a new card; if there is an error in the registered items of a resident identity card, the public security organ shall promptly correct it and issue a new card; when receiving a new card, the original card must be surrendered. If a resident identity card is lost, the citizen shall apply for a replacement.  
If a resident identity card of a citizen under the age of sixteen falls under the circumstances in the preceding paragraph, he or she may apply for a replacement, reissuance, or supplementary issuance of a new card.  
When a citizen undergoes procedures for permanent residence transfer, the public security organ shall record in the machine-readable items of the resident identity card the change in the address of the citizen's permanent residence and inform the citizen.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公民申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，公安机关应当按照规定及时予以办理。公安机关应当自公民提交《居民身份证申领登记表》之日起六十日内发放居民身份证；交通不便的地区，办理时间可以适当延长，但延长的时间不得超过三十日。  
公民在申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证期间，急需使用居民身份证的，可以申请领取临时居民身份证，公安机关应当按照规定及时予以办理。具体办法由国务院公安部门规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/现役军人和人民武装警察居民身份证申领发放办法.md#art-7" title="被《现役军人和人民武装警察居民身份证申领发放办法》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** Where a citizen applies for, renews, or replaces a resident identity card, the public security organ shall handle it in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions. The public security organ shall issue the resident identity card within sixty days from the date the citizen submits the "Resident Identity Card Application Registration Form"; in areas with inconvenient transportation, the processing time may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed thirty days.  
If a citizen urgently needs to use a resident identity card during the period of applying for, renewing, or replacing it, he or she may apply for a temporary resident identity card, and the public security organ shall handle it in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the public security department of the State Council.

### 第三章　使用和查验

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公民从事有关活动，需要证明身份的，有权使用  
居民身份证证明身份，有关单位及其工作人员不得拒绝。  
有关单位及其工作人员对履行职责或者提供服务过程中获得的居民身份证记载的公民个人信息，应当予以保密。

**Article 13** A citizen who needs to prove his or her identity when engaging in relevant activities shall have the right to use the resident identity card to prove his or her identity, and the relevant units and their staff shall not refuse.  
The relevant units and their staff shall keep confidential the personal information of citizens recorded in the resident identity card obtained in the course of performing duties or providing services.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　有下列情形之一的，公民应当出示居民身份证证明身份：  
（一）常住户口登记项目变更；  
（二）兵役登记；  
（三）婚姻登记、收养登记；  
（四）申请办理出境手续；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定需要用居民身份证证明身份的其他情形。  
依照本法规定未取得居民身份证的公民，从事前款规定的有关活动，可以使用符合国家规定的其他证明方式证明身份。

**Article 14** In any of the following circumstances, a citizen shall present a resident identity card to prove his or her identity:  
(1) Change of permanent residence registration items;  
(2) Military service registration;  
(3) Marriage registration or adoption registration;  
(4) Application for exit formalities;  
(5) Other circumstances where laws or administrative regulations require the use of a resident identity card to prove identity.  
A citizen who has not obtained a resident identity card in accordance with the provisions of this Law may use other means of proof that conform to national regulations to prove his or her identity when engaging in relevant activities specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民警察依法执行职务，遇有下列情形之一的，经出示执法证件，可以查验居民身份证：  
（一）对有违法犯罪嫌疑的人员，需要查明身份的；  
（二）依法实施现场管制时，需要查明有关人员身份的；  
（三）发生严重危害社会治安突发事件时，需要查明现场有关人员身份的；  
（四）在火车站、长途汽车站、港口、码头、机场或者在重大活动期间设区的市级人民政府规定的场所，需要查明有关人员身份的；  
（五）法律规定需要查明身份的其他情形。  
有前款所列情形之一，拒绝人民警察查验居民身份证的，依照有关法律规定，分别不同情形，采取措施予以处理。  
任何组织或者个人不得扣押居民身份证。但是，公安机关依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》执行监视居住强制措施的情形除外。

**Article 15** When performing duties according to law, the people's police may, upon presenting their law enforcement credentials, check resident identity cards in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the identity of a person suspected of illegal or criminal activities needs to be ascertained;  
(2) Where the identity of relevant persons needs to be ascertained when on-site control is implemented according to law;  
(3) Where the identity of relevant persons at the scene needs to be ascertained in the event of a serious emergency endangering public security;  
(4) Where the identity of relevant persons needs to be ascertained at railway stations, long-distance bus stations, ports, docks, airports, or at places specified by the people's government of a city divided into districts during major events;  
(5) Other circumstances where identity needs to be ascertained as provided by law.  
If a person refuses to be checked by the people's police under any of the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph, measures shall be taken to handle the matter according to the relevant laws, differentiated by the specific circumstances.  
No organization or individual may seize a resident identity card. However, this does not apply to circumstances where a public security organ executes compulsory measures of residential surveillance in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关给予警告，并处二百元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）使用虚假证明材料骗领居民身份证的；  
（二）出租、出借、转让居民身份证的；  
（三）非法扣押他人居民身份证的。

**Article 16** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning by the public security organ and shall also be fined not more than 200 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) Obtaining a resident identity card by fraud using false evidentiary materials;  
(2) Renting, lending, or transferring a resident identity card;  
(3) Unlawfully seizing another person's resident identity card.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关处二百元以上一千元以下罚款，或者处十日以下拘留，有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）冒用他人居民身份证或者使用骗领的居民身份证的；  
（二）购买、出售、使用伪造、变造的居民身份证的。  
伪造、变造的居民身份证和骗领的居民身份证，由公安机关予以收缴。

**Article 17** Any person who commits one of the following acts shall be fined by the public security organ not less than 200 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan, or shall be detained for not more than ten days; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) Using another person's resident identity card under false pretenses or using a fraudulently obtained resident identity card;  
(2) Purchasing, selling, or using forged or altered resident identity cards.  
Forged or altered resident identity cards and fraudulently obtained resident identity cards shall be confiscated by the public security organ.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　伪造、变造居民身份证的，依法追究刑事责任。  
有[本法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条所列行为之一，从事犯罪活动的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Any person who forges or alters a resident identity card shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.  
Any person who commits any of the acts listed in Article 16 or Article 17 of this Law and engages in criminal activities shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家机关或者金融、电信、交通、教育、医疗等单位的工作人员泄露在履行职责或者提供服务过程中获得的居民身份证记载的公民个人信息，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处五千元罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
单位有前款行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处十日以上十五日以下拘留，并处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
有前两款行为，对他人造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 19** If a staff member of a state organ or a unit such as a financial, telecommunications, transportation, education, or medical institution discloses the personal information of a citizen recorded in a resident identity card obtained in the course of performing duties or providing services, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose detention of not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days, and a fine of 5,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
If a unit commits the act in the preceding paragraph and it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose detention of not less than ten days but not more than fifteen days on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
If the acts in the preceding two paragraphs cause damage to others, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民警察有下列行为之一的，根据情节轻重，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）利用制作、发放、查验居民身份证的便利，收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
（二）非法变更公民身份号码，或者在居民身份证上登载[本法第三条](#art-3)第一款规定项目以外的信息或者故意登载虚假信息的；  
（三）无正当理由不在法定期限内发放居民身份证的；  
（四）违反规定查验、扣押居民身份证，侵害公民合法权益的；  
（五）泄露因制作、发放、查验、扣押居民身份证而知悉的公民个人信息，侵害公民合法权益的。

**Article 20** If a people's policeman commits one of the following acts, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction according to law based on the seriousness of the circumstances; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Taking advantage of the convenience of producing, issuing, or checking resident identity cards to accept property or seek other benefits from others;  
(2) Unlawfully altering a citizen identity number, or registering information other than the items specified in paragraph 1 of Article 3 of this Law on a resident identity card, or deliberately registering false information;  
(3) Failing to issue a resident identity card within the statutory time limit without justifiable reasons;  
(4) Violating regulations by checking or seizing resident identity cards, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens;  
(5) Disclosing personal information of citizens learned in the course of producing, issuing, checking, or seizing resident identity cards, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公民申请领取、换领、补领居民身份证，应当缴纳证件工本费。居民身份证工本费标准，由国务院价格主管部门会同国务院财政部门核定。  
对城市中领取最低生活保障金的居民、农村中有特殊生活困难的居民，在其初次申请领取和换领居民身份证时，免收工本费。对其他生活确有困难的居民，在其初次申请领取和换领居民身份证时，可以减收工本费。免收和减收工本费的具体办法，由国务院财政部门会同国务院价格主管部门规定。  
公安机关收取的居民身份证工本费，全部上缴国库。

**Article 21** A citizen who applies for, renews, or replaces a resident identity card shall pay the production cost of the card. The standard for the production cost of a resident identity card shall be verified by the competent department of price under the State Council together with the competent department of finance under the State Council.  
For residents in cities who receive minimum subsistence allowances and residents in rural areas with special living difficulties, the production cost shall be exempted when they initially apply for or renew a resident identity card. For other residents who indeed have living difficulties, the production cost may be reduced when they initially apply for or renew a resident identity card. The specific measures for exemption and reduction of the production cost shall be prescribed by the competent department of finance under the State Council together with the competent department of price under the State Council.  
The production costs of resident identity cards collected by public security organs shall all be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　现役的人民解放军军人、人民武装警察申请领取和发放居民身份证的具体办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会另行规定。

**Article 22** The specific measures for active-duty servicemen of the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police to apply for and be issued resident identity cards shall be separately prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本法自2004年1月1日起施行，《中华人民共和国居民身份证条例》同时废止。  
依照《中华人民共和国居民身份证条例》领取的居民身份证，自2013年1月1日起停止使用。依照本法在2012年1月1日以前领取的居民身份证，在其有效期内，继续有效。  
国家决定换发新一代居民身份证后，原居民身份证的停止使用日期由国务院决定。

**Article 23** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2004, and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Resident Identity Cards shall be abolished simultaneously.  
Resident identity cards obtained in accordance with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Resident Identity Cards shall cease to be valid as of January 1, 2013. Resident identity cards obtained in accordance with this Law before January 1, 2012 shall continue to be valid within their validity period.  
After the State decides to issue a new generation of resident identity cards, the date on which the original resident identity cards cease to be valid shall be decided by the State Council.
