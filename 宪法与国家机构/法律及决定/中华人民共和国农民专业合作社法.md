# 中华人民共和国农民专业合作社法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2017-12-27  
**生效日期**：2018-07-01

> （2006年10月31日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过　2017年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十一次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　设立和登记 第三章　成　　员 第四章　组织机构 第五章　财务管理 第六章　合并、分立、解散和清算 第七章　农民专业合作社联合社 第八章　扶持措施 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范农民专业合作社的组织和行为，鼓励、支持、引导农民专业合作社的发展，保护农民专业合作社及其成员的合法权益，推进农业农村现代化，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称农民专业合作社，是指在农村家庭承包经营基础上，农产品的生产经营者或者农业生产经营服务的提供者、利用者，自愿联合、民主管理的互助性经济组织。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范农民专业合作社的组织和行为，鼓励、支持、引导农民专业合作社的发展，保护农民专业合作社及其成员的合法权益，推进农业农村现代化，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating the organization and conduct of farmers' specialized cooperatives, encouraging, supporting and guiding the development of farmers' specialized cooperatives, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of farmers' specialized cooperatives and their members, and promoting the modernization of agriculture and rural areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农民专业合作社，是指在农村家庭承包经营基础上，农产品的生产经营者或者农业生产经营服务的提供者、利用者，自愿联合、民主管理的互助性经济组织。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "farmers' specialized cooperative" means a mutual-aid economic organization voluntarily united and democratically managed by producers and operators of agricultural products or providers and users of agricultural production and operation services on the basis of the rural household contract management system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　农民专业合作社以其成员为主要服务对象，开展以下一种或者多种业务：  
（一）农业生产资料的购买、使用；  
（二）农产品的生产、销售、加工、运输、贮藏及其他相关服务；  
（三）农村民间工艺及制品、休闲农业和乡村旅游资源的开发经营等；  
（四）与农业生产经营有关的技术、信息、设施建设运营等服务。

**Article 3** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall take its members as the main service targets and carry out one or more of the following businesses:  
(1) purchase and use of agricultural means of production;  
(2) production, sale, processing, transportation, storage and other related services of agricultural products;  
(3) development and operation of rural folk arts and crafts, leisure agriculture and rural tourism resources, etc.;  
(4) services such as technology, information, facility construction and operation related to agricultural production and operation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　农民专业合作社应当遵循下列原则：  
（一）成员以农民为主体；  
（二）以服务成员为宗旨，谋求全体成员的共同利益；  
（三）入社自愿、退社自由；  
（四）成员地位平等，实行民主管理；  
（五）盈余主要按照成员与农民专业合作社的交易量（额）比例返还。

**Article 4** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall follow the following principles:  
(1) members are mainly farmers;  
(2) aiming at serving the members and seeking common interests of all members;  
(3) voluntary membership and free withdrawal;  
(4) equal status of members and democratic management;  
(5) surplus is mainly returned in proportion to the volume (amount) of transactions between members and the cooperative.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　农民专业合作社依照本法登记，取得法人资格。  
农民专业合作社对由成员出资、公积金、国家财政直接补助、他人捐赠以及合法取得的其他资产所形成的财产，享有占有、使用和处分的权利，并以上述财产对债务承担责任。

**Article 5** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall, upon registration in accordance with this Law, acquire the status of a legal person.  
A farmers' specialized cooperative shall have the right to possess, use and dispose of the property formed by members' capital contributions, accumulation funds, direct subsidies from state finance, donations from others and other lawfully obtained assets, and shall be liable for its debts with the aforesaid property.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　农民专业合作社成员以其账户内记载的出资额和公积金份额为限对农民专业合作社承担责任。

**Article 6** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall bear liability for the cooperative to the extent of the capital contribution and the accumulation fund share recorded in his/her account.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家保障农民专业合作社享有与其他市场主体平等的法律地位。  
国家保护农民专业合作社及其成员的合法权益，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。

**Article 7** The State shall ensure that farmers' specialized cooperatives enjoy equal legal status with other market entities.  
The State shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of farmers' specialized cooperatives and their members, and no entity or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　农民专业合作社从事生产经营活动，应当遵守法律，遵守社会公德、商业道德，诚实守信，不得从事与章程规定无关的活动。

**Article 8** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall, when engaging in production and operation activities, abide by laws, social morality and business ethics, act in good faith, and shall not engage in activities irrelevant to the provisions of its articles of association.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　农民专业合作社为扩大生产经营和服务的规模，发展产业化经营，提高市场竞争力，可以依法自愿设立或者加入农民专业合作社联合社。

**Article 9** A farmers' specialized cooperative may, in order to expand the scale of production, operation and services, develop industrialized operation and enhance market competitiveness, voluntarily establish or join a farmers' specialized cooperative union according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家通过财政支持、税收优惠和金融、科技、人才的扶持以及产业政策引导等措施，促进农民专业合作社的发展。  
国家鼓励和支持公民、法人和其他组织为农民专业合作社提供帮助和服务。  
对发展农民专业合作社事业做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** The State shall promote the development of farmers' specialized cooperatives through measures such as financial support, tax preferences, support in finance, science and technology and human resources, and industrial policy guidance.  
The State shall encourage and support citizens, legal persons and other organizations to provide assistance and services to farmers' specialized cooperatives.  
Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of farmers' specialized cooperatives shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府应当建立农民专业合作社工作的综合协调机制，统筹指导、协调、推动农民专业合作社的建设和发展。  
县级以上人民政府农业主管部门、其他有关部门和组织应当依据各自职责，对农民专业合作社的建设和发展给予指导、扶持和服务。

**Article 11** The people's government at or above the county level shall establish a comprehensive coordination mechanism for the work on farmers' specialized cooperatives, and shall coordinate, guide and promote the construction and development of farmers' specialized cooperatives.  
The competent agricultural departments and other relevant departments and organizations of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, provide guidance, support and services for the construction and development of farmers' specialized cooperatives.

### 第二章　设立和登记

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设立农民专业合作社，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有五名以上符合[本法第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条规定的成员；  
（二）有符合本法规定的章程；  
（三）有符合本法规定的组织机构；  
（四）有符合法律、行政法规规定的名称和章程确定的住所；  
（五）有符合章程规定的成员出资。

**Article 12** To establish a farmers' specialized cooperative, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) there are five or more members who meet the requirements of Articles 19 and 20 of this Law;  
(2) there are articles of association that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(3) there are organizational structures that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) there is a name that complies with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and a domicile determined by the articles of association;  
(5) there are capital contributions from members that comply with the provisions of the articles of association.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农民专业合作社成员可以用货币出资，也可以用实物、知识产权、土地经营权、林权等可以用货币估价并可以依法转让的非货币财产，以及章程规定的其他方式作价出资；但是，法律、行政法规规定不得作为出资的财产除外。  
农民专业合作社成员不得以对该社或者其他成员的债权，充抵出资；不得以缴纳的出资，抵销对该社或者其他成员的债务。

**Article 13** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative may make capital contributions in currency, or in kind, intellectual property, land management right, forest right or other non-monetary property that can be valued in currency and transferred according to law, and other methods of contribution as prescribed in the articles of association; provided that property which may not be used as capital contribution under laws or administrative regulations shall be excluded.  
A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative may not set off capital contributions against claims held by such member against the cooperative or other members, nor may the member set off debts owed to the cooperative or other members against the contributed capital.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立农民专业合作社，应当召开由全体设立人参加的设立大会。设立时自愿成为该社成员的人为设立人。  
设立大会行使下列职权：  
（一）通过本社章程，章程应当由全体设立人一致通过；  
（二）选举产生理事长、理事、执行监事或者监事会成员；  
（三）审议其他重大事项。

**Article 14** To establish a farmers' specialized cooperative, an inaugural meeting attended by all founders shall be convened. The persons who voluntarily become members of the cooperative at the time of establishment shall be the founders.  
The inaugural meeting shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) to adopt the articles of association of the cooperative, which shall be unanimously adopted by all founders;  
(2) to elect the chairperson, directors, executive supervisor or members of the board of supervisors;  
(3) to deliberate on other major matters.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农民专业合作社章程应当载明下列事项：  
（一）名称和住所；  
（二）业务范围；  
（三）成员资格及入社、退社和除名；  
（四）成员的权利和义务；  
（五）组织机构及其产生办法、职权、任期、议事规则；  
（六）成员的出资方式、出资额，成员出资的转让、继承、担保；  
（七）财务管理和盈余分配、亏损处理；  
（八）章程修改程序；  
（九）解散事由和清算办法；  
（十）公告事项及发布方式；  
（十一）附加表决权的设立、行使方式和行使范围；  
（十二）需要载明的其他事项。

**Article 15** The articles of association of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the name and domicile;  
(2) the business scope;  
(3) membership qualifications, admission, withdrawal and expulsion;  
(4) the rights and obligations of members;  
(5) the organizational structure and the method of its formation, functions and powers, term of office, and rules of procedure;  
(6) the method and amount of capital contribution by members, and the transfer, succession and pledge of members' capital contributions;  
(7) financial management, surplus distribution and loss treatment;  
(8) the procedure for amending the articles of association;  
(9) the grounds for dissolution and the method of liquidation;  
(10) matters for public announcement and the means of publication;  
(11) the establishment, exercise method and scope of exercise of additional voting rights;  
(12) other matters that need to be specified.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　设立农民专业合作社，应当向工商行政管理部门提交下列文件，申请设立登记：  
（一）登记申请书；  
（二）全体设立人签名、盖章的设立大会纪要；  
（三）全体设立人签名、盖章的章程；  
（四）法定代表人、理事的任职文件及身份证明；  
（五）出资成员签名、盖章的出资清单；  
（六）住所使用证明；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他文件。  
登记机关应当自受理登记申请之日起二十日内办理完毕，向符合登记条件的申请者颁发营业执照，登记类型为农民专业合作社。  
农民专业合作社法定登记事项变更的，应当申请变更登记。  
登记机关应当将农民专业合作社的登记信息通报同级农业等有关部门。  
农民专业合作社登记办法由国务院规定。办理登记不得收取费用。

**Article 16** To establish a farmers' specialized cooperative, the following documents shall be submitted to the administrative department for industry and commerce for registration of establishment:  
(1) a written application for registration;  
(2) the minutes of the inaugural meeting signed and sealed by all founders;  
(3) the articles of association signed and sealed by all founders;  
(4) the appointment documents and identity certificates of the legal representative and directors;  
(5) a list of capital contributions signed and sealed by the contributing members;  
(6) proof of use of the domicile;  
(7) other documents as required by laws and administrative regulations.  
The registration authority shall complete the processing within twenty days from the date of accepting the application for registration, and issue a business license to the applicant that meets the registration conditions, with the registration type being "farmers' specialized cooperative".  
If the statutory registration items of a farmers' specialized cooperative change, an application for change of registration shall be filed.  
The registration authority shall notify the relevant departments such as the agricultural department at the same level of the registration information of the farmers' specialized cooperative.  
The measures for registration of farmers' specialized cooperatives shall be formulated by the State Council. No fees may be charged for registration.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　农民专业合作社应当按照国家有关规定，向登记机关报送年度报告，并向社会公示。

**Article 17** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, submit an annual report to the registration authority and make it public.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农民专业合作社可以依法向公司等企业投资，以其出资额为限对所投资企业承担责任。

**Article 18** A farmers' specialized cooperative may, according to law, invest in companies and other enterprises, and shall bear liability for the invested enterprise to the extent of its capital contribution.

### 第三章　成员

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　具有民事行为能力的公民，以及从事与农民专业合作社业务直接有关的生产经营活动的企业、事业单位或者社会组织，能够利用农民专业合作社提供的服务，承认并遵守农民专业合作社章程，履行章程规定的入社手续的，可以成为农民专业合作社的成员。但是，具有管理公共事务职能的单位不得加入农民专业合作社。  
农民专业合作社应当置备成员名册，并报登记机关。

**Article 19** Citizens with capacity for civil conduct, as well as enterprises, public institutions or social organizations that engage in production and operation activities directly related to the business of a farmers' specialized cooperative, who are able to utilize the services provided by the cooperative, recognize and abide by its articles of association, and fulfill the admission procedures prescribed in the articles of association, may become members of the cooperative. However, entities with the function of managing public affairs may not join a farmers' specialized cooperative.  
A farmers' specialized cooperative shall maintain a roster of members and submit it to the registration authority.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农民专业合作社的成员中，农民至少应当占成员总数的百分之八十。  
成员总数二十人以下的，可以有一个企业、事业单位或者社会组织成员；成员总数超过二十人的，企业、事业单位和社会组织成员不得超过成员总数的百分之五。

**Article 20** Among the members of a farmers' specialized cooperative, farmers shall account for at least eighty percent of the total number of members.  
If the total number of members is twenty or fewer, there may be one enterprise, public institution or social organization member; if the total number of members exceeds twenty, the number of members that are enterprises, public institutions or social organizations shall not exceed five percent of the total number of members.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农民专业合作社成员享有下列权利：  
（一）参加成员大会，并享有表决权、选举权和被选举权，按照章程规定对本社实行民主管理；  
（二）利用本社提供的服务和生产经营设施；  
（三）按照章程规定或者成员大会决议分享盈余；  
（四）查阅本社的章程、成员名册、成员大会或者成员代表大会记录、理事会会议决议、监事会会议决议、财务会计报告、会计账簿和财务审计报告；  
（五）章程规定的其他权利。

**Article 21** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) to participate in the members' meeting, and to enjoy the right to vote, the right to stand for election and the right to be elected, and to exercise democratic management over the cooperative in accordance with the articles of association;  
(2) to use the services and production and operation facilities provided by the cooperative;  
(3) to share in the surplus in accordance with the articles of association or the resolutions of the members' meeting;  
(4) to inspect the articles of association, the roster of members, the minutes of the members' meeting or the meeting of members' representatives, the resolutions of the board of directors, the resolutions of the board of supervisors, the financial accounting reports, the accounting books and the financial audit reports;  
(5) other rights as prescribed in the articles of association.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　农民专业合作社成员大会选举和表决，实行一人一票制，成员各享有一票的基本表决权。  
出资额或者与本社交易量（额）较大的成员按照章程规定，可以享有附加表决权。本社的附加表决权总票数，不得超过本社成员基本表决权总票数的百分之二十。享有附加表决权的成员及其享有的附加表决权数，应当在每次成员大会召开时告知出席会议的全体成员。

**Article 22** In the election and voting of the members' meeting of a farmers' specialized cooperative, the one-person-one-vote system shall be adopted, and each member shall have one basic vote.  
Members with larger capital contributions or larger transaction volumes (amounts) with the cooperative may, in accordance with the articles of association, have additional voting rights. The total number of additional voting rights of the cooperative shall not exceed twenty percent of the total number of basic votes of its members. The identities of the members entitled to additional voting rights and the number of additional voting rights they hold shall be notified to all members attending the meeting at each members' meeting.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农民专业合作社成员承担下列义务：  
（一）执行成员大会、成员代表大会和理事会的决议；  
（二）按照章程规定向本社出资；  
（三）按照章程规定与本社进行交易；  
（四）按照章程规定承担亏损；  
（五）章程规定的其他义务。

**Article 23** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to implement the resolutions of the members' meeting, the meeting of members' representatives and the board of directors;  
(2) to make capital contributions to the cooperative in accordance with the articles of association;  
(3) to conduct transactions with the cooperative in accordance with the articles of association;  
(4) to bear losses in accordance with the articles of association;  
(5) other obligations as prescribed in the articles of association.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　符合[本法第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条规定的公民、企业、事业单位或者社会组织，要求加入已成立的农民专业合作社，应当向理事长或者理事会提出书面申请，经成员大会或者成员代表大会表决通过后，成为本社成员。

**Article 24** A citizen, enterprise, public institution or social organization that meets the requirements of Articles 19 and 20 of this Law and requests to join an established farmers' specialized cooperative shall submit a written application to the chairperson or the board of directors, and shall become a member of the cooperative after being approved by a vote of the members' meeting or the meeting of members' representatives.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农民专业合作社成员要求退社的，应当在会计年度终了的三个月前向理事长或者理事会提出书面申请；其中，企业、事业单位或者社会组织成员退社，应当在会计年度终了的六个月前提出；章程另有规定的，从其规定。退社成员的成员资格自会计年度终了时终止。

**Article 25** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative who requests to withdraw from the cooperative shall submit a written application to the chairperson or the board of directors at least three months before the end of the fiscal year; members that are enterprises, public institutions or social organizations shall submit the application at least six months before the end of the fiscal year; if the articles of association provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail. The membership of a withdrawing member shall terminate at the end of the fiscal year.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农民专业合作社成员不遵守农民专业合作社的章程、成员大会或者成员代表大会的决议，或者严重危害其他成员及农民专业合作社利益的，可以予以除名。  
成员的除名，应当经成员大会或者成员代表大会表决通过。  
在实施前款规定时，应当为该成员提供陈述意见的机会。  
被除名成员的成员资格自会计年度终了时终止。

**Article 26** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative who fails to comply with the articles of association or the resolutions of the members' meeting or the meeting of members' representatives, or who seriously harms the interests of other members or the cooperative, may be expelled.  
The expulsion of a member shall be approved by a vote of the members' meeting or the meeting of members' representatives.  
When implementing the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the member concerned shall be given an opportunity to present his/her opinions.  
The membership of the expelled member shall terminate at the end of the fiscal year.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　成员在其资格终止前与农民专业合作社已订立的合同，应当继续履行；章程另有规定或者与本社另有约定的除外。

**Article 27** Where a member has entered into a contract with a farmers' specialized cooperative before the termination of his/her membership, such contract shall continue to be performed, unless otherwise provided in the articles of association or otherwise agreed with the cooperative.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　成员资格终止的，农民专业合作社应当按照章程规定的方式和期限，退还记载在该成员账户内的出资额和公积金份额；对成员资格终止前的可分配盈余，依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)的规定向其返还。  
资格终止的成员应当按照章程规定分摊资格终止前本社的亏损及债务。

**Article 28** Where a member's membership terminates, the farmers' specialized cooperative shall, in the manner and within the time limit prescribed in the articles of association, refund the capital contribution and the accumulation fund share recorded in the member's account; and shall return the distributable surplus before the termination of membership to such member in accordance with Article 44 of this Law.  
The member whose membership terminates shall, in accordance with the articles of association, share in the losses and debts of the cooperative incurred before the termination of membership.

### 第四章　组织机构

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农民专业合作社成员大会由全体成员组成，是本社的权力机构，行使下列职权：  
（一）修改章程；  
（二）选举和罢免理事长、理事、执行监事或者监事会成员；  
（三）决定重大财产处置、对外投资、对外担保和生产经营活动中的其他重大事项；  
（四）批准年度业务报告、盈余分配方案、亏损处理方案；  
（五）对合并、分立、解散、清算，以及设立、加入联合社等作出决议；  
（六）决定聘用经营管理人员和专业技术人员的数量、资格和任期；  
（七）听取理事长或者理事会关于成员变动情况的报告，对成员的入社、除名等作出决议；  
（八）公积金的提取及使用；  
（九）章程规定的其他职权。

**Article 29** The members' meeting of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall be composed of all members, and is the supreme authority of the cooperative, exercising the following functions and powers:  
(1) to amend the articles of association;  
(2) to elect and remove the chairperson, directors, executive supervisor or members of the board of supervisors;  
(3) to decide on major disposal of property, external investment, external guarantees and other major matters in the production and operation activities;  
(4) to approve the annual business report, the surplus distribution plan and the loss treatment plan;  
(5) to adopt resolutions on merger, division, dissolution, liquidation, and establishment of or joining a union;  
(6) to decide on the number, qualifications and term of office of the management personnel and professional technical personnel to be employed;  
(7) to hear the report of the chairperson or the board of directors on the changes of membership, and to adopt resolutions on the admission or expulsion of members;  
(8) the withdrawal and use of accumulation funds;  
(9) other functions and powers as prescribed in the articles of association.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农民专业合作社召开成员大会，出席人数应当达到成员总数三分之二以上。  
成员大会选举或者作出决议，应当由本社成员表决权总数过半数通过；作出修改章程或者合并、分立、解散，以及设立、加入联合社的决议应当由本社成员表决权总数的三分之二以上通过。章程对表决权数有较高规定的，从其规定。

**Article 30** When convening a members' meeting of a farmers' specialized cooperative, the number of attendees shall reach at least two-thirds of the total number of members.  
The election or adoption of resolutions by the members' meeting shall be approved by more than half of the total voting rights of the members of the cooperative; resolutions on amending the articles of association, merger, division, dissolution, and establishment of or joining a union shall be approved by at least two-thirds of the total voting rights of the members of the cooperative. If the articles of association provide for a higher requirement on the number of votes, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农民专业合作社成员大会每年至少召开一次，会议的召集由章程规定。有下列情形之一的，应当在二十日内召开临时成员大会：  
（一）百分之三十以上的成员提议；  
（二）执行监事或者监事会提议；  
（三）章程规定的其他情形。

**Article 31** The members' meeting of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall be convened at least once a year, and the convening of the meeting shall be governed by the articles of association. An interim members' meeting shall be convened within twenty days under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) proposed by more than thirty percent of the members;  
(2) proposed by the executive supervisor or the board of supervisors;  
(3) other circumstances as prescribed in the articles of association.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　农民专业合作社成员超过一百五十人的，可以按照章程规定设立成员代表大会。成员代表大会按照章程规定可以行使成员大会的部分或者全部职权。  
依法设立成员代表大会的，成员代表人数一般为成员总人数的百分之十，最低人数为五十一人。

**Article 32** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative has more than 150 members, a meeting of members' representatives may be established in accordance with the articles of association. The meeting of members' representatives may, in accordance with the articles of association, exercise part or all of the functions and powers of the members' meeting.  
If a meeting of members' representatives is established according to law, the number of representatives shall generally be ten percent of the total number of members, with a minimum of 51 representatives.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　农民专业合作社设理事长一名，可以设理事会。理事长为本社的法定代表人。  
农民专业合作社可以设执行监事或者监事会。理事长、理事、经理和财务会计人员不得兼任监事。  
理事长、理事、执行监事或者监事会成员，由成员大会从本社成员中选举产生，依照本法和章程的规定行使职权，对成员大会负责。  
理事会会议、监事会会议的表决，实行一人一票。

**Article 33** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall have one chairperson, and may have a board of directors. The chairperson shall be the legal representative of the cooperative.  
A farmers' specialized cooperative may have an executive supervisor or a board of supervisors. The chairperson, directors, manager and accountants may not concurrently serve as supervisors.  
The chairperson, directors, executive supervisor or members of the board of supervisors shall be elected by the members' meeting from among the members of the cooperative, shall exercise their functions and powers in accordance with this Law and the articles of association, and shall be responsible to the members' meeting.  
In the voting of meetings of the board of directors and the board of supervisors, the one-person-one-vote system shall be adopted.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　农民专业合作社的成员大会、成员代表大会、理事会、监事会，应当将所议事项的决定作成会议记录，出席会议的成员、成员代表、理事、监事应当在会议记录上签名。

**Article 34** The members' meeting, the meeting of members' representatives, the board of directors and the board of supervisors of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall make minutes of the decisions on the matters discussed, and the members, representatives, directors and supervisors attending the meeting shall sign the minutes.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农民专业合作社的理事长或者理事会可以按照成员大会的决定聘任经理和财务会计人员，理事长或者理事可以兼任经理。经理按照章程规定或者理事会的决定，可以聘任其他人员。  
经理按照章程规定和理事长或者理事会授权，负责具体生产经营活动。

**Article 35** The chairperson or the board of directors of a farmers' specialized cooperative may, in accordance with the decision of the members' meeting, appoint a manager and accountants. The chairperson or a director may concurrently serve as manager. The manager may, in accordance with the articles of association or the decision of the board of directors, appoint other personnel.  
The manager shall be responsible for the specific production and operation activities in accordance with the articles of association and the authorization of the chairperson or the board of directors.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　农民专业合作社的理事长、理事和管理人员不得有下列行为：  
（一）侵占、挪用或者私分本社资产；  
（二）违反章程规定或者未经成员大会同意，将本社资金借贷给他人或者以本社资产为他人提供担保；  
（三）接受他人与本社交易的佣金归为己有；  
（四）从事损害本社经济利益的其他活动。  
理事长、理事和管理人员违反前款规定所得的收入，应当归本社所有；给本社造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 36** The chairperson, directors and management personnel of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) embezzling, misappropriating or privately distributing the assets of the cooperative;  
(2) lending the funds of the cooperative to others or providing guarantees with the assets of the cooperative for others in violation of the articles of association or without the consent of the members' meeting;  
(3) accepting commissions from others in transactions with the cooperative for personal benefit;  
(4) engaging in other activities that harm the economic interests of the cooperative.  
Any income obtained by the chairperson, directors or management personnel in violation of the preceding paragraph shall belong to the cooperative; if losses are caused to the cooperative, they shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　农民专业合作社的理事长、理事、经理不得兼任业务性质相同的其他农民专业合作社的理事长、理事、监事、经理。

**Article 37** The chairperson, directors and managers of a farmers' specialized cooperative may not concurrently serve as chairperson, director, supervisor or manager of another farmers' specialized cooperative with the same nature of business.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　执行与农民专业合作社业务有关公务的人员，不得担任农民专业合作社的理事长、理事、监事、经理或者财务会计人员。

**Article 38** Persons who perform public duties related to the business of farmers' specialized cooperatives may not serve as the chairperson, director, supervisor, manager or accountant of a farmers' specialized cooperative.

### 第五章　财务管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　农民专业合作社应当按照国务院财政部门制定的财务会计制度进行财务管理和会计核算。

**Article 39** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall conduct financial management and accounting in accordance with the financial and accounting system formulated by the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　农民专业合作社的理事长或者理事会应当按照章程规定，组织编制年度业务报告、盈余分配方案、亏损处理方案以及财务会计报告，于成员大会召开的十五日前，置备于办公地点，供成员查阅。

**Article 40** The chairperson or the board of directors of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall, in accordance with the articles of association, organize the preparation of the annual business report, the surplus distribution plan, the loss treatment plan and the financial accounting reports, and make them available at the office premises for inspection by members fifteen days before the members' meeting is convened.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　农民专业合作社与其成员的交易、与利用其提供的服务的非成员的交易，应当分别核算。

**Article 41** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall account separately for transactions with its members and transactions with non-members who utilize the services provided by the cooperative.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　农民专业合作社可以按照章程规定或者成员大会决议从当年盈余中提取公积金。公积金用于弥补亏损、扩大生产经营或者转为成员出资。  
每年提取的公积金按照章程规定量化为每个成员的份额。

**Article 42** A farmers' specialized cooperative may, in accordance with the articles of association or a resolution of the members' meeting, withdraw an accumulation fund from the surplus of the current year. The accumulation fund shall be used to cover losses, expand production and operation, or be converted into members' capital contributions.  
The accumulation fund withdrawn each year shall be quantified into shares for each member in accordance with the articles of association.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　农民专业合作社应当为每个成员设立成员账户，主要记载下列内容：  
（一）该成员的出资额；  
（二）量化为该成员的公积金份额；  
（三）该成员与本社的交易量（额）。

**Article 43** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall establish a member account for each member, which shall mainly record the following:  
(1) the amount of capital contribution of the member;  
(2) the accumulation fund share quantified to the member;  
(3) the volume (amount) of transactions between the member and the cooperative.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在弥补亏损、提取公积金后的当年盈余，为农民专业合作社的可分配盈余。可分配盈余主要按照成员与本社的交易量（额）比例返还。  
可分配盈余按成员与本社的交易量（额）比例返还的返还总额不得低于可分配盈余的百分之六十；返还后的剩余部分，以成员账户中记载的出资额和公积金份额，以及本社接受国家财政直接补助和他人捐赠形成的财产平均量化到成员的份额，按比例分配给本社成员。  
经成员大会或者成员代表大会表决同意，可以将全部或者部分可分配盈余转为对农民专业合作社的出资，并记载在成员账户中。  
具体分配办法按照章程规定或者经成员大会决议确定。

**Article 44** The surplus of the current year after covering losses and withdrawing the accumulation fund shall be the distributable surplus of the farmers' specialized cooperative. The distributable surplus shall mainly be returned in proportion to the volume (amount) of transactions between members and the cooperative.  
The total amount returned in proportion to the volume (amount) of transactions between members and the cooperative shall not be less than sixty percent of the distributable surplus; the remaining part after the return shall be distributed to members in proportion to their capital contributions and accumulation fund shares recorded in their accounts, as well as the shares formed by the property of the cooperative from direct state financial subsidies and donations from others, which are equally quantified to each member.  
With the approval by a vote of the members' meeting or the meeting of members' representatives, all or part of the distributable surplus may be converted into capital contributions to the cooperative and recorded in the members' accounts.  
The specific distribution method shall be determined in accordance with the articles of association or a resolution of the members' meeting.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　设立执行监事或者监事会的农民专业合作社，由执行监事或者监事会负责对本社的财务进行内部审计，审计结果应当向成员大会报告。  
成员大会也可以委托社会中介机构对本社的财务进行审计。

**Article 45** For a farmers' specialized cooperative that has an executive supervisor or a board of supervisors, the executive supervisor or the board of supervisors shall be responsible for the internal audit of the cooperative's finance, and the audit results shall be reported to the members' meeting.  
The members' meeting may also entrust an independent social intermediary institution to conduct an audit of the cooperative's finance.

### 第六章　合并、分立、解散和清算

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　农民专业合作社合并，应当自合并决议作出之日起十日内通知债权人。合并各方的债权、债务应当由合并后存续或者新设的组织承继。

**Article 46** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative is to be merged, it shall notify the creditors within ten days from the date of the resolution on merger. The claims and debts of all parties to the merger shall be succeeded by the surviving or newly established organization after the merger.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　农民专业合作社分立，其财产作相应的分割，并应当自分立决议作出之日起十日内通知债权人。分立前的债务由分立后的组织承担连带责任。但是，在分立前与债权人就债务清偿达成的书面协议另有约定的除外。

**Article 47** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative is to be divided, its property shall be split correspondingly, and the cooperative shall notify the creditors within ten days from the date of the resolution on division. The debts before the division shall be borne jointly and severally by the organizations after the division, unless otherwise agreed in a written agreement reached with the creditors before the division on the settlement of debts.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　农民专业合作社因下列原因解散：  
（一）章程规定的解散事由出现；  
（二）成员大会决议解散；  
（三）因合并或者分立需要解散；  
（四）依法被吊销营业执照或者被撤销。  
因前款第一项、第二项、第四项原因解散的，应当在解散事由出现之日起十五日内由成员大会推举成员组成清算组，开始解散清算。逾期不能组成清算组的，成员、债权人可以向人民法院申请指定成员组成清算组进行清算，人民法院应当受理该申请，并及时指定成员组成清算组进行清算。

**Article 48** A farmers' specialized cooperative shall be dissolved for any of the following reasons:  
(1) the occurrence of the grounds for dissolution as specified in the articles of association;  
(2) a resolution of the members' meeting to dissolve;  
(3) dissolution required due to merger or division;  
(4) the business license being revoked or the cooperative being cancelled according to law.  
Where dissolution occurs for the reasons specified in items (1), (2) or (4) of the preceding paragraph, within fifteen days from the date of occurrence of the dissolution grounds, the members' meeting shall elect members to form a liquidation committee to commence dissolution and liquidation. If a liquidation committee cannot be formed within the time limit, the members or creditors may apply to the People's Court for designation of members to form a liquidation committee for liquidation. The People's Court shall accept the application and promptly designate members to form a liquidation committee for liquidation.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　清算组自成立之日起接管农民专业合作社，负责处理与清算有关未了结业务，清理财产和债权、债务，分配清偿债务后的剩余财产，代表农民专业合作社参与诉讼、仲裁或者其他法律程序，并在清算结束时办理注销登记。

**Article 49** The liquidation committee shall, from the date of its establishment, take over the farmers' specialized cooperative, and be responsible for handling unfinished business related to liquidation, liquidating property, claims and debts, distributing the remaining property after settling debts, representing the cooperative in litigation, arbitration or other legal proceedings, and handling the deregistration upon the completion of liquidation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　清算组应当自成立之日起十日内通知农民专业合作社成员和债权人，并于六十日内在报纸上公告。债权人应当自接到通知之日起三十日内，未接到通知的自公告之日起四十五日内，向清算组申报债权。如果在规定期间内全部成员、债权人均已收到通知，免除清算组的公告义务。  
债权人申报债权，应当说明债权的有关事项，并提供证明材料。清算组应当对债权进行审查、登记。  
在申报债权期间，清算组不得对债权人进行清偿。

**Article 50** The liquidation committee shall, within ten days from the date of its establishment, notify the members and creditors of the farmers' specialized cooperative, and shall make a public announcement in a newspaper within sixty days. Creditors shall, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the notice, or within forty-five days from the date of the public announcement if no notice is received, declare their claims to the liquidation committee. If all members and creditors have received the notice within the prescribed period, the liquidation committee shall be exempted from the obligation of public announcement.  
When declaring a claim, the creditor shall specify the relevant matters of the claim and provide supporting documents. The liquidation committee shall examine and register the claims.  
During the period for declaration of claims, the liquidation committee shall not make repayments to creditors.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　农民专业合作社因[本法第四十八条](#art-48)第一款的原因解散，或者人民法院受理破产申请时，不能办理成员退社手续。

**Article 51** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative is dissolved for the reasons specified in paragraph 1 of Article 48 of this Law, or when the People's Court accepts an application for bankruptcy, no member withdrawal procedures may be handled.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　清算组负责制定包括清偿农民专业合作社员工的工资及社会保险费用，清偿所欠税款和其他各项债务，以及分配剩余财产在内的清算方案，经成员大会通过或者申请人民法院确认后实施。  
清算组发现农民专业合作社的财产不足以清偿债务的，应当依法向人民法院申请破产。

**Article 52** The liquidation committee shall be responsible for formulating a liquidation plan including the payment of wages and social insurance premiums of the employees of the farmers' specialized cooperative, the payment of taxes due and other debts, and the distribution of the remaining property, and shall implement it after approval by the members' meeting or confirmation by the People's Court.  
If the liquidation committee finds that the property of the farmers' specialized cooperative is insufficient to pay off its debts, it shall apply to the People's Court for bankruptcy according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　农民专业合作社接受国家财政直接补助形成的财产，在解散、破产清算时，不得作为可分配剩余资产分配给成员，具体按照国务院财政部门有关规定执行。

**Article 53** The property of a farmers' specialized cooperative formed by direct state financial subsidies accepted by it shall not be distributed to members as distributable residual assets upon dissolution or bankruptcy liquidation, and the specific provisions shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　清算组成员应当忠于职守，依法履行清算义务，因故意或者重大过失给农民专业合作社成员及债权人造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 54** Members of the liquidation committee shall be loyal to their duties and perform liquidation obligations according to law. If losses are caused to the members or creditors of a farmers' specialized cooperative due to intentional acts or gross negligence, they shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　农民专业合作社破产适用企业破产法的有关规定。但是，破产财产在清偿破产费用和共益债务后，应当优先清偿破产前与农民成员已发生交易但尚未结清的款项。

**Article 55** The bankruptcy of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. However, after paying off the bankruptcy expenses and the debts for the common benefit, the bankruptcy property shall first be used to settle the outstanding amounts arising from transactions with farmer members before bankruptcy.

### 第七章　农民专业合作社联合社

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　三个以上的农民专业合作社在自愿的基础上，可以出资设立农民专业合作社联合社。  
农民专业合作社联合社应当有自己的名称、组织机构和住所，由联合社全体成员制定并承认的章程，以及符合章程规定的成员出资。

**Article 56** Three or more farmers' specialized cooperatives may, on a voluntary basis, establish a farmers' specialized cooperative union by making capital contributions.  
A farmers' specialized cooperative union shall have its own name, organizational structure and domicile, articles of association formulated and recognized by all members of the union, and capital contributions from members that comply with the provisions of the articles of association.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　农民专业合作社联合社依照本法登记，取得法人资格，领取营业执照，登记类型为农民专业合作社联合社。

**Article 57** A farmers' specialized cooperative union shall, upon registration in accordance with this Law, acquire the status of a legal person, obtain a business license, and the registration type shall be "farmers' specialized cooperative union".

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　农民专业合作社联合社以其全部财产对该社的债务承担责任；农民专业合作社联合社的成员以其出资额为限对农民专业合作社联合社承担责任。

**Article 58** A farmers' specialized cooperative union shall be liable for its debts with all of its property; the members of the farmers' specialized cooperative union shall be liable for the union to the extent of their respective capital contributions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　农民专业合作社联合社应当设立由全体成员参加的成员大会，其职权包括修改农民专业合作社联合社章程，选举和罢免农民专业合作社联合社理事长、理事和监事，决定农民专业合作社联合社的经营方案及盈余分配，决定对外投资和担保方案等重大事项。  
农民专业合作社联合社不设成员代表大会，可以根据需要设立理事会、监事会或者执行监事。理事长、理事应当由成员社选派的人员担任。

**Article 59** A farmers' specialized cooperative union shall establish a members' meeting attended by all members, whose functions and powers include amending the articles of association of the union, electing and removing the chairperson, directors and supervisors of the union, deciding on the business plan and surplus distribution of the union, and deciding on major matters such as external investment and guarantee plans.  
A farmers' specialized cooperative union shall not have a meeting of members' representatives, but may, as needed, establish a board of directors, a board of supervisors or an executive supervisor. The chairperson and directors shall be held by persons designated by the member cooperatives.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　农民专业合作社联合社的成员大会选举和表决，实行一社一票。

**Article 60** In the election and voting of the members' meeting of a farmers' specialized cooperative union, the one-cooperative-one-vote system shall be adopted.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　农民专业合作社联合社可分配盈余的分配办法，按照本法规定的原则由农民专业合作社联合社章程规定。

**Article 61** The method for distributing the distributable surplus of a farmers' specialized cooperative union shall be prescribed in the articles of association of the union in accordance with the principles stipulated in this Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　农民专业合作社联合社成员退社，应当在会计年度终了的六个月前以书面形式向理事会提出。退社成员的成员资格自会计年度终了时终止。

**Article 62** A member of a farmers' specialized cooperative union that withdraws from the union shall submit a written application to the board of directors at least six months before the end of the fiscal year. The membership of the withdrawing member shall terminate at the end of the fiscal year.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本章对农民专业合作社联合社没有规定的，适用本法关于农民专业合作社的规定。

**Article 63** Where this Chapter does not provide for matters concerning a farmers' specialized cooperative union, the provisions of this Law on farmers' specialized cooperatives shall apply.

### 第八章　扶持措施

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家支持发展农业和农村经济的建设项目，可以委托和安排有条件的农民专业合作社实施。

**Article 64** For construction projects that the State supports to develop agriculture and rural economy, such projects may be entrusted to and arranged for qualified farmers' specialized cooperatives to implement.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　中央和地方财政应当分别安排资金，支持农民专业合作社开展信息、培训、农产品标准与认证、农业生产基础设施建设、市场营销和技术推广等服务。国家对革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区和贫困地区的农民专业合作社给予优先扶助。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当依法加强对财政补助资金使用情况的监督。

**Article 65** The central and local finances shall respectively allocate funds to support farmers' specialized cooperatives in providing services such as information, training, agricultural product standards and certification, agricultural production infrastructure construction, marketing and technology promotion. The State shall give priority assistance to farmers' specialized cooperatives in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas and poverty-stricken areas.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen supervision over the use of financial subsidy funds.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家政策性金融机构应当采取多种形式，为农民专业合作社提供多渠道的资金支持。具体支持政策由国务院规定。  
国家鼓励商业性金融机构采取多种形式，为农民专业合作社及其成员提供金融服务。  
国家鼓励保险机构为农民专业合作社提供多种形式的农业保险服务。鼓励农民专业合作社依法开展互助保险。

**Article 66** State policy-oriented financial institutions shall, through various forms, provide multi-channel financial support to farmers' specialized cooperatives. The specific support policies shall be formulated by the State Council.  
The State shall encourage commercial financial institutions to provide financial services to farmers' specialized cooperatives and their members through various forms.  
The State shall encourage insurance institutions to provide various forms of agricultural insurance services to farmers' specialized cooperatives. Farmers' specialized cooperatives are encouraged to conduct mutual insurance according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　农民专业合作社享受国家规定的对农业生产、加工、流通、服务和其他涉农经济活动相应的税收优惠。

**Article 67** Farmers' specialized cooperatives shall enjoy the tax preferences corresponding to agricultural production, processing, circulation, services and other agriculture-related economic activities as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　农民专业合作社从事农产品初加工用电执行农业生产用电价格，农民专业合作社生产性配套辅助设施用地按农用地管理，具体办法由国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 68** The electricity used by a farmers' specialized cooperative for the primary processing of agricultural products shall be subject to the price for agricultural production electricity, and the land for production-related supporting auxiliary facilities of a farmers' specialized cooperative shall be managed as agricultural land. The specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　侵占、挪用、截留、私分或者以其他方式侵犯农民专业合作社及其成员的合法财产，非法干预农民专业合作社及其成员的生产经营活动，向农民专业合作社及其成员摊派，强迫农民专业合作社及其成员接受有偿服务，造成农民专业合作社经济损失的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 69** Whoever embezzles, misappropriates, intercepts, privately distributes or otherwise infringes upon the lawful property of a farmers' specialized cooperative or its members, unlawfully interferes with the production and operation activities of the cooperative or its members, makes exactions from the cooperative or its members, or compels the cooperative or its members to accept paid services, thereby causing economic losses to the cooperative, shall be subject to legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　农民专业合作社向登记机关提供虚假登记材料或者采取其他欺诈手段取得登记的，由登记机关责令改正，可以处五千元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销登记或者吊销营业执照。

**Article 70** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative submits false registration materials or obtains registration by other fraudulent means to the registration authority, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the registration shall be revoked or the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　农民专业合作社连续两年未从事经营活动的，吊销其营业执照。

**Article 71** If a farmers' specialized cooperative has not engaged in business activities for two consecutive years, its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　农民专业合作社在依法向有关主管部门提供的财务报告等材料中，作虚假记载或者隐瞒重要事实的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 72** Where a farmers' specialized cooperative makes false records or conceals important facts in the financial reports or other materials provided to the relevant competent authorities according to law, it shall be subject to legal liability according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国有农场、林场、牧场、渔场等企业中实行承包租赁经营、从事农业生产经营或者服务的职工，兴办农民专业合作社适用本法。

**Article 73** This Law shall apply to employees of state-owned farms, forestry farms, livestock farms, fisheries and other enterprises who engage in contractual or leasehold operations, and who are engaged in agricultural production and operation or services, and who establish farmers' specialized cooperatives.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法自2018年7月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2018.
