# 中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区驻军法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1999-06-28  
**生效日期**：1999-12-20

> （1999年6月28日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　澳门驻军的职责 第三章　澳门驻军与澳门特别行政区政府的关系 第四章　澳门驻军人员的义务与纪律 第五章　澳门驻军人员的司法管辖 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障中央人民政府派驻澳门特别行政区负责防务的军队依法履行职责，维护国家的主权、统一、领土完整和澳门的安全，根据宪法和澳门特别行政区基本法，制定本法。 第二条　中央人民政府派驻澳门特别行政区负责防务的军队，称中国人民解放军驻澳门部队（以下称澳门驻军）。 澳门驻军由中华人民共和国中央军事委员会领导，其部队组成、员额根据澳门特别行政区防务的需要确定。 澳门驻军实行人员轮换制度。 第三条　澳门驻军不干预澳门特别行政区的地方事务。澳门特别行政区政府在必要时，可以向中央人民政府请求澳门驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害。 第四条　澳门驻军人员除须遵守全国性的法律外，还须遵守澳门特别行政区的法律。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障中央人民政府派驻澳门特别行政区负责防务的军队依法履行职责，维护国家的主权、统一、领土完整和澳门的安全，根据宪法和澳门特别行政区基本法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution and the Basic Law of the Macao Special Administrative Region to ensure that the troops stationed by the Central People's Government in the Macao Special Administrative Region for defense perform their duties according to law, and to safeguard national sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity and the security of Macao.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中央人民政府派驻澳门特别行政区负责防务的军队，称中国人民解放军驻澳门部队（以下称澳门驻军）。  
澳门驻军由中华人民共和国中央军事委员会领导，其部队组成、员额根据澳门特别行政区防务的需要确定。  
澳门驻军实行人员轮换制度。

**Article 2** The troops stationed by the Central People's Government in the Macao Special Administrative Region for defense shall be called the Macao Garrison of the Chinese People's Liberation Army (hereinafter referred to as the Macao Garrison).  
The Macao Garrison shall be led by the Central Military Commission of the People's Republic of China, and its composition and strength shall be determined according to the needs of the defense of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
The Macao Garrison shall implement a system of rotation of personnel.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　澳门驻军不干预澳门特别行政区的地方事务。澳门特别行政区政府在必要时，可以向中央人民政府请求澳门驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害。

**Article 3** The Macao Garrison shall not interfere in the local affairs of the Macao Special Administrative Region. The Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region may, when necessary, request the Central People's Government for assistance from the Macao Garrison in maintaining public order and in disaster relief.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　澳门驻军人员除须遵守全国性的法律外，还须遵守澳门特别行政区的法律。

**Article 4** Members of the Macao Garrison must, in addition to abiding by national laws, also abide by the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　澳门驻军费用由中央人民政府负担。

**Article 5** The expenses of the Macao Garrison shall be borne by the Central People's Government.

### 第二章　澳门驻军的职责

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　澳门驻军履行下列防务职责：  
（一）防备和抵抗侵略，保卫澳门特别行政区的安全；  
（二）担负防卫勤务；  
（三）管理军事设施；  
（四）承办有关的涉外军事事宜。

**Article 6** The Macao Garrison shall perform the following defense duties:  
(1) to prevent and resist aggression and safeguard the security of the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(2) to undertake defense services;  
(3) to administer military facilities; and (4) to handle relevant foreign military affairs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定宣布战争状态或者因澳门特别行政区内发生澳门特别行政区政府不能控制的危及国家统一或者安全的动乱而决定澳门特别行政区进入紧急状态时，澳门驻军根据中央人民政府决定在澳门特别行政区实施的全国性法律的规定履行职责。

**Article 7** When the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decides to declare a state of war, or decides that the Macao Special Administrative Region is in a state of emergency due to the occurrence of turmoil within the Macao Special Administrative Region which endangers national unity or security and which the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region is unable to control, the Macao Garrison shall perform its duties in accordance with the provisions of the national laws that the Central People's Government decides to apply in the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　澳门驻军的飞行器、舰船等武器装备和物资以及持有澳门驻军制发的证件或者证明文件的执行职务的人员和车辆，不受澳门特别行政区执法人员检查、搜查和扣押。  
澳门驻军和澳门驻军人员并享有在澳门特别行政区实施的法律规定的其他权利和豁免。

**Article 8** The weapons, equipment and materials such as aircraft and vessels of the Macao Garrison, and the persons and vehicles on duty holding certificates or supporting documents issued by the Macao Garrison, shall not be subject to inspection, search or seizure by law enforcement personnel of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
The Macao Garrison and its members shall also enjoy other rights and immunities provided for by the laws applicable in the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　澳门驻军人员对妨碍其执行职务的行为，可以依照在澳门特别行政区实施的法律的规定采取措施予以制止。

**Article 9** Members of the Macao Garrison may take measures to stop acts that obstruct them in the performance of their duties in accordance with the provisions of the laws applicable in the Macao Special Administrative Region.

### 第三章　澳门驻军与澳门特别行政区政府的关系

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　澳门特别行政区政府应当支持澳门驻军履行防务职责，保障澳门驻军和澳门驻军人员的合法权益。  
澳门特别行政区以法律保障澳门驻军和澳门驻军人员履行职责时应当享有的权利和豁免。  
澳门特别行政区制定政策、拟定法案、草拟行政法规，涉及澳门驻军的，应当征求澳门驻军的意见。

**Article 10** The Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall support the Macao Garrison in performing its defense duties and guarantee the lawful rights and interests of the Macao Garrison and its members.  
The Macao Special Administrative Region shall provide by law for the rights and immunities that the Macao Garrison and its members shall enjoy in the performance of their duties.  
When the Macao Special Administrative Region formulates policies, drafts bills, or prepares administrative regulations that involve the Macao Garrison, it shall seek the views of the Macao Garrison.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　澳门驻军进行训练、演习等军事活动，涉及澳门特别行政区公共利益的，应当事先通报澳门特别行政区政府。

**Article 11** Where military activities such as training and exercises conducted by the Macao Garrison involve the public interests of the Macao Special Administrative Region, the Garrison shall notify the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region in advance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　澳门驻军和澳门特别行政区政府共同保护澳门特别行政区内的军事设施。  
澳门驻军会同澳门特别行政区政府划定军事禁区。军事禁区的位置、范围由澳门特别行政区政府宣布。  
澳门特别行政区政府应当协助澳门驻军维护军事禁区的安全，禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、危害军事设施。  
澳门驻军以外的人员、车辆、船舶和飞行器未经澳门驻军最高指挥官或者其授权的军官批准，不得进入军事禁区。军事禁区的警卫人员有权依法制止擅自进入军事禁区和破坏、危害军事设施的行为。  
澳门驻军对军事禁区内的自然资源、文物古迹以及非军事权益，应当依照澳门特别行政区的法律予以保护。

**Article 12** The Macao Garrison and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall jointly protect the military facilities within the Macao Special Administrative Region. The Macao Garrison shall, in conjunction with the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region, delimit military restricted zones. The locations and extents of the military restricted zones shall be announced by the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region. The Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall assist the Macao Garrison in maintaining the security of the military restricted zones and shall prohibit any organization or individual from damaging or endangering military facilities. No person, vehicle, vessel, or aircraft other than those belonging to the Macao Garrison may enter a military restricted zone without the approval of the highest commander of the Macao Garrison or an officer authorized by him or her. The guards of the military restricted zones shall have the right to stop, in accordance with law, any act of unauthorized entry into the military restricted zones or of damaging or endangering military facilities. The Macao Garrison shall protect natural resources, cultural relics and historic sites, and non-military rights and interests within the military restricted zones in accordance with the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　澳门驻军的军事用地由澳门特别行政区政府无偿提供。  
澳门驻军的军事用地，经中央人民政府批准不再用于防务目的的，无偿移交澳门特别行政区政府。  
澳门特别行政区政府如需将澳门驻军的部分军事用地用于公共用途，必须经中央人民政府批准；经批准的，澳门特别行政区政府应当在中央人民政府同意的地点，为澳门驻军重新提供军事用地和军事设施，并负担所有费用。

**Article 13** The military sites for the Macao Garrison shall be provided free of charge by the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
Where military sites of the Macao Garrison are, upon approval by the Central People's Government, no longer used for defense purposes, they shall be transferred free of charge to the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
If the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region needs to use part of the military sites of the Macao Garrison for public purposes, it must obtain the approval of the Central People's Government; upon such approval, the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall provide new military sites and facilities for the Macao Garrison at a location agreed by the Central People's Government and shall bear all the expenses incurred.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　澳门特别行政区政府向中央人民政府请求澳门驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害并经中央人民政府批准后，澳门驻军根据中央军事委员会的命令派出部队执行协助维持社会治安和救助灾害的任务，任务完成后即返回驻地。  
澳门驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害时，在澳门特别行政区政府的安排下，由澳门驻军最高指挥官或者其授权的军官实施指挥。  
澳门驻军人员在协助维持社会治安和救助灾害时，行使与其执行任务相适应的澳门特别行政区法律规定的相关执法人员的权力。

**Article 14** After the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region requests the Central People's Government for assistance from the Macao Garrison in maintaining public order or in disaster relief and the Central People's Government approves such a request, the Macao Garrison shall dispatch troops in accordance with orders from the Central Military Commission to carry out tasks of assisting in maintaining public order or in disaster relief. Upon completion of such tasks, the troops shall immediately return to their garrison. When the Macao Garrison assists in maintaining public order or in disaster relief, command shall be exercised, under the arrangement of the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region, by the highest commander of the Macao Garrison or an officer authorized by him or her. When members of the Macao Garrison assist in maintaining public order or in disaster relief, they shall exercise the powers that relevant law enforcement personnel are entitled to under the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region, to the extent appropriate for the performance of their tasks.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　澳门驻军和澳门特别行政区政府应当建立必要的联系，协商处理与驻军有关的事宜。

**Article 15** The Macao Garrison and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall establish necessary liaison to consult on and handle matters relating to the Garrison.

### 第四章　澳门驻军人员的义务与纪律

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　澳门驻军人员应当履行下列义务：  
（一）忠于祖国，履行职责，维护祖国的安全、荣誉和利益，维护澳门的安全；  
（二）遵守全国性的法律和澳门特别行政区的法律，遵守军队的纪律；  
（三）尊重澳门特别行政区政权机构，尊重澳门特别行政区的社会制度和生活方式；  
（四）爱护澳门特别行政区的公共财产和澳门居民及其他人的私有财产；  
（五）遵守社会公德，讲究文明礼貌。

**Article 16** Members of the Macao Garrison shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) to be loyal to the motherland, perform their duties, safeguard the security, honor and interests of the motherland, and safeguard the security of Macao;  
(2) to abide by national laws and the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region, and to observe military discipline;  
(3) to respect the organs of political power of the Macao Special Administrative Region, and to respect the social system and lifestyle of the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(4) to protect the public property of the Macao Special Administrative Region and the private property of Macao residents and other persons; and (5) to observe social ethics and to be courteous and civilized.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　澳门驻军人员不得参加澳门的政治组织、宗教组织和社会团体。

**Article 17** Members of the Macao Garrison shall not join any political, religious, or social organization in Macao.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　澳门驻军和澳门驻军人员不得以任何形式从事营利性经营活动。澳门驻军人员并不得从事与军人职责不相称的其他任何活动。

**Article 18** The Macao Garrison and its members shall not engage in any form of profit-making business activities. Members of the Macao Garrison shall not engage in any other activities which are incompatible with their duties as military personnel.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　澳门驻军人员违反全国性的法律和澳门特别行政区的法律的，依法追究法律责任。  
澳门驻军人员违反军队纪律的，给予纪律处分。

**Article 19** Where members of the Macao Garrison violate national laws or the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region, they shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.  
Where members of the Macao Garrison violate military discipline, they shall be given disciplinary sanctions.

### 第五章　澳门驻军人员的司法管辖

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　澳门驻军人员犯罪的案件由军事司法机关管辖；但是，澳门驻军人员非执行职务的行为，侵犯澳门居民、澳门驻军以外的其他人的人身权、财产权以及其他违反澳门特别行政区法律构成犯罪的案件，由澳门特别行政区司法机关管辖。  
军事司法机关和澳门特别行政区司法机关对各自管辖的澳门驻军人员犯罪的案件，如果认为由对方管辖更为适宜，经双方协商一致后，可以移交对方管辖。  
军事司法机关管辖的澳门驻军人员犯罪的案件中，涉及的被告人中的澳门居民、澳门驻军以外的其他人，由澳门特别行政区法院审判。

**Article 20** Cases involving crimes committed by members of the Macao Garrison shall be subject to the jurisdiction of military judicial authorities; provided that cases involving acts not performed in the course of their duties by members of the Macao Garrison which infringe upon the personal or property rights of Macao residents or other persons not belonging to the Macao Garrison, or other acts which constitute crimes under the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region, shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the judicial authorities of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
Where the military judicial authorities and the judicial authorities of the Macao Special Administrative Region consider that a case involving a crime by a member of the Macao Garrison which is within their respective jurisdiction is more appropriately tried by the other party, the case may be transferred to that party upon mutual agreement.  
In cases involving crimes by members of the Macao Garrison under the jurisdiction of the military judicial authorities, if the defendants involved include Macao residents or other persons not belonging to the Macao Garrison, such defendants shall be tried by the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　澳门特别行政区执法人员依法拘捕的涉嫌犯罪的人员，查明是澳门驻军人员的，应当移交澳门驻军羁押。被羁押的人员所涉及的案件，依照[本法第二十条](#art-20)的规定确定管辖。

**Article 21** Where a person suspected of a crime who is arrested according to law by a law enforcement officer of the Macao Special Administrative Region is found to be a member of the Macao Garrison, such person shall be handed over to the Macao Garrison for custody. The jurisdiction over the case involving the person in custody shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article 20 of this Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　澳门驻军人员被澳门特别行政区法院判处剥夺或者限制人身自由的刑罚或者保安处分的，依照澳门特别行政区的法律规定送交执行；但是，澳门特别行政区有关执法机关与军事司法机关对执行的地点另行协商确定的除外。

**Article 22** Where a member of the Macao Garrison is sentenced by a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region to a punishment or a security measure that deprives or restricts personal liberty, such member shall be delivered for execution in accordance with the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region; except where the relevant law enforcement authority of the Macao Special Administrative Region and the military judicial authority have otherwise agreed on the place of execution through consultation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　澳门驻军人员违反澳门特别行政区的法律，侵害澳门居民、澳门驻军以外的其他人的民事权利的，当事人可以通过协商、调解解决；不愿通过协商、调解解决或者协商、调解不成的，被侵权人可以向法院提起诉讼。澳门驻军人员非执行职务的行为引起的民事侵权案件，由澳门特别行政区法院管辖；执行职务的行为引起的民事侵权案件，由中华人民共和国最高人民法院管辖，侵权行为的损害赔偿适用澳门特别行政区法律。

**Article 23** Where a member of the Macao Garrison violates the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region and infringes upon the civil rights of Macao residents or other persons other than the Macao Garrison, the parties concerned may resolve the matter through negotiation or mediation; if they are unwilling to do so, or if negotiation or mediation fails, the infringed party may file a lawsuit in a court. Civil infringement cases arising from acts not in the performance of duties by members of the Macao Garrison shall be under the jurisdiction of the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region; civil infringement cases arising from acts in the performance of duties shall be under the jurisdiction of the Supreme People's Court of the People's Republic of China, and the compensation for damages from the infringing act shall be governed by the laws of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　澳门驻军的机关或者单位在澳门特别行政区与澳门居民、澳门驻军以外的其他人发生合同纠纷时，当事人可以通过协商、调解解决。当事人不愿通过协商、调解解决或者协商、调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，向仲裁机构申请仲裁。当事人没有在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向澳门特别行政区法院提起诉讼；但是，当事人对提起诉讼的法院另有约定的除外。

**Article 24** Where an organ or unit of the Macao Garrison enters into a contractual dispute in the Macao Special Administrative Region with a Macao resident or another person other than the Macao Garrison, the parties concerned may resolve the matter through negotiation or mediation. If the parties are unwilling to do so, or if negotiation or mediation fails, they may apply for arbitration with an arbitration institution in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached subsequently. If the parties have neither an arbitration clause in the contract nor a written arbitration agreement reached subsequently, they may file a lawsuit in a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region; except where the parties have otherwise agreed on the court where the lawsuit shall be filed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在澳门特别行政区法院的诉讼活动中，澳门驻军对澳门驻军人员身份、执行职务的行为等事实发出的证明文件为有效证据。但是，相反证据成立的除外。

**Article 25** In judicial proceedings of the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region, a certification document issued by the Macao Garrison regarding facts such as the identity of a member of the Macao Garrison or the nature of an act as being in the performance of duties shall be valid evidence. However, this shall not apply where contrary evidence is established.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　澳门驻军的国防等国家行为不受澳门特别行政区法院管辖。

**Article 26** State acts such as national defense of the Macao Garrison shall not be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　澳门特别行政区法院作出的判决、裁定涉及澳门驻军的机关或者单位的财产执行的，澳门驻军的机关或者单位必须履行；但是，澳门特别行政区法院不得对澳门驻军的武器装备、物资和其他财产实施强制执行。

**Article 27** Where a judgment or ruling rendered by a court of the Macao Special Administrative Region involves the enforcement against the property of an organ or unit of the Macao Garrison, the organ or unit of the Macao Garrison must perform such judgment or ruling; however, the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall not enforce compulsory execution against the weaponry, equipment, materials, and other property of the Macao Garrison.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　军事司法机关可以与澳门特别行政区司法机关和有关执法机关通过协商进行司法方面的联系和相互提供协助。

**Article 28** Military judicial authorities may, through consultation with the judicial authorities and relevant law enforcement authorities of the Macao Special Administrative Region, maintain judicial contacts and provide mutual assistance.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本法的解释权属于全国人民代表大会常务委员会。

**Article 29** The power of interpretation of this Law shall be vested in the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法自1999年12月20日起施行。

**Article 30** This Law shall come into force on December 20, 1999.
