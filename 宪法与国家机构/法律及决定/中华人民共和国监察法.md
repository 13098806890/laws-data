# 中华人民共和国监察法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-25  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （2018年3月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会第一次会议通过　根据2024年12月25日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十三次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国监察法〉的决定》修正）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了深入开展廉政建设和反腐败工作，加强对所有行使公权力的公职人员的监督，实现国家监察全面覆盖，持续深化国家监察体制改革，推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to further promote the work of integrity and anti-corruption, strengthen supervision over all public officials who exercise public power, achieve full coverage of state supervision, continuously deepen the reform of the national supervision system, and advance the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　坚持中国共产党对国家监察工作的领导，以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，构建集中统一、权威高效的中国特色国家监察体制。

**Article 2** The leadership of the Communist Party of China over the state supervision work shall be upheld, with Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Important Thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as the guide, to build a centralized, unified, authoritative, and efficient state supervision system with Chinese characteristics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级监察委员会是行使国家监察职能的专责机关，依照本法对所有行使公权力的公职人员（以下称公职人员）进行监察，调查职务违法和职务犯罪，开展廉政建设和反腐败工作，维护宪法和法律的尊严。

**Article 3** The supervisory commissions at all levels are the specialized organs exercising the state supervision function. They shall, in accordance with this Law, supervise all public officials who exercise public power (hereinafter referred to as public officials), investigate duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, carry out the work of integrity building and anti-corruption, and uphold the dignity of the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　监察委员会依照法律规定独立行使监察权，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。  
监察机关办理职务违法和职务犯罪案件，应当与审判机关、检察机关、执法部门互相配合，互相制约。  
监察机关在工作中需要协助的，有关机关和单位应当根据监察机关的要求依法予以协助。

**Article 4** The supervisory commission shall independently exercise the power of supervision according to law, and shall not be subject to interference by any administrative organ, social organization, or individual.  
When handling cases of duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, the supervisory organ shall cooperate with and be mutually constrained by the adjudicative organs, procuratorial organs, and law enforcement departments.  
Where the supervisory organ needs assistance in its work, the relevant organs and entities shall provide assistance according to law in accordance with the requirements of the supervisory organ.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家监察工作严格遵照宪法和法律，以事实为根据，以法律为准绳；权责对等，严格监督；遵守法定程序，公正履行职责；尊重和保障人权，在适用法律上一律平等，保障监察对象及相关人员的合法权益；惩戒与教育相结合，宽严相济。

**Article 5** State supervision work shall strictly comply with the Constitution and laws, take facts as the basis, and take law as the criterion; match powers with responsibilities, and exercise strict supervision; comply with statutory procedures and perform duties impartially; respect and safeguard human rights, and shall be equal in the application of law, ensuring the lawful rights and interests of the supervision targets and relevant persons; combine punishment with education, and apply leniency in some cases and severity in others.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家监察工作坚持标本兼治、综合治理，强化监督问责，严厉惩治腐败；深化改革、健全法治，有效制约和监督权力；加强法治教育和道德教育，弘扬中华优秀传统文化，构建不敢腐、不能腐、不想腐的长效机制。

**Article 6** State supervision work shall adhere to addressing both the symptoms and root causes, comprehensive measures, strengthening supervision and accountability, and severely punishing corruption; deepening reform and improving the rule of law, effectively restraining and supervising power; strengthening legal education and moral education, promoting the fine traditional Chinese culture, and establishing a long-term mechanism that ensures officials do not dare, are not able, and do not want to engage in corruption.

### 第二章　监察机关及其职责

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国国家监察委员会是最高监察机关。  
省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区设立监察委员会。

**Article 7** The National Commission of Supervision of the People's Republic of China is the highest supervisory organ.  
Supervisory commissions shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities, and municipal districts.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家监察委员会由全国人民代表大会产生，负责全国监察工作。  
国家监察委员会由主任、副主任若干人、委员若干人组成，主任由全国人民代表大会选举，副主任、委员由国家监察委员会主任提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
国家监察委员会主任每届任期同全国人民代表大会每届任期相同，连续任职不得超过两届。  
国家监察委员会对全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责，并接受其监督。

**Article 8** The National Commission of Supervision is formed by the National People's Congress and is responsible for national supervision work.  
The National Commission of Supervision is composed of a Chairperson, a number of Vice-Chairpersons, and a number of members. The Chairperson is elected by the National People's Congress. The Vice-Chairpersons and members are appointed and removed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress upon the recommendation of the Chairperson of the National Commission of Supervision.  
The term of office of the Chairperson of the National Commission of Supervision shall be the same as the term of office of the National People's Congress. The Chairperson shall serve no more than two consecutive terms.  
The National Commission of Supervision shall be responsible to the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee and shall accept their supervision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级监察委员会由本级人民代表大会产生，负责本行政区域内的监察工作。  
地方各级监察委员会由主任、副主任若干人、委员若干人组成，主任由本级人民代表大会选举，副主任、委员由监察委员会主任提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任免。  
地方各级监察委员会主任每届任期同本级人民代表大会每届任期相同。  
地方各级监察委员会对本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会和上一级监察委员会负责，并接受其监督。

**Article 9** Local supervisory commissions at all levels are formed by the people's congress at the corresponding level and are responsible for supervision work within their respective administrative areas.  
Each local supervisory commission is composed of a chairperson, a number of vice-chairpersons, and a number of members. The chairperson is elected by the people's congress at the corresponding level. The vice-chairpersons and members are appointed and removed by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level upon the recommendation of the chairperson of the supervisory commission.  
The term of office of the chairperson of a local supervisory commission shall be the same as the term of office of the people's congress at the corresponding level.  
Each local supervisory commission shall be responsible to the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee, and to the supervisory commission at the next higher level, and shall accept their supervision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家监察委员会领导地方各级监察委员会的工作，上级监察委员会领导下级监察委员会的工作。

**Article 10** The National Commission of Supervision shall lead the work of local supervisory commissions at all levels. A supervisory commission at a higher level shall lead the work of a supervisory commission at a lower level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　监察委员会依照本法和有关法律规定履行监督、调查、处置职责：  
（一）对公职人员开展廉政教育，对其依法履职、秉公用权、廉洁从政从业以及道德操守情况进行监督检查；  
（二）对涉嫌贪污贿赂、滥用职权、玩忽职守、权力寻租、利益输送、徇私舞弊以及浪费国家资财等职务违法和职务犯罪进行调查；  
（三）对违法的公职人员依法作出政务处分决定；对履行职责不力、失职失责的领导人员进行问责；对涉嫌职务犯罪的，将调查结果移送人民检察院依法审查、提起公诉；向监察对象所在单位提出监察建议。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第28条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** A supervisory commission shall, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of relevant laws, perform the following duties of supervision, investigation, and disposition:  
(1) Conduct integrity education for public officials, and supervise and inspect their performance of duties according to law, exercise of power impartially, integrity in politics and practice, and moral conduct;  
(2) Investigate duty-related violations and duty-related crimes, such as suspected embezzlement or bribery, abuse of power, dereliction of duty, power rent-seeking, interest transfer, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, and waste of state assets;  
(3) Make decisions on disciplinary sanctions for public officials who have violated the law according to law; hold accountable leading officials who have failed to perform their duties effectively or have been derelict in their duties; where duty-related crimes are suspected, transfer the investigation results to the People's Procuratorate for review and initiation of public prosecution according to law; and make supervisory recommendations to the entity to which the supervision target belongs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级监察委员会可以向本级中国共产党机关、国家机关、中国人民政治协商会议委员会机关、法律法规授权或者委托管理公共事务的组织和单位以及辖区内特定区域、国有企业、事业单位等派驻或者派出监察机构、监察专员。  
经国家监察委员会批准，国家监察委员会派驻本级实行垂直管理或者双重领导并以上级单位领导为主的单位、国有企业的监察机构、监察专员，可以向驻在单位的下一级单位再派出。  
经国家监察委员会批准，国家监察委员会派驻监察机构、监察专员，可以向驻在单位管理领导班子的普通高等学校再派出；国家监察委员会派驻国务院国有资产监督管理机构的监察机构，可以向驻在单位管理领导班子的国有企业再派出。  
监察机构、监察专员对派驻或者派出它的监察委员会或者监察机构、监察专员负责。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-14" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** Supervisory commissions at all levels may station or dispatch supervisory institutions or commissioners to the organs of the Communist Party of China at the corresponding level, state organs, the organs of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, organizations and units authorized or entrusted by laws and regulations to manage public affairs, as well as specific areas within their jurisdiction, state-owned enterprises, public institutions, etc.  
Upon approval by the National Commission of Supervision, the supervisory institutions or commissioners stationed by the National Commission of Supervision in units under vertical management or dual leadership with the primary leadership from the higher-level entity, or in state-owned enterprises, may further dispatch personnel to the next lower-level units of the units where they are stationed.  
Upon approval by the National Commission of Supervision, the supervisory institutions or commissioners stationed by the National Commission of Supervision may further dispatch personnel to ordinary institutions of higher learning whose leading bodies are managed by the units where they are stationed; and the supervisory institutions stationed by the National Commission of Supervision in the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council may further dispatch personnel to state-owned enterprises whose leading bodies are managed by the units where they are stationed.  
The dispatched or stationed supervisory institutions and commissioners shall be responsible to the supervisory commission, institution, or commissioner that dispatches or stations them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　派驻或者派出的监察机构、监察专员根据授权，按照管理权限依法对公职人员进行监督，提出监察建议，依法对公职人员进行调查、处置。

**Article 13** The dispatched or stationed supervisory institutions and commissioners shall, according to authorization and within their management authority, supervise public officials according to law, make supervisory recommendations, and investigate and dispose of public officials according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家实行监察官制度，依法确定监察官的等级设置、任免、考评和晋升等制度。

**Article 14** The state shall implement a system of supervisory officials, and shall determine according to law the systems for the grading, appointment and removal, assessment, and promotion of supervisory officials.

### 第三章　监察范围和管辖

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　监察机关对下列公职人员和有关人员进行监察：  
（一）中国共产党机关、人民代表大会及其常务委员会机关、人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院、中国人民政治协商会议各级委员会机关、民主党派机关和工商业联合会机关的公务员，以及参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的人员；  
（二）法律、法规授权或者受国家机关依法委托管理公共事务的组织中从事公务的人员；  
（三）国有企业管理人员；  
（四）公办的教育、科研、文化、医疗卫生、体育等单位中从事管理的人员；  
（五）基层群众性自治组织中从事管理的人员；  
（六）其他依法履行公职的人员。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-2" title="被《中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国公职人员政务处分法》第23条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-41" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第41条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-42" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第42条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-43" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第43条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-44" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第44条引用">[6]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-45" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第45条引用">[7]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-46" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第46条引用">[8]</a></sup>

**Article 15** Supervisory organs shall supervise the following public officials and relevant personnel:  
(1) Civil servants of the organs of the Communist Party of China, the organs of the People's Congress and its Standing Committee, the people's governments, supervisory commissions, People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, the organs of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference at all levels, the organs of democratic parties, and the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce, as well as personnel managed by reference to the Law of the People's Republic of China on Civil Servants;  
(2) Personnel engaged in public affairs in organizations authorized or entrusted by state organs according to law to manage public affairs under laws or regulations;  
(3) Management personnel of state-owned enterprises;  
(4) Management personnel of public institutions such as education, scientific research, culture, healthcare, sports, etc.;  
(5) Management personnel of grassroots mass self-governing organizations;  
(6) Other personnel who perform public duties according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级监察机关按照管理权限管辖本辖区内[本法第十五条](#art-15)规定的人员所涉监察事项。  
上级监察机关可以办理下一级监察机关管辖范围内的监察事项，必要时也可以办理所辖各级监察机关管辖范围内的监察事项。  
监察机关之间对监察事项的管辖有争议的，由其共同的上级监察机关确定。

**Article 16** Supervisory organs at all levels shall, within their management authority, have jurisdiction over supervisory matters involving personnel specified in Article 15 of this Law within their respective areas.  
A supervisory organ at a higher level may handle supervisory matters within the jurisdiction of a supervisory organ at the next lower level, and may also, when necessary, handle supervisory matters within the jurisdiction of supervisory organs at all levels under its jurisdiction.  
Where there is a dispute over the jurisdiction of a supervisory matter between supervisory organs, it shall be determined by their common superior supervisory organ.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　上级监察机关可以将其所管辖的监察事项指定下级监察机关管辖，也可以将下级监察机关有管辖权的监察事项指定给其他监察机关管辖。  
监察机关认为所管辖的监察事项重大、复杂，需要由上级监察机关管辖的，可以报请上级监察机关管辖。

**Article 17** A supervisory organ at a higher level may designate a supervisory matter under its jurisdiction to a supervisory organ at a lower level, and may also designate a supervisory matter within the jurisdiction of a supervisory organ at a lower level to another supervisory organ.  
Where a supervisory organ considers a supervisory matter under its jurisdiction to be significant or complex and requires jurisdiction by a supervisory organ at a higher level, it may petition the supervisory organ at the higher level for jurisdiction.

### 第四章　监察权限

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　监察机关行使监督、调查职权，有权依法向有关单位和个人了解情况，收集、调取证据。有关单位和个人应当如实提供。  
监察机关及其工作人员对监督、调查过程中知悉的国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，应当保密。  
任何单位和个人不得伪造、隐匿或者毁灭证据。

**Article 18** When exercising the powers of supervision and investigation, a supervisory organ shall have the authority to learn about the situation from relevant entities and individuals, and to collect and obtain evidence according to law. The relevant entities and individuals shall provide such information truthfully.  
The supervisory organ and its staff shall keep confidential any state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information that they become aware of during the process of supervision and investigation.  
No entity or individual shall forge, conceal, or destroy evidence.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对可能发生职务违法的监察对象，监察机关按照管理权限，可以直接或者委托有关机关、人员进行谈话，或者进行函询，要求说明情况。

**Article 19** Where a supervision target may commit a duty-related violation, the supervisory organ may, within its management authority, directly or through entrusting the relevant organ or person, conduct a talk with the target, or make an inquiry in writing, requesting an explanation of the situation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在调查过程中，对涉嫌职务违法的被调查人，监察机关可以进行谈话，要求其就涉嫌违法行为作出陈述，必要时向被调查人出具书面通知。  
对涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务犯罪的被调查人，监察机关可以进行讯问，要求其如实供述涉嫌犯罪的情况。

**Article 20** During an investigation, the supervisory organ may conduct a talk with a person under investigation who is suspected of a duty-related violation, requesting him or her to make a statement about the suspected violation. When necessary, a written notice shall be issued to the person under investigation.  
Where the person under investigation is suspected of duty-related crimes such as embezzlement, bribery, malfeasance, or dereliction of duty, the supervisory organ may interrogate him or her, requesting truthful confession of the suspected criminal facts.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　监察机关根据案件情况，经依法审批，可以强制涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪的被调查人到案接受调查。

**Article 21** A supervisory organ may, based on the circumstances of a case and after approval according to law, compulsorily bring a person under investigation who is suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime to appear before it for investigation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在调查过程中，监察机关可以询问证人等人员。

**Article 22** In the course of investigation, the supervisory organ may question witnesses and other persons.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被调查人涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪，并有下列情形之一的，经监察机关依法审批，可以对其采取责令候查措施：  
（一）不具有[本法第二十四条](#art-24)第一款所列情形的；  
（二）符合留置条件，但患有严重疾病、生活不能自理的，系怀孕或者正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女，或者生活不能自理的人的唯一扶养人；  
（三）案件尚未办结，但留置期限届满或者对被留置人员不需要继续采取留置措施的；  
（四）符合留置条件，但因为案件的特殊情况或者办理案件的需要，采取责令候查措施更为适宜的。  
被责令候查人员应当遵守以下规定：  
（一）未经监察机关批准不得离开所居住的直辖市、设区的市的城市市区或者不设区的市、县的辖区；  
（二）住址、工作单位和联系方式发生变动的，在二十四小时以内向监察机关报告；  
（三）在接到通知的时候及时到案接受调查；  
（四）不得以任何形式干扰证人作证；  
（五）不得串供或者伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据。  
被责令候查人员违反前款规定，情节严重的，可以依法予以留置。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-103" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第103条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-104" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第104条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-109" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第109条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Where a person under investigation is suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, after approval according to law, order such person to await investigation:  
(1) the circumstances listed in Article 24, paragraph 1 of this Law are not present;  
(2) the person meets the conditions for detention but suffers from a serious illness and is unable to take care of himself or herself, is a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant, or is the sole caregiver of a person unable to take care of himself or herself;  
(3) the case has not been concluded, but the detention period has expired or it is no longer necessary to continue detention for the detained person; or  
(4) the person meets the conditions for detention, but due to the special circumstances of the case or the needs of handling the case, an order to await investigation is more appropriate.  
A person ordered to await investigation shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) shall not leave the urban area of the municipality directly under the Central Government or the city with districts where he or she resides, or the jurisdiction of the city without districts or county, without the approval of the supervisory organ;  
(2) shall report to the supervisory organ within twenty-four hours any change in address, work unit, or contact information;  
(3) shall appear in a timely manner for investigation upon receiving notice;  
(4) shall not interfere with witness testimony in any form; and  
(5) shall not collude to make false statements or forge, conceal, or destroy evidence.  
Where a person ordered to await investigation violates the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, the person may be detained according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被调查人涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等严重职务违法或者职务犯罪，监察机关已经掌握其部分违法犯罪事实及证据，仍有重要问题需要进一步调查，并有下列情形之一的，经监察机关依法审批，可以将其留置在特定场所：  
（一）涉及案情重大、复杂的；  
（二）可能逃跑、自杀的；  
（三）可能串供或者伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据的；  
（四）可能有其他妨碍调查行为的。  
对涉嫌行贿犯罪或者共同职务犯罪的涉案人员，监察机关可以依照前款规定采取留置措施。  
留置场所的设置、管理和监督依照国家有关规定执行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-121" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第121条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-122" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第122条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-123" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第123条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-124" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第124条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 24** Where a person under investigation is suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime such as embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty, and the supervisory organ has obtained part of the facts and evidence of the illegal or criminal conduct but still needs further investigation into important issues, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, after approval according to law, detain the person in a designated place:  
(1) the case involves major or complex circumstances;  
(2) the person may attempt to escape or commit suicide;  
(3) the person may collude to make false statements or forge, conceal, or destroy evidence; or  
(4) there may be other conduct obstructing the investigation.  
The supervisory organ may take detention measures in accordance with the preceding paragraph against persons involved in suspected bribery crimes or joint duty-related crimes.  
The establishment, management, and supervision of detention places shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对于未被留置的下列人员，监察机关发现存在逃跑、自杀等重大安全风险的，经依法审批，可以进行管护：  
（一）涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪的自动投案人员；  
（二）在接受谈话、函询、询问过程中，交代涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪问题的人员；  
（三）在接受讯问过程中，主动交代涉嫌重大职务犯罪问题的人员。  
采取管护措施后，应当立即将被管护人员送留置场所，至迟不得超过二十四小时。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-113" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第113条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 25** For the following persons who are not detained, if the supervisory organ discovers that there is a major safety risk such as escape or suicide, it may, after approval according to law, place them under protective custody:  
(1) persons who voluntarily surrender and are suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime;  
(2) persons who, during the process of being interviewed, questioned by letter, or interrogated, confess to being suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime;  
(3) persons who, during the process of being interrogated, voluntarily confess to being suspected of major duty-related crime.  
After taking protective custody measures, the person under protective custody shall be immediately sent to a detention place, and in no case later than twenty-four hours.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　监察机关调查涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等严重职务违法或者职务犯罪，根据工作需要，可以依照规定查询、冻结涉案单位和个人的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产。有关单位和个人应当配合。  
冻结的财产经查明与案件无关的，应当在查明后三日内解除冻结，予以退还。

**Article 26** When investigating suspected serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime such as embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty, the supervisory organ may, as required by work, inquire into and freeze the deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, and other property of the involved entities and individuals in accordance with the provisions. The relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.  
Where the frozen property is found to be irrelevant to the case, the freezing shall be lifted and the property returned within three days after such finding.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　监察机关可以对涉嫌职务犯罪的被调查人以及可能隐藏被调查人或者犯罪证据的人的身体、物品、住处和其他有关地方进行搜查。在搜查时，应当出示搜查证，并有被搜查人或者其家属等见证人在场。  
搜查女性身体，应当由女性工作人员进行。  
监察机关进行搜查时，可以根据工作需要提请公安机关配合。公安机关应当依法予以协助。

**Article 27** The supervisory organ may search the body, articles, residence, and other relevant places of a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime and of any person who may conceal the person under investigation or criminal evidence. When conducting a search, a search warrant shall be presented, and the person being searched or his or her family members and other witnesses shall be present.  
A search of a female body shall be conducted by female personnel.  
When conducting a search, the supervisory organ may, as required by work, request the public security organ to cooperate. The public security organ shall provide assistance according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　监察机关在调查过程中，可以调取、查封、扣押用以证明被调查人涉嫌违法犯罪的财物、文件和电子数据等信息。采取调取、查封、扣押措施，应当收集原物原件，会同持有人或者保管人、见证人，当面逐一拍照、登记、编号，开列清单，由在场人员当场核对、签名，并将清单副本交财物、文件的持有人或者保管人。  
对调取、查封、扣押的财物、文件，监察机关应当设立专用账户、专门场所，确定专门人员妥善保管，严格履行交接、调取手续，定期对账核实，不得毁损或者用于其他目的。对价值不明物品应当及时鉴定，专门封存保管。  
查封、扣押的财物、文件经查明与案件无关的，应当在查明后三日内解除查封、扣押，予以退还。

**Article 28** In the course of investigation, the supervisory organ may collect, seal, or seize property, documents, electronic data, and other information that serve to prove that the person under investigation is suspected of illegality or crime. When taking measures to collect, seal, or seize, the original objects and documents shall be gathered, and in the presence of the holder or custodian and a witness, they shall be photographed, registered, and numbered one by one, a list shall be made, and the persons present shall check and sign the list on the spot, with a copy of the list given to the holder or custodian of the property or documents.  
The supervisory organ shall set up a special account and a special place, designate special personnel for proper custody, strictly follow the procedures for handover and collection, conduct regular account verification, and shall not damage or use the collected, sealed, or seized property and documents for other purposes. Items of unknown value shall be appraised in a timely manner and kept under special seal.  
Where the sealed or seized property or documents are found to be irrelevant to the case, the sealing or seizure shall be lifted and the property or documents returned within three days after such finding.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　监察机关在调查过程中，可以直接或者指派、聘请具有专门知识的人在调查人员主持下进行勘验检查。勘验检查情况应当制作笔录，由参加勘验检查的人员和见证人签名或者盖章。  
必要时，监察机关可以进行调查实验。调查实验情况应当制作笔录，由参加实验的人员签名或者盖章。

**Article 29** In the course of investigation, the supervisory organ may directly conduct or assign or engage persons with specialized knowledge to conduct inspection and examination under the direction of investigators. A record of the inspection and examination shall be made, which shall be signed or sealed by the persons participating in the inspection and examination and the witnesses.  
Where necessary, the supervisory organ may conduct investigation experiments. A record of the investigation experiment shall be made, which shall be signed or sealed by the persons participating in the experiment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　监察机关在调查过程中，对于案件中的专门性问题，可以指派、聘请有专门知识的人进行鉴定。鉴定人进行鉴定后，应当出具鉴定意见，并且签名。

**Article 30** In the course of investigation, the supervisory organ may assign or engage persons with specialized knowledge to conduct expert evaluations on specialized issues in the case. After conducting the evaluation, the expert evaluator shall issue an expert opinion and sign it.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　监察机关调查涉嫌重大贪污贿赂等职务犯罪，根据需要，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术调查措施，按照规定交有关机关执行。  
批准决定应当明确采取技术调查措施的种类和适用对象，自签发之日起三个月以内有效；对于复杂、疑难案件，期限届满仍有必要继续采取技术调查措施的，经过批准，有效期可以延长，每次不得超过三个月。对于不需要继续采取技术调查措施的，应当及时解除。

**Article 31** When investigating suspected major duty-related crimes such as embezzlement and bribery, the supervisory organ may, as required and after undergoing strict approval procedures, take technical investigation measures and submit them to the relevant organ for implementation in accordance with the provisions.  
The approval decision shall specify the type and the target of the technical investigation measures and shall be valid for a period not exceeding three months from the date of issuance; for complex or difficult cases, if it is still necessary to continue the technical investigation measures after the expiration of the period, the validity period may be extended upon approval, with each extension not exceeding three months. Where it is no longer necessary to continue the technical investigation measures, they shall be promptly terminated.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依法应当留置的被调查人如果在逃，监察机关可以决定在本行政区域内通缉，由公安机关发布通缉令，追捕归案。通缉范围超出本行政区域的，应当报请有权决定的上级监察机关决定。

**Article 32** If a person under investigation who should be detained according to law is at large, the supervisory organ may decide to issue a wanted notice within its administrative area, and the public security organ shall issue the wanted notice to apprehend and bring the person to justice. If the wanted notice is to extend beyond the administrative area, the supervisory organ shall report to the superior supervisory organ with the authority to decide for a decision.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　监察机关为防止被调查人及相关人员逃匿境外，经省级以上监察机关批准，可以对被调查人及相关人员采取限制出境措施，由公安机关依法执行。对于不需要继续采取限制出境措施的，应当及时解除。

**Article 33** To prevent the person under investigation and relevant persons from escaping abroad, the supervisory organ may, with the approval of a supervisory organ at or above the provincial level, take measures to restrict the exit from the country of the person under investigation and relevant persons, and the public security organ shall enforce them according to law. Where it is no longer necessary to continue the measures restricting exit from the country, they shall be promptly terminated.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　涉嫌职务犯罪的被调查人主动认罪认罚，有下列情形之一的，监察机关经领导人员集体研究，并报上一级监察机关批准，可以在移送人民检察院时提出从宽处罚的建议：  
（一）自动投案，真诚悔罪悔过的；  
（二）积极配合调查工作，如实供述监察机关还未掌握的违法犯罪行为的；  
（三）积极退赃，减少损失的；  
（四）具有重大立功表现或者案件涉及国家重大利益等情形的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-244" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第244条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-245" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第245条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-246" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第246条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-247" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第247条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-248" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第248条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 34** Where a person under investigation suspected of a duty-related crime voluntarily admits guilt and accepts punishment, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervisory organ may, after collective deliberation by its leading personnel and approval by the supervisory organ at the next higher level, make a recommendation for lenient punishment when transferring the case to the People's Procuratorate:  
(1) the person voluntarily surrenders and sincerely repents;  
(2) the person actively cooperates with the investigation and truthfully confesses illegal or criminal acts not yet known to the supervisory organ;  
(3) the person actively returns illicit gains and reduces losses; or  
(4) the person has performed major meritorious service or the case involves major national interests, among other circumstances.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　职务违法犯罪的涉案人员揭发有关被调查人职务违法犯罪行为，查证属实的，或者提供重要线索，有助于调查其他案件的，监察机关经领导人员集体研究，并报上一级监察机关批准，可以在移送人民检察院时提出从宽处罚的建议。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-249" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第249条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** Where an involved person in a duty-related illegality or crime reports the duty-related illegal or criminal conduct of the person under investigation and the report is verified, or provides important leads that help investigate other cases, the supervisory organ may, after collective deliberation by its leading personnel and approval by the supervisory organ at the next higher level, make a recommendation for lenient punishment when transferring the case to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　监察机关依照本法规定收集的物证、书证、证人证言、被调查人供述和辩解、视听资料、电子数据等证据材料，在刑事诉讼中可以作为证据使用。  
监察机关在收集、固定、审查、运用证据时，应当与刑事审判关于证据的要求和标准相一致。  
以非法方法收集的证据应当依法予以排除，不得作为案件处置的依据。

**Article 36** Physical evidence, documentary evidence, witness testimony, statements and exculpations of the person under investigation, audio-visual materials, electronic data, and other evidentiary materials collected by the supervisory organ in accordance with the provisions of this Law may be used as evidence in criminal proceedings.  
When collecting, fixing, reviewing, and using evidence, the supervisory organ shall be consistent with the requirements and standards for evidence in criminal adjudication.  
Evidence collected by illegal means shall be excluded according to law and shall not be used as a basis for case disposition.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、审计机关等国家机关在工作中发现公职人员涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务违法或者职务犯罪的问题线索，应当移送监察机关，由监察机关依法调查处置。  
被调查人既涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪，又涉嫌其他违法犯罪的，一般应当由监察机关为主调查，其他机关予以协助。

**Article 37** Where a state organ such as the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, public security organ, or audit institution discovers leads of problems concerning a public official suspected of duty-related illegality or duty-related crime such as embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty in the course of work, it shall transfer such leads to the supervisory organ, which shall investigate and handle them according to law.  
Where a person under investigation is suspected of both serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime and other illegality or crime, the supervisory organ shall generally take the lead in the investigation, with other organs providing assistance.

### 第五章　监察程序

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　监察机关对于报案或者举报，应当接受并按照有关规定处理。对于不属于本机关管辖的，应当移送主管机关处理。

**Article 38** The supervisory organ shall accept reports or tip-offs and handle them in accordance with the relevant provisions. Where a report or tip-off does not fall within its jurisdiction, it shall transfer it to the competent organ for handling.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　监察机关应当严格按照程序开展工作，建立问题线索处置、调查、审理各部门相互协调、相互制约的工作机制。  
监察机关应当加强对调查、处置工作全过程的监督管理，设立相应的工作部门履行线索管理、监督检查、督促办理、统计分析等管理协调职能。

**Article 39** The supervisory organ shall strictly carry out its work in accordance with procedures and establish a working mechanism in which the departments for problem lead handling, investigation, and adjudication coordinate with and check each other.  
The supervisory organ shall strengthen supervision and management over the entire process of investigation and disposition, and set up corresponding working departments to perform management and coordination functions such as lead management, supervision and inspection, supervision of handling, and statistical analysis.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　监察机关对监察对象的问题线索，应当按照有关规定提出处置意见，履行审批手续，进行分类办理。线索处置情况应当定期汇总、通报，定期检查、抽查。

**Article 40** The supervisory organ shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, propose disposition opinions on the problem leads concerning the persons subject to supervision, undergo approval procedures, and handle them by category. The status of lead disposition shall be regularly summarized and reported, and subjected to regular inspection and random checks.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　需要采取初步核实方式处置问题线索的，监察机关应当依法履行审批程序，成立核查组。初步核实工作结束后，核查组应当撰写初步核实情况报告，提出处理建议。承办部门应当提出分类处理意见。初步核实情况报告和分类处理意见报监察机关主要负责人审批。

**Article 41** Where it is necessary to adopt preliminary verification measures to handle clues of problems, the supervisory organ shall, according to law, undergo the examination and approval procedures and establish a verification team. Upon completion of the preliminary verification work, the verification team shall prepare a preliminary verification report and make recommendations on handling. The responsible department shall put forward opinions on classification and handling. The preliminary verification report and the classification and handling opinions shall be submitted to the principal person in charge of the supervisory organ for examination and approval.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　经过初步核实，对监察对象涉嫌职务违法犯罪，需要追究法律责任的，监察机关应当按照规定的权限和程序办理立案手续。  
监察机关主要负责人依法批准立案后，应当主持召开专题会议，研究确定调查方案，决定需要采取的调查措施。  
立案调查决定应当向被调查人宣布，并通报相关组织。涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪的，应当通知被调查人家属，并向社会公开发布。

**Article 42** Upon preliminary verification, if a person subject to supervision is suspected of job-related illegal acts or crimes and legal liability is to be investigated, the supervisory organ shall undergo the case-filing procedures in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.  
After the principal person in charge of the supervisory organ approves the case-filing according to law, he/she shall preside over a special meeting to study and determine the investigation plan and decide on the investigation measures to be taken.  
The decision of case-filing and investigation shall be announced to the person under investigation and notified to the relevant organization. If the case involves serious job-related illegal acts or job-related crimes, the family members of the person under investigation shall be notified, and the decision shall be published to the public.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　监察机关对职务违法和职务犯罪案件，应当进行调查，收集被调查人有无违法犯罪以及情节轻重的证据，查明违法犯罪事实，形成相互印证、完整稳定的证据链。  
调查人员应当依法文明规范开展调查工作。严禁以暴力、威胁、引诱、欺骗及其他非法方式收集证据，严禁侮辱、打骂、虐待、体罚或者变相体罚被调查人和涉案人员。  
监察机关及其工作人员在履行职责过程中应当依法保护企业产权和自主经营权，严禁利用职权非法干扰企业生产经营。需要企业经营者协助调查的，应当保障其人身权利、财产权利和其他合法权益，避免或者尽量减少对企业正常生产经营活动的影响。

**Article 43** A supervisory organ shall investigate cases of job-related illegal acts and job-related crimes, collect evidence on whether the person under investigation has committed illegal acts or crimes and on the severity of the circumstances, ascertain the facts of the illegal acts or crimes, and form an evidence chain that is mutually corroborative, complete and stable.  
Investigators shall conduct investigations according to law in a civilized and standardized manner. The collection of evidence by violence, threat, inducement, deception, or other illegal means is strictly prohibited, and insulting, beating, abusing, physically punishing, or covertly physically punishing the person under investigation or the persons involved in the case is strictly prohibited.  
Supervisory organs and their staff members shall, in performing their duties, protect the property rights and autonomous operation rights of enterprises according to law, and shall not use their powers to illegally interfere with the production and operation of enterprises. If the cooperation of business operators is required for an investigation, their personal rights, property rights, and other lawful rights and interests shall be protected, and the impact on the normal production and operation activities of the enterprise shall be avoided or minimized.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　调查人员采取讯问、询问、强制到案、责令候查、管护、留置、搜查、调取、查封、扣押、勘验检查等调查措施，均应当依照规定出示证件，出具书面通知，由二人以上进行，形成笔录、报告等书面材料，并由相关人员签名、盖章。  
调查人员进行讯问以及搜查、查封、扣押等重要取证工作，应当对全过程进行录音录像，留存备查。

**Article 44** When investigators take investigation measures such as interrogation, inquiry, compulsory appearance, order to await investigation, protective custody, detention, search, retrieval, sealing, seizure, and inspection, they shall present their credentials, issue written notices, carry out the measures by at least two persons, produce written materials such as transcripts and reports, and have the relevant persons sign and affix their seals.  
When investigators conduct interrogations and important evidence-collection tasks such as search, sealing, and seizure, they shall make audio and video recordings of the entire process for future reference.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　调查人员应当严格执行调查方案，不得随意扩大调查范围、变更调查对象和事项。  
对调查过程中的重要事项，应当集体研究后按程序请示报告。

**Article 45** Investigators shall strictly implement the investigation plan and shall not arbitrarily expand the scope of investigation or change the subjects and matters of investigation.  
Important matters during the investigation shall be decided through collective study and then reported and requested for instructions in accordance with procedures.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　采取强制到案、责令候查或者管护措施，应当按照规定的权限和程序，经监察机关主要负责人批准。  
强制到案持续的时间不得超过十二小时；需要采取管护或者留置措施的，强制到案持续的时间不得超过二十四小时。不得以连续强制到案的方式变相拘禁被调查人。  
责令候查最长不得超过十二个月。  
监察机关采取管护措施的，应当在七日以内依法作出留置或者解除管护的决定，特殊情况下可以延长一日至三日。

**Article 46** The adoption of measures of compulsory appearance, order to await investigation, or protective custody shall be subject to the approval of the principal person in charge of the supervisory organ in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.  
The duration of compulsory appearance shall not exceed twelve hours; if measures of protective custody or detention are required, the duration of compulsory appearance shall not exceed twenty-four hours. The person under investigation shall not be subject to disguised detention by continuous compulsory appearance.  
The maximum duration of an order to await investigation shall not exceed twelve months.  
Where a supervisory organ adopts the measure of protective custody, it shall, within seven days, make a decision on detention or removal of protective custody according to law; under special circumstances, the period may be extended by one to three days.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　监察机关采取留置措施，应当由监察机关领导人员集体研究决定。设区的市级以下监察机关采取留置措施，应当报上一级监察机关批准。省级监察机关采取留置措施，应当报国家监察委员会备案。

**Article 47** The adoption of the measure of detention by a supervisory organ shall be decided by the leading personnel of the supervisory organ through collective study. A supervisory organ at or below the level of a city divided into districts that intends to adopt the measure of detention shall report it to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval. A provincial supervisory organ that intends to adopt the measure of detention shall report it to the National Commission of Supervision for record.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　留置时间不得超过三个月。在特殊情况下，可以延长一次，延长时间不得超过三个月。省级以下监察机关采取留置措施的，延长留置时间应当报上一级监察机关批准。监察机关发现采取留置措施不当或者不需要继续采取留置措施的，应当及时解除或者变更为责令候查措施。  
对涉嫌职务犯罪的被调查人可能判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚，监察机关依照前款规定延长期限届满，仍不能调查终结的，经国家监察委员会批准或者决定，可以再延长二个月。  
省级以上监察机关在调查期间，发现涉嫌职务犯罪的被调查人另有与留置时的罪行不同种的重大职务犯罪或者同种的影响罪名认定、量刑档次的重大职务犯罪，经国家监察委员会批准或者决定，自发现之日起依照本条第一款的规定重新计算留置时间。留置时间重新计算以一次为限。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-132" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第132条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 48** The duration of detention shall not exceed three months. Under special circumstances, it may be extended once, and the extension shall not exceed three months. Where a supervisory organ below the provincial level adopts the measure of detention, the extension of detention time shall be reported to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for approval. Where a supervisory organ finds that the detention measure is improper or that continued detention is unnecessary, it shall promptly terminate or change it to an order to await investigation.  
For a person under investigation suspected of job-related crimes and likely to be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, if the investigation cannot be concluded within the extended period provided for in the preceding paragraph, with the approval or decision of the National Commission of Supervision, the period may be extended by another two months.  
During the investigation, if a supervisory organ at or above the provincial level discovers that the person under investigation suspected of job-related crimes has committed another major job-related crime of a different type from the crime for which he was detained, or a major job-related crime of the same type that affects the determination of the nature of the crime or the sentencing grade, with the approval or decision of the National Commission of Supervision, the detention period shall be recalculated from the date of discovery in accordance with the first paragraph of this Article. The detention period may be recalculated only once.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　监察机关采取强制到案、责令候查、管护、留置措施，可以根据工作需要提请公安机关配合。公安机关应当依法予以协助。  
省级以下监察机关留置场所的看护勤务由公安机关负责，国家监察委员会留置场所的看护勤务由国家另行规定。留置看护队伍的管理依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 49** Where a supervisory organ adopts measures of compulsory appearance, order to await investigation, protective custody, or detention, it may, based on work needs, request the public security organ to cooperate. The public security organ shall provide assistance according to law.  
The guard service of detention premises of supervisory organs below the provincial level shall be undertaken by the public security organ, while the guard service of detention premises of the National Commission of Supervision shall be separately prescribed by the State. The management of detention guard teams shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　采取管护或者留置措施后，应当在二十四小时以内，通知被管护人员、被留置人员所在单位和家属，但有可能伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据，干扰证人作证或者串供等有碍调查情形的除外。有碍调查的情形消失后，应当立即通知被管护人员、被留置人员所在单位和家属。解除管护或者留置的，应当及时通知被管护人员、被留置人员所在单位和家属。  
被管护人员、被留置人员及其近亲属有权申请变更管护、留置措施。监察机关收到申请后，应当在三日以内作出决定；不同意变更措施的，应当告知申请人，并说明不同意的理由。  
监察机关应当保障被强制到案人员、被管护人员以及被留置人员的饮食、休息和安全，提供医疗服务。对其谈话、讯问的，应当合理安排时间和时长，谈话笔录、讯问笔录由被谈话人、被讯问人阅看后签名。  
被管护人员、被留置人员涉嫌犯罪移送司法机关后，被依法判处管制、拘役或者有期徒刑的，管护、留置一日折抵管制二日，折抵拘役、有期徒刑一日。

**Article 50** After adopting the measure of protective custody or detention, the supervisory organ shall, within twenty-four hours, notify the unit and family members of the person under protective custody or detention, unless there are circumstances that would obstruct the investigation, such as the possibility of forging, concealing, or destroying evidence, interfering with witness testimony, or colluding to make consistent statements. After the circumstances that obstruct the investigation disappear, the unit and family members of the person under protective custody or detention shall be immediately notified. Upon the termination of protective custody or detention, the unit and family members of the person concerned shall be promptly notified.  
The person under protective custody or detention and his/her close relatives have the right to apply for a change of the measures of protective custody or detention. After receiving the application, the supervisory organ shall make a decision within three days; if the measure is not changed, the applicant shall be informed of the reasons for the denial.  
The supervisory organ shall ensure the food, rest, and safety of persons subjected to compulsory appearance, protective custody, or detention, and provide medical services. When conducting talks or interrogations with them, the time and duration shall be reasonably arranged, and transcripts of the talks or interrogations shall be signed by the persons after reading.  
If a person under protective custody or detention is transferred to the judicial authority for suspected crimes and is subsequently sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment according to law, one day of protective custody or detention shall be deemed as equivalent to two days of public surveillance, or one day of criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　监察机关在调查工作结束后，应当依法对案件事实和证据、性质认定、程序手续、涉案财物等进行全面审理，形成审理报告，提请集体审议。

**Article 51** After the conclusion of the investigation work, the supervisory organ shall, according to law, conduct a comprehensive trial of the case facts and evidence, determination of the nature, procedures and formalities, and property involved in the case, produce a trial report, and submit it for collective deliberation.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　监察机关根据监督、调查结果，依法作出如下处置：  
（一）对有职务违法行为但情节较轻的公职人员，按照管理权限，直接或者委托有关机关、人员，进行谈话提醒、批评教育、责令检查，或者予以诫勉；  
（二）对违法的公职人员依照法定程序作出警告、记过、记大过、降级、撤职、开除等政务处分决定；  
（三）对不履行或者不正确履行职责负有责任的领导人员，按照管理权限对其直接作出问责决定，或者向有权作出问责决定的机关提出问责建议；  
（四）对涉嫌职务犯罪的，监察机关经调查认为犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分的，制作起诉意见书，连同案卷材料、证据一并移送人民检察院依法审查、提起公诉；  
（五）对监察对象所在单位廉政建设和履行职责存在的问题等提出监察建议。  
监察机关经调查，对没有证据证明被调查人存在违法犯罪行为的，应当撤销案件，并通知被调查人所在单位。

**Article 52** Based on the results of supervision and investigation, the supervisory organ shall, according to law, make the following dispositions:  
(1) For public officials who have engaged in job-related illegal acts but with minor circumstances, carry out talk reminders, criticism education, require a review, or give admonition, directly or through entrusting relevant organs or personnel, in accordance with the management authority;  
(2) For public officials who have committed illegal acts, make decisions on disciplinary sanctions such as a warning, a demerit, a major demerit, demotion, removal from office, or expulsion, in accordance with statutory procedures;  
(3) For leading persons who are responsible for failing to perform or incorrectly performing their duties, directly make accountability decisions in accordance with the management authority, or propose accountability to the organs with the authority to make accountability decisions;  
(4) For crimes suspected of job-related crimes, if the supervisory organ considers through investigation that the criminal facts are clear and the evidence is credible and sufficient, prepare a prosecution opinion, and transfer it together with the case files and evidence to the People's Procuratorate for examination and initiation of public prosecution according to law;  
(5) Propose supervisory recommendations regarding issues existing in the construction of integrity and performance of duties of the unit where the person subject to supervision belongs.  
If, after investigation, there is no evidence proving that the person under investigation has committed illegal acts or crimes, the supervisory organ shall close the case and notify the unit of the person under investigation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　监察机关经调查，对违法取得的财物，依法予以没收、追缴或者责令退赔；对涉嫌犯罪取得的财物，应当随案移送人民检察院。

**Article 53** Upon investigation, the supervisory organ shall, according to law, confiscate, recover, or order the restitution of property obtained illegally; property obtained suspected of crimes shall be transferred together with the case to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对监察机关移送的案件，人民检察院依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》对被调查人采取强制措施。  
人民检察院经审查，认为犯罪事实已经查清，证据确实、充分，依法应当追究刑事责任的，应当作出起诉决定。  
人民检察院经审查，认为需要补充核实的，应当退回监察机关补充调查，必要时可以自行补充侦查。对于补充调查的案件，应当在一个月内补充调查完毕。补充调查以二次为限。  
人民检察院对于有《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》规定的不起诉的情形的，经上一级人民检察院批准，依法作出不起诉的决定。监察机关认为不起诉的决定有错误的，可以向上一级人民检察院提请复议。

**Article 54** For cases transferred by a supervisory organ, the People's Procuratorate shall adopt compulsory measures against the person under investigation in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If, after examination, the People's Procuratorate finds that the criminal facts are clearly ascertained, the evidence is credible and sufficient, and criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, it shall make a decision to initiate a prosecution.  
If, after examination, the People's Procuratorate finds that further verification is necessary, it shall return the case to the supervisory organ for supplementary investigation, and it may conduct supplementary investigation itself if necessary. For cases returned for supplementary investigation, the supplementary investigation shall be completed within one month. Supplementary investigation shall be limited to two times.  
If there are circumstances under which a prosecution shall not be initiated as provided for in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Procuratorate shall, with the approval of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level, make a decision not to initiate a prosecution according to law. If the supervisory organ considers that the decision not to initiate a prosecution is erroneous, it may apply to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for reconsideration.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　监察机关在调查贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务犯罪案件过程中，被调查人逃匿或者死亡，有必要继续调查的，应当继续调查并作出结论。被调查人逃匿，在通缉一年后不能到案，或者死亡的，由监察机关提请人民检察院依照法定程序，向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。

**Article 55** During the investigation by a supervisory organ of job-related crime cases such as embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty, or malfeasance, if the person under investigation absconds or dies and it is necessary to continue the investigation, the investigation shall be continued and a conclusion shall be reached. If the person under investigation absconds and cannot be brought to justice after being wanted for one year, or dies, the supervisory organ shall request the People's Procuratorate to submit an application to the People's Court for the confiscation of illegal gains in accordance with statutory procedures.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　监察对象对监察机关作出的涉及本人的处理决定不服的，可以在收到处理决定之日起一个月内，向作出决定的监察机关申请复审，复审机关应当在一个月内作出复审决定；监察对象对复审决定仍不服的，可以在收到复审决定之日起一个月内，向上一级监察机关申请复核，复核机关应当在二个月内作出复核决定。复审、复核期间，不停止原处理决定的执行。复核机关经审查，认定处理决定有错误的，原处理机关应当及时予以纠正。

**Article 56** If a person subject to supervision refuses to accept a decision made by a supervisory organ concerning himself/herself, he/she may, within one month from the date of receipt of the decision, apply to the supervisory organ that made the decision for review, and the reviewing organ shall make a review decision within one month. If the person subject to supervision still refuses to accept the review decision, he/she may, within one month from the date of receipt of the review decision, apply to the supervisory organ at the next higher level for reconsideration, and the reconsidering organ shall make a reconsideration decision within two months. During the period of review and reconsideration, the enforcement of the original decision shall not be suspended. If the reconsidering organ, after examination, determines that the decision was erroneous, the original decision-making organ shall promptly correct it.

### 第六章　反腐败国际合作

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家监察委员会统筹协调与其他国家、地区、国际组织开展的反腐败国际交流、合作，组织反腐败国际条约实施工作。

**Article 57** The National Commission of Supervision shall coordinate and organize international exchanges and cooperation with other countries, regions, and international organizations in the fight against corruption, and organize the implementation of international anti-corruption treaties.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家监察委员会会同有关单位加强与有关国家、地区、国际组织在反腐败方面开展引渡、移管被判刑人、遣返、联合调查、调查取证、资产追缴和信息交流等执法司法合作和司法协助。

**Article 58** The National Commission of Supervision shall, together with relevant entities, strengthen law enforcement and judicial cooperation and mutual legal assistance with relevant countries, regions, and international organizations in the fight against corruption, including in extradition, transfer of sentenced persons, repatriation, joint investigations, evidence collection, asset recovery, and information exchange.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家监察委员会加强对反腐败国际追逃追赃和防逃工作的组织协调，督促有关单位做好相关工作：  
（一）对于重大贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务犯罪案件，被调查人逃匿到国（境）外，掌握证据比较确凿的，通过开展境外追逃合作，追捕归案；  
（二）向赃款赃物所在国请求查询、冻结、扣押、没收、追缴、返还涉案资产；  
（三）查询、监控涉嫌职务犯罪的公职人员及其相关人员进出国（境）和跨境资金流动情况，在调查案件过程中设置防逃程序。

**Article 59** The National Commission of Supervision shall strengthen the organization and coordination of international fugitive pursuit, asset recovery, and prevention of flight, and urge relevant entities to properly carry out the following work:  
(1) For major job-related crime cases such as embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty, or malfeasance, if the person under investigation has fled abroad and relatively conclusive evidence is available, capture and bring the person to justice through overseas fugitive pursuit cooperation;  
(2) Request the country where the illicit money and property are located to inquire, freeze, seize, confiscate, recover, and return the assets involved in the case;  
(3) Inquire into and monitor the entry and exit (border) of public officials suspected of job-related crimes and their related persons, as well as cross-border capital flows, and set up anti-flight procedures during the investigation of cases.

### 第七章　对监察机关和监察人员的监督

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　各级监察委员会应当接受本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会的监督。  
各级人民代表大会常务委员会听取和审议本级监察委员会的专项工作报告，组织执法检查。  
县级以上各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会举行会议时，人民代表大会代表或者常务委员会组成人员可以依照法律规定的程序，就监察工作中的有关问题提出询问或者质询。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-283" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第283条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 60** Supervisory commissions at all levels shall accept the supervision of the People's Congress and its Standing Committee at the corresponding level.  
The Standing Committee of the People's Congress at each level shall hear and deliberate the special work reports of the supervisory commission at the same level, and organize law enforcement inspections.  
When the People's Congress and its Standing Committee at or above the county level hold meetings, deputies to the People's Congress or members of the Standing Committee may, in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law, raise questions or inquiries on relevant issues in supervisory work.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　监察机关应当依法公开监察工作信息，接受民主监督、社会监督、舆论监督。

**Article 61** : The supervisory organ shall, according to law, disclose information on supervisory work, and accept democratic supervision, social supervision, and public opinion supervision.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　监察机关根据工作需要，可以从各方面代表中聘请特约监察员。特约监察员按照规定对监察机关及其工作人员履行职责情况实行监督。

**Article 62** : A supervisory organ may, based on the needs of its work, appoint special supervisors from among representatives of all sectors. Special supervisors shall, in accordance with regulations, supervise the performance of duties by the supervisory organ and its staff.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　监察机关通过设立内部专门的监督机构等方式，加强对监察人员执行职务和遵守法律情况的监督，建设忠诚、干净、担当的监察队伍。

**Article 63** : A supervisory organ shall, by establishing specialized internal supervision bodies and other means, strengthen supervision over the performance of duties and compliance with the law by supervisory personnel, and build a supervisory team that is loyal, clean, and responsible.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　监察人员涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪，为防止造成更为严重的后果或者恶劣影响，监察机关经依法审批，可以对其采取禁闭措施。禁闭的期限不得超过七日。  
被禁闭人员应当配合监察机关调查。监察机关经调查发现被禁闭人员符合管护或者留置条件的，可以对其采取管护或者留置措施。  
[本法第五十条](#art-50)的规定，适用于禁闭措施。

**Article 64** : Where a supervisory officer is suspected of serious duty-related illegality or duty-related crime, in order to prevent more serious consequences or adverse effects, the supervisory organ may, after approval according to law, take the measure of solitary confinement against such officer. The period of solitary confinement shall not exceed seven days.  
The person under solitary confinement shall cooperate with the investigation of the supervisory organ. If, upon investigation, the supervisory organ finds that the confined person meets the conditions for protective custody or summons for investigation, it may take the measures of protective custody or summons for investigation against such person.  
The provisions of Article 50 of this Law shall apply to the measure of solitary confinement.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　监察人员必须模范遵守宪法和法律，忠于职守、秉公执法，清正廉洁、保守秘密；必须具有良好的政治素质，熟悉监察业务，具备运用法律、法规、政策和调查取证等能力，自觉接受监督。

**Article 65** : Supervisory personnel must model compliance with the Constitution and laws, be loyal to their duties, enforce the law impartially, be honest and clean, and maintain confidentiality; they must have good political qualities, be familiar with supervisory operations, possess the ability to apply laws, regulations, policies, and to conduct investigations and gather evidence, and consciously accept supervision.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对于监察人员打听案情、过问案件、说情干预的，办理监察事项的监察人员应当及时报告。有关情况应当登记备案。  
发现办理监察事项的监察人员未经批准接触被调查人、涉案人员及其特定关系人，或者存在交往情形的，知情人应当及时报告。有关情况应当登记备案。

**Article 66** : Where a supervisory officer makes inquiries about a case, intervenes in a case, or engages in intercession or interference, the supervisory officer handling the supervisory matter shall promptly report it. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and recorded.  
Where it is discovered that a supervisory officer handling a supervisory matter has contacted, without approval, the person under investigation, the person involved in the case, or their specific related persons, or there exist circumstances of interaction, the person with knowledge shall promptly report it. The relevant circumstances shall be registered and recorded.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　办理监察事项的监察人员有下列情形之一的，应当自行回避，监察对象、检举人及其他有关人员也有权要求其回避：  
（一）是监察对象或者检举人的近亲属的；  
（二）担任过本案的证人的；  
（三）本人或者其近亲属与办理的监察事项有利害关系的；  
（四）有可能影响监察事项公正处理的其他情形的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-295" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第295条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 67** : A supervisory officer handling a supervisory matter shall recuse himself in any of the following circumstances; the subject of supervision, the informant, and other relevant persons also have the right to request his recusal:  
(1) The officer is a close relative of the subject of supervision or the informant;  
(2) The officer has served as a witness in the case;  
(3) The officer or his close relative has an interest in the supervisory matter being handled;  
(4) Any other circumstance that may affect the fair handling of the supervisory matter.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　监察机关涉密人员离岗离职后，应当遵守脱密期管理规定，严格履行保密义务，不得泄露相关秘密。  
监察人员辞职、退休三年内，不得从事与监察和司法工作相关联且可能发生利益冲突的职业。

**Article 68** : After leaving their posts or resigning, personnel of a supervisory organ who have access to secrets shall comply with the management regulations on the period for lifting confidentiality obligations, strictly fulfill their confidentiality obligations, and shall not disclose relevant secrets.  
Within three years after resignation or retirement, supervisory personnel shall not engage in any occupation related to supervisory and judicial work that may cause a conflict of interest.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　监察机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，被调查人及其近亲属、利害关系人有权向该机关申诉：  
（一）采取强制到案、责令候查、管护、留置或者禁闭措施法定期限届满，不予以解除或者变更的；  
（二）查封、扣押、冻结与案件无关或者明显超出涉案范围的财物的；  
（三）应当解除查封、扣押、冻结措施而不解除的；  
（四）贪污、挪用、私分、调换或者违反规定使用查封、扣押、冻结的财物的；  
（五）利用职权非法干扰企业生产经营或者侵害企业经营者人身权利、财产权利和其他合法权益的；  
（六）其他违反法律法规、侵害被调查人合法权益的行为。  
受理申诉的监察机关应当在受理申诉之日起一个月内作出处理决定。申诉人对处理决定不服的，可以在收到处理决定之日起一个月内向上一级监察机关申请复查，上一级监察机关应当在收到复查申请之日起二个月内作出处理决定，情况属实的，及时予以纠正。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国监察法实施条例.md#art-308" title="被《中华人民共和国监察法实施条例》第308条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 69** : Where a supervisory organ or its staff commits any of the following acts, the person under investigation, his close relatives, or interested persons shall have the right to petition to the organ:  
(1) Failing to terminate or modify compulsory appearance, summons pending investigation, protective custody, summons for investigation, or solitary confinement measures upon expiration of the statutory time limit;  
(2) Sealing, seizing, or freezing property unrelated to the case or clearly beyond the scope of the case;  
(3) Failing to lift the measures of sealing, seizure, or freezing when they should be lifted;  
(4) Embezzling, misappropriating, privately dividing, swapping, or using in violation of regulations property that has been sealed, seized, or frozen;  
(5) Illegally interfering with the production and operation of an enterprise or infringing upon the personal rights, property rights, or other legitimate rights and interests of an enterprise operator by abusing authority;  
(6) Other acts that violate laws or regulations and infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the person under investigation.  
The supervisory organ that accepts a petition shall make a decision on the handling within one month from the date of accepting the petition. If the petitioner is dissatisfied with the handling decision, they may apply for review to the supervisory organ at the next higher level within one month from the date of receiving the handling decision, and the supervisory organ at the next higher level shall make a handling decision within two months from the date of receiving the review application; if the facts are verified, it shall correct them promptly.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　对调查工作结束后发现立案依据不充分或者失实，案件处置出现重大失误，监察人员严重违法的，应当追究负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员的责任。

**Article 70** : Where, after the conclusion of the investigation, it is discovered that the basis for filing the case was insufficient or untrue, there were major errors in the handling of the case, or supervisory personnel seriously violated the law, the liability of the responsible leaders and the directly responsible persons shall be investigated.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　有关单位拒不执行监察机关作出的处理决定，或者无正当理由拒不采纳监察建议的，由其主管部门、上级机关责令改正，对单位给予通报批评；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处理。

**Article 71** : Where a relevant unit refuses to execute a handling decision made by a supervisory organ, or refuses to adopt a supervisory suggestion without justifiable reasons, the competent department or higher-level organ shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism of the unit; the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be dealt with according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　有关人员违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由其所在单位、主管部门、上级机关或者监察机关责令改正，依法给予处理：  
（一）不按要求提供有关材料，拒绝、阻碍调查措施实施等拒不配合监察机关调查的；  
（二）提供虚假情况，掩盖事实真相的；  
（三）串供或者伪造、隐匿、毁灭证据的；  
（四）阻止他人揭发检举、提供证据的；  
（五）其他违反本法规定的行为，情节严重的。

**Article 72** : Where a relevant person violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, his unit, competent department, higher-level organ, or a supervisory organ shall order him to make corrections and impose sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to provide relevant materials as required, refusing or obstructing the implementation of investigation measures, or otherwise refusing to cooperate with an investigation by a supervisory organ;  
(2) Providing false information or concealing the truth;  
(3) Colluding to make false statements, or forging, concealing, or destroying evidence;  
(4) Preventing others from exposing, reporting, or providing evidence;  
(5) Other acts that violate the provisions of this Law, if the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　监察对象对控告人、检举人、证人或者监察人员进行报复陷害的；控告人、检举人、证人捏造事实诬告陷害监察对象的，依法给予处理。

**Article 73** : Where a subject of supervision retaliates against or frames an accuser, informant, witness, or supervisory officer; or where an accuser, informant, or witness fabricates facts to falsely accuse or frame a subject of supervision, they shall be dealt with according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　监察机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处理：  
（一）未经批准、授权处置问题线索，发现重大案情隐瞒不报，或者私自留存、处理涉案材料的；  
（二）利用职权或者职务上的影响干预调查工作、以案谋私的；  
（三）违法窃取、泄露调查工作信息，或者泄露举报事项、举报受理情况以及举报人信息的；  
（四）对被调查人或者涉案人员逼供、诱供，或者侮辱、打骂、虐待、体罚或者变相体罚的；  
（五）违反规定处置查封、扣押、冻结的财物的；  
（六）违反规定发生办案安全事故，或者发生安全事故后隐瞒不报、报告失实、处置不当的；  
（七）违反规定采取强制到案、责令候查、管护、留置或者禁闭措施，或者法定期限届满，不予以解除或者变更的；  
（八）违反规定采取技术调查、限制出境措施，或者不按规定解除技术调查、限制出境措施的；  
（九）利用职权非法干扰企业生产经营或者侵害企业经营者人身权利、财产权利和其他合法权益的；  
（十）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 74** : Where a supervisory organ or its staff commits any of the following acts, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be dealt with according to law:  
(1) Handling clues of problems without approval or authorization, concealing and failing to report the discovery of major case details, or privately retaining or disposing of case-related materials;  
(2) Using authority or the influence of their position to interfere with investigation work or seek personal gain from cases;  
(3) Illegally stealing or disclosing information about investigations, or disclosing information about reported matters, acceptance of reports, or the identity of informants;  
(4) Extorting confessions by torture or inducing confessions from the person under investigation or persons involved in the case, or insulting, scolding, abusing, physically punishing, or imposing disguised physical punishment on them;  
(5) Disposing of sealed, seized, or frozen property in violation of regulations;  
(6) Causing safety accidents in handling cases in violation of regulations, or concealing, falsely reporting, or improperly handling such accidents after they occur;  
(7) Taking measures of compulsory appearance, summons pending investigation, protective custody, summons for investigation, or solitary confinement in violation of regulations, or failing to terminate or modify such measures upon expiration of the statutory time limit;  
(8) Taking technical investigation measures or exit restriction measures in violation of regulations, or failing to lift such measures as required;  
(9) Illegally interfering with the production and operation of an enterprise or infringing upon the personal rights, property rights, or other legitimate rights and interests of an enterprise operator by abusing authority;  
(10) Other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** : Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　监察机关及其工作人员行使职权，侵犯公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益造成损害的，依法给予国家赔偿。

**Article 76** : Where a supervisory organ or its staff, in exercising their functions and powers, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization and causes damage, state compensation shall be given according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队开展监察工作，由中央军事委员会根据本法制定具体规定。

**Article 77** : The People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall conduct supervisory work, and the Central Military Commission shall formulate specific regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　本法自公布之日起施行。《中华人民共和国行政监察法》同时废止。

**Article 78** : This Law shall come into force on the date of promulgation. The Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China shall be abolished simultaneously.
