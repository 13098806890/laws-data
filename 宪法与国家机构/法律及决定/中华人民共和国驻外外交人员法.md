**公布日期**：2009-10-31

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建设高素质的驻外外交人员队伍，保证驻外外交机构依法履行职责，规范驻外外交人员的管理，保障驻外外交人员的合法权益，根据宪法和公务员法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution and the Civil Servant Law for the purposes of building a contingent of high-quality overseas diplomatic personnel, ensuring that overseas diplomatic missions perform their duties according to law, regulating the administration of overseas diplomatic personnel, and safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of overseas diplomatic personnel.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称驻外外交人员，是指在中华人民共和国驻外外交机构中从事外交、领事等工作，使用驻外行政编制，具有外交衔级的人员。  
本法所称驻外外交机构，是指中华人民共和国驻外国的使馆、领馆以及常驻联合国等政府间国际组织的代表团等代表机构。  
驻外外交人员的义务、权利和管理，适用本法。本法未作规定的，适用公务员法的规定。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "overseas diplomatic personnel" refers to persons who engage in diplomatic, consular or other work in the overseas diplomatic missions of the People's Republic of China, are subject to overseas administrative establishment, and hold diplomatic ranks.  
For the purposes of this Law, "overseas diplomatic missions" refer to the embassies, consulates, and representative missions such as permanent missions to intergovernmental international organizations, including the United Nations, of the People's Republic of China in foreign countries.  
The obligations, rights and administration of overseas diplomatic personnel shall be governed by this Law. Matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of the Civil Servant Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　驻外外交人员依法履行职责，受法律保护。

**Article 3** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall perform their duties according to law and be protected by law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外交部统一领导驻外外交机构的工作，会同其他派出部门对驻外外交人员实施管理。

**Article 4** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall exercise unified leadership over the work of overseas diplomatic missions and, jointly with other dispatching departments, administer overseas diplomatic personnel.

### 第二章　职责、条件、义务和权利

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　驻外外交人员应当根据职务和工作分工，履行下列职责：  
（一）维护国家主权、安全、荣誉和利益；  
（二）贯彻执行国家外交方针政策；  
（三）代表国家提出外交交涉；  
（四）发展中国与驻在国之间的关系，参与国际组织活动，促进双边和多边友好交流与合作；  
（五）维护中国公民和法人在国外的正当权益；  
（六）报告驻在国情况和有关地区、国际形势；  
（七）介绍中国情况和内外政策，增进驻在国和世界对中国的了解；  
（八）履行其他外交或者领事职责。  
特命全权大使是中华人民共和国在驻在国的代表。

**Article 5** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall, according to their positions and division of work, perform the following duties:  
(1) Safeguard the sovereignty, security, honor and interests of the State;  
(2) Implement and execute the State's foreign policies;  
(3) Make diplomatic representations on behalf of the State;  
(4) Develop relations between China and the host countries, participate in activities of international organizations, and promote bilateral and multilateral friendly exchanges and cooperation;  
(5) Protect the legitimate rights and interests of Chinese citizens and legal persons abroad;  
(6) Report on the situation of the host countries and the relevant regional and international situation;  
(7) Introduce China's conditions, domestic and foreign policies, and enhance the host countries' and the world's understanding of China;  
(8) Perform other diplomatic or consular duties.  
An ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary is the representative of the People's Republic of China in the host country.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　驻外外交人员应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）年满二十三周岁；  
（三）拥护中华人民共和国宪法；  
（四）具有良好的政治素质和品行；  
（五）具有胜任工作所需的专业知识、工作能力和语言能力；  
（六）具有常驻国外所要求的身体条件、心理素质和适应能力；  
（七）法律规定的其他条件。

**Article 6** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Have the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Be at least 23 years of age;  
(3) Uphold the Constitution of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) Have good political quality and conduct;  
(5) Have the professional knowledge, work ability and language proficiency necessary for the work;  
(6) Have the physical condition, psychological quality and adaptability required for permanent residence abroad;  
(7) Other conditions as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有下列情形之一的，不得任用为驻外外交人员：  
（一）曾因犯罪受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）曾被开除公职的；  
（三）曾被国家机关辞退的；  
（四）持有外国长期或者永久居留许可的；  
（五）配偶具有外国国籍、持有外国长期或者永久居留许可的；  
（六）不得任用为驻外外交人员的其他情形。

**Article 7** A person shall not be appointed as an overseas diplomatic officer under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having been subjected to criminal punishment for commission of a crime;  
(2) Having been discharged from public employment;  
(3) Having been dismissed by a State organ;  
(4) Holding a long-term or permanent residence permit of a foreign country;  
(5) The spouse having foreign nationality, or holding a long-term or permanent residence permit of a foreign country;  
(6) Other circumstances under which appointment as an overseas diplomatic officer is not allowed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　驻外外交人员应当履行下列义务：  
（一）忠于祖国和人民，维护国家尊严；  
（二）忠于中华人民共和国宪法和法律，尊重驻在国的法律和风俗习惯；  
（三）忠于职守，勤勉尽责，完成各项工作任务；  
（四）服从派出部门的调遣，遵守驻外外交机构规章制度和工作纪律；  
（五）严守国家秘密和工作秘密；  
（六）不得在驻外工作期间辞职；  
（七）按照规定向驻外外交机构和派出部门报告个人重大事项；  
（八）法律规定的其他义务。

**Article 8** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall fulfill the following obligations:  
(1) Be loyal to the motherland and the people, and safeguard the dignity of the State;  
(2) Be loyal to the Constitution and laws of the People's Republic of China, and respect the laws and customs of the host countries;  
(3) Be devoted to their duties, be diligent and responsible, and complete all work tasks;  
(4) Obey the assignment of the dispatching departments, and comply with the rules, regulations and work discipline of the overseas diplomatic missions;  
(5) Strictly keep State secrets and work secrets;  
(6) Not resign during the term of overseas service;  
(7) Report major personal matters to the overseas diplomatic missions and the dispatching departments as required; and  
(8) Other obligations as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　驻外外交人员享有下列权利：  
（一）获得履行职责应当具有的工作条件；  
（二）获得与常驻国外工作、生活相适应的工资福利保险待遇；  
（三）在驻外工作期间不被辞退；  
（四）派遣前和驻外工作期间参加培训；  
（五）法律规定的其他权利。

**Article 9** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) Obtain the working conditions necessary for performing their duties;  
(2) Obtain wages, benefits and insurance benefits commensurate with the work and life of being permanently stationed abroad;  
(3) Not be dismissed during the term of overseas service;  
(4) Receive training before dispatch and during the term of overseas service; and  
(5) Other rights as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　驻外外交人员依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约，在驻外工作期间享有相应的特权和豁免。  
驻外外交人员不得滥用特权和豁免，未经批准不得放弃特权和豁免。

**Article 10** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall, in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, enjoy corresponding privileges and immunities during their term of overseas service.  
Overseas diplomatic personnel shall not abuse privileges and immunities, and shall not waive privileges and immunities without approval.

### 第三章　职务和衔级

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　驻外外交人员的职务分为外交职务和领事职务。  
外交职务分为：特命全权大使、代表、副代表、公使、公使衔参赞、参赞、一等秘书、二等秘书、三等秘书、随员。  
领事职务分为：总领事、副总领事、领事、副领事、领事随员。

**Article 11** The posts of overseas diplomatic personnel are divided into diplomatic posts and consular posts.  
Diplomatic posts are divided into: ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary, representative, deputy representative, minister, minister-counselor, counselor, first secretary, second secretary, third secretary, and attaché.  
Consular posts are divided into: consul-general, deputy consul-general, consul, vice-consul, and consular attaché.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　驻外外交人员实行外交衔级制度。  
外交衔级设七级：大使衔、公使衔、参赞衔、一等秘书衔、二等秘书衔、三等秘书衔、随员衔。  
驻外外交人员的外交衔级，根据其在驻外外交机构中担任的职务、公务员职务级别和外交工作需要确定。

**Article 12** The system of diplomatic ranks shall be implemented for overseas diplomatic personnel.  
Diplomatic ranks are divided into seven grades: rank of ambassador, rank of minister, rank of counselor, rank of first secretary, rank of second secretary, rank of third secretary, and rank of attaché.  
The diplomatic rank of an overseas diplomatic officer shall be determined on the basis of his or her post in the overseas diplomatic mission, his or her civil service post level and the needs of diplomatic work.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外交职务与外交衔级的基本对应关系为：  
（一）特命全权大使：大使衔；  
（二）代表、副代表：大使衔、公使衔、参赞衔；  
（三）公使、公使衔参赞：公使衔；  
（四）参赞：参赞衔；  
（五）一等秘书：一等秘书衔；  
（六）二等秘书：二等秘书衔；  
（七）三等秘书：三等秘书衔；  
（八）随员：随员衔。

**Article 13** The basic correspondence between diplomatic posts and diplomatic ranks is as follows:  
(1) Ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary: rank of ambassador;  
(2) Representative and deputy representative: rank of ambassador, rank of minister or rank of counselor;  
(3) Minister and minister-counselor: rank of minister;  
(4) Counselor: rank of counselor;  
(5) First secretary: rank of first secretary;  
(6) Second secretary: rank of second secretary;  
(7) Third secretary: rank of third secretary;  
(8) Attaché: rank of attaché.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　领事职务与外交衔级的基本对应关系为：  
（一）总领事：大使衔、公使衔、参赞衔；  
（二）副总领事：参赞衔；  
（三）领事：参赞衔、一等秘书衔、二等秘书衔；  
（四）副领事：三等秘书衔、随员衔；  
（五）领事随员：随员衔。

**Article 14** The basic correspondence between consular posts and diplomatic ranks is as follows:  
(1) Consul-general: rank of ambassador, rank of minister or rank of counselor;  
(2) Deputy consul-general: rank of counselor;  
(3) Consul: rank of counselor, rank of first secretary or rank of second secretary;  
(4) Vice-consul: rank of third secretary or rank of attaché;  
(5) Consular attaché: rank of attaché.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　驻外外交人员的职务和外交衔级与公务员职务级别的对应关系另行规定。

**Article 15** The correspondence between the posts and diplomatic ranks of overseas diplomatic personnel and the civil service post levels shall be separately prescribed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　驻外外交人员的职务按照下列权限决定：  
（一）特命全权大使和代表、副代表为特命全权大使的，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定；  
（二）前项之外的代表、副代表，由国务院决定；  
（三）总领事，由外交部决定；  
（四）公使、公使衔参赞、参赞、副总领事以及其他驻外外交人员的职务，由外交部或者其他派出部门决定；其中，三等秘书、副领事以下职务，在驻外工作期间由驻外外交机构决定。

**Article 16** The posts of overseas diplomatic personnel shall be determined according to the following competence:  
(1) An ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary, and a representative or deputy representative who is an ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary, shall be determined by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress;  
(2) Representatives and deputy representatives other than those prescribed in the preceding subparagraph shall be determined by the State Council;  
(3) A consul-general shall be determined by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(4) The posts of minister, minister-counselor, counselor, deputy consul-general and other overseas diplomatic personnel shall be determined by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or other dispatching departments; among them, posts below third secretary and vice-consul shall be determined by the overseas diplomatic mission during the term of overseas service.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外交衔级按照下列权限批准和授予：  
（一）大使衔，由国务院总理批准授予；  
（二）公使衔、参赞衔，由外交部或者其他派出部门批准，外交部部长授予；  
（三）一等秘书衔、二等秘书衔，派遣时由派出部门批准和授予，驻外工作期间由派出部门根据驻外外交机构的意见批准和授予；  
（四）三等秘书衔、随员衔，派遣时由派出部门批准和授予，驻外工作期间由驻外外交机构批准和授予。

**Article 17** Diplomatic ranks shall be approved and conferred according to the following competence:  
(1) The rank of ambassador shall be approved and conferred by the Premier of the State Council;  
(2) The ranks of minister and counselor shall be approved by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or other dispatching departments and conferred by the Minister of Foreign Affairs;  
(3) The ranks of first secretary and second secretary shall be approved and conferred by the dispatching department at the time of dispatch, and shall be approved and conferred by the dispatching department based on the opinion of the overseas diplomatic mission during the term of overseas service;  
(4) The ranks of third secretary and attaché shall be approved and conferred by the dispatching department at the time of dispatch, and shall be approved and conferred by the overseas diplomatic mission during the term of overseas service.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　驻外外交人员晋升三等秘书、副领事以及相应的衔级，按照规定的晋升条件、期限，分别依照[本法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条规定的权限逐级晋升；经考核不具备晋升条件的，根据驻外外交机构的意见，经派出部门批准，延期晋升。  
驻外外交人员晋升二等秘书、一等秘书、参赞、公使衔参赞、公使或者领事、副总领事以及相应的衔级，根据晋升条件、期限和驻外外交机构提出的晋升意见，分别依照[本法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条规定的权限，逐级择优晋升。  
驻外外交人员晋升职务和衔级的条件、期限另行规定。

**Article 18** The promotion of an overseas diplomatic officer to the post of third secretary, vice-consul or the corresponding rank shall be conducted step by step according to the prescribed promotion conditions and time limits, and in accordance with the competence provided for in Articles 16 and 17 of this Law respectively; where, after assessment, the officer does not meet the conditions for promotion, the promotion shall be postponed based on the opinion of the overseas diplomatic mission and with the approval of the dispatching department.  
The promotion of an overseas diplomatic officer to the post of second secretary, first secretary, counselor, minister-counselor, minister or consul, deputy consul-general or the corresponding rank shall be conducted step by step on a selective basis according to the promotion conditions, time limits and the promotion opinion submitted by the overseas diplomatic mission, and in accordance with the competence provided for in Articles 16 and 17 of this Law respectively.  
The conditions and time limits for the promotion of overseas diplomatic personnel in posts and ranks shall be separately prescribed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　驻外外交人员在受处分期间，其职务和衔级不予晋升。

**Article 19** An overseas diplomatic officer shall not be promoted in post or rank while under disciplinary sanction.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　驻外外交人员受到降级、撤职处分的，应当按照规定降低衔级。降低衔级不适用于随员衔。  
驻外外交人员离任回国，其衔级相应终止。从事外交工作确有需要的，可以保留。具体办法由外交部会同国务院有关部门另行规定。

**Article 20** Where an overseas diplomatic officer is given the sanction of demotion in rank or removal from post, his or her diplomatic rank shall be reduced accordingly. Reduction of rank shall not apply to the rank of attaché.  
When an overseas diplomatic officer leaves the post and returns to China, his or her rank shall be terminated accordingly. Where such rank is truly necessary for engaging in diplomatic work, it may be retained. The specific measures shall be separately prescribed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第四章　馆长

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　馆长是驻外外交机构的行政首长。特命全权大使为大使馆的馆长。代表为常驻联合国等政府间国际组织的代表机构的馆长。总领事为总领事馆的馆长。领事为领事馆的馆长。  
驻外外交机构实行馆长负责制。馆长统一领导驻外外交机构的各项工作。

**Article 21** The head of mission is the chief executive of the overseas diplomatic mission. An ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary is the head of an embassy. A representative is the head of a representative mission to intergovernmental international organizations such as the United Nations. A consul-general is the head of a consulate-general. A consul is the head of a consulate.  
The system of the head of mission assuming full responsibility shall be implemented in an overseas diplomatic mission. The head of mission shall exercise unified leadership over all work of the overseas diplomatic mission.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　馆长缺位或者因故不能执行职务时，由被指定的人员代理馆长行使职责。

**Article 22** Where the head of mission is absent or is unable to perform duties for any reason, the person designated shall act as the head of mission and exercise the duties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　馆长应当向派出部门提交到任和离任的书面报告。  
馆长应当按期回国述职。

**Article 23** The head of mission shall submit written reports on assumption of office and departure from office to the dispatching department.  
The head of mission shall return to China for reporting work on schedule.

### 第五章　派遣、召回和调回

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　特命全权大使和代表、副代表为特命全权大使的，由中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定派遣和召回。  
前款以外的代表、副代表，由国务院或者派出部门派遣和调回。  
其他驻外外交人员，由外交部或者其他派出部门派遣和调回。

**Article 24** An ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary, and a representative or deputy representative who is an ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary, shall be dispatched and recalled by the President of the People's Republic of China upon the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
Representatives and deputy representatives other than those prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be dispatched and recalled by the State Council or the dispatching department.  
Other overseas diplomatic personnel shall be dispatched and recalled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or other dispatching departments.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　驻外外交人员实行任期制度。根据工作需要，经派出部门批准，驻外外交人员的任职期限可以适当缩短或者延长。

**Article 25** The term system shall be implemented for overseas diplomatic personnel. According to work needs and with the approval of the dispatching department, the term of office of an overseas diplomatic officer may be appropriately shortened or extended.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　驻外外交人员有下列情形之一的，应当提前调回：  
（一）另有工作安排的；  
（二）不能履行职责的；  
（三）触犯法律或者严重违纪的；  
（四）配偶取得外国国籍、外国长期或者永久居留许可的；  
（五）不宜继续在驻外外交机构工作的其他情形。

**Article 26** An overseas diplomatic officer shall be recalled in advance under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Another work assignment is arranged;  
(2) The officer is unable to perform duties;  
(3) The officer violates the law or commits serious disciplinary violations;  
(4) The spouse acquires foreign nationality or a foreign long-term or permanent residence permit; or  
(5) Other circumstances under which the officer is unsuitable to continue working in the overseas diplomatic mission.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家可以根据需要紧急召回、调回或者撤出相关驻外外交机构的部分人员或者全部人员。

**Article 27** The State may, as needed, urgently recall, withdraw or evacuate part or all of the personnel of a relevant overseas diplomatic mission.

### 第六章　考核、培训和交流

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对驻外外交人员的考核分为平时考核和定期考核，由驻外外交机构或者派出部门按照国家规定组织实施。  
对驻外外交人员的考核，以其承担的职责和工作任务为基本依据，全面考核德、能、勤、绩、廉，重点考核工作实绩。  
考核的结果作为调整驻外外交人员职务、衔级、级别、工资以及对驻外外交人员进行奖励、培训等的依据。

**Article 28** The assessment of overseas diplomatic personnel shall be divided into routine assessment and periodic assessment, and shall be organized and implemented by the overseas diplomatic mission or the dispatching department in accordance with the provisions of the State.  
The assessment of an overseas diplomatic officer shall take his or her responsibilities and work tasks as the basic basis, and shall comprehensively assess his or her morality, ability, diligence, performance and integrity, with emphasis on the assessment of work achievements.  
The results of assessment shall be the basis for adjusting the post, rank, level, and wage of an overseas diplomatic officer, and for granting rewards and providing training to overseas diplomatic personnel.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　派遣前和驻外工作期间，根据工作职责的要求，应当对驻外外交人员进行任职和专业培训。

**Article 29** Before dispatch and during the term of overseas service, overseas diplomatic personnel shall receive orientation and professional training according to the requirements of work duties.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　驻外外交人员应当在驻外外交机构之间进行任职交流；根据工作需要，也可以在派出部门或者其他机关之间进行任职交流。

**Article 30** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall undergo post exchange among overseas diplomatic missions; according to work needs, post exchange may also be carried out between dispatching departments or other organs.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　驻外外交人员的培训情况和任职交流经历，列入考核内容。

**Article 31** The training status and post exchange experience of an overseas diplomatic officer shall be included in the assessment content.

### 第七章　奖励和惩戒

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　驻外外交机构或者驻外外交人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予奖励：  
（一）为维护国家主权、安全、荣誉和利益作出重大贡献的；  
（二）为维护中国公民和法人在国外的人身、财产安全或者其他正当权益作出突出贡献的；  
（三）在应对重大突发事件中作出重大贡献的；  
（四）在战乱等特定艰苦环境中有突出事迹的；  
（五）为保护国家秘密作出突出贡献的；  
（六）遵守纪律，廉洁奉公，作风正派，办事公道，模范作用突出的；  
（七）尽职尽责，工作实绩突出的；  
（八）有其他突出表现应当给予奖励的。

**Article 32** An overseas diplomatic mission or an overseas diplomatic officer shall be granted rewards according to law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having made significant contributions to safeguarding the sovereignty, security, honor and interests of the State;  
(2) Having made outstanding contributions to protecting the personal safety, property safety or other legitimate rights and interests of Chinese citizens and legal persons abroad;  
(3) Having made significant contributions in responding to major emergencies;  
(4) Having outstanding deeds in specific harsh environments such as war;  
(5) Having made outstanding contributions to the protection of State secrets;  
(6) Having demonstrated outstanding exemplary conduct by observing discipline, being honest and devoted to public duties, having a correct style of work, conducting affairs impartially;  
(7) Having fulfilled duties with due diligence and achieved outstanding work performance; or  
(8) Having other outstanding performance deserving rewards.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　驻外外交人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予相应的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）损害国家主权、安全、荣誉和利益的；  
（二）擅自脱离驻外外交机构的；  
（三）泄露国家秘密或者工作秘密的；  
（四）利用职务之便为自己或者他人谋取私利的；  
（五）从事或者参与营利性活动，在企业或者其他营利性组织中兼任职务的；  
（六）玩忽职守，贻误工作的；  
（七）不服从调遣，拒绝赴派往的岗位工作的；  
（八）有其他违法或者违纪行为的。

**Article 33** An overseas diplomatic officer who commits any of the following acts shall be given corresponding sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Damaging the sovereignty, security, honor and interests of the State;  
(2) Leaving the overseas diplomatic mission without authorization;  
(3) Divulging State secrets or work secrets;  
(4) Taking advantage of his or her position to seek personal gain for himself or herself or others;  
(5) Engaging in or participating in profit-making activities, or concurrently holding posts in enterprises or other profit-making organizations;  
(6) Being derelict of duty and causing work delays;  
(7) Disobeying assignment and refusing to work at the post to which he or she is dispatched; or  
(8) Committing other illegal or disciplinary violations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外交部或者其他派出部门依照法定的权限和程序对驻外外交机构进行奖励，对驻外外交人员给予奖励或者惩戒。

**Article 34** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs or other dispatching departments shall, in accordance with the statutory competence and procedures, grant rewards to overseas diplomatic missions and give rewards or impose sanctions on overseas diplomatic personnel.

### 第八章　工资和福利

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　驻外外交人员实行职务、衔级与级别相结合的驻外工资制度。

**Article 35** The wage system for overseas diplomatic personnel shall combine post, rank and grade.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家建立驻外外交人员工资调整机制，适时调整驻外外交人员的工资和生活待遇。

**Article 36** The State shall establish a wage adjustment mechanism for overseas diplomatic personnel, and shall timely adjust the wages and living benefits of overseas diplomatic personnel.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　驻外外交人员按照国家规定享受津贴、补贴。

**Article 37** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall enjoy allowances and subsidies in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家为驻外外交人员提供必要的医疗保障和安全措施，并按照有关规定提供必要的人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 38** The State shall provide necessary medical security and safety measures for overseas diplomatic personnel, and provide necessary personal accident insurance in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　驻外外交人员享受国家规定的带薪年休假和任期假。

**Article 39** Overseas diplomatic personnel shall enjoy paid annual leave and term leave as prescribed by the State.

### 第九章　配偶和子女

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　驻外外交人员办理结婚手续前，应当按照规定如实向派出部门报告结婚对象的身份等情况。  
驻外外交人员离婚，应当及时向派出部门报告。

**Article 40** Before going through the marriage formalities, an overseas diplomatic officer shall, in accordance with the provisions, truthfully report to the dispatching department information such as the identity of the spouse-to-be.  
An overseas diplomatic officer who divorces shall promptly report to the dispatching department.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　驻外外交人员的配偶随任，应当经派出部门批准。  
驻外外交人员任期结束或者提前调回的，随任配偶应当同时结束随任。

**Article 41** The spouse of an overseas diplomatic officer who accompanies the officer on mission shall be subject to the approval of the dispatching department.  
Where an overseas diplomatic officer ends the term of office or is recalled in advance, the accompanying spouse shall end the accompanying period at the same time.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　驻外外交人员配偶随任期间，根据驻外外交人员的职务和衔级、任职年限和驻在国生活条件，享受规定的休假待遇。

**Article 42** During the period of accompanying, the spouse of an overseas diplomatic officer shall enjoy the prescribed leave benefits based on the officer's post and rank, length of service, and living conditions in the host country.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　驻外外交人员配偶为国家机关、事业单位在编人员，国有独资企业、国有独资公司、国有资本控股公司人员或者现役军人的，随任期间和结束随任回国后在原单位的工作安排，按照国家规定办理；原单位不得因其随任将其开除、辞退或者收取补偿金、管理费等费用。

**Article 43** Where the spouse of an overseas diplomatic officer is a regular employee of a State organ, public institution, wholly State-owned enterprise, wholly State-owned company, State-controlled company, or an active service serviceman, the work arrangement during the accompanying period and after returning to China at the end of the accompanying period shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the State; the original employer shall not discharge, dismiss or charge compensation, management fees or other fees due to the accompanying period.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　驻外外交人员配偶未随任的，经派出部门批准，可以赴驻外外交机构探亲，按照规定享受相应的探亲假、旅费和补贴。

**Article 44** Where the spouse of an overseas diplomatic officer does not accompany the officer, the spouse may, with the approval of the dispatching department, visit the officer at the overseas diplomatic mission and shall enjoy corresponding home leave, travel expenses and subsidies in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　驻外外交人员的未成年子女，经派出部门批准，可以赴驻外外交机构随居或者探亲。  
国家采取多种措施，保障驻外外交人员的未成年子女依法享有受教育的权利。

**Article 45** The minor children of an overseas diplomatic officer may, with the approval of the dispatching department, reside with the officer or visit the officer at the overseas diplomatic mission.  
The State shall adopt various measures to ensure that the minor children of overseas diplomatic personnel enjoy the right to education according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　随任、随居和探亲的驻外外交人员配偶、子女，应当遵守驻外外交机构规章制度，尊重驻在国的法律和风俗习惯。

**Article 46** The spouses and children of overseas diplomatic personnel who accompany, reside with or visit the officer shall comply with the rules and regulations of the overseas diplomatic mission and respect the laws and customs of the host country.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　驻外外交机构武官参照本法管理，具体办法由中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 47** The military attachés of overseas diplomatic missions shall be managed with reference to this Law, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本法自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 48** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2010.
