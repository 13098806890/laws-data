# 中华人民共和国国务院组织法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-11  
**生效日期**：2024-03-11

> （1982年12月10日第五届全国人民代表大会第五次会议通过并于同日公布施行　2024年3月11日第十四届全国人民代表大会第二次会议修订） 第一条　为了健全国务院的组织和工作制度，保障和规范国务院行使职权，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国国务院，即中央人民政府，是最高国家权力机关的执行机关，是最高国家行政机关。 第三条　国务院坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚决维护党中央权威和集中统一领导，坚决贯彻落实党中央决策部署，贯彻新发展理念，坚持依法行政，依照宪法和法律规定，全面正确履行政府职能。 国务院坚持以人民为中心、全心全意为人民服务，坚持和发展全过程人民民主，始终同人民保持密切联系，倾听人民的意见和建议，建设人民满意的法治政府、创新政府、廉洁政府和服务型政府。 第四条　国务院对全国人民代表大会负责并报告工作；在全国人民代表大会闭会期间，对全国人民代表大会常务委员会负责并报告工作。 国务院应当自觉接受全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的监督。 第五条　国务院由总理、副总理、国务委员、各部部长、各委员会主任、中国人民银行行长、审计长、秘书长组成。 国务院实行总理负责制。总理领导国务院的工作。 副总理、国务委员协助总理工作，按照分工负责分管领域工作；受总理委托，负责其他方面的工作或者专项任务；根据统一安排，代表国务院进行外事活动。 第六条　国务院行使宪法和有关法律规定的职权。 第七条　国务院实行国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议制度。国务院全体会议由国务院全体成员组成。国务院常务会议由总理、副总理、国务委员、秘书长组成。总理召集和主持国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议。国务院工作中的重大问题，必须经国务院常务会议或者国务院全体会议讨论决定。 第八条　国务院全体会议的主要任务是讨论决定政府工作报告、国民经济和社会发展规划等国务院工作中的重大事项，部署国务院的重要工作。 国务院常务会议的主要任务是讨论法律草案、审议行政法规草案，讨论、决定、通报国务院工作中的重要事项。 国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议讨论决定的事项，除依法需要保密的外，应当及时公布。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全国务院的组织和工作制度，保障和规范国务院行使职权，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to improve the organization and working system of the State Council, and to ensure and regulate the exercise of the functions and powers of the State Council.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国国务院，即中央人民政府，是最高国家权力机关的执行机关，是最高国家行政机关。

**Article 2** The State Council of the People's Republic of China, namely the Central People's Government, is the executive organ of the highest state power organ and the highest state administrative organ.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，坚决维护党中央权威和集中统一领导，坚决贯彻落实党中央决策部署，贯彻新发展理念，坚持依法行政，依照宪法和法律规定，全面正确履行政府职能。  
国务院坚持以人民为中心、全心全意为人民服务，坚持和发展全过程人民民主，始终同人民保持密切联系，倾听人民的意见和建议，建设人民满意的法治政府、创新政府、廉洁政府和服务型政府。

**Article 3** The State Council shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Theory of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, resolutely safeguard the authority and centralized unified leadership of the Party Central Committee, resolutely implement the decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee, implement the new development philosophy, persist in law-based administration, and fully and correctly perform government functions in accordance with the Constitution and laws.  
The State Council shall adhere to a people-centered approach, serve the people wholeheartedly, uphold and develop whole-process people's democracy, always maintain close ties with the people, listen to their opinions and suggestions, and build a law-based government, an innovative government, a clean government, and a service-oriented government that satisfies the people.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院对全国人民代表大会负责并报告工作；在全国人民代表大会闭会期间，对全国人民代表大会常务委员会负责并报告工作。  
国务院应当自觉接受全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的监督。

**Article 4** The State Council shall be responsible to and report on its work to the National People's Congress; when the National People's Congress is not in session, it shall be responsible to and report on its work to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
The State Council shall voluntarily accept the supervision of the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院由总理、副总理、国务委员、各部部长、各委员会主任、中国人民银行行长、审计长、秘书长组成。  
国务院实行总理负责制。总理领导国务院的工作。  
副总理、国务委员协助总理工作，按照分工负责分管领域工作；受总理委托，负责其他方面的工作或者专项任务；根据统一安排，代表国务院进行外事活动。

**Article 5** The State Council shall be composed of the Premier, Vice Premiers, State Councilors, Ministers in charge of ministries, Ministers in charge of commissions, the Governor of the People's Bank of China, the Auditor General, and the Secretary-General.  
The State Council shall practice the Premier accountability system. The Premier shall lead the work of the State Council.  
The Vice Premiers and the State Councilors shall assist the Premier in his work, and shall be responsible for the work in their respective areas according to the division of duties; upon entrustment by the Premier, they may be responsible for other aspects of work or specific tasks; and they may conduct foreign affairs on behalf of the State Council as uniformly arranged.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院行使宪法和有关法律规定的职权。

**Article 6** The State Council shall exercise the functions and powers prescribed by the Constitution and relevant laws.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院实行国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议制度。国务院全体会议由国务院全体成员组成。国务院常务会议由总理、副总理、国务委员、秘书长组成。总理召集和主持国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议。国务院工作中的重大问题，必须经国务院常务会议或者国务院全体会议讨论决定。

**Article 7** The State Council shall practice a system of plenary sessions and executive meetings of the State Council. The plenary sessions of the State Council shall be composed of all members of the State Council. The executive meetings of the State Council shall be composed of the Premier, Vice Premiers, State Councilors, and the Secretary-General. The Premier shall convene and preside over the plenary sessions and executive meetings of the State Council. Major issues in the work of the State Council must be discussed and decided by the executive meetings or plenary sessions of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院全体会议的主要任务是讨论决定政府工作报告、国民经济和社会发展规划等国务院工作中的重大事项，部署国务院的重要工作。  
国务院常务会议的主要任务是讨论法律草案、审议行政法规草案，讨论、决定、通报国务院工作中的重要事项。  
国务院全体会议和国务院常务会议讨论决定的事项，除依法需要保密的外，应当及时公布。  
国务院根据需要召开总理办公会议和国务院专题会议。

**Article 8** The main tasks of the plenary sessions of the State Council shall be to discuss and decide on major matters in the work of the State Council, such as the government work report and the national economic and social development plan, and to deploy important work of the State Council.  
The main tasks of the executive meetings of the State Council shall be to discuss draft laws, deliberate draft administrative regulations, and discuss, decide, and report on important matters in the work of the State Council.  
Matters discussed and decided at the plenary sessions and executive meetings of the State Council shall be made public in a timely manner, except those that need to be kept confidential according to law.  
The State Council may, as needed, convene the Premier's office meetings and the State Council's special meetings.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院发布的行政法规、决定、命令，向全国人民代表大会或者全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出的议案，任免人员，由总理签署。

**Article 9** Administrative regulations, decisions, and orders issued by the State Council, proposals submitted to the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee, and appointments and removals of personnel shall be signed by the Premier.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院秘书长在总理领导下，负责处理国务院的日常工作。  
国务院设副秘书长若干人，协助秘书长工作。  
国务院设立办公厅，由秘书长领导。

**Article 10** The Secretary-General of the State Council, under the leadership of the Premier, shall be responsible for handling the day-to-day work of the State Council.  
The State Council shall have a number of Deputy Secretaries-General to assist the Secretary-General in his work.  
The State Council shall establish a General Office to be led by the Secretary-General.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院组成部门的设立、撤销或者合并，经总理提出，由全国人民代表大会决定；在全国人民代表大会闭会期间，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。国务院组成部门确定或者调整后，由全国人民代表大会或者全国人民代表大会常务委员会公布。

**Article 11** The establishment, dissolution, or merger of constituent departments of the State Council shall be proposed by the Premier and decided by the National People's Congress; when the National People's Congress is not in session, it shall be decided by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. After the constituent departments of the State Council are determined or adjusted, they shall be announced by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院组成部门设部长（主任、行长、审计长）一人，副部长（副主任、副行长、副审计长）二至四人；委员会可以设委员五至十人。  
国务院组成部门实行部长（主任、行长、审计长）负责制。部长（主任、行长、审计长）领导本部门的工作，召集和主持部务（委务、行务、署务）会议，讨论决定本部门工作的重大问题；签署上报国务院的重要请示、报告和发布的命令、指示。副部长（副主任、副行长、副审计长）协助部长（主任、行长、审计长）工作。  
国务院副秘书长、各部副部长、各委员会副主任、中国人民银行副行长、副审计长由国务院任免。

**Article 12** Each constituent department of the State Council shall have one Minister (Minister in charge of a commission, Governor, or Auditor General) and two to four Deputy Ministers (Deputy Ministers in charge of a commission, Deputy Governors, or Deputy Auditors General); a commission may have five to ten members.  
Each constituent department of the State Council shall practice the accountability system of the Minister (Minister in charge of a commission, Governor, or Auditor General). The Minister (Minister in charge of a commission, Governor, or Auditor General) shall lead the work of the department, convene and preside over the departmental (commission, bank, or office) meetings to discuss and decide on major issues in the work of the department, and sign important requests for instructions and reports submitted to the State Council, as well as orders and directives issued. The Deputy Ministers (Deputy Ministers in charge of a commission, Deputy Governors, or Deputy Auditors General) shall assist the Minister (Minister in charge of a commission, Governor, or Auditor General) in his work.  
The Deputy Secretaries-General of the State Council, Deputy Ministers of ministries, Deputy Ministers in charge of commissions, Deputy Governors of the People's Bank of China, and Deputy Auditors General shall be appointed or removed by the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院可以根据工作需要和优化协同高效精简的原则，按照规定程序设立若干直属机构主管各项专门业务，设立若干办事机构协助总理办理专门事项。每个机构设负责人二至五人，由国务院任免。

**Article 13** The State Council may, in light of work needs and the principles of optimization, coordination, efficiency, and simplification, establish according to the prescribed procedures a number of directly affiliated organs to take charge of specific professional work, and establish a number of administrative offices to assist the Premier in handling specific matters. Each organ shall have two to five heads, who shall be appointed or removed by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院组成部门工作中的方针、政策、计划和重大行政措施，应当向国务院请示报告，由国务院决定。根据法律和国务院的行政法规、决定、命令，主管部门可以在本部门的权限范围内发布命令、指示。  
国务院组成部门和具有行政管理职能的直属机构以及法律规定的机构，可以根据法律和国务院的行政法规、决定、命令，在本部门的权限范围内，制定规章。

**Article 14** The principles, policies, plans, and major administrative measures in the work of the constituent departments of the State Council shall be reported to the State Council for instructions and be decided by the State Council. In accordance with laws and the administrative regulations, decisions, and orders of the State Council, the competent departments may issue orders and directives within the limits of their powers.  
The constituent departments of the State Council, the directly affiliated organs with administrative functions, and the organs prescribed by law may, in accordance with laws and the administrative regulations, decisions, and orders of the State Council, formulate rules within the limits of their powers.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院统一领导全国地方各级国家行政机关的工作。

**Article 15** The State Council shall exercise unified leadership over the work of local state administrative organs at all levels throughout the country.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院坚持科学决策、民主决策、依法决策，健全行政决策制度体系，规范重大行政决策程序，加强行政决策执行和评估，提高决策质量和效率。

**Article 16** The State Council shall adhere to scientific, democratic, and law-based decision-making, improve the system of administrative decision-making, regulate the procedures for major administrative decisions, strengthen the implementation and evaluation of administrative decisions, and enhance the quality and efficiency of decision-making.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院健全行政监督制度，加强行政复议、备案审查、行政执法监督、政府督查等工作，坚持政务公开，自觉接受各方面监督，强化对行政权力运行的制约和监督。

**Article 17** The State Council shall improve the administrative supervision system, strengthen work such as administrative reconsideration, archival review, supervision over administrative law enforcement, and government inspection, adhere to transparency in government affairs, voluntarily accept supervision from all sides, and reinforce the restraint and supervision over the exercise of administrative power.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院组成人员应当坚决维护党中央权威和集中统一领导，模范遵守宪法和法律，认真履行职责，带头反对形式主义、官僚主义，为民务实，严守纪律，勤勉廉洁。

**Article 18** Members of the State Council shall resolutely safeguard the authority and centralized unified leadership of the Party Central Committee, faithfully abide by the Constitution and laws, earnestly perform their duties, take the lead in opposing formalism and bureaucracy, be pragmatic for the people, strictly observe discipline, and be diligent and honest.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院组成部门、直属机构、办事机构应当各司其职、各负其责、加强协调、密切配合，确保党中央、国务院各项工作部署贯彻落实。

**Article 19** The constituent departments, directly affiliated organs, and administrative offices of the State Council shall perform their respective duties, assume their respective responsibilities, strengthen coordination, and cooperate closely to ensure that all work deployments of the Party Central Committee and the State Council are implemented and carried out.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 20** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
