### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府的组织和工作制度，保障和规范其行使职权，坚持和完善人民代表大会制度，保证人民当家作主，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to improve the organization and working systems of local people's congresses and local people's governments at various levels, to guarantee and regulate the exercise of their functions and powers, to uphold and improve the system of people's congresses, and to ensure the people are the masters of the country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地方各级人民代表大会是地方国家权力机关。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会是本级人民代表大会的常设机关。  
地方各级人民政府是地方各级国家权力机关的执行机关，是地方各级国家行政机关。

**Article 2** Local people's congresses at various levels are local organs of state power.  
The standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level are the permanent organs of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels.  
Local people's governments at various levels are the executive organs of the local organs of state power at the corresponding levels and are local state administrative organs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地方各级人民代表大会、县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会和地方各级人民政府坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，依照宪法和法律规定行使职权。

**Article 3** Local people's congresses at various levels, the standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level, and local people's governments at various levels shall uphold the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as their guide, and exercise their functions and powers in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民代表大会、县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会和地方各级人民政府坚持以人民为中心，坚持和发展全过程人民民主，始终同人民保持密切联系，倾听人民的意见和建议，为人民服务，对人民负责，受人民监督。

**Article 4** Local people's congresses at various levels, the standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level, and local people's governments at various levels shall adhere to a people-centered approach, uphold and develop whole-process people's democracy, maintain close ties with the people at all times, listen to the opinions and suggestions of the people, serve the people, be responsible to the people, and be subject to the supervision of the people.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民代表大会、县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会和地方各级人民政府遵循在中央的统一领导下、充分发挥地方的主动性积极性的原则，保证宪法、法律和行政法规在本行政区域的实施。

**Article 5** Local people's congresses at various levels, the standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level, and local people's governments at various levels shall follow the principle of giving full play to the initiative and enthusiasm of local authorities under the unified leadership of the central authorities, and ensure the implementation of the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　地方各级人民代表大会、县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会和地方各级人民政府实行民主集中制原则。  
地方各级人民代表大会和县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会应当充分发扬民主，集体行使职权。  
地方各级人民政府实行首长负责制。政府工作中的重大事项应当经集体讨论决定。

**Article 6** Local people's congresses at various levels, the standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level, and local people's governments at various levels shall practice the principle of democratic centralism.  
Local people's congresses at various levels and the standing committees of local people's congresses at and above the county level shall fully promote democracy and exercise their functions and powers collectively.  
Local people's governments at various levels shall practice the system of responsibility of the chief executive. Major matters in government work shall be decided through collective discussion.

### 第二章　地方各级人民代表大会

#### 第一节　地方各级人民代表大会的组成和任期

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇设立人民代表大会。

**Article 7** People's congresses shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities, districts under cities, townships, ethnic townships, and towns.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会代表由下一级的人民代表大会选举；县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表由选民直接选举。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表名额和代表产生办法由选举法规定。各行政区域内的少数民族应当有适当的代表名额。

**Article 8** Deputies to the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, and cities divided into districts shall be elected by the people's congresses at the next lower level; deputies to the people's congresses of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, districts under cities, townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall be directly elected by the voters.  
The numbers of deputies to local people's congresses at various levels and the methods of their election shall be prescribed by the Electoral Law. The minority nationalities in each administrative region shall have an appropriate number of deputies.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级人民代表大会每届任期五年。

**Article 9** The term of office of each local people's congress at any level shall be five years.

#### 第二节　地方各级人民代表大会的职权

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会根据本行政区域的具体情况和实际需要，在不同宪法、法律、行政法规相抵触的前提下，可以制定和颁布地方性法规，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案。  
设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会根据本行政区域的具体情况和实际需要，在不同宪法、法律、行政法规和本省、自治区的地方性法规相抵触的前提下，可以依照法律规定的权限制定地方性法规，报省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会批准后施行，并由省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会报全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市以及设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会根据区域协调发展的需要，可以开展协同立法。

**Article 10** The people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in light of the specific conditions and actual needs of its administrative region and on condition that it does not contravene the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations, formulate and promulgate local regulations, and shall report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for recordation.  
The people's congress of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture may, in light of the specific conditions and actual needs of its administrative region and on condition that it does not contravene the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the local regulations of the relevant province or autonomous region, and within the limits of its authority as prescribed by law, formulate local regulations, which shall come into force after approval by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province or autonomous region, and shall be reported by the standing committee of the people's congress of the province or autonomous region to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for recordation.  
The people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures may, in light of the needs for coordinated regional development, carry out coordinated legislation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会行使下列职权：  
（一）在本行政区域内，保证宪法、法律、行政法规和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会决议的遵守和执行，保证国家计划和国家预算的执行；  
（二）审查和批准本行政区域内的国民经济和社会发展规划纲要、计划和预算及其执行情况的报告，审查监督政府债务，监督本级人民政府对国有资产的管理；  
（三）讨论、决定本行政区域内的政治、经济、教育、科学、文化、卫生、生态环境保护、自然资源、城乡建设、民政、社会保障、民族等工作的重大事项和项目；  
（四）选举本级人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员；  
（五）选举省长、副省长，自治区主席、副主席，市长、副市长，州长、副州长，县长、副县长，区长、副区长；  
（六）选举本级监察委员会主任、人民法院院长和人民检察院检察长；选出的人民检察院检察长，须报经上一级人民检察院检察长提请该级人民代表大会常务委员会批准；  
（七）选举上一级人民代表大会代表；  
（八）听取和审议本级人民代表大会常务委员会的工作报告；  
（九）听取和审议本级人民政府和人民法院、人民检察院的工作报告；  
（十）改变或者撤销本级人民代表大会常务委员会的不适当的决议；  
（十一）撤销本级人民政府的不适当的决定和命令；  
（十二）保护社会主义的全民所有的财产和劳动群众集体所有的财产，保护公民私人所有的合法财产，维护社会秩序，保障公民的人身权利、民主权利和其他权利；  
（十三）保护各种经济组织的合法权益；  
（十四）铸牢中华民族共同体意识，促进各民族广泛交往交流交融，保障少数民族的合法权利和利益；  
（十五）保障宪法和法律赋予妇女的男女平等、同工同酬和婚姻自由等各项权利。

**Article 11** A local people's congress at or above the county level shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To ensure the observance and implementation of the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the resolutions of the people's congress at the next higher level and its standing committee within its administrative region, and to ensure the implementation of the state plan and state budget;  
(2) To examine and approve the outline of the national economic and social development plan, the plan, and the budget of its administrative region, as well as the reports on their implementation, to examine and supervise government debt, and to supervise the management of state-owned assets by the people's government at the corresponding level;  
(3) To discuss and decide on major matters and projects concerning political, economic, educational, scientific, cultural, public health, ecological and environmental protection, natural resources, urban and rural development, civil affairs, social security, and ethnic affairs within its administrative region;  
(4) To elect the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(5) To elect the governor and deputy governors of a province, the chairman and vice-chairmen of an autonomous region, the mayor and deputy mayors of a city, the prefect and deputy prefects of an autonomous prefecture, the county head and deputy county heads, and the district head and deputy district heads;  
(6) To elect the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, and the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate at the corresponding level; the elected chief procurator of the people's procuratorate shall be reported to the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for submission to the standing committee of the people's congress at that level for approval;  
(7) To elect deputies to the people's congress at the next higher level;  
(8) To hear and deliberate the work report of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(9) To hear and deliberate the work reports of the people's government, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate at the corresponding level;  
(10) To alter or annul inappropriate resolutions of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(11) To annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the people's government at the corresponding level;  
(12) To protect socialist property owned by the whole people and property collectively owned by the working masses, protect the lawful private property of citizens, maintain social order, and safeguard the personal rights, democratic rights, and other rights of citizens;  
(13) To protect the lawful rights and interests of various economic organizations;  
(14) To forge a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, promote extensive exchanges, interactions, and integration among all ethnic groups, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of minority nationalities;  
(15) To guarantee the rights of women, including equality between men and women, equal pay for equal work, and freedom of marriage, as conferred by the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会行使下列职权：  
（一）在本行政区域内，保证宪法、法律、行政法规和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会决议的遵守和执行；  
（二）在职权范围内通过和发布决议；  
（三）根据国家计划，决定本行政区域内的经济、文化事业和公共事业的建设计划和项目；  
（四）审查和批准本行政区域内的预算和预算执行情况的报告，监督本级预算的执行，审查和批准本级预算的调整方案，审查和批准本级决算；  
（五）决定本行政区域内的民政工作的实施计划；  
（六）选举本级人民代表大会主席、副主席；  
（七）选举乡长、副乡长，镇长、副镇长；  
（八）听取和审议乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府的工作报告；  
（九）听取和审议乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团的工作报告；  
（十）撤销乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府的不适当的决定和命令；  
（十一）保护社会主义的全民所有的财产和劳动群众集体所有的财产，保护公民私人所有的合法财产，维护社会秩序，保障公民的人身权利、民主权利和其他权利；  
（十二）保护各种经济组织的合法权益；  
（十三）铸牢中华民族共同体意识，促进各民族广泛交往交流交融，保障少数民族的合法权利和利益；  
（十四）保障宪法和法律赋予妇女的男女平等、同工同酬和婚姻自由等各项权利。  
少数民族聚居的乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会在行使职权的时候，可以依照法律规定的权限采取适合民族特点的具体措施。

**Article 12** A people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To ensure the observance and implementation of the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the resolutions of the people's congress at the next higher level and its standing committee within its administrative region;  
(2) To adopt and issue resolutions within the limits of its functions and powers;  
(3) To decide, in accordance with the state plan, the construction plans and projects for economic, cultural, and public undertakings within its administrative region;  
(4) To examine and approve the budget and the report on its implementation within its administrative region, supervise the execution of the budget at the corresponding level, examine and approve the adjusted budget plan, and examine and approve the final accounts at the corresponding level;  
(5) To decide on the implementation plan for civil affairs work within its administrative region;  
(6) To elect the chairman and vice-chairmen of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(7) To elect the township head and deputy township heads, and the town head and deputy town heads;  
(8) To hear and deliberate the work report of the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town;  
(9) To hear and deliberate the work report of the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town;  
(10) To annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the people's government of the township, ethnic township, or town;  
(11) To protect socialist property owned by the whole people and property collectively owned by the working masses, protect the lawful private property of citizens, maintain social order, and safeguard the personal rights, democratic rights, and other rights of citizens;  
(12) To protect the lawful rights and interests of various economic organizations;  
(13) To forge a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation, promote extensive exchanges, interactions, and integration among all ethnic groups, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of minority nationalities;  
(14) To guarantee the rights of women, including equality between men and women, equal pay for equal work, and freedom of marriage, as conferred by the Constitution and laws.  
When exercising their functions and powers, the people's congresses of townships, ethnic townships, and towns where minority nationalities live in concentrated communities may, within the limits of their authority as prescribed by law, adopt specific measures suited to the characteristics of the minority nationalities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民代表大会有权罢免本级人民政府的组成人员。县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会有权罢免本级人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员和由它选出的监察委员会主任、人民法院院长、人民检察院检察长。罢免人民检察院检察长，须报经上一级人民检察院检察长提请该级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。

**Article 13** A local people's congress at any level shall have the power to recall the members of the people's government at the corresponding level. A local people's congress at or above the county level shall have the power to recall the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, and the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate elected by it. The recall of the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate shall be reported to the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for submission to the standing committee of the people's congress at that level for approval.

#### 第三节　地方各级人民代表大会会议的举行

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地方各级人民代表大会会议每年至少举行一次。乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会会议一般每年举行两次。会议召开的日期由本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团决定，并予以公布。  
遇有特殊情况，县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团可以决定适当提前或者推迟召开会议。提前或者推迟召开会议的日期未能在当次会议上决定的，常务委员会或者其授权的主任会议，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团可以另行决定，并予以公布。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团认为必要，或者经过五分之一以上代表提议，可以临时召集本级人民代表大会会议。  
地方各级人民代表大会会议有三分之二以上的代表出席，始得举行。

**Article 14** A session of a local people's congress at any level shall be held at least once a year. Sessions of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town shall generally be held twice a year. The date of the session shall be decided by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town, and shall be announced.  
Under special circumstances, the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level or the presidium of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town may decide to convene the session appropriately earlier or later. If the date for convening the session earlier or later cannot be decided at the current session, the standing committee or its authorized council of chairpersons, or the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town, may make a separate decision and announce it.  
When the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level or the presidium of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town deems it necessary, or upon the proposal of more than one-fifth of the deputies, an interim session of the people's congress at the corresponding level may be convened.  
A session of a local people's congress at any level shall be held only when more than two-thirds of the deputies are present.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会会议由本级人民代表大会常务委员会召集。

**Article 15** Sessions of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall be convened by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方各级人民代表大会举行会议，应当合理安排会期和会议日程，提高议事质量和效率。

**Article 16** When holding a session, a local people's congress at any level shall reasonably arrange the duration and agenda of the session to improve the quality and efficiency of deliberations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会每次会议举行预备会议，选举本次会议的主席团和秘书长，通过本次会议的议程和其他准备事项的决定。  
预备会议由本级人民代表大会常务委员会主持。每届人民代表大会第一次会议的预备会议，由上届本级人民代表大会常务委员会主持。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，由主席团主持会议。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会会议设副秘书长若干人；副秘书长的人选由主席团决定。

**Article 17** Before each session of a local people's congress at or above the county level, a preparatory meeting shall be held to elect the presidium and the secretary-general of the session and to adopt the agenda of the session and decisions on other preparatory matters.  
The preparatory meeting shall be presided over by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level. The preparatory meeting for the first session of each people's congress shall be presided over by the standing committee of the previous people's congress at the corresponding level.  
When a session of a local people's congress at or above the county level is held, it shall be presided over by the presidium.  
A session of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall have several deputy secretaries-general, whose candidates shall be decided by the presidium.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会设主席，并可以设副主席一人至二人。主席、副主席由本级人民代表大会从代表中选出，任期同本级人民代表大会每届任期相同。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席不得担任国家行政机关的职务；如果担任国家行政机关的职务，必须向本级人民代表大会辞去主席、副主席的职务。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席在本级人民代表大会闭会期间负责联系本级人民代表大会代表，根据主席团的安排组织代表开展活动，反映代表和群众对本级人民政府工作的建议、批评和意见，并负责处理主席团的日常工作。

**Article 18** A people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town shall have a chairman, and may have one to two vice-chairmen. The chairman and vice-chairmen shall be elected by the people's congress at the corresponding level from among its deputies, and their term of office shall be the same as the term of office of the people's congress at the corresponding level.  
The chairman and vice-chairmen of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town shall not hold any post in a state administrative organ; if they hold such a post, they must resign from the posts of chairman and vice-chairmen to the people's congress at the corresponding level.  
During the period when the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town is not in session, the chairman and vice-chairmen shall be responsible for maintaining contact with the deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level, organizing the deputies to carry out activities according to the arrangements of the presidium, reflecting the suggestions, criticisms, and opinions of the deputies and the masses regarding the work of the people's government at the corresponding level, and handling the day-to-day work of the presidium.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会举行会议的时候，选举主席团。由主席团主持会议，并负责召集下一次的本级人民代表大会会议。乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席为主席团的成员。  
主席团在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，每年选择若干关系本地区群众切身利益和社会普遍关注的问题，有计划地安排代表听取和讨论本级人民政府的专项工作报告，对法律、法规实施情况进行检查，开展视察、调研等活动；听取和反映代表和群众对本级人民政府工作的建议、批评和意见。主席团在闭会期间的工作，向本级人民代表大会报告。

**Article 19** When a session of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town is held, a presidium shall be elected. The presidium shall preside over the session and be responsible for convening the next session of the people's congress at the corresponding level. The chairman and vice-chairmen of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town shall be members of the presidium.  
During the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, the presidium shall, each year, select a number of issues that concern the immediate interests of the local people and are of general public concern, and in a planned manner arrange for deputies to hear and discuss the special work reports of the people's government at the corresponding level, inspect the implementation of laws and regulations, and carry out inspection, research, and other activities; and hear and reflect the suggestions, criticisms, and opinions of the deputies and the masses regarding the work of the people's government at the corresponding level. The work of the presidium during the period when the people's congress is not in session shall be reported to the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民代表大会每届第一次会议，在本届人民代表大会代表选举完成后的两个月内，由上届本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的上次人民代表大会主席团召集。

**Article 20** The first session of each term of a local people's congress at any level shall be convened within two months after the completion of the election of the deputies to the current term of the people's congress by the standing committee of the previous term of the people's congress at the corresponding level or by the presidium of the previous session of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府组成人员和监察委员会主任、人民法院院长、人民检察院检察长，乡级的人民政府领导人员，列席本级人民代表大会会议；县级以上的其他有关机关、团体负责人，经本级人民代表大会常务委员会决定，可以列席本级人民代表大会会议。

**Article 21** The members of a local people's government at or above the county level, the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, and the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate, as well as the leading personnel of a people's government at the township level, shall attend, as non-voting participants, the session of the people's congress at the corresponding level; the responsible persons of other relevant organs and organizations at or above the county level may, subject to a decision by the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, attend, as non-voting participants, the session of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，主席团、常务委员会、各专门委员会、本级人民政府，可以向本级人民代表大会提出属于本级人民代表大会职权范围内的议案，由主席团决定提交人民代表大会会议审议，或者并交有关的专门委员会审议、提出报告，再由主席团审议决定提交大会表决。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会代表十人以上联名，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表五人以上联名，可以向本级人民代表大会提出属于本级人民代表大会职权范围内的议案，由主席团决定是否列入大会议程，或者先交有关的专门委员会审议，提出是否列入大会议程的意见，再由主席团决定是否列入大会议程。  
列入会议议程的议案，在交付大会表决前，提案人要求撤回的，经主席团同意，会议对该项议案的审议即行终止。

**Article 22** When a session of a local people's congress at any level is held, the presidium, the standing committee, the various special committees, and the people's government at the corresponding level may submit bills within the scope of the functions and powers of the people's congress at the corresponding level to the people's congress at the corresponding level. The presidium shall decide whether to refer such bills to the session of the people's congress for deliberation, or to refer them simultaneously to the relevant special committees for deliberation and the submission of reports, and then the presidium shall review the matter and decide whether to submit them to the session for voting.  
Ten or more deputies to a local people's congress at or above the county level, or five or more deputies to a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, may jointly submit bills within the scope of the functions and powers of the people's congress at the corresponding level to the people's congress at the corresponding level. The presidium shall decide whether to place such bills on the agenda of the session, or first refer them to the relevant special committees for deliberation and the submission of opinions on whether to place them on the agenda, and then the presidium shall decide whether to place them on the agenda.  
If, before a bill placed on the agenda is submitted to the session for voting, the proposer requests its withdrawal, and the presidium agrees, the deliberation on that bill shall be terminated.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在地方各级人民代表大会审议议案的时候，代表可以向有关地方国家机关提出询问，由有关机关派人说明。

**Article 23** When a local people's congress at any level is deliberating a bill, deputies may address inquiries to the relevant local state organs, and the relevant organs shall send persons to provide explanations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，代表十人以上联名可以书面提出对本级人民政府和它所属各工作部门以及监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院的质询案。质询案必须写明质询对象、质询的问题和内容。  
质询案由主席团决定交由受质询机关在主席团会议、大会全体会议或者有关的专门委员会会议上口头答复，或者由受质询机关书面答复。在主席团会议或者专门委员会会议上答复的，提质询案的代表有权列席会议，发表意见；主席团认为必要的时候，可以将答复质询案的情况报告印发会议。  
质询案以口头答复的，应当由受质询机关的负责人到会答复；质询案以书面答复的，应当由受质询机关的负责人签署，由主席团印发会议或者印发提质询案的代表。

**Article 24** When a session of a local people's congress at any level is held, ten or more deputies may jointly submit, in writing, a question to the people's government at the corresponding level and its working departments, as well as to the supervisory commission, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate. The question must specify the organ to which it is addressed, the issue questioned, and the content.  
The presidium shall decide to refer the question to the organ questioned for an oral reply at a meeting of the presidium, a plenary meeting of the session, or a meeting of the relevant special committee, or for a written reply by the organ questioned. If the reply is made at a meeting of the presidium or a special committee, the deputies who submitted the question shall have the right to attend the meeting as non-voting participants and express their opinions; if the presidium deems it necessary, it may have the report on the reply to the question printed and distributed at the session.  
If the question is to be replied to orally, the responsible person of the organ questioned shall attend the meeting to give the reply; if the question is to be replied to in writing, the reply shall be signed by the responsible person of the organ questioned and printed and distributed by the presidium to the session or to the deputies who submitted the question.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　地方各级人民代表大会进行选举和通过决议，以全体代表的过半数通过。

**Article 25** Elections and the adoption of resolutions by a local people's congress at any level shall be carried out by a majority vote of all the deputies.

#### 第四节　地方国家机关组成人员的选举、罢免和辞职

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会的组成人员，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席，省长、副省长，自治区主席、副主席，市长、副市长，州长、副州长，县长、副县长，区长、副区长，乡长、副乡长，镇长、副镇长，监察委员会主任，人民法院院长，人民检察院检察长的人选，由本级人民代表大会主席团或者代表依照本法规定联合提名。  
省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会代表三十人以上书面联名，设区的市和自治州的人民代表大会代表二十人以上书面联名，县级的人民代表大会代表十人以上书面联名，可以提出本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员，人民政府领导人员，监察委员会主任，人民法院院长，人民检察院检察长的候选人。乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表十人以上书面联名，可以提出本级人民代表大会主席、副主席，人民政府领导人员的候选人。不同选区或者选举单位选出的代表可以酝酿、联合提出候选人。  
主席团提名的候选人人数，每一代表与其他代表联合提名的候选人人数，均不得超过应选名额。  
提名人应当如实介绍所提名的候选人的情况。

**Article 26** The candidates for the members of the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level, the chairman and vice-chairmen of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, the governor and deputy governors of a province, the chairman and vice-chairmen of an autonomous region, the mayor and deputy mayors of a city, the prefect and deputy prefects of an autonomous prefecture, the county head and deputy county heads, the district head and deputy district heads, the township head and deputy township heads, the town head and deputy town heads, the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, and the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate shall be nominated jointly by the presidium of the people's congress at the corresponding level or by the deputies in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
Thirty or more deputies to a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, twenty or more deputies to a people's congress of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture, or ten or more deputies to a people's congress at the county level may, by joint nomination in writing, propose candidates for the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, the leading personnel of the people's government, the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, and the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate. Ten or more deputies to a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town may, by joint nomination in writing, propose candidates for the chairman and vice-chairmen of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the leading personnel of the people's government. Deputies elected from different electoral districts or electoral units may deliberate and jointly propose candidates.  
The number of candidates nominated by the presidium and the number of candidates jointly nominated by each deputy together with other deputies shall not exceed the number of persons to be elected.  
The nominator shall truthfully introduce the circumstances of the candidates nominated.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民代表大会常务委员会主任、秘书长，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席，人民政府正职领导人员，监察委员会主任，人民法院院长，人民检察院检察长的候选人数可以多一人，进行差额选举；如果提名的候选人只有一人，也可以等额选举。人民代表大会常务委员会副主任，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会副主席，人民政府副职领导人员的候选人数应比应选人数多一人至三人，人民代表大会常务委员会委员的候选人数应比应选人数多十分之一至五分之一，由本级人民代表大会根据应选人数在选举办法中规定具体差额数，进行差额选举。如果提名的候选人数符合选举办法规定的差额数，由主席团提交代表酝酿、讨论后，进行选举。如果提名的候选人数超过选举办法规定的差额数，由主席团提交代表酝酿、讨论后，进行预选，根据在预选中得票多少的顺序，按照选举办法规定的差额数，确定正式候选人名单，进行选举。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会换届选举本级国家机关领导人员时，提名、酝酿候选人的时间不得少于两天。

**Article 27** For the election of the chairman and secretary-general of the standing committee of a people's congress, the chairman of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, the principal leading personnel of a people's government, the director of a supervisory commission, the president of a people's court, and the chief procurator of a people's procuratorate, the number of candidates may be one more than the number of persons to be elected, and a contested election shall be held; if only one candidate is nominated, an uncontested election may also be held. For the election of the vice-chairmen of the standing committee of a people's congress, the vice-chairmen of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, and the deputy leading personnel of a people's government, the number of candidates shall be one to three more than the number of persons to be elected; for the election of the members of the standing committee of a people's congress, the number of candidates shall be one-tenth to one-fifth more than the number of persons to be elected. The specific number of candidates for a contested election shall be prescribed by the people's congress at the corresponding level in its election method based on the number of persons to be elected, and a contested election shall be held. If the number of candidates nominated conforms to the number for a contested election prescribed in the election method, the presidium shall submit the candidates to the deputies for deliberation and discussion, and then an election shall be held. If the number of candidates nominated exceeds the number for a contested election prescribed in the election method, the presidium shall submit the candidates to the deputies for deliberation and discussion, and then a primary election shall be held. The list of formal candidates shall be determined according to the order of the number of votes obtained in the primary election, following the number for a contested election prescribed in the election method, and an election shall be held.  
When a local people's congress at or above the county level holds an election for changing the term of office of the leading personnel of state organs at the corresponding level, the time for nomination and deliberation of candidates shall not be less than two days.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　选举采用无记名投票方式。代表对于确定的候选人，可以投赞成票，可以投反对票，可以另选其他任何代表或者选民，也可以弃权。

**Article 28** Elections shall be conducted by secret ballot. A deputy may vote for or against a determined candidate, may elect any other deputy or voter, or may abstain from voting.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级人民代表大会选举本级国家机关领导人员，获得过半数选票的候选人人数超过应选名额时，以得票多的当选。如遇票数相等不能确定当选人时，应当就票数相等的人再次投票，以得票多的当选。  
获得过半数选票的当选人数少于应选名额时，不足的名额另行选举。另行选举时，可以根据在第一次投票时得票多少的顺序确定候选人，也可以依照本法规定的程序另行提名、确定候选人。经本级人民代表大会决定，不足的名额的另行选举可以在本次人民代表大会会议上进行，也可以在下一次人民代表大会会议上进行。  
另行选举人民代表大会常务委员会副主任、委员，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会副主席，人民政府副职领导人员时，依照[本法第二十七条](#art-27)第一款的规定，确定差额数，进行差额选举。

**Article 29** When a local people's congress at any level elects the leading personnel of state organs at the corresponding level, if the number of candidates who have obtained a majority vote exceeds the number of persons to be elected, those who have obtained more votes shall be elected. If the votes are tied and it is impossible to determine the persons elected, a second ballot shall be held for those with tied votes, and those who obtain more votes shall be elected.  
If the number of persons elected who have obtained a majority vote is less than the number of persons to be elected, a by-election shall be held for the remaining vacancies. In the by-election, candidates may be determined in the order of the number of votes obtained in the first ballot, or candidates may be separately nominated and determined in accordance with the procedures prescribed in this Law. Subject to a decision by the people's congress at the corresponding level, the by-election for the remaining vacancies may be held at the current session of the people's congress or at the next session of the people's congress.  
When a by-election is held for the vice-chairmen and members of the standing committee of a people's congress, the vice-chairmen of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, or the deputy leading personnel of a people's government, the number of candidates for a contested election shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 27 of this Law, and a contested election shall be held.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　地方各级人民代表大会补选常务委员会主任、副主任、秘书长、委员，乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席，省长、副省长，自治区主席、副主席，市长、副市长，州长、副州长，县长、副县长，区长、副区长，乡长、副乡长，镇长、副镇长，监察委员会主任，人民法院院长，人民检察院检察长时，候选人数可以多于应选人数，也可以同应选人数相等。选举办法由本级人民代表大会决定。

**Article 30** When a local people's congress at any level holds a by-election for the chairman, vice-chairmen, secretary-general, or members of the standing committee, the chairman or vice-chairmen of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, the governor or deputy governors of a province, the chairman or vice-chairmen of an autonomous region, the mayor or deputy mayors of a city, the prefect or deputy prefects of an autonomous prefecture, the county head or deputy county heads, the district head or deputy district heads, the township head or deputy township heads, the town head or deputy town heads, the director of a supervisory commission, the president of a people's court, or the chief procurator of a people's procuratorate, the number of candidates may be greater than or equal to the number of persons to be elected. The election method shall be decided by the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会举行会议的时候，主席团、常务委员会或者十分之一以上代表联名，可以提出对本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员、人民政府组成人员、监察委员会主任、人民法院院长、人民检察院检察长的罢免案，由主席团提请大会审议。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会举行会议的时候，主席团或者五分之一以上代表联名，可以提出对人民代表大会主席、副主席，乡长、副乡长，镇长、副镇长的罢免案，由主席团提请大会审议。  
罢免案应当写明罢免理由。  
被提出罢免的人员有权在主席团会议或者大会全体会议上提出申辩意见，或者书面提出申辩意见。在主席团会议上提出的申辩意见或者书面提出的申辩意见，由主席团印发会议。  
向县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会提出的罢免案，由主席团交会议审议后，提请全体会议表决；或者由主席团提议，经全体会议决定，组织调查委员会，由本级人民代表大会下次会议根据调查委员会的报告审议决定。

**Article 31** When a session of a local people's congress at or above the county level is held, the presidium, the standing committee, or one-tenth or more of the deputies jointly may submit a proposal for the recall of a member of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, a member of the people's government, the director of the supervisory commission, the president of the people's court, or the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate. The presidium shall submit such proposal to the session for deliberation.  
When a session of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town is held, the presidium or one-fifth or more of the deputies jointly may submit a proposal for the recall of the chairman or vice-chairmen of the people's congress, the township head or deputy township heads, or the town head or deputy town heads. The presidium shall submit such proposal to the session for deliberation.  
The proposal for recall shall state the reasons for the recall.  
The person proposed for recall shall have the right to present a defense at a meeting of the presidium or at a plenary meeting of the session, or to present a defense in writing. A defense presented at a meeting of the presidium or in writing shall be printed and distributed by the presidium at the session.  
A proposal for recall submitted to a local people's congress at or above the county level shall, after being referred by the presidium to the session for deliberation, be submitted to the plenary meeting for voting; or, upon a proposal by the presidium and a decision by the plenary meeting, an investigation committee may be organized, and the next session of the people's congress at the corresponding level shall deliberate and decide based on the report of the investigation committee.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员、专门委员会组成人员和人民政府领导人员，监察委员会主任，人民法院院长，人民检察院检察长，可以向本级人民代表大会提出辞职，由大会决定是否接受辞职；大会闭会期间，可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出辞职，由常务委员会决定是否接受辞职。常务委员会决定接受辞职后，报本级人民代表大会备案。人民检察院检察长的辞职，须报经上一级人民检察院检察长提请该级人民代表大会常务委员会批准。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席、副主席，乡长、副乡长，镇长、副镇长，可以向本级人民代表大会提出辞职，由大会决定是否接受辞职。

**Article 32** Members of the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level, members of special committees, leading personnel of a people's government, the director of a supervisory commission, the president of a people's court, and the chief procurator of a people's procuratorate may tender their resignations to the people's congress at the corresponding level, and the congress shall decide whether to accept the resignations; during the period when the congress is not in session, they may tender their resignations to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, and the standing committee shall decide whether to accept the resignations. After the standing committee decides to accept a resignation, it shall report the matter to the people's congress at the corresponding level for recordation. The resignation of the chief procurator of a people's procuratorate shall be reported to the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate at the next higher level for submission to the standing committee of the people's congress at that level for approval.  
The chairman and vice-chairmen of a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town, and the township head and deputy township heads, or the town head and deputy town heads, may tender their resignations to the people's congress at the corresponding level, and the congress shall decide whether to accept the resignations.

#### 第五节　地方各级人民代表大会各委员会

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会根据需要，可以设法制委员会、财政经济委员会、教育科学文化卫生委员会、环境与资源保护委员会、社会建设委员会和其他需要设立的专门委员会；县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民代表大会根据需要，可以设法制委员会、财政经济委员会等专门委员会。  
各专门委员会受本级人民代表大会领导；在大会闭会期间，受本级人民代表大会常务委员会领导。

**Article 33** A people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts may, as needed, establish a law committee, a financial and economic committee, an education, science, culture, and public health committee, an environment and resources protection committee, a social development committee, and other special committees as required; a people's congress of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or district under a city may, as needed, establish a law committee, a financial and economic committee, and other special committees.  
Each special committee shall be under the leadership of the people's congress at the corresponding level; during the period when the congress is not in session, it shall be under the leadership of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各专门委员会的主任委员、副主任委员和委员的人选，由主席团在代表中提名，大会通过。在大会闭会期间，常务委员会可以任免专门委员会的个别副主任委员和部分委员，由主任会议提名，常务委员会会议通过。  
各专门委员会每届任期同本级人民代表大会每届任期相同，履行职责到下届人民代表大会产生新的专门委员会为止。

**Article 34** The candidates for the chairmen, vice-chairmen, and members of each special committee shall be nominated by the presidium from among the deputies and approved by the congress. During the period when the congress is not in session, the standing committee may appoint or remove individual vice-chairmen and some members of the special committees, upon nomination by the council of chairpersons and approval by the standing committee meeting.  
The term of office of each special committee shall be the same as the term of office of the people's congress at the corresponding level, and it shall perform its functions until the next term of the people's congress elects a new special committee.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各专门委员会在本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会领导下，开展下列工作：  
（一）审议本级人民代表大会主席团或者常务委员会交付的议案；  
（二）向本级人民代表大会主席团或者常务委员会提出属于本级人民代表大会或者常务委员会职权范围内同本委员会有关的议案，组织起草有关议案草案；  
（三）承担本级人民代表大会常务委员会听取和审议专项工作报告、执法检查、专题询问等的具体组织实施工作；  
（四）按照本级人民代表大会常务委员会工作安排，听取本级人民政府工作部门和监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院的专题汇报，提出建议；  
（五）对属于本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会职权范围内同本委员会有关的问题，进行调查研究，提出建议；  
（六）研究办理代表建议、批评和意见，负责有关建议、批评和意见的督促办理工作；  
（七）办理本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会交办的其他工作。

**Article 35** Each special committee shall, under the leadership of the people's congress at the corresponding level and its standing committee, carry out the following work:  
(1) To deliberate bills referred to it by the presidium of the people's congress or the standing committee at the corresponding level;  
(2) To submit to the presidium of the people's congress or the standing committee at the corresponding level bills that are within the scope of the functions and powers of the people's congress or its standing committee and that relate to the special committee, and to organize the drafting of the drafts of such bills;  
(3) To undertake the specific organization and implementation of the work of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level in hearing and deliberating special work reports, conducting law enforcement inspections, and holding special inquiries;  
(4) To, in accordance with the work arrangements of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level, hear special reports from the working departments of the people's government, the supervisory commission, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate at the corresponding level, and to offer suggestions;  
(5) To investigate and research issues within the scope of the functions and powers of the people's congress and its standing committee at the corresponding level that relate to the special committee, and to offer suggestions;  
(6) To study and handle the suggestions, criticisms, and opinions of deputies, and to be responsible for supervising the handling of such suggestions, criticisms, and opinions;  
(7) To handle other work assigned by the people's congress and its standing committee at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会可以组织关于特定问题的调查委员会。  
主席团或者十分之一以上代表书面联名，可以向本级人民代表大会提议组织关于特定问题的调查委员会，由主席团提请全体会议决定。  
调查委员会由主任委员、副主任委员和委员组成，由主席团在代表中提名，提请全体会议通过。  
调查委员会应当向本级人民代表大会提出调查报告。人民代表大会根据调查委员会的报告，可以作出相应的决议。人民代表大会可以授权它的常务委员会听取调查委员会的调查报告，常务委员会可以作出相应的决议，报人民代表大会下次会议备案。

**Article 36** A local people's congress at or above the county level may organize an investigation committee on a specific issue.  
The presidium or one-tenth or more of the deputies jointly, by written proposal, may propose to the people's congress at the corresponding level the organization of an investigation committee on a specific issue, and the presidium shall submit the matter to the plenary meeting for decision.  
An investigation committee shall be composed of a chairman, vice-chairmen, and members, who shall be nominated by the presidium from among the deputies and submitted to the plenary meeting for approval.  
The investigation committee shall submit an investigation report to the people's congress at the corresponding level. The people's congress may, based on the report of the investigation committee, adopt appropriate resolutions. The people's congress may authorize its standing committee to hear the investigation report of the investigation committee, and the standing committee may adopt appropriate resolutions, which shall be reported to the next session of the people's congress for recordation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　乡、民族乡、镇的每届人民代表大会第一次会议通过的代表资格审查委员会，行使职权至本届人民代表大会任期届满为止。

**Article 37** The credentials committee adopted at the first session of each term of the people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town shall exercise its functions and powers until the expiry of the term of office of the current people's congress.

#### 第六节　地方各级人民代表大会代表

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地方各级人民代表大会代表任期，从每届本级人民代表大会举行第一次会议开始，到下届本级人民代表大会举行第一次会议为止。

**Article 38** The term of office of a deputy to a local people's congress at any level shall begin from the first session of the current term of the people's congress at the corresponding level and end at the first session of the next term of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　地方各级人民代表大会代表、常务委员会组成人员，在人民代表大会和常务委员会会议上的发言和表决，不受法律追究。

**Article 39** The speeches and votes of deputies to a local people's congress at any level and members of its standing committee at meetings of the people's congress and the standing committee shall not be subject to legal liability.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会代表，非经本级人民代表大会主席团许可，在大会闭会期间，非经本级人民代表大会常务委员会许可，不受逮捕或者刑事审判。如果因为是现行犯被拘留，执行拘留的公安机关应当立即向该级人民代表大会主席团或者常务委员会报告。

**Article 40** No deputy to a local people's congress at or above the county level shall be arrested or subjected to criminal trial without the permission of the presidium of the people's congress at the corresponding level during the session, or without the permission of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level during the period when the congress is not in session. If a deputy is detained because he or she is caught in flagrante delicto, the public security organ executing the detention shall immediately report the matter to the presidium or the standing committee of the people's congress at that level.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　地方各级人民代表大会代表在出席人民代表大会会议和执行代表职务的时候，国家根据需要给予往返的旅费和必要的物质上的便利或者补贴。

**Article 41** When deputies to a local people's congress at any level attend sessions of the people's congress and perform their duties as deputies, the State shall, as necessary, provide them with travel expenses for round trips and necessary material facilities or subsidies.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会代表向本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会提出的对各方面工作的建议、批评和意见，由本级人民代表大会常务委员会的办事机构交有关机关和组织研究办理并负责答复。  
乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表向本级人民代表大会提出的对各方面工作的建议、批评和意见，由本级人民代表大会主席团交有关机关和组织研究办理并负责答复。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表的建议、批评和意见的办理情况，由县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会办事机构或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会主席团向本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会报告，并予以公开。

**Article 42** The suggestions, criticisms, and opinions on various aspects of work submitted by deputies to a local people's congress at or above the county level to the people's congress and its standing committee at the corresponding level shall be referred by the working office of the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level to the relevant organs and organizations for study and handling, and the responsible organs or organizations shall provide replies.  
The suggestions, criticisms, and opinions on various aspects of work submitted by deputies to a people's congress of a township, ethnic township, or town to the people's congress at the corresponding level shall be referred by the presidium of the people's congress at the corresponding level to the relevant organs and organizations for study and handling, and the responsible organs or organizations shall provide replies.  
The handling situation of the suggestions, criticisms, and opinions of deputies to a local people's congress at any level shall be reported by the working office of the standing committee of the local people's congress at or above the county level or the presidium of the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town to the standing committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level or the people's congress of the township, ethnic township, or town, and shall be made public.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　地方各级人民代表大会代表应当与原选区选民或者原选举单位和人民群众保持密切联系，听取和反映他们的意见和要求，充分发挥在发展全过程人民民主中的作用。  
省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会代表可以列席原选举单位的人民代表大会会议。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表分工联系选民，有代表三人以上的居民地区或者生产单位可以组织代表小组。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表应当向原选区选民或者原选举单位报告履职情况。

**Article 43** Deputies to a local people's congress at any level shall maintain close ties with the voters of their electoral districts or the electoral units that elected them and with the masses, listen to and reflect their opinions and demands, and give full play to their role in developing whole-process people's democracy.  
Deputies to a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts may attend, as non-voting participants, the sessions of the people's congress of the electoral unit that elected them.  
Deputies to a people's congress of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, district under a city, township, ethnic township, or town shall maintain contact with voters on a division of responsibility basis, and in residential areas or production units where there are three or more deputies, a deputy group may be organized.  
Deputies to a local people's congress at any level shall report to the voters of their electoral districts or the electoral units that elected them on their performance of duties.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会代表受原选举单位的监督；县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇的人民代表大会代表受选民的监督。  
地方各级人民代表大会代表的选举单位和选民有权随时罢免自己选出的代表。代表的罢免必须由原选举单位以全体代表的过半数通过，或者由原选区以选民的过半数通过。

**Article 44** Deputies to a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall be subject to supervision by the electoral units that elected them; deputies to a people's congress of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, district under a city, township, ethnic township, or town shall be subject to supervision by the voters.  
The electoral units that elect deputies to a local people's congress at any level and the voters shall have the power to recall the deputies they have elected at any time. The recall of a deputy must be adopted by a majority vote of all the deputies of the original electoral unit or by a majority vote of the voters of the original electoral district.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　地方各级人民代表大会代表因故不能担任代表职务的时候，由原选举单位或者由原选区选民补选。

**Article 45** When a deputy to a local people's congress at any level is unable to continue to serve as a deputy for some reason, a by-election shall be held by the original electoral unit or by the voters of the original electoral district.

### 第三章　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会

#### 第一节　常务委员会的组成和任期

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的人民代表大会设立常务委员会，对本级人民代表大会负责并报告工作。

**Article 46** A standing committee shall be established for the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, county, autonomous county, city, or district under a city. The standing committee shall be responsible to the people's congress at the corresponding level and shall report its work to it.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会常务委员会由本级人民代表大会在代表中选举主任、副主任若干人、秘书长、委员若干人组成。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民代表大会常务委员会由本级人民代表大会在代表中选举主任、副主任若干人和委员若干人组成。  
常务委员会的组成人员不得担任国家行政机关、监察机关、审判机关和检察机关的职务；如果担任上述职务，必须向常务委员会辞去常务委员会的职务。  
常务委员会组成人员的名额：  
（一）省、自治区、直辖市四十五人至七十五人，人口超过八千万的省不超过九十五人；  
（二）设区的市、自治州二十九人至五十一人，人口超过八百万的设区的市不超过六十一人；  
（三）县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区十五人至三十五人，人口超过一百万的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区不超过四十五人。  
省、自治区、直辖市每届人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员的名额，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会依照前款规定，按人口多少并结合常务委员会组成人员结构的需要确定。自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区每届人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员的名额，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会依照前款规定，按人口多少并结合常务委员会组成人员结构的需要确定。每届人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员的名额经确定后，在本届人民代表大会的任期内不再变动。

**Article 47** The standing committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall be composed of a chairman, several vice-chairmen, a secretary-general, and several members, elected by the people's congress at the corresponding level from among its deputies.  
The standing committee of a people's congress of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or district under a city shall be composed of a chairman, several vice-chairmen, and several members, elected by the people's congress at the corresponding level from among its deputies.  
Members of the standing committee shall not hold any post in a state administrative organ, supervisory organ, adjudicatory organ, or procuratorial organ; if they hold such a post, they must resign from the post on the standing committee.  
The number of members of the standing committee shall be:  
(1) For a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, forty-five to seventy-five persons, provided that for a province with a population of over 80 million, the number shall not exceed ninety-five persons;  
(2) For a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture, twenty-nine to fifty-one persons, provided that for a city divided into districts with a population of over 8 million, the number shall not exceed sixty-one persons;  
(3) For a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or district under a city, fifteen to thirty-five persons, provided that for a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or district under a city with a population of over 1 million, the number shall not exceed forty-five persons.  
The number of members of the standing committee of each term of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be determined by the people's congress of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the preceding paragraph, based on the size of the population and the needs of the composition of the standing committee. The number of members of the standing committee of each term of a people's congress of an autonomous prefecture, county, autonomous county, city, or district under a city shall be determined by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the preceding paragraph, based on the size of the population and the needs of the composition of the standing committee. Once the number of members of the standing committee of each term is determined, it shall not be altered during the term of office of the current people's congress.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会每届任期同本级人民代表大会每届任期相同，它行使职权到下届本级人民代表大会选出新的常务委员会为止。

**Article 48** The term of office of the standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall be the same as the term of office of the people's congress at the corresponding level, and it shall exercise its functions and powers until a new standing committee is elected by the next term of the people's congress at the corresponding level.

#### 第二节　常务委员会的职权

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，根据本行政区域的具体情况和实际需要，在不同宪法、法律、行政法规相抵触的前提下，可以制定和颁布地方性法规，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案。  
设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会常务委员会在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，根据本行政区域的具体情况和实际需要，在不同宪法、法律、行政法规和本省、自治区的地方性法规相抵触的前提下，可以依照法律规定的权限制定地方性法规，报省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会批准后施行，并由省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会报全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市以及设区的市、自治州的人民代表大会常务委员会根据区域协调发展的需要，可以开展协同立法。

**Article 49** The standing committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, during the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, in light of the specific conditions and actual needs of its administrative region and on condition that it does not contravene the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations, formulate and promulgate local regulations, and shall report them to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for recordation.  
The standing committee of a people's congress of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture may, during the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, in light of the specific conditions and actual needs of its administrative region and on condition that it does not contravene the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the local regulations of the relevant province or autonomous region, and within the limits of its authority as prescribed by law, formulate local regulations, which shall come into force after approval by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province or autonomous region, and shall be reported by the standing committee of the people's congress of the province or autonomous region to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for recordation.  
The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as cities divided into districts and autonomous prefectures may, in light of the needs for coordinated regional development, carry out coordinated legislation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会行使下列职权：  
（一）在本行政区域内，保证宪法、法律、行政法规和上级人民代表大会及其常务委员会决议的遵守和执行；  
（二）领导或者主持本级人民代表大会代表的选举；  
（三）召集本级人民代表大会会议；  
（四）讨论、决定本行政区域内的政治、经济、教育、科学、文化、卫生、生态环境保护、自然资源、城乡建设、民政、社会保障、民族等工作的重大事项和项目；  
（五）根据本级人民政府的建议，审查和批准本行政区域内的国民经济和社会发展规划纲要、计划和本级预算的调整方案；  
（六）监督本行政区域内的国民经济和社会发展规划纲要、计划和预算的执行，审查和批准本级决算，监督审计查出问题整改情况，审查监督政府债务；  
（七）监督本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院和人民检察院的工作，听取和审议有关专项工作报告，组织执法检查，开展专题询问等；联系本级人民代表大会代表，受理人民群众对上述机关和国家工作人员的申诉和意见；  
（八）监督本级人民政府对国有资产的管理，听取和审议本级人民政府关于国有资产管理情况的报告；  
（九）听取和审议本级人民政府关于年度环境状况和环境保护目标完成情况的报告；  
（十）听取和审议备案审查工作情况报告；  
（十一）撤销下一级人民代表大会及其常务委员会的不适当的决议；  
（十二）撤销本级人民政府的不适当的决定和命令；  
（十三）在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，决定副省长、自治区副主席、副市长、副州长、副县长、副区长的个别任免；在省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长和监察委员会主任、人民法院院长、人民检察院检察长因故不能担任职务的时候，根据主任会议的提名，从本级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院副职领导人员中决定代理的人选；决定代理检察长，须报上一级人民检察院和人民代表大会常务委员会备案；  
（十四）根据省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长的提名，决定本级人民政府秘书长、厅长、局长、委员会主任、科长的任免，报上一级人民政府备案；  
（十五）根据监察委员会主任的提名，任免监察委员会副主任、委员；  
（十六）按照人民法院组织法和人民检察院组织法的规定，任免人民法院副院长、庭长、副庭长、审判委员会委员、审判员，任免人民检察院副检察长、检察委员会委员、检察员，批准任免下一级人民检察院检察长；省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会根据主任会议的提名，决定在省、自治区内按地区设立的和在直辖市内设立的中级人民法院院长的任免，根据省、自治区、直辖市的人民检察院检察长的提名，决定人民检察院分院检察长的任免；  
（十七）在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，决定撤销个别副省长、自治区副主席、副市长、副州长、副县长、副区长的职务；决定撤销由它任命的本级人民政府其他组成人员和监察委员会副主任、委员，人民法院副院长、庭长、副庭长、审判委员会委员、审判员，人民检察院副检察长、检察委员会委员、检察员，中级人民法院院长，人民检察院分院检察长的职务；  
（十八）在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，补选上一级人民代表大会出缺的代表和罢免个别代表。  
常务委员会讨论前款第四项规定的本行政区域内的重大事项和项目，可以作出决定或者决议，也可以将有关意见、建议送有关地方国家机关或者单位研究办理。有关办理情况应当及时向常务委员会报告。

**Article 50** A standing committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To ensure the observance and implementation of the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and the resolutions of the people's congress at the next higher level and its standing committee within its administrative region;  
(2) To direct or preside over the election of deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(3) To convene sessions of the people's congress at the corresponding level;  
(4) To discuss and decide on major matters and projects concerning political, economic, educational, scientific, cultural, public health, ecological and environmental protection, natural resources, urban and rural development, civil affairs, social security, and ethnic affairs within its administrative region;  
(5) To examine and approve, based on the proposals of the people's government at the corresponding level, the adjustment plans for the outline of the national economic and social development plan, the plan, and the budget at the corresponding level within its administrative region;  
(6) To supervise the implementation of the outline of the national economic and social development plan, the plan, and the budget within its administrative region, to examine and approve the final accounts at the corresponding level, to supervise the rectification of problems identified through auditing, and to examine and supervise government debt;  
(7) To supervise the work of the people's government, the supervisory commission, the people's court, and the people's procuratorate at the corresponding level, to hear and deliberate relevant special work reports, to organize law enforcement inspections, and to conduct special inquiries, among other things; to maintain contact with deputies to the people's congress at the corresponding level, and to receive petitions and opinions from the masses concerning the aforementioned organs and state functionaries;  
(8) To supervise the management of state-owned assets by the people's government at the corresponding level, and to hear and deliberate the report of the people's government at the corresponding level on the management of state-owned assets;  
(9) To hear and deliberate the report of the people's government at the corresponding level on the state of the environment and the completion of environmental protection targets for the year;  
(10) To hear and deliberate the work report on the recordation and review of normative documents;  
(11) To annul inappropriate resolutions of the people's congress at the next lower level and its standing committee;  
(12) To annul inappropriate decisions and orders of the people's government at the corresponding level;  
(13) During the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, to decide on the individual appointment or removal of deputy governors of a province, vice-chairmen of an autonomous region, deputy mayors of a city, deputy prefects of an autonomous prefecture, deputy county heads, or deputy district heads; when the governor of a province, chairman of an autonomous region, mayor of a city, prefect of an autonomous prefecture, county head, district head, and the director of the supervisory commission, president of the people's court, or chief procurator of the people's procuratorate are unable to perform their duties for some reason, to decide, based on the nomination of the council of chairpersons, on acting candidates from among the deputy leading personnel of the people's government, supervisory commission, people's court, or people's procuratorate at the corresponding level; the decision on an acting chief procurator shall be reported to the people's procuratorate at the next higher level and the standing committee of the people's congress at that level for recordation;  
(14) To decide, based on the nomination of the governor of a province, chairman of an autonomous region, mayor of a city, prefect of an autonomous prefecture, county head, or district head, on the appointment or removal of the secretary-general, department directors, bureau directors, committee chairpersons, and section chiefs of the people's government at the corresponding level, and to report the same to the people's government at the next higher level for recordation;  
(15) To appoint or remove, based on the nomination of the director of the supervisory commission, the deputy directors and members of the supervisory commission;  
(16) In accordance with the provisions of the Organic Law of the People's Courts and the Organic Law of the People's Procuratorates, to appoint or remove the vice-presidents, presiding judges, deputy presiding judges, members of the judicial committee, and judges of the people's court, to appoint or remove the deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators of the people's procuratorate, and to approve the appointment or removal of the chief procurator of a people's procuratorate at the next lower level; the standing committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the nomination of the council of chairpersons, decide on the appointment or removal of the presidents of the intermediate people's courts established within the administrative region of a province or autonomous region by area, or within a municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall, based on the nomination of the chief procurator of the people's procuratorate of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, decide on the appointment or removal of the chief procurators of the branch procuratorates of the people's procuratorate;  
(17) During the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, to decide on the removal from office of individual deputy governors of a province, vice-chairmen of an autonomous region, deputy mayors of a city, deputy prefects of an autonomous prefecture, deputy county heads, or deputy district heads; to decide on the removal from office of other members of the people's government at the corresponding level appointed by it, as well as deputy directors and members of the supervisory commission, vice-presidents, presiding judges, deputy presiding judges, members of the judicial committee, and judges of the people's court, deputy chief procurators, members of the procuratorial committee, and procurators of the people's procuratorate, presidents of intermediate people's courts, and chief procurators of branch procuratorates of the people's procuratorate;  
(18) During the period when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, to hold by-elections for vacant positions of deputies to the people's congress at the next higher level and to recall individual deputies.  
When the standing committee discusses the major matters and projects within its administrative region as specified in item (4) of the preceding paragraph, it may make decisions or resolutions, or may refer relevant opinions and suggestions to the relevant local state organs or units for study and handling. The situation regarding such handling shall be promptly reported to the standing committee.

#### 第三节　常务委员会会议的举行

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　常务委员会会议由主任召集并主持，每两个月至少举行一次。遇有特殊需要时，可以临时召集常务委员会会议。主任可以委托副主任主持会议。  
县级以上的地方各级人民政府、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院的负责人，列席本级人民代表大会常务委员会会议。  
常务委员会会议有常务委员会全体组成人员过半数出席，始得举行。  
常务委员会的决议，由常务委员会以全体组成人员的过半数通过。

**Article 51** Meetings of the Standing Committee shall be convened and presided over by the chairperson, and shall be held at least once every two months. When there is a special need, a meeting of the Standing Committee may be convened on an ad hoc basis. The chairperson may entrust a vice-chairperson to preside over a meeting.  
Heads of the local people's government, supervisory commission, people's court, and people's procuratorate at or above the county level shall attend the meetings of the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level as non-voting participants.  
A meeting of the Standing Committee shall be held only when more than half of the total membership of the Standing Committee are present.  
A resolution of the Standing Committee shall be adopted by a majority vote of all the members of the Standing Committee.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会主任会议可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出属于常务委员会职权范围内的议案，由常务委员会会议审议。  
县级以上的地方各级人民政府、人民代表大会各专门委员会，可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出属于常务委员会职权范围内的议案，由主任会议决定提请常务委员会会议审议，或者先交有关的专门委员会审议、提出报告，再提请常务委员会会议审议。  
省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员五人以上联名，县级的人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员三人以上联名，可以向本级常务委员会提出属于常务委员会职权范围内的议案，由主任会议决定是否提请常务委员会会议审议，或者先交有关的专门委员会审议、提出报告，再决定是否提请常务委员会会议审议。

**Article 52** The council of chairpersons of the Standing Committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level may submit to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level proposals that fall within the scope of functions and powers of the Standing Committee, and the proposals shall be deliberated at a meeting of the Standing Committee.  
The local people's government at or above the county level and the special committees of the people's congress may submit to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level proposals that fall within the scope of functions and powers of the Standing Committee. The council of chairpersons shall decide whether to place such proposals on the agenda of a meeting of the Standing Committee for deliberation, or to refer them first to the relevant special committees for deliberation and submission of reports, and then place them on the agenda of a meeting of the Standing Committee for deliberation.  
Where five or more members of the Standing Committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts, or three or more members of the Standing Committee of a people's congress at the county level, jointly submit a proposal that falls within the scope of functions and powers of the Standing Committee to the Standing Committee at the corresponding level, the council of chairpersons shall decide whether to place such proposal on the agenda of a meeting of the Standing Committee for deliberation, or to refer it first to the relevant special committees for deliberation and submission of reports, and then decide whether to place it on the agenda of a meeting of the Standing Committee for deliberation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　在常务委员会会议期间，省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员五人以上联名，县级的人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员三人以上联名，可以向常务委员会书面提出对本级人民政府及其工作部门、监察委员会、人民法院、人民检察院的质询案。质询案必须写明质询对象、质询的问题和内容。  
质询案由主任会议决定交由受质询机关在常务委员会全体会议上或者有关的专门委员会会议上口头答复，或者由受质询机关书面答复。在专门委员会会议上答复的，提质询案的常务委员会组成人员有权列席会议，发表意见；主任会议认为必要的时候，可以将答复质询案的情况报告印发会议。  
质询案以口头答复的，应当由受质询机关的负责人到会答复；质询案以书面答复的，应当由受质询机关的负责人签署，由主任会议印发会议或者印发提质询案的常务委员会组成人员。

**Article 53** During a meeting of the Standing Committee, where five or more members of the Standing Committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts, or three or more members of the Standing Committee of a people's congress at the county level, jointly submit in writing a proposal for an inquiry to the Standing Committee concerning the people's government at the corresponding level and its working departments, the supervisory commission, the people's court, or the people's procuratorate, the proposal shall clearly state the subject of the inquiry, the issues inquired, and the content.  
The council of chairpersons shall decide to refer the proposal for an inquiry to the organ inquired for an oral reply at a plenary meeting of the Standing Committee or at a meeting of the relevant special committee, or for a written reply by the organ inquired. Where the reply is made at a meeting of a special committee, the members of the Standing Committee who proposed the inquiry shall have the right to attend the meeting as non-voting participants and express their opinions. When the council of chairpersons considers it necessary, a report on the situation of the reply to the inquiry may be printed and distributed at the meeting.  
Where the inquiry is replied orally, the responsible person of the organ inquired shall attend the meeting to reply. Where the inquiry is replied in writing, the reply shall be signed by the responsible person of the organ inquired, and the council of chairpersons shall have it printed and distributed at the meeting or to the members of the Standing Committee who proposed the inquiry.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民代表大会常务委员会主任、副主任和秘书长组成主任会议；县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民代表大会常务委员会主任、副主任组成主任会议。  
主任会议处理常务委员会的重要日常工作：  
（一）决定常务委员会每次会议的会期，拟订会议议程草案，必要时提出调整会议议程的建议；  
（二）对向常务委员会提出的议案和质询案，决定交由有关的专门委员会审议或者提请常务委员会全体会议审议；  
（三）决定是否将议案和决定草案、决议草案提请常务委员会全体会议表决，对暂不交付表决的，提出下一步处理意见；  
（四）通过常务委员会年度工作计划等；  
（五）指导和协调专门委员会的日常工作；  
（六）其他重要日常工作。

**Article 54** The council of chairpersons of the Standing Committee of a people's congress of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall be composed of the chairperson, vice-chairpersons, and secretary-general; the council of chairpersons of the Standing Committee of a people's congress of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall be composed of the chairperson and vice-chairpersons.  
The council of chairpersons shall deal with the following important routine work of the Standing Committee:  
(1) deciding the dates of each meeting of the Standing Committee, preparing the draft agenda for the meeting, and, when necessary, proposing adjustments to the agenda;  
(2) deciding whether to refer proposals and proposals for inquiries submitted to the Standing Committee to the relevant special committees for deliberation or to place them before a plenary meeting of the Standing Committee for deliberation;  
(3) deciding whether to place proposals, draft decisions, and draft resolutions before a plenary meeting of the Standing Committee for a vote, and, for those not submitted for a vote, proposing further handling opinions;  
(4) adopting the annual work plan of the Standing Committee, among other matters;  
(5) guiding and coordinating the routine work of the special committees; and  
(6) other important routine work.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　常务委员会主任因为健康情况不能工作或者缺位的时候，由常务委员会在副主任中推选一人代理主任的职务，直到主任恢复健康或者人民代表大会选出新的主任为止。

**Article 55** Where the chairperson of the Standing Committee is unable to work due to health reasons or the post is vacant, the Standing Committee shall elect one of the vice-chairpersons to act as the chairperson until the chairperson recovers health or the people's congress elects a new chairperson.

#### 第四节　常务委员会各委员会和工作机构

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会设立代表资格审查委员会。  
代表资格审查委员会的主任委员、副主任委员和委员的人选，由常务委员会主任会议在常务委员会组成人员中提名，常务委员会任免。

**Article 56** The Standing Committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall establish a Credentials Committee.  
The candidates for the chairperson, vice-chairpersons, and members of the Credentials Committee shall be nominated by the council of chairpersons of the Standing Committee from among the members of the Standing Committee, and the Standing Committee shall appoint or remove them.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　代表资格审查委员会审查代表的选举是否符合法律规定。

**Article 57** The Credentials Committee shall examine whether the election of deputies complies with the provisions of law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　主任会议或者五分之一以上的常务委员会组成人员书面联名，可以向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提议组织关于特定问题的调查委员会，由全体会议决定。  
调查委员会由主任委员、副主任委员和委员组成，由主任会议在常务委员会组成人员和其他代表中提名，提请全体会议通过。  
调查委员会应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会提出调查报告。常务委员会根据调查委员会的报告，可以作出相应的决议。

**Article 58** The council of chairpersons or one-fifth or more of the members of the Standing Committee jointly in writing may propose to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level to organize an investigation committee on a specific issue, and the decision shall be made by a plenary meeting.  
An investigation committee shall be composed of a chairperson, vice-chairpersons, and members. The candidates shall be nominated by the council of chairpersons from among the members of the Standing Committee and other deputies and shall be submitted to a plenary meeting for approval.  
The investigation committee shall submit an investigation report to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level. The Standing Committee may make a corresponding resolution based on the report of the investigation committee.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　常务委员会根据工作需要，设立办事机构和法制工作委员会、预算工作委员会、代表工作委员会等工作机构。  
省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会可以在地区设立工作机构。  
市辖区、不设区的市的人民代表大会常务委员会可以在街道设立工作机构。工作机构负责联系街道辖区内的人民代表大会代表，组织代表开展活动，反映代表和群众的建议、批评和意见，办理常务委员会交办的监督、选举以及其他工作，并向常务委员会报告工作。  
县、自治县的人民代表大会常务委员会可以比照前款规定，在街道设立工作机构。

**Article 59** The Standing Committee shall, based on work needs, establish administrative offices and working organs such as the Legislative Affairs Commission, the Budgetary Affairs Commission, and the Deputy Affairs Commission.  
The Standing Committee of a people's congress of a province or autonomous region may establish working organs in prefectures.  
The Standing Committee of a people's congress of a municipal district or a city not divided into districts may establish working organs in subdistricts. The working organs shall be responsible for maintaining contact with the deputies to the people's congress within the subdistrict area, organizing deputies to carry out activities, reflecting suggestions, criticisms, and opinions of deputies and the general public, handling supervision, election, and other tasks assigned by the Standing Committee, and reporting work to the Standing Committee.  
The Standing Committee of a people's congress of a county or autonomous county may, by analogy with the preceding paragraph, establish working organs in subdistricts.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会和各专门委员会、工作机构应当建立健全常务委员会组成人员和各专门委员会、工作机构联系代表的工作机制，支持和保障代表依法履职，扩大代表对各项工作的参与，充分发挥代表作用。  
县级以上的地方各级人民代表大会常务委员会通过建立基层联系点、代表联络站等方式，密切同人民群众的联系，听取对立法、监督等工作的意见和建议。

**Article 60** The Standing Committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level, along with the special committees and working organs, shall establish and improve the working mechanism for members of the Standing Committee, special committees, and working organs to maintain contact with deputies, support and guarantee deputies in performing their duties according to law, expand deputies' participation in various tasks, and give full play to the role of deputies.  
The Standing Committee of a local people's congress at or above the county level shall, by establishing grassroots contact points, deputy liaison stations, and other means, maintain close ties with the general public, and solicit opinions and suggestions on legislation, supervision, and other work.

### 第四章　地方各级人民政府

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区、乡、民族乡、镇设立人民政府。

**Article 61** People's governments shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities, municipal districts, townships, ethnic townships, and towns.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　地方各级人民政府应当维护宪法和法律权威，坚持依法行政，建设职能科学、权责法定、执法严明、公开公正、智能高效、廉洁诚信、人民满意的法治政府。

**Article 62** Local people's governments at all levels shall uphold the authority of the Constitution and laws, adhere to law-based administration, and build a law-based government that is scientifically functioned, with statutorily defined powers and responsibilities, strict law enforcement, openness and fairness, smart efficiency, incorruptibility and good faith, and satisfactory to the people.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　地方各级人民政府应当坚持以人民为中心，全心全意为人民服务，提高行政效能，建设服务型政府。

**Article 63** Local people's governments at all levels shall adhere to a people-centered approach, serve the people wholeheartedly, improve administrative efficiency, and build a service-oriented government.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　地方各级人民政府应当严格执行廉洁从政各项规定，加强廉政建设，建设廉洁政府。

**Article 64** Local people's governments at all levels shall strictly implement all provisions on clean governance, strengthen integrity building, and build a clean government.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　地方各级人民政府应当坚持诚信原则，加强政务诚信建设，建设诚信政府。

**Article 65** Local people's governments at all levels shall adhere to the principle of good faith, strengthen the construction of government integrity, and build a trustworthy government.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　地方各级人民政府应当坚持政务公开，全面推进决策、执行、管理、服务、结果公开，依法、及时、准确公开政府信息，推进政务数据有序共享，提高政府工作的透明度。

**Article 66** Local people's governments at all levels shall adhere to openness in government affairs, comprehensively advance the openness of decision-making, execution, management, services, and results, disclose government information according to law, in a timely and accurate manner, promote the orderly sharing of government data, and enhance the transparency of government work.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　地方各级人民政府应当坚持科学决策、民主决策、依法决策，提高决策的质量。

**Article 67** Local people's governments at all levels shall adhere to scientific, democratic, and law-based decision-making, and improve the quality of decision-making.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　地方各级人民政府应当依法接受监督，确保行政权力依法正确行使。

**Article 68** Local people's governments at all levels shall accept supervision according to law and ensure that administrative power is exercised correctly in accordance with law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　地方各级人民政府对本级人民代表大会和上一级国家行政机关负责并报告工作。县级以上的地方各级人民政府在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，对本级人民代表大会常务委员会负责并报告工作。  
全国地方各级人民政府都是国务院统一领导下的国家行政机关，都服从国务院。  
地方各级人民政府实行重大事项请示报告制度。

**Article 69** Local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible to and report on their work to the people's congress at the corresponding level and to the state administrative organ at the next higher level. Local people's governments at or above the county level, when the people's congress at the corresponding level is not in session, shall be responsible to and report on their work to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level.  
All local people's governments throughout the country are state administrative organs under the unified leadership of the State Council and are subordinate to the State Council.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall implement a system of reporting on and requesting instructions for major matters.

#### 第二节　地方各级人民政府的组成和任期

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民政府分别由省长、副省长，自治区主席、副主席，市长、副市长，州长、副州长和秘书长、厅长、局长、委员会主任等组成。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府分别由县长、副县长，市长、副市长，区长、副区长和局长、科长等组成。  
乡、民族乡的人民政府设乡长、副乡长。民族乡的乡长由建立民族乡的少数民族公民担任。镇人民政府设镇长、副镇长。

**Article 70** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall be composed, respectively, of the governor and deputy governors, the chairman and vice-chairmen of the autonomous region, the mayor and deputy mayors, the prefect and deputy prefects, and the secretary-general, department heads, bureau directors, and commission chairpersons, among others.  
The people's government of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall be composed, respectively, of the county head and deputy county heads, the mayor and deputy mayors, the district head and deputy district heads, and bureau directors and section chiefs, among others.  
The people's government of a township or ethnic township shall have a township head and deputy township heads. The township head of an ethnic township shall be a citizen of the ethnic minority that established the ethnic township. The people's government of a town shall have a town head and deputy town heads.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　新的一届人民政府领导人员依法选举产生后，应当在两个月内提请本级人民代表大会常务委员会任命人民政府秘书长、厅长、局长、委员会主任、科长。

**Article 71** After the leading persons of a new term of a people's government are elected according to law, they shall, within two months, submit to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level a request for the appointment of the secretary-general, department heads, bureau directors, commission chairpersons, and section chiefs of the people's government.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　地方各级人民政府每届任期五年。

**Article 72** Each term of office of a local people's government at any level shall be five years.

#### 第三节　地方各级人民政府的职权

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府行使下列职权：  
（一）执行本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会的决议，以及上级国家行政机关的决定和命令，规定行政措施，发布决定和命令；  
（二）领导所属各工作部门和下级人民政府的工作；  
（三）改变或者撤销所属各工作部门的不适当的命令、指示和下级人民政府的不适当的决定、命令；  
（四）依照法律的规定任免、培训、考核和奖惩国家行政机关工作人员；  
（五）编制和执行国民经济和社会发展规划纲要、计划和预算，管理本行政区域内的经济、教育、科学、文化、卫生、体育、城乡建设等事业和生态环境保护、自然资源、财政、民政、社会保障、公安、民族事务、司法行政、人口与计划生育等行政工作；  
（六）保护社会主义的全民所有的财产和劳动群众集体所有的财产，保护公民私人所有的合法财产，维护社会秩序，保障公民的人身权利、民主权利和其他权利；  
（七）履行国有资产管理职责；  
（八）保护各种经济组织的合法权益；  
（九）铸牢中华民族共同体意识，促进各民族广泛交往交流交融，保障少数民族的合法权利和利益，保障少数民族保持或者改革自己的风俗习惯的自由，帮助本行政区域内的民族自治地方依照宪法和法律实行区域自治，帮助各少数民族发展政治、经济和文化的建设事业；  
（十）保障宪法和法律赋予妇女的男女平等、同工同酬和婚姻自由等各项权利；  
（十一）办理上级国家行政机关交办的其他事项。

**Article 73** A local people's government at or above the county level shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) executing the resolutions of the people's congress at the corresponding level and its Standing Committee, as well as the decisions and orders of the state administrative organ at the next higher level, prescribing administrative measures, and issuing decisions and orders;  
(2) leading the work of its subordinate departments and of the people's governments at lower levels;  
(3) altering or annulling inappropriate orders and directives of its subordinate departments and inappropriate decisions and orders of the people's governments at lower levels;  
(4) appointing, removing, training, assessing, and rewarding or punishing staff members of state administrative organs in accordance with the provisions of law;  
(5) formulating and implementing the outline, plans, and budgets for national economic and social development, and managing the economic, educational, scientific, cultural, public health, sports, urban and rural construction, and other undertakings within its administrative area, as well as administrative work such as ecological and environmental protection, natural resources, finance, civil affairs, social security, public security, ethnic affairs, judicial administration, and population and family planning;  
(6) protecting socialist property owned by the whole people and property collectively owned by working people, protecting citizens' lawful private property, maintaining social order, and safeguarding citizens' personal rights, democratic rights, and other rights;  
(7) performing the functions of managing state-owned assets;  
(8) protecting the lawful rights and interests of various economic organizations;  
(9) consolidating the sense of community of the Chinese nation, promoting extensive interactions, exchanges, and integration among all ethnic groups, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of ethnic minorities, ensuring the freedom of ethnic minorities to maintain or reform their own customs and habits, assisting ethnic autonomous areas within its administrative area in exercising regional autonomy in accordance with the Constitution and laws, and helping all ethnic minorities in developing their political, economic, and cultural undertakings;  
(10) safeguarding the rights of women, such as equality between men and women, equal pay for equal work, and freedom of marriage, as granted by the Constitution and laws; and  
(11) handling other matters assigned by the state administrative organ at the next higher level.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府可以根据法律、行政法规和本省、自治区、直辖市的地方性法规，制定规章，报国务院和本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。设区的市、自治州的人民政府可以根据法律、行政法规和本省、自治区的地方性法规，依照法律规定的权限制定规章，报国务院和省、自治区的人民代表大会常务委员会、人民政府以及本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。  
依照前款规定制定规章，须经各该级政府常务会议或者全体会议讨论决定。

**Article 74** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may formulate rules in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the local regulations of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall report such rules to the State Council and the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for the record. The people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture may formulate rules in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the local regulations of the province or autonomous region, within the limits of authority prescribed by law, and shall report such rules to the State Council, the Standing Committee of the people's congress of the province or autonomous region, the people's government of the province or autonomous region, and the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for the record.  
The formulation of rules under the preceding paragraph shall be subject to discussion and decision by the executive meeting or plenary meeting of the corresponding people's government.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府制定涉及个人、组织权利义务的规范性文件，应当依照法定权限和程序，进行评估论证、公开征求意见、合法性审查、集体讨论决定，并予以公布和备案。

**Article 75** Where a local people's government at or above the county level formulates a regulatory document involving the rights and obligations of individuals or organizations, it shall, in accordance with statutory authority and procedures, conduct evaluation and demonstration, publicly solicit opinions, review legality, make a decision after collective discussion, and publish and file the document.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府行使下列职权：  
（一）执行本级人民代表大会的决议和上级国家行政机关的决定和命令，发布决定和命令；  
（二）执行本行政区域内的经济和社会发展计划、预算，管理本行政区域内的经济、教育、科学、文化、卫生、体育等事业和生态环境保护、财政、民政、社会保障、公安、司法行政、人口与计划生育等行政工作；  
（三）保护社会主义的全民所有的财产和劳动群众集体所有的财产，保护公民私人所有的合法财产，维护社会秩序，保障公民的人身权利、民主权利和其他权利；  
（四）保护各种经济组织的合法权益；  
（五）铸牢中华民族共同体意识，促进各民族广泛交往交流交融，保障少数民族的合法权利和利益，保障少数民族保持或者改革自己的风俗习惯的自由；  
（六）保障宪法和法律赋予妇女的男女平等、同工同酬和婚姻自由等各项权利；  
（七）办理上级人民政府交办的其他事项。

**Article 76** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) executing the resolutions of the people's congress at the corresponding level and the decisions and orders of the state administrative organ at the next higher level, and issuing decisions and orders;  
(2) executing the economic and social development plans and budgets within its administrative area, and managing the economic, educational, scientific, cultural, public health, sports, and other undertakings within its administrative area, as well as administrative work such as ecological and environmental protection, finance, civil affairs, social security, public security, judicial administration, and population and family planning;  
(3) protecting socialist property owned by the whole people and property collectively owned by working people, protecting citizens' lawful private property, maintaining social order, and safeguarding citizens' personal rights, democratic rights, and other rights;  
(4) protecting the lawful rights and interests of various economic organizations;  
(5) consolidating the sense of community of the Chinese nation, promoting extensive interactions, exchanges, and integration among all ethnic groups, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of ethnic minorities, and ensuring the freedom of ethnic minorities to maintain or reform their own customs and habits;  
(6) safeguarding the rights of women, such as equality between men and women, equal pay for equal work, and freedom of marriage, as granted by the Constitution and laws; and  
(7) handling other matters assigned by the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　地方各级人民政府分别实行省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长、乡长、镇长负责制。  
省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长、乡长、镇长分别主持地方各级人民政府的工作。

**Article 77** Local people's governments at all levels shall respectively implement the responsibility system of the governor, chairman of the autonomous region, mayor, prefect, county head, district head, township head, and town head.  
The governor, chairman of the autonomous region, mayor, prefect, county head, district head, township head, and town head shall, respectively, preside over the work of the local people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府会议分为全体会议和常务会议。全体会议由本级人民政府全体成员组成。省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民政府常务会议，分别由省长、副省长，自治区主席、副主席，市长、副市长，州长、副州长和秘书长组成。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区的人民政府常务会议，分别由县长、副县长，市长、副市长，区长、副区长组成。省长、自治区主席、市长、州长、县长、区长召集和主持本级人民政府全体会议和常务会议。政府工作中的重大问题，须经政府常务会议或者全体会议讨论决定。

**Article 78** Meetings of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be divided into plenary meetings and executive meetings. Plenary meetings shall be composed of all members of the people's government at the corresponding level. The executive meeting of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall be composed, respectively, of the governor and deputy governors, the chairman and vice-chairmen of the autonomous region, the mayor and deputy mayors, the prefect and deputy prefects, and the secretary-general. The executive meeting of the people's government of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall be composed, respectively, of the county head and deputy county heads, the mayor and deputy mayors, the district head and deputy district heads. The governor, chairman of the autonomous region, mayor, prefect, county head, or district head shall convene and preside over the plenary meetings and executive meetings of the people's government at the corresponding level. Major issues in the work of the government shall be subject to discussion and decision by the executive meeting or plenary meeting of the government.

#### 第四节　地方各级人民政府的机构设置

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　地方各级人民政府根据工作需要和优化协同高效以及精干的原则，设立必要的工作部门。  
县级以上的地方各级人民政府设立审计机关。地方各级审计机关依照法律规定独立行使审计监督权，对本级人民政府和上一级审计机关负责。  
省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府的厅、局、委员会等工作部门和自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的人民政府的局、科等工作部门的设立、增加、减少或者合并，按照规定程序报请批准，并报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 79** Local people's governments at all levels shall, based on work needs and the principles of optimization, coordination, efficiency, and compactness, establish necessary working departments.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish audit institutions. Local audit institutions at all levels shall independently exercise the power of audit supervision in accordance with the provisions of law, and shall be responsible to the people's government at the corresponding level and to the audit institution at the next higher level.  
The establishment, addition, reduction, or merger of working departments such as departments, bureaus, and commissions of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and of working departments such as bureaus and sections of the people's government of an autonomous prefecture, county, autonomous county, city, or municipal district, shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures and shall be reported to the Standing Committee of the people's congress at the corresponding level for the record.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府根据国家区域发展战略，结合地方实际需要，可以共同建立跨行政区划的区域协同发展工作机制，加强区域合作。  
上级人民政府应当对下级人民政府的区域合作工作进行指导、协调和监督。

**Article 80** Local people's governments at or above the county level may, in accordance with the national regional development strategy and in light of local actual needs, jointly establish cross-administrative regional coordinated development working mechanisms to strengthen regional cooperation.  
The people's government at a higher level shall guide, coordinate, and supervise the work of regional cooperation of the people's governments at lower levels.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　县级以上的地方各级人民政府根据应对重大突发事件的需要，可以建立跨部门指挥协调机制。

**Article 81** Local people's governments at or above the county level may, based on the need to respond to major emergencies, establish cross-departmental command and coordination mechanisms.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　各厅、局、委员会、科分别设厅长、局长、主任、科长，在必要的时候可以设副职。  
办公厅、办公室设主任，在必要的时候可以设副主任。  
省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、设区的市的人民政府设秘书长一人，副秘书长若干人。

**Article 82** Each department, bureau, commission, and section shall have a department head, bureau director, commission chairperson, or section chief, respectively, and, when necessary, deputies may be established.  
Each general office or office shall have a director, and, when necessary, deputy directors may be established.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, or city divided into districts shall have one secretary-general and a number of deputy secretaries-general.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府的各工作部门受人民政府统一领导，并且依照法律或者行政法规的规定受国务院主管部门的业务指导或者领导。  
自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的人民政府的各工作部门受人民政府统一领导，并且依照法律或者行政法规的规定受上级人民政府主管部门的业务指导或者领导。

**Article 83** The working departments of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be under the unified leadership of the people's government and, in accordance with laws or administrative regulations, shall be subject to the professional guidance or leadership of the competent departments under the State Council.  
The working departments of the people's government of an autonomous prefecture, county, autonomous county, city, or municipal district shall be under the unified leadership of the people's government and, in accordance with laws or administrative regulations, shall be subject to the professional guidance or leadership of the competent departments of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　省、自治区、直辖市、自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的人民政府应当协助设立在本行政区域内不属于自己管理的国家机关、企业、事业单位进行工作，并且监督它们遵守和执行法律和政策。

**Article 84** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefecture, county, autonomous county, city, or municipal district shall assist the state organs, enterprises, and institutions located in its administrative area but not under its own administration in carrying out their work, and shall supervise their compliance with and implementation of laws and policies.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　省、自治区的人民政府在必要的时候，经国务院批准，可以设立若干派出机关。  
县、自治县的人民政府在必要的时候，经省、自治区、直辖市的人民政府批准，可以设立若干区公所，作为它的派出机关。  
市辖区、不设区的市的人民政府，经上一级人民政府批准，可以设立若干街道办事处，作为它的派出机关。

**Article 85** The people's government of a province or autonomous region may, when necessary, establish a number of dispatched agencies with the approval of the State Council.  
The people's government of a county or autonomous county may, when necessary, establish a number of district offices as its dispatched agencies with the approval of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The people's government of a municipal district or a city not divided into districts may, with the approval of the people's government at the next higher level, establish a number of subdistrict offices as its dispatched agencies.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　街道办事处在本辖区内办理派出它的人民政府交办的公共服务、公共管理、公共安全等工作，依法履行综合管理、统筹协调、应急处置和行政执法等职责，反映居民的意见和要求。

**Article 86** A subdistrict office shall, within its jurisdiction, handle the tasks of public services, public management, and public safety assigned by the people's government that dispatched it, perform the duties of comprehensive management, overall coordination, emergency response, and administrative law enforcement according to law, and reflect the opinions and demands of residents.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府和市辖区、不设区的市的人民政府或者街道办事处对基层群众性自治组织的工作给予指导、支持和帮助。基层群众性自治组织协助乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府和市辖区、不设区的市的人民政府或者街道办事处开展工作。

**Article 87** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, and the people's government of a municipal district or a city not divided into districts, or a subdistrict office, shall provide guidance, support, and assistance to the work of grassroots self-governing mass organizations. Grassroots self-governing mass organizations shall assist the people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, and the people's government of a municipal district or a city not divided into districts, or a subdistrict office, in carrying out their work.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府和街道办事处可以根据实际情况建立居民列席有关会议的制度。

**Article 88** The people's government of a township, ethnic township, or town, and a subdistrict office, may, based on actual circumstances, establish a system for residents to attend relevant meetings as observers.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　自治区、自治州、自治县的自治机关除行使本法规定的职权外，同时依照宪法、民族区域自治法和其他法律规定的权限行使自治权。

**Article 89** The organs of self-government of autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures, and autonomous counties shall, in addition to exercising the functions and powers prescribed in this Law, exercise the powers of self-government in accordance with the limits of authority prescribed by the Constitution, the Law on Regional Ethnic Autonomy, and other laws.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会及其常务委员会可以根据本法和实际情况，对执行中的问题作具体规定。

**Article 90** The people's congress and its Standing Committee of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law and actual circumstances, make specific provisions on issues arising during implementation.
