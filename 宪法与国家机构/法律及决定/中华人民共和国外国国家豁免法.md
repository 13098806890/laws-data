# 中华人民共和国外国国家豁免法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2023-09-01  
**生效日期**：2024-01-01

> （2023年9月1日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第五次会议通过） 第一条　为了健全外国国家豁免制度，明确中华人民共和国的法院对涉及外国国家及其财产民事案件的管辖，保护当事人合法权益，维护国家主权平等，促进对外友好交往，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称的外国国家包括： （一）外国主权国家； （二）外国主权国家的国家机关或者组成部分； （三）外国主权国家授权行使主权权力且基于该项授权从事活动的组织或者个人。 第三条　外国国家及其财产在中华人民共和国的法院享有管辖豁免，本法另有规定的除外。 第四条　外国国家通过下列方式之一明示就特定事项或者案件接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖的，对于就该事项或者案件提起的诉讼，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免： （一）国际条约； （二）书面协议； （三）向处理案件的中华人民共和国的法院提交书面文件； （四）通过外交渠道等方式向中华人民共和国提交书面文件； （五）其他明示接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖的方式。 第五条　外国国家有下列情形之一的，视为就特定事项或者案件接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖： （一）作为原告向中华人民共和国的法院提起诉讼；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全外国国家豁免制度，明确中华人民共和国的法院对涉及外国国家及其财产民事案件的管辖，保护当事人合法权益，维护国家主权平等，促进对外友好交往，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of improving the system of foreign state immunity, clarifying the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China over civil cases involving foreign states and their property, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the parties, safeguarding the equality of state sovereignty, and promoting friendly international exchanges.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称的外国国家包括：  
（一）外国主权国家；  
（二）外国主权国家的国家机关或者组成部分；  
（三）外国主权国家授权行使主权权力且基于该项授权从事活动的组织或者个人。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "foreign state" includes:  
(1) a foreign sovereign state;  
(2) a state organ or constituent part of a foreign sovereign state;  
(3) an organization or individual authorized by a foreign sovereign state to exercise sovereign authority and engaging in activities based on such authorization.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外国国家及其财产在中华人民共和国的法院享有管辖豁免，本法另有规定的除外。

**Article 3** A foreign state and its property shall enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China, except as otherwise provided in this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国国家通过下列方式之一明示就特定事项或者案件接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖的，对于就该事项或者案件提起的诉讼，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免：  
（一）国际条约；  
（二）书面协议；  
（三）向处理案件的中华人民共和国的法院提交书面文件；  
（四）通过外交渠道等方式向中华人民共和国提交书面文件；  
（五）其他明示接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖的方式。

**Article 4** Where a foreign state expressly accepts the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China over a specific matter or case by one of the following means, such foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of any proceedings instituted on that matter or case:  
(1) an international treaty;  
(2) a written agreement;  
(3) submitting a written document to the court of the People's Republic of China handling the case;  
(4) submitting a written document to the People's Republic of China through diplomatic channels or other means;  
(5) any other means of expressly accepting the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外国国家有下列情形之一的，视为就特定事项或者案件接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖：  
（一）作为原告向中华人民共和国的法院提起诉讼；  
（二）作为被告参加中华人民共和国的法院受理的诉讼，并就案件实体问题答辩或者提出反诉；  
（三）作为第三人参加中华人民共和国的法院受理的诉讼；  
（四）在中华人民共和国的法院作为原告提起诉讼或者作为第三人提出诉讼请求时，由于与该起诉或者该诉讼请求相同的法律关系或者事实被提起反诉。  
外国国家有前款第二项规定的情形，但能够证明其作出上述答辩之前不可能知道有可主张豁免的事实的，可以在知道或者应当知道该事实后的合理时间内主张管辖豁免。

**Article 5** A foreign state shall be deemed to have accepted the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China over a specific matter or case in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it institutes proceedings as a plaintiff in the courts of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) it participates as a defendant in proceedings pending before a court of the People's Republic of China and files a defense on the merits or a counterclaim;  
(3) it participates as a third party in proceedings pending before a court of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) when it institutes proceedings as a plaintiff or files a claim as a third party in the courts of the People's Republic of China, a counterclaim is brought against it based on the same legal relationship or facts as the said proceedings or claim.  
Where a foreign state falls under the circumstances specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, but can prove that it could not have known of the facts on which immunity may be claimed before filing such defense, it may claim immunity from jurisdiction within a reasonable time after it knew or ought to have known of such facts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国国家有下列情形之一的，不视为接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖：  
（一）仅为主张豁免而应诉答辩；  
（二）外国国家的代表在中华人民共和国的法院出庭作证；  
（三）同意在特定事项或者案件中适用中华人民共和国的法律。

**Article 6** A foreign state shall not be deemed to have accepted the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it appears and answers only for the purpose of claiming immunity;  
(2) a representative of the foreign state appears as a witness in the courts of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) it agrees to apply the laws of the People's Republic of China in a specific matter or case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国国家与包括中华人民共和国在内的其他国家的组织或者个人进行的商业活动，在中华人民共和国领域内发生，或者虽然发生在中华人民共和国领域外但在中华人民共和国领域内产生直接影响的，对于该商业活动引起的诉讼，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免。  
本法所称商业活动是指非行使主权权力的关于货物或者服务的交易、投资、借贷以及其他商业性质的行为。中华人民共和国的法院在认定一项行为是否属于商业活动时，应当综合考虑该行为的性质和目的。

**Article 7** Where a foreign state engages in commercial activities with organizations or individuals of other states, including the People's Republic of China, which take place within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or, though taking place outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, have a direct effect within the territory of the People's Republic of China, such foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of proceedings arising from such commercial activities.  
For the purposes of this Law, "commercial activity" means transactions in goods or services, investments, loans, and other acts of a commercial nature, not in the exercise of sovereign authority. When determining whether an act constitutes a commercial activity, the courts of the People's Republic of China shall consider both the nature and the purpose of the act.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国国家为获得个人提供的劳动或者劳务而签订的合同全部或者部分在中华人民共和国领域内履行的，对于因该合同引起的诉讼，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）获得个人提供的劳动或者劳务是为了履行该外国国家行使主权权力的特定职能；  
（二）提供劳动或者劳务的个人是外交代表、领事官员、享有豁免的国际组织驻华代表机构工作人员或者其他享有相关豁免的人员；  
（三）提供劳动或者劳务的个人在提起诉讼时具有该外国国家的国籍，并且在中华人民共和国领域内没有经常居所；  
（四）该外国国家与中华人民共和国另有协议。

**Article 8** Where a contract concluded by a foreign state for the provision of labor or services by an individual is to be performed wholly or partly within the territory of the People's Republic of China, such foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of proceedings arising from that contract, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the labor or services provided by the individual are for the performance of a specific function of the foreign state in the exercise of its sovereign authority;  
(2) the individual providing the labor or services is a diplomatic agent, consular officer, a staff member of a representative office of an international organization enjoying immunity in China, or other person enjoying relevant immunity;  
(3) the individual providing the labor or services is a national of that foreign state at the time of instituting proceedings and does not have his or her habitual residence within the territory of the People's Republic of China; or  
(4) the foreign state and the People's Republic of China have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对于外国国家在中华人民共和国领域内的相关行为造成人身伤害、死亡或者造成动产、不动产损失引起的赔偿诉讼，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免。

**Article 9** A foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of proceedings for compensation arising from personal injury, death, or damage to movable or immovable property caused by relevant acts of the foreign state within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于下列财产事项的诉讼，外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免：  
（一）该外国国家对位于中华人民共和国领域内的不动产的任何权益或者义务；  
（二）该外国国家对动产、不动产的赠与、遗赠、继承或者因无人继承而产生的任何权益或者义务；  
（三）在管理信托财产、破产财产或者进行法人、非法人组织清算时涉及该外国国家的权益或者义务。

**Article 10** A foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of proceedings concerning the following property matters:  
(1) any interest or obligation of the foreign state in immovable property situated within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) any interest or obligation of the foreign state arising from a gift, bequest, succession, or escheat of movable or immovable property;  
(3) an interest or obligation of the foreign state involved in the administration of trust property, bankruptcy property, or liquidation of a legal person or unincorporated organization.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于下列知识产权事项的诉讼，外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免：  
（一）确定该外国国家受中华人民共和国法律保护的知识产权归属及相关权益；  
（二）该外国国家在中华人民共和国领域内侵害受中华人民共和国法律保护的知识产权及相关权益。

**Article 11** A foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of proceedings concerning the following intellectual property matters:  
(1) determining the ownership and related rights and interests of the foreign state in intellectual property protected by the laws of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) the foreign state's infringement within the territory of the People's Republic of China of intellectual property and related rights and interests protected by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国国家与包括中华人民共和国在内的其他国家的组织或者个人之间的商业活动产生的争议，根据书面协议被提交仲裁的，或者外国国家通过国际投资条约等书面形式同意将其与包括中华人民共和国在内的其他国家的组织或者个人产生的投资争端提交仲裁的，对于需要法院审查的下列事项，该外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免：  
（一）仲裁协议的效力；  
（二）仲裁裁决的承认和执行；  
（三）仲裁裁决的撤销；  
（四）法律规定的其他由中华人民共和国的法院对仲裁进行审查的事项。

**Article 12** Where a dispute arising from commercial activities between a foreign state and organizations or individuals of other states, including the People's Republic of China, is submitted to arbitration under a written agreement, or a foreign state agrees through an international investment treaty or other written form to submit an investment dispute with organizations or individuals of other states, including the People's Republic of China, to arbitration, such foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China in respect of the following matters that require judicial review:  
(1) the validity of the arbitration agreement;  
(2) the recognition and enforcement of the arbitral award;  
(3) the setting aside of the arbitral award;  
(4) other matters concerning judicial review of arbitration as provided for by law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国国家的财产在中华人民共和国的法院享有司法强制措施豁免。  
外国国家接受中华人民共和国的法院管辖，不视为放弃司法强制措施豁免。

**Article 13** The property of a foreign state shall enjoy immunity from compulsory measures in the courts of the People's Republic of China.  
Acceptance by a foreign state of the jurisdiction of the courts of the People's Republic of China shall not be deemed a waiver of immunity from compulsory measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　有下列情形之一的，外国国家的财产在中华人民共和国的法院不享有司法强制措施豁免：  
（一）外国国家以国际条约、书面协议或者向中华人民共和国的法院提交书面文件等方式明示放弃司法强制措施豁免；  
（二）外国国家已经拨出或者专门指定财产用于司法强制措施执行；  
（三）为执行中华人民共和国的法院的生效判决、裁定，对外国国家位于中华人民共和国领域内、用于商业活动且与诉讼有联系的财产采取司法强制措施。

**Article 14** The property of a foreign state shall not enjoy immunity from compulsory measures in the courts of the People's Republic of China in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the foreign state expressly waives immunity from compulsory measures by means of an international treaty, a written agreement, or a written document submitted to the court of the People's Republic of China, or by other means;  
(2) the foreign state has allocated or specifically designated property for the enforcement of compulsory measures; or  
(3) compulsory measures are taken against property of the foreign state that is situated within the territory of the People's Republic of China, used for commercial activities, and connected with the proceedings, for the purpose of enforcing a final judgment or ruling of a court of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　下列外国国家的财产不视为[本法第十四条](#art-14)第三项规定的用于商业活动的财产：  
（一）外交代表机构、领事机构、特别使团、驻国际组织代表团或者派往国际会议的代表团用于、意图用于公务的财产，包括银行账户款项；  
（二）属于军事性质的财产，或者用于、意图用于军事的财产；  
（三）外国和区域经济一体化组织的中央银行或者履行中央银行职能的金融管理机构的财产，包括现金、票据、银行存款、有价证券、外汇储备、黄金储备以及该中央银行或者该履行中央银行职能的金融管理机构的不动产和其他财产；  
（四）构成该国文化遗产或者档案的一部分，且非供出售或者意图出售的财产；  
（五）用于展览的具有科学、文化、历史价值的物品，且非供出售或者意图出售的财产；  
（六）中华人民共和国的法院认为不视为用于商业活动的其他财产。

**Article 15** The following property of a foreign state shall not be deemed property used for commercial activities as referred to in Article 14, item (3) of this Law:  
(1) property of diplomatic missions, consular posts, special missions, missions to international organizations, or delegations to international conferences, used or intended for official purposes, including bank account funds;  
(2) property of a military character, or property used or intended for military purposes;  
(3) property of the central bank or financial regulatory authority performing the functions of a central bank of a foreign state or a regional economic integration organization, including cash, instruments, bank deposits, negotiable securities, foreign exchange reserves, gold reserves, and immovable and other property of such central bank or financial regulatory authority;  
(4) property forming part of the cultural heritage or archives of the state, and not being for sale or intended for sale;  
(5) objects of scientific, cultural, or historical value exhibited and not being for sale or intended for sale; and  
(6) other property that the courts of the People's Republic of China deem not to be property used for commercial activities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对于外国国家及其财产民事案件的审判和执行程序，本法没有规定的，适用中华人民共和国的民事诉讼法律以及其他相关法律的规定。

**Article 16** Matters concerning the adjudication and enforcement procedures in civil cases involving a foreign state and its property that are not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of the civil procedure laws of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中华人民共和国的法院向外国国家送达传票或者其他诉讼文书，应当按照下列方式进行：  
（一）该外国国家与中华人民共和国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约规定的方式；  
（二）该外国国家接受且中华人民共和国法律不禁止的其他方式。  
通过前款方式无法完成送达的，可以通过外交照会方式送交该外国国家外交部门，外交照会发出之日视为完成送达。  
按照本条第一款、第二款规定的方式进行送达的诉讼文书，应当依照该外国国家与中华人民共和国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约的规定附上有关语言的译本，没有相关国际条约的，附上该外国国家官方语言的译本。  
向外国国家送达起诉状副本时，应当一并通知该外国国家在收到起诉状副本后三个月内提出答辩状。  
外国国家在对其提起的诉讼中就实体问题答辩后，不得再就诉讼文书的送达方式提出异议。

**Article 17** The courts of the People's Republic of China shall serve summons or other procedural documents on a foreign state in the following manner:  
(1) in the manner provided for in an international treaty concluded or jointly acceded to by the foreign state and the People's Republic of China;  
(2) in any other manner accepted by the foreign state and not prohibited by the laws of the People's Republic of China.  
Where service cannot be effected by the means specified in the preceding paragraph, service may be effected by delivering the documents by diplomatic note to the foreign ministry of the foreign state. The service shall be deemed to have been completed on the date on which the diplomatic note is dispatched.  
Procedural documents served in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be accompanied by a translation into the relevant language as provided for in an international treaty concluded or jointly acceded to by the foreign state and the People's Republic of China, or, in the absence of such a treaty, a translation into the official language of the foreign state.  
When serving a copy of the statement of claim on a foreign state, the court shall at the same time inform the foreign state to file a statement of defense within three months after receiving the copy of the statement of claim.  
After a foreign state has filed a defense on the merits in the proceedings instituted against it, it may no longer raise an objection to the manner of service of the procedural documents.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经送达完成，外国国家未在中华人民共和国的法院指定期限内出庭的，法院应当主动查明该外国国家是否享有管辖豁免。对于外国国家在中华人民共和国的法院不享有管辖豁免的案件，法院可以缺席判决，但应当在诉讼文书送达之日的六个月以后。  
中华人民共和国的法院对外国国家作出的缺席判决，应当按照[本法第十七条](#art-17)的规定送达。  
外国国家对中华人民共和国的法院缺席判决提起上诉的期限为六个月，从判决书送达之日起计算。

**Article 18** Where, after service is completed, a foreign state fails to appear within the time limit specified by a court of the People's Republic of China, the court shall ex officio ascertain whether the foreign state enjoys immunity from jurisdiction. For a case in which the foreign state does not enjoy immunity from jurisdiction in the courts of the People's Republic of China, the court may render a default judgment, but only after six months have elapsed from the date of service of the procedural documents.  
A default judgment rendered by a court of the People's Republic of China against a foreign state shall be served in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of this Law.  
The time limit for a foreign state to appeal a default judgment rendered by a court of the People's Republic of China shall be six months, calculated from the date of service of the judgment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中华人民共和国外交部就以下有关国家行为的事实问题出具的证明文件，中华人民共和国的法院应当采信：  
（一）案件中的相关国家是否构成[本法第二条](#art-2)第一项中的外国主权国家；  
（二）[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定的外交照会是否送达以及何时送达；  
（三）其他有关国家行为的事实问题。  
对于前款以外其他涉及外交事务等重大国家利益的问题，中华人民共和国外交部可以向中华人民共和国的法院出具意见。

**Article 19** A certificate issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China concerning the following questions of fact relating to acts of state shall be accepted as conclusive by the courts of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) whether the relevant state in the case constitutes a foreign sovereign state under Article 2, item (1) of this Law;  
(2) whether the diplomatic note referred to in Article 17 of this Law has been served and the date of service; and  
(3) any other question of fact relating to an act of state.  
For questions other than those specified in the preceding paragraph that involve major national interests such as foreign affairs, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China may present its views to the courts of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本法规定不影响外国的外交代表机构、领事机构、特别使团、驻国际组织代表团、派往国际会议的代表团及上述机构的相关人员根据中华人民共和国的法律、中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约享有的特权与豁免。  
本法规定不影响外国国家元首、政府首脑、外交部长及其他具有同等身份的官员根据中华人民共和国的法律、中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约以及国际习惯享有的特权与豁免。

**Article 20** The provisions of this Law shall not affect the privileges and immunities enjoyed by foreign diplomatic missions, consular posts, special missions, missions to international organizations, delegations to international conferences, and relevant personnel of the said missions under the laws of the People's Republic of China and the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.  
The provisions of this Law shall not affect the privileges and immunities enjoyed by foreign heads of state, heads of government, ministers for foreign affairs, and other officials of equivalent status under the laws of the People's Republic of China, the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, and international custom.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国给予中华人民共和国国家及其财产的豁免待遇低于本法规定的，中华人民共和国实行对等原则。

**Article 21** If a foreign state grants to the state and property of the People's Republic of China immunity treatment less favorable than that provided for in this Law, the People's Republic of China shall apply the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本法有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定，但中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 22** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of this Law, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply, except for clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本法自2024年1月1日起施行。

**Article 23** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2024.
