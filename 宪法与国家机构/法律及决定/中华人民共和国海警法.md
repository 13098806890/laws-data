# 中华人民共和国海警法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-01-22  
**生效日期**：2021-02-01

> （2021年1月22日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　机构和职责 第三章　海上安全保卫 第四章　海上行政执法 第五章　海上犯罪侦查 第六章　警械和武器使用 第七章　保障和协作 第八章　国际合作 第九章　监　　督 第十章　法律责任 第十一章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范和保障海警机构履行职责，维护国家主权、安全和海洋权益，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，制定本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和保障海警机构履行职责，维护国家主权、安全和海洋权益，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of regulating and safeguarding the performance of duties by the Coast Guard agencies, safeguarding national sovereignty, security and maritime rights and interests, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons and other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民武装警察部队海警部队即海警机构，统一履行海上维权执法职责。  
海警机构包括中国海警局及其海区分局和直属局、省级海警局、市级海警局、海警工作站。

**Article 2** The Coast Guard of the People's Armed Police Force, being the Coast Guard agencies, shall uniformly perform the functions and duties of maritime rights protection and law enforcement.  
The Coast Guard agencies include the China Coast Guard and its sea area branches and directly subordinate bureaus, provincial-level Coast Guard bureaus, municipal-level Coast Guard bureaus, and Coast Guard stations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海警机构在中华人民共和国管辖海域（以下简称我国管辖海域）及其上空开展海上维权执法活动，适用本法。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to the maritime rights protection and law enforcement activities carried out by the Coast Guard agencies in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the sea areas under China's jurisdiction) and the airspace over such sea areas.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海上维权执法工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，遵循依法管理、综合治理、规范高效、公正文明的原则。

**Article 4** The work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement a holistic approach to national security, and follow the principles of law-based management, comprehensive governance, standardized and efficient operation, and impartiality and civility.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海上维权执法工作的基本任务是开展海上安全保卫，维护海上治安秩序，打击海上走私、偷渡，在职责范围内对海洋资源开发利用、海洋生态环境保护、海洋渔业生产作业等活动进行监督检查，预防、制止和惩治海上违法犯罪活动。

**Article 5** The basic tasks of the work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement shall be to carry out maritime security protection, maintain public order at sea, combat maritime smuggling and illegal immigration, conduct supervision and inspection over activities such as the development and utilization of marine resources, protection of the marine ecological environment, and marine fishery production operations within the scope of functions, and prevent, stop and punish maritime illegal and criminal activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海警机构及其工作人员依法执行职务受法律保护，任何组织和个人不得非法干涉、拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 6** The Coast Guard agencies and their personnel shall be protected by law when performing their duties according to law, and no organization or individual may illegally interfere with, refuse, or obstruct them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海警机构工作人员应当遵守宪法和法律，崇尚荣誉，忠于职守，纪律严明，严格执法，清正廉洁。

**Article 7** Personnel of the Coast Guard agencies shall abide by the Constitution and the law, uphold honor, be loyal to their duties, maintain strict discipline, enforce the law strictly, and be honest and incorruptible.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立陆海统筹、分工合作、科学高效的海上维权执法协作配合机制。国务院有关部门、沿海地方人民政府、军队有关部门和海警机构应当相互加强协作配合，做好海上维权执法工作。

**Article 8** The State shall establish a land-sea coordinated, division-of-labor and cooperation-based, scientific and efficient mechanism for collaboration and coordination in maritime rights protection and law enforcement. The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments in coastal areas, the relevant departments of the armed forces, and the Coast Guard agencies shall strengthen mutual collaboration and coordination to properly perform the work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在海上维权执法活动中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，依照有关法律、法规的规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the activities of maritime rights protection and law enforcement shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

### 第二章　机构和职责

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家在沿海地区按照行政区划和任务区域编设中国海警局海区分局和直属局、省级海警局、市级海警局和海警工作站，分别负责所管辖区域的有关海上维权执法工作。中国海警局按照国家有关规定领导所属海警机构开展海上维权执法工作。

**Article 10** The State shall, in coastal areas, organize and establish the sea area branches and directly subordinate bureaus of the China Coast Guard, provincial-level Coast Guard bureaus, municipal-level Coast Guard bureaus, and Coast Guard stations in accordance with administrative divisions and mission areas, which shall be respectively responsible for the relevant maritime rights protection and law enforcement work within their respective jurisdictions. The China Coast Guard shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, lead the Coast Guard agencies under it to carry out the work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海警机构管辖区域应当根据海上维权执法工作的需要合理划定和调整，可以不受行政区划限制。  
海警机构管辖区域的划定和调整应当及时向社会公布，并通报有关机关。

**Article 11** The jurisdictional areas of the Coast Guard agencies shall be rationally delimited and adjusted according to the needs of the work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement, and may not be limited by administrative divisions.  
The delimitation and adjustment of the jurisdictional areas of the Coast Guard agencies shall be promptly announced to the public and notified to the relevant authorities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海警机构依法履行下列职责：  
（一）在我国管辖海域开展巡航、警戒，值守重点岛礁，管护海上界线，预防、制止、排除危害国家主权、安全和海洋权益的行为；  
（二）对海上重要目标和重大活动实施安全保卫，采取必要措施保护重点岛礁以及专属经济区和大陆架的人工岛屿、设施和结构安全；  
（三）实施海上治安管理，查处海上违反治安管理、入境出境管理的行为，防范和处置海上恐怖活动，维护海上治安秩序；  
（四）对海上有走私嫌疑的运输工具或者货物、物品、人员进行检查，查处海上走私违法行为；  
（五）在职责范围内对海域使用、海岛保护以及无居民海岛开发利用、海洋矿产资源勘查开发、海底电（光）缆和管道铺设与保护、海洋调查测量、海洋基础测绘、涉外海洋科学研究等活动进行监督检查，查处违法行为；  
（六）在职责范围内对海洋工程建设项目、海洋倾倒废弃物对海洋污染损害、自然保护地海岸线向海一侧保护利用等活动进行监督检查，查处违法行为，按照规定权限参与海洋环境污染事故的应急处置和调查处理；  
（七）对机动渔船底拖网禁渔区线外侧海域和特定渔业资源渔场渔业生产作业、海洋野生动物保护等活动进行监督检查，查处违法行为，依法组织或者参与调查处理海上渔业生产安全事故和渔业生产纠纷；  
（八）预防、制止和侦查海上犯罪活动；  
（九）按照国家有关职责分工，处置海上突发事件；  
（十）依照法律、法规和我国缔结、参加的国际条约，在我国管辖海域以外的区域承担相关执法任务；  
（十一）法律、法规规定的其他职责。  
海警机构与公安、自然资源、生态环境、交通运输、渔业渔政、海关等主管部门的职责分工，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 12** The Coast Guard agencies shall perform the following functions and duties according to law:  
(1) conducting cruises and surveillance in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, guarding key islands and reefs, managing and protecting maritime boundaries, and preventing, stopping, and eliminating acts that endanger national sovereignty, security and maritime rights and interests;  
(2) providing security protection for important maritime targets and major events, and taking necessary measures to protect the safety of key islands and reefs as well as artificial islands, facilities and structures in the exclusive economic zone and the continental shelf;  
(3) implementing maritime public security management, investigating and dealing with acts that violate public security management and exit/entry management at sea, preventing and handling maritime terrorist activities, and maintaining maritime public order;  
(4) inspecting means of transport or goods, articles, and persons suspected of smuggling at sea, and investigating and dealing with smuggling illegal acts at sea;  
(5) within the scope of duties, supervising and inspecting activities such as the use of sea areas, protection of islands and development and utilization of uninhabited islands, exploration and development of marine mineral resources, laying and protection of submarine electric (optical) cables and pipelines, marine survey and measurement, marine basic mapping, and foreign-related marine scientific research, and investigating and dealing with illegal acts;  
(6) within the scope of duties, supervising and inspecting activities such as marine engineering construction projects, pollution damage to the ocean caused by dumping of wastes, and protection and utilization of the seaward side of the coastline in nature reserves, investigating and dealing with illegal acts, and participating in the emergency response and investigation of marine environmental pollution accidents according to the prescribed authority;  
(7) supervising and inspecting activities such as fishery production operations in the sea area outside the motorized fishing vessel bottom trawl fishery closure line and in specific fishery resource fishing grounds, and marine wildlife protection, investigating and dealing with illegal acts, and organizing or participating in the investigation and handling of maritime fishery production safety accidents and fishery production disputes according to law;  
(8) preventing, stopping, and investigating maritime criminal activities;  
(9) handling maritime emergencies in accordance with the relevant division of functions and responsibilities of the State;  
(10) undertaking relevant law enforcement tasks in areas beyond the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, in accordance with laws, regulations, and international treaties concluded or acceded to by China;  
(11) other functions and duties provided for by laws and regulations.  
The division of functions and responsibilities between the Coast Guard agencies and the competent departments of public security, natural resources, ecological environment, transport, fisheries and fishery administration, customs, etc., shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海警机构接到因海上自然灾害、事故灾难等紧急求助，应当及时通报有关主管部门，并积极开展应急救援和救助。

**Article 13** Upon receiving emergency requests for help due to maritime natural disasters, accidents, and disasters, the Coast Guard agencies shall promptly notify the relevant competent departments and actively carry out emergency rescue and assistance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中央国家机关按照国家有关规定对海上维权执法工作实行业务指导。

**Article 14** The central State organs shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide professional guidance on the work of maritime rights protection and law enforcement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中国海警局及其海区分局按照国家有关规定，协调指导沿海地方人民政府海上执法队伍开展海域使用、海岛保护开发、海洋生态环境保护、海洋渔业管理等相关执法工作。  
根据海上维权执法工作需要，中国海警局及其海区分局可以统一协调组织沿海地方人民政府海上执法队伍的船舶、人员参与海上重大维权执法行动。

**Article 15** The China Coast Guard and its sea area branches shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, coordinate and guide the maritime law enforcement teams of the local people's governments in coastal areas to carry out relevant law enforcement work such as sea area use, island protection and development, marine ecological environment protection, and marine fishery management.  
According to the needs of maritime rights protection and law enforcement work, the China Coast Guard and its sea area branches may uniformly coordinate and organize the vessels and personnel of the maritime law enforcement teams of the local people's governments in coastal areas to participate in major maritime rights protection and law enforcement operations.

### 第三章　海上安全保卫

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　为维护海上安全和秩序，海警机构有权依法对在我国管辖海域航行、停泊、作业的外国船舶进行识别查证，判明船舶的基本信息及其航行、作业的基本情况。对有违法嫌疑的外国船舶，海警机构有权采取跟踪监视等措施。

**Article 16** In order to maintain maritime safety and order, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to identify and verify foreign vessels navigating, mooring, or operating in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction according to law, and ascertain the basic information of the vessels and the basic conditions of their navigation and operations. For foreign vessels suspected of illegal acts, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to take measures such as tracking and surveillance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对非法进入我国领海及其以内海域的外国船舶，海警机构有权责令其立即离开，或者采取扣留、强制驱离、强制拖离等措施。

**Article 17** For foreign vessels that illegally enter China's territorial sea or the sea areas within it, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to order them to leave immediately, or take measures such as detention, compulsory removal, or compulsory towing.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海警机构执行海上安全保卫任务，可以对在我国管辖海域航行、停泊、作业的船舶依法登临、检查。  
海警机构登临、检查船舶，应当通过明确的指令要求被检查船舶停船接受检查。被检查船舶应当按照指令停船接受检查，并提供必要的便利；拒不配合检查的，海警机构可以强制检查；现场逃跑的，海警机构有权采取必要的措施进行拦截、紧追。  
海警机构检查船舶，有权依法查验船舶和生产作业许可有关的证书、资料以及人员身份信息，检查船舶及其所载货物、物品，对有关违法事实进行调查取证。  
对外国船舶登临、检查、拦截、紧追，遵守我国缔结、参加的国际条约的有关规定。

**Article 18** When performing maritime security protection tasks, the Coast Guard agencies may board and inspect vessels navigating, mooring, or operating in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction according to law.  
When boarding and inspecting a vessel, the Coast Guard agencies shall, through clear instructions, require the vessel under inspection to stop and submit to inspection. The vessel under inspection shall stop and submit to inspection in accordance with the instructions and provide necessary convenience. If the vessel refuses to cooperate with the inspection, the Coast Guard agencies may conduct a compulsory inspection. If the vessel flees on the spot, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to take necessary measures to intercept and give hot pursuit.  
When inspecting a vessel, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to examine, according to law, the certificates and materials relating to the vessel and its production or operation permit, as well as the identity information of the persons on board, inspect the vessel and the cargo and articles carried on it, and investigate and collect evidence on the relevant illegal facts.  
The boarding, inspection, interception and hot pursuit of foreign vessels shall comply with the relevant provisions of international treaties concluded or acceded to by China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海警机构因处置海上突发事件的紧急需要，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）责令船舶停止航行、作业；  
（二）责令船舶改变航线或者驶向指定地点；  
（三）责令船舶上的人员下船，或者限制、禁止人员上船、下船；  
（四）责令船舶卸载货物，或者限制、禁止船舶卸载货物；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 19** Where urgently needed for handling maritime emergencies, the Coast Guard agencies may take the following measures:  
(1) ordering the vessel to stop navigation or operations;  
(2) ordering the vessel to change its course or proceed to a designated place;  
(3) ordering persons on board to disembark, or restricting or prohibiting persons from boarding or disembarking;  
(4) ordering the vessel to unload cargo, or restricting or prohibiting the vessel from unloading cargo;  
(5) other measures provided for by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　未经我国主管机关批准，外国组织和个人在我国管辖海域和岛礁建造建筑物、构筑物，以及布设各类固定或者浮动装置的，海警机构有权责令其停止上述违法行为或者限期拆除；对拒不停止违法行为或者逾期不拆除的，海警机构有权予以制止或者强制拆除。

**Article 20** Where foreign organizations or individuals, without approval from the competent authorities of China, construct buildings or structures, or deploy various fixed or floating installations in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction or on islands and reefs, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to order them to cease the above-mentioned illegal acts or to demolish them within a prescribed time limit. If they refuse to cease the illegal acts or fail to demolish them within the prescribed time limit, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to stop the acts or conduct compulsory demolition.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对外国军用船舶和用于非商业目的的外国政府船舶在我国管辖海域违反我国法律、法规的行为，海警机构有权采取必要的警戒和管制措施予以制止，责令其立即离开相关海域；对拒不离开并造成严重危害或者威胁的，海警机构有权采取强制驱离、强制拖离等措施。

**Article 21** For acts of foreign military vessels and foreign government vessels used for non-commercial purposes that violate Chinese laws and regulations in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to take necessary warning and control measures to stop such acts, and order them to immediately leave the relevant sea areas. If they refuse to leave and cause serious harm or threat, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to take measures such as compulsory removal or compulsory towing.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家主权、主权权利和管辖权在海上正在受到外国组织和个人的不法侵害或者面临不法侵害的紧迫危险时，海警机构有权依照本法和其他相关法律、法规，采取包括使用武器在内的一切必要措施制止侵害、排除危险。

**Article 22** Where the sovereignty, sovereign rights, and jurisdiction of the State at sea are being unlawfully infringed upon by foreign organizations or individuals, or are under imminent danger of such unlawful infringement, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right, in accordance with this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, to take all necessary measures, including the use of weapons, to stop the infringement and eliminate the danger.

### 第四章　海上行政执法

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　海警机构对违反海上治安、海关、海洋资源开发利用、海洋生态环境保护、海洋渔业管理等法律、法规、规章的组织和个人，依法实施包括限制人身自由在内的行政处罚、行政强制或者法律、法规规定的其他措施。  
海警机构依照海洋资源开发利用、海洋生态环境保护、海洋渔业管理等法律、法规的规定，对海上生产作业现场进行监督检查。  
海警机构因调查海上违法行为的需要，有权向有关组织和个人收集、调取证据。有关组织和个人应当如实提供证据。  
海警机构为维护海上治安秩序，对有违法犯罪嫌疑的人员进行当场盘问、检查或者继续盘问的，依照《中华人民共和国人民警察法》的规定执行。

**Article 23** For organizations and individuals that violate laws, regulations, and rules on maritime public security, customs, marine resource development and utilization, marine ecological environment protection, marine fishery management, etc., the Coast Guard agencies shall, according to law, impose administrative penalties, administrative compulsion, or other measures provided for by laws and regulations, including the restriction of personal freedom.  
The Coast Guard agencies shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations on marine resource development and utilization, marine ecological environment protection, marine fishery management, etc., conduct supervision and inspection at the scene of maritime production operations.  
When necessary for investigating maritime illegal acts, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to collect and obtain evidence from relevant organizations and individuals. The relevant organizations and individuals shall truthfully provide evidence.  
Where the Coast Guard agencies, in order to maintain maritime public order, conduct on-the-spot interrogation, inspection or continued interrogation of persons suspected of illegal or criminal activities, they shall do so in accordance with the provisions of the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海警机构因开展行政执法需要登临、检查、拦截、紧追相关船舶的，依照[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定执行。

**Article 24** Where the Coast Guard agencies need to board, inspect, intercept, or give hot pursuit of relevant vessels for the purpose of carrying out administrative law enforcement, they shall do so in accordance with the provisions of Article 18 of this Law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一，省级海警局以上海警机构可以在我国管辖海域划定海上临时警戒区，限制或者禁止船舶、人员通行、停留：  
（一）执行海上安全保卫任务需要的；  
（二）打击海上违法犯罪活动需要的；  
（三）处置海上突发事件需要的；  
（四）保护海洋资源和生态环境需要的；  
（五）其他需要划定海上临时警戒区的情形。  
划定海上临时警戒区，应当明确海上临时警戒区的区域范围、警戒期限、管理措施等事项并予以公告。其中，可能影响海上交通安全的，应当在划定前征求海事管理机构的意见，并按照相关规定向海事管理机构申请发布航行通告、航行警告；涉及军事用海或者可能影响海上军事设施安全和使用的，应当依法征得军队有关部门的同意。  
对于不需要继续限制或者禁止船舶、人员通行、停留的，海警机构应当及时解除警戒，并予公告。

**Article 25** In any of the following circumstances, a Coast Guard agency at or above the provincial level may delineate a maritime temporary warning zone in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, and restrict or prohibit the passage or stay of vessels and persons:  
(1) where it is necessary for carrying out maritime security protection tasks;  
(2) where it is necessary for cracking down on maritime illegal and criminal activities;  
(3) where it is necessary for handling maritime emergencies;  
(4) where it is necessary for protecting marine resources and ecological environment;  
(5) other circumstances where it is necessary to delineate a maritime temporary warning zone.  
When delineating a maritime temporary warning zone, the area of the zone, the duration of the warning, the management measures, and other matters shall be specified and publicly announced. If the warning zone may affect maritime traffic safety, the opinion of the maritime administrative agency shall be sought before delineation, and an application shall be made to the maritime administrative agency for issuing a navigation notice or navigation warning in accordance with the relevant provisions. If military use of the sea or the safety and use of military facilities at sea may be involved, the consent of the relevant departments of the armed forces shall be obtained according to law.  
Where it is no longer necessary to restrict or prohibit the passage or stay of vessels and persons, the Coast Guard agency shall promptly lift the warning and make an announcement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对涉嫌违法正在接受调查处理的船舶，海警机构可以责令其暂停航行、作业，在指定地点停泊或者禁止其离港。必要时，海警机构可以将嫌疑船舶押解至指定地点接受调查处理。

**Article 26** For a vessel suspected of illegal acts and under investigation and handling, the Coast Guard agency may order it to suspend navigation or operations, moor at a designated place, or prohibit it from leaving port. If necessary, the Coast Guard agency may escort the suspected vessel to a designated place for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国际组织、外国组织和个人的船舶经我国主管机关批准在我国管辖海域从事渔业生产作业以及其他自然资源勘查开发、海洋科学研究、海底电（光）缆和管道铺设等活动的，海警机构应当依法进行监管，可以派出执法人员随船监管。

**Article 27** Where vessels of international organizations, foreign organizations, or individuals, upon approval by the competent authorities of China, engage in fishery production operations or other activities such as exploration and exploitation of natural resources, marine scientific research, or laying of submarine electric (optical) cables and pipelines in the sea areas under China's jurisdiction, the Coast Guard agencies shall conduct supervision according to law, and may dispatch law enforcement personnel to conduct on-board supervision.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　为预防、制止和惩治在我国陆地领土、内水或者领海内违反有关安全、海关、财政、卫生或者入境出境管理法律、法规的行为，海警机构有权在毗连区行使管制权，依法实施行政强制措施或者法律、法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 28** In order to prevent, stop, and punish acts that violate laws and regulations concerning security, customs, finance, health, or entry-exit administration within the land territory, internal waters, or territorial sea of China, the Coast Guard agencies shall have the right to exercise the power of control in the contiguous zone, and to implement administrative compulsory measures or other measures provided for by laws and regulations according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违法事实确凿，并有下列情形之一，海警机构执法人员可以当场作出处罚决定：  
（一）对个人处五百元以下罚款或者警告、对单位处五千元以下罚款或者警告的；  
（二）罚款处罚决定不在海上当场作出，事后难以处罚的。  
当场作出的处罚决定，应当及时报所属海警机构备案。

**Article 29** Where the illegal facts are confirmed and one of the following circumstances exists, the law enforcement personnel of the Coast Guard agency may make an on-the-spot decision on punishment:  
(1) imposing a fine of 500 yuan or less or a warning on an individual, or imposing a fine of 5,000 yuan or less or a warning on a unit;  
(2) where if the fine penalty decision is not made on the spot at sea, it would be difficult to impose the penalty afterwards.  
Any on-the-spot decision on punishment shall be promptly reported to the Coast Guard agency to which the personnel belong for recordation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对不适用当场处罚，但事实清楚，当事人自愿认错认罚，且对违法事实和法律适用没有异议的海上行政案件，海警机构征得当事人书面同意后，可以通过简化取证方式和审核审批等措施快速办理。  
对符合快速办理条件的海上行政案件，当事人在自行书写材料或者询问笔录中承认违法事实、认错认罚，并有视听资料、电子数据、检查笔录等关键证据能够相互印证的，海警机构可以不再开展其他调查取证工作。  
使用执法记录仪等设备对询问过程录音录像的，可以替代书面询问笔录。必要时，对视听资料的关键内容和相应时间段等作文字说明。  
对快速办理的海上行政案件，海警机构应当在当事人到案后四十八小时内作出处理决定。

**Article 30** For maritime administrative cases that are not applicable to on-the-spot punishment but where the facts are clear, the party voluntarily admits the mistake and accepts the penalty, and has no objection to the illegal facts and the application of law, the Coast Guard agency may, after obtaining the party's written consent, handle the case quickly by means of simplified evidence collection and examination and approval procedures.  
For maritime administrative cases meeting the conditions for quick handling, if the party admits the illegal facts, admits the mistake, and accepts the penalty in written material voluntarily produced or in the interrogation record, and there is key evidence such as audio-visual materials, electronic data, or inspection records that can corroborate each other, the Coast Guard agency may no longer carry out other investigation and evidence collection work.  
Where equipment such as a law enforcement recorder is used to make audio-visual recordings of the interrogation process, such recordings may replace the written interrogation record. If necessary, a written description shall be made of the key content and corresponding time periods of the audio-visual materials.  
For maritime administrative cases handled quickly, the Coast Guard agency shall make a handling decision within 48 hours after the party arrives.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　海上行政案件有下列情形之一，不适用快速办理：  
（一）依法应当适用听证程序的；  
（二）可能作出十日以上行政拘留处罚的；  
（三）有重大社会影响的；  
（四）可能涉嫌犯罪的；  
（五）其他不宜快速办理的。

**Article 31** Maritime administrative cases with any of the following circumstances shall not be handled quickly:  
(1) where the hearing procedure is required to be applied according to law;  
(2) where an administrative detention penalty of 10 days or more may be imposed;  
(3) having a significant social impact;  
(4) possibly involving criminal offenses;  
(5) other circumstances that are not suitable for quick handling.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　海警机构实施行政强制措施前，执法人员应当向本单位负责人报告并经批准。情况紧急，需要在海上当场实施行政强制措施的，应当在二十四小时内向本单位负责人报告，抵岸后及时补办批准手续；因不可抗力无法在二十四小时内向本单位负责人报告的，应当在不可抗力影响消除后二十四小时内向本单位负责人报告。海警机构负责人认为不应当采取行政强制措施的，应当立即解除。

**Article 32** Before implementing administrative compulsory measures, the law enforcement personnel of the Coast Guard agency shall report to the person in charge of the unit and obtain approval. In an emergency situation where it is necessary to implement administrative compulsory measures on the spot at sea, they shall report to the person in charge of the unit within 24 hours, and timely complete the approval procedures after arriving at shore. If it is impossible to report to the person in charge of the unit within 24 hours due to force majeure, they shall report to the person in charge of the unit within 24 hours after the impact of the force majeure is eliminated. If the person in charge of the Coast Guard agency considers that the administrative compulsory measures should not have been taken, the measures shall be immediately lifted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　当事人逾期不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的海警机构可以依法采取下列措施：  
（一）到期不缴纳罚款的，每日按罚款数额的百分之三加处罚款；  
（二）将查封、扣押的财物依法拍卖、变卖或者将冻结的存款、汇款划拨抵缴罚款；  
（三）根据法律规定，采取其他行政强制执行方式。  
本法和其他法律没有规定海警机构可以实施行政强制执行的事项，海警机构应当申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 33** Where a party fails to perform a penalty decision within the prescribed time limit, the Coast Guard agency that made the penalty decision may take the following measures according to law:  
(1) if the fine is not paid when due, an additional fine of 3% of the amount of the fine shall be imposed for each day overdue;  
(2) auctioning or selling off by public sale the sealed-up or seized property, or transferring the frozen deposits or remittances to offset the fine, according to law;  
(3) taking other methods of administrative compulsory execution according to the provisions of the law.  
For matters where this Law and other laws do not provide that the Coast Guard agency may conduct administrative compulsory execution, the Coast Guard agency shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级海警机构对海上行政案件的管辖分工，由中国海警局规定。  
海警机构与其他机关对海上行政案件管辖有争议的，由海警机构与其他机关按照有利于案件调查处理的原则进行协商。

**Article 34** The division of jurisdiction over maritime administrative cases among Coast Guard agencies at various levels shall be stipulated by the China Coast Guard.  
If there is a dispute between a Coast Guard agency and another authority over the jurisdiction of a maritime administrative case, the Coast Guard agency and the other authority shall consult each other in accordance with the principle of facilitating the investigation and handling of the case.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　海警机构办理海上行政案件时，有证据证明当事人在海上实施将物品倒入海中等故意毁灭证据的行为，给海警机构举证造成困难的，可以结合其他证据，推定有关违法事实成立，但是当事人有证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 35** When handling a maritime administrative case, where there is evidence proving that a party has committed an act of deliberately destroying evidence, such as dumping articles into the sea, thereby causing difficulties for the Coast Guard agency in adducing evidence, the Coast Guard agency may, in combination with other evidence, presume that the relevant illegal facts are established, except where the party has sufficient evidence to rebut such presumption.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　海警机构开展巡航、警戒、拦截、紧追等海上执法工作，使用标示有专用标志的执法船舶、航空器的，即为表明身份。  
海警机构在进行行政执法调查或者检查时，执法人员不得少于两人，并应当主动出示执法证件表明身份。当事人或者其他有关人员有权要求执法人员出示执法证件。

**Article 36** Where the Coast Guard agencies carry out maritime law enforcement work such as cruises, surveillance, interception, and hot pursuit, and use law enforcement vessels or aircraft marked with special insignia, this shall constitute identification of identity.  
When conducting administrative law enforcement investigations or inspections, the law enforcement personnel shall be not less than two persons, and shall proactively produce their law enforcement credentials to identify themselves. A party or any other relevant person shall have the right to request the law enforcement personnel to produce their law enforcement credentials.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　海警机构开展海上行政执法的程序，本法未作规定的，适用《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》、《中华人民共和国行政强制法》、《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》等有关法律的规定。

**Article 37** Where this Law does not provide for the procedures for the Coast Guard agencies to carry out maritime administrative law enforcement, the provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China, the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant laws shall apply.

### 第五章　海上犯罪侦查

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　海警机构办理海上发生的刑事案件，依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和本法的有关规定行使侦查权，采取侦查措施和刑事强制措施。

**Article 38** When handling criminal cases occurring at sea, the Coast Guard agencies shall exercise the power of investigation and take investigative measures and criminal compulsory measures in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　海警机构在立案后，对于危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质的组织犯罪、重大毒品犯罪或者其他严重危害社会的犯罪案件，依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和有关规定，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦查措施，按照规定交由有关机关执行。  
追捕被通缉或者批准、决定逮捕的在逃的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，经过批准，可以采取追捕所必需的技术侦查措施。

**Article 39** After initiating a case, the Coast Guard agency may, for crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, organized crimes of a Mafia nature, major drug crimes, or other crimes seriously endangering society, take technical investigation measures in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant provisions, after strict approval procedures, and shall hand them over to the relevant authorities for execution according to the provisions.  
When pursuing a criminal suspect or defendant who is wanted or whose arrest has been approved or decided and who is at large, the Coast Guard agency may, after approval, take technical investigation measures necessary for the pursuit.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　应当逮捕的犯罪嫌疑人在逃，海警机构可以按照规定发布通缉令，采取有效措施，追捕归案。  
海警机构对犯罪嫌疑人发布通缉令的，可以商请公安机关协助追捕。

**Article 40** If a criminal suspect who should be arrested is at large, the Coast Guard agency may, in accordance with the provisions, issue a wanted order and take effective measures to pursue and bring the suspect to justice.  
Where the Coast Guard agency issues a wanted order for a criminal suspect, it may request the public security organ to assist in the pursuit.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　海警机构因办理海上刑事案件需要登临、检查、拦截、紧追相关船舶的，依照[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定执行。

**Article 41** Where the Coast Guard agency needs to board, inspect, intercept, or give hot pursuit of relevant vessels for the purpose of handling a maritime criminal case, it shall do so in accordance with the provisions of Article 18 of this Law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　海警机构、人民检察院、人民法院依法对海上刑事案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人决定取保候审的，由被取保候审人居住地的海警机构执行。被取保候审人居住地未设海警机构的，当地公安机关应当协助执行。

**Article 42** Where a Coast Guard agency, a People's Procuratorate, or a People's Court decides to release a criminal suspect or defendant on bail pending investigation or trial in a maritime criminal case according to law, the Coast Guard agency in the place where the released person resides shall enforce such decision. If there is no Coast Guard agency in the place where the released person resides, the local public security organ shall assist in the enforcement.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　海警机构、人民检察院、人民法院依法对海上刑事案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人决定监视居住的，由海警机构在被监视居住人住处执行；被监视居住人在负责办案的海警机构所在的市、县没有固定住处的，可以在指定的居所执行。对于涉嫌危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪，在住处执行可能有碍侦查的，经上一级海警机构批准，也可以在指定的居所执行。但是，不得在羁押场所、专门的办案场所执行。

**Article 43** Where a Coast Guard agency, a People's Procuratorate, or a People's Court decides to place a criminal suspect or defendant under residential surveillance in a maritime criminal case according to law, the Coast Guard agency shall enforce such decision at the residence of the person under surveillance. If the person under surveillance has no fixed residence in the city or county where the Coast Guard agency handling the case is located, the surveillance may be enforced at a designated residence. For a person suspected of crimes endangering national security or terrorist activities, if enforcement at the residence may hinder the investigation, the surveillance may, with the approval of the next higher level Coast Guard agency, also be enforced at a designated residence. However, enforcement shall not be carried out at detention facilities or special case-handling premises.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　海警工作站负责侦查发生在本管辖区域内的海上刑事案件。  
市级海警局以上海警机构负责侦查管辖区域内的重大的危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、涉外犯罪、经济犯罪、集团犯罪案件以及其他重大犯罪案件。  
上级海警机构认为有必要的，可以侦查下级海警机构管辖范围内的海上刑事案件；下级海警机构认为案情重大需要上级海警机构侦查的海上刑事案件，可以报请上级海警机构管辖。

**Article 44** The Coast Guard station shall be responsible for investigating maritime criminal cases occurring within its jurisdiction.  
A Coast Guard agency at or above the municipal level shall be responsible for investigating cases of major crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, foreign-related crimes, economic crimes, organized crimes, and other major criminal cases within its jurisdiction.  
If the higher-level Coast Guard agency deems it necessary, it may investigate maritime criminal cases within the jurisdiction of a lower-level Coast Guard agency. If a lower-level Coast Guard agency considers that a case is major and needs to be investigated by the higher-level Coast Guard agency, it may request the higher-level Coast Guard agency to take jurisdiction.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　海警机构办理海上刑事案件，需要提请批准逮捕或者移送起诉的，应当向所在地相应人民检察院提请或者移送。

**Article 45** Where the Coast Guard agency, in handling a maritime criminal case, needs to request approval for arrest or to transfer the case for prosecution, it shall make the request or transfer to the corresponding People's Procuratorate at the place where it is located.

### 第六章　警械和武器使用

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　有下列情形之一，海警机构工作人员可以使用警械或者现场的其他装备、工具：  
（一）依法登临、检查、拦截、紧追船舶时，需要迫使船舶停止航行的；  
（二）依法强制驱离、强制拖离船舶的；  
（三）依法执行职务过程中遭遇阻碍、妨害的；  
（四）需要现场制止违法犯罪行为的其他情形。

**Article 46** In any of the following circumstances, personnel of the Coast Guard agency may use police implements or other equipment or tools at the scene:  
(1) when, in the process of lawfully boarding, inspecting, intercepting, or giving hot pursuit of a vessel, it is necessary to compel the vessel to stop navigation;  
(2) when lawfully carrying out compulsory removal or compulsory towing of a vessel;  
(3) when encountering obstruction or hindrance in the course of lawfully performing duties;  
(4) other circumstances where it is necessary to stop illegal or criminal acts on the spot.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列情形之一，经警告无效的，海警机构工作人员可以使用手持武器：  
（一）有证据表明船舶载有犯罪嫌疑人或者非法载运武器、弹药、国家秘密资料、毒品等物品，拒不服从停船指令的；  
（二）外国船舶进入我国管辖海域非法从事生产作业活动，拒不服从停船指令或者以其他方式拒绝接受登临、检查，使用其他措施不足以制止违法行为的。

**Article 47** In any of the following circumstances, where a warning is ineffective, personnel of the Coast Guard agency may use handheld weapons:  
(1) where there is evidence that the vessel is carrying criminal suspects or illegally carrying weapons, ammunition, state secrets materials, drugs, or other articles, and refuses to comply with the order to stop;  
(2) where a foreign vessel enters the sea areas under China's jurisdiction to illegally engage in production or operation activities, refuses to comply with the order to stop or otherwise refuses to submit to boarding or inspection, and the use of other measures is insufficient to stop the illegal act.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有下列情形之一，海警机构工作人员除可以使用手持武器外，还可以使用舰载或者机载武器：  
（一）执行海上反恐怖任务的；  
（二）处置海上严重暴力事件的；  
（三）执法船舶、航空器受到武器或者其他危险方式攻击的。

**Article 48** In any of the following circumstances, in addition to using handheld weapons, personnel of the Coast Guard agency may also use shipborne or airborne weapons:  
(1) when carrying out maritime counter-terrorism missions;  
(2) when handling a serious violent incident at sea;  
(3) when the law enforcement vessel or aircraft is attacked by weapons or other dangerous means.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　海警机构工作人员依法使用武器，来不及警告或者警告后可能导致更为严重危害后果的，可以直接使用武器。

**Article 49** Where personnel of the Coast Guard agency lawfully use weapons, if there is no time to give a warning or if a warning would likely lead to more serious harmful consequences, they may directly use weapons.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　海警机构工作人员应当根据违法犯罪行为和违法犯罪行为人的危险性质、程度和紧迫性，合理判断使用武器的必要限度，尽量避免或者减少不必要的人员伤亡、财产损失。

**Article 50** Personnel of the Coast Guard agency shall, based on the nature, degree, and urgency of the danger of the illegal or criminal act and the perpetrator, reasonably determine the necessary limits for using weapons, and shall try to avoid or reduce unnecessary casualties and property losses.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　海警机构工作人员使用警械和武器，本法未作规定的，依照人民警察使用警械和武器的规定以及其他有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 51** Where the use of police implements and weapons by staff members of the maritime police authority is not provided for in this Law, it shall be executed in accordance with the provisions on the use of police implements and weapons by the people's police and other relevant laws and regulations.

### 第七章　保障和协作

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家建立与海警机构担负海上维权执法任务和建设发展相适应的经费保障机制。所需经费按照国家有关规定列入预算。

**Article 52** The State shall establish a funding guarantee mechanism commensurate with the maritime rights protection and law enforcement tasks undertaken by the maritime police authority and its construction and development. The necessary funds shall be included in the budget in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院有关部门、沿海县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门在编制国土空间规划和相关专项规划时，应当统筹海上维权执法工作需求，按照国家有关规定对海警机构执法办案、执勤训练、生活等场地和设施建设等予以保障。

**Article 53** The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas and their relevant departments shall, when formulating territorial space plans and relevant special plans, coordinate the needs of maritime rights protection and law enforcement work, and shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, ensure the construction of sites and facilities for the maritime police authority such as for law enforcement case handling, duty and training, and living.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　海警机构因海上维权执法紧急需要，可以依照法律、法规、规章的规定优先使用或者征用组织和个人的交通工具、通信工具、场地，用后应当及时归还，并支付适当费用；造成损失的，按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 54** Where urgently needed for maritime rights protection and law enforcement, the maritime police authority may, in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules, have priority in using or requisitioning means of transport, communication tools, and sites of organizations and individuals, and shall promptly return them after use and pay appropriate fees; if losses are caused, compensation shall be given in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　海警机构应当优化力量体系，建强人才队伍，加强教育培训，保障海警机构工作人员具备履行法定职责的知识、技能和素质，提高海上维权执法专业能力。  
海上维权执法实行持证上岗和资格管理制度。

**Article 55** The maritime police authority shall optimize the force system, build a strong talent team, strengthen education and training, ensure that its staff members possess the knowledge, skills, and qualities necessary to perform their statutory duties, and enhance the professional capability of maritime rights protection and law enforcement.  
A system of holding certificates for posts and qualification management shall be implemented for maritime rights protection and law enforcement.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家加强海上维权执法装备体系建设，保障海警机构配备与其履行职责相适应的船舶、航空器、武器以及其他装备。

**Article 56** The State shall strengthen the construction of a maritime rights protection and law enforcement equipment system, and ensure that the maritime police authority is equipped with vessels, aircraft, weapons, and other equipment commensurate with the performance of its duties.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　海警机构应当加强信息化建设，运用现代信息技术，促进执法公开，强化便民服务，提高海上维权执法工作效率。  
海警机构应当开通海上报警服务平台，及时受理人民群众报警、紧急求助。

**Article 57** The maritime police authority shall strengthen informatization construction, apply modern information technology to promote open law enforcement, enhance convenience services for the people, and improve the efficiency of maritime rights protection and law enforcement work.  
The maritime police authority shall establish a maritime reporting service platform to promptly receive reports and emergency requests from the public.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　海警机构分别与相应的外交（外事）、公安、自然资源、生态环境、交通运输、渔业渔政、应急管理、海关等主管部门，以及人民法院、人民检察院和军队有关部门建立信息共享和工作协作配合机制。  
有关主管部门应当及时向海警机构提供与开展海上维权执法工作相关的基础数据、行政许可、行政管理政策等信息服务和技术支持。  
海警机构应当将海上监督检查、查处违法犯罪等工作数据、信息，及时反馈有关主管部门，配合有关主管部门做好海上行政管理工作。海警机构依法实施行政处罚，认为需要吊销许可证件的，应当将相关材料移送发证机关处理。

**Article 58** The maritime police authority shall establish information sharing and work coordination mechanisms respectively with the competent departments of foreign affairs (foreign affairs), public security, natural resources, ecological environment, transport, fisheries and fishery administration, emergency management, customs, and the People's Courts, the People's Procuratorates, and relevant departments of the armed forces.  
The relevant competent departments shall promptly provide the maritime police authority with information services and technical support such as basic data, administrative licenses, and administrative management policies related to the conduct of maritime rights protection and law enforcement work.  
The maritime police authority shall promptly provide feedback to the relevant competent departments on work data and information such as maritime supervision and inspection, investigation and handling of illegal and criminal activities, and shall cooperate with the relevant competent departments in carrying out maritime administrative management. Where the maritime police authority imposes administrative penalties according to law and deems it necessary to revoke a license, it shall transfer the relevant materials to the issuing authority for handling.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　海警机构因开展海上维权执法工作需要，可以向有关主管部门提出协助请求。协助请求属于有关主管部门职责范围内的，有关主管部门应当配合。

**Article 59** Where necessary for carrying out maritime rights protection and law enforcement work, the maritime police authority may request assistance from the relevant competent departments. If the request for assistance falls within the scope of duties of the relevant competent department, the relevant competent department shall cooperate.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　海警机构对依法决定行政拘留的违法行为人和拘留审查的外国人，以及决定刑事拘留、执行逮捕的犯罪嫌疑人，分别送海警机构所在地拘留所或者看守所执行。

**Article 60** The maritime police authority shall, for persons illegally acting who are legally decided to be subjected to administrative detention and foreigners held for investigation, as well as criminal suspects decided to be criminally detained or arrested, respectively deliver them to the detention center or detention house in the locality of the maritime police authority for execution.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　海警机构对依法扣押、扣留的涉案财物，应当妥善保管，不得损毁或者擅自处理。但是，对下列货物、物品，经市级海警局以上海警机构负责人批准，可以先行依法拍卖或者变卖并通知所有人，所有人不明确的，通知其他当事人：  
（一）成品油等危险品；  
（二）鲜活、易腐、易失效等不宜长期保存的；  
（三）长期不使用容易导致机械性能下降、价值贬损的车辆、船舶等；  
（四）体量巨大难以保管的；  
（五）所有人申请先行拍卖或者变卖的。  
拍卖或者变卖所得款项由海警机构暂行保存，待结案后按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 61** The maritime police authority shall properly keep the property involved in the case that is seized or detained according to law, and shall not damage or dispose of it without authorization. However, for the following goods or articles, with the approval of the person in charge of the maritime police authority at or above the municipal level, they may be auctioned or sold by public sale in advance according to law, and the owner shall be notified; if the owner is unclear, the other parties shall be notified:  
(1) dangerous goods such as refined oil;  
(2) fresh, perishable, easily invalid, or other articles unsuitable for long-term storage;  
(3) vehicles, vessels, etc., that are prone to mechanical performance degradation and value depreciation due to prolonged non-use;  
(4) articles of huge volume that are difficult to keep;  
(5) articles for which the owner applies for advance auction or sale by public sale.  
The proceeds from the auction or sale by public sale shall be temporarily kept by the maritime police authority and shall be disposed of in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State after the case is closed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　海警机构对应当退还所有人或者其他当事人的涉案财物，通知所有人或者其他当事人在六个月内领取；所有人不明确的，应当采取公告方式告知所有人认领。在通知所有人、其他当事人或者公告后六个月内无人认领的，按无主财物处理，依法拍卖或者变卖后将所得款项上缴国库。遇有特殊情况的，可以延期处理，延长期限最长不超过三个月。

**Article 62** For property involved in the case that shall be returned to the owner or other party, the maritime police authority shall notify the owner or other party to claim it within six months; if the owner is unclear, it shall inform the owner to claim by means of public notice. If no one claims within six months after the notification to the owner, other party, or public notice, the property shall be treated as ownerless, and after auction or sale by public sale according to law, the proceeds shall be turned over to the State treasury. In special circumstances, the processing may be postponed, but the extension period shall not exceed three months at most.

### 第八章　国际合作

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　中国海警局根据中华人民共和国缔结、参加的国际条约或者按照对等、互利的原则，开展海上执法国际合作；在规定权限内组织或者参与有关海上执法国际条约实施工作，商签海上执法合作性文件。

**Article 63** The China Coast Guard shall carry out international cooperation on maritime law enforcement in accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or on the principles of reciprocity and mutual benefit; and shall, within the prescribed scope of authority, organize or participate in the implementation of relevant international treaties on maritime law enforcement, and negotiate and sign maritime law enforcement cooperative documents.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　海警机构开展海上执法国际合作的主要任务是参与处置涉外海上突发事件，协调解决海上执法争端，管控海上危机，与外国海上执法机构和有关国际组织合作打击海上违法犯罪活动，保护海洋资源环境，共同维护国际和地区海洋公共安全和秩序。

**Article 64** The main tasks of the maritime police authority in carrying out international cooperation on maritime law enforcement are to participate in handling foreign-related maritime emergencies, coordinate the resolution of maritime law enforcement disputes, manage maritime crises, cooperate with foreign maritime law enforcement agencies and relevant international organizations in combating maritime illegal and criminal activities, protect marine resources and the environment, and jointly maintain international and regional maritime public security and order.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　海警机构可以与外国海上执法机构和有关国际组织开展下列海上执法国际合作：  
（一）建立双边、多边海上执法合作机制，参加海上执法合作机制的活动；  
（二）交流和共享海上执法情报信息；  
（三）海上联合巡逻、检查、演练、训练；  
（四）教育培训交流；  
（五）互派海上执法国际合作联络人员；  
（六）其他海上执法国际合作活动。

**Article 65** The maritime police authority may carry out the following international cooperation on maritime law enforcement with foreign maritime law enforcement agencies and relevant international organizations:  
(1) establishing bilateral and multilateral maritime law enforcement cooperation mechanisms and participating in activities of such mechanisms;  
(2) exchanging and sharing maritime law enforcement intelligence and information;  
(3) joint maritime patrols, inspections, exercises, and training;  
(4) education and training exchanges;  
(5) reciprocal posting of liaison officers for international maritime law enforcement cooperation;  
(6) other activities of international cooperation on maritime law enforcement.

### 第九章　监督

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　海警机构及其工作人员应当依照法律、法规规定的条件、权限和程序履行职责、行使职权，不得滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，不得侵犯组织和个人的合法权益。

**Article 66** The maritime police authority and its staff members shall perform their duties and exercise their powers in accordance with the conditions, authority, and procedures prescribed by laws and regulations; they shall not abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of organizations and individuals.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　海警机构应当尊重和依法保障公民、法人和其他组织对海警机构执法工作的知情权、参与权和监督权，增强执法工作透明度和公信力。  
海警机构应当依法公开海上执法工作信息。

**Article 67** The maritime police authority shall respect and, according to law, guarantee the right to know, the right to participate, and the right to supervise the law enforcement work of the maritime police authority of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, and shall enhance the transparency and credibility of law enforcement work.  
The maritime police authority shall, according to law, disclose information on maritime law enforcement work.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　海警机构询问、讯问、继续盘问、辨认违法犯罪嫌疑人以及对违法犯罪嫌疑人进行安全检查、信息采集等执法活动，应当在办案场所进行。紧急情况下必须在现场进行询问、讯问或者有其他不宜在办案场所进行询问、讯问的情形除外。  
海警机构应当按照国家有关规定以文字、音像等形式，对海上维权执法活动进行全过程记录，归档保存。

**Article 68** The maritime police authority shall conduct law enforcement activities such as inquiring, interrogating, continuing to question, identifying criminal suspects, and conducting security checks and information collection on criminal suspects in the case-handling premises. Except in emergency situations where inquiry or interrogation must be conducted on site, or where there are other circumstances making it inappropriate to conduct inquiry or interrogation in the case-handling premises.  
The maritime police authority shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, make a full-process record of maritime rights protection and law enforcement activities in the form of text, audio and video, etc., and keep it on file.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　海警机构及其工作人员开展海上维权执法工作，依法接受检察机关、军队监察机关的监督。

**Article 69** The maritime police authority and its staff members shall, in carrying out maritime rights protection and law enforcement work, accept supervision from the procuratorial authorities and the military supervisory authorities according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　人民政府及其有关部门、公民、法人和其他组织对海警机构及其工作人员的违法违纪行为，有权向检察机关、军队监察机关通报、检举、控告。对海警机构及其工作人员正在发生的违法违纪或者失职行为，可以通过海上报警服务平台进行投诉、举报。  
对依法检举、控告或者投诉、举报的公民、法人和其他组织，任何机关和个人不得压制和打击报复。

**Article 70** People's governments and their relevant departments, citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the right to report, inform, or lodge complaints with the procuratorial authorities and the military supervisory authorities against illegal or disciplinary acts of the maritime police authority and its staff members. Complaints or reports may be made through the maritime reporting service platform against ongoing illegal or disciplinary acts or dereliction of duty of the maritime police authority and its staff members.  
No organ or individual may suppress or retaliate against citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that have, according to law, informed, reported, complained, or made a report.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　上级海警机构应当对下级海警机构的海上维权执法工作进行监督，发现其作出的处理措施或者决定有错误的，有权撤销、变更或者责令下级海警机构撤销、变更；发现其不履行法定职责的，有权责令其依法履行。

**Article 71** The superior maritime police authority shall supervise the maritime rights protection and law enforcement work of the subordinate maritime police authority; if it finds any error in the handling measures or decisions made by the latter, it shall have the power to revoke or modify them, or order the subordinate maritime police authority to revoke or modify them; if it finds that the subordinate maritime police authority fails to perform its statutory duties, it shall have the power to order it to perform them according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　中国海警局应当建立健全海上维权执法工作监督机制和执法过错责任追究制度。

**Article 72** The China Coast Guard shall establish and improve a supervision mechanism for maritime rights protection and law enforcement work and a system for investigating liability for law enforcement errors.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有下列阻碍海警机构及其工作人员依法执行职务的行为之一，由公安机关或者海警机构依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》关于阻碍人民警察依法执行职务的规定予以处罚：  
（一）侮辱、威胁、围堵、拦截、袭击海警机构工作人员的；  
（二）阻碍调查取证的；  
（三）强行冲闯海上临时警戒区的；  
（四）阻碍执行追捕、检查、搜查、救险、警卫等任务的；  
（五）阻碍执法船舶、航空器、车辆和人员通行的；  
（六）采取危险驾驶、设置障碍等方法驾驶船舶逃窜，危及执法船舶、人员安全的；  
（七）其他严重阻碍海警机构及其工作人员执行职务的行为。

**Article 73** Whoever commits any of the following acts of obstructing the maritime police authority and its staff members from performing their duties according to law shall be punished by the public security organ or the maritime police authority in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China on obstructing the people's police from performing their duties according to law:  
(1) insulting, threatening, surrounding, intercepting, or assaulting staff members of the maritime police authority;  
(2) obstructing investigation and evidence collection;  
(3) forcibly rushing into a maritime temporary警戒 area;  
(4) obstructing the execution of tasks such as pursuit, inspection, search, rescue, and guard;  
(5) obstructing the passage of law enforcement vessels, aircraft, vehicles, and personnel;  
(6) fleeing by driving a vessel through dangerous driving, setting up obstacles, or other methods, thereby endangering the safety of law enforcement vessels and personnel;  
(7) other serious acts of obstructing the maritime police authority and its staff members from performing their duties.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　海警机构工作人员在执行职务中，有下列行为之一，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定给予处分：  
（一）泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私的；  
（二）弄虚作假，隐瞒案情，包庇、纵容违法犯罪活动的；  
（三）刑讯逼供或者体罚、虐待违法犯罪嫌疑人的；  
（四）违反规定使用警械、武器的；  
（五）非法剥夺、限制人身自由，非法检查或者搜查人身、货物、物品、交通工具、住所或者场所的；  
（六）敲诈勒索，索取、收受贿赂或者接受当事人及其代理人请客送礼的；  
（七）违法实施行政处罚、行政强制，采取刑事强制措施或者收取费用的；  
（八）玩忽职守，不履行法定义务的；  
（九）其他违法违纪行为。

**Article 74** Where a staff member of the maritime police authority commits any of the following acts in the performance of duties, he or she shall be given a sanction in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission:  
(1) disclosing State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy;  
(2) practicing fraud, concealing case facts, or harboring or tolerating illegal or criminal activities;  
(3) extorting confessions by torture, or physically punishing or maltreating criminal suspects;  
(4) using police implements or weapons in violation of regulations;  
(5) unlawfully depriving or restricting personal freedom, or unlawfully inspecting or searching persons, goods, articles, means of transport, residences, or premises;  
(6) extorting, demanding, or accepting bribes, or accepting invitations for meals or gifts from parties or their agents;  
(7) illegally imposing administrative penalties, administrative compulsion, taking criminal compulsory measures, or collecting fees;  
(8) neglecting duties and failing to perform statutory obligations;  
(9) other illegal or disciplinary acts.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　组织和个人对海警机构作出的行政行为不服的，有权依照《中华人民共和国行政复议法》的规定向上一级海警机构申请行政复议；或者依照《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的规定向有管辖权的人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 76** Where an organization or individual is dissatisfied with an administrative act made by the maritime police authority, it or he shall have the right to apply for administrative reconsideration to the next higher maritime police authority in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China; or to institute an administrative action with the People's Court having jurisdiction in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　海警机构及其工作人员违法行使职权，侵犯组织和个人合法权益造成损害的，应当依照《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》和其他有关法律、法规的规定给予赔偿。

**Article 77** Where the maritime police authority and its staff members unlawfully exercise their powers, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of organizations or individuals and causing damage, compensation shall be made in accordance with the provisions of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
（一）省级海警局，是指直接由中国海警局领导，在沿海省、自治区、直辖市设立的海警局；市级海警局，是指由省级海警局领导，在沿海省、自治区下辖市和直辖市下辖区设立的海警局；海警工作站，通常是指由市级海警局领导，在沿海县级行政区域设立的基层海警机构。  
（二）船舶，是指各类排水或者非排水的船、艇、筏、水上飞行器、潜水器等移动式装置，不包括海上石油、天然气等作业平台。

**Article 78** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Provincial-level maritime police bureau" means a maritime police bureau directly under the leadership of the China Coast Guard and established in a coastal province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; "municipal-level maritime police bureau" means a maritime police bureau under the leadership of a provincial-level maritime police bureau and established in a city under the jurisdiction of a coastal province or autonomous region, or in a district under the jurisdiction of a coastal municipality directly under the Central Government; "maritime police station" normally means a grassroots maritime police agency under the leadership of a municipal-level maritime police bureau and established in a coastal county-level administrative region.  
(2) "Vessel" means various mobile devices such as ships, boats, rafts, water aircraft, and submersibles that are displacement or non-displacement, excluding offshore platforms such as those for oil and natural gas operations.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　外国在海上执法方面对我国公民、法人和其他组织采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他特别措施的，海警机构可以按照国家有关规定采取相应的对等措施。

**Article 79** Where a foreign country adopts discriminatory prohibitions, restrictions, or other special measures against Chinese citizens, legal persons, or other organizations in maritime law enforcement, the maritime police authority may take corresponding reciprocal measures in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本法规定的对船舶的维权执法措施适用于海上各种固定或者浮动建筑、装置，固定或者移动式平台。

**Article 80** The rights protection and law enforcement measures against vessels provided for in this Law shall apply to various fixed or floating structures, installations, and fixed or mobile platforms at sea.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　海警机构依照法律、法规和我国缔结、参加的国际条约，在我国管辖海域以外的区域执行执法任务时，相关程序可以参照本法有关规定执行。

**Article 81** Where the maritime police authority performs law enforcement tasks in areas beyond the sea areas under the jurisdiction of China in accordance with laws, regulations, and international treaties concluded or acceded to by China, the relevant procedures may be executed by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　中国海警局根据法律、行政法规和国务院、中央军事委员会的决定，就海上维权执法事项制定规章，并按照规定备案。

**Article 82** The China Coast Guard shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the decisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, formulate rules on matters of maritime rights protection and law enforcement, and file them for record in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　海警机构依照《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国人民武装警察法》等有关法律、军事法规和中央军事委员会的命令，执行防卫作战等任务。

**Article 83** The maritime police authority shall, in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Armed Police Force, and other relevant laws, military regulations, and orders of the Central Military Commission, perform tasks such as defensive operations.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本法自2021年2月1日起施行。

**Article 84** This Law shall come into force as of February 1, 2021.
