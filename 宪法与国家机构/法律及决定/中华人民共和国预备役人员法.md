# 中华人民共和国预备役人员法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2022-12-30  
**生效日期**：2023-03-01

> （2022年12月30日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十八次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　预备役军衔 第三章　选拔补充 第四章　教育训练和晋升任用 第五章　日常管理 第六章　征　　召 第七章　待遇保障 第八章　退出预备役 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了健全预备役人员制度，规范预备役人员管理，维护预备役人员合法权益，保障预备役人员有效履行职责使命，加强国防力量建设，根据宪法和《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国兵役法》，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称预备役人员，是指依法履行兵役义务，预编到中国人民解放军现役部队或者编入中国人民解放军预备役部队服预备役的公民。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全预备役人员制度，规范预备役人员管理，维护预备役人员合法权益，保障预备役人员有效履行职责使命，加强国防力量建设，根据宪法和《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国兵役法》，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution, the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China, for the purposes of improving the system for reservists, standardizing the management of reservists, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of reservists, ensuring that reservists effectively perform their duties and missions, and strengthening national defense capabilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称预备役人员，是指依法履行兵役义务，预编到中国人民解放军现役部队或者编入中国人民解放军预备役部队服预备役的公民。  
预备役人员分为预备役军官和预备役士兵。预备役士兵分为预备役军士和预备役兵。  
预备役人员是国家武装力量的成员，是战时现役部队兵员补充的重要来源。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "reservists" refer to citizens who perform the obligation of military service according to law and are pre-assigned to active units of the People's Liberation Army or incorporated into reserve units of the People's Liberation Army to serve in the reserve service.  
Reservists are divided into reserve officers and reserve soldiers. Reserve soldiers are divided into reserve non-commissioned officers and reserve soldiers.  
Reservists are members of the national armed forces and are an important source of replenishment for active units during wartime.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　预备役人员工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，坚持总体国家安全观，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，以军事需求为牵引，以备战打仗为指向，以质量建设为着力点，提高预备役人员履行使命任务的能力和水平。

**Article 3** The work concerning reservists shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement Xi Jinping's thinking on strengthening the military, uphold a holistic approach to national security, implement the military strategic guidelines for the new era, be driven by military needs, be oriented towards combat readiness and warfighting, focus on quality building, and enhance the capabilities and levels of reservists in performing their missions and tasks.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　预备役人员必须服从命令、严守纪律，英勇顽强、不怕牺牲，按照规定参加政治教育和军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务，随时准备应召参战，保卫祖国。  
国家依法保障预备役人员的地位和权益。预备役人员享有与其履行职责相应的荣誉和待遇。

**Article 4** Reservists must obey orders, strictly observe discipline, be brave and tenacious, and fear no sacrifice, participate in political education and military training, undertake combat readiness duties, and perform non-war military operations tasks as prescribed, and be ready at all times to respond to the call to participate in combat and defend the motherland.  
The State shall guarantee the status and rights and interests of reservists according to law. Reservists shall enjoy honors and benefits commensurate with the performance of their duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央军事委员会领导预备役人员工作。  
中央军事委员会政治工作部门负责组织指导预备役人员管理工作，中央军事委员会国防动员部门负责组织预备役人员编组、动员征集等有关工作，中央军事委员会机关其他部门按照职责分工负责预备役人员有关工作。  
中央国家机关、县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关按照职责分工做好预备役人员有关工作。  
编有预备役人员的部队（以下简称部队）负责所属预备役人员政治教育、军事训练、执行任务和有关选拔补充、日常管理、退出预备役等工作。

**Article 5** The Central Military Commission shall lead the work concerning reservists.  
The Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the management of reservists, the National Defense Mobilization Department of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for organizing the organization, mobilization, and recruitment of reservists, and other departments of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for work concerning reservists in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
State organs of the central level, local people's governments at or above the county level, and military organs at the same level shall properly carry out work concerning reservists in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
Units with reservists (hereinafter referred to as "units") shall be responsible for the political education, military training, task performance, and related selection and replenishment, daily management, and discharge from reserve service of the reservists under their command.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府和有关军事机关应当根据预备役人员工作需要召开军地联席会议，协调解决有关问题。  
县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关，应当将预备役人员工作情况作为拥军优属、拥政爱民评比和有关单位及其负责人考核评价的内容。

**Article 6** Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant military organs shall convene joint military-civilian meetings as required by the work concerning reservists to coordinate and resolve relevant issues.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and military organs at the same level shall include the work on reservists as part of the evaluation of supporting the military and favoring the families of military personnel and supporting the government and cherishing the people, and as part of the performance assessment of relevant units and their persons in charge.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当支持预备役人员履行预备役职责，协助做好预备役人员工作。

**Article 7** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and township people's governments and sub-district offices shall support reservists in performing their reserve duties and assist in carrying out work concerning reservists.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家加强预备役人员工作信息化建设。  
中央军事委员会政治工作部门会同中央国家机关、中央军事委员会机关有关部门，统筹做好信息数据系统的建设、维护、应用和信息安全管理等工作。  
有关部门和单位、个人应当对在预备役人员工作过程中知悉的国家秘密、军事秘密和个人隐私、个人信息予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。

**Article 8** The State shall strengthen the informatization development of work concerning reservists.  
The Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission, jointly with central state organs and relevant departments of the Central Military Commission, shall coordinate the construction, maintenance, application, and information security management of the information data system.  
Relevant departments, units, and individuals shall keep confidential the State secrets, military secrets, personal privacy, and personal information learned of in the course of work concerning reservists, and shall not disclose or illegally provide them to others.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　预备役人员工作所需经费，按照财政事权和支出责任划分原则列入中央和地方预算。

**Article 9** The funds required for work concerning reservists shall be included in the central and local budgets in accordance with the principle of division of financial powers and expenditure responsibilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　预备役人员在履行预备役职责中做出突出贡献的，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰和奖励。  
组织和个人在预备役人员工作中做出突出贡献的，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Reservists who make outstanding contributions in the performance of their reserve duties shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.  
Organizations and individuals who make outstanding contributions in work concerning reservists shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

### 第二章　预备役军衔

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家实行预备役军衔制度。  
预备役军衔是区分预备役人员等级、表明预备役人员身份的称号和标志，是党和国家给予预备役人员的地位和荣誉。

**Article 11** The State shall implement a system of reserve military ranks.  
Reserve military ranks are titles and symbols that distinguish the levels of reservists and indicate their identity, and are the status and honor granted to reservists by the Party and the State.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　预备役军衔分为预备役军官军衔、预备役军士军衔和预备役兵军衔。  
预备役军官军衔设二等七衔：  
（一）预备役校官：预备役大校、上校、中校、少校；  
（二）预备役尉官：预备役上尉、中尉、少尉。  
预备役军士军衔设三等七衔：  
（一）预备役高级军士：预备役一级军士长、二级军士长、三级军士长；  
（二）预备役中级军士：预备役一级上士、二级上士；  
（三）预备役初级军士：预备役中士、下士。  
预备役兵军衔设两衔：预备役上等兵、列兵。

**Article 12** Reserve military ranks are divided into reserve officer ranks, reserve non-commissioned officer (NCO) ranks, and reserve soldier ranks.  
Reserve officer ranks have two categories and seven grades:  
(1) Reserve field officers: Reserve Senior Colonel, Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, Major;  
(2) Reserve company officers: Reserve Captain, First Lieutenant, Second Lieutenant.  
Reserve NCO ranks have three categories and seven grades:  
(1) Reserve senior NCOs: Reserve First Class Sergeant Major, Second Class Sergeant Major, Third Class Sergeant Major;  
(2) Reserve intermediate NCOs: Reserve First Class Senior Sergeant, Second Class Senior Sergeant;  
(3) Reserve junior NCOs: Reserve Sergeant, Corporal.  
Reserve soldier ranks have two grades: Reserve Private First Class, Private.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　预备役军衔按照军种划分种类，在预备役军衔前冠以军种名称。  
预备役军官分为预备役指挥管理军官和预备役专业技术军官，分别授予预备役指挥管理军官军衔和预备役专业技术军官军衔。  
预备役军衔标志式样和佩带办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 13** Reserve military ranks are classified by service branch, and the name of the service branch shall be prefixed to the reserve military rank.  
Reserve officers are divided into reserve command and management officers and reserve professional and technical officers, who shall be awarded reserve command and management officer ranks and reserve professional and technical officer ranks respectively.  
The patterns and wearing methods of reserve military rank insignia shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　预备役军衔的授予和晋升，以预备役人员任职岗位、德才表现、服役时间和做出的贡献为依据，具体办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 14** The award and promotion of reserve military ranks shall be based on the position held, moral character and capability, length of service, and contributions made by the reservist, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　预备役人员退出预备役的，其预备役军衔予以保留，在其军衔前冠以“退役”。

**Article 15** Where a reservist is discharged from reserve service, his or her reserve military rank shall be retained, and the word "Retired" shall be prefixed to the rank.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对违反军队纪律的预备役人员，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定，可以降低其预备役军衔等级。  
依照本法规定取消预备役人员身份的，相应取消其预备役军衔；预备役人员犯罪或者退出预备役后犯罪，被依法判处剥夺政治权利或者有期徒刑以上刑罚的，应当剥夺其预备役军衔。  
批准取消或者剥夺预备役军衔的权限，与批准授予相应预备役军衔的权限相同。

**Article 16** For a reservist who violates military discipline, his or her reserve military rank may be demoted in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.  
Where the identity of a reservist is revoked in accordance with the provisions of this Law, his or her reserve military rank shall be correspondingly revoked; where a reservist commits a crime, or commits a crime after being discharged from reserve service, and is sentenced to deprivation of political rights or fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier punishment according to law, his or her reserve military rank shall be deprived.  
The authority to approve the revocation or deprivation of a reserve military rank shall be the same as the authority to approve the award of the corresponding reserve military rank.

### 第三章　选拔补充

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　预备役人员应当符合下列条件：  
（一）忠于祖国，忠于中国共产党，拥护社会主义制度，热爱人民，热爱国防和军队；  
（二）遵守宪法和法律，具有良好的政治素质和道德品行；  
（三）年满十八周岁；  
（四）具有履行职责的身体条件和心理素质；  
（五）具备岗位要求的文化程度和工作能力；  
（六）法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 17** A reservist shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) be loyal to the motherland, loyal to the Communist Party of China, support the socialist system, love the people, and love national defense and the military;  
(2) abide by the Constitution and laws, and have good political quality and moral character;  
(3) have attained the age of 18;  
(4) have the physical conditions and psychological qualities required for the performance of duties;  
(5) have the educational level and work ability required by the position;  
(6) other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　预备役人员主要从符合服预备役条件、经过预备役登记的退役军人和专业技术人才、专业技能人才中选拔补充。  
预备役登记依照《中华人民共和国兵役法》有关规定执行。

**Article 18** Reservists shall be mainly selected and replenished from among retired servicemen who meet the conditions for reserve service and have undergone reserve registration, as well as from among professional and technical personnel and skilled personnel.  
Reserve registration shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　预备役人员的选拔补充计划由中央军事委员会确定。中央军事委员会机关有关部门会同有关中央国家机关，指导部队和县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关实施。

**Article 19** The selection and replenishment plan for reservists shall be determined by the Central Military Commission. Relevant departments of the Central Military Commission, jointly with relevant central state organs, shall guide the implementation by units and the military service organs of local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　部队应当按照规定的标准条件，会同县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关遴选确定预备役人员。  
预备役人员服预备役的时间自批准服预备役之日起算。

**Article 20** Units shall, in accordance with the prescribed standards and conditions and jointly with the military service organs of local people's governments at or above the county level, select and determine reservists.  
The period of reserve service for a reservist shall be counted from the date of approval of reserve service.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关应当向部队及时、准确地提供本行政区域公民预备役登记信息，组织预备役人员选拔补充对象的政治考核、体格检查等工作，办理相关入役手续。

**Article 21** The military service organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide units with timely and accurate information on citizens' reserve registration within their administrative areas, organize political assessment, physical examination, and other work for the objects of reservist selection and replenishment, and handle relevant enlistment procedures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处，应当根据部队需要和县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关的安排，组织推荐本单位、本行政区域符合条件的人员参加预备役人员选拔补充。  
被推荐人员应当按照规定参加预备役人员选拔补充。

**Article 22** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and township people's governments and sub-district offices shall, in accordance with the needs of units and the arrangements of the military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts, organize the recommendation of persons who meet the conditions within their units or administrative areas to participate in the selection and replenishment of reservists.  
The recommended persons shall participate in the selection and replenishment of reservists in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　部队应当按照规定，对选拔补充的预备役人员授予预备役军衔、任用岗位职务。

**Article 23** Units shall, in accordance with the provisions, award reserve military ranks and appoint positions to the reservists selected and replenished.

### 第四章　教育训练和晋升任用

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　预备役人员的教育训练，坚持院校教育、训练实践、职业培训相结合，纳入国家和军队教育培训体系。  
军队和预备役人员所在单位应当按照有关规定开展预备役人员教育训练。

**Article 24** The education and training of reservists shall adhere to the combination of institutional education, training practice, and vocational training, and shall be incorporated into the education and training system of the State and the military.  
The military and the units to which reservists belong shall carry out education and training for reservists in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　预备役人员在被授予和晋升预备役军衔、任用岗位职务前，应当根据需要接受相应的教育训练。

**Article 25** Before being awarded or promoted in reserve military rank or appointed to a position, a reservist shall receive corresponding education and training as needed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　预备役人员应当按照规定参加军事训练，达到军事训练大纲规定的训练要求。  
年度军事训练时间由战区级以上军事机关根据需要确定。  
中央军事委员会可以决定对预备役人员实施临战训练，预备役人员必须按照要求接受临战训练。

**Article 26** Reservists shall participate in military training in accordance with the provisions and meet the training requirements specified in the military training outline.  
The annual military training time shall be determined by military organs at or above the theater command level as needed.  
The Central Military Commission may decide to conduct pre-combat training for reservists, and reservists must undergo pre-combat training as required.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　预备役人员在服预备役期间应当按照规定参加职业培训，提高履行预备役职责的能力。

**Article 27** During the period of reserve service, reservists shall participate in vocational training in accordance with the provisions to improve their ability to perform reserve duties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对预备役人员应当进行考核。考核工作由部队按照规定组织实施，考核结果作为其预备役军衔晋升、职务任用、待遇调整、奖励惩戒等的依据。  
预备役人员的考核结果应当通知本人和其预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关以及所在单位，并作为调整其职位、职务、职级、级别、工资和评定职称等的依据之一。

**Article 28** Assessment shall be conducted for reservists. The assessment work shall be organized and implemented by units in accordance with the provisions, and the assessment results shall serve as the basis for promotion in reserve military rank, appointment, adjustment of treatment, and reward and punishment.  
The assessment results of a reservist shall be notified to the reservist himself or herself, the military service organs of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the reservist's reserve registration is located, and the unit to which the reservist belongs, and shall serve as one of the bases for adjusting his or her position, post, rank, level, salary, and evaluating professional titles.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　预备役人员表现优秀、符合条件的，可以按照规定晋升预备役军衔、任用部队相应岗位职务。  
预备役兵服预备役满规定年限，根据军队需要和本人自愿，经批准可以选改为预备役军士。  
预备役人员任用岗位职务的批准权限由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 29** Reservists who perform excellently and meet the conditions may be promoted in reserve military rank or appointed to corresponding positions in units in accordance with the provisions.  
Reserve soldiers who have served the prescribed number of years may, with approval, be selected and transferred to become reserve non-commissioned officers based on military needs and their own volition.  
The authority to approve the appointment of reservists to positions shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

### 第五章　日常管理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　预备役人员有单位变更、迁居、出国（境）、患严重疾病、身体残疾等重要事项以及联系方式发生变化的，应当及时向部队报告。  
预备役人员有前款规定情况或者严重违纪违法、失踪、死亡的，预备役人员所在单位和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当及时报告县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关。  
部队应当与县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关建立相互通报制度，准确掌握预备役人员动态情况。

**Article 30** Where a reservist has important matters such as change of unit, change of residence, going abroad (crossing the border), contracting a serious illness, or physical disability, or change of contact information, the reservist shall promptly report to the unit.  
Where a reservist has the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph or is involved in serious violation of discipline or law, is missing, or dies, the unit to which the reservist belongs and the township people's government or sub-district office shall promptly report to the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district.  
The unit shall, together with the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district, establish a mutual notification system to accurately grasp the dynamic situation of reservists.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　预备役人员因迁居等原因需要变更预备役登记地的，相关县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关应当及时变更其预备役登记信息。

**Article 31** Where a reservist needs to change his or her reserve registration place due to relocation or other reasons, the relevant military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts shall promptly update the reserve registration information.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　预备役人员参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务等的召集，由部队通知本人，并通报其所在单位和预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关。  
召集预备役人员担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务，应当经战区级以上军事机关批准。  
预备役人员所在单位和预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关，应当协助召集预备役人员。  
预备役人员应当按照召集规定时间到指定地点报到。

**Article 32** The muster of reservists for participation in military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks shall be notified by the unit to the individual and reported to the unit to which the reservist belongs and the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the reservist's reserve registration is located.  
The muster of reservists for undertaking combat readiness duties or performing non-war military operations tasks shall be approved by a military organ at or above the theater command level.  
The unit to which the reservist belongs and the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the reservist's reserve registration is located shall assist in mustering the reservist.  
Reservists shall report to the designated location at the time specified in the muster notice.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　预备役人员参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务等期间，由部队按照军队有关规定管理。

**Article 33** During the period of participating in military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, reservists shall be managed by the unit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　预备役人员按照军队有关规定穿着预备役制式服装、佩带预备役标志服饰。  
任何单位和个人不得非法生产、买卖预备役制式服装和预备役标志服饰。

**Article 34** Reservists shall wear reserve service uniforms and wear reserve insignia and accessories in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.  
No unit or individual may illegally produce or trade in reserve service uniforms and reserve insignia and accessories.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　预备役人员应当落实军队战备工作有关规定，做好执行任务的准备。

**Article 35** Reservists shall implement the relevant provisions of the military on combat readiness work and be well prepared for performing tasks.

### 第六章　征召

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在国家发布动员令或者国务院、中央军事委员会依法采取必要的国防动员措施后，部队应当根据上级的命令，迅速向被征召的预备役人员下达征召通知，并通报其预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关和所在单位。  
预备役人员接到征召通知后，必须按照要求在规定时间到指定地点报到。国家发布动员令后，尚未接到征召通知的预备役人员，未经部队和预备役登记地兵役机关批准，不得离开预备役登记地；已经离开的，应当立即返回或者原地待命。

**Article 36** After the State issues a mobilization order or the State Council and the Central Military Commission take necessary national defense mobilization measures according to law, the unit shall, in accordance with the orders of the higher level, promptly issue call-up notices to the called-up reservists and notify the military service organs of the people's governments of the counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts where their reserve registration is located, and their units.  
Upon receiving the call-up notice, reservists must report to the designated location within the specified time in accordance with the requirements. After the State issues a mobilization order, reservists who have not yet received a call-up notice shall not leave the place of reserve registration without the approval of the unit and the military service organ of the place of reserve registration; those who have already left shall immediately return or stand by where they are.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关，预备役人员所在单位和乡镇人民政府、街道办事处，应当督促预备役人员响应征召，为预备役人员征召提供必要的支持和协助，帮助解决困难，维护预备役人员合法权益。  
从事交通运输的单位和个人应当优先运送被征召的预备役人员。  
预备役人员因被征召，诉讼、行政复议、仲裁活动不能正常进行的，适用有关时效中止和程序中止的规定，但是法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 37** The military service organs of the people's governments of counties, autonomous counties, cities not divided into districts, or municipal districts where the reserve registration is located, the unit to which the reservist belongs, and the township people's government or sub-district office shall urge reservists to respond to the call-up, provide necessary support and assistance for the call-up of reservists, help solve difficulties, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of reservists.  
Units and individuals engaged in transportation shall give priority to transporting called-up reservists.  
Where a reservist is called up and litigation, administrative reconsideration, or arbitration proceedings cannot be conducted normally, the provisions on suspension of limitation periods and suspension of proceedings shall apply, except as otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　预备役人员有下列情形之一的，经其预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关核实，并经部队批准，可以暂缓征召：  
（一）患严重疾病处于治疗期间暂时无法履行预备役职责；  
（二）家庭成员生活不能自理，且本人为唯一监护人、赡养人、扶养人，或者家庭发生重大变故必须由本人亲自处理；  
（三）女性预备役人员在孕期、产假、哺乳期内；  
（四）涉嫌严重职务违法或者职务犯罪正在被监察机关调查，或者涉嫌犯罪正在被侦查、起诉、审判；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 38** A reservist may be deferred from call-up under any of the following circumstances, after verification by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the reserve registration is located, and approval by the unit:  
(1) suffering from a serious illness and under treatment, temporarily unable to perform reserve duties;  
(2) family members are unable to take care of themselves and the reservist is the sole guardian, supporter, or provider, or a major family incident occurs that must be handled by the reservist personally;  
(3) female reservists are in pregnancy, maternity leave, or breastfeeding period;  
(4) suspected of serious duty-related illegal activity or duty-related crime and under investigation by supervisory organs, or suspected of a crime and under investigation, prosecution, or adjudication;  
(5) other circumstances prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　被征召的预备役人员，根据军队有关规定转服现役。  
预备役人员转服现役，由其预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关办理入伍手续。预备役人员转服现役的，按照有关规定改授相应军衔、任用相应岗位职务，履行军人职责。

**Article 39** Called-up reservists shall transfer to active service in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.  
The transfer of a reservist to active service shall be processed by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the reserve registration is located. Where a reservist transfers to active service, he or she shall be conferred corresponding ranks and appointed to corresponding positions in accordance with the relevant provisions, and shall perform the duties of a serviceman.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家解除国防动员后，由预备役人员转服现役的军人需要退出现役的，按照军人退出现役的有关规定由各级人民政府妥善安置。被征召的预备役人员未转服现役的，部队应当安排其返回，并通知其预备役登记地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关和所在单位。

**Article 40** After the State lifts the national defense mobilization, servicemen who have transferred from reservists to active service and need to be discharged from active service shall be properly resettled by people's governments at all levels in accordance with the relevant provisions on discharge from active service. For called-up reservists who have not transferred to active service, the unit shall arrange for their return and notify the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where their reserve registration is located and their unit.

### 第七章　待遇保障

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家建立激励与补偿相结合的预备役人员津贴补贴制度。  
预备役人员按照规定享受服役津贴；参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，按照规定享受任务津贴。  
预备役人员参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，按照规定享受相应补贴和伙食、交通等补助；其中，预备役人员是机关、团体、企业事业组织工作人员的，所在单位应当保持其原有的工资、奖金、福利和保险等待遇。  
预备役人员津贴补贴的标准及其调整办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 41** The State shall establish a system of allowances and subsidies for reservists that combines incentives and compensation.  
Reservists shall enjoy service allowances in accordance with the provisions; during periods of participation in combat, military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, they shall enjoy task allowances in accordance with the provisions.  
During periods of participation in combat, military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, reservists shall enjoy corresponding subsidies and allowances for meals, transportation, etc. in accordance with the provisions; where a reservist is a staff member of a state organ, organization, enterprise, or public institution, the unit to which he or she belongs shall maintain his or her original wages, bonuses, welfare, and insurance benefits.  
The standards for reservists' allowances and subsidies and the methods for their adjustment shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　预备役人员参战，享受军人同等医疗待遇；参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，按照规定享受国家和军队相应医疗待遇。  
军队医疗机构按照规定为预备役人员提供优先就医等服务。

**Article 42** Reservists who participate in combat shall enjoy the same medical treatment as servicemen; during periods of participating in military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, they shall enjoy corresponding medical treatment from the State and the military in accordance with the provisions.  
Military medical institutions shall provide reservists with priority medical services in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　预备役人员参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，军队为其购买人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 43** During periods of participating in military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, the military shall purchase personal accident insurance for reservists.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　预备役人员参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，其家庭因自然灾害、意外事故、重大疾病等原因，基本生活出现严重困难的，地方人民政府和部队应当按照有关规定给予救助和慰问。  
国家鼓励和支持人民团体、企业事业组织、社会组织和其他组织以及个人，为困难预备役人员家庭提供援助服务。

**Article 44** Where, during periods of a reservist's participation in combat, military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, the reservist's family encounters serious difficulties in basic living due to natural disasters, accidents, serious illnesses, or other reasons, the local people's government and the unit shall provide relief and condolences in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
The State encourages and supports people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations, as well as individuals, to provide assistance services to the families of reservists in difficulty.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　预备役人员所在单位不得因预备役人员履行预备役职责，对其作出辞退、解聘或者解除劳动关系、免职、降低待遇、处分等处理。

**Article 45** The unit to which a reservist belongs shall not dismiss, terminate, or dissolve the labor relationship with, remove from office, reduce treatment, or impose sanctions on the reservist because of the reservist's performance of reserve duties.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　预备役人员所在单位按照国家有关规定享受优惠和扶持政策。  
预备役人员创办小微企业、从事个体经营等活动，可以按照国家有关规定享受融资优惠等政策。

**Article 46** The unit to which a reservist belongs shall enjoy preferential and supportive policies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Reservists who establish small micro enterprises or engage in self-employment activities may enjoy policies such as preferential financing in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　预备役人员按照规定享受优待。  
预备役人员因参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务伤亡的，由县级以上地方人民政府按照国家有关规定给予抚恤。

**Article 47** Reservists shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the provisions.  
Where a reservist is injured or killed due to participation in combat, military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations tasks, the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　预备役人员被授予和晋升预备役军衔，获得功勋荣誉表彰，以及退出预备役时，部队应当举行仪式。

**Article 48** When a reservist is awarded or promoted in reserve military rank, receives an award for meritorious service or an honor, or is discharged from reserve service, the unit shall hold a ceremony.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　女性预备役人员的合法权益受法律保护。部队应当根据女性预备役人员的特点，合理安排女性预备役人员的岗位和任务。

**Article 49** The lawful rights and interests of female reservists shall be protected by law. Units shall, based on the characteristics of female reservists, reasonably arrange positions and tasks for female reservists.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　预备役人员退出预备役后，按照规定享受相应的荣誉和待遇。

**Article 50** After being discharged from reserve service, reservists shall enjoy corresponding honors and benefits in accordance with the provisions.

### 第八章　退出预备役

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　预备役军官、预备役军士在本衔级服预备役的最低年限为四年。  
预备役军官、预备役军士服预备役未满本衔级最低年限的，不得申请退出预备役；满最低年限的，本人提出申请、经批准可以退出预备役。  
预备役兵服预备役年限为四年，其中，预备役列兵、上等兵各为二年。预备役兵服预备役未满四年的，不得申请退出预备役。预备役兵服预备役满四年未被选改为预备役军士的，应当退出预备役。

**Article 51** The minimum period of reserve service for reserve officers and reserve non-commissioned officers at their respective ranks shall be four years.  
A reserve officer or reserve non-commissioned officer who has not completed the minimum period of reserve service at his or her rank shall not apply for discharge from reserve service; upon completion of the minimum period, he or she may be discharged from reserve service upon application and approval.  
The period of reserve service for reserve soldiers shall be four years, of which the periods for reserve privates and reserve privates first class shall each be two years. A reserve soldier who has not completed four years of reserve service shall not apply for discharge from reserve service. A reserve soldier who has completed four years of reserve service and has not been selected for re-designation as a reserve non-commissioned officer shall be discharged from reserve service.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　预备役人员服预备役达到最高年龄的，应当退出预备役。预备役人员服预备役的最高年龄：  
（一）预备役指挥管理军官：预备役尉官为四十五周岁，预备役校官为六十周岁；  
（二）预备役专业技术军官：预备役尉官为五十周岁，预备役校官为六十周岁；  
（三）预备役军士：预备役下士、中士、二级上士均为四十五周岁，预备役一级上士、三级军士长、二级军士长、一级军士长均为五十五周岁；  
（四）预备役兵为三十周岁。

**Article 52** A reservist who has reached the maximum age for reserve service shall be discharged from reserve service. The maximum ages for reserve service are as follows:  
(1) For reserve command and administrative officers: reserve junior officers at forty-five years of age, and reserve field-grade officers at sixty years of age;  
(2) For reserve technical and professional officers: reserve junior officers at fifty years of age, and reserve field-grade officers at sixty years of age;  
(3) For reserve non-commissioned officers: reserve sergeants, staff sergeants, and second-class sergeants each at forty-five years of age, and first-class sergeants, third-class master sergeants, second-class master sergeants, and first-class master sergeants each at fifty-five years of age;  
(4) Reserve soldiers at thirty years of age.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　预备役军官、预备役军士服预备役未满本衔级最低年限或者未达到最高年龄，预备役兵服预备役未满规定年限或者未达到最高年龄，有下列情形之一的，应当安排退出预备役：  
（一）被征集或者选拔补充服现役的；  
（二）因军队体制编制调整改革或者优化预备役人员队伍结构需要退出的；  
（三）因所在单位或者岗位变更等原因，不适合继续服预备役的；  
（四）因伤病残无法履行预备役职责的；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 53** Where a reserve officer or reserve non-commissioned officer has not completed the minimum period of reserve service at his or her rank or has not reached the maximum age, or a reserve soldier has not completed the prescribed period of reserve service or has not reached the maximum age, and any of the following circumstances exists, he or she shall be arranged to be discharged from reserve service:  
(1) He or she is conscripted or selected for active service;  
(2) He or she needs to be discharged due to adjustments and reforms in the military's organizational structure or optimization of the reserve personnel structure;  
(3) He or she is no longer suitable for continued reserve service due to changes in his or her unit or position;  
(4) He or she is unable to perform reserve duties due to injury, illness, or disability;  
(5) Other circumstances provided for by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　预备役军官、预备役军士服预备役满本衔级最低年限或者达到最高年龄，预备役兵服预备役满规定年限或者达到最高年龄，有下列情形之一的，不得退出预备役：  
（一）国家发布动员令或者国务院、中央军事委员会依法采取国防动员措施要求的；  
（二）正在参战或者担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务的；  
（三）涉嫌违反军队纪律正在接受审查或者调查、尚未作出结论的；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他情形。  
前款规定的情形消失的，预备役人员可以提出申请，经批准后退出预备役。

**Article 54** Where a reserve officer or reserve non-commissioned officer has completed the minimum period of reserve service at his or her rank or reached the maximum age, or a reserve soldier has completed the prescribed period of reserve service or reached the maximum age, and any of the following circumstances exists, he or she shall not be discharged from reserve service:  
(1) A mobilization order is issued by the state, or the State Council or the Central Military Commission takes measures for national defense mobilization according to law;  
(2) He or she is engaged in combat or undertaking combat readiness duties or performing non-war military operations;  
(3) He or she is suspected of violating military discipline and is undergoing review or investigation, with no conclusion yet reached;  
(4) Other circumstances provided for by laws or regulations.  
If the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph cease to exist, the reservist may apply and, upon approval, be discharged from reserve service.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　预备役人员有下列情形之一的，应当取消预备役人员身份：  
（一）预备役军官、预备役军士服预备役未满本衔级最低年限，预备役兵服预备役未满规定年限，本人要求提前退出预备役，经教育仍坚持退出预备役的；  
（二）连续两年部队考核不称职的；  
（三）因犯罪被追究刑事责任的；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 55** A reservist shall have his or her reservist status revoked if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) A reserve officer or reserve non-commissioned officer has not completed the minimum period of reserve service at his or her rank, or a reserve soldier has not completed the prescribed period of reserve service, and he or she requests early discharge from reserve service and persists in such discharge despite education;  
(2) He or she has been assessed as incompetent by the military for two consecutive years;  
(3) He or she has been investigated for criminal liability due to a crime;  
(4) Other circumstances provided for by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　预备役人员退出预备役的时间为下达退出预备役命令之日。

**Article 56** The time of discharge of a reservist from reserve service shall be the date on which the order for discharge from reserve service is issued.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　批准预备役人员退出预备役的权限，与批准晋升相应预备役军衔的权限相同。

**Article 57** The authority to approve a reservist's discharge from reserve service shall be the same as the authority to approve the promotion to the corresponding reserve rank.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　经过预备役登记的公民拒绝、逃避参加预备役人员选拔补充的，预备役人员拒绝、逃避参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务和征召的，由县级人民政府责令限期改正；逾期不改的，由县级人民政府强制其履行兵役义务，并处以罚款；属于公职人员的，还应当依法给予处分。  
预备役人员有前款规定行为的，部队应当按照有关规定停止其相关待遇。

**Article 58** If a citizen who has registered for reserve service refuses or evades participation in the selection and replenishment of reservists, or if a reservist refuses or evades participation in military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, performing non-war military operations, or being summoned, the people's government of the county level shall order him or her to rectify within a specified period; if he or she fails to rectify within the specified period, the people's government of the county level shall compel him or her to perform military service obligations and impose a fine; if he or she is a public official, a sanction shall also be imposed according to law.  
If a reservist commits an act specified in the preceding paragraph, the military shall suspend his or her relevant benefits in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　预备役人员参战、参加军事训练、担负战备勤务、执行非战争军事行动任务期间，违反纪律的，由部队按照有关规定给予处分。

**Article 59** If a reservist violates discipline during combat, military training, undertaking combat readiness duties, or performing non-war military operations, the military shall impose a sanction on him or her in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家机关及其工作人员、军队单位及其工作人员在预备役人员工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者有其他违反本法规定行为的，由其所在单位、主管部门或者上级机关责令改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 60** If a state organ or its staff member, or a military unit or its staff member, abuses power, neglects duty, engages in irregularities for personal gain, or commits any other act violating the provisions of this Law in the work concerning reservists, the unit to which he or she belongs, the competent department, or the superior organ shall order rectification; the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be sanctioned according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　机关、团体、企业事业组织拒绝完成本法规定的预备役人员工作任务的，阻挠公民履行预备役义务的，或者有其他妨害预备役人员工作行为的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令改正，并可以处以罚款；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分、处罚。  
非法生产、买卖预备役制式服装和预备役标志服饰的，依法予以处罚。

**Article 61** If an organ, organization, enterprise, or public institution refuses to complete the tasks concerning reservists as provided for in this Law, obstructs a citizen from performing the obligation of reserve service, or commits any other act hindering the work concerning reservists, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and may impose a fine; the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be sanctioned or penalized according to law.  
Anyone who illegally produces or deals in reserve service uniforms and insignia shall be penalized according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Any violation of the provisions of this Law that constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　[本法第五十八条](#art-58)、第六十一条第一款规定的处罚，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关会同有关部门查明事实，经同级地方人民政府作出处罚决定后，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关和有关部门按照职责分工具体执行。

**Article 63** The penalties provided for in Articles 58 and 61, paragraph 1 of this Law shall be imposed by the military service authority of the local people's government at or above the county level, together with the relevant departments, after ascertaining the facts; after the local people's government at the same level makes a decision on the penalty, the military service authority of the local people's government at or above the county level and the relevant departments shall specifically implement the penalty in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　中国人民武装警察部队退出现役的人员服预备役的，适用本法。

**Article 64** This Law shall apply to persons who have been discharged from active service in the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and serve in the reserve service.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2023年3月1日起施行。《中华人民共和国预备役军官法》同时废止。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2023. The Reserve Officers Law of the People's Republic of China shall be abolished simultaneously.
