### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治洪水，防御、减轻洪涝灾害，维护人民的生命和财产安全，保障社会主义现代化建设顺利进行，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of preventing and controlling floods, defending against and mitigating flood and waterlogging disasters, safeguarding the lives and property of the people, and ensuring the smooth progress of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　防洪工作实行全面规划、统筹兼顾、预防为主、综合治理、局部利益服从全局利益的原则。

**Article 2** The flood prevention work shall follow the principles of comprehensive planning, overall consideration, giving priority to prevention, comprehensive control, and subordinating local interests to overall interests.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　防洪工程设施建设，应当纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
防洪费用按照政府投入同受益者合理承担相结合的原则筹集。

**Article 3** The construction of flood prevention engineering facilities shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plan.  
Flood prevention funds shall be raised in accordance with the principle of combining government investment with reasonable contribution by beneficiaries.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　开发利用和保护水资源，应当服从防洪总体安排，实行兴利与除害相结合的原则。  
江河、湖泊治理以及防洪工程设施建设，应当符合流域综合规划，与流域水资源的综合开发相结合。  
本法所称综合规划是指开发利用水资源和防治水害的综合规划。

**Article 4** The development, utilization and protection of water resources shall be subject to the overall arrangements for flood prevention, and the principle of combining beneficial uses with hazard elimination shall be implemented.  
The regulation of rivers and lakes, as well as the construction of flood prevention engineering facilities, shall conform to the comprehensive plan of the river basin and be combined with the comprehensive development of water resources in the river basin.  
The comprehensive plan referred to in this Law means the comprehensive plan for the development and utilization of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　防洪工作按照流域或者区域实行统一规划、分级实施和流域管理与行政区域管理相结合的制度。

**Article 5** The flood prevention work shall adopt a system of unified planning, graded implementation, and combination of river basin management with administrative regional management, according to river basins or regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人都有保护防洪工程设施和依法参加防汛抗洪的义务。

**Article 6** Every unit and individual shall have the obligation to protect flood prevention engineering facilities and to participate in flood prevention and flood fighting according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当加强对防洪工作的统一领导，组织有关部门、单位，动员社会力量，依靠科技进步，有计划地进行江河、湖泊治理，采取措施加强防洪工程设施建设，巩固、提高防洪能力。  
各级人民政府应当组织有关部门、单位，动员社会力量，做好防汛抗洪和洪涝灾害后的恢复与救济工作。  
各级人民政府应当对蓄滞洪区予以扶持；蓄滞洪后，应当依照国家规定予以补偿或者救助。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the unified leadership over flood prevention work, organize relevant departments and units, mobilize social forces, rely on scientific and technological progress, carry out the regulation of rivers and lakes in a planned way, take measures to strengthen the construction of flood prevention engineering facilities, and consolidate and enhance flood prevention capabilities.  
People's governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments and units, mobilize social forces, and do a good job in flood prevention and flood fighting, as well as in the recovery and relief work after flood and waterlogging disasters.  
People's governments at all levels shall support flood storage and detention areas; after flood storage and detention, compensation or assistance shall be provided in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院水行政主管部门在国务院的领导下，负责全国防洪的组织、协调、监督、指导等日常工作。国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构，在所管辖的范围内行使法律、行政法规规定和国务院水行政主管部门授权的防洪协调和监督管理职责。  
国务院建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门在国务院的领导下，按照各自的职责，负责有关的防洪工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门在本级人民政府的领导下，负责本行政区域内防洪的组织、协调、监督、指导等日常工作。县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门在本级人民政府的领导下，按照各自的职责，负责有关的防洪工作。

**Article 8** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, under the leadership of the State Council, be responsible for the daily work of organizing, coordinating, supervising and guiding flood prevention throughout the country. The river basin management agencies established by the water administrative department of the State Council on the key rivers and lakes designated by the State shall, within the scope of their jurisdiction, exercise the functions of flood prevention coordination, supervision and management as provided by laws and administrative regulations and as authorized by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The construction administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments shall, under the leadership of the State Council, be responsible for the relevant flood prevention work according to their respective functions.  
The water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the leadership of the people's governments at the same level, be responsible for the daily work of organizing, coordinating, supervising and guiding flood prevention within their respective administrative regions. The construction administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, under the leadership of the people's governments at the same level, be responsible for the relevant flood prevention work according to their respective functions.

### 第二章　防洪规划

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　防洪规划是指为防治某一流域、河段或者区域的洪涝灾害而制定的总体部署，包括国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的流域防洪规划，其他江河、河段、湖泊的防洪规划以及区域防洪规划。  
防洪规划应当服从所在流域、区域的综合规划；区域防洪规划应当服从所在流域的流域防洪规划。  
防洪规划是江河、湖泊治理和防洪工程设施建设的基本依据。

**Article 9** Flood prevention planning means the overall plan formulated for preventing and controlling flood and waterlogging disasters in a certain river basin, river reach or region, including the river basin flood prevention plans for the key rivers and lakes designated by the State, the flood prevention plans for other rivers, river reaches and lakes, and the regional flood prevention plans.  
Flood prevention planning shall be subject to the comprehensive plan of the river basin or region where it is located; a regional flood prevention plan shall be subject to the river basin flood prevention plan of the river basin where it is located.  
Flood prevention planning shall be the basic basis for the regulation of rivers and lakes and the construction of flood prevention engineering facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的防洪规划，由国务院水行政主管部门依据该江河、湖泊的流域综合规划，会同有关部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制，报国务院批准。  
其他江河、河段、湖泊的防洪规划或者区域防洪规划，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门分别依据流域综合规划、区域综合规划，会同有关部门和有关地区编制，报本级人民政府批准，并报上一级人民政府水行政主管部门备案；跨省、自治区、直辖市的江河、河段、湖泊的防洪规划由有关流域管理机构会同江河、河段、湖泊所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门、有关主管部门拟定，分别经有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查提出意见后，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。  
城市防洪规划，由城市人民政府组织水行政主管部门、建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门依据流域防洪规划、上一级人民政府区域防洪规划编制，按照国务院规定的审批程序批准后纳入城市总体规划。  
修改防洪规划，应当报经原批准机关批准。

**Article 10** The flood prevention plans for the key rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be prepared by the water administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the comprehensive river basin plans of such rivers and lakes, in consultation with the relevant departments and the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
Flood prevention plans for other rivers, river reaches and lakes or regional flood prevention plans shall be prepared by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the comprehensive river basin plans or regional comprehensive plans respectively, in consultation with the relevant departments and regions, submitted to the people's governments at the same level for approval, and reported to the water administrative departments of the people's governments at the next higher level for recordation. Flood prevention plans for rivers, river reaches and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be drafted by the relevant river basin management agencies in consultation with the water administrative departments and relevant competent departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where such rivers, river reaches and lakes are located, and after being examined and commented on by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval.  
Urban flood prevention plans shall be prepared by the urban people's governments in conjunction with the water administrative departments, construction administrative departments and other relevant departments in accordance with the river basin flood prevention plans and the regional flood prevention plans of the people's governments at the next higher level, and after being approved in accordance with the approval procedures prescribed by the State Council, shall be incorporated into the overall urban plan.  
Modifications to flood prevention plans shall be subject to approval by the original approving authority.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　编制防洪规划，应当遵循确保重点、兼顾一般，以及防汛和抗旱相结合、工程措施和非工程措施相结合的原则，充分考虑洪涝规律和上下游、左右岸的关系以及国民经济对防洪的要求，并与国土规划和土地利用总体规划相协调。  
防洪规划应当确定防护对象、治理目标和任务、防洪措施和实施方案，划定洪泛区、蓄滞洪区和防洪保护区的范围，规定蓄滞洪区的使用原则。

**Article 11** In preparing flood prevention plans, the principles of ensuring key areas, taking into account general areas, combining flood prevention with drought relief, and combining engineering measures with non-engineering measures shall be followed. The laws of flood and waterlogging, the relationships between upstream and downstream and between left and right banks, and the requirements of the national economy for flood prevention shall be fully considered, and the plans shall be coordinated with the national land plan and the overall land use plan.  
Flood prevention plans shall determine the objects of protection, the objectives and tasks of control, flood prevention measures and implementation plans, delimit the scope of floodplains, flood storage and detention areas and flood protection zones, and prescribe the principles for the use of flood storage and detention areas.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　受风暴潮威胁的沿海地区的县级以上地方人民政府，应当把防御风暴潮纳入本地区的防洪规划，加强海堤（海塘）、挡潮闸和沿海防护林等防御风暴潮工程体系建设，监督建筑物、构筑物的设计和施工符合防御风暴潮的需要。

**Article 12** Local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas threatened by storm surges shall incorporate the prevention of storm surges into the flood prevention plans of their regions, strengthen the construction of storm surge prevention engineering systems such as seawalls (sea banks), tidal barriers and coastal shelter forests, and supervise the design and construction of buildings and structures to meet the requirements of storm surge prevention.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　山洪可能诱发山体滑坡、崩塌和泥石流的地区以及其他山洪多发地区的县级以上地方人民政府，应当组织负责地质矿产管理工作的部门、水行政主管部门和其他有关部门对山体滑坡、崩塌和泥石流隐患进行全面调查，划定重点防治区，采取防治措施。  
城市、村镇和其他居民点以及工厂、矿山、铁路和公路干线的布局，应当避开山洪威胁；已经建在受山洪威胁的地方的，应当采取防御措施。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where mountain torrents may induce landslides, collapses and debris flows, as well as other areas prone to mountain torrents, shall organize the departments responsible for geological and mineral resources management, the water administrative departments and other relevant departments to conduct comprehensive investigations of potential dangers of landslides, collapses and debris flows, delineate key prevention and control areas, and adopt prevention and control measures.  
The layout of cities, towns and villages, other settlements, as well as factories, mines, railways and main highways, shall avoid the threat of mountain torrents; those already built in places threatened by mountain torrents shall adopt prevention measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　平原、洼地、水网圩区、山谷、盆地等易涝地区的有关地方人民政府，应当制定除涝治涝规划，组织有关部门、单位采取相应的治理措施，完善排水系统，发展耐涝农作物种类和品种，开展洪涝、干旱、盐碱综合治理。  
城市人民政府应当加强对城区排涝管网、泵站的建设和管理。

**Article 14** The relevant local people's governments in waterlogging-prone areas such as plains, depressions, water network polder areas, valleys and basins shall formulate plans for waterlogging elimination and control, organize relevant departments and units to adopt corresponding control measures, improve drainage systems, develop waterlogging-tolerant crop species and varieties, and carry out comprehensive control of flood, waterlogging, drought and salinization.  
The people's governments of cities shall strengthen the construction and management of urban drainage pipe networks and pumping stations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定长江、黄河、珠江、辽河、淮河、海河入海河口的整治规划。  
在前款入海河口围海造地，应当符合河口整治规划。

**Article 15** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments and the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate regulation plans for the estuaries of the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, the Pearl River, the Liaohe River, the Huaihe River and the Haihe River.  
Reclamation of land from the sea at the estuaries mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall conform to the estuary regulation plans.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　防洪规划确定的河道整治计划用地和规划建设的堤防用地范围内的土地，经土地管理部门和水行政主管部门会同有关地区核定，报经县级以上人民政府按照国务院规定的权限批准后，可以划定为规划保留区；该规划保留区范围内的土地涉及其他项目用地的，有关土地管理部门和水行政主管部门核定时，应当征求有关部门的意见。  
规划保留区依照前款规定划定后，应当公告。  
前款规划保留区内不得建设与防洪无关的工矿工程设施；在特殊情况下，国家工矿建设项目确需占用前款规划保留区内的土地的，应当按照国家规定的基本建设程序报请批准，并征求有关水行政主管部门的意见。  
防洪规划确定的扩大或者开辟的人工排洪道用地范围内的土地，经省级以上人民政府土地管理部门和水行政主管部门会同有关部门、有关地区核定，报省级以上人民政府按照国务院规定的权限批准后，可以划定为规划保留区，适用前款规定。

**Article 16** Land within the planned land for river channel regulation and the planned land for levee construction as determined in flood prevention plans, after being verified by the land administrative departments and water administrative departments in conjunction with the relevant regions and approved by the people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council, may be designated as planned reserved areas. Where the land within such planned reserved areas involves land for other projects, the relevant land administrative departments and water administrative departments shall, when verifying, seek opinions from the relevant departments.  
After planned reserved areas are designated in accordance with the preceding paragraph, a public announcement shall be made.  
No industrial and mining engineering facilities unrelated to flood prevention may be constructed within the planned reserved areas mentioned in the preceding paragraph; under special circumstances, if a state industrial and mining construction project truly needs to occupy land within the planned reserved areas mentioned in the preceding paragraph, it shall apply for approval in accordance with the capital construction procedures prescribed by the state, and seek opinions from the relevant water administrative departments.  
Land within the planned land for expanding or opening new artificial flood channels as determined in flood prevention plans, after being verified by the land administrative departments and water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level in conjunction with the relevant departments and regions, and approved by the people's governments at or above the provincial level in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council, may be designated as planned reserved areas, and the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在江河、湖泊上建设防洪工程和其他水工程、水电站等，应当符合防洪规划的要求；水库应当按照防洪规划的要求留足防洪库容。  
前款规定的防洪工程和其他水工程、水电站未取得有关水行政主管部门签署的符合防洪规划要求的规划同意书的，建设单位不得开工建设。

**Article 17** The construction of flood prevention works and other hydraulic projects, hydropower stations, etc., on rivers and lakes shall meet the requirements of flood prevention plans; reservoirs shall reserve sufficient flood control storage capacity in accordance with the requirements of flood prevention plans.  
Where the flood prevention works and other hydraulic projects, hydropower stations, etc., as specified in the preceding paragraph, have not obtained a planning consent letter signed by the relevant water administrative department confirming compliance with the requirements of flood prevention plans, the construction unit shall not commence construction.

### 第三章　治理与防护

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　防治江河洪水，应当蓄泄兼施，充分发挥河道行洪能力和水库、洼淀、湖泊调蓄洪水的功能，加强河道防护，因地制宜地采取定期清淤疏浚等措施，保持行洪畅通。  
防治江河洪水，应当保护、扩大流域林草植被，涵养水源，加强流域水土保持综合治理。

**Article 18** In preventing and controlling river floods, both storage and release shall be employed, giving full play to the flood discharge capacity of river channels and the flood regulation and storage functions of reservoirs, depressions and lakes. River channel protection shall be strengthened, and measures such as regular dredging shall be taken according to local conditions to maintain smooth flood discharge.  
In preventing and controlling river floods, forest and grass vegetation in river basins shall be protected and expanded, water sources shall be conserved, and comprehensive soil and water conservation control in river basins shall be strengthened.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　整治河道和修建控制引导河水流向、保护堤岸等工程，应当兼顾上下游、左右岸的关系，按照规划治导线实施，不得任意改变河水流向。  
国家确定的重要江河的规划治导线由流域管理机构拟定，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。  
其他江河、河段的规划治导线由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门拟定，报本级人民政府批准；跨省、自治区、直辖市的江河、河段和省、自治区、直辖市之间的省界河道的规划治导线由有关流域管理机构组织江河、河段所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门拟定，经有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查提出意见后，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。

**Article 19** The regulation of river channels and the construction of works for controlling and guiding the flow of rivers and protecting banks and embankments shall take into account the relationship between upstream and downstream and between left and right banks, be carried out in accordance with the planned regulation alignment, and shall not arbitrarily change the flow direction of the river.  
The planned regulation alignment of key rivers designated by the State shall be drafted by the river basin management agencies and submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval.  
The planned regulation alignment of other rivers and river reaches shall be drafted by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and submitted to the people's governments at the same level for approval. The planned regulation alignment of rivers and river reaches crossing provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as provincial boundary channels between provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall be drafted by the relevant river basin management agencies in conjunction with the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where such rivers and river reaches are located, and after being examined and commented on by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　整治河道、湖泊，涉及航道的，应当兼顾航运需要，并事先征求交通主管部门的意见。整治航道，应当符合江河、湖泊防洪安全要求，并事先征求水行政主管部门的意见。  
在竹木流放的河流和渔业水域整治河道的，应当兼顾竹木水运和渔业发展的需要，并事先征求林业、渔业行政主管部门的意见。在河道中流放竹木，不得影响行洪和防洪工程设施的安全。

**Article 20** Regulation of river channels and lakes involving navigation channels shall take into account the needs of navigation, and opinions of the competent transport department shall be sought in advance. Regulation of navigation channels shall meet the flood prevention safety requirements of rivers and lakes, and opinions of the water administrative departments shall be sought in advance.  
Where river channels are regulated in rivers used for bamboo and timber drifting and in fishery waters, the needs of bamboo and timber water transport and fishery development shall be taken into account, and opinions of the competent forestry and fishery administrative departments shall be sought in advance. Drifting bamboo and timber in river channels shall not affect the safety of flood discharge and flood prevention engineering facilities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　河道、湖泊管理实行按水系统一管理和分级管理相结合的原则，加强防护，确保畅通。  
国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的主要河段，跨省、自治区、直辖市的重要河段、湖泊，省、自治区、直辖市之间的省界河道、湖泊以及国（边）界河道、湖泊，由流域管理机构和江河、湖泊所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门按照国务院水行政主管部门的划定依法实施管理。其他河道、湖泊，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照国务院水行政主管部门或者国务院水行政主管部门授权的机构的划定依法实施管理。  
有堤防的河道、湖泊，其管理范围为两岸堤防之间的水域、沙洲、滩地、行洪区和堤防及护堤地；无堤防的河道、湖泊，其管理范围为历史最高洪水位或者设计洪水位之间的水域、沙洲、滩地和行洪区。  
流域管理机构直接管理的河道、湖泊管理范围，由流域管理机构会同有关县级以上地方人民政府依照前款规定界定；其他河道、湖泊管理范围，由有关县级以上地方人民政府依照前款规定界定。

**Article 21** The administration of rivers and lakes shall combine unified administration by water systems with graded administration, and protection shall be strengthened to ensure unobstructed flow.  
The main reaches of major rivers and lakes determined by the State, the major reaches and lakes crossing provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the rivers and lakes forming provincial boundaries between provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and boundary (frontier) rivers and lakes shall be managed according to law by the river basin management authorities and the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the rivers and lakes are located, within the delimitations designated by the water administrative department of the State Council. Other rivers and lakes shall be managed according to law by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level, within the delimitations designated by the water administrative department of the State Council or an agency authorized by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
For rivers and lakes with dikes, the administration scope shall comprise the water areas, sandbars, floodplains, flood discharge zones, dikes, and dike protection zones between the two dikes; for rivers and lakes without dikes, the administration scope shall comprise the water areas, sandbars, floodplains, and flood discharge zones within the historical highest flood level or the design flood level.  
The administration scope of rivers and lakes directly managed by river basin management authorities shall be defined by such authorities jointly with the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph; the administration scope of other rivers and lakes shall be defined by the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　河道、湖泊管理范围内的土地和岸线的利用，应当符合行洪、输水的要求。  
禁止在河道、湖泊管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物，倾倒垃圾、渣土，从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动。  
禁止在行洪河道内种植阻碍行洪的林木和高秆作物。  
在船舶航行可能危及堤岸安全的河段，应当限定航速。限定航速的标志，由交通主管部门与水行政主管部门商定后设置。

**Article 22** The use of land and shorelines within the administration scope of rivers and lakes shall meet the requirements for flood discharge and water conveyance.  
It shall be prohibited to construct buildings or structures obstructing flood discharge, to dump garbage or slag, or to engage in activities affecting river regime stability, endangering dike safety, or otherwise obstructing flood discharge within the administration scope of rivers and lakes.  
It shall be prohibited to plant trees or tall crops that obstruct flood discharge in flood discharge channels.  
On river reaches where navigation may endanger dike safety, the navigation speed shall be limited. The signs for speed limits shall be set up by the competent transport department after consultation with the water administrative department.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止围湖造地。已经围垦的，应当按照国家规定的防洪标准进行治理，有计划地退地还湖。  
禁止围垦河道。确需围垦的，应当进行科学论证，经水行政主管部门确认不妨碍行洪、输水后，报省级以上人民政府批准。

**Article 23** It shall be prohibited to reclaim land from lakes. Where reclamation has already been carried out, such areas shall be treated in accordance with the State-prescribed flood control standards and shall be systematically restored to lakes by retreating the land.  
It shall be prohibited to reclaim land from rivers. Where reclamation is truly necessary, a scientific demonstration shall be conducted, and upon confirmation by the water administrative department that it does not obstruct flood discharge or water conveyance, the matter shall be reported to the people's government at or above the provincial level for approval.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对居住在行洪河道内的居民，当地人民政府应当有计划地组织外迁。

**Article 24** The local people's government shall organize the systematic relocation of residents living within flood discharge channels.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　护堤护岸的林木，由河道、湖泊管理机构组织营造和管理。护堤护岸林木，不得任意砍伐。采伐护堤护岸林木的，应当依法办理采伐许可手续，并完成规定的更新补种任务。

**Article 25** Trees planted for dike and bank protection shall be cultivated and managed by the river and lake administration authorities. Trees for dike and bank protection shall not be felled arbitrarily. Where felling of such trees is carried out, the felling permit procedures shall be completed according to law, and the prescribed tasks of renewal and supplementary planting shall be fulfilled.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对壅水、阻水严重的桥梁、引道、码头和其他跨河工程设施，根据防洪标准，有关水行政主管部门可以报请县级以上人民政府按照国务院规定的权限责令建设单位限期改建或者拆除。

**Article 26** Where bridges, approach roads, wharves, or other river-crossing engineering facilities cause serious backwater or obstruction of flow, the relevant water administrative department may, in accordance with the flood control standards, report to the people's government at or above the county level to order the construction entity to renovate or demolish such facilities within a time limit, as prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　建设跨河、穿河、穿堤、临河的桥梁、码头、道路、渡口、管道、缆线、取水、排水等工程设施，应当符合防洪标准、岸线规划、航运要求和其他技术要求，不得危害堤防安全、影响河势稳定、妨碍行洪畅通；其工程建设方案未经有关水行政主管部门根据前述防洪要求审查同意的，建设单位不得开工建设。  
前款工程设施需要占用河道、湖泊管理范围内土地，跨越河道、湖泊空间或者穿越河床的，建设单位应当经有关水行政主管部门对该工程设施建设的位置和界限审查批准后，方可依法办理开工手续；安排施工时，应当按照水行政主管部门审查批准的位置和界限进行。

**Article 27** The construction of engineering facilities such as bridges, docks, roads, ferries, pipelines, cables, water intakes, and drainage works that cross rivers, pass through rivers, pass through dikes, or are adjacent to rivers shall meet the flood control standards, shoreline plans, navigation requirements, and other technical requirements, and shall not endanger dike safety, affect river regime stability, or obstruct flood discharge; the construction entity shall not commence construction unless the engineering construction plan has been examined and approved by the relevant water administrative department in accordance with the aforesaid flood control requirements.  
Where the engineering facilities mentioned in the preceding paragraph need to occupy land within the administration scope of rivers and lakes, span the space of rivers and lakes, or pass through riverbeds, the construction entity shall, after the relevant water administrative department has examined and approved the location and boundaries for the construction of such engineering facilities, go through the commencement procedures according to law; in arranging construction, the construction entity shall proceed in accordance with the location and boundaries examined and approved by the water administrative department.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对于河道、湖泊管理范围内依照本法规定建设的工程设施，水行政主管部门有权依法检查；水行政主管部门检查时，被检查者应当如实提供有关的情况和资料。  
前款规定的工程设施竣工验收时，应当有水行政主管部门参加。

**Article 28** With respect to engineering facilities constructed within the administration scope of rivers and lakes in accordance with this Law, the water administrative department has the authority to inspect them according to law; when the water administrative department conducts an inspection, the inspected entity shall truthfully provide relevant information and materials.  
When the engineering facilities specified in the preceding paragraph undergo completion acceptance, the water administrative department shall participate.

### 第四章　防洪区和防洪工程设施的管理

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　防洪区是指洪水泛滥可能淹及的地区，分为洪泛区、蓄滞洪区和防洪保护区。  
洪泛区是指尚无工程设施保护的洪水泛滥所及的地区。  
蓄滞洪区是指包括分洪口在内的河堤背水面以外临时贮存洪水的低洼地区及湖泊等。  
防洪保护区是指在防洪标准内受防洪工程设施保护的地区。  
洪泛区、蓄滞洪区和防洪保护区的范围，在防洪规划或者防御洪水方案中划定，并报请省级以上人民政府按照国务院规定的权限批准后予以公告。

**Article 29** Flood control zones refer to areas that may be inundated by floodwaters, and are classified into floodplain areas, flood storage and detention areas, and flood control protected areas.  
Floodplain areas refer to areas that are inundated by floodwaters and are not yet protected by engineering facilities.  
Flood storage and detention areas refer to low-lying areas and lakes outside the landside of river dikes, including flood diversion inlets, that temporarily store floodwaters.  
Flood control protected areas refer to areas protected by flood control engineering facilities within the flood control standards.  
The scope of floodplain areas, flood storage and detention areas, and flood control protected areas shall be delineated in flood control plans or flood defense plans, and shall be announced after being reported to the people's government at or above the provincial level for approval in accordance with the authority prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级人民政府应当按照防洪规划对防洪区内的土地利用实行分区管理。

**Article 30** People's governments at all levels shall implement zonal management of land use within flood control zones in accordance with flood control plans.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对防洪区安全建设工作的领导，组织有关部门、单位对防洪区内的单位和居民进行防洪教育，普及防洪知识，提高水患意识；按照防洪规划和防御洪水方案建立并完善防洪体系和水文、气象、通信、预警以及洪涝灾害监测系统，提高防御洪水能力；组织防洪区内的单位和居民积极参加防洪工作，因地制宜地采取防洪避洪措施。

**Article 31** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over the safety construction of flood control zones, organize relevant departments and entities to conduct flood control education for entities and residents within flood control zones, disseminate flood control knowledge, and raise flood hazard awareness; establish and improve flood control systems, and hydrological, meteorological, communication, early warning, and flood disaster monitoring systems in accordance with flood control plans and flood defense plans to enhance flood defense capabilities; organize entities and residents within flood control zones to actively participate in flood control work, and adopt flood control and flood avoidance measures tailored to local conditions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　洪泛区、蓄滞洪区所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织有关地区和部门，按照防洪规划的要求，制定洪泛区、蓄滞洪区安全建设计划，控制蓄滞洪区人口增长，对居住在经常使用的蓄滞洪区的居民，有计划地组织外迁，并采取其他必要的安全保护措施。  
因蓄滞洪区而直接受益的地区和单位，应当对蓄滞洪区承担国家规定的补偿、救助义务。国务院和有关的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当建立对蓄滞洪区的扶持和补偿、救助制度。  
国务院和有关的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以制定洪泛区、蓄滞洪区安全建设管理办法以及对蓄滞洪区的扶持和补偿、救助办法。

**Article 32** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government where floodplain areas and flood storage and detention areas are located shall organize relevant areas and departments to formulate safety construction plans for floodplain areas and flood storage and detention areas in accordance with the requirements of flood control plans, control population growth in flood storage and detention areas, systematically relocate residents living in frequently used flood storage and detention areas, and adopt other necessary safety protection measures.  
Areas and entities that directly benefit from flood storage and detention areas shall bear the obligations of compensation and relief as prescribed by the State. The State Council and the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish a system of support, compensation, and relief for flood storage and detention areas.  
The State Council and the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate measures for the administration of safety construction in floodplain areas and flood storage and detention areas, as well as measures for the support, compensation, and relief of flood storage and detention areas.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在洪泛区、蓄滞洪区内建设非防洪建设项目，应当就洪水对建设项目可能产生的影响和建设项目对防洪可能产生的影响作出评价，编制洪水影响评价报告，提出防御措施。洪水影响评价报告未经有关水行政主管部门审查批准的，建设单位不得开工建设。  
在蓄滞洪区内建设的油田、铁路、公路、矿山、电厂、电信设施和管道，其洪水影响评价报告应当包括建设单位自行安排的防洪避洪方案。建设项目投入生产或者使用时，其防洪工程设施应当经水行政主管部门验收。  
在蓄滞洪区内建造房屋应当采用平顶式结构。

**Article 33** Where non-flood control construction projects are built within floodplain areas or flood storage and detention areas, an assessment shall be made of the possible impact of floods on the construction project and the possible impact of the construction project on flood control, a flood impact assessment report shall be prepared, and flood defense measures shall be proposed. Where the flood impact assessment report has not been examined and approved by the relevant water administrative department, the construction entity shall not commence construction.  
For oil fields, railways, highways, mines, power plants, telecommunications facilities, and pipelines built in flood storage and detention areas, their flood impact assessment reports shall include a flood control and flood avoidance plan arranged by the construction entity itself. When the construction project is put into production or use, its flood control engineering facilities shall be inspected and accepted by the water administrative department.  
Houses built in flood storage and detention areas shall adopt a flat-roof structure.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　大中城市，重要的铁路、公路干线，大型骨干企业，应当列为防洪重点，确保安全。  
受洪水威胁的城市、经济开发区、工矿区和国家重要的农业生产基地等，应当重点保护，建设必要的防洪工程设施。  
城市建设不得擅自填堵原有河道沟叉、贮水湖塘洼淀和废除原有防洪围堤。确需填堵或者废除的，应当经城市人民政府批准。

**Article 34** Large and medium-sized cities, major railway and highway trunk lines, and large key enterprises shall be designated as key flood control targets to ensure safety.  
Cities, economic development zones, industrial and mining areas, and important national agricultural production bases that are threatened by floods shall be given priority protection, and necessary flood control engineering facilities shall be constructed.  
Urban construction shall not arbitrarily fill or block existing river channels, ditches, water storage lakes, ponds, depressions, or abolish existing flood control dikes. Where filling, blocking, or abolition is truly necessary, it shall be approved by the urban people's government.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　属于国家所有的防洪工程设施，应当按照经批准的设计，在竣工验收前由县级以上人民政府按照国家规定，划定管理和保护范围。  
属于集体所有的防洪工程设施，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，划定保护范围。  
在防洪工程设施保护范围内，禁止进行爆破、打井、采石、取土等危害防洪工程设施安全的活动。

**Article 35** For flood control engineering facilities owned by the State, the management and protection scope shall be delineated by the people's government at or above the county level, after the design is approved and before completion acceptance, in accordance with State provisions.  
For flood control engineering facilities owned by collectives, the protection scope shall be delineated in accordance with the provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Within the protection scope of flood control engineering facilities, activities such as blasting, well drilling, quarrying, and earth taking that endanger the safety of flood control engineering facilities shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级人民政府应当组织有关部门加强对水库大坝的定期检查和监督管理。对未达到设计洪水标准、抗震设防要求或者有严重质量缺陷的险坝，大坝主管部门应当组织有关单位采取除险加固措施，限期消除危险或者重建，有关人民政府应当优先安排所需资金。对可能出现垮坝的水库，应当事先制定应急抢险和居民临时撤离方案。  
各级人民政府和有关主管部门应当加强对尾矿坝的监督管理，采取措施，避免因洪水导致垮坝。

**Article 36** People's governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments to strengthen regular inspection and supervisory management of reservoir dams. For dangerous dams that fail to meet the design flood standard or seismic fortification requirements or have serious quality defects, the dam competent department shall organize relevant entities to take reinforcement and danger elimination measures, eliminate dangers or rebuild within a time limit, and the relevant people's government shall give priority to allocating the necessary funds. For reservoirs where dam failure may occur, emergency rescue and temporary evacuation plans for residents shall be formulated in advance.  
People's governments at all levels and relevant competent departments shall strengthen the supervisory management of tailings dams and take measures to avoid dam failure due to floods.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得破坏、侵占、毁损水库大坝、堤防、水闸、护岸、抽水站、排水渠系等防洪工程和水文、通信设施以及防汛备用的器材、物料等。

**Article 37** No unit or individual shall damage, occupy, or destroy flood control engineering facilities such as reservoir dams, dikes, sluices, bank revetments, pumping stations, drainage canal systems, and hydrological and communication facilities, as well as flood prevention standby equipment, materials, etc.

### 第五章　防汛抗洪

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　防汛抗洪工作实行各级人民政府行政首长负责制，统一指挥、分级分部门负责。

**Article 38** The flood prevention and flood fighting work shall implement the responsibility system of administrative heads of people's governments at all levels, with unified command and division of responsibilities among levels and departments.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院设立国家防汛指挥机构，负责领导、组织全国的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在国务院水行政主管部门。  
在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊可以设立由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和该江河、湖泊的流域管理机构负责人等组成的防汛指挥机构，指挥所管辖范围内的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在流域管理机构。  
有防汛抗洪任务的县级以上地方人民政府设立由有关部门、当地驻军、人民武装部负责人等组成的防汛指挥机构，在上级防汛指挥机构和本级人民政府的领导下，指挥本地区的防汛抗洪工作，其办事机构设在同级水行政主管部门；必要时，经城市人民政府决定，防汛指挥机构也可以在建设行政主管部门设城市市区办事机构，在防汛指挥机构的统一领导下，负责城市市区的防汛抗洪日常工作。

**Article 39** The State Council shall establish a national flood control command organization, responsible for leading and organizing the national flood prevention and flood fighting work, and its administrative office shall be set up in the water administrative department of the State Council.  
For major rivers and lakes determined by the State, flood control command organizations may be established, composed of heads of the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and heads of the river basin management authorities of such rivers and lakes, to command the flood prevention and flood fighting work within their jurisdiction, and their administrative offices shall be set up in the river basin management authorities.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level that have flood prevention and flood fighting tasks shall establish flood control command organizations composed of heads of relevant departments, local garrisons, and the people's armed forces departments, which, under the leadership of the superior flood control command organization and the people's government at the same level, shall command the flood prevention and flood fighting work in their regions, and their administrative offices shall be set up in the water administrative department at the same level; when necessary, upon decision by the urban people's government, the flood control command organization may also set up an urban district administrative office in the competent construction administrative department, which, under the unified leadership of the flood control command organization, shall be responsible for the daily flood prevention and flood fighting work in the urban district.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有防汛抗洪任务的县级以上地方人民政府根据流域综合规划、防洪工程实际状况和国家规定的防洪标准，制定防御洪水方案（包括对特大洪水的处置措施）。  
长江、黄河、淮河、海河的防御洪水方案，由国家防汛指挥机构制定，报国务院批准；跨省、自治区、直辖市的其他江河的防御洪水方案，由有关流域管理机构会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定，报国务院或者国务院授权的有关部门批准。防御洪水方案经批准后，有关地方人民政府必须执行。  
各级防汛指挥机构和承担防汛抗洪任务的部门和单位，必须根据防御洪水方案做好防汛抗洪准备工作。

**Article 40** Local people's governments at or above the county level that have flood prevention and flood fighting tasks shall formulate flood defense plans (including measures for dealing with extraordinary floods) based on the comprehensive river basin plan, the actual condition of flood control engineering facilities, and the State-prescribed flood control standards.  
The flood defense plans for the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Huai River, and Hai River shall be formulated by the national flood control command organization and reported to the State Council for approval; the flood defense plans for other rivers crossing provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the relevant river basin management authorities jointly with the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and reported to the State Council or the relevant department authorized by the State Council for approval. Once the flood defense plans are approved, the relevant local people's governments must implement them.  
All flood control command organizations and departments and units undertaking flood prevention and flood fighting tasks must make flood prevention and flood fighting preparations in accordance with the flood defense plans.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府防汛指挥机构根据当地的洪水规律，规定汛期起止日期。  
当江河、湖泊的水情接近保证水位或者安全流量，水库水位接近设计洪水位，或者防洪工程设施发生重大险情时，有关县级以上人民政府防汛指挥机构可以宣布进入紧急防汛期。

**Article 41** The flood control headquarters of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on local flood patterns, prescribe the start and end dates of the flood season.  
When the water regime of rivers or lakes approaches the guaranteed water level or safe discharge, the reservoir water level approaches the design flood level, or a major dangerous situation occurs in flood control engineering facilities, the relevant flood control headquarters of the people's governments at or above the county level may declare the commencement of an emergency flood season.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对河道、湖泊范围内阻碍行洪的障碍物，按照谁设障、谁清除的原则，由防汛指挥机构责令限期清除；逾期不清除的，由防汛指挥机构组织强行清除，所需费用由设障者承担。  
在紧急防汛期，国家防汛指挥机构或者其授权的流域、省、自治区、直辖市防汛指挥机构有权对壅水、阻水严重的桥梁、引道、码头和其他跨河工程设施作出紧急处置。

**Article 42** For obstacles within river courses or lake areas that impede flood discharge, the flood control headquarters shall, in accordance with the principle that the party which creates the obstacle shall remove it, order removal within a specified time limit; if removal is not effected within the time limit, the flood control headquarters shall organize forcible removal, with the costs borne by the party that created the obstacle.  
During the emergency flood season, the national flood control headquarters or its authorized river basin, provincial, autonomous regional, or municipal flood control headquarters shall have the right to take emergency measures with respect to bridges, approach roads, docks, and other river-crossing engineering facilities that seriously retain or obstruct water flow.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在汛期，气象、水文、海洋等有关部门应当按照各自的职责，及时向有关防汛指挥机构提供天气、水文等实时信息和风暴潮预报；电信部门应当优先提供防汛抗洪通信的服务；运输、电力、物资材料供应等有关部门应当优先为防汛抗洪服务。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵应当执行国家赋予的抗洪抢险任务。

**Article 43** During the flood season, the meteorological, hydrological, oceanic and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, provide timely real-time information on weather and hydrology as well as storm surge forecasts to the relevant flood control headquarters; the telecommunications department shall give priority to providing communication services for flood control and flood fighting; the transportation, electric power, material supply and other relevant departments shall give priority to serving flood control and flood fighting.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia shall carry out the tasks of flood fighting and emergency rescue assigned by the State.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在汛期，水库、闸坝和其他水工程设施的运用，必须服从有关的防汛指挥机构的调度指挥和监督。  
在汛期，水库不得擅自在汛期限制水位以上蓄水，其汛期限制水位以上的防洪库容的运用，必须服从防汛指挥机构的调度指挥和监督。  
在凌汛期，有防凌汛任务的江河的上游水库的下泄水量必须征得有关的防汛指挥机构的同意，并接受其监督。

**Article 44** During the flood season, the operation of reservoirs, sluice gates, dams, and other water engineering facilities must be subject to the dispatching command and supervision of the relevant flood control headquarters.  
During the flood season, no reservoir may store water above the flood season limited water level without authorization; the use of flood control storage capacity above the flood season limited water level must be subject to the dispatching command and supervision of the flood control headquarters.  
During the ice flood season, the amount of water released from upstream reservoirs on rivers where ice flood prevention tasks exist must obtain the consent of the relevant flood control headquarters and be subject to its supervision.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在紧急防汛期，防汛指挥机构根据防汛抗洪的需要，有权在其管辖范围内调用物资、设备、交通运输工具和人力，决定采取取土占地、砍伐林木、清除阻水障碍物和其他必要的紧急措施；必要时，公安、交通等有关部门按照防汛指挥机构的决定，依法实施陆地和水面交通管制。  
依照前款规定调用的物资、设备、交通运输工具等，在汛期结束后应当及时归还；造成损坏或者无法归还的，按照国务院有关规定给予适当补偿或者作其他处理。取土占地、砍伐林木的，在汛期结束后依法向有关部门补办手续；有关地方人民政府对取土后的土地组织复垦，对砍伐的林木组织补种。

**Article 45** During the emergency flood season, the flood control headquarters shall, based on the needs of flood control and flood fighting, have the right to allocate materials, equipment, means of transport, and manpower within its jurisdiction, and decide to take measures such as earth borrowing, land occupation, tree felling, removal of obstacles to water flow, and other necessary emergency measures; when necessary, the public security, transportation, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with the decisions of the flood control headquarters, implement land and water traffic controls according to law.  
Materials, equipment, means of transport, etc., allocated in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be returned promptly after the end of the flood season; if damage is caused or return is impossible, appropriate compensation shall be given or other disposal shall be made in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council. For earth borrowing, land occupation, or tree felling, the procedures shall be completed retroactively with the relevant departments according to law after the end of the flood season; the relevant local people's government shall organize reclamation of the land from which earth has been borrowed and organize replanting of trees that have been felled.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　江河、湖泊水位或者流量达到国家规定的分洪标准，需要启用蓄滞洪区时，国务院，国家防汛指挥机构，流域防汛指挥机构，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，省、自治区、直辖市防汛指挥机构，按照依法经批准的防御洪水方案中规定的启用条件和批准程序，决定启用蓄滞洪区。依法启用蓄滞洪区，任何单位和个人不得阻拦、拖延；遇到阻拦、拖延时，由有关县级以上地方人民政府强制实施。

**Article 46** When the water level or discharge of rivers or lakes reaches the national standards for flood diversion and it is necessary to activate flood storage and detention areas, the State Council, the national flood control headquarters, river basin flood control headquarters, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the provincial, autonomous regional, and municipal flood control headquarters shall decide to activate the flood storage and detention areas in accordance with the activation conditions and approval procedures prescribed in the flood defense plans approved according to law. When flood storage and detention areas are activated according to law, no unit or individual may obstruct or delay such activation; in case of obstruction or delay, the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall enforce the activation by compulsion.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　发生洪涝灾害后，有关人民政府应当组织有关部门、单位做好灾区的生活供给、卫生防疫、救灾物资供应、治安管理、学校复课、恢复生产和重建家园等救灾工作以及所管辖地区的各项水毁工程设施修复工作。水毁防洪工程设施的修复，应当优先列入有关部门的年度建设计划。  
国家鼓励、扶持开展洪水保险。

**Article 47** After the occurrence of flood or waterlogging disasters, the relevant people's government shall organize the relevant departments and units to properly carry out disaster relief work such as providing living supplies, epidemic prevention, supply of disaster relief materials, public security management, reopening of schools, resumption of production, and reconstruction of homes, as well as the repair of various water-damaged engineering facilities within the areas under its jurisdiction. The repair of water-damaged flood control engineering facilities shall be given priority in the annual construction plans of the relevant departments.  
The State encourages and supports the development of flood insurance.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，提高防洪投入的总体水平。

**Article 48** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to increase the overall level of investment in flood control.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　江河、湖泊的治理和防洪工程设施的建设和维护所需投资，按照事权和财权相统一的原则，分级负责，由中央和地方财政承担。城市防洪工程设施的建设和维护所需投资，由城市人民政府承担。  
受洪水威胁地区的油田、管道、铁路、公路、矿山、电力、电信等企业、事业单位应当自筹资金，兴建必要的防洪自保工程。

**Article 49** The investments required for the regulation of rivers and lakes and for the construction and maintenance of flood control engineering facilities shall, in accordance with the principle of unifying the authority and financial power, be borne by the central and local fiscal authorities at different levels with respective responsibilities. The investments required for the construction and maintenance of urban flood control engineering facilities shall be borne by the urban people's government.  
Enterprises and institutions such as oil fields, pipelines, railways, highways, mines, electric power, telecommunications, etc., in areas threatened by floods shall raise funds on their own to construct necessary self-protection flood control projects.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　中央财政应当安排资金，用于国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的堤坝遭受特大洪涝灾害时的抗洪抢险和水毁防洪工程修复。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当在本级财政预算中安排资金，用于本行政区域内遭受特大洪涝灾害地区的抗洪抢险和水毁防洪工程修复。

**Article 50** The central fiscal authority shall allocate funds for flood fighting, emergency rescue, and the repair of water-damaged flood control projects on the dikes of major rivers and lakes designated by the State when they suffer from extraordinarily severe flood or waterlogging disasters. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall allocate funds in their respective fiscal budgets for flood fighting, emergency rescue, and the repair of water-damaged flood control projects in areas within their administrative regions that suffer from extraordinarily severe flood or waterlogging disasters.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家设立水利建设基金，用于防洪工程和水利工程的维护和建设。具体办法由国务院规定。  
受洪水威胁的省、自治区、直辖市为加强本行政区域内防洪工程设施建设，提高防御洪水能力，按照国务院的有关规定，可以规定在防洪保护区范围内征收河道工程修建维护管理费。

**Article 51** The State shall establish a water resources construction fund for the maintenance and construction of flood control projects and water conservancy projects. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government threatened by floods may, in order to strengthen the construction of flood control engineering facilities within their administrative regions and enhance their capacity to defend against floods, prescribe in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council the collection of fees for the construction, maintenance, and management of river engineering within the scope of flood protection areas.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何单位和个人不得截留、挪用防洪、救灾资金和物资。  
各级人民政府审计机关应当加强对防洪、救灾资金使用情况的审计监督。

**Article 52** No unit or individual may intercept or misappropriate funds and materials designated for flood control and disaster relief.  
Audit authorities of people's governments at all levels shall strengthen audit supervision over the use of funds for flood control and disaster relief.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)规定，未经水行政主管部门签署规划同意书，擅自在江河、湖泊上建设防洪工程和其他水工程、水电站的，责令停止违法行为，补办规划同意书手续；违反规划同意书的要求，严重影响防洪的，责令限期拆除；违反规划同意书的要求，影响防洪但尚可采取补救措施的，责令限期采取补救措施，可以处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 53** Whoever, in violation of Article 17 of this Law, constructs flood control projects, other water projects, or hydropower stations on rivers or lakes without the planning consent document signed by the water administrative department, shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and complete the procedures for the planning consent document; if the construction violates the requirements of the planning consent document and seriously affects flood control, the violator shall be ordered to demolish the construction within a specified time limit; if the construction violates the requirements of the planning consent document and affects flood control but remedial measures may still be taken, the violator shall be ordered to take remedial measures within a specified time limit and may be fined not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反[本法第十九条](#art-19)规定，未按照规划治导线整治河道和修建控制引导河水流向、保护堤岸等工程，影响防洪的，责令停止违法行为，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Whoever, in violation of Article 19 of this Law, fails to regulate river courses in accordance with the planned regulation alignment line or to construct projects for controlling and guiding river flow direction, protecting banks, etc., thereby affecting flood control, shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and restore the original condition or take other remedial measures, and may be fined not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第二款、第三款规定，有下列行为之一的，责令停止违法行为，排除阻碍或者采取其他补救措施，可以处五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）在河道、湖泊管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物的；  
（二）在河道、湖泊管理范围内倾倒垃圾、渣土，从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动的；  
（三）在行洪河道内种植阻碍行洪的林木和高秆作物的。

**Article 55** Whoever, in violation of paragraph 2 or 3 of Article 22 of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, remove the obstruction or take other remedial measures, and may be fined not more than RMB 50,000:  
(1) constructing buildings or structures that impede flood discharge within the management scope of river courses or lakes;  
(2) dumping garbage or waste soil, or engaging in activities that affect river regime stability, endanger the safety of riverbanks and dikes, or otherwise impede flood discharge in river courses within the management scope of river courses or lakes;  
(3) planting trees or high-stalk crops that impede flood discharge within flood discharge river courses.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反[本法第十五条](#art-15)第二款、第二十三条规定，围海造地、围湖造地、围垦河道的，责令停止违法行为，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，可以处五万元以下的罚款；既不恢复原状也不采取其他补救措施的，代为恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 56** Whoever, in violation of paragraph 2 of Article 15 or Article 23 of this Law, reclaims land from the sea, from lakes, or by enclosing river courses, shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and restore the original condition or take other remedial measures, and may be fined not more than RMB 50,000; if the violator neither restores the original condition nor takes other remedial measures, the relevant authority shall restore the original condition or take other remedial measures on behalf of the violator, with the costs borne by the violator.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定，未经水行政主管部门对其工程建设方案审查同意或者未按照有关水行政主管部门审查批准的位置、界限，在河道、湖泊管理范围内从事工程设施建设活动的，责令停止违法行为，补办审查同意或者审查批准手续；工程设施建设严重影响防洪的，责令限期拆除，逾期不拆除的，强行拆除，所需费用由建设单位承担；影响行洪但尚可采取补救措施的，责令限期采取补救措施，可以处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 57** Whoever, in violation of Article 27 of this Law, engages in the construction of engineering facilities within the management scope of river courses or lakes without the review and approval of the construction plan by the water administrative department or without following the location and boundaries reviewed and approved by the relevant water administrative department, shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and complete the procedures for review and approval or for review and consent; if the construction of the engineering facility seriously affects flood control, the violator shall be ordered to demolish it within a specified time limit; if demolition is not carried out within the time limit, forcible demolition shall be carried out, with the costs borne by the construction unit; if the construction affects flood discharge but remedial measures may still be taken, the violator shall be ordered to take remedial measures within a specified time limit and may be fined not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第一款规定，在洪泛区、蓄滞洪区内建设非防洪建设项目，未编制洪水影响评价报告或者洪水影响评价报告未经审查批准开工建设的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五万元以下的罚款。  
违反[本法第三十三条](#art-33)第二款规定，防洪工程设施未经验收，即将建设项目投入生产或者使用的，责令停止生产或者使用，限期验收防洪工程设施，可以处五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 58** Whoever, in violation of paragraph 1 of Article 33 of this Law, constructs a non-flood-control construction project within a floodplain or a flood storage and detention area without preparing a flood impact assessment report or without having the flood impact assessment report reviewed and approved before commencing construction, shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed.  
Whoever, in violation of paragraph 2 of Article 33 of this Law, puts a construction project into production or use without the acceptance check of the flood control engineering facilities, shall be ordered to stop production or use and to undergo the acceptance check of the flood control engineering facilities within a specified time limit, and may be fined not more than RMB 50,000.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[本法第三十四条](#art-34)规定，因城市建设擅自填堵原有河道沟叉、贮水湖塘洼淀和废除原有防洪围堤的，城市人民政府应当责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of Article 34 of this Law, original river branches, ditches, water storage ponds, depressions, or original flood control embankments are filled or blocked without authorization due to urban construction, the urban people's government shall order the cessation of the illegal act and the restoration of the original condition or the taking of other remedial measures within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，破坏、侵占、毁损堤防、水闸、护岸、抽水站、排水渠系等防洪工程和水文、通信设施以及防汛备用的器材、物料的，责令停止违法行为，采取补救措施，可以处五万元以下的罚款；造成损坏的，依法承担民事责任；应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, damages, occupies, or destroys dikes, sluice gates, revetments, pumping stations, drainage canal systems or other flood control projects, hydrological and communication facilities, or flood control standby equipment and materials, shall be ordered to cease the illegal act and take remedial measures, and may be fined not more than RMB 50,000; if damage is caused, the violator shall bear civil liability according to law; if a public security penalty is warranted, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Penalties; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　阻碍、威胁防汛指挥机构、水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构的工作人员依法执行职务，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚。

**Article 61** Whoever obstructs or threatens staff members of flood control command organizations, water administrative departments, or river basin authorities from performing their duties according to law shall, if the act constitutes a crime, be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime and a public security administration penalty shall be imposed, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　截留、挪用防洪、救灾资金和物资，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。

**Article 62** Whoever intercepts or misappropriates flood control or disaster relief funds or materials shall, if the act constitutes a crime, be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be given an administrative sanction.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　除[本法第五十九条](#art-59)的规定外，本章规定的行政处罚和行政措施，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门决定，或者由流域管理机构按照国务院水行政主管部门规定的权限决定。但是，[本法第六十条](#art-60)、第六十一条规定的治安管理处罚的决定机关，按照治安管理处罚法的规定执行。

**Article 63** Except for the provisions of Article 59 of this Law, the administrative penalties and administrative measures prescribed in this Chapter shall be decided by the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, or by the river basin authorities in accordance with the authority prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council. However, the decision-making organs for the public security administration penalties prescribed in Articles 60 and 61 of this Law shall act in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家工作人员，有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分：  
（一）违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)、第十九条、第二十二条第二款、第二十二条第三款、第二十七条或者第三十四条规定，严重影响防洪的；  
（二）滥用职权，玩忽职守，徇私舞弊，致使防汛抗洪工作遭受重大损失的；  
（三）拒不执行防御洪水方案、防汛抢险指令或者蓄滞洪方案、措施、汛期调度运用计划等防汛调度方案的；  
（四）违反本法规定，导致或者加重毗邻地区或者其他单位洪灾损失的。

**Article 64** Any functionary of the State who commits any of the following acts shall, if the act constitutes a crime, be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be given an administrative sanction:  
(1) violating the provisions of Articles 17, 19, paragraph 2 of Article 22, paragraph 3 of Article 22, Article 27, or Article 34 of this Law, thus seriously affecting flood control;  
(2) abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, thereby causing significant losses to flood prevention and flood fighting efforts;  
(3) refusing to implement flood prevention plans, flood control and rescue directives, flood storage and detention plans, measures, flood season dispatch and operation plans, or other flood control dispatch plans;  
(4) violating the provisions of this Law, thereby causing or aggravating flood losses to neighboring areas or other entities.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自1998年1月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1998.
