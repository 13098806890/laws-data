# 中华人民共和国国家情报法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2018-04-27  
**生效日期**：2018-04-27

> （2017年6月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十八次会议通过　根据2018年4月27日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法〉等六部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　国家情报工作机构职权 第三章　国家情报工作保障 第四章　法律责任 第五章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强和保障国家情报工作，维护国家安全和利益，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　国家情报工作坚持总体国家安全观，为国家重大决策提供情报参考，为防范和化解危害国家安全的风险提供情报支持，维护国家政权、主权、统一和领土完整、人民福祉、经济社会可持续发展和国家其他重大利益。 第三条　国家建立健全集中统一、分工协作、科学高效的国家情报体制。 中央国家安全领导机构对国家情报工作实行统一领导，制定国家情报工作方针政策，规划国家情报工作整体发展，建立健全国家情报工作协调机制，统筹协调各领域国家情报工作，研究决定国家情报工作中的重大事项。 中央军事委员会统一领导和组织军队情报工作。 第四条　国家情报工作坚持公开工作与秘密工作相结合、专门工作与群众路线相结合、分工负责与协作配合相结合的原则。 第五条　国家安全机关和公安机关情报机构、军队情报机构（以下统称国家情报工作机构）按照职责分工，相互配合，做好情报工作、开展情报行动。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强和保障国家情报工作，维护国家安全和利益，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen and safeguard national intelligence work, and to maintain national security and interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家情报工作坚持总体国家安全观，为国家重大决策提供情报参考，为防范和化解危害国家安全的风险提供情报支持，维护国家政权、主权、统一和领土完整、人民福祉、经济社会可持续发展和国家其他重大利益。

**Article 2** National intelligence work shall adhere to a holistic view of national security, provide intelligence reference for major national decisions, provide intelligence support for preventing and defusing risks endangering national security, and safeguard the state power, sovereignty, unity and territorial integrity, the well-being of the people, sustainable economic and social development, and other major national interests.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家建立健全集中统一、分工协作、科学高效的国家情报体制。  
中央国家安全领导机构对国家情报工作实行统一领导，制定国家情报工作方针政策，规划国家情报工作整体发展，建立健全国家情报工作协调机制，统筹协调各领域国家情报工作，研究决定国家情报工作中的重大事项。  
中央军事委员会统一领导和组织军队情报工作。

**Article 3** The State shall establish a centralized, unified, scientifically efficient national intelligence system with division of labor and cooperation.  
The central national security leadership organ shall exercise unified leadership over national intelligence work, formulate guidelines and policies for national intelligence work, plan the overall development of national intelligence work, establish and improve the coordination mechanism for national intelligence work, coordinate national intelligence work in all fields, and study and decide on major matters in national intelligence work.  
The Central Military Commission shall exercise unified leadership and organize military intelligence work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家情报工作坚持公开工作与秘密工作相结合、专门工作与群众路线相结合、分工负责与协作配合相结合的原则。

**Article 4** National intelligence work shall adhere to the principles of combining open work with secret work, combining specialized work with the mass line, and combining division of responsibilities with cooperation and coordination.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家安全机关和公安机关情报机构、军队情报机构（以下统称国家情报工作机构）按照职责分工，相互配合，做好情报工作、开展情报行动。  
各有关国家机关应当根据各自职能和任务分工，与国家情报工作机构密切配合。

**Article 5** The state security organs, the intelligence agencies of public security organs, and military intelligence agencies (hereinafter collectively referred to as the State intelligence work agencies) shall, in accordance with their respective functions and division of responsibilities, cooperate with each other, perform intelligence work well, and carry out intelligence operations.  
All relevant state organs shall, in accordance with their respective functions and task division, cooperate closely with the State intelligence work agencies.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家情报工作机构及其工作人员应当忠于国家和人民，遵守宪法和法律，忠于职守，纪律严明，清正廉洁，无私奉献，坚决维护国家安全和利益。

**Article 6** The State intelligence work agencies and their staff shall be loyal to the State and the people, abide by the Constitution and laws, be devoted to their duties, strictly disciplined, honest and upright, selfless, and resolutely safeguard national security and interests.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何组织和公民都应当依法支持、协助和配合国家情报工作，保守所知悉的国家情报工作秘密。  
国家对支持、协助和配合国家情报工作的个人和组织给予保护。

**Article 7** All organizations and citizens shall, according to law, support, assist and cooperate with national intelligence work, and keep confidential any State intelligence work secrets they become aware of.  
The State shall provide protection to individuals and organizations that support, assist and cooperate with national intelligence work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家情报工作应当依法进行，尊重和保障人权，维护个人和组织的合法权益。

**Article 8** National intelligence work shall be conducted according to law, respect and protect human rights, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of individuals and organizations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对在国家情报工作中作出重大贡献的个人和组织给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** The State shall give commendation and rewards to individuals and organizations that have made significant contributions in national intelligence work.

### 第二章　国家情报工作机构职权

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家情报工作机构根据工作需要，依法使用必要的方式、手段和渠道，在境内外开展情报工作。

**Article 10** The State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with work needs and according to law, use necessary methods, means and channels to carry out intelligence work within and outside the territory.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家情报工作机构应当依法搜集和处理境外机构、组织、个人实施或者指使、资助他人实施的，或者境内外机构、组织、个人相勾结实施的危害中华人民共和国国家安全和利益行为的相关情报，为防范、制止和惩治上述行为提供情报依据或者参考。

**Article 11** The State intelligence work agencies shall, according to law, collect and process relevant intelligence on acts that endanger the national security and interests of the People's Republic of China, which are committed by foreign institutions, organizations or individuals, or instigated or funded by them, or committed through collusion between domestic and foreign institutions, organizations or individuals, and provide intelligence basis or reference for preventing, stopping and punishing the above acts.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家情报工作机构可以按照国家有关规定，与有关个人和组织建立合作关系，委托开展相关工作。

**Article 12** The State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with relevant state provisions, establish cooperative relations with relevant individuals and organizations, and entrust them to carry out related work.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家情报工作机构可以按照国家有关规定，开展对外交流与合作。

**Article 13** The State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with relevant state provisions, conduct foreign exchanges and cooperation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家情报工作机构依法开展情报工作，可以要求有关机关、组织和公民提供必要的支持、协助和配合。

**Article 14** When carrying out intelligence work according to law, the State intelligence work agencies may request relevant organs, organizations and citizens to provide necessary support, assistance and cooperation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家情报工作机构根据工作需要，按照国家有关规定，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦察措施和身份保护措施。

**Article 15** The State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with work needs and relevant state provisions, and after undergoing strict approval procedures, take technical reconnaissance measures and identity protection measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家情报工作机构工作人员依法执行任务时，按照国家有关规定，经过批准，出示相应证件，可以进入限制进入的有关区域、场所，可以向有关机关、组织和个人了解、询问有关情况，可以查阅或者调取有关的档案、资料、物品。

**Article 16** When performing tasks according to law, the staff of State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with relevant state provisions, after approval and upon presentation of corresponding credentials, enter restricted relevant areas and premises, may inquire about relevant circumstances from relevant organs, organizations and individuals, and may consult or retrieve relevant archives, materials and articles.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家情报工作机构工作人员因执行紧急任务需要，经出示相应证件，可以享受通行便利。  
国家情报工作机构工作人员根据工作需要，按照国家有关规定，可以优先使用或者依法征用有关机关、组织和个人的交通工具、通信工具、场地和建筑物，必要时，可以设置相关工作场所和设备、设施，任务完成后应当及时归还或者恢复原状，并依照规定支付相应费用；造成损失的，应当补偿。

**Article 17** When carrying out urgent tasks, the staff of State intelligence work agencies may, upon presentation of corresponding credentials, enjoy passage facilitation.  
The staff of State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with work needs and relevant state provisions, give priority to using or legally requisition means of transportation, communication tools, sites and buildings of relevant organs, organizations and individuals, and if necessary, may set up related work premises and equipment and facilities. After the task is completed, they shall promptly return or restore them to their original condition, and pay corresponding fees in accordance with regulations; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家情报工作机构根据工作需要，按照国家有关规定，可以提请海关、出入境边防检查等机关提供免检等便利。

**Article 18** The State intelligence work agencies may, in accordance with work needs and relevant state provisions, request customs, exit-entry border inspection and other authorities to provide conveniences such as exemption from inspection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家情报工作机构及其工作人员应当严格依法办事，不得超越职权、滥用职权，不得侵犯公民和组织的合法权益，不得利用职务便利为自己或者他人谋取私利，不得泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息。

**Article 19** The State intelligence work agencies and their staff shall strictly act according to law, shall not exceed their authority or abuse their power, shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of citizens and organizations, shall not take advantage of their positions to seek private gains for themselves or others, and shall not divulge State secrets, trade secrets or personal information.

### 第三章　国家情报工作保障

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家情报工作机构及其工作人员依法开展情报工作，受法律保护。

**Article 20** The State intelligence work agencies and their staff shall be protected by law when carrying out intelligence work according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家加强国家情报工作机构建设，对其机构设置、人员、编制、经费、资产实行特殊管理，给予特殊保障。  
国家建立适应情报工作需要的人员录用、选调、考核、培训、待遇、退出等管理制度。

**Article 21** The State shall strengthen the construction of State intelligence work agencies, implement special management and provide special guarantees for their organizational setup, personnel, staffing, funds and assets.  
The State shall establish a management system for personnel recruitment, selection, assessment, training, treatment, and exit that meets the needs of intelligence work.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家情报工作机构应当适应情报工作需要，提高开展情报工作的能力。  
国家情报工作机构应当运用科学技术手段，提高对情报信息的鉴别、筛选、综合和研判分析水平。

**Article 22** The State intelligence work agencies shall adapt to the needs of intelligence work and improve their capacity to carry out intelligence work.  
The State intelligence work agencies shall use scientific and technological means to improve the level of identification, screening, synthesis and analysis of intelligence information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家情报工作机构工作人员因执行任务，或者与国家情报工作机构建立合作关系的人员因协助国家情报工作，其本人或者近亲属人身安全受到威胁时，国家有关部门应当采取必要措施，予以保护、营救。

**Article 23** When the personal safety of the staff of State intelligence work agencies due to performance of tasks, or of persons who have established cooperative relations with State intelligence work agencies due to assistance to national intelligence work, or of their close relatives, is threatened, the relevant state departments shall take necessary measures to protect and rescue them.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对为国家情报工作作出贡献并需要安置的人员，国家给予妥善安置。  
公安、民政、财政、卫生、教育、人力资源社会保障、退役军人事务、医疗保障等有关部门以及国有企业事业单位应当协助国家情报工作机构做好安置工作。

**Article 24** The State shall provide proper placement for persons who have contributed to national intelligence work and need to be placed.  
The relevant departments such as public security, civil affairs, finance, health, education, human resources and social security, veteran affairs, medical security, as well as State-owned enterprises and institutions, shall assist the State intelligence work agencies in carrying out the placement work.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对因开展国家情报工作或者支持、协助和配合国家情报工作导致伤残或者牺牲、死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予相应的抚恤优待。  
个人和组织因支持、协助和配合国家情报工作导致财产损失的，按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 25** Personnel who become disabled or sacrifice or die due to carrying out national intelligence work or supporting, assisting and cooperating with national intelligence work shall be given corresponding pension and preferential treatment in accordance with relevant state provisions.  
Individuals and organizations that suffer property losses due to supporting, assisting and cooperating with national intelligence work shall be given compensation in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家情报工作机构应当建立健全严格的监督和安全审查制度，对其工作人员遵守法律和纪律等情况进行监督，并依法采取必要措施，定期或者不定期进行安全审查。

**Article 26** The State intelligence work agencies shall establish and improve strict supervision and security review systems, supervise their staff's compliance with laws and disciplines, and take necessary measures according to law to conduct regular or irregular security reviews.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　任何个人和组织对国家情报工作机构及其工作人员超越职权、滥用职权和其他违法违纪行为，有权检举、控告。受理检举、控告的有关机关应当及时查处，并将查处结果告知检举人、控告人。  
对依法检举、控告国家情报工作机构及其工作人员的个人和组织，任何个人和组织不得压制和打击报复。  
国家情报工作机构应当为个人和组织检举、控告、反映情况提供便利渠道，并为检举人、控告人保密。

**Article 27** Any individual or organization shall have the right to report or accuse any State intelligence work agency or its staff of exceeding authority, abusing power or other illegal or disciplinary violations. The relevant organ that accepts the report or accusation shall promptly investigate and handle it, and inform the reporter or accuser of the investigation and handling result.  
No individual or organization shall suppress or retaliate against any individual or organization that lawfully reports or accuses State intelligence work agencies or their staff.  
The State intelligence work agencies shall provide convenient channels for individuals and organizations to report, accuse, or reflect situations, and keep the reporters and accusers confidential.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本法规定，阻碍国家情报工作机构及其工作人员依法开展情报工作的，由国家情报工作机构建议相关单位给予处分或者由国家安全机关、公安机关处警告或者十五日以下拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and obstructs a State intelligence work agency or its staff from carrying out intelligence work according to law shall be given a sanction by the relevant unit upon recommendation of the State intelligence work agency, or be given a warning or detained for not more than 15 days by the state security organ or public security organ; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　泄露与国家情报工作有关的国家秘密的，由国家情报工作机构建议相关单位给予处分或者由国家安全机关、公安机关处警告或者十五日以下拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Whoever divulges State secrets related to national intelligence work shall be given a sanction by the relevant unit upon recommendation of the State intelligence work agency, or be given a warning or detained for not more than 15 days by the state security organ or public security organ; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　冒充国家情报工作机构工作人员或者其他相关人员实施招摇撞骗、诈骗、敲诈勒索等行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Whoever impersonates a staff member of a State intelligence work agency or other relevant persons to commit acts such as posing as an official to swindle, fraud, extortion, etc., shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家情报工作机构及其工作人员有超越职权、滥用职权，侵犯公民和组织的合法权益，利用职务便利为自己或者他人谋取私利，泄露国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息等违法违纪行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** If a State intelligence work agency or its staff commits illegal or disciplinary violations such as exceeding authority, abusing power, infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of citizens and organizations, taking advantage of their positions to seek private gains for themselves or others, or divulging State secrets, trade secrets or personal information, they shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本法自2017年6月28日起施行。

**Article 32** This Law shall come into force as of June 28, 2017.
