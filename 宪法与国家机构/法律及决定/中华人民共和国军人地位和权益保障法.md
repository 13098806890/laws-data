# 中华人民共和国军人地位和权益保障法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2021-06-10  
**生效日期**：2021-08-01

> （2021年6月10日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　军人地位 第三章　荣誉维护 第四章　待遇保障 第五章　抚恤优待 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障军人地位和合法权益，激励军人履行职责使命，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，促进国防和军队现代化建设，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称军人，是指在中国人民解放军服现役的军官、军士、义务兵等人员。 第三条　军人肩负捍卫国家主权、安全、发展利益和保卫人民的和平劳动的神圣职责和崇高使命。 第四条　军人是全社会尊崇的职业。国家和社会尊重、优待军人，保障军人享有与其职业特点、担负职责使命和所做贡献相称的地位和权益，经常开展各种形式的拥军优属活动。 一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织都有依法保障军人地位和权益的责任，全体公民都应当依法维护军人合法权益。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障军人地位和合法权益，激励军人履行职责使命，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，促进国防和军队现代化建设，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of safeguarding the status and lawful rights and interests of servicemen, encouraging them to perform their duties and missions, making the military profession one respected by the whole society, and promoting the modernization of national defense and the armed forces.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称军人，是指在中国人民解放军服现役的军官、军士、义务兵等人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "servicemen" means officers, non-commissioned officers, enlisted soldiers and other personnel on active service in the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军人肩负捍卫国家主权、安全、发展利益和保卫人民的和平劳动的神圣职责和崇高使命。

**Article 3** Servicemen shall shoulder the sacred duty and noble mission of safeguarding national sovereignty, security, and development interests, and protecting the peaceful labor of the people.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　军人是全社会尊崇的职业。国家和社会尊重、优待军人，保障军人享有与其职业特点、担负职责使命和所做贡献相称的地位和权益，经常开展各种形式的拥军优属活动。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织都有依法保障军人地位和权益的责任，全体公民都应当依法维护军人合法权益。

**Article 4** The military profession is one respected by the whole society. The State and society shall respect and give preferential treatment to servicemen, ensure that they enjoy status and rights and interests commensurate with the characteristics of their profession, the duties and missions they shoulder, and the contributions they make, and regularly carry out various forms of activities to support the military and give preferential treatment to the families of servicemen.  
All State organs and armed forces, all political parties and people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations shall have the responsibility to safeguard the status and rights and interests of servicemen according to law, and all citizens shall uphold the lawful rights and interests of servicemen according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军人地位和权益保障工作，坚持中国共产党的领导，以服务军队战斗力建设为根本目的，遵循权利与义务相统一、物质保障与精神激励相结合、保障水平与国民经济和社会发展相适应的原则。

**Article 5** The work of safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, take serving the building of the combat effectiveness of the armed forces as the fundamental purpose, and follow the principles of unifying rights and obligations, combining material security with spiritual encouragement, and adapting the level of security to national economic and social development.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中央军事委员会政治工作部门、国务院退役军人工作主管部门以及中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会有关部门按照职责分工做好军人地位和权益保障工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府负责本行政区域内有关军人地位和权益保障工作。军队团级以上单位政治工作部门负责本单位的军人地位和权益保障工作。  
省军区（卫戍区、警备区）、军分区（警备区）和县、自治县、市、市辖区的人民武装部，负责所在行政区域人民政府与军队单位之间军人地位和权益保障方面的联系协调工作，并根据需要建立工作协调机制。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处、基层群众性自治组织应当按照职责做好军人地位和权益保障工作。

**Article 6** The Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission, the competent department of the State Council for the work of ex-servicemen, and relevant central and State organs and relevant departments of the Central Military Commission shall, according to their respective functions and duties, do a good job in safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work of safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen within their respective administrative areas. The political work departments of military units at or above the regimental level shall be responsible for the work of safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen in their respective units.  
The provincial military commands (garrison commands, garrison commands), sub-military commands (garrison commands), and the people's armed forces departments of counties, autonomous counties, cities, and municipal districts shall be responsible for liaison and coordination between the people's governments of their respective administrative areas and military units regarding the safeguarding of the status and rights and interests of servicemen, and shall establish working coordination mechanisms as needed.  
Township people's governments, sub-district offices, and grassroots autonomous mass organizations shall, according to their functions and duties, do a good job in safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军人地位和权益保障所需经费，由中央和地方按照事权和支出责任相适应的原则列入预算。

**Article 7** The funds required for safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen shall be included in the budgets of the central and local governments in accordance with the principle that their respective powers and expenditure responsibilities are consistent.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中央和国家有关机关、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、军队各级机关，应当将军人地位和权益保障工作情况作为拥军优属、拥政爱民等工作评比和有关单位负责人以及工作人员考核评价的重要内容。

**Article 8** Relevant central and State organs, local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, and military organs at all levels shall make the work of safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen an important part of the evaluation of activities such as supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to the families of servicemen, supporting the government and cherishing the people, and the assessment of the persons in charge and staff members of the relevant units.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和引导群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织、个人等社会力量依法通过捐赠、志愿服务等方式为军人权益保障提供支持，符合规定条件的，依法享受税收优惠等政策。

**Article 9** The State shall encourage and guide social forces such as people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and individuals to provide support for the safeguarding of the rights and interests of servicemen through donations, voluntary services, and other means according to law. Those meeting the prescribed conditions shall enjoy tax incentives and other policies according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　每年8月1日为中国人民解放军建军节。各级人民政府和军队单位应当在建军节组织开展庆祝、纪念等活动。

**Article 10** August 1 of each year shall be the Army Day of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. People's governments at all levels and military units shall organize and carry out celebrations, commemorations, and other activities on Army Day.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在军人地位和权益保障工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the work of safeguarding the status and rights and interests of servicemen shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　军人地位

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军人是中国共产党领导的国家武装力量基本成员，必须忠于祖国，忠于中国共产党，听党指挥，坚决服从命令，认真履行巩固中国共产党的领导和社会主义制度的重要职责使命。

**Article 12** Servicemen are basic members of the State's armed forces under the leadership of the Communist Party of China. They must be loyal to the motherland, loyal to the Communist Party of China, obey the Party's command, resolutely follow orders, and earnestly fulfill the important duties and missions of consolidating the leadership of the Communist Party of China and the socialist system.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军人是人民子弟兵，应当热爱人民，全心全意为人民服务，保卫人民生命财产安全，当遇到人民群众生命财产受到严重威胁时，挺身而出、积极救助。

**Article 13** Servicemen are the people's own army. They shall love the people, serve the people wholeheartedly, protect the safety of the people's lives and property, and when the lives and property of the people are seriously threatened, step forward and actively rescue them.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军人是捍卫国家主权、统一、领土完整的坚强力量，应当具备巩固国防、抵抗侵略、保卫祖国所需的战斗精神和能力素质，按照实战要求始终保持戒备状态，苦练杀敌本领，不怕牺牲，能打胜仗，坚决完成任务。

**Article 14** Servicemen are the staunch force for safeguarding national sovereignty, unity, and territorial integrity. They shall possess the combat spirit and capabilities and qualities required for consolidating national defense, resisting aggression, and defending the motherland, remain on alert at all times in accordance with the requirements of actual combat, train hard in combat skills to kill the enemy, fear no sacrifice, be capable of winning battles, and resolutely accomplish tasks.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军人是中国特色社会主义现代化建设的重要力量，应当积极投身全面建设社会主义现代化国家的事业，依法参加突发事件的应急救援和处置工作。

**Article 15** Servicemen are an important force in the modernization of socialism with Chinese characteristics. They shall actively devote themselves to the cause of building a modern socialist country in all respects, and participate in emergency rescue and disposal of emergencies according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军人享有宪法和法律规定的政治权利，依法参加国家权力机关组成人员选举，依法参加管理国家事务、管理经济和文化事业、管理社会事务。

**Article 16** Servicemen shall enjoy the political rights provided for in the Constitution and laws, participate in the election of the members of the State power organs according to law, and participate in the management of State affairs, economic and cultural undertakings, and social affairs according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军队实行官兵一致，军人之间在政治和人格上一律平等，应当互相尊重、平等对待。  
军队建立健全军人代表会议、军人委员会等民主制度，保障军人知情权、参与权、建议权和监督权。

**Article 17** The armed forces shall practice equality between officers and soldiers. Servicemen shall be equal in political status and personal dignity, and shall respect each other and treat each other equally.  
The armed forces shall establish and improve democratic systems such as servicemen's representative conferences and servicemen's committees, to safeguard servicemen's rights to be informed, to participate, to make suggestions, and to supervise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军人必须模范遵守宪法和法律，认真履行宪法和法律规定的公民义务，严格遵守军事法规、军队纪律，作风优良，带头践行社会主义核心价值观。

**Article 18** Servicemen must set an example in abiding by the Constitution and laws, earnestly fulfill the civic duties prescribed by the Constitution and laws, strictly observe military regulations and military discipline, maintain fine conduct, and take the lead in practicing the core socialist values.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家为军人履行职责提供保障，军人依法履行职责的行为受法律保护。  
军人因执行任务给公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益造成损害的，按照有关规定由国家予以赔偿或者补偿。  
公民、法人和其他组织应当为军人依法履行职责提供必要的支持和协助。

**Article 19** The State shall provide guarantee for servicemen to perform their duties, and the acts of servicemen performing their duties according to law shall be protected by law.  
Where a serviceman causes damage to the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization while performing a mission, the State shall provide compensation or indemnity in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall provide necessary support and assistance for servicemen to perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　军人因履行职责享有的特定权益、承担的特定义务，由本法和有关法律法规规定。

**Article 20** The specific rights and interests enjoyed and specific obligations assumed by servicemen due to the performance of their duties shall be provided for by this Law and relevant laws and regulations.

### 第三章　荣誉维护

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军人荣誉是国家、社会对军人献身国防和军队建设、社会主义现代化建设的褒扬和激励，是鼓舞军人士气、提升军队战斗力的精神力量。  
国家维护军人荣誉，激励军人崇尚和珍惜荣誉。

**Article 21** Honor of servicemen is the commendation and encouragement of the State and society to servicemen for their dedication to national defense and military building and socialist modernization, and is a spiritual force to boost the morale of servicemen and enhance the combat effectiveness of the armed forces.  
The State shall uphold the honor of servicemen and encourage them to uphold and cherish honor.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军队加强爱国主义、集体主义、革命英雄主义教育，强化军人的荣誉意识，培育有灵魂、有本事、有血性、有品德的新时代革命军人，锻造具有铁一般信仰、铁一般信念、铁一般纪律、铁一般担当的过硬部队。

**Article 22** The armed forces shall strengthen education in patriotism, collectivism, and revolutionary heroism, enhance servicemen's awareness of honor, cultivate revolutionary servicemen of the new era with soul, ability, courage, and moral integrity, and forge strong troops with iron faith, iron conviction, iron discipline, and iron responsibility.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家采取多种形式的宣传教育、奖励激励和保障措施，培育军人的职业使命感、自豪感和荣誉感，激发军人建功立业、报效国家的积极性、主动性、创造性。

**Article 23** The State shall adopt various forms of publicity and education, reward and incentive, and guarantee measures to cultivate servicemen's sense of professional mission, pride, and honor, and to stimulate their enthusiasm, initiative, and creativity in making contributions and serving the country.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　全社会应当学习中国人民解放军光荣历史，宣传军人功绩和牺牲奉献精神，营造维护军人荣誉的良好氛围。  
各级各类学校设置的国防教育课程中，应当包括中国人民解放军光荣历史、军人英雄模范事迹等内容。

**Article 24** The whole society shall learn the glorious history of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, publicize the achievements and the spirit of sacrifice and dedication of servicemen, and create a good atmosphere for maintaining the honor of servicemen.  
National defense education courses offered in schools of various levels and types shall include contents such as the glorious history of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the heroic and exemplary deeds of servicemen.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家建立健全军人荣誉体系，通过授予勋章、荣誉称号和记功、嘉奖、表彰、颁发纪念章等方式，对做出突出成绩和贡献的军人给予功勋荣誉表彰，褒扬军人为国家和人民做出的奉献和牺牲。

**Article 25** The State shall establish and improve a system of honor for servicemen, conferring decorations, honorary titles, and commendations such as citations of merit, awards, commendations, and issuance of medals to servicemen who have made outstanding achievements and contributions, and commending the dedication and sacrifice of servicemen to the State and the people.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　军人经军队单位批准可以接受地方人民政府、群团组织和社会组织等授予的荣誉，以及国际组织和其他国家、军队等授予的荣誉。

**Article 26** Servicemen may accept honors conferred by local people's governments, people's organizations, social organizations, etc., as well as honors conferred by international organizations, other countries, and foreign armed forces, subject to approval by their military units.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　获得功勋荣誉表彰的军人享受相应礼遇和待遇。军人执行作战任务获得功勋荣誉表彰的，按照高于平时的原则享受礼遇和待遇。  
获得功勋荣誉表彰和执行作战任务的军人的姓名和功绩，按照规定载入功勋簿、荣誉册、地方志等史志。

**Article 27** Servicemen who have received commendations or honors shall enjoy corresponding courtesies and benefits. Where servicemen have received commendations or honors for performing combat missions, they shall enjoy courtesies and benefits on the principle of being higher than in peacetime.  
The names and achievements of servicemen who have received commendations or honors and who have performed combat missions shall be recorded in chronicles such as the book of merits, the book of honor, and local chronicles in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中央和国家有关机关、地方和军队各级有关机关，以及广播、电视、报刊、互联网等媒体，应当积极宣传军人的先进典型和英勇事迹。

**Article 28** Relevant central and State organs, relevant local and military organs at all levels, and media such as broadcasting, television, newspapers, and the Internet shall actively publicize the advanced models and heroic deeds of servicemen.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家和社会尊崇、铭记为国家、人民、民族牺牲的军人，尊敬、礼遇其遗属。  
国家建立英雄烈士纪念设施供公众瞻仰，悼念缅怀英雄烈士，开展纪念和教育活动。  
国家推进军人公墓建设。军人去世后，符合规定条件的可以安葬在军人公墓。

**Article 29** The State and society shall respect and remember servicemen who have sacrificed for the State, the people, and the nation, and shall show respect and courtesy to their surviving family members.  
The State shall establish memorial facilities for heroes and martyrs for the public to visit, mourn, and remember them, and carry out commemorative and educational activities.  
The State shall advance the construction of military cemeteries. After the death of a serviceman, if conditions are met, he or she may be buried in a military cemetery.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家建立军人礼遇仪式制度。在公民入伍、军人退出现役等时机，应当举行相应仪式；在烈士和因公牺牲军人安葬等场合，应当举行悼念仪式。  
各级人民政府应当在重大节日和纪念日组织开展走访慰问军队单位、军人家庭和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属等活动，在举行重要庆典、纪念活动时邀请军人、军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属代表参加。

**Article 30** The State shall establish a system of ceremonies of courtesies for servicemen. At the time of enlistment of citizens and discharge of servicemen from active service, appropriate ceremonies shall be held; on occasions such as the burial of martyrs and servicemen who have died in the line of duty, a memorial ceremony shall be held.  
People's governments at all levels shall organize activities such as visiting and offering condolences to military units, families of servicemen, and surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness on major festivals and commemorative days, and shall invite servicemen, family members of servicemen, and representatives of the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness to participate in important celebrations and commemorative activities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　地方人民政府应当为军人和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属的家庭悬挂光荣牌。军人获得功勋荣誉表彰，由当地人民政府有关部门和军事机关给其家庭送喜报，并组织做好宣传工作。

**Article 31** Local people's governments shall hang an honorable plaque for the families of servicemen and the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness. Where a serviceman receives a commendation or honor, the relevant department of the local people's government and the military organ shall deliver a good news notice to his or her family and organize proper publicity.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　军人的荣誉和名誉受法律保护。  
军人获得的荣誉由其终身享有，非因法定事由、非经法定程序不得撤销。  
任何组织和个人不得以任何方式诋毁、贬损军人的荣誉，侮辱、诽谤军人的名誉，不得故意毁损、玷污军人的荣誉标识。

**Article 32** The honor and reputation of servicemen shall be protected by law.  
Honor obtained by a serviceman shall be enjoyed for life and shall not be revoked except on statutory grounds and through statutory procedures.  
No organization or individual may slander or belittle the honor of servicemen, or insult or defame the reputation of servicemen, and shall not intentionally damage or deface the honor symbols of servicemen.

### 第四章　待遇保障

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立军人待遇保障制度，保证军人履行职责使命，保障军人及其家庭的生活水平。  
对执行作战任务和重大非战争军事行动任务的军人，以及在艰苦边远地区、特殊岗位工作的军人，待遇保障从优。

**Article 33** The State shall establish a system for ensuring the treatment of servicemen to ensure that they perform their duties and missions, and to ensure the living standards of servicemen and their families.  
Servicemen who perform combat missions and major non-war military operations, as well as servicemen working in arduous and remote areas and special posts, shall receive preferential treatment guarantees.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家建立相对独立、特色鲜明、具有比较优势的军人工资待遇制度。军官和军士实行工资制度，义务兵实行供给制生活待遇制度。军人享受个人所得税优惠政策。  
国家建立军人工资待遇正常增长机制。  
军人工资待遇的结构、标准及其调整办法，由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 34** The State shall establish a relatively independent, distinctively characteristic, and comparatively advantageous salary and treatment system for servicemen. Officers and non-commissioned officers shall be subject to a salary system, and enlisted soldiers shall be subject to a supply system for living treatment. Servicemen shall enjoy preferential policies on personal income tax.  
The State shall establish a normal growth mechanism for the salary and treatment of servicemen.  
The structure, standards, and adjustment methods for the salary and treatment of servicemen shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家采取军队保障、政府保障与市场配置相结合，实物保障与货币补贴相结合的方式，保障军人住房待遇。  
军人符合规定条件的，享受军队公寓住房或者安置住房保障。  
国家建立健全军人住房公积金制度和住房补贴制度。军人符合规定条件购买住房的，国家给予优惠政策支持。

**Article 35** The State shall guarantee housing treatment for servicemen by combining military security, government security, and market allocation, and by combining in-kind security and monetary subsidies.  
Servicemen meeting the prescribed conditions shall enjoy military apartment housing or resettlement housing security.  
The State shall establish and improve the housing provident fund system and housing subsidy system for servicemen. Where servicemen meet the prescribed conditions for purchasing housing, the State shall grant preferential policy support.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家保障军人按照规定享受免费医疗和疾病预防、疗养、康复等待遇。  
军人在地方医疗机构就医所需费用，符合规定条件的，由军队保障。

**Article 36** The State shall ensure that servicemen enjoy free medical care and disease prevention, recuperation, rehabilitation, and other treatments in accordance with the provisions.  
Expenses incurred by servicemen for medical treatment in local medical institutions, if meeting the prescribed conditions, shall be covered by the armed forces.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家实行体现军人职业特点、与社会保险制度相衔接的军人保险制度，适时补充军人保险项目，保障军人的保险待遇。  
国家鼓励和支持商业保险机构为军人及其家庭成员提供专属保险产品。

**Article 37** The State shall implement a military insurance system that reflects the characteristics of the military profession and is linked to the social insurance system, supplement military insurance items in due course, and guarantee the insurance treatment of servicemen.  
The State shall encourage and support commercial insurance institutions to provide exclusive insurance products for servicemen and their family members.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　军人享有年休假、探亲假等休息休假的权利。对确因工作需要未休假或者未休满假的，给予经济补偿。  
军人配偶、子女与军人两地分居的，可以前往军人所在部队探亲。军人配偶前往部队探亲的，其所在单位应当按照规定安排假期并保障相应的薪酬待遇，不得因其享受探亲假期而辞退、解聘或者解除劳动关系。符合规定条件的军人配偶、未成年子女和不能独立生活的成年子女的探亲路费，由军人所在部队保障。

**Article 38** Servicemen shall have the right to rest and leave such as annual leave and home leave. Economic compensation shall be given to those who have not taken leave or have not taken all leave due to work needs.  
Where servicemen's spouses and children live apart from them due to different locations, they may go to the servicemen's units to visit them. Where a serviceman's spouse goes to the unit to visit the serviceman, the employer of the spouse shall arrange leave in accordance with the provisions and guarantee the corresponding salary and treatment, and shall not dismiss, terminate the employment contract, or dissolve the labor relationship because the spouse enjoys such visiting leave. The travel expenses for visiting a serviceman by the spouse, minor children, and adult children who cannot live independently meeting the prescribed conditions shall be guaranteed by the serviceman's unit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家建立健全军人教育培训体系，保障军人的受教育权利，组织和支持军人参加专业和文化学习培训，提高军人履行职责的能力和退出现役后的就业创业能力。

**Article 39** The State shall establish and improve a system of education and training for servicemen, guarantee their right to education, organize and support servicemen to participate in professional and cultural learning and training, and improve their ability to perform their duties and their ability to find employment and start businesses after discharge from active service.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　女军人的合法权益受法律保护。军队应当根据女军人的特点，合理安排女军人的工作任务和休息休假，在生育、健康等方面为女军人提供特别保护。

**Article 40** The lawful rights and interests of female servicemen shall be protected by law. The armed forces shall, based on the characteristics of female servicemen, reasonably arrange their work tasks and rest breaks, and provide special protection for female servicemen in terms of childbirth, health, etc.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家对军人的婚姻给予特别保护，禁止任何破坏军人婚姻的行为。

**Article 41** The State shall give special protection to the marriage of servicemen, and any act damaging the marriage of a serviceman shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　军官和符合规定条件的军士，其配偶、未成年子女和不能独立生活的成年子女可以办理随军落户；符合规定条件的军人父母可以按照规定办理随子女落户。夫妻双方均为军人的，其子女可以选择父母中的一方随军落户。  
军人服现役所在地发生变动的，已随军的家属可以随迁落户，或者选择将户口迁至军人、军人配偶原户籍所在地或者军人父母、军人配偶父母户籍所在地。  
地方人民政府有关部门、军队有关单位应当及时高效地为军人家属随军落户办理相关手续。

**Article 42** The spouses, minor children, and adult children who cannot live independently of officers and non-commissioned officers meeting the prescribed conditions may apply for household registration following the servicemen; the parents of servicemen meeting the prescribed conditions may apply for household registration following their children in accordance with the provisions. Where both husband and wife are servicemen, their children may choose to register their household following either parent in the military.  
Where the location of a serviceman's active service changes, the family members who have already followed may move and register their household there, or choose to move their household registration to the original location of the household registration of the serviceman or the serviceman's spouse, or to the location of the household registration of the serviceman's parents or the serviceman's spouse's parents.  
Relevant departments of local people's governments and relevant military units shall handle the relevant procedures for family members to register their household following servicemen in a timely and efficient manner.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家保障军人、军人家属的户籍管理和相关权益。  
公民入伍时保留户籍。  
符合规定条件的军人，可以享受服现役所在地户籍人口在教育、养老、医疗、住房保障等方面的相关权益。  
军人户籍管理和相关权益保障办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 43** The State shall guarantee the household registration management and related rights and interests of servicemen and their family members.  
A citizen shall retain his or her household registration upon enlistment.  
Servicemen meeting the prescribed conditions may enjoy the relevant rights and interests of registered residents in the location of active service in terms of education, pension, medical care, housing security, etc.  
Measures for the household registration management and protection of related rights and interests of servicemen shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家对依法退出现役的军人，依照退役军人保障法律法规的有关规定，给予妥善安置和相应优待保障。

**Article 44** The State shall provide proper resettlement and corresponding preferential treatment guarantees for servicemen discharged from active service according to law, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the laws and regulations on the protection of ex-servicemen.

### 第五章　抚恤优待

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家和社会尊重军人、军人家庭为国防和军队建设做出的奉献和牺牲，优待军人、军人家属，抚恤优待烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，保障残疾军人的生活。  
国家建立抚恤优待保障体系，合理确定抚恤优待标准，逐步提高抚恤优待水平。

**Article 45** The State and society shall respect the dedication and sacrifice made by servicemen and their families for national defense and military building, give preferential treatment to servicemen and their family members, provide pensions and preferential treatment to the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness, and guarantee the livelihood of disabled servicemen.  
The State shall establish a system for ensuring pensions and preferential treatment, reasonably determine the standards for pensions and preferential treatment, and gradually raise the level of pensions and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　军人家属凭有关部门制发的证件享受法律法规规定的优待保障。具体办法由国务院和中央军事委员会有关部门制定。

**Article 46** Family members of servicemen shall enjoy preferential treatment guarantees provided for by laws and regulations by virtue of certificates issued by the relevant departments. The specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　各级人民政府应当保障抚恤优待对象享受公民普惠待遇，同时享受相应的抚恤优待待遇。

**Article 47** People's governments at all levels shall ensure that recipients of pensions and preferential treatment enjoy the general benefits for citizens while also enjoying the corresponding pensions and preferential treatment.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家实行军人死亡抚恤制度。  
军人死亡后被评定为烈士的，国家向烈士遗属颁发烈士证书，保障烈士遗属享受规定的烈士褒扬金、抚恤金和其他待遇。  
军人因公牺牲、病故的，国家向其遗属颁发证书，保障其遗属享受规定的抚恤金和其他待遇。

**Article 48** The State shall implement a system of death pensions for servicemen.  
Where a serviceman is assessed as a martyr after death, the State shall issue a martyr's certificate to the surviving family members, and ensure that they enjoy the prescribed martyr's commendation allowance, pension, and other benefits.  
Where a serviceman dies in the line of duty or dies of illness, the State shall issue a certificate to his or her surviving family members, and ensure that they enjoy the prescribed pension and other benefits.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家实行军人残疾抚恤制度。  
军人因战、因公、因病致残的，按照国家有关规定评定残疾等级并颁发证件，享受残疾抚恤金和其他待遇，符合规定条件的以安排工作、供养、退休等方式妥善安置。

**Article 49** The State shall implement a system of disability pensions for servicemen.  
Where a serviceman becomes disabled due to war, duty, or illness, his or her disability level shall be assessed and a certificate shall be issued in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and he or she shall enjoy disability pensions and other benefits; those meeting the prescribed conditions shall receive proper resettlement by means of job placement, support, retirement, etc.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家对军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属予以住房优待。  
军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，符合规定条件申请保障性住房的，或者居住农村且住房困难的，由当地人民政府优先解决。  
烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属符合前款规定情形的，当地人民政府给予优惠。

**Article 50** The State shall grant housing preferential treatment to the family members of servicemen and the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness.  
Where family members of servicemen and the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness apply for indemnificatory housing meeting the prescribed conditions, or live in rural areas with housing difficulties, the local people's government shall give priority to solving the problem.  
Where the surviving family members of martyrs, servicemen who have died in the line of duty, and servicemen who have died of illness fall under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the local people's government shall grant them preferential treatment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公立医疗机构应当为军人就医提供优待服务。军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，在军队医疗机构和公立医疗机构就医享受医疗优待。  
国家鼓励民营医疗机构为军人、军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属就医提供优待服务。  
国家和社会对残疾军人的医疗依法给予特别保障。

**Article 51** Public medical institutions shall provide preferential services for military personnel in seeking medical treatment. The family members of military personnel and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness shall enjoy preferential medical treatment when seeking medical treatment at military medical institutions and public medical institutions.  
The State encourages private medical institutions to provide preferential services to military personnel, their family members, and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness in seeking medical treatment.  
The State and society shall provide special guarantees for the medical treatment of disabled military personnel according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家依法保障军人配偶就业安置权益。机关、群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织，应当依法履行接收军人配偶就业安置的义务。  
军人配偶随军前在机关或者事业单位工作的，由安置地人民政府按照有关规定安排到相应的工作单位；在其他单位工作或者无工作单位的，由安置地人民政府提供就业指导和就业培训，优先协助就业。烈士、因公牺牲军人的遗属和符合规定条件的军人配偶，当地人民政府应当优先安排就业。

**Article 52** The State shall protect the employment placement rights and interests of spouses of military personnel according to law. State organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other organizations shall fulfill the obligation to accept the employment placement of spouses of military personnel according to law.  
If a spouse of a military personnel worked in a state organ or public institution before following the military, the people's government of the place of resettlement shall arrange for them to go to a corresponding work unit in accordance with relevant regulations; if they worked in another unit or had no work unit, the people's government of the place of resettlement shall provide employment guidance and employment training, and give priority assistance in employment. For the survivors of martyrs and military personnel who died in the line of duty and the spouses of military personnel who meet the prescribed conditions, the local people's government shall give priority in arranging employment.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家鼓励有用工需求的用人单位优先安排随军家属就业。国有企业在新招录职工时，应当按照用工需求的适当比例聘用随军家属；有条件的民营企业在新招录职工时，可以按照用工需求的适当比例聘用随军家属。

**Article 53** The State encourages employers with labor demand to give priority in arranging employment for family members who follow the military. When recruiting new employees, state-owned enterprises shall employ family members who follow the military in an appropriate proportion according to the labor demand; conditional private enterprises may, when recruiting new employees, employ family members who follow the military in an appropriate proportion according to the labor demand.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家鼓励和扶持军人配偶自主就业、自主创业。军人配偶从事个体经营的，按照国家有关优惠政策给予支持。

**Article 54** The State encourages and supports spouses of military personnel in self-directed employment and starting their own businesses. Where spouses of military personnel engage in self-employment, the State shall provide support in accordance with the relevant preferential policies.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家对军人子女予以教育优待。地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当为军人子女提供当地优质教育资源，创造接受良好教育的条件。  
军人子女入读公办义务教育阶段学校和普惠性幼儿园，可以在本人、父母、祖父母、外祖父母或者其他法定监护人户籍所在地，或者父母居住地、部队驻地入学，享受当地军人子女教育优待政策。  
军人子女报考普通高中、中等职业学校，同等条件下优先录取；烈士、因公牺牲军人的子女和符合规定条件的军人子女，按照当地军人子女教育优待政策享受录取等方面的优待。  
因公牺牲军人的子女和符合规定条件的军人子女报考高等学校，按照国家有关规定优先录取；烈士子女享受加分等优待。  
烈士子女和符合规定条件的军人子女按照规定享受奖学金、助学金和有关费用免除等学生资助政策。  
国家鼓励和扶持具备条件的民办学校，为军人子女和烈士、因公牺牲军人的子女提供教育优待。

**Article 55** The State provides preferential education treatment to children of military personnel. Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall provide local high-quality educational resources for children of military personnel and create conditions for them to receive good education.  
Children of military personnel who enroll in public compulsory education stage schools and inclusive kindergartens may enroll in the place of domicile of themselves, their parents, grandparents, maternal grandparents, or other legal guardians, or in the place of residence of their parents or the station of the military unit, and enjoy preferential education treatment policies for children of military personnel in the locality.  
When children of military personnel apply for admission to ordinary senior high schools and secondary vocational schools, they shall be given priority admission under equal conditions; children of martyrs, children of military personnel who died in the line of duty, and children of military personnel who meet the prescribed conditions shall enjoy preferential treatment in admission and other aspects in accordance with the local preferential education treatment policies for children of military personnel.  
When children of military personnel who died in the line of duty and children of military personnel who meet the prescribed conditions apply for admission to institutions of higher learning, they shall be given priority admission in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State; children of martyrs shall enjoy preferential treatment such as extra points.  
Children of martyrs and children of military personnel who meet the prescribed conditions shall enjoy student financial aid policies such as scholarships, grants, and exemption from relevant fees in accordance with regulations.  
The State encourages and supports qualified private schools to provide preferential educational treatment to children of military personnel and children of martyrs and military personnel who died in the line of duty.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，符合规定条件申请在国家兴办的光荣院、优抚医院集中供养、住院治疗、短期疗养的，享受优先、优惠待遇；申请到公办养老机构养老的，同等条件下优先安排。

**Article 56** The family members of military personnel and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness, who meet the prescribed conditions and apply for centralized support, inpatient treatment, or short-term recuperation in Glory Homes or special care hospitals run by the State, shall enjoy priority and preferential treatment; if they apply for elderly care in public elderly care institutions, they shall be given priority arrangement under equal conditions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　军人、军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，享受参观游览公园、博物馆、纪念馆、展览馆、名胜古迹以及文化和旅游等方面的优先、优惠服务。  
军人免费乘坐市内公共汽车、电车、轮渡和轨道交通工具。军人和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，以及与其随同出行的家属，乘坐境内运行的火车、轮船、长途公共汽车以及民航班机享受优先购票、优先乘车（船、机）等服务，残疾军人享受票价优惠。

**Article 57** Military personnel, their family members, and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness shall enjoy priority and preferential services in visiting and touring parks, museums, memorial halls, exhibition halls, places of historic interest and scenic beauty, as well as cultural and tourism services.  
Military personnel shall ride free of charge on urban buses, trams, ferries, and rail transit vehicles. Military personnel and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness, as well as their accompanying family members, shall enjoy priority services such as priority ticket purchase and priority boarding (boarding the train, ship, or plane) when taking trains, ships, long-distance buses, and civil aviation flights operating within the territory; disabled military personnel shall enjoy fare discounts.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　地方人民政府和军队单位对因自然灾害、意外事故、重大疾病等原因，基本生活出现严重困难的军人家庭，应当给予救助和慰问。

**Article 58** Local people's governments and military units shall provide relief and condolence to military families whose basic livelihood has fallen into serious difficulty due to natural disasters, accidents, serious diseases, or other reasons.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　地方人民政府和军队单位对在未成年子女入学入托、老年人养老等方面遇到困难的军人家庭，应当给予必要的帮扶。  
国家鼓励和支持企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织以及个人为困难军人家庭提供援助服务。

**Article 59** Local people's governments and military units shall give necessary assistance to military families that encounter difficulties in such aspects as enrollment of minor children in schools or kindergartens and elderly care for senior citizens.  
The State encourages and supports enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, other organizations, and individuals to provide assistance services to needy military families.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　军人、军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人遗属的合法权益受到侵害的，有权向有关国家机关和军队单位提出申诉、控告。负责受理的国家机关和军队单位，应当依法及时处理，不得推诿、拖延。依法向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当优先立案、审理和执行，人民检察院可以支持起诉。

**Article 60** Where the legitimate rights and interests of military personnel, their family members, and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness are infringed upon, they have the right to submit petitions or accusations to relevant state organs and military units. The state organs and military units responsible for receiving such petitions or accusations shall handle them in a timely manner according to law, and shall not make excuses or delay. If a lawsuit is filed with a People's Court according to law, the People's Court shall give priority to filing, trial, and enforcement, and the People's Procuratorate may support the prosecution.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　军人、军人家属和烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属维护合法权益遇到困难的，法律援助机构应当依法优先提供法律援助，司法机关应当依法优先提供司法救助。

**Article 61** Where military personnel, their family members, and the survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness encounter difficulties in safeguarding their legitimate rights and interests, legal aid institutions shall give priority to providing legal aid according to law, and judicial organs shall give priority to providing judicial assistance according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　侵害军人荣誉、名誉和其他相关合法权益，严重影响军人有效履行职责使命，致使社会公共利益受到损害的，人民检察院可以根据民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法的相关规定提起公益诉讼。

**Article 62** Where the honor, reputation, and other related legitimate rights and interests of military personnel are infringed upon, seriously affecting the effective performance of their duties and missions and causing damage to social public interests, the People's Procuratorate may initiate a public interest lawsuit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家机关及其工作人员、军队单位及其工作人员违反本法规定，在军人地位和权益保障工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位、主管部门或者上级机关责令改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 63** Where a state organ or its staff member, or a military unit or its staff member, violates the provisions of this Law by abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in the work of protecting the status, rights, and interests of military personnel, the unit, the competent authority, or the superior organ shall order correction; the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　群团组织、企业事业单位、社会组织和其他组织违反本法规定，不履行优待义务的，由有关部门责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 64** Where a people's organization, enterprise, public institution, social organization, or other organization violates the provisions of this Law by failing to fulfill the obligation of preferential treatment, the relevant department shall order correction; the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，通过大众传播媒介或者其他方式，诋毁、贬损军人荣誉，侮辱、诽谤军人名誉，或者故意毁损、玷污军人的荣誉标识的，由公安、文化和旅游、新闻出版、电影、广播电视、网信或者其他有关主管部门依据各自的职权责令改正，并依法予以处理；造成精神损害的，受害人有权请求精神损害赔偿。

**Article 65** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, vilifies or demeans the honor of military personnel, insults or slanders the reputation of military personnel, or intentionally destroys or defiles the honorary symbols of military personnel through mass media or other means, the public security, culture and tourism, press and publication, film, radio and television, cyberspace, or other relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and powers, order correction and deal with it according to law; if mental damage is caused, the victim has the right to claim compensation for mental damage.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　冒领或者以欺诈、伪造证明材料等手段骗取本法规定的相关荣誉、待遇或者抚恤优待的，由有关部门予以取消，依法给予没收违法所得等行政处罚。

**Article 66** Where a person fraudulently claims or obtains by fraud, forgery of supporting documents, or other means the relevant honors, treatment, or pensions and preferential treatment provided for in this Law, the relevant department shall cancel them and impose administrative penalties such as confiscation of illegal gains according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，侵害军人的合法权益，造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。  
违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 67** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of military personnel, causing property loss or other damage, he or she shall bear civil liability according to law.  
Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits an act that constitutes a violation of public security administration, he or she shall be given a public security administration punishment according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法所称军人家属，是指军人的配偶、父母（扶养人）、未成年子女、不能独立生活的成年子女。  
本法所称烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的遗属，是指烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的配偶、父母（扶养人）、子女，以及由其承担抚养义务的兄弟姐妹。

**Article 68** For the purposes of this Law, 'family members of military personnel' means the spouse, parents (supporting persons), minor children, and adult children who cannot live independently of the military personnel.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'survivors of martyrs, military personnel who died in the line of duty, and military personnel who died of illness' means the spouse, parents (supporting persons), children, and siblings whom they have the obligation to support of the martyr, military personnel who died in the line of duty, or military personnel who died of illness.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　中国人民武装警察部队服现役的警官、警士和义务兵等人员，适用本法。

**Article 69** This Law shall apply to the officers, non-commissioned officers, and conscripts on active service of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　省、自治区、直辖市可以结合本地实际情况，根据本法制定保障军人地位和权益的具体办法。

**Article 70** Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of local actual conditions and in accordance with this Law, formulate specific measures for protecting the status and rights and interests of military personnel.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本法自2021年8月1日起施行。

**Article 71** This Law shall come into force as of August 1, 2021.
