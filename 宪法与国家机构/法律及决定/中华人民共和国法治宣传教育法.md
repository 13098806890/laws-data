### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强法治宣传教育，提升全民法治素养和社会治理法治化水平，形成全社会尊法学法守法用法的良好氛围，夯实全面依法治国的社会基础，推动建设更高水平的社会主义法治国家，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen legal publicity and education, enhance the legal literacy of the entire population and the rule-of-law level of social governance, create a favorable atmosphere for the whole society to respect, study, abide by and apply the law, consolidate the social foundation for law-based governance in all respects, and promote the building of a higher-level socialist country governed by law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　法治宣传教育坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，贯彻习近平法治思想，弘扬社会主义法治精神，推动法治成为社会共识和基本准则，为全面建设社会主义现代化国家营造良好法治环境。

**Article 2** Legal publicity and education shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, be guided by Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, implement Xi Jinping Thought on the Rule of Law, promote the spirit of socialist rule of law, promote the rule of law as a social consensus and basic norm, and create a favorable legal environment for comprehensively building a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　法治宣传教育坚持以人民为中心、服务大局、守正创新，坚持与依法治理、法治实践相融合，坚持依法治国和以德治国相结合，坚持依法治国和依规治党有机统一，践行社会主义核心价值观，健全党委领导、人大监督、政府主导、各方协同、社会参与的工作机制。

**Article 3** Legal publicity and education shall adhere to a people-centered approach, serve the overall situation, uphold integrity and innovation, integrate with law-based governance and rule-of-law practice, combine rule of law with rule of virtue, unify law-based governance of the country and rule-based governance over the Party, practice core socialist values, and improve the working mechanism of Party committee leadership, people's congress supervision, government leadership, coordination among all parties, and social participation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行公民终身法治教育制度，将法治教育纳入国民教育、干部教育、社会教育体系。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a system of lifelong legal education for citizens, and incorporate legal education into the systems of national education, education for officials, and social education.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　法治宣传教育的主要内容包括：  
（一）习近平法治思想；  
（二）宪法的规定、原则和精神；  
（三）法治原则、法律制度和法律常识；  
（四）全面依法治国的实践和成就；  
（五）社会主义法治文化、中华优秀传统法律文化；  
（六）其他应当纳入法治宣传教育的内容。

**Article 5** The main contents of legal publicity and education include:  
(1) Xi Jinping Thought on the Rule of Law;  
(2) The provisions, principles and spirit of the Constitution;  
(3) Principles of rule of law, legal system and common legal knowledge;  
(4) The practice and achievements of law-based governance in all respects;  
(5) The culture of socialist rule of law and excellent traditional Chinese legal culture;  
(6) Other contents that shall be included in legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家编制全国法治宣传教育规划。  
省、自治区、直辖市应当依据全国法治宣传教育规划，结合实际情况制定本行政区域相关规划。

**Article 6** The State shall formulate a national plan for legal publicity and education.  
Provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate relevant plans for their respective administrative regions in accordance with the national plan for legal publicity and education and in light of their actual conditions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院司法行政部门主管全国法治宣传教育工作。县级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门主管本行政区域的法治宣传教育工作。  
中央和国家机关各部门应当将法治宣传教育工作纳入本部门、本系统法治建设总体部署并组织落实。

**Article 7** The judicial administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the legal publicity and education work nationwide. The judicial administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the legal publicity and education work within their respective administrative regions.  
All departments of the central and state organs shall incorporate legal publicity and education work into the overall deployment of rule-of-law construction of their departments and systems and organize its implementation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各群团组织、事业单位，应当结合各自的职责组织开展法治宣传教育。  
鼓励和支持企业、其他组织以及个人开展和参与法治宣传教育。

**Article 8** State organs, the armed forces, all political parties, all mass organizations, and public institutions shall organize legal publicity and education in light of their respective functions and duties.  
Enterprises, other organizations and individuals shall be encouraged and supported to carry out and participate in legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家机关按照“谁执法谁普法”的精神，实行普法责任制。

**Article 9** State organs shall, in accordance with the principle of "whoever enforces the law shall popularize the law", implement the responsibility system for law popularization.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家加强涉外法治宣传教育，提升法治文化国际传播能力，积极阐释中国特色涉外法治理念、主张和成功实践。  
国家鼓励和支持开展法治宣传教育国际交流合作。

**Article 10** The State shall strengthen legal publicity and education concerning foreign affairs, enhance the capacity for international dissemination of the rule-of-law culture, and actively expound on Chinese-characteristic concepts, propositions and successful practices in foreign-related rule of law.  
The State shall encourage and support international exchanges and cooperation in legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　开展法治宣传教育，应当遵守宪法和法律，遵守公共秩序，尊重社会公德。

**Article 11** In carrying out legal publicity and education, the Constitution and laws shall be complied with, public order shall be observed, and social morality shall be respected.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在法治宣传教育工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in legal publicity and education work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant regulations of the State.

### 第二章　社会法治宣传教育

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家加强宪法宣传教育，推动宪法宣传教育常态化、长效化，增强全社会的宪法意识，弘扬宪法精神，加强宪法实施。  
每年12月4日为国家宪法日，国家通过多种形式开展宪法宣传教育活动。

**Article 13** The State shall strengthen publicity and education on the Constitution, promote the normalization and long-term effectiveness of such publicity and education, enhance the constitutional awareness of the whole society, promote the spirit of the Constitution, and strengthen the implementation of the Constitution.  
December 4 of each year shall be the National Constitution Day, and the State shall carry out publicity and education activities on the Constitution in various forms.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家加强宪法和特别行政区基本法宣传教育，增强香港特别行政区同胞、澳门特别行政区同胞维护宪法和基本法确定的特别行政区宪制秩序的意识。

**Article 14** The State shall strengthen publicity and education on the Constitution and the Basic Laws of the special administrative regions, and enhance the awareness of the compatriots of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region in upholding the constitutional order of the special administrative regions as established by the Constitution and the Basic Laws.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家通过多种形式开展国家安全法治宣传教育，增强全民国家安全意识。

**Article 15** The State shall, through various forms, carry out legal publicity and education on national security, and enhance the national security awareness of the entire people.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家利用重大节日、纪念日等，结合法律法规的公布施行，组织开展群众性法治宣传教育活动，持续深入开展全民普法，推动法治宣传教育融入法治实践、基层治理和日常生活，在全社会树立宪法法律至上、法律面前人人平等等基本法治观念。

**Article 16** The State shall, by utilizing major festivals, commemorative days, etc., and in conjunction with the promulgation and implementation of laws and regulations, organize and carry out mass legal publicity and education activities, continue to deepen law popularization among the entire people, and promote the integration of legal publicity and education into rule-of-law practice, grassroots governance and daily life, and establish in the whole society basic rule-of-law concepts such as the supremacy of the Constitution and laws and equality of all before the law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　法律法规制定机关应当加强立法全过程宣传解读，积极采取多种形式发布信息、介绍情况、回应关切；通过公开征求意见，召开座谈会、论证会、听证会，设立基层立法联系点等形式，扩大社会参与，将立法与法治宣传教育有机结合。

**Article 17** The authorities that formulate laws and regulations shall strengthen publicity and interpretation of the entire legislative process, actively adopt various forms to release information, introduce situations, and respond to concerns; expand social participation by soliciting public opinions, holding symposia, appraisal meetings, and hearings, and establishing grassroots legislative contact points, so as to organically integrate legislation with legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行政机关应当将法治宣传教育融入日常管理、服务、执法和争议解决过程，综合运用释法说理、疏导回应、指导示范、组织听证、发布解读典型案例等方式，开展法治宣传教育。

**Article 18** Administrative organs shall integrate legal publicity and education into the process of daily management, services, law enforcement, and dispute resolution, and comprehensively use methods such as explaining the law and reasoning, counseling and responding, providing guidance and demonstration, organizing hearings, and publishing and interpreting typical cases to carry out legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　监察机关、审判机关、检察机关等应当将法治宣传教育融入案件办理过程，结合各自的职责，运用依法公开审理案件、法律文书释法说理、发布解读典型案例等方式，开展法治宣传教育。

**Article 19** Supervisory organs, adjudicative organs, procuratorial organs, etc. shall integrate legal publicity and education into the process of handling cases, and in light of their respective functions and duties, use methods such as public trial of cases according to law, explanation of law and reasoning in legal documents, and publishing and interpreting typical cases to carry out legal publicity and education.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民族事务部门应当以铸牢中华民族共同体意识为主线，开展法治宣传教育，促进民族团结进步，增强中华民族凝聚力，推进中华民族共同体建设。

**Article 20** The departments for ethnic affairs shall, with forging a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation as the main line, carry out legal publicity and education, promote ethnic unity and progress, enhance the cohesion of the Chinese nation, and advance the building of the Chinese national community.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　宗教事务部门应当加强法治宣传教育，引导宗教界人士和信教群众自觉维护宪法法律权威，在法律法规规定范围内开展活动。

**Article 21** The departments for religious affairs shall strengthen legal publicity and education, guide religious personnel and followers to consciously uphold the authority of the Constitution and laws, and conduct activities within the scope prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　网信、电信、公安等部门应当加强对网络服务提供者和网络用户的法治宣传教育，引导其自觉遵守法律法规、尊重社会公德，营造文明安全的网络环境。

**Article 22** The departments of cyberspace administration, telecommunications, public security, etc. shall strengthen legal publicity and education for network service providers and network users, guide them to consciously abide by laws and regulations and respect social morality, and create a civilized and safe network environment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　应急管理、卫生健康、公安等部门应当组织开展自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件和社会安全事件应对的法治宣传教育，推动全社会提高突发事件预防和应对能力。

**Article 23** The departments of emergency management, health, public security, etc. shall organize legal publicity and education on the response to natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, and social security incidents, and promote the whole society to improve the ability to prevent and respond to emergencies.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民政、人力资源社会保障等部门应当根据老年人、残疾人、进城务工人员等群体的需求和特点，开展有针对性的法治宣传教育，提高其依法维护自身合法权益的意识和能力。  
对老年人、残疾人开展法治宣传教育，条件具备的同步采取语音、大字、盲文、手语等无障碍信息交流方式。

**Article 24** The departments of civil affairs, human resources and social security, etc. shall, in light of the needs and characteristics of the elderly, persons with disabilities, migrant workers in cities, and other groups, carry out targeted legal publicity and education, and enhance their awareness and ability to safeguard their lawful rights and interests according to law.  
When carrying out legal publicity and education for the elderly and persons with disabilities, where conditions permit, barrier-free information exchange methods such as voice, large print, Braille, sign language, etc. shall be adopted simultaneously.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级人民政府应当加强基层法治宣传教育，支持、指导和帮助村民委员会、居民委员会开展法治宣传教育，引导村民、居民依法维护自身合法权益、表达诉求、解决纠纷。

**Article 25** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen grassroots legal publicity and education, support, guide and assist villagers' committees and residents' committees in carrying out legal publicity and education, and guide villagers and residents to safeguard their lawful rights and interests, express demands, and resolve disputes according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级人民政府应当根据新经济新领域发展需要，加强对相关组织和人员的法治宣传教育，引导其依法维护自身合法权益、履行法定义务。

**Article 26** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the development needs of the new economy and new fields, strengthen legal publicity and education for relevant organizations and personnel, and guide them to safeguard their lawful rights and interests and perform their statutory obligations according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、工商业联合会、法学会、残疾人联合会等群团组织，应当在各自的职责范围内开展法治宣传教育。

**Article 27** Mass organizations such as trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, federations of industry and commerce, law societies, and federations of persons with disabilities shall carry out legal publicity and education within the scope of their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　行业协会、商会等加强对会员的法治宣传教育，提高会员维护自身合法权益、防范法律风险的能力，提升行业治理法治化水平。

**Article 28** Trade associations, chambers of commerce, etc. shall strengthen legal publicity and education for their members, enhance the members' ability to safeguard their lawful rights and interests and prevent legal risks, and improve the level of rule-of-law in industry governance.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　企业加强对职工的法治教育培训，建立并实施管理人员学法用法制度，提升管理法治化水平。

**Article 29** Enterprises shall strengthen legal education and training for employees, establish and implement a system for management personnel to study and use the law, and improve the level of rule-of-law in management.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　法律服务机构和法律服务工作者在提供法律服务、调解处理矛盾纠纷时，结合工作开展法治宣传教育。

**Article 30** Legal service institutions and legal service workers shall, when providing legal services and mediating and handling disputes, carry out legal publicity and education in conjunction with their work.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公共场所的经营者、管理者应当依法在其经营管理的场所内，围绕消费者权益保护、反电信网络诈骗、食品药品安全、消防安全、个人信息保护、产品责任等群众关心的问题，开展法治宣传教育。  
网络服务提供者应当依法对从业人员和网络用户开展法治宣传教育。  
金融机构应当依法对工作人员和客户开展法治宣传教育。

**Article 31** Operators and managers of public places shall, within their venues under management, carry out legal publicity and education according to law, focusing on issues of public concern such as consumer rights protection, anti-telecommunications and online fraud, food and drug safety, fire safety, protection of personal information, and product liability.  
Network service providers shall carry out legal publicity and education for their employees and network users according to law.  
Financial institutions shall carry out legal publicity and education for their staff and clients according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家加强对开展境外投资合作、对外援助的企业以及出境人员等的法治宣传教育，提供涉外法律服务，引导其遵守我国和当地法律，防范和应对法律风险。

**Article 32** The State shall strengthen legal publicity and education for enterprises engaged in overseas investment and cooperation, foreign aid, and outbound personnel, provide foreign-related legal services, and guide them to comply with the laws of China and the local laws, and prevent and respond to legal risks.

### 第三章　国家工作人员法治宣传教育

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家加强国家工作人员宪法宣传教育。国家工作人员应当加强宪法学习，树立宪法意识，恪守宪法原则，弘扬宪法精神，履行宪法使命。  
国家实行宪法宣誓制度，彰显宪法权威，激励和教育国家工作人员忠于宪法、遵守宪法、维护宪法。

**Article 33** The State shall strengthen publicity and education on the Constitution for state functionaries. State functionaries shall strengthen the study of the Constitution, establish constitutional awareness, abide by constitutional principles, promote the spirit of the Constitution, and fulfill the mission of the Constitution.  
The State shall implement the system of constitutional oath, to demonstrate the authority of the Constitution, and to inspire and educate state functionaries to be loyal to the Constitution, abide by the Constitution, and uphold the Constitution.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家工作人员应当带头尊法学法守法用法，树立依法履职的法治观念，提高运用法治思维和法治方式深化改革、推动发展、化解矛盾、维护稳定、应对风险的能力。

**Article 34** State functionaries shall take the lead in respecting, studying, abiding by and applying the law, establish the rule-of-law concept of performing duties according to law, and improve the ability to use rule-of-law thinking and rule-of-law methods to deepen reforms, promote development, resolve conflicts, maintain stability, and respond to risks.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　录用国家工作人员，应当将有关法律知识纳入录用考试内容。

**Article 35** When recruiting state functionaries, relevant legal knowledge shall be included in the content of the recruitment examination.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　健全国家工作人员日常学法制度。国家工作人员所在单位应当根据工作性质和特点，采取多种形式开展法治教育。  
有关部门应当将法治教育纳入国家工作人员教育培训规划、计划。

**Article 36** The system for state functionaries to study law on a daily basis shall be improved. The units where state functionaries work shall, based on the nature and characteristics of their work, adopt various forms to carry out legal education.  
Relevant departments shall incorporate legal education into the education and training plans and programs for state functionaries.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实行领导干部应知应会党内法规和法律法规清单制度。  
有关部门、单位应当将宪法和与履职密切相关的法律法规纳入领导干部应知应会法律法规清单，并动态调整。

**Article 37** A list system of inner-Party regulations and laws and regulations that leading officials shall know and be able to apply shall be implemented.  
Relevant departments and units shall include the Constitution and laws and regulations closely related to the performance of duties into the list of laws and regulations that leading officials shall know and be able to apply, and make dynamic adjustments.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　加强对领导干部学法用法情况的监督。领导干部学法用法情况应当按照规定列入年度述职内容。

**Article 38** Supervision over leading officials' study and application of law shall be strengthened. The situation of leading officials' study and application of law shall be included in their annual work reports in accordance with regulations.

### 第四章　青少年法治宣传教育

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家、社会、学校、家庭加强协同配合，根据青少年的身心特点和成长需求，对青少年开展法治宣传教育，普及青少年在家庭生活、校园学习、社会活动中所必需的法律知识，教育引导青少年树立守法意识、规则意识、诚信意识和防范违法犯罪的意识，自觉遵守法律规定，规范自身行为，维护自身合法权益，尊重他人合法权益。

**Article 39** The State, society, schools, and families shall strengthen coordination and cooperation, carry out legal publicity and education for juveniles based on their physical and mental characteristics and growth needs, popularize the legal knowledge necessary for juveniles in family life, campus study, and social activities, educate and guide juveniles to establish awareness of law-abiding, rules, integrity, and prevention of illegal and criminal activities, consciously abide by legal provisions, regulate their own conduct, safeguard their lawful rights and interests, and respect the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　教育行政部门应当将法治教育融入学校教育的各个阶段，明确各级各类学校法治教育目标、内容以及评价要求，推广法治实践教学和案例教学，将法治教育内容融入相关教材，将法治素养纳入学生综合素质评价范围。  
教育行政部门应当加强法治教育师资培养和培训，建立稳定的专职或者兼职法治教育师资队伍。  
教育行政部门应当加强对学校各类法治宣传教育活动的协调指导。

**Article 40** Education administrative departments shall integrate legal education into all stages of school education, clarify the objectives, contents and evaluation requirements of legal education for schools at various levels and types, promote practical teaching and case teaching on the rule of law, incorporate the contents of legal education into relevant textbooks, and include legal literacy in the evaluation scope of students' comprehensive quality.  
Education administrative departments shall strengthen the cultivation and training of teachers for legal education, and establish a stable team of full-time or part-time teachers for legal education.  
Education administrative departments shall strengthen the coordination and guidance of various legal publicity and education activities in schools.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级各类学校应当开设法治教育课程，利用青少年法治教育实践基地等场所开展法治实践教育。中小学校应当按照要求聘任法治副校长，配备受过专业培养或者经过专门培训、能够胜任法治教育任务的教师，可以聘任校外法治辅导员。  
各级各类学校应当将法治宣传教育融入学校教育与日常管理，开展多种形式的法治主题教育，加强校园法治文化建设。

**Article 41** Schools at various levels and types shall offer legal education courses, and use venues such as juvenile legal education practice bases to carry out practical legal education. Primary and secondary schools shall, as required, appoint vice principals for legal affairs, equip teachers who have received professional training or specialized training and are competent for legal education tasks, and may appoint off-campus legal counselors.  
Schools at various levels and types shall integrate legal publicity and education into school education and daily management, carry out various forms of thematic legal education, and strengthen the construction of campus rule-of-law culture.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　审判机关、检察机关以及公安、司法行政等部门，律师协会等社会组织，应当支持学校按照教育教学计划开展法治教育，提供法治实践教育资源，支持教育行政部门、学校开发法治教育课程。

**Article 42** Adjudicative organs, procuratorial organs, and departments of public security, judicial administration, etc., and social organizations such as bar associations, shall support schools in carrying out legal education in accordance with teaching plans, provide resources for practical legal education, and support education administrative departments and schools in developing legal education courses.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当提高对未成年人进行法治教育的意识和能力，针对不同年龄段未成年人的身心发展特点，教育未成年人增强自我保护意识，养成守法行为习惯。

**Article 43** Parents or other guardians of minors shall enhance their awareness and ability to provide legal education to minors, and, in light of the physical and mental development characteristics of minors of different age groups, educate minors to enhance their self-protection awareness and develop law-abiding behavioral habits.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　网络服务提供者应当依法开展未成年人网络法治宣传教育，履行未成年人网络保护义务，引导未成年人养成文明网络行为习惯，营造有利于未成年人身心健康的网络环境。

**Article 44** Network service providers shall carry out online legal publicity and education for minors according to law, fulfill the obligations of online protection for minors, guide minors to develop civilized online behavioral habits, and create a network environment conducive to the physical and mental health of minors.

### 第五章　保障与监督

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府应当将法治宣传教育工作纳入本地区法治建设总体部署，纳入精神文明建设和平安建设。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate legal publicity and education work into the overall deployment of rule-of-law construction in their regions, and into the construction of spiritual civilization and the construction of peace.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　司法行政部门应当会同有关部门、单位加强法治宣传教育人才队伍建设，分层分类开展培训。  
司法行政部门在基层群众中培养具有较好法治素养和基层工作经验，能发挥示范作用的人员，指导、支持其积极开展法治宣传教育，带头参与法治实践。

**Article 46** Judicial administration departments shall, together with relevant departments and units, strengthen the development of the talent team for legal publicity and education, and carry out training by levels and categories.  
Judicial administration departments shall cultivate among grassroots people individuals with good legal literacy and grassroots work experience who can play an exemplary role, and guide and support them to actively carry out legal publicity and education and take the lead in participating in rule-of-law practice.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　司法行政部门应当推进法治宣传教育专家库建设，选聘优秀法治人才组成普法讲师团，组织开展法治宣讲活动。其他部门可以根据需要，建设具有部门工作特点的法治宣传教育专家库或者讲师团。  
鼓励和支持国家工作人员，从事法学教育、研究工作的人员和法学专业学生，以及从事法律服务工作的人员等，开展普法志愿服务，参与有关部门和单位开展的法律咨询、法治讲座等活动。

**Article 47** Judicial administration departments shall advance the construction of an expert database for legal publicity and education, select and appoint outstanding rule-of-law talents to form a lecture group for law popularization, and organize and carry out legal lecture activities. Other departments may, as needed, build an expert database or lecture group for legal publicity and education with the characteristics of their work.  
State functionaries, personnel engaged in legal education and research, law students, and personnel engaged in legal services, etc., shall be encouraged and supported to carry out volunteer services for law popularization, and to participate in legal consultation, legal lectures, and other activities carried out by relevant departments and units.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家鼓励和支持法治宣传教育理论研究，加强法治传播、法治文化等相关人才培养。

**Article 48** The State shall encourage and support theoretical research on legal publicity and education, and strengthen the cultivation of relevant talents such as those in rule-of-law communication and rule-of-law culture.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家健全媒体公益普法制度，引导报刊、广播、电视等新闻媒体和网络服务提供者履行公益普法责任，开展公益法治宣传教育。  
报刊、广播、电视等新闻媒体和网络服务提供者应当通过开设法治专栏专题、刊播法治公益广告、报道法治新闻等形式，加强法治宣传教育。

**Article 49** The State shall improve the public-interest law popularization system of the media, and guide news media such as newspapers, radio, television and network service providers to fulfill their responsibilities for public-interest law popularization and carry out public-interest legal publicity and education.  
News media such as newspapers, radio, television and network service providers shall strengthen legal publicity and education by setting up legal columns and special topics, publishing and broadcasting legal public-interest advertisements, and reporting legal news.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　文化和旅游、新闻出版、广播电视、电影、网信等部门应当加强法治文化建设，鼓励和支持优秀法治文艺作品、出版物、广播电视、电影和网络视听作品的创作生产和宣传推广。

**Article 50** The departments of culture and tourism, press and publication, radio and television, film, cyberspace administration, etc. shall strengthen the construction of rule-of-law culture, and encourage and support the creation, production, publicity and promotion of outstanding rule-of-law literary and artistic works, publications, radio and television programs, films, and online audiovisual works.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对法治文化相关文物等文化遗产的保护和利用，宣传代表性人物的事迹和精神，弘扬社会主义法治文化，推动中华优秀传统法律文化的创造性转化、创新性发展。

**Article 51** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the protection and utilization of cultural relics and other cultural heritage related to the rule-of-law culture, publicize the deeds and spirit of representative figures, promote the socialist rule-of-law culture, and advance the creative transformation and innovative development of the excellent traditional Chinese legal culture.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上人民政府应当依据国土空间规划，因地制宜统筹规划、建设法治文化场所，在公共设施建设和公共空间利用时体现法治元素。  
司法行政部门应当建立法治文化场所名录，向社会公布。

**Article 52** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the territorial spatial plan, make overall plans and build rule-of-law cultural venues in light of local conditions, and reflect rule-of-law elements in the construction of public facilities and the utilization of public spaces.  
The judicial administration department shall establish a directory of rule-of-law cultural venues and make it available to the public.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　负有法治宣传教育职责的部门、单位应当根据不同地区、行业、群体的法治需求精准开展法治宣传教育，采取生动活泼、通俗易懂、群众喜闻乐见的形式和方法，发挥法治人物、见义勇为人员等典型示范作用，讲好中国宪法故事、中国法治故事，增强法治宣传教育的针对性和实效性，力戒形式主义。

**Article 53** The departments and entities responsible for rule-of-law publicity and education shall, based on the rule-of-law needs of different regions, industries, and groups, precisely carry out rule-of-law publicity and education, adopt lively, easy-to-understand, and popular forms and methods, give full play to the typical exemplary roles of rule-of-law figures and persons who act bravely for a just cause, tell well the stories of the Chinese Constitution and the Chinese rule of law, enhance the pertinence and effectiveness of rule-of-law publicity and education, and resolutely avoid formalism.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　鼓励创新法治宣传教育形式和方法，支持运用新平台新技术新产品和无障碍方式开展法治宣传教育。  
法治宣传教育的内容应当准确、清楚，避免误导公众。

**Article 54** The innovation of forms and methods of rule-of-law publicity and education shall be encouraged, and support shall be given to the use of new platforms, new technologies, new products, and accessible means to carry out rule-of-law publicity and education.  
The content of rule-of-law publicity and education shall be accurate and clear to avoid misleading the public.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上人民政府应当将法治宣传教育经费列入本级预算，加强资金管理，提高资金使用效益，保障法治宣传教育工作需要。  
国家机关、群团组织、事业单位应当合理安排经费，保障法治宣传教育工作开展。  
国家鼓励、引导社会资金参与法治宣传教育工作，并依法加强管理。

**Article 55** The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the funds for rule-of-law publicity and education in their respective budgets, strengthen fund management, improve the efficiency of fund use, and ensure the needs for rule-of-law publicity and education.  
State organs, people's organizations, and public institutions shall reasonably arrange funds to ensure the development of rule-of-law publicity and education.  
The State encourages and guides social funds to participate in rule-of-law publicity and education, and shall strengthen management according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　司法行政部门组织对法治宣传教育规划实施情况进行评估，并将评估结果向社会公布，接受社会监督。

**Article 56** The judicial administration department shall organize the evaluation of the implementation of the rule-of-law publicity and education plan, and shall publish the evaluation results to the public and accept public supervision.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府司法行政部门发现同级负有法治宣传教育职责的部门、单位，未依法履行法治宣传教育职责的，应当及时发出普法提示。有关部门、单位收到普法提示后，应当及时采取改进措施并反馈相关情况。

**Article 57** Where the judicial administration department of a people's government at or above the county level discovers that a department or entity at the same level responsible for rule-of-law publicity and education fails to perform its duties according to law, it shall promptly issue a law popularization reminder. The relevant department or entity, upon receiving the law popularization reminder, shall promptly take corrective measures and provide feedback on the relevant situation.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　各级人民代表大会常务委员会应当通过听取和审议专项工作报告、执法检查、专题询问、专题调研等方式，加强对法治宣传教育工作的监督。  
县级以上人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告法治宣传教育工作情况，依法接受监督。

**Article 58** The standing committees of the people's congresses at all levels shall strengthen supervision over the work of rule-of-law publicity and education by means such as hearing and deliberating special work reports, conducting law enforcement inspections, special inquiries, and special investigations.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding levels on the situation of rule-of-law publicity and education, and shall accept supervision according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上人民政府司法行政部门未依法履行法治宣传教育工作职责的，由本级人民政府或者上级司法行政部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，予以通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
有关部门、单位收到普法提示后，未及时采取改进措施的，予以通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 59** Where the judicial administration department of a people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its duties for rule-of-law publicity and education according to law, the people's government at the same level or the judicial administration department at a higher level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be given a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a relevant department or entity fails to take corrective measures in a timely manner after receiving a law popularization reminder, it shall be given a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反法治宣传教育经费管理规定的，由有关主管部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 60** Whoever violates the provisions on the management of funds for rule-of-law publicity and education shall be ordered by the competent authority to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　侵占、破坏法治宣传教育设施，损毁展品、器材的，由有关主管部门责令限期改正，并给予批评教育。

**Article 61** Whoever encroaches upon or damages rule-of-law publicity and education facilities, or destroys exhibits or equipment, shall be ordered by the competent authority to make corrections within a specified time limit and shall be given criticism and education.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定开展法治宣传教育，造成不良社会影响的，由有关主管部门予以制止，并给予批评教育。

**Article 62** Whoever carries out rule-of-law publicity and education in violation of the provisions of this Law, causing adverse social impact, shall be stopped by the competent authority and shall be given criticism and education.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，造成他人损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and causes harm to others shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security management, the person shall be given a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, the person shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队依照本法和中央军事委员会的有关规定开展法治宣传教育工作。

**Article 64** The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall carry out rule-of-law publicity and education in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2025年11月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of November 1, 2025.
