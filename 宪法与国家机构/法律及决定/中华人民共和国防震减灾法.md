# 中华人民共和国防震减灾法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2008-12-27  
**生效日期**：2009-05-01

> （1997年12月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过　2008年12月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　防震减灾规划 第三章　地震监测预报 第四章　地震灾害预防 第五章　地震应急救援 第六章　地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建 第七章　监督管理 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了防御和减轻地震灾害，保护人民生命和财产安全，促进经济社会的可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测预报、地震灾害预防、地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建等防震减灾活动，适用本法。 第三条　防震减灾工作，实行预防为主、防御与救助相结合的方针。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防御和减轻地震灾害，保护人民生命和财产安全，促进经济社会的可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of preventing and mitigating earthquake disasters, protecting the safety of people's lives and property, and promoting the sustainable development of the economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测预报、地震灾害预防、地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建等防震减灾活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities for earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, such as earthquake monitoring and prediction, earthquake disaster prevention, earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement, and recovery and reconstruction, conducted in the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　防震减灾工作，实行预防为主、防御与救助相结合的方针。

**Article 3** The work of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction shall follow the policy of giving priority to prevention and combining prevention with rescue.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对防震减灾工作的领导，将防震减灾工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费列入财政预算。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over the work of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, incorporate such work into the national economic and social development plans at the corresponding level, and include the necessary funds in the fiscal budget.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在国务院的领导下，国务院地震工作主管部门和国务院经济综合宏观调控、建设、民政、卫生、公安以及其他有关部门，按照职责分工，各负其责，密切配合，共同做好防震减灾工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构和其他有关部门在本级人民政府领导下，按照职责分工，各负其责，密切配合，共同做好本行政区域的防震减灾工作。

**Article 5** Under the leadership of the State Council, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the relevant departments of the State Council for comprehensive economic macro-control, construction, civil affairs, health, public security, and other departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, assume their own responsibilities, closely cooperate, and jointly perform the work of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, under the leadership of the people's government at the same level, according to their respective functions and duties, assume their own responsibilities, closely cooperate, and jointly perform the work of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院抗震救灾指挥机构负责统一领导、指挥和协调全国抗震救灾工作。县级以上地方人民政府抗震救灾指挥机构负责统一领导、指挥和协调本行政区域的抗震救灾工作。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，承担本级人民政府抗震救灾指挥机构的日常工作。

**Article 6** The command organization for earthquake relief under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified leadership, command, and coordination of earthquake relief work nationwide. The command organizations for earthquake relief under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the unified leadership, command, and coordination of earthquake relief work within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall undertake the daily work of the command organizations for earthquake relief at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当组织开展防震减灾知识的宣传教育，增强公民的防震减灾意识，提高全社会的防震减灾能力。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out publicity and education on knowledge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, enhance citizens' awareness of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, and improve the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction capacity of the whole society.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人都有依法参加防震减灾活动的义务。  
国家鼓励、引导社会组织和个人开展地震群测群防活动，对地震进行监测和预防。  
国家鼓励、引导志愿者参加防震减灾活动。

**Article 8** Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to participate in earthquake prevention and disaster reduction activities according to law.  
The State encourages and guides social organizations and individuals to carry out mass observation and prevention activities for earthquakes, and to monitor and prevent earthquakes.  
The State encourages and guides volunteers to participate in earthquake prevention and disaster reduction activities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织，依照本法以及其他有关法律、行政法规、军事法规的规定和国务院、中央军事委员会的命令，执行抗震救灾任务，保护人民生命和财产安全。

**Article 9** The Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organization shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws, administrative regulations, and military regulations, as well as orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, perform earthquake relief tasks to protect the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　从事防震减灾活动，应当遵守国家有关防震减灾标准。

**Article 10** Those engaged in earthquake prevention and disaster reduction activities shall comply with the relevant national standards for earthquake prevention and disaster reduction.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励、支持防震减灾的科学技术研究，逐步提高防震减灾科学技术研究经费投入，推广先进的科学研究成果，加强国际合作与交流，提高防震减灾工作水平。  
对在防震减灾工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 11** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, gradually increases investment in such research, promotes advanced scientific research results, strengthens international cooperation and exchange, and improves the level of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction work.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to earthquake prevention and disaster reduction shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　防震减灾规划

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院地震工作主管部门会同国务院有关部门组织编制国家防震减灾规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构会同同级有关部门，根据上一级防震减灾规划和本行政区域的实际情况，组织编制本行政区域的防震减灾规划，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施，并报上一级人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构备案。

**Article 12** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the formulation of the national earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, based on the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan of the next higher level and the actual conditions of their own administrative regions, organize the formulation of the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan for their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval before implementation, and shall be filed with the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　编制防震减灾规划，应当遵循统筹安排、突出重点、合理布局、全面预防的原则，以震情和震害预测结果为依据，并充分考虑人民生命和财产安全及经济社会发展、资源环境保护等需要。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当根据编制防震减灾规划的需要，及时提供有关资料。

**Article 13** The formulation of an earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan shall follow the principles of overall planning, highlighting priorities, rational layout, and comprehensive prevention, based on the results of earthquake situation and earthquake disaster prediction, and shall fully take into account the needs of people's life and property safety, economic and social development, and protection of resources and the environment.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to the needs of formulating the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan, provide relevant materials in a timely manner.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　防震减灾规划的内容应当包括：震情形势和防震减灾总体目标，地震监测台网建设布局，地震灾害预防措施，地震应急救援措施，以及防震减灾技术、信息、资金、物资等保障措施。  
编制防震减灾规划，应当对地震重点监视防御区的地震监测台网建设、震情跟踪、地震灾害预防措施、地震应急准备、防震减灾知识宣传教育等作出具体安排。

**Article 14** The contents of an earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan shall include: the earthquake situation and overall objectives for earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, the construction layout of earthquake monitoring networks, earthquake disaster prevention measures, earthquake emergency rescue measures, and safeguard measures such as technology, information, funds, and materials for earthquake prevention and disaster reduction.  
When formulating an earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan, specific arrangements shall be made for the construction of earthquake monitoring networks, earthquake situation tracking, earthquake disaster prevention measures, earthquake emergency preparedness, and publicity and education on knowledge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction in key earthquake monitoring and defense areas.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　防震减灾规划报送审批前，组织编制机关应当征求有关部门、单位、专家和公众的意见。  
防震减灾规划报送审批文件中应当附具意见采纳情况及理由。

**Article 15** Before submitting the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan for approval, the organizing and formulating body shall solicit opinions from relevant departments, units, experts, and the public.  
The document for submitting the earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan for approval shall include the status of adoption of opinions and the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　防震减灾规划一经批准公布，应当严格执行；因震情形势变化和经济社会发展的需要确需修改的，应当按照原审批程序报送审批。

**Article 16** Once an earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plan is approved and published, it shall be strictly implemented; if it is indeed necessary to amend it due to changes in the earthquake situation and the needs of economic and social development, it shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the original approval procedure.

### 第三章　地震监测预报

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家加强地震监测预报工作，建立多学科地震监测系统，逐步提高地震监测预报水平。

**Article 17** The State strengthens earthquake monitoring and prediction work, establishes a multidisciplinary earthquake monitoring system, and gradually raises the level of earthquake monitoring and prediction.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家对地震监测台网实行统一规划，分级、分类管理。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，按照国务院有关规定，制定地震监测台网规划。  
全国地震监测台网由国家级地震监测台网、省级地震监测台网和市、县级地震监测台网组成，其建设资金和运行经费列入财政预算。

**Article 18** The State implements unified planning for earthquake monitoring networks, with management at different levels and by different categories.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, formulate plans for earthquake monitoring networks.  
The national earthquake monitoring network shall be composed of national-level earthquake monitoring networks, provincial-level earthquake monitoring networks, and city- and county-level earthquake monitoring networks, and the funds for their construction and operation shall be included in the fiscal budget.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　水库、油田、核电站等重大建设工程的建设单位，应当按照国务院有关规定，建设专用地震监测台网或者强震动监测设施，其建设资金和运行经费由建设单位承担。

**Article 19** The construction entities of major construction projects such as reservoirs, oil fields, and nuclear power plants shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, construct dedicated earthquake monitoring networks or strong-motion monitoring facilities, and the funds for their construction and operation shall be borne by the construction entities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地震监测台网的建设，应当遵守法律、法规和国家有关标准，保证建设质量。

**Article 20** The construction of earthquake monitoring networks shall comply with laws, regulations, and relevant national standards to ensure construction quality.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　地震监测台网不得擅自中止或者终止运行。  
检测、传递、分析、处理、存贮、报送地震监测信息的单位，应当保证地震监测信息的质量和安全。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织相关单位为地震监测台网的运行提供通信、交通、电力等保障条件。

**Article 21** No earthquake monitoring network may suspend or terminate operation without authorization.  
Units that detect, transmit, analyze, process, store, and submit earthquake monitoring information shall ensure the quality and security of such information.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant units to provide supporting conditions such as communication, transportation, and electric power for the operation of earthquake monitoring networks.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　沿海县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当加强海域地震活动监测预测工作。海域地震发生后，县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当及时向海洋主管部门和当地海事管理机构等通报情况。  
火山所在地的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当利用地震监测设施和技术手段，加强火山活动监测预测工作。

**Article 22** The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall strengthen the monitoring and prediction of seismic activities in sea areas. After a sea-area earthquake occurs, such departments or agencies shall promptly notify the marine administration department and the local maritime administrative agency, among others, of the situation.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level in areas where volcanoes are located shall, using earthquake monitoring facilities and technical means, strengthen the monitoring and prediction of volcanic activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家依法保护地震监测设施和地震观测环境。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁损、拆除或者擅自移动地震监测设施。地震监测设施遭到破坏的，县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构应当采取紧急措施组织修复，确保地震监测设施正常运行。  
任何单位和个人不得危害地震观测环境。国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构会同同级有关部门，按照国务院有关规定划定地震观测环境保护范围，并纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划。

**Article 23** The State protects earthquake monitoring facilities and the earthquake observation environment according to law.  
No unit or individual may occupy, damage, dismantle, or move earthquake monitoring facilities without authorization. Where earthquake monitoring facilities are damaged, the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's government at or above the county level shall take emergency measures to organize repair and ensure the normal operation of the earthquake monitoring facilities.  
No unit or individual may endanger the earthquake observation environment. The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, delimit the scope for the protection of the earthquake observation environment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, and incorporate it into the overall land use plan and urban and rural plans.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，应当避免对地震监测设施和地震观测环境造成危害。建设国家重点工程，确实无法避免对地震监测设施和地震观测环境造成危害的，建设单位应当按照县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的要求，增建抗干扰设施；不能增建抗干扰设施的，应当新建地震监测设施。  
对地震观测环境保护范围内的建设工程项目，城乡规划主管部门在依法核发选址意见书时，应当征求负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的意见；不需要核发选址意见书的，城乡规划主管部门在依法核发建设用地规划许可证或者乡村建设规划许可证时，应当征求负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构的意见。

**Article 24** The construction, expansion, or renovation of construction projects shall avoid causing harm to earthquake monitoring facilities and the earthquake observation environment. Where a national key project is constructed, and it is truly impossible to avoid causing harm to earthquake monitoring facilities and the earthquake observation environment, the construction entity shall, at the request of the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's government at or above the county level, construct anti-interference facilities; if it is unable to construct such facilities, it shall construct new earthquake monitoring facilities.  
For a construction project within the scope of protection of the earthquake observation environment, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall, when lawfully issuing a site selection opinion, seek the opinions of the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration; where no site selection opinion is required to be issued, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall, when lawfully issuing a construction land planning permit or a rural construction planning permit, seek the opinions of the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院地震工作主管部门建立健全地震监测信息共享平台，为社会提供服务。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当将地震监测信息及时报送上一级人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构。  
专用地震监测台网和强震动监测设施的管理单位，应当将地震监测信息及时报送所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构。

**Article 25** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall establish and improve a platform for sharing earthquake monitoring information to provide services to society.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall submit earthquake monitoring information in a timely manner to the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the people's government at the next higher level.  
The management units of dedicated earthquake monitoring networks and strong-motion monitoring facilities shall submit earthquake monitoring information in a timely manner to the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，根据地震监测信息研究结果，对可能发生地震的地点、时间和震级作出预测。  
其他单位和个人通过研究提出的地震预测意见，应当向所在地或者所预测地的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构书面报告，或者直接向国务院地震工作主管部门书面报告。收到书面报告的部门或者机构应当进行登记并出具接收凭证。

**Article 26** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the research results of earthquake monitoring information, make predictions on the location, time, and magnitude of possible earthquakes.  
Earthquake prediction opinions proposed by other units or individuals through research shall be reported in writing to the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's government at or above the county level of the place or the predicted place, or directly to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council. The department or agency receiving the written report shall register it and issue a receipt.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　观测到可能与地震有关的异常现象的单位和个人，可以向所在地县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构报告，也可以直接向国务院地震工作主管部门报告。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构接到报告后，应当进行登记并及时组织调查核实。

**Article 27** Units and individuals that observe abnormal phenomena possibly related to earthquakes may report to the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's government at or above the county level of the location, or directly to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.  
After receiving such a report, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's government at or above the county level shall register it and promptly organize an investigation and verification.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院地震工作主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当组织召开震情会商会，必要时邀请有关部门、专家和其他有关人员参加，对地震预测意见和可能与地震有关的异常现象进行综合分析研究，形成震情会商意见，报本级人民政府；经震情会商形成地震预报意见的，在报本级人民政府前，应当进行评审，作出评审结果，并提出对策建议。

**Article 28** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize and convene earthquake situation consultation meetings, inviting relevant departments, experts, and other relevant personnel to participate when necessary, to conduct comprehensive analysis and research on earthquake prediction opinions and abnormal phenomena possibly related to earthquakes, form earthquake situation consultation opinions, and report them to the people's government at the same level; where earthquake forecast opinions are formed through such consultation, before reporting to the people's government at the same level, a review shall be conducted, review results shall be made, and countermeasures and suggestions shall be proposed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家对地震预报意见实行统一发布制度。  
全国范围内的地震长期和中期预报意见，由国务院发布。省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的地震预报意见，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照国务院规定的程序发布。  
除发表本人或者本单位对长期、中期地震活动趋势的研究成果及进行相关学术交流外，任何单位和个人不得向社会散布地震预测意见。任何单位和个人不得向社会散布地震预报意见及其评审结果。

**Article 29** The State implements a system of unified release of earthquake forecast opinions.  
Long-term and medium-term earthquake forecast opinions for the entire country shall be released by the State Council. Earthquake forecast opinions for the administrative regions of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be released by the people's governments of such provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State Council.  
Except for publishing their own research results on long-term and medium-term earthquake activity trends and engaging in relevant academic exchanges, no unit or individual may disseminate earthquake prediction opinions to the public. No unit or individual may disseminate earthquake forecast opinions or their review results to the public.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院地震工作主管部门根据地震活动趋势和震害预测结果，提出确定地震重点监视防御区的意见，报国务院批准。  
国务院地震工作主管部门应当加强地震重点监视防御区的震情跟踪，对地震活动趋势进行分析评估，提出年度防震减灾工作意见，报国务院批准后实施。  
地震重点监视防御区的县级以上地方人民政府应当根据年度防震减灾工作意见和当地的地震活动趋势，组织有关部门加强防震减灾工作。  
地震重点监视防御区的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当增加地震监测台网密度，组织做好震情跟踪、流动观测和可能与地震有关的异常现象观测以及群测群防工作，并及时将有关情况报上一级人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构。

**Article 30** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall, based on the trend of seismic activities and the results of earthquake disaster prediction, propose opinions on determining key earthquake monitoring and defense areas, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall strengthen earthquake situation tracking in key earthquake monitoring and defense areas, analyze and assess the trend of seismic activities, propose annual work opinions on earthquake prevention and disaster reduction, and submit them to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in key earthquake monitoring and defense areas shall, based on the annual work opinions on earthquake prevention and disaster reduction and the local trend of seismic activities, organize relevant departments to strengthen earthquake prevention and disaster reduction work.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level in key earthquake monitoring and defense areas shall increase the density of earthquake monitoring networks, organize and properly carry out earthquake situation tracking, mobile observation, observation of abnormal phenomena possibly related to earthquakes, and mass observation and prevention work, and promptly report the relevant circumstances to the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家支持全国地震烈度速报系统的建设。  
地震灾害发生后，国务院地震工作主管部门应当通过全国地震烈度速报系统快速判断致灾程度，为指挥抗震救灾工作提供依据。

**Article 31** The State supports the construction of a national rapid earthquake intensity reporting system.  
After an earthquake disaster occurs, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall, through the national rapid earthquake intensity reporting system, quickly assess the degree of disaster, so as to provide a basis for commanding earthquake relief work.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当对发生地震灾害的区域加强地震监测，在地震现场设立流动观测点，根据震情的发展变化，及时对地震活动趋势作出分析、判定，为余震防范工作提供依据。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构、地震监测台网的管理单位，应当及时收集、保存有关地震的资料和信息，并建立完整的档案。

**Article 32** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen earthquake monitoring in areas where earthquake disasters have occurred, set up mobile observation stations at the earthquake site, and, based on the development and changes of the earthquake situation, timely analyze and determine the trend of seismic activities, so as to provide a basis for aftershock prevention work.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council, the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level, and the management units of earthquake monitoring networks shall promptly collect and preserve relevant earthquake data and information, and establish complete archives.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测活动，必须经国务院地震工作主管部门会同有关部门批准，并采取与中华人民共和国有关部门或者单位合作的形式进行。

**Article 33** Foreign organizations or individuals conducting earthquake monitoring activities in the territory of the People's Republic of China or other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China must obtain approval from the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council together with the relevant departments, and shall conduct such activities in the form of cooperation with relevant departments or units of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　地震灾害预防

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院地震工作主管部门负责制定全国地震烈度区划图或者地震动参数区划图。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，负责审定建设工程的地震安全性评价报告，确定抗震设防要求。

**Article 34** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the national seismic intensity zoning map or the ground motion parameter zoning map.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for reviewing the seismic safety evaluation reports of construction projects and determining the requirements for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，应当达到抗震设防要求。  
重大建设工程和可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程，应当按照国务院有关规定进行地震安全性评价，并按照经审定的地震安全性评价报告所确定的抗震设防要求进行抗震设防。建设工程的地震安全性评价单位应当按照国家有关标准进行地震安全性评价，并对地震安全性评价报告的质量负责。  
前款规定以外的建设工程，应当按照地震烈度区划图或者地震动参数区划图所确定的抗震设防要求进行抗震设防；对学校、医院等人员密集场所的建设工程，应当按照高于当地房屋建筑的抗震设防要求进行设计和施工，采取有效措施，增强抗震设防能力。

**Article 35** Newly constructed, expanded, or renovated construction projects shall meet the requirements for seismic fortification.  
Major construction projects and construction projects that may cause serious secondary disasters shall undergo seismic safety evaluation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, and shall adopt seismic fortification in accordance with the requirements determined in the reviewed seismic safety evaluation report. The seismic safety evaluation unit of a construction project shall conduct the evaluation in accordance with relevant national standards and shall be responsible for the quality of the seismic safety evaluation report.  
Construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall adopt seismic fortification in accordance with the requirements determined in the seismic intensity zoning map or the ground motion parameter zoning map; for construction projects at places with dense populations such as schools and hospitals, the design and construction shall be carried out in accordance with seismic fortification requirements higher than those for local housing buildings, and effective measures shall be taken to enhance the seismic fortification capability.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有关建设工程的强制性标准，应当与抗震设防要求相衔接。

**Article 36** The mandatory standards for relevant construction projects shall be aligned with the requirements for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励城市人民政府组织制定地震小区划图。地震小区划图由国务院地震工作主管部门负责审定。

**Article 37** The State encourages the people's governments of cities to organize the formulation of seismic microzonation maps. The seismic microzonation maps shall be subject to review by the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　建设单位对建设工程的抗震设计、施工的全过程负责。  
设计单位应当按照抗震设防要求和工程建设强制性标准进行抗震设计，并对抗震设计的质量以及出具的施工图设计文件的准确性负责。  
施工单位应当按照施工图设计文件和工程建设强制性标准进行施工，并对施工质量负责。  
建设单位、施工单位应当选用符合施工图设计文件和国家有关标准规定的材料、构配件和设备。  
工程监理单位应当按照施工图设计文件和工程建设强制性标准实施监理，并对施工质量承担监理责任。

**Article 38** The construction entity shall be responsible for the entire process of seismic design and construction of the construction project.  
The design unit shall carry out seismic design in accordance with the seismic fortification requirements and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall be responsible for the quality of the seismic design and the accuracy of the construction drawing design documents issued.  
The construction unit shall carry out construction in accordance with the construction drawing design documents and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall be responsible for the quality of the construction.  
The construction entity and the construction unit shall select materials, components, and equipment that conform to the construction drawing design documents and the provisions of relevant national standards.  
The project supervision unit shall carry out supervision in accordance with the construction drawing design documents and mandatory standards for engineering construction, and shall assume supervision responsibility for the quality of the construction.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　已经建成的下列建设工程，未采取抗震设防措施或者抗震设防措施未达到抗震设防要求的，应当按照国家有关规定进行抗震性能鉴定，并采取必要的抗震加固措施：  
（一）重大建设工程；  
（二）可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程；  
（三）具有重大历史、科学、艺术价值或者重要纪念意义的建设工程；  
（四）学校、医院等人员密集场所的建设工程；  
（五）地震重点监视防御区内的建设工程。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/建设工程抗震管理条例.md#art-19" title="被《建设工程抗震管理条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** For the following completed construction projects, where seismic fortification measures have not been taken or have not met the seismic fortification requirements, a seismic performance appraisal shall be conducted in accordance with relevant national provisions, and necessary seismic strengthening measures shall be taken:  
(1) Major construction projects;  
(2) Construction projects that may cause serious secondary disasters;  
(3) Construction projects of significant historical, scientific, or artistic value or important commemorative significance;  
(4) Construction projects at places with dense populations such as schools and hospitals;  
(5) Construction projects within key earthquake monitoring and defense areas.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对农村村民住宅和乡村公共设施抗震设防的管理，组织开展农村实用抗震技术的研究和开发，推广达到抗震设防要求、经济适用、具有当地特色的建筑设计和施工技术，培训相关技术人员，建设示范工程，逐步提高农村村民住宅和乡村公共设施的抗震设防水平。  
国家对需要抗震设防的农村村民住宅和乡村公共设施给予必要支持。

**Article 40** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of seismic fortification for rural residential houses and rural public facilities, organize and carry out research and development of practical seismic technologies for rural areas, promote economical and applicable building designs and construction techniques that meet seismic fortification requirements and have local characteristics, train relevant technical personnel, construct demonstration projects, and gradually raise the level of seismic fortification for rural residential houses and rural public facilities.  
The State shall provide necessary support for rural residential houses and rural public facilities that require seismic fortification.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　城乡规划应当根据地震应急避难的需要，合理确定应急疏散通道和应急避难场所，统筹安排地震应急避难所必需的交通、供水、供电、排污等基础设施建设。

**Article 41** Urban and rural plans shall, based on the need for earthquake emergency shelter, reasonably determine emergency evacuation passages and emergency shelter sites, and make overall arrangements for the construction of infrastructure necessary for earthquake emergency shelter, such as transportation, water supply, electric power, and sewage discharge.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　地震重点监视防御区的县级以上地方人民政府应当根据实际需要，在本级财政预算和物资储备中安排抗震救灾资金、物资。

**Article 42** The local people's governments at or above the county level in key earthquake monitoring and defense areas shall, based on actual needs, allocate funds and materials for earthquake relief in their fiscal budgets and material reserves.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家鼓励、支持研究开发和推广使用符合抗震设防要求、经济实用的新技术、新工艺、新材料。

**Article 43** The State encourages and supports the research, development, and promotion of new technologies, new processes, and new materials that meet seismic fortification requirements and are economical and practical.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级人民政府及其有关部门和乡、镇人民政府、城市街道办事处等基层组织，应当组织开展地震应急知识的宣传普及活动和必要的地震应急救援演练，提高公民在地震灾害中自救互救的能力。  
机关、团体、企业、事业等单位，应当按照所在地人民政府的要求，结合各自实际情况，加强对本单位人员的地震应急知识宣传教育，开展地震应急救援演练。  
学校应当进行地震应急知识教育，组织开展必要的地震应急救援演练，培养学生的安全意识和自救互救能力。  
新闻媒体应当开展地震灾害预防和应急、自救互救知识的公益宣传。  
国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当指导、协助、督促有关单位做好防震减灾知识的宣传教育和地震应急救援演练等工作。

**Article 44** The people's governments at the county level and their relevant departments, as well as grassroots organizations such as township and town people's governments and urban sub-district offices, shall organize and carry out publicity and popularization activities on earthquake emergency knowledge and necessary earthquake emergency rescue drills, so as to enhance citizens' ability to self-rescue and mutual rescue in earthquake disasters.  
Organs, organizations, enterprises, and institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and in light of their own actual circumstances, strengthen the publicity and education on earthquake emergency knowledge for their personnel and carry out earthquake emergency rescue drills.  
Schools shall conduct education on earthquake emergency knowledge, organize necessary earthquake emergency rescue drills, and cultivate students' safety awareness and self-rescue and mutual rescue capabilities.  
News media shall carry out public service publicity on earthquake disaster prevention, emergency response, and self-rescue and mutual rescue knowledge.  
The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide, assist, and supervise relevant units in carrying out publicity and education on knowledge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction and earthquake emergency rescue drills, among other tasks.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家发展有财政支持的地震灾害保险事业，鼓励单位和个人参加地震灾害保险。

**Article 45** The State develops earthquake disaster insurance with financial support, and encourages units and individuals to participate in earthquake disaster insurance.

### 第五章　地震应急救援

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院地震工作主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定国家地震应急预案，报国务院批准。国务院有关部门根据国家地震应急预案，制定本部门的地震应急预案，报国务院地震工作主管部门备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门和乡、镇人民政府，应当根据有关法律、法规、规章、上级人民政府及其有关部门的地震应急预案和本行政区域的实际情况，制定本行政区域的地震应急预案和本部门的地震应急预案。省、自治区、直辖市和较大的市的地震应急预案，应当报国务院地震工作主管部门备案。  
交通、铁路、水利、电力、通信等基础设施和学校、医院等人员密集场所的经营管理单位，以及可能发生次生灾害的核电、矿山、危险物品等生产经营单位，应当制定地震应急预案，并报所在地的县级人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构备案。

**Article 46** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national earthquake emergency response plan, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval. The relevant departments of the State Council shall, based on the national earthquake emergency response plan, formulate their own departmental earthquake emergency response plans, which shall be filed with the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, as well as the people's governments of townships and towns, shall, based on relevant laws, regulations, rules, the earthquake emergency response plans of the people's government at the next higher level and its relevant departments, and the actual conditions of their own administrative regions, formulate earthquake emergency response plans for their respective administrative regions and departments. The earthquake emergency response plans of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and larger cities shall be filed with the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.  
The management and operation entities of infrastructure such as transportation, railways, water conservancy, electric power, and communications, as well as places with dense populations such as schools and hospitals, and the production and operation entities such as nuclear power plants, mines, and dangerous goods that may cause secondary disasters, shall formulate earthquake emergency response plans and file them with the department or agency responsible for earthquake administration under the people's government at the county level of the location.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　地震应急预案的内容应当包括：组织指挥体系及其职责，预防和预警机制，处置程序，应急响应和应急保障措施等。  
地震应急预案应当根据实际情况适时修订。

**Article 47** The contents of an earthquake emergency response plan shall include: the organizational command system and its responsibilities, prevention and early warning mechanisms, handling procedures, emergency response and emergency safeguard measures, and so on.  
Earthquake emergency response plans shall be revised in a timely manner based on actual circumstances.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　地震预报意见发布后，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据预报的震情可以宣布有关区域进入临震应急期；有关地方人民政府应当按照地震应急预案，组织有关部门做好应急防范和抗震救灾准备工作。

**Article 48** After an earthquake forecast opinion is released, the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the forecasted earthquake situation, declare the relevant areas to enter a period of imminent earthquake emergency; the relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the earthquake emergency response plan, organize relevant departments to make proper preparations for emergency prevention and earthquake relief.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　按照社会危害程度、影响范围等因素，地震灾害分为一般、较大、重大和特别重大四级。具体分级标准按照国务院规定执行。  
一般或者较大地震灾害发生后，地震发生地的市、县人民政府负责组织有关部门启动地震应急预案；重大地震灾害发生后，地震发生地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责组织有关部门启动地震应急预案；特别重大地震灾害发生后，国务院负责组织有关部门启动地震应急预案。

**Article 49** Based on factors such as the degree of social harm and the scope of impact, earthquake disasters are divided into four levels: ordinary, relatively large, major, and exceptionally major. The specific classification standards shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
After an ordinary or relatively large earthquake disaster occurs, the people's government of the city or county where the earthquake occurred shall organize relevant departments to activate the earthquake emergency response plan; after a major earthquake disaster occurs, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the earthquake occurred shall organize relevant departments to activate the earthquake emergency response plan; after an exceptionally major earthquake disaster occurs, the State Council shall organize relevant departments to activate the earthquake emergency response plan.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　地震灾害发生后，抗震救灾指挥机构应当立即组织有关部门和单位迅速查清受灾情况，提出地震应急救援力量的配置方案，并采取以下紧急措施：  
（一）迅速组织抢救被压埋人员，并组织有关单位和人员开展自救互救；  
（二）迅速组织实施紧急医疗救护，协调伤员转移和接收与救治；  
（三）迅速组织抢修毁损的交通、铁路、水利、电力、通信等基础设施；  
（四）启用应急避难场所或者设置临时避难场所，设置救济物资供应点，提供救济物品、简易住所和临时住所，及时转移和安置受灾群众，确保饮用水消毒和水质安全，积极开展卫生防疫，妥善安排受灾群众生活；  
（五）迅速控制危险源，封锁危险场所，做好次生灾害的排查与监测预警工作，防范地震可能引发的火灾、水灾、爆炸、山体滑坡和崩塌、泥石流、地面塌陷，或者剧毒、强腐蚀性、放射性物质大量泄漏等次生灾害以及传染病疫情的发生；  
（六）依法采取维持社会秩序、维护社会治安的必要措施。

**Article 50** After an earthquake disaster occurs, the command organization for earthquake relief shall immediately organize relevant departments and units to quickly ascertain the disaster situation, propose a plan for the deployment of earthquake emergency rescue forces, and take the following emergency measures:  
(1) Rapidly organize the rescue of buried persons, and organize relevant units and personnel to carry out self-rescue and mutual rescue;  
(2) Rapidly organize and implement emergency medical rescue, and coordinate the transfer, reception, and treatment of the wounded;  
(3) Rapidly organize the repair of damaged infrastructure such as transportation, railways, water conservancy, electric power, and communications;  
(4) Activate emergency shelter sites or set up temporary shelter sites, set up relief material supply points, provide relief items, simple housing, and temporary housing, promptly relocate and resettle affected people, ensure the disinfection and safety of drinking water, actively carry out sanitation and epidemic prevention, and properly arrange the lives of affected people;  
(5) Rapidly control danger sources, seal off dangerous areas, properly carry out the investigation, monitoring, and early warning of secondary disasters, and prevent secondary disasters such as fires, floods, explosions, landslides, collapses, mudslides, ground subsidence, or large-scale leakage of highly toxic, strongly corrosive, or radioactive substances that may be triggered by the earthquake, as well as the occurrence of infectious disease epidemics;  
(6) Take necessary measures to maintain social order and public security according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　特别重大地震灾害发生后，国务院抗震救灾指挥机构在地震灾区成立现场指挥机构，并根据需要设立相应的工作组，统一组织领导、指挥和协调抗震救灾工作。  
各级人民政府及有关部门和单位、中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织，应当按照统一部署，分工负责，密切配合，共同做好地震应急救援工作。

**Article 51** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily severe earthquake disaster, the command organization of the State Council for earthquake resistance and disaster relief shall establish an on-site command organization in the earthquake-stricken area and, as necessary, set up corresponding working groups to uniformly organize, lead, command and coordinate the earthquake resistance and disaster relief work.  
People's governments at all levels, the relevant departments and units, the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and the militia organizations shall, in accordance with the unified deployment, assume respective responsibilities, closely cooperate, and jointly carry out the earthquake emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府应当及时将地震震情和灾情等信息向上一级人民政府报告，必要时可以越级上报，不得迟报、谎报、瞒报。  
地震震情、灾情和抗震救灾等信息按照国务院有关规定实行归口管理，统一、准确、及时发布。

**Article 52** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken area shall promptly report the earthquake situation and disaster information to the people's government at the next higher level, and may by-pass the level when necessary, and shall not delay, falsify or conceal such reporting.  
The earthquake situation, disaster information and earthquake resistance and disaster relief information shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, be subject to centralized management and be released in a unified, accurate and timely manner.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家鼓励、扶持地震应急救援新技术和装备的研究开发，调运和储备必要的应急救援设施、装备，提高应急救援水平。

**Article 53** The State encourages and supports the research and development of new technologies and equipment for earthquake emergency rescue, and the transportation and storage of necessary emergency rescue facilities and equipment, so as to improve the level of emergency rescue.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院建立国家地震灾害紧急救援队伍。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和地震重点监视防御区的市、县人民政府可以根据实际需要，充分利用消防等现有队伍，按照一队多用、专职与兼职相结合的原则，建立地震灾害紧急救援队伍。  
地震灾害紧急救援队伍应当配备相应的装备、器材，开展培训和演练，提高地震灾害紧急救援能力。  
地震灾害紧急救援队伍在实施救援时，应当首先对倒塌建筑物、构筑物压埋人员进行紧急救援。

**Article 54** The State Council shall establish a national earthquake emergency rescue team.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities and counties in key earthquake monitoring and protection areas may, according to actual needs, make full use of existing teams such as firefighting teams and establish earthquake emergency rescue teams in accordance with the principle of one team with multiple uses and combining full-time with part-time personnel.  
Earthquake emergency rescue teams shall be equipped with corresponding equipment and instruments, and shall carry out training and drills to improve their earthquake emergency rescue capabilities.  
When carrying out rescue operations, earthquake emergency rescue teams shall first provide emergency rescue to persons buried under collapsed buildings and structures.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照职责分工，协调配合，采取有效措施，保障地震灾害紧急救援队伍和医疗救治队伍快速、高效地开展地震灾害紧急救援活动。

**Article 55** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, coordinate and cooperate, and adopt effective measures to ensure that earthquake emergency rescue teams and medical treatment teams can rapidly and efficiently carry out earthquake emergency rescue activities.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门可以建立地震灾害救援志愿者队伍，并组织开展地震应急救援知识培训和演练，使志愿者掌握必要的地震应急救援技能，增强地震灾害应急救援能力。

**Article 56** The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments may establish volunteer teams for earthquake disaster rescue, and organize and carry out training and drills on earthquake emergency rescue knowledge, so that the volunteers master necessary earthquake emergency rescue skills and enhance the earthquake disaster emergency rescue capabilities.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国务院地震工作主管部门会同有关部门和单位，组织协调外国救援队和医疗队在中华人民共和国开展地震灾害紧急救援活动。  
国务院抗震救灾指挥机构负责外国救援队和医疗队的统筹调度，并根据其专业特长，科学、合理地安排紧急救援任务。  
地震灾区的地方各级人民政府，应当对外国救援队和医疗队开展紧急救援活动予以支持和配合。

**Article 57** The earthquake work department under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments and units, organize and coordinate foreign rescue teams and medical teams to carry out earthquake emergency rescue activities in the People's Republic of China.  
The command organization of the State Council for earthquake resistance and disaster relief shall be responsible for the overall dispatch of foreign rescue teams and medical teams, and arrange emergency rescue tasks scientifically and rationally based on their professional expertise.  
The local people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken area shall provide support and cooperation to foreign rescue teams and medical teams in carrying out emergency rescue activities.

### 第六章　地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院或者地震灾区的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当及时组织对地震灾害损失进行调查评估，为地震应急救援、灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建提供依据。  
地震灾害损失调查评估的具体工作，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者地震灾区的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构和财政、建设、民政等有关部门按照国务院的规定承担。

**Article 58** The State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in the earthquake-stricken area shall promptly organize investigation and assessment of earthquake disaster losses, so as to provide basis for earthquake emergency rescue, post-disaster transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction.  
The specific work of investigating and assessing earthquake disaster losses shall be undertaken by the earthquake work department under the State Council, or by the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in the earthquake-stricken area, together with the relevant departments such as finance, construction and civil affairs, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　地震灾区受灾群众需要过渡性安置的，应当根据地震灾区的实际情况，在确保安全的前提下，采取灵活多样的方式进行安置。

**Article 59** Where the disaster-stricken people in the earthquake-stricken area need transitional resettlement, they shall be resettled in flexible and diverse ways according to the actual conditions of the earthquake-stricken area and under the premise of ensuring safety.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　过渡性安置点应当设置在交通条件便利、方便受灾群众恢复生产和生活的区域，并避开地震活动断层和可能发生严重次生灾害的区域。  
过渡性安置点的规模应当适度，并采取相应的防灾、防疫措施，配套建设必要的基础设施和公共服务设施，确保受灾群众的安全和基本生活需要。

**Article 60** Transitional resettlement sites shall be located in areas with convenient transportation conditions and convenient for the disaster-stricken people to resume production and life, and shall avoid active earthquake faults and areas where severe secondary disasters may occur.  
The scale of transitional resettlement sites shall be appropriate, and corresponding disaster prevention and epidemic prevention measures shall be adopted, with necessary infrastructure and public service facilities built in support, so as to ensure the safety and basic living needs of the disaster-stricken people.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　实施过渡性安置应当尽量保护农用地，并避免对自然保护区、饮用水水源保护区以及生态脆弱区域造成破坏。  
过渡性安置用地按照临时用地安排，可以先行使用，事后依法办理有关用地手续；到期未转为永久性用地的，应当复垦后交还原土地使用者。

**Article 61** In implementing transitional resettlement, agricultural land shall be protected as much as possible, and damage to nature reserves, drinking water source protection areas and ecologically fragile areas shall be avoided.  
Land for transitional resettlement shall be arranged as temporary land, may be used first, and the relevant land use procedures shall be handled according to law afterwards; if it is not converted into permanent land upon expiration, the land shall be reclaimed and returned to the original land user.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　过渡性安置点所在地的县级人民政府，应当组织有关部门加强对次生灾害、饮用水水质、食品卫生、疫情等的监测，开展流行病学调查，整治环境卫生，避免对土壤、水环境等造成污染。  
过渡性安置点所在地的公安机关，应当加强治安管理，依法打击各种违法犯罪行为，维护正常的社会秩序。

**Article 62** The people's government at the county level where the transitional resettlement site is located shall organize the relevant departments to strengthen monitoring of secondary disasters, drinking water quality, food hygiene, epidemics, etc., carry out epidemiological investigations, improve environmental sanitation, and avoid pollution to soil and water environment.  
The public security organ where the transitional resettlement site is located shall strengthen public security management, crack down on various illegal and criminal acts according to law, and maintain normal social order.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门和乡、镇人民政府，应当及时组织修复毁损的农业生产设施，提供农业生产技术指导，尽快恢复农业生产；优先恢复供电、供水、供气等企业的生产，并对大型骨干企业恢复生产提供支持，为全面恢复农业、工业、服务业生产经营提供条件。

**Article 63** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken area, their relevant departments, and the people's governments of townships and towns shall promptly organize the repair of damaged agricultural production facilities, provide technical guidance for agricultural production, and restore agricultural production as soon as possible; give priority to restoring production of enterprises such as power supply, water supply and gas supply, and provide support for the restoration of production of large backbone enterprises, so as to create conditions for the comprehensive restoration of agricultural, industrial and service industry production and operation.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　各级人民政府应当加强对地震灾后恢复重建工作的领导、组织和协调。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在本级人民政府领导下，按照职责分工，密切配合，采取有效措施，共同做好地震灾后恢复重建工作。

**Article 64** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the leadership, organization and coordination of post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction work.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the leadership of the people's government at the same level, closely cooperate in accordance with their respective functions and duties, adopt effective measures, and jointly carry out the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction work.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国务院有关部门应当组织有关专家开展地震活动对相关建设工程破坏机理的调查评估，为修订完善有关建设工程的强制性标准、采取抗震设防措施提供科学依据。

**Article 65** The relevant departments of the State Council shall organize relevant experts to carry out investigation and assessment of the damage mechanism of earthquake activities on relevant construction projects, so as to provide scientific basis for revising and improving the mandatory standards for relevant construction projects and taking seismic fortification measures.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　特别重大地震灾害发生后，国务院经济综合宏观调控部门会同国务院有关部门与地震灾区的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府共同组织编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，报国务院批准后组织实施；重大、较大、一般地震灾害发生后，由地震灾区的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际需要组织编制地震灾后恢复重建规划。  
地震灾害损失调查评估获得的地质、勘察、测绘、土地、气象、水文、环境等基础资料和经国务院地震工作主管部门复核的地震动参数区划图，应当作为编制地震灾后恢复重建规划的依据。  
编制地震灾后恢复重建规划，应当征求有关部门、单位、专家和公众特别是地震灾区受灾群众的意见；重大事项应当组织有关专家进行专题论证。

**Article 66** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily severe earthquake disaster, the comprehensive economic macro-control department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in the earthquake-stricken area, jointly organize the formulation of a post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan, and organize its implementation after approval by the State Council; after the occurrence of a major, relatively large or general earthquake disaster, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government in the earthquake-stricken area shall, according to actual needs, organize the formulation of a post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan.  
The basic data such as geology, survey, mapping, land, meteorology, hydrology and environment obtained from the investigation and assessment of earthquake disaster losses, and the ground motion parameter zoning map verified by the earthquake work department under the State Council, shall serve as the basis for formulating the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan.  
In formulating a post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan, opinions of the relevant departments, units, experts and the public, especially the disaster-stricken people in the earthquake-stricken area, shall be solicited; for major matters, relevant experts shall be organized to conduct special demonstration.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　地震灾后恢复重建规划应当根据地质条件和地震活动断层分布以及资源环境承载能力，重点对城镇和乡村的布局、基础设施和公共服务设施的建设、防灾减灾和生态环境以及自然资源和历史文化遗产保护等作出安排。  
地震灾区内需要异地新建的城镇和乡村的选址以及地震灾后重建工程的选址，应当符合地震灾后恢复重建规划和抗震设防、防灾减灾要求，避开地震活动断层或者生态脆弱和可能发生洪水、山体滑坡和崩塌、泥石流、地面塌陷等灾害的区域以及传染病自然疫源地。

**Article 67** The post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan shall, based on geological conditions, distribution of active earthquake faults and the carrying capacity of resources and environment, make arrangements focusing on the layout of cities and villages, the construction of infrastructure and public service facilities, disaster prevention and mitigation, ecological environment, and the protection of natural resources, historical and cultural heritage.  
The site selection for cities and villages that need to be rebuilt in a new location within the earthquake-stricken area, and the site selection for post-earthquake reconstruction projects, shall conform to the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan and the requirements for seismic fortification and disaster prevention and mitigation, and shall avoid active earthquake faults or areas that are ecologically fragile or prone to disasters such as floods, landslides, collapses, debris flows, ground subsidence, and natural foci of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　地震灾区的地方各级人民政府应当根据地震灾后恢复重建规划和当地经济社会发展水平，有计划、分步骤地组织实施地震灾后恢复重建。

**Article 68** The local people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken area shall, according to the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction plan and the local economic and social development level, organize and implement the post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction in a planned and step-by-step manner.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门和专家，根据地震灾害损失调查评估结果，制定清理保护方案，明确典型地震遗址、遗迹和文物保护单位以及具有历史价值与民族特色的建筑物、构筑物的保护范围和措施。  
对地震灾害现场的清理，按照清理保护方案分区、分类进行，并依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，妥善清理、转运和处置有关放射性物质、危险废物和有毒化学品，开展防疫工作，防止传染病和重大动物疫情的发生。

**Article 69** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken area shall organize the relevant departments and experts to formulate a cleaning and protection plan based on the results of investigation and assessment of earthquake disaster losses, and specify the protection scope and measures for typical earthquake sites and relics, cultural relics protection units, and buildings and structures with historical value and ethnic characteristics.  
The cleaning of the earthquake disaster site shall be carried out in different zones and categories in accordance with the cleaning and protection plan, and in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and relevant provisions of the State, the relevant radioactive substances, hazardous wastes and toxic chemicals shall be properly cleaned, transported and disposed of, and epidemic prevention work shall be carried out to prevent the occurrence of infectious diseases and major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　地震灾后恢复重建，应当统筹安排交通、铁路、水利、电力、通信、供水、供电等基础设施和市政公用设施，学校、医院、文化、商贸服务、防灾减灾、环境保护等公共服务设施，以及住房和无障碍设施的建设，合理确定建设规模和时序。  
乡村的地震灾后恢复重建，应当尊重村民意愿，发挥村民自治组织的作用，以群众自建为主，政府补助、社会帮扶、对口支援，因地制宜，节约和集约利用土地，保护耕地。  
少数民族聚居的地方的地震灾后恢复重建，应当尊重当地群众的意愿。

**Article 70** In post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, the construction of infrastructure and municipal public facilities such as transportation, railways, water conservancy, electric power, communications, water supply and power supply, public service facilities such as schools, hospitals, culture, commercial services, disaster prevention and mitigation, and environmental protection, as well as housing and barrier-free facilities, shall be coordinated and arranged, and the scale and sequence of construction shall be reasonably determined.  
The post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction of villages shall respect the wishes of the villagers, give play to the role of villagers' self-governing organizations, give priority to self-construction by the masses, with government subsidies, social assistance and counterpart support, adapt to local conditions, save and intensively use land, and protect arable land.  
The post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction in areas where ethnic minorities live in concentrated communities shall respect the wishes of the local people.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门和单位，抢救、保护与收集整理有关档案、资料，对因地震灾害遗失、毁损的档案、资料，及时补充和恢复。

**Article 71** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken area shall organize the relevant departments and units to rescue, protect, collect and sort out relevant archives and materials, and promptly replenish and restore archives and materials lost or damaged due to the earthquake disaster.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　地震灾后恢复重建应当坚持政府主导、社会参与和市场运作相结合的原则。  
地震灾区的地方各级人民政府应当组织受灾群众和企业开展生产自救，自力更生、艰苦奋斗、勤俭节约，尽快恢复生产。  
国家对地震灾后恢复重建给予财政支持、税收优惠和金融扶持，并提供物资、技术和人力等支持。

**Article 72** Post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction shall adhere to the principle of combining government leadership, social participation and market operation.  
The local people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken area shall organize the disaster-stricken people and enterprises to carry out production self-help, rely on their own efforts, work hard, be diligent and thrifty, and restore production as soon as possible.  
The State provides financial support, tax preferences and financial assistance to post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, and provides support in materials, technology and human resources.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　地震灾区的地方各级人民政府应当组织做好救助、救治、康复、补偿、抚慰、抚恤、安置、心理援助、法律服务、公共文化服务等工作。  
各级人民政府及有关部门应当做好受灾群众的就业工作，鼓励企业、事业单位优先吸纳符合条件的受灾群众就业。

**Article 73** The local people's governments at all levels in the earthquake-stricken area shall organize and carry out relief, medical treatment, rehabilitation, compensation, consolation, pension, resettlement, psychological assistance, legal services, public cultural services and other work.  
People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall do a good job in the employment of the disaster-stricken people, and encourage enterprises and public institutions to give priority to employing eligible disaster-stricken people.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对地震灾后恢复重建中需要办理行政审批手续的事项，有审批权的人民政府及有关部门应当按照方便群众、简化手续、提高效率的原则，依法及时予以办理。

**Article 74** For matters requiring administrative examination and approval procedures in post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, the people's governments and relevant departments with examination and approval authority shall handle them promptly according to law in accordance with the principles of convenience for the people, simplification of procedures and improvement of efficiency.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　县级以上人民政府依法加强对防震减灾规划和地震应急预案的编制与实施、地震应急避难场所的设置与管理、地震灾害紧急救援队伍的培训、防震减灾知识宣传教育和地震应急救援演练等工作的监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当加强对地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建的物资的质量安全的监督检查。

**Article 75** People's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen supervision and inspection of the formulation and implementation of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction plans and earthquake emergency plans, the establishment and management of earthquake emergency shelters, the training of earthquake emergency rescue teams, the publicity and education of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction knowledge, and earthquake emergency rescue drills.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the quality and safety of materials for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上人民政府建设、交通、铁路、水利、电力、地震等有关部门应当按照职责分工，加强对工程建设强制性标准、抗震设防要求执行情况和地震安全性评价工作的监督检查。

**Article 76** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, such as construction, transportation, railway, water conservancy, electric power and earthquake, shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen supervision and inspection of the implementation of mandatory standards for construction projects, the requirements for seismic fortification, and the work of seismic safety evaluation.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　禁止侵占、截留、挪用地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建的资金、物资。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门对地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建的资金、物资以及社会捐赠款物的使用情况，依法加强管理和监督，予以公布，并对资金、物资的筹集、分配、拨付、使用情况登记造册，建立健全档案。

**Article 77** It is prohibited to embezzle, intercept or misappropriate the funds and materials for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen the management and supervision of the use of funds, materials and social donations for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction, make them public, and register the collection, allocation, appropriation and use of funds and materials, and establish sound archives.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　地震灾区的地方人民政府应当定期公布地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建的资金、物资以及社会捐赠款物的来源、数量、发放和使用情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 78** The local people's governments in the earthquake-stricken area shall regularly publish the sources, quantities, distribution and use of funds, materials and social donations for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　审计机关应当加强对地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置和恢复重建的资金、物资的筹集、分配、拨付、使用的审计，并及时公布审计结果。

**Article 79** Audit institutions shall strengthen the audit of the collection, allocation, appropriation and use of funds and materials for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement and recovery and reconstruction, and promptly publish the audit results.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　监察机关应当加强对参与防震减灾工作的国家行政机关和法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织及其工作人员的监察。

**Article 80** Supervisory organs shall strengthen supervision over the state administrative organs, organizations authorized by laws and regulations to manage public affairs, and their staff members that participate in earthquake prevention and disaster reduction work.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　任何单位和个人对防震减灾活动中的违法行为，有权进行举报。  
接到举报的人民政府或者有关部门应当进行调查，依法处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 81** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any illegal act in earthquake prevention and disaster reduction activities.  
The people's government or relevant department that receives the report shall investigate it, handle it according to law, and keep the reporter confidential.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国务院地震工作主管部门、县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，以及其他依照本法规定行使监督管理权的部门，不依法作出行政许可或者办理批准文件的，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报后不予查处的，或者有其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 82** Where the earthquake work department under the State Council, the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the local people's governments at or above the county level, and other departments that exercise supervisory and administrative powers in accordance with the provisions of this Law fail to make administrative licenses or handle approval documents according to law, fail to investigate after discovering illegal acts or receiving reports of illegal acts, or have other acts of failing to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　未按照法律、法规和国家有关标准进行地震监测台网建设的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令改正，采取相应的补救措施；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 83** Where a seismic monitoring network is constructed not in accordance with laws, regulations and relevant national standards, the earthquake work department under the State Council or the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall order correction and take corresponding remedial measures; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令停止违法行为，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）侵占、毁损、拆除或者擅自移动地震监测设施的；  
（二）危害地震观测环境的；  
（三）破坏典型地震遗址、遗迹的。  
单位有前款所列违法行为，情节严重的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；个人有前款所列违法行为，情节严重的，处二千元以下的罚款。构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 84** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the earthquake work department under the State Council or the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition or adoption of other remedial measures; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) encroaching on, damaging, dismantling or unauthorized moving of earthquake monitoring facilities;  
(2) endangering the earthquake observation environment;  
(3) destroying typical earthquake sites and relics.  
Where a unit commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; where an individual commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph, if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，未按照要求增建抗干扰设施或者新建地震监测设施的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/地震监测管理条例.md#art-37" title="被《地震监测管理条例》第37条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 85** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to add anti-interference facilities or build new earthquake monitoring facilities as required, the earthquake work department under the State Council or the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，外国的组织或者个人未经批准，在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事地震监测活动的，由国务院地震工作主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收监测成果和监测设施，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
外国人有前款规定行为的，除依照前款规定处罚外，还应当依照外国人入境出境管理法律的规定缩短其在中华人民共和国停留的期限或者取消其在中华人民共和国居留的资格；情节严重的，限期出境或者驱逐出境。

**Article 86** Where a foreign organization or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law and without approval, engages in earthquake monitoring activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China or in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the earthquake work department under the State Council shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the monitoring results and monitoring facilities, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently.  
Where a foreigner commits an act as provided in the preceding paragraph, in addition to being punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the period of stay in the People's Republic of China shall be shortened or the qualification for residence in the People's Republic of China shall be cancelled in accordance with the provisions of the laws on the administration of entry and exit of foreigners; if the circumstances are serious, the foreigner shall be ordered to leave the country within a time limit or be deported.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　未依法进行地震安全性评价，或者未按照地震安全性评价报告所确定的抗震设防要求进行抗震设防的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 87** Where a seismic safety evaluation is not carried out according to law, or seismic fortification is not carried out in accordance with the seismic fortification requirements determined in the seismic safety evaluation report, the earthquake work department under the State Council or the department or institution responsible for earthquake management under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，向社会散布地震预测意见、地震预报意见及其评审结果，或者在地震灾后过渡性安置、地震灾后恢复重建中扰乱社会秩序，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 88** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, disseminates earthquake prediction opinions, earthquake forecast opinions and their review results to the public, or disrupts social order in post-earthquake transitional resettlement or post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, and such act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　地震灾区的县级以上地方人民政府迟报、谎报、瞒报地震震情、灾情等信息的，由上级人民政府责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 89** Where a local people's government at or above the county level in the earthquake-stricken area delays, falsifies or conceals the reporting of information such as earthquake situation and disaster, the people's government at the higher level shall order correction; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　侵占、截留、挪用地震应急救援、地震灾后过渡性安置或者地震灾后恢复重建的资金、物资的，由财政部门、审计机关在各自职责范围内，责令改正，追回被侵占、截留、挪用的资金、物资；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对单位给予警告或者通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 90** Where anyone embezzles, intercepts or misappropriates funds or materials for earthquake emergency rescue, post-earthquake transitional resettlement or post-earthquake recovery and reconstruction, the finance department or the audit institution shall, within their respective functions and duties, order correction, recover the embezzled, intercepted or misappropriated funds or materials; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the unit shall be given a warning or a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 91** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）地震监测设施，是指用于地震信息检测、传输和处理的设备、仪器和装置以及配套的监测场地。  
（二）地震观测环境，是指按照国家有关标准划定的保障地震监测设施不受干扰、能够正常发挥工作效能的空间范围。  
（三）重大建设工程，是指对社会有重大价值或者有重大影响的工程。  
（四）可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程，是指受地震破坏后可能引发水灾、火灾、爆炸，或者剧毒、强腐蚀性、放射性物质大量泄漏，以及其他严重次生灾害的建设工程，包括水库大坝和贮油、贮气设施，贮存易燃易爆或者剧毒、强腐蚀性、放射性物质的设施，以及其他可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程。  
（五）地震烈度区划图，是指以地震烈度（以等级表示的地震影响强弱程度）为指标，将全国划分为不同抗震设防要求区域的图件。  
（六）地震动参数区划图，是指以地震动参数（以加速度表示地震作用强弱程度）为指标，将全国划分为不同抗震设防要求区域的图件。  
（七）地震小区划图，是指根据某一区域的具体场地条件，对该区域的抗震设防要求进行详细划分的图件。

**Article 92** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) 'Earthquake monitoring facilities' means the equipment, instruments and devices used for detecting, transmitting and processing earthquake information, as well as the supporting monitoring sites.  
(2) 'Earthquake observation environment' means the spatial range delimited in accordance with the relevant national standards to ensure that earthquake monitoring facilities are free from interference and can function properly.  
(3) 'Major construction project' means a project that has significant value or significant impact on society.  
(4) 'Construction project that may cause severe secondary disasters' means a construction project that, after being damaged by an earthquake, may cause floods, fires, explosions, or large-scale leakage of highly toxic, strongly corrosive or radioactive substances, and other severe secondary disasters, including reservoir dams, oil and gas storage facilities, facilities for storing flammable, explosive, highly toxic, strongly corrosive or radioactive substances, and other construction projects that may cause severe secondary disasters.  
(5) 'Seismic intensity zoning map' means a map that divides the whole country into areas with different seismic fortification requirements based on seismic intensity (the degree of seismic impact expressed in grades) as an indicator.  
(6) 'Ground motion parameter zoning map' means a map that divides the whole country into areas with different seismic fortification requirements based on ground motion parameters (the degree of seismic effect expressed by acceleration) as an indicator.  
(7) 'Seismic microzonation map' means a map that, based on the specific site conditions of a certain area, divides the seismic fortification requirements of that area in detail.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　本法自2009年5月1日起施行。

**Article 93** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2009.
