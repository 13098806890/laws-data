# 中华人民共和国国防交通法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2016-09-03  
**生效日期**：2017-01-01

> （2016年9月3日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　国防交通规划 第三章　交通工程设施 第四章　民用运载工具 第五章　国防运输 第六章　国防交通保障 第七章　国防交通物资储备 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强国防交通建设，促进交通领域军民融合发展，保障国防活动顺利进行，制定本法。 第二条　以满足国防需要为目的，在铁路、道路、水路、航空、管道以及邮政等交通领域进行的规划、建设、管理和资源使用活动，适用本法。 第三条　国家坚持军民融合发展战略，推动军地资源优化配置、合理共享，提高国防交通平时服务、急时应急、战时应战的能力，促进经济建设和国防建设协调发展。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国防交通建设，促进交通领域军民融合发展，保障国防活动顺利进行，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening national defense transportation construction, promoting military-civilian integrated development in the transportation field, and ensuring the smooth conduct of national defense activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　以满足国防需要为目的，在铁路、道路、水路、航空、管道以及邮政等交通领域进行的规划、建设、管理和资源使用活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities of planning, construction, management and resource utilization in the transportation fields such as railways, roads, waterways, aviation, pipelines and postal services for the purpose of meeting national defense needs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家坚持军民融合发展战略，推动军地资源优化配置、合理共享，提高国防交通平时服务、急时应急、战时应战的能力，促进经济建设和国防建设协调发展。  
国防交通工作遵循统一领导、分级负责、统筹规划、平战结合的原则。

**Article 3** The State shall adhere to the military-civilian integrated development strategy, promote the optimal allocation and reasonable sharing of military and local resources, enhance the capabilities of national defense transportation for peacetime services, emergency response and wartime operations, and facilitate the coordinated development of economic construction and national defense construction.  
National defense transportation work shall follow the principles of unified leadership, graded responsibility, overall planning, and integration of peacetime and wartime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家国防交通主管机构负责规划、组织、指导和协调全国的国防交通工作。国家国防交通主管机构的设置和工作职责，由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。  
县级以上地方人民政府国防交通主管机构负责本行政区域的国防交通工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门和有关军事机关按照职责分工，负责有关的国防交通工作。  
省级以上人民政府有关部门和军队有关部门建立国防交通军民融合发展会商机制，相互通报交通建设和国防需求等情况，研究解决国防交通重大问题。

**Article 4** The national defense transportation competent authority of the State shall be responsible for planning, organizing, directing and coordinating national defense transportation work nationwide. The establishment and functions of the national defense transportation competent authority of the State shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The defense transportation competent authorities of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for national defense transportation work within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant military organs shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for related national defense transportation work.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level and the relevant military departments shall establish a consultation mechanism for military-civilian integrated development of national defense transportation, notify each other of the status of transportation construction and national defense requirements, and study and resolve major issues concerning national defense transportation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民和组织应当依法履行国防交通义务。  
国家鼓励公民和组织依法参与国防交通建设，并按照有关规定给予政策和经费支持。

**Article 5** Citizens and organizations shall perform national defense transportation obligations according to law.  
The State shall encourage citizens and organizations to participate in national defense transportation construction according to law, and provide policy and financial support in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国防交通经费按照事权划分的原则，列入政府预算。  
企业事业单位用于开展国防交通日常工作的合理支出，列入本单位预算，计入成本。

**Article 6** National defense transportation funds shall be included in government budgets in accordance with the principle of division of powers and responsibilities.  
Reasonable expenditures of enterprises and public institutions for carrying out daily national defense transportation work shall be included in their budgets and recorded as costs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府根据国防需要，可以依法征用民用运载工具、交通设施、交通物资等民用交通资源，有关组织和个人应当予以配合，履行相关义务。  
民用交通资源征用的组织实施和补偿，依照有关法律、行政法规执行。

**Article 7** The people's governments at or above the county level may, according to national defense needs, lawfully requisition civil transportation resources such as civil means of transport, transportation facilities and transportation materials, and the relevant organizations and individuals shall cooperate and perform relevant obligations.  
The organization, implementation and compensation for the requisition of civil transportation resources shall be carried out in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府应当将国防交通教育纳入全民国防教育，通过多种形式开展国防交通宣传活动，普及国防交通知识，增强公民的国防交通观念。  
各级铁路、道路、水路、航空、管道、邮政等行政管理部门（以下统称交通主管部门）和相关企业事业单位应当对本系统、本单位的人员进行国防交通教育。  
设有交通相关专业的院校应当将国防交通知识纳入相关专业课程或者单独开设国防交通相关课程。

**Article 8** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate national defense transportation education into national defense education for all citizens, carry out national defense transportation publicity activities in various forms, disseminate national defense transportation knowledge, and enhance citizens' awareness of national defense transportation.  
The administrative departments for railways, roads, waterways, aviation, pipelines, postal services, etc. at all levels (hereinafter collectively referred to as competent transportation departments) and relevant enterprises and public institutions shall conduct national defense transportation education for personnel of their own systems and units.  
Institutions of higher education with transportation-related specialties shall incorporate national defense transportation knowledge into relevant specialized courses or offer separate courses related to national defense transportation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何组织和个人对在国防交通工作中知悉的国家秘密和商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 9** Any organization or individual shall have the obligation to keep confidential the State secrets and trade secrets learned of in national defense transportation work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在国防交通工作中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in national defense transportation work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强国防交通信息化建设，为提高国防交通保障能力提供支持。

**Article 11** The State shall strengthen the informatization construction of national defense transportation to provide support for improving national defense transportation support capabilities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　战时和平时特殊情况下，需要在交通领域采取行业管制、为武装力量优先提供交通保障等国防动员措施的，依照《中华人民共和国国防法》、《中华人民共和国国防动员法》等有关法律执行。  
武装力量组织进行军事演习、训练，需要对交通采取临时性管制措施的，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 12** Where during wartime or under special circumstances in peacetime, it is necessary to adopt national defense mobilization measures in the transportation field such as industry control or providing priority transportation support for the armed forces, such measures shall be implemented in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China, the National Defense Mobilization Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws.  
Where the armed forces organize military exercises or training and need to adopt temporary traffic control measures, such measures shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　战时和平时特殊情况下，国家根据需要，设立国防交通联合指挥机构，统筹全国或者局部地区的交通运输资源，统一组织指挥全国或者局部地区的交通运输以及交通设施设备的抢修、抢建与防护。相关组织和个人应当服从统一指挥。

**Article 13** During wartime or under special circumstances in peacetime, the State may, as needed, establish a joint command organization for national defense transportation to coordinate transportation resources nationwide or in a specific region, and to uniformly organize and command the transportation and the emergency repair, emergency construction and protection of transportation facilities and equipment nationwide or in a specific region. Relevant organizations and individuals shall obey the unified command.

### 第二章　国防交通规划

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国防交通规划包括国防交通工程设施建设规划、国防交通专业保障队伍建设规划、国防交通物资储备规划、国防交通科研规划等。  
编制国防交通规划应当符合下列要求：  
（一）满足国防需要，有利于平战快速转换，保障国防活动顺利进行；  
（二）兼顾经济社会发展需要，突出重点，注重效益，促进资源融合共享；  
（三）符合城乡规划和土地利用总体规划，与国家综合交通运输体系发展规划相协调；  
（四）有利于加强边防、海防交通基础设施建设，扶持沿边、沿海经济欠发达地区交通运输发展；  
（五）保护环境，节约土地、能源等资源。

**Article 14** National defense transportation plans include plans for the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities, plans for the building of specialized national defense transportation support teams, plans for the stockpiling of national defense transportation materials, and plans for national defense transportation scientific research.  
The formulation of national defense transportation plans shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) satisfy national defense needs, facilitate rapid transition between peacetime and wartime, and ensure the smooth conduct of national defense activities;  
(2) take into account the needs of economic and social development, highlight key points, emphasize efficiency, and promote resource integration and sharing;  
(3) conform to urban and rural planning and overall land use planning, and be coordinated with the development plan of the national comprehensive transportation system;  
(4) facilitate the strengthening of border defense and coastal defense transportation infrastructure construction, and support the development of transportation in economically underdeveloped border and coastal areas;  
(5) protect the environment and conserve land, energy and other resources.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府应当将国防交通建设纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国务院及其有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定交通行业以及相关领域的发展战略、产业政策和规划交通网络布局，应当兼顾国防需要，提高国家综合交通运输体系保障国防活动的能力。  
国务院有关部门应当将有关国防要求纳入交通设施、设备的技术标准和规范。有关国防要求由国家国防交通主管机构征求军队有关部门意见后汇总提出。

**Article 15** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate national defense transportation construction into national economic and social development plans.  
The State Council and its relevant departments, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, when formulating development strategies, industrial policies and planning transportation network layouts for the transportation industry and related fields, shall take into account national defense needs and improve the capability of the national comprehensive transportation system to support national defense activities.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall incorporate relevant national defense requirements into the technical standards and specifications for transportation facilities and equipment. The relevant national defense requirements shall be consolidated and proposed by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State after soliciting opinions from the relevant military departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国防交通工程设施建设规划，由县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府交通主管部门编制，经本级人民政府发展改革部门审核后，报本级人民政府批准。  
下级国防交通工程设施建设规划应当依据上一级国防交通工程设施建设规划编制。  
编制国防交通工程设施建设规划，应当征求有关军事机关和本级人民政府有关部门的意见。县级以上人民政府有关部门编制综合交通运输体系发展规划和交通工程设施建设规划，应当征求本级人民政府国防交通主管机构的意见，并纳入国防交通工程设施建设的相关内容。

**Article 16** Plans for the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities shall be formulated by the defense transportation competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level jointly with the competent transportation department of the same level, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval after review by the development and reform department of the same level.  
Plans for the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities at lower levels shall be formulated on the basis of the plans at the next higher level.  
When formulating plans for the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities, opinions shall be solicited from the relevant military organs and the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level. When the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level formulate development plans for the comprehensive transportation system and plans for the construction of transportation engineering facilities, they shall solicit opinions from the defense transportation competent authority of the people's government at the same level, and incorporate relevant contents of the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国防交通专业保障队伍建设规划，由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门编制。

**Article 17** Plans for the building of specialized national defense transportation support teams shall be formulated by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military departments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国防交通物资储备规划，由国防交通主管机构会同军地有关部门编制。  
中央储备的国防交通物资，由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院交通主管部门和军队有关部门编制储备规划。  
地方储备的国防交通物资，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府有关部门和有关军事机关编制储备规划。

**Article 18** Plans for the stockpiling of national defense transportation materials shall be formulated by the defense transportation competent authority jointly with the relevant military and local departments.  
For national defense transportation materials stockpiled at the central level, stockpiling plans shall be formulated by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State jointly with the competent transportation department of the State Council and the relevant military departments.  
For national defense transportation materials stockpiled at the local level, stockpiling plans shall be formulated by the defense transportation competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and the relevant military organs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国防交通科研规划，由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门编制。

**Article 19** Plans for national defense transportation scientific research shall be formulated by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military departments.

### 第三章　交通工程设施

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建设国防交通工程设施，应当以国防交通工程设施建设规划为依据，保障战时和平时特殊情况下国防交通畅通。  
建设其他交通工程设施，应当依法贯彻国防要求，在建设中采用增强其国防功能的工程技术措施，提高国防交通保障能力。

**Article 20** The construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities shall be based on the plans for the construction of national defense transportation engineering facilities, and shall ensure the smooth flow of national defense transportation during wartime and under special circumstances in peacetime.  
The construction of other transportation engineering facilities shall, according to law, implement national defense requirements, adopt engineering technical measures to enhance their national defense functions during construction, and improve national defense transportation support capabilities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国防交通工程设施应当按照基本建设程序、相关技术标准和规范以及国防要求进行设计、施工和竣工验收。相关人民政府国防交通主管机构组织军队有关部门参与项目的设计审定、竣工验收等工作。  
交通工程设施建设中为增加国防功能修建的项目应当与主体工程同步设计、同步建设、同步验收。

**Article 21** National defense transportation engineering facilities shall be designed, constructed and subject to completion acceptance in accordance with the capital construction procedures, relevant technical standards and specifications, and national defense requirements. The relevant defense transportation competent authority of the people's government shall organize the relevant military departments to participate in the design review, completion acceptance and other work of the project.  
Items constructed in the construction of transportation engineering facilities to increase national defense functions shall be designed, constructed and accepted simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国防交通工程设施在满足国防活动需要的前提下，应当为经济社会活动提供便利。

**Article 22** On the premise of meeting the needs of national defense activities, national defense transportation engineering facilities shall provide convenience for economic and social activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国防交通工程设施管理单位负责国防交通工程设施的维护和管理，保持其国防功能。  
国防交通工程设施需要改变用途或者作报废处理的，由国防交通工程设施管理单位逐级上报国家国防交通主管机构或者其授权的国防交通主管机构批准。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强对国防交通工程设施维护管理工作的监督检查。

**Article 23** The management units of national defense transportation engineering facilities shall be responsible for the maintenance and management of such facilities, and shall preserve their national defense functions.  
Where a national defense transportation engineering facility needs to be repurposed or scrapped, the management unit shall report it level by level to the national defense transportation competent authority of the State or its authorized defense transportation competent authority for approval.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the maintenance and management of national defense transportation engineering facilities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何组织和个人进行生产和其他活动，不得影响国防交通工程设施的正常使用，不得危及国防交通工程设施的安全。

**Article 24** No organization or individual, when engaging in production or other activities, may affect the normal use of national defense transportation engineering facilities or endanger the safety of such facilities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构负责向本级人民政府交通主管部门以及相关企业事业单位了解交通工程设施建设项目的立项、设计、施工等情况；有关人民政府交通主管部门以及相关企业事业单位应当予以配合。

**Article 25** The defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for learning from the competent transportation department of the same level and relevant enterprises and public institutions about the project approval, design, construction and other conditions of transportation engineering facility construction projects; the relevant competent transportation department of the people's government and relevant enterprises and public institutions shall cooperate.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构应当及时向有关军事机关通报交通工程设施建设情况，并征求其贯彻国防要求的意见，汇总后提出需要贯彻国防要求的具体项目。

**Article 26** The defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall promptly inform the relevant military organs of the construction status of transportation engineering facilities, solicit their opinions on implementing national defense requirements, and after consolidation, propose specific items that need to implement national defense requirements.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对需要贯彻国防要求的交通工程设施建设项目，由有关人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府发展改革部门、财政部门、交通主管部门和有关军事机关，与建设单位协商确定贯彻国防要求的具体事宜。  
交通工程设施新建、改建、扩建项目因贯彻国防要求增加的费用由国家承担。有关部门应当对项目的实施予以支持和保障。

**Article 27** For construction projects of transportation engineering facilities that need to implement national defense requirements, the relevant defense transportation competent authority of the people's government shall, jointly with the development and reform department, the finance department, the competent transportation department of the same level and the relevant military organs, negotiate with the construction entity to determine the specific matters for implementing national defense requirements.  
The increased costs of new construction, reconstruction or expansion projects of transportation engineering facilities due to implementing national defense requirements shall be borne by the State. The relevant departments shall provide support and guarantee for the implementation of the projects.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级人民政府对国防交通工程设施建设项目和贯彻国防要求的交通工程设施建设项目，在土地使用、城乡规划、财政、税费等方面，按照国家有关规定给予政策支持。

**Article 28** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide policy support for construction projects of national defense transportation engineering facilities and transportation engineering facility construction projects that implement national defense requirements in terms of land use, urban and rural planning, finance, taxes and fees, etc.

### 第四章　民用运载工具

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家国防交通主管机构应当根据国防需要，会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门，确定需要贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具的类别和范围，及时向社会公布。  
国家鼓励公民和组织建造、购置、经营前款规定的类别和范围内的民用运载工具及其相关设备。

**Article 29** The national defense transportation competent authority of the State shall, according to national defense needs, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military departments, determine the categories and scope of civil means of transport that need to implement national defense requirements, and promptly announce them to the public.  
The State shall encourage citizens and organizations to build, purchase and operate civil means of transport and related equipment within the categories and scope specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构应当向民用运载工具登记管理部门和建造、购置人了解需要贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具的建造、购置、使用等情况，有关公民和组织应当予以配合。

**Article 30** The defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall learn from the registration management department of civil means of transport and the builders or purchasers about the construction, purchase, use and other conditions of civil means of transport that need to implement national defense requirements, and the relevant citizens and organizations shall cooperate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构应当及时将掌握的民用运载工具基本情况通报有关军事机关，并征求其贯彻国防要求的意见，汇总后提出需要贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具的具体项目。

**Article 31** The defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall promptly inform the relevant military organs of the basic information of civil means of transport it has obtained, solicit their opinions on implementing national defense requirements, and after consolidation, propose specific items of civil means of transport that need to implement national defense requirements.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对需要贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具的具体项目，由县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府财政部门、交通主管部门和有关军事机关，与有关公民和组织协商确定贯彻国防要求的具体事宜，并签订相关协议。

**Article 32** For specific items of civil means of transport that need to implement national defense requirements, the defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the finance department and the competent transportation department of the same level and the relevant military organs, negotiate with the relevant citizens and organizations to determine the specific matters for implementing national defense requirements, and sign relevant agreements.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民用运载工具因贯彻国防要求增加的费用由国家承担。有关部门应当对民用运载工具贯彻国防要求的实施予以支持和保障。  
各级人民政府对贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具在服务采购、运营范围等方面，按照有关规定给予政策支持。

**Article 33** The increased costs of civil means of transport due to implementing national defense requirements shall be borne by the State. The relevant departments shall provide support and guarantee for the implementation of national defense requirements in civil means of transport.  
People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, provide policy support for civil means of transport that implement national defense requirements in terms of service procurement, scope of operation, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　贯彻国防要求的民用运载工具所有权人、承租人、经营人负责民用运载工具的维护和管理，保障其使用效能。

**Article 34** The owners, lessees and operators of civil means of transport that implement national defense requirements shall be responsible for the maintenance and management of such means of transport, and shall ensure their service efficiency.

### 第五章　国防运输

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府交通主管部门会同军队有关交通运输部门按照统一计划、集中指挥、迅速准确、安全保密的原则，组织国防运输。  
承担国防运输任务的公民和组织应当优先安排国防运输任务。

**Article 35** The competent transportation departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the relevant military transportation departments, organize national defense transportation in accordance with the principles of unified planning, centralized command, promptness and accuracy, and security and confidentiality.  
Citizens and organizations undertaking national defense transportation tasks shall prioritize national defense transportation tasks.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家以大中型运输企业为主要依托，组织建设战略投送支援力量，增强战略投送能力，为快速组织远距离、大规模国防运输提供有效支持。  
承担战略投送支援任务的企业负责编组人员和装备，根据有关规定制定实施预案，进行必要的训练、演练，提高执行战略投送任务的能力。

**Article 36** The State shall, with large and medium-sized transport enterprises as the main foundation, organize the building of strategic projection support forces, enhance strategic projection capabilities, and provide effective support for rapidly organizing long-distance, large-scale national defense transportation.  
Enterprises undertaking strategic projection support tasks shall be responsible for organizing personnel and equipment, formulating implementation plans in accordance with relevant provisions, conducting necessary training and exercises, and improving their capabilities to perform strategic projection tasks.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　各级人民政府和军事机关应当加强国防运输供应、装卸等保障设施建设。  
县级以上地方人民政府和相关企业事业单位，应当根据国防运输的需要提供饮食饮水供应、装卸作业、医疗救护、通行与休整、安全警卫等方面的必要的服务或者保障。

**Article 37** People's governments at all levels and military organs shall strengthen the construction of support facilities such as supply and loading/unloading for national defense transportation.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant enterprises and public institutions shall, according to the needs of national defense transportation, provide necessary services or support in terms of food and water supply, loading and unloading operations, medical rescue, passage and rest, security guard, etc.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家驻外机构和我国从事国际运输业务的企业及其境外机构，应当为我国实施国际救援、海上护航和维护国家海外利益的军事行动的船舶、飞机、车辆和人员的补给、休整提供协助。  
国家有关部门应当对前款规定的机构和企业为海外军事行动提供协助所需的人员和运输工具、货物等的出境入境提供相关便利。

**Article 38** The overseas institutions of the State and the enterprises of China engaged in international transport business and their overseas institutions shall provide assistance for the replenishment and rest of ships, aircraft, vehicles and personnel of the military operations carried out by China for international rescue, maritime escort and protection of national overseas interests.  
The relevant departments of the State shall provide relevant facilitation for the exit and entry of personnel, means of transport, goods, etc. needed by the institutions and enterprises specified in the preceding paragraph for providing assistance to overseas military operations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　公民和组织完成国防运输任务所发生的费用，由使用单位按照不低于市场价格的原则支付。具体办法由国务院财政部门、交通主管部门和中央军事委员会后勤保障部规定。

**Article 39** The expenses incurred by citizens and organizations in completing national defense transportation tasks shall be paid by the using units in accordance with the principle of not less than the market price. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the finance department and the competent transportation department of the State Council and the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军队根据需要，可以在相关交通企业或者交通企业较为集中的地区派驻军事代表，会同有关单位共同完成国防运输和交通保障任务。  
军事代表驻在单位和驻在地人民政府有关部门，应当为军事代表开展工作提供便利。  
军事代表的派驻和工作职责，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 40** The armed forces may, as needed, station military representatives in relevant transport enterprises or areas where transport enterprises are relatively concentrated to jointly complete national defense transportation and transportation support tasks with relevant units.  
The units where military representatives are stationed and the relevant departments of the local people's government shall provide convenience for the military representatives to carry out their work.  
The stationing and work duties of military representatives shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第六章　国防交通保障

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级国防交通主管机构组织人民政府有关部门和有关军事机关制定国防交通保障方案，明确重点交通目标、线路以及保障原则、任务、技术措施和组织措施。

**Article 41** National defense transportation competent authorities at all levels shall organize the relevant departments of the people's government and the relevant military organs to formulate national defense transportation support plans, specifying key transportation targets, routes, and support principles, tasks, technical measures and organizational measures.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府按照职责分工，组织有关企业事业单位实施交通工程设施抢修、抢建和运载工具抢修，保障国防活动顺利进行。有关军事机关应当给予支持和协助。

**Article 42** The relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their functions and duties, organize relevant enterprises and public institutions to carry out emergency repair and emergency construction of transportation engineering facilities and emergency repair of means of transport, so as to ensure the smooth conduct of national defense activities. The relevant military organs shall provide support and assistance.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国防交通保障方案确定的重点交通目标的管理单位和预定承担保障任务的单位，应当根据有关规定编制重点交通目标保障预案，并做好相关准备。

**Article 43** The management units of key transportation targets determined in the national defense transportation support plan and the units scheduled to undertake support tasks shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, formulate support plans for key transportation targets and make relevant preparations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　重点交通目标的管理单位和预定承担保障任务的单位，在重点交通目标受到破坏威胁时，应当立即启动保障预案，做好相应准备；在重点交通目标遭受破坏时，应当按照任务分工，迅速组织实施工程加固和抢修、抢建，尽快恢复交通。  
与国防运输有关的其他交通工程设施遭到破坏的，其管理单位应当及时按照管理关系向上级报告，同时组织修复。

**Article 44** When key transportation targets are under threat of destruction, the management units and the units scheduled to undertake support tasks shall immediately activate the support plans and make corresponding preparations; when key transportation targets are damaged, they shall, according to the division of tasks, promptly organize and implement engineering reinforcement, emergency repair and emergency construction, and restore transportation as soon as possible.  
Where other transportation engineering facilities related to national defense transportation are damaged, their management units shall promptly report to the higher authorities in accordance with the management relationship and simultaneously organize repairs.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府国土资源、城乡规划等主管部门确定预定抢建重要国防交通工程设施的土地，作为国防交通控制范围，纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划。  
未经县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门、城乡规划主管部门和国防交通主管机构批准，任何组织和个人不得占用作为国防交通控制范围的土地。

**Article 45** The defense transportation competent authority of a people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the departments of land and resources, urban and rural planning and other relevant departments of the same level, determine the land designated for the emergency construction of important national defense transportation engineering facilities as the national defense transportation control area, and incorporate it into the overall land use plan and urban and rural plan.  
Without the approval of the competent departments of land and resources, urban and rural planning and the defense transportation competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level, no organization or individual may occupy the land designated as the national defense transportation control area.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　重点交通目标的对空、对海防御，由军队有关部门纳入对空、对海防御计划，统一组织实施。  
重点交通目标的地面防卫，由其所在地县级以上人民政府和有关军事机关共同组织实施。  
重点交通目标的工程技术防护，由其所在地县级以上人民政府交通主管部门会同本级人民政府国防交通主管机构、人民防空主管部门，组织指导其管理单位和保障单位实施。  
重点交通目标以外的其他交通设施的防护，由其所在地县级以上人民政府按照有关规定执行。

**Article 46** The air defense and sea defense of key transportation targets shall be incorporated into the air defense and sea defense plans by the relevant military departments and organized and implemented uniformly.  
The ground defense of key transportation targets shall be organized and implemented jointly by the local people's government at or above the county level and the relevant military organs.  
The engineering technical protection of key transportation targets shall be organized and directed by the competent transportation department of the local people's government at or above the county level jointly with the defense transportation competent authority and the civil air defense competent authority of the same level, and implemented by their management units and support units.  
The protection of other transportation facilities other than key transportation targets shall be implemented by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　因重大军事行动和国防科研生产试验以及与国防相关的保密物资、危险品运输等特殊需要，县级以上人民政府有关部门应当按照规定的权限和程序，在相关地区的陆域、水域、空域采取必要的交通管理措施和安全防护措施。有关军事机关应当给予协助。

**Article 47** Due to special needs such as major military operations, national defense scientific research and production tests, and transportation of confidential materials or dangerous goods related to national defense, the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures, take necessary traffic management measures and safety protection measures in the land areas, waters and airspace of the relevant regions. The relevant military organs shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府交通主管部门和有关军事机关、国防交通主管机构应当根据需要，组织相关企业事业单位开展国防交通专业保障队伍的训练、演练。  
国防交通专业保障队伍由企业事业单位按照有关规定组建。  
参加训练、演练的国防交通专业保障队伍人员的生活福利待遇，参照民兵参加军事训练的有关规定执行。

**Article 48** The competent transportation departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, the relevant military organs and the defense transportation competent authorities shall, as needed, organize relevant enterprises and public institutions to carry out training and exercises of specialized national defense transportation support teams.  
Specialized national defense transportation support teams shall be formed by enterprises and public institutions in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The living welfare benefits of personnel participating in training and exercises of specialized national defense transportation support teams shall be implemented with reference to the relevant provisions on militiamen participating in military training.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国防交通专业保障队伍执行国防交通工程设施抢修、抢建、防护和民用运载工具抢修以及人员物资抢运等任务，由县级以上人民政府国防交通主管机构会同本级人民政府交通主管部门统一调配。  
国防交通专业保障队伍的车辆、船舶和其他机动设备，执行任务时按照国家国防交通主管机构的规定设置统一标志，可以优先通行。

**Article 49** When specialized national defense transportation support teams perform tasks such as emergency repair, emergency construction and protection of national defense transportation engineering facilities, emergency repair of civil means of transport, and emergency transport of personnel and materials, they shall be uniformly deployed by the defense transportation competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level jointly with the competent transportation department of the same level.  
The vehicles, vessels and other motor equipment of specialized national defense transportation support teams, when performing tasks, shall display unified markings in accordance with the provisions of the national defense transportation competent authority of the State and may have the right of way.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府对承担国防交通保障任务的企业和个人，按照有关规定给予政策支持。

**Article 50** People's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, provide policy support to enterprises and individuals undertaking national defense transportation support tasks.

### 第七章　国防交通物资储备

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家建立国防交通物资储备制度，保证战时和平时特殊情况下国防交通顺畅的需要。  
国防交通物资储备应当布局合理、规模适度，储备的物资应当符合国家规定的质量标准。  
国防交通储备物资的品种由国家国防交通主管机构会同国务院有关部门和军队有关部门规定。

**Article 51** The State shall establish a system for the stockpiling of national defense transportation materials to ensure the needs of smooth national defense transportation in wartime and under special circumstances in peacetime.  
The stockpiling of national defense transportation materials shall be reasonably distributed and moderate in scale, and the stockpiled materials shall meet the quality standards prescribed by the State.  
The varieties of national defense transportation stockpiled materials shall be specified by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the military.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国务院交通主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国防交通主管机构，应当按照有关规定确定国防交通储备物资储存管理单位，监督检查国防交通储备物资管理工作。  
国防交通储备物资储存管理单位应当建立健全管理制度，按照国家有关规定和标准对储备物资进行保管、维护和更新，保证储备物资的使用效能和安全，不得挪用、损坏和丢失储备物资。

**Article 52** The competent department of transportation of the State Council and the national defense transportation competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, designate the storage management units for national defense transportation stockpiled materials, and supervise and inspect the management of such materials.  
The storage management units for national defense transportation stockpiled materials shall establish and improve the management system, keep, maintain, and update the stockpiled materials in accordance with the relevant regulations and standards of the State, ensure the use efficiency and safety of the stockpiled materials, and shall not misappropriate, damage, or lose the stockpiled materials.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　战时和平时特殊情况下执行交通防护和抢修、抢建任务，或者组织重大军事演习，抢险救灾以及国防交通专业保障队伍训练、演练等需要的，可以调用国防交通储备物资。  
调用中央储备的国防交通物资，由国家国防交通主管机构批准；调用地方储备的国防交通物资，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国防交通主管机构批准。  
国防交通储备物资储存管理单位，应当严格执行储备物资调用指令，不得拒绝或者拖延。  
未经批准，任何组织和个人不得动用国防交通储备物资。

**Article 53** Where it is necessary to carry out tasks of traffic protection, emergency repair, and emergency construction during wartime or under special circumstances in peacetime, or to organize major military exercises, disaster relief, and training or exercises of specialized national defense transportation support units, the national defense transportation stockpiled materials may be mobilized.  
The mobilization of nationally stockpiled national defense transportation materials shall be approved by the national defense transportation competent authority of the State; the mobilization of locally stockpiled national defense transportation materials shall be approved by the national defense transportation competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The storage management units for national defense transportation stockpiled materials shall strictly implement the orders for the mobilization of stockpiled materials, and shall not refuse or delay.  
Without approval, no organization or individual may use the national defense transportation stockpiled materials.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国防交通储备物资因产品技术升级、更新换代或者主要技术性能低于使用维护要求，丧失储备价值的，可以改变用途或者作报废处理。  
中央储备的国防交通物资需要改变用途或者作报废处理的，由国家国防交通主管机构组织技术鉴定并审核后，报国务院财政部门审批。  
地方储备的国防交通物资需要改变用途或者作报废处理的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府国防交通主管机构组织技术鉴定并审核后，报本级人民政府财政部门审批。  
中央和地方储备的国防交通物资改变用途或者报废获得的收益，应当上缴本级国库，纳入财政预算管理。

**Article 54** Where national defense transportation stockpiled materials have lost their stockpiling value due to product technology upgrades, model changes, or the main technical performance falling below the requirements for use and maintenance, they may have their use changed or be disposed of as scrap.  
For nationally stockpiled national defense transportation materials that need to have their use changed or be disposed of as scrap, the national defense transportation competent authority of the State shall organize an expert evaluation and review, and then submit the matter to the finance department of the State Council for approval.  
For locally stockpiled national defense transportation materials that need to have their use changed or be disposed of as scrap, the national defense transportation competent authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize an expert evaluation and review, and then submit the matter to the finance department of the people's government at the same level for approval.  
The proceeds obtained from changing the use or scrapping of nationally and locally stockpiled national defense transportation materials shall be turned over to the treasury at the same level and incorporated into the fiscal budget management.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府交通主管部门或者国防交通主管机构责令限期改正，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；有违法所得的，予以没收，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款：  
（一）擅自改变国防交通工程设施用途或者作报废处理的；  
（二）拒绝或者故意拖延执行国防运输任务的；  
（三）拒绝或者故意拖延执行重点交通目标抢修、抢建任务的；  
（四）拒绝或者故意拖延执行国防交通储备物资调用命令的；  
（五）擅自改变国防交通储备物资用途或者作报废处理的；  
（六）擅自动用国防交通储备物资的；  
（七）未按照规定保管、维护国防交通储备物资，造成损坏、丢失的。  
上述违法行为造成财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 55** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law by committing any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of transportation or the national defense transportation competent authority of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a time limit, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if there are illegal gains, such gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed:  
(1) Unauthorized change of the use of national defense transportation engineering facilities or disposal of them as scrap;  
(2) Refusal or intentional delay in performing national defense transportation tasks;  
(3) Refusal or intentional delay in performing tasks of emergency repair and emergency construction of key transportation targets;  
(4) Refusal or intentional delay in executing orders for the mobilization of national defense transportation stockpiled materials;  
(5) Unauthorized change of the use of national defense transportation stockpiled materials or disposal of them as scrap;  
(6) Unauthorized use of national defense transportation stockpiled materials;  
(7) Failure to store and maintain national defense transportation stockpiled materials according to regulations, causing damage or loss.  
If the above illegal acts cause property losses, the liable party shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国防交通主管机构、有关军事机关以及交通主管部门和其他相关部门的工作人员违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）滥用职权或者玩忽职守，给国防交通工作造成严重损失的；  
（二）贪污、挪用国防交通经费、物资的；  
（三）泄露在国防交通工作中知悉的国家秘密和商业秘密的；  
（四）在国防交通工作中侵害公民或者组织合法权益的。

**Article 56** Where functionaries of the national defense transportation competent authority, the relevant military organs, the competent department of transportation, and other relevant departments violate the provisions of this Law by committing any of the following acts, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Abuse of power or dereliction of duty, causing serious losses to national defense transportation work;  
(2) Embezzlement or misappropriation of national defense transportation funds or materials;  
(3) Disclosure of State secrets or trade secrets learned during national defense transportation work;  
(4) Infringement upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens or organizations in national defense transportation work.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes an act against public security administration shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本法所称国防交通工程设施，是指国家为国防目的修建的交通基础设施以及国防交通专用的指挥、检修、装卸、仓储等工程设施。  
本法所称国防运输，是指政府和军队为国防目的运用军民交通运输资源，运送人员、装备、物资的活动。军队运用自身资源进行的运输活动，按照中央军事委员会有关规定执行。

**Article 58** For the purposes of this Law, "national defense transportation engineering facilities" means the transportation infrastructure constructed by the State for national defense purposes and the command, maintenance, loading and unloading, warehousing, and other engineering facilities dedicated to national defense transportation.  
For the purposes of this Law, "national defense transportation" means the activities of the government and the military using military and civilian transportation resources to transport personnel, equipment, and materials for national defense purposes. Transportation activities conducted by the military using its own resources shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　与国防交通密切相关的信息设施、设备和专业保障队伍的建设、管理、使用活动，适用本法。  
国家对信息动员另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 59** This Law shall apply to the construction, management, and use of information facilities, equipment, and specialized support units closely related to national defense transportation.  
If the State has other provisions on information mobilization, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自2017年1月1日起施行。

**Article 60** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2017.
