# 中华人民共和国反间谍法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2023-04-26  
**生效日期**：2023-07-01

> （2014年11月1日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议通过　2023年4月26日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议修订）

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强反间谍工作，防范、制止和惩治间谍行为，维护国家安全，保护人民利益，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of strengthening counter-espionage work, preventing, stopping and punishing espionage activities, safeguarding national security, and protecting the interests of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　反间谍工作坚持党中央集中统一领导，坚持总体国家安全观，坚持公开工作与秘密工作相结合、专门工作与群众路线相结合，坚持积极防御、依法惩治、标本兼治，筑牢国家安全人民防线。

**Article 2** Counter-espionage work shall adhere to the centralized and unified leadership of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, adhere to a holistic approach to national security, adhere to the combination of open work and secret work, the combination of specialized work and the mass line, and adhere to active defense, punishment according to law, and addressing both symptoms and root causes, so as to build a solid people's defense line for national security.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　反间谍工作应当依法进行，尊重和保障人权，保障个人和组织的合法权益。

**Article 3** Counter-espionage work shall be conducted according to law, respect and safeguard human rights, and protect the lawful rights and interests of individuals and organizations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本法所称间谍行为，是指下列行为：  
（一）间谍组织及其代理人实施或者指使、资助他人实施，或者境内外机构、组织、个人与其相勾结实施的危害中华人民共和国国家安全的活动；  
（二）参加间谍组织或者接受间谍组织及其代理人的任务，或者投靠间谍组织及其代理人；  
（三）间谍组织及其代理人以外的其他境外机构、组织、个人实施或者指使、资助他人实施，或者境内机构、组织、个人与其相勾结实施的窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密、情报以及其他关系国家安全和利益的文件、数据、资料、物品，或者策动、引诱、胁迫、收买国家工作人员叛变的活动；  
（四）间谍组织及其代理人实施或者指使、资助他人实施，或者境内外机构、组织、个人与其相勾结实施针对国家机关、涉密单位或者关键信息基础设施等的网络攻击、侵入、干扰、控制、破坏等活动；  
（五）为敌人指示攻击目标；  
（六）进行其他间谍活动。  
间谍组织及其代理人在中华人民共和国领域内，或者利用中华人民共和国的公民、组织或者其他条件，从事针对第三国的间谍活动，危害中华人民共和国国家安全的，适用本法。

**Article 4** For the purposes of this Law, "espionage activities" means the following acts:  
(1) Activities conducted by espionage organizations or their agents, or by their instigation or funding, or activities conducted in collusion with them by any domestic or foreign institution, organization, or individual, that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Joining an espionage organization or accepting tasks assigned by an espionage organization or its agents, or defecting to an espionage organization or its agents;  
(3) Activities other than those in items (1) and (2) where foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals (other than espionage organizations or their agents) conduct, instigate, or fund others to conduct, or where domestic institutions, organizations, or individuals collude with them to steal, spy, buy, or illegally provide state secrets, intelligence, or other documents, data, information, or articles relating to national security and interests, or to incite, induce, coerce, or bribe state functionaries to defect;  
(4) Activities conducted by espionage organizations or their agents, or by their instigation or funding, or activities conducted in collusion with them by any domestic or foreign institution, organization, or individual, such as cyber attacks, intrusions, interference, control, or destruction against state organs, classified entities, or critical information infrastructure;  
(5) Indicating attack targets for the enemy;  
(6) Engaging in other espionage activities.  
Where espionage organizations or their agents engage in espionage activities against a third country within the territory of the People's Republic of China, or by using Chinese citizens, organizations, or other conditions, thereby endangering the national security of the People's Republic of China, this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立反间谍工作协调机制，统筹协调反间谍工作中的重大事项，研究、解决反间谍工作中的重大问题。

**Article 5** The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for counter-espionage work to coordinate major matters in counter-espionage work and to study and resolve major issues in counter-espionage work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家安全机关是反间谍工作的主管机关。  
公安、保密等有关部门和军队有关部门按照职责分工，密切配合，加强协调，依法做好有关工作。

**Article 6** The State security organs shall be the competent authorities for counter-espionage work.  
The public security, secrecy and other relevant departments, as well as the relevant departments of the military, shall, according to their respective functions and duties, closely cooperate and strengthen coordination, and shall properly perform relevant work according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国公民有维护国家的安全、荣誉和利益的义务，不得有危害国家的安全、荣誉和利益的行为。  
一切国家机关和武装力量、各政党和各人民团体、企业事业组织和其他社会组织，都有防范、制止间谍行为，维护国家安全的义务。  
国家安全机关在反间谍工作中必须依靠人民的支持，动员、组织人民防范、制止间谍行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 7** It shall be the duty of citizens of the People's Republic of China to safeguard the security, honor and interests of the State, and they shall not commit any act detrimental to the security, honor and interests of the State.  
All state organs, armed forces, political parties, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other social organizations shall have the duty to prevent and stop espionage activities and to safeguard national security.  
The State security organs must rely on the support of the people in counter-espionage work and shall mobilize and organize the people to prevent and stop espionage activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何公民和组织都应当依法支持、协助反间谍工作，保守所知悉的国家秘密和反间谍工作秘密。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 8** All citizens and organizations shall support and assist counter-espionage work according to law, and shall keep confidential any state secrets and counter-espionage work secrets that they have come to know.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对支持、协助反间谍工作的个人和组织给予保护。  
对举报间谍行为或者在反间谍工作中做出重大贡献的个人和组织，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** The State shall provide protection to individuals and organizations that support or assist counter-espionage work.  
Individuals and organizations that report espionage activities or make significant contributions in counter-espionage work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　境外机构、组织、个人实施或者指使、资助他人实施的，或者境内机构、组织、个人与境外机构、组织、个人相勾结实施的危害中华人民共和国国家安全的间谍行为，都必须受到法律追究。

**Article 10** Espionage activities endangering the national security of the People's Republic of China, whether conducted by foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals, or by their instigation or funding, or conducted by domestic institutions, organizations, or individuals in collusion with foreign institutions, organizations, or individuals, shall all be subject to legal investigation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家安全机关及其工作人员在工作中，应当严格依法办事，不得超越职权、滥用职权，不得侵犯个人和组织的合法权益。  
国家安全机关及其工作人员依法履行反间谍工作职责获取的个人和组织的信息，只能用于反间谍工作。对属于国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私、个人信息的，应当保密。

**Article 11** State security organs and their staff members shall strictly act according to law in their work, shall not exceed or abuse their powers, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of individuals or organizations.  
Information about individuals and organizations obtained by State security organs and their staff members in the performance of their counter-espionage duties according to law may only be used for counter-espionage work. Information that constitutes state secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or personal information shall be kept confidential.

### 第二章　安全防范

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家机关、人民团体、企业事业组织和其他社会组织承担本单位反间谍安全防范工作的主体责任，落实反间谍安全防范措施，对本单位的人员进行维护国家安全的教育，动员、组织本单位的人员防范、制止间谍行为。  
地方各级人民政府、相关行业主管部门按照职责分工，管理本行政区域、本行业有关反间谍安全防范工作。  
国家安全机关依法协调指导、监督检查反间谍安全防范工作。

**Article 12** State organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other social organizations shall assume the primary responsibility for counter-espionage security precautions within their respective units, implement counter-espionage security precaution measures, conduct education on safeguarding national security among their personnel, and mobilize and organize their personnel to prevent and stop espionage activities.  
Local people's governments at all levels and relevant competent industry departments shall, according to their respective functions and duties, manage counter-espionage security precautions within their respective administrative regions or industries.  
State security organs shall, according to law, coordinate, guide, supervise, and inspect counter-espionage security precautions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当组织开展反间谍安全防范宣传教育，将反间谍安全防范知识纳入教育、培训、普法宣传内容，增强全民反间谍安全防范意识和国家安全素养。  
新闻、广播、电视、文化、互联网信息服务等单位，应当面向社会有针对性地开展反间谍宣传教育。  
国家安全机关应当根据反间谍安全防范形势，指导有关单位开展反间谍宣传教育活动，提高防范意识和能力。

**Article 13** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall organize and carry out publicity and education on counter-espionage security precautions, incorporate knowledge of counter-espionage security precautions into education, training, and legal popularization publicity, and enhance the public's awareness of counter-espionage security precautions and national security literacy.  
Press, radio, television, culture, internet information services, and other entities shall carry out targeted counter-espionage publicity and education for the public.  
State security organs shall, based on the situation of counter-espionage security precautions, guide relevant entities in carrying out counter-espionage publicity and education activities to improve their awareness and capability of taking precautions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何个人和组织都不得非法获取、持有属于国家秘密的文件、数据、资料、物品。

**Article 14** No individual or organization may illegally obtain or possess documents, data, information, or articles that are state secrets.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　任何个人和组织都不得非法生产、销售、持有、使用间谍活动特殊需要的专用间谍器材。专用间谍器材由国务院国家安全主管部门依照国家有关规定确认。

**Article 15** No individual or organization may illegally produce, sell, possess, or use specialized espionage equipment specifically needed for espionage activities. Specialized espionage equipment shall be identified by the competent department of State security under the State Council in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　任何公民和组织发现间谍行为，应当及时向国家安全机关举报；向公安机关等其他国家机关、组织举报的，相关国家机关、组织应当立即移送国家安全机关处理。  
国家安全机关应当将受理举报的电话、信箱、网络平台等向社会公开，依法及时处理举报信息，并为举报人保密。

**Article 16** Any citizen or organization that discovers espionage activities shall promptly report them to the State security organs; if they report to other state organs or organizations such as public security organs, the relevant state organs or organizations shall immediately transfer the case to the State security organs for handling.  
The State security organs shall make public the telephone numbers, mailboxes, online platforms, etc. for receiving reports, handle the reported information promptly according to law, and keep the whistleblower confidential.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家建立反间谍安全防范重点单位管理制度。  
反间谍安全防范重点单位应当建立反间谍安全防范工作制度，履行反间谍安全防范工作要求，明确内设职能部门和人员承担反间谍安全防范职责。

**Article 17** The State shall establish a management system for key units for counter-espionage security precautions.  
Key units for counter-espionage security precautions shall establish a work system for counter-espionage security precautions, fulfill the requirements for counter-espionage security precautions, and designate internal functional departments and personnel to assume the responsibilities for counter-espionage security precautions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　反间谍安全防范重点单位应当加强对工作人员反间谍安全防范的教育和管理，对离岗离职人员脱密期内履行反间谍安全防范义务的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 18** Key units for counter-espionage security precautions shall strengthen the education and management of their staff members regarding counter-espionage security precautions, and shall supervise and inspect the performance of counter-espionage security precaution obligations by personnel who have left their posts or resigned during the period of declassification.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　反间谍安全防范重点单位应当加强对涉密事项、场所、载体等的日常安全防范管理，采取隔离加固、封闭管理、设置警戒等反间谍物理防范措施。

**Article 19** Key units for counter-espionage security precautions shall strengthen the daily security precaution management of classified matters, premises, and carriers, and shall take counter-espionage physical precaution measures such as isolation reinforcement, closed management, and setting up guard posts.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　反间谍安全防范重点单位应当按照反间谍技术防范的要求和标准，采取相应的技术措施和其他必要措施，加强对要害部门部位、网络设施、信息系统的反间谍技术防范。

**Article 20** Key units for counter-espionage security precautions shall, in accordance with the requirements and standards for counter-espionage technical precautions, take corresponding technical measures and other necessary measures to strengthen counter-espionage technical precautions for key departments and positions, network facilities, and information systems.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在重要国家机关、国防军工单位和其他重要涉密单位以及重要军事设施的周边安全控制区域内新建、改建、扩建建设项目的，由国家安全机关实施涉及国家安全事项的建设项目许可。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府编制国民经济和社会发展规划、国土空间规划等有关规划，应当充分考虑国家安全因素和划定的安全控制区域，征求国家安全机关的意见。  
安全控制区域的划定应当统筹发展和安全，坚持科学合理、确有必要的原则，由国家安全机关会同发展改革、自然资源、住房城乡建设、保密、国防科技工业等部门以及军队有关部门共同划定，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准并动态调整。  
涉及国家安全事项的建设项目许可的具体实施办法，由国务院国家安全主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 21** Where a construction project is newly built, expanded, or renovated within the surrounding security control areas of important state organs, national defense and military industrial units, other important classified units, and important military facilities, the state security organ shall implement the construction project permit concerning matters involving state security.  
When formulating relevant plans such as national economic and social development plans and territorial space plans, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall fully consider state security factors and the delineated security control areas, and seek opinions from the state security organ.  
The delineation of security control areas shall coordinate development and security, adhere to the principles of being scientific, reasonable, and truly necessary, and shall be jointly delineated by the state security organ together with the development and reform, natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, secrecy, defense science, technology and industry departments, as well as relevant military departments, and shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval and dynamic adjustment.  
The specific implementation measures for the construction project permit concerning matters involving state security shall be formulated by the state security department of the State Council together with relevant departments.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家安全机关根据反间谍工作需要，可以会同有关部门制定反间谍技术防范标准，指导有关单位落实反间谍技术防范措施，对存在隐患的单位，经过严格的批准手续，可以进行反间谍技术防范检查和检测。

**Article 22** The state security organ may, in light of the needs of counter-espionage work, formulate technical prevention standards against espionage jointly with relevant departments, guide relevant units to implement technical prevention measures against espionage, and may, after undergoing strict approval procedures, conduct technical prevention inspection and testing against espionage for units with existing risks.

### 第三章　调查处置

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家安全机关在反间谍工作中依法行使本法和有关法律规定的职权。

**Article 23** The state security organ shall, in counter-espionage work, exercise the functions and powers provided for in this Law and relevant laws according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务时，依照规定出示工作证件，可以查验中国公民或者境外人员的身份证明，向有关个人和组织问询有关情况，对身份不明、有间谍行为嫌疑的人员，可以查看其随带物品。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** When performing counter-espionage tasks according to law, the state security organ personnel may, after presenting their work credentials in accordance with regulations, examine the identity certificates of Chinese citizens or persons outside the territory, inquire about relevant circumstances from relevant individuals and organizations, and may inspect the articles carried by persons whose identity is unclear or who are suspected of espionage activities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务时，经设区的市级以上国家安全机关负责人批准，出示工作证件，可以查验有关个人和组织的电子设备、设施及有关程序、工具。查验中发现存在危害国家安全情形的，国家安全机关应当责令其采取措施立即整改。拒绝整改或者整改后仍存在危害国家安全隐患的，可以予以查封、扣押。  
对依照前款规定查封、扣押的电子设备、设施及有关程序、工具，在危害国家安全的情形消除后，国家安全机关应当及时解除查封、扣押。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-18" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 25** When performing counter-espionage tasks according to law, the state security organ personnel may, upon approval by the person in charge of the state security organ at or above the city divided into districts level and after presenting their work credentials, examine the electronic equipment, facilities, and relevant programs and tools of relevant individuals and organizations. If any situation endangering state security is discovered during the examination, the state security organ shall order the person concerned to take measures for immediate rectification. If the person refuses to rectify or, after rectification, there still exists a hidden danger endangering state security, the state security organ may seal up or seize the items.  
With respect to the electronic equipment, facilities, and relevant programs and tools sealed up or seized in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the state security organ shall promptly remove the sealing up or seizure after the situation endangering state security has been eliminated.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务时，根据国家有关规定，经设区的市级以上国家安全机关负责人批准，可以查阅、调取有关的文件、数据、资料、物品，有关个人和组织应当予以配合。查阅、调取不得超出执行反间谍工作任务所需的范围和限度。

**Article 26** When performing counter-espionage tasks according to law, the state security organ personnel may, in accordance with relevant state regulations and upon approval by the person in charge of the state security organ at or above the city divided into districts level, consult or obtain relevant documents, data, materials, and articles, and relevant individuals and organizations shall cooperate. The consultation or obtaining shall not exceed the scope and limits necessary for the performance of counter-espionage tasks.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　需要传唤违反本法的人员接受调查的，经国家安全机关办案部门负责人批准，使用传唤证传唤。对现场发现的违反本法的人员，国家安全机关工作人员依照规定出示工作证件，可以口头传唤，但应当在询问笔录中注明。传唤的原因和依据应当告知被传唤人。对无正当理由拒不接受传唤或者逃避传唤的人，可以强制传唤。  
国家安全机关应当在被传唤人所在市、县内的指定地点或者其住所进行询问。  
国家安全机关对被传唤人应当及时询问查证。询问查证的时间不得超过八小时；情况复杂，可能适用行政拘留或者涉嫌犯罪的，询问查证的时间不得超过二十四小时。国家安全机关应当为被传唤人提供必要的饮食和休息时间。严禁连续传唤。  
除无法通知或者可能妨碍调查的情形以外，国家安全机关应当及时将传唤的原因通知被传唤人家属。在上述情形消失后，应当立即通知被传唤人家属。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-20" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Where a person who violates this Law needs to be summoned for investigation, the summons shall be made by a summons certificate upon approval by the person in charge of the case-handling department of the state security organ. For a person who is found on the spot to have violated this Law, the state security organ personnel may, after presenting their work credentials in accordance with regulations, make an oral summons, but shall indicate this in the interrogation record. The reason and basis for the summons shall be informed to the person summoned. A person who, without justifiable reasons, refuses to accept the summons or evades the summons may be summoned by force.  
The state security organ shall conduct the interrogation at a designated place within the city or county where the person summoned is located, or at the person's residence.  
The state security organ shall promptly interrogate and verify the person summoned. The time for interrogation and verification shall not exceed eight hours; where the circumstances are complex and administrative detention may be applicable or the person is suspected of committing a crime, the time for interrogation and verification shall not exceed twenty-four hours. The state security organ shall provide the person summoned with necessary food and rest time. Continuous summons is strictly prohibited.  
Except where notification is impossible or may hinder the investigation, the state security organ shall promptly notify the family members of the person summoned of the reason for the summons. After the above circumstances have disappeared, the family members shall be notified immediately.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家安全机关调查间谍行为，经设区的市级以上国家安全机关负责人批准，可以依法对涉嫌间谍行为的人身、物品、场所进行检查。  
检查女性身体的，应当由女性工作人员进行。

**Article 28** When investigating espionage activities, the state security organ may, upon approval by the person in charge of the state security organ at or above the city divided into districts level, inspect the person, articles, and premises suspected of being involved in espionage activities according to law.  
Where a female person's body is inspected, the inspection shall be carried out by female staff.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家安全机关调查间谍行为，经设区的市级以上国家安全机关负责人批准，可以查询涉嫌间谍行为人员的相关财产信息。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-21" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** When investigating espionage activities, the state security organ may, upon approval by the person in charge of the state security organ at or above the city divided into districts level, inquire about the relevant property information of a person suspected of espionage activities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家安全机关调查间谍行为，经设区的市级以上国家安全机关负责人批准，可以对涉嫌用于间谍行为的场所、设施或者财物依法查封、扣押、冻结；不得查封、扣押、冻结与被调查的间谍行为无关的场所、设施或者财物。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-22" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第23条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 30** When investigating espionage activities, the state security organ may, upon approval by the person in charge of the state security organ at or above the city divided into districts level, seal up, seize, or freeze the premises, facilities, or property suspected of being used for espionage activities according to law; it shall not seal up, seize, or freeze premises, facilities, or property unrelated to the espionage activities under investigation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家安全机关工作人员在反间谍工作中采取查阅、调取、传唤、检查、查询、查封、扣押、冻结等措施，应当由二人以上进行，依照有关规定出示工作证件及相关法律文书，并由相关人员在有关笔录等书面材料上签名、盖章。  
国家安全机关工作人员进行检查、查封、扣押等重要取证工作，应当对全过程进行录音录像，留存备查。

**Article 31** When taking measures such as consultation, obtaining, summons, inspection, inquiry, sealing up, seizure, and freezing in counter-espionage work, state security organ personnel shall be carried out by two or more persons, present their work credentials and relevant legal documents in accordance with relevant regulations, and have relevant persons sign and seal the relevant written materials such as records.  
Where state security organ personnel conduct important evidence collection work such as inspection, sealing up, and seizure, they shall make audio and video recordings of the entire process and retain them for future reference.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在国家安全机关调查了解有关间谍行为的情况、收集有关证据时，有关个人和组织应当如实提供，不得拒绝。

**Article 32** When the state security organ investigates and understands the circumstances of relevant espionage activities and collects relevant evidence, relevant individuals and organizations shall truthfully provide information and shall not refuse.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对出境后可能对国家安全造成危害，或者对国家利益造成重大损失的中国公民，国务院国家安全主管部门可以决定其在一定期限内不准出境，并通知移民管理机构。  
对涉嫌间谍行为人员，省级以上国家安全机关可以通知移民管理机构不准其出境。

**Article 33** For a Chinese citizen who, after leaving the country, may cause harm to state security or cause significant losses to state interests, the state security department of the State Council may decide that such person shall not be allowed to leave the country within a certain period and shall notify the immigration administration authority.  
For a person suspected of espionage activities, the state security organ at or above the provincial level may notify the immigration administration authority that such person shall not be allowed to leave the country.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对入境后可能进行危害中华人民共和国国家安全活动的境外人员，国务院国家安全主管部门可以通知移民管理机构不准其入境。

**Article 34** For a person outside the territory who, after entering the country, may engage in activities endangering the security of the People's Republic of China, the state security department of the State Council may notify the immigration administration authority that such person shall not be allowed to enter the country.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对国家安全机关通知不准出境或者不准入境的人员，移民管理机构应当按照国家有关规定执行；不准出境、入境情形消失的，国家安全机关应当及时撤销不准出境、入境决定，并通知移民管理机构。

**Article 35** For a person notified by the state security organ as not allowed to leave or enter the country, the immigration administration authority shall execute the decision in accordance with relevant state regulations; where the circumstances giving rise to the prohibition on leaving or entering the country have disappeared, the state security organ shall promptly revoke the decision on prohibiting leaving or entering the country and notify the immigration administration authority.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家安全机关发现涉及间谍行为的网络信息内容或者网络攻击等风险，应当依照《中华人民共和国网络安全法》规定的职责分工，及时通报有关部门，由其依法处置或者责令电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者及时采取修复漏洞、加固网络防护、停止传输、消除程序和内容、暂停相关服务、下架相关应用、关闭相关网站等措施，保存相关记录。情况紧急，不立即采取措施将对国家安全造成严重危害的，由国家安全机关责令有关单位修复漏洞、停止相关传输、暂停相关服务，并通报有关部门。  
经采取相关措施，上述信息内容或者风险已经消除的，国家安全机关和有关部门应当及时作出恢复相关传输和服务的决定。

**Article 36** Where the state security organ discovers network information content or risks such as network attacks involving espionage activities, it shall, in accordance with the division of responsibilities provided for in the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, promptly notify the relevant departments, which shall handle the matter according to law or order telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers to promptly take measures such as fixing vulnerabilities, strengthening network protection, stopping transmission, removing programs and content, suspending relevant services, removing relevant applications, and closing relevant websites, and shall preserve relevant records. In an emergency, where failure to take immediate measures will cause serious harm to state security, the state security organ shall order the relevant units to fix vulnerabilities, stop relevant transmission, and suspend relevant services, and shall notify the relevant departments.  
After the relevant measures have been taken and the above-mentioned information content or risks have been eliminated, the state security organ and the relevant departments shall promptly make a decision to restore relevant transmission and services.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家安全机关因反间谍工作需要，根据国家有关规定，经过严格的批准手续，可以采取技术侦察措施和身份保护措施。

**Article 37** For the needs of counter-espionage work, the state security organ may, in accordance with relevant state regulations and after undergoing strict approval procedures, adopt technical investigation measures and identity protection measures.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对违反本法规定，涉嫌犯罪，需要对有关事项是否属于国家秘密或者情报进行鉴定以及需要对危害后果进行评估的，由国家保密部门或者省、自治区、直辖市保密部门按照程序在一定期限内进行鉴定和组织评估。

**Article 38** Where a person violates the provisions of this Law and is suspected of committing a crime, and it is necessary to appraise whether the relevant matter constitutes a state secret or intelligence and to assess the harmful consequences, the state secrecy department or the secrecy department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the procedures, conduct the appraisal and organize the assessment within a certain time limit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家安全机关经调查，发现间谍行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定立案侦查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/公安司法/中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** Where, after investigation, the state security organ discovers that espionage activities are suspected of constituting a crime, it shall file a case for investigation in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　保障与监督

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家安全机关工作人员依法履行职责，受法律保护。

**Article 40** State security organ personnel who perform their duties according to law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家安全机关依法调查间谍行为，邮政、快递等物流运营单位和电信业务经营者、互联网服务提供者应当提供必要的支持和协助。

**Article 41** State security organs shall, according to law, investigate espionage activities. Postal, courier and other logistics operation units, as well as telecommunications business operators and Internet service providers, shall provide necessary support and assistance.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家安全机关工作人员因执行紧急任务需要，经出示工作证件，享有优先乘坐公共交通工具、优先通行等通行便利。

**Article 42** State security organ staff, when required to perform urgent tasks, upon producing their work credentials, shall enjoy priority in taking public transport, priority passage and other traffic conveniences.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行任务时，依照规定出示工作证件，可以进入有关场所、单位；根据国家有关规定，经过批准，出示工作证件，可以进入限制进入的有关地区、场所、单位。

**Article 43** When state security organ staff perform tasks according to law, they may, by producing their work credentials in accordance with regulations, enter relevant premises and units; pursuant to relevant state regulations and upon approval, they may, by producing their work credentials, enter restricted areas, premises, and units.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家安全机关因反间谍工作需要，根据国家有关规定，可以优先使用或者依法征用国家机关、人民团体、企业事业组织和其他社会组织以及个人的交通工具、通信工具、场地和建筑物等，必要时可以设置相关工作场所和设施设备，任务完成后应当及时归还或者恢复原状，并依照规定支付相应费用；造成损失的，应当给予补偿。

**Article 44** For the needs of counter-espionage work, state security organs may, pursuant to relevant state regulations, give priority to using or lawfully requisition the means of transport, communication tools, sites, and buildings of state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, other social organizations, and individuals. When necessary, they may set up relevant work premises and facilities. After the task is completed, they shall promptly return or restore them to their original condition, and shall pay the corresponding fees in accordance with regulations; if losses are caused, compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家安全机关因反间谍工作需要，根据国家有关规定，可以提请海关、移民管理等检查机关对有关人员提供通关便利，对有关资料、器材等予以免检。有关检查机关应当依法予以协助。

**Article 45** For the needs of counter-espionage work, state security organs may, pursuant to relevant state regulations, request customs, immigration administration and other inspection authorities to provide customs clearance facilitation for relevant personnel and to exempt relevant materials and equipment from inspection. The relevant inspection authorities shall provide assistance according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家安全机关工作人员因执行任务，或者个人因协助执行反间谍工作任务，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全受到威胁时，国家安全机关应当会同有关部门依法采取必要措施，予以保护、营救。  
个人因支持、协助反间谍工作，本人或者其近亲属的人身安全面临危险的，可以向国家安全机关请求予以保护。国家安全机关应当会同有关部门依法采取保护措施。  
个人和组织因支持、协助反间谍工作导致财产损失的，根据国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 46** When state security organ staff, due to performing tasks, or individuals, due to assisting in counter-espionage tasks, face threats to the personal safety of themselves or their close relatives, the state security organ shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, take necessary measures according to law to protect and rescue them.  
Where individuals, due to supporting or assisting counter-espionage work, face danger to the personal safety of themselves or their close relatives, they may request protection from the state security organ. The state security organ shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, take protective measures according to law.  
Where individuals or organizations suffer property losses due to supporting or assisting counter-espionage work, compensation shall be given in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对为反间谍工作做出贡献并需要安置的人员，国家给予妥善安置。  
公安、民政、财政、卫生健康、教育、人力资源和社会保障、退役军人事务、医疗保障、移民管理等有关部门以及国有企业事业单位应当协助国家安全机关做好安置工作。

**Article 47** The State shall provide proper placement for personnel who have made contributions to counter-espionage work and need placement.  
Public security, civil affairs, finance, health, education, human resources and social security, veterans affairs, medical security, immigration administration and other relevant departments, as well as state-owned enterprises and institutions, shall assist state security organs in performing the placement work.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对因开展反间谍工作或者支持、协助反间谍工作导致伤残或者牺牲、死亡的人员，根据国家有关规定给予相应的抚恤优待。

**Article 48** For personnel who become disabled, sacrifice, or die due to carrying out counter-espionage work or supporting/assisting counter-espionage work, corresponding pensions and preferential treatment shall be given in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家鼓励反间谍领域科技创新，发挥科技在反间谍工作中的作用。

**Article 49** The State encourages scientific and technological innovation in the field of counter-espionage and shall give play to the role of science and technology in counter-espionage work.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家安全机关应当加强反间谍专业力量人才队伍建设和专业训练，提升反间谍工作能力。  
对国家安全机关工作人员应当有计划地进行政治、理论和业务培训。培训应当坚持理论联系实际、按需施教、讲求实效，提高专业能力。

**Article 50** State security organs shall strengthen the construction of professional talent teams and professional training in counter-espionage, and enhance counter-espionage work capability.  
Political, theoretical, and professional training shall be provided to state security organ staff in a planned manner. Training shall adhere to the principles of integrating theory with practice, teaching according to needs, and emphasizing practical results, so as to improve professional competence.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家安全机关应当严格执行内部监督和安全审查制度，对其工作人员遵守法律和纪律等情况进行监督，并依法采取必要措施，定期或者不定期进行安全审查。

**Article 51** State security organs shall strictly implement the internal supervision and security review system, supervise their staff's compliance with laws and discipline, and take necessary measures according to law to conduct regular or irregular security reviews.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　任何个人和组织对国家安全机关及其工作人员超越职权、滥用职权和其他违法行为，都有权向上级国家安全机关或者监察机关、人民检察院等有关部门检举、控告。受理检举、控告的国家安全机关或者监察机关、人民检察院等有关部门应当及时查清事实，依法处理，并将处理结果及时告知检举人、控告人。  
对支持、协助国家安全机关工作或者依法检举、控告的个人和组织，任何个人和组织不得压制和打击报复。

**Article 52** Any individual or organization shall have the right to report or accuse to the higher-level state security organ, supervision organ, People's Procuratorate, or other relevant departments regarding state security organs and their staff who exceed their authority, abuse power, or commit other illegal acts. The state security organ, supervision organ, People's Procuratorate, or other relevant department that accepts the report or accusation shall promptly ascertain the facts, handle the matter according to law, and inform the informant or accuser of the outcome in a timely manner.  
No individual or organization may suppress or retaliate against individuals or organizations that support or assist the work of state security organs or that lawfully make reports or accusations.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　实施间谍行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Whoever commits espionage conduct that constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　个人实施间谍行为，尚不构成犯罪的，由国家安全机关予以警告或者处十五日以下行政拘留，单处或者并处五万元以下罚款，违法所得在五万元以上的，单处或者并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款，并可以由有关部门依法予以处分。  
明知他人实施间谍行为，为其提供信息、资金、物资、劳务、技术、场所等支持、协助，或者窝藏、包庇，尚不构成犯罪的，依照前款的规定处罚。  
单位有前两款行为的，由国家安全机关予以警告，单处或者并处五十万元以下罚款，违法所得在五十万元以上的，单处或者并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照第一款的规定处罚。  
国家安全机关根据相关单位、人员违法情节和后果，可以建议有关主管部门依法责令停止从事相关业务、提供相关服务或者责令停产停业、吊销有关证照、撤销登记。有关主管部门应当将作出行政处理的情况及时反馈国家安全机关。

**Article 54** Where an individual commits espionage conduct that does not yet constitute a crime, the state security organ shall give a warning or impose administrative detention of not more than 15 days, and impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan alone or concurrently; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed alone or concurrently, and the relevant departments may impose a sanction according to law.  
Where a person, knowing that another person commits espionage conduct, provides support or assistance such as information, funds, materials, labor services, technology, or premises, or harbors or covers up for the other person, and the conduct does not yet constitute a crime, he or she shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
Where a unit commits any of the acts mentioned in the preceding two paragraphs, the state security organ shall give a warning, and impose a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan alone or concurrently; if the illegal gains are 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed alone or concurrently; and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph.  
Based on the illegal circumstances and consequences of the relevant unit or person, the state security organ may propose that the relevant competent department order the cessation of the relevant business or provision of the relevant services, or order suspension of production or business operation, revocation of relevant licenses, or cancellation of registration according to law. The relevant competent department shall promptly inform the state security organ of the administrative handling situation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　实施间谍行为，有自首或者立功表现的，可以从轻、减轻或者免除处罚；有重大立功表现的，给予奖励。  
在境外受胁迫或者受诱骗参加间谍组织、敌对组织，从事危害中华人民共和国国家安全的活动，及时向中华人民共和国驻外机构如实说明情况，或者入境后直接或者通过所在单位及时向国家安全机关如实说明情况，并有悔改表现的，可以不予追究。

**Article 55** Where a person who commits espionage conduct has voluntary surrender or meritorious service, he or she may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment or be exempted from punishment; where there is major meritorious service, a reward shall be given.  
Where a person, under coercion or inducement abroad, joins an espionage organization or hostile organization, or engages in activities that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China, and promptly reports truthfully to the overseas mission of the People's Republic of China, or after entering the country, promptly reports truthfully to the state security organ directly or through the unit, and shows repentance, the matter may not be pursued.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家机关、人民团体、企业事业组织和其他社会组织未按照本法规定履行反间谍安全防范义务的，国家安全机关可以责令改正；未按照要求改正的，国家安全机关可以约谈相关负责人，必要时可以将约谈情况通报该单位上级主管部门；产生危害后果或者不良影响的，国家安全机关可以予以警告、通报批评；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，由有关部门依法予以处分。

**Article 56** Where state organs, people's organizations, enterprises, public institutions, or other social organizations fail to perform their counter-espionage security prevention obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the state security organ may order them to make corrections; if they fail to make corrections as required, the state security organ may interview the relevant responsible persons, and if necessary, may notify the situation of the interview to the higher-level competent department of the unit; if harmful consequences or adverse effects occur, the state security organ may give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the leading persons responsible and directly liable persons shall be given sanctions by the relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定新建、改建、扩建建设项目的，由国家安全机关责令改正，予以警告；拒不改正或者情节严重的，责令停止建设或者使用、暂扣或者吊销许可证件，或者建议有关主管部门依法予以处理。

**Article 57** Whoever constructs, reconstructs, or expands a construction project in violation of Article 21 of this Law shall be ordered by the state security organ to make corrections and be given a warning; if the party refuses to make corrections or if the circumstances are serious, the state security organ shall order suspension of construction or use, suspend or revoke the permit, or propose that the relevant competent department handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本法第四十一条](#art-41)规定的，由国家安全机关责令改正，予以警告或者通报批评；拒不改正或者情节严重的，由有关主管部门依照相关法律法规予以处罚。

**Article 58** Whoever violates Article 41 of this Law shall be ordered by the state security organ to make corrections and be given a warning or a circulating notice of criticism; if the party refuses to make corrections or if the circumstances are serious, the relevant competent department shall impose a punishment in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，拒不配合数据调取的，由国家安全机关依照《中华人民共和国数据安全法》的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 59** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and refuses to cooperate with data retrieval shall be punished by the state security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由国家安全机关予以警告或者处十日以下行政拘留，可以并处三万元以下罚款：  
（一）泄露有关反间谍工作的国家秘密；  
（二）明知他人有间谍犯罪行为，在国家安全机关向其调查有关情况、收集有关证据时，拒绝提供；  
（三）故意阻碍国家安全机关依法执行任务；  
（四）隐藏、转移、变卖、损毁国家安全机关依法查封、扣押、冻结的财物；  
（五）明知是间谍行为的涉案财物而窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒；  
（六）对依法支持、协助国家安全机关工作的个人和组织进行打击报复。

**Article 60** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, if constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if not constituting a crime, shall be given a warning by the state security organ or administrative detention of not more than 10 days, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) Divulging State secrets concerning counter-espionage work;  
(2) Knowing that another person has committed espionage crimes, refusing to provide information when the state security organ investigates the relevant circumstances and collects relevant evidence;  
(3) Intentionally obstructing the state security organ from performing its tasks according to law;  
(4) Concealing, transferring, selling, or damaging property that has been sealed up, seized, or frozen by the state security organ according to law;  
(5) Knowing that property is involved in espionage conduct, harboring, transferring, purchasing, selling on commission, or otherwise disguising or concealing it;  
(6) Retaliating against individuals or organizations that lawfully support or assist the work of state security organs.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　非法获取、持有属于国家秘密的文件、数据、资料、物品，以及非法生产、销售、持有、使用专用间谍器材，尚不构成犯罪的，由国家安全机关予以警告或者处十日以下行政拘留。

**Article 61** Whoever illegally obtains or holds documents, data, materials, or articles that are State secrets, or illegally produces, sells, holds, or uses specialized espionage equipment, if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be given a warning by the State security authority or be detained for not more than ten days as an administrative penalty.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家安全机关对依照本法查封、扣押、冻结的财物，应当妥善保管，并按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）涉嫌犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关法律的规定处理；  
（二）尚不构成犯罪，有违法事实的，对依法应当没收的予以没收，依法应当销毁的予以销毁；  
（三）没有违法事实的，或者与案件无关的，应当解除查封、扣押、冻结，并及时返还相关财物；造成损失的，应当依法予以赔偿。

**Article 62** The State security authority shall properly keep the property sealed up, distrained, or frozen in accordance with this Law, and shall handle it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the property is suspected of being involved in a crime, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws;  
(2) If the act does not constitute a crime but there are illegal facts, the property that shall be confiscated according to law shall be confiscated, and the property that shall be destroyed according to law shall be destroyed;  
(3) If there are no illegal facts or the property is not related to the case, the sealing up, distraint, or freezing shall be lifted, and the relevant property shall be returned in a timely manner; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　涉案财物符合下列情形之一的，应当依法予以追缴、没收，或者采取措施消除隐患：  
（一）违法所得的财物及其孳息、收益，供实施间谍行为所用的本人财物；  
（二）非法获取、持有的属于国家秘密的文件、数据、资料、物品；  
（三）非法生产、销售、持有、使用的专用间谍器材。

**Article 63** Where the property involved in the case falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be recovered or confiscated according to law, or measures shall be taken to eliminate the hidden danger:  
(1) The property obtained illegally and its fruits and proceeds, and the personal property used for committing espionage;  
(2) The documents, data, materials, and articles that are State secrets illegally obtained or held;  
(3) The specialized espionage equipment illegally produced, sold, held, or used.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　行为人及其近亲属或者其他相关人员，因行为人实施间谍行为从间谍组织及其代理人获取的所有利益，由国家安全机关依法采取追缴、没收等措施。

**Article 64** All benefits obtained by the actor and his close relatives or other related persons from the espionage organization and its agents as a result of the actor's commission of espionage shall be subject to measures such as recovery and confiscation taken by the State security authority according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家安全机关依法收缴的罚款以及没收的财物，一律上缴国库。

**Article 65** All fines collected and property confiscated by the State security authority according to law shall be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　境外人员违反本法的，国务院国家安全主管部门可以决定限期出境，并决定其不准入境的期限。未在规定期限内离境的，可以遣送出境。  
对违反本法的境外人员，国务院国家安全主管部门决定驱逐出境的，自被驱逐出境之日起十年内不准入境，国务院国家安全主管部门的处罚决定为最终决定。

**Article 66** Where a person outside the territory violates this Law, the State security department of the State Council may decide to order him to leave the territory within a specified time limit and decide the period during which he is not allowed to re-enter. If he fails to leave the territory within the specified time limit, he may be repatriated.  
For a person outside the territory who violates this Law, if the State security department of the State Council decides to deport him, he shall not be allowed to re-enter the territory within ten years from the date of his deportation, and the penalty decision of the State security department of the State Council shall be final.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家安全机关作出行政处罚决定之前，应当告知当事人拟作出的行政处罚内容及事实、理由、依据，以及当事人依法享有的陈述、申辩、要求听证等权利，并依照《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》的有关规定实施。

**Article 67** Before making a decision on administrative penalty, the State security authority shall inform the party of the content, facts, reasons, and basis of the proposed administrative penalty, as well as the rights that the party enjoys according to law to make statements, to defend, and to request a hearing, and shall implement it in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　当事人对行政处罚决定、行政强制措施决定、行政许可决定不服的，可以自收到决定书之日起六十日内，依法申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自收到复议决定书之日起十五日内，依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 68** If a party is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, a decision on administrative compulsion, or a decision on administrative license, he may, within sixty days from the date of receipt of the decision letter, apply for reconsideration according to law; if he is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, he may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision letter, bring a lawsuit to the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家安全机关工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者有非法拘禁、刑讯逼供、暴力取证、违反规定泄露国家秘密、工作秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私、个人信息等行为，依法予以处分，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** Where a staff member of the State security authority abuses his power, neglects his duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or commits acts such as illegal detention, extorting confessions by torture, collecting evidence by violence, or illegally disclosing State secrets, work secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or personal information in violation of regulations, he shall be given a sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国家安全机关依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，履行防范、制止和惩治间谍行为以外的危害国家安全行为的职责，适用本法的有关规定。  
公安机关在依法履行职责过程中发现、惩治危害国家安全的行为，适用本法的有关规定。

**Article 70** Where the State security authority performs the duty of preventing, stopping, and punishing acts endangering national security other than espionage in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant State provisions, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.  
Where a public security organ discovers and punishes acts endangering national security in the course of lawfully performing its duties, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本法自2023年7月1日起施行。

**Article 71** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2023.
