# 中华人民共和国国家勋章和国家荣誉称号法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2015-12-27  
**生效日期**：2016-01-01

> （2015年12月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议通过） 第一条　为了褒奖在中国特色社会主义建设中作出突出贡献的杰出人士，弘扬民族精神和时代精神，激发全国各族人民建设富强、民主、文明、和谐的社会主义现代化国家的积极性，实现中华民族伟大复兴，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号为国家最高荣誉。 国家勋章和国家荣誉称号的设立和授予，适用本法。 第三条　国家设立“共和国勋章”，授予在中国特色社会主义建设和保卫国家中作出巨大贡献、建立卓越功勋的杰出人士。 国家设立“友谊勋章”，授予在我国社会主义现代化建设和促进中外交流合作、维护世界和平中作出杰出贡献的外国人。 第四条　国家设立国家荣誉称号，授予在经济、社会、国防、外交、教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育等各领域各行业作出重大贡献、享有崇高声誉的杰出人士。 国家荣誉称号的名称冠以“人民”，也可以使用其他名称。国家荣誉称号的具体名称由全国人民代表大会常务委员会在决定授予时确定。 第五条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会委员长会议根据各方面的建议，向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的议案。 国务院、中央军事委员会可以向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的议案。 第六条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定授予国家勋章和国家荣誉称号。 第七条　中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，向国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号奖章，签发证书。 第八条　中华人民共和国主席进行国事活动，可以直接授予外国政要、国际友人等人士“友谊勋章”。 第九条　国家在国庆日或者其他重大节日、纪念日，举行颁授国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的仪式；必要时，也可以在其他时间举行颁授国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的仪式。 第十条　国家设立国家功勋簿，记载国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者及其功绩。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了褒奖在中国特色社会主义建设中作出突出贡献的杰出人士，弘扬民族精神和时代精神，激发全国各族人民建设富强、民主、文明、和谐的社会主义现代化国家的积极性，实现中华民族伟大复兴，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to commend outstanding persons who have made remarkable contributions to the building of socialism with Chinese characteristics, to promote the national spirit and the spirit of the times, to stimulate the initiative of the people of all ethnic groups in the country in building a modern socialist country that is prosperous, strong, democratic, culturally advanced, and harmonious, and to realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号为国家最高荣誉。  
国家勋章和国家荣誉称号的设立和授予，适用本法。

**Article 2** The national medals and national honorary titles shall be the highest honors of the State.  
The establishment and conferment of national medals and national honorary titles shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家设立“共和国勋章”，授予在中国特色社会主义建设和保卫国家中作出巨大贡献、建立卓越功勋的杰出人士。  
国家设立“友谊勋章”，授予在我国社会主义现代化建设和促进中外交流合作、维护世界和平中作出杰出贡献的外国人。

**Article 3** The State shall establish the "Medal of the Republic" to be conferred on outstanding persons who have made tremendous contributions and rendered outstanding services in the building and defense of the socialist cause with Chinese characteristics.  
The State shall establish the "Friendship Medal" to be conferred on foreigners who have made outstanding contributions to the socialist modernization of China, to promoting exchanges and cooperation between China and foreign countries, and to maintaining world peace.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家设立国家荣誉称号，授予在经济、社会、国防、外交、教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育等各领域各行业作出重大贡献、享有崇高声誉的杰出人士。  
国家荣誉称号的名称冠以“人民”，也可以使用其他名称。国家荣誉称号的具体名称由全国人民代表大会常务委员会在决定授予时确定。

**Article 4** The State shall establish national honorary titles to be conferred on outstanding persons who have made significant contributions and enjoy high reputations in various fields and sectors, such as economy, society, national defense, diplomacy, education, science and technology, culture, health, and sports.  
The name of a national honorary title shall be prefixed with "People's" or may use other names. The specific name of a national honorary title shall be determined by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress when it decides to confer it.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会委员长会议根据各方面的建议，向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的议案。  
国务院、中央军事委员会可以向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的议案。

**Article 5** The Council of Chairpersons of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall, based on suggestions from various sources, submit to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress bills on conferring national medals or national honorary titles.  
The State Council and the Central Military Commission may submit to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress bills on conferring national medals or national honorary titles.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定授予国家勋章和国家荣誉称号。

**Article 6** The Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall decide on the conferment of national medals and national honorary titles.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中华人民共和国主席根据全国人民代表大会常务委员会的决定，向国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号奖章，签发证书。

**Article 7** The President of the People's Republic of China shall, in accordance with the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, confer national medals or national honorary title medals on the recipients of national medals or national honorary titles and issue certificates.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中华人民共和国主席进行国事活动，可以直接授予外国政要、国际友人等人士“友谊勋章”。

**Article 8** The President of the People's Republic of China may, when carrying out state activities, directly confer the "Friendship Medal" on foreign political leaders, international friends, and other persons.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家在国庆日或者其他重大节日、纪念日，举行颁授国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的仪式；必要时，也可以在其他时间举行颁授国家勋章、国家荣誉称号的仪式。

**Article 9** The State shall hold ceremonies to confer national medals or national honorary titles on the National Day or other major festivals or commemorative days; if necessary, such ceremonies may also be held at other times.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家设立国家功勋簿，记载国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者及其功绩。

**Article 10** The State shall establish a national merit register to record the recipients of national medals and national honorary titles and their achievements.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者应当受到国家和社会的尊重，享有受邀参加国家庆典和其他重大活动等崇高礼遇和国家规定的待遇。

**Article 11** The recipients of national medals or national honorary titles shall be respected by the State and society, and shall be entitled to such high honors as being invited to participate in national celebrations and other major events, as well as the treatment prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家和社会通过多种形式，宣传国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者的卓越功绩和杰出事迹。

**Article 12** The State and society shall, through various forms, publicize the outstanding achievements and deeds of the recipients of national medals and national honorary titles.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号为其获得者终身享有，但依照本法规定被撤销的除外。

**Article 13** The national medals and national honorary titles shall be held for life by their recipients, unless revoked in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者应当按照规定佩带国家勋章、国家荣誉称号奖章，妥善保管勋章、奖章及证书。

**Article 14** The recipients of national medals or national honorary titles shall wear the national medals or national honorary title medals as required, and shall properly keep the medals, medals, and certificates.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者去世的，其获得的勋章、奖章及证书由其继承人或者指定的人保存；没有继承人或者被指定人的，可以由国家收存。  
国家勋章、国家荣誉称号奖章及证书不得出售、出租或者用于从事其他营利性活动。

**Article 15** Where a recipient of a national medal or national honorary title passes away, the medals, medals, and certificates obtained by him or her shall be kept by his or her successor or designated person; if there is no successor or designated person, they may be kept by the State.  
The national medals, national honorary title medals, and certificates shall not be sold, leased, or used for other profit-making activities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生前作出突出贡献符合本法规定授予国家勋章、国家荣誉称号条件的人士，本法施行后去世的，可以向其追授国家勋章、国家荣誉称号。

**Article 16** If a person who has made outstanding contributions during his or her lifetime and meets the conditions for conferring national medals or national honorary titles under this Law passes away after this Law comes into force, the national medals or national honorary titles may be conferred posthumously.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者，应当珍视并保持国家给予的荣誉，模范地遵守宪法和法律，努力为人民服务，自觉维护国家勋章和国家荣誉称号的声誉。

**Article 17** The recipients of national medals or national honorary titles shall cherish and maintain the honors granted by the State, exemplarily abide by the Constitution and laws, strive to serve the people, and consciously uphold the reputation of national medals and national honorary titles.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号获得者因犯罪被依法判处刑罚或者有其他严重违法、违纪等行为，继续享有国家勋章、国家荣誉称号将会严重损害国家最高荣誉的声誉的，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定撤销其国家勋章、国家荣誉称号并予以公告。

**Article 18** Where a recipient of a national medal or national honorary title is sentenced to criminal punishment according to law for committing a crime, or commits other serious illegal or disciplinary acts, and the continued enjoyment of the national medal or national honorary title will seriously damage the reputation of the highest national honors, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress shall decide to revoke the national medal or national honorary title and announce it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家勋章和国家荣誉称号的有关具体事项，由国家功勋荣誉表彰有关工作机构办理。

**Article 19** The specific matters concerning national medals and national honorary titles shall be handled by the relevant working bodies for national merit and honor recognition.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院、中央军事委员会可以在各自的职权范围内开展功勋荣誉表彰奖励工作。

**Article 20** The State Council and the Central Military Commission may carry out work on merit and honor recognition and awards within their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本法自2016年1月1日起施行。

**Article 21** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2016.
