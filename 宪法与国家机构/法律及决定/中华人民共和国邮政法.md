### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障邮政普遍服务，加强对邮政市场的监督管理，维护邮政通信与信息安全，保护通信自由和通信秘密，保护用户合法权益，促进邮政业健康发展，适应经济社会发展和人民生活需要，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of ensuring universal postal services, strengthening supervision and administration of the postal market, safeguarding postal communication and information security, protecting freedom and secrecy of correspondence, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of users, promoting the healthy development of the postal industry, and meeting the needs of economic and social development and the people's life.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家保障中华人民共和国境内的邮政普遍服务。  
邮政企业按照国家规定承担提供邮政普遍服务的义务。  
国务院和地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，支持邮政企业提供邮政普遍服务。  
本法所称邮政普遍服务，是指按照国家规定的业务范围、服务标准，以合理的资费标准，为中华人民共和国境内所有用户持续提供的邮政服务。

**Article 2** The State shall ensure universal postal services within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Postal enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, assume the obligation to provide universal postal services.  
The State Council and local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall take measures to support postal enterprises in providing universal postal services.  
For the purposes of this Law, universal postal services refer to postal services continuously provided to all users within the territory of the People's Republic of China in accordance with the business scope and service standards prescribed by the State and at reasonable tariff standards.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公民的通信自由和通信秘密受法律保护。除因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪的需要，由公安机关、国家安全机关或者检察机关依照法律规定的程序对通信进行检查外，任何组织或者个人不得以任何理由侵犯公民的通信自由和通信秘密。  
除法律另有规定外，任何组织或者个人不得检查、扣留邮件、汇款。

**Article 3** The freedom and secrecy of correspondence of citizens shall be protected by law. Except when it is necessary for the purpose of national security or investigation of criminal offenses, and the public security organ, the State security organ, or the procuratorial organ shall inspect correspondence in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law, no organization or individual may, for any reason, infringe upon the freedom and secrecy of correspondence of citizens.  
Unless otherwise provided by law, no organization or individual may inspect or detain mail or remittances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院邮政管理部门负责对全国的邮政普遍服务和邮政市场实施监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构负责对本行政区域的邮政普遍服务和邮政市场实施监督管理。  
按照国务院规定设立的省级以下邮政管理机构负责对本辖区的邮政普遍服务和邮政市场实施监督管理。  
国务院邮政管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构以及省级以下邮政管理机构（以下统称邮政管理部门）对邮政市场实施监督管理，应当遵循公开、公平、公正以及鼓励竞争、促进发展的原则。

**Article 4** The postal administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of universal postal services and the postal market nationwide.  
The postal administration authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of universal postal services and the postal market within their respective administrative regions.  
The postal administration authorities below the provincial level established in accordance with the provisions of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of universal postal services and the postal market within their respective jurisdictions.  
The postal administration department under the State Council, the postal administration authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the postal administration authorities below the provincial level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the postal administration departments) shall, in supervising and administering the postal market, follow the principles of openness, fairness, and justice, as well as encouraging competition and promoting development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院规定范围内的信件寄递业务，由邮政企业专营。

**Article 5** The delivery of letters within the scope prescribed by the State Council shall be operated exclusively by postal enterprises.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　邮政企业应当加强服务质量管理，完善安全保障措施，为用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便的服务。

**Article 6** Postal enterprises shall strengthen service quality management, improve safety and security measures, and provide users with prompt, accurate, safe, and convenient services.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　邮政管理部门、公安机关、国家安全机关和海关应当相互配合，建立健全安全保障机制，加强对邮政通信与信息安全的监督管理，确保邮政通信与信息安全。

**Article 7** The postal administration departments, the public security organs, the State security organs, and the customs shall cooperate with each other, establish and improve safety and security mechanisms, and strengthen the supervision and administration of postal communication and information security to ensure the security of postal communication and information.

### 第二章　邮政设施

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　邮政设施的布局和建设应当满足保障邮政普遍服务的需要。  
地方各级人民政府应当将邮政设施的布局和建设纳入城乡规划，对提供邮政普遍服务的邮政设施的建设给予支持，重点扶持农村边远地区邮政设施的建设。  
建设城市新区、独立工矿区、开发区、住宅区或者对旧城区进行改建，应当同时建设配套的提供邮政普遍服务的邮政设施。  
提供邮政普遍服务的邮政设施等组成的邮政网络是国家重要的通信基础设施。

**Article 8** The layout and construction of postal facilities shall meet the needs of ensuring universal postal services.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall incorporate the layout and construction of postal facilities into urban and rural planning, provide support for the construction of postal facilities for providing universal postal services, and give priority support to the construction of postal facilities in remote rural areas.  
When constructing new urban districts, independent industrial and mining areas, development zones, or residential areas, or when reconstructing old urban areas, supporting postal facilities for providing universal postal services shall be constructed simultaneously.  
The postal network composed of postal facilities for providing universal postal services, among others, is an important communication infrastructure of the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　邮政设施应当按照国家规定的标准设置。  
较大的车站、机场、港口、高等院校和宾馆应当设置提供邮政普遍服务的邮政营业场所。  
邮政企业设置、撤销邮政营业场所，应当事先书面告知邮政管理部门；撤销提供邮政普遍服务的邮政营业场所，应当经邮政管理部门批准并予以公告。

**Article 9** Postal facilities shall be installed in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State.  
Major railway stations, airports, ports, institutions of higher learning, and hotels shall install postal business premises for providing universal postal services.  
When a postal enterprise installs or removes a postal business premise, it shall inform the postal administration department in writing in advance; where it removes a postal business premise for providing universal postal services, it shall obtain approval from the postal administration department and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　机关、企业事业单位应当设置接收邮件的场所。农村地区应当逐步设置村邮站或者其他接收邮件的场所。  
建设城镇居民楼应当设置接收邮件的信报箱，并按照国家规定的标准验收。建设单位未按照国家规定的标准设置信报箱的，由邮政管理部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，由邮政管理部门指定其他单位设置信报箱，所需费用由该居民楼的建设单位承担。

**Article 10** Government organs, enterprises, and institutions shall install premises for receiving mail. In rural areas, village postal stations or other premises for receiving mail shall be gradually installed.  
When constructing urban residential buildings, mailboxes for receiving mail shall be installed and accepted in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State. Where the construction entity fails to install mailboxes in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, the postal administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the postal administration department shall designate another entity to install the mailboxes, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the construction entity of the residential building.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　邮件处理场所的设计和建设，应当符合国家安全机关和海关依法履行职责的要求。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/快递暂行条例.md#art-48" title="被《快递暂行条例》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** The design and construction of mail processing premises shall meet the requirements for the State security organs and the customs to perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　征收邮政营业场所或者邮件处理场所的，城乡规划主管部门应当根据保障邮政普遍服务的要求，对邮政营业场所或者邮件处理场所的重新设置作出妥善安排；未作出妥善安排前，不得征收。  
邮政营业场所或者邮件处理场所重新设置前，邮政企业应当采取措施，保证邮政普遍服务的正常进行。

**Article 12** Where a postal business premise or a mail processing premise is to be expropriated, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall, in accordance with the requirements for ensuring universal postal services, make proper arrangements for the reinstallation of the postal business premise or mail processing premise; no expropriation shall be carried out before proper arrangements have been made.  
Before the postal business premise or mail processing premise is reinstalled, the postal enterprise shall take measures to ensure the normal provision of universal postal services.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　邮政企业应当对其设置的邮政设施进行经常性维护，保证邮政设施的正常使用。  
任何单位和个人不得损毁邮政设施或者影响邮政设施的正常使用。

**Article 13** Postal enterprises shall regularly maintain the postal facilities installed by them to ensure the normal use thereof.  
No unit or individual may damage postal facilities or affect the normal use thereof.

### 第三章　邮政服务

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　邮政企业经营下列业务：  
（一）邮件寄递；  
（二）邮政汇兑、邮政储蓄；  
（三）邮票发行以及集邮票品制作、销售；  
（四）国内报刊、图书等出版物发行；  
（五）国家规定的其他业务。

**Article 14** Postal enterprises shall operate the following businesses:  
(1) delivery of mail;  
(2) postal remittance and postal savings;  
(3) issuance of postage stamps, and preparation and sale of philatelic items;  
(4) distribution of domestic newspapers, periodicals, books, and other publications; and  
(5) other businesses as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　邮政企业应当对信件、单件重量不超过五千克的印刷品、单件重量不超过十千克的包裹的寄递以及邮政汇兑提供邮政普遍服务。  
邮政企业按照国家规定办理机要通信、国家规定报刊的发行，以及义务兵平常信函、盲人读物和革命烈士遗物的免费寄递等特殊服务业务。  
未经邮政管理部门批准，邮政企业不得停止办理或者限制办理前两款规定的业务；因不可抗力或者其他特殊原因暂时停止办理或者限制办理的，邮政企业应当及时公告，采取相应的补救措施，并向邮政管理部门报告。  
邮政普遍服务标准，由国务院邮政管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定；邮政普遍服务监督管理的具体办法，由国务院邮政管理部门制定。

**Article 15** Postal enterprises shall provide universal postal services for the delivery of letters, printed matters with a single piece weight not exceeding five kilograms, parcels with a single piece weight not exceeding ten kilograms, and postal remittance.  
Postal enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, handle special services such as confidential communications, distribution of newspapers and periodicals prescribed by the State, and free delivery of ordinary letters of conscripts, reading materials for the blind, and relics of revolutionary martyrs.  
Without the approval of the postal administration department, a postal enterprise may not cease or restrict the handling of the services specified in the preceding two paragraphs; where it temporarily ceases or restricts such services due to force majeure or other special reasons, it shall promptly make a public announcement, take corresponding remedial measures, and report to the postal administration department.  
The standards for universal postal services shall be formulated by the postal administration department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council; the specific measures for the supervision and administration of universal postal services shall be formulated by the postal administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对邮政企业提供邮政普遍服务、特殊服务给予补贴，并加强对补贴资金使用的监督。

**Article 16** The State shall grant subsidies to postal enterprises for providing universal postal services and special services, and shall strengthen supervision over the use of subsidy funds.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家设立邮政普遍服务基金。邮政普遍服务基金征收、使用和监督管理的具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准后公布施行。

**Article 17** The State shall establish a universal postal service fund. The specific measures for the levy, use, and supervision and administration of the universal postal service fund shall be formulated by the finance department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before being promulgated and implemented.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　邮政企业的邮政普遍服务业务与竞争性业务应当分业经营。

**Article 18** The universal postal service business and competitive business of a postal enterprise shall be operated separately.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　邮政企业在城市每周的营业时间应当不少于六天，投递邮件每天至少一次；在乡、镇人民政府所在地每周的营业时间应当不少于五天，投递邮件每周至少五次。  
邮政企业在交通不便的边远地区和乡、镇其他地区每周的营业时间以及投递邮件的频次，国务院邮政管理部门可以另行规定。

**Article 19** Postal enterprises shall have business hours of not less than six days per week in urban areas, and shall deliver mail at least once a day; in the seat of the people's government of townships and towns, the business hours shall be not less than five days per week, and mail shall be delivered at least five times per week.  
The postal administration department under the State Council may separately prescribe the weekly business hours and mail delivery frequency of postal enterprises in remote areas with inconvenient transportation and other areas of townships and towns.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　邮政企业寄递邮件，应当符合国务院邮政管理部门规定的寄递时限和服务规范。

**Article 20** When delivering mail, postal enterprises shall comply with the delivery time limits and service specifications prescribed by the postal administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　邮政企业应当在其营业场所公示或者以其他方式公布其服务种类、营业时间、资费标准、邮件和汇款的查询及损失赔偿办法以及用户对其服务质量的投诉办法。

**Article 21** A postal enterprise shall display at its business premises or otherwise publish the types of services, business hours, tariff standards, methods for inquiry and compensation for loss of mail and remittance, as well as the methods for users to lodge complaints about the quality of its services.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　邮政企业采用其提供的格式条款确定与用户的权利义务的，该格式条款适用《中华人民共和国合同法》关于合同格式条款的规定。

**Article 22** Where a postal enterprise adopts the standard clauses it provides to determine the rights and obligations with users, such standard clauses shall be subject to the provisions of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China on standard clauses of contracts.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　用户交寄邮件，应当清楚、准确地填写收件人姓名、地址和邮政编码。邮政企业应当在邮政营业场所免费为用户提供邮政编码查询服务。  
邮政编码由邮政企业根据国务院邮政管理部门制定的编制规则编制。邮政管理部门依法对邮政编码的编制和使用实施监督。

**Article 23** A user that sends mail shall clearly and accurately fill in the name, address and postcode of the addressee. A postal enterprise shall provide free of charge postcode inquiry services to users at postal business premises.  
Postcodes shall be compiled by postal enterprises in accordance with the compilation rules formulated by the postal administrative department of the State Council. The postal administrative department shall, according to law, supervise the compilation and use of postcodes.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　邮政企业收寄邮件和用户交寄邮件，应当遵守法律、行政法规以及国务院和国务院有关部门关于禁止寄递或者限制寄递物品的规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/快递暂行条例.md#art-31" title="被《快递暂行条例》第31条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** A postal enterprise that receives mail and a user that sends mail shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council and relevant departments under the State Council on articles prohibited or restricted from being sent through the post.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　邮政企业应当依法建立并执行邮件收寄验视制度。  
对用户交寄的信件，必要时邮政企业可以要求用户开拆，进行验视，但不得检查信件内容。用户拒绝开拆的，邮政企业不予收寄。  
对信件以外的邮件，邮政企业收寄时应当当场验视内件。用户拒绝验视的，邮政企业不予收寄。

**Article 25** A postal enterprise shall, according to law, establish and implement the system of inspecting mail upon acceptance.  
For letters sent by users, a postal enterprise may, when necessary, require the user to open the letter for inspection, but shall not examine the content of the letter. If the user refuses to open the letter, the postal enterprise shall not accept it.  
For mail other than letters, a postal enterprise shall inspect the contents on the spot when accepting them. If the user refuses the inspection, the postal enterprise shall not accept the mail.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　邮政企业发现邮件内夹带禁止寄递或者限制寄递的物品的，应当按照国家有关规定处理。  
进出境邮件中夹带国家禁止进出境或者限制进出境的物品的，由海关依法处理。

**Article 26** Where a postal enterprise discovers that mail contains articles prohibited or restricted from being sent through the post, it shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where incoming or outgoing mail contains articles prohibited or restricted from being imported or exported by the State, the matter shall be handled by the Customs according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对提供邮政普遍服务的邮政企业交运的邮件，铁路、公路、水路、航空等运输企业应当优先安排运输，车站、港口、机场应当安排装卸场所和出入通道。

**Article 27** For mail delivered by a postal enterprise providing universal postal services, transportation enterprises such as railway, highway, waterway, and aviation shall give priority to the arrangement of transportation, and stations, ports, and airports shall arrange loading and unloading areas and access passages.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　带有邮政专用标志的车船进出港口、通过渡口时，应当优先放行。  
带有邮政专用标志的车辆运递邮件，确需通过公安机关交通管理部门划定的禁行路段或者确需在禁止停车的地点停车的，经公安机关交通管理部门同意，在确保安全的前提下，可以通行或者停车。  
邮政企业不得利用带有邮政专用标志的车船从事邮件运递以外的经营性活动，不得以出租等方式允许其他单位或者个人使用带有邮政专用标志的车船。

**Article 28** Vehicles and vessels bearing the special postal insignia shall be given priority when entering or leaving ports or passing through ferries.  
Where a vehicle bearing the special postal insignia transporting mail indeed needs to pass through a road section designated as a no-entry zone by the traffic control department of the public security organ or indeed needs to park at a place where parking is prohibited, it may, with the consent of the traffic control department of the public security organ and subject to safety assurance, pass through or park.  
A postal enterprise shall not use vehicles or vessels bearing the special postal insignia for business activities other than mail transportation, nor shall it allow, by way of lease or otherwise, any other unit or individual to use vehicles or vessels bearing the special postal insignia.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　邮件通过海上运输时，不参与分摊共同海损。

**Article 29** When mail is transported by sea, it shall not contribute to the general average.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　海关依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的规定，对进出境的国际邮袋、邮件集装箱和国际邮递物品实施监管。

**Article 30** The Customs shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, exercise supervision and control over international mail bags, mail containers, and international postal items entering or leaving the country.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　进出境邮件的检疫，由进出境检验检疫机构依法实施。

**Article 31** Quarantine of incoming and outgoing mail shall be carried out by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　邮政企业采取按址投递、用户领取或者与用户协商的其他方式投递邮件。  
机关、企业事业单位、住宅小区管理单位等应当为邮政企业投递邮件提供便利。单位用户地址变更的，应当及时通知邮政企业。

**Article 32** A postal enterprise shall deliver mail by delivery to the address, collection by the user, or other means agreed with the user.  
Government organs, enterprises, public institutions, and residential community management units shall provide facilities for postal enterprises to deliver mail. Where the address of a unit user is changed, it shall promptly notify the postal enterprise.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　邮政企业对无法投递的邮件，应当退回寄件人。  
无法投递又无法退回的信件，自邮政企业确认无法退回之日起超过六个月无人认领的，由邮政企业在邮政管理部门的监督下销毁。无法投递又无法退回的其他邮件，按照国务院邮政管理部门的规定处理；其中无法投递又无法退回的进境国际邮递物品，由海关依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的规定处理。

**Article 33** A postal enterprise shall return undeliverable mail to the sender.  
For letters that are undeliverable and cannot be returned, if no one claims them within more than six months from the date the postal enterprise confirms that they cannot be returned, the postal enterprise shall destroy them under the supervision of the postal administrative department. For other mail that is undeliverable and cannot be returned, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the postal administrative department of the State Council; for incoming international postal items that are undeliverable and cannot be returned, the Customs shall handle them in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　邮政汇款的收款人应当自收到汇款通知之日起六十日内，凭有效身份证件到邮政企业兑领汇款。  
收款人逾期未兑领的汇款，由邮政企业退回汇款人。自兑领汇款期限届满之日起一年内无法退回汇款人，或者汇款人自收到退汇通知之日起一年内未领取的汇款，由邮政企业上缴国库。

**Article 34** The payee of a postal remittance shall, within sixty days from the date of receipt of the remittance notice, present a valid identity document at the postal enterprise to cash the remittance.  
If the payee fails to cash the remittance within the time limit, the postal enterprise shall return the remittance to the remitter. If the remittance cannot be returned to the remitter within one year from the expiry of the time limit for cashing, or if the remitter fails to collect the remittance within one year from the date of receipt of the notice of return, the postal enterprise shall turn it over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　任何单位和个人不得私自开拆、隐匿、毁弃他人邮件。  
除法律另有规定外，邮政企业及其从业人员不得向任何单位或者个人泄露用户使用邮政服务的信息。

**Article 35** No unit or individual may privately open, conceal, destroy, or discard the mail of another person.  
Unless otherwise provided by law, a postal enterprise and its employees shall not disclose to any unit or individual information on the use of postal services by users.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪的需要，公安机关、国家安全机关或者检察机关可以依法检查、扣留有关邮件，并可以要求邮政企业提供相关用户使用邮政服务的信息。邮政企业和有关单位应当配合，并对有关情况予以保密。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/中华人民共和国邮政法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国邮政法实施细则》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/综合司法解释/中华人民共和国邮政法实施细则.md#art-61" title="被《中华人民共和国邮政法实施细则》第61条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 36** Where necessary for national security or for the investigation of criminal offenses, the public security organs, state security organs, or procuratorial organs may, according to law, inspect and detain relevant mail, and may require the postal enterprise to provide information on the use of postal services by relevant users. The postal enterprise and relevant units shall cooperate and shall keep such information confidential.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得利用邮件寄递含有下列内容的物品：  
（一）煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度或者分裂国家、破坏国家统一，危害国家安全的；  
（二）泄露国家秘密的；  
（三）散布谣言扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
（四）煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结的；  
（五）宣扬邪教或者迷信的；  
（六）散布淫秽、赌博、恐怖信息或者教唆犯罪的；  
（七）法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may send through the mail articles containing the following content:  
(1) inciting subversion of the State power, overthrow of the socialist system, or division of the country, undermining national unity, or endangering national security;  
(2) divulging State secrets;  
(3) spreading rumors to disturb social order or undermine social stability;  
(4) inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, or undermining ethnic unity;  
(5) promoting cults or superstition;  
(6) spreading obscene, gambling, or terrorist information, or instigating crimes; or  
(7) other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　任何单位和个人不得有下列行为：  
（一）扰乱邮政营业场所正常秩序；  
（二）阻碍邮政企业从业人员投递邮件；  
（三）非法拦截、强登、扒乘带有邮政专用标志的车辆；  
（四）冒用邮政企业名义或者邮政专用标志；  
（五）伪造邮政专用品或者倒卖伪造的邮政专用品。

**Article 38** No unit or individual may engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) disrupting the normal order of postal business premises;  
(2) obstructing the delivery of mail by employees of a postal enterprise;  
(3) illegally intercepting, forcibly boarding, or clinging to vehicles bearing the special postal insignia;  
(4) fraudulently using the name of a postal enterprise or the special postal insignia; or  
(5) forging special postal supplies or trafficking in forged special postal supplies.

### 第四章　邮政资费

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　实行政府指导价或者政府定价的邮政业务范围，以中央政府定价目录为依据，具体资费标准由国务院价格主管部门会同国务院财政部门、国务院邮政管理部门制定。  
邮政企业的其他业务资费实行市场调节价，资费标准由邮政企业自主确定。

**Article 39** The scope of postal services subject to government-guided pricing or government-set pricing shall be based on the central government pricing catalogue, and the specific tariff standards shall be formulated by the pricing department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council and the postal administrative department of the State Council.  
Tariffs for other services of a postal enterprise shall be subject to market-regulated pricing, and the tariff standards shall be independently determined by the postal enterprise.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院有关部门制定邮政业务资费标准，应当听取邮政企业、用户和其他有关方面的意见。  
邮政企业应当根据国务院价格主管部门、国务院财政部门和国务院邮政管理部门的要求，提供准确、完备的业务成本数据和其他有关资料。

**Article 40** When formulating tariff standards for postal services, the relevant departments of the State Council shall solicit opinions from postal enterprises, users, and other relevant parties.  
A postal enterprise shall, in accordance with the requirements of the pricing department of the State Council, the finance department of the State Council, and the postal administrative department of the State Council, provide accurate and complete business cost data and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　邮件资费的交付，以邮资凭证、证明邮资已付的戳记以及有关业务单据等表示。  
邮资凭证包括邮票、邮资符志、邮资信封、邮资明信片、邮资邮简、邮资信卡等。  
任何单位和个人不得伪造邮资凭证或者倒卖伪造的邮资凭证，不得擅自仿印邮票和邮资图案。

**Article 41** The payment of postage for mail items shall be indicated by postage certificates, postage-paid stamps, and relevant business documents, among others.  
Postage certificates include stamps, postage franking marks, stamped envelopes, stamped postcards, stamped letter sheets, stamped greeting cards, etc.  
No entity or individual may forge postage certificates or sell forged postage certificates, nor may they reproduce stamps or postage patterns without authorization.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　普通邮票发行数量由邮政企业按照市场需要确定，报国务院邮政管理部门备案；纪念邮票和特种邮票发行计划由邮政企业根据市场需要提出，报国务院邮政管理部门审定。国务院邮政管理部门负责纪念邮票的选题和图案审查。  
邮政管理部门依法对邮票的印制、销售实施监督。

**Article 42** The quantity of ordinary stamps to be issued shall be determined by the postal enterprise based on market demand and shall be reported to the postal administration department of the State Council for record; the issuance plans for commemorative stamps and special stamps shall be proposed by the postal enterprise according to market demand and shall be submitted to the postal administration department of the State Council for approval. The postal administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for the selection of themes and review of designs for commemorative stamps.  
The postal administration department shall supervise the printing and sale of stamps according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　邮资凭证售出后，邮资凭证持有人不得要求邮政企业兑换现金。  
停止使用邮资凭证，应当经国务院邮政管理部门批准，并在停止使用九十日前予以公告，停止销售。邮资凭证持有人可以自公告之日起一年内，向邮政企业换取等值的邮资凭证。

**Article 43** After postage certificates are sold, the holder thereof may not demand that the postal enterprise exchange them for cash.  
Discontinuation of the use of postage certificates shall be subject to approval by the postal administration department of the State Council, and shall be announced and sales shall be stopped ninety days before such discontinuation. The holder of postage certificates may, within one year from the date of the announcement, exchange them with the postal enterprise for postage certificates of equivalent value.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　下列邮资凭证不得使用：  
（一）经国务院邮政管理部门批准停止使用的；  
（二）盖销或者划销的；  
（三）污损、残缺或者褪色、变色，难以辨认的。  
从邮资信封、邮资明信片、邮资邮简、邮资信卡上剪下的邮资图案，不得作为邮资凭证使用。

**Article 44** The following postage certificates may not be used:  
(1) Those whose use has been discontinued upon approval by the postal administration department of the State Council;  
(2) Those that have been cancelled or obliterated;  
(3) Those that are soiled, damaged, faded, or discolored to the extent that they are difficult to identify.  
Postage patterns cut from stamped envelopes, stamped postcards, stamped letter sheets, or stamped greeting cards may not be used as postage certificates.

### 第五章　损失赔偿

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　邮政普遍服务业务范围内的邮件和汇款的损失赔偿，适用本章规定。  
邮政普遍服务业务范围以外的邮件的损失赔偿，适用有关民事法律的规定。  
邮件的损失，是指邮件丢失、损毁或者内件短少。

**Article 45** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to compensation for loss of mail items and remittances within the scope of universal postal services.  
Compensation for loss of mail items beyond the scope of universal postal services shall be governed by the provisions of relevant civil laws.  
Loss of a mail item refers to the loss, damage, or shortage of contents of the mail item.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　邮政企业对平常邮件的损失不承担赔偿责任。但是，邮政企业因故意或者重大过失造成平常邮件损失的除外。

**Article 46** The postal enterprise shall not bear compensation liability for loss of ordinary mail items, unless the loss is caused by the intentional act or gross negligence of the postal enterprise.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　邮政企业对给据邮件的损失依照下列规定赔偿：  
（一）保价的给据邮件丢失或者全部损毁的，按照保价额赔偿；部分损毁或者内件短少的，按照保价额与邮件全部价值的比例对邮件的实际损失予以赔偿。  
（二）未保价的给据邮件丢失、损毁或者内件短少的，按照实际损失赔偿，但最高赔偿额不超过所收取资费的三倍；挂号信件丢失、损毁的，按照所收取资费的三倍予以赔偿。  
邮政企业应当在营业场所的告示中和提供给用户的给据邮件单据上，以足以引起用户注意的方式载明前款规定。  
邮政企业因故意或者重大过失造成给据邮件损失，或者未履行前款规定义务的，无权援用本条第一款的规定限制赔偿责任。

**Article 47** The postal enterprise shall compensate for loss of registered mail items in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If a registered mail item with declared value is lost or completely damaged, compensation shall be made according to the declared value; if it is partially damaged or its contents are short, compensation shall be made for the actual loss in proportion to the ratio between the declared value and the total value of the mail item.  
(2) If a registered mail item without declared value is lost, damaged, or its contents are short, compensation shall be made according to the actual loss, but the maximum compensation shall not exceed three times the postage collected; if a registered letter is lost or damaged, compensation shall be made at three times the postage collected.  
The postal enterprise shall set forth the provisions of the preceding paragraph in notices at its business premises and on the documents for registered mail items provided to users, in a manner sufficient to draw the user's attention.  
If the loss of a registered mail item is caused by the intentional act or gross negligence of the postal enterprise, or if the postal enterprise fails to perform the obligation under the preceding paragraph, it shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article to limit its compensation liability.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　因下列原因之一造成的给据邮件损失，邮政企业不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不可抗力，但因不可抗力造成的保价的给据邮件的损失除外；  
（二）所寄物品本身的自然性质或者合理损耗；  
（三）寄件人、收件人的过错。

**Article 48** The postal enterprise shall not bear compensation liability for loss of a registered mail item caused by any of the following reasons:  
(1) Force majeure, except for loss of a registered mail item with declared value caused by force majeure;  
(2) The natural nature or reasonable loss of the item sent;  
(3) Fault of the sender or the recipient.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　用户交寄给据邮件后，对国内邮件可以自交寄之日起一年内持收据向邮政企业查询，对国际邮件可以自交寄之日起一百八十日内持收据向邮政企业查询。  
查询国际邮件或者查询国务院邮政管理部门规定的边远地区的邮件的，邮政企业应当自用户查询之日起六十日内将查询结果告知用户；查询其他邮件的，邮政企业应当自用户查询之日起三十日内将查询结果告知用户。查复期满未查到邮件的，邮政企业应当依照[本法第四十七条](#art-47)的规定予以赔偿。  
用户在本条第一款规定的查询期限内未向邮政企业查询又未提出赔偿要求的，邮政企业不再承担赔偿责任。

**Article 49** After a user sends a registered mail item, the user may, within one year from the date of sending for domestic mail items, or within 180 days from the date of sending for international mail items, inquire with the postal enterprise by presenting the receipt.  
If the inquiry concerns an international mail item or a mail item in a remote area designated by the postal administration department of the State Council, the postal enterprise shall inform the user of the result within 60 days from the date of the user's inquiry; for inquiries concerning other mail items, the postal enterprise shall inform the user of the result within 30 days from the date of the user's inquiry. If the mail item is not found upon the expiration of the inquiry period, the postal enterprise shall make compensation in accordance with Article 47 of this Law.  
If the user fails to make an inquiry or submit a claim for compensation within the inquiry period specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the postal enterprise shall no longer bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　邮政汇款的汇款人自汇款之日起一年内，可以持收据向邮政企业查询。邮政企业应当自用户查询之日起二十日内将查询结果告知汇款人。查复期满未查到汇款的，邮政企业应当向汇款人退还汇款和汇款费用。

**Article 50** The remitter of a postal remittance may, within one year from the date of remittance, make an inquiry with the postal enterprise by presenting the receipt. The postal enterprise shall inform the remitter of the result within 20 days from the date of the user's inquiry. If the remittance is not found upon the expiration of the inquiry period, the postal enterprise shall refund the remittance and the remittance fee to the remitter.

### 第六章　快递业务

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　经营快递业务，应当依照本法规定取得快递业务经营许可；未经许可，任何单位和个人不得经营快递业务。  
外商不得投资经营信件的国内快递业务。  
国内快递业务，是指从收寄到投递的全过程均发生在中华人民共和国境内的快递业务。

**Article 51** To operate express delivery services, an entity shall obtain an express delivery service business permit in accordance with the provisions of this Law; without such a permit, no entity or individual may operate express delivery services.  
No foreign investor may invest in or operate domestic express delivery services for letters.  
Domestic express delivery services refer to express delivery services the entire process of which, from collection to delivery, takes place within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　申请快递业务经营许可，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）符合企业法人条件；  
（二）在省、自治区、直辖市范围内经营的，注册资本不低于人民币五十万元，跨省、自治区、直辖市经营的，注册资本不低于人民币一百万元，经营国际快递业务的，注册资本不低于人民币二百万元；  
（三）有与申请经营的地域范围相适应的服务能力；  
（四）有严格的服务质量管理制度和完备的业务操作规范；  
（五）有健全的安全保障制度和措施；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/快递暂行条例.md#art-18" title="被《快递暂行条例》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 52** To apply for an express delivery service business permit, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Meeting the requirements for an enterprise legal person;  
(2) For operations within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the registered capital shall be no less than 500,000 RMB; for operations across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the registered capital shall be no less than 1,000,000 RMB; for operating international express delivery services, the registered capital shall be no less than 2,000,000 RMB;  
(3) Having service capabilities commensurate with the geographical scope of the applied operation;  
(4) Having a strict service quality management system and complete operational procedures;  
(5) Having sound security guarantee systems and measures;  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　申请快递业务经营许可，在省、自治区、直辖市范围内经营的，应当向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构提出申请，跨省、自治区、直辖市经营或者经营国际快递业务的，应当向国务院邮政管理部门提出申请；申请时应当提交申请书和有关申请材料。  
受理申请的邮政管理部门应当自受理申请之日起四十五日内进行审查，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发快递业务经营许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
邮政管理部门审查快递业务经营许可的申请，应当考虑国家安全等因素，并征求有关部门的意见。  
申请人凭快递业务经营许可证向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记后，方可经营快递业务。

**Article 53** To apply for an express delivery service business permit, if the operation is within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the application shall be filed with the local postal administrative body of the province, autonomous region, or municipality; if the operation is across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, or involves international express delivery services, the application shall be filed with the postal administration department of the State Council. When applying, a written application and relevant supporting materials shall be submitted.  
The postal administration department that accepts the application shall conduct a review within 45 days from the date of acceptance and make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approved, it shall issue an express delivery service business permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
When reviewing an application for an express delivery service business permit, the postal administration department shall consider factors such as national security and consult with relevant departments.  
The applicant may operate express delivery services only after registering with the industry and commerce administration department according to law on the strength of the express delivery service business permit.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　邮政企业以外的经营快递业务的企业（以下称快递企业）设立分支机构或者合并、分立的，应当向邮政管理部门备案。

**Article 54** If an enterprise operating express delivery services other than a postal enterprise (hereinafter referred to as an express delivery enterprise) establishes a branch or undergoes merger or division, it shall report to the postal administration department for record.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　快递企业不得经营由邮政企业专营的信件寄递业务，不得寄递国家机关公文。

**Article 55** An express delivery enterprise may not operate the delivery of letters that are exclusively operated by the postal enterprise, nor may it deliver official documents of state organs.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　快递企业经营邮政企业专营业务范围以外的信件快递业务，应当在信件封套的显著位置标注信件字样。  
快递企业不得将信件打包后作为包裹寄递。

**Article 56** If an express delivery enterprise operates express delivery services for letters beyond the scope of the exclusive business of the postal enterprise, it shall mark the word "letter" in a prominent position on the envelope of such letters.  
An express delivery enterprise may not bundle letters together and deliver them as parcels.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　经营国际快递业务应当接受邮政管理部门和有关部门依法实施的监管。邮政管理部门和有关部门可以要求经营国际快递业务的企业提供报关数据。

**Article 57** Operating international express delivery services shall be subject to the supervision implemented by the postal administration department and relevant departments according to law. The postal administration department and relevant departments may require an enterprise operating international express delivery services to provide customs declaration data.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　快递企业停止经营快递业务的，应当书面告知邮政管理部门，交回快递业务经营许可证，并对尚未投递的快件按照国务院邮政管理部门的规定妥善处理。

**Article 58** If an express delivery enterprise ceases to operate express delivery services, it shall notify the postal administration department in writing, return the express delivery service business permit, and properly handle the undelivered express items in accordance with the provisions of the postal administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　[本法第六条](#art-6)、第二十一条、第二十二条、第二十四条、第二十五条、第二十六条第一款、第三十五条第二款、第三十六条关于邮政企业及其从业人员的规定，适用于快递企业及其从业人员；第十一条关于邮件处理场所的规定，适用于快件处理场所；第三条第二款、第二十六条第二款、第三十五条第一款、第三十六条、第三十七条关于邮件的规定，适用于快件；第四十五条第二款关于邮件的损失赔偿的规定，适用于快件的损失赔偿。

**Article 59** The provisions of Articles 6, 21, 22, 24, 25, paragraph 1 of Article 26, paragraph 2 of Article 35, and Article 36 of this Law concerning the postal enterprise and its employees shall apply to express delivery enterprises and their employees; the provisions of Article 11 concerning mail processing sites shall apply to express item processing sites; the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 3, paragraph 2 of Article 26, paragraph 1 of Article 35, Article 36, and Article 37 concerning mail items shall apply to express items; the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 45 concerning compensation for loss of mail items shall apply to compensation for loss of express items.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　经营快递业务的企业依法成立的行业协会，依照法律、行政法规及其章程规定，制定快递行业规范，加强行业自律，为企业提供信息、培训等方面的服务，促进快递行业的健康发展。  
经营快递业务的企业应当对其从业人员加强法制教育、职业道德教育和业务技能培训。

**Article 60** An industry association formed according to law by enterprises operating express delivery services shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and its articles of association, formulate express delivery industry standards, strengthen industry self-discipline, and provide services such as information and training for enterprises, so as to promote the healthy development of the express delivery industry.  
Enterprises operating express delivery services shall strengthen legal education, professional ethics education, and professional skills training for their employees.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　邮政管理部门依法履行监督管理职责，可以采取下列监督检查措施：  
（一）进入邮政企业、快递企业或者涉嫌发生违反本法活动的其他场所实施现场检查；  
（二）向有关单位和个人了解情况；  
（三）查阅、复制有关文件、资料、凭证；  
（四）经邮政管理部门负责人批准，查封与违反本法活动有关的场所，扣押用于违反本法活动的运输工具以及相关物品，对信件以外的涉嫌夹带禁止寄递或者限制寄递物品的邮件、快件开拆检查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../宪法与国家机构/行政法规/快递暂行条例.md#art-48" title="被《快递暂行条例》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 61** When performing supervision and administration duties according to law, the postal regulatory authority may adopt the following supervision and inspection measures:  
(1) Entering postal enterprises, express enterprises, or other places suspected of engaging in activities violating this Law to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Inquiring about the circumstances with relevant entities and individuals;  
(3) Inspecting and copying relevant documents, materials, and vouchers;  
(4) With the approval of the head of the postal regulatory authority, sealing up places related to activities violating this Law, detaining means of transport and related articles used for activities violating this Law, and opening and inspecting mail items and express items other than letters that are suspected of containing articles prohibited or restricted for posting.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　邮政管理部门根据履行监督管理职责的需要，可以要求邮政企业和快递企业报告有关经营情况。

**Article 62** The postal regulatory authority may, as necessary for performing its supervision and administration duties, require postal enterprises and express enterprises to report relevant business information.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　邮政管理部门进行监督检查时，监督检查人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件。对邮政管理部门依法进行的监督检查，有关单位和个人应当配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 63** When the postal regulatory authority conducts supervision and inspection, there shall be at least two inspectors, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials. Relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted by the postal regulatory authority according to law, and shall not refuse or obstruct it.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　邮政管理部门工作人员对监督检查中知悉的商业秘密，负有保密义务。

**Article 64** Staff members of the postal regulatory authority shall have the obligation to keep confidential any trade secrets learned in the course of supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　邮政企业和快递企业应当及时、妥善处理用户对服务质量提出的异议。用户对处理结果不满意的，可以向邮政管理部门申诉，邮政管理部门应当及时依法处理，并自接到申诉之日起三十日内作出答复。

**Article 65** Postal enterprises and express enterprises shall timely and properly handle users' complaints regarding service quality. If a user is dissatisfied with the handling result, the user may file a petition with the postal regulatory authority, and the postal regulatory authority shall handle it promptly according to law and render a reply within 30 days from the date of receiving the petition.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　任何单位和个人对违反本法规定的行为，有权向邮政管理部门举报。邮政管理部门接到举报后，应当及时依法处理。

**Article 66** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any act violating the provisions of this Law to the postal regulatory authority. Upon receiving such a report, the postal regulatory authority shall promptly handle it according to law.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　邮政企业提供邮政普遍服务不符合邮政普遍服务标准的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，可以处一万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 67** Where a postal enterprise provides universal postal services that do not meet the universal postal service standards, the postal regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; and sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　邮政企业未经邮政管理部门批准，停止办理或者限制办理邮政普遍服务业务和特殊服务业务，或者撤销提供邮政普遍服务的邮政营业场所的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，可以处二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 68** Where a postal enterprise, without approval from the postal regulatory authority, ceases or restricts the handling of universal postal service business and special service business, or cancels a postal business premises for providing universal postal services, the postal regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; and sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　邮政企业利用带有邮政专用标志的车船从事邮件运递以外的经营性活动，或者以出租等方式允许其他单位或者个人使用带有邮政专用标志的车船的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，可以并处二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。  
邮政企业从业人员利用带有邮政专用标志的车船从事邮件运递以外的活动的，由邮政企业责令改正，给予处分。

**Article 69** Where a postal enterprise uses vehicles or vessels bearing postal specific signs to engage in business activities other than mail transportation, or by means of leasing or otherwise allows other entities or individuals to use vehicles or vessels bearing postal specific signs, the postal regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; and sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons.  
Where an employee of a postal enterprise uses a vehicle or vessel bearing postal specific signs to engage in activities other than mail transportation, the postal enterprise shall order corrections and impose sanctions.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　邮政企业从业人员故意延误投递邮件的，由邮政企业给予处分。

**Article 70** Where an employee of a postal enterprise intentionally delays the delivery of mail, the postal enterprise shall impose sanctions on the employee.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　冒领、私自开拆、隐匿、毁弃或者非法检查他人邮件、快件，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 71** Where a person fraudulently claims, privately opens, conceals, destroys, discards, or illegally inspects another person's mail or express items, which does not yet constitute a crime, that person shall be given a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　未取得快递业务经营许可经营快递业务，或者邮政企业以外的单位或者个人经营由邮政企业专营的信件寄递业务或者寄递国家机关公文的，由邮政管理部门或者工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对快递企业，还可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证。  
违反[本法第五十一条](#art-51)第二款的规定，经营信件的国内快递业务的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 72** Where a person engages in express business without obtaining an express business operation permit, or an entity or individual other than a postal enterprise operates the letter posting business exclusively operated by postal enterprises or posts official documents of state organs, the postal regulatory authority or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; and for an express enterprise, it may also be ordered to suspend business for rectification until its express business operation permit is revoked.  
Where a person violates the provisions of Article 51, paragraph 2 of this Law and operates domestic express business for letters, that person shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　快递企业有下列行为之一的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，可以处一万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿：  
（一）设立分支机构、合并、分立，未向邮政管理部门备案的；  
（二）未在信件封套的显著位置标注信件字样的；  
（三）将信件打包后作为包裹寄递的；  
（四）停止经营快递业务，未书面告知邮政管理部门并交回快递业务经营许可证，或者未按照国务院邮政管理部门的规定妥善处理尚未投递的快件的。

**Article 73** Where an express enterprise commits any of the following acts, the postal regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it may also be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) Failing to file with the postal regulatory authority when establishing a branch, merging, or splitting;  
(2) Failing to mark the word "letter" in a prominent position on the envelope of a letter;  
(3) Bundling letters and posting them as parcels;  
(4) Ceasing express business operation without notifying the postal regulatory authority in writing and returning the express business operation permit, or failing to properly handle the express items that have not yet been delivered in accordance with the provisions of the State Council postal regulatory authority.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　邮政企业、快递企业未按照规定向用户明示其业务资费标准，或者有其他价格违法行为的，由政府价格主管部门依照《中华人民共和国价格法》的规定处罚。

**Article 74** Where a postal enterprise or express enterprise fails to explicitly disclose its service tariff standards to users in accordance with regulations, or commits other price-related illegal acts, the government price authority shall penalize it in accordance with the provisions of the "Price Law of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　邮政企业、快递企业不建立或者不执行收件验视制度，或者违反法律、行政法规以及国务院和国务院有关部门关于禁止寄递或者限制寄递物品的规定收寄邮件、快件的，对邮政企业直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分；对快递企业，邮政管理部门可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证。  
用户在邮件、快件中夹带禁止寄递或者限制寄递的物品，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
有前两款规定的违法行为，造成人身伤害或者财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
邮政企业、快递企业经营国际寄递业务，以及用户交寄国际邮递物品，违反《中华人民共和国海关法》及其他有关法律、行政法规的规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 75** Where a postal enterprise or express enterprise fails to establish or implement the system of inspecting items upon receipt, or posts or sends mail or express items in violation of the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council and relevant departments under the State Council regarding articles prohibited or restricted for posting, sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the postal enterprise; for the express enterprise, the postal regulatory authority may order it to suspend business for rectification until its express business operation permit is revoked.  
Where a user encloses articles prohibited or restricted for posting in mail or express items, which does not yet constitute a crime, that user shall be given a public security penalty according to law.  
Where an illegal act as provided in the preceding two paragraphs causes personal injury or property damage, the liable person shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where a postal enterprise or express enterprise operates international posting business, and a user submits international postal items, in violation of the provisions of the "Customs Law of the People's Republic of China" and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, that person shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　邮政企业、快递企业违法提供用户使用邮政服务或者快递服务的信息，尚不构成犯罪的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；对邮政企业直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分；对快递企业，邮政管理部门还可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证。  
邮政企业、快递企业从业人员有前款规定的违法行为，尚不构成犯罪的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处五千元以上一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 76** Where a postal enterprise or express enterprise illegally provides information about users' use of postal services or express services, which does not yet constitute a crime, the postal regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the postal enterprise; and for the express enterprise, the postal regulatory authority may also order it to suspend business for rectification until its express business operation permit is revoked.  
Where an employee of a postal enterprise or express enterprise commits an illegal act as provided in the preceding paragraph, which does not yet constitute a crime, the postal regulatory authority shall order corrections, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　邮政企业、快递企业拒绝、阻碍依法实施的监督检查，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚；对快递企业，邮政管理部门还可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证。

**Article 77** Where a postal enterprise or express enterprise refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection conducted according to law, which does not yet constitute a crime, it shall be given a public security penalty according to law; and for the express enterprise, the postal regulatory authority may also order it to suspend business for rectification until its express business operation permit is revoked.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　邮政企业及其从业人员、快递企业及其从业人员在经营活动中有危害国家安全行为的，依法追究法律责任；对快递企业，并由邮政管理部门吊销其快递业务经营许可证。

**Article 78** Where a postal enterprise and its employees, or an express enterprise and its employees, commit acts endangering national security in business operations, they shall be investigated for legal liability according to law; and for the express enterprise, the postal regulatory authority shall also revoke its express business operation permit.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　冒用邮政企业名义或者邮政专用标志，或者伪造邮政专用品或者倒卖伪造的邮政专用品的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收伪造的邮政专用品以及违法所得，并处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 79** Where a person falsely uses the name or postal specific signs of a postal enterprise, or forges postal specific articles or resells forged postal specific articles, the postal regulatory authority shall order corrections, confiscate the forged postal specific articles and illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚：  
（一）盗窃、损毁邮政设施或者影响邮政设施正常使用的；  
（二）伪造邮资凭证或者倒卖伪造的邮资凭证的；  
（三）扰乱邮政营业场所、快递企业营业场所正常秩序的；  
（四）非法拦截、强登、扒乘运送邮件、快件的车辆的。

**Article 80** Where a person commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime, that person shall be given a public security penalty according to law:  
(1) Stealing or damaging postal facilities or affecting the normal use of postal facilities;  
(2) Forging postage certificates or reselling forged postage certificates;  
(3) Disturbing the normal order of postal business premises or express enterprise business premises;  
(4) Illegally intercepting, forcibly boarding, or clinging to vehicles transporting mail or express items.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定被吊销快递业务经营许可证的，自快递业务经营许可证被吊销之日起三年内，不得申请经营快递业务。  
快递企业被吊销快递业务经营许可证的，应当依法向工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记。

**Article 81** Whoever has their express business permit revoked for violating this Law shall not apply to operate express business within three years from the date of revocation of the express business permit.  
An express enterprise whose express business permit is revoked shall, according to law, undergo modification or cancellation of registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Whoever violates this Law, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　邮政管理部门工作人员在监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 83** If a staff member of the postal administrative department abuses their power, neglects their duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in supervision and administration work, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本法下列用语的含义：  
邮政企业，是指中国邮政集团公司及其提供邮政服务的全资企业、控股企业。  
寄递，是指将信件、包裹、印刷品等物品按照封装上的名址递送给特定个人或者单位的活动，包括收寄、分拣、运输、投递等环节。  
快递，是指在承诺的时限内快速完成的寄递活动。  
邮件，是指邮政企业寄递的信件、包裹、汇款通知、报刊和其他印刷品等。  
快件，是指快递企业递送的信件、包裹、印刷品等。  
信件，是指信函、明信片。信函是指以套封形式按照名址递送给特定个人或者单位的缄封的信息载体，不包括书籍、报纸、期刊等。  
包裹，是指按照封装上的名址递送给特定个人或者单位的独立封装的物品，其重量不超过五十千克，任何一边的尺寸不超过一百五十厘米，长、宽、高合计不超过三百厘米。  
平常邮件，是指邮政企业在收寄时不出具收据，投递时不要求收件人签收的邮件。  
给据邮件，是指邮政企业在收寄时向寄件人出具收据，投递时由收件人签收的邮件。  
邮政设施，是指用于提供邮政服务的邮政营业场所、邮件处理场所、邮筒（箱）、邮政报刊亭、信报箱等。  
邮件处理场所，是指邮政企业专门用于邮件分拣、封发、储存、交换、转运、投递等活动的场所。  
国际邮递物品，是指中华人民共和国境内的用户与其他国家或者地区的用户相互寄递的包裹和印刷品等。  
邮政专用品，是指邮政日戳、邮资机、邮政业务单据、邮政夹钳、邮袋和其他邮件专用容器。

**Article 84** The following terms as used in this Law have the following meanings:  
Postal enterprise means China Post Group and its wholly-owned enterprises and holding enterprises providing postal services.  
Dispatch means the activity of delivering letters, parcels, printed matters, and other items to specific individuals or entities according to the address on the packaging, including collection, sorting, transportation, delivery, and other steps.  
Express delivery means a dispatch activity completed quickly within the promised time limit.  
Mail means letters, parcels, money order notices, newspapers and periodicals, and other printed matters dispatched by postal enterprises.  
Express item means letters, parcels, printed matters, and other items delivered by express enterprises.  
Letter means correspondence and postcards. Correspondence means sealed information carriers delivered to specific individuals or entities according to the address in an enveloped form, excluding books, newspapers, periodicals, etc.  
Parcel means an independently packaged item delivered to a specific individual or entity according to the address on the packaging, with a weight not exceeding fifty kilograms, any side dimension not exceeding one hundred and fifty centimeters, and length, width, and height not exceeding three hundred centimeters in total.  
Ordinary mail means mail for which the postal enterprise does not issue a receipt upon collection and does not require the recipient's signature upon delivery.  
Registered mail means mail for which the postal enterprise issues a receipt to the sender upon collection and requires the recipient's signature upon delivery.  
Postal facilities means postal business premises, mail processing premises, post boxes (letter boxes), postal newspaper and periodical kiosks, mailboxes, etc., used for providing postal services.  
Mail processing premises means premises specially used by postal enterprises for activities such as sorting, sealing, storage, exchange, transfer, and delivery of mail.  
International dispatch items means parcels, printed matters, and other items dispatched between users within the territory of the People's Republic of China and users in other countries or regions.  
Postal special articles means postal date stamps, postage meters, postal business documents, postal clamps, mailbags, and other special containers for mail.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法公布前按照国家有关规定，经国务院对外贸易主管部门批准或者备案，并向工商行政管理部门依法办理登记后经营国际快递业务的国际货物运输代理企业，凭批准或者备案文件以及营业执照，到国务院邮政管理部门领取快递业务经营许可证。国务院邮政管理部门应当将企业领取快递业务经营许可证的情况向其原办理登记的工商行政管理部门通报。  
除前款规定的企业外，本法公布前依法向工商行政管理部门办理登记后经营快递业务的企业，不具备本法规定的经营快递业务的条件的，应当在国务院邮政管理部门规定的期限内达到本法规定的条件，逾期达不到本法规定的条件的，不得继续经营快递业务。

**Article 85** International freight forwarding enterprises that, prior to the promulgation of this Law, have been approved or filed with the competent department of foreign trade under the State Council in accordance with relevant state regulations and have registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law to operate international express business, shall, with the approval or filing documents and business license, obtain an express business permit from the postal administrative department under the State Council. The postal administrative department under the State Council shall notify the original administrative department for industry and commerce where the enterprise registered of the fact that the enterprise has obtained the express business permit.  
Except for the enterprises specified in the preceding paragraph, enterprises that have registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law and operated express business prior to the promulgation of this Law, if they do not meet the conditions for operating express business as provided in this Law, shall meet such conditions within the time limit prescribed by the postal administrative department under the State Council; if they fail to meet the conditions by the deadline, they shall not continue to operate express business.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　省、自治区、直辖市应当根据本地区的实际情况，制定支持邮政企业提供邮政普遍服务的具体办法。

**Article 86** Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the actual circumstances of their regions, formulate specific measures to support postal enterprises in providing universal postal services.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法自2009年10月1日起施行。

**Article 87** This Law shall come into force on October 1, 2009.
