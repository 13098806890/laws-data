# 中华人民共和国领事特权与豁免条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1990-10-30  
**生效日期**：1990-10-30

> （1990年10月30日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十六次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为确定外国驻中国领馆和领馆成员的领事特权与豁免，便于外国驻中国领馆在领区内代表其国家有效地执行职务，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of determining the consular privileges and immunities of foreign consular posts in China and their members, and to facilitate foreign consular posts in China to effectively perform their functions on behalf of their countries within their consular districts.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　领事官员应当是具有派遣国国籍的人。如果委派具有中国或者第三国国籍的人或者派遣国在中国永久居留的人为领事官员，必须征得中国主管机关的同意。中国主管机关可以随时撤销此项同意。

**Article 2** A consular officer shall be a person who possesses the nationality of the sending State. If a person who has the nationality of China or a third State, or a person who has permanent residence in China of the sending State, is appointed as a consular officer, the consent of the competent authorities of China must be obtained. The competent authorities of China may revoke such consent at any time.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　领馆及其馆长有权在领馆馆舍、馆长寓所和馆长执行职务所乘用的交通工具上，使用派遣国国旗或者国徽。

**Article 3** A consular post and its head shall have the right to use the national flag or emblem of the sending State on the premises of the consular post, the residence of the head of the consular post, and the means of transport used by the head of the consular post in the performance of official functions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　领馆馆舍不受侵犯。中国国家工作人员进入领馆馆舍，须经领馆馆长或者派遣国使馆馆长或者他们两人中一人授权的人员同意。遇有火灾或者其他灾害须迅速采取保护行动时，可以推定领馆馆长已经同意。中国有关机关应当采取适当措施保护领馆馆舍免受侵犯或者损害。

**Article 4** The premises of a consular post shall be inviolable. Chinese state personnel may enter the premises of a consular post only with the consent of the head of the consular post, the head of the diplomatic mission of the sending State, or a person authorized by either of them. In the event of a fire or other disaster requiring prompt protective action, the consent of the head of the consular post may be presumed. The relevant Chinese authorities shall take appropriate measures to protect the premises of a consular post against intrusion or damage.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　领馆馆舍和馆长寓所免纳捐税，但为其提供特定服务所收的费用不在此限。  
领馆办理公务所收规费和手续费免纳捐税。

**Article 5** The premises of a consular post and the residence of the head of the consular post shall be exempt from all dues and taxes, except charges levied for specific services rendered.  
Fees and charges collected by a consular post in the course of its official duties shall be exempt from all dues and taxes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　领馆的档案和文件不受侵犯。

**Article 6** The archives and documents of a consular post shall be inviolable.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　领馆成员在中国境内有行动和旅行的自由，但中国政府规定禁止或者限制进入的区域除外。

**Article 7** Members of a consular post shall have freedom of movement and travel within the territory of China, except for areas to which entry is prohibited or restricted by the regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　领馆为公务目的可以同派遣国政府以及派遣国使馆和其他领馆自由通讯。通讯可以采用一切适当方法，包括外交信使或者领事信使、外交邮袋或者领事邮袋和明码、密码电信在内。

**Article 8** A consular post may, for official purposes, communicate freely with the Government of the sending State, the diplomatic mission of the sending State and other consular posts of the sending State. Such communications may be made by all appropriate means, including diplomatic or consular couriers, diplomatic or consular bags, and telecommunication in plain language or in code.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　领馆设置和使用无线电收发信机，必须经中国政府同意。领馆运进上述设备，按照中国政府的有关规定办理。

**Article 9** The installation and use of a radio transmitter by a consular post must be subject to the consent of the Chinese Government. The importation of such equipment by a consular post shall be in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　领事邮袋不得开拆或者扣留。  
领事邮袋以装载来往公文、公务文件及公务用品为限，应予加封并附有可资识别的外部标记。如中国有关机关有重大理由认为领事邮袋装有上述物品以外的物品时，可要求领事官员或者其授权的人员在中国有关机关人员在场的情况下开拆；如领事官员拒绝此项要求，领事邮袋应予退回至原发送地点。

**Article 10** A consular bag may not be opened or detained. A consular bag shall contain only official correspondence, documents, and articles for official use, and shall be sealed and bear an external mark that is recognizable.  
If the competent Chinese authorities have serious reason to believe that a consular bag contains items other than those mentioned above, they may request the consular officer or a person authorized by him or her to open the bag in the presence of personnel from the competent Chinese authorities; if such a request is refused by the consular officer, the consular bag shall be returned to its original place of dispatch.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　领事信使必须是具有派遣国国籍的人，并且不得是在中国永久居留的。领事信使必须持有派遣国主管机关出具的信使证明书。领事信使人身不受侵犯，不受逮捕或者拘留。  
临时领事信使必须持有派遣国主管机关出具的临时信使证明书，在其负责携带领事邮袋期间享有与领事信使同等的豁免。  
商业飞机机长或者商业船舶船长受委托可以转递领事邮袋，但机长或者船长必须持有委托国官方证明文件，注明所携带的领事邮袋件数。机长或者船长不得视为领事信使。经与中国地方人民政府主管机关商定，领馆可以派领馆成员与机长或者船长接交领事邮袋。

**Article 11** A consular courier shall be a person who possesses the nationality of the sending State and shall not have permanent residence in China. A consular courier must hold a courier certificate issued by the competent authorities of the sending State. The person of a consular courier shall be inviolable and shall not be subject to arrest or detention. An ad hoc consular courier must hold an ad hoc courier certificate issued by the competent authorities of the sending State and shall enjoy the same immunities as a consular courier while in charge of the consular bag. The captain of a commercial aircraft or the master of a commercial ship may, upon authorization, be entrusted with the transmission of a consular bag, provided that the captain or master holds an official document from the entrusting State specifying the number of consular bags carried. The captain or master shall not be considered a consular courier. Subject to agreement with the competent authority of the local people's government of China, a consular post may send a member to take delivery of the consular bag from or hand it over to the captain or master.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　领事官员人身不受侵犯。中国有关机关应当采取适当措施，防止领事官员的人身自由和尊严受到侵犯。  
领事官员不受逮捕或者拘留，但有严重犯罪情形，依照法定程序予以逮捕或者拘留的不在此限。  
领事官员不受监禁，但为执行已经发生法律效力的判决的不在此限。

**Article 12** The person of a consular officer shall be inviolable. The relevant Chinese authorities shall take appropriate measures to prevent any infringement on the personal freedom and dignity of a consular officer. A consular officer shall not be subject to arrest or detention, except in the case of a serious crime and in accordance with legal procedures for such arrest or detention. A consular officer shall not be subject to imprisonment, except in execution of a judgment that has become legally effective.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　领事官员的寓所不受侵犯。  
领事官员的文书和信件不受侵犯。  
领事官员的财产不受侵犯，但本条例第十四条另有规定的除外。

**Article 13** The residence of a consular officer shall be inviolable.  
The documents and correspondence of a consular officer shall be inviolable.  
The property of a consular officer shall be inviolable, except as otherwise provided in Article 14 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　领事官员和领馆行政技术人员执行职务的行为享有司法和行政管辖豁免。领事官员执行职务以外的行为的管辖豁免，按照中国与外国签订的双边条约、协定或者根据对等原则办理。  
领事官员和领馆行政技术人员享有的司法管辖豁免不适用于下列各项民事诉讼：  
（一）涉及未明示以派遣国代表身份所订的契约的诉讼；  
（二）涉及在中国境内的私有不动产的诉讼，但以派遣国代表身份所拥有的为领馆使用的不动产不在此限；  
（三）以私人身份进行的遗产继承的诉讼；  
（四）因车辆、船舶或者航空器在中国境内造成的事故涉及损害赔偿的诉讼。

**Article 14** Consular officers and administrative and technical staff of a consular post shall enjoy immunity from judicial and administrative jurisdiction in respect of acts performed in the exercise of their official functions. The immunity from jurisdiction in respect of acts performed by consular officers outside the scope of their official functions shall be governed by bilateral treaties or agreements concluded between China and foreign States, or on the basis of reciprocity. The immunity from judicial jurisdiction enjoyed by consular officers and administrative and technical staff of a consular post shall not apply to the following types of civil actions:  
(1) actions relating to a contract not concluded expressly in the capacity of a representative of the sending State;  
(2) actions relating to private immovable property located in China, except immovable property held as a representative of the sending State for the use of the consular post;  
(3) actions relating to succession to an estate in which they are involved in a private capacity; and (4) actions relating to damages arising from an accident caused by a vehicle, vessel, or aircraft in China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　领馆成员可以被要求在司法或者行政程序中到场作证，但没有义务就其执行职务所涉及事项作证。领馆成员有权拒绝以鉴定人身份就派遣国的法律提出证词。  
领事官员拒绝作证，不得对其采取强制措施或者给予处罚。  
领馆行政技术人员和领馆服务人员除执行职务所涉及事项外，不得拒绝作证。

**Article 15** Members of a consular post may be required to appear as witnesses in judicial or administrative proceedings, but they shall not be obliged to give testimony on matters relating to the exercise of their official functions. Members of a consular post shall have the right to refuse to give testimony as expert witnesses on the laws of the sending State. No compulsory measures or penalties shall be imposed on a consular officer who refuses to give testimony. Administrative and technical staff and service staff of a consular post may not refuse to give testimony, except in respect of matters relating to the exercise of their official functions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例规定的有关人员所享有的管辖豁免可以由派遣国政府明确表示放弃。  
依照本条例规定享有管辖豁免的人员如果主动提起诉讼，对与本诉直接有关的反诉，不得援用管辖豁免。  
放弃民事管辖豁免或者行政管辖豁免，不包括对判决的执行也放弃豁免。放弃对判决执行的豁免须由派遣国政府另作明确表示。

**Article 16** The immunity from jurisdiction enjoyed by the persons concerned under these Regulations may be waived by the Government of the sending State in an express manner.  
If a person who enjoys immunity from jurisdiction under these Regulations initiates legal proceedings, he or she may not invoke such immunity in respect of any counterclaim directly related to the principal claim.  
The waiver of immunity from civil or administrative jurisdiction shall not be deemed to imply a waiver of immunity in respect of the execution of a judgment, for which a separate express waiver by the Government of the sending State shall be required.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　领事官员和领馆行政技术人员免纳捐税，但下列各项除外：  
（一）通常计入商品价格或者服务价格内的捐税；  
（二）对在中国境内私有不动产所征的捐税，但用作领馆馆舍的不在此限；  
（三）有关遗产的各种捐税，但领事官员亡故，其在中国境内的动产的有关遗产的各种捐税免纳；  
（四）对来源于中国境内的私人收入所征的捐税；  
（五）为其提供特定服务所收的费用。  
领馆服务人员在领馆服务所得工资，免纳捐税。

**Article 17** Consular officers and administrative and technical staff of a consular post shall be exempt from all dues and taxes, except the following:  
(1) dues and taxes normally incorporated in the price of goods or services;  
(2) dues and taxes levied on private immovable property located in China, except that used as premises of a consular post;  
(3) dues and taxes on succession to an estate, except that on the movable property of a consular officer who has died and is located in China;  
(4) dues and taxes on private income originating in China; and (5) charges levied for specific services rendered. Service staff of a consular post shall be exempt from dues and taxes on wages received in respect of their service with the consular post.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　领馆成员免除一切个人和公共劳务以及军事义务。  
领事官员和领馆行政技术人员免除中国法律、法规关于外国人登记和居留许可所规定的义务。

**Article 18** Members of a consular post shall be exempt from all personal and public services and military obligations.  
Consular officers and administrative and technical staff of a consular post shall be exempt from obligations under Chinese laws and regulations relating to the registration and residence permits of aliens.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　领馆运进的公务用品，领事官员运进的自用物品和领馆行政技术人员到任后半年内运进的自用物品包括安家物品，按照中国政府的有关规定免纳关税和其他捐税，但保管、运输及类似服务费用除外。  
领事官员和领馆行政技术人员运进的前款所述自用物品，不得超过直接需要的数量。  
领事官员的私人行李免受查验，但中国有关机关有重大理由认为其中装有不属于本条第一款规定的自用物品或者中国法律和政府规定禁止运进、运出或者管制的物品，可以查验。查验时，须有领事官员或者其授权的人员到场。

**Article 19** Official articles imported by a consular post, articles for personal use imported by a consular officer, and articles for personal use including household effects imported by administrative and technical staff of a consular post within six months of their arrival, shall be exempt from customs duties and other dues and taxes in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government, except the charges for storage, transport, and similar services. The articles for personal use imported by consular officers and administrative and technical staff as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall not exceed the quantities directly necessary. The personal baggage of a consular officer shall be exempt from inspection, except that the competent Chinese authorities may inspect it if they have serious reason to believe that it contains articles not for personal use as specified in the first paragraph of this Article, or articles the import, export, or possession of which is prohibited or controlled under Chinese laws and regulations. Such inspection shall be carried out in the presence of the consular officer or a person authorized by him or her.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　领馆和领馆成员携带自用的枪支、子弹入出境，必须经中国政府批准，并且按照中国政府的有关规定办理。

**Article 20** The import or export of firearms and ammunition for personal use by a consular post or its members must be approved by the Chinese Government and handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the Chinese Government.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　与领事官员、领馆行政技术人员、领馆服务人员共同生活的配偶及未成年子女，分别享有领事官员、领馆行政技术人员、领馆服务人员根据本条例第七条、第十七条、第十八条、第十九条的规定所享有的特权与豁免，但身为中国公民或者在中国永久居留的外国人除外。

**Article 21** The spouses and minor children living with consular officers, administrative and technical staff of the consulate, and service staff of the consulate shall enjoy the privileges and immunities that such consular officers, administrative and technical staff, and service staff respectively enjoy under Articles 7, 17, 18 and 19 of these Regulations, except those who are Chinese citizens or foreigners with permanent residence in China.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　领事官员如果是中国公民或者在中国永久居留的外国人，仅就其执行职务的行为，享有本条例规定的特权与豁免。  
领馆行政技术人员或者领馆服务人员如果是中国公民或者在中国永久居留的外国人，除没有义务就其执行职务所涉及事项作证外，不享有本条例规定的特权与豁免。  
私人服务人员不享有本条例规定的特权与豁免。

**Article 22** Consular officers who are Chinese citizens or foreigners with permanent residence in China shall only enjoy the privileges and immunities provided for in these Regulations in respect of their acts performed in the exercise of their functions.  
Administrative and technical staff or service staff of the consulate who are Chinese citizens or foreigners with permanent residence in China shall not enjoy the privileges and immunities provided for in these Regulations, except that they shall have no obligation to testify on matters relating to the exercise of their functions.  
Private service personnel shall not enjoy the privileges and immunities provided for in these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　下列人员在中国过境或者逗留期间享有所必需的豁免和不受侵犯：  
（一）途经中国的外国驻第三国的领事官员和与其共同生活的配偶及未成年子女；  
（二）持有中国外交签证或者持有与中国互免签证国家外交护照的外国领事官员。

**Article 23** The following persons shall enjoy the necessary immunity and inviolability during their transit or stay in China:  
(1) consular officers of a foreign state accredited to a third state who are passing through China, and their spouses and minor children living with them;  
(2) foreign consular officers holding a Chinese diplomatic visa or holding diplomatic passports of countries which have entered into a visa exemption agreement with China.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　享有领事特权与豁免的人员：  
（一）应当尊重中国的法律、法规；  
（二）不得干涉中国的内政；  
（三）不得将领馆馆舍和领馆成员的寓所充作与执行领事职务不相符合的用途。

**Article 24** Persons enjoying consular privileges and immunities:  
(1) shall respect the laws and regulations of China;  
(2) shall not interfere in the internal affairs of China;  
(3) shall not use the premises of the consulate and the residences of consular members for purposes incompatible with the exercise of consular functions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　领事官员不得在中国境内为私人利益从事任何职务范围以外的职业或者商业活动。

**Article 25** Consular officers shall not engage in any professional or commercial activity for personal profit within the territory of China outside the scope of their official functions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　如果外国给予中国驻该国领馆、领馆成员以及途经或者临时去该国的中国驻第三国领事官员的领事特权与豁免，不同于中国给予该国驻中国领馆、领馆成员以及途经或者临时来中国的该国驻第三国领事官员的领事特权与豁免，中国政府根据对等原则，可以给予该国驻中国领馆、领馆成员以及途经或者临时来中国的该国驻第三国领事官员以相应的领事特权与豁免。

**Article 26** If a foreign state grants to the consulate, members of the consulate of China accredited to that state, and Chinese consular officers accredited to a third state who are passing through or temporarily in that state consular privileges and immunities which are different from those China grants to the consulate, members of the consulate of that state accredited to China, and consular officers of that state accredited to a third state who are passing through or temporarily in China, the Chinese Government may, on the basis of reciprocity, grant corresponding consular privileges and immunities to the consulate, members of the consulate of that state accredited to China, and consular officers of that state accredited to a third state who are passing through or temporarily in China.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　中国缔结或者参加的国际条约对领事特权与豁免另有规定的，按照国际条约的规定办理，但中国声明保留的条款除外。  
中国与外国签订的双边条约或者协定对领事特权与豁免另有规定的，按照条约或者协定的规定执行。

**Article 27** Where international treaties concluded or acceded to by China have different provisions on consular privileges and immunities, such provisions of international treaties shall prevail, except for clauses on which China has made reservations.  
Where bilateral treaties or agreements between China and foreign states have different provisions on consular privileges and immunities, such provisions of such treaties or agreements shall prevail.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例中下列用语的含义是：  
（一）“领馆”是指总领事馆、领事馆、副领事馆或者领事代理处；  
（二）“领区”是指为领馆执行领事职务而设定的区域；  
（三）“领馆馆长”是指派遣国委派领导领馆的总领事、领事、副领事或者领事代理人；  
（四）“领事官员”是指总领事、副总领事、领事、副领事、领事随员或者领事代理人；  
（五）“领馆行政技术人员”是指从事领馆行政或者技术工作的人员；  
（六）“领馆服务人员”是指从事领馆服务工作的人员；  
（七）“领馆成员”是指领事官员、领馆行政技术人员和领馆服务人员；  
（八）“私人服务人员”是指领馆成员私人雇用的服务人员；  
（九）“领馆馆舍”是指专供领馆使用的建筑物或者部分建筑物及其附属的土地。

**Article 28** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) 'Consulate' means a consulate-general, consulate, vice-consulate or consular agency;  
(2) 'Consular district' means the area designated for the consulate to exercise its consular functions;  
(3) 'Head of consulate' means the consul-general, consul, vice-consul or consular agent accredited by the sending state to lead the consulate;  
(4) 'Consular officer' means the consul-general, deputy consul-general, consul, vice-consul, consular attaché or consular agent;  
(5) 'Administrative and technical staff of the consulate' means the persons engaged in the administrative or technical work of the consulate;  
(6) 'Service staff of the consulate' means the persons engaged in the service work of the consulate;  
(7) 'Members of the consulate' means consular officers, administrative and technical staff of the consulate, and service staff of the consulate;  
(8) 'Private service personnel' means persons employed privately by a member of the consulate for service;  
(9) 'Premises of the consulate' means the buildings or parts of buildings and the ancillary land used exclusively for the purposes of the consulate.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
