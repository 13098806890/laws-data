# 中华人民共和国国籍法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1980-09-10  
**生效日期**：1980-09-10

> （1980年9月10日第五届全国人民代表大会第三次会议通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　中华人民共和国国籍的取得、丧失和恢复，都适用本法。


**Article 1** This Law shall apply to the acquisition, loss, and restoration of the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国是统一的多民族的国家，各民族的人都具有中国国籍。


**Article 2** The People's Republic of China is a unified multi-ethnic country; persons of all ethnic groups shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国不承认中国公民具有双重国籍。


**Article 3** The People's Republic of China shall not recognize dual nationality for any Chinese citizen.
<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　父母双方或一方为中国公民，本人出生在中国，具有中国国籍。


**Article 4** Any person born in China whose parents are both Chinese citizens or one of whose parents is a Chinese citizen shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　父母双方或一方为中国公民，本人出生在外国，具有中国国籍；但父母双方或一方为中国公民并定居在外国，本人出生时即具有外国国籍的，不具有中国国籍。


**Article 5** Any person born abroad whose parents are both Chinese citizens or one of whose parents is a Chinese citizen shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China. However, if both parents or one of the parents are Chinese citizens and have settled abroad, and the person acquires a foreign nationality at birth, such person shall not have the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　父母无国籍或国籍不明，定居在中国，本人出生在中国，具有中国国籍。


**Article 6** Any person born in China whose parents are stateless or of uncertain nationality and have settled in China shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国人或无国籍人，愿意遵守中国宪法和法律，并具有下列条件之一的，可以经申请批准加入中国国籍：  

**Article 7** Aliens or stateless persons who are willing to abide by the Constitution and laws of China and who meet one of the following conditions may apply for naturalization as Chinese citizens: 1.  
They are close relatives of Chinese citizens; 2.  
They have settled in China; or 3.  
They have other legitimate reasons.
一、中国人的近亲属；  
二、定居在中国的；  
三、有其它正当理由。

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请加入中国国籍获得批准的，即取得中国国籍；被批准加入中国国籍的，不得再保留外国国籍。


**Article 8** Any person whose application for naturalization as a Chinese citizen is approved shall acquire the nationality of the People's Republic of China; any person approved for naturalization shall not retain any foreign nationality.
<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　定居外国的中国公民，自愿加入或取得外国国籍的，即自动丧失中国国籍。


**Article 9** Any Chinese citizen who has settled abroad and who voluntarily applies for or acquires foreign nationality shall automatically lose the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国公民具有下列条件之一的，可以经申请批准退出中国国籍：  

**Article 10** Chinese citizens who meet one of the following conditions may apply for renunciation of Chinese nationality: 1.  
They are close relatives of aliens; 2.  
They have settled abroad; or 3.  
They have other legitimate reasons.
一、外国人的近亲属；  
二、定居在外国的；  
三、有其它正当理由。

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请退出中国国籍获得批准的，即丧失中国国籍。


**Article 11** Any person whose application for renunciation of Chinese nationality is approved shall lose the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家工作人员和现役军人，不得退出中国国籍。


**Article 12** State functionaries and active-duty military personnel shall not renounce the nationality of the People's Republic of China.
<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　曾有过中国国籍的外国人，具有正当理由，可以申请恢复中国国籍；被批准恢复中国国籍的，不得再保留外国国籍。


**Article 13** Aliens who formerly held the nationality of the People's Republic of China may apply for restoration of Chinese nationality if they have legitimate reasons; any person whose application for restoration of Chinese nationality is approved shall not retain any foreign nationality.
<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中国国籍的取得、丧失和恢复，除第九条规定的以外，必须办理申请手续。未满十八周岁的人，可由其父母或其他法定代理人代为办理申请。


**Article 14** Except for the circumstances provided in Article 9, the acquisition, loss, and restoration of the nationality of the People's Republic of China must undergo the application formalities. Persons under the age of 18 may have their applications processed by their parents or other legal agents.
<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　受理国籍申请的机关，在国内为当地市、县公安局，在国外为中国外交代表机关和领事机关。


**Article 15** The authorities responsible for accepting applications for nationality shall be the local municipal or county public security bureaus within China, and the diplomatic missions and consular posts of China abroad.
<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　加入、退出和恢复中国国籍的申请，由中华人民共和国公安部审批。经批准的，由公安部发给证书。


**Article 16** Applications for naturalization, renunciation, and restoration of the nationality of the People's Republic of China shall be examined and approved by the Ministry of Public Security of the People's Republic of China. Approved applicants shall be issued a certificate by the Ministry of Public Security.
<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本法公布前，已经取得中国国籍的或已经丧失中国国籍的，继续有效。


**Article 17** The nationality of the People's Republic of China that has been acquired or lost prior to the promulgation of this Law shall remain valid.
<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本法自公布之日起施行。


**Article 18** This Law shall come into force on the date of its promulgation.
