# 中华人民共和国突发事件应对法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2024-06-28  
**生效日期**：2024-11-01

> （2007年8月30日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过　2024年6月28日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　管理与指挥体制 第三章　预防与应急准备 第四章　监测与预警 第五章　应急处置与救援 第六章　事后恢复与重建 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和减少突发事件的发生，控制、减轻和消除突发事件引起的严重社会危害，提高突发事件预防和应对能力，规范突发事件应对活动，保护人民生命财产安全，维护国家安全、公共安全、生态环境安全和社会秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称突发事件，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成严重社会危害，需要采取应急处置措施予以应对的自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件和社会安全事件。 突发事件的预防与应急准备、监测与预警、应急处置与救援、事后恢复与重建等应对活动，适用本法。 《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》等有关法律对突发公共卫生事件应对作出规定的，适用其规定。有关法律没有规定的，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和减少突发事件的发生，控制、减轻和消除突发事件引起的严重社会危害，提高突发事件预防和应对能力，规范突发事件应对活动，保护人民生命财产安全，维护国家安全、公共安全、生态环境安全和社会秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of preventing and reducing the occurrence of emergencies, controlling, mitigating and eliminating the serious social harm caused by emergencies, enhancing the capability to prevent and respond to emergencies, regulating emergency response activities, protecting the safety of people's lives and property, and safeguarding national security, public safety, ecological and environmental security, and social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称突发事件，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成严重社会危害，需要采取应急处置措施予以应对的自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件和社会安全事件。  
突发事件的预防与应急准备、监测与预警、应急处置与救援、事后恢复与重建等应对活动，适用本法。  
《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》等有关法律对突发公共卫生事件应对作出规定的，适用其规定。有关法律没有规定的，适用本法。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, an emergency means a natural disaster, accident disaster, public health incident, or public security incident that occurs suddenly and causes or may cause serious social harm and requires emergency response measures to be taken.  
This Law shall apply to emergency response activities such as the prevention and emergency preparedness, monitoring and early warning, emergency response and rescue, and post-incident recovery and reconstruction of emergencies.  
Where the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and other relevant laws provide for responses to public health emergencies, such provisions shall apply. Where there are no provisions in relevant laws, this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　按照社会危害程度、影响范围等因素，突发自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件分为特别重大、重大、较大和一般四级。法律、行政法规或者国务院另有规定的，从其规定。  
突发事件的分级标准由国务院或者国务院确定的部门制定。

**Article 3** According to factors such as the degree of social harm and the scope of impact, natural disasters, accident disasters, and public health incidents are classified into four levels: extraordinarily serious, serious, relatively large, and general. Where laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.  
The classification criteria for emergencies shall be formulated by the State Council or the department designated by the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　突发事件应对工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观、习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，建立健全集中统一、高效权威的中国特色突发事件应对工作领导体制，完善党委领导、政府负责、部门联动、军地联合、社会协同、公众参与、科技支撑、法治保障的治理体系。

**Article 4** The emergency response work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, be guided by Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the important thought of Three Represents, the Scientific Outlook on Development, and Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, establish and improve a centralized, unified, efficient, and authoritative leadership system for emergency response work with Chinese characteristics, and improve the governance system featuring Party committee leadership, government responsibility, departmental coordination, military-civilian joint action, social synergy, public participation, scientific and technological support, and the rule of law guarantee.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　突发事件应对工作应当坚持总体国家安全观，统筹发展与安全；坚持人民至上、生命至上；坚持依法科学应对，尊重和保障人权；坚持预防为主、预防与应急相结合。

**Article 5** In the emergency response work, it shall adhere to a holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security; adhere to the primacy of the people and the primacy of life; adhere to law-based and scientific response, respect and protect human rights; adhere to the principle of prevention first, combining prevention with emergency response.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立有效的社会动员机制，组织动员企业事业单位、社会组织、志愿者等各方力量依法有序参与突发事件应对工作，增强全民的公共安全和防范风险的意识，提高全社会的避险救助能力。

**Article 6** The State shall establish an effective social mobilization mechanism, organize and mobilize enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, volunteers, and other forces to participate in emergency response work in an orderly manner according to law, enhance the public's awareness of public safety and risk prevention, and improve the whole society's ability to avoid dangers and provide assistance.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立健全突发事件信息发布制度。有关人民政府和部门应当及时向社会公布突发事件相关信息和有关突发事件应对的决定、命令、措施等信息。  
任何单位和个人不得编造、故意传播有关突发事件的虚假信息。有关人民政府和部门发现影响或者可能影响社会稳定、扰乱社会和经济管理秩序的虚假或者不完整信息的，应当及时发布准确的信息予以澄清。

**Article 7** The State shall establish and improve an emergency information release system. The relevant people's governments and departments shall promptly disclose to the public information relating to emergencies and the decisions, orders, and measures concerning emergency response.  
No entity or individual may fabricate or intentionally disseminate false information concerning emergencies. Where the relevant people's governments and departments discover false or incomplete information that affects or may affect social stability, or disrupts social and economic order, they shall promptly release accurate information to clarify the situation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立健全突发事件新闻采访报道制度。有关人民政府和部门应当做好新闻媒体服务引导工作，支持新闻媒体开展采访报道和舆论监督。  
新闻媒体采访报道突发事件应当及时、准确、客观、公正。  
新闻媒体应当开展突发事件应对法律法规、预防与应急、自救与互救知识等的公益宣传。

**Article 8** The State shall establish and improve a system for news interview and reporting of emergencies. The relevant people's governments and departments shall provide good services and guidance to news media, and support news media in conducting interview reporting and public opinion supervision.  
News media shall conduct interview reporting on emergencies in a timely, accurate, objective, and fair manner.  
News media shall conduct public interest publicity on laws and regulations concerning emergency response, prevention and emergency response, and knowledge of self-rescue and mutual rescue.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家建立突发事件应对工作投诉、举报制度，公布统一的投诉、举报方式。  
对于不履行或者不正确履行突发事件应对工作职责的行为，任何单位和个人有权向有关人民政府和部门投诉、举报。  
接到投诉、举报的人民政府和部门应当依照规定立即组织调查处理，并将调查处理结果以适当方式告知投诉人、举报人；投诉、举报事项不属于其职责的，应当及时移送有关机关处理。  
有关人民政府和部门对投诉人、举报人的相关信息应当予以保密，保护投诉人、举报人的合法权益。

**Article 9** The State shall establish a complaint and reporting system for emergency response work, and publish uniform methods for complaints and reports.  
For any act of failing to perform or failing to correctly perform the duties of emergency response work, any entity or individual shall have the right to lodge a complaint or report with the relevant people's government or department.  
The people's government or department that receives a complaint or report shall immediately organize an investigation and disposition in accordance with the provisions, and inform the complainant or reporter of the investigation and disposition results in an appropriate manner; if the complaint or reporting matter is not within its functions, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the relevant authority for handling.  
The relevant people's government or department shall keep confidential the relevant information of the complainant or reporter, and protect the lawful rights and interests of the complainant or reporter.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　突发事件应对措施应当与突发事件可能造成的社会危害的性质、程度和范围相适应；有多种措施可供选择的，应当选择有利于最大程度地保护公民、法人和其他组织权益，且对他人权益损害和生态环境影响较小的措施，并根据情况变化及时调整，做到科学、精准、有效。

**Article 10** Emergency response measures shall be commensurate with the nature, degree, and scope of the social harm that the emergency may cause; where multiple measures are available, the measure that is most conducive to protecting the rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, and that causes less damage to the rights and interests of others and less impact on the ecological environment shall be chosen, and timely adjustments shall be made in light of changes in the situation to ensure that the measures are scientific, precise, and effective.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家在突发事件应对工作中，应当对未成年人、老年人、残疾人、孕产期和哺乳期的妇女、需要及时就医的伤病人员等群体给予特殊、优先保护。

**Article 11** In the emergency response work, the State shall provide special and priority protection to groups such as minors, the elderly, persons with disabilities, women during pregnancy and lactation, and injured or sick persons who need timely medical treatment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府及其部门为应对突发事件的紧急需要，可以征用单位和个人的设备、设施、场地、交通工具等财产。被征用的财产在使用完毕或者突发事件应急处置工作结束后，应当及时返还。财产被征用或者征用后毁损、灭失的，应当给予公平、合理的补偿。

**Article 12** For urgent needs in responding to emergencies, the people's governments at or above the county level and their departments may requisition property such as equipment, facilities, sites, and means of transport of entities and individuals. The requisitioned property shall be promptly returned after use is completed or after the emergency response work ends. Where the property is damaged or lost after being requisitioned, fair and reasonable compensation shall be given.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因依法采取突发事件应对措施，致使诉讼、监察调查、行政复议、仲裁、国家赔偿等活动不能正常进行的，适用有关时效中止和程序中止的规定，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 13** Where the adoption of emergency response measures according to law causes litigation, supervisory investigation, administrative reconsideration, arbitration, state compensation, or other activities to be unable to proceed normally, the provisions on suspension of limitation and suspension of procedures shall apply, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中华人民共和国政府在突发事件的预防与应急准备、监测与预警、应急处置与救援、事后恢复与重建等方面，同外国政府和有关国际组织开展合作与交流。

**Article 14** The Government of the People's Republic of China shall carry out cooperation and exchanges with foreign governments and relevant international organizations in aspects such as the prevention and emergency preparedness, monitoring and early warning, emergency response and rescue, and post-incident recovery and reconstruction of emergencies.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对在突发事件应对工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 15** Units and individuals that make outstanding contributions in emergency response work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　管理与指挥体制

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家建立统一指挥、专常兼备、反应灵敏、上下联动的应急管理体制和综合协调、分类管理、分级负责、属地管理为主的工作体系。

**Article 16** The State shall establish an emergency management system featuring unified command, integration of specialized and regular forces, agile response, and vertical linkage, and a working system focusing on comprehensive coordination, categorized management, graded responsibility, and territorial management.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级人民政府对本行政区域内突发事件的应对管理工作负责。突发事件发生后，发生地县级人民政府应当立即采取措施控制事态发展，组织开展应急救援和处置工作，并立即向上一级人民政府报告，必要时可以越级上报，具备条件的，应当进行网络直报或者自动速报。  
突发事件发生地县级人民政府不能消除或者不能有效控制突发事件引起的严重社会危害的，应当及时向上级人民政府报告。上级人民政府应当及时采取措施，统一领导应急处置工作。  
法律、行政法规规定由国务院有关部门对突发事件应对管理工作负责的，从其规定；地方人民政府应当积极配合并提供必要的支持。

**Article 17** A people's government at the county level shall be responsible for the management of emergency response within its administrative area. After an emergency occurs, the people's government at the county level of the place where the emergency occurs shall immediately take measures to control the development of the incident, organize emergency rescue and response work, and immediately report to the people's government at the next higher level; when necessary, it may report to a higher level beyond the immediate superior, and if conditions permit, it shall make a direct online report or an automatic quick report.  
Where the people's government at the county level of the place where the emergency occurs cannot eliminate or cannot effectively control the serious social harm caused by the emergency, it shall promptly report to the people's government at the higher level. The people's government at the higher level shall promptly take measures and take unified leadership of the emergency response work.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide that the relevant department of the State Council is responsible for the management of emergency response, such provisions shall prevail; local people's governments shall actively cooperate and provide necessary support.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　突发事件涉及两个以上行政区域的，其应对管理工作由有关行政区域共同的上一级人民政府负责，或者由各有关行政区域的上一级人民政府共同负责。共同负责的人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，建立信息共享和协调配合机制。根据共同应对突发事件的需要，地方人民政府之间可以建立协同应对机制。

**Article 18** Where an emergency involves two or more administrative areas, the management of its response shall be the responsibility of the common people's government at the next higher level of the relevant administrative areas, or the joint responsibility of the respective people's governments at the next higher level of the relevant administrative areas. The people's government(s) with joint responsibility shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish an information sharing and coordination mechanism. In light of the need to respond jointly to the emergency, local people's governments may establish a collaborative response mechanism.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府是突发事件应对管理工作的行政领导机关。  
国务院在总理领导下研究、决定和部署特别重大突发事件的应对工作；根据实际需要，设立国家突发事件应急指挥机构，负责突发事件应对工作；必要时，国务院可以派出工作组指导有关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府设立由本级人民政府主要负责人、相关部门负责人、国家综合性消防救援队伍和驻当地中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队有关负责人等组成的突发事件应急指挥机构，统一领导、协调本级人民政府各有关部门和下级人民政府开展突发事件应对工作；根据实际需要，设立相关类别突发事件应急指挥机构，组织、协调、指挥突发事件应对工作。

**Article 19** The people's governments at or above the county level are the administrative leading organs for the management of emergency response work.  
The State Council, under the leadership of the Premier, shall study, decide, and deploy the response work for extraordinarily serious emergencies; establish, according to actual needs, a national emergency command agency to be responsible for emergency response work; when necessary, the State Council may dispatch working groups to guide relevant work.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish an emergency command agency composed of the principal responsible persons of the people's government at the same level, responsible persons of relevant departments, the national comprehensive fire rescue teams, and relevant responsible persons of the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police Force stationed locally, to provide unified leadership and coordination for the emergency response work of the departments of the people's government at the same level and the people's governments at lower levels; according to actual needs, establish relevant categories of emergency command agencies to organize, coordinate, and command emergency response work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　突发事件应急指挥机构在突发事件应对过程中可以依法发布有关突发事件应对的决定、命令、措施。突发事件应急指挥机构发布的决定、命令、措施与设立它的人民政府发布的决定、命令、措施具有同等效力，法律责任由设立它的人民政府承担。

**Article 20** The emergency command agency may, in the process of emergency response, issue decisions, orders, and measures concerning emergency response according to law. The decisions, orders, and measures issued by the emergency command agency shall have the same effect as those issued by the people's government that established it, and the legal liability shall be borne by the people's government that established it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府应急管理部门和卫生健康、公安等有关部门应当在各自职责范围内做好有关突发事件应对管理工作，并指导、协助下级人民政府及其相应部门做好有关突发事件的应对管理工作。

**Article 21** The emergency management departments and the health, public security, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions, properly carry out the management of emergency response, and guide and assist the people's governments at lower levels and their corresponding departments in properly carrying out the management of emergency response.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　乡级人民政府、街道办事处应当明确专门工作力量，负责突发事件应对有关工作。  
居民委员会、村民委员会依法协助人民政府和有关部门做好突发事件应对工作。

**Article 22** The people's governments at the township level and sub-district offices shall designate special working forces to be responsible for the relevant work of emergency response.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall, according to law, assist the people's governments and relevant departments in properly carrying out emergency response work.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公民、法人和其他组织有义务参与突发事件应对工作。

**Article 23** Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the obligation to participate in emergency response work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规、军事法规的规定以及国务院、中央军事委员会的命令，参加突发事件的应急救援和处置工作。

**Article 24** The People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organizations shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws, administrative regulations, and military regulations, and the orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, participate in the emergency rescue and response work for emergencies.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府及其设立的突发事件应急指挥机构发布的有关突发事件应对的决定、命令、措施，应当及时报本级人民代表大会常务委员会备案；突发事件应急处置工作结束后，应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会作出专项工作报告。

**Article 25** The decisions, orders, and measures concerning emergency response issued by the people's governments at or above the county level and the emergency command agencies established by them shall be submitted promptly to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the same level for recordation; after the emergency response work is completed, a special work report shall be made to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the same level.

### 第三章　预防与应急准备

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立健全突发事件应急预案体系。  
国务院制定国家突发事件总体应急预案，组织制定国家突发事件专项应急预案；国务院有关部门根据各自的职责和国务院相关应急预案，制定国家突发事件部门应急预案并报国务院备案。  
地方各级人民政府和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门根据有关法律、法规、规章、上级人民政府及其有关部门的应急预案以及本地区、本部门的实际情况，制定相应的突发事件应急预案并按国务院有关规定备案。

**Article 26** The State shall establish and improve the emergency plan system for emergencies.  
The State Council shall formulate the national overall emergency plan for emergencies, and organize the formulation of national specialized emergency plans for emergencies; the relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and the relevant emergency plans of the State Council, formulate national departmental emergency plans for emergencies and submit them to the State Council for recordation.  
Local people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with relevant laws, regulations, rules, the emergency plans of the people's governments at higher levels and their relevant departments, and the actual conditions of the region and the department, formulate corresponding emergency plans for emergencies and submit them for recordation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府应急管理部门指导突发事件应急预案体系建设，综合协调应急预案衔接工作，增强有关应急预案的衔接性和实效性。

**Article 27** The emergency management departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall guide the construction of the emergency plan system, comprehensively coordinate the linkage of emergency plans, and enhance the connectivity and effectiveness of relevant emergency plans.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　应急预案应当根据本法和其他有关法律、法规的规定，针对突发事件的性质、特点和可能造成的社会危害，具体规定突发事件应对管理工作的组织指挥体系与职责和突发事件的预防与预警机制、处置程序、应急保障措施以及事后恢复与重建措施等内容。  
应急预案制定机关应当广泛听取有关部门、单位、专家和社会各方面意见，增强应急预案的针对性和可操作性，并根据实际需要、情势变化、应急演练中发现的问题等及时对应急预案作出修订。  
应急预案的制定、修订、备案等工作程序和管理办法由国务院规定。

**Article 28** Emergency plans shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, specify the organizational command system and responsibilities for the management of emergency response, and the prevention and early warning mechanisms, response procedures, emergency support measures, and post-incident recovery and reconstruction measures for emergencies, based on the nature and characteristics of the emergency and the social harm it may cause.  
The organ formulating the emergency plan shall extensively solicit opinions from relevant departments, units, experts, and all sectors of society, enhance the pertinence and operability of the emergency plan, and timely revise the emergency plan based on actual needs, changes in circumstances, and problems found in emergency drills.  
The procedures for formulating, revising, and recordation of emergency plans and the management measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府应当将突发事件应对工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。县级以上人民政府有关部门应当制定突发事件应急体系建设规划。

**Article 29** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate emergency response work into the national economic and social development plan. The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate plans for the construction of the emergency system.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国土空间规划等规划应当符合预防、处置突发事件的需要，统筹安排突发事件应对工作所必需的设备和基础设施建设，合理确定应急避难、封闭隔离、紧急医疗救治等场所，实现日常使用和应急使用的相互转换。

**Article 30** Plans such as the territorial space plan shall meet the needs of preventing and responding to emergencies, make overall arrangements for the equipment and infrastructure necessary for emergency response work, reasonably determine places for emergency shelter, isolation, and emergency medical treatment, and achieve mutual conversion between daily use and emergency use.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院应急管理部门会同卫生健康、自然资源、住房城乡建设等部门统筹、指导全国应急避难场所的建设和管理工作，建立健全应急避难场所标准体系。县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内应急避难场所的规划、建设和管理工作。

**Article 31** The emergency management department of the State Council shall, together with the health, natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, and other departments, coordinate and guide the construction and management of emergency shelters nationwide, and establish and improve the standard system for emergency shelters. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the planning, construction, and management of emergency shelters within their administrative areas.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立健全突发事件风险评估体系，对可能发生的突发事件进行综合性评估，有针对性地采取有效防范措施，减少突发事件的发生，最大限度减轻突发事件的影响。

**Article 32** The State shall establish and improve the risk assessment system for emergencies, conduct comprehensive assessments of emergencies that may occur, take targeted and effective preventive measures to reduce the occurrence of emergencies, and minimize the impact of emergencies to the greatest extent.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级人民政府应当对本行政区域内容易引发自然灾害、事故灾难和公共卫生事件的危险源、危险区域进行调查、登记、风险评估，定期进行检查、监控，并责令有关单位采取安全防范措施。  
省级和设区的市级人民政府应当对本行政区域内容易引发特别重大、重大突发事件的危险源、危险区域进行调查、登记、风险评估，组织进行检查、监控，并责令有关单位采取安全防范措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据情况变化，及时调整危险源、危险区域的登记。登记的危险源、危险区域及其基础信息，应当按照国家有关规定接入突发事件信息系统，并及时向社会公布。

**Article 33** The people's government at the county level shall investigate, register, and conduct risk assessments of hazard sources and hazard areas within its administrative area that are likely to cause natural disasters, accident disasters, and public health incidents, conduct regular inspections and monitoring, and order the relevant units to take safety precautions.  
The people's governments at the provincial level and at the level of a city divided into districts shall investigate, register, and conduct risk assessments of hazard sources and hazard areas within their administrative areas that are likely to cause extraordinarily serious or serious emergencies, organize inspections and monitoring, and order the relevant units to take safety precautions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of changes in circumstances, timely adjust the registration of hazard sources and hazard areas. The registered hazard sources, hazard areas, and their basic information shall be connected to the emergency information system in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall be promptly made public.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级人民政府及其有关部门、乡级人民政府、街道办事处、居民委员会、村民委员会应当及时调解处理可能引发社会安全事件的矛盾纠纷。

**Article 34** The people's governments at the county level and their relevant departments, the people's governments at the township level, sub-district offices, residents' committees, and villagers' committees shall timely mediate and handle disputes that may cause social security incidents.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　所有单位应当建立健全安全管理制度，定期开展危险源辨识评估，制定安全防范措施；定期检查本单位各项安全防范措施的落实情况，及时消除事故隐患；掌握并及时处理本单位存在的可能引发社会安全事件的问题，防止矛盾激化和事态扩大；对本单位可能发生的突发事件和采取安全防范措施的情况，应当按照规定及时向所在地人民政府或者有关部门报告。

**Article 35** All units shall establish and improve safety management systems, regularly carry out hazard source identification and assessment, and formulate safety precautions; regularly inspect the implementation of various safety precautions in the unit to promptly eliminate potential accident hazards; keep abreast of and promptly handle problems existing in the unit that may cause social security incidents to prevent escalation of conflicts and expansion of incidents; and report to the local people's government or relevant departments in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions on the emergencies that may occur in the unit and the safety precautions taken.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　矿山、金属冶炼、建筑施工单位和易燃易爆物品、危险化学品、放射性物品等危险物品的生产、经营、运输、储存、使用单位，应当制定具体应急预案，配备必要的应急救援器材、设备和物资，并对生产经营场所、有危险物品的建筑物、构筑物及周边环境开展隐患排查，及时采取措施管控风险和消除隐患，防止发生突发事件。

**Article 36** Mining, metal smelting, and construction units, and units that produce, operate, transport, store, or use dangerous articles such as inflammable and explosive articles, hazardous chemicals, and radioactive articles, shall formulate specific emergency plans, equip necessary emergency rescue equipment, devices, and materials, and conduct hazard investigation on the production and operation sites, buildings and structures containing dangerous articles, and the surrounding environment, and promptly take measures to control risks and eliminate hazards to prevent the occurrence of emergencies.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公共交通工具、公共场所和其他人员密集场所的经营单位或者管理单位应当制定具体应急预案，为交通工具和有关场所配备报警装置和必要的应急救援设备、设施，注明其使用方法，并显著标明安全撤离的通道、路线，保证安全通道、出口的畅通。  
有关单位应当定期检测、维护其报警装置和应急救援设备、设施，使其处于良好状态，确保正常使用。

**Article 37** The operating units or management units of public transportation vehicles, public places, and other densely populated places shall formulate specific emergency plans, equip the vehicles and relevant places with alarm devices and necessary emergency rescue equipment and facilities, indicate their methods of use, and conspicuously mark the safe evacuation passages and routes, and ensure the unobstructed access to safe passages and exits.  
The relevant units shall regularly inspect and maintain their alarm devices and emergency rescue equipment and facilities to keep them in good condition and ensure normal use.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全突发事件应对管理培训制度，对人民政府及其有关部门负有突发事件应对管理职责的工作人员以及居民委员会、村民委员会有关人员定期进行培训。

**Article 38** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve training systems for emergency response management, and conduct regular training for the staff of the people's governments and their relevant departments who are responsible for emergency response management, as well as relevant personnel of residents' committees and villagers' committees.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家综合性消防救援队伍是应急救援的综合性常备骨干力量，按照国家有关规定执行综合应急救援任务。县级以上人民政府有关部门可以根据实际需要设立专业应急救援队伍。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门可以建立由成年志愿者组成的应急救援队伍。乡级人民政府、街道办事处和有条件的居民委员会、村民委员会可以建立基层应急救援队伍，及时、就近开展应急救援。单位应当建立由本单位职工组成的专职或者兼职应急救援队伍。  
国家鼓励和支持社会力量建立提供社会化应急救援服务的应急救援队伍。社会力量建立的应急救援队伍参与突发事件应对工作应当服从履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府、突发事件应急指挥机构的统一指挥。  
县级以上人民政府应当推动专业应急救援队伍与非专业应急救援队伍联合培训、联合演练，提高合成应急、协同应急的能力。

**Article 39** The national comprehensive fire rescue teams are the comprehensive standing backbone forces for emergency rescue, and shall perform comprehensive emergency rescue tasks in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level may, according to actual needs, establish professional emergency rescue teams.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments may establish emergency rescue teams composed of adult volunteers. The people's governments at the township level, sub-district offices, and eligible residents' committees and villagers' committees may establish grassroots emergency rescue teams to carry out timely and nearby emergency rescue. Units shall establish full-time or part-time emergency rescue teams composed of their own employees.  
The State encourages and supports social forces to establish emergency rescue teams providing socialized emergency rescue services. Emergency rescue teams established by social forces, when participating in emergency response work, shall be subject to the unified command of the people's government that has unified leadership or is organizing the response to the emergency, or the emergency command agency.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall promote joint training and joint drills between professional emergency rescue teams and non-professional emergency rescue teams to improve the capability of integrated and coordinated emergency response.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　地方各级人民政府、县级以上人民政府有关部门、有关单位应当为其组建的应急救援队伍购买人身意外伤害保险，配备必要的防护装备和器材，防范和减少应急救援人员的人身伤害风险。  
专业应急救援人员应当具备相应的身体条件、专业技能和心理素质，取得国家规定的应急救援职业资格，具体办法由国务院应急管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 40** Local people's governments at all levels, the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and relevant units shall purchase personal accident insurance for the emergency rescue teams they have formed, equip them with necessary protective equipment and devices, and prevent and reduce the risk of personal injury to emergency rescue personnel.  
Professional emergency rescue personnel shall possess corresponding physical conditions, professional skills, and psychological qualities, and obtain the emergency rescue professional qualifications prescribed by the State. The specific measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织应当有计划地组织开展应急救援的专门训练。

**Article 41** The People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organizations shall organize and carry out specialized training for emergency rescue in a planned manner.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级人民政府及其有关部门、乡级人民政府、街道办事处应当组织开展面向社会公众的应急知识宣传普及活动和必要的应急演练。  
居民委员会、村民委员会、企业事业单位、社会组织应当根据所在地人民政府的要求，结合各自的实际情况，开展面向居民、村民、职工等的应急知识宣传普及活动和必要的应急演练。

**Article 42** The people's governments at the county level and their relevant departments, the people's governments at the township level, and sub-district offices shall organize and carry out activities to popularize emergency knowledge among the general public and necessary emergency drills.  
Residents' committees, villagers' committees, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and in light of their actual circumstances, carry out activities to popularize emergency knowledge among residents, villagers, employees, etc., and necessary emergency drills.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　各级各类学校应当把应急教育纳入教育教学计划，对学生及教职工开展应急知识教育和应急演练，培养安全意识，提高自救与互救能力。  
教育主管部门应当对学校开展应急教育进行指导和监督，应急管理等部门应当给予支持。

**Article 43** Schools of all levels and types shall incorporate emergency education into their education and teaching plans, carry out emergency knowledge education and emergency drills for students and staff, cultivate safety awareness, and improve the ability of self-rescue and mutual rescue.  
The education competent department shall provide guidance and supervision for schools in conducting emergency education, and the emergency management and other departments shall provide support.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　各级人民政府应当将突发事件应对工作所需经费纳入本级预算，并加强资金管理，提高资金使用绩效。

**Article 44** People's governments at all levels shall include the expenses required for emergency response work into their respective budgets, strengthen the management of funds, and improve the performance of fund use.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家按照集中管理、统一调拨、平时服务、灾时应急、采储结合、节约高效的原则，建立健全应急物资储备保障制度，动态更新应急物资储备品种目录，完善重要应急物资的监管、生产、采购、储备、调拨和紧急配送体系，促进安全应急产业发展，优化产业布局。  
国家储备物资品种目录、总体发展规划，由国务院发展改革部门会同国务院有关部门拟订。国务院应急管理等部门依据职责制定应急物资储备规划、品种目录，并组织实施。应急物资储备规划应当纳入国家储备总体发展规划。

**Article 45** The State shall, in accordance with the principles of centralized management, unified allocation, peacetime service, emergency response in disasters, combination of procurement and storage, and economy and efficiency, establish and improve the emergency material reserve guarantee system, dynamically update the catalog of emergency material reserve varieties, improve the supervision, production, procurement, storage, allocation, and emergency distribution system for important emergency materials, promote the development of the safety emergency industry, and optimize the industrial layout.  
The catalog of national reserve material varieties and the overall development plan shall be drafted by the development and reform department of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council. The emergency management department of the State Council and other departments shall, in accordance with their functions, formulate emergency material reserve plans and variety catalogs, and organize their implementation. The emergency material reserve plans shall be included in the overall national reserve development plan.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　设区的市级以上人民政府和突发事件易发、多发地区的县级人民政府应当建立应急救援物资、生活必需品和应急处置装备的储备保障制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本地区的实际情况和突发事件应对工作的需要，依法与有条件的企业签订协议，保障应急救援物资、生活必需品和应急处置装备的生产、供给。有关企业应当根据协议，按照县级以上地方人民政府要求，进行应急救援物资、生活必需品和应急处置装备的生产、供给，并确保符合国家有关产品质量的标准和要求。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织储备基本的应急自救物资和生活必需品。有关部门可以向社会公布相关物资、物品的储备指南和建议清单。

**Article 46** The people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts and the people's governments at the county level in areas prone to or frequent emergencies shall establish a reserve guarantee system for emergency rescue materials, daily necessities, and emergency response equipment.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the actual conditions of the region and the needs of emergency response work, enter into agreements with qualified enterprises according to law to ensure the production and supply of emergency rescue materials, daily necessities, and emergency response equipment. The relevant enterprises shall, in accordance with the agreements and the requirements of the local people's governments at or above the county level, produce and supply emergency rescue materials, daily necessities, and emergency response equipment, and ensure that they meet the national standards and requirements on product quality.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to stock up on basic emergency self-rescue materials and daily necessities. Relevant departments may make public the storage guidelines and recommended lists of relevant materials and items.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家建立健全应急运输保障体系，统筹铁路、公路、水运、民航、邮政、快递等运输和服务方式，制定应急运输保障方案，保障应急物资、装备和人员及时运输。  
县级以上地方人民政府和有关主管部门应当根据国家应急运输保障方案，结合本地区实际做好应急调度和运力保障，确保运输通道和客货运枢纽畅通。  
国家发挥社会力量在应急运输保障中的积极作用。社会力量参与突发事件应急运输保障，应当服从突发事件应急指挥机构的统一指挥。

**Article 47** The State shall establish and improve the emergency transport guarantee system, coordinate railway, highway, waterway, civil aviation, postal, express delivery, and other transport and service modes, formulate emergency transport guarantee plans, and ensure the timely transport of emergency materials, equipment, and personnel.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with the national emergency transport guarantee plan and in light of local realities, carry out emergency dispatch and transport capacity guarantee to ensure the smooth flow of transport channels and passenger and freight hubs.  
The State shall bring into play the positive role of social forces in emergency transport guarantee. Social forces participating in emergency transport guarantee shall be subject to the unified command of the emergency command agency.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家建立健全能源应急保障体系，提高能源安全保障能力，确保受突发事件影响地区的能源供应。

**Article 48** The State shall establish and improve the energy emergency guarantee system, enhance the capability of energy security, and ensure the energy supply in areas affected by emergencies.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家建立健全应急通信、应急广播保障体系，加强应急通信系统、应急广播系统建设，确保突发事件应对工作的通信、广播安全畅通。

**Article 49** The State shall establish and improve the emergency communication and emergency broadcasting guarantee system, strengthen the construction of emergency communication systems and emergency broadcasting systems, and ensure the safe and smooth communication and broadcasting for emergency response work.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家建立健全突发事件卫生应急体系，组织开展突发事件中的医疗救治、卫生学调查处置和心理援助等卫生应急工作，有效控制和消除危害。

**Article 50** The State shall establish and improve the health emergency system for emergencies, organize and carry out health emergency work such as medical treatment, hygiene investigation and disposal, and psychological assistance in emergencies, and effectively control and eliminate harm.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上人民政府应当加强急救医疗服务网络的建设，配备相应的医疗救治物资、设施设备和人员，提高医疗卫生机构应对各类突发事件的救治能力。

**Article 51** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of emergency medical service networks, allocate corresponding medical treatment materials, facilities, equipment, and personnel, and enhance the treatment capacity of medical and health institutions in response to various emergencies.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织为突发事件应对工作提供物资、资金、技术支持和捐赠。  
接受捐赠的单位应当及时公开接受捐赠的情况和受赠财产的使用、管理情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 52** The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to provide materials, funds, technical support, and donations for the response to emergencies.  
The unit receiving donations shall promptly disclose the acceptance of donations and the use and management of donated property, and shall accept social supervision.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　红十字会在突发事件中，应当对伤病人员和其他受害者提供紧急救援和人道救助，并协助人民政府开展与其职责相关的其他人道主义服务活动。有关人民政府应当给予红十字会支持和资助，保障其依法参与应对突发事件。  
慈善组织在发生重大突发事件时开展募捐和救助活动，应当在有关人民政府的统筹协调、有序引导下依法进行。有关人民政府应当通过提供必要的需求信息、政府购买服务等方式，对慈善组织参与应对突发事件、开展应急慈善活动予以支持。

**Article 53** In emergencies, the Red Cross Society shall provide emergency rescue and humanitarian assistance to the injured, sick, and other victims, and assist the people's government in conducting other humanitarian service activities related to its functions. The relevant people's government shall provide support and funding to the Red Cross Society, ensuring its participation in emergency response according to law.  
When charitable organizations conduct fundraising and relief activities during major emergencies, such activities shall be carried out according to law under the overall coordination and orderly guidance of the relevant people's government. The relevant people's government shall support charitable organizations in participating in emergency response and conducting emergency charity activities by providing necessary demand information, government procurement of services, and other means.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　有关单位应当加强应急救援资金、物资的管理，提高使用效率。  
任何单位和个人不得截留、挪用、私分或者变相私分应急救援资金、物资。

**Article 54** Relevant units shall strengthen the management of emergency rescue funds and materials, and improve the efficiency of their use.  
No unit or individual may withhold, misappropriate, privately divide, or privately divide in disguised form the emergency rescue funds or materials.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家发展保险事业，建立政府支持、社会力量参与、市场化运作的巨灾风险保险体系，并鼓励单位和个人参加保险。

**Article 55** The State develops the insurance industry, establishes a catastrophe risk insurance system with government support, social force participation, and market-based operation, and encourages units and individuals to participate in insurance.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家加强应急管理基础科学、重点行业领域关键核心技术的研究，加强互联网、云计算、大数据、人工智能等现代技术手段在突发事件应对工作中的应用，鼓励、扶持有条件的教学科研机构、企业培养应急管理人才和科技人才，研发、推广新技术、新材料、新设备和新工具，提高突发事件应对能力。

**Article 56** The State strengthens research on basic sciences for emergency management and key core technologies in key industries and fields, enhances the application of modern technological means such as the Internet, cloud computing, big data, and artificial intelligence in emergency response, and encourages and supports eligible teaching and research institutions and enterprises in cultivating emergency management talents and scientific and technological talents, and in developing and promoting new technologies, new materials, new equipment, and new tools, so as to improve the capacity to respond to emergencies.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当建立健全突发事件专家咨询论证制度，发挥专业人员在突发事件应对工作中的作用。

**Article 57** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish and improve a system for expert consultation and demonstration on emergencies, and give play to the role of professionals in emergency response.

### 第四章　监测与预警

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家建立健全突发事件监测制度。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当根据自然灾害、事故灾难和公共卫生事件的种类和特点，建立健全基础信息数据库，完善监测网络，划分监测区域，确定监测点，明确监测项目，提供必要的设备、设施，配备专职或者兼职人员，对可能发生的突发事件进行监测。

**Article 58** The State establishes and improves a monitoring system for emergencies.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, based on the types and characteristics of natural disasters, accident disasters, and public health incidents, establish and improve basic information databases, perfect monitoring networks, divide monitoring areas, determine monitoring points, specify monitoring items, provide necessary equipment and facilities, and assign full-time or part-time personnel to monitor potential emergencies.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院建立全国统一的突发事件信息系统。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当建立或者确定本地区统一的突发事件信息系统，汇集、储存、分析、传输有关突发事件的信息，并与上级人民政府及其有关部门、下级人民政府及其有关部门、专业机构、监测网点和重点企业的突发事件信息系统实现互联互通，加强跨部门、跨地区的信息共享与情报合作。

**Article 59** The State Council establishes a unified national emergency information system.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish or designate a unified emergency information system for their respective regions, collect, store, analyze, and transmit information on emergencies, and achieve interconnection and intercommunication with the emergency information systems of the people's government at the higher level and its relevant departments, the people's government at the lower level and its relevant departments, professional institutions, monitoring stations, and key enterprises, and strengthen cross-departmental and cross-regional information sharing and intelligence cooperation.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门、专业机构应当通过多种途径收集突发事件信息。  
县级人民政府应当在居民委员会、村民委员会和有关单位建立专职或者兼职信息报告员制度。  
公民、法人或者其他组织发现发生突发事件，或者发现可能发生突发事件的异常情况，应当立即向所在地人民政府、有关主管部门或者指定的专业机构报告。接到报告的单位应当按照规定立即核实处理，对于不属于其职责的，应当立即移送相关单位核实处理。

**Article 60** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, and professional institutions shall collect information on emergencies through various channels.  
The people's government at the county level shall establish a system of full-time or part-time information reporters in residents' committees, villagers' committees, and relevant units.  
Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, upon discovering that an emergency has occurred or an abnormal situation that may lead to an emergency, shall immediately report to the local people's government, the relevant competent department, or the designated professional institution. The unit receiving the report shall immediately verify and handle it according to regulations; if the matter is not within its functions, it shall immediately transfer it to the relevant unit for verification and handling.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　地方各级人民政府应当按照国家有关规定向上级人民政府报送突发事件信息。县级以上人民政府有关主管部门应当向本级人民政府相关部门通报突发事件信息，并报告上级人民政府主管部门。专业机构、监测网点和信息报告员应当及时向所在地人民政府及其有关主管部门报告突发事件信息。  
有关单位和人员报送、报告突发事件信息，应当做到及时、客观、真实，不得迟报、谎报、瞒报、漏报，不得授意他人迟报、谎报、瞒报，不得阻碍他人报告。

**Article 61** Local people's governments at various levels shall submit information on emergencies to the people's government at the higher level in accordance with relevant State regulations. The relevant competent departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall notify the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level of emergency information and report to the competent department of the people's government at the higher level. Professional institutions, monitoring stations, and information reporters shall promptly report emergency information to the local people's government and its relevant departments.  
The relevant units and individuals submitting or reporting emergency information shall do so in a timely, objective, and truthful manner, and shall not delay, falsify, conceal, or omit reporting, nor instruct others to delay, falsify, or conceal reporting, nor obstruct others from reporting.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当及时汇总分析突发事件隐患和监测信息，必要时组织相关部门、专业技术人员、专家学者进行会商，对发生突发事件的可能性及其可能造成的影响进行评估；认为可能发生重大或者特别重大突发事件的，应当立即向上级人民政府报告，并向上级人民政府有关部门、当地驻军和可能受到危害的毗邻或者相关地区的人民政府通报，及时采取预防措施。

**Article 62** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly aggregate and analyze hidden dangers of emergencies and monitoring information, and when necessary, organize relevant departments, professional technicians, experts, and scholars to hold consultations to assess the possibility of an emergency occurring and its potential impact; if they believe that a major or particularly major emergency may occur, they shall immediately report to the people's government at the higher level, and notify the relevant departments of the people's government at the higher level, the local military garrison, and the people's governments of adjacent or relevant areas that may be affected, and take preventive measures in a timely manner.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家建立健全突发事件预警制度。  
可以预警的自然灾害、事故灾难和公共卫生事件的预警级别，按照突发事件发生的紧急程度、发展势态和可能造成的危害程度分为一级、二级、三级和四级，分别用红色、橙色、黄色和蓝色标示，一级为最高级别。  
预警级别的划分标准由国务院或者国务院确定的部门制定。

**Article 63** The State establishes and improves a warning system for emergencies.  
The warning levels for natural disasters, accident disasters, and public health incidents that can be warned are classified into Level I, Level II, Level III, and Level IV according to the urgency, development trend, and potential harm of the emergency, indicated by red, orange, yellow, and blue respectively, with Level I being the highest.  
The standards for classifying warning levels shall be formulated by the State Council or a department designated by the State Council.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　可以预警的自然灾害、事故灾难或者公共卫生事件即将发生或者发生的可能性增大时，县级以上地方人民政府应当根据有关法律、行政法规和国务院规定的权限和程序，发布相应级别的警报，决定并宣布有关地区进入预警期，同时向上一级人民政府报告，必要时可以越级上报；具备条件的，应当进行网络直报或者自动速报；同时向当地驻军和可能受到危害的毗邻或者相关地区的人民政府通报。  
发布警报应当明确预警类别、级别、起始时间、可能影响的范围、警示事项、应当采取的措施、发布单位和发布时间等。

**Article 64** When a natural disaster, accident disaster, or public health incident that may be warned is imminent or the likelihood of its occurrence increases, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the authority and procedures prescribed by the State Council, issue a warning of the corresponding level, decide and announce that the relevant area enters a warning period, and simultaneously report to the people's government at the next higher level; when necessary, it may report by skipping levels; if conditions permit, it shall conduct direct online reporting or automatic rapid reporting; and simultaneously notify the local military garrison and the people's governments of adjacent or relevant areas that may be affected.  
The issuance of a warning shall specify the category, level, start time, scope of potential impact, items to be warned, measures to be taken, issuing unit, and time of issuance.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家建立健全突发事件预警发布平台，按照有关规定及时、准确向社会发布突发事件预警信息。  
广播、电视、报刊以及网络服务提供者、电信运营商应当按照国家有关规定，建立突发事件预警信息快速发布通道，及时、准确、无偿播发或者刊载突发事件预警信息。  
公共场所和其他人员密集场所，应当指定专门人员负责突发事件预警信息接收和传播工作，做好相关设备、设施维护，确保突发事件预警信息及时、准确接收和传播。

**Article 65** The State establishes and improves a platform for issuing emergency warnings, and shall promptly and accurately release emergency warning information to the public in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Radio, television, newspapers, Internet service providers, and telecommunications operators shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, establish fast channels for issuing emergency warning information, and promptly, accurately, and without charge broadcast or publish emergency warning information.  
Public places and other densely populated places shall designate dedicated personnel responsible for receiving and disseminating emergency warning information, maintain relevant equipment and facilities, and ensure timely and accurate receipt and dissemination of emergency warning information.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　发布三级、四级警报，宣布进入预警期后，县级以上地方人民政府应当根据即将发生的突发事件的特点和可能造成的危害，采取下列措施：  
（一）启动应急预案；  
（二）责令有关部门、专业机构、监测网点和负有特定职责的人员及时收集、报告有关信息，向社会公布反映突发事件信息的渠道，加强对突发事件发生、发展情况的监测、预报和预警工作；  
（三）组织有关部门和机构、专业技术人员、有关专家学者，随时对突发事件信息进行分析评估，预测发生突发事件可能性的大小、影响范围和强度以及可能发生的突发事件的级别；  
（四）定时向社会发布与公众有关的突发事件预测信息和分析评估结果，并对相关信息的报道工作进行管理；  
（五）及时按照有关规定向社会发布可能受到突发事件危害的警告，宣传避免、减轻危害的常识，公布咨询或者求助电话等联络方式和渠道。

**Article 66** After issuing a Level III or Level IV warning and announcing the entry into a warning period, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the characteristics of the impending emergency and the harm it may cause, take the following measures:  
(1) activate emergency response plans;  
(2) order relevant departments, professional institutions, monitoring stations, and personnel with specific duties to promptly collect and report relevant information, disclose to the public the channels for reporting emergency information, and strengthen the monitoring, forecasting, and warning of the occurrence and development of the emergency;  
(3) organize relevant departments, institutions, professional technicians, and relevant experts and scholars to promptly analyze and assess emergency information, predict the probability, scope, and intensity of the potential emergency, and the level of the potential emergency;  
(4) periodically release to the public the prediction information and analysis and assessment results concerning the emergency that are relevant to the public, and manage the reporting of relevant information;  
(5) promptly, in accordance with relevant regulations, issue warnings to the public about potential harm from the emergency, publicize knowledge on avoiding or mitigating harm, and publish contact information and channels such as consultation or assistance hotlines.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　发布一级、二级警报，宣布进入预警期后，县级以上地方人民政府除采取[本法第六十六条](#art-66)规定的措施外，还应当针对即将发生的突发事件的特点和可能造成的危害，采取下列一项或者多项措施：  
（一）责令应急救援队伍、负有特定职责的人员进入待命状态，并动员后备人员做好参加应急救援和处置工作的准备；  
（二）调集应急救援所需物资、设备、工具，准备应急设施和应急避难、封闭隔离、紧急医疗救治等场所，并确保其处于良好状态、随时可以投入正常使用；  
（三）加强对重点单位、重要部位和重要基础设施的安全保卫，维护社会治安秩序；  
（四）采取必要措施，确保交通、通信、供水、排水、供电、供气、供热、医疗卫生、广播电视、气象等公共设施的安全和正常运行；  
（五）及时向社会发布有关采取特定措施避免或者减轻危害的建议、劝告；  
（六）转移、疏散或者撤离易受突发事件危害的人员并予以妥善安置，转移重要财产；  
（七）关闭或者限制使用易受突发事件危害的场所，控制或者限制容易导致危害扩大的公共场所的活动；  
（八）法律、法规、规章规定的其他必要的防范性、保护性措施。

**Article 67** After issuing a Level I or Level II warning and announcing the entry into a warning period, in addition to the measures specified in Article 66 of this Law, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the characteristics of the impending emergency and the harm it may cause, take one or more of the following measures:  
(1) order emergency rescue teams and personnel with specific duties to enter a state of readiness, and mobilize reserve personnel to prepare for participation in emergency rescue and response operations;  
(2) assemble materials, equipment, and tools needed for emergency rescue, prepare emergency facilities and places such as emergency shelters, closed isolation areas, and emergency medical treatment sites, and ensure that they are in good condition and ready for normal use at any time;  
(3) strengthen the security of key units, important sites, and critical infrastructure, and maintain social order;  
(4) take necessary measures to ensure the safety and normal operation of public facilities such as transportation, communications, water supply, drainage, electricity, gas, heating, medical and health services, radio and television, and meteorology;  
(5) promptly issue to the public suggestions and advice on taking specific measures to avoid or mitigate harm;  
(6) transfer, evacuate, or remove persons vulnerable to the emergency and properly shelter them, and relocate important property;  
(7) close or restrict the use of places vulnerable to the emergency, and control or restrict activities in public places that may easily lead to the escalation of harm;  
(8) other necessary preventive and protective measures as provided for by laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　发布警报，宣布进入预警期后，县级以上人民政府应当对重要商品和服务市场情况加强监测，根据实际需要及时保障供应、稳定市场。必要时，国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以按照《中华人民共和国价格法》等有关法律规定采取相应措施。

**Article 68** After issuing a warning and announcing the entry into a warning period, the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen monitoring of the market for important goods and services, and ensure supply and stabilize the market in a timely manner based on actual needs. When necessary, the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may take corresponding measures in accordance with relevant legal provisions such as the Price Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　对即将发生或者已经发生的社会安全事件，县级以上地方人民政府及其有关主管部门应当按照规定向上一级人民政府及其有关主管部门报告，必要时可以越级上报，具备条件的，应当进行网络直报或者自动速报。

**Article 69** For a social security incident that is imminent or has already occurred, the local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant competent departments shall, in accordance with regulations, report to the people's government at the next higher level and its relevant competent departments; when necessary, they may report by skipping levels; if conditions permit, they shall conduct direct online reporting or automatic rapid reporting.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　发布突发事件警报的人民政府应当根据事态的发展，按照有关规定适时调整预警级别并重新发布。  
有事实证明不可能发生突发事件或者危险已经解除的，发布警报的人民政府应当立即宣布解除警报，终止预警期，并解除已经采取的有关措施。

**Article 70** The people's government that issued the emergency warning shall, based on the development of the situation, adjust the warning level in a timely manner according to relevant regulations and reissue the warning.  
If facts prove that an emergency cannot occur or the danger has been lifted, the people's government that issued the warning shall immediately declare the warning lifted, terminate the warning period, and cancel the relevant measures that have been taken.

### 第五章　应急处置与救援

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家建立健全突发事件应急响应制度。  
突发事件的应急响应级别，按照突发事件的性质、特点、可能造成的危害程度和影响范围等因素分为一级、二级、三级和四级，一级为最高级别。  
突发事件应急响应级别划分标准由国务院或者国务院确定的部门制定。县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当在突发事件应急预案中确定应急响应级别。

**Article 71** The State establishes and improves an emergency response system for emergencies.  
The emergency response levels for emergencies are classified into Level I, Level II, Level III, and Level IV based on factors such as the nature, characteristics, potential harm, and scope of impact of the emergency, with Level I being the highest.  
The standards for classifying emergency response levels shall be formulated by the State Council or a department designated by the State Council. People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall determine the emergency response level in their emergency response plans.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　突发事件发生后，履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府应当针对其性质、特点、危害程度和影响范围等，立即启动应急响应，组织有关部门，调动应急救援队伍和社会力量，依照法律、法规、规章和应急预案的规定，采取应急处置措施，并向上级人民政府报告；必要时，可以设立现场指挥部，负责现场应急处置与救援，统一指挥进入突发事件现场的单位和个人。  
启动应急响应，应当明确响应事项、级别、预计期限、应急处置措施等。  
履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府，应当建立协调机制，提供需求信息，引导志愿服务组织和志愿者等社会力量及时有序参与应急处置与救援工作。

**Article 72** After an emergency occurs, the people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency shall, based on its nature, characteristics, degree of harm, and scope of impact, immediately activate the emergency response, organize relevant departments, mobilize emergency rescue teams and social forces, and take emergency response measures in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, and emergency response plans, and report to the people's government at the higher level; when necessary, it may establish an on-site command post to be responsible for on-site emergency response and rescue, and to exercise unified command over units and individuals entering the emergency site.  
The activation of the emergency response shall specify the response matters, level, expected duration, and emergency response measures.  
The people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency shall establish coordination mechanisms, provide information on needs, and guide social forces such as volunteer service organizations and volunteers to participate in emergency response and rescue work in a timely and orderly manner.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　自然灾害、事故灾难或者公共卫生事件发生后，履行统一领导职责的人民政府应当采取下列一项或者多项应急处置措施：  
（一）组织营救和救治受害人员，转移、疏散、撤离并妥善安置受到威胁的人员以及采取其他救助措施；  
（二）迅速控制危险源，标明危险区域，封锁危险场所，划定警戒区，实行交通管制、限制人员流动、封闭管理以及其他控制措施；  
（三）立即抢修被损坏的交通、通信、供水、排水、供电、供气、供热、医疗卫生、广播电视、气象等公共设施，向受到危害的人员提供避难场所和生活必需品，实施医疗救护和卫生防疫以及其他保障措施；  
（四）禁止或者限制使用有关设备、设施，关闭或者限制使用有关场所，中止人员密集的活动或者可能导致危害扩大的生产经营活动以及采取其他保护措施；  
（五）启用本级人民政府设置的财政预备费和储备的应急救援物资，必要时调用其他急需物资、设备、设施、工具；  
（六）组织公民、法人和其他组织参加应急救援和处置工作，要求具有特定专长的人员提供服务；  
（七）保障食品、饮用水、药品、燃料等基本生活必需品的供应；  
（八）依法从严惩处囤积居奇、哄抬价格、牟取暴利、制假售假等扰乱市场秩序的行为，维护市场秩序；  
（九）依法从严惩处哄抢财物、干扰破坏应急处置工作等扰乱社会秩序的行为，维护社会治安；  
（十）开展生态环境应急监测，保护集中式饮用水水源地等环境敏感目标，控制和处置污染物；  
（十一）采取防止发生次生、衍生事件的必要措施。

**Article 73** After a natural disaster, accident disaster, or public health incident occurs, the people's government that exercises unified leadership shall take one or more of the following emergency response measures:  
(1) organize the rescue and treatment of affected persons, transfer, evacuate, remove, and properly shelter threatened persons, and take other relief measures;  
(2) rapidly control sources of danger, mark dangerous areas, seal off dangerous places, delineate cordons, implement traffic control, restrict movement of persons, implement closed management, and take other control measures;  
(3) immediately repair damaged public facilities such as transportation, communications, water supply, drainage, electricity, gas, heating, medical and health services, radio and television, and meteorology; provide shelter and necessities to affected persons; implement medical treatment and epidemic prevention; and take other support measures;  
(4) prohibit or restrict the use of relevant equipment and facilities, close or restrict the use of relevant places, suspend activities where people gather or production and business activities that may lead to the escalation of harm, and take other protective measures;  
(5) activate the fiscal reserve funds set aside by the people's government at the same level and the reserve emergency rescue materials, and when necessary, requisition other urgently needed materials, equipment, facilities, and tools;  
(6) organize citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in emergency rescue and response operations, and require persons with specific expertise to provide services;  
(7) ensure the supply of basic necessities such as food, drinking water, medicines, and fuel;  
(8) severely punish, according to law, acts that disrupt market order such as hoarding and profiteering, price gouging, seeking exorbitant profits, and producing and selling counterfeit goods, so as to maintain market order;  
(9) severely punish, according to law, acts that disrupt social order such as looting of property and interfering with or sabotaging emergency response operations, so as to maintain public security;  
(10) conduct ecological and environmental emergency monitoring, protect environmental sensitive targets such as centralized drinking water sources, and control and dispose of pollutants;  
(11) take necessary measures to prevent secondary and derivative incidents.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　社会安全事件发生后，组织处置工作的人民政府应当立即启动应急响应，组织有关部门针对事件的性质和特点，依照有关法律、行政法规和国家其他有关规定，采取下列一项或者多项应急处置措施：  
（一）强制隔离使用器械相互对抗或者以暴力行为参与冲突的当事人，妥善解决现场纠纷和争端，控制事态发展；  
（二）对特定区域内的建筑物、交通工具、设备、设施以及燃料、燃气、电力、水的供应进行控制；  
（三）封锁有关场所、道路，查验现场人员的身份证件，限制有关公共场所内的活动；  
（四）加强对易受冲击的核心机关和单位的警卫，在国家机关、军事机关、国家通讯社、广播电台、电视台、外国驻华使领馆等单位附近设置临时警戒线；  
（五）法律、行政法规和国务院规定的其他必要措施。

**Article 74** After a social security incident occurs, the people's government that organizes the response shall immediately activate the emergency response, organize relevant departments to take, based on the nature and characteristics of the incident and in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant State regulations, one or more of the following emergency response measures:  
(1) forcibly isolate the parties who confront each other using instruments or participate in the conflict with violent acts, properly resolve on-site disputes and conflicts, and control the development of the situation;  
(2) control buildings, means of transport, equipment, facilities, and the supply of fuel, gas, electricity, and water within a specific area;  
(3) seal off relevant places and roads, inspect the identity documents of persons on site, and restrict activities in relevant public places;  
(4) strengthen the guarding of core organs and units vulnerable to attack, and set up temporary cordons near State organs, military organs, the State news agency, radio stations, television stations, foreign diplomatic missions in China, and other units;  
(5) other necessary measures as provided for by laws, administrative regulations, and the State Council.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　发生突发事件，严重影响国民经济正常运行时，国务院或者国务院授权的有关主管部门可以采取保障、控制等必要的应急措施，保障人民群众的基本生活需要，最大限度地减轻突发事件的影响。

**Article 75** When an emergency occurs that severely affects the normal operation of the national economy, the State Council or the relevant competent department authorized by the State Council may take necessary emergency measures such as safeguards and controls to ensure the basic living needs of the people and minimize the impact of the emergency.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府及其有关部门，必要时可以向单位和个人征用应急救援所需设备、设施、场地、交通工具和其他物资，请求其他地方人民政府及其有关部门提供人力、物力、财力或者技术支援，要求生产、供应生活必需品和应急救援物资的企业组织生产、保证供给，要求提供医疗、交通等公共服务的组织提供相应的服务。  
履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府和有关主管部门，应当组织协调运输经营单位，优先运送处置突发事件所需物资、设备、工具、应急救援人员和受到突发事件危害的人员。  
履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府及其有关部门，应当为受突发事件影响无人照料的无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人提供及时有效帮助；建立健全联系帮扶应急救援人员家庭制度，帮助解决实际困难。

**Article 76** The people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency, and its relevant departments, may, when necessary, requisition from units and individuals the equipment, facilities, sites, means of transport, and other materials needed for emergency rescue, request other local people's governments and their relevant departments to provide human, material, financial, or technical support, require enterprises that produce and supply daily necessities and emergency rescue materials to organize production and ensure supply, and require organizations providing public services such as medical care and transportation to provide corresponding services.  
The people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency, and the relevant competent departments, shall organize and coordinate with transportation operators to give priority to the transport of materials, equipment, tools, emergency rescue personnel, and persons affected by the emergency that are needed for response.  
The people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency, and its relevant departments, shall provide timely and effective assistance to persons with no or limited capacity for civil conduct who are left unattended due to the emergency; establish and improve a system for contacting and assisting the families of emergency rescue personnel, and help solve practical difficulties.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　突发事件发生地的居民委员会、村民委员会和其他组织应当按照当地人民政府的决定、命令，进行宣传动员，组织群众开展自救与互救，协助维护社会秩序；情况紧急的，应当立即组织群众开展自救与互救等先期处置工作。

**Article 77** Residents' committees, villagers' committees, and other organizations in the place where the emergency occurs shall, in accordance with the decisions and orders of the local people's government, conduct publicity and mobilization, organize the masses to carry out self-rescue and mutual rescue, and assist in maintaining social order; in urgent situations, they shall immediately organize the masses to carry out preliminary response operations such as self-rescue and mutual rescue.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　受到自然灾害危害或者发生事故灾难、公共卫生事件的单位，应当立即组织本单位应急救援队伍和工作人员营救受害人员，疏散、撤离、安置受到威胁的人员，控制危险源，标明危险区域，封锁危险场所，并采取其他防止危害扩大的必要措施，同时向所在地县级人民政府报告；对因本单位的问题引发的或者主体是本单位人员的社会安全事件，有关单位应当按照规定上报情况，并迅速派出负责人赶赴现场开展劝解、疏导工作。  
突发事件发生地的其他单位应当服从人民政府发布的决定、命令，配合人民政府采取的应急处置措施，做好本单位的应急救援工作，并积极组织人员参加所在地的应急救援和处置工作。

**Article 78** A unit affected by a natural disaster or where an accident disaster or public health incident occurs shall immediately organize its emergency rescue teams and staff to rescue victims, evacuate, remove, and shelter threatened persons, control sources of danger, mark dangerous areas, seal off dangerous places, and take other necessary measures to prevent the escalation of harm, and simultaneously report to the local people's government at the county level; for a social security incident triggered by its own problems or predominantly involving its own personnel, the relevant unit shall report the situation in accordance with regulations, and promptly dispatch responsible persons to the scene to carry out persuasion and mediation.  
Other units in the place where the emergency occurs shall obey the decisions and orders issued by the people's government, cooperate with the emergency response measures taken by the people's government, carry out their own emergency rescue work, and actively organize personnel to participate in local emergency rescue and response operations.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　突发事件发生地的个人应当依法服从人民政府、居民委员会、村民委员会或者所属单位的指挥和安排，配合人民政府采取的应急处置措施，积极参加应急救援工作，协助维护社会秩序。

**Article 79** Individuals in the place where the emergency occurs shall, according to law, obey the command and arrangements of the people's government, residents' committees, villagers' committees, or their own units, cooperate with the emergency response measures taken by the people's government, actively participate in emergency rescue work, and assist in maintaining social order.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国家支持城乡社区组织健全应急工作机制，强化城乡社区综合服务设施和信息平台应急功能，加强与突发事件信息系统数据共享，增强突发事件应急处置中保障群众基本生活和服务群众能力。

**Article 80** The State supports urban and rural community organizations in improving emergency working mechanisms, strengthening the emergency functions of comprehensive community service facilities and information platforms, enhancing data sharing with emergency information systems, and improving the capacity to ensure the basic living needs of the people and serve the people during emergency response.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　国家采取措施，加强心理健康服务体系和人才队伍建设，支持引导心理健康服务人员和社会工作者对受突发事件影响的各类人群开展心理健康教育、心理评估、心理疏导、心理危机干预、心理行为问题诊治等心理援助工作。

**Article 81** The State adopts measures to strengthen the mental health service system and the development of a talent team, and supports and guides mental health service personnel and social workers to carry out psychological assistance work, including mental health education, psychological assessment, psychological counseling, crisis intervention, and diagnosis and treatment of psychological and behavioral issues, for various groups of people affected by emergencies.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　对于突发事件遇难人员的遗体，应当按照法律和国家有关规定，科学规范处置，加强卫生防疫，维护逝者尊严。对于逝者的遗物应当妥善保管。

**Article 82** The remains of persons who died in an emergency shall be handled scientifically and properly in accordance with laws and relevant State regulations, with enhanced hygiene and epidemic prevention, and the dignity of the deceased shall be maintained. The belongings of the deceased shall be properly kept.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门根据突发事件应对工作需要，在履行法定职责所必需的范围和限度内，可以要求公民、法人和其他组织提供应急处置与救援需要的信息。公民、法人和其他组织应当予以提供，法律另有规定的除外。县级以上人民政府及其有关部门对获取的相关信息，应当严格保密，并依法保护公民的通信自由和通信秘密。

**Article 83** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments may, based on the needs of emergency response, within the scope and limits necessary for the performance of their statutory duties, require citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to provide information needed for emergency response and rescue. Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall provide such information, unless otherwise provided by law. The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall strictly keep confidential the information obtained, and protect citizens' freedom and secrecy of communication according to law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　在突发事件应急处置中，有关单位和个人因依照本法规定配合突发事件应对工作或者履行相关义务，需要获取他人个人信息的，应当依照法律规定的程序和方式取得并确保信息安全，不得非法收集、使用、加工、传输他人个人信息，不得非法买卖、提供或者公开他人个人信息。

**Article 84** In the emergency response to emergencies, if relevant units and individuals, for the purpose of cooperating with emergency response or performing related obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law, need to obtain others' personal information, they shall obtain it in accordance with the procedures and methods prescribed by law and ensure information security, and shall not illegally collect, use, process, or transmit others' personal information, nor illegally buy, sell, provide, or disclose others' personal information.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　因依法履行突发事件应对工作职责或者义务获取的个人信息，只能用于突发事件应对，并在突发事件应对工作结束后予以销毁。确因依法作为证据使用或者调查评估需要留存或者延期销毁的，应当按照规定进行合法性、必要性、安全性评估，并采取相应保护和处理措施，严格依法使用。

**Article 85** Personal information obtained due to the performance of duties or obligations for emergency response according to law shall only be used for emergency response, and shall be destroyed after the emergency response work ends. If such information truly needs to be retained or its destruction deferred for use as evidence or for investigation and assessment according to law, a legality, necessity, and security assessment shall be conducted in accordance with regulations, and corresponding protection and processing measures shall be taken, and it shall be used strictly according to law.

### 第六章　事后恢复与重建

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　突发事件的威胁和危害得到控制或者消除后，履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府应当宣布解除应急响应，停止执行依照本法规定采取的应急处置措施，同时采取或者继续实施必要措施，防止发生自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件的次生、衍生事件或者重新引发社会安全事件，组织受影响地区尽快恢复社会秩序。

**Article 86** After the threat and harm of the emergency are controlled or eliminated, the people's government that exercises unified leadership or organizes the response to the emergency shall declare the lifting of the emergency response, cease the implementation of emergency response measures taken in accordance with the provisions of this Law, and at the same time take or continue to implement necessary measures to prevent the occurrence of secondary or derivative incidents of natural disasters, accident disasters, or public health incidents, or the re-triggering of social security incidents, and organize the affected areas to restore social order as soon as possible.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　突发事件应急处置工作结束后，履行统一领导职责的人民政府应当立即组织对突发事件造成的影响和损失进行调查评估，制定恢复重建计划，并向上一级人民政府报告。  
受突发事件影响地区的人民政府应当及时组织和协调应急管理、卫生健康、公安、交通、铁路、民航、邮政、电信、建设、生态环境、水利、能源、广播电视等有关部门恢复社会秩序，尽快修复被损坏的交通、通信、供水、排水、供电、供气、供热、医疗卫生、水利、广播电视等公共设施。

**Article 87** After the emergency response work ends, the people's government that exercised unified leadership shall immediately organize an investigation and assessment of the impact and losses caused by the emergency, formulate a recovery and reconstruction plan, and report to the people's government at the next higher level.  
The people's government of the area affected by the emergency shall promptly organize and coordinate relevant departments such as emergency management, health, public security, transportation, railways, civil aviation, postal services, telecommunications, construction, ecology and environment, water resources, energy, and radio and television to restore social order, and repair damaged public facilities such as transportation, communications, water supply, drainage, electricity, gas, heating, medical and health services, water resources, and radio and television as soon as possible.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　受突发事件影响地区的人民政府开展恢复重建工作需要上一级人民政府支持的，可以向上一级人民政府提出请求。上一级人民政府应当根据受影响地区遭受的损失和实际情况，提供资金、物资支持和技术指导，组织协调其他地区和有关方面提供资金、物资和人力支援。

**Article 88** If the people's government of the area affected by the emergency needs support from the people's government at the next higher level for recovery and reconstruction, it may submit a request to the people's government at the next higher level. The people's government at the next higher level shall, based on the losses suffered and the actual situation of the affected area, provide financial and material support and technical guidance, and organize and coordinate other regions and relevant parties to provide financial, material, and human resources support.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　国务院根据受突发事件影响地区遭受损失的情况，制定扶持该地区有关行业发展的优惠政策。  
受突发事件影响地区的人民政府应当根据本地区遭受的损失和采取应急处置措施的情况，制定救助、补偿、抚慰、抚恤、安置等善后工作计划并组织实施，妥善解决因处置突发事件引发的矛盾纠纷。

**Article 89** The State Council shall, based on the losses suffered by the area affected by the emergency, formulate preferential policies to support the development of relevant industries in that area.  
The people's government of the area affected by the emergency shall, based on the losses suffered and the emergency response measures taken, formulate and organize the implementation of plans for post-incident work such as relief, compensation, consolation, pensions, and resettlement, and properly resolve disputes and conflicts arising from the emergency response.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　公民参加应急救援工作或者协助维护社会秩序期间，其所在单位应当保证其工资待遇和福利不变，并可以按照规定给予相应补助。

**Article 90** During the period when a citizen participates in emergency rescue work or assists in maintaining social order, the unit to which he or she belongs shall ensure that his or her wages, benefits, and welfare remain unchanged, and may provide corresponding subsidies in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　县级以上人民政府对在应急救援工作中伤亡的人员依法落实工伤待遇、抚恤或者其他保障政策，并组织做好应急救援工作中致病人员的医疗救治工作。

**Article 91** People's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, implement work-related injury benefits, pensions, or other security policies for persons who are killed or injured in emergency rescue work, and organize the medical treatment of persons who become ill during emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　履行统一领导职责的人民政府在突发事件应对工作结束后，应当及时查明突发事件的发生经过和原因，总结突发事件应急处置工作的经验教训，制定改进措施，并向上一级人民政府提出报告。

**Article 92** After the emergency response work ends, the people's government that exercised unified leadership shall promptly ascertain the process and causes of the emergency, summarize the experiences and lessons learned from the emergency response, formulate improvement measures, and submit a report to the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　突发事件应对工作中有关资金、物资的筹集、管理、分配、拨付和使用等情况，应当依法接受审计机关的审计监督。

**Article 93** The raising, management, distribution, allocation, and use of relevant funds and materials in emergency response work shall, according to law, be subject to audit supervision by audit authorities.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　国家档案主管部门应当建立健全突发事件应对工作相关档案收集、整理、保护、利用工作机制。突发事件应对工作中形成的材料，应当按照国家规定归档，并向相关档案馆移交。

**Article 94** The national archives administrative department shall establish and improve a working mechanism for the collection, organization, protection, and utilization of archives related to emergency response work. Materials formed during emergency response work shall be archived in accordance with State regulations and transferred to relevant archives.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门违反本法规定，不履行或者不正确履行法定职责的，由其上级行政机关责令改正；有下列情形之一，由有关机关综合考虑突发事件发生的原因、后果、应对处置情况、行为人过错等因素，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未按照规定采取预防措施，导致发生突发事件，或者未采取必要的防范措施，导致发生次生、衍生事件的；  
（二）迟报、谎报、瞒报、漏报或者授意他人迟报、谎报、瞒报以及阻碍他人报告有关突发事件的信息，或者通报、报送、公布虚假信息，造成后果的；  
（三）未按照规定及时发布突发事件警报、采取预警期的措施，导致损害发生的；  
（四）未按照规定及时采取措施处置突发事件或者处置不当，造成后果的；  
（五）违反法律规定采取应对措施，侵犯公民生命健康权益的；  
（六）不服从上级人民政府对突发事件应急处置工作的统一领导、指挥和协调的；  
（七）未及时组织开展生产自救、恢复重建等善后工作的；  
（八）截留、挪用、私分或者变相私分应急救援资金、物资的；  
（九）不及时归还征用的单位和个人的财产，或者对被征用财产的单位和个人不按照规定给予补偿的。

**Article 95** If a local people's government at any level or a relevant department of a people's government at or above the county level violates the provisions of this Law by failing to perform or incorrectly performing its statutory duties, the administrative organ at the higher level shall order it to make corrections; if any of the following circumstances occurs, the relevant organ shall, after comprehensively considering factors such as the cause, consequences, response and handling of the emergency, and the fault of the actor, impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law:  
(1) failing to take preventive measures in accordance with regulations, resulting in the occurrence of an emergency, or failing to take necessary preventive measures, resulting in the occurrence of secondary or derivative incidents;  
(2) delaying, falsifying, concealing, or omitting reporting, or instructing others to delay, falsify, or conceal reporting, or obstructing others from reporting information on the emergency, or reporting, submitting, or publishing false information, resulting in consequences;  
(3) failing to promptly issue an emergency warning or take measures during the warning period in accordance with regulations, resulting in damage;  
(4) failing to promptly take measures to respond to an emergency in accordance with regulations or responding improperly, resulting in consequences;  
(5) taking response measures in violation of legal provisions, infringing upon the life, health, or lawful rights and interests of citizens;  
(6) disobeying the unified leadership, command, and coordination of the people's government at the higher level for emergency response work;  
(7) failing to promptly organize post-incident work such as production self-rescue, recovery, and reconstruction;  
(8) withholding, misappropriating, privately dividing, or privately dividing in disguised form emergency rescue funds or materials;  
(9) failing to promptly return requisitioned property of units or individuals, or failing to provide compensation according to regulations to units or individuals whose property has been requisitioned.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　有关单位有下列情形之一，由所在地履行统一领导职责的人民政府有关部门责令停产停业，暂扣或者吊销许可证件，并处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节特别严重的，并处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定采取预防措施，导致发生较大以上突发事件的；  
（二）未及时消除已发现的可能引发突发事件的隐患，导致发生较大以上突发事件的；  
（三）未做好应急物资储备和应急设备、设施日常维护、检测工作，导致发生较大以上突发事件或者突发事件危害扩大的；  
（四）突发事件发生后，不及时组织开展应急救援工作，造成严重后果的。  
其他法律对前款行为规定了处罚的，依照较重的规定处罚。

**Article 96** If a relevant unit has any of the following circumstances, the relevant department of the people's government that exercises unified leadership in the place where it is located shall order the suspension of production or business, temporarily seize or revoke the permit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are particularly serious, it shall also impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to take preventive measures in accordance with regulations, resulting in the occurrence of a relatively large or above emergency;  
(2) failing to promptly eliminate discovered hidden dangers that may trigger an emergency, resulting in the occurrence of a relatively large or above emergency;  
(3) failing to properly carry out the stockpiling of emergency materials and the daily maintenance and inspection of emergency equipment and facilities, resulting in the occurrence of a relatively large or above emergency or the escalation of the harm of an emergency;  
(4) failing to promptly organize emergency rescue work after the occurrence of an emergency, resulting in serious consequences.  
If other laws provide for penalties for the acts mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the heavier penalty shall apply.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　违反本法规定，编造并传播有关突发事件的虚假信息，或者明知是有关突发事件的虚假信息而进行传播的，责令改正，给予警告；造成严重后果的，依法暂停其业务活动或者吊销其许可证件；负有直接责任的人员是公职人员的，还应当依法给予处分。

**Article 97** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fabricates and disseminates false information about an emergency, or disseminates such false information knowing it to be false, shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning; if serious consequences are caused, his or her business activities shall be suspended or his or her permit shall be revoked according to law; if the person directly responsible is a public official, he or she shall also be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　单位或者个人违反本法规定，不服从所在地人民政府及其有关部门依法发布的决定、命令或者不配合其依法采取的措施的，责令改正；造成严重后果的，依法给予行政处罚；负有直接责任的人员是公职人员的，还应当依法给予处分。

**Article 98** If a unit or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to obey the decisions or orders issued according to law by the local people's government and its relevant departments, or fails to cooperate with the measures taken by them according to law, it or he or she shall be ordered to make corrections; if serious consequences are caused, an administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the person directly responsible is a public official, he or she shall also be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　单位或者个人违反[本法第八十四条](#art-84)、第八十五条关于个人信息保护规定的，由主管部门依照有关法律规定给予处罚。

**Article 99** If a unit or individual violates the provisions on the protection of personal information in Articles 84 and 85 of this Law, the competent department shall impose a penalty in accordance with relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　单位或者个人违反本法规定，导致突发事件发生或者危害扩大，造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 100** If a unit or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes the occurrence of an emergency or the escalation of harm, resulting in personal injury, property damage, or other damage, it or he or she shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　为了使本人或者他人的人身、财产免受正在发生的危险而采取避险措施的，依照《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国刑法》等法律关于紧急避险的规定处理。

**Article 101** Where a person takes avoidance measures to protect the personal safety or property of himself or herself or others from an imminent danger, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions on act of rescue in laws such as the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 102** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administrative penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　发生特别重大突发事件，对人民生命财产安全、国家安全、公共安全、生态环境安全或者社会秩序构成重大威胁，采取本法和其他有关法律、法规、规章规定的应急处置措施不能消除或者有效控制、减轻其严重社会危害，需要进入紧急状态的，由全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者国务院依照宪法和其他有关法律规定的权限和程序决定。  
紧急状态期间采取的非常措施，依照有关法律规定执行或者由全国人民代表大会常务委员会另行规定。

**Article 103** When an extraordinarily serious emergency occurs, posing a major threat to the safety of people's lives and property, national security, public security, ecological environmental security, or social order, and the emergency response measures provided for in this Law and other relevant laws, regulations, and rules cannot eliminate or effectively control or mitigate its serious social harm, and it is necessary to enter a state of emergency, the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the State Council shall decide in accordance with the authority and procedures prescribed by the Constitution and other relevant laws.  
Extraordinary measures taken during the period of a state of emergency shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws or shall be otherwise prescribed by the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　中华人民共和国领域外发生突发事件，造成或者可能造成中华人民共和国公民、法人和其他组织人身伤亡、财产损失的，由国务院外交部门会同国务院其他有关部门、有关地方人民政府，按照国家有关规定做好应对工作。

**Article 104** Where an emergency occurs outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, causing or likely to cause personal injury or death or property damage to citizens, legal persons, and other organizations of the People's Republic of China, the foreign affairs department of the State Council shall, together with other relevant departments of the State Council and relevant local people's governments, properly handle the response work in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　在中华人民共和国境内的外国人、无国籍人应当遵守本法，服从所在地人民政府及其有关部门依法发布的决定、命令，并配合其依法采取的措施。

**Article 105** Foreigners and stateless persons within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with this Law, obey the decisions and orders lawfully issued by the local people's government and its relevant departments, and cooperate with the measures lawfully taken by them.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　本法自2024年11月1日起施行。

**Article 106** This Law shall come into force as of November 1, 2024.
