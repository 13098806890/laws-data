# 中华人民共和国香港特别行政区驻军法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：1996-12-30  
**生效日期**：1997-07-01

> （1996年12月30日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　香港驻军的职责 第三章　香港驻军与香港特别行政区政府的关系 第四章　香港驻军人员的义务与纪律 第五章　香港驻军人员的司法管辖 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障中央人民政府派驻香港特别行政区负责防务的军队依法履行职责，维护国家的主权、统一、领土完整和香港的安全，根据宪法和香港特别行政区基本法，制定本法。 第二条　中央人民政府派驻香港特别行政区负责防务的军队，由中国人民解放军陆军、海军、空军部队组成，称中国人民解放军驻香港部队（以下称香港驻军）。 第三条　香港驻军由中华人民共和国中央军事委员会领导，其员额根据香港特别行政区防务的需要确定。 香港驻军实行人员轮换制度。 第四条　香港驻军费用由中央人民政府负担。 第二章　香港驻军的职责

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障中央人民政府派驻香港特别行政区负责防务的军队依法履行职责，维护国家的主权、统一、领土完整和香港的安全，根据宪法和香港特别行政区基本法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution and the Basic Law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, for the purposes of ensuring that the forces stationed by the Central People's Government in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for the purpose of defense perform their duties according to law, and safeguarding the sovereignty, unity, territorial integrity and security of the State and Hong Kong.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中央人民政府派驻香港特别行政区负责防务的军队，由中国人民解放军陆军、海军、空军部队组成，称中国人民解放军驻香港部队（以下称香港驻军）。

**Article 2** The forces stationed by the Central People's Government in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for the purpose of defense shall be composed of units of the Army, Navy and Air Force of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, and shall be known as the Chinese People's Liberation Army Hong Kong Garrison (hereinafter referred to as the Hong Kong Garrison).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　香港驻军由中华人民共和国中央军事委员会领导，其员额根据香港特别行政区防务的需要确定。  
香港驻军实行人员轮换制度。

**Article 3** The Hong Kong Garrison shall be led by the Central Military Commission of the People's Republic of China, and its strength shall be determined based on the requirements of defense of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
A system of rotation of personnel shall be implemented in the Hong Kong Garrison.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　香港驻军费用由中央人民政府负担。

**Article 4** The expenses of the Hong Kong Garrison shall be borne by the Central People's Government.

### 第二章　香港驻军的职责

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　香港驻军履行下列防务职责：  
（一）防备和抵抗侵略，保卫香港特别行政区的安全；  
（二）担负防卫勤务；  
（三）管理军事设施；  
（四）承办有关的涉外军事事宜。

**Article 5** The Hong Kong Garrison shall perform the following defense duties:  
(1) preparing against and resisting aggression, and safeguarding the security of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(2) undertaking defense duties;  
(3) managing military facilities; and (4) handling matters concerning foreign military affairs.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定宣布战争状态或者因香港特别行政区内发生香港特别行政区政府不能控制的危及国家统一或者安全的动乱而决定香港特别行政区进入紧急状态时，香港驻军根据中央人民政府决定在香港特别行政区实施的全国性法律的规定履行职责。

**Article 6** When the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decides to declare a state of war, or, by reason of the occurrence within the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of a turmoil endangering national unity or security which is beyond the control of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, decides that the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is in a state of emergency, the Hong Kong Garrison shall perform its duties in accordance with the provisions of the national laws which, as decided by the Central People's Government, shall be implemented in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　香港驻军的飞行器、舰船等武器装备和物资以及持有香港驻军制发的证件或者证明文件的执行职务的人员和车辆，不受香港特别行政区执法人员检查、搜查和扣押。  
香港驻军和香港驻军人员并享有在香港特别行政区实施的法律规定的其他权利和豁免。

**Article 7** The weaponry, equipment and materials such as aircraft and vessels of the Hong Kong Garrison, and the personnel and vehicles performing duties who hold certificates or identification documents issued by the Hong Kong Garrison, shall not be subject to inspection, search or seizure by law enforcement personnel of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
The Hong Kong Garrison and its personnel shall also enjoy other rights and immunities provided for by laws implemented in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　香港驻军人员对妨碍其执行职务的行为，可以依照在香港特别行政区实施的法律的规定采取措施予以制止。

**Article 8** Personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison may, in accordance with the provisions of laws implemented in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, take measures to stop any act that obstructs the performance of their duties.

### 第三章　香港驻军与香港特别行政区政府的关系

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　香港驻军不干预香港特别行政区的地方事务。

**Article 9** The Hong Kong Garrison shall not interfere in the local affairs of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　香港特别行政区政府应当支持香港驻军履行防务职责，保障香港驻军和香港驻军人员的合法权益。  
香港特别行政区制定政策和拟定法案，涉及香港驻军的，应当征求香港驻军的意见。

**Article 10** The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall support the Hong Kong Garrison in performing its defense duties, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the Hong Kong Garrison and its personnel.  
When the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region formulates policies or drafts legislation concerning the Hong Kong Garrison, it shall consult the Hong Kong Garrison for its opinions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　香港驻军进行训练、演习等军事活动，涉及香港特别行政区公共利益的，应当事先通报香港特别行政区政府。

**Article 11** Where military activities such as training or exercises conducted by the Hong Kong Garrison involve the public interest of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Hong Kong Garrison shall notify the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in advance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　香港驻军和香港特别行政区政府共同保护香港特别行政区内的军事设施。  
香港驻军会同香港特别行政区政府划定军事禁区。军事禁区的位置、范围由香港特别行政区政府宣布。  
香港特别行政区政府应当协助香港驻军维护军事禁区的安全。  
香港驻军以外的人员、车辆、船舶和飞行器未经香港驻军最高指挥官或者其授权的军官批准，不得进入军事禁区。军事禁区的警卫人员有权依法制止擅自进入军事禁区和破坏、危害军事设施的行为。  
香港驻军对军事禁区内的自然资源、文物古迹以及非军事权益，应当依照香港特别行政区的法律予以保护。

**Article 12** The Hong Kong Garrison and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall jointly protect the military facilities within the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. The Hong Kong Garrison shall, jointly with the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, designate military restricted zones. The locations and scope of military restricted zones shall be announced by the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall assist the Hong Kong Garrison in maintaining the security of military restricted zones. No person, vehicle, vessel or aircraft other than those of the Hong Kong Garrison may enter a military restricted zone without the approval of the highest commander of the Hong Kong Garrison or an officer authorized by him. The guards of military restricted zones shall have the authority, in accordance with the law, to stop any unauthorized entry into a military restricted zone or any act that destroys or endangers military facilities. The Hong Kong Garrison shall, in accordance with the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, protect natural resources, cultural relics and historical sites, and non-military rights and interests within military restricted zones.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　香港驻军的军事用地，经中央人民政府批准不再用于防务目的的，无偿交由香港特别行政区政府处理。  
香港特别行政区政府如需将香港驻军的部分军事用地用于公共用途，必须经中央人民政府批准；经批准的，香港特别行政区政府应当在中央人民政府同意的地点，为香港驻军重新提供军事用地和军事设施，并负担所有费用。

**Article 13** Where military land of the Hong Kong Garrison is, upon approval by the Central People's Government, no longer to be used for defense purposes, it shall be transferred to the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for disposal without compensation.  
If the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region needs to use part of the military land of the Hong Kong Garrison for public purposes, it must obtain the approval of the Central People's Government; upon approval, the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall, at a location agreed by the Central People's Government, provide the Hong Kong Garrison with replacement military land and military facilities, and bear all expenses.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　香港特别行政区政府根据香港特别行政区基本法的规定，在必要时可以向中央人民政府请求香港驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害。  
香港特别行政区政府的请求经中央人民政府批准后，香港驻军根据中央军事委员会的命令派出部队执行协助维持社会治安和救助灾害的任务，任务完成后即返回驻地。  
香港驻军协助维持社会治安和救助灾害时，在香港特别行政区政府的安排下，由香港驻军最高指挥官或者其授权的军官实施指挥。  
香港驻军人员在协助维持社会治安和救助灾害时，行使香港特别行政区法律规定的权力。

**Article 14** The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region may, when necessary, request the Central People's Government for the assistance of the Hong Kong Garrison in maintaining social order and providing disaster relief, in accordance with the provisions of the Basic Law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
After the request of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is approved by the Central People's Government, the Hong Kong Garrison shall, in accordance with the orders of the Central Military Commission, dispatch troops to carry out tasks of maintaining social order and providing disaster relief, and shall return to their posts immediately after the tasks are completed.  
When the Hong Kong Garrison assists in maintaining social order and providing disaster relief, it shall be under the command of the highest commander of the Hong Kong Garrison or an officer authorized by him, under the arrangements of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.  
When personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison assist in maintaining social order and providing disaster relief, they shall exercise the powers provided for by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　香港驻军和香港特别行政区政府应当建立必要的联系，协商处理与驻军有关的事宜。

**Article 15** The Hong Kong Garrison and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall establish necessary liaison to discuss and handle matters related to the Garrison.

### 第四章　香港驻军人员的义务与纪律

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　香港驻军人员应当履行下列义务：  
（一）忠于祖国，履行职责，维护祖国的安全、荣誉和利益，维护香港的安全；  
（二）遵守全国性的法律和香港特别行政区的法律，遵守军队的纪律；  
（三）尊重香港特别行政区政权机构，尊重香港特别行政区的社会制度和生活方式；  
（四）爱护香港特别行政区的公共财产和香港居民及其他人的私有财产；  
（五）遵守社会公德，讲究文明礼貌。

**Article 16** Personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) be loyal to the motherland, perform their duties, safeguard the security, honor and interests of the motherland, and safeguard the security of Hong Kong;  
(2) abide by the national laws and the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, and abide by the military discipline;  
(3) respect the organs of political power of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, and respect the social system and lifestyle of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(4) protect public property of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the private property of Hong Kong residents and other persons; and (5) observe social morality and be civilized and courteous.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　香港驻军人员不得参加香港的政治组织、宗教组织和社会团体。

**Article 17** Personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison shall not join political organizations, religious organizations or social organizations in Hong Kong.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　香港驻军和香港驻军人员不得以任何形式从事营利性经营活动。香港驻军人员并不得从事与军人职责不相称的其他任何活动。

**Article 18** The Hong Kong Garrison and its personnel shall not engage in any form of profit-making business activities. Personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison shall not engage in any other activities incompatible with the duties of a serviceman.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　香港驻军人员违反全国性的法律和香港特别行政区的法律的，依法追究法律责任。  
香港驻军人员违反军队纪律的，给予纪律处分。

**Article 19** Where a person of the Hong Kong Garrison violates the national laws or the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, legal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person of the Hong Kong Garrison violates military discipline, a disciplinary sanction shall be imposed.

### 第五章　香港驻军人员的司法管辖

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　香港驻军人员犯罪的案件由军事司法机关管辖；但是，香港驻军人员非执行职务的行为，侵犯香港居民、香港驻军以外的其他人的人身权、财产权以及其他违反香港特别行政区法律构成犯罪的案件，由香港特别行政区法院以及有关的执法机关管辖。  
军事司法机关和香港特别行政区法院以及有关的执法机关对各自管辖的香港驻军人员犯罪的案件，如果认为由对方管辖更为适宜，经双方协商一致后，可以移交对方管辖。  
军事司法机关管辖的香港驻军人员犯罪的案件中，涉及的被告人中的香港居民、香港驻军人员以外的其他人，由香港特别行政区法院审判。

**Article 20** Cases of crimes committed by personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison shall be under the jurisdiction of the military judicial authorities; however, cases of crimes committed by personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison through acts not performed in the course of their duties, which infringe upon the personal or property rights of Hong Kong residents or other persons not belonging to the Hong Kong Garrison, or which violate other laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and constitute crimes, shall be under the jurisdiction of the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the relevant law enforcement organs.  
Where the military judicial authorities and the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the relevant law enforcement organs consider that a case of a crime committed by a person of the Hong Kong Garrison within their respective jurisdiction is more suitably tried by the other party, they may, upon mutual consultation and agreement, transfer the case to the other party for jurisdiction.  
In cases of crimes committed by personnel of the Hong Kong Garrison under the jurisdiction of the military judicial authorities, where the accused include a Hong Kong resident or a person other than a member of the Hong Kong Garrison, such accused persons shall be tried by a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　香港特别行政区执法人员依法拘捕的涉嫌犯罪的人员，查明是香港驻军人员的，应当移交香港驻军羁押。被羁押的人员所涉及的案件，依照[本法第二十条](#art-20)的规定确定管辖。

**Article 21** Where a person suspected of a crime who is arrested according to law by law enforcement personnel of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is found to be a member of the Hong Kong Garrison, he or she shall be handed over to the Hong Kong Garrison for custody. The jurisdiction over the case involving the person in custody shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article 20 of this Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　香港驻军人员被香港特别行政区法院判处剥夺或者限制人身自由的刑罚的，依照香港特别行政区的法律规定送交执行；但是，香港特别行政区有关执法机关与军事司法机关对执行的地点另行协商确定的除外。

**Article 22** Where a member of the Hong Kong Garrison is sentenced by the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to a penalty of deprivation or restriction of personal freedom, the execution shall be carried out in accordance with the legal provisions of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; except that the relevant law enforcement organ of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the military judicial authority separately agree through consultation on the place of execution.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　香港驻军人员违反香港特别行政区的法律，侵害香港居民、香港驻军以外的其他人的民事权利的，当事人可以通过协商、调解解决；不愿通过协商、调解解决或者协商、调解不成的，被侵权人可以向法院提起诉讼。香港驻军人员非执行职务的行为引起的民事侵权案件，由香港特别行政区法院管辖；执行职务的行为引起的民事侵权案件，由中华人民共和国最高人民法院管辖，侵权行为的损害赔偿适用香港特别行政区法律。

**Article 23** Where a member of the Hong Kong Garrison violates the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and infringes upon the civil rights of Hong Kong residents or other persons other than the Hong Kong Garrison, the parties may resolve the matter through consultation or mediation; if the parties are unwilling to resolve it through consultation or mediation, or if consultation or mediation fails, the infringed party may file a lawsuit in a court. Civil infringement cases caused by acts not in the performance of duties by members of the Hong Kong Garrison shall be under the jurisdiction of the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; civil infringement cases caused by acts in the performance of duties shall be under the jurisdiction of the Supreme People's Court of the People's Republic of China, and the compensation for damages arising from the infringing acts shall be governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　香港驻军的机关或者单位在香港特别行政区与香港居民、香港驻军以外的其他人发生合同纠纷时，当事人可以通过协商、调解解决。当事人不愿通过协商、调解解决或者协商、调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，向仲裁机构申请仲裁。当事人没有在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向香港特别行政区法院提起诉讼；但是，当事人对提起诉讼的法院另有约定的除外。

**Article 24** Where an organ or unit of the Hong Kong Garrison has a contract dispute with Hong Kong residents or other persons other than the Hong Kong Garrison in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the parties may resolve it through consultation or mediation. If the parties are unwilling to resolve it through consultation or mediation, or if consultation or mediation fails, they may apply for arbitration to an arbitration institution in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards. If the parties have not included an arbitration clause in the contract and have not reached a written arbitration agreement afterwards, they may file a lawsuit in the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; except that the parties have otherwise agreed on the court for filing the lawsuit.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在香港特别行政区法院的诉讼活动中，香港驻军对香港驻军人员身份、执行职务的行为等事实发出的证明文件为有效证据。但是，相反证据成立的除外。

**Article 25** In litigation proceedings in the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the certification documents issued by the Hong Kong Garrison regarding facts such as the identity of a member of the Hong Kong Garrison or acts in the performance of duties shall be valid evidence, except where evidence to the contrary has been established.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　香港驻军的国防等国家行为不受香港特别行政区法院管辖。

**Article 26** National acts such as national defense by the Hong Kong Garrison shall not be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　香港特别行政区法院作出的判决、裁定涉及香港驻军的机关或者单位的财产执行的，香港驻军的机关或者单位必须履行；但是，香港特别行政区法院不得对香港驻军的武器装备、物资和其他财产实施强制执行。

**Article 27** Where a judgment or ruling made by a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region involves the enforcement of property of an organ or unit of the Hong Kong Garrison, such organ or unit must comply; however, the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall not enforce compulsory execution against the weapons, equipment, materials, or other property of the Hong Kong Garrison.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　军事司法机关可以与香港特别行政区法院和有关执法机关通过协商进行司法方面的联系和相互提供协助。

**Article 28** Military judicial authorities may, through consultation with the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and relevant law enforcement organs, maintain judicial liaison and provide mutual assistance.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本法的解释权属于全国人民代表大会常务委员会。

**Article 29** The power to interpret this Law shall vest in the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法自1997年7月1日起施行。

**Article 30** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 1997.
