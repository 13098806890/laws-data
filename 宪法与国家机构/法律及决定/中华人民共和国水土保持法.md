# 中华人民共和国水土保持法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2010-12-25  
**生效日期**：2011-03-01

> （1991年6月29日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正　2010年12月25日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十八次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　规　　划 第三章　预　　防 第四章　治　　理 第五章　监测和监督 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和治理水土流失，保护和合理利用水土资源，减轻水、旱、风沙灾害，改善生态环境，保障经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事水土保持活动，应当遵守本法。 本法所称水土保持，是指对自然因素和人为活动造成水土流失所采取的预防和治理措施。 第三条　水土保持工作实行预防为主、保护优先、全面规划、综合治理、因地制宜、突出重点、科学管理、注重效益的方针。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对水土保持工作的统一领导，将水土保持工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，对水土保持规划确定的任务，安排专项资金，并组织实施。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和治理水土流失，保护和合理利用水土资源，减轻水、旱、风沙灾害，改善生态环境，保障经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of preventing and controlling soil erosion, protecting and rationally utilizing soil and water resources, mitigating hazards from floods, droughts, wind and sand, improving ecological environment, and ensuring sustainable development of economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事水土保持活动，应当遵守本法。  
本法所称水土保持，是指对自然因素和人为活动造成水土流失所采取的预防和治理措施。

**Article 2** Any activity related to soil and water conservation conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with this Law.  
For the purpose of this Law, "soil and water conservation" refers to the prevention and control measures taken against soil erosion caused by natural factors and human activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　水土保持工作实行预防为主、保护优先、全面规划、综合治理、因地制宜、突出重点、科学管理、注重效益的方针。

**Article 3** The work of soil and water conservation shall follow the policy of giving priority to prevention, protecting first, overall planning, comprehensive control, adapting measures to local conditions, highlighting key points, scientific management, and emphasizing effectiveness.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对水土保持工作的统一领导，将水土保持工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，对水土保持规划确定的任务，安排专项资金，并组织实施。  
国家在水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区，实行地方各级人民政府水土保持目标责任制和考核奖惩制度。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen unified leadership over soil and water conservation work, incorporate soil and water conservation into their national economic and social development plans, allocate special funds for tasks identified in soil and water conservation plans, and organize implementation.  
The State shall implement a target responsibility system for soil and water conservation and an assessment, reward and punishment system for local people's governments at all levels in key prevention areas and key control areas of soil erosion.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院水行政主管部门主管全国的水土保持工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构（以下简称流域管理机构），在所管辖范围内依法承担水土保持监督管理职责。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门主管本行政区域的水土保持工作。  
县级以上人民政府林业、农业、国土资源等有关部门按照各自职责，做好有关的水土流失预防和治理工作。

**Article 5** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of soil and water conservation nationwide.  
The river basin management agencies (hereinafter referred to as river basin management agencies) established by the water administrative department of the State Council in major rivers and lakes designated by the State shall, within their jurisdiction, perform the supervision and management duties for soil and water conservation according to law.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of soil and water conservation within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of forestry, agriculture, land and resources, etc., of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions, properly carry out the prevention and control of soil erosion.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强水土保持宣传和教育工作，普及水土保持科学知识，增强公众的水土保持意识。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall strengthen publicity and education on soil and water conservation, popularize scientific knowledge of soil and water conservation, and enhance public awareness of soil and water conservation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和支持水土保持科学技术研究，提高水土保持科学技术水平，推广先进的水土保持技术，培养水土保持科学技术人才。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on soil and water conservation, improves the level of science and technology in soil and water conservation, promotes advanced soil and water conservation technologies, and cultivates scientific and technological talents for soil and water conservation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人都有保护水土资源、预防和治理水土流失的义务，并有权对破坏水土资源、造成水土流失的行为进行举报。

**Article 8** Any unit or individual has the obligation to protect soil and water resources, prevent and control soil erosion, and has the right to report any act that destroys soil and water resources or causes soil erosion.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持社会力量参与水土保持工作。  
对水土保持工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports social forces to participate in soil and water conservation work.  
Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in soil and water conservation work shall be commended and rewarded by people's governments at or above the county level.

### 第二章　规划

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　水土保持规划应当在水土流失调查结果及水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区划定的基础上，遵循统筹协调、分类指导的原则编制。

**Article 10** Soil and water conservation plans shall be formulated on the basis of soil erosion survey results and the delimitation of key prevention areas and key control areas for soil erosion, following the principles of overall coordination and classified guidance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院水行政主管部门应当定期组织全国水土流失调查并公告调查结果。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门负责本行政区域的水土流失调查并公告调查结果，公告前应当将调查结果报国务院水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 11** The water administrative department of the State Council shall regularly organize national soil erosion surveys and announce the survey results.  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for soil erosion surveys in their respective administrative regions and announce the survey results; before announcement, they shall report the survey results to the water administrative department of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府应当依据水土流失调查结果划定并公告水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区。  
对水土流失潜在危险较大的区域，应当划定为水土流失重点预防区；对水土流失严重的区域，应当划定为水土流失重点治理区。

**Article 12** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on soil erosion survey results, delimit and announce key prevention areas and key control areas for soil erosion.  
Areas with relatively high potential danger of soil erosion shall be delimited as key prevention areas for soil erosion; areas with severe soil erosion shall be delimited as key control areas for soil erosion.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　水土保持规划的内容应当包括水土流失状况、水土流失类型区划分、水土流失防治目标、任务和措施等。  
水土保持规划包括对流域或者区域预防和治理水土流失、保护和合理利用水土资源作出的整体部署，以及根据整体部署对水土保持专项工作或者特定区域预防和治理水土流失作出的专项部署。  
水土保持规划应当与土地利用总体规划、水资源规划、城乡规划和环境保护规划等相协调。  
编制水土保持规划，应当征求专家和公众的意见。

**Article 13** The contents of a soil and water conservation plan shall include the status of soil erosion, classification of soil erosion types, objectives, tasks, and measures for soil erosion prevention and control.  
A soil and water conservation plan includes an overall deployment for the prevention and control of soil erosion and the protection and rational utilization of soil and water resources for a river basin or region, as well as special deployments for specific soil and water conservation work or for prevention and control of soil erosion in specific areas based on the overall deployment.  
Soil and water conservation plans shall be coordinated with overall land use plans, water resource plans, urban and rural plans, and environmental protection plans.  
When formulating a soil and water conservation plan, opinions of experts and the public shall be solicited.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级人民政府有关部门编制水土保持规划，报本级人民政府或者其授权的部门批准后，由水行政主管部门组织实施。  
水土保持规划一经批准，应当严格执行；经批准的规划根据实际情况需要修改的，应当按照规划编制程序报原批准机关批准。

**Article 14** The water administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the same people's government, formulate a soil and water conservation plan, which shall be implemented by the water administrative department after being approved by the same people's government or its authorized department.  
Once approved, a soil and water conservation plan shall be strictly implemented; if modification is necessary due to actual circumstances, it shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval in accordance with the planning formulation procedures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有关基础设施建设、矿产资源开发、城镇建设、公共服务设施建设等方面的规划，在实施过程中可能造成水土流失的，规划的组织编制机关应当在规划中提出水土流失预防和治理的对策和措施，并在规划报请审批前征求本级人民政府水行政主管部门的意见。

**Article 15** For plans concerning infrastructure construction, mineral resources development, urban construction, public service facility construction, etc., which may cause soil erosion during implementation, the organizing and formulating organs of the plans shall propose countermeasures and measures for soil erosion prevention and control in the plans, and solicit opinions from the water administrative department of the people's government at the same level before submitting the plans for approval.

### 第三章　预防

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方各级人民政府应当按照水土保持规划，采取封育保护、自然修复等措施，组织单位和个人植树种草，扩大林草覆盖面积，涵养水源，预防和减轻水土流失。

**Article 16** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with soil and water conservation plans, adopt measures such as enclosure protection and natural restoration, organize units and individuals to plant trees and grass, expand forest and grass coverage, conserve water sources, and prevent and mitigate soil erosion.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对取土、挖砂、采石等活动的管理，预防和减轻水土流失。  
禁止在崩塌、滑坡危险区和泥石流易发区从事取土、挖砂、采石等可能造成水土流失的活动。崩塌、滑坡危险区和泥石流易发区的范围，由县级以上地方人民政府划定并公告。崩塌、滑坡危险区和泥石流易发区的划定，应当与地质灾害防治规划确定的地质灾害易发区、重点防治区相衔接。

**Article 17** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the management of activities such as soil extraction, sand digging, and stone quarrying to prevent and mitigate soil erosion.  
It is prohibited to engage in activities such as soil extraction, sand digging, and stone quarrying that may cause soil erosion in areas prone to collapse, landslide, and debris flow. The scope of areas prone to collapse, landslide, and debris flow shall be delimited and announced by local people's governments at or above the county level. The delimitation of areas prone to collapse, landslide, and debris flow shall be coordinated with the areas prone to geological disasters and key control areas determined in geological disaster prevention plans.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　水土流失严重、生态脆弱的地区，应当限制或者禁止可能造成水土流失的生产建设活动，严格保护植物、沙壳、结皮、地衣等。  
在侵蚀沟的沟坡和沟岸、河流的两岸以及湖泊和水库的周边，土地所有权人、使用权人或者有关管理单位应当营造植物保护带。禁止开垦、开发植物保护带。

**Article 18** In areas with severe soil erosion and fragile ecology, production and construction activities that may cause soil erosion shall be restricted or prohibited, and plants, sand crust, crust, lichens, etc. shall be strictly protected.  
On the slopes and banks of erosion gullies, on both sides of rivers, and around lakes and reservoirs, owners, users of land or relevant management units shall create plant protection belts. It is prohibited to reclaim or develop plant protection belts.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　水土保持设施的所有权人或者使用权人应当加强对水土保持设施的管理与维护，落实管护责任，保障其功能正常发挥。

**Article 19** Owners or users of soil and water conservation facilities shall strengthen the management and maintenance of such facilities, implement maintenance responsibilities, and ensure their normal functioning.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　禁止在二十五度以上陡坡地开垦种植农作物。在二十五度以上陡坡地种植经济林的，应当科学选择树种，合理确定规模，采取水土保持措施，防止造成水土流失。  
省、自治区、直辖市根据本行政区域的实际情况，可以规定小于二十五度的禁止开垦坡度。禁止开垦的陡坡地的范围由当地县级人民政府划定并公告。

**Article 20** It is prohibited to reclaim and plant crops on steep slopes with a gradient of 25 degrees or more. For planting economic forests on steep slopes with a gradient of 25 degrees or more, tree species shall be scientifically selected, the scale shall be reasonably determined, and soil and water conservation measures shall be taken to prevent soil erosion.  
Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on actual conditions in their respective administrative regions, stipulate a lower prohibited reclaiming gradient than 25 degrees. The scope of steep slopes where reclamation is prohibited shall be delimited and announced by the local county people's government.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止毁林、毁草开垦和采集发菜。禁止在水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区铲草皮、挖树兜或者滥挖虫草、甘草、麻黄等。

**Article 21** It is prohibited to reclaim land by destroying forests or grasslands, and to collect Facai (a type of algae). It is prohibited to peel off turf, dig tree roots, or dig Cordyceps, licorice, ephedra, etc., excessively in key prevention areas and key control areas for soil erosion.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　林木采伐应当采用合理方式，严格控制皆伐；对水源涵养林、水土保持林、防风固沙林等防护林只能进行抚育和更新性质的采伐；对采伐区和集材道应当采取防止水土流失的措施，并在采伐后及时更新造林。  
在林区采伐林木的，采伐方案中应当有水土保持措施。采伐方案经林业主管部门批准后，由林业主管部门和水行政主管部门监督实施。

**Article 22** Forest logging shall adopt reasonable methods, and clear-cutting shall be strictly controlled; for protection forests such as water conservation forests, soil and water conservation forests, and windbreak and sand-fixing forests, only tending and regeneration logging shall be carried out; measures to prevent soil erosion shall be taken in logging areas and skidding roads, and timely reforestation shall be carried out after logging.  
For logging in forest areas, the logging plan shall include soil and water conservation measures. After the logging plan is approved by the forestry department, the forestry department and the water administrative department shall supervise its implementation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在五度以上坡地植树造林、抚育幼林、种植中药材等，应当采取水土保持措施。  
在禁止开垦坡度以下、五度以上的荒坡地开垦种植农作物，应当采取水土保持措施。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市根据本行政区域的实际情况规定。

**Article 23** For afforestation, tending young forests, planting Chinese medicinal materials, etc., on slopes with a gradient of 5 degrees or more, soil and water conservation measures shall be taken.  
For reclaiming and planting crops on barren slopes with a gradient below the prohibited reclaiming gradient but 5 degrees or more, soil and water conservation measures shall be taken. Specific measures shall be prescribed by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on actual conditions in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　生产建设项目选址、选线应当避让水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区；无法避让的，应当提高防治标准，优化施工工艺，减少地表扰动和植被损坏范围，有效控制可能造成的水土流失。

**Article 24** The site selection and route selection of production and construction projects shall avoid key prevention areas and key control areas for soil erosion; where avoidance is impossible, the prevention and control standards shall be improved, construction techniques shall be optimized, the scope of surface disturbance and vegetation damage shall be reduced, and potential soil erosion shall be effectively controlled.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在山区、丘陵区、风沙区以及水土保持规划确定的容易发生水土流失的其他区域开办可能造成水土流失的生产建设项目，生产建设单位应当编制水土保持方案，报县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门审批，并按照经批准的水土保持方案，采取水土流失预防和治理措施。没有能力编制水土保持方案的，应当委托具备相应技术条件的机构编制。  
水土保持方案应当包括水土流失预防和治理的范围、目标、措施和投资等内容。  
水土保持方案经批准后，生产建设项目的地点、规模发生重大变化的，应当补充或者修改水土保持方案并报原审批机关批准。水土保持方案实施过程中，水土保持措施需要作出重大变更的，应当经原审批机关批准。  
生产建设项目水土保持方案的编制和审批办法，由国务院水行政主管部门制定。

**Article 25** When launching production and construction projects in mountainous, hilly, sandstorm areas, and other areas prone to soil erosion determined by soil and water conservation plans that may cause soil erosion, the construction unit shall prepare a soil and water conservation plan and submit it to the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level for approval, and shall adopt soil erosion prevention and control measures in accordance with the approved plan. If the unit lacks the capacity to prepare a plan, it shall entrust an institution with the corresponding technical conditions to prepare it.  
A soil and water conservation plan shall include the scope, objectives, measures, and investment for soil erosion prevention and control.  
If major changes occur in the location or scale of the production and construction project after the plan is approved, the plan shall be supplemented or revised and submitted to the original approving authority for approval. If major changes need to be made to the soil and water conservation measures during implementation, approval from the original approving authority shall be obtained.  
The measures for preparing and approving soil and water conservation plans for production and construction projects shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　依法应当编制水土保持方案的生产建设项目，生产建设单位未编制水土保持方案或者水土保持方案未经水行政主管部门批准的，生产建设项目不得开工建设。

**Article 26** For a production and construction project that is required by law to prepare a soil and water conservation plan, if the construction unit fails to prepare the plan or the plan has not been approved by the water administrative department, the project shall not commence construction.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　依法应当编制水土保持方案的生产建设项目中的水土保持设施，应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用；生产建设项目竣工验收，应当验收水土保持设施；水土保持设施未经验收或者验收不合格的，生产建设项目不得投产使用。

**Article 27** The soil and water conservation facilities in a production and construction project that is required by law to prepare a soil and water conservation plan shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project; upon completion and acceptance of the production and construction project, the soil and water conservation facilities shall be accepted; if the soil and water conservation facilities have not been accepted or fail the acceptance, the production and construction project shall not be put into operation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　依法应当编制水土保持方案的生产建设项目，其生产建设活动中排弃的砂、石、土、矸石、尾矿、废渣等应当综合利用；不能综合利用，确需废弃的，应当堆放在水土保持方案确定的专门存放地，并采取措施保证不产生新的危害。

**Article 28** For a production and construction project that is required by law to prepare a soil and water conservation plan, the sand, stone, soil, gangue, tailings, waste residues, etc., discharged during its production and construction activities shall be comprehensively utilized; if comprehensive utilization is impossible and disposal is indeed necessary, they shall be stacked in the designated storage sites determined in the soil and water conservation plan, and measures shall be taken to ensure that no new hazards are caused.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、流域管理机构，应当对生产建设项目水土保持方案的实施情况进行跟踪检查，发现问题及时处理。

**Article 29** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and river basin management agencies shall conduct follow-up inspections on the implementation of soil and water conservation plans for production and construction projects, and promptly address any problems discovered.

### 第四章　治理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家加强水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区的坡耕地改梯田、淤地坝等水土保持重点工程建设，加大生态修复力度。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当加强对水土保持重点工程的建设管理，建立和完善运行管护制度。

**Article 30** The State shall strengthen the construction of key soil and water conservation projects such as converting sloping farmland to terraced fields and silt retention dams in key prevention areas and key control areas for soil erosion, and increase ecological restoration efforts.  
The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction management of key soil and water conservation projects, and establish and improve operation and maintenance management systems.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家加强江河源头区、饮用水水源保护区和水源涵养区水土流失的预防和治理工作，多渠道筹集资金，将水土保持生态效益补偿纳入国家建立的生态效益补偿制度。

**Article 31** The State shall strengthen the prevention and control of soil erosion in river source areas, drinking water source protection areas, and water conservation areas, raise funds through multiple channels, and incorporate soil and water conservation ecological benefit compensation into the national ecological benefit compensation system.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　开办生产建设项目或者从事其他生产建设活动造成水土流失的，应当进行治理。  
在山区、丘陵区、风沙区以及水土保持规划确定的容易发生水土流失的其他区域开办生产建设项目或者从事其他生产建设活动，损坏水土保持设施、地貌植被，不能恢复原有水土保持功能的，应当缴纳水土保持补偿费，专项用于水土流失预防和治理。专项水土流失预防和治理由水行政主管部门负责组织实施。水土保持补偿费的收取使用管理办法由国务院财政部门、国务院价格主管部门会同国务院水行政主管部门制定。  
生产建设项目在建设过程中和生产过程中发生的水土保持费用，按照国家统一的财务会计制度处理。

**Article 32** If soil erosion is caused by launching a production and construction project or engaging in other production and construction activities, it shall be controlled.  
For launching production and construction projects or engaging in other production and construction activities in mountainous, hilly, sandstorm areas, and other areas prone to soil erosion determined by soil and water conservation plans, which damage soil and water conservation facilities or landform vegetation and cannot restore the original soil and water conservation functions, a soil and water conservation compensation fee shall be paid, which shall be used exclusively for soil erosion prevention and control. The special soil erosion prevention and control shall be organized and implemented by the water administrative department. The measures for the collection and use management of soil and water conservation compensation fees shall be formulated by the finance department and the price department of the State Council jointly with the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The soil and water conservation costs incurred during the construction and production processes of production and construction projects shall be handled in accordance with the unified financial accounting system of the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家鼓励单位和个人按照水土保持规划参与水土流失治理，并在资金、技术、税收等方面予以扶持。

**Article 33** The State encourages units and individuals to participate in soil erosion control in accordance with soil and water conservation plans, and provides support in terms of funds, technology, taxation, etc.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家鼓励和支持承包治理荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩，防治水土流失，保护和改善生态环境，促进土地资源的合理开发和可持续利用，并依法保护土地承包合同当事人的合法权益。  
承包治理荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩和承包水土流失严重地区农村土地的，在依法签订的土地承包合同中应当包括预防和治理水土流失责任的内容。

**Article 34** The State encourages and supports the contracting of barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, and barren beaches for control, to prevent and control soil erosion, protect and improve the ecological environment, promote rational development and sustainable use of land resources, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of parties to land contracts according to law.  
For contracts to control barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, barren beaches, and to contract rural land in areas with severe soil erosion, the land contract signed according to law shall include the responsibility for preventing and controlling soil erosion.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在水力侵蚀地区，地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织单位和个人，以天然沟壑及其两侧山坡地形成的小流域为单元，因地制宜地采取工程措施、植物措施和保护性耕作等措施，进行坡耕地和沟道水土流失综合治理。  
在风力侵蚀地区，地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织单位和个人，因地制宜地采取轮封轮牧、植树种草、设置人工沙障和网格林带等措施，建立防风固沙防护体系。  
在重力侵蚀地区，地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织单位和个人，采取监测、径流排导、削坡减载、支挡固坡、修建拦挡工程等措施，建立监测、预报、预警体系。

**Article 35** In water erosion areas, local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall organize units and individuals, taking small watersheds formed by natural gullies and their side slopes as units, to adopt engineering measures, plant measures, conservation tillage, etc., according to local conditions, to carry out comprehensive control of soil erosion on sloping farmland and gullies.  
In wind erosion areas, local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall organize units and individuals, according to local conditions, to adopt measures such as rotational grazing enclosure, tree and grass planting, setting up artificial sand barriers and grid forest belts, to establish a windbreak and sand-fixing protection system.  
In gravity erosion areas, local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall organize units and individuals to adopt measures such as monitoring, runoff drainage, slope cutting and load reduction, support and slope stabilization, construction of retaining works, to establish a monitoring, forecasting, and early warning system.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在饮用水水源保护区，地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织单位和个人，采取预防保护、自然修复和综合治理措施，配套建设植物过滤带，积极推广沼气，开展清洁小流域建设，严格控制化肥和农药的使用，减少水土流失引起的面源污染，保护饮用水水源。

**Article 36** In drinking water source protection areas, local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall organize units and individuals to adopt measures such as prevention protection, natural restoration, and comprehensive control, build plant filter belts, actively promote biogas, carry out clean small watershed construction, strictly control the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides, reduce non-point source pollution caused by soil erosion, and protect drinking water sources.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　已在禁止开垦的陡坡地上开垦种植农作物的，应当按照国家有关规定退耕，植树种草；耕地短缺、退耕确有困难的，应当修建梯田或者采取其他水土保持措施。  
在禁止开垦坡度以下的坡耕地上开垦种植农作物的，应当根据不同情况，采取修建梯田、坡面水系整治、蓄水保土耕作或者退耕等措施。

**Article 37** Where crops have already been planted on steep slopes where reclamation is prohibited, the land shall be returned to forest or grass in accordance with relevant State regulations; if reclamation withdrawal is truly difficult due to cropland shortage, terraced fields shall be constructed or other soil and water conservation measures shall be taken.  
For planting crops on sloping farmland with a gradient below the prohibited reclaiming gradient, measures such as building terraced fields, slope water system management, water and soil conservation tillage, or reclamation withdrawal shall be taken according to different situations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对生产建设活动所占用土地的地表土应当进行分层剥离、保存和利用，做到土石方挖填平衡，减少地表扰动范围；对废弃的砂、石、土、矸石、尾矿、废渣等存放地，应当采取拦挡、坡面防护、防洪排导等措施。生产建设活动结束后，应当及时在取土场、开挖面和存放地的裸露土地上植树种草、恢复植被，对闭库的尾矿库进行复垦。  
在干旱缺水地区从事生产建设活动，应当采取防止风力侵蚀措施，设置降水蓄渗设施，充分利用降水资源。

**Article 38** The topsoil of land occupied by production and construction activities shall be stripped, preserved, and utilized in layers to achieve balance in earthwork excavation and backfilling, and reduce the scope of surface disturbance; for storage sites of discarded sand, stone, soil, gangue, tailings, waste residues, etc., measures such as retaining walls, slope protection, flood control and drainage shall be taken. After the production and construction activities are completed, trees and grass shall be promptly planted on the bare land of borrow pits, excavation faces, and storage sites to restore vegetation, and the closed tailings ponds shall be reclaimed.  
For production and construction activities in arid and water-deficient areas, measures to prevent wind erosion shall be taken, precipitation storage and infiltration facilities shall be installed, and precipitation resources shall be fully utilized.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家鼓励和支持在山区、丘陵区、风沙区以及容易发生水土流失的其他区域，采取下列有利于水土保持的措施：  
（一）免耕、等高耕作、轮耕轮作、草田轮作、间作套种等；  
（二）封禁抚育、轮封轮牧、舍饲圈养；  
（三）发展沼气、节柴灶，利用太阳能、风能和水能，以煤、电、气代替薪柴等；  
（四）从生态脆弱地区向外移民；  
（五）其他有利于水土保持的措施。

**Article 39** The State encourages and supports the adoption of the following measures beneficial to soil and water conservation in mountainous, hilly, sandstorm areas and other areas prone to soil erosion:  
(1) No-tillage, contour farming, rotation tillage and rotation cropping, grass-crop rotation, intercropping and interplanting, etc.;  
(2) Closure and tending, rotational enclosure and rotational grazing, stable feeding and penning;  
(3) Development of biogas, energy-saving stoves, utilization of solar energy, wind energy and hydropower, substitution of coal, electricity, gas for firewood, etc.;  
(4) Migration from ecologically fragile areas;  
(5) Other measures beneficial to soil and water conservation.

### 第五章　监测和监督

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当加强水土保持监测工作，发挥水土保持监测工作在政府决策、经济社会发展和社会公众服务中的作用。县级以上人民政府应当保障水土保持监测工作经费。  
国务院水行政主管部门应当完善全国水土保持监测网络，对全国水土流失进行动态监测。

**Article 40** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen soil and water conservation monitoring work, and give full play to the role of soil and water conservation monitoring in government decision-making, economic and social development, and public services. People's governments at or above the county level shall ensure funding for soil and water conservation monitoring.  
The water administrative department of the State Council shall improve the national soil and water conservation monitoring network and carry out dynamic monitoring of soil erosion nationwide.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对可能造成严重水土流失的大中型生产建设项目，生产建设单位应当自行或者委托具备水土保持监测资质的机构，对生产建设活动造成的水土流失进行监测，并将监测情况定期上报当地水行政主管部门。  
从事水土保持监测活动应当遵守国家有关技术标准、规范和规程，保证监测质量。

**Article 41** For large and medium-sized production and construction projects that may cause severe soil erosion, the construction unit shall, on its own or by entrusting an institution qualified for soil and water conservation monitoring, monitor the soil erosion caused by the production and construction activities, and regularly report the monitoring situation to the local water administrative department.  
Entities engaged in soil and water conservation monitoring activities shall comply with relevant national technical standards, specifications, and procedures to ensure monitoring quality.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院水行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当根据水土保持监测情况，定期对下列事项进行公告：  
（一）水土流失类型、面积、强度、分布状况和变化趋势；  
（二）水土流失造成的危害；  
（三）水土流失预防和治理情况。

**Article 42** The water administrative department of the State Council and the water administrative departments of people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on soil and water conservation monitoring conditions, regularly announce the following matters:  
(1) Types, areas, intensity, distribution, and change trends of soil erosion;  
(2) Hazards caused by soil erosion;  
(3) Status of soil erosion prevention and control.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门负责对水土保持情况进行监督检查。流域管理机构在其管辖范围内可以行使国务院水行政主管部门的监督检查职权。

**Article 43** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for supervising and inspecting soil and water conservation conditions. River basin management agencies may exercise the supervision and inspection authority of the water administrative department of the State Council within their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　水政监督检查人员依法履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位或者个人提供有关文件、证照、资料；  
（二）要求被检查单位或者个人就预防和治理水土流失的有关情况作出说明；  
（三）进入现场进行调查、取证。  
被检查单位或者个人拒不停止违法行为，造成严重水土流失的，报经水行政主管部门批准，可以查封、扣押实施违法行为的工具及施工机械、设备等。

**Article 44** When performing supervision and inspection duties according to law, water law enforcement and supervision personnel shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) Require the unit or individual under inspection to provide relevant documents, permits, and materials;  
(2) Require the unit or individual under inspection to provide explanations on the prevention and control of soil erosion;  
(3) Enter the site for investigation and evidence collection.  
If the unit or individual under inspection refuses to stop the illegal act, causing severe soil erosion, with the approval of the water administrative department, tools, construction machinery, equipment, etc., used for the illegal act may be sealed up or seized.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　水政监督检查人员依法履行监督检查职责时，应当出示执法证件。被检查单位或者个人对水土保持监督检查工作应当给予配合，如实报告情况，提供有关文件、证照、资料；不得拒绝或者阻碍水政监督检查人员依法执行公务。

**Article 45** When performing supervision and inspection duties according to law, water law enforcement and supervision personnel shall show their law enforcement credentials. The unit or individual under inspection shall cooperate with the soil and water conservation supervision and inspection, truthfully report the situation, and provide relevant documents, permits, and materials; they shall not refuse or obstruct water law enforcement and supervision personnel from performing official duties.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　不同行政区域之间发生水土流失纠纷应当协商解决；协商不成的，由共同的上一级人民政府裁决。

**Article 46** Disputes over soil erosion between different administrative regions shall be resolved through consultation; if consultation fails, they shall be adjudicated by the common people's government at the next higher level.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　水行政主管部门或者其他依照本法规定行使监督管理权的部门，不依法作出行政许可决定或者办理批准文件的，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处的，或者有其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 47** Where the water administrative department or any other department exercising supervision and management authority pursuant to this Law fails to make an administrative licensing decision or handle approval documents according to law, fails to investigate upon discovering an illegal act or upon receiving a report of an illegal act, or commits any other act of failing to perform duties in accordance with this Law, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本法规定，在崩塌、滑坡危险区或者泥石流易发区从事取土、挖砂、采石等可能造成水土流失的活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，对个人处一千元以上一万元以下的罚款，对单位处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** In violation of the provisions of this Law, engaging in activities such as soil extraction, sand digging, and stone quarrying in areas prone to collapse, landslide, or debris flow that may cause soil erosion, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on an individual, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on a unit.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本法规定，在禁止开垦坡度以上陡坡地开垦种植农作物，或者在禁止开垦、开发的植物保护带内开垦、开发的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，采取退耕、恢复植被等补救措施；按照开垦或者开发面积，可以对个人处每平方米二元以下的罚款、对单位处每平方米十元以下的罚款。

**Article 49** In violation of the provisions of this Law, reclaiming and planting crops on steep slopes with a gradient above the prohibited reclaiming gradient, or reclaiming or developing within plant protection belts where reclamation or development is prohibited, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and take remedial measures such as returning farmland to forest or restoring vegetation; according to the area reclaimed or developed, a fine of not more than 2 yuan per square meter may be imposed on an individual, and a fine of not more than 10 yuan per square meter on a unit.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，毁林、毁草开垦的，依照《中华人民共和国森林法》、《中华人民共和国草原法》的有关规定处罚。

**Article 50** In violation of the provisions of this Law, reclaiming land by destroying forests or grasslands, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China and the Grassland Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本法规定，采集发菜，或者在水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区铲草皮、挖树兜、滥挖虫草、甘草、麻黄等的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，采取补救措施，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以处五万元以下的罚款。  
在草原地区有前款规定违法行为的，依照《中华人民共和国草原法》的有关规定处罚。

**Article 51** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, collects hair weeds, or removes turf, digs tree stumps, or indiscriminately digs Cordyceps sinensis, licorice, or ephedra, etc. in key prevention areas and key control areas of soil erosion, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, take remedial measures, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently be imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed.  
Anyone who commits the illegal act as prescribed in the preceding paragraph in grassland areas shall be penalized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Grassland Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在林区采伐林木不依法采取防止水土流失措施的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门、水行政主管部门责令限期改正，采取补救措施；造成水土流失的，由水行政主管部门按照造成水土流失的面积处每平方米二元以上十元以下的罚款。

**Article 52** Anyone who, when felling trees in forest areas, fails to take measures to prevent soil erosion according to law, shall be ordered by the forestry department or the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and take remedial measures; if soil erosion is caused, the water administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than two yuan but not more than ten yuan per square meter based on the area of soil erosion.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期补办手续；逾期不补办手续的，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；对生产建设单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）依法应当编制水土保持方案的生产建设项目，未编制水土保持方案或者编制的水土保持方案未经批准而开工建设的；  
（二）生产建设项目的地点、规模发生重大变化，未补充、修改水土保持方案或者补充、修改的水土保持方案未经原审批机关批准的；  
（三）水土保持方案实施过程中，未经原审批机关批准，对水土保持措施作出重大变更的。

**Article 53** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act and go through the formalities within a prescribed time limit; if the formalities are not completed within the time limit, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; the person directly in charge and other personnel directly liable for the construction unit shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) A production or construction project that shall prepare a soil and water conservation plan according to law commences construction without preparing a plan or with a plan that has not been approved;  
(2) Where major changes occur in the location or scale of a production or construction project, the project fails to supplement or modify the soil and water conservation plan, or the supplemented or modified plan has not been approved by the original approving authority;  
(3) Major modifications are made to soil and water conservation measures during the implementation of the plan without approval from the original approving authority.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，水土保持设施未经验收或者验收不合格将生产建设项目投产使用的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止生产或者使用，直至验收合格，并处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, puts a production or construction project into operation or use without the soil and water conservation facilities having been inspected or having passed inspection, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease production or use until the inspection is passed, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，在水土保持方案确定的专门存放地以外的区域倾倒砂、石、土、矸石、尾矿、废渣等的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期清理，按照倾倒数量处每立方米十元以上二十元以下的罚款；逾期仍不清理的，县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门可以指定有清理能力的单位代为清理，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 55** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, dumps sand, stone, soil, gangue, tailings, waste residue, etc. in areas other than the special storage sites designated in the soil and water conservation plan, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act and clean up within a prescribed time limit, and a fine of not less than ten yuan but not more than twenty yuan per cubic meter based on the volume dumped shall be imposed; if the cleanup is not completed within the time limit, the water administrative department may designate a unit with cleanup capability to carry out the cleanup on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，开办生产建设项目或者从事其他生产建设活动造成水土流失，不进行治理的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期治理；逾期仍不治理的，县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 56** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, causes soil erosion by launching a production or construction project or engaging in other production and construction activities and fails to carry out treatment, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to treat the soil erosion within a prescribed time limit; if treatment is still not carried out within the time limit, the water administrative department may designate a unit with treatment capability to carry out the treatment on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，拒不缴纳水土保持补偿费的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期缴纳；逾期不缴纳的，自滞纳之日起按日加收滞纳部分万分之五的滞纳金，可以处应缴水土保持补偿费三倍以下的罚款。

**Article 57** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, refuses to pay the soil and water conservation compensation fee, shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to pay within a prescribed time limit; if payment is not made within the time limit, a daily surcharge of 0.05% of the overdue amount shall be imposed from the date of default, and a fine of not more than three times the payable soil and water conservation compensation fee may be imposed.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，造成水土流失危害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, causes harm from soil erosion, shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上地方人民政府根据当地实际情况确定的负责水土保持工作的机构，行使本法规定的水行政主管部门水土保持工作的职责。

**Article 59** The institution designated by the local people's government at or above the county level according to local conditions to be responsible for soil and water conservation work shall exercise the functions and duties of the water administrative department concerning soil and water conservation as prescribed in this Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本法自2011年3月1日起施行。

**Article 60** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2011.
