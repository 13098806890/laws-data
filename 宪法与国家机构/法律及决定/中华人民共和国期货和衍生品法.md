**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2022-04-20  
**生效日期**：2022-08-01

> （2022年4月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十四次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　期货交易和衍生品交易 第一节　一般规定 第二节　期货交易 第三节　衍生品交易 第三章　期货结算与交割 第四章　期货交易者 第五章　期货经营机构 第六章　期货交易场所 第七章　期货结算机构 第八章　期货服务机构 第九章　期货业协会 第十章　监督管理

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范期货交易和衍生品交易行为，保障各方合法权益，维护市场秩序和社会公共利益，促进期货市场和衍生品市场服务国民经济，防范化解金融风险，维护国家经济安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating futures trading and derivatives trading activities, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of all parties, maintaining market order and public interests, promoting the futures market and the derivatives market to serve the national economy, preventing and mitigating financial risks, and maintaining national economic security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，期货交易和衍生品交易及相关活动，适用本法。  
在中华人民共和国境外的期货交易和衍生品交易及相关活动，扰乱中华人民共和国境内市场秩序，损害境内交易者合法权益的，依照本法有关规定处理并追究法律责任。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to futures trading and derivatives trading and related activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Futures trading and derivatives trading and related activities outside the territory of the People's Republic of China that disrupt the market order within the territory of the People's Republic of China or harm the legitimate rights and interests of domestic traders shall be handled and legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称期货交易，是指以期货合约或者标准化期权合约为交易标的的交易活动。  
本法所称衍生品交易，是指期货交易以外的，以互换合约、远期合约和非标准化期权合约及其组合为交易标的的交易活动。  
本法所称期货合约，是指期货交易场所统一制定的、约定在将来某一特定的时间和地点交割一定数量标的物的标准化合约。  
本法所称期权合约，是指约定买方有权在将来某一时间以特定价格买入或者卖出约定标的物（包括期货合约）的标准化或非标准化合约。  
本法所称互换合约，是指约定在将来某一特定时间内相互交换特定标的物的金融合约。  
本法所称远期合约，是指期货合约以外的，约定在将来某一特定的时间和地点交割一定数量标的物的金融合约。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "futures trading" means trading activities with futures contracts or standardized option contracts as the trading subject matter.  
"Derivatives trading" means trading activities other than futures trading, with swap contracts, forward contracts, non-standardized option contracts, and combinations thereof as the trading subject matter.  
"Futures contract" means a standardized contract uniformly formulated by a futures trading venue, stipulating the delivery of a certain quantity of subject matter at a specific time and place in the future.  
"Option contract" means a standardized or non-standardized contract stipulating that the buyer has the right to buy or sell a specified subject matter (including futures contracts) at a specific price at a future time.  
"Swap contract" means a financial contract stipulating the mutual exchange of specified subject matters at a specific future time.  
"Forward contract" means a financial contract other than a futures contract, stipulating the delivery of a certain quantity of subject matter at a specific time and place in the future.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家支持期货市场健康发展，发挥发现价格、管理风险、配置资源的功能。  
国家鼓励利用期货市场和衍生品市场从事套期保值等风险管理活动。  
国家采取措施推动农产品期货市场和衍生品市场发展，引导国内农产品生产经营。  
本法所称套期保值，是指交易者为管理因其资产、负债等价值变化产生的风险而达成与上述资产、负债等基本吻合的期货交易和衍生品交易的活动。

**Article 4** The State supports the healthy development of the futures market and gives play to its functions of price discovery, risk management, and resource allocation.  
The State encourages the use of the futures market and the derivatives market to engage in risk management activities such as hedging.  
The State takes measures to promote the development of agricultural product futures markets and derivatives markets, and to guide the production and operation of domestic agricultural products.  
For the purposes of this Law, "hedging" means the activity of a trader entering into futures trading and derivatives trading that substantially matches the aforementioned assets, liabilities, etc. for the purpose of managing risks arising from changes in the value of its assets, liabilities, etc.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　期货市场和衍生品市场应当建立和完善风险的监测监控与化解处置制度机制，依法限制过度投机行为，防范市场系统性风险。

**Article 5** The futures market and the derivatives market shall establish and improve systems and mechanisms for monitoring, controlling, and resolving risks, restrict excessive speculation according to law, and prevent systemic risks in the market.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　期货交易和衍生品交易活动，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，遵循公开、公平、公正的原则，禁止欺诈、操纵市场和内幕交易的行为。

**Article 6** Futures trading and derivatives trading activities shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State, follow the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality, and prohibit acts of fraud, market manipulation, and insider trading.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　参与期货交易和衍生品交易活动的各方具有平等的法律地位，应当遵守自愿、有偿、诚实信用的原则。

**Article 7** All parties participating in futures trading and derivatives trading activities shall have equal legal status and shall observe the principles of voluntariness, compensation, good faith, and credit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法对全国期货市场实行集中统一监督管理。国务院对利率、汇率期货的监督管理另有规定的，适用其规定。  
衍生品市场由国务院期货监督管理机构或者国务院授权的部门按照职责分工实行监督管理。

**Article 8** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall exercise centralized and unified supervision and administration over the national futures market according to law. Where the State Council has otherwise provided for the supervision and administration of interest rate and exchange rate futures, such provisions shall apply.  
The derivatives market shall be subject to supervision and administration by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council in accordance with the division of responsibilities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　期货和衍生品行业协会依法实行自律管理。

**Article 9** The futures and derivatives industry association shall exercise self-regulation according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家审计机关依法对期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、国务院期货监督管理机构进行审计监督。

**Article 10** The State audit authority shall conduct audit supervision over futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, and the futures regulatory authority of the State Council according to law.

### 第二章　期货交易和衍生品交易

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　期货交易应当在依法设立的期货交易所或者国务院期货监督管理机构依法批准组织开展期货交易的其他期货交易场所（以下统称期货交易场所），采用公开的集中交易方式或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的其他方式进行。  
禁止在期货交易场所之外进行期货交易。  
衍生品交易，可以采用协议交易或者国务院规定的其他交易方式进行。

**Article 11** Futures trading shall be conducted on a lawfully established futures exchange or other futures trading venues (hereinafter collectively referred to as futures trading venues) that are lawfully approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council to organize and carry out futures trading, by adopting open centralized trading methods or other methods approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Futures trading outside futures trading venues is prohibited.  
Derivatives trading may adopt agreement trading or other trading methods prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位和个人不得操纵期货市场或者衍生品市场。  
禁止以下列手段操纵期货市场，影响或者意图影响期货交易价格或者期货交易量：  
（一）单独或者合谋，集中资金优势、持仓优势或者利用信息优势联合或者连续买卖合约；  
（二）与他人串通，以事先约定的时间、价格和方式相互进行期货交易；  
（三）在自己实际控制的账户之间进行期货交易；  
（四）利用虚假或者不确定的重大信息，诱导交易者进行期货交易；  
（五）不以成交为目的，频繁或者大量申报并撤销申报；  
（六）对相关期货交易或者合约标的物的交易作出公开评价、预测或者投资建议，并进行反向操作或者相关操作；  
（七）为影响期货市场行情囤积现货；  
（八）在交割月或者临近交割月，利用不正当手段规避持仓限额，形成持仓优势；  
（九）利用在相关市场的活动操纵期货市场；  
（十）操纵期货市场的其他手段。

**Article 12** No entity or individual shall manipulate the futures market or the derivatives market.  
It is prohibited to manipulate the futures market by the following means to influence or attempt to influence futures trading prices or futures trading volumes:  
(1) Concentrating capital advantage, position advantage, or using information advantage, alone or in collusion with others, to jointly or continuously buy or sell contracts;  
(2) Colluding with others to conduct futures trading with each other at a pre-arranged time, price, and method;  
(3) Conducting futures trading between accounts under one's actual control;  
(4) Using false or uncertain material information to induce traders to conduct futures trading;  
(5) Frequently or substantially submitting and canceling orders without the intention to conclude transactions;  
(6) Making public evaluations, predictions, or investment recommendations on relevant futures trading or trading of the underlying subject matter of the contracts, and taking opposite or related actions;  
(7) Hoarding spot goods to influence the futures market sentiment;  
(8) Using improper means to evade position limits and create a position advantage in or near the delivery month;  
(9) Manipulating the futures market through activities in relevant markets;  
(10) Other means of manipulating the futures market.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　期货交易和衍生品交易的内幕信息的知情人和非法获取内幕信息的人，在内幕信息公开前不得从事相关期货交易或者衍生品交易，明示、暗示他人从事与内幕信息有关的期货交易或者衍生品交易，或者泄露内幕信息。

**Article 13** Persons who have access to inside information on futures trading and derivatives trading and persons who have illegally obtained inside information shall not, prior to the disclosure of such inside information, engage in relevant futures trading or derivatives trading, express or imply to others to engage in futures trading or derivatives trading related to the inside information, or disclose the inside information.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本法所称内幕信息，是指可能对期货交易或者衍生品交易的交易价格产生重大影响的尚未公开的信息。  
期货交易的内幕信息包括：  
（一）国务院期货监督管理机构以及其他相关部门正在制定或者尚未发布的对期货交易价格可能产生重大影响的政策、信息或者数据；  
（二）期货交易场所、期货结算机构作出的可能对期货交易价格产生重大影响的决定；  
（三）期货交易场所会员、交易者的资金和交易动向；  
（四）相关市场中的重大异常交易信息；  
（五）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的对期货交易价格有重大影响的其他信息。

**Article 14** For the purposes of this Law, inside information means information that is not yet publicly disclosed and may have a material impact on the trading prices of futures trading or derivatives trading.  
Inside information on futures trading includes:  
(1) Policies, information, or data that are being formulated or have not yet been released by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments and that may have a material impact on futures trading prices;  
(2) Decisions made by futures trading venues or futures clearing institutions that may have a material impact on futures trading prices;  
(3) Capital and trading trends of members of futures trading venues and traders;  
(4) Material abnormal trading information in relevant markets;  
(5) Other information specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council that has a material impact on futures trading prices.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本法所称内幕信息的知情人，是指由于经营地位、管理地位、监督地位或者职务便利等，能够接触或者获得内幕信息的单位和个人。  
期货交易的内幕信息的知情人包括：  
（一）期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构的有关人员；  
（二）国务院期货监督管理机构和其他有关部门的工作人员；  
（三）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的可以获取内幕信息的其他单位和个人。

**Article 15** For the purposes of this Law, persons who have access to inside information mean entities and individuals that, by virtue of their operational status, management status, supervisory status, or convenience of position, are able to access or obtain inside information.  
Persons who have access to inside information on futures trading include:  
(1) Relevant personnel of futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, and futures service institutions;  
(2) Staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments;  
(3) Other entities and individuals specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council that may obtain inside information.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　禁止任何单位和个人编造、传播虚假信息或者误导性信息，扰乱期货市场和衍生品市场。  
禁止期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构及其从业人员，组织、开展衍生品交易的场所、机构及其从业人员，期货和衍生品行业协会、国务院期货监督管理机构、国务院授权的部门及其工作人员，在期货交易和衍生品交易及相关活动中作出虚假陈述或者信息误导。  
各种传播媒介传播期货市场和衍生品市场信息应当真实、客观，禁止误导。传播媒介及其从事期货市场和衍生品市场信息报道的工作人员不得从事与其工作职责发生利益冲突的期货交易和衍生品交易及相关活动。

**Article 16** Any entity or individual is prohibited from fabricating or disseminating false information or misleading information to disrupt the futures market and the derivatives market.  
Futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions and their employees, entities and institutions organizing and carrying out derivatives trading and their employees, futures and derivatives industry associations, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, departments authorized by the State Council and their staff members are prohibited from making false statements or information misrepresentations in futures trading and derivatives trading and related activities.  
All communication media shall disseminate information on the futures market and the derivatives market truthfully and objectively, and misleading dissemination is prohibited. Personnel of communication media engaged in reporting information on the futures market and derivatives market shall not engage in futures trading and derivatives trading and related activities that conflict with their work responsibilities.

#### 第二节　期货交易

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　期货合约品种和标准化期权合约品种的上市应当符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，由期货交易场所依法报经国务院期货监督管理机构注册。  
期货合约品种和标准化期权合约品种的中止上市、恢复上市、终止上市应当符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，由期货交易场所决定并向国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
期货合约品种和标准化期权合约品种应当具有经济价值，合约不易被操纵，符合社会公共利益。

**Article 17** The listing of futures contract varieties and standardized option contract varieties shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall be reported by the futures trading venue to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for registration according to law.  
The suspension of listing, resumption of listing, and termination of listing of futures contract varieties and standardized option contract varieties shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall be decided by the futures trading venue and filed with the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Futures contract varieties and standardized option contract varieties shall have economic value, be not easily manipulated, and be in line with the public interest.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　期货交易实行账户实名制。交易者进行期货交易的，应当持有证明身份的合法证件，以本人名义申请开立账户。  
任何单位和个人不得违反规定，出借自己的期货账户或者借用他人的期货账户从事期货交易。

**Article 18** Futures trading shall implement a real-name account system. A trader conducting futures trading shall hold legal documents certifying his or her identity and apply for opening an account in his or her own name.  
No entity or individual shall, in violation of regulations, lend its or his or her own futures account or borrow another person's futures account to engage in futures trading.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在期货交易场所进行期货交易的，应当是期货交易场所会员或者符合国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他参与者。

**Article 19** Those conducting futures trading on a futures trading venue shall be members of the futures trading venue or other participants that meet the requirements prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　交易者委托期货经营机构进行交易的，可以通过书面、电话、自助终端、网络等方式下达交易指令。交易指令应当明确、具体、全面。

**Article 20** When a trader entrusts a futures operating institution with trading, the trader may issue trading orders in writing, by telephone, through self-service terminals, via the internet, or by other means. The trading order shall be clear, specific, and comprehensive.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　通过计算机程序自动生成或者下达交易指令进行程序化交易的，应当符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，并向期货交易场所报告，不得影响期货交易场所系统安全或者正常交易秩序。

**Article 21** Those conducting programmatic trading by automatically generating or issuing trading orders through computer programs shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall report to the futures trading venue, and shall not affect the security of the futures trading venue's systems or the normal trading order.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　期货交易实行保证金制度，期货结算机构向结算参与人收取保证金，结算参与人向交易者收取保证金。保证金用于结算和履约保障。  
保证金的形式包括现金，国债、股票、基金份额、标准仓单等流动性强的有价证券，以及国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他财产。以有价证券等作为保证金的，可以依法通过质押等具有履约保障功能的方式进行。  
期货结算机构、结算参与人收取的保证金的形式、比例等应当符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定。  
交易者进行标准化期权合约交易的，卖方应当缴纳保证金，买方应当支付权利金。  
前款所称权利金是指买方支付的用于购买标准化期权合约的资金。

**Article 22** Futures trading shall implement a margin system. The futures clearing institution shall collect margins from clearing participants, and clearing participants shall collect margins from traders. Margins shall be used for settlement and performance guarantee.  
The forms of margin include cash, government bonds, stocks, fund shares, standard warehouse receipts, and other marketable securities with strong liquidity, as well as other property prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council. Where marketable securities, etc., are used as margin, it may be conducted by means such as pledge that have the function of performance guarantee according to law.  
The forms, proportions, etc., of margins collected by futures clearing institutions and clearing participants shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where a trader conducts standardized option contract trading, the seller shall pay margin and the buyer shall pay premium.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, premium means the funds paid by the buyer to purchase standardized option contracts.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　期货交易实行持仓限额制度，防范合约持仓过度集中的风险。  
从事套期保值等风险管理活动的，可以申请持仓限额豁免。  
持仓限额、套期保值的管理办法由国务院期货监督管理机构制定。

**Article 23** Futures trading shall implement a position limit system to prevent the risk of excessive concentration of contract positions.  
Those engaging in risk management activities such as hedging may apply for exemption from position limits.  
The administrative measures for position limits and hedging shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　期货交易实行交易者实际控制关系报备管理制度。交易者应当按照国务院期货监督管理机构的规定向期货经营机构或者期货交易场所报备实际控制关系。

**Article 24** Futures trading shall implement a system of reporting and filing actual control relationships by traders. Traders shall report and file their actual control relationships to the futures operating institution or the futures trading venue in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　期货交易的收费应当合理，收费项目、收费标准和管理办法应当公开。

**Article 25** Fees for futures trading shall be reasonable. The items of fees, standards of fees, and management measures shall be made public.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　依照期货交易场所依法制定的业务规则进行的交易，不得改变其交易结果，[本法第八十九条](#art-89)第二款规定的除外。

**Article 26** The results of transactions conducted in accordance with the business rules lawfully formulated by the futures trading venue shall not be altered, except as provided in Article 89, paragraph 2 of this Law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　期货交易场所会员和交易者应当按照国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，报告有关交易、持仓、保证金等重大事项。

**Article 27** Members of futures trading venues and traders shall report material matters concerning trading, positions, margins, etc., in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人不得违规使用信贷资金、财政资金进行期货交易。

**Article 28** No entity or individual shall use credit funds or fiscal funds for futures trading in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构等机构及其从业人员对发现的禁止的交易行为，应当及时向国务院期货监督管理机构报告。

**Article 29** Futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions, and other institutions and their employees shall promptly report any prohibited trading activities they discover to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

#### 第三节　衍生品交易

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　依法设立的场所，经国务院授权的部门或者国务院期货监督管理机构审批，可以组织开展衍生品交易。  
组织开展衍生品交易的场所制定的交易规则，应当公平保护交易参与各方合法权益和防范市场风险，并报国务院授权的部门或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准。

**Article 30** Venues lawfully established may, upon approval by a department authorized by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, organize and carry out derivatives trading.  
The trading rules formulated by venues organizing and carrying out derivatives trading shall fairly protect the legitimate rights and interests of all parties involved in the trading and prevent market risks, and shall be submitted to a department authorized by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　金融机构开展衍生品交易业务，应当依法经过批准或者核准，履行交易者适当性管理义务，并应当遵守国家有关监督管理规定。

**Article 31** Financial institutions conducting derivatives trading business shall obtain approval or verification according to law, perform obligations of suitability management for traders, and shall comply with the relevant state regulatory provisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　衍生品交易采用主协议方式的，主协议、主协议项下的全部补充协议以及交易双方就各项具体交易作出的约定等，共同构成交易双方之间一个完整的单一协议，具有法律约束力。

**Article 32** Where derivatives trading adopts the master agreement approach, the master agreement, all supplementary agreements under the master agreement, and the agreements made by the parties to the transaction on each specific transaction, etc., together constitute a complete single agreement between the parties, which shall have legally binding force.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　[本法第三十二条](#art-32)规定的主协议等合同范本，应当按照国务院授权的部门或者国务院期货监督管理机构的规定报送备案。

**Article 33** The master agreement and other model contracts prescribed in Article 32 of this Law shall be submitted for recordation in accordance with the provisions of the department authorized by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　进行衍生品交易，可以依法通过质押等方式提供履约保障。

**Article 34** For derivatives trading, performance guarantees may be provided by means of pledge, etc. according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　依法采用主协议方式从事衍生品交易的，发生约定的情形时，可以依照协议约定终止交易，并按净额对协议项下的全部交易盈亏进行结算。  
依照前款规定进行的净额结算，不因交易任何一方依法进入破产程序而中止、无效或者撤销。

**Article 35** Where derivatives trading is conducted by adopting the master agreement approach according to law, in the event of the agreed circumstances, the transaction may be terminated in accordance with the provisions of the agreement, and the profits and losses of all transactions under the agreement shall be settled on a net basis.  
Net settlement conducted in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall not be suspended, rendered invalid, or revoked due to any party to the transaction entering into bankruptcy proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院授权的部门、国务院期货监督管理机构应当建立衍生品交易报告库，对衍生品交易标的、规模、对手方等信息进行集中收集、保存、分析和管理，并按照规定及时向市场披露有关信息。具体办法由国务院授权的部门、国务院期货监督管理机构规定。

**Article 36** The department authorized by the State Council and the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish a derivatives trading repository to centrally collect, store, analyze, and manage information such as the underlying assets, scale, and counterparties of derivatives transactions, and shall timely disclose relevant information to the market in accordance with the regulations. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the department authorized by the State Council and the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　衍生品交易，由国务院授权的部门或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的结算机构作为中央对手方进行集中结算的，可以依法进行终止净额结算；结算财产应当优先用于结算和交割，不得被查封、冻结、扣押或者强制执行；在结算和交割完成前，任何人不得动用。  
依法进行的集中结算，不因参与结算的任何一方依法进入破产程序而中止、无效或者撤销。

**Article 37** Where derivatives transactions are centrally cleared by a clearing institution approved by the department authorized by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council as a central counterparty, termination net settlement may be conducted according to law; the settlement property shall be prioritized for settlement and delivery and shall not be sealed up, frozen, distrained, or subject to compulsory execution; before the completion of settlement and delivery, no person may use it.  
Centralized clearing conducted according to law shall not be suspended, rendered invalid, or revoked due to any party participating in the clearing entering into bankruptcy proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对衍生品交易及相关活动进行规范和监督管理的具体办法，由国务院依照本法的原则规定。

**Article 38** The specific measures for regulating and supervising derivatives transactions and related activities shall be prescribed by the State Council in accordance with the principles of this Law.

### 第三章　期货结算与交割

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　期货交易实行当日无负债结算制度。在期货交易场所规定的时间，期货结算机构应当在当日按照结算价对结算参与人进行结算；结算参与人应当根据期货结算机构的结算结果对交易者进行结算。结算结果应当在当日及时通知结算参与人和交易者。

**Article 39** Futures trading shall implement a daily mark-to-market settlement system. At the time prescribed by the futures trading venue, the futures clearing institution shall settle with clearing participants on the same day based on the settlement price; clearing participants shall settle with traders based on the settlement results of the futures clearing institution. The settlement results shall be promptly notified to the clearing participants and traders on the same day.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　期货结算机构、结算参与人收取的保证金、权利金等，应当与其自有资金分开，按照国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，在期货保证金存管机构专户存放，分别管理，禁止违规挪用。

**Article 40** The margin, premiums, and other funds collected by futures clearing institutions and clearing participants shall be kept separate from their own funds, deposited in dedicated accounts at margin depository institutions in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, managed separately, and misappropriation in violation of regulations is prohibited.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　结算参与人的保证金不符合期货结算机构业务规则规定标准的，期货结算机构应当按照业务规则的规定通知结算参与人在规定时间内追加保证金或者自行平仓；结算参与人未在规定时间内追加保证金或者自行平仓的，通知期货交易场所强行平仓。  
交易者的保证金不符合结算参与人与交易者约定标准的，结算参与人应当按照约定通知交易者在约定时间内追加保证金或者自行平仓；交易者未在约定时间内追加保证金或者自行平仓的，按照约定强行平仓。  
以有价证券等作为保证金，期货结算机构、结算参与人按照前两款规定强行平仓的，可以对有价证券等进行处置。

**Article 41** If the margin of a clearing participant does not meet the standards prescribed by the business rules of the futures clearing institution, the futures clearing institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the business rules, notify the clearing participant to add margin or close positions on its own within a prescribed time limit; if the clearing participant fails to add margin or close positions within the prescribed time limit, the futures trading venue shall be notified to forcibly close positions.  
If the margin of a trader does not meet the standards agreed upon between the clearing participant and the trader, the clearing participant shall, in accordance with the agreement, notify the trader to add margin or close positions on its own within the agreed time limit; if the trader fails to add margin or close positions within the agreed time limit, the positions shall be forcibly closed in accordance with the agreement.  
Where marketable securities, etc. are used as margin, and the futures clearing institution or clearing participant forcibly closes positions in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs, the marketable securities, etc. may be disposed of.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　结算参与人在结算过程中违约的，期货结算机构按照业务规则动用结算参与人的保证金、结算担保金以及结算机构的风险准备金、自有资金等完成结算；期货结算机构以其风险准备金、自有资金等完成结算的，可以依法对该结算参与人进行追偿。  
交易者在结算过程中违约的，其委托的结算参与人按照合同约定动用该交易者的保证金以及结算参与人的风险准备金和自有资金完成结算；结算参与人以其风险准备金和自有资金完成结算的，可以依法对该交易者进行追偿。  
本法所称结算担保金，是指结算参与人以自有资金向期货结算机构缴纳的，用于担保履约的资金。

**Article 42** If a clearing participant defaults during settlement, the futures clearing institution shall, in accordance with the business rules, use the clearing participant's margin, clearing guarantee funds, and the clearing institution's risk reserve, own funds, etc. to complete the settlement; if the futures clearing institution completes the settlement using its risk reserve, own funds, etc., it may seek recourse from the clearing participant according to law.  
If a trader defaults during settlement, the clearing participant entrusted by the trader shall, in accordance with the provisions of the contract, use the trader's margin and the clearing participant's risk reserve and own funds to complete the settlement; if the clearing participant completes the settlement using its risk reserve and own funds, it may seek recourse from the trader according to law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "clearing guarantee fund" means the funds paid by a clearing participant to the futures clearing institution with its own funds for the guarantee of performance.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　期货结算机构依照其业务规则收取和提取的保证金、权利金、结算担保金、风险准备金等资产，应当优先用于结算和交割，不得被查封、冻结、扣押或者强制执行。  
在结算和交割完成之前，任何人不得动用用于担保履约和交割的保证金、进入交割环节的交割财产。  
依法进行的结算和交割，不因参与结算的任何一方依法进入破产程序而中止、无效或者撤销。

**Article 43** The margin, premiums, clearing guarantee funds, risk reserves, and other assets collected and withdrawn by the futures clearing institution in accordance with its business rules shall be used preferentially for settlement and delivery, and shall not be sealed up, frozen, detained, or subject to compulsory execution.  
Before settlement and delivery are completed, no person may use the margin used to guarantee performance and delivery or the delivery property that has entered the delivery link.  
Settlement and delivery carried out according to law shall not be suspended, be void, or be revoked due to the fact that any party involved in the settlement enters into bankruptcy proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　期货合约到期时，交易者应当通过实物交割或者现金交割，了结到期未平仓合约。  
在标准化期权合约规定的时间，合约的买方有权以约定的价格买入或者卖出标的物，或者按照约定进行现金差价结算，合约的卖方应当按照约定履行相应的义务。标准化期权合约的行权，由期货结算机构组织进行。

**Article 44** When a futures contract expires, the trader shall close out the open contracts that have not been closed out at expiry through physical delivery or cash settlement.  
At the time prescribed in a standardized option contract, the buyer of the contract shall have the right to buy or sell the subject matter at the agreed price, or settle the cash difference in accordance with the agreement, and the seller of the contract shall perform the corresponding obligations in accordance with the agreement. The exercise of standardized option contracts shall be organized by the futures clearing institution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　期货合约采取实物交割的，由期货结算机构负责组织货款与标准仓单等合约标的物权利凭证的交付。  
期货合约采取现金交割的，由期货结算机构以交割结算价为基础，划付持仓双方的盈亏款项。  
本法所称标准仓单，是指交割库开具并经期货交易场所登记的标准化提货凭证。

**Article 45** Where a futures contract adopts physical delivery, the futures clearing institution shall be responsible for organizing the delivery of the payment and the title documents of the contract subject matter such as standard warrants.  
Where a futures contract adopts cash settlement, the futures clearing institution shall, based on the delivery settlement price, allocate the profit and loss amounts between the parties with long and short positions.  
For the purposes of this Law, "standard warrant" means a standardized delivery certificate issued by a delivery warehouse and registered with the futures trading venue.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　期货交易的实物交割在期货交易场所指定的交割库、交割港口或者其他符合期货交易场所要求的地点进行。实物交割不得限制交割总量。采用标准仓单以外的单据凭证或者其他方式进行实物交割的，期货交易场所应当明确规定交割各方的权利和义务。

**Article 46** Physical delivery under futures trading shall be conducted at the delivery warehouse, delivery port, or other locations designated by the futures trading venue and meeting the requirements of the futures trading venue. Physical delivery shall not limit the total delivery quantity. Where physical delivery is carried out using documents other than standard warrants or other methods, the futures trading venue shall clearly stipulate the rights and obligations of each delivery party.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　结算参与人在交割过程中违约的，期货结算机构有权对结算参与人的标准仓单等合约标的物权利凭证进行处置。  
交易者在交割过程中违约的，结算参与人有权对交易者的标准仓单等合约标的物权利凭证进行处置。

**Article 47** If a clearing participant defaults during delivery, the futures clearing institution shall have the right to dispose of the clearing participant's standard warrants and other title documents of the contract subject matter.  
If a trader defaults during delivery, the clearing participant shall have the right to dispose of the trader's standard warrants and other title documents of the contract subject matter.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　不符合结算参与人条件的期货经营机构可以委托结算参与人代为其客户进行结算。不符合结算参与人条件的期货经营机构与结算参与人、交易者之间的权利义务关系，参照本章关于结算参与人与交易者之间权利义务的规定执行。

**Article 48** A futures operating institution that does not meet the conditions for a clearing participant may entrust a clearing participant to settle on behalf of its clients. The rights and obligations between such a futures operating institution and the clearing participant and the trader shall be handled by reference to the provisions of this Chapter on the rights and obligations between clearing participants and traders.

### 第四章　期货交易者

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　期货交易者是指依照本法从事期货交易，承担交易结果的自然人、法人和非法人组织。  
期货交易者从事期货交易，除国务院期货监督管理机构另有规定外，应当委托期货经营机构进行。

**Article 49** Futures traders refer to natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that engage in futures trading in accordance with this Law and bear the trading results.  
When engaging in futures trading, futures traders shall entrust a futures operating institution to conduct the trading, unless otherwise provided by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　期货经营机构向交易者提供服务时，应当按照规定充分了解交易者的基本情况、财产状况、金融资产状况、交易知识和经验、专业能力等相关信息；如实说明服务的重要内容，充分揭示交易风险；提供与交易者上述状况相匹配的服务。  
交易者在参与期货交易和接受服务时，应当按照期货经营机构明示的要求提供前款所列真实信息。拒绝提供或者未按照要求提供信息的，期货经营机构应当告知其后果，并按照规定拒绝提供服务。  
期货经营机构违反第一款规定导致交易者损失的，应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 50** When providing services to traders, a futures operating institution shall, in accordance with regulations, fully understand the basic information, property status, financial asset status, trading knowledge and experience, professional competence, and other relevant information of the trader; faithfully explain the important contents of the services, and fully disclose the trading risks; and provide services that match the aforementioned conditions of the trader.  
When participating in futures trading and receiving services, a trader shall provide truthful information as listed in the preceding paragraph in accordance with the explicit requirements of the futures operating institution. If the trader refuses to provide or fails to provide information as required, the futures operating institution shall inform the trader of the consequences and refuse to provide services in accordance with regulations.  
If a futures operating institution violates the provisions of the first paragraph and causes losses to a trader, it shall bear the corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　根据财产状况、金融资产状况、交易知识和经验、专业能力等因素，交易者可以分为普通交易者和专业交易者。专业交易者的标准由国务院期货监督管理机构规定。  
普通交易者与期货经营机构发生纠纷的，期货经营机构应当证明其行为符合法律、行政法规以及国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，不存在误导、欺诈等情形。期货经营机构不能证明的，应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 51** Based on factors such as property status, financial asset status, trading knowledge and experience, and professional competence, traders may be classified as ordinary traders and professional traders. The standards for professional traders shall be prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If a dispute arises between an ordinary trader and a futures operating institution, the futures operating institution shall prove that its conduct complies with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and that there is no misleading, fraud, or other such circumstances. If the futures operating institution fails to prove this, it shall bear the corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　参与期货交易的法人和非法人组织，应当建立与其交易合约类型、规模、目的等相适应的内部控制制度和风险控制制度。

**Article 52** Legal persons and unincorporated organizations participating in futures trading shall establish internal control systems and risk control systems that are appropriate to the type, scale, purpose, etc., of their trading contracts.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构的从业人员，国务院期货监督管理机构、期货业协会的工作人员，以及法律、行政法规和国务院期货监督管理机构规定禁止参与期货交易的其他人员，不得进行期货交易。

**Article 53** Employees of futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, and futures clearing institutions; staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and the futures industry association; and other persons who are prohibited from participating in futures trading by laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall not engage in futures trading.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　交易者有权查询其委托记录、交易记录、保证金余额、与其接受服务有关的其他重要信息。

**Article 54** A trader shall have the right to query its entrustment records, trading records, margin balance, and other important information related to the services it receives.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构及其工作人员应当依法为交易者的信息保密，不得非法买卖、提供或者公开交易者的信息。  
期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构及其工作人员不得泄露所知悉的商业秘密。

**Article 55** Futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions, and their staff members shall keep the information of traders confidential according to law, and shall not illegally buy, sell, provide, or disclose traders' information.  
Futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions, and their staff members shall not disclose any trade secrets known to them.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　交易者与期货经营机构等发生纠纷的，双方可以向行业协会等申请调解。普通交易者与期货经营机构发生期货业务纠纷并提出调解请求的，期货经营机构不得拒绝。

**Article 56** If a dispute arises between a trader and a futures operating institution, etc., both parties may apply for mediation to the industry association, etc. If an ordinary trader has a futures business dispute with a futures operating institution and submits a mediation request, the futures operating institution shall not refuse.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　交易者提起操纵市场、内幕交易等期货民事赔偿诉讼时，诉讼标的是同一种类，且当事人一方人数众多的，可以依法推选代表人进行诉讼。

**Article 57** When a trader initiates a civil compensation lawsuit for futures such as market manipulation or insider trading, if the subject matter of the action is of the same kind and one party involves a large number of persons, representatives may be elected according to law to conduct the litigation.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家设立期货交易者保障基金。期货交易者保障基金的筹集、管理和使用的具体办法，由国务院期货监督管理机构会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 58** The State establishes a futures trader protection fund. The specific measures for the raising, management, and use of the futures trader protection fund shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council together with the finance department of the State Council.

### 第五章　期货经营机构

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　期货经营机构是指依照《中华人民共和国公司法》和本法设立的期货公司以及国务院期货监督管理机构核准从事期货业务的其他机构。

**Article 59** Futures operating institutions refer to futures companies established in accordance with the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and this Law, and other institutions approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council to engage in futures business.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　设立期货公司，应当具备下列条件，并经国务院期货监督管理机构核准：  
（一）有符合法律、行政法规规定的公司章程；  
（二）主要股东及实际控制人具有良好的财务状况和诚信记录，净资产不低于国务院期货监督管理机构规定的标准，最近三年无重大违法违规记录；  
（三）注册资本不低于人民币一亿元，且应当为实缴货币资本；  
（四）从事期货业务的人员符合本法规定的条件，董事、监事和高级管理人员具备相应的任职条件；  
（五）有良好的公司治理结构、健全的风险管理制度和完善的内部控制制度；  
（六）有合格的经营场所、业务设施和信息技术系统；  
（七）法律、行政法规和国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他条件。  
国务院期货监督管理机构根据审慎监管原则和各项业务的风险程度，可以提高注册资本最低限额。  
国务院期货监督管理机构应当自受理期货公司设立申请之日起六个月内依照法定条件、法定程序和审慎监管原则进行审查，作出核准或者不予核准的决定，并通知申请人；不予核准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 60** To establish a futures company, the following conditions shall be satisfied, and approval shall be obtained from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) having articles of association that comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations;  
(2) the principal shareholders and actual controllers have good financial conditions and integrity records, with net assets not lower than the standards prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and no record of major illegal or non-compliant acts in the last three years;  
(3) the registered capital is not less than RMB 100 million yuan and shall be fully paid-in monetary capital;  
(4) the personnel engaged in futures business meet the conditions prescribed by this Law, and the directors, supervisors, and senior officers have the corresponding qualifications for their positions;  
(5) having a good corporate governance structure, a sound risk management system, and a complete internal control system;  
(6) having qualified business premises, business facilities, and information technology systems;  
(7) other conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may raise the minimum registered capital limit based on the prudential supervision principle and the risk level of each business.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of accepting the application for establishment of a futures company, conduct a review in accordance with the statutory conditions, statutory procedures, and the prudential supervision principle, make a decision to approve or disapprove, and notify the applicant; if the application is disapproved, the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　期货公司应当在其名称中标明“期货”字样，国务院期货监督管理机构另有规定的除外。

**Article 61** A futures company shall indicate the word "futures" in its name, unless otherwise provided by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　期货公司办理下列事项，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构核准：  
（一）合并、分立、停业、解散或者申请破产；  
（二）变更主要股东或者公司的实际控制人；  
（三）变更注册资本且调整股权结构；  
（四）变更业务范围；  
（五）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他重大事项。  
前款第三项、第五项所列事项，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自受理申请之日起二十日内作出核准或者不予核准的决定；前款所列其他事项，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自受理申请之日起六十日内作出核准或者不予核准的决定。

**Article 62** A futures company that handles the following matters shall obtain approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) merger, division, suspension of business, dissolution, or filing for bankruptcy;  
(2) change of principal shareholders or the actual controller of the company;  
(3) change of registered capital and adjustment of equity structure;  
(4) change of business scope;  
(5) other major matters prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
For the matters listed in items (3) and (5) of the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision to approve or disapprove within twenty days from the date of accepting the application; for other matters listed in the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision to approve or disapprove within sixty days from the date of accepting the application.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　期货公司经国务院期货监督管理机构核准可以从事下列期货业务：  
（一）期货经纪；  
（二）期货交易咨询；  
（三）期货做市交易；  
（四）其他期货业务。  
期货公司从事资产管理业务的，应当符合《中华人民共和国证券投资基金法》等法律、行政法规的规定。  
未经国务院期货监督管理机构核准，任何单位和个人不得设立或者变相设立期货公司，经营或者变相经营期货经纪业务、期货交易咨询业务，也不得以经营为目的使用“期货”、“期权”或者其他可能产生混淆或者误导的名称。

**Article 63** A futures company, upon approval by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, may engage in the following futures businesses:  
(1) futures brokerage;  
(2) futures trading consultation;  
(3) futures market-making trading;  
(4) other futures businesses.  
If a futures company engages in asset management business, it shall comply with the provisions of the Securities Investment Fund Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and administrative regulations.  
Without the approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, no entity or individual may establish or establish a futures company in disguised form, operate or operate in disguised form futures brokerage business or futures trading consultation business, nor use the terms "futures," "options," or other names that may cause confusion or misunderstanding for business purposes.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　期货公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员，应当正直诚实、品行良好，熟悉期货法律、行政法规，具有履行职责所需的经营管理能力。期货公司任免董事、监事、高级管理人员，应当报国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
有下列情形之一的，不得担任期货公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员：  
（一）存在《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的不得担任公司董事、监事和高级管理人员的情形；  
（二）因违法行为或者违纪行为被解除职务的期货经营机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员，或者期货交易场所、期货结算机构的负责人，自被解除职务之日起未逾五年；  
（三）因违法行为或者违纪行为被吊销执业证书或者被取消资格的注册会计师、律师或者其他期货服务机构的专业人员，自被吊销执业证书或者被取消资格之日起未逾五年。

**Article 64** The directors, supervisors, and senior officers of a futures company shall be upright, honest, of good conduct, familiar with futures laws and administrative regulations, and possess the operational management capabilities necessary to perform their duties. The appointment and removal of directors, supervisors, and senior officers of a futures company shall be reported to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.  
No person under any of the following circumstances may serve as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a futures company:  
(1) having a circumstance provided in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China under which a person may not serve as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a company;  
(2) having been removed from position as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a futures operating institution, or as a responsible person of a futures trading venue or futures clearing institution due to illegal acts or disciplinary violations, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of removal;  
(3) having had their practice certificate revoked or their qualification cancelled as a certified public accountant, lawyer, or other professional of a futures service institution due to illegal acts or disciplinary violations, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of revocation or cancellation.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　期货经营机构应当依法经营，勤勉尽责，诚实守信。期货经营机构应当建立健全内部控制制度，采取有效隔离措施，防范经营机构与客户之间、不同客户之间的利益冲突。  
期货经营机构应当将其期货经纪业务、期货做市交易业务、资产管理业务和其他相关业务分开办理，不得混合操作。  
期货经营机构应当依法建立并执行反洗钱制度。

**Article 65** A futures operating institution shall operate according to law, be diligent and responsible, and act in good faith. A futures operating institution shall establish and improve internal control systems, and adopt effective segregation measures to prevent conflicts of interest between the institution and clients and between different clients.  
A futures operating institution shall handle its futures brokerage business, futures market-making trading business, asset management business, and other related businesses separately, and shall not commingle operations.  
A futures operating institution shall establish and implement an anti-money laundering system according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　期货经营机构接受交易者委托为其进行期货交易，应当签订书面委托合同，以自己的名义为交易者进行期货交易，交易结果由交易者承担。  
期货经营机构从事经纪业务，不得接受交易者的全权委托。

**Article 66** When a futures operating institution accepts a trader's entrustment to conduct futures trading on its behalf, it shall enter into a written entrustment contract, conduct futures trading in its own name for the trader, and the trading results shall be borne by the trader.  
When engaging in brokerage business, a futures operating institution shall not accept a trader's discretionary entrustment.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　期货经营机构从事资产管理业务，接受客户委托，运用客户资产进行投资的，应当公平对待所管理的不同客户资产，不得违背受托义务。

**Article 67** When engaging in asset management business, accepting client entrustment, and using client assets for investment, a futures operating institution shall treat the different client assets it manages fairly, and shall not breach its fiduciary duties.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　期货经营机构不得违反规定为其股东、实际控制人或者股东、实际控制人的关联人提供融资或者担保，不得违反规定对外担保。

**Article 68** A futures operating institution shall not, in violation of regulations, provide financing or guarantees for its shareholders, actual controllers, or related persons of its shareholders or actual controllers, and shall not provide external guarantees in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　期货经营机构从事期货业务的人员应当正直诚实、品行良好，具备从事期货业务所需的专业能力。  
期货经营机构从事期货业务的人员不得私下接受客户委托从事期货交易。  
期货经营机构从事期货业务的人员在从事期货业务活动中，执行所属的期货经营机构的指令或者利用职务违反期货交易规则的，由所属的期货经营机构承担全部责任。

**Article 69** Personnel engaging in futures business at a futures operating institution shall be honest and trustworthy, have good conduct, and possess the professional competence necessary for engaging in futures business.  
Personnel engaging in futures business at a futures operating institution shall not privately accept client mandates to engage in futures trading.  
Where personnel engaging in futures business at a futures operating institution, in the course of engaging in futures business activities, follow instructions of the futures operating institution to which they belong or take advantage of their positions to violate futures trading rules, the futures operating institution to which they belong shall bear all liability.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国务院期货监督管理机构应当制定期货经营机构持续性经营规则，对期货经营机构及其分支机构的经营条件、风险管理、内部控制、保证金存管、合规管理、风险监管指标、关联交易等方面作出规定。期货经营机构应当符合持续性经营规则。

**Article 70** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall formulate rules for the sustainable operation of futures operating institutions, and make provisions regarding the operating conditions, risk management, internal control, margin custody, compliance management, risk supervision indicators, connected transactions, etc., of futures operating institutions and their branches. Futures operating institutions shall comply with the rules for sustainable operation.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　期货经营机构应当按照规定向国务院期货监督管理机构报送业务、财务等经营管理信息和资料。国务院期货监督管理机构有权要求期货经营机构及其主要股东、实际控制人、其他关联人在指定的期限内提供有关信息、资料。  
期货经营机构及其主要股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人向国务院期货监督管理机构报送或者提供的信息、资料，应当真实、准确、完整。

**Article 71** A futures operating institution shall, in accordance with the provisions, submit business, financial, and other operational management information and materials to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council. The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the authority to require the futures operating institution and its principal shareholders, actual controllers, and other related persons to provide relevant information and materials within a specified period.  
The information and materials submitted or provided by the futures operating institution and its principal shareholders, actual controllers, or other related persons to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall be truthful, accurate, and complete.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　期货经营机构涉及重大诉讼、仲裁，股权被冻结或者用于担保，以及发生其他重大事件时，应当自该事件发生之日起五日内向国务院期货监督管理机构提交书面报告。  
期货经营机构的控股股东或者实际控制人应当配合期货经营机构履行前款规定的义务。

**Article 72** Where a futures operating institution is involved in major litigation or arbitration, its equity interests are frozen or used for security, or any other major event occurs, it shall submit a written report to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council within five days from the date of occurrence of such event.  
The controlling shareholder or actual controller of a futures operating institution shall cooperate with the futures operating institution in performing the obligation specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　期货经营机构不符合持续性经营规则或者出现经营风险的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当责令其限期改正；期货经营机构逾期未改正的，或者其行为严重危及该期货经营机构的稳健运行、损害交易者合法权益的，或者涉嫌严重违法违规正在被国务院期货监督管理机构调查的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以区别情形，对其采取下列措施：  
（一）限制或者暂停部分业务；  
（二）停止核准新增业务；  
（三）限制分配红利，限制向董事、监事、高级管理人员支付报酬、提供福利；  
（四）限制转让财产或者在财产上设定其他权利；  
（五）责令更换董事、监事、高级管理人员或者有关业务部门、分支机构的负责人员，或者限制其权利；  
（六）限制期货经营机构自有资金或者风险准备金的调拨和使用；  
（七）认定负有责任的董事、监事、高级管理人员为不适当人选；  
（八）责令负有责任的股东转让股权，限制负有责任的股东行使股东权利。  
对经过整改符合有关法律、行政法规规定以及持续性经营规则要求的期货经营机构，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自验收完毕之日起三日内解除对其采取的有关措施。  
对经过整改仍未达到持续性经营规则要求，严重影响正常经营的期货经营机构，国务院期货监督管理机构有权撤销其部分或者全部期货业务许可、关闭其部分或者全部分支机构。

**Article 73** Where a futures operating institution fails to comply with the rules for sustainable operation or experiences operational risks, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to rectify within a specified period; if the futures operating institution fails to rectify within the specified period, or its conduct seriously endangers the sound operation of the futures operating institution or damages the legitimate rights and interests of traders, or if it is suspected of serious violations of laws or regulations and is under investigation by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may, depending on the circumstances, take the following measures against it:  
(1) restrict or suspend part of its business;  
(2) cease approving new business;  
(3) restrict profit distribution, restrict the payment of remuneration and provision of benefits to directors, supervisors, and senior officers;  
(4) restrict the transfer of property or the creation of other rights over property;  
(5) order the replacement of directors, supervisors, senior officers, or responsible persons of relevant business departments or branches, or restrict their rights;  
(6) restrict the allocation and use of the futures operating institution's own funds or risk reserves;  
(7) determine that responsible directors, supervisors, or senior officers are unsuitable persons;  
(8) order the responsible shareholders to transfer their equity interests, and restrict the exercise of shareholders' rights by responsible shareholders.  
For a futures operating institution that, after rectification, complies with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the requirements of the rules for sustainable operation, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within three days from the date of completion of inspection, lift the relevant measures taken against it.  
For a futures operating institution that, after rectification, still fails to meet the requirements of the rules for sustainable operation and severely affects its normal operation, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the authority to revoke part or all of its futures business permits and close part or all of its branches.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　期货经营机构违法经营或者出现重大风险，严重危害期货市场秩序、损害交易者利益的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以对该期货经营机构采取责令停业整顿、指定其他机构托管或者接管等监督管理措施。  
期货经营机构有前款所列情形，经国务院期货监督管理机构批准，可以对该期货经营机构直接负责的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员采取以下措施：  
（一）决定并通知出境入境管理机关依法阻止其出境；  
（二）申请司法机关禁止其转移、转让或者以其他方式处分财产，或者在财产上设定其他权利。

**Article 74** Where a futures operating institution engages in illegal operations or experiences major risks, seriously endangering the order of the futures market and harming the interests of traders, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may take regulatory measures against the futures operating institution, such as ordering it to suspend business for rectification, designating another institution to take over its custody or management.  
Where a futures operating institution falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, with the approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, the following measures may be taken against the directly responsible directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other directly responsible persons of the futures operating institution:  
(1) decide and notify the exit and entry administrative authority to prevent their exit from the country according to law;  
(2) apply to a judicial authority to prohibit them from transferring, assigning, or otherwise disposing of property, or creating other rights over property.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　期货经营机构的股东有虚假出资、抽逃出资行为的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当责令其限期改正，并可责令其转让所持期货经营机构的股权。  
在股东依照前款规定的要求改正违法行为、转让所持期货经营机构的股权前，国务院期货监督管理机构可以限制其股东权利。

**Article 75** Where a shareholder of a futures operating institution makes a false capital contribution or withdraws capital contribution, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to rectify within a specified period, and may order it to transfer its equity interests in the futures operating institution.  
Before a shareholder corrects its illegal conduct and transfers its equity interests in the futures operating institution in accordance with the requirements of the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may restrict its shareholders' rights.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　期货经营机构有下列情形之一的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当依法办理相关业务许可证注销手续：  
（一）营业执照被依法吊销；  
（二）成立后无正当理由超过三个月未开始营业，或者开业后无正当理由停业连续三个月以上；  
（三）主动提出注销申请；  
（四）《中华人民共和国行政许可法》和国务院期货监督管理机构规定应当注销行政许可的其他情形。  
期货经营机构在注销相关业务许可证前，应当结清相关期货业务，并依法返还交易者的保证金和其他资产。

**Article 76** Where a futures operating institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall handle the cancellation of the relevant business license according to law:  
(1) its business license is revoked according to law;  
(2) it fails to commence business without justifiable reasons for more than three months after establishment, or after commencing business, it suspends business without justifiable reasons for three consecutive months or more;  
(3) it voluntarily applies for cancellation;  
(4) other circumstances under which the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council stipulate that the administrative license shall be cancelled.  
Before cancelling the relevant business license, the futures operating institution shall settle the relevant futures business and return the margin and other assets of the traders according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国务院期货监督管理机构认为必要时，可以委托期货服务机构对期货经营机构的财务状况、内部控制状况、资产价值进行审计或者评估。具体办法由国务院期货监督管理机构会同有关主管部门制定。

**Article 77** Where the futures regulatory authority of the State Council deems it necessary, it may entrust a futures service institution to audit or evaluate the financial condition, internal control condition, and asset value of a futures operating institution. The specific measures shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council jointly with the relevant competent authorities.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　禁止期货经营机构从事下列损害交易者利益的行为：  
（一）向交易者作出保证其资产本金不受损失或者取得最低收益承诺；  
（二）与交易者约定分享利益、共担风险；  
（三）违背交易者委托进行期货交易；  
（四）隐瞒重要事项或者使用其他不正当手段，诱骗交易者交易；  
（五）以虚假或者不确定的重大信息为依据向交易者提供交易建议；  
（六）向交易者提供虚假成交回报；  
（七）未将交易者交易指令下达到期货交易场所；  
（八）挪用交易者保证金；  
（九）未依照规定在期货保证金存管机构开立保证金账户，或者违规划转交易者保证金；  
（十）利用为交易者提供服务的便利，获取不正当利益或者转嫁风险；  
（十一）其他损害交易者权益的行为。

**Article 78** Futures operating institutions are prohibited from engaging in the following acts that harm the interests of traders:  
(1) making commitments to traders guaranteeing that their principal will not be lost or that they will obtain minimum returns;  
(2) agreeing with traders to share benefits and bear risks jointly;  
(3) conducting futures trading in violation of the traders' mandates;  
(4) concealing important matters or using other improper means to induce traders to trade;  
(5) providing trading advice to traders based on false or uncertain material information;  
(6) providing false trade confirmation to traders;  
(7) failing to transmit traders' trading orders to the futures trading venue;  
(8) misappropriating traders' margin;  
(9) failing to open a margin account with a margin custody institution in accordance with the provisions, or illegally transferring traders' margin;  
(10) taking advantage of the convenience of providing services to traders to obtain illegitimate benefits or pass on risks;  
(11) other acts that harm the rights and interests of traders.

### 第六章　期货交易场所

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　期货交易场所应当遵循社会公共利益优先原则，为期货交易提供场所和设施，组织和监督期货交易，维护市场的公平、有序和透明，实行自律管理。

**Article 79** A futures trading venue shall follow the principle of prioritizing social public interests, provide venues and facilities for futures trading, organize and supervise futures trading, maintain a fair, orderly, and transparent market, and exercise self-regulation.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　设立、变更和解散期货交易所，应当由国务院期货监督管理机构批准。  
设立期货交易所应当制定章程。期货交易所章程的制定和修改，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准。

**Article 80** The establishment, change, and dissolution of a futures exchange shall be subject to the approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures exchange shall formulate its articles of association upon establishment. The formulation and amendment of the articles of association of a futures exchange shall be subject to the approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　期货交易所应当在其名称中标明“商品交易所”或者“期货交易所”等字样。其他任何单位或者个人不得使用期货交易所或者其他可能产生混淆或者误导的名称。

**Article 81** A futures exchange shall indicate the words "commodity exchange" or "futures exchange" in its name. No other entity or individual may use the name of a futures exchange or any other name that may cause confusion or misrepresentation.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　期货交易所可以采取会员制或者公司制的组织形式。  
会员制期货交易所的组织机构由其章程规定。

**Article 82** A futures exchange may adopt a membership-based or a corporate organizational form.  
The organizational structure of a membership-based futures exchange shall be prescribed by its articles of association.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　期货交易所依照法律、行政法规和国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，制定有关业务规则；其中交易规则的制定和修改应当报国务院期货监督管理机构批准。  
期货交易所业务规则应当体现公平保护会员、交易者等市场相关各方合法权益的原则。  
在期货交易所从事期货交易及相关活动，应当遵守期货交易所依法制定的业务规则。违反业务规则的，由期货交易所给予纪律处分或者采取其他自律管理措施。

**Article 83** A futures exchange shall formulate relevant business rules in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council; the formulation and amendment of trading rules therein shall be reported to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for approval.  
The business rules of a futures exchange shall reflect the principle of fairly protecting the lawful rights and interests of members, traders, and other relevant parties in the market.  
Any person engaging in futures trading and related activities on a futures exchange shall comply with the business rules formulated by the futures exchange according to law. Anyone violating the business rules shall be subject to disciplinary sanctions or other self-regulatory measures imposed by the futures exchange.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　期货交易所的负责人由国务院期货监督管理机构提名或者任免。  
有《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的不适合担任公司董事、监事、高级管理人员的情形或者下列情形之一的，不得担任期货交易所的负责人：  
（一）因违法行为或者违纪行为被解除职务的期货经营机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员，或者期货交易场所、期货结算机构的负责人，自被解除职务之日起未逾五年；  
（二）因违法行为或者违纪行为被吊销执业证书或者被取消资格的注册会计师、律师或者其他期货服务机构的专业人员，自被吊销执业证书或者被取消资格之日起未逾五年。

**Article 84** The responsible persons of a futures exchange shall be nominated or appointed and removed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A person who is under any of the circumstances specified in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China that renders him or her unsuitable to serve as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a company, or falls under any of the following circumstances, shall not serve as a responsible person of a futures exchange:  
(1) having been removed from the position of director, supervisor, or senior officer of a futures operating institution, or as a responsible person of a futures trading venue or a futures clearing institution due to illegal or disciplinary acts, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of such removal;  
(2) having had his or her practice certificate revoked or his or her qualification cancelled as a certified public accountant, lawyer, or other professional of a futures service institution due to illegal or disciplinary acts, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of such revocation or cancellation.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　期货交易场所应当依照本法和国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，加强对交易活动的风险控制和对会员以及交易场所工作人员的监督管理，依法履行下列职责：  
（一）提供交易的场所、设施和服务；  
（二）设计期货合约、标准化期权合约品种，安排期货合约、标准化期权合约品种上市；  
（三）对期货交易进行实时监控和风险监测；  
（四）依照章程和业务规则对会员、交易者、期货服务机构等进行自律管理；  
（五）开展交易者教育和市场培育工作；  
（六）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他职责。  
期货交易场所不得直接或者间接参与期货交易。未经国务院批准，期货交易场所不得从事信托投资、股票投资、非自用不动产投资等与其职责无关的业务。

**Article 85** A futures trading venue shall, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, strengthen risk control over trading activities and supervision and management over members and staff of the trading venue, and perform the following duties according to law:  
(1) providing trading venues, facilities, and services;  
(2) designing futures contracts and standardized options contract varieties, and arranging for the listing of futures contracts and standardized options contract varieties;  
(3) conducting real-time monitoring and risk surveillance of futures trading;  
(4) exercising self-regulation over members, traders, futures service institutions, etc., in accordance with the articles of association and business rules;  
(5) carrying out trader education and market cultivation;  
(6) other duties prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures trading venue shall not participate directly or indirectly in futures trading. Without the approval of the State Council, a futures trading venue shall not engage in trust investment, stock investment, non-self-use real estate investment, or any other business unrelated to its duties.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　期货交易所的所得收益按照国家有关规定管理和使用，应当首先用于保证期货交易的场所、设施的运行和改善。

**Article 86** The income derived by a futures exchange shall be managed and used in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall first be used to ensure the operation and improvement of the futures trading venues and facilities.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　期货交易场所应当加强对期货交易的风险监测，出现异常情况的，期货交易场所可以依照业务规则，单独或者会同期货结算机构采取下列紧急措施，并立即报告国务院期货监督管理机构：  
（一）调整保证金；  
（二）调整涨跌停板幅度；  
（三）调整会员、交易者的交易限额或持仓限额标准；  
（四）限制开仓；  
（五）强行平仓；  
（六）暂时停止交易；  
（七）其他紧急措施。  
异常情况消失后，期货交易场所应当及时取消紧急措施。

**Article 87** A futures trading venue shall strengthen risk surveillance of futures trading. If an abnormal situation occurs, the futures trading venue may, in accordance with the business rules, take the following emergency measures alone or jointly with the futures clearing institution, and immediately report to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) adjusting margin;  
(2) adjusting the price limit range;  
(3) adjusting the trading or position limits for members or traders;  
(4) restricting the opening of positions;  
(5) forced liquidation;  
(6) temporary suspension of trading;  
(7) other emergency measures.  
After the abnormal situation disappears, the futures trading venue shall promptly cancel the emergency measures.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　期货交易场所应当实时公布期货交易即时行情，并按交易日制作期货市场行情表，予以公布。  
期货交易行情的权益由期货交易场所享有。未经期货交易场所许可，任何单位和个人不得发布期货交易行情。  
期货交易场所不得发布价格预测信息。  
期货交易场所应当依照国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，履行信息报告义务。

**Article 88** A futures trading venue shall publish real-time quotes of futures trading in a timely manner, and produce a futures market quotation table on each trading day and publish it.  
The rights and interests in futures trading quotes shall be enjoyed by the futures trading venue. Without the permission of the futures trading venue, no entity or individual may release futures trading quotes.  
A futures trading venue shall not release price forecast information.  
A futures trading venue shall fulfill its obligation to report information in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　因突发性事件影响期货交易正常进行时，为维护期货交易正常秩序和市场公平，期货交易场所可以按照本法和业务规则规定采取必要的处置措施，并应当及时向国务院期货监督管理机构报告。  
因前款规定的突发性事件导致期货交易结果出现重大异常，按交易结果进行结算、交割将对期货交易正常秩序和市场公平造成重大影响的，期货交易场所可以按照业务规则采取取消交易等措施，并应当及时向国务院期货监督管理机构报告并公告。

**Article 89** Where a sudden event affects the normal conduct of futures trading, in order to maintain the normal order of futures trading and market fairness, a futures trading venue may take necessary disposal measures in accordance with this Law and its business rules, and shall promptly report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where a sudden event as specified in the preceding paragraph causes material anomalies in the results of futures trading, and settlement or delivery based on the trading results would materially affect the normal order of futures trading and market fairness, the futures trading venue may take measures such as canceling transactions in accordance with its business rules, and shall promptly report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　期货交易场所对其依照[本法第八十七条](#art-87)、第八十九条规定采取措施造成的损失，不承担民事赔偿责任，但存在重大过错的除外。

**Article 90** A futures trading venue shall not bear civil compensation liability for losses caused by measures taken in accordance with Articles 87 and 89 of this Law, unless there is gross negligence.

### 第七章　期货结算机构

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　期货结算机构是指依法设立，为期货交易提供结算、交割服务，实行自律管理的法人。  
期货结算机构包括内部设有结算部门的期货交易场所、独立的期货结算机构和经国务院期货监督管理机构批准从事与证券业务相关的期货交易结算、交割业务的证券结算机构。

**Article 91** A futures clearing institution means a legal person established according to law that provides settlement and delivery services for futures trading and conducts self-regulation.  
Futures clearing institutions include futures trading venues with an internal settlement department, independent futures clearing institutions, and securities clearing institutions approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council to engage in settlement and delivery services for futures trading related to securities business.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　独立的期货结算机构的设立、变更和解散，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准。  
设立独立的期货结算机构，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具备良好的财务状况，注册资本最低限额符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定；  
（二）有具备任职专业知识和业务工作经验的高级管理人员；  
（三）具备完善的治理结构、内部控制制度和风险控制制度；  
（四）具备符合要求的营业场所、信息技术系统以及与期货交易的结算有关的其他设施；  
（五）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他条件。  
承担期货结算机构职责的期货交易场所，应当具备本条第二款规定的条件。  
国务院期货监督管理机构应当根据审慎监管原则进行审查，在六个月内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。

**Article 92** The establishment, alteration, and dissolution of an independent futures clearing institution shall be subject to approval by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The establishment of an independent futures clearing institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having a sound financial condition, with the minimum registered capital complying with the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(2) Having senior officers with the requisite professional knowledge and business work experience;  
(3) Having a complete governance structure, internal control system, and risk control system;  
(4) Having business premises, information technology systems, and other facilities related to the settlement of futures trading that meet the requirements;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures trading venue assuming the functions of a futures clearing institution shall meet the conditions specified in paragraph 2 of this Article.  
The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall conduct review in accordance with the principle of prudential supervision and shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within six months.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　期货结算机构作为中央对手方，是结算参与人共同对手方，进行净额结算，为期货交易提供集中履约保障。

**Article 93** A futures clearing institution, as a central counterparty, is the common counterparty of clearing participants, conducts netting settlement, and provides centralized performance guarantee for futures trading.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　期货结算机构履行下列职责：  
（一）组织期货交易的结算、交割；  
（二）按照章程和业务规则对交易者、期货经营机构、期货服务机构、非期货经营机构结算参与人等进行自律管理；  
（三）办理与期货交易的结算、交割有关的信息查询业务；  
（四）国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他职责。

**Article 94** A futures clearing institution performs the following duties:  
(1) Organizing the settlement and delivery of futures trading;  
(2) Conducting self-regulation of traders, futures operating institutions, futures service institutions, non-futures operating institution clearing participants, etc. in accordance with its articles of association and business rules;  
(3) Handling information inquiry services related to the settlement and delivery of futures trading;  
(4) Other duties prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　期货结算机构应当按照国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，在其业务规则中规定结算参与人制度、风险控制制度、信息安全管理制度、违规违约处理制度、应急处理及临时处置措施等事项。期货结算机构制定和修改章程、业务规则，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准。参与期货结算，应当遵守期货结算机构制定的业务规则。  
期货结算机构制定和执行业务规则，应当与期货交易场所的相关制度衔接、协调。

**Article 95** A futures clearing institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, prescribe in its business rules matters such as the clearing participant system, risk control system, information security management system, violation and default handling system, emergency handling and temporary disposal measures. The formulation and amendment of the articles of association and business rules of a futures clearing institution shall be approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. Participation in futures clearing shall comply with the business rules formulated by the futures clearing institution.  
The formulation and implementation of business rules by a futures clearing institution shall be consistent and coordinated with the relevant systems of the futures trading venue.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　期货结算机构应当建立流动性管理制度，保障结算活动的稳健运行。

**Article 96** A futures clearing institution shall establish a liquidity management system to ensure the stable operation of settlement activities.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　[本法第八十四条](#art-84)，第八十五条第二款，第八十六条，第八十八条第三款、第四款的规定，适用于独立的期货结算机构和经批准从事期货交易结算、交割业务的证券结算机构。

**Article 97** The provisions of Article 84, paragraph 2 of Article 85, Article 86, and paragraphs 3 and 4 of Article 88 of this Law shall apply to independent futures clearing institutions and securities clearing institutions approved to engage in futures trading settlement and delivery services.

### 第八章　期货服务机构

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构、期货保证金存管机构、交割库、信息技术服务机构等期货服务机构，应当勤勉尽责、恪尽职守，按照相关业务规则为期货交易及相关活动提供服务，并按照国务院期货监督管理机构的要求提供相关资料。

**Article 98** Futures service institutions such as accounting firms, law firms, asset appraisal institutions, futures margin depository institutions, delivery warehouses, and information technology service institutions shall be diligent and conscientious, provide services for futures trading and related activities in accordance with the relevant business rules, and provide relevant materials as required by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等期货服务机构接受期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构的委托出具审计报告、法律意见书等文件，应当对所依据的文件资料内容的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查和验证。

**Article 99** Where futures service institutions such as accounting firms, law firms, and asset appraisal institutions accept entrustment from futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, or futures clearing institutions to issue audit reports, legal opinions, or other documents, they shall verify and validate the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the content of the documents and materials relied upon.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　交割库包括交割仓库和交割厂库等。交割库为期货交易的交割提供相关服务，应当符合期货交易场所规定的条件。期货交易场所应当与交割库签订协议，明确双方的权利和义务。  
交割库不得有下列行为：  
（一）出具虚假仓单；  
（二）违反期货交易场所的业务规则，限制交割商品的出库、入库；  
（三）泄露与期货交易有关的商业秘密；  
（四）违反国家有关规定参与期货交易；  
（五）违反国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他行为。

**Article 100** Delivery warehouses include delivery storage facilities and delivery factories, among others. A delivery warehouse that provides relevant services for the delivery of futures trading shall meet the conditions prescribed by the futures trading venue. A futures trading venue shall enter into an agreement with the delivery warehouse, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
A delivery warehouse shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) Issuing false warehouse receipts;  
(2) Violating the business rules of the futures trading venue by restricting the outbound or inbound delivery of commodities;  
(3) Disclosing trade secrets related to futures trading;  
(4) Participating in futures trading in violation of relevant state provisions;  
(5) Other acts violating the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　为期货交易及相关活动提供信息技术系统服务的机构，应当符合国家及期货行业信息安全相关的技术管理规定和标准，并向国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
国务院期货监督管理机构可以依法要求信息技术服务机构提供前款规定的信息技术系统的相关材料。

**Article 101** An institution providing information technology system services for futures trading and related activities shall comply with the technical management regulations and standards of the state and the futures industry on information security, and shall file with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The securities regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to law, require an information technology service institution to provide relevant materials concerning the information technology system as specified in the preceding paragraph.

### 第九章　期货业协会

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　期货业协会是期货行业的自律性组织，是社会团体法人。  
期货经营机构应当加入期货业协会。期货服务机构可以加入期货业协会。

**Article 102** The Futures Industry Association is a self-regulatory organization of the futures industry and a social organization legal person.  
Futures operating institutions shall join the Futures Industry Association. Futures service institutions may join the Futures Industry Association.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　期货业协会的权力机构为会员大会。  
期货业协会的章程由会员大会制定，并报国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
期货业协会设理事会。理事会成员依照章程的规定选举产生。

**Article 103** The authority body of the Futures Industry Association is the general assembly of members.  
The articles of association of the Futures Industry Association shall be formulated by the general assembly of members and filed with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The Futures Industry Association shall have a council. Members of the council shall be elected in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　期货业协会履行下列职责：  
（一）制定和实施行业自律规则，监督、检查会员的业务活动及从业人员的执业行为，对违反法律、行政法规、国家有关规定、协会章程和自律规则的，按照规定给予纪律处分或者实施其他自律管理措施；  
（二）对会员之间、会员与交易者之间发生的纠纷进行调解；  
（三）依法维护会员的合法权益，向国务院期货监督管理机构反映会员的建议和要求；  
（四）组织期货从业人员的业务培训，开展会员间的业务交流；  
（五）教育会员和期货从业人员遵守期货法律法规和政策，组织开展行业诚信建设，建立行业诚信激励约束机制；  
（六）开展交易者教育和保护工作，督促会员落实交易者适当性管理制度，开展期货市场宣传；  
（七）对会员的信息安全工作实行自律管理，督促会员执行国家和行业信息安全相关规定和技术标准；  
（八）组织会员就期货行业的发展、运作及有关内容进行研究，收集整理、发布期货相关信息，提供会员服务，组织行业交流，引导行业创新发展；  
（九）期货业协会章程规定的其他职责。

**Article 104** The Futures Industry Association performs the following duties:  
(1) Formulating and implementing industry self-regulatory rules, supervising and inspecting the business activities of members and the practice conduct of practitioners, and imposing disciplinary sanctions or implementing other self-regulatory management measures according to regulations on those who violate laws, administrative regulations, relevant state provisions, the articles of association, or self-regulatory rules;  
(2) Mediating disputes between members, and between members and traders;  
(3) Safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of members according to law, and reflecting the suggestions and demands of members to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(4) Organizing professional training for futures practitioners and conducting business exchanges among members;  
(5) Educating members and futures practitioners to comply with futures laws, regulations, and policies, organizing and carrying out industry integrity building, and establishing an industry integrity incentive and restraint mechanism;  
(6) Conducting trader education and protection work, urging members to implement the trader suitability management system, and conducting futures market publicity;  
(7) Exercising self-regulation over members' information security work, and urging members to implement relevant national and industry information security regulations and technical standards;  
(8) Organizing members to conduct research on the development, operation, and related content of the futures industry, collecting, collating, and publishing futures-related information, providing member services, organizing industry exchanges, and guiding industry innovation and development;  
(9) Other duties as specified in the articles of association of the Futures Industry Association.

### 第十章　监督管理

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法对期货市场实行监督管理，维护期货市场公开、公平、公正，防范系统性风险，维护交易者合法权益，促进期货市场健康发展。  
国务院期货监督管理机构在对期货市场实施监督管理中，依法履行下列职责：  
（一）制定有关期货市场监督管理的规章、规则，并依法进行审批、核准、注册，办理备案；  
（二）对品种的上市、交易、结算、交割等期货交易及相关活动，进行监督管理；  
（三）对期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人等市场相关参与者的期货业务活动，进行监督管理；  
（四）制定期货从业人员的行为准则，并监督实施；  
（五）监督检查期货交易的信息公开情况；  
（六）维护交易者合法权益、开展交易者教育；  
（七）对期货违法行为进行查处；  
（八）监测监控并防范、处置期货市场风险；  
（九）对期货行业金融科技和信息安全进行监管；  
（十）对期货业协会的自律管理活动进行指导和监督；  
（十一）法律、行政法规规定的其他职责。  
国务院期货监督管理机构根据需要可以设立派出机构，依照授权履行监督管理职责。

**Article 105** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall exercise supervision and administration over the futures market according to law, maintain openness, fairness, and impartiality in the futures market, prevent systemic risks, protect the legitimate rights and interests of traders, and promote the healthy development of the futures market.  
In exercising supervision and administration over the futures market, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall perform the following duties according to law:  
(1) Formulating regulations and rules concerning the supervision and administration of the futures market, and conducting examination, approval, verification, registration, and filing according to law;  
(2) Supervising and administering the listing, trading, settlement, delivery, and other futures trading and related activities of varieties;  
(3) Supervising and administering the futures business activities of market participants such as futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions, and non-futures operating institution clearing participants;  
(4) Formulating codes of conduct for futures practitioners and supervising their implementation;  
(5) Supervising and inspecting the information disclosure of futures trading;  
(6) Safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of traders and conducting trader education;  
(7) Investigating and punishing violations of futures laws;  
(8) Monitoring, preventing, and disposing of futures market risks;  
(9) Supervising the financial technology and information security of the futures industry;  
(10) Guiding and supervising the self-regulatory activities of the Futures Industry Association;  
(11) Other duties as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The securities regulatory authority of the State Council may, as needed, establish dispatched agencies to perform supervision and administration duties as authorized.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）对期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构进行现场检查，并要求其报送有关的财务会计、业务活动、内部控制等资料；  
（二）进入涉嫌违法行为发生场所调查取证；  
（三）询问当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明，或者要求其按照指定的方式报送与被调查事件有关的文件和资料；  
（四）查阅、复制与被调查事件有关的财产权登记、通讯记录等文件和资料；  
（五）查阅、复制当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的期货交易记录、财务会计资料及其他相关文件和资料；对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件资料，可以予以封存、扣押；  
（六）查询当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的保证金账户和银行账户以及其他具有支付、托管、结算等功能的账户信息，可以对有关文件和资料进行复制；对有证据证明已经或者可能转移或者隐匿违法资金等涉案财产或者隐匿、伪造、毁损重要证据的，经国务院期货监督管理机构主要负责人或者其授权的其他负责人批准，可以冻结、查封，期限为六个月；因特殊原因需要延长的，每次延长期限不得超过三个月，最长期限不得超过二年；  
（七）在调查操纵期货市场、内幕交易等重大违法行为时，经国务院期货监督管理机构主要负责人或者其授权的其他负责人批准，可以限制被调查事件当事人的交易，但限制的时间不得超过三个月；案情复杂的，可以延长三个月；  
（八）决定并通知出境入境管理机关依法阻止涉嫌违法人员、涉嫌违法单位的主管人员和其他直接责任人员出境。  
为防范期货市场风险，维护市场秩序，国务院期货监督管理机构可以采取责令改正、监管谈话、出具警示函等措施。

**Article 106** When performing its duties according to law, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Conducting on-site inspections of futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, and futures clearing institutions, and requiring them to submit relevant financial accounting, business activity, internal control, and other materials;  
(2) Entering the place where an alleged illegal act occurs to investigate and collect evidence;  
(3) Inquiring of parties and units and individuals related to the matter under investigation, and requiring them to explain matters related to the matter under investigation, or to submit documents and materials related to the matter under investigation in a specified manner;  
(4) Inspecting and copying documents and materials such as property rights registration records and communication records related to the matter under investigation;  
(5) Inspecting and copying the futures trading records, financial accounting materials, and other relevant documents and materials of the parties and units and individuals related to the matter under investigation; sealing or seizing documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed, or destroyed;  
(6) Inquiring into the margin accounts, bank accounts, and other accounts with payment, custody, settlement, or other functions of the parties and units and individuals related to the matter under investigation, and copying relevant documents and materials; where there is evidence that illegal funds or other property involved in the case has been or may be transferred or concealed, or that important evidence has been or may be concealed, falsified, or destroyed, freezing or sealing such property with the approval of the principal responsible person of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council or another responsible person authorized thereby, for a period of six months; where an extension is necessary due to special reasons, each extension shall not exceed three months, and the maximum period shall not exceed two years;  
(7) When investigating major illegal acts such as market manipulation or insider trading, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council or another responsible person authorized thereby, restricting the trading of the parties under investigation, but the restriction period shall not exceed three months; if the case is complex, it may be extended by three months;  
(8) Deciding and notifying the exit-entry administration authority to prevent, according to law, the departure from the country of persons suspected of illegal acts, the principal responsible persons of units suspected of illegal acts, and other persons directly responsible.  
In order to prevent risks in the futures market and maintain market order, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may take measures such as ordering corrections, supervisory interviews, and issuing warning letters.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，进行监督检查或者调查，其监督检查、调查的人员不得少于二人，并应当出示执法证件和检查、调查、查询等相关执法文书。监督检查、调查的人员少于二人或者未出示执法证件和有关执法文书的，被检查、调查的单位或者个人有权拒绝。

**Article 107** When the securities regulatory authority of the State Council performs its duties according to law and conducts supervision, inspection, or investigation, the number of personnel conducting such supervision, inspection, or investigation shall be no less than two, and they shall produce law enforcement credentials and relevant law enforcement documents such as inspection, investigation, and inquiry documents. Where the number of personnel conducting supervision, inspection, or investigation is less than two, or where they fail to produce law enforcement credentials and relevant law enforcement documents, the unit or individual under inspection or investigation shall have the right to refuse.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　国务院期货监督管理机构的工作人员，应当依法办事，忠于职守，公正廉洁，保守国家秘密和有关当事人的商业秘密，不得利用职务便利牟取不正当利益。

**Article 108** The staff of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall act according to law, be faithful to their duties, be impartial and honest, keep state secrets and the trade secrets of the parties concerned, and shall not seek illegitimate benefits by taking advantage of their positions.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，被检查、调查的单位和个人应当配合，如实作出说明或者提供有关文件和资料，不得拒绝、阻碍和隐瞒。  
国务院期货监督管理机构与其他相关部门，应当建立信息共享等监督管理协调配合机制。国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，进行监督检查或者调查时，有关部门应当予以配合。

**Article 109** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall perform its duties according to law, and the entities and individuals subject to inspection or investigation shall cooperate, truthfully provide explanations or relevant documents and materials, and shall not refuse, obstruct, or conceal.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments shall establish coordination and cooperation mechanisms for supervision and administration, such as information sharing. When the futures regulatory authority of the State Council performs its duties according to law and conducts supervision, inspection, or investigation, the relevant departments shall cooperate.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　对涉嫌期货违法、违规行为，任何单位和个人有权向国务院期货监督管理机构举报。  
对涉嫌重大违法、违规行为的实名举报线索经查证属实的，国务院期货监督管理机构按照规定给予举报人奖励。  
国务院期货监督管理机构应当对举报人的身份信息保密。

**Article 110** Any entity or individual may report to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council any suspected illegal or irregular futures activities.  
Where a real-name report clue involving suspected major illegal or irregular activities is verified as true, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions, give a reward to the whistleblower.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall keep the identity information of the whistleblower confidential.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　国务院期货监督管理机构制定的规章、规则和监督管理工作制度应当依法公开。  
国务院期货监督管理机构依据调查结果，对期货违法行为作出的处罚决定，应当依法公开。

**Article 111** The rules, regulations, and supervision and administration work systems formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall be made public according to law.  
The penalty decisions made by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on futures illegal activities based on the results of investigations shall be made public according to law.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　国务院期货监督管理机构对涉嫌期货违法的单位或者个人进行调查期间，被调查的当事人书面申请，承诺在国务院期货监督管理机构认可的期限内纠正涉嫌违法行为，赔偿有关交易者损失，消除损害或者不良影响的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以决定中止调查。被调查的当事人履行承诺的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以决定终止调查；被调查的当事人未履行承诺或者有国务院规定的其他情形的，应当恢复调查。具体办法由国务院规定。  
国务院期货监督管理机构中止或者终止调查的，应当按照规定公开相关信息。

**Article 112** During the period when the futures regulatory authority of the State Council investigates an entity or individual suspected of futures illegal activities, if the investigated party makes a written application and undertakes to rectify the suspected illegal activities within a time limit recognized by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, compensate the losses of relevant traders, and eliminate the harm or adverse effects, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may decide to suspend the investigation. If the investigated party fulfills the undertaking, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may decide to terminate the investigation; if the investigated party fails to fulfill the undertaking or there are other circumstances prescribed by the State Council, the investigation shall be resumed. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
If the futures regulatory authority of the State Council suspends or terminates the investigation, it shall make relevant information public in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法将有关期货市场主体遵守本法的情况纳入期货市场诚信档案。

**Article 113** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to law, incorporate the information on whether relevant futures market entities comply with this Law into the futures market good faith records.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，发现期货违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法将案件移送司法机关处理；发现公职人员涉嫌职务违法或者职务犯罪的，应当依法移送监察机关处理。

**Article 114** When performing its duties according to law, if the futures regulatory authority of the State Council discovers that a futures illegal activity is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall transfer the case to the judicial authorities for handling according to law; if it discovers that a public official is suspected of job-related illegality or job-related crime, it shall transfer the case to the supervisory authorities for handling according to law.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　国务院期货监督管理机构应当建立健全期货市场监测监控制度，通过专门机构加强保证金安全存管监控。

**Article 115** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish and improve a futures market monitoring and control system, and strengthen the safe deposit monitoring and control of margin through specialized institutions.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　为防范交易及结算的风险，期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人应当从业务收入中按照国务院期货监督管理机构、国务院财政部门的规定提取、管理和使用风险准备金。

**Article 116** For the purpose of preventing risks in trading and clearing, futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, and clearing participants that are not futures operating institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and the finance department of the State Council, set aside, manage, and use risk reserves from their business income.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人等应当按照规定妥善保存与业务经营相关的资料和信息，任何人不得泄露、隐匿、伪造、篡改或者毁损。期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人的信息和资料的保存期限不得少于二十年；期货服务机构的信息和资料的保存期限不得少于十年。

**Article 117** Futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, futures service institutions, and clearing participants that are not futures operating institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions, properly preserve materials and information related to their business operations. No person shall disclose, conceal, forge, tamper with, or destroy them. The retention period for information and materials of futures operating institutions, futures trading venues, futures clearing institutions, and clearing participants that are not futures operating institutions shall not be less than twenty years; the retention period for information and materials of futures service institutions shall not be less than ten years.

### 第十一章　跨境交易与监管协作

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　境外期货交易场所向境内单位或者个人提供直接接入该交易场所交易系统进行交易服务的，应当向国务院期货监督管理机构申请注册，接受国务院期货监督管理机构的监督管理，国务院期货监督管理机构另有规定的除外。

**Article 118** Where an overseas futures trading venue provides services for entities or individuals within the territory to directly access its trading system for trading, it shall apply for registration with the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and accept the supervision and administration of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, unless otherwise provided by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　境外期货交易场所上市的期货合约、期权合约和衍生品合约，以境内期货交易场所上市的合约价格进行挂钩结算的，应当符合国务院期货监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 119** Where futures contracts, options contracts, and derivative contracts listed on overseas futures trading venues are settled by linking to the prices of contracts listed on domestic futures trading venues, they shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　境内单位或者个人从事境外期货交易，应当委托具有境外期货经纪业务资格的境内期货经营机构进行，国务院另有规定的除外。  
境内期货经营机构转委托境外期货经营机构从事境外期货交易的，该境外期货经营机构应当向国务院期货监督管理机构申请注册，接受国务院期货监督管理机构的监督管理，国务院期货监督管理机构另有规定的除外。

**Article 120** Where any domestic entity or individual engages in overseas futures trading, it/he shall entrust a domestic futures operating institution with the qualification for overseas futures brokerage business to conduct such trading, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.  
Where a domestic futures operating institution sub-entrusts an overseas futures operating institution to engage in overseas futures trading, such overseas futures operating institution shall apply for registration with the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and accept the supervision and administration of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, unless otherwise provided by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　境外期货交易场所在境内设立代表机构的，应当向国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
境外期货交易场所代表机构及其工作人员，不得从事或者变相从事任何经营活动。

**Article 121** Where an overseas futures trading venue establishes a representative office within the territory, it shall file with the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The representative office of an overseas futures trading venue and its staff members shall not engage in or engage in any business activities in disguised form.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　境外机构在境内从事期货市场营销、推介及招揽活动，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准，适用本法的相关规定。  
境内机构为境外机构在境内从事期货市场营销、推介及招揽活动，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准。  
任何单位或者个人不得从事违反前两款规定的期货市场营销、推介及招揽活动。

**Article 122** Where an overseas institution engages in futures marketing, promotion, and solicitation activities within the territory, it shall be approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, and the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.  
Where a domestic institution engages in futures marketing, promotion, and solicitation activities within the territory on behalf of an overseas institution, it shall be approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
No entity or individual may engage in futures marketing, promotion, and solicitation activities in violation of the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　国务院期货监督管理机构可以和境外期货监督管理机构建立监督管理合作机制，或者加入国际组织，实施跨境监督管理。  
国务院期货监督管理机构应境外期货监督管理机构请求提供协助的，应当遵循国家法律、法规的规定和对等互惠的原则，不得泄露国家秘密，不得损害国家利益和社会公共利益。

**Article 123** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may establish supervision and administration cooperation mechanisms with overseas futures regulatory authorities, or join international organizations, to implement cross-border supervision and administration.  
Where the futures regulatory authority of the State Council provides assistance at the request of an overseas futures regulatory authority, it shall comply with the provisions of national laws and regulations and the principle of reciprocity, and shall not disclose state secrets or harm national interests and social public interests.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　国务院期货监督管理机构可以按照与境外期货监督管理机构达成的监管合作安排，接受境外期货监督管理机构的请求，依照本法规定的职责和程序为其进行调查取证。境外期货监督管理机构应当提供有关案件材料，并说明其正在就被调查当事人涉嫌违反请求方当地期货法律法规的行为进行调查。境外期货监督管理机构不得在中华人民共和国境内直接进行调查取证等活动。  
未经国务院期货监督管理机构和国务院有关主管部门同意，任何单位和个人不得擅自向境外监督管理机构提供与期货业务活动有关的文件和资料。  
国务院期货监督管理机构可以依照与境外期货监督管理机构达成的监管合作安排，请求境外期货监督管理机构进行调查取证。

**Article 124** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may, in accordance with the regulatory cooperation arrangement reached with the overseas futures regulatory authority, accept a request from the overseas futures regulatory authority and conduct investigations and evidence collection for it in accordance with the functions and procedures specified in this Law. The overseas futures regulatory authority shall provide relevant case materials and explain that it is investigating the party concerned for suspected violation of the local futures laws and regulations of the requesting party. The overseas futures regulatory authority shall not directly conduct investigations, evidence collection, or other activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Without the consent of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and the relevant competent department of the State Council, no entity or individual may provide documents and materials related to futures business activities to an overseas regulatory authority without authorization.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may, in accordance with the regulatory cooperation arrangement reached with the overseas futures regulatory authority, request the overseas futures regulatory authority to conduct investigations and evidence collection.

### 第十二章　法律责任

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　违反[本法第十二条](#art-12)的规定，操纵期货市场或者衍生品市场的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，处以一百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款。单位操纵市场的，还应当对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。  
操纵市场行为给交易者造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 125** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 12 of this Law by manipulating the futures market or the derivatives market shall be ordered to make corrections, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 1 million, a fine of not less than RMB 1 million but not more than RMB 10 million shall be imposed. Where an entity manipulates the market, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall also be given a warning and imposed a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5 million.  
Where the act of market manipulation causes losses to traders, the perpetrator shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　违反[本法第十三条](#art-13)的规定从事内幕交易的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。单位从事内幕交易的，还应当对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。  
国务院期货监督管理机构、国务院授权的部门、期货交易场所、期货结算机构的工作人员从事内幕交易的，从重处罚。  
内幕交易行为给交易者造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 126** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 13 of this Law by engaging in insider trading shall be ordered to make corrections, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5 million shall be imposed. Where an entity engages in insider trading, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall also be given a warning and imposed a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2 million.  
Where staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, the department authorized by the State Council, a futures trading venue, or a futures clearing institution engage in insider trading, they shall be given a severe punishment.  
Where the act of insider trading causes losses to traders, the perpetrator shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第一款、第三款的规定，编造、传播虚假信息或者误导性信息，扰乱期货市场、衍生品市场的，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。  
违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第二款的规定，在期货交易、衍生品交易活动中作出虚假陈述或者信息误导的，责令改正，处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，还应当依法给予处分。  
传播媒介及其从事期货市场、衍生品市场信息报道的工作人员违反[本法第十六条](#art-16)第三款的规定，从事与其工作职责发生利益冲突的期货交易、衍生品交易的，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，处以十万元以下的罚款。  
编造、传播有关期货交易、衍生品交易的虚假信息，或者在期货交易、衍生品交易中作出信息误导，给交易者造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 127** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 3 of Article 16 of this Law by fabricating and disseminating false information or misleading information, disrupting the futures market or the derivatives market, shall have the illegal gains confiscated and be imposed a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2 million shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and imposed a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1 million.  
Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 16 of this Law by making false statements or providing misleading information in futures trading or derivatives trading activities shall be ordered to make corrections and imposed a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2 million; if the violator is a state functionary, he shall also be given a sanction according to law.  
Where a communication medium or its staff member engaged in reporting information on the futures market or the derivatives market violates the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 16 of this Law by engaging in futures trading or derivatives trading that conflicts with his work duties, the illegal gains shall be confiscated and a fine of not more than one time the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000, a fine of not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed.  
Where the fabrication and dissemination of false information regarding futures trading or derivatives trading, or the provision of misleading information in futures trading or derivatives trading, causes losses to traders, the perpetrator shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　违反[本法第十八条](#art-18)第二款的规定，出借自己的期货账户或者借用他人的期货账户从事期货交易的，责令改正，给予警告，可以处五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 128** Whoever violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 18 of this Law by lending his own futures account or borrowing another person's futures account to engage in futures trading shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and may be imposed a fine of not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　违反[本法第二十一条](#art-21)的规定，采取程序化交易影响期货交易场所系统安全或者正常交易秩序的，责令改正，并处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 129** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Article 21 of this Law, engages in programmatic trading that affects the system security or normal trading order of a futures trading venue shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　违反[本法第二十七条](#art-27)规定，未报告有关重大事项的，责令改正，给予警告，可以处一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 130** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Article 27 of this Law, fails to report relevant material matters shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and may be fined not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　法律、行政法规和国务院期货监督管理机构规定禁止参与期货交易的人员，违反[本法第五十三条](#art-53)的规定，直接或者以化名、借他人名义参与期货交易的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，还应当依法给予处分。

**Article 131** Any person who is prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, or the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council from participating in futures trading, but directly or under an alias or in the name of another person, in violation of the provisions of Article 53 of this Law, participates in futures trading shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if such person is a state functionary, disciplinary action shall also be taken according to law.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　非法设立期货公司，或者未经核准从事相关期货业务的，予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，处以一百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 132** Any person who illegally establishes a futures company, or engages in relevant futures business without approval, shall be banned, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 1,000,000, a fine of not less than RMB 1,000,000 but not more than RMB 10,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　提交虚假申请文件或者采取其他欺诈手段骗取期货公司设立许可、重大事项变更核准或者期货经营机构期货业务许可的，撤销相关许可，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 133** Any person who submits false application documents or uses other fraudulent means to obtain a license for the establishment of a futures company, approval for a material change, or a license for futures business of a futures operating institution shall have the relevant license revoked, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　期货经营机构违反[本法第四十条](#art-40)、第六十二条、第六十五条、第六十八条、第七十一条、第七十二条的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销期货业务许可证。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
期货经营机构有前款所列违法情形，给交易者造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。  
期货经营机构的主要股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人违反[本法第七十一条](#art-71)规定的，依照本条第一款的规定处罚。

**Article 134** A futures operating institution that violates Articles 40, 62, 65, 68, 71, or 72 of this Law shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000 shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its futures business license revoked. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.  
  
If a futures operating institution commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph and causes losses to traders, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
  
If a major shareholder, actual controller, or other related person of a futures operating institution violates the provisions of Article 71 of this Law, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　期货经营机构违反[本法第五十条](#art-50)交易者适当性管理规定，或者违反[本法第六十六条](#art-66)规定从事经纪业务接受交易者全权委托，或者有第七十八条损害交易者利益行为的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销相关业务许可。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。  
期货经营机构有[本法第七十八条](#art-78)规定的行为，给交易者造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 135** A futures operating institution that violates the provisions on trader suitability management in Article 50 of this Law, or violates the provisions of Article 66 of this Law by engaging in brokerage business and accepting discretionary authorization from traders, or commits any act under Article 78 that harms the interests of traders shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant business license shall be revoked. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000.  
  
If a futures operating institution commits any act specified in Article 78 of this Law and causes losses to traders, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)、第八十条、第九十二条规定，非法设立期货交易场所、期货结算机构，或者以其他形式组织期货交易的，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，处以一百万元以上一千万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。非法设立期货交易场所的，由县级以上人民政府予以取缔。  
违反[本法第三十条](#art-30)规定，未经批准组织开展衍生品交易的，或者金融机构违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)规定，未经批准、核准开展衍生品交易的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 136** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Articles 11, 80, and 92 of this Law, illegally establishes a futures trading venue or a futures clearing institution, or organizes futures trading in other forms shall have the illegal gains confiscated and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 1,000,000, a fine of not less than RMB 1,000,000 but not more than RMB 10,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000. If a futures trading venue is illegally established, it shall be banned by the people's government at or above the county level.  
  
Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Article 30 of this Law, organizes derivative transactions without approval, or any financial institution that, in violation of the provisions of Article 31 of this Law, conducts derivative transactions without approval or authorization shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　期货交易场所、期货结算机构违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)、第四十条、第八十五条第二款规定的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 137** A futures trading venue or futures clearing institution that violates the provisions of Article 17, Article 40, or paragraph 2 of Article 85 of this Law shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000 shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　期货交易场所、期货结算机构违反[本法第八十八条](#art-88)第三款规定发布价格预测信息的，责令改正，给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 138** A futures trading venue or futures clearing institution that, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 88 of this Law, releases price forecast information shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　期货服务机构违反[本法第九十八条](#art-98)的规定，从事期货服务业务未按照要求提供相关资料的，责令改正，可以处二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 139** A futures service institution that, in violation of the provisions of Article 98 of this Law, fails to provide relevant materials as required when engaging in futures service business shall be ordered to make corrections and may be fined not more than RMB 200,000.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等期货服务机构违反[本法第九十九条](#art-99)的规定，未勤勉尽责，所制作、出具的文件有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，责令改正，没收业务收入，并处以业务收入一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有业务收入或者业务收入不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。  
期货服务机构有前款所列违法行为，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 140** An accounting firm, law firm, asset appraisal institution, or other futures service institution that, in violation of the provisions of Article 99 of this Law, fails to discharge its duties with due diligence, and the documents produced or issued contain false records, misleading statements, or material omissions shall be ordered to make corrections, have the business income confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the business income; if there is no business income or the business income is less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000.  
  
If a futures service institution commits any of the illegal acts listed in the preceding paragraph and causes losses to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　交割库有[本法第一百条](#art-100)所列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令期货交易场所暂停或者取消其交割库资格。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 141** A delivery warehouse that commits any of the acts listed in Article 100 of this Law shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the futures trading venue shall be ordered to suspend or revoke its qualification as a delivery warehouse. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　信息技术服务机构违反[本法第一百零一条](#art-101)规定未报备案的，责令改正，可以处二十万元以下的罚款。  
信息技术服务机构违反[本法第一百零一条](#art-101)规定，提供的服务不符合国家及期货行业信息安全相关的技术管理规定和标准的，责令改正，没收业务收入，并处以业务收入一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有业务收入或者业务收入不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 142** An information technology service institution that, in violation of the provisions of Article 101 of this Law, fails to file for recordation shall be ordered to make corrections and may be fined not more than RMB 200,000.  
  
An information technology service institution that, in violation of the provisions of Article 101 of this Law, provides services that do not comply with the national and futures industry technical management regulations and standards on information security shall be ordered to make corrections, have the business income confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the business income; if there is no business income or the business income is less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　违反[本法第一百一十六条](#art-116)的规定，期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人未按照规定提取、管理和使用风险准备金的，责令改正，给予警告。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 143** A futures operating institution, futures trading venue, futures clearing institution, or non-futures operating institution clearing participant that, in violation of the provisions of Article 116 of this Law, fails to set aside, manage, and use the risk reserve fund in accordance with regulations shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　违反[本法第一百一十七条](#art-117)的规定，期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构、期货服务机构和非期货经营机构结算参与人等未按照规定妥善保存与业务经营相关的资料和信息的，责令改正，给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；泄露、隐匿、伪造、篡改或者毁损有关文件资料的，责令改正，给予警告，并处以二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款，并暂停、吊销相关业务许可或者禁止从事相关业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 144** A futures operating institution, futures trading venue, futures clearing institution, futures service institution, or non-futures operating institution clearing participant that, in violation of the provisions of Article 117 of this Law, fails to properly retain materials and information related to business operations in accordance with regulations shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if such person divulges, conceals, forges, alters, or destroys relevant documents or materials, it shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and be fined not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 2,000,000; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed, and the relevant business license shall be suspended or revoked, or it shall be prohibited from engaging in the relevant business. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　境外期货交易场所和期货经营机构违反[本法第一百一十八条](#art-118)和第一百二十条的规定，未向国务院期货监督管理机构申请注册的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 145** An overseas futures trading venue or futures operating institution that, in violation of the provisions of Articles 118 and 120 of this Law, fails to apply for registration with the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall be ordered to make corrections, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　境内单位或者个人违反[本法第一百二十条](#art-120)第一款规定的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂停其境外期货交易。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 146** Any domestic entity or individual that violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 120 of this Law shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if the circumstances are serious, its or his overseas futures trading shall be suspended. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　境外期货交易场所在境内设立的代表机构及其工作人员违反[本法第一百二十一条](#art-121)的规定，从事或者变相从事任何经营活动的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 147** A representative office established in China by an overseas futures trading venue and its staff members that, in violation of the provisions of Article 121 of this Law, engage in or engage in any business activities in disguised form shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　违反[本法第一百二十二条](#art-122)的规定在境内从事市场营销、推介及招揽活动的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处以违法所得一倍以上十倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，处以五十万元以上五百万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 148** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Article 122 of this Law, engages in marketing, promotion, or solicitation activities within the territory of China shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, have the illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 500,000, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000 shall be imposed. The directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　拒绝、阻碍国务院期货监督管理机构或者国务院授权的部门及其工作人员依法行使监督检查、调查职权的，责令改正，处以十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，并由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 149** Where a party refuses or obstructs the State Council's futures regulatory authority or a department authorized by the State Council and its staff members from exercising their supervisory inspection or investigation powers according to law, the party shall be ordered to make corrections, shall be imposed with a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan, and the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　违反法律、行政法规或者国务院期货监督管理机构的有关规定，情节严重的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以对有关责任人员采取期货市场禁入的措施。  
前款所称期货市场禁入，是指在一定期限内直至终身不得进行期货交易、从事期货业务，不得担任期货经营机构、期货交易场所、期货结算机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员或者负责人的制度。

**Article 150** Where a party violates laws, administrative regulations, or the relevant provisions of the State Council's futures regulatory authority, and the circumstances are serious, the State Council's futures regulatory authority may impose a measure of futures market ban on the relevant responsible persons.  
The term "futures market ban" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to a system under which a person is prohibited from engaging in futures trading or futures business, and from serving as a director, supervisor, senior officer, or person-in-charge of a futures operating institution, futures exchange or futures clearing institution, for a specified period or for life.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　本法规定的行政处罚，由国务院期货监督管理机构、国务院授权的部门按照国务院规定的职责分工作出决定；法律、行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 151** The administrative penalties provided for in this Law shall be decided by the State Council's futures regulatory authority or the department authorized by the State Council in accordance with the division of functions and duties as prescribed by the State Council; where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise, those provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　国务院期货监督管理机构或者国务院授权的部门的工作人员，不履行本法规定的职责，滥用职权、玩忽职守，利用职务便利牟取不正当利益，或者泄露所知悉的有关单位和个人的商业秘密的，依法追究法律责任。

**Article 152** Where a staff member of the State Council's futures regulatory authority or the department authorized by the State Council fails to perform the duties as prescribed by this Law, abuses authority, neglects duty, takes advantage of his position to seek illegitimate interests, or discloses the trade secrets of relevant entities or individuals that he has come to know, he shall be subject to legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 153** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金、违法所得，违法行为人的财产不足以支付的，优先用于承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 154** Where a party violates the provisions of this Law, and shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines, penalty monies, or illegal gains, if his property is insufficient to cover such payments, it shall be used first to satisfy the civil compensation liability.

### 第十三章　附则

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　本法自2022年8月1日起施行。

**Article 155** This Law shall come into force as of August 1, 2022.
