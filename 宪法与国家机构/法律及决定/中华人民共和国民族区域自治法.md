# 中华人民共和国民族区域自治法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：宪法相关法  
**公布日期**：2001-02-28  
**生效日期**：2001-02-28

> （1984年5月31日第六届全国人民代表大会第二次会议通过　根据2001年2月28日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国民族区域自治法〉的决定》修正） 目　　录 序　　言 第一章　总　　则 第二章　民族自治地方的建立和自治机关的组成 第三章　自治机关的自治权 第四章　民族自治地方的人民法院和人民检察院 第五章　民族自治地方内的民族关系 第六章　上级国家机关的职责 第七章　附　　则 序　　言 中华人民共和国是全国各族人民共同缔造的统一的多民族国家。民族区域自治是中国共产党运用马克思列宁主义解决我国民族问题的基本政策，是国家的一项基本政治制度。 民族区域自治是在国家统一领导下，各少数民族聚居的地方实行区域自治，设立自治机关，行使自治权。实行民族区域自治，体现了国家充分尊重和保障各少数民族管理本民族内部事务权利的精神，体现了国家坚持实行各民族平等、团结和共同繁荣的原则。 实行民族区域自治，对发挥各族人民当家作主的积极性，发展平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系，巩固国家的统一，促进民族自治地方和全国社会主义建设事业的发展，都起了巨大的作用。今后，继续坚持和完善民族区域自治制度，使这一制度在国家的社会主义现代化建设进程中发挥更大的作用。 实践证明，坚持实行民族区域自治，必须切实保障民族自治地方根据本地实际情况贯彻执行国家的法律和政策；必须大量培养少数民族的各级干部、各种专业人才和技术工人；民族自治地方必须发扬自力更生、艰苦奋斗精神，努力发展本地方的社会主义建设事业，为国家建设作出贡献；国家根据国民经济和社会发展计划，努力帮助民族自治地方加速经济和文化的发展。在维护民族团结的斗争中，要反对大民族主义，主要是大汉族主义，也要反对地方民族主义。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　中华人民共和国民族区域自治法，根据中华人民共和国宪法制定。

**Article 1** The Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional National Autonomy is enacted in accordance with the Constitution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各少数民族聚居的地方实行区域自治。  
民族自治地方分为自治区、自治州、自治县。  
各民族自治地方都是中华人民共和国不可分离的部分。

**Article 2** Regional autonomy shall be practised in areas where minority nationalities live in concentrated communities.  
National autonomous areas shall be divided into autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures and autonomous counties.  
All national autonomous areas are integral parts of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民族自治地方设立自治机关，自治机关是国家的一级地方政权机关。  
民族自治地方的自治机关实行民主集中制的原则。

**Article 3** Organs of self-government shall be established in national autonomous areas. Such organs shall be local state organs at a particular level.  
The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall apply the principle of democratic centralism.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民族自治地方的自治机关行使宪法第三章第五节规定的地方国家机关的职权，同时依照宪法和本法以及其他法律规定的权限行使自治权，根据本地方的实际情况贯彻执行国家的法律、政策。  
自治州的自治机关行使下设区、县的市的地方国家机关的职权，同时行使自治权。

**Article 4** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall exercise the functions and powers of local state organs as specified in Section 5, Chapter III of the Constitution. At the same time, they shall, in accordance with the limits of their authority prescribed by the Constitution, this Law and other laws, exercise the power of autonomy and implement the laws and policies of the state in the light of local conditions.  
The organs of self-government of autonomous prefectures shall exercise the functions and powers of local state organs for cities divided into districts and counties, and at the same time exercise the power of autonomy.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民族自治地方的自治机关必须维护国家的统一，保证宪法和法律在本地方的遵守和执行。

**Article 5** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas must uphold the unity of the state and ensure that the Constitution and other laws are observed and implemented in their areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民族自治地方的自治机关领导各族人民集中力量进行社会主义现代化建设。  
民族自治地方的自治机关根据本地方的情况，在不违背宪法和法律的原则下，有权采取特殊政策和灵活措施，加速民族自治地方经济、文化建设事业的发展。  
民族自治地方的自治机关在国家计划的指导下，从实际出发，不断提高劳动生产率和经济效益，发展社会生产力，逐步提高各民族的物质生活水平。  
民族自治地方的自治机关继承和发扬民族文化的优良传统，建设具有民族特点的社会主义精神文明，不断提高各民族人民的社会主义觉悟和科学文化水平。

**Article 6** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall lead the people of all nationalities in concentrating their efforts on socialist modernization.  
The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall, in the light of local conditions and without violating the principles of the Constitution and other laws, have the power to adopt special policies and flexible measures to speed up the development of economic and cultural undertakings in their areas.  
Under the guidance of state plans and proceeding from actual conditions, the organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall constantly raise labour productivity and economic efficiency, develop the social productive forces and gradually improve the material living standards of the people of all nationalities.  
The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall inherit and carry forward the fine traditions of national cultures and build a socialist spiritual civilization with national characteristics, constantly raising the socialist consciousness and scientific and cultural levels of the people of all nationalities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　民族自治地方的自治机关要把国家的整体利益放在首位，积极完成上级国家机关交给的各项任务。

**Article 7** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall place the overall interests of the state above everything else and actively fulfil the tasks assigned by state organs at higher levels.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　上级国家机关保障民族自治地方的自治机关行使自治权，并且依据民族自治地方的特点和需要，努力帮助民族自治地方加速发展社会主义建设事业。

**Article 8** State organs at higher levels shall ensure that the organs of self-government of national autonomous areas exercise their power of autonomy, and shall, in accordance with the characteristics and needs of national autonomous areas, make efforts to help them accelerate the development of socialist construction.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　上级国家机关和民族自治地方的自治机关维护和发展各民族的平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系。禁止对任何民族的歧视和压迫，禁止破坏民族团结和制造民族分裂的行为。

**Article 9** State organs at higher levels and the organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall maintain and develop the socialist ethnic relations of equality, unity and mutual assistance. Discrimination against or oppression of any nationality shall be prohibited. Any act that undermines the unity of the nationalities or instigates their division shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民族自治地方的自治机关保障本地方各民族都有使用和发展自己的语言文字的自由，都有保持或者改革自己的风俗习惯的自由。

**Article 10** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall ensure that all nationalities in their areas have freedom to use and develop their own spoken and written languages and freedom to preserve or reform their own folkways and customs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　民族自治地方的自治机关保障各民族公民有宗教信仰自由。  
任何国家机关、社会团体和个人不得强制公民信仰宗教或者不信仰宗教，不得歧视信仰宗教的公民和不信仰宗教的公民。  
国家保护正常的宗教活动。  
任何人不得利用宗教进行破坏社会秩序、损害公民身体健康、妨碍国家教育制度的活动。  
宗教团体和宗教事务不受外国势力的支配。

**Article 11** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall ensure that citizens of all nationalities enjoy freedom of religious belief.  
No state organ, public organization or individual may compel citizens to believe in, or not to believe in, any religion, nor may they discriminate against citizens who believe in, or do not believe in, any religion.  
The state shall protect normal religious activities.  
No one may make use of religion to engage in activities that disrupt public order, impair the health of citizens or interfere with the educational system of the state.  
Religious groups and religious affairs shall not be subject to the domination of foreign forces.

### 第二章　民族自治地方的建立和自治机关的组成

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　少数民族聚居的地方，根据当地民族关系、经济发展等条件，并参酌历史情况，可以建立以一个或者几个少数民族聚居区为基础的自治地方。  
民族自治地方内其他少数民族聚居的地方，建立相应的自治地方或者民族乡。  
民族自治地方依据本地方的实际情况，可以包括一部分汉族或者其他民族的居民区和城镇。

**Article 12** An autonomous area may be established in a place where one or more minority nationalities live in concentrated communities, taking into account local relations among nationalities, economic development and other conditions, and with reference to historical circumstances.  
In a national autonomous area, corresponding autonomous areas or nationality townships may be established in places where other minority nationalities live in concentrated communities.  
A national autonomous area may include part of the residential areas and towns of the Han nationality or other nationalities in the light of actual local conditions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　民族自治地方的名称，除特殊情况外，按照地方名称、民族名称、行政地位的顺序组成。

**Article 13** With the exception of special circumstances, the name of a national autonomous area shall be composed of the name of the place, the name of the nationality and the word indicating the administrative status, in that order.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民族自治地方的建立、区域界线的划分、名称的组成，由上级国家机关会同有关地方的国家机关，和有关民族的代表充分协商拟定，按照法律规定的程序报请批准。  
民族自治地方一经建立，未经法定程序，不得撤销或者合并；民族自治地方的区域界线一经确定，未经法定程序，不得变动；确实需要撤销、合并或者变动的，由上级国家机关的有关部门和民族自治地方的自治机关充分协商拟定，按照法定程序报请批准。

**Article 14** The establishment of a national autonomous area, the delimitation of its boundaries and the composition of its name shall be proposed through full consultation between the state organ at a higher level and the state organ of the locality concerned, and with the representatives of the nationality or nationalities involved, and shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law.  
Once a national autonomous area is established, it may not be abolished or merged without going through the procedures prescribed by law. Once the boundaries of a national autonomous area are defined, they may not be altered without going through the procedures prescribed by law. When it is truly necessary to abolish, merge or alter a national autonomous area or its boundaries, the matter shall be proposed through full consultation between the relevant departments of the state organs at a higher level and the organs of self-government of the national autonomous area, and shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民族自治地方的自治机关是自治区、自治州、自治县的人民代表大会和人民政府。  
民族自治地方的人民政府对本级人民代表大会和上一级国家行政机关负责并报告工作，在本级人民代表大会闭会期间，对本级人民代表大会常务委员会负责并报告工作。各民族自治地方的人民政府都是国务院统一领导下的国家行政机关，都服从国务院。  
民族自治地方的自治机关的组织和工作，根据宪法和法律，由民族自治地方的自治条例或者单行条例规定。

**Article 15** The organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall be the people's congresses and people's governments of autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures and autonomous counties.  
The people's governments of national autonomous areas shall be responsible to the people's congresses at the corresponding levels and to the state administrative organs at the next higher level and shall report on their work. When the people's congresses at the corresponding levels are not in session, the people's governments of national autonomous areas shall be responsible to the standing committees of those people's congresses and shall report on their work. All people's governments of national autonomous areas shall be state administrative organs under the unified leadership of the State Council and shall be subordinate to it.  
The organization and work of the organs of self-government of national autonomous areas shall be prescribed by their regulations on the exercise of autonomy or separate regulations in accordance with the Constitution and other laws.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民族自治地方的人民代表大会中，除实行区域自治的民族的代表外，其他居住在本行政区域内的民族也应当有适当名额的代表。  
民族自治地方的人民代表大会中，实行区域自治的民族和其他少数民族代表的名额和比例，根据法律规定的原则，由省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会决定，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。  
民族自治地方的人民代表大会常务委员会中应当有实行区域自治的民族的公民担任主任或者副主任。

**Article 16** Among the deputies to the people's congress of a national autonomous area, in addition to the deputies representing the nationality exercising regional autonomy, the other nationalities inhabiting the administrative area shall also be appropriately represented.  
The number of deputies to the people's congress of a national autonomous area from the nationality exercising regional autonomy and from other minority nationalities, as well as the proportion of such deputies, shall be decided by the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the principles prescribed by law, and shall be reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for the record.  
Among the chairman and vice-chairmen of the standing committee of the people's congress of a national autonomous area, there shall be one or more citizens of the nationality exercising regional autonomy.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　自治区主席、自治州州长、自治县县长由实行区域自治的民族的公民担任。自治区、自治州、自治县的人民政府的其他组成人员，应当合理配备实行区域自治的民族和其他少数民族的人员。  
民族自治地方的人民政府实行自治区主席、自治州州长、自治县县长负责制。自治区主席、自治州州长、自治县县长，分别主持本级人民政府工作。

**Article 17** The chairman of an autonomous region, the prefect of an autonomous prefecture and the head of an autonomous county shall be citizens of the nationality exercising regional autonomy in the area concerned. Among the other members of the people's government of an autonomous region, autonomous prefecture or autonomous county, there shall be an appropriate number of members of the nationality exercising regional autonomy and of other minority nationalities.  
The people's government of a national autonomous area shall apply the system of responsibility of the chairman of the autonomous region, the prefect of the autonomous prefecture or the head of the autonomous county. The chairman of the autonomous region, the prefect of the autonomous prefecture or the head of the autonomous county shall respectively assume overall responsibility for the work of the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民族自治地方的自治机关所属工作部门的干部中，应当合理配备实行区域自治的民族和其他少数民族的人员。

**Article 18** Among the cadres of the departments under the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area, there shall be an appropriate number of persons of the nationality exercising regional autonomy and of other minority nationalities.

### 第三章　自治机关的自治权

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民族自治地方的人民代表大会有权依照当地民族的政治、经济和文化的特点，制定自治条例和单行条例。自治区的自治条例和单行条例，报全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准后生效。自治州、自治县的自治条例和单行条例报省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会批准后生效，并报全国人民代表大会常务委员会和国务院备案。

**Article 19** The people's congress of a national autonomous area shall have the power to enact regulations on the exercise of autonomy and separate regulations in the light of the political, economic and cultural characteristics of the nationality or nationalities in the area concerned. The regulations on the exercise of autonomy and separate regulations of an autonomous region shall be submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for approval before they come into force. The regulations on the exercise of autonomy and separate regulations of an autonomous prefecture or autonomous county shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval before they come into force, and shall be reported to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　上级国家机关的决议、决定、命令和指示，如有不适合民族自治地方实际情况的，自治机关可以报经该上级国家机关批准，变通执行或者停止执行；该上级国家机关应当在收到报告之日起六十日内给予答复。

**Article 20** If a resolution, decision, order or instruction of a state organ at a higher level does not suit the actual conditions of a national autonomous area, the organ of self-government of the area may, subject to the approval of the state organ at a higher level, implement it with certain modifications or suspend its implementation. The state organ at a higher level shall give a reply within 60 days from the date of receipt of the report.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民族自治地方的自治机关在执行职务的时候，依照本民族自治地方自治条例的规定，使用当地通用的一种或者几种语言文字；同时使用几种通用的语言文字执行职务的，可以以实行区域自治的民族的语言文字为主。

**Article 21** When performing their functions, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of the regulations on the exercise of autonomy of the area, use one or more of the spoken and written languages commonly used in the locality. When using more than one commonly used spoken and written language in the performance of their functions, the language of the nationality exercising regional autonomy may be used as the main language.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民族自治地方的自治机关根据社会主义建设的需要，采取各种措施从当地民族中大量培养各级干部、各种科学技术、经营管理等专业人才和技术工人，充分发挥他们的作用，并且注意在少数民族妇女中培养各级干部和各种专业技术人才。  
民族自治地方的自治机关录用工作人员的时候，对实行区域自治的民族和其他少数民族的人员应当给予适当的照顾。  
民族自治地方的自治机关可以采取特殊措施，优待、鼓励各种专业人员参加自治地方各项建设工作。

**Article 22** In accordance with the needs of socialist construction, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall adopt various measures to train large numbers of cadres, specialized personnel of various kinds, including scientific, technological and managerial personnel, and skilled workers from among the local nationalities, and shall give full play to their roles. They shall also pay attention to the training of cadres and specialized technical personnel of various kinds from among the women of minority nationalities.  
When recruiting personnel, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall give appropriate preference to persons of the nationality exercising regional autonomy and other minority nationalities.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area may adopt special measures to give preferential treatment and encouragement to specialized personnel of various kinds to participate in the construction of the area.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民族自治地方的企业、事业单位依照国家规定招收人员时，优先招收少数民族人员，并且可以从农村和牧区少数民族人口中招收。

**Article 23** When recruiting personnel in accordance with state regulations, enterprises and institutions in a national autonomous area shall give priority to persons of minority nationalities and may recruit them from among the minority nationality populations in rural and pastoral areas.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民族自治地方的自治机关依照国家的军事制度和当地的实际需要，经国务院批准，可以组织本地方维护社会治安的公安部队。

**Article 24** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area may, in accordance with the military system of the state and the actual local needs and with the approval of the State Council, organize local public security forces for the maintenance of public order.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民族自治地方的自治机关在国家计划的指导下，根据本地方的特点和需要，制定经济建设的方针、政策和计划，自主地安排和管理地方性的经济建设事业。

**Article 25** Under the guidance of state plans and in the light of local characteristics and needs, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall formulate the guidelines, policies and plans for economic construction, and shall autonomously arrange and administer local economic development.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民族自治地方的自治机关在坚持社会主义原则的前提下，根据法律规定和本地方经济发展的特点，合理调整生产关系和经济结构，努力发展社会主义市场经济。  
民族自治地方的自治机关坚持公有制为主体、多种所有制经济共同发展的基本经济制度，鼓励发展非公有制经济。

**Article 26** While adhering to the socialist principle, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law and the characteristics of local economic development, rationally readjust the relations of production and the economic structure and strive to develop the socialist market economy.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall adhere to the basic economic system under which public ownership is dominant and diverse forms of ownership develop side by side, and shall encourage the development of the non-public sector of the economy.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民族自治地方的自治机关根据法律规定，确定本地方内草场和森林的所有权和使用权。  
民族自治地方的自治机关保护、建设草原和森林，组织和鼓励植树种草。禁止任何组织或者个人利用任何手段破坏草原和森林。严禁在草原和森林毁草毁林开垦耕地。

**Article 27** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law, determine the ownership and use rights of grasslands and forests in the area.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall protect and develop grasslands and forests, organize and encourage afforestation and grass planting. No organization or individual may destroy grasslands or forests by any means. Reclamation of land from grasslands or forests for cultivation shall be strictly prohibited.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民族自治地方的自治机关依照法律规定，管理和保护本地方的自然资源。  
民族自治地方的自治机关根据法律规定和国家的统一规划，对可以由本地方开发的自然资源，优先合理开发利用。

**Article 28** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law, administer and protect the natural resources of the area.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law and the unified plans of the state, give priority to the rational exploitation and utilization of the natural resources that may be developed by the area.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民族自治地方的自治机关在国家计划的指导下，根据本地方的财力、物力和其他具体条件，自主地安排地方基本建设项目。

**Article 29** Under the guidance of state plans and in the light of the financial and material resources and other specific conditions of the area, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously arrange local capital construction projects.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民族自治地方的自治机关自主地管理隶属于本地方的企业、事业。

**Article 30** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously administer the enterprises and institutions under them.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　民族自治地方依照国家规定，可以开展对外经济贸易活动，经国务院批准，可以开辟对外贸易口岸。  
与外国接壤的民族自治地方经国务院批准，开展边境贸易。  
民族自治地方在对外经济贸易活动中，享受国家的优惠政策。

**Article 31** In accordance with state regulations, a national autonomous area may engage in foreign economic and trade activities and may, with the approval of the State Council, open foreign trade ports.  
With the approval of the State Council, a national autonomous area that borders on a foreign country may conduct border trade.  
A national autonomous area shall enjoy preferential policies of the state in foreign economic and trade activities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　民族自治地方的财政是一级财政，是国家财政的组成部分。  
民族自治地方的自治机关有管理地方财政的自治权。凡是依照国家财政体制属于民族自治地方的财政收入，都应当由民族自治地方的自治机关自主地安排使用。  
民族自治地方在全国统一的财政体制下，通过国家实行的规范的财政转移支付制度，享受上级财政的照顾。  
民族自治地方的财政预算支出，按照国家规定，设机动资金，预备费在预算中所占比例高于一般地区。  
民族自治地方的自治机关在执行财政预算过程中，自行安排使用收入的超收和支出的节余资金。

**Article 32** The finance of a national autonomous area shall be a level of finance and shall constitute a component part of the state finance.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall have the power of autonomy in administering local finance. All revenues accruing to a national autonomous area under the state financial system shall be autonomously arranged and used by the organs of self-government of the area.  
Under the unified state financial system, a national autonomous area shall, through the standard financial transfer payment system implemented by the state, enjoy the favour of financial assistance from the state at a higher level.  
In the budget of a national autonomous area, a reserve fund shall be set up in accordance with state regulations. The proportion of the reserve fund in the budget shall be higher than that of other areas.  
When executing the budget, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall arrange for the use of revenue surplus and expenditure savings on their own.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民族自治地方的自治机关对本地方的各项开支标准、定员、定额，根据国家规定的原则，结合本地方的实际情况，可以制定补充规定和具体办法。自治区制定的补充规定和具体办法，报国务院备案；自治州、自治县制定的补充规定和具体办法，须报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 33** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area may, in accordance with the principles prescribed by the state and in the light of local conditions, make supplementary provisions and concrete measures concerning the expenditure standards, sizes of staff and quotas of various items in the area. The supplementary provisions and concrete measures made by an autonomous region shall be reported to the State Council for the record. The supplementary provisions and concrete measures made by an autonomous prefecture or autonomous county shall be submitted to the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　民族自治地方的自治机关在执行国家税法的时候，除应由国家统一审批的减免税收项目以外，对属于地方财政收入的某些需要从税收上加以照顾和鼓励的，可以实行减税或者免税。自治州、自治县决定减税或者免税，须报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 34** When implementing the state tax laws, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area may, with the exception of items of tax reduction or exemption which must be examined and approved by the state in a unified way, grant tax reduction or exemption to certain items of local revenue which require such treatment as incentives and encouragement. Tax reduction or exemption decided by an autonomous prefecture or autonomous county shall be submitted to the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　民族自治地方根据本地方经济和社会发展的需要，可以依照法律规定设立地方商业银行和城乡信用合作组织。

**Article 35** In accordance with the needs of local economic and social development and with the provisions of law, a national autonomous area may establish local commercial banks and urban and rural credit cooperative organizations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　民族自治地方的自治机关根据国家的教育方针，依照法律规定，决定本地方的教育规划，各级各类学校的设置、学制、办学形式、教学内容、教学用语和招生办法。

**Article 36** In accordance with the educational policies of the state and the provisions of law, the organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall decide on the local educational plans, the establishment of schools of various types and at various levels, their length of schooling, forms of school operation, curricula, languages of instruction and methods of enrollment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　民族自治地方的自治机关自主地发展民族教育，扫除文盲，举办各类学校，普及九年义务教育，采取多种形式发展普通高级中等教育和中等职业技术教育，根据条件和需要发展高等教育，培养各少数民族专业人才。  
民族自治地方的自治机关为少数民族牧区和经济困难、居住分散的少数民族山区，设立以寄宿为主和助学金为主的公办民族小学和民族中学，保障就读学生完成义务教育阶段的学业。办学经费和助学金由当地财政解决，当地财政困难的，上级财政应当给予补助。  
招收少数民族学生为主的学校（班级）和其他教育机构，有条件的应当采用少数民族文字的课本，并用少数民族语言讲课；根据情况从小学低年级或者高年级起开设汉语文课程，推广全国通用的普通话和规范汉字。  
各级人民政府要在财政方面扶持少数民族文字的教材和出版物的编译和出版工作。

**Article 37** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously develop education for minority nationalities, eliminate illiteracy, set up various kinds of schools, spread compulsory nine-year education, develop regular senior secondary education and secondary vocational and technical education in various forms, and develop higher education according to conditions and needs in order to train specialized personnel from among minority nationalities.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall set up, in pastoral areas and in mountainous areas where minority nationalities live in scattered communities with economic difficulties, public primary and secondary schools for minority nationalities, which are mainly boarding schools and schools providing grants-in-aid, in order to ensure that the students complete their compulsory education. The expenses for school operation and grants-in-aid shall be defrayed by the local finance. If the local finance has difficulty, the finance of the state at a higher level shall provide subsidies.  
In schools (classes) and other educational institutions where students of minority nationalities constitute the majority, textbooks in the languages of minority nationalities shall be used where conditions permit, and classes shall be taught in the languages of minority nationalities. Depending on the circumstances, courses in the Chinese language shall be offered from the lower or higher primary grades in order to popularize the common speech of the whole country and the standard Chinese characters.  
People's governments at all levels shall give financial support to the compilation, translation and publication of textbooks and publications in the languages of minority nationalities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　民族自治地方的自治机关自主地发展具有民族形式和民族特点的文学、艺术、新闻、出版、广播、电影、电视等民族文化事业，加大对文化事业的投入，加强文化设施建设，加快各项文化事业的发展。  
民族自治地方的自治机关组织、支持有关单位和部门收集、整理、翻译和出版民族历史文化书籍，保护民族的名胜古迹、珍贵文物和其他重要历史文化遗产，继承和发展优秀的民族传统文化。

**Article 38** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously develop cultural undertakings of minority nationalities with national characteristics and style, including literature, art, the press, publishing, broadcasting, film and television, increase investment in cultural undertakings, strengthen the building of cultural facilities and speed up the development of cultural undertakings of various kinds.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall organize and support relevant units and departments in collecting, collating, translating and publishing historical and cultural books of minority nationalities, and in protecting scenic spots and historical sites, valuable cultural relics and other important historical and cultural heritage of minority nationalities, and shall inherit and develop the fine traditional culture of minority nationalities.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　民族自治地方的自治机关自主地决定本地方的科学技术发展规划，普及科学技术知识。

**Article 39** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously decide on the local plan for the development of science and technology, and shall popularize scientific and technical knowledge.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民族自治地方的自治机关，自主地决定本地方的医疗卫生事业的发展规划，发展现代医药和民族传统医药。  
民族自治地方的自治机关加强对传染病、地方病的预防控制工作和妇幼卫生保健，改善医疗卫生条件。

**Article 40** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously decide on the local plan for the development of medical and health services, and shall develop modern medicine and traditional medicine of minority nationalities.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall strengthen the prevention and control of infectious and endemic diseases and the work of maternal and child health care, and shall improve the conditions for medical and health services.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　民族自治地方的自治机关自主地发展体育事业，开展民族传统体育活动，增强各族人民的体质。

**Article 41** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall autonomously develop sports undertakings, carry out traditional ethnic sports activities and enhance the physique of the people of all nationalities.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民族自治地方的自治机关积极开展和其他地方的教育、科学技术、文化艺术、卫生、体育等方面的交流和协作。  
自治区、自治州的自治机关依照国家规定，可以和国外进行教育、科学技术、文化艺术、卫生、体育等方面的交流。

**Article 42** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall actively conduct exchanges and cooperation with other areas in education, science and technology, culture and the arts, health and physical culture.  
In accordance with state regulations, the organs of self-government of autonomous regions and autonomous prefectures may conduct exchanges with foreign countries in education, science and technology, culture and the arts, health and physical culture.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民族自治地方的自治机关根据法律规定，制定管理流动人口的办法。

**Article 43** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law, formulate measures for the administration of the floating population.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　民族自治地方实行计划生育和优生优育，提高各民族人口素质。  
民族自治地方的自治机关根据法律规定，结合本地方的实际情况，制定实行计划生育的办法。

**Article 44** A national autonomous area shall practise family planning and encourage the practice of good prenatal and postnatal care in order to improve the quality of the population of all nationalities.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall, in accordance with the provisions of law and in the light of local conditions, formulate measures for the implementation of family planning.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　民族自治地方的自治机关保护和改善生活环境和生态环境，防治污染和其他公害，实现人口、资源和环境的协调发展。

**Article 45** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall protect and improve the living environment and the ecological environment, prevent and control pollution and other public hazards, and achieve a coordinated development of the population, resources and the environment.

### 第四章　民族自治地方的人民法院和人民检察院

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　民族自治地方的人民法院和人民检察院对本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会负责。民族自治地方的人民检察院并对上级人民检察院负责。  
民族自治地方人民法院的审判工作，受最高人民法院和上级人民法院监督。民族自治地方的人民检察院的工作，受最高人民检察院和上级人民检察院领导。  
民族自治地方的人民法院和人民检察院的领导成员和工作人员中，应当有实行区域自治的民族的人员。

**Article 46** The people's courts and people's procuratorates of a national autonomous area shall be responsible to the people's congresses at the corresponding levels and their standing committees. The people's procuratorates of a national autonomous area shall also be responsible to the people's procuratorates at higher levels.  
The adjudicative work of the people's courts of a national autonomous area shall be subject to the supervision of the Supreme People's Court and the people's courts at higher levels. The work of the people's procuratorates of a national autonomous area shall be subject to the leadership of the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the people's procuratorates at higher levels.  
Among the leading members and personnel of the people's courts and people's procuratorates of a national autonomous area, there shall be persons of the nationality exercising regional autonomy.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　民族自治地方的人民法院和人民检察院应当用当地通用的语言审理和检察案件，并合理配备通晓当地通用的少数民族语言文字的人员。对于不通晓当地通用的语言文字的诉讼参与人，应当为他们提供翻译。法律文书应当根据实际需要，使用当地通用的一种或者几种文字。保障各民族公民都有使用本民族语言文字进行诉讼的权利。

**Article 47** The people's courts and people's procuratorates of a national autonomous area shall hear and handle cases in the spoken and written languages commonly used in the locality, and shall rationally allocate persons who are familiar with the spoken and written languages of minority nationalities commonly used in the locality. Interpretation shall be provided for any party to the proceedings who is not familiar with the spoken or written language commonly used in the locality. Legal documents shall, in the light of actual needs, be written in one or more of the languages commonly used in the locality. The right of citizens of all nationalities to use their own spoken and written languages in court proceedings shall be guaranteed.

### 第五章　民族自治地方内的民族关系

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　民族自治地方的自治机关保障本地方内各民族都享有平等权利。  
民族自治地方的自治机关团结各民族的干部和群众，充分调动他们的积极性，共同建设民族自治地方。

**Article 48** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall ensure that all nationalities in the area enjoy equal rights.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall unite the cadres and masses of all nationalities and give full play to their initiative in jointly building the national autonomous area.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　民族自治地方的自治机关教育和鼓励各民族的干部互相学习语言文字。汉族干部要学习当地少数民族的语言文字，少数民族干部在学习、使用本民族语言文字的同时，也要学习全国通用的普通话和规范汉字。  
民族自治地方的国家工作人员，能够熟练使用两种以上当地通用的语言文字的，应当予以奖励。

**Article 49** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall educate and encourage the cadres of all nationalities to learn each other's spoken and written languages. Han cadres shall learn the spoken and written languages of minority nationalities in the locality. Cadres of minority nationalities shall, while learning and using their own spoken and written languages, also learn the common speech of the whole country and the standard Chinese characters.  
State functionaries in a national autonomous area who are proficient in two or more of the spoken and written languages commonly used in the locality shall be rewarded.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　民族自治地方的自治机关帮助聚居在本地方的其他少数民族，建立相应的自治地方或者民族乡。  
民族自治地方的自治机关帮助本地方各民族发展经济、教育、科学技术、文化、卫生、体育事业。  
民族自治地方的自治机关照顾本地方散居民族的特点和需要。

**Article 50** The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall help other minority nationalities living in concentrated communities in the area establish corresponding autonomous areas or nationality townships.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall help all nationalities in the area develop their economy, education, science and technology, culture, health and sports undertakings.  
The organs of self-government of a national autonomous area shall attend to the characteristics and needs of the nationalities living in scattered communities in the area.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　民族自治地方的自治机关在处理涉及本地方各民族的特殊问题的时候，必须与他们的代表充分协商，尊重他们的意见。

**Article 51** The autonomous organs of national autonomous areas must conduct full consultation with the representatives of the various nationalities in dealing with special problems concerning the various nationalities of such areas, and shall respect their opinions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民族自治地方的自治机关保障本地方内各民族公民都享有宪法规定的公民权利，并且教育他们履行公民应尽的义务。

**Article 52** The autonomous organs of national autonomous areas shall ensure the citizens of the various nationalities of such areas enjoy the rights of citizens as prescribed by the Constitution and shall educate them in the fulfillment of their obligations as citizens.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民族自治地方的自治机关提倡爱祖国、爱人民、爱劳动、爱科学、爱社会主义的公德，对本地方内各民族公民进行爱国主义、共产主义和民族政策的教育。教育各民族的干部和群众互相信任，互相学习，互相帮助，互相尊重语言文字、风俗习惯和宗教信仰，共同维护国家的统一和各民族的团结。

**Article 53** The autonomous organs of national autonomous areas shall advocate the social ethics of love for the motherland, love for the people, love for labor, love for science, and love for socialism, and shall conduct education among the citizens of the various nationalities of such areas in patriotism, communism, and national policies. They shall educate the cadres and masses of the various nationalities in mutual trust, mutual learning, mutual assistance, mutual respect for spoken and written languages, customs and habits, and religious beliefs, and jointly safeguard the unification of the country and the unity of all nationalities.

### 第六章　上级国家机关的职责

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　上级国家机关有关民族自治地方的决议、决定、命令和指示，应当适合民族自治地方的实际情况。

**Article 54** Resolutions, decisions, orders and directives of the organs of the State at higher levels concerning national autonomous areas shall be suited to the actual conditions of such areas.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　上级国家机关应当帮助、指导民族自治地方经济发展战略的研究、制定和实施，从财政、金融、物资、技术和人才等方面，帮助各民族自治地方加速发展经济、教育、科学技术、文化、卫生、体育等事业。  
国家制定优惠政策，引导和鼓励国内外资金投向民族自治地方。  
上级国家机关在制定国民经济和社会发展计划的时候，应当照顾民族自治地方的特点和需要。

**Article 55** The organs of the State at higher levels shall assist and guide the national autonomous areas in studying, formulating and implementing economic development strategies, and shall, from the aspects of finance, banking, materials, technology and human resources, assist the various national autonomous areas in accelerating the development of economic, educational, scientific and technological, cultural, health and sports undertakings.  
The State shall formulate preferential policies to guide and encourage domestic and foreign capital to invest in national autonomous areas.  
When formulating national economic and social development plans, the organs of the State at higher levels shall take into consideration the characteristics and needs of national autonomous areas.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家根据统一规划和市场需求，优先在民族自治地方合理安排资源开发项目和基础设施建设项目。国家在重大基础设施投资项目中适当增加投资比重和政策性银行贷款比重。  
国家在民族自治地方安排基础设施建设，需要民族自治地方配套资金的，根据不同情况给予减少或者免除配套资金的照顾。  
国家帮助民族自治地方加快实用科技开发和成果转化，大力推广实用技术和有条件发展的高新技术，积极引导科技人才向民族自治地方合理流动。国家向民族自治地方提供转移建设项目的时候，根据当地的条件，提供先进、适用的设备和工艺。

**Article 56** The State, in accordance with the unified plan and market demands, shall give priority to the rational arrangement of resource development projects and infrastructure construction projects in national autonomous areas. The State shall appropriately increase the proportion of investment and the proportion of policy-related bank loans in major infrastructure investment projects.  
Where the State arranges for infrastructure construction in a national autonomous area and requires matching funds from such area, it shall provide the favor of reducing or exempting such matching funds according to different circumstances.  
The State shall assist national autonomous areas in accelerating the development and transformation of applied science and technology results, vigorously promote applied technology and high technology that can be developed under favorable conditions, and actively guide the rational flow of scientific and technological personnel to national autonomous areas. When the State provides transferred construction projects to national autonomous areas, it shall provide advanced and suitable equipment and technology in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家根据民族自治地方的经济发展特点和需要，综合运用货币市场和资本市场，加大对民族自治地方的金融扶持力度。金融机构对民族自治地方的固定资产投资项目和符合国家产业政策的企业，在开发资源、发展多种经济方面的合理资金需求，应当给予重点扶持。  
国家鼓励商业银行加大对民族自治地方的信贷投入，积极支持当地企业的合理资金需求。

**Article 57** The State, in accordance with the characteristics and needs of the economic development of national autonomous areas, shall comprehensively utilize the money market and capital market to increase financial support for national autonomous areas. Financial institutions shall give key support to fixed asset investment projects in national autonomous areas and to enterprises that comply with the industrial policies of the State in respect of their reasonable capital demands in resource development and multiple economic development.  
The State shall encourage commercial banks to increase credit input to national autonomous areas and actively support the reasonable capital demands of local enterprises.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　上级国家机关从财政、金融、人才等方面帮助民族自治地方的企业进行技术创新，促进产业结构升级。  
上级国家机关应当组织和鼓励民族自治地方的企业管理人员和技术人员到经济发达地区学习，同时引导和鼓励经济发达地区的企业管理人员和技术人员到民族自治地方的企业工作。

**Article 58** The organs of the State at higher levels shall assist the enterprises of national autonomous areas in technological innovation and promote the upgrading of industrial structure from such aspects as finance, banking and human resources.  
The organs of the State at higher levels shall organize and encourage the enterprise management personnel and technical personnel of national autonomous areas to go to economically developed areas to study, and at the same time guide and encourage the enterprise management personnel and technical personnel of economically developed areas to work in the enterprises of national autonomous areas.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家设立各项专用资金，扶助民族自治地方发展经济文化建设事业。  
国家设立的各项专用资金和临时性的民族补助专款，任何部门不得扣减、截留、挪用，不得用以顶替民族自治地方的正常的预算收入。

**Article 59** The State shall establish various special funds to assist national autonomous areas in the development of economic and cultural undertakings.  
No department may deduct, withhold, misappropriate or use to replace the normal budgetary revenue of national autonomous areas the various special funds established by the State and the temporary special national subsidies.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　上级国家机关根据国家的民族贸易政策和民族自治地方的需要，对民族自治地方的商业、供销和医药企业，从投资、金融、税收等方面给予扶持。

**Article 60** The organs of the State at higher levels shall, in accordance with the national trade policies of the State and the needs of national autonomous areas, give support to the commercial, supply and marketing, and medical and pharmaceutical enterprises of national autonomous areas in such aspects as investment, banking and taxation.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家制定优惠政策，扶持民族自治地方发展对外经济贸易，扩大民族自治地方生产企业对外贸易经营自主权，鼓励发展地方优势产品出口，实行优惠的边境贸易政策。

**Article 61** The State shall formulate preferential policies to support the development of foreign economic relations and trade of national autonomous areas, expand the autonomy of manufacturing enterprises in national autonomous areas in foreign trade operations, encourage the development of local advantageous products for export, and implement preferential border trade policies.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　随着国民经济的发展和财政收入的增长，上级财政逐步加大对民族自治地方财政转移支付力度。通过一般性财政转移支付、专项财政转移支付、民族优惠政策财政转移支付以及国家确定的其他方式，增加对民族自治地方的资金投入，用于加快民族自治地方经济发展和社会进步，逐步缩小与发达地区的差距。

**Article 62** With the development of the national economy and the growth of fiscal revenue, the higher-level finances shall gradually increase the intensity of fiscal transfer payments to national autonomous areas. Through general fiscal transfer payments, special fiscal transfer payments, fiscal transfer payments for national preferential policies, and other methods determined by the State, the capital input to national autonomous areas shall be increased for accelerating the economic development and social progress of national autonomous areas and gradually narrowing the gap with developed areas.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　上级国家机关在投资、金融、税收等方面扶持民族自治地方改善农业、牧业、林业等生产条件和水利、交通、能源、通信等基础设施；扶持民族自治地方合理利用本地资源发展地方工业、乡镇企业、中小企业以及少数民族特需商品和传统手工业品的生产。

**Article 63** The organs of the State at higher levels shall support national autonomous areas in improving agricultural, pastoral, forestry and other production conditions and water conservancy, communications, energy, telecommunications and other infrastructure in such aspects as investment, banking and taxation; and support national autonomous areas in rationally utilizing local resources to develop local industry, township enterprises, small and medium enterprises, and the production of commodities specially needed by minority nationalities and traditional handicraft products.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　上级国家机关应当组织、支持和鼓励经济发达地区与民族自治地方开展经济、技术协作和多层次、多方面的对口支援，帮助和促进民族自治地方经济、教育、科学技术、文化、卫生、体育事业的发展。

**Article 64** The organs of the State at higher levels shall organize, support and encourage economically developed areas to carry out economic and technical cooperation and multi-level and multi-faceted counterpart support with national autonomous areas, and assist and promote the development of the economic, educational, scientific and technological, cultural, health and sports undertakings of national autonomous areas.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家在民族自治地方开发资源、进行建设的时候，应当照顾民族自治地方的利益，作出有利于民族自治地方经济建设的安排，照顾当地少数民族的生产和生活。国家采取措施，对输出自然资源的民族自治地方给予一定的利益补偿。  
国家引导和鼓励经济发达地区的企业按照互惠互利的原则，到民族自治地方投资，开展多种形式的经济合作。

**Article 65** When developing resources and carrying out construction in national autonomous areas, the State shall take care of the interests of such areas, make arrangements favorable to the economic construction of such areas, and take care of the production and livelihood of the local minority nationalities. The State shall adopt measures to give certain benefit compensation to national autonomous areas that export natural resources.  
The State shall guide and encourage enterprises of economically developed areas to invest in national autonomous areas in accordance with the principle of mutual benefit and carry out various forms of economic cooperation.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　上级国家机关应当把民族自治地方的重大生态平衡、环境保护的综合治理工程项目纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，统一部署。  
民族自治地方为国家的生态平衡、环境保护作出贡献的，国家给予一定的利益补偿。  
任何组织和个人在民族自治地方开发资源、进行建设的时候，要采取有效措施，保护和改善当地的生活环境和生态环境，防治污染和其他公害。

**Article 66** The organs of the State at higher levels shall incorporate major comprehensive ecological balance and environmental protection projects of national autonomous areas into the national economic and social development plans for unified deployment.  
Where a national autonomous area makes contributions to the ecological balance and environmental protection of the State, the State shall give certain benefit compensation.  
Any organization or individual, when developing resources or carrying out construction in a national autonomous area, must adopt effective measures to protect and improve the local living environment and ecological environment, and prevent and control pollution and other public hazards.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　上级国家机关隶属的在民族自治地方的企业、事业单位依照国家规定招收人员时，优先招收当地少数民族人员。  
在民族自治地方的企业、事业单位，应当尊重当地自治机关的自治权，遵守当地自治条例、单行条例和地方性法规、规章，接受当地自治机关的监督。

**Article 67** When enterprises and institutions subordinate to the organs of the State at higher levels located in national autonomous areas recruit personnel in accordance with the regulations of the State, they shall give priority to recruiting local minority personnel.  
Enterprises and institutions located in national autonomous areas shall respect the autonomous rights of the local autonomous organs, abide by the local autonomous regulations, separate regulations, local regulations and rules, and accept the supervision of the local autonomous organs.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　上级国家机关非经民族自治地方自治机关同意，不得改变民族自治地方所属企业的隶属关系。

**Article 68** The organs of the State at higher levels shall not change the subordinate relationship of enterprises owned by national autonomous areas without the consent of the autonomous organs of such areas.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家和上级人民政府应当从财政、金融、物资、技术、人才等方面加大对民族自治地方的贫困地区的扶持力度，帮助贫困人口尽快摆脱贫困状况，实现小康。

**Article 69** The State and the higher-level people's governments shall increase support from such aspects as finance, banking, materials, technology and human resources to the poverty-stricken areas of national autonomous areas, and assist the poverty-stricken population in quickly getting rid of poverty and achieving moderate prosperity.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　上级国家机关帮助民族自治地方从当地民族中大量培养各级干部、各种专业人才和技术工人；根据民族自治地方的需要，采取多种形式调派适当数量的教师、医生、科学技术和经营管理人员，参加民族自治地方的工作，对他们的生活待遇给予适当照顾。

**Article 70** The organs of the State at higher levels shall assist national autonomous areas in training large numbers of cadres at various levels, professional personnel of various types, and skilled workers from among the local nationalities; and, in accordance with the needs of such areas, shall assign, through various forms, an appropriate number of teachers, doctors, scientific and technological personnel, and management personnel to participate in the work of such areas, and give appropriate care to their living treatment.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家加大对民族自治地方的教育投入，并采取特殊措施，帮助民族自治地方加速普及九年义务教育和发展其他教育事业，提高各民族人民的科学文化水平。  
国家举办民族高等学校，在高等学校举办民族班、民族预科，专门或者主要招收少数民族学生，并且可以采取定向招生、定向分配的办法。高等学校和中等专业学校招收新生的时候，对少数民族考生适当放宽录取标准和条件，对人口特少的少数民族考生给予特殊照顾。各级人民政府和学校应当采取多种措施帮助家庭经济困难的少数民族学生完成学业。  
国家在发达地区举办民族中学或者在普通中学开设民族班，招收少数民族学生实施中等教育。  
国家帮助民族自治地方培养和培训各民族教师。国家组织和鼓励各民族教师和符合任职条件的各民族毕业生到民族自治地方从事教育教学工作，并给予他们相应的优惠待遇。

**Article 71** The State shall increase its educational input in national autonomous areas and adopt special measures to assist such areas in accelerating the popularization of nine-year compulsory education and developing other educational undertakings, so as to raise the scientific and cultural level of the people of all nationalities.  
The State shall establish institutions of higher learning for nationalities, and set up special classes for nationalities and preparatory courses for nationalities in institutions of higher learning, for the purpose of enrolling exclusively or mainly students of minority nationalities, and may adopt methods of designated enrollment and designated job assignment. When enrolling new students in institutions of higher learning and secondary specialized schools, the State shall appropriately relax the admission standards and conditions for minority candidates and give special care to candidates of minority nationalities with a very small population. People's governments at all levels and schools shall adopt various measures to assist minority students with family financial difficulties in completing their studies.  
The State shall establish middle schools for nationalities in developed areas or set up special classes for nationalities in ordinary middle schools to enroll students of minority nationalities for secondary education.  
The State shall assist national autonomous areas in training and training teachers of all nationalities. The State shall organize and encourage teachers of all nationalities and graduates of all nationalities who meet the conditions for appointment to engage in educational and teaching work in national autonomous areas, and shall give them corresponding preferential treatment.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　上级国家机关应当对各民族的干部和群众加强民族政策的教育，经常检查民族政策和有关法律的遵守和执行。

**Article 72** The organs of the State at higher levels shall strengthen education in national policies among the cadres and masses of all nationalities, and regularly inspect the observance and enforcement of national policies and relevant laws.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院及其有关部门应当在职权范围内，为实施本法分别制定行政法规、规章、具体措施和办法。  
自治区和辖有自治州、自治县的省、直辖市的人民代表大会及其常务委员会结合当地实际情况，制定实施本法的具体办法。

**Article 73** The State Council and the relevant departments thereof shall, within the scope of their functions and powers, formulate administrative regulations, rules, specific measures and methods for the implementation of this Law respectively.  
The people's congresses and their standing committees of autonomous regions and of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have autonomous prefectures or autonomous counties under their jurisdiction shall, in light of local actual conditions, formulate specific methods for the implementation of this Law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本法由全国人民代表大会通过，自1984年10月1日起施行。

**Article 74** This Law is adopted by the National People's Congress and shall come into force as of October 1, 1984.
