### 最高人民法院关于土改时部分确权、部分未确权的祖遗房产应如何继承问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于土改时部分确权、部分未确权的祖遗房产  
  
应如何继承问题的批复  
  
（1987）民他字第48号  
  
湖南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院1986年湘字第1号《关于处理房屋纠纷的有关问题的请示报告》及1987年2月24日补充报告收悉。  
  
据你院报告称：双方讼争的4间房屋系刘验福（1927年故）、田二妹（1960年故）夫妇于1912年所建。1952年土改时，将其中3间房屋确权为田二妹及养子刘志国（1982年故）、儿媳向翠莲、孙儿刘仁4人所有，另1间房屋未确权。田二妹之女刘志珍1927年出嫁。1953年刘志珍迁回与其母田二妹一起生活，对田二妹的生养死葬等尽了主要义务。1983年刘志珍以四间房屋系父母遗产，她应继承二分之一为由，诉讼到法院。  
  
经研究，同意你院审判委员会的意见，即处理这类案件一般应以土改时确定的产权为准。1952年确权的3间房屋，应归田二妹与其养子刘志国、儿媳向翠莲、孙儿刘仁4人所共有。该共有房屋中属于田二妹的那一部分房屋和土改未确权的1间房屋，可以作为田二妹的遗产，由其法定继承人共同继承。因刘志珍与其母共同生活，尽了主要赡养义务，分配遗产时可以多分。  
  
此复  
  
1987年4月25日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Official Reply on How to Adjudicate the Succession of Ancestral Estate Partially Confirmed and Partially Unconfirmed in Ownership During Land Reform  
(1987) Min Ta Zi No. 48  
Higher People's Court of Hunan Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on Relevant Issues Concerning the Handling of Housing Disputes, Hunan Zi No. 1 of 1986, and the supplementary report dated February 24, 1987.  
According to your report, the four houses in dispute were built in 1912 by Liu Yanfu (died in 1927) and Tian Ermei (died in 1960), a couple. During the land reform in 1952, the ownership rights of three of the houses were confirmed as belonging to four persons: Tian Ermei, her adopted son Liu Zhiguo (died in 1982), her daughter-in-law Xiang Cuilian, and her grandson Liu Ren. The other house was not confirmed in ownership. Tian Ermei's daughter, Liu Zhizhen, married in 1927. In 1953, Liu Zhizhen moved back to live with her mother Tian Ermei and performed principal obligations in supporting and burying Tian Ermei. In 1983, Liu Zhizhen filed a lawsuit with the court on the grounds that the four houses constituted her parents' estate and that she should inherit one half thereof.  
Upon deliberation, we agree with the opinion of the Adjudication Committee of your court, namely that the handling of such cases shall generally be based on the property rights confirmed during the land reform. The three houses whose ownership was confirmed in 1952 shall be the common property of the four persons: Tian Ermei, her adopted son Liu Zhiguo, her daughter-in-law Xiang Cuilian, and her grandson Liu Ren. The portion of such common property belonging to Tian Ermei and the one house whose ownership was not confirmed during the land reform may be taken as Tian Ermei's estate and shall be jointly inherited by her statutory successors. Since Liu Zhizhen lived together with her mother and performed principal obligations of support, she may be given a larger share upon distribution of the estate.  
Hereby reply.  
April 25, 1987
