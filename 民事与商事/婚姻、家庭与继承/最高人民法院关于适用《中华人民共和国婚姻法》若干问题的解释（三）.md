# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》若干问题的解释（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕18号  
**公布日期**：2011-07-04  
**生效日期**：2011-08-13

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国婚姻法〉若干问题的解释（三）》已于2011年7月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1525次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年8月13日起施行。 2011年8月9日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》 若干问题的解释（三）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  当事人以婚姻法第十条规定以外的情形申请宣告婚姻无效的，人民法院应当判决驳回当事人的申请。  
当事人以结婚登记程序存在瑕疵为由提起民事诉讼，主张撤销结婚登记的，告知其可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 1** Where a party applies for a declaration of invalidity of marriage on grounds other than those specified in Article 10 of the Marriage Law, the People's Court shall dismiss the party's application by judgment.  
Where a party files a civil action on the ground of defects in the marriage registration procedure and claims to revoke the marriage registration, the party shall be informed that it may apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  夫妻一方向人民法院起诉请求确认亲子关系不存在，并已提供必要证据予以证明，另一方没有相反证据又拒绝做亲子鉴定的，人民法院可以推定请求确认亲子关系不存在一方的主张成立。  
当事人一方起诉请求确认亲子关系，并提供必要证据予以证明，另一方没有相反证据又拒绝做亲子鉴定的，人民法院可以推定请求确认亲子关系一方的主张成立。

**Article 2** Where one spouse files a lawsuit with the People's Court requesting confirmation that a parent-child relationship does not exist and has provided necessary evidence to prove it, and the other party has no contrary evidence and refuses to undergo a parentage test, the People's Court may presume that the claim of the party requesting confirmation of the nonexistence of the parent-child relationship is established.  
Where one party files a lawsuit requesting confirmation of a parent-child relationship and provides necessary evidence to prove it, and the other party has no contrary evidence and refuses to undergo a parentage test, the People's Court may presume that the claim of the party requesting confirmation of the parent-child relationship is established.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  婚姻关系存续期间，父母双方或者一方拒不履行抚养子女义务，未成年或者不能独立生活的子女请求支付抚养费的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** During the existence of the marital relationship, where both parents or one parent refuses to fulfill the obligation to support their children, and a minor child or a child who cannot live independently requests payment of child support, the People's Court shall sustain the request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻一方请求分割共同财产的，人民法院不予支持，但有下列重大理由且不损害债权人利益的除外：  
（一）一方有隐藏、转移、变卖、毁损、挥霍夫妻共同财产或者伪造夫妻共同债务等严重损害夫妻共同财产利益行为的；  
（二）一方负有法定扶养义务的人患重大疾病需要医治，另一方不同意支付相关医疗费用的。

**Article 4** During the existence of the marital relationship, where one spouse requests partition of community property, the People's Court shall not sustain the request, unless there are the following serious reasons and the interests of creditors are not harmed:  
(1) one party has engaged in acts that seriously impair the interests of the community property, such as concealing, transferring, selling, damaging, or squandering the community property, or forging community debts;  
(2) a person to whom one party owes a statutory duty of support suffers from a serious illness requiring medical treatment, and the other party refuses to pay the relevant medical expenses.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  夫妻一方个人财产在婚后产生的收益，除孳息和自然增值外，应认定为夫妻共同财产。

**Article 5** The income derived from the personal property of one spouse after marriage, except for fruits and natural appreciation, shall be deemed as community property of the spouses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  婚前或者婚姻关系存续期间，当事人约定将一方所有的房产赠与另一方，赠与方在赠与房产变更登记之前撤销赠与，另一方请求判令继续履行的，人民法院可以按照合同法第一百八十六条的规定处理。

**Article 6** Before marriage or during the existence of the marital relationship, where the parties agree that the real property owned by one party shall be donated to the other party, and the donating party revokes the donation before the registration of the change of ownership of the donated real property, and the other party requests a judgment ordering continued performance, the People's Court may handle the case in accordance with the provisions of Article 186 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  婚后由一方父母出资为子女购买的不动产，产权登记在出资人子女名下的，可按照婚姻法第十八条第（三）项的规定，视为只对自己子女一方的赠与，该不动产应认定为夫妻一方的个人财产。  
由双方父母出资购买的不动产，产权登记在一方子女名下的，该不动产可认定为双方按照各自父母的出资份额按份共有，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 7** Where, after marriage, the parents of one party make a contribution to purchase immovable property for their child, and the property right is registered in the name of the child of the contributing parents, it may be deemed, in accordance with the provisions of Article 18(3) of the Marriage Law, as a gift only to that party's child, and the immovable property shall be deemed as the personal property of that spouse.  
Where immovable property is purchased with contributions from both parties' parents, and the property right is registered in the name of the child of one party, the immovable property may be deemed as co-owned by the parties in proportion to the contribution of their respective parents, unless the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  无民事行为能力人的配偶有虐待、遗弃等严重损害无民事行为能力一方的人身权利或者财产权益行为，其他有监护资格的人可以依照特别程序要求变更监护关系；变更后的监护人代理无民事行为能力一方提起离婚诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 8** Where the spouse of a person with no capacity for civil conduct commits acts such as abuse, abandonment, or other acts that seriously impair the personal rights or property rights and interests of the party with no capacity for civil conduct, other persons with guardianship qualifications may apply to change the guardianship relationship in accordance with special procedures; where the changed guardian, on behalf of the party with no capacity for civil conduct, institutes an action for divorce, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  夫以妻擅自中止妊娠侵犯其生育权为由请求损害赔偿的，人民法院不予支持；夫妻双方因是否生育发生纠纷，致使感情确已破裂，一方请求离婚的，人民法院经调解无效，应依照婚姻法第三十二条第三款第（五）项的规定处理。

**Article 9** Where a husband requests damages on the ground that his wife's unauthorized termination of pregnancy infringes upon his reproductive right, the People's Court shall not sustain the request; where the spouses have a dispute over whether to bear children, resulting in the complete breakdown of mutual affection, and one party requests divorce, the People's Court shall, if mediation fails, handle the case in accordance with the provisions of Article 32, paragraph 3, item (5) of the Marriage Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  夫妻一方婚前签订不动产买卖合同，以个人财产支付首付款并在银行贷款，婚后用夫妻共同财产还贷，不动产登记于首付款支付方名下的，离婚时该不动产由双方协议处理。  
依前款规定不能达成协议的，人民法院可以判决该不动产归产权登记一方，尚未归还的贷款为产权登记一方的个人债务。双方婚后共同还贷支付的款项及其相对应财产增值部分，离婚时应根据婚姻法第三十九条第一款规定的原则，由产权登记一方对另一方进行补偿。

**Article 10** Where before marriage, one spouse signs a contract for the purchase of immovable property, pays the down payment with personal property, and obtains a bank loan, and after marriage, the spouses repay the loan with community property, and the immovable property is registered in the name of the party who paid the down payment, upon divorce, the immovable property shall be dealt with by agreement between the parties.  
Where an agreement cannot be reached in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may decide by judgment that the immovable property belongs to the party in whose name the property right is registered, and the unpaid loan shall be the personal debt of that party. The amounts paid by both spouses after marriage for the joint repayment of the loan and the corresponding appreciation of the property shall, upon divorce, be compensated by the party in whose name the property right is registered to the other party in accordance with the principles set forth in Article 39, paragraph 1 of the Marriage Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  一方未经另一方同意出售夫妻共同共有的房屋，第三人善意购买、支付合理对价并办理产权登记手续，另一方主张追回该房屋的，人民法院不予支持。  
夫妻一方擅自处分共同共有的房屋造成另一方损失，离婚时另一方请求赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where one party, without the consent of the other party, sells a house jointly owned by the spouses, and a third party purchases it in good faith, pays a reasonable price, and completes the registration of property right, and the other party claims to recover the house, the People's Court shall not sustain the claim.  
Where one spouse, without authorization, disposes of a jointly owned house and causes losses to the other spouse, and upon divorce the other spouse requests compensation for the losses, the People's Court shall sustain the request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  婚姻关系存续期间，双方用夫妻共同财产出资购买以一方父母名义参加房改的房屋，产权登记在一方父母名下，离婚时另一方主张按照夫妻共同财产对该房屋进行分割的，人民法院不予支持。购买该房屋时的出资，可以作为债权处理。

**Article 12** During the existence of the marital relationship, where both spouses use community property to contribute to the purchase of a house under the housing reform in the name of one spouse's parents, and the property right is registered in the name of that spouse's parents, upon divorce, where the other spouse claims to divide the house as community property, the People's Court shall not sustain the claim. The contribution made for the purchase of the house may be dealt with as a creditor's right.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  离婚时夫妻一方尚未退休、不符合领取养老保险金条件，另一方请求按照夫妻共同财产分割养老保险金的，人民法院不予支持；婚后以夫妻共同财产缴付养老保险费，离婚时一方主张将养老金账户中婚姻关系存续期间个人实际缴付部分作为夫妻共同财产分割的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where, at the time of divorce, one spouse has not yet retired and does not meet the conditions for receiving pension insurance benefits, and the other party requests partition of the pension insurance benefits as community property, the People's Court shall not sustain the request; where after marriage, pension insurance premiums are paid with community property, and at the time of divorce, one party claims that the portion actually paid by the individual during the existence of the marital relationship in the pension account shall be divided as community property, the People's Court shall sustain the request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  当事人达成的以登记离婚或者到人民法院协议离婚为条件的财产分割协议，如果双方协议离婚未成，一方在离婚诉讼中反悔的，人民法院应当认定该财产分割协议没有生效，并根据实际情况依法对夫妻共同财产进行分割。

**Article 14** Where the parties reach an agreement on property division conditional upon registration of divorce or consensual divorce at the People's Court, if the consensual divorce fails and one party repudiates the agreement in the divorce proceedings, the People's Court shall find that the property division agreement has not taken effect, and shall divide the community property according to law based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  婚姻关系存续期间，夫妻一方作为继承人依法可以继承的遗产，在继承人之间尚未实际分割，起诉离婚时另一方请求分割的，人民法院应当告知当事人在继承人之间实际分割遗产后另行起诉。

**Article 15** During the existence of the marital relationship, where one spouse, as an heir, may inherit an estate according to law, but the estate has not yet been actually divided among the heirs, and at the time of filing a divorce action the other party requests partition, the People's Court shall inform the parties to file a separate action after the actual division of the estate among the heirs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  夫妻之间订立借款协议，以夫妻共同财产出借给一方从事个人经营活动或用于其他个人事务的，应视为双方约定处分夫妻共同财产的行为，离婚时可按照借款协议的约定处理。

**Article 16** Where the spouses enter into a loan agreement, and lend community property to one spouse for engaging in personal business operations or for other personal affairs, it shall be deemed as an act of the parties agreeing to dispose of community property, and upon divorce, it may be dealt with in accordance with the terms of the loan agreement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  夫妻双方均有婚姻法第四十六条规定的过错情形，一方或者双方向对方提出离婚损害赔偿请求的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 17** Where both spouses are in the situation of fault as specified in Article 46 of the Marriage Law, and one party or both parties claim damages for divorce from the other party, the People's Court shall not sustain the request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  离婚后，一方以尚有夫妻共同财产未处理为由向人民法院起诉请求分割的，经审查该财产确属离婚时未涉及的夫妻共同财产，人民法院应当依法予以分割。

**Article 18** After divorce, where one party files a lawsuit with the People's Court requesting partition on the ground that there is still community property that has not been dealt with, and upon examination, the property is indeed community property not involved at the time of divorce, the People's Court shall divide it according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  本解释施行后，最高人民法院此前作出的相关司法解释与本解释相抵触的，以本解释为准。

**Article 19** After this Interpretation comes into force, if any relevant judicial interpretation previously made by the Supreme People's Court conflicts with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
