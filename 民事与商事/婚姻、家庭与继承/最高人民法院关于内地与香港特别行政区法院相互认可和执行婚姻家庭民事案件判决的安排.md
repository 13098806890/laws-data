# 最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行婚姻家庭民事案件判决的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕4号  
**公布日期**：2017-05-22  
**生效日期**：2022-02-15

> 《最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行婚姻家庭民事案件判决的安排》已于2017年5月22日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1718次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年2月15日起施行。 2022年2月14日 最高人民法院 关于内地与香港特别行政区法院 相互认可和执行婚姻家庭民事案件判决的安排 法释〔2022〕4号 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府经协商，现就婚姻家庭民事案件判决的认可和执行问题作出如下安排。 第一条　当事人向香港特别行政区法院申请认可和执行内地人民法院就婚姻家庭民事案件作出的生效判决，或者向内地人民法院申请认可和执行香港特别行政区法院就婚姻家庭民事案件作出的生效判决的，适用本安排。 当事人向香港特别行政区法院申请认可内地民政部门所发的离婚证，或者向内地人民法院申请认可依据《婚姻制度改革条例》（香港法例第178章）第V部、第VA部规定解除婚姻的协议书、备忘录的，参照适用本安排。 第二条　本安排所称生效判决： （一）在内地，是指第二审判决，依法不准上诉或者超过法定期限没有上诉的第一审判决，以及依照审判监督程序作出的上述判决； （二）在香港特别行政区，是指终审法院、高等法院上诉法庭及原讼法庭和区域法院作出的已经发生法律效力的判决，包括依据香港法律可以在生效后作出更改的命令。 前款所称判决，在内地包括判决、裁定、调解书，在香港特别行政区包括判决、命令、判令、讼费评定证明书、定额讼费证明书，但不包括双方依据其法律承认的其他国家和地区法院作出的判决。 第三条　本安排所称婚姻家庭民事案件： （一）在内地是指：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人向香港特别行政区法院申请认可和执行内地人民法院就婚姻家庭民事案件作出的生效判决，或者向内地人民法院申请认可和执行香港特别行政区法院就婚姻家庭民事案件作出的生效判决的，适用本安排。  
当事人向香港特别行政区法院申请认可内地民政部门所发的离婚证，或者向内地人民法院申请认可依据《婚姻制度改革条例》（香港法例第178章）第V部、第VA部规定解除婚姻的协议书、备忘录的，参照适用本安排。

**Article 1** Where a party applies to a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for recognition and enforcement of an effective judgment rendered by a People's Court in the Mainland regarding a matrimonial and family civil case, or applies to a People's Court in the Mainland for recognition and enforcement of an effective judgment rendered by a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region regarding a matrimonial and family civil case, this Arrangement shall apply.  
Where a party applies to a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for recognition of a divorce certificate issued by the civil affairs department in the Mainland, or applies to a People's Court in the Mainland for recognition of an agreement or memorandum for dissolution of marriage pursuant to Part V and Part VA of the Marriage Reform Ordinance (Chapter 178 of the Laws of Hong Kong), this Arrangement shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本安排所称生效判决：  
（一）在内地，是指第二审判决，依法不准上诉或者超过法定期限没有上诉的第一审判决，以及依照审判监督程序作出的上述判决；  
（二）在香港特别行政区，是指终审法院、高等法院上诉法庭及原讼法庭和区域法院作出的已经发生法律效力的判决，包括依据香港法律可以在生效后作出更改的命令。  
前款所称判决，在内地包括判决、裁定、调解书，在香港特别行政区包括判决、命令、判令、讼费评定证明书、定额讼费证明书，但不包括双方依据其法律承认的其他国家和地区法院作出的判决。

**Article 2** The term "effective judgment" as used in this Arrangement means:  
(1) in the Mainland, a judgment of second instance, a judgment of first instance which according to law is not subject to appeal or for which no appeal has been lodged within the statutory time limit, and the aforesaid judgments rendered in accordance with the trial supervision procedure;  
(2) in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, a judgment that has come into force rendered by the Court of Final Appeal, the Court of Appeal and the Court of First Instance of the High Court, and the District Court, including an order that may be varied after it takes effect under Hong Kong law.  
The term "judgment" as used in the preceding paragraph includes, in the Mainland, judgments, rulings, and mediation documents, and in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, judgments, orders, decrees, certificates of taxation of costs, and fixed costs certificates, but does not include judgments made by courts of other countries or regions that are recognized by the law of each side.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本安排所称婚姻家庭民事案件：  
（一）在内地是指：  
1.婚内夫妻财产分割纠纷案件；  
2.离婚纠纷案件；  
3.离婚后财产纠纷案件；  
4.婚姻无效纠纷案件；  
5.撤销婚姻纠纷案件；  
6.夫妻财产约定纠纷案件；  
7.同居关系子女抚养纠纷案件；  
8.亲子关系确认纠纷案件；  
9.抚养纠纷案件；  
10.扶养纠纷案件（限于夫妻之间扶养纠纷）；  
11.确认收养关系纠纷案件；  
12.监护权纠纷案件（限于未成年子女监护权纠纷）；  
13.探望权纠纷案件；  
14.申请人身安全保护令案件。  
（二）在香港特别行政区是指：  
1.依据香港法例第179章《婚姻诉讼条例》第III部作出的离婚绝对判令；  
2.依据香港法例第179章《婚姻诉讼条例》第IV部作出的婚姻无效绝对判令；  
3.依据香港法例第192章《婚姻法律程序与财产条例》作出的在讼案待决期间提供赡养费令；  
4.依据香港法例第13章《未成年人监护条例》、第16章《分居令及赡养令条例》、第192章《婚姻法律程序与财产条例》第II部、第IIA部作出的赡养令；  
5.依据香港法例第13章《未成年人监护条例》、第192章《婚姻法律程序与财产条例》第II部、第IIA部作出的财产转让及出售财产令；  
6.依据香港法例第182章《已婚者地位条例》作出的有关财产的命令；  
7.依据香港法例第192章《婚姻法律程序与财产条例》在双方在生时作出的修改赡养协议的命令；  
8.依据香港法例第290章《领养条例》作出的领养令；  
9.依据香港法例第179章《婚姻诉讼条例》、第429章《父母与子女条例》作出的父母身份、婚生地位或者确立婚生地位的宣告；  
10.依据香港法例第13章《未成年人监护条例》、第16章《分居令及赡养令条例》、第192章《婚姻法律程序与财产条例》作出的管养令；  
11.就受香港法院监护的未成年子女作出的管养令；  
12.依据香港法例第189章《家庭及同居关系暴力条例》作出的禁制骚扰令、驱逐令、重返令或者更改、暂停执行就未成年子女的管养令、探视令。

**Article 3** The term "matrimonial and family civil case" as used in this Arrangement means:  
(1) in the Mainland:  
1. cases concerning disputes over division of spousal property during marriage;  
2. cases concerning divorce disputes;  
3. cases concerning disputes over property after divorce;  
4. cases concerning disputes over nullity of marriage;  
5. cases concerning disputes over annulment of marriage;  
6. cases concerning disputes over marital property agreements;  
7. cases concerning disputes over child support in cohabitation relationships;  
8. cases concerning disputes over confirmation of parent-child relationship;  
9. cases concerning disputes over child support;  
10. cases concerning disputes over spousal support (limited to disputes between husband and wife);  
11. cases concerning disputes over confirmation of adoption relationship;  
12. cases concerning disputes over custody rights (limited to disputes over custody of minor children);  
13. cases concerning disputes over visitation rights;  
14. cases concerning applications for personal safety protection orders.  
(2) in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region:  
1. absolute decrees of divorce made under Part III of the Matrimonial Causes Ordinance (Chapter 179 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
2. absolute decrees of nullity of marriage made under Part IV of the Matrimonial Causes Ordinance (Chapter 179 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
3. orders for maintenance pending suit made under the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Chapter 192 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
4. maintenance orders made under the Guardianship of Minors Ordinance (Chapter 13 of the Laws of Hong Kong), the Separation and Maintenance Orders Ordinance (Chapter 16 of the Laws of Hong Kong), and Part II and Part IIA of the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Chapter 192 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
5. orders for transfer and sale of property made under the Guardianship of Minors Ordinance (Chapter 13 of the Laws of Hong Kong) and Part II and Part IIA of the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Chapter 192 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
6. orders relating to property made under the Married Persons Status Ordinance (Chapter 182 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
7. orders varying maintenance agreements during the lifetime of both parties made under the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Chapter 192 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
8. adoption orders made under the Adoption Ordinance (Chapter 290 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
9. declarations of parentage, legitimacy, or legitimation made under the Matrimonial Causes Ordinance (Chapter 179 of the Laws of Hong Kong) and the Parent and Child Ordinance (Chapter 429 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
10. custody orders made under the Guardianship of Minors Ordinance (Chapter 13 of the Laws of Hong Kong), the Separation and Maintenance Orders Ordinance (Chapter 16 of the Laws of Hong Kong), and the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Chapter 192 of the Laws of Hong Kong);  
11. custody orders in respect of minor children under the guardianship of the Hong Kong court;  
12. orders for injunction against molestation, ouster orders, return orders, or orders varying or suspending custody orders or access orders in respect of minor children made under the Domestic and Cohabitation Relationships Violence Ordinance (Chapter 189 of the Laws of Hong Kong).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请认可和执行本安排规定的判决：  
（一）在内地向申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被申请人住所地、经常居住地、财产所在地的中级人民法院提出；  
（二）在香港特别行政区向区域法院提出。  
申请人应当向符合前款第一项规定的其中一个人民法院提出申请。向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院提出申请的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。

**Article 4** An application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment under this Arrangement shall be made:  
(1) in the Mainland, to the Intermediate People's Court of the place of domicile or habitual residence of the applicant or the place of domicile, habitual residence, or location of property of the respondent;  
(2) in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, to the District Court.  
The applicant shall make the application to one of the People's Courts that meets the conditions under subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph. Where an application is made to two or more People's Courts that have jurisdiction, the People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　申请认可和执行本安排第一条第一款规定的判决的，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）经作出生效判决的法院盖章的判决副本；  
（三）作出生效判决的法院出具的证明书，证明该判决属于本安排规定的婚姻家庭民事案件生效判决；  
（四）判决为缺席判决的，应当提交法院已经合法传唤当事人的证明文件，但判决已经对此予以明确说明或者缺席方提出申请的除外；  
（五）经公证的身份证件复印件。  
申请认可本安排第一条第二款规定的离婚证或者协议书、备忘录的，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）经公证的离婚证复印件，或者经公证的协议书、备忘录复印件；  
（三）经公证的身份证件复印件。  
向内地人民法院提交的文件没有中文文本的，应当提交准确的中文译本。

**Article 5** To apply for recognition and enforcement of a judgment under Article 1, paragraph 1 of this Arrangement, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) a copy of the judgment sealed by the court that rendered the effective judgment;  
(3) a certificate issued by the court that rendered the effective judgment, certifying that the judgment is an effective judgment in a matrimonial and family civil case under this Arrangement;  
(4) if the judgment is a default judgment, a document certifying that the party has been legally summoned by the court shall be submitted, unless the judgment has expressly stated this or the defaulting party applies;  
(5) a notarized copy of the identity document.  
To apply for recognition of a divorce certificate or agreement or memorandum under Article 1, paragraph 2 of this Arrangement, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) a notarized copy of the divorce certificate, or a notarized copy of the agreement or memorandum;  
(3) a notarized copy of the identity document.  
Where a document submitted to a People's Court in the Mainland does not have a Chinese text, an accurate Chinese translation shall be submitted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况，包括姓名、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；  
（二）请求事项和理由，申请执行的，还需提供被申请人的财产状况和财产所在地；  
（三）判决是否已在其他法院申请执行和执行情况。

**Article 6** The written application shall state the following:  
(1) basic information of the parties, including names, domiciles, identity document information, means of communication, etc.;  
(2) the requested matters and reasons, and if enforcement is applied for, the property status and location of the respondent shall also be provided;  
(3) whether the judgment has been applied for enforcement in other courts and the status of such enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请认可和执行判决的期间、程序和方式，应当依据被请求方法律的规定。

**Article 7** The time limit, procedure, and method for applying for recognition and enforcement of a judgment shall be governed by the law of the requested party.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　法院应当尽快审查认可和执行的请求，并作出裁定或者命令。

**Article 8** The court shall examine the request for recognition and enforcement as soon as possible and render a ruling or order.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请认可和执行的判决，被申请人提供证据证明有下列情形之一的，法院审查核实后，不予认可和执行：  
（一）根据原审法院地法律，被申请人未经合法传唤，或者虽经合法传唤但未获得合理的陈述、辩论机会的；  
（二）判决是以欺诈方法取得的；  
（三）被请求方法院受理相关诉讼后，请求方法院又受理就同一争议提起的诉讼并作出判决的；  
（四）被请求方法院已经就同一争议作出判决，或者已经认可和执行其他国家和地区法院就同一争议所作出的判决的。  
内地人民法院认为认可和执行香港特别行政区法院判决明显违反内地法律的基本原则或者社会公共利益，香港特别行政区法院认为认可和执行内地人民法院判决明显违反香港特别行政区法律的基本原则或者公共政策的，不予认可和执行。  
申请认可和执行的判决涉及未成年子女的，在根据前款规定审查决定是否认可和执行时，应当充分考虑未成年子女的最佳利益。

**Article 9** Where the respondent provides evidence proving the existence of any of the following circumstances in respect of a judgment for which recognition and enforcement is applied, the court, upon examination and verification, shall not recognize and enforce:  
(1) under the law of the original trial court, the respondent was not legally summoned, or although legally summoned, did not have a reasonable opportunity to make statements or pleadings;  
(2) the judgment was obtained by fraud;  
(3) after the court of the requested party accepted the relevant lawsuit, the court of the requesting party accepted a lawsuit on the same dispute and rendered a judgment;  
(4) the court of the requested party has already rendered a judgment on the same dispute, or has recognized and enforced a judgment made by a court of another country or region on the same dispute.  
Where a People's Court in the Mainland considers that recognizing and enforcing a judgment of a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region would manifestly violate the basic principles of the law of the Mainland or social public interests, or where a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region considers that recognizing and enforcing a judgment of a People's Court in the Mainland would manifestly violate the basic principles of the law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or public policy, recognition and enforcement shall not be granted.  
Where a judgment for which recognition and enforcement is applied involves minor children, when reviewing and deciding whether to recognize and enforce in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the best interests of the minor children shall be fully considered.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被请求方法院不能对判决的全部判项予以认可和执行时，可以认可和执行其中的部分判项。

**Article 10** Where the court of the requested party cannot recognize and enforce all items of a judgment, it may recognize and enforce part of the items.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于香港特别行政区法院作出的判决，一方当事人已经提出上诉，内地人民法院审查核实后，可以中止认可和执行程序。经上诉，维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。  
内地人民法院就已经作出的判决裁定再审的，香港特别行政区法院审查核实后，可以中止认可和执行程序。经再审，维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。

**Article 11** Where an appeal has been lodged by one party against a judgment of a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the People's Court in the Mainland, after examination and verification, may suspend the recognition and enforcement proceedings. If the original judgment is wholly or partially upheld on appeal, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be resumed; if the original judgment is completely altered, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be terminated.  
Where a People's Court in the Mainland has ordered a retrial of a judgment already rendered, the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, after examination and verification, may suspend the recognition and enforcement proceedings. If the original judgment is wholly or partially maintained upon retrial, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be resumed; if the original judgment is completely altered, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be terminated.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在本安排下，内地人民法院作出的有关财产归一方所有的判项，在香港特别行政区将被视为命令一方向另一方转让该财产。

**Article 12** Under this Arrangement, an item of judgment made by a People's Court in the Mainland ordering that property belongs to one party shall be regarded in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region as an order directing one party to transfer the property to the other party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被申请人在内地和香港特别行政区均有可供执行财产的，申请人可以分别向两地法院申请执行。  
两地法院执行财产的总额不得超过判决确定的数额。应对方法院要求，两地法院应当相互提供本院执行判决的情况。

**Article 13** Where the respondent has property available for enforcement in both the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the applicant may apply for enforcement to the courts of both places separately.  
The total amount of property enforced by the courts of the two places shall not exceed the amount determined in the judgment. Upon request by the other court, the courts of the two places shall provide each other with information on the enforcement of the judgment by their respective courts.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行的财产给付范围，包括判决确定的给付财产和相应的利息、迟延履行金、诉讼费，不包括税收、罚款。  
前款所称诉讼费，在香港特别行政区是指讼费评定证明书、定额讼费证明书核定或者命令支付的费用。

**Article 14** The scope of property payment for mutual recognition and enforcement between the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall include the property payment determined by the judgment and the corresponding interest, late performance damages, and litigation costs, but shall not include taxes and fines.  
The term "litigation costs" in the preceding paragraph refers, in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, to the costs assessed or ordered to be paid by a certificate of taxation of costs or a fixed costs certificate.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被请求方法院就认可和执行的申请作出裁定或者命令后，当事人不服的，在内地可以于裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，在香港特别行政区可以依据其法律规定提出上诉。

**Article 15** After the court of the requested party renders a ruling or order on an application for recognition and enforcement, if a party is dissatisfied, in the Mainland, the party may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling; in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the party may appeal in accordance with its legal provisions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在审理婚姻家庭民事案件期间，当事人申请认可和执行另一地法院就同一争议作出的判决的，应当受理。受理后，有关诉讼应当中止，待就认可和执行的申请作出裁定或者命令后，再视情终止或者恢复诉讼。

**Article 16** During the adjudication of a matrimonial and family civil case, if a party applies for recognition and enforcement of a judgment rendered by a court of the other place on the same dispute, the application shall be accepted. After acceptance, the relevant proceedings shall be suspended, and upon a ruling or order on the application for recognition and enforcement, the proceedings shall be terminated or resumed as the case may be.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　审查认可和执行判决申请期间，当事人就同一争议提起诉讼的，不予受理；已经受理的，驳回起诉。  
判决获得认可和执行后，当事人又就同一争议提起诉讼的，不予受理。  
判决未获认可和执行的，申请人不得再次申请认可和执行，但可以就同一争议向被请求方法院提起诉讼。

**Article 17** During the period of reviewing an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, if a party institutes a lawsuit on the same dispute, the lawsuit shall not be accepted; if already accepted, the prosecution shall be dismissed.  
After a judgment is recognized and enforced, if a party institutes a lawsuit on the same dispute, the lawsuit shall not be accepted.  
If a judgment is not recognized and enforced, the applicant shall not apply again for recognition and enforcement, but may institute a lawsuit on the same dispute in the court of the requested party.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被请求方法院在受理认可和执行判决的申请之前或者之后，可以依据其法律规定采取保全或者强制措施。

**Article 18** The court of the requested party, before or after accepting an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, may take preservation or compulsory measures in accordance with its legal provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请认可和执行判决的，应当依据被请求方有关诉讼收费的法律和规定交纳费用。

**Article 19** To apply for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, fees shall be paid in accordance with the laws and regulations on litigation costs of the requested party.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　内地与香港特别行政区法院自本安排生效之日起作出的判决，适用本安排。

**Article 20** This Arrangement shall apply to judgments rendered by the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region from the date of its entry into force.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本安排在执行过程中遇有问题或者需要修改的，由最高人民法院和香港特别行政区政府协商解决。

**Article 21** If any problems arise or amendment is needed during the implementation of this Arrangement, the Supreme People's Court and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall resolve them through consultation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本安排自2022年2月15日起施行。

**Article 22** This Arrangement shall come into force as of February 15, 2022.
