# 婚姻登记条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2025-04-06  
**生效日期**：2025-05-10

> （2003年8月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第387号公布　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　2025年4月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第804号第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范婚姻登记工作，保障婚姻自由、一夫一妻、男女平等的婚姻制度的实施，保护婚姻当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本条例。 第二条　内地居民办理婚姻登记的机关是县级人民政府民政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照便民原则确定的乡（镇）人民政府。 中国公民同外国人，内地居民同香港特别行政区居民（以下简称香港居民）、澳门特别行政区居民（以下简称澳门居民）、台湾地区居民（以下简称台湾居民）、华侨办理婚姻登记的机关是省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门确定的机关。 第三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施提升婚姻登记服务水平，加强对婚姻登记场所的规范化、便利化建设，为办理婚姻登记提供保障。 第四条　国务院民政部门统筹规划、完善全国婚姻基础信息库，会同外交、公安等有关部门以及最高人民法院建立健全信息共享机制，保障婚姻信息准确、及时、完整、安全。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门负责统筹本地区婚姻登记信息系统的建设、管理、维护和信息安全工作。 第五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强综合性婚姻家庭服务指导工作和婚姻家庭辅导服务体系建设，治理高额彩礼问题，倡导文明婚俗，促进家庭和谐，引导树立正确的婚恋观、生育观、家庭观。 婚姻登记机关应当提供婚姻家庭辅导服务，充分发挥婚姻家庭辅导师等专业人员和其他社会力量在婚前教育、婚姻家庭关系辅导等方面的作用。妇女联合会等组织协助和配合婚姻登记机关开展婚姻家庭辅导服务。 民政部门应当加强婚姻家庭辅导服务专业人员队伍建设，组织开展婚姻家庭辅导师职业培训，持续提升婚姻家庭辅导服务专业人员的职业素质和业务技能水平。 第六条　婚姻登记机关从事婚姻登记的工作人员应当接受婚姻登记业务培训，依照有关规定经考核合格，方可从事婚姻登记工作。 婚姻登记机关办理婚姻登记，不得收取费用。 婚姻登记机关及其工作人员在婚姻登记工作中发现疑似被拐卖、绑架的妇女的，应当依法及时向有关部门报告；发现当事人遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力的现实危险的，应当及时劝阻并告知受害人寻求救助的途径。 婚姻登记机关及其工作人员应当对在婚姻登记工作中知悉的个人隐私、个人信息予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。 第二章　结婚登记

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范婚姻登记工作，保障婚姻自由、一夫一妻、男女平等的婚姻制度的实施，保护婚姻当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Code) for the purposes of regulating marriage registration work, ensuring the implementation of the marriage system of freedom of marriage, monogamy, and equality between men and women, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of the parties to marriage.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　内地居民办理婚姻登记的机关是县级人民政府民政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照便民原则确定的乡（镇）人民政府。  
中国公民同外国人，内地居民同香港特别行政区居民（以下简称香港居民）、澳门特别行政区居民（以下简称澳门居民）、台湾地区居民（以下简称台湾居民）、华侨办理婚姻登记的机关是省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门确定的机关。

**Article 2** The authority for handling marriage registration for mainland residents shall be the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level or the people's government of a township (town) designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the principle of convenience.  
The authority for handling marriage registration between Chinese citizens and foreigners, between mainland residents and residents of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (hereinafter referred to as Hong Kong residents), residents of the Macao Special Administrative Region (hereinafter referred to as Macao residents), residents of the Taiwan region (hereinafter referred to as Taiwan residents), or overseas Chinese shall be the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or an authority designated by the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施提升婚姻登记服务水平，加强对婚姻登记场所的规范化、便利化建设，为办理婚姻登记提供保障。

**Article 3** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to improve the service level of marriage registration, strengthen the standardization and convenience of marriage registration venues, and provide guarantees for handling marriage registration.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院民政部门统筹规划、完善全国婚姻基础信息库，会同外交、公安等有关部门以及最高人民法院建立健全信息共享机制，保障婚姻信息准确、及时、完整、安全。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门负责统筹本地区婚姻登记信息系统的建设、管理、维护和信息安全工作。

**Article 4** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall make overall planning and perfect the national marriage basic information database, and shall, jointly with the relevant departments such as foreign affairs and public security as well as the Supreme People's Court, establish and improve an information sharing mechanism to ensure that marriage information is accurate, timely, complete, and secure. The civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the overall planning, management, maintenance, and information security of the marriage registration information system in its region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强综合性婚姻家庭服务指导工作和婚姻家庭辅导服务体系建设，治理高额彩礼问题，倡导文明婚俗，促进家庭和谐，引导树立正确的婚恋观、生育观、家庭观。  
婚姻登记机关应当提供婚姻家庭辅导服务，充分发挥婚姻家庭辅导师等专业人员和其他社会力量在婚前教育、婚姻家庭关系辅导等方面的作用。妇女联合会等组织协助和配合婚姻登记机关开展婚姻家庭辅导服务。  
民政部门应当加强婚姻家庭辅导服务专业人员队伍建设，组织开展婚姻家庭辅导师职业培训，持续提升婚姻家庭辅导服务专业人员的职业素质和业务技能水平。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen comprehensive marriage and family service guidance and the construction of a marriage and family counseling service system, address the issue of high bride prices, advocate civilized marriage customs, promote family harmony, and guide the establishment of correct views on love, marriage, childbirth, and family.  
Marriage registration authorities shall provide marriage and family counseling services, and fully leverage the role of professional personnel such as marriage and family counselors and other social forces in pre-marital education, marriage and family relationship counseling, and other aspects. Organizations such as the Women's Federation shall assist and cooperate with marriage registration authorities in carrying out marriage and family counseling services.  
Civil affairs departments shall strengthen the construction of a professional team for marriage and family counseling services, organize and carry out vocational training for marriage and family counselors, and continuously improve the professional quality and business skill level of marriage and family counseling service professionals.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　婚姻登记机关从事婚姻登记的工作人员应当接受婚姻登记业务培训，依照有关规定经考核合格，方可从事婚姻登记工作。  
婚姻登记机关办理婚姻登记，不得收取费用。  
婚姻登记机关及其工作人员在婚姻登记工作中发现疑似被拐卖、绑架的妇女的，应当依法及时向有关部门报告；发现当事人遭受家庭暴力或者面临家庭暴力的现实危险的，应当及时劝阻并告知受害人寻求救助的途径。  
婚姻登记机关及其工作人员应当对在婚姻登记工作中知悉的个人隐私、个人信息予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。

**Article 6** Staff members engaged in marriage registration at marriage registration authorities shall receive training on marriage registration business, and may only engage in marriage registration work after passing the assessment in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Marriage registration authorities shall not charge any fees for handling marriage registration.  
Where a marriage registration authority or its staff members discover a woman suspected of being trafficked or kidnapped during marriage registration work, they shall promptly report to the relevant departments according to law; where they discover that a party is suffering from domestic violence or faces a real risk of domestic violence, they shall promptly dissuade and inform the victim of the means to seek assistance.  
Marriage registration authorities and their staff members shall keep confidential the personal privacy and personal information learned during marriage registration work, and shall not disclose or illegally provide them to others.

### 第二章　结婚登记

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　内地居民结婚，男女双方应当亲自到婚姻登记机关共同申请结婚登记。  
中国公民同外国人在中国内地结婚的，内地居民同香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民、华侨在中国内地结婚的，男女双方应当亲自到[本条例第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的婚姻登记机关共同申请结婚登记。  
婚姻登记机关可以结合实际为结婚登记当事人提供预约、颁证仪式等服务。鼓励当事人邀请双方父母等参加颁证仪式。

**Article 7** For marriage between mainland residents, both the man and woman shall appear in person at the marriage registration authority to jointly apply for marriage registration.  
For marriage between a Chinese citizen and a foreigner within the mainland of China, or between a mainland resident and a Hong Kong resident, Macao resident, Taiwan resident, or overseas Chinese within the mainland of China, both the man and woman shall appear in person at the marriage registration authority specified in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of these Regulations to jointly apply for marriage registration.  
Marriage registration authorities may, based on actual circumstances, provide services such as appointment scheduling and certificate awarding ceremonies for parties applying for marriage registration. Parties are encouraged to invite both parents and others to attend the certificate awarding ceremony.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请结婚登记的内地居民应当出具下列证件和书面材料：  
（一）本人的居民身份证；  
（二）本人无配偶以及与对方当事人没有直系血亲和三代以内旁系血亲关系的签字声明。  
申请结婚登记的香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民应当出具下列证件和书面材料：  
（一）本人的有效通行证或者港澳台居民居住证、身份证；  
（二）经居住地公证机构公证的本人无配偶以及与对方当事人没有直系血亲和三代以内旁系血亲关系的声明。  
申请结婚登记的华侨应当出具下列证件和书面材料：  
（一）本人的有效护照；  
（二）居住国公证机构或者有权机关出具的、经中华人民共和国驻该国使（领）馆认证的本人无配偶以及与对方当事人没有直系血亲和三代以内旁系血亲关系的证明，或者中华人民共和国驻该国使（领）馆出具的本人无配偶以及与对方当事人没有直系血亲和三代以内旁系血亲关系的证明。中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的，按照国际条约规定的证明手续办理。  
申请结婚登记的外国人应当出具下列证件和书面材料：  
（一）本人的有效护照或者其他有效的国际旅行证件，或者外国人永久居留身份证等中国政府主管机关签发的身份证件；  
（二）所在国公证机构或者有权机关出具的、经中华人民共和国驻该国使（领）馆认证或者该国驻华使（领）馆认证的本人无配偶的证明，或者所在国驻华使（领）馆出具的本人无配偶的证明。中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的，按照国际条约规定的证明手续办理。  
申请结婚登记的当事人对外国主管机关依据本条第三款、第四款提及的国际条约出具的证明文书的真实性负责，并签署书面声明。

**Article 8** A mainland resident applying for marriage registration shall produce the following certificates and written materials:  
(1) his/her resident identity card;  
(2) a signed statement declaring that he/she has no spouse and has no direct blood relationship or collateral blood relationship within three generations with the other party.  
A Hong Kong resident, Macao resident, or Taiwan resident applying for marriage registration shall produce the following certificates and written materials:  
(1) his/her valid travel permit or residence permit for Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan residents, and identity card;  
(2) a declaration, notarized by a notarial institution at the place of residence, stating that he/she has no spouse and has no direct blood relationship or collateral blood relationship within three generations with the other party.  
An overseas Chinese applying for marriage registration shall produce the following certificates and written materials:  
(1) his/her valid passport;  
(2) a certificate issued by a notarial institution or competent authority of the country of residence, authenticated by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country, stating that he/she has no spouse and has no direct blood relationship or collateral blood relationship within three generations with the other party, or a certificate issued by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country stating that he/she has no spouse and has no direct blood relationship or collateral blood relationship within three generations with the other party. Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China provides otherwise, the certification procedures specified in the international treaty shall be followed.  
A foreigner applying for marriage registration shall produce the following certificates and written materials:  
(1) his/her valid passport or other valid international travel document, or identity document issued by the competent authority of the Chinese government, such as the foreign permanent residence identity card;  
(2) a certificate issued by a notarial institution or competent authority of the country of nationality, authenticated by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country or by the embassy or consulate of that country in China, stating that he/she has no spouse, or a certificate issued by the embassy or consulate of the country of nationality in China stating that he/she has no spouse. Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China provides otherwise, the certification procedures specified in the international treaty shall be followed.  
A party applying for marriage registration shall be responsible for the authenticity of the certifying documents issued by a foreign competent authority pursuant to the international treaty referred to in paragraphs 3 and 4 of this Article, and shall sign a written declaration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请结婚登记的当事人有下列情形之一的，婚姻登记机关不予登记：  
（一）未到法定结婚年龄的；  
（二）非男女双方完全自愿的；  
（三）一方或者双方已有配偶的；  
（四）属于直系血亲或者三代以内旁系血亲的。

**Article 9** Where a party applying for marriage registration falls under any of the following circumstances, the marriage registration authority shall not register the marriage:  
(1) failing to reach the legal age for marriage;  
(2) not being entirely voluntary on the part of both the man and woman;  
(3) one or both parties already have a spouse;  
(4) being a direct blood relative or a collateral blood relative within three generations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　婚姻登记机关应当核对结婚登记当事人出具的证件、书面材料，询问相关情况，并对当事人的身份以及婚姻状况信息进行联网核对，依法维护当事人的权益。对当事人符合结婚条件的，应当当场予以登记，发给结婚证；对当事人不符合结婚条件不予登记的，应当向当事人说明理由。

**Article 10** The marriage registration authority shall verify the certificates and written materials produced by the parties applying for marriage registration, inquire about relevant circumstances, and conduct online verification of the parties' identity and marital status information, so as to protect the parties' rights and interests according to law. Where the parties meet the conditions for marriage, the authority shall register them on the spot and issue marriage certificates; where the parties do not meet the conditions for marriage and registration is not granted, the authority shall inform the parties of the reasons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　要求结婚的男女双方未办理结婚登记的，应当补办登记。男女双方补办结婚登记的，适用本条例结婚登记的规定。

**Article 11** Where a man and woman who intend to marry have not undergone marriage registration, they shall go through the registration retroactively. Where a man and woman go through marriage registration retroactively, the provisions on marriage registration in these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因胁迫结婚的，受胁迫的当事人可以依据[民法典第一千零五十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1052)的规定向人民法院请求撤销婚姻。一方当事人患有重大疾病的，应当在结婚登记前如实告知另一方当事人；不如实告知的，另一方当事人可以依据[民法典第一千零五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1053)的规定向人民法院请求撤销婚姻。

**Article 12** Where marriage is concluded under coercion, the coerced party may, in accordance with Article 1052 of the Civil Code, petition the People's Court to annul the marriage. Where a party suffers from a serious disease, that party shall truthfully inform the other party before marriage registration; failing to do so, the other party may, in accordance with Article 1053 of the Civil Code, petition the People's Court to annul the marriage.

### 第三章　离婚登记

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　内地居民自愿离婚的，男女双方应当签订书面离婚协议，亲自到婚姻登记机关共同申请离婚登记。  
中国公民同外国人在中国内地自愿离婚的，内地居民同香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民、华侨在中国内地自愿离婚的，男女双方应当签订书面离婚协议，亲自到[本条例第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的婚姻登记机关共同申请离婚登记。  
离婚协议应当载明双方自愿离婚的意思表示和对子女抚养、财产以及债务处理等事项协商一致的意见。

**Article 13** Where mainland residents voluntarily divorce, both the man and woman shall sign a written divorce agreement and appear in person at the marriage registration authority to jointly apply for divorce registration.  
Where a Chinese citizen and a foreigner voluntarily divorce within the mainland of China, or a mainland resident voluntarily divorces a Hong Kong resident, Macao resident, Taiwan resident, or overseas Chinese within the mainland of China, both the man and woman shall sign a written divorce agreement and appear in person at the marriage registration authority specified in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of these Regulations to jointly apply for divorce registration.  
The divorce agreement shall specify the expression of mutual willingness to divorce and the consensus reached on matters such as child support, property division, and debt settlement.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请离婚登记的当事人有下列情形之一的，婚姻登记机关不予受理：  
（一）未达成离婚协议的；  
（二）属于无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的；  
（三）其结婚登记不是在中国内地办理的。

**Article 14** Where a party applying for divorce registration falls under any of the following circumstances, the marriage registration authority shall not accept the application:  
(1) failing to reach a divorce agreement;  
(2) being a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct;  
(3) the marriage registration was not handled in the mainland of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请离婚登记的内地居民应当出具下列证件：  
（一）本人的居民身份证；  
（二）本人的结婚证。  
申请离婚登记的香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民、华侨、外国人除应当出具前款第二项规定的证件外，香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民还应当出具本人的有效通行证或者港澳台居民居住证、身份证；华侨、外国人还应当出具本人的有效护照或者其他有效的国际旅行证件，或者外国人永久居留身份证等中国政府主管机关签发的身份证件。

**Article 15** A mainland resident applying for divorce registration shall produce the following certificates:  
(1) his/her resident identity card;  
(2) his/her marriage certificate.  
A Hong Kong resident, Macao resident, Taiwan resident, overseas Chinese, or foreigner applying for divorce registration shall, in addition to producing the certificate specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, also produce: for Hong Kong residents, Macao residents, and Taiwan residents, their valid travel permit or residence permit for Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan residents, and identity card; for overseas Chinese and foreigners, their valid passport or other valid international travel document, or identity document issued by the competent authority of the Chinese government, such as the foreign permanent residence identity card.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　婚姻登记机关应当在法律规定期限内，根据当事人的申请，核对离婚登记当事人出具的证件、书面材料并询问相关情况。对当事人确属自愿离婚，并已经对子女抚养、财产以及债务处理等事项协商一致，男女双方亲自到收到离婚登记申请的婚姻登记机关共同申请发给离婚证的，婚姻登记机关应当当场予以登记，发给离婚证。  
当事人未在法律规定期限内申请发给离婚证的，视为撤回离婚登记申请，离婚登记程序终止。

**Article 16** The marriage registration authority shall, within the time limit prescribed by law and based on the parties' application, verify the certificates and written materials produced by the parties applying for divorce registration and inquire about relevant circumstances. Where the parties are indeed voluntarily divorcing and have reached consensus on matters such as child support, property division, and debt settlement, and both the man and woman appear in person at the marriage registration authority that received the divorce registration application to jointly apply for the issuance of a divorce certificate, the marriage registration authority shall register them on the spot and issue a divorce certificate.  
Where the parties do not apply for the issuance of a divorce certificate within the time limit prescribed by law, the application for divorce registration shall be deemed withdrawn, and the divorce registration procedure shall be terminated.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　婚姻登记机关在办理离婚登记过程中，可以根据情况及时对离婚登记当事人开展心理辅导、调解等工作。

**Article 17** In the course of handling divorce registration, the marriage registration authority may, depending on the circumstances, promptly carry out psychological counseling, mediation, and other work for the parties applying for divorce registration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　离婚后，男女双方自愿恢复婚姻关系的，应当依照本条例规定到婚姻登记机关重新申请结婚登记。

**Article 18** After divorce, where both the man and woman voluntarily resume the marital relationship, they shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, go to the marriage registration authority to reapply for marriage registration.

### 第四章　婚姻登记档案管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　婚姻登记机关应当建立婚姻登记档案。婚姻登记档案应当长期保管并按规定为当事人或者有权机关提供查询服务。具体管理办法由国务院民政部门会同国家档案管理部门规定。

**Article 19** The marriage registration authority shall establish marriage registration archives. The marriage registration archives shall be kept for a long term, and query services shall be provided to the parties or competent authorities in accordance with regulations. The specific management measures shall be prescribed by the civil affairs department of the State Council jointly with the national archives administration department.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　婚姻登记机关收到人民法院确认婚姻无效或者撤销婚姻的判决书副本后，应当在当事人的婚姻登记档案中及时备注婚姻无效或者撤销婚姻的信息，并将相关信息上传至全国婚姻基础信息库。

**Article 20** After receiving a copy of a judgment from the People's Court confirming a marriage nullification or annulment, the marriage registration authority shall promptly note the information on the nullity or annulment of the marriage in the parties' marriage registration archives, and upload the relevant information to the national marriage basic information database.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人需要补领结婚证或者离婚证的，可以持居民身份证或者[本条例第八条](#art-8)第二款至第四款规定的有效身份证件向婚姻登记机关申请办理。  
婚姻登记机关对当事人的婚姻登记档案进行查证，确认属实的，应当为当事人补发结婚证或者离婚证。

**Article 21** Where a party needs to reissue a marriage certificate or divorce certificate, the party may, with his/her resident identity card or a valid identity document specified in paragraphs 2 to 4 of Article 8 of these Regulations, apply to the marriage registration authority for handling.  
Where the marriage registration authority verifies the parties' marriage registration archives and confirms the truth, the authority shall reissue the marriage certificate or divorce certificate to the party.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　婚姻登记机关及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法依规给予处分：  
（一）为不符合婚姻登记条件的当事人办理婚姻登记的；  
（二）违反规定泄露或者向他人非法提供婚姻登记工作中知悉的个人隐私或者个人信息的；  
（三）玩忽职守造成婚姻登记档案损毁、灭失的；  
（四）办理婚姻登记收取费用的；  
（五）其他违反本条例规定的行为。  
违反前款第四项规定收取的费用，应当退还当事人。

**Article 22** Where a marriage registration authority or its staff member commits any of the following acts, the leading persons responsible and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with laws and regulations:  
(1) handling marriage registration for a party who does not meet the conditions for marriage registration;  
(2) violating provisions by disclosing or illegally providing personal privacy or personal information learned during marriage registration work to others;  
(3) being derelict of duty resulting in damage or loss of marriage registration archives;  
(4) charging fees for handling marriage registration;  
(5) other acts violating the provisions of these Regulations.  
Any fees charged in violation of item (4) of the preceding paragraph shall be refunded to the party.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人应当对所出具证件和书面材料的真实性、合法性负责，出具虚假证件或者书面材料的，应当承担相应法律责任，相关信息按照国家有关规定记入信用记录，并纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 23** A party shall be responsible for the authenticity and legality of the certificates and written materials produced; if the party produces false certificates or written materials, the party shall bear corresponding legal liability, and the relevant information shall be recorded in the credit record in accordance with relevant state regulations and incorporated into the national credit information sharing platform.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　中华人民共和国驻外使（领）馆可以依照本条例的有关规定，为男女双方均居住于驻在国的中国公民办理婚姻登记。

**Article 25** The embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China abroad may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, handle marriage registration for Chinese citizens both of whom reside in the country where the embassy or consulate is located.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　男女双方均非内地居民的中国公民在内地办理婚姻登记的具体办法，由国务院民政部门另行制定。

**Article 26** The specific measures for Chinese citizens, neither of whom is a mainland resident, to handle marriage registration in the mainland shall be separately formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例规定的婚姻登记证由国务院民政部门规定式样并监制。

**Article 27** The marriage registration certificates specified in these Regulations shall have their styles prescribed and be manufactured under the supervision of the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自2025年5月10日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force on May 10, 2025.
