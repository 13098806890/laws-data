### 最高人民法院关于未成年的养子女，其养父在国外死亡后回生母处生活，仍有权继承其养父的遗产的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于未成年的养子女，其养父在国外死亡后  
  
回生母处生活，仍有权继承其养父的遗产的批复  
  
（1986）民他字第22号  
  
福建省高级人民法院：  
  
你院一九八五年十二月十日《关于泉州市戴玉芳与戴文良析产、继承上诉案中黄钦辉有无继承权的请示报告》收悉。  
  
据报告称，戴文化、戴文良兄弟二人于一九二九年至一九三一年先后从菲律宾回国在泉州市新街四十一号建楼房一座，由其父母戴淑和、林英蕊等人居住。一九四二年戴母林英蕊收黄钦辉为戴文化的养子。黄钦辉与祖母林英蕊共同生活，由其养父戴文化从国外寄给生活费和教育费，直至一九五五年戴文化在国外去世。当时黄钦辉尚未成年，后因生活无来源于一九五七年回到生母处。一九八○年黄钦辉向法院提起诉讼，要求继承其养父戴文化新街四十一号楼房遗产。  
  
经我们研究认为：黄钦辉于一九四二年被戴文化之母林英蕊收养为戴文化的养子，直至一九五五年戴文化去世，在长达十三年的时间里，其生活费和教育费一直由戴文化供给。这一收养关系戴文化生前及其亲属、当地基层组织和群众都承认，应依法予以保护。  
  
关于黄钦辉是否自动解除收养关系或放弃继承权的问题，黄钦辉因养父戴文化一九五五年在国外去世，当时本人尚未成年，在无人供给生活费，又无其他经济来源的情况下，不得不于一九五七年回到生母处生活，对此不能认为黄钦辉自动解除了收养关系。黄钦辉在继承开始和遗产处理前，没有明确表示放弃继承，应当依法准许其继承戴文化的遗产。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年五月十九日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Official Reply Concerning Whether a Minor Adopted Child, After the Adoptive Father's Death Abroad, Who Returns to Live with the Biological Mother, Still Has the Right to Inherit the Adoptive Father's Estate  
Civil Communication No. 22 (1986)  
  
Fujian Higher People's Court:  
  
We have received your Request for Instructions dated December 10, 1985, titled "Report Requesting Instructions on Whether Huang Qinhui Has the Right of Succession in the Case of Dai Yufang v. Dai Wenliang Concerning Property Division and Succession Appeal in Quanzhou."  
  
According to the report, the two brothers Dai Wenhua and Dai Wenliang successively returned from the Philippines to China between 1929 and 1931 and built a building at No. 41 Xinjie, Quanzhou, which was inhabited by their parents Dai Shuhe and Lin Yingrui and others. In 1942, Lin Yingrui, the mother of Dai Wenhua, adopted Huang Qinhui as the adopted son of Dai Wenhua. Huang Qinhui lived together with his grandmother Lin Yingrui, and his living expenses and education fees were remitted from abroad by his adoptive father Dai Wenhua until Dai Wenhua died abroad in 1955. At that time, Huang Qinhui was still a minor. Later, due to having no source of livelihood, he returned to his biological mother in 1957. In 1980, Huang Qinhui filed a lawsuit with the court, claiming inheritance of the building estate at No. 41 Xinjie from his adoptive father Dai Wenhua.  
  
Upon our study, we are of the opinion that: Huang Qinhui was adopted by Dai Wenhua's mother Lin Yingrui as the adopted son of Dai Wenhua in 1942, and until Dai Wenhua's death in 1955, for a period of thirteen years, his living expenses and education fees were continuously provided by Dai Wenhua. This adoptive relationship was acknowledged by Dai Wenhua during his lifetime, as well as by his relatives, local grassroots organizations, and the public, and shall be protected according to law.  
  
As to whether Huang Qinhui automatically terminated the adoptive relationship or waived the right of inheritance: Huang Qinhui, because his adoptive father Dai Wenhua died abroad in 1955, was still a minor at that time. Under circumstances where no one provided living expenses and he had no other economic resources, he had no choice but to return to his biological mother in 1957 to live. This shall not be deemed as Huang Qinhui automatically terminating the adoptive relationship. Before the commencement of succession and the disposition of the estate, Huang Qinhui did not expressly indicate the waiver of inheritance. Therefore, he shall be permitted to inherit the estate of Dai Wenhua according to law.  
  
This is our reply.  
  
May 19, 1986
