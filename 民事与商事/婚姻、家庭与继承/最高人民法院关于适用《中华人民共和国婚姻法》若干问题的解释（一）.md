# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕30号  
**公布日期**：2001-12-24  
**生效日期**：2001-12-27

> （2001年12月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1202次会议通过，自2001年12月27日起施行） 法释〔2001〕30号 为了正确审理婚姻家庭纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国婚姻法》（以下简称婚姻法）、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，对人民法院适用婚姻法的有关问题作出如下解释： 第一条 婚姻法第三条、第三十二条、第四十三条、第四十五条、第四十六条所称的“家庭暴力”，是指行为人以殴打、捆绑、残害、强行限制人身自由或者其他手段，给其家庭成员的身体、精神等方面造成一定伤害后果的行为。持续性、经常性的家庭暴力，构成虐待。 第二条 婚姻法第三条、第三十二条、第四十六条规定的“有配偶者与他人同居”的情形，是指有配偶者与婚外异性，不以夫妻名义，持续、稳定地共同居住。 第三条 当事人仅以婚姻法第四条为依据提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。 第四条 男女双方根据婚姻法第八条规定补办结婚登记的，婚姻关系的效力从双方均符合婚姻法所规定的结婚的实质要件时起算。 第五条 未按婚姻法第八条规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，起诉到人民法院要求离婚的，应当区别对待： （一）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以前，男女双方已经符合结婚实质要件的，按事实婚姻处理； （二）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以后，男女双方符合结婚实质要件的，人民法院应当告知其在案件受理前补办结婚登记；未补办结婚登记的，按解除同居关系处理。 第六条 未按婚姻法第八条规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，一方死亡，另一方以配偶身份主张享有继承权的，按照本解释第五条的原则处理。 第七条 有权依据婚姻法第十条规定向人民法院就已办理结婚登记的婚姻申请宣告婚姻无效的主体，包括婚姻当事人及利害关系人。利害关系人包括： （一）以重婚为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为当事人的近亲属及基层组织。 （二）以未到法定婚龄为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为未达法定婚龄者的近亲属。 （三）以有禁止结婚的亲属关系为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为当事人的近亲属。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 婚姻法第三条、第三十二条、第四十三条、第四十五条、第四十六条所称的“家庭暴力”，是指行为人以殴打、捆绑、残害、强行限制人身自由或者其他手段，给其家庭成员的身体、精神等方面造成一定伤害后果的行为。持续性、经常性的家庭暴力，构成虐待。

**Article 1** The term 'domestic violence' as used in Articles 3, 32, 43, 45 and 46 of the Marriage Law means conduct that causes certain harmful consequences to the body, spirit, etc. of a family member by means of beating, binding, injuring, forcibly restricting personal freedom, or other means. Persistent and frequent domestic violence constitutes maltreatment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 婚姻法第三条、第三十二条、第四十六条规定的“有配偶者与他人同居”的情形，是指有配偶者与婚外异性，不以夫妻名义，持续、稳定地共同居住。

**Article 2** The circumstance of 'a person who has a spouse cohabits with another person' as provided in Articles 3, 32 and 46 of the Marriage Law means that a person who has a spouse cohabits with a person of the opposite sex other than the spouse in a continuous and stable manner without using the name of husband and wife.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 当事人仅以婚姻法第四条为依据提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 3** Where a party files a lawsuit solely on the basis of Article 4 of the Marriage Law, the People's Court shall not accept it; if it has already been accepted, it shall rule to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 男女双方根据婚姻法第八条规定补办结婚登记的，婚姻关系的效力从双方均符合婚姻法所规定的结婚的实质要件时起算。

**Article 4** Where a man and a woman retroactively register their marriage in accordance with Article 8 of the Marriage Law, the validity of the marital relationship shall be calculated from the time when both parties meet the substantive requirements for marriage as provided in the Marriage Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 未按婚姻法第八条规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，起诉到人民法院要求离婚的，应当区别对待：  
（一）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以前，男女双方已经符合结婚实质要件的，按事实婚姻处理；  
（二）1994年2月1日民政部《婚姻登记管理条例》公布实施以后，男女双方符合结婚实质要件的，人民法院应当告知其在案件受理前补办结婚登记；未补办结婚登记的，按解除同居关系处理。

**Article 5** Where a man and a woman who have not undergone marriage registration in accordance with Article 8 of the Marriage Law but live together in the name of husband and wife, and they file a lawsuit with the People's Court demanding divorce, the following distinctions shall be made:  
(1) If both parties already meet the substantive requirements for marriage before the promulgation and implementation of the Ministry of Civil Affairs' "Regulations on Marriage Registration Administration" on February 1, 1994, the case shall be handled as a de facto marriage;  
(2) If both parties meet the substantive requirements for marriage after the promulgation and implementation of the "Regulations on Marriage Registration Administration" on February 1, 1994, the People's Court shall inform them to retroactively register their marriage before the case is accepted; if they fail to do so, the case shall be handled as dissolution of cohabitation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 未按婚姻法第八条规定办理结婚登记而以夫妻名义共同生活的男女，一方死亡，另一方以配偶身份主张享有继承权的，按照[本解释第五条](#art-5)的原则处理。

**Article 6** Where a man and a woman who have not undergone marriage registration in accordance with Article 8 of the Marriage Law but live together in the name of husband and wife, and one party dies, the other party claims the right to inheritance as a spouse, the case shall be handled in accordance with the principle set forth in Article 5 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 有权依据婚姻法第十条规定向人民法院就已办理结婚登记的婚姻申请宣告婚姻无效的主体，包括婚姻当事人及利害关系人。利害关系人包括：  
（一）以重婚为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为当事人的近亲属及基层组织。  
（二）以未到法定婚龄为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为未达法定婚龄者的近亲属。  
（三）以有禁止结婚的亲属关系为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为当事人的近亲属。  
（四）以婚前患有医学上认为不应当结婚的疾病，婚后尚未治愈为由申请宣告婚姻无效的，为与患病者共同生活的近亲属。

**Article 7** The subjects entitled to apply to the People's Court for a declaration of invalidity of a marriage that has been registered pursuant to Article 10 of the Marriage Law include the parties to the marriage and interested persons. Interested persons include:  
(1) For an application for declaration of invalidity of marriage on the ground of bigamy, the near relatives of the parties and the grassroots organization;  
(2) For an application for declaration of invalidity of marriage on the ground of failure to reach the statutory marriage age, the near relatives of the person who has not reached the statutory marriage age;  
(3) For an application for declaration of invalidity of marriage on the ground of the existence of a prohibited kinship relation, the near relatives of the parties;  
(4) For an application for declaration of invalidity of marriage on the ground that one party suffers from a disease which is medically considered as not suitable for marriage before marriage and has not been cured after marriage, the near relatives who live together with the sick person.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 当事人依据婚姻法第十条规定向人民法院申请宣告婚姻无效的，申请时，法定的无效婚姻情形已经消失的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** Where a party applies to the People's Court for a declaration of invalidity of marriage in accordance with Article 10 of the Marriage Law, and at the time of application, the statutory circumstances for invalidity of marriage have already disappeared, the People's Court shall not uphold the application.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院审理宣告婚姻无效案件，对婚姻效力的审理不适用调解，应当依法作出判决；有关婚姻效力的判决一经作出，即发生法律效力。  
涉及财产分割和子女抚养的，可以调解。调解达成协议的，另行制作调解书。对财产分割和子女抚养问题的判决不服的，当事人可以上诉。

**Article 9** When the People's Court hears a case on declaration of invalidity of marriage, mediation shall not apply to the trial of the validity of the marriage, and a judgment shall be made according to law; once a judgment concerning the validity of the marriage is made, it shall take immediate legal effect.  
Where property division and child custody are involved, mediation may be conducted. If an agreement is reached through mediation, a separate mediation document shall be prepared. If a party is dissatisfied with the judgment on property division and child custody issues, the party may appeal.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 婚姻法第十一条所称的“胁迫”，是指行为人以给另一方当事人或者其近亲属的生命、身体健康、名誉、财产等方面造成损害为要挟，迫使另一方当事人违背真实意愿结婚的情况。  
因受胁迫而请求撤销婚姻的，只能是受胁迫一方的婚姻关系当事人本人。

**Article 10** The term 'duress' as used in Article 11 of the Marriage Law refers to the situation where an actor, by threatening to cause damage to the life, physical health, reputation, property, etc. of the other party or his or her near relatives, compels the other party to marry against his or her true will.  
A request for rescission of marriage due to duress may only be made by the party to the marital relationship who is subjected to the duress.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院审理婚姻当事人因受胁迫而请求撤销婚姻的案件，应当适用简易程序或者普通程序。

**Article 11** When the People's Court hears a case where a party to a marriage requests the rescission of marriage due to duress, it shall apply the summary procedure or the ordinary procedure.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 婚姻法第十一条规定的“一年”，不适用诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长的规定。

**Article 12** The 'one year' as provided in Article 11 of the Marriage Law shall not be subject to the provisions on suspension, interruption, or extension of the limitation of action.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 婚姻法第十二条所规定的自始无效，是指无效或者可撤销婚姻在依法被宣告无效或被撤销时，才确定该婚姻自始不受法律保护。

**Article 13** The 'invalid ab initio' as provided in Article 12 of the Marriage Law means that a void or voidable marriage is only determined to be not protected by law from the beginning when it is declared void or rescinded according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 人民法院根据当事人的申请，依法宣告婚姻无效或者撤销婚姻的，应当收缴双方的结婚证书并将生效的判决书寄送当地婚姻登记管理机关。

**Article 14** Where the People's Court declares a marriage void or rescinds a marriage according to law upon the application of a party, it shall confiscate the marriage certificates of both parties and send the effective judgment to the local marriage registration administration authority.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 被宣告无效或被撤销的婚姻，当事人同居期间所得的财产，按共同共有处理。但有证据证明为当事人一方所有的除外。

**Article 15** For a marriage that is declared void or rescinded, the property acquired during the cohabitation period of the parties shall be treated as jointly owned, unless there is evidence to prove that it belongs to one party alone.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 人民法院审理重婚导致的无效婚姻案件时，涉及财产处理的，应当准许合法婚姻当事人作为有独立请求权的第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 16** When the People's Court hears a case of void marriage caused by bigamy involving property disposition, it shall permit the party to the lawful marriage to participate in the proceedings as a third party with independent claims.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 婚姻法第十七条关于“夫妻对共同所有的财产，有平等的处理权 ”的规定，应当理解为：  
（一）夫或妻在处理夫妻共同财产上的权利是平等的。因日常生活需要而处理夫妻共同财产的，任何一方均有权决定。  
（二）夫或妻非因日常生活需要对夫妻共同财产做重要处理决定，夫妻双方应当平等协商，取得一致意见。他人有理由相信其为夫妻双方共同意思表示的，另一方不得以不同意或不知道为由对抗善意第三人。

**Article 17** The provision in Article 17 of the Marriage Law that 'the husband and wife have equal right to handle the jointly owned property' shall be understood as follows:  
(1) The rights of the husband or wife in handling the jointly owned property of the husband and wife are equal. For handling jointly owned property due to daily living needs, either party has the right to decide.  
(2) Where the husband or wife makes an important decision on handling the jointly owned property not due to daily living needs, the husband and wife shall consult on an equal footing and reach a consensus. Where a third party has reason to believe that it is the common intention of both husband and wife, the other party may not oppose the bona fide third party on the grounds of disagreement or ignorance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 婚姻法第十九条所称“第三人知道该约定的”，夫妻一方对此负有举证责任。

**Article 18** Where Article 19 of the Marriage Law states 'the third party knows of such agreement', the husband or wife shall bear the burden of adducing evidence to prove it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 婚姻法第十八条规定为夫妻一方所有的财产，不因婚姻关系的延续而转化为夫妻共同财产。但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 19** Property that is owned by one spouse as provided in Article 18 of the Marriage Law shall not be converted into jointly owned property of the husband and wife due to the continuation of the marital relationship, unless the parties have agreed otherwise.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 婚姻法第二十一条规定的“不能独立生活的子女”，是指尚在校接受高中及其以下学历教育，或者丧失或未完全丧失劳动能力等非因主观原因而无法维持正常生活的成年子女。

**Article 20** 'Children who cannot live independently' as provided in Article 21 of the Marriage Law refers to adult children who are still receiving education at the level of high school or below, or who have lost or not completely lost the ability to work and cannot maintain a normal life due to non-subjective reasons.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 婚姻法第二十一条所称“抚养费”，包括子女生活费、教育费、医疗费等费用。

**Article 21** The term 'child support' as used in Article 21 of the Marriage Law includes expenses such as children's living expenses, education expenses, and medical expenses.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 人民法院审理离婚案件，符合第三十二条第二款规定“应准予离婚”情形的，不应当因当事人有过错而判决不准离婚。

**Article 22** When the People's Court hears a divorce case that meets the circumstance of 'divorce shall be granted' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 32, it shall not rule to disallow the divorce on the ground that the party is at fault.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 婚姻法第三十三条所称的“军人一方有重大过错”，可以依据婚姻法第三十二条第三款前三项规定及军人有其他重大过错导致夫妻感情破裂的情形予以判断。

**Article 23** The term 'one spouse as a serviceman has gross fault' as used in Article 33 of the Marriage Law may be determined in accordance with the first three items of paragraph 3 of Article 32 of the Marriage Law and the circumstance where the serviceman has other gross faults causing the breakdown of the husband-wife relationship.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 人民法院作出的生效的离婚判决中未涉及探望权，当事人就探望权问题单独提起诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 24** Where an effective divorce judgment made by the People's Court does not involve the right of visitation, and a party separately files a lawsuit on the issue of visitation, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 当事人在履行生效判决、裁定或者调解书的过程中，请求中止行使探望权的，人民法院在征询双方当事人意见后，认为需要中止行使探望权的，依法作出裁定。中止探望的情形消失后，人民法院应当根据当事人的申请通知其恢复探望权的行使。

**Article 25** Where a party requests suspension of the exercise of the right of visitation during the enforcement of an effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document, the People's Court, after hearing the opinions of both parties, shall make a ruling in accordance with law if it considers that the right of visitation needs to be suspended. After the circumstances for suspension of visitation have disappeared, the People's Court shall, upon the application of the party, notify the party to resume the exercise of the right of visitation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 未成年子女、直接抚养子女的父或母及其他对未成年子女负担抚养、教育义务的法定监护人，有权向人民法院提出中止探望权的请求。

**Article 26** The minor child, the father or mother who directly supports the child, and other legal guardians who bear the obligation to support and educate the minor child have the right to request the People's Court to suspend the right of visitation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 婚姻法第四十二条所称“一方生活困难”，是指依靠个人财产和离婚时分得的财产无法维持当地基本生活水平。  
一方离婚后没有住处的，属于生活困难。  
离婚时，一方以个人财产中的住房对生活困难者进行帮助的形式，可以是房屋的居住权或者房屋的所有权。

**Article 27** The term 'one party has living difficulties' as used in Article 42 of the Marriage Law means that the party cannot maintain the local basic living standard by relying on his or her personal property and the property obtained at the time of divorce.  
If a party has no residence after divorce, it shall be considered as living difficulties.  
At the time of divorce, the form of assistance provided by one party from his or her personal property, such as housing, to the party with living difficulties may be the right of use of the house or the ownership of the house.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 婚姻法第四十六条规定的“损害赔偿”，包括物质损害赔偿和精神损害赔偿。涉及精神损害赔偿的，适用最高人民法院《关于确定民事侵权精神损害赔偿责任若干问题的解释》的有关规定。

**Article 28** The 'damages' as provided in Article 46 of the Marriage Law include compensation for material damage and compensation for mental damage. Where compensation for mental damage is involved, the relevant provisions of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation on Several Issues Concerning the Determination of Compensation Liability for Mental Damage in Civil Torts shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 承担婚姻法第四十六条规定的损害赔偿责任的主体，为离婚诉讼当事人中无过错方的配偶。  
人民法院判决不准离婚的案件，对于当事人基于婚姻法第四十六条提出的损害赔偿请求，不予支持。  
在婚姻关系存续期间，当事人不起诉离婚而单独依据该条规定提起损害赔偿请求的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 29** The subject bearing the liability for damages as provided in Article 46 of the Marriage Law shall be the spouse of the non-fault party in the divorce proceedings.  
For a case where the People's Court decides not to grant divorce, a claim for damages raised by a party based on Article 46 of the Marriage Law shall not be upheld.  
During the continuation of the marital relationship, if a party does not file for divorce but separately raises a claim for damages based on the said Article, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 人民法院受理离婚案件时，应当将婚姻法第四十六条等规定中当事人的有关权利义务，书面告知当事人。在适用婚姻法第四十六条时，应当区分以下不同情况：  
（一）符合婚姻法第四十六条规定的无过错方作为原告基于该条规定向人民法院提起损害赔偿请求的，必须在离婚诉讼的同时提出。  
（二）符合婚姻法第四十六条规定的无过错方作为被告的离婚诉讼案件，如果被告不同意离婚也不基于该条规定提起损害赔偿请求的，可以在离婚后一年内就此单独提起诉讼。  
（三）无过错方作为被告的离婚诉讼案件，一审时被告未基于婚姻法第四十六条规定提出损害赔偿请求，二审期间提出的，人民法院应当进行调解，调解不成的，告知当事人在离婚后一年内另行起诉。

**Article 30** When the People's Court accepts a divorce case, it shall inform the parties in writing of the relevant rights and obligations of the parties under Article 46 and other provisions of the Marriage Law. When applying Article 46 of the Marriage Law, the following different circumstances shall be distinguished:  
(1) Where the non-fault party meeting the requirements of Article 46 of the Marriage Law, as the plaintiff, files a claim for damages with the People's Court based on the said Article, the claim must be raised simultaneously with the divorce lawsuit.  
(2) In a divorce lawsuit where the non-fault party meeting the requirements of Article 46 of the Marriage Law is the defendant, if the defendant neither agrees to the divorce nor raises a claim for damages based on the said Article, the defendant may separately file a lawsuit on this matter within one year after the divorce.  
(3) In a divorce lawsuit where the non-fault party is the defendant, if the defendant does not raise a claim for damages based on Article 46 of the Marriage Law at the first instance, but raises it during the second instance, the People's Court shall conduct mediation; if mediation fails, the party shall be informed to separately file a lawsuit within one year after the divorce.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 当事人依据婚姻法第四十七条的规定向人民法院提起诉讼，请求再次分割夫妻共同财产的诉讼时效为两年，从当事人发现之次日起计算。

**Article 31** The limitation of action for a party to file a lawsuit with the People's Court in accordance with Article 47 of the Marriage Law to request a further division of jointly owned property of the husband and wife shall be two years, calculated from the day after the party discovers it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 婚姻法第四十八条关于对拒不执行有关探望子女等判决和裁定的，由人民法院依法强制执行的规定，是指对拒不履行协助另一方行使探望权的有关个人和单位采取拘留、罚款等强制措施，不能对子女的人身、探望行为进行强制执行。

**Article 32** The provision in Article 48 of the Marriage Law that the People's Court shall enforce compulsory execution in accordance with law against those who refuse to execute judgments and rulings concerning visitation with children, etc., means that compulsory measures such as detention and fine shall be taken against relevant individuals and units that refuse to perform the obligation to assist the other party in exercising the right of visitation, but the personal body of the child and the act of visitation shall not be subject to compulsory execution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 婚姻法修改后正在审理的一、二审婚姻家庭纠纷案件，一律适用修改后的婚姻法。此前最高人民法院作出的相关司法解释如与本解释相抵触，以本解释为准。

**Article 33** For marriage and family dispute cases that are being heard at first instance or second instance after the amendment of the Marriage Law, the amended Marriage Law shall apply in all cases. If any relevant judicial interpretation previously made by the Supreme People's Court conflicts with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 本解释自公布之日起施行。

**Article 34** This Interpretation shall come into force on the date of its promulgation.
