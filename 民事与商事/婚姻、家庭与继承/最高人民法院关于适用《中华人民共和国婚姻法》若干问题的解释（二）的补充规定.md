# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》若干问题的解释（二）的补充规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕6号  
**公布日期**：2017-02-20  
**生效日期**：2017-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国婚姻法〉若干问题的解释（二）的补充规定》已于2017年2月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1710次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年3月1日起施行。 2017年2月28日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》若干问题的解释（二）的补充规定 法释〔2017〕6号 在最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国婚姻法〉若干问题的解释（二）》第二十四条的基础上增加两款，分别作为该条第二款和第三款： 夫妻一方与第三人串通，虚构债务，第三人主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。 夫妻一方在从事赌博、吸毒等违法犯罪活动中所负债务，第三人主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。 附件：最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国婚姻法》若干问题的解释(二)

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人起诉请求解除同居关系的，人民法院不予受理。但当事人请求解除的同居关系，属于婚姻法第三条、第三十二条、第四十六条规定的“有配偶者与他人同居”的，人民法院应当受理并依法予以解除。  
当事人因同居期间财产分割或者子女抚养纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 1** Where a party initiates a prosecution requesting the dissolution of a cohabitation relationship, the People's Court shall not accept the case. However, if the cohabitation relationship requested by the party to be dissolved falls under the circumstance of "a person having a spouse cohabiting with another person" as prescribed in Articles 3, 32, and 46 of the Marriage Law, the People's Court shall accept the case and dissolve the relationship according to law.  
Where a party initiates a prosecution due to a dispute over property division or child custody during the period of cohabitation, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院受理申请宣告婚姻无效案件后，经审查确属无效婚姻的，应当依法作出宣告婚姻无效的判决。原告申请撤诉的，不予准许。

**Article 2** After the People's Court accepts a case applying for a declaration of invalidity of a marriage, if upon examination it is confirmed to be an invalid marriage, the People's Court shall render a judgment declaring the marriage invalid according to law. If the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the prosecution, it shall not be permitted.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院受理离婚案件后，经审查确属无效婚姻的，应当将婚姻无效的情形告知当事人，并依法作出宣告婚姻无效的判决。

**Article 3** After the People's Court accepts a divorce case, if upon examination it is confirmed to be an invalid marriage, the People's Court shall inform the parties of the circumstance of the invalidity of the marriage and render a judgment declaring the marriage invalid according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院审理无效婚姻案件，涉及财产分割和子女抚养的，应当对婚姻效力的认定和其他纠纷的处理分别制作裁判文书。

**Article 4** Where the People's Court tries a case concerning an invalid marriage involving property division and child custody, it shall separately prepare adjudicative documents for the determination of the validity of the marriage and the handling of other disputes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　夫妻一方或者双方死亡后一年内，生存一方或者利害关系人依据婚姻法第十条的规定申请宣告婚姻无效的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 5** Within one year after the death of one or both spouses, if the surviving spouse or an interested party applies for a declaration of invalidity of the marriage pursuant to Article 10 of the Marriage Law, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　利害关系人依据婚姻法第十条的规定，申请人民法院宣告婚姻无效的，利害关系人为申请人，婚姻关系当事人双方为被申请人。  
夫妻一方死亡的，生存一方为被申请人。  
夫妻双方均已死亡的，不列被申请人。

**Article 6** Where an interested party applies to the People's Court for a declaration of invalidity of a marriage pursuant to Article 10 of the Marriage Law, the interested party shall be the applicant, and both parties to the marital relationship shall be the respondents.  
Where one spouse dies, the surviving spouse shall be the respondent.  
Where both spouses have died, no respondent shall be listed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院就同一婚姻关系分别受理了离婚和申请宣告婚姻无效案件的，对于离婚案件的审理，应当待申请宣告婚姻无效案件作出判决后进行。  
前款所指的婚姻关系被宣告无效后，涉及财产分割和子女抚养的，应当继续审理。

**Article 7** Where the People's Court has separately accepted both a divorce case and a case applying for a declaration of invalidity of the same marital relationship, the trial of the divorce case shall be conducted after a judgment is rendered in the case applying for a declaration of invalidity of the marriage.  
After the marital relationship referred to in the preceding paragraph is declared invalid, if property division and child custody are involved, the trial shall continue.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　离婚协议中关于财产分割的条款或者当事人因离婚就财产分割达成的协议，对男女双方具有法律约束力。  
当事人因履行上述财产分割协议发生纠纷提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 8** The clauses on property division in a divorce agreement or the agreement reached by the parties on property division due to divorce shall have legal binding force on both the man and the woman.  
Where a party initiates a prosecution due to a dispute arising from the performance of the aforesaid property division agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　男女双方协议离婚后一年内就财产分割问题反悔，请求变更或者撤销财产分割协议的，人民法院应当受理。  
人民法院审理后，未发现订立财产分割协议时存在欺诈、胁迫等情形的，应当依法驳回当事人的诉讼请求。

**Article 9** Where within one year after the man and the woman divorce by agreement, a party repents on the issue of property division and requests modification or rescission of the property division agreement, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
After trial, if the People's Court finds no circumstances such as fraud or duress existed at the time of concluding the property division agreement, it shall reject the party's claim according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人请求返还按照习俗给付的彩礼的，如果查明属于以下情形，人民法院应当予以支持：  
（一）双方未办理结婚登记手续的；  
（二）双方办理结婚登记手续但确未共同生活的；  
（三）婚前给付并导致给付人生活困难的。  
适用前款第（二）、（三）项的规定，应当以双方离婚为条件。

**Article 10** Where a party requests the return of a bride price given in accordance with customs, if it is ascertained that one of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) The parties have not gone through the marriage registration procedures;  
(2) The parties have gone through the marriage registration procedures but have not actually lived together;  
(3) The bride price was given before marriage and causes the giver to live in difficulty.  
The application of the provisions of items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be subject to the condition that the parties divorce.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　婚姻关系存续期间，下列财产属于婚姻法第十七条规定的“其他应当归共同所有的财产”：  
（一）一方以个人财产投资取得的收益；  
（二）男女双方实际取得或者应当取得的住房补贴、住房公积金；  
（三）男女双方实际取得或者应当取得的养老保险金、破产安置补偿费。

**Article 11** During the existence of the marital relationship, the following property shall belong to the "other property that shall be owned in common" as prescribed in Article 17 of the Marriage Law:  
(1) Income derived from investment of one party's individual property;  
(2) Housing subsidies and housing provident funds actually obtained or shall be obtained by the man or the woman;  
(3) Pension insurance funds and bankruptcy resettlement compensation fees actually obtained or shall be obtained by the man or the woman.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　婚姻法第十七条第三项规定的“知识产权的收益”，是指婚姻关系存续期间，实际取得或者已经明确可以取得的财产性收益。

**Article 12** The "income from intellectual property" as prescribed in Article 17(3) of the Marriage Law refers to the property income actually obtained or clearly obtainable during the existence of the marital relationship.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军人的伤亡保险金、伤残补助金、医药生活补助费属于个人财产。

**Article 13** The death and injury insurance benefits, disability subsidies, and medical and living subsidies of a military serviceman shall be individual property.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割发放到军人名下的复员费、自主择业费等一次性费用的，以夫妻婚姻关系存续年限乘以年平均值，所得数额为夫妻共同财产。  
前款所称年平均值，是指将发放到军人名下的上述费用总额按具体年限均分得出的数额。其具体年限为人均寿命七十岁与军人入伍时实际年龄的差额。

**Article 14** Where the People's Court tries a divorce case involving the division of one-time expenses such as demobilization fees and voluntary job-selection fees paid to a military serviceman, the amount obtained by multiplying the number of years of the marital relationship by the average annual amount shall be community property of the husband and wife.  
The "average annual amount" referred to in the preceding paragraph means the amount obtained by equally dividing the total amount of the aforesaid expenses paid to the military serviceman over a specific number of years. The specific number of years shall be the difference between the average life expectancy of seventy years and the actual age of the military serviceman at the time of enlistment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　夫妻双方分割共同财产中的股票、债券、投资基金份额等有价证券以及未上市股份有限公司股份时，协商不成或者按市价分配有困难的，人民法院可以根据数量按比例分配。

**Article 15** Where the husband and wife divide stocks, bonds, investment fund units and other negotiable securities, as well as shares of an unlisted joint stock limited company, among community property, if negotiation fails or distribution at market price is difficult, the People's Court may distribute them proportionally according to quantity.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割夫妻共同财产中以一方名义在有限责任公司的出资额，另一方不是该公司股东的，按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）夫妻双方协商一致将出资额部分或者全部转让给该股东的配偶，过半数股东同意、其他股东明确表示放弃优先购买权的，该股东的配偶可以成为该公司股东；  
（二）夫妻双方就出资额转让份额和转让价格等事项协商一致后，过半数股东不同意转让，但愿意以同等价格购买该出资额的，人民法院可以对转让出资所得财产进行分割。过半数股东不同意转让，也不愿意以同等价格购买该出资额的，视为其同意转让，该股东的配偶可以成为该公司股东。  
用于证明前款规定的过半数股东同意的证据，可以是股东会决议，也可以是当事人通过其他合法途径取得的股东的书面声明材料。

**Article 16** Where the People's Court tries a divorce case involving the division of a capital contribution in a limited liability company made in the name of one spouse from community property of the husband and wife, and the other spouse is not a shareholder of the company, the case shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the husband and wife reach a consensus on transferring part or all of the capital contribution to the spouse of the shareholder, and a majority of the other shareholders consent and the other shareholders explicitly waive their right of first refusal, the spouse of the shareholder may become a shareholder of the company;  
(2) After the husband and wife reach a consensus on matters such as the transfer portion and transfer price of the capital contribution, if a majority of the other shareholders do not consent to the transfer but are willing to purchase the capital contribution at the same price, the People's Court may divide the property obtained from the transfer of the capital contribution. If a majority of the other shareholders neither consent to the transfer nor are willing to purchase the capital contribution at the same price, they shall be deemed to have consented to the transfer, and the spouse of the shareholder may become a shareholder of the company.  
The evidence used to prove the consent of the majority of the other shareholders as prescribed in the preceding paragraph may be the resolution of the shareholders' meeting or written declaration materials of the shareholders obtained by the parties through other lawful means.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院审理离婚案件，涉及分割夫妻共同财产中以一方名义在合伙企业中的出资,另一方不是该企业合伙人的，当夫妻双方协商一致，将其合伙企业中的财产份额全部或者部分转让给对方时，按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）其他合伙人一致同意的，该配偶依法取得合伙人地位；  
（二）其他合伙人不同意转让，在同等条件下行使优先受让权的，可以对转让所得的财产进行分割；  
（三）其他合伙人不同意转让，也不行使优先受让权，但同意该合伙人退伙或者退还部分财产份额的，可以对退还的财产进行分割；  
（四）其他合伙人既不同意转让，也不行使优先受让权，又不同意该合伙人退伙或者退还部分财产份额的，视为全体合伙人同意转让，该配偶依法取得合伙人地位。

**Article 17** Where the People's Court tries a divorce case involving the division of a capital contribution in a partnership enterprise made in the name of one spouse from community property of the husband and wife, and the other spouse is not a partner of the enterprise, if the husband and wife reach a consensus on transferring all or part of the property share in the partnership enterprise to the other spouse, the case shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the other partners unanimously consent, the spouse shall obtain the status of a partner according to law;  
(2) If the other partners do not consent to the transfer but exercise the right of first refusal under the same conditions, the property obtained from the transfer may be divided;  
(3) If the other partners do not consent to the transfer and do not exercise the right of first refusal, but consent to the partner's withdrawal from the partnership or refund of part of the property share, the refunded property may be divided;  
(4) If the other partners neither consent to the transfer nor exercise the right of first refusal, nor consent to the partner's withdrawal from the partnership or refund of part of the property share, all partners shall be deemed to have consented to the transfer, and the spouse shall obtain the status of a partner according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　夫妻以一方名义投资设立独资企业的，人民法院分割夫妻在该独资企业中的共同财产时，应当按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）一方主张经营该企业的，对企业资产进行评估后，由取得企业一方给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（二）双方均主张经营该企业的，在双方竞价基础上，由取得企业的一方给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（三）双方均不愿意经营该企业的，按照《中华人民共和国个人独资企业法》等有关规定办理。

**Article 18** Where the husband and wife invest to establish a wholly individually-owned enterprise in the name of one spouse, when the People's Court divides the community property of the husband and wife in such enterprise, it shall handle the case separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If one spouse claims to operate the enterprise, after the enterprise assets are assessed, the spouse obtaining the enterprise shall provide corresponding compensation to the other spouse;  
(2) If both spouses claim to operate the enterprise, on the basis of bidding between the two spouses, the spouse obtaining the enterprise shall provide corresponding compensation to the other spouse;  
(3) If neither spouse is willing to operate the enterprise, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Wholly Individually-Owned Enterprises and other regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　由一方婚前承租、婚后用共同财产购买的房屋，房屋权属证书登记在一方名下的，应当认定为夫妻共同财产。

**Article 19** Where a house is leased by one spouse before marriage and purchased with community property after marriage, with the title certificate registered under one spouse's name, it shall be recognized as community property of the husband and wife.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　双方对夫妻共同财产中的房屋价值及归属无法达成协议时，人民法院按以下情形分别处理：  
（一）双方均主张房屋所有权并且同意竞价取得的，应当准许；  
（二）一方主张房屋所有权的，由评估机构按市场价格对房屋作出评估，取得房屋所有权的一方应当给予另一方相应的补偿；  
（三）双方均不主张房屋所有权的，根据当事人的申请拍卖房屋，就所得价款进行分割。

**Article 20** Where the two parties cannot reach an agreement on the value and ownership of a house that is community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court shall handle the case separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If both parties claim ownership of the house and agree to acquire it through bidding, it shall be permitted;  
(2) If one party claims ownership of the house, the appraisal institution shall appraise the house at market price, and the party obtaining ownership of the house shall provide corresponding compensation to the other party;  
(3) If neither party claims ownership of the house, the house shall be auctioned upon the party's application, and the proceeds obtained shall be divided.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　离婚时双方对尚未取得所有权或者尚未取得完全所有权的房屋有争议且协商不成的，人民法院不宜判决房屋所有权的归属，应当根据实际情况判决由当事人使用。  
当事人就前款规定的房屋取得完全所有权后，有争议的，可以另行向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 21** Where at the time of divorce, the two parties have a dispute over a house for which ownership has not yet been obtained or full ownership has not yet been obtained, and negotiation fails, the People's Court shall not render a judgment on the attribution of ownership of the house, but shall render a judgment on the use of the house by the parties based on the actual circumstances.  
After a party obtains full ownership of the house referred to in the preceding paragraph, if a dispute arises, the party may separately initiate a prosecution with the People's Court.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人结婚前，父母为双方购置房屋出资的，该出资应当认定为对自己子女的个人赠与，但父母明确表示赠与双方的除外。  
当事人结婚后，父母为双方购置房屋出资的，该出资应当认定为对夫妻双方的赠与，但父母明确表示赠与一方的除外。

**Article 22** Before the parties marry, if the parents contribute funds for the purchase of a house for the two parties, such contribution shall be recognized as a personal gift to their own child, unless the parents explicitly indicate that the gift is made to both parties.  
After the parties marry, if the parents contribute funds for the purchase of a house for the two parties, such contribution shall be recognized as a gift to both the husband and wife, unless the parents explicitly indicate that the gift is made to one party.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　债权人就一方婚前所负个人债务向债务人的配偶主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。但债权人能够证明所负债务用于婚后家庭共同生活的除外。

**Article 23** Where a creditor asserts a claim against the spouse of the debtor for a personal debt incurred by one party before marriage, the People's Court shall not support the claim. However, this does not apply if the creditor can prove that the debt incurred was used for the common family life after marriage.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　债权人就婚姻关系存续期间夫妻一方以个人名义所负债务主张权利的，应当按夫妻共同债务处理。但夫妻一方能够证明债权人与债务人明确约定为个人债务，或者能够证明属于婚姻法第十九条第三款规定情形的除外。  
夫妻一方与第三人串通，虚构债务，第三人主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。  
夫妻一方在从事赌博、吸毒等违法犯罪活动中所负债务，第三人主张权利的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** Where a creditor asserts a claim for a debt incurred by one spouse in his or her own name during the existence of the marital relationship, the debt shall be treated as a joint debt of the husband and wife. However, this does not apply if the spouse can prove that the creditor and the debtor explicitly agreed that the debt was a personal debt, or can prove that the circumstances fall under the provisions of Article 19(3) of the Marriage Law.  
Where one spouse colludes with a third party to fabricate a debt, and the third party asserts a claim, the People's Court shall not support the claim.  
Where one spouse incurs a debt in illegal or criminal activities such as gambling or drug abuse, and a third party asserts a claim, the People's Court shall not support the claim.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人的离婚协议或者人民法院的判决书、裁定书、调解书已经对夫妻财产分割问题作出处理的，债权人仍有权就夫妻共同债务向男女双方主张权利。  
一方就共同债务承担连带清偿责任后，基于离婚协议或者人民法院的法律文书向另一方主张追偿的，人民法院应当支持。

**Article 25** Where the parties' divorce agreement or the People's Court's judgment, ruling, or mediation document has already dealt with the issue of division of marital property, the creditor shall still have the right to assert a claim against both the man and the woman for the joint debt of the husband and wife.  
After one party has borne joint and several liability for the joint debt, if that party claims recourse against the other party based on the divorce agreement or the legal document of the People's Court, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　夫或妻一方死亡的，生存一方应当对婚姻关系存续期间的共同债务承担连带清偿责任。

**Article 26** Where one spouse dies, the surviving spouse shall bear joint and several liability for the joint debts incurred during the existence of the marital relationship.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　当事人在婚姻登记机关办理离婚登记手续后，以婚姻法第四十六条规定为由向人民法院提出损害赔偿请求的，人民法院应当受理。但当事人在协议离婚时已经明确表示放弃该项请求，或者在办理离婚登记手续一年后提出的，不予支持。

**Article 27** After a party completes the divorce registration formalities at the marriage registration authority, if the party files a claim for damages with the People's Court on the grounds provided in Article 46 of the Marriage Law, the People's Court shall accept the case. However, if the party has explicitly waived such claim at the time of the divorce agreement, or if the claim is filed more than one year after completing the divorce registration formalities, the claim shall not be supported.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　夫妻一方申请对配偶的个人财产或者夫妻共同财产采取保全措施的，人民法院可以在采取保全措施可能造成损失的范围内，根据实际情况，确定合理的财产担保数额。

**Article 28** Where one spouse applies for preservation measures against the other spouse's individual property or community property of the husband and wife, the People's Court may, within the scope of losses that may be caused by the preservation measures, determine a reasonable amount of property security based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本解释自2004年4月1日起施行。  
本解释施行后，人民法院新受理的一审婚姻家庭纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
本解释施行后，此前最高人民法院作出的相关司法解释与本解释相抵触的，以本解释为准。

**Article 29** This Interpretation shall come into force as of April 1, 2004.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, the newly accepted first-instance cases of marital and family disputes by the People's Courts shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, if any relevant judicial interpretations previously made by the Supreme People's Court conflict with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
