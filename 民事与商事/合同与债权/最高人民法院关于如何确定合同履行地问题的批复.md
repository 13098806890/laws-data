### 最高人民法院关于如何确定合同履行地问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于如何确定合同履行地问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1988〕20号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院〔87〕豫法经字第 11号关于对最高人民法院（经）复〔985〕39号如何确定合同履行地的批复的意见收悉。经研究，现对该批复补充如下：  
  
民法通则第七十二条规定，按照合同取得财产的，财产所有权从财产交付时起转移，法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。根据民法通则的上述规定，合同标的为实物的，标的物交付的地点，就是产品所有权转移的地点，也就是合同履行地。但工矿产品购销合同或者农副产品购销合同，除供需双方在合同中有特殊约定外，按照上述原则确定合同履行地时，因交货方式不同应有所不同：  
  
（一）凡按照合同规定实行代运制，即由供方代需方向承运部门办理货物托运手续，运杂费由需方负担的，产品发运地为合同履行地。按国家规定的木材、煤炭送货办法送货的，也是一种代运制，应以产品发运地为合同履行地。  
  
（二）凡合同规定实行送货制，但又不属于国家规定送货办法范围的，即由供方自备运输工具送货或者由供方将货物交承运部门运送给需方，运杂费由供方负担的，产品送达地为合同履行地。  
  
（三）凡合同规定由需方自提的，合同标的物在产品提货地交付，提货地为合同履行地。  
  
此复  
  
1988年4月22日

**Article 1** Reply on the Issue of How to Determine the Place of Contract Performance  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1988] No. 20  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
We have received your court's document [87] Yu Fa Jing Zi No. 11, requesting an opinion on the Reply of the Supreme People's Court (Jing) Fu [985] No. 39 concerning the determination of the place of contract performance. Upon study, we now supplement that reply as follows:  
Article 72 of the General Principles of the Civil Law provides that, where property is acquired under a contract, the ownership of the property shall be transferred at the time when the property is delivered, unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed by the parties. According to the aforesaid provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law, where the subject matter of the contract is physical goods, the place of delivery of the subject matter is the place where the ownership of the product is transferred, which is also the place of contract performance. However, for purchase and sale contracts of industrial and mineral products or agricultural and sideline products, unless otherwise specially agreed by the supply and demand parties in the contract, when determining the place of contract performance in accordance with the above principles, it shall differ depending on the mode of delivery, as follows:  
(1) Where the contract provides for the consignment system (dai yun zhi), i.e., the supplier handles the consignment procedures on behalf of the demander with the carrier, and the freight and miscellaneous expenses are borne by the demander, the place of dispatch of the product shall be the place of contract performance. Where delivery is made in accordance with the state-prescribed methods for delivering timber and coal, this also constitutes a consignment system, and the place of dispatch of the product shall be the place of contract performance.  
(2) Where the contract provides for a delivery system (song huo zhi), but not within the scope of the state-prescribed delivery methods, i.e., the supplier delivers by its own means of transport or delivers the goods to the carrier for transportation to the demander, and the freight and miscellaneous expenses are borne by the supplier, the place of arrival of the product shall be the place of contract performance.  
(3) Where the contract provides that the demander shall pick up the goods by itself, the subject matter of the contract shall be delivered at the place of pickup, and the place of pickup shall be the place of contract performance.  
Hereby this reply.  
April 22, 1988
