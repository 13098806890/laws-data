# 最高人民法院关于审理建设工程施工合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕25号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 第1825次会议通过，自2021年1月1日起施行） 为正确审理建设工程施工合同纠纷案件，依法保护当事人合法权益，维护建筑市场秩序，促进建筑市场健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国建筑法》《中华人民共和国招标投标法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　建设工程施工合同具有下列情形之一的，应当依据民法典第一百五十三条第一款的规定，认定无效： （一）承包人未取得建筑业企业资质或者超越资质等级的； （二）没有资质的实际施工人借用有资质的建筑施工企业名义的； （三）建设工程必须进行招标而未招标或者中标无效的。 承包人因转包、违法分包建设工程与他人签订的建设工程施工合同，应当依据民法典第一百五十三条第一款及第七百九十一条第二款、第三款的规定，认定无效。 第二条　招标人和中标人另行签订的建设工程施工合同约定的工程范围、建设工期、工程质量、工程价款等实质性内容，与中标合同不一致，一方当事人请求按照中标合同确定权利义务的，人民法院应予支持。 招标人和中标人在中标合同之外就明显高于市场价格购买承建房产、无偿建设住房配套设施、让利、向建设单位捐赠财物等另行签订合同，变相降低工程价款，一方当事人以该合同背离中标合同实质性内容为由请求确认无效的，人民法院应予支持。 第三条　当事人以发包人未取得建设工程规划许可证等规划审批手续为由，请求确认建设工程施工合同无效的，人民法院应予支持，但发包人在起诉前取得建设工程规划许可证等规划审批手续的除外。 发包人能够办理审批手续而未办理，并以未办理审批手续为由请求确认建设工程施工合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。 第四条　承包人超越资质等级许可的业务范围签订建设工程施工合同，在建设工程竣工前取得相应资质等级，当事人请求按照无效合同处理的，人民法院不予支持。 第五条　具有劳务作业法定资质的承包人与总承包人、分包人签订的劳务分包合同，当事人请求确认无效的，人民法院依法不予支持。 第六条　建设工程施工合同无效，一方当事人请求对方赔偿损失的，应当就对方过错、损失大小、过错与损失之间的因果关系承担举证责任。 损失大小无法确定，一方当事人请求参照合同约定的质量标准、建设工期、工程价款支付时间等内容确定损失大小的，人民法院可以结合双方过错程度、过错与损失之间的因果关系等因素作出裁判。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　建设工程施工合同具有下列情形之一的，应当依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)第一款的规定，认定无效：  
（一）承包人未取得建筑业企业资质或者超越资质等级的；  
（二）没有资质的实际施工人借用有资质的建筑施工企业名义的；  
（三）建设工程必须进行招标而未招标或者中标无效的。  
承包人因转包、违法分包建设工程与他人签订的建设工程施工合同，应当依据[民法典第一百五十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-153)第一款及第七百九十一条第二款、第三款的规定，认定无效。

**Article 1** Where a construction project construction contract falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined void in accordance with the provisions of Article 153, paragraph 1 of the Civil Code:  
(1) The contractor has not obtained the qualification of a construction enterprise or exceeds the qualification grade;  
(2) An unqualified actual constructor borrows the name of a qualified construction enterprise;  
(3) The construction project must be subject to tender but has not been tendered or the tender is void.  
A construction project construction contract entered into by the contractor with others due to subcontracting or illegal subcontracting of the construction project shall be determined void in accordance with the provisions of Article 153, paragraph 1 and Article 791, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　招标人和中标人另行签订的建设工程施工合同约定的工程范围、建设工期、工程质量、工程价款等实质性内容，与中标合同不一致，一方当事人请求按照中标合同确定权利义务的，人民法院应予支持。  
招标人和中标人在中标合同之外就明显高于市场价格购买承建房产、无偿建设住房配套设施、让利、向建设单位捐赠财物等另行签订合同，变相降低工程价款，一方当事人以该合同背离中标合同实质性内容为由请求确认无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where the substantive contents such as the project scope, construction period, project quality, and project price agreed in a construction project construction contract separately concluded by the tenderee and the winning bidder are inconsistent with the winning contract, and a party requests to determine the rights and obligations in accordance with the winning contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
Where the tenderee and the winning bidder separately conclude a contract outside the winning contract, such as purchasing the constructed property at a price obviously higher than the market price, constructing housing supporting facilities without compensation, making concessions, or donating property to the construction unit, thereby indirectly reducing the project price, and a party requests confirmation of the invalidity of the contract on the ground that it deviates from the substantive contents of the winning contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人以发包人未取得建设工程规划许可证等规划审批手续为由，请求确认建设工程施工合同无效的，人民法院应予支持，但发包人在起诉前取得建设工程规划许可证等规划审批手续的除外。  
发包人能够办理审批手续而未办理，并以未办理审批手续为由请求确认建设工程施工合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where a party requests confirmation of the invalidity of a construction project construction contract on the ground that the developer has not obtained the planning approval procedures such as the construction project planning permit, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except that the developer has obtained the planning approval procedures such as the construction project planning permit before the prosecution.  
Where the developer is able to go through the approval procedures but fails to do so, and requests confirmation of the invalidity of the construction project construction contract on the ground of not having gone through the approval procedures, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　承包人超越资质等级许可的业务范围签订建设工程施工合同，在建设工程竣工前取得相应资质等级，当事人请求按照无效合同处理的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** Where the contractor enters into a construction project construction contract beyond the business scope permitted by its qualification grade, and obtains the corresponding qualification grade before the completion of the construction project, and a party requests treatment as a void contract, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　具有劳务作业法定资质的承包人与总承包人、分包人签订的劳务分包合同，当事人请求确认无效的，人民法院依法不予支持。

**Article 5** Where a party requests confirmation of the invalidity of a labor subcontract contract entered into by a contractor with statutory qualification for labor work and the general contractor or subcontractor, the People's Court shall not uphold such request according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　建设工程施工合同无效，一方当事人请求对方赔偿损失的，应当就对方过错、损失大小、过错与损失之间的因果关系承担举证责任。  
损失大小无法确定，一方当事人请求参照合同约定的质量标准、建设工期、工程价款支付时间等内容确定损失大小的，人民法院可以结合双方过错程度、过错与损失之间的因果关系等因素作出裁判。

**Article 6** Where a construction project construction contract is void, and one party requests the other party to compensate for losses, the requesting party shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the other party's fault, the amount of loss, and the causal relationship between the fault and the loss.  
If the amount of loss cannot be determined, and one party requests to determine the amount of loss by reference to the contractually agreed contents such as the quality standard, construction period, and time for payment of project price, the People's Court may make a judgment by taking into account factors such as the degree of fault of both parties and the causal relationship between the fault and the loss.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　缺乏资质的单位或者个人借用有资质的建筑施工企业名义签订建设工程施工合同，发包人请求出借方与借用方对建设工程质量不合格等因出借资质造成的损失承担连带赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a unit or individual lacking qualification enters into a construction project construction contract by borrowing the name of a qualified construction enterprise, and the developer requests the lender and the borrower to bear joint and several compensation liability for losses caused by the lending of qualification, such as unqualified construction project quality, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人对建设工程开工日期有争议的，人民法院应当分别按照以下情形予以认定：  
（一）开工日期为发包人或者监理人发出的开工通知载明的开工日期；开工通知发出后，尚不具备开工条件的，以开工条件具备的时间为开工日期；因承包人原因导致开工时间推迟的，以开工通知载明的时间为开工日期。  
（二）承包人经发包人同意已经实际进场施工的，以实际进场施工时间为开工日期。  
（三）发包人或者监理人未发出开工通知，亦无相关证据证明实际开工日期的，应当综合考虑开工报告、合同、施工许可证、竣工验收报告或者竣工验收备案表等载明的时间，并结合是否具备开工条件的事实，认定开工日期。

**Article 8** Where the parties dispute the actual commencement date of a construction project, the People's Court shall determine it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) The commencement date shall be the date of commencement indicated in the notice of commencement issued by the developer or the supervisor; where the conditions for commencement are not yet satisfied after the notice is issued, the date when the conditions are satisfied shall be the commencement date; where the commencement is delayed due to the contractor's reasons, the date indicated in the notice of commencement shall be the commencement date.  
(2) Where the contractor has actually commenced construction with the consent of the developer, the actual time of commencement of construction shall be the commencement date.  
(3) Where the developer or the supervisor has not issued a notice of commencement, and there is no relevant evidence to prove the actual commencement date, the commencement date shall be determined by comprehensively considering the times indicated in the commencement report, contract, construction permit, completion acceptance report, or completion acceptance record form, and in combination with the fact of whether the conditions for commencement are satisfied.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人对建设工程实际竣工日期有争议的，人民法院应当分别按照以下情形予以认定：  
（一）建设工程经竣工验收合格的，以竣工验收合格之日为竣工日期；  
（二）承包人已经提交竣工验收报告，发包人拖延验收的，以承包人提交验收报告之日为竣工日期；  
（三）建设工程未经竣工验收，发包人擅自使用的，以转移占有建设工程之日为竣工日期。

**Article 9** Where the parties dispute the actual completion date of a construction project, the People's Court shall determine it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the construction project passes the completion acceptance, the date of passing the completion acceptance shall be the completion date;  
(2) Where the contractor has submitted a completion acceptance report and the developer delays the acceptance, the date on which the contractor submits the report shall be the completion date;  
(3) Where the construction project is used without authorization by the developer before completion acceptance, the date of transfer of possession of the construction project shall be the completion date.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人约定顺延工期应当经发包人或者监理人签证等方式确认，承包人虽未取得工期顺延的确认，但能够证明在合同约定的期限内向发包人或者监理人申请过工期顺延且顺延事由符合合同约定，承包人以此为由主张工期顺延的，人民法院应予支持。  
当事人约定承包人未在约定期限内提出工期顺延申请视为工期不顺延的，按照约定处理，但发包人在约定期限后同意工期顺延或者承包人提出合理抗辩的除外。

**Article 10** Where the parties agree that an extension of the construction period shall be confirmed by means such as a visa from the developer or the supervisor, and the contractor, although not having obtained confirmation of the extension, can prove that it applied for an extension within the contractually agreed period and the reason for extension complies with the contract, and the contractor claims an extension on this ground, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where the parties agree that the failure of the contractor to apply for an extension within the agreed period shall be deemed as no extension, such agreement shall be followed, except that the developer agrees to the extension after the agreed period or the contractor raises a reasonable defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设工程竣工前，当事人对工程质量发生争议，工程质量经鉴定合格的，鉴定期间为顺延工期期间。

**Article 11** Where the parties dispute the project quality before the completion of the construction project, and the project quality is determined to be qualified through expert evaluation, the period of expert evaluation shall be the period for extension of the construction period.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因承包人的原因造成建设工程质量不符合约定，承包人拒绝修理、返工或者改建，发包人请求减少支付工程价款的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where the project quality fails to conform to the contract due to the contractor's reasons, and the contractor refuses to repair, rework, or reconstruct, and the developer requests a reduction in the payment of the project price, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　发包人具有下列情形之一，造成建设工程质量缺陷，应当承担过错责任：  
（一）提供的设计有缺陷；  
（二）提供或者指定购买的建筑材料、建筑构配件、设备不符合强制性标准；  
（三）直接指定分包人分包专业工程。  
承包人有过错的，也应当承担相应的过错责任。

**Article 13** Where the developer falls under any of the following circumstances, causing defects in the quality of the construction project, it shall bear the liability for fault:  
(1) The design provided is defective;  
(2) The building materials, building components, and equipment provided or designated for purchase do not comply with mandatory standards;  
(3) Directly appoints a subcontractor to subcontract specialized works.  
Where the contractor is also at fault, it shall bear the corresponding liability for fault.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设工程未经竣工验收，发包人擅自使用后，又以使用部分质量不符合约定为由主张权利的，人民法院不予支持；但是承包人应当在建设工程的合理使用寿命内对地基基础工程和主体结构质量承担民事责任。

**Article 14** Where, after the construction project has not undergone completion acceptance, the developer uses it without authorization and then claims rights on the ground that the quality of the used part does not conform to the contract, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim; however, the contractor shall bear civil liability for the quality of the foundation works and the main structure within the reasonable service life of the construction project.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　因建设工程质量发生争议的，发包人可以以总承包人、分包人和实际施工人为共同被告提起诉讼。

**Article 15** Where a dispute arises over the quality of a construction project, the developer may initiate a civil action with the general contractor, subcontractor, and actual constructor as joint defendants.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　发包人在承包人提起的建设工程施工合同纠纷案件中，以建设工程质量不符合合同约定或者法律规定为由，就承包人支付违约金或者赔偿修理、返工、改建的合理费用等损失提出反诉的，人民法院可以合并审理。

**Article 16** Where, in a construction project construction contract dispute case brought by the contractor, the developer files a counterclaim on the ground that the construction project quality does not conform to the contract or legal provisions, claiming that the contractor pays liquidated damages or compensates for losses such as the reasonable costs of repair, rework, or reconstruction, the People's Court may consolidate the hearing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有下列情形之一，承包人请求发包人返还工程质量保证金的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）当事人约定的工程质量保证金返还期限届满；  
（二）当事人未约定工程质量保证金返还期限的，自建设工程通过竣工验收之日起满二年；  
（三）因发包人原因建设工程未按约定期限进行竣工验收的，自承包人提交工程竣工验收报告九十日后当事人约定的工程质量保证金返还期限届满；当事人未约定工程质量保证金返还期限的，自承包人提交工程竣工验收报告九十日后起满二年。  
发包人返还工程质量保证金后，不影响承包人根据合同约定或者法律规定履行工程保修义务。

**Article 17** Where any of the following circumstances exists, and the contractor requests the developer to return the project quality guarantee, the People's Court shall uphold such request:  
(1) The agreed period for return of the project quality guarantee has expired;  
(2) Where the parties have not agreed on the period for return of the project quality guarantee, two years have elapsed from the date of passing the completion acceptance;  
(3) Where, due to the developer's reasons, the construction project has not undergone completion acceptance within the agreed period, the agreed period for return of the project quality guarantee expires ninety days after the contractor submits the completion acceptance report; where the parties have not agreed on the period for return of the project quality guarantee, two years have elapsed from ninety days after the contractor submits the completion acceptance report.  
After the developer returns the project quality guarantee, it shall not affect the contractor's obligation to perform the project warranty in accordance with the contract or legal provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　因保修人未及时履行保修义务，导致建筑物毁损或者造成人身损害、财产损失的，保修人应当承担赔偿责任。  
保修人与建筑物所有人或者发包人对建筑物毁损均有过错的，各自承担相应的责任。

**Article 18** Where the warrantor fails to timely perform its warranty obligations, resulting in damage to the building or causing personal injury or property loss, the warrantor shall bear compensation liability.  
Where both the warrantor and the building owner or the developer are at fault for the damage to the building, each shall bear its corresponding liability.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人对建设工程的计价标准或者计价方法有约定的，按照约定结算工程价款。  
因设计变更导致建设工程的工程量或者质量标准发生变化，当事人对该部分工程价款不能协商一致的，可以参照签订建设工程施工合同时当地建设行政主管部门发布的计价方法或者计价标准结算工程价款。  
建设工程施工合同有效，但建设工程经竣工验收不合格的，依照[民法典第五百七十七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-577)规定处理。

**Article 19** Where the parties have agreed on the pricing standard or pricing method for the construction project, the project price shall be settled according to the agreement.  
Where a design change leads to a change in the quantity or quality standard of the construction project, and the parties cannot reach a consensus on the project price for that part, they may settle the project price by reference to the pricing method or pricing standard published by the local construction administrative department at the time of signing the construction project construction contract.  
If the construction project construction contract is valid but the construction project fails to pass the completion acceptance, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 577 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人对工程量有争议的，按照施工过程中形成的签证等书面文件确认。承包人能够证明发包人同意其施工，但未能提供签证文件证明工程量发生的，可以按照当事人提供的其他证据确认实际发生的工程量。

**Article 20** Where the parties dispute the quantity of works, it shall be confirmed based on written documents such as visas formed during the construction process. Where the contractor can prove that the developer agreed to its construction but fails to provide visa documents to prove the occurrence of the quantity of works, the actual quantity of works that occurred may be confirmed based on other evidence provided by the parties.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人约定，发包人收到竣工结算文件后，在约定期限内不予答复，视为认可竣工结算文件的，按照约定处理。承包人请求按照竣工结算文件结算工程价款的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** Where the parties agree that if the developer fails to respond within the agreed period after receiving the completion settlement documents, it shall be deemed that the completion settlement documents are accepted, such agreement shall be followed. Where the contractor requests to settle the project price in accordance with the completion settlement documents, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人签订的建设工程施工合同与招标文件、投标文件、中标通知书载明的工程范围、建设工期、工程质量、工程价款不一致，一方当事人请求将招标文件、投标文件、中标通知书作为结算工程价款的依据的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 22** Where the construction project construction contract entered into by the parties is inconsistent with the project scope, construction period, project quality, and project price indicated in the tender documents, bid documents, and notice of award, and one party requests to use the tender documents, bid documents, and notice of award as the basis for settling the project price, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　发包人将依法不属于必须招标的建设工程进行招标后，与承包人另行订立的建设工程施工合同背离中标合同的实质性内容，当事人请求以中标合同作为结算建设工程价款依据的，人民法院应予支持，但发包人与承包人因客观情况发生了在招标投标时难以预见的变化而另行订立建设工程施工合同的除外。

**Article 23** Where the developer, after tendering a construction project that according to law is not required to be tendered, separately enters into a construction project construction contract with the contractor that deviates from the substantive contents of the winning contract, and a party requests to use the winning contract as the basis for settling the construction project price, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except that the developer and the contractor separately enter into a construction project construction contract due to objective circumstances that were difficult to foresee at the time of tendering.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人就同一建设工程订立的数份建设工程施工合同均无效，但建设工程质量合格，一方当事人请求参照实际履行的合同关于工程价款的约定折价补偿承包人的，人民法院应予支持。  
实际履行的合同难以确定，当事人请求参照最后签订的合同关于工程价款的约定折价补偿承包人的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 24** Where several construction project construction contracts entered into by the parties for the same construction project are all void, but the construction project quality is qualified, and one party requests to compensate the contractor at a discounted price by reference to the provisions on the project price in the contract actually performed, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
If the contract actually performed is difficult to determine, and the party requests to compensate the contractor at a discounted price by reference to the provisions on the project price in the last contract entered into, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人对垫资和垫资利息有约定，承包人请求按照约定返还垫资及其利息的，人民法院应予支持，但是约定的利息计算标准高于垫资时的同类贷款利率或者同期贷款市场报价利率的部分除外。  
当事人对垫资没有约定的，按照工程欠款处理。  
当事人对垫资利息没有约定，承包人请求支付利息的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 25** Where the parties have agreed on the advance payment and interest on the advance payment, and the contractor requests the return of the advance payment and its interest according to the agreement, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except for the part where the agreed interest calculation standard is higher than the similar loan interest rate or the contemporaneous loan market quoted rate at the time of the advance payment.  
Where the parties have no agreement on the advance payment, it shall be treated as arrears of project payment.  
Where the parties have no agreement on interest on the advance payment, and the contractor requests payment of interest, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人对欠付工程价款利息计付标准有约定的，按照约定处理。没有约定的，按照同期同类贷款利率或者同期贷款市场报价利率计息。

**Article 26** Where the parties have agreed on the standard for calculating interest on overdue project price, such agreement shall be followed. Where there is no agreement, interest shall be calculated according to the contemporaneous similar loan interest rate or the contemporaneous loan market quoted rate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　利息从应付工程价款之日开始计付。当事人对付款时间没有约定或者约定不明的，下列时间视为应付款时间：  
（一）建设工程已实际交付的，为交付之日；  
（二）建设工程没有交付的，为提交竣工结算文件之日；  
（三）建设工程未交付，工程价款也未结算的，为当事人起诉之日。

**Article 27** Interest shall be calculated from the date on which the project price becomes payable. Where the parties have no agreement or the agreement is unclear on the time for payment, the following times shall be deemed as the time for payment:  
(1) Where the construction project has been actually delivered, the date of delivery;  
(2) Where the construction project has not been delivered, the date of submission of the completion settlement documents;  
(3) Where the construction project has not been delivered and the project price has not been settled, the date on which the party initiates prosecution.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人约定按照固定价结算工程价款，一方当事人请求对建设工程造价进行鉴定的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** Where the parties agree to settle the project price according to a fixed price, and one party requests expert evaluation of the construction project cost, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　当事人在诉讼前已经对建设工程价款结算达成协议，诉讼中一方当事人申请对工程造价进行鉴定的，人民法院不予准许。

**Article 29** Where the parties have already reached an agreement on the settlement of the construction project price before the litigation, and during the litigation one party applies for expert evaluation of the project cost, the People's Court shall not grant permission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　当事人在诉讼前共同委托有关机构、人员对建设工程造价出具咨询意见，诉讼中一方当事人不认可该咨询意见申请鉴定的，人民法院应予准许，但双方当事人明确表示受该咨询意见约束的除外。

**Article 30** Where the parties jointly entrust relevant institutions or personnel to issue an advisory opinion on the construction project cost before the litigation, and during the litigation one party does not recognize the advisory opinion and applies for expert evaluation, the People's Court shall grant permission, except that both parties have expressly indicated that they are bound by the advisory opinion.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对部分案件事实有争议的，仅对有争议的事实进行鉴定，但争议事实范围不能确定，或者双方当事人请求对全部事实鉴定的除外。

**Article 31** Where the parties dispute part of the case facts, expert evaluation shall be conducted only on the disputed facts, except that the scope of the disputed facts cannot be determined or both parties request expert evaluation on all facts.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人对工程造价、质量、修复费用等专门性问题有争议，人民法院认为需要鉴定的，应当向负有举证责任的当事人释明。当事人经释明未申请鉴定，虽申请鉴定但未支付鉴定费用或者拒不提供相关材料的，应当承担举证不能的法律后果。  
一审诉讼中负有举证责任的当事人未申请鉴定，虽申请鉴定但未支付鉴定费用或者拒不提供相关材料，二审诉讼中申请鉴定，人民法院认为确有必要的，应当依照[民事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-170)第一款第三项的规定处理。

**Article 32** Where the parties dispute specialized issues such as the project cost, quality, and repair costs, and the People's Court considers that expert evaluation is necessary, it shall explain to the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence. Where the party, after explanation, fails to apply for expert evaluation, or although applying for expert evaluation fails to pay the evaluation fees or refuses to provide relevant materials, it shall bear the legal consequences of failure to adduce evidence.  
Where the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence in the first instance fails to apply for expert evaluation, or although applying for expert evaluation fails to pay the evaluation fees or refuses to provide relevant materials, and applies for expert evaluation in the second instance, if the People's Court considers it truly necessary, it shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 170, paragraph 1, subparagraph 3 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院准许当事人的鉴定申请后，应当根据当事人申请及查明案件事实的需要，确定委托鉴定的事项、范围、鉴定期限等，并组织当事人对争议的鉴定材料进行质证。

**Article 33** After granting the party's application for expert evaluation, the People's Court shall, based on the party's application and the need to ascertain the case facts, determine the matters, scope, and period for entrusting expert evaluation, and organize the parties to cross-examine the disputed evaluation materials.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民法院应当组织当事人对鉴定意见进行质证。鉴定人将当事人有争议且未经质证的材料作为鉴定依据的，人民法院应当组织当事人就该部分材料进行质证。经质证认为不能作为鉴定依据的，根据该材料作出的鉴定意见不得作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 34** The People's Court shall organize the parties to cross-examine the expert evaluation opinion. Where the expert evaluator uses materials disputed by the parties and not cross-examined as the basis for evaluation, the People's Court shall organize the parties to cross-examine that part of the materials. If after cross-examination it is determined that such materials cannot be used as the basis for evaluation, the evaluation opinion made based on such materials shall not be used as the basis for determining the case facts.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　与发包人订立建设工程施工合同的承包人，依据[民法典第八百零七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-807)的规定请求其承建工程的价款就工程折价或者拍卖的价款优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 35** Where a contractor that has entered into a construction project construction contract with the developer, pursuant to the provisions of Article 807 of the Civil Code, requests that the price of the project it constructs be given priority compensation from the discounted or auctioned proceeds of the project, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　承包人根据[民法典第八百零七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-807)规定享有的建设工程价款优先受偿权优于抵押权和其他债权。

**Article 36** The right to priority compensation for the construction project price enjoyed by the contractor in accordance with Article 807 of the Civil Code shall have priority over mortgage rights and other claims.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　装饰装修工程具备折价或者拍卖条件，装饰装修工程的承包人请求工程价款就该装饰装修工程折价或者拍卖的价款优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 37** Where a decoration and fit-out project meets the conditions for discount or auction, and the contractor of the decoration and fit-out project requests that the project price be given priority compensation from the discounted or auctioned proceeds of the decoration and fit-out project, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　建设工程质量合格，承包人请求其承建工程的价款就工程折价或者拍卖的价款优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 38** Where the construction project quality is qualified, and the contractor requests that the price of the project it constructs be given priority compensation from the discounted or auctioned proceeds of the project, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　未竣工的建设工程质量合格，承包人请求其承建工程的价款就其承建工程部分折价或者拍卖的价款优先受偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 39** Where an uncompleted construction project is of qualified quality, and the contractor requests that the price of the project it constructs be given priority compensation from the discounted or auctioned proceeds of the part it constructed, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　承包人建设工程价款优先受偿的范围依照国务院有关行政主管部门关于建设工程价款范围的规定确定。  
承包人就逾期支付建设工程价款的利息、违约金、损害赔偿金等主张优先受偿的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 40** The scope of the contractor's priority compensation for the construction project price shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the relevant administrative department of the State Council on the scope of the construction project price.  
Where the contractor claims priority compensation for interest, liquidated damages, damages, etc. arising from overdue payment of the construction project price, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　承包人应当在合理期限内行使建设工程价款优先受偿权，但最长不得超过十八个月，自发包人应当给付建设工程价款之日起算。

**Article 41** The contractor shall exercise the right to priority compensation for the construction project price within a reasonable period, but in no case shall it exceed eighteen months, calculated from the date on which the developer shall pay the construction project price.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　发包人与承包人约定放弃或者限制建设工程价款优先受偿权，损害建筑工人利益，发包人根据该约定主张承包人不享有建设工程价款优先受偿权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 42** Where the developer and the contractor agree to waive or restrict the right to priority compensation for the construction project price, harming the interests of construction workers, and the developer claims based on such agreement that the contractor does not enjoy the right to priority compensation for the construction project price, the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　实际施工人以转包人、违法分包人为被告起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
实际施工人以发包人为被告主张权利的，人民法院应当追加转包人或者违法分包人为本案第三人，在查明发包人欠付转包人或者违法分包人建设工程价款的数额后，判决发包人在欠付建设工程价款范围内对实际施工人承担责任。

**Article 43** Where an actual constructor initiates a prosecution against the subcontractor or illegal subcontractor as defendant, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where an actual constructor claims rights against the developer as defendant, the People's Court shall add the subcontractor or illegal subcontractor as a third party in the case, and after ascertaining the amount of the construction project price that the developer owes to the subcontractor or illegal subcontractor, it shall order the developer to bear liability within the scope of the overdue construction project price to the actual constructor.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　实际施工人依据[民法典第五百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-535)规定，以转包人或者违法分包人怠于向发包人行使到期债权或者与该债权有关的从权利，影响其到期债权实现，提起代位权诉讼的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 44** Where an actual constructor, in accordance with Article 535 of the Civil Code, initiates a subrogation action on the ground that the subcontractor or illegal subcontractor is negligent in exercising its due claims or ancillary rights related to such claims against the developer, affecting the realization of its own due claims, the People's Court shall uphold such action.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本解释自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 45** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
