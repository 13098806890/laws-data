# 最高人民法院关于购销合同履行地的特殊约定问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1990-08-19

---

### 正文

一、我院法（经）复〔1985〕39号、〔1988〕20号（分别见本刊1985年第3号、1988年第2号）批复中所称的供需双方特殊约定的合同履行地，是指当事人在购销合同中明确约定的交货地点，即在合同的交货地点或履行地点栏目中填写的地点，或者在合同的履行条款或其他条款中写明的交货地点（货物交接地、交付地点）。若当事人约定在某地安装调试或验收完毕才算交货的，该安装调试或验收地即为双方特殊约定的合同履行地。在合同的到货地（到达站、到达港、到站地）或验收地栏目中填写的地点，不应当视为当事人特殊约定的合同履行地。

**Article None** I. The place of contract performance specially agreed upon by the supplier and the demander referred to in the replies of our court Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 39 and [1988] No. 20 (respectively published in Issue No. 3 of 1985 and Issue No. 2 of 1988 of this journal) means the place of delivery expressly agreed upon by the parties in a sales contract, i.e., the place filled in the column for place of delivery or place of performance in the contract, or the place of delivery (place of goods handover, place of delivery) specified in the performance clause or other clauses of the contract. If the parties agree that delivery shall be completed only after installation, commissioning, or acceptance inspection at a certain place, such place of installation, commissioning, or acceptance inspection shall be the place of contract performance specially agreed upon by the parties. The place filled in the column for place of arrival (arrival station, arrival port, arrival destination) or place of acceptance inspection in the contract shall not be deemed as the place of contract performance specially agreed upon by the parties.

二、当事人在合同中明确约定的交货地点与合同其他条款的规定不一致的，以合同明确约定的交货地点为合同履行地。在供需双方已实际交接货物的情况下，若实际交接地点与合同原约定的交货地点不一致的，人民法院在按合同履行地实施管辖时，以货物的实际交接地视为合同履行地，但不影响在交货地点上违约一方应承担的相应责任。

**Article None** II. If the place of delivery expressly agreed upon by the parties in the contract is inconsistent with the provisions of other clauses of the contract, the place of delivery expressly agreed upon in the contract shall be the place of contract performance. Where the supplier and the demander have actually exchanged the goods, if the actual place of exchange is inconsistent with the place of delivery originally agreed upon in the contract, the People's Court, when exercising jurisdiction based on the place of contract performance, shall regard the actual place of goods exchange as the place of contract performance; however, this shall not affect the corresponding liability to be borne by the breaching party with respect to the place of delivery.

三、当事人在合同中没有明确约定交货地点的，仍按我院法（经）复〔1988〕20号批复的规定，区别不同的交货方式确定合同履行地。  
1990年8月19日

**Article None** III. If the parties have not expressly agreed upon the place of delivery in the contract, the place of contract performance shall still be determined by distinguishing different methods of delivery in accordance with the provisions of the reply of our court Fa (Jing) Fu [1988] No. 20.  
August 19, 1990
