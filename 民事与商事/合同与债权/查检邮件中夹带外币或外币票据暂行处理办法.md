# 查检邮件中夹带外币或外币票据暂行处理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：1950-09-01  
**生效日期**：1950-09-01

> (政务院批准　1950年9月1日中国人民银行、邮电部发布  自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为严格执行外汇管理，防止利用国内或国际邮件夹带外币或外币票据互相转让流通及私营外汇等情事，特制定本办法。 第二条　本办法所称外币指一切外国币券，所称外币票据系指各种以外币支付之汇票、支票、旅行支票、期票及其他付款凭证等。 第三条　本办法所指邮件之查检工作，应由各地海关或公安机关执行之；其查获外币及外币票据之存兑事宜，由中国人民银行总行授权各地中国银行办理之，无中国银行机构设立之地区则由人民银行办理之。 第四条　在本国境内互寄或寄往国外之邮件中，如夹带外币一经查获，应报由司法机关一律全部没收之。 第五条　在本国境内互寄或寄往国外之邮件中，如夹带外币票据一经查获，应由主管检查机关视其情节轻重，报由司法机关令其存兑，处以罚金或予以没收。 第六条　由国外寄至国内邮件中，如夹带外币或外币票据，一经发现，可由各该邮局暂时代为保管，俟与收件人联系后，持向中国银行换取外汇存单或按牌价兑换人民币，予以发还。 第七条　经主管检查机关查获应予没收之外币或外币票据，应交由各相关邮局，随时解送所在地之中国银行或人民银行，按牌价折合人民币，由中行或人行签发国库局抬头支票，由各该邮局送交当地国库换取收据，再由邮局将收据备函寄交寄件人(无中行或人行设立之地区，可由各相关邮局解送附近设有中行或人行地区之邮局代办解库手续，俟取得国库收据后，仍交由各相关邮局处理之)。 第八条　所有经主管检查机关查获应予没收外币或外币票据之相关邮件，应由各该邮局于邮件内加盖“内装已被主管机关查扣”戳记后，随时封发或投递，不得稽延积压，并由各该邮局通知寄件人。 第九条　凡中国银行或国内经人民银行指定经营外汇业务之银行，基于正当外汇业务所开国外付款之票据，或国外银行所开国内中国银行及指定银行之票据，可不受本办法第五条及第六条条文之限制。 第十条　凡查获应予没收之外币票据属于抬头人性质者，应由主管检查机关移送司法机关，勒令寄件人签妥空白背书或重开支票之手续。 第十一条　凡无中国银行或人民银行机构设立之边远或乡村地区，其持有外币或外币票据拟寄递至附近地区中国银行或人民银行存兑者，经当地区级以上政府证明后，可不受本办法第四条及第五条条文之限制。 第十二条　本办法自发布之日起实行。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为严格执行外汇管理，防止利用国内或国际邮件夹带外币或外币票据互相转让流通及私营外汇等情事，特制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for strict enforcement of foreign exchange control, to prevent incidents such as using domestic or international mail to smuggle foreign currency or foreign currency instruments for mutual transfer and circulation, and private foreign exchange dealings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称外币指一切外国币券，所称外币票据系指各种以外币支付之汇票、支票、旅行支票、期票及其他付款凭证等。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Measures, "foreign currency" means all foreign currency notes and coins, and "foreign currency instruments" means various types of drafts, checks, traveler's checks, promissory notes, and other payment instruments payable in foreign currency.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法所指邮件之查检工作，应由各地海关或公安机关执行之；其查获外币及外币票据之存兑事宜，由中国人民银行总行授权各地中国银行办理之，无中国银行机构设立之地区则由人民银行办理之。

**Article 3** The inspection of mail referred to in these Measures shall be carried out by local customs or public security organs; the deposit and exchange matters concerning the foreign currency and foreign currency instruments discovered shall be handled by local Bank of China offices authorized by the People's Bank of China, and in areas where no Bank of China office is established, by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在本国境内互寄或寄往国外之邮件中，如夹带外币一经查获，应报由司法机关一律全部没收之。

**Article 4** If foreign currency is found smuggled in mail exchanged within the territory of this country or sent abroad, once discovered, it shall be reported to the judicial authorities, and shall be confiscated in its entirety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在本国境内互寄或寄往国外之邮件中，如夹带外币票据一经查获，应由主管检查机关视其情节轻重，报由司法机关令其存兑，处以罚金或予以没收。

**Article 5** If foreign currency instruments are found smuggled in mail exchanged within the territory of this country or sent abroad, once discovered, the competent inspection authority shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, report the matter to the judicial authorities, which shall order the deposit and exchange, impose a fine, or confiscate the same.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　由国外寄至国内邮件中，如夹带外币或外币票据，一经发现，可由各该邮局暂时代为保管，俟与收件人联系后，持向中国银行换取外汇存单或按牌价兑换人民币，予以发还。

**Article 6** If foreign currency or foreign currency instruments are found smuggled in mail sent from abroad to within the country, once discovered, they may be temporarily held in custody by the relevant post office. After contacting the addressee, they shall be taken to the Bank of China to be exchanged for foreign exchange deposit certificates or converted into Renminbi at the official rate, and then returned.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经主管检查机关查获应予没收之外币或外币票据，应交由各相关邮局，随时解送所在地之中国银行或人民银行，按牌价折合人民币，由中行或人行签发国库局抬头支票，由各该邮局送交当地国库换取收据，再由邮局将收据备函寄交寄件人(无中行或人行设立之地区，可由各相关邮局解送附近设有中行或人行地区之邮局代办解库手续，俟取得国库收据后，仍交由各相关邮局处理之)。

**Article 7** Foreign currency or foreign currency instruments that shall be confiscated upon discovery by the competent inspection authority shall be handed over to the relevant post office, which shall promptly remit them to the local Bank of China or People's Bank of China for conversion into Renminbi at the official rate. The Bank of China or the People's Bank of China shall issue a check payable to the Bureau of the Treasury. The post office shall deliver the check to the local treasury to obtain a receipt, and then the post office shall send the receipt by letter to the sender. (In areas where no Bank of China or People's Bank of China is established, the relevant post office may remit the items to a post office in a nearby area where such a bank is established for handling the treasury deposit procedures. After obtaining the treasury receipt, it shall still be handled by the relevant post office.)

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　所有经主管检查机关查获应予没收外币或外币票据之相关邮件，应由各该邮局于邮件内加盖“内装已被主管机关查扣”戳记后，随时封发或投递，不得稽延积压，并由各该邮局通知寄件人。

**Article 8** For all mail in which foreign currency or foreign currency instruments subject to confiscation are discovered by the competent inspection authority, the relevant post office shall stamp the mail with a seal reading "Contents have been seized by the competent authority" before promptly dispatching or delivering the same without delay or backlog, and shall notify the sender thereof.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　凡中国银行或国内经人民银行指定经营外汇业务之银行，基于正当外汇业务所开国外付款之票据，或国外银行所开国内中国银行及指定银行之票据，可不受[本办法第五条](#art-5)及第六条条文之限制。

**Article 9** Any instrument payable abroad issued by the Bank of China or a domestic bank designated by the People's Bank of China to engage in foreign exchange business based on legitimate foreign exchange transactions, or an instrument issued by a foreign bank payable to the Bank of China or a designated bank in China, may be exempted from the restrictions under Articles 5 and 6 of these Measures.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　凡查获应予没收之外币票据属于抬头人性质者，应由主管检查机关移送司法机关，勒令寄件人签妥空白背书或重开支票之手续。

**Article 10** Where the foreign currency instruments discovered and subject to confiscation are of an order type, the competent inspection authority shall transfer the matter to the judicial authorities, which shall compel the sender to complete the procedures of signing a blank endorsement or reissuing a check.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　凡无中国银行或人民银行机构设立之边远或乡村地区，其持有外币或外币票据拟寄递至附近地区中国银行或人民银行存兑者，经当地区级以上政府证明后，可不受[本办法第四条](#art-4)及第五条条文之限制。

**Article 11** In remote or rural areas where no Bank of China or People's Bank of China office is established, if a person holds foreign currency or foreign currency instruments and intends to send them by mail to a nearby area for deposit and exchange at the Bank of China or the People's Bank of China, after obtaining certification from the local government at the district level or above, such person may be exempted from the restrictions under Articles 4 and 5 of these Measures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本办法自发布之日起实行。

**Article 12** These Measures shall come into effect as of the date of promulgation.
