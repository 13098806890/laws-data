### 最高人民法院关于审理中外合资经营合同纠纷案件如何清算合资企业问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于审理中外合资经营合同纠纷案件  
  
如何清算合资企业问题的批复  
  
法释〔1998〕1号  
  
（1997年12月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第 950次会议通过，自 1998年1月15日起施行）  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1996）鲁经初字第44号《关于审理中外合资经营合同纠纷案件如何清算合资企业问题的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
同意你院请示报告中的第一种意见，即中外合资经营企业一方当事人向人民法院提起诉讼，要求解散合营企业并追究对方违约责任的，人民法院仅应对合营合同效力、是否终止合营合同、违约责任等作出判决。合营企业清算问题则应根据《中华人民共和国中外合资经营企业法实施条例》、《外商投资企业清算办法》的有关规定办理，人民法院组织清算没有法律依据。  
  
国内有限责任公司有类似情形的，应依据《中华人民共和国公司法》的有关规定办理。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your court's Request for Instructions Report No. 44 (1996) Lu Jing Chu Zi No. 44 on the Issue of How to Liquidate a Joint Venture When Hearing a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture Contract Dispute Case has been received. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
We agree with the first opinion in your court's request for instructions report, that is, where one party to a Sino-foreign equity joint venture institutes a prosecution in the People's Court requesting dissolution of the joint venture and pursuit of liability for breach of contract against the other party, the People's Court shall only make a judgment on the validity of the joint venture contract, whether to terminate the joint venture contract, liability for breach of contract, etc. The issue of liquidation of the joint venture shall be handled pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Regulations for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Ventures and the Measures for the Liquidation of Foreign-invested Enterprises, and there is no legal basis for the People's Court to organize liquidation.  
For similar circumstances of domestic limited liability companies, the matter shall be handled pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.  
This is in reply.
