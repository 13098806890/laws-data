# 最高人民法院关于审理买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕8号  
**公布日期**：2012-05-10  
**生效日期**：2012-07-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》已于2012年3月31日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1545次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年7月1日起施行。 2012年5月10日 最高人民法院 关于审理买卖合同纠纷案件 适用法律问题的解释 法释〔2012〕8号 （2012年3月31日最高人民法院审判委员会第1545次会议通过　2012年5月10日最高人民法院公告公布　自2012年7月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人之间没有书面合同，一方以送货单、收货单、结算单、发票等主张存在买卖合同关系的，人民法院应当结合当事人之间的交易方式、交易习惯以及其他相关证据，对买卖合同是否成立作出认定。  
对账确认函、债权确认书等函件、凭证没有记载债权人名称，买卖合同当事人一方以此证明存在买卖合同关系的，人民法院应予支持，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 1** Where there is no written contract between the parties, and one party claims the existence of a sales contract relationship by virtue of delivery notes, receipt notes, settlement statements, invoices, etc., the People's Court shall, in light of the transaction method, transaction practices, and other relevant evidence between the parties, make a determination as to whether the sales contract is formed.  
Where a confirmation letter of account reconciliation, a debt confirmation letter, or other such letters or documents do not record the name of the creditor, and a party to the sales contract uses such documents to prove the existence of a sales contract relationship, the People's Court shall uphold it, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary to rebut it.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人签订认购书、订购书、预订书、意向书、备忘录等预约合同，约定在将来一定期限内订立买卖合同，一方不履行订立买卖合同的义务，对方请求其承担预约合同违约责任或者要求解除预约合同并主张损害赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** Where the parties sign a subscription agreement, order agreement, booking agreement, letter of intent, memorandum, or other preliminary contract, agreeing to conclude a sales contract within a certain period in the future, and one party fails to perform the obligation to conclude the sales contract, and the other party requests that it bear liability for breach of the preliminary contract or demands dissolution of the preliminary contract and claims damages, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人一方以出卖人在缔约时对标的物没有所有权或者处分权为由主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。  
出卖人因未取得所有权或者处分权致使标的物所有权不能转移，买受人要求出卖人承担违约责任或者要求解除合同并主张损害赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where one party claims that the contract is void on the ground that the seller had no ownership or right of disposal over the subject matter at the time of contracting, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
Where the seller's failure to obtain ownership or right of disposal results in the inability to transfer ownership of the subject matter, and the buyer demands that the seller bear liability for breach of contract or demands dissolution of the contract and claims damages, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院在按照合同法的规定认定电子交易合同的成立及效力的同时，还应当适用电子签名法的相关规定。  
二、标的物交付和所有权转移

**Article 4** When determining the formation and validity of an electronic transaction contract in accordance with the provisions of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall also apply the relevant provisions of the Electronic Signature Law.  
II. Delivery of Subject Matter and Transfer of Ownership

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　标的物为无需以有形载体交付的电子信息产品，当事人对交付方式约定不明确，且依照合同法第六十一条的规定仍不能确定的，买受人收到约定的电子信息产品或者权利凭证即为交付。

**Article 5** Where the subject matter is an electronic information product that does not need to be delivered by tangible medium, and the parties' agreement on the method of delivery is unclear, and it still cannot be determined in accordance with Article 61 of the Contract Law, the buyer's receipt of the agreed electronic information product or rights certificate shall constitute delivery.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　根据合同法第一百六十二条的规定，买受人拒绝接收多交部分标的物的，可以代为保管多交部分标的物。买受人主张出卖人负担代为保管期间的合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。  
买受人主张出卖人承担代为保管期间非因买受人故意或者重大过失造成的损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** In accordance with Article 162 of the Contract Law, where the buyer refuses to accept the excess part of the subject matter, it may keep the excess part of the subject matter on behalf of the seller. Where the buyer claims that the seller shall bear the reasonable expenses during the period of custody on behalf, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
Where the buyer claims that the seller shall bear the losses caused during the period of custody on behalf not due to the buyer's intentional act or gross negligence, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　合同法第一百三十六条规定的“提取标的物单证以外的有关单证和资料”，主要应当包括保险单、保修单、普通发票、增值税专用发票、产品合格证、质量保证书、质量鉴定书、品质检验证书、产品进出口检疫书、原产地证明书、使用说明书、装箱单等。

**Article 7** The 'relevant documents and materials other than the documents for taking delivery of the subject matter' as provided in Article 136 of the Contract Law shall mainly include insurance policies, warranty certificates, ordinary invoices, special VAT invoices, product certificates of conformity, quality assurance certificates, quality appraisal certificates, quality inspection certificates, product import and export quarantine certificates, certificates of origin, instruction manuals, packing lists, etc.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出卖人仅以增值税专用发票及税款抵扣资料证明其已履行交付标的物义务，买受人不认可的，出卖人应当提供其他证据证明交付标的物的事实。  
合同约定或者当事人之间习惯以普通发票作为付款凭证，买受人以普通发票证明已经履行付款义务的，人民法院应予支持，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 8** Where the seller only proves that it has performed the obligation of delivering the subject matter by using a special VAT invoice and tax deduction materials, and the buyer does not recognize it, the seller shall provide other evidence to prove the fact of delivery of the subject matter.  
Where the contract agrees or it is customary between the parties to use ordinary invoices as payment vouchers, and the buyer proves that it has performed the payment obligation by using an ordinary invoice, the People's Court shall uphold it, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary to rebut it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出卖人就同一普通动产订立多重买卖合同，在买卖合同均有效的情况下，买受人均要求实际履行合同的，应当按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）先行受领交付的买受人请求确认所有权已经转移的，人民法院应予支持；  
（二）均未受领交付，先行支付价款的买受人请求出卖人履行交付标的物等合同义务的，人民法院应予支持；  
（三）均未受领交付，也未支付价款，依法成立在先合同的买受人请求出卖人履行交付标的物等合同义务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where the seller concludes multiple sales contracts for the same ordinary movable property, and all the sales contracts are valid, and all buyers demand actual performance of the contract, it shall be dealt with according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where a buyer that first takes delivery requests a confirmation that the ownership has been transferred, the People's Court shall uphold it;  
(2) Where none has taken delivery, and a buyer that first pays the price requests the seller to perform the obligation of delivering the subject matter and other contractual obligations, the People's Court shall uphold it;  
(3) Where none has taken delivery nor paid the price, and a buyer whose contract is first validly formed requests the seller to perform the obligation of delivering the subject matter and other contractual obligations, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　出卖人就同一船舶、航空器、机动车等特殊动产订立多重买卖合同，在买卖合同均有效的情况下，买受人均要求实际履行合同的，应当按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）先行受领交付的买受人请求出卖人履行办理所有权转移登记手续等合同义务的，人民法院应予支持；  
（二）均未受领交付，先行办理所有权转移登记手续的买受人请求出卖人履行交付标的物等合同义务的，人民法院应予支持；  
（三）均未受领交付，也未办理所有权转移登记手续，依法成立在先合同的买受人请求出卖人履行交付标的物和办理所有权转移登记手续等合同义务的，人民法院应予支持；  
（四）出卖人将标的物交付给买受人之一，又为其他买受人办理所有权转移登记，已受领交付的买受人请求将标的物所有权登记在自己名下的，人民法院应予支持。  
三、标的物风险负担

**Article 10** Where the seller concludes multiple sales contracts for the same special movable property such as a ship, aircraft, motor vehicle, etc., and all the sales contracts are valid, and all buyers demand actual performance of the contract, it shall be dealt with according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where a buyer that first takes delivery requests the seller to perform the contractual obligation of undergoing the formalities for registration of ownership transfer, etc., the People's Court shall uphold it;  
(2) Where none has taken delivery, and a buyer that first undergoes the formalities for registration of ownership transfer requests the seller to perform the obligation of delivering the subject matter and other contractual obligations, the People's Court shall uphold it;  
(3) Where none has taken delivery nor undergone the formalities for registration of ownership transfer, and a buyer whose contract is first validly formed requests the seller to perform the obligation of delivering the subject matter and undergoing the formalities for registration of ownership transfer, etc., the People's Court shall uphold it;  
(4) Where the seller delivers the subject matter to one of the buyers and also undergoes the registration of ownership transfer for another buyer, and the buyer that has taken delivery requests that the ownership of the subject matter be registered in its own name, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
III. Risk Burden of Subject Matter

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　合同法第一百四十一条第二款第（一）项规定的“标的物需要运输的”，是指标的物由出卖人负责办理托运，承运人系独立于买卖合同当事人之外的运输业者的情形。标的物毁损、灭失的风险负担，按照合同法第一百四十五条的规定处理。

**Article 11** The phrase 'the subject matter needs to be transported' as provided in Article 141, paragraph 2, item (1) of the Contract Law refers to the situation where the seller is responsible for arranging shipment, and the carrier is a transport operator independent of the parties to the sales contract. The risk of damage to or loss of the subject matter shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 145 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　出卖人根据合同约定将标的物运送至买受人指定地点并交付给承运人后，标的物毁损、灭失的风险由买受人负担，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 12** Where the seller, in accordance with the contract, transports the subject matter to the place designated by the buyer and delivers it to the carrier, the risk of damage to or loss of the subject matter shall be borne by the buyer, unless the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　出卖人出卖交由承运人运输的在途标的物，在合同成立时知道或者应当知道标的物已经毁损、灭失却未告知买受人，买受人主张出卖人负担标的物毁损、灭失的风险的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where the seller sells the subject matter in transit that is being transported by a carrier, and at the time the contract is formed, it knows or should have known that the subject matter has been damaged or lost but fails to inform the buyer, and the buyer claims that the seller shall bear the risk of damage to or loss of the subject matter, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人对风险负担没有约定，标的物为种类物，出卖人未以装运单据、加盖标记、通知买受人等可识别的方式清楚地将标的物特定于买卖合同，买受人主张不负担标的物毁损、灭失的风险的，人民法院应予支持。  
四、标的物检验

**Article 14** Where the parties have no agreement on the risk burden, the subject matter is fungible goods, and the seller has not clearly identified the subject matter to the sales contract by identifiable means such as shipping documents, affixing marks, notifying the buyer, etc., and the buyer claims that it shall not bear the risk of damage to or loss of the subject matter, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
IV. Inspection of Subject Matter

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人对标的物的检验期间未作约定，买受人签收的送货单、确认单等载明标的物数量、型号、规格的，人民法院应当根据合同法第一百五十七条的规定，认定买受人已对数量和外观瑕疵进行了检验，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 15** Where the parties have not agreed on the inspection period for the subject matter, and the delivery note, confirmation note, etc. signed and received by the buyer specify the quantity, model, and specifications of the subject matter, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 157 of the Contract Law, determine that the buyer has inspected the quantity and apparent defects, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary to rebut it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出卖人依照买受人的指示向第三人交付标的物，出卖人和买受人之间约定的检验标准与买受人和第三人之间约定的检验标准不一致的，人民法院应当根据合同法第六十四条的规定，以出卖人和买受人之间约定的检验标准为标的物的检验标准。

**Article 16** Where the seller delivers the subject matter to a third party according to the buyer's instructions, and the inspection standard agreed between the seller and the buyer is inconsistent with the inspection standard agreed between the buyer and the third party, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 64 of the Contract Law, take the inspection standard agreed between the seller and the buyer as the inspection standard for the subject matter.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院具体认定合同法第一百五十八条第二款规定的“合理期间”时，应当综合当事人之间的交易性质、交易目的、交易方式、交易习惯、标的物的种类、数量、性质、安装和使用情况、瑕疵的性质、买受人应尽的合理注意义务、检验方法和难易程度、买受人或者检验人所处的具体环境、自身技能以及其他合理因素，依据诚实信用原则进行判断。  
合同法第一百五十八条第二款规定的“两年”是最长的合理期间。该期间为不变期间，不适用诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长的规定。

**Article 17** When specifically determining the 'reasonable period' as provided in Article 158, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the nature of the transaction, purpose of the transaction, method of transaction, transaction practices, type, quantity, nature, installation and usage of the subject matter, nature of the defects, reasonable duty of care that the buyer should exercise, inspection methods and difficulty, the specific environment and skills of the buyer or inspector, and other reasonable factors, and make a judgment in accordance with the principle of good faith.  
The 'two years' as provided in Article 158, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law is the longest reasonable period. That period is an unchangeable period and shall not be subject to the suspension, interruption, or extension of the limitation of action.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　约定的检验期间过短，依照标的物的性质和交易习惯，买受人在检验期间内难以完成全面检验的，人民法院应当认定该期间为买受人对外观瑕疵提出异议的期间，并根据[本解释第十七条](#art-17)第一款的规定确定买受人对隐蔽瑕疵提出异议的合理期间。  
约定的检验期间或者质量保证期间短于法律、行政法规规定的检验期间或者质量保证期间的，人民法院应当以法律、行政法规规定的检验期间或者质量保证期间为准。

**Article 18** Where the agreed inspection period is too short, and in light of the nature of the subject matter and transaction practices, it is difficult for the buyer to complete a full inspection within the inspection period, the People's Court shall determine that such period is the period for the buyer to raise objections to apparent defects, and shall determine the reasonable period for the buyer to raise objections to latent defects in accordance with Article 17, paragraph 1 of this Interpretation.  
Where the agreed inspection period or quality guarantee period is shorter than the inspection period or quality guarantee period prescribed by laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court shall take the inspection period or quality guarantee period prescribed by laws or administrative regulations as the standard.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　买受人在合理期间内提出异议，出卖人以买受人已经支付价款、确认欠款数额、使用标的物等为由，主张买受人放弃异议的，人民法院不予支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 19** Where the buyer raises an objection within a reasonable period, and the seller claims that the buyer has waived the objection on the ground that the buyer has paid the price, confirmed the amount of debt, used the subject matter, etc., the People's Court shall not uphold it, unless the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　合同法第一百五十八条规定的检验期间、合理期间、两年期间经过后，买受人主张标的物的数量或者质量不符合约定的，人民法院不予支持。  
出卖人自愿承担违约责任后，又以上述期间经过为由翻悔的，人民法院不予支持。  
五、违约责任

**Article 20** After the passage of the inspection period, reasonable period, or two-year period as provided in Article 158 of the Contract Law, if the buyer claims that the quantity or quality of the subject matter does not conform to the agreement, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
Where the seller voluntarily assumes liability for breach of contract and later reneges on the ground that the aforesaid periods have elapsed, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
V. Liability for Breach of Contract

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　买受人依约保留部分价款作为质量保证金，出卖人在质量保证期间未及时解决质量问题而影响标的物的价值或者使用效果，出卖人主张支付该部分价款的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Where the buyer retains part of the price as quality guarantee money in accordance with the contract, and the seller fails to timely resolve the quality problem within the quality guarantee period, thereby affecting the value or use effect of the subject matter, and the seller claims payment of that part of the price, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　买受人在检验期间、质量保证期间、合理期间内提出质量异议，出卖人未按要求予以修理或者因情况紧急，买受人自行或者通过第三人修理标的物后，主张出卖人负担因此发生的合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 22** Where the buyer raises a quality objection within the inspection period, quality guarantee period, or reasonable period, and the seller fails to make repairs as required, or due to an urgent situation, the buyer repairs the subject matter itself or through a third party, and claims that the seller shall bear the reasonable expenses incurred thereby, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　标的物质量不符合约定，买受人依照合同法第一百一十一条的规定要求减少价款的，人民法院应予支持。当事人主张以符合约定的标的物和实际交付的标的物按交付时的市场价值计算差价的，人民法院应予支持。  
价款已经支付，买受人主张返还减价后多出部分价款的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 23** Where the quality of the subject matter does not conform to the agreement, and the buyer demands a reduction in price in accordance with Article 111 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall uphold it. Where a party claims to calculate the price difference based on the market value at the time of delivery between the subject matter conforming to the agreement and the subject matter actually delivered, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
Where the price has been paid, and the buyer claims a refund of the excess price after the reduction, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　买卖合同对付款期限作出的变更，不影响当事人关于逾期付款违约金的约定，但该违约金的起算点应当随之变更。  
买卖合同约定逾期付款违约金，买受人以出卖人接受价款时未主张逾期付款违约金为由拒绝支付该违约金的，人民法院不予支持。  
买卖合同约定逾期付款违约金，但对账单、还款协议等未涉及逾期付款责任，出卖人根据对账单、还款协议等主张欠款时请求买受人依约支付逾期付款违约金的，人民法院应予支持，但对账单、还款协议等明确载有本金及逾期付款利息数额或者已经变更买卖合同中关于本金、利息等约定内容的除外。  
买卖合同没有约定逾期付款违约金或者该违约金的计算方法，出卖人以买受人违约为由主张赔偿逾期付款损失的，人民法院可以中国人民银行同期同类人民币贷款基准利率为基础，参照逾期罚息利率标准计算。

**Article 24** A change in the payment period under a sales contract shall not affect the parties' agreement on liquidated damages for overdue payment, but the starting point for calculating such liquidated damages shall be changed accordingly.  
Where a sales contract provides for liquidated damages for overdue payment, and the buyer refuses to pay such liquidated damages on the ground that the seller did not claim liquidated damages for overdue payment when accepting the price, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
Where a sales contract provides for liquidated damages for overdue payment, but the statement of account, repayment agreement, etc. do not involve the liability for overdue payment, and the seller, when claiming the amount owed based on the statement of account, repayment agreement, etc., requests the buyer to pay liquidated damages for overdue payment in accordance with the contract, the People's Court shall uphold it, except where the statement of account, repayment agreement, etc. explicitly contain the principal amount and the amount of interest for overdue payment or have changed the contents regarding the principal amount, interest, etc. in the sales contract.  
Where a sales contract does not provide for liquidated damages for overdue payment or the method for calculating such liquidated damages, and the seller claims compensation for losses due to overdue payment on the ground that the buyer has breached the contract, the People's Court may calculate based on the benchmark interest rate for RMB loans of the same type and term issued by the People's Bank of China at the same time, with reference to the standard interest rate for overdue penalty.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出卖人没有履行或者不当履行从给付义务，致使买受人不能实现合同目的，买受人主张解除合同的，人民法院应当根据合同法第九十四条第（四）项的规定，予以支持。

**Article 25** Where the seller fails to perform or improperly performs its ancillary payment obligation, causing the buyer to be unable to achieve the purpose of the contract, and the buyer claims dissolution of the contract, the People's Court shall uphold it in accordance with Article 94, item (4) of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　买卖合同因违约而解除后，守约方主张继续适用违约金条款的，人民法院应予支持；但约定的违约金过分高于造成的损失的，人民法院可以参照合同法第一百一十四条第二款的规定处理。

**Article 26** After a sales contract is dissolved due to breach of contract, if the non-breaching party claims that the liquidated damages clause shall continue to apply, the People's Court shall uphold it; however, if the agreed liquidated damages are excessively higher than the losses caused, the People's Court may deal with it by reference to the provisions of Article 114, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　买卖合同当事人一方以对方违约为由主张支付违约金，对方以合同不成立、合同未生效、合同无效或者不构成违约等为由进行免责抗辩而未主张调整过高的违约金的，人民法院应当就法院若不支持免责抗辩，当事人是否需要主张调整违约金进行释明。  
一审法院认为免责抗辩成立且未予释明，二审法院认为应当判决支付违约金的，可以直接释明并改判。

**Article 27** Where one party to a sales contract claims payment of liquidated damages on the ground that the other party has breached the contract, and the other party raises a defense of exemption from liability on the ground that the contract is not formed, has not taken effect, is void, or does not constitute a breach of contract, etc., without claiming adjustment of the excessive liquidated damages, the People's Court shall provide an explanation as to whether the party needs to claim adjustment of the liquidated damages if the court does not uphold the defense of exemption from liability.  
Where the court of first instance considers that the defense of exemption from liability is established and does not provide an explanation, and the court of second instance considers that a judgment should be made to order payment of liquidated damages, it may directly provide an explanation and amend the judgment.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　买卖合同约定的定金不足以弥补一方违约造成的损失，对方请求赔偿超过定金部分的损失的，人民法院可以并处，但定金和损失赔偿的数额总和不应高于因违约造成的损失。

**Article 28** Where the deposit agreed in a sales contract is insufficient to compensate for the losses caused by one party's breach of contract, and the other party requests compensation for the losses exceeding the deposit, the People's Court may award both, but the total amount of the deposit and the compensation for losses shall not exceed the losses caused by the breach of contract.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　买卖合同当事人一方违约造成对方损失，对方主张赔偿可得利益损失的，人民法院应当根据当事人的主张，依据合同法第一百一十三条、第一百一十九条、[本解释第三十条](#art-30)、第三十一条等规定进行认定。

**Article 29** Where one party to a sales contract breaches the contract and causes losses to the other party, and the other party claims compensation for loss of expected benefits, the People's Court shall make a determination based on the party's claim and in accordance with the provisions of Articles 113 and 119 of the Contract Law and Articles 30 and 31 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　买卖合同当事人一方违约造成对方损失，对方对损失的发生也有过错，违约方主张扣减相应的损失赔偿额的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 30** Where one party to a sales contract breaches the contract and causes losses to the other party, and the other party also has fault for the occurrence of the losses, and the breaching party claims a reduction of the corresponding amount of compensation for losses, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　买卖合同当事人一方因对方违约而获有利益，违约方主张从损失赔偿额中扣除该部分利益的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 31** Where one party to a sales contract obtains benefits due to the other party's breach of contract, and the breaching party claims to deduct such benefits from the amount of compensation for losses, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　合同约定减轻或者免除出卖人对标的物的瑕疵担保责任，但出卖人故意或者因重大过失不告知买受人标的物的瑕疵，出卖人主张依约减轻或者免除瑕疵担保责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 32** Where the contract provides for the reduction or exemption of the seller's warranty liability against defects in the subject matter, but the seller intentionally or due to gross negligence fails to inform the buyer of the defects in the subject matter, and the seller claims reduction or exemption of the warranty liability against defects in accordance with the contract, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　买受人在缔约时知道或者应当知道标的物质量存在瑕疵，主张出卖人承担瑕疵担保责任的，人民法院不予支持，但买受人在缔约时不知道该瑕疵会导致标的物的基本效用显著降低的除外。  
六、所有权保留

**Article 33** Where the buyer knows or should have known that there are defects in the quality of the subject matter at the time of contracting, and claims that the seller shall bear the warranty liability against defects, the People's Court shall not uphold it, except where the buyer did not know at the time of contracting that such defects would significantly reduce the basic utility of the subject matter.  
VI. Retention of Ownership

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　买卖合同当事人主张合同法第一百三十四条关于标的物所有权保留的规定适用于不动产的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 34** Where a party to a sales contract claims that the provisions of Article 134 of the Contract Law regarding retention of ownership of the subject matter apply to immovable property, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人约定所有权保留，在标的物所有权转移前，买受人有下列情形之一，对出卖人造成损害，出卖人主张取回标的物的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）未按约定支付价款的；  
（二）未按约定完成特定条件的；  
（三）将标的物出卖、出质或者作出其他不当处分的。  
取回的标的物价值显著减少，出卖人要求买受人赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 35** Where the parties agree on retention of ownership, before the transfer of ownership of the subject matter, if the buyer commits any of the following circumstances, causing damage to the seller, and the seller claims to take back the subject matter, the People's Court shall uphold it:  
(1) Failing to pay the price as agreed;  
(2) Failing to fulfill specific conditions as agreed;  
(3) Selling, pledging, or otherwise improperly disposing of the subject matter.  
Where the value of the recovered subject matter has significantly decreased, and the seller demands that the buyer compensate for the losses, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　买受人已经支付标的物总价款的百分之七十五以上，出卖人主张取回标的物的，人民法院不予支持。  
在[本解释第三十五条](#art-35)第一款第（三）项情形下，第三人依据物权法第一百零六条的规定已经善意取得标的物所有权或者其他物权，出卖人主张取回标的物的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 36** Where the buyer has paid seventy-five percent or more of the total price of the subject matter, and the seller claims to take back the subject matter, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
In the circumstance under Article 35, paragraph 1, item (3) of this Interpretation, if a third party has bona fide acquired the ownership or other real rights in the subject matter in accordance with Article 106 of the Property Law, and the seller claims to take back the subject matter, the People's Court shall not uphold it.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　出卖人取回标的物后，买受人在双方约定的或者出卖人指定的回赎期间内，消除出卖人取回标的物的事由，主张回赎标的物的，人民法院应予支持。  
买受人在回赎期间内没有回赎标的物的，出卖人可以另行出卖标的物。  
出卖人另行出卖标的物的，出卖所得价款依次扣除取回和保管费用、再交易费用、利息、未清偿的价金后仍有剩余的，应返还原买受人；如有不足，出卖人要求原买受人清偿的，人民法院应予支持，但原买受人有证据证明出卖人另行出卖的价格明显低于市场价格的除外。  
七、特种买卖

**Article 37** After the seller takes back the subject matter, if the buyer, within the redemption period agreed by the parties or designated by the seller, eliminates the reasons for the seller's taking back of the subject matter and claims to redeem the subject matter, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
If the buyer does not redeem the subject matter within the redemption period, the seller may sell the subject matter separately.  
Where the seller sells the subject matter separately, if there is a surplus after deducting in order the costs of taking back and storage, retransaction costs, interest, and unpaid price from the proceeds of sale, such surplus shall be returned to the original buyer; if there is a deficiency, and the seller demands that the original buyer make up the deficiency, the People's Court shall uphold it, unless the original buyer has evidence to prove that the price at which the seller separately sold the subject matter was significantly lower than the market price.  
VII. Special Types of Sales

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　合同法第一百六十七条第一款规定的“分期付款”，系指买受人将应付的总价款在一定期间内至少分三次向出卖人支付。  
分期付款买卖合同的约定违反合同法第一百六十七条第一款的规定，损害买受人利益，买受人主张该约定无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 38** The 'installment payment' as provided in Article 167, paragraph 1 of the Contract Law means that the buyer shall pay the total amount payable to the seller in at least three installments within a certain period.  
Where the provisions of an installment payment sales contract violate Article 167, paragraph 1 of the Contract Law and harm the buyer's interests, and the buyer claims that such provisions are void, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　分期付款买卖合同约定出卖人在解除合同时可以扣留已受领价金，出卖人扣留的金额超过标的物使用费以及标的物受损赔偿额，买受人请求返还超过部分的，人民法院应予支持。  
当事人对标的物的使用费没有约定的，人民法院可以参照当地同类标的物的租金标准确定。

**Article 39** Where an installment payment sales contract provides that the seller may retain the price already received upon dissolution of the contract, and the amount retained by the seller exceeds the usage fee for the subject matter and the compensation for damage to the subject matter, and the buyer requests a refund of the excess, the People's Court shall uphold it.  
Where the parties have no agreement on the usage fee for the subject matter, the People's Court may determine it by reference to the rental standard for similar subject matter in the locality.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　合同约定的样品质量与文字说明不一致且发生纠纷时当事人不能达成合意，样品封存后外观和内在品质没有发生变化的，人民法院应当以样品为准；外观和内在品质发生变化，或者当事人对是否发生变化有争议而又无法查明的，人民法院应当以文字说明为准。

**Article 40** Where the quality of the sample agreed in the contract is inconsistent with the written description, and the parties cannot reach an agreement when a dispute arises, if the appearance and intrinsic quality of the sample have not changed after sealing, the People's Court shall take the sample as the standard; if the appearance and intrinsic quality have changed, or if there is a dispute between the parties as to whether they have changed and it cannot be ascertained, the People's Court shall take the written description as the standard.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　试用买卖的买受人在试用期内已经支付一部分价款的，人民法院应当认定买受人同意购买，但合同另有约定的除外。  
在试用期内，买受人对标的物实施了出卖、出租、设定担保物权等非试用行为的，人民法院应当认定买受人同意购买。

**Article 41** Where the buyer in a trial sale has paid part of the price within the trial period, the People's Court shall determine that the buyer agrees to purchase, unless the contract provides otherwise.  
Where, within the trial period, the buyer has committed acts such as selling, leasing, or creating security interests in the subject matter that are not for trial use, the People's Court shall determine that the buyer agrees to purchase.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　买卖合同存在下列约定内容之一的，不属于试用买卖。买受人主张属于试用买卖的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）约定标的物经过试用或者检验符合一定要求时，买受人应当购买标的物；  
（二）约定第三人经试验对标的物认可时，买受人应当购买标的物；  
（三）约定买受人在一定期间内可以调换标的物；  
（四）约定买受人在一定期间内可以退还标的物。

**Article 42** Where a sales contract contains any of the following provisions, it shall not be a trial sale. If the buyer claims that it is a trial sale, the People's Court shall not uphold it:  
(1) It is agreed that when the subject matter meets certain requirements after trial or inspection, the buyer shall purchase the subject matter;  
(2) It is agreed that when a third party approves the subject matter after trial, the buyer shall purchase the subject matter;  
(3) It is agreed that the buyer may exchange the subject matter within a certain period;  
(4) It is agreed that the buyer may return the subject matter within a certain period.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　试用买卖的当事人没有约定使用费或者约定不明确，出卖人主张买受人支付使用费的，人民法院不予支持。  
八、其他问题

**Article 43** Where the parties to a trial sale have no agreement on the usage fee or the agreement is unclear, and the seller claims that the buyer shall pay a usage fee, the People's Court shall not uphold it.  
VIII. Other Issues

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　出卖人履行交付义务后诉请买受人支付价款，买受人以出卖人违约在先为由提出异议的，人民法院应当按照下列情况分别处理：  
（一）买受人拒绝支付违约金、拒绝赔偿损失或者主张出卖人应当采取减少价款等补救措施的，属于提出抗辩；  
（二）买受人主张出卖人应支付违约金、赔偿损失或者要求解除合同的，应当提起反诉。

**Article 44** After the seller performs its delivery obligation, it sues the buyer for payment of the price, and the buyer raises an objection on the ground that the seller breached the contract first, the People's Court shall deal with it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the buyer refuses to pay liquidated damages, refuses to compensate for losses, or claims that the seller shall take remedial measures such as reducing the price, it constitutes raising a defense;  
(2) Where the buyer claims that the seller shall pay liquidated damages, compensate for losses, or demands dissolution of the contract, it shall file a counterclaim.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　法律或者行政法规对债权转让、股权转让等权利转让合同有规定的，依照其规定；没有规定的，人民法院可以根据合同法第一百二十四条和第一百七十四条的规定，参照适用买卖合同的有关规定。  
权利转让或者其他有偿合同参照适用买卖合同的有关规定的，人民法院应当首先引用合同法第一百七十四条的规定，再引用买卖合同的有关规定。

**Article 45** Where laws or administrative regulations have provisions on contracts for the transfer of rights such as assignment of claims and transfer of equity, those provisions shall be followed; where there are no provisions, the People's Court may, in accordance with Articles 124 and 174 of the Contract Law, apply the relevant provisions of the sales contract by reference.  
Where a contract for the transfer of rights or other onerous contract applies the relevant provisions of the sales contract by reference, the People's Court shall first cite Article 174 of the Contract Law, and then cite the relevant provisions of the sales contract.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本解释施行前本院发布的有关购销合同、销售合同等有偿转移标的物所有权的合同的规定，与本解释抵触的，自本解释施行之日起不再适用。  
本解释施行后尚未终审的买卖合同纠纷案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。

**Article 46** Where the provisions previously issued by this Court before the coming into force of this Interpretation regarding contracts such as purchase and sales contracts and sales contracts for the onerous transfer of ownership of the subject matter conflict with this Interpretation, they shall no longer apply from the date this Interpretation comes into force.  
For cases of dispute over sales contracts that have not been finally adjudicated after the coming into force of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply; for cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, where a party applies for retrial or a decision is made to retry the case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
