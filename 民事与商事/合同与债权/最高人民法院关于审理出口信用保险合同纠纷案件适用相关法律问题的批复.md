适用相关法律问题的批复  
  
法释〔2013〕13号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于出口信用保险合同法律适用问题的请示》（粤高法〔2012〕442号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
对出口信用保险合同的法律适用问题，保险法没有作出明确规定。鉴于出口信用保险的特殊性，人民法院审理出口信用保险合同纠纷案件，可以参照适用保险法的相关规定；出口信用保险合同另有约定的，从其约定。

**Article 1** May 2, 2013  
  
Reply of the Supreme People's Court on Issues concerning the Application of Relevant Laws in the Trial of Disputes over Export Credit Insurance Contracts  
Fa Shi [2013] No. 13  
  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
  
The Request for Instructions on Issues concerning the Application of Laws to Export Credit Insurance Contracts (Yue Gao Fa [2012] No. 442) submitted by your court has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
Where the Insurance Law does not explicitly provide for the application of laws to export credit insurance contracts, given the special nature of export credit insurance, the People's Court may, when trying a dispute over an export credit insurance contract, apply mutatis mutandis the relevant provisions of the Insurance Law; if the export credit insurance contract otherwise provides, such provision shall prevail.
