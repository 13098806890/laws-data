# 最高人民法院关于审理经济合同纠纷案件有关保证的若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕8号  
**公布日期**：1994-04-15

---

### 正文

一、保证合同成立的认定  
1.保证人与债权人就保证问题依法达成书面协议的，保证合同成立。  
2.保证人以书面形式向债权人表示，当被保证人不履行债务时，由其代为履行或者承担连带责任并为债权人接受的，保证合同成立。  
3.保证人在债权人与被保证人签订的订有保证条款的主合同上，以保证人的身份签字或者盖章；或者主合同中虽没有保证条款，但保证人在主合同上以保证人的身份签字或者盖章的，视为保证合同成立。

**Article None** I. Determination of Establishment of Guarantee Contract  
1. If the guarantor and the creditor reach a written agreement on the guarantee issue according to law, the guarantee contract shall be established.  
2. If the guarantor indicates in writing to the creditor that, when the guaranteed party fails to perform the debt, the guarantor shall perform on behalf or bear joint and several liability, and the creditor accepts, the guarantee contract shall be established.  
3. If the guarantor signs or seals on the main contract, which contains guarantee clauses, signed by the creditor and the guaranteed party, in the capacity of a guarantor; or if the main contract does not contain guarantee clauses, but the guarantor signs or seals on the main contract in the capacity of a guarantor, the guarantee contract shall be deemed established.

二、有效保证合同保证人的责任  
4.保证合同依法成立后，被保证人不履行债务的，保证人应当按照保证合同约定的范围、方式和期限承担保证责任。  
5.保证合同明确约定保证人承担代为履行责任的，经债权人请求被保证人履行合同，被保证人拒不履行时，债权人可请求保证人履行。保证人不能代为履行合同，且强制执行被保证人的财产仍不足以清偿其债务的，由保证人承担赔偿责任。  
6.保证合同明确约定保证人承担连带责任的，当被保证人到期不履行合同时，债权人既可向被保证人求偿，也可直接向保证人求偿。  
7.保证合同没有约定保证人承担何种保证责任，或者约定不明确的，视为保证人承担赔偿责任。当被保证人不履行合同时，债权人应当首先请求被保证人清偿债务。强制执行被保证人的财产仍不足以清偿其债务的，由保证人承担赔偿责任。  
8.保证合同对保证范围有明确约定的，保证人在约定的保证范围内承担责任；保证合同没有约定保证范围或者对保证范围约定不明确的，保证人应当对被保证人的全部债务承担保证责任。  
9.向债权人保证监督支付专款专用的，作出该项保证的人，在履行了监督支付专款专用义务后，不再承担责任。未尽监督义务造成资金流失的，应对流失的资金承担连带责任。  
10.保证合同中约定有保证责任期限的，保证人在约定的保证责任期限内承担保证责任。债权人在保证责任期限内未向保证人主张权利的，保证人不再承担保证责任。  
11.保证合同中没有约定保证责任期限或者约定不明确的，保证人应当在被保证人承担责任的期限内承担保证责任。保证人如果在主合同履行期限届满后，书面要求债权人向被保证人为诉讼上的请求，而债权人在收到保证人的书面请求后一个月内未行使诉讼请求权的，保证人不再承担保证责任。  
12.债权人与被保证人未经保证人同意，变更主合同履行期限的，如保证合同中约定有保证责任期限，保证人仍在原保证责任期限内承担保证责任；如保证合同中未约定保证责任期限，保证人仍在被保证人原承担责任的期限内承担保证责任。  
债权人与被保证人未经保证人同意，在主合同履行期限内变更合同其他内容而使被保证人债务增加的，保证人对增加的债务不承担保证责任。  
13.债权人在保证责任期限内，将债权转移给他人，并通知保证人的，保证人应向债权受让人承担保证责任。  
14.被保证人经债权人同意在保证责任期限内，将债务转移给他人，未经保证人同意的，保证人不再承担保证责任，但保证人追认的除外。  
15.债权人在保证责任期限内，无正当理由拒绝被保证人履行债务的，保证人不再承担保证责任；债权人放弃抵押权的，保证人就放弃抵押权的部分不再承担保证责任。但保证人同意继续承担保证责任的除外。  
16.依照法律规定或者当事人约定，免除被保证人部分或者全部债务的，保证人相应的保证责任得以免除。

**Article None** II. Liability of Guarantor under Valid Guarantee Contract  
4. After a guarantee contract is established according to law, if the guaranteed party fails to perform the debt, the guarantor shall bear the guarantee liability within the scope, manner, and time limit agreed in the guarantee contract.  
5. If the guarantee contract explicitly stipulates that the guarantor bears the liability to perform on behalf, and after the creditor requests the guaranteed party to perform the contract and the guaranteed party refuses to perform, the creditor may request the guarantor to perform. If the guarantor cannot perform the contract on behalf, and compulsory execution of the guaranteed party's property is still insufficient to satisfy the debt, the guarantor shall bear compensation liability.  
6. If the guarantee contract explicitly stipulates that the guarantor bears joint and several liability, when the guaranteed party fails to perform the contract at maturity, the creditor may seek compensation from the guaranteed party or directly from the guarantor.  
7. If the guarantee contract does not stipulate which kind of guarantee liability the guarantor bears, or the stipulation is unclear, the guarantor shall be deemed to bear compensation liability. When the guaranteed party fails to perform the contract, the creditor shall first request the guaranteed party to discharge the debt. If compulsory execution of the guaranteed party's property is still insufficient to satisfy the debt, the guarantor shall bear compensation liability.  
8. If the guarantee contract has explicit stipulation on the scope of guarantee, the guarantor shall bear liability within the agreed scope; if the guarantee contract has no stipulation on the scope of guarantee or the stipulation is unclear, the guarantor shall bear guarantee liability for the entire debt of the guaranteed party.  
9. If a person guarantees to the creditor to supervise the payment of designated funds, that person shall no longer bear liability after fulfilling the obligation to supervise the payment of designated funds. If the supervision obligation is not fulfilled, causing loss of funds, the person shall bear joint and several liability for the lost funds.  
10. If a guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, the guarantor shall bear guarantee liability within the agreed guarantee liability period. If the creditor does not assert rights against the guarantor within the guarantee liability period, the guarantor shall no longer bear guarantee liability.  
11. If the guarantee contract does not stipulate a guarantee liability period or the stipulation is unclear, the guarantor shall bear guarantee liability within the period during which the guaranteed party bears liability. If, after the expiry of the performance period of the main contract, the guarantor requests in writing that the creditor bring a claim in court against the guaranteed party, and the creditor fails to exercise the claim within one month after receiving the written request, the guarantor shall no longer bear guarantee liability.  
12. If the creditor and the guaranteed party change the performance period of the main contract without the consent of the guarantor, if a guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, the guarantor shall still bear guarantee liability within the original guarantee liability period; if no guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, the guarantor shall still bear guarantee liability within the original period during which the guaranteed party bears liability.  
If the creditor and the guaranteed party change other contents of the main contract within the performance period of the main contract without the consent of the guarantor, thereby increasing the debt of the guaranteed party, the guarantor shall not bear guarantee liability for the increased debt.  
13. If the creditor transfers the creditor's rights to another person within the guarantee liability period and notifies the guarantor, the guarantor shall bear guarantee liability to the transferee of the creditor's rights.  
14. If the guaranteed party, with the consent of the creditor, transfers the debt to another person within the guarantee liability period, but without the consent of the guarantor, the guarantor shall no longer bear guarantee liability, except where the guarantor subsequently ratifies.  
15. If the creditor, within the guarantee liability period, without justifiable reasons, refuses the guaranteed party's performance of the debt, the guarantor shall no longer bear guarantee liability; if the creditor waives the mortgage, the guarantor shall no longer bear guarantee liability for the part of the mortgage waived, except where the guarantor agrees to continue bearing guarantee liability.  
16. If, according to law or by agreement of the parties, part or all of the debt of the guaranteed party is exempted, the corresponding guarantee liability of the guarantor shall be exempted.

三、无效保证合同的认定及保证人的责任  
17.法人的分支机构未经法人同意，为他人提供保证的，保证合同无效，保证人不承担保证责任，但应当根据其过错大小，承担相应的赔偿责任。法人的分支机构管理的财产不足以承担赔偿责任的，由法人承担。  
金融部门的分支机构提供保证的，如无其他导致保证合同无效的因素，保证人应当承担保证责任。  
18.法人的内部职能部门未经法人同意，为他人提供保证的，保证合同无效，保证人不承担保证责任，但应当根据其过错大小，由法人承担相应的赔偿责任。  
19.主合同债权人一方或者双方当事人采取欺诈、胁迫等手段，或者恶意串通，使保证人在违背真实意思情况下提供保证的，保证合同无效，保证人不承担责任。  
20.主合同无效，保证合同也无效，保证人不承担保证责任。但保证人知道或者应当知道主合同无效而仍然为之提供保证的，主合同被确认无效后，保证人与被保证人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article None** III. Determination of Void Guarantee Contract and Liability of Guarantor  
17. If a branch of a legal person provides a guarantee for another person without the consent of the legal person, the guarantee contract shall be void, and the guarantor shall not bear guarantee liability, but shall bear corresponding compensation liability according to the degree of fault. If the property managed by the branch of the legal person is insufficient to bear compensation liability, the legal person shall bear it.  
If a branch of a financial institution provides a guarantee, if there is no other factor causing the guarantee contract to be void, the guarantor shall bear guarantee liability.  
18. If an internal functional department of a legal person provides a guarantee for another person without the consent of the legal person, the guarantee contract shall be void, and the guarantor shall not bear guarantee liability, but the legal person shall bear corresponding compensation liability according to the degree of fault.  
19. If one party or both parties to the main contract use fraud, coercion, or other means, or collude maliciously, causing the guarantor to provide the guarantee against its true intention, the guarantee contract shall be void, and the guarantor shall not bear liability.  
20. If the main contract is void, the guarantee contract shall also be void, and the guarantor shall not bear guarantee liability. However, if the guarantor knew or should have known that the main contract was void and still provided the guarantee, after the main contract is confirmed void, the guarantor and the guaranteed party shall bear joint and several compensation liability.

四、在诉讼中为当事人提供的保证  
21.人民法院在案件审理过程中，决定对财产采取保全措施时，保证人为申请人或者被申请人提供保证的，在案件审理终结后，如果被保证人无财产可供执行或者其财产不足以清偿债务时，人民法院可以直接裁定执行保证人在其保证范围内的财产。  
22.在案件执行过程中，为被执行人提供保证的，被执行人逾期无财产可供执行或者其财产不足以清偿债务时，人民法院可以直接裁定执行保证人在其保证范围内的财产。

**Article None** IV. Guarantee Provided for Parties in Litigation  
21. When a People's Court decides to adopt preservation measures for property during the trial of a case, if a guarantor provides guarantee for the applicant or the respondent, after the case is concluded, if the guaranteed party has no property available for execution or its property is insufficient to satisfy the debt, the People's Court may directly order the enforcement of the property of the guarantor within the scope of the guarantee.  
22. During the execution of a case, if a guarantor provides guarantee for the person subject to enforcement, if the person subject to enforcement has no property available for execution upon expiry or its property is insufficient to satisfy the debt, the People's Court may directly order the enforcement of the property of the guarantor within the scope of the guarantee.

五、被保证人破产后保证人的责任  
23.被保证人被宣告破产的，债权人参加破产程序受偿后，对受偿不足的部分，保证人仍应承担保证责任。  
24.人民法院已审理终结的设有保证的合同纠纷案件，在执行终结前被保证人被宣告破产的，债权人可以生效法律文书确认的债权数额作为破产债权申报；债务已部分偿还的，以未偿还的部分作为债权申报。对经破产程序未受清偿的部分，保证人仍应承担保证责任。  
25.保证人代被保证人偿还债务后，尚未从被保证人处获偿被保证人即宣告破产的，保证人可以其代为清偿的数额作为破产债权申报。  
26.被保证人被宣告破产，债权人不申报债权的，在确认保证人的责任时，应当扣除债权人可以在破产程序中得到清偿的部分。

**Article None** V. Liability of Guarantor after Bankruptcy of Guaranteed Party  
23. If the guaranteed party is declared bankrupt, after the creditor participates in the bankruptcy proceedings and receives compensation, the guarantor shall still bear guarantee liability for the part insufficiently compensated.  
24. If the guaranteed party is declared bankrupt before the conclusion of execution of a guarantee contract dispute case that has been concluded by the People's Court, the creditor may file the amount of the creditor's rights confirmed by the effective legal document as bankruptcy claims; if part of the debt has been repaid, the unpaid part shall be filed as bankruptcy claims. For the part not satisfied through bankruptcy proceedings, the guarantor shall still bear guarantee liability.  
25. If the guarantor repays the debt on behalf of the guaranteed party, and the guaranteed party is declared bankrupt before the guarantor has obtained compensation from the guaranteed party, the guarantor may file the amount it has paid on behalf as a bankruptcy claim.  
26. If the guaranteed party is declared bankrupt and the creditor does not file claims, when determining the liability of the guarantor, the part that the creditor could have obtained compensation from the bankruptcy proceedings shall be deducted.

六、保证合同的诉讼时效  
27.保证合同约定有保证责任期限的，债权人应当在保证责任期限届满前向保证人主张权利。保证人拒绝承担保证责任的，债权人向人民法院请求保护其权利的诉讼时效期间，适用民法通则的有关诉讼时效的规定。  
28.保证合同约定有保证责任期限，但在保证责任期限内，债权人仅向被保证人主张权利而未向保证人主张权利的，主债务诉讼时效中断，保证债务的诉讼时效不中断。  
29.保证合同未约定保证责任期限的，主债务的诉讼时效中断，保证债务的诉讼时效亦中断。  
30.依照《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百三十九条的规定，主债务诉讼时效中止的，保证债务的诉讼时效同时中止。

**Article None** VI. Limitation of Action for Guarantee Contract  
27. If a guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, the creditor shall assert rights against the guarantor before the expiry of the guarantee liability period. If the guarantor refuses to bear guarantee liability, the limitation of action for the creditor to request protection of his rights from the People's Court shall be subject to the provisions on limitation of action in the General Principles of the Civil Law.  
28. If a guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, but within the guarantee liability period, the creditor only asserts rights against the guaranteed party and does not assert rights against the guarantor, the limitation of action for the principal debt shall be interrupted, but the limitation of action for the guarantee debt shall not be interrupted.  
29. If no guarantee liability period is stipulated in the guarantee contract, the limitation of action for the principal debt shall be interrupted, and the limitation of action for the guarantee debt shall also be interrupted.  
30. In accordance with the provisions of Article 139 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, if the limitation of action for the principal debt is suspended, the limitation of action for the guarantee debt shall be suspended simultaneously.

七、其他  
31.本院以前关于保证问题的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准，但已审结的案件，不得适用本规定进行再审。

**Article None** VII. Miscellaneous  
31. Where the previous judicial interpretations of this Court on guarantee issues are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail; however, for cases that have already been concluded, these Provisions shall not be applied for retrial.
