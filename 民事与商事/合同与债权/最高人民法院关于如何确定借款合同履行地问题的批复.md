### 最高人民法院关于如何确定借款合同履行地问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于如何确定借款合同履行地问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕10号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鲁高法函〔1993〕44号《关于如何确定借款合同履行地问题的请示》收悉。经研究，现答复如下：  
  
合同履行地是指当事人履行合同约定义务的地点。借款合同是双务合同，标的物为货币。贷款方与借款方均应按照合同约定分别承担贷出款项与偿还贷款及利息的义务，贷款方与借款方所在地都是履行合同约定义务的地点。依照借款合同的约定，贷款方应先将借款划出，从而履行了贷款方所应承担的义务。因此，除当事人另有约定外，确定贷款方所在地为合同履行地。  
  
1993年11月17日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Determine the Place of Performance of a Loan Contract  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your court's Lu Gao Fa Han [1993] No. 44, the "Request for Instructions on How to Determine the Place of Performance of a Loan Contract," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
The place of performance of a contract refers to the place where the parties perform the obligations stipulated in the contract. A loan contract is a bilateral contract, and the subject matter is currency. The lender and the borrower shall each undertake the obligations of disbursing the loan and repaying the loan and interest in accordance with the contract, and the locations of the lender and the borrower are both places for performing the obligations stipulated in the contract. In accordance with the provisions of the loan contract, the lender shall first transfer the loan, thereby fulfilling the obligations that the lender shall bear. Therefore, unless otherwise agreed by the parties, the location of the lender shall be determined as the place of performance of the contract.  
November 17, 1993
