### 最高人民法院关于如何确定合同签订地问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于如何确定合同签订地问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔 1986〕15号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院赣法经（ 1986）01号请示报告收悉。关于你省南昌市青山城乡企业产品贸易中心诉福建省宁化县工业贸易公司购销合同纠纷案，应如何确定合同签订地问题，经我们研究认为：凡书面合同写明了合同签订地点的，以合同写明的为准；未写明的，以双方在合同上共同签字盖章的地点为合同签订地；双方签字盖章不在同一地点的，以最后一方签字盖章的地点为合同签订地。本案的合同是在南昌市协议拟定并盖了原告单位的公章，最后由被告在宁化县盖了公章，因此，本案的合同签订地为宁化县。依据民事诉讼法（试行）第二十三条和第三十二条的规定，南昌市中级人民法院应将本案移送福建省宁化县人民法院管辖。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年四月十一日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of How to Determine the Place of Contract Formation  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1986] No. 15  
Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province:  
Your court's petition report Gan Fa Jing (1986) No. 01 has been received. Regarding the case of the dispute over a sales contract between Qingshan Urban-Rural Enterprise Product Trade Center of Nanchang City, Jiangxi Province, and Ninghua County Industrial Trade Company of Fujian Province, and the issue of how to determine the place of contract formation, upon our study, it is held that: Where a written contract specifies the place of contract formation, such specified place shall prevail; where it is not specified, the place where both parties jointly sign and affix their seals on the contract shall be the place of contract formation; where the parties sign and affix seals at different places, the place where the last party signs and affixes its seal shall be the place of contract formation. In this case, the contract was drafted and sealed with the plaintiff's official seal in Nanchang, and was finally sealed with the defendant's official seal in Ninghua County. Therefore, the place of contract formation in this case is Ninghua County. Pursuant to Articles 23 and 32 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), the Nanchang Intermediate People's Court shall transfer this case to the Ninghua County People's Court of Fujian Province for jurisdiction.  
This is our reply.  
April 11, 1986
