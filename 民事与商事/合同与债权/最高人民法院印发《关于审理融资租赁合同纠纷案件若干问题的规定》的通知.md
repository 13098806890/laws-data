# 最高人民法院印发《关于审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件若干问题的规定》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕19号  
**公布日期**：1996-05-27

> 五、融资租赁合同所涉及的项目应当报经有关部门批准而未经批准的，应认定融资租赁合同不生效。 六、有下列情形之一的，应认定融资租赁合同为无效合同： （一）出租人不具有从事融资租赁经营范围的； （二）承租人与供货人恶意串通，骗取出租人资金的； （三）以融资租赁合同形式规避国家有关法律、法规的； （四）依照有关法律、法规规定应认定为无效的。 七、融资租赁合同被确定为无效后，应区分下列情形分别处理： （一）因承租人的过错造成合同无效，出租人不要求返还租赁物的，租赁物可以不予返还，但承租人应赔偿因其过错给出租人造成的损失； （二）因出租人的过错造成合同无效，承租人要求退还租赁物的，可以退还租赁物，如有损失，出租人应赔偿相应损失； （三）因出租人和承租人的共同过错造成合同无效的，可以返还租赁物，并根据过错大小各自承担相应的损失和赔偿责任。 租赁物正在继续使用且发挥效益的，对租赁物是否返还，可以协商解决；协商不成的，由法院根据实际情况作出判决。 八、在《最高人民法院关于贯彻执行〈中华人民共和国民法通则〉若干问题的意见（试行）》中规定国家机关不能担任保证人之后，国家机关所作的保证应认定无效。因保证无效而给债权人造成损失的，提供保证的国家机关应当承担相应的赔偿责任。 九、租赁物从境外购买的，融资租赁合同当事人约定用外币支付租金，应认定为有效。 十、在租赁合同履行完毕之前，承租人未经出租人同意，将租赁物进行抵押、转让、转租或投资入股，其行为无效，出租人有权收回租赁物，并要求承租人赔偿损失。因承租人的无效行为给第三人造成损失的，第三人有权要求承租人赔偿。 十一、在融资租赁合同有效期间内，出租人非法干预承租人对租赁物的正常使用或者擅自取回租赁物，而造成承租人损失的，出租人应承担赔偿责任。

---

### 正文

六、有下列情形之一的，应认定融资租赁合同为无效合同：  
（一）出租人不具有从事融资租赁经营范围的；  
（二）承租人与供货人恶意串通，骗取出租人资金的；  
（三）以融资租赁合同形式规避国家有关法律、法规的；  
（四）依照有关法律、法规规定应认定为无效的。

**Article None** 6. A financial leasing contract shall be determined as a void contract in any of the following circumstances:  
(a) The lessor does not have the business scope for engaging in financial leasing;  
(b) The lessee and the supplier maliciously collude to defraud the lessor of funds;  
(c) The form of a financial leasing contract is used to circumvent relevant national laws and regulations;  
(d) It shall be determined as void in accordance with provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

七、融资租赁合同被确定为无效后，应区分下列情形分别处理：  
（一）因承租人的过错造成合同无效，出租人不要求返还租赁物的，租赁物可以不予返还，但承租人应赔偿因其过错给出租人造成的损失；  
（二）因出租人的过错造成合同无效，承租人要求退还租赁物的，可以退还租赁物，如有损失，出租人应赔偿相应损失；  
（三）因出租人和承租人的共同过错造成合同无效的，可以返还租赁物，并根据过错大小各自承担相应的损失和赔偿责任。  
租赁物正在继续使用且发挥效益的，对租赁物是否返还，可以协商解决；协商不成的，由法院根据实际情况作出判决。

**Article None** 7. After a financial leasing contract is determined as void, it shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(a) If the contract is void due to the fault of the lessee, and the lessor does not request the return of the leased property, the leased property may not be returned, but the lessee shall compensate the lessor for the losses caused by the lessee's fault;  
(b) If the contract is void due to the fault of the lessor, and the lessee requests the return of the leased property, the leased property may be returned; if there are losses, the lessor shall compensate for the corresponding losses;  
(c) If the contract is void due to the joint fault of the lessor and the lessee, the leased property may be returned, and each party shall bear the corresponding losses and compensation liability according to the degree of fault.  
Where the leased property is still in use and generating benefits, the parties may negotiate on whether to return the leased property; if negotiation fails, the People's Court shall make a judgment based on the actual circumstances.

八、在《最高人民法院关于贯彻执行〈中华人民共和国民法通则〉若干问题的意见（试行）》中规定国家机关不能担任保证人之后，国家机关所作的保证应认定无效。因保证无效而给债权人造成损失的，提供保证的国家机关应当承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article None** 8. After the Supreme People's Court's Opinions on Several Issues concerning the Implementation of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) provides that a state organ may not act as a surety, any guarantee made by a state organ shall be determined as void. If the invalidity of the guarantee causes losses to the creditor, the state organ that provided the guarantee shall bear the corresponding compensation liability.

九、租赁物从境外购买的，融资租赁合同当事人约定用外币支付租金，应认定为有效。

**Article None** 9. If the leased property is purchased from abroad, the agreement between the parties to a financial leasing contract to pay rent in foreign currency shall be determined as valid.

十、在租赁合同履行完毕之前，承租人未经出租人同意，将租赁物进行抵押、转让、转租或投资入股，其行为无效，出租人有权收回租赁物，并要求承租人赔偿损失。因承租人的无效行为给第三人造成损失的，第三人有权要求承租人赔偿。

**Article None** 10. Before the performance of the leasing contract is completed, if the lessee, without the consent of the lessor, mortgages, transfers, subleases, or contributes the leased property as an investment, such act shall be void. The lessor shall have the right to retrieve the leased property and demand compensation for losses from the lessee. If the lessee's void act causes losses to a third party, the third party shall have the right to demand compensation from the lessee.

十一、在融资租赁合同有效期间内，出租人非法干预承租人对租赁物的正常使用或者擅自取回租赁物，而造成承租人损失的，出租人应承担赔偿责任。

**Article None** 11. During the validity period of a financial leasing contract, if the lessor unlawfully interferes with the lessee's normal use of the leased property or retrieves the leased property without authorization, thereby causing losses to the lessee, the lessor shall bear compensation liability.

十二、在供货人有迟延交货或交付的租赁物质量、数量存在问题以及其他违反供货合同约定的行为时，对其进行索赔应区别不同情形予以处理：  
（一）供货合同或租赁合同中未约定转让索赔权的，对供货人的索赔应由出租人享有和行使，承租人应提供有关证据；  
（二）在供货合同和租赁合同中均约定转让索赔权的，应由承租人直接向供货人索赔。

**Article None** 12. Where the supplier delays delivery, or the quality or quantity of the delivered leased property is defective, or there are other acts violating the provisions of the supply contract, the claim against the supplier shall be handled differently according to the circumstances:  
(a) If the supply contract or the leasing contract does not stipulate the assignment of the right to claim, the right to claim against the supplier shall be enjoyed and exercised by the lessor, and the lessee shall provide relevant evidence;  
(b) If both the supply contract and the leasing contract stipulate the assignment of the right to claim, the lessee shall directly claim against the supplier.

十三、有下列情形之一的，当租赁物质量、数量等存在问题，在对供货人索赔不着或不足时，出租人应承担赔偿责任：  
（一）出租人根据租赁合同的约定完全是利用自己的技能和判断为承租人选择供货人或租赁物的；  
（二）出租人为承租人指定供货人或租赁物的；  
（三）出租人擅自变更承租人已选定的供货人或租赁物的。  
除上列情形外，出租人对租赁物的质量、数量等问题一般不承担责任。

**Article None** 13. In any of the following circumstances, where there are problems with the quality or quantity of the leased property, and the claim against the supplier is unsuccessful or insufficient, the lessor shall bear compensation liability:  
(a) The lessor, in accordance with the provisions of the leasing contract, entirely uses its own skills and judgment to select the supplier or the leased property for the lessee;  
(b) The lessor designates the supplier or the leased property for the lessee;  
(c) The lessor unilaterally changes the supplier or the leased property already selected by the lessee.  
Except for the circumstances listed above, the lessor shall generally not be liable for issues such as the quality or quantity of the leased property.

十四、在出租人无过错的情形下，对供货人索赔的费用和结果，均由承租人承担和享有。如因出租人的过错造成索赔逾期或索赔不着，出租人应承担相应的责任。

**Article None** 14. Where the lessor is without fault, the costs and results of claiming against the supplier shall be borne and enjoyed by the lessee. If the claim is overdue or unsuccessful due to the fault of the lessor, the lessor shall bear the corresponding liability.

十五、因租赁物的质量、数量等问题对供货人索赔，如出租人无过错，不影响出租人向承租人行使收取租金的权利。

**Article None** 15. If a claim against the supplier is made due to issues with the quality or quantity of the leased property, and the lessor is without fault, it shall not affect the lessor's right to collect rent from the lessee.

十六、承租人未按合同约定支付部分或全部租金，属违约行为，承租人应按合同约定支付租金、逾期利息，并赔偿出租人相应的损失。

**Article None** 16. If the lessee fails to pay part or all of the rent in accordance with the contract, it shall constitute a breach of contract. The lessee shall pay the rent and overdue interest as stipulated in the contract, and compensate the lessor for the corresponding losses.

十七、在承租人破产时，出租人可以将租赁物收回；也可以申请受理破产案件的法院拍卖租赁物，将拍卖所得款用以清偿承租人所欠出租人的债务。租赁物价值大于出租人债权的，其超出部分应退还承租人；租赁物价值小于出租人债权的，其未受清偿的债权应作为一般债权参加破产清偿程序，或者要求承租人的保证人清偿。

**Article None** 17. When the lessee is bankrupt, the lessor may retrieve the leased property; or may apply to the People's Court that accepts the bankruptcy case to auction the leased property, and use the auction proceeds to satisfy the lessee's debts owed to the lessor. If the value of the leased property exceeds the lessor's claim, the excess shall be returned to the lessee; if the value of the leased property is less than the lessor's claim, the unsatisfied part of the claim shall participate in the bankruptcy liquidation procedure as an ordinary claim, or the lessor may demand payment from the lessee's surety.

十八、在承租人破产时，出租人可以作为破产债权人申报债权，参加破产清偿程序；出租人的债权有第三人提供保证的，出租人也可以要求保证人履行保证责任。

**Article None** 18. When the lessee is bankrupt, the lessor may report its claim as a bankruptcy creditor and participate in the bankruptcy liquidation procedure; if the lessor's claim is guaranteed by a third party, the lessor may also demand that the surety perform the suretyship obligation.

十九、出租人在参加承租人破产清偿后，其债权未能全部受偿的，可就不足部分向保证人追偿。

**Article None** 19. After the lessor participates in the bankruptcy liquidation of the lessee, if its claim is not fully satisfied, it may seek recourse from the surety for the shortfall.

二十、出租人决定不参加承租人破产清偿程序的，应及时通知承租人的保证人，保证人可以就保证债务的数额申报债权参加破产分配。

**Article None** 20. If the lessor decides not to participate in the bankruptcy liquidation procedure of the lessee, it shall promptly notify the surety of the lessee; the surety may report a claim for the amount of the guaranteed debt and participate in the bankruptcy distribution.

二十一、融资租赁合同当事人请求法院保护其权利的诉讼时效应适用《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百三十五条的规定。

**Article None** 21. The limitation of action for a party to a financial leasing contract to request the People's Court to protect its rights shall be governed by the provisions of Article 135 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China.
