### 最高人民法院关于国内工矿产品购销合同、农副产品购销合同中的合同履行地如何确定的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于国内工矿产品购销合同、农副产品购销合同  
  
中的合同履行地如何确定的批复  
  
一九八五年七月四日法（经）复〔1985〕39号  
  
河北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院经济庭（85）冀法经字第8号函收悉。关于河北省大城县水利局食品厂（需方）诉上海市物资交易所综合门市部（供方）购销饼干机合同质量纠纷一案的管辖问题，经我们研究，答复如下：  
  
民事诉讼法（试行）第二十三条规定：“因合同纠纷提起的诉讼，由合同履行地或者合同签订地人民法院管辖。”合同履行地是指履行合同规定义务的地点，合同标的为实物的，一般是指标的交付的地点。具体到经济合同法中的工矿产品购销合同或农副产品购销合同，其合同履行地除供需双方在合同中有特殊约定的以外，因交货方式不同而有所不同，凡合同规定由供方送货、代运的，合同履行地为产品发运地；凡合同规定由需方自提的，合同履行地为产品提货地。  
  
本案的合同是在上海市签订的，产品是由供方在上海市交承运部门运出的，因此，合同签订地和履行地均为上海市。你院以供方承担产品安装调试义务而认定合同履行地为需方所在地是欠妥的。河北省大城县人民法院应依照民事诉讼法（试行）第二十三条、第三十二条的规定，将本案移送上海市供方所在地的区人民法院审理。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Determine the Place of Performance of Contracts for the Purchase and Sale of Industrial and Mineral Products and Agricultural and Sideline Products  
July 4, 1985, Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 39  
Higher People's Court of Hebei Province:  
We received your Economic Tribunal's letter No. (85) Ji Fa Jing Zi No. 8. Regarding the jurisdiction issue in the case of a dispute over the quality of a biscuit machine purchase and sale contract between the Dacheng County Water Resources Bureau Food Factory (buyer) of Hebei Province and the Shanghai Material Exchange Comprehensive Department Store (seller), after our study, we reply as follows:  
Article 23 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation) provides: "An action arising from a contract dispute shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place of performance of the contract or the place where the contract was concluded." The place of performance of a contract refers to the place where the obligations stipulated in the contract are performed. If the subject matter of the contract is a physical object, it generally refers to the place where the subject matter is delivered. Specifically, for contracts for the purchase and sale of industrial and mineral products or agricultural and sideline products under the Economic Contract Law, the place of performance of the contract, unless otherwise specially agreed by the parties in the contract, shall differ depending on the mode of delivery. Where the contract provides that the seller shall deliver or arrange for shipment of the products, the place of performance shall be the place where the products are dispatched; where the contract provides that the buyer shall take delivery in person, the place of performance shall be the place where the products are taken.  
In this case, the contract was concluded in Shanghai, and the products were shipped by the seller through a carrier in Shanghai. Therefore, both the place of conclusion and the place of performance of the contract are Shanghai. It is improper for your court to determine the place of performance as the buyer's locality on the ground that the seller assumed the obligation of product installation and commissioning. The Dacheng County People's Court of Hebei Province shall, in accordance with Articles 23 and 32 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), transfer this case to the district People's Court of the seller's locality in Shanghai for trial.  
This reply is given.
