### 最高人民法院关于对企业借贷合同借款方逾期不归还借款的应如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对企业借贷合同借款方逾期不归  
  
还借款的应如何处理问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕15号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1995〕223号《关于企业拆借合同期限届满后借款方不归还本金是否计算逾期利息及如何判决的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
企业借贷合同违反有关金融法规，属无效合同。对于合同期限届满后，借款方逾期不归还本金，当事人起诉到人民法院的，人民法院除应按照最高人民法院法（经）发〔1990〕27号《关于审理联营合同纠纷案件若干问题的解答》第四条第二项的有关规定判决外，对自双方当事人约定的还款期满之日起，至法院判决确定借款人返还本金期满期间内的利息，应当收缴，该利息按借贷双方原约定的利率计算，如果双方当事人对借款利息未约定，按同期银行贷款利率计算。借款人未按判决确定的期限归还本金的，应当依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-232)的规定加倍支付迟延履行期间的利息。  
  
1996年9月23日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Handle Cases Where the Borrower of an Enterprise Loan Contract Fails to Repay the Loan upon Expiration  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 15  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your Request for Instructions, Chuan Gao Fa [1995] No. 223, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether to Calculate Overdue Interest and How to Adjudicate Where the Borrower Fails to Repay the Principal after the Expiration of an Enterprise Loan Contract" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
An enterprise loan contract that violates relevant financial regulations is an invalid contract. Where the borrower fails to repay the principal upon the expiration of the contract term and the party initiates a prosecution with a People's Court, the People's Court shall, in addition to adjudicating in accordance with the relevant provisions of Item 2 of Article 4 of the Supreme People's Court's Reply on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Joint Operation Contract Disputes (Fa (Jing) Fa [1990] No. 27), confiscate the interest accrued from the date agreed by the parties for repayment to the date determined by the court for the borrower's repayment of the principal. Such interest shall be calculated at the interest rate originally agreed upon by the borrowing and lending parties; if no agreement on the loan interest has been reached by the parties, it shall be calculated at the bank loan interest rate for the same period. Where the borrower fails to repay the principal within the time limit determined by the judgment, it shall pay double the interest for the period of delayed performance in accordance with Article 232 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
September 23, 1996
