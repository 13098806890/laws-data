# 最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕20号  
**公布日期**：2004-12-16  
**生效日期**：2005-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2004年11月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1335次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年1月1日起施行。 2004年12月16日 最高人民法院 关于审理技术合同纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2004〕20号 （2004年11月30日最高人民法院审判委员会第1335次会议通过　2004年12月16日最高人民法院公告公布　自2005年1月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　技术成果，是指利用科学技术知识、信息和经验作出的涉及产品、工艺、材料及其改进等的技术方案，包括专利、专利申请、技术秘密、计算机软件、集成电路布图设计、植物新品种等。  
技术秘密，是指不为公众所知悉、具有商业价值并经权利人采取保密措施的技术信息。

**Article 1** A technological achievement refers to a technical solution involving products, processes, materials, and improvements thereof, made by utilizing scientific and technological knowledge, information, and experience, including patents, patent applications, technical secrets, computer software, layout designs of integrated circuits, new plant varieties, etc.  
A technical secret refers to technical information that is not known to the public, has commercial value, and for which the right holder has adopted confidentiality measures.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　合同法第三百二十六条第二款所称“执行法人或者其他组织的工作任务”，包括：  
（一）履行法人或者其他组织的岗位职责或者承担其交付的其他技术开发任务；  
（二）离职后一年内继续从事与其原所在法人或者其他组织的岗位职责或者交付的任务有关的技术开发工作，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
法人或者其他组织与其职工就职工在职期间或者离职以后所完成的技术成果的权益有约定的，人民法院应当依约定确认。

**Article 2** The term "performing the tasks of a legal person or other organization" as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 326 of the Contract Law includes:  
(1) fulfilling the post responsibilities of a legal person or other organization or undertaking other technological development tasks assigned by it;  
(2) continuing to engage in technological development work related to the post responsibilities or assigned tasks of the original legal person or other organization within one year after leaving the post, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a legal person or other organization and its employee have an agreement on the rights and interests in the technological achievement completed by the employee during the period of employment or after leaving the post, the People's Court shall confirm such rights and interests in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　合同法第三百二十六条第二款所称“物质技术条件”，包括资金、设备、器材、原材料、未公开的技术信息和资料等。

**Article 3** The term "material and technical conditions" as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 326 of the Contract Law includes funds, equipment, apparatus, raw materials, undisclosed technical information and materials, etc.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　合同法第三百二十六条第二款所称“主要利用法人或者其他组织的物质技术条件”，包括职工在技术成果的研究开发过程中，全部或者大部分利用了法人或者其他组织的资金、设备、器材或者原材料等物质条件，并且这些物质条件对形成该技术成果具有实质性的影响；还包括该技术成果实质性内容是在法人或者其他组织尚未公开的技术成果、阶段性技术成果基础上完成的情形。但下列情况除外：  
（一）对利用法人或者其他组织提供的物质技术条件，约定返还资金或者交纳使用费的；  
（二）在技术成果完成后利用法人或者其他组织的物质技术条件对技术方案进行验证、测试的。

**Article 4** The term "mainly utilizing the material and technical conditions of the legal person or other organization" as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 326 of the Contract Law includes: the employee, during the research and development process of the technological achievement, wholly or largely utilizes the material conditions such as funds, equipment, apparatus, or raw materials of the legal person or other organization, and such material conditions have a substantial impact on the formation of the technological achievement; it also includes the situation where the substantive content of the technological achievement is completed on the basis of technological achievements or phased technological achievements that have not yet been disclosed by the legal person or other organization. However, the following circumstances are excluded:  
(1) where there is an agreement to repay funds or pay usage fees for the utilization of the material and technical conditions provided by the legal person or other organization;  
(2) where, after the completion of the technological achievement, the material and technical conditions of the legal person or other organization are used for verification or testing of the technical solution.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　个人完成的技术成果，属于执行原所在法人或者其他组织的工作任务，又主要利用了现所在法人或者其他组织的物质技术条件的，应当按照该自然人原所在和现所在法人或者其他组织达成的协议确认权益。不能达成协议的，根据对完成该项技术成果的贡献大小由双方合理分享。

**Article 5** Where a technological achievement completed by an individual falls under the performance of the tasks of the original legal person or other organization and also mainly utilizes the material and technical conditions of the current legal person or other organization, the rights and interests shall be confirmed in accordance with the agreement reached between the original and current legal persons or other organizations of the natural person. If no agreement can be reached, the rights and interests shall be reasonably shared between the two parties based on their respective contributions to the completion of the technological achievement.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　合同法第三百二十六条、第三百二十七条所称完成技术成果的“个人”，包括对技术成果单独或者共同作出创造性贡献的人，也即技术成果的发明人或者设计人。人民法院在对创造性贡献进行认定时，应当分解所涉及技术成果的实质性技术构成。提出实质性技术构成并由此实现技术方案的人，是作出创造性贡献的人。  
提供资金、设备、材料、试验条件，进行组织管理，协助绘制图纸、整理资料、翻译文献等人员，不属于完成技术成果的个人。

**Article 6** The term "individual" who completes a technological achievement as mentioned in Articles 326 and 327 of the Contract Law includes a person who has made creative contributions, individually or jointly, to the technological achievement, i.e., the inventor or designer of the technological achievement. When determining creative contributions, the People's Court shall break down the substantive technical components of the technological achievement involved. A person who proposes the substantive technical components and thereby realizes the technical solution shall be regarded as a person who has made creative contributions.  
Persons who provide funds, equipment, materials, test conditions, carry out organizational management, assist in drawing diagrams, compiling data, translating literature, etc., shall not be regarded as individuals who complete the technological achievement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　不具有民事主体资格的科研组织订立的技术合同，经法人或者其他组织授权或者认可的，视为法人或者其他组织订立的合同，由法人或者其他组织承担责任；未经法人或者其他组织授权或者认可的，由该科研组织成员共同承担责任，但法人或者其他组织因该合同受益的，应当在其受益范围内承担相应责任。  
前款所称不具有民事主体资格的科研组织，包括法人或者其他组织设立的从事技术研究开发、转让等活动的课题组、工作室等。

**Article 7** Where a technology contract is concluded by a scientific research organization without the qualification of a civil subject, if it is authorized or ratified by a legal person or other organization, it shall be deemed as a contract concluded by the legal person or other organization, and the legal person or other organization shall bear liability; if it is not authorized or ratified by the legal person or other organization, the members of the scientific research organization shall bear joint liability, except that if the legal person or other organization has benefited from the contract, it shall bear corresponding liability within the scope of its benefits.  
The term "scientific research organization without the qualification of a civil subject" in the preceding paragraph includes research groups, studios, etc., established by a legal person or other organization to engage in technological research and development, transfer, and other activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产产品或者提供服务依法须经有关部门审批或者取得行政许可，而未经审批或者许可的，不影响当事人订立的相关技术合同的效力。  
当事人对办理前款所称审批或者许可的义务没有约定或者约定不明确的，人民法院应当判令由实施技术的一方负责办理，但法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 8** Where the production of a product or the provision of a service requires, according to law, approval or an administrative license from the relevant authority, but no such approval or license has been obtained, the validity of the relevant technology contract concluded by the parties shall not be affected.  
Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the obligation to handle the approval or license mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall order the party implementing the technology to be responsible for handling it, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人一方采取欺诈手段，就其现有技术成果作为研究开发标的与他人订立委托开发合同收取研究开发费用，或者就同一研究开发课题先后与两个或者两个以上的委托人分别订立委托开发合同重复收取研究开发费用的，受损害方依照合同法第五十四条第二款规定请求变更或者撤销合同的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 9** Where one party, by fraudulent means, concludes a commissioned development contract with another party by treating its existing technological achievement as the subject of research and development and collects research and development fees, or concludes separate commissioned development contracts with two or more clients successively for the same research and development subject and collects research and development fees repeatedly, the injured party requests the modification or revocation of the contract in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 54 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　下列情形，属于合同法第三百二十九条所称的“非法垄断技术、妨碍技术进步”：  
（一）限制当事人一方在合同标的技术基础上进行新的研究开发或者限制其使用所改进的技术，或者双方交换改进技术的条件不对等，包括要求一方将其自行改进的技术无偿提供给对方、非互惠性转让给对方、无偿独占或者共享该改进技术的知识产权；  
（二）限制当事人一方从其他来源获得与技术提供方类似技术或者与其竞争的技术；  
（三）阻碍当事人一方根据市场需求，按照合理方式充分实施合同标的技术，包括明显不合理地限制技术接受方实施合同标的技术生产产品或者提供服务的数量、品种、价格、销售渠道和出口市场；  
（四）要求技术接受方接受并非实施技术必不可少的附带条件，包括购买非必需的技术、原材料、产品、设备、服务以及接收非必需的人员等；  
（五）不合理地限制技术接受方购买原材料、零部件、产品或者设备等的渠道或者来源；  
（六）禁止技术接受方对合同标的技术知识产权的有效性提出异议或者对提出异议附加条件。

**Article 10** The following circumstances fall within the term "illegally monopolizing technology and hindering technological progress" as mentioned in Article 329 of the Contract Law:  
(1) restricting one party from conducting new research and development on the basis of the technology that is the subject matter of the contract, or restricting its use of the improved technology, or imposing unequal conditions for the exchange of improved technologies between the parties, including requiring a party to provide its self-improved technology to the other party free of charge, to transfer it to the other party on a non-reciprocal basis, or to exclusively or jointly own the intellectual property rights in such improved technology free of charge;  
(2) restricting one party from obtaining similar technology from other sources or technology that competes with the technology provided;  
(3) hindering one party from fully implementing the technology that is the subject matter of the contract in a reasonable manner according to market demand, including manifestly unreasonably restricting the quantity, variety, price, sales channels, and export markets of the products produced or services provided by the technology recipient through the implementation of the contract subject technology;  
(4) requiring the technology recipient to accept ancillary conditions that are not essential for implementing the technology, including purchasing non-essential technology, raw materials, products, equipment, services, and accepting non-essential personnel, etc.;  
(5) unreasonably restricting the channels or sources from which the technology recipient purchases raw materials, components, products, or equipment, etc.;  
(6) prohibiting the technology recipient from challenging the validity of the intellectual property rights in the technology that is the subject matter of the contract, or attaching conditions to such a challenge.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　技术合同无效或者被撤销后，技术开发合同研究开发人、技术转让合同让与人、技术咨询合同和技术服务合同的受托人已经履行或者部分履行了约定的义务，并且造成合同无效或者被撤销的过错在对方的，对其已履行部分应当收取的研究开发经费、技术使用费、提供咨询服务的报酬，人民法院可以认定为因对方原因导致合同无效或者被撤销给其造成的损失。  
技术合同无效或者被撤销后，因履行合同所完成新的技术成果或者在他人技术成果基础上完成后续改进技术成果的权利归属和利益分享，当事人不能重新协议确定的，人民法院可以判决由完成技术成果的一方享有。

**Article 11** After a technology contract becomes void or is revoked, if the research and development party under a technology development contract, the transferor under a technology transfer contract, or the commissioned party under a technology consulting contract or a technology service contract has performed or partially performed the agreed obligations, and the fault causing the contract to be void or revoked lies with the other party, the People's Court may determine that the research and development funds, technology use fees, and remuneration for consulting services that should have been collected for the performed part shall be deemed as losses caused to such party due to the other party's fault resulting in the contract being void or revoked.  
After a technology contract becomes void or is revoked, where the parties cannot reach a new agreement on the ownership of rights and the sharing of benefits with respect to new technological achievements completed in the performance of the contract or subsequent improved technological achievements completed on the basis of others' technological achievements, the People's Court may rule that such achievements shall be enjoyed by the party that completed them.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　根据合同法第三百二十九条的规定，侵害他人技术秘密的技术合同被确认无效后，除法律、行政法规另有规定的以外，善意取得该技术秘密的一方当事人可以在其取得时的范围内继续使用该技术秘密，但应当向权利人支付合理的使用费并承担保密义务。  
当事人双方恶意串通或者一方知道或者应当知道另一方侵权仍与其订立或者履行合同的，属于共同侵权，人民法院应当判令侵权人承担连带赔偿责任和保密义务，因此取得技术秘密的当事人不得继续使用该技术秘密。

**Article 12** Pursuant to Article 329 of the Contract Law, after a technology contract that infringes upon another person's technical secret is confirmed void, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, the party that acquired the technical secret in good faith may continue to use the technical secret within the scope of its acquisition, provided that it shall pay reasonable royalties to the right holder and assume the obligation of confidentiality.  
Where both parties act in bad faith collusion, or one party knew or should have known that the other party was infringing but still concluded or performed the contract with it, this constitutes joint infringement, and the People's Court shall order the infringers to bear joint and several compensation liability and assume the obligation of confidentiality; the party that thereby obtained the technical secret shall not continue to use it.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　依照前条第一款规定可以继续使用技术秘密的人与权利人就使用费支付发生纠纷的，当事人任何一方都可以请求人民法院予以处理。继续使用技术秘密但又拒不支付使用费的，人民法院可以根据权利人的请求判令使用人停止使用。  
人民法院在确定使用费时，可以根据权利人通常对外许可该技术秘密的使用费或者使用人取得该技术秘密所支付的使用费，并考虑该技术秘密的研究开发成本、成果转化和应用程度以及使用人的使用规模、经济效益等因素合理确定。  
不论使用人是否继续使用技术秘密，人民法院均应当判令其向权利人支付已使用期间的使用费。使用人已向无效合同的让与人支付的使用费应当由让与人负责返还。

**Article 13** If a dispute arises between a person who may continue to use a technical secret in accordance with paragraph 1 of the preceding article and the right holder regarding the payment of royalties, either party may request the People's Court to handle the matter. If the person continues to use the technical secret but refuses to pay royalties, the People's Court may, upon the request of the right holder, order the user to cease use.  
When determining the amount of royalties, the People's Court may reasonably determine it by reference to the royalties normally charged by the right holder for licensing the technical secret to others or the royalties paid by the user to obtain the technical secret, and taking into account factors such as the research and development costs of the technical secret, the degree of its transformation and application, and the user's scale of use and economic benefits.  
Regardless of whether the user continues to use the technical secret, the People's Court shall order it to pay royalties to the right holder for the period during which it has used the secret. Royalties that the user has already paid to the transferor under the void contract shall be returned by the transferor.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对技术合同的价款、报酬和使用费，当事人没有约定或者约定不明确的，人民法院可以按照以下原则处理：  
（一）对于技术开发合同和技术转让合同，根据有关技术成果的研究开发成本、先进性、实施转化和应用的程度，当事人享有的权益和承担的责任，以及技术成果的经济效益等合理确定；  
（二）对于技术咨询合同和技术服务合同，根据有关咨询服务工作的技术含量、质量和数量，以及已经产生和预期产生的经济效益等合理确定。  
技术合同价款、报酬、使用费中包含非技术性款项的，应当分项计算。

**Article 14** Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the price, remuneration, and royalties of a technology contract, the People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) For technology development contracts and technology transfer contracts, the amount shall be reasonably determined based on the research and development costs of the relevant technological achievement, its advancement, the degree of its implementation, transformation, and application, the rights and interests enjoyed and liabilities borne by the parties, and the economic benefits of the technological achievement, etc.;  
(2) For technology consultation contracts and technology service contracts, the amount shall be reasonably determined based on the technical content, quality, and quantity of the relevant consulting service work, as well as the economic benefits already generated or expected to be generated, etc.  
Where the price, remuneration, or royalties of a technology contract include non-technical amounts, they shall be calculated separately.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　技术合同当事人一方迟延履行主要债务，经催告后在30日内仍未履行，另一方依据合同法第九十四条第（三）项的规定主张解除合同的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
当事人在催告通知中附有履行期限且该期限超过30日的，人民法院应当认定该履行期限为合同法第九十四条第（三）项规定的合理期限。

**Article 15** Where one party to a technology contract delays in performing its principal obligation and fails to perform within 30 days after receiving a demand for performance, the other party claims dissolution of the contract in accordance with paragraph (3) of Article 94 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.  
Where the demand notice includes a performance period that exceeds 30 days, the People's Court shall determine that such performance period is the reasonable period provided for in paragraph (3) of Article 94 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人以技术成果向企业出资但未明确约定权属，接受出资的企业主张该技术成果归其享有的，人民法院一般应当予以支持，但是该技术成果价值与该技术成果所占出资额比例明显不合理损害出资人利益的除外。  
当事人对技术成果的权属约定有比例的，视为共同所有，其权利使用和利益分配，按共有技术成果的有关规定处理，但当事人另有约定的，从其约定。  
当事人对技术成果的使用权约定有比例的，人民法院可以视为当事人对实施该项技术成果所获收益的分配比例，但当事人另有约定的，从其约定。  
二、技术开发合同

**Article 16** Where a party makes a capital contribution to an enterprise with a technological achievement but does not clearly stipulate the ownership, and the enterprise that accepts the contribution claims that the technological achievement belongs to it, the People's Court shall generally uphold such claim, unless the value of the technological achievement is manifestly disproportionate to the contributed amount of the capital contribution represented by the technological achievement, thereby unreasonably harming the interests of the contributor.  
Where the parties stipulate a proportion for the ownership of the technological achievement, it shall be deemed as joint ownership, and the use of rights and distribution of benefits shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on jointly owned technological achievements, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
Where the parties stipulate a proportion for the right to use the technological achievement, the People's Court may regard this as the proportion for distributing the proceeds obtained from implementing the technological achievement, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
II. Technology Development Contracts

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　合同法第三百三十条所称“新技术、新产品、新工艺、新材料及其系统”，包括当事人在订立技术合同时尚未掌握的产品、工艺、材料及其系统等技术方案，但对技术上没有创新的现有产品的改型、工艺变更、材料配方调整以及对技术成果的验证、测试和使用除外。

**Article 17** The term "new technology, new product, new process, new material, and systems thereof" as mentioned in Article 330 of the Contract Law includes technical solutions such as products, processes, materials, and systems thereof that the parties have not yet mastered at the time of concluding the technology contract; however, it excludes modifications of existing products with no technological innovation, changes in processes, adjustments of material formulations, and the verification, testing, and use of technological achievements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　合同法第三百三十条第四款规定的“当事人之间就具有产业应用价值的科技成果实施转化订立的”技术转化合同，是指当事人之间就具有实用价值但尚未实现工业化应用的科技成果包括阶段性技术成果，以实现该科技成果工业化应用为目标，约定后续试验、开发和应用等内容的合同。

**Article 18** The "technology transformation contract" provided for in paragraph 4 of Article 330 of the Contract Law refers to a contract concluded between parties concerning scientific and technological achievements that have practical value but have not yet achieved industrial application, including phased technological achievements, with the objective of realizing the industrial application of such scientific and technological achievements, and under which the parties agree on subsequent trials, development, application, and other matters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　合同法第三百三十五条所称“分工参与研究开发工作”，包括当事人按照约定的计划和分工，共同或者分别承担设计、工艺、试验、试制等工作。  
技术开发合同当事人一方仅提供资金、设备、材料等物质条件或者承担辅助协作事项，另一方进行研究开发工作的，属于委托开发合同。

**Article 19** The term "participating in research and development work through division of labor" as mentioned in Article 335 of the Contract Law includes the parties jointly or separately undertaking work such as design, process, testing, and trial production in accordance with the agreed plan and division of labor.  
Where one party to a technology development contract merely provides material conditions such as funds, equipment, and materials, or undertakes ancillary coordination matters, and the other party carries out the research and development work, the contract shall be a commissioned development contract.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　合同法第三百四十一条所称“当事人均有使用和转让的权利”，包括当事人均有不经对方同意而自己使用或者以普通使用许可的方式许可他人使用技术秘密，并独占由此所获利益的权利。当事人一方将技术秘密成果的转让权让与他人，或者以独占或者排他使用许可的方式许可他人使用技术秘密，未经对方当事人同意或者追认的，应当认定该让与或者许可行为无效。

**Article 20** The term "each party has the right to use and transfer" as mentioned in Article 341 of the Contract Law includes the right of each party to use the technical secret itself or to license others to use it by way of a non-exclusive license without the consent of the other party, and to exclusively enjoy the benefits derived therefrom. Where one party transfers the right to transfer the technical secret achievement to another person, or licenses another person to use the technical secret by way of an exclusive or sole license, without the consent or ratification of the other party, such transfer or license shall be determined void.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　技术开发合同当事人依照合同法的规定或者约定自行实施专利或使用技术秘密，但因其不具备独立实施专利或者使用技术秘密的条件，以一个普通许可方式许可他人实施或者使用的，可以准许。  
三、技术转让合同

**Article 21** Where a party to a technology development contract, in accordance with the provisions of the Contract Law or the agreement, implements the patent or uses the technical secret itself, but, because it does not have the conditions to independently implement the patent or use the technical secret, it licenses another person to implement or use it by way of a non-exclusive license, this may be permitted.  
III. Technology Transfer Contracts

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　合同法第三百四十二条规定的“技术转让合同”，是指合法拥有技术的权利人，包括其他有权对外转让技术的人，将现有特定的专利、专利申请、技术秘密的相关权利让与他人，或者许可他人实施、使用所订立的合同。但就尚待研究开发的技术成果或者不涉及专利、专利申请或者技术秘密的知识、技术、经验和信息所订立的合同除外。  
技术转让合同中关于让与人向受让人提供实施技术的专用设备、原材料或者提供有关的技术咨询、技术服务的约定，属于技术转让合同的组成部分。因此发生的纠纷，按照技术转让合同处理。  
当事人以技术入股方式订立联营合同，但技术入股人不参与联营体的经营管理，并且以保底条款形式约定联营体或者联营对方支付其技术价款或者使用费的，视为技术转让合同。

**Article 22** The term "technology transfer contract" as mentioned in Article 342 of the Contract Law refers to a contract concluded by a right holder who lawfully owns the technology, including other persons entitled to transfer the technology to others, for the transfer of the relevant rights in existing specific patents, patent applications, or technical secrets to another person, or for licensing another person to implement or use them. However, it excludes contracts concluded regarding technological achievements yet to be researched and developed, or regarding knowledge, technology, experience, and information that do not involve patents, patent applications, or technical secrets.  
An agreement in a technology transfer contract concerning the transferor providing the transferee with specialized equipment or raw materials for implementing the technology, or providing relevant technical consultation or technical services, shall be an integral part of the technology transfer contract. Any dispute arising therefrom shall be handled in accordance with the technology transfer contract.  
Where the parties conclude a joint venture contract by way of technology contribution, but the technology contributor does not participate in the management of the joint venture and, by way of a guaranteed minimum return clause, agrees that the joint venture or the other party shall pay it technology consideration or royalties, such contract shall be deemed a technology transfer contract.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　专利申请权转让合同当事人以专利申请被驳回或者被视为撤回为由请求解除合同，该事实发生在依照[专利法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-10)第三款的规定办理专利申请权转让登记之前的，人民法院应当予以支持；发生在转让登记之后的，不予支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
专利申请因专利申请权转让合同成立时即存在尚未公开的同样发明创造的在先专利申请被驳回，当事人依据合同法第五十四条第一款第（二）项的规定请求予以变更或者撤销合同的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 23** Where a party to a patent application right transfer contract requests dissolution of the contract on the ground that the patent application has been rejected or is deemed to have been withdrawn, and such fact occurs before the registration of the patent application right transfer is handled in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 10 of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall uphold the request; if it occurs after the registration of the transfer, the request shall not be upheld, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
Where a patent application is rejected because, at the time of conclusion of the patent application right transfer contract, there already existed a prior patent application for the same invention-creation that had not yet been published, and a party requests modification or revocation of the contract in accordance with item (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 54 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall uphold the request.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　订立专利权转让合同或者专利申请权转让合同前，让与人自己已经实施发明创造，在合同生效后，受让人要求让与人停止实施的，人民法院应当予以支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
让与人与受让人订立的专利权、专利申请权转让合同，不影响在合同成立前让与人与他人订立的相关专利实施许可合同或者技术秘密转让合同的效力。

**Article 24** Where, before concluding a patent right transfer contract or a patent application right transfer contract, the transferor has already implemented the invention-creation itself, and after the contract takes effect, the transferee requests the transferor to cease such implementation, the People's Court shall uphold the request, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
A patent right or patent application right transfer contract concluded between the transferor and the transferee shall not affect the validity of any relevant patent exploitation license contract or technical secret transfer contract concluded between the transferor and another person before the conclusion of the transfer contract.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　专利实施许可包括以下方式：  
（一）独占实施许可，是指让与人在约定许可实施专利的范围内，将该专利仅许可一个受让人实施，让与人依约定不得实施该专利；  
（二）排他实施许可，是指让与人在约定许可实施专利的范围内，将该专利仅许可一个受让人实施，但让与人依约定可以自行实施该专利；  
（三）普通实施许可，是指让与人在约定许可实施专利的范围内许可他人实施该专利，并且可以自行实施该专利。  
当事人对专利实施许可方式没有约定或者约定不明确的，认定为普通实施许可。专利实施许可合同约定受让人可以再许可他人实施专利的，认定该再许可为普通实施许可，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
技术秘密的许可使用方式，参照本条第一、二款的规定确定。

**Article 25** Patent exploitation licenses include the following types:  
(1) An exclusive license means that, within the scope of the patent exploitation licensed under the agreement, the transferor licenses only one transferee to exploit the patent, and the transferor shall not exploit the patent in accordance with the agreement;  
(2) A sole license means that, within the scope of the patent exploitation licensed under the agreement, the transferor licenses only one transferee to exploit the patent, but the transferor may itself exploit the patent in accordance with the agreement;  
(3) A non-exclusive license means that, within the scope of the patent exploitation licensed under the agreement, the transferor licenses another person to exploit the patent and may itself exploit the patent.  
Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the type of patent exploitation license, it shall be determined as a non-exclusive license. Where a patent exploitation license contract stipulates that the transferee may sublicense others to exploit the patent, such sublicense shall be determined as a non-exclusive license, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
The method of licensing the use of a technical secret shall be determined by reference to the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this article.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　专利实施许可合同让与人负有在合同有效期内维持专利权有效的义务，包括依法缴纳专利年费和积极应对他人提出宣告专利权无效的请求，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 26** The transferor under a patent exploitation license contract shall have the obligation to maintain the validity of the patent right during the term of the contract, including paying annual patent fees according to law and actively responding to requests from others for a declaration of invalidity of the patent right, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　排他实施许可合同让与人不具备独立实施其专利的条件，以一个普通许可的方式许可他人实施专利的，人民法院可以认定为让与人自己实施专利，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 27** Where the transferor under a sole license contract does not have the conditions to independently exploit its patent and licenses another person to exploit the patent by way of a non-exclusive license, the People's Court may determine this as the transferor exploiting the patent itself, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　合同法第三百四十三条所称“实施专利或者使用技术秘密的范围”，包括实施专利或者使用技术秘密的期限、地域、方式以及接触技术秘密的人员等。  
当事人对实施专利或者使用技术秘密的期限没有约定或者约定不明确的，受让人实施专利或者使用技术秘密不受期限限制。

**Article 28** The term "scope of exploiting the patent or using the technical secret" as mentioned in Article 343 of the Contract Law includes the term, region, method of exploiting the patent or using the technical secret, and the persons having access to the technical secret, etc.  
Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the term of exploiting the patent or using the technical secret, the transferee shall not be subject to any term limitation in exploiting the patent or using the technical secret.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　合同法第三百四十七条规定技术秘密转让合同让与人承担的“保密义务”，不限制其申请专利，但当事人约定让与人不得申请专利的除外。  
当事人之间就申请专利的技术成果所订立的许可使用合同，专利申请公开以前，适用技术秘密转让合同的有关规定；发明专利申请公开以后、授权以前，参照适用专利实施许可合同的有关规定；授权以后，原合同即为专利实施许可合同，适用专利实施许可合同的有关规定。  
人民法院不以当事人就已经申请专利但尚未授权的技术订立专利实施许可合同为由，认定合同无效。  
四、技术咨询合同和技术服务合同

**Article 29** The "confidentiality obligation" of the transferor under a technical secret transfer contract as provided in Article 347 of the Contract Law shall not restrict it from applying for a patent, unless the parties have agreed that the transferor shall not apply for a patent.  
With respect to a license contract concluded between parties concerning a technological achievement for which a patent application has been filed, before the publication of the patent application, the relevant provisions on technical secret transfer contracts shall apply; after the publication of the invention patent application but before the grant of the patent, the relevant provisions on patent exploitation license contracts shall apply by reference; after the grant of the patent, the original contract shall be a patent exploitation license contract, and the relevant provisions on patent exploitation license contracts shall apply.  
The People's Court shall not determine a contract void on the ground that the parties have concluded a patent exploitation license contract concerning technology for which a patent application has been filed but the patent has not yet been granted.  
IV. Technology Consultation Contracts and Technology Service Contracts

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　合同法第三百五十六条第一款所称“特定技术项目”，包括有关科学技术与经济社会协调发展的软科学研究项目，促进科技进步和管理现代化、提高经济效益和社会效益等运用科学知识和技术手段进行调查、分析、论证、评价、预测的专业性技术项目。

**Article 30** The term "specific technical projects" as mentioned in paragraph 1 of Article 356 of the Contract Law includes soft science research projects concerning the coordinated development of science and technology and the economy and society, and professional technical projects that use scientific knowledge and technical means to conduct investigation, analysis, demonstration, evaluation, and forecasting for the purposes of promoting scientific and technological progress and management modernization, and improving economic and social benefits.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对技术咨询合同受托人进行调查研究、分析论证、试验测定等所需费用的负担没有约定或者约定不明确的，由受托人承担。  
当事人对技术咨询合同委托人提供的技术资料和数据或者受托人提出的咨询报告和意见未约定保密义务，当事人一方引用、发表或者向第三人提供的，不认定为违约行为，但侵害对方当事人对此享有的合法权益的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 31** Where the parties have not agreed on or have not made clear the bearing of expenses necessary for the consultant under a technology consulting contract to conduct investigations, studies, analysis, demonstrations, tests, measurements, etc., such expenses shall be borne by the consultant.  
Where the parties have not agreed on a confidentiality obligation with respect to the technical materials and data provided by the client under a technology consulting contract or the consulting report and opinions provided by the consultant, and one party quotes, publishes, or provides them to a third party, such act shall not be deemed as a breach of contract; however, if the act infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the other party in this regard, the infringing party shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　技术咨询合同受托人发现委托人提供的资料、数据等有明显错误或者缺陷，未在合理期限内通知委托人的，视为其对委托人提供的技术资料、数据等予以认可。委托人在接到受托人的补正通知后未在合理期限内答复并予补正的，发生的损失由委托人承担。

**Article 32** Where the consultant under a technology consulting contract discovers obvious errors or defects in the materials, data, etc. provided by the client but fails to notify the client within a reasonable period, it shall be deemed that the consultant has acknowledged the technical materials, data, etc. provided by the client. If the client, after receiving the consultant's notice of correction, fails to respond and make corrections within a reasonable period, the losses incurred shall be borne by the client.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　合同法第三百五十六条第二款所称“特定技术问题”，包括需要运用专业技术知识、经验和信息解决的有关改进产品结构、改良工艺流程、提高产品质量、降低产品成本、节约资源能耗、保护资源环境、实现安全操作、提高经济效益和社会效益等专业技术问题。

**Article 33** The term "specific technical problems" as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 356 of the Contract Law includes professional and technical problems that require the application of professional technical knowledge, experience, and information to solve, such as improving product structure, improving process flow, enhancing product quality, reducing product cost, saving resources and energy, protecting resources and the environment, achieving safe operation, and improving economic and social benefits.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　当事人一方以技术转让的名义提供已进入公有领域的技术，或者在技术转让合同履行过程中合同标的技术进入公有领域，但是技术提供方进行技术指导、传授技术知识，为对方解决特定技术问题符合约定条件的，按照技术服务合同处理，约定的技术转让费可以视为提供技术服务的报酬和费用，但是法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
依照前款规定，技术转让费视为提供技术服务的报酬和费用明显不合理的，人民法院可以根据当事人的请求合理确定。

**Article 34** Where one party provides technology that has entered the public domain in the name of a technology transfer, or the technology that is the subject matter of the contract enters the public domain during the performance of the technology transfer contract, but the technology provider provides technical guidance, imparts technical knowledge, and solves specific technical problems for the other party in compliance with the agreed conditions, the contract shall be handled in accordance with a technology service contract, and the agreed technology transfer fee may be regarded as remuneration and expenses for providing technology services, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the technology transfer fee regarded as remuneration and expenses for providing technology services is manifestly unreasonable, the People's Court may reasonably determine it upon the request of a party.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人对技术服务合同受托人提供服务所需费用的负担没有约定或者约定不明确的，由受托人承担。  
技术服务合同受托人发现委托人提供的资料、数据、样品、材料、场地等工作条件不符合约定，未在合理期限内通知委托人的，视为其对委托人提供的工作条件予以认可。委托人在接到受托人的补正通知后未在合理期限内答复并予补正的，发生的损失由委托人承担。

**Article 35** Where the parties have not agreed on or have not made clear the bearing of expenses necessary for the service provider under a technology service contract to provide services, such expenses shall be borne by the service provider.  
Where the service provider under a technology service contract discovers that the working conditions provided by the client, such as materials, data, samples, materials, and sites, do not conform to the contract but fails to notify the client within a reasonable period, it shall be deemed that the service provider has acknowledged the working conditions provided by the client. If the client, after receiving the service provider's notice of correction, fails to respond and make corrections within a reasonable period, the losses incurred shall be borne by the client.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　合同法第三百六十四条规定的“技术培训合同”，是指当事人一方委托另一方对指定的学员进行特定项目的专业技术训练和技术指导所订立的合同，不包括职业培训、文化学习和按照行业、法人或者其他组织的计划进行的职工业余教育。

**Article 36** The "technology training contract" as provided in Article 364 of the Contract Law refers to a contract concluded by one party entrusting the other party to provide professional technical training and technical guidance on a specific project to designated trainees. It does not include vocational training, cultural studies, or spare-time education for employees carried out in accordance with the plans of the industry, a legal person, or other organization.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人对技术培训必需的场地、设施和试验条件等工作条件的提供和管理责任没有约定或者约定不明确的，由委托人负责提供和管理。  
技术培训合同委托人派出的学员不符合约定条件，影响培训质量的，由委托人按照约定支付报酬。  
受托人配备的教员不符合约定条件，影响培训质量，或者受托人未按照计划和项目进行培训，导致不能实现约定培训目标的，应当减收或者免收报酬。  
受托人发现学员不符合约定条件或者委托人发现教员不符合约定条件，未在合理期限内通知对方，或者接到通知的一方未在合理期限内按约定改派的，应当由负有履行义务的当事人承担相应的民事责任。

**Article 37** Where the parties have not agreed on or have not made clear the responsibility for providing and managing working conditions such as venues, facilities, and testing conditions necessary for technology training, the client shall be responsible for providing and managing them.  
If the trainees assigned by the client under a technology training contract do not meet the agreed conditions and thus affect the training quality, the client shall pay the remuneration as agreed.  
If the instructors assigned by the service provider do not meet the agreed conditions and affect the training quality, or if the service provider fails to conduct the training in accordance with the plan and project, resulting in failure to achieve the agreed training objectives, the service provider shall reduce or waive the remuneration.  
If the service provider discovers that the trainees do not meet the agreed conditions or the client discovers that the instructors do not meet the agreed conditions, and either fails to notify the other party within a reasonable period, or the party receiving the notice fails to make a replacement in accordance with the agreement within a reasonable period, the party that bears the obligation of performance shall bear corresponding civil liability.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　合同法第三百六十四条规定的“技术中介合同”，是指当事人一方以知识、技术、经验和信息为另一方与第三人订立技术合同进行联系、介绍以及对履行合同提供专门服务所订立的合同。

**Article 38** The "technology intermediary contract" as provided in Article 364 of the Contract Law refers to a contract concluded by one party to make contacts and introduce the other party to a third party for the conclusion of a technology contract, and to provide specialized services for the performance of the contract, using its knowledge, technology, experience, and information.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　中介人从事中介活动的费用，是指中介人在委托人和第三人订立技术合同前，进行联系、介绍活动所支出的通信、交通和必要的调查研究等费用。中介人的报酬，是指中介人为委托人与第三人订立技术合同以及对履行该合同提供服务应当得到的收益。  
当事人对中介人从事中介活动的费用负担没有约定或者约定不明确的，由中介人承担。当事人约定该费用由委托人承担但未约定具体数额或者计算方法的，由委托人支付中介人从事中介活动支出的必要费用。  
当事人对中介人的报酬数额没有约定或者约定不明确的，应当根据中介人所进行的劳务合理确定，并由委托人承担。仅在委托人与第三人订立的技术合同中约定中介条款，但未约定给付中介人报酬或者约定不明确的，应当支付的报酬由委托人和第三人平均承担。

**Article 39** The expenses for an intermediary's intermediary activities refer to the expenses incurred by the intermediary for communication, transportation, and necessary investigations and research in the course of making contacts and introductions before the client and the third party conclude a technology contract. The intermediary's remuneration refers to the earnings that the intermediary shall be entitled to receive for facilitating the conclusion of a technology contract between the client and the third party and for providing services for the performance of the contract.  
Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the bearing of the expenses for the intermediary's intermediary activities, such expenses shall be borne by the intermediary. Where the parties agree that such expenses shall be borne by the client but do not agree on a specific amount or calculation method, the client shall pay the necessary expenses incurred by the intermediary for the intermediary activities.  
Where the parties have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the amount of the intermediary's remuneration, it shall be reasonably determined based on the labor provided by the intermediary, and the client shall bear it. Where only an intermediary clause is stipulated in the technology contract concluded between the client and the third party, but no agreement on the payment of remuneration to the intermediary is made or the agreement is unclear, the remuneration payable shall be borne equally by the client and the third party.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　中介人未促成委托人与第三人之间的技术合同成立的，其要求支付报酬的请求，人民法院不予支持；其要求委托人支付其从事中介活动必要费用的请求，应当予以支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
中介人隐瞒与订立技术合同有关的重要事实或者提供虚假情况，侵害委托人利益的，应当根据情况免收报酬并承担赔偿责任。

**Article 40** Where an intermediary fails to facilitate the conclusion of a technology contract between the client and the third party, its request for payment of remuneration shall not be upheld by the People's Court; its request for the client to pay the necessary expenses incurred in its intermediary activities shall be upheld, unless the parties have otherwise agreed.  
Where an intermediary conceals important facts relating to the conclusion of a technology contract or provides false information, thereby harming the interests of the client, it shall, depending on the circumstances, be exempted from receiving remuneration and shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　中介人对造成委托人与第三人之间的技术合同的无效或者被撤销没有过错，并且该技术合同的无效或者被撤销不影响有关中介条款或者技术中介合同继续有效，中介人要求按照约定或者本解释的有关规定给付从事中介活动的费用和报酬的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
中介人收取从事中介活动的费用和报酬不应当被视为委托人与第三人之间的技术合同纠纷中一方当事人的损失。  
五、与审理技术合同纠纷有关的程序问题

**Article 41** Where an intermediary is not at fault for causing the technology contract between the client and the third party to be void or revoked, and the voidness or revocation of the technology contract does not affect the continued validity of the relevant intermediary clause or the technology intermediary contract, and the intermediary requests payment of expenses and remuneration for its intermediary activities in accordance with the agreement or the relevant provisions of this Interpretation, the People's Court shall uphold such request.  
The expenses and remuneration received by an intermediary for its intermediary activities shall not be regarded as losses of a party in the dispute over the technology contract between the client and the third party.  
V. Procedural Issues Relating to the Trial of Technology Contract Disputes

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　当事人将技术合同和其他合同内容或者将不同类型的技术合同内容订立在一个合同中的，应当根据当事人争议的权利义务内容，确定案件的性质和案由。  
技术合同名称与约定的权利义务关系不一致的，应当按照约定的权利义务内容，确定合同的类型和案由。  
技术转让合同中约定让与人负责包销或者回购受让人实施合同标的技术制造的产品，仅因让与人不履行或者不能全部履行包销或者回购义务引起纠纷，不涉及技术问题的，应当按照包销或者回购条款约定的权利义务内容确定案由。

**Article 42** Where a party incorporates the contents of a technology contract and other contracts, or incorporates the contents of different types of technology contracts, into one contract, the nature of the case and the cause of action shall be determined based on the rights and obligations in dispute between the parties.  
Where the name of a technology contract is inconsistent with the agreed rights and obligations relationship, the type of contract and the cause of action shall be determined based on the agreed rights and obligations.  
Where a technology transfer contract stipulates that the transferor shall be responsible for exclusive distribution or repurchase of the products manufactured by the transferee through the implementation of the contract subject technology, and a dispute arises solely because the transferor fails to perform or fails to fully perform the exclusive distribution or repurchase obligation and does not involve technical issues, the cause of action shall be determined based on the rights and obligations agreed in the exclusive distribution or repurchase clause.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　技术合同纠纷案件一般由中级以上人民法院管辖。  
各高级人民法院根据本辖区的实际情况并报经最高人民法院批准，可以指定若干基层人民法院管辖第一审技术合同纠纷案件。  
其他司法解释对技术合同纠纷案件管辖另有规定的，从其规定。  
合同中既有技术合同内容，又有其他合同内容，当事人就技术合同内容和其他合同内容均发生争议的，由具有技术合同纠纷案件管辖权的人民法院受理。

**Article 43** Cases of technology contract disputes shall generally be under the jurisdiction of intermediate people's courts or above.  
Each Higher People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction and upon approval by the Supreme People's Court, designate certain primary people's courts to have jurisdiction over first-instance technology contract dispute cases.  
Where other judicial interpretations provide otherwise regarding the jurisdiction over technology contract dispute cases, such provisions shall prevail.  
Where a contract contains both technology contract contents and other contract contents, and the parties dispute both the technology contract contents and the other contract contents, the case shall be accepted by a people's court that has jurisdiction over technology contract dispute cases.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　一方当事人以诉讼争议的技术合同侵害他人技术成果为由请求确认合同无效，或者人民法院在审理技术合同纠纷中发现可能存在该无效事由的，人民法院应当依法通知有关利害关系人，其可以作为有独立请求权的第三人参加诉讼或者依法向有管辖权的人民法院另行起诉。  
利害关系人在接到通知后15日内不提起诉讼的，不影响人民法院对案件的审理。

**Article 44** Where one party requests confirmation that the technology contract under dispute is void on the ground that it infringes upon another person's technological achievement, or where the People's Court, during the trial of a technology contract dispute, discovers that such a ground for invalidity may exist, the People's Court shall notify the relevant interested party according to law, and such interested party may participate in the lawsuit as a third party with independent claims or separately file a lawsuit with a competent people's court according to law.  
Where the interested party fails to file a lawsuit within 15 days after receiving the notice, it shall not affect the People's Court's trial of the case.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　第三人向受理技术合同纠纷案件的人民法院就合同标的技术提出权属或者侵权请求时，受诉人民法院对此也有管辖权的，可以将权属或者侵权纠纷与合同纠纷合并审理；受诉人民法院对此没有管辖权的，应当告知其向有管辖权的人民法院另行起诉或者将已经受理的权属或者侵权纠纷案件移送有管辖权的人民法院。权属或者侵权纠纷另案受理后，合同纠纷应当中止诉讼。  
专利实施许可合同诉讼中，受让人或者第三人向专利复审委员会请求宣告专利权无效的，人民法院可以不中止诉讼。在案件审理过程中专利权被宣告无效的，按照[专利法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-47)第二款和第三款的规定处理。  
六、其　　他

**Article 45** Where a third party, before the people's court hearing the technology contract dispute, raises a claim for ownership or infringement concerning the technology that is the subject matter of the contract, if the court before which the case is brought also has jurisdiction over such claim, the ownership or infringement dispute may be tried together with the contract dispute; if the court does not have jurisdiction over such claim, it shall inform the third party to file a separate lawsuit with a competent people's court or transfer the already accepted ownership or infringement dispute case to a competent people's court. After the ownership or infringement dispute is accepted as a separate case, the contract dispute shall be stayed.  
In a lawsuit concerning a patent exploitation license contract, where the transferee or a third party requests the Patent Reexamination Board to declare the patent right invalid, the People's Court may decide not to stay the lawsuit. If the patent right is declared invalid during the trial of the case, it shall be handled in accordance with paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 47 of the Patent Law.  
VI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　集成电路布图设计、植物新品种许可使用和转让等合同争议，相关行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定；没有规定的，适用合同法总则的规定，并可以参照合同法第十八章和本解释的有关规定处理。  
计算机软件开发、许可使用和转让等合同争议，著作权法以及其他法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定；没有规定的，适用合同法总则的规定，并可以参照合同法第十八章和本解释的有关规定处理。

**Article 46** For contract disputes concerning the license and transfer of layout designs of integrated circuits and new plant varieties, if relevant administrative regulations provide otherwise, those regulations shall apply; if there are no such provisions, the general provisions of the Contract Law shall apply, and the relevant provisions of Chapter 18 of the Contract Law and this Interpretation may be referred to for handling.  
For contract disputes concerning the development, license, and transfer of computer software, if the Copyright Law or other laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise, those provisions shall apply; if there are no such provisions, the general provisions of the Contract Law shall apply, and the relevant provisions of Chapter 18 of the Contract Law and this Interpretation may be referred to for handling.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本解释自2005年1月1日起施行。

**Article 47** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2005.
