### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范票据行为，保障票据活动中当事人的合法权益，维护社会经济秩序，促进社会主义市场经济的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating negotiable instrument activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of parties involved in negotiable instrument activities, maintaining the social and economic order, and promoting the development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的票据活动，适用本法。  
本法所称票据，是指汇票、本票和支票。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to negotiable instrument activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purpose of this Law, negotiable instruments refer to bills of exchange, promissory notes and cheques.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　票据活动应当遵守法律、行政法规，不得损害社会公共利益。

**Article 3** Negotiable instrument activities shall comply with laws and administrative regulations and shall not harm the public interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　票据出票人制作票据，应当按照法定条件在票据上签章，并按照所记载的事项承担票据责任。  
持票人行使票据权利，应当按照法定程序在票据上签章，并出示票据。  
其他票据债务人在票据上签章的，按照票据所记载的事项承担票据责任。  
本法所称票据权利，是指持票人向票据债务人请求支付票据金额的权利，包括付款请求权和追索权。  
本法所称票据责任，是指票据债务人向持票人支付票据金额的义务。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 4** The drawer of a negotiable instrument shall, when making the instrument, sign it in accordance with the statutory conditions and shall bear the liability on the instrument according to the matters recorded thereon.  
The holder, when exercising the rights on the instrument, shall sign the instrument in accordance with the statutory procedures and present the instrument.  
Other obligors on the instrument who sign the instrument shall bear the liability on the instrument according to the matters recorded thereon.  
For the purpose of this Law, rights on an instrument refer to the right of the holder to demand payment of the sum payable on the instrument from the obligor on the instrument, including the right of payment and the right of recourse.  
For the purpose of this Law, liability on an instrument refers to the obligation of the obligor on the instrument to pay the sum payable on the instrument to the holder.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　票据当事人可以委托其代理人在票据上签章，并应当在票据上表明其代理关系。  
没有代理权而以代理人名义在票据上签章的，应当由签章人承担票据责任；代理人超越代理权限的，应当就其超越权限的部分承担票据责任。

**Article 5** A party to a negotiable instrument may authorize an agent to sign the instrument and shall indicate the agency relationship on the instrument.  
Where a person signs an instrument in the capacity of an agent without the authority of agency, the signer shall bear the liability on the instrument; where an agent exceeds the scope of authority, the agent shall bear the liability on the instrument for the part exceeding the authority.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人在票据上签章的，其签章无效，但是不影响其他签章的效力。

**Article 6** Where a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity signs a negotiable instrument, his signature shall be invalid, but this shall not affect the validity of other signatures on the instrument.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　票据上的签章，为签名、盖章或者签名加盖章。  
法人和其他使用票据的单位在票据上的签章，为该法人或者该单位的盖章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签章。  
在票据上的签名，应当为该当事人的本名。

**Article 7** A signature on a negotiable instrument means a signature, a seal or a signature plus a seal.  
The signature of a legal person or other entity using the instrument on the instrument shall be the seal of the legal person or the entity plus the signature of its legal representative or its authorized agent.  
The signature on the instrument shall be the true name of the party.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　票据金额以中文大写和数码同时记载，二者必须一致，二者不一致的，票据无效。

**Article 8** The sum payable on a negotiable instrument shall be recorded in both Chinese capital characters and numerals, and the two shall be consistent; if they are inconsistent, the instrument shall be invalid.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　票据上的记载事项必须符合本法的规定。  
票据金额、日期、收款人名称不得更改，更改的票据无效。  
对票据上的其他记载事项，原记载人可以更改，更改时应当由原记载人签章证明。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-43" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第43条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 9** The matters recorded on a negotiable instrument shall comply with the provisions of this Law.  
The sum payable, date and name of the payee on the instrument shall not be altered; any alteration shall render the instrument invalid.  
Other matters recorded on the instrument may be altered by the original recorder, and when altered, the original recorder shall sign as evidence of the alteration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　票据的签发、取得和转让，应当遵循诚实信用的原则，具有真实的交易关系和债权债务关系。  
票据的取得，必须给付对价，即应当给付票据双方当事人认可的相对应的代价。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第14条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 10** The issuance, acquisition and transfer of negotiable instruments shall follow the principle of good faith and shall be based on a genuine transaction relationship and a relationship of rights and obligations.  
The acquisition of a negotiable instrument must be made for a consideration, that is, a consideration corresponding to the value recognized by both parties to the instrument shall be given.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因税收、继承、赠与可以依法无偿取得票据的，不受给付对价的限制。但是，所享有的票据权利不得优于其前手的权利。  
前手是指在票据签章人或者持票人之前签章的其他票据债务人。

**Article 11** Where a negotiable instrument is acquired gratuitously according to law due to tax, succession or gift, the restriction on the giving of consideration shall not apply. However, the rights on the instrument enjoyed by such a holder shall not be superior to those of his prior party.  
Prior party means other obligors on the instrument who signed the instrument before the signer or the holder.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　以欺诈、偷盗或者胁迫等手段取得票据的，或者明知有前列情形，出于恶意取得票据的，不得享有票据权利。  
持票人因重大过失取得不符合本法规定的票据的，也不得享有票据权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 12** Where a negotiable instrument is acquired by means of fraud, theft or coercion, or is acquired with knowledge of the above circumstances and in bad faith, the acquirer shall not enjoy the rights on the instrument.  
Where a holder acquires an instrument that does not comply with the provisions of this Law through gross negligence, he shall not enjoy the rights on the instrument either.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　票据债务人不得以自己与出票人或者与持票人的前手之间的抗辩事由，对抗持票人。但是，持票人明知存在抗辩事由而取得票据的除外。  
票据债务人可以对不履行约定义务的与自己有直接债权债务关系的持票人，进行抗辩。  
本法所称抗辩，是指票据债务人根据本法规定对票据债权人拒绝履行义务的行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 13** An obligor on a negotiable instrument shall not set up defenses against the holder based on the grounds of defenses between himself and the drawer or between himself and the holder's prior party, unless the holder acquired the instrument with knowledge of the existence of such grounds of defenses.  
An obligor on an instrument may raise defenses against a holder who has a direct relationship of rights and obligations with him and who fails to perform the agreed obligations.  
For the purpose of this Law, defenses refer to the act of an obligor on an instrument refusing to perform his obligations towards the creditor on the instrument in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　票据上的记载事项应当真实，不得伪造、变造。伪造、变造票据上的签章和其他记载事项的，应当承担法律责任。  
票据上有伪造、变造的签章的，不影响票据上其他真实签章的效力。  
票据上其他记载事项被变造的，在变造之前签章的人，对原记载事项负责；在变造之后签章的人，对变造之后的记载事项负责；不能辨别是在票据被变造之前或者之后签章的，视同在变造之前签章。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-67" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第67条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 14** The matters recorded on a negotiable instrument shall be genuine and shall not be forged or altered. Anyone who forges or alters the signature or other matters recorded on the instrument shall bear legal liability.  
Where there are forged or altered signatures on the instrument, the validity of other genuine signatures on the instrument shall not be affected.  
Where other matters recorded on the instrument are altered, the person who signed before the alteration shall be liable for the originally recorded matters; the person who signed after the alteration shall be liable for the matters as altered; if it cannot be determined whether the signing occurred before or after the alteration, the signing shall be deemed to have occurred before the alteration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　票据丧失，失票人可以及时通知票据的付款人挂失止付，但是，未记载付款人或者无法确定付款人及其代理付款人的票据除外。  
收到挂失止付通知的付款人，应当暂停支付。  
失票人应当在通知挂失止付后三日内，也可以在票据丧失后，依法向人民法院申请公示催告，或者向人民法院提起诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-26" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 15** In the event of loss of a negotiable instrument, the person losing the instrument may promptly notify the payer of the instrument to stop payment, except where the payer is not recorded on the instrument or the payer and its agent for payment cannot be determined.  
The payer who receives the notice to stop payment shall suspend payment.  
The person losing the instrument shall, within three days after notifying the stop-payment, or may, after the loss of the instrument, apply to the People's Court for a public summons for exhortation according to law, or initiate a prosecution with the People's Court.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　持票人对票据债务人行使票据权利，或者保全票据权利，应当在票据当事人的营业场所和营业时间内进行，票据当事人无营业场所的，应当在其住所进行。

**Article 16** The exercise of rights on an instrument or the preservation of such rights by the holder against the obligor on the instrument shall be carried out at the business place and during the business hours of the party to the instrument; where the party has no business place, it shall be carried out at his domicile.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　票据权利在下列期限内不行使而消灭：  
（一）持票人对票据的出票人和承兑人的权利，自票据到期日起二年。见票即付的汇票、本票，自出票日起二年；  
（二）持票人对支票出票人的权利，自出票日起六个月；  
（三）持票人对前手的追索权，自被拒绝承兑或者被拒绝付款之日起六个月；  
（四）持票人对前手的再追索权，自清偿日或者被提起诉讼之日起三个月。  
票据的出票日、到期日由票据当事人依法确定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第18条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第20条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 17** The rights on a negotiable instrument shall be extinguished if not exercised within the following periods:  
(1) The right of the holder against the drawer and the acceptor of the instrument shall be two years from the date of maturity. For a bill of exchange or promissory note payable on sight, it shall be two years from the date of issue;  
(2) The right of the holder against the drawer of a cheque shall be six months from the date of issue;  
(3) The right of recourse of the holder against the prior party shall be six months from the date of dishonor by non-acceptance or non-payment;  
(4) The right of recourse of the holder against a prior party for a further recourse shall be three months from the date of settlement or from the date the holder is sued.  
The date of issue and the date of maturity of a negotiable instrument shall be determined by the parties to the instrument according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　持票人因超过票据权利时效或者因票据记载事项欠缺而丧失票据权利的，仍享有民事权利，可以请求出票人或者承兑人返还其与未支付的票据金额相当的利益。

**Article 18** Where a holder loses his rights on a negotiable instrument due to the expiry of the limitation period for rights on the instrument or due to deficiencies in the matters recorded on the instrument, he shall still enjoy civil rights and may request the drawer or the acceptor to return an amount equivalent to the unpaid sum payable on the instrument.

### 第二章　汇票

#### 第一节　出票

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　汇票是出票人签发的，委托付款人在见票时或者在指定日期无条件支付确定的金额给收款人或者持票人的票据。  
汇票分为银行汇票和商业汇票。

**Article 19** A bill of exchange is a negotiable instrument issued by the drawer and instructing the payer to pay unconditionally a certain amount to the payee or holder at sight or on a specified date.  
Bills of exchange are divided into bank bills of exchange and commercial bills of exchange.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出票是指出票人签发票据并将其交付给收款人的票据行为。

**Article 20** Issuance means the act of a negotiable instrument where the drawer signs and delivers the instrument to the payee.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　汇票的出票人必须与付款人具有真实的委托付款关系，并且具有支付汇票金额的可靠资金来源。  
不得签发无对价的汇票用以骗取银行或者其他票据当事人的资金。

**Article 21** The drawer of a bill of exchange must have a genuine trust payment relationship with the payer and have a reliable source of funds to pay the amount of the bill.  
No bill of exchange without consideration shall be issued to defraud the funds of a bank or other parties to the negotiable instrument.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　汇票必须记载下列事项：  
（一）表明“汇票”的字样；  
（二）无条件支付的委托；  
（三）确定的金额；  
（四）付款人名称；  
（五）收款人名称；  
（六）出票日期；  
（七）出票人签章。  
汇票上未记载前款规定事项之一的，汇票无效。

**Article 22** A bill of exchange must bear the following particulars:  
(1) the words 'bill of exchange';  
(2) an unconditional order for payment;  
(3) a definite amount;  
(4) the name of the payer;  
(5) the name of the payee;  
(6) the date of issuance;  
(7) the signature of the drawer.  
If any of the particulars specified in the preceding paragraph is not recorded on the bill of exchange, the bill of exchange shall be void.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　汇票上记载付款日期、付款地、出票地等事项的，应当清楚、明确。  
汇票上未记载付款日期的，为见票即付。  
汇票上未记载付款地的，付款人的营业场所、住所或者经常居住地为付款地。  
汇票上未记载出票地的，出票人的营业场所、住所或者经常居住地为出票地。

**Article 23** The matters such as the date of payment, place of payment, and place of issuance recorded on a bill of exchange shall be clear and definite.  
If the date of payment is not recorded on the bill of exchange, it shall be payable at sight.  
If the place of payment is not recorded on the bill of exchange, the business place, domicile or habitual residence of the payer shall be the place of payment.  
If the place of issuance is not recorded on the bill of exchange, the business place, domicile or habitual residence of the drawer shall be the place of issuance.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　汇票上可以记载本法规定事项以外的其他出票事项，但是该记载事项不具有汇票上的效力。

**Article 24** Other matters concerning issuance besides those specified in this Law may be recorded on a bill of exchange, but such recorded matters shall have no effect on the bill of exchange.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　付款日期可以按照下列形式之一记载：  
（一）见票即付；  
（二）定日付款；  
（三）出票后定期付款；  
（四）见票后定期付款。  
前款规定的付款日期为汇票到期日。

**Article 25** The date of payment may be recorded in one of the following forms:  
(1) payable at sight;  
(2) payable at a fixed date;  
(3) payable at a fixed period after issuance;  
(4) payable at a fixed period after sight.  
The date of payment specified in the preceding paragraph shall be the maturity date of the bill of exchange.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　出票人签发汇票后，即承担保证该汇票承兑和付款的责任。出票人在汇票得不到承兑或者付款时，应当向持票人清偿[本法第七十条](#art-70)、第七十一条规定的金额和费用。

**Article 26** After a drawer issues a bill of exchange, he shall assume the liability to guarantee the acceptance and payment of the bill. Where the bill of exchange is not accepted or paid, the drawer shall pay to the holder the amount and expenses as specified in Articles 70 and 71 of this Law.

#### 第二节　背书

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　持票人可以将汇票权利转让给他人或者将一定的汇票权利授予他人行使。  
出票人在汇票上记载“不得转让”字样的，汇票不得转让。  
持票人行使第一款规定的权利时，应当背书并交付汇票。  
背书是指在票据背面或者粘单上记载有关事项并签章的票据行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-48" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-49" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第49条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-53" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第53条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 27** The holder may transfer the rights under a bill of exchange to another person or authorize another person to exercise certain rights under the bill.  
If the drawer records the words 'non-negotiable' on the bill, the bill shall not be negotiated.  
When the holder exercises the right specified in the first paragraph, he shall endorse and deliver the bill.  
Endorsement means the act of negotiable instrument where relevant matters are recorded and signed on the back of the instrument or on an allonge.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　票据凭证不能满足背书人记载事项的需要，可以加附粘单，粘附于票据凭证上。  
粘单上的第一记载人，应当在汇票和粘单的粘接处签章。

**Article 28** If the instrument paper cannot meet the needs of the endorser for recording matters, an allonge may be attached to the instrument paper.  
The first person who records on the allonge shall sign at the juncture where the bill of exchange and the allonge are attached.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　背书由背书人签章并记载背书日期。  
背书未记载日期的，视为在汇票到期日前背书。

**Article 29** An endorsement shall be signed by the endorser and the date of endorsement shall be recorded.  
If the date is not recorded in the endorsement, the endorsement shall be deemed to have been made before the maturity date of the bill of exchange.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　汇票以背书转让或者以背书将一定的汇票权利授予他人行使时，必须记载被背书人名称。

**Article 30** When a bill of exchange is transferred by endorsement or certain rights under a bill are granted to another person by endorsement, the name of the endorsee must be recorded.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　以背书转让的汇票，背书应当连续。持票人以背书的连续，证明其汇票权利；非经背书转让，而以其他合法方式取得汇票的，依法举证，证明其汇票权利。  
前款所称背书连续，是指在票据转让中，转让汇票的背书人与受让汇票的被背书人在汇票上的签章依次前后衔接。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-50" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 31** For a bill of exchange transferred by endorsement, the endorsements shall be consecutive. The holder shall prove his rights under the bill by the continuity of endorsements; if a bill is obtained not by endorsement but by other lawful means, the holder shall provide evidence according to law to prove his rights under the bill.  
'Consecutive endorsements' as referred to in the preceding paragraph means that in the transfer of a negotiable instrument, the signatures of the endorser transferring the bill and the endorsee receiving the bill on the instrument are successively linked.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　以背书转让的汇票，后手应当对其直接前手背书的真实性负责。  
后手是指在票据签章人之后签章的其他票据债务人。

**Article 32** For a bill of exchange transferred by endorsement, a subsequent party shall be responsible for the authenticity of the endorsement of his immediate prior party.  
'Subsequent party' means other obligors of the negotiable instrument who sign after the signer of the instrument.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　背书不得附有条件。背书时附有条件的，所附条件不具有汇票上的效力。  
将汇票金额的一部分转让的背书或者将汇票金额分别转让给二人以上的背书无效。

**Article 33** An endorsement shall not be conditional. If an endorsement is made with conditions, the conditions attached shall have no effect on the bill of exchange.  
An endorsement that transfers a part of the amount of the bill or transfers the amount of the bill to two or more persons separately shall be void.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　背书人在汇票上记载“不得转让”字样，其后手再背书转让的，原背书人对后手的被背书人不承担保证责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-51" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-54" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第54条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 34** If an endorser records the words 'non-negotiable' on a bill of exchange, and his subsequent party further endorses and transfers it, the original endorser shall not bear the liability of guarantee to the endorsee of the subsequent party.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　背书记载“委托收款”字样的，被背书人有权代背书人行使被委托的汇票权利。但是，被背书人不得再以背书转让汇票权利。  
汇票可以设定质押；质押时应当以背书记载“质押”字样。被背书人依法实现其质权时，可以行使汇票权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-55" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** If the words 'collection on behalf' are recorded in the endorsement, the endorsee shall have the right to exercise the entrusted rights under the bill on behalf of the endorser. However, the endorsee shall not further transfer the rights under the bill by endorsement.  
A bill of exchange may be pledged; when it is pledged, the words 'pledge' shall be recorded in the endorsement. When the endorsee realizes his pledge right according to law, he may exercise the rights under the bill.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　汇票被拒绝承兑、被拒绝付款或者超过付款提示期限的，不得背书转让；背书转让的，背书人应当承担汇票责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-58" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第58条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 36** If a bill of exchange is dishonored by non-acceptance or non-payment, or exceeds the time limit for presentment for payment, it shall not be transferred by endorsement; if it is transferred by endorsement, the endorser shall bear liability on the bill.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　背书人以背书转让汇票后，即承担保证其后手所持汇票承兑和付款的责任。背书人在汇票得不到承兑或者付款时，应当向持票人清偿[本法第七十条](#art-70)、第七十一条规定的金额和费用。

**Article 37** After an endorser transfers a bill of exchange by endorsement, he shall assume the liability to guarantee the acceptance and payment of the bill held by his subsequent party. Where the bill of exchange is not accepted or paid, the endorser shall pay to the holder the amount and expenses as specified in Articles 70 and 71 of this Law.

#### 第三节　承兑

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　承兑是指汇票付款人承诺在汇票到期日支付汇票金额的票据行为。

**Article 38** Acceptance means the act of negotiable instrument where the payer of a bill of exchange promises to pay the amount of the bill on its maturity date.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　定日付款或者出票后定期付款的汇票，持票人应当在汇票到期日前向付款人提示承兑。  
提示承兑是指持票人向付款人出示汇票，并要求付款人承诺付款的行为。

**Article 39** For a bill payable on a fixed date or payable at a fixed period after the date of issue, the holder shall present the bill to the drawee for acceptance before the maturity date of the bill.  
Presenting for acceptance means the act of the holder showing the bill to the drawee and requesting the drawee to promise to pay.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　见票后定期付款的汇票，持票人应当自出票日起一个月内向付款人提示承兑。  
汇票未按照规定期限提示承兑的，持票人丧失对其前手的追索权。  
见票即付的汇票无需提示承兑。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 40** For a bill payable at a fixed period after sight, the holder shall present the bill to the drawee for acceptance within one month from the date of issue.  
If the bill is not presented for acceptance within the prescribed time limit, the holder loses the right of recourse against the prior parties thereof.  
A bill payable at sight need not be presented for acceptance.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　付款人对向其提示承兑的汇票，应当自收到提示承兑的汇票之日起三日内承兑或者拒绝承兑。  
付款人收到持票人提示承兑的汇票时，应当向持票人签发收到汇票的回单。回单上应当记明汇票提示承兑日期并签章。

**Article 41** The drawee of a bill presented for acceptance shall accept or reject acceptance within three days from the date of receipt of the bill presented for acceptance.  
When the drawee receives the bill presented for acceptance by the holder, the drawee shall issue a receipt for the bill to the holder. The receipt shall indicate the date of presentment for acceptance and be signed and sealed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　付款人承兑汇票的，应当在汇票正面记载“承兑”字样和承兑日期并签章；见票后定期付款的汇票，应当在承兑时记载付款日期。  
汇票上未记载承兑日期的，以前条第一款规定期限的最后一日为承兑日期。

**Article 42** If the drawee accepts the bill, the drawee shall record the word "accepted" and the date of acceptance on the face of the bill and sign and seal it; for a bill payable at a fixed period after sight, the date of payment shall be recorded at the time of acceptance.  
If the date of acceptance is not recorded on the bill, the last day of the time limit prescribed in paragraph 1 of the preceding article shall be deemed the date of acceptance.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　付款人承兑汇票，不得附有条件；承兑附有条件的，视为拒绝承兑。

**Article 43** The drawee's acceptance of a bill shall not be conditional; if the acceptance is conditional, it shall be deemed a rejection of acceptance.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　付款人承兑汇票后，应当承担到期付款的责任。

**Article 44** After accepting a bill, the drawee shall bear the liability to pay the amount at maturity.

#### 第四节　保证

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　汇票的债务可以由保证人承担保证责任。  
保证人由汇票债务人以外的他人担当。

**Article 45** The obligation on a bill may be guaranteed by a guarantor.  
The guarantor shall be a person other than the obligor on the bill.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　保证人必须在汇票或者粘单上记载下列事项：  
（一）表明“保证”的字样；  
（二）保证人名称和住所；  
（三）被保证人的名称；  
（四）保证日期；  
（五）保证人签章。

**Article 46** The guarantor must record the following particulars on the bill or the allonge:  
(1) the word "guaranteed";  
(2) the name and domicile of the guarantor;  
(3) the name of the guaranteed party;  
(4) the date of the guarantee;  
(5) the signature and seal of the guarantor.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　保证人在汇票或者粘单上未记载前条第（三）项的，已承兑的汇票，承兑人为被保证人；未承兑的汇票，出票人为被保证人。  
保证人在汇票或者粘单上未记载前条第（四）项的，出票日期为保证日期。

**Article 47** If the guarantor does not record the particulars specified in item (3) of the preceding article on the bill or the allonge, for an accepted bill, the acceptor shall be the guaranteed party; for an unaccepted bill, the drawer shall be the guaranteed party.  
If the guarantor does not record the particulars specified in item (4) of the preceding article on the bill or the allonge, the date of issue shall be the date of the guarantee.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　保证不得附有条件；附有条件的，不影响对汇票的保证责任。

**Article 48** A guarantee shall not be conditional; if it is conditional, the guarantor's liability on the bill shall not be affected.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　保证人对合法取得汇票的持票人所享有的汇票权利，承担保证责任。但是，被保证人的债务因汇票记载事项欠缺而无效的除外。

**Article 49** A guarantor shall bear the guarantee liability for the rights on the bill enjoyed by the holder who has lawfully acquired the bill, except where the obligation of the guaranteed party is null and void due to a defect in the items recorded on the bill.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　被保证的汇票，保证人应当与被保证人对持票人承担连带责任。汇票到期后得不到付款的，持票人有权向保证人请求付款，保证人应当足额付款。

**Article 50** For a guaranteed bill, the guarantor shall bear joint and several liability with the guaranteed party to the holder. If the bill is not paid at maturity, the holder shall be entitled to demand payment from the guarantor, and the guarantor shall make full payment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　保证人为二人以上的，保证人之间承担连带责任。

**Article 51** If there are two or more guarantors, the guarantors shall bear joint and several liability among themselves.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　保证人清偿汇票债务后，可以行使持票人对被保证人及其前手的追索权。

**Article 52** After the guarantor discharges the obligation on the bill, the guarantor may exercise the right of recourse of the holder against the guaranteed party and the prior parties thereof.

#### 第五节　付款

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　持票人应当按照下列期限提示付款：  
（一）见票即付的汇票，自出票日起一个月内向付款人提示付款；  
（二）定日付款、出票后定期付款或者见票后定期付款的汇票，自到期日起十日内向承兑人提示付款。  
持票人未按照前款规定期限提示付款的，在作出说明后，承兑人或者付款人仍应当继续对持票人承担付款责任。  
通过委托收款银行或者通过票据交换系统向付款人提示付款的，视同持票人提示付款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-59" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 53** The holder shall present the bill for payment within the following time limits:  
(1) for a bill payable at sight, present it to the drawee for payment within one month from the date of issue;  
(2) for a bill payable on a fixed date, payable at a fixed period after the date of issue, or payable at a fixed period after sight, present it to the acceptor for payment within ten days from the date of maturity.  
If the holder fails to present the bill for payment within the time limit prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the acceptor or drawee shall still continue to bear the liability for payment to the holder after the holder makes an explanation.  
Presentment for payment to the drawee through a collecting bank or through a bill clearing system shall be deemed presentment for payment by the holder.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　持票人依照前条规定提示付款的，付款人必须在当日足额付款。

**Article 54** If the holder presents the bill for payment in accordance with the preceding article, the drawee shall make full payment on the same day.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　持票人获得付款的，应当在汇票上签收，并将汇票交给付款人。持票人委托银行收款的，受委托的银行将代收的汇票金额转账收入持票人账户，视同签收。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/行政法规/税务财政/票据管理实施办法.md#art-25" title="被《票据管理实施办法》第25条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 55** After obtaining payment, the holder shall sign a receipt on the bill and deliver the bill to the drawee. If the holder entrusts a bank to collect payment, the entrusted bank's transfer of the collected bill amount into the holder's account shall be deemed a signed receipt.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　持票人委托的收款银行的责任，限于按照汇票上记载事项将汇票金额转入持票人账户。  
付款人委托的付款银行的责任，限于按照汇票上记载事项从付款人账户支付汇票金额。

**Article 56** The liability of the collecting bank entrusted by the holder shall be limited to transferring the bill amount into the holder's account in accordance with the items recorded on the bill.  
The liability of the paying bank entrusted by the drawee shall be limited to paying the bill amount from the drawee's account in accordance with the items recorded on the bill.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　付款人及其代理付款人付款时，应当审查汇票背书的连续，并审查提示付款人的合法身份证明或者有效证件。  
付款人及其代理付款人以恶意或者有重大过失付款的，应当自行承担责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-52" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-69" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第69条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-70" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第70条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 57** When making payment, the drawee and its agent for payment shall examine the continuity of the endorsements on the bill and also examine the legal identity certificate or valid document of the person presenting the bill for payment.  
If the drawee or its agent for payment makes payment with malice or gross negligence, they shall bear the liability themselves.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　对定日付款、出票后定期付款或者见票后定期付款的汇票，付款人在到期日前付款的，由付款人自行承担所产生的责任。

**Article 58** For a bill payable on a fixed date, payable at a fixed period after the date of issue, or payable at a fixed period after sight, if the drawee makes payment before the maturity date, the drawee shall bear the liability arising therefrom.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　汇票金额为外币的，按照付款日的市场汇价，以人民币支付。  
汇票当事人对汇票支付的货币种类另有约定的，从其约定。

**Article 59** The amount of a bill of exchange denominated in foreign currency shall be paid in RMB according to the market exchange rate on the date of payment.  
If the parties to the bill have otherwise agreed on the currency for payment, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　付款人依法足额付款后，全体汇票债务人的责任解除。

**Article 60** After the drawee pays the full amount according to law, the liability of all obligors on the bill of exchange shall be discharged.

#### 第六节　追索权

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　汇票到期被拒绝付款的，持票人可以对背书人、出票人以及汇票的其他债务人行使追索权。  
汇票到期日前，有下列情形之一的，持票人也可以行使追索权：  
（一）汇票被拒绝承兑的；  
（二）承兑人或者付款人死亡、逃匿的；  
（三）承兑人或者付款人被依法宣告破产的或者因违法被责令终止业务活动的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 61** If a bill of exchange is dishonored by non-payment at maturity, the holder may exercise the right of recourse against the endorsers, the drawer, and other obligors on the bill.  
Before the maturity date of the bill of exchange, the holder may also exercise the right of recourse under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the bill is dishonored by non-acceptance;  
(2) the acceptor or the drawee dies or absconds;  
(3) the acceptor or the drawee is declared bankrupt according to law or is ordered to terminate its business activities due to illegal acts.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　持票人行使追索权时，应当提供被拒绝承兑或者被拒绝付款的有关证明。  
持票人提示承兑或者提示付款被拒绝的，承兑人或者付款人必须出具拒绝证明，或者出具退票理由书。未出具拒绝证明或者退票理由书的，应当承担由此产生的民事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/行政法规/税务财政/票据管理实施办法.md#art-27" title="被《票据管理实施办法》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 62** When exercising the right of recourse, the holder shall provide relevant evidence of dishonor by non-acceptance or non-payment.  
If the holder's presentation for acceptance or for payment is dishonored, the acceptor or the drawee must issue a certificate of dishonor or a statement of reasons for dishonor. If it fails to issue a certificate of dishonor or a statement of reasons for dishonor, it shall bear the civil liability arising therefrom.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　持票人因承兑人或者付款人死亡、逃匿或者其他原因，不能取得拒绝证明的，可以依法取得其他有关证明。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-71" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第71条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/行政法规/税务财政/票据管理实施办法.md#art-28" title="被《票据管理实施办法》第28条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 63** If the holder is unable to obtain a certificate of dishonor due to the death, abscondence, or other reasons of the acceptor or the drawee, the holder may obtain other relevant evidence according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　承兑人或者付款人被人民法院依法宣告破产的，人民法院的有关司法文书具有拒绝证明的效力。  
承兑人或者付款人因违法被责令终止业务活动的，有关行政主管部门的处罚决定具有拒绝证明的效力。

**Article 64** If the acceptor or the drawee is declared bankrupt according to law by a People's Court, the relevant judicial documents of the People's Court shall have the effect of a certificate of dishonor.  
If the acceptor or the drawee is ordered to terminate its business activities due to illegal acts, the punishment decision of the relevant administrative department shall have the effect of a certificate of dishonor.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　持票人不能出示拒绝证明、退票理由书或者未按照规定期限提供其他合法证明的，丧失对其前手的追索权。但是，承兑人或者付款人仍应当对持票人承担责任。

**Article 65** If the holder fails to present a certificate of dishonor, a statement of reasons for dishonor, or fails to provide other lawful evidence within the prescribed time limit, the holder shall lose the right of recourse against its prior parties. However, the acceptor or the drawee shall still be liable to the holder.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　持票人应当自收到被拒绝承兑或者被拒绝付款的有关证明之日起三日内，将被拒绝事由书面通知其前手；其前手应当自收到通知之日起三日内书面通知其再前手。持票人也可以同时向各汇票债务人发出书面通知。  
未按照前款规定期限通知的，持票人仍可以行使追索权。因延期通知给其前手或者出票人造成损失的，由没有按照规定期限通知的汇票当事人，承担对该损失的赔偿责任，但是所赔偿的金额以汇票金额为限。  
在规定期限内将通知按照法定地址或者约定的地址邮寄的，视为已经发出通知。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 66** The holder shall, within three days from the date of receipt of the relevant evidence of dishonor by non-acceptance or non-payment, notify its prior party in writing of the fact of dishonor; the prior party shall, within three days from the date of receipt of the notice, notify its own prior party in writing. The holder may also send a written notice to all obligors on the bill at the same time.  
If the holder fails to give notice within the time limit prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the holder may still exercise the right of recourse. If any loss is caused to the prior party or the drawer due to delay in giving notice, the party to the bill that failed to give notice within the prescribed time limit shall be liable for compensation for such loss, provided that the amount of compensation shall not exceed the amount of the bill.  
If the notice is sent by mail within the prescribed time limit to the legal address or the agreed address, it shall be deemed that the notice has been given.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　依照前条第一款所作的书面通知，应当记明汇票的主要记载事项，并说明该汇票已被退票。

**Article 67** The written notice made in accordance with the first paragraph of the preceding article shall specify the main particulars of the bill of exchange and state that the bill has been dishonored.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　汇票的出票人、背书人、承兑人和保证人对持票人承担连带责任。  
持票人可以不按照汇票债务人的先后顺序，对其中任何一人、数人或者全体行使追索权。  
持票人对汇票债务人中的一人或者数人已经进行追索的，对其他汇票债务人仍可以行使追索权。被追索人清偿债务后，与持票人享有同一权利。

**Article 68** The drawer, endorsers, acceptor, and guarantors of a bill of exchange shall bear joint and several liability to the holder.  
The holder may exercise the right of recourse against any one, several, or all of the obligors on the bill, regardless of their order.  
If the holder has already exercised recourse against one or several of the obligors on the bill, the holder may still exercise recourse against other obligors on the bill. After the party against whom recourse is taken discharges the debt, it shall have the same rights as the holder.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　持票人为出票人的，对其前手无追索权。持票人为背书人的，对其后手无追索权。

**Article 69** If the holder is the drawer, the holder has no right of recourse against its prior parties. If the holder is an endorser, the holder has no right of recourse against its subsequent parties.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　持票人行使追索权，可以请求被追索人支付下列金额和费用：  
（一）被拒绝付款的汇票金额；  
（二）汇票金额自到期日或者提示付款日起至清偿日止，按照中国人民银行规定的利率计算的利息；  
（三）取得有关拒绝证明和发出通知书的费用。  
被追索人清偿债务时，持票人应当交出汇票和有关拒绝证明，并出具所收到利息和费用的收据。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第22条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/行政法规/税务财政/票据管理实施办法.md#art-29" title="被《票据管理实施办法》第29条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 70** When exercising the right of recourse, the holder may request the party against whom recourse is taken to pay the following amounts and expenses:  
(1) the amount of the bill that has been dishonored by non-payment;  
(2) interest on the amount of the bill calculated at the interest rate prescribed by the People's Bank of China from the date of maturity or the date of presentment for payment to the date of payment;  
(3) the expenses for obtaining the relevant certificate of dishonor and for sending the notice.  
When the party against whom recourse is taken discharges the debt, the holder shall deliver the bill and the relevant certificate of dishonor, and issue a receipt for the interest and expenses received.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　被追索人依照前条规定清偿后，可以向其他汇票债务人行使再追索权，请求其他汇票债务人支付下列金额和费用：  
（一）已清偿的全部金额；  
（二）前项金额自清偿日起至再追索清偿日止，按照中国人民银行规定的利率计算的利息；  
（三）发出通知书的费用。  
行使再追索权的被追索人获得清偿时，应当交出汇票和有关拒绝证明，并出具所收到利息和费用的收据。

**Article 71** After the party against whom recourse is taken discharges the debt in accordance with the preceding article, it may exercise a right of further recourse against other obligors on the bill, requesting them to pay the following amounts and expenses:  
(1) the total amount already discharged;  
(2) interest on the amount mentioned in item (1) calculated at the interest rate prescribed by the People's Bank of China from the date of discharge to the date of discharge of the further recourse;  
(3) the expenses for sending the notice.  
When the party exercising the right of further recourse obtains satisfaction, it shall deliver the bill and the relevant certificate of dishonor, and issue a receipt for the interest and expenses received.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　被追索人依照前二条规定清偿债务后，其责任解除。

**Article 72** After the party against whom recourse is taken discharges the debt in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two articles, its liability shall be discharged.

### 第三章　本票

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本票是出票人签发的，承诺自己在见票时无条件支付确定的金额给收款人或者持票人的票据。  
本法所称本票，是指银行本票。

**Article 73** A promissory note is a negotiable instrument issued by the maker promising to pay unconditionally a certain sum to the payee or the holder on sight.  
For the purpose of this Law, promissory note refers to a bank promissory note.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本票的出票人必须具有支付本票金额的可靠资金来源，并保证支付。

**Article 74** The maker of a promissory note must have a reliable source of funds to pay the sum payable on the promissory note and shall guarantee payment.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本票必须记载下列事项：  
（一）表明“本票”的字样；  
（二）无条件支付的承诺；  
（三）确定的金额；  
（四）收款人名称；  
（五）出票日期；  
（六）出票人签章。  
本票上未记载前款规定事项之一的，本票无效。

**Article 75** A promissory note must record the following matters:  
(1) the words indicating a "promissory note";  
(2) an unconditional promise to pay;  
(3) a definite amount;  
(4) the name of the payee;  
(5) the date of issue;  
(6) the signature or seal of the maker.  
If a promissory note fails to record any of the matters prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the promissory note shall be invalid.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　本票上记载付款地、出票地等事项的，应当清楚、明确。  
本票上未记载付款地的，出票人的营业场所为付款地。  
本票上未记载出票地的，出票人的营业场所为出票地。

**Article 76** If a promissory note records matters such as the place of payment or the place of issue, such recording shall be clear and definite.  
If the place of payment is not recorded on the promissory note, the business place of the maker shall be the place of payment.  
If the place of issue is not recorded on the promissory note, the business place of the maker shall be the place of issue.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本票的出票人在持票人提示见票时，必须承担付款的责任。

**Article 77** The maker of a promissory note must bear the liability to pay when the holder presents the note for sight.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　本票自出票日起，付款期限最长不得超过二个月。

**Article 78** The time limit for payment of a promissory note shall not exceed two months from the date of issue.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　本票的持票人未按照规定期限提示见票的，丧失对出票人以外的前手的追索权。

**Article 79** If the holder of a promissory note fails to present the note for sight within the prescribed time limit, the holder shall lose the right of recourse against any prior party other than the maker.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本票的背书、保证、付款行为和追索权的行使，除本章规定外，适用本法第二章有关汇票的规定。  
本票的出票行为，除本章规定外，适用[本法第二十四条](#art-24)关于汇票的规定。

**Article 80** The endorsement, guarantee, payment and exercise of the right of recourse on a promissory note, except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter II of this Law concerning bills of exchange.  
The making of a promissory note, except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, shall be governed by the provisions of Article 24 of this Law concerning bills of exchange.

### 第四章　支票

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　支票是出票人签发的，委托办理支票存款业务的银行或者其他金融机构在见票时无条件支付确定的金额给收款人或者持票人的票据。

**Article 81** A check is a bill issued by the drawer authorizing a bank or other financial institution handling check deposit business to pay unconditionally a definite amount to the payee or the holder at sight.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　开立支票存款账户，申请人必须使用其本名，并提交证明其身份的合法证件。  
开立支票存款账户和领用支票，应当有可靠的资信，并存入一定的资金。  
开立支票存款账户，申请人应当预留其本名的签名式样和印鉴。

**Article 82** When opening a check deposit account, the applicant must use his or her real name and submit legal documents certifying his or her identity.  
To open a check deposit account and obtain checks, the applicant shall have reliable credit standing and deposit a certain amount of funds.  
When opening a check deposit account, the applicant shall leave a specimen of his or her signature and seal of his or her real name on file.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　支票可以支取现金，也可以转账，用于转账时，应当在支票正面注明。  
支票中专门用于支取现金的，可以另行制作现金支票，现金支票只能用于支取现金。  
支票中专门用于转账的，可以另行制作转账支票，转账支票只能用于转账，不得支取现金。

**Article 83** A check may be used to withdraw cash or to transfer accounts. When it is used for transfer, this fact shall be indicated on the face of the check.  
A check specifically used for cash withdrawal may be separately made as a cash check, and a cash check may only be used to withdraw cash.  
A check specifically used for transfer may be separately made as a transfer check, and a transfer check may only be used for transfer and shall not be used to withdraw cash.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　支票必须记载下列事项：  
（一）表明“支票”的字样；  
（二）无条件支付的委托；  
（三）确定的金额；  
（四）付款人名称；  
（五）出票日期；  
（六）出票人签章。  
支票上未记载前款规定事项之一的，支票无效。

**Article 84** A check must record the following matters:  
(1) the words indicating a "check";  
(2) an unconditional authorization to pay;  
(3) a definite amount;  
(4) the name of the drawee;  
(5) the date of issue;  
(6) the signature or seal of the drawer.  
If a check fails to record any of the matters prescribed in the preceding paragraph, the check shall be invalid.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　支票上的金额可以由出票人授权补记，未补记前的支票，不得使用。

**Article 85** The amount on a check may be made up by the drawer’s authorization. A check before the amount is made up shall not be used.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　支票上未记载收款人名称的，经出票人授权，可以补记。  
支票上未记载付款地的，付款人的营业场所为付款地。  
支票上未记载出票地的，出票人的营业场所、住所或者经常居住地为出票地。  
出票人可以在支票上记载自己为收款人。

**Article 86** If the name of the payee is not recorded on a check, it may be made up upon authorization of the drawer.  
If the place of payment is not recorded on a check, the business place of the drawee shall be the place of payment.  
If the place of issue is not recorded on a check, the business place, domicile or habitual residence of the drawer shall be the place of issue.  
The drawer may record himself or herself as the payee on the check.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　支票的出票人所签发的支票金额不得超过其付款时在付款人处实有的存款金额。  
出票人签发的支票金额超过其付款时在付款人处实有的存款金额的，为空头支票。禁止签发空头支票。

**Article 87** The amount of a check issued by the drawer shall not exceed the actual deposit balance at the drawee’s bank at the time of payment.  
If the amount of a check issued by the drawer exceeds the actual deposit balance at the drawee’s bank at the time of payment, the check is a rubber check. It is prohibited to issue a rubber check.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　支票的出票人不得签发与其预留本名的签名式样或者印鉴不符的支票。

**Article 88** The drawer of a check shall not issue a check which is not in conformity with the specimen of his or her signature or seal of his or her real name left on file.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　出票人必须按照签发的支票金额承担保证向该持票人付款的责任。  
出票人在付款人处的存款足以支付支票金额时，付款人应当在当日足额付款。

**Article 89** The drawer must bear the liability to guarantee payment to the holder according to the amount of the check issued.  
When the drawer’s deposit at the drawee’s bank is sufficient to pay the check amount, the drawee shall pay the full amount on the same day.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　支票限于见票即付，不得另行记载付款日期。另行记载付款日期的，该记载无效。

**Article 90** A check is limited to payment at sight. No other date of payment shall be additionally recorded. If a date of payment is additionally recorded, such recording shall be invalid.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　支票的持票人应当自出票日起十日内提示付款；异地使用的支票，其提示付款的期限由中国人民银行另行规定。  
超过提示付款期限的，付款人可以不予付款；付款人不予付款的，出票人仍应当对持票人承担票据责任。

**Article 91** The holder of a check shall present the check for payment within ten days from the date of issue. For checks used in different places, the time limit for presentment for payment shall be separately prescribed by the People's Bank of China.  
If the time limit for presentment for payment is exceeded, the drawee may refuse to pay. If the drawee refuses to pay, the drawer shall still bear the instrument liability to the holder.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　付款人依法支付支票金额的，对出票人不再承担受委托付款的责任，对持票人不再承担付款的责任。但是，付款人以恶意或者有重大过失付款的除外。

**Article 92** If the drawee pays the check amount according to law, the drawee shall no longer bear the liability to be entrusted to pay to the drawer, and shall no longer bear the liability to pay to the holder. However, this shall not apply if the drawee pays with malice or gross negligence.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　支票的背书、付款行为和追索权的行使，除本章规定外，适用本法第二章有关汇票的规定。  
支票的出票行为，除本章规定外，适用[本法第二十四条](#art-24)、第二十六条关于汇票的规定。

**Article 93** The endorsement, payment and exercise of the right of recourse on a check, except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter II of this Law concerning bills of exchange.  
The issuance of a check, except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, shall be governed by the provisions of Articles 24 and 26 of this Law concerning bills of exchange.

### 第五章　涉外票据的法律适用

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　涉外票据的法律适用，依照本章的规定确定。  
前款所称涉外票据，是指出票、背书、承兑、保证、付款等行为中，既有发生在中华人民共和国境内又有发生在中华人民共和国境外的票据。

**Article 94** The application of law to negotiable instruments involving foreign elements shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.  
"Negotiable instruments involving foreign elements" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to negotiable instruments in which the acts of issuance, endorsement, acceptance, guarantee and payment occur both inside and outside the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定。但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。  
本法和中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约没有规定的，可以适用国际惯例。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理涉外民商事案件适用国际条约和国际惯例若干问题的解释.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉外民商事案件适用国际条约和国际惯例若干问题的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 95** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply, except those clauses to which the People's Republic of China has declared reservation.  
Where neither this Law nor any international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions, international practice may apply.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　票据债务人的民事行为能力，适用其本国法律。  
票据债务人的民事行为能力，依照其本国法律为无民事行为能力或者为限制民事行为能力而依照行为地法律为完全民事行为能力的，适用行为地法律。

**Article 96** The capacity for civil acts of a negotiable instrument debtor shall be governed by the law of his own country.  
Where the capacity for civil acts of a negotiable instrument debtor is governed by the law of his own country as having no or limited capacity for civil acts but is governed by the law of the place of act as having full capacity for civil acts, the law of the place of act shall apply.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　汇票、本票出票时的记载事项，适用出票地法律。  
支票出票时的记载事项，适用出票地法律，经当事人协议，也可以适用付款地法律。

**Article 97** The particulars recorded at the time of drawing of a bill of exchange or a promissory note shall be governed by the law of the place of drawing.  
The particulars recorded at the time of drawing of a cheque shall be governed by the law of the place of drawing, but may also be governed by the law of the place of payment upon agreement of the parties.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　票据的背书、承兑、付款和保证行为，适用行为地法律。

**Article 98** The acts of endorsement, acceptance, payment and guarantee of a negotiable instrument shall be governed by the law of the place where the act is performed.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　票据追索权的行使期限，适用出票地法律。

**Article 99** The time limit for exercising the right of recourse on a negotiable instrument shall be governed by the law of the place of drawing.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　票据的提示期限、有关拒绝证明的方式、出具拒绝证明的期限，适用付款地法律。

**Article 100** The time limit for presentation of a negotiable instrument, the manner of certification of dishonor, and the time limit for issuing a certificate of dishonor shall be governed by the law of the place of payment.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　票据丧失时，失票人请求保全票据权利的程序，适用付款地法律。

**Article 101** When a negotiable instrument is lost, the procedures for the person who loses the instrument to apply for preservation of the rights under the negotiable instrument shall be governed by the law of the place of payment.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　有下列票据欺诈行为之一的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）伪造、变造票据的；  
（二）故意使用伪造、变造的票据的；  
（三）签发空头支票或者故意签发与其预留的本名签名式样或者印鉴不符的支票，骗取财物的；  
（四）签发无可靠资金来源的汇票、本票，骗取资金的；  
（五）汇票、本票的出票人在出票时作虚假记载，骗取财物的；  
（六）冒用他人的票据，或者故意使用过期或者作废的票据，骗取财物的；  
（七）付款人同出票人、持票人恶意串通，实施前六项所列行为之一的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/行政法规/税务财政/票据管理实施办法.md#art-30" title="被《票据管理实施办法》第30条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 102** Whoever commits any of the following acts of fraud involving negotiable instruments shall, according to law, be investigated for criminal liability:  
(1) forging or altering a negotiable instrument;  
(2) intentionally using a forged or altered negotiable instrument;  
(3) drawing a cheque against insufficient funds or intentionally drawing a cheque which is inconsistent with the specimen of his signature or seal originally reserved at the bank, so as to obtain money or goods under false pretenses;  
(4) drawing a bill of exchange or a promissory note without reliable sources of funds, so as to obtain funds under false pretenses;  
(5) causing false records to be made at the time of drawing a bill of exchange or a promissory note, so as to obtain money or goods under false pretenses;  
(6) fraudulently using another person's negotiable instrument or intentionally using an overdue or cancelled negotiable instrument, so as to obtain money or goods under false pretenses;  
(7) a payer acting in malicious collusion with the drawer or the holder to commit any of the acts listed in the preceding six items.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　有前条所列行为之一，情节轻微，不构成犯罪的，依照国家有关规定给予行政处罚。

**Article 103** Where an act listed in the preceding article is minor in nature and does not constitute a crime, an administrative penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　金融机构工作人员在票据业务中玩忽职守，对违反本法规定的票据予以承兑、付款或者保证的，给予处分；造成重大损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
由于金融机构工作人员因前款行为给当事人造成损失的，由该金融机构和直接责任人员依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 104** Where an employee of a financial institution, through neglect of duty in negotiable instrument business, accepts, pays or guarantees a negotiable instrument which violates the provisions of this Law, he shall be given disciplinary sanction; if heavy losses are caused and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated against him according to law.  
Where losses are caused to a party due to the act of an employee of a financial institution as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the financial institution and the person directly responsible shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　票据的付款人对见票即付或者到期的票据，故意压票，拖延支付的，由金融行政管理部门处以罚款，对直接责任人员给予处分。  
票据的付款人故意压票，拖延支付，给持票人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-75" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第75条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 105** Where a payer of a negotiable instrument intentionally delays payment of a negotiable instrument payable at sight or at maturity, the financial administration department shall impose a fine on him and give disciplinary sanction to the person directly responsible.  
Where a payer of a negotiable instrument intentionally delays payment and causes losses to the holder, the payer shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　依照本法规定承担赔偿责任以外的其他违反本法规定的行为，给他人造成损失的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 106** Any other act violating the provisions of this Law, other than those for which compensation liability shall be borne according to the provisions of this Law, which causes losses to another person, shall be subject to civil liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　本法规定的各项期限的计算，适用民法通则关于计算期间的规定。  
按月计算期限的，按到期月的对日计算；无对日的，月末日为到期日。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-76" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第76条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 107** The calculation of the various time limits provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law concerning the calculation of time periods.  
Where a time limit is calculated by month, it shall be calculated according to the day corresponding to the due month; if there is no corresponding day, the last day of the month shall be the due date.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　汇票、本票、支票的格式应当统一。  
票据凭证的格式和印制管理办法，由中国人民银行规定。

**Article 108** The forms of bills of exchange, promissory notes and cheques shall be uniform.  
The forms of negotiable instrument vouchers and the administrative measures for printing them shall be prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　票据管理的具体实施办法，由中国人民银行依照本法制定，报国务院批准后施行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-40" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第40条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 109** The specific measures for the administration of negotiable instruments shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China in accordance with this Law, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　本法自1996年1月1日起施行。

**Article 110** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1996.
