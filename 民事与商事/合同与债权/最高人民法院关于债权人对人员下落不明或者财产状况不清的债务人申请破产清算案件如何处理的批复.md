### 最高人民法院关于债权人对人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的债务人申请破产清算案件如何处理的批复

公      告  
  
最高人民法院《关于债权人对人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的债务人申请破产清算案件如何处理的批复》已于2008年8月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1450次会议通过。现予公布，自2008年8月18日起施行。  
  
2008年8月7日  
  
最高人民法院  
  
关于债权人对人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的债务人申请破产清算案件如何处理的批复  
  
法释〔2008〕10号  
  
贵州省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于企业法人被吊销营业执照后，依法负有清算责任的人未向法院申请破产，债权人是否可以申请被吊销营业执照的企业破产的请示》（〔2007〕黔高民二破请终字1号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
债权人对人员下落不明或者财产状况不清的债务人申请破产清算，符合企业破产法规定的，人民法院应依法予以受理。债务人能否依据[企业破产法第十一条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-11)第二款的规定向人民法院提交财产状况说明、债权债务清册等相关材料，并不影响对债权人申请的受理。  
  
人民法院受理上述破产案件后，应当依据企业破产法的有关规定指定管理人追收债务人财产；经依法清算，债务人确无财产可供分配的，应当宣告债务人破产并终结破产程序；破产程序终结后二年内发现有依法应当追回的财产或者有应当供分配的其他财产的，债权人可以请求人民法院追加分配。  
  
债务人的有关人员不履行法定义务，人民法院可依据有关法律规定追究其相应法律责任；其行为导致无法清算或者造成损失，有关权利人起诉请求其承担相应民事责任的，人民法院应依法予以支持。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** August 7, 2008  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Handle Cases Where Creditors Apply for Bankruptcy Liquidation against Debtors Whose Whereabouts Are Unknown or Whose Property Status Is Unclear  
Fa Shi [2008] No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Guizhou Province:  
Your court's Request for Instruction on Whether Creditors May Apply for Bankruptcy of an Enterprise Whose Business License Has Been Revoked after the Person Liable for Liquidation According to Law Fails to Apply to the Court for Bankruptcy (〔2007〕Qian Gao Min Er Po Qing Zhong Zi No. 1) has been received. Upon review, the reply is as follows:  
When a creditor applies for bankruptcy liquidation against a debtor whose whereabouts are unknown or whose property status is unclear, if the application meets the requirements of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, the People's Court shall accept it according to law. Whether the debtor can submit to the People's Court documents such as a statement of property status and a register of claims and debts pursuant to Article 11, Paragraph 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law does not affect the acceptance of the creditor's application.  
After accepting the above bankruptcy case, the People's Court shall, pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, appoint an administrator to recover the debtor's property; after liquidation according to law, if the debtor indeed has no property available for distribution, the People's Court shall declare the debtor bankrupt and terminate the bankruptcy proceedings; within two years after the termination of the bankruptcy proceedings, if property that should be recovered according to law or other property that should be distributed is discovered, the creditor may request the People's Court to make additional distribution.  
If the relevant personnel of the debtor fail to perform their statutory obligations, the People's Court may, pursuant to relevant legal provisions, pursue their corresponding legal liability; if their actions cause the inability to liquidate or cause losses, and the relevant right holder files a lawsuit requesting them to bear corresponding civil liability, the People's Court shall support such a lawsuit according to law.  
It is so replied.
