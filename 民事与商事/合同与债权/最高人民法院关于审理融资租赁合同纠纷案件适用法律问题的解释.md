# 最高人民法院关于审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕3号  
**公布日期**：2014-02-24  
**生效日期**：2014-03-01

> 最高人民法院 《关于审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》已于2013年11月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1597次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年3月1日起施行。 2014年2月24日 最高人民法院 关于审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件适用 法律问题的解释 法释〔2014〕3号 为正确审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国物权法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 一、融资租赁合同的认定及效力 第一条　人民法院应当根据合同法第二百三十七条的规定，结合标的物的性质、价值、租金的构成以及当事人的合同权利和义务，对是否构成融资租赁法律关系作出认定。 对名为融资租赁合同，但实际不构成融资租赁法律关系的，人民法院应按照其实际构成的法律关系处理。 第二条　承租人将其自有物出卖给出租人，再通过融资租赁合同将租赁物从出租人处租回的，人民法院不应仅以承租人和出卖人系同一人为由认定不构成融资租赁法律关系。 第三条　根据法律、行政法规规定，承租人对于租赁物的经营使用应当取得行政许可的，人民法院不应仅以出租人未取得行政许可为由认定融资租赁合同无效。 第四条　融资租赁合同被认定无效，当事人就合同无效情形下租赁物归属有约定的，从其约定；未约定或者约定不明，且当事人协商不成的，租赁物应当返还出租人。但因承租人原因导致合同无效，出租人不要求返还租赁物，或者租赁物正在使用，返还出租人后会显著降低租赁物价值和效用的，人民法院可以判决租赁物所有权归承租人，并根据合同履行情况和租金支付情况，由承租人就租赁物进行折价补偿。 二、合同的履行和租赁物的公示 第五条　出卖人违反合同约定的向承租人交付标的物的义务，承租人因下列情形之一拒绝受领租赁物的，人民法院应予支持：

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院应当根据合同法第二百三十七条的规定，结合标的物的性质、价值、租金的构成以及当事人的合同权利和义务，对是否构成融资租赁法律关系作出认定。  
对名为融资租赁合同，但实际不构成融资租赁法律关系的，人民法院应按照其实际构成的法律关系处理。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 237 of the Contract Law, and in light of the nature and value of the subject matter, the composition of the rental, and the contractual rights and obligations of the parties, determine whether a financial leasing legal relationship is formed.  
For a contract named as a financial leasing contract that does not actually form a financial leasing legal relationship, the People's Court shall handle it in accordance with the legal relationship actually formed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　承租人将其自有物出卖给出租人，再通过融资租赁合同将租赁物从出租人处租回的，人民法院不应仅以承租人和出卖人系同一人为由认定不构成融资租赁法律关系。

**Article 2** Where a lessee sells its own property to a lessor and then leases the leased property back from the lessor through a financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall not determine that a financial leasing legal relationship is not formed solely on the ground that the lessee and the seller are the same person.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　根据法律、行政法规规定，承租人对于租赁物的经营使用应当取得行政许可的，人民法院不应仅以出租人未取得行政许可为由认定融资租赁合同无效。

**Article 3** Where, according to laws and administrative regulations, the lessee shall obtain an administrative license for the operation and use of the leased property, the People's Court shall not determine that the financial leasing contract is void solely on the ground that the lessor has not obtained the administrative license.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　融资租赁合同被认定无效，当事人就合同无效情形下租赁物归属有约定的，从其约定；未约定或者约定不明，且当事人协商不成的，租赁物应当返还出租人。但因承租人原因导致合同无效，出租人不要求返还租赁物，或者租赁物正在使用，返还出租人后会显著降低租赁物价值和效用的，人民法院可以判决租赁物所有权归承租人，并根据合同履行情况和租金支付情况，由承租人就租赁物进行折价补偿。  
二、合同的履行和租赁物的公示

**Article 4** Where a financial leasing contract is determined to be void, if the parties have an agreement on the attribution of the leased property under the circumstance of the contract being void, such agreement shall prevail; if there is no agreement or the agreement is unclear, and the parties fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, the leased property shall be returned to the lessor. However, if the contract is void due to the lessee's reason, the lessor does not request the return of the leased property, or the leased property is in use and its value and utility would be significantly reduced after being returned to the lessor, the People's Court may decide that the ownership of the leased property vests in the lessee, and the lessee shall make compensation by way of discount based on the leased property, taking into account the performance of the contract and the payment of the rental.  
II. Performance of the Contract and Publicity of the Leased Property

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　出卖人违反合同约定的向承租人交付标的物的义务，承租人因下列情形之一拒绝受领租赁物的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）租赁物严重不符合约定的；  
（二）出卖人未在约定的交付期间或者合理期间内交付租赁物，经承租人或者出租人催告，在催告期满后仍未交付的。  
承租人拒绝受领租赁物，未及时通知出租人，或者无正当理由拒绝受领租赁物，造成出租人损失，出租人向承租人主张损害赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where the seller violates the obligation agreed in the contract to deliver the subject matter to the lessee, and the lessee refuses to accept the leased property under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall support such refusal:  
(1) The leased property seriously fails to conform to the agreement;  
(2) The seller fails to deliver the leased property within the agreed delivery period or a reasonable period, and after being urged by the lessee or the lessor, still fails to deliver upon the expiry of the urging period.  
Where the lessee refuses to accept the leased property but fails to promptly notify the lessor, or refuses to accept the leased property without justifiable reasons, thereby causing losses to the lessor, and the lessor claims damages from the lessee, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　承租人对出卖人行使索赔权，不影响其履行融资租赁合同项下支付租金的义务，但承租人以依赖出租人的技能确定租赁物或者出租人干预选择租赁物为由，主张减轻或者免除相应租金支付义务的除外。

**Article 6** The lessee's exercise of the right of claim against the seller shall not affect its obligation to pay the rental under the financial leasing contract, unless the lessee claims to reduce or be exempted from the corresponding rental payment obligation on the ground that the lessee relied on the lessor's skill to determine the leased property or that the lessor interfered with the selection of the leased property.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承租人占有租赁物期间，租赁物毁损、灭失的风险由承租人承担，出租人要求承租人继续支付租金的，人民法院应予支持。但当事人另有约定或者法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 7** During the period when the lessee is in possession of the leased property, the risk of damage or loss of the leased property shall be borne by the lessee. Where the lessor requests the lessee to continue paying the rental, the People's Court shall support such request, unless the parties have agreed otherwise or the law provides otherwise.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出租人转让其在融资租赁合同项下的部分或者全部权利，受让方以此为由请求解除或者变更融资租赁合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** Where the lessor transfers part or all of its rights under the financial leasing contract, and the transferee requests dissolution or modification of the financial leasing contract on this ground, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承租人或者租赁物的实际使用人，未经出租人同意转让租赁物或者在租赁物上设立其他物权，第三人依据物权法第一百零六条的规定取得租赁物的所有权或者其他物权，出租人主张第三人物权权利不成立的，人民法院不予支持，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）出租人已在租赁物的显著位置作出标识，第三人在与承租人交易时知道或者应当知道该物为租赁物的；  
（二）出租人授权承租人将租赁物抵押给出租人并在登记机关依法办理抵押权登记的；  
（三）第三人与承租人交易时，未按照法律、行政法规、行业或者地区主管部门的规定在相应机构进行融资租赁交易查询的；  
（四）出租人有证据证明第三人知道或者应当知道交易标的物为租赁物的其他情形。

**Article 9** Where a lessee or the actual user of the leased property, without the lessor's consent, transfers the leased property or creates any other real right on it, and a third party acquires the ownership or other real right of the leased property in accordance with Article 106 of the Property Law, and the lessor claims that the third party's real right is not established, the People's Court shall not support such claim, except under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The lessor has made a mark in a conspicuous position on the leased property, and the third party knew or should have known at the time of the transaction with the lessee that the property was a leased property;  
(2) The lessor authorized the lessee to mortgage the leased property to the lessor and registered the mortgage right with the registration authority in accordance with the law;  
(3) The third party, when transacting with the lessee, failed to conduct a financial leasing transaction inquiry at the relevant institution in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, industry or regional competent authorities;  
(4) Other circumstances where the lessor has evidence to prove that the third party knew or should have known that the subject matter of the transaction was a leased property.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人约定租赁期间届满后租赁物归出租人的，因租赁物毁损、灭失或者附合、混同于他物导致承租人不能返还，出租人要求其给予合理补偿的，人民法院应予支持。  
三、合同的解除

**Article 10** Where the parties agree that after the expiration of the lease term, the leased property shall belong to the lessor, and the lessee cannot return it due to damage, loss, attachment to or commingling with other property, and the lessor requests the lessee to make reasonable compensation, the People's Court shall support such request.  
III. Dissolution of the Contract

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一，出租人或者承租人请求解除融资租赁合同的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）出租人与出卖人订立的买卖合同解除、被确认无效或者被撤销，且双方未能重新订立买卖合同的；  
（二）租赁物因不可归责于双方的原因意外毁损、灭失，且不能修复或者确定替代物的；  
（三）因出卖人的原因致使融资租赁合同的目的不能实现的。

**Article 11** Under any of the following circumstances, where the lessor or the lessee requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The sale contract concluded between the lessor and the seller is dissolved, confirmed as void, or revoked, and the parties fail to conclude a new sale contract;  
(2) The leased property is accidentally damaged or lost for reasons not attributable to either party, and cannot be repaired or a substitute cannot be determined;  
(3) The purpose of the financial leasing contract cannot be achieved due to the seller's reasons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一，出租人请求解除融资租赁合同的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）承租人未经出租人同意，将租赁物转让、转租、抵押、质押、投资入股或者以其他方式处分租赁物的；  
（二）承租人未按照合同约定的期限和数额支付租金，符合合同约定的解除条件，经出租人催告后在合理期限内仍不支付的；  
（三）合同对于欠付租金解除合同的情形没有明确约定，但承租人欠付租金达到两期以上，或者数额达到全部租金百分之十五以上，经出租人催告后在合理期限内仍不支付的；  
（四）承租人违反合同约定，致使合同目的不能实现的其他情形。

**Article 12** Under any of the following circumstances, where the lessor requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The lessee, without the lessor's consent, transfers, subleases, mortgages, pledges, invests as capital, or otherwise disposes of the leased property;  
(2) The lessee fails to pay the rental in the amount and within the time limit agreed in the contract, and the conditions for dissolution agreed in the contract are met, and after being urged by the lessor, the lessee still fails to pay within a reasonable period;  
(3) The contract does not clearly specify the circumstances for dissolution due to arrears of rental, but the lessee is in arrears for two or more periods, or the amount in arrears reaches 15% or more of the total rental, and after being urged by the lessor, the lessee still fails to pay within a reasonable period;  
(4) Other circumstances where the lessee violates the contract, resulting in the failure to achieve the purpose of the contract.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因出租人的原因致使承租人无法占有、使用租赁物，承租人请求解除融资租赁合同的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where the lessee is unable to possess or use the leased property due to the lessor's reasons, and the lessee requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人在一审诉讼中仅请求解除融资租赁合同，未对租赁物的归属及损失赔偿提出主张的，人民法院可以向当事人进行释明。

**Article 14** Where a party in the first-instance proceedings only requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract but does not assert claims on the attribution of the leased property and compensation for losses, the People's Court may provide clarification to the party.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　融资租赁合同因租赁物交付承租人后意外毁损、灭失等不可归责于当事人的原因而解除，出租人要求承租人按照租赁物折旧情况给予补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where a financial leasing contract is dissolved due to reasons not attributable to the parties, such as accidental damage or loss of the leased property after its delivery to the lessee, and the lessor requests the lessee to provide compensation based on the depreciation of the leased property, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　融资租赁合同因买卖合同被解除、被确认无效或者被撤销而解除，出租人根据融资租赁合同约定，或者以融资租赁合同虽未约定或约定不明，但出卖人及租赁物系由承租人选择为由，主张承租人赔偿相应损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
出租人的损失已经在买卖合同被解除、被确认无效或者被撤销时获得赔偿的，应当免除承租人相应的赔偿责任。  
四、违约责任

**Article 16** Where a financial leasing contract is dissolved because the sale contract is dissolved, confirmed as void, or revoked, and the lessor, in accordance with the provisions of the financial leasing contract, or on the ground that although the financial leasing contract does not provide or the provisions are unclear, the seller and the leased property were selected by the lessee, claims that the lessee shall compensate for corresponding losses, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
If the lessor's losses have already been compensated when the sale contract was dissolved, confirmed as void, or revoked, the lessee's corresponding liability for compensation shall be exempted.  
IV. Liability for Breach of Contract

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　出租人有下列情形之一，影响承租人对租赁物的占有和使用，承租人依照合同法第二百四十五条的规定，要求出租人赔偿相应损失的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）无正当理由收回租赁物；  
（二）无正当理由妨碍、干扰承租人对租赁物的占有和使用；  
（三）因出租人的原因导致第三人对租赁物主张权利；  
（四）不当影响承租人对租赁物占有、使用的其他情形。

**Article 17** Where the lessor commits any of the following acts, affecting the lessee's possession and use of the leased property, and the lessee, in accordance with Article 245 of the Contract Law, requests the lessor to compensate for the corresponding losses, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) Withdrawing the leased property without justifiable reasons;  
(2) Without justifiable reasons, obstructing or interfering with the lessee's possession and use of the leased property;  
(3) Due to the lessor's reasons, causing a third party to assert rights over the leased property;  
(4) Other circumstances that improperly affect the lessee's possession or use of the leased property.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　出租人有下列情形之一，导致承租人对出卖人索赔逾期或者索赔失败，承租人要求出租人承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）明知租赁物有质量瑕疵而不告知承租人的；  
（二）承租人行使索赔权时，未及时提供必要协助的；  
（三）怠于行使融资租赁合同中约定的只能由出租人行使对出卖人的索赔权的；  
（四）怠于行使买卖合同中约定的只能由出租人行使对出卖人的索赔权的。

**Article 18** Where the lessor commits any of the following acts, causing the lessee to exceed the time limit for claiming against the seller or to fail in such claim, and the lessee requests the lessor to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) Knowing of a quality defect in the leased property but failing to inform the lessee;  
(2) Failing to provide necessary assistance in a timely manner when the lessee exercises the right of claim;  
(3) Negligently failing to exercise the right of claim against the seller that may only be exercised by the lessor as agreed in the financial leasing contract;  
(4) Negligently failing to exercise the right of claim against the seller that may only be exercised by the lessor as agreed in the sale contract.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　租赁物不符合融资租赁合同的约定且出租人实施了下列行为之一，承租人依照合同法第二百四十一条、第二百四十四条的规定，要求出租人承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）出租人在承租人选择出卖人、租赁物时，对租赁物的选定起决定作用的；  
（二）出租人干预或者要求承租人按照出租人意愿选择出卖人或者租赁物的；  
（三）出租人擅自变更承租人已经选定的出卖人或者租赁物的。  
承租人主张其系依赖出租人的技能确定租赁物或者出租人干预选择租赁物的，对上述事实承担举证责任。

**Article 19** Where the leased property does not conform to the provisions of the financial leasing contract and the lessor has committed any of the following acts, and the lessee, in accordance with Articles 241 and 244 of the Contract Law, requests the lessor to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The lessor played a decisive role in the selection of the leased property when the lessee was selecting the seller or the leased property;  
(2) The lessor interfered with or required the lessee to select the seller or the leased property in accordance with the lessor's wishes;  
(3) The lessor unilaterally changed the seller or the leased property that the lessee had already selected.  
Where the lessee claims that it relied on the lessor's skill to determine the leased property or that the lessor interfered with the selection of the leased property, the lessee shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the above facts.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　承租人逾期履行支付租金义务或者迟延履行其他付款义务，出租人按照融资租赁合同的约定要求承租人支付逾期利息、相应违约金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where the lessee fails to perform the obligation to pay the rental within the time limit or delays the performance of other payment obligations, and the lessor, in accordance with the provisions of the financial leasing contract, requests the lessee to pay overdue interest and corresponding liquidated damages, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　出租人既请求承租人支付合同约定的全部未付租金又请求解除融资租赁合同的，人民法院应告知其依照合同法第二百四十八条的规定作出选择。  
出租人请求承租人支付合同约定的全部未付租金，人民法院判决后承租人未予履行，出租人再行起诉请求解除融资租赁合同、收回租赁物的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 21** Where the lessor both requests the lessee to pay all the unpaid rental as agreed in the contract and requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall inform it to make a choice in accordance with Article 248 of the Contract Law.  
Where the lessor requests the lessee to pay all the unpaid rental as agreed in the contract, and after the People's Court renders a judgment, the lessee fails to perform, and the lessor subsequently files another lawsuit requesting dissolution of the financial leasing contract and recovery of the leased property, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　出租人依照[本解释第十二条](#art-12)的规定请求解除融资租赁合同，同时请求收回租赁物并赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款规定的损失赔偿范围为承租人全部未付租金及其他费用与收回租赁物价值的差额。合同约定租赁期间届满后租赁物归出租人所有的，损失赔偿范围还应包括融资租赁合同到期后租赁物的残值。

**Article 22** Where the lessor, in accordance with Article 12 of this Interpretation, requests dissolution of the financial leasing contract and simultaneously requests recovery of the leased property and compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support such request.  
The scope of compensation for losses provided in the preceding paragraph shall be the difference between all unpaid rental and other expenses of the lessee and the value of the recovered leased property. If the contract agrees that after the expiration of the lease term, the leased property shall belong to the lessor, the scope of compensation for losses shall also include the residual value of the leased property after the expiration of the financial leasing contract.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　诉讼期间承租人与出租人对租赁物的价值有争议的，人民法院可以按照融资租赁合同的约定确定租赁物价值；融资租赁合同未约定或者约定不明的，可以参照融资租赁合同约定的租赁物折旧以及合同到期后租赁物的残值确定租赁物价值。  
承租人或者出租人认为依前款确定的价值严重偏离租赁物实际价值的，可以请求人民法院委托有资质的机构评估或者拍卖确定。  
五、其他规定

**Article 23** Where, during the proceedings, the lessee and the lessor dispute the value of the leased property, the People's Court may determine the value of the leased property in accordance with the provisions of the financial leasing contract; if the financial leasing contract does not provide or the provisions are unclear, the People's Court may determine the value of the leased property by reference to the depreciation of the leased property as agreed in the financial leasing contract and the residual value of the leased property after the expiration of the contract.  
Where the lessee or the lessor considers that the value determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph seriously deviates from the actual value of the leased property, it may request the People's Court to entrust a qualified institution to appraise or determine the value by auction.  
V. Miscellaneous Provisions

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　出卖人与买受人因买卖合同发生纠纷，或者出租人与承租人因融资租赁合同发生纠纷，当事人仅对其中一个合同关系提起诉讼，人民法院经审查后认为另一合同关系的当事人与案件处理结果有法律上的利害关系的，可以通知其作为第三人参加诉讼。  
承租人与租赁物的实际使用人不一致，融资租赁合同当事人未对租赁物的实际使用人提起诉讼，人民法院经审查后认为租赁物的实际使用人与案件处理结果有法律上的利害关系的，可以通知其作为第三人参加诉讼。  
承租人基于买卖合同和融资租赁合同直接向出卖人主张受领租赁物、索赔等买卖合同权利的，人民法院应通知出租人作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 24** Where a dispute arises between the seller and the buyer under a sale contract, or between the lessor and the lessee under a financial leasing contract, and a party only initiates prosecution with respect to one of the contractual relationships, the People's Court, after examination, if it considers that the party to the other contractual relationship has a legal interest in the outcome of the case, may notify such party to participate in the proceedings as a third party.  
Where the lessee and the actual user of the leased property are not the same person, and the party to the financial leasing contract does not initiate prosecution against the actual user of the leased property, the People's Court, after examination, if it considers that the actual user of the leased property has a legal interest in the outcome of the case, may notify such user to participate in the proceedings as a third party.  
Where the lessee directly claims against the seller for taking delivery of the leased property, making claims, and other rights under the sale contract based on both the sale contract and the financial leasing contract, the People's Court shall notify the lessor to participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　当事人因融资租赁合同租金欠付争议向人民法院请求保护其权利的诉讼时效期间为两年，自租赁期限届满之日起计算。

**Article 25** The limitation of action for a party to request the People's Court to protect its rights due to a dispute over arrears of rental under a financial leasing contract shall be two years, commencing from the date of expiration of the lease term.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释自2014年3月1日起施行。最高人民法院《关于审理融资租赁合同纠纷案件若干问题的规定》（法发〔1996〕19号）同时废止。  
本解释施行后尚未终审的融资租赁合同纠纷案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。

**Article 26** This Interpretation shall come into force as of March 1, 2014. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Trial of Cases of Disputes over Financial Leasing Contracts (Fa Fa [1996] No. 19) shall be abolished simultaneously.  
For cases of disputes over financial leasing contracts that have not yet been finally adjudicated after this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall apply; for cases that have been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force, if a party applies for retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
