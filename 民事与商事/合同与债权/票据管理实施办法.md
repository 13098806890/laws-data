# 票据管理实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1997-10-01

> (1997年6月23日国务院批准　1997年8月21日中国人民银行令第2号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强票据管理，维护金融秩序，根据《中华人民共和国票据法》(以下简称票据法)的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的票据管理，适用本办法。 第三条　中国人民银行是票据的管理部门。 票据管理应当遵守票据法和本办法以及有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得损害票据当事人的合法权益。 第四条　票据当事人应当依法从事票据活动，行使票据权利，履行票据义务。 第五条　票据当事人应当使用中国人民银行规定的统一格式的票据。 第六条　银行汇票的出票人，为经中国人民银行批准办理银行汇票业务的银行。 第七条　银行本票的出票人，为经中国人民银行批准办理银行本票业务的银行。 第八条　商业汇票的出票人，为银行以外的企业和其他组织。 向银行申请办理汇票承兑的商业汇票的出票人，必须具备下列条件： (一)在承兑银行开立存款账户； (二)资信状况良好，并具有支付汇票金额的可靠资金来源。 第九条　承兑商业汇票的银行，必须具备下列条件： (一)与出票人具有真实的委托付款关系；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强票据管理，维护金融秩序，根据《中华人民共和国票据法》(以下简称票据法)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Negotiable Instruments Law) for the purpose of strengthening the administration of negotiable instruments and maintaining financial order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的票据管理，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the administration of negotiable instruments within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国人民银行是票据的管理部门。  
票据管理应当遵守票据法和本办法以及有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得损害票据当事人的合法权益。

**Article 3** The People's Bank of China shall be the administrative department for negotiable instruments.  
The administration of negotiable instruments shall comply with the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law, these Measures, and relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall not harm the lawful rights and interests of the parties to negotiable instruments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　票据当事人应当依法从事票据活动，行使票据权利，履行票据义务。

**Article 4** The parties to negotiable instruments shall engage in negotiable instrument activities according to law, exercise the rights under the negotiable instruments, and perform the obligations under the negotiable instruments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　票据当事人应当使用中国人民银行规定的统一格式的票据。

**Article 5** The parties to negotiable instruments shall use negotiable instruments in the uniform format prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　银行汇票的出票人，为经中国人民银行批准办理银行汇票业务的银行。

**Article 6** The drawer of a banker's draft shall be a bank approved by the People's Bank of China to handle banker's draft business.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　银行本票的出票人，为经中国人民银行批准办理银行本票业务的银行。

**Article 7** The drawer of a promissory note shall be a bank approved by the People's Bank of China to handle promissory note business.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　商业汇票的出票人，为银行以外的企业和其他组织。  
向银行申请办理汇票承兑的商业汇票的出票人，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)在承兑银行开立存款账户；  
(二)资信状况良好，并具有支付汇票金额的可靠资金来源。

**Article 8** The drawer of a commercial acceptance bill shall be an enterprise or other organization other than a bank.  
A drawer of a commercial acceptance bill who applies to a bank for acceptance of the bill shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having opened a deposit account with the accepting bank;  
(2) having a good credit standing and having reliable sources of funds to pay the amount of the bill.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承兑商业汇票的银行，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)与出票人具有真实的委托付款关系；  
(二)具有支付汇票金额的可靠资金。

**Article 9** A bank accepting a commercial acceptance bill shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a genuine relationship of entrusted payment with the drawer;  
(2) having reliable funds to pay the amount of the bill.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　向银行申请办理票据贴现的商业汇票的持票人，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)在银行开立存款账户；  
(二)与出票人、前手之间具有真实的交易关系和债权债务关系。

**Article 10** A holder of a commercial acceptance bill who applies to a bank for discount of the bill shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having opened a deposit account with the bank;  
(2) having a genuine transaction relationship and a relationship of rights and obligations with the drawer and prior parties.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　支票的出票人，为在经中国人民银行批准办理支票存款业务的银行、城市信用合作社和农村信用合作社开立支票存款账户的企业、其他组织和个人。

**Article 11** The drawer of a check shall be an enterprise, other organization, or individual that has opened a check deposit account with a bank, urban credit cooperative, or rural credit cooperative approved by the People's Bank of China to handle check deposit business.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　票据法所称“保证人”，是指具有代为清偿票据债务能力的法人、其他组织或者个人。  
国家机关、以公益为目的的事业单位、社会团体、企业法人的分支机构和职能部门不得为保证人；但是，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 12** The term "guarantor" as used in the Negotiable Instruments Law refers to a legal person, other organization, or individual with the ability to pay negotiable instrument debts on behalf of the debtor.  
State organs, public institutions established for public welfare purposes, social organizations, and branches and functional departments of enterprise legal persons shall not serve as guarantors; however, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　银行汇票上的出票人的签章、银行承兑商业汇票的签章，为该银行的汇票专用章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章。  
银行本票上的出票人的签章，为该银行的本票专用章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章。  
银行汇票专用章、银行本票专用章须经中国人民银行批准。

**Article 13** The seal of the drawer on a bank draft and the seal of a bank that accepts a commercial draft shall be the special seal for drafts of such bank plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or its authorized agent.  
The seal of the drawer on a bank promissory note shall be the special seal for promissory notes of such bank plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or its authorized agent.  
The special seal for bank drafts and the special seal for bank promissory notes must be approved by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　商业汇票上的出票人的签章，为该单位的财务专用章或者公章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章。

**Article 14** The seal of the drawer on a commercial draft shall be the financial special seal or official seal of such unit plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or its authorized agent.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　支票上的出票人的签章，出票人为单位的，为与该单位在银行预留签章一致的财务专用章或者公章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章；出票人为个人的，为与该个人在银行预留签章一致的签名或者盖章。

**Article 15** The seal of the drawer on a check, where the drawer is a unit, shall be the financial special seal or official seal consistent with the seal reserved by such unit with the bank, plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or its authorized agent; where the drawer is an individual, it shall be the signature or seal consistent with the seal reserved by such individual with the bank.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　票据法所称“本名”，是指符合法律、行政法规以及国家有关规定的身份证件上的姓名。

**Article 16** The term "real name" as used in the Negotiable Instruments Law refers to the name on an identity document that conforms to laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　出票人在票据上的签章不符合票据法和本办法规定的，票据无效；背书人、承兑人、保证人在票据上的签章不符合票据法和本办法规定的，其签章无效，但是不影响票据上其他签章的效力。

**Article 17** If the seal of the drawer on a negotiable instrument does not conform to the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law and these Measures, the negotiable instrument shall be void; if the seal of an endorser, acceptor, or guarantor on a negotiable instrument does not conform to the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law and these Measures, such seal shall be void, but the validity of other seals on the negotiable instrument shall not be affected.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　票据法所称“代理付款人”，是指根据付款人的委托，代其支付票据金额的银行、城市信用合作社和农村信用合作社。

**Article 18** The term "paying agent" as used in the Negotiable Instruments Law refers to a bank, urban credit cooperative, or rural credit cooperative that, pursuant to the entrustment of the payer, pays the amount of the negotiable instrument on behalf of the payer.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　票据法规定可以办理挂失止付的票据丧失的，失票人可以依照票据法的规定及时通知付款人或者代理付款人挂失止付。  
失票人通知票据的付款人或者代理付款人挂失止付时，应当填写挂失止付通知书并签章。挂失止付通知书应当记载下列事项：  
(一)票据丧失的时间和事由；  
(二)票据种类、号码、金额、出票日期、付款日期、付款人名称、收款人名称；  
(三)挂失止付人的名称、营业场所或者住所以及联系方法。

**Article 19** If a negotiable instrument which may be reported lost and stop-payment in accordance with the Negotiable Instruments Law is lost, the loser may promptly notify the payer or the paying agent to report the loss and stop payment in accordance with the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law.  
When the loser notifies the payer or the paying agent of the negotiable instrument to report the loss and stop payment, the loser shall fill out a notice of reporting loss and stop payment and affix a seal. The notice of reporting loss and stop payment shall include the following particulars:  
(1) the time and reason for the loss of the negotiable instrument;  
(2) the type, number, amount, date of issue, date of payment, name of payer, and name of payee of the negotiable instrument;  
(3) the name, business premises or domicile, and contact information of the person reporting the loss and requesting stop payment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　付款人或者代理付款人收到挂失止付通知书，应当立即暂停支付。付款人或者代理付款人自收到挂失止付通知书之日起12日内没有收到人民法院的止付通知书的，自第13日起，挂失止付通知书失效。

**Article 20** Upon receipt of a notice of reporting loss and stop payment, the payer or paying agent shall immediately suspend payment. If the payer or paying agent does not receive a stop payment order from a People's Court within 12 days from the date of receipt of the notice of reporting loss and stop payment, the notice of reporting loss and stop payment shall cease to be effective from the 13th day.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　付款人或者代理付款人在收到挂失止付通知书前，已经依法向持票人付款的，不再接受挂失止付。

**Article 21** If the drawee or the agent drawee has paid the holder according to law before receiving the notice of reporting loss and stopping payment, it shall no longer accept the reporting loss and stopping payment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请人申请开立支票存款账户的，银行、城市信用合作社和农村信用合作社可以与申请人约定在支票上使用支付密码，作为支付支票金额的条件。

**Article 22** If an applicant applies to open a checking account, banks, urban credit cooperatives, and rural credit cooperatives may agree with the applicant on the use of a payment password on the check as a condition for paying the check amount.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　保证人应当依照票据法的规定，在票据或者其粘单上记载保证事项。保证人为出票人、付款人、承兑人保证的，应当在票据的正面记载保证事项；保证人为背书人保证的，应当在票据的背面或者其粘单上记载保证事项。

**Article 23** A guarantor shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law, record the guaranty matters on the instrument or its allonge. If a guarantor guarantees for the drawer, drawee, or acceptor, the guaranty matters shall be recorded on the front of the instrument; if a guarantor guarantees for an endorser, the guaranty matters shall be recorded on the back of the instrument or its allonge.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　依法背书转让的票据，任何单位和个人不得冻结票据款项；但是，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 24** No entity or individual may freeze the funds of an instrument that has been endorsed and transferred according to law, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　[票据法第五十五条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-55)所称“签收”，是指持票人在票据的正面签章，表明持票人已经获得付款。

**Article 25** 'Signing for receipt' as mentioned in Article 55 of the Negotiable Instruments Law means that the holder signs and seals on the front of the instrument to indicate that the holder has received payment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　通过委托收款银行或者通过票据交换系统向付款人提示付款的，持票人向银行提交票据日为提示付款日。

**Article 26** If the holder presents the instrument for payment through a collecting bank or through the bill exchange system, the date on which the holder submits the instrument to the bank shall be the date of presentment for payment.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　[票据法第六十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-62)所称“拒绝证明”应当包括下列事项：  
(一)被拒绝承兑、付款的票据的种类及其主要记载事项；  
(二)拒绝承兑、付款的事实依据和法律依据；  
(三)拒绝承兑、付款的时间；  
(四)拒绝承兑人、拒绝付款人的签章。  
[票据法第六十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-62)所称“退票理由书”应当包括下列事项：  
(一)所退票据的种类；  
(二)退票的事实依据和法律依据；  
(三)退票时间；  
(四)退票人签章。

**Article 27** 'Proof of dishonor' as mentioned in Article 62 of the Negotiable Instruments Law shall include the following matters:  
(1) The type of the instrument that was dishonored by acceptance or payment and its main recorded matters;  
(2) The factual and legal basis for the dishonor of acceptance or payment;  
(3) The time of dishonor of acceptance or payment;  
(4) The signature and seal of the person who dishonored acceptance and the person who dishonored payment.  
'Written statement of reasons for dishonor' as mentioned in Article 62 of the Negotiable Instruments Law shall include the following matters:  
(1) The type of the dishonored instrument;  
(2) The factual and legal basis for the dishonor;  
(3) The time of dishonor;  
(4) The signature and seal of the person who dishonored.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　[票据法第六十三条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-63)规定的“其他有关证明”是指：  
(一)医院或者有关单位出具的承兑人、付款人死亡的证明；  
(二)司法机关出具的承兑人、付款人逃匿的证明；  
(三)公证机关出具的具有拒绝证明效力的文书。

**Article 28** 'Other relevant certificates' as provided in Article 63 of the Negotiable Instruments Law means:  
(1) Certificates issued by a hospital or relevant entity proving the death of the acceptor or drawee;  
(2) Certificates issued by a judicial authority proving the absconding of the acceptor or drawee;  
(3) Documents issued by a notarial authority that have the effect of a certificate of dishonor.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　[票据法第七十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-70)第一款第(二)项、第七十一条第一款第(二)项规定的“利率”，是指中国人民银行规定的流动资金贷款利率。

**Article 29** 'Interest rate' as provided in Article 70, paragraph 1, item (2) and Article 71, paragraph 1, item (2) of the Negotiable Instruments Law means the loan interest rate for working capital prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有[票据法第一百零二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-102)所列行为之一，情节轻微，不构成犯罪的，由公安机关依法予以处罚。

**Article 30** If a person commits any of the acts listed in Article 102 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, and the circumstances are minor and do not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　签发空头支票或者签发与其预留的签章不符的支票，不以骗取财物为目的的，由中国人民银行处以票面金额5%但不低于1000元的罚款；持票人有权要求出票人赔偿支票金额2%的赔偿金。

**Article 31** Whosoever issues a rubber check or a check inconsistent with the reserved seal, without the purpose of defrauding property, shall be fined by the People's Bank of China at the rate of 5% of the face amount but not less than 1,000 yuan; the holder shall have the right to claim from the drawer compensation at the rate of 2% of the check amount.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　金融机构的工作人员在票据业务中玩忽职守，对违反票据法和本办法规定的票据予以承兑、付款、保证或者贴现的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过、撤职或者开除的处分；造成重大损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where any staff member of a financial institution neglects his duty in negotiable instrument business and accepts, pays, guarantees or discounts a negotiable instrument in violation of the Negotiable Instrument Law and these Measures, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions such as a warning, demerit recording, removal from office, or expulsion; if the case causes heavy losses and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　票据的付款人对见票即付或者到期的票据，故意压票、拖延支付的，由中国人民银行处以压票、拖延支付期间内每日票据金额0.7‰的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过、撤职或者开除的处分。

**Article 33** Where the drawee of a negotiable instrument intentionally delays payment of a negotiable instrument payable at sight or at maturity, the People's Bank of China shall impose a fine at the rate of 0.7‰ of the amount of the negotiable instrument per day during the period of delay; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions such as a warning, demerit recording, removal from office, or expulsion.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反中国人民银行规定，擅自印制票据的，由中国人民银行责令改正，处以1万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，中国人民银行有权提请有关部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 34** Whosoever prints negotiable instruments without authorization in violation of the provisions of the People's Bank of China shall be ordered by the People's Bank of China to make corrections and be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the People's Bank of China shall have the right to request the relevant department to revoke its business license.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　票据的格式、联次、颜色、规格及防伪技术要求和印制，由中国人民银行规定。  
中国人民银行在确定票据格式时，可以根据少数民族地区和外国驻华使领馆的实际需要，在票据格式中增加少数民族文字或者外国文字。

**Article 35** The format, copies, color, specifications, anti-counterfeiting technical requirements, and printing of negotiable instruments shall be prescribed by the People's Bank of China.  
When determining the format of negotiable instruments, the People's Bank of China may, in light of the actual needs of ethnic minority areas and foreign embassies and consulates in China, add ethnic minority scripts or foreign scripts to the format.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本办法自1997年10月1日起施行。

**Article 36** These Measures shall come into force as of October 1, 1997.
