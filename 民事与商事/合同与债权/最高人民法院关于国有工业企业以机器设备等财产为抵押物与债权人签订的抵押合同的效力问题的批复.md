### 最高人民法院关于国有工业企业以机器设备等财产为抵押物与债权人签订的抵押合同的效力问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于国有工业企业以机器设备等财产为抵押物与债权人  
  
签订的抵押合同的效力问题的批复  
  
（2002年6月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1225次会议通过，自2002年6月22日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2002〕14号  
  
重庆市高级人民法院：  
  
你院渝高法〔2001〕37号《关于认定国有工业企业以机器设备、厂房为抵押物与债权人签订的抵押合同的法律效力的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国担保法》第三十四条和最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国合同法〉若干问题的解释（一）》第九条规定的精神，国有工业企业以机器设备、厂房等财产与债权人签订的抵押合同，如无其它法定的无效情形，不应当仅以未经政府主管部门批准为由认定抵押合同无效。  
  
本批复施行后，正在审理或者尚未审理的案件，适用本批复，但判决、裁定已经发生法律效力的案件提起再审的除外。  
  
此复。  
  
2002年6月18日

**Article 1** Judicial Interpretation [2002] No. 14  
  
Chongqing Higher People's Court:  
  
Your Court's Yu Gao Fa [2001] No. 37 Request for Instructions on Determining the Legal Validity of Mortgage Contracts Signed by State-Owned Industrial Enterprises with Creditors Using Machinery, Equipment, and Factory Buildings as Mortgage Property has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
In accordance with the spirit of Article 34 of the Security Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 9 of the Supreme People's Court's Interpretation (I) on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, a mortgage contract signed by a state-owned industrial enterprise with a creditor using property such as machinery, equipment, and factory buildings shall not be determined as a void contract solely on the ground that it has not been approved by the competent government authority, unless there are other statutory invalidating circumstances.  
  
After this Reply comes into force, cases currently being tried or not yet tried shall apply this Reply, except for cases where judgments or rulings have already become legally effective and are brought for retrial.  
  
Hereby reply.  
  
June 18, 2002
