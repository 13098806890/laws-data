# 最高人民法院关于专业银行、信用社担保的经济合同被确认无效后保证人是否应当承担连带责任问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1987〕5号  
**公布日期**：1987-02-05

---

### 正文

一、依照《中华人民共和国民法通则》第89条“保证人向债权人保证债务人履行债务，债务人不履行债务的，按照约定由保证人履行或者承担连带责任”及《中华人民共和国经济合同法》第15条的规定，专业银行、信用社若作为经济合同当事人一方的保证人，在被保证人不履行合同义务时，应当按照约定由保证人履行或者承担连带责任。

**Article None** 1. In accordance with the provisions of Article 89 of the General Principles of Civil Law of the People's Republic of China that "a guarantor shall guarantee to the creditor that the debtor shall perform the debt; if the debtor fails to perform the debt, the guarantor shall perform or bear joint and several liability as agreed" and Article 15 of the Economic Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, if a specialized bank or credit cooperative acts as a guarantor for a party to an economic contract, then when the guaranteed party fails to perform the contractual obligations, the guarantor shall perform or bear joint and several liability as agreed.

二、经济合同被依法确认无效后，当事人依据该合同所取得的财产应当返还给对方。有过错的一方应当赔偿对方因此所受的损失；如果双方都有过错，应当各自承担相应的责任。因此，在担保的经济合同被确认无效后，若被保证人应当返还财产而不能返还或者应当赔偿损失而不能赔偿时，除有特殊约定外，保证人应当承担连带责任。  
此复  
1987年2月5日

**Article None** 2. After an economic contract is confirmed invalid according to law, the property obtained by a party under such contract shall be returned to the other party. The party at fault shall compensate the other party for losses incurred thereby; if both parties are at fault, each shall bear the corresponding liability. Therefore, after a guaranteed economic contract is confirmed invalid, if the guaranteed party is required to return the property but fails to do so, or is required to compensate for losses but fails to do so, the guarantor shall bear joint and several liability, unless otherwise specially agreed.  
This is our reply.  
February 5, 1987
