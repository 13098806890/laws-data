# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国合同法》若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕5号  
**公布日期**：2009-02-09  
**生效日期**：2009-05-13

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国合同法〉若干问题的解释（二）》已于2009年2月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1462次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年5月13日起施行。 2009年4月24日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国合同法》 若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  当事人对合同是否成立存在争议，人民法院能够确定当事人名称或者姓名、标的和数量的，一般应当认定合同成立。但法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。  
对合同欠缺的前款规定以外的其他内容，当事人达不成协议的，人民法院依照合同法第六十一条、第六十二条、第一百二十五条等有关规定予以确定。

**Article 1** Where a dispute arises between the parties as to whether a contract is established, if the People's Court can determine the name or title of the party, the subject matter and the quantity, the contract shall generally be deemed to be established, unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed by the parties.  
Where the parties fail to reach an agreement on other contents of the contract not covered by the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall determine such contents in accordance with the relevant provisions of Articles 61, 62 and 125 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  当事人未以书面形式或者口头形式订立合同，但从双方从事的民事行为能够推定双方有订立合同意愿的，人民法院可以认定是以合同法第十条第一款中的“其他形式”订立的合同。但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 2** Where the parties do not conclude a contract in writing or orally, but it can be inferred from the civil acts carried out by both parties that they have the intention to conclude a contract, the People's Court may determine that the contract is concluded in the “other form” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 10 of the Contract Law, except where otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  悬赏人以公开方式声明对完成一定行为的人支付报酬，完成特定行为的人请求悬赏人支付报酬的，人民法院依法予以支持。但悬赏有合同法第五十二条规定情形的除外。

**Article 3** Where a reward offeror publicly declares that a reward shall be paid to a person who completes a certain act, and the person who completes the specific act requests the reward offeror to pay the reward, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law, except where the reward falls under the circumstances specified in Article 52 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  采用书面形式订立合同，合同约定的签订地与实际签字或者盖章地点不符的，人民法院应当认定约定的签订地为合同签订地；合同没有约定签订地，双方当事人签字或者盖章不在同一地点的，人民法院应当认定最后签字或者盖章的地点为合同签订地。

**Article 4** Where a contract is concluded in writing, and the place of execution agreed in the contract is inconsistent with the actual place of signature or seal, the People's Court shall determine the agreed place of execution as the place of execution of the contract; where the contract does not stipulate a place of execution and the parties sign or affix seals in different places, the People's Court shall determine the place where the last signature or seal is made as the place of execution of the contract.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  当事人采用合同书形式订立合同的，应当签字或者盖章。当事人在合同书上摁手印的，人民法院应当认定其具有与签字或者盖章同等的法律效力。

**Article 5** Where the parties conclude a contract in the form of a written instrument, they shall sign or affix their seals. Where a party affixes a thumbprint on the contract instrument, the People's Court shall determine that it has the same legal effect as a signature or seal.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  提供格式条款的一方对格式条款中免除或者限制其责任的内容，在合同订立时采用足以引起对方注意的文字、符号、字体等特别标识，并按照对方的要求对该格式条款予以说明的，人民法院应当认定符合合同法第三十九条所称“采取合理的方式”。  
提供格式条款一方对已尽合理提示及说明义务承担举证责任。

**Article 6** Where the party providing standard terms adopts special markings such as words, symbols, fonts, etc., that are sufficient to draw the other party's attention to the content that exempts or limits its liability in the standard terms at the time of conclusion of the contract, and explains the standard terms at the request of the other party, the People's Court shall determine that such action complies with the “reasonable means” referred to in Article 39 of the Contract Law.  
The party providing standard terms shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that it has fulfilled the duty of reasonable reminder and explanation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  下列情形，不违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的，人民法院可以认定为合同法所称“交易习惯”：  
（一）在交易行为当地或者某一领域、某一行业通常采用并为交易对方订立合同时所知道或者应当知道的做法；  
（二）当事人双方经常使用的习惯做法。  
对于交易习惯，由提出主张的一方当事人承担举证责任。

**Article 7** Under the following circumstances, provided that they do not violate any mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, the People's Court may determine them as “trade practices” referred to in the Contract Law:  
(1) practices that are commonly adopted in the locality of the transaction or in a particular field or industry and are known or ought to be known to the other party at the time of concluding the contract; and  
(2) habitual practices frequently used between the parties.  
The burden of adducing evidence as to trade practices shall be borne by the party asserting such practices.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  依照法律、行政法规的规定经批准或者登记才能生效的合同成立后，有义务办理申请批准或者申请登记等手续的一方当事人未按照法律规定或者合同约定办理申请批准或者未申请登记的，属于合同法第四十二条第（三）项规定的“其他违背诚实信用原则的行为”，人民法院可以根据案件的具体情况和相对人的请求，判决相对人自己办理有关手续；对方当事人对由此产生的费用和给相对人造成的实际损失，应当承担损害赔偿责任。  
二、合同的效力

**Article 8** After a contract that requires approval or registration for it to take effect in accordance with laws or administrative regulations is established, if the party obliged to apply for approval or registration fails to do so in accordance with legal provisions or the contract, such failure constitutes an “other act contrary to the principle of good faith” as provided in subparagraph (3) of Article 42 of the Contract Law. The People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case and at the request of the counterparty, rule that the counterparty may handle the relevant procedures itself; the other party shall be liable for damages for the expenses incurred thereby and the actual losses caused to the counterparty.  
II. Validity of Contract

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  提供格式条款的一方当事人违反合同法第三十九条第一款关于提示和说明义务的规定，导致对方没有注意免除或者限制其责任的条款，对方当事人申请撤销该格式条款的，人民法院应当支持。

**Article 9** Where the party providing standard terms violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 39 of the Contract Law regarding the duty of reminder and explanation, causing the other party to fail to take notice of the clauses that exempt or limit its liability, and the other party applies to revoke such standard terms, the People's Court shall support the application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  提供格式条款的一方当事人违反合同法第三十九条第一款的规定，并具有合同法第四十条规定的情形之一的，人民法院应当认定该格式条款无效。

**Article 10** Where the party providing standard terms violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 39 of the Contract Law and also falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 40 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall determine such standard terms to be null and void.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  根据合同法第四十七条、第四十八条的规定，追认的意思表示自到达相对人时生效，合同自订立时起生效。

**Article 11** Pursuant to the provisions of Articles 47 and 48 of the Contract Law, the manifestation of intention of ratification shall take effect upon reaching the counterparty, and the contract shall take effect from the time of its conclusion.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  无权代理人以被代理人的名义订立合同，被代理人已经开始履行合同义务的，视为对合同的追认。

**Article 12** Where an unauthorized agent concludes a contract in the name of the principal and the principal has already begun to perform the contractual obligations, such performance shall be deemed as ratification of the contract.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  被代理人依照合同法第四十九条的规定承担有效代理行为所产生的责任后，可以向无权代理人追偿因代理行为而遭受的损失。

**Article 13** After the principal assumes the liability arising from the valid agency act in accordance with Article 49 of the Contract Law, it may seek recourse from the unauthorized agent for the losses suffered due to the agency act.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  合同法第五十二条第（五）项规定的“强制性规定”，是指效力性强制性规定。

**Article 14** The “mandatory provisions” as provided in subparagraph (5) of Article 52 of the Contract Law refer to mandatory provisions on validity.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  出卖人就同一标的物订立多重买卖合同，合同均不具有合同法第五十二条规定的无效情形，买受人因不能按照合同约定取得标的物所有权，请求追究出卖人违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
三、合同的履行

**Article 15** Where a seller concludes multiple sales contracts for the same subject matter and none of the contracts has any of the invalid circumstances specified in Article 52 of the Contract Law, and a buyer, due to being unable to obtain the ownership of the subject matter in accordance with the contract, requests to hold the seller liable for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support the claim.  
III. Performance of Contract

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  人民法院根据具体案情可以将合同法第六十四条、第六十五条规定的第三人列为无独立请求权的第三人，但不得依职权将其列为该合同诉讼案件的被告或者有独立请求权的第三人。

**Article 16** The People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, list the third party as provided in Articles 64 and 65 of the Contract Law as a third party without independent claims, but may not, ex officio, list it as the defendant or a third party with independent claims in the litigation case concerning such contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  债权人以境外当事人为被告提起的代位权诉讼，人民法院根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百四十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-241)的规定确定管辖。

**Article 17** Where a creditor institutes an action for subrogation against an overseas party as defendant, the People's Court shall determine jurisdiction in accordance with Article 241 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  债务人放弃其未到期的债权或者放弃债权担保，或者恶意延长到期债权的履行期，对债权人造成损害，债权人依照合同法第七十四条的规定提起撤销权诉讼的，人民法院应当支持。

**Article 18** Where the debtor waives its claim that is not yet due or waives the security for the claim, or maliciously extends the performance period of a matured claim, thereby causing damage to the creditor, and the creditor institutes an action for revocation in accordance with Article 74 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  对于合同法第七十四条规定的“明显不合理的低价”，人民法院应当以交易当地一般经营者的判断，并参考交易当时交易地的物价部门指导价或者市场交易价，结合其他相关因素综合考虑予以确认。  
转让价格达不到交易时交易地的指导价或者市场交易价百分之七十的，一般可以视为明显不合理的低价；对转让价格高于当地指导价或者市场交易价百分之三十的，一般可以视为明显不合理的高价。  
债务人以明显不合理的高价收购他人财产，人民法院可以根据债权人的申请，参照合同法第七十四条的规定予以撤销。

**Article 19** With respect to the “obviously unreasonable low price” as provided in Article 74 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall confirm it by reference to the judgment of an ordinary operator in the locality of the transaction, and by considering the guidance price of the price authority at the place of transaction at the time of the transaction or the market transaction price, in conjunction with other relevant factors.  
If the transfer price is less than 70% of the guidance price or the market transaction price at the place of transaction at the time of transfer, it may generally be deemed as an obviously unreasonable low price; if the transfer price is more than 30% higher than the local guidance price or the market transaction price, it may generally be deemed as an obviously unreasonable high price.  
Where the debtor purchases another person's property at an obviously unreasonable high price, the People's Court may, at the request of the creditor, revoke such purchase by reference to Article 74 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  债务人的给付不足以清偿其对同一债权人所负的数笔相同种类的全部债务，应当优先抵充已到期的债务；几项债务均到期的，优先抵充对债权人缺乏担保或者担保数额最少的债务；担保数额相同的，优先抵充债务负担较重的债务；负担相同的，按照债务到期的先后顺序抵充；到期时间相同的，按比例抵充。但是，债权人与债务人对清偿的债务或者清偿抵充顺序有约定的除外。

**Article 20** Where the debtor's performance is insufficient to discharge all of several debts of the same kind owed to the same creditor, such performance shall first be applied to discharge the matured debts; where all debts are matured, it shall first be applied to discharge the debt that lacks security or has the least amount of security; where the amounts of security are the same, it shall first be applied to discharge the debt with the heavier burden; where the burdens are the same, it shall be applied in the order of the maturity dates of the debts; and where the maturity dates are the same, it shall be applied in proportion. However, this shall not apply if the creditor and the debtor have otherwise agreed on the debts to be discharged or the order of discharge.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  债务人除主债务之外还应当支付利息和费用，当其给付不足以清偿全部债务时，并且当事人没有约定的，人民法院应当按照下列顺序抵充：  
（一）实现债权的有关费用；  
（二）利息；  
（三）主债务。  
四、合同的权利义务终止

**Article 21** Where the debtor is also obligated to pay interest and expenses in addition to the principal debt, and its performance is insufficient to discharge all debts, and the parties have no agreement, the People's Court shall apply the performance in the following order:  
(1) expenses related to the realization of the claim;  
(2) interest;  
(3) principal debt.  
IV. Termination of Rights and Obligations under Contract

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  当事人一方违反合同法第九十二条规定的义务，给对方当事人造成损失，对方当事人请求赔偿实际损失的，人民法院应当支持。

**Article 22** Where a party violates the obligations set forth in Article 92 of the Contract Law and causes losses to the other party, and the other party requests compensation for actual losses, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  对于依照合同法第九十九条的规定可以抵销的到期债权，当事人约定不得抵销的，人民法院可以认定该约定有效。

**Article 23** With respect to matured claims that may be set off in accordance with Article 99 of the Contract Law, if the parties agree that they shall not be set off, the People's Court may determine such agreement to be valid.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  当事人对合同法第九十六条、第九十九条规定的合同解除或者债务抵销虽有异议，但在约定的异议期限届满后才提出异议并向人民法院起诉的，人民法院不予支持；当事人没有约定异议期间，在解除合同或者债务抵销通知到达之日起三个月以后才向人民法院起诉的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** Where a party objects to the dissolution of a contract or the set-off of debts as provided in Articles 96 and 99 of the Contract Law, but raises such objection and files a lawsuit with the People's Court only after the agreed objection period has expired, the People's Court shall not support the claim; where the parties have not agreed on an objection period, and the party files a lawsuit with the People's Court only after three months from the date when the notice of dissolution or set-off reaches it, the People's Court shall not support the claim.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  依照合同法第一百零一条的规定，债务人将合同标的物或者标的物拍卖、变卖所得价款交付提存部门时，人民法院应当认定提存成立。  
提存成立的，视为债务人在其提存范围内已经履行债务。

**Article 25** Where the debtor delivers the subject matter of the contract or the proceeds from the auction or sale of the subject matter to the escrow authority in accordance with Article 101 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall determine that the escrow is established.  
Once the escrow is established, the debtor shall be deemed to have performed the debt to the extent of the escrow.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  合同成立以后客观情况发生了当事人在订立合同时无法预见的、非不可抗力造成的不属于商业风险的重大变化，继续履行合同对于一方当事人明显不公平或者不能实现合同目的，当事人请求人民法院变更或者解除合同的，人民法院应当根据公平原则，并结合案件的实际情况确定是否变更或者解除。  
五、违约责任

**Article 26** After the contract is established, if there is a material change in objective circumstances that was unforeseeable by the parties at the time of concluding the contract, is not caused by force majeure, and does not constitute a commercial risk, and the continued performance of the contract is manifestly unfair to one party or makes the purpose of the contract unattainable, and the party requests the People's Court to modify or dissolve the contract, the People's Court shall determine whether to modify or dissolve the contract in accordance with the principle of fairness and in light of the actual circumstances of the case.  
V. Liability for Breach of Contract

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  当事人通过反诉或者抗辩的方式，请求人民法院依照合同法第一百一十四条第二款的规定调整违约金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 27** Where a party, by way of counterclaim or defense, requests the People's Court to adjust the liquidated damages in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 114 of the Contract Law, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  当事人依照合同法第一百一十四条第二款的规定，请求人民法院增加违约金的，增加后的违约金数额以不超过实际损失额为限。增加违约金以后，当事人又请求对方赔偿损失的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** Where a party requests the People's Court to increase the liquidated damages in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 114 of the Contract Law, the amount of liquidated damages after the increase shall not exceed the amount of actual losses. After the liquidated damages are increased, if the party further requests compensation for losses from the other party, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  当事人主张约定的违约金过高请求予以适当减少的，人民法院应当以实际损失为基础，兼顾合同的履行情况、当事人的过错程度以及预期利益等综合因素，根据公平原则和诚实信用原则予以衡量，并作出裁决。  
当事人约定的违约金超过造成损失的百分之三十的，一般可以认定为合同法第一百一十四条第二款规定的“过分高于造成的损失”。  
六、附则

**Article 29** Where a party claims that the agreed liquidated damages are excessively high and requests an appropriate reduction, the People's Court shall take the actual losses as the basis, take into account comprehensive factors such as the performance of the contract, the degree of fault of the parties, and the anticipated benefits, and make a ruling in accordance with the principles of fairness and good faith.  
If the agreed liquidated damages exceed 30% of the losses caused, they may generally be determined as the liquidated damages that are “excessively higher than the losses caused” as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 114 of the Contract Law.  
VI. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  合同法施行后成立的合同发生纠纷的案件，本解释施行后尚未终审的，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。

**Article 30** For cases involving disputes over contracts established after the Contract Law came into force, if the case has not yet become final after this Interpretation comes into force, this Interpretation shall apply; if the case has become final before this Interpretation comes into force, and the parties apply for retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
