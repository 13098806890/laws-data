# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国合同法》若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1999〕19号  
**公布日期**：1999-12-01  
**生效日期**：1999-12-29

> （1999年12月1日最高人民法院审判委员会第1090次会议通过，自1999年12月29日起施行。） 法释〔1999〕19号 为了正确审理合同纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国合同法》（以下简称合同法）的规定，对人民法院适用合同法的有关问题作出如下解释： 一、法律适用范围 第一条 合同法实施以后成立的合同发生纠纷起诉到人民法院的，适用合同法的规定；合同法实施以前成立的合同发生纠纷起诉到人民法院的，除本解释另有规定的以外，适用当时的法律规定，当时没有法律规定的，可以适用合同法的有关规定。 第二条 合同成立于合同法实施之前，但合同约定的履行期限跨越合同法实施之日或者履行期限在合同法实施之后，因履行合同发生的纠纷，适用合同法第四章的有关规定。 第三条 人民法院确认合同效力时，对合同法实施以前成立的合同，适用当时的法律合同无效而适用合同法合同有效的，则适用合同法。 第四条 合同法实施以后，人民法院确认合同无效，应当以全国人大及其常委会制定的法律和国务院制定的行政法规为依据，不得以地方性法规、行政规章为依据。 第五条 人民法院对合同法实施以前已经作出终审裁决的案件进行再审，不适用合同法。 二、诉讼时效 第六条 技术合同争议当事人的权利受到侵害的事实发生在合同法实施之前，自当事人知道或者应当知道其权利受到侵害之日起至合同法实施之日超过一年的，人民法院不予保护；尚未超过一年的，其提起诉讼的时效期间为二年。 第七条 技术进出口合同争议当事人的权利受到侵害的事实发生在合同法实施之前，自当事人知道或者应当知道其权利受到侵害之日起至合同法施行之日超过二年的，人民法院不予保护；尚未超过二年的，其提起诉讼的时效期间为四年。 第八条 合同法第五十五条规定的“一年”、第七十五条和第一百零四条第二款规定的“五年”为不变期间，不适用诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长的规定。 三、合同效力 第九条 依照合同法第四十四条第二款的规定，法律、行政法规规定合同应当办理批准手续，或者办理批准、登记等手续才生效，在一审法庭辩论终结前当事人仍未办理批准手续的，或者仍未办理批准、登记等手续的，人民法院应当认定该合同未生效；法律、行政法规规定合同应当办理登记手续，但未规定登记后生效的，当事人未办理登记手续不影响合同的效力，合同标的物所有权及其他物权不能转移。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 合同法实施以后成立的合同发生纠纷起诉到人民法院的，适用合同法的规定；合同法实施以前成立的合同发生纠纷起诉到人民法院的，除本解释另有规定的以外，适用当时的法律规定，当时没有法律规定的，可以适用合同法的有关规定。

**Article 1** Where a dispute arising from a contract concluded after the Contract Law comes into force is prosecuted before a People's Court, the provisions of the Contract Law shall apply; where a dispute arising from a contract concluded before the Contract Law comes into force is prosecuted before a People's Court, unless otherwise provided in this Interpretation, the laws in effect at that time shall apply, and if there were no such laws at that time, the relevant provisions of the Contract Law may apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 合同成立于合同法实施之前，但合同约定的履行期限跨越合同法实施之日或者履行期限在合同法实施之后，因履行合同发生的纠纷，适用合同法第四章的有关规定。

**Article 2** Where a contract is concluded before the Contract Law comes into force, but the performance period agreed in the contract spans the date on which the Contract Law comes into force or the performance period is after the Contract Law comes into force, disputes arising from the performance of the contract shall be governed by the relevant provisions of Chapter IV of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 人民法院确认合同效力时，对合同法实施以前成立的合同，适用当时的法律合同无效而适用合同法合同有效的，则适用合同法。

**Article 3** When a People's Court confirms the validity of a contract, if, for a contract concluded before the Contract Law comes into force, the application of the laws in effect at that time would render the contract void but the application of the Contract Law would render the contract valid, then the Contract Law shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 合同法实施以后，人民法院确认合同无效，应当以全国人大及其常委会制定的法律和国务院制定的行政法规为依据，不得以地方性法规、行政规章为依据。

**Article 4** After the Contract Law comes into force, a People's Court, when confirming a contract as void, shall base its decision on laws enacted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee and administrative regulations formulated by the State Council, and shall not base its decision on local regulations or administrative rules.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院对合同法实施以前已经作出终审裁决的案件进行再审，不适用合同法。  
二、诉讼时效

**Article 5** A People's Court shall not apply the Contract Law when conducting a retrial of a case for which a final judgment has been rendered before the Contract Law comes into force.  
II. Limitation of Action

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 技术合同争议当事人的权利受到侵害的事实发生在合同法实施之前，自当事人知道或者应当知道其权利受到侵害之日起至合同法实施之日超过一年的，人民法院不予保护；尚未超过一年的，其提起诉讼的时效期间为二年。

**Article 6** Where the fact that the rights of a party to a technology contract dispute are infringed occurs before the Contract Law comes into force, and more than one year has elapsed from the date on which the party knew or should have known that its rights were infringed to the date on which the Contract Law comes into force, the People's Court shall not grant protection; if less than one year has elapsed, the limitation period for prosecution shall be two years.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 技术进出口合同争议当事人的权利受到侵害的事实发生在合同法实施之前，自当事人知道或者应当知道其权利受到侵害之日起至合同法施行之日超过二年的，人民法院不予保护；尚未超过二年的，其提起诉讼的时效期间为四年。

**Article 7** Where the fact that the rights of a party to a technology import or export contract dispute are infringed occurs before the Contract Law comes into force, and more than two years have elapsed from the date on which the party knew or should have known that its rights were infringed to the date on which the Contract Law comes into force, the People's Court shall not grant protection; if less than two years have elapsed, the limitation period for prosecution shall be four years.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 合同法第五十五条规定的“一年”、第七十五条和第一百零四条第二款规定的“五年”为不变期间，不适用诉讼时效中止、中断或者延长的规定。  
三、合同效力

**Article 8** The 'one year' prescribed in Article 55 and the 'five years' prescribed in Article 75 and Article 104, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law are unalterable periods, and the provisions on suspension, interruption or extension of limitation of action shall not apply.  
III. Validity of Contracts

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 依照合同法第四十四条第二款的规定，法律、行政法规规定合同应当办理批准手续，或者办理批准、登记等手续才生效，在一审法庭辩论终结前当事人仍未办理批准手续的，或者仍未办理批准、登记等手续的，人民法院应当认定该合同未生效；法律、行政法规规定合同应当办理登记手续，但未规定登记后生效的，当事人未办理登记手续不影响合同的效力，合同标的物所有权及其他物权不能转移。  
合同法第七十七条第二款、第八十七条、第九十六条第二款所列合同变更、转让、解除等情形，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 9** Where, in accordance with the provisions of Article 44, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law, laws or administrative regulations stipulate that a contract shall undergo approval procedures, or shall undergo approval, registration or other procedures before taking effect, and the parties have not yet undergone the approval procedures, or have not yet undergone the approval, registration or other procedures, by the end of the court debate in the first instance, the People's Court shall determine that the contract has not taken effect; where laws or administrative regulations stipulate that a contract shall undergo registration procedures but do not stipulate that the contract takes effect upon registration, the failure of the parties to undergo registration procedures shall not affect the validity of the contract, but the ownership and other real rights in the subject matter of the contract shall not be transferred.  
Where a contract is modified, assigned, or terminated under circumstances listed in Article 77, paragraph 2, Article 87, and Article 96, paragraph 2 of the Contract Law, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 当事人超越经营范围订立合同，人民法院不因此认定合同无效。但违反国家限制经营、特许经营以及法律、行政法规禁止经营规定的除外。  
四、代位权

**Article 10** Where a party concludes a contract beyond its business scope, the People's Court shall not determine the contract void on that ground, unless the contract violates the provisions on restricted operations, franchised operations, or operations prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.  
IV. Subrogation Right

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 债权人依照合同法第七十三条的规定提起代位权诉讼，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）债权人对债务人的债权合法；  
（二）债务人怠于行使其到期债权，对债权人造成损害；  
（三）债务人的债权已到期；  
（四）债务人的债权不是专属于债务人自身的债权。

**Article 11** A creditor who institutes an action for subrogation pursuant to Article 73 of the Contract Law shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The creditor's claim against the debtor is lawful;  
(2) The debtor is indolent in exercising its due claim, thereby causing damage to the creditor;  
(3) The debtor's claim has become due; and  
(4) The debtor's claim is not exclusively personal to the debtor.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 合同法第七十三条第一款规定的专属于债务人自身的债权，是指基于扶养关系、抚养关系、赡养关系、继承关系产生的给付请求权和劳动报酬、退休金、养老金、抚恤金、安置费、人寿保险、人身伤害赔偿请求权等权利。

**Article 12** The claim exclusively personal to the debtor as provided in Article 73, paragraph 1 of the Contract Law means the right to claim payment arising from relationships of maintenance, upbringing, support, or succession, and rights such as labor remuneration, retirement pension, old-age pension, pension for the disabled or the family of the deceased, resettlement fee, life insurance, and claims for personal injury compensation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 合同法第七十三条规定的“债务人怠于行使其到期债权，对债权人造成损害的”，是指债务人不履行其对债权人的到期债务，又不以诉讼方式或者仲裁方式向其债务人主张其享有的具有金钱给付内容的到期债权，致使债权人的到期债权未能实现。  
次债务人（即债务人的债务人）不认为债务人有怠于行使其到期债权情况的，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 13** The phrase 'the debtor is indolent in exercising its due claim, thereby causing damage to the creditor' as provided in Article 73 of the Contract Law means that the debtor fails to perform its due obligations to the creditor and does not assert its due claim with monetary payment content against its own debtor through litigation or arbitration, thereby causing the creditor's due claim not to be realized.  
Where the secondary debtor (i.e., the debtor of the debtor) does not consider that the debtor is indolent in exercising its due claim, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 债权人依照合同法第七十三条的规定提起代位权诉讼的，由被告住所地人民法院管辖。

**Article 14** Where a creditor institutes an action for subrogation pursuant to Article 73 of the Contract Law, the action shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the defendant.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 债权人向人民法院起诉债务人以后，又向同一人民法院对次债务人提起代位权诉讼，符合[本解释第十四条](#art-14)的规定和[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-108)规定的起诉条件的，应当立案受理；不符合[本解释第十四条](#art-14)规定的，告知债权人向次债务人住所地人民法院另行起诉。  
受理代位权诉讼的人民法院在债权人起诉债务人的诉讼裁决发生法律效力以前，应当依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-136)第（五）项的规定中止代位权诉讼。

**Article 15** Where, after instituting an action against the debtor before a People's Court, a creditor institutes an action for subrogation against the secondary debtor before the same People's Court, if such action meets the conditions for prosecution set forth in Article 14 of this Interpretation and Article 108 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the court shall place the case on the docket and accept it; if it does not meet the conditions set forth in Article 14 of this Interpretation, the creditor shall be notified to institute a separate action before the People's Court at the domicile of the secondary debtor.  
Before the judgment or ruling in the action instituted by the creditor against the debtor takes legal effect, the People's Court accepting the action for subrogation shall suspend the action for subrogation in accordance with Article 136, item (5) of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 债权人以次债务人为被告向人民法院提起代位权诉讼，未将债务人列为第三人的，人民法院可以追加债务人为第三人。  
两个或者两个以上债权人以同一次债务人为被告提起代位权诉讼的，人民法院可以合并审理。

**Article 16** Where a creditor institutes an action for subrogation before a People's Court against the secondary debtor as defendant without listing the debtor as a third party, the People's Court may add the debtor as a third party.  
Where two or more creditors institute actions for subrogation against the same secondary debtor as defendant, the People's Court may consolidate the hearings.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 在代位权诉讼中，债权人请求人民法院对次债务人的财产采取保全措施的，应当提供相应的财产担保。

**Article 17** In an action for subrogation, where a creditor requests the People's Court to adopt preservation measures against the property of the secondary debtor, the creditor shall provide corresponding property guarantee.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 在代位权诉讼中，次债务人对债务人的抗辩，可以向债权人主张。  
债务人在代位权诉讼中对债权人的债权提出异议，经审查异议成立的，人民法院应当裁定驳回债权人的起诉。

**Article 18** In an action for subrogation, the secondary debtor may assert against the creditor any defense that it has against the debtor.  
Where the debtor raises an objection to the creditor's claim in the action for subrogation, and upon examination the objection is found to be established, the People's Court shall issue a ruling to dismiss the creditor's action.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 在代位权诉讼中，债权人胜诉的，诉讼费由次债务人负担，从实现的债权中优先支付。

**Article 19** In an action for subrogation, where the creditor wins the case, the litigation costs shall be borne by the secondary debtor and shall be paid in priority from the realized claim.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 债权人向次债务人提起的代位权诉讼经人民法院审理后认定代位权成立的，由次债务人向债权人履行清偿义务，债权人与债务人、债务人与次债务人之间相应的债权债务关系即予消灭。

**Article 20** Where, upon trial by a People's Court, the subrogation right is established in an action for subrogation initiated by a creditor against the secondary debtor, the secondary debtor shall perform its obligation of satisfaction to the creditor, and the corresponding creditor-debtor relationships between the creditor and the debtor, and between the debtor and the secondary debtor shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 在代位权诉讼中，债权人行使代位权的请求数额超过债务人所负债务额或者超过次债务人对债务人所负债务额的，对超出部分人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** In an action for subrogation, where the amount claimed by the creditor in exercising the subrogation right exceeds the amount of the debt owed by the debtor or exceeds the amount of the debt owed by the secondary debtor to the debtor, the People's Court shall not support the excess portion.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 债务人在代位权诉讼中，对超过债权人代位请求数额的债权部分起诉次债务人的。人民法院应当告知其向有管辖权的人民法院另行起诉。  
债务人的起诉符合法定条件的，人民法院应当受理；受理债务人起诉的人民法院在代位权诉讼裁决发生法律效力以前，应当依法中止。  
五、撤销权

**Article 22** Where, in an action for subrogation, the debtor institutes an action against the secondary debtor with respect to the part of its claim exceeding the amount claimed by the creditor in subrogation, the People's Court shall notify the debtor to institute a separate action before a People's Court having jurisdiction.  
Where the debtor's action meets the statutory conditions, the People's Court shall accept it; the People's Court that accepts the debtor's action shall suspend the action according to law before the ruling on the action for subrogation takes legal effect.  
V. Right of Revocation

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 债权人依照合同法第七十四条的规定提起撤销权诉讼的，由被告住所地人民法院管辖。

**Article 23** Where a creditor institutes an action for revocation pursuant to Article 74 of the Contract Law, the action shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the defendant.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 债权人依照合同法第七十四条的规定提起撤销权诉讼时只以债务人为被告，未将受益人或者受让人列为第三人的，人民法院可以追加该受益人或者受让人为第三人。

**Article 24** Where, when instituting an action for revocation pursuant to Article 74 of the Contract Law, a creditor only names the debtor as defendant and does not list the beneficiary or the assignee as a third party, the People's Court may add the beneficiary or the assignee as a third party.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 债权人依照合同法第七十四条的规定提起撤销权诉讼，请求人民法院撤销债务人放弃债权或转让财产的行为，人民法院应当就债权人主张的部分进行审理，依法撤销的，该行为自始无效。  
两个或者两个以上债权人以同一债务人为被告，就同一标的提起撤销权诉讼的，人民法院可以合并审理。

**Article 25** Where a creditor institutes an action for revocation pursuant to Article 74 of the Contract Law, requesting the People's Court to revoke the debtor's act of relinquishing a claim or transferring property, the People's Court shall try the case with respect to the part claimed by the creditor; if it revokes the act according to law, the act shall be void ab initio.  
Where two or more creditors, naming the same debtor as defendant, institute actions for revocation with respect to the same subject matter, the People's Court may consolidate the hearings.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 债权人行使撤销权所支付的律师代理费、差旅费等必要费用，由债务人负担；第三人有过错的，应当适当分担。  
六、合同转让中的第三人

**Article 26** The necessary expenses such as attorney's fees and travel expenses paid by the creditor in exercising the right of revocation shall be borne by the debtor; if a third party is at fault, the third party shall bear a proper share.  
VI. Third Party in Assignment of Contracts

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 债权人转让合同权利后，债务人与受让人之间因履行合同发生纠纷诉至人民法院，债务人对债权人的权利提出抗辩的，可以将债权人列为第三人。

**Article 27** After the creditor assigns its contractual rights, where a dispute arising from the performance of the contract between the debtor and the assignee is brought before a People's Court, and the debtor raises a defense against the creditor's rights, the creditor may be listed as a third party.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 经债权人同意，债务人转移合同义务后，受让人与债权人之间因履行合同发生纠纷诉至人民法院，受让人就债务人对债权人的权利提出抗辩的，可以将债务人列为第三人。

**Article 28** After the debtor transfers its contractual obligations with the consent of the creditor, where a dispute arising from the performance of the contract between the assignee and the creditor is brought before a People's Court, and the assignee raises a defense against the debtor's rights vis-à-vis the creditor, the debtor may be listed as a third party.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 合同当事人一方经对方同意将其在合同中的权利义务一并转让给受让人，对方与受让人因履行合同发生纠纷诉至人民法院，对方就合同权利义务提出抗辩的，可以将出让方列为第三人。  
七、请求权竞合

**Article 29** Where one party to a contract, with the consent of the other party, assigns its rights and obligations under the contract to an assignee, and a dispute arising from the performance of the contract between the other party and the assignee is brought before a People's Court, and the other party raises a defense regarding the contractual rights and obligations, the assigning party may be listed as a third party.  
VII. Concurrence of Claims

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 债权人依照合同法第一百二十二条的规定向人民法院起诉时作出选择后，在一审开庭以前又变更诉讼请求的，人民法院应当准许。对方当事人提出管辖权异议，经审查异议成立的，人民法院应当驳回起诉。

**Article 30** Where a creditor, after making a choice when instituting an action before a People's Court pursuant to Article 122 of the Contract Law, changes its claim before the opening of the court session in the first instance, the People's Court shall permit the change. If the other party raises an objection to jurisdiction, and upon examination the objection is found to be established, the People's Court shall dismiss the action.
