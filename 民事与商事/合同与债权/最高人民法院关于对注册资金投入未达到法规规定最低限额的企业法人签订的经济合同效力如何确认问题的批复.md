# 最高人民法院关于对注册资金投入未达到法规规定最低限额的企业法人签订的经济合同效力如何确认问题的批复

### 最高人民法院关于对注册资金投入未达到法规规定最低限额的企业法人签订的经济合同效力如何确认问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对注册资金投入未达到法规规定  
  
最低限额的企业法人签订的经济合同效力  
  
如何确认问题的批复  
  
法复〔1997〕2号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1996）苏经请字第 4号《关于对注册资金投入未达到法规规定最低限额的企业法人签订的经济合同效力如何确认问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
企业法人注册资金投入未达到法规规定的最低限额，在对外承担民事责任时，应根据本院1994年3月30日法复〔1994〕4号《关于企业开办的其他企业被撤销或者歇业后民事责任承担问题的批复》第一条第三项的规定处理，即其民事责任由开办该企业的企业法人承担。为了稳定经济秩序，保护权利人的合法权益，对这类企业法人被依法吊销《企业法人营业执照》之前签订的经济合同，不宜因其注册资金投入未达到法规规定的最低限额而确认为无效。  
  
此复。  
  
1997年2月25日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Confirm the Effect of Economic Contracts Signed by Enterprise Legal Persons Whose Registered Capital Investment Has Not Reached the Statutory Minimum  
Fa Fu [1997] No. 2  
Higher People's Court of Jiangsu Province:  
Your Court's Petition (1996) Su Jing Qing Zi No. 4, "Petition on How to Confirm the Effect of Economic Contracts Signed by Enterprise Legal Persons Whose Registered Capital Investment Has Not Reached the Statutory Minimum," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where the registered capital investment of an enterprise legal person has not reached the statutory minimum, when it bears civil liability externally, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Item (3) of Article 1 of this Court's Reply No. Fa Fu [1994] No. 4 dated March 30, 1994, "Reply on Issues Concerning the Assumption of Civil Liability After Other Enterprises Established by an Enterprise Are Dissolved or Closed Down," i.e., the civil liability shall be borne by the enterprise legal person that established such enterprise. In order to stabilize the economic order and protect the lawful rights and interests of obligees, the economic contracts signed before such enterprise legal persons are lawfully revoked of their "Business License for Enterprise Legal Persons" shall not be confirmed as null and void merely because their registered capital investment has not reached the statutory minimum.  
Reply hereby.  
February 25, 1997
