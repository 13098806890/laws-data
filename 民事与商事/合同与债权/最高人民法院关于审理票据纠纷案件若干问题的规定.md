# 最高人民法院关于审理票据纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕32号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-14  
**生效日期**：2000-11-21

> （2000年2月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1102次会议通过　2000年11月14日最高人民法院公告公布　自2000年11月21日起施行） 为了正确适用《中华人民共和国票据法》（以下简称票据法），公正、及时审理票据纠纷案件，保护票据当事人的合法权益，维护金融秩序和金融安全，根据票据法及其他有关法律的规定，结合审判实践，现对人民法院审理票据纠纷案件的若干问题规定如下： 一、受理和管辖 第一条　因行使票据权利或者票据法上的非票据权利而引起的纠纷，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条　依照票据法第十条的规定，票据债务人（即出票人）以在票据未转让时的基础关系违法、双方不具有真实的交易关系和债权债务关系、持票人应付对价而未付对价为由，要求返还票据而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第三条　依照票据法第三十六条的规定，票据被拒绝承兑、被拒绝付款或者汇票、支票超过提示付款期限后，票据持有人背书转让的，被背书人以背书人为被告行使追索权而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第四条　持票人不先行使付款请求权而先行使追索权遭拒绝提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。除有票据法第六十一条第二款和本规定第三条所列情形外，持票人只能在首先向付款人行使付款请求权而得不到付款时，才可以行使追索权。 第五条　付款请求权是持票人享有的第一顺序权利，追索权是持票人享有的第二顺序权利，即汇票到期被拒绝付款或者具有票据法第六十一条第二款所列情形的，持票人请求背书人、出票人以及汇票的其他债务人支付票据法第七十条第一款所列金额和费用的权利。 第六条　因票据权利纠纷提起的诉讼，依法由票据支付地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。 票据支付地是指票据上载明的付款地，票据上未载明付款地的，汇票付款人或者代理付款人的营业场所、住所或者经常居住地，本票出票人的营业场所，支票付款人或者代理付款人的营业场所所在地为票据付款地。代理付款人即付款人的委托代理人，是指根据付款人的委托代为支付票据金额的银行、信用合作社等金融机构。 第七条　因非票据权利纠纷提起的诉讼，依法由被告住所地人民法院管辖。 二、票据保全 第八条　人民法院在审理、执行票据纠纷案件时，对具有下列情形之一的票据，经当事人申请并提供担保，可以依法采取保全措施或者执行措施： （一）不履行约定义务，与票据债务人有直接债权债务关系的票据当事人所持有的票据； （二）持票人恶意取得的票据；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　因行使票据权利或者票据法上的非票据权利而引起的纠纷，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** A People's Court shall accept and hear according to law any dispute arising from the exercise of any instrument right or any non-instrument right under the Law of Negotiable Instruments.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依照[票据法第十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-10)的规定，票据债务人（即出票人）以在票据未转让时的基础关系违法、双方不具有真实的交易关系和债权债务关系、持票人应付对价而未付对价为由，要求返还票据而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 2** Where, in accordance with Article 10 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the drawer, as the instrument debtor, brings an action on the grounds that the underlying relationship prior to the transfer of the instrument is illegal, the parties do not have a genuine transaction relationship or creditor-debtor relationship, or the holder should have paid consideration but failed to do so, and requests the return of the instrument, the People's Court shall accept and hear the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依照[票据法第三十六条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-36)的规定，票据被拒绝承兑、被拒绝付款或者汇票、支票超过提示付款期限后，票据持有人背书转让的，被背书人以背书人为被告行使追索权而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 3** Where, in accordance with Article 36 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, after an instrument has been dishonored by acceptance or dishonored by payment, or after a bill of exchange or cheque has exceeded the time limit for presentment for payment, the holder endorses and transfers the instrument, and the endorsee brings an action against the endorser as the defendant to exercise the right of recourse, the People's Court shall accept and hear the case according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　持票人不先行使付款请求权而先行使追索权遭拒绝提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。除有[票据法第六十一条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-61)第二款和[本规定第三条](#art-3)所列情形外，持票人只能在首先向付款人行使付款请求权而得不到付款时，才可以行使追索权。

**Article 4** Where a holder, without first exercising the right of claim for payment, exercises the right of recourse and, after being refused, brings an action, the People's Court shall not accept the case. Except for the circumstances set forth in Article 61, paragraph 2 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments and Article 3 of these Provisions, the holder may only exercise the right of recourse when it has first exercised the right of claim for payment against the payer and failed to obtain payment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　付款请求权是持票人享有的第一顺序权利，追索权是持票人享有的第二顺序权利，即汇票到期被拒绝付款或者具有[票据法第六十一条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-61)第二款所列情形的，持票人请求背书人、出票人以及汇票的其他债务人支付[票据法第七十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-70)第一款所列金额和费用的权利。

**Article 5** The right of claim for payment is the right of the first order enjoyed by the holder, and the right of recourse is the right of the second order enjoyed by the holder. That is, where a bill of exchange is dishonored by payment at maturity or where any of the circumstances set forth in Article 61, paragraph 2 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments occurs, the holder has the right to request the endorser, the drawer, and other obligors on the bill of exchange to pay the amounts and expenses set forth in Article 70, paragraph 1 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　因票据权利纠纷提起的诉讼，依法由票据支付地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
票据支付地是指票据上载明的付款地，票据上未载明付款地的，汇票付款人或者代理付款人的营业场所、住所或者经常居住地，本票出票人的营业场所，支票付款人或者代理付款人的营业场所所在地为票据付款地。代理付款人即付款人的委托代理人，是指根据付款人的委托代为支付票据金额的银行、信用合作社等金融机构。

**Article 6** An action brought on a dispute over instrument rights shall have jurisdiction, according to law, in the People's Court of the place of instrument payment or of the defendant's domicile.  
The place of instrument payment refers to the place of payment specified on the instrument. If no place of payment is specified on the instrument, the place of instrument payment shall be the business premises, domicile, or habitual residence of the payer or agent payer for a bill of exchange, the business premises of the drawer for a promissory note, and the location of the business premises of the payer or agent payer for a cheque. An agent payer, i.e., an authorized agent of the payer, refers to a bank, credit cooperative, or other financial institution that makes payment of the instrument amount on behalf of the payer according to the payer's authorization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　因非票据权利纠纷提起的诉讼，依法由被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
二、票据保全

**Article 7** An action brought on a dispute over non-instrument rights shall have jurisdiction, according to law, in the People's Court of the defendant's domicile.  
II. Preservation of Instruments

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院在审理、执行票据纠纷案件时，对具有下列情形之一的票据，经当事人申请并提供担保，可以依法采取保全措施或者执行措施：  
（一）不履行约定义务，与票据债务人有直接债权债务关系的票据当事人所持有的票据；  
（二）持票人恶意取得的票据；  
（三）应付对价而未付对价的持票人持有的票据；  
（四）记载有“不得转让”字样而用于贴现的票据；  
（五）记载有“不得转让”字样而用于质押的票据；  
（六）法律或者司法解释规定有其他情形的票据。  
三、举证责任

**Article 8** When a People's Court is hearing or enforcing a case concerning an instrument dispute, it may, upon application by a party and provision of security, take preservation or enforcement measures with respect to an instrument in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The instrument is held by a party that has a direct creditor-debtor relationship with the instrument debtor and fails to perform its agreed obligations;  
(2) The instrument is acquired by the holder in bad faith;  
(3) The instrument is held by a holder that should have paid consideration but fails to do so;  
(4) The instrument bears the words 'not transferable' and is used for discount;  
(5) The instrument bears the words 'not transferable' and is used for pledge;  
(6) Other circumstances provided by laws or judicial interpretations.  
III. Burden of Adducing Evidence

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　票据诉讼的举证责任由提出主张的一方当事人承担。  
依照[票据法第四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-4)第二款、第十条、第十二条、第二十一条的规定，向人民法院提起诉讼的持票人有责任提供诉争票据。该票据的出票、承兑、交付、背书转让涉嫌欺诈、偷盗、胁迫、恐吓、暴力等非法行为的，持票人对持票的合法性应当负责举证。

**Article 9** The burden of adducing evidence in an instrument action shall be borne by the party making the claim.  
In accordance with Article 4, paragraph 2, Article 10, Article 12, and Article 21 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the holder bringing an action in a People's Court has the responsibility to provide the instrument in dispute. Where the drawing, acceptance, delivery, endorsement, or transfer of the instrument is suspected to involve illegal acts such as fraud, theft, coercion, intimidation, or violence, the holder shall bear the burden of proving the legality of holding the instrument.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　票据债务人依照[票据法第十三条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-13)的规定，对与其有直接债权债务关系的持票人提出抗辩，人民法院合并审理票据关系和基础关系的，持票人应当提供相应的证据证明已经履行了约定义务。

**Article 10** Where an instrument debtor raises a defense against a holder with whom it has a direct creditor-debtor relationship in accordance with Article 13 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, and the People's Court consolidates the hearing of the instrument relationship and the underlying relationship, the holder shall provide corresponding evidence proving that it has performed the agreed obligations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　付款人或者承兑人被人民法院依法宣告破产的，持票人因行使追索权而向人民法院提起诉讼时，应当向受理法院提供人民法院依法作出的宣告破产裁定书或者能够证明付款人或者承兑人破产的其他证据。

**Article 11** Where the payer or acceptor is declared bankrupt according to law by a People's Court, a holder bringing an action in a People's Court to exercise the right of recourse shall provide to the accepting court the ruling of declaration of bankruptcy made according to law by the People's Court, or other evidence capable of proving the bankruptcy of the payer or acceptor.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在票据诉讼中，负有举证责任的票据当事人应当在一审人民法院法庭辩论结束以前提供证据。因客观原因不能在上述举证期限以内提供的，应当在举证期限届满以前向人民法院申请延期。延长的期限由人民法院根据案件的具体情况决定。  
票据当事人在一审人民法院审理期间隐匿票据、故意有证不举，应当承担相应的诉讼后果。  
四、票据权利及抗辩

**Article 12** In an instrument action, the instrument party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall provide evidence before the conclusion of the court debate in the first-instance People's Court. If it is unable to provide evidence within the above-mentioned time limit for adducing evidence due to objective reasons, it shall apply to the People's Court for an extension before the expiration of the time limit. The extended time limit shall be determined by the People's Court according to the specific circumstances of the case.  
If an instrument party conceals the instrument or intentionally withholds evidence during the trial by the first-instance People's Court, it shall bear the corresponding litigation consequences.  
IV. Instrument Rights and Defenses

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[票据法第十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-17)第一款第（一）、（二）项规定的持票人对票据的出票人和承兑人的权利，包括付款请求权和追索权。

**Article 13** The rights of a holder against the drawer and acceptor of an instrument as provided in Article 17, paragraph 1, items (1) and (2) of the Law of Negotiable Instruments shall include the right of claim for payment and the right of recourse.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　票据债务人以[票据法第十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-10)、第二十一条的规定为由，对业经背书转让票据的持票人进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where an instrument debtor defends against a holder of an instrument that has been endorsed and transferred on the grounds of Article 10 or Article 21 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　票据债务人依照[票据法第十二条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-12)、第十三条的规定，对持票人提出下列抗辩的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）与票据债务人有直接债权债务关系并且不履行约定义务的；  
（二）以欺诈、偷盗或者胁迫等非法手段取得票据，或者明知有前列情形，出于恶意取得票据的；  
（三）明知票据债务人与出票人或者与持票人的前手之间存在抗辩事由而取得票据的；  
（四）因重大过失取得票据的；  
（五）其他依法不得享有票据权利的。

**Article 15** Where an instrument debtor raises the following defenses against a holder in accordance with Articles 12 and 13 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the People's Court shall support such defenses:  
(1) The holder has a direct creditor-debtor relationship with the instrument debtor and fails to perform its agreed obligations;  
(2) The holder acquires the instrument by illegal means such as fraud, theft, or coercion, or acquires the instrument in bad faith knowing the existence of the foregoing circumstances;  
(3) The holder acquires the instrument knowing that there are grounds for defense between the instrument debtor and the drawer or between the instrument debtor and the prior party of the holder;  
(4) The holder acquires the instrument through gross negligence;  
(5) Other circumstances where the holder is not entitled to instrument rights according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　票据债务人依照[票据法第九条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-9)、第十七条、第十八条、第二十二条和第三十一条的规定，对持票人提出下列抗辩的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）欠缺法定必要记载事项或者不符合法定格式的；  
（二）超过票据权利时效的；  
（三）人民法院作出的除权判决已经发生法律效力的；  
（四）以背书方式取得但背书不连续的；  
（五）其他依法不得享有票据权利的。

**Article 16** Where an instrument debtor raises the following defenses against a holder in accordance with Articles 9, 17, 18, 22, and 31 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the People's Court shall support such defenses:  
(1) The instrument lacks mandatory statutory particulars or does not conform to statutory form;  
(2) The time limit for instrument rights has expired;  
(3) A judgment of nullity rendered by the People's Court has become legally effective;  
(4) The instrument is acquired by endorsement but the endorsements are not consecutive;  
(5) Other circumstances where the holder is not entitled to instrument rights according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　票据出票人或者背书人被宣告破产的，而付款人或者承兑人不知其事实而付款或者承兑，因此所产生的追索权可以登记为破产债权，付款人或者承兑人为债权人。

**Article 17** Where the drawer or endorser of an instrument is declared bankrupt, and the payer or acceptor makes payment or acceptance without knowledge of such fact, the right of recourse arising therefrom may be registered as a bankruptcy claim, with the payer or acceptor as the creditor.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[票据法第十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-17)第一款第（三）、（四）项规定的持票人对前手的追索权，不包括对票据出票人的追索权。

**Article 18** The right of recourse of a holder against prior parties as provided in Article 17, paragraph 1, items (3) and (4) of the Law of Negotiable Instruments shall not include the right of recourse against the drawer of the instrument.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[票据法第四十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-40)第二款和第六十五条规定的持票人丧失对其前手的追索权，不包括对票据出票人的追索权。

**Article 19** The loss of the right of recourse of a holder against its prior parties as provided in Article 40, paragraph 2 and Article 65 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments shall not include the right of recourse against the drawer of the instrument.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　[票据法第十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-17)规定的票据权利时效发生中断的，只对发生时效中断事由的当事人有效。

**Article 20** Where the time limit for instrument rights as provided in Article 17 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments is interrupted, such interruption shall only be effective against the party that causes the interruption.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[票据法第六十六条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-66)第一款规定的书面通知是否逾期，以持票人或者其前手发出书面通知之日为准；以信函通知的，以信函投寄邮戳记载之日为准。

**Article 21** Whether the written notice as provided in Article 66, paragraph 1 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments is overdue shall be determined by the date on which the holder or its prior party dispatches the written notice; if notification is made by letter, the date shall be the date recorded on the postmark of the letter.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[票据法第七十条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-70)、第七十一条所称中国人民银行规定的利率，是指中国人民银行规定的企业同期流动资金贷款利率。

**Article 22** The interest rate prescribed by the People's Bank of China as referred to in Articles 70 and 71 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments shall mean the interest rate for working capital loans for enterprises for the same period as prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　代理付款人在人民法院公示催告公告发布以前按照规定程序善意付款后，承兑人或者付款人以已经公示催告为由拒付代理付款人已经垫付的款项的，人民法院不予支持。  
五、失票救济

**Article 23** Where an agent payer makes payment in good faith in accordance with the prescribed procedures before the People's Court publishes the announcement of public summons for exhortation, and the acceptor or payer refuses to pay the amount advanced by the agent payer on the ground that the public summons for exhortation has been made, the People's Court shall not support such refusal.  
V. Remedies for Loss of Instrument

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　票据丧失后，失票人直接向人民法院申请公示催告或者提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 24** After an instrument is lost, if the lost instrument person directly applies to the People's Court for public summons for exhortation or brings an action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出票人已经签章的授权补记的支票丧失后，失票人依法向人民法院申请公示催告的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 25** After a cheque that has been signed by the drawer and is authorized to be supplemented by recording is lost, if the lost instrument person applies to the People's Court for public summons for exhortation according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　[票据法第十五条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-15)第三款规定的可以申请公示催告的失票人，是指按照规定可以背书转让的票据在丧失票据占有以前的最后合法持票人。

**Article 26** The lost instrument person that may apply for public summons for exhortation as provided in Article 15, paragraph 3 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments shall refer to the last lawful holder of an instrument that is transferable by endorsement according to the relevant provisions, before the loss of possession of the instrument.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　出票人已经签章但未记载代理付款人的银行汇票丧失后，失票人依法向付款人即出票银行所在地人民法院申请公示催告的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 27** After a bank draft signed by the drawer but without specifying the agent payer is lost, if the lost instrument person applies to the People's Court of the place where the payer, i.e., the drawer bank, is located for public summons for exhortation according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　超过付款提示期限的票据丧失以后，失票人申请公示催告的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 28** After an instrument exceeding the time limit for presentment for payment is lost, if the lost instrument person applies for public summons for exhortation, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　失票人通知票据付款人挂失止付后3日内向人民法院申请公示催告的，公示催告申请书应当载明下列内容：  
（一）票面金额；  
（二）出票人、持票人、背书人；  
（三）申请的理由、事实；  
（四）通知票据付款人或者代理付款人挂失止付的时间；  
（五）付款人或者代理付款人的名称、通信地址、电话号码等。

**Article 29** Where the lost instrument person applies to the People's Court for public summons for exhortation within three days after notifying the instrument payer to report the loss and stop payment, the application for public summons for exhortation shall state the following:  
(1) The face amount of the instrument;  
(2) The drawer, holder, and endorser;  
(3) The reasons and facts of the application;  
(4) The time of notifying the instrument payer or agent payer to report the loss and stop payment;  
(5) The name, mailing address, telephone number, etc., of the payer or agent payer.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院决定受理公示催告申请，应当同时通知付款人及代理付款人停止支付，并自立案之日起3日内发出公告。

**Article 30** Where a People's Court decides to accept an application for public summons for exhortation, it shall simultaneously notify the payer and the agent payer to stop payment, and shall issue an announcement within three days from the date of case filing.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　付款人或者代理付款人收到人民法院发出的止付通知，应当立即停止支付，直至公示催告程序终结。非经发出止付通知的人民法院许可擅自解付的，不得免除票据责任。

**Article 31** Upon receipt of the notice to stop payment issued by the People's Court, the payer or agent payer shall immediately stop payment until the conclusion of the public summons for exhortation proceedings. If any payment is made without the permission of the People's Court that issued the notice to stop payment, the instrument liability shall not be discharged.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院决定受理公示催告申请后发布的公告应当在全国性的报刊上登载。

**Article 32** The announcement issued by the People's Court after it decides to accept an application for public summons for exhortation shall be published in national newspapers or periodicals.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法）第一百九十四条的规定，公示催告的期间，国内票据自公告发布之日起60日，涉外票据可根据具体情况适当延长，但最长不得超过90日。

**Article 33** In accordance with Article 194 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Procedure Law), the period for public summons for exhortation shall be 60 days from the date of publication of the announcement for domestic instruments, and may be appropriately extended for foreign-related instruments based on specific circumstances, but shall not exceed 90 days at most.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　依照[民事诉讼法第一百九十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-195)第二款的规定，在公示催告期间，以公示催告的票据质押、贴现，因质押、贴现而接受该票据的持票人主张票据权利的，人民法院不予支持，但公示催告期间届满以后人民法院作出除权判决以前取得该票据的除外。

**Article 34** In accordance with Article 195, paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law, during the period of public summons for exhortation, if an instrument under public summons for exhortation is pledged or discounted, and the holder who accepts the instrument as a result of the pledge or discount claims instrument rights, the People's Court shall not support such claim, unless the instrument was acquired after the expiration of the period of public summons for exhortation but before the People's Court renders a judgment of nullity.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　票据丧失后，失票人在票据权利时效届满以前请求出票人补发票据，或者请求债务人付款，在提供相应担保的情况下因债务人拒绝付款或者出票人拒绝补发票据提起诉讼的，由被告住所地或者票据支付地人民法院管辖。

**Article 35** After an instrument is lost, if the lost instrument person requests the drawer to issue a duplicate instrument or requests the obligor to make payment before the expiration of the time limit for instrument rights, and brings an action due to the obligor's refusal to pay or the drawer's refusal to issue a duplicate instrument while providing corresponding security, the action shall have jurisdiction in the People's Court of the defendant's domicile or the place of instrument payment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　失票人因请求出票人补发票据或者请求债务人付款遭到拒绝而向人民法院提起诉讼的，被告为与失票人具有票据债权债务关系的出票人、拒绝付款的票据付款人或者承兑人。

**Article 36** Where the lost instrument person brings an action in a People's Court because its request to the drawer for issuance of a duplicate instrument or to the obligor for payment is refused, the defendant shall be the drawer that has a creditor-debtor instrument relationship with the lost instrument person, or the payer or acceptor of the instrument that refuses to make payment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　失票人为行使票据所有权，向非法持有票据人请求返还票据的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 37** Where the lost instrument person requests the return of the instrument from a person who illegally holds the instrument in order to exercise ownership of the instrument, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　失票人向人民法院提起诉讼的，除向人民法院说明曾经持有票据及丧失票据的情形外，还应当提供担保。担保的数额相当于票据载明的金额。

**Article 38** Where the lost instrument person brings an action in a People's Court, it shall, in addition to explaining to the People's Court the circumstances of having previously held the instrument and the loss of the instrument, also provide security. The amount of security shall be equivalent to the amount specified on the instrument.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对于伪报票据丧失的当事人，人民法院在查明事实，裁定终结公示催告或者诉讼程序后，可以参照[民事诉讼法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)的规定，追究伪报人的法律责任。  
六、票据效力

**Article 39** For a party that falsely reports the loss of an instrument, after ascertaining the facts and ruling to conclude the public summons for exhortation or litigation proceedings, the People's Court may, by reference to Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law, investigate the legal liability of the person making the false report.  
VI. Validity of Instruments

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依照[票据法第一百零九条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-109)以及经国务院批准的《票据管理实施办法》的规定，票据当事人使用的不是中国人民银行规定的统一格式票据的，按照《票据管理实施办法》的规定认定，但在中国境外签发的票据除外。

**Article 40** In accordance with Article 109 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments and the Measures for the Implementation of the Administration of Negotiable Instruments approved by the State Council, if an instrument party uses an instrument that is not in the uniform format prescribed by the People's Bank of China, the matter shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Implementation of the Administration of Negotiable Instruments, except for instruments issued outside the territory of China.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　票据出票人在票据上的签章上不符合票据法以及下述规定的，该签章不具有票据法上的效力：  
（一）商业汇票上的出票人的签章，为该法人或者该单位的财务专用章或者公章加其法定代表人、单位负责人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章；  
（二）银行汇票上的出票人的签章和银行承兑汇票的承兑人的签章，为该银行汇票专用章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章；  
（三）银行本票上的出票人的签章，为该银行的本票专用章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章；  
（四）支票上的出票人的签章，出票人为单位的，为与该单位在银行预留签章一致的财务专用章或者公章加其法定代表人或者其授权的代理人的签名或者盖章；出票人为个人的，为与该个人在银行预留签章一致的签名或者盖章。

**Article 41** Where the drawer's signature or seal on an instrument does not conform to the Law of Negotiable Instruments and the following provisions, such signature or seal shall have no effect under the Law of Negotiable Instruments:  
(1) The drawer's signature or seal on a commercial bill of exchange shall be the financial seal or official seal of the legal person or unit plus the signature or seal of its legal representative, responsible person, or authorized agent;  
(2) The drawer's signature or seal on a bank draft and the acceptor's signature or seal on a bank acceptance bill shall be the special seal for bank drafts of the bank plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or authorized agent;  
(3) The drawer's signature or seal on a bank promissory note shall be the special seal for promissory notes of the bank plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or authorized agent;  
(4) The drawer's signature or seal on a cheque: if the drawer is a unit, it shall be the financial seal or official seal consistent with the seal reserved by the unit at the bank plus the signature or seal of its legal representative or authorized agent; if the drawer is an individual, it shall be the signature or seal consistent with the signature or seal reserved by the individual at the bank.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　银行汇票、银行本票的出票人以及银行承兑汇票的承兑人在票据上未加盖规定的专用章而加盖该银行的公章，支票的出票人在票据上未加盖与该单位在银行预留签章一致的财务专用章而加盖该出票人公章的，签章人应当承担票据责任。

**Article 42** Where the drawer of a bank draft or bank promissory note or the acceptor of a bank acceptance bill fails to affix the prescribed special seal on the instrument but affixes the official seal of the bank, or where the drawer of a cheque fails to affix the financial seal consistent with the seal reserved by the unit at the bank on the instrument but affixes the official seal of the drawer, the person affixing the seal shall bear instrument liability.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　依照[票据法第九条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-9)以及《票据管理实施办法》的规定，票据金额的中文大写与数码不一致，或者票据载明的金额、出票日期或者签发日期、收款人名称更改，或者违反规定加盖银行部门印章代替专用章，付款人或者代理付款人对此类票据付款的，应当承担责任。

**Article 43** In accordance with Article 9 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments and the Measures for the Implementation of the Administration of Negotiable Instruments, if the amount in words does not correspond to the amount in figures, or the amount, the date of drawing or issuance, or the name of the payee specified on the instrument is altered, or the seal of a bank department is affixed in violation of the provisions in place of the special seal, and the payer or agent payer makes payment on such instrument, the payer or agent payer shall bear liability.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　因更改银行汇票的实际结算金额引起纠纷而提起诉讼，当事人请求认定汇票效力的，人民法院应当认定该银行汇票无效。

**Article 44** Where an action is brought due to a dispute arising from the alteration of the actual settlement amount of a bank draft, and a party requests a determination of the validity of the draft, the People's Court shall determine that the bank draft is null and void.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　空白授权票据的持票人行使票据权利时未对票据必须记载事项补充完全，因付款人或者代理付款人拒绝接收该票据而提起诉讼的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 45** Where the holder of an instrument with blank authorization fails to complete the mandatory particulars of the instrument when exercising instrument rights, and brings an action due to the payer's or agent payer's refusal to accept the instrument, the People's Court shall not support the action.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　票据的背书人、承兑人、保证人在票据上的签章不符合票据法以及《票据管理实施办法》规定的，或者无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人在票据上签章的，其签章无效，但不影响人民法院对票据上其他签章效力的认定。  
七、票据背书

**Article 46** Where the signature or seal of the endorser, acceptor, or guarantor of an instrument on the instrument does not conform to the provisions of the Law of Negotiable Instruments and the Measures for the Implementation of the Administration of Negotiable Instruments, or where a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity signs or seals on the instrument, such signature or seal shall be null and void, but shall not affect the determination by the People's Court of the validity of other signatures or seals on the instrument.  
VII. Endorsement of Instruments

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　因票据质权人以质押票据再行背书质押或者背书转让引起纠纷而提起诉讼的，人民法院应当认定背书行为无效。

**Article 47** Where an action is brought due to a dispute arising from a pledgee of an instrument re-endorsing the pledged instrument for pledge or endorsement for transfer, the People's Court shall determine that the endorsement act is null and void.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　依照[票据法第二十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-27)的规定，票据的出票人在票据上记载“不得转让”字样，票据持有人背书转让的，背书行为无效。背书转让后的受让人不得享有票据权利，票据的出票人、承兑人对受让人不承担票据责任。

**Article 48** In accordance with Article 27 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, if the drawer of an instrument records the words 'not transferable' on the instrument, and the holder endorses and transfers the instrument, the endorsement act shall be null and void. The transferee after such endorsement and transfer shall not enjoy instrument rights, and the drawer and acceptor of the instrument shall not bear instrument liability to the transferee.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　依照[票据法第二十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-27)和第三十条的规定，背书人未记载被背书人名称即将票据交付他人的，持票人在票据被背书人栏内记载自己的名称与背书人记载具有同等法律效力。

**Article 49** In accordance with Articles 27 and 30 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, if the endorser delivers the instrument to another person without recording the name of the endorsee, the recording by the holder of its own name in the endorsee column on the instrument shall have the same legal effect as the endorser's recording.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　依照[票据法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-31)的规定，连续背书的第一背书人应当是在票据上记载的收款人，最后的票据持有人应当是最后一次背书的被背书人。

**Article 50** In accordance with Article 31 of the Law of Negotiable Instruments, the first endorser of a consecutive endorsement shall be the payee recorded on the instrument, and the final holder of the instrument shall be the endorsee of the last endorsement.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　依照[票据法第三十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-34)和第三十五条的规定，背书人在票据上记载“不得转让”、“委托收款”、“质押”字样，其后手再背书转让、委托收款或者质押的，原背书人对后手的被背书人不承担票据责任，但不影响出票人、承兑人以及原背书人之前手的票据责任。

**Article 51** In accordance with the provisions of Articles 34 and 35 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where an endorser records on the instrument the words "not transferable," "collection on behalf," or "pledge," and the subsequent party re-endorses, transfers, collects on behalf, or pledges the instrument, the original endorser shall not bear instrument liability to the subsequent party's endorsee; however, this shall not affect the instrument liability of the drawer, the acceptor, and the persons prior to the original endorser.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　依照[票据法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-57)第二款的规定，贷款人恶意或者有重大过失从事票据质押贷款的，人民法院应当认定质押行为无效。

**Article 52** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 57 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where a lender engages in instrument pledge loans with malice or gross negligence, the People's Court shall determine that the pledge act is void.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　依照[票据法第二十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-27)的规定，出票人在票据上记载“不得转让”字样，其后手以此票据进行贴现、质押的，通过贴现、质押取得票据的持票人主张票据权利的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 53** In accordance with the provisions of Article 27 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where the drawer records on the instrument the words "not transferable," and the subsequent party discounts or pledges the instrument, and the holder who obtains the instrument through discount or pledge claims instrument rights, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依照[票据法第三十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-34)和第三十五条的规定，背书人在票据上记载“不得转让”字样，其后手以此票据进行贴现、质押的，原背书人对后手的被背书人不承担票据责任。

**Article 54** In accordance with the provisions of Articles 34 and 35 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where an endorser records on the instrument the words "not transferable," and the subsequent party discounts or pledges the instrument, the original endorser shall not bear instrument liability to the subsequent party's endorsee.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　依照[票据法第三十五条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-35)第二款的规定，以汇票设定质押时，出质人在汇票上只记载了“质押”字样未在票据上签章的，或者出质人未在汇票、粘单上记载“质押”字样而另行签订质押合同、质押条款的，不构成票据质押。

**Article 55** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 35 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where a pledgor, when creating a pledge on a bill of exchange, records only the word "pledge" on the bill but does not sign on the instrument, or where the pledgor does not record the word "pledge" on the bill or allonge but separately concludes a pledge contract or pledge clauses, the pledge shall not constitute an instrument pledge.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　商业汇票的持票人向其非开户银行申请贴现，与向自己开立存款账户的银行申请贴现具有同等法律效力。但是，持票人有恶意或者与贴现银行恶意串通的除外。

**Article 56** An application by the holder of a commercial bill of exchange to a bank other than its account bank for discount shall have the same legal effect as an application to the bank where it has a deposit account. However, this shall not apply where the holder acts with malice or colludes with the discount bank.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反规定区域出票，背书转让银行汇票，或者违反票据管理规定跨越票据交换区域出票、背书转让银行本票、支票的，不影响出票人、背书人依法应当承担的票据责任。

**Article 57** Where a drawer issues a bank draft or an endorser transfers it by endorsement in violation of the prescribed area, or where a drawer issues or an endorser transfers by endorsement a bank promissory note or cheque across the instrument exchange area in violation of the instrument administration regulations, this shall not affect the instrument liability that the drawer or endorser shall bear according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　依照[票据法第三十六条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-36)的规定，票据被拒绝承兑、被拒绝付款或者超过提示付款期限，票据持有人背书转让的，背书人应当承担票据责任。

**Article 58** In accordance with the provisions of Article 36 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where the instrument has been dishonored by acceptance, dishonored by payment, or has exceeded the time limit for presentment for payment, and the holder endorses and transfers it, the endorser shall bear instrument liability.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　承兑人或者付款人依照[票据法第五十三条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-53)第二款的规定对逾期提示付款的持票人付款与按照规定的期限付款具有同等法律效力。  
八、票据保证

**Article 59** Payment by the acceptor or payer to a holder who presents for payment after the time limit in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 53 of the Negotiable Instruments Law shall have the same legal effect as payment within the prescribed time limit.  
VIII. Instrument Guarantee

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家机关、以公益为目的的事业单位、社会团体、企业法人的分支机构和职能部门作为票据保证人的，票据保证无效，但经国务院批准为使用外国政府或者国际经济组织贷款进行转贷，国家机关提供票据保证的，以及企业法人的分支机构在法人书面授权范围内提供票据保证的除外。

**Article 60** Where a state organ, a public institution for public welfare purposes, a social organization, or a branch or functional department of an enterprise legal person acts as an instrument guarantor, the instrument guarantee shall be void; except where a state organ provides instrument guarantee with the approval of the State Council for on-lending using loans from foreign governments or international economic organizations, or where a branch of an enterprise legal person provides instrument guarantee within the scope of written authorization by the legal person.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　票据保证无效的，票据的保证人应当承担与其过错相应的民事责任。

**Article 61** Where the instrument guarantee is void, the guarantor of the instrument shall bear civil liability corresponding to its fault.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　保证人未在票据或者粘单上记载“保证”字样而另行签订保证合同或者保证条款的，不属于票据保证，人民法院应当适用《中华人民共和国担保法》的有关规定。  
九、法律适用

**Article 62** Where the guarantor does not record the word "guarantee" on the instrument or allonge but separately concludes a guarantee contract or guarantee clauses, such shall not constitute an instrument guarantee, and the People's Court shall apply the relevant provisions of the Security Law of the People's Republic of China.  
IX. Application of Law

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民法院审理票据纠纷案件，适用票据法的规定；票据法没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国担保法》等民商事法律以及国务院制定的行政法规。  
中国人民银行制定并公布施行的有关行政规章与法律、行政法规不抵触的，可以参照适用。

**Article 63** When hearing instrument dispute cases, the People's Court shall apply the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law; where the Negotiable Instruments Law has no provisions, it shall apply the civil and commercial laws such as the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Security Law of the People's Republic of China, as well as the administrative regulations formulated by the State Council.  
Where the relevant administrative regulations formulated and promulgated by the People's Bank of China do not conflict with the laws and administrative regulations, they may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　票据当事人因对金融行政管理部门的具体行政行为不服提起诉讼的，适用《中华人民共和国行政处罚法》、票据法以及《票据管理实施办法》等有关票据管理的规定。  
中国人民银行制定并公布施行的有关行政规章与法律、行政法规不抵触的，可以参照适用。

**Article 64** Where a party to an instrument initiates a lawsuit due to dissatisfaction with a specific administrative act of the financial administrative department, the provisions of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China, the Negotiable Instruments Law, and the Measures for the Implementation of the Administration of Negotiable Instruments and other relevant provisions on instrument administration shall apply.  
Where the relevant administrative regulations formulated and promulgated by the People's Bank of China do not conflict with the laws and administrative regulations, they may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　人民法院对票据法施行以前已经作出终审裁决的票据纠纷案件进行再审，不适用票据法。  
十、法律责任

**Article 65** Where the People's Court conducts a retrial of an instrument dispute case for which a final judgment has been made before the come into force of the Negotiable Instruments Law, the Negotiable Instruments Law shall not apply.  
X. Legal Liability

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　具有下列情形之一的票据，未经背书转让的，票据债务人不承担票据责任；已经背书转让的，票据无效不影响其他真实签章的效力：  
（一）出票人签章不真实的；  
（二）出票人为无民事行为能力人的；  
（三）出票人为限制民事行为能力人的。

**Article 66** An instrument that has any of the following circumstances, if not endorsed and transferred, the instrument debtor shall not bear instrument liability; if already endorsed and transferred, the invalidity of the instrument shall not affect the validity of other genuine signatures:  
(1) the signature of the drawer is not authentic;  
(2) the drawer is a person without civil capacity;  
(3) the drawer is a person with limited civil capacity.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　依照[票据法第十四条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-14)、第一百零三条、第一百零四条的规定，伪造、变造票据者除应当依法承担刑事、行政责任外，给他人造成损失的，还应当承担民事赔偿责任。被伪造签章者不承担票据责任。

**Article 67** In accordance with the provisions of Articles 14, 103, and 104 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, a person who forges or alters an instrument shall, in addition to bearing criminal and administrative liability according to law, also bear civil liability for compensation if causing losses to others. The person whose signature is forged shall not bear instrument liability.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　对票据未记载事项或者未完全记载事项作补充记载，补充事项超出授权范围的，出票人对补充后的票据应当承担票据责任。给他人造成损失的，出票人还应当承担相应的民事责任。

**Article 68** Where supplementary records are made for matters not recorded or not completely recorded on an instrument, and the supplementary matters exceed the scope of authorization, the drawer shall bear instrument liability for the supplemented instrument. If losses are caused to others, the drawer shall also bear corresponding civil liability.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　付款人或者代理付款人未能识别出伪造、变造的票据或者身份证件而错误付款，属于[票据法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-57)规定的“重大过失”，给持票人造成损失的，应当依法承担民事责任。付款人或者代理付款人承担责任后有权向伪造者、变造者依法追偿。  
持票人有过错的，也应当承担相应的民事责任。

**Article 69** Where the payer or agent payer makes erroneous payment due to failure to identify a forged or altered instrument or identity document, which constitutes "gross negligence" as provided in Article 57 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, and causes losses to the holder, it shall bear civil liability according to law. After bearing liability, the payer or agent payer shall have the right to seek recourse from the forger or alterer according to law.  
If the holder is at fault, it shall also bear corresponding civil liability.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　付款人及其代理付款人有下列情形之一的，应当自行承担责任：  
（一）未依照[票据法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-57)的规定对提示付款人的合法身份证明或者有效证件以及汇票背书的连续性履行审查义务而错误付款的；  
（二）公示催告期间对公示催告的票据付款的；  
（三）收到人民法院的止付通知后付款的；  
（四）其他以恶意或者重大过失付款的。

**Article 70** Where the payer or its agent payer has any of the following circumstances, it shall bear liability on its own:  
(1) making erroneous payment by failing to perform the obligation to examine the legal identity certificate or valid document of the presenter for payment and the continuity of endorsements on the bill of exchange in accordance with Article 57 of the Negotiable Instruments Law;  
(2) making payment on an instrument subject to public summons for exhortation during the period of public summons;  
(3) making payment after receiving a notice of stop-payment from the People's Court;  
(4) other payments made with malice or gross negligence.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　[票据法第六十三条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-63)所称“其他有关证明”是指：  
（一）人民法院出具的宣告承兑人、付款人失踪或者死亡的证明、法律文书；  
（二）公安机关出具的承兑人、付款人逃匿或者下落不明的证明；  
（三）医院或者有关单位出具的承兑人、付款人死亡的证明；  
（四）公证机构出具的具有拒绝证明效力的文书。

**Article 71** The term "other relevant certificates" as mentioned in Article 63 of the Negotiable Instruments Law means:  
(1) certificates or legal documents issued by the People's Court declaring the acceptor or payer missing or deceased;  
(2) certificates issued by the public security organ proving that the acceptor or payer has absconded or is whereabouts unknown;  
(3) certificates issued by a hospital or relevant unit proving the death of the acceptor or payer;  
(4) documents issued by a notarization institution having the effect of a certificate of dishonor.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　当事人因申请票据保全错误而给他人造成损失的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 72** A party that causes losses to others due to erroneous application for instrument preservation shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　因出票人签发空头支票、与其预留本名的签名式样或者印鉴不符的支票给他人造成损失的，支票的出票人和背书人应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 73** Where the drawer issues a non-sufficient funds check or a check with a signature or seal that does not match the reserved specimen of its name, causing losses to others, the drawer and endorser of the check shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　人民法院在审理票据纠纷案件时，发现与本案有牵连但不属同一法律关系的票据欺诈犯罪嫌疑线索的，应当及时将犯罪嫌疑线索提供给有关公安机关，但票据纠纷案件不应因此而中止审理。

**Article 74** Where the People's Court, when hearing an instrument dispute case, discovers clues of suspected instrument fraud crimes that are related to the case but do not belong to the same legal relationship, it shall promptly provide such clues to the relevant public security organ; however, the instrument dispute case shall not be suspended for this reason.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　依照[票据法第一百零五条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-105)的规定，由于金融机构工作人员在票据业务中玩忽职守，对违反票据法规定的票据予以承兑、付款、贴现或者保证，给当事人造成损失的，由该金融机构与直接责任人员依法承担连带责任。

**Article 75** In accordance with the provisions of Article 105 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where personnel of a financial institution, due to dereliction of duty in instrument business, accept, pay, discount, or guarantee an instrument that violates the provisions of the Negotiable Instruments Law, causing losses to the parties, the financial institution and the directly responsible persons shall bear joint and several liability according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　依照[票据法第一百零七条](../../民事与商事/合同与债权/中华人民共和国票据法.md#art-107)的规定，由于出票人制作票据，或者其他票据债务人未按照法定条件在票据上签章，给他人造成损失的，除应当按照所记载事项承担票据责任外，还应当承担相应的民事责任。  
持票人明知或者应当知道前款情形而接受的，可以适当减轻出票人或者票据债务人的责任。

**Article 76** In accordance with the provisions of Article 107 of the Negotiable Instruments Law, where the drawer creates an instrument or other instrument debtors sign on the instrument not in accordance with statutory conditions, causing losses to others, they shall, in addition to bearing instrument liability in accordance with the matters recorded, also bear corresponding civil liability.  
Where the holder accepts the instrument knowing or ought to know the circumstances in the preceding paragraph, the liability of the drawer or instrument debtor may be appropriately mitigated.
