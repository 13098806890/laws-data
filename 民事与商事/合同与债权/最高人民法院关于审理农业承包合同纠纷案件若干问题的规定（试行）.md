# 最高人民法院关于审理农业承包合同纠纷案件若干问题的规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1999〕15号  
**公布日期**：1999-06-05  
**生效日期**：1999-07-08

> （1999年6月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第1066次会议通过，自1999年7月8日起施行。） 为了正确、及时地审理农业承包合同纠纷案件，保障保护当事人的合法权益，促进以家庭承包经营为基农村经济体制改革不断深入，保持农业和农村经济持续稳定发展，根据有关法律、法规和政策的规定，结合审判实践，特制定本规定。 一、受理与管辖 第一条 农业承包合同的当事人因承包合同发生纠纷向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条 发包方所属的半数以上村民，以签订承包合同时违反《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国村民委员会组织法》等法律规定的民主议定原则，或者其所签合同内容违背多数村民意志，损害集体和村民利益为由，以发包方为被告，要求确认承包合同的效力提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理，并可通知承包方作为第三人参加诉讼。 第三条 农业承包合同纠纷案件，由承包合同履行地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。 第四条 共同承包人数众多的，应当推选代表人进行诉讼。共同承包人推选不出代表人的，由人民法院提出代表人名单，要求共同承包人协商，协商不成的，由人民法院在共同承包人中指定代表人。 诉讼代表人的诉讼行为对全体共同承包人发生效力，但代表人变更、放弃诉讼请求或者承认对方当事人的诉讼请求、进行和解，必须经所代表的当事人同意。 第五条 农业承包合同签订后，经发包方同意，承包方增加承包人的，新增加的承包人可以作为诉讼当事人。发包方知道或者应当知道增加了承包人，但在六个月内未提出异议的，视为发包方对该事实的认可，新增加的承包人可以作为诉讼当事人。 二、案件的审理 第六条 人民法院审理农业承包合同纠纷案件，应当遵循依照有关法律、法规，维护农村政策的稳定，促进农村经济发展，保护农民合法权益的原则。 第七条 人民法院对农业承包合同纠纷案件，应当依法及时受理，尽快审结。对因发生纠纷可能影响生产的，可裁定先行恢复生产；承包方恢复生产确有困难或者拒绝恢复生产的，由发包方负责组织先行恢复生产，恢复生产所发生的费用由过错方承担。 第八条 人民法院在审理农业承包合同纠纷案件时，对承包合同中所约定的承包方应当承担的义务中，超过有关法律、法规和政策规定的，超过的部分不予保护。 前款规定不适用专业承包和招标承包的农业承包合同纠纷案件。 第九条 农业承包合同中，对承包金额或交纳承包金的比例或者范围没有约定或者约定不明确的，人民法院可在查清事实，分清责任的基础上主持双方当事人进行调解。当事人不愿调解或调解不成的，依照本规定第八条的规定处理。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 农业承包合同的当事人因承包合同发生纠纷向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where a party to an agricultural contract brings a prosecution before a People's Court due to a dispute arising from the contract, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 发包方所属的半数以上村民，以签订承包合同时违反《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国村民委员会组织法》等法律规定的民主议定原则，或者其所签合同内容违背多数村民意志，损害集体和村民利益为由，以发包方为被告，要求确认承包合同的效力提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理，并可通知承包方作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 2** Where more than half of the villagers under the party awarding the contract, on the grounds that the conclusion of the contract violated the democratic deliberation principles stipulated in the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Organic Law of the Villagers Committees of the People's Republic of China and other laws, or that the content of the contract contravened the will of the majority of villagers and harmed the interests of the collective and villagers, bring a lawsuit against the party awarding the contract as the defendant to request confirmation of the validity of the contract, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law and may notify the contractor to participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 农业承包合同纠纷案件，由承包合同履行地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。

**Article 3** Cases involving disputes over agricultural contracts shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the place of performance of the contract or the domicile of the defendant.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 共同承包人数众多的，应当推选代表人进行诉讼。共同承包人推选不出代表人的，由人民法院提出代表人名单，要求共同承包人协商，协商不成的，由人民法院在共同承包人中指定代表人。  
诉讼代表人的诉讼行为对全体共同承包人发生效力，但代表人变更、放弃诉讼请求或者承认对方当事人的诉讼请求、进行和解，必须经所代表的当事人同意。

**Article 4** Where there are a large number of joint contractors, they shall elect representatives to conduct the litigation. If the joint contractors fail to elect representatives, the People's Court shall propose a list of representatives and require the joint contractors to negotiate; if negotiation fails, the People's Court shall designate representatives from among the joint contractors.  
The litigation acts of the representative shall be effective for all joint contractors, provided that the representative's modification or waiver of claims, or admission of the opposing party's claims, or settlement, must be approved by the parties represented.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 农业承包合同签订后，经发包方同意，承包方增加承包人的，新增加的承包人可以作为诉讼当事人。发包方知道或者应当知道增加了承包人，但在六个月内未提出异议的，视为发包方对该事实的认可，新增加的承包人可以作为诉讼当事人。  
二、案件的审理

**Article 5** After an agricultural contract is concluded, if the contractor, with the consent of the party awarding the contract, adds a new contractor, the newly added contractor may be a litigant. If the party awarding the contract knew or should have known of the addition of a contractor but did not raise any objection within six months, it shall be deemed that the party awarding the contract has acknowledged the fact, and the newly added contractor may be a litigant.  
II. Trial of Cases

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院审理农业承包合同纠纷案件，应当遵循依照有关法律、法规，维护农村政策的稳定，促进农村经济发展，保护农民合法权益的原则。

**Article 6** In trying cases involving disputes over agricultural contracts, the People's Court shall follow the principles of acting in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, maintaining the stability of rural policies, promoting the development of the rural economy, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of farmers.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 人民法院对农业承包合同纠纷案件，应当依法及时受理，尽快审结。对因发生纠纷可能影响生产的，可裁定先行恢复生产；承包方恢复生产确有困难或者拒绝恢复生产的，由发包方负责组织先行恢复生产，恢复生产所发生的费用由过错方承担。

**Article 7** The People's Court shall accept cases involving disputes over agricultural contracts promptly according to law and conclude the trial as soon as possible. Where the dispute may affect production, the court may rule for the resumption of production first; if the contractor encounters genuine difficulty in resuming production or refuses to resume production, the party awarding the contract shall be responsible for organizing the resumption of production first, and the costs incurred for the resumption of production shall be borne by the party at fault.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院在审理农业承包合同纠纷案件时，对承包合同中所约定的承包方应当承担的义务中，超过有关法律、法规和政策规定的，超过的部分不予保护。  
前款规定不适用专业承包和招标承包的农业承包合同纠纷案件。

**Article 8** When trying a case involving a dispute over an agricultural contract, the People's Court shall not protect the portion of obligations stipulated in the contract to be borne by the contractor that exceed the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and policies.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to cases involving disputes over agricultural contracts for specialized contracts or contracts awarded through bidding.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 农业承包合同中，对承包金额或交纳承包金的比例或者范围没有约定或者约定不明确的，人民法院可在查清事实，分清责任的基础上主持双方当事人进行调解。当事人不愿调解或调解不成的，依照[本规定第八条](#art-8)的规定处理。

**Article 9** Where the amount of the contracting fee or the proportion or scope of payment of the contracting fee is not stipulated or is unclear in an agricultural contract, the People's Court may, on the basis of ascertaining the facts and distinguishing the liabilities, preside over mediation between the parties. If the parties are unwilling to mediate or if mediation fails, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 8 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 当事人因欠交承包金而达成还款协议或者承包方向发包方出具欠条的，一般应当按照债务纠纷案件处理。但是，还款协议或者欠条中对承包金数额约定不明或者当事人对所欠承包金数额有争议的，应当按照农业承包合同纠纷案件处理。

**Article 10** Where a party reaches a repayment agreement due to arrears of the contracting fee, or the contractor issues an IOU to the party awarding the contract, the case shall generally be handled as a debt dispute. However, if the repayment agreement or IOU does not clearly stipulate the amount of the contracting fee, or if the parties dispute the amount of the overdue contracting fee, the case shall be handled as a dispute over an agricultural contract.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院在审理涉及承包经营荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩的农业承包合同纠纷案件中，对承包方所种植的林木及收获的果实的所有权和因治理水土流失而新增加的土地使用权，应当予以保护。

**Article 11** In trying a case involving a dispute over an agricultural contract for the contracting of barren hills, gullies, mounds, or beaches, the People's Court shall protect the ownership of the trees planted and fruits harvested by the contractor, and the right to use the land newly added due to soil erosion control.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 承包方因承包经营的土地被依法征用或者被依法批准使用后，要求发包方按照有关法律、法规的规定给予补偿或者要求发包方对其为改良土地的实际投入给予适当补偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 12** Where, after the land under contract has been lawfully expropriated or lawfully approved for use, the contractor requests the party awarding the contract to provide compensation in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, or to provide appropriate compensation for the contractor's actual investment in land improvement, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 对实行专业承包或者招标承包的承包方，在承包期满后对原承包经营的标的物在同等条件下主张优先承包经营权的，人民法院应当予以保护。  
三、权利义务的转让

**Article 13** Where a contractor under a specialized contract or a contract awarded through bidding claims a priority right to contract the original subject matter under equal conditions after the expiration of the contract term, the People's Court shall protect such claim.  
III. Transfer of Rights and Obligations

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 承包方未经发包方同意，转让承包合同，转包或者互换承包经营标的物的，人民法院应当认定该转让、转包、互换行为无效。

**Article 14** Where the contractor, without the consent of the party awarding the contract, transfers the contract, subcontracts, or swaps the subject matter of the contract, the People's Court shall determine that such transfer, subcontract, or swap is void.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 承包方转让承包合同，转包或者互换承包经营标的物时，违反《中华人民共和国土地管理法》关于“农民集体所有的土地由本集体经济组织以外的单位或者个人承包经营的，必须经村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表同意，并报乡（镇）人民政府批准”的规定的，人民法院应当认定其转让、转包、互换行为无效。

**Article 15** Where, when transferring the contract, subcontracting, or swapping the subject matter of the contract, the contractor violates the provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China that "land collectively owned by farmers that is to be contracted to entities or individuals outside the collective economic organization must be approved by at least two-thirds of the members of the villagers' meeting or at least two-thirds of the villagers' representatives, and reported to the township (town) people's government for approval," the People's Court shall determine that the transfer, subcontract, or swap is void.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 在承包合同有效期内，发包方强制承包方转让承包经营权，强制承包方转包或者互换承包经营的标的物的，人民法院应当认定该转让、转包、互换行为无效。

**Article 16** During the validity period of the contract, if the party awarding the contract forces the contractor to transfer the contractual right to management, or forces the contractor to subcontract or swap the subject matter of the contract, the People's Court shall determine that such transfer, subcontract, or swap is void.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 承包方经发包方同意，将承包经营的标的物全部或者部分转包给第三人的，承包方与发包方之间仍应按照原承包合同的约定行使权利和承担义务。  
承包方与转包后的承包方之间按转包合同的约定行使权利和承担义务。

**Article 17** Where the contractor, with the consent of the party awarding the contract, subcontracts all or part of the subject matter to a third party, the contractor and the party awarding the contract shall still exercise rights and assume obligations in accordance with the original contract.  
The contractor and the subcontractor shall exercise rights and assume obligations in accordance with the subcontract.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 人民法院对承包方用已取得承包经营权的土地等抵偿债务的，应当认定其行为无效。

**Article 18** If the contractor uses the land or other subject matter for which it has obtained the contractual right to management to offset debts, the People's Court shall determine that such act is void.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 对有偿转让承包经营权的转让费超过政府规定的最高额的部分，人民法院不予保护。

**Article 19** The People's Court shall not protect the portion of the transfer fee for the paid transfer of the contractual right to management that exceeds the maximum amount stipulated by the government.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 经发包方同意，各承包方之间将各自承包经营的标的物全部或者部分进行互换，并约定对不等值的部分进行适当补偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 20** Where, with the consent of the party awarding the contract, contractors swap all or part of their respective subject matters and agree on appropriate compensation for any unequal value, the People's Court shall uphold such arrangement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 承包方依照法律、法规和政策的规定，以其承包经营的标的物入股，仍按照承包合同的约定，向发包方行使权利和承担义务的，人民法院应当准许。  
四、合同的变更和终止

**Article 21** Where the contractor, in accordance with laws, regulations, and policies, invests its subject matter as shares, and still exercises rights and assumes obligations toward the party awarding the contract in accordance with the contract, the People's Court shall permit such action.  
IV. Modification and Termination of Contracts

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 对承包合同中约定的承包金，符合法律、法规和政策规定的，人民法院应当予以保护。对当事人单方要求变更承包金的，不予支持。

**Article 22** If the contracting fee stipulated in the contract complies with the provisions of laws, regulations, and policies, the People's Court shall protect it. Where a party unilaterally requests a modification of the contracting fee, such request shall not be upheld.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 承包合同的当事人在合同中对可能发生引起合同变更的事项作出约定的，人民法院在审理农业承包合同纠纷案件时，可从其约定。

**Article 23** Where the parties to a contract have made provisions in the contract regarding matters that may cause modification of the contract, the People's Court may, when trying a case involving a dispute over an agricultural contract, follow such provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 有下列情形之一，当事人请求终止承包合同的，人民法院应当允许：  
（1）承包合同中约定的终止或者解除合同的条件成就的；  
（2）承包人在承包期内因健康原因丧失承包能力或者死亡，继承人无力承包或者放弃继承，且又不进行转让、转包和入股的；  
（3）因一方不履行承包合同约定的义务，致使承包合同无法继续履行的；  
（4）承包经营耕地的承包人弃耕抛荒的；  
（5）承包方进行破坏性或者掠夺性生产经营，经发包方劝阻无效的；  
（6）承包方随意改变土地用途，经发包方劝阻无效的。  
五、责任的承担

**Article 24** Where a party requests termination of the contract under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall grant the request:  
(1) The conditions for termination or dissolution of the contract stipulated in the contract have been fulfilled;  
(2) The contractor loses the capacity to contract due to health reasons or dies during the contract period, and the successor is unable to contract or renounces the succession, and does not transfer, subcontract, or invest the subject matter as shares;  
(3) The contract cannot be further performed due to one party's failure to perform its obligations stipulated in the contract;  
(4) The contractor who contracts cultivated land abandons the land and leaves it idle;  
(5) The contractor engages in destructive or predatory production and operation, and the party awarding the contract's dissuasion is ineffective;  
(6) The contractor arbitrarily changes the use of the land, and the party awarding the contract's dissuasion is ineffective.  
V. Assumption of Liability

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 人民法院在审理依[本规定第二条](#art-2)所起诉的案件中，对发包方违背集体经济组织成员大会或者成员代表大会决议，越权发包的，应当认定该承包合同为无效合同，并根据当事人的过错，确定其应承担的相应责任。  
属本条前款规定的情形，自承包合同签订之日起超过一年，或者虽未超过一年，但承包人已实际做了大量的投入的，对原告方要求确认该承包合同无效或者要求终止该承包合同的，人民法院不予支持。但可根据实际情况，依照公平原则，对该承包合同的有关内容进行适当调整。

**Article 25** In trying a case brought under Article 2 of these Provisions, if the party awarding the contract, in violation of a resolution of the general meeting or representative meeting of the collective economic organization members, awards the contract ultra vires, the People's Court shall determine that the contract is void and, based on the fault of the parties, determine the corresponding liability to be borne by them.  
Where the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph apply and more than one year has elapsed since the contract was concluded, or if less than one year has elapsed but the contractor has actually made substantial investments, the People's Court shall not uphold the plaintiff's request for confirmation of the voidness of the contract or for termination of the contract. However, the People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances and in accordance with the principle of fairness, make appropriate adjustments to the relevant contents of the contract.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 承包合同转包后，因发包方的原因，致使转包合同不能履行，给转包后的承包方造成损失的，转包后的承包方起诉承包方时，人民法院可以通知发包方作为第三人参加诉讼，并根据其过错，确定其应承担的相应责任。

**Article 26** After the contract has been subcontracted, if due to the reasons of the party awarding the contract, the subcontract cannot be performed, causing losses to the subcontractor, the subcontractor may bring a lawsuit against the contractor. The People's Court may notify the party awarding the contract to participate in the proceedings as a third party and, based on its fault, determine the corresponding liability to be borne.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 承包合同转包后，因转包后的承包方的原因，致使承包合同不能履行，发包方起诉承包方时，人民法院可以通知转包后的承包方作为第三人参加诉讼，并根据其过错，确定其应承担的相应责任。

**Article 27** After the contract has been subcontracted, if due to the reasons of the subcontractor, the contract cannot be performed, and the party awarding the contract brings a lawsuit against the contractor, the People's Court may notify the subcontractor to participate in the proceedings as a third party and, based on its fault, determine the corresponding liability to be borne.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 因承包方随意改变土地用途或者对承包经营的标的物进行破坏性或者掠夺性生产经营，发包方要求承包方对造成的损失给予赔偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 28** Where the contractor arbitrarily changes the use of the land or engages in destructive or predatory production and operation on the subject matter, and the party awarding the contract requests the contractor to compensate for the losses caused, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 承包期间因发生自然灾害，或者因承包方自身以外的原因，致使承包方交纳承包金有困难，承包方要求缓交、减交或免交承包金的，人民法院可以根据实际情况，允许承包方缓交、减交或者免交承包金。

**Article 29** Where during the contract period, due to natural disasters or reasons beyond the contractor, the contractor has difficulty in paying the contracting fee, and the contractor requests a deferment, reduction, or exemption of the contracting fee, the People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances, permit the contractor to defer, reduce, or be exempted from the contracting fee.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 在承包合同履行期间，承包方分户时，其家庭内部就承包经营的权利义务未能达成协议，或者虽有协议，但是以分户的方式逃避履行承包合同义务，损害发包方利益的，所分各户之间，应对承包合同所确定的义务，承担连带责任。

**Article 30** Where during the performance of the contract, the contractor's household splits, and the members of the household fail to reach an agreement on the rights and obligations under the contract, or although an agreement is reached, the splitting is a means to evade performance of the obligations under the contract to the detriment of the interests of the party awarding the contract, the households resulting from the split shall bear joint and several liability for the obligations stipulated in the contract.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 以个人名义签订承包合同，但有证据证明其承包经营收益的主要部分是供家庭成员享用的，应当以其家庭财产承担责任。

**Article 31** Where a contract is signed in the name of an individual, but there is evidence proving that the main portion of the income from the contracted operation is used for the enjoyment of family members, the liability shall be borne with the family property.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 共同承包人中途退出承包的，应当享有和承担共同承包期间承包合同所约定的权利义务。  
因共同承包人中途退出承包，给发包方或者其他承包人造成损失，发包方或者其他承包人要求给予赔偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 32** Where a joint contractor withdraws from the contract midway, it shall enjoy and assume the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract during the period of joint contracting.  
If the withdrawal of a joint contractor midway causes losses to the party awarding the contract or other contractors, and the party awarding the contract or other contractors request compensation, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 当事人在承包合同中约定了违约责任，且对违约金的比例、金额或者计算方法等内容约定明确的，从其约定。但所约定的违约金低于或者过份高于所造成的损失的，人民法院可应当事人的请求和实际情况适当予以减少或者增加。  
当事人在承包合同中没有约定违约金的，无权请求支付违约金。

**Article 33** Where the parties have stipulated liability for breach of contract in the contract, and have clearly stipulated the proportion, amount, or calculation method of liquidated damages, such stipulation shall be followed. However, if the stipulated liquidated damages are lower than the losses caused or are excessively higher than the losses caused, the People's Court may, upon the request of the parties and based on the actual circumstances, appropriately reduce or increase the amount.  
If the parties have not stipulated liquidated damages in the contract, neither party shall have the right to claim payment of liquidated damages.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 承包方是夫妻的，在承包合同履行期间解除婚姻关系时，就其承包经营的权利义务未达成协议，且双方均具有承包经营主体资格的，人民法院在处理其离婚案件时，应当按照家庭人口、老人的赡养、未成年子女的抚养等具体情况，对其承包经营权进行分割。  
六、其他

**Article 34** Where the contractors are a married couple, and during the performance of the contract, they dissolve the marital relationship without reaching an agreement on the rights and obligations under the contract, and both parties have the legal capacity to engage in contract operation, the People's Court, when handling the divorce case, shall divide the contractual right to operation in accordance with specific circumstances such as family size, support of the elderly, and maintenance of minor children.  
VI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 本规定所称的农业承包合同，包括书面合同、口头合同、任务下达书，以及其他能够证明承包经营法律关系的事实和文件。

**Article 35** The term "agricultural contract" as used in these Provisions shall include written contracts, oral contracts, task assignment documents, and other facts and documents that can prove the legal relationship of contract operation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 本规定所称发包方，是指村内各农村集体经济组织或者村民小组、村集体经济组织或者村民委员会、乡（镇）农村集体经济组织等。

**Article 36** The term "party awarding the contract" as used in these Provisions shall refer to the various rural collective economic organizations within a village or villagers' groups, village collective economic organizations or villagers' committees, township (town) rural collective economic organizations, etc.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 本规定所称承包金，是指承包方依据承包合同的约定向发包方交纳的款项、实物或者应当履行的劳务。

**Article 37** The term "contracting fee" as used in these Provisions shall refer to the money, in-kind goods, or labor to be performed that the contractor pays to the party awarding the contract in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 本规定适用于发包方就开发、经营和利用本集体经济组织所有的自然资源和其他资产，以及依法取得使用权的国家资源，与承包方所签订的承包合同，以及各承包方之间所签订的转让、转包和互换承包经营标的物的合同。

**Article 38** These Provisions shall apply to contracts concluded between the party awarding the contract and the contractor for the development, operation, and utilization of natural resources and other assets owned by the collective economic organization, as well as state resources over which the right to use has been lawfully acquired, and to contracts concluded among contractors for the transfer, subcontract, or swap of the subject matter of the contract.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 人民法院在审理专业承包和招标承包的农业承包合同纠纷案件时，除适用本规定的有关规定外，还应依照合同订立时的有关法律、法规和政策的规定处理。

**Article 39** In trying cases involving disputes over agricultural contracts for specialized contracts or contracts awarded through bidding, the People's Court shall, in addition to applying the relevant provisions of these Provisions, also handle the case in accordance with the relevant laws, regulations, and policies in effect at the time of the contract's conclusion.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 本规定自公布之日起试行，1986年4月12日最高人民法院印发的《关于审理农村承包合同纠纷案件若干问题的意见》同时废止。

**Article 40** These Provisions shall be implemented on a trial basis as of the date of promulgation, and the Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Disputes over Rural Contracts (promulgated by the Supreme People's Court on April 12, 1986) shall be abolished simultaneously.
