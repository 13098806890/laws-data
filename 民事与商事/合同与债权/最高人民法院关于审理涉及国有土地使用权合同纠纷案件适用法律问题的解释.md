# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及国有土地使用权合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕5号  
**公布日期**：2005-06-18  
**生效日期**：2005-08-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉及国有土地使用权合同纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释》已于2004年11月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1334次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年8月1日起施行。 2005年6月18日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉及国有土地使用权合同 纠纷案件适用法律问题的解释 法释〔2005〕5号 （2004年11月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1334次会议通过　2005年6月18日最高人民法院公告公布　自2005年8月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本解释所称的土地使用权出让合同，是指市、县人民政府土地管理部门作为出让方将国有土地使用权在一定年限内让与受让方，受让方支付土地使用权出让金的协议。

**Article 1** The land use right grant contract as referred to in this Interpretation means an agreement whereby the land administration department of the people's government at the city or county level, as the grantor, grants the right to use state-owned land to the grantee for a certain period of time, and the grantee pays the grant price for the land use right.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　开发区管理委员会作为出让方与受让方订立的土地使用权出让合同，应当认定无效。  
本解释实施前，开发区管理委员会作为出让方与受让方订立的土地使用权出让合同，起诉前经市、县人民政府土地管理部门追认的，可以认定合同有效。

**Article 2** A land use right grant contract entered into by the development zone management committee as the grantor with a grantee shall be determined to be void.  
For a land use right grant contract entered into by the development zone management committee as the grantor with a grantee prior to the implementation of this Interpretation, if it is ratified by the land administration department of the people's government at the city or county level before the prosecution, the contract may be determined to be valid.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经市、县人民政府批准同意以协议方式出让的土地使用权，土地使用权出让金低于订立合同时当地政府按照国家规定确定的最低价的，应当认定土地使用权出让合同约定的价格条款无效。  
当事人请求按照订立合同时的市场评估价格交纳土地使用权出让金的，应予支持；受让方不同意按照市场评估价格补足，请求解除合同的，应予支持。因此造成的损失，由当事人按照过错承担责任。

**Article 3** If, for a land use right granted by agreement with the approval of the people's government at the city or county level, the land use right grant price is lower than the minimum price determined by the local government according to the state provisions at the time of entering into the contract, the price clause stipulated in the land use right grant contract shall be determined to be void.  
If a party requests to pay the land use right grant price based on the market appraisal price at the time of entering into the contract, such request shall be supported; if the grantee does not agree to make up the difference based on the market appraisal price and requests dissolution of the contract, such request shall be supported. The losses caused thereby shall be borne by the parties according to their fault.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　土地使用权出让合同的出让方因未办理土地使用权出让批准手续而不能交付土地，受让方请求解除合同的，应予支持。

**Article 4** If the grantor of a land use right grant contract is unable to deliver the land because it has not gone through the approval procedures for the land use right grant, and the grantee requests dissolution of the contract, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　受让方经出让方和市、县人民政府城市规划行政主管部门同意，改变土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途，当事人请求按照起诉时同种用途的土地出让金标准调整土地出让金的，应予支持。

**Article 5** If the grantee changes the land use purpose stipulated in the land use right grant contract with the consent of the grantor and the urban planning administrative department of the people's government at the city or county level, and a party requests to adjust the land use right grant price according to the land use right grant price standard for the same type of land use at the time of prosecution, such request shall be supported.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　受让方擅自改变土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途，出让方请求解除合同的，应予支持。  
二、土地使用权转让合同纠纷

**Article 6** If the grantee changes the land use purpose stipulated in the land use right grant contract without authorization, and the grantor requests dissolution of the contract, such request shall be supported.  
II. Disputes over Land Use Right Transfer Contracts

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本解释所称的土地使用权转让合同，是指土地使用权人作为转让方将出让土地使用权转让于受让方，受让方支付价款的协议。

**Article 7** The land use right transfer contract as referred to in this Interpretation means an agreement whereby the holder of a land use right, as the transferor, transfers the granted land use right to the transferee, and the transferee pays the price.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　土地使用权人作为转让方与受让方订立土地使用权转让合同后，当事人一方以双方之间未办理土地使用权变更登记手续为由，请求确认合同无效的，不予支持。

**Article 8** After the holder of a land use right, as the transferor, enters into a land use right transfer contract with the transferee, if a party requests to confirm that the contract is void on the ground that the procedures for the change registration of the land use right have not been completed between the two parties, such request shall not be supported.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　转让方未取得出让土地使用权证书与受让方订立合同转让土地使用权，起诉前转让方已经取得出让土地使用权证书或者有批准权的人民政府同意转让的，应当认定合同有效。

**Article 9** If the transferor enters into a contract to transfer the land use right without having obtained the certificate of granted land use right, but before the prosecution the transferor has obtained the certificate of granted land use right or the people's government with approval authority has consented to the transfer, the contract shall be determined to be valid.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　土地使用权人作为转让方就同一出让土地使用权订立数个转让合同，在转让合同有效的情况下，受让方均要求履行合同的，按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）已经办理土地使用权变更登记手续的受让方，请求转让方履行交付土地等合同义务的，应予支持；  
（二）均未办理土地使用权变更登记手续，已先行合法占有投资开发土地的受让方请求转让方履行土地使用权变更登记等合同义务的，应予支持；  
（三）均未办理土地使用权变更登记手续，又未合法占有投资开发土地，先行支付土地转让款的受让方请求转让方履行交付土地和办理土地使用权变更登记等合同义务的，应予支持；  
（四）合同均未履行，依法成立在先的合同受让方请求履行合同的，应予支持。  
未能取得土地使用权的受让方请求解除合同、赔偿损失的，按照《中华人民共和国合同法》的有关规定处理。

**Article 10** Where the holder of a land use right, as the transferor, enters into several transfer contracts with respect to the same granted land use right, and if the transfer contracts are valid, and all transferees request performance of the contracts, the matter shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If a transferee has already completed the procedures for change registration of the land use right, and requests the transferor to perform contractual obligations such as delivery of the land, such request shall be supported;  
(2) If none of the transferees has completed the procedures for change registration of the land use right, and a transferee has lawfully taken possession of the invested and developed land first, and requests the transferor to perform contractual obligations such as change registration of the land use right, such request shall be supported;  
(3) If none of the transferees has completed the procedures for change registration of the land use right nor lawfully taken possession of the invested and developed land, and a transferee has paid the land transfer price first, and requests the transferor to perform contractual obligations such as delivery of the land and completion of change registration of the land use right, such request shall be supported;  
(4) If none of the contracts has been performed, and the transferee under the contract that was established earlier according to law requests performance of the contract, such request shall be supported.  
If a transferee that fails to obtain the land use right requests dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地使用权人未经有批准权的人民政府批准，与受让方订立合同转让划拨土地使用权的，应当认定合同无效。但起诉前经有批准权的人民政府批准办理土地使用权出让手续的，应当认定合同有效。

**Article 11** If the holder of a land use right, without the approval of the people's government with approval authority, enters into a contract to transfer the allocated land use right with a transferee, the contract shall be determined to be void. However, if before the prosecution, the approval of the people's government with approval authority has been obtained to go through the procedures for the grant of the land use right, the contract shall be determined to be valid.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地使用权人与受让方订立合同转让划拨土地使用权，起诉前经有批准权的人民政府同意转让，并由受让方办理土地使用权出让手续的，土地使用权人与受让方订立的合同可以按照补偿性质的合同处理。

**Article 12** If the holder of a land use right enters into a contract to transfer the allocated land use right with a transferee, and before the prosecution, the people's government with approval authority consents to the transfer, and the transferee goes through the procedures for the grant of the land use right, the contract entered into between the holder of the land use right and the transferee may be treated as a contract of a compensatory nature.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　土地使用权人与受让方订立合同转让划拨土地使用权，起诉前经有批准权的人民政府决定不办理土地使用权出让手续，并将该划拨土地使用权直接划拨给受让方使用的，土地使用权人与受让方订立的合同可以按照补偿性质的合同处理。  
三、合作开发房地产合同纠纷

**Article 13** If the holder of a land use right enters into a contract to transfer the allocated land use right with a transferee, and before the prosecution, the people's government with approval authority decides not to go through the procedures for the grant of the land use right, and directly allocates the said allocated land use right to the transferee for use, the contract entered into between the holder of the land use right and the transferee may be treated as a contract of a compensatory nature.  
III. Disputes over Contracts for Cooperative Development of Real Estate

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本解释所称的合作开发房地产合同，是指当事人订立的以提供出让土地使用权、资金等作为共同投资，共享利润、共担风险合作开发房地产为基本内容的协议。

**Article 14** The contract for cooperative development of real estate as referred to in this Interpretation means an agreement whereby the parties agree to make joint investments, share profits, and bear risks in the cooperative development of real estate, with the basic content being the provision of granted land use right, funds, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　合作开发房地产合同的当事人一方具备房地产开发经营资质的，应当认定合同有效。  
当事人双方均不具备房地产开发经营资质的，应当认定合同无效。但起诉前当事人一方已经取得房地产开发经营资质或者已依法合作成立具有房地产开发经营资质的房地产开发企业的，应当认定合同有效。

**Article 15** If one party to a contract for cooperative development of real estate possesses the qualification for real estate development and operation, the contract shall be determined to be valid.  
If neither party possesses the qualification for real estate development and operation, the contract shall be determined to be void. However, if before the prosecution, one party has obtained the qualification for real estate development and operation or has established a real estate development enterprise with the qualification for real estate development and operation through cooperation according to law, the contract shall be determined to be valid.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　土地使用权人未经有批准权的人民政府批准，以划拨土地使用权作为投资与他人订立合同合作开发房地产的，应当认定合同无效。但起诉前已经办理批准手续的，应当认定合同有效。

**Article 16** If the holder of a land use right, without the approval of the people's government with approval authority, uses the allocated land use right as an investment to enter into a contract with another party for cooperative development of real estate, the contract shall be determined to be void. However, if the approval procedures have been completed before the prosecution, the contract shall be determined to be valid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　投资数额超出合作开发房地产合同的约定，对增加的投资数额的承担比例，当事人协商不成的，按照当事人的过错确定；因不可归责于当事人的事由或者当事人的过错无法确定的，按照约定的投资比例确定；没有约定投资比例的，按照约定的利润分配比例确定。

**Article 17** If the amount of investment exceeds the amount stipulated in the contract for cooperative development of real estate, and the parties fail to reach an agreement on the proportion of bearing the increased investment amount, the proportion shall be determined according to the fault of the parties; if it is unable to be determined due to reasons not attributable to the parties or the fault of the parties, it shall be determined according to the agreed investment ratio; if no investment ratio is agreed upon, it shall be determined according to the agreed profit distribution ratio.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　房屋实际建筑面积少于合作开发房地产合同的约定，对房屋实际建筑面积的分配比例，当事人协商不成的，按照当事人的过错确定；因不可归责于当事人的事由或者当事人过错无法确定的，按照约定的利润分配比例确定。

**Article 18** If the actual floor area of the building is less than that stipulated in the contract for cooperative development of real estate, and the parties fail to reach an agreement on the distribution proportion of the actual floor area, the proportion shall be determined according to the fault of the parties; if it is unable to be determined due to reasons not attributable to the parties or the fault of the parties, it shall be determined according to the agreed profit distribution ratio.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在下列情形下，合作开发房地产合同的当事人请求分配房地产项目利益的，不予受理；已经受理的，驳回起诉：  
（一）依法需经批准的房地产建设项目未经有批准权的人民政府主管部门批准；  
（二）房地产建设项目未取得建设工程规划许可证；  
（三）擅自变更建设工程规划。  
因当事人隐瞒建设工程规划变更的事实所造成的损失，由当事人按照过错承担。

**Article 19** Under the following circumstances, if a party to a contract for cooperative development of real estate requests distribution of the benefits from the real estate project, the request shall not be accepted; if it has already been accepted, the prosecution shall be dismissed:  
(1) The real estate construction project that requires approval according to law has not been approved by the competent department of the people's government with approval authority;  
(2) The real estate construction project has not obtained the construction project planning permit;  
(3) The construction project planning has been changed without authorization.  
The losses caused by the party's concealment of the fact that the construction project planning has been changed shall be borne by the party according to its fault.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　房屋实际建筑面积超出规划建筑面积，经有批准权的人民政府主管部门批准后，当事人对超出部分的房屋分配比例协商不成的，按照约定的利润分配比例确定。对增加的投资数额的承担比例，当事人协商不成的，按照约定的投资比例确定；没有约定投资比例的，按照约定的利润分配比例确定。

**Article 20** If the actual floor area of the building exceeds the planned floor area, and after approval by the competent department of the people's government with approval authority, the parties fail to reach an agreement on the distribution proportion of the excess part of the building, the proportion shall be determined according to the agreed profit distribution ratio. If the parties fail to reach an agreement on the proportion of bearing the increased investment amount, it shall be determined according to the agreed investment ratio; if no investment ratio is agreed upon, it shall be determined according to the agreed profit distribution ratio.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人违反规划开发建设的房屋，被有批准权的人民政府主管部门认定为违法建筑责令拆除，当事人对损失承担协商不成的，按照当事人过错确定责任；过错无法确定的，按照约定的投资比例确定责任；没有约定投资比例的，按照约定的利润分配比例确定责任。

**Article 21** If the building developed and constructed by a party in violation of the planning is determined by the competent department of the people's government with approval authority to be an illegal building and ordered to be demolished, and the parties fail to reach an agreement on the assumption of losses, the liability shall be determined according to the fault of the parties; if the fault cannot be determined, the liability shall be determined according to the agreed investment ratio; if no investment ratio is agreed upon, the liability shall be determined according to the agreed profit distribution ratio.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　合作开发房地产合同约定仅以投资数额确定利润分配比例，当事人未足额交纳出资的，按照当事人的实际投资比例分配利润。

**Article 22** If a contract for cooperative development of real estate stipulates that the profit distribution ratio shall be determined solely by the amount of investment, and a party fails to make full capital contribution, the profit shall be distributed according to the actual investment proportion of the parties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　合作开发房地产合同的当事人要求将房屋预售款充抵投资参与利润分配的，不予支持。

**Article 23** If a party to a contract for cooperative development of real estate requests to offset the investment with the advance sales proceeds of the building and participate in profit distribution, such request shall not be supported.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　合作开发房地产合同约定提供土地使用权的当事人不承担经营风险，只收取固定利益的，应当认定为土地使用权转让合同。

**Article 24** If a contract for cooperative development of real estate stipulates that the party providing the land use right does not bear the operational risk and only receives fixed benefits, the contract shall be determined to be a land use right transfer contract.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　合作开发房地产合同约定提供资金的当事人不承担经营风险，只分配固定数量房屋的，应当认定为房屋买卖合同。

**Article 25** If a contract for cooperative development of real estate stipulates that the party providing funds does not bear the operational risk and only distributes a fixed quantity of buildings, the contract shall be determined to be a building sale contract.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　合作开发房地产合同约定提供资金的当事人不承担经营风险，只收取固定数额货币的，应当认定为借款合同。

**Article 26** If a contract for cooperative development of real estate stipulates that the party providing funds does not bear the operational risk and only receives a fixed amount of money, the contract shall be determined to be a loan contract.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　合作开发房地产合同约定提供资金的当事人不承担经营风险，只以租赁或者其他形式使用房屋的，应当认定为房屋租赁合同。  
四、其　　他

**Article 27** If a contract for cooperative development of real estate stipulates that the party providing funds does not bear the operational risk and only uses the building by way of lease or other forms, the contract shall be determined to be a building lease contract.  
IV. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本解释自2005年8月1日起施行；施行后受理的第一审案件适用本解释。  
本解释施行前最高人民法院发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 28** This Interpretation shall come into force as of August 1, 2005; this Interpretation shall apply to first-instance cases accepted after its entry into force.  
If any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court before the entry into force of this Interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
