# 最高人民法院关于审理涉外民事或商事合同纠纷案件法律适用若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕14号  
**公布日期**：2007-06-11  
**生效日期**：2007-08-08

> 《最高人民法院关于审理涉外民事或商事合同纠纷案件法律适用若干问题的规定》已于2007年6月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1429次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年8月8日起施行。 2007年7月23日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉外民事或商事合同纠纷案件 法律适用若干问题的规定 法释〔2007〕14号 为正确审理涉外民事或商事合同纠纷案件，准确适用法律，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》等有关规定，制定本规定。 第一条  涉外民事或商事合同应适用的法律，是指有关国家或地区的实体法，不包括冲突法和程序法。 第二条  本规定所称合同争议包括合同的订立、合同的效力、合同的履行、合同的变更和转让、合同的终止以及违约责任等争议。 第三条  当事人选择或者变更选择合同争议应适用的法律，应当以明示的方式进行。 第四条  当事人在一审法庭辩论终结前通过协商一致，选择或者变更选择合同争议应适用的法律的，人民法院应予准许。 当事人未选择合同争议应适用的法律，但均援引同一国家或者地区的法律且未提出法律适用异议的，应当视为当事人已经就合同争议应适用的法律作出选择。 第五条  当事人未选择合同争议应适用的法律的，适用与合同有最密切联系的国家或者地区的法律。 人民法院根据最密切联系原则确定合同争议应适用的法律时，应根据合同的特殊性质，以及某一方当事人履行的义务最能体现合同的本质特性等因素，确定与合同有最密切联系的国家或者地区的法律作为合同的准据法。 （一）买卖合同，适用合同订立时卖方住所地法；如果合同是在买方住所地谈判并订立的，或者合同明确规定卖方须在买方住所地履行交货义务的，适用买方住所地法。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  涉外民事或商事合同应适用的法律，是指有关国家或地区的实体法，不包括冲突法和程序法。

**Article 1** The law applicable to foreign-related civil or commercial contracts shall refer to the substantive law of the relevant country or region, excluding conflict of laws and procedural law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  本规定所称合同争议包括合同的订立、合同的效力、合同的履行、合同的变更和转让、合同的终止以及违约责任等争议。

**Article 2** The term "contract disputes" as used in these Provisions shall include disputes concerning the formation of the contract, the validity of the contract, the performance of the contract, the modification and assignment of the contract, the termination of the contract, and liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  当事人选择或者变更选择合同争议应适用的法律，应当以明示的方式进行。

**Article 3** A party's choice or change of choice of the law applicable to contract disputes shall be made in an express manner.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  当事人在一审法庭辩论终结前通过协商一致，选择或者变更选择合同争议应适用的法律的，人民法院应予准许。  
当事人未选择合同争议应适用的法律，但均援引同一国家或者地区的法律且未提出法律适用异议的，应当视为当事人已经就合同争议应适用的法律作出选择。

**Article 4** If the parties, through consensus, choose or change the choice of the law applicable to contract disputes before the conclusion of the debate in the first instance trial, the People's Court shall permit it.  
If the parties have not chosen the law applicable to contract disputes, but all invoke the law of the same country or region and raise no objection to the application of law, it shall be deemed that the parties have made a choice of the law applicable to the contract disputes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  当事人未选择合同争议应适用的法律的，适用与合同有最密切联系的国家或者地区的法律。  
人民法院根据最密切联系原则确定合同争议应适用的法律时，应根据合同的特殊性质，以及某一方当事人履行的义务最能体现合同的本质特性等因素，确定与合同有最密切联系的国家或者地区的法律作为合同的准据法。  
（一）买卖合同，适用合同订立时卖方住所地法；如果合同是在买方住所地谈判并订立的，或者合同明确规定卖方须在买方住所地履行交货义务的，适用买方住所地法。  
（二）来料加工、来件装配以及其他各种加工承揽合同，适用加工承揽人住所地法。  
（三）成套设备供应合同，适用设备安装地法。  
（四）不动产买卖、租赁或者抵押合同，适用不动产所在地法。  
（五）动产租赁合同，适用出租人住所地法。  
（六）动产质押合同，适用质权人住所地法。  
（七）借款合同，适用贷款人住所地法。  
（八）保险合同，适用保险人住所地法。  
（九）融资租赁合同，适用承租人住所地法。  
（十）建设工程合同，适用建设工程所在地法。  
（十一）仓储、保管合同，适用仓储、保管人住所地法。  
（十二）保证合同，适用保证人住所地法。  
（十三）委托合同，适用受托人住所地法。  
（十四）债券的发行、销售和转让合同，分别适用债券发行地法、债券销售地法和债券转让地法。  
（十五）拍卖合同，适用拍卖举行地法。  
（十六）行纪合同，适用行纪人住所地法。  
（十七）居间合同，适用居间人住所地法。  
如果上述合同明显与另一国家或者地区有更密切联系的，适用该另一国家或者地区的法律。

**Article 5** If the parties have not chosen the law applicable to contract disputes, the law of the country or region most closely connected with the contract shall apply.  
When the People's Court determines the law applicable to contract disputes in accordance with the principle of the closest connection, it shall, based on factors such as the special nature of the contract and which party's obligations best reflect the essential characteristics of the contract, determine the law of the country or region most closely connected with the contract as the governing law of the contract.  
(1) For a sales contract, the law of the seller's domicile at the time of conclusion of the contract shall apply; if the contract was negotiated and concluded at the buyer's domicile, or if the contract expressly provides that the seller shall perform the delivery obligation at the buyer's domicile, the law of the buyer's domicile shall apply.  
(2) For contracts for processing with supplied materials, assembling with supplied parts, and other processing or work contracts, the law of the domicile of the processor or contractor shall apply.  
(3) For contracts for the supply of complete sets of equipment, the law of the place of installation of the equipment shall apply.  
(4) For contracts for the sale, lease, or mortgage of immovable property, the law of the location of the immovable property shall apply.  
(5) For contracts for the lease of movable property, the law of the lessor's domicile shall apply.  
(6) For contracts for the pledge of movable property, the law of the pledgee's domicile shall apply.  
(7) For loan contracts, the law of the lender's domicile shall apply.  
(8) For insurance contracts, the law of the insurer's domicile shall apply.  
(9) For financial leasing contracts, the law of the lessee's domicile shall apply.  
(10) For construction project contracts, the law of the location of the construction project shall apply.  
(11) For warehousing and storage contracts, the law of the warehouse keeper's or bailee's domicile shall apply.  
(12) For guarantee contracts, the law of the guarantor's domicile shall apply.  
(13) For mandate contracts, the law of the mandatory's domicile shall apply.  
(14) For contracts for the issuance, sale, and transfer of bonds, the law of the place of issuance, the law of the place of sale, and the law of the place of transfer shall apply respectively.  
(15) For auction contracts, the law of the place where the auction is held shall apply.  
(16) For commission agency contracts, the law of the commission agent's domicile shall apply.  
(17) For brokerage contracts, the law of the broker's domicile shall apply.  
If the above contract is obviously more closely connected with another country or region, the law of that other country or region shall apply.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  当事人规避中华人民共和国法律、行政法规的强制性规定的行为，不发生适用外国法律的效力，该合同争议应当适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 6** The act of a party evading the mandatory provisions of the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China shall not have the effect of applying foreign law, and such contract disputes shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  适用外国法律违反中华人民共和国社会公共利益的，该外国法律不予适用，而应当适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 7** If the application of foreign law violates the social public interest of the People's Republic of China, such foreign law shall not apply, and the laws of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  在中华人民共和国领域内履行的下列合同，适用中华人民共和国法律：  
（一）中外合资经营企业合同；  
（二）中外合作经营企业合同；  
（三）中外合作勘探、开发自然资源合同；  
（四）中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业、外商独资企业股份转让合同；  
（五）外国自然人、法人或者其他组织承包经营在中华人民共和国领域内设立的中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业的合同；  
（六）外国自然人、法人或者其他组织购买中华人民共和国领域内的非外商投资企业股东的股权的合同；  
（七）外国自然人、法人或者其他组织认购中华人民共和国领域内的非外商投资有限责任公司或者股份有限公司增资的合同；  
（八）外国自然人、法人或者其他组织购买中华人民共和国领域内的非外商投资企业资产的合同；  
（九）中华人民共和国法律、行政法规规定应适用中华人民共和国法律的其他合同。

**Article 8** The following contracts performed within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Contracts for Sino-foreign equity joint venture enterprises;  
(2) Contracts for Sino-foreign contractual joint venture enterprises;  
(3) Contracts for Sino-foreign cooperative exploration and development of natural resources;  
(4) Contracts for the transfer of shares in Sino-foreign equity joint venture enterprises, Sino-foreign contractual joint venture enterprises, and wholly foreign-owned enterprises;  
(5) Contracts under which foreign natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations contract to operate Sino-foreign equity joint venture enterprises or Sino-foreign contractual joint venture enterprises established within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(6) Contracts under which foreign natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations purchase the equity of shareholders of non-foreign-invested enterprises within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(7) Contracts under which foreign natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations subscribe to the capital increase of non-foreign-invested limited liability companies or joint stock limited companies within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(8) Contracts under which foreign natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations purchase the assets of non-foreign-invested enterprises within the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(9) Other contracts for which the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China stipulate that the laws of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  当事人选择或者变更选择合同争议应适用的法律为外国法律时，由当事人提供或者证明该外国法律的相关内容。  
人民法院根据最密切联系原则确定合同争议应适用的法律为外国法律时，可以依职权查明该外国法律，亦可以要求当事人提供或者证明该外国法律的内容。  
当事人和人民法院通过适当的途径均不能查明外国法律的内容的，人民法院可以适用中华人民共和国法律。

**Article 9** Where the parties choose or change the choice of the law applicable to contract disputes to be foreign law, the parties shall provide or prove the relevant content of such foreign law.  
Where the People's Court determines, in accordance with the principle of the closest connection, that the law applicable to contract disputes is foreign law, it may ascertain such foreign law ex officio, and may also require the parties to provide or prove the content of such foreign law.  
Where neither the parties nor the People's Court can ascertain the content of the foreign law through appropriate means, the People's Court may apply the laws of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  当事人对查明的外国法律内容经质证后无异议的，人民法院应予确认。当事人有异议的，由人民法院审查认定。

**Article 10** Where, after cross-examination, the parties have no objection to the ascertained content of the foreign law, the People's Court shall confirm it. Where the parties have objections, the People's Court shall examine and determine it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的民事或商事合同的法律适用，参照本规定。

**Article 11** The application of law to civil or commercial contracts involving the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be governed by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  本院以前发布的规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 12** Where any provisions previously issued by this court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
