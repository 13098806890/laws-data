# 最高人民法院关于不服工商行政管理机关的确认经济合同无效及财产损失的处理决定的案件应属行政案件的复函

**分类**：司法解释

**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1992-04-01

### 最高人民法院关于不服工商行政管理机关的确认经济合同无效及财产损失的处理决定的案件应属行政案件的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于不服工商行政管理机关的确认  
  
经济合同无效及财产损失的处理决定的案件  
  
应属行政案件的复函  
  
（1992）行他字第5号  
  
湖南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院湘高法行（ 1992）1号《关于当事人不服工商行政管理机关对无效经济合同的财产损失的处理决定而起诉是否为行政案件的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
工商行政管理机关作出确认经济合同无效的决定时，对财产损失作了调解解决的，一方或双方当事人事后反悔，依法就有关财产问题向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应作为经济纠纷案件受理；公民、法人或者其他组织对工商行政管理机关所作的确认经济合同无效及财产损失的处理决定不服，向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应作为行政案件受理。  
  
1992年4月1日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Cases Concerning Discontent with the Confirmation of Void Economic Contracts and Decisions on Handling Property Losses by Administrative Authorities for Industry and Commerce  
Which Shall Be Administrative Cases  
(1992) Xing Ta Zi No. 5  
Higher People's Court of Hunan Province:  
We have received your court's Xiang Gao Fa Xing (1992) No. 1 "Request for Instructions on Whether a Prosecution Initiated by a Party Who is Dissatisfied with the Decision of the Administrative Authority for Industry and Commerce on the Handling of Property Losses from a Void Economic Contract Constitutes an Administrative Case." Upon study, we reply as follows:  
When an administrative authority for industry and commerce, in making a decision to confirm a void economic contract, has mediated and resolved the property losses, and one or both parties later renege and initiate prosecution before the People's Court regarding relevant property matters according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case as an economic dispute case; if a citizen, legal person, or other organization is dissatisfied with the decision made by the administrative authority for industry and commerce to confirm the void economic contract and handle the property losses, and initiates prosecution before the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case as an administrative case.  
April 1, 1992
