# 最高人民法院关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕7号  
**公布日期**：2003-04-28  
**生效日期**：2003-06-01

> （2003年3月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1267次会议通过　2003年4月28日最高人民法院公告公布　自2003年6月1日起施行） 为正确、及时审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》、《中华人民共和国担保法》等相关法律，结合民事审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　本解释所称的商品房买卖合同，是指房地产开发企业（以下统称为出卖人）将尚未建成或者已竣工的房屋向社会销售并转移房屋所有权于买受人，买受人支付价款的合同。 第二条　出卖人未取得商品房预售许可证明，与买受人订立的商品房预售合同，应当认定无效，但是在起诉前取得商品房预售许可证明的，可以认定有效。 第三条　商品房的销售广告和宣传资料为要约邀请，但是出卖人就商品房开发规划范围内的房屋及相关设施所作的说明和允诺具体确定，并对商品房买卖合同的订立以及房屋价格的确定有重大影响的，应当视为要约。该说明和允诺即使未载入商品房买卖合同，亦应当视为合同内容，当事人违反的，应当承担违约责任。 第四条　出卖人通过认购、订购、预订等方式向买受人收受定金作为订立商品房买卖合同担保的，如果因当事人一方原因未能订立商品房买卖合同，应当按照法律关于定金的规定处理；因不可归责于当事人双方的事由，导致商品房买卖合同未能订立的，出卖人应当将定金返还买受人。 第五条　商品房的认购、订购、预订等协议具备《商品房销售管理办法》第十六条规定的商品房买卖合同的主要内容，并且出卖人已经按照约定收受购房款的，该协议应当认定为商品房买卖合同。 第六条　当事人以商品房预售合同未按照法律、行政法规规定办理登记备案手续为由，请求确认合同无效的，不予支持。 当事人约定以办理登记备案手续为商品房预售合同生效条件的，从其约定，但当事人一方已经履行主要义务，对方接受的除外。 第七条　拆迁人与被拆迁人按照所有权调换形式订立拆迁补偿安置协议，明确约定拆迁人以位置、用途特定的房屋对被拆迁人予以补偿安置，如果拆迁人将该补偿安置房屋另行出卖给第三人，被拆迁人请求优先取得补偿安置房屋的，应予支持。 被拆迁人请求解除拆迁补偿安置协议的，按照本解释第八条的规定处理。 第八条　具有下列情形之一，导致商品房买卖合同目的不能实现的，无法取得房屋的买受人可以请求解除合同、返还已付购房款及利息、赔偿损失，并可以请求出卖人承担不超过已付购房款一倍的赔偿责任： （一）商品房买卖合同订立后，出卖人未告知买受人又将该房屋抵押给第三人； （二）商品房买卖合同订立后，出卖人又将该房屋出卖给第三人。 第九条　出卖人订立商品房买卖合同时，具有下列情形之一，导致合同无效或者被撤销、解除的，买受人可以请求返还已付购房款及利息、赔偿损失，并可以请求出卖人承担不超过已付购房款一倍的赔偿责任：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本解释所称的商品房买卖合同，是指房地产开发企业（以下统称为出卖人）将尚未建成或者已竣工的房屋向社会销售并转移房屋所有权于买受人，买受人支付价款的合同。

**Article 1** The contract for the sale of commercial housing referred to in this Interpretation means a contract under which a real estate development enterprise (hereinafter collectively referred to as the seller) sells to the public houses that have not yet been constructed or have been completed and transfers the ownership of the houses to the buyer, and the buyer pays the price.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　出卖人未取得商品房预售许可证明，与买受人订立的商品房预售合同，应当认定无效，但是在起诉前取得商品房预售许可证明的，可以认定有效。

**Article 2** If a seller concludes a commercial housing pre-sale contract with a buyer without obtaining a commercial housing pre-sale permit certificate, the contract shall be determined void; however, if the seller obtains the commercial housing pre-sale permit certificate before the prosecution, it may be determined valid.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　商品房的销售广告和宣传资料为要约邀请，但是出卖人就商品房开发规划范围内的房屋及相关设施所作的说明和允诺具体确定，并对商品房买卖合同的订立以及房屋价格的确定有重大影响的，应当视为要约。该说明和允诺即使未载入商品房买卖合同，亦应当视为合同内容，当事人违反的，应当承担违约责任。

**Article 3** Sales advertisements and promotional materials for commercial housing shall be an invitation to offer; however, if the seller's explanations and promises regarding the houses and related facilities within the scope of the commercial housing development plan are specific and definite, and have a significant impact on the conclusion of the commercial housing sale contract and the determination of the house price, they shall be regarded as an offer. Even if such explanations and promises are not included in the commercial housing sale contract, they shall still be regarded as part of the contract content, and a party that violates them shall bear liability for breach of contract.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　出卖人通过认购、订购、预订等方式向买受人收受定金作为订立商品房买卖合同担保的，如果因当事人一方原因未能订立商品房买卖合同，应当按照法律关于定金的规定处理；因不可归责于当事人双方的事由，导致商品房买卖合同未能订立的，出卖人应当将定金返还买受人。

**Article 4** If a seller collects a deposit from a buyer by means of subscription, order, reservation, or similar means as security for concluding a commercial housing sale contract, if the commercial housing sale contract fails to be concluded due to the reason of one party, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the law on deposits; if the commercial housing sale contract fails to be concluded due to reasons not attributable to either party, the seller shall return the deposit to the buyer.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　商品房的认购、订购、预订等协议具备《商品房销售管理办法》第十六条规定的商品房买卖合同的主要内容，并且出卖人已经按照约定收受购房款的，该协议应当认定为商品房买卖合同。

**Article 5** If an agreement for subscription, order, reservation, or similar for commercial housing contains the main content of a commercial housing sale contract as stipulated in Article 16 of the Measures for the Administration of Commercial Housing Sales, and the seller has already accepted the purchase price in accordance with the agreement, such agreement shall be determined as a commercial housing sale contract.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人以商品房预售合同未按照法律、行政法规规定办理登记备案手续为由，请求确认合同无效的，不予支持。  
当事人约定以办理登记备案手续为商品房预售合同生效条件的，从其约定，但当事人一方已经履行主要义务，对方接受的除外。

**Article 6** If a party requests confirmation of the invalidity of a commercial housing pre-sale contract on the ground that the registration and filing procedures have not been completed in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, the request shall not be supported.  
If the parties agree that the completion of registration and filing procedures shall be a condition for the taking effect of the commercial housing pre-sale contract, such agreement shall prevail; however, this shall not apply if one party has already performed the main obligations and the other party has accepted them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拆迁人与被拆迁人按照所有权调换形式订立拆迁补偿安置协议，明确约定拆迁人以位置、用途特定的房屋对被拆迁人予以补偿安置，如果拆迁人将该补偿安置房屋另行出卖给第三人，被拆迁人请求优先取得补偿安置房屋的，应予支持。  
被拆迁人请求解除拆迁补偿安置协议的，按照[本解释第八条](#art-8)的规定处理。

**Article 7** If the demolition party and the demolished party conclude a demolition compensation and resettlement agreement in the form of ownership exchange, expressly agreeing that the demolition party shall compensate and resettle the demolished party with a house of specific location and use, and if the demolition party sells such compensation and resettlement house to a third party, the request of the demolished party to obtain the compensation and resettlement house with priority shall be supported.  
If the demolished party requests dissolution of the demolition compensation and resettlement agreement, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 8 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　具有下列情形之一，导致商品房买卖合同目的不能实现的，无法取得房屋的买受人可以请求解除合同、返还已付购房款及利息、赔偿损失，并可以请求出卖人承担不超过已付购房款一倍的赔偿责任：  
（一）商品房买卖合同订立后，出卖人未告知买受人又将该房屋抵押给第三人；  
（二）商品房买卖合同订立后，出卖人又将该房屋出卖给第三人。

**Article 8** In any of the following circumstances, which frustrates the purpose of the commercial housing sale contract and prevents the buyer from obtaining the house, the buyer may request dissolution of the contract, return of the paid purchase price and interest, compensation for losses, and may request the seller to bear liability for compensation not exceeding one time the paid purchase price:  
(1) After the conclusion of the commercial housing sale contract, the seller mortgages the house to a third party without informing the buyer;  
(2) After the conclusion of the commercial housing sale contract, the seller sells the house to a third party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出卖人订立商品房买卖合同时，具有下列情形之一，导致合同无效或者被撤销、解除的，买受人可以请求返还已付购房款及利息、赔偿损失，并可以请求出卖人承担不超过已付购房款一倍的赔偿责任：  
（一）故意隐瞒没有取得商品房预售许可证明的事实或者提供虚假商品房预售许可证明；  
（二）故意隐瞒所售房屋已经抵押的事实；  
（三）故意隐瞒所售房屋已经出卖给第三人或者为拆迁补偿安置房屋的事实。

**Article 9** If, at the time of concluding a commercial housing sale contract, the seller is in any of the following circumstances, causing the contract to be void or to be revoked or dissolved, the buyer may request return of the paid purchase price and interest, compensation for losses, and may request the seller to bear liability for compensation not exceeding one time the paid purchase price:  
(1) Intentionally concealing the fact that no commercial housing pre-sale permit certificate has been obtained, or providing a false commercial housing pre-sale permit certificate;  
(2) Intentionally concealing the fact that the house sold has been mortgaged;  
(3) Intentionally concealing the fact that the house sold has already been sold to a third party or is a house for demolition compensation and resettlement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　买受人以出卖人与第三人恶意串通，另行订立商品房买卖合同并将房屋交付使用，导致其无法取得房屋为由，请求确认出卖人与第三人订立的商品房买卖合同无效的，应予支持。

**Article 10** If a buyer, on the ground that the seller maliciously colluded with a third party to enter into another commercial housing sale contract and delivered the house for use, thereby preventing the buyer from obtaining the house, requests confirmation of the invalidity of the commercial housing sale contract concluded between the seller and the third party, the request shall be supported.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对房屋的转移占有，视为房屋的交付使用，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
房屋毁损、灭失的风险，在交付使用前由出卖人承担，交付使用后由买受人承担；买受人接到出卖人的书面交房通知，无正当理由拒绝接收的，房屋毁损、灭失的风险自书面交房通知确定的交付使用之日起由买受人承担，但法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 11** Transfer of possession of a house shall be deemed as delivery for use, unless the parties agree otherwise.  
The risk of damage to or loss of the house shall be borne by the seller before delivery for use, and by the buyer after delivery for use; if the buyer receives a written notice of house delivery from the seller and refuses to accept delivery without justifiable reasons, the risk of damage to or loss of the house shall be borne by the buyer from the date of delivery for use specified in the written notice of house delivery, unless the law provides otherwise or the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因房屋主体结构质量不合格不能交付使用，或者房屋交付使用后，房屋主体结构质量经核验确属不合格，买受人请求解除合同和赔偿损失的，应予支持。

**Article 12** If a house cannot be delivered for use due to substandard quality of the main structure, or if after delivery for use, the quality of the main structure of the house is verified to be indeed substandard, the buyer's request for dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses shall be supported.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因房屋质量问题严重影响正常居住使用，买受人请求解除合同和赔偿损失的，应予支持。  
交付使用的房屋存在质量问题，在保修期内，出卖人应当承担修复责任；出卖人拒绝修复或者在合理期限内拖延修复的，买受人可以自行或者委托他人修复。修复费用及修复期间造成的其他损失由出卖人承担。

**Article 13** If quality problems of the house seriously affect normal habitation and use, the buyer's request for dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses shall be supported.  
If a house delivered for use has quality problems, the seller shall bear the responsibility for repair within the warranty period; if the seller refuses to repair or delays repair within a reasonable period, the buyer may repair the house on its own or entrust another person to do so. The repair costs and other losses incurred during the repair period shall be borne by the seller.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　出卖人交付使用的房屋套内建筑面积或者建筑面积与商品房买卖合同约定面积不符，合同有约定的，按照约定处理；合同没有约定或者约定不明确的，按照以下原则处理：  
（一）面积误差比绝对值在3%以内（含3%），按照合同约定的价格据实结算，买受人请求解除合同的，不予支持；  
（二）面积误差比绝对值超出3%，买受人请求解除合同、返还已付购房款及利息的，应予支持。买受人同意继续履行合同，房屋实际面积大于合同约定面积的，面积误差比在3%以内（含3%）部分的房价款由买受人按照约定的价格补足，面积误差比超出3%部分的房价款由出卖人承担，所有权归买受人；房屋实际面积小于合同约定面积的，面积误差比在3%以内（含3%）部分的房价款及利息由出卖人返还买受人，面积误差比超过3%部分的房价款由出卖人双倍返还买受人。

**Article 14** If the floor area within the set or the building area of a house delivered by the seller for use does not conform to the area agreed in the commercial housing sale contract, if the contract has an agreement, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the agreement; if the contract has no agreement or the agreement is unclear, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) If the absolute value of the area error ratio is within 3% (including 3%), settlement shall be made at the price agreed in the contract; the buyer's request for dissolution of the contract shall not be supported;  
(2) If the absolute value of the area error ratio exceeds 3%, the buyer's request for dissolution of the contract and return of the paid purchase price and interest shall be supported. If the buyer agrees to continue performing the contract, and the actual area of the house is larger than the area agreed in the contract, the part of the price for the area error ratio within 3% (including 3%) shall be made up by the buyer at the agreed price, and the part of the price for the area error ratio exceeding 3% shall be borne by the seller, with ownership belonging to the buyer; if the actual area of the house is smaller than the area agreed in the contract, the part of the price and interest for the area error ratio within 3% (including 3%) shall be returned by the seller to the buyer, and the part of the price for the area error ratio exceeding 3% shall be returned by the seller to the buyer at double the amount.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　根据《合同法》第九十四条的规定，出卖人迟延交付房屋或者买受人迟延支付购房款，经催告后在三个月的合理期限内仍未履行，当事人一方请求解除合同的，应予支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。  
法律没有规定或者当事人没有约定，经对方当事人催告后，解除权行使的合理期限为三个月。对方当事人没有催告的，解除权应当在解除权发生之日起一年内行使；逾期不行使的，解除权消灭。

**Article 15** In accordance with Article 94 of the Contract Law, if the seller delays in delivering the house or the buyer delays in paying the purchase price, and after a demand, neither party performs within a reasonable period of three months, a party's request for dissolution of the contract shall be supported, unless the parties agree otherwise.  
If the law has no provision or the parties have no agreement, after a demand by the other party, the reasonable period for exercising the right of dissolution shall be three months. If the other party does not make a demand, the right of dissolution shall be exercised within one year from the date on which the right of dissolution arises; if it is not exercised within that period, the right of dissolution shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人以约定的违约金过高为由请求减少的，应当以违约金超过造成的损失30%为标准适当减少；当事人以约定的违约金低于造成的损失为由请求增加的，应当以违约造成的损失确定违约金数额。

**Article 16** If a party requests a reduction on the ground that the agreed liquidated damages are excessively high, the reduction shall be made appropriately based on the standard that the liquidated damages exceed 30% of the losses caused; if a party requests an increase on the ground that the agreed liquidated damages are lower than the losses caused, the amount of liquidated damages shall be determined based on the losses caused by the breach.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　商品房买卖合同没有约定违约金数额或者损失赔偿额计算方法，违约金数额或者损失赔偿额可以参照以下标准确定：  
逾期付款的，按照未付购房款总额，参照中国人民银行规定的金融机构计收逾期贷款利息的标准计算。  
逾期交付使用房屋的，按照逾期交付使用房屋期间有关主管部门公布或者有资格的房地产评估机构评定的同地段同类房屋租金标准确定。

**Article 17** If a commercial housing sale contract does not stipulate the amount of liquidated damages or the method for calculating the amount of compensation for losses, the amount of liquidated damages or the amount of compensation for losses may be determined by reference to the following standards:  
For overdue payment, it shall be calculated on the basis of the total unpaid purchase price, by reference to the standard of interest on overdue loans charged by financial institutions as prescribed by the People's Bank of China.  
For overdue delivery of the house for use, it shall be determined by reference to the standard of rent for houses of the same type in the same area as published by the relevant competent authority or assessed by a qualified real estate appraisal institution during the period of overdue delivery for use.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　由于出卖人的原因，买受人在下列期限届满未能取得房屋权属证书的，除当事人有特殊约定外，出卖人应当承担违约责任：  
（一）商品房买卖合同约定的办理房屋所有权登记的期限；  
（二）商品房买卖合同的标的物为尚未建成房屋的，自房屋交付使用之日起90日；  
（三）商品房买卖合同的标的物为已竣工房屋的，自合同订立之日起90日。  
合同没有约定违约金或者损失数额难以确定的，可以按照已付购房款总额，参照中国人民银行规定的金融机构计收逾期贷款利息的标准计算。

**Article 18** If, due to the seller's reason, the buyer fails to obtain the certificate of title to the house upon the expiry of any of the following periods, the seller shall bear liability for breach of contract, unless the parties have a special agreement:  
(1) The period for handling the registration of house ownership as agreed in the commercial housing sale contract;  
(2) If the subject matter of the commercial housing sale contract is a house that has not yet been constructed, 90 days from the date of delivery of the house for use;  
(3) If the subject matter of the commercial housing sale contract is a completed house, 90 days from the date of conclusion of the contract.  
If the contract does not stipulate liquidated damages or the amount of losses is difficult to determine, it may be calculated on the basis of the total paid purchase price, by reference to the standard of interest on overdue loans charged by financial institutions as prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　商品房买卖合同约定或者[《城市房地产开发经营管理条例》第三十三条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/城市房地产开发经营管理条例.md#art-33)规定的办理房屋所有权登记的期限届满后超过一年，由于出卖人的原因，导致买受人无法办理房屋所有权登记，买受人请求解除合同和赔偿损失的，应予支持。

**Article 19** If, more than one year after the expiry of the period for handling house ownership registration as agreed in the commercial housing sale contract or as provided in Article 33 of the Regulations on the Administration of Urban Real Estate Development and Management, the buyer is unable to undergo house ownership registration due to the seller's reason, the buyer's request for dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses shall be supported.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出卖人与包销人订立商品房包销合同，约定出卖人将其开发建设的房屋交由包销人以出卖人的名义销售的，包销期满未销售的房屋，由包销人按照合同约定的包销价格购买，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 20** If a seller and an exclusive sales agent conclude a commercial housing exclusive sale contract agreeing that the seller shall entrust the houses it develops and constructs to the exclusive sales agent for sale in the name of the seller, the houses not sold upon the expiry of the exclusive sale period shall be purchased by the exclusive sales agent at the exclusive sale price agreed in the contract, unless the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　出卖人自行销售已经约定由包销人包销的房屋，包销人请求出卖人赔偿损失的，应予支持，但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 21** If the seller sells on its own the houses that have been agreed to be exclusively sold by the exclusive sales agent, the exclusive sales agent's request for compensation for losses from the seller shall be supported, unless the parties agree otherwise.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对于买受人因商品房买卖合同与出卖人发生的纠纷，人民法院应当通知包销人参加诉讼；出卖人、包销人和买受人对各自的权利义务有明确约定的，按照约定的内容确定各方的诉讼地位。

**Article 22** For disputes between a buyer and a seller arising from a commercial housing sale contract, the People's Court shall notify the exclusive sales agent to participate in the litigation; if the seller, the exclusive sales agent, and the buyer have clear agreements on their respective rights and obligations, the litigation status of each party shall be determined in accordance with the agreed content.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　商品房买卖合同约定，买受人以担保贷款方式付款、因当事人一方原因未能订立商品房担保贷款合同并导致商品房买卖合同不能继续履行的，对方当事人可以请求解除合同和赔偿损失，因不可归责于当事人双方的事由未能订立商品房担保贷款合同并导致商品房买卖合同不能继续履行的，当事人可以请求解除合同，出卖人应当将收受的购房款本金及其利息或者定金返还买受人。

**Article 23** If a commercial housing sale contract stipulates that the buyer shall pay by way of guaranteed loan, and if the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing fails to be concluded due to the reason of one party, causing the commercial housing sale contract to be unable to be further performed, the other party may request dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses; if the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing fails to be concluded due to reasons not attributable to either party, causing the commercial housing sale contract to be unable to be further performed, the parties may request dissolution of the contract, and the seller shall return the principal and interest of the purchase price or the deposit received to the buyer.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因商品房买卖合同被确认无效或者被撤销、解除，致使商品房担保贷款合同的目的无法实现，当事人请求解除商品房担保贷款合同的，应予支持。

**Article 24** If the commercial housing sale contract is confirmed void or is revoked or dissolved, causing the purpose of the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing to be frustrated, a party's request for dissolution of the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing shall be supported.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　以担保贷款为付款方式的商品房买卖合同的当事人一方请求确认商品房买卖合同无效或者撤销、解除合同的，如果担保权人作为有独立请求权第三人提出诉讼请求，应当与商品房担保贷款合同纠纷合并审理；未提出诉讼请求的，仅处理商品房买卖合同纠纷。担保权人就商品房担保贷款合同纠纷另行起诉的，可以与商品房买卖合同纠纷合并审理。  
商品房买卖合同被确认无效或者被撤销、解除后，商品房担保贷款合同也被解除的，出卖人应当将收受的购房贷款和购房款的本金及利息分别返还担保权人和买受人。

**Article 25** If a party to a commercial housing sale contract with guaranteed loan as the method of payment requests confirmation of the invalidity of the commercial housing sale contract or revocation or dissolution of the contract, if the security holder files a claim as a third party with independent claims, the case shall be consolidated with the dispute over the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing; if no claim is filed, only the dispute over the commercial housing sale contract shall be handled. If the security holder separately initiates a lawsuit regarding the dispute over the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing, it may be consolidated with the dispute over the commercial housing sale contract.  
After the commercial housing sale contract is confirmed void or is revoked or dissolved, and the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing is also dissolved, the seller shall return the principal and interest of the purchase loan and the purchase price received to the security holder and the buyer respectively.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　买受人未按照商品房担保贷款合同的约定偿还贷款，亦未与担保权人办理商品房抵押登记手续，担保权人起诉买受人，请求处分商品房买卖合同项下买受人合同权利的，应当通知出卖人参加诉讼；担保权人同时起诉出卖人时，如果出卖人为商品房担保贷款合同提供保证的，应当列为共同被告。

**Article 26** If a buyer fails to repay the loan in accordance with the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing and also fails to undergo the commercial housing mortgage registration procedures with the security holder, and the security holder sues the buyer, requesting disposition of the buyer's contractual rights under the commercial housing sale contract, the seller shall be notified to participate in the litigation; if the security holder simultaneously sues the seller, and if the seller has provided a guarantee for the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing, the seller shall be listed as a co-defendant.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　买受人未按照商品房担保贷款合同的约定偿还贷款，但是已经取得房屋权属证书并与担保权人办理了商品房抵押登记手续，抵押权人请求买受人偿还贷款或者就抵押的房屋优先受偿的，不应当追加出卖人为当事人，但出卖人提供保证的除外。

**Article 27** If a buyer fails to repay the loan in accordance with the guaranteed loan contract for commercial housing, but has obtained the certificate of title to the house and undergone the commercial housing mortgage registration procedures with the security holder, if the mortgagee requests the buyer to repay the loan or to be compensated in priority from the mortgaged house, the seller shall not be added as a party, unless the seller has provided a guarantee.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本解释自2003年6月1日起施行。  
《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》施行后订立的商品房买卖合同发生的纠纷案件，本解释公布施行后尚在一审、二审阶段的，适用本解释。  
《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》施行后订立的商品房买卖合同发生的纠纷案件，在本解释公布施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的，不适用本解释。  
《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》施行前发生的商品房买卖行为，适用当时的法律、法规和《最高人民法院〈关于审理房地产管理法施行前房地产开发经营案件若干问题的解答〉》。

**Article 28** This Interpretation shall come into force as of June 1, 2003.  
For dispute cases arising from commercial housing sale contracts concluded after the coming into force of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Urban Real Estate, which are still in the first instance or second instance stage after the promulgation and coming into force of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply.  
For dispute cases arising from commercial housing sale contracts concluded after the coming into force of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Urban Real Estate, which have been finally adjudicated before the promulgation and coming into force of this Interpretation, where a party applies for retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.  
For commercial housing sale transactions occurring before the coming into force of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Urban Real Estate, the laws and regulations in force at that time and the Supreme People's Court's Answers to Several Questions Concerning Real Estate Development and Management Cases Prior to the Implementation of the Real Estate Administration Law shall apply.
