### 最高人民法院关于借款合同双方当事人未经保证人同意达成延期还款协议后保证人是否继续承担担保责任的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于借款合同双方当事人  
  
未经保证人同意达成延期还款协议后  
  
保证人是否继续承担担保责任的批复  
  
法（经）复〔 1988〕4号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1987）豫法经字第 3号请示收悉。经研究，并征询有关部门意见，答复如下：  
  
在保证合同中，保证人只应对经他同意、签字（盖章）的保证内容承担担保责任。你院请示的案件中，南乐县公路管理段在借款方南乐县大华贸易公司与贷款方中国工商银行南乐县支行 1985年 3月所签订的借款合同中担保单位栏内盖章，故对该合同中借款方所应履行的义务承担担保责任。但借、贷双方在合同履行中，不通知担保人，亦未征得担保人同意，于同年 4月下旬达成书面协议，将还款日期延长 1个月，这一变更，应视为成立了新的法律关系，解除了原来的担保合同。因此，南乐县公路管理段不应再承担保证责任。  
  
此复。  
  
1988年 1月9日

**Article 1** Reply on Whether the Guarantor Shall Continue to Bear the Guarantee Liability After the Two Parties to a Loan Contract Reach an Agreement on Extension of Repayment Without the Consent of the Guarantor  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1988] No. 4  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
We have received your request for instructions, Case No. (1987) Yu Fa Jing Zi No. 3. Upon study and consultation with relevant departments, we reply as follows:  
In a guarantee contract, the guarantor shall only bear the guarantee liability for the guaranteed content that has been agreed to and signed (or sealed) by him. In the case submitted by your court, Nanle County Highway Management Section stamped its seal in the guarantee unit column on the loan contract signed in March 1985 between the borrower, Nanle County Dahua Trade Company, and the lender, Nanle County Sub-branch of Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, and therefore it shall bear the guarantee liability for the obligations to be performed by the borrower under that contract. However, during the performance of the contract, the borrower and the lender, without notifying the guarantor or obtaining its consent, reached a written agreement in late April of the same year to extend the repayment date by one month. This modification shall be deemed to have established a new legal relationship and dissolved the original guarantee contract. Therefore, Nanle County Highway Management Section shall no longer bear the guarantee liability.  
This reply is hereby given.  
January 9, 1988
