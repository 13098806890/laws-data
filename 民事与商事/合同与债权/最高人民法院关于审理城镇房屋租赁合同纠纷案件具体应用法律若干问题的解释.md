# 最高人民法院关于审理城镇房屋租赁合同纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕11号  
**公布日期**：2009-07-30  
**生效日期**：2009-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理城镇房屋租赁合同纠纷案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年6月22日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1469次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年9月1日起施行。 2009年7月30日 最高人民法院 关于审理城镇房屋租赁合同纠纷案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕11号 （2009年6月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1469次会议通过　2009年7月30日最高人民法院公告公布　自2009年9月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本解释所称城镇房屋，是指城市、镇规划区内的房屋。  
乡、村庄规划区内的房屋租赁合同纠纷案件，可以参照本解释处理。但法律另有规定的，适用其规定。  
当事人依照国家福利政策租赁公有住房、廉租住房、经济适用住房产生的纠纷案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 1** For the purposes of this Interpretation, "urban housing" means housing within the urban and town planning areas.  
Dispute cases over housing lease contracts within the planning areas of townships and villages may be handled by reference to this Interpretation. However, if the law provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.  
This Interpretation shall not apply to dispute cases arising from the lease of public housing, low-rent housing, or affordable housing by the parties in accordance with the national welfare policies.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　出租人就未取得建设工程规划许可证或者未按照建设工程规划许可证的规定建设的房屋，与承租人订立的租赁合同无效。但在一审法庭辩论终结前取得建设工程规划许可证或者经主管部门批准建设的，人民法院应当认定有效。

**Article 2** Where a lessor concludes a lease contract with a lessee for housing constructed without obtaining a construction project planning permit or not in accordance with the provisions of the construction project planning permit, the lease contract shall be void. However, if the construction project planning permit is obtained or the construction is approved by the competent authority before the conclusion of the debate in the first instance trial, the People's Court shall uphold the contract as valid.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　出租人就未经批准或者未按照批准内容建设的临时建筑，与承租人订立的租赁合同无效。但在一审法庭辩论终结前经主管部门批准建设的，人民法院应当认定有效。  
租赁期限超过临时建筑的使用期限，超过部分无效。但在一审法庭辩论终结前经主管部门批准延长使用期限的，人民法院应当认定延长使用期限内的租赁期间有效。

**Article 3** Where a lessor concludes a lease contract with a lessee for a temporary building constructed without approval or not in accordance with the approved content, the lease contract shall be void. However, if the construction is approved by the competent authority before the conclusion of the debate in the first instance trial, the People's Court shall uphold the contract as valid.  
Where the lease term exceeds the service life of the temporary building, the excess portion shall be void. However, if the extension of the service life is approved by the competent authority before the conclusion of the debate in the first instance trial, the People's Court shall uphold the lease term during the extended period as valid.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人以房屋租赁合同未按照法律、行政法规规定办理登记备案手续为由，请求确认合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。  
当事人约定以办理登记备案手续为房屋租赁合同生效条件的，从其约定。但当事人一方已经履行主要义务，对方接受的除外。

**Article 4** Where a party requests confirmation of a contract as void on the ground that the housing lease contract has not undergone the registration and filing procedures as required by laws and administrative regulations, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
Where the parties agree that the completion of the registration and filing procedures is a condition for the housing lease contract to take effect, such agreement shall be followed, except where one party has already performed the principal obligations and the other party has accepted them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　房屋租赁合同无效，当事人请求参照合同约定的租金标准支付房屋占有使用费的，人民法院一般应予支持。  
当事人请求赔偿因合同无效受到的损失，人民法院依照合同法的有关规定和本司法解释第九条、第十三条、第十四条的规定处理。

**Article 5** Where a housing lease contract is void and a party requests the payment of a fee for the use and possession of the housing by reference to the rent standard stipulated in the contract, the People's Court shall generally uphold such request.  
Where a party requests compensation for losses incurred due to the void contract, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Contract Law and Articles 9, 13, and 14 of this Judicial Interpretation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　出租人就同一房屋订立数份租赁合同，在合同均有效的情况下，承租人均主张履行合同的，人民法院按照下列顺序确定履行合同的承租人：  
（一）已经合法占有租赁房屋的；  
（二）已经办理登记备案手续的；  
（三）合同成立在先的。  
不能取得租赁房屋的承租人请求解除合同、赔偿损失的，依照合同法的有关规定处理。

**Article 6** Where a lessor concludes several lease contracts for the same housing, and all contracts are valid, and all lessees claim performance of the contract, the People's Court shall determine the lessee who shall perform the contract in the following order:  
(1) The lessee who has legally taken possession of the leased housing;  
(2) The lessee who has completed the registration and filing procedures;  
(3) The lessee whose contract was concluded earlier.  
Where a lessee who cannot obtain the leased housing requests dissolution of the contract and compensation for losses, such matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承租人擅自变动房屋建筑主体和承重结构或者扩建，在出租人要求的合理期限内仍不予恢复原状，出租人请求解除合同并要求赔偿损失的，人民法院依照合同法第二百一十九条的规定处理。

**Article 7** Where a lessee without authorization alters the main structure and load-bearing structure of the housing or expands it, and fails to restore the housing to its original condition within a reasonable period required by the lessor, and the lessor requests dissolution of the contract and demands compensation for losses, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 219 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　因下列情形之一，导致租赁房屋无法使用，承租人请求解除合同的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）租赁房屋被司法机关或者行政机关依法查封的；  
（二）租赁房屋权属有争议的；  
（三）租赁房屋具有违反法律、行政法规关于房屋使用条件强制性规定情况的。

**Article 8** Where the leased housing becomes unusable due to any of the following circumstances, and the lessee requests dissolution of the contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request:  
(1) The leased housing is lawfully sealed up by a judicial organ or administrative organ;  
(2) There is a dispute over the ownership of the leased housing;  
(3) The leased housing violates the mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations regarding the conditions for use of housing.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承租人经出租人同意装饰装修，租赁合同无效时，未形成附合的装饰装修物，出租人同意利用的，可折价归出租人所有；不同意利用的，可由承租人拆除。因拆除造成房屋毁损的，承租人应当恢复原状。  
已形成附合的装饰装修物，出租人同意利用的，可折价归出租人所有；不同意利用的，由双方各自按照导致合同无效的过错分担现值损失。

**Article 9** Where the lessee decorates or fits up the housing with the consent of the lessor, and the lease contract is void, for decorations and fittings that are not attached to the housing, if the lessor agrees to use them, they may be transferred to the lessor at a depreciated value; if the lessor does not agree to use them, the lessee may remove them. If removal causes damage to the housing, the lessee shall restore the housing to its original condition.  
For decorations and fittings that are attached to the housing, if the lessor agrees to use them, they may be transferred to the lessor at a depreciated value; if the lessor does not agree to use them, the losses in current value shall be shared between the parties in proportion to their respective fault in causing the contract to be void.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　承租人经出租人同意装饰装修，租赁期间届满或者合同解除时，除当事人另有约定外，未形成附合的装饰装修物，可由承租人拆除。因拆除造成房屋毁损的，承租人应当恢复原状。

**Article 10** Where the lessee decorates or fits up the housing with the consent of the lessor, upon expiry of the lease term or dissolution of the contract, unless otherwise agreed by the parties, the decorations and fittings that are not attached to the housing may be removed by the lessee. If removal causes damage to the housing, the lessee shall restore the housing to its original condition.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　承租人经出租人同意装饰装修，合同解除时，双方对已形成附合的装饰装修物的处理没有约定的，人民法院按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）因出租人违约导致合同解除，承租人请求出租人赔偿剩余租赁期内装饰装修残值损失的，应予支持；  
（二）因承租人违约导致合同解除，承租人请求出租人赔偿剩余租赁期内装饰装修残值损失的，不予支持。但出租人同意利用的，应在利用价值范围内予以适当补偿；  
（三）因双方违约导致合同解除，剩余租赁期内的装饰装修残值损失，由双方根据各自的过错承担相应的责任；  
（四）因不可归责于双方的事由导致合同解除的，剩余租赁期内的装饰装修残值损失，由双方按照公平原则分担。法律另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 11** Where the lessee decorates or fits up the housing with the consent of the lessor, and upon dissolution of the contract, the parties have no agreement on the handling of the decorations and fittings that are attached to the housing, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the contract is dissolved due to the lessor's breach, and the lessee requests the lessor to compensate for the loss of the residual value of the decorations and fittings during the remaining lease term, such request shall be upheld;  
(2) If the contract is dissolved due to the lessee's breach, and the lessee requests the lessor to compensate for the loss of the residual value of the decorations and fittings during the remaining lease term, such request shall not be upheld. However, if the lessor agrees to use them, it shall provide appropriate compensation within the scope of the value of use;  
(3) If the contract is dissolved due to breach by both parties, the loss of the residual value of the decorations and fittings during the remaining lease term shall be borne by the parties in proportion to their respective fault;  
(4) If the contract is dissolved due to reasons not attributable to either party, the loss of the residual value of the decorations and fittings during the remaining lease term shall be shared between the parties in accordance with the principle of fairness. If the law provides otherwise, such provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　承租人经出租人同意装饰装修，租赁期间届满时，承租人请求出租人补偿附合装饰装修费用的，不予支持。但当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 12** Where the lessee decorates or fits up the housing with the consent of the lessor, and upon expiry of the lease term, the lessee requests the lessor to compensate for the costs of the attached decorations and fittings, such request shall not be upheld, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　承租人未经出租人同意装饰装修或者扩建发生的费用，由承租人负担。出租人请求承租人恢复原状或者赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where the lessee decorates, fits up, or expands the housing without the consent of the lessor, the expenses incurred shall be borne by the lessee. If the lessor requests the lessee to restore the housing to its original condition or compensate for losses, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　承租人经出租人同意扩建，但双方对扩建费用的处理没有约定的，人民法院按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）办理合法建设手续的，扩建造价费用由出租人负担；  
（二）未办理合法建设手续的，扩建造价费用由双方按照过错分担。

**Article 14** Where the lessee expands the housing with the consent of the lessor, but the parties have no agreement on the handling of the expansion costs, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the legal construction procedures have been completed, the cost of the expansion construction shall be borne by the lessor;  
(2) If the legal construction procedures have not been completed, the cost of the expansion construction shall be shared between the parties in proportion to their fault.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　承租人经出租人同意将租赁房屋转租给第三人时，转租期限超过承租人剩余租赁期限的，人民法院应当认定超过部分的约定无效。但出租人与承租人另有约定的除外。

**Article 15** Where the lessee subleases the leased housing to a third party with the consent of the lessor, and the sublease term exceeds the remaining lease term of the lessee, the People's Court shall hold that the agreement for the excess portion is void, unless otherwise agreed by the lessor and the lessee.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出租人知道或者应当知道承租人转租，但在六个月内未提出异议，其以承租人未经同意为由请求解除合同或者认定转租合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。  
因租赁合同产生的纠纷案件，人民法院可以通知次承租人作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 16** Where the lessor knows or should have known that the lessee subleases the housing but fails to raise an objection within six months, and then requests dissolution of the contract or a determination that the sublease contract is void on the ground that the lessee did not obtain consent, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
In a dispute case arising from the lease contract, the People's Court may notify the sublessee to participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　因承租人拖欠租金，出租人请求解除合同时，次承租人请求代承租人支付欠付的租金和违约金以抗辩出租人合同解除权的，人民法院应予支持。但转租合同无效的除外。  
次承租人代为支付的租金和违约金超出其应付的租金数额，可以折抵租金或者向承租人追偿。

**Article 17** Where the lessor requests dissolution of the contract due to the lessee's default in paying rent, and the sublessee requests to pay the overdue rent and liquidated damages on behalf of the lessee in order to defend against the lessor's right to dissolve the contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except where the sublease contract is void.  
If the rent and liquidated damages paid by the sublessee on behalf of the lessee exceed the amount of rent that the sublessee is liable to pay, such excess may be set off against rent or the sublessee may seek recourse from the lessee.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　房屋租赁合同无效、履行期限届满或者解除，出租人请求负有腾房义务的次承租人支付逾期腾房占有使用费的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 18** Where the housing lease contract is void, its performance term expires, or it is dissolved, and the lessor requests the sublessee who is obligated to vacate the housing to pay a fee for the use and possession during the period of delayed vacation, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　承租人租赁房屋用于以个体工商户或者个人合伙方式从事经营活动，承租人在租赁期间死亡、宣告失踪或者宣告死亡，其共同经营人或者其他合伙人请求按照原租赁合同租赁该房屋的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where a lessee leases housing for business operations in the form of an individual industrial and commercial household or a personal partnership, and the lessee dies, is declared missing, or is declared dead during the lease term, and the joint operator or other partner requests to lease the housing in accordance with the original lease contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　租赁房屋在租赁期间发生所有权变动，承租人请求房屋受让人继续履行原租赁合同的，人民法院应予支持。但租赁房屋具有下列情形或者当事人另有约定的除外：  
（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；  
（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的。

**Article 20** Where the ownership of the leased housing changes during the lease term, and the lessee requests the transferee of the housing to continue to perform the original lease contract, the People's Court shall uphold such request, except where the leased housing falls under any of the following circumstances or the parties have otherwise agreed:  
(1) A mortgage has been established on the housing before the lease, and the ownership changes due to the realization of the mortgage by the mortgagee;  
(2) The housing has been lawfully sealed up by the People's Court before the lease.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　出租人出卖租赁房屋未在合理期限内通知承租人或者存在其他侵害承租人优先购买权情形，承租人请求出租人承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。但请求确认出租人与第三人签订的房屋买卖合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 21** Where the lessor sells the leased housing without notifying the lessee within a reasonable period or otherwise infringes upon the lessee's preemptive right to purchase, and the lessee requests the lessor to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request. However, if the lessee requests confirmation that the housing sale contract concluded between the lessor and a third party is void, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　出租人与抵押权人协议折价、变卖租赁房屋偿还债务，应当在合理期限内通知承租人。承租人请求以同等条件优先购买房屋的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 22** Where the lessor agrees with the mortgagee to convert the leased housing into money by discount or sale to repay the debt, the lessor shall notify the lessee within a reasonable period. If the lessee requests to purchase the housing under the same conditions as a preemptive right, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出租人委托拍卖人拍卖租赁房屋，应当在拍卖5日前通知承租人。承租人未参加拍卖的，人民法院应当认定承租人放弃优先购买权。

**Article 23** Where the lessor entrusts an auctioneer to auction the leased housing, the lessor shall notify the lessee five days before the auction. If the lessee does not participate in the auction, the People's Court shall find that the lessee has waived the preemptive right to purchase.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　具有下列情形之一，承租人主张优先购买房屋的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）房屋共有人行使优先购买权的；  
（二）出租人将房屋出卖给近亲属，包括配偶、父母、子女、兄弟姐妹、祖父母、外祖父母、孙子女、外孙子女的；  
（三）出租人履行通知义务后，承租人在十五日内未明确表示购买的；  
（四）第三人善意购买租赁房屋并已经办理登记手续的。

**Article 24** Where any of the following circumstances exists, and the lessee claims a preemptive right to purchase the housing, the People's Court shall not support such claim:  
(1) A co-owner of the housing exercises the preemptive right to purchase;  
(2) The lessor sells the housing to a close relative, including a spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, maternal grandparent, grandchild, or maternal grandchild;  
(3) After the lessor performs the obligation of notification, the lessee does not clearly express an intention to purchase within fifteen days;  
(4) A third party purchases the leased housing in good faith and has completed the registration procedures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 25** This Interpretation shall not apply to cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and for which the parties apply for a retrial or a retrial is decided pursuant to the adjudication supervision procedure after the coming into force of this Interpretation.
