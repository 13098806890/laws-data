# 最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞和触碰案件财产损害赔偿的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1995-10-18

> 第735次会议讨论通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院 审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改 〈最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入 破产财产等问题的批复〉等二十九件商事类司法解释的决定》修正）

---

### 正文

一、请求人可以请求赔偿对船舶碰撞或者触碰所造成的财产损失，船舶碰撞或者触碰后相继发生的有关费用和损失，为避免或者减少损害而产生的合理费用和损失，以及预期可得利益的损失。  
因请求人的过错造成的损失或者使损失扩大的部分，不予赔偿。

**Article None** 1. A claimant may claim compensation for property damage caused by collision or striking of a ship, relevant expenses and losses arising consecutively after the collision or striking, reasonable expenses and losses incurred to avoid or mitigate damage, and loss of anticipated profits.  
Losses caused by the claimant's fault or the portion of losses that the claimant's fault enlarged shall not be compensated.

二、赔偿应当尽量达到恢复原状，不能恢复原状的折价赔偿。

**Article None** 2. Compensation shall, to the extent possible, achieve restoration to the original condition. Where restoration to the original condition is impossible, compensation shall be made at a depreciated value.

三、船舶损害赔偿分为全损赔偿和部分损害赔偿。  
（一）船舶全损的赔偿包括：  
船舶价值损失；  
未包括在船舶价值内的船舶上的燃料、物料、备件、供应品，渔船上的捕捞设备、网具、渔具等损失；  
船员工资、遣返费及其他合理费用。  
（二）船舶部分损害的赔偿包括：合理的船舶临时修理费、永久修理费及辅助费用、维持费用，但应满足下列条件：  
船舶应就近修理，除非请求人能证明在其他地方修理更能减少损失和节省费用，或者有其他合理的理由。如果船舶经临时修理可继续营运，请求人有责任进行临时修理；  
船舶碰撞部位的修理，同请求人为保证船舶适航，或者因另外事故所进行的修理，或者与船舶例行的检修一起进行时，赔偿仅限于修理本次船舶碰撞的受损部位所需的费用和损失。  
（三）船舶损害赔偿还包括：  
合理的救助费，沉船的勘查、打捞和清除费用，设置沉船标志费用；  
拖航费用，本航次的租金或者运费损失，共同海损分摊；  
合理的船期损失；  
其他合理的费用。

**Article None** 3. Compensation for ship damage is divided into compensation for total loss and compensation for partial damage.  
(1) Compensation for total loss of a ship includes:  
Loss of ship value;  
Loss of fuel, stores, spare parts, supplies on board the ship not included in the ship value, and loss of fishing equipment, nets, fishing gear, etc. on fishing vessels;  
Crew wages, repatriation expenses, and other reasonable expenses.  
(2) Compensation for partial damage to a ship includes: reasonable temporary repair costs, permanent repair costs, auxiliary expenses, and maintenance expenses of the ship, provided that the following conditions are satisfied:  
The ship shall be repaired nearby, unless the claimant can prove that repair elsewhere can better reduce losses and save expenses, or there are other reasonable grounds. Where the ship can continue operation after temporary repair, the claimant has a duty to carry out temporary repair;  
When the repair of the collision-damaged part is carried out together with repairs to ensure the ship's seaworthiness, or repairs due to another accident, or the ship's routine overhaul, compensation is limited to the costs and losses required to repair the part damaged in this collision.  
(3) Compensation for ship damage also includes:  
Reasonable salvage expenses, expenses for survey, refloating and removal of the sunken ship, expenses for marking the sunken ship;  
Towage expenses, loss of charter hire or freight for this voyage, general average contribution;  
Reasonable loss of time;  
Other reasonable expenses.

四、船上财产的损害赔偿包括：  
船上财产的灭失或者部分损坏引起的贬值损失；  
合理的修复或者处理费用；  
合理的财产救助、打捞和清除费用，共同海损分摊；  
其他合理费用。

**Article None** 4. Compensation for damage to property on board a ship includes:  
Loss due to depreciation caused by the destruction or partial damage of property on board;  
Reasonable repair or disposal costs;  
Reasonable property salvage, refloating and removal expenses, general average contribution;  
Other reasonable expenses.

五、船舶触碰造成设施损害的赔偿包括：  
设施的全损或者部分损坏修复费用；  
设施修复前不能正常使用所产生的合理的收益损失。

**Article None** 5. Compensation for damage to a structure caused by the striking of a ship includes:  
Repair costs for total loss or partial damage of the structure;  
Reasonable loss of income arising from the structure's inability to be used normally before repair.

六、船舶碰撞或者触碰造成第三人财产损失的，应予赔偿。

**Article None** 6. Where a ship collision or striking causes property loss to a third party, compensation shall be made.

七、除赔偿本金外，利息损失也应赔偿。

**Article None** 7. In addition to compensation of the principal, loss of interest shall also be compensated.

八、船舶价值损失的计算，以船舶碰撞发生地当时类似船舶的市价确定；碰撞发生地无类似船舶市价的，以船舶船籍港类似船舶的市价确定，或者以其他地区类似船舶市价的平均价确定；没有市价的，以原船舶的造价或者购置价，扣除折旧（折旧率按年4-10%）计算；折旧后没有价值的按残值计算。  
船舶被打捞后尚有残值的，船舶价值应扣除残值。

**Article None** 8. The calculation of loss of ship value shall be determined by the market price of a similar ship at the place where the collision occurred at the time of the collision. If there is no market price for a similar ship at the place of collision, the market price of a similar ship at the ship's port of registry shall be used, or the average market price of similar ships in other areas shall be used. If there is no market price, the original construction cost or purchase price, less depreciation (depreciation rate at 4%-10% per annum), shall be used for calculation. If no value remains after depreciation, the residual value shall be used for calculation.  
Where the ship still has residual value after being refloated, the ship value shall be reduced by the residual value.

九、船上财产损失的计算：  
（一）货物灭失的，按照货物的实际价值，即以货物装船时的价值加运费加请求人已支付的货物保险费计算，扣除可节省的费用；  
（二）货物损坏的，以修复所需的费用，或者以货物的实际价值扣除残值和可节省的费用计算；  
（三）由于船舶碰撞在约定的时间内迟延交付所产生的损失，按迟延交付货物的实际价值加预期可得利润与到岸时的市价的差价计算，但预期可得利润不得超过货物实际价值的10%；  
（四）船上捕捞的鱼货，以实际的鱼货价值计算。鱼货价值参照海事发生时当地市价，扣除可节省的费用。  
（五）船上渔具、网具的种类和数量，以本次出海捕捞作业所需量扣减现存量计算，但所需量超过渔政部门规定或者许可的种类和数量的，不予认定；渔具、网具的价值，按原购置价或者原造价扣除折旧费用和残值计算；  
（六）旅客行李、物品（包括自带行李）的损失，属本船旅客的损失，依照海商法的规定处理；属他船旅客的损失，可参照旅客运输合同中有关旅客行李灭失或者损坏的赔偿规定处理；  
（七）船员个人生活必需品的损失，按实际损失适当予以赔偿；  
（八）承运人与旅客书面约定由承运人保管的货币、金银、珠宝、有价证券或者其他贵重物品的损失，依海商法的规定处理；船员、旅客、其他人员个人携带的货币、金银、珠宝、有价证券或者其他贵重物品的损失，不予认定；  
（九）船上其他财产的损失，按其实际价值计算。

**Article None** 9. Calculation of loss of property on board:  
(1) Where cargo is destroyed, the calculation shall be based on the actual value of the cargo, i.e., the value of the cargo at the time of loading plus freight plus cargo insurance premiums paid by the claimant, less expenses that may be saved;  
(2) Where cargo is damaged, the calculation shall be based on the cost of repair, or on the actual value of the cargo less residual value and expenses that may be saved;  
(3) Losses caused by delayed delivery within the agreed time due to the collision shall be calculated according to the difference between the actual value of the delayed cargo plus anticipated profits and the market price at the time of arrival, provided that the anticipated profits shall not exceed 10% of the actual value of the cargo;  
(4) The value of fish caught on board shall be calculated based on the actual value of the fish, with reference to the local market price at the time of the maritime incident, less expenses that may be saved;  
(5) The type and quantity of fishing gear and nets on board shall be calculated based on the quantity required for this fishing trip less the existing stock. However, where the required quantity exceeds the type and quantity prescribed or permitted by the fishery administration department, it shall not be recognized. The value of fishing gear and nets shall be calculated based on the original purchase price or construction cost less depreciation expenses and residual value;  
(6) Loss of passengers' luggage and articles (including accompanied luggage): for losses of the ship's own passengers, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Maritime Code; for losses of passengers from other ships, the matter may be handled with reference to the compensation provisions regarding the destruction or damage of passengers' luggage in the passenger transport contract;  
(7) Loss of crew members' personal necessities shall be appropriately compensated based on the actual loss;  
(8) Loss of currency, gold, silver, jewelry, negotiable securities, or other valuables that the carrier has agreed in writing with passengers to keep in custody shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Maritime Code; loss of currency, gold, silver, jewelry, negotiable securities, or other valuables carried personally by crew members, passengers, or other persons shall not be recognized;  
(9) Loss of other property on board shall be calculated based on its actual value.

十、船期损失的计算：  
期限：船舶全损的，以找到替代船所需的合理期间为限，但最长不得超过两个月；船舶部分损害的修船期限，以实际修复所需的合理期间为限，其中包括联系、住坞、验船等所需的合理时间；渔业船舶，按上述期限扣除休渔期为限，或者以一个渔汛期为限。  
船期损失，一般以船舶碰撞前后各两个航次的平均净盈利计算；无前后各两个航次可参照的，以其他相应航次的平均净盈利计算。  
渔船渔汛损失，以该渔船前3年的同期渔汛平均净收益计算，或者以本年内同期同类渔船的平均净收益计算。计算渔汛损失时，应当考虑到碰 撞渔船在对船捕渔作业或者围网灯光捕渔作业中的作用等因素。

**Article None** 10. Calculation of loss of time:  
Period: In the case of total loss of the ship, the period shall be limited to the reasonable time needed to find a substitute ship, but shall not exceed two months at most. In the case of partial damage to the ship, the repair period shall be limited to the reasonable time actually needed for repair, including reasonable time for contacting, docking, surveying, etc. For fishing vessels, the period shall be the above period less the fishing moratorium period, or limited to one fishing season.  
Loss of time shall generally be calculated based on the average net profit of the two voyages before and two voyages after the collision. Where no reference can be made to the two voyages before and two voyages after, the average net profit of other corresponding voyages shall be used.  
The loss of a fishing season for a fishing vessel shall be calculated based on the average net income of the same fishing season in the previous three years of that vessel, or based on the average net income of similar vessels of the same season in the current year. When calculating the loss of a fishing season, factors such as the role of the collided vessel in pair trawling or purse seine light fishing operations shall be taken into account.

十一、租金或者运费损失的计算：  
碰撞导致期租合同承租人停租或者不付租金的，以停租或者不付租金额，扣除可节省的费用计算。  
因货物灭失或者损坏导致到付运费损失的，以尚未收取的运费金额扣除可节省的费用计算。

**Article None** 11. Calculation of loss of charter hire or freight:  
Where the collision causes the charterer under a time charter to suspend payment or not to pay hire, the calculation shall be based on the amount of suspended or unpaid hire, less expenses that may be saved.  
Where loss of freight to be collected on delivery is caused by destruction or damage of cargo, the calculation shall be based on the amount of freight not yet collected, less expenses that may be saved.

十二、设施损害赔偿的计算：  
期限：以实际停止使用期间扣除常规检修的期间为限；  
设施部分损坏或者全损，分别以合理的修复费用或者重新建造的费用，扣除已使用年限的折旧费计算；  
设施使用的收益损失，以实际减少的净收益，即按停止使用前3个月的平均净盈利计算；部分使用并有收益的，应当扣减。

**Article None** 12. Calculation of compensation for damage to structures:  
Period: The period shall be limited to the actual period of disuse less the period of routine maintenance;  
For partial damage or total loss of a structure, the calculation shall be based on reasonable repair costs or reconstruction costs, less depreciation for the years of use, respectively;  
Loss of income from the use of the structure shall be calculated based on the actual net income reduced, i.e., the average net profit for the three months prior to the cessation of use; where partial use with income continues, the amount shall be deducted.

十三、利息损失的计算：  
船舶价值的损失利息，从船期损失停止计算之日起至判决或者调解指定的应付之日止；  
其他各项损失的利息，从损失发生之日或者费用产生之日起计算至判决或调解指定的应付之日止；  
利息按本金性质的同期利率计算。

**Article None** 13. Calculation of loss of interest:  
Interest on loss of ship value shall be calculated from the date on which the calculation of loss of time ceases to the date of payment specified in the judgment or mediation;  
Interest on other losses shall be calculated from the date on which the loss occurs or the expense arises to the date of payment specified in the judgment or mediation;  
Interest shall be calculated at the contemporaneous interest rate applicable to the nature of the principal.

十四、计算损害赔偿的货币，当事人有约定的，依约定；没有约定的，按以下相关的货币计算：  
按船舶营运或者生产经营所使用的货币计算；  
船载进、出口货物的价值，按买卖合同或者提单、运单记明的货币计算；  
以特别提款权计算损失的，按法院判决或者调解之日的兑换率换算成相应的货币。

**Article None** 14. The currency for calculating damages and compensation: if the parties have agreed on it, the agreement shall prevail; if there is no agreement, the calculation shall be made in the following relevant currencies:  
In the currency used in the ship's operation or production and business;  
The value of imported or exported cargo on board shall be calculated in the currency specified in the sales contract or the bill of lading or waybill;  
Where losses are calculated in Special Drawing Rights, the amount shall be converted into the corresponding currency at the exchange rate on the date of the court judgment or mediation.

十五、本规定不包括对船舶碰撞或者触碰责任的确定，不影响船舶所有人或者承运人依法享受免责和责任限制的权利。

**Article None** 15. These Provisions do not include the determination of liability for collision or striking of a ship, and shall not affect the right of the shipowner or carrier to enjoy immunity from liability and limitation of liability according to law.

十六、本规定中下列用语的含义：  
“船舶”是指所有用作或者能够用作水上运输工具的各类水上船筏，包括非排水船舶和水上飞机。但是用于军事的和政府公务的船舶除外。  
“设施”是指人为设置的固定或者可移动的构造物，包括固定平台、浮鼓、码头、堤坝、桥梁、敷设或者架设的电缆、管道等。  
“船舶碰撞”是指在海上或者与海相通的可航水域，两艘或者两艘以上的船舶之间发生接触或者没有直接接触，造成财产损害的事故。  
“船舶触碰”是指船舶与设施或者障碍物发生接触并造成财产损害的事故。  
“船舶全损”是指船舶实际全部损失，或者损坏已达到相当严重的程度，以至于救助、打捞、修理费等费用之和达到或者超过碰撞或者触碰发生前的船舶价值。  
“辅助费用”是指为进行修理而产生的合理费用，包括必要的进坞费、清航除气费、排放油污水处理费、港口使费、引航费、检验费以及修船期间所产生的住坞费、码头费等费用，但不限于上述费用。  
“维持费用”是指船舶修理期间，船舶和船员日常消耗的费用，包括燃料、物料、淡水及供应品的消耗和船员工资等。

**Article None** 16. The meanings of the following terms in these Provisions:  
"Ship" means all types of waterborne vessels that are used or can be used as means of transportation on water, including non-displacement craft and seaplanes, except for ships used for military or governmental public service.  
"Structure" means a fixed or movable construction set up artificially, including fixed platforms, buoys, docks, dams, bridges, laid or erected cables, pipelines, etc.  
"Collision of ships" means an accident between two or more ships, with or without direct contact, occurring on the sea or other navigable waters connected to the sea, causing property damage.  
"Striking of a ship" means an accident in which a ship comes into contact with a structure or obstacle, causing property damage.  
"Total loss of a ship" means the actual total loss of the ship, or damage so severe that the sum of salvage, refloating, repair, and other expenses equals or exceeds the ship's value immediately before the collision or striking.  
"Auxiliary expenses" means reasonable expenses incurred for repair, including necessary costs for docking, cleaning and gas-freeing, discharging oily water treatment, port charges, pilotage fees, survey fees, and docking and wharf fees during repair, but not limited to the above.  
"Maintenance expenses" means the daily consumption costs of the ship and crew during repair, including consumption of fuel, stores, fresh water, supplies, and crew wages.

十七、本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article None** 17. These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
