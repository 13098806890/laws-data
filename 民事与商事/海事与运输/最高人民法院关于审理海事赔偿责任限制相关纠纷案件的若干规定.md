# 最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕11号  
**公布日期**：2010-08-27  
**生效日期**：2010-09-15

> 最高人民法院《关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》已于2010年3月22日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1484次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年9月15日起施行。 2010年8月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理海事赔偿责任限制 相关纠纷案件的若干规定 法释〔2010〕11号 （2010年3月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1484次会议通过　2010年8月27日最高人民法院公告公布　自2010年9月15日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件，适用海事诉讼特别程序法、海商法的规定；海事诉讼特别程序法、海商法没有规定的，适用其他相关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 1** In the adjudication of cases involving disputes relating to limitation of liability for maritime claims, the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law and the Maritime Code shall apply; matters not provided for in the Special Maritime Procedure Law and the Maritime Code shall be governed by other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　同一海事事故中，不同的责任人在起诉前依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-102)的规定向不同的海事法院申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，后立案的海事法院应当依照民事诉讼法的规定，将案件移送先立案的海事法院管辖。

**Article 2** In the same maritime accident, if different persons liable, before prosecution, apply to different maritime courts for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims pursuant to Article 102 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law, the maritime court that subsequently dockets the case shall, in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law, transfer the case to the maritime court that first docketed the case for jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　责任人在诉讼中申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，应当向受理相关海事纠纷案件的海事法院提出。  
相关海事纠纷由不同海事法院受理，责任人申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，应当依据诉讼管辖协议向最先立案的海事法院提出；当事人之间未订立诉讼管辖协议的，向最先立案的海事法院提出。

**Article 3** If a person liable applies during litigation for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims, the application shall be made to the maritime court that accepts the relevant maritime dispute case.  
If the relevant maritime dispute is accepted by different maritime courts, and the person liable applies for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims, the application shall be made pursuant to the litigation jurisdiction agreement to the maritime court that first docketed the case; if no litigation jurisdiction agreement is concluded between the parties, the application shall be made to the maritime court that first docketed the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海事赔偿责任限制基金设立后，设立基金的海事法院对海事请求人就与海事事故相关纠纷向责任人提起的诉讼具有管辖权。  
海事请求人向其他海事法院提起诉讼的，受理案件的海事法院应当依照民事诉讼法的规定，将案件移送设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的海事法院，但当事人之间订有诉讼管辖协议的除外。

**Article 4** After a limitation fund for maritime claims is established, the maritime court that established the fund shall have jurisdiction over any action brought by a maritime claimant against the person liable concerning a dispute relating to the maritime accident.  
If a maritime claimant brings an action in another maritime court, the maritime court accepting the case shall, in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law, transfer the case to the maritime court that established the limitation fund for maritime claims, unless the parties have concluded a litigation jurisdiction agreement.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-106)第二款规定的海事法院在十五日内作出裁定的期间，自海事法院受理设立海事赔偿责任限制基金申请的最后一次公告发布之次日起第三十日开始计算。

**Article 5** The period for the maritime court to make a ruling within fifteen days as provided in Article 106, paragraph 2 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law shall be calculated from the thirtieth day after the day following the date of publication of the last announcement of the maritime court's acceptance of the application for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-112)规定的申请债权登记期间的届满之日，为海事法院受理设立海事赔偿责任限制基金申请的最后一次公告发布之次日起第六十日。

**Article 6** The expiry date of the period for applying for registration of claims as provided in Article 112 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law shall be the sixtieth day after the day following the date of publication of the last announcement of the maritime court's acceptance of the application for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　债权人申请登记债权，符合有关规定的，海事法院应当在海事赔偿责任限制基金设立后，依照[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-114)的规定作出裁定；海事赔偿责任限制基金未依法设立的，海事法院应当裁定终结债权登记程序。债权人已经交纳的申请费由申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的人负担。

**Article 7** If a creditor applies for registration of a claim and meets the relevant requirements, the maritime court shall, after the limitation fund for maritime claims is established, make a ruling in accordance with Article 114 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law; if the limitation fund for maritime claims is not established according to law, the maritime court shall rule to terminate the claim registration procedure. The application fee already paid by the creditor shall be borne by the person who applied for establishment of the limitation fund for maritime claims.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海事赔偿责任限制基金设立后，海事请求人基于责任人依法不能援引海事赔偿责任限制抗辩的海事赔偿请求，可以对责任人的财产申请保全。

**Article 8** After a limitation fund for maritime claims is established, a maritime claimant may, based on a maritime claim for compensation for which the person liable cannot invoke the defense of limitation of liability for maritime claims according to law, apply for preservation of the property of the person liable.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　海事赔偿责任限制基金设立后，海事请求人就同一海事事故产生的属于[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)规定的可以限制赔偿责任的海事赔偿请求，以行使船舶优先权为由申请扣押船舶的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 9** After a limitation fund for maritime claims is established, if a maritime claimant applies for arrest of a ship on the ground of exercising maritime liens in respect of a maritime claim for compensation arising from the same maritime accident that falls under the category of claims subject to limitation of liability as provided in Article 207 of the Maritime Code, the People's Court shall not support such application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　债权人提起确权诉讼时，依据[海商法第二百零九条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-209)的规定主张责任人无权限制赔偿责任的，应当以书面形式提出。案件的审理不适用海事诉讼特别程序法规定的确权诉讼程序，当事人对海事法院作出的判决、裁定可以依法提起上诉。  
两个以上债权人主张责任人无权限制赔偿责任的，海事法院可以将相关案件合并审理。

**Article 10** When a creditor institutes an action for confirmation of rights, if the creditor claims that the person liable is not entitled to limit liability in accordance with Article 209 of the Maritime Code, the claim shall be made in writing. The trial of the case shall not apply the procedure for action for confirmation of rights as provided in the Special Maritime Procedure Law, and the parties may appeal against the judgment or ruling made by the maritime court according to law.  
If two or more creditors claim that the person liable is not entitled to limit liability, the maritime court may consolidate the relevant cases for trial.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　债权人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-116)第一款的规定提起确权诉讼后，需要判定碰撞船舶过失程度比例的，案件的审理不适用海事诉讼特别程序法规定的确权诉讼程序，当事人对海事法院作出的判决、裁定可以依法提起上诉。

**Article 11** After a creditor institutes an action for confirmation of rights in accordance with Article 116, paragraph 1 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law, if it is necessary to determine the proportion of fault of the colliding ships, the trial of the case shall not apply the procedure for action for confirmation of rights as provided in the Special Maritime Procedure Law, and the parties may appeal against the judgment or ruling made by the maritime court according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　[海商法第二百零四条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-204)规定的船舶经营人是指登记的船舶经营人，或者接受船舶所有人委托实际使用和控制船舶并应当承担船舶责任的人，但不包括无船承运业务经营者。

**Article 12** The ship operator as provided in Article 204 of the Maritime Code refers to the registered ship operator, or a person who, entrusted by the shipowner, actually uses and controls the ship and shall bear the liability of the ship, but does not include a non-vessel operating common carrier.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　责任人未申请设立海事赔偿责任限制基金，不影响其在诉讼中对[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)规定的海事请求提出海事赔偿责任限制抗辩。

**Article 13** The failure of the person liable to apply for establishment of a limitation fund for maritime claims shall not affect the person's right to raise a defense of limitation of liability for maritime claims in litigation against a maritime claim as provided in Article 207 of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　责任人未提出海事赔偿责任限制抗辩的，海事法院不应主动适用海商法关于海事赔偿责任限制的规定进行裁判。

**Article 14** If the person liable does not raise a defense of limitation of liability for maritime claims, the maritime court shall not apply the provisions of the Maritime Code on limitation of liability for maritime claims on its own initiative in making an adjudication.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　责任人在一审判决作出前未提出海事赔偿责任限制抗辩，在二审、再审期间提出的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 15** If the person liable fails to raise a defense of limitation of liability for maritime claims before the first-instance judgment is rendered, but raises it during the second-instance or retrial proceedings, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　责任人对[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)规定的海事赔偿请求未提出海事赔偿责任限制抗辩，债权人依据有关生效裁判文书或者仲裁裁决书，申请执行责任人海事赔偿责任限制基金以外的财产的，人民法院应予支持，但债权人以上述文书作为债权证据申请登记债权并经海事法院裁定准予的除外。

**Article 16** If the person liable does not raise a defense of limitation of liability for maritime claims in respect of a maritime claim for compensation as provided in Article 207 of the Maritime Code, and a creditor applies for enforcement against the property of the person liable other than the limitation fund for maritime claims on the basis of a relevant effective judgment document or arbitration award, the People's Court shall support it, except where the creditor uses the aforesaid documents as evidence of the claim to apply for registration of the claim and the maritime court has ruled to approve it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)规定的可以限制赔偿责任的海事赔偿请求不包括因沉没、遇难、搁浅或者被弃船舶的起浮、清除、拆毁或者使之无害提起的索赔，或者因船上货物的清除、拆毁或者使之无害提起的索赔。  
由于船舶碰撞致使责任人遭受前款规定的索赔，责任人就因此产生的损失向对方船舶追偿时，被请求人主张依据[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)的规定限制赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** The maritime claims for compensation subject to limitation of liability as provided in Article 207 of the Maritime Code do not include claims raised in respect of the raising, removal, destruction, or rendering harmless of a ship which is sunk, wrecked, stranded, or abandoned, or claims raised in respect of the removal, destruction, or rendering harmless of cargo on board the ship.  
If a person liable suffers a claim as provided in the preceding paragraph as a result of a collision between ships, and the person liable seeks recourse against the other ship for the losses thus incurred, and the person claimed against claims limitation of liability in accordance with Article 207 of the Maritime Code, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[海商法第二百零九条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-209)规定的“责任人”是指海事事故的责任人本人。

**Article 18** The "person liable" as provided in Article 209 of the Maritime Code refers to the person liable for the maritime accident himself.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海事请求人以发生海事事故的船舶不适航为由主张责任人无权限制赔偿责任，但不能证明引起赔偿请求的损失是由于责任人本人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 19** If a maritime claimant claims that the person liable is not entitled to limit liability on the ground that the ship involved in the maritime accident is unseaworthy, but fails to prove that the loss giving rise to the compensation claim was caused by the intentional act or recklessness of the person liable with knowledge that such loss would probably result, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海事赔偿责任限制基金应当以人民币设立，其数额按法院准予设立基金的裁定生效之日的特别提款权对人民币的换算办法计算。

**Article 20** A limitation fund for maritime claims shall be established in Renminbi, and its amount shall be calculated according to the conversion method of Special Drawing Rights to Renminbi on the date when the court's ruling approving the establishment of the fund takes effect.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[海商法第二百一十三条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-213)规定的利息，自海事事故发生之日起至基金设立之日止，按中国人民银行确定的金融机构同期一年期贷款基准利率计算。  
以担保方式设立海事赔偿责任限制基金的，基金设立期间的利息按中国人民银行确定的金融机构同期一年期贷款基准利率计算。

**Article 21** The interest as provided in Article 213 of the Maritime Code shall be calculated from the date of the maritime accident to the date of establishment of the fund, at the one-year benchmark lending rate for financial institutions determined by the People's Bank of China for the corresponding period.  
If a limitation fund for maritime claims is established by means of a guarantee, the interest during the period of establishment of the fund shall be calculated at the one-year benchmark lending rate for financial institutions determined by the People's Bank of China for the corresponding period.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定施行前已经终审的案件，人民法院进行再审时，不适用本规定。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall not apply to cases for which a final judgment has been rendered before these Provisions come into force, when the People's Court conducts a retrial.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定施行前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 23** If any judicial interpretation issued by this Court before these Provisions come into force is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
