# 最高人民法院关于审理铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （2010年1月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1482次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定》修正） 为正确审理铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件，依法维护各方当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国铁路法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，就有关适用法律问题作如下解释： 第一条　人民法院审理铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成的铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件，适用本解释。 与铁路运输企业建立劳动合同关系或者形成劳动关系的铁路职工在执行职务中发生的人身损害，依照有关调整劳动关系的法律规定及其他相关法律规定处理。 第二条　铁路运输人身损害的受害人、依法由受害人承担扶养义务的被扶养人以及死亡受害人的近亲属为赔偿权利人，有权请求赔偿。 第三条　赔偿权利人要求对方当事人承担侵权责任的，由事故发生地、列车最先到达地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖；赔偿权利人依照民法典第三编要求承运人承担违约责任予以人身损害赔偿的，由运输始发地、目的地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖。 第四条　铁路运输造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；法律另有规定的，依照其规定。 第五条　铁路运输中发生人身损害，铁路运输企业举证证明有下列情形之一的，不承担赔偿责任： （一）不可抗力造成的； （二）受害人故意以卧轨、碰撞等方式造成的。 第六条　因受害人翻越、穿越、损毁、移动铁路线路两侧防护围墙、栅栏或者其他防护设施穿越铁路线路，偷乘货车，攀附行进中的列车，在未设置人行通道的铁路桥梁、隧道内通行，攀爬高架铁路线路，以及其他未经许可进入铁路线路、车站、货场等铁路作业区域的过错行为，造成人身损害的，应当根据受害人的过错程度适当减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，并按照以下情形分别处理： （一）铁路运输企业未充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人有上述过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损失的百分之八十至百分之二十之间承担赔偿责任； （二）铁路运输企业已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人仍施以上述过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损失的百分之二十至百分之十之间承担赔偿责任。 第七条　受害人横向穿越未封闭的铁路线路时存在过错，造成人身损害的，按照前条规定处理。 受害人不听从值守人员劝阻或者无视禁行警示信号、标志硬行通过铁路平交道口、人行过道，或者沿铁路线路纵向行走，或者在铁路线路上坐卧，造成人身损害，铁路运输企业举证证明已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务的，不承担赔偿责任。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理铁路行车事故及其他铁路运营事故造成的铁路运输人身损害赔偿纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
与铁路运输企业建立劳动合同关系或者形成劳动关系的铁路职工在执行职务中发生的人身损害，依照有关调整劳动关系的法律规定及其他相关法律规定处理。

**Article 1** This Interpretation shall apply to the People's Court when adjudicating disputes over compensation for personal injury caused by railway traffic accidents and other railway operation accidents in railway transport.  
Personal injury suffered by a railway employee who has established a labor contract relationship or formed a labor relationship with a railway transport enterprise in the course of performing duties shall be handled in accordance with the legal provisions regulating labor relationships and other relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　铁路运输人身损害的受害人、依法由受害人承担扶养义务的被扶养人以及死亡受害人的近亲属为赔偿权利人，有权请求赔偿。

**Article 2** The victim of personal injury in railway transport, the person to whom the victim has a statutory obligation to provide support, and the close relatives of a deceased victim shall be the compensation right holders and shall have the right to claim compensation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿权利人要求对方当事人承担侵权责任的，由事故发生地、列车最先到达地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖；赔偿权利人依照民法典第三编要求承运人承担违约责任予以人身损害赔偿的，由运输始发地、目的地或者被告住所地铁路运输法院管辖。

**Article 3** Where a compensation right holder requests the opposing party to assume tort liability, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the railway transport court of the place where the accident occurred, the first place the train arrived, or the defendant's domicile; where a compensation right holder requests the carrier to assume liability for breach of contract and pay compensation for personal injury in accordance with Book Three of the Civil Code, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the railway transport court of the place of departure, destination, or the defendant's domicile.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　铁路运输造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；法律另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 4** Where railway transport causes personal injury, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the law provides otherwise, the provisions thereof shall apply.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　铁路运输中发生人身损害，铁路运输企业举证证明有下列情形之一的，不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不可抗力造成的；  
（二）受害人故意以卧轨、碰撞等方式造成的。

**Article 5** Where personal injury occurs in railway transport, if the railway transport enterprise adduces evidence to prove any of the following circumstances, it shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) Caused by force majeure;  
(2) Intentionally caused by the victim by means such as lying on the tracks or collision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　因受害人翻越、穿越、损毁、移动铁路线路两侧防护围墙、栅栏或者其他防护设施穿越铁路线路，偷乘货车，攀附行进中的列车，在未设置人行通道的铁路桥梁、隧道内通行，攀爬高架铁路线路，以及其他未经许可进入铁路线路、车站、货场等铁路作业区域的过错行为，造成人身损害的，应当根据受害人的过错程度适当减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，并按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）铁路运输企业未充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人有上述过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损失的百分之八十至百分之二十之间承担赔偿责任；  
（二）铁路运输企业已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务，受害人仍施以上述过错行为的，铁路运输企业应当在全部损失的百分之二十至百分之十之间承担赔偿责任。

**Article 6** Where personal injury is caused by the victim's fault acts such as climbing over, crossing, damaging, or moving protective walls, fences, or other protective facilities on both sides of the railway line to cross the railway line, secretly riding on a freight train, clinging to a moving train, passing through railway bridges or tunnels without pedestrian walkways, climbing elevated railway lines, or other unauthorized entry into railway operation areas such as railway lines, stations, or freight yards, the railway transport enterprise's compensation liability shall be appropriately mitigated according to the degree of the victim's fault, and shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the railway transport enterprise has not fully performed its obligations of safety protection and warning, and the victim has committed the above fault act, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability between 80% and 20% of the total loss;  
(2) If the railway transport enterprise has fully performed its obligations of safety protection and warning, and the victim still commits the above fault act, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability between 20% and 10% of the total loss.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　受害人横向穿越未封闭的铁路线路时存在过错，造成人身损害的，按照前条规定处理。  
受害人不听从值守人员劝阻或者无视禁行警示信号、标志硬行通过铁路平交道口、人行过道，或者沿铁路线路纵向行走，或者在铁路线路上坐卧，造成人身损害，铁路运输企业举证证明已充分履行安全防护、警示等义务的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 7** Where the victim cross an unenclosed railway line laterally with fault, causing personal injury, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding article.  
If the victim, without heeding the dissuasion of the attendant or disregarding warning signals or signs prohibiting passage, forcibly crosses a railway level crossing or pedestrian crossing, or walks longitudinally along the railway line, or sits or lies on the railway line, causing personal injury, and the railway transport enterprise adduces evidence proving that it has fully performed its obligations of safety protection and warning, it shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　铁路运输造成无民事行为能力人人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；监护人有过错的，按照过错程度减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，但铁路运输企业承担的赔偿责任应当不低于全部损失的百分之五十。  
铁路运输造成限制民事行为能力人人身损害的，铁路运输企业应当承担赔偿责任；监护人及受害人自身有过错的，按照过错程度减轻铁路运输企业的赔偿责任，但铁路运输企业承担的赔偿责任应当不低于全部损失的百分之四十。

**Article 8** Where railway transport causes personal injury to a person with no capacity for civil conduct, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the guardian is at fault, the railway transport enterprise's compensation liability shall be mitigated according to the degree of fault, but the compensation liability borne by the railway transport enterprise shall not be less than 50% of the total loss.  
Where railway transport causes personal injury to a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability; if the guardian and the victim himself are at fault, the railway transport enterprise's compensation liability shall be mitigated according to the degree of fault, but the compensation liability borne by the railway transport enterprise shall not be less than 40% of the total loss.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　铁路机车车辆与机动车发生碰撞造成机动车驾驶人员以外的人人身损害的，由铁路运输企业与机动车一方对受害人承担连带赔偿责任。铁路运输企业与机动车一方之间，按照各自的过错分担责任；双方均无过错的，按照公平原则分担责任。对受害人实际承担赔偿责任超出应当承担份额的一方，有权向另一方追偿。  
铁路机车车辆与机动车发生碰撞造成机动车驾驶人员人身损害的，按照[本解释第四条](#art-4)至第七条的规定处理。

**Article 9** Where a collision between a railway locomotive or vehicle and a motor vehicle causes personal injury to a person other than the motor vehicle driver, the railway transport enterprise and the motor vehicle party shall bear joint and several compensation liability to the victim. Between the railway transport enterprise and the motor vehicle party, they shall apportion liability according to their respective faults; if neither party is at fault, they shall apportion liability according to the principle of fairness. A party that has actually borne compensation liability to the victim in excess of its share shall have the right to seek recourse from the other party.  
Where a collision between a railway locomotive or vehicle and a motor vehicle causes personal injury to the motor vehicle driver, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Articles 4 to 7 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在非铁路运输企业实行监护的铁路无人看守道口发生事故造成人身损害的，由铁路运输企业按照本解释的有关规定承担赔偿责任。道口管理单位有过错的，铁路运输企业对赔偿权利人承担赔偿责任后，有权向道口管理单位追偿。

**Article 10** Where an accident occurs at an unattended railway crossing not under the guardianship of a railway transport enterprise, causing personal injury, the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation. If the crossing management unit is at fault, after the railway transport enterprise has borne compensation liability to the compensation right holder, it shall have the right to seek recourse from the crossing management unit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于铁路桥梁、涵洞等设施负有管理、维护等职责的单位，因未尽职责使该铁路桥梁、涵洞等设施不能正常使用，导致行人、车辆穿越铁路线路造成人身损害的，铁路运输企业按照本解释有关规定承担赔偿责任后，有权向该单位追偿。

**Article 11** Where a unit responsible for the management, maintenance, and other duties of railway bridges, culverts, and other facilities fails to perform its duties, rendering such railway bridges, culverts, and other facilities unable to function normally, thereby causing pedestrians or vehicles to cross the railway line and resulting in personal injury, after the railway transport enterprise has borne compensation liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation, it shall have the right to seek recourse from that unit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　铁路旅客运送期间发生旅客人身损害，赔偿权利人要求铁路运输企业承担违约责任的，人民法院应当依照[民法典第八百一十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-811)、第八百二十二条、第八百二十三条等规定，确定铁路运输企业是否承担责任及责任的大小；赔偿权利人要求铁路运输企业承担侵权赔偿责任的，人民法院应当依照有关侵权责任的法律规定，确定铁路运输企业是否承担赔偿责任及责任的大小。

**Article 12** Where personal injury to a passenger occurs during railway passenger transport, if the compensation right holder requests the railway transport enterprise to assume liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall determine whether the railway transport enterprise shall bear liability and the extent thereof in accordance with Articles 811, 822, and 823 of the Civil Code, among other provisions; if the compensation right holder requests the railway transport enterprise to assume tort compensation liability, the People's Court shall determine whether the railway transport enterprise shall bear compensation liability and the extent thereof in accordance with the legal provisions on tort liability.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　铁路旅客运送期间因第三人侵权造成旅客人身损害的，由实施侵权行为的第三人承担赔偿责任。铁路运输企业有过错的，应当在能够防止或者制止损害的范围内承担相应的补充赔偿责任。铁路运输企业承担赔偿责任后，有权向第三人追偿。  
车外第三人投掷石块等击打列车造成车内旅客人身损害，赔偿权利人要求铁路运输企业先予赔偿的，人民法院应当予以支持。铁路运输企业赔付后，有权向第三人追偿。

**Article 13** Where personal injury to a passenger is caused by a third party's tort during railway passenger transport, the third party who committed the tort shall bear compensation liability. If the railway transport enterprise is at fault, it shall bear corresponding supplementary compensation liability within the scope in which it could have prevented or stopped the damage. After bearing compensation liability, the railway transport enterprise shall have the right to seek recourse from the third party.  
Where a third party outside the train throws stones or other objects to strike the train, causing personal injury to a passenger inside the train, and the compensation right holder requests the railway transport enterprise to make compensation first, the People's Court shall support such request. After paying compensation, the railway transport enterprise shall have the right to seek recourse from the third party.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　有权作出事故认定的组织依照《铁路交通事故应急救援和调查处理条例》等有关规定制作的事故认定书，经庭审质证，对于事故认定书所认定的事实，当事人没有相反证据和理由足以推翻的，人民法院应当作为认定事实的根据。

**Article 14** The accident determination document produced by an organization authorized to make accident determinations in accordance with the relevant provisions such as the Regulations on Emergency Rescue, Investigation and Handling of Railway Traffic Accidents shall, after being cross-examined in court, be taken by the People's Court as the basis for determining the facts if the parties have no sufficient contrary evidence and reasons to overturn the facts determined in the accident determination document.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在专用铁路及铁路专用线上因运输造成人身损害，依法应当由肇事工具或者设备的所有人、使用人或者管理人承担赔偿责任的，适用本解释。

**Article 15** Where personal injury is caused by transport on a special railway or a dedicated railway line, and according to law the owner, user, or manager of the causative tool or equipment shall bear compensation liability, this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。  
本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 16** Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.  
Cases that have been finally adjudicated before this Interpretation comes into force, and for which the parties apply for retrial after this Interpretation comes into force or which are decided to be retried according to the trial supervision procedure, shall not be governed by this Interpretation.
