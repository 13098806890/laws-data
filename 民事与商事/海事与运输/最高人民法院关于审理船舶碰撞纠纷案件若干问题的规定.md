# 最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕7号  
**公布日期**：2008-05-19  
**生效日期**：2008-05-23

> 最高人民法院《关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2008年4月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1446次会议通过。现予公布，自2008年5月23日起施行。 2008年5月19日 最高人民法院 关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2008〕7号 （2008年4月28日最高人民法院审判委员会第1446次会议通过　2008年5月19日最高人民法院公告公布　自2008年5月23日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称船舶碰撞，是指[海商法第一百六十五条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-165)所指的船舶碰撞，不包括内河船舶之间的碰撞。  
[海商法第一百七十条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-170)所指的损害事故，适用本规定。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, a collision of vessels shall refer to the collision of vessels as referred to in Article 165 of the Maritime Code, excluding collisions between inland river vessels.  
The damage accidents as referred to in Article 170 of the Maritime Code shall be governed by these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件，依照海商法第八章的规定确定碰撞船舶的赔偿责任。

**Article 2** When hearing a case of dispute over a collision of vessels, the compensation liability of the colliding vessels shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VIII of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因船舶碰撞导致船舶触碰引起的侵权纠纷，依照海商法第八章的规定确定碰撞船舶的赔偿责任。  
非因船舶碰撞导致船舶触碰引起的侵权纠纷，依照民法通则的规定确定触碰船舶的赔偿责任，但不影响海商法第八章之外其他规定的适用。

**Article 3** Where an infringement dispute arises from a vessel's contact caused by a collision of vessels, the compensation liability of the colliding vessels shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VIII of the Maritime Code.  
Where an infringement dispute arises from a vessel's contact not caused by a collision of vessels, the compensation liability of the contacting vessel shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law, provided that the application of other provisions beyond Chapter VIII of the Maritime Code shall not be affected.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶碰撞产生的赔偿责任由船舶所有人承担，碰撞船舶在光船租赁期间并经依法登记的，由光船承租人承担。

**Article 4** The compensation liability arising from a collision of vessels shall be borne by the owner of the vessel; where the colliding vessel is under bareboat charter and has been registered according to law, such liability shall be borne by the bareboat charterer.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　因船舶碰撞发生的船上人员的人身伤亡属于[海商法第一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-169)第三款规定的第三人的人身伤亡。

**Article 5** Personal injuries or deaths of persons onboard arising from a collision of vessels shall fall within the category of personal injuries or deaths of third parties as provided in Article 169(3) of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　碰撞船舶互有过失造成船载货物损失，船载货物的权利人对承运货物的本船提起违约赔偿之诉，或者对碰撞船舶一方或者双方提起侵权赔偿之诉的，人民法院应当依法予以受理。

**Article 6** Where a collision vessel's mutual fault causes loss of cargo on board, and the cargo claimant institutes an action for breach of contract for compensation against the vessel carrying the cargo, or an action for tortious compensation against one or both of the colliding vessels, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船载货物的权利人因船舶碰撞造成其货物损失向承运货物的本船提起诉讼的，承运船舶可以依照[海商法第一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-169)第二款的规定主张按照过失程度的比例承担赔偿责任。  
前款规定不影响承运人和实际承运人援用海商法第四章关于承运人抗辩理由和限制赔偿责任的规定。

**Article 7** Where the cargo claimant, due to loss of cargo caused by a collision of vessels, institutes an action against the vessel carrying the cargo, the carrying vessel may claim to bear compensation liability in proportion to the degree of fault in accordance with the provisions of Article 169(2) of the Maritime Code.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not affect the carrier and the actual carrier's right to invoke the provisions of Chapter IV of the Maritime Code concerning the carrier's grounds for defense and limitation of compensation liability.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　碰撞船舶船载货物权利人或者第三人向碰撞船舶一方或者双方就货物或其他财产损失提出赔偿请求的，由碰撞船舶方提供证据证明过失程度的比例。无正当理由拒不提供证据的，由碰撞船舶一方承担全部赔偿责任或者由双方承担连带赔偿责任。  
前款规定的证据指具有法律效力的判决书、裁定书、调解书和仲裁裁决书。对于碰撞船舶提交的国外的判决书、裁定书、调解书和仲裁裁决书，依照[民事诉讼法第二百六十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-266)和第二百六十七条规定的程序审查。

**Article 8** Where a cargo claimant of a colliding vessel or a third party makes a claim for compensation against one or both of the colliding vessels for loss of cargo or other property, the colliding vessel shall provide evidence to prove the proportion of the degree of fault. Where the colliding vessel refuses to provide evidence without justifiable reasons, one colliding vessel shall bear full compensation liability, or both vessels shall bear joint and several compensation liability.  
The evidence as referred to in the preceding paragraph shall mean judgments, rulings, mediation documents, and arbitration awards that have legal effect. Where the colliding vessel submits foreign judgments, rulings, mediation documents, and arbitration awards, such documents shall be reviewed in accordance with the procedures provided in Articles 266 and 267 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　因起浮、清除、拆毁由船舶碰撞造成的沉没、遇难、搁浅或被弃船舶及船上货物或者使其无害的费用提出的赔偿请求，责任人不能依照海商法第十一章的规定享受海事赔偿责任限制。

**Article 9** Where a claim for compensation is made for expenses incurred in raising, removing, destroying, or rendering harmless a vessel that is sunk, wrecked, stranded, or abandoned, or cargo on board, as a result of a collision of vessels, the person liable shall not be entitled to limit his maritime liability in accordance with the provisions of Chapter XI of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件时，人民法院根据当事人的申请进行证据保全取得的或者向有关部门调查收集的证据，应当在当事人完成举证并出具完成举证说明书后出示。

**Article 10** When hearing a case of dispute over a collision of vessels, the evidence obtained by the People's Court through preservation of evidence upon application by a party or through investigation and collection from relevant departments shall be presented after the parties have completed the submission of evidence and issued a certificate of completion of evidence submission.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶碰撞事故发生后，主管机关依法进行调查取得并经过事故当事人和有关人员确认的碰撞事实调查材料，可以作为人民法院认定案件事实的证据，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 11** After a collision accident occurs, the investigation materials on the collision facts obtained by the competent authority through investigation according to law and confirmed by the parties and relevant persons of the accident may serve as evidence for the People's Court to determine the facts of the case, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to overturn them.
