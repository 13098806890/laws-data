### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了调整海上运输关系、船舶关系，保护当事人各方的合法权益，加强海洋生态环境保护，促进海上运输和经济贸易高质量发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating maritime transport relations and ship relations, protecting the lawful rights and interests of all parties, strengthening marine ecological and environmental protection, and promoting high-quality development of maritime transport and economic trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称海上运输，是指海上货物运输和海上旅客运输，包括海江之间、江海之间的直达运输。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "maritime transport" means the carriage of goods and passengers by sea, including direct transport between sea and inland waters or between inland waters and sea.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称船舶，是指海船和其他海上移动式装置，但是用于军事的、政府公务的船舶和20总吨以下的小型船艇除外。船舶包括船舶属具。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "ship" means seagoing ships and other mobile offshore units, except those used for military or government public service purposes, and small vessels of less than 20 gross tons. Ships include their appurtenances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶经依法登记取得中华人民共和国国籍，有权悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行。  
船舶未依法悬挂中华人民共和国国旗或者违法悬挂其他国家、地区或者组织的旗帜航行的，由有关机关责令改正，并依法追究法律责任。

**Article 4** A ship that has obtained the nationality of the People's Republic of China upon registration according to law shall be entitled to fly the national flag of the People's Republic of China while navigating.  
If a ship navigates without flying the national flag of the People's Republic of China according to law, or illegally flies the flag of any other country, region or organization, the relevant authority shall order it to make correction and shall pursue legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国港口之间的海上运输和拖航，由依法取得中华人民共和国国籍的船舶经营。但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
非经国务院交通运输主管部门依法批准，外国籍船舶不得经营中华人民共和国港口之间的海上运输和拖航。

**Article 5** Maritime transport and towage between ports of the People's Republic of China shall be operated by ships that have obtained the nationality of the People's Republic of China according to law, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
No foreign-flag ship may operate maritime transport or towage between ports of the People's Republic of China without the approval of the competent transport department of the State Council according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院交通运输主管部门、有关地方人民政府及其交通运输主管部门依法对海上运输活动实施监督管理。

**Article 6** The competent transport department of the State Council, the relevant local people's governments and their competent transport departments shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over maritime transport activities.

### 第二章　船舶

#### 第一节　船舶所有权

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船舶所有权，是指船舶所有人依法对其船舶享有占有、使用、收益和处分的权利。

**Article 7** Ownership of a ship means the right of the shipowner to possess, use, benefit from and dispose of the ship according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶所有权的设立、变更、转让和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。  
船舶融资租赁合同出租人对融资租赁船舶享有的所有权，未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。承租人应当按照船舶融资租赁合同支付租金。承租人经催告后在合理期限内仍不支付租金的，出租人可以请求支付全部租金；也可以解除船舶融资租赁合同并收回融资租赁船舶。  
船舶所有权的转让，应当签订书面合同。

**Article 8** The creation, modification, transfer and extinction of ownership of a ship shall be registered with the ship registration authority; without registration, it shall not be set up against a bona fide third party.  
The ownership of a ship under a ship financial lease contract enjoyed by the lessor over the ship leased under financial lease, without registration, shall not be set up against a bona fide third party. The lessee shall pay the rent in accordance with the ship financial lease contract. If the lessee fails to pay the rent within a reasonable period after being urged, the lessor may request payment of all the rent, or may dissolve the ship financial lease contract and take back the ship under financial lease.  
The transfer of ownership of a ship shall be made by a written contract.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船舶由两个以上的法人、非法人组织或者自然人共有的，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。  
船舶权利人、利害关系人、有关国家机关可以依法查询船舶所有权的登记状况。

**Article 9** Where a ship is owned by two or more legal persons, unincorporated organizations or natural persons in common, the co-ownership shall be registered with the ship registration authority; without registration, it shall not be set up against a bona fide third party.  
The person entitled to the ship, interested parties and relevant state organs may, according to law, inquire into the registered status of the ownership of the ship.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建造中的船舶的所有权，当事人对其归属有约定的，按照约定；没有约定或者约定不明确的，归造船人所有。

**Article 10** Where the parties have an agreement on the ownership of a ship under construction, such agreement shall prevail; in the absence of such agreement or if the agreement is unclear, the ownership shall belong to the shipbuilder.

#### 第二节　船舶抵押权

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶抵押权，是指抵押权人对于抵押人提供的作为债务担保的船舶，在债务人不履行到期债务或者发生当事人约定的实现抵押权情形时，依法享有的就该船舶优先受偿的权利。

**Article 11** A ship mortgage means the right enjoyed by the mortgagee, according to law, to be paid in priority out of the ship provided by the mortgagor as a security for the debt, in case the debtor fails to perform the due debt or the circumstances for realizing the mortgage agreed upon by the parties occur.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　船舶所有人或者船舶所有人授权的人可以设立船舶抵押权。  
船舶抵押权的设立，应当签订书面合同。

**Article 12** The shipowner or a person authorized by the shipowner may create a ship mortgage.  
The creation of a ship mortgage shall be made by a written contract.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　船舶抵押权的设立、变更、转让和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记，不得对抗善意第三人。  
船舶抵押权登记，由抵押权人和抵押人共同向船舶登记机关办理。  
船舶抵押权登记，包括下列主要项目：  
（一）船舶抵押权人和抵押人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（二）抵押船舶的名称、国籍、船舶所有权证书的颁发机关和证书号码；  
（三）所担保的债权数额、受偿期限；  
（四）抵押权登记的时间。  
船舶抵押权的登记状况，允许公众查询。

**Article 13** The creation, modification, transfer and extinction of a ship mortgage shall be registered with the ship registration authority; without registration, it shall not be set up against a bona fide third party.  
The registration of a ship mortgage shall be made jointly by the mortgagee and the mortgagor with the ship registration authority.  
The registration of a ship mortgage shall include the following main items:  
(1) the name or title and domicile of the mortgagee and the mortgagor;  
(2) the name, nationality of the mortgaged ship, the authority that issued the ship ownership certificate and the certificate number;  
(3) the amount of the secured claim and the term for repayment;  
(4) the time of registration of the mortgage.  
The registered status of a ship mortgage shall be open to public inquiry.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建造中的船舶可以设立船舶抵押权。  
建造中的船舶办理抵押权登记，应当依照有关规定向船舶登记机关提交有关文件。

**Article 14** A ship under construction may be mortgaged.  
To register a mortgage on a ship under construction, relevant documents shall be submitted to the ship registration authority in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　除合同另有约定外，抵押人应当对抵押船舶进行保险；未保险的，抵押权人有权对该船舶进行保险，保险费由抵押人负担。

**Article 15** Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the mortgagor shall insure the mortgaged ship; if the ship is not insured, the mortgagee shall have the right to insure the ship, and the insurance premium shall be borne by the mortgagor.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　除船舶共有人另有约定外，以共有船舶设立抵押权的，按照下列情形处理：  
（一）船舶按份共有的，应当取得持有三分之二以上份额的共有人的同意；  
（二）船舶共同共有的，应当取得全体共有人的同意。  
船舶共有人设立的抵押权，不因船舶共有权的分割而受影响。

**Article 16** Unless otherwise agreed by the co-owners of the ship, where a mortgage is created on a ship owned in common, the following rules shall apply:  
(1) if the ship is owned in shares, the consent of co-owners holding more than two-thirds of the shares shall be obtained;  
(2) if the ship is owned jointly, the consent of all co-owners shall be obtained.  
A mortgage created by the co-owners of a ship shall not be affected by the partition of the co-ownership of the ship.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　船舶抵押权设立后，抵押人可以转让抵押船舶。当事人另有约定的，按照其约定。抵押船舶转让的，船舶抵押权不受影响。

**Article 17** After a ship mortgage is created, the mortgagor may transfer the mortgaged ship. If the parties have otherwise agreed, such agreement shall prevail. The transfer of the mortgaged ship shall not affect the ship mortgage.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　船舶抵押担保的债权转让的，抵押权一并转让，但是法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 18** Where the claim secured by a ship mortgage is transferred, the mortgage shall be transferred together therewith, unless otherwise provided by law or agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　同一船舶设立两个以上船舶抵押权的，其清偿顺序按照以下情形确定：  
（一）船舶抵押权已经登记的，按照登记的时间先后顺序受偿；  
（二）船舶抵押权已经登记的先于未登记的受偿；  
（三）船舶抵押权未登记的，按照担保的债权比例受偿。

**Article 19** Where two or more ship mortgages are created on the same ship, the order of satisfaction shall be determined as follows:  
(1) where the ship mortgages have been registered, they shall be satisfied in the order of the time of registration;  
(2) a registered ship mortgage shall be satisfied prior to an unregistered one;  
(3) where the ship mortgages have not been registered, they shall be satisfied in proportion to the amount of the secured claims.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　抵押船舶毁损、灭失或者被征收的，抵押权人可以就获得的保险金、赔偿金、补偿金等优先受偿。

**Article 20** Where the mortgaged ship is damaged, lost or expropriated, the mortgagee may be satisfied in priority out of the insurance proceeds, compensation or indemnity received.

#### 第三节　船舶优先权

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　船舶优先权，是指海事请求人依照本法规定，向船舶所有人、光船承租人、船舶管理人或者船舶经营人提出海事请求，对产生该海事请求的船舶具有优先受偿的权利。

**Article 21** A maritime lien means the right of the maritime claimant, according to the provisions of this Law, to be paid in priority out of the ship which gave rise to the maritime claim, when the claim is made against the shipowner, bareboat charterer, ship manager or ship operator.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　下列各项海事请求具有船舶优先权：  
（一）船长、船员和其他船上在岗人员因在船上工作产生的工资、其他劳动报酬、船员遣返费用、社会保险费用的给付请求；  
（二）在船舶营运中发生的人身伤亡的赔偿请求；  
（三）船舶吨税、引航费和其他港口规费的缴付请求；  
（四）海难救助的救助款项的给付请求；  
（五）在船舶营运中因侵权行为产生的财产赔偿请求，但是不包括本船所载的货物、集装箱和旅客行李的灭失或者损坏的赔偿请求。  
根据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约或者法律、行政法规的强制性规定，已经取得民事责任保险或者具有相应的财务保证的，在该保险或者财务保证的范围内，前款第二项、第五项规定的海事请求不具有船舶优先权。

**Article 22** The following maritime claims shall be secured by a maritime lien:  
(1) claims for wages, other labor remuneration, crew repatriation expenses and social insurance costs arising from the work on board the ship by the master, crew members and other on-duty personnel on the ship;  
(2) claims for compensation for personal injury or death occurring in the operation of the ship;  
(3) claims for payment of ship tonnage dues, pilotage dues and other port charges;  
(4) claims for payment of salvage remuneration for salvage of the ship;  
(5) claims for compensation for property loss or damage caused by tort in the operation of the ship, excluding claims for loss or damage of cargo, containers and passengers' luggage carried on board the ship.  
Where, in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China or the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, civil liability insurance covering such claims has been obtained or corresponding financial security has been provided, the maritime claims specified in paragraph (2) and (5) of the preceding paragraph shall not be secured by a maritime lien to the extent covered by such insurance or financial security.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款所列各项海事请求，按照顺序受偿。但是，第四项海事请求，后于第一项至第三项发生的，应当先于第一项至第三项受偿。  
[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款第一项、第二项、第三项、第五项中有两个以上海事请求的，不分先后，同时受偿，不足受偿的，按照比例受偿；第四项中有两个以上海事请求的，后发生的先受偿。

**Article 23** The maritime claims listed in the first paragraph of Article 22 of this Law shall be satisfied in order. However, if the maritime claim under item (4) arises after those under items (1) to (3), it shall be satisfied prior to those under items (1) to (3).  
Where there are two or more maritime claims under items (1), (2), (3) and (5) of the first paragraph of Article 22 of this Law, they shall be satisfied concurrently without priority, and if insufficient, in proportion; where there are two or more maritime claims under item (4), the later-arising claim shall be satisfied prior to the earlier-arising one.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因行使船舶优先权产生的诉讼费用，保存、拍卖或者变卖船舶和分配船舶价款产生的费用，以及为海事请求人的共同利益而支付的其他费用，应当从船舶拍卖或者变卖所得价款中先行拨付。

**Article 24** The litigation costs arising from the enforcement of a maritime lien, the costs for preserving, auctioning or selling the ship and for distributing the proceeds of the ship, and other expenses paid for the common interest of the maritime claimants shall be first deducted from the proceeds of the auction or sale of the ship.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　船舶优先权不因船舶所有权的转让而消灭。但是，船舶转让时，船舶优先权自法院应受让人申请予以公告之日起满六十日不行使的除外。

**Article 25** A maritime lien shall not be extinguished by the transfer of the ownership of the ship. However, if, upon the transfer of the ship, the maritime lien is not enforced within sixty days from the date on which the court, upon the application of the transferee, publishes a public notice, it shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定的具有船舶优先权的海事请求发生转让或者代位的，该项船舶优先权随之转移。

**Article 26** Where a maritime claim secured by a maritime lien as provided in Article 22 of this Law is transferred or subrogated, the maritime lien shall be transferred together therewith.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　船舶优先权应当通过法院扣押产生优先权的船舶行使。

**Article 27** A maritime lien shall be enforced by the court arresting the vessel to which the lien attaches.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　除[本法第二十五条](#art-25)规定的情形外，船舶优先权因下列原因之一而消灭：  
（一）自产生之日起满一年不行使；  
（二）船舶被法院依法拍卖或者变卖；  
（三）船舶灭失。  
前款第一项规定的一年期限不得中止或者中断。  
[本法第二十二条](#art-22)第一款第一项规定的海事请求具有的船舶优先权的一年期限，自海事请求人从其任职的船舶上离船之日起算。

**Article 28** Except for the circumstances provided in Article 25 of this Law, a maritime lien shall be extinguished due to any of the following reasons:  
(1) it has not been exercised within one year from the date of its creation;  
(2) the vessel has been auctioned or sold by the court according to law;  
(3) the vessel is lost.  
The one-year period provided in paragraph 1, item (1) shall not be suspended or interrupted.  
The one-year period for the maritime lien of maritime claims under paragraph 1, item (1) of Article 22 of this Law shall be calculated from the date when the maritime claimant disembarks from the vessel on which he is employed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本节规定不影响本法第十一章关于海事赔偿责任限制规定的实施。

**Article 29** The provisions of this Section shall not affect the implementation of the provisions of Chapter XI of this Law concerning limitation of liability for maritime claims.

#### 第四节　船舶留置权

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　船舶留置权，是指造船人、修船人在合同另一方未按照约定支付船舶建造或者修理费用时，留置已经合法占有的船舶，并就该船舶优先受偿的权利。  
船舶留置权在造船人、修船人不再占有建造或者修理的船舶时消灭。  
船舶留置权先于船舶抵押权受偿，后于船舶优先权受偿。

**Article 30** A lien on a vessel means the right of a shipbuilder or ship repairer to retain the vessel that it has lawfully taken possession of when the other party to the contract fails to pay the cost of construction or repair as agreed, and to be compensated from the vessel in priority.  
The lien on a vessel shall be extinguished when the shipbuilder or ship repairer ceases to possess the vessel under construction or repair.  
A lien on a vessel shall be satisfied before a mortgage on the vessel and after a maritime lien.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　除另有约定外，造船人、修船人留置船舶满六十日后，债务人仍不履行债务的，可以与债务人协议以留置船舶折价，也可以就拍卖、变卖留置船舶所得的价款优先受偿。

**Article 31** Unless otherwise agreed, if after the shipbuilder or ship repairer has retained the vessel for sixty days the debtor still fails to perform the obligation, the shipbuilder or ship repairer may agree with the debtor to offset the vessel retained against the debt, or may be compensated in priority from the proceeds of the auction or sale of the vessel retained.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在留置期间，船舶被法院扣押并拍卖、变卖的，不影响船舶留置权人优先受偿的权利。

**Article 32** If during the period of retention, the vessel is arrested and auctioned or sold by the court, such action shall not affect the right of the lien holder to be compensated in priority.

### 第三章　船员

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　船员，是指包括船长在内的船上一切任职人员。

**Article 33** A seafarer means any person employed on board a vessel, including the master.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　中国籍船员应当依照有关船员管理的法律、行政法规的规定取得相应的船员适任证书和健康证明。  
外国籍船员在中国籍船舶上工作的，按照有关船员管理的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 34** A Chinese seafarer shall obtain the corresponding seafarer's certificate of competency and health certificate in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on seafarer management.  
For a foreign seafarer working on a Chinese-flagged vessel, the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on seafarer management shall apply.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从事国际航行船舶的中国籍船员，应当依法持有中华人民共和国海事管理机构颁发的海员证和有关证书。

**Article 35** A Chinese seafarer engaged on a vessel engaged in international navigation shall hold a seafarer's passport and relevant certificates issued by the maritime administration authority of the People's Republic of China according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　船员用人单位应当依照有关法律、行政法规和中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关船员劳动与社会保障国际条约的规定，与船员订立劳动合同。  
船员的任用和船员劳动与社会保障方面的权利义务，本法没有规定的，适用有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 36** The crew's employer shall conclude a labor contract with the seafarer in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and international treaties on seafarer labor and social security concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.  
For the employment of seafarers and their rights and obligations in respect of labor and social security, matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

#### 第二节　船长

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　船长负责管理和指挥船舶。  
船长在其职权范围内发布的命令，船员、旅客和其他在船人员应当执行。  
船长应当采取必要的措施，保护船舶和在船人员、文件、邮件、货物以及其他财产，防治船舶污染生态环境。

**Article 37** The master shall be responsible for the management and navigation of the vessel.  
Orders issued by the master within the scope of his authority shall be executed by the seafarers, passengers, and other persons on board.  
The master shall take necessary measures to protect the vessel and the persons, documents, mail, cargo, and other property on board, and to prevent and control pollution of the ecological environment by the vessel.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　为保障在船人员和船舶的安全，船长有权对在船上涉嫌进行违法犯罪活动的人员采取禁闭或者其他必要措施，并防止其隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据。  
船长采取前款措施，应当制作案情报告书，由船长和两名以上在船人员签字，连同涉嫌违法犯罪活动的人员送交有关机关处理。

**Article 38** To ensure the safety of persons and the vessel on board, the master shall have the right to take confinement or other necessary measures against any person suspected of engaging in illegal or criminal activities on board, and to prevent them from concealing, destroying, or forging evidence.  
When taking measures as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the master shall prepare a case report, which shall be signed by the master and at least two other persons on board, and shall be submitted together with the person suspected of illegal or criminal activities to the relevant authorities for handling.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　船长应当将船上发生的出生或者死亡事件记入航海日志，并在两名证人的参加下制作证明书。死亡证明书应当附有死者遗物清单。死者有遗嘱的，船长应当予以证明。死亡证明书和遗嘱由船长负责保管，并送交家属或者有关方面。

**Article 39** The master shall record in the logbook any birth or death occurring on board, and shall prepare a certificate in the presence of two witnesses. The death certificate shall be accompanied by a list of the deceased's effects. If the deceased left a will, the master shall certify it. The death certificate and the will shall be kept by the master and delivered to the family or the relevant authorities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　船舶发生海上事故，危及在船人员和财产安全时，船长应当组织船员和其他在船人员尽力施救。在船舶沉没、毁灭不可避免的情况下，船长有权作出弃船决定。  
弃船时，船长应当采取一切措施，首先组织旅客安全离船，然后安排船员离船，船长应当最后离船。在离船前，船长应当指挥船员尽力抢救航海日志、轮机日志、油类记录簿、无线电记录簿、本航次使用过的海图和文件，以及贵重物品、邮件和现金，指挥船员关闭油舱阀门等设备以防止或者减少污染发生。

**Article 40** When a maritime accident occurs to a vessel endangering the safety of persons and property on board, the master shall organize the seafarers and other persons on board to do their best in rescue. Where the sinking or destruction of the vessel is inevitable, the master shall have the right to make a decision to abandon the vessel.  
When abandoning the vessel, the master shall take all measures, first to organize the safe disembarkation of passengers, then to arrange the disembarkation of seafarers, and the master shall be the last to leave. Before leaving the vessel, the master shall direct the seafarers to do their utmost to salvage the deck logbook, engine logbook, oil record book, radio log, charts and documents used during the current voyage, as well as valuables, mail, and cash, and shall direct the seafarers to close oil tank valves and other equipment to prevent or minimize pollution.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　船长管理船舶和指挥船舶的责任，不因引航员引领船舶而解除。

**Article 41** The responsibility of the master for the management and navigation of the vessel shall not be relieved by the presence of a pilot on board.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　船长在航行中死亡或者因故不能执行职务时，应当由驾驶员中职务最高的人代理船长职务；在下一个港口开航前，船舶所有人、船舶经营人或者船舶管理人应当指派新船长接任。

**Article 42** If the master dies during a voyage or is unable to perform his duties for any reason, the duty officer with the highest rank among the deck officers shall act as master; before the vessel departs from the next port, the shipowner, ship operator, or ship manager shall appoint a new master.

### 第四章　海上货物运输合同

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　海上货物运输合同，是指承运人收取运费，负责将托运人托运的货物经海路由一港运至另一港的合同，包括国际海上货物运输合同和中华人民共和国港口之间的国内海上货物运输合同。

**Article 43** A contract of carriage of goods by sea means a contract under which the carrier, against payment of freight, undertakes to carry the goods consigned by the shipper by sea from one port to another, including contracts of international carriage of goods by sea and contracts of domestic carriage of goods by sea between ports of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本章下列用语的含义：  
（一）承运人，是指本人或者委托他人以本人名义与托运人订立海上货物运输合同的人。  
（二）实际承运人，是指接受承运人委托或者转委托，实际履行[本法第四十九条](#art-49)规定的全部或者部分承运人义务的人。  
（三）托运人，是指：  
1.本人或者委托他人以本人名义或者委托他人为本人与承运人订立海上货物运输合同的人。  
2.本人或者委托他人以本人名义或者委托他人为本人将货物交给与海上货物运输合同有关的承运人的人。  
（四）收货人，是指有权提取货物的人。  
（五）货物，包括活动物和由托运人提供的用于集装货物的集装箱、货盘、车辆或者类似的装运器具。  
（六）运输单证，是指提单、海运单以及其他证明海上货物运输合同和货物已经由承运人接收或者装船的单证。  
（七）提单，是指用以证明海上货物运输合同和货物已经由承运人接收或者装船，以及承运人保证据以交付货物的单证。

**Article 44** For the purposes of this Chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) 'Carrier' means a person who concludes a contract of carriage of goods by sea with a shipper, either by himself or by another person acting on his behalf.  
(2) 'Actual carrier' means a person to whom the performance of all or part of the carrier's obligations as provided in Article 49 of this Law has been entrusted by the carrier or by a sub-entrustment, and who actually performs such obligations.  
(3) 'Shipper' means:  
1. a person who concludes a contract of carriage of goods by sea with a carrier, either by himself or by another person acting on his behalf or for him;  
2. a person who delivers goods to the carrier in connection with a contract of carriage of goods by sea, either by himself or by another person acting on his behalf or for him.  
(4) 'Consignee' means a person entitled to take delivery of the goods.  
(5) 'Goods' includes live animals and containers, pallets, vehicles, or similar articles of transport supplied by the shipper for the purpose of consolidating goods.  
(6) 'Transport document' means a bill of lading, sea waybill, or other document evidencing a contract of carriage of goods by sea and that the goods have been received or loaded by the carrier.  
(7) 'Bill of lading' means a document used to evidence a contract of carriage of goods by sea and that the goods have been received or loaded by the carrier, and under which the carrier undertakes to deliver the goods against surrender of the document.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本章规定的海上货物运输合同和作为合同证明的提单或者其他运输单证中的条款，以及将货物的保险利益转让给承运人的条款或者类似条款无效。此类条款的无效，不影响该合同和提单或者其他运输单证中其他条款的效力。

**Article 45** Any clause in a contract of carriage of goods by sea, or in a bill of lading or other transport document evidencing such contract, that violates the provisions of this Chapter, and any clause assigning the insurance interest in the goods to the carrier or any similar clause, shall be null and void. The nullity of such clauses shall not affect the validity of other clauses in the contract, bill of lading, or other transport document.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　[本法第四十五条](#art-45)的规定不影响承运人在本章规定的承运人责任和义务之外，增加其责任和义务。

**Article 46** The provisions of Article 45 of this Law shall not affect the carrier's ability to assume additional responsibilities and obligations beyond those provided in this Chapter.

#### 第二节　承运人的责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　承运人对集装箱装运的货物的责任期间，是指从装货港接收货物时起至卸货港交付货物时止，货物处于承运人掌管之下的全部期间。承运人对非集装箱装运的货物的责任期间，是指从货物装上船时起至卸下船时止，货物处于承运人掌管之下的全部期间。在承运人的责任期间，货物发生灭失或者损坏，除本节另有规定外，承运人应当承担赔偿责任。  
前款规定，不影响承运人就非集装箱装运的货物在装船前和卸船后所承担的责任达成任何协议。

**Article 47** The period of responsibility of the carrier for the goods carried in containers covers the entire period during which the carrier is in charge of the goods from the time when the carrier receives the goods at the port of loading until the time when the goods are delivered at the port of discharge. The period of responsibility of the carrier for the goods carried in non-containerized covers the entire period during which the carrier is in charge of the goods from the time when the goods are loaded on board until the time when they are discharged from the ship. During the period of responsibility of the carrier, if the goods are lost or damaged, the carrier shall bear compensation liability unless otherwise provided for in this Section.  
The provisions in the preceding paragraph shall not affect any agreement reached by the carrier on the responsibility for the goods carried in non-containerized before loading and after discharge.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　承运人在船舶开航前和开航当时，应当谨慎处理，使船舶处于适航状态，妥善配备船员、装备船舶和配备供应品，并使货舱、船舶其他载货处所以及承运人提供的载货集装箱适于并能安全收受、载运和保管货物。  
国内海上货物运输的承运人，还应当在航程中尽到前款义务。

**Article 48** The carrier shall exercise due diligence before and at the beginning of the voyage to make the ship seaworthy, properly man, equip and supply the ship, and to make the holds, all other parts of the ship in which goods are carried and the containers provided by the carrier fit and safe for the reception, carriage and preservation of the goods.  
The carrier in domestic maritime cargo transport shall also perform the obligations in the preceding paragraph during the voyage.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　承运人应当妥善、谨慎地接收、装载、搬移、积载、运输、保管、照料、卸载和交付所运货物。

**Article 49** The carrier shall properly and carefully receive, load, stow, carry, keep, care for, discharge and deliver the goods carried.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　承运人应当按照约定的或者习惯的或者地理上的航线，将货物运往卸货港。  
船舶在海上或者与海相通的可航水域为救助或者企图救助人命或者财产而发生的绕航或者其他合理绕航，不属于违反前款规定的行为。

**Article 50** The carrier shall carry the goods to the port of discharge on the route agreed upon, or the customary or the geographical route.  
Any deviation for the purpose of saving or attempting to save life or property at sea or in any navigable waters communicating with the sea, or any other reasonable deviation shall not be deemed as an act violating the provisions in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　货物未能在明确约定的期限内，在约定的卸货港交付的，构成迟延交付；国内海上货物运输中，货物未能在合理期限内交付的，也构成迟延交付。  
除依照本章规定承运人不承担赔偿责任的情形外，由于承运人的过错，致使货物因迟延交付而灭失、损坏或者遭受其他经济损失的，承运人应当承担赔偿责任。  
承运人未能在本条第一款规定的期限届满六十日内交付货物，有权对货物灭失提出赔偿请求的人可以认为货物已经灭失。

**Article 51** If the goods are not delivered at the agreed port of discharge within the expressly agreed time limit, it constitutes delayed delivery. In domestic maritime cargo transport, if the goods are not delivered within a reasonable time limit, it also constitutes delayed delivery.  
Except for the circumstances where the carrier is not liable for compensation in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, if the goods are lost, damaged or suffer other economic losses due to delayed delivery caused by the fault of the carrier, the carrier shall bear compensation liability.  
If the carrier fails to deliver the goods within sixty days after the expiry of the time limit specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, the person entitled to claim for loss of the goods may consider the goods as lost.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在责任期间货物发生的灭失、损坏，或者迟延交付是由于下列原因之一造成的，承运人不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）船长、船员、引航员或者承运人的其他受雇人在驾驶船舶或者管理船舶中的过错；  
（二）船上火灾，但是由于承运人本人的过错所造成的除外；  
（三）天灾，海上或者与海相通的可航水域的危险或者意外事故；  
（四）战争或者武装冲突、海盗或者恐怖活动；  
（五）政府或者主管部门的行为、检疫限制，或者非因承运人、实际承运人或者其受雇人、代理人原因引起的司法扣押；  
（六）罢工、停工或者劳动受到限制；  
（七）在海上或者与海相通的可航水域救助、企图救助人命或者财产；  
（八）托运人、货物所有人、收货人或者他们的受雇人、代理人的行为；  
（九）货物的自然特性或者固有缺陷；  
（十）货物包装不当或者标志欠缺、不清；  
（十一）经谨慎处理仍不能发现的船舶潜在缺陷；  
（十二）非由于承运人、实际承运人或者其受雇人、代理人的过错造成的其他原因。  
前款第一项、第二项不适用于国内海上货物运输。  
承运人依照本条第一款规定免除赔偿责任的，除第二项规定的原因外，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 52** The carrier shall not bear compensation liability if the loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods occurring during the period of responsibility is caused by any of the following causes:  
(1) Fault of the master, crew members, pilot or the other servants of the carrier in the navigation or management of the ship;  
(2) Fire on board, except where the fire is caused by the fault of the carrier himself;  
(3) Act of God, perils, dangers and accidents of the sea or any other navigable waters communicating with the sea;  
(4) War or armed conflict, piracy or terrorist activities;  
(5) Any act of the government or competent authorities, quarantine restrictions, or judicial seizure not attributable to the carrier, the actual carrier or their servants or agents;  
(6) Strikes, stoppages or restraints of labor;  
(7) Saving or attempting to save life or property at sea or in any navigable waters communicating with the sea;  
(8) Any act of the shipper, owner of the goods, consignee or their servants or agents;  
(9) The natural characteristics or inherent defects of the goods;  
(10) Improper packing or insufficiency or illegibility of marks;  
(11) Latent defects of the ship which cannot be discovered with due diligence;  
(12) Any other cause not attributable to the fault of the carrier, the actual carrier or their servants or agents.  
Items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to domestic maritime cargo transport.  
If the carrier is exempted from compensation liability in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence except for the cause specified in item (2).

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　因运输活动物固有的特殊风险造成活动物灭失、损害或者迟延交付的，承运人不承担赔偿责任。但是，承运人应当证明其已经履行托运人关于运输活动物的特别要求，并证明根据实际情况，灭失、损害或者迟延交付是由于运输活动物固有的特殊风险造成的。

**Article 53** The carrier shall not bear compensation liability for the loss, damage or delayed delivery of live animals caused by the special risks inherent in the carriage of live animals. However, the carrier shall prove that it has complied with the special instructions of the shipper with respect to the carriage of live animals, and that the loss, damage or delayed delivery is caused by the special risks inherent in the carriage of live animals under the circumstances.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　承运人在舱面上载运货物，应当与托运人达成协议，或者符合航运惯例，或者符合有关法律、行政法规的规定。  
承运人依照前款规定将货物装载在舱面上，对由于此种载运的特殊风险造成的货物灭失、损坏或者迟延交付，不承担赔偿责任。  
承运人与托运人达成协议在舱面上载运货物的，应当在提单中载明；未载明的，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 54** The carrier may carry the goods on deck only if an agreement is reached with the shipper, or if it is in accordance with the custom of the trade, or if it is in compliance with the provisions of relevant laws or administrative regulations.  
If the carrier carries the goods on deck in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, it shall not bear compensation liability for the loss, damage or delayed delivery caused by the special risks of such carriage.  
If the carrier and the shipper have reached an agreement to carry the goods on deck, the carrier shall state it in the bill of lading; failing such statement, the carrier may not invoke it against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付是由于承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人不能免除赔偿责任的原因和其他原因共同造成的，承运人仅在其不能免除赔偿责任的范围内承担赔偿责任；但是，承运人对其他原因造成的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付应当承担举证责任。

**Article 55** If the loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods is caused jointly by a cause for which the carrier or its servants or agents cannot be exempted from compensation liability and by other causes, the carrier shall bear compensation liability only to the extent of the cause for which it cannot be exempted; however, the carrier shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the loss, damage or delayed delivery caused by other causes.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　货物灭失的赔偿额，按照货物的实际价值计算；货物损坏的赔偿额，按照货物受损前后实际价值的差额或者货物的修复费用计算。  
货物的实际价值，按照在交货地交付时的市场价格计算；不能确定在交货地交付时的市场价格的，按照货物装船时的价值加保险费加运费计算。  
前款规定的货物实际价值，赔偿时应当减去因货物灭失或者损坏而少付或者免付的有关费用。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 56** The amount of compensation for the loss of the goods shall be calculated on the basis of the actual value of the goods; the amount of compensation for the damage to the goods shall be calculated on the basis of the difference between the actual value of the goods before and after the damage, or the cost of repair of the goods.  
The actual value of the goods shall be calculated on the basis of the market price at the time of delivery at the place of delivery; if the market price at the time of delivery at the place of delivery cannot be determined, it shall be calculated on the basis of the value of the goods at the time of loading plus insurance and freight.  
When compensating, the relevant expenses saved or exempted due to the loss or damage of the goods shall be deducted from the actual value of the goods as provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　承运人对货物灭失或者损坏的赔偿限额，按照货物件数或者其他货运单位数计算，每件或者每个其他货运单位为666.67计算单位，或者按照货物毛重计算，每千克为2计算单位，以其中赔偿限额较高的为准。但是，托运人在货物装运前已经申报其性质和价值并在提单中载明，或者承运人与托运人已经另行约定高于本条规定的赔偿限额的除外。  
货物用集装箱、货盘、车辆或者类似装运器具集装的，提单中载明装在此类装运器具中的货物件数或者其他货运单位数，视为前款规定的货物件数或者其他货运单位数；未载明的，每一装运器具视为一件货物或者一个其他货运单位。  
装运器具不属于承运人所有或者非由承运人提供的，装运器具本身应当视为一件货物或者一个其他货运单位。

**Article 57** The limitation of liability of the carrier for loss or damage to the goods shall be calculated on the basis of the number of packages or other shipping units, subject to a limitation of 666.67 units of account per package or other shipping unit, or 2 units of account per kilogram of the gross weight of the goods, whichever is higher. However, this shall not apply if the shipper has declared the nature and value of the goods before shipment and such declaration is inserted in the bill of lading, or if a higher limitation of liability than that provided for in this Article has been agreed upon between the carrier and the shipper.  
Where goods are consolidated in a container, pallet, vehicle or similar means of transport, the number of packages or other shipping units specified in the bill of lading as being packed in such means of transport shall be deemed to be the number of packages or other shipping units as provided in the preceding paragraph. If no such specification is made, each means of transport shall be deemed to be one package or one other shipping unit.  
If the means of transport is not owned by the carrier or not provided by the carrier, such means of transport itself shall be deemed to be one package or one other shipping unit.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　承运人对于货物因迟延交付造成经济损失的赔偿限额，为所迟延交付的货物的运费数额。货物的灭失、损坏和迟延交付同时发生的，承运人的赔偿责任限额适用[本法第五十七条](#art-57)第一款规定的限额。

**Article 58** The limitation of liability of the carrier for economic losses caused by delayed delivery of the goods shall be the amount of the freight for the goods so delayed. If the loss, damage and delayed delivery of the goods occur at the same time, the limitation of liability of the carrier shall apply the limitation provided in paragraph 1 of Article 57 of this Law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　就海上货物运输合同所涉及的货物灭失、损坏或者迟延交付通过诉讼、仲裁等方式对承运人提起的任何赔偿请求，无论海事请求人是否为合同的一方，也无论是根据合同或者侵权行为提出请求，均适用本章关于承运人的抗辩理由和限制赔偿责任的规定。  
对承运人的受雇人或者代理人提起前款赔偿请求的，承运人的受雇人或者代理人证明其行为在受雇或者受委托的范围内的，适用前款规定。

**Article 59** The provisions of this Chapter regarding the grounds for defense and limitation of liability of the carrier shall apply to any claim for compensation brought against the carrier in respect of loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods involved in the contract of carriage of goods by sea, whether by action or arbitration, and whether the maritime claimant is a party to the contract or not, and whether the claim is based on contract or tort.  
If a claim for compensation as provided in the preceding paragraph is brought against the servants or agents of the carrier, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply if such servants or agents prove that they acted within the scope of their employment or agency.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　经证明，货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付是由于承运人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，承运人不得援用[本法第五十七条](#art-57)或者第五十八条限制赔偿责任的规定。  
经证明，货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付是由于承运人的受雇人、代理人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，承运人的受雇人或者代理人不得援用[本法第五十七条](#art-57)或者第五十八条限制赔偿责任的规定。

**Article 60** If it is proved that the loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods resulted from an act or omission of the carrier done with intent to cause such loss, damage or delayed delivery, or recklessly and with knowledge that such loss, damage or delayed delivery would probably result, the carrier shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of Article 57 or 58 of this Law which limit its liability.  
If it is proved that the loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods resulted from an act or omission of the servant or agent of the carrier done with intent to cause such loss, damage or delayed delivery, or recklessly and with knowledge that such loss, damage or delayed delivery would probably result, such servant or agent of the carrier shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of Article 57 or 58 of this Law which limit its liability.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　承运人将货物运输或者部分运输委托给实际承运人履行的，承运人仍然应当依照本章规定对全部运输负责。对实际承运人承担的运输，承运人应当对实际承运人的行为或者实际承运人的受雇人、代理人在受雇或者受委托的范围内的行为负责。  
虽有前款规定，在海上货物运输合同中明确约定合同所包括的特定的部分运输由承运人以外的指定的实际承运人履行的，合同可以同时约定，货物在指定的实际承运人掌管期间发生的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付，承运人不承担赔偿责任。但是，此种约定应当在提单中载明；未载明的，不得对抗善意第三人。

**Article 61** Where the carrier entrusts the carriage or part thereof to an actual carrier, the carrier shall nevertheless be responsible for the entire carriage in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. With respect to the carriage performed by the actual carrier, the carrier shall be liable for the acts of the actual carrier or the acts of the servants or agents of the actual carrier within the scope of their employment or agency.  
Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, where a specific part of the carriage covered by the contract of carriage of goods by sea is expressly agreed to be performed by a designated actual carrier other than the carrier, the contract may also stipulate that the carrier shall not bear compensation liability for the loss, damage or delayed delivery occurring during the period when the goods are in the charge of the designated actual carrier. However, such a stipulation shall be stated in the bill of lading; failing such statement, it may not be invoked against a bona fide third party.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本章关于承运人责任的规定，适用于实际承运人。对实际承运人的受雇人、代理人提起赔偿请求的，适用[本法第五十九条](#art-59)第二款和第六十条第二款的规定。

**Article 62** The provisions of this Chapter concerning the liability of the carrier shall apply to the actual carrier. If a claim for compensation is brought against the servants or agents of the actual carrier, the provisions of Article 59, paragraph 2, and Article 60, paragraph 2 of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　承运人承担本章未规定的义务或者放弃本章赋予的权利的任何特别协议，经实际承运人书面明确同意的，对实际承运人发生效力；实际承运人是否同意，不影响此项特别协议对承运人的效力。

**Article 63** Any special agreement under which the carrier assumes obligations not provided for in this Chapter or waives rights conferred by this Chapter shall be binding on the actual carrier only if the actual carrier has expressly agreed thereto in writing. Whether the actual carrier agrees or not shall not affect the validity of such special agreement against the carrier.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　承运人与实际承运人均负有赔偿责任的，应当在此项责任范围内承担连带责任。

**Article 64** If both the carrier and the actual carrier are liable for compensation, they shall bear joint and several liability within the scope of such liability.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　就货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付分别向承运人、实际承运人以及他们的受雇人、代理人提出赔偿请求的，赔偿总额不得超过[本法第五十七条](#art-57)或者第五十八条规定的限额。

**Article 65** If claims for compensation are made separately against the carrier, the actual carrier, and their servants or agents in respect of the loss, damage or delayed delivery of the goods, the total amount of compensation shall not exceed the limitation provided in Article 57 or 58 of this Law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　[本法第六十一条](#art-61)至第六十五条的规定，不影响承运人和实际承运人之间相互追偿。

**Article 66** The provisions of Articles 61 to 65 of this Law shall not affect the right of recourse between the carrier and the actual carrier.

#### 第三节　托运人的责任

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　托运人应当按照海上货物运输合同的约定将货物交付承运人运输，并保证交付运输的货物适于约定的运输。

**Article 67** The shipper shall deliver the cargo to the carrier for carriage in accordance with the provisions of the contract of carriage of goods by sea, and shall ensure that the cargo delivered for carriage is suitable for the agreed carriage.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　托运人托运货物，应当妥善包装，并向承运人保证货物装船时所提供货物的品名、标志、包数或者件数、重量或者体积的正确性；由于包装不当或者上述资料不正确，对承运人造成损失的，托运人应当承担赔偿责任。  
承运人依照前款规定享有的受偿权利，不影响其根据海上货物运输合同对托运人以外的人所承担的责任。

**Article 68** The shipper shall properly pack the cargo when shipping it, and shall guarantee to the carrier the accuracy of the name, marks, number of packages or pieces, weight or volume of the cargo provided at the time of loading. The shipper shall bear compensation liability for any loss caused to the carrier due to improper packing or inaccuracy of the aforesaid information.  
The carrier's right to compensation under the preceding paragraph shall not affect its liability under the contract of carriage of goods by sea to persons other than the shipper.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　托运人应当及时向港口、海关和其他主管机关办理货物运输所需要的各项手续，并将已办理各项手续的单证送交承运人；因办理各项手续不及时、不完备或者不正确，使承运人的利益受到损害的，托运人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 69** The shipper shall promptly attend to all necessary formalities for the carriage of the goods with the port, customs and other competent authorities, and shall deliver to the carrier the documents evidencing completion of such formalities. If the interests of the carrier are harmed due to the shipper's failure to attend to these formalities in a timely, complete or correct manner, the shipper shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　托运人托运危险货物，应当按照有关海上危险货物运输的规定妥善包装，作出危险品标志和标签，并将货物正式名称和性质以及应当采取的预防危害措施、应急处置措施书面通知承运人；托运人未通知或者通知有误的，承运人可以在任何时间、任何地点根据情况需要将货物卸下、销毁或者消除危险性，而不承担赔偿责任。托运人对承运人因运输此类货物所受到的损害，应当承担赔偿责任。  
承运人知道危险货物的性质并已同意装运的，仍然可以在该项货物对于船舶、人员或者其他货物构成实际危险时，将货物卸下、销毁或者消除危险性，并且不承担赔偿责任。但是，本款规定不影响共同海损的分摊。

**Article 70** Where the shipper ships dangerous goods, it shall properly pack them in accordance with the relevant provisions on the carriage of dangerous goods by sea, affix danger marks and labels, and notify the carrier in writing of the proper name and nature of the goods, as well as the precautionary measures and emergency response measures to be taken. If the shipper fails to notify or notifies incorrectly, the carrier may at any time and at any place, as the circumstances require, unload, destroy or render the goods innocuous, without bearing compensation liability. The shipper shall bear compensation liability for any damage suffered by the carrier as a result of the carriage of such goods.  
If the carrier is aware of the nature of the dangerous goods and has consented to their shipment, it may nevertheless unload, destroy or render the goods innocuous at any time without bearing compensation liability when such goods constitute an actual danger to the ship, persons or other goods. However, the provisions of this paragraph shall not affect the adjustment of general average.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　托运人应当按照约定向承运人支付运费。  
托运人与承运人可以约定运费由收货人支付；但是，此项约定应当在运输单证中载明。

**Article 71** The shipper shall pay the freight to the carrier as agreed.  
The shipper and the carrier may agree that the freight shall be paid by the consignee; however, such agreement shall be stated in the transport document.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　托运人对承运人、实际承运人所遭受的损失或者船舶所遭受的损坏，不承担赔偿责任；但是，此种损失或者损坏是由于托运人或者托运人的受雇人、代理人的过错造成的除外。  
托运人的受雇人、代理人对承运人、实际承运人所遭受的损失或者船舶所遭受的损坏，不承担赔偿责任；但是，此种损失或者损坏是由于托运人的受雇人、代理人的过错造成的除外。

**Article 72** The shipper shall not bear compensation liability for losses suffered by the carrier or the actual carrier or for damage to the ship, unless such loss or damage is caused by the fault of the shipper or the shipper's employees or agents.  
The shipper's employees or agents shall not bear compensation liability for losses suffered by the carrier or the actual carrier or for damage to the ship, unless such loss or damage is caused by the fault of the shipper's employees or agents.

#### 第四节　运输单证

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　货物由承运人接收或者装船后，应托运人的要求，承运人应当签发提单。提单可以由承运人授权的人签发；由载货船舶的船长签发的，视为代表承运人签发。

**Article 73** After the carrier receives the goods or loads them on board, the carrier shall, at the request of the shipper, issue a bill of lading. The bill of lading may be signed by a person authorized by the carrier; if it is signed by the master of the carrying ship, it shall be deemed to be signed on behalf of the carrier.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　提单内容包括下列各项：  
（一）货物的品名、标志、包数或者件数、重量或者体积，以及运输危险货物时对危险性质的说明；  
（二）承运人的名称和主营业地；  
（三）船舶名称；  
（四）托运人的名称；  
（五）收货人的名称或者关于收货人的指示说明；  
（六）装货港；  
（七）卸货港；  
（八）多式联运提单增列接收货物地点和交付货物地点；  
（九）提单的签发日期、地点和份数；  
（十）运费的支付；  
（十一）承运人或者其代表的签名。  
提单缺少前款规定的一项或者几项的，不影响提单的性质；但是，提单应当符合[本法第四十四条](#art-44)第七项的规定。

**Article 74** A bill of lading shall contain the following particulars:  
(1) the name, marks, number of packages or pieces, weight or volume of the cargo, and a statement of the dangerous nature thereof when dangerous goods are carried;  
(2) the name and principal place of business of the carrier;  
(3) the name of the ship;  
(4) the name of the shipper;  
(5) the name of the consignee or instructions concerning the consignee;  
(6) the port of loading;  
(7) the port of discharge;  
(8) for a multimodal transport bill of lading, additionally the place of receipt and place of delivery;  
(9) the date, place and number of copies of issuance of the bill of lading;  
(10) payment of the freight;  
(11) the signature of the carrier or its representative.  
The absence of one or more of the particulars referred to in the preceding paragraph shall not affect the nature of the bill of lading; however, the bill of lading shall comply with the provisions of Article 44, item (7) of this Law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　货物装船前，承运人已经应托运人的要求签发收货待运提单或者其他单证的，货物装船完毕，托运人可以将收货待运提单或者其他单证退还承运人，换取已装船提单；承运人也可以在收货待运提单上加注承运船舶的船名和装船日期，加注后的收货待运提单视为已装船提单。

**Article 75** If, before the goods are loaded on board, the carrier has issued a received-for-shipment bill of lading or other document at the request of the shipper, the shipper may, after the goods are loaded, surrender the said bill of lading or other document to the carrier in exchange for a shipped bill of lading. The carrier may also annotate on the received-for-shipment bill of lading the name of the carrying ship and the date of loading, and the received-for-shipment bill of lading so annotated shall be deemed to be a shipped bill of lading.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　承运人或者代其签发提单的人知道或者有合理的根据怀疑提单记载的货物的品名、标志、包数或者件数、重量或者体积与实际接收的货物不符，在签发已装船提单的情况下怀疑与已装船的货物不符，或者没有适当的方法核对提单记载的，可以在提单上批注，说明不符之处、怀疑的根据或者说明无法核对。

**Article 76** If the carrier or the person signing the bill of lading on its behalf knows or has reasonable grounds to suspect that the particulars of the goods recorded in the bill of lading, such as name, marks, number of packages or pieces, weight or volume, do not correspond to the goods actually received or, in the case of a shipped bill of lading, do not correspond to the goods actually loaded on board, or if there is no proper means of checking such particulars, the carrier or such person may make a notation on the bill of lading, specifying the inaccuracies, the grounds for suspicion, or stating that the particulars cannot be checked.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　承运人或者代其签发提单的人未在提单上批注货物表面状况的，视为货物的表面状况良好。

**Article 77** If the carrier or the person signing the bill of lading on its behalf makes no notation on the bill of lading as to the apparent condition of the goods, the goods shall be deemed to be in apparent good condition.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　除依据[本法第七十六条](#art-76)的规定作出批注外，承运人或者代其签发提单的人签发的提单，是承运人已经按照提单所载状况收到货物或者货物已经装船的初步证据；承运人向包括收货人在内的善意第三人提出的货物状况与提单记载不同的证据，不予承认。

**Article 78** Except for notations made in accordance with the provisions of Article 76 of this Law, the bill of lading issued by the carrier or the person signing it on its behalf shall be prima facie evidence that the carrier has received the goods or that the goods have been loaded on board as described in the bill of lading. Evidence of a different condition of the goods from that recorded in the bill of lading, adduced by the carrier against a bona fide third party, including the consignee, shall not be admitted.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　承运人与收货人、提单持有人之间的权利义务关系，按照提单确定。未签发提单的，承运人与收货人之间的权利义务关系适用本章有关规定。  
收货人、提单持有人不承担在装货港发生的滞期费、亏舱费和其他与装货有关的费用，但是提单中明确载明上述费用由收货人、提单持有人承担的除外。

**Article 79** The relationship of rights and obligations between the carrier and the consignee or holder of the bill of lading shall be determined by the bill of lading. If no bill of lading has been issued, the relevant provisions of this Chapter shall apply to the relationship of rights and obligations between the carrier and the consignee.  
The consignee or holder of the bill of lading shall not be liable for demurrage, dead freight and other charges relating to loading incurred at the port of loading, unless the bill of lading expressly states that such charges shall be borne by the consignee or holder of the bill of lading.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　记名提单不得转让。  
指示提单经过记名背书或者空白背书转让。  
不记名提单无需背书即可转让。

**Article 80** A straight bill of lading shall not be negotiable.  
An order bill of lading may be negotiated by endorsement, either special or in blank.  
A bearer bill of lading may be negotiated without endorsement.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　承运人签发提单以外的单证用以证明收到待运货物的，此项单证即为订立海上货物运输合同和承运人接收该单证中所列货物的初步证据。  
承运人签发的此类单证不得转让。

**Article 81** If the carrier issues a document other than a bill of lading to evidence receipt of goods for carriage, such document shall be prima facie evidence of the conclusion of the contract of carriage of goods by sea and of the carrier's receipt of the goods listed therein.  
Such documents issued by the carrier shall not be negotiable.

#### 第五节　电子运输记录

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　电子运输记录，是指承运人根据海上货物运输合同，以电子通信方式发出，证明海上货物运输合同和货物已经由承运人接收或者装船的信息，包括可转让电子运输记录和不可转让电子运输记录。  
符合法律、行政法规规定条件的电子运输记录与运输单证具有同等效力。电子运输记录不得仅因采取电子形式而否定其法律效力。  
本法有关运输单证的规定适用于电子运输记录。

**Article 82** An electronic transport record means information issued by the carrier by means of electronic communication pursuant to the contract of carriage of goods by sea, evidencing the contract of carriage of goods by sea and that the goods have been received by the carrier or loaded on board, including a negotiable electronic transport record and a non-negotiable electronic transport record.  
An electronic transport record that meets the requirements provided for in laws and administrative regulations shall have the same effect as a transport document. The legal effect of an electronic transport record shall not be denied solely on the ground that it is in electronic form.  
The provisions of this Law concerning transport documents shall apply to electronic transport records.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　承运人和托运人协商一致，可以签发、使用电子运输记录。

**Article 83** The carrier and the shipper may, by agreement, issue and use electronic transport records.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　电子运输记录应当符合下列要求：  
（一）记录信息包含[本法第七十四条](#art-74)相关内容，并可供调取查用；  
（二）记录信息内容完整、准确；  
（三）签发人能够被识别；  
（四）持有人能够证明其身份。

**Article 84** An electronic transport record shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) the recorded information includes the relevant contents of Article 74 of this Law and is accessible for retrieval and examination;  
(2) the content of the recorded information is complete and accurate;  
(3) the issuer can be identified; and  
(4) the holder can prove its identity.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　可转让电子运输记录除符合[本法第八十四条](#art-84)规定外，还应当包括可转让性信息和转让程序。  
可转让电子运输记录应当采取可靠的方法或者通过可靠的交易系统，确保记录的单一性和完整性，保障持有人对记录的排他性控制。  
可转让电子运输记录的转让和排他性控制、记录形式的转换方式、可靠方法或者交易系统的认定标准等，由国家网信部门会同国务院交通运输主管部门另行制定。

**Article 85** In addition to complying with the provisions of Article 84 of this Law, a negotiable electronic transport record shall also include negotiability information and transfer procedures.  
A negotiable electronic transport record shall adopt reliable methods or a reliable transaction system to ensure the singularity and integrity of the record and to ensure the holder's exclusive control over the record.  
The transfer and exclusive control of negotiable electronic transport records, the method of conversion of the form of records, and the criteria for determining reliable methods or transaction systems shall be formulated separately by the national cyberspace administration together with the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　经承运人和托运人、运输单证持有人协商一致，电子运输记录与运输单证之间可以互相转换。  
电子运输记录与运输单证互相转换，应当注明转换的说明信息，保证记载内容转换前后一致。单证形式的转换不改变当事人的权利义务。  
电子运输记录与运输单证完成转换后，原运输单证或者电子运输记录随即失效。

**Article 86** By agreement between the carrier, the shipper and the holder of the transport document, an electronic transport record and a transport document may be converted into each other.  
When an electronic transport record and a transport document are converted into each other, explanatory information on the conversion shall be noted, and the recorded content shall remain consistent before and after the conversion. The conversion of the document form shall not alter the rights and obligations of the parties.  
Upon completion of the conversion between the electronic transport record and the transport document, the original transport document or electronic transport record shall immediately cease to have effect.

#### 第六节　货物交付

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　承运人交付货物，按照下列规定执行：  
（一）签发记名提单的，凭提单向记名收货人交付；  
（二）签发指示提单的，凭提单向被背书的提单受让人交付；  
（三）签发不记名提单或者签发指示提单经空白背书的，凭提单向提单持有人交付；  
（四）签发可转让电子运输记录的，向电子运输记录的持有人交付；  
（五）其他情形，凭收货人的身份证明向收货人交付。

**Article 87** When the carrier delivers the goods, it shall do so in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Where a straight bill of lading is issued, the goods shall be delivered against the bill of lading to the named consignee;  
(2) Where an order bill of lading is issued, the goods shall be delivered against the bill of lading to the endorsee of the bill of lading;  
(3) Where a bearer bill of lading or an order bill of lading endorsed in blank is issued, the goods shall be delivered against the bill of lading to the holder thereof;  
(4) Where a negotiable electronic transport record is issued, the goods shall be delivered to the holder of the electronic transport record;  
(5) In other cases, the goods shall be delivered to the consignee upon presentation of his identity certificate.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　承运人向收货人交付货物时，收货人应当将货物灭失或者损坏的情况书面通知承运人；未通知的，此项交付视为承运人已经按照运输单证的记载交付以及货物状况良好的初步证据。  
货物灭失或者损坏的情况非显而易见的，收货人应当在非集装箱货物交付的次日起连续七日内或者集装箱货物交付的次日起连续十五日内，向承运人提交书面通知；未提交的，适用前款规定。  
货物交付时，收货人已经与承运人对货物进行联合检查或者检验的，无需就所查明的灭失或者损坏的情况提交书面通知。

**Article 88** When the carrier delivers the goods to the consignee, the consignee shall notify the carrier in writing of the loss or damage to the goods; if no such notification is given, the delivery shall be deemed prima facie evidence that the carrier has delivered the goods as described in the transport document and that the goods were in good condition.  
Where the loss or damage is not apparent, the consignee shall, within seven consecutive days from the next day after delivery of non-containerized goods, or within fifteen consecutive days from the next day after delivery of containerized goods, submit a written notice to the carrier; if no such notice is submitted, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.  
If, at the time of delivery, the consignee and the carrier have jointly inspected or examined the goods, no written notice shall be required for the loss or damage ascertained in such inspection or examination.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　承运人自向收货人交付货物的次日起连续六十日内，未收到收货人就货物因迟延交付造成经济损失而提交的书面通知的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 89** The carrier shall not bear compensation liability if it has not received, within sixty consecutive days from the next day after delivery of the goods to the consignee, a written notice from the consignee of economic loss caused by delay in delivery.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　收货人在目的港提取货物前或者承运人在目的港交付货物前，可以要求检验机构对货物状况进行检验；要求检验的一方应当支付检验费用，但是有权向造成货物损失的责任方追偿。

**Article 90** Before the consignee takes delivery of the goods at the port of destination or before the carrier delivers the goods at the port of destination, the consignee or the carrier may require the goods to be inspected by an inspection body; the party requesting the inspection shall pay the inspection costs, but shall have the right to recover from the party liable for the loss or damage.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　承运人和收货人对[本法第八十八条](#art-88)和第九十条规定的检验，应当相互提供合理的便利条件。

**Article 91** The carrier and the consignee shall provide each other with reasonable facilities for the inspection provided for in Articles 88 and 90 of this Law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　货物由实际承运人交付的，收货人依据[本法第八十八条](#art-88)的规定向实际承运人提交的书面通知，与向承运人提交书面通知具有同等效力；向承运人提交的书面通知，与向实际承运人提交书面通知具有同等效力。

**Article 92** Where the goods are delivered by the actual carrier, a written notice submitted by the consignee to the actual carrier in accordance with Article 88 of this Law shall have the same effect as a written notice submitted to the carrier; a written notice submitted to the carrier shall have the same effect as a written notice submitted to the actual carrier.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　在卸货港无人提取货物的，船长可以将货物卸在仓库或者其他适当场所，由此产生的费用和风险由托运人承担，但是应当及时通知托运人。  
收货人已经行使海上货物运输合同权利但是迟延、拒绝提取货物的，船长可以按照前款规定处理货物，由此产生的费用和风险由收货人承担。

**Article 93** If no one takes delivery of the goods at the port of discharge, the master may discharge the goods into a warehouse or other appropriate place, and the expenses and risks thereby incurred shall be borne by the shipper; provided that the shipper shall be notified promptly.  
If the consignee has exercised his rights under the contract of carriage of goods by sea but delays or refuses to take delivery, the master may deal with the goods in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and the expenses and risks thereby incurred shall be borne by the consignee.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　应当向承运人支付的运费、共同海损分摊、滞期费和承运人为货物垫付的必要费用以及应当向承运人支付的其他费用没有付清，又没有提供适当担保的，承运人可以留置相应的货物。  
运输单证载明运费预付或者类似性质声明的，承运人不得以运费未支付为由留置货物，但是收货人为托运人的除外。

**Article 94** If the freight, general average contribution, demurrage, necessary expenses advanced by the carrier for the goods and other charges payable to the carrier have not been paid in full, and no appropriate security has been provided, the carrier may have a lien on the goods.  
If the transport document contains a statement that freight is prepaid or a similar declaration, the carrier shall not have a lien on the goods on the ground that the freight has not been paid, except where the consignee is the shipper.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　承运人依据[本法第九十四条](#art-94)规定留置的货物，自船舶抵达卸货港的次日起满六十日无人提取的，承运人可以申请法院拍卖；货物易腐烂变质或者货物的保管费用加上应当向承运人支付的费用可能超过货物价值的，承运人可以申请提前拍卖。  
拍卖所得价款，用于清偿保管、拍卖货物的费用和运费以及应当向承运人支付的其他有关费用；不足的金额，承运人有权向托运人追偿；剩余的金额，退还托运人；无法退还、自拍卖之日起满一年又无人领取的，归国家所有。

**Article 95** If the goods placed under a lien by the carrier in accordance with Article 94 of this Law are not taken delivery of within sixty days from the next day after the vessel's arrival at the port of discharge, the carrier may apply to the court for an auction; if the goods are perishable or if the costs of storage plus the charges payable to the carrier may exceed the value of the goods, the carrier may apply for an earlier auction.  
The proceeds from the auction shall be used to satisfy the costs of storage and auction, the freight and other relevant charges payable to the carrier; for any deficiency, the carrier shall have the right to recover from the shipper; any surplus shall be returned to the shipper; if it cannot be returned and no one claims it within one year from the date of auction, the surplus shall belong to the State.

#### 第七节　合同的变更和解除

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　在承运人责任期间内，托运人可以书面通知承运人中止运输、返还货物、变更卸货港或者将货物交给其他收货人，但是应当赔偿承运人因此遭受的损失。  
有下列情形之一的，承运人可以拒绝托运人的要求，但是应当立即通知托运人：  
（一）承运人因客观条件无法满足托运人的要求，或者满足此种要求将影响承运人正常营运；  
（二）承运人预计满足托运人的要求将产生额外费用或者使承运人遭受经济损失，要求托运人提供相应担保，托运人未提供担保；  
（三）托运人未按照承运人的要求出示已签发的运输单证。

**Article 96** During the carrier's period of responsibility, the shipper may, by written notice to the carrier, suspend the carriage, return the goods, change the port of discharge or deliver the goods to another consignee, but shall compensate the carrier for any loss thereby sustained.  
The carrier may refuse the shipper's request in any of the following circumstances, but shall notify the shipper promptly:  
(1) The carrier cannot comply with the shipper's request due to objective conditions, or compliance with such request would affect the carrier's normal operations;  
(2) The carrier anticipates that compliance with the shipper's request would give rise to additional expenses or cause economic loss to the carrier, and the carrier requests the shipper to provide appropriate security, but the shipper fails to provide it;  
(3) The shipper fails to present the issued transport document as required by the carrier.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　船舶在装货港开航前，托运人可以要求解除合同。但是，除合同另有约定外，托运人应当向承运人支付约定运费的一半；货物已经装船的，并应当负担装货、卸货和其他与此有关的费用。

**Article 97** Before the vessel sails from the port of loading, the shipper may request the dissolution of the contract. However, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the shipper shall pay half of the agreed freight to the carrier; if the goods have been loaded, the shipper shall also bear the expenses of loading, unloading and other related charges.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　船舶在装货港开航前，因不可抗力或者其他不能归责于承运人和托运人的原因致使合同不能履行的，双方均可以解除合同，并互相不承担赔偿责任。除合同另有约定外，运费已经支付的，承运人应当将运费退还给托运人；货物已经装船的，托运人应当承担装卸费用；已经签发运输单证的，托运人应当将运输单证退还承运人。

**Article 98** If, before the vessel sails from the port of loading, the contract cannot be performed due to force majeure or other causes not attributable to the carrier or the shipper, either party may dissolve the contract, and neither party shall bear compensation liability to the other. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, if freight has been paid, the carrier shall refund the freight to the shipper; if the goods have been loaded, the shipper shall bear the loading and unloading expenses; if a transport document has been issued, the shipper shall return it to the carrier.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　因不可抗力或者其他不能归责于承运人和托运人的原因致使船舶不能在合同约定的目的港卸货的，除合同另有约定外，船长有权将货物在目的港邻近的安全港口或者地点卸载，视为已经履行合同。  
船长决定将货物卸载的，应当及时通知托运人或者收货人，并考虑托运人或者收货人的利益。

**Article 99** If, due to force majeure or other causes not attributable to the carrier or the shipper, the vessel cannot discharge the goods at the port of destination as agreed in the contract, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the master shall have the right to discharge the goods at a safe port or place near the port of destination, and such discharge shall be deemed to have fulfilled the contract.  
If the master decides to discharge the goods, he shall promptly notify the shipper or the consignee and take into account the interests of the shipper or the consignee.

#### 第八节　多式联运合同的特别规定

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　本法所称多式联运合同，是指多式联运经营人以包括海上运输方式在内的两种以上的不同运输方式，负责将货物从接收地运至目的地交付收货人，并收取全程运费的合同。  
前款所称多式联运经营人，是指本人或者委托他人以本人名义与托运人订立多式联运合同的人。

**Article 100** For the purposes of this Law, a multimodal transport contract means a contract under which a multimodal transport operator, by using two or more different modes of transport including a sea carriage, undertakes to transport the goods from the place of receipt to the place of delivery and to deliver them to the consignee, and to collect the freight for the entire carriage.  
A multimodal transport operator as referred to in the preceding paragraph means a person who enters into a multimodal transport contract with the shipper by himself or by another person acting in his name.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　多式联运经营人对多式联运货物的责任期间，自接收货物时起至交付货物时止。

**Article 101** The period of responsibility of a multimodal transport operator for the goods under multimodal transport shall commence from the time he takes over the goods and continue until the time he delivers the goods.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　多式联运经营人负责履行或者组织履行多式联运合同，并对全程运输负责。  
多式联运经营人与参加多式联运的各区段承运人，可以就多式联运合同的各区段运输，另以合同约定相互之间的责任。但是，此项合同不得影响多式联运经营人对全程运输所承担的责任。

**Article 102** A multimodal transport operator shall be responsible for performing or arranging the performance of the multimodal transport contract and shall be liable for the entire carriage.  
The multimodal transport operator and the carriers participating in the segments of the multimodal transport may, by another contract, agree on their respective responsibilities regarding the transport of each segment. However, such contract shall not affect the liability of the multimodal transport operator for the entire carriage.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付发生于多式联运的某一运输区段的，多式联运经营人的赔偿责任、责任限额和时效，依照调整该区段运输方式的有关法律确定。

**Article 103** If loss of or damage to the goods or delay in delivery occurs during a particular segment of the multimodal transport, the compensation liability, limitation of liability and prescription of the multimodal transport operator shall be determined in accordance with the relevant law governing the mode of transport of that segment.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　货物的灭失、损坏或者迟延交付发生的运输区段不能确定的，多式联运经营人应当依照本章关于承运人赔偿责任、责任限额和本法关于时效的规定承担赔偿责任。

**Article 104** If the segment of transport during which the loss, damage or delay in delivery occurred cannot be ascertained, the multimodal transport operator shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter concerning the carrier's compensation liability and limitation of liability, and the provisions of this Law concerning prescription.

### 第五章　海上旅客运输合同

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　海上旅客运输合同，是指承运人以客轮、邮轮等适合的船舶经海路运输旅客及其行李，由旅客支付票款的合同。

**Article 105** A contract for carriage of passengers by sea means a contract under which a carrier, by means of passenger ships, cruise ships or other suitable ships, transports passengers and their luggage by sea, and the passengers pay fare.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　本章下列用语的含义：  
（一）承运人，是指本人或者委托他人以本人名义与旅客订立海上旅客运输合同的人。  
（二）实际承运人，是指接受承运人委托或者转委托，从事旅客及其行李全部运输或者部分运输的人。  
（三）旅客，是指根据海上旅客运输合同运输的人；经承运人同意，根据海上货物运输合同，随船护送货物的人，视为旅客。  
（四）行李，是指根据海上旅客运输合同由承运人载运的任何物品或者车辆，但是活动物除外。  
（五）自带行李，是指旅客自行携带、保管或者放置在客舱中的行李。

**Article 106** For the purposes of this Chapter, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Carrier" means a person who concludes a contract for carriage of passengers by sea with a passenger, either personally or through another person acting on his or her behalf.  
(2) "Actual carrier" means a person who, by acceptance of an entrustment or a sub-entrustment from the carrier, undertakes the whole or part of the carriage of passengers and their luggage.  
(3) "Passenger" means a person who is transported under a contract for carriage of passengers by sea; a person who, with the consent of the carrier, escorts cargo on board the ship under a contract for carriage of goods by sea shall be deemed a passenger.  
(4) "Luggage" means any article or vehicle carried by the carrier under a contract for carriage of passengers by sea, except live animals.  
(5) "Cabin luggage" means luggage which the passenger keeps in his or her own custody, or places in the cabin.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　旅客客票是海上旅客运输合同成立的凭证。

**Article 107** A passenger ticket shall be evidence of the formation of a contract for carriage of passengers by sea.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　海上旅客运输合同中含有下列内容之一的条款无效：  
（一）免除承运人对旅客应当承担的法定责任；  
（二）降低本章规定的承运人责任限额；  
（三）对本章规定的举证责任作出相反的约定；  
（四）限制旅客提出赔偿请求的权利。  
前款规定的合同条款的无效，不影响合同其他条款的效力。

**Article 108** Any clause in a contract for carriage of passengers by sea that contains any of the following contents shall be null and void:  
(1) exonerating the carrier from the statutory liability that shall be borne to the passenger;  
(2) reducing the limits of liability of the carrier as provided for in this Chapter;  
(3) making a contrary agreement on the burden of adducing evidence provided for in this Chapter; or  
(4) restricting the passenger's right to claim compensation.  
The invalidity of any contractual clause as provided for in the preceding paragraph shall not affect the validity of other clauses of the contract.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　海上旅客运输的运输期间，自旅客登船时起至旅客离船时止。客票票价含接送费用的，运输期间包括承运人经水路将旅客从岸上接到船上和从船上送到岸上的时间，但是不包括旅客在港站内、码头上或者在港口其他设施内的时间。  
旅客自带行李的运输期间适用前款规定。旅客自带行李由承运人或者其受雇人、代理人接收尚未交还给旅客的，运输期间包括旅客在港站内、码头上或者在港口其他设施内的时间。  
旅客自带行李以外的其他行李，运输期间自旅客将行李交付承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人时起至承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人交还旅客时止。

**Article 109** The period of carriage for the carriage of passengers by sea shall be from the time the passenger embarks to the time the passenger disembarks. If the ticket price includes the cost of transfer, the period of carriage shall include the time during which the carrier transports the passenger by water from shore to ship and from ship to shore, excluding the period when the passenger is inside a port station, on a wharf, or in other port facilities.  
The preceding paragraph shall apply to the period of carriage for cabin luggage. If the cabin luggage is taken over by the carrier, his or her servants or agents and has not yet been returned to the passenger, the period of carriage shall include the period when the passenger is inside a port station, on a wharf, or in other port facilities.  
For luggage other than cabin luggage, the period of carriage shall be from the time the passenger delivers the luggage to the carrier, his or her servants or agents to the time the carrier, his or her servants or agents return it to the passenger.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　旅客无票乘船、越级乘船、超程乘船或者持不符合减价条件的优惠客票乘船的，应当按照规定补足票款，承运人可以按照规定加收票款；拒不支付票款的，船长有权在适当地点令其离船，承运人有权要求其支付。

**Article 110** If a passenger travels without a ticket, in a higher class than purchased, beyond the destination specified on the ticket, or with a discount ticket that does not meet the conditions for the discount, the passenger shall make up the fare according to the relevant provisions, and the carrier may charge additional fare according to the relevant provisions; if the passenger refuses to pay the fare, the master shall be entitled to order the passenger to disembark at a suitable place, and the carrier shall be entitled to claim payment from the passenger.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　旅客不得随身携带或者在行李中夹带违禁品或者易燃、易爆、有毒、有腐蚀性、有放射性以及其他有可能危及船上人身和财产安全的危险品。  
承运人有权将旅客随身携带或者在行李中夹带的违禁品、危险品卸下、销毁或者消除危险性，或者送交有关部门，而不承担赔偿责任。  
旅客违反本条第一款规定，造成损害的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 111** A passenger shall not carry on his or her person or pack in his or her luggage any contraband goods or dangerous goods such as inflammable, explosive, toxic, corrosive or radioactive substances or other goods that may endanger the safety of persons and property on board.  
The carrier shall be entitled to unload, destroy or render harmless such contraband or dangerous goods carried on the passenger's person or packed in the passenger's luggage, or deliver them to the relevant authority, without bearing compensation liability.  
If the passenger causes damage by violating the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article, the passenger shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　在[本法第一百零九条](#art-109)规定的旅客及其行李的运输期间，因承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人在受雇或者受委托的范围内的过错引起事故，造成旅客人身伤亡或者行李灭失、损坏的，承运人应当承担赔偿责任。  
请求人对承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人的过错，应当承担举证责任；但是，本条第三款、第四款规定的情形除外。  
旅客的人身伤亡或者自带行李、旅客车辆所载行李的灭失、损坏，是因船舶的沉没、碰撞、搁浅、爆炸、火灾或者船舶的缺陷引起的，承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人除非提出相反证据，应当视为其有过错。  
旅客自带行李、旅客车辆所载行李以外的其他行李的灭失或者损坏，无论因何种事故引起，承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人除非提出相反证据，应当视为其有过错。

**Article 112** If, during the period of carriage of the passenger and his or her luggage as specified in Article 109 of this Law, an accident is caused by the fault of the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents acting within the scope of their employment or agency, resulting in personal injury or death of the passenger or loss of or damage to the luggage, the carrier shall bear compensation liability.  
The claimant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to the fault of the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents, except in the circumstances provided for in paragraphs 3 and 4 of this Article.  
If the personal injury or death of the passenger, or the loss of or damage to the cabin luggage or the luggage contained in the passenger's vehicle, is caused by the sinking, collision, stranding, explosion, fire or defects of the ship, the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents shall be presumed to be at fault unless they prove otherwise.  
If the loss of or damage to luggage other than cabin luggage and luggage contained in the passenger's vehicle arises from any accident whatsoever, the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents shall be presumed to be at fault unless they prove otherwise.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　经承运人证明，旅客的人身伤亡或者行李的灭失、损坏是因旅客本人的过失或者旅客和承运人的共同过失造成的，可以免除或者相应减轻承运人的赔偿责任。  
经承运人证明，旅客的人身伤亡或者行李的灭失、损坏是因旅客本人的故意造成的，或者旅客的人身伤亡是旅客本人健康原因造成的，承运人不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 113** If the carrier proves that the passenger's personal injury or death, or the loss of or damage to the luggage, was caused by the fault of the passenger or by the concurrent fault of the passenger and the carrier, the carrier may be exonerated from or have his or her compensation liability reduced accordingly.  
If the carrier proves that the passenger's personal injury or death, or the loss of or damage to the luggage, was caused by the intention of the passenger, or that the personal injury or death of the passenger was caused by the passenger's own state of health, the carrier shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　承运人对旅客的货币、金银、珠宝、有价证券或者其他贵重物品所发生的灭失、损坏，不承担赔偿责任。  
旅客与承运人约定将前款规定的物品交由承运人保管的，承运人应当依据[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)的规定承担赔偿责任；双方书面约定的赔偿数额高于[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)规定的赔偿责任限额的，承运人应当按照约定的数额承担赔偿责任。

**Article 114** The carrier shall not bear compensation liability for the loss of or damage to the passenger's money, gold, silver, jewels, valuable securities or other valuables.  
If the passenger and the carrier have agreed that the articles specified in the preceding paragraph shall be deposited with the carrier for custody, the carrier shall bear compensation liability in accordance with Article 115 of this Law; if the amount of compensation agreed in writing between the parties is higher than the limits of compensation liability provided for in Article 115 of this Law, the carrier shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the agreed amount.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　承运人在每次海上旅客运输中的赔偿责任限额，按照下列规定执行：  
（一）旅客人身伤亡的，每名旅客不超过175000计算单位；  
（二）旅客自带行李灭失或者损坏的，每名旅客不超过1800计算单位；  
（三）旅客车辆包括该车辆所载行李灭失或者损坏的，每一车辆不超过10000计算单位；  
（四）本款第二项、第三项以外的旅客其他行李灭失或者损坏的，每名旅客不超过2700计算单位。  
承运人和旅客可以约定承运人对旅客车辆和旅客车辆以外的其他行李损失的免赔额。但是，对每一车辆损失的免赔额不得超过300计算单位，对每名旅客的车辆以外的其他行李损失的免赔额不得超过135计算单位。在计算每一车辆或者每名旅客的车辆以外的其他行李的损失赔偿数额时，应当扣除约定的承运人免赔额。  
承运人和旅客可以书面约定高于本条第一款规定的赔偿责任限额。

**Article 115** The limits of compensation liability of the carrier for each carriage of passengers by sea shall be as follows:  
(1) for personal injury or death of a passenger, not exceeding 175,000 units of account per passenger;  
(2) for loss of or damage to cabin luggage, not exceeding 1,800 units of account per passenger;  
(3) for loss of or damage to vehicles including luggage contained therein, not exceeding 10,000 units of account per vehicle;  
(4) for loss of or damage to luggage other than those specified in items (2) and (3) of this paragraph, not exceeding 2,700 units of account per passenger.  
The carrier and the passenger may agree on the deductible amount to be borne by the carrier in respect of loss of or damage to vehicles and luggage other than vehicles. However, the deductible in respect of loss of or damage to each vehicle shall not exceed 300 units of account, and the deductible in respect of loss of or damage to luggage other than vehicles per passenger shall not exceed 135 units of account. When calculating the amount of compensation for loss of or damage to each vehicle or to luggage other than vehicles per passenger, the agreed deductible shall be deducted.  
The carrier and the passenger may agree in writing on the limits of compensation liability higher than those provided for in paragraph 1 of this Article.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　经证明，旅客的人身伤亡或者行李的灭失、损坏是因承运人的故意或者明知可能造成损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，承运人不得援用[本法第一百一十四条](#art-114)和第一百一十五条限制赔偿责任的规定。  
经证明，旅客的人身伤亡或者行李的灭失、损坏是因承运人的受雇人、代理人的故意或者明知可能造成损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，承运人的受雇人、代理人不得援用[本法第一百一十四条](#art-114)和第一百一十五条限制赔偿责任的规定。

**Article 116** If it is proved that the passenger's personal injury or death, or the loss of or damage to the luggage, resulted from the intention of the carrier, or from the carrier's reckless act or omission with knowledge that such damage would probably result, the carrier shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of Articles 114 and 115 of this Law limiting his or her compensation liability.  
If it is proved that the passenger's personal injury or death, or the loss of or damage to the luggage, resulted from the intention of the carrier's servants or agents, or from their reckless act or omission with knowledge that such damage would probably result, the carrier's servants or agents shall not be entitled to invoke the provisions of Articles 114 and 115 of this Law limiting their compensation liability.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　行李发生明显损坏的，旅客应当按照下列规定向承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人提交书面通知：  
（一）自带行李、旅客车辆所载行李，应当在旅客离船前或者离船时提交；  
（二）其他行李，应当在行李交还前或者交还时提交。  
行李的损坏不明显，旅客在离船时或者行李交还时难以发现的，以及行李发生灭失的，旅客应当在离船或者行李交还或者应当交还之日起十五日内，向承运人或者承运人的受雇人、代理人提交书面通知。  
旅客未依据本条第一款、第二款规定及时提交书面通知的，除非提出相反证据，视为已经完整无损地收到行李。  
行李交还时，旅客已经与承运人对行李进行联合检查或者检验的，无需提交书面通知。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/交通运输/中华人民共和国港口间海上旅客运输赔偿责任限额规定.md#art-1" title="被《中华人民共和国港口间海上旅客运输赔偿责任限额规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 117** If the luggage is damaged apparently, the passenger shall submit a written notice to the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for cabin luggage and luggage contained in the passenger's vehicle, the notice shall be submitted before or at the time of disembarkation;  
(2) for other luggage, the notice shall be submitted before or at the time of its redelivery.  
If the damage to the luggage is not apparent and is difficult to discover at the time of disembarkation or redelivery, or if the luggage is lost, the passenger shall submit a written notice to the carrier or the carrier's servants or agents within fifteen days from the date of disembarkation, or from the date of redelivery or the date when redelivery should have taken place.  
If the passenger fails to submit a written notice in a timely manner in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, the luggage shall be deemed to have been received by the passenger in good condition, unless the passenger proves otherwise.  
If the passenger and the carrier have jointly inspected or examined the luggage at the time of its redelivery, no written notice shall be required.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　就旅客人身伤亡或者行李灭失、损坏通过诉讼、仲裁等方式对承运人提起的任何赔偿请求，无论请求人是否为合同的一方，也无论是根据合同或者侵权行为提出请求，均适用本章关于承运人的抗辩理由和限制赔偿责任的规定。  
对承运人的受雇人、代理人提起前款赔偿请求的，承运人的受雇人或者代理人证明其行为在受雇或者受委托的范围内的，适用前款规定。

**Article 118** Any claim for compensation brought against the carrier by means of litigation, arbitration or otherwise in respect of personal injury or death of a passenger, or loss of or damage to luggage, whether or not the claimant is a party to the contract, and whether the claim is based on contract or tort, shall be subject to the provisions of this Chapter concerning the carrier's grounds for defense and limitation of compensation liability.  
If a claim for compensation as described in the preceding paragraph is brought against the carrier's servants or agents, and such servants or agents prove that their acts were within the scope of their employment or agency, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　承运人将旅客及其行李全部运输或者部分运输委托给实际承运人履行的，仍然应当依照本章规定，对全部运输负责。实际承运人履行运输的，承运人应当对实际承运人的行为或者实际承运人的受雇人、代理人在受雇或者受委托的范围内的行为负责。

**Article 119** If the carrier entrusts the performance of the whole or part of the carriage of the passenger and his or her luggage to an actual carrier, the carrier shall nevertheless remain liable for the whole carriage in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. If the actual carrier performs the carriage, the carrier shall be liable for the acts of the actual carrier or for the acts of the actual carrier's servants or agents acting within the scope of their employment or agency.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　本章关于承运人责任的规定，适用于实际承运人。本章关于承运人的受雇人、代理人责任的规定，适用于实际承运人的受雇人、代理人。

**Article 120** The provisions of this Chapter relating to the liability of the carrier shall apply to the actual carrier. The provisions of this Chapter relating to the liability of the carrier's servants or agents shall apply to the servants or agents of the actual carrier.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　承运人承担本章未规定的义务或者放弃本章赋予的权利的任何特别协议，经实际承运人书面明确同意的，对实际承运人发生效力；实际承运人是否同意，不影响此项特别协议对承运人的效力。

**Article 121** Any special agreement under which the carrier assumes obligations not provided for in this Chapter or waives rights conferred by this Chapter shall be binding on the actual carrier only if the actual carrier has expressly agreed in writing; whether or not the actual carrier consents shall not affect the validity of such special agreement with respect to the carrier.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　承运人与实际承运人均负有赔偿责任的，应当在此项责任范围内承担连带责任。

**Article 122** If both the carrier and the actual carrier are liable for compensation, they shall bear joint and several liability within the scope of such liability.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　就旅客的人身伤亡或者行李的灭失、损坏，分别向承运人、实际承运人以及他们的受雇人、代理人提出赔偿请求的，赔偿总额不得超过[本法第一百一十五条](#art-115)规定的限额。

**Article 123** If claims for compensation in respect of personal injury or death of a passenger, or loss of or damage to luggage, are brought separately against the carrier, the actual carrier, and their servants or agents, the total amount of compensation shall not exceed the limits provided for in Article 115 of this Law.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　[本法第一百一十九条](#art-119)至第一百二十三条的规定，不影响承运人和实际承运人之间相互追偿。

**Article 124** The provisions of Articles 119 through 123 of this Law shall not affect the right of recourse between the carrier and the actual carrier.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　承运人或者实际承运人应当对旅客人身伤亡赔偿责任进行责任保险或者取得相应的财务保证。

**Article 125** The carrier or the actual carrier shall effect liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial security in respect of compensation liability for personal injury or death of passengers.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　旅客人身伤亡损害赔偿请求可以直接向责任保险人或者财务保证人提出。承运人或者实际承运人丧失本章规定的限制赔偿责任权利的，不影响其责任保险人或者财务保证人援用本章规定限制赔偿责任。  
责任保险人或者财务保证人有权以损害是因承运人或者实际承运人的故意造成的进行抗辩，并有权援用承运人或者实际承运人除破产或者清算之外的理由进行抗辩。

**Article 126** A claim for compensation for personal injury or death of a passenger may be brought directly against the liability insurer or the financial guarantor. If the carrier or the actual carrier loses the right to limit his or her compensation liability under this Chapter, this shall not affect the right of his or her liability insurer or financial guarantor to invoke the provisions of this Chapter to limit compensation liability.  
The liability insurer or the financial guarantor shall be entitled to defend on the ground that the damage was caused by the intention of the carrier or the actual carrier, and shall be entitled to invoke any ground other than bankruptcy or liquidation of the carrier or the actual carrier for defense.

### 第六章　租船合同

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　本法规定的租船合同，包括航次租船合同、定期租船合同和光船租赁合同。租船合同应当采用书面形式订立。

**Article 127** The charter parties provided for in this Law include voyage charter parties, time charter parties and bareboat charter parties. A charter party shall be concluded in writing.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　本章关于出租人和承租人之间权利义务的规定，仅在租船合同没有约定或者没有不同约定时适用。

**Article 128** The provisions of this Chapter concerning the rights and obligations between the shipowner and the charterer shall apply only when the charter party does not contain any stipulation or different stipulation in this regard.

#### 第二节　航次租船合同

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　航次租船合同，是指船舶出租人向承租人提供船舶或者船舶的部分舱位，装运约定的货物，从一港运至另一港，由承租人支付约定运费的合同。  
航次租船合同对出租人和承租人之间的权利义务没有约定或者约定不明时，除适用本节规定外，还适用本法第四章有关当事人之间权利义务的规定。  
航次租船合同违反[本法第四十八条](#art-48)和第五十条规定的条款无效。

**Article 129** A voyage charter party is a contract under which the shipowner provides the charterer with a ship or a part thereof to carry the agreed cargo from one port to another, and the charterer pays the agreed freight.  
Where a voyage charter party contains no provisions or unclear provisions regarding the rights and obligations of the shipowner and the charterer, the provisions in Chapter IV of this Law concerning the rights and obligations of the parties shall also apply in addition to the provisions of this Section.  
Any clause in a voyage charter party that violates the provisions of Articles 48 and 50 of this Law shall be null and void.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　航次租船合同的内容，主要包括出租人和承租人的名称、船名、船籍、载货重量、容积、货名、装货港和目的港、受载期限、装卸期限、运费、滞期费、速遣费以及其他有关事项。

**Article 130** The contents of a voyage charter party mainly include the names of the shipowner and the charterer, the name of the ship, its nationality, deadweight capacity, cubic capacity, description of the cargo, port of loading and port of destination, laydays, laytime, freight, demurrage, dispatch money and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　对于按照航次租船合同运输的货物签发的提单，提单持有人不是承租人的，承运人与该提单持有人之间的权利义务关系适用提单的约定。但是，提单中载明适用航次租船合同条款的，适用该航次租船合同的条款。

**Article 131** Where a bill of lading is issued for cargo carried under a voyage charter party, and the holder of the bill of lading is not the charterer, the rights and obligations between the carrier and such holder shall be governed by the provisions of the bill of lading. However, if the bill of lading expressly states that the terms of the voyage charter party apply, the terms of such voyage charter party shall apply.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　出租人应当提供约定的船舶；经承租人同意，可以更换船舶。出租人提供或者更换的船舶不符合合同约定的，承租人有权拒绝或者解除合同。  
因出租人的过错未提供约定的船舶致使承租人遭受损失的，出租人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 132** The shipowner shall provide the agreed ship; with the consent of the charterer, the shipowner may substitute the ship. Where the ship provided or substituted by the shipowner does not conform to the contract, the charterer shall have the right to reject or rescind the contract.  
If the shipowner, due to its fault, fails to provide the agreed ship and thereby causes losses to the charterer, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　出租人在约定的受载期限内未能提供船舶的，承租人有权解除合同。但是，出租人将船舶延误情况和船舶预期抵达装货港的日期通知承租人的，承租人应当自收到通知时起四十八小时内，将是否解除合同的决定通知出租人。  
因出租人的过错延误提供船舶致使承租人遭受损失的，出租人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 133** Where the shipowner fails to provide the ship within the agreed laydays, the charterer shall have the right to rescind the contract. However, if the shipowner notifies the charterer of the delay and the expected date of the ship's arrival at the port of loading, the charterer shall, within forty-eight hours from receipt of such notification, notify the shipowner of its decision whether to rescind the contract.  
If the shipowner, due to its fault, delays in providing the ship and thereby causes losses to the charterer, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　航次租船合同的装货、卸货期限及其计算办法，超过装货、卸货期限后的滞期费和提前完成装货、卸货的速遣费，由双方约定。

**Article 134** The laytime for loading and discharging, the method of calculating such laytime, the demurrage payable after the expiry of the laytime, and the dispatch money payable for completion of loading or discharging ahead of time shall be agreed upon by the parties.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　承租人可以将其租用的船舶转租；转租后，原合同约定的权利和义务不受影响。

**Article 135** The charterer may sublet the ship it has chartered; after such subletting, the rights and obligations agreed in the original contract shall not be affected.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　承租人应当提供约定的货物；经出租人同意，可以更换货物。但是，更换的货物对出租人不利的，出租人有权拒绝或者解除合同。  
因未提供约定的货物致使出租人遭受损失的，承租人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 136** The charterer shall provide the agreed cargo; with the consent of the shipowner, the charterer may substitute the cargo. However, if the substituted cargo is detrimental to the shipowner, the shipowner shall have the right to reject or rescind the contract.  
If the charterer fails to provide the agreed cargo and thereby causes losses to the shipowner, the charterer shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　出租人应当在合同约定的卸货港卸货。合同订有承租人选择卸货港条款的，在承租人未按照合同约定及时通知确定的卸货港时，船长可以从约定的选卸港中自行选定一港卸货。承租人未按照合同约定及时通知确定的卸货港，致使出租人遭受损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。出租人未按照合同约定，擅自选定港口卸货致使承租人遭受损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 137** The shipowner shall discharge the cargo at the port of discharge agreed in the contract. Where the contract contains a clause entitling the charterer to choose the port of discharge, if the charterer fails to notify the shipowner in a timely manner of the determined port of discharge in accordance with the contract, the master may choose one port from the agreed optional ports of discharge for unloading. If the charterer fails to notify the shipowner in a timely manner of the determined port of discharge in accordance with the contract, thereby causing losses to the shipowner, the charterer shall bear compensation liability. If the shipowner, without complying with the contract, arbitrarily selects a port for discharge and thereby causes losses to the charterer, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

#### 第三节　定期租船合同

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　定期租船合同，是指船舶出租人向承租人提供约定的由出租人配备船员的船舶，由承租人在约定的期间内按照约定的用途使用，并支付租金的合同。

**Article 138** A time charter party is a contract under which the shipowner provides the charterer with an agreed ship, manned by the shipowner's crew, and the charterer uses the ship for the agreed duration and for the agreed purpose, and pays the hire.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　定期租船合同的内容，主要包括出租人和承租人的名称、船名、船籍、船级、吨位、容积、船速、燃料消耗、航区、用途、租船期间、交船和还船的时间和地点以及条件、租金及其支付，以及其他有关事项。

**Article 139** The contents of a time charter party mainly include the names of the shipowner and the charterer, the name of the ship, its nationality, class, tonnage, cubic capacity, speed, fuel consumption, trading area, purpose, charter period, time and place and conditions of delivery and redelivery of the ship, hire and its payment, and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　出租人应当按照合同约定的时间交付船舶。  
出租人违反前款规定的，承租人有权解除合同。出租人将船舶延误情况和船舶预期抵达交船港的日期通知承租人的，承租人应当自接到通知时起四十八小时内，将解除合同或者继续租用船舶的决定通知出租人。  
因出租人的过错延误提供船舶致使承租人遭受损失的，出租人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 140** The shipowner shall deliver the ship within the time agreed in the contract.  
If the shipowner violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the charterer shall have the right to rescind the contract. Where the shipowner notifies the charterer of the delay and the expected date of the ship's arrival at the port of delivery, the charterer shall, within forty-eight hours from receipt of such notification, notify the shipowner of its decision whether to rescind the contract or to continue chartering the ship.  
If the shipowner, due to its fault, delays in providing the ship and thereby causes losses to the charterer, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　出租人交付船舶时，应当做到谨慎处理，使船舶处于适航状态。交付的船舶应当适于约定的用途。  
出租人违反前款规定的，承租人有权解除合同，并有权要求赔偿因此遭受的损失。

**Article 141** When delivering the ship, the shipowner shall exercise due diligence to make the ship seaworthy. The ship delivered shall be fit for the agreed purpose.  
If the shipowner violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the charterer shall have the right to rescind the contract and to claim compensation for any losses suffered thereby.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　船舶在租期内不符合约定的适航状态或者其他状态，出租人应当采取可能采取的合理措施，使船舶状态尽快恢复。  
船舶不符合约定的适航状态或者其他状态，不能正常营运连续满二十四小时的，对因此而损失的营运时间，承租人不付租金，但是上述状态是由承租人造成的除外。

**Article 142** Where the ship during the charter period is not in the agreed seaworthy condition or other condition, the shipowner shall take such reasonable measures as are possible to restore the ship to the required condition as soon as possible.  
Where the ship is not in the agreed seaworthy condition or other condition and cannot operate normally for a continuous period of twenty-four hours, the charterer shall not pay hire for the operating time lost thereby, unless such condition is caused by the charterer.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　承租人应当保证船舶在约定航区内的安全港口或者地点之间从事约定的海上运输。  
承租人违反前款规定的，出租人有权解除合同，并有权要求赔偿因此遭受的损失。

**Article 143** The charterer shall ensure that the ship is employed in the agreed marine transport between safe ports or places within the agreed trading area.  
If the charterer violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the shipowner shall have the right to rescind the contract and to claim compensation for any losses suffered thereby.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　承租人应当保证船舶用于运输约定的合法货物。  
承租人将船舶用于运输活动物或者危险货物的，应当事先征得出租人同意。  
承租人违反本条规定致使出租人遭受损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 144** The charterer shall ensure that the ship is used for carrying the agreed lawful cargo.  
Where the charterer uses the ship for carrying live animals or dangerous goods, the charterer shall obtain the prior consent of the shipowner.  
If the charterer violates the provisions of this Article and thereby causes losses to the shipowner, the charterer shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　承租人有权就船舶的营运向船长发出指示，但是不得违反定期租船合同的约定。

**Article 145** The charterer shall have the right to give instructions to the master with respect to the operation of the ship, provided that such instructions shall not violate the provisions of the time charter party.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　承租人可以将租用的船舶转租，但是应当将转租的情况及时通知出租人。租用的船舶转租后，原租船合同约定的权利和义务不受影响。

**Article 146** The charterer may sublet the ship it has chartered, but shall promptly notify the shipowner of the subletting. After the subletting of the chartered ship, the rights and obligations agreed in the original charter party shall not be affected.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　船舶所有人转让已经租出的船舶的所有权，定期租船合同约定的当事人的权利和义务不受影响，但是应当及时通知承租人。船舶所有权转让后，原租船合同由受让人和承租人继续履行。

**Article 147** Where the shipowner transfers the ownership of a ship that has already been chartered out, the rights and obligations of the parties as agreed in the time charter party shall not be affected, provided that the shipowner shall promptly notify the charterer. After the transfer of the ship's ownership, the original charter party shall continue to be performed by the transferee and the charterer.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　在合同期间，船舶进行海难救助的，承租人有权获得扣除救助费用、损失赔偿、船员应得部分以及其他费用后的救助款项的百分之五十。

**Article 148** Where the ship renders salvage services during the contract period, the charterer shall be entitled to fifty percent of the salvage proceeds after deducting the salvage expenses, compensation for damage, the crew's share and other expenses.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　承租人应当按照合同约定支付租金；未按照合同约定支付租金的，出租人有权解除合同，并有权要求赔偿因此遭受的损失。

**Article 149** The charterer shall pay the hire as agreed in the contract; if the charterer fails to pay the hire as agreed in the contract, the shipowner has the right to dissolve the contract and claim compensation for the losses thus suffered.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　承租人未向出租人支付租金或者合同约定的其他款项的，出租人对船上属于承租人的货物和财产以及转租船舶的收入享有留置权。

**Article 150** Where the charterer fails to pay the hire or other sums as agreed in the contract to the shipowner, the shipowner shall have a lien on the charterer's cargo and property on board and on the income from the sub-chartering of the ship.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　承租人向出租人交还船舶时，该船舶应当具有与出租人交船时相同的良好状态，但是船舶本身的自然磨损除外。  
船舶未能保持与交船时相同的良好状态的，承租人应当负责修复或者给予赔偿。

**Article 151** When the charterer redelivers the ship to the shipowner, the ship shall be in the same good condition as when delivered by the shipowner, except for normal wear and tear of the ship.  
Where the ship fails to be maintained in the same good condition as when delivered, the charterer shall be liable to repair the same or compensate for the damage.

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　经合理计算，完成最后航次的日期约为合同约定的还船日期，但是可能超过合同约定的还船日期的，承租人有权超期用船以完成该航次。超期期间，承租人应当按照合同约定的租金率支付租金；市场租金率高于合同约定的租金率的，承租人应当按照市场租金率支付租金。

**Article 152** Where, after reasonable calculation, the date of completion of the last voyage is approximately the date for redelivery as agreed in the contract, but may exceed the agreed redelivery date, the charterer is entitled to continue to use the ship beyond the agreed period to complete that voyage. During the extended period, the charterer shall pay the hire at the rate agreed in the contract; if the market rate of hire is higher than the rate agreed in the contract, the charterer shall pay the hire at the market rate.

#### 第四节　光船租赁合同

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　光船租赁合同，是指船舶出租人向承租人提供不配备船员的船舶，在约定的期间内由承租人占有、使用和营运，并向出租人支付租金的合同。

**Article 153** A bareboat charter party is a contract whereby the shipowner provides the charterer with an unmanned ship, to be possessed, used and operated by the charterer within the agreed period, and the charterer pays the hire to the shipowner.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　光船租赁合同的内容，主要包括出租人和承租人的名称、船名、船籍、船级、吨位、容积、航区、用途、租船期间、交船和还船的时间和地点以及条件、船舶检验、船舶的保养维修、租金及其支付、船舶保险、合同解除的时间和条件，以及其他有关事项。

**Article 154** The contents of a bareboat charter party mainly include the names of the shipowner and the charterer, the name, nationality, class, tonnage, capacity, trading area, purposes, charter period, time and place of delivery and redelivery and conditions thereof, ship inspection, maintenance and repair of the ship, hire and payment thereof, ship insurance, time and conditions for dissolution of the contract, and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　出租人应当在合同约定的港口或者地点，按照合同约定的时间，向承租人交付船舶以及船舶证书。交付船舶时，出租人应当做到谨慎处理，使船舶处于适航状态。交付的船舶应当适于合同约定的用途。  
出租人违反前款规定的，承租人有权解除合同，并有权要求赔偿因此遭受的损失。

**Article 155** The shipowner shall deliver the ship and the ship's certificates to the charterer at the port or place and within the time as agreed in the contract. At the time of delivery, the shipowner shall exercise due diligence to put the ship in a seaworthy condition. The ship delivered shall be fit for the intended service as agreed in the contract.  
Where the shipowner violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the charterer has the right to dissolve the contract and claim compensation for the losses thus suffered.

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条　在光船租赁期间，承租人负责船舶的保养、维修。

**Article 156** During the bareboat charter period, the charterer shall be responsible for the maintenance and repair of the ship.

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条　在光船租赁期间，承租人应当按照合同约定的船舶价值，以出租人同意的保险方式为船舶进行保险，并负担保险费用。

**Article 157** During the bareboat charter period, the charterer shall insure the ship in the manner agreed by the shipowner at the value as agreed in the contract, and shall bear the insurance premiums.

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条　在光船租赁期间，因承租人对船舶占有、使用和营运的原因使出租人的利益受到影响或者遭受损失的，承租人应当负责消除影响或者赔偿损失。  
因船舶所有权争议或者出租人所负的债务致使船舶被扣押的，出租人应当保证承租人的利益不受影响；致使承租人遭受损失的，出租人应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 158** During the bareboat charter period, if the shipowner's interests are affected or losses are suffered due to the charterer's possession, use and operation of the ship, the charterer shall be liable to remove the adverse effects or compensate for the losses.  
Where the ship is arrested due to a dispute over the ownership of the ship or debts owed by the shipowner, the shipowner shall ensure that the charterer's interests are not affected; if the charterer suffers losses thereby, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条　在光船租赁期间，未经出租人书面同意，承租人不得转让合同的权利和义务或者以光船租赁的方式将船舶进行转租。

**Article 159** During the bareboat charter period, without the written consent of the shipowner, the charterer shall not assign the rights and obligations under the contract or sub-charter the ship under a bareboat charter party.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条　未经承租人事先书面同意，出租人不得在光船租赁期间对船舶设立抵押权。  
出租人违反前款规定，致使承租人遭受损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 160** Without the prior written consent of the charterer, the shipowner shall not create a mortgage on the ship during the bareboat charter period.  
Where the shipowner violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph and causes losses to the charterer, the shipowner shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条　承租人应当按照合同约定支付租金。承租人未按照合同约定的时间支付租金连续超过七日的，出租人有权解除合同，并有权要求赔偿因此遭受的损失。  
船舶灭失或者失踪的，租金应当自船舶灭失或者得知其最后消息之日起停止支付，预付租金应当按照比例退还。

**Article 161** The charterer shall pay the hire as agreed in the contract. Where the charterer fails to pay the hire at the time agreed in the contract for seven consecutive days or more, the shipowner has the right to dissolve the contract and claim compensation for the losses thus suffered.  
If the ship is lost or missing, the hire shall cease to be payable from the date of the loss of the ship or the date on which the last news was received, and any hire paid in advance shall be returned pro rata.

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条　[本法第一百四十三条](#art-143)、第一百四十四条第一款、第一百五十一条和第一百五十二条的规定，适用于光船租赁合同。

**Article 162** The provisions of Articles 143, the first paragraph of Article 144, Article 151 and Article 152 of this Law shall apply to bareboat charter parties.

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条　订有租购条款的光船租赁合同，承租人按照合同约定向出租人付清租购费时，船舶所有权即归于承租人。

**Article 163** Where a bareboat charter party contains a hire-purchase clause, the ownership of the ship shall vest in the charterer upon the charterer's payment of the full hire-purchase price to the shipowner as agreed in the contract.

### 第七章　海上拖航合同

<a id="art-164"></a>第一百六十四条　海上拖航合同，是指承拖方用拖轮将被拖物经海路从一地拖至另一地，而由被拖方支付拖航费的合同。  
本章规定不适用于在港区内对船舶提供的拖轮服务。

**Article 164** A contract of maritime towage means a contract under which the tugowner undertakes to tow an object by a tug from one place to another by sea, and the tow party pays the towage fee.  
The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to tug services provided for ships within a port area.

<a id="art-165"></a>第一百六十五条　海上拖航合同应当采用书面形式订立。海上拖航合同的内容，主要包括承拖方和被拖方的名称和住所、拖轮和被拖物的名称和主要尺度、拖轮马力、起拖地和目的地、起拖日期、拖航费及其支付方式，以及其他有关事项。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 165** A contract of maritime towage shall be concluded in writing. The contents of a contract of maritime towage mainly include the names and domiciles of the tugowner and the tow party, the names and main dimensions of the tug and the tow, the horsepower of the tug, the place of commencement and the destination, the date of commencement of towage, the towage fee and its payment method, and other relevant matters.

<a id="art-166"></a>第一百六十六条　承拖方在起拖前和起拖当时，应当谨慎处理，使拖轮处于适航、适拖状态，妥善配备船员，配置拖航索具和配备供应品以及该航次必备的其他装置、设备。  
被拖方在起拖前和起拖当时，应当做好被拖物的拖航准备，谨慎处理，使被拖物处于适拖状态，并向承拖方如实说明被拖物的情况，按照规定提供有关检验机构签发的被拖物适合拖航的证书和有关文件。

**Article 166** The tugowner shall, before and at the commencement of the towage, exercise due diligence to make the tug seaworthy and tow-worthy, properly man the tug, supply the towing gear and stores, and provide other necessary equipment and devices for the voyage.  
The tow party shall, before and at the commencement of the towage, make the tow ready for towage, exercise due diligence to make the tow in a tow-worthy condition, truthfully inform the tugowner of the condition of the tow, and provide, in accordance with the relevant provisions, the certificate and relevant documents issued by the relevant inspection authority certifying that the tow is suitable for towage.

<a id="art-167"></a>第一百六十七条　起拖前，因不可抗力或者其他不能归责于双方的原因致使合同不能履行的，双方均可以解除合同，并互相不承担赔偿责任。除合同另有约定外，拖航费已经支付的，承拖方应当退还给被拖方。

**Article 167** If, before the commencement of the towage, the contract cannot be performed due to force majeure or other causes not attributable to either party, both parties may dissolve the contract, and neither party shall bear compensation liability to the other. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, if the towage fee has been paid, the tugowner shall refund it to the tow party.

<a id="art-168"></a>第一百六十八条　起拖后，因不可抗力或者其他不能归责于双方的原因致使合同不能继续履行的，双方均可以解除合同，并互相不承担赔偿责任。除合同另有约定外，拖航费按照实际完成的拖航部分确定。

**Article 168** If, after the commencement of the towage, the contract cannot be further performed due to force majeure or other causes not attributable to either party, both parties may dissolve the contract, and neither party shall bear compensation liability to the other. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the towage fee shall be determined based on the part of the towage actually completed.

<a id="art-169"></a>第一百六十九条　因不可抗力或者其他不能归责于双方的原因致使被拖物不能拖至目的地的，除合同另有约定外，承拖方可以在目的地的邻近地点或者拖轮船长选定的安全的港口或者锚泊地，将被拖物移交给被拖方或者其代理人，视为已经履行合同。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第7条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 169** If the tow cannot be towed to the destination due to force majeure or other causes not attributable to either party, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the tugowner may deliver the tow to the tow party or his or her agent at a place near the destination or at a safe port or anchorage selected by the master of the tug, and the contract shall be deemed to have been performed.

<a id="art-170"></a>第一百七十条　被拖方未按照约定支付拖航费和其他合理费用的，承拖方对被拖物享有留置权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 170** If the tow party fails to pay the towage fee and other reasonable expenses as agreed, the tugowner shall have a lien on the tow.

<a id="art-171"></a>第一百七十一条　在海上拖航过程中，承拖方或者被拖方遭受的损失，由一方的过错造成的，有过错的一方应当承担赔偿责任；由双方过错造成的，各方按照过错程度的比例承担赔偿责任。  
虽有前款规定，经承拖方证明，被拖方的损失是由于下列原因之一造成的，承拖方不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）拖轮船长、船员、引航员或者承拖方的其他受雇人、代理人在驾驶拖轮或者管理拖轮中的过错；  
（二）拖轮在海上救助或者企图救助人命或者财产时的过错。  
本条规定仅在海上拖航合同没有约定或者没有不同约定时适用。

**Article 171** During the process of maritime towage, if loss is sustained by the tugowner or the tow party due to the fault of one party, the party at fault shall bear compensation liability; if loss is sustained due to the fault of both parties, each party shall bear compensation liability in proportion to the degree of their respective fault.  
Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, if the tugowner proves that the loss of the tow party was caused by any of the following circumstances, the tugowner shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) fault of the master, crew, pilot or other servants or agents of the tugowner in the navigation or management of the tug; or  
(2) fault of the tug in saving or attempting to save life or property at sea.  
The provisions of this Article shall apply only when the contract of maritime towage does not stipulate or stipulates otherwise.

<a id="art-172"></a>第一百七十二条　在海上拖航过程中，由于承拖方或者被拖方的过错，造成第三人人身伤亡或者财产损失的，承拖方和被拖方对第三人承担连带赔偿责任。除合同另有约定外，一方支付的赔偿超过其应当承担的比例的，对另一方有追偿权。

**Article 172** If, during the process of maritime towage, personal injury or death of a third party, or loss of or damage to property of a third party, is caused by the fault of the tugowner or the tow party, the tugowner and the tow party shall bear joint and several liability to the third party for compensation. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, if one party pays compensation in excess of the proportion it shall bear, it shall have the right of recourse against the other party.

<a id="art-173"></a>第一百七十三条　拖轮所有人拖带其所有的或者经营的驳船载运货物，经海路由一港运至另一港的，视为海上货物运输。

**Article 173** If the owner of a tug tows a barge owned or operated by it carrying cargo from one port to another by sea, such operation shall be deemed as carriage of goods by sea.

### 第八章　船舶碰撞

<a id="art-174"></a>第一百七十四条　船舶碰撞，是指船舶在海上或者与海相通的可航水域发生接触造成损害的事故。  
船舶与船舶以外的任何其他非用于军事或者政府公务的船艇之间发生碰撞，适用本章的规定。

**Article 174** Collision of ships means an accident in which ships come into contact with each other on the sea or other navigable waters connected therewith, causing damage.  
The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to collisions between a ship and any vessel other than a ship, which is not used for military or government public service purposes.

<a id="art-175"></a>第一百七十五条　船舶发生碰撞，当事船舶的船长在不严重危及本船和船上人员安全的情况下，对于相碰的船舶和船上人员应当尽力施救。  
碰撞船舶的船长应当尽可能将其船舶名称、船籍港、出发港和目的港通知对方。

**Article 175** When a collision occurs between ships, the master of each ship involved shall, insofar as such action does not seriously endanger the safety of his or her own ship and the persons on board, do his or her utmost to render assistance to the other ship and the persons on board.  
The master of each ship involved in the collision shall, as far as possible, communicate to the other ship the name of his or her own ship, its port of registry, the port of departure and the port of destination.

<a id="art-176"></a>第一百七十六条　船舶发生碰撞，是由于不可抗力或者其他不能归责于任何一方的原因或者无法查明的原因造成的，碰撞各方互相不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 176** Where a collision between ships is caused by force majeure or other causes not attributable to any party, or if the cause cannot be ascertained, the parties to the collision shall not bear compensation liability to each other.

<a id="art-177"></a>第一百七十七条　船舶发生碰撞，是由于一船的过错造成的，由有过错的船舶承担赔偿责任。

**Article 177** Where a collision between ships is caused by the fault of one ship, the ship at fault shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-178"></a>第一百七十八条　船舶发生碰撞，碰撞的船舶互有过错的，各船按照过错程度的比例承担赔偿责任；过错程度相当或者过错程度的比例无法判定的，平均承担赔偿责任。  
互有过错的船舶，对碰撞造成的船舶以及船上货物和其他财产的损失，依照前款规定的比例承担赔偿责任。碰撞造成第三人财产损失的，各船的赔偿责任均不超过其应当承担的比例。  
互有过错的船舶，对造成的第三人的人身伤亡，承担连带赔偿责任。一船支付的赔偿超过本条第一款规定的比例的，有权向其他有过错的船舶追偿。

**Article 178** Where a collision between ships occurs and the colliding ships are both at fault, each ship shall bear compensation liability in proportion to the degree of its fault; if the degrees of fault are equal or if the proportion of fault cannot be determined, the compensation liability shall be borne equally.  
  
Where ships are both at fault, they shall bear compensation liability for the loss of or damage to the ships, cargo on board and other property caused by the collision in accordance with the proportion specified in the preceding paragraph. Where the collision causes loss of or damage to the property of a third party, the compensation liability of each ship shall not exceed the proportion it shall bear.  
  
Where ships are both at fault, they shall bear joint and several liability for the personal injury or death of a third party caused by the collision. Where a ship has paid compensation exceeding the proportion specified in Paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall have the right to recover from the other ship(s) at fault.

<a id="art-179"></a>第一百七十九条　船舶因操纵不当或者不遵守航行规章，虽然实际上没有与其他船舶发生碰撞，但是使其他船舶以及船上的人员、货物或者其他财产遭受损失的，适用本章的规定。

**Article 179** Where a ship, by improper maneuvering or by failing to comply with navigation regulations, causes loss or damage to another ship or to persons, cargo or other property on board that ship, even though no actual collision occurred, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

### 第九章　海难救助

<a id="art-180"></a>第一百八十条　本章规定适用于对海上或者与海相通的可航水域内遇险的船舶或者其他财产进行的救助。  
船舶与船舶以外的任何其他非用于军事或者政府公务的船艇之间发生的救助关系，适用本章的规定。  
除[本法第一百八十五条](#art-185)关于撤销或者变更救助合同和第一百八十六条、第一百八十七条关于防止或者减轻生态环境损害的规定外，本章其他规定仅在救助合同没有约定或者没有不同约定时适用。

**Article 180** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to the salvage of ships or other property in distress at sea or in any navigable waters connected therewith.  
  
The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to a salvage relationship between a ship and any other vessel not used for military or governmental public service.  
  
Except for the provisions of Article 185 of this Law concerning the annulment or modification of salvage contracts and Articles 186 and 187 concerning the prevention or minimization of damage to the ecological environment, the other provisions of this Chapter shall apply only where the salvage contract does not stipulate otherwise or where there is no differing stipulation.

<a id="art-181"></a>第一百八十一条　本章下列用语的含义：  
（一）财产，是指非永久地和非有意地依附于岸线的任何财产，包括有风险的运费。  
（二）救助款项，是指依照本章规定，被救助方应当向救助方支付的任何救助报酬、酬金或者补偿。

**Article 181** For the purposes of this Chapter:  
(1) "Property" means any property not permanently and intentionally attached to the shoreline, including freight at risk.  
(2) "Salvage payment" means any salvage reward, remuneration or compensation payable by the salved party to the salvor in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-182"></a>第一百八十二条　本章规定不适用于海上已经就位的从事海底矿物资源的勘探、开发或者生产的固定式、浮动式平台和移动式近海钻井装置。

**Article 182** The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to fixed or floating platforms or to mobile offshore drilling units which, being already in position at sea, are engaged in the exploration, exploitation or production of seabed mineral resources.

<a id="art-183"></a>第一百八十三条　船长在不严重危及本船和船上人员安全的情况下，有义务尽力救助海上人命。

**Article 183** The master shall, insofar as he can do so without serious danger to his ship and the persons on board, be duty bound to render all possible assistance to save life at sea.

<a id="art-184"></a>第一百八十四条　救助方与被救助方就海难救助达成协议，救助合同成立。  
遇险船舶的船长有权代表船舶所有人订立救助合同。遇险船舶的船长或者船舶所有人有权代表船上财产所有人订立救助合同。

**Article 184** A salvage contract is concluded when the salvor and the salved party reach an agreement on salvage operations at sea.  
  
The master of a ship in distress shall have the authority to conclude a salvage contract on behalf of the shipowner. The master of a ship in distress or the shipowner shall have the authority to conclude a salvage contract on behalf of the owners of the property on board.

<a id="art-185"></a>第一百八十五条　有下列情形之一，一方当事人起诉或者双方当事人协议仲裁的，受理争议的法院或者仲裁机构可以撤销、变更救助合同：  
（一）合同在受胁迫或者危险情况的影响下订立，并且合同条款显失公平；  
（二）根据合同支付的救助款项明显过高或者过低于实际提供的救助服务。

**Article 185** Where one party prosecutes or both parties agree to arbitration under any of the following circumstances, the court or arbitration institution accepting the dispute may annul or modify the salvage contract:  
(1) The contract was concluded under the influence of duress or danger, and its terms are manifestly unfair;  
(2) The salvage payment under the contract is manifestly too high or too low in relation to the salvage services actually rendered.

<a id="art-186"></a>第一百八十六条　在救助作业过程中，救助方对被救助方负有下列义务：  
（一）以应有的谨慎进行救助；  
（二）以应有的谨慎防止或者减少生态环境损害；  
（三）在合理需要的情况下，寻求其他救助方援助；  
（四）接受被救助方要求其他救助方参与救助作业的合理要求，但是要求不合理的，原救助方的救助报酬金额不受影响。

**Article 186** During the course of salvage operations, the salvor shall be under the following obligations towards the salved party:  
(1) to carry out the salvage with due care;  
(2) to prevent or minimize damage to the ecological environment with due care;  
(3) to seek assistance from other salvors where reasonably necessary;  
(4) to accept the reasonable request of the salved party to seek the participation of other salvors in the salvage operations; provided that if such request is not reasonable, the amount of the original salvor's salvage reward shall not be affected.

<a id="art-187"></a>第一百八十七条　在救助作业过程中，被救助方对救助方负有下列义务：  
（一）与救助方通力合作；  
（二）以应有的谨慎防止或者减少生态环境损害；  
（三）获救的船舶或者其他财产被送至安全地点时，及时接受救助方提出的合理的移交要求。

**Article 187** During the course of salvage operations, the salved party shall be under the following obligations towards the salvor:  
(1) to cooperate fully with the salvor;  
(2) to prevent or minimize damage to the ecological environment with due care;  
(3) when the ship or other property salved has been brought to a place of safety, to accept the reasonable request of the salvor for redelivery in a timely manner.

<a id="art-188"></a>第一百八十八条　救助方对遇险的船舶和其他财产的救助，取得效果的，有权获得救助报酬；未取得效果的，除[本法第一百九十一条](#art-191)或者其他法律另有规定外，无权获得救助款项。

**Article 188** Where salvage operations carried out by the salvor in respect of a ship or other property in distress have a useful result, the salvor shall be entitled to a salvage reward. Where the salvage operations have no useful result, the salvor shall not be entitled to any salvage payment, except as otherwise provided for in Article 191 of this Law or in other laws.

<a id="art-189"></a>第一百八十九条　确定救助报酬，应当体现对救助作业的鼓励，并综合考虑下列各项因素：  
（一）船舶和其他财产的获救价值；  
（二）救助方在防止或者减少生态环境损害方面的技能和努力；  
（三）救助方的救助成效；  
（四）危险的性质和程度；  
（五）救助方在救助船舶、其他财产和人命方面的技能和努力；  
（六）救助方所用的时间、支出的费用和遭受的损失；  
（七）救助方或者救助设备可能面临的责任风险和其他风险；  
（八）救助方提供救助服务的及时性；  
（九）用于救助作业的船舶和其他设备的可用性和使用情况；  
（十）救助设备的备用状况、效能和设备的价值。  
救助报酬不得超过船舶和其他财产的获救价值。

**Article 189** The salvage reward shall be fixed with a view to encouraging salvage operations, taking into account the following factors comprehensively:  
(1) the salved value of the ship and other property;  
(2) the skill and efforts of the salvor in preventing or minimizing damage to the ecological environment;  
(3) the measure of success obtained by the salvor;  
(4) the nature and degree of the danger;  
(5) the skill and efforts of the salvor in salving the ship, other property and life;  
(6) the time used, expenses incurred and losses suffered by the salvor;  
(7) the risk of liability and other risks to which the salvor or the salvor's equipment may be exposed;  
(8) the promptness of the services rendered by the salvor;  
(9) the availability and use of ships or other equipment used for the salvage operations;  
(10) the state of readiness and efficiency of the salvor's equipment and the value thereof.  
The salvage reward shall not exceed the salved value of the ship and other property.

<a id="art-190"></a>第一百九十条　船舶和其他财产的获救价值，是指船舶和其他财产获救后的估计价值或者实际出卖的收入，扣除有关税款和海关、检疫、检验费用以及进行卸载、保管、估价、出卖而产生的费用后的价值。  
前款规定的获救价值，不包括获救的船员私人物品和旅客自带行李的价值。

**Article 190** The salved value of a ship and other property means the estimated value or the actual proceeds from the sale of the ship and other property after salvage, after deducting relevant taxes and customs, quarantine and inspection fees, as well as expenses incurred in discharging, storage, appraisal and sale.  
  
The salved value specified in the preceding paragraph shall not include the value of the personal effects of the crew members or the luggage of the passengers.

<a id="art-191"></a>第一百九十一条　对构成生态环境损害危险的船舶或者船上货物进行的救助，救助方依据[本法第一百八十九条](#art-189)规定获得的救助报酬，少于依据本条规定可以得到的特别补偿的，救助方有权从船舶所有人处获得相当于救助费用的特别补偿。  
救助方进行前款规定的救助作业，取得防止或者减少生态环境损害效果的，船舶所有人依照前款规定应当向救助方支付的特别补偿可以另行增加，增加的数额可以达到救助费用的百分之三十。受理争议的法院或者仲裁机构认为适当，并考虑[本法第一百八十九条](#art-189)第一款的规定，可以判决或者裁决进一步增加特别补偿数额；但是，在任何情况下，增加部分不得超过救助费用的百分之一百。  
本条所称救助费用，是指救助方在救助作业中直接支付的合理费用以及实际使用救助设备、投入救助人员的合理费用。确定救助费用应当考虑[本法第一百八十九条](#art-189)第一款第八项至第十项的规定。  
在任何情况下，本条规定的全部特别补偿，只有在超过救助方依据[本法第一百八十九条](#art-189)规定能够获得的救助报酬时，方可支付，支付金额为特别补偿超过救助报酬的差额部分。  
因救助方的过失未能防止或者减少生态环境损害的，可以全部或者部分剥夺救助方获得特别补偿的权利。  
本条规定不影响船舶所有人对其他被救助方的追偿权。

**Article 191** Where salvage operations are carried out in respect of a ship or cargo on board which threatened damage to the ecological environment, if the salvage reward obtained by the salvor pursuant to Article 189 of this Law is less than the special compensation that may be obtained under this Article, the salvor shall be entitled to special compensation from the shipowner equivalent to the salvor's expenses.  
  
Where the salvor, by carrying out salvage operations as specified in the preceding paragraph, has prevented or minimized damage to the ecological environment, the special compensation payable by the shipowner to the salvor under the preceding paragraph may be increased by an amount of up to 30 per cent of the expenses incurred. The court or arbitration institution accepting the dispute may, if it deems fair and just and having regard to the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 189 of this Law, increase the special compensation further; provided that in no event shall the total increase exceed 100 per cent of the expenses incurred by the salvor.  
  
For the purposes of this Article, "expenses" means the reasonable expenses directly incurred by the salvor in the salvage operations and the reasonable expenses for actually using the salvage equipment and deploying the salvage personnel. The determination of such expenses shall take into account the provisions of Items (8) to (10) of Paragraph 1 of Article 189 of this Law.  
  
In any event, the total special compensation under this Article shall be paid only if and to the extent that it exceeds the salvage reward obtainable by the salvor pursuant to Article 189 of this Law, the payment amount being the excess of the special compensation over the salvage reward.  
  
If the salvor, by reason of its fault, has failed to prevent or minimize damage to the ecological environment, the right of the salvor to special compensation may be totally or partially taken away.  
  
The provisions of this Article shall not affect the right of recourse of the shipowner against any other salved party.

<a id="art-192"></a>第一百九十二条　救助报酬的金额，应当由获救的船舶和其他财产的各所有人，按照船舶和其他各项财产各自的获救价值占全部获救价值的比例承担。

**Article 192** The amount of the salvage reward shall be borne by the respective owners of the salved ship and other property in proportion to the respective salved values of the ship and the other property to the total salved value.

<a id="art-193"></a>第一百九十三条　参加同一救助作业的各救助方的救助报酬，依据[本法第一百八十九条](#art-189)的规定标准，由各方协商确定；协商不成的，可以向法院起诉或者经各方协议申请仲裁。

**Article 193** The salvage reward to be paid to each of the salvors participating in the same salvage operations shall be determined by negotiation among them in accordance with the standards set out in Article 189 of this Law; if negotiation fails, prosecution may be brought in a court or arbitration may be applied for by agreement among the parties.

<a id="art-194"></a>第一百九十四条　在救助作业中救助人命的救助方，对获救人员不得请求酬金，但是有权从救助船舶或者其他财产、防止或者减少生态环境损害的救助方获得的救助款项中，获得合理的份额。

**Article 194** A salvor who has saved human life in connection with salvage operations shall not demand any remuneration from the persons saved, but shall be entitled to a fair share of the salvage payment obtained from the salvor of the ship or other property or from the salvor who prevented or minimized damage to the ecological environment.

<a id="art-195"></a>第一百九十五条　下列救助行为无权获得救助款项：  
（一）正常履行拖航合同或者其他服务合同的义务进行救助的，但是提供不属于履行上述义务的特殊劳务除外；  
（二）不顾遇险船舶的船长、船舶所有人或者其他财产所有人明确、合理的拒绝，仍然进行救助。

**Article 195** The following salvage operations shall not be entitled to any salvage payment:  
(1) salvage operations carried out in the normal performance of a towage contract or other service contract, except where special services are rendered which are not part of the performance of the above-mentioned contracts;  
(2) salvage operations carried out despite the express and reasonable refusal of the master, shipowner or other property owner of the ship in distress.

<a id="art-196"></a>第一百九十六条　因救助方的过失致使救助作业成为必需或者更加困难的，或者救助方有欺诈或者其他不诚实行为的，应当取消或者减少向救助方支付的救助款项。

**Article 196** If, by reason of the fault of the salvor, the salvage operations have become necessary or more difficult, or if the salvor has been guilty of fraud or other dishonest conduct, the salvage payment payable to the salvor shall be cancelled or reduced.

<a id="art-197"></a>第一百九十七条　被救助方在救助作业结束后，应当根据救助方的要求，对救助款项提供满意的担保。  
在不影响前款规定的情况下，获救船舶的船舶所有人应当在获救的货物交还前，尽力使货物的所有人对其应当承担的救助款项提供满意的担保。  
在未根据救助方的要求对获救的船舶或者其他财产提供满意的担保以前，未经救助方同意，不得将获救的船舶和其他财产从救助作业完成后最初到达的港口或者地点移走。

**Article 197** Upon the completion of the salvage operations, the salved party shall, at the request of the salvor, provide satisfactory security for the salvage payment.  
  
Without prejudice to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the owner of the salved ship shall, before the salved cargo is released, use its best endeavors to cause the owner of the cargo to provide satisfactory security for the salvage payment which the cargo owner shall bear.  
  
Unless satisfactory security has been provided for the salved ship or other property as requested by the salvor, the salved ship and other property shall not be removed from the port or place at which they first arrived after the completion of the salvage operations without the consent of the salvor.

<a id="art-198"></a>第一百九十八条　受理救助款项请求的法院或者仲裁机构根据具体情况，在合理的条件下，可以裁定或者裁决被救助方向救助方先行支付适当的金额。  
被救助方根据前款规定先行支付金额后，其根据[本法第一百九十七条](#art-197)规定提供的担保金额应当相应扣减。

**Article 198** The court or arbitration institution having jurisdiction over the claim for salvage payment may, in light of the specific circumstances and upon reasonable conditions, order or award that the salved party make an interim payment to the salvor of an appropriate amount.  
  
Where the salved party has made an interim payment in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the amount of security provided by it pursuant to Article 197 of this Law shall be reduced accordingly.

<a id="art-199"></a>第一百九十九条　对于获救满九十日的船舶和其他财产，被救助方不支付救助款项也不提供满意的担保的，救助方可以申请法院裁定强制拍卖；对于无法保管、不易保管或者保管费用可能超过其价值的获救的船舶和其他财产，可以申请提前拍卖。  
拍卖所得价款，在扣除保管和拍卖过程中的全部费用后，依照本法规定支付救助款项；剩余的金额，退还被救助方；无法退还、自拍卖之日起满一年又无人认领的，归国家所有；不足的金额，救助方有权向被救助方追偿。

**Article 199** If the salved party does not pay the salvage payment nor provide satisfactory security for the ship and other property that have been salved for ninety days, the salvor may apply to the court for an order of compulsory sale by auction. For the salved ship and other property which cannot be kept, are not easy to keep, or the cost of which may exceed the value thereof, application may be made for an earlier sale by auction.  
  
The proceeds of the sale by auction, after deducting all costs incurred in the keeping and the sale by auction, shall be used to pay the salvage payment in accordance with the provisions of this Law; the surplus shall be returned to the salved party; if the surplus cannot be returned and remains unclaimed for one year from the date of the sale by auction, it shall vest in the State; if there is a deficiency, the salvor shall have the right to recover the deficiency from the salved party.

<a id="art-200"></a>第二百条　同一船舶所有人的船舶之间进行的救助，救助方获得救助款项的权利适用本章规定。

**Article 200** The right of a salvor to salvage payment for salvage operations carried out between ships belonging to the same owner shall be governed by the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-201"></a>第二百零一条　国家有关主管机关从事或者控制的救助作业，救助方有权享受本章规定的关于救助作业的权利和补偿。

**Article 201** Where salvage operations are carried out or controlled by the relevant competent authority of the State, the salvor shall be entitled to enjoy the rights and compensation provided for in this Chapter in respect of the salvage operations.

### 第十章　共同海损

<a id="art-202"></a>第二百零二条　共同海损，是指在同一海上航程中，船舶、货物和其他财产遭遇共同危险，为了共同安全，有意合理地采取措施所直接造成的特殊牺牲、支付的特殊费用。  
在航程中或者在航程结束后发生的船舶或者货物因迟延所造成的损失，包括船期损失和行市损失以及其他间接损失，均不得列入共同海损。  
同一海上航程中的船舶、货物或者其他财产泄漏污染物造成的损失或者支付的费用，不得列入共同海损。

**Article 202** General average means a particular sacrifice or a particular expenditure intentionally and reasonably made or incurred for the common safety for the purpose of preserving from peril the ship, cargo and other property involved in a common maritime adventure.  
  
Losses arising from delay to the ship or cargo during or after the voyage, including loss of use of the ship and loss of market, and other indirect losses, shall not be allowed in general average.  
  
Losses or expenses caused by the leakage of pollutants from the ship, cargo or other property in the same maritime adventure shall not be allowed in general average.

<a id="art-203"></a>第二百零三条　船舶因发生意外、牺牲或者其他特殊情况而损坏时，为了安全完成本航程，驶入避难港口、避难地点或者驶回装货港口、装货地点进行必要的修理，在该港口或者地点额外停留期间所支付的港口费，船员工资、给养，船舶所消耗的燃料、物料，为修理而卸载、储存、重装或者搬移船上货物、燃料、物料以及其他财产所造成的损失、支付的费用，应当列入共同海损。

**Article 203** Where a ship is damaged by accident, sacrifice or other exceptional circumstances and, for the safe completion of the voyage, puts into a port or place of refuge or returns to the port or place of loading for necessary repairs, the port charges, wages and maintenance of the crew, fuel and stores consumed by the ship during the extra period of stay in such port or place, and the losses and expenses incurred in discharging, storing, reloading or moving the cargo, fuel, stores and other property on board for the purpose of repairs, shall be allowed in general average.

<a id="art-204"></a>第二百零四条　为代替可以列为共同海损的特殊费用而支付的额外费用，可以作为代替费用列入共同海损；但是，列入共同海损的代替费用的金额，不得超过被代替的共同海损的特殊费用。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-12" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 204** Extra expenses incurred in place of a particular expense that would have been allowable in general average shall be admitted in general average as substitute expenses; provided that the amount of the substitute expenses allowable in general average shall not exceed the amount of the particular expense which has been replaced.

<a id="art-205"></a>第二百零五条　提出共同海损分摊请求的一方应当承担举证责任，证明其损失应当列入共同海损。

**Article 205** A party claiming a general average contribution shall bear the burden of adducing evidence to prove that its loss shall be allowed in general average.

<a id="art-206"></a>第二百零六条　引起共同海损特殊牺牲、特殊费用的事故，可能是由航程中一方的过错造成的，不影响该方要求分摊共同海损的权利；但是，非过错方或者过错方可以就此项过错提出赔偿请求或者进行抗辩。

**Article 206** Where the event which gave rise to the particular sacrifice or particular expenditure in general average may have been caused by the fault of a party to the adventure, such party's right to claim a general average contribution shall not be affected; provided that the party not at fault or the party at fault may bring a claim for damages or make a defense in respect of such fault.

<a id="art-207"></a>第二百零七条　船舶、货物和运费的共同海损牺牲的金额，按照下列规定确定：  
（一）船舶共同海损牺牲的金额，按照实际支付的合理修理费，减除合理的以新换旧的扣减额计算。船舶尚未修理的，按照牺牲造成的合理贬值计算，但是不得超过估计的修理费。  
船舶发生实际全损或者修理费用超过修复后的船舶价值的，共同海损牺牲金额按照该船舶在完好状态下的估计价值，减除不属于共同海损损坏的估计的修理费和该船舶受损后的价值的余额计算。  
（二）货物共同海损牺牲的金额，货物灭失的，按照货物在装船时的价值加保险费加运费，减除由于牺牲无需支付的运费计算。货物损坏，在就损坏程度达成协议前售出的，按照货物在装船时的价值加保险费加运费，与出售货物净得的差额计算。  
（三）运费共同海损牺牲的金额，按照货物遭受牺牲造成的运费的损失金额，减除为取得该项运费本应支付，但是由于牺牲无需支付的营运费用计算。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第13条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第16条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-17" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第17条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第19条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 207** The amount of the general average sacrifice of the ship, cargo and freight shall be determined as follows:  
(1) The amount of the general average sacrifice of the ship shall be calculated on the basis of the reasonable cost of repairs actually paid, less a reasonable deduction for "new for old". Where the ship has not been repaired, the amount shall be calculated on the basis of the reasonable depreciation resulting from the sacrifice, but shall not exceed the estimated cost of repairs.  
Where the ship is an actual total loss or the cost of repairs would exceed the value of the ship when repaired, the amount of the general average sacrifice shall be calculated by the difference between the estimated sound value of the ship after deducting the estimated cost of repairing damage which is not general average, and the value of the ship after damage.  
(2) The amount of the general average sacrifice of cargo: if the cargo is lost, the amount shall be calculated on the basis of its value at the time of shipment plus insurance and freight, less any freight which would have been payable but which has been saved by reason of the sacrifice. If the cargo is damaged and sold before agreement on the extent of the damage, the amount shall be calculated on the basis of the difference between the value at the time of shipment plus insurance and freight and the net proceeds of the sale.  
(3) The amount of the general average sacrifice of freight shall be calculated on the basis of the loss of freight resulting from the sacrifice of the cargo, less the operating expenses which would have been incurred in earning such freight but which have been saved by reason of the sacrifice.

<a id="art-208"></a>第二百零八条　共同海损应当由受益方按照各自的分摊价值的比例分摊。  
船舶、货物和运费的共同海损分摊价值，分别按照下列规定确定：  
（一）船舶共同海损分摊价值，按照船舶在航程终止时的完好价值，减除不属于共同海损的损失金额计算，或者按照船舶在航程终止时的实际价值，加上共同海损牺牲的金额计算。  
（二）货物共同海损分摊价值，按照货物在装船时的价值加保险费加运费，减除不属于共同海损的损失金额和承运人承担风险的运费计算。货物在抵达目的港以前售出的，按照出售净得金额，加上共同海损牺牲的金额计算。  
旅客的行李和私人物品，不分摊共同海损。  
（三）运费分摊价值，按照承运人承担风险并于航程终止时有权收取的运费，减除为取得该项运费而在共同海损事故发生后，为完成本航程所支付的营运费用，加上共同海损牺牲的金额计算。

**Article 208** General average shall be contributed to by the parties benefited in proportion to their respective contributory values.  
  
The contributory values of the ship, cargo and freight shall be determined as follows:  
(1) The contributory value of the ship shall be calculated on the basis of the sound value of the ship at the termination of the voyage, less the amount of loss which is not general average, or on the basis of the actual value of the ship at the termination of the voyage plus the amount of the general average sacrifice.  
(2) The contributory value of cargo shall be calculated on the basis of the value of the cargo at the time of shipment plus insurance and freight, less the amount of loss which is not general average and the freight at the risk of the carrier. Where the cargo has been sold before arrival at the port of destination, its contributory value shall be calculated on the basis of the net proceeds of the sale plus the amount of the general average sacrifice.  
Luggage and personal effects of passengers shall not contribute to general average.  
(3) The contributory value of freight shall be calculated on the basis of the freight at the risk of the carrier and which the carrier is entitled to collect at the termination of the voyage, less the operating expenses incurred after the general average event in earning such freight for the completion of the voyage, plus the amount of the general average sacrifice.

<a id="art-209"></a>第二百零九条　未申报的货物或者谎报的货物，应当参加共同海损分摊；其遭受的特殊牺牲，不得列入共同海损。  
不正当地以低于货物实际价值作为申报价值的，按照实际价值分摊共同海损；发生共同海损牺牲时，按照申报价值计算牺牲金额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第18条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 209** Cargo which has not been declared or has been misdeclared shall be compelled to contribute to general average; the particular sacrifice suffered by such cargo shall not be allowed in general average.  
  
Where the value of the cargo has been improperly declared at a value lower than its actual value, the general average contribution shall be made on the basis of the actual value; where a general average sacrifice occurs, the amount of the sacrifice shall be calculated on the basis of the declared value.

<a id="art-210"></a>第二百一十条　对共同海损特殊牺牲和垫付的共同海损特殊费用，应当计算利息。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定.md#art-1" title="被《关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定.md#art-4" title="被《关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定》第4条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定.md#art-5" title="被《关于不满300总吨船舶及沿海运输、沿海作业船舶海事赔偿限额的规定》第5条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 210** Interest shall be allowed on the particular sacrifice in general average and on the particular expenses advanced in general average.

<a id="art-211"></a>第二百一十一条　经利害关系人要求，各分摊方应当提供共同海损担保。  
以提供保证金方式进行共同海损担保的，保证金应当交由海损理算机构以保管人名义存入银行。  
保证金的提供、使用或者退还，不影响各方最终的分摊责任。

**Article 211** Upon the request of any interested party, each party liable to contribute shall provide security for general average.  
  
Where the security for general average is provided in the form of a cash deposit, such deposit shall be deposited with an average adjuster in the name of a depositary in a bank.  
  
The provision, use or refund of the cash deposit shall not affect the ultimate liability to contribute of the parties.

<a id="art-212"></a>第二百一十二条　共同海损理算，适用合同约定的理算规则；合同未约定的，适用本章的规定。

**Article 212** The adjustment of general average shall be governed by the adjustment rules agreed upon in the contract; where the contract does not stipulate such rules, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.

### 第十一章　海事赔偿责任限制

<a id="art-213"></a>第二百一十三条　船舶所有人、海难救助方，对[本法第二百一十六条](#art-216)规定的海事赔偿请求，可以依照本章规定限制赔偿责任。  
船舶承租人、船舶经营人和船舶管理人适用本章有关船舶所有人的规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/海事与运输/最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海事赔偿责任限制相关纠纷案件的若干规定》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 213** A shipowner or a salvor may limit its liability for the maritime claims set out in Article 216 of this Law in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.  
  
The provisions of this Chapter relating to shipowners shall apply to a charterer, operator and manager of a ship.

<a id="art-214"></a>第二百一十四条　[本法第二百一十六条](#art-216)规定的海事赔偿请求向船舶所有人、海难救助方之外的人员提出，船舶所有人、海难救助方对该人员的行为、过错负有责任的，该人员可以依照本章规定限制赔偿责任。

**Article 214** Where a maritime claim specified in Article 216 of this Law is brought against a person other than the shipowner or salvor, such person may limit its liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter if the shipowner or salvor is liable for the act or fault of such person.

<a id="art-215"></a>第二百一十五条　被保险人依照本章规定可以限制赔偿责任的，对该海事赔偿请求承担责任的保险人，有权依照本章规定享受相同的赔偿责任限制。

**Article 215** Where the assured is entitled to limit its liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, the insurer liable for the maritime claim shall be entitled to the same limitation of liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-216"></a>第二百一十六条　下列海事赔偿请求，除[本法第二百一十七条](#art-217)和第二百一十八条另有规定外，无论赔偿责任的基础有何不同，责任人均可以依照本章规定限制赔偿责任：  
（一）在船上发生的或者与船舶营运、救助作业直接相关的人身伤亡或者财产的灭失、损坏，包括对港口工程、港池、航道和助航设施造成的损坏，以及由此引起的相应损失的赔偿请求；  
（二）海上货物运输因迟延交付或者旅客及其行李运输因迟延到达造成损失的赔偿请求；  
（三）与船舶营运或者救助作业直接相关的，侵犯非合同权利的行为造成其他损失的赔偿请求；  
（四）责任人以外的其他人，为避免或者减少责任人依照本章规定可以限制赔偿责任的损失而采取措施的赔偿请求，以及因此项措施造成进一步损失的赔偿请求。  
前款所列赔偿请求，无论提出的方式有何不同，均可以限制赔偿责任。但是，第四项涉及责任人以合同约定支付报酬的，不得援用本条赔偿责任限制的规定。

**Article 216** The following maritime claims, unless otherwise provided in Articles 217 and 218 of this Law, irrespective of the basis on which the liability is founded, the person liable may limit his liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter:  
(1) Claims in respect of personal injury or loss of life, or loss of or damage to property occurring on board or in direct connection with the operation of the ship or with salvage operations, including damage to port works, basins, waterways and navigational aids, and loss caused thereby;  
(2) Claims for loss resulting from delay in delivery of goods carried by sea or from delay in the arrival of passengers or their luggage;  
(3) Claims for other losses caused by an act infringing non-contractual rights in direct connection with the operation of the ship or salvage operations;  
(4) Claims of persons other than the person liable for measures taken to avoid or reduce loss for which the person liable may limit his liability in accordance with this Chapter, and further loss caused by such measures.  
Claims set out in the preceding paragraph may be subject to limitation of liability regardless of the manner in which they are brought. However, if a claim referred to in item (4) involves remuneration payable by the person liable under a contract, the provisions on limitation of liability in this Article shall not be invoked.

<a id="art-217"></a>第二百一十七条　本章规定不适用于下列各项：  
（一）对救助款项或者共同海损分摊的请求；  
（二）本法第十二章第二节规定的船舶油类污染损害责任的赔偿请求；  
（三）有关沉没、遇难、搁浅或者被弃船舶及船上物件、货物的打捞、清除、拆毁或者无害化费用的请求；  
（四）中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际核能损害责任限制公约规定的核能损害的赔偿请求；  
（五）核动力船舶造成的核能损害的赔偿请求；  
（六）船舶所有人或者海难救助方的受雇人提出的赔偿请求，依照相关法律船舶所有人或者海难救助方对该类赔偿请求无权限制赔偿责任，或者该项法律作了高于本章规定的赔偿责任限额的规定。

**Article 217** The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to:  
(1) Claims for salvage payments or contribution in general average;  
(2) Claims for liability for oil pollution damage from ships as provided for in Section 2 of Chapter XII of this Law;  
(3) Claims relating to the raising, removal, destruction or rendering harmless of a ship that is sunk, wrecked, stranded or abandoned, including anything that is or has been on board such ship, or relating to the cargo thereof;  
(4) Claims for nuclear damage covered by any international convention on limitation of liability for nuclear damage to which the People's Republic of China is a party or which it has acceded to;  
(5) Claims for nuclear damage caused by nuclear-powered ships;  
(6) Claims by servants of the shipowner or salvor, if under the law governing the contract of service the shipowner or salvor is not entitled to limit his liability in respect of such claims, or if he is by such law permitted to limit his liability to an amount greater than that provided for in this Chapter.

<a id="art-218"></a>第二百一十八条　经证明，引起赔偿请求的损失是因责任人的故意或者明知可能造成损失而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，该责任人无权依照本章规定限制赔偿责任。

**Article 218** A person liable shall not be entitled to limit his liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter if it is proved that the loss resulting from the claim arose from his act or omission done with intent to cause such loss, or recklessly and with knowledge that such loss would probably result.

<a id="art-219"></a>第二百一十九条　除[本法第二百二十条](#art-220)另有规定外，海事赔偿责任限制，依照下列规定计算赔偿责任限额：  
（一）关于人身伤亡的赔偿请求  
1.300总吨至500总吨的船舶，赔偿责任限额为500000计算单位；  
2.超过500总吨的船舶，500总吨以下部分适用本项第1目的规定，500总吨以上的部分，应当增加下列数额：  
501总吨至2000总吨的部分，每总吨增加1000计算单位；  
2001总吨至30000总吨的部分，每总吨增加800计算单位；  
30001总吨至70000总吨的部分，每总吨增加600计算单位；  
超过70000总吨的部分，每总吨增加400计算单位。  
（二）关于非人身伤亡的赔偿请求  
1.300总吨至500总吨的船舶，赔偿责任限额为250000计算单位；  
2.超过500总吨的船舶，500总吨以下部分适用本项第1目的规定，500总吨以上的部分，应当增加下列数额：  
501总吨至2000总吨的部分，每总吨增加500计算单位；  
2001总吨至30000总吨的部分，每总吨增加400计算单位；  
30001总吨至70000总吨的部分，每总吨增加300计算单位；  
超过70000总吨的部分，每总吨增加200计算单位。  
（三）依照第一项规定的限额，不足以支付全部人身伤亡的赔偿请求的，其差额应当与非人身伤亡的赔偿请求并列，从第二项数额中按照比例受偿。  
（四）在不影响第三项关于人身伤亡赔偿请求的情况下，就港口工程、港池、航道和助航设施的损害提出的赔偿请求，应当较第二项中的其他赔偿请求优先受偿。  
（五）不以船舶进行救助作业或者在被救船舶上进行救助作业的海难救助方，其赔偿责任限额按照1500总吨的船舶计算。  
不满300总吨的船舶，以及从事沿海作业的船舶，其赔偿责任限额由国务院交通运输主管部门制定，报国务院批准后施行。  
与船舶发生碰撞的非用于军事的或者政府公务的其他船艇，适用同一赔偿责任限额。

**Article 219** Unless otherwise provided in Article 220 of this Law, the limits of liability for maritime claims shall be calculated as follows:  
(1) Claims in respect of personal injury or loss of life:  
a. For a ship of 300 gross tonnage to 500 gross tonnage, the limit of liability shall be 500,000 Units of Account;  
b. For a ship exceeding 500 gross tonnage, the limit for the portion up to 500 gross tonnage shall be as provided in sub-paragraph (a) of this item, and for the portion exceeding 500 gross tonnage, the following amounts shall be added:  
For the portion from 501 to 2,000 gross tonnage, 1,000 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion from 2,001 to 30,000 gross tonnage, 800 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion from 30,001 to 70,000 gross tonnage, 600 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion exceeding 70,000 gross tonnage, 400 Units of Account per ton.  
(2) Claims in respect of loss of or damage to property:  
a. For a ship of 300 gross tonnage to 500 gross tonnage, the limit of liability shall be 250,000 Units of Account;  
b. For a ship exceeding 500 gross tonnage, the limit for the portion up to 500 gross tonnage shall be as provided in sub-paragraph (a) of this item, and for the portion exceeding 500 gross tonnage, the following amounts shall be added:  
For the portion from 501 to 2,000 gross tonnage, 500 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion from 2,001 to 30,000 gross tonnage, 400 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion from 30,001 to 70,000 gross tonnage, 300 Units of Account per ton;  
For the portion exceeding 70,000 gross tonnage, 200 Units of Account per ton.  
(3) Where the limit provided for in item (1) is insufficient to pay all claims for personal injury or loss of life, the balance shall rank proportionally with claims under item (2) for payment from the amount provided for in item (2).  
(4) Without prejudice to the priority for claims for personal injury or loss of life under item (3), claims in respect of damage to port works, basins, waterways and navigational aids shall be paid in priority to other claims under item (2).  
(5) For a salvor not operating from any ship or operating solely on the ship being salved, the limit of liability shall be calculated in accordance with a ship of 1,500 gross tonnage.  
For a ship of less than 300 gross tonnage and a ship engaged in coastal operations, the limits of liability shall be prescribed by the competent authority of the State Council for transport and shall come into force after approval by the State Council.  
For other vessels not used for military or governmental purposes that collide with a ship, the same limits of liability shall apply.

<a id="art-220"></a>第二百二十条　海上旅客运输的旅客人身伤亡赔偿责任限制，按照175000计算单位乘以船舶证书规定的载客定额计算赔偿责任限额。

**Article 220** The limit of liability for claims for personal injury or loss of life of passengers carried by sea shall be calculated by multiplying 175,000 Units of Account by the number of passengers which the ship is authorized to carry according to the ship's certificate.

<a id="art-221"></a>第二百二十一条　[本法第二百一十九条](#art-219)和第二百二十条规定的赔偿责任限额，适用于同一特定事故引起的，向船舶所有人、海难救助方本人和他们对其行为、过错负有责任的人员提出的请求的总额。

**Article 221** The limits of liability provided for in Articles 219 and 220 of this Law shall apply to the aggregate of all claims arising from the same distinct event and brought against the shipowner, the salvor personally and any person for whose act, neglect or default they are responsible.

<a id="art-222"></a>第二百二十二条　责任人要求依照本法规定限制赔偿责任的，可以在有管辖权的法院设立责任限制基金。基金数额分别为[本法第二百一十九条](#art-219)、第二百二十条规定的限额，加上自事故发生之日起至基金设立之日止的相应利息。  
本章规定的任一责任人设立的基金应当认定为由所有责任人设立。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 222** A person liable who seeks to limit his liability in accordance with the provisions of this Law may constitute a limitation fund in a court having jurisdiction. The amount of the fund shall be the limit provided for in Articles 219 and 220 of this Law, together with interest thereon from the date of the occurrence giving rise to the liability to the date of constitution of the fund.  
A fund constituted by any person liable under this Chapter shall be deemed to be constituted by all persons liable.

<a id="art-223"></a>第二百二十三条　责任人设立责任限制基金后，有权依据[本法第二百一十六条](#art-216)规定提出请求的任何人，不得对责任人的任何财产行使任何权利；已设立责任限制基金的责任人的船舶或者其他财产已经被采取保全措施，或者基金设立人已经提交担保的，法院应当及时解除保全措施或者退还担保。

**Article 223** Where a person liable has constituted a limitation fund, any person who has a claim as provided for in Article 216 of this Law shall not be entitled to exercise any rights against any property of the person liable; where a ship or other property of the person liable who has constituted a limitation fund has been arrested or where security has been provided by the person constituting the fund, the court shall promptly order the release of the arrest or the return of the security.

<a id="art-224"></a>第二百二十四条　享受本章规定的责任限制的人，就同一事故向请求人提出反请求的，双方的请求金额应当相互抵销，本章规定的赔偿责任限额仅适用于两个请求金额之间的差额。

**Article 224** Where a person entitled to limitation of liability under this Chapter has a counterclaim against the claimant arising from the same occurrence, the respective claims shall be set off against each other, and the provisions of this Chapter shall only apply to the balance, if any.

### 第十二章　船舶油污损害责任

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-225"></a>第二百二十五条　在中华人民共和国管辖海域和与海相通的可航水域的船舶油污损害赔偿，适用本章规定。  
船舶油污损害赔偿范围包括：  
（一）船舶油污造成该船舶之外的财产损害以及由此引起的收入损失；  
（二）为防止或者减轻船舶油污损害采取预防措施所发生的费用，以及预防措施造成的损失；  
（三）因油污造成生态环境损害所引起的收入损失；  
（四）对受污染的生态环境已采取或者将要采取合理恢复措施的费用。

**Article 225** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to compensation for damage from pollution of ships in the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China and in navigable waters connected with the sea.  
The scope of compensation for damage from oil pollution from ships includes:  
(1) damage to property other than the ship caused by oil pollution from ships and the loss of income resulting therefrom;  
(2) costs of preventive measures taken to prevent or mitigate oil pollution damage from ships, and losses caused by such preventive measures;  
(3) loss of income resulting from ecological environmental damage caused by oil pollution; and  
(4) costs of reasonable restoration measures already taken or to be taken for the polluted ecological environment.

<a id="art-226"></a>第二百二十六条　漏油船舶的船舶所有人承担船舶油污损害赔偿责任。  
船舶所有人证明油污损害完全由于下列情形之一造成，经过及时采取合理措施，仍然不能避免对生态环境造成损害的，不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）战争、武装冲突、恐怖活动或者不可抗拒的自然灾害；  
（二）第三人故意；  
（三）负责灯塔或者其他助航设备的管理部门，在执行职责时的疏忽，或者其他过错行为。  
船舶所有人证明油污损害全部或者部分是因受害人的过错造成的，可以全部或者部分免除船舶所有人对该受害人所承担的赔偿责任。

**Article 226** The owner of the ship from which oil has escaped shall bear liability for compensation for damage from oil pollution.  
Where the shipowner proves that the oil pollution damage was caused entirely by one of the following circumstances and, despite the timely adoption of reasonable measures, damage to the ecological environment could still not be avoided, the shipowner shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) war, armed conflict, terrorist activities or irresistible natural disaster;  
(2) intentional act of a third party; or  
(3) negligence or other wrongful act of the department responsible for the maintenance of lighthouses or other navigational aids in the performance of its duties.  
Where the shipowner proves that the oil pollution damage was caused wholly or partly by the fault of the victim, the shipowner's liability for compensation to that victim may be wholly or partly exempted.

<a id="art-227"></a>第二百二十七条　船舶所有人可以依照本章规定限制赔偿责任。但是，船舶油污损害是因船舶所有人故意或者明知可能造成损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，船舶所有人不得依照本章规定限制其赔偿责任。

**Article 227** The shipowner may limit liability for compensation in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. However, if the oil pollution damage is caused by the shipowner's intentional act or reckless act or omission with knowledge that such damage would probably result, the shipowner shall not be entitled to limit its liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-228"></a>第二百二十八条　两艘及以上船舶造成油污损害的，各船舶所有人应当对无法合理分开的损害承担连带责任。  
前款规定不影响被索赔的船舶所有人依照本章规定限制赔偿责任。

**Article 228** Where oil pollution damage is caused by two or more ships, each shipowner shall bear joint and several liability for the damage which cannot be reasonably separated.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not affect the right of the shipowner against whom a claim is made to limit liability in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-229"></a>第二百二十九条　国家建立船舶油污损害责任保险制度。船舶所有人应当按照国家有关规定投保船舶油污损害民事责任保险或者取得相应的财务保证。  
船舶油污损害赔偿请求可以直接向船舶所有人的责任保险人或者财务保证人提出，但是责任保险人、财务保证人承担的赔偿责任不超过[本法第二百三十三条](#art-233)、第二百三十九条规定的赔偿责任限额。船舶所有人丧失本章规定的限制赔偿责任权利的，不影响其责任保险人或者财务保证人援用本章规定限制赔偿责任。  
对于前款规定的船舶油污损害赔偿请求，责任保险人或者财务保证人有权以损害是因船舶所有人的故意造成的进行抗辩，并有权援用船舶所有人除破产或者清算之外的理由进行抗辩。  
国家依法完善并实施船舶油污损害赔偿基金制度。

**Article 229** The State shall establish a system of compulsory insurance for liability for damage from oil pollution from ships. The shipowner shall, in accordance with the relevant State provisions, take out insurance for civil liability for damage from oil pollution from ships or obtain other appropriate financial security.  
Claims for compensation for damage from oil pollution from ships may be made directly against the insurer or financial guarantor of the shipowner's liability, provided that the compensation liability of the insurer or financial guarantor shall not exceed the limitation of liability for compensation as provided in Articles 233 and 239 of this Law. Where the shipowner loses the right to limit liability under this Chapter, such loss shall not affect the right of its insurer or financial guarantor to invoke the provisions of this Chapter to limit liability.  
In respect of a claim for compensation for damage from oil pollution from ships as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the insurer or financial guarantor has the right to defend on the ground that the damage is caused by the intentional act of the shipowner, and has the right to invoke any ground of defence available to the shipowner other than bankruptcy or liquidation.  
The State shall, according to law, improve and implement the system of compensation fund for oil pollution damage from ships.

<a id="art-230"></a>第二百三十条　本章规定不影响船舶所有人向第三人追偿的权利。

**Article 230** The provisions of this Chapter shall not affect the right of the shipowner to claim recourse against a third party.

#### 第二节　船舶油类污染损害责任

<a id="art-231"></a>第二百三十一条　船舶载运散装油类造成的油污损害责任适用本节规定。  
为运输散装油类货物而建造或者改建的船舶，以及能够运输散装油类和其他货物的船舶，在实际运输散装油类货物及之后的任何航行期间，除能够证明船上没有散装油类货物的残余物外，适用本节规定。  
本节所称油类，是指作为运输货物或者船舶燃料的任何持久性烃类矿物油，包括润滑油。

**Article 231** The provisions of this Section shall apply to the liability for oil pollution damage caused by a ship carrying bulk oil.  
Ships constructed or converted for the carriage of bulk oil cargo, and ships capable of transporting bulk oil and other cargo, shall be subject to the provisions of this Section during any voyage when they are actually transporting bulk oil cargo and for any subsequent voyage, unless it can be proved that no residues of such bulk oil cargo remain on board.  
For the purposes of this Section, "oil" means any persistent hydrocarbon mineral oil, including lubricating oil, carried as cargo or used as fuel for the ship.

<a id="art-232"></a>第二百三十二条　下列人员对船舶油类污染损害不承担赔偿责任，但是损害是因其本人故意或者明知可能造成损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的除外：  
（一）船舶所有人的受雇人或者代理人；  
（二）引航员或者除船员外为船舶提供服务的其他任何人；  
（三）船舶承租人、船舶经营人或者船舶管理人；  
（四）经船舶所有人同意或者按照政府主管部门的指令进行救助作业的任何人；  
（五）除船舶所有人外，采取预防措施的任何人；  
（六）本条第三项至第五项规定人员的受雇人或者代理人。

**Article 232** The following persons shall not be liable for compensation for oil pollution damage caused by a ship, unless the damage resulted from their own intentional act or recklessness with knowledge that such damage would probably result:  
(1) the servants or agents of the shipowner;  
(2) the pilot or any other person who, not being a member of the crew, renders services for the ship;  
(3) the charterer, operator or manager of the ship;  
(4) any person performing salvage operations with the consent of the shipowner or on the instructions of a competent public authority;  
(5) any person taking preventive measures, other than the shipowner;  
(6) the servants or agents of the persons referred to in paragraphs (3), (4) and (5) of this Article.

<a id="art-233"></a>第二百三十三条　船舶载运油类造成油污损害的，船舶所有人对每一次事故按照下列规定计算赔偿责任限额：  
（一）5000总吨以下的船舶，赔偿责任限额为4510000计算单位；  
（二）5001总吨以上的船舶，5000总吨以下部分适用前项的规定，5001总吨以上的部分，每增加1总吨，增加631计算单位，但是赔偿责任限额在任何情况下不超过89770000计算单位。

**Article 233** Where a ship carrying oil causes oil pollution damage, the shipowner shall calculate the limit of compensation liability for each incident in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for a ship of less than 5,000 gross tonnage, the limit of compensation liability shall be 4,510,000 Units of Account;  
(2) for a ship of 5,001 gross tonnage or more, the part of less than 5,000 gross tonnage shall be subject to the provision of the preceding subparagraph, and for the part of 5,001 gross tonnage or more, each additional gross ton shall add 631 Units of Account, provided that the limit of compensation liability shall in no case exceed 89,770,000 Units of Account.

<a id="art-234"></a>第二百三十四条　船舶所有人或者其责任保险人、财务保证人援用本节赔偿责任限制的规定的，应当在有管辖权的法院设立责任限制基金。  
前款规定的任一责任人设立的基金，应当认定为由所有责任人设立。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 234** Where the shipowner or his liability insurer or financial guarantor invokes the provisions on limitation of liability under this Section, he shall constitute a limitation fund with the court having jurisdiction.  
A fund constituted by any of the persons liable as provided for in the preceding paragraph shall be deemed to be constituted by all persons liable.

<a id="art-235"></a>第二百三十五条　责任限制基金已经设立且船舶所有人有权限制其赔偿责任的，油污损害赔偿请求人不得申请法院对船舶所有人的财产行使保全措施；已经采取的财产保全措施应当解除，船舶所有人为避免或者解除保全措施已经提供的保证金或者其他担保应当退还。

**Article 235** Where a limitation fund has been constituted and the shipowner is entitled to limit his liability, a claimant for compensation for oil pollution damage shall not apply to the court for the exercise of preservation measures against the property of the shipowner; any property preservation measures already taken shall be lifted, and any security or other guarantee already provided by the shipowner to avoid or lift such preservation measures shall be returned.

<a id="art-236"></a>第二百三十六条　油污损害赔偿请求人应当按照损害赔偿额的比例分配责任限制基金。  
责任限制基金分配之前，船舶所有人或者其责任保险人、财务保证人已经向油污损害赔偿请求人作出赔付的，或者由第三人作出赔付的，在赔付的数额内代位取得该请求人所具有的权利。  
船舶所有人采取预防措施的费用以及因采取预防措施而造成的损失，与其他油污损害赔偿请求人在责任限制基金分配中处于同等地位。

**Article 236** Claimants for compensation for oil pollution damage shall apportion the limitation fund in proportion to the amounts of their established claims.  
If, before the distribution of the limitation fund, the shipowner or his liability insurer or financial guarantor has made payment to a claimant for compensation for oil pollution damage, or a third party has made such payment, the payer shall, to the extent of the amount paid, subrogate to the rights which that claimant would have enjoyed.  
The expenses incurred by the shipowner in taking preventive measures and the losses caused by such measures shall rank equally with other claims for compensation for oil pollution damage in the distribution of the limitation fund.

#### 第三节　船舶燃油污染损害责任

<a id="art-237"></a>第二百三十七条　本章第二节规定的船舶装载的非持久性燃油以及除第二节规定的船舶之外的其他船舶装载的燃油造成的油污损害责任适用本节规定。  
本节所称燃油，是指作为船舶燃料的烃类矿物油，包括润滑油。  
本节关于船舶所有人的规定，适用于光船承租人、船舶经营人和船舶管理人。

**Article 237** The provisions of this Section shall apply to the liability for oil pollution damage caused by non-persistent fuel oil loaded on ships as specified in Section 2 of this Chapter, and by fuel oil loaded on ships other than those specified in Section 2.  
For the purposes of this Section, "fuel oil" means hydrocarbon mineral oil used as fuel for the ship, including lubricating oil.  
The provisions of this Section concerning the shipowner shall apply to a bareboat charterer, ship operator and ship manager.

<a id="art-238"></a>第二百三十八条　下列人员对本节规定的燃油污染损害不承担赔偿责任，但是损害是因其本人故意或者明知可能造成损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的除外：  
（一）船舶所有人的受雇人或者代理人；  
（二）引航员或者除船员外为船舶提供服务的其他任何人；  
（三）船舶航次承租人或者定期承租人；  
（四）经船舶所有人同意或者按照政府主管部门的指令进行救助作业的任何人；  
（五）除船舶所有人外，采取预防措施的任何人；  
（六）本条第三项至第五项规定人员的受雇人或者代理人。

**Article 238** The following persons shall not be liable for compensation for fuel oil pollution damage provided for in this Section, unless the damage resulted from their own intentional act or recklessness with knowledge that such damage would probably result:  
(1) the servants or agents of the shipowner;  
(2) the pilot or any other person who, not being a member of the crew, renders services for the ship;  
(3) a voyage charterer or time charterer of the ship;  
(4) any person performing salvage operations with the consent of the shipowner or on the instructions of a competent public authority;  
(5) any person taking preventive measures, other than the shipowner;  
(6) the servants or agents of the persons referred to in paragraphs (3), (4) and (5) of this Article.

<a id="art-239"></a>第二百三十九条　本节规定的燃油污染损害赔偿责任限额适用本法第十一章的规定。  
同一事故造成燃油污染损害和[本法第二百一十六条](#art-216)规定的可以限制赔偿责任的其他损害的，责任人有权依据[本法第二百一十九条](#art-219)的规定在同一赔偿责任限额内限制赔偿责任。

**Article 239** The limits of liability for fuel oil pollution damage provided for in this Section shall be subject to the provisions of Chapter XI of this Law.  
Where an incident causes both fuel oil pollution damage and other damage for which liability may be limited as provided for in Article 216 of this Law, the person liable shall be entitled to limit his liability within the same limit in accordance with the provisions of Article 219 of this Law.

### 第十三章　海上保险合同

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-240"></a>第二百四十条　海上保险合同，是指保险人按照约定，对被保险人遭受海上保险事故造成保险标的的损失和产生的责任负责赔偿，而由被保险人支付保险费的合同。  
海上保险事故，是指保险人与被保险人约定的任何海上事故，包括与海上航行有关的发生于内河或者陆上的事故。

**Article 240** A marine insurance contract is a contract whereby the insurer agrees to indemnify the assured against loss of or liability arising from a marine perils to the subject matter insured, as agreed, and the assured pays the premium.  
Marine perils mean any perils of the sea agreed upon between the insurer and the assured, including perils occurring on inland waters or on land which are incidental to a sea voyage.

<a id="art-241"></a>第二百四十一条　海上保险合同的内容，主要包括下列各项：  
（一）保险人名称；  
（二）被保险人名称；  
（三）保险标的；  
（四）保险价值；  
（五）保险金额；  
（六）保险责任和除外责任；  
（七）保险期间；  
（八）保险费。

**Article 241** The contents of a marine insurance contract shall mainly include the following items:  
(1) name of the insurer;  
(2) name of the assured;  
(3) subject matter insured;  
(4) insured value;  
(5) sum insured;  
(6) insurance coverage and exclusions;  
(7) period of insurance;  
(8) premium.

<a id="art-242"></a>第二百四十二条　下列各项可以作为保险标的：  
（一）船舶；  
（二）货物；  
（三）船舶营运收入，包括运费、租金、旅客票款；  
（四）货物预期利润；  
（五）船员工资和其他报酬；  
（六）对第三人的责任；  
（七）由于发生海上保险事故可能受到损失的其他财产和产生的责任、费用。  
保险人可以将对前款保险标的的保险进行再保险。除合同另有约定外，原被保险人不得享有再保险的利益。

**Article 242** The following items may be the subject matter insured:  
(1) a ship;  
(2) cargo;  
(3) the operating revenue of a ship, including freight, charter hire and passenger ticket fare;  
(4) the expected profit on cargo;  
(5) the wages and other remuneration of the crew;  
(6) liability to a third party;  
(7) other property which may sustain loss and liability or expenses arising from the occurrence of a marine peril.  
The insurer may reinsure the insurance of the subject matter insured mentioned in the preceding paragraph. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the original assured shall not benefit from the reinsurance.

<a id="art-243"></a>第二百四十三条　保险标的的保险价值由保险人与被保险人书面约定；保险标的发生损失时，以约定的保险价值为赔偿计算标准。  
保险人与被保险人未约定保险价值的，保险价值按照下列规定计算：  
（一）船舶的保险价值，是保险责任开始时船舶的价值，包括船壳、机器、设备的价值，以及船上燃料、物料、索具、给养、淡水的价值和保险费的总和；  
（二）货物的保险价值，是保险责任开始时货物在起运地的发票价格或者非贸易商品在起运地的实际价值以及运费和保险费的总和；  
（三）运费的保险价值，是保险责任开始时承运人应收运费总额和保险费的总和；  
（四）其他保险标的的保险价值，是保险责任开始时保险标的的实际价值和保险费的总和。

**Article 243** The insured value of the subject matter insured shall be agreed in writing between the insurer and the assured. Where the subject matter insured sustains loss, the agreed insured value shall be the basis for calculating compensation.  
Where the insured value is not agreed between the insurer and the assured, the insured value shall be calculated in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) the insured value of a ship shall be the value of the ship at the commencement of the insurance liability, including the value of the hull, machinery, equipment, and the value of fuel, stores, rigging, provisions, fresh water on board, plus the premium;  
(2) the insured value of cargo shall be the invoice price of the cargo at the place of shipment at the commencement of the insurance liability, or the actual value of non-trade goods at the place of shipment, plus freight and premium;  
(3) the insured value of freight shall be the total amount of freight receivable by the carrier at the commencement of the insurance liability, plus the premium;  
(4) the insured value of other subject matter insured shall be the actual value of such subject matter at the commencement of the insurance liability, plus the premium.

<a id="art-244"></a>第二百四十四条　保险金额由保险人与被保险人约定。保险金额不得超过保险价值；超过保险价值的，超过部分无效。

**Article 244** The sum insured shall be agreed between the insurer and the assured. The sum insured shall not exceed the insured value; if the sum insured exceeds the insured value, the part in excess shall be null and void.

<a id="art-245"></a>第二百四十五条　建造中的船舶保险合同适用本章有关规定。  
建造中的船舶保险合同，是指保险人按照约定，对被保险人在船厂建造、试航和交船过程中的船舶遭受海上保险事故造成的损失和产生的责任负责赔偿，而由被保险人支付保险费的合同。  
建造中的船舶包括符合船舶建造合同约定，用于建造船舶的材料、机械和设备等。

**Article 245** The relevant provisions of this Chapter shall apply to an insurance contract for a ship under construction.  
An insurance contract for a ship under construction is a contract whereby the insurer agrees to indemnify the assured against loss of or liability arising from a marine peril to a ship which is under construction at the shipyard, undergoing trial voyages or delivery, as agreed, and the assured pays the premium.  
A ship under construction includes materials, machinery and equipment conforming to the ship construction contract and used for the construction of the ship.

#### 第二节　合同的订立、解除和转让

<a id="art-246"></a>第二百四十六条　被保险人提出保险要求，经保险人同意承保，并就海上保险合同的条款达成协议后，合同成立。保险人应当及时向被保险人签发保险单或者其他保险单证，并在保险单或者其他保险单证中载明当事人双方约定的合同内容。

**Article 246** After the assured makes a proposal for insurance, the insurer agrees to underwrite and an agreement is reached on the terms of the marine insurance contract, the contract shall be concluded. The insurer shall promptly issue an insurance policy or other insurance certificate to the assured, and shall specify in the policy or other certificate the contract contents agreed upon by the parties.

<a id="art-247"></a>第二百四十七条　合同订立前，被保险人应当将其知道的或者在通常业务中应当知道的有关影响保险人据以确定保险费率或者确定是否同意承保的重要情况，如实告知保险人。  
保险人知道或者在通常业务中应当知道的相关情况，保险人没有询问的，被保险人无需告知。

**Article 247** Before the contract is concluded, the assured shall, to the best of his knowledge and in the ordinary course of business, disclose to the insurer truthfully all material circumstances which are known to him or ought to be known to him in the ordinary course of business and which influence the insurer in determining the premium rate or whether to agree to underwrite.  
Where the insurer knows or ought to know in the ordinary course of business the relevant circumstances, and the insurer has not inquired about them, the assured need not disclose them.

<a id="art-248"></a>第二百四十八条　因被保险人的故意，未将[本法第二百四十七条](#art-247)第一款规定的重要情况如实告知保险人的，保险人有权解除合同，并不退还保险费。合同解除前发生海上保险事故造成损失的，保险人不承担赔偿责任。  
非因被保险人的故意，未将[本法第二百四十七条](#art-247)第一款规定的重要情况如实告知保险人的，保险人有权解除合同或者要求相应增加保险费。保险人在保险责任开始前解除合同的，应当退还全部保险费，但是有权收取手续费。保险人在保险责任开始后解除合同的，应当将自合同解除之日起至保险期间届满之日止的保险费退还被保险人，但是解除航次保险合同的，保险人可以不退还保险费。对于合同解除前发生海上保险事故造成的损失，保险人应当承担赔偿责任，但是未告知或者错误告知的重要情况对海上保险事故的发生有影响的除外。  
本条规定的合同解除权，自保险人知道或者应当知道有解除事由之日起超过三十日不行使而消灭。

**Article 248** If, due to the intent of the assured, the material circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 247 of this Law are not truthfully disclosed to the insurer, the insurer shall be entitled to rescind the contract and shall not refund the premium. In the event of loss caused by a marine peril occurring before the rescission of the contract, the insurer shall not be liable for compensation.  
If, not due to the intent of the assured, the material circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 247 of this Law are not truthfully disclosed to the insurer, the insurer shall be entitled to rescind the contract or demand a corresponding increase in the premium. If the insurer rescinds the contract before the commencement of the insurance liability, the insurer shall refund the full premium, but shall be entitled to charge handling fees. If the insurer rescinds the contract after the commencement of the insurance liability, the insurer shall refund the premium for the period from the date of rescission to the expiry of the insurance period to the assured; however, if a voyage insurance contract is rescinded, the insurer may not refund the premium. For the loss caused by a marine peril occurring before the rescission of the contract, the insurer shall be liable for compensation, except where the non-disclosed or misstated material circumstance had an effect on the occurrence of the marine peril.  
The right of rescission provided for in this Article shall be extinguished if it is not exercised within thirty days from the date on which the insurer knows or ought to know the grounds for rescission.

<a id="art-249"></a>第二百四十九条　海上保险合同采用保险人提供的格式条款的，对于保险合同中免除或者减轻保险人责任等与被保险人有重大利害关系的条款，保险人在订立合同时应当作出足以引起被保险人注意的提示。被保险人要求说明的，保险人对该条款应当予以明确说明。  
保险人未依照前款规定履行提示或者说明义务，致使被保险人没有注意或者理解与其有重大利害关系的条款的，被保险人可以主张该条款不成为合同的内容，但是被保险人知道或者应当知道该条款内容的除外。

**Article 249** Where a marine insurance contract adopts standard terms provided by the insurer, with respect to the terms in the insurance contract that exclude or limit the liability of the insurer and other terms having a major bearing on the interests of the assured, the insurer shall, at the time of concluding the contract, give a warning sufficient to draw the attention of the assured. If the assured requests an explanation, the insurer shall clearly explain such terms.  
If the insurer fails to perform the obligation of warning or explanation as provided for in the preceding paragraph, with the result that the assured does not notice or understand the terms having a major bearing on his interests, the assured may claim that such terms do not form part of the contract, unless the assured knew or ought to have known the content of such terms.

<a id="art-250"></a>第二百五十条　订立合同时，被保险人已经知道或者应当知道保险标的已经因发生海上保险事故而遭受损失的，保险人不承担赔偿责任，但是有权收取保险费；保险人已经知道或者应当知道保险标的已经不可能因发生海上保险事故而遭受损失的，被保险人有权收回已经支付的保险费。

**Article 250** Where, at the time of concluding the contract, the insured has known or ought to have known that the subject matter insured has already suffered a loss due to a marine insurance accident, the insurer shall not bear compensation liability, but shall have the right to collect the premium; where the insurer has known or ought to have known that the subject matter insured could not possibly suffer a loss due to a marine insurance accident, the insured shall have the right to recover the premium already paid.

<a id="art-251"></a>第二百五十一条　被保险人对同一保险标的、同一保险利益就同一海上保险事故向两个以上保险人重复订立合同，致使该保险标的的保险金额总和超过保险价值的，除合同另有约定外，被保险人可以向任何保险人提出赔偿请求，但是被保险人获得的赔偿金额总和不得超过保险标的的受损价值。  
各保险人按照其承保的保险金额同保险金额总和的比例承担赔偿责任；任何一个保险人支付的赔偿金额超过其应当承担的赔偿责任的，有权向未按照其应当承担的赔偿责任支付赔偿金额的保险人追偿。  
本条第一款所称保险价值，以各保险合同中最高的保险价值为准；受损价值是指按照各保险合同中最高的保险价值为标准计算所得的受损额。

**Article 251** Where the insured concludes multiple contracts with two or more insurers on the same subject matter insured, the same insurable interest and the same marine insurance accident, resulting in the total sum insured of the subject matter insured exceeding the insurance value, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insured may claim compensation from any insurer, but the total amount of compensation obtained by the insured shall not exceed the damaged value of the subject matter insured.  
Each insurer shall bear compensation liability in proportion to the sum insured it underwrites to the total sum insured; if any insurer pays compensation in an amount exceeding the compensation liability it shall bear, it shall have the right to claim recourse from the insurer that has not paid the compensation in accordance with the compensation liability it shall bear.  
The insurance value referred to in the first paragraph of this Article shall be the highest insurance value among the various insurance contracts; the damaged value means the amount of damage calculated based on the highest insurance value among the various insurance contracts.

<a id="art-252"></a>第二百五十二条　保险责任开始前，被保险人可以要求解除合同，但是应当向保险人支付手续费，保险人应当退还保险费。

**Article 252** Before the insurance liability commences, the insured may request dissolution of the contract, but shall pay a handling fee to the insurer, and the insurer shall refund the premium.

<a id="art-253"></a>第二百五十三条　除法律另有规定或者合同另有约定外，保险责任开始后，被保险人和保险人均不得解除合同。  
根据合同约定在保险责任开始后可以解除合同的，被保险人要求解除合同，保险人有权收取自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止的保险费，剩余部分予以退还；保险人要求解除合同，应当将自合同解除之日起至保险期间届满之日止的保险费退还被保险人。

**Article 253** Unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed in the contract, after the insurance liability commences, neither the insured nor the insurer may dissolve the contract.  
Where dissolution of the contract after the insurance liability commences is permitted under the contract, if the insured requests dissolution, the insurer shall have the right to collect the premium from the date of commencement of insurance liability to the date of dissolution, and shall refund the remaining portion; if the insurer requests dissolution, it shall refund to the insured the premium from the date of dissolution to the expiry of the insurance period.

<a id="art-254"></a>第二百五十四条　海上货物运输和船舶的航次保险，保险责任开始后，被保险人不得要求解除合同。

**Article 254** For voyage insurance of marine cargo transportation and vessels, after the insurance liability commences, the insured may not request dissolution of the contract.

<a id="art-255"></a>第二百五十五条　海上货物运输保险合同可以由被保险人背书或者以其他方式转让，合同的权利义务随之转移。合同转让时尚未支付保险费的，被保险人和合同受让人承担连带支付责任。

**Article 255** A marine cargo transportation insurance contract may be transferred by the insured through endorsement or other means, and the rights and obligations under the contract shall be transferred accordingly. If the premium has not been paid at the time of transfer, the insured and the assignee of the contract shall bear joint and several liability for payment.

<a id="art-256"></a>第二百五十六条　因船舶转让而转让船舶保险合同的，应当取得保险人同意。未经保险人同意，船舶保险合同从船舶转让时起解除；船舶转让发生在航次之中的，船舶保险合同至航次终了时解除。  
合同解除后，保险人应当将自合同解除之日起至保险期间届满之日止的保险费退还被保险人。

**Article 256** Where a vessel insurance contract is transferred due to the transfer of the vessel, the consent of the insurer shall be obtained. If the consent of the insurer is not obtained, the vessel insurance contract shall be dissolved from the time of the transfer of the vessel; if the transfer of the vessel occurs during a voyage, the vessel insurance contract shall be dissolved at the end of the voyage.  
After the contract is dissolved, the insurer shall refund to the insured the premium from the date of dissolution to the expiry of the insurance period.

<a id="art-257"></a>第二百五十七条　预约保险合同是指保险人按照约定对于被保险人将来一定期间内分批运输的货物承担保险责任，而由被保险人支付保险费的合同。  
预约保险合同应当采用书面形式订立。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 257** An open cover contract refers to a contract under which the insurer, in accordance with the agreement, undertakes insurance liability for goods to be shipped in batches by the insured within a certain future period, and the insured pays the premium.  
An open cover contract shall be concluded in writing.

<a id="art-258"></a>第二百五十八条　应被保险人要求，保险人应当对按照预约保险合同分批运输的货物分别签发保险单证。  
保险人分别签发的保险单证的内容与预约保险合同的内容不一致的，以分别签发的保险单证为准。

**Article 258** At the request of the insured, the insurer shall issue insurance certificates separately for goods shipped in batches under the open cover contract.  
If the contents of the insurance certificates separately issued by the insurer are inconsistent with those of the open cover contract, the separately issued insurance certificates shall prevail.

<a id="art-259"></a>第二百五十九条　预约保险合同下分批运输货物的名称、数量，装运货物的船名、航线，保险价值和保险金额等具体事项，被保险人应当在每一次运输前向保险人如实申报。  
因被保险人故意，未申报或者错误申报运输货物的，对于该次货物运输发生海上保险事故造成的损失，保险人不承担赔偿责任，但是有权收取保险费。  
非因被保险人的故意，未申报或者错误申报运输货物的，被保险人有权补报或者更正，补报或者更正不影响其向保险人要求保险赔偿的权利，但是保险人有权依据[本法第二百四十三条](#art-243)第二款规定的保险价值为赔偿计算标准。  
本条规定仅在预约保险合同没有约定或者没有不同约定时适用。

**Article 259** The insured shall truthfully declare to the insurer before each shipment the specific particulars such as the name and quantity of goods shipped in batches under the open cover contract, the name and route of the carrying vessel, the insurance value and the sum insured.  
If, due to the insured's intention, the insured fails to declare or incorrectly declares the shipped goods, the insurer shall not bear compensation liability for losses caused by a marine insurance accident occurring during such shipment, but shall have the right to collect the premium.  
If the failure to declare or the incorrect declaration of the shipped goods is not due to the insured's intention, the insured shall have the right to make a supplementary declaration or correction, and such supplementary declaration or correction shall not affect its right to claim insurance compensation from the insurer, provided that the insurer shall have the right to take the insurance value as specified in the second paragraph of Article 243 of this Law as the basis for calculating compensation.  
The provisions of this Article shall apply only where the open cover contract does not contain any agreement or does not contain a different agreement.

#### 第三节　被保险人的义务

<a id="art-260"></a>第二百六十条　除合同另有约定外，被保险人应当在合同订立后立即支付保险费；被保险人支付保险费前，保险人可以拒绝签发保险单证。

**Article 260** Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insured shall pay the premium immediately after the conclusion of the contract; before the insured pays the premium, the insurer may refuse to issue the insurance certificate.

<a id="art-261"></a>第二百六十一条　被保险人违反合同约定的保证条款的，保险人有权解除合同或者要求修改承保条件、增加保险费。保险人解除合同的，应当书面通知被保险人，合同自通知到达被保险人时解除。  
对于被保险人违反保证条款前发生海上保险事故造成的损失，保险人应当承担赔偿责任。  
对于被保险人违反保证条款之时至解除合同通知到达前发生海上保险事故造成的损失，保险人不承担保险责任，但是被保险人能够证明属于下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）被保险人违反保证条款对海上保险事故的发生没有影响；  
（二）海上保险事故发生在被保险人已纠正违反保证条款的行为之后。

**Article 261** If the insured breaches the warranty clause agreed in the contract, the insurer shall have the right to dissolve the contract or request modification of the underwriting conditions and an increase in the premium. If the insurer dissolves the contract, it shall notify the insured in writing, and the contract shall be dissolved when the notice reaches the insured.  
For losses caused by a marine insurance accident occurring prior to the insured's breach of the warranty clause, the insurer shall bear compensation liability.  
For losses caused by a marine insurance accident occurring from the time of the insured's breach of the warranty clause until the arrival of the notice of dissolution, the insurer shall not bear insurance liability, unless the insured proves that it falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the insured's breach of the warranty clause has no impact on the occurrence of the marine insurance accident;  
(2) the marine insurance accident occurred after the insured had rectified the breach of the warranty clause.

<a id="art-262"></a>第二百六十二条　发生海上保险事故，被保险人应当立即通知保险人，并采取必要的合理措施，防止或者减少损失。被保险人收到保险人发出的有关采取防止或者减少损失的合理措施的特别通知的，应当按照保险人通知的要求处理。  
对于被保险人违反前款规定所造成的扩大的损失，保险人不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 262** Upon the occurrence of a marine insurance accident, the insured shall immediately notify the insurer and take necessary and reasonable measures to prevent or mitigate the loss. If the insured receives a special notice from the insurer concerning the adoption of reasonable measures to prevent or mitigate the loss, it shall act in accordance with the requirements of the insurer's notice.  
For losses aggravated as a result of the insured's violation of the preceding paragraph, the insurer shall not bear compensation liability.

#### 第四节　保险人的责任

<a id="art-263"></a>第二百六十三条　发生海上保险事故造成损失后，保险人应当及时向被保险人支付保险赔偿。

**Article 263** After a marine insurance accident causes a loss, the insurer shall promptly pay insurance compensation to the insured.

<a id="art-264"></a>第二百六十四条　保险人赔偿海上保险事故造成的损失，以保险金额为限。保险金额低于保险价值的，在保险标的发生部分损失时，保险人按照保险金额与保险价值的比例承担赔偿责任。

**Article 264** The insurer's compensation for losses caused by a marine insurance accident shall be limited to the sum insured. If the sum insured is lower than the insurance value, when the subject matter insured suffers a partial loss, the insurer shall bear compensation liability in proportion to the ratio of the sum insured to the insurance value.

<a id="art-265"></a>第二百六十五条　保险标的在保险期间发生几次海上保险事故所造成的损失，即使损失金额的总和超过保险金额，保险人也应当赔偿。但是，对发生部分损失后未经修复又发生全部损失的，保险人按照全部损失赔偿。

**Article 265** Where the subject matter insured suffers losses caused by several marine insurance accidents during the insurance period, even if the total amount of losses exceeds the sum insured, the insurer shall still compensate. However, if a total loss occurs after a partial loss without repair, the insurer shall compensate on the basis of total loss.

<a id="art-266"></a>第二百六十六条　被保险人为防止或者减少根据合同可以得到赔偿的损失而支出的必要的合理费用，为确定海上保险事故的性质、程度而支出的检验、估价等合理费用，以及为执行保险人的特别通知而支出的费用，应当由保险人在保险标的损失赔偿之外另行支付。  
保险人对前款规定的费用的支付，以相当于保险金额的数额为限。  
保险金额低于保险价值的，除合同另有约定外，保险人应当按照保险金额与保险价值的比例，支付本条规定的费用。

**Article 266** The necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the insured for preventing or mitigating a loss compensable under the contract, the reasonable expenses for survey and assessment incurred to determine the nature and extent of a marine insurance accident, and the expenses incurred for following the insurer's special notice shall be paid by the insurer separately in addition to the compensation for the loss of the subject matter insured.  
The insurer's payment of the expenses specified in the preceding paragraph shall be limited to an amount equal to the sum insured.  
If the sum insured is lower than the insurance value, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer shall pay the expenses specified in this Article in proportion to the ratio of the sum insured to the insurance value.

<a id="art-267"></a>第二百六十七条　保险金额低于共同海损分摊价值的，保险人按照保险金额同分摊价值的比例赔偿共同海损分摊。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理无正本提单交付货物案件适用法律若干问题的规定》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 267** If the sum insured is lower than the general average contribution value, the insurer shall compensate for the general average contribution in proportion to the ratio of the sum insured to the contribution value.

<a id="art-268"></a>第二百六十八条　对于被保险人故意造成的损失，保险人不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 268** The insurer shall not bear compensation liability for losses intentionally caused by the insured.

<a id="art-269"></a>第二百六十九条　除合同另有约定外，因下列原因之一造成货物损失的，保险人不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）航行迟延、交货迟延或者行市变化；  
（二）货物的自然损耗、本身的缺陷和自然特性；  
（三）包装不当。

**Article 269** Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer shall not bear compensation liability for cargo losses caused by any of the following reasons:  
(1) delay in navigation, delay in delivery or change in market conditions;  
(2) natural wear and tear, inherent defect or natural characteristics of the cargo;  
(3) improper packing.

<a id="art-270"></a>第二百七十条　除合同另有约定外，因下列原因之一造成保险船舶损失的，保险人不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）船舶开航时不适航，但是在船舶定期保险中被保险人不知道且不应当知道的除外；  
（二）船舶自然磨损或者锈蚀。  
运费保险适用前款规定。

**Article 270** Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer shall not bear compensation liability for the loss of an insured vessel caused by any of the following reasons:  
(1) Unseaworthiness of the vessel at the time of sailing, except where the insured did not know and ought not to have known such unseaworthiness in the case of a time policy on the vessel;  
(2) Natural wear and tear or rust of the vessel.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to freight insurance.

#### 第五节　保险标的的损失和委付

<a id="art-271"></a>第二百七十一条　保险标的发生海上保险事故后灭失，或者受到严重损坏完全失去原有形体、效用，或者不能再归被保险人所拥有的，为实际全损。

**Article 271** Where the subject matter insured is lost after the occurrence of a marine insurance peril, or is so seriously damaged that it has completely lost its original form and utility, or can no longer be owned by the insured, such loss shall constitute an actual total loss.

<a id="art-272"></a>第二百七十二条　船舶发生海上保险事故后，认为实际全损已经不可避免，或者为避免发生实际全损所需支付的费用超过保险价值的，为推定全损。  
货物发生海上保险事故后，认为实际全损已经不可避免，或者为避免发生实际全损所需支付的费用与继续将货物运抵目的地的费用之和超过保险价值的，为推定全损。

**Article 272** Where, after the occurrence of a marine insurance peril to a vessel, it is deemed that an actual total loss is unavoidable, or the expenses necessary to avoid an actual total loss exceed the insured value of the vessel, such loss shall constitute a constructive total loss.  
Where, after the occurrence of a marine insurance peril to cargo, it is deemed that an actual total loss is unavoidable, or the sum of the expenses necessary to avoid an actual total loss and the expenses of continuing to carry the cargo to its destination exceeds the insured value of the cargo, such loss shall constitute a constructive total loss.

<a id="art-273"></a>第二百七十三条　不属于实际全损和推定全损的损失，为部分损失。

**Article 273** A loss that is neither an actual total loss nor a constructive total loss shall be a partial loss.

<a id="art-274"></a>第二百七十四条　船舶在合理时间内未从被获知最后消息的地点抵达目的地，除合同另有约定外，满六十日后仍没有获知其消息的，为船舶失踪。船舶失踪视为实际全损。

**Article 274** Where a vessel fails to arrive at its destination within a reasonable time from the place where it was last heard of, and no news of the vessel has been received after the expiration of sixty days, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the vessel shall be deemed missing. A missing vessel shall be deemed an actual total loss.

<a id="art-275"></a>第二百七十五条　保险标的发生推定全损，被保险人要求保险人按照全部损失赔偿的，应当在合理的时间内向保险人委付保险标的。保险人可以接受委付，也可以不接受委付，但是应当在合理的时间内将接受委付或者不接受委付的决定通知被保险人。保险人未在合理的时间内通知被保险人是否接受委付的，视为不接受委付。  
委付不得附带任何条件。委付一经保险人接受，不得撤回。

**Article 275** Where the subject matter insured suffers a constructive total loss, if the insured requests the insurer to indemnify on the basis of a total loss, the insured shall abandon the subject matter insured to the insurer within a reasonable time. The insurer may accept or refuse the abandonment, but shall notify the insured of its decision to accept or refuse the abandonment within a reasonable time. If the insurer fails to notify the insured within a reasonable time whether it accepts the abandonment, it shall be deemed to have refused the abandonment.  
The abandonment shall not be made subject to any conditions. Once the abandonment is accepted by the insurer, it shall not be revoked.

<a id="art-276"></a>第二百七十六条　保险人接受委付的，被保险人对委付财产的全部权利和义务转移给保险人。

**Article 276** Where the insurer accepts the abandonment, all rights and obligations of the insured in respect of the abandoned property shall be transferred to the insurer.

#### 第六节　保险赔偿的支付

<a id="art-277"></a>第二百七十七条　海上保险事故发生后，保险人向被保险人支付保险赔偿前，可以要求被保险人提供与确认海上保险事故性质和损失程度有关的证明和资料。

**Article 277** After the occurrence of a marine insurance peril, before the insurer pays insurance indemnity to the insured, the insurer may require the insured to provide evidence and materials relevant to confirming the nature of the marine insurance peril and the extent of the loss.

<a id="art-278"></a>第二百七十八条　保险标的发生保险责任范围内的损失是由第三人造成的，被保险人向第三人要求赔偿的权利，自保险人支付赔偿之日起，相应转移给保险人。  
被保险人应当向保险人提供必要的文件和其所需要知道的情况，并尽力协助保险人向第三人追偿。

**Article 278** Where a loss of the subject matter insured within the scope of insurance cover is caused by a third party, the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party shall, from the date the insurer pays the indemnity, be subrogated to the insurer to the extent of the indemnity paid.  
The insured shall provide the insurer with necessary documents and information known to it, and shall use its best efforts to assist the insurer in recovering from the third party.

<a id="art-279"></a>第二百七十九条　被保险人未经保险人同意放弃向第三人要求赔偿的权利，或者因过错致使保险人不能行使追偿权利的，保险人可以相应扣减保险赔偿。

**Article 279** Where the insured, without the consent of the insurer, waives its right to claim compensation from the third party, or by its fault causes the insurer to be unable to exercise the right of recourse, the insurer may deduct the corresponding amount from the insurance indemnity.

<a id="art-280"></a>第二百八十条　保险人支付保险赔偿时，可以从应支付的赔偿额中相应扣减被保险人已经从第三人取得的赔偿。  
保险人从第三人取得的赔偿，超过其支付的保险赔偿的，超过部分应当退还给被保险人。

**Article 280** When paying the insurance indemnity, the insurer may deduct the corresponding amount of compensation that the insured has already obtained from the third party from the amount payable.  
Where the compensation obtained by the insurer from the third party exceeds the insurance indemnity paid by it, the excess shall be refunded to the insured.

<a id="art-281"></a>第二百八十一条　发生海上保险事故后，保险人有权放弃对保险标的的权利，全额支付合同约定的保险赔偿，以解除对保险标的的义务。  
保险人行使前款规定的权利，应当自收到被保险人有关赔偿损失的通知之日起的七日内通知被保险人；被保险人收到通知前为避免或者减少损失而支付的必要的合理费用，由保险人偿还。

**Article 281** After the occurrence of a marine insurance peril, the insurer shall have the right to waive its rights to the subject matter insured and pay the full insurance indemnity as agreed in the contract in order to discharge its obligations in respect of the subject matter insured.  
Where the insurer exercises the right provided in the preceding paragraph, it shall notify the insured within seven days from the date of receipt of the insured's notice of the loss; any necessary and reasonable expenses paid by the insured before receipt of such notice to avoid or mitigate the loss shall be reimbursed by the insurer.

<a id="art-282"></a>第二百八十二条　除[本法第二百八十一条](#art-281)规定外，保险标的发生全损，保险人支付全部保险金额的，取得对保险标的的全部权利；但是，在不足额保险的情况下，保险人按照保险金额与保险价值的比例取得对保险标的的部分权利。

**Article 282** Except as provided in Article 281 of this Law, where the subject matter insured suffers a total loss and the insurer pays the full sum of insurance, the insurer shall acquire all rights to the subject matter insured; however, in the case of under-insurance, the insurer shall acquire a partial right to the subject matter insured in proportion to the ratio of the sum insured to the insured value.

### 第十四章　时效

<a id="art-283"></a>第二百八十三条　发生海事纠纷的，当事人可以依法提起诉讼或者申请仲裁。

**Article 283** In the event of a maritime dispute, the parties may, according to law, initiate prosecution or apply for arbitration.

<a id="art-284"></a>第二百八十四条　海上货物运输请求赔偿的请求权，时效期间为一年。其中，请求承运人、实际承运人赔偿的，时效自货物交付或者应当交付之日起计算；请求托运人、收货人或者运输单证持有人赔偿的，时效自权利人知道或者应当知道权利被侵害之日起计算。  
前款规定的时效期间已经届满或者至届满不足九十日的，被认定为负有责任的人对第三人享有的追偿请求权，时效期间为九十日，自追偿请求人解决原赔偿请求之日起计算。

**Article 284** The time limit for claims for compensation under contracts of carriage of goods by sea shall be one year. Among these, the time limit for claims against the carrier or actual carrier shall be calculated from the date of delivery or the date on which delivery ought to have been made; the time limit for claims against the shipper, consignee or holder of the transport document shall be calculated from the date on which the right holder knows or ought to have known the infringement of his right.  
Where the time limit provided for in the preceding paragraph has expired or is less than ninety days before expiry, the time limit for recourse claims of a person held liable against a third party shall be ninety days, calculated from the date on which the recourse claimant settles the original claim.

<a id="art-285"></a>第二百八十五条　海上旅客运输请求赔偿的请求权，时效期间为二年，分别按照下列规定计算：  
（一）有关旅客死亡的请求权，发生在运输期间的，自旅客应当离船之日起计算；因运输期间内的伤害而导致旅客离船后死亡的，自旅客死亡之日起计算，但是此期限自旅客离船之日起不得超过三年；  
（二）有关旅客人身伤害、行李灭失或者损坏的请求权，自旅客离船或者应当离船之日起计算。

**Article 285** The time limit for claims for compensation under contracts of carriage of passengers by sea shall be two years, calculated respectively as follows:  
(1) Claims relating to the death of a passenger occurring during carriage shall be calculated from the date on which the passenger ought to have disembarked; where death results from injury sustained during carriage and occurs after the passenger's disembarkation, the time limit shall be calculated from the date of death, provided that this period shall not exceed three years from the date of disembarkation;  
(2) Claims relating to personal injury of a passenger or loss of or damage to luggage shall be calculated from the date of disembarkation or the date on which the passenger ought to have disembarked.

<a id="art-286"></a>第二百八十六条　有关租船合同的请求权，时效期间为二年，自知道或者应当知道权利被侵害之日起计算。

**Article 286** The time limit for claims relating to charter parties shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the right holder knows or ought to have known the infringement of his right.

<a id="art-287"></a>第二百八十七条　有关海上拖航合同的请求权，时效期间为一年，自知道或者应当知道权利被侵害之日起计算。

**Article 287** The time limit for claims relating to towage contracts at sea shall be one year, calculated from the date on which the right holder knows or ought to have known the infringement of his right.

<a id="art-288"></a>第二百八十八条　有关船舶碰撞的请求权，时效期间为二年，自碰撞事故发生之日起计算。  
[本法第一百七十八条](#art-178)第三款规定的追偿请求权，时效期间为一年，自当事人连带支付损害赔偿之日起计算。  
依据[本法第一百七十九条](#art-179)规定产生的请求权或者追偿请求权的时效，适用本条规定。

**Article 288** The time limit for claims relating to collisions of ships shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the collision occurred.  
The time limit for recourse claims provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 178 of this Law shall be one year, calculated from the date on which the parties jointly pay the compensation for damage.  
The time limit for claims or recourse claims arising under Article 179 of this Law shall be subject to the provisions of this Article.

<a id="art-289"></a>第二百八十九条　有关海难救助的请求权，时效期间为二年，自救助作业终止之日起计算。

**Article 289** The time limit for claims relating to salvage at sea shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the salvage operations are terminated.

<a id="art-290"></a>第二百九十条　有关共同海损分摊的请求权，时效期间为一年，自共同海损理算结束之日起计算，但是不得超过从共同航程终止之日起六年。

**Article 290** The time limit for claims relating to contribution in general average shall be one year, calculated from the date on which the general average adjustment is completed, but shall not exceed six years from the termination of the common maritime adventure.

<a id="art-291"></a>第二百九十一条　根据海上保险合同向保险人请求保险赔偿的请求权，时效期间为二年，自知道或者应当知道海上保险事故发生之日起计算。

**Article 291** The time limit for claims against an insurer under a marine insurance contract for insurance compensation shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the right holder knows or ought to have known the occurrence of the marine insurance peril.

<a id="art-292"></a>第二百九十二条　有关船舶油污损害的请求权，时效期间为三年，自损害发生之日起计算，但是不得超过从造成损害的事故发生之日起六年。

**Article 292** The time limit for claims relating to oil pollution damage from ships shall be three years, calculated from the date on which the damage occurred, but shall not exceed six years from the date of the occurrence which caused the damage.

<a id="art-293"></a>第二百九十三条　在时效期间的最后六个月内，因不可抗力或者其他障碍不能行使请求权的，时效中止。自中止时效的原因消除之日起满六个月，时效期间届满。

**Article 293** Where, within the last six months of the limitation period, the right holder is unable to exercise his claim due to force majeure or other obstacles, the limitation period shall be suspended. The limitation period shall expire six months after the date on which the cause of suspension ceases to exist.

<a id="art-294"></a>第二百九十四条　时效因请求人提出履行请求、提起诉讼、申请仲裁或者被请求人同意履行义务而中断。  
请求人申请扣船的，时效自申请扣船之日起中断。  
自中断、有关程序终结时起，时效期间重新计算。

**Article 294** The limitation period shall be interrupted by the claimant bringing a claim for performance, initiating prosecution, applying for arbitration, or by the person against whom the claim is brought agreeing to perform the obligation.  
Where the claimant applies for arrest of a ship, the limitation period shall be interrupted from the date of the application for arrest.  
The limitation period shall run anew from the time of the interruption or the conclusion of the relevant proceedings.

### 第十五章　涉外关系的法律适用

<a id="art-295"></a>第二百九十五条　合同当事人可以选择合同适用的法律，但是法律另有规定的除外。合同当事人没有选择的，适用与合同有最密切联系的国家的法律。  
装货港或者卸货港位于中华人民共和国境内的国际海上货物运输合同，适用本法第四章的规定。

**Article 295** The parties to a contract may choose the law applicable to the contract, unless otherwise provided by law. Where the parties have not made such a choice, the law of the country most closely connected with the contract shall apply.  
For international contracts of carriage of goods by sea where the port of loading or the port of discharge is located in the territory of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of Chapter IV of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-296"></a>第二百九十六条　船舶所有权，适用船旗国法律。  
船旗发生变更的，变更后的船舶所有权，适用新船旗国法律，但是光船租赁引起的船旗变更除外。  
建造中的船舶所有权，船舶已经登记的，适用登记国法律；未经登记，适用船舶建造地法律。

**Article 296** Ownership of a ship shall be governed by the law of the flag State.  
Where the flag of a ship changes, the ownership of the ship after the change shall be governed by the law of the new flag State, except in the case of a change of flag resulting from a bareboat charter.  
For a ship under construction, where it is registered, ownership shall be governed by the law of the State of registration; where it is not registered, it shall be governed by the law of the State where the ship is being built.

<a id="art-297"></a>第二百九十七条　船舶抵押权，适用船旗国法律。  
船舶在光船租赁以前或者光船租赁期间，设立船舶抵押权的，适用原船舶登记国法律。  
建造中的船舶抵押权，船舶已经登记的，适用登记国法律；未经登记，适用船舶建造地法律。

**Article 297** A mortgage on a ship shall be governed by the law of the flag State.  
Where a ship is mortgaged before or during a bareboat charter, the mortgage shall be governed by the law of the State in which the ship was originally registered.  
For a mortgage on a ship under construction, where it is registered, it shall be governed by the law of the State of registration; where it is not registered, it shall be governed by the law of the State where the ship is being built.

<a id="art-298"></a>第二百九十八条　船舶优先权，适用法院地法律。

**Article 298** Maritime liens shall be governed by the law of the forum.

<a id="art-299"></a>第二百九十九条　船舶留置权，适用船舶被留置地法律。

**Article 299** A lien on a ship shall be governed by the law of the State where the ship is subject to the lien.

<a id="art-300"></a>第三百条　船舶优先权、船舶留置权和船舶抵押权相互之间的受偿顺序，适用法院地法律。

**Article 300** The priority of order among maritime liens, liens on ships, and mortgages on ships shall be governed by the law of the forum.

<a id="art-301"></a>第三百零一条　船舶碰撞责任，适用侵权行为地法律。船舶碰撞发生在公海的，适用法院地法律。同一国籍船舶发生碰撞的，碰撞船舶之间的责任，适用船旗国法律。  
船舶碰撞发生后，当事人协议选择适用法律的，按照其协议。

**Article 301** Liability for collisions of ships shall be governed by the law of the State where the tort occurred. Where a collision occurs on the high seas, the law of the forum shall apply. Where the colliding ships are of the same nationality, the liability between the colliding ships shall be governed by the law of the flag State.  
Where, after a collision, the parties agree to choose the applicable law, such agreement shall be followed.

<a id="art-302"></a>第三百零二条　共同海损理算，适用理算地法律。  
当事人可以协议选择共同海损分摊请求适用的法律；当事人未选择的，适用航程终止地法律。

**Article 302** General average adjustment shall be governed by the law of the State where the adjustment is made.  
The parties may agree to choose the law applicable to claims for contribution in general average; where the parties have not made such a choice, the law of the State where the voyage terminates shall apply.

<a id="art-303"></a>第三百零三条　海事赔偿责任限制，适用法院地法律。

**Article 303** Limitation of liability for maritime claims shall be governed by the law of the forum.

<a id="art-304"></a>第三百零四条　船舶油污损害责任，适用油污损害结果发生地法律。

**Article 304** Liability for oil pollution damage from ships shall be governed by the law of the State where the oil pollution damage occurs.

<a id="art-305"></a>第三百零五条　依照本章规定适用外国法律，不得损害中华人民共和国的公共利益。

**Article 305** The application of foreign law in accordance with this Chapter shall not harm the public interests of the People's Republic of China.

### 第十六章　附则

<a id="art-306"></a>第三百零六条　本法所称计算单位，是指国际货币基金组织规定的特别提款权；其人民币数额为法院判决之日、仲裁机构裁决之日或者当事人协议之日，按照国际货币基金组织发布的特别提款权对人民币的汇率计算得出的人民币数额。

**Article 306** The term "Unit of Account" as used in this Law means the Special Drawing Right as defined by the International Monetary Fund; the amount thereof in Renminbi shall be calculated as the amount of Renminbi converted at the exchange rate of Renminbi to the Special Drawing Right published by the International Monetary Fund on the date of the court's judgment, the date of the arbitration institution's award, or the date of the parties' agreement.

<a id="art-307"></a>第三百零七条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约与本法有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。  
中华人民共和国法律和中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约没有规定的，可以适用国际惯例。适用国际惯例，不得损害中华人民共和国的公共利益。

**Article 307** Where an international treaty to which the People's Republic of China is a party or which it has acceded to contains provisions different from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply, unless the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.  
Where neither the laws of the People's Republic of China nor the international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party or which it has acceded to contain provisions, international customs may apply. The application of international customs shall not harm the public interests of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-308"></a>第三百零八条　国家支持开展国际海运事务合作，推动海运业健康发展。  
任何国家或者地区在海上运输和船舶建造相关领域对中华人民共和国采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况对有关国家或者地区采取相应的措施。

**Article 308** The State supports international cooperation in maritime transport affairs and promotes the healthy development of the shipping industry.  
Where any State or region adopts discriminatory prohibitions, restrictions, or other similar measures against the People's Republic of China in the fields of maritime transport and shipbuilding, the People's Republic of China may, according to the actual circumstances, take corresponding measures against the State or region concerned.

<a id="art-309"></a>第三百零九条　船舶所有人、船舶经营人、船舶管理人或者船舶承租人等作为会员自愿组成互保组织，按照章程收取会费，对会员在船舶营运中发生的损失、产生的责任或者支付的费用承担赔偿责任。

**Article 309** Shipowners, ship operators, ship managers, or charterers, among others, may voluntarily form mutual insurance organizations as members, collect membership fees in accordance with the articles of association, and bear compensation liability for losses incurred, liabilities arising, or expenses paid by members in the operation of ships.

<a id="art-310"></a>第三百一十条　本法自2026年5月1日起施行。

**Article 310** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2026.
