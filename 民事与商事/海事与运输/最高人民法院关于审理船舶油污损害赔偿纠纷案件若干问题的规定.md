# 最高人民法院关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕14号  
**公布日期**：2011-05-04  
**生效日期**：2011-07-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2011年1月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1509次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年7月1日起施行。 2011年5月4日 最高人民法院 关于审理船舶油污损害赔偿 纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2011〕14号 （2011年1月10日最高人民法院审判委员会第1509次会议通过　2011年5月4日最高人民法院公告公布　自2011年7月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　船舶发生油污事故，对中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域造成油污损害或者形成油污损害威胁，人民法院审理相关船舶油污损害赔偿纠纷案件，适用本规定。

**Article 1** Where a ship oil pollution accident occurs, causing oil pollution damage or creating a threat of oil pollution damage to the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, the People's Court shall apply these Provisions when trying relevant ship oil pollution damage compensation dispute cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　当事人就油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害提起诉讼、申请设立油污损害赔偿责任限制基金，由船舶油污事故发生地海事法院管辖。  
油轮装载持久性油类引起的船舶油污事故，发生在中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域外，对中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域造成油污损害或者形成油污损害威胁，当事人就船舶油污事故造成的损害提起诉讼、申请设立油污损害赔偿责任限制基金，由油污损害结果地或者采取预防油污措施地海事法院管辖。

**Article 2** Where a party files a lawsuit or applies to establish a limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability for oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, the maritime court of the place where the ship oil pollution accident occurred shall have jurisdiction.  
Where a ship oil pollution accident caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker occurs outside the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, causing oil pollution damage or creating a threat of oil pollution damage to the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, and the party files a lawsuit for the damage caused by the ship oil pollution accident or applies to establish a limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability, the maritime court of the place where the oil pollution damage results or where preventive measures against oil pollution are taken shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　两艘或者两艘以上船舶泄漏油类造成油污损害，受损害人请求各泄漏油船舶所有人承担赔偿责任，按照泄漏油数量及泄漏油类对环境的危害性等因素能够合理分开各自造成的损害，由各泄漏油船舶所有人分别承担责任；不能合理分开各自造成的损害，各泄漏油船舶所有人承担连带责任。但泄漏油船舶所有人依法免予承担责任的除外。  
各泄漏油船舶所有人对受损害人承担连带责任的，相互之间根据各自责任大小确定相应的赔偿数额；难以确定责任大小的，平均承担赔偿责任。泄漏油船舶所有人支付超出自己应赔偿的数额，有权向其他泄漏油船舶所有人追偿。

**Article 3** Where two or more ships discharge oil causing oil pollution damage, if the damage caused by each ship can be reasonably separated based on factors such as the amount of oil discharged and the harmfulness of the discharged oil to the environment, the owner of each ship that discharged oil shall bear liability separately; if the damage cannot be reasonably separated, the owners of all ships that discharged oil shall bear joint and several liability. However, the owner of a ship that discharged oil is exempted from liability according to law.  
Where the owners of all ships that discharged oil bear joint and several liability to the injured party, the amount of compensation shall be determined among themselves based on the respective degree of liability; if the degree of liability is difficult to determine, they shall bear compensation liability equally. The owner of a ship that discharged oil who pays more than its share of compensation shall be entitled to seek recourse from other owners of ships that discharged oil.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶互有过失碰撞引起油类泄漏造成油污损害的，受损害人可以请求泄漏油船舶所有人承担全部赔偿责任。

**Article 4** Where oil leakage is caused by a collision in which both ships are at fault, resulting in oil pollution damage, the injured party may claim full compensation from the owner of the ship that discharged oil.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　油轮装载的持久性油类造成油污损害的，应依照《防治船舶污染海洋环境管理条例》、《1992年国际油污损害民事责任公约》的规定确定赔偿限额。  
油轮装载的非持久性燃油或者非油轮装载的燃油造成油污损害的，应依照海商法关于海事赔偿责任限制的规定确定赔偿限额。

**Article 5** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, the limitation of liability shall be determined in accordance with the Regulations on the Administration of Prevention and Control of Pollution to the Marine Environment by Ships and the 1992 International Convention on Civil Liability for Oil Pollution Damage.  
For oil pollution damage caused by non-persistent fuel oil loaded on an oil tanker or fuel oil loaded on a non-oil tanker ship, the limitation of liability shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the Maritime Code on limitation of liability for maritime claims.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经证明油污损害是由于船舶所有人的故意或者明知可能造成此种损害而轻率地作为或者不作为造成的，船舶所有人主张限制赔偿责任，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 6** Where it is proved that oil pollution damage is caused by the shipowner's intentional act or reckless act or omission with knowledge that such damage would probably occur, the People's Court shall not support the shipowner's claim to limit liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　油污损害是由于船舶所有人故意造成的，受损害人请求船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人赔偿，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** Where oil pollution damage is caused intentionally by the shipowner, the People's Court shall not support the injured party's claim for compensation against the insurer or financial guarantor of the shipowner's oil pollution damage liability.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　受损害人直接向船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人提起诉讼，船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人可以对受损害人主张船舶所有人的抗辩。  
除船舶所有人故意造成油污损害外，船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人向受损害人主张其对船舶所有人的抗辩，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 8** Where an injured party directly files a lawsuit against the insurer or financial guarantor of the shipowner's oil pollution damage liability, the insurer or financial guarantor may assert the shipowner's defenses against the injured party.  
Except where the oil pollution damage is caused intentionally by the shipowner, the People's Court shall not support the insurer or financial guarantor's assertion of defenses against the shipowner against the injured party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船舶油污损害赔偿范围包括：  
（一）为防止或者减轻船舶油污损害采取预防措施所发生的费用，以及预防措施造成的进一步灭失或者损害；  
（二）船舶油污事故造成该船舶之外的财产损害以及由此引起的收入损失；  
（三）因油污造成环境损害所引起的收入损失；  
（四）对受污染的环境已采取或将要采取合理恢复措施的费用。

**Article 9** The scope of compensation for ship oil pollution damage includes:  
(1) the costs of preventive measures taken to prevent or mitigate ship oil pollution damage, and further loss or damage caused by the preventive measures;  
(2) damage to property other than the ship itself caused by the ship oil pollution accident and resulting loss of income;  
(3) loss of income arising from environmental damage caused by oil pollution;  
(4) the costs of reasonable restoration measures that have been or will be taken for the contaminated environment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对预防措施费用以及预防措施造成的进一步灭失或者损害，人民法院应当结合污染范围、污染程度、油类泄漏量、预防措施的合理性、参与清除油污人员及投入使用设备的费用等因素合理认定。

**Article 10** With respect to the costs of preventive measures and further loss or damage caused by preventive measures, the People's Court shall make a reasonable determination based on factors such as the scope of pollution, the degree of pollution, the amount of oil discharged, the reasonableness of the preventive measures, and the costs of personnel engaged in oil removal and equipment used.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对遇险船舶实施防污措施，作业开始时的主要目的仅是为防止、减轻油污损害的，所发生的费用应认定为预防措施费用。  
作业具有救助遇险船舶、其他财产和防止、减轻油污损害的双重目的，应根据目的的主次比例合理划分预防措施费用与救助措施费用；无合理依据区分主次目的的，相关费用应平均分摊。但污染危险消除后发生的费用不应列为预防措施费用。

**Article 11** Where the main purpose of anti-pollution measures implemented on a ship in distress at the commencement of the operation is solely to prevent or mitigate oil pollution damage, the costs incurred shall be recognized as costs of preventive measures.  
Where the operation has the dual purpose of salvaging the ship in distress or other property and preventing or mitigating oil pollution damage, the costs of preventive measures and salvage measures shall be reasonably apportioned according to the primary and secondary purposes; if the primary and secondary purposes cannot be reasonably distinguished, the relevant costs shall be apportioned equally. However, costs incurred after the pollution danger has been eliminated shall not be classified as costs of preventive measures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　船舶泄漏油类污染其他船舶、渔具、养殖设施等财产，受损害人请求油污责任人赔偿因清洗、修复受污染财产支付的合理费用，人民法院应予支持。  
受污染财产无法清洗、修复，或者清洗、修复成本超过其价值的，受损害人请求油污责任人赔偿合理的更换费用，人民法院应予支持，但应参照受污染财产实际使用年限与预期使用年限的比例作合理扣除。

**Article 12** Where a ship discharges oil contaminating other ships, fishing gear, aquaculture facilities or other property, and the injured party requests compensation for reasonable costs incurred in cleaning and repairing the contaminated property from the oil pollution liable person, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.  
Where the contaminated property cannot be cleaned or repaired, or the cost of cleaning or repair exceeds its value, and the injured party requests compensation for reasonable replacement costs from the oil pollution liable person, the People's Court shall uphold the claim, but shall deduct an appropriate amount by reference to the ratio of the actual service life to the expected service life of the contaminated property.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　受损害人因其财产遭受船舶油污，不能正常生产经营的，其收入损失应以财产清洗、修复或者更换所需合理期间为限进行计算。

**Article 13** Where an injured party is unable to carry out normal production or business operations due to ship oil pollution damage to its property, the loss of income shall be calculated based on the reasonable period required for cleaning, repairing or replacing the property.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海洋渔业、滨海旅游业及其他用海、临海经营单位或者个人请求因环境污染所遭受的收入损失，具备下列全部条件，由此证明收入损失与环境污染之间具有直接因果关系的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）请求人的生产经营活动位于或者接近污染区域；  
（二）请求人的生产经营活动主要依赖受污染资源或者海岸线；  
（三）请求人难以找到其他替代资源或者商业机会；  
（四）请求人的生产经营业务属于当地相对稳定的产业。

**Article 14** Where a marine fishery, coastal tourism, or other sea-use or coastal business unit or individual claims loss of income suffered due to environmental pollution, the People's Court shall uphold the claim if all of the following conditions are met, thereby proving that there is a direct causal relationship between the loss of income and the environmental pollution:  
(1) the claimant's production or business operations are located in or near the polluted area;  
(2) the claimant's production or business operations mainly rely on the polluted resources or coastline;  
(3) the claimant cannot easily find alternative resources or business opportunities;  
(4) the claimant's production or business is a relatively stable industry in the locality.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　未经相关行政主管部门许可，受损害人从事海上养殖、海洋捕捞，主张收入损失的，人民法院不予支持；但请求赔偿清洗、修复、更换养殖或者捕捞设施的合理费用，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where an injured party engages in marine aquaculture or marine fishing without a license from the relevant competent administrative department and claims loss of income, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim; however, if the injured party requests compensation for reasonable costs of cleaning, repairing or replacing aquaculture or fishing facilities, the People's Court shall uphold the claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　受损害人主张因其财产受污染或者因环境污染造成的收入损失，应以其前三年同期平均净收入扣减受损期间的实际净收入计算，并适当考虑影响收入的其他相关因素予以合理确定。  
按照前款规定无法认定收入损失的，可以参考政府部门的相关统计数据和信息，或者同区域同类生产经营者的同期平均收入合理认定。  
受损害人采取合理措施避免收入损失，请求赔偿合理措施的费用，人民法院应予支持，但以其避免发生的收入损失数额为限。

**Article 16** Where an injured party claims loss of income due to contamination of its property or due to environmental pollution, the loss shall be calculated based on the average net income for the same period in the preceding three years minus the actual net income during the damaged period, and shall be reasonably determined with due consideration of other relevant factors affecting income.  
Where the loss of income cannot be determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it may be reasonably determined by reference to relevant statistical data and information from government departments, or the average income for the same period of similar business operators in the same region.  
Where the injured party takes reasonable measures to avoid loss of income and requests compensation for the costs of such reasonable measures, the People's Court shall uphold the claim, but only to the extent of the amount of loss of income avoided.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　船舶油污事故造成环境损害的，对环境损害的赔偿应限于已实际采取或者将要采取的合理恢复措施的费用。恢复措施的费用包括合理的监测、评估、研究费用。

**Article 17** Where a ship oil pollution accident causes environmental damage, compensation for environmental damage shall be limited to the costs of reasonable restoration measures that have actually been taken or will be taken. The costs of restoration measures include reasonable monitoring, assessment, and research costs.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　船舶取得有效的油污损害民事责任保险或者具有相应财务保证的，油污受损害人主张船舶优先权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 18** Where a ship has obtained valid civil liability insurance for oil pollution damage or has corresponding financial security, the People's Court shall not uphold the claim of an injured party for oil pollution damage asserting a maritime lien.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对油轮装载的非持久性燃油、非油轮装载的燃油造成油污损害的赔偿请求，适用海商法关于海事赔偿责任限制的规定。  
同一海事事故造成前款规定的油污损害和[海商法第二百零七条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-207)规定的可以限制赔偿责任的其他损害，船舶所有人依照海商法第十一章的规定主张在同一赔偿限额内限制赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** For claims for compensation for oil pollution damage caused by non-persistent fuel oil loaded on an oil tanker or fuel oil loaded on a non-oil tanker, the provisions of the Maritime Code on limitation of liability for maritime claims shall apply.  
Where the same maritime accident causes oil pollution damage as specified in the preceding paragraph and other damages for which liability may be limited as specified in Article 207 of the Maritime Code, the People's Court shall uphold the shipowner's claim to limit liability within the same limitation amount in accordance with the provisions of Chapter XI of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　为避免油轮装载的非持久性燃油、非油轮装载的燃油造成油污损害，对沉没、搁浅、遇难船舶采取起浮、清除或者使之无害措施，船舶所有人对由此发生的费用主张依照海商法第十一章的规定限制赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 20** Where measures are taken to refloat, remove, or render harmless a sunken, stranded, or distressed ship in order to avoid oil pollution damage caused by non-persistent fuel oil loaded on an oil tanker or fuel oil loaded on a non-oil tanker, the People's Court shall not support the shipowner's claim to limit liability for the costs incurred in accordance with the provisions of Chapter XI of the Maritime Code.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，船舶所有人，或者船舶油污责任保险人、财务保证人主张责任限制的，应当设立油污损害赔偿责任限制基金。  
油污损害赔偿责任限制基金以现金方式设立的，基金数额为《防治船舶污染海洋环境管理条例》、《1992年国际油污损害民事责任公约》规定的赔偿限额。以担保方式设立基金的，担保数额为基金数额及其在基金设立期间的利息。

**Article 21** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, the shipowner, or the insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability, who claims limitation of liability shall establish a limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability.  
Where the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability is established in cash, the amount of the fund shall be the limitation amount specified in the Regulations on the Administration of Prevention and Control of Pollution to the Marine Environment by Ships and the 1992 International Convention on Civil Liability for Oil Pollution Damage. Where the fund is established by way of guarantee, the guaranteed amount shall be the amount of the fund plus interest thereon during the period of establishment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　船舶所有人、船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人申请设立油污损害赔偿责任限制基金，利害关系人对船舶所有人主张限制赔偿责任有异议的，应当在[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百零六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-106)第一款规定的异议期内以书面形式提出，但提出该异议不影响基金的设立。

**Article 22** Where a shipowner, or an insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability, applies to establish a limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability, and an interested party objects to the shipowner's claim to limitation of liability, the objection shall be made in writing within the objection period specified in paragraph 1 of Article 106 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; however, the filing of such objection shall not affect the establishment of the fund.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，利害关系人没有在异议期内对船舶所有人主张限制赔偿责任提出异议，油污损害赔偿责任限制基金设立后，海事法院应当解除对船舶所有人的财产采取的保全措施或者发还为解除保全措施而提供的担保。

**Article 23** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, where no interested party objects to the shipowner's claim to limitation of liability within the objection period, after the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability is established, the maritime court shall lift the preservative measures taken against the shipowner's property or return the guarantee provided for the lifting of the preservative measures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，利害关系人在异议期内对船舶所有人主张限制赔偿责任提出异议的，人民法院在认定船舶所有人有权限制赔偿责任的裁决生效后，应当解除对船舶所有人的财产采取的保全措施或者发还为解除保全措施而提供的担保。

**Article 24** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, where an interested party objects to the shipowner's claim to limitation of liability within the objection period, after the judgment that the shipowner is entitled to limit liability takes effect, the maritime court shall lift the preservative measures taken against the shipowner's property or return the guarantee provided for the lifting of the preservative measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，受损害人提起诉讼时主张船舶所有人无权限制赔偿责任的，海事法院对船舶所有人是否有权限制赔偿责任的争议，可以先行审理并作出判决。

**Article 25** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, where the injured party, when filing a lawsuit, claims that the shipowner is not entitled to limit liability, the maritime court may hear the dispute as to whether the shipowner is entitled to limit liability in advance and render a judgment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，受损害人没有在规定的债权登记期间申请债权登记的，视为放弃在油污损害赔偿责任限制基金中受偿的权利。

**Article 26** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, where the injured party fails to apply for registration of claims within the prescribed period for registration of claims, it shall be deemed to have waived the right to be satisfied from the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　油污损害赔偿责任限制基金不足以清偿有关油污损害的，应根据确认的赔偿数额依法按比例分配。

**Article 27** Where the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability is insufficient to satisfy all claims for oil pollution damage, the fund shall be distributed proportionally according to the confirmed amounts of compensation in accordance with law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害，船舶所有人、船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人申请设立油污损害赔偿责任限制基金、受损害人申请债权登记与受偿，本规定没有规定的，适用海事诉讼特别程序法及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 28** For oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker, where these Provisions do not provide for matters concerning the application for establishment of a limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability by the shipowner, insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability, or the application for registration of claims and satisfaction by the injured party, the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在油污损害赔偿责任限制基金分配以前，船舶所有人、船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人，已先行赔付油污损害的，可以书面申请从基金中代位受偿。代位受偿应限于赔付的范围，并不超过接受赔付的人依法可获得的赔偿数额。  
海事法院受理代位受偿申请后，应书面通知所有对油污损害赔偿责任限制基金提出主张的利害关系人。利害关系人对申请人主张代位受偿的权利有异议的，应在收到通知之日起十五日内书面提出。  
海事法院经审查认定申请人代位受偿权利成立，应裁定予以确认；申请人主张代位受偿的权利缺乏事实或者法律依据的，裁定驳回其申请。当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定书之日起十日内提起上诉。

**Article 29** Where, prior to the distribution of the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability, the shipowner, or the insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability has already paid compensation for oil pollution damage in advance, it may apply in writing for subrogation from the fund. The subrogation shall be limited to the scope of the payment made, and shall not exceed the amount of compensation that the person receiving the payment is legally entitled to obtain.  
After the maritime court accepts an application for subrogation, it shall notify in writing all interested parties who have made claims against the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability. If an interested party objects to the applicant's right of subrogation, it shall file a written objection within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice.  
If, upon examination, the maritime court finds that the applicant's right of subrogation is established, it shall issue a ruling confirming it; if the applicant's claim to subrogation lacks factual or legal basis, it shall issue a ruling dismissing the application. If a party disagrees with the ruling, it may file an appeal within ten days from the date of receipt of the written ruling.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　船舶所有人为主动防止、减轻油污损害而支出的合理费用或者所作的合理牺牲，请求参与油污损害赔偿责任限制基金分配的，人民法院应予支持，比照[本规定第二十九条](#art-29)第二款、第三款的规定处理。

**Article 30** Where a shipowner claims to participate in the distribution of the limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability for reasonable expenses incurred or reasonable sacrifices made for proactively preventing or mitigating oil pollution damage, the People's Court shall uphold the claim, and the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 29 of these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本规定中下列用语的含义是：  
（一）船舶，是指非用于军事或者政府公务的海船和其他海上移动式装置，包括航行于国际航线和国内航线的油轮和非油轮。其中，油轮是指为运输散装持久性货油而建造或者改建的船舶，以及实际装载散装持久性货油的其他船舶。  
（二）油类，是指烃类矿物油及其残余物，限于装载于船上作为货物运输的持久性货油、装载用于本船运行的持久性和非持久性燃油，不包括装载于船上作为货物运输的非持久性货油。  
（三）船舶油污事故，是指船舶泄漏油类造成油污损害，或者虽未泄漏油类但形成严重和紧迫油污损害威胁的一个或者一系列事件。一系列事件因同一原因而发生的，视为同一事故。  
（四）船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人，是指海事事故中泄漏油类或者直接形成油污损害威胁的船舶一方的油污责任保险人或者财务保证人。  
（五）油污损害赔偿责任限制基金，是指船舶所有人、船舶油污损害责任保险人或者财务保证人，对油轮装载持久性油类造成的油污损害申请设立的赔偿责任限制基金。

**Article 31** For the purposes of these Provisions, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Ship" means a sea-going ship or other mobile offshore installation not used for military or government public service, including oil tankers and non-oil tankers navigating on international and domestic routes. An "oil tanker" means a ship constructed or adapted for the carriage of bulk persistent cargo oil, and any other ship which is actually carrying bulk persistent cargo oil.  
(2) "Oil" means hydrocarbon mineral oil and its residues, limited to persistent cargo oil carried on board as cargo, persistent and non-persistent fuel oil carried for the ship's own operation, and excluding non-persistent cargo oil carried on board as cargo.  
(3) "Ship oil pollution accident" means an event or a series of events that cause oil pollution damage from the discharge of oil from a ship, or that create a serious and imminent threat of oil pollution damage even if no oil is discharged. A series of events arising from the same cause shall be deemed to be the same accident.  
(4) "Insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability" means the oil pollution liability insurer or financial guarantor of the ship that has discharged oil or directly created a threat of oil pollution damage in a maritime accident.  
(5) "Limitation fund for oil pollution damage liability" means a fund for limitation of liability established by the shipowner, or the insurer or financial guarantor of the ship's oil pollution damage liability, for oil pollution damage caused by persistent oil loaded on an oil tanker.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本规定实施前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
本规定施行前已经终审的案件，人民法院进行再审时，不适用本规定。

**Article 32** Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court before the implementation of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
Where a case has been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall not apply when the People's Court conducts a retrial thereof.
