# 最高人民法院印发《关于审理船舶碰撞和触碰案件财产损害赔偿的规定》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1995〕17号  
**公布日期**：1995-08-18  
**生效日期**：1995-08-18

> 《最高人民法院关于审理船舶碰撞和触碰案件财产损害赔偿的规定》已经最高人民法院审判委员会第七百三十五次会议讨论通过，现印发给你们，请依照执行。各地在执行本规定中有什么情况和问题，请及时报告我院。 附： 最高人民法院 关于审理船舶碰撞和触碰案件 财产损害赔偿的规定 （最高人民法院审判委员会第七百三十五次会议讨论通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、请求人可以请求赔偿对船舶碰撞或者触碰所造成的财产损失，船舶碰撞或者触碰后相继发生的有关费用和损失，为避免或者减少损害而产生的合理费用和损失，以及预期可得利益的损失。  
因请求人的过错造成的损失或者使损失扩大的部分，不予赔偿。

**Article 1** 1. A claimant may claim compensation for property loss caused by collision or contact between vessels, the relevant expenses and losses successively incurred after the collision or contact, reasonable expenses and losses incurred to avoid or mitigate damage, and loss of anticipated profits.  
The loss caused by or the portion of loss aggravated by the fault of the claimant shall not be compensated.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、赔偿应当尽量达到恢复原状，不能恢复原状的折价赔偿。

**Article 2** 2. Compensation shall, to the extent possible, achieve restitution in integrum; where restitution in integrum is impossible, compensation shall be made in monetary terms.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、船舶损害赔偿分为全损赔偿和部分损害赔偿。  
（一）船舶全损的赔偿包括：  
船舶价值损失；  
未包括在船舶价值内的船舶上的燃料、物料、备件、供应品，渔船上的捕捞设备、网具、渔具等损失；  
船员工资、遣返费及其他合理费用。  
（二）船舶部分损害的赔偿包括：合理的船舶临时修理费、永久修理费及辅助费用、维持费用，但应满足下列条件：  
船舶应就近修理，除非请求人能证明在其他地方修理更能减少损失和节省费用，或者有其他合理的理由。如果船舶经临时修理可继续营运，请求人有责任进行临时修理；  
船舶碰撞部位的修理，同请求人为保证船舶适航，或者因另外事故所进行的修理，或者与船舶例行的检修一起进行时，赔偿仅限于修理本次船舶碰撞的受损部位所需的费用和损失。  
（三）船舶损害赔偿还包括：  
合理的救助费，沉船的勘查、打捞和清除费用，设置沉船标志费用；  
拖航费用，本航次的租金或者运费损失，共同海损分摊；  
合理的船期损失；  
其他合理的费用。

**Article 3** 3. Compensation for damage to a vessel shall be divided into compensation for total loss and compensation for partial damage.  
(1) Compensation for total loss of a vessel includes:  
Loss of the vessel's value;  
Loss of fuel, stores, spare parts, supplies on board not included in the vessel's value, and loss of fishing equipment, nets, fishing gear on fishing vessels;  
Wages, repatriation costs and other reasonable expenses of the crew.  
(2) Compensation for partial damage to a vessel includes: reasonable temporary repair costs, permanent repair costs, auxiliary expenses and maintenance costs of the vessel, provided that the following conditions are satisfied:  
The vessel shall be repaired at a nearby port, unless the claimant proves that repair elsewhere can reduce loss or save costs more effectively, or there are other reasonable grounds. If the vessel can continue operations after temporary repair, the claimant shall be obligated to carry out temporary repair;  
Where repair of the collision-damaged part is carried out together with repair for ensuring the vessel's seaworthiness, repair due to another accident, or routine overhaul of the vessel, compensation shall be limited to the costs and loss necessary for repairing the damaged part of the collision.  
(3) Compensation for vessel damage also includes:  
Reasonable salvage costs, costs of survey, salvage and removal of the sunken vessel, costs of marking the sunken vessel;  
Towage costs, loss of charter hire or freight for the current voyage, general average contribution;  
Reasonable loss of time;  
Other reasonable expenses.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、船上财产的损害赔偿包括：  
船上财产的灭失或者部分损坏引起的贬值损失；  
合理的修复或者处理费用；  
合理的财产救助、打捞和清除费用，共同海损分摊；  
其他合理费用。

**Article 4** 4. Compensation for damage to property on board a vessel includes:  
Loss of depreciation caused by destruction or partial damage to property on board;  
Reasonable repair or disposal costs;  
Reasonable costs of salvage, recovery and removal of property, general average contribution;  
Other reasonable expenses.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、船舶触碰造成设施损害的赔偿包括：  
设施的全损或者部分损坏修复费用；  
设施修复前不能正常使用所产生的合理的收益损失。

**Article 5** 5. Compensation for damage to installations caused by contact with a vessel includes:  
Cost of repair for total loss or partial damage of the installation;  
Reasonable loss of income arising from the inability to use the installation normally before its repair.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、船舶碰撞或者触碰造成第三人财产损失的，应予赔偿。

**Article 6** 6. Where a collision or contact between vessels causes property loss to a third party, compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、除赔偿本金外，利息损失也应赔偿。

**Article 7** 7. In addition to the principal amount, loss of interest shall also be compensated.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、船舶价值损失的计算，以船舶碰撞发生地当时类似船舶的市价确定；碰撞发生地无类似船舶市价的，以船舶船籍港类似船舶的市价确定，或者以其他地区类似船舶市价的平均价确定；没有市价的，以原船舶的造价或者购置价，扣除折旧（折旧率按年4－10%）计算；折旧后没有价值的按残值计算。  
船舶被打捞后尚有残值的，船舶价值应扣除残值。

**Article 8** 8. The calculation of loss of vessel value shall be determined by the market price of similar vessels at the place and time of the collision; if there is no market price for similar vessels at the place of collision, the market price at the vessel's port of registry or the average market price of similar vessels in other regions shall be used; if there is no market price, the original construction cost or purchase price minus depreciation (depreciation rate at 4-10% per annum) shall be used; if no value remains after depreciation, the residual value shall be taken.  
If a vessel still has residual value after being raised, the residual value shall be deducted from the vessel's value.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、船上财产损失的计算：  
（一）货物灭失的，按照货物的实际价值，即以货物装船时的价值加运费加请求人已支付的货物保险费计算，扣除可节省的费用；  
（二）货物损坏的，以修复所需的费用，或者以货物的实际价值扣除残值和可节省的费用计算；  
（三）由于船舶碰撞在约定的时间内迟延交付所产生的损失，按迟延交付货物的实际价值加预期可得利润与到岸时的市价的差价计算，但预期可得利润不得超过货物实际价值的10%；  
（四）船上捕捞的鱼货，以实际的鱼货价值计算。鱼货价值参照海事发生时当地市价，扣除可节省的费用。  
（五）船上渔具、网具的种类和数量，以本次出海捕捞作业所需量扣减现存量计算，但所需量超过渔政部门规定或者许可的种类和数量的，不予认定；渔具、网具的价值，按原购置价或者原造价扣除折旧费用和残值计算；  
（六）旅客行李、物品（包括自带行李）的损失，属本船旅客的损失，依照海商法的规定处理；属他船旅客的损失，可参照旅客运输合同中有关旅客行李灭失或者损坏的赔偿规定处理；  
（七）船员个人生活必需品的损失，按实际损失适当予以赔偿；  
（八）承运人与旅客书面约定由承运人保管的货币、金银、珠宝、有价证券或者其他贵重物品的损失，依海商法的规定处理；船员、旅客、其他人员个人携带的货币、金银、珠宝、有价证券或者其他贵重物品的损失，不予认定；  
（九）船上其他财产的损失，按其实际价值计算。

**Article 9** 9. Calculation of loss of property on board a vessel:  
(1) For loss of cargo, calculation shall be based on the actual value of the cargo, i.e., the value at the time of loading plus freight plus cargo insurance premium paid by the claimant, minus expenses that can be saved;  
(2) For damage to cargo, calculation shall be based on the cost necessary for repair, or on the actual value of the cargo minus residual value and expenses that can be saved;  
(3) For loss caused by delayed delivery of cargo within the agreed time period due to vessel collision, calculation shall be based on the difference between the actual value of the delayed cargo plus anticipated profits and the market price at the time of arrival, provided that the anticipated profits shall not exceed 10% of the actual value of the cargo;  
(4) For fish catch on board, calculation shall be based on the actual value of the fish catch. The value of fish catch shall refer to the local market price at the time of the maritime incident, minus expenses that can be saved.  
(5) For the types and quantities of fishing gear and nets on board, calculation shall be based on the quantity required for the current fishing operation minus the existing stock; however, if the required quantity exceeds the types and quantities stipulated or permitted by the fishery administrative department, it shall not be recognized; the value of fishing gear and nets shall be calculated by deducting depreciation and residual value from the original purchase price or construction cost;  
(6) For loss of passengers' luggage and articles (including carry-on luggage), if the loss belongs to passengers on the vessel, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the Maritime Code; if the loss belongs to passengers on another vessel, the provisions on compensation for loss of or damage to passengers' luggage in the passenger transport contract may be referred to;  
(7) For loss of crew members' personal necessities, appropriate compensation shall be made based on actual loss;  
(8) For loss of currency, gold, silver, jewelry, negotiable securities or other valuables which, by written agreement between the carrier and the passenger, are kept by the carrier, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the Maritime Code; loss of currency, gold, silver, jewelry, negotiable securities or other valuables carried personally by crew members, passengers or other persons shall not be recognized;  
(9) For loss of other property on board, calculation shall be based on its actual value.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、船期损失的计算：  
期限：船舶全损的，以找到替代船所需的合理期间为限，但最长不得超过两个月；船舶部分损害的修船期限，以实际修复所需的合理期间为限，其中包括联系、住坞、验船等所需的合理时间；渔业船舶，按上述期限扣除休渔期为限，或者以一个渔汛期为限。  
船期损失，一般以船舶碰撞前后各两个航次的平均净盈利计算；无前后各两个航次可参照的，以其他相应航次的平均净盈利计算。  
渔船渔汛损失，以该渔船前3年的同期渔汛平均净收益计算，或者以本年内同期同类渔船的平均净收益计算。计算渔汛损失时，应当考虑到碰撞渔船在对船捕渔作业或者围网灯光捕渔作业中的作用等因素。

**Article 10** 10. Calculation of loss of time:  
Time limit: For total loss of a vessel, the time limit shall be the reasonable period needed to find a replacement vessel, but shall not exceed two months at most; for partial damage, the repair period shall be the reasonable period actually needed for repair, including the reasonable time for contacting, docking, survey, etc.; for fishing vessels, the time limit shall be the above period minus the fishing moratorium period, or be limited to one fishing season.  
Loss of time shall generally be calculated based on the average net profit of the two voyages before and the two voyages after the collision; if no such voyages are available for reference, the average net profit of other corresponding voyages shall be used.  
Loss of fishing season for a fishing vessel shall be calculated based on the average net profit of the same fishing season in the previous three years, or based on the average net profit of similar fishing vessels in the same period of the current year. When calculating loss of fishing season, consideration shall be given to factors such as the role of the collision vessel in pair trawling or purse seine light fishing operations.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、租金或者运费损失的计算：  
碰撞导致期租合同承租人停租或者不付租金的，以停租或者不付租金额，扣除可节省的费用计算。  
因货物灭失或者损坏导致到付运费损失的，以尚未收取的运费金额扣除可节省的费用计算。

**Article 11** 11. Calculation of loss of charter hire or freight:  
Where a collision causes the charterer under a time charter party to suspend payment or not pay hire, calculation shall be based on the amount of hire suspended or unpaid, minus expenses that can be saved.  
Where loss of freight payable at destination is caused by loss of or damage to cargo, calculation shall be based on the amount of freight not yet collected, minus expenses that can be saved.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、设施损害赔偿的计算：  
期限：以实际停止使用期间扣除常规检修的期间为限；  
设施部分损坏或者全损，分别以合理的修复费用或者重新建造的费用，扣除已使用年限的折旧费计算；  
设施使用的收益损失，以实际减少的净收益，即按停止使用前3个月的平均净盈利计算；部分使用并有收益的，应当扣减。

**Article 12** 12. Calculation of compensation for damage to installations:  
Time limit: The period of actual cessation of use minus the period of routine maintenance;  
For partial damage or total loss of an installation, calculation shall be based on reasonable repair costs or reconstruction costs respectively, minus depreciation for the years of use;  
Loss of income from the use of the installation shall be calculated based on the actual reduction in net income, i.e., the average net profit for the three months prior to the cessation of use; if partial use with income exists, a deduction shall be made.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、利息损失的计算：  
船舶价值的损失利息，从船期损失停止计算之日起至判决或者调解指定的应付之日止；  
其他各项损失的利息，从损失发生之日或者费用产生之日起计算至判决或调解指定的应付之日止；  
利息按本金性质的同期利率计算。

**Article 13** 13. Calculation of loss of interest:  
Interest on loss of vessel value shall run from the date on which the calculation of loss of time ceases to the date designated for payment in the judgment or mediation;  
Interest on other losses shall run from the date on which the loss or expense arises to the date designated for payment in the judgment or mediation;  
Interest shall be calculated at the contemporaneous interest rate for the nature of the principal amount.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、计算损害赔偿的货币，当事人有约定的，依约定；没有约定的，按以下相关的货币计算：  
按船舶营运或者生产经营所使用的货币计算；  
船载进、出口货物的价值，按买卖合同或者提单、运单记明的货币计算；  
以特别提款权计算损失的，按法院判决或者调解之日的兑换率换算成相应的货币。

**Article 14** 14. The currency for calculating damages shall, if the parties have agreed, be in accordance with the agreement; in the absence of agreement, the following relevant currencies shall be used:  
The currency used in the vessel's operation or production and business;  
The value of cargoes imported or exported on board shall be calculated in the currency stated in the sale contract, bill of lading or waybill;  
If losses are calculated in Special Drawing Rights, they shall be converted into the corresponding currency at the exchange rate on the date of the court judgment or mediation.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、本规定不包括对船舶碰撞或者触碰责任的确定，不影响船舶所有人或者承运人依法享受免责和责任限制的权利。

**Article 15** 15. These Provisions do not include the determination of liability for collision or contact between vessels, and shall not affect the right of the shipowner or carrier to enjoy exemption from liability and limitation of liability according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>十六、本规定中下列用语的含义：  
“船舶”是指所有用作或者能够用作水上运输工具的各类水上船筏，包括非排水船舶和水上飞机。但是用于军事的和政府公务的船舶除外。  
“设施”是指人为设置的固定或者可移动的构造物，包括固定平台、浮鼓、码头、堤坝、桥梁、敷设或者架设的电缆、管道等。  
“船舶碰撞”是指在海上或者与海相通的可航水域，两艘或者两艘以上的船舶之间发生接触或者没有直接接触，造成财产损害的事故。  
“船舶触碰”是指船舶与设施或者障碍物发生接触并造成财产损害的事故。  
“船舶全损”是指船舶实际全部损失，或者损坏已达到相当严重的程度，以至于救助、打捞、修理费等费用之和达到或者超过碰撞或者触碰发生前的船舶价值。  
“辅助费用”是指为进行修理而产生的合理费用，包括必要的进坞费、清航除气费、排放油污水处理费、港口使费、引航费、检验费以及修船期间所产生的住坞费、码头费等费用，但不限于上述费用。  
“维持费用”是指船舶修理期间，船舶和船员日常消耗的费用，包括燃料、物料、淡水及供应品的消耗和船员工资等。

**Article 16** 16. For the purposes of these Provisions, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
"Vessel" means all types of watercraft, including non-displacement vessels and seaplanes, used or capable of being used as a means of transportation on water. However, vessels used for military or government service are excluded.  
"Installation" means a fixed or movable structure placed artificially, including fixed platforms, buoys, docks, dikes, bridges, cables and pipelines laid or erected, etc.  
"Collision between vessels" means an incident in which two or more vessels come into contact or do not come into direct contact with each other on the sea or navigable waters connected with the sea, causing property damage.  
"Contact with a vessel" means an incident in which a vessel comes into contact with an installation or obstacle, causing property damage.  
"Total loss of a vessel" means that the vessel is actually totally lost, or damage has reached such a serious degree that the sum of salvage, recovery and repair costs equals or exceeds the value of the vessel before the collision or contact.  
"Auxiliary expenses" means reasonable expenses incurred for repair, including necessary dry-docking charges, cleaning and gas-freeing charges, charges for oil and oily water disposal, port charges, pilotage fees, survey fees, and charges for berthing and dock usage during repair, but not limited to the above.  
"Maintenance expenses" means expenses for daily consumption of the vessel and crew during repair, including consumption of fuel, stores, fresh water and supplies, and crew wages.

<a id="art-17"></a>十七、本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 17** 17. These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
