# 最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕1号  
**公布日期**：2016-03-21  
**生效日期**：2016-04-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2016年1月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1676次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年4月1日起施行。 2016年3月21日 最高人民法院 关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律 若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　权利要求书有两项以上权利要求的，权利人应当在起诉状中载明据以起诉被诉侵权人侵犯其专利权的权利要求。起诉状对此未记载或者记载不明的，人民法院应当要求权利人明确。经释明，权利人仍不予明确的，人民法院可以裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 1** Where a patent claim has two or more claims, the right holder shall specify in the statement of claim the claim on which the prosecution is based against the alleged infringer for infringement of the patent right. If the statement of claim fails to record or unclearly records this, the People's Court shall require the right holder to clarify. If the right holder still fails to clarify after explanation, the People's Court may rule to reject the prosecution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　权利人在专利侵权诉讼中主张的权利要求被专利复审委员会宣告无效的，审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件的人民法院可以裁定驳回权利人基于该无效权利要求的起诉。  
有证据证明宣告上述权利要求无效的决定被生效的行政判决撤销的，权利人可以另行起诉。  
专利权人另行起诉的，诉讼时效期间从本条第二款所称行政判决书送达之日起计算。

**Article 2** If the claim asserted by the right holder in a patent infringement action is declared invalid by the Patent Reexamination Board, the People's Court handling the dispute over infringement of a patent right may rule to reject the prosecution based on the invalid claim.  
If there is evidence proving that the decision declaring the aforesaid claim invalid is revoked by an effective administrative judgment, the right holder may separately file a prosecution.  
If the patentee separately files a prosecution, the limitation of action shall be calculated from the date of service of the administrative judgment referred to in the second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因明显违反[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第三款、第四款导致说明书无法用于解释权利要求，且不属于[本解释第四条](#art-4)规定的情形，专利权因此被请求宣告无效的，审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件的人民法院一般应当裁定中止诉讼；在合理期限内专利权未被请求宣告无效的，人民法院可以根据权利要求的记载确定专利权的保护范围。

**Article 3** If the specification cannot be used to interpret the claims due to an obvious violation of Article 26, paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Patent Law, and the circumstances do not fall under the circumstances specified in Article 4 of this Interpretation, and thus the patent right is requested to be declared invalid, the People's Court handling the dispute over infringement of a patent right shall generally rule to suspend the action; if the patent right is not requested to be declared invalid within a reasonable period, the People's Court may determine the scope of protection of the patent right based on the records in the claims.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　权利要求书、说明书及附图中的语法、文字、标点、图形、符号等存有歧义，但本领域普通技术人员通过阅读权利要求书、说明书及附图可以得出唯一理解的，人民法院应当根据该唯一理解予以认定。

**Article 4** If there are ambiguities in grammar, words, punctuation, graphics, symbols, etc., in the claims, specification, and drawings, but a person of ordinary skill in the art can reach a unique understanding by reading the claims, specification, and drawings, the People's Court shall make determination based on that unique understanding.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在人民法院确定专利权的保护范围时，独立权利要求的前序部分、特征部分以及从属权利要求的引用部分、限定部分记载的技术特征均有限定作用。

**Article 5** When the People's Court determines the scope of protection of a patent right, the technical features recorded in the preamble part and the characterizing part of an independent claim, as well as the reference part and the limiting part of a dependent claim, shall all have a limiting effect.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院可以运用与涉案专利存在分案申请关系的其他专利及其专利审查档案、生效的专利授权确权裁判文书解释涉案专利的权利要求。  
专利审查档案，包括专利审查、复审、无效程序中专利申请人或者专利权人提交的书面材料，国务院专利行政部门及其专利复审委员会制作的审查意见通知书、会晤记录、口头审理记录、生效的专利复审请求审查决定书和专利权无效宣告请求审查决定书等。

**Article 6** The People's Court may use other patents that have a divisional application relationship with the patent involved in the case, and their patent examination files, as well as effective judicial decisions on patent grant and validation, to interpret the claims of the patent involved in the case.  
Patent examination files include written materials submitted by the patent applicant or patentee in patent examination, reexamination, and invalidation procedures, examination opinion notices, meeting records, oral hearing records, effective patent reexamination request examination decisions, and patent invalidation request examination decisions, etc., made by the patent administrative department of the State Council and its Patent Reexamination Board.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被诉侵权技术方案在包含封闭式组合物权利要求全部技术特征的基础上增加其他技术特征的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权技术方案未落入专利权的保护范围，但该增加的技术特征属于不可避免的常规数量杂质的除外。  
前款所称封闭式组合物权利要求，一般不包括中药组合物权利要求。

**Article 7** If the alleged infringing technical solution adds other technical features on the basis of including all the technical features of a closed composition claim, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing technical solution does not fall within the scope of protection of the patent right, unless the added technical features are routine inevitable quantitative impurities.  
The closed composition claim referred to in the preceding paragraph generally does not include claims for traditional Chinese medicine compositions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　功能性特征，是指对于结构、组分、步骤、条件或其之间的关系等，通过其在发明创造中所起的功能或者效果进行限定的技术特征，但本领域普通技术人员仅通过阅读权利要求即可直接、明确地确定实现上述功能或者效果的具体实施方式的除外。  
与说明书及附图记载的实现前款所称功能或者效果不可缺少的技术特征相比，被诉侵权技术方案的相应技术特征是以基本相同的手段，实现相同的功能，达到相同的效果，且本领域普通技术人员在被诉侵权行为发生时无需经过创造性劳动就能够联想到的，人民法院应当认定该相应技术特征与功能性特征相同或者等同。

**Article 8** Functional features refer to technical features that define structure, components, steps, conditions, or their relationships by the functions or effects they perform in the invention-creation, unless a person of ordinary skill in the art can directly and clearly determine the specific implementation manner for realizing the aforesaid functions or effects merely by reading the claims.  
If, compared with the technical features indispensable for realizing the functions or effects referred to in the preceding paragraph as recorded in the specification and drawings, the corresponding technical features of the alleged infringing technical solution achieve the same function and the same effect by substantially the same means, and a person of ordinary skill in the art could have conceived of them without creative work at the time of the alleged infringing act, the People's Court shall determine that the corresponding technical features are identical or equivalent to the functional features.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被诉侵权技术方案不能适用于权利要求中使用环境特征所限定的使用环境的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权技术方案未落入专利权的保护范围。

**Article 9** If the alleged infringing technical solution cannot be applied to the use environment defined by the use environment feature in the claim, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing technical solution does not fall within the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于权利要求中以制备方法界定产品的技术特征，被诉侵权产品的制备方法与其不相同也不等同的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权技术方案未落入专利权的保护范围。

**Article 10** For a technical feature of a product defined by a preparation method in a claim, if the preparation method of the alleged infringing product is neither identical nor equivalent to it, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing technical solution does not fall within the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　方法权利要求未明确记载技术步骤的先后顺序，但本领域普通技术人员阅读权利要求书、说明书及附图后直接、明确地认为该技术步骤应当按照特定顺序实施的，人民法院应当认定该步骤顺序对于专利权的保护范围具有限定作用。

**Article 11** If a method claim does not explicitly record the order of the technical steps, but a person of ordinary skill in the art, after reading the claims, specification, and drawings, directly and clearly considers that the technical steps should be performed in a specific order, the People's Court shall determine that the order of the steps has a limiting effect on the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　权利要求采用“至少”“不超过”等用语对数值特征进行界定，且本领域普通技术人员阅读权利要求书、说明书及附图后认为专利技术方案特别强调该用语对技术特征的限定作用，权利人主张与其不相同的数值特征属于等同特征的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 12** If a claim uses expressions such as "at least," "not exceeding," etc., to define a numerical feature, and a person of ordinary skill in the art, after reading the claims, specification, and drawings, considers that the patented technical solution particularly emphasizes the limiting effect of such expressions on the technical feature, and the right holder asserts that a different numerical feature is an equivalent feature, the People's Court shall not support such assertion.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　权利人证明专利申请人、专利权人在专利授权确权程序中对权利要求书、说明书及附图的限缩性修改或者陈述被明确否定的，人民法院应当认定该修改或者陈述未导致技术方案的放弃。

**Article 13** If the right holder proves that the patent applicant or patentee's limiting amendment or statement made during the patent grant and validation proceedings with respect to the claims, specification, and drawings has been explicitly denied, the People's Court shall determine that such amendment or statement has not resulted in the abandonment of the technical solution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在认定一般消费者对于外观设计所具有的知识水平和认知能力时，一般应当考虑被诉侵权行为发生时授权外观设计所属相同或者相近种类产品的设计空间。设计空间较大的，人民法院可以认定一般消费者通常不容易注意到不同设计之间的较小区别；设计空间较小的，人民法院可以认定一般消费者通常更容易注意到不同设计之间的较小区别。

**Article 14** When determining the level of knowledge and cognitive ability of an ordinary consumer with respect to a design, the People's Court shall generally consider the design space of products of the same or similar type to which the granted design pertains at the time of the alleged infringing act. If the design space is large, the People's Court may determine that an ordinary consumer usually does not easily notice minor differences between different designs; if the design space is small, the People's Court may determine that an ordinary consumer usually more easily notices minor differences between different designs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对于成套产品的外观设计专利，被诉侵权设计与其一项外观设计相同或者近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计落入专利权的保护范围。

**Article 15** For a design patent for a set of products, if the alleged infringing design is identical or similar to one of its designs, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design falls within the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对于组装关系唯一的组件产品的外观设计专利，被诉侵权设计与其组合状态下的外观设计相同或者近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计落入专利权的保护范围。  
对于各构件之间无组装关系或者组装关系不唯一的组件产品的外观设计专利，被诉侵权设计与其全部单个构件的外观设计均相同或者近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计落入专利权的保护范围；被诉侵权设计缺少其单个构件的外观设计或者与之不相同也不近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计未落入专利权的保护范围。

**Article 16** For a design patent for a component product with a unique assembly relationship, if the alleged infringing design is identical or similar to the design in its assembled state, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design falls within the scope of protection of the patent right.  
For a design patent for a component product where there is no assembly relationship among the components or the assembly relationship is not unique, if the alleged infringing design is identical or similar to all the designs of the individual components, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design falls within the scope of protection of the patent right; if the alleged infringing design lacks the design of an individual component or is neither identical nor similar to it, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design does not fall within the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于变化状态产品的外观设计专利，被诉侵权设计与变化状态图所示各种使用状态下的外观设计均相同或者近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计落入专利权的保护范围；被诉侵权设计缺少其一种使用状态下的外观设计或者与之不相同也不近似的，人民法院应当认定被诉侵权设计未落入专利权的保护范围。

**Article 17** For a design patent for a product with variable states, if the alleged infringing design is identical or similar to the designs shown in the various states of use in the state-of-use drawings, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design falls within the scope of protection of the patent right; if the alleged infringing design lacks the design of one state of use or is neither identical nor similar to it, the People's Court shall determine that the alleged infringing design does not fall within the scope of protection of the patent right.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　权利人依据[专利法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-13)诉请在发明专利申请公布日至授权公告日期间实施该发明的单位或者个人支付适当费用的，人民法院可以参照有关专利许可使用费合理确定。  
发明专利申请公布时申请人请求保护的范围与发明专利公告授权时的专利权保护范围不一致，被诉技术方案均落入上述两种范围的，人民法院应当认定被告在前款所称期间内实施了该发明；被诉技术方案仅落入其中一种范围的，人民法院应当认定被告在前款所称期间内未实施该发明。  
发明专利公告授权后，未经专利权人许可，为生产经营目的使用、许诺销售、销售在本条第一款所称期间内已由他人制造、销售、进口的产品，且该他人已支付或者书面承诺支付[专利法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-13)规定的适当费用的，对于权利人关于上述使用、许诺销售、销售行为侵犯专利权的主张，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 18** If the right holder, pursuant to Article 13 of the Patent Law, files a lawsuit requesting a reasonable fee from the entity or individual that implemented the invention during the period from the publication date of the patent application to the announcement date of the patent grant, the People's Court may reasonably determine the fee by reference to the patent license fee.  
If the scope of protection requested by the applicant at the time of publication of the patent application for invention is inconsistent with the scope of protection of the patent right at the time of announcement and grant of the patent for invention, and the alleged technical solution falls within both scopes, the People's Court shall determine that the defendant implemented the invention during the period referred to in the preceding paragraph; if the alleged technical solution falls within only one of the scopes, the People's Court shall determine that the defendant did not implement the invention during the period referred to in the preceding paragraph.  
After the announcement and grant of the patent for invention, if, without the permission of the patentee, for production and business purposes, a person uses, offers to sell, or sells a product that was manufactured, sold, or imported by another person during the period referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, and that other person has paid or made a written commitment to pay the reasonable fee specified in Article 13 of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall not support the right holder's claim that the aforesaid use, offer to sell, or sale infringes the patent right.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　产品买卖合同依法成立的，人民法院应当认定属于[专利法第十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-11)规定的销售。

**Article 19** If a product sales contract is established according to law, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes a sale as specified in Article 11 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对于将依照专利方法直接获得的产品进一步加工、处理而获得的后续产品，进行再加工、处理的，人民法院应当认定不属于[专利法第十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-11)规定的“使用依照该专利方法直接获得的产品”。

**Article 20** If further processing or treatment of a subsequent product obtained by further processing or treating a product directly obtained by the patented method, the People's Court shall determine that it does not constitute "use of the product directly obtained by the patented method" as specified in Article 11 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　明知有关产品系专门用于实施专利的材料、设备、零部件、中间物等，未经专利权人许可，为生产经营目的将该产品提供给他人实施了侵犯专利权的行为，权利人主张该提供者的行为属于侵权责任法第九条规定的帮助他人实施侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持。  
明知有关产品、方法被授予专利权，未经专利权人许可，为生产经营目的积极诱导他人实施了侵犯专利权的行为，权利人主张该诱导者的行为属于侵权责任法第九条规定的教唆他人实施侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** If a person knows that a product is specifically used for implementing a patent, such as materials, equipment, parts, intermediates, etc., and without the permission of the patentee, for production and business purposes, provides the product to another person who has committed an act of infringing the patent right, and the right holder claims that the act of the provider constitutes assisting another person in committing an infringing act as specified in Article 9 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall support the claim.  
If a person knows that a product or method is granted a patent right, and without the permission of the patentee, for production and business purposes, actively induces another person to commit an act of infringing the patent right, and the right holder claims that the act of the inducer constitutes instigating another person to commit an infringing act as specified in Article 9 of the Tort Liability Law, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对于被诉侵权人主张的现有技术抗辩或者现有设计抗辩，人民法院应当依照专利申请日时施行的专利法界定现有技术或者现有设计。

**Article 22** For a prior art defense or prior design defense asserted by the alleged infringer, the People's Court shall define the prior art or prior design in accordance with the Patent Law in force at the time of the patent application date.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　被诉侵权技术方案或者外观设计落入在先的涉案专利权的保护范围，被诉侵权人以其技术方案或者外观设计被授予专利权为由抗辩不侵犯涉案专利权的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** If the alleged infringing technical solution or design falls within the scope of protection of the prior patent right involved, and the alleged infringer defends that it does not infringe the patent right involved on the ground that its technical solution or design has been granted a patent right, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　推荐性国家、行业或者地方标准明示所涉必要专利的信息，被诉侵权人以实施该标准无需专利权人许可为由抗辩不侵犯该专利权的，人民法院一般不予支持。  
推荐性国家、行业或者地方标准明示所涉必要专利的信息，专利权人、被诉侵权人协商该专利的实施许可条件时，专利权人故意违反其在标准制定中承诺的公平、合理、无歧视的许可义务，导致无法达成专利实施许可合同，且被诉侵权人在协商中无明显过错的，对于权利人请求停止标准实施行为的主张，人民法院一般不予支持。  
本条第二款所称实施许可条件，应当由专利权人、被诉侵权人协商确定。经充分协商，仍无法达成一致的，可以请求人民法院确定。人民法院在确定上述实施许可条件时，应当根据公平、合理、无歧视的原则，综合考虑专利的创新程度及其在标准中的作用、标准所属的技术领域、标准的性质、标准实施的范围和相关的许可条件等因素。  
法律、行政法规对实施标准中的专利另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 24** If a recommended national, industry, or local standard explicitly discloses information on the essential patents involved, and the alleged infringer defends that it does not infringe the patent right on the ground that implementing the standard does not require permission from the patentee, the People's Court shall generally not support such defense.  
If a recommended national, industry, or local standard explicitly discloses information on the essential patents involved, and during the negotiation of the implementation license conditions for the patent between the patentee and the alleged infringer, the patentee intentionally violates the fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory licensing obligation it committed during the standard-setting process, resulting in the failure to reach a patent implementation license contract, and the alleged infringer has no obvious fault in the negotiation, the People's Court shall generally not support the right holder's claim to stop the standard implementation act.  
The implementation license conditions referred to in the second paragraph of this Article shall be negotiated by the patentee and the alleged infringer. If, after full negotiation, an agreement still cannot be reached, the parties may request the People's Court to determine them. When determining the aforesaid implementation license conditions, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the principle of fairness, reasonableness, and non-discrimination, comprehensively consider factors such as the degree of innovation of the patent and its role in the standard, the technical field to which the standard belongs, the nature of the standard, the scope of implementation of the standard, and relevant licensing conditions.  
If laws or administrative regulations have other provisions on the implementation of patents in standards, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　为生产经营目的使用、许诺销售或者销售不知道是未经专利权人许可而制造并售出的专利侵权产品，且举证证明该产品合法来源的，对于权利人请求停止上述使用、许诺销售、销售行为的主张，人民法院应予支持，但被诉侵权产品的使用者举证证明其已支付该产品的合理对价的除外。  
本条第一款所称不知道，是指实际不知道且不应当知道。  
本条第一款所称合法来源，是指通过合法的销售渠道、通常的买卖合同等正常商业方式取得产品。对于合法来源，使用者、许诺销售者或者销售者应当提供符合交易习惯的相关证据。

**Article 25** If a person, for production and business purposes, uses, offers to sell, or sells a patented infringing product that was manufactured and sold without the permission of the patentee and without knowing it, and proves the lawful source of the product, the People's Court shall support the right holder's claim to stop the aforesaid use, offer to sell, or sale, unless the user of the alleged infringing product proves that it has paid a reasonable consideration for the product.  
The term "without knowing" in the first paragraph of this Article means that the person actually did not know and should not have known.  
The term "lawful source" in the first paragraph of this Article means that the product was obtained through a normal commercial manner, such as a lawful sales channel or a usual sales contract. For the lawful source, the user, offeror, or seller shall provide relevant evidence consistent with trade practices.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　被告构成对专利权的侵犯，权利人请求判令其停止侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持，但基于国家利益、公共利益的考量，人民法院可以不判令被告停止被诉行为，而判令其支付相应的合理费用。

**Article 26** If the defendant constitutes infringement of the patent right, and the right holder requests a court order to stop the infringing act, the People's Court shall support the claim, unless, based on considerations of national interest or public interest, the People's Court may not order the defendant to stop the alleged act and instead order it to pay corresponding reasonable expenses.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失难以确定的，人民法院应当依照[专利法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)第一款的规定，要求权利人对侵权人因侵权所获得的利益进行举证；在权利人已经提供侵权人所获利益的初步证据，而与专利侵权行为相关的账簿、资料主要由侵权人掌握的情况下，人民法院可以责令侵权人提供该账簿、资料；侵权人无正当理由拒不提供或者提供虚假的账簿、资料的，人民法院可以根据权利人的主张和提供的证据认定侵权人因侵权所获得的利益。

**Article 27** If the actual losses suffered by the right holder due to the infringement are difficult to determine, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 65, paragraph 1 of the Patent Law, require the right holder to provide evidence of the profits obtained by the infringer from the infringement; if the right holder has provided preliminary evidence of the profits obtained by the infringer, and the account books and materials related to the patent infringement are mainly in the possession of the infringer, the People's Court may order the infringer to provide such account books and materials; if the infringer, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide or provides false account books and materials, the People's Court may determine the profits obtained by the infringer from the infringement based on the right holder's claims and the evidence provided.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　权利人、侵权人依法约定专利侵权的赔偿数额或者赔偿计算方法，并在专利侵权诉讼中主张依据该约定确定赔偿数额的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 28** If the right holder and the infringer have lawfully agreed on the amount of damages or the method of calculating damages for patent infringement and assert in the patent infringement action that the damages shall be determined in accordance with such agreement, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　宣告专利权无效的决定作出后，当事人根据该决定依法申请再审，请求撤销专利权无效宣告前人民法院作出但未执行的专利侵权的判决、调解书的，人民法院可以裁定中止再审审查，并中止原判决、调解书的执行。  
专利权人向人民法院提供充分、有效的担保，请求继续执行前款所称判决、调解书的，人民法院应当继续执行；侵权人向人民法院提供充分、有效的反担保，请求中止执行的，人民法院应当准许。人民法院生效裁判未撤销宣告专利权无效的决定的，专利权人应当赔偿因继续执行给对方造成的损失；宣告专利权无效的决定被人民法院生效裁判撤销，专利权仍有效的，人民法院可以依据前款所称判决、调解书直接执行上述反担保财产。

**Article 29** After a decision declaring the patent right invalid is made, if a party, based on that decision, applies for a retrial according to law and requests the revocation of a judgment or mediation statement on patent infringement made by the People's Court before the declaration of invalidity of the patent right but not yet enforced, the People's Court may rule to suspend the retrial review and suspend the enforcement of the original judgment or mediation statement.  
If the patentee provides sufficient and effective security to the People's Court and requests continued enforcement of the judgment or mediation statement referred to in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall continue enforcement; if the infringer provides sufficient and effective counter-security to the People's Court and requests suspension of enforcement, the People's Court shall grant the request. If the effective judgment of the People's Court does not revoke the decision declaring the patent right invalid, the patentee shall compensate for the losses caused to the other party by the continued enforcement; if the decision declaring the patent right invalid is revoked by an effective judgment of the People's Court and the patent right remains valid, the People's Court may directly enforce the aforesaid counter-security property based on the judgment or mediation statement referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在法定期限内对宣告专利权无效的决定不向人民法院起诉或者起诉后生效裁判未撤销该决定，当事人根据该决定依法申请再审，请求撤销宣告专利权无效前人民法院作出但未执行的专利侵权的判决、调解书的，人民法院应当再审。当事人根据该决定，依法申请终结执行宣告专利权无效前人民法院作出但未执行的专利侵权的判决、调解书的，人民法院应当裁定终结执行。

**Article 30** If, within the statutory period, a party does not file a lawsuit with the People's Court against a decision declaring the patent right invalid, or after filing a lawsuit, the effective judgment does not revoke the decision, and the party, based on that decision, applies for a retrial according to law and requests the revocation of a judgment or mediation statement on patent infringement made by the People's Court before the declaration of invalidity of the patent right but not yet enforced, the People's Court shall conduct a retrial. If the party, based on that decision, applies according to law for the termination of enforcement of a judgment or mediation statement on patent infringement made by the People's Court before the declaration of invalidity of the patent right but not yet enforced, the People's Court shall rule to terminate enforcement.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本解释自2016年4月1日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的相关司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 31** This Interpretation shall come into force as of April 1, 2016. If any relevant judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
