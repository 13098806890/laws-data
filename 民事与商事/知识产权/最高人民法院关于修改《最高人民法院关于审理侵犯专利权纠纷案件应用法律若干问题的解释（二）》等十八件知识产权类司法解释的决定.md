# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》等十八件知识产权类司法解释的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕19号  
**公布日期**：2020-12-23  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）〉等十八件知识产权类司法解释的决定》已于2020年12月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年1月1日起施行。 2020年12月29日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷 案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》等 十八件知识产权类司法解释的决定 法释〔2020〕19号 根据审判实践需要，经最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》等十八件司法解释作如下修改： 一、修改《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》 1.将引言修改为： “为正确审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国专利法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。” 2.将第二条修改为： “权利人在专利侵权诉讼中主张的权利要求被国务院专利行政部门宣告无效的，审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件的人民法院可以裁定驳回权利人基于该无效权利要求的起诉。 有证据证明宣告上述权利要求无效的决定被生效的行政判决撤销的，权利人可以另行起诉。 专利权人另行起诉的，诉讼时效期间从本条第二款所称行政判决书送达之日起计算。”

### 正文

一、修改《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》  
1.将引言修改为：  
“为正确审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国专利法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。”  
2.将第二条修改为：  
“权利人在专利侵权诉讼中主张的权利要求被国务院专利行政部门宣告无效的，审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件的人民法院可以裁定驳回权利人基于该无效权利要求的起诉。  
有证据证明宣告上述权利要求无效的决定被生效的行政判决撤销的，权利人可以另行起诉。  
专利权人另行起诉的，诉讼时效期间从本条第二款所称行政判决书送达之日起计算。”  
3.将第六条修改为：  
“人民法院可以运用与涉案专利存在分案申请关系的其他专利及其专利审查档案、生效的专利授权确权裁判文书解释涉案专利的权利要求。  
专利审查档案，包括专利审查、复审、无效程序中专利申请人或者专利权人提交的书面材料，国务院专利行政部门制作的审查意见通知书、会晤记录、口头审理记录、生效的专利复审请求审查决定书和专利权无效宣告请求审查决定书等。”  
4.将第二十一条修改为：  
“明知有关产品系专门用于实施专利的材料、设备、零部件、中间物等，未经专利权人许可，为生产经营目的将该产品提供给他人实施了侵犯专利权的行为，权利人主张该提供者的行为属于民法典第一千一百六十九条规定的帮助他人实施侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持。  
明知有关产品、方法被授予专利权，未经专利权人许可，为生产经营目的积极诱导他人实施了侵犯专利权的行为，权利人主张该诱导者的行为属于民法典第一千一百六十九条规定的教唆他人实施侵权行为的，人民法院应予支持。”

**Article None** I. Amendments to the Interpretation (II) of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Patent Infringement Dispute Cases  
1. The preamble is amended to read:  
"For the purpose of correctly trying patent infringement dispute cases, this Interpretation is formulated in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, and in light of trial practice."  
2. Article 2 is amended to read:  
"Where a claim asserted by the right holder in a patent infringement lawsuit is declared invalid by the patent administrative department of the State Council, the People's Court trying the patent infringement dispute case may rule to dismiss the prosecution of the right holder based on the invalid claim.  
Where there is evidence proving that the decision declaring the said claim invalid is revoked by an effective administrative judgment, the right holder may institute a separate prosecution.  
Where the patentee institutes a separate prosecution, the limitation of action shall be calculated from the date of service of the administrative judgment referred to in the second paragraph of this Article."  
3. Article 6 is amended to read:  
"A People's Court may use other patents that have a divisional application relationship with the patent involved in the case and their patent examination files, and effective patent grant and validity adjudication documents to interpret the claims of the patent involved in the case.  
Patent examination files include written materials submitted by the patent applicant or patentee in the patent examination, reexamination, and invalidation proceedings, examination opinions notices, meeting records, oral hearing records, effective patent reexamination request examination decisions, and patent invalidation declaration request examination decisions made by the patent administrative department of the State Council, etc."  
4. Article 21 is amended to read:  
"Where a person knowingly provides a product that is specifically used for implementing a patent, such as materials, equipment, components, or intermediates, to another person for the purpose of production and business operations without the permission of the patentee, and the other person commits patent infringement, and the right holder claims that the act of the provider constitutes assisting another person in committing an infringement as stipulated in Article 1169 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support the claim.  
Where a person knowingly that a product or a method is granted a patent, and actively induces another person to commit patent infringement for the purpose of production and business operations without the permission of the patentee, and the right holder claims that the act of the inducer constitutes instigating another person in committing an infringement as stipulated in Article 1169 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support the claim."

四、修改《最高人民法院关于审理商标民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》  
1.将引言修改为：  
“为了正确审理商标纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国商标法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，就适用法律若干问题解释如下：”  
2.将第一条修改为：  
“下列行为属于商标法第五十七条第（七）项规定的给他人注册商标专用权造成其他损害的行为：  
（一）将与他人注册商标相同或者相近似的文字作为企业的字号在相同或者类似商品上突出使用，容易使相关公众产生误认的；  
（二）复制、摹仿、翻译他人注册的驰名商标或其主要部分在不相同或者不相类似商品上作为商标使用，误导公众，致使该驰名商标注册人的利益可能受到损害的；  
（三）将与他人注册商标相同或者相近似的文字注册为域名，并且通过该域名进行相关商品交易的电子商务，容易使相关公众产生误认的。”  
3.将第二条修改为：  
“依据商标法第十三条第二款的规定，复制、摹仿、翻译他人未在中国注册的驰名商标或其主要部分，在相同或者类似商品上作为商标使用，容易导致混淆的，应当承担停止侵害的民事法律责任。”  
4.将第三条修改为：  
“商标法第四十三条规定的商标使用许可包括以下三类：  
（一）独占使用许可，是指商标注册人在约定的期间、地域和以约定的方式，将该注册商标仅许可一个被许可人使用，商标注册人依约定不得使用该注册商标；  
（二）排他使用许可，是指商标注册人在约定的期间、地域和以约定的方式，将该注册商标仅许可一个被许可人使用，商标注册人依约定可以使用该注册商标但不得另行许可他人使用该注册商标；  
（三）普通使用许可，是指商标注册人在约定的期间、地域和以约定的方式，许可他人使用其注册商标，并可自行使用该注册商标和许可他人使用其注册商标。”  
5.将第四条修改为：

（四）要求技术接受方接受并非实施技术必不可少的附带条件，包括购买非必需的技术、原材料、产品、设备、服务以及接收非必需的人员等；  
（五）不合理地限制技术接受方购买原材料、零部件、产品或者设备等的渠道或者来源；  
（六）禁止技术接受方对合同标的技术知识产权的有效性提出异议或者对提出异议附加条件。”

**Article None** 17. Revision of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Technology Contract Dispute Cases  
1. The preamble is revised as follows:  
"For the purpose of correctly hearing technology contract dispute cases, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws, and in light of judicial practice, the following interpretation is hereby made on the relevant issues."  
2. Article 1 is revised as follows:  
"A 'technological achievement' means a technical solution relating to a product, process, material, or improvement thereof, made by using scientific and technological knowledge, information, and experience, including patents, patent applications, technical secrets, computer software, layout designs of integrated circuits, new plant varieties, etc.  
A 'technical secret' means technical information that is not known to the public, has commercial value, and for which the rights holder has taken appropriate confidentiality measures."  
3. Article 2 is revised as follows:  
"'Performing the tasks assigned by the legal person or unincorporated organization' as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 847 of the Civil Code includes:  
(1) fulfilling the job responsibilities of the legal person or unincorporated organization or undertaking other technology development tasks assigned by it;  
(2) continuing to engage in technology development work related to the job responsibilities or tasks assigned by the original legal person or unincorporated organization within one year after leaving office, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where the legal person or unincorporated organization and its employee have an agreement on the rights and interests of the technological achievements completed by the employee during the term of office or after leaving office, the People's Court shall confirm it in accordance with the agreement."  
4. Article 3 is revised as follows:  
"'Material and technical conditions' as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 847 of the Civil Code includes funds, equipment, apparatus, raw materials, undisclosed technical information and materials, etc."  
5. Article 4 is revised as follows:  
"'Primarily using the material and technical conditions of the legal person or unincorporated organization' as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 847 of the Civil Code includes the circumstances where the employee, during the research and development process of the technological achievement, uses all or most of the material conditions such as funds, equipment, apparatus, or raw materials of the legal person or unincorporated organization, and these material conditions have a substantial impact on the formation of the technological achievement; it also includes the circumstance where the substantive content of the technological achievement is completed on the basis of the legal person's or unincorporated organization's undisclosed technological achievement or staged technological achievement. However, the following circumstances are excluded:  
(1) where an agreement has been made to return the funds or pay usage fees for using the material and technical conditions provided by the legal person or unincorporated organization;  
(2) where, after the completion of the technological achievement, the material and technical conditions of the legal person or unincorporated organization are used to verify or test the technical solution."  
6. Article 5 is revised as follows:  
"Where a technological achievement completed by an individual belongs to the tasks assigned by the original legal person or unincorporated organization and also primarily uses the material and technical conditions of the current legal person or unincorporated organization, the rights and interests shall be confirmed in accordance with the agreement reached between the natural person's original and current legal person or unincorporated organization. If no agreement can be reached, the rights and interests shall be reasonably shared by both parties according to their respective contributions to the completion of the technological achievement."  
7. Article 6 is revised as follows:  
"'The person who completed the job-related technological achievement' as mentioned in Article 847 of the Civil Code and 'the individual who completed the technological achievement' as mentioned in Article 848 include those who have made creative contributions to the technological achievement individually or jointly, that is, the inventor or designer of the technological achievement. When identifying creative contributions, the People's Court shall decompose the substantive technical components of the technological achievement involved. The person who proposes the substantive technical components and thereby realizes the technical solution is the person who has made creative contributions.  
Persons who provide funds, equipment, materials, test conditions, carry out organization and management, assist in drawing, collating materials, translating literature, etc., shall not be considered as the persons who completed the job-related technological achievement or the individuals who completed the technological achievement."  
8. Article 7 is revised as follows:  
"A technology contract concluded by a research organization without the status of a civil subject, if authorized or ratified by a legal person or unincorporated organization, shall be deemed as a contract concluded by the legal person or unincorporated organization, and the legal person or unincorporated organization shall bear the liability; if not authorized or ratified by the legal person or unincorporated organization, the members of the research organization shall jointly bear the liability, unless the legal person or unincorporated organization has benefited from the contract, in which case it shall bear corresponding liability within the scope of its benefit.  
The research organization without the status of a civil subject as mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes a research group, studio, etc., established by a legal person or unincorporated organization to engage in technology research and development, transfer, and other activities."  
9. Article 9 is revised as follows:  
"Where a party, by fraudulent means, enters into an entrusted development contract with another party for the purpose of developing its existing technological achievement as the subject of research and development, collecting research and development fees, or enters into entrusted development contracts with two or more clients successively for the same research and development project, repeatedly collecting research and development fees, thereby causing the other party to conclude a contract contrary to its true intention, and the damaged party requests the revocation of the contract in accordance with Article 148 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such a request."  
10. Article 10 is revised as follows:  
"The following circumstances fall under 'illegal monopoly of technology' as mentioned in Article 850 of the Civil Code:  
(1) restricting one party from conducting new research and development based on the technology subject matter of the contract or restricting its use of the improved technology, or the conditions for the exchange of improvements between the parties are unequal, including requiring one party to provide its self-improved technology to the other party free of charge, transfer it on a non-reciprocal basis, or exclusively or jointly own the intellectual property rights of the improved technology free of charge;  
(2) restricting one party from obtaining technologies similar to or competing with those provided by the technology provider from other sources;  
(3) hindering one party from fully implementing the technology subject matter of the contract in a reasonable manner according to market demand, including unreasonably restricting the quantity, variety, price, sales channels, and export markets of products or services produced by the technology recipient using the technology subject matter of the contract;  
(4) requiring the technology recipient to accept ancillary conditions that are not essential for the implementation of the technology, including purchasing unnecessary technology, raw materials, products, equipment, services, and accepting unnecessary personnel;  
(5) unreasonably restricting the channels or sources for the technology recipient to purchase raw materials, components, products, or equipment;  
(6) prohibiting the technology recipient from challenging the validity of the intellectual property rights related to the technology subject matter of the contract or attaching conditions to such challenges."  
11. Article 11 is revised as follows:  
"After a technology contract is invalidated or revoked, if the research developer under a technology development contract, the assignor under a technology assignment contract, the licensor under a technology license contract, or the受托人 (trustee) under a technology consultation contract or a technology service contract has performed or partially performed the agreed obligations, and the fault causing the invalidity or revocation lies with the other party, the People's Court may determine that the research and development funds, technology use fees, and remuneration for consultation services that shall be collected for the performed part constitute losses incurred by the other party due to the invalidity or revocation.  
After a technology contract is invalidated or revoked, if the parties cannot reach a new agreement on the ownership and benefit-sharing of a new technological achievement completed in performance of the contract or a subsequent improvement based on another person's technological achievement, the People's Court may rule that the party who completed the technological achievement shall enjoy the rights."  
12. Article 12 is revised as follows:  
"According to the provisions of Article 850 of the Civil Code, after a technology contract that infringes upon another person's technical secrets is confirmed invalid, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, a party who has acquired the technical secret in good faith may continue to use the technical secret within the scope as acquired, but shall pay a reasonable usage fee to the rights holder and shall bear the obligation of confidentiality.  
Where the parties maliciously collude, or one party knows or should have known that the other party is infringing yet still enters into or performs a contract with it, it shall constitute a joint infringement, and the People's Court shall order the infringers to bear joint and several compensation liability and the obligation of confidentiality, and the party who acquired the technical secret as a result shall not continue to use it."  
13. Article 13 is revised as follows:  
"Where a dispute arises between a person who may continue to use the technical secret in accordance with paragraph 1 of the preceding article and the rights holder over the payment of usage fees, either party may request the People's Court to handle the matter. If the person continues to use the technical secret but refuses to pay the usage fees, the People's Court may, upon the request of the rights holder, order the user to cease use.  
When determining the usage fee, the People's Court may reasonably determine it based on the usage fee usually charged by the rights holder for licensing the technical secret, or the usage fee paid by the user for obtaining the technical secret, taking into account the research and development costs of the technical secret, the degree of transformation and application of the achievement, and the scale of use and economic benefits of the user.  
Regardless of whether the user continues to use the technical secret, the People's Court shall order the user to pay the usage fee for the period during which the technical secret has been used. The usage fee already paid by the user to the assignor or licensor under the invalid contract shall be returned by the assignor or licensor."  
14. Article 14 is revised as follows:  
"Where the parties have not agreed or have made an unclear agreement on the price, remuneration, and usage fee of a technology contract, the People's Court may handle the matter according to the following principles:  
(1) For technology development contracts, technology assignment contracts, and technology license contracts, reasonably determine based on the research and development costs, advancement, degree of implementation, transformation, and application of the relevant technological achievement, the rights and interests enjoyed and liabilities borne by the parties, and the economic benefits of the technological achievement;  
(2) For technology consultation contracts and technology service contracts, reasonably determine based on the technical content, quality, and quantity of the relevant consultation and service work, as well as the economic benefits already generated and expected to be generated.  
If the price, remuneration, or usage fee of a technology contract includes non-technical amounts, they shall be calculated separately."  
15. Article 15 is revised as follows:  
"Where one party to a technology contract delays in performing its main obligation, and after a demand for performance has been made, fails to perform within 30 days, the other party claims to rescind the contract pursuant to Article 563, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such a claim.  
If a period for performance is attached to the demand notice and such period exceeds 30 days, the People's Court shall determine that such period constitutes the reasonable period specified in Article 563, paragraph 1, item (3) of the Civil Code."  
16. Article 17 is revised as follows:  
"The terms 'new technology, new product, new process, new variety, or new material and its system' mentioned in paragraph 1 of Article 851 of the Civil Code include technical solutions such as products, processes, materials, and their systems that the parties did not possess at the time of concluding the technology contract, except for the modification of existing products, changes in processes, adjustments of material formulations that are not technologically innovative, and the verification, testing, and use of technological achievements."  
17. Article 18 is revised as follows:  
"A 'technology transformation contract' as specified in paragraph 4 of Article 851 of the Civil Code, which refers to 'a contract concluded between the parties for the transformation and implementation of technological achievements of practical value', means a contract between parties concerning technological achievements of practical value that have not yet been industrialized, including staged technological achievements, aiming at the industrialization application of such technological achievements, and stipulating subsequent experimentation, development, application, and other content."  
18. Article 19 is revised as follows:  
"'Participating in research and development work through division of labor' as mentioned in Article 855 of the Civil Code includes the parties jointly or separately undertaking work such as design, process, experimentation, and trial production according to the agreed plan and division of labor.  
Where one party to a technology development contract only provides material conditions such as funds, equipment, and materials or undertakes auxiliary coordination matters, and the other party undertakes the research and development work, the contract shall be classified as an entrusted development contract."  
19. Article 20 is revised as follows:  
"'Both parties have the right to use and transfer' as mentioned in Article 861 of the Civil Code includes the right of each party to use the technical secret itself or license others to use it by way of an ordinary license, without the consent of the other party, and to exclusively enjoy the profits derived therefrom. If one party assigns the right to transfer the technical secret achievement to another person, or licenses another person to use the technical secret by way of an exclusive or sole license, without the consent or ratification of the other party, such assignment or license shall be deemed invalid."  
20. Article 21 is revised as follows:  
"A party to a technology development contract who, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code or the agreement, implements the patent or uses the technical secret itself, but due to the lack of conditions for independent implementation or use, licenses others to implement or use it by way of an ordinary license, may be permitted."  
21. "III. Technology Assignment Contracts" is revised as:  
"III. Technology Assignment Contracts and Technology License Contracts."  
22. Article 22 is revised as follows:  
"A contract concluded regarding technological achievements yet to be researched and developed, or knowledge, technology, experience, and information not involving patents, patent applications, or technical secrets, shall not be considered a technology assignment contract or technology license contract as specified in Article 862 of the Civil Code.  
A technology assignment contract that includes provisions concerning the assignor providing special equipment, raw materials, or providing relevant technical consultation or technical services to the assignee for the implementation of the technology shall be a component part of the technology assignment contract. Disputes arising therefrom shall be handled in accordance with the provisions on technology assignment contracts.  
Where parties enter into a joint venture contract by way of technology contribution, but the technology contributor does not participate in the management and operation of the joint venture, and the joint venture or the other party is agreed to pay the technology price or usage fee in the form of a guaranteed minimum clause, such contract shall be deemed as a technology assignment contract or a technology license contract."  
23. Article 23 is revised as follows:  
"Where a party to a patent application right assignment contract requests the rescission of the contract on the grounds that the patent application has been rejected or is deemed withdrawn, if such fact occurs before the registration of the assignment of the patent application right in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 10 of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall support the request; if it occurs after the registration of the assignment, the request shall not be supported, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
Where a patent application is rejected due to the existence of an earlier patent application for the same invention that has not been published at the time the patent application right assignment contract is concluded, and a party requests the rescission of the contract pursuant to Article 563, paragraph 1, item (4) of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such a request."  
24. Article 25 is revised as follows:  
"Patent exploitation licensing includes the following forms:  
(1) Exclusive exploitation license: the licensor licenses the patent to only one licensee for exploitation within the scope agreed upon, and the licensor shall not exploit the patent in accordance with the agreement;  
(2) Sole exploitation license: the licensor licenses the patent to only one licensee for exploitation within the scope agreed upon, but the licensor may exploit the patent itself in accordance with the agreement;  
(3) Ordinary exploitation license: the licensor licenses the patent to another person for exploitation within the scope agreed upon, and may also exploit the patent itself.  
If the parties have not agreed or have made an unclear agreement on the form of patent exploitation license, it shall be determined as an ordinary exploitation license. If a patent exploitation license contract stipulates that the licensee may sublicense the patent to others for exploitation, such sublicense shall be determined as an ordinary exploitation license, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
The method of licensing technical secrets shall be determined by reference to the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this article."  
25. Article 26 is revised as follows:  
"The licensor under a patent exploitation license contract shall have the obligation to maintain the validity of the patent right during the term of the contract, including paying the annual patent fees in accordance with the law and actively responding to requests from others for a declaration of invalidity of the patent right, unless otherwise agreed by the parties."  
26. Article 27 is revised as follows:  
"Where the licensor under a sole exploitation license contract does not have the conditions to independently exploit its patent, and licenses another person to exploit the patent by way of an ordinary license, the People's Court may determine that the licensor itself is exploiting the patent, unless otherwise agreed by the parties."  
27. Article 28 is revised as follows:  
"'The scope of exploiting the patent or using the technical secret' as mentioned in Article 864 of the Civil Code includes the term, territory, method of exploiting the patent or using the technical secret, and the personnel who have access to the technical secret, etc.  
If the parties have not agreed or have made an unclear agreement on the term for exploiting the patent or using the technical secret, the assignee or licensee shall not be limited by any term in exploiting the patent or using the technical secret."  
28. Article 29 is revised as follows:  
"With respect to a license contract concluded between the parties concerning a technological achievement for which a patent application has been filed, before the patent application is published, the relevant provisions on technical secret license contracts shall apply; after the application for invention patent is published but before the patent is granted, the relevant provisions on patent exploitation license contracts shall apply by reference; after the patent is granted, the original contract shall be a patent exploitation license contract, and the relevant provisions on patent exploitation license contracts shall apply.  
The People's Court shall not determine a contract invalid on the ground that the parties have concluded a patent exploitation license contract with respect to technology for which a patent application has been filed but not yet granted."  
29. Article 30 is revised as follows:  
"'Specific technical project' as mentioned in paragraph 1 of Article 878 of the Civil Code includes soft science research projects concerning the coordinated development of science and technology and the economy and society, and professional technical projects involving investigation, analysis, demonstration, evaluation, and prediction using scientific knowledge and technical means to promote scientific and technological progress and management modernization, improve economic and social benefits, etc."  
30. Paragraph 1 of Article 31 and paragraph 1 of Article 35 are deleted.  
31. Article 33 is revised as follows:  
"'Specific technical issue' as mentioned in paragraph 2 of Article 878 of the Civil Code includes professional and technical issues that require the use of professional technical knowledge, experience, and information to solve, such as improving product structure, improving process flow, improving product quality, reducing product costs, saving resources and energy, protecting the resource environment, achieving safe operation, and improving economic and social benefits."  
32. Article 34 is revised as follows:  
"Where a party provides technology that has entered the public domain in the name of technology assignment or technology license, or where the technology subject matter of a technology assignment contract or technology license contract enters the public domain during the performance of the contract, but the technology provider provides technical guidance, imparts technical knowledge, and solves specific technical issues for the other party in accordance with the agreed conditions, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions on technology service contracts, and the agreed technology assignment fee or usage fee may be deemed as the remuneration and expenses for providing technical services, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
If, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the technology assignment fee or usage fee considered as the remuneration and expenses for providing technical services is evidently unreasonable, the People's Court may reasonably determine it upon the request of the parties."  
33. Article 36 is revised as follows:  
"A 'technology training contract' as specified in Article 887 of the Civil Code means a contract concluded by one party entrusting another to carry out professional technical training and guidance on a specific project for designated trainees, excluding vocational training, cultural studies, and spare-time education according to the plan of the industry, legal person, or unincorporated organization."  
34. Article 38 is revised as follows:  
"A 'technology intermediary contract' as specified in Article 887 of the Civil Code means a contract concluded by one party to use its knowledge, technology, experience, and information to make contacts and introductions for another party and a third party for concluding a technology contract, and to provide specialized services for the performance of the contract."  
35. Article 42 is revised as follows:  
"Where a party incorporates the content of a technology contract and other contracts, or the content of different types of technology contracts, into one single contract, the nature and cause of action of the case shall be determined based on the rights and obligations in dispute between the parties.  
If the name of a technology contract is inconsistent with the agreed rights and obligations, the type and cause of action of the contract shall be determined based on the agreed content of rights and obligations.  
Where a technology assignment contract or a technology license contract stipulates that the assignor or licensor is responsible for exclusive sales or buyback of products manufactured by the assignee or licensee using the technology subject matter of the contract, and a dispute arises solely due to the assignor's or licensor's failure to perform or fully perform the exclusive sales or buyback obligation, and no technical issues are involved, the cause of action shall be determined based on the content of rights and obligations agreed in the exclusive sales or buyback clause."  
36. Article 45 is revised as follows:  
"When a third party files a claim with the People's Court hearing a technology contract dispute case regarding the ownership or infringement of the technology subject matter of the contract, if the court receiving the case also has jurisdiction over such ownership or infringement dispute, it may consolidate the trial of the ownership or infringement dispute with the contract dispute; if the court receiving the case does not have jurisdiction over such dispute, it shall inform the party to file a separate lawsuit with the competent People's Court or transfer the already accepted ownership or infringement dispute case to the competent People's Court. After the ownership or infringement dispute is accepted in a separate case, the proceedings in the contract dispute shall be suspended.  
In a lawsuit concerning a patent exploitation license contract, if the licensee or a third party requests the National Intellectual Property Administration to declare the patent right invalid, the People's Court may not suspend the proceedings. If the patent right is declared invalid during the trial of the case, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 47 of the Patent Law."  
37. Article 46 is revised as follows:  
"Where there are disputes over computer software development contracts, and the Copyright Law or other laws or administrative regulations have provisions, those provisions shall apply; if there are no such provisions, the provisions of Part III, Division I of the Civil Code shall apply, and the provisions of Part III, Division II, Chapter 20 of the Civil Code and the relevant provisions of this Interpretation may be applied by reference."

十八、修改《最高人民法院关于北京、上海、广州知识产权法院案件管辖的规定》
