### 最高人民法院关于假冒商标案件两个问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于假冒商标案件两个问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔 1988〕73号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川法研〔 1988〕32号《关于假冒商标案件确认犯罪主体和适用法律问题的请示》收悉。经我们研究，并征得有关部门同意，答复如下：  
  
（一）我院 1985年 5月 9日法（研）复（ 1985）28号批复规定，企业事业单位或者个体工商业者，假冒他人注册商标，包括非法制造或者销售他人注册商标标识的，可以直接依照刑法第 1 2 7条定罪判刑。今后，对于没有营业执照的个人，违反商标法规，假冒他人注册商标，包括非法制造或者销售他人注册商标标识，构成犯罪的，也应按假冒商标罪论处。  
  
（二）为获取非法利润，假冒他人注册商标，包括非法制造或者销售他人注册商标标识，非法经营或者非法获利的数额巨大，情节严重的，其行为触犯了假冒商标罪，也触犯了投机倒把罪，应按其中的重罪即投机倒把罪定罪处刑。  
  
1988年12月26日

**Article 1** Reply on Two Issues Concerning Cases of Counterfeiting Trademarks  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1988] No. 73  
Sichuan Higher People's Court:  
Your court's Chuan Fa Yan [1988] No. 32, "Request for Instructions on the Subject of Criminal Liability and Applicable Law in Cases of Counterfeiting Trademarks," has been received. Upon our study and with the consent of the relevant departments, the reply is as follows:  
(I) The Reply Fa (Yan) Fu (1985) No. 28 of May 9, 1985, issued by our court, stipulates that enterprises, institutions, or individual industrial and commercial households that counterfeit another person's registered trademark, including illegally manufacturing or selling another person's registered trademark marks, may be directly convicted and sentenced in accordance with Article 127 of the Criminal Law. Henceforth, any individual without a business license who, in violation of trademark regulations, counterfeits another person's registered trademark, including illegally manufacturing or selling such marks, thereby committing a crime, shall also be punished under the crime of counterfeiting trademarks.  
(II) For the purpose of obtaining illegal profits, a person who counterfeits another person's registered trademark (including illegally manufacturing or selling such marks), if the amount of illegal business or illegal profits is huge and the circumstances are serious, the act violates both the crime of counterfeiting trademarks and the crime of speculation and profiteering. Punishment shall be imposed according to the more serious of the two crimes, namely, the crime of speculation and profiteering.  
December 26, 1988
