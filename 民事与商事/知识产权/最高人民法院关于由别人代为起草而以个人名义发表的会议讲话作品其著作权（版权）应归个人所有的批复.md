### 最高人民法院关于由别人代为起草而以个人名义发表的会议讲话作品其著作权（版权）应归个人所有的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于由别人代为起草而以个人名义发表的  
  
会议讲话作品其著作权（版权）应归个人所有的批复  
  
（1988）民他字第21号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1988）沪高民他字第 3号《关于金文明与罗竹风著作权纠纷案的请示报告》收悉。经研究并征求有关部门意见，答复如下：  
  
《汉语大词典》主编罗竹风，在中国语言学会成立大会上关于介绍《汉语大词典》编纂工作进展情况的发言稿，虽然是由《汉语大词典》编纂处工作人员金文明等四人分头执笔起草，但他们在起草时就明确是为罗竹风个人发言作准备的；罗竹风也是以主编身份组织、主持拟定发言提纲，并自行修改定稿，嗣后以其个人名义在大会上作发言。因此，罗竹风的发言稿不属于共同创作，其著作权（版权）应归罗竹风个人所有。罗竹风同意在其他刊物署名刊载发言稿全文，不构成侵害他人著作权。对金文明等人在执笔起草发言稿中付出的劳动，罗竹风在获得稿酬后，可给予适当的劳务报酬。  
  
此复  
  
1988年6月9日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue that the Copyright of a Speech Work Drafted by Others but Published in the Individual's Name Shall Belong to the Individual  
(1988) Min Ta Zi No. 21  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
Your court's Request for Instructions Report No. (1988) Hu Gao Min Ta Zi No. 3 on the Copyright Dispute Case between Jin Wenming and Luo Zhufeng has been received. After study and consultation with relevant departments, the reply is as follows:  
Luo Zhufeng, editor-in-chief of the Chinese Great Dictionary, gave a speech at the founding meeting of the Chinese Language Society regarding the progress of the compilation work of the Chinese Great Dictionary. Although the draft speech was written by four staff members of the Chinese Great Dictionary Compilation Office, including Jin Wenming, they were clearly preparing for Luo Zhufeng's personal speech when drafting; Luo Zhufeng also organized and presided over the formulation of the speech outline in his capacity as editor-in-chief, and revised and finalized the draft himself, and later delivered the speech at the meeting in his personal capacity. Therefore, Luo Zhufeng's speech draft does not constitute joint creation, and its copyright shall belong to Luo Zhufeng personally. Luo Zhufeng's consent to publish the full text of the speech under his name in other publications does not constitute infringement of others' copyright. For the labor contributed by Jin Wenming and others in drafting the speech, Luo Zhufeng may pay appropriate remuneration after receiving the manuscript fee.  
This is the reply.  
June 9, 1988
