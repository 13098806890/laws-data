# 最高人民法院关于侵犯商标专用权如何计算损失赔偿额和侵权期间问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1985〕53号

---

### 正文

一、在侵犯商标专用权案件中，被侵权人可以按其所受的实际损失额请求偿。也可以请求将侵权人在侵权期间因侵权所获的利润（指除成本和税金外所有利润）作为赔偿额。对于以上两种计算方法，被侵权人有选择权。

**Article None** 1. In cases of infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark, the infringed party may request compensation based on the amount of actual losses suffered. The infringed party may also request that the profits gained by the infringer during the infringement period due to the infringement (referring to all profits except costs and taxes) be taken as the amount of compensation. The infringed party shall have the right to choose between the above two calculation methods.

二、关于请求损失赔偿的期间计算问题，如果侵权行为发生在《商标法》施前并延续到《商标法》实施后的，侵权期间从《商标法》实施之日（一九三年三月一日）起计算；如果侵权行为发生在《商标法》实施后的，从侵权日起计算。

**Article None** 2. Regarding the calculation of the period for claiming damages, if the infringement occurred before the implementation of the Trademark Law and continued after its implementation, the infringement period shall be calculated from the date of implementation of the Trademark Law (March 1, 1993); if the infringement occurred after the implementation of the Trademark Law, it shall be calculated from the date of infringement.
