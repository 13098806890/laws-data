### 最高人民法院关于指定重庆市中级人民法院办理部分专利纠纷案件的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于指定重庆市中级人民法院  
  
办理部分专利纠纷案件的批复  
  
一九八五年三月二十七日法（经）复〔1985〕18号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（85）川法经字第19号“关于拟指定重庆市中级人民法院办理部分专利纠纷案件的请示报告”收悉。经研究，同意指定重庆市中级人民法院作为审理其辖内属于最高人民法院法（经）发（1985）3号文件（编者注：即《最高人民法院关于开展专利审判工作的几个问题的通知》）所列5—7类专利纠纷案件的第一审法院。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Designating Chongqing Intermediate People's Court  
To Handle Certain Patent Dispute Cases  
March 27, 1985 Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 18  
Sichuan Higher People's Court:  
Your Court's (85) Chuan Fa Jing Zi No. 19 "Request for Instructions on Proposing to Designate Chongqing Intermediate People's Court to Handle Certain Patent Dispute Cases" has been received. Upon study, it is agreed to designate Chongqing Intermediate People's Court as the court of first instance for trial of patent dispute cases of categories 5 to 7 as listed in Document Fa (Jing) Fa [1985] No. 3 of the Supreme People's Court (Editor's note: i.e., the "Notice of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Adjudication of Patent Cases") within its jurisdiction.  
This reply is hereby given.
