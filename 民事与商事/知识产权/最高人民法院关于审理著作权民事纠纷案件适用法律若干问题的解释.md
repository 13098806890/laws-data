# 最高人民法院关于审理著作权民事纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕31号  
**公布日期**：2002-10-12  
**生效日期**：2002-10-15

> （2002年10月12日最高人民法院审判委员会第1246次会议通过　2002年10月12日最高人民法院公告公布　自2002年10月15日起施行） 为了正确审理著作权民事纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国著作权法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律的规定，就适用法律若干问题解释如下： 第一条　人民法院受理以下著作权民事纠纷案件： （一）著作权及与著作权有关权益权属、侵权、合同纠纷案件； （二）申请诉前停止侵犯著作权、与著作权有关权益行为，申请诉前财产保全、诉前证据保全案件； （三）其他著作权、与著作权有关权益纠纷案件。 第二条　著作权民事纠纷案件，由中级以上人民法院管辖。 各高级人民法院根据本辖区的实际情况，可以确定若干基层人民法院管辖第一审著作权民事纠纷案件。 第三条　对著作权行政管理部门查处的侵犯著作权行为，当事人向人民法院提起诉讼追究该行为人民事责任的，人民法院应当受理。 人民法院审理已经过著作权行政管理部门处理的侵犯著作权行为的民事纠纷案件，应当对案件事实进行全面审查。 第四条　因侵犯著作权行为提起的民事诉讼，由著作权法第四十六条、第四十七条所规定侵权行为的实施地、侵权复制品储藏地或者查封扣押地、被告住所地人民法院管辖。 前款规定的侵权复制品储藏地，是指大量或者经常性储存、隐匿侵权复制品所在地；查封扣押地，是指海关、版权、工商等行政机关依法查封、扣押侵权复制品所在地。 第五条　对涉及不同侵权行为实施地的多个被告提起的共同诉讼，原告可以选择其中一个被告的侵权行为实施地人民法院管辖；仅对其中某一被告提起的诉讼，该被告侵权行为实施地的人民法院有管辖权。 第六条　依法成立的著作权集体管理组织，根据著作权人的书面授权，以自己的名义提起诉讼，人民法院应当受理。 第七条　当事人提供的涉及著作权的底稿、原件、合法出版物、著作权登记证书、认证机构出具的证明、取得权利的合同等，可以作为证据。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院受理以下著作权民事纠纷案件：  
（一）著作权及与著作权有关权益权属、侵权、合同纠纷案件；  
（二）申请诉前停止侵犯著作权、与著作权有关权益行为，申请诉前财产保全、诉前证据保全案件；  
（三）其他著作权、与著作权有关权益纠纷案件。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall accept the following civil copyright disputes:  
(1) Cases concerning ownership, infringement, or contract disputes over copyright and rights related to copyright;  
(2) Cases concerning applications for pre-litigation injunctions to cease infringement of copyright or rights related to copyright, and applications for pre-litigation property preservation or pre-litigation evidence preservation;  
(3) Other cases concerning disputes over copyright or rights related to copyright.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　著作权民事纠纷案件，由中级以上人民法院管辖。  
各高级人民法院根据本辖区的实际情况，可以确定若干基层人民法院管辖第一审著作权民事纠纷案件。

**Article 2** Civil copyright disputes shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate people's courts or above.  
Each Higher People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction, designate certain Primary People's Courts to have jurisdiction over first-instance civil copyright disputes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对著作权行政管理部门查处的侵犯著作权行为，当事人向人民法院提起诉讼追究该行为人民事责任的，人民法院应当受理。  
人民法院审理已经过著作权行政管理部门处理的侵犯著作权行为的民事纠纷案件，应当对案件事实进行全面审查。

**Article 3** Where a party initiates an action before the People's Court to impose civil liability on a person for infringement of copyright that has been investigated and punished by the copyright administrative authority, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
When hearing a civil dispute over infringement of copyright that has already been handled by the copyright administrative authority, the People's Court shall conduct a comprehensive review of the facts of the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　因侵犯著作权行为提起的民事诉讼，由[著作权法第四十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-46)、第四十七条所规定侵权行为的实施地、侵权复制品储藏地或者查封扣押地、被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
前款规定的侵权复制品储藏地，是指大量或者经常性储存、隐匿侵权复制品所在地；查封扣押地，是指海关、版权、工商等行政机关依法查封、扣押侵权复制品所在地。

**Article 4** A civil action brought for infringement of copyright shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the place where the infringing act provided for in Articles 46 and 47 of the Copyright Law is committed, or at the place where the infringing reproductions are stored or sealed up or seized, or at the domicile of the defendant.  
The place where infringing reproductions are stored as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means the place where a large quantity of or frequently stored or concealed infringing reproductions are located; the place where the reproductions are sealed up or seized means the place where the customs, copyright, industry and commerce, or other administrative authorities have sealed up or seized the infringing reproductions according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对涉及不同侵权行为实施地的多个被告提起的共同诉讼，原告可以选择其中一个被告的侵权行为实施地人民法院管辖；仅对其中某一被告提起的诉讼，该被告侵权行为实施地的人民法院有管辖权。

**Article 5** For a joint action brought against multiple defendants involving different places where the infringing acts are committed, the plaintiff may choose the People's Court at the place where the infringing act of one of the defendants is committed to have jurisdiction; for an action brought only against a certain defendant, the People's Court at the place where the infringing act of that defendant is committed shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　依法成立的著作权集体管理组织，根据著作权人的书面授权，以自己的名义提起诉讼，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 6** Where a copyright collective management organization established according to law institutes an action in its own name under a written authorization from the copyright owner, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人提供的涉及著作权的底稿、原件、合法出版物、著作权登记证书、认证机构出具的证明、取得权利的合同等，可以作为证据。  
在作品或者制品上署名的自然人、法人或者其他组织视为著作权、与著作权有关权益的权利人，但有相反证明的除外。

**Article 7** The following materials provided by the parties concerning copyright may be used as evidence: manuscripts, originals, legal publications, copyright registration certificates, certificates issued by certification authorities, contracts for obtaining rights, etc.  
A natural person, legal person, or other organization whose name is affixed to a work or product shall be considered the owner of the copyright or rights related to copyright, unless there is evidence to the contrary.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人自行或者委托他人以定购、现场交易等方式购买侵权复制品而取得的实物、发票等，可以作为证据。  
公证人员在未向涉嫌侵权的一方当事人表明身份的情况下，如实对另一方当事人按照前款规定的方式取得的证据和取证过程出具的公证书，应当作为证据使用，但有相反证据的除外。

**Article 8** The physical objects, invoices, etc. obtained by a party itself or through an authorized agent by purchasing infringing reproductions through ordering, on-site transaction, or other means may be used as evidence.  
A notarial certificate issued by a notary public, without disclosing his/her identity to the party suspected of infringement, truthfully recording the evidence obtained by the other party in accordance with the method provided in the preceding paragraph and the process of obtaining evidence, shall be used as evidence, unless there is contrary evidence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[著作权法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-10)第（一）项规定的“公之于众”，是指著作权人自行或者经著作权人许可将作品向不特定的人公开，但不以公众知晓为构成条件。

**Article 9** The term "making public" as provided in Article 10(1) of the Copyright Law means that the copyright owner itself or with the permission of the copyright owner discloses the work to unspecified persons, and the fact that the public becomes aware of the work is not a constitutive element.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[著作权法第十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-15)第二款所指的作品，著作权人是自然人的，其保护期适用[著作权法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-21)第一款的规定；著作权人是法人或其他组织的，其保护期适用[著作权法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-21)第二款的规定。

**Article 10** For the works referred to in Article 15, paragraph 2 of the Copyright Law, if the copyright owner is a natural person, the term of protection shall be governed by the provisions of Article 21, paragraph 1 of the Copyright Law; if the copyright owner is a legal person or other organization, the term of protection shall be governed by the provisions of Article 21, paragraph 2 of the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因作品署名顺序发生的纠纷，人民法院按照下列原则处理：有约定的按约定确定署名顺序；没有约定的，可以按照创作作品付出的劳动、作品排列、作者姓氏笔画等确定署名顺序。

**Article 11** For disputes arising from the order of authorship attribution, the People's Court shall handle them according to the following principles: if there is an agreement, the order of authorship shall be determined in accordance with the agreement; if there is no agreement, the order of authorship may be determined based on the amount of labor contributed to the creation of the work, the arrangement of the work, the number of strokes in the authors' surnames, etc.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　按照[著作权法第十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-17)规定委托作品著作权属于受托人的情形，委托人在约定的使用范围内享有使用作品的权利；双方没有约定使用作品范围的，委托人可以在委托创作的特定目的范围内免费使用该作品。

**Article 12** Where, under the circumstances provided in Article 17 of the Copyright Law, the copyright in a commissioned work belongs to the commissioned person, the commissioning person shall enjoy the right to use the work within the agreed scope of use; if the parties have not agreed on the scope of use, the commissioning person may use the work free of charge within the specific purpose for which the work was commissioned.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　除[著作权法第十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-11)第三款规定的情形外，由他人执笔，本人审阅定稿并以本人名义发表的报告、讲话等作品，著作权归报告人或者讲话人享有。著作权人可以支付执笔人适当的报酬。

**Article 13** Except for the circumstances provided in Article 11, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Law, for works such as reports or speeches that are written by another person but reviewed and finalized by the person who then publishes them under that person's own name, the copyright shall vest in the reporter or speaker. The copyright owner may pay appropriate remuneration to the writer.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人合意以特定人物经历为题材完成的自传体作品，当事人对著作权权属有约定的，依其约定；没有约定的，著作权归该特定人物享有，执笔人或整理人对作品完成付出劳动的，著作权人可以向其支付适当的报酬。

**Article 14** For an autobiographical work completed by the parties based on their agreement on the experiences of a specific person, if the parties have agreed on the ownership of copyright, such agreement shall prevail; if there is no agreement, the copyright shall vest in that specific person. If the writer or compiler contributed labor to the completion of the work, the copyright owner may pay appropriate remuneration to him/her.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　由不同作者就同一题材创作的作品，作品的表达系独立完成并且有创作性的，应当认定作者各自享有独立著作权。

**Article 15** For works created by different authors on the same subject matter, if the expression of the works is independently completed and is original, it shall be determined that each author enjoys an independent copyright.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　通过大众传播媒介传播的单纯事实消息属于[著作权法第五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-5)第（二）项规定的时事新闻。传播报道他人采编的时事新闻，应当注明出处。

**Article 16** Pure factual news disseminated by mass media falls within the category of current affairs news as provided in Article 5(2) of the Copyright Law. When disseminating or reporting current affairs news gathered and edited by others, the source shall be indicated.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[著作权法第三十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-32)第二款规定的转载，是指报纸、期刊登载其他报刊已发表作品的行为。转载未注明被转载作品的作者和最初登载的报刊出处的，应当承担消除影响、赔礼道歉等民事责任。

**Article 17** The term "reprinting" as provided in Article 32, paragraph 2 of the Copyright Law means the act of a newspaper or periodical publishing a work that has already been published in another newspaper or periodical. If the reprint does not indicate the author of the reprinted work and the source of the original newspaper or periodical, the person reprinting shall bear civil liability such as eliminating the adverse effects and making an apology.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[著作权法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-22)第（十）项规定的室外公共场所的艺术作品，是指设置或者陈列在室外社会公众活动处所的雕塑、绘画、书法等艺术作品。  
对前款规定艺术作品的临摹、绘画、摄影、录像人，可以对其成果以合理的方式和范围再行使用，不构成侵权。

**Article 18** The term "works of art located in an outdoor public place" as provided in Article 22(10) of the Copyright Law means works of art such as sculptures, paintings, and calligraphy works that are installed or displayed in outdoor places where the public engages in activities.  
A person who makes a copy, painting, photograph, or video recording of a work of art as provided in the preceding paragraph may further use the results in a reasonable manner and within a reasonable scope, and such use shall not constitute infringement.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　出版者、制作者应当对其出版、制作有合法授权承担举证责任，发行者、出租者应当对其发行或者出租的复制品有合法来源承担举证责任。举证不能的，依据[著作权法第四十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-46)、第四十七条的相应规定承担法律责任。

**Article 19** A publisher or producer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that its publication or production is under lawful authorization; a distributor or lessor shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the reproductions it distributes or leases have a lawful source. If it fails to adduce evidence, it shall bear legal liability pursuant to the relevant provisions of Articles 46 and 47 of the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　出版物侵犯他人著作权的，出版者应当根据其过错、侵权程度及损害后果等承担民事赔偿责任。  
出版者对其出版行为的授权、稿件来源和署名、所编辑出版物的内容等未尽到合理注意义务的，依据[著作权法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)的规定，承担赔偿责任。  
出版者尽了合理注意义务，著作权人也无证据证明出版者应当知道其出版涉及侵权的，依据民法通则第一百一十七条第一款的规定，出版者承担停止侵权、返还其侵权所得利润的民事责任。  
出版者所尽合理注意义务情况，由出版者承担举证责任。

**Article 20** If a publication infringes upon another person's copyright, the publisher shall bear civil compensation liability according to its fault, the degree of infringement, and the consequences of damage.  
If the publisher fails to exercise reasonable care regarding the authorization of its publishing act, the source and authorship of the manuscript, the content of the edited publication, etc., it shall bear compensation liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 48 of the Copyright Law.  
If the publisher has exercised reasonable care and the copyright owner has no evidence to prove that the publisher should have known that its publication involves infringement, the publisher shall bear the civil liability of ceasing the infringement and returning the profits obtained from the infringement, in accordance with Article 117(1) of the General Principles of the Civil Law.  
The publisher shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the reasonable care it has exercised.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　计算机软件用户未经许可或者超过许可范围商业使用计算机软件的，依据[著作权法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-47)第（一）项、[《计算机软件保护条例》第二十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/计算机软件保护条例.md#art-24)第（一）项的规定承担民事责任。

**Article 21** Where a user of computer software commercially uses computer software without permission or beyond the scope of permission, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with Article 47(1) of the Copyright Law and Article 24(1) of the Regulations on the Protection of Computer Software.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　著作权转让合同未采取书面形式的，人民法院依据合同法第三十六条、第三十七条的规定审查合同是否成立。

**Article 22** If a copyright transfer contract is not in written form, the People's Court shall examine whether the contract is established in accordance with Articles 36 and 37 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出版者将著作权人交付出版的作品丢失、毁损致使出版合同不能履行的，依据[著作权法第五十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-53)、民法通则第一百一十七条以及合同法第一百二十二条的规定追究出版者的民事责任。

**Article 23** If a publisher loses or damages the work delivered by the copyright owner for publication, causing the publishing contract to be unperformable, the civil liability of the publisher shall be pursued in accordance with Article 53 of the Copyright Law, Article 117 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and Article 122 of the Contract Law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　权利人的实际损失，可以根据权利人因侵权所造成复制品发行减少量或者侵权复制品销售量与权利人发行该复制品单位利润乘积计算。发行减少量难以确定的，按照侵权复制品市场销售量确定。

**Article 24** The actual loss of the right holder may be calculated according to the decrease in the distribution volume of reproductions caused by the infringement or the product of the sales volume of infringing reproductions and the unit profit of the reproductions distributed by the right holder. If the decrease in distribution volume is difficult to determine, it shall be determined according to the market sales volume of infringing reproductions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　权利人的实际损失或者侵权人的违法所得无法确定的，人民法院根据当事人的请求或者依职权适用[著作权法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)第二款的规定确定赔偿数额。  
人民法院在确定赔偿数额时，应当考虑作品类型、合理使用费、侵权行为性质、后果等情节综合确定。  
当事人按照本条第一款的规定就赔偿数额达成协议的，应当准许。

**Article 25** If the actual loss of the right holder or the illegal gains of the infringer cannot be determined, the People's Court shall, upon request of the parties or ex officio, apply the provisions of Article 48(2) of the Copyright Law to determine the amount of damages.  
When determining the amount of damages, the People's Court shall consider factors such as the type of the work, reasonable royalty, the nature and consequences of the infringement, and other circumstances comprehensively.  
If the parties reach an agreement on the amount of damages in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article, such agreement shall be allowed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　[著作权法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)第一款规定的制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支，包括权利人或者委托代理人对侵权行为进行调查、取证的合理费用。  
人民法院根据当事人的诉讼请求和具体案情，可以将符合国家有关部门规定的律师费用计算在赔偿范围内。

**Article 26** The reasonable expenses incurred for stopping the infringing act as provided in Article 48(1) of the Copyright Law include the reasonable expenses incurred by the right holder or its authorized agent for investigating and adducing evidence of the infringement.  
The People's Court may, based on the litigation claims of the parties and the specific circumstances of the case, include the attorney's fees that comply with the regulations of the relevant state authorities within the scope of compensation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在著作权法修改决定施行前发生的侵犯著作权行为起诉的案件，人民法院于该决定施行后做出判决的，可以参照适用[著作权法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)的规定。

**Article 27** For cases in which an action is brought for an infringement of copyright that occurred before the coming into force of the Decision on the Revision of the Copyright Law, if the People's Court renders a judgment after the coming into force of that Decision, it may apply the provisions of Article 48 of the Copyright Law by reference.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　侵犯著作权的诉讼时效为二年，自著作权人知道或者应当知道侵权行为之日起计算。权利人超过二年起诉的，如果侵权行为在起诉时仍在持续，在该著作权保护期内，人民法院应当判决被告停止侵权行为；侵权损害赔偿数额应当自权利人向人民法院起诉之日起向前推算二年计算。

**Article 28** The limitation of action for infringement of copyright shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the copyright owner knew or should have known of the infringing act. If the right holder brings an action after the expiration of two years, but the infringing act is still ongoing at the time of prosecution, within the copyright protection period, the People's Court shall order the defendant to cease the infringing act; the amount of damages for infringement shall be calculated by counting back two years from the date on which the right holder filed the action with the People's Court.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对[著作权法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-47)规定的侵权行为，人民法院根据当事人的请求除追究行为人民事责任外，还可以依据民法通则第一百三十四条第三款的规定给予民事制裁，罚款数额可以参照《中华人民共和国著作权法实施条例》的有关规定确定。  
著作权行政管理部门对相同的侵权行为已经给予行政处罚的，人民法院不再予以民事制裁。

**Article 29** For the infringing acts provided in Article 47 of the Copyright Law, the People's Court may, in addition to pursuing the civil liability of the infringer upon the request of the parties, also impose civil sanctions in accordance with Article 134(3) of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and the amount of the fine may be determined by reference to the relevant provisions of the Regulations for the Implementation of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the copyright administrative authority has already imposed an administrative penalty for the same infringing act, the People's Court shall not impose civil sanctions again.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对2001年10月27日前发生的侵犯著作权行为，当事人于2001年10月27日后向人民法院提出申请采取责令停止侵权行为或者证据保全措施的，适用[著作权法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-49)、第五十条的规定。  
人民法院采取诉前措施，参照《最高人民法院关于诉前停止侵犯注册商标专用权行为和保全证据适用法律问题的解释》的规定办理。

**Article 30** For infringement of copyright that occurred before October 27, 2001, if a party files an application with the People's Court after October 27, 2001, for an order to cease the infringing act or for evidence preservation, the provisions of Articles 49 and 50 of the Copyright Law shall apply.  
When the People's Court takes pre-litigation measures, it shall act by reference to the provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of Laws in Pre-litigation Injunctions to Cease Infringement of the Exclusive Right to Use a Registered Trademark and Evidence Preservation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　除本解释另行规定外，2001年10月27日以后人民法院受理的著作权民事纠纷案件，涉及2001年10月27日前发生的民事行为的，适用修改前著作权法的规定；涉及该日期以后发生的民事行为的，适用修改后著作权法的规定；涉及该日期前发生，持续到该日期后的民事行为的，适用修改后著作权法的规定。

**Article 31** Unless otherwise provided in this Interpretation, for civil copyright disputes accepted by the People's Court after October 27, 2001, if the civil act involved occurred before October 27, 2001, the provisions of the Copyright Law before revision shall apply; if the civil act occurred after that date, the provisions of the Copyright Law after revision shall apply; if the civil act began before that date and continues after that date, the provisions of the Copyright Law after revision shall apply.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　以前的有关规定与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 32** If any previous relevant provisions are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
