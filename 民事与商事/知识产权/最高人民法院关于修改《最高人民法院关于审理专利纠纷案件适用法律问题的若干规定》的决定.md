# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕4号  
**公布日期**：2015-01-19  
**生效日期**：2015-02-01

> 最高人民法院《关于修改〈最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定〉的决定》已于2015年1月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1641次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年2月1日起施行。 2015年1月29日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件 适用法律问题的若干规定》的决定 法释〔2015〕4号 根据最高人民法院审判委员会第1641次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》作如下修改： 一、将第五条第二款修改为:“侵权行为地包括：被诉侵犯发明、实用新型专利权的产品的制造、使用、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；专利方法使用行为的实施地，依照该专利方法直接获得的产品的使用、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；外观设计专利产品的制造、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；假冒他人专利的行为实施地。上述侵权行为的侵权结果发生地。” 二、将第八条第一款修改为:“对申请日在2009年10月1日前（不含该日）的实用新型专利提起侵犯专利权诉讼，原告可以出具由国务院专利行政部门作出的检索报告；对申请日在2009年10月1日以后的实用新型或者外观设计专利提起侵犯专利权诉讼，原告可以出具由国务院专利行政部门作出的专利权评价报告。根据案件审理需要，人民法院可以要求原告提交检索报告或者专利权评价报告。原告无正当理由不提交的，人民法院可以裁定中止诉讼或者判令原告承担可能的不利后果。” 三、将第九条第一项修改为:“（一）原告出具的检索报告或者专利权评价报告未发现导致实用新型或者外观设计专利权无效的事由的；” 四、将第十七条修改为:“专利法第五十九条第一款所称的‘发明或者实用新型专利权的保护范围以其权利要求的内容为准，说明书及附图可以用于解释权利要求的内容’，是指专利权的保护范围应当以权利要求记载的全部技术特征所确定的范围为准，也包括与该技术特征相等同的特征所确定的范围。 “等同特征，是指与所记载的技术特征以基本相同的手段，实现基本相同的功能，达到基本相同的效果，并且本领域普通技术人员在被诉侵权行为发生时无需经过创造性劳动就能够联想到的特征。” 五、将第十八条修改为:“侵犯专利权行为发生在2001年７月１日以前的，适用修改前专利法的规定确定民事责任；发生在2001年７月１日以后的，适用修改后专利法的规定确定民事责任。” 六、将第十九条修改为:“假冒他人专利的，人民法院可以依照专利法第六十三条的规定确定其民事责任。管理专利工作的部门未给予行政处罚的，人民法院可以依照民法通则第一百三十四条第三款的规定给予民事制裁，适用民事罚款数额可以参照专利法第六十三条的规定确定。” 七、删除第二十条第一款，第二款改为第一款并修改为:“专利法第六十五条规定的权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失可以根据专利权人的专利产品因侵权所造成销售量减少的总数乘以每件专利产品的合理利润所得之积计算。权利人销售量减少的总数难以确定的，侵权产品在市场上销售的总数乘以每件专利产品的合理利润所得之积可以视为权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失。”

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 人民法院受理下列专利纠纷案件：  
1.专利申请权纠纷案件；  
2.专利权权属纠纷案件；  
3.专利权、专利申请权转让合同纠纷案件；  
4.侵犯专利权纠纷案件；  
5.假冒他人专利纠纷案件；  
6.发明专利申请公布后、专利权授予前使用费纠纷案件；  
7.职务发明创造发明人、设计人奖励、报酬纠纷案件；  
8.诉前申请停止侵权、财产保全案件；  
9.发明人、设计人资格纠纷案件；  
10.不服专利复审委员会维持驳回申请复审决定案件；  
11.不服专利复审委员会专利权无效宣告请求决定案件；  
12.不服国务院专利行政部门实施强制许可决定案件；  
13.不服国务院专利行政部门实施强制许可使用费裁决案件；  
14.不服国务院专利行政部门行政复议决定案件；  
15.不服管理专利工作的部门行政决定案件；  
16.其他专利纠纷案件。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall accept the following patent dispute cases:  
1. Cases concerning disputes over the right to apply for a patent;  
2. Cases concerning disputes over the ownership of a patent right;  
3. Cases concerning disputes over a contract for assignment of a patent right or the right to apply for a patent;  
4. Cases concerning disputes over infringement of a patent right;  
5. Cases concerning disputes over passing off the patent of another person;  
6. Cases concerning disputes over the fee for use of an invention after the publication of the application for a patent for invention but before the grant of the patent right;  
7. Cases concerning disputes over the reward and remuneration for the inventor or designer of a service invention-creation;  
8. Cases concerning an application for an injunction against infringement and property preservation before prosecution;  
9. Cases concerning disputes over the qualification of the inventor or designer;  
10. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the decision of the Patent Reexamination Board to uphold the rejection of the application upon reexamination;  
11. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the decision of the Patent Reexamination Board on a request for a declaration of invalidity of a patent right;  
12. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the decision of the patent administrative department of the State Council on granting a compulsory license;  
13. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the ruling of the patent administrative department of the State Council on the fee for a compulsory license;  
14. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the decision of the patent administrative department of the State Council on administrative reconsideration;  
15. Cases concerning the dissatisfaction with the administrative decision of the department in charge of patent administration;  
16. Other patent dispute cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 专利纠纷第一审案件，由各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在地的中级人民法院和最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。  
最高人民法院根据实际情况，可以指定基层人民法院管辖第一审专利纠纷案件。

**Article 2** Cases of first instance concerning patent disputes shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Courts located in the seat of the people's government of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and the Intermediate People's Courts designated by the Supreme People's Court.  
The Supreme People's Court may, according to the actual circumstances, designate a Primary People's Court to have jurisdiction over first instance patent dispute cases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 当事人对专利复审委员会于2001年7月1日以后作出的关于实用新型、外观设计专利权撤销请求复审决定不服向人民法院起诉的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 3** Where a party is dissatisfied with the decision made by the Patent Reexamination Board after July 1, 2001, on the reexamination of a request for revocation of a patent right for utility model or design and institutes a prosecution in the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 当事人对专利复审委员会于2001年7月1日以后作出的关于维持驳回实用新型、外观设计专利申请的复审决定，或者关于实用新型、外观设计专利权无效宣告请求的决定不服向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 4** Where a party is dissatisfied with the decision made by the Patent Reexamination Board after July 1, 2001, on the reexamination upholding the rejection of an application for a patent for utility model or design, or with the decision on a request for a declaration of invalidity of a patent right for utility model or design, and institutes a prosecution in the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 因侵犯专利权行为提起的诉讼，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。  
侵权行为地包括：被诉侵犯发明、实用新型专利权的产品的制造、使用、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；专利方法使用行为的实施地，依照该专利方法直接获得的产品的使用、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；外观设计专利产品的制造、许诺销售、销售、进口等行为的实施地；假冒他人专利的行为实施地。上述侵权行为的侵权结果发生地。

**Article 5** A lawsuit arising from an act of patent infringement shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the infringing act is committed or of the place where the defendant has its domicile.  
The place where the infringing act is committed includes: the place where the act of manufacturing, using, offering for sale, selling, or importing the product alleged to have infringed the patent right for invention or utility model is committed; the place where the act of using the patented method is committed, and the place where the act of using, offering for sale, selling, or importing the product directly obtained by the patented method is committed; the place where the act of manufacturing, offering for sale, selling, or importing the product of the design patent is committed; and the place where the act of passing off the patent of another person is committed. The place where the result of infringement of the above infringing acts occurs.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 原告仅对侵权产品制造者提起诉讼，未起诉销售者，侵权产品制造地与销售地不一致的，制造地人民法院有管辖权；以制造者与销售者为共同被告起诉的，销售地人民法院有管辖权。  
销售者是制造者分支机构，原告在销售地起诉侵权产品制造者制造、销售行为的，销售地人民法院有管辖权。

**Article 6** Where the plaintiff institutes a lawsuit only against the manufacturer of the infringing product and does not sue the seller, and the place of manufacture of the infringing product is inconsistent with the place of sale, the People's Court of the place of manufacture shall have jurisdiction; where the manufacturer and the seller are sued as joint defendants, the People's Court of the place of sale shall have jurisdiction.  
Where the seller is a branch of the manufacturer, and the plaintiff sues the manufacturer of the infringing product for the acts of manufacture and sale in the place of sale, the People's Court of the place of sale shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 原告根据1993年1月1日以前提出的专利申请和根据该申请授予的方法发明专利权提起的侵权诉讼，参照[本规定第五条](#art-5)、第六条的规定确定管辖。  
人民法院在上述案件实体审理中依法适用方法发明专利权不延及产品的规定。

**Article 7** For an infringement lawsuit instituted by the plaintiff based on a patent application filed before January 1, 1993, and the patent right for a process invention granted based on such application, jurisdiction shall be determined with reference to the provisions of Articles 5 and 6 of these Provisions.  
In the substantive trial of the above-mentioned case, the People's Court shall apply, according to law, the provision that the patent right for a process invention shall not extend to the products.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 对申请日在2009年10月1日前（不含该日）的实用新型专利提起侵犯专利权诉讼，原告可以出具由国务院专利行政部门作出的检索报告；对申请日在2009年10月1日以后的实用新型或者外观设计专利提起侵犯专利权诉讼，原告可以出具由国务院专利行政部门作出的专利权评价报告。根据案件审理需要，人民法院可以要求原告提交检索报告或者专利权评价报告。原告无正当理由不提交的，人民法院可以裁定中止诉讼或者判令原告承担可能的不利后果。  
侵犯实用新型、外观设计专利权纠纷案件的被告请求中止诉讼的，应当在答辩期内对原告的专利权提出宣告无效的请求。

**Article 8** Where a lawsuit for infringement of a patent right is instituted against a utility model patent with a filing date before October 1, 2009 (excluding that date), the plaintiff may produce a search report issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council; where a lawsuit for infringement of a patent right is instituted against a utility model or design patent with a filing date on or after October 1, 2009, the plaintiff may produce a patent evaluation report issued by the patent administrative department of the State Council. If necessary for the trial of the case, the People's Court may require the plaintiff to submit a search report or a patent evaluation report. If the plaintiff fails to do so without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may decide to suspend the proceedings or order the plaintiff to bear the possible adverse consequences.  
Where the defendant in a dispute case concerning infringement of a utility model or design patent right requests the suspension of the proceedings, it shall, within the period for defense, file a request with the People's Court for a declaration of invalidity of the plaintiff's patent right.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院受理的侵犯实用新型、外观设计专利权纠纷案件，被告在答辩期间内请求宣告该项专利权无效的，人民法院应当中止诉讼，但具备下列情形之一的，可以不中止诉讼：  
（一）原告出具的检索报告或者专利权评价报告未发现导致实用新型或者外观设计专利权无效的事由的；  
（二）被告提供的证据足以证明其使用的技术已经公知的；  
（三）被告请求宣告该项专利权无效所提供的证据或者依据的理由明显不充分的；  
（四）人民法院认为不应当中止诉讼的其他情形。

**Article 9** For a case of dispute concerning infringement of a utility model or design patent right accepted by the People's Court, if the defendant requests a declaration of invalidity of the said patent right within the period for defense, the People's Court shall suspend the proceedings, except under any of the following circumstances where the proceedings may not be suspended:  
(1) The search report or patent evaluation report produced by the plaintiff does not reveal any grounds that would render the utility model or design patent right invalid;  
(2) The evidence provided by the defendant is sufficient to prove that the technology it uses has been publicly known;  
(3) The evidence or grounds provided by the defendant in support of its request for a declaration of invalidity of the said patent right are obviously insufficient;  
(4) Other circumstances under which the People's Court deems that the proceedings should not be suspended.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院受理的侵犯实用新型、外观设计专利权纠纷案件，被告在答辩期间届满后请求宣告该项专利权无效的，人民法院不应当中止诉讼，但经审查认为有必要中止诉讼的除外。

**Article 10** For a case of dispute concerning infringement of a utility model or design patent right accepted by the People's Court, if the defendant requests a declaration of invalidity of the said patent right after the expiration of the period for defense, the People's Court shall not suspend the proceedings, unless it deems it necessary to suspend the proceedings after review.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院受理的侵犯发明专利权纠纷案件或者经专利复审委员会审查维持专利权的侵犯实用新型、外观设计专利权纠纷案件，被告在答辩期间内请求宣告该项专利权无效的，人民法院可以不中止诉讼。

**Article 11** For a case of dispute concerning infringement of a patent right for invention accepted by the People's Court, or for a case of dispute concerning infringement of a utility model or design patent right where the patent right has been maintained by the Patent Reexamination Board after review, if the defendant requests a declaration of invalidity of the said patent right within the period for defense, the People's Court may not suspend the proceedings.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院决定中止诉讼，专利权人或者利害关系人请求责令被告停止有关行为或者采取其他制止侵权损害继续扩大的措施，并提供了担保，人民法院经审查符合有关法律规定的，可以在裁定中止诉讼的同时一并作出有关裁定。

**Article 12** Where the People's Court decides to suspend the proceedings, and the patentee or an interested party requests an order to the defendant to cease the relevant acts or take other measures to prevent the continued expansion of the infringement damage, and provides security, and upon examination the People's Court finds that the request complies with the relevant legal provisions, it may, together with the ruling to suspend the proceedings, make a relevant ruling simultaneously.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 人民法院对专利权进行财产保全，应当向国务院专利行政部门发出协助执行通知书，载明要求协助执行的事项，以及对专利权保全的期限，并附人民法院作出的裁定书。  
对专利权保全的期限一次不得超过六个月，自国务院专利行政部门收到协助执行通知书之日起计算。如果仍然需要对该专利权继续采取保全措施的，人民法院应当在保全期限届满前向国务院专利行政部门另行送达继续保全的协助执行通知书。保全期限届满前未送达的，视为自动解除对该专利权的财产保全。  
人民法院对出质的专利权可以采取财产保全措施，质权人的优先受偿权不受保全措施的影响；专利权人与被许可人已经签订的独占实施许可合同，不影响人民法院对该专利权进行财产保全。  
人民法院对已经进行保全的专利权，不得重复进行保全。

**Article 13** Where the People's Court takes property preservation against a patent right, it shall issue a notice of assistance in enforcement to the patent administrative department of the State Council, specifying the matters requiring assistance in enforcement and the term of preservation of the patent right, and attach the written ruling made by the People's Court.  
The term of preservation of a patent right shall not exceed six months at a time, calculated from the date on which the patent administrative department of the State Council receives the notice of assistance in enforcement. If it is still necessary to continue the preservation measures against the said patent right, the People's Court shall serve another notice of assistance in enforcement for continued preservation on the patent administrative department of the State Council before the expiration of the term of preservation. If no such notice is served before the expiration of the term of preservation, the property preservation against the patent right shall be deemed to be automatically lifted.  
The People's Court may take property preservation measures against a pledged patent right, and the pledgee's priority right to be repaid shall not be affected by the preservation measures; the exclusive license contract already concluded between the patentee and the licensee shall not affect the People's Court's property preservation against the patent right.  
The People's Court shall not take duplicative preservation against a patent right that has already been preserved.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 2001年7月1日以前利用本单位的物质技术条件所完成的发明创造，单位与发明人或者设计人订有合同，对申请专利的权利和专利权的归属作出约定的，从其约定。

**Article 14** For an invention-creation completed before July 1, 2001, by using the material and technical conditions of the entity, if the entity and the inventor or designer have entered into a contract stipulating the ownership of the right to apply for a patent and the patent right, such stipulation shall prevail.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 人民法院受理的侵犯专利权纠纷案件，涉及权利冲突的，应当保护在先依法享有权利的当事人的合法权益。

**Article 15** Where a case of dispute concerning infringement of a patent right accepted by the People's Court involves a conflict of rights, the lawful rights and interests of the party who has acquired the right earlier according to law shall be protected.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 [专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)所称的在先取得的合法权利包括：商标权、著作权、企业名称权、肖像权、知名商品特有包装或者装潢使用权等。

**Article 16** The legitimate rights acquired earlier as referred to in Article 23 of the Patent Law include: trademark rights, copyright, enterprise name rights, portrait rights, the right to use the unique packaging or decoration of a well-known product, and the like.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 [专利法第五十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-59)第一款所称的“发明或者实用新型专利权的保护范围以其权利要求的内容为准，说明书及附图可以用于解释权利要求的内容”，是指专利权的保护范围应当以权利要求记载的全部技术特征所确定的范围为准，也包括与该技术特征相等同的特征所确定的范围。  
等同特征，是指与所记载的技术特征以基本相同的手段，实现基本相同的功能，达到基本相同的效果，并且本领域普通技术人员在被诉侵权行为发生时无需经过创造性劳动就能够联想到的特征。

**Article 17** The phrase "the extent of protection of the patent right for invention or utility model shall be determined by the terms of the claims, and the description and the appended drawings may be used to interpret the claims" in the first paragraph of Article 59 of the Patent Law means that the extent of protection of the patent right shall be determined by the scope defined by all the technical features recorded in the claims, and shall also include the scope defined by features equivalent to such technical features.  
Equivalent features refer to features that, compared with the recorded technical features, use substantially the same means, perform substantially the same functions, and achieve substantially the same effects, and can be conceived by a person of ordinary skill in the art without inventive efforts at the time of the alleged infringing act.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 侵犯专利权行为发生在2001年７月１日以前的，适用修改前专利法的规定确定民事责任；发生在2001年７月１日以后的，适用修改后专利法的规定确定民事责任。

**Article 18** Where an act of patent infringement occurs before July 1, 2001, the provisions of the Patent Law before amendment shall apply to determine civil liability; where it occurs on or after July 1, 2001, the provisions of the Patent Law after amendment shall apply to determine civil liability.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 假冒他人专利的，人民法院可以依照[专利法第六十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-63)的规定确定其民事责任。管理专利工作的部门未给予行政处罚的，人民法院可以依照民法通则第一百三十四条第三款的规定给予民事制裁，适用民事罚款数额可以参照[专利法第六十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-63)的规定确定。

**Article 19** Where a person passes off the patent of another person, the People's Court may determine its civil liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 63 of the Patent Law. If the department in charge of patent administration has not imposed an administrative penalty, the People's Court may impose a civil sanction in accordance with the provisions of the third paragraph of Article 134 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, and the amount of the civil fine may be determined with reference to the provisions of Article 63 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 [专利法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)规定的权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失可以根据专利权人的专利产品因侵权所造成销售量减少的总数乘以每件专利产品的合理利润所得之积计算。权利人销售量减少的总数难以确定的，侵权产品在市场上销售的总数乘以每件专利产品的合理利润所得之积可以视为权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失。  
[专利法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)规定的侵权人因侵权所获得的利益可以根据该侵权产品在市场上销售的总数乘以每件侵权产品的合理利润所得之积计算。侵权人因侵权所获得的利益一般按照侵权人的营业利润计算，对于完全以侵权为业的侵权人，可以按照销售利润计算。

**Article 20** The actual losses suffered by the right holder as a result of infringement as provided for in Article 65 of the Patent Law may be calculated by multiplying the total decrease in sales volume of the patentee's patented product caused by the infringement by the reasonable profit per unit of the patented product. Where the total decrease in sales volume of the right holder is difficult to determine, the product of the total number of infringing products sold on the market multiplied by the reasonable profit per unit of the patented product may be deemed as the actual losses suffered by the right holder as a result of infringement.  
The profits gained by the infringer from infringement as provided for in Article 65 of the Patent Law may be calculated by multiplying the total number of infringing products sold on the market by the reasonable profit per unit of the infringing product. The profits gained by the infringer from infringement shall generally be calculated based on the infringer's operating profit; for an infringer who is entirely engaged in infringement, the profit may be calculated based on the sales profit.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 权利人的损失或者侵权人获得的利益难以确定，有专利许可使用费可以参照的，人民法院可以根据专利权的类型、侵权行为的性质和情节、专利许可的性质、范围、时间等因素，参照该专利许可使用费的倍数合理确定赔偿数额；没有专利许可使用费可以参照或者专利许可使用费明显不合理的，人民法院可以根据专利权的类型、侵权行为的性质和情节等因素，依照[专利法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)第二款的规定确定赔偿数额。

**Article 21** Where the losses of the right holder or the profits gained by the infringer are difficult to determine, and there is a patent license royalty to refer to, the People's Court may, taking into account factors such as the type of the patent right, the nature and circumstances of the infringement, and the nature, scope, and duration of the patent license, reasonably determine the amount of damages by reference to a multiple of the said patent license royalty; where there is no patent license royalty to refer to or the patent license royalty is obviously unreasonable, the People's Court may, taking into account factors such as the type of the patent right and the nature and circumstances of the infringement, determine the amount of damages in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 65 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 权利人主张其为制止侵权行为所支付合理开支的，人民法院可以在[专利法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-65)确定的赔偿数额之外另行计算。

**Article 22** Where the right holder claims the reasonable expenses it has paid for stopping the infringement, the People's Court may calculate such expenses separately in addition to the amount of damages determined in accordance with Article 65 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 侵犯专利权的诉讼时效为二年，自专利权人或者利害关系人知道或者应当知道侵权行为之日起计算。权利人超过二年起诉的，如果侵权行为在起诉时仍在继续，在该项专利权有效期内，人民法院应当判决被告停止侵权行为，侵权损害赔偿数额应当自权利人向人民法院起诉之日起向前推算二年计算。

**Article 23** The limitation of action for infringement of a patent right shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the patentee or the interested party knows or should have known of the infringing act. Where the right holder institutes a prosecution after the expiration of two years, if the infringing act is still continuing at the time of prosecution, and within the term of validity of the said patent right, the People's Court shall order the defendant to cease the infringing act, and the amount of damages for infringement shall be calculated retroactively for two years from the date on which the right holder institutes the prosecution in the People's Court.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 [专利法第十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-11)、第六十九条所称的许诺销售，是指以做广告、在商店橱窗中陈列或者在展销会上展出等方式作出销售商品的意思表示。

**Article 24** The term "offering for sale" as referred to in Articles 11 and 69 of the Patent Law means an expression of intention to sell a commodity by means such as making advertisements, displaying in a shop window, or exhibiting at a trade fair.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 人民法院受理的侵犯专利权纠纷案件，已经过管理专利工作的部门作出侵权或者不侵权认定的，人民法院仍应当就当事人的诉讼请求进行全面审查。

**Article 25** Where a case of dispute concerning infringement of a patent right accepted by the People's Court has already been determined by the department in charge of patent administration as constituting or not constituting infringement, the People's Court shall still conduct a comprehensive review of the claims of the parties.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 以前的有关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 26** Where any previous relevant judicial interpretation is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
