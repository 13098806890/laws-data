# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕48号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-22  
**生效日期**：2000-12-21

> （2000年11月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1144次会议通过，自2000年12月21日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕48号 为了正确审理涉及计算机网络的著作权纠纷案件，根据民法通则、著作权法和民事诉讼法等法律的规定，对这类案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 网络著作权侵权纠纷案件由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括实施被诉侵权行为的网络服务器、计算机终端等设备所在地。对难以确定侵权行为地和被告住所地的，原告发现侵权内容的计算机终端等设备所在地可以视为侵权行为地。 第二条 受著作权法保护的作品，包括著作权法第三条规定的各类作品的数字化形式。在网络环境下无法归于著作权法第三条列举的作品范围，但在文学、艺术和科学领域内具有独创性并能以某种有形形式复制的其他智力创作成果，人民法院应当予以保护。 著作权法第十条对著作权各项权利的规定均适用于数字化作品的著作权。将作品通过网络向公众传播，属于著作权法规定的使用作品的方式，著作权人享有以该种方式使用或者许可他人使用作品，并由此获得报酬的权利。 第三条 已在报刊上刊登或者网络上传播的作品，除著作权人声明或者上载该作品的网络服务提供者受著作权人的委托声明不得转载、摘编的以外，网站予以转载、摘编并按有关规定支付报酬、注明出处的，不构成侵权。但网站转载、摘编作品超过有关报刊转载作品范围的，应当认定为侵权。 第四条 网络服务提供者通过网络参与他人侵犯著作权行为，或者通过网络教唆、帮助他人实施侵犯著作权行为的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与其他行为人或者直接实施侵权行为人的共同侵权责任。 第五条 提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，明知网络用户通过网络实施侵犯他人著作权的行为，或者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告，但仍不采取移除侵权内容等措施以消除侵权后果的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与该网络用户的共同侵权责任。 第六条 提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，对著作权人要求其提供侵权行为人在其网络的注册资料以追究行为人的侵权责任，无正当理由拒绝提供的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百零六条的规定，追究其相应的侵权责任。 第七条 著作权人发现侵权信息向网络服务提供者提出警告或者索要侵权行为人网络注册资料时，不能出示身份证明、著作权权属证明及侵权情况证明的，视为未提出警告或者未提出索要请求。 著作权人出示上述证明后网络服务提供者仍不采取措施的，可以在提起诉讼时申请人民法院先行裁定停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除影响，人民法院应予准许。 第八条 网络服务提供者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告而采取移除被控侵权内容等措施，被控侵权人要求网络服务提供者承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。 著作权人指控侵权不实，被控侵权人因网络服务提供者采取措施遭受损失而请求赔偿的，人民法院应当判令由提出警告的人承担赔偿责任。 第九条 人民法院审理网络著作权侵权纠纷案件，应当根据案件不同情况，分别适用下列法律：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 网络著作权侵权纠纷案件由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括实施被诉侵权行为的网络服务器、计算机终端等设备所在地。对难以确定侵权行为地和被告住所地的，原告发现侵权内容的计算机终端等设备所在地可以视为侵权行为地。

**Article 1** A People's Court where the infringing act is committed or where the defendant has its domicile shall have jurisdiction over a case concerning a dispute over copyright infringement on the Internet. The place where the infringing act is committed includes the place where the network server, computer terminal or other equipment used to commit the alleged infringing act is located. If it is difficult to determine the place where the infringing act is committed or the domicile of the defendant, the place where the computer terminal or other equipment on which the plaintiff discovers the infringing content is located may be deemed as the place where the infringing act is committed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 受著作权法保护的作品，包括[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)规定的各类作品的数字化形式。在网络环境下无法归于[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)列举的作品范围，但在文学、艺术和科学领域内具有独创性并能以某种有形形式复制的其他智力创作成果，人民法院应当予以保护。  
[著作权法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-10)对著作权各项权利的规定均适用于数字化作品的著作权。将作品通过网络向公众传播，属于著作权法规定的使用作品的方式，著作权人享有以该种方式使用或者许可他人使用作品，并由此获得报酬的权利。

**Article 2** Works protected by the Copyright Law include the digital forms of various types of works as provided for in Article 3 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China. Other intellectual creations that cannot be classified under the works enumerated in Article 3 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China in the network environment, but are original and can be reproduced in a tangible form in the fields of literature, art, and science, shall be protected by the People's Court.  
The provisions of Article 10 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China on various rights of copyright shall apply to copyright in digital works. The transmission of a work to the public through the network shall be a way of using the work as provided for in the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, and the copyright owner shall have the right to use or authorize others to use the work in such a way and to receive remuneration therefrom.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 已在报刊上刊登或者网络上传播的作品，除著作权人声明或者上载该作品的网络服务提供者受著作权人的委托声明不得转载、摘编的以外，网站予以转载、摘编并按有关规定支付报酬、注明出处的，不构成侵权。但网站转载、摘编作品超过有关报刊转载作品范围的，应当认定为侵权。

**Article 3** Where a work has been published in a newspaper or periodical or disseminated on the Internet, unless the copyright owner has declared or the network service provider that uploaded the work has declared on commission of the copyright owner that the work may not be reproduced or excerpted, a website that reproduces or excerpts the work and pays remuneration and indicates the source in accordance with the relevant provisions shall not constitute infringement. However, if the website's reproduction or excerpt of the work exceeds the scope of reproduction of works by relevant newspapers or periodicals, it shall be deemed as infringement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 网络服务提供者通过网络参与他人侵犯著作权行为，或者通过网络教唆、帮助他人实施侵犯著作权行为的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与其他行为人或者直接实施侵权行为人的共同侵权责任。

**Article 4** Where a network service provider participates in another person's act of copyright infringement through the Internet, or instigates or assists another person to commit an act of copyright infringement through the Internet, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, pursue its joint infringement liability together with the other actor or the person who directly committed the infringing act.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，明知网络用户通过网络实施侵犯他人著作权的行为，或者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告，但仍不采取移除侵权内容等措施以消除侵权后果的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与该网络用户的共同侵权责任。

**Article 5** Where a network service provider that provides content services, knowing that a network user is committing an act of copyright infringement through the Internet, or after receiving a warning with evidence from the copyright owner, still fails to take measures such as removing the infringing content to eliminate the consequences of the infringement, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, pursue its joint infringement liability together with the network user.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，对著作权人要求其提供侵权行为人在其网络的注册资料以追究行为人的侵权责任，无正当理由拒绝提供的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百零六条的规定，追究其相应的侵权责任。

**Article 6** Where a network service provider that provides content services, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide the copyright owner with the registration information of the infringer in its network as requested by the copyright owner in order to pursue the infringer's infringement liability, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 106 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, pursue its corresponding infringement liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 著作权人发现侵权信息向网络服务提供者提出警告或者索要侵权行为人网络注册资料时，不能出示身份证明、著作权权属证明及侵权情况证明的，视为未提出警告或者未提出索要请求。  
著作权人出示上述证明后网络服务提供者仍不采取措施的，可以在提起诉讼时申请人民法院先行裁定停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除影响，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 7** Where a copyright owner, when giving a warning to a network service provider or requesting the network registration information of the infringer upon discovering infringing information, fails to present identity certification, copyright ownership certification, and evidence of the infringement, it shall be deemed as not having given the warning or made the request.  
After the copyright owner presents the above certifications, if the network service provider still fails to take measures, the copyright owner may, when filing a lawsuit, apply to the People's Court for a preliminary ruling to cease the infringement, remove the obstacle, and eliminate the effects, and the People's Court shall grant such application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 网络服务提供者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告而采取移除被控侵权内容等措施，被控侵权人要求网络服务提供者承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
著作权人指控侵权不实，被控侵权人因网络服务提供者采取措施遭受损失而请求赔偿的，人民法院应当判令由提出警告的人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 8** Where a network service provider takes measures such as removing the alleged infringing content after receiving a warning with evidence from the copyright owner, and the alleged infringer requests the network service provider to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
If the copyright owner's accusation of infringement is unfounded, and the alleged infringer, having suffered losses due to the measures taken by the network service provider, claims compensation, the People's Court shall order the person who gave the warning to bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院审理网络著作权侵权纠纷案件，应当根据案件不同情况，分别适用下列法律：  
（一）认定侵害发表权等著作人身权的，适用[著作权法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-45)第（一）、（二）、（三）、（四）项的规定；  
（二）认定向公众传播作品侵害使用权的，适用[著作权法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-45)第（五）项的规定；  
（三）认定侵害获得报酬权的，适用[著作权法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-45)第（六）项的规定；  
（四）认定侵害录音录像制作者、表演者、广播电视组织等邻接权，或者认定故意去除或者改变著作权管理信息而导致侵权后果的行为构成侵权的，适用[著作权法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-45)第（八）项的规定；  
（五）认定剽窃、抄袭他人作品的，适用[著作权法第四十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-46)第（一）项的规定。

**Article 9** When trying a case concerning a dispute over copyright infringement on the Internet, the People's Court shall, depending on the circumstances of the case, apply the following laws respectively:  
(1) If an act infringes upon the moral rights of the author such as the right of publication, the provisions of subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), and (4) of Article 45 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply;  
(2) If the right of use is infringed by transmitting the work to the public, the provisions of subparagraph (5) of Article 45 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply;  
(3) If the right to remuneration is infringed, the provisions of subparagraph (6) of Article 45 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply;  
(4) If neighboring rights such as those of sound recording and video recording producers, performers, and broadcasting organizations are infringed, or if the intentional removal or alteration of copyright management information leading to infringement consequences constitutes infringement, the provisions of subparagraph (8) of Article 45 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply;  
(5) If plagiarism or copying of another person's work is identified, the provisions of subparagraph (1) of Article 46 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院在确定侵权赔偿数额时，可以根据被侵权人的请求，按照其因侵权行为所受直接经济损失和所失预期应得利益计算赔偿数额；也可以按照侵权人因侵权行为所得利益计算赔偿数额。侵权人不能证明其成本或者必要费用的，其因侵权行为所得收入，即为所得利益。  
被侵权人损失额不能确定的，人民法院依被侵权人的请求，可以根据侵害情节在人民币500元以上30万元以下确定赔偿数额，最多不得超过人民币50万元。

**Article 10** When determining the amount of compensation for infringement, the People's Court may, at the request of the infringed party, calculate the amount of compensation based on the direct economic losses suffered by the infringed party as a result of the infringement and the expected benefits lost; it may also calculate the amount of compensation based on the benefits obtained by the infringer from the infringement. If the infringer cannot prove its costs or necessary expenses, the income obtained by the infringer from the infringement shall be deemed as the benefits obtained.  
If the amount of loss of the infringed party cannot be determined, the People's Court may, at the request of the infringed party, determine the amount of compensation within the range of RMB 500 to RMB 300,000 based on the circumstances of the infringement, and the maximum amount shall not exceed RMB 500,000.
