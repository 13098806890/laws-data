# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕1号  
**公布日期**：2003-12-23  
**生效日期**：2004-01-07

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2003年12月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1302次会议通过。现予公布，自2004年1月7日起施行。 2004年1月2日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理 涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律 若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2004〕1号 根据2001年10月27日第九届全国人大常委会第二十四次会议审议修改的《中华人民共和国著作权法》的规定，最高人民法院审判委员会第1302次会议决定对《最高人民法院关于审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》作如下修改： 一、删去第二条第二款。 二、第三条修改为：“已在报刊上刊登或者网络上传播的作品，除著作权人声明或者报社、期刊社、网络服务提供者受著作权人委托声明不得转载、摘编的以外，在网络进行转载、摘编并按有关规定支付报酬、注明出处的，不构成侵权。但转载、摘编作品超过有关报刊转载作品范围的，应当认定为侵权”。 三、增加一条，作为第七条：“网络服务提供者明知专门用于故意避开或者破坏他人著作权技术保护措施的方法、设备或者材料，而上载、传播、提供的，人民法院应当根据当事人的诉讼请求和具体案情，依照著作权法第四十七条第（六）项的规定，追究网络服务提供者的民事侵权责任”。 四、第七条改为第八条，第二款修改为：“著作权人出示上述证明后网络服务提供者仍不采取措施的，著作权人可以依照著作权法第四十九条、第五十条的规定在诉前申请人民法院作出停止有关行为和财产保全、证据保全的裁定，也可以在提起诉讼时申请人民法院先行裁定停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除影响”。 五、删除第九条。 六、删除第十条。 此外，根据本决定对条文顺序作相应调整。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　网络著作权侵权纠纷案件由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括实施被诉侵权行为的网络服务器、计算机终端等设备所在地。对难以确定侵权行为地和被告住所地的，原告发现侵权内容的计算机终端等设备所在地可以视为侵权行为地。

**Article 1** A People's Court of the place where the infringing act is committed or where the defendant has his domicile shall have jurisdiction over a network copyright infringement dispute. The place where the infringing act is committed includes the place where the network server, computer terminal or other equipment on which the alleged infringing act is carried out is located. Where it is difficult to determine the place where the infringing act is committed and the defendant's domicile, the place where the computer terminal or other equipment on which the plaintiff discovers the infringing content is located may be deemed as the place where the infringing act is committed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　受著作权法保护的作品，包括[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)规定的各类作品的数字化形式。在网络环境下无法归于[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)列举的作品范围，但在文学、艺术和科学领域内具有独创性并能以某种有形形式复制的其他智力创作成果，人民法院应当予以保护。

**Article 2** Works protected under the Copyright Law shall include the digital forms of various types of works as prescribed in Article 3 of the Copyright Law. Any other intellectual creation that cannot be classified into the categories of works enumerated in Article 3 of the Copyright Law in the network environment, but is original in the fields of literature, art, or science and can be reproduced in some tangible form, shall be protected by the People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　已在报刊上刊登或者网络上传播的作品，除著作权人声明或者报刊、期刊社、网络服务提供者受著作权人委托声明不得转载、摘编的以外，在网络进行转载、摘编并按有关规定支付报酬、注明出处的，不构成侵权。但转载、摘编作品超过有关报刊转载作品范围的，应当认定为侵权。

**Article 3** Where a work has been published in a newspaper or periodical or disseminated on the Internet, unless the copyright holder has declared or the newspaper, periodical publisher, or Internet service provider, upon the copyright holder's commission, has declared that the work shall not be reproduced or excerpted, the reproduction or excerpting of such work on the Internet with payment of remuneration and indication of the source in accordance with relevant provisions shall not constitute infringement. However, if the scope of reproduction or excerpting exceeds the scope of reproduction of works by relevant newspapers or periodicals, it shall be deemed as infringement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　网络服务提供者通过网络参与他人侵犯著作权行为，或者通过网络教唆、帮助他人实施侵犯著作权行为的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与其他行为人或者直接实施侵权行为人的共同侵权责任。

**Article 4** Where an Internet service provider participates in another person's infringement of copyright through the Internet, or induces or assists another person in committing an act of copyright infringement through the Internet, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it jointly and severally liable with the other actors or the person who directly committed the infringing act.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，明知网络用户通过网络实施侵犯他人著作权的行为，或者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告，但仍不采取移除侵权内容等措施以消除侵权后果的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与该网络用户的共同侵权责任。

**Article 5** Where an Internet service provider that provides content services, knowing that a network user is infringing another person's copyright through the Internet, or after receiving a well-founded warning from the copyright holder, fails to take measures such as removing the infringing content to eliminate the consequences of the infringement, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it jointly and severally liable with the network user.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，对著作权人要求其提供侵权行为人在其网络的注册资料以追究行为人的侵权责任，无正当理由拒绝提供的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百零六条的规定，追究其相应的侵权责任。

**Article 6** Where an Internet service provider that provides content services, upon the copyright holder's request for the provision of the registration information of the infringer on its network to pursue the infringer's liability, refuses to provide such information without justifiable reasons, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 106 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it liable for the corresponding infringement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　网络服务提供者明知专门用于故意避开或者破坏他人著作权技术保护措施的方法、设备或者材料，而上载、传播、提供的，人民法院应当根据当事人的诉讼请求和具体案情，依照[著作权法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-47)第（六）项的规定，追究网络服务提供者的民事侵权责任。

**Article 7** Where an Internet service provider, knowing that methods, devices, or materials are specifically used for intentionally circumventing or destroying others' technological measures for copyright protection, uploads, disseminates, or provides them, the People's Court shall, based on the party's claim and the specific circumstances of the case, in accordance with Article 47(6) of the Copyright Law, hold the Internet service provider civilly liable for infringement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　著作权人发现侵权信息向网络服务提供者提出警告或者索要侵权行为人网络注册资料时，不能出示身份证明、著作权权属证明及侵权情况证明的，视为未提出警告或者未提出索要请求。  
著作权人出示上述证明后网络服务提供者仍不采取措施的，著作权人可以依照[著作权法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-49)、第五十条的规定在诉前申请人民法院作出停止有关行为和财产保全、证据保全的裁定，也可以在提起诉讼时申请人民法院先行裁定停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除影响，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 8** Where a copyright holder, upon discovering infringing information, issues a warning to the Internet service provider or requests the provider to provide the network registration information of the infringer, if the copyright holder fails to present identity certification, copyright ownership certification, and certification of the infringement, the warning or request shall be deemed as not having been made.  
If the Internet service provider still fails to take measures after the copyright holder presents the above certifications, the copyright holder may, in accordance with Articles 49 and 50 of the Copyright Law, apply to the People's Court for an order to cease the relevant acts and for property preservation or evidence preservation before prosecution, or may apply to the People's Court for a preliminary order to cease the infringement, remove obstacles, and eliminate effects when filing the lawsuit, and the People's Court shall approve such application.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　网络服务提供者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告而采取移除被控侵权内容等措施，被控侵权人要求网络服务提供者承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
著作权人指控侵权不实，被控侵权人因网络服务提供者采取措施遭受损失而请求赔偿的，人民法院应当判令由提出警告的人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 9** Where an Internet service provider, after receiving a well-founded warning from the copyright holder, takes measures such as removing the allegedly infringing content, if the alleged infringer requests the Internet service provider to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not uphold such request.  
If the copyright holder's accusation of infringement is untrue and the alleged infringer suffers losses due to the measures taken by the Internet service provider and requests compensation, the People's Court shall order the person who issued the warning to bear compensation liability.
