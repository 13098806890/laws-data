# 中华人民共和国专利法

**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2020-10-17  
**生效日期**：2021-06-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护专利权人的合法权益，鼓励发明创造，推动发明创造的应用，提高创新能力，促进科学技术进步和经济社会发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of protecting the lawful rights and interests of patentees, encouraging inventions and creations, promoting the application of inventions and creations, improving innovation capacity, enhancing scientific and technological progress, and promoting economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称的发明创造是指发明、实用新型和外观设计。  
发明，是指对产品、方法或者其改进所提出的新的技术方案。  
实用新型，是指对产品的形状、构造或者其结合所提出的适于实用的新的技术方案。  
外观设计，是指对产品的整体或者局部的形状、图案或者其结合以及色彩与形状、图案的结合所作出的富有美感并适于工业应用的新设计。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第59条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-69" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第69条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "invention and creation" means inventions, utility models and designs.  
Any invention refers to any new technical solution relating to a product, a process or an improvement thereof.  
Any utility model refers to any new technical solution relating to the shape, structure, or any combination thereof of a product, which is fit for practical use.  
Any design refers to any new design of the shape, the pattern, or the combination thereof, or the combination of the color and shape or pattern of a product, which creates an aesthetic feeling and is fit for industrial application.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院专利行政部门负责管理全国的专利工作；统一受理和审查专利申请，依法授予专利权。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门负责本行政区域内的专利管理工作。

**Article 3** The patent administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for the patent work nationwide. It shall accept and examine patent applications uniformly and grant patent rights in accordance with law.  
The patent administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the patent administration within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请专利的发明创造涉及国家安全或者重大利益需要保密的，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 4** Where an invention or creation for which a patent is applied for involves national security or major interests and is required to be kept confidential, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对违反法律、社会公德或者妨害公共利益的发明创造，不授予专利权。  
对违反法律、行政法规的规定获取或者利用遗传资源，并依赖该遗传资源完成的发明创造，不授予专利权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-10" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第59条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-69" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第69条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 5** No patent right shall be granted for any invention or creation that violates the law, social morality, or impairs public interests.  
No patent right shall be granted for any invention or creation that is accomplished by obtaining or utilizing genetic resources in violation of the provisions of laws or administrative regulations and that relies on such genetic resources.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　执行本单位的任务或者主要是利用本单位的物质技术条件所完成的发明创造为职务发明创造。职务发明创造申请专利的权利属于该单位，申请被批准后，该单位为专利权人。该单位可以依法处置其职务发明创造申请专利的权利和专利权，促进相关发明创造的实施和运用。  
非职务发明创造，申请专利的权利属于发明人或者设计人；申请被批准后，该发明人或者设计人为专利权人。  
利用本单位的物质技术条件所完成的发明创造，单位与发明人或者设计人订有合同，对申请专利的权利和专利权的归属作出约定的，从其约定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-13" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 6** An invention or creation made in the course of performing the duties of an entity or mainly by utilizing the material and technical conditions of the entity shall be a service invention and creation. The right to apply for a patent for a service invention and creation shall belong to the entity. After the application is approved, the entity shall be the patentee. The entity may dispose of its right to apply for a patent and the patent right for a service invention and creation in accordance with law, so as to promote the implementation and application of the relevant invention and creation.  
For a non-service invention and creation, the right to apply for a patent shall belong to the inventor or designer. After the application is approved, the inventor or designer shall be the patentee.  
Where an invention or creation is made by utilizing the material and technical conditions of an entity and the entity and the inventor or designer have entered into a contract stipulating the ownership of the right to apply for a patent and the patent right, the provisions of the contract shall prevail.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对发明人或者设计人的非职务发明创造专利申请，任何单位或者个人不得压制。

**Article 7** No entity or individual shall suppress an application for a patent for a non-service invention or creation made by the inventor or designer.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　两个以上单位或者个人合作完成的发明创造、一个单位或者个人接受其他单位或者个人委托所完成的发明创造，除另有协议的以外，申请专利的权利属于完成或者共同完成的单位或者个人；申请被批准后，申请的单位或者个人为专利权人。

**Article 8** Where an invention or creation is made jointly by two or more entities or individuals, or is made by one entity or individual upon commission by another entity or individual, the right to apply for a patent shall, unless otherwise agreed upon, belong to the entity or individual which or who made or jointly made the invention or creation. After the application is approved, the entity or individual that applied for the patent shall be the patentee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　同样的发明创造只能授予一项专利权。但是，同一申请人同日对同样的发明创造既申请实用新型专利又申请发明专利，先获得的实用新型专利权尚未终止，且申请人声明放弃该实用新型专利权的，可以授予发明专利权。  
两个以上的申请人分别就同样的发明创造申请专利的，专利权授予最先申请的人。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-47" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第59条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-69" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第69条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 9** Only one patent right may be granted for the same invention or creation. However, where the same applicant on the same day applies for both a utility model patent and an invention patent for the same invention or creation, the invention patent may be granted if the utility model patent obtained earlier has not yet been terminated and the applicant declares the abandonment of such utility model patent.  
Where two or more applicants apply for patents for the same invention or creation respectively, the patent right shall be granted to the applicant who applied first.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　专利申请权和专利权可以转让。  
中国单位或者个人向外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织转让专利申请权或者专利权的，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理手续。  
转让专利申请权或者专利权的，当事人应当订立书面合同，并向国务院专利行政部门登记，由国务院专利行政部门予以公告。专利申请权或者专利权的转让自登记之日起生效。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第15条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 10** The right to apply for a patent and the patent right may be assigned.  
Where any Chinese entity or individual assigns the right to apply for a patent or the patent right to any foreigner, foreign enterprise or any other foreign organization, it shall go through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Where the right to apply for a patent or the patent right is assigned, the parties shall conclude a written contract and shall register it with the patent administration department under the State Council, which shall announce the assignment. The assignment of the right to apply for a patent or the patent right shall take effect as of the date of registration.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　发明和实用新型专利权被授予后，除本法另有规定的以外，任何单位或者个人未经专利权人许可，都不得实施其专利，即不得为生产经营目的制造、使用、许诺销售、销售、进口其专利产品，或者使用其专利方法以及使用、许诺销售、销售、进口依照该专利方法直接获得的产品。  
外观设计专利权被授予后，任何单位或者个人未经专利权人许可，都不得实施其专利，即不得为生产经营目的制造、许诺销售、销售、进口其外观设计专利产品。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-24" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第24条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第19条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第20条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 11** After the grant of the patent right for an invention or utility model, except as otherwise provided for in this Law, no entity or individual shall, without the authorization of the patentee, exploit the patent, i.e., shall not manufacture, use, offer to sell, sell, or import the patented product, or use the patented process, or use, offer to sell, sell, or import the product directly obtained by the patented process, for production or business purposes.  
After the grant of the patent right for a design, no entity or individual shall, without the authorization of the patentee, exploit the patent, i.e., shall not manufacture, offer to sell, sell, or import the patented design product, for production or business purposes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位或者个人实施他人专利的，应当与专利权人订立实施许可合同，向专利权人支付专利使用费。被许可人无权允许合同规定以外的任何单位或者个人实施该专利。

**Article 12** Any entity or individual exploiting another person's patent shall conclude an exploitation license contract with the patentee and pay the patent royalties to the patentee. The licensee shall have no right to allow any entity or individual other than those stipulated in the contract to exploit the patent.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　发明专利申请公布后，申请人可以要求实施其发明的单位或者个人支付适当的费用。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-132" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第132条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 13** After the publication of an application for a patent for invention, the applicant may require the entity or individual exploiting the invention to pay an appropriate fee.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　专利申请权或者专利权的共有人对权利的行使有约定的，从其约定。没有约定的，共有人可以单独实施或者以普通许可方式许可他人实施该专利；许可他人实施该专利的，收取的使用费应当在共有人之间分配。  
除前款规定的情形外，行使共有的专利申请权或者专利权应当取得全体共有人的同意。

**Article 14** Where co-owners of the right to apply for a patent or the patent right have an agreement on the exercise of such right, the agreement shall prevail. In the absence of such an agreement, the co-owners may individually exploit the patent or license others to exploit the patent by means of an ordinary license; where the patent is licensed to others for exploitation, the royalties collected shall be distributed among the co-owners.  
Except for the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, the exercise of the co-owned right to apply for a patent or the co-owned patent right shall obtain the consent of all co-owners.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被授予专利权的单位应当对职务发明创造的发明人或者设计人给予奖励；发明创造专利实施后，根据其推广应用的范围和取得的经济效益，对发明人或者设计人给予合理的报酬。  
国家鼓励被授予专利权的单位实行产权激励，采取股权、期权、分红等方式，使发明人或者设计人合理分享创新收益。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-92" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第92条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-93" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第93条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-94" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第94条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 15** The entity to which a patent right is granted shall reward the inventor or designer of a service invention or creation; after the patent for the invention or creation is exploited, the entity shall provide a reasonable remuneration to the inventor or designer based on the scope of its promotion and application and the economic benefits obtained.  
The State encourages entities to which patent rights are granted to implement property right incentives, adopting methods such as equity, options, and dividends, so that the inventor or designer may reasonably share in the innovation benefits.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　发明人或者设计人有权在专利文件中写明自己是发明人或者设计人。  
专利权人有权在其专利产品或者该产品的包装上标明专利标识。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-99" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第99条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 16** The inventor or designer shall have the right to state in the patent documents that he or she is the inventor or designer.  
The patentee shall have the right to affix a patent mark on the patented product or on the package of such product.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在中国没有经常居所或者营业所的外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织在中国申请专利的，依照其所属国同中国签订的协议或者共同参加的国际条约，或者依照互惠原则，根据本法办理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-44" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第44条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 17** Where any foreigner, foreign enterprise or any other foreign organization having no habitual residence or business establishment in China files an application for a patent in China, the application shall be handled in accordance with this Law pursuant to any agreement concluded between the country to which the applicant belongs and China, or any international treaty to which both countries are parties, or on the basis of the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在中国没有经常居所或者营业所的外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织在中国申请专利和办理其他专利事务的，应当委托依法设立的专利代理机构办理。  
中国单位或者个人在国内申请专利和办理其他专利事务的，可以委托依法设立的专利代理机构办理。  
专利代理机构应当遵守法律、行政法规，按照被代理人的委托办理专利申请或者其他专利事务；对被代理人发明创造的内容，除专利申请已经公布或者公告的以外，负有保密责任。专利代理机构的具体管理办法由国务院规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-18" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第18条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-65" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第65条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-70" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第70条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 18** Where any foreigner, foreign enterprise or any other foreign organization having no habitual residence or business establishment in China applies for a patent or handles other patent matters in China, it shall entrust a patent agency established in accordance with law with the handling of such matters.  
Where any Chinese entity or individual applies for a patent or handles other patent matters domestically, it may entrust a patent agency established in accordance with law with the handling of such matters.  
A patent agency shall comply with laws and administrative regulations and handle the patent application or other patent matters as entrusted by its principal; it shall, with respect to the contents of the principal's invention or creation, keep confidential, except for those which have been published or announced. The specific measures for the administration of patent agencies shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位或者个人将在中国完成的发明或者实用新型向外国申请专利的，应当事先报经国务院专利行政部门进行保密审查。保密审查的程序、期限等按照国务院的规定执行。  
中国单位或者个人可以根据中华人民共和国参加的有关国际条约提出专利国际申请。申请人提出专利国际申请的，应当遵守前款规定。  
国务院专利行政部门依照中华人民共和国参加的有关国际条约、本法和国务院有关规定处理专利国际申请。  
对违反本条第一款规定向外国申请专利的发明或者实用新型，在中国申请专利的，不授予专利权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-118" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第118条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-136" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第136条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 19** Where any entity or individual intends to apply for a patent for an invention or utility model completed in China in a foreign country, it shall first report to the patent administration department under the State Council for confidentiality review. The procedures and time limit for confidentiality review shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
Chinese entities or individuals may file international patent applications in accordance with the relevant international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party. Any applicant filing an international patent application shall comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
The patent administration department under the State Council shall handle international patent applications in accordance with the relevant international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party, this Law, and the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Where an invention or utility model for which a patent is applied for in a foreign country in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article applies for a patent in China, no patent right shall be granted.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请专利和行使专利权应当遵循诚实信用原则。不得滥用专利权损害公共利益或者他人合法权益。  
滥用专利权，排除或者限制竞争，构成垄断行为的，依照《中华人民共和国反垄断法》处理。

**Article 20** The principle of good faith shall be followed in applying for patents and exercising patent rights. No patent right may be abused to harm public interests or the lawful rights and interests of others.  
Where any patent right is abused to eliminate or restrict competition, constituting a monopolistic act, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the Anti-monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院专利行政部门应当按照客观、公正、准确、及时的要求，依法处理有关专利的申请和请求。  
国务院专利行政部门应当加强专利信息公共服务体系建设，完整、准确、及时发布专利信息，提供专利基础数据，定期出版专利公报，促进专利信息传播与利用。  
在专利申请公布或者公告前，国务院专利行政部门的工作人员及有关人员对其内容负有保密责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-132" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第132条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 21** The patent administration department under the State Council shall process patent applications and requests in accordance with law and with the requirements of objectivity, fairness, accuracy, and timeliness.  
The patent administration department under the State Council shall strengthen the construction of the patent information public service system, release patent information completely, accurately, and in a timely manner, provide basic patent data, regularly publish patent gazettes, and promote the dissemination and utilization of patent information.  
Before the publication or announcement of a patent application, the staff members of the patent administration department under the State Council and the relevant persons shall have the duty to keep the contents thereof confidential.

### 第二章　授予专利权的条件

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　授予专利权的发明和实用新型，应当具备新颖性、创造性和实用性。  
新颖性，是指该发明或者实用新型不属于现有技术；也没有任何单位或者个人就同样的发明或者实用新型在申请日以前向国务院专利行政部门提出过申请，并记载在申请日以后公布的专利申请文件或者公告的专利文件中。  
创造性，是指与现有技术相比，该发明具有突出的实质性特点和显著的进步，该实用新型具有实质性特点和进步。  
实用性，是指该发明或者实用新型能够制造或者使用，并且能够产生积极效果。  
本法所称现有技术，是指申请日以前在国内外为公众所知的技术。

**Article 22** Any invention or utility model for which a patent right is granted shall possess novelty, inventiveness and utility.  
"Novelty" means that the invention or utility model does not belong to the prior art nor has any entity or individual filed an application for the same invention or utility model with the patent administration department under the State Council before the date of filing and recorded it in the patent application documents published or the patent documents announced after the date of filing.  
"Inventiveness" means that, compared with the prior art, the invention has prominent substantive features and represents a notable progress, and the utility model has substantive features and represents progress.  
"Utility" means that the invention or utility model can be manufactured or used and can produce positive effects.  
For the purposes of this Law, "prior art" means technology that is known to the public in China or abroad before the date of filing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　授予专利权的外观设计，应当不属于现有设计；也没有任何单位或者个人就同样的外观设计在申请日以前向国务院专利行政部门提出过申请，并记载在申请日以后公告的专利文件中。  
授予专利权的外观设计与现有设计或者现有设计特征的组合相比，应当具有明显区别。  
授予专利权的外观设计不得与他人在申请日以前已经取得的合法权利相冲突。  
本法所称现有设计，是指申请日以前在国内外为公众所知的设计。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-70" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第70条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 23** Any design for which a patent right is granted shall not belong to an existing design, nor shall any entity or individual have filed an application for the same design with the patent administration department under the State Council before the date of filing and recorded it in the patent documents announced after the date of filing.  
A design for which a patent right is granted shall be clearly distinguishable from an existing design or a combination of features of existing designs.  
A design for which a patent right is granted shall not conflict with any lawful rights that others have acquired before the date of filing.  
For the purposes of this Law, "existing design" means a design that is known to the public in China or abroad before the date of filing.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请专利的发明创造在申请日以前六个月内，有下列情形之一的，不丧失新颖性：  
（一）在国家出现紧急状态或者非常情况时，为公共利益目的首次公开的；  
（二）在中国政府主办或者承认的国际展览会上首次展出的；  
（三）在规定的学术会议或者技术会议上首次发表的；  
（四）他人未经申请人同意而泄露其内容的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-6" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-33" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第33条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-124" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第124条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-140" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第140条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 24** An invention or creation for which a patent is applied for shall not lose its novelty if, within six months before the date of filing, any of the following events occurs:  
(1) It is first disclosed for public interest purposes during a state of emergency or an exceptional circumstance in the country.  
(2) It is first exhibited at an international exhibition sponsored or recognized by the Chinese Government.  
(3) It is first published at a prescribed academic or technical conference.  
(4) It is disclosed by another person without the consent of the applicant.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对下列各项，不授予专利权：  
（一）科学发现；  
（二）智力活动的规则和方法；  
（三）疾病的诊断和治疗方法；  
（四）动物和植物品种；  
（五）原子核变换方法以及用原子核变换方法获得的物质；  
（六）对平面印刷品的图案、色彩或者二者的结合作出的主要起标识作用的设计。  
对前款第（四）项所列产品的生产方法，可以依照本法规定授予专利权。

**Article 25** No patent right shall be granted for any of the following:  
(1) Scientific discoveries.  
(2) Rules and methods for mental activities.  
(3) Methods for the diagnosis or treatment of diseases.  
(4) Animal and plant varieties.  
(5) Methods of nuclear transformation and substances obtained by means of nuclear transformation.  
(6) Designs that serve mainly as identification marks regarding patterns, colors, or a combination of the two on flat printed matter.  
Patent right may be granted, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, for the production methods of the products listed in item (4) of the preceding paragraph.

### 第三章　专利的申请

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请发明或者实用新型专利的，应当提交请求书、说明书及其摘要和权利要求书等文件。  
请求书应当写明发明或者实用新型的名称，发明人的姓名，申请人姓名或者名称、地址，以及其他事项。  
说明书应当对发明或者实用新型作出清楚、完整的说明，以所属技术领域的技术人员能够实现为准；必要的时候，应当有附图。摘要应当简要说明发明或者实用新型的技术要点。  
权利要求书应当以说明书为依据，清楚、简要地限定要求专利保护的范围。  
依赖遗传资源完成的发明创造，申请人应当在专利申请文件中说明该遗传资源的直接来源和原始来源；申请人无法说明原始来源的，应当陈述理由。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 26** To apply for a patent for an invention or utility model, the applicant shall submit a request, a description and an abstract thereof, and a claim or claims.  
The request shall state the title of the invention or utility model, the name of the inventor, the name or title and address of the applicant, and other related matters.  
The description shall set forth the invention or utility model in a manner sufficiently clear and complete so as to enable a person skilled in the relevant field of technology to carry it out; when necessary, drawings shall be attached. The abstract shall briefly state the technical essentials of the invention or utility model.  
The claim or claims shall be supported by the description and shall define the scope of patent protection in a clear and concise manner.  
Where an invention or creation is accomplished relying on genetic resources, the applicant shall indicate in the patent application documents the direct and original sources of such genetic resources; if the applicant is unable to indicate the original source, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请外观设计专利的，应当提交请求书、该外观设计的图片或者照片以及对该外观设计的简要说明等文件。  
申请人提交的有关图片或者照片应当清楚地显示要求专利保护的产品的外观设计。

**Article 27** To apply for a patent for a design, the applicant shall submit a request, a drawing or photograph of the design, a brief description of the design, and other related documents.  
The relevant drawing or photograph submitted by the applicant shall clearly show the design of the product for which patent protection is sought.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院专利行政部门收到专利申请文件之日为申请日。如果申请文件是邮寄的，以寄出的邮戳日为申请日。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第12条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-119" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第119条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-137" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第137条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 28** The date on which the patent administration department under the State Council receives the patent application documents shall be the date of filing. If the application documents are sent by mail, the postmark date of mailing shall be the date of filing.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　申请人自发明或者实用新型在外国第一次提出专利申请之日起十二个月内，或者自外观设计在外国第一次提出专利申请之日起六个月内，又在中国就相同主题提出专利申请的，依照该外国同中国签订的协议或者共同参加的国际条约，或者依照相互承认优先权的原则，可以享有优先权。  
申请人自发明或者实用新型在中国第一次提出专利申请之日起十二个月内，或者自外观设计在中国第一次提出专利申请之日起六个月内，又向国务院专利行政部门就相同主题提出专利申请的，可以享有优先权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-36" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第36条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** Where, within twelve months from the date on which an applicant first filed an application for a patent for an invention or utility model in a foreign country, or within six months from the date on which the applicant first filed an application for a patent for a design in a foreign country, the applicant files an application for a patent for the same subject matter in China, it may enjoy a right of priority in accordance with any agreement concluded between the said foreign country and China, or in accordance with any international treaty to which both countries are parties, or on the basis of the principle of mutual recognition of the right of priority.  
Where, within twelve months from the date on which an applicant first filed an application for a patent for an invention or utility model in China, or within six months from the date on which the applicant first filed an application for a patent for a design in China, the applicant files an application for a patent for the same subject matter with the patent administration department under the State Council, it may enjoy a right of priority.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请人要求发明、实用新型专利优先权的，应当在申请的时候提出书面声明，并且在第一次提出申请之日起十六个月内，提交第一次提出的专利申请文件的副本。  
申请人要求外观设计专利优先权的，应当在申请的时候提出书面声明，并且在三个月内提交第一次提出的专利申请文件的副本。  
申请人未提出书面声明或者逾期未提交专利申请文件副本的，视为未要求优先权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-34" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第34条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-127" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第127条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-139" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第139条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Where an applicant claims a right of priority for an invention or utility model patent, it shall make a written declaration at the time of filing and submit a copy of the patent application documents first filed within sixteen months from the date of the first filing.  
Where an applicant claims a right of priority for a design patent, it shall make a written declaration at the time of filing and submit a copy of the patent application documents first filed within three months.  
If the applicant fails to make a written declaration or fails to submit a copy of the patent application documents within the time limit, the claim for priority shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　一件发明或者实用新型专利申请应当限于一项发明或者实用新型。属于一个总的发明构思的两项以上的发明或者实用新型，可以作为一件申请提出。  
一件外观设计专利申请应当限于一项外观设计。同一产品两项以上的相似外观设计，或者用于同一类别并且成套出售或者使用的产品的两项以上外观设计，可以作为一件申请提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-39" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第39条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-40" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第40条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-48" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第48条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 31** An application for a patent for an invention or utility model shall be limited to one invention or utility model. Two or more inventions or utility models belonging to a single general inventive concept may be filed in one application.  
An application for a patent for a design shall be limited to one design. Two or more similar designs for the same product, or two or more designs for products that fall within the same class and are sold or used in sets, may be filed in one application.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　申请人可以在被授予专利权之前随时撤回其专利申请。

**Article 32** An applicant may withdraw its patent application at any time before the patent right is granted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　申请人可以对其专利申请文件进行修改，但是，对发明和实用新型专利申请文件的修改不得超出原说明书和权利要求书记载的范围，对外观设计专利申请文件的修改不得超出原图片或者照片表示的范围。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** An applicant may amend its patent application documents, but the amendment to the application documents for an invention or utility model patent shall not go beyond the scope of the original description and claims, and the amendment to the application documents for a design patent shall not go beyond the scope of the original drawings or photographs.

### 第四章　专利申请的审查和批准

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院专利行政部门收到发明专利申请后，经初步审查认为符合本法要求的，自申请日起满十八个月，即行公布。国务院专利行政部门可以根据申请人的请求早日公布其申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-50" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第50条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 34** After receiving an application for a patent for invention, the patent administration department under the State Council, upon preliminary examination and if it finds that the application meets the requirements of this Law, shall publish the application promptly upon the expiry of eighteen months from the date of filing. The patent administration department under the State Council may, upon the request of the applicant, publish the application earlier.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　发明专利申请自申请日起三年内，国务院专利行政部门可以根据申请人随时提出的请求，对其申请进行实质审查；申请人无正当理由逾期不请求实质审查的，该申请即被视为撤回。  
国务院专利行政部门认为必要的时候，可以自行对发明专利申请进行实质审查。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-56" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第56条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 35** Within three years from the date of filing of an application for a patent for invention, the patent administration department under the State Council may, upon the request made at any time by the applicant, conduct substantive examination of the application. If the applicant, without justifiable reasons, fails to request substantive examination within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.  
The patent administration department under the State Council may, when it deems it necessary, conduct substantive examination of an application for a patent for invention on its own.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　发明专利的申请人请求实质审查的时候，应当提交在申请日前与其发明有关的参考资料。  
发明专利已经在外国提出过申请的，国务院专利行政部门可以要求申请人在指定期限内提交该国为审查其申请进行检索的资料或者审查结果的资料；无正当理由逾期不提交的，该申请即被视为撤回。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-55" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第55条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** When requesting substantive examination of an invention patent application, the applicant shall submit reference materials relevant to the invention available before the date of filing.  
Where an invention patent application has been filed in a foreign country, the patent administration department under the State Council may require the applicant to submit, within a specified time limit, materials concerning the search or the results of examination made in that country for the purpose of examining the application. If the applicant, without justifiable reasons, fails to submit the materials within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院专利行政部门对发明专利申请进行实质审查后，认为不符合本法规定的，应当通知申请人，要求其在指定的期限内陈述意见，或者对其申请进行修改；无正当理由逾期不答复的，该申请即被视为撤回。

**Article 37** After conducting substantive examination of an application for a patent for invention, if the patent administration department under the State Council finds that the application does not conform to the provisions of this Law, it shall notify the applicant and require it to state its observations or to amend its application within a specified time limit. If the applicant, without justifiable reasons, fails to respond within the time limit, the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发明专利申请经申请人陈述意见或者进行修改后，国务院专利行政部门仍然认为不符合本法规定的，应当予以驳回。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 38** After the applicant has stated its observations or made amendments, if the patent administration department under the State Council still finds that the application for a patent for invention does not conform to the provisions of this Law, it shall reject the application.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　发明专利申请经实质审查没有发现驳回理由的，由国务院专利行政部门作出授予发明专利权的决定，发给发明专利证书，同时予以登记和公告。发明专利权自公告之日起生效。

**Article 39** Where no reason for rejection is found after substantive examination of the application for a patent for invention, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant the invention patent right, issue an invention patent certificate, and simultaneously register and announce the same. The invention patent right shall take effect as of the date of announcement.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　实用新型和外观设计专利申请经初步审查没有发现驳回理由的，由国务院专利行政部门作出授予实用新型专利权或者外观设计专利权的决定，发给相应的专利证书，同时予以登记和公告。实用新型专利权和外观设计专利权自公告之日起生效。

**Article 40** Where no reason for rejection is found after preliminary examination of the application for a patent for utility model or design, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision to grant the utility model patent right or the design patent right, issue the corresponding patent certificate, and simultaneously register and announce the same. The utility model patent right and the design patent right shall take effect as of the date of announcement.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　专利申请人对国务院专利行政部门驳回申请的决定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起三个月内向国务院专利行政部门请求复审。国务院专利行政部门复审后，作出决定，并通知专利申请人。  
专利申请人对国务院专利行政部门的复审决定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起三个月内向人民法院起诉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-65" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第65条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 41** Where a patent applicant disagrees with the decision of the patent administration department under the State Council to reject an application, the applicant may, within three months from the date of receipt of the notification, request the patent administration department under the State Council to conduct a reexamination. After reexamination, the patent administration department under the State Council shall make a decision and notify the patent applicant.  
Where a patent applicant disagrees with the reexamination decision of the patent administration department under the State Council, the applicant may, within three months from the date of receipt of the notification, institute legal proceedings in a People's Court.

### 第五章　专利权的期限、终止和无效

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　发明专利权的期限为二十年，实用新型专利权的期限为十年，外观设计专利权的期限为十五年，均自申请日起计算。  
自发明专利申请日起满四年，且自实质审查请求之日起满三年后授予发明专利权的，国务院专利行政部门应专利权人的请求，就发明专利在授权过程中的不合理延迟给予专利权期限补偿，但由申请人引起的不合理延迟除外。  
为补偿新药上市审评审批占用的时间，对在中国获得上市许可的新药相关发明专利，国务院专利行政部门应专利权人的请求给予专利权期限补偿。补偿期限不超过五年，新药批准上市后总有效专利权期限不超过十四年。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-77" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第77条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-78" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第78条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-79" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第79条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-80" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第80条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-81" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第81条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-82" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第82条引用">[6]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-84" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第84条引用">[7]</a></sup>

**Article 42** The duration of a patent right for invention shall be twenty years, the duration of a patent right for utility model shall be ten years, and the duration of a patent right for design shall be fifteen years, all commencing from the date of filing.  
Where, after the expiration of four years from the date of filing an invention patent application and three years from the date of requesting substantive examination, an invention patent is granted, the patent administration department under the State Council shall, upon the request of the patentee, provide a patent term compensation for the unreasonable delay during the grant process of the invention patent, except for the unreasonable delay caused by the applicant.  
To compensate for the time taken for review and approval of new drugs on the market, the patent administration department under the State Council shall, upon the request of the patentee, provide a patent term compensation for a relevant invention patent relating to a new drug that has obtained marketing approval in China. The compensation term shall not exceed five years, and the total effective patent term after the new drug is approved for marketing shall not exceed fourteen years.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　专利权人应当自被授予专利权的当年开始缴纳年费。

**Article 43** The patentee shall pay an annual fee from the year in which the patent right is granted.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　有下列情形之一的，专利权在期限届满前终止：  
（一）没有按照规定缴纳年费的；  
（二）专利权人以书面声明放弃其专利权的。  
专利权在期限届满前终止的，由国务院专利行政部门登记和公告。

**Article 44** In any of the following circumstances, a patent right shall terminate before the expiration of its duration:  
(1) where the annual fee is not paid in accordance with the provisions; or  
(2) where the patentee abandons the patent right by a written declaration.  
Where a patent right terminates before the expiration of its duration, it shall be registered and announced by the patent administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　自国务院专利行政部门公告授予专利权之日起，任何单位或者个人认为该专利权的授予不符合本法有关规定的，可以请求国务院专利行政部门宣告该专利权无效。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-69" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第69条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 45** From the date of announcement of the grant of a patent right by the patent administration department under the State Council, any unit or individual that considers that the grant of the patent right is not in conformity with the relevant provisions of this Law may request the patent administration department under the State Council to declare the patent right invalid.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院专利行政部门对宣告专利权无效的请求应当及时审查和作出决定，并通知请求人和专利权人。宣告专利权无效的决定，由国务院专利行政部门登记和公告。  
对国务院专利行政部门宣告专利权无效或者维持专利权的决定不服的，可以自收到通知之日起三个月内向人民法院起诉。人民法院应当通知无效宣告请求程序的对方当事人作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 46** The patent administration department under the State Council shall promptly examine a request for declaring a patent right invalid and make a decision, and shall notify the requester and the patentee. The decision declaring a patent right invalid shall be registered and announced by the patent administration department under the State Council.  
Where a party disagrees with the decision of the patent administration department under the State Council to declare a patent right invalid or to maintain the patent right, the party may, within three months from the date of receipt of the notification, institute legal proceedings in a People's Court. The People's Court shall notify the opposing party in the invalidation procedure to participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　宣告无效的专利权视为自始即不存在。  
宣告专利权无效的决定，对在宣告专利权无效前人民法院作出并已执行的专利侵权的判决、调解书，已经履行或者强制执行的专利侵权纠纷处理决定，以及已经履行的专利实施许可合同和专利权转让合同，不具有追溯力。但是因专利权人的恶意给他人造成的损失，应当给予赔偿。  
依照前款规定不返还专利侵权赔偿金、专利使用费、专利权转让费，明显违反公平原则的，应当全部或者部分返还。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-45" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理技术合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第45条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** A patent right that has been declared invalid shall be deemed to have never existed from the beginning.  
A decision declaring a patent right invalid shall have no retroactive effect on any judgment or mediation letter on patent infringement that has been made and enforced by a People's Court prior to the declaration of invalidity, on any decision on handling a patent infringement dispute that has been performed or compulsorily executed, or on any patent exploitation license contract and patent right assignment contract that have been performed prior to the declaration of invalidity. However, compensation shall be made for losses caused to others by the patentee's bad faith.  
Where, in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the failure to return the compensation for patent infringement, the patent exploitation fee, or the patent assignment fee is obviously contrary to the principle of fairness, all or part of such amounts shall be returned.

### 第六章　专利实施的特别许可

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院专利行政部门、地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门应当会同同级相关部门采取措施，加强专利公共服务，促进专利实施和运用。

**Article 48** The patent administration department under the State Council and the local government departments responsible for patent work shall, in conjunction with relevant departments at the same level, adopt measures to strengthen public services for patents and promote the implementation and application of patents.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国有企业事业单位的发明专利，对国家利益或者公共利益具有重大意义的，国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报经国务院批准，可以决定在批准的范围内推广应用，允许指定的单位实施，由实施单位按照国家规定向专利权人支付使用费。

**Article 49** Where an invention patent owned by a state-owned enterprise or institution is of significant importance to national interests or public interests, the relevant competent department under the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may, upon approval by the State Council, decide to promote and apply the patent within the approved scope, permit designated entities to exploit it, and the exploiting entity shall pay a royalty to the patentee in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　专利权人自愿以书面方式向国务院专利行政部门声明愿意许可任何单位或者个人实施其专利，并明确许可使用费支付方式、标准的，由国务院专利行政部门予以公告，实行开放许可。就实用新型、外观设计专利提出开放许可声明的，应当提供专利权评价报告。  
专利权人撤回开放许可声明的，应当以书面方式提出，并由国务院专利行政部门予以公告。开放许可声明被公告撤回的，不影响在先给予的开放许可的效力。

**Article 50** Where a patentee voluntarily makes a written declaration to the patent administration department under the State Council that it is willing to permit any unit or individual to exploit its patent, and specifies the method and standard for paying the license fee, the patent administration department under the State Council shall announce such declaration, and an open license shall be implemented. Where a declaration of open license is made for a utility model or design patent, a patent evaluation report shall be provided.  
Where a patentee withdraws the declaration of open license, it shall make a written request therefor, and the patent administration department under the State Council shall announce the withdrawal. The announcement of the withdrawal of a declaration of open license shall not affect the validity of an open license previously granted.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　任何单位或者个人有意愿实施开放许可的专利的，以书面方式通知专利权人，并依照公告的许可使用费支付方式、标准支付许可使用费后，即获得专利实施许可。  
开放许可实施期间，对专利权人缴纳专利年费相应给予减免。  
实行开放许可的专利权人可以与被许可人就许可使用费进行协商后给予普通许可，但不得就该专利给予独占或者排他许可。

**Article 51** Any unit or individual that intends to exploit a patent under open license shall notify the patentee in writing and pay the license fee in accordance with the announced method and standard of payment, thereby obtaining a patent exploitation license.  
During the implementation period of an open license, a corresponding reduction or exemption of the annual patent fee payable by the patentee shall be granted.  
A patentee implementing an open license may grant a non-exclusive license after negotiating the license fee with the licensee, but shall not grant an exclusive license or a sole license with respect to that patent.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　当事人就实施开放许可发生纠纷的，由当事人协商解决；不愿协商或者协商不成的，可以请求国务院专利行政部门进行调解，也可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 52** Where a dispute arises between the parties concerning the implementation of an open license, the parties shall resolve it through negotiation; if they are unwilling to negotiate or fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, they may request the patent administration department under the State Council to conduct mediation, or may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有下列情形之一的，国务院专利行政部门根据具备实施条件的单位或者个人的申请，可以给予实施发明专利或者实用新型专利的强制许可：  
（一）专利权人自专利权被授予之日起满三年，且自提出专利申请之日起满四年，无正当理由未实施或者未充分实施其专利的；  
（二）专利权人行使专利权的行为被依法认定为垄断行为，为消除或者减少该行为对竞争产生的不利影响的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-89" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第89条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 53** In any of the following circumstances, the patent administration department under the State Council may, upon the application of a unit or individual with the conditions for exploitation, grant a compulsory license to exploit an invention patent or a utility model patent:  
(1) where, after the expiration of three years from the date of grant of the patent right, and after the expiration of four years from the date of filing the patent application, the patentee has not exploited or has not fully exploited the patent without justifiable reasons; or  
(2) where the patentee's exercise of the patent right is legally determined to be a monopolistic act, and a compulsory license is necessary to eliminate or reduce the adverse effects of such act on competition.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　在国家出现紧急状态或者非常情况时，或者为了公共利益的目的，国务院专利行政部门可以给予实施发明专利或者实用新型专利的强制许可。

**Article 54** In the event of a national emergency or extraordinary circumstances, or for the purpose of public interests, the patent administration department under the State Council may grant a compulsory license to exploit an invention patent or a utility model patent.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　为了公共健康目的，对取得专利权的药品，国务院专利行政部门可以给予制造并将其出口到符合中华人民共和国参加的有关国际条约规定的国家或者地区的强制许可。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-89" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第89条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-90" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第90条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 55** For the purpose of public health, the patent administration department under the State Council may grant a compulsory license for the manufacture and export of a patented pharmaceutical product to countries or regions that meet the requirements of relevant international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　一项取得专利权的发明或者实用新型比前已经取得专利权的发明或者实用新型具有显著经济意义的重大技术进步，其实施又有赖于前一发明或者实用新型的实施的，国务院专利行政部门根据后一专利权人的申请，可以给予实施前一发明或者实用新型的强制许可。  
在依照前款规定给予实施强制许可的情形下，国务院专利行政部门根据前一专利权人的申请，也可以给予实施后一发明或者实用新型的强制许可。

**Article 56** Where an invention or utility model for which a patent right has been granted constitutes a major technological advancement with significant economic significance compared with an earlier invention or utility model for which a patent right has been granted, and the exploitation of the later invention or utility model depends on the exploitation of the earlier invention or utility model, the patent administration department under the State Council may, upon the application of the later patentee, grant a compulsory license to exploit the earlier invention or utility model.  
Under the circumstance where a compulsory license is granted in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the patent administration department under the State Council may, upon the application of the earlier patentee, also grant a compulsory license to exploit the later invention or utility model.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　强制许可涉及的发明创造为半导体技术的，其实施限于公共利益的目的和[本法第五十三条](#art-53)第（二）项规定的情形。

**Article 57** Where the invention-creation involved in a compulsory license is semiconductor technology, the exploitation shall be limited to the purpose of public interests and the circumstances specified in Article 53, paragraph (2) of this Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　除依照[本法第五十三条](#art-53)第（二）项、第五十五条规定给予的强制许可外，强制许可的实施应当主要为了供应国内市场。

**Article 58** Except for compulsory licenses granted in accordance with Article 53, paragraph (2) and Article 55 of this Law, the exploitation of compulsory licenses shall be primarily for the supply of the domestic market.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　依照[本法第五十三条](#art-53)第（一）项、第五十六条规定申请强制许可的单位或者个人应当提供证据，证明其以合理的条件请求专利权人许可其实施专利，但未能在合理的时间内获得许可。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-17" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 59** A unit or individual applying for a compulsory license in accordance with Article 53, paragraph (1) or Article 56 of this Law shall provide evidence showing that it has requested the patentee for a license to exploit the patent on reasonable terms but has not been able to obtain such a license within a reasonable period of time.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院专利行政部门作出的给予实施强制许可的决定，应当及时通知专利权人，并予以登记和公告。  
给予实施强制许可的决定，应当根据强制许可的理由规定实施的范围和时间。强制许可的理由消除并不再发生时，国务院专利行政部门应当根据专利权人的请求，经审查后作出终止实施强制许可的决定。

**Article 60** The decision made by the patent administration department under the State Council to grant a compulsory license shall be promptly notified to the patentee, and shall be registered and announced.  
The decision to grant a compulsory license shall specify the scope and duration of exploitation based on the reasons for the compulsory license. When the reasons for the compulsory license cease to exist and are unlikely to recur, the patent administration department under the State Council shall, upon the request of the patentee, make a decision to terminate the compulsory license after review.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人不享有独占的实施权，并且无权允许他人实施。

**Article 61** The entity or individual that has obtained a compulsory license shall not have an exclusive right of implementation, and shall have no right to authorize others to implement the patent.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人应当付给专利权人合理的使用费，或者依照中华人民共和国参加的有关国际条约的规定处理使用费问题。付给使用费的，其数额由双方协商；双方不能达成协议的，由国务院专利行政部门裁决。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-91" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第91条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 62** The entity or individual that has obtained a compulsory license shall pay a reasonable royalty to the patentee, or deal with the issue of royalty in accordance with the provisions of the relevant international treaties to which the People's Republic of China is a party. Where a royalty is paid, the amount thereof shall be negotiated by the parties; where the parties fail to reach an agreement, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall make a ruling.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　专利权人对国务院专利行政部门关于实施强制许可的决定不服的，专利权人和取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人对国务院专利行政部门关于实施强制许可的使用费的裁决不服的，可以自收到通知之日起三个月内向人民法院起诉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 63** Where a patentee is dissatisfied with a decision of the patent administrative department of the State Council on a compulsory license, or where the patentee and the entity or individual that has obtained a compulsory license are dissatisfied with a ruling of the patent administrative department of the State Council on the royalty for a compulsory license, they may, within three months from the date of receipt of the notification, institute legal proceedings in a People's Court.

### 第七章　专利权的保护

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　发明或者实用新型专利权的保护范围以其权利要求的内容为准，说明书及附图可以用于解释权利要求的内容。  
外观设计专利权的保护范围以表示在图片或者照片中的该产品的外观设计为准，简要说明可以用于解释图片或者照片所表示的该产品的外观设计。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-135" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第135条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 64** The scope of protection of the patent right for an invention or utility model shall be determined by the content of its claims. The description and the appended drawings may be used to interpret the content of the claims.  
The scope of protection of the patent right for a design shall be determined by the design of the product as shown in the drawings or photographs. The brief description may be used to interpret the design of the product as shown in the drawings or photographs.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　未经专利权人许可，实施其专利，即侵犯其专利权，引起纠纷的，由当事人协商解决；不愿协商或者协商不成的，专利权人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院起诉，也可以请求管理专利工作的部门处理。管理专利工作的部门处理时，认定侵权行为成立的，可以责令侵权人立即停止侵权行为，当事人不服的，可以自收到处理通知之日起十五日内依照《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》向人民法院起诉；侵权人期满不起诉又不停止侵权行为的，管理专利工作的部门可以申请人民法院强制执行。进行处理的管理专利工作的部门应当事人的请求，可以就侵犯专利权的赔偿数额进行调解；调解不成的，当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》向人民法院起诉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第20条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第21条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理专利纠纷案件适用法律问题的若干规定》第22条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-27" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理侵犯专利权纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》第27条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-102" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第102条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 65** Where any person, without permission of the patentee, exploits the patent, thereby infringing the patent right, and a dispute arises, the matter shall be settled through consultation by the parties. Where the parties are not willing to consult or where consultation fails, the patentee or interested party may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court, or may request the patent administration department to handle the matter. When the patent administration department handling the matter finds that the infringement is established, it may order the infringer to stop the infringing act immediately. If the party is dissatisfied, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notification of the order, institute legal proceedings in a People's Court in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. If the infringer neither institutes legal proceedings within the time limit nor stops the infringing act, the patent administration department may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement. Upon the request of the party, the patent administration department handling the matter may mediate the amount of compensation for the infringement of the patent right. If mediation fails, the party may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　专利侵权纠纷涉及新产品制造方法的发明专利的，制造同样产品的单位或者个人应当提供其产品制造方法不同于专利方法的证明。  
专利侵权纠纷涉及实用新型专利或者外观设计专利的，人民法院或者管理专利工作的部门可以要求专利权人或者利害关系人出具由国务院专利行政部门对相关实用新型或者外观设计进行检索、分析和评价后作出的专利权评价报告，作为审理、处理专利侵权纠纷的证据；专利权人、利害关系人或者被控侵权人也可以主动出具专利权评价报告。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/最高人民法院关于审理涉专利权评价报告案件适用法律问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉专利权评价报告案件适用法律问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-62" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第62条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 66** Where a patent infringement dispute involves an invention patent for a method of manufacturing a new product, the entity or individual manufacturing the same product shall provide proof that the method of manufacturing its or his product is different from the patented method.  
Where a patent infringement dispute involves a utility model patent or a design patent, the People's Court or the patent administration department may require the patentee or interested party to produce a patent evaluation report made by the patent administrative department of the State Council after searching, analyzing and evaluating the relevant utility model or design, as evidence for the trial or handling of the patent infringement dispute. The patentee, interested party or the alleged infringer may also voluntarily produce a patent evaluation report.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在专利侵权纠纷中，被控侵权人有证据证明其实施的技术或者设计属于现有技术或者现有设计的，不构成侵犯专利权。

**Article 67** In a patent infringement dispute, if the alleged infringer has evidence to prove that the technology or design it or he exploits is prior art or prior design, it or he shall not constitute infringement of the patent right.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　假冒专利的，除依法承担民事责任外，由负责专利执法的部门责令改正并予公告，没收违法所得，可以处违法所得五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得在五万元以下的，可以处二十五万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-101" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第101条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 68** Any person who passes off a patent shall, in addition to bearing civil liability according to law, be ordered by the patent enforcement department to make a correction and the order shall be announced, and shall confiscate the illegal gains and may be imposed a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains. If there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are not more than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan may be imposed. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　负责专利执法的部门根据已经取得的证据，对涉嫌假冒专利行为进行查处时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）询问有关当事人，调查与涉嫌违法行为有关的情况；  
（二）对当事人涉嫌违法行为的场所实施现场检查；  
（三）查阅、复制与涉嫌违法行为有关的合同、发票、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
（四）检查与涉嫌违法行为有关的产品；  
（五）对有证据证明是假冒专利的产品，可以查封或者扣押。  
管理专利工作的部门应专利权人或者利害关系人的请求处理专利侵权纠纷时，可以采取前款第（一）项、第（二）项、第（四）项所列措施。  
负责专利执法的部门、管理专利工作的部门依法行使前两款规定的职权时，当事人应当予以协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 69** When the patent enforcement department investigates and handles suspected acts of passing off a patent on the basis of evidence already obtained, it shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) to inquire of the relevant parties and investigate the circumstances related to the suspected illegal act;  
(2) to carry out on-site inspection of the premises where the party is suspected of committing the illegal act;  
(3) to consult and copy contracts, invoices, account books and other relevant materials related to the suspected illegal act;  
(4) to inspect the products related to the suspected illegal act;  
(5) to seal up or seize the products that are proved to be ones passing off a patent.  
When the patent administration department handles a patent infringement dispute at the request of the patentee or an interested party, it may take the measures listed in items (1), (2) and (4) of the preceding paragraph.  
When the patent enforcement department or the patent administration department exercises the functions and powers provided for in the preceding two paragraphs according to law, the parties shall render assistance and cooperation, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国务院专利行政部门可以应专利权人或者利害关系人的请求处理在全国有重大影响的专利侵权纠纷。  
地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门应专利权人或者利害关系人请求处理专利侵权纠纷，对在本行政区域内侵犯其同一专利权的案件可以合并处理；对跨区域侵犯其同一专利权的案件可以请求上级地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法实施细则.md#art-96" title="被《中华人民共和国专利法实施细则》第96条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 70** The patent administrative department of the State Council may, upon the request of the patentee or an interested party, handle patent infringement disputes that have a significant impact nationwide.  
The patent administration department of the local people's government shall, upon the request of the patentee or an interested party, handle patent infringement disputes. It may consolidate the cases of infringement of the same patent right within its administrative region. For cases of infringement of the same patent right across different regions, it may request the patent administration department of the higher-level local people's government to handle them.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　侵犯专利权的赔偿数额按照权利人因被侵权所受到的实际损失或者侵权人因侵权所获得的利益确定；权利人的损失或者侵权人获得的利益难以确定的，参照该专利许可使用费的倍数合理确定。对故意侵犯专利权，情节严重的，可以在按照上述方法确定数额的一倍以上五倍以下确定赔偿数额。  
权利人的损失、侵权人获得的利益和专利许可使用费均难以确定的，人民法院可以根据专利权的类型、侵权行为的性质和情节等因素，确定给予三万元以上五百万元以下的赔偿。  
赔偿数额还应当包括权利人为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支。  
人民法院为确定赔偿数额，在权利人已经尽力举证，而与侵权行为相关的账簿、资料主要由侵权人掌握的情况下，可以责令侵权人提供与侵权行为相关的账簿、资料；侵权人不提供或者提供虚假的账簿、资料的，人民法院可以参考权利人的主张和提供的证据判定赔偿数额。

**Article 71** The amount of compensation for infringement of the patent right shall be determined based on the actual losses suffered by the right holder due to the infringement or the profits gained by the infringer from the infringement. If it is difficult to determine the losses of the right holder or the profits gained by the infringer, it shall be determined reasonably by reference to the multiple of the royalty for the patent license. For intentional infringement of a patent right, if the circumstances are serious, the amount of compensation may be determined at not less than one time but not more than five times the amount determined in accordance with the above methods.  
If it is difficult to determine both the losses of the right holder, the profits gained by the infringer and the royalty for the patent license, the People's Court may, taking into account factors such as the type of patent right, the nature and circumstances of the infringement, determine an amount of compensation of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.  
The amount of compensation shall also include the reasonable expenses paid by the right holder to stop the infringement.  
In order to determine the amount of compensation, where the right holder has made every effort to provide evidence but the account books and materials related to the infringement are mainly under the control of the infringer, the People's Court may order the infringer to provide such account books and materials. If the infringer fails to provide them or provides false account books and materials, the People's Court may determine the amount of compensation with reference to the right holder's claims and the evidence provided.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　专利权人或者利害关系人有证据证明他人正在实施或者即将实施侵犯专利权、妨碍其实现权利的行为，如不及时制止将会使其合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的，可以在起诉前依法向人民法院申请采取财产保全、责令作出一定行为或者禁止作出一定行为的措施。

**Article 72** Where a patentee or an interested party has evidence to prove that another person is implementing or is about to implement acts that infringe the patent right or hinder the realization of its right, and that if such acts are not stopped in time, its lawful rights and interests will suffer irreparable damage, it may, before instituting legal proceedings, apply to a People's Court according to law for measures including property preservation, ordering the performance of certain acts or prohibiting the performance of certain acts.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　为了制止专利侵权行为，在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，专利权人或者利害关系人可以在起诉前依法向人民法院申请保全证据。

**Article 73** In order to stop the infringing act on a patent, where evidence may be destroyed or may be difficult to obtain at a later time, the patentee or an interested party may, before instituting legal proceedings, apply to a People's Court according to law for preservation of evidence.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　侵犯专利权的诉讼时效为三年，自专利权人或者利害关系人知道或者应当知道侵权行为以及侵权人之日起计算。  
发明专利申请公布后至专利权授予前使用该发明未支付适当使用费的，专利权人要求支付使用费的诉讼时效为三年，自专利权人知道或者应当知道他人使用其发明之日起计算，但是，专利权人于专利权授予之日前即已知道或者应当知道的，自专利权授予之日起计算。

**Article 74** The statute of limitations for bringing an action for infringement of a patent right shall be three years, counted from the date on which the patentee or the interested party knew or should have known of the infringement and the infringer.  
Where an invention patent is used without payment of an appropriate royalty from the date of publication of the patent application to the date of grant of the patent right, the statute of limitations for the patentee to claim the royalty shall be three years, counted from the date on which the patentee knew or should have known of the use of its invention by another person. However, if the patentee knew or should have known before the date of grant of the patent right, the statute of limitations shall be counted from the date of grant of the patent right.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　有下列情形之一的，不视为侵犯专利权：  
（一）专利产品或者依照专利方法直接获得的产品，由专利权人或者经其许可的单位、个人售出后，使用、许诺销售、销售、进口该产品的；  
（二）在专利申请日前已经制造相同产品、使用相同方法或者已经作好制造、使用的必要准备，并且仅在原有范围内继续制造、使用的；  
（三）临时通过中国领陆、领水、领空的外国运输工具，依照其所属国同中国签订的协议或者共同参加的国际条约，或者依照互惠原则，为运输工具自身需要而在其装置和设备中使用有关专利的；  
（四）专为科学研究和实验而使用有关专利的；  
（五）为提供行政审批所需要的信息，制造、使用、进口专利药品或者专利医疗器械的，以及专门为其制造、进口专利药品或者专利医疗器械的。

**Article 75** None of the following shall be deemed as infringement of a patent right:  
(1) After the sale of a patented product or a product directly obtained by a patented method by the patentee or an entity or individual authorized by the patentee, using, offering to sell, selling or importing that product;  
(2) Before the date of patent application, having already manufactured the same product, used the same method, or made necessary preparations for manufacture and use, and continuing to manufacture and use only within the original scope;  
(3) The temporary passage through the territorial land, territorial waters, or territorial air of China of a foreign means of transport, which, in accordance with an agreement between the country to which it belongs and China, or an international treaty to which both are parties, or on the principle of reciprocity, uses the relevant patent in its devices and equipment for its own needs;  
(4) Using the relevant patent solely for scientific research and experimentation;  
(5) Manufacturing, using, or importing patented medicines or patented medical devices for the purpose of providing information required for administrative examination and approval, and manufacturing or importing such patented medicines or patented medical devices specially for this purpose.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　药品上市审评审批过程中，药品上市许可申请人与有关专利权人或者利害关系人，因申请注册的药品相关的专利权产生纠纷的，相关当事人可以向人民法院起诉，请求就申请注册的药品相关技术方案是否落入他人药品专利权保护范围作出判决。国务院药品监督管理部门在规定的期限内，可以根据人民法院生效裁判作出是否暂停批准相关药品上市的决定。  
药品上市许可申请人与有关专利权人或者利害关系人也可以就申请注册的药品相关的专利权纠纷，向国务院专利行政部门请求行政裁决。  
国务院药品监督管理部门会同国务院专利行政部门制定药品上市许可审批与药品上市许可申请阶段专利权纠纷解决的具体衔接办法，报国务院同意后实施。

**Article 76** Where a dispute arises between an applicant for a drug marketing authorization and a relevant patentee or interested party concerning the patent right related to the drug for which registration is applied during the process of drug marketing review and approval, the relevant parties may institute legal proceedings in a People's Court, requesting a judgment on whether the technical scheme related to the drug for which registration is applied falls within the scope of protection of another person's drug patent right. The drug supervision and administration department of the State Council may, within the prescribed time limit, decide whether to suspend the approval of the marketing of the relevant drug based on the effective judgment of the People's Court.  
An applicant for a drug marketing authorization and the relevant patentee or interested party may also request an administrative ruling from the patent administrative department of the State Council on a patent dispute related to the drug for which registration is applied.  
The drug supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, jointly with the patent administrative department of the State Council, formulate specific connection measures for resolving patent disputes during the stage of drug marketing authorization review and approval and drug marketing authorization application, and implement them after reporting to and obtaining the consent of the State Council.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　为生产经营目的使用、许诺销售或者销售不知道是未经专利权人许可而制造并售出的专利侵权产品，能证明该产品合法来源的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 77** Any person who, for the purpose of production and business operations, uses, offers to sell, or sells a patented infringing product which is manufactured and sold without the permission of the patentee and which the person does not know to be such, shall not be liable for compensation if he can prove the lawful source of the product.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反[本法第十九条](#art-19)规定向外国申请专利，泄露国家秘密的，由所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of Article 19 of this Law, files a patent application in a foreign country, thereby divulging a state secret, shall be given an administrative sanction by the entity to which he belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　管理专利工作的部门不得参与向社会推荐专利产品等经营活动。  
管理专利工作的部门违反前款规定的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令改正，消除影响，有违法收入的予以没收；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 79** The patent administration department shall not participate in business activities such as recommending patented products to the public.  
Where the patent administration department violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, its superior organ or the supervisory organ shall order it to make a correction and eliminate the adverse effects, and confiscate any illegal gains. If the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　从事专利管理工作的国家机关工作人员以及其他有关国家机关工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 80** Any functionary of a state organ engaged in patent administration work or any other relevant functionary of a state organ who neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law. If the act does not constitute a crime, he shall be given a sanction according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　向国务院专利行政部门申请专利和办理其他手续，应当按照规定缴纳费用。

**Article 81** An applicant for a patent and a person handling other matters with the patent administration department of the State Council shall pay the fees as prescribed.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本法自1985年4月1日起施行。

**Article 82** This Law shall come into force as of April 1, 1985.
