# 最高人民法院关于审查知识产权纠纷行为保全案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕21号  
**公布日期**：2018-11-26  
**生效日期**：2019-01-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审查知识产权纠纷行为保全案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2018年11月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1755次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年1月1日起施行。 2018年12月12日 最高人民法院 关于审查知识产权纠纷行为保全案件 适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕21号 为正确审查知识产权纠纷行为保全案件，及时有效保护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国专利法》《中华人民共和国商标法》《中华人民共和国著作权法》等有关法律规定，结合审判、执行工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定中的知识产权纠纷是指《民事案件案由规定》中的知识产权与竞争纠纷。 第二条　知识产权纠纷的当事人在判决、裁定或者仲裁裁决生效前，依据民事诉讼法第一百条、第一百零一条规定申请行为保全的，人民法院应当受理。 知识产权许可合同的被许可人申请诉前责令停止侵害知识产权行为的，独占许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他许可合同的被许可人在权利人不申请的情况下，可以单独提出申请；普通许可合同的被许可人经权利人明确授权以自己的名义起诉的，可以单独提出申请。 第三条　申请诉前行为保全，应当向被申请人住所地具有相应知识产权纠纷管辖权的人民法院或者对案件具有管辖权的人民法院提出。 当事人约定仲裁的，应当向前款规定的人民法院申请行为保全。 第四条　向人民法院申请行为保全，应当递交申请书和相应证据。申请书应当载明下列事项： （一）申请人与被申请人的身份、送达地址、联系方式； （二）申请采取行为保全措施的内容和期限；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定中的知识产权纠纷是指《民事案件案由规定》中的知识产权与竞争纠纷。

**Article 1** . Intellectual property disputes as mentioned in these Provisions refer to intellectual property and competition disputes as defined in the Provisions on Causes of Action in Civil Cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　知识产权纠纷的当事人在判决、裁定或者仲裁裁决生效前，依据[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)、第一百零一条规定申请行为保全的，人民法院应当受理。  
知识产权许可合同的被许可人申请诉前责令停止侵害知识产权行为的，独占许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他许可合同的被许可人在权利人不申请的情况下，可以单独提出申请；普通许可合同的被许可人经权利人明确授权以自己的名义起诉的，可以单独提出申请。

**Article 2** . Where a party to an intellectual property dispute applies for a preservation of acts in accordance with Articles 100 and 101 of the Civil Procedure Law before a judgment, ruling or arbitration award takes effect, the People's Court shall accept the application.  
Where a licensee under an intellectual property license contract applies for a pre-litigation order to cease an act of intellectual property infringement, a licensee under an exclusive license contract may file an application with the People's Court separately; a licensee under a sole license contract, if the right holder fails to apply, may file an application separately; a licensee under a non-exclusive license contract, if expressly authorized by the right holder to bring an action in its own name, may file an application separately.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请诉前行为保全，应当向被申请人住所地具有相应知识产权纠纷管辖权的人民法院或者对案件具有管辖权的人民法院提出。  
当事人约定仲裁的，应当向前款规定的人民法院申请行为保全。

**Article 3** . An application for pre-litigation preservation of acts shall be filed with the People's Court having jurisdiction over the corresponding intellectual property dispute at the domicile of the respondent or with the People's Court having jurisdiction over the case.  
Where the parties have agreed on arbitration, an application for preservation of acts shall be filed with the People's Court as specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　向人民法院申请行为保全，应当递交申请书和相应证据。申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）申请人与被申请人的身份、送达地址、联系方式；  
（二）申请采取行为保全措施的内容和期限；  
（三）申请所依据的事实、理由，包括被申请人的行为将会使申请人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害或者造成案件裁决难以执行等损害的具体说明；  
（四）为行为保全提供担保的财产信息或资信证明，或者不需要提供担保的理由；  
（五）其他需要载明的事项。

**Article 4** . An application for preservation of acts filed with the People's Court shall be accompanied by a written application and corresponding evidence. The written application shall specify the following matters:  
(1) The identities, service addresses and contact information of the applicant and the respondent;  
(2) The content and duration of the preservation of acts measures sought;  
(3) The facts and grounds on which the application is based, including a specific description of the damage that the respondent's conduct will cause to the applicant's lawful rights and interests that is difficult to remedy or will make the judgment in the case difficult to enforce;  
(4) Information on property or creditworthiness certificates for the provision of security for the preservation of acts, or reasons why security is not required;  
(5) Other matters that need to be specified.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院裁定采取行为保全措施前，应当询问申请人和被申请人，但因情况紧急或者询问可能影响保全措施执行等情形除外。  
人民法院裁定采取行为保全措施或者裁定驳回申请的，应当向申请人、被申请人送达裁定书。向被申请人送达裁定书可能影响采取保全措施的，人民法院可以在采取保全措施后及时向被申请人送达裁定书，至迟不得超过五日。  
当事人在仲裁过程中申请行为保全的，应当通过仲裁机构向人民法院提交申请书、仲裁案件受理通知书等相关材料。人民法院裁定采取行为保全措施或者裁定驳回申请的，应当将裁定书送达当事人，并通知仲裁机构。

**Article 5** . Before ruling to take preservation of acts measures, the People's Court shall hear the applicant and the respondent, except where the circumstances are urgent or where hearing may affect the execution of the preservation measures.  
Where the People's Court rules to take preservation of acts measures or rules to reject the application, it shall serve the written ruling on the applicant and the respondent. If serving the written ruling on the respondent may affect the taking of preservation measures, the People's Court may serve the written ruling on the respondent in a timely manner after taking the preservation measures, but no later than five days.  
Where a party applies for preservation of acts during arbitration proceedings, it shall submit the application, the notice of acceptance of the arbitration case, and other relevant materials to the People's Court through the arbitration institution. Where the People's Court rules to take preservation of acts measures or rules to reject the application, it shall serve the written ruling on the parties and notify the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有下列情况之一，不立即采取行为保全措施即足以损害申请人利益的，应当认定属于[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)、第一百零一条规定的“情况紧急”：  
（一）申请人的商业秘密即将被非法披露；  
（二）申请人的发表权、隐私权等人身权利即将受到侵害；  
（三）诉争的知识产权即将被非法处分；  
（四）申请人的知识产权在展销会等时效性较强的场合正在或者即将受到侵害；  
（五）时效性较强的热播节目正在或者即将受到侵害；  
（六）其他需要立即采取行为保全措施的情况。

**Article 6** . Where any of the following circumstances exists, and failure to immediately take preservation of acts measures would be sufficient to harm the interests of the applicant, it shall be deemed an "urgent situation" as provided in Articles 100 and 101 of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) The applicant's trade secrets are about to be illegally disclosed;  
(2) The applicant's personal rights such as the right of publication and the right to privacy are about to be infringed;  
(3) The intellectual property rights in dispute are about to be illegally disposed of;  
(4) The applicant's intellectual property rights are being or are about to be infringed at venues with strong timeliness such as trade fairs;  
(5) Hot-broadcast programs with strong timeliness are being or are about to be infringed;  
(6) Other circumstances where immediate preservation of acts measures are necessary.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院审查行为保全申请，应当综合考量下列因素：  
（一）申请人的请求是否具有事实基础和法律依据，包括请求保护的知识产权效力是否稳定；  
（二）不采取行为保全措施是否会使申请人的合法权益受到难以弥补的损害或者造成案件裁决难以执行等损害；  
（三）不采取行为保全措施对申请人造成的损害是否超过采取行为保全措施对被申请人造成的损害；  
（四）采取行为保全措施是否损害社会公共利益；  
（五）其他应当考量的因素。

**Article 7** . When reviewing an application for preservation of acts, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) Whether the applicant's request has a factual basis and legal basis, including whether the validity of the intellectual property rights sought to be protected is stable;  
(2) Whether failure to take preservation of acts measures would cause damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests that is difficult to remedy or would cause damage such as making the judgment in the case difficult to enforce;  
(3) Whether the damage caused to the applicant by not taking preservation of acts measures exceeds the damage caused to the respondent by taking such measures;  
(4) Whether taking preservation of acts measures damages the public interest;  
(5) Other factors that shall be considered.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院审查判断申请人请求保护的知识产权效力是否稳定，应当综合考量下列因素：  
（一）所涉权利的类型或者属性；  
（二）所涉权利是否经过实质审查；  
（三）所涉权利是否处于宣告无效或者撤销程序中以及被宣告无效或者撤销的可能性；  
（四）所涉权利是否存在权属争议；  
（五）其他可能导致所涉权利效力不稳定的因素。

**Article 8** . When reviewing and determining whether the validity of the intellectual property rights sought to be protected by the applicant is stable, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) The type or nature of the rights involved;  
(2) Whether the rights involved have undergone substantive examination;  
(3) Whether the rights involved are in the process of being declared invalid or revoked, and the possibility of being declared invalid or revoked;  
(4) Whether there is a dispute over the ownership of the rights involved;  
(5) Other factors that may cause instability in the validity of the rights involved.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请人以实用新型或者外观设计专利权为依据申请行为保全的，应当提交由国务院专利行政部门作出的检索报告、专利权评价报告或者专利复审委员会维持该专利权有效的决定。申请人无正当理由拒不提交的，人民法院应当裁定驳回其申请。

**Article 9** . Where an applicant applies for preservation of acts based on a utility model or design patent right, it shall submit a search report, a patent right evaluation report made by the patent administrative department of the State Council, or a decision of the Patent Reexamination Board maintaining the validity of the patent right. If the applicant refuses to submit such documents without justifiable reasons, the People's Court shall rule to reject its application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在知识产权与不正当竞争纠纷行为保全案件中，有下列情形之一的，应当认定属于[民事诉讼法第一百零一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-101)规定的“难以弥补的损害”：  
（一）被申请人的行为将会侵害申请人享有的商誉或者发表权、隐私权等人身性质的权利且造成无法挽回的损害；  
（二）被申请人的行为将会导致侵权行为难以控制且显著增加申请人损害；  
（三）被申请人的侵害行为将会导致申请人的相关市场份额明显减少；  
（四）对申请人造成其他难以弥补的损害。

**Article 10** . In cases concerning preservation of acts in intellectual property and unfair competition disputes, where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "damage difficult to remedy" as provided in Article 101 of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) The respondent's conduct will infringe upon the applicant's goodwill or personal rights such as the right of publication and the right to privacy, causing irreparable damage;  
(2) The respondent's conduct will make the infringement difficult to control and significantly increase the damage to the applicant;  
(3) The respondent's infringing conduct will cause a significant reduction in the applicant's relevant market share;  
(4) Other damage difficult to remedy is caused to the applicant.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请人申请行为保全的，应当依法提供担保。  
申请人提供的担保数额，应当相当于被申请人可能因执行行为保全措施所遭受的损失，包括责令停止侵权行为所涉产品的销售收益、保管费用等合理损失。  
在执行行为保全措施过程中，被申请人可能因此遭受的损失超过申请人担保数额的，人民法院可以责令申请人追加相应的担保。申请人拒不追加的，可以裁定解除或者部分解除保全措施。

**Article 11** . An applicant applying for preservation of acts shall provide security according to law.  
The amount of security provided by the applicant shall be equivalent to the losses that the respondent may suffer as a result of the execution of the preservation of acts measures, including reasonable losses such as the sales revenue of the products involved in the order to cease the infringing act, storage fees, etc.  
During the execution of the preservation of acts measures, if the losses that the respondent may suffer exceed the amount of security provided by the applicant, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide additional corresponding security. If the applicant refuses to provide additional security, the People's Court may rule to dissolve or partially dissolve the preservation measures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院采取的行为保全措施，一般不因被申请人提供担保而解除，但是申请人同意的除外。

**Article 12** . The preservation of acts measures taken by the People's Court shall generally not be dissolved due to the provision of security by the respondent, except where the applicant consents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院裁定采取行为保全措施的，应当根据申请人的请求或者案件具体情况等因素合理确定保全措施的期限。  
裁定停止侵害知识产权行为的效力，一般应当维持至案件裁判生效时止。  
人民法院根据申请人的请求、追加担保等情况，可以裁定继续采取保全措施。申请人请求续行保全措施的，应当在期限届满前七日内提出。

**Article 13** . Where the People's Court rules to take preservation of acts measures, it shall reasonably determine the duration of the preservation measures based on factors such as the applicant's request or the specific circumstances of the case.  
The effect of the ruling to cease the act of intellectual property infringement shall generally be maintained until the judgment in the case takes effect.  
The People's Court may, based on the applicant's request, additional security, and other circumstances, rule to continue the preservation measures. If the applicant requests an extension of the preservation measures, it shall file the request within seven days before the expiration of the period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人不服行为保全裁定申请复议的，人民法院应当在收到复议申请后十日内审查并作出裁定。

**Article 14** . Where a party applies for reconsideration of a ruling on preservation of acts, the People's Court shall review and make a ruling within ten days after receiving the application for reconsideration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院采取行为保全的方法和措施，依照执行程序相关规定处理。

**Article 15** . The methods and measures for the People's Court to take preservation of acts shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on enforcement procedures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　有下列情形之一的，应当认定属于[民事诉讼法第一百零五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-105)规定的“申请有错误”：  
（一）申请人在采取行为保全措施后三十日内不依法提起诉讼或者申请仲裁；  
（二）行为保全措施因请求保护的知识产权被宣告无效等原因自始不当；  
（三）申请责令被申请人停止侵害知识产权或者不正当竞争，但生效裁判认定不构成侵权或者不正当竞争；  
（四）其他属于申请有错误的情形。

**Article 16** . Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "the application is erroneous" as provided in Article 105 of the Civil Procedure Law:  
(1) The applicant fails to file a lawsuit or apply for arbitration according to law within 30 days after the preservation of acts measures are taken;  
(2) The preservation of acts measures are improper ab initio due to the invalidation of the intellectual property rights sought to be protected, or other reasons;  
(3) The application seeks to order the respondent to cease the infringement of intellectual property rights or unfair competition, but the effective judgment determines that no infringement or unfair competition exists;  
(4) Other circumstances where the application is erroneous.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人申请解除行为保全措施，人民法院收到申请后经审查符合《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第一百六十六条规定的情形的，应当在五日内裁定解除。  
申请人撤回行为保全申请或者申请解除行为保全措施的，不因此免除[民事诉讼法第一百零五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-105)规定的赔偿责任。

**Article 17** . Where a party applies for the dissolution of preservation of acts measures, the People's Court, after receiving the application and upon review, if the circumstances meet the provisions of Article 166 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, shall rule to dissolve within five days.  
Where the applicant withdraws the application for preservation of acts or applies for the dissolution of preservation of acts measures, the liability for compensation provided in Article 105 of the Civil Procedure Law shall not be exempted thereby.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被申请人依据[民事诉讼法第一百零五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-105)规定提起赔偿诉讼，申请人申请诉前行为保全后没有起诉或者当事人约定仲裁的，由采取保全措施的人民法院管辖；申请人已经起诉的，由受理起诉的人民法院管辖。

**Article 18** . Where the respondent files a compensation action pursuant to Article 105 of the Civil Procedure Law, and the applicant has not filed a lawsuit after applying for pre-litigation preservation of acts or the parties have agreed on arbitration, the People's Court that took the preservation measures shall have jurisdiction; where the applicant has already filed a lawsuit, the People's Court that accepted the lawsuit shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人同时申请行为保全、财产保全或者证据保全的，人民法院应当依法分别审查不同类型保全申请是否符合条件，并作出裁定。  
为避免被申请人实施转移财产、毁灭证据等行为致使保全目的无法实现，人民法院可以根据案件具体情况决定不同类型保全措施的执行顺序。

**Article 19** . Where the applicant simultaneously applies for preservation of acts, preservation of property or preservation of evidence, the People's Court shall separately review whether the different types of preservation applications meet the conditions according to law, and make a ruling.  
To prevent the respondent from transferring property, destroying evidence, or engaging in other acts that may render the preservation purpose impossible to achieve, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, determine the order of execution of different types of preservation measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请人申请行为保全，应当依照《诉讼费用交纳办法》关于申请采取行为保全措施的规定交纳申请费。

**Article 20** . An applicant applying for preservation of acts shall pay the application fee in accordance with the provisions of the Measures on the Payment of Litigation Fees regarding the application for taking preservation of acts measures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定自2019年1月1日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的相关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 21** . These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2019. Where any relevant judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
