# 最高人民法院关于审理申请注册的药品相关的专利权纠纷民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕13号  
**公布日期**：2021-05-24  
**生效日期**：2021-07-05

> 《最高人民法院关于审理申请注册的药品相关的专利权纠纷民事案件适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2021年5月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1839次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年7月5日起施行。 2021年7月4日 最高人民法院 关于审理申请注册的药品相关的专利权纠纷民事 案件适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2021〕13号 为正确审理申请注册的药品相关的专利权纠纷民事案件，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合知识产权审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条　当事人依据专利法第七十六条规定提起的确认是否落入专利权保护范围纠纷的第一审案件，由北京知识产权法院管辖。 第二条　专利法第七十六条所称相关的专利，是指适用国务院有关行政部门关于药品上市许可审批与药品上市许可申请阶段专利权纠纷解决的具体衔接办法（以下简称衔接办法）的专利。 专利法第七十六条所称利害关系人，是指前款所称专利的被许可人、相关药品上市许可持有人。 第三条　专利权人或者利害关系人依据专利法第七十六条起诉的，应当按照民事诉讼法第一百一十九条第三项的规定提交下列材料： （一）国务院有关行政部门依据衔接办法所设平台中登记的相关专利信息，包括专利名称、专利号、相关的权利要求等； （二）国务院有关行政部门依据衔接办法所设平台中公示的申请注册药品的相关信息，包括药品名称、药品类型、注册类别以及申请注册药品与所涉及的上市药品之间的对应关系等； （三）药品上市许可申请人依据衔接办法作出的四类声明及声明依据。 药品上市许可申请人应当在一审答辩期内，向人民法院提交其向国家药品审评机构申报的、与认定是否落入相关专利权保护范围对应的必要技术资料副本。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人依据[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)规定提起的确认是否落入专利权保护范围纠纷的第一审案件，由北京知识产权法院管辖。

**Article 1** The first instance case of a dispute brought by a party in accordance with Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China to determine whether it falls within the protection scope of a patent right shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称相关的专利，是指适用国务院有关行政部门关于药品上市许可审批与药品上市许可申请阶段专利权纠纷解决的具体衔接办法（以下简称衔接办法）的专利。  
[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称利害关系人，是指前款所称专利的被许可人、相关药品上市许可持有人。

**Article 2** The relevant patents referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China mean patents to which the specific transitional measures of the relevant administrative department of the State Council concerning the resolution of patent disputes in the stage of drug marketing authorization review and drug marketing authorization application (hereinafter referred to as the Transitional Measures) apply.  
The interested parties referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China mean the licensees of the patents mentioned in the preceding paragraph and the relevant drug marketing authorization holders.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　专利权人或者利害关系人依据[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)起诉的，应当按照[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)第三项的规定提交下列材料：  
（一）国务院有关行政部门依据衔接办法所设平台中登记的相关专利信息，包括专利名称、专利号、相关的权利要求等；  
（二）国务院有关行政部门依据衔接办法所设平台中公示的申请注册药品的相关信息，包括药品名称、药品类型、注册类别以及申请注册药品与所涉及的上市药品之间的对应关系等；  
（三）药品上市许可申请人依据衔接办法作出的四类声明及声明依据。  
药品上市许可申请人应当在一审答辩期内，向人民法院提交其向国家药品审评机构申报的、与认定是否落入相关专利权保护范围对应的必要技术资料副本。

**Article 3** Where a patentee or interested party files a lawsuit under Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall submit the following materials in accordance with the provisions of Item 3 of Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the relevant patent information registered in the platform set up by the relevant administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the Transitional Measures, including the patent name, patent number, relevant claims, etc.;  
(2) the relevant information on the drug under application for registration publicly published in the platform set up by the relevant administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the Transitional Measures, including the drug name, drug type, registration category, and the correspondence between the drug under application for registration and the involved listed drug, etc.;  
(3) the four types of declarations made by the drug marketing authorization applicant in accordance with the Transitional Measures and the basis for the declarations.  
The drug marketing authorization applicant shall, within the defense period in the first instance, submit to the People's Court copies of the necessary technical information that it has submitted to the national drug review authority and that corresponds to the determination of whether it falls within the protection scope of the relevant patent right.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　专利权人或者利害关系人在衔接办法规定的期限内未向人民法院提起诉讼的，药品上市许可申请人可以向人民法院起诉，请求确认申请注册药品未落入相关专利权保护范围。

**Article 4** If the patentee or interested party does not file a lawsuit with the People's Court within the time limit prescribed in the Transitional Measures, the drug marketing authorization applicant may file a lawsuit with the People's Court, requesting a confirmation that the drug under application for registration does not fall within the protection scope of the relevant patent right.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人以国务院专利行政部门已经受理[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称行政裁决请求为由，主张不应当受理[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称诉讼或者申请中止诉讼的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where a party, on the ground that the patent administrative department of the State Council has accepted a request for administrative adjudication referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, argues that the lawsuit referred to in the said Article shall not be accepted or applies for suspension of the lawsuit, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人依据[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)起诉后，以国务院专利行政部门已经受理宣告相关专利权无效的请求为由，申请中止诉讼的，人民法院一般不予支持。

**Article 6** After a party files a lawsuit under Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, if it applies for suspension of the lawsuit on the ground that the patent administrative department of the State Council has accepted a request for invalidation of the relevant patent right, the People's Court shall generally not support the application.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　药品上市许可申请人主张具有[专利法第六十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-67)、第七十五条第二项等规定情形的，人民法院经审查属实，可以判决确认申请注册的药品相关技术方案未落入相关专利权保护范围。

**Article 7** Where the drug marketing authorization applicant claims that the circumstances provided for in Article 67 and Item 2 of Article 75 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China exist, and upon examination, the People's Court finds it to be true, it may render a judgment confirming that the relevant technical solution of the drug under application for registration does not fall within the protection scope of the relevant patent right.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人对其在诉讼中获取的商业秘密或者其他需要保密的商业信息负有保密义务，擅自披露或者在该诉讼活动之外使用、允许他人使用的，应当依法承担民事责任。构成[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)规定情形的，人民法院应当依法处理。

**Article 8** A party that acquires trade secrets or other business information that needs to be kept confidential in the course of litigation shall have the obligation to keep it confidential. If the party discloses it without authorization or uses or allows others to use it outside the litigation activity, it shall bear civil liability according to law. If such conduct constitutes the circumstances provided for in Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall handle it according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　药品上市许可申请人向人民法院提交的申请注册的药品相关技术方案，与其向国家药品审评机构申报的技术资料明显不符，妨碍人民法院审理案件的，人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定处理。

**Article 9** Where the relevant technical solution of the drug under application for registration submitted by the drug marketing authorization applicant to the People's Court is clearly inconsistent with the technical information it has submitted to the national drug review authority, and hinders the People's Court in trying the case, the People's Court shall handle it in accordance with the provisions of Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　专利权人或者利害关系人在[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称诉讼中申请行为保全，请求禁止药品上市许可申请人在相关专利权有效期内实施[专利法第十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-11)规定的行为的，人民法院依照专利法、民事诉讼法有关规定处理；请求禁止药品上市申请行为或者审评审批行为的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** If, in the lawsuit referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, the patentee or interested party applies for behavior preservation, requesting to prohibit the drug marketing authorization applicant from engaging in the acts provided for in Article 11 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China within the term of validity of the relevant patent right, the People's Court shall handle it in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China and the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; if the request is to prohibit the drug marketing application act or the review and approval act, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在针对同一专利权和申请注册药品的侵害专利权或者确认不侵害专利权诉讼中，当事人主张依据[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称诉讼的生效判决认定涉案药品技术方案是否落入相关专利权保护范围的，人民法院一般予以支持。但是，有证据证明被诉侵权药品技术方案与申请注册的药品相关技术方案不一致或者新主张的事由成立的除外。

**Article 11** In a lawsuit for patent infringement or a lawsuit for confirmation of non-infringement concerning the same patent right and the same drug under application for registration, if a party argues that it shall rely on the effective judgment in the lawsuit referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China to determine whether the technical solution of the involved drug falls within the protection scope of the relevant patent right, the People's Court shall generally support such argument. However, this shall not apply where there is evidence proving that the technical solution of the accused infringing drug is inconsistent with the relevant technical solution of the drug under application for registration, or where the grounds for a new claim are established.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　专利权人或者利害关系人知道或者应当知道其主张的专利权应当被宣告无效或者申请注册药品的相关技术方案未落入专利权保护范围，仍提起[专利法第七十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-76)所称诉讼或者请求行政裁决的，药品上市许可申请人可以向北京知识产权法院提起损害赔偿之诉。

**Article 12** Where a patentee or interested party knows or should have known that the patent right it asserts should be declared invalid or that the relevant technical solution of the drug under application for registration does not fall within the protection scope of the patent right, but still initiates the lawsuit referred to in Article 76 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China or requests administrative adjudication, the drug marketing authorization applicant may file a lawsuit for damages with the Beijing Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院依法向当事人在国务院有关行政部门依据衔接办法所设平台登载的联系人、通讯地址、电子邮件等进行的送达，视为有效送达。当事人向人民法院提交送达地址确认书后，人民法院也可以向该确认书载明的送达地址送达。

**Article 13** Service by the People's Court in accordance with law to the contact person, mailing address, email address, etc. of a party registered in the platform set up by the relevant administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the Transitional Measures shall be deemed effective service. After a party submits a confirmation of service address to the People's Court, the People's Court may also serve at the service address stated in such confirmation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定自2021年7月5日起施行。本院以前发布的相关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 14** These Provisions shall come into force on July 5, 2021. If any relevant judicial interpretation previously issued by this Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
