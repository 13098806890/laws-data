# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及驰名商标保护的民事纠纷案件应用法律若干问题的解释

**发布机关**：最高人民法院

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本解释所称驰名商标，是指在中国境内为相关公众广为知晓的商标。

**Article 1** A well-known trademark as referred to in this Interpretation means a trademark that is widely known by the relevant public within the territory of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在下列民事纠纷案件中，当事人以商标驰名作为事实根据，人民法院根据案件具体情况，认为确有必要的，对所涉商标是否驰名作出认定：  
（一）以违反[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)的规定为由，提起的侵犯商标权诉讼；  
（二）以企业名称与其驰名商标相同或者近似为由，提起的侵犯商标权或者不正当竞争诉讼；  
（三）符合[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定的抗辩或者反诉的诉讼。

**Article 2** In the following civil dispute cases, where a party takes a trademark's well-known status as a factual basis, and the People's Court finds it truly necessary based on the specific circumstances of the case, it shall make a determination as to whether the involved trademark is well known:  
(1) an action for infringement of trademark right filed on the ground of violating Article 13 of the Trademark Law;  
(2) an action for infringement of trademark right or unfair competition filed on the ground that an enterprise name is identical or similar to its well-known trademark; or  
(3) an action involving a defense or counterclaim that meets the requirements of Article 6 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在下列民事纠纷案件中，人民法院对于所涉商标是否驰名不予审查：  
（一）被诉侵犯商标权或者不正当竞争行为的成立不以商标驰名为事实根据的；  
（二）被诉侵犯商标权或者不正当竞争行为因不具备法律规定的其他要件而不成立的。  
原告以被告注册、使用的域名与其注册商标相同或者近似，并通过该域名进行相关商品交易的电子商务，足以造成相关公众误认为由，提起的侵权诉讼，按照前款第（一）项的规定处理。

**Article 3** In the following civil dispute cases, the People's Court shall not examine whether the involved trademark is well known:  
(1) the establishment of the alleged infringement of trademark right or unfair competition act is not based on the fact that the trademark is well known; or  
(2) the alleged infringement of trademark right or unfair competition act does not constitute such an act due to the lack of other elements prescribed by law.  
Where a plaintiff files an infringement action on the ground that a domain name registered or used by the defendant is identical or similar to its registered trademark and through such domain name the defendant conducts e-commerce for related commodities, which is sufficient to cause misidentification by the relevant public, the case shall be handled in accordance with subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院认定商标是否驰名，应当以证明其驰名的事实为依据，综合考虑[商标法第十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-14)规定的各项因素，但是根据案件具体情况无需考虑该条规定的全部因素即足以认定商标驰名的情形除外。

**Article 4** In determining whether a trademark is well known, the People's Court shall base its determination on the facts proving its well-known status and comprehensively consider the factors set forth in Article 14 of the Trademark Law, unless the specific circumstances of the case make it unnecessary to consider all the factors set forth in that Article for sufficient determination of the trademark's well-known status.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人主张商标驰名的，应当根据案件具体情况，提供下列证据，证明被诉侵犯商标权或者不正当竞争行为发生时，其商标已属驰名：  
（一）使用该商标的商品的市场份额、销售区域、利税等；  
（二）该商标的持续使用时间；  
（三）该商标的宣传或者促销活动的方式、持续时间、程度、资金投入和地域范围；  
（四）该商标曾被作为驰名商标受保护的记录；  
（五）该商标享有的市场声誉；  
（六）证明该商标已属驰名的其他事实。  
前款所涉及的商标使用的时间、范围、方式等，包括其核准注册前持续使用的情形。  
对于商标使用时间长短、行业排名、市场调查报告、市场价值评估报告、是否曾被认定为著名商标等证据，人民法院应当结合认定商标驰名的其他证据，客观、全面地进行审查。

**Article 5** Where a party claims that a trademark is well known, it shall, according to the specific circumstances of the case, provide the following evidence to prove that at the time of the alleged infringement of trademark right or unfair competition act, its trademark had become well known:  
(1) the market share, sales area, taxes and profits, etc., of the goods using the trademark;  
(2) the duration of continuous use of the trademark;  
(3) the manner, duration, extent, capital investment, and geographical scope of publicity or promotion activities for the trademark;  
(4) records of the trademark having been protected as a well-known trademark;  
(5) the market reputation enjoyed by the trademark; and  
(6) other facts proving that the trademark has become well known.  
The time, scope, and manner of use of the trademark as referred to in the preceding paragraph include the circumstances of continuous use prior to its approval and registration.  
With respect to evidence such as the length of time the trademark has been in use, industry rankings, market survey reports, market value assessment reports, and whether the trademark has been recognized as a famous trademark, the People's Court shall conduct an objective and comprehensive review in conjunction with other evidence for determining the trademark's well-known status.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　原告以被诉商标的使用侵犯其注册商标专用权为由提起民事诉讼，被告以原告的注册商标复制、摹仿或者翻译其在先未注册驰名商标为由提出抗辩或者提起反诉的，应当对其在先未注册商标驰名的事实负举证责任。

**Article 6** Where a plaintiff files a civil action on the ground that the use of the alleged trademark infringes its exclusive right to use a registered trademark, and the defendant raises a defense or counterclaim on the ground that the plaintiff's registered trademark copies, imitates, or translates its prior unregistered well-known trademark, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the fact that its prior unregistered trademark is well known.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被诉侵犯商标权或者不正当竞争行为发生前，曾被人民法院或者国务院工商行政管理部门认定驰名的商标，被告对该商标驰名的事实不持异议的，人民法院应当予以认定。被告提出异议的，原告仍应当对该商标驰名的事实负举证责任。  
除本解释另有规定外，人民法院对于商标驰名的事实，不适用民事诉讼证据的自认规则。

**Article 7** Where, before the alleged infringement of trademark right or unfair competition act, a trademark has been recognized as well known by the People's Court or the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council, and the defendant does not object to the fact that the trademark is well known, the People's Court shall affirm such fact. If the defendant raises an objection, the plaintiff shall still bear the burden of adducing evidence for the fact that the trademark is well known.  
Unless otherwise provided in this Interpretation, the People's Court shall not apply the rule of admission of evidence in civil actions to the fact that a trademark is well known.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对于在中国境内为社会公众广为知晓的商标，原告已提供其商标驰名的基本证据，或者被告不持异议的，人民法院对该商标驰名的事实予以认定。

**Article 8** Where the plaintiff has provided basic evidence that its trademark is well known to the relevant public within the territory of China, or the defendant does not object, the People's Court shall affirm the fact that the trademark is well known.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　足以使相关公众对使用驰名商标和被诉商标的商品来源产生误认，或者足以使相关公众认为使用驰名商标和被诉商标的经营者之间具有许可使用、关联企业关系等特定联系的，属于[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)第一款规定的“容易导致混淆”。  
足以使相关公众认为被诉商标与驰名商标具有相当程度的联系，而减弱驰名商标的显著性、贬损驰名商标的市场声誉，或者不正当利用驰名商标的市场声誉的，属于[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)第二款规定的“误导公众，致使该驰名商标注册人的利益可能受到损害”。

**Article 9** Where the use of a well-known trademark and the alleged trademark is sufficient to cause misidentification by the relevant public as to the source of the goods, or is sufficient to cause the relevant public to believe that there is a specific relationship such as a licensing or affiliated enterprise relationship between the operators using the well-known trademark and the alleged trademark, this shall constitute "likely to cause confusion" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 13 of the Trademark Law.  
Where the alleged trademark is sufficient to cause the relevant public to believe that there is a considerable degree of connection with the well-known trademark, thereby diluting the distinctiveness of the well-known trademark, disparaging the market reputation of the well-known trademark, or unfairly exploiting the market reputation of the well-known trademark, this shall constitute "misleading the public, so that the interests of the registrant of the well-known trademark may be prejudiced" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 13 of the Trademark Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　原告请求禁止被告在不相类似商品上使用与原告驰名的注册商标相同或者近似的商标或者企业名称的，人民法院应当根据案件具体情况，综合考虑以下因素后作出裁判：  
（一）该驰名商标的显著程度；  
（二）该驰名商标在使用被诉商标或者企业名称的商品的相关公众中的知晓程度；  
（三）使用驰名商标的商品与使用被诉商标或者企业名称的商品之间的关联程度；  
（四）其他相关因素。

**Article 10** Where the plaintiff requests to prohibit the defendant from using, on goods that are not similar, a trademark or enterprise name that is identical or similar to the plaintiff's well-known registered trademark, the People's Court shall, according to the specific circumstances of the case, make a judgment after comprehensively considering the following factors:  
(1) the degree of distinctiveness of the well-known trademark;  
(2) the degree of awareness of the well-known trademark among the relevant public of the goods on which the alleged trademark or enterprise name is used;  
(3) the degree of association between the goods using the well-known trademark and the goods using the alleged trademark or enterprise name; and  
(4) other relevant factors.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被告使用的注册商标违反[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)的规定，复制、摹仿或者翻译原告驰名商标，构成侵犯商标权的，人民法院应当根据原告的请求，依法判决禁止被告使用该商标，但被告的注册商标有下列情形之一的，人民法院对原告的请求不予支持：  
（一）已经超过[商标法第四十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-41)第二款规定的请求撤销期限的；  
（二）被告提出注册申请时，原告的商标并不驰名的。

**Article 11** Where the registered trademark used by the defendant violates Article 13 of the Trademark Law by copying, imitating, or translating the plaintiff's well-known trademark, thus constituting infringement of trademark right, the People's Court shall, upon the plaintiff's request, according to law render a judgment prohibiting the defendant from using such trademark. However, if the defendant's registered trademark falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not support the plaintiff's request:  
(1) the time limit for requesting cancellation as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 41 of the Trademark Law has expired; or  
(2) at the time the defendant filed the application for registration, the plaintiff's trademark was not well known.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人请求保护的未注册驰名商标，属于[商标法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-10)、第十一条、第十二条规定不得作为商标使用或者注册情形的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 12** Where an unregistered well-known trademark for which the party requests protection falls under the circumstances provided in Articles 10, 11, or 12 of the Trademark Law, where use or registration as a trademark is prohibited, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在涉及驰名商标保护的民事纠纷案件中，人民法院对于商标驰名的认定，仅作为案件事实和判决理由，不写入判决主文；以调解方式审结的，在调解书中对商标驰名的事实不予认定。

**Article 13** In civil dispute cases involving the protection of well-known trademarks, the determination by the People's Court of a trademark's well-known status shall serve only as a case fact and a reason for judgment, and shall not be written into the main text of the judgment; where the case is concluded by mediation, the fact that the trademark is well known shall not be affirmed in the mediation document.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本院以前有关司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 14** Where the previous relevant judicial interpretations of this Court are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
