# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕10号  
**公布日期**：2023-10-16  
**生效日期**：2023-11-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定〉的决定》已于2023年10月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1901次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年11月1日起施行。 2023年10月21日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭 若干问题的规定》的决定 法释〔2023〕10号 最高人民法院审判委员会第1901次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定》作如下修改： 一、将第二条修改为：“知识产权法庭审理下列上诉案件： （一）专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计授权确权行政上诉案件； （二）发明专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计权属、侵权民事和行政上诉案件； （三）重大、复杂的实用新型专利、技术秘密、计算机软件权属、侵权民事和行政上诉案件； （四）垄断民事和行政上诉案件。 知识产权法庭审理下列其他案件： （一）前款规定类型的全国范围内重大、复杂的第一审民事和行政案件； （二）对前款规定的第一审民事和行政案件已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书依法申请再审、抗诉、再审等适用审判监督程序的案件；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　最高人民法院设立知识产权法庭，主要审理专利等专业技术性较强的知识产权上诉案件。  
知识产权法庭是最高人民法院派出的常设审判机构，设在北京市。  
知识产权法庭作出的判决、裁定、调解书和决定，是最高人民法院的判决、裁定、调解书和决定。

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court shall establish an Intellectual Property Court, which shall primarily adjudicate appeal cases involving intellectual property with strong technical expertise, such as patents.  
The Intellectual Property Court is a permanent adjudicative body dispatched by the Supreme People's Court and shall be located in Beijing.  
Judgments, rulings, mediation statements, and decisions rendered by the Intellectual Property Court shall be the judgments, rulings, mediation statements, and decisions of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　知识产权法庭审理下列上诉案件：  
（一）专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计授权确权行政上诉案件；  
（二）发明专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计权属、侵权民事和行政上诉案件；  
（三）重大、复杂的实用新型专利、技术秘密、计算机软件权属、侵权民事和行政上诉案件；  
（四）垄断民事和行政上诉案件。  
知识产权法庭审理下列其他案件：  
（一）前款规定类型的全国范围内重大、复杂的第一审民事和行政案件；  
（二）对前款规定的第一审民事和行政案件已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书依法申请再审、抗诉、再审等适用审判监督程序的案件；  
（三）前款规定的第一审民事和行政案件管辖权争议，行为保全裁定申请复议，罚款、拘留决定申请复议，报请延长审限等案件；  
（四）最高人民法院认为应当由知识产权法庭审理的其他案件。

**Article 2** The Intellectual Property Court shall adjudicate the following appeal cases:  
(1) administrative appeal cases concerning the grant and validation of patents, new plant varieties, and layout designs of integrated circuits;  
(2) civil and administrative appeal cases concerning the ownership and infringement of invention patents, new plant varieties, and layout designs of integrated circuits;  
(3) major and complex civil and administrative appeal cases concerning the ownership and infringement of utility model patents, trade secrets, and computer software;  
(4) civil and administrative appeal cases concerning monopoly.  
The Intellectual Property Court shall adjudicate the following other cases:  
(1) first-instance civil and administrative cases of the types specified in the preceding paragraph that are major and complex within the whole country;  
(2) cases applying trial supervision procedures such as applying for retrial, protest, and retrial in accordance with law against legally effective judgments, rulings, and mediation statements of the first-instance civil and administrative cases specified in the preceding paragraph;  
(3) cases concerning jurisdictional disputes over the first-instance civil and administrative cases specified in the preceding paragraph, applications for reconsideration of rulings on act preservation, applications for reconsideration of decisions on fines and detention, and requests for extension of the trial period;  
(4) other cases that the Supreme People's Court considers shall be adjudicated by the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　审理[本规定第二条](#art-2)所称案件的下级人民法院应当按照规定及时向知识产权法庭移送纸质、电子卷宗。

**Article 3** The lower people's courts that adjudicate the cases mentioned in Article 2 of these Provisions shall, in accordance with the provisions, timely transfer the paper and electronic case files to the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　知识产权法庭可以要求当事人披露涉案知识产权相关权属、侵权、授权确权等关联案件情况。当事人拒不如实披露的，可以作为认定其是否遵循诚实信用原则和构成滥用权利等的考量因素。

**Article 4** The Intellectual Property Court may require the parties to disclose information on related cases involving the ownership, infringement, grant, and validation of the intellectual property rights involved in the case. If a party refuses to disclose truthfully, this may serve as a factor to consider in determining whether the party has followed the principle of good faith and constitutes an abuse of rights.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　知识产权法庭可以根据案件情况到实地或者原审人民法院所在地巡回审理案件。

**Article 5** The Intellectual Property Court may, based on the circumstances of the case, conduct circuit adjudication at the actual location or the place where the original trial people's court is located.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　知识产权法庭采取保全等措施，依照执行程序相关规定办理。

**Article 6** The Intellectual Property Court shall handle measures such as preservation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the enforcement procedures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　知识产权法庭审理的案件的立案信息、合议庭组成人员、审判流程、裁判文书等依法公开。

**Article 7** The case filing information, composition of the collegial panel, trial procedures, and adjudicative documents of cases adjudicated by the Intellectual Property Court shall be made public in accordance with law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　知识产权法庭法官会议由庭长、副庭长和若干资深法官组成，讨论重大、疑难、复杂案件等。

**Article 8** The judge committee of the Intellectual Property Court shall be composed of the President, Vice-Presidents, and a number of senior judges, and shall discuss major, difficult, and complex cases, among others.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　知识产权法庭应当加强对有关案件审判工作的调研，及时总结裁判标准和审理规则，指导下级人民法院审判工作。

**Article 9** The Intellectual Property Court shall strengthen research on the adjudication work of relevant cases, timely summarize adjudication standards and trial rules, and guide the adjudication work of lower people's courts.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对知识产权法院、中级人民法院已经发生法律效力的[本规定第二条](#art-2)第一款规定类型的第一审民事和行政案件判决、裁定、调解书，省级人民检察院向高级人民法院提出抗诉的，高级人民法院应当告知其由最高人民检察院依法向最高人民法院提出，并由知识产权法庭审理。

**Article 10** Where a provincial people's procuratorate files a protest with a higher people's court against a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation statement of a first-instance civil or administrative case of the type specified in the first paragraph of Article 2 of these Provisions rendered by an intellectual property court or an intermediate people's court, the higher people's court shall inform it that the Supreme People's Procuratorate shall file the protest to the Supreme People's Court according to law, and the case shall be adjudicated by the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定自2019年1月1日起施行。最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 11** These Provisions shall come into force on January 1, 2019. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
