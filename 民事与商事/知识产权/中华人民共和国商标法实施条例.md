# 中华人民共和国商标法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2014-04-29  
**生效日期**：2014-05-01

> (2002年8月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第358号公布　2014年4月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第651号修订通过　自2014年5月1日起施行)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国商标法》(以下简称商标法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Trademark Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Trademark Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例有关商品商标的规定，适用于服务商标。

**Article 2** The provisions of these Regulations concerning goods trademarks shall apply to service trademarks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　商标持有人依照[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)规定请求驰名商标保护的，应当提交其商标构成驰名商标的证据材料。商标局、商标评审委员会应当依照[商标法第十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-14)的规定，根据审查、处理案件的需要以及当事人提交的证据材料，对其商标驰名情况作出认定。

**Article 3** Where a trademark holder requests protection of a well-known trademark in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of the Trademark Law, it shall submit evidentiary materials that its trademark constitutes a well-known trademark. The Trademark Office and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of the Trademark Law, make a determination on the well-known status of the trademark based on the needs of examination and case handling and the evidentiary materials submitted by the parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[商标法第十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-16)规定的地理标志，可以依照商标法和本条例的规定，作为证明商标或者集体商标申请注册。  
以地理标志作为证明商标注册的，其商品符合使用该地理标志条件的自然人、法人或者其他组织可以要求使用该证明商标，控制该证明商标的组织应当允许。以地理标志作为集体商标注册的，其商品符合使用该地理标志条件的自然人、法人或者其他组织，可以要求参加以该地理标志作为集体商标注册的团体、协会或者其他组织，该团体、协会或者其他组织应当依据其章程接纳为会员；不要求参加以该地理标志作为集体商标注册的团体、协会或者其他组织的，也可以正当使用该地理标志，该团体、协会或者其他组织无权禁止。

**Article 4** Geographical indications as defined in Article 16 of the Trademark Law may, in accordance with the Trademark Law and these Regulations, be applied for registration as certification marks or collective marks.  
Where a geographical indication is registered as a certification mark, any natural person, legal person, or other organization whose goods meet the conditions for using the geographical indication may request to use the certification mark, and the organization that controls the certification mark shall permit such use. Where a geographical indication is registered as a collective mark, any natural person, legal person, or other organization whose goods meet the conditions for using the geographical indication may request to join the group, association, or other organization that registered the geographical indication as a collective mark, and such group, association, or other organization shall admit them as members in accordance with its articles of association; if they do not request to join the group, association, or other organization that registered the geographical indication as a collective mark, they may also use the geographical indication legitimately, and such group, association, or other organization shall have no right to prohibit such use.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人委托商标代理机构申请商标注册或者办理其他商标事宜，应当提交代理委托书。代理委托书应当载明代理内容及权限；外国人或者外国企业的代理委托书还应当载明委托人的国籍。  
外国人或者外国企业的代理委托书及与其有关的证明文件的公证、认证手续，按照对等原则办理。  
申请商标注册或者转让商标，商标注册申请人或者商标转让受让人为外国人或者外国企业的，应当在申请书中指定中国境内接收人负责接收商标局、商标评审委员会后继商标业务的法律文件。商标局、商标评审委员会后继商标业务的法律文件向中国境内接收人送达。  
[商标法第十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-18)所称外国人或者外国企业，是指在中国没有经常居所或者营业所的外国人或者外国企业。

**Article 5** Where a party entrusts a trademark agency to file an application for trademark registration or to handle other trademark matters, it shall submit a power of attorney. The power of attorney shall specify the content and scope of the agency; for a foreign national or foreign enterprise, the power of attorney shall also specify the nationality of the principal.  
The notarization and legalization procedures for the power of attorney and related supporting documents of a foreign national or foreign enterprise shall be handled on the principle of reciprocity.  
Where an applicant for trademark registration or a transferee of a trademark assignment is a foreign national or foreign enterprise, the applicant shall designate a recipient within the territory of China in the application form to receive legal documents from the Trademark Office and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board concerning subsequent trademark matters. The legal documents from the Trademark Office and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board concerning subsequent trademark matters shall be served on the recipient within the territory of China.  
The term "foreign national or foreign enterprise" as used in Article 18 of the Trademark Law refers to a foreign national or foreign enterprise that has no habitual residence or business office in China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请商标注册或者办理其他商标事宜，应当使用中文。  
依照商标法和本条例规定提交的各种证件、证明文件和证据材料是外文的，应当附送中文译文；未附送的，视为未提交该证件、证明文件或者证据材料。

**Article 6** Applications for trademark registration or the handling of other trademark matters shall be in Chinese.  
Where the various certificates, supporting documents, and evidentiary materials submitted in accordance with the Trademark Law and these Regulations are in a foreign language, a Chinese translation shall be attached; if not attached, the certificates, supporting documents, or evidentiary materials shall be deemed not to have been submitted.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　商标局、商标评审委员会工作人员有下列情形之一的，应当回避，当事人或者利害关系人可以要求其回避：  
(一)是当事人或者当事人、代理人的近亲属的；  
(二)与当事人、代理人有其他关系，可能影响公正的；  
(三)与申请商标注册或者办理其他商标事宜有利害关系的。

**Article 7** Where any staff member of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board falls under any of the following circumstances, such staff member shall withdraw, and the parties or interested persons may request their withdrawal:  
(1) being a close relative of a party or an agent of a party;  
(2) having another relationship with a party or an agent that could affect impartiality;  
(3) having an interest in the application for trademark registration or the handling of other trademark matters.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　以[商标法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-22)规定的数据电文方式提交商标注册申请等有关文件，应当按照商标局或者商标评审委员会的规定通过互联网提交。

**Article 8** Where documents related to trademark registration applications are submitted by means of data messages as specified in Article 22 of the Trademark Law, they shall be submitted via the Internet in accordance with the provisions of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　除[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的情形外，当事人向商标局或者商标评审委员会提交文件或者材料的日期，直接递交的，以递交日为准；邮寄的，以寄出的邮戳日为准；邮戳日不清晰或者没有邮戳的，以商标局或者商标评审委员会实际收到日为准，但是当事人能够提出实际邮戳日证据的除外。通过邮政企业以外的快递企业递交的，以快递企业收寄日为准；收寄日不明确的，以商标局或者商标评审委员会实际收到日为准，但是当事人能够提出实际收寄日证据的除外。以数据电文方式提交的，以进入商标局或者商标评审委员会电子系统的日期为准。  
当事人向商标局或者商标评审委员会邮寄文件，应当使用给据邮件。  
当事人向商标局或者商标评审委员会提交文件，以书面方式提交的，以商标局或者商标评审委员会所存档案记录为准；以数据电文方式提交的，以商标局或者商标评审委员会数据库记录为准，但是当事人确有证据证明商标局或者商标评审委员会档案、数据库记录有错误的除外。

**Article 9** Except for the circumstances provided in Article 18 of these Regulations, the date on which a party submits documents or materials to the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall, in the case of direct delivery, be the date of delivery; in the case of mailing, be the date of the postmark; if the postmark date is unclear or there is no postmark, it shall be the date of actual receipt by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, unless the party can provide evidence of the actual postmark date. In the case of delivery through a courier service other than a postal enterprise, it shall be the date of acceptance by the courier service; if the date of acceptance is unclear, it shall be the date of actual receipt by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, unless the party can provide evidence of the actual date of acceptance. In the case of submission by data message, it shall be the date on which it enters the electronic system of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board.  
When a party mails documents to the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, it shall use registered mail.  
When a party submits documents to the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, if submitted in writing, the records kept in the archives of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall prevail; if submitted by data message, the records in the database of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall prevail, unless the party has evidence proving that the archives or database records of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board are erroneous.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　商标局或者商标评审委员会的各种文件，可以通过邮寄、直接递交、数据电文或者其他方式送达当事人；以数据电文方式送达当事人的，应当经当事人同意。当事人委托商标代理机构的，文件送达商标代理机构视为送达当事人。  
商标局或者商标评审委员会向当事人送达各种文件的日期，邮寄的，以当事人收到的邮戳日为准；邮戳日不清晰或者没有邮戳的，自文件发出之日起满15日视为送达当事人，但是当事人能够证明实际收到日的除外；直接递交的，以递交日为准；以数据电文方式送达的，自文件发出之日起满15日视为送达当事人，但是当事人能够证明文件进入其电子系统日期的除外。文件通过上述方式无法送达的，可以通过公告方式送达，自公告发布之日起满30日，该文件视为送达当事人。

**Article 10** Various documents of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board may be served on parties by mail, direct delivery, data message, or other means; if served on a party by data message, the consent of the party shall be obtained. Where a party has entrusted a trademark agency, service of documents on the trademark agency shall be deemed as service on the party.  
The date on which the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board serves various documents on a party shall, in the case of mailing, be the date of the postmark as received by the party; if the postmark date is unclear or there is no postmark, the document shall be deemed served on the party upon the expiration of 15 days from the date of dispatch, unless the party can prove the actual date of receipt; in the case of direct delivery, it shall be the date of delivery; in the case of service by data message, the document shall be deemed served on the party upon the expiration of 15 days from the date of dispatch, unless the party can prove the date on which the document entered its electronic system. If a document cannot be served through the above methods, it may be served by announcement, and the document shall be deemed served on the party upon the expiration of 30 days from the date of publication of the announcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　下列期间不计入商标审查、审理期限：  
(一)商标局、商标评审委员会文件公告送达的期间；  
(二)当事人需要补充证据或者补正文件的期间以及因当事人更换需要重新答辩的期间；  
(三)同日申请提交使用证据及协商、抽签需要的期间；  
(四)需要等待优先权确定的期间；  
(五)审查、审理过程中，依案件申请人的请求等待在先权利案件审理结果的期间。

**Article 11** The following periods shall not be counted in the time limit for trademark examination and adjudication:  
(1) the period for service of documents by announcement by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board;  
(2) the period for a party to supplement evidence or correct documents, and the period for a renewed defense due to the replacement of a party;  
(3) the period needed for submitting evidence of use, consultation, and drawing lots in the case of applications filed on the same day;  
(4) the period needed for waiting for the determination of priority;  
(5) the period for waiting for the result of the adjudication of a prior right case at the request of the applicant during examination or adjudication.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　除本条第二款规定的情形外，商标法和本条例规定的各种期限开始的当日不计算在期限内。期限以年或者月计算的，以期限最后一月的相应日为期限届满日；该月无相应日的，以该月最后一日为期限届满日；期限届满日是节假日的，以节假日后的第一个工作日为期限届满日。  
[商标法第三十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-39)、第四十条规定的注册商标有效期从法定日开始起算，期限最后一月相应日的前一日为期限届满日，该月无相应日的，以该月最后一日为期限届满日。

**Article 12** Except for the circumstances provided in paragraph 2 of this Article, the day on which various time limits prescribed in the Trademark Law and these Regulations begin shall not be counted within the time limit. Where a time limit is calculated by year or month, the corresponding day of the last month of the time limit shall be the expiry date; if there is no corresponding day in that month, the last day of that month shall be the expiry date; if the expiry date falls on a holiday, the first working day after the holiday shall be the expiry date.  
The validity period of a registered trademark as provided in Articles 39 and 40 of the Trademark Law shall commence from the statutory date, and the day preceding the corresponding day of the last month of the period shall be the expiry date; if there is no corresponding day in that month, the last day of that month shall be the expiry date.

### 第二章　商标注册的申请

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请商标注册，应当按照公布的商品和服务分类表填报。每一件商标注册申请应当向商标局提交《商标注册申请书》1份、商标图样1份；以颜色组合或者着色图样申请商标注册的，应当提交着色图样，并提交黑白稿1份；不指定颜色的，应当提交黑白图样。  
商标图样应当清晰，便于粘贴，用光洁耐用的纸张印制或者用照片代替，长和宽应当不大于10厘米，不小于5厘米。  
以三维标志申请商标注册的，应当在申请书中予以声明，说明商标的使用方式，并提交能够确定三维形状的图样，提交的商标图样应当至少包含三面视图。  
以颜色组合申请商标注册的，应当在申请书中予以声明，说明商标的使用方式。  
以声音标志申请商标注册的，应当在申请书中予以声明，提交符合要求的声音样本，对申请注册的声音商标进行描述，说明商标的使用方式。对声音商标进行描述，应当以五线谱或者简谱对申请用作商标的声音加以描述并附加文字说明；无法以五线谱或者简谱描述的，应当以文字加以描述；商标描述与声音样本应当一致。  
申请注册集体商标、证明商标的，应当在申请书中予以声明，并提交主体资格证明文件和使用管理规则。  
商标为外文或者包含外文的，应当说明含义。

**Article 13** Applications for trademark registration shall be filled in accordance with the published classification of goods and services. For each application for trademark registration, one Application for Trademark Registration and one trademark sample shall be submitted to the Trademark Office; if the application is for a color combination or a colored design, a colored design shall be submitted, together with one black-and-white draft; if no color is specified, a black-and-white design shall be submitted.  
The trademark sample shall be clear, easy to affix, printed on smooth and durable paper or replaced by a photograph, and its length and width shall not exceed 10 cm and shall not be less than 5 cm.  
Where an application for trademark registration is filed with a three-dimensional mark, a declaration shall be made in the application, the manner of use of the mark shall be stated, and a design that can determine the three-dimensional shape shall be submitted; the submitted design shall include at least three views.  
Where an application for trademark registration is filed with a color combination, a declaration shall be made in the application, and the manner of use of the mark shall be stated.  
Where an application for trademark registration is filed with a sound mark, a declaration shall be made in the application, a sound sample meeting the requirements shall be submitted, the sound trademark applied for registration shall be described, and the manner of use of the mark shall be stated. The description of the sound trademark shall be made by using staff notation or numbered musical notation to describe the sound to be used as a trademark, together with a written explanation; if staff notation or numbered musical notation cannot be used, a written description shall be provided; the description of the trademark shall be consistent with the sound sample.  
Where an application for registration of a collective mark or a certification mark is filed, a declaration shall be made in the application, and documents certifying the eligibility of the applicant and the rules governing the use and management shall be submitted.  
If the trademark is in a foreign language or contains a foreign language, its meaning shall be explained.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请商标注册的，申请人应当提交其身份证明文件。商标注册申请人的名义与所提交的证明文件应当一致。  
前款关于申请人提交其身份证明文件的规定适用于向商标局提出的办理变更、转让、续展、异议、撤销等其他商标事宜。

**Article 14** An applicant for trademark registration shall submit its identity certification documents. The name of the applicant for trademark registration shall be consistent with the submitted certification documents.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph concerning an applicant's submission of its identity certification documents shall apply to other trademark matters such as applications to the Trademark Office for change, assignment, renewal, opposition, or cancellation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　商品或者服务项目名称应当按照商品和服务分类表中的类别号、名称填写；商品或者服务项目名称未列入商品和服务分类表的，应当附送对该商品或者服务的说明。  
商标注册申请等有关文件以纸质方式提出的，应当打字或者印刷。  
本条第二款规定适用于办理其他商标事宜。

**Article 15** The names of goods or service items shall be filled in according to the class numbers and names in the classification of goods and services; if a name of a goods or service item is not listed in the classification of goods and services, a description of the goods or service shall be attached.  
If documents such as applications for trademark registration are submitted in paper form, they shall be typed or printed.  
The provisions of paragraph 2 of this Article shall apply to the handling of other trademark matters.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　共同申请注册同一商标或者办理其他共有商标事宜的，应当在申请书中指定一个代表人；没有指定代表人的，以申请书中顺序排列的第一人为代表人。  
商标局和商标评审委员会的文件应当送达代表人。

**Article 16** Where two or more persons jointly apply for registration of the same trademark or handle other matters concerning a jointly owned trademark, a representative shall be designated in the application; if no representative is designated, the first person listed in the application shall be the representative.  
Documents of the Trademark Office and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall be served on the representative.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请人变更其名义、地址、代理人、文件接收人或者删减指定的商品的，应当向商标局办理变更手续。  
申请人转让其商标注册申请的，应当向商标局办理转让手续。

**Article 17** Where an applicant changes its name, address, agent, document recipient, or deletes designated goods, it shall go through the change formalities with the Trademark Office.  
Where an applicant assigns its trademark registration application, it shall go through the assignment formalities with the Trademark Office.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　商标注册的申请日期以商标局收到申请文件的日期为准。  
商标注册申请手续齐备、按照规定填写申请文件并缴纳费用的，商标局予以受理并书面通知申请人；申请手续不齐备、未按照规定填写申请文件或者未缴纳费用的，商标局不予受理，书面通知申请人并说明理由。申请手续基本齐备或者申请文件基本符合规定，但是需要补正的，商标局通知申请人予以补正，限其自收到通知之日起30日内，按照指定内容补正并交回商标局。在规定期限内补正并交回商标局的，保留申请日期；期满未补正的或者不按照要求进行补正的，商标局不予受理并书面通知申请人。  
本条第二款关于受理条件的规定适用于办理其他商标事宜。

**Article 18** The date of filing of an application for trademark registration shall be the date on which the Trademark Office receives the application documents.  
Where the application formalities for trademark registration are complete, the application documents are filled in according to the provisions, and the fees are paid, the Trademark Office shall accept the application and notify the applicant in writing; where the application formalities are incomplete, the application documents are not filled in according to the provisions, or the fees are not paid, the Trademark Office shall not accept the application, shall notify the applicant in writing, and shall state the reasons. Where the application formalities are substantially complete or the application documents substantially comply with the provisions but need correction, the Trademark Office shall notify the applicant to make corrections, and the applicant shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice, make corrections according to the specified content and return the corrected documents to the Trademark Office. If the corrections are made and returned within the specified time limit, the date of filing shall be retained; if no correction is made upon expiration of the time limit or the correction is not made as required, the Trademark Office shall not accept the application and shall notify the applicant in writing.  
The provisions of paragraph 2 of this Article concerning the conditions for acceptance shall apply to the handling of other trademark matters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　两个或者两个以上的申请人，在同一种商品或者类似商品上，分别以相同或者近似的商标在同一天申请注册的，各申请人应当自收到商标局通知之日起30日内提交其申请注册前在先使用该商标的证据。同日使用或者均未使用的，各申请人可以自收到商标局通知之日起30日内自行协商，并将书面协议报送商标局；不愿协商或者协商不成的，商标局通知各申请人以抽签的方式确定一个申请人，驳回其他人的注册申请。商标局已经通知但申请人未参加抽签的，视为放弃申请，商标局应当书面通知未参加抽签的申请人。

**Article 19** Where two or more applicants apply for registration of identical or similar trademarks for the same or similar goods on the same day, each applicant shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Trademark Office, submit evidence of prior use of the trademark before the filing of the application. If all applicants used the trademark on the same day or none used it, the applicants may, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Trademark Office, negotiate among themselves, and submit a written agreement to the Trademark Office; if they are unwilling to negotiate or fail to reach an agreement, the Trademark Office shall notify each applicant to determine one applicant by drawing lots, and shall reject the registration applications of the others. If the Trademark Office has notified but an applicant does not participate in the drawing of lots, it shall be deemed to have abandoned the application, and the Trademark Office shall notify in writing the applicant that did not participate in the drawing of lots.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　依照[商标法第二十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-25)规定要求优先权的，申请人提交的第一次提出商标注册申请文件的副本应当经受理该申请的商标主管机关证明，并注明申请日期和申请号。

**Article 20** Where priority is claimed in accordance with Article 25 of the Trademark Law, the copy of the documents of the first application for trademark registration submitted by the applicant shall be certified by the trademark authority that accepted the application, and shall indicate the date of filing and the application number.

### 第三章　商标注册申请的审查

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　商标局对受理的商标注册申请，依照商标法及本条例的有关规定进行审查，对符合规定或者在部分指定商品上使用商标的注册申请符合规定的，予以初步审定，并予以公告；对不符合规定或者在部分指定商品上使用商标的注册申请不符合规定的，予以驳回或者驳回在部分指定商品上使用商标的注册申请，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 21** The Trademark Office shall, in accordance with the Trademark Law and the relevant provisions of these Regulations, examine trademark registration applications it accepts. For an application that conforms to the provisions, or for an application for the use of a trademark on some of the designated goods that conforms to the provisions, it shall make a preliminary approval and announce it; for an application that does not conform to the provisions, or for an application for the use of a trademark on some of the designated goods that does not conform to the provisions, it shall reject the application or reject the application for the use of a trademark on some of the designated goods, and notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　商标局对一件商标注册申请在部分指定商品上予以驳回的，申请人可以将该申请中初步审定的部分申请分割成另一件申请，分割后的申请保留原申请的申请日期。  
需要分割的，申请人应当自收到商标局《商标注册申请部分驳回通知书》之日起15日内，向商标局提出分割申请。  
商标局收到分割申请后，应当将原申请分割为两件，对分割出来的初步审定申请生成新的申请号，并予以公告。

**Article 22** Where the Trademark Office rejects a trademark registration application with respect to some of the designated goods, the applicant may divide the part of the application that has been preliminarily approved into another application, and the divided application shall retain the filing date of the original application.  
Where division is necessary, the applicant shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the "Notice of Partial Rejection of Trademark Registration Application" from the Trademark Office, submit a division application to the Trademark Office.  
Upon receiving the division application, the Trademark Office shall divide the original application into two applications, generate a new application number for the divided application that has been preliminarily approved, and announce it.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　依照[商标法第二十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-29)规定，商标局认为对商标注册申请内容需要说明或者修正的，申请人应当自收到商标局通知之日起15日内作出说明或者修正。

**Article 23** Where, pursuant to Article 29 of the Trademark Law, the Trademark Office considers that explanations or amendments are needed for the content of a trademark registration application, the applicant shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice from the Trademark Office, make explanations or amendments.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对商标局初步审定予以公告的商标提出异议的，异议人应当向商标局提交下列商标异议材料一式两份并标明正、副本：  
(一)商标异议申请书；  
(二)异议人的身份证明；  
(三)以违反[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)第二款和第三款、第十五条、第十六条第一款、第三十条、第三十一条、第三十二条规定为由提出异议的，异议人作为在先权利人或者利害关系人的证明。  
商标异议申请书应当有明确的请求和事实依据，并附送有关证据材料。

**Article 24** Where an opposition is filed against a trademark that has been preliminarily approved and announced by the Trademark Office, the opponent shall submit to the Trademark Office the following trademark opposition materials in duplicate, indicating the original and the copy:  
(1) the trademark opposition application;  
(2) the identity certificate of the opponent;  
(3) where the opposition is filed on the ground of violation of Article 13, paragraphs 2 and 3, Article 15, Article 16, paragraph 1, Article 30, Article 31, and Article 32 of the Trademark Law, the certificate that the opponent is a prior right holder or an interested party.  
The trademark opposition application shall contain clear claims and factual basis, and shall be accompanied by relevant evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　商标局收到商标异议申请书后，经审查，符合受理条件的，予以受理，向申请人发出受理通知书。

**Article 25** After receiving the trademark opposition application, the Trademark Office shall, upon examination, accept it if it meets the acceptance conditions, and issue a notice of acceptance to the opponent.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　商标异议申请有下列情形的，商标局不予受理，书面通知申请人并说明理由：  
(一)未在法定期限内提出的；  
(二)申请人主体资格、异议理由不符合[商标法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-33)规定的；  
(三)无明确的异议理由、事实和法律依据的；  
(四)同一异议人以相同的理由、事实和法律依据针对同一商标再次提出异议申请的。

**Article 26** Where a trademark opposition application falls under any of the following circumstances, the Trademark Office shall not accept it, notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons:  
(1) it is not filed within the statutory time limit;  
(2) the applicant's qualifications or the opposition grounds do not conform to the provisions of Article 33 of the Trademark Law;  
(3) there are no clear opposition grounds, facts, or legal basis;  
(4) the same opponent files another opposition application against the same trademark with the same grounds, facts, and legal basis.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　商标局应当将商标异议材料副本及时送交被异议人，限其自收到商标异议材料副本之日起30日内答辩。被异议人不答辩的，不影响商标局作出决定。  
当事人需要在提出异议申请或者答辩后补充有关证据材料的，应当在商标异议申请书或者答辩书中声明，并自提交商标异议申请书或者答辩书之日起3个月内提交；期满未提交的，视为当事人放弃补充有关证据材料。但是，在期满后生成或者当事人有其他正当理由未能在期满前提交的证据，在期满后提交的，商标局将证据交对方当事人并质证后可以采信。

**Article 27** The Trademark Office shall promptly send a copy of the trademark opposition materials to the opposed party, and require it to file a defense within 30 days from the date of receipt of the copy of the trademark opposition materials. Failure of the opposed party to file a defense shall not affect the Trademark Office's decision.  
Where a party needs to supplement relevant evidentiary materials after filing the opposition application or the defense, it shall state such intention in the trademark opposition application or the defense, and submit the materials within 3 months from the date of submission of the trademark opposition application or the defense; if not submitted within the time limit, the party shall be deemed to have abandoned the supplementation of relevant evidentiary materials. However, if evidence is generated after the expiration of the time limit, or if the party has other legitimate reasons for failing to submit it before the expiration, and it is submitted after the expiration, the Trademark Office may, after providing the evidence to the other party and cross-examining, admit it.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　[商标法第三十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-35)第三款和第三十六条第一款所称不予注册决定，包括在部分指定商品上不予注册决定。  
被异议商标在商标局作出准予注册决定或者不予注册决定前已经刊发注册公告的，撤销该注册公告。经审查异议不成立而准予注册的，在准予注册决定生效后重新公告。

**Article 28** The decision of refusal of registration referred to in Article 35, paragraph 3 and Article 36, paragraph 1 of the Trademark Law includes a decision of refusal of registration with respect to some of the designated goods.  
Where the opposed trademark has already been published in a registration announcement before the Trademark Office makes a decision to approve registration or refuse registration, such registration announcement shall be revoked. Where the opposition is found unjustified and registration is approved, a new announcement shall be made after the decision to approve registration takes effect.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　商标注册申请人或者商标注册人依照[商标法第三十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-38)规定提出更正申请的，应当向商标局提交更正申请书。符合更正条件的，商标局核准后更正相关内容；不符合更正条件的，商标局不予核准，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
已经刊发初步审定公告或者注册公告的商标经更正的，刊发更正公告。

**Article 29** Where a trademark registration applicant or a trademark registrant files a correction application pursuant to Article 38 of the Trademark Law, it shall submit a correction application to the Trademark Office. If the correction conditions are met, the Trademark Office shall, after approval, correct the relevant content; if the correction conditions are not met, the Trademark Office shall not approve it, and shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Where a trademark that has already been published in a preliminary approval announcement or a registration announcement is corrected, a correction announcement shall be published.

### 第四章　注册商标的变更、转让、续展

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　变更商标注册人名义、地址或者其他注册事项的，应当向商标局提交变更申请书。变更商标注册人名义的，还应当提交有关登记机关出具的变更证明文件。商标局核准的，发给商标注册人相应证明，并予以公告；不予核准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
变更商标注册人名义或者地址的，商标注册人应当将其全部注册商标一并变更；未一并变更的，由商标局通知其限期改正；期满未改正的，视为放弃变更申请，商标局应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 30** Where a change is made to the name, address, or other registration matters of a trademark registrant, an application for change shall be submitted to the Trademark Office. Where the name of the trademark registrant is changed, a change certificate issued by the relevant registration authority shall also be submitted. If the Trademark Office approves the change, it shall issue the corresponding certificate to the trademark registrant and announce it; if it does not approve, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Where the name or address of a trademark registrant is changed, the trademark registrant shall change all its registered trademarks together; if not changed together, the Trademark Office shall notify it to rectify within a time limit; if it fails to rectify upon expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed to have abandoned the change application, and the Trademark Office shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　转让注册商标的，转让人和受让人应当向商标局提交转让注册商标申请书。转让注册商标申请手续应当由转让人和受让人共同办理。商标局核准转让注册商标申请的，发给受让人相应证明，并予以公告。  
转让注册商标，商标注册人对其在同一种或者类似商品上注册的相同或者近似的商标未一并转让的，由商标局通知其限期改正；期满未改正的，视为放弃转让该注册商标的申请，商标局应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 31** Where a registered trademark is assigned, the assignor and the assignee shall submit an application for assignment of a registered trademark to the Trademark Office. The formalities for the application for assignment of a registered trademark shall be handled jointly by the assignor and the assignee. If the Trademark Office approves the application for assignment of a registered trademark, it shall issue the corresponding certificate to the assignee and announce it.  
Where a registered trademark is assigned, and the trademark registrant has not assigned together the identical or similar trademarks registered on the same or similar goods, the Trademark Office shall notify it to rectify within a time limit; if it fails to rectify upon expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed to have abandoned the application for assignment of the registered trademark, and the Trademark Office shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　注册商标专用权因转让以外的继承等其他事由发生移转的，接受该注册商标专用权的当事人应当凭有关证明文件或者法律文书到商标局办理注册商标专用权移转手续。  
注册商标专用权移转的，注册商标专用权人在同一种或者类似商品上注册的相同或者近似的商标，应当一并移转；未一并移转的，由商标局通知其限期改正；期满未改正的，视为放弃该移转注册商标的申请，商标局应当书面通知申请人。  
商标移转申请经核准的，予以公告。接受该注册商标专用权移转的当事人自公告之日起享有商标专用权。

**Article 32** Where the exclusive right to use a registered trademark is transferred due to inheritance or other reasons other than assignment, the party that accepts the exclusive right to use the registered trademark shall, on the basis of relevant certification documents or legal instruments, go through the formalities for the transfer of the exclusive right to use the registered trademark at the Trademark Office.  
Where the exclusive right to use a registered trademark is transferred, the identical or similar trademarks registered on the same or similar goods by the owner of the exclusive right to use the registered trademark shall be transferred together; if not transferred together, the Trademark Office shall notify it to rectify within a time limit; if it fails to rectify upon expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed to have abandoned the application for transfer of the registered trademark, and the Trademark Office shall notify the applicant in writing.  
Where the application for trademark transfer is approved, it shall be announced. The party that accepts the transfer of the exclusive right to use the registered trademark shall enjoy the exclusive right to use the trademark from the date of announcement.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　注册商标需要续展注册的，应当向商标局提交商标续展注册申请书。商标局核准商标注册续展申请的，发给相应证明并予以公告。

**Article 33** Where the registration of a registered trademark needs to be renewed, an application for renewal of trademark registration shall be submitted to the Trademark Office. If the Trademark Office approves the application for renewal of trademark registration, it shall issue the corresponding certificate and announce it.

### 第五章　商标国际注册

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　[商标法第二十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-21)规定的商标国际注册，是指根据《商标国际注册马德里协定》(以下简称马德里协定)、《商标国际注册马德里协定有关议定书》(以下简称马德里议定书)及《商标国际注册马德里协定及该协定有关议定书的共同实施细则》的规定办理的马德里商标国际注册。  
马德里商标国际注册申请包括以中国为原属国的商标国际注册申请、指定中国的领土延伸申请及其他有关的申请。

**Article 34** The international registration of trademarks as provided for in Article 21 of the Trademark Law means the Madrid international registration of trademarks handled in accordance with the Madrid Agreement Concerning the International Registration of Marks (hereinafter referred to as the Madrid Agreement), the Protocol Relating to the Madrid Agreement Concerning the International Registration of Marks (hereinafter referred to as the Madrid Protocol), and the Common Regulations under the Madrid Agreement Concerning the International Registration of Marks and the Protocol Relating to that Agreement.  
Applications for Madrid international registration of trademarks include applications for international registration of trademarks with China as the country of origin, territorial extension applications designating China, and other related applications.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　以中国为原属国申请商标国际注册的，应当在中国设有真实有效的营业所，或者在中国有住所，或者拥有中国国籍。

**Article 35** An applicant that applies for international registration of a trademark with China as the country of origin shall have a real and effective business establishment in China, or have a domicile in China, or possess Chinese nationality.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　符合[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)规定的申请人，其商标已在商标局获得注册的，可以根据马德里协定申请办理该商标的国际注册。  
符合[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)规定的申请人，其商标已在商标局获得注册，或者已向商标局提出商标注册申请并被受理的，可以根据马德里议定书申请办理该商标的国际注册。

**Article 36** An applicant that meets the requirements of Article 35 of these Regulations and whose trademark has been registered with the Trademark Office may, in accordance with the Madrid Agreement, apply for international registration of the trademark.  
An applicant that meets the requirements of Article 35 of these Regulations and whose trademark has been registered with the Trademark Office, or has filed a trademark registration application with the Trademark Office and it has been accepted, may, in accordance with the Madrid Protocol, apply for international registration of the trademark.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　以中国为原属国申请商标国际注册的，应当通过商标局向世界知识产权组织国际局(以下简称国际局)申请办理。  
以中国为原属国的，与马德里协定有关的商标国际注册的后期指定、放弃、注销，应当通过商标局向国际局申请办理；与马德里协定有关的商标国际注册的转让、删减、变更、续展，可以通过商标局向国际局申请办理，也可以直接向国际局申请办理。  
以中国为原属国的，与马德里议定书有关的商标国际注册的后期指定、转让、删减、放弃、注销、变更、续展，可以通过商标局向国际局申请办理，也可以直接向国际局申请办理。

**Article 37** Where an applicant applies for international registration of a trademark with China as the country of origin, it shall file the application through the Trademark Office with the International Bureau of the World Intellectual Property Organization (hereinafter referred to as the International Bureau).  
Where China is the country of origin, subsequent designations, renunciations, and cancellations of international registrations of trademarks related to the Madrid Agreement shall be filed through the Trademark Office with the International Bureau; assignments, limitations, changes, and renewals of international registrations of trademarks related to the Madrid Agreement may be filed through the Trademark Office with the International Bureau, or may be filed directly with the International Bureau.  
Where China is the country of origin, subsequent designations, assignments, limitations, renunciations, cancellations, changes, and renewals of international registrations of trademarks related to the Madrid Protocol may be filed through the Trademark Office with the International Bureau, or may be filed directly with the International Bureau.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　通过商标局向国际局申请商标国际注册及办理其他有关申请的，应当提交符合国际局和商标局要求的申请书和相关材料。

**Article 38** Where an applicant files an application for international registration of a trademark with the International Bureau through the Trademark Office or handles other related applications, it shall submit the application form and relevant materials that meet the requirements of the International Bureau and the Trademark Office.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　商标国际注册申请指定的商品或者服务不得超出国内基础申请或者基础注册的商品或者服务的范围。

**Article 39** The goods or services designated in an application for international registration of a trademark shall not exceed the scope of the goods or services in the domestic basic application or basic registration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　商标国际注册申请手续不齐备或者未按照规定填写申请书的，商标局不予受理，申请日不予保留。  
申请手续基本齐备或者申请书基本符合规定，但需要补正的，申请人应当自收到补正通知书之日起30日内予以补正，逾期未补正的，商标局不予受理，书面通知申请人。

**Article 40** Where the formalities for an application for international registration of a trademark are incomplete or the application form is not filled in accordance with the provisions, the Trademark Office shall not accept it, and the filing date shall not be retained.  
Where the formalities are basically complete or the application form basically conforms to the provisions but requires rectification, the applicant shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice of rectification, make rectification; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the Trademark Office shall not accept it, and shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　通过商标局向国际局申请商标国际注册及办理其他有关申请的，应当按照规定缴纳费用。  
申请人应当自收到商标局缴费通知单之日起15日内，向商标局缴纳费用。期满未缴纳的，商标局不受理其申请，书面通知申请人。

**Article 41** An applicant that files an application for international trademark registration with the International Bureau through the Trademark Office, or handles other related applications, shall pay fees in accordance with the provisions.  
The applicant shall pay the fees to the Trademark Office within 15 days from the date of receipt of the payment notice from the Trademark Office. If the fees are not paid upon expiration of the time limit, the Trademark Office shall not accept the application and shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　商标局在马德里协定或者马德里议定书规定的驳回期限(以下简称驳回期限)内，依照商标法和本条例的有关规定对指定中国的领土延伸申请进行审查，作出决定，并通知国际局。商标局在驳回期限内未发出驳回或者部分驳回通知的，该领土延伸申请视为核准。

**Article 42** Within the time limit for refusal prescribed by the Madrid Agreement or the Madrid Protocol (hereinafter referred to as the time limit for refusal), the Trademark Office shall, in accordance with the Trademark Law and the relevant provisions of these Regulations, examine the application for territorial extension designating China, make a decision, and notify the International Bureau. If the Trademark Office does not issue a notice of refusal or partial refusal within the time limit for refusal, the application for territorial extension shall be deemed to have been approved.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　指定中国的领土延伸申请人，要求将三维标志、颜色组合、声音标志作为商标保护或者要求保护集体商标、证明商标的，自该商标在国际局国际注册簿登记之日起3个月内，应当通过依法设立的商标代理机构，向商标局提交[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定的相关材料。未在上述期限内提交相关材料的，商标局驳回该领土延伸申请。

**Article 43** Where an applicant for territorial extension designating China requests the protection of a three-dimensional mark, a combination of colors, or a sound mark as a trademark, or requests the protection of a collective mark or certification mark, the applicant shall, within three months from the date of registration of the mark in the International Register of the International Bureau, submit the relevant materials prescribed in Article 13 of these Regulations to the Trademark Office through a trademark agency established according to law. If the relevant materials are not submitted within the above-mentioned time limit, the Trademark Office shall reject the application for territorial extension.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　世界知识产权组织对商标国际注册有关事项进行公告，商标局不再另行公告。

**Article 44** The World Intellectual Property Organization shall publish matters concerning international trademark registration, and the Trademark Office shall not make a separate publication.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对指定中国的领土延伸申请，自世界知识产权组织《国际商标公告》出版的次月1日起3个月内，符合[商标法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-33)规定条件的异议人可以向商标局提出异议申请。  
商标局在驳回期限内将异议申请的有关情况以驳回决定的形式通知国际局。  
被异议人可以自收到国际局转发的驳回通知书之日起30日内进行答辩，答辩书及相关证据材料应当通过依法设立的商标代理机构向商标局提交。

**Article 45** For an application for territorial extension designating China, an opponent who meets the conditions prescribed in Article 33 of the Trademark Law may file an opposition application with the Trademark Office within three months from the first day of the month following the publication of the International Trademark Bulletin by the World Intellectual Property Organization.  
The Trademark Office shall, within the time limit for refusal, notify the International Bureau of the relevant information about the opposition application in the form of a refusal decision.  
The opposed party may file a defense within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice of refusal forwarded by the International Bureau. The defense and relevant evidence materials shall be submitted to the Trademark Office through a trademark agency established according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在中国获得保护的国际注册商标，有效期自国际注册日或者后期指定日起算。在有效期届满前，注册人可以向国际局申请续展，在有效期内未申请续展的，可以给予6个月的宽展期。商标局收到国际局的续展通知后，依法进行审查。国际局通知未续展的，注销该国际注册商标。

**Article 46** For an internationally registered trademark that has obtained protection in China, the term of validity shall be calculated from the date of international registration or the date of subsequent designation. Before the expiration of the term of validity, the registrant may apply to the International Bureau for renewal. If no application for renewal is filed within the term of validity, a grace period of six months may be granted. Upon receipt of the renewal notice from the International Bureau, the Trademark Office shall conduct examination according to law. If the International Bureau notifies that the renewal has not been made, the international trademark registration shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　指定中国的领土延伸申请办理转让的，受让人应当在缔约方境内有真实有效的营业所，或者在缔约方境内有住所，或者是缔约方国民。  
转让人未将其在相同或者类似商品或者服务上的相同或者近似商标一并转让的，商标局通知注册人自发出通知之日起3个月内改正；期满未改正或者转让容易引起混淆或者有其他不良影响的，商标局作出该转让在中国无效的决定，并向国际局作出声明。

**Article 47** Where a transfer is effected for an application for territorial extension designating China, the assignee shall have a real and effective establishment in the territory of a Contracting Party, or have a domicile in the territory of a Contracting Party, or be a national of a Contracting Party.  
If the assignor has not assigned the identical or similar trademark in respect of identical or similar goods or services at the same time, the Trademark Office shall notify the registrant to make corrections within three months from the date of issuance of the notice; if no correction is made upon expiration of the time limit, or the transfer is likely to cause confusion or has other adverse effects, the Trademark Office shall make a decision that the transfer is invalid in China and declare it to the International Bureau.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　指定中国的领土延伸申请办理删减，删减后的商品或者服务不符合中国有关商品或者服务分类要求或者超出原指定商品或者服务范围的，商标局作出该删减在中国无效的决定，并向国际局作出声明。

**Article 48** Where a limitation is effected for an application for territorial extension designating China, if the goods or services after limitation do not meet the classification requirements for goods or services in China or exceed the scope of the original designated goods or services, the Trademark Office shall make a decision that such limitation is invalid in China and declare it to the International Bureau.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　依照[商标法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-49)第二款规定申请撤销国际注册商标，应当自该商标国际注册申请的驳回期限届满之日起满3年后向商标局提出申请；驳回期限届满时仍处在驳回复审或者异议相关程序的，应当自商标局或者商标评审委员会作出的准予注册决定生效之日起满3年后向商标局提出申请。  
依照[商标法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-44)第一款规定申请宣告国际注册商标无效的，应当自该商标国际注册申请的驳回期限届满后向商标评审委员会提出申请；驳回期限届满时仍处在驳回复审或者异议相关程序的，应当自商标局或者商标评审委员会作出的准予注册决定生效后向商标评审委员会提出申请。  
依照[商标法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-45)第一款规定申请宣告国际注册商标无效的，应当自该商标国际注册申请的驳回期限届满之日起5年内向商标评审委员会提出申请；驳回期限届满时仍处在驳回复审或者异议相关程序的，应当自商标局或者商标评审委员会作出的准予注册决定生效之日起5年内向商标评审委员会提出申请。对恶意注册的，驰名商标所有人不受5年的时间限制。

**Article 49** An application for cancellation of an internationally registered trademark under the second paragraph of Article 49 of the Trademark Law shall be filed with the Trademark Office after three years from the expiration of the time limit for refusal of the international trademark registration application; if the application is still in the review upon refusal or opposition-related procedures at the expiration of the time limit for refusal, the application shall be filed with the Trademark Office after three years from the date when the decision on approval of registration made by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board takes effect.  
An application for a declaration of invalidity of an internationally registered trademark under the first paragraph of Article 44 of the Trademark Law shall be filed with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board after the expiration of the time limit for refusal of the international trademark registration application; if the application is still in the review upon refusal or opposition-related procedures at the expiration of the time limit for refusal, the application shall be filed with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board after the date when the decision on approval of registration made by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board takes effect.  
An application for a declaration of invalidity of an internationally registered trademark under the first paragraph of Article 45 of the Trademark Law shall be filed with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board within five years from the expiration of the time limit for refusal of the international trademark registration application; if the application is still in the review upon refusal or opposition-related procedures at the expiration of the time limit for refusal, the application shall be filed with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board within five years from the date when the decision on approval of registration made by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board takes effect. For malicious registration, the owner of a well-known trademark shall not be subject to the five-year time limit.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　商标法和本条例下列条款的规定不适用于办理商标国际注册相关事宜：  
(一)[商标法第二十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-28)、第三十五条第一款关于审查和审理期限的规定；  
(二)[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)、第三十条第二款；  
(三)[商标法第四十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-42)及[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)关于商标转让由转让人和受让人共同申请并办理手续的规定。

**Article 50** The provisions of the following articles of the Trademark Law and these Regulations shall not apply to matters concerning international trademark registration:  
(1) The provisions of Article 28 and the first paragraph of Article 35 of the Trademark Law concerning the time limits for examination and adjudication;  
(2) Article 22 and the second paragraph of Article 30 of these Regulations;  
(3) The provisions of Article 42 of the Trademark Law and Article 31 of these Regulations concerning the joint application and formalities for trademark transfer by the assignor and assignee.

### 第六章　商标评审

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　商标评审是指商标评审委员会依照[商标法第三十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-34)、第三十五条、第四十四条、第四十五条、第五十四条的规定审理有关商标争议事宜。当事人向商标评审委员会提出商标评审申请，应当有明确的请求、事实、理由和法律依据，并提供相应证据。  
商标评审委员会根据事实，依法进行评审。

**Article 51** Trademark review and adjudication refers to the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board's adjudication of relevant trademark disputes in accordance with Articles 34, 35, 44, 45, and 54 of the Trademark Law. A party that files an application for trademark review and adjudication with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall have clear claims, facts, reasons, and legal basis, and shall provide corresponding evidence.  
The Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall conduct the review and adjudication according to law based on the facts.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　商标评审委员会审理不服商标局驳回商标注册申请决定的复审案件，应当针对商标局的驳回决定和申请人申请复审的事实、理由、请求及评审时的事实状态进行审理。  
商标评审委员会审理不服商标局驳回商标注册申请决定的复审案件，发现申请注册的商标有违反[商标法第十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-10)、第十一条、第十二条和第十六条第一款规定情形，商标局并未依据上述条款作出驳回决定的，可以依据上述条款作出驳回申请的复审决定。商标评审委员会作出复审决定前应当听取申请人的意见。

**Article 52** When adjudicating a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to reject a trademark registration application, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall adjudicate with respect to the Trademark Office's rejection decision and the facts, reasons, claims, and the factual status at the time of review presented by the applicant in the review application.  
Where, during the adjudication of a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to reject a trademark registration application, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board finds that the trademark applied for registration violates the provisions of Articles 10, 11, 12, and the first paragraph of Article 16 of the Trademark Law, and the Trademark Office did not make a rejection decision based on the above provisions, it may make a review decision to reject the application based on the above provisions. Before making a review decision, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall hear the opinions of the applicant.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　商标评审委员会审理不服商标局不予注册决定的复审案件，应当针对商标局的不予注册决定和申请人申请复审的事实、理由、请求及原异议人提出的意见进行审理。  
商标评审委员会审理不服商标局不予注册决定的复审案件，应当通知原异议人参加并提出意见。原异议人的意见对案件审理结果有实质影响的，可以作为评审的依据；原异议人不参加或者不提出意见的，不影响案件的审理。

**Article 53** When adjudicating a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to refuse registration, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall adjudicate with respect to the Trademark Office's decision to refuse registration and the facts, reasons, claims presented by the applicant in the review application, as well as the opinions raised by the original opponent.  
When adjudicating a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to refuse registration, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall notify the original opponent to participate and raise opinions. If the opinions of the original opponent have a material impact on the outcome of the case, they may serve as the basis for the adjudication; if the original opponent does not participate or fails to raise opinions, the adjudication of the case shall not be affected.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　商标评审委员会审理依照[商标法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-44)、第四十五条规定请求宣告注册商标无效的案件，应当针对当事人申请和答辩的事实、理由及请求进行审理。

**Article 54** When adjudicating a case requesting a declaration of invalidity of a registered trademark under Articles 44 and 45 of the Trademark Law, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall adjudicate with respect to the facts, reasons, and claims presented in the application and defense of the parties.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　商标评审委员会审理不服商标局依照[商标法第四十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-44)第一款规定作出宣告注册商标无效决定的复审案件，应当针对商标局的决定和申请人申请复审的事实、理由及请求进行审理。

**Article 55** When adjudicating a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to declare a registered trademark invalid under the first paragraph of Article 44 of the Trademark Law, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall adjudicate with respect to the Trademark Office's decision and the facts, reasons, and claims presented by the applicant in the review application.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　商标评审委员会审理不服商标局依照[商标法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-49)规定作出撤销或者维持注册商标决定的复审案件，应当针对商标局作出撤销或者维持注册商标决定和当事人申请复审时所依据的事实、理由及请求进行审理。

**Article 56** When adjudicating a case of review against the Trademark Office's decision to cancel or maintain a registered trademark under Article 49 of the Trademark Law, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall adjudicate with respect to the facts, reasons, and claims on which the Trademark Office based its decision to cancel or maintain the registered trademark and on which the party based its review application.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　申请商标评审，应当向商标评审委员会提交申请书，并按照对方当事人的数量提交相应份数的副本；基于商标局的决定书申请复审的，还应当同时附送商标局的决定书副本。  
商标评审委员会收到申请书后，经审查，符合受理条件的，予以受理；不符合受理条件的，不予受理，书面通知申请人并说明理由；需要补正的，通知申请人自收到通知之日起30日内补正。经补正仍不符合规定的，商标评审委员会不予受理，书面通知申请人并说明理由；期满未补正的，视为撤回申请，商标评审委员会应当书面通知申请人。  
商标评审委员会受理商标评审申请后，发现不符合受理条件的，予以驳回，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 57** An application for trademark review and adjudication shall be submitted to the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board with a written application, along with corresponding copies according to the number of the other parties; if the review is filed against a decision of the Trademark Office, a copy of the Trademark Office's decision shall also be attached.  
Upon receipt of the application, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall, after examination, accept it if it meets the acceptance conditions; if it does not meet the acceptance conditions, it shall not accept it, notify the applicant in writing, and state the reasons; if corrections are required, it shall notify the applicant to make corrections within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice. If the application still does not meet the requirements after corrections, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall not accept it, notify the applicant in writing, and state the reasons; if no corrections are made upon expiration of the time limit, the application shall be deemed withdrawn, and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall notify the applicant in writing.  
After accepting an application for trademark review and adjudication, if the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board finds that it does not meet the acceptance conditions, it shall reject it, notify the applicant in writing, and state the reasons.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　商标评审委员会受理商标评审申请后应当及时将申请书副本送交对方当事人，限其自收到申请书副本之日起30日内答辩；期满未答辩的，不影响商标评审委员会的评审。

**Article 58** After accepting an application for trademark review and adjudication, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall promptly send a copy of the application to the other party, requiring it to file a defense within 30 days from the date of receipt of the copy of the application; failure to file a defense upon expiration of the time limit shall not affect the adjudication of the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　当事人需要在提出评审申请或者答辩后补充有关证据材料的，应当在申请书或者答辩书中声明，并自提交申请书或者答辩书之日起3个月内提交；期满未提交的，视为放弃补充有关证据材料。但是，在期满后生成或者当事人有其他正当理由未能在期满前提交的证据，在期满后提交的，商标评审委员会将证据交对方当事人并质证后可以采信。

**Article 59** If a party needs to supplement relevant evidence materials after filing an application for review and adjudication or a defense, it shall state so in the application or defense, and submit such materials within three months from the date of submission of the application or defense; if not submitted upon expiration of the time limit, it shall be deemed as waiver of the right to supplement relevant evidence materials. However, for evidence generated after the expiration of the time limit or evidence that the party has other justified reasons for failing to submit before the expiration of the time limit, if submitted after the expiration, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board may admit such evidence after delivering it to the other party and conducting cross-examination.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　商标评审委员会根据当事人的请求或者实际需要，可以决定对评审申请进行口头审理。  
商标评审委员会决定对评审申请进行口头审理的，应当在口头审理15日前书面通知当事人，告知口头审理的日期、地点和评审人员。当事人应当在通知书指定的期限内作出答复。  
申请人不答复也不参加口头审理的，其评审申请视为撤回，商标评审委员会应当书面通知申请人；被申请人不答复也不参加口头审理的，商标评审委员会可以缺席评审。

**Article 60** The Trademark Review and Adjudication Board may, upon the request of a party or based on actual needs, decide to hold an oral hearing for the review application.  
If the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board decides to hold an oral hearing for the review application, it shall notify the parties in writing 15 days before the oral hearing, informing them of the date, place, and adjudicators of the oral hearing. The parties shall respond within the time limit specified in the notice.  
If the applicant neither responds nor attends the oral hearing, its review application shall be deemed withdrawn, and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall notify the applicant in writing; if the respondent neither responds nor attends the oral hearing, the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board may conduct the adjudication in absentia.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　申请人在商标评审委员会作出决定、裁定前，可以书面向商标评审委员会要求撤回申请并说明理由，商标评审委员会认为可以撤回的，评审程序终止。

**Article 61** Before the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a decision or ruling, the applicant may request in writing to withdraw the application and state the reasons. If the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board deems the withdrawal acceptable, the review procedure shall terminate.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　申请人撤回商标评审申请的，不得以相同的事实和理由再次提出评审申请。商标评审委员会对商标评审申请已经作出裁定或者决定的，任何人不得以相同的事实和理由再次提出评审申请。但是，经不予注册复审程序予以核准注册后向商标评审委员会提起宣告注册商标无效的除外。

**Article 62** Where an applicant withdraws an application for trademark review, it shall not file another review application on the same facts and grounds. Where the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board has already made a ruling or decision on an application for trademark review, no person may file another review application on the same facts and grounds, except that an application for a declaration of invalidity of a registered trademark is filed with the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board after the trademark is approved for registration through the procedure for review of refusal of registration.

### 第七章　商标使用的管理

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　使用注册商标，可以在商品、商品包装、说明书或者其他附着物上标明“注册商标”或者注册标记。  
注册标记包括和®。使用注册标记，应当标注在商标的右上角或者右下角。

**Article 63** When using a registered trademark, the words "registered trademark" or a registration mark may be indicated on the goods, packaging, instructions or other attachments.  
Registration marks include a circled "注" and ®. Where a registration mark is used, it shall be marked at the upper right or lower right corner of the trademark.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　《商标注册证》遗失或者破损的，应当向商标局提交补发《商标注册证》申请书。《商标注册证》遗失的，应当在《商标公告》上刊登遗失声明。破损的《商标注册证》，应当在提交补发申请时交回商标局。  
商标注册人需要商标局补发商标变更、转让、续展证明，出具商标注册证明，或者商标申请人需要商标局出具优先权证明文件的，应当向商标局提交相应申请书。符合要求的，商标局发给相应证明；不符合要求的，商标局不予办理，通知申请人并告知理由。  
伪造或者变造《商标注册证》或者其他商标证明文件的，依照刑法关于伪造、变造国家机关证件罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** Where the Trademark Registration Certificate is lost or damaged, an application for reissuance of the Trademark Registration Certificate shall be submitted to the Trademark Office. Where the Trademark Registration Certificate is lost, a loss declaration shall be published in the Trademark Gazette. Where the Trademark Registration Certificate is damaged, it shall be returned to the Trademark Office when submitting the application for reissuance.  
Where the trademark registrant needs the Trademark Office to reissue a certificate of trademark change, assignment, or renewal, or to issue a trademark registration certificate, or where a trademark applicant needs the Trademark Office to issue a priority document, a corresponding application shall be submitted to the Trademark Office. If the application conforms to the requirements, the Trademark Office shall issue the corresponding certificate; if it does not conform, the Trademark Office shall not process it, shall notify the applicant, and shall state the reasons.  
Where a person forges or alters the Trademark Registration Certificate or other trademark certification documents, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of forging or altering certificates of state organs or other crimes.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　有[商标法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-49)规定的注册商标成为其核定使用的商品通用名称情形的，任何单位或者个人可以向商标局申请撤销该注册商标，提交申请时应当附送证据材料。商标局受理后应当通知商标注册人，限其自收到通知之日起2个月内答辩；期满未答辩的，不影响商标局作出决定。

**Article 65** Where a registered trademark has become the generic name of the designated goods as provided in Article 49 of the Trademark Law, any entity or individual may apply to the Trademark Office for cancellation of the registered trademark, and shall attach evidentiary materials when submitting the application. After accepting the application, the Trademark Office shall notify the trademark registrant and require it to respond within two months from the date of receipt of the notice; if no response is made within the time limit, it shall not affect the Trademark Office's decision.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有[商标法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-49)规定的注册商标无正当理由连续3年不使用情形的，任何单位或者个人可以向商标局申请撤销该注册商标，提交申请时应当说明有关情况。商标局受理后应当通知商标注册人，限其自收到通知之日起2个月内提交该商标在撤销申请提出前使用的证据材料或者说明不使用的正当理由；期满未提供使用的证据材料或者证据材料无效并没有正当理由的，由商标局撤销其注册商标。  
前款所称使用的证据材料，包括商标注册人使用注册商标的证据材料和商标注册人许可他人使用注册商标的证据材料。  
以无正当理由连续3年不使用为由申请撤销注册商标的，应当自该注册商标注册公告之日起满3年后提出申请。

**Article 66** Where a registered trademark has not been used for three consecutive years without justifiable reason as provided in Article 49 of the Trademark Law, any entity or individual may apply to the Trademark Office for cancellation of the registered trademark, and shall state the relevant circumstances when submitting the application. After accepting the application, the Trademark Office shall notify the trademark registrant and require it to submit, within two months from the date of receipt of the notice, evidence of use of the trademark prior to the filing of the cancellation application or to state the justifiable reasons for non-use; if no evidence of use is submitted within the time limit or the evidence is invalid and there is no justifiable reason, the Trademark Office shall cancel the registered trademark.  
The evidence of use referred to in the preceding paragraph includes evidence of use of the registered trademark by the trademark registrant and evidence of use of the registered trademark by a licensee authorized by the trademark registrant.  
Where an application for cancellation of a registered trademark is filed on the ground of non-use for three consecutive years without justifiable reason, the application shall be filed after the expiration of three years from the date of publication of the registration of the registered trademark.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　下列情形属于[商标法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-49)规定的正当理由：  
(一)不可抗力；  
(二)政府政策性限制；  
(三)破产清算；  
(四)其他不可归责于商标注册人的正当事由。

**Article 67** The following circumstances constitute justifiable reasons as provided in Article 49 of the Trademark Law:  
(1) force majeure;  
(2) government policy restrictions;  
(3) bankruptcy liquidation;  
(4) other legitimate reasons not attributable to the trademark registrant.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　商标局、商标评审委员会撤销注册商标或者宣告注册商标无效，撤销或者宣告无效的理由仅及于部分指定商品的，对在该部分指定商品上使用的商标注册予以撤销或者宣告无效。

**Article 68** Where the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board cancels a registered trademark or declares a registered trademark invalid, and the grounds for cancellation or declaration of invalidity only relate to some of the designated goods, the trademark registration with respect to that part of the designated goods shall be cancelled or declared invalid.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　许可他人使用其注册商标的，许可人应当在许可合同有效期内向商标局备案并报送备案材料。备案材料应当说明注册商标使用许可人、被许可人、许可期限、许可使用的商品或者服务范围等事项。

**Article 69** Where a person licenses another to use its registered trademark, the licensor shall, within the validity period of the license contract, file for record with the Trademark Office and submit the filing materials. The filing materials shall specify the licensor, licensee, term of license, and scope of goods or services for which the license is granted.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　以注册商标专用权出质的，出质人与质权人应当签订书面质权合同，并共同向商标局提出质权登记申请，由商标局公告。

**Article 70** Where the exclusive right to use a registered trademark is pledged, the pledgor and the pledgee shall enter into a written pledge contract and jointly file an application for pledge registration with the Trademark Office, and the Trademark Office shall announce it.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反[商标法第四十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-43)第二款规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停止销售，拒不停止销售的，处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Where a person violates the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 43 of the Trademark Law, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order correction within a time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, it shall order cessation of sales; if the person refuses to cease sales, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　商标持有人依照[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)规定请求驰名商标保护的，可以向工商行政管理部门提出请求。经商标局依照[商标法第十四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-14)规定认定为驰名商标的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止违反[商标法第十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-13)规定使用商标的行为，收缴、销毁违法使用的商标标识；商标标识与商品难以分离的，一并收缴、销毁。

**Article 72** Where the holder of a trademark requests protection as a well-known trademark in accordance with Article 13 of the Trademark Law, it may file a request with the administrative department for industry and commerce. Where the Trademark Office, in accordance with Article 14 of the Trademark Law, has recognized the trademark as a well-known trademark, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order cessation of the use of the trademark in violation of Article 13 of the Trademark Law, and shall seize and destroy the illegally used trademark marks; if the trademark marks are inseparable from the goods, they shall be seized and destroyed together.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　商标注册人申请注销其注册商标或者注销其商标在部分指定商品上的注册的，应当向商标局提交商标注销申请书，并交回原《商标注册证》。  
商标注册人申请注销其注册商标或者注销其商标在部分指定商品上的注册，经商标局核准注销的，该注册商标专用权或者该注册商标专用权在该部分指定商品上的效力自商标局收到其注销申请之日起终止。

**Article 73** Where a trademark registrant applies for cancellation of its registered trademark or cancellation of its registration in respect of some of the designated goods, it shall submit an application for trademark cancellation to the Trademark Office and return the original Trademark Registration Certificate.  
Where a trademark registrant applies for cancellation of its registered trademark or cancellation of its registration in respect of some of the designated goods, and the Trademark Office approves the cancellation, the exclusive right to use the registered trademark or the effect of the exclusive right to use the registered trademark in respect of that part of the designated goods shall terminate from the date on which the Trademark Office receives the application for cancellation.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　注册商标被撤销或者依照[本条例第七十三条](#art-73)的规定被注销的，原《商标注册证》作废，并予以公告；撤销该商标在部分指定商品上的注册的，或者商标注册人申请注销其商标在部分指定商品上的注册的，重新核发《商标注册证》，并予以公告。

**Article 74** Where a registered trademark is cancelled or is revoked in accordance with Article 73 of these Regulations, the original Trademark Registration Certificate shall become invalid and a public announcement shall be made; where the registration of the trademark in respect of some of the designated goods is cancelled, or the trademark registrant applies for revocation of its trademark registration in respect of some of the designated goods, a new Trademark Registration Certificate shall be issued and a public announcement shall be made.

### 第八章　注册商标专用权的保护

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　为侵犯他人商标专用权提供仓储、运输、邮寄、印制、隐匿、经营场所、网络商品交易平台等，属于[商标法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-57)第六项规定的提供便利条件。

**Article 75** Providing storage, transportation, mailing, printing, concealment, business premises, online commodity trading platforms, etc., for infringing the exclusive right to use a trademark of another person, constitutes "providing convenience" as provided in item (6) of Article 57 of the Trademark Law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　在同一种商品或者类似商品上将与他人注册商标相同或者近似的标志作为商品名称或者商品装潢使用，误导公众的，属于[商标法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-57)第二项规定的侵犯注册商标专用权的行为。

**Article 76** Using a mark that is identical or similar to another person's registered trademark on the same or similar goods as a trade name or trade dress, thereby misleading the public, constitutes an act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark as provided in item (2) of Article 57 of the Trademark Law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　对侵犯注册商标专用权的行为，任何人可以向工商行政管理部门投诉或者举报。

**Article 77** With respect to any act of infringing the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, any person may file a complaint or report with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　计算[商标法第六十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-60)规定的违法经营额，可以考虑下列因素：  
(一)侵权商品的销售价格；  
(二)未销售侵权商品的标价；  
(三)已查清侵权商品实际销售的平均价格；  
(四)被侵权商品的市场中间价格；  
(五)侵权人因侵权所产生的营业收入；  
(六)其他能够合理计算侵权商品价值的因素。

**Article 78** When calculating the illegal business turnover as provided in Article 60 of the Trademark Law, the following factors may be considered:  
(1) the selling price of the infringing goods;  
(2) the marked price of unsold infringing goods;  
(3) the average actual selling price of the infringing goods that has been ascertained;  
(4) the market median price of the infringed goods;  
(5) the business revenue generated by the infringer from the infringement;  
(6) other factors that can reasonably calculate the value of the infringing goods.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　下列情形属于[商标法第六十条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-60)规定的能证明该商品是自己合法取得的情形：  
(一)有供货单位合法签章的供货清单和货款收据且经查证属实或者供货单位认可的；  
(二)有供销双方签订的进货合同且经查证已真实履行的；  
(三)有合法进货发票且发票记载事项与涉案商品对应的；  
(四)其他能够证明合法取得涉案商品的情形。

**Article 79** The following circumstances constitute the circumstances where it can be proved that the goods were lawfully obtained as provided in Article 60 of the Trademark Law:  
(1) there is a supply list and payment receipt legally signed and sealed by the supplying unit, and the facts have been verified and found to be true or have been confirmed by the supplying unit;  
(2) there is a purchase contract signed by the supplier and the buyer, and it has been verified that the contract has been actually performed;  
(3) there is a lawful purchase invoice, and the matters recorded in the invoice correspond to the goods involved;  
(4) other circumstances that can prove the lawful acquisition of the goods involved.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　销售不知道是侵犯注册商标专用权的商品，能证明该商品是自己合法取得并说明提供者的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止销售，并将案件情况通报侵权商品提供者所在地工商行政管理部门。

**Article 80** Where a person sells goods that it does not know infringe the exclusive right to use a registered trademark, and can prove that the goods were lawfully obtained and can identify the supplier, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order cessation of sales and shall notify the administrative department for industry and commerce in the place where the supplier of the infringing goods is located of the case circumstances.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　涉案注册商标权属正在商标局、商标评审委员会审理或者人民法院诉讼中，案件结果可能影响案件定性的，属于[商标法第六十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-62)第三款规定的商标权属存在争议。

**Article 81** Where the ownership of the trademark involved is under examination by the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, or in litigation in the People's Court, and the outcome of the case may affect the characterization of the case, it shall be deemed that there is a dispute over the trademark ownership as provided for in Article 62, paragraph 3 of the Trademark Law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　在查处商标侵权案件过程中，工商行政管理部门可以要求权利人对涉案商品是否为权利人生产或者其许可生产的产品进行辨认。

**Article 82** In the process of investigating and handling trademark infringement cases, the administrative department for industry and commerce may require the right holder to identify whether the goods involved in the case are produced by the right holder or a producer licensed by the right holder.

### 第九章　商标代理

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　商标法所称商标代理，是指接受委托人的委托，以委托人的名义办理商标注册申请、商标评审或者其他商标事宜。

**Article 83** For the purposes of the Trademark Law, "trademark agency" means accepting the entrustment of a client and, in the name of the client, handling trademark registration applications, trademark review, or other trademark matters.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　商标法所称商标代理机构，包括经工商行政管理部门登记从事商标代理业务的服务机构和从事商标代理业务的律师事务所。  
商标代理机构从事商标局、商标评审委员会主管的商标事宜代理业务的，应当按照下列规定向商标局备案：  
(一)交验工商行政管理部门的登记证明文件或者司法行政部门批准设立律师事务所的证明文件并留存复印件；  
(二)报送商标代理机构的名称、住所、负责人、联系方式等基本信息；  
(三)报送商标代理从业人员名单及联系方式。  
工商行政管理部门应当建立商标代理机构信用档案。商标代理机构违反商标法或者本条例规定的，由商标局或者商标评审委员会予以公开通报，并记入其信用档案。

**Article 84** For the purposes of the Trademark Law, "trademark agency" includes service institutions registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce to engage in trademark agency business, and law firms engaging in trademark agency business.  
Where a trademark agency engages in the agency of trademark matters under the charge of the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board, it shall file a record with the Trademark Office in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Submit the registration certificate issued by the administrative department for industry and commerce or the approval document for the establishment of a law firm issued by the judicial administrative department for inspection, and retain a copy;  
(2) Submit the basic information of the trademark agency, including its name, domicile, responsible person, contact information, etc.;  
(3) Submit the list and contact information of the trademark agency practitioners.  
The administrative department for industry and commerce shall establish a credit archive for trademark agencies. Where a trademark agency violates the Trademark Law or these Regulations, the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall make a public announcement and record it in its credit archive.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　商标法所称商标代理从业人员，是指在商标代理机构中从事商标代理业务的工作人员。  
商标代理从业人员不得以个人名义自行接受委托。

**Article 85** For the purposes of the Trademark Law, "trademark agency practitioners" means the staff members who engage in trademark agency business in a trademark agency.  
A trademark agency practitioner shall not accept a mandate in his/her own name.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　商标代理机构向商标局、商标评审委员会提交的有关申请文件，应当加盖该代理机构公章并由相关商标代理从业人员签字。

**Article 86** The relevant application documents submitted by a trademark agency to the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall be affixed with the official seal of the agency and signed by the relevant trademark agency practitioners.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　商标代理机构申请注册或者受让其代理服务以外的其他商标，商标局不予受理。

**Article 87** Where a trademark agency applies for registration of or acquires by assignment any trademark other than those for its agency services, the Trademark Office shall not accept the application.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　下列行为属于[商标法第六十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-68)第一款第二项规定的以其他不正当手段扰乱商标代理市场秩序的行为：  
(一)以欺诈、虚假宣传、引人误解或者商业贿赂等方式招徕业务的；  
(二)隐瞒事实，提供虚假证据，或者威胁、诱导他人隐瞒事实，提供虚假证据的；  
(三)在同一商标案件中接受有利益冲突的双方当事人委托的。

**Article 88** The following acts shall constitute acts of disrupting the order of the trademark agency market by other improper means as provided for in Article 68, paragraph 1, item 2 of the Trademark Law:  
(1) Soliciting business by means of fraud, false propaganda, misleading publicity, or commercial bribery;  
(2) Concealing facts, providing false evidence, or threatening or inducing others to conceal facts and provide false evidence;  
(3) Accepting the mandate of both parties with conflicting interests in the same trademark case.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　商标代理机构有[商标法第六十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-68)规定行为的，由行为人所在地或者违法行为发生地县级以上工商行政管理部门进行查处并将查处情况通报商标局。

**Article 89** Where a trademark agency commits an act as provided for in Article 68 of the Trademark Law, the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level in the place where the actor is located or where the illegal act occurs shall investigate and handle the case, and shall notify the Trademark Office of the investigation and handling situation.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　商标局、商标评审委员会依照[商标法第六十八条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-68)规定停止受理商标代理机构办理商标代理业务的，可以作出停止受理该商标代理机构商标代理业务6个月以上直至永久停止受理的决定。停止受理商标代理业务的期间届满，商标局、商标评审委员会应当恢复受理。  
商标局、商标评审委员会作出停止受理或者恢复受理商标代理的决定应当在其网站予以公告。

**Article 90** Where the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board suspends the acceptance of trademark agency business of a trademark agency in accordance with Article 68 of the Trademark Law, it may make a decision to suspend acceptance of the trademark agency business of the agency for a period of not less than six months up to permanent suspension. Upon expiry of the period of suspension, the Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall resume acceptance.  
The Trademark Office or the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall publish the decision to suspend or resume acceptance of trademark agency on its website.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　工商行政管理部门应当加强对商标代理行业组织的监督和指导。

**Article 91** The administrative department for industry and commerce shall strengthen supervision and guidance over the trademark agency industry organizations.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　连续使用至1993年7月1日的服务商标，与他人在相同或者类似的服务上已注册的服务商标相同或者近似的，可以继续使用；但是，1993年7月1日后中断使用3年以上的，不得继续使用。  
已连续使用至商标局首次受理新放开商品或者服务项目之日的商标，与他人在新放开商品或者服务项目相同或者类似的商品或者服务上已注册的商标相同或者近似的，可以继续使用；但是，首次受理之日后中断使用3年以上的，不得继续使用。

**Article 92** A service mark that has been continuously used until July 1, 1993, and is identical or similar to a registered service mark used on the same or similar services by another party, may continue to be used; however, if the use has been interrupted for more than three years after July 1, 1993, it shall not continue to be used.  
A mark that has been continuously used until the date when the Trademark Office first accepts a newly liberalized class of goods or services, and is identical or similar to a registered mark used on the same or similar newly liberalized goods or services by another party, may continue to be used; however, if the use has been interrupted for more than three years after the date of first acceptance, it shall not continue to be used.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　商标注册用商品和服务分类表，由商标局制定并公布。  
申请商标注册或者办理其他商标事宜的文件格式，由商标局、商标评审委员会制定并公布。  
商标评审委员会的评审规则由国务院工商行政管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 93** The Classification of Goods and Services for Trademark Registration shall be formulated and published by the Trademark Office.  
The format of documents for filing an application for trademark registration or handling other trademark matters shall be formulated and published by the Trademark Office and the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board.  
The review rules of the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board shall be formulated and published by the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　商标局设置《商标注册簿》，记载注册商标及有关注册事项。

**Article 94** The Trademark Office shall keep a "Trademark Register", which records registered trademarks and relevant registration particulars.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　《商标注册证》及相关证明是权利人享有注册商标专用权的凭证。《商标注册证》记载的注册事项，应当与《商标注册簿》一致；记载不一致的，除有证据证明《商标注册簿》确有错误外，以《商标注册簿》为准。

**Article 95** The "Trademark Registration Certificate" and relevant certificates shall be the evidence that the right holder enjoys the exclusive right to use a registered trademark. The registration particulars recorded in the "Trademark Registration Certificate" shall be consistent with those in the "Trademark Register"; in case of any inconsistency, unless there is evidence proving that the "Trademark Register" is indeed erroneous, the "Trademark Register" shall prevail.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　商标局发布《商标公告》，刊发商标注册及其他有关事项。  
《商标公告》采用纸质或者电子形式发布。  
除送达公告外，公告内容自发布之日起视为社会公众已经知道或者应当知道。

**Article 96** The Trademark Office shall publish the "Trademark Gazette", which contains announcements of trademark registrations and other relevant matters.  
The "Trademark Gazette" shall be published in paper or electronic form.  
Except for service announcements, the content of the announcements shall be deemed to be known or should have been known to the public as of the date of publication.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　申请商标注册或者办理其他商标事宜，应当缴纳费用。缴纳费用的项目和标准，由国务院财政部门、国务院价格主管部门分别制定。

**Article 97** An applicant for trademark registration or for handling other trademark matters shall pay fees. The items and standards for the fees shall be separately formulated by the financial department of the State Council and the competent pricing department of the State Council.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　本条例自2014年5月1日起施行。

**Article 98** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2014.
