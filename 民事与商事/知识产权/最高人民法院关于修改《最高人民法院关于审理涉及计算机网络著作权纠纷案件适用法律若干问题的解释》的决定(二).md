# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理涉及计算机网络著作权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》的决定(二)

**公布日期**：2006-11-20

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  网络著作权侵权纠纷案件由侵权行为地或者被告住所地人民法院管辖。侵权行为地包括实施被诉侵权行为的网络服务器、计算机终端等设备所在地。对难以确定侵权行为地和被告住所地的，原告发现侵权内容的计算机终端等设备所在地可以视为侵权行为地。

**Article 1** Cases concerning disputes over copyright infringement on the Internet shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the infringing act is committed or where the defendant has its domicile. The place where the infringing act is committed includes the location of equipment such as the network server or computer terminal on which the alleged infringing act is carried out. Where it is difficult to determine the place where the infringing act is committed or the defendant's domicile, the location of the computer terminal or other equipment on which the plaintiff discovers the infringing content may be deemed the place where the infringing act is committed.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  受著作权法保护的作品，包括[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)规定的各类作品的数字化形式。在网络环境下无法归于[著作权法第三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-3)列举的作品范围，但在文学、艺术和科学领域内具有独创性并能以某种有形形式复制的其他智力创作成果，人民法院应当予以保护。

**Article 2** Works protected by the Copyright Law shall include the digital forms of all types of works as specified in Article 3 of the Copyright Law. Where other intellectual creations that cannot be classified under the categories of works listed in Article 3 of the Copyright Law in the Internet environment, but are original in the fields of literature, art, and science and can be reproduced in a tangible form, the People's Court shall protect them.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  网络服务提供者通过网络参与他人侵犯著作权行为，或者通过网络教唆、帮助他人实施侵犯著作权行为的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与其他行为人或者直接实施侵权行为人的共同侵权责任。

**Article 3** Where an Internet service provider participates in another person's act of copyright infringement through the Internet, or instigates or assists another person in committing an act of copyright infringement through the Internet, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it jointly and severally liable for infringement together with the other actor or the person directly committing the infringing act.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，明知网络用户通过网络实施侵犯他人著作权的行为，或者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告，但仍不采取移除侵权内容等措施以消除侵权后果的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百三十条的规定，追究其与该网络用户的共同侵权责任。

**Article 4** Where an Internet service provider that provides content services, knowing that a network user is committing an act of copyright infringement through the Internet, or upon receiving a warning from the copyright owner with sufficient evidence, still fails to take measures such as removing the infringing content to eliminate the consequences of the infringement, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 130 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it jointly and severally liable for infringement with the network user.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  提供内容服务的网络服务提供者，对著作权人要求其提供侵权行为人在其网络的注册资料以追究行为人的侵权责任，无正当理由拒绝提供的，人民法院应当根据民法通则第一百零六条的规定，追究其相应的侵权责任。

**Article 5** Where an Internet service provider that provides content services, without justified reasons, refuses to provide the registration information of the infringer on its network as requested by the copyright owner for the purpose of holding the infringer liable, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 106 of the General Principles of the Civil Law, hold it liable for the corresponding infringement.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  网络服务提供者明知专门用于故意避开或者破坏他人著作权技术保护措施的方法、设备或者材料，而上载、传播、提供的，人民法院应当根据当事人的诉讼请求和具体案情，依照[著作权法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-47)第（六）项的规定，追究网络服务提供者的民事侵权责任。

**Article 6** Where an Internet service provider, knowing that a method, device, or material is specifically used for intentionally circumventing or destroying the technological protection measures of another person's copyright, uploads, disseminates, or provides it, the People's Court shall, based on the party's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, and in accordance with Article 47(6) of the Copyright Law, hold the Internet service provider civilly liable for infringement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  著作权人发现侵权信息向网络服务提供者提出警告或者索要侵权行为人网络注册资料时，不能出示身份证明、著作权权属证明及侵权情况证明的，视为未提出警告或者未提出索要请求。  
著作权人出示上述证明后网络服务提供者仍不采取措施的，著作权人可以依照[著作权法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-49)、第五十条的规定在诉前申请人民法院作出停止有关行为和财产保全、证据保全的裁定，也可以在提起诉讼时申请人民法院先行裁定停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除影响，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 7** Where a copyright owner, upon discovering infringing information, issues a warning to an Internet service provider or requests the registration information of the infringer on the network, but fails to present identification documents, proof of copyright ownership, and proof of the infringing circumstances, it shall be deemed that no warning was issued or no request was made.  
After the copyright owner presents the aforementioned proofs, if the Internet service provider still fails to take measures, the copyright owner may, in accordance with Articles 49 and 50 of the Copyright Law, apply to the People's Court before prosecution for a ruling to cease the relevant act and for property preservation and evidence preservation, or may, when filing the lawsuit, apply to the People's Court for an advance ruling to cease the infringement, remove the obstruction, and eliminate the effects, and the People's Court shall grant such application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  网络服务提供者经著作权人提出确有证据的警告而采取移除被控侵权内容等措施，被控侵权人要求网络服务提供者承担违约责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
著作权人指控侵权不实，被控侵权人因网络服务提供者采取措施遭受损失而请求赔偿的，人民法院应当判令由提出警告的人承担赔偿责任。

**Article 8** Where an Internet service provider takes measures such as removing the allegedly infringing content after receiving a warning with sufficient evidence from the copyright owner, and the alleged infringer demands that the Internet service provider bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall not support such demand.  
Where the copyright owner's accusation of infringement is untrue, and the alleged infringer suffers losses due to the measures taken by the Internet service provider and seeks compensation, the People's Court shall order the person who issued the warning to bear compensation liability.
