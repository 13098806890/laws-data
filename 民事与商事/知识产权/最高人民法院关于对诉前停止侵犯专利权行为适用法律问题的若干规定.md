# 最高人民法院关于对诉前停止侵犯专利权行为适用法律问题的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕20号  
**公布日期**：2001-06-05  
**生效日期**：2001-07-01

> （2001年6月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第1179次会议通过，自2001年7月1日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕20号 为切实保护专利权人和其他利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国专利法》（以下简称专利法）、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》（以下简称民事诉讼法）的有关规定，现就有关诉前停止侵犯专利权行为适用法律若干问题规定如下： 第一条 根据专利法第六十一条的规定，专利权人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院提出诉前责令被申请人停止侵犯专利权行为的申请。 提出申请的利害关系人，包括专利实施许可合同的被许可人、专利财产权利的合法继承人等。专利实施许可合同被许可人中，独占实施许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他实施许可合同的被许可人在专利权人不申请的情况下，可以提出申请。 第二条 诉前责令停止侵犯专利权行为的申请，应当向有专利侵权案件管辖权的人民法院提出。 第三条 专利权人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出申请，应当递交书面申请状；申请状应当载明当事人及其基本情况、申请的具体内容、范围和理由等事项。申请的理由包括有关行为如不及时制止会使申请人合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的具体说明。 第四条 申请人提出申请时，应当提交下列证据： （一）专利权人应当提交证明其专利权真实有效的文件，包括专利证书、权利要求书、说明书、专利年费交纳凭证。提出的申请涉及实用新型专利的，申请人应当提交国务院专利行政部门出具的检索报告。 （二）利害关系人应当提供有关专利实施许可合同及其在国务院专利行政部门备案的证明材料，未经备案的应当提交专利权人的证明，或者证明其享有权利的其他证据。 排他实施许可合同的被许可人单独提出申请的，应当提交专利权人放弃申请的证明材料。 专利财产权利的继承人应当提交已经继承或者正在继承的证据材料。 （三）提交证明被申请人正在实施或者即将实施侵犯其专利权的行为的证据，包括被控侵权产品以及专利技术与被控侵权产品技术特征对比材料等。 第五条 人民法院作出诉前停止侵犯专利权行为的裁定事项，应当限于专利权人或者利害关系人申请的范围。 第六条 申请人提出申请时应当提供担保，申请人不提供担保的，驳回申请。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 根据[专利法第六十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-61)的规定，专利权人或者利害关系人可以向人民法院提出诉前责令被申请人停止侵犯专利权行为的申请。  
提出申请的利害关系人，包括专利实施许可合同的被许可人、专利财产权利的合法继承人等。专利实施许可合同被许可人中，独占实施许可合同的被许可人可以单独向人民法院提出申请；排他实施许可合同的被许可人在专利权人不申请的情况下，可以提出申请。

**Article 1** In accordance with Article 61 of the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, the patentee or an interested party may file an application with the People's Court for an order before litigation to enjoin the respondent from committing acts of patent infringement.  
An interested party who may file the application includes a licensee under a patent exploitation license contract, a legal successor of patent property rights, etc. Among licensees under a patent exploitation license contract, a licensee under an exclusive exploitation license contract may file the application with the People's Court independently; a licensee under a sole exploitation license contract may file the application if the patentee fails to do so.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 诉前责令停止侵犯专利权行为的申请，应当向有专利侵权案件管辖权的人民法院提出。

**Article 2** An application for an order to enjoin the respondent from committing acts of patent infringement before litigation shall be submitted to the People's Court that has jurisdiction over patent infringement cases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 专利权人或者利害关系人向人民法院提出申请，应当递交书面申请状；申请状应当载明当事人及其基本情况、申请的具体内容、范围和理由等事项。申请的理由包括有关行为如不及时制止会使申请人合法权益受到难以弥补的损害的具体说明。

**Article 3** A patentee or an interested party that files an application with the People's Court shall submit a written petition; the petition shall state the parties and their basic information, the specific content, scope, and reasons for the application, among other matters. The reasons for the application shall include a specific explanation that, if the relevant acts are not promptly stopped, the applicant's lawful rights and interests would suffer irreparable harm.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 申请人提出申请时，应当提交下列证据：  
（一）专利权人应当提交证明其专利权真实有效的文件，包括专利证书、权利要求书、说明书、专利年费交纳凭证。提出的申请涉及实用新型专利的，申请人应当提交国务院专利行政部门出具的检索报告。  
（二）利害关系人应当提供有关专利实施许可合同及其在国务院专利行政部门备案的证明材料，未经备案的应当提交专利权人的证明，或者证明其享有权利的其他证据。  
排他实施许可合同的被许可人单独提出申请的，应当提交专利权人放弃申请的证明材料。  
专利财产权利的继承人应当提交已经继承或者正在继承的证据材料。  
（三）提交证明被申请人正在实施或者即将实施侵犯其专利权的行为的证据，包括被控侵权产品以及专利技术与被控侵权产品技术特征对比材料等。

**Article 4** When filing an application, the applicant shall submit the following evidence:  
(1) The patentee shall submit documents evidencing the authenticity and validity of the patent right, including the patent certificate, claims, specification, and proof of payment of annual patent fees. If the application involves a utility model patent, the applicant shall submit a search report issued by the patent administrative department under the State Council.  
(2) An interested party shall provide relevant evidence of the patent exploitation license contract and its recordal with the patent administrative department under the State Council; if not recorded, it shall submit a certificate from the patentee or other evidence proving its entitlement to the right.  
A licensee under a sole exploitation license contract that independently files an application shall submit evidence that the patentee has abandoned the application.  
A successor of patent property rights shall submit evidence that the succession has occurred or is in progress.  
(3) Submit evidence proving that the respondent is committing or is about to commit acts infringing the patent right, including the allegedly infringing product and materials comparing the technical features of the patented technology with those of the allegedly infringing product, etc.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院作出诉前停止侵犯专利权行为的裁定事项，应当限于专利权人或者利害关系人申请的范围。

**Article 5** The matters ordered by the People's Court in a ruling to enjoin the respondent from committing acts of patent infringement before litigation shall be limited to the scope of the application filed by the patentee or interested party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 申请人提出申请时应当提供担保，申请人不提供担保的，驳回申请。  
当事人提供保证、抵押等形式的担保合理、有效的，人民法院应当准予。  
人民法院确定担保范围时，应当考虑责令停止有关行为所涉及产品的销售收入，以及合理的仓储、保管等费用；被申请人停止有关行为可能造成的损失，以及人员工资等合理费用支出；其他因素。

**Article 6** The applicant shall provide security when filing the application; if the applicant fails to provide security, the application shall be rejected.  
If the party provides reasonable and effective security in the form of a guarantee, mortgage, or other means, the People's Court shall approve it.  
When determining the scope of security, the People's Court shall consider the sales revenue of the products involved in the order for suspension of the relevant acts, as well as reasonable storage, custody, and other costs; the losses the respondent may suffer from suspension of the relevant acts, as well as reasonable personnel salaries and other expenses; and other factors.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 在执行停止有关行为裁定过程中，被申请人可能因采取该项措施造成更大损失的，人民法院可以责令申请人追加相应的担保。申请人不追加担保的，解除有关停止措施。

**Article 7** If, during the enforcement of the ruling to suspend the relevant acts, the respondent may suffer greater losses as a result of such measures, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide additional security correspondingly. If the applicant fails to provide additional security, the suspension measures shall be lifted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 停止侵犯专利权行为裁定所采取的措施，不因被申请人提出反担保而解除。

**Article 8** The measures taken under the ruling to enjoin the acts of patent infringement shall not be lifted because the respondent provides counter-security.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院接受专利权人或者利害关系人提出责令停止侵犯专利权行为的申请后，经审查符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)的，应当在四十八小时内作出书面裁定；裁定责令被申请人停止侵犯专利权行为的，应当立即开始执行。  
人民法院在前述期限内，需要对有关事实进行核对的，可以传唤单方或双方当事人进行询问，然后再及时作出裁定。  
人民法院作出诉前责令被申请人停止有关行为的裁定，应当及时通知被申请人，至迟不得超过五日。

**Article 9** After accepting an application filed by a patentee or an interested party for an order to enjoin acts of patent infringement, the People's Court shall, if upon examination it conforms to Article 4 hereof, make a written ruling within forty-eight hours; if the ruling orders the respondent to cease the patent infringement acts, it shall be enforced immediately.  
If, within the aforesaid time limit, the People's Court needs to verify relevant facts, it may summon one or both parties for questioning, and then promptly make a ruling.  
The People's Court shall promptly notify the respondent of the ruling ordering the respondent before litigation to cease the relevant acts, no later than five days.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 当事人对裁定不服的，可以在收到裁定之日起十日内申请复议一次。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 10** If a party disagrees with the ruling, it may apply for one reconsideration within ten days from the date of receipt of the ruling. During the period of reconsideration, enforcement of the ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院对当事人提出的复议申请应当从以下方面进行审查：  
（一）被申请人正在实施或即将实施的行为是否构成侵犯专利权；  
（二）不采取有关措施，是否会给申请人合法权益造成难以弥补的损害；  
（三）申请人提供担保的情况；  
（四）责令被申请人停止有关行为是否损害社会公共利益。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall review the reconsideration application filed by a party from the following aspects:  
(1) whether the acts being or about to be committed by the respondent constitute patent infringement;  
(2) whether failure to adopt the relevant measures would cause irreparable harm to the applicant's lawful rights and interests;  
(3) the circumstances of the security provided by the applicant;  
(4) whether ordering the respondent to cease the relevant acts would harm the public interest.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 专利权人或者利害关系人在人民法院采取停止有关行为的措施后十五日内不起诉的，人民法院解除裁定采取的措施。

**Article 12** If the patentee or interested party fails to file a prosecution within fifteen days after the People's Court adopts measures to suspend the relevant acts, the People's Court shall dissolve the measures taken under the ruling.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 申请人不起诉或者申请错误造成被申请人损失的，被申请人可以向有管辖权的人民法院起诉请求申请人赔偿，也可以在专利权人或者利害关系人提起的专利权侵权诉讼中提出损害赔偿的请求，人民法院可以一并处理。

**Article 13** If the applicant fails to file a prosecution or the application is erroneous and causes losses to the respondent, the respondent may file a prosecution with the People's Court having jurisdiction, requesting the applicant to compensate for the losses, or may raise a claim for damages in the patent infringement action filed by the patentee or interested party, and the People's Court may handle it together.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 停止侵犯专利权行为裁定的效力，一般应维持到终审法律文书生效时止。人民法院也可以根据案情，确定具体期限；期限届满时，根据当事人的请求仍可作出继续停止有关行为的裁定。

**Article 14** The validity of a ruling to enjoin acts of patent infringement shall generally be maintained until the final adjudicative document takes effect. The People's Court may also determine a specific time limit based on the circumstances of the case; upon expiration of the time limit, a ruling may still be made to continue the suspension of the relevant acts upon the request of a party.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 被申请人违反人民法院责令停止有关行为裁定的，依照[民事诉讼法第一百零二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-102)规定处理。

**Article 15** If the respondent violates the People's Court's ruling ordering the suspension of the relevant acts, it shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 102 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 人民法院执行诉前停止侵犯专利权行为的措施时，可以根据当事人的申请，参照[民事诉讼法第七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-74)的规定，同时进行证据保全。  
人民法院可以根据当事人的申请，依照[民事诉讼法第九十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-92)、第九十三条的规定进行财产保全。

**Article 16** When enforcing measures to suspend acts of patent infringement before litigation, the People's Court may, upon the application of a party, also carry out evidence preservation by reference to Article 74 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The People's Court may, upon the application of a party, carry out property preservation in accordance with Articles 92 and 93 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 专利权人或者利害关系人向人民法院提起专利侵权诉讼时，同时提出先行停止侵犯专利权行为请求的，人民法院可以先行作出裁定。

**Article 17** If a patentee or interested party, when filing a patent infringement action with the People's Court, simultaneously requests an interim order to enjoin the acts of patent infringement, the People's Court may render a ruling in advance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 诉前停止侵犯专利权行为的案件，申请人应当按照《人民法院诉讼收费办法》及其补充规定交纳费用。

**Article 18** For cases concerning the suspension of patent infringement acts before litigation, the applicant shall pay the fees in accordance with the Measures for Litigation Fees of the People's Court and its supplementary provisions.
