### 最高人民法院关于同意指定青岛市中级人民法院为审理专利纠纷案件第一审法院的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于同意指定青岛市中级人民法院  
  
为审理专利纠纷案件第一审法院的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1988〕22号  
  
山东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 87）鲁法（研）第 133号《关于指定青岛市、烟台市中级人民法院为审理专利纠纷案件的第一审法院的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
同意指定青岛市中级人民法院作为审理其辖区内属于最高人民法院法（经）发〔1985〕3号文件关于收案范围中所列 5—— 7类专利纠纷案件（见《最高人民法院公报》 1985年第1号）和法（经）发〔1987〕29号文件第二条所列三种专利申请权纠纷案件（见《最高人民法院公报》 1987年第4号）的第一审法院。  
  
此复  
  
1988年5月14日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Agreeing to Designate the Qingdao Intermediate People's Court  
as the Court of First Instance for the Trial of Patent Dispute Cases  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1988] No. 22  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your Request for Instructions on Designating the Qingdao and Yantai Intermediate People's Courts as the Courts of First Instance for the Trial of Patent Dispute Cases (Lu Fa (Yan) No. 133 [1987]) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
It is agreed that the Qingdao Intermediate People's Court shall be designated as the court of first instance for the trial of patent dispute cases falling within categories 5 to 7 as listed in the scope of case acceptance under Document Fa (Jing) Fa [1985] No. 3 of the Supreme People's Court (see Gazette of the Supreme People's Court, No. 1 of 1985) and the three categories of patent application right dispute cases as listed in Article 2 of Document Fa (Jing) Fa [1987] No. 29 (see Gazette of the Supreme People's Court, No. 4 of 1987) within its jurisdiction.  
So replied.  
May 14, 1988
