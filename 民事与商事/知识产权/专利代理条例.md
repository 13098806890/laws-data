# 专利代理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2018-11-06  
**生效日期**：2019-03-01

> (1991年3月4日中华人民共和国国务院令第76号发布　2018年9月6日国务院第23次常务会议修订通过　2018年11月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第706号公布　自2019年3月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范专利代理行为，保障委托人、专利代理机构和专利代理师的合法权益，维护专利代理活动的正常秩序，促进专利代理行业健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称专利代理，是指专利代理机构接受委托，以委托人的名义在代理权限范围内办理专利申请、宣告专利权无效等专利事务的行为。 第三条　任何单位和个人可以自行在国内申请专利和办理其他专利事务，也可以委托依法设立的专利代理机构办理，法律另有规定的除外。 专利代理机构应当按照委托人的委托办理专利事务。 第四条　专利代理机构和专利代理师执业应当遵守法律、行政法规，恪守职业道德、执业纪律，维护委托人的合法权益。 专利代理机构和专利代理师依法执业受法律保护。 第五条　国务院专利行政部门负责全国的专利代理管理工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门负责本行政区域内的专利代理管理工作。 第六条　专利代理机构和专利代理师可以依法成立和参加专利代理行业组织。 专利代理行业组织应当制定专利代理行业自律规范。专利代理行业自律规范不得与法律、行政法规相抵触。 国务院专利行政部门依法对专利代理行业组织进行监督、指导。 第二章　专利代理机构和专利代理师 第七条　专利代理机构的组织形式应当为合伙企业、有限责任公司等。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范专利代理行为，保障委托人、专利代理机构和专利代理师的合法权益，维护专利代理活动的正常秩序，促进专利代理行业健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国专利法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating patent agency activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of principals, patent agencies, and patent agents, maintaining the normal order of patent agency activities, and promoting the healthy development of the patent agency industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称专利代理，是指专利代理机构接受委托，以委托人的名义在代理权限范围内办理专利申请、宣告专利权无效等专利事务的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "patent agency" refers to the act where a patent agency, upon acceptance of a mandate, handles patent affairs such as patent applications and declarations of patent invalidity within the scope of its agency authority in the name of the principal.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　任何单位和个人可以自行在国内申请专利和办理其他专利事务，也可以委托依法设立的专利代理机构办理，法律另有规定的除外。  
专利代理机构应当按照委托人的委托办理专利事务。

**Article 3** Any entity or individual may file a patent application and handle other patent affairs within China on its/his own, or may entrust a patent agency established according to law to handle such matters, unless otherwise provided by law.  
A patent agency shall handle patent affairs in accordance with the mandate of the principal.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　专利代理机构和专利代理师执业应当遵守法律、行政法规，恪守职业道德、执业纪律，维护委托人的合法权益。  
专利代理机构和专利代理师依法执业受法律保护。

**Article 4** Patent agencies and patent agents shall, in practicing, abide by laws and administrative regulations, observe professional ethics and practice discipline, and protect the lawful rights and interests of principals.  
The lawful practice of patent agencies and patent agents shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院专利行政部门负责全国的专利代理管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门负责本行政区域内的专利代理管理工作。

**Article 5** The patent administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of patent agency nationwide.  
The departments for patent administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the administration of patent agency within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　专利代理机构和专利代理师可以依法成立和参加专利代理行业组织。  
专利代理行业组织应当制定专利代理行业自律规范。专利代理行业自律规范不得与法律、行政法规相抵触。  
国务院专利行政部门依法对专利代理行业组织进行监督、指导。

**Article 6** Patent agencies and patent agents may, according to law, establish and join patent agency industry organizations.  
Patent agency industry organizations shall formulate self-disciplinary norms for the patent agency industry. Such self-disciplinary norms shall not conflict with laws and administrative regulations.  
The patent administrative department under the State Council shall, according to law, supervise and guide the patent agency industry organizations.

### 第二章　专利代理机构和专利代理师

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　专利代理机构的组织形式应当为合伙企业、有限责任公司等。

**Article 7** The organizational form of a patent agency shall be a partnership, a limited liability company, or the like.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　合伙企业、有限责任公司形式的专利代理机构从事专利代理业务应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有符合法律、行政法规规定的专利代理机构名称；  
(二)有书面合伙协议或者公司章程；  
(三)有独立的经营场所；  
(四)合伙人、股东符合国家有关规定。

**Article 8** A patent agency in the form of a partnership or a limited liability company that engages in patent agency business shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name of the patent agency that complies with laws and administrative regulations;  
(2) having a written partnership agreement or articles of association;  
(3) having an independent business premises;  
(4) the partners or shareholders meet the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事专利代理业务，应当向国务院专利行政部门提出申请，提交有关材料，取得专利代理机构执业许可证。国务院专利行政部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出是否颁发专利代理机构执业许可证的决定。  
专利代理机构合伙人、股东或者法定代表人等事项发生变化的，应当办理变更手续。

**Article 9** To engage in patent agency business, an application shall be filed with the patent administrative department under the State Council, together with relevant materials, to obtain a patent agency practice license. The patent administrative department under the State Council shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on whether to issue the patent agency practice license.  
If any changes occur in the partners, shareholders, or legal representative of a patent agency, the formalities for alteration shall be handled.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　具有高等院校理工科专业专科以上学历的中国公民可以参加全国专利代理师资格考试；考试合格的，由国务院专利行政部门颁发专利代理师资格证。专利代理师资格考试办法由国务院专利行政部门制定。

**Article 10** Chinese citizens with an associate degree or higher in a science or engineering discipline from an institution of higher education may take the national patent agent qualification examination; those who pass the examination shall be issued a patent agent qualification certificate by the patent administrative department under the State Council. The measures for the patent agent qualification examination shall be formulated by the patent administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　专利代理师执业应当取得专利代理师资格证，在专利代理机构实习满1年，并在一家专利代理机构从业。

**Article 11** A patent agent, to practice, shall obtain a patent agent qualification certificate, complete an internship of at least one year at a patent agency, and work in one patent agency.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　专利代理师首次执业，应当自执业之日起30日内向专利代理机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门备案。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门应当为专利代理师通过互联网备案提供方便。

**Article 12** A patent agent who practices for the first time shall, within 30 days from the date of starting practice, file for record with the administrative department for patent affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the patent agency is located.  
The administrative department for patent affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall provide convenience for patent agents to file for record through the Internet.

### 第三章　专利代理执业

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　专利代理机构可以接受委托，代理专利申请、宣告专利权无效、转让专利申请权或者专利权以及订立专利实施许可合同等专利事务，也可以应当事人要求提供专利事务方面的咨询。

**Article 13** A patent agency may accept entrustment to act as an agent for patent affairs such as patent applications, invalidation of patent rights, assignment of patent application rights or patent rights, and conclusion of patent exploitation licensing contracts, and may also provide consultation on patent affairs at the request of a party.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　专利代理机构接受委托，应当与委托人订立书面委托合同。专利代理机构接受委托后，不得就同一专利申请或者专利权的事务接受有利益冲突的其他当事人的委托。  
专利代理机构应当指派在本机构执业的专利代理师承办专利代理业务，指派的专利代理师本人及其近亲属不得与其承办的专利代理业务有利益冲突。

**Article 14** When accepting entrustment, a patent agency shall conclude a written entrustment contract with the client. After accepting entrustment, a patent agency shall not accept entrustment from another party that has a conflict of interest regarding the same patent application or patent right.  
A patent agency shall assign a patent agent practicing in the agency to undertake the patent agency business, and the assigned patent agent and his close relatives shall not have a conflict of interest with the patent agency business undertaken by the agent.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专利代理机构解散或者被撤销、吊销执业许可证的，应当妥善处理各种尚未办结的专利代理业务。

**Article 15** Where a patent agency is dissolved, or its practice license is revoked or cancelled, it shall properly handle all patent agency businesses that are still pending.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专利代理师应当根据专利代理机构的指派承办专利代理业务，不得自行接受委托。  
专利代理师不得同时在两个以上专利代理机构从事专利代理业务。  
专利代理师对其签名办理的专利代理业务负责。

**Article 16** A patent agent shall undertake patent agency business in accordance with the assignment of the patent agency and shall not accept entrustment on his own.  
A patent agent shall not engage in patent agency business in two or more patent agencies at the same time.  
A patent agent shall be responsible for the patent agency business for which he signs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　专利代理机构和专利代理师对其在执业过程中了解的发明创造的内容，除专利申请已经公布或者公告的以外，负有保守秘密的义务。

**Article 17** A patent agency and a patent agent shall have the obligation to keep confidential the content of inventions and creations learned during their practice, except for those that have been published or announced in patent applications.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　专利代理机构和专利代理师不得以自己的名义申请专利或者请求宣告专利权无效。

**Article 18** A patent agency and a patent agent shall not apply for patents or request invalidation of patent rights in their own names.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院专利行政部门和地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门的工作人员离职后，在法律、行政法规规定的期限内不得从事专利代理工作。  
曾在国务院专利行政部门或者地方人民政府管理专利工作的部门任职的专利代理师，不得对其审查、审理或者处理过的专利申请或专利案件进行代理。

**Article 19** After leaving office, the staff of the Patent Administrative Department of the State Council and the administrative departments for patent affairs of local people's governments shall not engage in patent agency work within the period prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
A patent agent who has previously served in the Patent Administrative Department of the State Council or an administrative department for patent affairs of a local people's government shall not act as an agent for any patent application or patent case that he has examined, tried, or handled.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　专利代理机构收费应当遵循自愿、公平和诚实信用原则，兼顾经济效益和社会效益。  
国家鼓励专利代理机构和专利代理师为小微企业以及无收入或者低收入的发明人、设计人提供专利代理援助服务。

**Article 20** When collecting fees, a patent agency shall follow the principles of voluntariness, fairness, and good faith, and balance economic benefits and social benefits.  
The State encourages patent agencies and patent agents to provide patent agency aid services for small and micro enterprises as well as inventors or designers with no or low income.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　专利代理行业组织应当加强对会员的自律管理，组织开展专利代理师业务培训和职业道德、执业纪律教育，对违反行业自律规范的会员实行惩戒。

**Article 21** Patent agency industry organizations shall strengthen self-discipline management of their members, organize and carry out professional training for patent agents and education on professional ethics and practice discipline, and impose sanctions on members who violate industry self-discipline norms.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院专利行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门应当采取随机抽查等方式，对专利代理机构和专利代理师的执业活动进行检查、监督，发现违反本条例规定的，及时依法予以处理，并向社会公布检查、处理结果。检查不得收取任何费用。

**Article 22** The patent administrative department of the State Council and the departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work shall, through random inspections and other methods, inspect and supervise the practice activities of patent agencies and patent agents. If any violation of these Regulations is discovered, they shall promptly handle it according to law and publish the inspection and handling results to the public. No fees shall be charged for inspection.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院专利行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门应当加强专利代理公共信息发布，为公众了解专利代理机构经营情况、专利代理师执业情况提供查询服务。

**Article 23** The patent administrative department of the State Council and the departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work shall strengthen the publication of public information on patent agency, and provide inquiry services for the public to understand the business operations of patent agencies and the practice status of patent agents.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　以隐瞒真实情况、弄虚作假手段取得专利代理机构执业许可证、专利代理师资格证的，由国务院专利行政部门撤销专利代理机构执业许可证、专利代理师资格证。  
专利代理机构取得执业许可证后，因情况变化不再符合本条例规定的条件的，由国务院专利行政部门责令限期整改；逾期未改正或者整改不合格的，撤销执业许可证。

**Article 24** Where a patent agency practice license or a patent agent qualification certificate is obtained by concealing the true situation or resorting to deception, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall revoke the patent agency practice license or the patent agent qualification certificate.  
After a patent agency obtains a practice license, if due to changes in circumstances it no longer meets the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit or the correction is not satisfactory, the practice license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　专利代理机构有下列行为之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门责令限期改正，予以警告，可以处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重或者逾期未改正的，由国务院专利行政部门责令停止承接新的专利代理业务6个月至12个月，直至吊销专利代理机构执业许可证：  
(一)合伙人、股东或者法定代表人等事项发生变化未办理变更手续；  
(二)就同一专利申请或者专利权的事务接受有利益冲突的其他当事人的委托；  
(三)指派专利代理师承办与其本人或者其近亲属有利益冲突的专利代理业务；  
(四)泄露委托人的发明创造内容，或者以自己的名义申请专利或请求宣告专利权无效；  
(五)疏于管理，造成严重后果。  
专业代理机构在执业过程中泄露委托人的发明创造内容，涉及泄露国家秘密、侵犯商业秘密的，或者向有关行政、司法机关的工作人员行贿，提供虚假证据的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定承担法律责任；由国务院专利行政部门吊销专利代理机构执业许可证。

**Article 25** Where a patent agency commits any of the following acts, the department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, issue a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall order it to suspend undertaking new patent agency business for a period of 6 to 12 months, up to revoking the patent agency practice license:  
(1) Failing to go through the modification procedures when matters such as partners, shareholders, or legal representatives change;  
(2) Accepting the entrustment of other parties with conflicting interests in the same patent application or patent right matter;  
(3) Assigning a patent agent to handle patent agency business that has a conflict of interest with the patent agent himself or his close relatives;  
(4) Disclosing the client's invention and creation content, or applying for a patent or requesting a declaration of patent invalidity in its own name;  
(5) Poor management causing serious consequences.  
Where a patent agency, in the course of practice, discloses the client's invention and creation content, which involves divulging state secrets or infringing trade secrets, or bribes the staff of relevant administrative or judicial organs, or provides false evidence, it shall assume legal liability in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; the patent administrative department of the State Council shall revoke the patent agency practice license.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　专利代理师有下列行为之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门责令限期改正，予以警告，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重或者逾期未改正的，由国务院专利行政部门责令停止承办新的专利代理业务6个月至12个月，直至吊销专利代理师资格证：  
(一)未依照本条例规定进行备案；  
(二)自行接受委托办理专利代理业务；  
(三)同时在两个以上专利代理机构从事专利代理业务；  
(四)违反本条例规定对其审查、审理或者处理过的专利申请或专利案件进行代理；  
(五)泄露委托人的发明创造内容，或者以自己的名义申请专利或请求宣告专利权无效。  
专利代理师在执业过程中泄露委托人的发明创造内容，涉及泄露国家秘密、侵犯商业秘密的，或者向有关行政、司法机关的工作人员行贿，提供虚假证据的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定承担法律责任；由国务院专利行政部门吊销专利代理师资格证。

**Article 26** Where a patent agent commits any of the following acts, the department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, issue a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious or it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the patent administrative department of the State Council shall order it to suspend undertaking new patent agency business for a period of 6 to 12 months, up to revoking the patent agent qualification certificate:  
(1) Failing to file for the record in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) Accepting entrustment on its own to handle patent agency business;  
(3) Engaging in patent agency business in two or more patent agencies at the same time;  
(4) Handling patent applications or patent cases that it has examined, tried, or processed in violation of these Regulations;  
(5) Disclosing the client's invention and creation content, or applying for a patent or requesting a declaration of patent invalidity in its own name.  
Where a patent agent, in the course of practice, discloses the client's invention and creation content, which involves divulging state secrets or infringing trade secrets, or bribes the staff of relevant administrative or judicial organs, or provides false evidence, it shall assume legal liability in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; the patent administrative department of the State Council shall revoke the patent agent qualification certificate.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定擅自开展专利代理业务的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, carries out patent agency business without authorization, the department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work shall order it to cease the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院专利行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府管理专利工作的部门的工作人员违反本条例规定，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where a staff member of the patent administrative department of the State Council or the department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for patent-related work violates these Regulations, abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　外国专利代理机构在中华人民共和国境内设立常驻代表机构，须经国务院专利行政部门批准。

**Article 29** A foreign patent agency that establishes a permanent representative office within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall obtain approval from the patent administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　律师事务所可以依据《中华人民共和国律师法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律、行政法规开展与专利有关的业务，但从事代理专利申请、宣告专利权无效业务应当遵守本条例规定，具体办法由国务院专利行政部门商国务院司法行政部门另行制定。

**Article 30** Law firms may, in accordance with the Lawyers Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and administrative regulations, carry out business related to patents. However, engaging in the agency of patent applications and declarations of patent invalidity shall comply with the provisions of these Regulations. Specific measures shall be separately formulated by the patent administrative department of the State Council in consultation with the judicial administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　代理国防专利事务的专利代理机构和专利代理师的管理办法，由国务院专利行政部门商国家国防专利机构主管机关另行制定。

**Article 31** The administrative measures for a patent agency and a patent agent handling defense patent affairs shall be separately formulated by the patent administration department of the State Council in consultation with the competent authority of the national defense patent institution.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自2019年3月1日起施行。  
本条例施行前依法设立的专利代理机构以及依法执业的专利代理人，在本条例施行后可以继续以专利代理机构、专利代理师的名义开展专利代理业务。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2019.  
A patent agency established according to law and a patent agent practicing according to law before these Regulations come into force may continue to carry out patent agency business in the name of a patent agency and a patent agent after these Regulations come into force.
