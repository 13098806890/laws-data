# 最高人民法院关于审理专利授权确权行政案件适用法律若干问题的规定（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕8号  
**公布日期**：2020-08-24  
**生效日期**：2020-09-12

> 《最高人民法院关于审理专利授权确权行政案件适用法律若干问题的规定（一）》已于2020年8月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1810次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年9月12日起施行。 2020年9月10日 最高人民法院 关于审理专利授权确权行政案件 适用法律若干问题的规定（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称专利授权行政案件，是指专利申请人因不服国务院专利行政部门作出的专利复审请求审查决定，向人民法院提起诉讼的案件。  
本规定所称专利确权行政案件，是指专利权人或者无效宣告请求人因不服国务院专利行政部门作出的专利无效宣告请求审查决定，向人民法院提起诉讼的案件。  
本规定所称被诉决定，是指国务院专利行政部门作出的专利复审请求审查决定、专利无效宣告请求审查决定。

**Article 1** For the purpose of these Provisions, the term "administrative cases concerning patent grant" refers to cases in which a patent applicant, dissatisfied with a patent reexamination request review decision made by the patent administrative department of the State Council, files a prosecution with a People's Court.  
For the purpose of these Provisions, the term "administrative cases concerning patent confirmation" refers to cases in which a patent holder or an invalidation requester, dissatisfied with a patent invalidation request review decision made by the patent administrative department of the State Council, files a prosecution with a People's Court.  
For the purpose of these Provisions, the term "the sued decision" refers to the patent reexamination request review decision or patent invalidation request review decision made by the patent administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院应当以所属技术领域的技术人员在阅读权利要求书、说明书及附图后所理解的通常含义，界定权利要求的用语。权利要求的用语在说明书及附图中有明确定义或者说明的，按照其界定。  
依照前款规定不能界定的，可以结合所属技术领域的技术人员通常采用的技术词典、技术手册、工具书、教科书、国家或者行业技术标准等界定。

**Article 2** A People's Court shall define the terms of a claim by their ordinary meaning as understood by a person skilled in the relevant technical field after reading the claims, specification and drawings. If a term of a claim is explicitly defined or explained in the specification and drawings, it shall be defined accordingly.  
If the definition cannot be made under the preceding paragraph, the definition may be made with reference to technical dictionaries, technical manuals, reference books, textbooks, national or industry technical standards commonly used by a person skilled in the relevant technical field.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院在专利确权行政案件中界定权利要求的用语时，可以参考已被专利侵权民事案件生效裁判采纳的专利权人的相关陈述。

**Article 3** When defining the terms of a claim in an administrative case concerning patent confirmation, a People's Court may refer to the relevant statements of the patent holder that have been adopted in a legally effective judgment of a civil case concerning patent infringement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　权利要求书、说明书及附图中的语法、文字、数字、标点、图形、符号等有明显错误或者歧义，但所属技术领域的技术人员通过阅读权利要求书、说明书及附图可以得出唯一理解的，人民法院应当根据该唯一理解作出认定。

**Article 4** If there are obvious errors or ambiguities in grammar, wording, figures, punctuation, drawings, symbols, etc., in the claims, specification and drawings, but a person skilled in the relevant technical field, after reading the claims, specification and drawings, can reach a single understanding, the People's Court shall make a determination based on such single understanding.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人有证据证明专利申请人、专利权人违反诚实信用原则，虚构、编造说明书及附图中的具体实施方式、技术效果以及数据、图表等有关技术内容，并据此主张相关权利要求不符合专利法有关规定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** If a party has evidence proving that the patent applicant or patent holder, in violation of the principle of good faith, fabricated or made up the specific embodiments, technical effects, data, charts and other relevant technical content in the specification and drawings, and accordingly claims that the relevant claim does not comply with the relevant provisions of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall uphold such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　说明书未充分公开特定技术内容，导致在专利申请日有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定说明书及与该特定技术内容相关的权利要求不符合[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第三款的规定：  
（一）权利要求限定的技术方案不能实施的；  
（二）实施权利要求限定的技术方案不能解决发明或者实用新型所要解决的技术问题的；  
（三）确认权利要求限定的技术方案能够解决发明或者实用新型所要解决的技术问题，需要付出过度劳动的。  
当事人仅依据前款规定的未充分公开的特定技术内容，主张与该特定技术内容相关的权利要求符合[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第四款关于“权利要求书应当以说明书为依据”的规定的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 6** If the specification does not sufficiently disclose a specific technical content, resulting in any of the following circumstances as of the patent application date, the People's Court shall determine that the specification and the claim relating to such specific technical content do not comply with Article 26, Paragraph 3 of the Patent Law:  
(1) The technical solution defined in the claim cannot be implemented;  
(2) Implementing the technical solution defined in the claim cannot solve the technical problem to be solved by the invention or utility model;  
(3) Confirming that the technical solution defined in the claim can solve the technical problem to be solved by the invention or utility model requires undue effort.  
If a party, solely on the basis of the insufficiently disclosed specific technical content as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, claims that the claim relating to such specific technical content complies with the provision of Article 26, Paragraph 4 of the Patent Law that "the claims shall be supported by the specification," the People's Court shall not uphold such claim.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　所属技术领域的技术人员根据说明书及附图，认为权利要求有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定该权利要求不符合[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第四款关于清楚地限定要求专利保护的范围的规定：  
（一）限定的发明主题类型不明确的；  
（二）不能合理确定权利要求中技术特征的含义的；  
（三）技术特征之间存在明显矛盾且无法合理解释的。

**Article 7** If, according to the specification and drawings, a person skilled in the relevant technical field believes that the claim has any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that the claim does not comply with the provision of Article 26, Paragraph 4 of the Patent Law regarding clearly defining the scope of patent protection sought:  
(1) The type of the invention subject matter defined is unclear;  
(2) The meaning of the technical features in the claim cannot be reasonably determined;  
(3) There is an obvious contradiction between the technical features that cannot be reasonably explained.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　所属技术领域的技术人员阅读说明书及附图后，在申请日不能得到或者合理概括得出权利要求限定的技术方案的，人民法院应当认定该权利要求不符合[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第四款关于“权利要求书应当以说明书为依据”的规定。

**Article 8** If, after reading the specification and drawings, a person skilled in the relevant technical field cannot derive or reasonably generalize the technical solution defined in the claim as of the application date, the People's Court shall determine that the claim does not comply with the provision of Article 26, Paragraph 4 of the Patent Law that "the claims shall be supported by the specification."

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　以功能或者效果限定的技术特征，是指对于结构、组分、步骤、条件等技术特征或者技术特征之间的相互关系等，仅通过其在发明创造中所起的功能或者效果进行限定的技术特征，但所属技术领域的技术人员通过阅读权利要求即可直接、明确地确定实现该功能或者效果的具体实施方式的除外。  
对于前款规定的以功能或者效果限定的技术特征，权利要求书、说明书及附图未公开能够实现该功能或者效果的任何具体实施方式的，人民法院应当认定说明书和具有该技术特征的权利要求不符合[专利法第二十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-26)第三款的规定。

**Article 9** A technical feature limited by function or effect refers to a technical feature that defines a structure, component, step, condition, or the relationship between technical features only by the function or effect it performs in the invention-creation, except where a person skilled in the relevant technical field can directly and clearly determine the specific embodiment for achieving such function or effect upon reading the claims.  
With respect to a technical feature limited by function or effect as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, if the claims, specification and drawings do not disclose any specific embodiment that can achieve such function or effect, the People's Court shall determine that the specification and the claim containing such technical feature do not comply with Article 26, Paragraph 3 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　药品专利申请人在申请日以后提交补充实验数据，主张依赖该数据证明专利申请符合[专利法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-22)第三款、第二十六条第三款等规定的，人民法院应予审查。

**Article 10** If a pharmaceutical patent applicant submits supplementary experimental data after the application date and claims to rely on such data to prove that the patent application complies with Article 22, Paragraph 3, Article 26, Paragraph 3, or other relevant provisions of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall examine such data.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人对实验数据的真实性产生争议的，提交实验数据的一方当事人应当举证证明实验数据的来源和形成过程。人民法院可以通知实验负责人到庭，就实验原料、步骤、条件、环境或者参数以及完成实验的人员、机构等作出说明。

**Article 11** If a dispute arises between parties over the authenticity of experimental data, the party submitting the experimental data shall provide evidence to prove the source and formation process of the experimental data. The People's Court may notify the person in charge of the experiment to appear in court to give explanations on the experimental materials, steps, conditions, environment or parameters, as well as the personnel and institutions that conducted the experiment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院确定权利要求限定的技术方案的技术领域，应当综合考虑主题名称等权利要求的全部内容、说明书关于技术领域和背景技术的记载，以及该技术方案所实现的功能和用途等。

**Article 12** When determining the technical field of the technical solution defined in the claim, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the entire content of the claim including the subject-matter title, the description of the technical field and background technology in the specification, as well as the functions and uses achieved by such technical solution.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　说明书及附图未明确记载区别技术特征在权利要求限定的技术方案中所能达到的技术效果的，人民法院可以结合所属技术领域的公知常识，根据区别技术特征与权利要求中其他技术特征的关系，区别技术特征在权利要求限定的技术方案中的作用等，认定所属技术领域的技术人员所能确定的该权利要求实际解决的技术问题。  
被诉决定对权利要求实际解决的技术问题未认定或者认定错误的，不影响人民法院对权利要求的创造性依法作出认定。

**Article 13** If the specification and drawings do not explicitly record the technical effect that can be achieved by the distinguishing technical feature in the technical solution defined in the claim, the People's Court may, in combination with common general knowledge in the relevant technical field, determine the actual technical problem solved by the claim that can be identified by a person skilled in the relevant technical field, based on the relationship between the distinguishing technical feature and other technical features in the claim, the role of the distinguishing technical feature in the technical solution defined in the claim, etc.  
If the sued decision fails to determine or erroneously determines the actual technical problem solved by the claim, it shall not affect the People's Court's determination on the inventiveness of the claim according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院认定外观设计专利产品的一般消费者所具有的知识水平和认知能力，应当考虑申请日时外观设计专利产品的设计空间。设计空间较大的，人民法院可以认定一般消费者通常不容易注意到不同设计之间的较小区别；设计空间较小的，人民法院可以认定一般消费者通常更容易注意到不同设计之间的较小区别。  
对于前款所称设计空间的认定，人民法院可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）产品的功能、用途；  
（二）现有设计的整体状况；  
（三）惯常设计；  
（四）法律、行政法规的强制性规定；  
（五）国家、行业技术标准；  
（六）需要考虑的其他因素。

**Article 14** When determining the knowledge level and cognitive ability of the ordinary consumer of a design patent product, the People's Court shall take into account the design space of the design patent product as of the application date. If the design space is relatively large, the People's Court may determine that the ordinary consumer is generally not likely to notice minor differences between different designs; if the design space is relatively small, the People's Court may determine that the ordinary consumer is generally more likely to notice minor differences between different designs.  
In determining the design space referred to in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) The function and use of the product;  
(2) The overall state of the prior designs;  
(3) Commonplace designs;  
(4) Mandatory provisions of laws and administrative regulations;  
(5) National or industry technical standards;  
(6) Other factors that need to be considered.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外观设计的图片、照片存在矛盾、缺失或者模糊不清等情形，导致一般消费者无法根据图片、照片及简要说明确定所要保护的外观设计的，人民法院应当认定其不符合[专利法第二十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-27)第二款关于“清楚地显示要求专利保护的产品的外观设计”的规定。

**Article 15** If the drawings or photographs of a design contain contradictions, omissions, or are unclear, such that the ordinary consumer cannot determine the design to be protected based on the drawings, photographs and brief explanation, the People's Court shall determine that it does not comply with the provision of Article 27, Paragraph 2 of the Patent Law regarding "clearly showing the design of the product for which patent protection is sought."

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院认定外观设计是否符合[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)的规定，应当综合判断外观设计的整体视觉效果。  
为实现特定技术功能必须具备或者仅有有限选择的设计特征，对于外观设计专利视觉效果的整体观察和综合判断不具有显著影响。

**Article 16** When determining whether a design complies with Article 23 of the Patent Law, the People's Court shall make a comprehensive judgment on the overall visual effect of the design.  
Design features that are necessary to achieve a specific technical function or have only limited options shall not have a significant impact on the overall observation and comprehensive judgment of the visual effect of the design patent.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外观设计与相同或者相近种类产品的一项现有设计相比，整体视觉效果相同或者属于仅具有局部细微区别等实质相同的情形的，人民法院应当认定其构成[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第一款规定的“属于现有设计”。  
除前款规定的情形外，外观设计与相同或者相近种类产品的一项现有设计相比，二者的区别对整体视觉效果不具有显著影响的，人民法院应当认定其不具有[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第二款规定的“明显区别”。  
人民法院应当根据外观设计产品的用途，认定产品种类是否相同或者相近。确定产品的用途，可以参考外观设计的简要说明、外观设计产品分类表、产品的功能以及产品销售、实际使用的情况等因素。

**Article 17** If a design, compared with a prior design of the same or similar kind of product, is identical in overall visual effect or falls under a substantially identical situation such as only having minor local differences, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes "being a prior design" as specified in Article 23, Paragraph 1 of the Patent Law.  
Except for the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, if the difference between a design and a prior design of the same or similar kind of product does not have a significant impact on the overall visual effect, the People's Court shall determine that it lacks "obvious difference" as specified in Article 23, Paragraph 2 of the Patent Law.  
The People's Court shall determine whether the product types are the same or similar based on the use of the design product. In determining the use of the product, reference may be made to factors such as the brief explanation of the design, the classification table of design products, the function of the product, and the sales and actual use of the product.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外观设计专利与相同种类产品上同日申请的另一项外观设计专利相比，整体视觉效果相同或者属于仅具有局部细微区别等实质相同的情形的，人民法院应当认定其不符合[专利法第九条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-9)关于“同样的发明创造只能授予一项专利权”的规定。

**Article 18** If a design patent, compared with another design patent filed on the same day for the same kind of product, is identical in overall visual effect or falls under a substantially identical situation such as only having minor local differences, the People's Court shall determine that it does not comply with the provision of Article 9 of the Patent Law that "only one patent right shall be granted for the same invention-creation."

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外观设计与申请日以前提出申请、申请日以后公告，且属于相同或者相近种类产品的另一项外观设计相比，整体视觉效果相同或者属于仅具有局部细微区别等实质相同的情形的，人民法院应当认定其构成[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第一款规定的“同样的外观设计”。

**Article 19** If a design, compared with another design that was filed before the application date but published after the application date, and belongs to the same or similar kind of product, is identical in overall visual effect or falls under a substantially identical situation such as only having minor local differences, the People's Court shall determine that it constitutes "the same design" as specified in Article 23, Paragraph 1 of the Patent Law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　根据现有设计整体上给出的设计启示，以一般消费者容易想到的设计特征转用、拼合或者替换等方式，获得与外观设计专利的整体视觉效果相同或者仅具有局部细微区别等实质相同的外观设计，且不具有独特视觉效果的，人民法院应当认定该外观设计专利与现有设计特征的组合相比不具有[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第二款规定的“明显区别”。  
具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定存在前款所称的设计启示：  
（一）将相同种类产品上不同部分的设计特征进行拼合或者替换的；  
（二）现有设计公开了将特定种类产品的设计特征转用于外观设计专利产品的；  
（三）现有设计公开了将不同的特定种类产品的外观设计特征进行拼合的；  
（四）将现有设计中的图案直接或者仅做细微改变后用于外观设计专利产品的；  
（五）将单一自然物的特征转用于外观设计专利产品的；  
（六）单纯采用基本几何形状或者仅做细微改变后得到外观设计的；  
（七）使用一般消费者公知的建筑物、作品、标识等的全部或者部分设计的。

**Article 20** If, based on the design inspiration given by the prior designs as a whole, an appearance design is obtained by means of transferring, combining or replacing design features that are readily conceivable by the ordinary consumer, resulting in a design that is identical in overall visual effect to the design patent or falls under a substantially identical situation such as only having minor local differences, and does not have a unique visual effect, the People's Court shall determine that such design patent does not have "obvious difference" compared with the combination of prior design features as specified in Article 23, Paragraph 2 of the Patent Law.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may determine that there is design inspiration as referred to in the preceding paragraph:  
(1) Combining or replacing design features from different parts of a product of the same kind;  
(2) The prior designs disclose the transfer of design features of a specific kind of product to the design patent product;  
(3) The prior designs disclose the combination of design features from different specific kinds of products;  
(4) Using a pattern from the prior designs directly or with only minor modifications on the design patent product;  
(5) Transferring the features of a single natural object to the design patent product;  
(6) Simply adopting basic geometric shapes or obtaining the design with only minor modifications;  
(7) Using all or part of the designs of buildings, works, logos, etc., that are well known to the ordinary consumer.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院在认定[本规定第二十条](#art-20)所称的独特视觉效果时，可以综合考虑下列因素：  
（一）外观设计专利产品的设计空间；  
（二）产品种类的关联度；  
（三）转用、拼合、替换的设计特征的数量和难易程度；  
（四）需要考虑的其他因素。

**Article 21** When determining the unique visual effect referred to in Article 20 of these Provisions, the People's Court may comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) The design space of the design patent product;  
(2) The relevance of the product types;  
(3) The number and difficulty of the design features transferred, combined or replaced;  
(4) Other factors that need to be considered.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[专利法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-23)第三款所称的“合法权利”，包括就作品、商标、地理标志、姓名、企业名称、肖像，以及有一定影响的商品名称、包装、装潢等享有的合法权利或者权益。

**Article 22** The term "lawful rights" as used in Article 23, Paragraph 3 of the Patent Law includes lawful rights or interests in works, trademarks, geographical indications, names, enterprise names, portraits, as well as well-known product names, packaging, and ornamentation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人主张专利复审、无效宣告请求审查程序中的下列情形属于[行政诉讼法第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-70)第三项规定的“违反法定程序的”，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）遗漏当事人提出的理由和证据，且对当事人权利产生实质性影响的；  
（二）未依法通知应当参加审查程序的专利申请人、专利权人及无效宣告请求人等，对其权利产生实质性影响的；  
（三）未向当事人告知合议组组成人员，且合议组组成人员存在法定回避事由而未回避的；  
（四）未给予被诉决定对其不利的一方当事人针对被诉决定所依据的理由、证据和认定的事实陈述意见的机会的；  
（五）主动引入当事人未主张的公知常识或者惯常设计，未听取当事人意见且对当事人权利产生实质性影响的；  
（六）其他违反法定程序，可能对当事人权利产生实质性影响的。

**Article 23** If a party claims that any of the following circumstances in the patent reexamination or invalidation request review procedure constitutes "violation of statutory procedures" as specified in Item (3) of Article 70 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court shall uphold such claim:  
(1) Omitting reasons and evidence raised by the party, and having a substantive impact on the party's rights;  
(2) Failing to notify, according to law, the patent applicant, patent holder, invalidation requester, or other persons who should participate in the review procedure, thereby having a substantive impact on their rights;  
(3) Failing to inform the party of the composition of the collegial panel, and a member of the collegial panel has a statutory cause for withdrawal but fails to withdraw;  
(4) Failing to provide the party against whom the sued decision is adverse with an opportunity to state its opinions on the reasons, evidence and facts found on which the sued decision is based;  
(5) Actively introducing common general knowledge or commonplace design that the party did not assert, without hearing the party's opinions, and having a substantive impact on the party's rights;  
(6) Other violations of statutory procedures that may have a substantive impact on the party's rights.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　被诉决定有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以依照[行政诉讼法第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-70)的规定，判决部分撤销：  
（一）被诉决定对于权利要求书中的部分权利要求的认定错误，其余正确的；  
（二）被诉决定对于[专利法第三十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国专利法.md#art-31)第二款规定的“一件外观设计专利申请”中的部分外观设计认定错误，其余正确的；  
（三）其他可以判决部分撤销的情形。

**Article 24** If the sued decision falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, in accordance with Article 70 of the Administrative Procedure Law, render a judgment of partial revocation:  
(1) The sued decision is erroneous in its determination of some claims in the claims, while the rest are correct;  
(2) The sued decision is erroneous in its determination of some designs in a "single design patent application" as specified in Article 31, Paragraph 2 of the Patent Law, while the rest are correct;  
(3) Other circumstances where a judgment of partial revocation may be rendered.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被诉决定对当事人主张的全部无效理由和证据均已评述并宣告权利要求无效，人民法院认为被诉决定认定该权利要求无效的理由均不能成立的，应当判决撤销或者部分撤销该决定，并可视情判决被告就该权利要求重新作出审查决定。

**Article 25** If the sued decision has reviewed and adjudicated all invalidation grounds and evidence asserted by the party and declared the claim invalid, and the People's Court considers that none of the reasons for the sued decision's determination that the claim is invalid can be sustained, the People's Court shall render a judgment revoking or partially revoking the decision, and may, as the case may be, order the defendant to make a new review decision on such claim.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　审查决定系直接依据生效裁判重新作出且未引入新的事实和理由，当事人对该决定提起诉讼的，人民法院依法裁定不予受理；已经受理的，依法裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 26** If a review decision is directly made anew based on a legally effective judgment and does not introduce new facts or reasons, and a party files a prosecution against such decision, the People's Court shall, according to law, rule not to accept the case; if the case has already been accepted, the People's Court shall, according to law, rule to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被诉决定查明事实或者适用法律确有不当，但对专利授权确权的认定结论正确的，人民法院可以在纠正相关事实查明和法律适用的基础上判决驳回原告的诉讼请求。

**Article 27** If the sued decision is indeed improper in finding facts or applying law, but the conclusion on the determination of patent grant or confirmation is correct, the People's Court may, on the basis of correcting the relevant factual findings and legal application, render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人主张有关技术内容属于公知常识或者有关设计特征属于惯常设计的，人民法院可以要求其提供证据证明或者作出说明。

**Article 28** If a party claims that certain technical content constitutes common general knowledge or that certain design features constitute commonplace design, the People's Court may require the party to provide evidence or give an explanation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　专利申请人、专利权人在专利授权确权行政案件中提供新的证据，用于证明专利申请不应当被驳回或者专利权应当维持有效的，人民法院一般应予审查。

**Article 29** If a patent applicant or patent holder provides new evidence in an administrative case concerning patent grant or confirmation to prove that the patent application should not be rejected or that the patent right should be maintained valid, the People's Court shall generally examine such evidence.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　无效宣告请求人在专利确权行政案件中提供新的证据，人民法院一般不予审查，但下列证据除外：  
（一）证明在专利无效宣告请求审查程序中已主张的公知常识或者惯常设计的；  
（二）证明所属技术领域的技术人员或者一般消费者的知识水平和认知能力的；  
（三）证明外观设计专利产品的设计空间或者现有设计的整体状况的；  
（四）补强在专利无效宣告请求审查程序中已被采信证据的证明力的；  
（五）反驳其他当事人在诉讼中提供的证据的。

**Article 30** If an invalidation requester provides new evidence in an administrative case concerning patent confirmation, the People's Court shall generally not examine such evidence, except for the following evidence:  
(1) Evidence proving common general knowledge or commonplace design that had been asserted in the patent invalidation request review procedure;  
(2) Evidence proving the knowledge level and cognitive ability of a person skilled in the relevant technical field or an ordinary consumer;  
(3) Evidence proving the design space of the design patent product or the overall state of the prior designs;  
(4) Evidence reinforcing the probative force of evidence that has been admitted in the patent invalidation request review procedure;  
(5) Evidence rebutting evidence provided by other parties in the litigation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院可以要求当事人提供[本规定第二十九条](#art-29)、第三十条规定的新的证据。  
当事人向人民法院提供的证据系其在专利复审、无效宣告请求审查程序中被依法要求提供但无正当理由未提供的，人民法院一般不予采纳。

**Article 31** The People's Court may require a party to provide the new evidence specified in Articles 29 and 30 of these Provisions.  
If the evidence provided by a party to the People's Court is evidence that the party was required according to law to provide in the patent reexamination or invalidation request review procedure but failed to provide without justifiable reasons, the People's Court shall generally not admit such evidence.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本规定自2020年9月12日起施行。  
本规定施行后，人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件适用本规定；施行前已经作出生效裁判的案件，不适用本规定再审。

**Article 32** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 12, 2020.  
After these Provisions come into force, cases of first instance and second instance that are being tried by the People's Court shall be governed by these Provisions; cases in which a legally effective judgment has already been rendered before these Provisions come into force shall not be governed by these Provisions in retrial.
