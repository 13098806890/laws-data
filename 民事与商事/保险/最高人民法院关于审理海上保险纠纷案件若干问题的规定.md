# 最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕10号  
**公布日期**：2006-11-23  
**生效日期**：2007-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2006年11月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1405次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年1月1日起施行。 2006年11月23日 最高人民法院 关于审理海上保险纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2006〕10号 （2006年11月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1405次会议通过　2006年11月23日最高人民法院公告公布　自2007年1月1日起施行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　审理海上保险合同纠纷案件，适用海商法的规定；海商法没有规定的，适用保险法的有关规定；海商法、保险法均没有规定的，适用合同法等其他相关法律的规定。

**Article 1** In adjudicating disputes over marine insurance contracts, the provisions of the Maritime Code shall apply; where the Maritime Code has no provisions, the relevant provisions of the Insurance Law shall apply; where neither the Maritime Code nor the Insurance Law has provisions, the Contract Law and other relevant laws shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　审理非因海上事故引起的港口设施或者码头作为保险标的的保险合同纠纷案件，适用保险法等法律的规定。

**Article 2** In adjudicating disputes over insurance contracts where the subject matter of insurance is port facilities or wharves not caused by maritime accidents, the provisions of the Insurance Law and other laws shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　审理保险人因发生船舶触碰港口设施或者码头等保险事故，行使代位请求赔偿权利向造成保险事故的第三人追偿的案件，适用海商法的规定。

**Article 3** In adjudicating cases where an insurer, due to an insurance accident such as a ship colliding with port facilities or a wharf, exercises the right of subrogation to recover from the third party that caused the insurance accident, the provisions of the Maritime Code shall apply.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保险人知道被保险人未如实告知[海商法第二百二十二条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-222)第一款规定的重要情况，仍收取保险费或者支付保险赔偿，保险人又以被保险人未如实告知重要情况为由请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** Where the insurer knew that the insured failed to truthfully disclose material circumstances as specified in Article 222, paragraph 1 of the Maritime Code, yet still collected insurance premiums or paid insurance indemnity, and subsequently requests dissolution of the contract on the grounds that the insured failed to truthfully disclose material circumstances, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　被保险人未按照[海商法第二百三十四条](../../民事与商事/海事与运输/中华人民共和国海商法.md#art-234)的规定向保险人支付约定的保险费的，保险责任开始前，保险人有权解除保险合同，但保险人已经签发保险单证的除外；保险责任开始后，保险人以被保险人未支付保险费请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** Where the insured fails to pay the agreed insurance premium to the insurer in accordance with Article 234 of the Maritime Code, before the commencement of insurance liability, the insurer has the right to dissolve the insurance contract, except where the insurer has already issued an insurance certificate; after the commencement of insurance liability, where the insurer requests dissolution of the contract on the grounds that the insured failed to pay the insurance premium, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保险人以被保险人违反合同约定的保证条款未立即书面通知保险人为由，要求从违反保证条款之日起解除保险合同的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 6** Where the insurer requests dissolution of the insurance contract from the date of violation of the warranty clause on the grounds that the insured violated the warranty clause as agreed in the contract and failed to immediately notify the insurer in writing, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保险人收到被保险人违反合同约定的保证条款书面通知后仍支付保险赔偿，又以被保险人违反合同约定的保证条款为由请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** Where the insurer still pays insurance indemnity after receiving written notice from the insured of violation of the warranty clause as agreed in the contract, and subsequently requests dissolution of the contract on the grounds that the insured violated the warranty clause, the People's Court shall not support the request.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保险人收到被保险人违反合同约定的保证条款的书面通知后，就修改承保条件、增加保险费等事项与被保险人协商未能达成一致的，保险合同于违反保证条款之日解除。

**Article 8** Where the insurer, after receiving written notice from the insured of violation of the warranty clause as agreed in the contract, fails to reach an agreement with the insured on matters such as modifying underwriting conditions or increasing insurance premiums, the insurance contract shall be dissolved on the date of the violation of the warranty clause.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在航次之中发生船舶转让的，未经保险人同意转让的船舶保险合同至航次终了时解除。船舶转让时起至航次终了时止的船舶保险合同的权利、义务由船舶出让人享有、承担，也可以由船舶受让人继受。  
船舶受让人根据前款规定向保险人请求赔偿时，应当提交有效的保险单证及船舶转让合同的证明。

**Article 9** Where a ship is transferred during a voyage, if the transfer is not consented to by the insurer, the ship insurance contract shall be dissolved upon the completion of the voyage. The rights and obligations under the ship insurance contract from the time of the ship transfer until the completion of the voyage shall be enjoyed and borne by the ship transferor, and may also be assumed by the ship transferee.  
Where a ship transferee claims compensation from the insurer in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall submit a valid insurance certificate and proof of the ship transfer contract.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　保险人与被保险人在订立保险合同时均不知道保险标的已经发生保险事故而遭受损失，或者保险标的已经不可能因发生保险事故而遭受损失的，不影响保险合同的效力。

**Article 10** Where, at the time of concluding the insurance contract, neither the insurer nor the insured knew that the subject matter of insurance had suffered a loss due to the occurrence of an insured accident, or that the subject matter of insurance could no longer suffer a loss due to an insured accident, the validity of the insurance contract shall not be affected.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海上货物运输中因承运人无正本提单交付货物造成的损失不属于保险人的保险责任范围。保险合同当事人另有约定的，依约定。

**Article 11** Losses caused by the carrier's delivery of goods without the original bill of lading in maritime cargo transportation shall not fall within the scope of the insurer's insurance liability. If the parties to the insurance contract have agreed otherwise, such agreement shall prevail.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　发生保险事故后，被保险人为防止或者减少损失而采取的合理措施没有效果，要求保险人支付由此产生的合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** After the occurrence of an insured accident, where the insured takes reasonable measures to prevent or mitigate the loss but such measures prove ineffective, and the insured requests the insurer to pay the reasonable expenses incurred thereby, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　保险人在行使代位请求赔偿权利时，未依照海事诉讼特别程序法的规定，向人民法院提交其已经向被保险人实际支付保险赔偿凭证的，人民法院不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 13** Where an insurer, when exercising the right of subrogation, fails to submit to the People's Court the certificate of actual payment of insurance indemnity to the insured in accordance with the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law, the People's Court shall not accept the case; if the case has already been accepted, it shall rule to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　受理保险人行使代位请求赔偿权利纠纷案件的人民法院应当仅就造成保险事故的第三人与被保险人之间的法律关系进行审理。

**Article 14** The People's Court that accepts a dispute case concerning the insurer's exercise of the right of subrogation shall only adjudicate the legal relationship between the third party that caused the insured accident and the insured.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　保险人取得代位请求赔偿权利后，以被保险人向第三人提起诉讼、提交仲裁、申请扣押船舶或者第三人同意履行义务为由主张诉讼时效中断的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where the insurer, after acquiring the right of subrogation, claims that the limitation of action is interrupted on the grounds that the insured has initiated a prosecution against the third party, submitted to arbitration, applied for arrest of the ship, or the third party has agreed to perform its obligations, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保险人取得代位请求赔偿权利后，主张享有被保险人因申请扣押船舶取得的担保权利的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where the insurer, after acquiring the right of subrogation, claims to enjoy the security rights obtained by the insured from applying for the arrest of the ship, the People's Court shall support the claim.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定自2007年1月1日起施行。

**Article 17** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2007.
