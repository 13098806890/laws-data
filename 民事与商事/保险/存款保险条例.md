# 存款保险条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2015-02-17  
**生效日期**：2015-05-01

> (2014年10月29日国务院第67次常务会议通过　2015年2月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第660号公布　自2015年5月1日起施行) 第一条　为了建立和规范存款保险制度，依法保护存款人的合法权益，及时防范和化解金融风险，维护金融稳定，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内设立的商业银行、农村合作银行、农村信用合作社等吸收存款的银行业金融机构(以下统称投保机构)，应当依照本条例的规定投保存款保险。 投保机构在中华人民共和国境外设立的分支机构，以及外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的分支机构不适用前款规定。但是，中华人民共和国与其他国家或者地区之间对存款保险制度另有安排的除外。 第三条　本条例所称存款保险，是指投保机构向存款保险基金管理机构交纳保费，形成存款保险基金，存款保险基金管理机构依照本条例的规定向存款人偿付被保险存款，并采取必要措施维护存款以及存款保险基金安全的制度。 第四条　被保险存款包括投保机构吸收的人民币存款和外币存款。但是，金融机构同业存款、投保机构的高级管理人员在本投保机构的存款以及存款保险基金管理机构规定不予保险的其他存款除外。 第五条　存款保险实行限额偿付，最高偿付限额为人民币50万元。中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门可以根据经济发展、存款结构变化、金融风险状况等因素调整最高偿付限额，报国务院批准后公布执行。 同一存款人在同一家投保机构所有被保险存款账户的存款本金和利息合并计算的资金数额在最高偿付限额以内的，实行全额偿付；超出最高偿付限额的部分，依法从投保机构清算财产中受偿。 存款保险基金管理机构偿付存款人的被保险存款后，即在偿付金额范围内取得该存款人对投保机构相同清偿顺序的债权。 社会保险基金、住房公积金存款的偿付办法由中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门另行制定，报国务院批准。 第六条　存款保险基金的来源包括： (一)投保机构交纳的保费； (二)在投保机构清算中分配的财产； (三)存款保险基金管理机构运用存款保险基金获得的收益； (四)其他合法收入。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建立和规范存款保险制度，依法保护存款人的合法权益，及时防范和化解金融风险，维护金融稳定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of establishing and regulating the deposit insurance system, protecting the lawful rights and interests of depositors according to law, preventing and mitigating financial risks in a timely manner, and maintaining financial stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内设立的商业银行、农村合作银行、农村信用合作社等吸收存款的银行业金融机构(以下统称投保机构)，应当依照本条例的规定投保存款保险。  
投保机构在中华人民共和国境外设立的分支机构，以及外国银行在中华人民共和国境内设立的分支机构不适用前款规定。但是，中华人民共和国与其他国家或者地区之间对存款保险制度另有安排的除外。

**Article 2** Commercial banks, rural cooperative banks, rural credit cooperatives and other banking financial institutions that absorb deposits established within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter collectively referred to as "insured institutions") shall purchase deposit insurance in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to branches established by an insured institution outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, nor to branches established within the territory of the People's Republic of China by a foreign bank, unless otherwise agreed between the People's Republic of China and other countries or regions regarding the deposit insurance system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称存款保险，是指投保机构向存款保险基金管理机构交纳保费，形成存款保险基金，存款保险基金管理机构依照本条例的规定向存款人偿付被保险存款，并采取必要措施维护存款以及存款保险基金安全的制度。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "deposit insurance" means a system whereby insured institutions pay premiums to the deposit insurance fund management institution to form a deposit insurance fund, and the deposit insurance fund management institution, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, compensates depositors for insured deposits and takes necessary measures to safeguard the security of deposits and the deposit insurance fund.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　被保险存款包括投保机构吸收的人民币存款和外币存款。但是，金融机构同业存款、投保机构的高级管理人员在本投保机构的存款以及存款保险基金管理机构规定不予保险的其他存款除外。

**Article 4** Insured deposits include Renminbi deposits and foreign currency deposits absorbed by insured institutions, except for interbank deposits of financial institutions, deposits of senior officers of an insured institution in that same insured institution, and other deposits that the deposit insurance fund management institution specifies as not insured.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　存款保险实行限额偿付，最高偿付限额为人民币50万元。中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门可以根据经济发展、存款结构变化、金融风险状况等因素调整最高偿付限额，报国务院批准后公布执行。  
同一存款人在同一家投保机构所有被保险存款账户的存款本金和利息合并计算的资金数额在最高偿付限额以内的，实行全额偿付；超出最高偿付限额的部分，依法从投保机构清算财产中受偿。  
存款保险基金管理机构偿付存款人的被保险存款后，即在偿付金额范围内取得该存款人对投保机构相同清偿顺序的债权。  
社会保险基金、住房公积金存款的偿付办法由中国人民银行会同国务院有关部门另行制定，报国务院批准。

**Article 5** Deposit insurance shall apply limited compensation, with the maximum compensation limit being RMB 500,000 yuan. The People's Bank of China may, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, adjust the maximum compensation limit based on factors such as economic development, changes in deposit structure, and financial risk conditions, and shall report to the State Council for approval before promulgating and implementing the adjustment.  
For a single depositor, where the total amount of deposit principal and interest in all insured deposit accounts at the same insured institution, calculated on a combined basis, is within the maximum compensation limit, full compensation shall be paid; the portion exceeding the maximum compensation limit shall be satisfied from the liquidation property of the insured institution according to law.  
After the deposit insurance fund management institution compensates a depositor for insured deposits, it shall acquire the depositor's claim against the insured institution with the same priority of satisfaction within the amount of compensation paid.  
The compensation method for social insurance funds and housing provident fund deposits shall be separately formulated by the People's Bank of China in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be reported to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　存款保险基金的来源包括：  
(一)投保机构交纳的保费；  
(二)在投保机构清算中分配的财产；  
(三)存款保险基金管理机构运用存款保险基金获得的收益；  
(四)其他合法收入。

**Article 6** The sources of the deposit insurance fund shall include:  
(1) premiums paid by insured institutions;  
(2) property distributed in the liquidation of insured institutions;  
(3) income obtained by the deposit insurance fund management institution from utilizing the deposit insurance fund;  
(4) other lawful income.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　存款保险基金管理机构履行下列职责：  
(一)制定并发布与其履行职责有关的规则；  
(二)制定和调整存款保险费率标准，报国务院批准；  
(三)确定各投保机构的适用费率；  
(四)归集保费；  
(五)管理和运用存款保险基金；  
(六)依照本条例的规定采取早期纠正措施和风险处置措施；  
(七)在本条例规定的限额内及时偿付存款人的被保险存款；  
(八)国务院批准的其他职责。  
存款保险基金管理机构由国务院决定。

**Article 7** The deposit insurance fund management institution shall perform the following duties:  
(1) formulate and publish rules related to the performance of its duties;  
(2) formulate and adjust the deposit insurance premium rate standards, and report to the State Council for approval;  
(3) determine the applicable premium rates for each insured institution;  
(4) collect premiums;  
(5) manage and utilize the deposit insurance fund;  
(6) take early corrective measures and risk disposal measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(7) compensate depositors for insured deposits in a timely manner within the limits prescribed by these Regulations;  
(8) other duties approved by the State Council.  
The deposit insurance fund management institution shall be determined by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本条例施行前已开业的吸收存款的银行业金融机构，应当在存款保险基金管理机构规定的期限内办理投保手续。  
本条例施行后开业的吸收存款的银行业金融机构，应当自工商行政管理部门颁发营业执照之日起6个月内，按照存款保险基金管理机构的规定办理投保手续。

**Article 8** Banking financial institutions that absorb deposits and were already in operation prior to the coming into force of these Regulations shall go through the insurance procedures within the time limit prescribed by the deposit insurance fund management institution.  
Banking financial institutions that absorb deposits and commence operation after the coming into force of these Regulations shall go through the insurance procedures within six months from the date the administrative department for industry and commerce issues the business license, in accordance with the provisions of the deposit insurance fund management institution.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　存款保险费率由基准费率和风险差别费率构成。费率标准由存款保险基金管理机构根据经济金融发展状况、存款结构情况以及存款保险基金的累积水平等因素制定和调整，报国务院批准后执行。  
各投保机构的适用费率，由存款保险基金管理机构根据投保机构的经营管理状况和风险状况等因素确定。

**Article 9** The deposit insurance premium rate shall consist of a benchmark rate and a risk-differentiated rate. The rate standards shall be formulated and adjusted by the deposit insurance fund management institution based on factors such as economic and financial development conditions, deposit structure, and the accumulation level of the deposit insurance fund, and shall be reported to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The applicable rate for each insured institution shall be determined by the deposit insurance fund management institution based on factors such as the operational management status and risk condition of the insured institution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　投保机构应当交纳的保费，按照本投保机构的被保险存款和存款保险基金管理机构确定的适用费率计算，具体办法由存款保险基金管理机构规定。  
投保机构应当按照存款保险基金管理机构的要求定期报送被保险存款余额、存款结构情况以及与确定适用费率、核算保费、偿付存款相关的其他必要资料。  
投保机构应当按照存款保险基金管理机构的规定，每6个月交纳一次保费。

**Article 10** The premiums to be paid by an insured institution shall be calculated based on the insured deposits of that insured institution and the applicable rate determined by the deposit insurance fund management institution. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the deposit insurance fund management institution.  
An insured institution shall, in accordance with the requirements of the deposit insurance fund management institution, periodically submit the balance of insured deposits, deposit structure, and other necessary information related to determining the applicable rate, calculating premiums, and compensating deposits.  
An insured institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the deposit insurance fund management institution, pay premiums once every six months.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　存款保险基金的运用，应当遵循安全、流动、保值增值的原则，限于下列形式：  
(一)存放在中国人民银行；  
(二)投资政府债券、中央银行票据、信用等级较高的金融债券以及其他高等级债券；  
(三)国务院批准的其他资金运用形式。

**Article 11** The use of deposit insurance funds shall follow the principles of safety, liquidity, and preservation and appreciation of value, and shall be limited to the following forms:  
(1) deposited with the People's Bank of China;  
(2) investment in government bonds, central bank bills, financial bonds with relatively high credit ratings, and other high-grade bonds;  
(3) other forms of fund use approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　存款保险基金管理机构应当自每一会计年度结束之日起3个月内编制存款保险基金收支的财务会计报告、报表，并编制年度报告，按照国家有关规定予以公布。  
存款保险基金的收支应当遵守国家统一的财务会计制度，并依法接受审计机关的审计监督。

**Article 12** The deposit insurance fund management institution shall, within three months from the end of each fiscal year, prepare financial accounting reports and statements on the revenues and expenditures of the deposit insurance fund, and prepare an annual report, and shall publish them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The revenues and expenditures of the deposit insurance fund shall comply with the uniform financial accounting system of the State, and shall, according to law, accept the audit supervision of the audit authorities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　存款保险基金管理机构履行职责，发现有下列情形之一的，可以进行核查：  
(一)投保机构风险状况发生变化，可能需要调整适用费率的，对涉及费率计算的相关情况进行核查；  
(二)投保机构保费交纳基数可能存在问题的，对其存款的规模、结构以及真实性进行核查；  
(三)对投保机构报送的信息、资料的真实性进行核查。  
对核查中发现的重大问题，应当告知银行业监督管理机构。

**Article 13** When the deposit insurance fund management institution performs its duties and discovers any of the following circumstances, it may conduct verification:  
(1) where the risk status of an insured institution changes and it may be necessary to adjust the applicable rate, verify the relevant circumstances concerning the rate calculation;  
(2) where there may be problems with the base for premium payment of the insured institution, verify the scale, structure, and authenticity of its deposits;  
(3) verify the authenticity of the information and materials submitted by the insured institution.  
Major issues discovered during verification shall be reported to the banking regulatory authority.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　存款保险基金管理机构参加金融监督管理协调机制，并与中国人民银行、银行业监督管理机构等金融管理部门、机构建立信息共享机制。  
存款保险基金管理机构应当通过信息共享机制获取有关投保机构的风险状况、检查报告和评级情况等监督管理信息。  
前款规定的信息不能满足控制存款保险基金风险、保证及时偿付、确定差别费率等需要的，存款保险基金管理机构可以要求投保机构及时报送其他相关信息。

**Article 14** The deposit insurance fund management institution shall participate in the financial supervision and management coordination mechanism, and shall establish an information sharing mechanism with the People's Bank of China, the banking regulatory authority, and other financial supervision and management departments and institutions.  
The deposit insurance fund management institution shall, through the information sharing mechanism, obtain supervision and management information concerning the risk status, inspection reports, and rating status of insured institutions.  
Where the information specified in the preceding paragraph cannot meet the needs of controlling the risk of the deposit insurance fund, ensuring timely compensation, or determining differential rates, the deposit insurance fund management institution may require the insured institution to promptly submit other relevant information.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　存款保险基金管理机构发现投保机构存在资本不足等影响存款安全以及存款保险基金安全的情形的，可以对其提出风险警示。

**Article 15** Where the deposit insurance fund management institution discovers that an insured institution has insufficient capital or other circumstances affecting the safety of deposits and the safety of the deposit insurance fund, it may issue a risk warning to it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　投保机构因重大资产损失等原因导致资本充足率大幅度下降，严重危及存款安全以及存款保险基金安全的，投保机构应当按照存款保险基金管理机构、中国人民银行、银行业监督管理机构的要求及时采取补充资本、控制资产增长、控制重大交易授信、降低杠杆率等措施。  
投保机构有前款规定情形，且在存款保险基金管理机构规定的期限内未改进的，存款保险基金管理机构可以提高其适用费率。

**Article 16** Where an insured institution, due to significant asset losses or other reasons, experiences a substantial decline in its capital adequacy ratio, seriously endangering the safety of deposits and the safety of the deposit insurance fund, the insured institution shall promptly take measures such as replenishing capital, controlling asset growth, controlling credit for major transactions, and reducing leverage ratio in accordance with the requirements of the deposit insurance fund management institution, the People's Bank of China, and the banking regulatory authority.  
Where an insured institution falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph and fails to make improvements within the time limit prescribed by the deposit insurance fund management institution, the deposit insurance fund management institution may increase its applicable rate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　存款保险基金管理机构发现投保机构有[《中华人民共和国银行业监督管理法》第三十八条](../../经济、税务与金融/金融、证券与保险/中华人民共和国银行业监督管理法.md#art-38)、第三十九条规定情形的，可以建议银行业监督管理机构依法采取相应措施。

**Article 17** Where the deposit insurance fund management institution discovers that an insured institution has any of the circumstances provided for in Article 38 and Article 39 of the Banking Supervision and Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, it may recommend that the banking regulatory authority take corresponding measures according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　存款保险基金管理机构可以选择下列方式使用存款保险基金，保护存款人利益：  
(一)在本条例规定的限额内直接偿付被保险存款；  
(二)委托其他合格投保机构在本条例规定的限额内代为偿付被保险存款；  
(三)为其他合格投保机构提供担保、损失分摊或者资金支持，以促成其收购或者承担被接管、被撤销或者申请破产的投保机构的全部或者部分业务、资产、负债。  
存款保险基金管理机构在拟订存款保险基金使用方案选择前款规定方式时，应当遵循基金使用成本最小的原则。

**Article 18** The deposit insurance fund management institution may choose the following methods to use the deposit insurance fund to protect the interests of depositors:  
(1) directly pay insured deposits within the limits specified in these Regulations;  
(2) entrust other qualified insured institutions to pay insured deposits on its behalf within the limits specified in these Regulations;  
(3) provide guarantees, loss sharing, or financial support to other qualified insured institutions to facilitate their acquisition or assumption of all or part of the business, assets, and liabilities of the insured institution that is taken over, cancelled, or applying for bankruptcy.  
When the deposit insurance fund management institution formulates a plan for using the deposit insurance fund by selecting the methods specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall follow the principle of minimizing the cost of using the fund.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列情形之一的，存款人有权要求存款保险基金管理机构在本条例规定的限额内，使用存款保险基金偿付存款人的被保险存款：  
(一)存款保险基金管理机构担任投保机构的接管组织；  
(二)存款保险基金管理机构实施被撤销投保机构的清算；  
(三)人民法院裁定受理对投保机构的破产申请；  
(四)经国务院批准的其他情形。  
存款保险基金管理机构应当依照本条例的规定，在前款规定情形发生之日起7个工作日内足额偿付存款。

**Article 19** Under any of the following circumstances, a depositor has the right to request that the deposit insurance fund management institution use the deposit insurance fund to pay the depositor's insured deposits within the limits specified in these Regulations:  
(1) the deposit insurance fund management institution serves as the takeover organization of the insured institution;  
(2) the deposit insurance fund management institution implements the liquidation of the cancelled insured institution;  
(3) a People's Court renders a ruling accepting a bankruptcy application against the insured institution;  
(4) other circumstances approved by the State Council.  
The deposit insurance fund management institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, pay deposits in full within seven working days from the date of occurrence of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　存款保险基金管理机构的工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
(一)违反规定收取保费；  
(二)违反规定使用、运用存款保险基金；  
(三)违反规定不及时、足额偿付存款。  
存款保险基金管理机构的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、泄露国家秘密或者所知悉的商业秘密的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where any staff member of the deposit insurance fund management institution commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) collecting premiums in violation of provisions;  
(2) using or applying deposit insurance funds in violation of provisions;  
(3) failing to pay deposits in a timely and full manner in violation of provisions.  
Where a staff member of the deposit insurance fund management institution abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duties, or divulges State secrets or trade secrets known to him or her, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　投保机构有下列情形之一的，由存款保险基金管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正或者情节严重的，予以记录并作为调整该投保机构的适用费率的依据：  
(一)未依法投保；  
(二)未依法及时、足额交纳保费；  
(三)未按照规定报送信息、资料或者报送虚假的信息、资料；  
(四)拒绝或者妨碍存款保险基金管理机构依法进行的核查；  
(五)妨碍存款保险基金管理机构实施存款保险基金使用方案。  
投保机构有前款规定情形的，存款保险基金管理机构可以对投保机构的主管人员和直接责任人员予以公示。投保机构有前款第二项规定情形的，存款保险基金管理机构还可以按日加收未交纳保费部分0.05%的滞纳金。

**Article 21** Where an insured institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the deposit insurance fund management institution shall order it to make rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit or if the circumstances are serious, it shall record such circumstance and use it as a basis for adjusting the applicable premium rate of the insured institution:  
(1) failing to obtain insurance according to law;  
(2) failing to pay premiums in a timely manner and in full according to law;  
(3) failing to submit information or materials in accordance with regulations, or submitting false information or materials;  
(4) refusing or obstructing the inspection conducted by the deposit insurance fund management institution according to law;  
(5) obstructing the deposit insurance fund management institution from implementing the deposit insurance fund utilization plan.  
Where an insured institution falls under the circumstance specified in the preceding paragraph, the deposit insurance fund management institution may make a public announcement of the person in charge and the directly responsible persons of the insured institution. Where an insured institution falls under the circumstance specified in Item (2) of the preceding paragraph, the deposit insurance fund management institution may also charge a late fee of 0.05% per day on the unpaid premium portion.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例施行前，已被国务院银行业监督管理机构依法决定接管、撤销或者人民法院已受理破产申请的吸收存款的银行业金融机构，不适用本条例。

**Article 22** Before these Regulations come into force, these Regulations shall not apply to banking financial institutions that absorb deposits and have been ordered to be taken over or dissolved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council according to law, or have had their bankruptcy applications accepted by a People's Court.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自2015年5月1日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2015.
