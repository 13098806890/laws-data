关于基本医疗保险基金先行支付申请条件法律适用问题的批复  
  
法释〔2026〕1号  
  
安徽省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于基本医疗保险基金先行支付申请条件法律适用问题的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国社会保险法》第三十条](../../劳动与社会保障/社会保险与福利/中华人民共和国社会保险法.md#art-30)和《社会保险基金先行支付暂行办法》第二条、第三条的规定，参加基本医疗保险的个人（以下简称参保人）由于第三人的侵权行为造成伤病，医疗费用依法应当由第三人负担的部分，第三人不支付或者无法确定第三人的，由基本医疗保险基金先行支付。参保人向参保地社会保险经办机构书面申请先行支付时，应告知造成其伤病的原因和第三人不支付医疗费用或者无法确定第三人的情况。社会保险经办机构依法审核后应当按照统筹地区基本医疗保险基金支付的规定先行支付相应部分的医疗费用。  
  
参保人依法享有的申请基本医疗保险基金先行支付权利，不受在医疗费用结算时是否已自行支付医疗费用的影响；社会保险经办机构仅以在医疗费用结算时参保人已经自行支付医疗费用为由，不予先行支付，参保人起诉请求责令社会保险经办机构予以先行支付的，人民法院依法予以支持。社会保险经办机构对依法先行支付的医疗费用，可依据《社会保险基金先行支付暂行办法》第十一条、第十二条的规定进行追偿。

**Article 1** January 5, 2026  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Application of Law Concerning the Conditions for Application for Advance Payment by the Basic Medical Insurance Fund  
Fa Shi [2026] No. 1  
Higher People's Court of Anhui Province:  
Your Request for Instructions on the Application of Law Concerning the Conditions for Application for Advance Payment by the Basic Medical Insurance Fund has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Pursuant to Article 30 of the Social Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China and Articles 2 and 3 of the Interim Measures for Advance Payment by the Social Insurance Fund, where an individual participating in basic medical insurance (hereinafter referred to as the participant) sustains illness or injury due to a tortious act of a third party, and the portion of medical expenses that shall be borne by the third party according to law is not paid by the third party or the third party cannot be identified, the basic medical insurance fund shall make advance payment. When the participant submits a written application for advance payment to the social insurance agency at the place of participation, the participant shall inform the agency of the cause of the illness or injury and the circumstances that the third party has not paid the medical expenses or cannot be identified. After examination according to law, the social insurance agency shall make advance payment of the corresponding portion of medical expenses in accordance with the provisions on payment by the basic medical insurance fund of the coordinated region.  
The participant's right to apply for advance payment by the basic medical insurance fund according to law shall not be affected by whether the participant has already paid the medical expenses at the time of settlement; where the social insurance agency, solely on the ground that the participant has already paid the medical expenses at the time of settlement, refuses to make advance payment, and the participant prosecutes requesting an order for the social insurance agency to make advance payment, the People's Court shall support it according to law. The social insurance agency may, pursuant to Articles 11 and 12 of the Interim Measures for Advance Payment by the Social Insurance Fund, seek recourse for the medical expenses advanced according to law.
