# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕14号  
**公布日期**：2013-05-31  
**生效日期**：2013-06-08

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国保险法〉若干问题的解释（二）》已于2013年5月6日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1577次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年6月8日起施行。 2013年5月31日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》 若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　财产保险中，不同投保人就同一保险标的分别投保，保险事故发生后，被保险人在其保险利益范围内依据保险合同主张保险赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 1** In property insurance, where different applicants separately insure the same subject matter of insurance, and after the occurrence of an insured event, the insured claims insurance compensation within the scope of its insurable interest pursuant to the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人身保险中，因投保人对被保险人不具有保险利益导致保险合同无效，投保人主张保险人退还扣减相应手续费后的保险费的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 2** In personal insurance, where the insurance contract is void because the applicant has no insurable interest in the insured, and the applicant claims the return of the premium after deducting the corresponding handling fees from the insurer, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　投保人或者投保人的代理人订立保险合同时没有亲自签字或者盖章，而由保险人或者保险人的代理人代为签字或者盖章的，对投保人不生效。但投保人已经交纳保险费的，视为其对代签字或者盖章行为的追认。  
保险人或者保险人的代理人代为填写保险单证后经投保人签字或者盖章确认的，代为填写的内容视为投保人的真实意思表示。但有证据证明保险人或者保险人的代理人存在[保险法第一百一十六条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-116)、第一百三十一条相关规定情形的除外。

**Article 3** Where an applicant or the applicant's agent does not personally sign or affix a seal when concluding an insurance contract, and the insurer or the insurer's agent signs or affixes a seal on behalf of the applicant, the contract shall not become effective with respect to the applicant. However, if the applicant has already paid the insurance premium, it shall be deemed as ratification of the act of signing or affixing a seal on its behalf.  
Where the insurer or the insurer's agent fills in the insurance policy and other documents on behalf of the applicant and the applicant thereafter signs or affixes a seal for confirmation, the content filled in on behalf of the applicant shall be deemed as the true expression of intention of the applicant, unless there is evidence proving that the insurer or the insurer's agent has acted under the circumstances specified in Articles 116 and 131 of the Insurance Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保险人接受了投保人提交的投保单并收取了保险费，尚未作出是否承保的意思表示，发生保险事故，被保险人或者受益人请求保险人按照保险合同承担赔偿或者给付保险金责任，符合承保条件的，人民法院应予支持；不符合承保条件的，保险人不承担保险责任，但应当退还已经收取的保险费。  
保险人主张不符合承保条件的，应承担举证责任。

**Article 4** Where the insurer has accepted the application form submitted by the applicant and collected the insurance premium, but has not yet expressed its intention whether to underwrite the insurance, and an insured event occurs, and the insured or beneficiary requests the insurer to assume the liability to pay compensation or insurance benefits in accordance with the insurance contract, if the conditions for underwriting are met, the People's Court shall support such request; if the conditions for underwriting are not met, the insurer shall not assume the insurance liability but shall refund the insurance premium already collected.  
If the insurer claims that the conditions for underwriting are not met, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　保险合同订立时，投保人明知的与保险标的或者被保险人有关的情况，属于[保险法第十六条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-16)第一款规定的投保人“应当如实告知”的内容。

**Article 5** When an insurance contract is concluded, the circumstances actually known by the applicant relating to the subject matter of insurance or the insured shall be within the scope of the applicant's obligation to "truthfully disclose" as provided for in Article 16, paragraph 1 of the Insurance Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　投保人的告知义务限于保险人询问的范围和内容。当事人对询问范围及内容有争议的，保险人负举证责任。  
保险人以投保人违反了对投保单询问表中所列概括性条款的如实告知义务为由请求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。但该概括性条款有具体内容的除外。

**Article 6** The applicant's duty of disclosure shall be limited to the scope and content of the insurer's inquiry. Where the parties dispute the scope and content of the inquiry, the insurer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
Where the insurer requests dissolution of the contract on the ground that the applicant has violated the duty of truthful disclosure with respect to a general clause listed in the questionnaire of the application form, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless such general clause has specific content.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保险人在保险合同成立后知道或者应当知道投保人未履行如实告知义务，仍然收取保险费，又依照[保险法第十六条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-16)第二款的规定主张解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** Where the insurer, after the formation of the insurance contract, knows or should have known that the applicant has failed to perform the duty of truthful disclosure but nevertheless collects the insurance premium, and thereafter claims dissolution of the contract in accordance with Article 16, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保险人未行使合同解除权，直接以存在[保险法第十六条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-16)第四款、第五款规定的情形为由拒绝赔偿的，人民法院不予支持。但当事人就拒绝赔偿事宜及保险合同存续另行达成一致的情况除外。

**Article 8** Where the insurer, without exercising its right to dissolve the contract, directly refuses to pay compensation on the ground that the circumstances specified in Article 16, paragraphs 4 and 5 of the Insurance Law exist, the People's Court shall not support such refusal, unless the parties have otherwise reached an agreement on the matter of refusal to pay compensation and the continuation of the insurance contract.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　保险人提供的格式合同文本中的责任免除条款、免赔额、免赔率、比例赔付或者给付等免除或者减轻保险人责任的条款，可以认定为[保险法第十七条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-17)第二款规定的“免除保险人责任的条款”。  
保险人因投保人、被保险人违反法定或者约定义务，享有解除合同权利的条款，不属于[保险法第十七条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-17)第二款规定的“免除保险人责任的条款”。

**Article 9** Clauses in the standard contract text provided by the insurer that exempt or mitigate the insurer's liability, such as liability exemption clauses, deductibles, franchise, proportional payment or benefits, etc., may be determined as "clauses exempting the insurer from liability" as provided for in Article 17, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law.  
Clauses under which the insurer has the right to dissolve the contract because the applicant or the insured violates statutory or contractual obligations shall not be regarded as "clauses exempting the insurer from liability" as provided for in Article 17, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　保险人将法律、行政法规中的禁止性规定情形作为保险合同免责条款的免责事由，保险人对该条款作出提示后，投保人、被保险人或者受益人以保险人未履行明确说明义务为由主张该条款不生效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where the insurer takes the circumstances of prohibitive provisions in laws or administrative regulations as the ground for exemption under an exemption clause of the insurance contract, and the insurer has made a reminder of such clause, and the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary claims that such clause does not take effect on the ground that the insurer has failed to perform the obligation of clear explanation, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　保险合同订立时，保险人在投保单或者保险单等其他保险凭证上，对保险合同中免除保险人责任的条款，以足以引起投保人注意的文字、字体、符号或者其他明显标志作出提示的，人民法院应当认定其履行了[保险法第十七条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-17)第二款规定的提示义务。  
保险人对保险合同中有关免除保险人责任条款的概念、内容及其法律后果以书面或者口头形式向投保人作出常人能够理解的解释说明的，人民法院应当认定保险人履行了[保险法第十七条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-17)第二款规定的明确说明义务。

**Article 11** When concluding an insurance contract, if the insurer, on the application form, the insurance policy or other insurance documents, makes a reminder of the clauses exempting the insurer from liability in the insurance contract by using words, fonts, symbols or other conspicuous marks sufficient to draw the applicant's attention, the People's Court shall determine that the insurer has performed the obligation of reminder as provided for in Article 17, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law.  
If the insurer, in writing or orally, provides the applicant with an explanation of the concept, content and legal consequences of the clauses exempting the insurer from liability in the insurance contract in a manner that an ordinary person can understand, the People's Court shall determine that the insurer has performed the obligation of clear explanation as provided for in Article 17, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　通过网络、电话等方式订立的保险合同，保险人以网页、音频、视频等形式对免除保险人责任条款予以提示和明确说明的，人民法院可以认定其履行了提示和明确说明义务。

**Article 12** For insurance contracts concluded through the internet, telephone or other means, if the insurer makes a reminder and a clear explanation of the clauses exempting the insurer from liability in the form of web pages, audio, video, etc., the People's Court may determine that the insurer has performed the obligations of reminder and clear explanation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　保险人对其履行了明确说明义务负举证责任。  
投保人对保险人履行了符合[本解释第十一条](#art-11)第二款要求的明确说明义务在相关文书上签字、盖章或者以其他形式予以确认的，应当认定保险人履行了该项义务。但另有证据证明保险人未履行明确说明义务的除外。

**Article 13** The insurer shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that it has performed the obligation of clear explanation.  
If the applicant confirms by signing, affixing a seal or in other forms on the relevant documents that the insurer has performed the obligation of clear explanation that meets the requirements of paragraph 2 of Article 11 of this Interpretation, it shall be determined that the insurer has performed such obligation, unless there is other evidence proving that the insurer has not performed the obligation of clear explanation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保险合同中记载的内容不一致的，按照下列规则认定：  
（一）投保单与保险单或者其他保险凭证不一致的，以投保单为准。但不一致的情形系经保险人说明并经投保人同意的，以投保人签收的保险单或者其他保险凭证载明的内容为准；  
（二）非格式条款与格式条款不一致的，以非格式条款为准；  
（三）保险凭证记载的时间不同的，以形成时间在后的为准；  
（四）保险凭证存在手写和打印两种方式的，以双方签字、盖章的手写部分的内容为准。

**Article 14** Where the contents recorded in the insurance contract are inconsistent, the determination shall be made according to the following rules:  
(1) If the application form is inconsistent with the insurance policy or other insurance documents, the application form shall prevail. However, if the inconsistency arises from an explanation given by the insurer and is consented to by the applicant, the content stated in the insurance policy or other insurance documents signed and accepted by the applicant shall prevail;  
(2) If non-standard clauses are inconsistent with standard clauses, the non-standard clauses shall prevail;  
(3) If the time recorded on the insurance documents differs, the one formed later shall prevail;  
(4) If the insurance documents contain both handwritten and printed parts, the content of the handwritten part signed and sealed by both parties shall prevail.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[保险法第二十三条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-23)规定的三十日核定期间，应自保险人初次收到索赔请求及投保人、被保险人或者受益人提供的有关证明和资料之日起算。  
保险人主张扣除投保人、被保险人或者受益人补充提供有关证明和资料期间的，人民法院应予支持。扣除期间自保险人根据[保险法第二十二条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-22)规定作出的通知到达投保人、被保险人或者受益人之日起，至投保人、被保险人或者受益人按照通知要求补充提供的有关证明和资料到达保险人之日止。

**Article 15** The thirty-day verification period as provided for in Article 23 of the Insurance Law shall be calculated from the date when the insurer first receives the claim request and the relevant certificates and materials provided by the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary.  
If the insurer claims to deduct the period during which the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary supplements the relevant certificates and materials, the People's Court shall support such claim. The deducted period shall run from the date when the notice made by the insurer pursuant to Article 22 of the Insurance Law reaches the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary, to the date when the relevant certificates and materials supplemented by the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary as required by the notice reach the insurer.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保险人应以自己的名义行使保险代位求偿权。  
根据[保险法第六十条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-60)第一款的规定，保险人代位求偿权的诉讼时效期间应自其取得代位求偿权之日起算。

**Article 16** The insurer shall exercise the right of subrogation in its own name.  
Pursuant to Article 60, paragraph 1 of the Insurance Law, the limitation of action for the insurer's right of subrogation shall be calculated from the date when it acquires the right of subrogation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　保险人在其提供的保险合同格式条款中对非保险术语所作的解释符合专业意义，或者虽不符合专业意义，但有利于投保人、被保险人或者受益人的，人民法院应予认可。

**Article 17** Where the insurer's interpretation of non-insurance terms in the standard clauses of the insurance contract provided by it conforms to the professional meaning, or although it does not conform to the professional meaning, it is favorable to the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary, the People's Court shall recognize such interpretation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行政管理部门依据法律规定制作的交通事故认定书、火灾事故认定书等，人民法院应当依法审查并确认其相应的证明力，但有相反证据能够推翻的除外。

**Article 18** With respect to traffic accident determination certificates, fire accident determination certificates, etc. made by administrative authorities according to law, the People's Court shall review and confirm their corresponding probative force according to law, unless there is contrary evidence that can overturn them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保险事故发生后，被保险人或者受益人起诉保险人，保险人以被保险人或者受益人未要求第三者承担责任为由抗辩不承担保险责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
财产保险事故发生后，被保险人就其所受损失从第三者取得赔偿后的不足部分提起诉讼，请求保险人赔偿的，人民法院应予依法受理。

**Article 19** After the occurrence of an insured event, if the insured or beneficiary sues the insurer, and the insurer defends on the ground that the insured or beneficiary has not demanded the third party to assume liability, claiming that it shall not bear insurance liability, the People's Court shall not support such defense.  
After the occurrence of a property insurance event, if the insured brings a lawsuit for the shortfall after obtaining compensation from the third party for the losses suffered, and requests the insurer to pay compensation, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　保险公司依法设立并取得营业执照的分支机构属于[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第四十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-48)规定的其他组织，可以作为保险合同纠纷案件的当事人参加诉讼。

**Article 20** A branch established according to law by an insurance company and having obtained a business license shall be an "other organization" as provided for in Article 48 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and may participate in litigation as a party in insurance contract dispute cases.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本解释施行后尚未终审的保险合同纠纷案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 21** This Interpretation shall apply to insurance contract dispute cases for which the final adjudication has not yet been rendered after this Interpretation comes into force; for cases for which the final adjudication has already been rendered before this Interpretation comes into force, and for which a party applies for retrial or a decision is made to retry the case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
