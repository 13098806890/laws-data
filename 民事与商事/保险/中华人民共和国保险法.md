### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范保险活动，保护保险活动当事人的合法权益，加强对保险业的监督管理，维护社会经济秩序和社会公共利益，促进保险事业的健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating insurance activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of parties to insurance activities, strengthening the supervision and administration of the insurance industry, maintaining the social economic order and public interests, and promoting the healthy development of the insurance industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称保险，是指投保人根据合同约定，向保险人支付保险费，保险人对于合同约定的可能发生的事故因其发生所造成的财产损失承担赔偿保险金责任，或者当被保险人死亡、伤残、疾病或者达到合同约定的年龄、期限等条件时承担给付保险金责任的商业保险行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "insurance" means a commercial insurance activity whereby the policyholder pays premiums to the insurer in accordance with the contract, and the insurer bears the liability for paying insurance benefits for property losses caused by the occurrence of the contingencies insured against as agreed in the contract, or bears the liability for paying insurance benefits when the insured person dies, is injured or disabled, suffers from disease, or reaches the age or term agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国境内从事保险活动，适用本法。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to insurance activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事保险活动必须遵守法律、行政法规，尊重社会公德，不得损害社会公共利益。

**Article 4** Engaging in insurance activities must comply with laws and administrative regulations, respect social ethics, and shall not harm public interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　保险活动当事人行使权利、履行义务应当遵循诚实信用原则。

**Article 5** Parties to insurance activities shall abide by the principle of good faith when exercising their rights and performing their obligations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保险业务由依照本法设立的保险公司以及法律、行政法规规定的其他保险组织经营，其他单位和个人不得经营保险业务。

**Article 6** Insurance business shall be operated by insurance companies established in accordance with this Law and by other insurance organizations prescribed by laws and administrative regulations. No other entity or individual may operate insurance business.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在中华人民共和国境内的法人和其他组织需要办理境内保险的，应当向中华人民共和国境内的保险公司投保。

**Article 7** Legal persons and other organizations within the territory of the People's Republic of China that need to purchase domestic insurance shall take out insurance policies with insurance companies within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保险业和银行业、证券业、信托业实行分业经营、分业管理，保险公司与银行、证券、信托业务机构分别设立。国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 8** The insurance industry, banking industry, securities industry, and trust industry shall adopt separate operation and separate management. Insurance companies shall be established separately from banks, securities companies, and trust institutions, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院保险监督管理机构依法对保险业实施监督管理。  
国务院保险监督管理机构根据履行职责的需要设立派出机构。派出机构按照国务院保险监督管理机构的授权履行监督管理职责。

**Article 9** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, in accordance with law, exercise supervision and administration over the insurance industry.  
The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may establish dispatched offices as needed for the performance of its duties. The dispatched offices shall perform supervision and administration functions as authorized by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第二章　保险合同

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　保险合同是投保人与保险人约定保险权利义务关系的协议。  
投保人是指与保险人订立保险合同，并按照合同约定负有支付保险费义务的人。  
保险人是指与投保人订立保险合同，并按照合同约定承担赔偿或者给付保险金责任的保险公司。

**Article 10** An insurance contract is an agreement under which the applicant and the insurer agree on the rights and obligations of insurance.  
An applicant is a person who concludes an insurance contract with an insurer and is obligated to pay the insurance premium in accordance with the contract.  
An insurer is an insurance company that concludes an insurance contract with an applicant and is obligated to assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　订立保险合同，应当协商一致，遵循公平原则确定各方的权利和义务。  
除法律、行政法规规定必须保险的外，保险合同自愿订立。

**Article 11** To conclude an insurance contract, the parties shall reach a consensus through negotiation and determine their respective rights and obligations in accordance with the principle of fairness.  
Except for insurance that is compulsory under laws or administrative regulations, insurance contracts shall be concluded voluntarily.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人身保险的投保人在保险合同订立时，对被保险人应当具有保险利益。  
财产保险的被保险人在保险事故发生时，对保险标的应当具有保险利益。  
人身保险是以人的寿命和身体为保险标的的保险。  
财产保险是以财产及其有关利益为保险标的的保险。  
被保险人是指其财产或者人身受保险合同保障，享有保险金请求权的人。投保人可以为被保险人。  
保险利益是指投保人或者被保险人对保险标的具有的法律上承认的利益。

**Article 12** An applicant for personal insurance shall have an insurable interest in the insured at the time of conclusion of the insurance contract.  
An insured under property insurance shall have an insurable interest in the subject matter insured at the time of occurrence of the insured event.  
Personal insurance is insurance with the life and body of a person as the subject matter insured.  
Property insurance is insurance with property and related interests as the subject matter insured.  
An insured is a person whose property or person is protected by an insurance contract and who is entitled to claim insurance benefits. An applicant may be the insured.  
Insurable interest means the interest of the applicant or the insured in the subject matter insured that is legally recognized.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　投保人提出保险要求，经保险人同意承保，保险合同成立。保险人应当及时向投保人签发保险单或者其他保险凭证。  
保险单或者其他保险凭证应当载明当事人双方约定的合同内容。当事人也可以约定采用其他书面形式载明合同内容。  
依法成立的保险合同，自成立时生效。投保人和保险人可以对合同的效力约定附条件或者附期限。

**Article 13** Where an applicant applies for insurance and the insurer agrees to underwrite the insurance, an insurance contract is formed. The insurer shall issue an insurance policy or other insurance certificate to the applicant in a timely manner.  
The insurance policy or other insurance certificate shall specify the contents of the contract agreed upon by the parties. The parties may also agree to specify the contract contents in other written forms.  
An insurance contract lawfully formed shall take effect upon its formation. The applicant and the insurer may agree to attach conditions or a time limit to the effect of the contract.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保险合同成立后，投保人按照约定交付保险费，保险人按照约定的时间开始承担保险责任。

**Article 14** After an insurance contract is formed, the applicant shall pay the insurance premium in accordance with the agreement, and the insurer shall commence assuming insurance liability as from the agreed time.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　除本法另有规定或者保险合同另有约定外，保险合同成立后，投保人可以解除合同，保险人不得解除合同。

**Article 15** Unless otherwise provided by this Law or agreed upon in the insurance contract, after an insurance contract is formed, the applicant may rescind the contract, but the insurer shall not rescind the contract.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　订立保险合同，保险人就保险标的或者被保险人的有关情况提出询问的，投保人应当如实告知。  
投保人故意或者因重大过失未履行前款规定的如实告知义务，足以影响保险人决定是否同意承保或者提高保险费率的，保险人有权解除合同。  
前款规定的合同解除权，自保险人知道有解除事由之日起，超过三十日不行使而消灭。自合同成立之日起超过二年的，保险人不得解除合同；发生保险事故的，保险人应当承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任。  
投保人故意不履行如实告知义务的，保险人对于合同解除前发生的保险事故，不承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任，并不退还保险费。  
投保人因重大过失未履行如实告知义务，对保险事故的发生有严重影响的，保险人对于合同解除前发生的保险事故，不承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任，但应当退还保险费。  
保险人在合同订立时已经知道投保人未如实告知的情况的，保险人不得解除合同；发生保险事故的，保险人应当承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任。  
保险事故是指保险合同约定的保险责任范围内的事故。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第7条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第8条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 16** Where the insurer inquires about the subject matter insured or the circumstances of the insured when concluding an insurance contract, the applicant shall truthfully inform the insurer.  
Where the applicant intentionally or through gross negligence fails to perform the obligation of truthful disclosure as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and such failure is sufficient to affect the insurer's decision on whether to agree to underwrite the insurance or to increase the insurance premium rate, the insurer shall have the right to rescind the contract.  
The right to rescind the contract as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall lapse if it is not exercised within thirty days from the date on which the insurer becomes aware of the cause for rescission. If more than two years have elapsed from the date of formation of the contract, the insurer shall not rescind the contract; if an insured event occurs, the insurer shall assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits.  
Where the applicant intentionally fails to perform the obligation of truthful disclosure, the insurer shall not assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits for an insured event that occurs prior to the rescission of the contract, and shall not refund the insurance premium.  
Where the applicant fails to perform the obligation of truthful disclosure through gross negligence, and such failure has a material impact on the occurrence of the insured event, the insurer shall not assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits for an insured event that occurs prior to the rescission of the contract, but shall refund the insurance premium.  
Where the insurer was already aware of the applicant's failure to truthfully disclose at the time of concluding the contract, the insurer shall not rescind the contract; if an insured event occurs, the insurer shall assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits.  
Insured event means an event that falls within the scope of insurance liability under the insurance contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　订立保险合同，采用保险人提供的格式条款的，保险人向投保人提供的投保单应当附格式条款，保险人应当向投保人说明合同的内容。  
对保险合同中免除保险人责任的条款，保险人在订立合同时应当在投保单、保险单或者其他保险凭证上作出足以引起投保人注意的提示，并对该条款的内容以书面或者口头形式向投保人作出明确说明；未作提示或者明确说明的，该条款不产生效力。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-9" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第11条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 17** Where an insurance contract is concluded using standard clauses provided by the insurer, the insurance application form provided by the insurer to the applicant shall have such standard clauses attached, and the insurer shall explain the contents of the contract to the applicant.  
With respect to clauses in an insurance contract that exclude the insurer's liability, the insurer shall, at the time of concluding the contract, make a clear indication on the insurance application form, insurance policy, or other insurance certificate that is sufficient to draw the applicant's attention, and shall provide a clear explanation of the contents of such clauses to the applicant in writing or orally; if no such indication or clear explanation is made, such clauses shall not take effect.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　保险合同应当包括下列事项：  
（一）保险人的名称和住所；  
（二）投保人、被保险人的姓名或者名称、住所，以及人身保险的受益人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（三）保险标的；  
（四）保险责任和责任免除；  
（五）保险期间和保险责任开始时间；  
（六）保险金额；  
（七）保险费以及支付办法；  
（八）保险金赔偿或者给付办法；  
（九）违约责任和争议处理；  
（十）订立合同的年、月、日。  
投保人和保险人可以约定与保险有关的其他事项。  
受益人是指人身保险合同中由被保险人或者投保人指定的享有保险金请求权的人。投保人、被保险人可以为受益人。  
保险金额是指保险人承担赔偿或者给付保险金责任的最高限额。

**Article 18** An insurance contract shall include the following particulars:  
(1) the name and domicile of the insurer;  
(2) the name or title and domicile of the applicant and the insured, and the name or title and domicile of the beneficiary under personal insurance;  
(3) the subject matter insured;  
(4) insurance liability and exclusions from liability;  
(5) the insurance period and the commencement time of insurance liability;  
(6) the sum insured;  
(7) the insurance premium and the method of payment;  
(8) the method of paying compensation or insurance benefits;  
(9) liability for breach of contract and dispute resolution; and  
(10) the year, month and day of conclusion of the contract.  
The applicant and the insurer may agree on other matters related to the insurance.  
A beneficiary is a person designated by the insured or the applicant under a personal insurance contract who is entitled to claim insurance benefits. The applicant or the insured may be the beneficiary.  
Sum insured means the maximum amount for which the insurer assumes the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　采用保险人提供的格式条款订立的保险合同中的下列条款无效：  
（一）免除保险人依法应承担的义务或者加重投保人、被保险人责任的；  
（二）排除投保人、被保险人或者受益人依法享有的权利的。

**Article 19** The following clauses in an insurance contract concluded using standard clauses provided by the insurer shall be void:  
(1) clauses that exclude the obligations that the insurer shall assume according to law or aggravate the liability of the applicant or the insured; and  
(2) clauses that exclude the rights that the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary enjoy according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　投保人和保险人可以协商变更合同内容。  
变更保险合同的，应当由保险人在保险单或者其他保险凭证上批注或者附贴批单，或者由投保人和保险人订立变更的书面协议。

**Article 20** The applicant and the insurer may negotiate and modify the contents of the contract.  
Where an insurance contract is modified, the insurer shall endorse the insurance policy or other insurance certificate or attach an endorsement slip thereto, or the applicant and the insurer shall enter into a written agreement on the modification.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　投保人、被保险人或者受益人知道保险事故发生后，应当及时通知保险人。故意或者因重大过失未及时通知，致使保险事故的性质、原因、损失程度等难以确定的，保险人对无法确定的部分，不承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任，但保险人通过其他途径已经及时知道或者应当及时知道保险事故发生的除外。

**Article 21** After becoming aware of the occurrence of an insured event, the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary shall promptly notify the insurer. If, due to intentional failure or gross negligence in giving prompt notice, the nature, cause, extent of loss, etc. of the insured event become difficult to ascertain, the insurer shall not assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits for the part that cannot be ascertained, except where the insurer has already learned or should have learned of the occurrence of the insured event in a timely manner through other means.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　保险事故发生后，按照保险合同请求保险人赔偿或者给付保险金时，投保人、被保险人或者受益人应当向保险人提供其所能提供的与确认保险事故的性质、原因、损失程度等有关的证明和资料。  
保险人按照合同的约定，认为有关的证明和资料不完整的，应当及时一次性通知投保人、被保险人或者受益人补充提供。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 22** After the occurrence of an insured event, when claiming compensation or insurance benefits from the insurer under the insurance contract, the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary shall provide the insurer with such evidence and materials as they can provide to verify the nature, cause, extent of loss, etc. of the insured event.  
Where the insurer considers, in accordance with the contract, that the relevant evidence and materials are incomplete, the insurer shall promptly notify the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary in one notice to supplement such evidence and materials.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　保险人收到被保险人或者受益人的赔偿或者给付保险金的请求后，应当及时作出核定；情形复杂的，应当在三十日内作出核定，但合同另有约定的除外。保险人应当将核定结果通知被保险人或者受益人；对属于保险责任的，在与被保险人或者受益人达成赔偿或者给付保险金的协议后十日内，履行赔偿或者给付保险金义务。保险合同对赔偿或者给付保险金的期限有约定的，保险人应当按照约定履行赔偿或者给付保险金义务。  
保险人未及时履行前款规定义务的，除支付保险金外，应当赔偿被保险人或者受益人因此受到的损失。  
任何单位和个人不得非法干预保险人履行赔偿或者给付保险金的义务，也不得限制被保险人或者受益人取得保险金的权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 23** After receiving a request for compensation or insurance benefits from the insured or the beneficiary, the insurer shall promptly assess the matter; if the circumstances are complex, the insurer shall make the assessment within thirty days, unless otherwise agreed in the contract. The insurer shall notify the insured or the beneficiary of the result of the assessment; if the matter falls within the scope of insurance liability, the insurer shall perform the obligation of paying compensation or insurance benefits within ten days after reaching an agreement with the insured or the beneficiary on the amount of compensation or insurance benefits. If the insurance contract specifies a time limit for paying compensation or insurance benefits, the insurer shall perform the obligation of paying compensation or insurance benefits in accordance with such time limit.  
Where the insurer fails to perform the obligations under the preceding paragraph in a timely manner, in addition to paying the insurance benefits, the insurer shall compensate the insured or the beneficiary for the losses incurred thereby.  
No entity or individual shall unlawfully interfere with the insurer's obligation to pay compensation or insurance benefits, nor restrict the right of the insured or the beneficiary to obtain insurance benefits.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　保险人依照[本法第二十三条](#art-23)的规定作出核定后，对不属于保险责任的，应当自作出核定之日起三日内向被保险人或者受益人发出拒绝赔偿或者拒绝给付保险金通知书，并说明理由。

**Article 24** After making an assessment in accordance with Article 23 of this Law, if the matter does not fall within the scope of insurance liability, the insurer shall, within three days from the date of making the assessment, issue a notice of refusal to pay compensation or insurance benefits to the insured or the beneficiary, and shall state the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　保险人自收到赔偿或者给付保险金的请求和有关证明、资料之日起六十日内，对其赔偿或者给付保险金的数额不能确定的，应当根据已有证明和资料可以确定的数额先予支付；保险人最终确定赔偿或者给付保险金的数额后，应当支付相应的差额。

**Article 25** If, within sixty days from the date of receipt of the request for compensation or insurance benefits and the relevant evidence and materials, the insurer is unable to determine the amount of compensation or insurance benefits, the insurer shall first make a payment based on the amount that can be determined from the existing evidence and materials; after the insurer finally determines the amount of compensation or insurance benefits, the insurer shall pay the corresponding difference.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人寿保险以外的其他保险的被保险人或者受益人，向保险人请求赔偿或者给付保险金的诉讼时效期间为二年，自其知道或者应当知道保险事故发生之日起计算。  
人寿保险的被保险人或者受益人向保险人请求给付保险金的诉讼时效期间为五年，自其知道或者应当知道保险事故发生之日起计算。

**Article 26** The limitation of action for an insured or beneficiary under any insurance other than life insurance to claim compensation or insurance benefits from the insurer shall be two years, counting from the date on which he knows or should know of the occurrence of the insured event.  
The limitation of action for an insured or beneficiary under life insurance to claim insurance benefits from the insurer shall be five years, counting from the date on which he knows or should know of the occurrence of the insured event.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　未发生保险事故，被保险人或者受益人谎称发生了保险事故，向保险人提出赔偿或者给付保险金请求的，保险人有权解除合同，并不退还保险费。  
投保人、被保险人故意制造保险事故的，保险人有权解除合同，不承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任；除[本法第四十三条](#art-43)规定外，不退还保险费。  
保险事故发生后，投保人、被保险人或者受益人以伪造、变造的有关证明、资料或者其他证据，编造虚假的事故原因或者夸大损失程度的，保险人对其虚报的部分不承担赔偿或者给付保险金的责任。  
投保人、被保险人或者受益人有前三款规定行为之一，致使保险人支付保险金或者支出费用的，应当退回或者赔偿。

**Article 27** Where no insured event occurs, but the insured or the beneficiary falsely claims an insured event has occurred and submits a request for compensation or insurance benefits to the insurer, the insurer shall have the right to rescind the contract and shall not refund the insurance premium.  
Where the applicant or the insured intentionally causes an insured event, the insurer shall have the right to rescind the contract and shall not assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits; except as provided in Article 43 of this Law, the insurer shall not refund the insurance premium.  
After the occurrence of an insured event, where the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary fabricates the cause of the event or exaggerates the extent of loss by using forged or altered relevant evidence, materials or other proof, the insurer shall not assume the liability for paying compensation or insurance benefits for the falsely reported part.  
Where the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary commits any of the acts specified in the preceding three paragraphs, causing the insurer to pay insurance benefits or incur expenses, such person shall return the amount or compensate for the losses.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　保险人将其承担的保险业务，以分保形式部分转移给其他保险人的，为再保险。  
应再保险接受人的要求，再保险分出人应当将其自负责任及原保险的有关情况书面告知再保险接受人。

**Article 28** Where an insurer partially transfers the insurance business it undertakes to other insurers in the form of reinsurance, it is called reinsurance.  
Upon the request of the reinsurer, the ceding insurer shall inform the reinsurer in writing of its own retained liability and relevant information about the original insurance.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　再保险接受人不得向原保险的投保人要求支付保险费。  
原保险的被保险人或者受益人不得向再保险接受人提出赔偿或者给付保险金的请求。  
再保险分出人不得以再保险接受人未履行再保险责任为由，拒绝履行或者迟延履行其原保险责任。

**Article 29** The reinsurer shall not demand payment of the insurance premium from the applicant of the original insurance.  
The insured or beneficiary of the original insurance shall not make any claim for compensation or insurance benefits against the reinsurer.  
The ceding insurer shall not, on the ground that the reinsurer fails to perform its reinsurance liability, refuse to perform or delay the performance of its liability under the original insurance.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　采用保险人提供的格式条款订立的保险合同，保险人与投保人、被保险人或者受益人对合同条款有争议的，应当按照通常理解予以解释。对合同条款有两种以上解释的，人民法院或者仲裁机构应当作出有利于被保险人和受益人的解释。

**Article 30** Where a dispute arises between the insurer and the applicant, the insured or the beneficiary over the clauses of an insurance contract concluded using the standard clauses provided by the insurer, such clauses shall be interpreted in accordance with common understanding. Where there are two or more interpretations of the contract clauses, the People's Court or an arbitration institution shall render an interpretation favorable to the insured and the beneficiary.

#### 第二节　人身保险合同

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　投保人对下列人员具有保险利益：  
（一）本人；  
（二）配偶、子女、父母；  
（三）前项以外与投保人有抚养、赡养或者扶养关系的家庭其他成员、近亲属；  
（四）与投保人有劳动关系的劳动者。  
除前款规定外，被保险人同意投保人为其订立合同的，视为投保人对被保险人具有保险利益。  
订立合同时，投保人对被保险人不具有保险利益的，合同无效。

**Article 31** An applicant shall have an insurable interest in the following persons:  
(1) the applicant himself or herself;  
(2) the applicant's spouse, children, and parents;  
(3) other family members and close relatives who, other than those specified in the preceding subparagraph, have a relationship of support, maintenance, or care with the applicant;  
(4) workers who have a labor relationship with the applicant.  
In addition to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, if the insured agrees that the applicant enters into a contract for him or her, the applicant shall be deemed to have an insurable interest in the insured.  
Where, at the time of concluding the contract, the applicant has no insurable interest in the insured, the contract shall be void.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　投保人申报的被保险人年龄不真实，并且其真实年龄不符合合同约定的年龄限制的，保险人可以解除合同，并按照合同约定退还保险单的现金价值。保险人行使合同解除权，适用[本法第十六条](#art-16)第三款、第六款的规定。  
投保人申报的被保险人年龄不真实，致使投保人支付的保险费少于应付保险费的，保险人有权更正并要求投保人补交保险费，或者在给付保险金时按照实付保险费与应付保险费的比例支付。  
投保人申报的被保险人年龄不真实，致使投保人支付的保险费多于应付保险费的，保险人应当将多收的保险费退还投保人。

**Article 32** If the age of the insured declared by the applicant is untrue and the actual age does not meet the age limit agreed in the contract, the insurer may dissolve the contract and refund the cash value of the insurance policy according to the contract. The exercise by the insurer of the right to dissolve the contract shall be subject to the provisions of paragraphs 3 and 6 of Article 16 of this Law.  
If the untrue age of the insured declared by the applicant results in the applicant paying less premium than the amount payable, the insurer shall have the right to make corrections and demand that the applicant make up the premium, or, when paying the insurance proceeds, shall pay in proportion to the actual premium paid to the premium payable.  
If the untrue age of the insured declared by the applicant results in the applicant paying more premium than the amount payable, the insurer shall refund the excess premium to the applicant.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　投保人不得为无民事行为能力人投保以死亡为给付保险金条件的人身保险，保险人也不得承保。  
父母为其未成年子女投保的人身保险，不受前款规定限制。但是，因被保险人死亡给付的保险金总和不得超过国务院保险监督管理机构规定的限额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** An applicant shall not apply for, and an insurer shall not underwrite, personal insurance for a person without civil capacity where the payment of insurance proceeds is conditioned on death.  
Personal insurance taken out by a parent for his or her minor child shall not be subject to the restriction in the preceding paragraph. However, the total amount of insurance benefits payable upon the death of the insured shall not exceed the limit prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同，未经被保险人同意并认可保险金额的，合同无效。  
按照以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同所签发的保险单，未经被保险人书面同意，不得转让或者质押。  
父母为其未成年子女投保的人身保险，不受本条第一款规定限制。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第2条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 34** A contract under which the payment of insurance proceeds is conditioned on death shall be void if the insured has not consented to and confirmed the sum insured.  
The insurance policy issued under a contract under which the payment of insurance proceeds is conditioned on death shall not be transferred or pledged without the written consent of the insured.  
Personal insurance taken out by a parent for his or her minor child shall not be subject to the restriction in paragraph 1 of this Article.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　投保人可以按照合同约定向保险人一次支付全部保险费或者分期支付保险费。

**Article 35** The applicant may, in accordance with the contract, pay the entire premium to the insurer in a lump sum or pay the premium in installments.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　合同约定分期支付保险费，投保人支付首期保险费后，除合同另有约定外，投保人自保险人催告之日起超过三十日未支付当期保险费，或者超过约定的期限六十日未支付当期保险费的，合同效力中止，或者由保险人按照合同约定的条件减少保险金额。  
被保险人在前款规定期限内发生保险事故的，保险人应当按照合同约定给付保险金，但可以扣减欠交的保险费。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-8" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 36** Where the contract stipulates payment of the premium in installments, after the applicant has paid the first installment, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, if the applicant fails to pay the current premium within 30 days from the date of the insurer's demand for payment, or within 60 days from the expiry of the agreed time limit for payment, the validity of the contract shall be suspended, or the insurer may reduce the sum insured in accordance with the conditions agreed in the contract.  
If an insured event occurs within the period specified in the preceding paragraph, the insurer shall pay the insurance proceeds in accordance with the contract, but may deduct the overdue premium.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　合同效力依照[本法第三十六条](#art-36)规定中止的，经保险人与投保人协商并达成协议，在投保人补交保险费后，合同效力恢复。但是，自合同效力中止之日起满二年双方未达成协议的，保险人有权解除合同。  
保险人依照前款规定解除合同的，应当按照合同约定退还保险单的现金价值。

**Article 37** Where the validity of a contract is suspended in accordance with Article 36 of this Law, the validity of the contract shall be restored after the insurer and the applicant have negotiated and reached an agreement and the applicant has paid the overdue premium. However, if no agreement has been reached between the parties within two years from the date of suspension of the validity of the contract, the insurer shall have the right to dissolve the contract.  
Where the insurer dissolves the contract in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall refund the cash value of the insurance policy in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　保险人对人寿保险的保险费，不得用诉讼方式要求投保人支付。

**Article 38** No insurer may demand payment of a premium for life insurance from the applicant by means of litigation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人身保险的受益人由被保险人或者投保人指定。  
投保人指定受益人时须经被保险人同意。投保人为与其有劳动关系的劳动者投保人身保险，不得指定被保险人及其近亲属以外的人为受益人。  
被保险人为无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人的，可以由其监护人指定受益人。

**Article 39** The beneficiary of personal insurance shall be designated by the insured or the applicant.  
When designating a beneficiary, the applicant shall obtain the consent of the insured. Where an applicant takes out personal insurance for a worker with whom it has a labor relationship, it shall not designate any person other than the insured and the insured's close relatives as the beneficiary.  
Where the insured is a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, the beneficiary may be designated by the insured's guardian.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　被保险人或者投保人可以指定一人或者数人为受益人。  
受益人为数人的，被保险人或者投保人可以确定受益顺序和受益份额；未确定受益份额的，受益人按照相等份额享有受益权。

**Article 40** The insured or the applicant may designate one or more persons as beneficiaries.  
Where there are multiple beneficiaries, the insured or the applicant may determine the order of entitlement and the shares of benefits; if the shares of benefits are not determined, the beneficiaries shall enjoy the right to benefits in equal shares.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　被保险人或者投保人可以变更受益人并书面通知保险人。保险人收到变更受益人的书面通知后，应当在保险单或者其他保险凭证上批注或者附贴批单。  
投保人变更受益人时须经被保险人同意。

**Article 41** The insured or the applicant may change the beneficiary and shall notify the insurer in writing. After receiving the written notice of the change of beneficiary, the insurer shall endorse or attach an endorsement on the insurance policy or other insurance certificate.  
When the applicant changes the beneficiary, the consent of the insured shall be obtained.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　被保险人死亡后，有下列情形之一的，保险金作为被保险人的遗产，由保险人依照《中华人民共和国继承法》的规定履行给付保险金的义务：  
（一）没有指定受益人，或者受益人指定不明无法确定的；  
（二）受益人先于被保险人死亡，没有其他受益人的；  
（三）受益人依法丧失受益权或者放弃受益权，没有其他受益人的。  
受益人与被保险人在同一事件中死亡，且不能确定死亡先后顺序的，推定受益人死亡在先。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第15条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 42** After the death of the insured, if any of the following circumstances exists, the insurance proceeds shall be treated as the estate of the insured, and the insurer shall perform the obligation to pay the insurance proceeds in accordance with the provisions of the Law of Succession of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) no beneficiary is designated, or the designation of the beneficiary is unclear and cannot be determined;  
(2) the beneficiary dies before the insured and there are no other beneficiaries;  
(3) the beneficiary loses or waives the right to benefits according to law and there are no other beneficiaries.  
Where the beneficiary and the insured die in the same event and the order of death cannot be determined, the beneficiary shall be presumed to have died first.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　投保人故意造成被保险人死亡、伤残或者疾病的，保险人不承担给付保险金的责任。投保人已交足二年以上保险费的，保险人应当按照合同约定向其他权利人退还保险单的现金价值。  
受益人故意造成被保险人死亡、伤残、疾病的，或者故意杀害被保险人未遂的，该受益人丧失受益权。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 43** Where the applicant intentionally causes the death, disability, or illness of the insured, the insurer shall not be liable for the payment of insurance proceeds. If the applicant has paid the premiums in full for more than two years, the insurer shall refund the cash value of the insurance policy to the other entitled persons in accordance with the contract.  
Where a beneficiary intentionally causes the death, disability, or illness of the insured, or attempts to kill the insured, such beneficiary shall lose the right to benefits.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　以被保险人死亡为给付保险金条件的合同，自合同成立或者合同效力恢复之日起二年内，被保险人自杀的，保险人不承担给付保险金的责任，但被保险人自杀时为无民事行为能力人的除外。  
保险人依照前款规定不承担给付保险金责任的，应当按照合同约定退还保险单的现金价值。

**Article 44** For a contract under which the payment of insurance proceeds is conditioned on the death of the insured, if the insured commits suicide within two years from the date of conclusion of the contract or the restoration of its validity, the insurer shall not be liable for the payment of insurance proceeds, except where the insured was without civil capacity at the time of suicide.  
Where, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the insurer is not liable for the payment of insurance proceeds, it shall refund the cash value of the insurance policy in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　因被保险人故意犯罪或者抗拒依法采取的刑事强制措施导致其伤残或者死亡的，保险人不承担给付保险金的责任。投保人已交足二年以上保险费的，保险人应当按照合同约定退还保险单的现金价值。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第22条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）》第23条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 45** Where the insured suffers disability or death as a result of intentionally committing a crime or resisting criminal compulsory measures taken according to law, the insurer shall not be liable for the payment of insurance proceeds. If the applicant has paid the premiums in full for more than two years, the insurer shall refund the cash value of the insurance policy in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　被保险人因第三者的行为而发生死亡、伤残或者疾病等保险事故的，保险人向被保险人或者受益人给付保险金后，不享有向第三者追偿的权利，但被保险人或者受益人仍有权向第三者请求赔偿。

**Article 46** Where an insured event such as death, disability, or illness of the insured occurs due to the act of a third party, after the insurer pays the insurance proceeds to the insured or the beneficiary, the insurer shall not have the right of recourse against the third party; however, the insured or the beneficiary shall still have the right to claim compensation from the third party.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　投保人解除合同的，保险人应当自收到解除合同通知之日起三十日内，按照合同约定退还保险单的现金价值。

**Article 47** Where the applicant dissolves the contract, the insurer shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice of dissolution of the contract, refund the cash value of the insurance policy in accordance with the contract.

#### 第三节　财产保险合同

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　保险事故发生时，被保险人对保险标的不具有保险利益的，不得向保险人请求赔偿保险金。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 48** Where, at the time of the occurrence of an insured event, the insured has no insurable interest in the subject matter insured, the insured shall not claim insurance proceeds from the insurer.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　保险标的转让的，保险标的的受让人承继被保险人的权利和义务。  
保险标的转让的，被保险人或者受让人应当及时通知保险人，但货物运输保险合同和另有约定的合同除外。  
因保险标的转让导致危险程度显著增加的，保险人自收到前款规定的通知之日起三十日内，可以按照合同约定增加保险费或者解除合同。保险人解除合同的，应当将已收取的保险费，按照合同约定扣除自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止应收的部分后，退还投保人。  
被保险人、受让人未履行本条第二款规定的通知义务的，因转让导致保险标的危险程度显著增加而发生的保险事故，保险人不承担赔偿保险金的责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 49** Where the subject matter insured is assigned, the assignee of the subject matter insured shall succeed to the rights and obligations of the insured.  
Where the subject matter insured is assigned, the insured or the assignee shall notify the insurer in a timely manner, except for cargo insurance contracts and contracts otherwise agreed.  
Where the assignment of the subject matter insured results in a material increase in the degree of risk, the insurer may, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notice specified in the preceding paragraph, increase the premium or dissolve the contract in accordance with the contract. If the insurer dissolves the contract, it shall refund the collected premium to the applicant after deducting the portion receivable from the commencement of insurance liability to the date of dissolution of the contract in accordance with the contract.  
Where the insured or the assignee fails to perform the obligation to give notice under paragraph 2 of this Article, and an insured event occurs due to the material increase in the degree of risk of the subject matter insured caused by the assignment, the insurer shall not be liable for the payment of insurance proceeds.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　货物运输保险合同和运输工具航程保险合同，保险责任开始后，合同当事人不得解除合同。

**Article 50** For a cargo transportation insurance contract or a voyage insurance contract for a means of transportation, the parties to the contract shall not dissolve the contract after the insurance liability commences.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　被保险人应当遵守国家有关消防、安全、生产操作、劳动保护等方面的规定，维护保险标的的安全。  
保险人可以按照合同约定对保险标的的安全状况进行检查，及时向投保人、被保险人提出消除不安全因素和隐患的书面建议。  
投保人、被保险人未按照约定履行其对保险标的的安全应尽责任的，保险人有权要求增加保险费或者解除合同。  
保险人为维护保险标的的安全，经被保险人同意，可以采取安全预防措施。

**Article 51** The insured shall comply with the state regulations on fire control, safety, production operations, labor protection, etc., and maintain the safety of the subject matter insured.  
The insurer may, in accordance with the contract, inspect the safety condition of the subject matter insured, and promptly make written proposals to the proposer and the insured for eliminating unsafe factors and hidden dangers.  
If the proposer or the insured fails to perform its obligations for the safety of the subject matter insured as agreed, the insurer shall have the right to demand an increase in the premium or dissolve the contract.  
The insurer may take safety prevention measures for maintaining the safety of the subject matter insured with the consent of the insured.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在合同有效期内，保险标的的危险程度显著增加的，被保险人应当按照合同约定及时通知保险人，保险人可以按照合同约定增加保险费或者解除合同。保险人解除合同的，应当将已收取的保险费，按照合同约定扣除自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止应收的部分后，退还投保人。  
被保险人未履行前款规定的通知义务的，因保险标的的危险程度显著增加而发生的保险事故，保险人不承担赔偿保险金的责任。

**Article 52** If, during the term of the contract, the risk level of the subject matter insured significantly increases, the insured shall promptly notify the insurer in accordance with the contract. The insurer may increase the premium or dissolve the contract in accordance with the contract. If the insurer dissolves the contract, it shall refund the premium already collected to the proposer after deducting the portion due from the commencement of insurance liability to the date of dissolution in accordance with the contract.  
If the insured fails to perform the notification obligation as provided in the preceding paragraph, the insurer shall not be liable for compensation for insurance losses arising from an insured event caused by the significant increase in the risk level of the subject matter insured.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　有下列情形之一的，除合同另有约定外，保险人应当降低保险费，并按日计算退还相应的保险费：  
（一）据以确定保险费率的有关情况发生变化，保险标的的危险程度明显减少的；  
（二）保险标的的保险价值明显减少的。

**Article 53** Under any of the following circumstances, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer shall reduce the premium and refund the corresponding premium calculated on a daily basis:  
(1) the relevant circumstances on which the premium rate is determined have changed, and the risk level of the subject matter insured has significantly decreased;  
(2) the insured value of the subject matter insured has significantly decreased.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　保险责任开始前，投保人要求解除合同的，应当按照合同约定向保险人支付手续费，保险人应当退还保险费。保险责任开始后，投保人要求解除合同的，保险人应当将已收取的保险费，按照合同约定扣除自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止应收的部分后，退还投保人。

**Article 54** Before the commencement of insurance liability, if the proposer requests dissolution of the contract, it shall pay a handling fee to the insurer in accordance with the contract, and the insurer shall refund the premium. After the commencement of insurance liability, if the proposer requests dissolution of the contract, the insurer shall refund the premium already collected to the proposer after deducting the portion due from the commencement of insurance liability to the date of dissolution in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　投保人和保险人约定保险标的的保险价值并在合同中载明的，保险标的发生损失时，以约定的保险价值为赔偿计算标准。  
投保人和保险人未约定保险标的的保险价值的，保险标的发生损失时，以保险事故发生时保险标的的实际价值为赔偿计算标准。  
保险金额不得超过保险价值。超过保险价值的，超过部分无效，保险人应当退还相应的保险费。  
保险金额低于保险价值的，除合同另有约定外，保险人按照保险金额与保险价值的比例承担赔偿保险金的责任。

**Article 55** If the proposer and the insurer agree on the insured value of the subject matter insured and specify it in the contract, in the event of loss of the subject matter insured, the agreed insured value shall be the standard for calculating compensation.  
If the proposer and the insurer have not agreed on the insured value of the subject matter insured, in the event of loss of the subject matter insured, the actual value of the subject matter insured at the time of the occurrence of the insured event shall be the standard for calculating compensation.  
The sum insured shall not exceed the insured value. If the sum insured exceeds the insured value, the excessive part shall be void, and the insurer shall refund the corresponding premium.  
If the sum insured is lower than the insured value, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer shall be liable for compensation in proportion to the sum insured against the insured value.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　重复保险的投保人应当将重复保险的有关情况通知各保险人。  
重复保险的各保险人赔偿保险金的总和不得超过保险价值。除合同另有约定外，各保险人按照其保险金额与保险金额总和的比例承担赔偿保险金的责任。  
重复保险的投保人可以就保险金额总和超过保险价值的部分，请求各保险人按比例返还保险费。  
重复保险是指投保人对同一保险标的、同一保险利益、同一保险事故分别与两个以上保险人订立保险合同，且保险金额总和超过保险价值的保险。

**Article 56** The proposer of double insurance shall notify each insurer of the relevant information regarding the double insurance.  
The total amount of compensation for insurance losses paid by each insurer of double insurance shall not exceed the insured value. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, each insurer shall be liable for compensation in proportion to its sum insured against the total sum insured.  
The proposer of double insurance may request each insurer to refund the premium proportionally for the portion where the total sum insured exceeds the insured value.  
Double insurance refers to insurance where the proposer enters into insurance contracts with two or more insurers separately for the same subject matter insured, the same insurable interest, and the same insured event, and the total sum insured exceeds the insured value.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　保险事故发生时，被保险人应当尽力采取必要的措施，防止或者减少损失。  
保险事故发生后，被保险人为防止或者减少保险标的的损失所支付的必要的、合理的费用，由保险人承担；保险人所承担的费用数额在保险标的损失赔偿金额以外另行计算，最高不超过保险金额的数额。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-6" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第6条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 57** When an insured event occurs, the insured shall make every effort to take necessary measures to prevent or mitigate losses.  
After the occurrence of an insured event, the necessary and reasonable expenses paid by the insured to prevent or mitigate the loss of the subject matter insured shall be borne by the insurer. The amount of expenses borne by the insurer shall be calculated separately from the compensation for the loss of the subject matter insured, and shall not exceed the sum insured.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　保险标的发生部分损失的，自保险人赔偿之日起三十日内，投保人可以解除合同；除合同另有约定外，保险人也可以解除合同，但应当提前十五日通知投保人。  
合同解除的，保险人应当将保险标的未受损失部分的保险费，按照合同约定扣除自保险责任开始之日起至合同解除之日止应收的部分后，退还投保人。

**Article 58** If the subject matter insured suffers a partial loss, the proposer may dissolve the contract within thirty days from the date of the insurer's compensation. Unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the insurer may also dissolve the contract, but shall notify the proposer fifteen days in advance.  
If the contract is dissolved, the insurer shall refund the premium for the portion of the subject matter insured not lost to the proposer after deducting the portion due from the commencement of insurance liability to the date of dissolution in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　保险事故发生后，保险人已支付了全部保险金额，并且保险金额等于保险价值的，受损保险标的的全部权利归于保险人；保险金额低于保险价值的，保险人按照保险金额与保险价值的比例取得受损保险标的的部分权利。

**Article 59** After the occurrence of an insured event, if the insurer has paid the full sum insured and the sum insured equals the insured value, all rights to the damaged subject matter insured shall vest in the insurer. If the sum insured is lower than the insured value, the insurer shall acquire partial rights to the damaged subject matter insured in proportion to the sum insured against the insured value.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　因第三者对保险标的的损害而造成保险事故的，保险人自向被保险人赔偿保险金之日起，在赔偿金额范围内代位行使被保险人对第三者请求赔偿的权利。  
前款规定的保险事故发生后，被保险人已经从第三者取得损害赔偿的，保险人赔偿保险金时，可以相应扣减被保险人从第三者已取得的赔偿金额。  
保险人依照本条第一款规定行使代位请求赔偿的权利，不影响被保险人就未取得赔偿的部分向第三者请求赔偿的权利。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第7条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 60** If an insured event is caused by damage to the subject matter insured by a third party, the insurer shall, from the date of paying the insurance compensation to the insured, exercise subrogation rights against the third party within the scope of the compensation paid.  
After the occurrence of the insured event as provided in the preceding paragraph, if the insured has already obtained compensation for the damage from the third party, the insurer may deduct the amount of compensation already obtained by the insured from the third party when paying the insurance compensation.  
The exercise by the insurer of the right to claim compensation by subrogation in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article shall not affect the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party for the part for which compensation has not been obtained.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　保险事故发生后，保险人未赔偿保险金之前，被保险人放弃对第三者请求赔偿的权利的，保险人不承担赔偿保险金的责任。  
保险人向被保险人赔偿保险金后，被保险人未经保险人同意放弃对第三者请求赔偿的权利的，该行为无效。  
被保险人故意或者因重大过失致使保险人不能行使代位请求赔偿的权利的，保险人可以扣减或者要求返还相应的保险金。

**Article 61** If, after the occurrence of an insured event but before the insurer pays the insurance compensation, the insured waives the right to claim compensation from the third party, the insurer shall not be liable for paying the insurance compensation.  
If, after the insurer pays the insurance compensation to the insured, the insured waives the right to claim compensation from the third party without the insurer's consent, such act shall be void.  
If the insured, intentionally or through gross negligence, prevents the insurer from exercising the right to claim compensation by subrogation, the insurer may deduct or demand the return of the corresponding insurance compensation.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　除被保险人的家庭成员或者其组成人员故意造成[本法第六十条](#art-60)第一款规定的保险事故外，保险人不得对被保险人的家庭成员或者其组成人员行使代位请求赔偿的权利。

**Article 62** Except where the insured event as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 60 of this Law is intentionally caused by a family member or a staff member of the insured, the insurer shall not exercise the right to claim compensation by subrogation against such family member or staff member of the insured.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　保险人向第三者行使代位请求赔偿的权利时，被保险人应当向保险人提供必要的文件和所知道的有关情况。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-11" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 63** When the insurer exercises the right to claim compensation by subrogation against a third party, the insured shall provide the insurer with necessary documents and relevant information within its knowledge.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　保险人、被保险人为查明和确定保险事故的性质、原因和保险标的的损失程度所支付的必要的、合理的费用，由保险人承担。

**Article 64** The necessary and reasonable expenses incurred by the insurer and the insured to ascertain and determine the nature, cause and extent of loss of the subject matter insured in an insured event shall be borne by the insurer.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　保险人对责任保险的被保险人给第三者造成的损害，可以依照法律的规定或者合同的约定，直接向该第三者赔偿保险金。  
责任保险的被保险人给第三者造成损害，被保险人对第三者应负的赔偿责任确定的，根据被保险人的请求，保险人应当直接向该第三者赔偿保险金。被保险人怠于请求的，第三者有权就其应获赔偿部分直接向保险人请求赔偿保险金。  
责任保险的被保险人给第三者造成损害，被保险人未向该第三者赔偿的，保险人不得向被保险人赔偿保险金。  
责任保险是指以被保险人对第三者依法应负的赔偿责任为保险标的的保险。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第15条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）》第20条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 65** The insurer may, in accordance with the provisions of the law or the terms of the contract, directly pay insurance compensation to the third party for damage caused by the insured of liability insurance to such third party.  
If damage is caused to a third party by the insured of liability insurance, and the liability for compensation that the insured owes to the third party is determined, the insurer shall, upon the request of the insured, directly pay insurance compensation to the third party. If the insured fails to make such request, the third party shall have the right to directly claim insurance compensation from the insurer for the portion of compensation it is entitled to receive.  
If damage is caused to a third party by the insured of liability insurance, and the insured has not compensated the third party, the insurer shall not pay insurance compensation to the insured.  
Liability insurance refers to insurance where the subject matter insured is the liability for compensation that the insured owes to a third party according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　责任保险的被保险人因给第三者造成损害的保险事故而被提起仲裁或者诉讼的，被保险人支付的仲裁或者诉讼费用以及其他必要的、合理的费用，除合同另有约定外，由保险人承担。

**Article 66** If the insured of liability insurance is subject to arbitration or litigation due to an insured event that causes damage to a third party, the arbitration or litigation costs and other necessary and reasonable expenses paid by the insured shall be borne by the insurer, unless otherwise agreed in the contract.

### 第三章　保险公司

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　设立保险公司应当经国务院保险监督管理机构批准。  
国务院保险监督管理机构审查保险公司的设立申请时，应当考虑保险业的发展和公平竞争的需要。

**Article 67** The establishment of an insurance company shall be subject to the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
When examining an application for the establishment of an insurance company, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall take into account the development of the insurance industry and the need for fair competition.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　设立保险公司应当具备下列条件：  
（一）主要股东具有持续盈利能力，信誉良好，最近三年内无重大违法违规记录，净资产不低于人民币二亿元；  
（二）有符合本法和《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的章程；  
（三）有符合本法规定的注册资本；  
（四）有具备任职专业知识和业务工作经验的董事、监事和高级管理人员；  
（五）有健全的组织机构和管理制度；  
（六）有符合要求的营业场所和与经营业务有关的其他设施；  
（七）法律、行政法规和国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他条件。

**Article 68** To establish an insurance company, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) the main shareholders shall have sustained profitability, a good record of credit standing, no record of any major violation of laws or regulations in the past three years, and a net asset of not less than RMB 200 million yuan;  
(2) a company constitution that conforms to the provisions of this Law and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) a registered capital that conforms to the provisions of this Law;  
(4) directors, supervisors, and senior officers with the professional knowledge and work experience necessary for their positions;  
(5) a sound organizational structure and management system;  
(6) business premises and other facilities that meet the requirements and are necessary for the operation of insurance business;  
(7) other conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　设立保险公司，其注册资本的最低限额为人民币二亿元。  
国务院保险监督管理机构根据保险公司的业务范围、经营规模，可以调整其注册资本的最低限额，但不得低于本条第一款规定的限额。  
保险公司的注册资本必须为实缴货币资本。

**Article 69** The minimum registered capital for the establishment of an insurance company shall be RMB 200 million yuan.  
The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may adjust the minimum amount of registered capital according to the scope of business and scale of operations of the insurance company, but such amount shall not be lower than the minimum amount provided for in the first paragraph of this Article.  
The registered capital of an insurance company must be paid-in monetary capital.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　申请设立保险公司，应当向国务院保险监督管理机构提出书面申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）设立申请书，申请书应当载明拟设立的保险公司的名称、注册资本、业务范围等；  
（二）可行性研究报告；  
（三）筹建方案；  
（四）投资人的营业执照或者其他背景资料，经会计师事务所审计的上一年度财务会计报告；  
（五）投资人认可的筹备组负责人和拟任董事长、经理名单及本人认可证明；  
（六）国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他材料。

**Article 70** An applicant for the establishment of an insurance company shall submit a written application to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council and provide the following materials:  
(1) an application letter stating the name, registered capital, and scope of business of the proposed insurance company;  
(2) a feasibility study report;  
(3) a preparation plan;  
(4) the business license of the investors or other background information, and the financial accounting report for the previous year audited by an accounting firm;  
(5) the list of the person in charge of the preparatory group recognized by the investors and the proposed chairman and manager, and their letters of acceptance;  
(6) other materials prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国务院保险监督管理机构应当对设立保险公司的申请进行审查，自受理之日起六个月内作出批准或者不批准筹建的决定，并书面通知申请人。决定不批准的，应当书面说明理由。

**Article 71** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall examine the application for the establishment of an insurance company, and shall, within six months from the date of acceptance, make a decision on whether to approve the preparation, and notify the applicant in writing. In the case of disapproval, the applicant shall be notified in writing of the reasons.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　申请人应当自收到批准筹建通知之日起一年内完成筹建工作；筹建期间不得从事保险经营活动。

**Article 72** The applicant shall complete the preparation work within one year from the date of receipt of the notice of approval for preparation; during the preparation period, no insurance business activities shall be conducted.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　筹建工作完成后，申请人具备[本法第六十八条](#art-68)规定的设立条件的，可以向国务院保险监督管理机构提出开业申请。  
国务院保险监督管理机构应当自受理开业申请之日起六十日内，作出批准或者不批准开业的决定。决定批准的，颁发经营保险业务许可证；决定不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 73** Upon completion of the preparation work, where the applicant satisfies the conditions for establishment as provided in Article 68 of this Law, the applicant may submit an application for commencing business to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of acceptance of the application for commencing business, make a decision on whether to approve the commencement of business. In the case of approval, the authority shall issue a license for insurance business operations; in the case of disapproval, the applicant shall be notified in writing of the reasons.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　保险公司在中华人民共和国境内设立分支机构，应当经保险监督管理机构批准。  
保险公司分支机构不具有法人资格，其民事责任由保险公司承担。

**Article 74** The establishment of a branch by an insurance company within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be subject to the approval of the insurance regulatory authority.  
A branch of an insurance company does not possess the status of a legal person, and its civil liability shall be borne by the insurance company.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　保险公司申请设立分支机构，应当向保险监督管理机构提出书面申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）设立申请书；  
（二）拟设机构三年业务发展规划和市场分析材料；  
（三）拟任高级管理人员的简历及相关证明材料；  
（四）国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他材料。

**Article 75** An insurance company applying to establish a branch shall submit a written application to the insurance regulatory authority and provide the following materials:  
(1) an application letter;  
(2) a three-year business development plan of the proposed branch and market analysis materials;  
(3) resumes and relevant supporting documents of the proposed senior officers;  
(4) other materials prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　保险监督管理机构应当对保险公司设立分支机构的申请进行审查，自受理之日起六十日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。决定批准的，颁发分支机构经营保险业务许可证；决定不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 76** The insurance regulatory authority shall examine the application for the establishment of a branch by an insurance company, and shall, within 60 days from the date of acceptance, make a decision on whether to approve the establishment. In the case of approval, the authority shall issue a license for insurance business operations of the branch; in the case of disapproval, the applicant shall be notified in writing of the reasons.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　经批准设立的保险公司及其分支机构，凭经营保险业务许可证向工商行政管理机关办理登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 77** An insurance company or its branch that has been approved for establishment shall, by presenting the license for insurance business operations, register with the administrative authority for industry and commerce and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　保险公司及其分支机构自取得经营保险业务许可证之日起六个月内，无正当理由未向工商行政管理机关办理登记的，其经营保险业务许可证失效。

**Article 78** Where an insurance company or its branch fails to register with the administrative authority for industry and commerce within six months from the date of obtaining the insurance business permit without justifiable cause, its insurance business permit shall cease to have effect.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　保险公司在中华人民共和国境外设立子公司、分支机构，应当经国务院保险监督管理机构批准。

**Article 79** An insurance company that establishes subsidiaries or branches outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall obtain approval from the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　外国保险机构在中华人民共和国境内设立代表机构，应当经国务院保险监督管理机构批准。代表机构不得从事保险经营活动。

**Article 80** A foreign insurance institution that establishes a representative office within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall obtain approval from the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council. The representative office shall not engage in insurance business operations.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　保险公司的董事、监事和高级管理人员，应当品行良好，熟悉与保险相关的法律、行政法规，具有履行职责所需的经营管理能力，并在任职前取得保险监督管理机构核准的任职资格。  
保险公司高级管理人员的范围由国务院保险监督管理机构规定。

**Article 81** The directors, supervisors and senior officers of an insurance company shall have good conduct, be familiar with the laws and administrative regulations relating to insurance, possess the operational management ability required for performing their duties, and obtain the qualifications approved by the insurance regulatory authority before taking office.  
The scope of senior officers of an insurance company shall be prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　有[《中华人民共和国公司法》第一百四十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-146)规定的情形或者下列情形之一的，不得担任保险公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员：  
（一）因违法行为或者违纪行为被金融监督管理机构取消任职资格的金融机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员，自被取消任职资格之日起未逾五年的；  
（二）因违法行为或者违纪行为被吊销执业资格的律师、注册会计师或者资产评估机构、验证机构等机构的专业人员，自被吊销执业资格之日起未逾五年的。

**Article 82** A person who falls under the circumstances specified in Article 146 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China or under any of the following circumstances shall not serve as a director, supervisor or senior officer of an insurance company:  
(1) a director, supervisor or senior officer of a financial institution whose qualifications for holding office have been disqualified by a financial regulatory authority due to an illegal or disciplinary act, where fewer than five years have elapsed since the date of such disqualification;  
(2) a lawyer, certified public accountant, or a professional of an asset appraisal institution, verification institution or other institution whose practicing qualification has been revoked due to an illegal or disciplinary act, where fewer than five years have elapsed since the date of such revocation.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　保险公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员执行公司职务时违反法律、行政法规或者公司章程的规定，给公司造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 83** Where a director, supervisor or senior officer of an insurance company violates any law, administrative regulation or the company's articles of association while performing his duties for the company, causing losses to the company, he shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　保险公司有下列情形之一的，应当经保险监督管理机构批准：  
（一）变更名称；  
（二）变更注册资本；  
（三）变更公司或者分支机构的营业场所；  
（四）撤销分支机构；  
（五）公司分立或者合并；  
（六）修改公司章程；  
（七）变更出资额占有限责任公司资本总额百分之五以上的股东，或者变更持有股份有限公司股份百分之五以上的股东；  
（八）国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他情形。

**Article 84** An insurance company shall obtain approval from the insurance regulatory authority in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) change of name;  
(2) change of registered capital;  
(3) change of business premises of the company or a branch;  
(4) dissolution of a branch;  
(5) division or merger of the company;  
(6) amendment of the company's articles of association;  
(7) change of a shareholder whose capital contribution accounts for 5% or more of the total capital of a limited liability company, or change of a shareholder holding 5% or more of the shares of a joint stock limited company; or  
(8) other circumstances prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　保险公司应当聘用专业人员，建立精算报告制度和合规报告制度。

**Article 85** An insurance company shall employ professionals and establish an actuarial reporting system and a compliance reporting system.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　保险公司应当按照保险监督管理机构的规定，报送有关报告、报表、文件和资料。  
保险公司的偿付能力报告、财务会计报告、精算报告、合规报告及其他有关报告、报表、文件和资料必须如实记录保险业务事项，不得有虚假记载、误导性陈述和重大遗漏。

**Article 86** An insurance company shall submit relevant reports, statements, documents and materials in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority.  
The solvency report, financial accounting report, actuarial report, compliance report and other relevant reports, statements, documents and materials of an insurance company shall truthfully record insurance business matters and shall not contain any false records, misleading statements or material omissions.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　保险公司应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定妥善保管业务经营活动的完整账簿、原始凭证和有关资料。  
前款规定的账簿、原始凭证和有关资料的保管期限，自保险合同终止之日起计算，保险期间在一年以下的不得少于五年，保险期间超过一年的不得少于十年。

**Article 87** An insurance company shall properly keep complete account books, original vouchers and relevant materials of its business operations in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The retention period for the account books, original vouchers and relevant materials as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be calculated from the date of termination of the insurance contract, and shall be not less than five years if the insurance period is less than one year, and not less than ten years if the insurance period exceeds one year.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　保险公司聘请或者解聘会计师事务所、资产评估机构、资信评级机构等中介服务机构，应当向保险监督管理机构报告；解聘会计师事务所、资产评估机构、资信评级机构等中介服务机构，应当说明理由。

**Article 88** Where an insurance company engages or dismisses an accounting firm, asset appraisal institution, credit rating agency or other intermediary service institution, it shall report to the insurance regulatory authority; where it dismisses an accounting firm, asset appraisal institution, credit rating agency or other intermediary service institution, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　保险公司因分立、合并需要解散，或者股东会、股东大会决议解散，或者公司章程规定的解散事由出现，经国务院保险监督管理机构批准后解散。  
经营有人寿保险业务的保险公司，除因分立、合并或者被依法撤销外，不得解散。  
保险公司解散，应当依法成立清算组进行清算。

**Article 89** An insurance company that needs to dissolve due to division or merger, or is dissolved by a resolution of the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders, or upon the occurrence of the dissolution event specified in the company's articles of association, shall dissolve after obtaining approval from the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
An insurance company engaged in life insurance business shall not dissolve except due to division, merger or being lawfully revoked.  
Where an insurance company dissolves, it shall establish a liquidation group according to law to conduct liquidation.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　保险公司有[《中华人民共和国企业破产法》第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)规定情形的，经国务院保险监督管理机构同意，保险公司或者其债权人可以依法向人民法院申请重整、和解或者破产清算；国务院保险监督管理机构也可以依法向人民法院申请对该保险公司进行重整或者破产清算。

**Article 90** Where an insurance company falls under the circumstances specified in Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China, with the consent of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, the insurance company or its creditors may apply to the People's Court for reorganization, settlement or bankruptcy liquidation according to law; the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may also apply to the People's Court for reorganization or bankruptcy liquidation of such insurance company according to law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　破产财产在优先清偿破产费用和共益债务后，按照下列顺序清偿：  
（一）所欠职工工资和医疗、伤残补助、抚恤费用，所欠应当划入职工个人账户的基本养老保险、基本医疗保险费用，以及法律、行政法规规定应当支付给职工的补偿金；  
（二）赔偿或者给付保险金；  
（三）保险公司欠缴的除第（一）项规定以外的社会保险费用和所欠税款；  
（四）普通破产债权。  
破产财产不足以清偿同一顺序的清偿要求的，按照比例分配。  
破产保险公司的董事、监事和高级管理人员的工资，按照该公司职工的平均工资计算。

**Article 91** After priority payment of bankruptcy expenses and debts of common interest, the bankruptcy property shall be distributed in the following order:  
(1) wages and medical, disability and death subsidies owed to employees, the basic old-age insurance premiums and basic medical insurance premiums that shall be transferred to the personal accounts of employees, and the compensation that shall be paid to employees as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations;  
(2) insurance compensation or insurance benefits payable;  
(3) social insurance premiums owed by the insurance company other than those specified in item (1) and taxes owed; and  
(4) ordinary bankruptcy claims.  
Where the bankruptcy property is insufficient to satisfy all claims of the same order, distribution shall be made on a pro rata basis.  
The wages of the directors, supervisors and senior officers of the bankrupt insurance company shall be calculated according to the average wage of the employees of such company.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　经营有人寿保险业务的保险公司被依法撤销或者被依法宣告破产的，其持有的人寿保险合同及责任准备金，必须转让给其他经营有人寿保险业务的保险公司；不能同其他保险公司达成转让协议的，由国务院保险监督管理机构指定经营有人寿保险业务的保险公司接受转让。  
转让或者由国务院保险监督管理机构指定接受转让前款规定的人寿保险合同及责任准备金的，应当维护被保险人、受益人的合法权益。

**Article 92** Where an insurance company engaged in life insurance business is lawfully revoked or declared bankrupt, its life insurance contracts and liability reserves shall be transferred to another insurance company engaged in life insurance business; if no transfer agreement can be reached with another insurance company, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall designate an insurance company engaged in life insurance business to accept the transfer.  
Where the life insurance contracts and liability reserves as prescribed in the preceding paragraph are transferred or are designated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council to be accepted, the lawful rights and interests of the insured and beneficiaries shall be protected.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　保险公司依法终止其业务活动，应当注销其经营保险业务许可证。

**Article 93** Where an insurance company terminates its business activities according to law, its insurance business permit shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　保险公司，除本法另有规定外，适用《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定。

**Article 94** Unless otherwise provided in this Law, an insurance company shall be governed by the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第四章　保险经营规则

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　保险公司的业务范围：  
（一）人身保险业务，包括人寿保险、健康保险、意外伤害保险等保险业务；  
（二）财产保险业务，包括财产损失保险、责任保险、信用保险、保证保险等保险业务；  
（三）国务院保险监督管理机构批准的与保险有关的其他业务。  
保险人不得兼营人身保险业务和财产保险业务。但是，经营财产保险业务的保险公司经国务院保险监督管理机构批准，可以经营短期健康保险业务和意外伤害保险业务。  
保险公司应当在国务院保险监督管理机构依法批准的业务范围内从事保险经营活动。

**Article 95** The business scope of an insurance company:  
(1) personal insurance business, including life insurance, health insurance, accident insurance and other insurance businesses;  
(2) property insurance business, including property loss insurance, liability insurance, credit insurance, guarantee insurance and other insurance businesses; and  
(3) other insurance-related businesses approved by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
An insurer shall not concurrently engage in personal insurance business and property insurance business. However, an insurance company engaged in property insurance business may, with the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, engage in short-term health insurance business and accident insurance business.  
An insurance company shall engage in insurance business operations within the business scope approved according to law by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　经国务院保险监督管理机构批准，保险公司可以经营[本法第九十五条](#art-95)规定的保险业务的下列再保险业务：  
（一）分出保险；  
（二）分入保险。

**Article 96** With the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, an insurance company may engage in the following reinsurance business for the insurance business as prescribed in Article 95 of this Law:  
(1) outward reinsurance; and  
(2) inward reinsurance.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　保险公司应当按照其注册资本总额的百分之二十提取保证金，存入国务院保险监督管理机构指定的银行，除公司清算时用于清偿债务外，不得动用。

**Article 97** An insurance company shall set aside a deposit equal to 20% of its total registered capital and deposit it in a bank designated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council; such deposit shall not be used except for repaying debts upon liquidation of the company.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　保险公司应当根据保障被保险人利益、保证偿付能力的原则，提取各项责任准备金。  
保险公司提取和结转责任准备金的具体办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构制定。

**Article 98** An insurance company shall, in accordance with the principles of safeguarding the interests of the insured and ensuring solvency, set aside various liability reserves.  
The specific measures for an insurance company to set aside and carry forward liability reserves shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　保险公司应当依法提取公积金。

**Article 99** An insurance company shall, according to law, withdraw statutory surplus funds.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　保险公司应当缴纳保险保障基金。  
保险保障基金应当集中管理，并在下列情形下统筹使用：  
（一）在保险公司被撤销或者被宣告破产时，向投保人、被保险人或者受益人提供救济；  
（二）在保险公司被撤销或者被宣告破产时，向依法接受其人寿保险合同的保险公司提供救济；  
（三）国务院规定的其他情形。  
保险保障基金筹集、管理和使用的具体办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 100** An insurance company shall contribute to an insurance protection fund.  
The insurance protection fund shall be managed centrally and be used in a coordinated manner under the following circumstances:  
(1) To provide relief to policy holders, the insured or beneficiaries when the insurance company is dissolved or declared bankrupt;  
(2) To provide relief to an insurance company that accepts the life insurance contracts of the insurance company dissolved or declared bankrupt according to law; and  
(3) Other circumstances as prescribed by the State Council.  
The specific measures for the raising, management and use of the insurance protection fund shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　保险公司应当具有与其业务规模和风险程度相适应的最低偿付能力。保险公司的认可资产减去认可负债的差额不得低于国务院保险监督管理机构规定的数额；低于规定数额的，应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的要求采取相应措施达到规定的数额。

**Article 101** An insurance company shall have a minimum solvency margin commensurate with its business scale and risk level. The balance of its admissible assets minus its admissible liabilities shall not be lower than the amount prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council; if it is lower than the prescribed amount, the insurance company shall take corresponding measures as required by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council to reach the prescribed amount.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　经营财产保险业务的保险公司当年自留保险费，不得超过其实有资本金加公积金总和的四倍。

**Article 102** The retained premiums in the current year of an insurance company engaged in property insurance business shall not exceed four times the sum of its paid-in capital and statutory surplus funds.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　保险公司对每一危险单位，即对一次保险事故可能造成的最大损失范围所承担的责任，不得超过其实有资本金加公积金总和的百分之十；超过的部分应当办理再保险。  
保险公司对危险单位的划分应当符合国务院保险监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 103** The liability undertaken by an insurance company for each risk unit, i.e., the maximum possible loss from a single insurance accident, shall not exceed ten percent of the sum of its paid-in capital and statutory surplus funds; the portion exceeding such limit shall be ceded for reinsurance.  
The classification of risk units by an insurance company shall comply with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　保险公司对危险单位的划分方法和巨灾风险安排方案，应当报国务院保险监督管理机构备案。

**Article 104** An insurance company shall report the method for classifying risk units and the arrangement plan for catastrophic risks to the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　保险公司应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定办理再保险，并审慎选择再保险接受人。

**Article 105** An insurance company shall arrange reinsurance in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council and prudently select reinsurers.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　保险公司的资金运用必须稳健，遵循安全性原则。  
保险公司的资金运用限于下列形式：  
（一）银行存款；  
（二）买卖债券、股票、证券投资基金份额等有价证券；  
（三）投资不动产；  
（四）国务院规定的其他资金运用形式。  
保险公司资金运用的具体管理办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构依照前两款的规定制定。

**Article 106** The utilization of funds of an insurance company must be sound and follow the principle of safety.  
The utilization of funds of an insurance company shall be limited to the following forms:  
(1) Bank deposits;  
(2) Trading of bonds, stocks, securities investment fund units and other marketable securities;  
(3) Investment in real estate; and  
(4) Other forms of fund utilization as prescribed by the State Council.  
The specific regulatory measures for the utilization of funds of an insurance company shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　经国务院保险监督管理机构会同国务院证券监督管理机构批准，保险公司可以设立保险资产管理公司。  
保险资产管理公司从事证券投资活动，应当遵守《中华人民共和国证券法》等法律、行政法规的规定。  
保险资产管理公司的管理办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 107** With the approval of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council in conjunction with the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, an insurance company may establish an insurance asset management company.  
An insurance asset management company conducting securities investment activities shall comply with the provisions of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws and administrative regulations.  
The regulatory measures for insurance asset management companies shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　保险公司应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定，建立对关联交易的管理和信息披露制度。

**Article 108** An insurance company shall, in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council, establish a management and information disclosure system for related-party transactions.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　保险公司的控股股东、实际控制人、董事、监事、高级管理人员不得利用关联交易损害公司的利益。

**Article 109** The controlling shareholders, actual controllers, directors, supervisors and senior officers of an insurance company shall not use related-party transactions to harm the interests of the company.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　保险公司应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定，真实、准确、完整地披露财务会计报告、风险管理状况、保险产品经营情况等重大事项。

**Article 110** An insurance company shall, in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council, truthfully, accurately and completely disclose its financial and accounting reports, risk management status, operation of insurance products and other major matters.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　保险公司从事保险销售的人员应当品行良好，具有保险销售所需的专业能力。保险销售人员的行为规范和管理办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构规定。

**Article 111** The personnel engaged in insurance sales of an insurance company shall have good conduct and possess the professional competence required for insurance sales. The code of conduct and regulatory measures for insurance sales personnel shall be prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　保险公司应当建立保险代理人登记管理制度，加强对保险代理人的培训和管理，不得唆使、诱导保险代理人进行违背诚信义务的活动。

**Article 112** An insurance company shall establish a registration and management system for insurance agents, strengthen the training and management of insurance agents, and shall not abet or induce insurance agents to engage in activities that breach their fiduciary duties.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　保险公司及其分支机构应当依法使用经营保险业务许可证，不得转让、出租、出借经营保险业务许可证。

**Article 113** An insurance company and its branches shall use the insurance business permit according to law, and shall not transfer, lease or lend the insurance business permit.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　保险公司应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定，公平、合理拟订保险条款和保险费率，不得损害投保人、被保险人和受益人的合法权益。  
保险公司应当按照合同约定和本法规定，及时履行赔偿或者给付保险金义务。

**Article 114** An insurance company shall, in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council, formulate insurance clauses and premium rates in a fair and reasonable manner, and shall not harm the legitimate rights and interests of policy holders, the insured and beneficiaries.  
An insurance company shall timely perform its obligation to pay compensation or insurance benefits in accordance with the contract and the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　保险公司开展业务，应当遵循公平竞争的原则，不得从事不正当竞争。

**Article 115** An insurance company shall, when conducting business, follow the principle of fair competition and shall not engage in unfair competition.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　保险公司及其工作人员在保险业务活动中不得有下列行为：  
（一）欺骗投保人、被保险人或者受益人；  
（二）对投保人隐瞒与保险合同有关的重要情况；  
（三）阻碍投保人履行本法规定的如实告知义务，或者诱导其不履行本法规定的如实告知义务；  
（四）给予或者承诺给予投保人、被保险人、受益人保险合同约定以外的保险费回扣或者其他利益；  
（五）拒不依法履行保险合同约定的赔偿或者给付保险金义务；  
（六）故意编造未曾发生的保险事故、虚构保险合同或者故意夸大已经发生的保险事故的损失程度进行虚假理赔，骗取保险金或者牟取其他不正当利益；  
（七）挪用、截留、侵占保险费；  
（八）委托未取得合法资格的机构从事保险销售活动；  
（九）利用开展保险业务为其他机构或者个人牟取不正当利益；  
（十）利用保险代理人、保险经纪人或者保险评估机构，从事以虚构保险中介业务或者编造退保等方式套取费用等违法活动；  
（十一）以捏造、散布虚假事实等方式损害竞争对手的商业信誉，或者以其他不正当竞争行为扰乱保险市场秩序；  
（十二）泄露在业务活动中知悉的投保人、被保险人的商业秘密；  
（十三）违反法律、行政法规和国务院保险监督管理机构规定的其他行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/保险/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（二）》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 116** An insurance company and its staff members shall not have the following acts in insurance business activities:  
(1) Defrauding policy holders, the insured or beneficiaries;  
(2) Concealing from policy holders material information related to the insurance contract;  
(3) Obstructing policy holders from fulfilling the obligation of truthful disclosure as provided for in this Law, or inducing them not to fulfill such obligation;  
(4) Giving or promising to give policy holders, the insured or beneficiaries insurance premium rebates or other benefits not specified in the insurance contract;  
(5) Refusing to fulfill the obligation of paying compensation or insurance benefits as agreed in the insurance contract according to law;  
(6) Intentionally fabricating insurance accidents that have not occurred, fabricating insurance contracts, or intentionally exaggerating the extent of loss of an insurance accident that has occurred to make false claims, thereby defrauding insurance benefits or seeking other illegitimate interests;  
(7) Embezzling, intercepting or misappropriating insurance premiums;  
(8) Entrusting an institution without legal qualifications to engage in insurance sales activities;  
(9) Using the conduct of insurance business to seek illegitimate interests for other institutions or individuals;  
(10) Using insurance agents, insurance brokers or insurance assessment institutions to engage in illegal activities such as fabricating insurance intermediary business or fabricating policy cancellations to obtain fees;  
(11) Damaging the business reputation of competitors by fabricating and spreading false facts, or disrupting the order of the insurance market by other unfair competition methods;  
(12) Disclosing trade secrets of policy holders or the insured learned in the course of business activities;  
(13) Other acts violating laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

### 第五章　保险代理人和保险经纪人

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　保险代理人是根据保险人的委托，向保险人收取佣金，并在保险人授权的范围内代为办理保险业务的机构或者个人。  
保险代理机构包括专门从事保险代理业务的保险专业代理机构和兼营保险代理业务的保险兼业代理机构。

**Article 117** An insurance agent is an institution or individual that, upon the commission of an insurer, collects commissions from the insurer and handles insurance business on behalf of the insurer within the scope authorized by the insurer.  
Insurance agency institutions include specialized insurance agency institutions that exclusively engage in insurance agency business and concurrent insurance agency institutions that concurrently engage in insurance agency business.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　保险经纪人是基于投保人的利益，为投保人与保险人订立保险合同提供中介服务，并依法收取佣金的机构。

**Article 118** An insurance broker is an institution that, based on the interests of the proposer, provides intermediary services for the conclusion of an insurance contract between the proposer and the insurer, and collects commissions according to law.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　保险代理机构、保险经纪人应当具备国务院保险监督管理机构规定的条件，取得保险监督管理机构颁发的经营保险代理业务许可证、保险经纪业务许可证。

**Article 119** An insurance agency and an insurance broker shall meet the conditions prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council and obtain an insurance agency business license and an insurance brokerage business license issued by the insurance regulatory authority.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　以公司形式设立保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人，其注册资本最低限额适用《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定。  
国务院保险监督管理机构根据保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人的业务范围和经营规模，可以调整其注册资本的最低限额，但不得低于《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的限额。  
保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人的注册资本或者出资额必须为实缴货币资本。

**Article 120** Where a specialized insurance agency or an insurance broker is established in the form of a company, the minimum registered capital shall be governed by the provisions of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to the business scope and scale of operation of the specialized insurance agency or insurance broker, adjust the minimum registered capital, but the adjusted amount shall not be lower than the minimum prescribed in the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The registered capital or capital contribution of a specialized insurance agency or an insurance broker must be paid-in monetary capital.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人的高级管理人员，应当品行良好，熟悉保险法律、行政法规，具有履行职责所需的经营管理能力，并在任职前取得保险监督管理机构核准的任职资格。

**Article 121** The senior officers of a specialized insurance agency or an insurance broker shall have good conduct, be familiar with insurance laws and administrative regulations, possess the operational management capability necessary to perform their duties, and obtain the qualification for appointment approved by the insurance regulatory authority before taking office.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　个人保险代理人、保险代理机构的代理从业人员、保险经纪人的经纪从业人员，应当品行良好，具有从事保险代理业务或者保险经纪业务所需的专业能力。

**Article 122** A personal insurance agent, the agency practitioners of an insurance agency, and the brokerage practitioners of an insurance broker shall have good conduct and possess the professional capability necessary to engage in insurance agency business or insurance brokerage business.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　保险代理机构、保险经纪人应当有自己的经营场所，设立专门账簿记载保险代理业务、经纪业务的收支情况。

**Article 123** An insurance agency and an insurance broker shall have their own business premises and set up special account books to record the revenue and expenditure of the insurance agency business and insurance brokerage business.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　保险代理机构、保险经纪人应当按照国务院保险监督管理机构的规定缴存保证金或者投保职业责任保险。

**Article 124** An insurance agency and an insurance broker shall, in accordance with the provisions of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, deposit a guarantee deposit or take out professional liability insurance.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　个人保险代理人在代为办理人寿保险业务时，不得同时接受两个以上保险人的委托。

**Article 125** When handling life insurance business on behalf of an insurer, a personal insurance agent shall not accept entrustment from two or more insurers at the same time.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　保险人委托保险代理人代为办理保险业务，应当与保险代理人签订委托代理协议，依法约定双方的权利和义务。

**Article 126** Where an insurer entrusts an insurance agent to handle insurance business on its behalf, it shall sign an entrustment agency agreement with the insurance agent, stipulating the rights and obligations of both parties according to law.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　保险代理人根据保险人的授权代为办理保险业务的行为，由保险人承担责任。  
保险代理人没有代理权、超越代理权或者代理权终止后以保险人名义订立合同，使投保人有理由相信其有代理权的，该代理行为有效。保险人可以依法追究越权的保险代理人的责任。

**Article 127** An act performed by an insurance agent in handling insurance business on behalf of an insurer under the authorization of the insurer shall be borne by the insurer.  
Where an insurance agent concludes a contract in the name of the insurer without authority, beyond its authority, or after the termination of its authority, and the proposer has reason to believe that it has such authority, the agency act shall be effective. The insurer may, according to law, investigate the liability of the insurance agent that has exceeded its authority.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　保险经纪人因过错给投保人、被保险人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 128** Where an insurance broker, due to its fault, causes losses to the proposer or the insured, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　保险活动当事人可以委托保险公估机构等依法设立的独立评估机构或者具有相关专业知识的人员，对保险事故进行评估和鉴定。  
接受委托对保险事故进行评估和鉴定的机构和人员，应当依法、独立、客观、公正地进行评估和鉴定，任何单位和个人不得干涉。  
前款规定的机构和人员，因故意或者过失给保险人或者被保险人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 129** A party to an insurance activity may entrust an independent assessment institution established according to law, such as an insurance assessment institution, or a person with relevant professional knowledge, to assess and conduct expert evaluation on an insurance accident.  
The institutions and persons that accept entrustment to assess and conduct expert evaluation on an insurance accident shall carry out the assessment and expert evaluation according to law, independently, objectively and impartially, and no entity or individual may interfere.  
Where the institutions and persons specified in the preceding paragraph cause losses to the insurer or the insured due to intentional acts or negligence, they shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　保险佣金只限于向保险代理人、保险经纪人支付，不得向其他人支付。

**Article 130** Insurance commission shall be paid only to an insurance agent or an insurance broker, and shall not be paid to any other person.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　保险代理人、保险经纪人及其从业人员在办理保险业务活动中不得有下列行为：  
（一）欺骗保险人、投保人、被保险人或者受益人；  
（二）隐瞒与保险合同有关的重要情况；  
（三）阻碍投保人履行本法规定的如实告知义务，或者诱导其不履行本法规定的如实告知义务；  
（四）给予或者承诺给予投保人、被保险人或者受益人保险合同约定以外的利益；  
（五）利用行政权力、职务或者职业便利以及其他不正当手段强迫、引诱或者限制投保人订立保险合同；  
（六）伪造、擅自变更保险合同，或者为保险合同当事人提供虚假证明材料；  
（七）挪用、截留、侵占保险费或者保险金；  
（八）利用业务便利为其他机构或者个人牟取不正当利益；  
（九）串通投保人、被保险人或者受益人，骗取保险金；  
（十）泄露在业务活动中知悉的保险人、投保人、被保险人的商业秘密。

**Article 131** An insurance agent, an insurance broker, and their practitioners shall not commit any of the following acts in the course of handling insurance business:  
(1) deceiving the insurer, the proposer, the insured or the beneficiary;  
(2) concealing material information relating to the insurance contract;  
(3) hindering the proposer from fulfilling the obligation of truthful disclosure provided for in this Law, or inducing the proposer not to fulfill such obligation;  
(4) giving or promising to give the proposer, the insured or the beneficiary benefits other than those stipulated in the insurance contract;  
(5) using administrative power, position or occupational convenience, or other improper means to compel, induce or restrict the proposer to conclude an insurance contract;  
(6) forging or altering an insurance contract without authorization, or providing false evidentiary materials for a party to an insurance contract;  
(7) misappropriating, intercepting or embezzling the insurance premium or insurance money;  
(8) using business convenience to seek improper benefits for other entities or individuals;  
(9) colluding with the proposer, the insured or the beneficiary to obtain insurance money by fraud;  
(10) disclosing the trade secrets of the insurer, the proposer or the insured that are learned in the course of insurance business activities.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　[本法第八十六条](#art-86)第一款、第一百一十三条的规定，适用于保险代理机构和保险经纪人。

**Article 132** The provisions of Article 86, paragraph 1 and Article 113 of this Law shall apply to insurance agencies and insurance brokers.

### 第六章　保险业监督管理

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　保险监督管理机构依照本法和国务院规定的职责，遵循依法、公开、公正的原则，对保险业实施监督管理，维护保险市场秩序，保护投保人、被保险人和受益人的合法权益。

**Article 133** The insurance regulatory authority shall, in accordance with this Law and the duties prescribed by the State Council, follow the principles of legality, openness and fairness, exercise supervision and administration over the insurance industry, maintain the order of the insurance market, and protect the lawful rights and interests of the proposer, the insured and the beneficiary.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　国务院保险监督管理机构依照法律、行政法规制定并发布有关保险业监督管理的规章。

**Article 134** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall, in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, formulate and issue rules concerning the supervision and administration of the insurance industry.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　关系社会公众利益的保险险种、依法实行强制保险的险种和新开发的人寿保险险种等的保险条款和保险费率，应当报国务院保险监督管理机构批准。国务院保险监督管理机构审批时，应当遵循保护社会公众利益和防止不正当竞争的原则。其他保险险种的保险条款和保险费率，应当报保险监督管理机构备案。  
保险条款和保险费率审批、备案的具体办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构依照前款规定制定。

**Article 135** The insurance clauses and premium rates of insurance types which concern the public interest, insurance types for which compulsory insurance is implemented according to law, and newly developed life insurance types shall be submitted to the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council for approval. When examining and approving, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall follow the principles of protecting the public interest and preventing unfair competition. The insurance clauses and premium rates of other insurance types shall be submitted to the insurance regulatory authority for recordation.  
Specific measures for the approval and recordation of insurance clauses and premium rates shall be formulated by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　保险公司使用的保险条款和保险费率违反法律、行政法规或者国务院保险监督管理机构的有关规定的，由保险监督管理机构责令停止使用，限期修改；情节严重的，可以在一定期限内禁止申报新的保险条款和保险费率。

**Article 136** Where the insurance clauses and premium rates used by an insurance company violate laws, administrative regulations or relevant provisions of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, the insurance regulatory authority shall order the cessation of use and modification within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, it may prohibit the submission of new insurance clauses and premium rates for a certain period.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　国务院保险监督管理机构应当建立健全保险公司偿付能力监管体系，对保险公司的偿付能力实施监控。

**Article 137** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish and improve a solvency supervision system for insurance companies, and monitor the solvency of insurance companies.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　对偿付能力不足的保险公司，国务院保险监督管理机构应当将其列为重点监管对象，并可以根据具体情况采取下列措施：  
（一）责令增加资本金、办理再保险；  
（二）限制业务范围；  
（三）限制向股东分红；  
（四）限制固定资产购置或者经营费用规模；  
（五）限制资金运用的形式、比例；  
（六）限制增设分支机构；  
（七）责令拍卖不良资产、转让保险业务；  
（八）限制董事、监事、高级管理人员的薪酬水平；  
（九）限制商业性广告；  
（十）责令停止接受新业务。

**Article 138** For an insurance company with insufficient solvency, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall list it as a key regulatory target, and may take the following measures according to the specific circumstances:  
(1) order it to increase capital or arrange reinsurance;  
(2) restrict the scope of business;  
(3) restrict distribution of dividends to shareholders;  
(4) restrict the scale of fixed asset acquisition or operating expenses;  
(5) restrict the form and proportion of capital utilization;  
(6) restrict the establishment of additional branches;  
(7) order auction of non-performing assets or transfer of insurance business;  
(8) restrict the compensation level of directors, supervisors, and senior officers;  
(9) restrict commercial advertising;  
(10) order it to cease accepting new business.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　保险公司未依照本法规定提取或者结转各项责任准备金，或者未依照本法规定办理再保险，或者严重违反本法关于资金运用的规定的，由保险监督管理机构责令限期改正，并可以责令调整负责人及有关管理人员。

**Article 139** Where an insurance company fails to set aside or carry forward various liability reserves in accordance with this Law, or fails to arrange reinsurance in accordance with this Law, or seriously violates the provisions of this Law on capital utilization, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, and may order it to replace its responsible persons and relevant management personnel.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　保险监督管理机构依照[本法第一百三十九条](#art-139)的规定作出限期改正的决定后，保险公司逾期未改正的，国务院保险监督管理机构可以决定选派保险专业人员和指定该保险公司的有关人员组成整顿组，对公司进行整顿。  
整顿决定应当载明被整顿公司的名称、整顿理由、整顿组成员和整顿期限，并予以公告。

**Article 140** After the insurance regulatory authority makes a decision ordering rectification within a time limit in accordance with Article 139 of this Law, if the insurance company fails to make corrections within the time limit, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may decide to select insurance professionals and designate relevant personnel of the insurance company to form a rectification group to rectify the company.  
The rectification decision shall specify the name of the company under rectification, the reasons for rectification, the members of the rectification group, and the rectification period, and shall be publicly announced.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　整顿组有权监督被整顿保险公司的日常业务。被整顿公司的负责人及有关管理人员应当在整顿组的监督下行使职权。

**Article 141** The rectification group has the power to supervise the daily business of the insurance company under rectification. The responsible persons and relevant management personnel of the company under rectification shall exercise their powers under the supervision of the rectification group.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　整顿过程中，被整顿保险公司的原有业务继续进行。但是，国务院保险监督管理机构可以责令被整顿公司停止部分原有业务、停止接受新业务，调整资金运用。

**Article 142** During the rectification process, the existing business of the insurance company under rectification shall continue. However, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may order the company under rectification to cease part of its existing business, cease accepting new business, or adjust capital utilization.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　被整顿保险公司经整顿已纠正其违反本法规定的行为，恢复正常经营状况的，由整顿组提出报告，经国务院保险监督管理机构批准，结束整顿，并由国务院保险监督管理机构予以公告。

**Article 143** Where the insurance company under rectification has corrected its acts violating the provisions of this Law through rectification and has restored normal business operations, the rectification group shall submit a report. Upon approval by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, the rectification shall be terminated, and the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　保险公司有下列情形之一的，国务院保险监督管理机构可以对其实行接管：  
（一）公司的偿付能力严重不足的；  
（二）违反本法规定，损害社会公共利益，可能严重危及或者已经严重危及公司的偿付能力的。  
被接管的保险公司的债权债务关系不因接管而变化。

**Article 144** An insurance company may be placed under receivership by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the company's solvency is seriously insufficient;  
(2) the company violates the provisions of this Law, harms the public interest, and may seriously jeopardize or has already seriously jeopardized the company's solvency.  
The creditor-debtor relationship of the insurance company under receivership shall not change as a result of the receivership.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　接管组的组成和接管的实施办法，由国务院保险监督管理机构决定，并予以公告。

**Article 145** The composition of the receivership group and the implementation measures for the receivership shall be decided by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council and shall be publicly announced.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　接管期限届满，国务院保险监督管理机构可以决定延长接管期限，但接管期限最长不得超过二年。

**Article 146** Upon expiration of the receivership period, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may decide to extend the receivership period, but the total receivership period shall not exceed two years.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　接管期限届满，被接管的保险公司已恢复正常经营能力的，由国务院保险监督管理机构决定终止接管，并予以公告。

**Article 147** Upon expiration of the receivership period, if the insurance company under receivership has restored its normal business capacity, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall decide to terminate the receivership and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　被整顿、被接管的保险公司有[《中华人民共和国企业破产法》第二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国企业破产法.md#art-2)规定情形的，国务院保险监督管理机构可以依法向人民法院申请对该保险公司进行重整或者破产清算。

**Article 148** Where an insurance company under rectification or receivership falls under the circumstances specified in Article 2 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may, according to law, apply to the People's Court for reorganization or bankruptcy liquidation of the insurance company.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　保险公司因违法经营被依法吊销经营保险业务许可证的，或者偿付能力低于国务院保险监督管理机构规定标准，不予撤销将严重危害保险市场秩序、损害公共利益的，由国务院保险监督管理机构予以撤销并公告，依法及时组织清算组进行清算。

**Article 149** Where an insurance company's insurance business license is lawfully revoked due to illegal operations, or its solvency is below the standards prescribed by the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council and failure to revoke it would seriously harm the order of the insurance market and damage the public interest, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall revoke it and make a public announcement, and shall lawfully and promptly organize a liquidation group to carry out liquidation.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　国务院保险监督管理机构有权要求保险公司股东、实际控制人在指定的期限内提供有关信息和资料。

**Article 150** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council has the power to require the shareholders and actual controllers of an insurance company to provide relevant information and materials within a specified period.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　保险公司的股东利用关联交易严重损害公司利益，危及公司偿付能力的，由国务院保险监督管理机构责令改正。在按照要求改正前，国务院保险监督管理机构可以限制其股东权利；拒不改正的，可以责令其转让所持的保险公司股权。

**Article 151** Where a shareholder of an insurance company seriously damages the interests of the company through connected transactions and endangers the company's solvency, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall order corrections. Before the corrections are made as required, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may restrict the shareholder's rights; if the shareholder refuses to make corrections, it may order the shareholder to transfer the equity it holds in the insurance company.

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　保险监督管理机构根据履行监督管理职责的需要，可以与保险公司董事、监事和高级管理人员进行监督管理谈话，要求其就公司的业务活动和风险管理的重大事项作出说明。

**Article 152** The insurance regulatory authority may, as necessary for performing its supervisory duties, hold regulatory talks with the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of an insurance company, requiring them to explain major matters concerning the company's business activities and risk management.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　保险公司在整顿、接管、撤销清算期间，或者出现重大风险时，国务院保险监督管理机构可以对该公司直接负责的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员采取以下措施：  
（一）通知出境管理机关依法阻止其出境；  
（二）申请司法机关禁止其转移、转让或者以其他方式处分财产，或者在财产上设定其他权利。

**Article 153** During the period of rectification, receivership, or revocation and liquidation of an insurance company, or when a major risk occurs, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may take the following measures against the directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other directly responsible persons of the company who are directly responsible:  
(1) notify the exit administration authority to prevent them from leaving the country according to law;  
(2) apply to the judicial authority to prohibit them from transferring, assigning, or otherwise disposing of property, or from creating other rights on the property.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　保险监督管理机构依法履行职责，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）对保险公司、保险代理人、保险经纪人、保险资产管理公司、外国保险机构的代表机构进行现场检查；  
（二）进入涉嫌违法行为发生场所调查取证；  
（三）询问当事人及与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明；  
（四）查阅、复制与被调查事件有关的财产权登记等资料；  
（五）查阅、复制保险公司、保险代理人、保险经纪人、保险资产管理公司、外国保险机构的代表机构以及与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的财务会计资料及其他相关文件和资料；对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件和资料予以封存；  
（六）查询涉嫌违法经营的保险公司、保险代理人、保险经纪人、保险资产管理公司、外国保险机构的代表机构以及与涉嫌违法事项有关的单位和个人的银行账户；  
（七）对有证据证明已经或者可能转移、隐匿违法资金等涉案财产或者隐匿、伪造、毁损重要证据的，经保险监督管理机构主要负责人批准，申请人民法院予以冻结或者查封。  
保险监督管理机构采取前款第（一）项、第（二）项、第（五）项措施的，应当经保险监督管理机构负责人批准；采取第（六）项措施的，应当经国务院保险监督管理机构负责人批准。  
保险监督管理机构依法进行监督检查或者调查，其监督检查、调查的人员不得少于二人，并应当出示合法证件和监督检查、调查通知书；监督检查、调查的人员少于二人或者未出示合法证件和监督检查、调查通知书的，被检查、调查的单位和个人有权拒绝。

**Article 154** When performing its duties according to law, the insurance regulatory authority may take the following measures:  
(1) conduct on-site inspections of insurance companies, insurance agents, insurance brokers, insurance asset management companies, and representative offices of foreign insurance institutions;  
(2) enter the premises where suspected illegal acts occur to investigate and collect evidence;  
(3) question the parties and units and individuals related to the event under investigation, and require them to explain matters related to the event under investigation;  
(4) inspect and copy materials such as property rights registrations related to the event under investigation;  
(5) inspect and copy the financial accounting materials and other relevant documents and materials of insurance companies, insurance agents, insurance brokers, insurance asset management companies, representative offices of foreign insurance institutions, and units and individuals related to the event under investigation; seal up documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed, or damaged;  
(6) inquire into the bank accounts of insurance companies, insurance agents, insurance brokers, insurance asset management companies, representative offices of foreign insurance institutions, and units and individuals suspected of illegal operations or related to suspected illegal matters;  
(7) where there is evidence proving that illegal funds or other property involved in the case have been or may be transferred or concealed, or important evidence has been or may be concealed, forged, or damaged, with the approval of the principal person in charge of the insurance regulatory authority, apply to the People's Court for freezing or sealing up.  
When the insurance regulatory authority takes the measures specified in items (1), (2), and (5) of the preceding paragraph, it shall obtain approval from the person in charge of the insurance regulatory authority; when taking the measure specified in item (6), it shall obtain approval from the person in charge of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.  
When the insurance regulatory authority conducts supervision, inspection, or investigation according to law, there shall be no fewer than two persons conducting the supervision, inspection, or investigation, and they shall present their legal credentials and the notice of supervision, inspection, or investigation; if there are fewer than two persons conducting the supervision, inspection, or investigation, or if they fail to present their legal credentials and the notice of supervision, inspection, or investigation, the unit or individual under inspection or investigation has the right to refuse.

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条　保险监督管理机构依法履行职责，被检查、调查的单位和个人应当配合。

**Article 155** When the insurance regulatory authority performs its duties according to law, the units and individuals under inspection or investigation shall cooperate.

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条　保险监督管理机构工作人员应当忠于职守，依法办事，公正廉洁，不得利用职务便利牟取不正当利益，不得泄露所知悉的有关单位和个人的商业秘密。

**Article 156** Personnel of the insurance regulatory authority shall be devoted to their duties, act according to law, be impartial and honest, and shall not take advantage of their positions to seek illegitimate benefits, nor shall they disclose any trade secrets of relevant units and individuals that they have come to know.

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条　国务院保险监督管理机构应当与中国人民银行、国务院其他金融监督管理机构建立监督管理信息共享机制。  
保险监督管理机构依法履行职责，进行监督检查、调查时，有关部门应当予以配合。

**Article 157** The insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish a mechanism for sharing supervisory information with the People's Bank of China and other financial regulatory authorities of the State Council.  
When the insurance regulatory authority performs its duties according to law and conducts supervision, inspection, or investigation, the relevant departments shall cooperate.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条　违反本法规定，擅自设立保险公司、保险资产管理公司或者非法经营商业保险业务的，由保险监督管理机构予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 158** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, establishes an insurance company or an insurance asset management company without authorization, or illegally engages in commercial insurance business, the insurance regulatory authority shall ban it, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条　违反本法规定，擅自设立保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人，或者未取得经营保险代理业务许可证、保险经纪业务许可证从事保险代理业务、保险经纪业务的，由保险监督管理机构予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五万元的，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 159** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, establishes a specialized insurance agency or an insurance broker without authorization, or engages in insurance agency business or insurance brokerage business without obtaining a license for such business, the insurance regulatory authority shall ban it, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条　保险公司违反本法规定，超出批准的业务范围经营的，由保险监督管理机构责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，责令停业整顿或者吊销业务许可证。

**Article 160** Where an insurance company, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in business beyond the approved scope of business, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed. If it fails to make corrections within the time limit or causes serious consequences, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条　保险公司有[本法第一百一十六条](#art-116)规定行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，限制其业务范围、责令停止接受新业务或者吊销业务许可证。

**Article 161** Where an insurance company commits any of the acts specified in Article 116 of this Law, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its business scope shall be restricted, it shall be ordered to cease accepting new business, or its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条　保险公司违反[本法第八十四条](#art-84)规定的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 162** Where an insurance company violates the provisions of Article 84 of this Law, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条　保险公司违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）超额承保，情节严重的；  
（二）为无民事行为能力人承保以死亡为给付保险金条件的保险的。

**Article 163** Where an insurance company, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan:  
(1) Over-insuring, if the circumstances are serious;  
(2) Underwriting insurance for a person without civil capacity where death is a condition for payment of insurance benefits.

<a id="art-164"></a>第一百六十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以限制其业务范围、责令停止接受新业务或者吊销业务许可证：  
（一）未按照规定提存保证金或者违反规定动用保证金的；  
（二）未按照规定提取或者结转各项责任准备金的；  
（三）未按照规定缴纳保险保障基金或者提取公积金的；  
（四）未按照规定办理再保险的；  
（五）未按照规定运用保险公司资金的；  
（六）未经批准设立分支机构的；  
（七）未按照规定申请批准保险条款、保险费率的。

**Article 164** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its business scope may be restricted, it may be ordered to cease accepting new business, or its business license may be revoked:  
(1) Failing to deposit guarantee funds as required or using guarantee funds in violation of regulations;  
(2) Failing to withdraw or carry over various liability reserves as required;  
(3) Failing to pay the insurance guarantee fund or withdraw the provident fund as required;  
(4) Failing to handle reinsurance as required;  
(5) Failing to use insurance company funds as required;  
(6) Establishing branches without approval;  
(7) Failing to apply for approval of insurance clauses or insurance premium rates as required.

<a id="art-165"></a>第一百六十五条　保险代理机构、保险经纪人有[本法第一百三十一条](#art-131)规定行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销业务许可证。

**Article 165** Where an insurance agency or an insurance broker commits any of the acts specified in Article 131 of this Law, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-166"></a>第一百六十六条　保险代理机构、保险经纪人违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销业务许可证：  
（一）未按照规定缴存保证金或者投保职业责任保险的；  
（二）未按照规定设立专门账簿记载业务收支情况的。

**Article 166** Where an insurance agency or an insurance broker, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its business license shall be revoked:  
(1) Failing to deposit guarantee funds or purchase professional liability insurance as required;  
(2) Failing to set up special books to record business income and expenses as required.

<a id="art-167"></a>第一百六十七条　违反本法规定，聘任不具有任职资格的人员的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 167** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, employs a person who does not have the requisite qualifications, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-168"></a>第一百六十八条　违反本法规定，转让、出租、出借业务许可证的，由保险监督管理机构处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销业务许可证。

**Article 168** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, transfers, leases, or lends a business license, the insurance regulatory authority shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification or its business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-169"></a>第一百六十九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定报送或者保管报告、报表、文件、资料的，或者未按照规定提供有关信息、资料的；  
（二）未按照规定报送保险条款、保险费率备案的；  
（三）未按照规定披露信息的。

**Article 169** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Failing to submit or maintain reports, statements, documents, or materials as required, or failing to provide relevant information or materials as required;  
(2) Failing to submit insurance clauses or insurance premium rates for record as required;  
(3) Failing to disclose information as required.

<a id="art-170"></a>第一百七十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以限制其业务范围、责令停止接受新业务或者吊销业务许可证：  
（一）编制或者提供虚假的报告、报表、文件、资料的；  
（二）拒绝或者妨碍依法监督检查的；  
（三）未按照规定使用经批准或者备案的保险条款、保险费率的。

**Article 170** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its business scope may be restricted, it may be ordered to cease accepting new business, or its business license may be revoked:  
(1) Preparing or providing false reports, statements, documents, or materials;  
(2) Refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection according to law;  
(3) Failing to use approved or recorded insurance clauses or insurance premium rates as required.

<a id="art-171"></a>第一百七十一条　保险公司、保险资产管理公司、保险专业代理机构、保险经纪人违反本法规定的，保险监督管理机构除分别依照[本法第一百六十条](#art-160)至第一百七十条的规定对该单位给予处罚外，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，撤销任职资格。

**Article 171** Where an insurance company, an insurance asset management company, a specialized insurance agency, or an insurance broker violates the provisions of this Law, the insurance regulatory authority shall, in addition to imposing penalties on the entity in accordance with Articles 160 to 170 of this Law respectively, give a warning to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, their qualifications for holding office shall be revoked.

<a id="art-172"></a>第一百七十二条　个人保险代理人违反本法规定的，由保险监督管理机构给予警告，可以并处二万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 172** Where an individual insurance agent violates the provisions of this Law, the insurance regulatory authority shall give him a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-173"></a>第一百七十三条　外国保险机构未经国务院保险监督管理机构批准，擅自在中华人民共和国境内设立代表机构的，由国务院保险监督管理机构予以取缔，处五万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。  
外国保险机构在中华人民共和国境内设立的代表机构从事保险经营活动的，由保险监督管理机构责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足二十万元的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；对其首席代表可以责令撤换；情节严重的，撤销其代表机构。

**Article 173** Where a foreign insurance institution, without the approval of the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council, establishes a representative office within the territory of the People's Republic of China without authorization, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council shall ban it and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.  
Where a representative office established by a foreign insurance institution within the territory of the People's Republic of China engages in insurance business activities, the insurance regulatory authority shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; its chief representative may be ordered to be replaced; if the circumstances are serious, the representative office shall be dissolved.

<a id="art-174"></a>第一百七十四条　投保人、被保险人或者受益人有下列行为之一，进行保险诈骗活动，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处罚：  
（一）投保人故意虚构保险标的，骗取保险金的；  
（二）编造未曾发生的保险事故，或者编造虚假的事故原因或者夸大损失程度，骗取保险金的；  
（三）故意造成保险事故，骗取保险金的。  
保险事故的鉴定人、评估人、证明人故意提供虚假的证明文件，为投保人、被保险人或者受益人进行保险诈骗提供条件的，依照前款规定给予处罚。

**Article 174** Where any policy holder, insured, or beneficiary commits any of the following acts to engage in insurance fraud, which does not constitute a crime, an administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) The policy holder intentionally fabricates the subject matter of the insurance to defraud insurance money;  
(2) Fabricating an insurance accident that has not occurred, or fabricating a false cause of an accident or exaggerating the extent of loss to defraud insurance money;  
(3) Intentionally causing an insurance accident to defraud insurance money.  
Where an expert evaluator, assessor, or certifier of an insurance accident intentionally provides false certification documents to facilitate insurance fraud by a policy holder, insured, or beneficiary, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-175"></a>第一百七十五条　违反本法规定，给他人造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 175** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes damage to another person, he shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-176"></a>第一百七十六条　拒绝、阻碍保险监督管理机构及其工作人员依法行使监督检查、调查职权，未使用暴力、威胁方法的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 176** Where anyone refuses or obstructs the insurance regulatory authority or its staff members from lawfully exercising their powers of supervision, inspection, or investigation, without using violence or threats, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-177"></a>第一百七十七条　违反法律、行政法规的规定，情节严重的，国务院保险监督管理机构可以禁止有关责任人员一定期限直至终身进入保险业。

**Article 177** Where anyone violates the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, if the circumstances are serious, the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council may prohibit the relevant responsible persons from entering the insurance industry for a certain period up to life.

<a id="art-178"></a>第一百七十八条　保险监督管理机构从事监督管理工作的人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予处分：  
（一）违反规定批准机构的设立的；  
（二）违反规定进行保险条款、保险费率审批的；  
（三）违反规定进行现场检查的；  
（四）违反规定查询账户或者冻结资金的；  
（五）泄露其知悉的有关单位和个人的商业秘密的；  
（六）违反规定实施行政处罚的；  
（七）滥用职权、玩忽职守的其他行为。

**Article 178** Where any staff member of the insurance regulatory authority engaging in the supervision and administration work has any of the following circumstances, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) approving the establishment of an institution in violation of the provisions;  
(2) examining and approving the insurance clauses and insurance premium rates in violation of the provisions;  
(3) conducting on-site inspections in violation of the provisions;  
(4) inquiring into accounts or freezing funds in violation of the provisions;  
(5) disclosing the trade secrets of relevant entities and individuals known to him or her;  
(6) imposing administrative penalties in violation of the provisions;  
(7) other acts of abusing powers or neglecting duties.

<a id="art-179"></a>第一百七十九条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 179** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-180"></a>第一百八十条　保险公司应当加入保险行业协会。保险代理人、保险经纪人、保险公估机构可以加入保险行业协会。  
保险行业协会是保险业的自律性组织，是社会团体法人。

**Article 180** An insurance company shall join the insurance association. An insurance agent, an insurance broker, and an insurance assessment institution may join the insurance association.  
The insurance association is a self-regulatory organization of the insurance industry and is a social organization legal person.

<a id="art-181"></a>第一百八十一条　保险公司以外的其他依法设立的保险组织经营的商业保险业务，适用本法。

**Article 181** The commercial insurance business operated by other insurance organizations established according to law other than insurance companies shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-182"></a>第一百八十二条　海上保险适用《中华人民共和国海商法》的有关规定；《中华人民共和国海商法》未规定的，适用本法的有关规定。

**Article 182** Marine insurance shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China; for matters not provided for in the Maritime Law of the People's Republic of China, the relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-183"></a>第一百八十三条　中外合资保险公司、外资独资保险公司、外国保险公司分公司适用本法规定；法律、行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 183** Chinese-foreign equity joint venture insurance companies, wholly foreign-owned insurance companies, and branches of foreign insurance companies shall be governed by the provisions of this Law; where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise, such provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-184"></a>第一百八十四条　国家支持发展为农业生产服务的保险事业。农业保险由法律、行政法规另行规定。  
强制保险，法律、行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 184** The State supports the development of insurance undertakings serving agricultural production. Agricultural insurance shall be separately provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
For compulsory insurance, where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise, such provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-185"></a>第一百八十五条　本法自2009年10月1日起施行。

**Article 185** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2009.
