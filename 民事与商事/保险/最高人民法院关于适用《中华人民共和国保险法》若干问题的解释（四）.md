# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（四）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕13号  
**公布日期**：2018-07-31  
**生效日期**：2018-09-01

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国保险法〉若干问题的解释（四）》已于2018年5月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1738次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年9月1日起施行。 2018年7月31日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》 若干问题的解释（四）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　保险标的已交付受让人，但尚未依法办理所有权变更登记，承担保险标的毁损灭失风险的受让人，依照[保险法第四十八条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-48)、第四十九条的规定主张行使被保险人权利的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 1** Where the subject matter of insurance has been delivered to the transferee but the ownership transfer registration has not yet been completed according to law, and the transferee who bears the risk of damage or loss of the subject matter of insurance claims to exercise the rights of the insured in accordance with Articles 48 and 49 of the Insurance Law, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　保险人已向投保人履行了保险法规定的提示和明确说明义务，保险标的受让人以保险标的转让后保险人未向其提示或者明确说明为由，主张免除保险人责任的条款不生效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 2** Where the insurer has performed the duty of presentation and clear explanation as provided for in the Insurance Law to the applicant, and the transferee of the subject matter of insurance claims that the clause exempting the insurer's liability does not take effect on the ground that the insurer failed to present or clearly explain to it after the transfer of the subject matter of insurance, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被保险人死亡，继承保险标的的当事人主张承继被保险人的权利和义务的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where the insured dies, and the party who succeeds to the subject matter of insurance claims to succeed to the rights and obligations of the insured, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院认定保险标的是否构成[保险法第四十九条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-49)、第五十二条规定的“危险程度显著增加”时，应当综合考虑以下因素：  
（一）保险标的用途的改变；  
（二）保险标的使用范围的改变；  
（三）保险标的所处环境的变化；  
（四）保险标的因改装等原因引起的变化；  
（五）保险标的使用人或者管理人的改变；  
（六）危险程度增加持续的时间；  
（七）其他可能导致危险程度显著增加的因素。  
保险标的危险程度虽然增加，但增加的危险属于保险合同订立时保险人预见或者应当预见的保险合同承保范围的，不构成危险程度显著增加。

**Article 4** When determining whether the subject matter of insurance constitutes a "material increase in the degree of risk" as provided for in Articles 49 and 52 of the Insurance Law, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors:  
(1) change in the use of the subject matter of insurance;  
(2) change in the scope of use of the subject matter of insurance;  
(3) change in the environment in which the subject matter of insurance is located;  
(4) change in the subject matter of insurance caused by modification or other reasons;  
(5) change in the user or manager of the subject matter of insurance;  
(6) duration of the increase in the degree of risk;  
(7) other factors that may lead to a material increase in the degree of risk.  
Where the degree of risk of the subject matter of insurance increases, but the increased risk falls within the coverage of the insurance contract that was foreseen or should have been foreseen by the insurer at the time of conclusion of the insurance contract, it shall not constitute a material increase in the degree of risk.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　被保险人、受让人依法及时向保险人发出保险标的转让通知后，保险人作出答复前，发生保险事故，被保险人或者受让人主张保险人按照保险合同承担赔偿保险金的责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where, after the insured or the transferee has given timely notice of the transfer of the subject matter of insurance to the insurer according to law, and before the insurer makes a reply, an insured event occurs, and the insured or the transferee claims that the insurer shall bear the liability to pay insurance compensation in accordance with the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保险事故发生后，被保险人依照[保险法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-57)的规定，请求保险人承担为防止或者减少保险标的的损失所支付的必要、合理费用，保险人以被保险人采取的措施未产生实际效果为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 6** After the occurrence of an insured event, where the insured, in accordance with Article 57 of the Insurance Law, requests the insurer to bear the necessary and reasonable expenses paid for preventing or reducing the loss of the subject matter of insurance, and the insurer defends on the ground that the measures taken by the insured have not produced actual effect, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　保险人依照[保险法第六十条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-60)的规定，主张代位行使被保险人因第三者侵权或者违约等享有的请求赔偿的权利的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where the insurer, in accordance with Article 60 of the Insurance Law, claims to subrogatively exercise the right of the insured to claim compensation arising from tort or breach of contract by a third party, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　投保人和被保险人为不同主体，因投保人对保险标的的损害而造成保险事故，保险人依法主张代位行使被保险人对投保人请求赔偿的权利的，人民法院应予支持，但法律另有规定或者保险合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 8** Where the applicant and the insured are different persons, and an insured event is caused by damage to the subject matter of insurance by the applicant, and the insurer claims according to law to subrogatively exercise the right of the insured to claim compensation from the applicant, the People's Court shall support such claim, unless otherwise provided by law or otherwise agreed in the insurance contract.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在保险人以第三者为被告提起的代位求偿权之诉中，第三者以被保险人在保险合同订立前已放弃对其请求赔偿的权利为由进行抗辩，人民法院认定上述放弃行为合法有效，保险人就相应部分主张行使代位求偿权的，人民法院不予支持。  
保险合同订立时，保险人就是否存在上述放弃情形提出询问，投保人未如实告知，导致保险人不能代位行使请求赔偿的权利，保险人请求返还相应保险金的，人民法院应予支持，但保险人知道或者应当知道上述情形仍同意承保的除外。

**Article 9** In an action for subrogation right brought by the insurer against a third party as defendant, where the third party defends on the ground that the insured has waived its right to claim compensation from the third party before the conclusion of the insurance contract, and the People's Court determines that the aforesaid waiver is lawful and valid, and the insurer claims to exercise the subrogation right in respect of the corresponding part, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where, at the time of conclusion of the insurance contract, the insurer makes an inquiry as to whether the aforesaid waiver exists, and the applicant fails to truthfully inform, thereby causing the insurer to be unable to subrogatively exercise the right to claim compensation, and the insurer requests the return of the corresponding insurance compensation, the People's Court shall support such request, unless the insurer knew or should have known of the aforesaid circumstances and still agreed to underwrite the insurance.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　因第三者对保险标的的损害而造成保险事故，保险人获得代位请求赔偿的权利的情况未通知第三者或者通知到达第三者前，第三者在被保险人已经从保险人处获赔的范围内又向被保险人作出赔偿，保险人主张代位行使被保险人对第三者请求赔偿的权利的，人民法院不予支持。保险人就相应保险金主张被保险人返还的，人民法院应予支持。  
保险人获得代位请求赔偿的权利的情况已经通知到第三者，第三者又向被保险人作出赔偿，保险人主张代位行使请求赔偿的权利，第三者以其已经向被保险人赔偿为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where an insured event is caused by damage to the subject matter of insurance by a third party, and before the circumstance that the insurer has obtained the right to claim compensation by subrogation is notified to the third party or before such notification reaches the third party, the third party has made compensation to the insured within the scope of the compensation already received by the insured from the insurer, and the insurer claims to subrogatively exercise the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party, the People's Court shall not support such claim. Where the insurer claims that the insured shall return the corresponding insurance compensation, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where the circumstance that the insurer has obtained the right to claim compensation by subrogation has been notified to the third party, but the third party nevertheless makes compensation to the insured, and the insurer claims to subrogatively exercise the right to claim compensation, and the third party defends on the ground that it has already compensated the insured, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被保险人因故意或者重大过失未履行[保险法第六十三条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-63)规定的义务，致使保险人未能行使或者未能全部行使代位请求赔偿的权利，保险人主张在其损失范围内扣减或者返还相应保险金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where the insured, due to intentional act or gross negligence, fails to perform the obligations provided for in Article 63 of the Insurance Law, thereby causing the insurer to be unable to exercise or fully exercise the right of subrogation to claim compensation, and the insurer claims to deduct or recover the corresponding insurance compensation within the scope of its loss, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　保险人以造成保险事故的第三者为被告提起代位求偿权之诉的，以被保险人与第三者之间的法律关系确定管辖法院。

**Article 12** Where the insurer brings an action for subrogation right against the third party who caused the insured event as defendant, the competent court shall be determined by the legal relationship between the insured and the third party.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　保险人提起代位求偿权之诉时，被保险人已经向第三者提起诉讼的，人民法院可以依法合并审理。  
保险人行使代位求偿权时，被保险人已经向第三者提起诉讼，保险人向受理该案的人民法院申请变更当事人，代位行使被保险人对第三者请求赔偿的权利，被保险人同意的，人民法院应予准许；被保险人不同意的，保险人可以作为共同原告参加诉讼。

**Article 13** Where, at the time the insurer brings an action for subrogation right, the insured has already instituted an action against the third party, the People's Court may consolidate the trials according to law.  
Where, when the insurer exercises the subrogation right, the insured has already instituted an action against the third party, and the insurer applies to the People's Court that accepted the case for change of parties to subrogatively exercise the right of the insured to claim compensation from the third party, if the insured consents, the People's Court shall permit it; if the insured does not consent, the insurer may participate in the proceedings as a co-plaintiff.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　具有下列情形之一的，被保险人可以依照[保险法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-65)第二款的规定请求保险人直接向第三者赔偿保险金：  
（一）被保险人对第三者所负的赔偿责任经人民法院生效裁判、仲裁裁决确认；  
（二）被保险人对第三者所负的赔偿责任经被保险人与第三者协商一致；  
（三）被保险人对第三者应负的赔偿责任能够确定的其他情形。  
前款规定的情形下，保险人主张按照保险合同确定保险赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Under any of the following circumstances, the insured may, in accordance with Article 65, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law, request the insurer to directly pay insurance compensation to the third party:  
(1) the compensation liability borne by the insured to the third party has been confirmed by a legally effective judgment of the People's Court or an arbitral award;  
(2) the compensation liability borne by the insured to the third party has been agreed upon by the insured and the third party through consultation;  
(3) other circumstances where the compensation liability that the insured shall bear to the third party can be determined.  
Under the circumstances provided for in the preceding paragraph, where the insurer claims to determine the insurance compensation liability in accordance with the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被保险人对第三者应负的赔偿责任确定后，被保险人不履行赔偿责任，且第三者以保险人为被告或者以保险人与被保险人为共同被告提起诉讼时，被保险人尚未向保险人提出直接向第三者赔偿保险金的请求的，可以认定为属于[保险法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-65)第二款规定的“被保险人怠于请求”的情形。

**Article 15** After the compensation liability that the insured shall bear to the third party is determined, if the insured fails to perform the compensation liability, and when the third party institutes an action against the insurer as defendant or against the insurer and the insured as co-defendants, the insured has not yet made a request to the insurer for direct payment of insurance compensation to the third party, this may be determined as falling under the circumstances of "the insured is remiss in making a request" as provided for in Article 65, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　责任保险的被保险人因共同侵权依法承担连带责任，保险人以该连带责任超出被保险人应承担的责任份额为由，拒绝赔付保险金的，人民法院不予支持。保险人承担保险责任后，主张就超出被保险人责任份额的部分向其他连带责任人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where the insured under liability insurance bears joint and several liability according to law due to joint tort, and the insurer refuses to pay insurance compensation on the ground that such joint and several liability exceeds the share of liability that the insured shall bear, the People's Court shall not support such refusal. Where, after bearing the insurance liability, the insurer claims to recover from other jointly and severally liable persons the part exceeding the insured's share of liability, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　责任保险的被保险人对第三者所负的赔偿责任已经生效判决确认并已进入执行程序，但未获得清偿或者未获得全部清偿，第三者依法请求保险人赔偿保险金，保险人以前述生效判决已进入执行程序为由抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 17** Where the compensation liability borne by the insured under liability insurance to the third party has been confirmed by a legally effective judgment and has entered into the enforcement procedure, but the compensation has not been obtained or has not been fully obtained, and the third party requests the insurer to pay insurance compensation according to law, and the insurer defends on the ground that the aforesaid legally effective judgment has entered into the enforcement procedure, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　商业责任险的被保险人向保险人请求赔偿保险金的诉讼时效期间，自被保险人对第三者应负的赔偿责任确定之日起计算。

**Article 18** The limitation of action for the insured under commercial liability insurance to request the insurer to pay insurance compensation shall commence from the date on which the compensation liability that the insured shall bear to the third party is determined.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　责任保险的被保险人与第三者就被保险人的赔偿责任达成和解协议且经保险人认可，被保险人主张保险人在保险合同范围内依据和解协议承担保险责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
被保险人与第三者就被保险人的赔偿责任达成和解协议，未经保险人认可，保险人主张对保险责任范围以及赔偿数额重新予以核定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where the insured under liability insurance reaches a settlement agreement with the third party regarding the insured's compensation liability, and such agreement has been recognized by the insurer, and the insured claims that the insurer shall bear insurance liability within the scope of the insurance contract in accordance with the settlement agreement, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where the insured reaches a settlement agreement with the third party regarding the insured's compensation liability, but such agreement has not been recognized by the insurer, and the insurer claims to re-assess the scope of insurance liability and the amount of compensation, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　责任保险的保险人在被保险人向第三者赔偿之前向被保险人赔偿保险金，第三者依照[保险法第六十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-65)第二款的规定行使保险金请求权时，保险人以其已向被保险人赔偿为由拒绝赔偿保险金的，人民法院不予支持。保险人向第三者赔偿后，请求被保险人返还相应保险金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where the insurer under liability insurance pays insurance compensation to the insured before the insured compensates the third party, and when the third party exercises the right to claim insurance compensation in accordance with Article 65, paragraph 2 of the Insurance Law, the insurer refuses to pay insurance compensation on the ground that it has already compensated the insured, the People's Court shall not support such refusal. Where, after paying compensation to the third party, the insurer requests the insured to return the corresponding insurance compensation, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本解释自2018年9月1日起施行。  
本解释施行后人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 21** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 1, 2018.  
For cases of first instance and second instance that are being tried by the People's Court after the coming into force of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply; for cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and for which a party applies for retrial or which are decided to be retried in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
