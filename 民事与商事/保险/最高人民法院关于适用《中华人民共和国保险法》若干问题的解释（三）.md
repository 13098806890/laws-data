# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国保险法》若干问题的解释（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕21号  
**公布日期**：2015-11-25  
**生效日期**：2015-12-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国保险法〉若干问题的解释（三）》已于2015年9月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1661次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年12月1日起施行。 2015年11月25日 最高人民法院 《关于适用〈中华人民共和国保险法〉 若干问题的解释（三）》 法释〔2015〕21号 为正确审理保险合同纠纷案件，切实维护当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国保险法》《中华人民共和国合同法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，就保险法中关于保险合同章人身保险部分有关法律适用问题解释如下： 第一条　当事人订立以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同，根据保险法第三十四条的规定，“被保险人同意并认可保险金额”可以采取书面形式、口头形式或者其他形式；可以在合同订立时作出，也可以在合同订立后追认。 有下列情形之一的，应认定为被保险人同意投保人为其订立保险合同并认可保险金额： （一）被保险人明知他人代其签名同意而未表示异议的； （二）被保险人同意投保人指定的受益人的； （三）有证据足以认定被保险人同意投保人为其投保的其他情形。 第二条　被保险人以书面形式通知保险人和投保人撤销其依据保险法第三十四条第一款规定所作出的同意意思表示的，可认定为保险合同解除。 第三条　人民法院审理人身保险合同纠纷案件时，应主动审查投保人订立保险合同时是否具有保险利益，以及以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同是否经过被保险人同意并认可保险金额。 第四条　保险合同订立后，因投保人丧失对被保险人的保险利益，当事人主张保险合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　当事人订立以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同，根据[保险法第三十四条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-34)的规定，“被保险人同意并认可保险金额”可以采取书面形式、口头形式或者其他形式；可以在合同订立时作出，也可以在合同订立后追认。  
有下列情形之一的，应认定为被保险人同意投保人为其订立保险合同并认可保险金额：  
（一）被保险人明知他人代其签名同意而未表示异议的；  
（二）被保险人同意投保人指定的受益人的；  
（三）有证据足以认定被保险人同意投保人为其投保的其他情形。

**Article 1** Where a party concludes a contract that takes death as the condition for payment of insurance benefits, pursuant to Article 34 of the Insurance Law, the "consent of the insured and recognition of the insured amount" may be given in written form, oral form or other forms; it may be given at the time of conclusion of the contract, or may be ratified after the conclusion of the contract.  
Under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined that the insured consents to the applicant concluding the insurance contract on his/her behalf and recognizes the insured amount:  
(1) The insured, knowing that another person has signed consent on his/her behalf, does not express any objection;  
(2) The insured consents to the beneficiary designated by the applicant;  
(3) Other circumstances where there is sufficient evidence to determine that the insured consents to the applicant insuring him/her.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　被保险人以书面形式通知保险人和投保人撤销其依据[保险法第三十四条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-34)第一款规定所作出的同意意思表示的，可认定为保险合同解除。

**Article 2** Where the insured notifies the insurer and the applicant in writing to revoke the expression of consent made by him/her in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 34 of the Insurance Law, it may be determined that the insurance contract is dissolved.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院审理人身保险合同纠纷案件时，应主动审查投保人订立保险合同时是否具有保险利益，以及以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同是否经过被保险人同意并认可保险金额。

**Article 3** When the People's Court hears a dispute over a personal insurance contract, it shall proactively examine whether the applicant has an insurable interest at the time of concluding the insurance contract, and whether the contract that takes death as the condition for payment of insurance benefits has obtained the consent of the insured and recognition of the insured amount.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保险合同订立后，因投保人丧失对被保险人的保险利益，当事人主张保险合同无效的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** After the conclusion of an insurance contract, where the applicant loses the insurable interest in the insured and a party claims that the insurance contract is void, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　保险合同订立时，被保险人根据保险人的要求在指定医疗服务机构进行体检，当事人主张投保人如实告知义务免除的，人民法院不予支持。  
保险人知道被保险人的体检结果，仍以投保人未就相关情况履行如实告知义务为由要求解除合同的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 5** At the time of conclusion of the insurance contract, where the insured undergoes a physical examination at a designated medical service institution as required by the insurer, and a party claims that the applicant's obligation of truthful disclosure is exempted, the People's Court shall not support such claim.  
Where the insurer knows the results of the insured's physical examination but still requests dissolution of the contract on the ground that the applicant failed to fulfill the obligation of truthful disclosure with respect to the relevant circumstances, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　未成年人父母之外的其他履行监护职责的人为未成年人订立以死亡为给付保险金条件的合同，当事人主张参照[保险法第三十三条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-33)第二款、第三十四条第三款的规定认定该合同有效的，人民法院不予支持，但经未成年人父母同意的除外。

**Article 6** Where a person other than the parents of a minor who performs guardianship duties concludes for the minor a contract that takes death as the condition for payment of insurance benefits, and a party claims that the contract shall be determined valid by reference to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 33 and paragraph 3 of Article 34 of the Insurance Law, the People's Court shall not support such claim, unless the minor's parents have consented.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人以被保险人、受益人或者他人已经代为支付保险费为由，主张投保人对应的交费义务已经履行的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 7** Where a party claims that the applicant's corresponding obligation to pay premiums has been fulfilled on the ground that the insured, the beneficiary or another person has paid the premiums on behalf of the applicant, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保险合同效力依照[保险法第三十六条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-36)规定中止，投保人提出恢复效力申请并同意补交保险费，除被保险人的危险程度在中止期间显著增加外，保险人拒绝恢复效力的，人民法院不予支持。  
保险人在收到恢复效力申请后，三十日内未明确拒绝的，应认定为同意恢复效力。  
保险合同自投保人补交保险费之日恢复效力。保险人要求投保人补交相应利息的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where the validity of an insurance contract is suspended in accordance with Article 36 of the Insurance Law, and the applicant submits an application for reinstatement of validity and agrees to pay back premiums, if the insurer refuses to reinstate validity unless the insured's risk level has significantly increased during the suspension period, the People's Court shall not support such refusal.  
If the insurer does not expressly refuse within thirty days after receiving the application for reinstatement, it shall be deemed to have consented to the reinstatement of validity.  
The insurance contract shall be reinstated on the date when the applicant pays back the premiums. Where the insurer requests the applicant to pay back corresponding interest, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　投保人指定受益人未经被保险人同意的，人民法院应认定指定行为无效。  
当事人对保险合同约定的受益人存在争议，除投保人、被保险人在保险合同之外另有约定外，按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）受益人约定为“法定”或者“法定继承人”的，以继承法规定的法定继承人为受益人；  
（二）受益人仅约定为身份关系，投保人与被保险人为同一主体的，根据保险事故发生时与被保险人的身份关系确定受益人；投保人与被保险人为不同主体的，根据保险合同成立时与被保险人的身份关系确定受益人；  
（三）受益人的约定包括姓名和身份关系，保险事故发生时身份关系发生变化的，认定为未指定受益人。

**Article 9** Where the applicant designates a beneficiary without the consent of the insured, the People's Court shall determine that the designation is invalid.  
Where a party disputes the beneficiary stipulated in the insurance contract, unless otherwise agreed by the applicant and the insured outside the insurance contract, the following shall be handled separately according to the circumstances:  
(1) If the beneficiary is stipulated as "statutory" or "statutory successor", the statutory successors under the Law of Succession shall be the beneficiary;  
(2) If the beneficiary is stipulated only by family relationship, and the applicant and the insured are the same person, the beneficiary shall be determined according to the family relationship with the insured at the time of the occurrence of the insured event; if the applicant and the insured are different persons, the beneficiary shall be determined according to the family relationship with the insured at the time of the conclusion of the insurance contract;  
(3) If the stipulation of the beneficiary includes both name and family relationship, and the family relationship changes at the time of the occurrence of the insured event, it shall be determined that no beneficiary is designated.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　投保人或者被保险人变更受益人，当事人主张变更行为自变更意思表示发出时生效的，人民法院应予支持。  
投保人或者被保险人变更受益人未通知保险人，保险人主张变更对其不发生效力的，人民法院应予支持。  
投保人变更受益人未经被保险人同意的，人民法院应认定变更行为无效。

**Article 10** Where the applicant or the insured changes the beneficiary, and a party claims that the change takes effect from the time the expression of intention to change is made, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where the applicant or the insured changes the beneficiary without notifying the insurer, and the insurer claims that the change is not effective against it, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where the applicant changes the beneficiary without the consent of the insured, the People's Court shall determine that the change is invalid.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　投保人或者被保险人在保险事故发生后变更受益人，变更后的受益人请求保险人给付保险金的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 11** Where the applicant or the insured changes the beneficiary after the occurrence of the insured event, and the changed beneficiary requests the insurer to pay the insurance benefits, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　投保人或者被保险人指定数人为受益人，部分受益人在保险事故发生前死亡、放弃受益权或者依法丧失受益权的，该受益人应得的受益份额按照保险合同的约定处理；保险合同没有约定或者约定不明的，该受益人应得的受益份额按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）未约定受益顺序和受益份额的，由其他受益人平均享有；  
（二）未约定受益顺序但约定受益份额的，由其他受益人按照相应比例享有；  
（三）约定受益顺序但未约定受益份额的，由同顺序的其他受益人平均享有；同一顺序没有其他受益人的，由后一顺序的受益人平均享有；  
（四）约定受益顺序和受益份额的，由同顺序的其他受益人按照相应比例享有；同一顺序没有其他受益人的，由后一顺序的受益人按照相应比例享有。

**Article 12** Where the applicant or the insured designates several persons as beneficiaries, and part of the beneficiaries die, waive the right to benefits or lose the right to benefits according to law before the occurrence of the insured event, the share of benefits that such beneficiary should receive shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the insurance contract; if the insurance contract has no provisions or the provisions are unclear, the share of benefits that such beneficiary should receive shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If neither the order of benefits nor the share of benefits is stipulated, the other beneficiaries shall share equally;  
(2) If the order of benefits is not stipulated but the share of benefits is stipulated, the other beneficiaries shall share in proportion to their respective shares;  
(3) If the order of benefits is stipulated but the share of benefits is not stipulated, the other beneficiaries in the same order shall share equally; if there are no other beneficiaries in the same order, the beneficiaries in the next order shall share equally;  
(4) If both the order of benefits and the share of benefits are stipulated, the other beneficiaries in the same order shall share in proportion to their respective shares; if there are no other beneficiaries in the same order, the beneficiaries in the next order shall share in proportion to their respective shares.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　保险事故发生后，受益人将与本次保险事故相对应的全部或者部分保险金请求权转让给第三人，当事人主张该转让行为有效的，人民法院应予支持，但根据合同性质、当事人约定或者法律规定不得转让的除外。

**Article 13** After the occurrence of an insured event, if the beneficiary transfers to a third party all or part of the claim for insurance benefits corresponding to that insured event, and a party claims that such transfer is valid, the People's Court shall support such claim, unless the transfer is prohibited by the nature of the contract, the agreement of the parties, or the provisions of the law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保险金根据[保险法第四十二条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-42)规定作为被保险人的遗产，被保险人的继承人要求保险人给付保险金，保险人以其已向持有保险单的被保险人的其他继承人给付保险金为由抗辩的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where the insurance benefits are regarded as the estate of the insured in accordance with Article 42 of the Insurance Law, and the heirs of the insured request the insurer to pay the insurance benefits, if the insurer defends on the ground that it has already paid the insurance benefits to other heirs of the insured who hold the insurance policy, the People's Court shall support such defense.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　受益人与被保险人存在继承关系，在同一事件中死亡且不能确定死亡先后顺序的，人民法院应根据[保险法第四十二条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-42)第二款的规定推定受益人死亡在先，并按照保险法及本解释的相关规定确定保险金归属。

**Article 15** Where the beneficiary and the insured have a succession relationship and die in the same event, and the order of death cannot be determined, the People's Court shall presume that the beneficiary died earlier in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 42 of the Insurance Law, and determine the attribution of the insurance benefits in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Insurance Law and this Interpretation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　保险合同解除时，投保人与被保险人、受益人为不同主体，被保险人或者受益人要求退还保险单的现金价值的，人民法院不予支持，但保险合同另有约定的除外。  
投保人故意造成被保险人死亡、伤残或者疾病，保险人依照[保险法第四十三条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-43)规定退还保险单的现金价值的，其他权利人按照被保险人、被保险人继承人的顺序确定。

**Article 16** When an insurance contract is dissolved, if the applicant, the insured, and the beneficiary are different persons, and the insured or the beneficiary requests a refund of the cash value of the insurance policy, the People's Court shall not support such request, unless otherwise agreed in the insurance contract.  
Where the applicant intentionally causes the death, disability or illness of the insured, and the insurer refunds the cash value of the insurance policy in accordance with Article 43 of the Insurance Law, the other entitled persons shall be determined in the order of the insured, followed by the heirs of the insured.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　投保人解除保险合同，当事人以其解除合同未经被保险人或者受益人同意为由主张解除行为无效的，人民法院不予支持，但被保险人或者受益人已向投保人支付相当于保险单现金价值的款项并通知保险人的除外。

**Article 17** Where the applicant dissolves the insurance contract, and a party claims that the dissolution is invalid on the ground that it was made without the consent of the insured or the beneficiary, the People's Court shall not support such claim, unless the insured or the beneficiary has paid to the applicant an amount equivalent to the cash value of the insurance policy and notified the insurer.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　保险人给付费用补偿型的医疗费用保险金时，主张扣减被保险人从公费医疗或者社会医疗保险取得的赔偿金额的，应当证明该保险产品在厘定医疗费用保险费率时已经将公费医疗或者社会医疗保险部分相应扣除，并按照扣减后的标准收取保险费。

**Article 18** When an insurer pays medical expense insurance benefits of the expense reimbursement type and claims to deduct the amount of compensation received by the insured from public medical care or social medical insurance, the insurer shall prove that when determining the premium rate for medical expense insurance, the corresponding portion of public medical care or social medical insurance has been deducted, and the premium was collected according to the deducted standard.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保险合同约定按照基本医疗保险的标准核定医疗费用，保险人以被保险人的医疗支出超出基本医疗保险范围为由拒绝给付保险金的，人民法院不予支持；保险人有证据证明被保险人支出的费用超过基本医疗保险同类医疗费用标准，要求对超出部分拒绝给付保险金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where an insurance contract stipulates that medical expenses shall be verified in accordance with the standards of basic medical insurance, and the insurer refuses to pay insurance benefits on the ground that the insured's medical expenses exceed the scope of basic medical insurance, the People's Court shall not support such refusal; if the insurer has evidence proving that the expenses incurred by the insured exceed the standard for similar medical expenses under basic medical insurance and requests to refuse payment of insurance benefits for the excess portion, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　保险人以被保险人未在保险合同约定的医疗服务机构接受治疗为由拒绝给付保险金的，人民法院应予支持，但被保险人因情况紧急必须立即就医的除外。

**Article 20** Where the insurer refuses to pay insurance benefits on the ground that the insured did not receive treatment at a medical service institution agreed upon in the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such refusal, unless the insured had to seek immediate medical treatment due to an emergency.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　保险人以被保险人自杀为由拒绝给付保险金的，由保险人承担举证责任。  
受益人或者被保险人的继承人以被保险人自杀时无民事行为能力为由抗辩的，由其承担举证责任。

**Article 21** Where the insurer refuses to pay insurance benefits on the ground that the insured committed suicide, the insurer bears the burden of adducing evidence.  
If the beneficiary or the heirs of the insured defend on the ground that the insured lacked civil capacity at the time of suicide, they shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　[保险法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-45)规定的“被保险人故意犯罪”的认定，应当以刑事侦查机关、检察机关和审判机关的生效法律文书或者其他结论性意见为依据。

**Article 22** The determination of "the insured intentionally commits a crime" as provided in Article 45 of the Insurance Law shall be based on the effective legal documents or other conclusive opinions of the criminal investigation authorities, procuratorial authorities, and adjudicative authorities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　保险人主张根据[保险法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-45)的规定不承担给付保险金责任的，应当证明被保险人的死亡、伤残结果与其实施的故意犯罪或者抗拒依法采取的刑事强制措施的行为之间存在因果关系。  
被保险人在羁押、服刑期间因意外或者疾病造成伤残或者死亡，保险人主张根据[保险法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/保险/中华人民共和国保险法.md#art-45)的规定不承担给付保险金责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 23** Where the insurer claims that it shall not bear the obligation to pay insurance benefits in accordance with Article 45 of the Insurance Law, it shall prove that there is a causal relationship between the death or disability of the insured and the insured's commission of an intentional crime or resistance to criminal compulsory measures taken according to law.  
If the insured suffers disability or death due to an accident or illness while in custody or serving a sentence, and the insurer claims that it shall not bear the obligation to pay insurance benefits in accordance with Article 45 of the Insurance Law, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　投保人为被保险人订立以死亡为给付保险金条件的保险合同，被保险人被宣告死亡后，当事人要求保险人按照保险合同约定给付保险金的，人民法院应予支持。  
被保险人被宣告死亡之日在保险责任期间之外，但有证据证明下落不明之日在保险责任期间之内，当事人要求保险人按照保险合同约定给付保险金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 24** Where the applicant concludes an insurance contract for the insured that takes death as the condition for payment of insurance benefits, and after the insured is declared dead, a party requests the insurer to pay insurance benefits in accordance with the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such request.  
If the date on which the insured is declared dead is outside the period of insurance liability, but there is evidence proving that the date of disappearance is within the period of insurance liability, and a party requests the insurer to pay insurance benefits in accordance with the insurance contract, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被保险人的损失系由承保事故或者非承保事故、免责事由造成难以确定，当事人请求保险人给付保险金的，人民法院可以按照相应比例予以支持。

**Article 25** Where it is difficult to determine that the insured's loss is caused by an insured event or a non-insured event or a cause of exemption, and a party requests the insurer to pay insurance benefits, the People's Court may support the request in a corresponding proportion.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释自2015年12月1日起施行。本解释施行后尚未终审的保险合同纠纷案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 26** This Interpretation shall come into force as of December 1, 2015. Disputes over insurance contracts that have not been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation shall be governed by this Interpretation; cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation, where a party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided through the trial supervision procedure, shall not be governed by this Interpretation.
