# 中华人民共和国军人保险法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2012-04-27  
**生效日期**：2012-07-01

> （2012年4月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　军人伤亡保险 第三章　退役养老保险 第四章　退役医疗保险 第五章　随军未就业的军人配偶保险 第六章　军人保险基金 第七章　保险经办与监督 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范军人保险关系，维护军人合法权益，促进国防和军队建设，制定本法。 第二条　国家建立军人保险制度。 军人伤亡保险、退役养老保险、退役医疗保险和随军未就业的军人配偶保险的建立、缴费和转移接续等适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范军人保险关系，维护军人合法权益，促进国防和军队建设，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of regulating military personnel insurance relations, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of military personnel, and promoting national defense and military construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家建立军人保险制度。  
军人伤亡保险、退役养老保险、退役医疗保险和随军未就业的军人配偶保险的建立、缴费和转移接续等适用本法。

**Article 2** The State shall establish a military personnel insurance system.  
This Law shall apply to the establishment, contribution, and transfer of military personnel casualty insurance, retirement pension insurance, retiree medical insurance, and insurance for military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军人保险制度应当体现军人职业特点，与社会保险制度相衔接，与经济社会发展水平相适应。  
国家根据社会保险制度的发展，适时补充完善军人保险制度。

**Article 3** The military personnel insurance system shall reflect the occupational characteristics of military personnel, be coordinated with the social insurance system, and be compatible with the level of economic and social development.  
The State shall, in light of the development of the social insurance system, supplement and improve the military personnel insurance system in a timely manner.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家促进军人保险事业的发展，为军人保险提供财政拨款和政策支持。

**Article 4** The State shall promote the development of military personnel insurance, and shall provide financial appropriations and policy support for military personnel insurance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中国人民解放军军人保险主管部门负责全军的军人保险工作。国务院社会保险行政部门、财政部门和军队其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的军人保险工作。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门负责承办军人保险登记、个人权益记录、军人保险待遇支付等工作。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构，按照各自职责办理军人保险与社会保险关系转移接续手续。

**Article 5** The competent department for military personnel insurance of the People's Liberation Army shall be responsible for military personnel insurance work throughout the armed forces. The social insurance administrative department of the State Council, the finance department, and other relevant departments of the military shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant military personnel insurance work.  
The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall be responsible for undertaking such work as registration of military personnel insurance, recording of individual rights and interests, and payment of military personnel insurance benefits.  
The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and local social insurance handling agencies shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, handle the procedures for the transfer of military personnel insurance and social insurance relationships.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军人依法参加军人保险并享受相应的保险待遇。  
军人有权查询、核对个人缴费记录和个人权益记录，要求军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构依法办理养老、医疗等保险关系转移接续手续，提供军人保险和社会保险咨询等相关服务。

**Article 6** Military personnel shall, according to law, participate in military personnel insurance and enjoy the corresponding insurance benefits.  
Military personnel shall have the right to inquire about and verify individual contribution records and individual rights and interest records, and to request that the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and local social insurance handling agencies, according to law, handle the procedures for the transfer of pension, medical, and other insurance relationships, and provide related services such as consultation on military personnel insurance and social insurance.

### 第二章　军人伤亡保险

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军人因战、因公死亡的，按照认定的死亡性质和相应的保险金标准，给付军人死亡保险金。

**Article 7** Where a military personnel dies due to war or duty, the military personnel death insurance benefit shall be paid according to the determined nature of death and the corresponding insurance benefit standard.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军人因战、因公、因病致残的，按照评定的残疾等级和相应的保险金标准，给付军人残疾保险金。

**Article 8** Where a military personnel becomes disabled due to war, duty, or illness, the military personnel disability insurance benefit shall be paid according to the assessed disability grade and the corresponding insurance benefit standard.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军人死亡和残疾的性质认定、残疾等级评定和相应的保险金标准，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 9** The determination of the nature of death and disability, the assessment of disability grade, and the corresponding insurance benefit standards for military personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军人因下列情形之一死亡或者致残的，不享受军人伤亡保险待遇：  
（一）故意犯罪的；  
（二）醉酒或者吸毒的；  
（三）自残或者自杀的；  
（四）法律、行政法规和军事法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 10** Where a military personnel dies or becomes disabled due to any of the following circumstances, he or she shall not enjoy military personnel casualty insurance benefits:  
(1) intentionally committing a crime;  
(2) being intoxicated or using drugs;  
(3) committing self-injury or suicide;  
(4) other circumstances as provided for by laws, administrative regulations, and military regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　已经评定残疾等级的因战、因公致残的军人退出现役参加工作后旧伤复发的，依法享受相应的工伤待遇。

**Article 11** Where a military personnel who has been assessed with a disability grade due to war or duty, after being discharged from active service and entering employment, has a recurrence of the old injury, he or she shall, according to law, enjoy the corresponding work-related injury benefits.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军人伤亡保险所需资金由国家承担，个人不缴纳保险费。

**Article 12** The funds required for military personnel casualty insurance shall be borne by the State, and individuals shall not pay insurance premiums.

### 第三章　退役养老保险

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军人退出现役参加基本养老保险的，国家给予退役养老保险补助。

**Article 13** Where military personnel who are discharged from active service participate in basic pension insurance, the State shall grant a subsidy for retirement pension insurance.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军人退役养老保险补助标准，由中国人民解放军总后勤部会同国务院有关部门，按照国家规定的基本养老保险缴费标准、军人工资水平等因素拟订，报国务院、中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 14** The subsidy standard for military personnel retirement pension insurance shall be drafted by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, based on such factors as the basic pension insurance contribution standards and the salary levels of military personnel as prescribed by the State, and shall be submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军人入伍前已经参加基本养老保险的，由地方社会保险经办机构和军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门办理基本养老保险关系转移接续手续。

**Article 15** Where a military personnel has already participated in basic pension insurance before enlistment, the local social insurance handling agency and the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall handle the procedures for the transfer of the basic pension insurance relationship.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军人退出现役后参加职工基本养老保险的，由军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门将军人退役养老保险关系和相应资金转入地方社会保险经办机构，地方社会保险经办机构办理相应的转移接续手续。  
军人服现役年限与入伍前和退出现役后参加职工基本养老保险的缴费年限合并计算。

**Article 16** Where a military personnel participates in the basic pension insurance for employees after being discharged from active service, the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall transfer the military personnel retirement pension insurance relationship and the corresponding funds to the local social insurance handling agency, and the local social insurance handling agency shall handle the corresponding transfer procedures.  
The number of years of active service of the military personnel shall be combined with the number of contribution years for the basic pension insurance for employees before enlistment and after discharge from active service for calculation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军人退出现役后参加新型农村社会养老保险或者城镇居民社会养老保险的，按照国家有关规定办理转移接续手续。

**Article 17** Where a military personnel participates in the new rural social pension insurance or the urban resident social pension insurance after being discharged from active service, the transfer procedures shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军人退出现役到公务员岗位或者参照公务员法管理的工作人员岗位的，以及现役军官、文职干部退出现役自主择业的，其养老保险办法按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 18** Where military personnel are discharged from active service to take up civil servant positions or positions of staff members managed by reference to the Civil Servant Law, and where active duty officers and civilian cadres are discharged from active service and choose their own occupations, their pension insurance methods shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　军人退出现役采取退休方式安置的，其养老办法按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 19** Where military personnel are resettled in the form of retirement after being discharged from active service, their pension methods shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

### 第四章　退役医疗保险

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　参加军人退役医疗保险的军官、文职干部和士官应当缴纳军人退役医疗保险费，国家按照个人缴纳的军人退役医疗保险费的同等数额给予补助。  
义务兵和供给制学员不缴纳军人退役医疗保险费，国家按照规定的标准给予军人退役医疗保险补助。

**Article 20** Officers, civilian cadres, and non-commissioned officers participating in the military personnel retiree medical insurance shall pay military personnel retiree medical insurance premiums, and the State shall grant subsidies in an amount equal to the individual contributions of the military personnel retiree medical insurance premiums.  
Conscripts and trainees under the supply system shall not pay military personnel retiree medical insurance premiums, and the State shall grant subsidies for military personnel retiree medical insurance according to the prescribed standards.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军人退役医疗保险个人缴费标准和国家补助标准，由中国人民解放军总后勤部会同国务院有关部门，按照国家规定的缴费比例、军人工资水平等因素确定。

**Article 21** The individual contribution standards and State subsidy standards for military personnel retiree medical insurance shall be determined by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, based on such factors as the contribution ratios and the salary levels of military personnel as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军人入伍前已经参加基本医疗保险的，由地方社会保险经办机构和军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门办理基本医疗保险关系转移接续手续。

**Article 22** Where a military personnel has already participated in basic medical insurance before enlistment, the local social insurance handling agency and the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall handle the procedures for the transfer of the basic medical insurance relationship.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　军人退出现役后参加职工基本医疗保险的，由军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门将军人退役医疗保险关系和相应资金转入地方社会保险经办机构，地方社会保险经办机构办理相应的转移接续手续。  
军人服现役年限视同职工基本医疗保险缴费年限，与入伍前和退出现役后参加职工基本医疗保险的缴费年限合并计算。

**Article 23** Where a military personnel participates in the basic medical insurance for employees after being discharged from active service, the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall transfer the military personnel retiree medical insurance relationship and the corresponding funds to the local social insurance handling agency, and the local social insurance handling agency shall handle the corresponding transfer procedures.  
The number of years of active service of the military personnel shall be deemed as contribution years for the basic medical insurance for employees, and shall be combined with the contribution years for the basic medical insurance for employees before enlistment and after discharge from active service for calculation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　军人退出现役后参加新型农村合作医疗或者城镇居民基本医疗保险的，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 24** Where a military personnel participates in the new rural cooperative medical care or the basic medical insurance for urban residents after being discharged from active service, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第五章　随军未就业的军人配偶保险

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家为随军未就业的军人配偶建立养老保险、医疗保险等。随军未就业的军人配偶参加保险，应当缴纳养老保险费和医疗保险费，国家给予相应的补助。  
随军未就业的军人配偶保险个人缴费标准和国家补助标准，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 25** The State shall establish pension insurance, medical insurance, etc. for military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military. Military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military and participate in insurance shall pay pension insurance premiums and medical insurance premiums, and the State shall grant corresponding subsidies.  
The individual contribution standards and the State subsidy standards for insurance for military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　随军未就业的军人配偶随军前已经参加社会保险的，由地方社会保险经办机构和军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门办理保险关系转移接续手续。

**Article 26** Where a military spouse who is unemployed while accompanying the military has already participated in social insurance before accompanying the military, the local social insurance handling agency and the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall handle the procedures for the transfer of the insurance relationship.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　随军未就业的军人配偶实现就业或者军人退出现役时，由军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门将其养老保险、医疗保险关系和相应资金转入地方社会保险经办机构，地方社会保险经办机构办理相应的转移接续手续。  
军人配偶在随军未就业期间的养老保险、医疗保险缴费年限与其在地方参加职工基本养老保险、职工基本医疗保险的缴费年限合并计算。

**Article 27** When a military spouse who is unemployed while accompanying the military becomes employed or when the military personnel is discharged from active service, the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall transfer the pension insurance and medical insurance relationships and the corresponding funds to the local social insurance handling agency, and the local social insurance handling agency shall handle the corresponding transfer procedures.  
The number of contribution years for pension insurance and medical insurance of the military spouse during the period of unemployment while accompanying the military shall be combined with the number of contribution years for the basic pension insurance for employees and the basic medical insurance for employees participated in by the military spouse locally for calculation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　随军未就业的军人配偶达到国家规定的退休年龄时，按照国家有关规定确定退休地，由军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门将其养老保险关系和相应资金转入退休地社会保险经办机构，享受相应的基本养老保险待遇。

**Article 28** When a military spouse who is unemployed while accompanying the military reaches the retirement age prescribed by the State, the place of retirement shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall transfer the pension insurance relationship and the corresponding funds to the social insurance handling agency in the place of retirement, for the spouse to enjoy the corresponding basic pension insurance benefits.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方人民政府和有关部门应当为随军未就业的军人配偶提供就业指导、培训等方面的服务。  
随军未就业的军人配偶无正当理由拒不接受当地人民政府就业安置，或者无正当理由拒不接受当地人民政府指定部门、机构介绍的适当工作、提供的就业培训的，停止给予保险缴费补助。

**Article 29** Local people's governments and relevant departments shall provide services such as employment guidance and training for military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military.  
Where a military spouse who is unemployed while accompanying the military, without justifiable reasons, refuses to accept employment placement by the local people's government, or refuses to accept suitable work introduced by the departments or agencies designated by the local people's government or employment training provided thereby, the subsidy for insurance contributions shall be suspended.

### 第六章　军人保险基金

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军人保险基金包括军人伤亡保险基金、军人退役养老保险基金、军人退役医疗保险基金和随军未就业的军人配偶保险基金。各项军人保险基金按照军人保险险种分别建账，分账核算，执行军队的会计制度。

**Article 30** Military personnel insurance funds shall include the military personnel casualty insurance fund, the military personnel retirement pension insurance fund, the military personnel retiree medical insurance fund, and the insurance fund for military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military. Separate accounts shall be established and accounting shall be conducted separately for each type of military personnel insurance fund according to the categories of military personnel insurance, and the military accounting system shall be implemented.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　军人保险基金由个人缴费、中央财政负担的军人保险资金以及利息收入等资金构成。

**Article 31** The military personnel insurance funds shall be composed of individual contributions, military personnel insurance funds borne by the central finance, and interest income, among other funds.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　军人应当缴纳的保险费，由其所在单位代扣代缴。  
随军未就业的军人配偶应当缴纳的保险费，由军人所在单位代扣代缴。

**Article 32** The insurance premiums payable by military personnel shall be withheld and paid by their units on their behalf.  
The insurance premiums payable by military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military shall be withheld and paid by the military personnel's units on their behalf.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中央财政负担的军人保险资金，由国务院财政部门纳入年度国防费预算。

**Article 33** The military personnel insurance funds borne by the central finance shall be included by the finance department of the State Council in the annual national defense expenditure budget.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　军人保险基金按照国家和军队的预算管理制度，实行预算、决算管理。

**Article 34** The military personnel insurance funds shall be subject to budget and final account management in accordance with the budget management system of the State and the military.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　军人保险基金实行专户存储，具体管理办法按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 35** The military personnel insurance funds shall be stored in special accounts, and the specific management measures shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　军人保险基金由中国人民解放军总后勤部军人保险基金管理机构集中管理。  
军人保险基金管理机构应当严格管理军人保险基金，保证基金安全。

**Article 36** The military personnel insurance funds shall be centrally managed by the military personnel insurance fund management institution of the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army.  
The military personnel insurance fund management institution shall strictly manage the military personnel insurance funds and ensure their safety.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　军人保险基金应当专款专用，按照规定的项目、范围和标准支出，任何单位和个人不得贪污、侵占、挪用，不得变更支出项目、扩大支出范围或者改变支出标准。

**Article 37** The military personnel insurance funds shall be used exclusively for designated purposes, and shall be expended according to the prescribed items, scope, and standards. No unit or individual shall embezzle, misappropriate, or divert the funds, or alter the expenditure items, expand the expenditure scope, or change the expenditure standards.

### 第七章　保险经办与监督

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构应当建立健全军人保险经办管理制度。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门应当按时足额支付军人保险金。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构应当及时办理军人保险和社会保险关系转移接续手续。

**Article 38** The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and local social insurance handling agencies shall establish and improve the management system for military personnel insurance handling.  
The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall pay the military personnel insurance benefits in full and on time.  
The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and local social insurance handling agencies shall, in a timely manner, handle the procedures for the transfer of military personnel insurance and social insurance relationships.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门应当为军人及随军未就业的军人配偶建立保险档案，及时、完整、准确地记录其个人缴费和国家补助，以及享受军人保险待遇等个人权益记录，并定期将个人权益记录单送达本人。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构应当为军人及随军未就业的军人配偶提供军人保险和社会保险咨询等相关服务。

**Article 39** The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall establish insurance archives for military personnel and military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military, and shall promptly, completely, and accurately record their individual contributions and State subsidies, as well as personal rights and interest records such as enjoyment of military personnel insurance benefits, and shall regularly deliver the personal rights and interest records to the individuals.  
The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and local social insurance handling agencies shall provide related services such as consultation on military personnel insurance and social insurance for military personnel and military spouses who are unemployed while accompanying the military.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　军人保险信息系统由中国人民解放军总后勤部负责统一建设。

**Article 40** The military personnel insurance information system shall be uniformly constructed by the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　中国人民解放军总后勤部财务部门和中国人民解放军审计机关按照各自职责，对军人保险基金的收支和管理情况实施监督。

**Article 41** The finance department of the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army and the audit authority of the People's Liberation Army shall, within their respective functions and duties, supervise the revenue, expenditure, and management of the military personnel insurance funds.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　军队后勤（联勤）机关、地方社会保险行政部门，应当对单位和个人遵守本法的情况进行监督检查。  
军队后勤（联勤）机关、地方社会保险行政部门实施监督检查时，被检查单位和个人应当如实提供与军人保险有关的资料，不得拒绝检查或者谎报、瞒报。

**Article 42** The military logistics (combined logistics) organs and local social insurance administrative departments shall supervise and inspect the compliance of units and individuals with this Law.  
When the military logistics (combined logistics) organs and local social insurance administrative departments conduct supervision and inspection, the units and individuals under inspection shall truthfully provide materials related to military personnel insurance, and shall not refuse inspection or make false statements or conceal information.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门和地方社会保险经办机构及其工作人员，应当依法为军队单位和军人的信息保密，不得以任何形式泄露。

**Article 43** The finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ, local social insurance handling agencies, and their staff members shall, according to law, keep confidential the information of military units and military personnel, and shall not disclose such information in any form.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　任何单位或者个人有权对违反本法规定的行为进行举报、投诉。  
军队和地方有关部门、机构对属于职责范围内的举报、投诉，应当依法处理；对不属于本部门、本机构职责范围的，应当书面通知并移交有权处理的部门、机构处理。有权处理的部门、机构应当及时处理，不得推诿。

**Article 44** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or complain about any act violating the provisions of this Law.  
The relevant departments and agencies of the military and the locality shall, according to law, handle reports and complaints that fall within their functions and duties; for those that do not fall within the functions and duties of their department or agency, they shall notify in writing and transfer the matter to the department or agency with authority to handle it. The department or agency with authority to handle it shall handle the matter in a timely manner and shall not shirk responsibility.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军队后勤（联勤）机关财务部门、社会保险经办机构，有下列情形之一的，由军队后勤（联勤）机关或者社会保险行政部门责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）不按照规定建立、转移接续军人保险关系的；  
（二）不按照规定收缴、上缴个人缴纳的保险费的；  
（三）不按照规定给付军人保险金的；  
（四）篡改或者丢失个人缴费记录等军人保险档案资料的；  
（五）泄露军队单位和军人的信息的；  
（六）违反规定划拨、存储军人保险基金的；  
（七）有违反法律、法规损害军人保险权益的其他行为的。

**Article 45** Where the finance department of the military logistics (combined logistics) organ or a social insurance handling agency has any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered by the military logistics (combined logistics) organ or the social insurance administrative department to make corrections; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if losses are caused, liability for compensation shall be borne according to law:  
(1) failing to establish or transfer the military personnel insurance relationship in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to collect and turn over the individual insurance premiums in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to pay the military personnel insurance benefits in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) altering or losing military personnel insurance archives and materials such as individual contribution records;  
(5) disclosing information of military units and military personnel;  
(6) allocating or storing military personnel insurance funds in violation of the provisions;  
(7) having other acts in violation of laws and regulations that impair the rights and interests of military personnel insurance.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　贪污、侵占、挪用军人保险基金的，由军队后勤（联勤）机关责令限期退回，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 46** Where anyone embezzles, misappropriates, or diverts military personnel insurance funds, the military logistics (combined logistics) organ shall order the return of the funds within a time limit, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　以欺诈、伪造证明材料等手段骗取军人保险待遇的，由军队后勤（联勤）机关和社会保险行政部门责令限期退回，并依法给予处分。

**Article 47** Where anyone obtains military personnel insurance benefits by means of fraud, forgery of evidentiary materials, or other means, the military logistics (combined logistics) organ and the social insurance administrative department shall order the return of the benefits within a time limit, and shall give sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　军人退出现役后参加失业保险的，其服现役年限视同失业保险缴费年限，与入伍前和退出现役后参加失业保险的缴费年限合并计算。

**Article 49** Where a military personnel participates in unemployment insurance after being discharged from active service, the number of years of active service shall be deemed as the number of contribution years for unemployment insurance, and shall be combined with the number of contribution years for unemployment insurance before enlistment and after discharge from active service for calculation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法关于军人保险权益和义务的规定，适用于人民武装警察；中国人民武装警察部队保险基金管理，按照中国人民武装警察部队资金管理体制执行。

**Article 50** The provisions of this Law regarding the rights and obligations of military personnel insurance shall apply to the People's Armed Police; the management of insurance funds of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be implemented in accordance with the fund management system of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本法自2012年7月1日起施行。

**Article 51** This Law shall come into force on July 1, 2012.
