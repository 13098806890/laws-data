# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法》若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕12号  
**公布日期**：2023-08-30  
**生效日期**：2024-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法〉若干问题的解释（二）》已于2023年8月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1898次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年1月1日起施行。 2023年11月30日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系 法律适用法》若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理涉外民商事案件适用外国法律的，应当根据[涉外民事关系法律适用法第十条](../../民事与商事/外商与涉外/中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法.md#art-10)第一款的规定查明该国法律。  
当事人选择适用外国法律的，应当提供该国法律。  
当事人未选择适用外国法律的，由人民法院查明该国法律。

**Article 1** Where a People's Court applies foreign law in hearing a foreign-related civil or commercial case, it shall ascertain the law of that country pursuant to Article 10(1) of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations.  
Where a party chooses to apply foreign law, it shall provide the law of that country.  
Where the parties do not choose to apply foreign law, the People's Court shall ascertain the law of that country.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院可以通过下列途径查明外国法律：  
（一）由当事人提供；  
（二）通过司法协助渠道由对方的中央机关或者主管机关提供；  
（三）通过最高人民法院请求我国驻该国使领馆或者该国驻我国使领馆提供；  
（四）由最高人民法院建立或者参与的法律查明合作机制参与方提供；  
（五）由最高人民法院国际商事专家委员会专家提供；  
（六）由法律查明服务机构或者中外法律专家提供；  
（七）其他适当途径。  
人民法院通过前款规定的其中一项途径无法获得外国法律或者获得的外国法律内容不明确、不充分的，应当通过该款规定的不同途径补充查明。  
人民法院依据本条第一款第一项的规定要求当事人协助提供外国法律的，不得仅以当事人未予协助提供为由认定外国法律不能查明。

**Article 2** A People's Court may ascertain foreign law through the following channels:  
(1) provision by the parties;  
(2) provision by the central authority or competent authority of the other party through the channel of judicial assistance;  
(3) requesting, through the Supreme People's Court, the Chinese embassy or consulate in that country or the embassy or consulate of that country in China to provide it;  
(4) provision by participants in a legal ascertainment cooperation mechanism established or participated in by the Supreme People's Court;  
(5) provision by experts of the International Commercial Expert Committee of the Supreme People's Court;  
(6) provision by legal ascertainment service institutions or Chinese or foreign legal experts;  
(7) other appropriate channels.  
Where a People's Court cannot obtain foreign law through any of the channels provided in the preceding paragraph, or the content of the foreign law obtained is unclear or insufficient, it shall supplement the ascertainment through different channels provided in that paragraph.  
Where a People's Court requires a party to assist in providing foreign law pursuant to item (1) of the first paragraph of this Article, it shall not determine that the foreign law cannot be ascertained solely on the ground that the party has not provided assistance.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　当事人提供外国法律的，应当提交该国法律的具体规定并说明获得途径、效力情况、与案件争议的关联性等。外国法律为判例法的，还应当提交判例全文。

**Article 3** Where a party provides foreign law, it shall submit the specific provisions of the law of that country and explain the channels through which it was obtained, its validity, its relevance to the dispute in the case, etc. Where the foreign law is case law, the full text of the case shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　法律查明服务机构、法律专家提供外国法律的，除提交[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定的材料外，还应当提交法律查明服务机构的资质证明、法律专家的身份及资历证明，并附与案件无利害关系的书面声明。

**Article 4** Where a legal ascertainment service institution or a legal expert provides foreign law, in addition to submitting the materials provided for in Article 3 of this Interpretation, it shall also submit the qualification certificate of the legal ascertainment service institution, the identity and qualification certificate of the legal expert, and attach a written declaration of no interest in the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　查明的外国法律的相关材料均应当在法庭上出示。人民法院应当听取各方当事人对外国法律的内容及其理解与适用的意见。

**Article 5** All relevant materials of the ascertained foreign law shall be produced in court. The People's Court shall hear the opinions of all parties on the content of the foreign law and its understanding and application.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院可以召集庭前会议或者以其他适当方式，确定需要查明的外国法律的范围。

**Article 6** The People's Court may convene a pre-trial conference or adopt other appropriate means to determine the scope of foreign law that needs to be ascertained.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院认为有必要的，可以通知提供外国法律的法律查明服务机构或者法律专家出庭接受询问。当事人申请法律查明服务机构或者法律专家出庭，人民法院认为有必要的，可以准许。  
法律查明服务机构或者法律专家现场出庭确有困难的，可以在线接受询问，但法律查明服务机构或者法律专家所在国法律对跨国在线参与庭审有禁止性规定的除外。  
出庭的法律查明服务机构或者法律专家只围绕外国法律及其理解发表意见，不参与其他法庭审理活动。

**Article 7** Where a People's Court considers it necessary, it may notify the legal ascertainment service institution or legal expert that provided the foreign law to appear in court to be questioned. Where a party applies for the legal ascertainment service institution or legal expert to appear in court, the People's Court may permit it if it considers it necessary.  
Where it is truly difficult for the legal ascertainment service institution or legal expert to appear in court physically, they may be questioned online, except where the law of the country where the legal ascertainment service institution or legal expert is located has prohibitive provisions on cross-border online participation in court hearings.  
The legal ascertainment service institution or legal expert who appears in court shall only express opinions on the foreign law and its understanding, and shall not participate in other court hearing activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院对外国法律的内容及其理解与适用，根据以下情形分别作出处理：  
（一）当事人对外国法律的内容及其理解与适用均无异议的，人民法院可以予以确认；  
（二）当事人对外国法律的内容及其理解与适用有异议的，应当说明理由。人民法院认为有必要的，可以补充查明或者要求当事人补充提供材料。经过补充查明或者补充提供材料，当事人仍有异议的，由人民法院审查认定；  
（三）外国法律的内容已为人民法院生效裁判所认定的，人民法院应当予以确认，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 8** A People's Court shall handle the content of foreign law and its understanding and application in the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the parties have no objection to the content of the foreign law and its understanding and application, the People's Court may confirm it;  
(2) Where the parties have objections to the content of the foreign law and its understanding and application, they shall state the reasons. Where the People's Court considers it necessary, it may supplement the ascertainment or require the parties to supplement materials. Where, after the supplement of ascertainment or the supplement of materials, the parties still have objections, the People's Court shall examine and determine the matter;  
(3) Where the content of the foreign law has been ascertained in a valid judgment of the People's Court, the People's Court shall confirm it, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院应当根据外国法律查明办理相关手续等所需时间确定当事人提供外国法律的期限。当事人有具体理由说明无法在人民法院确定的期限内提供外国法律而申请适当延长期限的，人民法院视情可予准许。  
当事人选择适用外国法律，其在人民法院确定的期限内无正当理由未提供该外国法律的，人民法院可以认定为不能查明外国法律。

**Article 9** A People's Court shall determine the time limit for a party to provide foreign law based on the time required for going through relevant procedures for ascertaining foreign law. Where a party has specific reasons to explain that it cannot provide the foreign law within the time limit determined by the People's Court and applies for an appropriate extension of the time limit, the People's Court may grant it as it sees fit.  
Where a party chooses to apply foreign law and fails to provide that foreign law within the time limit determined by the People's Court without justifiable reasons, the People's Court may determine that the foreign law cannot be ascertained.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院依法适用外国法律审理案件，应当在裁判文书中载明外国法律的查明过程及外国法律的内容；人民法院认定外国法律不能查明的，应当载明不能查明的理由。

**Article 10** Where a People's Court applies foreign law according to law in hearing a case, it shall state in the judgment document the process of ascertaining the foreign law and the content of the foreign law; where the People's Court determines that the foreign law cannot be ascertained, it shall state the reasons why it cannot be ascertained.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对查明外国法律的费用负担，当事人有约定的，从其约定；没有约定的，人民法院可以根据当事人的诉讼请求和具体案情，在作出裁判时确定上述合理费用的负担。

**Article 11** Where the parties have an agreement on the burden of the costs of ascertaining foreign law, such agreement shall prevail; where there is no agreement, the People's Court may, based on the parties' claims and the specific circumstances of the case, determine the burden of the above reasonable costs when rendering a judgment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院查明香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的法律，可以参照适用本解释。有关法律和司法解释对查明香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的法律另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 12** A People's Court may apply this Interpretation mutatis mutandis when ascertaining the law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region. Where relevant laws and judicial interpretations have other provisions on ascertaining the law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本解释自2024年1月1日起施行。  
本解释公布施行后，最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 13** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2024.  
After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
