# 国务院关于鼓励外商投资的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：1986-10-11  
**生效日期**：1986-10-11

> （1986年10月11日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行） 第一条　为了改善投资环境，更好地吸收外商投资，引进先进技术，提高产品质量，扩大出口创汇，发展国民经济，特制定本规定。 第二条　国家鼓励外国的公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人(以下简称外国投资者)，在中国境内举办中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业和外资企业(以下简称外商投资企业)。 国家对下列外商投资企业给予特别优惠： 一、产品主要用于出口，年度外汇总收入额减除年度生产经营外汇支出额和外国投资者汇出分得利润所需外汇额以后，外汇有结余的生产型企业(以下简称产品出口企业)； 二、外国投资者提供先进技术，从事新产品开发，实现产品升级换代，以增加出口创汇或者替代进口的生产型企业(以下简称先进技术企业)。 第三条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业，除按照国家规定支付或者提取中方职工劳动保险、福利费用和住房补助基金外，免缴国家对职工的各项补贴。 第四条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业的场地使用费，除大城市市区繁华地段外，按下列标准计收： 一、开发费和使用费综合计收的地区，为每年每平方米五元至二十元； 二、开发费一次性计收或者上述企业自行开发场地的地区，使用费最高为每年每平方米三元。 前款规定的费用，地方人民政府可以酌情在一定期限内免收。 第五条　对产品出口企业和先进技术企业优先提供生产经营所需的水、电、运输条件和通信设施，按照当地国营企业收费标准计收费用。 第六条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业在生产和流通过程中需要借贷的短期周转资金，以及其他必需的信贷资金，经中国银行审核后，优先贷放。 第七条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业的外国投资者，将其从企业分得的利润汇出境外时，免缴汇出额的所得税。 第八条　产品出口企业按照国家规定减免企业所得税期满后，凡当年企业出口产品产值达到当年企业产品产值70%以上的，可以按照现行税率减半缴纳企业所得税。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了改善投资环境，更好地吸收外商投资，引进先进技术，提高产品质量，扩大出口创汇，发展国民经济，特制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of improving the investment environment, better absorbing foreign investment, introducing advanced technology, improving product quality, expanding export and foreign exchange earnings, and developing the national economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家鼓励外国的公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人(以下简称外国投资者)，在中国境内举办中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业和外资企业(以下简称外商投资企业)。  
国家对下列外商投资企业给予特别优惠：  
一、产品主要用于出口，年度外汇总收入额减除年度生产经营外汇支出额和外国投资者汇出分得利润所需外汇额以后，外汇有结余的生产型企业(以下简称产品出口企业)；  
二、外国投资者提供先进技术，从事新产品开发，实现产品升级换代，以增加出口创汇或者替代进口的生产型企业(以下简称先进技术企业)。

**Article 2** The State encourages foreign companies, enterprises and other economic organizations or individuals (hereinafter referred to as foreign investors) to establish, within the territory of China, Sino-foreign equity joint ventures, Sino-foreign contractual joint ventures and wholly foreign-owned enterprises (hereinafter referred to as foreign-invested enterprises).  
The State shall grant special preferential treatment to the following foreign-invested enterprises:  
1. Production-type enterprises whose products are mainly for export, which have a surplus of foreign exchange after deducting from their total annual foreign exchange revenue the annual foreign exchange expenditure for production and operation and the foreign exchange needed for the remittance abroad of the profits distributed to foreign investors (hereinafter referred to as product export enterprises);  
2. Production-type enterprises which are provided by foreign investors with advanced technology, engage in the development of new products, and realize product upgrading to increase export and foreign exchange earnings or replace imports (hereinafter referred to as technologically advanced enterprises).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业，除按照国家规定支付或者提取中方职工劳动保险、福利费用和住房补助基金外，免缴国家对职工的各项补贴。

**Article 3** Product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises shall, in addition to paying or appropriating in accordance with State regulations labor insurance, welfare expenses and housing subsidy funds for their Chinese employees, be exempted from paying various State subsidies to employees.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业的场地使用费，除大城市市区繁华地段外，按下列标准计收：  
一、开发费和使用费综合计收的地区，为每年每平方米五元至二十元；  
二、开发费一次性计收或者上述企业自行开发场地的地区，使用费最高为每年每平方米三元。  
前款规定的费用，地方人民政府可以酌情在一定期限内免收。

**Article 4** The site use fees for product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises, except for the busy urban districts of large cities, shall be calculated and collected according to the following standards:  
1. In areas where development fees and use fees are collectively calculated and collected, the fee shall be five to twenty yuan per square meter per year;  
2. In areas where the development fee is collected in a lump sum or where the enterprises themselves develop the site, the use fee shall be not more than three yuan per square meter per year.  
The fees specified in the preceding paragraph may, at the discretion of the local people's government, be exempted within a certain period.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对产品出口企业和先进技术企业优先提供生产经营所需的水、电、运输条件和通信设施，按照当地国营企业收费标准计收费用。

**Article 5** Product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises shall be given priority in obtaining the water, electricity, transportation conditions and communication facilities needed for production and operation, and the fees shall be collected according to the charging standards for local State-owned enterprises.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业在生产和流通过程中需要借贷的短期周转资金，以及其他必需的信贷资金，经中国银行审核后，优先贷放。

**Article 6** Short-term revolving funds needed by product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises in the course of production and circulation, as well as other necessary credit funds, shall be given priority in lending after examination and approval by the Bank of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　产品出口企业和先进技术企业的外国投资者，将其从企业分得的利润汇出境外时，免缴汇出额的所得税。

**Article 7** Where foreign investors of product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises remit abroad the profits distributed to them from the enterprises, they shall be exempted from income tax on the remitted amount.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　产品出口企业按照国家规定减免企业所得税期满后，凡当年企业出口产品产值达到当年企业产品产值70%以上的，可以按照现行税率减半缴纳企业所得税。  
经济特区和经济技术开发区的以及其他已经按15%的税率缴纳企业所得税的产品出口企业，符合前款条件的，减按10%的税率缴纳企业所得税。

**Article 8** After the expiration of the period of reduction or exemption of enterprise income tax in accordance with State regulations, any product export enterprise whose annual export product output value reaches 70% or more of its total annual product output value in that year may pay enterprise income tax at half the current tax rate.  
Product export enterprises in the special economic zones and the economic and technological development zones and other product export enterprises that have already paid enterprise income tax at a rate of 15%, which meet the conditions in the preceding paragraph, shall pay enterprise income tax at a reduced rate of 10%.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　先进技术企业按照国家规定减免企业所得税期满后，可以延长三年减半缴纳企业所得税。

**Article 9** After the expiration of the period of reduction or exemption of enterprise income tax in accordance with State regulations, a technologically advanced enterprise may have the period of paying enterprise income tax at half the rate extended for three years.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外国投资者将其从企业分得的利润，在中国境内再投资举办、扩建产品出口企业或者先进技术企业，经营期不少于五年的，经申请税务机关核准，全部退还其再投资部分已缴纳的企业所得税税款。经营期不足五年撤出该项投资的，应当缴回已退的企业所得税税款。

**Article 10** Where a foreign investor reinvests in China the profits distributed to it from the enterprise to establish or expand a product export enterprise or a technologically advanced enterprise for a period of operation of not less than five years, upon application and approval by the tax authorities, it shall be refunded the full amount of the enterprise income tax already paid on the reinvested portion. If the investment is withdrawn within a period of less than five years, the amount of enterprise income tax refunded shall be paid back.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对外商投资企业的出口产品，除原油、成品油和国家另有规定的产品外，免征工商统一税。

**Article 11** The export products of foreign-invested enterprises, except crude oil, finished oil and products for which the State has otherwise provided, shall be exempted from the consolidated industrial and commercial tax.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外商投资企业可以自行组织其产品出口，也可以按照国家规定委托代理出口。属于需要申领出口许可证的产品，按照企业年度出口计划，每半年申领一次许可证。

**Article 12** Foreign-invested enterprises may organize the export of their products on their own, or may entrust the export to agents in accordance with State regulations. For products for which an export license is required, the license shall be applied for once every six months according to the enterprise's annual export plan.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外商投资企业为履行其产品出口合同，需要进口(包括国家限制进口)的机械设备、生产用的车辆、原材料、燃料、散件、零部件、元器件、配套件，不再报请审批，免领进口许可证，由海关实行监管，凭企业合同或者进出口合同验放。  
前款所述进口料、件，只限于本企业自用，不得在国内市场出售；如用于内销产品，应当按照规定补办进口手续，并照章补税。

**Article 13** Where foreign-invested enterprises, for the purpose of fulfilling their product export contracts, need to import (including imports subject to State restrictions) machinery and equipment, vehicles for production use, raw materials, fuel, CKD/SKD parts, spare parts, components, and supporting parts, they shall no longer be required to apply for approval, shall be exempted from the requirement of an import license, and the customs shall exercise supervision and control and release the goods upon presentation of the enterprise contract or the import and export contract.  
The imported materials and parts as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be used only by the enterprise itself and shall not be sold on the domestic market; if they are used for products sold domestically, the import formalities shall be completed retroactively in accordance with regulations, and the taxes shall be paid retroactively in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外商投资企业之间，在外汇管理部门监管下，可以相互调剂外汇余缺。  
中国银行以及经中国人民银行指定的其他银行，可以对外商投资企业开办现汇抵押业务，贷放人民币资金。

**Article 14** Foreign-invested enterprises may, under the supervision of the foreign exchange control authorities, adjust their foreign exchange surpluses and deficiencies among themselves.  
The Bank of China and other banks designated by the People's Bank of China may provide to foreign-invested enterprises the business of mortgage of spot foreign exchange and make loans in Renminbi funds.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级人民政府和有关主管部门应当保障外商投资企业的自主权，支持外商投资企业按照国际上先进的科学方法管理企业。  
外商投资企业有权在批准的合同范围内，自行制定生产经营计划，筹措、运用资金，采购生产资料，销售产品；自行确定工资标准、工资形式和奖励、津贴制度。  
外商投资企业可以根据生产经营需要，自行确定其机构设置和人员编制，聘用或者辞退高级经营管理人员，增加或者辞退职工；可以在当地招聘和招收技术人员、管理人员和工人，被录用人员所在单位应当给予支持，允许流动；对违反规章制度，造成一定后果的职工，可以根据情节轻重，给予不同处分，直至开除。外商投资企业招聘、招收、辞退或者开除职工，应当向当地劳动人事部门备案。

**Article 15** The people's governments at all levels and the relevant competent departments shall safeguard the autonomy of foreign-invested enterprises and support these enterprises in managing themselves in accordance with internationally advanced scientific methods.  
Foreign-invested enterprises shall have the right, within the scope of the approved contract, to formulate their own production and operation plans, raise and use funds, purchase means of production, and sell products; and to determine on their own their wage standards, wage forms, and systems of rewards and subsidies.  
Foreign-invested enterprises may, according to their production and operation needs, determine on their own their organizational structure and staffing, appoint or dismiss senior managerial personnel, and increase or dismiss employees; they may recruit and hire technical personnel, managerial personnel and workers locally. The units to which the hired personnel belong shall give support and allow the flow of personnel. Employees who violate the enterprise's rules and regulations and cause certain consequences may, depending on the seriousness of the case, be given different disciplinary sanctions up to and including dismissal. Where foreign-invested enterprises recruit, hire, dismiss or expel employees, they shall report to the local labor and personnel departments for the record.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各地区、各部门必须执行《国务院关于坚决制止向企业乱摊派的通知》，由省级人民政府制定具体办法，加强监督管理。  
外商投资企业遇有不合理收费的情况可以拒交；也可以向当地经济委员会直到国家经济委员会申诉。

**Article 16** All localities and departments shall implement the Circular of the State Council on Resolutely Stopping the Arbitrary Collection of Fees from Enterprises, and the provincial people's governments shall formulate specific measures to strengthen supervision and management.  
Foreign-invested enterprises may refuse to pay any unreasonable charges; and they may also petition the local economic commission or even the State Economic Commission.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级人民政府和有关主管部门，应当加强协调工作，提高办事效率，及时审批外商投资企业申报的需要批复和解决的事宜。由国务院主管部门审批的外商投资企业的协议、合同、章程，审批机关必须在收到全部文件之日起三个月以内决定批准或者不批准。

**Article 17** The people's governments at all levels and the relevant competent departments shall strengthen coordination, improve work efficiency, and promptly examine and approve matters submitted by foreign-invested enterprises that require approval and solution. With respect to the agreements, contracts and articles of association of foreign-invested enterprises which are subject to approval by the competent department under the State Council, the examining and approving organ shall, within three months from the date of receipt of all documents, decide whether to approve or disapprove them.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定所指产品出口企业和先进技术企业，由该企业所在地的对外经济贸易部门会同有关部门根据企业合同确认，并出具证明。  
产品出口企业的年度出口实绩，如果未能实现企业合同规定的外汇平衡有结余的目标，应当在下一年度内补缴上一年度已经减免的税、费。

**Article 18** The product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises referred to in these Provisions shall be confirmed by the local department of foreign economic relations and trade in conjunction with the relevant departments on the basis of the enterprise contract, and a certificate shall be issued.  
If the annual export performance of a product export enterprise fails to achieve the target of foreign exchange balance with a surplus as specified in the enterprise contract, it shall, within the following year, pay back the taxes and fees that were reduced or exempted in the previous year.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定除明确规定适用于产品出口企业或者先进技术企业的条款外，其他条款适用于所有外商投资企业。  
本规定施行之日前获准举办的外商投资企业，凡符合本规定的优惠条件的，自施行之日起适用本规定。

**Article 19** Except for the provisions explicitly applicable to product export enterprises or technologically advanced enterprises, all other provisions of these Provisions shall apply to all foreign-invested enterprises.  
Where a foreign-invested enterprise approved for establishment before the date on which these Provisions come into force meets the preferential conditions of these Provisions, these Provisions shall apply to it from the date of coming into force.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　香港、澳门、台湾的公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人投资举办的企业，参照本规定执行。

**Article 20** Enterprises invested in and established by companies, enterprises and other economic organizations or individuals from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan shall be handled by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定由对外经济贸易部负责解释。

**Article 21** The Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Provisions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
