# 最高人民法院关于一方当事人在国内居住、另一方当事人在国外居住的涉外民事案件的上诉期应如何确定的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

### 最高人民法院关于一方当事人在国内居住、另一方当事人在国外居住的涉外民事案件的上诉期应如何确定的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于一方当事人在国内居住、  
  
另一方当事人在国外居住的涉外民事案件的上诉期  
  
应如何确定的批复  
  
一九八五年六月十一日法（研）复〔1985〕34号  
  
北京市高级人民法院：  
  
对你院四月二十八日请示的问题，我们意见如下：根据民事诉讼法（试行）第一百九十八条、第一百八十五条及第一百四十五条的规定，人民法院在处理涉外民事诉讼案件中，对在中华人民共和国领域内居住的一方当事人，不服第一审人民法院判决的上诉期为十五日；对不在中华人民共和国领域内居住的另一方当事人，不服第一审人民法院判决的上诉期为六十日。十五日期满后，在中华人民共和国领域内居住的一方当事人没有上诉的，不得上诉。但是由于不在中华人民共和国领域内居住的另一方当事人的上诉期限末满，本案的第一审人民法院的判决还不能发生法律效力。只有上诉期均已届满而双方当事人没有上诉时，第一审人民法院的判决才具有法律效力。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on How to Determine the Appeal Period for Foreign-Related Civil Cases Where One Party Resides Domestically and the Other Party Resides Abroad  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1985] No. 34, June 11, 1985  
Higher People's Court of Beijing Municipality:  
Regarding the question raised in your court's submission of April 28, our opinion is as follows: According to Articles 198, 185, and 145 of the Civil Procedure Law (Trial Implementation), when a People's Court handles a foreign-related civil action, for a party residing within the territory of the People's Republic of China who disagrees with the judgment of the first-instance People's Court, the appeal period shall be fifteen days; for the other party not residing within the territory of the People's Republic of China who disagrees with the judgment of the first-instance People's Court, the appeal period shall be sixty days. After the expiration of the fifteen-day period, if the party residing within the territory of the People's Republic of China has not appealed, they shall not appeal. However, because the appeal period for the other party not residing within the territory of the People's Republic of China has not yet expired, the judgment of the first-instance People's Court in this case shall not yet become legally effective. Only when both appeal periods have expired and neither party has appealed shall the judgment of the first-instance People's Court have legal effect.  
This is the reply.
