# 外商投资电信企业管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了适应电信业对外开放的需要，促进电信业的发展，根据有关外商投资的法律、行政法规和《中华人民共和国电信条例》（以下简称电信条例），制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in order to meet the needs of the opening up of the telecommunications industry to foreign investment, promote the development of the telecommunications industry, and in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations on foreign investment and the Telecommunications Regulations of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Telecommunications Regulations).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外商投资电信企业，是指外国投资者依法在中华人民共和国境内设立的经营电信业务的企业。

**Article 2** Foreign-invested telecommunications enterprises refer to enterprises established within the territory of the People's Republic of China by foreign investors according to law to operate telecommunications services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外商投资电信企业从事电信业务经营活动，除必须遵守本规定外，还必须遵守电信条例和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 3** Foreign-invested telecommunications enterprises engaged in telecommunications business operations must comply with these Provisions, and must also comply with the Telecommunications Regulations and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外商投资电信企业可以经营基础电信业务、增值电信业务，具体业务分类依照电信条例的规定执行。  
外商投资电信企业经营业务的地域范围，由国务院工业和信息化主管部门按照有关规定确定。

**Article 4** Foreign-invested telecommunications enterprises may operate basic telecommunications services and value-added telecommunications services, and the specific classification of services shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Telecommunications Regulations.  
The geographical scope within which foreign-invested telecommunications enterprises may operate services shall be determined by the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外商投资电信企业的注册资本应当符合下列规定：  
（一）经营全国的或者跨省、自治区、直辖市范围的基础电信业务的，其注册资本最低限额为10亿元人民币；经营增值电信业务的，其注册资本最低限额为1000万元人民币；  
（二）经营省、自治区、直辖市范围内的基础电信业务的，其注册资本最低限额为1亿元人民币；经营增值电信业务的，其注册资本最低限额为100万元人民币。

**Article 5** The registered capital of a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) For those operating basic telecommunications services on a nationwide or cross-province, autonomous region, or directly-administered municipality basis, the minimum registered capital shall be RMB 1 billion; for those operating value-added telecommunications services, the minimum registered capital shall be RMB 10 million;  
(2) For those operating basic telecommunications services within a province, autonomous region, or directly-administered municipality, the minimum registered capital shall be RMB 100 million; for those operating value-added telecommunications services, the minimum registered capital shall be RMB 1 million.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营基础电信业务（无线寻呼业务除外）的外商投资电信企业的外方投资者在企业中的出资比例，最终不得超过49%，国家另有规定的除外。  
经营增值电信业务（包括基础电信业务中的无线寻呼业务）的外商投资电信企业的外方投资者在企业中的出资比例，最终不得超过50%，国家另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** The proportion of capital contribution by foreign investors in a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise operating basic telecommunications services (excluding wireless paging services) shall ultimately not exceed 49%, unless otherwise provided by the State.  
The proportion of capital contribution by foreign investors in a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise operating value-added telecommunications services (including wireless paging services in basic telecommunications services) shall ultimately not exceed 50%, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外商投资电信企业经营电信业务，除应当符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)、第五条、第六条规定的条件外，还应当符合电信条例规定的经营基础电信业务或者经营增值电信业务应当具备的条件。

**Article 7** In operating telecommunications services, foreign-invested telecommunications enterprises shall, in addition to meeting the conditions specified in Articles 4, 5, and 6 of these Provisions, also meet the conditions required for operating basic telecommunications services or value-added telecommunications services as stipulated in the Telecommunications Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营基础电信业务的外商投资电信企业的中方主要投资者应当符合下列条件：  
（一）是依法设立的公司；  
（二）有与从事经营活动相适应的资金和专业人员；  
（三）符合国务院工业和信息化主管部门规定的审慎的和特定行业的要求。  
前款所称外商投资电信企业的中方主要投资者，是指在全体中方投资者中出资数额最多且占中方全体投资者出资总额的30%以上的出资者。

**Article 8** The Chinese principal investors in a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise operating basic telecommunications services shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) They are companies established according to law;  
(2) They have funds and professional personnel commensurate with the business activities;  
(3) They meet the prudential and specific industry requirements prescribed by the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council.  
The term "Chinese principal investors" in the preceding paragraph refers to the investors that contribute the largest amount among all Chinese investors and account for more than 30% of the total capital contribution of all Chinese investors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营基础电信业务的外商投资电信企业的外方主要投资者应当符合下列条件：  
（一）具有企业法人资格；  
（二）在注册的国家或者地区取得基础电信业务经营许可证；  
（三）有与从事经营活动相适应的资金和专业人员。  
前款所称外商投资电信企业的外方主要投资者，是指在外方全体投资者中出资数额最多且占全体外方投资者出资总额的30%以上的出资者。

**Article 9** The foreign principal investors in a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise operating basic telecommunications services shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) They have the status of a legal person enterprise;  
(2) They have obtained a basic telecommunications services operation license in the country or region of their registration;  
(3) They have funds and professional personnel commensurate with the business activities.  
The term "foreign principal investors" in the preceding paragraph refers to the investors that contribute the largest amount among all foreign investors and account for more than 30% of the total capital contribution of all foreign investors.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外商投资电信企业，经依法办理市场主体登记后，向国务院工业和信息化主管部门申请电信业务经营许可并报送下列文件：  
（一）投资者情况说明书；  
（二）[本规定第八条](#art-8)、第九条规定的投资者的资格证明或者有关确认文件；  
（三）电信条例规定的经营基础电信业务或者增值电信业务应当具备的其他条件的证明或者确认文件。  
国务院工业和信息化主管部门应当自收到申请之日起对前款规定的有关文件进行审查。属于基础电信业务的，应当在受理申请之日起180日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定；属于增值电信业务的，应当在收到申请之日起60日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发《电信业务经营许可证》；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 10** After completing market entity registration according to law, a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise shall apply to the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council for a telecommunications business operation license and submit the following documents:  
(1) An explanatory statement on investors;  
(2) The qualification certificates or relevant confirmation documents of the investors as specified in Articles 8 and 9 of these Provisions;  
(3) Certificates or confirmation documents for the other conditions required for operating basic telecommunications services or value-added telecommunications services as stipulated in the Telecommunications Regulations.  
The competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council shall, from the date of receipt of the application, examine the relevant documents specified in the preceding paragraph. For basic telecommunications services, the examination shall be completed within 180 days from the date of acceptance of the application, and a decision of approval or disapproval shall be made; for value-added telecommunications services, the examination shall be completed within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, and a decision of approval or disapproval shall be made. If approval is granted, a "Telecommunications Business Operation License" shall be issued; if approval is not granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons stated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外商投资电信企业投资者情况说明书的主要内容包括：投资者的名称和基本情况、各方出资比例、外方投资者对外商投资电信企业的控制情况等。

**Article 11** The main contents of the explanatory statement on investors of a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise include: the names and basic information of the investors, the proportion of capital contribution by each party, and the control of the foreign investors over the foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise, etc.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外商投资电信企业经营跨境电信业务，必须经国务院工业和信息化主管部门批准，并通过国务院工业和信息化主管部门批准设立的国际电信出入口局进行。

**Article 12** A foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise operating cross-border telecommunications services must obtain approval from the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council and shall conduct such services through the international telecommunications gateway bureau established with the approval of the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　违反[本规定第六条](#art-6)规定的，由国务院工业和信息化主管部门责令限期改正，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，吊销《电信业务经营许可证》。

**Article 13** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 6 of these Provisions shall be ordered by the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council to make corrections within a specified time limit, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if corrections are not made within the time limit, the "Telecommunications Business Operation License" shall be revoked.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请设立外商投资电信企业，提供虚假、伪造的资格证明或者确认文件骗取批准的，批准无效，由国务院工业和信息化主管部门处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款，吊销《电信业务经营许可证》。

**Article 14** If an applicant for the establishment of a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise provides false or forged qualification certificates or confirmation documents to obtain approval by fraudulent means, the approval shall be invalid, and the competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 1 million and revoke the "Telecommunications Business Operation License".

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外商投资电信企业经营电信业务，违反电信条例和其他有关法律、行政法规规定的，由有关机关依法给予处罚。

**Article 15** If a foreign-invested telecommunications enterprise violates the provisions of the Telecommunications Regulations and other relevant laws and administrative regulations when operating telecommunications services, the relevant authorities shall impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的公司、企业在内地投资经营电信业务，比照适用本规定。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis to companies and enterprises from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region investing and operating telecommunications services in the mainland.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 17** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
