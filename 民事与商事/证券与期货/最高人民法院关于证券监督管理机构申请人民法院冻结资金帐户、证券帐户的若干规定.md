# 最高人民法院关于证券监督管理机构申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕2号  
**公布日期**：2005-01-18  
**生效日期**：2005-05-01

> 《最高人民法院关于证券监督管理机构申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的若干规定》已于2005年1月18日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1341次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年5月1日起施行。 2005年4月29日 最高人民法院 关于证券监督管理机构申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的若干规定 法释〔2005〕2号 根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国证券法》、《中华人民共和国证券投资基金法》及《期货交易管理暂行条例》等法律法规的有关规定，现就证券监督管理机构依法申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的有关问题规定如下： 第一条 中国证券监督管理委员会及其下属各省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市证券监督管理局作为申请人，在履行证券、期货监督管理职责中，对有证据证明被申请人有转移或者隐匿违法资金、证券或者其他财产迹象，依法向人民法院申请冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条 申请人依法申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户，应当提交申请书，并提供申请冻结的事实依据和法律、法规依据，被申请冻结的资金帐户、证券帐户的情况以及人民法院认为应当提供的其他材料。 第三条 申请人提供的事实依据主要包括下列材料： （一）银行转帐单复印件； （二）财务凭证复印件； （三）举报材料复印件； （四）交易记录复印件； （五）证券监督管理机构认定意见书； （六）其他书证。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 中国证券监督管理委员会及其下属各省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市证券监督管理局作为申请人，在履行证券、期货监督管理职责中，对有证据证明被申请人有转移或者隐匿违法资金、证券或者其他财产迹象，依法向人民法院申请冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where the China Securities Regulatory Commission and its subordinate securities regulatory bureaus in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status, as applicants, when performing the duties of securities and futures supervision and administration, and having evidence proving that the respondent has signs of transferring or concealing illegal funds, securities, or other property, apply to the People's Court according to law for freezing the capital account or securities account, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 申请人依法申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户，应当提交申请书，并提供申请冻结的事实依据和法律、法规依据，被申请冻结的资金帐户、证券帐户的情况以及人民法院认为应当提供的其他材料。

**Article 2** An applicant applying to the People's Court according to law for freezing a capital account or securities account shall submit a written application and provide the factual basis and legal and regulatory basis for the freezing application, information on the capital account or securities account to be frozen, and other materials that the People's Court considers necessary to be provided.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 申请人提供的事实依据主要包括下列材料：  
（一）银行转帐单复印件；  
（二）财务凭证复印件；  
（三）举报材料复印件；  
（四）交易记录复印件；  
（五）证券监督管理机构认定意见书；  
（六）其他书证。

**Article 3** The factual basis provided by the applicant mainly includes the following materials:  
(1) photocopies of bank transfer receipts;  
(2) photocopies of financial vouchers;  
(3) photocopies of reporting materials;  
(4) photocopies of transaction records;  
(5) written opinions of the securities regulatory authority;  
(6) other documentary evidence.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 申请人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的，由申请人或者被申请人所在地的中级人民法院管辖。  
因特殊原因需要指定管辖的，由高级人民法院决定。

**Article 4** An application to the People's Court for freezing a capital account or securities account shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the applicant or the respondent is located.  
If designation of jurisdiction is required due to special reasons, the Higher People's Court shall make a decision.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院收到申请人的冻结申请后，对情况紧急的，应当在48小时内作出冻结或者不予受理的裁定。  
裁定冻结的，应当立即执行。

**Article 5** After receiving an application for freezing from the applicant, if the circumstances are urgent, the People's Court shall make a ruling on freezing or non-acceptance within 48 hours.  
If the ruling is to freeze, it shall be executed immediately.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院应当组成合议庭对冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的申请进行书面审查。有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理：  
（一）超越法定职权；  
（二）明显缺乏事实依据；  
（三）明显违反法定程序；  
（四）适用法律、法规错误；  
（五）其他不宜冻结的情形。

**Article 6** The People's Court shall form a collegial panel to conduct a written review of the application for freezing the capital account or securities account. In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule for non-acceptance:  
(1) exceeding statutory authority;  
(2) manifest lack of factual basis;  
(3) manifestly violating statutory procedures;  
(4) erroneous application of laws or regulations;  
(5) other circumstances where freezing is not appropriate.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 申请人对人民法院不予受理或者被申请人对人民法院冻结的裁定有异议的，可以自收到裁定书之日起七日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请后的十五日内作出决定。  
复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 7** If the applicant disagrees with the People's Court's ruling of non-acceptance, or if the respondent disagrees with the People's Court's ruling of freezing, either party may, within seven days from the date of receipt of the written ruling, apply to the People's Court at the next higher level for reconsideration. The People's Court at the next higher level shall make a decision within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration application.  
The execution of the ruling shall not be suspended during the reconsideration period.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 人民法院冻结资金帐户、证券帐户的期限为三个月。期满未申请继续冻结的，冻结自动解除。  
冻结期满后需要继续冻结的，申请人应当在期满之日的十日前向人民法院提出继续冻结的申请。人民法院应当在期满前作出是否继续冻结的裁定。

**Article 8** The period for the People's Court to freeze a capital account or securities account shall be three months. If no application for extension of the freeze is made upon expiry, the freeze shall be automatically lifted.  
If an extension of the freeze is needed after the expiry of the freeze period, the applicant shall submit an application for extension to the People's Court ten days before the expiry date. The People's Court shall make a ruling on whether to extend the freeze before the expiry date.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 申请冻结资金帐户、证券帐户违法或者不当，给被申请人合法权益造成损失的，由申请人依法承担行政赔偿责任。

**Article 9** If the application for freezing a capital account or securities account is illegal or improper, causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of the respondent, the applicant shall bear administrative compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 本规定自公布之日起实施。

**Article 10** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
