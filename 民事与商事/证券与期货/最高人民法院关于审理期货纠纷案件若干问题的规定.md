# 最高人民法院关于审理期货纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕10号  
**公布日期**：2003-06-18  
**生效日期**：2003-07-01

> （2003年5月16日最高人民法院审判委员会第1270次会议通过　2003年6月18日最高人民法院公告公布　自2003年7月1日起施行） 为了正确审理期货纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》、《中华人民共和国合同法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律、行政法规的规定，结合审判实践经验，对审理期货纠纷案件的若干问题制定本规定。 一、一般规定 第一条　人民法院审理期货纠纷案件，应当依法保护当事人的合法权益，正确确定其应承担的风险责任，并维护期货市场秩序。 第二条　人民法院审理期货合同纠纷案件，应当严格按照当事人在合同中的约定确定违约方承担的责任，当事人的约定违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的除外。 第三条　人民法院审理期货侵权纠纷和无效的期货交易合同纠纷案件，应当根据各方当事人是否有过错，以及过错的性质、大小，过错和损失之间的因果关系，确定过错方承担的民事责任。 二、管　　辖 第四条　人民法院应当依据民事诉讼法第二十四条、第二十五条和第二十九条的规定确定期货纠纷案件的管辖。 第五条　在期货公司的分公司、营业部等分支机构进行期货交易的，该分支机构住所地为合同履行地。 因实物交割发生纠纷的，期货交易所住所地为合同履行地。 第六条　侵权与违约竞合的期货纠纷案件，依当事人选择的诉由确定管辖。当事人既以违约又以侵权起诉的，以当事人起诉状中在先的诉讼请求确定管辖。 第七条　期货纠纷案件由中级人民法院管辖。 高级人民法院根据需要可以确定部分基层人民法院受理期货纠纷案件。 三、承担责任的主体 第八条　期货公司的从业人员在本公司经营范围内从事期货交易行为产生的民事责任，由其所在的期货公司承担。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理期货纠纷案件，应当依法保护当事人的合法权益，正确确定其应承担的风险责任，并维护期货市场秩序。

**Article 1** When hearing futures dispute cases, the People's Court shall protect the lawful rights and interests of the parties according to law, correctly determine the risk liability they shall bear, and maintain the order of the futures market.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院审理期货合同纠纷案件，应当严格按照当事人在合同中的约定确定违约方承担的责任，当事人的约定违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的除外。

**Article 2** When hearing futures contract dispute cases, the People's Court shall strictly determine the liability to be borne by the breaching party according to the agreement of the parties in the contract, unless the agreement of the parties violates the mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院审理期货侵权纠纷和无效的期货交易合同纠纷案件，应当根据各方当事人是否有过错，以及过错的性质、大小，过错和损失之间的因果关系，确定过错方承担的民事责任。  
二、管　　辖

**Article 3** When hearing futures tort dispute cases and void futures transaction contract dispute cases, the People's Court shall determine the civil liability to be borne by the faulty party based on whether each party is at fault, the nature and extent of the fault, and the causal relationship between the fault and the loss.  
II. Jurisdiction

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当依据[民事诉讼法第二十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-24)、第二十五条和第二十九条的规定确定期货纠纷案件的管辖。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall determine the jurisdiction over futures dispute cases pursuant to Articles 24, 25 and 29 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在期货公司的分公司、营业部等分支机构进行期货交易的，该分支机构住所地为合同履行地。  
因实物交割发生纠纷的，期货交易所住所地为合同履行地。

**Article 5** Where futures transactions are conducted at a branch, business department or other branch office of a futures company, the domicile of such branch office shall be the place of performance of the contract.  
Where a dispute arises from physical delivery, the domicile of the futures exchange shall be the place of performance of the contract.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　侵权与违约竞合的期货纠纷案件，依当事人选择的诉由确定管辖。当事人既以违约又以侵权起诉的，以当事人起诉状中在先的诉讼请求确定管辖。

**Article 6** For a futures dispute case where tort and breach of contract concur, jurisdiction shall be determined according to the cause of action chosen by the party. Where a party prosecutes on both grounds of breach of contract and tort, jurisdiction shall be determined by the claim that appears first in the party's complaint.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　期货纠纷案件由中级人民法院管辖。  
高级人民法院根据需要可以确定部分基层人民法院受理期货纠纷案件。  
三、承担责任的主体

**Article 7** Futures dispute cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court.  
The Higher People's Court may, as needed, designate certain Primary People's Courts to accept futures dispute cases.  
III. Subject Bearing Liability

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　期货公司的从业人员在本公司经营范围内从事期货交易行为产生的民事责任，由其所在的期货公司承担。

**Article 8** The civil liability arising from the futures trading activities conducted by an employee of a futures company within the scope of the company's business shall be borne by the futures company with which he or she is employed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　期货公司授权非本公司人员以本公司的名义从事期货交易行为的，期货公司应当承担由此产生的民事责任；非期货公司人员以期货公司名义从事期货交易行为，具备合同法第四十九条所规定的表见代理条件的，期货公司应当承担由此产生的民事责任。

**Article 9** Where a futures company authorizes a person who is not its employee to engage in futures trading activities in the name of the company, the futures company shall bear the civil liability arising therefrom; where a person who is not an employee of a futures company engages in futures trading activities in the name of the futures company, and the conditions for apparent agency as prescribed in Article 49 of the Contract Law are satisfied, the futures company shall bear the civil liability arising therefrom.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公民、法人受期货公司或者客户的委托，作为居间人为其提供订约的机会或者订立期货经纪合同的中介服务的，期货公司或者客户应当按照约定向居间人支付报酬。居间人应当独立承担基于居间经纪关系所产生的民事责任。

**Article 10** Where a citizen or legal person, entrusted by a futures company or a client, acts as an intermediary to provide the opportunity for concluding a contract or intermediary services for concluding a futures brokerage contract, the futures company or the client shall pay remuneration to the intermediary according to the agreement. The intermediary shall independently bear the civil liability arising from the intermediary brokerage relationship.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　不以真实身份从事期货交易的单位或者个人，交易行为符合期货交易所交易规则的，交易结果由其自行承担。

**Article 11** Where an entity or individual engages in futures trading without using their true identity, and the trading activities comply with the trading rules of the futures exchange, the trading results shall be borne by themselves.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　期货公司设立的取得营业执照和经营许可证的分公司、营业部等分支机构超出经营范围开展经营活动所产生的民事责任，该分支机构不能承担的，由期货公司承担。  
客户有过错的，应当承担相应的民事责任。  
四、无效合同责任

**Article 12** Where a branch, business department or other branch office established by a futures company that has obtained a business license and an operating permit carries out business activities beyond its scope of operation, and such branch office cannot bear the civil liability arising therefrom, the futures company shall bear such liability.  
Where the client is at fault, it shall bear the corresponding civil liability.  
IV. Liability for Void Contracts

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，应当认定期货经纪合同无效：  
（一）没有从事期货经纪业务的主体资格而从事期货经纪业务的；  
（二）不具备从事期货交易主体资格的客户从事期货交易的；  
（三）违反法律、法规禁止性规定的。

**Article 13** A futures brokerage contract shall be determined void under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) engaging in futures brokerage business without having the subject qualification for such business;  
(2) a client who does not have the subject qualification for engaging in futures transactions engages in futures trading;  
(3) violating the prohibitive provisions of laws or regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　因期货经纪合同无效给客户造成经济损失的，应当根据无效行为与损失之间的因果关系确定责任的承担。一方的损失系对方行为所致，应当由对方赔偿损失；双方有过错的，根据过错大小各自承担相应的民事责任。

**Article 14** Where economic losses are caused to a client due to a void futures brokerage contract, the liability shall be determined based on the causal relationship between the void act and the loss. If the loss of one party is caused by the other party's act, the other party shall compensate for the loss; if both parties are at fault, each shall bear the corresponding civil liability according to the extent of their respective fault.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　不具有主体资格的经营机构因从事期货经纪业务而导致期货经纪合同无效，该机构按客户的交易指令入市交易的，收取的佣金应当返还给客户，交易结果由客户承担。  
该机构未按客户的交易指令入市交易，客户没有过错的，该机构应当返还客户的保证金并赔偿客户的损失。赔偿损失的范围包括交易手续费、税金及利息。  
五、交易行为责任

**Article 15** Where an operating institution without subject qualification causes a futures brokerage contract to be void due to its engagement in futures brokerage business, and such institution enters the market for trading according to the client's trading instructions, the commissions collected shall be returned to the client, and the trading results shall be borne by the client.  
Where such institution fails to enter the market for trading according to the client's trading instructions, and the client is not at fault, such institution shall return the client's margin and compensate for the client's losses. The scope of compensation for losses includes trading fees, taxes and interest.  
V. Liability for Trading Activities

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　期货公司在与客户订立期货经纪合同时，未提示客户注意《期货交易风险说明书》内容，并由客户签字或者盖章，对于客户在交易中的损失，应当依据合同法第四十二条第（三）项的规定承担相应的赔偿责任。但是，根据以往交易结果记载，证明客户已有交易经历的，应当免除期货公司的责任。

**Article 16** Where, when concluding a futures brokerage contract with a client, a futures company fails to draw the client's attention to the contents of the "Risk Disclosure Statement for Futures Trading" and have the client sign or affix its seal, the futures company shall bear the corresponding compensation liability for the client's losses in trading pursuant to Article 42(3) of the Contract Law. However, if it is proven by the records of previous trading results that the client has had trading experience, the futures company's liability shall be exempted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　期货公司接受客户全权委托进行期货交易的，对交易产生的损失，承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的80%，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 17** Where a futures company accepts a client's full authority to conduct futures trading, it shall bear the primary compensation liability for the losses arising from the trading, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 80% of the losses, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　期货公司与客户签订的期货经纪合同对下达交易指令的方式未作约定或者约定不明确的，期货公司不能证明其所进行的交易是依据客户交易指令进行的，对该交易造成客户的损失，期货公司应当承担赔偿责任，客户予以追认的除外。

**Article 18** Where the futures brokerage contract concluded between a futures company and a client does not stipulate or does not clearly stipulate the method for giving trading instructions, and the futures company cannot prove that the transaction conducted was based on the client's trading instructions, the futures company shall bear the compensation liability for the losses caused to the client by such transaction, unless the client subsequently ratifies it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　期货公司执行非受托人的交易指令造成客户损失，应当由期货公司承担赔偿责任，非受托人承担连带责任，客户予以追认的除外。

**Article 19** Where a futures company executes a trading instruction given by a person other than the authorized person and causes losses to the client, the futures company shall bear the compensation liability, and the person other than the authorized person shall bear joint and several liability, unless the client subsequently ratifies it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　客户下达的交易指令没有品种、数量、买卖方向的，期货公司未予拒绝而进行交易造成客户的损失，由期货公司承担赔偿责任，客户予以追认的除外。

**Article 20** Where a client gives a trading instruction that lacks the variety, quantity, or direction of buying or selling, and the futures company fails to reject it but conducts the transaction, thereby causing losses to the client, the futures company shall bear the compensation liability, unless the client subsequently ratifies it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　客户下达的交易指令数量和买卖方向明确，没有有效期限的，应当视为当日有效；没有成交价格的，应当视为按市价交易；没有开平仓方向的，应当视为开仓交易。

**Article 21** Where a client gives a trading instruction with clear quantity and direction of buying or selling but without a validity period, it shall be deemed valid for the current day; where no transaction price is given, it shall be deemed as a market price transaction; where no opening or closing direction is given, it shall be deemed as an opening transaction.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　期货公司错误执行客户交易指令，除客户认可的以外，交易的后果由期货公司承担，并按下列方式分别处理：  
（一）交易数量发生错误的，多于指令数量的部分由期货公司承担，少于指令数量的部分，由期货公司补足或者赔偿直接损失；  
（二）交易价格超出客户指令价位范围的，交易差价损失或者交易结果由期货公司承担。

**Article 22** Where a futures company erroneously executes a client's trading instruction, unless the client approves, the consequences of the transaction shall be borne by the futures company, and shall be dealt with separately in the following manner:  
(1) if an error occurs in the transaction quantity, the portion exceeding the instructed quantity shall be borne by the futures company, and the portion falling short of the instructed quantity shall be made up by the futures company or the direct losses shall be compensated;  
(2) if the transaction price exceeds the range of the client's instructed price, the transaction price difference loss or the transaction result shall be borne by the futures company.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　期货公司不当延误执行客户交易指令给客户造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任，但由于市场原因致客户交易指令未能全部或者部分成交的，期货公司不承担责任。

**Article 23** Where a futures company improperly delays the execution of a client's trading instruction and causes losses to the client, it shall bear the compensation liability; however, if due to market reasons the client's trading instruction cannot be wholly or partially executed, the futures company shall not bear liability.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　期货公司超出客户指令价位的范围，将高于客户指令价格卖出或者低于客户指令价格买入后的差价利益占为己有的，客户要求期货公司返还的，人民法院应予支持，期货公司与客户另有约定的除外。

**Article 24** Where a futures company, beyond the range of the client's instructed price, appropriates the price difference benefit obtained from selling at a price higher than the client's instructed price or buying at a price lower than the client's instructed price, and the client requests the futures company to return such benefit, the People's Court shall support such request, unless otherwise agreed between the futures company and the client.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　期货交易所未按交易规则规定的期限、方式，将交易或者持仓头寸的结算结果通知期货公司，造成期货公司损失的，由期货交易所承担赔偿责任。  
期货公司未按期货经纪合同约定的期限、方式，将交易或者持仓头寸的结算结果通知客户，造成客户损失的，由期货公司承担赔偿责任。

**Article 25** Where a futures exchange fails to notify a futures company of the settlement results of transactions or open positions within the time limit and in the manner prescribed by the trading rules, thereby causing losses to the futures company, the futures exchange shall bear the compensation liability.  
Where a futures company fails to notify a client of the settlement results of transactions or open positions within the time limit and in the manner stipulated in the futures brokerage contract, thereby causing losses to the client, the futures company shall bear the compensation liability.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　期货公司与客户对交易结算结果的通知方式未作约定或者约定不明确，期货公司未能提供证据证明已经发出上述通知的，对客户因继续持仓而造成扩大的损失，应当承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的80%。

**Article 26** Where a futures company and a client have not agreed or have not clearly agreed on the method of notification of trading settlement results, and the futures company fails to provide evidence proving that the above-mentioned notification has been sent, the futures company shall bear the primary compensation liability for the losses that are expanded due to the client's continued holding of positions, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 80% of the losses.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　客户对当日交易结算结果的确认，应当视为对该日之前所有持仓和交易结算结果的确认，所产生的交易后果由客户自行承担。

**Article 27** A client's confirmation of the settlement results of the current day's transactions shall be deemed as confirmation of all positions and transaction settlement results prior to that day, and the resulting consequences of the transactions shall be borne by the client itself.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　期货公司对交易结算结果提出异议，期货交易所未及时采取措施导致损失扩大的，对造成期货公司扩大的损失应当承担赔偿责任。  
客户对交易结算结果提出异议，期货公司未及时采取措施导致损失扩大的，期货公司对造成客户扩大的损失应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 28** Where a futures company raises an objection to the trading settlement results, and the futures exchange fails to take timely measures, resulting in the expansion of losses, the futures exchange shall bear the compensation liability for the losses expanded to the futures company.  
Where a client raises an objection to the trading settlement results, and the futures company fails to take timely measures, resulting in the expansion of losses, the futures company shall bear the compensation liability for the losses expanded to the client.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　期货公司对期货交易所或者客户对期货公司的交易结算结果有异议，而未在期货交易所交易规则规定或者期货经纪合同约定的时间内提出的，视为期货公司或者客户对交易结算结果已予以确认。

**Article 29** Where a futures company has an objection to the trading settlement results of a futures exchange, or a client has an objection to the trading settlement results of a futures company, but fails to raise it within the time limit prescribed by the trading rules of the futures exchange or stipulated in the futures brokerage contract, it shall be deemed that the futures company or the client has confirmed the trading settlement results.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　期货公司进行混码交易的，客户不承担责任，但期货公司能够举证证明其已按照客户交易指令入市交易的，客户应当承担相应的交易结果。  
六、透支交易责任

**Article 30** Where a futures company conducts mixed-code transactions, the client shall not bear liability; however, if the futures company can adduce evidence to prove that it has entered the market for trading in accordance with the client's trading instructions, the client shall bear the corresponding trading results.  
VI. Liability for Overdraft Transactions

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　期货交易所在期货公司没有保证金或者保证金不足的情况下，允许期货公司开仓交易或者继续持仓，应当认定为透支交易。  
期货公司在客户没有保证金或者保证金不足的情况下，允许客户开仓交易或者继续持仓，应当认定为透支交易。  
审查期货公司或者客户是否透支交易，应当以期货交易所规定的保证金比例为标准。

**Article 31** Where a futures exchange permits a futures company to open a position or continue holding positions when the futures company has no margin or insufficient margin, it shall be determined as an overdraft transaction.  
Where a futures company permits a client to open a position or continue holding positions when the client has no margin or insufficient margin, it shall be determined as an overdraft transaction.  
The examination of whether a futures company or a client engages in an overdraft transaction shall be based on the margin ratio prescribed by the futures exchange.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　期货公司的交易保证金不足，期货交易所未按规定通知期货公司追加保证金的，由于行情向持仓不利的方向变化导致期货公司透支发生的扩大损失，期货交易所应当承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的60%。  
客户的交易保证金不足，期货公司未按约定通知客户追加保证金的，由于行情向持仓不利的方向变化导致客户透支发生的扩大损失，期货公司应当承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的80%。

**Article 32** Where the trading margin of a futures company is insufficient, and the futures exchange fails to notify the futures company to increase the margin according to the regulations, resulting in expanded losses caused by the futures company's overdraft due to market movements unfavorable to the positions, the futures exchange shall bear the primary compensation liability, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 60% of the losses.  
Where the trading margin of a client is insufficient, and the futures company fails to notify the client to increase the margin according to the agreement, resulting in expanded losses caused by the client's overdraft due to market movements unfavorable to the positions, the futures company shall bear the primary compensation liability, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 80% of the losses.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　期货公司的交易保证金不足，期货交易所履行了通知义务，而期货公司未及时追加保证金，期货公司要求保留持仓并经书面协商一致的，对保留持仓期间造成的损失，由期货公司承担；穿仓造成的损失，由期货交易所承担。  
客户的交易保证金不足，期货公司履行了通知义务而客户未及时追加保证金，客户要求保留持仓并经书面协商一致的，对保留持仓期间造成的损失，由客户承担；穿仓造成的损失，由期货公司承担。

**Article 33** Where the trading margin of a futures company is insufficient, the futures exchange has performed its obligation to notify, but the futures company fails to increase the margin in time, and the futures company requests to retain the positions and the parties reach a written agreement, the losses incurred during the period of retaining the positions shall be borne by the futures company; the losses caused by a blowout shall be borne by the futures exchange.  
Where the trading margin of a client is insufficient, the futures company has performed its obligation to notify, but the client fails to increase the margin in time, and the client requests to retain the positions and the parties reach a written agreement, the losses incurred during the period of retaining the positions shall be borne by the client; the losses caused by a blowout shall be borne by the futures company.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　期货交易所允许期货公司开仓透支交易的，对透支交易造成的损失，由期货交易所承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的60%。  
期货公司允许客户开仓透支交易的，对透支交易造成的损失，由期货公司承担主要赔偿责任，赔偿额不超过损失的80%。

**Article 34** Where a futures exchange permits a futures company to open a position through an overdraft transaction, the futures exchange shall bear the primary compensation liability for the losses caused by the overdraft transaction, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 60% of the losses.  
Where a futures company permits a client to open a position through an overdraft transaction, the futures company shall bear the primary compensation liability for the losses caused by the overdraft transaction, with the amount of compensation not exceeding 80% of the losses.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　期货交易所允许期货公司透支交易，并与其约定分享利益，共担风险的，对透支交易造成的损失，期货交易所承担相应的赔偿责任。  
期货公司允许客户透支交易，并与其约定分享利益，共担风险的，对透支交易造成的损失，期货公司承担相应的赔偿责任。  
七、强行平仓责任

**Article 35** Where a futures exchange permits a futures company to engage in overdraft transactions and agrees with it to share profits and jointly bear risks, the futures exchange shall bear the corresponding compensation liability for the losses caused by the overdraft transactions.  
Where a futures company permits a client to engage in overdraft transactions and agrees with it to share profits and jointly bear risks, the futures company shall bear the corresponding compensation liability for the losses caused by the overdraft transactions.  
VII. Liability for Forced Liquidation

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　期货公司的交易保证金不足，又未能按期货交易所规定的时间追加保证金的，按交易规则的规定处理；规定不明确的，期货交易所有权就其未平仓的期货合约强行平仓，强行平仓所造成的损失，由期货公司承担。  
客户的交易保证金不足，又未能按期货经纪合同约定的时间追加保证金的，按期货经纪合同的约定处理；约定不明确的，期货公司有权就其未平仓的期货合约强行平仓，强行平仓造成的损失，由客户承担。

**Article 36** Where the trading margin of a futures company is insufficient and it fails to increase the margin within the time limit prescribed by the futures exchange, the matter shall be dealt with according to the provisions of the trading rules; if the provisions are not clear, the futures exchange shall have the right to forcibly liquidate the futures company's open futures contracts, and the losses caused by the forced liquidation shall be borne by the futures company.  
Where the trading margin of a client is insufficient and it fails to increase the margin within the time limit stipulated in the futures brokerage contract, the matter shall be dealt with according to the stipulations of the futures brokerage contract; if the stipulations are not clear, the futures company shall have the right to forcibly liquidate the client's open futures contracts, and the losses caused by the forced liquidation shall be borne by the client.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　期货交易所因期货公司违规超仓或者其他违规行为而必须强行平仓的，强行平仓所造成的损失，由期货公司承担。  
期货公司因客户违规超仓或者其他违规行为而必须强行平仓的，强行平仓所造成的损失，由客户承担。

**Article 37** Where a futures exchange must forcibly liquidate positions due to a futures company's violation of position limits or other violations, the losses caused by the forced liquidation shall be borne by the futures company.  
Where a futures company must forcibly liquidate positions due to a client's violation of position limits or other violations, the losses caused by the forced liquidation shall be borne by the client.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　期货公司或者客户交易保证金不足，符合强行平仓条件后，应当自行平仓而未平仓造成的扩大损失，由期货公司或者客户自行承担。法律、行政法规另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 38** Where the trading margin of a futures company or a client is insufficient and the conditions for forced liquidation are satisfied, the expanded losses caused by failure to voluntarily liquidate positions when they should have done so shall be borne by the futures company or the client itself, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations or otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　期货交易所或者期货公司强行平仓数额应当与期货公司或者客户需追加的保证金数额基本相当。因超量平仓引起的损失，由强行平仓者承担。

**Article 39** The amount of forced liquidation by a futures exchange or a futures company shall be basically equivalent to the amount of margin required to be increased by the futures company or the client. Losses caused by excessive liquidation shall be borne by the party that conducted the forced liquidation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　期货交易所对期货公司、期货公司对客户未按期货交易所交易规则规定或者期货经纪合同约定的强行平仓条件、时间、方式进行强行平仓，造成期货公司或者客户损失的，期货交易所或者期货公司应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 40** Where a futures exchange conducts forced liquidation against a futures company, or a futures company conducts forced liquidation against a client, not in accordance with the conditions, time, and methods for forced liquidation as prescribed by the trading rules of the futures exchange or stipulated in the futures brokerage contract, thereby causing losses to the futures company or the client, the futures exchange or the futures company shall bear the compensation liability.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　期货交易所依法或依交易规则强行平仓发生的费用，由被平仓的期货公司承担；期货公司承担责任后有权向有过错的客户追偿。  
期货公司依法或依约定强行平仓所发生的费用，由客户承担。  
八、实物交割责任

**Article 41** The expenses incurred by a futures exchange in conducting forced liquidation according to law or pursuant to trading rules shall be borne by the futures company whose positions were liquidated; after bearing such liability, the futures company shall have the right to seek recourse against the client at fault.  
The expenses incurred by a futures company in conducting forced liquidation according to law or pursuant to agreement shall be borne by the client.  
VIII. Liability for Physical Delivery

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　交割仓库未履行货物验收职责或者因保管不善给仓单持有人造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 42** Where a delivery warehouse fails to perform its duty of inspecting the goods or causes losses to the holder of a warehouse receipt due to improper storage, it shall bear the compensation liability.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　期货公司没有代客户履行申请交割义务的，应当承担违约责任；造成客户损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Where a futures company fails to perform the obligation of applying for delivery on behalf of the client, it shall bear the liability for breach of contract; if it causes losses to the client, it shall bear the compensation liability.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在交割日，卖方期货公司未向期货交易所交付标准仓单，或者买方期货公司未向期货交易所账户交付足额货款，构成交割违约。  
构成交割违约的，违约方应当承担违约责任；具有合同法第九十四条第（四）项规定情形的，对方有权要求终止交割或者要求违约方继续交割。  
征购或者竞卖失败的，应当由违约方按照交易所有关赔偿办法的规定承担赔偿责任。

**Article 44** On the delivery date, if the selling futures company fails to deliver standard warehouse receipts to the futures exchange, or the buying futures company fails to deliver sufficient payment to the futures exchange account, a delivery default is constituted.  
Where a delivery default is constituted, the defaulting party shall bear the liability for breach of contract; if the circumstances provided in Article 94(4) of the Contract Law exist, the other party shall have the right to demand termination of the delivery or demand that the defaulting party continue the delivery.  
If the purchase or competitive sale fails, the defaulting party shall bear the compensation liability in accordance with the relevant provisions on compensation of the exchange.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在期货合约交割期内，买方或者卖方客户违约的，期货交易所应当代期货公司、期货公司应当代客户向对方承担违约责任。

**Article 45** During the delivery period of a futures contract, if the buyer or seller client defaults, the futures exchange shall assume the liability for breach of contract on behalf of the futures company, and the futures company shall assume such liability on behalf of the client to the other party.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　买方客户未在期货交易所交易规则规定的期限内对货物的质量、数量提出异议的，应视为其对货物的数量、质量无异议。

**Article 46** If a buyer client fails to raise an objection to the quality or quantity of the goods within the time limit prescribed by the trading rules of the futures exchange, it shall be deemed that it has no objection to the quantity or quality of the goods.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　交割仓库不能在期货交易所交易规则规定的期限内，向标准仓单持有人交付符合期货合约要求的货物，造成标准仓单持有人损失的，交割仓库应当承担责任，期货交易所承担连带责任。  
期货交易所承担责任后，有权向交割仓库追偿。  
九、保证合约履行责任

**Article 47** Where a delivery warehouse fails to deliver goods that meet the requirements of the futures contract to the holder of a standard warehouse receipt within the time limit prescribed by the trading rules of the futures exchange, thereby causing losses to the holder of the standard warehouse receipt, the delivery warehouse shall bear liability, and the futures exchange shall bear joint and several liability.  
After the futures exchange bears liability, it shall have the right to seek recourse against the delivery warehouse.  
IX. Liability for Guaranteeing Contract Performance

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　期货公司未按照每日无负债结算制度的要求，履行相应的金钱给付义务，期货交易所亦未代期货公司履行，造成交易对方损失的，期货交易所应当承担赔偿责任。  
期货交易所代期货公司履行义务或者承担赔偿责任后，有权向不履行义务的一方追偿。

**Article 48** Where a futures company fails to perform the corresponding monetary payment obligation in accordance with the requirements of the daily mark-to-market settlement system, and the futures exchange also fails to perform on behalf of the futures company, causing losses to the counterparty, the futures exchange shall bear the compensation liability.  
After the futures exchange performs the obligation or bears the compensation liability on behalf of the futures company, it shall have the right to seek recourse against the party that failed to perform the obligation.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　期货交易所未代期货公司履行期货合约，期货公司应当根据客户请求向期货交易所主张权利。  
期货公司拒绝代客户向期货交易所主张权利的，客户可直接起诉期货交易所，期货公司可作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 49** Where a futures exchange fails to perform the futures contract on behalf of the futures company, the futures company shall assert the rights against the futures exchange at the request of the client.  
Where the futures company refuses to assert the rights against the futures exchange on behalf of the client, the client may directly prosecute the futures exchange, and the futures company may participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　因期货交易所的过错导致信息发布、交易指令处理错误，造成期货公司或者客户直接经济损失的，期货交易所应当承担赔偿责任，但其能够证明系不可抗力的除外。

**Article 50** Where, due to the fault of a futures exchange, errors occur in information release or trading instruction processing, causing direct economic losses to a futures company or a client, the futures exchange shall bear the compensation liability, unless it can prove that such losses are caused by force majeure.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　期货交易所依据有关规定对期货市场出现的异常情况采取合理的紧急措施造成客户损失的，期货交易所不承担赔偿责任。  
期货公司执行期货交易所的合理的紧急措施造成客户损失的，期货公司不承担赔偿责任。  
十、侵权行为责任

**Article 51** Where a futures exchange takes reasonable emergency measures against abnormal situations occurring in the futures market according to relevant provisions and causes losses to a client, the futures exchange shall not bear compensation liability.  
Where a futures company executes the reasonable emergency measures of a futures exchange and causes losses to a client, the futures company shall not bear compensation liability.  
X. Tort Liability

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　期货交易所、期货公司故意提供虚假信息误导客户下单的，由此造成客户的经济损失由期货交易所、期货公司承担。

**Article 52** Where a futures exchange or a futures company intentionally provides false information to mislead a client into placing orders, the economic losses thus caused to the client shall be borne by the futures exchange or the futures company.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　期货公司私下对冲、与客户对赌等不将客户指令入市交易的行为，应当认定为无效，期货公司应当赔偿由此给客户造成的经济损失；期货公司与客户均有过错的，应当根据过错大小，分别承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 53** Acts such as a futures company conducting off-exchange offset or betting against a client, or failing to enter a client's instruction into market trading, shall be deemed void, and the futures company shall compensate the economic losses thus caused to the client; if both the futures company and the client are at fault, they shall, according to the degree of fault, respectively bear corresponding compensation liability.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　期货公司擅自以客户的名义进行交易，客户对交易结果不予追认的，所造成的损失由期货公司承担。

**Article 54** Where a futures company trades in the name of a client without authorization, and the client does not ratify the trading results, the losses thus caused shall be borne by the futures company.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　期货公司挪用客户保证金，或者违反有关规定划转客户保证金造成客户损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。  
十一、举证责任

**Article 55** Where a futures company misappropriates a client's margin, or transfers a client's margin in violation of relevant provisions and causes losses to the client, it shall bear compensation liability.  
XI. Burden of Adducing Evidence

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　期货公司应当对客户的交易指令是否入市交易承担举证责任。  
确认期货公司是否将客户下达的交易指令入市交易，应当以期货交易所的交易记录、期货公司通知的交易结算结果与客户交易指令记录中的品种、买卖方向是否一致，价格、交易时间是否相符为标准，指令交易数量可以作为参考。但客户有相反证据证明其交易指令未入市交易的除外。

**Article 56** A futures company shall bear the burden of adducing evidence as to whether a client's trading instruction has been entered into market trading.  
To determine whether a futures company has entered a client's trading instruction into market trading, the standard shall be whether the variety and direction of trading in the futures exchange's trading records and the settlement results notified by the futures company are consistent with those in the client's trading instruction records, and whether the price and trading time match; the quantity of instruction trades may serve as reference, except where the client has evidence to the contrary proving that its trading instruction was not entered into market trading.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　期货交易所通知期货公司追加保证金，期货公司否认收到上述通知的，由期货交易所承担举证责任。  
期货公司向客户发出追加保证金的通知，客户否认收到上述通知的，由期货公司承担举证责任。  
十二、保全和执行

**Article 57** Where a futures exchange notifies a futures company to increase margin, and the futures company denies receipt of the said notice, the futures exchange shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
Where a futures company issues a notice to a client to increase margin, and the client denies receipt of the said notice, the futures company shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
XII. Preservation and Enforcement

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　人民法院保全与会员资格相应的会员资格费或者交易席位，应当依法裁定不得转让该会员资格，但不得停止该会员交易席位的使用。人民法院在执行过程中，有权依法采取强制措施转让该交易席位。

**Article 58** Where a People's Court preserves the membership qualification fee or trading seat corresponding to the membership qualification, it shall, according to law, rule that the membership qualification shall not be transferred, but shall not stop the use of the member's trading seat. During enforcement, the People's Court shall have the right to take compulsory measures according to law to transfer the trading seat.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　期货交易所、期货公司为债务人的，人民法院不得冻结、划拨期货公司在期货交易所或者客户在期货公司保证金账户中的资金。  
有证据证明该保证金账户中有超出期货公司、客户权益资金的部分，期货交易所、期货公司在人民法院指定的合理期限内不能提出相反证据的，人民法院可以依法冻结、划拨该账户中属于期货交易所、期货公司的自有资金。

**Article 59** Where a futures exchange or a futures company is a debtor, a People's Court shall not freeze or transfer the funds in the margin account of a futures company at a futures exchange or of a client at a futures company.  
Where there is evidence proving that there is a part of the margin account exceeding the rights and interests of the futures company or client, and the futures exchange or futures company fails to provide contrary evidence within a reasonable period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court may, according to law, freeze or transfer the self-owned funds in such account belonging to the futures exchange or futures company.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　期货公司为债务人的，人民法院不得冻结、划拨专用结算账户中未被期货合约占用的用于担保期货合约履行的最低限额的结算准备金；期货公司已经结清所有持仓并清偿客户资金的，人民法院可以对结算准备金依法予以冻结、划拨。  
期货公司有其他财产的，人民法院应当依法先行冻结、查封、执行期货公司的其他财产。

**Article 60** Where a futures company is a debtor, a People's Court shall not freeze or transfer the minimum amount of settlement reserves in the special settlement account that are not occupied by futures contracts and are used to guarantee the performance of futures contracts; if the futures company has closed out all positions and settled client funds, the People's Court may, according to law, freeze or transfer the settlement reserves.  
Where the futures company has other property, the People's Court shall, according to law, first freeze, seize, or enforce the other property of the futures company.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　客户、自营会员为债务人的，人民法院可以对其保证金、持仓依法采取保全和执行措施。  
十三、其　　他

**Article 61** Where a client or a self-operating member is a debtor, a People's Court may take preservation and enforcement measures against its margin and positions according to law.  
XIII. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本规定所称期货公司是指经依法批准代理投资者从事期货交易业务的经营机构及其分公司、营业部等分支机构。客户是指委托期货公司从事期货交易的投资者。

**Article 62** For the purposes of these Provisions, 'futures company' means a business institution approved according to law to act as an agent for investors in engaging in futures trading business, and its branches, such as branch companies and business departments. 'Client' means an investor that entrusts a futures company to engage in futures trading.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本规定自2003年7月1日起施行。  
2003年7月1日前发生的期货交易行为或者侵权行为，适用当时的有关规定；当时规定不明确的，参照本规定处理。

**Article 63** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 2003.  
For futures trading acts or tortious acts occurring before July 1, 2003, the relevant provisions then in force shall apply; where the provisions then in force are unclear, these Provisions shall be referred to for handling.
