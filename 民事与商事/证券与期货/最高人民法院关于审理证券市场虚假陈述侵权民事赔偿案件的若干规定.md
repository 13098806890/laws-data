# 最高人民法院关于审理证券市场虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕2号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-30  
**生效日期**：2022-01-22

> 《最高人民法院关于审理证券市场虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件的若干规定》已于2021年12月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1860次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月22日起施行。 2022年1月21日 最高人民法院 关于审理证券市场虚假陈述 侵权民事赔偿案件的若干规定 法释〔2022〕2号 为正确审理证券市场虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件，规范证券发行和交易行为，保护投资者合法权益，维护公开、公平、公正的证券市场秩序，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国证券法》《中华人民共和国公司法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 一、一般规定 第一条　信息披露义务人在证券交易场所发行、交易证券过程中实施虚假陈述引发的侵权民事赔偿案件，适用本规定。 按照国务院规定设立的区域性股权市场中发生的虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件，可以参照适用本规定。 第二条　原告提起证券虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿诉讼，符合民事诉讼法第一百二十二条规定，并提交以下证据或者证明材料的，人民法院应当受理： （一）证明原告身份的相关文件； （二）信息披露义务人实施虚假陈述的相关证据； （三）原告因虚假陈述进行交易的凭证及投资损失等相关证据。 人民法院不得仅以虚假陈述未经监管部门行政处罚或者人民法院生效刑事判决的认定为由裁定不予受理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　信息披露义务人在证券交易场所发行、交易证券过程中实施虚假陈述引发的侵权民事赔偿案件，适用本规定。  
按照国务院规定设立的区域性股权市场中发生的虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件，可以参照适用本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to tortious civil compensation cases arising from false statements made by information disclosure obligors in the course of issuing or trading securities on a securities trading venue.  
For cases of tortious civil compensation for false statements occurring in a regional equity market established in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, these Provisions may be applied by reference.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　原告提起证券虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿诉讼，符合[民事诉讼法第一百二十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-122)规定，并提交以下证据或者证明材料的，人民法院应当受理：  
（一）证明原告身份的相关文件；  
（二）信息披露义务人实施虚假陈述的相关证据；  
（三）原告因虚假陈述进行交易的凭证及投资损失等相关证据。  
人民法院不得仅以虚假陈述未经监管部门行政处罚或者人民法院生效刑事判决的认定为由裁定不予受理。

**Article 2** Where a plaintiff institutes a civil compensation action for securities false statement torts and meets the requirements of Article 122 of the Civil Procedure Law and submits the following evidence or evidentiary materials, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) relevant documents proving the identity of the plaintiff;  
(2) relevant evidence that the information disclosure obligor made a false statement;  
(3) relevant evidence such as the trading vouchers and investment losses of the plaintiff resulting from the false statement.  
The People's Court shall not rule to dismiss the case solely on the ground that the false statement has not been subjected to administrative penalty by the regulatory authority or found guilty by an effective criminal judgment of a People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　证券虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件，由发行人住所地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在的市、计划单列市和经济特区中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。《最高人民法院关于证券纠纷代表人诉讼若干问题的规定》等对管辖另有规定的，从其规定。  
省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本辖区的实际情况，确定管辖第一审证券虚假陈述侵权民事赔偿案件的其他中级人民法院，报最高人民法院备案。  
二、虚假陈述的认定

**Article 3** Cases of civil compensation for securities false statement torts shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court or the special People's Court of the city where the provincial, autonomous regional, or directly-administered municipal people's government is located, the city under separate state planning, or the special economic zone where the issuer is domiciled. Where the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning Representative Actions in Securities Disputes and other regulations provide otherwise on jurisdiction, such provisions shall prevail.  
The Higher People's Court of a province, autonomous region, or directly-administered municipality may, based on the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction, designate other Intermediate People's Courts to have jurisdiction over first-instance civil compensation cases for securities false statement torts, and shall report such designations to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.  
II. Determination of False Statements

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　信息披露义务人违反法律、行政法规、监管部门制定的规章和规范性文件关于信息披露的规定，在披露的信息中存在虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，人民法院应当认定为虚假陈述。  
虚假记载，是指信息披露义务人披露的信息中对相关财务数据进行重大不实记载，或者对其他重要信息作出与真实情况不符的描述。  
误导性陈述，是指信息披露义务人披露的信息隐瞒了与之相关的部分重要事实，或者未及时披露相关更正、确认信息，致使已经披露的信息因不完整、不准确而具有误导性。  
重大遗漏，是指信息披露义务人违反关于信息披露的规定，对重大事件或者重要事项等应当披露的信息未予披露。

**Article 4** Where an information disclosure obligor violates the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the rules and normative documents formulated by the regulatory authority concerning information disclosure, and the disclosed information contains false records, misleading statements, or material omissions, the People's Court shall determine that a false statement exists.  
"False record" means that the information disclosed by the information disclosure obligor contains material untrue records of relevant financial data, or describes other important information in a manner inconsistent with the true circumstances.  
"Misleading statement" means that the information disclosed by the information disclosure obligor conceals some relevant important facts, or fails to disclose relevant corrections or confirmations in a timely manner, thereby making the already disclosed information misleading due to incompleteness or inaccuracy.  
"Material omission" means that the information disclosure obligor, in violation of the provisions on information disclosure, fails to disclose information that should have been disclosed, such as material events or important matters.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[证券法第八十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-85)规定的“未按照规定披露信息”，是指信息披露义务人未按照规定的期限、方式等要求及时、公平披露信息。  
信息披露义务人“未按照规定披露信息”构成虚假陈述的，依照本规定承担民事责任；构成内幕交易的，依照[证券法第五十三条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-53)的规定承担民事责任；构成[公司法第一百五十二条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-152)规定的损害股东利益行为的，依照该法承担民事责任。

**Article 5** "Failure to disclose information in accordance with the provisions" as referred to in Article 85 of the Securities Law means that the information disclosure obligor fails to disclose information in a timely and fair manner in accordance with the required time limits, methods, and other requirements.  
Where the information disclosure obligor's "failure to disclose information in accordance with the provisions" constitutes a false statement, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with these Provisions; where it constitutes insider trading, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with Article 53 of the Securities Law; where it constitutes an act harming shareholders' interests as provided in Article 152 of the Company Law, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with that Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　原告以信息披露文件中的盈利预测、发展规划等预测性信息与实际经营情况存在重大差异为由主张发行人实施虚假陈述的，人民法院不予支持，但有下列情形之一的除外：  
（一）信息披露文件未对影响该预测实现的重要因素进行充分风险提示的；  
（二）预测性信息所依据的基本假设、选用的会计政策等编制基础明显不合理的；  
（三）预测性信息所依据的前提发生重大变化时，未及时履行更正义务的。  
前款所称的重大差异，可以参照监管部门和证券交易场所的有关规定认定。

**Article 6** Where a plaintiff claims that the issuer made a false statement on the ground that there is a material difference between the predictive information such as profit forecasts, development plans, etc. in the information disclosure documents and the actual operating situation, the People's Court shall not support such claim, except in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the information disclosure documents fail to provide sufficient risk warnings regarding the important factors affecting the realization of such predictions;  
(2) the basic assumptions underlying the predictive information, the accounting policies selected, and other preparation bases are clearly unreasonable;  
(3) when a material change occurs in the premises on which the predictive information is based, the obligation to make corrections is not fulfilled in a timely manner.  
The "material difference" referred to in the preceding paragraph may be determined by reference to the relevant provisions of the regulatory authority and the securities trading venue.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　虚假陈述实施日，是指信息披露义务人作出虚假陈述或者发生虚假陈述之日。  
信息披露义务人在证券交易场所的网站或者符合监管部门规定条件的媒体上公告发布具有虚假陈述内容的信息披露文件，以披露日为实施日；通过召开业绩说明会、接受新闻媒体采访等方式实施虚假陈述的，以该虚假陈述的内容在具有全国性影响的媒体上首次公布之日为实施日。信息披露文件或者相关报导内容在交易日收市后发布的，以其后的第一个交易日为实施日。  
因未及时披露相关更正、确认信息构成误导性陈述，或者未及时披露重大事件或者重要事项等构成重大遗漏的，以应当披露相关信息期限届满后的第一个交易日为实施日。

**Article 7** The date of making a false statement means the date on which the information disclosure obligor makes the false statement or the date on which the false statement occurs.  
Where an information disclosure obligor announces and publishes information disclosure documents containing false statements on the website of a securities trading venue or in media that meet the conditions prescribed by the regulatory authority, the date of disclosure shall be the date of making; where a false statement is made by means of holding performance briefings, accepting interviews with news media, etc., the date on which the content of such false statement is first disclosed in media with nationwide influence shall be the date of making. Where the information disclosure documents or relevant reports are published after the close of a trading day, the first trading day thereafter shall be the date of making.  
Where a misleading statement is constituted by failing to disclose relevant corrections or confirmations in a timely manner, or a material omission is constituted by failing to disclose material events or important matters in a timely manner, the first trading day after the expiration of the period for disclosing the relevant information shall be the date of making.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　虚假陈述揭露日，是指虚假陈述在具有全国性影响的报刊、电台、电视台或监管部门网站、交易场所网站、主要门户网站、行业知名的自媒体等媒体上，首次被公开揭露并为证券市场知悉之日。  
人民法院应当根据公开交易市场对相关信息的反应等证据，判断投资者是否知悉了虚假陈述。  
除当事人有相反证据足以反驳外，下列日期应当认定为揭露日：  
（一）监管部门以涉嫌信息披露违法为由对信息披露义务人立案调查的信息公开之日；  
（二）证券交易场所等自律管理组织因虚假陈述对信息披露义务人等责任主体采取自律管理措施的信息公布之日。  
信息披露义务人实施的虚假陈述呈连续状态的，以首次被公开揭露并为证券市场知悉之日为揭露日。信息披露义务人实施多个相互独立的虚假陈述的，人民法院应当分别认定其揭露日。

**Article 8** The date of revelation of a false statement means the date on which the false statement is first publicly revealed and becomes known to the securities market in media such as newspapers, radio stations, television stations with nationwide influence, or the websites of regulatory authorities, trading venue websites, major portals, or well-known self-media in the industry.  
The People's Court shall determine whether investors have become aware of the false statement based on evidence such as the reaction of the public trading market to the relevant information.  
Unless the parties have contrary evidence sufficient to rebut, the following dates shall be determined as the date of revelation:  
(1) the date on which information about the regulatory authority placing the information disclosure obligor under investigation for alleged illegal information disclosure is made public;  
(2) the date on which information about self-regulatory management measures taken by securities trading venues or other self-regulatory organizations against the information disclosure obligor or other responsible parties due to false statements is made public.  
Where the false statement made by the information disclosure obligor is of a continuous nature, the date on which it is first publicly revealed and becomes known to the securities market shall be the date of revelation. Where the information disclosure obligor makes multiple independent false statements, the People's Court shall determine the date of revelation for each separately.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　虚假陈述更正日，是指信息披露义务人在证券交易场所网站或者符合监管部门规定条件的媒体上，自行更正虚假陈述之日。  
三、重大性及交易因果关系

**Article 9** The date of correction of a false statement means the date on which the information disclosure obligor voluntarily corrects the false statement on the website of a securities trading venue or in media that meet the conditions prescribed by the regulatory authority.  
III. Materiality and Transactional Causation

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定虚假陈述的内容具有重大性：  
（一）虚假陈述的内容属于[证券法第八十条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-80)第二款、第八十一条第二款规定的重大事件；  
（二）虚假陈述的内容属于监管部门制定的规章和规范性文件中要求披露的重大事件或者重要事项；  
（三）虚假陈述的实施、揭露或者更正导致相关证券的交易价格或者交易量产生明显的变化。  
前款第一项、第二项所列情形，被告提交证据足以证明虚假陈述并未导致相关证券交易价格或者交易量明显变化的，人民法院应当认定虚假陈述的内容不具有重大性。  
被告能够证明虚假陈述不具有重大性，并以此抗辩不应当承担民事责任的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 10** Under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that the content of the false statement is material:  
(1) the content of the false statement pertains to a material event as specified in Article 80, paragraph 2, or Article 81, paragraph 2 of the Securities Law;  
(2) the content of the false statement pertains to a material event or important matter required to be disclosed in the rules and normative documents formulated by the regulatory authority;  
(3) the making, revelation, or correction of the false statement causes a significant change in the trading price or trading volume of the relevant securities.  
Where, in the circumstances specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, the defendant submits evidence sufficient to prove that the false statement did not cause a significant change in the trading price or trading volume of the relevant securities, the People's Court shall determine that the content of the false statement is not material.  
Where the defendant can prove that the false statement is not material and defends on that ground that it shall not bear civil liability, the People's Court shall support such defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　原告能够证明下列情形的，人民法院应当认定原告的投资决定与虚假陈述之间的交易因果关系成立：  
（一）信息披露义务人实施了虚假陈述；  
（二）原告交易的是与虚假陈述直接关联的证券；  
（三）原告在虚假陈述实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之前实施了相应的交易行为，即在诱多型虚假陈述中买入了相关证券，或者在诱空型虚假陈述中卖出了相关证券。

**Article 11** Where the plaintiff can prove the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that the transactional causation between the plaintiff's investment decision and the false statement is established:  
(1) the information disclosure obligor made a false statement;  
(2) the securities traded by the plaintiff are directly related to the false statement;  
(3) the plaintiff carried out the corresponding trading behavior after the date of making the false statement and before the date of revelation or correction, i.e., purchased the relevant securities in a bullish false statement, or sold the relevant securities in a bearish false statement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被告能够证明下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定交易因果关系不成立：  
（一）原告的交易行为发生在虚假陈述实施前，或者是在揭露或更正之后；  
（二）原告在交易时知道或者应当知道存在虚假陈述，或者虚假陈述已经被证券市场广泛知悉；  
（三）原告的交易行为是受到虚假陈述实施后发生的上市公司的收购、重大资产重组等其他重大事件的影响；  
（四）原告的交易行为构成内幕交易、操纵证券市场等证券违法行为的；  
（五）原告的交易行为与虚假陈述不具有交易因果关系的其他情形。  
四、过错认定

**Article 12** Where the defendant can prove any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that the transactional causation is not established:  
(1) the plaintiff's trading behavior occurred before the making of the false statement, or after its revelation or correction;  
(2) at the time of trading, the plaintiff knew or should have known of the existence of the false statement, or the false statement had already become widely known in the securities market;  
(3) the plaintiff's trading behavior was influenced by other material events occurring after the false statement, such as the acquisition of the listed company, material asset restructuring, etc.;  
(4) the plaintiff's trading behavior constitutes insider trading, market manipulation, or other securities illegal acts;  
(5) other circumstances where the plaintiff's trading behavior has no transactional causation with the false statement.  
IV. Determination of Fault

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[证券法第八十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-85)、第一百六十三条所称的过错，包括以下两种情形：  
（一）行为人故意制作、出具存在虚假陈述的信息披露文件，或者明知信息披露文件存在虚假陈述而不予指明、予以发布；  
（二）行为人严重违反注意义务，对信息披露文件中虚假陈述的形成或者发布存在过失。

**Article 13** The term "fault" as used in Articles 85 and 163 of the Securities Law includes the following two circumstances:  
(1) the actor intentionally prepares or issues information disclosure documents containing false statements, or knowingly fails to point out or publishes information disclosure documents with false statements;  
(2) the actor seriously violates the duty of care and is negligent in the formation or publication of false statements in information disclosure documents.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发行人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员主张对虚假陈述没有过错的，人民法院应当根据其工作岗位和职责、在信息披露资料的形成和发布等活动中所起的作用、取得和了解相关信息的渠道、为核验相关信息所采取的措施等实际情况进行审查认定。  
前款所列人员不能提供勤勉尽责的相应证据，仅以其不从事日常经营管理、无相关职业背景和专业知识、相信发行人或者管理层提供的资料、相信证券服务机构出具的专业意见等理由主张其没有过错的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 14** Where directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other persons directly responsible of the issuer claim that they are not at fault for the false statement, the People's Court shall review and determine based on the actual circumstances, including their positions and duties, their role in the formation and publication of the information disclosure materials, the channels through which they obtained and became aware of relevant information, and the measures they took to verify the relevant information.  
Where the persons listed in the preceding paragraph fail to provide corresponding evidence of diligence and merely claim that they have no fault on grounds such as that they are not engaged in daily operation and management, lack relevant professional background and expertise, trust the materials provided by the issuer or management, or trust the professional opinions issued by securities service institutions, the People's Court shall not support such claims.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发行人的董事、监事、高级管理人员依照[证券法第八十二条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-82)第四款的规定，以书面方式发表附具体理由的意见并依法披露的，人民法院可以认定其主观上没有过错，但在审议、审核信息披露文件时投赞成票的除外。

**Article 15** Where directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the issuer, in accordance with Article 82, paragraph 4 of the Securities Law, express opinions with specific reasons in writing and disclose them according to law, the People's Court may determine that they subjectively have no fault, except where they voted in favor when reviewing or approving the information disclosure documents.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　独立董事能够证明下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定其没有过错：  
（一）在签署相关信息披露文件之前，对不属于自身专业领域的相关具体问题，借助会计、法律等专门职业的帮助仍然未能发现问题的；  
（二）在揭露日或更正日之前，发现虚假陈述后及时向发行人提出异议并监督整改或者向证券交易场所、监管部门书面报告的；  
（三）在独立意见中对虚假陈述事项发表保留意见、反对意见或者无法表示意见并说明具体理由的，但在审议、审核相关文件时投赞成票的除外；  
（四）因发行人拒绝、阻碍其履行职责，导致无法对相关信息披露文件是否存在虚假陈述作出判断，并及时向证券交易场所、监管部门书面报告的；  
（五）能够证明勤勉尽责的其他情形。  
独立董事提交证据证明其在履职期间能够按照法律、监管部门制定的规章和规范性文件以及公司章程的要求履行职责的，或者在虚假陈述被揭露后及时督促发行人整改且效果较为明显的，人民法院可以结合案件事实综合判断其过错情况。  
外部监事和职工监事，参照适用前两款规定。

**Article 16** Where an independent director can prove any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that he or she has no fault:  
(1) before signing the relevant information disclosure documents, with respect to specific issues not within his or her own professional field, he or she sought assistance from accounting, legal, or other specialized professions but still failed to identify the problem;  
(2) after discovering the false statement before the date of revelation or correction, he or she timely raised objections to the issuer and supervised rectification, or made a written report to the securities trading venue or regulatory authority;  
(3) he or she expressed a qualified opinion, adverse opinion, or disclaimer of opinion on the false statement matters in the independent opinion and stated specific reasons, except where he or she voted in favor when reviewing or approving the relevant documents;  
(4) due to the issuer's refusal or obstruction of his or her performance of duties, he or she was unable to determine whether the relevant information disclosure documents contained false statements, and timely made a written report to the securities trading venue or regulatory authority;  
(5) other circumstances that can prove diligence.  
Where an independent director submits evidence proving that during his or her tenure, he or she performed duties in accordance with laws, the rules and normative documents formulated by the regulatory authority, and the company's articles of association, or that after the false statement was revealed, he or she timely urged the issuer to rectify and achieved relatively obvious effects, the People's Court may comprehensively assess the fault status based on the facts of the case.  
External supervisors and employee supervisors shall apply the preceding two paragraphs by reference.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　保荐机构、承销机构等机构及其直接责任人员提交的尽职调查工作底稿、尽职调查报告、内部审核意见等证据能够证明下列情形的，人民法院应当认定其没有过错：  
（一）已经按照法律、行政法规、监管部门制定的规章和规范性文件、相关行业执业规范的要求，对信息披露文件中的相关内容进行了审慎尽职调查；  
（二）对信息披露文件中没有证券服务机构专业意见支持的重要内容，经过审慎尽职调查和独立判断，有合理理由相信该部分内容与真实情况相符；  
（三）对信息披露文件中证券服务机构出具专业意见的重要内容，经过审慎核查和必要的调查、复核，有合理理由排除了职业怀疑并形成合理信赖。  
在全国中小企业股份转让系统从事挂牌和定向发行推荐业务的证券公司，适用前款规定。

**Article 17** Where sponsoring institutions, underwriting institutions, and other institutions and their directly responsible persons submit evidence such as due diligence working papers, due diligence reports, and internal review opinions that can prove the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that they have no fault:  
(1) they have conducted prudent due diligence on the relevant content in the information disclosure documents in accordance with the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, the rules and normative documents formulated by the regulatory authority, and relevant industry practice standards;  
(2) with respect to important content in the information disclosure documents that is not supported by professional opinions of securities service institutions, after prudent due diligence and independent judgment, they have reasonable grounds to believe that such content is consistent with the true circumstances;  
(3) with respect to important content in the information disclosure documents for which securities service institutions have issued professional opinions, after prudent verification and necessary investigation and review, they have reasonable grounds to eliminate professional skepticism and form reasonable reliance.  
Securities companies engaged in the recommendation business for listing and targeted issuance on the National Equities Exchange and Quotations shall apply the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资信评级机构、资产评估机构、财务顾问等证券服务机构制作、出具的文件存在虚假陈述的，人民法院应当按照法律、行政法规、监管部门制定的规章和规范性文件，参考行业执业规范规定的工作范围和程序要求等内容，结合其核查、验证工作底稿等相关证据，认定其是否存在过错。  
证券服务机构的责任限于其工作范围和专业领域。证券服务机构依赖保荐机构或者其他证券服务机构的基础工作或者专业意见致使其出具的专业意见存在虚假陈述，能够证明其对所依赖的基础工作或者专业意见经过审慎核查和必要的调查、复核，排除了职业怀疑并形成合理信赖的，人民法院应当认定其没有过错。

**Article 18** Where documents prepared and issued by securities service institutions such as accounting firms, law firms, credit rating agencies, asset appraisal institutions, and financial advisors contain false statements, the People's Court shall determine whether fault exists in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the rules and normative documents formulated by the regulatory authority, with reference to the scope of work and procedural requirements stipulated by industry practice standards, and in conjunction with relevant evidence such as their verification and validation working papers.  
The liability of a securities service institution shall be limited to its scope of work and professional field. Where a securities service institution, by relying on the foundational work or professional opinions of a sponsoring institution or other securities service institution, causes its issued professional opinions to contain false statements, but can prove that it has conducted prudent verification and necessary investigation and review of the relied-upon foundational work or professional opinions, eliminated professional skepticism, and formed reasonable reliance, the People's Court shall determine that it has no fault.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　会计师事务所能够证明下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定其没有过错：  
（一）按照执业准则、规则确定的工作程序和核查手段并保持必要的职业谨慎，仍未发现被审计的会计资料存在错误的；  
（二）审计业务必须依赖的金融机构、发行人的供应商、客户等相关单位提供不实证明文件，会计师事务所保持了必要的职业谨慎仍未发现的；  
（三）已对发行人的舞弊迹象提出警告并在审计业务报告中发表了审慎审计意见的；  
（四）能够证明没有过错的其他情形。  
五、责任主体

**Article 19** Where an accounting firm can prove any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall determine that it has no fault:  
(1) it followed the work procedures and verification methods determined by the professional standards and rules and maintained necessary professional care, but still failed to discover errors in the audited accounting materials;  
(2) the financial institutions, the issuer's suppliers, customers, or other relevant units that the audit must rely on provided false supporting documents, and the accounting firm maintained necessary professional care but still failed to discover this;  
(3) it had already issued warnings about signs of fraud by the issuer and expressed prudent audit opinions in the audit report;  
(4) other circumstances that can prove no fault.  
V. Liable Parties

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发行人的控股股东、实际控制人组织、指使发行人实施虚假陈述，致使原告在证券交易中遭受损失的，原告起诉请求直接判令该控股股东、实际控制人依照本规定赔偿损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。  
控股股东、实际控制人组织、指使发行人实施虚假陈述，发行人在承担赔偿责任后要求该控股股东、实际控制人赔偿实际支付的赔偿款、合理的律师费、诉讼费用等损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 20** Where the controlling shareholder or actual controller of the issuer organizes or instructs the issuer to make a false statement, thereby causing the plaintiff to suffer losses in securities trading, and the plaintiff institutes an action requesting a direct judgment ordering such controlling shareholder or actual controller to compensate for the losses in accordance with these Provisions, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the controlling shareholder or actual controller organizes or instructs the issuer to make a false statement, and after the issuer bears compensation liability, it requests such controlling shareholder or actual controller to compensate for the losses actually paid as compensation, reasonable attorney fees, litigation costs, etc., the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公司重大资产重组的交易对方所提供的信息不符合真实、准确、完整的要求，导致公司披露的相关信息存在虚假陈述，原告起诉请求判令该交易对方与发行人等责任主体赔偿由此导致的损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 21** Where the information provided by the counterparty to a material asset restructuring of a company fails to meet the requirements of truthfulness, accuracy, and completeness, resulting in false statements in the relevant information disclosed by the company, and the plaintiff institutes an action requesting a judgment ordering such counterparty and the issuer and other liable parties to compensate for losses caused thereby, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有证据证明发行人的供应商、客户，以及为发行人提供服务的金融机构等明知发行人实施财务造假活动，仍然为其提供相关交易合同、发票、存款证明等予以配合，或者故意隐瞒重要事实致使发行人的信息披露文件存在虚假陈述，原告起诉请求判令其与发行人等责任主体赔偿由此导致的损失的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 22** Where there is evidence proving that the issuer's suppliers, customers, and financial institutions providing services to the issuer, while knowing that the issuer is engaged in financial fraud activities, still provide relevant transaction contracts, invoices, deposit certificates, etc. to cooperate, or intentionally conceal important facts, causing the issuer's information disclosure documents to contain false statements, and the plaintiff institutes an action requesting a judgment ordering them to compensate for losses caused thereby together with the issuer and other liable parties, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承担连带责任的当事人之间的责任分担与追偿，按照[民法典第一百七十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-178)的规定处理，但[本规定第二十条](#art-20)第二款规定的情形除外。  
保荐机构、承销机构等责任主体以存在约定为由，请求发行人或者其控股股东、实际控制人补偿其因虚假陈述所承担的赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。  
六、损失认定

**Article 23** The allocation and recovery of liability among parties bearing joint and several liability shall be handled in accordance with Article 178 of the Civil Code, except for the circumstances specified in Article 20, paragraph 2 of these Provisions.  
Where a sponsoring institution, underwriting institution, or other liable party, on the ground that there is an agreement, requests the issuer or its controlling shareholder or actual controller to compensate for the compensation liability it has borne due to the false statement, the People's Court shall not support such request.  
VI. Determination of Losses

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　发行人在证券发行市场虚假陈述，导致原告损失的，原告有权请求按照[本规定第二十五条](#art-25)的规定赔偿损失。

**Article 24** Where the issuer makes a false statement in the securities issuance market, causing losses to the plaintiff, the plaintiff shall have the right to request compensation for losses in accordance with Article 25 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　信息披露义务人在证券交易市场承担民事赔偿责任的范围，以原告因虚假陈述而实际发生的损失为限。原告实际损失包括投资差额损失、投资差额损失部分的佣金和印花税。

**Article 25** The scope of civil compensation liability borne by an information disclosure obligor in the securities trading market shall be limited to the actual losses incurred by the plaintiff due to the false statement. The plaintiff's actual losses include the investment differential loss, and the commission and stamp duty on the portion of the investment differential loss.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　投资差额损失计算的基准日，是指在虚假陈述揭露或更正后，为将原告应获赔偿限定在虚假陈述所造成的损失范围内，确定损失计算的合理期间而规定的截止日期。  
在采用集中竞价的交易市场中，自揭露日或更正日起，被虚假陈述影响的证券集中交易累计成交量达到可流通部分100%之日为基准日。  
自揭露日或更正日起，集中交易累计换手率在10个交易日内达到可流通部分100%的，以第10个交易日为基准日；在30个交易日内未达到可流通部分100%的，以第30个交易日为基准日。  
虚假陈述揭露日或更正日起至基准日期间每个交易日收盘价的平均价格，为损失计算的基准价格。  
无法依前款规定确定基准价格的，人民法院可以根据有专门知识的人的专业意见，参考对相关行业进行投资时的通常估值方法，确定基准价格。

**Article 26** The reference date for calculating the investment differential loss refers to the cut-off date prescribed to define the reasonable period for calculating losses, so as to limit the compensation to be received by the plaintiff to the losses caused by the false statement after its revelation or correction.  
In a centralized bidding trading market, starting from the date of revelation or correction, the date on which the cumulative trading volume of the securities affected by the false statement reaches 100% of the tradable portion shall be the reference date.  
If, starting from the date of revelation or correction, the cumulative turnover rate of centralized trading reaches 100% of the tradable portion within 10 trading days, the 10th trading day shall be the reference date; if it does not reach 100% of the tradable portion within 30 trading days, the 30th trading day shall be the reference date.  
The average price of the closing prices on each trading day from the date of revelation or correction to the reference date shall be the benchmark price for loss calculation.  
If the benchmark price cannot be determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may determine the benchmark price based on the professional opinions of persons with specialized knowledge, with reference to the usual valuation methods used when investing in the relevant industry.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在采用集中竞价的交易市场中，原告因虚假陈述买入相关股票所造成的投资差额损失，按照下列方法计算：  
（一）原告在实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之前买入，在揭露日或更正日之后、基准日之前卖出的股票，按买入股票的平均价格与卖出股票的平均价格之间的差额，乘以已卖出的股票数量；  
（二）原告在实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之前买入，基准日之前未卖出的股票，按买入股票的平均价格与基准价格之间的差额，乘以未卖出的股票数量。

**Article 27** In a centralized bidding trading market, the investment differential loss caused to the plaintiff by purchasing relevant stocks due to a false statement shall be calculated as follows:  
(1) for stocks purchased by the plaintiff after the date of making and before the date of revelation or correction, and sold after the date of revelation or correction and before the reference date, the difference between the average purchase price and the average sale price shall be multiplied by the number of stocks sold;  
(2) for stocks purchased by the plaintiff after the date of making and before the date of revelation or correction, and not sold before the reference date, the difference between the average purchase price and the benchmark price shall be multiplied by the number of stocks not sold.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在采用集中竞价的交易市场中，原告因虚假陈述卖出相关股票所造成的投资差额损失，按照下列方法计算：  
（一）原告在实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之前卖出，在揭露日或更正日之后、基准日之前买回的股票，按买回股票的平均价格与卖出股票的平均价格之间的差额，乘以买回的股票数量；  
（二）原告在实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之前卖出，基准日之前未买回的股票，按基准价格与卖出股票的平均价格之间的差额，乘以未买回的股票数量。

**Article 28** In a centralized bidding trading market, the investment differential loss caused to the plaintiff by selling relevant stocks due to a false statement shall be calculated as follows:  
(1) for stocks sold by the plaintiff after the date of making and before the date of revelation or correction, and repurchased after the date of revelation or correction and before the reference date, the difference between the average repurchase price and the average sale price shall be multiplied by the number of stocks repurchased;  
(2) for stocks sold by the plaintiff after the date of making and before the date of revelation or correction, and not repurchased before the reference date, the difference between the benchmark price and the average sale price shall be multiplied by the number of stocks not repurchased.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　计算投资差额损失时，已经除权的证券，证券价格和证券数量应当复权计算。

**Article 29** When calculating the investment differential loss, for securities that have undergone ex-rights adjustments, the securities prices and quantities shall be calculated on a rights-adjusted basis.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　证券公司、基金管理公司、保险公司、信托公司、商业银行等市场参与主体依法设立的证券投资产品，在确定因虚假陈述导致的损失时，每个产品应当单独计算。  
投资者及依法设立的证券投资产品开立多个证券账户进行投资的，应当将各证券账户合并，所有交易按照成交时间排序，以确定其实际交易及损失情况。

**Article 30** For securities investment products lawfully established by market participants such as securities companies, fund management companies, insurance companies, trust companies, and commercial banks, when determining the losses caused by a false statement, each product shall be calculated separately.  
Where an investor or a lawfully established securities investment product opens multiple securities accounts for investment, all such securities accounts shall be consolidated, and all transactions shall be sorted by transaction time to determine the actual trading and loss situation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院应当查明虚假陈述与原告损失之间的因果关系，以及导致原告损失的其他原因等案件基本事实，确定赔偿责任范围。  
被告能够举证证明原告的损失部分或者全部是由他人操纵市场、证券市场的风险、证券市场对特定事件的过度反应、上市公司内外部经营环境等其他因素所导致的，对其关于相应减轻或者免除责任的抗辩，人民法院应当予以支持。  
七、诉讼时效

**Article 31** The People's Court shall ascertain the basic facts of the case, including the causation between the false statement and the plaintiff's losses, as well as other causes of the plaintiff's losses, to determine the scope of compensation liability.  
Where the defendant can adduce evidence proving that the plaintiff's losses are partially or wholly caused by other factors such as market manipulation by others, risks of the securities market, excessive reaction of the securities market to specific events, or the internal and external business environment of the listed company, the People's Court shall support its defense for corresponding reduction or exemption of liability.  
VII. Limitation of Action

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人主张以揭露日或更正日起算诉讼时效的，人民法院应当予以支持。揭露日与更正日不一致的，以在先的为准。  
对于虚假陈述责任人中的一人发生诉讼时效中断效力的事由，应当认定对其他连带责任人也发生诉讼时效中断的效力。

**Article 32** Where a party claims that the limitation of action shall be calculated from the date of exposure or the date of correction, the People's Court shall uphold the claim. If the date of exposure and the date of correction are inconsistent, the earlier date shall prevail.  
Where a cause for interruption of the limitation of action occurs to one of the persons liable for the misrepresentation, such interruption shall be deemed to have also occurred to the other persons bearing joint and several liability.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在诉讼时效期间内，部分投资者向人民法院提起人数不确定的普通代表人诉讼的，人民法院应当认定该起诉行为对所有具有同类诉讼请求的权利人发生时效中断的效果。  
在普通代表人诉讼中，未向人民法院登记权利的投资者，其诉讼时效自权利登记期间届满后重新开始计算。向人民法院登记权利后申请撤回权利登记的投资者，其诉讼时效自撤回权利登记之次日重新开始计算。  
投资者保护机构依照[证券法第九十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-95)第三款的规定作为代表人参加诉讼后，投资者声明退出诉讼的，其诉讼时效自声明退出之次日起重新开始计算。  
八、附则

**Article 33** During the period of limitation of action, where some investors file a common representative action with an indefinite number of persons with the People's Court, the People's Court shall deem that such act of prosecution has the effect of interrupting the limitation of action for all right holders with similar claims.  
In a common representative action, for investors who have not registered their rights with the People's Court, the limitation of action shall recommence after the expiration of the rights registration period. For investors who have withdrawn their rights registration after registration, the limitation of action shall recommence from the day following the date of withdrawal of rights registration.  
After an investor protection institution participates in the action as a representative in accordance with the provisions of Article 95, paragraph 3 of the Securities Law, if an investor declares withdrawal from the action, the limitation of action of such investor shall recommence from the day following the date of such declaration of withdrawal.  
VIII. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本规定所称证券交易场所，是指证券交易所、国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所。  
本规定所称监管部门，是指国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院授权的部门及有关主管部门。  
本规定所称发行人，包括证券的发行人、上市公司或者挂牌公司。  
本规定所称实施日之后、揭露日或更正日之后、基准日之前，包括该日；所称揭露日或更正日之前，不包括该日。

**Article 34** The term "securities trading venues" as used in these Provisions refers to stock exchanges and other nationwide securities trading venues approved by the State Council.  
The term "regulatory authorities" as used in these Provisions refers to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, departments authorized by the State Council, and relevant competent authorities.  
The term "issuer" as used in these Provisions includes the issuer of securities, a listed company, or a company listed on the National Equities Exchange and Quotations (NEEQ).  
The terms "after the implementation date", "after the date of exposure or the date of correction", and "before the base date" as used in these Provisions include the day in question; the term "before the date of exposure or the date of correction" does not include the day in question.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本规定自2022年1月22日起施行。《最高人民法院关于受理证券市场因虚假陈述引发的民事侵权纠纷案件有关问题的通知》《最高人民法院关于审理证券市场因虚假陈述引发的民事赔偿案件的若干规定》同时废止。《最高人民法院关于审理涉及会计师事务所在审计业务活动中民事侵权赔偿案件的若干规定》与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
本规定施行后尚未终审的案件，适用本规定。本规定施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 35** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 22, 2022. The Notice of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Acceptance of Civil Tort Dispute Cases Arising from Misrepresentation in the Securities Market and the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Trial of Civil Compensation Cases Arising from Misrepresentation in the Securities Market shall be abolished simultaneously. If the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Trial of Civil Tort Compensation Cases Involving Accounting Firms in Audit Business Activities are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
Cases that have not been finally adjudicated after the coming into force of these Provisions shall be governed by these Provisions. For cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of these Provisions, where a party applies for a retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, these Provisions shall not apply.
