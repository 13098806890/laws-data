# 证券交易所风险基金管理暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障证券交易系统的安全运转，妥善管理和使用证券交易所风险基金，根据《中华人民共和国证券法》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of ensuring the safe operation of the securities trading system and properly managing and using the risk fund of a stock exchange.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称证券交易所风险基金(以下简称“本基金”)是指用于弥补证券交易所重大经济损失，防范与证券交易所业务活动有关的重大风险事故，以保证证券交易活动正常进行而设立的专项基金。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Measures, the risk fund of a stock exchange (hereinafter referred to as 'the Fund') refers to the special fund established to compensate for significant economic losses of the stock exchange, to prevent significant risk accidents related to the business activities of the stock exchange, and to ensure the normal operation of securities trading activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本基金来源：  
(一)按证券交易所收取交易经手费的20%提取，作为风险基金单独列账；  
(二)按证券交易所收取席位年费的10%提取，作为风险基金单独列账；  
(三)按证券交易所收取会员费10%的比例一次性提取，作为风险基金单独列账；  
(四)按本办法施行之日新股申购冻结资金利差账面余额的15%，一次性提取；  
(五)对违规会员的罚款、罚息收入。

**Article 3** The sources of the Fund:  
(1) 20% of the transaction fees collected by a stock exchange shall be extracted as the risk fund and separately accounted for;  
(2) 10% of the annual seat fees collected by a stock exchange shall be extracted as the risk fund and separately accounted for;  
(3) 10% of the membership fees collected by a stock exchange shall be extracted at one time as the risk fund and separately accounted for;  
(4) 15% of the book balance of the interest margin from frozen funds for new share subscriptions as of the date when these Measures come into force shall be extracted at one time;  
(5) Fines and penalty interest income imposed on non-compliant members.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　每一个财政年度终了，本基金净资产达到或超过10亿元后，下一年度不再根据[本办法第三条](#art-3)第(一)、(二)项提取资金。

**Article 4** After the net assets of the Fund reach or exceed RMB 1 billion at the end of each fiscal year, no funds shall be extracted in the following year in accordance with subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 3 of these Measures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　每一个财政年度终了，本基金净资产不足10亿元，下一年度应按[本办法第三条](#art-3)第(一)、(二)项规定继续提取资金。

**Article 5** If the net assets of the Fund are less than RMB 1 billion at the end of each fiscal year, funds shall continue to be extracted in the following year in accordance with subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 3 of these Measures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中国证券监督管理委员会(以下简称“证监会”)会同财政部可以根据市场风险情况，适当调整本基金规模、资金提取和交纳方式、比例。

**Article 6** The China Securities Regulatory Commission (hereinafter referred to as the 'CSRC'), jointly with the Ministry of Finance, may, based on market risk conditions, appropriately adjust the size of the Fund and the methods and proportions of fund extraction and payment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本基金由证券交易所理事会管理。理事会应当指定机构，负责本基金的日常管理和使用。

**Article 7** The Fund shall be managed by the board of directors of the stock exchange. The board of directors shall designate an institution to be responsible for the daily management and use of the Fund.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本基金应当以专户方式全部存入国有商业银行，存款利息全部转入基金专户。

**Article 8** The Fund shall be deposited in full in a special account with a state-owned commercial bank, and all deposit interest shall be transferred into the special fund account.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本基金资产与证券交易所资产分开列账。本基金应当下设分类账，分别记录按[本办法第三条](#art-3)各项所形成的本基金资产、利息收入及对应的资产本息使用情况。

**Article 9** The assets of the Fund shall be separately accounted for from the assets of the stock exchange. The Fund shall maintain sub-ledgers to separately record the Fund assets formed under each item of Article 3 of these Measures, interest income, and the use of the corresponding principal and interest of the assets.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本基金最低支付限额2000万元。

**Article 10** The minimum payment limit of the Fund is RMB 20 million.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　按[本办法第三条](#art-3)第(四)项所提取的资金，应当在该条其他项资金支付完毕后才能动用。

**Article 11** Funds extracted under subparagraph (4) of Article 3 of these Measures shall be used only after the funds under other subparagraphs of that Article have been fully paid.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　证券交易所应当按照有关法律、法规的规定，建立和完善业务规则、内部管理制度及会员监管制度，最大限度地避免风险事故发生。

**Article 12** A stock exchange shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, establish and improve its business rules, internal management systems, and member supervision systems, so as to minimize the occurrence of risk accidents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　动用本基金后，证券交易所应当向有关责任方追偿，追偿款转入本基金；同时，应及时修订和完善业务规则、内部管理制度及会员监管制度。

**Article 13** After using the Fund, the stock exchange shall seek recovery from the relevant responsible parties, and the recovered amounts shall be transferred into the Fund; at the same time, it shall promptly revise and improve its business rules, internal management systems, and member supervision systems.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经证监会批准，本基金作相应变更、清算时，由证监会会同财政部另行决定本基金剩余资产中应当上交财政和退还有关出资人的比例和数额。

**Article 14** When the Fund undergoes corresponding changes or liquidation with the approval of the CSRC, the CSRC, jointly with the Ministry of Finance, shall separately decide on the proportion and amount of the remaining assets of the Fund to be turned over to the treasury and refunded to the relevant contributors.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本基金的财务核算与管理办法由财政部制定。

**Article 15** The financial accounting and management measures for the Fund shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法由证监会负责解释。

**Article 16** These Measures shall be interpreted by the CSRC.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自颁布之日起施行。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
