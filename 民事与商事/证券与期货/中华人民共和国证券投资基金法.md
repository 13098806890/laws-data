### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范证券投资基金活动，保护投资人及相关当事人的合法权益，促进证券投资基金和资本市场的健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of regulating securities investment fund activities, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of investors and relevant parties, and promoting the healthy development of securities investment funds and the capital market.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内，公开或者非公开募集资金设立证券投资基金（以下简称基金），由基金管理人管理，基金托管人托管，为基金份额持有人的利益，进行证券投资活动，适用本法；本法未规定的，适用《中华人民共和国信托法》、《中华人民共和国证券法》和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 2** The raising of funds through public or non-public offering to establish securities investment funds (hereinafter referred to as funds) within the territory of the People's Republic of China, which are managed by fund managers and custodied by fund custodians for the benefit of fund share holders in conducting securities investment activities, shall be governed by this Law; where this Law does not provide, the provisions of the Trust Law of the People's Republic of China, the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基金管理人、基金托管人和基金份额持有人的权利、义务，依照本法在基金合同中约定。  
基金管理人、基金托管人依照本法和基金合同的约定，履行受托职责。  
通过公开募集方式设立的基金（以下简称公开募集基金）的基金份额持有人按其所持基金份额享受收益和承担风险，通过非公开募集方式设立的基金（以下简称非公开募集基金）的收益分配和风险承担由基金合同约定。

**Article 3** The rights and obligations of fund managers, fund custodians and fund share holders shall be stipulated in the fund contract in accordance with this Law.  
Fund managers and fund custodians shall perform their fiduciary duties in accordance with this Law and the fund contract.  
Fund share holders of a fund established through public offering (hereinafter referred to as a publicly offered fund) shall enjoy the benefits and bear the risks in proportion to their fund shares held, while the distribution of benefits and bearing of risks of a fund established through non-public offering (hereinafter referred to as a privately offered fund) shall be stipulated in the fund contract.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事证券投资基金活动，应当遵循自愿、公平、诚实信用的原则，不得损害国家利益和社会公共利益。

**Article 4** In conducting securities investment fund activities, the principles of voluntariness, fairness, good faith and credit shall be observed, and no harm shall be done to the national interests or the public interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　基金财产的债务由基金财产本身承担，基金份额持有人以其出资为限对基金财产的债务承担责任。但基金合同依照本法另有约定的，从其约定。  
基金财产独立于基金管理人、基金托管人的固有财产。基金管理人、基金托管人不得将基金财产归入其固有财产。  
基金管理人、基金托管人因基金财产的管理、运用或者其他情形而取得的财产和收益，归入基金财产。  
基金管理人、基金托管人因依法解散、被依法撤销或者被依法宣告破产等原因进行清算的，基金财产不属于其清算财产。

**Article 5** The debts of fund property shall be borne by the fund property itself, and fund share holders shall be liable for the debts of the fund property to the extent of their capital contributions. However, if the fund contract provides otherwise in accordance with this Law, such provisions shall prevail.  
Fund property shall be independent of the proprietary property of the fund manager and the fund custodian. A fund manager or a fund custodian shall not incorporate fund property into its proprietary property.  
Any property and income acquired by a fund manager or a fund custodian through the management, use or other circumstances of fund property shall be included into the fund property.  
Where a fund manager or a fund custodian goes into liquidation due to dissolution according to law, being revoked according to law, or being declared bankrupt according to law, fund property shall not be part of its liquidation property.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　基金财产的债权，不得与基金管理人、基金托管人固有财产的债务相抵销；不同基金财产的债权债务，不得相互抵销。

**Article 6** The claims of fund property shall not be offset against the debts of the proprietary property of the fund manager or the fund custodian; the claims and debts of different fund properties shall not be offset against each other.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　非因基金财产本身承担的债务，不得对基金财产强制执行。

**Article 7** No compulsory execution shall be enforced against fund property for debts not borne by the fund property itself.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　基金财产投资的相关税收，由基金份额持有人承担，基金管理人或者其他扣缴义务人按照国家有关税收征收的规定代扣代缴。

**Article 8** The relevant taxes on the investment of fund property shall be borne by the fund share holders, and the fund manager or other withholding agent shall withhold and pay such taxes in accordance with the relevant state provisions on tax collection.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　基金管理人、基金托管人管理、运用基金财产，基金服务机构从事基金服务活动，应当恪尽职守，履行诚实信用、谨慎勤勉的义务。  
基金管理人运用基金财产进行证券投资，应当遵守审慎经营规则，制定科学合理的投资策略和风险管理制度，有效防范和控制风险。  
基金从业人员应当具备基金从业资格，遵守法律、行政法规，恪守职业道德和行为规范。

**Article 9** Fund managers and fund custodians in managing and using fund property, and fund service institutions in engaging in fund service activities, shall perform their duties with due diligence, and fulfill the obligations of good faith, prudence and diligence.  
A fund manager in using fund property to conduct securities investment shall comply with prudent operation rules, formulate scientific and reasonable investment strategies and risk management systems, and effectively prevent and control risks.  
Fund practitioners shall possess fund practice qualifications, comply with laws and administrative regulations, and abide by professional ethics and codes of conduct.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　基金管理人、基金托管人和基金服务机构，应当依照本法成立证券投资基金行业协会（以下简称基金行业协会），进行行业自律，协调行业关系，提供行业服务，促进行业发展。

**Article 10** Fund managers, fund custodians and fund service institutions shall, in accordance with this Law, establish a Securities Investment Fund Industry Association (hereinafter referred to as the Fund Industry Association) to conduct industry self-regulation, coordinate industry relations, provide industry services and promote the development of the industry.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法对证券投资基金活动实施监督管理；其派出机构依照授权履行职责。

**Article 11** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over securities investment fund activities; its local offices shall perform their duties in accordance with the authorization.

### 第二章　基金管理人

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　基金管理人由依法设立的公司或者合伙企业担任。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人，由基金管理公司或者经国务院证券监督管理机构按照规定核准的其他机构担任。

**Article 12** A fund manager shall be a company or a partnership enterprise established according to law.  
The fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall be a fund management company or another institution approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立管理公开募集基金的基金管理公司，应当具备下列条件，并经国务院证券监督管理机构批准：  
（一）有符合本法和《中华人民共和国公司法》规定的章程；  
（二）注册资本不低于一亿元人民币，且必须为实缴货币资本；  
（三）主要股东应当具有经营金融业务或者管理金融机构的良好业绩、良好的财务状况和社会信誉，资产规模达到国务院规定的标准，最近三年没有违法记录；  
（四）取得基金从业资格的人员达到法定人数；  
（五）董事、监事、高级管理人员具备相应的任职条件；  
（六）有符合要求的营业场所、安全防范设施和与基金管理业务有关的其他设施；  
（七）有良好的内部治理结构、完善的内部稽核监控制度、风险控制制度；  
（八）法律、行政法规规定的和经国务院批准的国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他条件。

**Article 13** The establishment of a fund management company for managing publicly offered funds shall meet the following conditions and be subject to the approval of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) having articles of association that comply with this Law and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) having a registered capital of not less than RMB 100 million yuan, which must be paid-in monetary capital;  
(3) the principal shareholders shall have good performance records in engaging in financial business or managing financial institutions, a sound financial condition and good social reputation, with asset sizes meeting the standards prescribed by the State Council, and no records of illegal acts in the most recent three years;  
(4) having the statutory number of personnel who have obtained fund practice qualifications;  
(5) the directors, supervisors and senior officers having the corresponding qualifications for their positions;  
(6) having business premises, security facilities and other facilities related to fund management business that meet the requirements;  
(7) having a sound internal governance structure, a comprehensive internal audit and monitoring system, and a risk control system;  
(8) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations and by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council with the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院证券监督管理机构应当自受理基金管理公司设立申请之日起六个月内依照[本法第十三条](#art-13)规定的条件和审慎监管原则进行审查，作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人；不予批准的，应当说明理由。  
基金管理公司变更持有百分之五以上股权的股东，变更公司的实际控制人，或者变更其他重大事项，应当报经国务院证券监督管理机构批准。国务院证券监督管理机构应当自受理申请之日起六十日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并通知申请人；不予批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 14** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of accepting an application for the establishment of a fund management company, conduct examination in accordance with the conditions specified in Article 13 of this Law and the principle of prudential supervision, and make a decision to approve or disapprove, and shall notify the applicant; if the application is disapproved, the reasons therefor shall be stated.  
Where a fund management company changes shareholders holding more than five percent of its equity, changes the actual controller of the company, or changes other material matters, it shall report to and obtain the approval of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within sixty days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove, and notify the applicant; if the application is disapproved, the reasons therefor shall be stated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任公开募集基金的基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员：  
（一）因犯有贪污贿赂、渎职、侵犯财产罪或者破坏社会主义市场经济秩序罪，被判处刑罚的；  
（二）对所任职的公司、企业因经营不善破产清算或者因违法被吊销营业执照负有个人责任的董事、监事、厂长、高级管理人员，自该公司、企业破产清算终结或者被吊销营业执照之日起未逾五年的；  
（三）个人所负债务数额较大，到期未清偿的；  
（四）因违法行为被开除的基金管理人、基金托管人、证券交易所、证券公司、证券登记结算机构、期货交易所、期货公司及其他机构的从业人员和国家机关工作人员；  
（五）因违法行为被吊销执业证书或者被取消资格的律师、注册会计师和资产评估机构、验证机构的从业人员、投资咨询从业人员；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定不得从事基金业务的其他人员。

**Article 15** No person under any of the following circumstances may serve as a director, supervisor, senior officer or other practitioner of a fund manager of a publicly offered fund:  
(1) having been sentenced to criminal punishment for committing the crimes of embezzlement, bribery, dereliction of duty, infringement of property rights or undermining the order of the socialist market economy;  
(2) being a director, supervisor, factory manager or senior officer who bears personal responsibility for the bankruptcy liquidation due to poor management or the revocation of business license due to illegal acts of the company or enterprise where he/she previously served, and less than five years have elapsed since the completion of the bankruptcy liquidation or the revocation of the business license of such company or enterprise;  
(3) having a relatively large amount of personal debt that is overdue and unpaid;  
(4) having been dismissed from a fund manager, fund custodian, stock exchange, securities company, securities central depository and clearing institution, futures exchange, futures company or other institution, or as a state organ staff member, due to illegal acts;  
(5) being a lawyer, certified public accountant, or practitioner of an asset appraisal institution, verification institution, or investment consulting institution whose practicing certificate has been revoked or qualification has been cancelled due to illegal acts;  
(6) other persons who are prohibited by laws and administrative regulations from engaging in fund business.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公开募集基金的基金管理人的董事、监事和高级管理人员，应当熟悉证券投资方面的法律、行政法规，具有三年以上与其所任职务相关的工作经历；高级管理人员还应当具备基金从业资格。

**Article 16** Directors, supervisors and senior officers of a fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall be familiar with the laws and administrative regulations on securities investment and have at least three years of work experience related to their respective positions; senior officers shall also have fund practice qualifications.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公开募集基金的基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员，其本人、配偶、利害关系人进行证券投资，应当事先向基金管理人申报，并不得与基金份额持有人发生利益冲突。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人应当建立前款规定人员进行证券投资的申报、登记、审查、处置等管理制度，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。

**Article 17** Where a director, supervisor, senior officer or other practitioner of a fund manager of a publicly offered fund, or his/her spouse or interested person, engages in securities investment, he/she shall declare the investment in advance to the fund manager, and shall not have a conflict of interest with the fund share holders.  
A fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall establish management systems for the declaration, registration, review and disposition of securities investments by the persons specified in the preceding paragraph, and shall report such systems to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公开募集基金的基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员，不得担任基金托管人或者其他基金管理人的任何职务，不得从事损害基金财产和基金份额持有人利益的证券交易及其他活动。

**Article 18** Directors, supervisors, senior officers and other practitioners of a fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall not hold any position in a fund custodian or any other fund manager, and shall not engage in securities transactions or other activities that harm the interests of fund property and fund share holders.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公开募集基金的基金管理人应当履行下列职责：  
（一）依法募集资金，办理基金份额的发售和登记事宜；  
（二）办理基金备案手续；  
（三）对所管理的不同基金财产分别管理、分别记账，进行证券投资；  
（四）按照基金合同的约定确定基金收益分配方案，及时向基金份额持有人分配收益；  
（五）进行基金会计核算并编制基金财务会计报告；  
（六）编制中期和年度基金报告；  
（七）计算并公告基金资产净值，确定基金份额申购、赎回价格；  
（八）办理与基金财产管理业务活动有关的信息披露事项；  
（九）按照规定召集基金份额持有人大会；  
（十）保存基金财产管理业务活动的记录、账册、报表和其他相关资料；  
（十一）以基金管理人名义，代表基金份额持有人利益行使诉讼权利或者实施其他法律行为；  
（十二）国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他职责。

**Article 19** A fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall perform the following duties:  
(1) raising funds according to law, and handling matters relating to the offering and registration of fund shares;  
(2) going through the formalities for fund recordation;  
(3) managing different fund properties under its management separately, keeping separate accounts, and conducting securities investment;  
(4) determining the fund income distribution plan in accordance with the fund contract and distributing income to fund share holders in a timely manner;  
(5) conducting fund accounting and preparing fund financial accounting reports;  
(6) preparing interim and annual fund reports;  
(7) calculating and announcing the net asset value of the fund, and determining the subscription and redemption prices of fund shares;  
(8) handling information disclosure matters relating to fund property management business activities;  
(9) convening meetings of fund share holders in accordance with the provisions;  
(10) keeping records, account books, statements and other relevant materials of fund property management business activities;  
(11) exercising litigation rights or performing other legal acts in the name of the fund manager on behalf of the interests of fund share holders;  
(12) other duties prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公开募集基金的基金管理人及其董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员不得有下列行为：  
（一）将其固有财产或者他人财产混同于基金财产从事证券投资；  
（二）不公平地对待其管理的不同基金财产；  
（三）利用基金财产或者职务之便为基金份额持有人以外的人牟取利益；  
（四）向基金份额持有人违规承诺收益或者承担损失；  
（五）侵占、挪用基金财产；  
（六）泄露因职务便利获取的未公开信息、利用该信息从事或者明示、暗示他人从事相关的交易活动；  
（七）玩忽职守，不按照规定履行职责；  
（八）法律、行政法规和国务院证券监督管理机构规定禁止的其他行为。

**Article 20** A fund manager of a publicly offered fund and its directors, supervisors, senior officers and other practitioners shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) mixing its proprietary property or others' property with fund property for securities investment;  
(2) treating different fund properties under its management unfairly;  
(3) using fund property or taking advantage of positions to seek benefits for persons other than fund share holders;  
(4) making illegal promises of income or bearing losses to fund share holders;  
(5) embezzling or misappropriating fund property;  
(6) disclosing non-public information acquired by virtue of positions, using such information to engage in, or explicitly or implicitly instructing others to engage in, relevant trading activities;  
(7) neglecting duties and failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions;  
(8) other acts prohibited by laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公开募集基金的基金管理人应当建立良好的内部治理结构，明确股东会、董事会、监事会和高级管理人员的职责权限，确保基金管理人独立运作。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人可以实行专业人士持股计划，建立长效激励约束机制。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人的股东、董事、监事和高级管理人员在行使权利或者履行职责时，应当遵循基金份额持有人利益优先的原则。

**Article 21** The fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall establish a sound internal governance structure, clearly define the duties and authorities of the shareholders' meeting, board of directors, board of supervisors, and senior officers, and ensure the independent operation of the fund manager.  
The fund manager of a publicly offered fund may implement a professional shareholding plan and establish a long-term incentive and restraint mechanism.  
The shareholders, directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall, when exercising rights or performing duties, follow the principle of giving priority to the interests of fund unit holders.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公开募集基金的基金管理人应当从管理基金的报酬中计提风险准备金。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人因违法违规、违反基金合同等原因给基金财产或者基金份额持有人合法权益造成损失，应当承担赔偿责任的，可以优先使用风险准备金予以赔偿。

**Article 22** The fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall set aside a risk reserve from the remuneration for managing the fund.  
Where the fund manager of a publicly offered fund causes losses to fund property or the lawful rights and interests of fund unit holders due to violations of laws or regulations, breach of the fund contract, or other reasons, and shall bear compensation liability, it may use the risk reserve preferentially for compensation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公开募集基金的基金管理人的股东、实际控制人应当按照国务院证券监督管理机构的规定及时履行重大事项报告义务，并不得有下列行为：  
（一）虚假出资或者抽逃出资；  
（二）未依法经股东会或者董事会决议擅自干预基金管理人的基金经营活动；  
（三）要求基金管理人利用基金财产为自己或者他人牟取利益，损害基金份额持有人利益；  
（四）国务院证券监督管理机构规定禁止的其他行为。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人的股东、实际控制人有前款行为或者股东不再符合法定条件的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当责令其限期改正，并可视情节责令其转让所持有或者控制的基金管理人的股权。  
在前款规定的股东、实际控制人按照要求改正违法行为、转让所持有或者控制的基金管理人的股权前，国务院证券监督管理机构可以限制有关股东行使股东权利。

**Article 23** The shareholders and actual controllers of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall timely fulfill the obligation to report major matters in accordance with the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) making false capital contributions or withdrawing capital contributions;  
(2) interfering without authorization in the fund management activities of the fund manager without a resolution of the shareholders' meeting or board of directors according to law;  
(3) requiring the fund manager to use fund property to seek benefits for themselves or others, thereby harming the interests of fund unit holders;  
(4) other acts prohibited by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where the shareholders or actual controllers of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund commit the acts in the preceding paragraph, or the shareholders no longer meet the statutory conditions, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall order them to make corrections within a specified period, and may, depending on the circumstances, order them to transfer the equity they hold or control in the fund manager.  
Before the shareholders and actual controllers specified in the preceding paragraph correct the illegal acts and transfer the equity they hold or control in the fund manager as required, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may restrict the relevant shareholders from exercising their shareholder rights.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公开募集基金的基金管理人违法违规，或者其内部治理结构、稽核监控和风险控制管理不符合规定的，国务院证券监督管理机构应当责令其限期改正；逾期未改正，或者其行为严重危及该基金管理人的稳健运行、损害基金份额持有人合法权益的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以区别情形，对其采取下列措施：  
（一）限制业务活动，责令暂停部分或者全部业务；  
（二）限制分配红利，限制向董事、监事、高级管理人员支付报酬、提供福利；  
（三）限制转让固有财产或者在固有财产上设定其他权利；  
（四）责令更换董事、监事、高级管理人员或者限制其权利；  
（五）责令有关股东转让股权或者限制有关股东行使股东权利。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人整改后，应当向国务院证券监督管理机构提交报告。国务院证券监督管理机构经验收，符合有关要求的，应当自验收完毕之日起三日内解除对其采取的有关措施。

**Article 24** Where the fund manager of a publicly offered fund violates laws or regulations, or its internal governance structure, audit supervision, and risk control management do not comply with the provisions, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, or its conduct seriously endangers the stable operation of the fund manager and harms the lawful rights and interests of fund unit holders, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may, depending on the circumstances, take the following measures against it:  
(1) restricting business activities, ordering the suspension of part or all of its business;  
(2) restricting the distribution of dividends, restricting the payment of remuneration and provision of welfare to directors, supervisors, and senior officers;  
(3) restricting the transfer of its own property or creation of other rights on its own property;  
(4) ordering the replacement of directors, supervisors, or senior officers, or restricting their rights;  
(5) ordering the relevant shareholders to transfer their equity or restricting the relevant shareholders from exercising their shareholder rights.  
After the fund manager of a publicly offered fund has made corrections, it shall submit a report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. Where the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, upon inspection, finds that the fund manager meets the relevant requirements, it shall lift the measures taken against it within three days from the date of completion of the inspection.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公开募集基金的基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员未能勤勉尽责，致使基金管理人存在重大违法违规行为或者重大风险的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以责令更换。

**Article 25** Where the directors, supervisors, or senior officers of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund fail to exercise due diligence, causing the fund manager to have major violations of laws or regulations or major risks, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may order their replacement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公开募集基金的基金管理人违法经营或者出现重大风险，严重危害证券市场秩序、损害基金份额持有人利益的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以对该基金管理人采取责令停业整顿、指定其他机构托管、接管、取消基金管理资格或者撤销等监管措施。

**Article 26** Where the fund manager of a publicly offered fund operates illegally or experiences major risks, seriously endangering the order of the securities market and harming the interests of fund unit holders, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may take regulatory measures such as ordering a suspension of business for rectification, designating another institution for custodianship, taking over, canceling the fund management qualification, or revoking the fund manager.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在公开募集基金的基金管理人被责令停业整顿、被依法指定托管、接管或者清算期间，或者出现重大风险时，经国务院证券监督管理机构批准，可以对该基金管理人直接负责的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员采取下列措施：  
（一）通知出境管理机关依法阻止其出境；  
（二）申请司法机关禁止其转移、转让或者以其他方式处分财产，或者在财产上设定其他权利。

**Article 27** During the period when the fund manager of a publicly offered fund is ordered to suspend business for rectification, is designated for custodianship, taken over, or liquidated according to law, or when major risks arise, with the approval of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, the following measures may be taken against the directly responsible directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other directly responsible persons of the fund manager:  
(1) notifying the exit management authority to legally prevent their exit from the country;  
(2) applying to the judicial authority to prohibit them from transferring, assigning, or otherwise disposing of property, or creating other rights on the property.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列情形之一的，公开募集基金的基金管理人职责终止：  
（一）被依法取消基金管理资格；  
（二）被基金份额持有人大会解任；  
（三）依法解散、被依法撤销或者被依法宣告破产；  
（四）基金合同约定的其他情形。

**Article 28** The duties of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the fund management qualification is cancelled according to law;  
(2) it is removed by the fund unit holders' meeting;  
(3) it is dissolved, revoked, or declared bankrupt according to law;  
(4) other circumstances specified in the fund contract.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公开募集基金的基金管理人职责终止的，基金份额持有人大会应当在六个月内选任新基金管理人；新基金管理人产生前，由国务院证券监督管理机构指定临时基金管理人。  
公开募集基金的基金管理人职责终止的，应当妥善保管基金管理业务资料，及时办理基金管理业务的移交手续，新基金管理人或者临时基金管理人应当及时接收。

**Article 29** Where the duties of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund are terminated, the fund unit holders' meeting shall select and appoint a new fund manager within six months; before the new fund manager is appointed, a temporary fund manager shall be designated by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where the duties of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund are terminated, the fund manager shall properly preserve the fund management business records and promptly handle the transfer procedures for the fund management business, and the new fund manager or temporary fund manager shall promptly take over.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公开募集基金的基金管理人职责终止的，应当按照规定聘请会计师事务所对基金财产进行审计，并将审计结果予以公告，同时报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。

**Article 30** Where the duties of the fund manager of a publicly offered fund are terminated, it shall engage an accounting firm to audit the fund property in accordance with the provisions, and shall announce the audit results, and at the same time report them to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对非公开募集基金的基金管理人进行规范的具体办法，由国务院金融监督管理机构依照本章的原则制定。

**Article 31** The specific measures for regulating the fund manager of a non-publicly offered fund shall be formulated by the financial regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the principles of this Chapter.

### 第三章　基金托管人

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　基金托管人由依法设立的商业银行或者其他金融机构担任。  
商业银行担任基金托管人的，由国务院证券监督管理机构会同国务院银行业监督管理机构核准；其他金融机构担任基金托管人的，由国务院证券监督管理机构核准。

**Article 32** A fund custodian shall be a commercial bank or other financial institution established according to law.  
Where a commercial bank acts as a fund custodian, it shall be approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in conjunction with the banking regulatory authority of the State Council; where another financial institution acts as a fund custodian, it shall be approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　担任基金托管人，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）净资产和风险控制指标符合有关规定；  
（二）设有专门的基金托管部门；  
（三）取得基金从业资格的专职人员达到法定人数；  
（四）有安全保管基金财产的条件；  
（五）有安全高效的清算、交割系统；  
（六）有符合要求的营业场所、安全防范设施和与基金托管业务有关的其他设施；  
（七）有完善的内部稽核监控制度和风险控制制度；  
（八）法律、行政法规规定的和经国务院批准的国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院银行业监督管理机构规定的其他条件。

**Article 33** To act as a fund custodian, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) the net assets and risk control indicators comply with relevant provisions;  
(2) a specialized fund custody department is established;  
(3) the number of full-time personnel who have obtained fund practice qualifications reaches the statutory number;  
(4) having the conditions for safely safekeeping fund property;  
(5) having a safe and efficient clearing and settlement system;  
(6) having business premises, security facilities, and other facilities related to fund custody business that meet requirements;  
(7) having a comprehensive internal audit supervision system and risk control system;  
(8) other conditions stipulated by laws and administrative regulations and by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council with the approval of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　[本法第十五条](#art-15)、第十七条、第十八条的规定，适用于基金托管人的专门基金托管部门的高级管理人员和其他从业人员。  
[本法第十六条](#art-16)的规定，适用于基金托管人的专门基金托管部门的高级管理人员。

**Article 34** The provisions of Articles 15, 17, and 18 of this Law shall apply to the senior officers and other employees of the specialized fund custody department of a fund custodian.  
The provisions of Article 16 of this Law shall apply to the senior officers of the specialized fund custody department of a fund custodian.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　基金托管人与基金管理人不得为同一机构，不得相互出资或者持有股份。

**Article 35** A fund custodian and a fund manager shall not be the same institution, and shall not make capital contributions to each other or hold shares in each other.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　基金托管人应当履行下列职责：  
（一）安全保管基金财产；  
（二）按照规定开设基金财产的资金账户和证券账户；  
（三）对所托管的不同基金财产分别设置账户，确保基金财产的完整与独立；  
（四）保存基金托管业务活动的记录、账册、报表和其他相关资料；  
（五）按照基金合同的约定，根据基金管理人的投资指令，及时办理清算、交割事宜；  
（六）办理与基金托管业务活动有关的信息披露事项；  
（七）对基金财务会计报告、中期和年度基金报告出具意见；  
（八）复核、审查基金管理人计算的基金资产净值和基金份额申购、赎回价格；  
（九）按照规定召集基金份额持有人大会；  
（十）按照规定监督基金管理人的投资运作；  
（十一）国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他职责。

**Article 36** A fund custodian shall perform the following duties:  
(1) safely safekeeping fund property;  
(2) opening fund property capital accounts and securities accounts in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) setting up separate accounts for different fund properties under its custody to ensure the integrity and independence of fund property;  
(4) maintaining records, ledgers, statements, and other relevant materials of fund custody business activities;  
(5) handling clearance and settlement matters promptly in accordance with the fund contract and based on the investment instructions of the fund manager;  
(6) handling information disclosure matters related to fund custody business activities;  
(7) providing opinions on the fund's financial accounting reports, semi-annual and annual fund reports;  
(8) reviewing and verifying the net asset value of the fund and the subscription and redemption prices of fund units calculated by the fund manager;  
(9) convening the fund unit holders' meeting in accordance with the provisions;  
(10) supervising the investment operations of the fund manager in accordance with the provisions;  
(11) other duties specified by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　基金托管人发现基金管理人的投资指令违反法律、行政法规和其他有关规定，或者违反基金合同约定的，应当拒绝执行，立即通知基金管理人，并及时向国务院证券监督管理机构报告。  
基金托管人发现基金管理人依据交易程序已经生效的投资指令违反法律、行政法规和其他有关规定，或者违反基金合同约定的，应当立即通知基金管理人，并及时向国务院证券监督管理机构报告。

**Article 37** Where a fund custodian discovers that an investment instruction of the fund manager violates laws, administrative regulations, or other relevant provisions, or breaches the fund contract, it shall refuse to execute it, immediately notify the fund manager, and promptly report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Where a fund custodian discovers that an investment instruction of the fund manager that has taken effect in accordance with the trading procedures violates laws, administrative regulations, or other relevant provisions, or breaches the fund contract, it shall immediately notify the fund manager and promptly report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　[本法第二十条](#art-20)、第二十二条的规定，适用于基金托管人。

**Article 38** The provisions of Articles 20 and 22 of this Law shall apply to fund custodians.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　基金托管人不再具备本法规定的条件，或者未能勤勉尽责，在履行本法规定的职责时存在重大失误的，国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院银行业监督管理机构应当责令其改正；逾期未改正，或者其行为严重影响所托管基金的稳健运行、损害基金份额持有人利益的，国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院银行业监督管理机构可以区别情形，对其采取下列措施：  
（一）限制业务活动，责令暂停办理新的基金托管业务；  
（二）责令更换负有责任的专门基金托管部门的高级管理人员。  
基金托管人整改后，应当向国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院银行业监督管理机构提交报告；经验收，符合有关要求的，应当自验收完毕之日起三日内解除对其采取的有关措施。

**Article 39** Where a fund custodian no longer meets the conditions specified in this Law, or fails to exercise due diligence and has major errors in performing its duties under this Law, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, or its conduct seriously affects the stable operation of the fund under its custody and harms the interests of fund unit holders, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may, depending on the circumstances, take the following measures against it:  
(1) restricting business activities, ordering the suspension of new fund custody business;  
(2) ordering the replacement of the responsible senior officers of the specialized fund custody department.  
After the fund custodian has made corrections, it shall submit a report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council; upon inspection, if it meets the relevant requirements, the measures taken against it shall be lifted within three days from the date of completion of the inspection.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院证券监督管理机构、国务院银行业监督管理机构对有下列情形之一的基金托管人，可以取消其基金托管资格：  
（一）连续三年没有开展基金托管业务的；  
（二）违反本法规定，情节严重的；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 40** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council and the banking regulatory authority of the State Council may cancel the fund custody qualification of a fund custodian under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it has not conducted fund custody business for three consecutive years;  
(2) it violates the provisions of this Law and the circumstances are serious;  
(3) other circumstances stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列情形之一的，基金托管人职责终止：  
（一）被依法取消基金托管资格；  
（二）被基金份额持有人大会解任；  
（三）依法解散、被依法撤销或者被依法宣告破产；  
（四）基金合同约定的其他情形。

**Article 41** In any of the following circumstances, the duties of the fund custodian shall be terminated:  
(1) The fund custodian qualification is revoked according to law;  
(2) The fund custodian is removed by the fund share holders' meeting;  
(3) The fund custodian is dissolved, revoked or declared bankrupt according to law;  
(4) Other circumstances as agreed in the fund contract.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　基金托管人职责终止的，基金份额持有人大会应当在六个月内选任新基金托管人；新基金托管人产生前，由国务院证券监督管理机构指定临时基金托管人。  
基金托管人职责终止的，应当妥善保管基金财产和基金托管业务资料，及时办理基金财产和基金托管业务的移交手续，新基金托管人或者临时基金托管人应当及时接收。

**Article 42** Where the duties of the fund custodian are terminated, the fund share holders' meeting shall appoint a new fund custodian within six months; before the new fund custodian is appointed, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall designate an interim fund custodian.  
Where the duties of the fund custodian are terminated, it shall properly preserve the fund property and the fund custody business materials, and timely handle the transfer procedures for the fund property and fund custody business; the new fund custodian or the interim fund custodian shall timely take over.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　基金托管人职责终止的，应当按照规定聘请会计师事务所对基金财产进行审计，并将审计结果予以公告，同时报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。

**Article 43** Where the duties of the fund custodian are terminated, it shall, in accordance with the provisions, appoint an accounting firm to audit the fund property, and shall announce the audit results, and at the same time report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.

### 第四章　基金的运作方式和组织

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　基金合同应当约定基金的运作方式。

**Article 44** The fund contract shall stipulate the mode of operation of the fund.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　基金的运作方式可以采用封闭式、开放式或者其他方式。  
采用封闭式运作方式的基金（以下简称封闭式基金），是指基金份额总额在基金合同期限内固定不变，基金份额持有人不得申请赎回的基金；采用开放式运作方式的基金（以下简称开放式基金），是指基金份额总额不固定，基金份额可以在基金合同约定的时间和场所申购或者赎回的基金。  
采用其他运作方式的基金的基金份额发售、交易、申购、赎回的办法，由国务院证券监督管理机构另行规定。

**Article 45** The mode of operation of a fund may adopt a closed-end, open-end or other mode.  
A fund that adopts the closed-end mode of operation (hereinafter referred to as a closed-end fund) refers to a fund in which the total amount of fund shares is fixed and unchanged during the term of the fund contract, and fund share holders may not apply for redemption; a fund that adopts the open-end mode of operation (hereinafter referred to as an open-end fund) refers to a fund in which the total amount of fund shares is not fixed, and fund shares may be purchased or redeemed at the time and place agreed in the fund contract.  
The measures for the offering, trading, purchase and redemption of fund shares of a fund adopting other modes of operation shall be separately prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　基金份额持有人享有下列权利：  
（一）分享基金财产收益；  
（二）参与分配清算后的剩余基金财产；  
（三）依法转让或者申请赎回其持有的基金份额；  
（四）按照规定要求召开基金份额持有人大会或者召集基金份额持有人大会；  
（五）对基金份额持有人大会审议事项行使表决权；  
（六）对基金管理人、基金托管人、基金服务机构损害其合法权益的行为依法提起诉讼；  
（七）基金合同约定的其他权利。  
公开募集基金的基金份额持有人有权查阅或者复制公开披露的基金信息资料；非公开募集基金的基金份额持有人对涉及自身利益的情况，有权查阅基金的财务会计账簿等财务资料。

**Article 46** Fund share holders shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) To share in the income of the fund property;  
(2) To participate in the distribution of the remaining fund property after liquidation;  
(3) To transfer or apply for redemption of the fund shares they hold according to law;  
(4) To request the convening of a fund share holders' meeting or to convene a fund share holders' meeting in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) To exercise the right to vote on matters considered by the fund share holders' meeting;  
(6) To institute legal proceedings according to law against any act of the fund manager, fund custodian or fund service institution that damages their lawful rights and interests;  
(7) Other rights as agreed in the fund contract.  
Fund share holders of a publicly offered fund shall have the right to consult or copy the publicly disclosed fund information and materials; fund share holders of a non-publicly offered fund shall have the right to consult financial materials such as the fund's accounting books and records in respect of matters involving their own interests.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　基金份额持有人大会由全体基金份额持有人组成，行使下列职权：  
（一）决定基金扩募或者延长基金合同期限；  
（二）决定修改基金合同的重要内容或者提前终止基金合同；  
（三）决定更换基金管理人、基金托管人；  
（四）决定调整基金管理人、基金托管人的报酬标准；  
（五）基金合同约定的其他职权。

**Article 47** The fund share holders' meeting shall be composed of all fund share holders and shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To decide on the expansion of the fund or the extension of the term of the fund contract;  
(2) To decide on the modification of important contents of the fund contract or the early termination of the fund contract;  
(3) To decide on the replacement of the fund manager or fund custodian;  
(4) To decide on the adjustment of the remuneration standards of the fund manager and fund custodian;  
(5) Other functions and powers as agreed in the fund contract.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　按照基金合同约定，基金份额持有人大会可以设立日常机构，行使下列职权：  
（一）召集基金份额持有人大会；  
（二）提请更换基金管理人、基金托管人；  
（三）监督基金管理人的投资运作、基金托管人的托管活动；  
（四）提请调整基金管理人、基金托管人的报酬标准；  
（五）基金合同约定的其他职权。  
前款规定的日常机构，由基金份额持有人大会选举产生的人员组成；其议事规则，由基金合同约定。

**Article 48** In accordance with the provisions of the fund contract, the fund share holders' meeting may establish a standing body to exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To convene the fund share holders' meeting;  
(2) To propose the replacement of the fund manager or fund custodian;  
(3) To supervise the investment operations of the fund manager and the custody activities of the fund custodian;  
(4) To propose the adjustment of the remuneration standards of the fund manager and fund custodian;  
(5) Other functions and powers as agreed in the fund contract.  
The standing body prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be composed of persons elected by the fund share holders' meeting; its rules of procedure shall be stipulated in the fund contract.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　基金份额持有人大会及其日常机构不得直接参与或者干涉基金的投资管理活动。

**Article 49** The fund share holders' meeting and its standing body may not directly participate in or interfere with the investment management activities of the fund.

### 第五章　基金的公开募集

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公开募集基金，应当经国务院证券监督管理机构注册。未经注册，不得公开或者变相公开募集基金。  
前款所称公开募集基金，包括向不特定对象募集资金、向特定对象募集资金累计超过二百人，以及法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。  
公开募集基金应当由基金管理人管理，基金托管人托管。

**Article 50** A publicly offered fund shall be registered with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. Without registration, no person may publicly or in disguised form publicly offer a fund.  
The term 'publicly offered fund' as mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes the raising of funds from unspecified persons, the raising of funds from specified persons with an aggregate of more than 200 persons, and other circumstances prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
A publicly offered fund shall be managed by a fund manager and custodied by a fund custodian.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　注册公开募集基金，由拟任基金管理人向国务院证券监督管理机构提交下列文件：  
（一）申请报告；  
（二）基金合同草案；  
（三）基金托管协议草案；  
（四）招募说明书草案；  
（五）律师事务所出具的法律意见书；  
（六）国务院证券监督管理机构规定提交的其他文件。

**Article 51** To register a publicly offered fund, the proposed fund manager shall submit the following documents to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) An application report;  
(2) A draft fund contract;  
(3) A draft fund custody agreement;  
(4) A draft prospectus;  
(5) A legal opinion issued by a law firm;  
(6) Other documents required to be submitted as prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　公开募集基金的基金合同应当包括下列内容：  
（一）募集基金的目的和基金名称；  
（二）基金管理人、基金托管人的名称和住所；  
（三）基金的运作方式；  
（四）封闭式基金的基金份额总额和基金合同期限，或者开放式基金的最低募集份额总额；  
（五）确定基金份额发售日期、价格和费用的原则；  
（六）基金份额持有人、基金管理人和基金托管人的权利、义务；  
（七）基金份额持有人大会召集、议事及表决的程序和规则；  
（八）基金份额发售、交易、申购、赎回的程序、时间、地点、费用计算方式，以及给付赎回款项的时间和方式；  
（九）基金收益分配原则、执行方式；  
（十）基金管理人、基金托管人报酬的提取、支付方式与比例；  
（十一）与基金财产管理、运用有关的其他费用的提取、支付方式；  
（十二）基金财产的投资方向和投资限制；  
（十三）基金资产净值的计算方法和公告方式；  
（十四）基金募集未达到法定要求的处理方式；  
（十五）基金合同解除和终止的事由、程序以及基金财产清算方式；  
（十六）争议解决方式；  
（十七）当事人约定的其他事项。

**Article 52** The fund contract of a publicly offered fund shall include the following contents:  
(1) The purpose of the fund offering and the name of the fund;  
(2) The names and domiciles of the fund manager and fund custodian;  
(3) The mode of operation of the fund;  
(4) The total amount of fund shares of a closed-end fund and the term of the fund contract, or the minimum amount of shares to be offered of an open-end fund;  
(5) The principles for determining the date, price and fees for the offering of fund shares;  
(6) The rights and obligations of the fund share holders, fund manager and fund custodian;  
(7) The procedures and rules for the convening, deliberation and voting of the fund share holders' meeting;  
(8) The procedures, time, place, fee calculation method for the offering, trading, purchase and redemption of fund shares, and the time and method for payment of redemption proceeds;  
(9) The principles and implementation methods for the distribution of fund income;  
(10) The method and proportion for the withdrawal and payment of remuneration of the fund manager and fund custodian;  
(11) The method for the withdrawal and payment of other expenses related to the management and use of fund property;  
(12) The investment direction and investment restrictions of fund property;  
(13) The calculation method and announcement method of the net asset value of the fund;  
(14) The method for handling the failure to meet the statutory requirements for fund offering;  
(15) The causes and procedures for the dissolution and termination of the fund contract and the liquidation method of fund property;  
(16) The method for dispute resolution;  
(17) Other matters agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　公开募集基金的基金招募说明书应当包括下列内容：  
（一）基金募集申请的准予注册文件名称和注册日期；  
（二）基金管理人、基金托管人的基本情况；  
（三）基金合同和基金托管协议的内容摘要；  
（四）基金份额的发售日期、价格、费用和期限；  
（五）基金份额的发售方式、发售机构及登记机构名称；  
（六）出具法律意见书的律师事务所和审计基金财产的会计师事务所的名称和住所；  
（七）基金管理人、基金托管人报酬及其他有关费用的提取、支付方式与比例；  
（八）风险警示内容；  
（九）国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他内容。

**Article 53** The prospectus of a publicly offered fund shall include the following contents:  
(1) The name of the document approving the registration of the fund offering application and the date of registration;  
(2) The basic information of the fund manager and fund custodian;  
(3) A summary of the contents of the fund contract and the fund custody agreement;  
(4) The offering date, price, fees and term of fund shares;  
(5) The offering method, offering institution and name of the registration institution of fund shares;  
(6) The names and domiciles of the law firm issuing the legal opinion and the accounting firm auditing the fund property;  
(7) The method and proportion for the withdrawal and payment of remuneration of the fund manager and fund custodian and other relevant expenses;  
(8) Risk warning contents;  
(9) Other contents as prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院证券监督管理机构应当自受理公开募集基金的募集注册申请之日起六个月内依照法律、行政法规及国务院证券监督管理机构的规定进行审查，作出注册或者不予注册的决定，并通知申请人；不予注册的，应当说明理由。

**Article 54** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of accepting the application for registration of a publicly offered fund, conduct review in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, make a decision to register or not to register, and notify the applicant; if it decides not to register, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　基金募集申请经注册后，方可发售基金份额。  
基金份额的发售，由基金管理人或者其委托的基金销售机构办理。

**Article 55** After the application for fund offering is registered, the fund shares may be offered.  
The offering of fund shares shall be handled by the fund manager or the fund sales institution entrusted by it.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　基金管理人应当在基金份额发售的三日前公布招募说明书、基金合同及其他有关文件。  
前款规定的文件应当真实、准确、完整。  
对基金募集所进行的宣传推介活动，应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得有[本法第七十七条](#art-77)所列行为。

**Article 56** The fund manager shall, three days before the offering of fund shares, publish the prospectus, fund contract and other relevant documents.  
The documents prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be truthful, accurate and complete.  
Any promotional or publicity activities conducted for the fund offering shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall not involve any act listed in Article 77 of this Law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　基金管理人应当自收到准予注册文件之日起六个月内进行基金募集。超过六个月开始募集，原注册的事项未发生实质性变化的，应当报国务院证券监督管理机构备案；发生实质性变化的，应当向国务院证券监督管理机构重新提交注册申请。  
基金募集不得超过国务院证券监督管理机构准予注册的基金募集期限。基金募集期限自基金份额发售之日起计算。

**Article 57** The fund manager shall conduct the fund offering within six months from the date of receipt of the document approving registration. If the offering commences after six months, and the registered matters have not undergone substantive changes, it shall report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation; if substantive changes have occurred, it shall submit a new registration application to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The fund offering shall not exceed the fund offering period approved by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. The fund offering period shall be calculated from the date on which the fund shares are offered.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　基金募集期限届满，封闭式基金募集的基金份额总额达到准予注册规模的百分之八十以上，开放式基金募集的基金份额总额超过准予注册的最低募集份额总额，并且基金份额持有人人数符合国务院证券监督管理机构规定的，基金管理人应当自募集期限届满之日起十日内聘请法定验资机构验资，自收到验资报告之日起十日内，向国务院证券监督管理机构提交验资报告，办理基金备案手续，并予以公告。

**Article 58** Upon expiration of the fund offering period, if the total amount of fund shares raised for a closed-end fund reaches 80% or more of the registered size, or the total amount of fund shares raised for an open-end fund exceeds the minimum amount of shares to be offered as registered, and the number of fund share holders meets the requirements prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, the fund manager shall, within ten days from the date of expiration of the offering period, engage a statutory capital verification institution to verify the capital, and within ten days from the date of receipt of the capital verification report, submit the capital verification report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, handle the fund filing procedures, and make an announcement.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　基金募集期间募集的资金应当存入专门账户，在基金募集行为结束前，任何人不得动用。

**Article 59** Funds raised during the fund offering period shall be deposited in a special account, and no person may use them before the conclusion of the fund offering activities.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　投资人交纳认购的基金份额的款项时，基金合同成立；基金管理人依照[本法第五十八条](#art-58)的规定向国务院证券监督管理机构办理基金备案手续，基金合同生效。  
基金募集期限届满，不能满足[本法第五十八条](#art-58)规定的条件的，基金管理人应当承担下列责任：  
（一）以其固有财产承担因募集行为而产生的债务和费用；  
（二）在基金募集期限届满后三十日内返还投资人已交纳的款项，并加计银行同期存款利息。

**Article 60** When an investor pays the subscription amount for fund shares, the fund contract shall be established; when the fund manager handles the fund filing procedures with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with Article 58 of this Law, the fund contract shall take effect.  
If, upon expiration of the fund offering period, the conditions prescribed in Article 58 of this Law cannot be satisfied, the fund manager shall assume the following responsibilities:  
(1) To bear the debts and expenses arising from the offering activities with its own property;  
(2) To refund the amounts paid by investors within 30 days after the expiration of the fund offering period, plus interest at the bank deposit rate for the same period.

### 第六章　公开募集基金的基金份额的交易、申购与赎回

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　申请基金份额上市交易，基金管理人应当向证券交易所提出申请，证券交易所依法审核同意的，双方应当签订上市协议。

**Article 61** To apply for listing and trading of fund units, the fund manager shall submit an application to the stock exchange, and if the stock exchange approves the application after examination according to law, the parties shall sign a listing agreement.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　基金份额上市交易，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）基金的募集符合本法规定；  
（二）基金合同期限为五年以上；  
（三）基金募集金额不低于二亿元人民币；  
（四）基金份额持有人不少于一千人；  
（五）基金份额上市交易规则规定的其他条件。

**Article 62** The listing and trading of fund units shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the offering of the fund complies with the provisions of this Law;  
(2) the term of the fund contract is five years or more;  
(3) the total amount raised for the fund is not less than 200 million yuan;  
(4) the number of fund unit holders is not less than 1,000;  
(5) other conditions provided for in the rules on listing and trading of fund units.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　基金份额上市交易规则由证券交易所制定，报国务院证券监督管理机构批准。

**Article 63** The rules on listing and trading of fund units shall be formulated by the stock exchange and submitted to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　基金份额上市交易后，有下列情形之一的，由证券交易所终止其上市交易，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案：  
（一）不再具备[本法第六十二条](#art-62)规定的上市交易条件；  
（二）基金合同期限届满；  
（三）基金份额持有人大会决定提前终止上市交易；  
（四）基金合同约定的或者基金份额上市交易规则规定的终止上市交易的其他情形。

**Article 64** After the listing and trading of fund units, if any of the following circumstances occurs, the stock exchange shall terminate the listing and trading thereof, and shall report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) the conditions for listing and trading as provided for in Article 62 of this Law are no longer met;  
(2) the term of the fund contract expires;  
(3) the fund unit holders' meeting decides to terminate the listing and trading prematurely;  
(4) other circumstances for termination of listing and trading as stipulated in the fund contract or as provided for in the rules on listing and trading of fund units.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　开放式基金的基金份额的申购、赎回、登记，由基金管理人或者其委托的基金服务机构办理。

**Article 65** The subscription, redemption and registration of units of an open-ended fund shall be handled by the fund manager or a fund service institution authorized by it.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　基金管理人应当在每个工作日办理基金份额的申购、赎回业务；基金合同另有约定的，从其约定。  
投资人交付申购款项，申购成立；基金份额登记机构确认基金份额时，申购生效。  
基金份额持有人递交赎回申请，赎回成立；基金份额登记机构确认赎回时，赎回生效。

**Article 66** The fund manager shall handle subscription and redemption of fund units on each business day; if the fund contract stipulates otherwise, such stipulation shall prevail.  
When an investor pays the subscription amount, the subscription is established; when the fund unit registration institution confirms the fund units, the subscription takes effect.  
When a fund unit holder submits a redemption application, the redemption is established; when the fund unit registration institution confirms the redemption, the redemption takes effect.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　基金管理人应当按时支付赎回款项，但是下列情形除外：  
（一）因不可抗力导致基金管理人不能支付赎回款项；  
（二）证券交易场所依法决定临时停市，导致基金管理人无法计算当日基金资产净值；  
（三）基金合同约定的其他特殊情形。  
发生上述情形之一的，基金管理人应当在当日报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。  
本条第一款规定的情形消失后，基金管理人应当及时支付赎回款项。

**Article 67** The fund manager shall pay redemption proceeds on time, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) the fund manager is unable to pay redemption proceeds due to force majeure;  
(2) the securities trading venue decides on a temporary suspension of trading according to law, causing the fund manager to be unable to calculate the net asset value of the fund on that day;  
(3) other special circumstances stipulated in the fund contract.  
If any of the above circumstances occurs, the fund manager shall report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation on the same day.  
After the circumstances provided for in the first paragraph of this Article have disappeared, the fund manager shall pay redemption proceeds in a timely manner.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　开放式基金应当保持足够的现金或者政府债券，以备支付基金份额持有人的赎回款项。基金财产中应当保持的现金或者政府债券的具体比例，由国务院证券监督管理机构规定。

**Article 68** An open-ended fund shall maintain sufficient cash or government bonds to meet redemption proceeds due to fund unit holders. The specific proportion of cash or government bonds to be maintained in the fund property shall be prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　基金份额的申购、赎回价格，依据申购、赎回日基金份额净值加、减有关费用计算。

**Article 69** The subscription and redemption prices of fund units shall be calculated based on the net asset value per unit on the subscription or redemption date, plus or minus relevant fees.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　基金份额净值计价出现错误时，基金管理人应当立即纠正，并采取合理的措施防止损失进一步扩大。计价错误达到基金份额净值百分之零点五时，基金管理人应当公告，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。  
因基金份额净值计价错误造成基金份额持有人损失的，基金份额持有人有权要求基金管理人、基金托管人予以赔偿。

**Article 70** If an error occurs in the valuation of net asset value per unit, the fund manager shall immediately correct it and take reasonable measures to prevent further expansion of losses. When the valuation error reaches 0.5% of the net asset value per unit, the fund manager shall make a public announcement and report to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.  
If fund unit holders suffer losses due to an error in the valuation of net asset value per unit, the fund unit holders shall have the right to demand compensation from the fund manager or the fund custodian.

### 第七章　公开募集基金的投资与信息披露

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　基金管理人运用基金财产进行证券投资，除国务院证券监督管理机构另有规定外，应当采用资产组合的方式。  
资产组合的具体方式和投资比例，依照本法和国务院证券监督管理机构的规定在基金合同中约定。

**Article 71** When the fund manager uses fund property for securities investment, it shall adopt an asset portfolio approach, unless otherwise prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The specific method of asset portfolio and investment ratios shall be stipulated in the fund contract in accordance with this Law and the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　基金财产应当用于下列投资：  
（一）上市交易的股票、债券；  
（二）国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他证券及其衍生品种。

**Article 72** Fund property shall be used for the following investments:  
(1) listed and traded stocks and bonds;  
(2) other securities and their derivatives prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　基金财产不得用于下列投资或者活动：  
（一）承销证券；  
（二）违反规定向他人贷款或者提供担保；  
（三）从事承担无限责任的投资；  
（四）买卖其他基金份额，但是国务院证券监督管理机构另有规定的除外；  
（五）向基金管理人、基金托管人出资；  
（六）从事内幕交易、操纵证券交易价格及其他不正当的证券交易活动；  
（七）法律、行政法规和国务院证券监督管理机构规定禁止的其他活动。  
运用基金财产买卖基金管理人、基金托管人及其控股股东、实际控制人或者与其有其他重大利害关系的公司发行的证券或承销期内承销的证券，或者从事其他重大关联交易的，应当遵循基金份额持有人利益优先的原则，防范利益冲突，符合国务院证券监督管理机构的规定，并履行信息披露义务。

**Article 73** Fund property shall not be used for the following investments or activities:  
(1) underwriting securities;  
(2) making loans to others or providing guarantees in violation of regulations;  
(3) making investments that entail unlimited liability;  
(4) buying or selling other fund units, unless otherwise prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(5) contributing capital to the fund manager or fund custodian;  
(6) engaging in insider trading, manipulating securities trading prices, or other improper securities trading activities;  
(7) other activities prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If fund property is used to buy or sell securities issued by the fund manager, fund custodian, their controlling shareholders, actual controllers, or companies with which they have other material conflicts of interest, or securities underwritten during the underwriting period, or to engage in other material related-party transactions, the principle of prioritizing the interests of fund unit holders shall be followed, conflicts of interest shall be prevented, and the provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall be complied with, and the disclosure obligations shall be fulfilled.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　基金管理人、基金托管人和其他基金信息披露义务人应当依法披露基金信息，并保证所披露信息的真实性、准确性和完整性。

**Article 74** The fund manager, fund custodian, and other fund information disclosure obligors shall disclose fund information according to law, and shall guarantee the truthfulness, accuracy, and completeness of the disclosed information.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　基金信息披露义务人应当确保应予披露的基金信息在国务院证券监督管理机构规定时间内披露，并保证投资人能够按照基金合同约定的时间和方式查阅或者复制公开披露的信息资料。

**Article 75** Fund information disclosure obligors shall ensure that fund information subject to disclosure is disclosed within the time limit prescribed by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, and shall guarantee that investors can consult or copy publicly disclosed information materials at the time and in the manner stipulated in the fund contract.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　公开披露的基金信息包括：  
（一）基金招募说明书、基金合同、基金托管协议；  
（二）基金募集情况；  
（三）基金份额上市交易公告书；  
（四）基金资产净值、基金份额净值；  
（五）基金份额申购、赎回价格；  
（六）基金财产的资产组合季度报告、财务会计报告及中期和年度基金报告；  
（七）临时报告；  
（八）基金份额持有人大会决议；  
（九）基金管理人、基金托管人的专门基金托管部门的重大人事变动；  
（十）涉及基金财产、基金管理业务、基金托管业务的诉讼或者仲裁；  
（十一）国务院证券监督管理机构规定应予披露的其他信息。

**Article 76** Publicly disclosed fund information includes:  
(1) the fund prospectus, fund contract, and fund custody agreement;  
(2) the fund offering status;  
(3) the listing and trading announcement of fund units;  
(4) the net asset value of the fund and the net asset value per unit;  
(5) the subscription and redemption prices of fund units;  
(6) quarterly portfolio reports, financial accounting reports, and interim and annual fund reports of fund property;  
(7) interim reports;  
(8) resolutions of the fund unit holders' meeting;  
(9) major personnel changes in the fund manager or the specialized fund custody department of the fund custodian;  
(10) litigation or arbitration involving fund property, fund management business, or fund custody business;  
(11) other information that the securities regulatory authority of the State Council requires to be disclosed.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　公开披露基金信息，不得有下列行为：  
（一）虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏；  
（二）对证券投资业绩进行预测；  
（三）违规承诺收益或者承担损失；  
（四）诋毁其他基金管理人、基金托管人或者基金销售机构；  
（五）法律、行政法规和国务院证券监督管理机构规定禁止的其他行为。

**Article 77** When publicly disclosing fund information, the following acts shall not be committed:  
(1) false records, misleading statements, or material omissions;  
(2) making predictions on securities investment performance;  
(3) making improper promises of returns or bearing losses;  
(4) slandering other fund managers, fund custodians, or fund sales institutions;  
(5) other acts prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第八章　公开募集基金的基金合同的变更、终止与基金财产清算

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　按照基金合同的约定或者基金份额持有人大会的决议，基金可以转换运作方式或者与其他基金合并。

**Article 78** According to the stipulations of the fund contract or the resolution of the fund unit holders' meeting, a fund may change its operation method or merge with other funds.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　封闭式基金扩募或者延长基金合同期限，应当符合下列条件，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案：  
（一）基金运营业绩良好；  
（二）基金管理人最近二年内没有因违法违规行为受到行政处罚或者刑事处罚；  
（三）基金份额持有人大会决议通过；  
（四）本法规定的其他条件。

**Article 79** A closed-end fund's expansion of offering or extension of the fund contract term shall meet the following conditions and be reported to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) the fund has good operating performance;  
(2) the fund manager has not been subject to administrative penalty or criminal punishment for illegal or non-compliant acts within the last two years;  
(3) the resolution of the fund unit holders' meeting is passed;  
(4) other conditions provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　有下列情形之一的，基金合同终止：  
（一）基金合同期限届满而未延期；  
（二）基金份额持有人大会决定终止；  
（三）基金管理人、基金托管人职责终止，在六个月内没有新基金管理人、新基金托管人承接；  
（四）基金合同约定的其他情形。

**Article 80** A fund contract shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the term of the fund contract expires and is not extended;  
(2) the fund unit holders' meeting decides to terminate;  
(3) the responsibilities of the fund manager or fund custodian are terminated, and no new fund manager or fund custodian takes over within six months;  
(4) other circumstances stipulated in the fund contract.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　基金合同终止时，基金管理人应当组织清算组对基金财产进行清算。  
清算组由基金管理人、基金托管人以及相关的中介服务机构组成。  
清算组作出的清算报告经会计师事务所审计，律师事务所出具法律意见书后，报国务院证券监督管理机构备案并公告。

**Article 81** Upon termination of the fund contract, the fund management institution shall organize a liquidation group to liquidate the fund property.  
The liquidation group shall be composed of the fund management institution, the fund custodian, and relevant intermediary service institutions.  
After the liquidation report prepared by the liquidation group is audited by an accounting firm and a legal opinion is issued by a law firm, it shall be filed with the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for the record and announced.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　清算后的剩余基金财产，应当按照基金份额持有人所持份额比例进行分配。

**Article 82** The remaining fund property after liquidation shall be distributed in proportion to the fund shares held by the fund share holders.

### 第九章　公开募集基金的基金份额持有人权利行使

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　基金份额持有人大会由基金管理人召集。基金份额持有人大会设立日常机构的，由该日常机构召集；该日常机构未召集的，由基金管理人召集。基金管理人未按规定召集或者不能召集的，由基金托管人召集。  
代表基金份额百分之十以上的基金份额持有人就同一事项要求召开基金份额持有人大会，而基金份额持有人大会的日常机构、基金管理人、基金托管人都不召集的，代表基金份额百分之十以上的基金份额持有人有权自行召集，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。

**Article 83** The fund share holders' meeting shall be convened by the fund management institution. If a daily operating body is established for the fund share holders' meeting, such daily operating body shall convene the meeting; if the daily operating body fails to convene the meeting, the fund management institution shall convene it. If the fund management institution fails to convene the meeting as required or is unable to do so, the fund custodian shall convene it.  
If fund share holders representing more than ten percent of the total fund shares request to convene a meeting of fund share holders on the same matter, and the daily operating body of the fund share holders' meeting, the fund management institution, and the fund custodian all fail to convene it, such fund share holders shall have the right to convene the meeting on their own and shall report to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　召开基金份额持有人大会，召集人应当至少提前三十日公告基金份额持有人大会的召开时间、会议形式、审议事项、议事程序和表决方式等事项。  
基金份额持有人大会不得就未经公告的事项进行表决。

**Article 84** When convening a fund share holders' meeting, the convener shall announce the time, form, matters for deliberation, procedure, and voting method of the meeting at least thirty days in advance.  
The fund share holders' meeting shall not vote on matters that have not been announced.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　基金份额持有人大会可以采取现场方式召开，也可以采取通讯等方式召开。  
每一基金份额具有一票表决权，基金份额持有人可以委托代理人出席基金份额持有人大会并行使表决权。

**Article 85** The fund share holders' meeting may be held in person or by means of communication, among others.  
Each fund share shall have one voting right, and a fund share holder may appoint an authorized agent to attend the meeting and exercise the voting rights.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　基金份额持有人大会应当有代表二分之一以上基金份额的持有人参加，方可召开。  
参加基金份额持有人大会的持有人的基金份额低于前款规定比例的，召集人可以在原公告的基金份额持有人大会召开时间的三个月以后、六个月以内，就原定审议事项重新召集基金份额持有人大会。重新召集的基金份额持有人大会应当有代表三分之一以上基金份额的持有人参加，方可召开。  
基金份额持有人大会就审议事项作出决定，应当经参加大会的基金份额持有人所持表决权的二分之一以上通过；但是，转换基金的运作方式、更换基金管理人或者基金托管人、提前终止基金合同、与其他基金合并，应当经参加大会的基金份额持有人所持表决权的三分之二以上通过。  
基金份额持有人大会决定的事项，应当依法报国务院证券监督管理机构备案，并予以公告。

**Article 86** A fund share holders' meeting may be convened only if holders representing more than one-half of the total fund shares attend.  
If the fund shares held by the attendees at the meeting are less than the proportion specified in the preceding paragraph, the convener may reconvene the meeting on the originally scheduled matters within three to six months after the originally announced meeting time. The reconvened meeting may be held only if holders representing more than one-third of the total fund shares attend.  
A decision made by the fund share holders' meeting on the matters under deliberation shall be adopted by more than one-half of the voting rights held by the attending fund share holders; however, decisions on changing the fund's operation method, replacing the fund management institution or the fund custodian, terminating the fund contract ahead of time, or merging with other funds shall be adopted by more than two-thirds of the voting rights held by the attending fund share holders.  
Matters decided by the fund share holders' meeting shall be filed with the securities regulatory authority under the State Council for the record according to law and shall be announced.

### 第十章　非公开募集基金

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　非公开募集基金应当向合格投资者募集，合格投资者累计不得超过二百人。  
前款所称合格投资者，是指达到规定资产规模或者收入水平，并且具备相应的风险识别能力和风险承担能力、其基金份额认购金额不低于规定限额的单位和个人。  
合格投资者的具体标准由国务院证券监督管理机构规定。

**Article 87** Non-publicly offered funds shall be raised from qualified investors, and the total number of qualified investors shall not exceed 200.  
"Qualified investors" as referred to in the preceding paragraph means units and individuals that meet the prescribed asset scale or income level, have corresponding risk identification and risk tolerance capabilities, and whose subscription amount for fund shares is not less than the prescribed minimum.  
The specific standards for qualified investors shall be prescribed by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　除基金合同另有约定外，非公开募集基金应当由基金托管人托管。

**Article 88** Unless otherwise agreed in the fund contract, non-publicly offered funds shall be custodied by a fund custodian.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　担任非公开募集基金的基金管理人，应当按照规定向基金行业协会履行登记手续，报送基本情况。

**Article 89** A fund management institution acting as a manager of a non-publicly offered fund shall go through registration formalities with the fund industry association according to the provisions and submit its basic information.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　未经登记，任何单位或者个人不得使用“基金”或者“基金管理”字样或者近似名称进行证券投资活动；但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 90** Without registration, no unit or individual may use the words "fund" or "fund management" or similar names to engage in securities investment activities; except as otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　非公开募集基金，不得向合格投资者之外的单位和个人募集资金，不得通过报刊、电台、电视台、互联网等公众传播媒体或者讲座、报告会、分析会等方式向不特定对象宣传推介。

**Article 91** Non-publicly offered funds shall not raise funds from units or individuals other than qualified investors, and shall not promote or solicit to unspecified persons through public media such as newspapers, radio, television, the Internet, or through lectures, reports, seminars, etc.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　非公开募集基金，应当制定并签订基金合同。基金合同应当包括下列内容：  
（一）基金份额持有人、基金管理人、基金托管人的权利、义务；  
（二）基金的运作方式；  
（三）基金的出资方式、数额和认缴期限；  
（四）基金的投资范围、投资策略和投资限制；  
（五）基金收益分配原则、执行方式；  
（六）基金承担的有关费用；  
（七）基金信息提供的内容、方式；  
（八）基金份额的认购、赎回或者转让的程序和方式；  
（九）基金合同变更、解除和终止的事由、程序；  
（十）基金财产清算方式；  
（十一）当事人约定的其他事项。  
基金份额持有人转让基金份额的，应当符合[本法第八十七条](#art-87)、第九十一条的规定。

**Article 92** For non-publicly offered funds, a fund contract shall be formulated and signed. The fund contract shall include the following contents:  
(1) Rights and obligations of fund share holders, the fund management institution, and the fund custodian;  
(2) The fund's operation method;  
(3) The method, amount, and subscription period of capital contributions to the fund;  
(4) The fund's investment scope, investment strategy, and investment restrictions;  
(5) Principles and methods for distribution of fund income;  
(6) Relevant expenses borne by the fund;  
(7) Content and method of fund information provision;  
(8) Procedures and methods for subscription, redemption, or transfer of fund shares;  
(9) Causes and procedures for modification, rescission, and termination of the fund contract;  
(10) Method of liquidation of fund property;  
(11) Other matters agreed upon by the parties.  
If fund share holders transfer fund shares, they shall comply with the provisions of Articles 87 and 91 of this Law.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　按照基金合同约定，非公开募集基金可以由部分基金份额持有人作为基金管理人负责基金的投资管理活动，并在基金财产不足以清偿其债务时对基金财产的债务承担无限连带责任。  
前款规定的非公开募集基金，其基金合同还应载明：  
（一）承担无限连带责任的基金份额持有人和其他基金份额持有人的姓名或者名称、住所；  
（二）承担无限连带责任的基金份额持有人的除名条件和更换程序；  
（三）基金份额持有人增加、退出的条件、程序以及相关责任；  
（四）承担无限连带责任的基金份额持有人和其他基金份额持有人的转换程序。

**Article 93** In accordance with the fund contract, for a non-publicly offered fund, some fund share holders may act as the fund management institution responsible for the fund's investment management activities and shall assume unlimited joint and several liability for the fund property's debts if the fund property is insufficient to pay off its debts.  
For the non-publicly offered fund specified in the preceding paragraph, the fund contract shall also specify:  
(1) The names or titles and domiciles of the fund share holders assuming unlimited joint and several liability and the other fund share holders;  
(2) Conditions for removal and procedures for replacement of the fund share holders assuming unlimited joint and several liability;  
(3) Conditions, procedures, and related responsibilities for the addition and withdrawal of fund share holders;  
(4) Procedures for conversion between fund share holders assuming unlimited joint and several liability and the other fund share holders.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　非公开募集基金募集完毕，基金管理人应当向基金行业协会备案。对募集的资金总额或者基金份额持有人的人数达到规定标准的基金，基金行业协会应当向国务院证券监督管理机构报告。  
非公开募集基金财产的证券投资，包括买卖公开发行的股份有限公司股票、债券、基金份额，以及国务院证券监督管理机构规定的其他证券及其衍生品种。

**Article 94** After the fund raising of non-publicly offered funds is completed, the fund management institution shall file with the fund industry association for the record. For funds whose total raised capital or number of fund share holders reaches the prescribed standards, the fund industry association shall report to the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.  
The securities investment of non-publicly offered fund property includes buying and selling publicly issued shares of joint stock limited companies, bonds, fund shares, and other securities and their derivatives prescribed by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　基金管理人、基金托管人应当按照基金合同的约定，向基金份额持有人提供基金信息。

**Article 95** The fund management institution and the fund custodian shall provide fund information to fund share holders in accordance with the fund contract.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　专门从事非公开募集基金管理业务的基金管理人，其股东、高级管理人员、经营期限、管理的基金资产规模等符合规定条件的，经国务院证券监督管理机构核准，可以从事公开募集基金管理业务。

**Article 96** If the fund management institution that specializes in managing non-publicly offered funds meets the prescribed conditions in terms of its shareholders, senior officers, term of operation, and scale of managed fund assets, it may, upon approval by the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, engage in managing publicly offered funds.

### 第十一章　基金服务机构

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　从事公开募集基金的销售、销售支付、份额登记、估值、投资顾问、评价、信息技术系统服务等基金服务业务的机构，应当按照国务院证券监督管理机构的规定进行注册或者备案。

**Article 97** Institutions engaging in fund service businesses such as sales, sales payment, share registration, valuation, investment advisory, evaluation, and information technology system services for publicly offered funds shall register or file for the record in accordance with the provisions of the securities regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　基金销售机构应当向投资人充分揭示投资风险，并根据投资人的风险承担能力销售不同风险等级的基金产品。

**Article 98** Fund sales institutions shall fully disclose investment risks to investors and sell fund products of different risk levels according to the investors' risk tolerance.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　基金销售支付机构应当按照规定办理基金销售结算资金的划付，确保基金销售结算资金安全、及时划付。

**Article 99** Fund sales payment institutions shall handle the transfer of fund sales settlement funds in accordance with provisions, ensuring the safe and timely transfer of such funds.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　基金销售结算资金、基金份额独立于基金销售机构、基金销售支付机构或者基金份额登记机构的自有财产。基金销售机构、基金销售支付机构或者基金份额登记机构破产或者清算时，基金销售结算资金、基金份额不属于其破产财产或者清算财产。非因投资人本身的债务或者法律规定的其他情形，不得查封、冻结、扣划或者强制执行基金销售结算资金、基金份额。  
基金销售机构、基金销售支付机构、基金份额登记机构应当确保基金销售结算资金、基金份额的安全、独立，禁止任何单位或者个人以任何形式挪用基金销售结算资金、基金份额。

**Article 100** Fund sales settlement funds and fund shares shall be independent from the proprietary property of the fund sales institution, fund sales payment institution, or fund share registration institution. In the event of bankruptcy or liquidation of the fund sales institution, fund sales payment institution, or fund share registration institution, the fund sales settlement funds and fund shares shall not be part of the bankruptcy or liquidation property. Unless due to the investor's own debts or other circumstances prescribed by law, the fund sales settlement funds and fund shares shall not be attached, frozen, deducted, or subjected to compulsory execution.  
Fund sales institutions, fund sales payment institutions, and fund share registration institutions shall ensure the safety and independence of fund sales settlement funds and fund shares. No unit or individual may misappropriate fund sales settlement funds or fund shares in any form.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　基金管理人可以委托基金服务机构代为办理基金的份额登记、核算、估值、投资顾问等事项，基金托管人可以委托基金服务机构代为办理基金的核算、估值、复核等事项，但基金管理人、基金托管人依法应当承担的责任不因委托而免除。

**Article 101** A fund manager may authorize a fund service institution to handle on its behalf matters such as fund share registration, accounting, valuation, and investment advisory, and a fund custodian may authorize a fund service institution to handle on its behalf matters such as fund accounting, valuation, and review; provided that the responsibilities that the fund manager and the fund custodian shall bear according to law shall not be exempted by such authorization.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　基金份额登记机构以电子介质登记的数据，是基金份额持有人权利归属的根据。基金份额持有人以基金份额出质的，质权自基金份额登记机构办理出质登记时设立。  
基金份额登记机构应当妥善保存登记数据，并将基金份额持有人名称、身份信息及基金份额明细等数据备份至国务院证券监督管理机构认定的机构。其保存期限自基金账户销户之日起不得少于二十年。  
基金份额登记机构应当保证登记数据的真实、准确、完整，不得隐匿、伪造、篡改或者毁损。

**Article 102** The data registered by a fund share registration institution through electronic media shall be the basis for the attribution of rights of fund share holders. Where a fund share holder pledges fund shares, the pledge shall be established at the time when the fund share registration institution handles the pledge registration.  
  
The fund share registration institution shall properly preserve the registration data, and shall back up the data including the names of fund share holders, identity information, and details of fund shares to an institution recognized by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. The retention period shall be no less than twenty years from the date of cancellation of the fund account.  
  
The fund share registration institution shall ensure the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the registration data, and shall not conceal, forge, tamper with, or destroy the data.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　基金投资顾问机构及其从业人员提供基金投资顾问服务，应当具有合理的依据，对其服务能力和经营业绩进行如实陈述，不得以任何方式承诺或者保证投资收益，不得损害服务对象的合法权益。

**Article 103** A fund investment advisory institution and its practitioners providing fund investment advisory services shall have reasonable grounds, make truthful statements about their service capability and business performance, and shall not in any manner promise or guarantee investment returns, nor harm the lawful rights and interests of the service recipients.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　基金评价机构及其从业人员应当客观公正，按照依法制定的业务规则开展基金评价业务，禁止误导投资人，防范可能发生的利益冲突。

**Article 104** A fund rating institution and its practitioners shall be objective and impartial, conduct fund rating business in accordance with business rules formulated according to law, and shall be prohibited from misleading investors and shall prevent possible conflicts of interest.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　基金管理人、基金托管人、基金服务机构的信息技术系统，应当符合规定的要求。国务院证券监督管理机构可以要求信息技术系统服务机构提供该信息技术系统的相关资料。

**Article 105** The information technology systems of a fund manager, fund custodian, and fund service institution shall meet the prescribed requirements. The securities regulatory authority of the State Council may require the information technology system service institution to provide relevant materials of such information technology system.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　律师事务所、会计师事务所接受基金管理人、基金托管人的委托，为有关基金业务活动出具法律意见书、审计报告、内部控制评价报告等文件，应当勤勉尽责，对所依据的文件资料内容的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查和验证。其制作、出具的文件有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏，给他人财产造成损失的，应当与委托人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 106** A law firm or accounting firm authorized by a fund manager or fund custodian to issue documents such as legal opinions, audit reports, and internal control evaluation reports for relevant fund business activities shall exercise due diligence, and shall verify and validate the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the content of the documents and materials on which it relies. Where the documents produced or issued contain false records, misleading statements, or major omissions, thereby causing losses to the property of others, it shall bear joint and several liability for compensation with the principal.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　基金服务机构应当勤勉尽责、恪尽职守，建立应急等风险管理制度和灾难备份系统，不得泄露与基金份额持有人、基金投资运作相关的非公开信息。

**Article 107** A fund service institution shall exercise due diligence and discharge its duties faithfully, establish a risk management system for contingencies and a disaster backup system, and shall not disclose non-public information related to fund share holders or fund investment operations.

### 第十二章　基金行业协会

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　基金行业协会是证券投资基金行业的自律性组织，是社会团体法人。  
基金管理人、基金托管人应当加入基金行业协会，基金服务机构可以加入基金行业协会。

**Article 108** The fund industry association is a self-regulatory organization of the securities investment fund industry and a social organization legal person.  
  
Fund managers and fund custodians shall join the fund industry association; fund service institutions may join the fund industry association.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　基金行业协会的权力机构为全体会员组成的会员大会。  
基金行业协会设理事会。理事会成员依章程的规定由选举产生。

**Article 109** The governing body of the fund industry association shall be the general meeting of members composed of all members.  
  
The fund industry association shall have a board of directors. The members of the board of directors shall be elected in accordance with the provisions of the association's articles.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　基金行业协会章程由会员大会制定，并报国务院证券监督管理机构备案。

**Article 110** The articles of association of the fund industry association shall be formulated by the general meeting of members and shall be filed with the securities regulatory authority of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　基金行业协会履行下列职责：  
（一）教育和组织会员遵守有关证券投资的法律、行政法规，维护投资人合法权益；  
（二）依法维护会员的合法权益，反映会员的建议和要求；  
（三）制定和实施行业自律规则，监督、检查会员及其从业人员的执业行为，对违反自律规则和协会章程的，按照规定给予纪律处分；  
（四）制定行业执业标准和业务规范，组织基金从业人员的从业考试、资质管理和业务培训；  
（五）提供会员服务，组织行业交流，推动行业创新，开展行业宣传和投资人教育活动；  
（六）对会员之间、会员与客户之间发生的基金业务纠纷进行调解；  
（七）依法办理非公开募集基金的登记、备案；  
（八）协会章程规定的其他职责。

**Article 111** The fund industry association shall perform the following duties:  
  
(1) educating and organizing its members to comply with the laws and administrative regulations concerning securities investment, and safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of investors;  
  
(2) protecting the lawful rights and interests of its members according to law, and reflecting the suggestions and demands of its members;  
  
(3) formulating and implementing industry self-regulatory rules, supervising and inspecting the professional conduct of its members and their practitioners, and imposing disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the rules for those who violate the self-regulatory rules and the articles of association;  
  
(4) formulating industry professional standards and business norms, organizing qualification examinations, qualification management, and professional training for fund practitioners;  
  
(5) providing member services, organizing industry exchanges, promoting industry innovation, and conducting industry publicity and investor education activities;  
  
(6) mediating fund business disputes between members, and between members and clients;  
  
(7) handling the registration and filing of non-publicly offered funds according to law; and  
  
(8) other duties as provided in the articles of association.

### 第十三章　监督管理

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法履行下列职责：  
（一）制定有关证券投资基金活动监督管理的规章、规则，并行使审批、核准或者注册权；  
（二）办理基金备案；  
（三）对基金管理人、基金托管人及其他机构从事证券投资基金活动进行监督管理，对违法行为进行查处，并予以公告；  
（四）制定基金从业人员的资格标准和行为准则，并监督实施；  
（五）监督检查基金信息的披露情况；  
（六）指导和监督基金行业协会的活动；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他职责。

**Article 112** The securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall, according to law, perform the following duties:  
  
(1) formulate rules and regulations concerning the supervision and administration of securities investment fund activities, and exercise the power of examination, approval, or registration;  
  
(2) handle fund filings;  
  
(3) supervise and administer fund managers, fund custodians, and other institutions engaged in securities investment fund activities, investigate and punish illegal acts, and make public announcements thereof;  
  
(4) formulate qualification standards and codes of conduct for fund practitioners and supervise their implementation;  
  
(5) supervise and inspect the disclosure of fund information;  
  
(6) guide and supervise the activities of the fund industry association; and  
  
(7) other duties as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法履行职责，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）对基金管理人、基金托管人、基金服务机构进行现场检查，并要求其报送有关的业务资料；  
（二）进入涉嫌违法行为发生场所调查取证；  
（三）询问当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明；  
（四）查阅、复制与被调查事件有关的财产权登记、通讯记录等资料；  
（五）查阅、复制当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的证券交易记录、登记过户记录、财务会计资料及其他相关文件和资料；对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件和资料，可以予以封存；  
（六）查询当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的资金账户、证券账户和银行账户；对有证据证明已经或者可能转移或者隐匿违法资金、证券等涉案财产或者隐匿、伪造、毁损重要证据的，经国务院证券监督管理机构主要负责人批准，可以冻结或者查封；  
（七）在调查操纵证券市场、内幕交易等重大证券违法行为时，经国务院证券监督管理机构主要负责人批准，可以限制被调查事件当事人的证券买卖，但限制的期限不得超过十五个交易日；案情复杂的，可以延长十五个交易日。

**Article 113** When performing its duties according to law, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall have the power to take the following measures:  
  
(1) carry out on-site inspections of fund managers, fund custodians, and fund service institutions, and require them to submit relevant business materials;  
  
(2) enter the place where an alleged illegal act occurred to investigate and collect evidence;  
  
(3) question the parties, and the units and individuals related to the matter under investigation, and require them to provide explanations on matters related to the matter under investigation;  
  
(4) consult and copy materials such as property rights registration records and communication records related to the matter under investigation;  
  
(5) consult and copy securities transaction records, registration and transfer records, financial accounting materials, and other relevant documents and materials of the parties and the units and individuals related to the matter under investigation; and may seal up documents and materials that are likely to be transferred, concealed, or destroyed;  
  
(6) query the fund accounts, securities accounts, and bank accounts of the parties and the units and individuals related to the matter under investigation; and where there is evidence that illegal funds, securities, or other involved property have been or may be transferred or concealed, or that important evidence has been or may be concealed, forged, or destroyed, may, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, freeze or seal up such property or evidence; and  
  
(7) when investigating major securities illegal acts such as market manipulation or insider trading, may, with the approval of the principal responsible person of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, restrict the securities trading of the parties concerned in the matter under investigation; provided, however, that the period of restriction shall not exceed fifteen trading days; where the case is complicated, the period may be extended by fifteen trading days.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　国务院证券监督管理机构工作人员依法履行职责，进行调查或者检查时，不得少于二人，并应当出示合法证件；对调查或者检查中知悉的商业秘密负有保密的义务。

**Article 114** When performing their duties according to law and conducting investigations or inspections, the staff members of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall be no less than two persons, and shall present their lawful credentials; they shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets learned during the investigation or inspection.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　国务院证券监督管理机构工作人员应当忠于职守，依法办事，公正廉洁，接受监督，不得利用职务牟取私利。

**Article 115** The staff members of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall be devoted to their duties, handle matters according to law, be impartial and honest, accept supervision, and shall not use their positions to seek personal gains.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法履行职责时，被调查、检查的单位和个人应当配合，如实提供有关文件和资料，不得拒绝、阻碍和隐瞒。

**Article 116** When the securities regulatory authority of the State Council performs its duties according to law, the units and individuals under investigation or inspection shall cooperate, truthfully provide relevant documents and materials, and shall not refuse, obstruct, or conceal.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　国务院证券监督管理机构依法履行职责，发现违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当将案件移送司法机关处理。

**Article 117** When performing its duties according to law, if the securities regulatory authority of the State Council discovers that an illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall transfer the case to the judicial authorities for handling.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　国务院证券监督管理机构工作人员在任职期间，或者离职后在《中华人民共和国公务员法》规定的期限内，不得在被监管的机构中担任职务。

**Article 118** The staff members of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council shall not, during their tenure of office, or within the period prescribed by the Public Servants Law of the People's Republic of China after leaving office, hold any position in an institution under its supervision.

### 第十四章　法律责任

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　违反本法规定，未经批准擅自设立基金管理公司或者未经核准从事公开募集基金管理业务的，由证券监督管理机构予以取缔或者责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。  
基金管理公司违反本法规定，擅自变更持有百分之五以上股权的股东、实际控制人或者其他重大事项的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员给予警告，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 119** Any person who, in violation of this Law, establishes a fund management company without approval or engages in the business of publicly offered fund management without authorization shall be banned or ordered to rectify by the securities regulatory authority, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be concurrently imposed. The person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and concurrently fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.  
  
Where a fund management company, in violation of this Law, changes without authorization the shareholders holding five percent or more of the equity, the actual controller, or other major matters, it shall be ordered to rectify, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed. The person directly in charge shall be given a warning and concurrently fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员，基金托管人的专门基金托管部门的高级管理人员和其他从业人员，未按照[本法第十七条](#art-17)第一款规定申报的，责令改正，处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。  
基金管理人、基金托管人违反[本法第十七条](#art-17)第二款规定的，责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 120** Where directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other practitioners of a fund manager, or senior officers and other practitioners of the specialized fund custody department of a fund custodian, fail to make declarations as required by the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 17 of this Law, they shall be ordered to rectify, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
  
Where a fund manager or fund custodian violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 17 of this Law, it shall be ordered to rectify, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund practice qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　基金管理人的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员，基金托管人的专门基金托管部门的高级管理人员和其他从业人员违反[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；情节严重的，撤销基金从业资格。

**Article 121** Where a director, supervisor, senior officer or any other employee of a fund management institution, or a senior officer or any other employee of a specialized fund custodian department of a fund custodian violates Article 18 of this Law, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the fund business qualification shall be revoked.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　基金管理人、基金托管人违反本法规定，未对基金财产实行分别管理或者分账保管，责令改正，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 122** Where a fund management institution or a fund custodian violates the provisions of this Law by failing to manage the fund property separately or keep the fund property in separate accounts, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, and their fund business qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条　基金管理人、基金托管人及其董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员有[本法第二十条](#art-20)所列行为之一的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；基金管理人、基金托管人有上述行为的，还应当对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。  
基金管理人、基金托管人及其董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他从业人员侵占、挪用基金财产而取得的财产和收益，归入基金财产。但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 123** Where a fund management institution, a fund custodian, or any director, supervisor, senior officer or other employee thereof commits any of the acts listed in Article 20 of this Law, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; where a fund management institution or a fund custodian commits the aforesaid act, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall also be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.  
Any property and income obtained by a fund management institution, a fund custodian, or any director, supervisor, senior officer or other employee thereof from embezzling or misappropriating fund property shall be included in the fund property. However, if otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条　基金管理人的股东、实际控制人违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金或证券从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 124** Where a shareholder or actual controller of a fund management institution violates Article 23 of this Law, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund or securities business qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条　未经核准，擅自从事基金托管业务的，责令停止，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 125** Where a party engages in fund custodian business without approval, it shall be ordered to stop, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条　基金管理人、基金托管人违反本法规定，相互出资或者持有股份的，责令改正，可以处十万元以下罚款。

**Article 126** Where a fund management institution or a fund custodian violates the provisions of this Law by making capital contributions to each other or holding each other's shares, it shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条　违反本法规定，擅自公开或者变相公开募集基金的，责令停止，返还所募资金和加计的银行同期存款利息，没收违法所得，并处所募资金金额百分之一以上百分之五以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 127** Where a party violates the provisions of this Law by publicly or in a disguised form publicly raising funds without authorization, it shall be ordered to stop, return the raised funds plus the interest calculated at the bank deposit rate for the corresponding period, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one percent but not more than five percent of the amount of the raised funds shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条　违反[本法第五十九条](#art-59)规定，动用募集的资金的，责令返还，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五十万元的，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 128** Where a party violates Article 59 of this Law by using the raised funds, it shall be ordered to return the funds, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条　基金管理人、基金托管人有[本法第七十三条](#art-73)第一款第一项至第五项和第七项所列行为之一，或者违反[本法第七十三条](#art-73)第二款规定的，责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。  
基金管理人、基金托管人有前款行为，运用基金财产而取得的财产和收益，归入基金财产。但是，法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 129** Where a fund management institution or a fund custodian commits any of the acts listed in Items 1 to 5 and Item 7 of Paragraph 1 of Article 73 of this Law, or violates Paragraph 2 of Article 73 of this Law, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.  
Any property and income obtained by a fund management institution or a fund custodian from using fund property in the act set forth in the preceding paragraph shall be included in the fund property. However, if otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条　基金管理人、基金托管人有[本法第七十三条](#art-73)第一款第六项规定行为的，除依照《中华人民共和国证券法》的有关规定处罚外，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员暂停或者撤销基金从业资格。

**Article 130** Where a fund management institution or a fund custodian commits the act specified in Item 6 of Paragraph 1 of Article 73 of this Law, in addition to punishment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China, the fund business qualification of the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条　基金信息披露义务人不依法披露基金信息或者披露的信息有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 131** Where a fund information disclosure obligor fails to disclose fund information according to law, or the disclosed information contains false records, misleading statements, or material omissions, it shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条　基金管理人或者基金托管人不按照规定召集基金份额持有人大会的，责令改正，可以处五万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，暂停或者撤销基金从业资格。

**Article 132** Where a fund management institution or a fund custodian fails to convene a fund unit holders' meeting as required, it shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, and their fund business qualification shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条　违反本法规定，未经登记，使用“基金”或者“基金管理”字样或者近似名称进行证券投资活动的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 133** Where a party violates the provisions of this Law by using the words "fund" or "fund management" or similar names to conduct securities investment activities without registration, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条　违反本法规定，非公开募集基金募集完毕，基金管理人未备案的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 134** Where a fund management institution violates the provisions of this Law by failing to file a record after the completion of the raising of a non-publicly offered fund, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条　违反本法规定，向合格投资者之外的单位或者个人非公开募集资金或者转让基金份额的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 135** Where a party violates the provisions of this Law by non-publicly raising funds from or transferring fund units to entities or individuals other than qualified investors, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条　违反本法规定，擅自从事公开募集基金的基金服务业务的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足三十万元的，并处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 136** Where a party violates the provisions of this Law by engaging in fund service business for publicly offered funds without authorization, it shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条　基金销售机构未向投资人充分揭示投资风险并误导其购买与其风险承担能力不相当的基金产品的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 137** Where a fund sales institution fails to fully disclose the investment risks to investors and misleads them into purchasing fund products that are not suitable for their risk tolerance, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to stop its fund service business. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条　基金销售支付机构未按照规定划付基金销售结算资金的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 138** Where a fund sales payment institution fails to transfer the fund sales settlement funds in accordance with the provisions, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to stop its fund service business. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条　挪用基金销售结算资金或者基金份额的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足一百万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 139** Where a party misappropriates the fund sales settlement funds or fund units, it shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than one million yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条　基金份额登记机构未妥善保存或者备份基金份额登记数据的，责令改正，给予警告，并处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。  
基金份额登记机构隐匿、伪造、篡改、毁损基金份额登记数据的，责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款，并责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 140** Where a fund unit registration institution fails to properly keep or back up the fund unit registration data, it shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to stop its fund service business. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.  
Where a fund unit registration institution conceals, forges, alters, or destroys the fund unit registration data, it shall be ordered to make corrections, fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than one million yuan, and be ordered to stop its fund service business. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, their fund business qualification shall be revoked, and they shall be fined not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条　基金投资顾问机构、基金评价机构及其从业人员违反本法规定开展投资顾问、基金评价服务的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 141** Where a fund investment advisory institution, a fund evaluation institution, or any of their employees violates the provisions of this Law in conducting investment advisory or fund evaluation services, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the institution shall be ordered to cease its fund service business. The directly responsible主管人员 and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning, their fund practice qualification shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条　信息技术系统服务机构未按照规定向国务院证券监督管理机构提供相关信息技术系统资料，或者提供的信息技术系统资料虚假、有重大遗漏的，责令改正，处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处一万元以上三万元以下罚款。

**Article 142** Where an information technology system service institution fails to provide relevant information technology system materials to the securities regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions, or provides false or materially incomplete information technology system materials, it shall be ordered to make corrections and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible主管人员 and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条　会计师事务所、律师事务所未勤勉尽责，所出具的文件有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，责令改正，没收业务收入，暂停或者撤销相关业务许可，并处业务收入一倍以上五倍以下罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 143** Where an accounting firm or a law firm fails to exercise due diligence and the documents it issues contain false records, misleading statements, or material omissions, it shall be ordered to make corrections, its business income shall be confiscated, the relevant business license shall be suspended or revoked, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the business income shall be imposed. The directly responsible主管人员 and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条　基金服务机构未建立应急等风险管理制度和灾难备份系统，或者泄露与基金份额持有人、基金投资运作相关的非公开信息的，处十万元以上三十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令其停止基金服务业务。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，撤销基金从业资格，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 144** Where a fund service institution fails to establish risk management systems such as contingency plans and a disaster backup system, or discloses non-public information relating to fund holders or the investment operation of the fund, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to cease its fund service business. The directly responsible主管人员 and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning, their fund practice qualification shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条　违反本法规定，给基金财产、基金份额持有人或者投资人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
基金管理人、基金托管人在履行各自职责的过程中，违反本法规定或者基金合同约定，给基金财产或者基金份额持有人造成损害的，应当分别对各自的行为依法承担赔偿责任；因共同行为给基金财产或者基金份额持有人造成损害的，应当承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 145** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law causes damage to fund property, fund holders, or investors, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
If, in the course of performing their respective duties, a fund manager or a fund custodian violates the provisions of this Law or the fund contract and causes damage to fund property or fund holders, each shall bear compensation liability according to law for its own act; if a joint act causes damage to fund property or fund holders, they shall bear joint and several compensation liability.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条　证券监督管理机构工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊或者利用职务上的便利索取或者收受他人财物的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 146** Where a staff member of the securities regulatory authority neglects duties, abuses powers, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or takes advantage of his position to extort or accept property from others, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条　拒绝、阻碍证券监督管理机构及其工作人员依法行使监督检查、调查职权未使用暴力、威胁方法的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 147** Where a person refuses or obstructs the securities regulatory authority or its staff from exercising the powers of supervision, inspection, and investigation according to law without using violence or threats, a public security penalty shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条　违反法律、行政法规或者国务院证券监督管理机构的有关规定，情节严重的，国务院证券监督管理机构可以对有关责任人员采取证券市场禁入的措施。

**Article 148** Where a person violates laws, administrative regulations, or the relevant provisions of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council, and the circumstances are serious, the securities regulatory authority of the State Council may take measures to ban the relevant responsible persons from entering the securities market.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 149** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条　违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金，其财产不足以同时支付时，先承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 150** Where a person who violates the provisions of this Law shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines or penal sums, and his property is insufficient to pay both at the same time, the civil compensation liability shall be borne first.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条　依照本法规定，基金管理人、基金托管人、基金服务机构应当承担的民事赔偿责任和缴纳的罚款、罚金，由基金管理人、基金托管人、基金服务机构以其固有财产承担。  
依法收缴的罚款、罚金和没收的违法所得，应当全部上缴国库。

**Article 151** According to the provisions of this Law, a fund manager, fund custodian, or fund service institution that shall bear civil compensation liability and pay fines or penal sums shall bear such liability and pay such sums with its own property.  
Fines, penal sums confiscated and illegal gains seized according to law shall all be turned over to the State treasury.

### 第十五章　附则

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条　在中华人民共和国境内募集投资境外证券的基金，以及合格境外投资者在境内进行证券投资，应当经国务院证券监督管理机构批准，具体办法由国务院证券监督管理机构会同国务院有关部门规定，报国务院批准。

**Article 152** Funds raised within the territory of the People's Republic of China to invest in overseas securities, and qualified foreign investors making securities investments within the territory, shall be subject to approval by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council. The specific measures shall be formulated by the securities regulatory authority of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条　公开或者非公开募集资金，以进行证券投资活动为目的设立的公司或者合伙企业，资产由基金管理人或者普通合伙人管理的，其证券投资活动适用本法。

**Article 153** Where a company or a partnership is established with publicly or non-publicly raised funds for the purpose of engaging in securities investment activities, and its assets are managed by a fund manager or a general partner, this Law shall apply to its securities investment activities.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条　本法自2013年6月1日起施行。

**Article 154** This Law shall come into force as of June 1, 2013.
