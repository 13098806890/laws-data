# 最高人民法院关于审理期货纠纷案件若干问题的规定（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：民法商法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕1号  
**公布日期**：2010-12-31  
**生效日期**：2011-01-17

> 最高人民法院《关于审理期货纠纷案件若干问题的规定（二）》已于2010年12月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1507次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年1月17日起施行。 2010年12月31日 最高人民法院 关于审理期货纠纷案件若干问题的规定（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　以期货交易所为被告或者第三人的因期货交易所履行职责引起的商事案件，由期货交易所所在地的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 1** . A commercial case arising from a futures exchange's performance of its duties, in which the futures exchange is the defendant or a third party, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the futures exchange.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　期货交易所履行职责引起的商事案件是指：  
（一）期货交易所会员及其相关人员、保证金存管银行及其相关人员、客户、其他期货市场参与者，以期货交易所违反法律法规以及国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，履行监督管理职责不当，造成其损害为由提起的商事诉讼案件；  
（二）期货交易所会员及其相关人员、保证金存管银行及其相关人员、客户、其他期货市场参与者，以期货交易所违反其章程、交易规则、实施细则的规定以及业务协议的约定，履行监督管理职责不当，造成其损害为由提起的商事诉讼案件；  
（三）期货交易所因履行职责引起的其他商事诉讼案件。

**Article 2** . The commercial cases arising from a futures exchange's performance of its duties refer to:  
(1) commercial litigation cases filed by members of the futures exchange and their relevant personnel, the margin depository bank and its relevant personnel, clients, or other participants in the futures market on the grounds that the futures exchange, by violating laws, regulations, and the rules of the securities regulatory authority under the State Council, improperly performed its supervisory and administrative duties, thereby causing damage to them;  
(2) commercial litigation cases filed by members of the futures exchange and their relevant personnel, the margin depository bank and its relevant personnel, clients, or other participants in the futures market on the grounds that the futures exchange, by violating its articles of association, trading rules, detailed implementation rules, and the stipulations of business agreements, improperly performed its supervisory and administrative duties, thereby causing damage to them;  
(3) other commercial litigation cases arising from a futures exchange's performance of its duties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　期货交易所为债务人，债权人请求冻结、划拨以下账户中资金或者有价证券的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）期货交易所会员在期货交易所保证金账户中的资金；  
（二）期货交易所会员向期货交易所提交的用于充抵保证金的有价证券。

**Article 3** . Where a futures exchange is the debtor and a creditor requests the freezing or transfer of funds or securities in any of the following accounts, the People's Court shall not support the request:  
(1) the funds in the margin account of a member of the futures exchange held with the futures exchange;  
(2) the securities submitted by a member of the futures exchange to the futures exchange for the purpose of offsetting margin.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　期货公司为债务人，债权人请求冻结、划拨以下账户中资金或者有价证券的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）客户在期货公司保证金账户中的资金；  
（二）客户向期货公司提交的用于充抵保证金的有价证券。

**Article 4** . Where a futures company is the debtor and a creditor requests the freezing or transfer of funds or securities in any of the following accounts, the People's Court shall not support the request:  
(1) the funds in the margin account of a client held with the futures company;  
(2) the securities submitted by a client to the futures company for the purpose of offsetting margin.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　实行会员分级结算制度的期货交易所的结算会员为债务人，债权人请求冻结、划拨结算会员以下资金或者有价证券的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）非结算会员在结算会员保证金账户中的资金；  
（二）非结算会员向结算会员提交的用于充抵保证金的有价证券。

**Article 5** . Where a clearing member of a futures exchange that adopts a member-based tiered clearing system is the debtor and a creditor requests the freezing or transfer of the following funds or securities of the clearing member, the People's Court shall not support the request:  
(1) the funds in the margin account of a non-clearing member held with the clearing member;  
(2) the securities submitted by a non-clearing member to the clearing member for the purpose of offsetting margin.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有证据证明保证金账户中有超过上述第三条、第四条、第五条规定的资金或者有价证券部分权益的，期货交易所、期货公司或者期货交易所结算会员在人民法院指定的合理期限内不能提出相反证据的，人民法院可以依法冻结、划拨超出部分的资金或者有价证券。  
有证据证明期货交易所、期货公司、期货交易所结算会员自有资金与保证金发生混同，期货交易所、期货公司或者期货交易所结算会员在人民法院指定的合理期限内不能提出相反证据的，人民法院可以依法冻结、划拨相关账户内的资金或者有价证券。

**Article 6** . If there is evidence proving that the margin account contains funds or securities exceeding the portions of rights and interests specified in Articles 3, 4, and 5 above, and the futures exchange, futures company, or clearing member of the futures exchange cannot produce contrary evidence within a reasonable period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court may, according to law, freeze or transfer the funds or securities for the excess part.  
If there is evidence proving that the own funds of the futures exchange, futures company, or clearing member of the futures exchange are commingled with the margin, and the futures exchange, futures company, or clearing member of the futures exchange cannot produce contrary evidence within a reasonable period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court may, according to law, freeze or transfer the funds or securities in the relevant accounts.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实行会员分级结算制度的期货交易所或者其结算会员为债务人，债权人请求冻结、划拨期货交易所向其结算会员依法收取的结算担保金的，人民法院不予支持。  
有证据证明结算会员在结算担保金专用账户中有超过交易所要求的结算担保金数额部分的，结算会员在人民法院指定的合理期限内不能提出相反证据的，人民法院可以依法冻结、划拨超出部分的资金。

**Article 7** . Where a futures exchange that adopts a member-based tiered clearing system or its clearing member is the debtor and a creditor requests the freezing or transfer of the clearing guarantee deposits collected by the futures exchange from its clearing members according to law, the People's Court shall not support the request.  
If there is evidence proving that the clearing member has funds in the dedicated account for clearing guarantee deposits that exceed the amount of clearing guarantee deposits required by the exchange, and the clearing member cannot produce contrary evidence within a reasonable period designated by the People's Court, the People's Court may, according to law, freeze or transfer the funds in excess.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院在办理案件过程中，依法需要通过期货交易所、期货公司查询、冻结、划拨资金或者有价证券的，期货交易所、期货公司应当予以协助。应当协助而拒不协助的，按照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/三大诉讼法/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-103)之规定办理。

**Article 8** . When a People's Court, in the course of handling a case, needs to inquire about, freeze, or transfer funds or securities through a futures exchange or futures company according to law, the futures exchange or futures company shall provide assistance. If it is obligated to provide assistance but refuses to do so, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 103 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本规定施行前已经受理的上述案件不再移送。

**Article 9** . The aforementioned cases already accepted before the coming into force of these Provisions shall no longer be transferred.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本规定施行前本院作出的有关司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 10** . Where any relevant judicial interpretation issued by this Court before the coming into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
