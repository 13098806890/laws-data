### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下简称著作权法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Copyright Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　著作权法所称作品，是指文学、艺术和科学领域内具有独创性并能以某种有形形式复制的智力成果。

**Article 2** For the purposes of the Copyright Law, "works" mean intellectual achievements that are original and can be reproduced in a tangible form in the fields of literature, art, and science.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　著作权法所称创作，是指直接产生文学、艺术和科学作品的智力活动。  
为他人创作进行组织工作，提供咨询意见、物质条件，或者进行其他辅助工作，均不视为创作。

**Article 3** For the purposes of the Copyright Law, "creation" means the intellectual activity that directly produces literary, artistic, and scientific works.  
Organizing work for the creation of others, providing consulting opinions, material conditions, or performing other auxiliary work shall not be deemed as creation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　著作权法和本条例中下列作品的含义：  
(一)文字作品，是指小说、诗词、散文、论文等以文字形式表现的作品；  
(二)口述作品，是指即兴的演说、授课、法庭辩论等以口头语言形式表现的作品；  
(三)音乐作品，是指歌曲、交响乐等能够演唱或者演奏的带词或者不带词的作品；  
(四)戏剧作品，是指话剧、歌剧、地方戏等供舞台演出的作品；  
(五)曲艺作品，是指相声、快书、大鼓、评书等以说唱为主要形式表演的作品；  
(六)舞蹈作品，是指通过连续的动作、姿势、表情等表现思想情感的作品；  
(七)杂技艺术作品，是指杂技、魔术、马戏等通过形体动作和技巧表现的作品；  
(八)美术作品，是指绘画、书法、雕塑等以线条、色彩或者其他方式构成的有审美意义的平面或者立体的造型艺术作品；  
(九)建筑作品，是指以建筑物或者构筑物形式表现的有审美意义的作品；  
(十)摄影作品，是指借助器械在感光材料或者其他介质上记录客观物体形象的艺术作品；  
(十一)电影作品和以类似摄制电影的方法创作的作品，是指摄制在一定介质上，由一系列有伴音或者无伴音的画面组成，并且借助适当装置放映或者以其他方式传播的作品；  
(十二)图形作品，是指为施工、生产绘制的工程设计图、产品设计图，以及反映地理现象、说明事物原理或者结构的地图、示意图等作品；  
(十三)模型作品，是指为展示、试验或者观测等用途，根据物体的形状和结构，按照一定比例制成的立体作品。

**Article 4** The meanings of the following works in the Copyright Law and these Regulations are:  
(1) Written works mean works expressed in written form, such as novels, poems, essays, and theses;  
(2) Oral works mean works expressed in oral language form, such as impromptu speeches, lectures, and court debates;  
(3) Musical works mean works that can be sung or performed, with or without words, such as songs and symphonies;  
(4) Dramatic works mean works for stage performance, such as plays, operas, and local operas;  
(5) Quyi works mean works performed mainly by talking and singing, such as cross talk, clapper talk, drum singing, and storytelling;  
(6) Choreographic works mean works that express thoughts and feelings through continuous movements, postures, and expressions;  
(7) Acrobatic art works mean works expressed through physical movements and skills, such as acrobatics, magic, and circus;  
(8) Art works mean two-dimensional or three-dimensional plastic art works with aesthetic significance, composed of lines, colors, or other means, such as paintings, calligraphy, and sculptures;  
(9) Architectural works mean works with aesthetic significance expressed in the form of buildings or structures;  
(10) Photographic works mean artistic works that record images of objective objects on photosensitive materials or other media by means of instruments;  
(11) Cinematographic works and works created by a process analogous to cinematography mean works that are fixed on a certain medium, consisting of a series of images with or without accompanying sound, and which can be projected or otherwise disseminated with the aid of appropriate devices;  
(12) Graphic works mean works such as engineering design drawings, product design drawings for construction or production, and maps, schematic diagrams, etc., that reflect geographical phenomena or explain the principles or structures of things;  
(13) Model works mean three-dimensional works made according to the shape and structure of an object in a certain proportion for purposes of display, testing, or observation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　著作权法和本条例中下列用语的含义：  
(一)时事新闻，是指通过报纸、期刊、广播电台、电视台等媒体报道的单纯事实消息；  
(二)录音制品，是指任何对表演的声音和其他声音的录制品；  
(三)录像制品，是指电影作品和以类似摄制电影的方法创作的作品以外的任何有伴音或者无伴音的连续相关形象、图像的录制品；  
(四)录音制作者，是指录音制品的首次制作人；  
(五)录像制作者，是指录像制品的首次制作人；  
(六)表演者，是指演员、演出单位或者其他表演文学、艺术作品的人。

**Article 5** The meanings of the following terms in the Copyright Law and these Regulations are:  
(1) "Current affairs news" means mere factual news reported through media such as newspapers, periodicals, radio stations, and television stations;  
(2) "Sound recordings" mean any recordings of performance sounds and other sounds;  
(3) "Video recordings" mean any recordings of a series of related images or pictures with or without accompanying sound, other than cinematographic works and works created by a process analogous to cinematography;  
(4) "Sound recording producers" mean the persons who first produce sound recordings;  
(5) "Video recording producers" mean the persons who first produce video recordings;  
(6) "Performers" mean actors, performing units, or other persons who perform literary or artistic works.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　著作权自作品创作完成之日起产生。

**Article 6** Copyright shall arise upon the completion of the creation of a work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[著作权法第二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-2)第三款规定的首先在中国境内出版的外国人、无国籍人的作品，其著作权自首次出版之日起受保护。

**Article 7** The copyright in a work of a foreigner or stateless person that is first published within the territory of China, as provided in Article 2, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Law, shall be protected from the date of first publication.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国人、无国籍人的作品在中国境外首先出版后，30日内在中国境内出版的，视为该作品同时在中国境内出版。

**Article 8** Where a work of a foreigner or stateless person is first published outside the territory of China and then published within the territory of China within 30 days, it shall be deemed to have been published simultaneously in China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　合作作品不可以分割使用的，其著作权由各合作作者共同享有，通过协商一致行使；不能协商一致，又无正当理由的，任何一方不得阻止他方行使除转让以外的其他权利，但是所得收益应当合理分配给所有合作作者。

**Article 9** Where a work of joint authorship cannot be used separately, the copyright shall be jointly owned by all co-authors and shall be exercised through consensus; if consensus cannot be reached without a justified reason, no co-author shall prevent the others from exercising rights other than assignment, provided that the proceeds obtained shall be reasonably distributed among all co-authors.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　著作权人许可他人将其作品摄制成电影作品和以类似摄制电影的方法创作的作品的，视为已同意对其作品进行必要的改动，但是这种改动不得歪曲篡改原作品。

**Article 10** Where a copyright owner licenses another person to make cinematographic works or works created by a process analogous to cinematography based on his work, he shall be deemed to have consented to necessary modifications to the work, provided that such modifications shall not distort or mutilate the original work.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　[著作权法第十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-16)第一款关于职务作品的规定中的“工作任务”，是指公民在该法人或者该组织中应当履行的职责。  
[著作权法第十六条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-16)第二款关于职务作品的规定中的“物质技术条件”，是指该法人或者该组织为公民完成创作专门提供的资金、设备或者资料。

**Article 11** The term "work tasks" in the provisions regarding works created in the course of employment as stipulated in Article 16, paragraph 1 of the Copyright Law means the duties that a citizen shall perform in the legal person or organization.  
The term "material and technical conditions" in the provisions regarding works created in the course of employment as stipulated in Article 16, paragraph 2 of the Copyright Law means the funds, equipment, or materials specially provided by the legal person or organization to the citizen for completing the creation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　职务作品完成两年内，经单位同意，作者许可第三人以与单位使用的相同方式使用作品所获报酬，由作者与单位按约定的比例分配。  
作品完成两年的期限，自作者向单位交付作品之日起计算。

**Article 12** Within two years after the completion of a work created in the course of employment, if the author, with the consent of the entity, licenses a third party to use the work in the same manner as the entity uses it, the remuneration obtained shall be distributed between the author and the entity in the proportion agreed upon.  
The two-year period after the completion of the work shall be counted from the date on which the author delivers the work to the entity.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　作者身份不明的作品，由作品原件的所有人行使除署名权以外的著作权。作者身份确定后，由作者或者其继承人行使著作权。

**Article 13** For a work of unknown authorship, the owner of the original work shall exercise the copyright except the right of authorship. Once the authorship is determined, the copyright shall be exercised by the author or his successor.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　合作作者之一死亡后，其对合作作品享有的[著作权法第十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-10)第一款第五项至第十七项规定的权利无人继承又无人受遗赠的，由其他合作作者享有。

**Article 14** Where, after the death of one of the co-authors, the rights provided in Article 10, paragraph 1, items (5) to (17) of the Copyright Law in the work of joint authorship are inherited by no one and bequeathed to no one, they shall be enjoyed by the other co-authors.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　作者死亡后，其著作权中的署名权、修改权和保护作品完整权由作者的继承人或者受遗赠人保护。  
著作权无人继承又无人受遗赠的，其署名权、修改权和保护作品完整权由著作权行政管理部门保护。

**Article 15** After the death of an author, the right of authorship, the right of revision, and the right of integrity in his copyright shall be protected by his successor or legatee.  
Where there is neither a successor nor a legatee for the copyright, the right of authorship, the right of revision, and the right of integrity shall be protected by the copyright administrative department.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家享有著作权的作品的使用，由国务院著作权行政管理部门管理。

**Article 16** The use of works in which the State owns the copyright shall be administered by the copyright administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　作者生前未发表的作品，如果作者未明确表示不发表，作者死亡后50年内，其发表权可由继承人或者受遗赠人行使；没有继承人又无人受遗赠的，由作品原件的所有人行使。

**Article 17** For unpublished works of an author, if the author has not explicitly stated that they shall not be published, within 50 years after the death of the author, the right of publication may be exercised by the successor or legatee; where there is neither a successor nor a legatee, it shall be exercised by the owner of the original work.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　作者身份不明的作品，其[著作权法第十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-10)第一款第五项至第十七项规定的权利的保护期截止于作品首次发表后第50年的12月31日。作者身份确定后，适用[著作权法第二十一条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-21)的规定。

**Article 18** For a work of unknown authorship, the protection period of the rights provided in Article 10, paragraph 1, items (5) to (17) of the Copyright Law shall expire on December 31 of the 50th year after the first publication of the work. After the authorship is determined, the provisions of Article 21 of the Copyright Law shall apply.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　使用他人作品的，应当指明作者姓名、作品名称；但是，当事人另有约定或者由于作品使用方式的特性无法指明的除外。

**Article 19** Where a work of another person is used, the name of the author and the title of the work shall be indicated, except where otherwise agreed by the parties or where it is impossible to indicate due to the nature of the way the work is used.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　著作权法所称已经发表的作品，是指著作权人自行或者许可他人公之于众的作品。

**Article 20** "Published works" as referred to in the Copyright Law mean works that have been made available to the public by the copyright owner himself or with his authorization.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　依照著作权法有关规定，使用可以不经著作权人许可的已经发表的作品的，不得影响该作品的正常使用，也不得不合理地损害著作权人的合法利益。

**Article 21** Where a published work is used in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Copyright Law without the permission of the copyright owner, such use shall not affect the normal use of the work nor unreasonably prejudice the legitimate interests of the copyright owner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　依照[著作权法第二十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-23)、第三十三条第二款、第四十条第三款的规定使用作品的付酬标准，由国务院著作权行政管理部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定、公布。

**Article 22** The standards for the payment of remuneration for the use of works in accordance with the provisions of Article 23, Article 33, paragraph 2, and Article 40, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Law shall be formulated and published by the copyright administrative department under the State Council jointly with the price authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　使用他人作品应当同著作权人订立许可使用合同，许可使用的权利是专有使用权的，应当采取书面形式，但是报社、期刊社刊登作品除外。

**Article 23** A license contract shall be concluded with the copyright owner for the use of another person's work. Where the licensed right is an exclusive right of use, it shall be in written form, except for the publication of works by newspapers or periodicals.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　[著作权法第二十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-24)规定的专有使用权的内容由合同约定，合同没有约定或者约定不明的，视为被许可人有权排除包括著作权人在内的任何人以同样的方式使用作品；除合同另有约定外，被许可人许可第三人行使同一权利，必须取得著作权人的许可。

**Article 24** The content of the exclusive right of use as provided in Article 24 of the Copyright Law shall be stipulated in the contract. If the contract does not stipulate or the stipulation is unclear, the licensee shall be deemed to have the right to exclude any person, including the copyright owner, from using the work in the same manner; unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the licensee must obtain the permission of the copyright owner to license a third party to exercise the same right.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　与著作权人订立专有许可使用合同、转让合同的，可以向著作权行政管理部门备案。

**Article 25** A contract for exclusive license or assignment concluded with the copyright owner may be filed with the copyright administrative department.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　著作权法和本条例所称与著作权有关的权益，是指出版者对其出版的图书和期刊的版式设计享有的权利，表演者对其表演享有的权利，录音录像制作者对其制作的录音录像制品享有的权利，广播电台、电视台对其播放的广播、电视节目享有的权利。

**Article 26** The rights and interests related to copyright as referred to in the Copyright Law and these Regulations mean the rights enjoyed by publishers in the typographical design of the books and periodicals they publish, the rights enjoyed by performers in their performances, the rights enjoyed by sound and video recording producers in the sound and video recordings they produce, and the rights enjoyed by radio stations and television stations in the broadcasting and television programs they broadcast.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　出版者、表演者、录音录像制作者、广播电台、电视台行使权利，不得损害被使用作品和原作品著作权人的权利。

**Article 27** When publishers, performers, sound and video recording producers, radio stations, and television stations exercise their rights, they shall not prejudice the rights of the copyright owners of the used works and the original works.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　图书出版合同中约定图书出版者享有专有出版权但没有明确其具体内容的，视为图书出版者享有在合同有效期限内和在合同约定的地域范围内以同种文字的原版、修订版出版图书的专有权利。

**Article 28** Where a book publishing contract stipulates that the book publisher has an exclusive right to publish but does not specify its specific content, the book publisher shall be deemed to have the exclusive right to publish the book in the same language in its original edition and revised edition within the term of validity of the contract and within the territorial scope agreed upon in the contract.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　著作权人寄给图书出版者的两份订单在6个月内未能得到履行，视为[著作权法第三十二条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-32)所称图书脱销。

**Article 29** Where two orders for the book sent by the copyright owner to the book publisher are not fulfilled within six months, the book shall be deemed to be out of stock as referred to in Article 32 of the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　著作权人依照[著作权法第三十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-33)第二款声明不得转载、摘编其作品的，应当在报纸、期刊刊登该作品时附带声明。

**Article 30** Where a copyright owner declares, in accordance with Article 33, paragraph 2 of the Copyright Law, that his work shall not be reprinted or excerpted, he shall attach such a declaration when the work is published in a newspaper or periodical.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　著作权人依照[著作权法第四十条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-40)第三款声明不得对其作品制作录音制品的，应当在该作品合法录制为录音制品时声明。

**Article 31** Where a copyright owner declares, in accordance with Article 40, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Law, that sound recordings shall not be made of his work, he shall make the declaration when the work is lawfully recorded as a sound recording.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　依照[著作权法第二十三条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-23)、第三十三条第二款、第四十条第三款的规定，使用他人作品的，应当自使用该作品之日起2个月内向著作权人支付报酬。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../民事与商事/行政法规/教育科技/著作权集体管理条例.md#art-47" title="被《著作权集体管理条例》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** Where a work is used in accordance with the provisions of Article 23, Article 33, paragraph 2, and Article 40, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Law, remuneration shall be paid to the copyright owner within two months from the date of use of the work.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国人、无国籍人在中国境内的表演，受著作权法保护。  
外国人、无国籍人根据中国参加的国际条约对其表演享有的权利，受著作权法保护。

**Article 33** The performances of foreigners and stateless persons within the territory of China shall be protected by the Copyright Law.  
The rights enjoyed by foreigners and stateless persons in their performances under international treaties to which China is a party shall be protected by the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外国人、无国籍人在中国境内制作、发行的录音制品，受著作权法保护。  
外国人、无国籍人根据中国参加的国际条约对其制作、发行的录音制品享有的权利，受著作权法保护。

**Article 34** The sound recordings produced and distributed by foreigners and stateless persons within the territory of China shall be protected by the Copyright Law.  
The rights enjoyed by foreigners and stateless persons in the sound recordings they produce and distribute under international treaties to which China is a party shall be protected by the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　外国的广播电台、电视台根据中国参加的国际条约对其播放的广播、电视节目享有的权利，受著作权法保护。

**Article 35** The rights enjoyed by foreign radio stations and television stations in the broadcasting and television programs they broadcast under international treaties to which China is a party shall be protected by the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有[著作权法第四十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)所列侵权行为，同时损害社会公共利益，非法经营额5万元以上的，著作权行政管理部门可处非法经营额1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有非法经营额或者非法经营额5万元以下的，著作权行政管理部门根据情节轻重，可处25万元以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where an act of infringement listed in Article 48 of the Copyright Law is committed, which also harms the public interest, and the illegal business turnover is 50,000 yuan or more, the copyright administrative department may impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal business turnover; where there is no illegal business turnover or the illegal business turnover is less than 50,000 yuan, the copyright administrative department may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有[著作权法第四十八条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-48)所列侵权行为，同时损害社会公共利益的，由地方人民政府著作权行政管理部门负责查处。  
国务院著作权行政管理部门可以查处在全国有重大影响的侵权行为。

**Article 37** Where an act of infringement listed in Article 48 of the Copyright Law is committed, which also harms the public interest, the copyright administrative department of the local people's government shall be responsible for investigating and handling the matter.  
The copyright administrative department under the State Council may investigate and handle acts of infringement that have significant nationwide impact.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自2002年9月15日起施行。1991年5月24日国务院批准、1991年5月30日国家版权局发布的《中华人民共和国著作权法实施条例》同时废止。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 15, 2002. The Regulations for the Implementation of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, approved by the State Council on May 24, 1991 and promulgated by the National Copyright Administration on May 30, 1991, shall be abolished simultaneously.
